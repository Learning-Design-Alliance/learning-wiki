# Orient both students and faculty and communicate learning objectives consistently before and during case-based flipped learning

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Effective implementation requires "clear, consistent, and ongoing communication among all participants". Orientation sessions for students and faculty build shared understanding and commitment; faculty accustomed to lecturing need to understand the goals, structure, and pedagogical rationale of the approach, and multi-instructor courses need coordinated preparation supported by a detailed syllabus. For students, learning objectives should be clearly communicated and reinforced throughout so they understand expectations, why each task matters, and how tasks contribute to learning progression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Orientation sessions for both students and faculty; a detailed syllabus outlining components, objectives, strategies, and assessment; reinforcement of learning objectives throughout the course
#### Constraints
- Many faculty members are more accustomed to traditional lecture formats, and multi-instructor case-based courses require coordinated preparation across diverse clinical backgrounds

### Target Learners
- Preclinical medical students and the faculty implementing flipped learning

### Target Learning Goals
- Shared understanding of and commitment to case-based flipped learning for clinical reasoning development
