# Gamified team point system with public tracking and a final excellence award

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports a "low-tech and transparent" gamified incentive: student teams chose names, and participation points in TPS discussions were marked publicly on a whiteboard by the instructor. The top team at module end received a monetary prize, the 'Excellence in Cardiovascular Pharmacology' award, and formal recognition. The authors describe this team-based award system as "an additional motivational strategy to enhance student engagement throughout the course."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor commitment to track and publicly display points throughout the course
- A tangible incentive (monetary prize and formal recognition)
#### Constraints
- Implemented in a single course module; motivational effect was not separately measured

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pharmacy students

### Target Learning Goals
- sustained participation in collaborative learning activities
