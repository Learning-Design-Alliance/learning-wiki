# Give initial teacher support to pupils at very low reading levels before expecting independent reading

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The process evaluation found that AR assumes pupils can read independently, but the weakest readers could not yet do so. The report's key conclusion states that "Pupils at very low levels of reading may not be independent readers and would need initial support from teacher to start reading books". Teachers supporting such pupils used the independent reading time for loud reading and one-to-one tutoring, since AR itself contains no tutoring element.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher time within independent reading sessions for one-to-one support with the lowest-level readers
#### Constraints
- AR has no element of teachers tutoring pupils who are at very low reading levels

### Target Learners
- Pupils at very low levels of reading

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- Book selection within ZPD
