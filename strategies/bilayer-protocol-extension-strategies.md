# Extend the bilayer protocol with pharmacology, noise analysis, optical monitoring, and tailored software

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose refinements for students attending more laboratory sessions: repeating single-channel gramicidin recordings in the presence of pharmacological agents affecting channel gating, e.g. 50 mM fluoxetine; introducing advanced topics such as determining single-channel current and mean opening times via autocorrelation analysis of current noise; adapting the chamber for simultaneous optical monitoring of bilayer formation using thin-film interference (the black lipid membrane appearance); and developing open-source data acquisition and visualization software tailored to the protocol, which the authors state they are currently developing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The existing OpenPicoAmp setup and protocol; recording faster events from other channels may require adapting the amplifier electronics
#### Constraints
- At this stage the authors do not provide any open source software for data acquisition and visualization

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students attending multiple advanced laboratory sessions

### Target Learning Goals
- studying channel pharmacology and gating
- noise analysis of single-channel currents
- optical observation of bilayer thinning
