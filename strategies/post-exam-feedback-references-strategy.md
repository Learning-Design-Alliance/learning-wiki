# Provide post-exam feedback with correct answers and guideline references to increase educational value

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose enhancing the exam's formative function by giving students feedback after completion: "Providing correct answers with references to guidelines or other resources—potentially supported by AI—could increase the examination's educational value and promote student learning." Currently students receive no direct feedback after completing the examination.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Correct answers with references to guidelines or other resources after exam completion
#### Constraints
- The impact of such feedback on learning has not yet been evaluated; the authors call for future research

### Target Learners
- Fifth- and sixth-year medical students taking the European Prescribing Exam

### Target Learning Goals
- Safe prescribing knowledge and skills
