# Deploy video-based student-tutor courses to expand ultrasound course availability and relieve faculty staffing constraints

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend the combined video-based, student tutor concept as a way to meet rising demand for undergraduate ultrasound education where faculty-student ratios are low and courses are overbooked. They state it "has the potential to increase course availability and FS resource allocation", offering flexibility for faculty staff and university infrastructure, while keeping tutor supervision in place given limited standalone video acceptance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained student tutors with a faculty back-up supervisor
- Teaching videos aligned with the same learning objectives as the faculty-led course
#### Constraints
- Acceptance of the teaching videos alone was limited; participants still deemed tutor support necessary
- Future studies are warranted in larger student numbers including teaching of pathologies

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Target Learning Goals
- basic and advanced point-of-care ultrasound practical skills
