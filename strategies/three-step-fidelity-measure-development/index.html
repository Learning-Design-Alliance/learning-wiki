Three-step process for developing an intervention-specific fidelity measure¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article presents a replicable process: "(1) identifying key components, (2) establishing a measurement system (i.e. how to measure the key components and how to determine if the intervention was implemented with acceptable fidelity), and (3) evaluating reliability evidence". Key components are derived from the theory of change and analysis of sample videos, criteria are set a priori from exemplar sessions, and coders iterate training and definition refinement until reaching at least 80% agreement before double-coding a reliability subsample. The authors state the process "can serve as a model for fidelity measurement development and implementation in intervention research".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Iterative coder training and clarification until acceptable agreement (≥80%) on four videos, with coders not involved in component identification

Constraints¶

  • Development of an appropriate fidelity measure and determination of reliability evidence takes time and effort; fidelity was examined with cross-sectional data from one session per child

Target Learners¶

  • researchers and therapists evaluating interventional fidelity in rehabilitation and early intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • documenting adherence to intervention protocols in randomized controlled trials
  • differentiating an experimental intervention from the control condition

Affordances¶

  • Five Dimensions Intervention Fidelity

Claims¶

  • Some START-Play adherence measures and therapist effectiveness in engaging parents correlate positively with parent engagement; therapist experience showed no significant correlation with adherence or delivery quality [+W]
  • START-Play and usual early intervention differed significantly on all fidelity variables, differentiating the interventions [+W]
  • Most variables of the START-Play fidelity measure show good to excellent interrater reliability, with ICCs ranging from 0.77 to 0.95 [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Assess fidelity separately for instructional components and include student application when evaluating strategy instruction
