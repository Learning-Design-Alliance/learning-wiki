# Develop the CSA through a structured competency blueprint with systematic validity methodologies and nationwide professor cooperation

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends preparing CSA implementation by structuring assessment methods as a blueprint for systematic competency evaluation, establishing methodologies such as multifaceted analysis to develop valid and reliable items, and securing cooperation among professors in nationwide KM colleges. It states that "The method of assessing DKMs should be structured as a blueprint for the systematic evaluation of their competencies to help prepare for a future introduction of CSA." The survey's priority competencies and topics are offered as inputs to that blueprint.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cooperation among professors in nationwide KM colleges
- Systematic methodologies such as multifaceted analysis established to develop valid and reliable items
#### Constraints
- Existing CPX and OSCE studies in KM are mainly case reports of individual classes with limited validity and reliability evaluation
- Clinical professors were divided between 2025 and 2029 as the appropriate introduction time

### Target Learners
- Doctors of Korean Medicine candidates for licensure

### Target Learning Goals
- Clinical skills, patient care, communication, and clinical management competencies assessed at licensure
