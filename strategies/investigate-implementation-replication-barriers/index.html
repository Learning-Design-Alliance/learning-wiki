Investigate potential barriers to implementation and replication when documenting a successful program¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Alongside describing a program's effective elements, examine what could hinder implementation and replication. The paper states it "investigates potential barriers to implementation and replication" of the BELL summer program, pairing element documentation with an analysis of obstacles adopters may face.
