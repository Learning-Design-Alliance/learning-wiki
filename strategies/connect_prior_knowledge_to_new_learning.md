# Connect Prior Knowledge to New Learning

## Description
Building connections to prior understandings and experiences helps learners integrate new information into their existing knowledge base. This includes activating prior knowledge before introducing new concepts. It also involves relating new information to learners' personal experiences and real-world contexts. The goal is to make learning more meaningful and relevant for learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructors to be aware of learners' prior knowledge and experiences. Instructors need to be trained in effective strategies for activating prior knowledge and making connections to new learning.
#### Constraints
- May require more time and effort. Some learners may have gaps in their prior knowledge. It's essential to provide additional support and scaffolding as needed.

### Target Learning Goals
<!-- Link to sub-claims -->

### Affordances
- Supports comprehension by connecting new information to prior knowledge, improves retention by making learning more meaningful and relevant, promotes meaningful learning by relating new information to learners' personal experiences, and supports transfer of knowledge by helping learners apply what they have learned in new contexts.

### Personalization
- Tailor instruction to learners' prior knowledge and experiences. Provide opportunities for learners to share their own experiences and perspectives. Use analogies and metaphors to connect new concepts to familiar ones.
