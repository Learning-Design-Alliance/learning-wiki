# Assess procedural skill transfer with blinded video-based workplace assessment using a structured checklist

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study video-recorded each resident's real-patient cannulation with the ultrasound monitor image on one screen, then a group-allocation-blinded expert scored performance with a 17-item checklist scored 1 or 0 per question. The authors ground this in Miller's pyramid, noting "workplace-based assessment results show a significant correlation with skill proficiency in clinical practice".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video recording of both technique and ultrasound monitor image on one screen
- A checklist based on published training objectives and an assessor blinded to allocation
#### Constraints
- The checklist was non-validated because no standardised assessment tool exists for this procedure; all questions were equally weighted

### Target Learners
- anesthesiology residents

### Target Learning Goals
- ultrasound-guided radial artery cannulation proficiency
