# Integrate structured handoff communication training earlier in the nurse anesthesia curriculum, potentially within the first year of study

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A curriculum design recommendation from the article's implications for practice: programs should consider integrating structured handoff communication training earlier in the curriculum, potentially within the first year of study, to establish foundational communication patterns before clinical rotations. The article further recommends designing simulation scenarios to incorporate multiple opportunities for handoff communication across different clinical contexts (preoperative, intraoperative, postoperative, emergency situations) to build versatility and confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation scenarios designed to incorporate multiple opportunities for handoff communication across different clinical contexts.
#### Constraints
- The article notes this recommendation is based on qualitative feedback from a single nurse anesthesia program at one institution with a small sample size (N = 15).

### Target Learners
- Doctoral nurse anesthesia students early in their program

### Target Learning Goals
- Establish foundational handoff communication patterns before clinical rotations
- Build versatility and confidence in handoff communication across clinical contexts
