# Establish a national Ed-tech Product Information Exchange website

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends a national online "Ed-tech Product Information Exchange" website that would list and describe available ed-tech products, report formal research studies and pilot studies with results, report consumer reactions, and facilitate networking between providers, districts, and evaluators. It would serve as a one-stop-shop combining product information, pricing guidance, evaluation findings, and customer satisfaction reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comprehensive descriptions of products in different areas
- Evaluation evidence from pilots and rigorous studies
- Consumer satisfaction reports
#### Constraints
- Existing networks or websites listing products are not seen as necessarily representing full ranges of products or assurance of evidence or peer review

### Target Learners
- K-12 school district administrators and ed-tech providers

### Target Learning Goals
- Facilitating discovery and evaluation of ed-tech products
