# Plan and structure video feedback around signposts and feed-forward points before recording.

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article suggests that staff think about the generic feedback and identify 'signposts' before recording a video, producing a structured response that highlights what was expected and what is expected to improve future performance. This planning time improves feedback quality by encouraging staff to consider how to use the feedback opportunity more wisely. It also helps avoid the problem of repetitive written feedback containing comments that are unlikely to be usable by students.
