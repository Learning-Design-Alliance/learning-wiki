# Use AI-handled simulation mechanics to shift class time and assessment toward reflection on parameter choices

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For curricular integration, the article proposes assignments in which students use NMC2025 to identify and simulate a candidate material, while instructors verify process via the JSON and input outputs. The authors write that "the real assessment can focus on reflection – e.g., a report where students discuss what they learned from the AI feedback and how they chose their final parameters." Because the tool handles input-file mechanics, instructors can reallocate class time to conceptual discussion (e.g., why a material has a larger band gap) rather than debugging input files, and different sessions can emphasize literature research or DFT technical skills.
