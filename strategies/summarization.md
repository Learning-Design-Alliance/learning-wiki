# Summarization

## Description
Explicit teaching of the elements of a summary of a text leads to improved ability and increased confidence in writing summaries. Having learners write summaries about what they read is a key recommendation from Writing to Read. In addition, summarization is an increasingly common expectation as students advance in their education and are assigned more complex texts to read and comprehend. Connect this instruction and practice with increasingly complex texts to reinforce learners’ comprehension as well as writing skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit instruction in summarization, complex texts for practice, feedback on summaries
#### Constraints
- May require significant time and effort, effectiveness depends on text complexity and instruction quality
