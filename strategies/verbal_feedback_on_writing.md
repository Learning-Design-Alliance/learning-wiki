# Verbal Feedback on Writing

## Description
Verbal feedback uses voice recording technology to provide balanced feedback and promote a growth mindset. Instead of writing comments on student papers, the teacher makes symbols or writes numbers and speaks comments into a voice recorder. The first comment should express high expectations and a growth mindset. Students listen to the feedback on devices and revise their work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher familiarity with voice recording technology, student access to devices for listening to feedback, and a rubric for the assignment.
#### Constraints
- Requires access to technology and may be time-consuming for the teacher.
