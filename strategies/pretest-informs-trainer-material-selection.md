# Administer a pretest before training so instructors can tailor methods and materials to participants' prior understanding

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before the training content is delivered, all participants take a pretest: "Seluruh peserta diklat sebelum mengikuti diklat berjenjang wajib untuk mengikuti kegiatan pretest terlebih dahulu." The pretest results are used by the committee as discussion material with the instructors, so that each narasumber can deliver content using appropriate methods and teaching materials, with a set schedule ensuring all material is covered and understood.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pretest must be completed by all participants before material delivery, and results must be communicated to the instructors
