# Include case studies, worksheets and conceptual change texts in textbooks, and vary fossil examples beyond animals

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that textbooks incorporate the material types their intervention used, because each played a distinct role: case studies link concepts to daily life, conceptual change texts replace misconceptions with scientific truths, and worksheets reinforce group learning. They also recommend that textbooks include different fossil examples in addition to animals, since students' drawings showed a dinosaur-heavy conception of fossils.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases should reflect reality and attract students' attention
#### Constraints
- Recommendation grounded in one quasi-experimental study with 40 students in a single school

### Target Learners
- 5th-grade primary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Eliminating misconceptions and building conceptual understanding in science topics

## Claims

- [Students' fossil drawings overwhelmingly focused on the animal category, with a marked preference for dinosaurs](../claims/fossil-drawings-focus-on-animals-dinosaurs.md) [+W]
- [The most widely-voiced student misconception about fossils was that fossils are \"the fuel obtained from under the ground\", voiced pre-test by 4 experimental and 11 control students](../claims/fossil-fuel-misconception-prevalence.md) [+W]
- [5E-based guidance materials produced significantly better post-test conceptual understanding of \"What is the Earth's Crust Composed of?\" than the existing textbook, with no pre-test difference between groups](../claims/5e-guidance-materials-improve-earth-crust-conceptual-understanding.md) [+W]
- [Natural-monument misconceptions (e.g., \"It is the name used for mausoleums\") were largely eliminated post-test, with 18 experimental and 12 control students reaching complete understanding](../claims/natural-monument-misconceptions-eliminated-posttest.md) [+W]
