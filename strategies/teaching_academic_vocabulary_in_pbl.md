# Teaching Academic Vocabulary in PBL

## Description
In project-based learning (PBL), teaching academic vocabulary involves identifying specialized or technical words that students will encounter during a project and planning instruction to teach these words. This ensures that all students, particularly English Language Learners (ELLs), have the necessary vocabulary to succeed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Careful selection of academic vocabulary relevant to the project, explicit instruction strategies, and opportunities for practice and application.
#### Constraints
- Requires additional planning and instructional time, and may necessitate ongoing adjustments based on student progress and assessment.
