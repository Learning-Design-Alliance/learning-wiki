Use a reference table linking SAMR levels to context of mediation, teaching intention, use strategies and teacher reflection¶

Description¶

The article presents a table (adapted from the SAMR model) to "help teachers analyze how ICTs can add different values to practices, always linking them to the context and its conditions of mediation". It organizes Substitution, Augmentation/Modification and Redefinition by context of ICTs mediation, tendency of each level, teaching intentions, ICT use strategies, teacher reflection questions and added value (conservative, enriching or transformative).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers analyze pedagogical uses of ICTs within the scope of the joint activity involving teachers, students and contents.

Constraints¶

  • The table supports both horizontal and vertical reading because of the fluid nature of the act of teaching and learning.

Claims¶

  • Of the 116 teachers, 66.4% registered a free-text experience in which ICTs redefined their teaching practice [+W]
  • Practices P1, P2, P3, P4 and P7 received the highest percentages at the Redefinition level [+W]
  • Practices P10–P14 received the highest percentages at the Substitution level, indicating ICTs did not contribute significantly to their development [+W]
  • Teachers in a Brazilian public institution reported ICT integration concentrated at the Augmentation and Modification levels of the SAMR model during emergency remote teaching [+W]
  • Reports of transformation in teaching practices are associated with changes in the roles of teachers and students, with transforming uses focused on active methodologies [+W]
  • Most ITS applications augment existing mathematics teaching practices rather than transform instruction, indicating technological sophistication does not independently determine pedagogical value [+W]
