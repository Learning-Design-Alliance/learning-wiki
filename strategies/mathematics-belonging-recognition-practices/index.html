Cultivate mathematics belonging by publicly recognizing every student as a mathematical thinker¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Beyond general social belonging, teachers cultivated a sense of "mathematics belonging" by explicitly communicating that each and every student could be successful at math, routinely recognizing students' abilities as mathematical thinkers in front of their peers, and highlighting the learning and success of students who doubted their mathematical abilities. Teachers also gained knowledge of each student as an individual and designed opportunities so every student could contribute to the classroom math community.
