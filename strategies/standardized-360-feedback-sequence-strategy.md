# Standardized 360° feedback sequence: self-reflection, peer feedback, SP feedback, then trainer synopsis

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article prescribes a fixed order for the feedback process after each role-play: the trainer first asks the active student for self-reflection, then observing students give behavior-based feedback and feed-forward from their notes, then the SP re-enters to give the patient perspective, and finally the trainer summarizes key points with an expert opinion in a written synopsis. The SP "had left the room after the role play" so that "SP feedback could not influence the group discussion and vice versa".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SPs trained beforehand in providing behavioral feedback
- Observing students hold structured monitoring tasks and notes from the theoretical introduction
#### Constraints
- At all levels feedback must be constructive and related to visible and modifiable behaviors

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduates in small-group communication skills training

### Target Learning Goals
- giving and receiving specific, behavior-orientated feedback; internalizing communication standards

### Affordances
- Cst 360 Behavior Feedback Concept
