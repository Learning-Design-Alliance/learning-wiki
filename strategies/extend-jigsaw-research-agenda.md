In the discussion the author proposes a future research agenda: "various studies can be made in connection with both various topics of grammar and speaking, writing, reading and listening skills", and that studying not only Jigsaw I but also other cooperative learning techniques "will lead to more reliable results." This frames the present null result as limited to one topic area and one technique in one school.
