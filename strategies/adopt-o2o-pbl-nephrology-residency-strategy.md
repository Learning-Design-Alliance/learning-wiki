# Adopt O2O combined with PBL as a teaching modality in standardized nephrology residency training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend promoting their combined online-to-offline and problem-based learning approach in nephrology residency training. The strategy involves distributing pre-class and after-class learning materials through the Ding Talk network platform, assigning pre-class and after-class homework, initiating live broadcast during face-to-face teaching, and facilitating study-group discussion of instructor-raised clinical problems. They state the method "could be promoted in the standardized residency training of nephrology" based on their comparative results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors trained in both O2O/PBL and LBT methods who have passed regular assessments
- An online platform (Ding Talk) and smart electronic screens as teaching media
- Alignment with the residency training teaching syllabus
#### Constraints
- The authors note the study was conducted only in their institution with a relatively small number of learners, and only short-term results via test scores were assessed

### Target Learners
- Residency trainees in internal medicine, particularly nephrology rotations

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and clinical thinking examination performance without reducing teaching satisfaction
