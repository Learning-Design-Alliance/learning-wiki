# Use a three-step process—measure selection and refinement, exogeneity confirmation, and moderation analysis—to examine moderation of early-intervention effects by later school experiences

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a roadmap for examining whether later school and classroom experiences moderate preschool intervention effects. The authors state that "The 3-step process followed in this demonstration (measure selection and refinement, confirmation that elementary school experiences can be treated as exogenous variables, and moderation analyses) will be made relevant to other applications." The first step selects measures empirically associated with child development, assessed similarly across time points and with acceptable internal consistency reliability; the second confirms exogeneity of the moderators; the third tests condition-by-quality interactions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Measures must have prior evidence of empirical association with children's development, similar assessment methods at each time point, and acceptable internal consistency reliability.
#### Constraints
- The article reports no substantive findings; the roadmap is presented as a demonstration whose usefulness will be shown in other applications.

### Target Learners
- Children transitioning from preschool into elementary school

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining early intervention impacts on cognitive and social-emotional outcomes into elementary school
