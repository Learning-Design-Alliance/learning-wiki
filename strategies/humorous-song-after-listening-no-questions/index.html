Play humorous songs immediately after regular course-material listening, without follow-up questioning, to keep learners relaxed¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The treatment procedure had learners listen to a humorous song for several minutes immediately after working on the course material's listening part. "The instructors were required not to ask any questions after the songs ended to be sure that the students were relaxed while listening to the songs." This no-questioning rule is the implementable core of the strategy: the song functions as a relaxed, enjoyable listening experience rather than a tested task.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Songs must follow the regular course-material listening activity
  • Instructors must not ask questions after the songs end

Constraints¶

  • Materials should be adapted to non-native learners, with higher quality sound and slower text reading pace

Target Learners¶

  • intermediate EFL learners in language institutes

Target Learning Goals¶

  • listening comprehension
  • relaxation and reduced anxiety during language learning

Claims¶

  • Humorous-song treatment shows no significant difference between EFL learners' immediate and delayed listening recall [~W]
  • EFL learners who listened to humorous songs gained significantly on a listening comprehension test while a control group taught without the songs did not, though the groups were not compared directly [+W]
