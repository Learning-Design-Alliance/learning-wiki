# Leveraging Strengths Thinking

## Description
Shifting from deficit to strengths thinking, which views families as having significant expertise and resources that can inform and improve student learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training resources, cultural competency experts, and a supportive school administration.
#### Constraints
- Requires ongoing effort and commitment from school staff. May be challenging to change ingrained attitudes and biases.
