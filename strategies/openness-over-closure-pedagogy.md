# Embrace pedagogical strategies that emphasize openness rather than closure and posit change as the norm

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because dialogism treats flux as the natural order of the rhetorical situation, the paper recommends embracing pedagogical strategies which emphasize openness, rather than closure, and which posit change as the norm. The author illustrates with restoring the ratios to Burke's Pentad, even though they admittedly complicate things for teachers and students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Acceptance that the rhetorical situation is in relentless change rather than static
#### Constraints
- Resisting closure is difficult for teachers accustomed to static rhetorical elements

### Target Learners
- college composition students

### Target Learning Goals
- appreciating the composing process as continuously changing
