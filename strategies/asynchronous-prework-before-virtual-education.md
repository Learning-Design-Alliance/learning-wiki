# Use asynchronous pre-work to improve learner engagement and preparation before virtual education sessions

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before the videoconference-based session, all learners in both arms completed online asynchronous pre-work in a learning management system: an unfolding case scenario with five multiple-choice questions covering lactation physiology, physical assessment, clinical decision making, and management of clinical lactation concerns. The authors report this design choice directly: "We used asynchronous prework for all learners to improve engagement and preparation for the educational session, either telesimulation or didactics."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online learning management system to host the pre-work
- Case content and learning objectives held identical across educational formats
