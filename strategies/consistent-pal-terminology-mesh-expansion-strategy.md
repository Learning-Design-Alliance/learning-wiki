# Adopt consistent PAL terminology based on the six proposed groupings and expand MeSH terms

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article recommends that future PAL research and programme descriptions use the six proposed terms consistently, so that programme implementation, evidence building, and cross-institution communication are eased. It states: "We urge consistent use of the PAL terms based on the suggested groupings offered in this paper. Expansion of the MeSH (Medical Subject Headings) terms is necessary." The authors acknowledge consensus may be slow but argue long-term benefits outweigh the hindrance.

## Design Implications
