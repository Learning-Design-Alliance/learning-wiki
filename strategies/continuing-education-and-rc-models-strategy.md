# Strengthen RC competence through continuing education and RC models supporting learning from experience

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends strengthening nursing professionals' restorative care competence through regular continuing education and the development of RC models. It states "Continuing educational investments, such as giving nursing professionals access to evidence-based continuing education online courses in RC, are necessary" and that "the establishment of RC models that support learning from experience would be beneficial" because much learning occurs during everyday practice. Nurse leaders are identified as playing an important role in creating effective learning environments and enabling continuing education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurse leaders enabling access to continuing education and creating supportive learning environments
#### Constraints
- A major part of learning occurs during everyday practices, so education alone is insufficient

### Target Learners
- Nursing professionals in geriatric care units

### Target Learning Goals
- Restorative care competence development, including assessment, goal-oriented practice, environmental modification, and cooperation with relatives
