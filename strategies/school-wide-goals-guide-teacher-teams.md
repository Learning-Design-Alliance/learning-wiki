# Use school-wide instructional goals to focus teacher-team collaboration on common instructional issues

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The case-study schools used school-wide instructional goals—such as literacy integration across disciplines, assessments, or improving differentiation—to give collaborative teacher-team work concrete focus and content. One leader explained, "We make a priority of what our professional development goals are for the year," and school-level goals filtered down to department and grade-level meetings, countering the tendency for bureaucratic issues to dominate meeting agendas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
