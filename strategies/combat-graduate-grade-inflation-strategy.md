# Combat graduate grade inflation with standardized grading rubrics, faculty assessment coaching, rethinking student evaluations in tenure decisions, and alternative feedback methods

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (7 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 5 studies (2 associational, 2 design, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 5 report an effect size · 10 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' discussion lays out responses to graduate grade inflation. They recommend that "educators might consider implementing standardized grading rubrics or offering coaching sessions to help faculty develop standardized assessment practices" and that institutions "rethink the use of student evaluations in tenure and promotion decisions" because instructors may be incentivized toward leniency. They also point to peer assessment and narrative feedback as alternatives when grades lose signal.
