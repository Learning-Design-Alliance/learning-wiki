# Personalised, strengths-based university support combining ASD awareness training with individualised support networks

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that university support systems increase awareness of ASD among staff and students, consider individual differences, and capitalise on autistic students' strengths: "newly developed support systems should focus on increasing awareness of ASD among staff and other students, while considering individual differences between students with autism, and trying to capitalise on potential strengths." They propose initial screening of individual strengths and competencies to tailor support, citing a peer-mentoring pilot with flexible individualised support that found better academic and social performance and higher retention.

## Design Implications
