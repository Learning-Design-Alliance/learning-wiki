# Safeguards for equitable and unbiased educational GAI deployment: access subsidies, bias testing, and diverse training datasets

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends concrete measures for addressing ethical challenges of GAI in education. For equitable access, it endorses "providing subsidies for technology access in economically disadvantaged areas or offering additional discounts in less developed regions." For bias, it calls for extensive testing phases to identify and amend biased AI responses before they affect students, and for using diverse datasets in AI training to avoid biased educational outcomes. Stakeholders including authorities, teachers, and students must collaboratively engage to cultivate a more equitable educational framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaborative engagement of educational stakeholders, including authorities, teachers, and students
#### Constraints
- Fair access to AI-enhanced educational tools may be impeded by socioeconomic disparities

### Target Learners
- Students in economically disadvantaged or less developed regions

### Target Learning Goals
- Equitable access to AI-enhanced educational tools and unbiased educational outcomes
