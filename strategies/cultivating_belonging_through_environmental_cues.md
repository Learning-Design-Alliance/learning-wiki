# Cultivating Belonging Through Environmental Cues

## Description
This strategy involves using simple yet powerful cues in the classroom environment to promote a sense of belonging among students. This includes ensuring representations of all students, providing cues that signal safety and recognition, and creating inclusive classroom scenarios. The aim is to mitigate feelings of stereotype threat and foster a more inclusive environment where students feel valued and supported. The strategies promote belonging through simple cues and by including representations of all students in your classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need to be mindful of the messages conveyed by the classroom environment. Requires careful selection of materials and resources.
#### Constraints
- May require significant changes to classroom setup. Risk of tokenism if not implemented thoughtfully.
