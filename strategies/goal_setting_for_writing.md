# Goal Setting for Writing

## Description
Setting clear goals for writing, encouraging peer support, self-assessment, and providing feedback on progress. Clear goals for writing are specific to the purpose and type of writing task. Learners set explicit goals to guide their writing, and work with them to track progress. Specific goals (e.g., “to persuade a voter”) are more effective than general goals (e.g., “write a 200-word essay”). Discuss writing quality with learners and identify areas for improvement. This supports motivation and improves writing quality.
