# Recommendations for future interprofessional OMP workshops: balance professions, stress efficiency, add a follow-up session, and refine micro-skill wording

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on their findings, the authors recommend holding interprofessional OMP workshops with teachers representing both professions present for credibility and inclusivity, balancing the distribution of physicians and nurses across workshop groups to prevent power imbalances, stressing the OMP's efficiency further since both professions cited lack of time, further highlighting the value of experience-based knowledge in rules of thumb, and adding "a follow-up session some weeks after the workshop would likely be beneficial to reinforce the OMP and address any questions and concerns". They also suggest the phrasing of one micro-skill may need further refinement for nursing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Material adapted for both physicians and nurses
- Teachers representing both professions present at workshops
#### Constraints
- The phrasing of one micro-skill was perceived by one group as emphasising diagnostics too narrowly and may need further refinement

### Target Learners
- Clinical tutors (physicians and nurses) in primary health care

### Target Learning Goals
- Implementation of structured clinical tutoring using the OMP
