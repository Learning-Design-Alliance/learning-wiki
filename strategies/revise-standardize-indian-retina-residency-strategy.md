# Revise and standardize Indian ophthalmology residency to include basic retinal diagnosis and treatment, with wet-lab and virtual/augmented reality surgical training

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend that the current ophthalmology residency program be revised and standardized to include adequate training for common noncommunicable eye diseases such as diabetic retinopathy and AMD. Because "surgical training in vitreoretinal disorders is not possible during the residency", they propose that it "could be initiated with wet labs and virtual and/or augmented reality training". This addresses the large variation they document across programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized curriculum with comparable outputs or board certification, as now available for some post-residency fellowship programs.
#### Constraints
- Limited residency time makes in-depth vitreoretinal surgical training during residency impossible; surgical practice must come via wet labs and simulation.

### Target Learners
- Ophthalmology residents in India

### Target Learning Goals
- Diagnosis and medical management of common retinal diseases; basic vitreoretinal surgical exposure
