Future research should identify the instructional, learner, and contextual conditions under which GenAI most effectively supports motivation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Given very high heterogeneity, the article recommends that "Future research should identify the instructional, learner, and contextual conditions under which GenAI can most effectively support students' motivation." This directs researchers toward experimentally manipulating candidate boundary conditions (e.g., education stage, device type, subject domain) rather than relying on study-level correlational moderators. It follows from the authors' caution that subgroup results are exploratory signals, not causal moderation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • New studies should experimentally manipulate contextual and design conditions rather than treat them as study-level characteristics

Constraints¶

  • Current moderator findings are exploratory and confounded with study-level characteristics

Target Learners¶

  • students in formal educational settings from primary school through university

Target Learning Goals¶

  • learning motivation
