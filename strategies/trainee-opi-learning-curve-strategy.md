# Use longitudinal OPI learning curves combined with operative segmentation of trainee-autonomous case portions for trainee skill assessment

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose extending their expert-surgeon learning-curve methodology to trainees: capturing OPIs combined with operative segmentation during robotic hernia repair, particularly for portions with complete trainee autonomy, and validating multi-institutionally. This would "enable for meaningful derivation of OPI-based learning curves for trainees" compared against the expert's eight-year longitudinal curves, providing objective measures of surgical skill acquisition rather than case-log counts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- OPI capture combined with operative segmentation isolating portions of complete trainee autonomy
- Multi-institutional validation
#### Constraints
- Findings derive from a single-expert series; trainee comparisons in this study were limited by shorter trainee tenure and lower consecutive case volume

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees learning robotic ventral hernia repair

### Target Learning Goals
- objective assessment of technical skill acquisition during training
