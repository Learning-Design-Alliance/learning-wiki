# Implement process-oriented procedural training programs and then assess transfer of the approach to real-world clinical settings

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' forward-looking recommendation is to move beyond the simulated environment: "the next step is the implementation of a training program, based on a process-oriented approach, for ultrasound-guided central venous access placement and the subsequent assessment of transferring this approach to real-world clinical settings." The goal is to determine whether process-oriented training produces differences in relevant clinical outcomes compared with traditional teaching methodologies, since translation of procedural skills to real-world settings is described as crucial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated reference process model for the target procedure
- A simulation-based training program already shown to develop the target competence
#### Constraints
- This study provided no evidence of transfer to a clinical setting, a limitation the authors state explicitly

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate medical residents training in invasive procedures

### Target Learning Goals
- Transfer of procedurally proficient, low-rework skill execution from simulation to clinical practice
