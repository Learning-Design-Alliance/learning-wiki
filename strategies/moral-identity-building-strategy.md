# Strengthen students' moral education and moral identity as a buffer against the antisocial effects of achievement and praise

## Description
The article recommends that schools improve junior high students' moral standards because moral identity is a boundary condition: with high moral identity, no level of academic performance or praise promotes antisocial behavior. It states administrators "should strengthen students' moral education and improve students' level of moral standards", noting this "can not only rely on the moral courses but also embed the moral disciplines in school culture".
