Implement the grouping method from the start of the classes rather than only in the final period¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on student feedback and the study's conclusions, the article recommends applying the grouping method across the whole course rather than only during the final period. A student suggested, "I think it's better to start the grouping from the beginning not only in the final period", and the conclusions state that implementing the grouping method from the start of the classes deserves attention for raising achievement further.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A grouping method with incentive points operating at the seatwork stage

Constraints¶

  • The study itself implemented the method only during the final term covering four topics, so full-course effects were not tested

Target Learners¶

  • University students in Integral Calculus

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Achievement in Integral Calculus problem solving

Claims¶

  • Students taught with Razalas' Method of Grouping achieved significantly higher gain scores than students in Individual Learning in Integral Calculus [+M]
  • Qualitative data indicated the Razalas' Method of grouping developed self-confidence, encouraged effective communication and facilitated exchange of ideas toward a common goal [+W]
  • Both groups improved significantly from pretest to posttest, and the experimental group's posttest scores significantly exceeded the control group's [+M]
