# Adapt the paper dome for tactile use by blind or visually impaired students using relief stars and marked lines

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The dome's tangible format makes it adaptable for tactile perception. When printed on A3 paper the maximum diameter increases to 34 cm, facilitating recognition of smaller structures. "Stars and Solar System bodies can be represented in relief using beads, grains of sand, or raised-ink pens", while horizon, constellation, and ecliptic lines can be marked with threads of suitable thickness. The expectation is that the dome enables recognition of both individual celestial objects and their spatial orientation.
