# Provide welcoming schools with more ongoing training, time for reflection, and targeted support when integrating displaced students and staff

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on the report's conclusion, school leaders in receiving schools "did not know what it took to be a successful welcoming school, suggesting a need for training that is more ongoing, along with time for reflections and targeted support." Staff also said planning for mergers was insufficient and that they wished they had more training and support on welcoming staff and students who had just lost their schools. The strategy addresses integration of grieving displaced populations into receiving school communities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must provide transition planning time, principal transition coordinators, and sustained training and support for welcoming school staff rather than one-time resources
#### Constraints
- One-time influxes of extra funds and supports were hard to sustain after the first year due to subsequent budget cuts

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in receiving schools
- Displaced students from closed schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining academic progress during school transitions
- Building cohesive school communities and relationships after mergers
