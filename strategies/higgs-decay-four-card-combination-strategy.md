# Represent Higgs decay modes as four-card lepton combinations in the card game

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The game maps the decay modes the ATLAS and CMS experiments found — H0 → γγ, H → Z0Z0 and H0 → W+W- — onto combinations of black-and-white lepton cards listed in Table 1, where photons are represented as pairs of charged leptons. The most exciting part of play is "the search for a combination of four black-and-white cards, that represent a possible Higgs boson decay". Turning two cards that could be completed to such a combination lets a player take a risk and turn two more.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The Table 1 list of possible Higgs decays as represented by Quark Matter cards
- Knowledge of the valid lepton-pair, meson and baryon combinations
#### Constraints
- If the Higgs attempt is unsuccessful, the player must return all four cards face down and the next player continues

### Target Learners
- school students
- lay-persons

### Target Learning Goals
- Higgs boson decay channels
- final-state particles of the 2012 LHC discovery
