# Staged mixed model integrating AI across orthopaedic training progression

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review recommends a staged approach to AI integration in orthopaedic education: "early integration of adaptive platforms and automated assessments, intermediate use of VR/AR simulation with AI-driven feedback and faculty debriefings, and advanced supervised clinical exposure supported by AI-based performance monitoring." This model positions AI as a complement to human-centred training rather than a replacement, with strict oversight including expert review of generative AI outputs and audits of assessments across all stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional capacity-building and equitable access must be central to implementation
- Strict oversight including expert review of generative AI outputs and audits of assessments
#### Constraints
- AI should complement, not replace, human-centred orthopaedic training and mentorship

### Target Learners
- Orthopaedic trainees (medical students, residents, fellows)

### Target Learning Goals
- Surgical technical skills
- Clinical reasoning
- Procedural knowledge
