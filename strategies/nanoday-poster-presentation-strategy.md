# End-of-semester public poster session (NanoDay) to advertise student work and build conference-style confidence

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a planned future development, students display their final course project in a poster presentation. The authors state that "Such an activity will not only advertise the students work and excite newcomers to this area, but also give them an opportunity of conference atmosphere and confidence of answering questions on one-to-one basis". It is proposed as part of the final course project alongside the "make something useful Nano under $5" device project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A final course project or student work suitable for poster display
- An event venue that gathers newcomers as an audience
#### Constraints
- Listed under Future Developments, i.e., proposed rather than implemented in the reported offerings

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- science communication
- answering technical questions one-to-one
- sharing researched information with the public and greater community
