# Question-selection rules for non-repetitive, full-coverage, all-level testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The expert tutor extracts pre-test and post-test questions from a question base under three rules: "First, the questions should not be repetitious even if a learner would be trained one concept several times. Second, the question must be planned for all sections of a concept entirely. Third, expert tutor plans questions in all level." Sequence, number and level of questions are then determined intelligently according to learner level and learning type.
