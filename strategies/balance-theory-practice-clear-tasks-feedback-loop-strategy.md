# Improve active bioinformatics lessons by balancing theory with practice, clarifying tasks, providing detailed software tutorials, and running a regular feedback loop

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors' recommendations for future interventions drawn from survey comments and assessment results: "This suggests the need for a balanced integration of theoretical content and practical applications to provide a solid foundation, reduce confusion and reinforce key concepts at an early stage." They further recommend clearer task instructions, more comprehensive resources and tutorials for software and database use, simplification of conceptual content for first-year students, and a regular feedback loop based on continuous student input to adjust teaching, assessment and resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding students' prior knowledge and curiosity when designing training instructions
- Development of more comprehensive resources and tutorials
#### Constraints
- The recommendations arise from first-year students encountering sequence analysis content for the first time

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduate Biology students

### Target Learning Goals
- Reducing confusion in bioinformatics concepts
- Task engagement during practical sessions
- Linking taught information to application
