# Provide need-based scholarships conditioned on limiting outside work to fewer than 10 hours per week

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Cal-Bridge scholars receive two years of need-based scholarship support, up to $10,000 per year, at their CSU campus to supplement existing grants. Because many scholars have worked 20 to 30 hours per week, or full time, during their first two years of college, "as a condition of participation, Cal-Bridge scholars are limited to working fewer than 10 hours per week." The article states that this financial support allows students to remain engaged in their classes and in program activities.
