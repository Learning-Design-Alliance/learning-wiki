# Integrate real-time engagement analytics into large-class clinical instruction to support data-informed teaching

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that instructors leverage in-class behavioral data collected via digital platforms to monitor learning dynamics at scale and respond to performance trends. It concludes that "integrating real-time engagement analytics into classroom instruction can support more responsive, data-informed teaching decisions", particularly in high-enrollment clinical courses where individualized support is constrained. This strategy rests on the finding that engagement behaviors predict quiz performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A digital platform that automatically logs student interactions and generates session-level behavioral logs
#### Constraints
- Behavioral log data may not fully capture cognitive depth or out-of-class self-directed learning

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in large-enrollment clinical courses

### Target Learning Goals
- improving in-class quiz performance through engagement monitoring
