# Offer multiple training formats alongside each other and blend digital learning with face-to-face expert contact

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because users differed in interface preference, the authors recommend that "different training formats should be offered alongside each other", possibly integrating e-learning and chatbot interfaces so presentation format is user-defined. They also suggest that "A blended learning approach, combining the advantages of technology with traditional teaching strategies, may therefore be the key to uptake in this context", pairing asynchronous e-learning with synchronous on-site learning and expert feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal coordination of tool use in practice (e.g., clear deadlines, facilitation by a coordinator, criteria for success, accreditation points)
#### Constraints
- Face-to-face feedback was considered critical by participants; fully digitalizing communication skills training was questioned

### Target Learners
- oncologists
- junior clinicians in training

### Target Learning Goals
- acquisition and transfer of information-provision communication skills to clinical consultations
