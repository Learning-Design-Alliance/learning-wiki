## Description
The review derives design implications for future AI decision-support systems in higher education, stating that "the study derives design implications for the development of more transparent, context-sensitive, and hybrid intelligence decision-support systems in higher education." It highlights the open question of how alternative forms of student data visibility, attentional guidance, and interface design could support lecturer decisions currently underrepresented, such as emotional, ethical, and curriculum decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Design should address underrepresented decision domains by making less observable processes visible and guiding lecturer attention beyond measurable behavioral indicators
#### Constraints
- Current systems predominantly make behavioral student data visible, so supporting motivation, metacognition, emotions, and learning environments requires alternative data visibility and interface approaches

### Target Learners
- Higher education lecturers

### Target Learning Goals
- Pedagogical decision-making across instructional, feedback, assessment, emotional, ethical, and curriculum domains

### Affordances
