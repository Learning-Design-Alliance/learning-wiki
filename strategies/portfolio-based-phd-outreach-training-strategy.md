# Design outreach training as a portfolio-based programme spread across the PhD timeline with a pluralistic product range

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structuring researcher outreach training around building a personal portfolio of multiple implementable products over an extended timeline rather than one or two outputs. Its stated novelty is "(a) pluralistic approach of creating a range of implementable outreach products during a PhD -training timeline and (b) building an outreach portfolio for real -world use". The approach emphasizes the professional benefits of outreach and integrates training with the students' study or research timeline to provide opportunities for practice and sustainable progress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A longer time frame that integrates with the trainees' study or research timeline
- Sessions combining theoretical background with hands-on product creation activities
- Iterative peer and expert feedback on each product
#### Constraints
- The programme described was mandatory for the PhD students as a funded network work package; the article notes shorter informal programmes benefit only a subset of motivated participants

### Target Learners
- early-stage researchers (PhD students) in STEM

### Target Learning Goals
- outreach and science communication skills
- awareness of the transferable career benefits of outreach
