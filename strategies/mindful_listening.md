# Mindful Listening

## Description
Mindful listening is a practice of focusing on sound to introduce mindfulness. It is often easier to focus on something more concrete like sound; the instruction to “notice your breath” can be a bit abstract, especially for elementary students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a quiet space and brief periods of time (e.g., 1-5 minutes). May use guided audio, videos, or instructor-led exercises.
#### Constraints
- Some learners may find it difficult to focus or may be skeptical of the practice. Requires consistent implementation to see benefits.
