# Four ways to implement Algodoo in physics lessons: design-after-lecture, problem-check, lecture-in-simulation, and variable-testing

> **Strategy** · [All strategies](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes four implementation modes for Algodoo in lessons. First, lecture chapter by chapter, then students "design a simple case study related to the subject on the simulation". Second, solve a written problem from a prepared simulation's values after the lecture, then test the theoretical result in the simulation. Third, lecture directly on Algodoo: show a situation, pose questions, explain, then re-analyse with theory. Fourth, in a case study students determine relationships between variables by testing in the program, such as angle versus maximum height in projectile motion.
