Apps's four-category framework for a working philosophy of adult education¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Apps proposes a framework adult educators can use to identify, analyze, judge, and evaluate their beliefs about adult education. He writes that "This framework includes four categories:" — the learner, the overall purpose of adult education, content or subject matter, and the learning process. The framework functions as "the skeleton on which we hang our ideas", organizing reflection on beliefs across the chapters that follow.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The educator must be willing to analyze beliefs about each of the four categories; the framework is one option among others and should remain open to criticism and change.

Constraints¶

  • Apps states it is "by no means the only one that can be used" and warns against becoming slaves to frameworks.

Target Learners¶

  • adult educators (professors, students, administrators, practitioners)

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • developing a personal working philosophy of adult education
