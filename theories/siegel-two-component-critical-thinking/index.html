Siegel's two-component account of critical thinking: reason assessment ability plus critical attitude¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper adopts Harvey Siegel's definition of the critical thinker as one who is "appropriately moved by reasons." The account has two components: a reason assessment component, "the principled assessment of reasons and their ability to warrant beliefs, claims, and actions," and a critical attitude component, the disposition to engage in such assessment. The fully critical thinker is therefore both able and disposed to assess reasons, including challenges to her own beliefs.
