# Social network analysis as a methodological approach to measuring and mapping relationships

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief presents social network analysis as a methodological approach for program evaluation. It defines SNA as 'a methodological approach to measuring and mapping relationships' and specifies two units of analysis: 'whole networks, all of the ties within a defined group, or connections that individuals have in their personal communities.' The framework organizes how evaluators can map relationship structures to study programs.
