# SkillsFWD guiding principles for equitable LER technology design

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The toolkit presents three SkillsFWD guiding principles used during assessment: Collaborative, which "Ensures the creation of LER technologies that include perspectives from diverse communities and stakeholders"; Designed for All Users, covering jargon-free, functional, and intuitive experiences regardless of ability; and Equity-Focused, ensuring access "regardless of background, bandwidth, or socio-economic status." Each principle is elaborated with items, example results, and guiding questions for evaluation.
