# Special participant observer role stepping within the artist's artistic serial

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because arting is a unitless psychic phenomenon centered in the artist's stream of consciousness, the report argues the researcher must adopt a special participant observer role. The author states that "the researcher must step within the artistic serial of which, the situation is a part, and move as closely as possible to the creating stream of consciousness." The observer takes cues from the artist's readiness and treats first-person-singular statements as having undeniable validity even when historical inaccuracy is verifiable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Indirect access to the artist's creating experience through in-process traces and stimulated process recall
#### Constraints
- The researcher must take responsibility for his impact on the art making; the ideal of leaving the phenomena untouched is not honest in passional, artistic affairs

### Target Learners
- artists in drawing series studied by researchers

### Target Learning Objectives
- qualitative, historical, contextual description of expressive situations
