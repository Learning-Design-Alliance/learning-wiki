# NSI conceptual framework: intermediary-led school networks using equity-centered continuous improvement to improve on-track outcomes

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (2 for, 5 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The evaluation team's conceptual framework describes how intermediaries create and support networks of schools using continuous improvement to improve practices related to their outcome area. Schools form CI teams of teachers, counselors, administrators, and other staff expected to engage in six core CI parameters, with the initiative centering equity for Black, Latino, and students experiencing poverty at intermediary, network, and school levels. The framework links network participation to improved educator practices and student supports, and ultimately to improved on-track outcomes, with effective strategies shared across schools and districts over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intermediaries lead networks of schools and support school CI teams in conducting continuous improvement work
#### Constraints
- The framework was used to guide an evaluation whose interim impact findings cover only schools' second year of participation during pandemic-affected school years

### Target Learners
- Black students, Latino students, and students experiencing poverty in middle and high school

### Target Learning Objectives
- Increasing the proportion of students on track for high school graduation and college enrollment
