# Five new design concepts for AI in learning: orchestrating, augmenting, expanding natural interactions, broadening competencies, and revealing connections

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Beyond familiar personalized, adaptive, or blended learning, the panel identified five recurrent design concepts. The report offers that "design concepts of orchestrating, augmenting, expanding natural interactions, broadening competencies, and revealing connections may stimulate additional avenues of conversation with practitioners and policy makers." Orchestrating treats learning as coordinated social activity over time; augmenting extends human intelligence in partnership; naturalistic interaction escapes keyboard-and-pointer confines; broadening competencies goes beyond 20th-century measurement; revealing connections uncovers patterns such as cross-course equivalencies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cross-sector conversations need shared design vocabulary beyond the limited language of personalized learning
#### Constraints
- The concepts are "not fully worked out today"; they are intended to illuminate possibilities and risks, not describe mature systems

### Target Learners
- Learners in classrooms using AI-supported environments, including collaborative groups

### Target Learning Objectives
- Expanding what learning experiences AI can support beyond individualization and adaptation
