# CRISS Strategic Learning framework: three concepts from cognitive psychology and brain research

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Project CRISS® is grounded in three primary concepts derived from cognitive psychology and brain research: students "monitoring their learning to assess when they have understood content," "integrating new information with prior knowledge," and "being actively involved in the learning process through discussing, writing, organizing information, and analyzing the structure of text to help improve comprehension." Teachers incorporate these concepts through comprehension strategies, and students learn when and how to apply the strategies that work best for them. The CRISS Strategic Learning Plan guides selection of content, setting of learning goals and objectives, assessment of student learning, and planning of instruction.
