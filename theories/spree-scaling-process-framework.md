# SPREE: a process for scaling evidence-based programs grounded in evaluation and implementation science

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
SPREE (Scaling Programs with Research Evidence and Effectiveness) is a process that helps funders and their grantees scale successfully. The article states it is "grounded in evaluation and implementation science frameworks" and "has been applied since 2016 by the Corporation for National and Community Service" to ensure funded interventions are likely to improve outcomes and extend reach through successful scaling.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Foundations and grantees use evaluation research to determine which interventions are likely to produce desired outcomes and which organizations are ready to scale them
#### Constraints
- The article notes the process might work best when foundations and grantees have a culture of measurement, learning and evaluation

### Target Learners
- Funders and grantee organizations scaling evidence-based programs in education, human services, and youth development

### Target Learning Objectives
- Identifying effective interventions ready to be scaled and building organizational capacity for measurement and evaluation
