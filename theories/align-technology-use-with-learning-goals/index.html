Align technology use with specific learning goals rather than adopting technology for its own sake¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The guide's overarching framework holds that the value of educational technology lies in how it is used, not in the technology itself, because technologies evolve rapidly. Every recommendation rests on one idea: "any use of technology suggested in this guide must be aligned with learning goals or objectives." Instructors should explore available technologies and select those that best align with the pedagogical approach of their courses, using technology to engage students more deeply in content, activate learning processes, and build social connections.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors should explore the range of available technologies and select those aligned with their pedagogical approach and course goals

Constraints¶

  • Specific technologies named in the guide may change; the focus is on uses that transfer to emerging technologies
  • The panel notes rigorous research on technological interventions is limited and recommendations rest on moderate or minimal evidence

Target Learners¶

  • postsecondary instructors, instructional designers, administrators, and advisors

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • effective selection and deployment of learning technologies in service of learning outcomes

  • Teachers' overarching conceptions expanded to value multiple technologies, including technologies for communication, collaboration, and inquiry, in more student-centered ways [+W]
