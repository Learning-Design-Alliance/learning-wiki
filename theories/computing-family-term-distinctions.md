# Distinguishing computing, computer science, computational thinking, and programming

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide's primer distinguishes four related terms to demystify computational thinking for educators. Programming is developing and debugging instructions a computer executes; computer science is the study of computers and algorithmic processes; computational thinking is a problem-solving approach drawing on computer science concepts; and computing is any activity leveraging computational methods. The guide quotes Wing's definition that computational thinking is "a way of solving problems, designing systems, and understanding human behavior that draws on concepts fundamental to computer science… a fundamental skill for everyone, not just computer scientists."
