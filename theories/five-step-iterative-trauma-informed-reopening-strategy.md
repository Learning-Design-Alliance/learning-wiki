# Five-step iterative trauma-informed school reopening strategy

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The fact sheet presents an organizing framework for trauma-informed school reopening drawn from crisis response guidance. It states that responding to COVID-19-related trauma "will require an iterative strategy that schools should initiate before the start of the school year" and that this strategy "involves five steps: assessing, planning, building capacity, implementing, and reassessing." The framework positions reassessment as a recurring phase rather than a terminal one, making planning cyclical.
