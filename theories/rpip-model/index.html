Research-Practice-Industry Partnership (RPIP) model¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 5 studies (4 design, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The RPIP model extends research-practice partnerships by bringing industry into a three-sector collaboration in which "representatives from all three sectors collaborate to develop new learnings that benefit all parties". It builds on participatory design and creates an "intentional feedback loop that transforms the knowledge, action, or goals of all involved parties". The article argues RPIP-developed products build inherent utility so they require less training, and that bringing learning sciences evidence into development yields products more likely to be used, used appropriately, and to have impact for learners.
