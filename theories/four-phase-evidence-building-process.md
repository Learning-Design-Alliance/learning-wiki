# Four-phase evidence-building process for designing and validating solutions

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The checklist organizes evidence building into four sequential phases: Design the Solution (Phase 1), Refine the Solution (Phase 2), Assess for Early Evidence of Success (Phase 3), and Validate Effectiveness (Phase 4). Each phase has its own document in the checklist. The framework moves teams from initial solution design grounded in a theory of change through refinement, early evidence assessment, and eventual validation of effectiveness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each phase is supported by its own checklist document
#### Constraints
- The process is iterative rather than strictly linear, as described in the checklist's iterative-learning principle

### Target Learners
- students in a priority community
- teachers

### Target Learning Objectives
- building evidence that a solution improves outcomes
