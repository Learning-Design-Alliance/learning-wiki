## Description
The CASEL 5 names five interrelated areas of competence that form the building blocks of SEL: self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship skills, and responsible decision-making. The page states, "The building blocks of SEL are the CASEL 5, five interrelated areas of competence." Each competency is defined with example capacities, such as identifying one's emotions, managing stress, taking others' perspectives, communicating effectively, and making reasoned judgments.
