Four topical areas organize Chicago school reform research: governance, teaching and learning, quality of schools, and systemic change¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Consortium organizes its research agenda around four major topical areas, each containing key concerns that pass what it calls an essential-strand test for reweaving an urban school system. The areas cover local school governance (Local School Councils, principal roles, parent involvement), the technical core of teaching and learning, schools as workplaces and community institutions, and system-level functions such as equity of opportunity. The framework was synthesized from stakeholder interviews, focus groups, position statements, and a research conference.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The framework must remain responsive to the concerns of multiple audiences, including public officials, school communities, and citizens

Constraints¶

  • The agenda is built for Chicago's particular circumstances, though the authors note it has broader implications

Target Learners¶

  • Chicago Public Schools students from elementary through high school

Target Learning Objectives¶
