# Computerized adaptive testing as a technology-enabled alternative to factory-style assessment

## Description
The article frames schools and accountability policies as built on a factory model that produces a consistent product, and presents computerized adaptive testing (CAT) as a different approach enabled by technology and used by increasing numbers of schools. CAT "provides a more precise, accurate picture of the achievement levels of both low-achieving and high-achieving students by adjusting questions as the testing goes along." The framework links adaptive measurement to instructional use of results.
