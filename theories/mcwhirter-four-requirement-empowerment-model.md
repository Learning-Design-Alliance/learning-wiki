# McWhirter's four-requirement empowerment model as a framework for adventure therapy programs

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper adopts McWhirter's (1991) model, in which empowerment is broader than autonomy or efficacy and reaches outside the individual. It specifies four necessary requirements: awareness of power dynamics, developing skills and capacity for control, exercising control without infringing others' rights, and supporting the empowerment of others. The paper uses these four requirements as the transferable structure of an adventure therapy program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All four requirements must be addressed for empowerment to be complete; the paper states that until the person supports the empowerment of others, true empowerment has not taken place
#### Constraints
- The model is contextualised for Western industrialised countries, per the paper's framing of the fishing adage in that context

### Target Learners
- participants in adventure therapy and training programs, including young people and dispossessed groups

### Target Learning Objectives
- becoming one's own agent of change
- gaining control over one's life
- supporting others' empowerment
