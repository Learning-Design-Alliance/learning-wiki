# Four-phase measurement and evaluation approach for evidence-based solution development

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Mathematica's measurement and evaluation (M&E) approach is a road map for generating evidence about new or evolving solutions. As the publication states, "Mathematica's measurement and evaluation (M&E) approach and associated toolkit tailor learning in four phases to fuel evidence-based, innovation." The four phases structure how evidence is gathered and used to decide whether a solution advances, continues development, or returns to an earlier phase.
