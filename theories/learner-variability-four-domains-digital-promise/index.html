Digital Promise's learner variability framework: every learner has a jagged learning profile across four domains¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The framework holds that learner variability is the rule, not the exception, and that every learner has a unique profile across what Digital Promise identifies as four domains: content, cognition, social and emotional, and biographical backgrounds. It implies teachers must act like doctors—diagnosing where learners stand on progressions and tailoring instruction—rather than like line workers, and must uncover their own biases to avoid inequitable practices. The publication uses the framework as the foundational rationale for personalized, competency-based, digitally informed constructs for teaching and learning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must be empowered and have the skills and tools to identify where learners stand on progressions toward learning standards and to tailor instruction accordingly

Constraints¶

  • The publication states the framework applies to every learner, both those struggling and those thriving, and offers no empirical test of it in this document

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 learners, including historically and systematically excluded populations

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Personalized, competency-based mastery of standards-aligned knowledge and skills
