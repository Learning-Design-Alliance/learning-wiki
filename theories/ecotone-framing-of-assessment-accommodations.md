# Ecotone framing: accommodations occupy a transitional zone between two stable states in assessment

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a metaphorical framework in which accommodations sit "like an ecotone of sorts" — a transitional zone — between two ports. In this framing, accommodations "make shore" in a unique spot with assessments but remain transitional rather than organic, and external forces such as changing expectations and new technology push them to drift in new directions. The article uses this framing to organize its review of the accommodations landscape.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The framing presupposes that accommodations are adopted into assessments but not fully integrated, per the article's transitional-status claim.
#### Constraints
- The article presents the ecotone as a metaphor for the accommodations landscape, not as a tested model; it notes accommodations "rarely seem like an organic and fully equal part of an assessment."

- All students taking educational assessments
