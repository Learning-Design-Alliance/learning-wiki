Fight-pair comparison as an identification strategy for detecting discrimination in student discipline¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article's methodological framework is a within-pair comparison design: rather than comparing discipline outcomes across schools or students generally, it compares the punishments given to the two participants in the same fight, one Black or poor and one White or nonpoor. The authors state that "comparing the punishments given to Black and White (or poor and nonpoor) students who fight one another" addresses "a selection challenge that has kept prior studies from identifying discrimination in student discipline." Because both students in a pair faced the same incident, differences in punishment are attributed to differential treatment rather than differing behavior.
