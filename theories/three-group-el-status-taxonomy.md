# Three-group taxonomy of English Learner service statuses for achievement analysis

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article organizes its analysis around three distinct English Learner groups defined by service eligibility: "ever-ELs who were ever eligible for service;" "current-ELs who continue to require service;" and "dually-identified students eligible for both EL and Special Education services." This taxonomy allows the study to separate students who exit EL service from those who continue to need it and from those with concurrent special education eligibility, each compared against never-EL peers and the all-student average. It structures all reported achievement and gain comparisons in grades K-8.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longitudinal assessment data linking students' EL service eligibility status over time
- A never-EL comparison group and an all-student average within the same schools and grades
#### Constraints
- The taxonomy distinguishes groups by service eligibility status, not by instructional approach or program type

### Target Learners
- English Learners in grades K-8
- Dually-identified EL and Special Education students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Mathematics achievement
- Reading achievement
- Academic growth across grades
