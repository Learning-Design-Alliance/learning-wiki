# Framework of four logics of accountability crossing process-outcome and external-internal dimensions

## Description
The article theorizes accountability as four logics generated by crossing two analytical dimensions: process versus outcome accountability and external versus internal accountability. The authors state, "we use two analytical dimensions: (1) process versus outcome accountability (Patil, Vieider, & Tetlock, 2014) and (2) external versus internal accountability (Newmann, 1997)." Crossing these yields a 2x2 matrix of control-based, professional-based, test-based, and process-based accountability, each defined by where external control over outcomes and processes sits.
