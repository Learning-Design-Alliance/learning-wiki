## Description
Computerized adaptive test (CAT) designs are treated in the article as a test-design dimension whose choices, together with item bank distribution, shape two outcomes of a reading comprehension assessment: content coverage and efficiency. The article frames CAT design as something that can be varied experimentally, investigating "the effects of CAT test design and bank distribution on the content coverage and the efficiency of the tests."
