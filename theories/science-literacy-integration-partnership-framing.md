# Science–literacy integration as a partnership for student success

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The brief's organizing framing treats science and literacy not as separate subjects but as a "Powerful Partnership for Student Success", in which instruction in each domain supports the other. On this view, elementary teaching should be designed around the "connection between science and literacy instruction", with integration presented as the mechanism through which benefits to students are realized.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must be designed around the connection between the science and literacy domains rather than taught in isolation.
#### Constraints
- The framing is advanced for the elementary level, per the brief's description.

### Target Learners
- Elementary students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Science learning
- Literacy learning
