# Five essential supports framework for urban school improvement

## Description
The Consortium's organizing framework for Chicago school improvement, based on broad research on urban school improvement. It "posits that improvements in student learning require effective school leadership, parental involvement, professional development and collaboration among teachers, a climate focused on student learning, and quality instructional programs." This report examines the fourth support, a student-centered learning climate, encompassing school safety and order, teachers' personal concern for students, and expectations for serious academic work, and synthesizes findings on four of the five supports as they relate to the city's lowest-performing schools.
