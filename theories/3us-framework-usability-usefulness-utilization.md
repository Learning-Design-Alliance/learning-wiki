# The 3Us framework: usability, usefulness, and utilization as three indicators for evaluating new solutions

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide organizes implementation measurement around three indicators: usability, usefulness, and utilization of a new solution. It states that "Understanding the usability, usefulness, and utilization of a new solution is critical for implementing organizations and their funders." The framework gives developers and funders a shared vocabulary for assessing whether a solution works in practice, not only whether it is effective in principle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizations implementing or funding a new solution must be willing to measure all three indicators during the solution's development and early implementation
#### Constraints
- The guide positions the framework for use during development and early implementation of new solutions, not as a mature-program evaluation tool

### Target Learners
- implementing organizations and funders of educational solutions

### Target Learning Objectives
- evaluating and improving the implementation of educational solutions
