Three guiding principles of the Digital Equity Frameworks: Inclusive Innovation, Sustainability, Continuous Improvement¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The frameworks are animated by three guiding principles that form the belief system underlying domain actions. A belief in Inclusive Innovation holds that digital equity work must be done in collaboration with communities, including the most impacted, underserved, and underrepresented, grounding efforts in targeted universalism. Sustainability recognizes that digital equity efforts are society-building and mission critical, requiring long-term planning. Continuous Improvement accepts that schools and districts are always striving to achieve more equitable outcomes efficiently and effectively.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Principles must animate each domain's actions in schools and districts.

Constraints¶

  • The principles remind that pursuit of digital equity is a continual effort that will change over time as technology, communities, and understanding of teaching practice change.

  • Higher education students
