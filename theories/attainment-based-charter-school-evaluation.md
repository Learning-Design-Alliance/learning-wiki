# Attainment-based evaluation of charter schools as a complement to achievement-test-based studies

## Description
The report frames its contribution as moving beyond the standard outcome measure in charter school research. It states that "past charter school studies focused on student achievement," whereas this study "analyzed the relationship between attendance and educational attainment in charter high schools." The approach treats high school completion and college attendance as the outcomes by which charter school impact is judged, complementing test-score-based evaluations.
