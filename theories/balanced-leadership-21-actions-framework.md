# Balanced Leadership framework of 21 leadership actions and behaviors associated with improved student outcomes

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The content of the Balanced Leadership® professional development is organized around a framework of leadership actions and behaviors derived from research on school leaders. The report states that "The professional development focuses on 21 specific actions and behaviors of school leaders that may be associated with improved student outcomes, including designing and implementing school curriculum, instruction, and assessment; fostering shared beliefs and community; and monitoring teaching and student learning." Year-one sessions cover the framework overview, initiating and leading school change, maintaining a focus on school performance, and fostering shared beliefs and a purposeful school community, taught through a case study approach.
