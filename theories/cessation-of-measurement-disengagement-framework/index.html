Target Learners¶

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Accurate measurement of achievement despite test-taker disengagement

Claims¶

  • Rapid Guessing Differs From Solution Behavior [+M]
  • Test takers generally disengage during only a portion of their items, and these disengaged responses reflect a momentary cessation of measurement [+W]
  • Rapid guesses on computer-based tests reflect a construct-irrelevant response process rather than the tested knowledge, skills, and abilities [+W]
  • Including rapid guesses in scoring introduces systematic measurement errors that can seriously distort test scores [+W]
  • Test-taking engagement plays an essential role in the pursuit of valid scores [+W]
