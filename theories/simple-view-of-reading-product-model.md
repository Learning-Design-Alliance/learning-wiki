# Simple View of Reading: comprehension as the product of word recognition and language comprehension

## Description
The Simple View of Reading, proposed by Gough and Tunmer in 1986, holds that reading comprehension is the product of word recognition and language comprehension. The paper uses it as a foundational framework: MAP Reading Fluency with Coach's learning progression and reports are organized around these two components, and the Foundational Skills Summary report presents data for decoding and language comprehension, which the paper says are "part of the Simple View of Reading."
