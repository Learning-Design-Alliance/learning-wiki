# Seven key elements of Tier 2 reading interventions (MiMTSS framework)

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report adopts the Michigan Multi-Tiered System of Supports Technical Assistance Center's framework of seven key elements of Tier 2 reading interventions: "explicit instruction, small group instruction, matching instruction to student needs, opportunities to practice, frequent feedback, progress monitoring, and fading supports". Each element is defined in box 3—for example, small group instruction typically involves groups of three to four students meeting three to five times a week for 20–40 minutes. The study team validated these elements by reviewing literature including a WWC practice guide, and used them as the organizing framework for coding discrete intervention strategies in the supplemental matrix.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The framework was selected because it was developed by experts in the field and was not the product of an intervention developer
#### Constraints
- The definitions were drawn from the Michigan Multi-Tiered System of Supports Technical Assistance Center’s description of Tier 2 reading supports

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K–3 at risk of not meeting reading proficiency benchmarks

### Target Learning Objectives
- Reading and writing achievement across phonics, fluency, comprehension, and vocabulary
