# Four categories of desired OpenSciEd outcomes: student, teacher capacity, system, and resources and innovations

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper identifies "four broad categories of desired outcomes for OpenSciEd: student outcomes; increased teacher capacity; system-level outcomes (e.g., districts, states, and policies); and resources and innovations that support curriculum implementation and student learning." These are desired outcomes reflecting what designers hope to achieve rather than what outcome research has gathered evidence about to date. Student outcomes go beyond NGSS-based learning to engagement, autonomy in practices, and equity; teacher capacity includes sustainable implementation and equitable engagement practices; system outcomes include deep and wide district adoption and teacher collaboration cultures; innovations include local customizations, technology tools, and assessment systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Outcomes result from factors at multiple system levels, e.g., system structures support teacher professional learning which improves classroom implementation
#### Constraints
- The goal is not an exhaustive account of possible outcomes; certain outcomes are closely tied to certain aspects of OpenSciEd

### Target Learners
- K-12 science students, teachers, and district/state systems

### Target Learning Objectives
- equitable three-dimensional science proficiency, teacher capacity, and sustainable adoption
