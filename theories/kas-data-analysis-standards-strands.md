# Kentucky Data & Analysis standards organize learning into storage, collection/visualization/transformation, inference & models, and data ethics strands

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Kentucky Academic Standards for Computer Science structure Data & Analysis for K-8 into named strands: Storage (E-DA-01, appropriately store and modify digital files), Collection, Visualization & Transformation (E-DA-02, collect and visually display data; E-DA-03, "Analyzing data for trends and relationships"), plus an Impacts of Computing strand on proper use of data (E-IC-04). The session maps its activities to these standards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alignment of classroom data activities to the printed standard codes and grade-band indicators
#### Constraints
- Indicators quoted are for grades 3-5, with guidance and support from adults

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-8

### Target Learning Objectives
- Storing, collecting, visualizing, and analyzing data
- Understanding proper use of data and information
