# Charter school effects as a multi-dimensional policy question spanning achievement, attainment, integration, and competition

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report organizes its evaluation of charter schools around four outcome dimensions named in its title: "Effects on Achievement, Attainment, Integration, and Competition." This framing treats charter schooling as a policy intervention whose value cannot be judged by test scores alone; attainment outcomes such as graduation and college entry, school integration patterns, and effects on competition with traditional public schools are examined alongside achievement. The framework shapes how the report weighs its mixed evidence, giving weight to favorable attainment results even where test-score impacts are neutral.
