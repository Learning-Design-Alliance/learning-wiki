IDC theory's three interconnected loops of interest, creativity, and habit as a learning design philosophy¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

IDC theory organizes learning activity design around three cycles: sparking student interest, fostering individual creativity, and inculcating lifelong learning habits. The commentary emphasizes that each loop calls for a doing orientation rather than mere appreciation, noting that the theory's articulation of these ideas pulls together many literatures in integrative ways that are rare in scholarly work and yet entirely practical. The authors read IDC as a liberal education philosophy centered on student agency.
