# Metacognition as a framework for making thinking visible across artistic and academic learning

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents metacognition as "the awareness and understanding of one's thinking and learning processes," introduced through training with evidence behind embedding it instructionally. NMSA educators already helped students externalize their thoughts through writing, speaking, or artistic media, and metacognition gave them "a framework for understanding this process of making thinking visible," supporting continual reflection and improvement.
