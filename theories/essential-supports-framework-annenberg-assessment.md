# Model of Essential Supports for Student Learning as framework for assessing school improvement

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report uses the Model of Essential Supports for Student Learning as its organizing framework, assessing seven areas of school improvement: "quality of classroom instruction; student learning climate; school leadership; teacher professional community; parent and community support; relational trust; and instructional program coherence." Survey measures, field research indicators, and achievement analyses were organized around these supports, with figures tracking each from 1994 or 1997 to 2001.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Survey and administrative measures covering each of the seven support areas
- Longitudinal data allowing trend comparison across years
#### Constraints
- Applied to elementary schools in the Chicago public school system; the report focuses only on elementary schools because few high schools participated and data on them were uneven

### Target Learners
- elementary school students in urban public schools

### Target Learning Objectives
- student academic achievement
- student social and psychological outcomes
