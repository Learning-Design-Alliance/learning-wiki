# Three-layer definition of AI for learning: computational intelligence, specific capabilities, and a futures toolkit

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report frames AI for educators through three layers: AI as computational intelligence brought to bear on educational challenges as an additional resource; AI as a set of specific rapidly advancing capabilities (perception, representation and reasoning, learning, natural interaction, societal impact) engineered into solutions; and AI as a toolkit for imagining futures. The report states: "we conceptualize three aspects of AI—as an ambitious leading edge of computing, as a set of specific capabilities that are rapidly advancing, and as a toolkit for synthesizing and exploring possible futures for learning and teaching." This framing underpins the panel's scenarios and design concepts.
