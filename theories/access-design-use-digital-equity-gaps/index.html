Three commonplace digital equity gaps for AI and emerging educational technologies: access, design, and use¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Building on the US National Educational Technology Plan, the brief identifies "three commonplace gaps that impact digital equity for AI and all emerging educational technologies: gaps in access, design, and use." For AI uses to be compatible with Digital Equity, access must be ensured, the design of AI in education must address learner variability and be responsive to students' needs, strengths, and backgrounds, and use must be fair and equitable for all students.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Ensured access; design that addresses learner variability and is responsive to students' needs, strengths, and backgrounds; fair and equitable use

Constraints¶

  • Applies to AI and all emerging educational technologies, per the US National Educational Technology Plan

Target Learners¶

  • all students
  • including students historically blocked from opportunities to learn

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • equitable access to and fair use of AI in learning
