# Five EdTech Quality Indicators framework for evaluating edtech products

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
The EdTech Quality Collaborative published a five-indicator framework for evaluating effective edtech products. Safe means tools adhere to "industry standards, data minimization, and data transparency to protect user privacy and prevent data breaches." Evidence-Based means grounded in rigorous research aligned with ESSA Tiers of Evidence; Inclusive prioritizes accessibility and equitable design while mitigating algorithmic bias; Usable focuses on intuitive design that eliminates technical barriers; Interoperable uses open data standards to connect with existing district technologies. The guide applies the framework across every step of district procurement and data governance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts integrate the indicators into procurement processes and data governance plans, articulating explicit evaluation criteria across all five indicators
#### Constraints
- The framework is intended for education leaders in local education agencies and schools, including public charter schools

### Target Learners
- K-12 district and school leaders making edtech procurement decisions

### Target Learning Objectives
- Selecting high-quality, evidence-based edtech tools that improve student outcomes
