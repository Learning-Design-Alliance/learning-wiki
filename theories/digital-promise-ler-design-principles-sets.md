# Digital Promise's three principle sets for inclusive LER design

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The toolkit catalogs three Digital Promise principle sets developed with front-line workers, educators, employers, and other stakeholders: Inclusive Design principles (2022) such as "Demonstrates evidence of skills and competencies in addition to work history"; Onboarding and Education principles (2023) including "Use inclusive language and design"; and Learning Transitions principles (2023) including "Connect people to supports" and "Connect people to opportunities." Design principles both guide development work and "serve as evaluation criteria for designs that already exist."
