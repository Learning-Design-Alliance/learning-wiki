# Automaticity Theory: fluency develops through repeated practice that frees attention for comprehension

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
Automaticity Theory (AT) is presented as a main theory in developing reading fluency, emphasizing word recognition. LaBerge and Samuels viewed automaticity as a bottom-up reading process in which readers recognize letters through repeated reading before developing focus on letter sounds at the synthesis stage. Readers go through decoding, comprehension and attention processes; as attention is freed from decoding, comprehension is supported. The article notes the theory "focuses on a lot of practice and drilling in the classroom to ensure students' success".
