Three-stage framework of research use across ed-tech product development¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report organizes research use in ed-tech product development into three categories representing "research use in early, mid, and ending stages of product development": Learning Science (using existing scientific research on learning to inform product design), User Research (gathering information about how users interact with the product or prototypes to make improvements), and Evaluation Research (a formal study of whether the product promotes learning). The framework structures the campaign's submission categories, its scoring rubric, and the report's sections.
