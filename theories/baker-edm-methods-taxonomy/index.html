Baker's five-category taxonomy of educational data mining methods¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review presents Baker's [in press] classification of EDM methods into five categories: prediction (with sub-categories classification, regression, and density estimation), clustering, relationship mining (association rule mining, correlation mining, sequential pattern mining, causal data mining), distillation of data for human judgment, and discovery with models. The article explains that in "discovery with models, a model of a phenomenon is developed through any process that can be validated in some fashion... and this model is then used as a component in another analysis". It contrasts this taxonomy with Romero and Ventura's web-mining-focused categorization.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Methods are drawn from literatures including data mining and machine learning, psychometrics and other areas of statistics, information visualization, and computational modeling

Constraints¶

  • The first three categories' sub-categories are directly drawn from Moore's categorization of data mining methods

Target Learners¶

  • educational data mining researchers

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • selecting and categorizing methods for analyzing educational data

Claims¶

  • Discovery With Models Emerged Second Category [+M]
  • Modeling frameworks from Item Response Theory, Bayes Nets, and Markov Decision Processes appeared in 28% of EDM2008 and EDM2009 papers after being rare early in the field [+W]
