The CASEL Wheel: a framework positioning five SEL competencies within key learning settings¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The CASEL Wheel is CASEL's full framework for SEL, positioning five interrelated competencies within the settings where children and young people live and learn. The page states, "Our framework positions these competencies within the various settings where children and young people live and learn." The wheel organizes SEL around four settings: classrooms, schools, families and caregivers, and communities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • SEL competencies are taught and applied across multiple settings, not in one context alone

Constraints¶

  • The framework is CASEL's own organizing model for SEL rather than a tested causal model

Target Learners¶

  • all young people and adults, from childhood to adulthood

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • social and emotional competencies

Claims¶

  • Casel 5 Competency Taxonomy [+M]

Related Theories¶

Examples¶

  • Align school and community partner SEL efforts through common language and coordinated communication
  • Build authentic school-family partnerships to reinforce students' social and emotional development
  • Social Emotional Learning Curricula
