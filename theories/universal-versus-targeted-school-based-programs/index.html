Distinction between universal and targeted school-based programs¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report draws a scope distinction among school-based programs that determines who receives the intervention. It defines "universal programs—programs that are designed to address the needs of all children in a school" in contrast to "targeted school-based programs—programs that are designed to address the needs of a subset of the children in a school." The SACD evaluation restricted itself to universal programs delivered schoolwide to whole elementary populations.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A program must be classifiable by whether its intended audience is all children in a school or a subset

Constraints¶

  • The SACD Research Program evaluated universal programs, not targeted ones
