# Taxonomy of seven TCSA models by interim blueprint design and score aggregation method

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review organizes through-course summative assessment models along two axes: interim blueprint design (distributed vs. cumulative) and summative score aggregation method (simple vs. complex). It states that "Table 2.1 presents a matrix of seven models found in the literature based on combinations of blueprint designs and aggregation methods." Distributed blueprints divide "the summative blueprint...into mutually exclusive parts where each part is assigned to an interim time period," while cumulative blueprints measure all content taught to date. Complex aggregation uses latent trait or IRT/MIRT models.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A TCSA model must answer how interim blueprints ensure full-domain coverage by year end and what aggregation method combines scores
#### Constraints
- None of the reviewed TCSAs described a comprehensive blueprint approach that samples the summative blueprint at each test event

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in state accountability systems

### Target Learning Objectives
- Measuring grade-level standards proficiency and academic growth for accountability
