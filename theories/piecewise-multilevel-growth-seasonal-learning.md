# Piecewise multilevel growth modeling of seasonal learning patterns

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article applies "a piecewise multilevel growth model to administrative data from a large school district that enrolls a substantial English Learner student population." The piecewise structure separates within-school-year growth rates from between-summer learning loss rates, allowing the study to show that dual language participants' math advantage during school years was offset by greater summer loss, while their reading pattern showed the reverse. This methodological framework makes seasonal trade-offs in achievement growth visible that a single annual growth rate would obscure.
