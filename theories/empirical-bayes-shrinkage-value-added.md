# Empirical Bayes shrinkage as a precision-based adjustment to teacher value-added estimates in accountability systems

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes empirical Bayes shrinkage as a procedure "common in the implementation of teacher accountability systems" that is used "to adjust teacher value-added estimates by their level of precision." Estimates based on fewer students, or on students whose achievement is hard to predict, are less precise and are therefore shrunk more. The article uses this framework to ask whether the adjustment differentially affects teachers of hard-to-predict students.
