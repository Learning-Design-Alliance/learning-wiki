# 4P4CT framework: four learner-centered pedagogies for teaching computational thinking across all subjects

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The 4P4CT (Four Pedagogies for CT) framework is a conceptual framework for teaching CT to all students in all subject matters, rooted in constructivism and constructionism. The course implementing it "implements four learner-centered pedagogies: active learning, project-based learning, product-based learning, and learning in context". Product-based learning is realized as developing a simulation of a computational process in any field of knowledge of the student's choice, and context-based learning holds that knowledge must be built within a relevant domain rather than as theory alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive learning environment that maintains motivation, and guided, consistent reflection on learners' personal understanding of CT concepts
#### Constraints
- The framework was examined in an academic CT course for prospective teachers; its effectiveness for K–12 pupils is left to future research

### Target Learners
- prospective teachers of all disciplines and age groups, and school pupils generally

### Target Learning Objectives
- acquiring and applying computational thinking skills across all knowledge domains
