# FAST logic model linking family strengthening to school turnaround

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (1 for, 3 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The FAST program rests on a logic model (Exhibit 1) in which parent-child relationship building, parent-school engagement, parental social support, and improved home environment lead to improved child behavior and classroom climate, which in turn produce improved school climate and ultimately school turnaround. In the validation design, FAST was expected to overcome nonacademic barriers in low-performing schools by engaging parents to increase comfort with school, countering parent and child stress through trusting relationships, and reducing family conflict by empowering positive parent-child interaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High participation penetration (60 percent of kindergarteners) was considered necessary for the program to meaningfully contribute to whole-school turnaround
#### Constraints
- The evaluation found the program unlikely to meaningfully contribute to turning around low-performing schools in this context

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten students and families in persistently low-performing schools

### Target Learning Objectives
- Family engagement
- School climate improvement
- Early academic outcomes
