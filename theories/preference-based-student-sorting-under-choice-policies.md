# Preference-based sorting of students into schools under different school-choice policies

## Description
The brief organizes its policy analysis around how parental preferences translate into the sorting of students across schools under alternative school-choice policies. It states: "This brief describes (1) what parents look for when they choose a school and (2) how these preferences affect the sorting of students into schools under different school-choice policies." The framework links measured parent preferences (distance, demographics, academics, and variation across parents) to the equilibrium assignment of students produced by choice-policy designs.
