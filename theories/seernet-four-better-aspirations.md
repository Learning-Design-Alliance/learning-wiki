# SEERNet's four 'better' aspirations for next-generation research: science, engineering, practice, and community

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
At the Chicago convening, participants articulated "four kinds of advances in educational research" they want SEERNet to enable: better science (greater openness, external validity, utility, replicability), better engineering (privacy protection, uptake of results into products, learning engineers), better contributions to practice (research that involves practitioners and informs everyday teaching), and better community (more inclusive communities of researchers, practitioners, and developers). These aspirations, named as Wave 3, go beyond the 5-year SEERNet research agenda toward improving research infrastructure and practices overall.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Realizing these aspirations requires moving beyond the specific Wave 2 research requirements
#### Constraints
- Plans for the third wave are acknowledged as necessarily less precise; participants vary in which 'better' they are most aligned with

### Target Learners
- education researchers
- educational software developers
- practitioners
- emerging scholars including those at HBCUs and minority-serving institutions

### Target Learning Objectives
- conducting use-inspired foundational research in Pasteur's Quadrant
- integrating evidence-based learning insights into educational platforms
