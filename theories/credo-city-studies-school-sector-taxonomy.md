# Three-sector taxonomy of public schools for city-level performance comparison

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 11 claims (11 for) · 5 studies (3 associational, 2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 5 report an effect size · 11 claims rest on one study

## Description
The City Studies project organizes urban school performance analysis around a sector taxonomy: charter schools (public schools "operated independently from the traditional school district, with autonomy in adapting school designs and held accountable for education results"), selective magnet schools (district-run with academically selective admission), and other district-run schools. Charters are further split into CMO-affiliated (overseeing at least three schools) and independent charters (one or two schools). The taxonomy structures the report's benchmarking of each sector's growth against the state average and against other Austin sectors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student-level growth data for each sector and state benchmark data accounting for student characteristics
#### Constraints
- Selective magnet results are redacted in Austin due to the small number of such schools with growth scores

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in urban public schools

### Target Learning Objectives
- Annual academic growth in reading and mathematics by school sector
