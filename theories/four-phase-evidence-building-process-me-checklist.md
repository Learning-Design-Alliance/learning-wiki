# Four-phase evidence-building process for designing, refining, testing, and validating learning solutions

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The M&E Checklist organizes evidence building into four sequential phases, each with its own document: "Design the Solution (Phase 1), Refine the Solution (Phase 2), Assess for Early Evidence of Success (Phase 3), and Validate Effectiveness (Phase 4)." The framework structures how developers of learning solutions move from initial design through piloting to validation of effectiveness, always in collaboration with priority-community stakeholders.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stakeholder collaboration from a priority community throughout the process
#### Constraints
- The checklist activities focus on iterative learning, so phases may be repeated, revisited, or abandoned rather than completed linearly

### Target Learners
- students in a priority community

### Target Learning Objectives
- designing and validating effective teaching and learning solutions
