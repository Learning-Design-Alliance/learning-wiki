Basic Systems Model for staff development planning with six sequential decision points¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q1 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The workbook teaches a Basic Systems Model in which staff development planning proceeds through sequential decision points: Rationale, Objectives, Criterion Measures, Techniques and Strategies, Field Test, and Evaluation. The article defines the approach as "a plan for solving problems that describes the interrelation of sequential decision points", with feedback communicating information from later decision points to earlier ones. Each decision point answers a guiding question (Why? What? How much? How?), and each is elaborated into sub-decision points such as felt needs, verified needs, and problem statement under Rationale.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Learners must be able to recall the components of the systems model and work through its sub-decision points to apply it to a staff development plan

Constraints¶

  • The model presented is compatible with but not identical to the Hill model in How Schools Can Apply Systems Analysis

Target Learners¶

  • inservice educational personnel in Georgia schools and districts

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • apply a systems approach to developing a staff development plan
  • apply the systems approach to educational problems other than staff development

Claims¶

  • In a systems approach to staff development, felt needs must be legitimized by data before planning proceeds [+W]
  • Field Test II with students should not be implemented until Field Test I evaluation yields a go decision on staff competencies [+W]
  • Simulation allows the expense and effect of a policy change to be evaluated before implementation [+W]

Related Theories¶

Examples¶

  • Prioritize felt needs using matrix ranking or paired comparisons before verification
  • Conduct and evaluate a field test before implementing a staff development plan at scale
