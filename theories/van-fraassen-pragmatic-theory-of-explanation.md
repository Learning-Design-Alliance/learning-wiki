# Van Fraassen pragmatic theory of explanation as a theory of why-questions

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents Van Fraassen's (VF) pragmatic theory of explanation as "essentally a theory of why-questons". Formulating a theory of explanation poses two main problems: the provision of an account of justifiable rejection of explanation-requests, and the accounting for the asymmetries of explanation. To this end, "he takes explanaton as a ternary relatonship that holds between theories, facts and contexts", with why-questions and their answers individuated relative to a context, placing explanation on grounds respectable from an empiricist standpoint.
