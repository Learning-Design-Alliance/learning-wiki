Model-based multivariate true score norming procedure¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The norming approach, employed previously in the course-specific MAP Growth Mathematics norming study (Thum & He, 2019), uses "a multivariate true score model that accounts for the known imprecision of scores from the fall, winter, and spring terms." Observed scores are decomposed into true scores and error scores, with each score's standard error of measurement introduced into the model. True scores are assumed multivariate normal, and the resulting joint distribution of predicted fall, winter, and spring scores provides the basis for achievement norms (marginal distributions) and growth norms (between-term gains).

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Standard statistics packages such as SAS via PROC MIXED can obtain the parameter estimates; the joint normality assumption of scores must be reasonable for inferences

Constraints¶

  • Inferences depend on the joint normality assumption, which the report checks with Q-Q plots, CDF curves, and model residuals

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades K-3 assessed with Spanish MAP Reading Fluency

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Norm-referenced interpretation of achievement and growth in foundational reading skills
