# Propensity score stratification within multilevel models as a quasi-experimental approach to school achievement effects

## Description
The article employs a combined methodological framework: propensity score stratification is used to balance key covariates between treatment and control groups, and multilevel models accommodate the nesting of students within schools in a cross-state sample. The authors state they "employ propensity score stratification and multilevel models to balance key covariates between treatment and control groups of a cross-state sample of students". The framework is presented as suited to estimating school transfer effects when randomization is not possible, providing "a more complex picture of charter school achievement effects in a quasi-experimental context".
