# Foundations for Young Adult Success developmental framework

## Description
A developmental framework (Nagaoka, Farrington, Ehrlich, & Heath, 2015) that the author uses to organize how settings influence character development. The article states it proposes that "10 particular 'developmental experiences,' specific opportunities provided for young people to act and reflect, are key mechanisms whereby settings influence the development of character." The framework links these experiences to three outcomes: agency, an integrated identity, and a set of competencies supporting success in young adulthood.
