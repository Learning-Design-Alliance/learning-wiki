# Automaticity theory as the theoretical basis of repeated reading

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report grounds repeated reading in the theory of automaticity, developed in the 1960s and 1970s. The theory holds that "fluent readers decode reading text automatically, enabling the reader to focus on comprehension." Repeated reading is designed as a practice that builds automaticity in word recognition through repeated oral reading of a passage, with the expectation that automatic decoding frees cognitive resources for comprehension. The report states that "Repeated reading aims to build automaticity, and thus, improve reading fluency and comprehension."
