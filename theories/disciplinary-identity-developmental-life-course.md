# Disciplinary identity as a developmental process across the academic life course

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames disciplinary identity, or "connection to a particular academic discipline", as something "constructed through a developmental process across a scholar's academic life course". This framing motivates studying how motivations for participating in research relate to identity among undergraduate researchers. It positions identity as formed over time rather than fixed, making undergraduate research participation a site where disciplinary connection can develop.
