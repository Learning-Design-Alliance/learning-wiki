# Three-type taxonomy of Philadelphia public high schools: alternative disciplinary, alternative accelerated, and neighborhood-zoned

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report organizes its descriptive analysis around a three-part classification of Philadelphia public high schools. As the report states, it covers "alternative disciplinary schools; alternative accelerated schools for students who struggled academically in regular public high schools; and regular, neighborhood-zoned high schools." This taxonomy defines the comparison groups for all enrollment, characteristics, test score, and graduation analyses in the report.
