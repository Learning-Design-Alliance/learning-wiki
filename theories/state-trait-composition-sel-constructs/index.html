State-trait composition framework for social-emotional learning constructs¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q1 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The page names an associated journal article, "Examining the state-trait composition of social-emotional learning constructs: Implications for practice, policy, and evaluation", which frames SEL competencies as constructs whose composition includes stable trait-like and time-varying state-like components. The brief on this page investigates the practical consequence: whether SEL competencies "are stable over time". The framework organizes measurement, evaluation, and policy uses of SEL scores around that state-trait distinction.
