# Vygotsky's genetic method: studying higher psychological functions across four time scales

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The genetic (developmental) method is Vygotsky's historically based methodology for explaining adult higher psychological functions by tracing their formation backwards in time rather than studying the finished product. Lantolf explains that Vygotsky proposed "four different time scales for studying the formation of higher psychological functions: phylogenesis, sociocultural history..., ontogenesis, and microgenesis". The method underlies the instrumental (experimental-developmental) method, in which participants receive mediation such as linguistic signs to solve tasks beyond current ability, allowing observation of mediated mental activity forming in the laboratory.
