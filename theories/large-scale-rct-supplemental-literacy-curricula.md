# Large-scale randomized controlled trial design for evaluating supplemental literacy curricula

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's organizing method is "a large-scale randomized controlled trial of the effects of four supplemental reading comprehension curricula." Impact analyses are conducted separately by study year, allowing effects to differ across years and across teacher subgroups defined by experience using the intervention. This design supports causal claims about each curriculum's impact on students' reading comprehension scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Random assignment of participants to curriculum conditions
- Impact analyses conducted separately for each study year
#### Constraints
- Findings are specific to the four curricula and study years examined

### Target Learners
- Students in schools implementing supplemental reading comprehension curricula

### Target Learning Objectives
- Reading comprehension achievement
