Social justice impact spectrum for OEP: transformative, ameliorative, neutral or negative¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article extends Hodgkinson-Williams and Trotter's framework, built on Fraser's model of social justice, to expansive process-centric OEP. It defines four levels of impact: "Transformative refers to addressing systemic/structural roots of injustice, affirmative/ameliorative refers to addressing surface injustice, neutral refers to not having a social justice impact, and negative means reproducing or even exacerbating injustice". Table 2 maps each OEP onto this spectrum across contexts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Analysts must consider the full range of potential impacts because OEP do not necessarily result in positive effects

Constraints¶

  • OEP may neglect factors that maintain or widen the digital divide, ignore accessibility, and/or disregard data privacy

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students and educators

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • evaluating social justice impact of open practices

Claims¶

  • Renewable assignments such as student-created quiz questions transcend classroom boundaries and can ameliorate economic injustice [+W]
  • Wikipedia editing reproduces inequalities in editors, topics and credible knowledge, but feminist edit-a-thons can redress cultural injustice [+W]
  • Positioning personal experience as an epistemic resource supports expansion from inward to outward critical orientations [+W]

Examples¶

  • Collaborative web annotation (Marginal Syllabus): low-bandwidth asynchronous discussion with social justice intent
  • Equity Unbound: an open connected course explicitly designed with social justice principles
  • Design OEP for accessibility and marginalized participation rather than open for all
  • Virtually Connecting: equitable conference conversations facilitated by marginalized scholars
