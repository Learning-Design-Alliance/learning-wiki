Demonstrated interest: applicant contacts with admissions offices as signals of enrollment probability¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article frames applicants' contacts with admissions offices, such as campus visits, as signals that admissions officers read as interest in enrolling. Because colleges pursuing selectivity and class quality care whether high-quality applicants accept offers, the article states that "applicants' contacts with admissions offices, such as campus visits, can be viewed positively by the officers as demonstrated interest in the colleges." This signaling account motivates the empirical analysis of contact behavior and admission outcomes.
