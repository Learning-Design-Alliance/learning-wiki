Three rural setting characteristics distinguishing rural SIG schools' improvement contexts¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The brief organizes rural context around three characteristics that distinguish rural SIG schools from nonrural ones and may influence improvement efforts: "distance to an urban center or metropolitan area," "geographic spread with low population density," and "small community size." Distance affects staffing and access to external professional development and support providers; geographic spread affects tax base, transportation, and after-school participation; small community size means small staffs, multi-subject teaching loads, but also stronger staff-student relationships.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Data on school locale, distance to urban centers, and county population density to categorize a school's rural context

Constraints¶

  • The nine schools are a purposive sample not necessarily representative of rural SIG schools nationwide

Target Learners¶

  • Students in rural low-performing schools receiving SIG funds

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Understand how community context shapes school improvement efforts in staffing, parent involvement, and instructional improvement

Claims¶

  • Rural Sig Schools Additional Location Challenges [+M]
  • Rural Teacher Commutes Hinder Staffing [+M]
  • Rural SIG schools were more likely than nonrural SIG schools to adopt the transformation model (95 versus 74 percent) [+W]
  • Rural SIG schools perceived transportation access and home-school distance as location-related barriers to parent involvement [+W]
  • Transportation costs and catchment size led rural SIG schools to increase learning time within the existing schedule rather than extend the school day [+W]
  • Small rural teaching staffs forced PLCs across grades and subjects, and teachers in four schools felt isolated from same-subject colleagues [+W]
