# Strong Field Framework adapted to Breakthrough R&D in Education with five conditions

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ATS Hub adapts the Bridgespan Group's Strong Field Framework to education R&D, premised on the idea that field building is a lever for social change. The adapted version "identifies five conditions for a strong field: shared language and identity, essential practices, knowledge base, leadership and ecosystem, and pathways to success." Essential practices are "a set of agreed upon methods, standards, and processes that a field recognizes and adopts to achieve its goals." The framework guides the Hub's effort to codify practices so the field can mature into a coordinated ecosystem producing breakthroughs at scale.
