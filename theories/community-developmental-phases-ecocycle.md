# Community developmental phases in place-based systems change

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Community Ecocycle frames place-based systems change as moving through "dynamic, natural, and necessary developmental phases through which communities move." Rather than treating communities as static recipients of investment, the framework positions communities as developing entities whose current phase shapes their assets and needs. Funders use this framing to time and shape their engagement with communities appropriately.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funders must assess which developmental phase a community is currently in before designing investments
#### Constraints
- The framework is scoped to place-based systems change efforts, not to other grantmaking contexts

### Target Learners
- Funders and foundation staff

### Target Learning Objectives
- Understanding community development as phased and dynamic to inform systems-change investment
