A data-to-difference improvement process running from collecting data to evaluating progress¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article outlines key characteristics of a process for moving from data to making a difference in education. The process is described as one "that begins with collecting data and ends with evaluating progress," positioning data use as a staged cycle rather than a single act of reporting scores. The article presents this process as a way of responding to education problems that lack clear definitions and simple solutions, though only the abstract's summary of the process is available here.
