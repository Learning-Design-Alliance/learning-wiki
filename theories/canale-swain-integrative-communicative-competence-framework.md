# Canale and Swain's integrative framework of communicative competence synthesizes grammatical, sociolinguistic, and discourse knowledge

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The packet presents Canale & Swain's (1980) framework as an integrative theory in which communicative competence is a synthesis of grammatical knowledge, knowledge of how language performs communicative functions in social contexts, and knowledge of how utterances and functions combine according to principles of discourse. The framework is depicted in Figure 1 with grammatical, sociolinguistic, and strategic components, and is described as integrative because it covers speaking, reading, and writing rather than a subset of skill areas.
