# Cultivate framework of nine classroom learning conditions plus learning strategies and beliefs

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Cultivate survey is organized around a research-based framework of nine classroom learning conditions—such as Student Voice, Supportive Teaching, and Classroom Community—each accompanied by a guide outlining the principles underlying the condition, research background, practice ideas, scripted activities, reflection questions, and additional resources. Alongside the learning conditions, the survey measures student self-reflections on their own learning strategies and learning beliefs (such as Belonging and Agency) in the context of the same core class. The framework positions survey systems as practical, actionable, and catalytic tools within a comprehensive approach to understanding and improving learning conditions, aligned with the CPS Instructional Core centered on identity, community, and relationships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators and leaders need foundational knowledge of the survey and framework to use it well
- Learning condition guides linked on the Cultivate reporting site support interpretation
#### Constraints
- The framework addresses classroom learning conditions in core subject classes (math, English, social studies, science) in grades 5-12

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 5-12
- Educators, school leaders, and network/district staff using the data

### Target Learning Objectives
- Improving classroom learning conditions
- Strengthening student belonging, agency, and engagement
