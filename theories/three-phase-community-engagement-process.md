# Three-phase process for community engagement: Before, During, and After

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide organizes community engagement work into three phases: 'Before, During, and After community engagement.' Phase 1 lays groundwork (objectives, questions, engagement type, piloted materials), Phase 2 covers recording strategy and standardized protocols during sessions, and Phase 3 covers preparing data, coding, validating themes, and sharing findings. The guide states that 'the foundational steps in Phase 1 set the stage for meaningful and successful community engagement in the later phases,' and offers entry points for users at different starting points.
