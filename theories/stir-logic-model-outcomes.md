# STIR logic model linking intervention activities to short-, medium-, and long-term outcomes

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The partnership organizes its theory of change as a logic model moving from "Implement STIR intervention" through "Refine" and "Assess usability/feasibility ... across multiple cohorts" to studying long-term outcomes. Short-term outcomes are educator and leader understanding of evidence-based practices and data use; medium-term outcomes are their incorporation of those practices "within the context of a tiered delivery system"; long-term outcomes are increased students scoring at or above benchmarks. A diagnostic and implementation monitoring tool provides formative feedback across stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Iterative cohorts for refinement and usability/feasibility assessment
- A diagnostic and implementation monitoring tool for formative feedback
#### Constraints
- Long-term outcomes are anticipated, not yet measured in this document

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten and grade 1 students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Early literacy decoding skills
- Educator capacity for evidence-based literacy instruction
