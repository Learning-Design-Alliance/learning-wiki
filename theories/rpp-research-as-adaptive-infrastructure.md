# Research-practice partnership model treating research as public, relational, and adaptive infrastructure

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The UChicago Consortium's model treats research "as an active force for continuous improvement, not a static product," using mixed-methods inquiry embedded in practice rather than expecting practitioners to implement programs proven effective elsewhere with fidelity. Knowledge production is reciprocal: inquiry shapes practice and practice shapes inquiry, through co-design, participatory inquiry, and cross-disciplinary synthesis. The model rests on long-term, place-based engagement and trust-based partnerships with students, families, educators, and system leaders, positioning research as "public, relational, and adaptive infrastructure."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained, trust-based partnerships with students, families, educators, community organizations, and system leaders whose perspectives are essential to credible, relevant, actionable knowledge
#### Constraints
- Contrasts with paradigms positioning research outside of practice; the document states decades of evidence show fidelity-based implementation of externally proven programs is insufficient

### Target Learners
- All students in Chicago Public Schools from early childhood through post-secondary education

### Target Learning Objectives
- Equitable school improvement; continuous system-level learning and improvement
