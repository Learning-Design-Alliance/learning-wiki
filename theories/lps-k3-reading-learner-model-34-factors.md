# The LPS K-3 Reading Learner Model organizes learner variability into 34 Factors across four research areas

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Learner Positioning Systems K-3 Reading Learner Model is a framework synthesizing research from Language and Literacy, Cognition, Social Emotional Learning, and Student Biographical Background. Using a rigorous selection process, the team identified 34 Factors that affect how students learn to read, such as Decoding, Working Memory, Emotion, and Home Literacy Environment. Each Factor is described on the LPS website with its influence on reading, its interactions with other Factors, and links to research-based instructional strategies and design guidance for product developers.
