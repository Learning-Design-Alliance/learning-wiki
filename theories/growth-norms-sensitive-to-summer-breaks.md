# Growth norms modeled to be sensitive to summer breaks and instructional exposure

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The 2020 norms model learning trajectories as a function of instructional exposure so that the expected growth between terms reflects summer breaks and the ebb of instructional time. The report states: "We have modeled the norms to be sensitive to the impact of summ er breaks on learning." This allows term-to-term growth norms (fall to winter, winter to spring, spring to fall) that differ according to the instructional days separating the test events, rather than assuming uniform growth across calendar time.
