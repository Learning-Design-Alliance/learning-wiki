## Description
The brief organizes its content around a landscape of teacher factors that drive improvement in secondary writing outcomes. It summarizes "instructional skills and practices that support improvements in writing for students in grades 6-12," treating teacher instructional practices, engagement, support, and feedback as factors that shape writing outcomes. The landscape was built from an academic literature scan conducted in partnership with teacher development and writing instruction experts.
