# Nine common pain points districts encounter when implementing student success technology platforms

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From a Digital Promise field scan including "conversations with 50 individuals from 30 organizations including schools and districts, technology vendors, intermediaries, researchers, and strategic partners," the report derives a taxonomy of nine interconnected pain points: data quality, fragmented data systems, data access, actionable data visualizations, intervention tracking, system customization, system usability, cost barriers, and edtech marketplace volatility. The report states "Nine common pain points emerged that districts consistently encounter when implementing technology platforms and systems to drive student success work" and notes they are deeply interconnected across the ecosystem from data integration to market dynamics.
