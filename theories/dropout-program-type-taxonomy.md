# Taxonomy of dropout prevention and intervention program types

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review organizes the dropout intervention literature into a set of program types, presented in Table 1 with descriptions and study counts. Types include "Supplemental academic services" (remedial education, tutoring, homework assistance), "School or class restructuring" (small learning communities, block schedules, career academies, small class size), college preparation, vocational training, "Attendance monitoring & financial incentives", child care services for teenage mothers, CBT or skills training, case management, mentoring/counseling, and community service programs coupled with a weekly life skills curriculum. This taxonomy structures the comparative effectiveness analyses of the meta-analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must be school-based, school-affiliated, or community-based psychological, educational, or behavioral prevention or intervention programs with potential beneficial effects on student recipients
#### Constraints
- The taxonomy covers programs for school-aged youth approximately ages 4-18, including recent dropouts ages 18-21 only when the program explicitly targets secondary school completion

### Target Learners
- school-aged children and youth in preschool through 12th grade
- students at risk for dropping out, including low-SES, minority, and teenage-parent students

### Target Learning Objectives
- increasing school completion
- reducing school dropout
- improving attendance, engagement, academic performance, and school conduct
