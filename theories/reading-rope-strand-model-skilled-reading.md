# Reading Rope (Strand Model of Skilled Reading): word recognition and language comprehension as multifaceted strands

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Scarborough's 2001 Strand Model, or Reading Rope, elaborates the Simple View by treating each component as multifaceted: the word recognition strand encompasses phonological awareness, decoding, and sight recognition, while the language comprehension strand includes background knowledge, vocabulary, language structures, verbal reasoning, and literacy knowledge. The paper organizes Coach's microintervention inventory by these critical elements, stating the microinterventions are "aligned to the reading rope."
