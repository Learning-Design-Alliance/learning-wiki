# SMDEP pipeline theory: enrichment raises applicant competitiveness, ultimately diversifying the medical and dental workforce

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program rests on a pipeline logic: the "immediate goal of the program is to help students become more competitive applicants to medical and dental schools," while the "ultimate goal of the study is increasing the number of successful applicants from underrepresented groups and eventually diversifying the medical and dental labor force." The evaluation is structured around this chain from enrichment to workforce diversity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A residential enrichment program that makes participants more competitive applicants to medical or dental school
#### Constraints
- Aimed at underrepresented and socioeconomically disadvantaged students; workforce-diversification effects depend on downstream admission outcomes

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students from underrepresented and disadvantaged backgrounds

### Target Learning Objectives
- Preparation for medical and dental school admission
