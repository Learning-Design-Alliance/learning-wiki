Co-design collaborative team model for early science curriculum development¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The program was co-designed by teachers, families, curricula and media developers, and researchers. The document names five process components: "Building collaborative relationships", "Centering children & families (asset-based approach)", "Co-designing, pilot testing and refining", "Evaluating implementation & outcomes", and "Disseminating lessons and resources broadly". Each partner contributed distinct roles: teachers shared classroom needs, families and educators' insights informed curricular resources and digital tools, and researchers documented learning goals and facilitated research activities to inform iterations.
