Three ways to engage with AI in educational contexts: Interact, Create, and Apply¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Building on the research-based 4 As framework for families' AI literacy dimensions, the brief distinguishes "three ways to engage with AI in educational contexts: Interact, Create, and Apply." This taxonomy organizes how learners and educators can engage with AI, and understanding AI is described as a technical knowledge set that applies and extends computer science and computational thinking practices such as algorithmic thinking, pattern recognition, abstraction, and decomposition.
