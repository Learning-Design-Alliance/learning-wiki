# Four-phase evidence-building framework for designing and testing education solutions

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The M&E Checklist organizes evidence building for education solutions into four sequential phases, each with its own checklist document: Design the Solution (Phase 1), Refine the Solution (Phase 2), Assess for Early Evidence of Success (Phase 3), and Validate Effectiveness (Phase 4). The framework structures how solution developers move from initial design through refinement, early evidence gathering, and validation of effectiveness, with stakeholder collaboration from a priority community throughout.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each phase requires its own checklist document and activities; the process is carried out in collaboration with stakeholders from a priority community.
#### Constraints
- The framework is iterative rather than strictly linear: users may complete a phase multiple times, move backwards to a previous phase, or abandon a solution altogether.

### Target Learners
- Students in priority communities targeted by education solutions, such as middle years math students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Developing, refining, and validating effective teaching and learning solutions
