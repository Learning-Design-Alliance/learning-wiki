# Weick's enactment model: understanding the environment by actively transforming it through action

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on Weick (1979, 2009), the paper defines enactment as "the process of actively transforming the environment through action": people in organizations understand their environment by playing an active role in constructing it. Applied to schools, teachers enact the curriculum as a way to understand it, and understanding is a social practice rather than an intrinsic process of isolated individuals. The model also explains how people's understanding of policy messages is evidenced in their practices during implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Analysis must treat people as attached to their environments through action and practices rather than as isolated subjects
