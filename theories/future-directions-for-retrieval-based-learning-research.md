# Future Directions for Retrieval-Based Learning Research

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
The chapter closes by "highlighting four possible avenues": deepening understanding of mechanisms and linking them to global models of memory; resolving how to balance retrieval success and effort; adopting a contextual perspective that tests whether effects interact with learners, materials, and assessments; and integrating retrieval practice into educational activities beyond classroom quizzing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Manipulate features of materials or aspects of final assessments to test interactions rather than only generalizing effects.
#### Constraints
- The chapter states that generality across learner characteristics is established to a lesser extent than across materials and final assessments.

### Target Learners
- Researchers and educators working on retrieval practice

### Target Learning Objectives
- Identify mechanisms and boundary conditions of retrieval-based learning
- Develop new educational activities that incorporate retrieval
