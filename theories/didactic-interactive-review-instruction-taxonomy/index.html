Didactic, interactive, and review: a three-approach taxonomy of elementary instruction¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

This report organizes elementary classroom instruction into three approaches distinguished by teacher and student behaviors. Didactic instruction involves lecture, single-answer questions, and correctness-based assessment; interactive instruction involves coaching, open questions, discussion, and process-oriented assessment; review involves revisiting previously taught content. The authors state that "in this report we use the terms didactic and interactive to distinguish between the first and the second approach to instruction," and add review as a third dimension examined separately. The taxonomy structures all subsequent analyses of exposure and achievement effects.
