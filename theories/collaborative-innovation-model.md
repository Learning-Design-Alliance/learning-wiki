# Collaborative Innovation: a six-phase co-research and co-design R&D model

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Collaborative Innovation is "a structured approach to research and development that brings together districts, schools, communities, and program partners to co-research educational challenges and co-design solutions that directly beneﬁt students furthest from opportunity." It is grounded in co-leadership, co-research, and co-design, and "unfolds through six phases: Readiness; Connect and Commit; Inquire and Investigate; Design and Develop; Implement and Iterate; and Sustain and Scale." It extends the earlier Inclusive Innovation framework developed with more than 30 districts across 10 projects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District-community teams build relationships, co-research challenges, and co-design solutions responsive to their local context across the phases
#### Constraints
- The model is designed for students furthest from opportunity and centers equity, lived experience, and community expertise at every stage

### Target Learners
- Students furthest from opportunity
- District and school leaders
- Families and community members

### Target Learning Objectives
- Capacity to identify and solve local educational challenges
- Co-research and co-design skills
- Sustaining equity-centered R&D beyond a single project
