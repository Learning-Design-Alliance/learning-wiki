# Five Essential Supports for Student Learning

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report organizes its survey results around a framework of "the five essential supports for student learning, initially presented in Annenberg's" work, which are also included in the Chicago Public Schools' School Improvement Plan for Advancing Academic Achievement (SIP-AA). The framework structures the ten profile graphs and the detailed Part II scale pages: school leadership, parent and community partnerships, student-centered learning climate, professional development and collaboration (professional community, workplace, and development), and quality instructional program, with student outcomes reported as complementary dispositions. The report states that "Many of the questions in the teacher and student surveys are directly related to the five essential supports for student learning."
