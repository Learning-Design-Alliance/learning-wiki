Cultivate framework: classroom learning conditions shape students' learning beliefs, which shape learning and well-being¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Cultivate framework is an explanatory model in which students' experiences of nine classroom learning conditions (Meaningful Work, Learning Goals, Teacher Caring, Feedback for Growth, Supportive Teaching, Affirming Identities, Classroom Community, Student Voice, Well-Organized Classroom) predict learning beliefs (valued, can grow, class meaningful, empowered, capable), which in turn predict learning and well-being. The playbook states that "students' experiences of classroom learning conditions, measured by the Cultivate Survey predict… students' beliefs about themselves as learners, which then predict… students' learning & well-being." It is used by school teams to interpret survey data and target improvement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • School teams use Cultivate survey data as a starting point to partner with students on improving classroom environments

Constraints¶

  • The playbook presents the meditational chain as research-based but prints no coefficients or effect sizes in this document

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 5-12

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Improving classroom learning conditions, student learning beliefs, learning, and well-being

Claims¶

  • Student Experience Survey Reports Predict School Outcomes [+M]
  • Each individual learning condition predicts I am Valued gains, with Classroom Community the largest (15.0 middle grades, 16.9 high school percentile points) [+W]
  • All nine Cultivate learning condition measures show strong Rasch person reliability (0.82-0.87) in CPS administrations [+W]
  • Within-year improvements in overall learning conditions predict large gains in students' I am Valued beliefs [+W]

Related Theories¶

  • Cultivate framework of nine classroom learning conditions plus learning strategies and beliefs
  • Elevate learning conditions framework (Teacher Caring, Meaningful Work, Feedback for Growth)

Examples¶

  • Cultivate Survey: anonymous twice-yearly student survey of classroom learning conditions in grades 5-12
  • Use student experience data in an iterative inquire-change-monitor cycle with students as partners
  • Use Cultivate data as a framework for action to identify strengths and improvement areas in learning environments
  • Cultivate Survey: twice-yearly student voice survey of classroom learning conditions and learning beliefs
  • Cultivate student experience survey and reporting site
