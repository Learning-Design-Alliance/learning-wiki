NNPS framework of six types of involvement with an action team approach¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The NNPS model is organized around "a framework of six types of involvement and an action team approach." Rather than treating family engagement as isolated events, the framework structures partnership work into distinct types of involvement that a school-based team plans, schedules, and evaluates over the school year. The framework is operationalized through the Action Team for Partnerships, which writes a One-Year Action Plan connecting engagement activities to school improvement goals, and through NNPS-provided coaching, training, and planning materials that support implementation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An Action Team for Partnerships that meets monthly, shares leadership, and conducts activities engaging all families in their children's education.

Constraints¶

  • The framework is implemented through NNPS membership, which provides the coaching, training, and planning materials described.

Target Learners¶

  • All families in a school community, elementary through high school

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Family and community engagement connected to student success and welcoming-climate goals

Claims¶

  • NNPS implementation increases elementary school average daily attendance more than matched comparison schools (effect size +0.07) [+W]
