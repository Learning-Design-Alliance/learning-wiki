# Andragogy: Knowles' framework of assumptions about adult learning, distinct from pedagogy

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Andragogy is Malcolm Knowles' theoretical framework of assumptions about how adults learn, argued since 1968 under the banner of "andragogy, not pedagogy." The essay explains that the term predates Knowles, going "back to nineteenth-century Germany," and that Lindeman brought it to American education discourse by 1926. Knowles set forth six assumptions in 1980, and the framework treats andragogy as a set of assumptions that includes the pedagogical ones and adds to them.
