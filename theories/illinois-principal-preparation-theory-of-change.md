# Illinois Principal Preparation Program Theory of Change with feedback loop

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Illinois principal preparation redesign is organized around a theory of change depicting a pipeline from selection of high-potential candidates, through course work and school-based learning experiences including internships, to performance-based assessments, principal certification, placement in schools, highly effective principals and assistant principals, and schools demonstrating positive change. The theory of change evolved over a 10 year period and "identifies the critical elements of the new policy." A feedback loop involves data gathered and analyzed for external evaluation to inform effective policies and practices for continuous improvement.
