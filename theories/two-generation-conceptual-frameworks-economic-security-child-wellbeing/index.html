Two conceptual frameworks for intentionally combined parent-and-child programs¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q3–q4 · 0 of 2 report an effect size

Description¶

The brief presents two conceptual frameworks intended to guide programs serving low-income parents and children through combined activities. The authors state the frameworks "have the potential to expand our understanding of programs that aim to meet the needs of low-income parents and children through intentionally combined activities and approaches." The frameworks organize thinking about which services are involved, how they relate to outcomes for both generations, and how delivery can be coordinated.
