# K-12 Digital Equity Framework: five interdependent domains

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The K-12 Digital Equity Framework organizes digital equity work in schools and districts into five interdependent domains: Leadership for Digital Transformation; Coherent Systems, Resources, and Policies; Consistent Access to Devices and Connectivity; Digital Competency; and Powerful Learning Propelled by Technology. The framework states that "Activating all five domains over time is essential" because domains trigger one another. Each domain is animated by guiding principles and made actionable through domain indicators with essential questions and suggested evidence and artifacts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools and districts must address all five domains over time, since operationalizing one domain is in part triggered by implementing another.
#### Constraints
- The framework warns that failing to address all five domains over time could exacerbate digital divides.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Digital competency
- Equitable academic, social, and economic outcomes
- Powerful learning propelled by technology
