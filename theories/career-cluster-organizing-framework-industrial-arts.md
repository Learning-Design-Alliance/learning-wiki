# Career-cluster organization of industrial arts content around the U.S. Office of Education's fifteen career clusters

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide organizes industrial arts content around career clusters rather than isolated tool skills. It explains that "The U. S. Office of Education suggests fifteen career clusters as a working base for the study of a substantial number of the existing career opportunities," and that this middle school guide develops four clusters with potential for primary emphasis in industrial arts: construction, manufacturing, transportation, and communications. Within communications, content is further organized into four interrelated areas that "supplement and complement each other."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A working base of career clusters for studying existing career opportunities
#### Constraints
- Components of at least five other career clusters also have primary emphasis in industrial arts, so the four-cluster treatment is partial

### Target Learners
- Middle school students in grades 6 through 9

### Target Learning Objectives
- Understanding basic concepts of career clusters
- Exploring careers related to an industrial society
