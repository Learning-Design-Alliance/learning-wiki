# Three-method framework for accounting for co-teaching in value-added models

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper organizes the problem of isolating each teacher's contribution when "the student is taught the same subject by more than one teacher" around three estimation approaches: the Partial Credit Method, the Teacher Team Method, and the Full Roster Method. Each method apportions student achievement credit differently across the multiple teachers of a subject. The paper compares the three to estimate teacher effects in value-added models.
