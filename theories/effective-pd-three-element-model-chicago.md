# Model of effective professional development: frequency of participation, content exposure, and pedagogical quality linked to instruction and innovation

## Description
The report presents a model of effective professional development built on a consensus view in the field. At its center are "three basic elements of professional development practice: (a) the frequency of teacher participation, (b) exposure to content, and (c) pedagogical quality." These elements are linked to two outcomes, classroom instruction and school orientation toward innovation, with an indirect path to student achievement through changed practice. Pedagogical quality is defined as experiential, collaborative, grounded in teachers' work and research, focused on subject matter, sustained, intensive, and supported by follow-up.
