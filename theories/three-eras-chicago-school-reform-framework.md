# Three eras of Chicago school reform: Decentralization, Accountability, Diversification

## Description
The report organizes 20 years of Chicago school reform into three eras defined by district leadership and central reform policies. Era 1 (1988–1995, "Decentralization") devolved authority to Local School Councils; Era 2 (1996–2001, "Accountability") under Paul Vallas introduced test-based promotion standards, probation, and consequences; Era 3 (2002–2009, "Diversification") under Arne Duncan opened new charter and contract schools, closed poorly performing schools, and expanded data use. The framework serves as the organizing axis for all trend analyses in the report, which emphasizes that "each era of reform built on the reforms of the prior period."
