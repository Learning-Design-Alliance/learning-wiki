# Test-taking disengagement as a measurement construct in assessment programs

## Description
Test-taking disengagement is treated in this article as a measurable construct whose presence in assessment programs — especially international ones — undermines score validity. The author frames it as a phenomenon with a measurable dimension, noting "notable advances in both the measurement of disengagement" over the past 20 years, and as one whose distortive effects propagate to individual and aggregated scores. The article's contribution is to organize research insights about this construct and derive implications for how assessment programs should respond.
