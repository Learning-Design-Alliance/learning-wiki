Critique of dominant research paradigms in mathematics learning and a shift toward interdisciplinary, asset-based approaches¶

Description¶

The chapter's organizing argument is that prevailing research paradigms for understanding mathematics learning are inadequate and should be replaced by interdisciplinary and asset-based approaches. The overview states: "This chapter critiques the dominant research paradigms for understanding mathematics learning and argues for a shift toward interdisciplinary and asset-based approaches." The framework reorients research away from deficit-oriented paradigms toward approaches that treat marginalized learners' assets as central, and it is addressed to researchers and other stakeholders in mathematics education.
