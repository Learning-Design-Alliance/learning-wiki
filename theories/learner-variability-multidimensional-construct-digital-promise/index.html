Learner variability as a multidimensional construct spanning cognitive, social, emotional, and background factors¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The survey defines "learner variability" as a construct covering the abilities students have and the challenges they bring to learning. As the report states, "Factors include their cognitive, social and emotional skills, as well as personal background, including health and psychological well-being, among other things." The survey used this definition to measure public, parent, and teacher recognition of variability between students and across subjects, and to frame questions about tailored instruction and edtech support for diverse learners.
