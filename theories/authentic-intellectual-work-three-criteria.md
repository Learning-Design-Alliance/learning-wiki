# Authentic intellectual work framework: construction of knowledge through disciplined inquiry producing products with value beyond school

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report defines authentic intellectual work as a standard for judging the intellectual quality of teaching and learning, contrasting it with routine school tasks. It states that "authentic intellectual work involves original application of knowledge and skills" rather than routine use of facts and procedures, entails disciplined inquiry, and yields products with meaning beyond school success. The three criteria are construction of knowledge, disciplined inquiry (prior knowledge base, in-depth understanding, elaborated communication), and value beyond school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All three criteria must be present for work to be fully authentic; the report states all three criteria are important and that authenticity is diminished when any one is missing
- All three criteria are important; an assignment meeting only some criteria falls short of authentic intellectual work as the article's examples show
#### Constraints
- The standards do not prescribe precise knowledge, skills, or dispositions for the curriculum; repetitive practice and memorization remain necessary foundations and are not to be abolished
- The framework was applied to writing and mathematics assignments in grades three, six, and eight of Chicago elementary schools

### Target Learners
- elementary and middle-grades students in urban public schools
- elementary students in grades 3, 6, and 8 in urban public schools

### Target Learning Objectives
- complex intellectual work: construction of knowledge, disciplined inquiry, elaborated communication, and products with value beyond school
- complex intellectual activity including interpretation, analysis, synthesis, and elaborated communication, alongside basic skills

# Authentic Intellectual Work framework: construction of knowledge through disciplined inquiry producing products with value beyond school

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
The framework defines authentic intellectual work as involving "original application of knowledge and skills, rather than just routine use of facts and procedures." Its three criteria are construction of knowledge (organizing, interpreting, evaluating, or synthesizing prior knowledge to solve novel problems), disciplined inquiry (use of a prior knowledge base, in-depth understanding, and elaborated communication), and production of discourse, products, or performances with utilitarian, aesthetic, or personal "value beyond school." The study adapted this framework, developed by the Center on Organization and Restructuring of Schools, into scoring standards for classroom assignments.
