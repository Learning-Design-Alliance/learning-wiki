# Agent-based simulation methodology for decomposing teacher quality gap sources

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article introduces "a novel simulation methodology" that models teacher workforce dynamics to attribute teacher quality gaps to their underlying processes. It models "three interrelated processes—teacher attrition from the state teaching workforce, teacher mobility between teaching positions, and teacher hiring for open positions" and their joint contribution to TQGs in K-12 public schools. The methodology is applied empirically to Washington State administrative data to generate counterfactual gap-closing estimates.

## Design Implications

### Context
