Six core tenets of Collaborative Innovation, organized as Practice and Impact tenets¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The model is anchored in six core tenets that "deﬁne how the work is carried out and what it is designed to achieve." They are "organized into two categories: Practice Tenets, which guide how the work is done, and Impact Tenets, which deﬁne what success looks like." Practice tenets are Collective Ownership, Student Voice and Leadership, Context Expertise and Proximity, and Continuity of Commitment; Impact tenets are Reimagine Progress and Build Capacity. The tenets ground the model's phases, protocols, and activities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Practitioners should revisit the tenets throughout an engagement, particularly when adapting the model to different timelines, settings, or organizational conditions

Constraints¶

  • The tenets are not supplementary values; they distinguish Collaborative Innovation from conventional R&D approaches

Target Learners¶

  • District-community teams
  • Students historically excluded from educational decision-making
  • Educators and program partners

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Equity-centered research and design practice
  • Shared ownership and co-leadership
  • Multidimensional definitions of progress
