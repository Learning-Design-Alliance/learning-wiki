# Edtech Procurement Framework: a six-step process for education leaders to select, implement, evaluate, and sustain edtech

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 3 studies (3 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size

## Description
The framework is "A step-by-step process to help education leaders develop and improve infrastructure around edtech selection, implementation, evaluation, and procurement to drive student learning." It organizes procurement into six numbered steps: Analyze & Inventory, Discover & Decide, Design & Plan, Mobilize & Implement, Evaluate & Reflect, and Sustain & Scale. Each step specifies concrete leader actions, from cross-departmental data analysis through sharing pilot results with critical partners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders collaborate with educators and school leaders at the planning step and share results with critical partners at the sustainability step
#### Constraints
- Framed for education leaders procuring edtech for schools; not a classroom-level instructional method

### Target Learners
- K-12 school and district leaders procuring edtech

### Target Learning Objectives
- Aligning edtech purchases with targeted teaching and learning goals
