# ICAP framework: four engagement modes from Interactive to Passive

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The primer introduces the ICAP framework as a way of categorizing how students engage in learning activities based on observable behaviors. It states that behaviors "can be classified into four modes, from highest to lowest engagement: Interactive, Constructive, Active, and Passive." Instruction should move students beyond passive and active engagement toward constructive and interactive modes where they make sense of information and build on each other's ideas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction aims to move students toward constructive and interactive engagement modes rather than passive or active ones
#### Constraints
- The primer presents ICAP as a framework attributed to Chi & Wylie (2014) and Chi & Boucher (2023), not as a tested result of its own

### Target Learners
- K-12 STEM students

### Target Learning Objectives
- deeper engagement and conceptual understanding through classroom discussion
