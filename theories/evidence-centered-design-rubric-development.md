## Description
The article applies Evidence Centered Design (Mislevy, Almond, & Lukas, 2003) as a principled assessment development approach. As the article states, "Ze XVed an eYidence-cenWeUed deVign aSSURach (MiVleY\, AlmRnd, & LXkaV, 2003) WR deYelRS Whe UXbUicV", because the approach helps ensure clear connections between what is observed in assignments and student work and what is inferred about them. The process involved articulating claims, defining assessment targets, considering what evidence would imply targets are met, and considering tasks that would bring about the evidence; each decision in the development pathway needed adequate justification.
