# OpenSciEd research logic model

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reproduces a logic model articulated in a companion paper, which "describes the expected outcomes from an intervention and details the rationale for expecting impact, based on learning sciences principles". It links OpenSciEd's distinctive principles and key affordances to three nested ecosystem levels (classroom interactions, teacher supports, system alignment) and four desired outcome categories: equitable student outcomes, teacher capacity, system-level outcomes, and resources and innovations. The model is used to organize and map the emergent research themes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Research questions should be situated within the model's specified relationships among principles, ecosystem levels, and outcomes
#### Constraints
- The article notes outcomes are desired outcomes reflecting designer hopes, "rather than what outcomes research has gathered evidence about to date"

### Target Learners
- K-12 science students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Guiding science education research design and system-level curriculum implementation
