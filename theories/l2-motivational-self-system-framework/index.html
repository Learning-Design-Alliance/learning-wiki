Dörnyei's L2 Motivational Self System as a three-component framework for L2 learning motivation¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article presents Dörnyei's (2005) L2 Motivational Self System as a comprehensive framework for L2 learning motivation, derived from Higgins' Self-Discrepancy Theory. It comprises the ideal L2 self, the ought-to L2 self, and L2 learning experience, described as three primary sources of motivation: "the learner's vision of oneself as an effective L2 speaker, the social pressure coming from the learner's environment, and positive learning experiences". The study operationalizes it via ten sub-factors measured with the Persian-version questionnaire validated by Papi (2010).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Measured with a validated multi-subscale questionnaire covering ideal L2 self, ought-to L2 self, and learning-experience-related factors

Constraints¶

  • The third component, L2 learning experience, is conceptualized at a different level from the two self-guides as a bottom-up process representing the learning environment

Target Learners¶

  • EFL learners in language institutes and universities

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Second/foreign language learning motivation
  • L2 self-efficacy

Claims¶

  • Ideal L2 self positively and significantly predicts student self-efficacy, generalizing prior qualitative findings quantitatively [+W]
  • In the motivational profile of Iranian EFL learners, instrumentality-promotion received the highest mean, followed by ideal L2 self [+W]
  • Overall L2 motivation is a significant positive predictor of L2 self-efficacy among Iranian EFL learners [+W]
  • Criterion measures, attitudes towards learning English, instrumentality promotion, and ideal L2 self are the strongest predictors of L2 self-efficacy [+W]
  • All ten sub-factors of the L2 motivational self-system correlate significantly and positively with L2 self-efficacy [+W]
