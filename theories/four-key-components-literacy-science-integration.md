# Four key components framework for effective elementary literacy-science integration

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The guide organizes effective integration of literacy and science instruction at the elementary level around four components: authentic literacy tasks and phenomenon-based instruction, rich science vocabulary, cumulative structured learning, and scientific discourse. It states that integration creates environments where "Neither subject takes a back seat," with literacy instruction supporting science and vice versa. The guide uses icons to flag lesson elements aligned to each component, and its nine-lesson sequence is designed to bring all four to life.
