Six-topic taxonomy for guiding GenAI responsible use policy content¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The RAPID GenAI Working Group identified six topics for creating responsible/acceptable use policy language about generative AI: AI literacy, safety, ethics, transparency, implementation guidelines, and evaluation and impact. The deck states these were identified "Based on the findings and activities of different members within the GenAI Working Group," including the EDSAFE AI SAFE Framework and Digital Promise's AI Literacy Framework. Each topic is defined and then elaborated with a rationale section citing evidence-based research and an example section showing real district policy language.
