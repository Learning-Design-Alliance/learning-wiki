Seven-element framework for effective professional development¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report organizes its professional learning analysis around seven elements that a meta-analysis by Darling-Hammond, Hyler, and Gardner (2017) identified in effective PD: content focus, active learning, collaboration supports, models of effective practice, coaching and expert support, feedback and reflection, and sustained duration. The report states "High-quality PD creates space for teachers to share ideas and collaborate in their learning, often in job-embedded contexts." Each element is then mapped onto a concrete PLI practice with associated analytics evidence.
