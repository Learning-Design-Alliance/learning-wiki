# Inclusive Innovation R&D model: six core tenets for equity-centered education research and development

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Inclusive Innovation R&D model is an equity-centered education research and development framework organized around six core tenets that Digital Promise developed and refined over three years with over 30 school districts. Four practice tenets (Collective Ownership, Student Voice and Leadership, Context Expertise and Proximity, Continuity of Equity) describe how the R&D process is conducted, and two impact tenets (Reimagine Progress, Build Capacity) describe the outcomes it targets. The model is premised on the understanding that "practice informs product" so that solutions enabling historically and systematically excluded students to thrive must reflect inclusive practices. It defines historically and systematically excluded learners as those from low-income backgrounds, rural learners, learners with learning differences, and Black, Indigenous, and Latino/a learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must engage in partnership with communities and students rather than conducting R&D independently
- Communities must be resourced to sustain capacity for R&D into the future
#### Constraints
- Findings are described as emerging from exploratory research on pilot projects launched since 2020, with sustainability research ongoing

### Target Learners
- historically and systematically excluded learners including low-income, rural, learning-difference, Black, Indigenous, and Latino/a students

### Target Learning Objectives
- educational equity
- student engagement
- powerful learning opportunities
