# The Fund's theory of action: concentrating talented teachers and leaders in a school moves it toward improvement

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (6 for, 3 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report evaluates The Chicago Public Education Fund's theory of action, which holds that a concentration of talented teachers and leadership in a school will move the school toward improvement. The most positive findings, particularly the LAUNCH/NBCT cluster results, 'begin to suggest support for The Fund's theory of action, that a concentration of talented teachers and leaders in a school will move the school toward improvement.' The theory was tested by comparing schools combining trained principals with NBCT clusters against schools with similarly experienced principals.
