## Description
The brief treats seasonal learning patterns and summer loss as a measurable phenomenon with direct consequences for how schools are evaluated. Its description centers on "the effect of seasonality on estimates of school effectiveness", positioning out-of-school periods as a source of variance that school effectiveness models must account for. The brief connects this phenomenon to policy by showing that models omitting it can mislabel schools. In the wiki it serves as an explanatory account of why growth-based, seasonally aware metrics matter for accountability.
