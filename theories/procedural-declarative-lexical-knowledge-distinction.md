# Procedural-declarative distinction in lexical knowledge

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Robinson distinguishes the 'declarative' knowledge 'that' words have particular meanings from the 'procedural' knowledge of 'how' to realise those meanings in use, adopting Widdowson's term. He argues the lexicon is not a static repository but 'fluid', a medium through which meanings are carried and negotiated, so that communicative competence includes both kinds of knowledge mobilised in time-constrained, goal-oriented discourse. This framework underpins his critique of definitional vocabulary teaching and his exercise designs.
