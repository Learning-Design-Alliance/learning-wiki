# Two framings of SEL for measurement: discrete skills versus broad competency

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The brief organizes the measurement problem around two alternative conceptualizations of social-emotional learning. One framing treats SEL as "a set of discrete skills", which favors narrower, more targeted measurement; the other treats SEL as "a broader competency", which favors broader measurement instruments. The brief positions new research as providing insights into which framing better serves measurement purposes, making this a foundational axis for deciding what an SEL assessment should target.
