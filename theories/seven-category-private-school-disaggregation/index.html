Seven-category disaggregation of U.S. private schools in the specialty school dashboard¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The appendix organizes private schools covered by the dashboard into a seven-part classification by religious affiliation and secular status. It states: "Within private schools, we also disaggregate further into the following categories of private schools: (a) Catholic schools, (b) Jewish schools, (c) Lutheran schools, (d) Islamic schools, (e) Seventh-day Adventists, (f) other religious schools, and (g) non-religious schools." This taxonomy structures how growth results are reported for private school subtypes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Private school data must be classifiable into the seven affiliation categories to populate the disaggregation

Constraints¶

  • The taxonomy applies only within U.S. private schools in this dashboard

Target Learners¶

  • Students enrolled in U.S. private schools

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Academic growth measurement disaggregated by private school type
