## Description
The report organizes alternative measures of student achievement growth used in teacher evaluation into three types that do not rely on traditional annual state assessments. It summarizes evidence on "commercially available assessments, locally developed common assessments, and teacher-developed student learning objectives." This tripartite structure frames the literature review's coverage of non-traditional growth measures for evaluating teacher performance.
