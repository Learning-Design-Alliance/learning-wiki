# Four-domain SEL construct framework: growth mindset, self-efficacy, social awareness, and self-management

## Description
The article organizes its stability analysis around four distinct social-emotional learning domains: "growth mindset, self-efficacy, social awareness, and self-management." These four constructs are treated as measurable SEL domains whose variance can be decomposed into stable versus time- and context-specific components, and compared against academic achievement domains (math and reading) on stability over time. The framework frames SEL as partly state-like rather than purely trait-like, which drives the article's implications for how SEL measures should be used in accountability and evaluation.
