# Widdowson's account of grammar as mediator between words and context for the achievement of meaning

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The bibliography reports, citing Widdowson (1988), the argument that grammar is more than a collection of sentence patterns: it is "the mediator between words and context for the achievement of meaning". Lexical meaning and the context of shared knowledge need various features of grammar to make meaning apparent. For the language learner, knowing how grammar mediates is essential to achieving meaning, supporting the position that language learning is essentially grammar learning.
