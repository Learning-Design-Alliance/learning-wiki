Five types of local school improvement initiatives, grouped into three major approaches¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Drawing on field observations from the case-study synthesis, the report identifies five types of school improvement initiatives constituting three major approaches: improving social relations, unfocused academic initiatives, and a systemic approach. Environmental order issues took precedence in many schools, turning schools into a "safe haven that was secure for children, welcoming of parents, and providing an orderly work environment for professional staff." Peripheral academic changes add programs haphazardly, while Christmas tree schools are showcases whose pursuit of new initiatives distracts from systematic examination of core operations.
