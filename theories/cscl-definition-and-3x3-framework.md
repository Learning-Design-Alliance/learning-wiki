# Computer-supported collaborative learning: definition and Kirschner and Erken's 3x3 framework

## Description
The presentation situates intelligence augmentation within the field of computer-supported collaborative learning (CSCL), which it says has been a field of the learning sciences for about 30 years with a scholarly society, handbook, and international conference series. It quotes a definition of collaborative learning as "coordinated, synchronous activity that is the result of a continued attempt to construct and maintain a shared conception of a problem", and presents Kirschner and Erken's 3x3 framework crossing cognitive, motivational, and social aspects of learning; community, small group, and individual levels; and tools for representation, discourse, and process. Scripts are described as understanding roles, responsibilities, and dynamics of collaborative learning and how computers can provide enabling supports.
