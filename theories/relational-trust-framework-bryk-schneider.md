# Relational trust: effective social relationships in and around schools as a prime resource for school improvement

## Description
Relational trust is the concept Bryk and Schneider develop to name "how effective social relationships—which they term relational trust—can serve as a prime resource for school improvement." It is built from case studies of "the network of relationships that make up the school community" and examines how "the myriad social exchanges that make up daily life in a school community generate, or fail to generate, a successful educational environment." The framework holds that the quality of social relationships operating in and around schools is central to their functioning and strongly predicts positive student outcomes, and that a broad base of trust across a school community is a critical resource when professionals and parents embark on major reforms.
