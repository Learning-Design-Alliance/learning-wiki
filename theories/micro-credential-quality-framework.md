# Digital Promise quality-assurance framework for research-backed, evidence-based micro-credentials

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To ensure quality and rigor, Digital Promise developed a framework ensuring micro-credentials are "research-backed and evidence-based." The framework has five components: a "definition of the specific competency;" a "key method to achieve that competency;" "research and resources to support the key method and competency;" a "description of the evidence and artifacts that must be submitted to demonstrate competency;" and a "rubric and scoring guide for how that evidence will be assessed." This structure underlies how each credential in the ecosystem defines, supports, and assesses a discrete educator competency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Every micro-credential must include all five framework components, including a rubric and scoring guide used by trained assessors
#### Constraints
- The report states researchers must still validate the assessment and scoring process

### Target Learners
- K-12 educators earning credentials
- issuing organizations and assessors

### Target Learning Objectives
- validating educator competence in discrete skills
- quality control of professional learning recognition
