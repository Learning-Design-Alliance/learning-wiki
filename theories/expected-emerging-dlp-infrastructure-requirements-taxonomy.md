# Two-category taxonomy of requirements for DLPs as research infrastructure: expected technical work versus emerging organizational and community work

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper organizes infrastructure-building work into two kinds: (1) expected technical platform modifications (data enhancement and documentation, content authoring, privacy, experimental apparatus, student user experience) and (2) emergent organizational and community work that was less salient. The authors state, "Specifically, this paper articulates two kinds of infrastructure-building work: (1) the work SEERNet members expected and (2) the emergent work that was either less salient or not as obvious."
