Elevate learning conditions framework (Teacher Caring, Meaningful Work, Feedback for Growth)¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Elevate framework organizes classroom experience into measurable learning conditions that predict academic outcomes. The report examines “three learning conditions—Teacher Caring, Meaningful Work, and Feedback for Growth,” rating students' agreement on Likert scales and compositing the scores. It posits that positive conditions support learning by enhancing motivation and by mitigating anxieties that divide attention and reduce the brain's capacity to process information, and that conditions function as early, actionable indicators of learning.

Requirements¶
