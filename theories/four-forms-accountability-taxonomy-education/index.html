Four-form taxonomy of accountability: outcome-based, rule-based, market-based, and professional¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article organizes accountability in K-12 education into distinct forms reviewed side by side: "rule-based, market-based, and professional accountability alongside outcome-based accountability." Each form is treated as invoking its own motivational mechanisms, and the review uses "evidence from the laboratory and the field" to describe how each operates. This taxonomy is the review's organizing framework for rethinking accountability design under the Every Student Succeeds Act.
