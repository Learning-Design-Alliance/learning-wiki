# Bildung as a theoretical framework for open education

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article introduces Bildung (self-cultivation, self-realization) as "a powerful reflective tool and framework for approaching open education." Bildung is understood as a free, dialogical, dialectical interplay between the individual and the world supporting self-realization, a never-ending attempt to live a good life. The authors argue its associated values — autonomy, critical reflection, inclusivity, and embracing self-development — suit open education and provide a critical lens for assessing contemporary educational models.
