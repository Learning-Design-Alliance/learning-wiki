# Expanded accountability

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lens developed by Dr. Eve L. Ewing's Beyond Schools Lab that argues for "a more holistic view of accountability that interrogates and holds non-educational decision-makers accountable" for how their actions shape the environment in which students, teachers, and schools try to succeed. The report uses it to connect students' lived experiences in communities to their educational attainment, and to argue that addressing enduring disinvestments in communities of color is an underutilized avenue for improving attainment. It urges those working in housing and transportation to consider how their work relates to school outcomes.

### Target Learners
- CPS students, particularly Black and Brown students in communities facing disinvestment

### Target Learning Objectives
- Improving high school and college attainment through cross-policy, community-centered improvement efforts
