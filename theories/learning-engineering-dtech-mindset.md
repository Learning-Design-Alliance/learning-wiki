# Learning engineering: educators take research and turn it into classroom solutions using engineering problem-solving processes

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Learning engineering, as described in this report, applies engineering problem-solving processes to problems of educational practice: Uncapher described 'the synergy around design thinking and science of learning' as part of 'what we're calling learning engineering.' Teachers who adopt the mindset treat the classroom like a lab, use research to design solutions, prototype, test, collect data, and iterate. Montgomery framed it as staff turning research into something usable for students, the end users.
