# SEL frameworks as organizing tools distinct from the competencies they structure

## Description
The brief distinguishes frameworks from competencies: competencies are the knowledge, attitudes, and skills people need to be socially and emotionally competent, while a framework organizes and names those competencies, their relationships, and the learning process and contexts. As the brief puts it, "a framework is a tool that helps to organize ideas in order to provide a foundation for thinking, communicating, and acting." Frameworks can have different numbers and levels of competencies; this brief addresses frameworks rather than each specific competency.
