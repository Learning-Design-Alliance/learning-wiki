# Three-group engagement taxonomy of level 1 physics students based on attendance and responsiveness

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study, `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article breaks the student population into three broad categories by absenteeism from compulsory continuous assessment and responsiveness to contact. Group 1 (~90%) attended fully and responded to contact; Group 2 (~5%) missed multiple sessions and responded sporadically; Group 3 (~5%) "rarely responded to any attempt to contact them" and generally did not improve. Academic performance declined across the groups, from A/D-range in Group 1 to E-grade and below in Group 3.
