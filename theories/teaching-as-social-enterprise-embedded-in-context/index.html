Teaching and learning as social enterprises embedded within school and community contexts¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The chapter's organizing framework holds that teaching and learning are "social enterprises which are embedded within school and community contexts." It explains school improvement through four interdependent social factors examined with Chicago research: community context, teacher collaboration, family involvement, and inclusive school leadership that builds teachers' capacity. This reframes improvement as an organizational and relational problem rather than a purely instructional one.
