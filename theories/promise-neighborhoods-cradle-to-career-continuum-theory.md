# Promise Neighborhoods' cradle-to-career continuum model of community-wide poverty mitigation

## Description
The Promise Neighborhoods model holds that the effects of growing up in poverty can be offset by building a comprehensive continuum of supports spanning early childhood through career. The article states that Promise Neighborhoods "seek to offset the effects of growing up in poverty by building a comprehensive continuum of 'cradle-to-career' supports that enable children to reach their potential." Grantees implement this continuum through federally funded place-based programs.
