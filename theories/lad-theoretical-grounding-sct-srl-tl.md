# Theoretical grounding of LAD design in SCT, SRL, constructivism, social cognitive theory, and transformative learning

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues LAD design should be anchored in established educational theoretical frameworks, identifying that very few LAD designs articulate their grounding. It organizes relevant theory around social comparison theory (internal/external reference frames), self-regulated learning (goal-setting, monitoring, strategy adjustment), constructivism, social cognitive theory (self-efficacy, vicarious learning), and transformative learning (critical self-reflection triggered by predictive and prescriptive outputs). The authors state "a key contribution of this paper is the rich grounding of LAD development within existing educational theory frameworks."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dashboard designs should articulate how their features link to established educational frameworks
#### Constraints
- Jivet et al. (2018) note mixed results in using peer comparisons: for low-performing students the social comparison frame was perceived as stressful, while high-performing students were motivated
