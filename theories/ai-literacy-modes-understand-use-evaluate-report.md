# AI Literacy Framework's three Modes of Engagement: Understand, Use, Evaluate

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report presents Digital Promise's AI Literacy Framework as three interconnected Modes of Engagement supporting educators and students. It states the framework 'establishes three Modes of Engagement to support educators and students engaging in AI literacy': Understand (acquiring basic knowledge of what AI can do and how it works), Use (interacting, creating, and problem-solving with AI across contexts), and Evaluate (centering human judgment and justice to critically consider benefits and costs of AI). The report argues hands-on experience with both cloud-based and locally-run LLMs helps students move through these modes rather than remaining passive users.
