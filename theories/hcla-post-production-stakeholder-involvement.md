# Human-centred learning analytics (HCLA) with active stakeholder involvement in the post-production phase

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
HCLA is an approach emphasizing human factors in LA by including stakeholders in designing, developing, and evaluating the LA process. The article identifies a gap: stakeholder involvement is usually limited to design phases, while implementation of high-fidelity indicators is left to developers. It proposes involving students and teachers in the post-production phase by "giving them agency in implementing LA indicators that can meet their real-world educational needs and preferences," ensuring system flexibility and adaptability after deployment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- HCI approaches such as design thinking, HCD, participatory design, co-design, and value-sensitive design brought into LA
#### Constraints
- Prior HCLA dashboards mostly provide manually pre-configured indicators that lack adaptability to new end-user requirements after deployment

### Target Learners
- students
- teachers

### Target Learning Objectives
- aligning LA systems with educational goals after deployment
