Social network analysis as a methodological approach to measuring and mapping relationships¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief presents social network analysis as a methodological approach for program evaluation. It defines SNA as 'a methodological approach to measuring and mapping relationships' and specifies two units of analysis: 'whole networks, all of the ties within a defined group, or connections that individuals have in their personal communities.' The framework organizes how evaluators can map relationship structures to study programs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires data on relationships (ties) that can be measured and mapped, either within a defined group or around individuals in their personal communities.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Program evaluators studying educational or health programs

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Understanding and applying social network analysis concepts in program evaluation

Related Theories¶

Examples¶

  • Use SNA in both process and outcome evaluations of programs
  • Use SNA at the whole-network level to study all ties within a defined group
