Competency-based education defined by variable-time mastery of measurable competencies¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief defines competency-based education as an approach whose "key feature is that students are to master clearly defined and measurable learning outcomes (the required 'competencies'), but the time a student takes to demonstrate each competency may vary." It notes there is no single authoritative definition and positions CBE against traditional fixed-time course models in which outcomes vary but time is constant.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Clearly defined and measurable learning outcomes (competencies) must exist for each student to demonstrate

Constraints¶

  • The brief states there is no single, authoritative definition of competency-based education

Target Learners¶

  • Community college students in TAACCCT-funded programs

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Mastery of defined competencies in information technology programs

Claims¶

Related Theories¶

  • Competency-based education measures learning rather than class time, letting students progress at a flexible pace upon demonstrating mastery
  • Competency-based education: students advance through material independently upon demonstrating content mastery

Examples¶

  • TAACCCT consortium implementing flexibly paced CBE programs in information technology
  • Use external consulting and technical assistance to help colleges adapt programs to time-variant CBE models
  • Mastery Learning
  • Competency Based Learning
  • Competency Based Progression
  • Mastery Based Progression
  • Promote student agency by letting students decide the pace of their work and whether to complete it independently or collaboratively
