# External conceptual resources as cross-context frameworks, tools, and concepts in teacher learning ecologies

## Description
The article defines external conceptual resources as "frameworks, tools, and concepts that transcend different teacher learning environments, which are external to the local school context and echo voices external to the local team." Examples include teaching practices from workshops, curricula from previous schools, and strategies from conferences. Attending to these resources offers a method to study teacher conversations as embedded in broader learning ecologies rather than only the immediate setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Analysis must trace references in conversation back to settings outside the local team and school
#### Constraints
- Current research on teacher collaborative discourse rarely attends to broader contexts, focusing primarily on the immediate setting or intervention, per Lefstein et al. (2019) as cited by the authors

### Target Learners
- secondary mathematics teachers in school-based teams

### Target Learning Objectives
- collaborative sensemaking about instruction across learning contexts
