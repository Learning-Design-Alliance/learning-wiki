# Merit awards as labor-market signals of principal effectiveness

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
The article advances a signaling account of merit awards: although awards are intended to induce effort, they may also convey new information to outside employers. As the article states, a merit award system "may also produce unintended consequences if it provides new information to the labor market that enables outside employers to differentiate workers more accurately by their effectiveness." The observed exit pattern of award winners is read as consistent with this account.
