# Adaptive expertise in teaching: adaptivity as a key characteristic of effective teaching, with identifiable core practices

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article works within a framework in which being adaptive is central to effective teaching: "While being adaptive is a key characteristic of effective teaching, teachers can be resistant to change." The framework identifies "core practices of adaptive experts" that teachers can enact, and distinguishes ordinary adaptation from "transformative teaching practices." The study uses extreme change in teaching conditions as a window on how teachers exercise and develop this adaptivity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Investigating adaptive expertise requires observing teacher responses to changed teaching conditions, as the study did with the sudden shift to distance learning
#### Constraints
- The framework is applied here to K-8 teachers in the U.S. during the Spring 2020 pandemic transition to distance learning

### Target Learners
- K-8 teachers

### Target Learning Objectives
- Teacher professional adaptation and transfer of distance-teaching learning to future in-person instruction
