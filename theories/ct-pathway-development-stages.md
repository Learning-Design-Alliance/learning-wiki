# Three-stage model of developing district computational thinking pathways: Getting Started, Getting Going, Getting Better

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The presentation proposes a three-stage developmental model for how a district CT pathway matures. In Getting Started, the "District has CT activity and local expertise, but little consistency, cumulativity, etc."; in Getting Going there is "wide buy-in to a progression that is cumulative, consistent, competency-based"; in Getting Better there is "wide implementation with metrics" and the district "knows where improvement is needed next." Each stage is paired with verbs such as Informing, Exploring, Committing, Documenting, Communicating, Leading, Implementing, Improving, and Seeing results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District-wide buy-in and a cumulative, consistent, competency-based progression are needed to move beyond the Getting Started stage
#### Constraints
- The model describes district-level pathway development over multiple school years (SY19-20 through SY21-22), not single-classroom adoption

### Target Learners
- K-8 students in rural districts

### Target Learning Objectives
- computational thinking
- computer science
- coding
