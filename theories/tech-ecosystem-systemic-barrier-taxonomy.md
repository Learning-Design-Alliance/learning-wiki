# Systemic and economic barrier taxonomy across the technology learning and workforce ecosystem

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 5 studies (3 review, 1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The scan organizes barriers to Black learners' and workers' technology career entry and advancement into six ecosystem layers: educational and training opportunities, social capital access, financial capital access and economic mobility, career entry, career advancement, and career retention supports (Table 1), with a second axis of compounding intersectional barriers (Table 2). It states these barriers "are particularly persistent and compounding for intersecting identities of Black learners and workers." The taxonomy underpins the report's three strategic areas of action for employers, educators, and policymakers.
