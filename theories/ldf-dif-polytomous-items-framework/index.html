Logistic discriminant function analysis for DIF detection in polytomously scored items¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

LDF is a DIF-detection method, drawn from Miller and Spray (1993), in which the probability of group membership is modeled from total score plus item score, and "the response variable, U, need not be restricted to only two categories...but can take on any one of the J values associated with each item." Nonuniform DIF is tested against a hierarchical model and uniform DIF against a null probability model, with Scheffe simultaneous confidence bands. It suits performance assessments whose items are scored on ordinal scales with more than two categories.

Requirements¶

  • Requires the discriminant-analysis assumption that variables and dispersion and covariance matrices are normally distributed, to which the technique is quite sensitive

Constraints¶

  • The article applies it to one state-level science performance assessment; generalization beyond that setting is not established

Target Learners¶

  • examinees of a state-level performance-based science assessment (approximately 16,000 students)

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • accurate detection of differential item functioning in polytomously scored performance assessment items
