Whole School Safety (WSS) Framework¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Whole School Safety Framework is a district framework developed by Chicago Public Schools with community-based organizations that "defines school safety holistically with three pillars of 1) physical safety, 2) emotional safety, and 3) relational trust." Schools establish WSS committees of school community members to reflect on proactive and reactive measures supporting each pillar and develop WSS Plans. In this study the framework structured the choice of outcomes: the 5Essentials-based physical safety and Student-Teacher Trust measures were selected as the survey outcomes most closely related to its pillars.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools participating in SRO retention or reinvestment funding were required to assemble a WSS Committee and participate in WSS planning co-designed with five community-based organizations.

Constraints¶

  • The brief acknowledges the examined survey measures were not designed to be aligned with the framework's pillars and capture only portions of physical safety and relational trust.

Target Learners¶

  • CPS high school students in grades 9-12

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • School climate and safety conditions that support student learning and engagement
