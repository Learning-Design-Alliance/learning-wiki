Five constitutive dimensions of affordances: perceptibility, learning valence, compositionality, normativity, intentionality¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The paper draws on prior work identifying five constitutive dimensions that define an affordance's nature: perceptibility, learning valence, compositionality, normativity, and intentionality. These are described as essential conditions determining whether an affordance exists and exerts educational influence, not supplementary variables. Because these dimensions are too abstract to score directly, the paper treats them as theoretical scaffolding that the six assessment foci make observable in classroom interaction.
