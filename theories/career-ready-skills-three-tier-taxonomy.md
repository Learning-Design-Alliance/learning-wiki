# Career-ready skills comprise three tiers: academic skills, employability skills, and technical skills

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
Drawing on a Career Readiness Paper from ACTE (source URL printed on the slide), the presentation organizes career-ready skills into three numbered tiers: "Academic Skills" (reading, research, writing, mathematical calculations), "Employability Skills" (teamwork, communication, problem-solving, adaptability, professionalism), and "Technical Skills" (coding, data analysis, graphic design, financial modeling).
