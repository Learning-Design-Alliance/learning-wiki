# Simple View of Reading as the design basis for MAP Reading Fluency

## Description
The assessment's test design is explicitly grounded in the Simple View of Reading model (Gough & Tunmer, 1986), which the report describes as "a research-validated model of reading development that proposes that two broad factors enable or limit comprehension: decoding and language comprehension." Accordingly, MAP Reading Fluency assesses oral reading fluency plus foundational skills in both decoding (phonological awareness, phonics and word recognition) and language comprehension (picture vocabulary, listening comprehension). The report notes that when decoding is weak, even a student with excellent oral language comprehension cannot fully comprehend the text.
