# Four-type taxonomy of semantic relations in grapho-phonemically related Polish-English word pairs

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
The article organizes formally similar English/Polish word pairs by their semantic relation, following Lyons's distributional types. The system comprises equivalence (E alphabet : P alfabet), inclusion in either direction (E fiction : P fikcja; E protocol : P protokol), overlapping (E platform : P platforma), and contrast (E lecture : P lektura). The taxonomy structures the paper's analysis of lexical interference risk for translators and learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pairs must show grapho-phonemic similarity of stems; phraseological pairs without formal similarity are explicitly excluded from the classification
#### Constraints
- Equivalence is stated to be 'in the majority of cases only relative', and the classification is confined to formally related pairs

### Target Learners
- Advanced learners of English who are native speakers of Polish
- Translators working between Polish and English

### Target Learning Objectives
- Avoiding lexical interference errors in second-language vocabulary use and translation
