## Description
Digital Promise's AI Literacy Framework defines AI literacy as "the knowledge and skills that enable humans to critically understand, use, and evaluate AI systems and tools to safely and ethically participate in an increasingly digital world." The presentation positions AI literacy as the foundation for equity and ethics decisions about AI integration, alongside educator-facing supports such as a glossary of AI terms for educators and classroom-ready resources.
