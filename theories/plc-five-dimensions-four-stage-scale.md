# Professional Learning Communities defined by five dimensions with a four-stage development scale

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article adopts the PLC construct as "a group of teachers who share a common understanding of education, reflect on their work, and exchange their practices to improve student learning", organized into five dimensions: "shared leadership (D1), shared vision and values (D2), collective learning (D3), shared personal practice (D4), and supportive conditions (D5)". Development is assessed on a four-stage scale adapted from Bolívar (2017): not initiated, initiated, implemented, and institutionalized. The study operationalizes the dimensions with five validated Likert questionnaire items (Cronbach's Alpha 0.86) to gauge teams' degree of evolution as PLCs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questionnaire items adapted from Bolívar (2017) validated by 12 experts with Aiken's V > 0.70 for all questions
#### Constraints
- The institutionalized stage is defined for school culture; the article applies the scale to university teams

### Target Learners
- University academic community members (faculty, students, alumni, administrative staff)

### Target Learning Objectives
- Collaborative professional development and improvement of teaching practice through PLCs
