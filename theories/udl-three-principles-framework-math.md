# Universal Design for Learning framework: three principles for supporting learner variability

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The UDL framework, developed by CAST, is organized around three brain networks and their aligned principles. As the report states, "Those networks are aligned with three principles: provide multiple means of Engagement, Representation, and Action & Expression," with Guidelines and Checkpoints offering implementation suggestions. The report uses UDL as its conceptual framework, grounding its research questions, coding categories, and analysis of how digital math tools can support inclusive mathematics instruction. Each checkpoint is described as grounded in learning sciences research with a supporting bibliography.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation suggestions are organized into Guidelines with Checkpoints that offer suggestions for implementation
#### Constraints
- The report notes a limited body of research has brought UDL and mathematics together, and even fewer studies have considered technology's role in making mathematics accessible through UDL

### Target Learners
- K-12 mathematics students, including students with learning disabilities and students from historically and systematically excluded backgrounds

### Target Learning Objectives
- Accessible, inclusive mathematics learning; developing understanding of mathematical concepts through multiple representations
