# Thematic classification of creativity applications into creativity-based learning, problem-based learning, and differentiated instruction, plus method and approach categories

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article organizes creativity-related applications along two axes. First, studies were "thematized under three groups": creativity-based learning (CBL), problem-based learning (PBL), and differentiated instruction (DI), each meta-analyzed separately. Second, additional applications were "categorized as 'method' and 'approach'" and their effect sizes calculated separately: methods included drama-based learning, discovery learning, teaching with analogy, and cooperative learning, while approaches included project-based learning and constructivist learning environments. This taxonomy structures both the pooled analyses and the per-study effect-size comparisons.
