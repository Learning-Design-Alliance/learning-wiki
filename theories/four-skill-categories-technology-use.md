# Four skill categories for technology use: Digital Skills, Media and Meaning, Point of View, and Audience

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Toolkit organizes the skills students must master with technology into four categories. Digital Skills covers executing the mechanics of technology use; Media and Meaning covers conveying ideas in technology's various forms and choosing the best form for a message; Point of View covers media decisions "directed by the opinions she intends to express in her work"; and Audience covers choosing media that will most effectively convey a message to particular audiences. This taxonomy is the organizing axis of every Toolkit component.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each category is defined relative to specific forms of student work with technology, such as live presentations or web pages
#### Constraints
- The categories are applied to the five forms of classroom technology use the Toolkit currently addresses

### Target Learners
- K-12 students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Technology fluency, media literacy, and communication with digital media
