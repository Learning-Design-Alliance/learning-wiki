Six-dimension construct map for measuring ELA and math curriculum-shift implementation¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article presents a construct map that organizes measurement of curriculum shifts into six dimensions: support for instructional leaders, instructional leadership, teacher professional learning, teacher mindsets and beliefs, teacher instructional practice, and student outcomes. Each dimension carries a construct statement, such as "Instructional leaders build routines, skills, and beliefs to support CBPL implementation (Construct)", and lettered sub-constructs. The map is intended for measurement and progress monitoring of CBPL and HQIM implementation in ELA and math.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Measurement and progress monitoring use requires scoring each dimension through its lettered sub-constructs as printed in the map

Constraints¶

  • The map is scoped to ELA and math curriculum shifts, with a key noting "Items specific to ELA and Math"

Target Learners¶

  • instructional leaders
  • teachers
  • K-12 students in ELA and math

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • implementing and adapting high-quality instructional materials (HQIM) through curriculum-based professional learning (CBPL)
