# Challenge-Based Learning is a framework for learning while solving real-world challenges through three phases: Engage, Investigate, and Act

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The presentation defines Challenge-Based Learning as "A framework for learning while solving real-world challenges" that "Takes participants through three phases: Engage, Investigate, and Act." It positions CBL as fostering global competence and employability skills such as leadership and collaboration, and as promoting teachers as designers and facilitators rather than transmitters of content.
