Inclusive Innovation: a co-research and co-design R&D framework enabling co-leadership among students, teachers, parents, community members, and district leaders¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Inclusive Innovation is a paradigm for education R&D in which district-community Core Teams of 7-10 participants (district leaders, teachers, parents, community members, students) share leadership. The report states it is "a paradigm shift in research and development (R&D), enabling co-leadership among students, teachers, parents, community members, and district leaders." Teams progress through Inquire and Investigate, Design and Develop, and Implement and Iterate stages, formulating problem statements, defining outcomes, co-designing solution prototypes, and assessing effectiveness through local knowledge and lived experience. Core Tenets center students and families from historically and systematically excluded communities throughout the process.
