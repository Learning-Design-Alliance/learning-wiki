# Differentiated knowledge measures framework: static/dynamic, modality, and quality categories

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that multimedia learning effects should be evaluated with knowledge measures differentiated along three categories. As printed: "We defined three knowledge categories: static/dynamic (process or facts knowledge), modality (verbal or visual knowledge), and quality (passive or active knowledge)." Static/dynamic distinguishes facts (objects, events, symbols) from processes (flows of interdependent causalities); modality derives from dual coding theory's separate verbal and visual channels; quality contrasts active (production/reconstruction) with passive (recognition) knowledge. The framework let the study show that media effects differ by knowledge category rather than being uniform.

### Target Learning Objectives
