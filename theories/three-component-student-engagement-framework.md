# Three-component model of student engagement: behavioral, emotional, and cognitive

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The synthesis organizes student engagement as three interrelated components. Behavioral engagement is doing academic work such as attending class and completing assignments; emotional engagement covers interest, enjoyment, or anxiety about material and feelings about teachers and school; cognitive engagement is actively working to master new skills. The review states that "Engagement has behavioral, emotional, and cognitive components" and that "In practice, all of these are interrelated," with only behavioral engagement observable by others, making it a crucial signal educators must attend to.
