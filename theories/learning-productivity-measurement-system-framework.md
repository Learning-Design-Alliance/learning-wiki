# The learning productivity measurement system as a framework for using student and school descriptive indicators of learning progress

## Description
The article presents a measurement framework that moves from the conditional growth index toward a learning productivity measurement system for describing learning progress. Its title frames this as "From the conditional growth index to the learning productivity measurement system", indicating a system that organizes student and school descriptive indicators of learning progress into a coherent measurement approach for interpreting growth over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires student achievement score data across grades from which growth indicators such as the conditional growth index can be computed
#### Constraints
- The framework operates in the context of growth modeling and value-added modeling under accountability policies such as NCLB and RTTP

### Target Learners
- K-12 students whose learning progress is tracked with standardized achievement assessments

### Target Learning Objectives
- Describing and interpreting student learning progress and growth over time
