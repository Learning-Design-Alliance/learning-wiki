Framework for understanding the critical properties of data that matter for student achievement¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a framework offered by Elaine Allensworth for understanding "the critical properties of data" and the tools educators need to focus on the metrics most relevant to student achievement. It is positioned as bridging theory and practice, rooted in two decades of research on educational policy and practice and real-world success stories from Chicago Public Schools, and is intended to help educators build a culture of data-driven collaboration.
