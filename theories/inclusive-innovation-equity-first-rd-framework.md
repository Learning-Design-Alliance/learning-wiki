# Inclusive Innovation: an equity-first education R&D framework that reimagines authority, decision-making, and risk

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Inclusive Innovation is an overarching framework for education R&D grounded in what the authors call a radical commitment to equity. It reimagines authority, decision-making, and risk so that people closest to the challenge lead, participate in, and benefit from innovation. The paper states it provides an overarching framework for authentically engaging underrepresented stakeholders at the earliest stages and shifting their roles to leaders, participants, and beneficiaries, placing them at the center of the innovation ecosystem rather than merely inviting them in.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Infrastructure and processes designed not only to invite, but to prioritize, community voices and perspectives
- Intentionally built partnerships and relationships with community organizations providing direct touchpoints to Black, Latinx and high-poverty populations
#### Constraints
- The authors state the model is grounded in experience from adjacent fields and that opportunities to test the process in whole or in part in education would result in a refined approach

### Target Learners
- Black, Latinx, and high-poverty students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Equitable solutions to major educational challenges
- Increased capacity of practitioners and community members to engage with research and evidence
