# Model of Essential Supports for Student Learning

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
The Model of Essential Supports for Student Learning identifies seven areas of school organization and practice that support ambitious academic learning: school leadership, teacher professional community, parent and community support, student-centered learning climate, quality instruction, social trust, and instructional program coherence. The framework emerged from a stakeholder consultation process begun in 1994 and was developed through Consortium research. It groups the supports into organizational capacities, organizational practices, and two overarching supports, and holds that the supports "function in organizational patterns" rather than independently. The model became the template for annual school improvement planning in Chicago public schools and frames this study of Annenberg school development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must be assessed on all seven supports, which operate systemically; change in one support likely promotes change in others.
#### Constraints
- Empirical validation to date uses gains on the ITBS as the achievement measure, though the model is also theoretically consistent with more rigorous and authentic intellectual achievement.

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in Chicago public schools

### Target Learning Objectives
- Ambitious academic learning that goes beyond basic knowledge and skills to deep understanding of subject matter
