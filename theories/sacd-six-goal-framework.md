# SACD six-goal framework for social and character development programming

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SACD Research Program organizes school-based social and character development around six specific goals promoted through SACD activities. The report defines SACD goals as "six specific goals defined by the SACD Research Program and promoted through the use of SACD activities to support students' social and character development," spanning violence prevention, social-emotional development, character education, tolerance, health promotion, and civic responsibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs and activities must be mapped to one or more of the six SACD goals to count as social and character development programming in this framework
