# Working definition of breakthrough innovation in education

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article defines breakthrough innovation in education as "a highly novel advance that fundamentally redefines an entrenched problem or its opportunity space, enables significant improvements in learning outcomes, meaningfully expands participation and access, and/or propels system-level change." It builds on social innovation—the development of novel solutions to social problems—by adding solutions that break through prior limitations and alter how problems are understood, addressed, and measured. The definition frames the ATS Hub's mission of developing use-inspired, evidence-based products that harness emerging technologies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Innovations must be highly novel and fundamentally redefine an entrenched problem or opportunity space, not merely incrementally improve practice
#### Constraints
- Education systems are described as structurally resistant to change, shaped by deeply embedded norms, routines, and institutional infrastructure

### Target Learners
- All learners across education systems targeted by ATS-style innovation efforts

### Target Learning Objectives
- Significant improvements in learning outcomes
- Expanded participation and access
- System-level change
