What Works Clearinghouse evidence-based standards for reviewing transition program research¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review applies a structured evidence-rating framework in which programs receive categorical effectiveness ratings such as mixed effects and potentially positive effects for specific outcome domains like employment, postsecondary education, and independent living. The article states it "uses evidence-based standards developed by the What Works Clearinghouse to review the research literature on programs that help students with disabilities make transitions after high school." The framework organizes findings by program type and outcome domain rather than by single pooled estimates.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Research literature on transition programs must be screened and rated against What Works Clearinghouse evidence standards

Constraints¶

  • Ratings are categorical (e.g., potentially positive, mixed) and, as printed here, are not accompanied by pooled effect sizes

  • transition-age students with disabilities
