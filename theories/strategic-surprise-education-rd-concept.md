# Strategic surprise as an organizing concept for education R&D

## Description
The paper defines strategic surprise as an exogenous or emergent shift that outruns existing sensing and response cycles, forcing rapid reorientation. It names generative AI, the shift from data scarcity to data abundance, and the move from cottage-industry to infrastructure-based research as current surprises. The concept frames the paper's driving question: what would an R&D system look like if organized primarily for detecting, absorbing, and generating such surprises rather than only for rigor and administrative efficiency.
