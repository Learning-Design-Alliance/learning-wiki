# Five essential supports for student learning framework

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report organizes all survey measures around five essential supports for student learning, initially presented in the CPS Pathways to Achievement self-analysis guide and included in the SIPAAA planning process. The supports are School Leadership; Parent and Community Partnerships; Student-Centered Learning Climate; Professional Development and Collaboration; and Quality Instructional Program. The report states that "Many of the questions in the teacher and student surveys are directly relevant to the five essential supports for student learning" and that "The survey results are organized according to the five essential supports." Each support is operationalized as multiple survey scales, with student academic and social attitudes reported separately as schooling outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Survey measures must map onto the five supports so schools can use results in the SIPAAA self-analysis and improvement planning process.
#### Constraints
- The framework is applied to Chicago Public Schools' improvement planning context as printed in the report.

### Target Learners
- Chicago Public Schools students in grades 6, 8, and 10

### Target Learning Objectives
- School improvement planning for advancing academic achievement
