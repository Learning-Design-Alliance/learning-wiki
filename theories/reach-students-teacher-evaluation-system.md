# REACH Students teacher evaluation system combining professional practice and student growth measures

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 12 claims (12 for) · 4 studies (4 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 12 claims rest on one study

## Description
REACH (Recognizing Educators Advancing Chicago's Students) is CPS's teacher evaluation system instituted in fall 2012, replacing a 1970s-era checklist system. It "incorporates a professional practice score and up to two measures of student growth in a teacher's evaluation score." Professional practice is evaluated through four observations using the CPS Framework for Teaching, a modified version of the Danielson Framework, with 19 components across four domains. Student growth includes value-added scores and performance tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ratings from four observations conducted by principals or assistant principals who have been trained and certified on the rubric
- Value-added scores require teachers in tested subjects and grades; most non-tested teachers receive a school-wide average value-added score in literacy
#### Constraints
- Charter schools do not utilize REACH and are not included in analyses
- Librarians, counselors, clinicians, and other education support specialists rated on a different framework were not included in observation analyses
- In 2013-14, observations comprised 75 percent of a teacher's overall REACH score

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Objectives
- Improving instructional practice through focused feedback
- Identifying teachers who excelled or needed improvement
