# Four-category taxonomy of state EL reclassification policy structures by kind and number of test-based criteria

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief organizes state EL reclassification policies into four groups arranged in ascending complexity: single ELP criterion, multiple ELP criteria, optional non-ELP criteria, and mandated non-ELP criteria. It states: "we identify four distinct groups arranged in ascending order of reclassification policy complexity according to the kind of reclassification requirements". The taxonomy is built from a database of state policy documents collected as of December 2023, distinguishing ELP assessments from academic content evaluations and whether non-ELP requirements are mandated or optional.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Policy documents from each state's Department of Education website are needed to classify states into the four structures
#### Constraints
- The District of Columbia is counted as a state in the classification

### Target Learners
- K-12 English Learner students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Attaining English proficiency and exiting EL status through reclassification
