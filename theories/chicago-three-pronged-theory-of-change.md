# Chicago's three-pronged theory of change: classroom instruction, schools as the unit of change, and differentiated district support

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 against) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The district's reform strategy rests on a three-tenet theory of change. The first tenet holds that "improved student learning requires improved instruction" at the classroom level. The second recognizes that "schools are the unit of change for instructional improvement and principals are the leaders of that change." The third holds that area and central offices provide critical support for instructional improvement and differentiate that support based on school performance and need. CPS operationalized this theory through a portfolio approach offering a menu of 144 high schools with different features and programs, plus varying levels of autonomy and support by school performance and capacity.
