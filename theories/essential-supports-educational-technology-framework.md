# Essential Supports for Educational Technology framework

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article organizes the conditions for school technology use into a framework of interacting supports: student-side factors (technology availability to students, home use/background, belief in technology) and staff-side factors (human resources, support for technology, technology availability to teachers, home use/background, teacher professional use, teacher assignment, belief in technology, professional development, professional community, and principal leadership). It states that "Many distinct components are necessary to make technology a regular part of students' academic lives," with adequate resources and teacher capacity the two most important.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must secure funding not only for equipment and software but also for technical assistance and upgrades
#### Constraints
- A one-time infusion of computers or a few professional development seminars on basic use is insufficient

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in urban public school districts

### Target Learning Objectives
- Integration of computers and the Internet into instruction across subjects
