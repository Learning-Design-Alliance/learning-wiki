# Convergent science: integrating learning sciences and computer science expertise

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report defines convergent science as "an emerging approach to research that integrates different types of expertise and findings from multiple disciplines to address problems," with the learning sciences and computer science as two key disciplines researchers synthesize. Learning scientists develop theories of learning and methods that guide design and research, while computer scientists develop perspectives, methods, and tools that spur technological innovation. The report claims this orientation allows cyberlearning research to tackle deep, complex problems, connect scholars across fields, and seek impact on practices, policies, and products through partnerships.
