# The Five Essential Supports framework organizes school development measures

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The UChicago Consortium groups roughly 25 survey composite measures of school development under five essential supports for student learning, which Consortium research identified as School Leadership, Parent and Community Partnerships, Student-Centered Learning Climate, Professional Capacity, and Quality Instructional Program. The framework is operationalized through a biennial survey of teachers and students, and the report presents 2003 snapshots and 1994-2003 trends for each measure, with endorsement "ranging from weak to strong."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Biennial survey administration to teachers and sixth- through tenth-grade students, with responses aggregated into composite measures
#### Constraints
- Measures cover only what teachers and students report about Chicago public schools; technology measures are tracked only from 2001

### Target Learners
- Chicago public school students in grades 6 through 10

### Target Learning Objectives
- School climate and conditions supporting student learning
