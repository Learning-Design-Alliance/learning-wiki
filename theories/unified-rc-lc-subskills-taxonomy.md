# Unified taxonomy of subskills shared between and exclusive to reading and listening comprehension

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article prepares a taxonomy that, for the first time in its account, lists reading and listening subskills in a unified fashion, distinguishing shared from modality-exclusive subskills. The common list includes "Understanding a main idea and general information", "Inferring indirect information from the context", and "Transferring information to pictures, maps, tables, or diagrams". Reading-exclusive items include matching headings and inserting sentences into gaps; listening-exclusive items include perceiving individual sounds and predicting the continuation of a message. The taxonomy operationalizes subskills drawn from theory, tests, and coursebooks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Subskills were compiled from theoretical and empirical applied linguistics papers, nine proficiency test manuals, and reading/listening tasks in five coursebook series, with repetitions eliminated and lists cross-checked by two experienced EFL teachers.
#### Constraints
- The article states that subskills in diagnostic tests could have been investigated as well, and that follow-up work testing the subskills is still needed.

### Target Learners
- EFL/ESL learners assessed with international proficiency tests or taught with widely used coursebooks

### Target Learning Objectives
- Reading comprehension and listening comprehension subskills in a second language
