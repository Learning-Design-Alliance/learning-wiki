# Holistic paradigm: underachievement as a multi-factor, multi-level phenomenon

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes the Holistic paradigm as a third approach that, despite difficulties of application, "seems to account for most of the deficiencies of the other two models". It treats underachievement as arising from interactions among people and institutions rather than from any single vertex, and the review organizes recent studies under it using Pianta and Walsh's Contextual Systems Model. The review concludes the field still lacks "a theoretical treatment of the evolution of the mathematical discourse" and its social, cultural, and political relations.
