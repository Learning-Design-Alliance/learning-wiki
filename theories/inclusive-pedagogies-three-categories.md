# Inclusive pedagogies taxonomy: accessible instruction, connecting to homes and communities, and combating inequity

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report organizes inclusive computing pedagogy into three categories. As printed, "The pedagogies are divided into three categories to emphasize different pedagogical approaches to inclusivity." Designing Accessible Instruction covers strategies such as Universal Design for Learning, pair programming, POGIL, explicit expectations, normalizing mistakes, and student choice of computational artifacts. Connecting to Students' Interests, Homes, and Communities covers drawing on student experiences, family perspectives, translanguaging, and community showcase. Acknowledging and Combating Inequity covers addressing power dynamics, recruiting marginalized students, challenging stereotypes, teaching about algorithmic bias, and creating artifacts that counter inequities. The taxonomy adapts prior frameworks from Israel et al., the Kapor Center, Madkins et al., and NCWIT.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators should attend to each of the three approaches as they plan and teach lessons, especially related to computing
#### Constraints
- The practices listed are those most relevant to environments in which computing is occurring, whether standalone CS or integrated CT lessons

### Target Learners
