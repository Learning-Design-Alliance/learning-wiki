# Five Essential Supports framework for organizing school improvement conditions

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report organizes all school survey profiles around five domains: school leadership, parent and community partnerships, student-centered learning climate, professional capacity, and quality of the instructional program. These five domains are frequently referred to as the Essential Supports for Student Learning, and the report states that schools strong in these supports are more likely to improve academically while weak schools are more likely to be non-improving. It emphasizes that it is the systemic blending of initiatives that makes a material difference in student learning, so profiles should be read in relation to one another.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Survey measures across all five domains must be collected so a school's profile on each essential support can be considered in relation to its performance on others
#### Constraints
- The framework is grounded in Consortium studies of Chicago public schools and is used to structure school self-assessment and SIPAAA planning

### Target Learners
- Chicago public school students in elementary and high schools

### Target Learning Objectives
- Student achievement improvement through school organizational conditions
