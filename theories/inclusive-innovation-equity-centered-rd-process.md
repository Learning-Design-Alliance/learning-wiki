# Inclusive Innovation: an equity-centered R&D process with five phases and six Core Tenets

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Inclusive Innovation is an equity-centered research and development process in which "district-community equity challenges are co-researched and innovative equity-centered solutions centering the needs of HSE students are co-designed in partnership with district and community stakeholders." Districts and communities work on Core Teams that co-construct an inclusive team culture enabling power-sharing and equitable decision-making. The process moves through five phases — Connect and Commit, Inquire and Investigate, Design and Develop, Implement and Iterate, and Sustain and Scale — and is anchored by six Core Tenets (Collective Leadership; Student Voice and Leadership; Context Expertise and Proximity; Continuity of Equity; Reimagine Progress; Build Capacity). The RCS project used this process to develop Socratic Circles OER.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A district-community Core Team reflecting diverse role types (district leaders, teachers, parents, community members, students) with co-leads guiding research and design activities
#### Constraints
- The report notes the contentious sociopolitical climate surrounding racial discourse in schools magnified over the project and introduced complexity into the process

### Target Learners
- Historically and systematically excluded K-12 students
- Teachers in racially diverse districts

### Target Learning Objectives
- Supporting teachers to facilitate classroom racial equity discourse
- Equity-centered co-research and co-design capacity in district-community teams
