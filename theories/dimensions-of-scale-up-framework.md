# Framework of the dimensions of scale-up explaining why effect sizes might decrease as scale increases

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
The article develops a framework of the dimensions of scale-up, an explanatory account of why intervention effect sizes might shrink as interventions move from small studies to broader adoption. The authors state: "we develop a framework of the dimensions of scale-up to explain why effect sizes might decrease as scale increases." They then use this framework to organize a comparison of causal estimates from the present state-level study with those from earlier, smaller studies of Pre-K Mathematics, and report the observed decline as consistent with it.
