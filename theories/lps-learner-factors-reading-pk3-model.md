# LPS Learner Factors model for Reading PK-3

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Learner Positioning Systems (LPS) initiative at Digital Promise Global distills existing research on cognitive, social and emotional, content area, and background Learner Factors that affect learning in domains such as reading and math. Its first Learner Model, Reading PK-3 (Sheppard, 2017), organizes factors including Inhibition, Working Memory, Decoding, Primary Language, Emotion and Motivation, Vision, and Attention, and is used to review edtech products and recommend evidence-based features that support learner variability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers must translate learning sciences work into actionable recommendations for educators, parents, and edtech product developers
#### Constraints
- The report notes over 70 percent of the most popular literacy apps make no reference to learning sciences research, so models must be actively translated into products

### Target Learners
- Pre-kindergarten through third grade students with varying needs and backgrounds

### Target Learning Objectives
- Reading comprehension, decoding, and motivation in early literacy
