# Hanging out, messing around, and geeking out: a three-level participation framework instantiated in YOUmedia's space design

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report organizes teen digital media participation along a continuum identified through Mimi Ito et al.'s ethnographic research: "hanging out" is primarily social and friendship driven, "messing around" is where interest takes more concrete form as skills develop, and "geeking out" is the advanced level where teens develop specialized expertise. YOUmedia's 5,500-square-foot physical space was designed as an instantiation of these three forms, with lounging furniture and social gaming for hanging out, production kiosks and a recording studio for messing around, and a serious work space with conference tables and a SMART board for geeking out.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A physical or online environment must offer distinct zones or affordances matching each participation level so teens can socialize, explore, or master increasingly complex skills.
#### Constraints
- The framework was identified through Ito et al.'s three-year ethnographic project across 23 field sites; the report applies it descriptively rather than testing it.

### Target Learners
- High school-aged teens

### Target Learning Objectives
- Digital media literacy
- Interest-driven skill development
- Creative production
