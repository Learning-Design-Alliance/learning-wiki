OpenSciEd research logic model linking inputs, three ecosystem levels, and four outcome categories¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper's central framework is a logic model that "links two types of inputs, three levels of the U.S. educational ecosystem that are targeted by OpenSciEd intervention, and four broad categories of desired outcomes (system, teacher capacity, students, and resources and innovations)." It details the rationale for expected impact based on learning sciences principles and design features, and clarifies intended relationships among OpenSciEd principles, components and implementation, and desired outcomes. These relationships outline hypotheses to be tested in research studies, and the model highlights knowledge gaps for the field.
