# Freshman and Sophomore Success Categories: a four-level indicator taxonomy for monitoring students

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report proposes parallel four-category systems for incoming sophomores (Freshman Success Categories) and for monitoring during sophomore year (Sophomore Success Categories). Each system layers GPA, course failures, and attendance onto on-track status: "This system contains four Freshman Success Categories: 1) Off-Track, 2) On-Track Warning, 3) On-Track Below 3.0, and 4) On-Track Above 3.0." The categories identify distinct groups needing different supports, from credit recovery for off-track students to grade maintenance for students above 3.0 seeking selective post-secondary access.
