## Brief test

Old against new page, two briefs each (complete, sparse), answers by Kimi K3, graded by Gemini 3.8 Flash and
DeepSeek V4 Pro, blind pairs in both orders.

- **First round:** new won 75 of 120 pairs (complete 31–29, sparse 44–16); affordance score 5.9 → 9.4, accuracy
  3.2 → 3.6.
- **Rework.** Seven pages lost a brief. The causes were general, and the fixes went in as rules: run the brief's own
  assessment or rubric as given (a rubric that scores tool use keeps that criterion, with an unaided check beside
  it); fit a single or short session (one product revised once, minutes that add up, pairs that fit the count); add
  no test the brief did not ask for; use the learners' observed error as a compared case; concrete phone-only
  accommodations; name race when talk slides to euphemism, and tell discomfort from harm.
- **Second round (the seven):** new won 33 of 56 (complete 15–13, sparse 18–10). Still not won: discussing race
  (both briefs), rhetorical skill (sparse), technology (complete); internet search and rhetorical skill (complete)
  tie. The judges' reasons are about one answer's execution (a four-minute breakout, a misattributed finding) or a
  preference for one simple session routine over a staged one; no further rework.
- DeepSeek picked the first-shown answer 26 of 28 times in round two (50 of 60 in round one), so with both orders
  its votes cancel and Gemini decides most briefs.
