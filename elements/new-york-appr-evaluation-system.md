## Description
The Annual Professional Performance Review system (APPR) was announced in 2012 by New York education officials and the state's teachers union, described as "one of the nation's most aggressive programs for teacher and principal evaluation." It required that 40% of an educator's evaluation be based on student growth, using a combination of the state assessment and locally selected assessments, with the other 60% based primarily on classroom observations.
