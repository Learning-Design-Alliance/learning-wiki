# Caring School Community™ program with four schoolwide components

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Caring School Community™ (formerly the Child Development Project) is a multiyear school improvement program for all students in grades K–6 that aims to promote core values, prosocial behavior, and a schoolwide feeling of community. The report states "The program consists of four elements originally developed for the Child Development Project: class meeting lessons, cross-age “buddies” programs, “homeside” activities, and schoolwide community." Class meeting lessons use scripted sessions to teach values such as fairness, caring, and respect; buddies activities pair older and younger classes; homeside activities are conversational activities sent home to families; and schoolwide activities build new school traditions. All four components are designed to be introduced over one year, though schools may introduce them more gradually.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All four components are designed to be introduced over the course of one year, with teacher training via a one-day workshop
#### Constraints
- The revised CSC dropped two CDP elements (Developmental Discipline and the literature-based reading program) found too difficult for many teachers to implement

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in grades K–6

### Target Learning Goals
- Core values, prosocial behavior, interpersonal understanding, and a schoolwide feeling of community
