Spalding's The Writing Road to Reading: comprehensive K-6 speech-to-print literacy program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A comprehensive literacy program for K-6 students implemented as a whole-class program, typically daily during dedicated literacy blocks. It "integrates explicit, systematic, and multi-modal instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, spelling, writing, and reading comprehension" and follows "a speech-to-print approach, helping students connect spoken language with written text through structured, diagnostic teaching methods." Its structured format is intended to support differentiated instruction for diverse learners, including struggling readers and English learners.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Professional development for teachers in Spalding Method strategies, with teachers modeling skills, providing guided practice, and scaffolding student learning

Constraints¶

  • No specific technology requirements; core materials are printed manuals, phonogram cards, reference notebooks, and physical writing materials, with the coordinator needing access to assessment tracking systems

Target Learners¶

  • K-6 students
  • struggling readers
  • English learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • phonemic awareness
  • phonics
  • spelling
  • writing
  • reading comprehension

Claims¶

  • Students tracked from grades 2-4 into grades 3-5 in Spalding schools scored significantly higher on the AASA (effect size +0.39), reaching 48% versus 29% proficiency [+W]
  • Kindergarteners followed into 1st grade in Spalding schools showed higher FastBridge earlyReading gains (effect size +0.21) than matched comparison peers [+W]
  • Below-grade-level students in Spalding schools sustained significantly higher AASA performance through Spring 2025 (effect size +0.33), with twice the proficiency rate of comparison peers [+W]
  • Spalding's The Writing Road to Reading improves whole-class literacy achievement with an average effect size of +0.35 across one study of 1,915 students [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Designate a Spalding coordinator and provide 45-70 hours of formal teacher training with ongoing mentoring to support implementation fidelity
