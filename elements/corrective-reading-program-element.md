# Corrective Reading: scripted, structured lessons for upper-elementary struggling readers

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Corrective Reading is "a program for upper-elementary struggling readers that uses scripted, structured lessons focused on phonics, fluency, and comprehension". Explicit and systematic methods engage students in a rapid series of activities to build word-level skills, and oral reading of carefully constructed stories builds rate and fluency while countering word-guessing habits. It was evaluated in one qualifying study with 206 students.
