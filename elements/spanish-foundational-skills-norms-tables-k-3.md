# Spanish Foundational Skills achievement and growth norms tables (grades K-3)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Twelve snapshot tables (Tables 3.3-3.14) present achievement and growth norms for each domain in grades K-3, released in Fall 2026. Grey columns give percentile ranks from 5 to 95, blue columns give achievement norm scores per term, green columns give expected between-term growth and SDs for each percentile, and yellow and mixed-colored cells support normative evaluation of a student's actual gain between terms. Achievement norms cover fall, winter, and spring; growth norms cover fall-to-winter, winter-to-spring, and fall-to-spring growth.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users should refer to score reports for student-specific normative-referenced performance information, since the tables present only a select group of percentiles
#### Constraints
- Only a select group of percentiles (5 to 95 in increments of 5) is provided in the snapshots for ease of presentation

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-3 assessed with Spanish MAP Reading Fluency

### Target Learning Goals
- Norm-referenced evaluation of achievement and between-term growth in foundational reading skills
