## Description
The evaluation method used for this report: a quasi-experimental, matched comparison group design estimating the Kauffman School's impacts on student achievement, attendance, and suspensions. The report states, "This paper uses a quasi-experimental, matched comparison group design to estimate the impacts of the Kauffman School on student achievement, attendance, and suspensions."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A matched comparison group of comparable students to estimate impacts absent random assignment
#### Constraints
- Quasi-experimental rather than randomized; impacts are estimated relative to matched comparison schools

### Target Learners
- K-12 charter school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Estimating school-level impacts on achievement growth, attendance, and suspensions
