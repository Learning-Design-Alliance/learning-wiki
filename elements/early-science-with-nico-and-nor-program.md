# Early Science with Nico and Nor preschool science program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Early Science with Nico and Nor is a preschool science program "co-designed by teachers, families, curricula and media developers, and researchers." It "promotes early science learning through playful learning experiences inspired by children’s and families’ interests and every day experiences," links science with engineering and math, includes digital apps to support STEM learning, and includes a Family and Teacher guide that connect home and school learning. A Spanish version, Ciencia Preescolar con Nico and Nor, is also provided.
