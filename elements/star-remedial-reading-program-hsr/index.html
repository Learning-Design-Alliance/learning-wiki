STAR (Strategies and Techniques for Advancement in Reading) remedial reading program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

STAR is the intensive remedial reading program offered within High School Redirection schools "for students with serious literacy problems." It is part of the model's emphasis on basic skill development, with a particular focus on reading skills, and was listed among the key features that the Stockton, Wichita, and Cincinnati replication schools included as specified by the ASDP evaluation. The report does not describe STAR's internal instructional design beyond its role as the schools' remedial reading offering.
