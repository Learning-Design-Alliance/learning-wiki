Vocabulary Improvement Program (VIP) vocabulary development curriculum for grades 4-6¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

VIP is a "vocabulary development curriculum for English language learners and native English speakers (grades 4–6)". The 15-week program runs 30-45 minutes of whole-class and small-group activities four days a week, targeting 10-12 words per week drawn from a weekly reading assignment on immigration-related texts. Materials include detailed lesson plans, quasi-scripted lesson guides, overhead transparencies, worksheets, homework assignments, and reading texts; it was developed by Diane August, Maria S. Carlo, Barry McLaughlin, and Catherine Snow and published by Brookes Publishing.

Requirements¶

  • Requires teachers to deliver structured weekly lessons using the provided lesson plans, quasi-scripted lesson guides, transparencies, worksheets, and reading texts

  • The published Brookes version is an adaptation of the research version and is structured slightly differently

Target Learners¶

  • English language learners in grades 4-6
  • native English speaking classmates

Target Learning Goals¶

  • vocabulary knowledge and word learning
  • reading achievement
  • English language development

Claims¶

  • VIP effects did not differ between English language learners and fluent English speakers in the study authors' analyses [~M]
  • VIP shows no statistically significant or substantively important impacts on PPVT-R and Morphology outcomes for English language learners [-M]
  • VIP shows potentially positive effects on reading achievement for fifth-grade English language learners (effect size 0.50, not statistically significant) [+M]
  • VIP produces a statistically significant effect on Word Mastery (taught-word definition selection) for English language learners, effect size 1.03 [+M]
