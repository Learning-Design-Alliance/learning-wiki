# Pre-K Mathematics supplemental preschool program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (1 for, 4 mixed, 1 against) · 6 studies (5 quant-synthesis, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 6 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Pre-K Mathematics is a supplemental mathematics program for preschool children that develops informal mathematical knowledge through three components: a set of 24 in-class, small-group activities with manipulatives; a set of 24 linked caregiver-child home math activities with letters in English and Spanish; and optional mathematics software practice. The report describes "teacher-led, small-group mathematics activities that are engaging and hands-on, as well as caregiver-child activities that are linked to the classroom activities to support children's math learning at home."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers receive 4 days of professional development, ongoing in-class coaching during the initial year, and developer-provided materials including a curriculum binder and manipulatives kit
#### Constraints
- The mathematics software component was used in four of five studies and its impact cannot be separated from the curriculum's impact

### Target Learners
- preschool children in Head Start and state-funded preschool programs, predominantly from families with incomes below federal poverty guidelines

### Target Learning Goals
- informal mathematical knowledge including number sense, arithmetic operations, spatial sense and geometry, pattern knowledge, and measurement and data
