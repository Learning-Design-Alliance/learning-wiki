# Bridgit platform for tracking pre-matriculation tasks

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Bridgit is "a schoolwide intervention used by school counselors to track students' progress toward college enrollment". Students create profiles identifying their chosen college and admissions status; the system then prioritizes pre-matriculation steps tailored to that college. Counselors monitor prioritized task lists and communicate via text messages directly in the platform, with phone calls for follow-up. It was evaluated in a cluster randomized trial of six intervention and six comparison schools in Tennessee and Missouri (3,281 students).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 2-day training was conducted for counselors using the Bridgit system; eligible schools had at least 60% free/reduced-price lunch eligibility, at least 50% of graduating seniors submitting a FAFSA by June 2014, and at least 100 FAFSA completers
#### Constraints
- The evaluated effect on fall college enrollment was an effect size of 0.09 (+3 improvement index), not statistically significant; the WWC characterized the finding as indeterminate

### Target Learners
- College-intending high school graduates in high-poverty urban school districts

### Target Learning Goals
- College enrollment
- Completion of pre-matriculation tasks
