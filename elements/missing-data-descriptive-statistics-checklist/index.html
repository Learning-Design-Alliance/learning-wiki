Checklist 5: reporting missing data and descriptive statistics¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The fifth checklist covers "missing data and descriptive statistics," prompting researchers to report how much data were missing and to provide descriptive statistics for their measures. As the final component of the brief's five-checklist framework, it addresses the baseline statistical information readers need to interpret the measures reported in an education research study.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Descriptive statistics and missing data information must be computed for the study's measures

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • education researchers preparing reports of studies using measures

Target Learning Goals¶

  • complete reporting of missing data and descriptive statistics

Affordances¶

  • Five Checklist Measures Reporting Guide

Claims¶

  • RCTs must achieve much lower missing-data rates or justify that missing data are unrelated to outcomes [+W]

Examples¶

  • Use the checklists against a draft methods and results section to check completeness of reporting
