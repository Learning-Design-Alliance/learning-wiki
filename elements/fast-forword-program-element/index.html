Fast ForWord® adaptive computer-based reading program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 9 claims (9 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Fast ForWord® is "a computer-based reading program intended to help students develop and strengthen the cognitive skills necessary for successful reading and learning." It comprises a Language/Literacy series building memory, attention, processing, sequencing, listening accuracy, and phonological awareness, and a Reading series targeting sound-letter associations, word recognition, vocabulary, and comprehension. The software adapts content and difficulty to individual responses so students respond correctly approximately 80% of the time, with usage options from 30 minutes a day for 12 to 16 weeks to 90 to 100 minutes a day for 4 to 8 weeks.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Designed for use 30 to 100 minutes a day, five days a week, for 4 to 16 weeks; students listen through headphones and respond using the mouse

Constraints¶

  • A single Fast ForWord® Language license costs $900 and each Reading license $500, with limited quantity discounts

Target Learners¶

  • adolescent learners in grades 4-12
  • students struggling with reading, language, and learning problems

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading comprehension
  • phonological awareness
  • vocabulary
  • reading fluency
  • cognitive skills such as memory, attention, processing, and sequencing

Claims¶

  • Both studies of Fast ForWord in the alphabetics domain showed indeterminate effects, with no statistically significant or substantively important effects [~W]
  • In the comprehension domain, one study of Fast ForWord showed statistically significant positive effects, two showed substantively important positive effects, and three showed indeterminate effects [~W]
  • Of 305 studies of Fast ForWord reviewed for adolescent learners, two met WWC evidence standards, six met standards with reservations, and 297 met neither [~W]
  • The extent of evidence for Fast ForWord is small for alphabetics and reading fluency and medium to large for comprehension and general literacy achievement [~W]
  • All five studies of Fast ForWord in the general literacy achievement domain showed indeterminate effects [~W]
  • The WWC improvement indices for Fast ForWord average +2 (alphabetics), +17 (reading fluency), +8 (comprehension), and +3 (general literacy achievement) percentile points [~W]
  • Fast ForWord Language has potentially positive effects on English language development of elementary school English language learners [~W]
  • Fast ForWord Language shows no discernible effects on reading achievement of elementary school English language learners [~W]
  • Fast ForWord has no discernible effects on alphabetics and general literacy achievement and potentially positive effects on reading fluency and comprehension for adolescent learners [~W]

Related Elements¶

  • Reading Plus web-based literacy program for grades 3-12+

Examples¶

  • Implement Fast ForWord Language at high intensity over a compressed period (100 minutes a day for four to six weeks, or 50 minutes a day for 8 to 12 weeks)
