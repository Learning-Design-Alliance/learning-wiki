# Phonologization: final segment applying phonology to the bracketed utterance

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Phonologization is the last of the five segments in Labov's production model. The note states that "Phonologization then takes place a la Sound Patterns," meaning it applies phonological rules in the manner of the Sound Patterns framework to the labeled bracketings supplied by the grammaticalization component, yielding the phonological form of the utterance.
