## Description
A 2020 NWEA Research presentation (CEPA seminar) in which Megan Kuhfeld discusses "a series of projections of COVID-19-related learning loss and its potential effect on test scores in the 2020-21 school year". It also outlines ongoing work to measure student learning between the 2019-20 and 2020-21 school years in the context of mostly remote fall test administration. Listed topics include COVID-19 & schools, growth modeling, and seasonal learning patterns & summer loss.
