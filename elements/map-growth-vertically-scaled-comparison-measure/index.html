NWEA MAP Growth vertically scaled assessment as a common comparison measure¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MAP Growth is NWEA's assessment used in this study as a common assessment against which SEDA's district growth estimates are compared. The article describes it as a "vertically scaled" assessment from which district growth estimates in mathematics and ELA can be derived, serving as an independent benchmark for the convergent validity analysis of SEDA. Its estimates show a high degree of congruence with SEDA's, with small systematic discrepancies in some contexts.

Claims¶

  • Discrepancies between SEDA and MAP Growth ELA growth estimates are slightly more pronounced in high-socioeconomic-status districts [+W]
  • Small systematic discrepancies imply SEDA growth estimates may not generalize to MAP Growth-based estimates in some states [+W]
  • SEDA and MAP Growth district growth estimates in ELA correlate at .82 (precision-adjusted) [+W]
  • SEDA growth estimates show a high degree of congruence with MAP Growth-based estimates overall [+W]
  • SEDA and MAP Growth district growth estimates in mathematics correlate at .90 (precision-adjusted) [+W]
