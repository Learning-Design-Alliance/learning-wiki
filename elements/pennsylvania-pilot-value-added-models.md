# Value-added models developed for estimating Pennsylvania teacher and principal contributions to student achievement growth

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report describes value-added models developed to estimate the contributions of Pennsylvania teachers and principals toward the achievement growth of their students. The estimates were produced during the first phase of a multiyear pilot intended to develop new evaluation systems for teachers and principals, making the models an operational component of that pilot rather than a purely research exercise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student achievement growth data for Pennsylvania students and links between students and the teachers and principals being evaluated
#### Constraints
- Estimates were obtained during the first phase of a multiyear pilot, so they reflect an early stage of the evaluation-system development effort

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in Pennsylvania schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Student achievement growth in subjects covered by the state assessment system
