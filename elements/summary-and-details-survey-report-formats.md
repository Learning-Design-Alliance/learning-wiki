# Summary and details report formats for school survey results

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Consortium produces two report formats from the survey. The "summary reports show how a school's teachers and students responded on survey measures over several years," while the "details reports show how respondents answered specific survey questions." Both formats place a school's responses in comparison to other schools across the school system.
