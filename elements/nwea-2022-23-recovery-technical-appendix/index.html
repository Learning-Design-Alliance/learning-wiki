Description¶

A technical appendix document released by NWEA Research in July 2023 by Karyn Lewis, Megan Kuhfeld, and Jazmin Isaacs. Its stated purpose is to "share more detailed results and to describe more fully the sample and methods" behind the brief on 2022-23 achievement data and stalled pandemic recovery. It is tagged under the topics COVID-19 & schools and Equity.
