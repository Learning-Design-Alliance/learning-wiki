BARR program: a K–12 training and coaching model built on Relationships and Data pillars¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

BARR is "a training and coaching model that serves K–12 schools with a structured system and tools to improve student outcomes." It is "Grounded in two core pillars – Relationships and Data," strengthening staff-student, staff-staff, and student-student relationships while embedding consistent use of academic and non-academic data into daily practice. Core strategies include teacher teams meeting regularly to discuss each student's strengths and progress, Community Connect meetings linking students to out-of-school resources, extensive professional development and coaching, and family engagement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A staff member must be selected as the school's BARR coordinator, who can be an assistant principal, school counselor, teacher, or another staff member
  • Teams of teachers need to meet weekly using the BARR process

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K–12 students in grades 7–12 in the evaluated studies

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading achievement
  • Math achievement
  • Attendance
  • Social-emotional learning outcomes

Claims¶

  • A large multi-site cluster randomized study found a positive PSAT/NMSQT reading effect in one cohort (+0.13) but a smaller effect in a second cohort (+0.03) [+W]
  • BARR improves reading achievement across grades 7–12 with an average effect size of +0.08 across three randomized studies [+W]
  • Two student-level randomized studies in California, Maine, and Minnesota high schools found significant positive impacts on NWEA MAP reading (average effect size +0.09) [+W]
  • In a large suburban southern California high school, BARR produced a significant positive reading effect (+0.14), larger for struggling readers (+0.21) [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Implement BARR with a dedicated coordinator, weekly teacher-team meetings, multi-year professional development, and intensive coaching
