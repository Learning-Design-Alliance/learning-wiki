College Experiences Survey¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The College Experiences Survey is a data collection instrument whose procedures and results are summarized in this methodological report. The report states that "This report summarizes the College Experiences Survey data collection procedures and results." It was fielded as part of a larger evaluation effort on college persistence.
