# Professional Learning Communities at Work (PLC at Work) process

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
PLC at Work is a school-improvement approach that enhances student learning through teacher collaboration. It fosters "a culture of collective responsibility among educators, who work in recurring cycles of collaborative planning, assessment, and targeted intervention to meet students' needs". The model is guided by three core principles—focus on learning, collaborative culture, and results orientation—and provides a framework for schools to build effective teaching communities where all staff contribute to every student's academic and social success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation was supported by Solution Tree with up to 50 days of on-site coaching per year and training resources tailored to each school's needs
#### Constraints
- No additional staff required; coaches work with leadership, classroom teachers, and building level coaches
- No additional technology required

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in participating schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Student achievement in math and ELA
