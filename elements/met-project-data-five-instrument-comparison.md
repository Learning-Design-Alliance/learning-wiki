# Measures of Effective Teaching project data as the evidence base for comparing five observation instruments

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report draws on data from the Measures of Effective Teaching project to compare five widely used teacher observation instruments. It states it "compares five widely used observation instruments on the practices they measure, their relationship to student learning, and whether they are affected by the characteristics of students in a teacher's classroom." The comparison covers content, predictive power, and potential bias.
