Four principal performance measures based on student test scores¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study assessed four principal performance measures based on student test scores: average achievement, adjusted average achievement, school value-added, and adjusted school value-added. The first two "do not account for students' past achievement," while the latter two do. The brief evaluates how accurately each reflects principals' contributions to student achievement in future years using Pennsylvania statewide data.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires statewide data linking students and principals over multiple years, as in Pennsylvania's 2007/08 to 2013/14 data

Constraints¶

  • Measures not accounting for students' past achievement provide no predictive information for future principal contributions

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in Pennsylvania public schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Student achievement as measured by test scores

Claims¶

  • Average achievement and adjusted average achievement provide no information for predicting principals' contributions to student achievement in the following year [+W]
  • Averaging principal performance measures across multiple recent years does not improve their accuracy for predicting principals' contributions in the following year [+W]
  • The study used two sets of analyses—rating stability and the stable component's link to future contributions—to simulate each measure's predictive accuracy [+W]
  • School value-added and adjusted school value-added provide, at most, a small amount of information for predicting principals' contributions in the following year [+W]
