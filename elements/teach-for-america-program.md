# Teach For America alternative certification program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Teach For America (TFA) is a highly selective route to teacher certification that places non-traditionally trained teachers in high-need public schools, requiring a commitment of "at least 2 years of teaching". Recruits complete 5-7 weeks of summer training including practice teaching, then receive ongoing coaching from a manager of teacher leadership and development, regional learning teams, and subject- and grade-tailored toolkits during a 2-year commitment. Admission is highly selective; in 2015 TFA admitted 15% of applicants.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applicants must be college graduates with either an undergraduate GPA of at least 2.50 or a graduate school GPA of at least 3.50 on a 4.00 scale
#### Constraints
- TFA teachers receive the same salary and benefits as other first- or second-year teachers in their school or district, and the program targets schools in low-income communities

### Target Learners
- pre-K-12 students in high-need public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics achievement
- English language arts achievement
- science achievement
- social studies achievement
