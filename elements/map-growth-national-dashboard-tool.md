# MAP Growth National Dashboard public tool for K–8 reading and math achievement and growth

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The MAP Growth National Dashboard is a public tool showing how K–8 students perform in reading and math, built on data from "over seven million students in 20,000 schools—about one in four US public schools." It is updated three times a year and organized into five tabbed sections—National Trends, Group Insights, State Trends, State Groups, and Trends Over Time—each displaying one comparison type at a time, with dropdown controls for grades, student groups, state, and years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Updated three times a year (fall, winter, and spring testing terms) to provide timely national and state reference points
#### Constraints
- Includes achievement and growth only for reading and math in grades K–8
- Does not currently support state-to-state or district-to-district comparisons
- Growth metrics appear only beginning with the winter update, since they require at least two test scores

### Target Learners
- K–8 students in US public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading and mathematics achievement and growth monitoring
- Pandemic academic recovery tracking
