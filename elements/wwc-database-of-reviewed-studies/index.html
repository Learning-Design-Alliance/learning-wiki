What Works Clearinghouse (WWC) database of reviewed studies¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A database maintained by the What Works Clearinghouse, established by the Institute of Education Sciences in 2002, containing studies reviewed against WWC evidence standards. The article states it used "the WWC's database of reviewed studies" to explore relationships between study characteristics and dispositions, differences by topic area, and changes over time, making it the data source for assessing education research quality trends.

Context¶

  • WWC design standards may have contributed to observed improvement in education research quality over the past 15 years [+W]
  • The levels and rates of studies meeting WWC evidence standards have increased over the life of the What Works Clearinghouse [+W]
