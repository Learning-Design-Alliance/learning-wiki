# SSI Youth Solutions project set of 12 proposed intervention papers

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of 12 papers commissioned by ODEP's SSI Youth Solutions project, initiated in 2019 to "generate testable ideas for improving outcomes among youth receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI)." Subject matter experts proposed program and policy solutions, "both those based on existing research and practice and those that represented novel, untested ideas." This report presents and contrasts the papers' characteristics, strengths, and limitations for decision makers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The proposals were gathered from subject matter experts as testable program and policy solutions for youth receiving SSI
#### Constraints
- The papers include novel, untested ideas, and the report notes gaps in evidence on employment effects, costs, and implementation challenges

### Target Learners
- youth receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI) with disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- employment outcomes and transition to adulthood
