# NNPS professional development: institutes, workshops, and quarterly series

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NNPS builds implementation capacity through a professional development infrastructure: it "conducts institutes and workshops that build leaders' abilities to strengthen and sustain their partnership programs." Partners also get access to two quarterly professional development series designed to build a professional community around partnerships and to learn about partnership research and evidence-based family engagement best practices. NNPS facilitators work with partners to support the development and implementation of strong programs throughout the schools they are guiding.
