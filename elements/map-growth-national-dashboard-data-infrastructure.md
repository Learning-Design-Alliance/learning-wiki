# MAP Growth assessment and National Dashboard as the data infrastructure for NWEA COVID-recovery research

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MAP Growth is NWEA's assessment product, and the MAP Growth National Dashboard is the data source NWEA researchers use for post-COVID academic recovery analyses. The page repeatedly names it: reports 'focus on academic growth trends post-COVID' 'Using data from the MAP Growth National Dashboard', and one related brief analyzed 'MAP Growth data from 2,000 US school districts.' The 2025 update to MAP Growth national norms and an enhanced item selection algorithm (EISA) are also named as changing how norm-referenced metrics are interpreted.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Analyses depend on MAP Growth test score data collected from U.S. school districts and accessed through the MAP Growth National Dashboard
#### Constraints
- A 2025 update to MAP Growth national norms, together with the enhanced item selection algorithm (EISA), calls for careful review of commonly used norm-referenced metrics in accountability contexts, per the related white paper description

### Target Learners
- U.S. K-12 students taking MAP Growth assessments

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring student achievement and growth in reading and mathematics over time, including pandemic-era disruption and recovery
