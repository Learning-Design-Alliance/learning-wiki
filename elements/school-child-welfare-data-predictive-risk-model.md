# Predictive-model approach using school and child welfare agency data to flag near-term academic risk

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report describes an approach for developing a predictive model that identifies students likely to have near-term academic problems, including absenteeism, suspensions, poor grades, and low performance on state tests. It is intended for administrators, researchers, and student support staff in local education agencies who want to identify at-risk students early. The model draws on data shared between schools and child welfare agencies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data-sharing partnerships between schools and child welfare or human services agencies
#### Constraints
- Developed and assessed with data from two local education agencies in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in local education agencies

### Target Learning Goals
- Early identification of students at risk of absenteeism, suspensions, poor grades, and low state-test performance
