# OpenSciEd teacher supports: unit-specific PD themes and professional learning activities

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
OpenSciEd teacher supports are "a comprehensive system of materials and structured professional learning experiences that enable teachers to implement the OpenSciEd vision successfully." Unit-specific materials foreground four themes: content understanding, unit storyline, goals for specific lessons and activities, and logistical and materials strategies. Professional learning takes the form of four-day (or more) workshops in which teachers analyze video of problems of practice, focus on navigation routines, immerse themselves in content from the student perspective by switching hats, and collaborate and reflect with peers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A substantial amount of professional learning time for teachers to implement OpenSciEd successfully
#### Constraints
- Implementing the model is challenging and requires many teachers to dramatically change beliefs about what students are capable of and how science should be learned and taught

### Target Learners
- K-12 science teachers with wide-ranging experience

### Target Learning Goals
- facilitating sensemaking discussions, unit navigation, and equitable instructional practices
