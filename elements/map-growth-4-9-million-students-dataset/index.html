MAP Growth test-score dataset covering 4.9 million U.S. students in Grades 3-8¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study's evidence base is a large-scale dataset of test scores from U.S. students, described in the abstract as follows: "The data included test scores from 4.9 million U.S. students in Grades 3 through 8." The authors used these scores to model student achievement trends prior to and during the pandemic, with particular focus on growth in 2020-2021. The dataset spans math and reading outcomes across elementary and middle school grades.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Test scores from students in Grades 3 through 8 in math and reading, spanning pre-pandemic and 2020-2021 periods, to model achievement trends against prepandemic averages.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • U.S. students in Grades 3 through 8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Mathematics achievement
  • Reading achievement

Claims¶

  • Math growth in 2020-2021 was more variable than in prior years, with much of the gains among initially high-performing students [+W]
  • The average student demonstrated positive math and reading gains during the 2020-2021 school year [+W]
  • By spring 2021 students remained behind typical prepandemic achievement averages in both math and reading [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • MAP Growth assessment dataset used to measure pandemic achievement effects (8.3 million students, grades 3-8)
  • NWEA MAP Growth assessment dataset (3.4 million students) as the evidence base for seasonal learning analysis
  • MAP Growth assessment dataset covering 5.5 million students in grades 3-8 in reading and math
  • MAP Growth reading and mathematics assessment dataset (7 million students, grades 3-8, 25,000 schools, 2020-21 to fall 2022)

  • NWEA MAP Growth assessment and longitudinal achievement database
  • Multi-season math and reading score dataset for over seven million K-8 students (2016-17)
