# Vivaldi individual school report (Unit 8888) as a longitudinal outcome-tracking artifact

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This artifact is a school-specific tracking report that follows one elementary school's graduates into Chicago Public Schools high schools and Academic Preparatory Centers. It combines a five-year outcomes tracking design, in which it "tracks for five years members of the eighth-grade class of 1997", with a one-year on-track design covering the eighth-grade classes of 1997 to 2001. It presents results in ten tables and two figures broken out by gender, eighth-grade ITBS achievement level, and destination high school, and is explicitly meant to be informative, not evaluative.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to CPS Student Information System codes for dropout, graduation, and leaving-CPS status, and to high school transcript data
#### Constraints
- The report cannot follow students into non-CPS high schools, so outcomes for those enrollees are not reported
- Data do not adjust for student population factors such as low-income percentage, special education eligibility, or English language learner status

### Target Learners
- Eighth-grade graduates of Vivaldi Elementary School entering Chicago Public Schools high schools or Academic Preparatory Centers

### Target Learning Goals
- Monitoring post-elementary achievement and progress toward high school graduation
