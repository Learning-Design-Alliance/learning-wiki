# ASSISTments online math homework platform

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
ASSISTments is an online homework tool coupled with teacher training that "provid[es] students with math assistance and teachers with assessments of student progress." It has two content types: problem sets linked to existing textbook homework, giving teachers flexibility in assignment, and mastery-oriented skill practice content. The web-based platform is available at no cost to schools, but students need computer access at home.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 5 days of professional development plus three in-person or remote coaching visits accompany implementation
- students need computer access at home
#### Constraints
- the web-based platform is free to schools but assumes home computer access for students

### Target Learners
- middle school students, specifically grade 7 math classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- middle school mathematics achievement
