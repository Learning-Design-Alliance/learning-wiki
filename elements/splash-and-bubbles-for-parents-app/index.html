Splash and Bubbles for Parents second-screen app¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A second-screen digital resource designed to work alongside the Splash and Bubbles ocean science television show, aimed at "supporting and promoting science-rich talk between adults and children and facilitating joint engagement with media." It provides related content, conversation starters for before, during, and after viewing (separate first- and repeat-viewing prompts), and Activity Cove hands-on activities. An Automatic Content Recognition feature syncs the app to the episode being watched so parents can access related content easily.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires families to have access to the show episodes and a device on which to run the app alongside viewing

Constraints¶

  • The report notes many families would benefit from support navigating the app given it represents a new kind of tool, and some families were unaware of repeat-viewing prompts possibly due to the app design utilizing a Switch Viewing button

Target Learners¶

  • Parents and caregivers of pre-kindergarten and kindergarten-aged children

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Young children's ocean science core ideas (structure and function, interdependent relationships, adaptations) and science practices through family conversation

Claims¶

  • Nearly all participating families reported using the Splash and Bubbles for Parents app and found it helpful for supporting children's science learning [+W]
  • Families used multiple sections of the Splash and Bubbles for Parents app, with Dive In the most used and Let's Talk About It conversation starters rated most helpful within it [+W]
  • Families showed widespread variability in which Ocean Guide and Activities components they found most helpful, often citing child age and interest [~W]
  • Attrition in the station study was higher than expected, and partial-data families rated app components slightly lower than complete-data families [~W]
