## Description
Lexia Academy is a self-paced eLearning platform that "supports educators’ professional growth". Its courses "integrate resources and strategies to support blended learning, literacy instruction and product implementation". It is one of several professional development offerings alongside customized trainings from a dedicated customer success team, and it can be engaged with by school and district staff.
