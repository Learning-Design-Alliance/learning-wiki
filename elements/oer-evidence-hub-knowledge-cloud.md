# OER Evidence Hub and OER Knowledge Cloud as open education support infrastructures

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes two infrastructures supporting the open education movement. The OER Evidence Hub, developed by the Knowledge Media Institute and the Institute of Educational Technology at The Open University, "aims to provide an environment to systematically interrogate the open education movement" on people, projects, organizations, challenges, solutions, and claims. The OER Knowledge Cloud was established to identify, collect, preserve, and disseminate documents of enduring value to researchers and informal learners.
