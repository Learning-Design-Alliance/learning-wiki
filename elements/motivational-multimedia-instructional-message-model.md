# Model of the motivational and multimedia instructional message with six activity groups for lesson introductions

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A model developed by Bistrovic (2017) and used in this study, in which the introductory 7-10 minutes of each lesson is delivered as a multimedia instructional and motivational message built from "six groups of activities: association games, videos, interactive stories, interactive maps, visual stimuli, and crossword puzzles and quizzes", each group containing three activities. In the experimental classes the activities were presented via PowerPoint and Prezi in frontal, whole-class mode; control classes received the same content via posters, printed photographs, maps and paper tasks. The authors conclude the model "showed as suitable for use in teaching the subject of Nature and Society".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multimedia presentations created according to multimedia learning principles, delivered by the regular class teacher during the introductory part of the lesson
#### Constraints
- Activities were presented frontally to the whole class, not on individual computers; no multimedia was used in the central part of the lesson

### Target Learners
- 4th-grade elementary school students

### Target Learning Goals
- Science and social science content knowledge in the subject Nature and Society
- Motivation to learn at the start of lessons
