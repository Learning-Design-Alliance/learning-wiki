PSLC DataShop public repository of online learning data¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The Pittsburgh Science of Learning Center's DataShop is a public data repository that, per the review, "makes substantial quantities of data from a variety of online learning environments available, for free, to any researcher worldwide". The review reports that 14% of papers published in EDM2008 and EDM2009 utilized data from it, marking a shift away from the earlier necessity for researchers to collect their own educational data. It enables external validation and model comparison against strong baselines.
