MAP Growth assessment and National Dashboard as data sources for NWEA COVID-recovery research¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

MAP Growth is NWEA's assessment product, and the MAP Growth National Dashboard is the data source NWEA researchers use for post-COVID achievement and growth trend analyses. The page repeatedly names it as the underlying data source: "Using data from the MAP Growth National Dashboard, this research brief highlights an urgent challenge in academic recovery: middle school reading." It supports NWEA's series of briefs on pandemic recovery, absenteeism, and classroom academic diversity.
