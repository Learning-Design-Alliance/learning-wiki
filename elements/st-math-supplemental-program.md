# ST Math: a PreK-8 supplemental math program based on visual problem solving

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
ST Math is described as "a PreK-8 supplemental program built on the science of how the brain learns", inviting students to "explore, make sense of, and build confidence in math through visual problem solving". It is used by existing general ed teachers or math specialists during regular instructional time, on modern devices from desktops to tablets.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designed for use by existing general ed teachers or math specialists during regular school instructional time, with no additional staff time required.
#### Constraints
- Smartphone screen size is too small for interactivity; any modern device and O/S from desktops to Chromebooks to tablets is required.

### Target Learners
- PreK-8 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Elementary and middle school mathematics achievement

## Claims

- [Across four studies, ST Math shows an average effect size of +0.07 on math achievement](../claims/st-math-average-effect-size-007.md) [+W]
- [First cluster-randomized trial: ST Math students outperformed controls after one year (+0.09), with less robust results after two years (+0.03)](../claims/st-math-crt1-year-one-009-year-two-003.md) [+W]
- [Second cluster-randomized trial in 52 high-poverty schools shows small but statistically significant math gains (+0.04), qualifying ST Math for ESSA Tier 1](../claims/st-math-crt2-essa-tier-one.md) [+W]
- [Retrospective comparisons show stronger math achievement in ST Math schools than business-as-usual schools](../claims/st-math-retrospective-stronger-results.md) [+W]
