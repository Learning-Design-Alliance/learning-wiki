## Description
The study relies on what the authors call "a novel data set" used "to examine how and where discipline disparities arise." The data set's distinguishing feature, as described, is that it permits comparing the punishments given to both students involved in the same fight, enabling the within-pair contrast between Black and White (or poor and nonpoor) students that the authors' identification strategy requires.
