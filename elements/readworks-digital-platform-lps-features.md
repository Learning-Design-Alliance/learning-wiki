# ReadWorks digital reading platform with LPS-informed learner variability features

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
ReadWorks is a nonprofit providing free K-12 resources including a library of curated nonfiction and literary articles, reading comprehension and vocabulary curricular supports, assessments, and teacher resources. After partnering with LPS in 2017, its digital platform added audio supports (human voice or text-to-speech), a text magnifier with three font size options, and paragraph numbering; planned features include a guided reading pane and a split screen option. As of January 2018 it had 3.2 million students and 143,000 teachers actively using its digital products.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need awareness of the features so the supports are actually used in the classroom
#### Constraints
- At interview time, student choice features including text magnification had only recently gone live, and some choice features were planned but not yet implemented

### Target Learners
- K-8 students, including English language learners and students with dyslexia or on the Autism Spectrum

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension, vocabulary, fluency, and positive attitudes toward reading
