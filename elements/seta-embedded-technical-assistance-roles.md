# SETA (Scientific and Engineering Technical Assistance) roles as embedded technical expertise in education R&D

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SETAs are embedded technical experts in the ARPA tradition who provide continuity, systems-level insight, and rapid response capacity across short-term, high-ambition research programs. In education, the paper describes SETA-like roles as translators between learning science, data science, and classroom practice, helping teams navigate real-time data pipelines, IRB compliance, and platform integration. The paper recommends funders make such expertise-and-infrastructure-on-demand models a default feature of ambitious programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SETA roles require funding for technical, contextual, and managerial fluency, and management practices that shift from grant oversight to program stewardship.
#### Constraints
- The paper lists SETA-style staffing among capabilities that are not yet universal in education R&D.

### Target Learners
- Education research performer teams and the students their programs serve

### Target Learning Goals
- Sustaining agility and rigor in education R&D programs through embedded technical support
