Four elementary math curricula compared in the study¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study compared four published elementary mathematics curricula, enumerated by the brief as "(1) Investigations in Number, Data, and Space; (2) Math Expressions; (3) Saxon Math; and (4) Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics". These four curricula constitute the treatments whose effects on grade 1-2 achievement the evaluation estimated over two years. The brief identifies them as the complete set of curricula under study.

Claims¶

  • First-grade math achievement is significantly higher under Math Expressions and Saxon Math than under Investigations in Number, Data, and Space and Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics [+M]
  • After two years of implementation, three of four elementary math curricula outperformed the fourth on student achievement in grades 1–2 [+M]
  • The better-performing early math curricula raised achievement for several student subgroups, including students in low-scoring and high-poverty schools [+M]
  • The study is the largest experimental-design study of multiple math curricula, covering 110 schools with this report based on the first cohort of 39 schools [+M]
