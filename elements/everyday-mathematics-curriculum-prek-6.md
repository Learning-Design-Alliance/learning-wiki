# Everyday Mathematics® core curriculum for grades pre-K–6

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Everyday Mathematics® is "a comprehensive curriculum for students in grades pre-K–6 that exposes students to material over extended periods of time by repeatedly reinforcing material both within and across grade levels." The article states "The curriculum includes six key features": real-life problem solving, balanced instruction, multiple opportunities for basic skills practice, emphasis on communication, home/school partnerships, and use of technology. It is organized around Program Goals (the same at all grade levels, organized by content strand) refined through approximately 20–25 Grade-Level Goals per grade, with teacher guides, student materials, manipulatives, games, and online resources.
