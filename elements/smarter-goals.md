# SMARTER Goals

## Description
SMARTER goals are a structured goal-setting element that asks learners to define goals that are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound, evaluated, and revised or rewarded, depending on the local formulation. In this wiki, the main instructional value of SMARTER goals is not the acronym itself but the discipline it brings to making goals concrete enough to monitor and adjust.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A meaningful learning target that can be broken into observable progress markers
- Time to define the goal clearly and revisit it later
- Some mechanism for evaluation, feedback, or revision

#### Constraints
- Overly rigid templates can produce compliant wording without genuine commitment
- Not every worthwhile learning goal is equally easy to quantify
- Goals need monitoring and feedback to matter instructionally

### Target Learners
- Learners building self-regulation and planning habits
- Novices who benefit from proximal, concrete targets
- Adults or adolescents managing longer learning sequences

### Target Learning Goals
- Making goals interpretable and trackable
- Supporting progress monitoring and revision
- Turning vague aspiration into a usable plan

### Affordances
- Clarifies what success looks like
- Supports monitoring, feedback, and persistence
- Helps instructors and learners align on the next step
