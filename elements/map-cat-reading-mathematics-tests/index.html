MAP Reading and Mathematics computerized adaptive tests (NWEA)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (3 associational), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MAP tests are computerized adaptive assessments published by NWEA since 2000 for grades 3-9, intended "to provide educators with information to inform teaching and learning in Reading, Mathematics, and Science". The Reading test has about 40 multiple-choice items and four goals; the Mathematics test has 50 items and seven goals. Tests are aligned to state content standards, and "the marginal reliabilities of tests across 50 states and grades are consistently in the low to mid 0.90's".

Claims¶

  • A one-factor unidimensional model fits MAP computerized adaptive Reading and Mathematics tests across grades, terms, and ten states [+W]
  • MAP tests show metric invariance across grades for all studied tests except Michigan Mathematics tests [+W]
  • Chi-square difference tests show significant loading invariance constraints for Kansas Reading but all constraints significant for Michigan Mathematics [+W]
  • The longitudinal achievement constructs of MAP reading and mathematics CATs remain invariant across five time points in ten states (except Indiana mathematics) [+W]
