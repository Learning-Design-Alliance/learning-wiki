Shared Measures Construct Map instrument for ELA and math curriculum shifts¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The instrument is a one-page construct map titled "Shared Measures Construct Map" within a practical guide for measurement and progress monitoring of ELA and math curriculum shifts. It prints six numbered dimensions, each with a construct statement and lettered sub-constructs, and a KEY that defines "(Sub-constructs)", marks "Unidirectional" and "Bidirectional" relations, and flags "Items specific to ELA and Math". It is designed so instructional leaders can monitor implementation progress across the system.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Users must interpret dimensions together with the KEY, which distinguishes sub-constructs, unidirectional and bidirectional relations, and ELA/math-specific items

Constraints¶

  • The instrument covers only ELA and math curriculum shifts, per its title and key

Target Learners¶

  • instructional leaders
  • district leaders
  • principals
  • instructional coaches

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring and monitoring progress of curriculum-based professional learning and HQIM implementation

Affordances¶

  • Shared Measures Construct Map Curriculum Shifts

Claims¶

  • Classroom student characteristics affect teacher observation results for some instruments, more often in English language arts than in math classes [~W]

Related Elements¶

  • Student outcomes dimension: engagement, mindsets, belonging, and grade-level proficiency
  • Teacher instructional practice dimension: implementing HQIM with integrity while adapting to students

Examples¶

  • Use the construct map for progress monitoring of CBPL and HQIM implementation
