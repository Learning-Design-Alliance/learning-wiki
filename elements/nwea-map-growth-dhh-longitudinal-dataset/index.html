NWEA MAP Growth longitudinal dataset for DHH students, grades 2–8, seven reading and mathematics domains over 5 years¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

This element is the longitudinal achievement dataset analyzed in the study: NWEA MAP® Growth™ data covering DHH students from grades 2 to 8. The article states it "provides an analysis of NWEA MAP® Growth™ data from grades 2 to 8 across seven reading and mathematics domains over a period of 5 years," making it a multi-domain, multi-year record of academic growth used to model how DHH students' reading and mathematics achievement progresses over time.
