Matched comparison group design for charter school impact evaluation¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The evaluation method used for this report: a quasi-experimental, matched comparison group design estimating the Kauffman School's impacts on student achievement, attendance, and suspensions. The report states, "This paper uses a quasi-experimental, matched comparison group design to estimate the impacts of the Kauffman School on student achievement, attendance, and suspensions."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A matched comparison group of comparable students to estimate impacts absent random assignment

Constraints¶

  • Quasi-experimental rather than randomized; impacts are estimated relative to matched comparison schools

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 charter school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Estimating school-level impacts on achievement growth, attendance, and suspensions

Claims¶

  • The Kauffman School had positive, statistically significant, educationally meaningful impacts on student achievement growth in mathematics, reading, and science during 2011-2012 through 2013-2014 [+W]
  • The Kauffman School had positive, statistically significant impacts on student achievement growth in mathematics, reading, and science across 2011-2012 through 2013-2014 [+W]
  • The Kauffman School raised student attendance by approximately 1 percentage point relative to comparison students [+W]
  • The Kauffman School suspends students at a substantially higher rate than other schools in Kansas City [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Matched-comparison evaluation design for the Kauffman School impact study

Examples¶

  • Use a matched comparison group design plus qualitative mechanism exploration to evaluate a new charter school's educational and behavioral impacts
  • Use a matched comparison group design with qualitative mechanism exploration to evaluate charter school impacts
  • Evaluate charter school impacts across college enrollment, graduation, attendance, and suspensions over multiple years
  • Pair implementation description with quasi-experimental impact estimation in program evaluation
