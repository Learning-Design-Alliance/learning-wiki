Studies of Arkansas AIIM and THRIVE with findings presentations and infographic products¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The logic model lists two studies as partnership activities: "Study of Arkansas AIIM" and "Study of Arkansas THRIVE." Each is paired with dissemination products — presenting findings and producing infographics summarizing them — intended to build ADE staff understanding of program progress, trends, successes, and challenges and of districts' implementation of AIIM. A virtual panel of district leaders implementing THRIVE is planned to increase education and awareness of the program.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Findings must be presented and infographics produced so that ADE staff use evidence from the Task 5 studies to improve THRIVE and AIIM, as the medium-term outcomes state.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Arkansas Department of Education staff
  • district leaders implementing THRIVE

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding of AIIM and THRIVE program progress, trends, successes, and challenges
  • data use for program improvement

Claims¶

  • The BPSS logic model targets increased college readiness among Arkansas student populations with persistent outcome disparities [+W]
  • The BPSS logic model sets reduced discipline disparities as a long-term student outcome for THRIVE and AIIM districts [+W]
