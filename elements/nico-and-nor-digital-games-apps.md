# Nico and Nor digital games and apps for school and home STEM learning

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The program includes free digital apps for iPad described as digital games that "support children’s learning accross school and home." The games "provide children opportunities to observe science phenomena and easily test and revise solutions." The overview names eight games: Wonder Farm, Puppy Park, Coconut Star, Coconut Canyon, Shadow Play, Berry Garden, Farmers Market, and Shadow Cave. The Spanish text adds that the games let children practice math and test and revise engineering solutions.
