# Ignite Reading one-to-one virtual literacy tutoring program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Ignite Reading pairs young learners with "highly-trained reading tutors for daily 15-minute sessions focused on foundational literacy skills, including phonemic awareness, phonics, decoding, and fluency, using a structured and research-based instructional sequence." It is 100% virtual on a FERPA-compliant video platform with an interactive whiteboard, integrates with MTSS, and aligns with Science of Reading principles. Literacy specialists monitor progress and provide monthly data reports to educators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools provide devices, required headphones, and internet speed of at least 5Mb/s download and at least 3Mb/s upload per student
- A District Champion and School Champion oversee implementation and support attendance goals
#### Constraints
- Program is 100% virtual and requires a FERPA-compliant video conferencing platform with Clever and ClassLink compatibility

### Target Learners
- first-grade students with early reading skill gaps

### Target Learning Goals
- phonemic awareness
- phonics
- decoding
- fluency
