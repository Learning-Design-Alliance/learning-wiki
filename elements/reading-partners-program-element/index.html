Reading Partners one-to-one volunteer tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Reading Partners is "a one-to-one tutoring program delivered by community volunteers who are recruited, trained, and supported by Reading Partners staff and AmeriCorps members at a reading center within a school." It serves grade 2-4 students reading one to twenty-five months below grade level who are fluent in English and have no IEPs. Students attend 45-minute individualized sessions twice weekly, managed by a full-time site coordinator provided to partner schools.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Partner schools must have a dedicated space for a reading center, usually a classroom
  • AmeriCorps members and volunteers are required
  • Reading Partners provides a full-time site coordinator to manage the program

Constraints¶

  • Serves students in grades 2-4 who are reading one to twenty five months below grade level, are fluent in English, and do not have IEPs

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 2-4 reading one to twenty-five months below grade level, fluent in English, without IEPs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading comprehension and specific reading skills

Claims¶

  • Reading Partners targets a defined struggling-reader population: grades 2-4, one to twenty-five months below grade level, fluent in English, without IEPs [+W]
  • Reading Partners tutoring significantly improves reading comprehension for grade 2-4 struggling readers, with a total effect size of +0.10 [+M]
