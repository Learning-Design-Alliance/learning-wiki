DreamBox Math adaptive online K-8 mathematics program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

DreamBox Math is "an adaptive, online program designed to complement classroom instruction". It "personalizes K-8 learning with a supplemental curriculum beyond instructional support software", combining "a motivating, game-like environment with a rigorous, standards-aligned curriculum" and continuously adapting to learners' actions to build competency with math concepts and fluency strategies. It runs on Mac, PC, ChromeBooks, or iPad.

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 students
  • high-poverty charter school K-1 populations
  • grades K-2 and 4-5 in district schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • math concepts competency
  • fluency and application strategies

Claims¶

  • Averaging across the two randomized DreamBox studies and measures, the effect size is +0.10, supporting a Strong rating with a badge for two randomized studies with significant positive results [+M]
  • In a randomized study in three high-poverty Bay Area charter schools, K-1 students using DreamBox gained significantly more than control students on the NWEA MAP Measurement and Geometry subtest (ES +0.16) and scored higher overall (ES +0.11) [+W]
  • In a 24-school North Carolina randomized study, K-2 students using DreamBox significantly outperformed controls on the Number Knowledge Test (ES +0.12), while 4th-5th grade effects on the state End-of-Grade assessment were not meaningfully higher (ES +0.03) [~W]
