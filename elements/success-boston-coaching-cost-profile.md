# Cost profile of Success Boston Coaching: $5,762 per student during scale-up, decreasing to $3,037

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report documents the major resources needed to implement Success Boston Coaching, listing cost ingredients covering personnel, facilities, equipment and materials, direct assistance, and other supports. In the 2015-16 academic year, when the intervention was scaled from serving about 300 students to 1,000 students per cohort annually, "the estimated total cost was $5,762 per student". Without scale-up administrative costs, the cost per student decreased to $3,037 in 2016-17. Funding came from The Boston Foundation, a Social Innovation Fund grant, and coaching partner organizations.
