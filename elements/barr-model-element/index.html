BARR: a K–12 training and coaching model grounded in Relationships and Data¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

BARR is "a training and coaching model that serves K–12 schools with a structured system and tools to improve student outcomes." It is "Grounded in two core pillars – Relationships and Data," strengthening "staff-student, staff-staff, and student-student relationships while embedding the consistent use of academic and non-academic data into daily practice." A staff coordinator manages, coordinates, and integrates the model, and no specific technology is required.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A staff member must be selected to serve as the school's BARR coordinator, who can be an assistant principal, school counselor, teacher, or another staff member.
  • Teams of teachers need to meet weekly using the BARR process.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K–12 students
  • ninth-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improving student outcomes including attendance, math, reading, and social-emotional learning

Claims¶

  • BARR reduces ninth-grade chronic absenteeism in a large school-level randomized study [+M]
  • BARR is rated Strong for Math, Reading, and SEL impacts in addition to Attendance [+M]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Community Connect meetings to coordinate out-of-school resources for students
  • Weekly teacher team meetings to review each student's strengths, progress, and obstacles
  • Implement BARR with a dedicated coordinator, weekly teacher-team meetings, multi-year professional development, and intensive coaching
