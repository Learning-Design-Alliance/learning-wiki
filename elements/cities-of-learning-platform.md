# Cities of Learning digital platform

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Cities of Learning (CoL), led by Nichole Pinkard with NSF support, is a digital platform promoting the idea that learning and teaching occur everywhere in a city. The report states "CoL provides a digital platform that aggregates, maps, and describes the learning resources available to young people in a given city (such as Chicago, Dallas, and Pittsburgh)." It tracks pathways young people take between locations, alerts them and invested adults about other learning opportunities along a route, motivates students with badges, and promotes intergenerational learning by connecting youth with adult community members to discuss local issues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Location-aware mobile technologies and city-wide learning resources to aggregate and map
#### Constraints
- The report notes the platform also reveals gaps, informing policymakers about where learning resources are lacking

### Target Learners
- urban youth, including those from underrepresented groups

### Target Learning Goals
