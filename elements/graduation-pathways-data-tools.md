# CPS Graduation Pathways data tools: freshman watch list, monthly freshmen success reports, and credit recovery reports

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Office of Graduation Pathways, working with Consortium researchers, developed a set of real-time data tools for identifying and monitoring ninth-graders at risk of going off-track. These included "a “freshman watch list” that flagged incoming ninth-graders at risk of weak attendance and poor academic performance," monthly "freshmen success reports" identifying students needing intervention, and a "credit recovery" report flagging spring-semester students needing to make up core credits. The tools filled a gap, since principals previously could not link cumulative days absent with course outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires school access to real-time data on attendance, grades, and credits, which CPS high schools lacked before the initiative
#### Constraints
- The article notes the tools alone did not drive improvement; the strength of the Freshman OnTrack indicator lies in the multi-faceted responses it elicits from school staff

### Target Learners
- First-time ninth-grade students in urban public high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Course passing and credit accumulation in ninth grade toward on-time high school graduation
