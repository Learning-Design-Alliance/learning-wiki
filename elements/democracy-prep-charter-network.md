# Democracy Prep Public Schools charter network

## Description
Democracy Prep is a charter school network that, in the report's words, "seeks to prepare students for effective citizenship." The network operates middle and high-school grades, admitted early cohorts through admissions lotteries, and its civic outcomes were evaluated by matching lottery records to a national registration and voting database.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Admission via randomized admissions lotteries, which the study uses to identify enrolled and unmatched applicants
#### Constraints
- Effects measured for students offered admission in the network's early years of operation who were old enough to vote by the 2016 election

### Target Learners
- Middle and high school students in the Democracy Prep charter network

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparation for effective citizenship and civic participation
