# Meta-analysis corpus of 21 published policies and papers on Indonesian bilingual higher education (2015–2019)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's sample is a corpus of published policies and papers limited to the last five years (2015–2019), drawn from "reputable documents indexed in Scopus and Web of Science." Table 1 lists 11 journal articles (coded A1–A11), 2 books or book chapters (A12–A13), 5 university documents (A14–A17), and 3 official government documents (A20–A22), including Indonesian higher education and national education system laws. Documents were gathered via ministry websites and keyword searches.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Documents must be published policies or papers on language policy, bilingualism, and bilingual education in Indonesian higher education
#### Constraints
- Limited to documents from the last five years (2015-2019) to probe current policy and debate themes

### Target Learners
- university students and teacher candidates in Indonesian bilingual programs

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding language policy implementation in bilingual higher education
