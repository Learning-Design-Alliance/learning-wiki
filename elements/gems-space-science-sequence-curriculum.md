# GEMS® Space Science Sequence curriculum for grades 3–5

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The GEMS® Space Science Sequence is an instructional curriculum for grades 3–5 covering "planetary sizes and distance, the Earth’s shape and movement, gravity, and moon phases and eclipses", using the solar system as the focal point for learning. It comprises 24 sixty-minute class sessions in four units (How Big and How Far?, Earth’s Shape and Gravity, How Does the Earth Move?, Moon Phases and Eclipses), each of which can stand alone or build into an overall learning progression. It comes with a teacher’s guide, materials kit, master copies, and a CD-ROM of resources, and is available from Carolina Curriculum for $515 (kit) and $149.95 (teacher’s guide).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher’s guide, a materials kit, and master copies for duplication or electronic presentation
- More than 60 GEMS® network sites and centers provide ongoing training and support to teachers
#### Constraints
- The reviewed evidence covers the grades 3–5 sequence only; a separate grades 6–8 sequence exists but is not covered by this report
- Not all units must be taught—each can stand alone

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in grades 3–5 (study sample: grades 4 and 5)

### Target Learning Goals
- Fundamental space science concepts: size and scale relative to distance, Earth’s shape and gravity, Earth’s movement, moon phases and eclipses
- The role of models and evidence in science
