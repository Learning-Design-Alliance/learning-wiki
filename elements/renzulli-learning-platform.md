# Renzulli Learning online personalized-learning platform

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Renzulli Learning is an online platform that helps teachers differentiate instruction and foster student engagement. It focuses on "individual strengths and interests" to cultivate critical thinking, creativity, and 21st-century skills, supporting personalized learning for students from Pre-K to 12th grade. It is a cloud-based SaaS platform with a support team, SSO, rostering, LMS integrations, and device compatibility documented on its technology page.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cloud-based SaaS delivery with supported devices; the page points to documented Technology Requirements, Accessibility, SSO, Rostering and LMS Integrations
#### Constraints
- Does not require additional staffing; online professional development costs $1,200 per day, with free 1-1 Quick Start sessions available

### Target Learners
- Pre-K to 12th grade students, including elementary and middle school students in high-poverty urban and suburban settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Critical thinking
- creativity
- 21st-century skills
- oral reading fluency
- personalized learning and equitable opportunities
