Tutoring with the Lightning Squad: small-group, pair-based, game-like decoding and fluency tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Tutoring with the Lightning Squad is a small group reading intervention for grades 1-5 focusing on mastery of early decoding skills and development of fluency. Tutors work with groups of up to four students in daily half hour sessions, in which "students take turns as reader and coach to help each other succeed" using interactive, game-like, web-based activities. The software tracks individual and team success, adapts lesson presentation based on students' responses, and includes built-in assessments for placement and progress monitoring with reports to students, parents, and homeroom teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Computers and broadband are required.
  • Training and coaching support, including periodic data reviews, are included as part of a license and service contract.

Constraints¶

  • In most sites tutors are employed by the school or a community partner; most tutors have a college degree but are not certified teachers.

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 1-5
  • Grade 2-3 students performing in the lowest 33rd percentile on standardized measures

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Mastery of early decoding skills
  • Development of reading fluency

Claims¶

  • Adequate-dose tutoring showed marginally significant effects for third graders on the NWEA MAP (ES = +0.27) [+W]
  • The average effect size of Tutoring with the Lightning Squad across outcome measures is +0.07 [+W]
  • Principals, site coordinators, and teachers reported strongly positive reactions to the program [+W]
  • Tutoring with the Lightning Squad significantly improves Word Attack scores versus control in a cluster randomized study (ES = +0.18) [+W]
