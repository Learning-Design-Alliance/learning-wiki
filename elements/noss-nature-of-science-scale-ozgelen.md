# Nature of Science Scale (NOSS)

## Description
The Nature of Science Scale is a quantitative instrument developed by Özgelen (2013) used in this study as pre-test, post-test and retention test. The article states "The NOSS consisted of 30 items developed by Özgelen (2013), and it was applied to 655 pre-service teachers in four different universities." Exploratory factor analysis reduced it to five factors and 19 items, with reliability of .83 and a confirmatory factor analysis χ2/df ratio of 0.83. Sample items target tentative, empirical, subjective, creative, socio-cultural, observation-inference, and theory-law characteristics.
