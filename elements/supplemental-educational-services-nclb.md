# Supplemental Educational Services (SEdS) under No Child Left Behind

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SEdS are "tutoring or other academic support services offered outside the regular school day, at no charge to students or their families, by public or private organizations that have been approved by the state." The program was created by a modification of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act (No Child Left Behind) and targeted parents of low-income students in low-performing schools. This study evaluates its effects on test scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must be oversubscribed, in which case they are "required to make SEdS available to the lowest achieving students among eligible applicants"
#### Constraints
- Available only to low-income students in low-performing schools, and offered outside the regular school day

### Target Learners
- low-income students in low-performing schools

### Target Learning Goals
- academic achievement as measured by test scores
