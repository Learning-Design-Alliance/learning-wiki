# MAP Growth test-score dataset covering 4.9 million U.S. students in Grades 3-8

## Description
The study's evidence base is a large-scale dataset of test scores from U.S. students, described in the abstract as follows: "The data included test scores from 4.9 million U.S. students in Grades 3 through 8." The authors used these scores to model student achievement trends prior to and during the pandemic, with particular focus on growth in 2020-2021. The dataset spans math and reading outcomes across elementary and middle school grades.
