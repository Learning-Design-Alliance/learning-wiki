Find the Fit: personalized planning materials, student text messaging, and advisor training webinars for enhanced college advising¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Find the Fit is an enhanced college-advising intervention evaluated in the Upward Bound program. It "combines personalized planning materials and text messaging for students, and training webinars for advisors." The first report of a six-year study credits it with increasing the number and selectivity of colleges to which students apply and with changing advising within Upward Bound.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivery depends on advisors receiving the program's training webinars and on students receiving the personalized planning materials and text messages.

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated within the Upward Bound program context; the findings reported come from the first report of a six-year study.

Target Learners¶

  • High school students in Upward Bound programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • College application behavior and informed choice of a more selective college

Claims¶

  • Find the Fit led to some changes in advising within Upward Bound and in students' actions related to enrolling in a more selective college [+W]
  • Find the Fit had no impact on the importance students placed on academic quality in choosing a college [~W]
  • Find the Fit led Upward Bound students to apply to more-selective colleges [+M]
  • Find the Fit's selectivity shift came with no higher out-of-pocket cost and no increase in dropping out or transferring down [+M]
  • Find the Fit increased the share of Upward Bound students who applied to four or more colleges [+M]
  • Find the Fit had no impact on the importance students placed on academic quality in choosing a college [~M]
  • Find the Fit did not affect the share of students completing the FAFSA early [~M]
  • Find the Fit previously shifted application behavior, increasing applications to four or more colleges and to very competitive colleges [+M]
