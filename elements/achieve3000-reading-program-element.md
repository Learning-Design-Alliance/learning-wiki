# Achieve3000 leveled-reading program for English Learners

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Achieve3000 is a reading-based edtech program piloted in Cajon Valley Unified with 521 elementary and middle school ELs at risk of becoming Long Term English Language Learners. It "provides students with individually-leveled articles that integrate phonics, vocabulary, and fluency practice along with linguistic scaffolds, through a focus on nonfiction science and social studies." It includes an embedded LevelSet Lexile assessment, usage tracking, and professional learning support including modeling blended instruction for educators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educator training (a pre-pilot session was held) and ongoing vendor support; individual student-level usage and assessment data for evaluation
#### Constraints
- High cost concerned district leaders; the embedded assessment raised familiarity confounds, and the article recommends negotiating limited licenses or 6-month subscriptions

### Target Learners
- Elementary and middle school English Learners at risk of becoming Long Term English Language Learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Improved reading comprehension and English literacy skills

## Claims

- [EL students using Achieve3000 scored significantly higher on adjusted post-pilot Lexile scores than comparison students who did not use the tool](../claims/achieve3000-significant-lexile-gains-vs-comparison.md) [+M]
- [Achieve3000 usage predicted about 3.5 percent of current Lexile scores after controlling for pre-test Lexile, motivation, and age](../claims/achieve3000-usage-predicts-lexile-regression.md) [+M]
- [Student attitudes toward learning English and school declined slightly over both pilots, with the decline in believing school is a good place to learn statistically significant in both districts](../claims/student-attitudes-declined-over-pilots.md) [-M]
