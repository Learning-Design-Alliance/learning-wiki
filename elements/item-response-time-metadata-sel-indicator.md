# Item response time metadata from math assessments as an indicator of social-emotional learning

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This element is the test metadata source the article examines: the length of time students spend answering math test questions, treated as information that might bear on social-emotional learning. The article describes it as "response time metadata" whose use to "measure SEL" shows "promise and limitations." It functions as a low-cost, already-collected byproduct of digital assessment that researchers can analyze for signals about motivation and self-efficacy, rather than a purpose-built SEL instrument.
