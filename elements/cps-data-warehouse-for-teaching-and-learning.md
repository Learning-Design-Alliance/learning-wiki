# Data warehouse linking student and teacher data to support instructional improvement

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report recommends that DRE "develop and manage a data warehouse to support teaching and karning." Currently, "except for ITBS data, there is no source of data to inform and sup- port local or system-wide efforts to improve teach- ing and learning"; data are scattered across the student information system, school databases, teacher grade books, and personnel records. The report states: "An integrated database, especially one IinLng students and their teachers, will greatly facil- itate the kind of research needed to support instruc- tional improvement in CPS."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identification, integration, maintenance, and accessible distribution of student, teacher, and school-specific information
#### Constraints
- The report states this "will be an expensive, long-term project"

### Target Learners
- CPS principals, teachers, and central office staff

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding the relationship between teacher practices, teacher learning, and student learning
