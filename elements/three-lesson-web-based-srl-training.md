# Three-Lesson Web-Based SRL Training

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 12 claims (9 for, 3 mixed) · 7 studies (4 causal, 2 quant-synthesis, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 3 of 7 report an effect size · 12 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based training on self-regulated learning that "can be attended by virtually unlimited numbers of participants" choosing their own time and place. "The WBT comprises three lessons of approximately 90 min each", covering the pre-action, action and post-action phases, and using videos, presentations, self-tests, exercises and bulletin boards. In this study lessons unlocked at 1-week intervals, videos used real actors, and mean self-reported compliance was 82.18%.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Three lessons unlocked consecutively in 1-week intervals, each worked through within three days
- Lesson content mapped to the process model phases: goal-setting and time management; volition, cognitive and metacognitive strategies; attribution, reflection and motivation
- Exercises that transfer strategies to learners' own course, such as a SMART learning goal and a four-week learning plan
#### Constraints
- Tested only in a four-week voluntary online mathematics preparation course with a predominantly male sample

### Target Learners
- Prospective university students in mathematically oriented fields (computer science, civil engineering, mechanical engineering, mathematics) taking a voluntary online mathematics preparation course

### Target Learning Goals
- Declarative SRL knowledge
- Self-reported SRL behavior, especially planning and metacognition
- Self-efficacy
