# Stress Awareness field test measures for Spanish suprasegmental phonology

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Beginning Fall 2024, NWEA field tested three new Stress Awareness measures: Syllable Segmentation, Stressed Syllable, and Word Classification, all untimed. The guide states "Stress awareness is the strongest predictor of reading success in Spanish and is directly linked to word recognition (Levis, 2018)," categorizing it under suprasegmental phonology, measuring abilities that Phonological Awareness measures do not.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Released initially as field test measures with limited reporting before operational release
#### Constraints
- Currently field test only; operational release is stated as expected in the future

### Target Learners
- Students learning to read in Spanish

### Target Learning Goals
- segmenting words into syllables
- identifying the stressed syllable
- classifying words by stress position
