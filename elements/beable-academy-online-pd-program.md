## Description
Beable Academy is the platform's accompanying professional development offering. Educators have "unlimited access to the Beable Academy, the self-paced, online professional development program," alongside a comprehensive orientation for teachers and administrators that can be onsite or online, synchronous or asynchronous, plus ongoing support including account management and help desk access.
