Description¶

The data source for this brief is a nationwide sample of MAP Growth scores for middle school students, which NWEA researchers used to study Algebra placement. The description states the researchers worked "Using a nationwide sample of MAP Growth scores for middle schoolers" to examine readiness and enrollment. It serves as the evidence base for the brief's placement and outcome analyses.
