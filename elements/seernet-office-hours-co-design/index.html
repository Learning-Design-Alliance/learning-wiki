SEERNet Office Hours: a co-design process pairing practitioners and researchers to generate research questions for five Digital Learning Platforms¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Office Hours is a SEERNet initiative that brings practitioners and researchers together over roughly a 3-month period to co-design research questions grounded in real-world challenges with representatives from the five DLPs. Conversations were synthesized by a Digital Promise researcher, participants gave feedback, and "The output was five research question documents, one customized for each DLP." The sessions drew 31 participants, of whom 65% identified as researcher, 6% as practitioner, and 29% as both.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Facilitator synthesis of conversations and participant feedback on drafted research questions
  • Strategic communication before sessions, including pre-readings and an introductory meeting

Constraints¶

  • The report notes Office Hours do not reflect a sustained feedback loop where large-scale research is conducted

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 educators from Title I districts and higher education practitioners, including Minority Serving Institutions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Co-designing education technology research questions addressing personalization, equity, and problems of practice

Claims¶

  • Practitioners identified classroom complexities and fidelity-of-implementation variability as considerations experimental research should account for beyond causal effects [+W]
  • Practitioner engagement initiatives reflected buy-in rather than ownership because leadership developed ideas and decisions before seeking context-expert input [~W]

Related Elements¶

  • SEERNet network connecting platforms, researchers, and educators

Examples¶

  • Run conversational office hours in which practitioners and researchers co-design research questions with platform representatives
  • Engaging practitioners through an advisory board, co-design office hours, and guiding-principles publications
