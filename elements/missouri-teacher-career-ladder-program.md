# Missouri Teacher Career Ladder program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A state teacher performance-incentive program operating in Missouri since 1987, in which districts participate and teachers can earn career-ladder rewards. The article evaluates it as a district-level program using "longitudinal data on district math and reading scores for 524 Missouri school districts over a nine-year period," comparing participating with matched nonparticipating districts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District participation in the Career Ladder program
#### Constraints
- The article examines effects on student achievement only; companion papers on program operations and teacher retention were not yet complete

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in participating Missouri school districts

### Target Learning Goals
