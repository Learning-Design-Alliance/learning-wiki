## Description
UBMS is a math and science initiative established in 1990 within Upward Bound, a federal grant program for "disadvantaged high school students" preparing for college. It "awards grants to colleges and universities to provide participants with hands-on experience in laboratories, computer facilities, and at field sites," plus learning from mathematicians and scientists. By 2004 there were 127 projects serving nearly 7,000 students at a total cost of $33 million.
