NWEA technical brief on COVID's impact on science achievement, 2019–2024¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), q1–q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A technical brief published by NWEA Research in September 2024 by Susan Kowalski, Scott J. Peters, Megan Kuhfeld, Gustave Robinson, and Karyn Lewis. Per its description, it is "the accompanying technical brief to the new report" examining "the pandemic's impacts on academic achievement and gain in Science," continuing NWEA's ongoing research program on COVID-19 and associated school closures and student learning. The page tags it under the topics COVID-19 & schools, Equity, and Growth.
