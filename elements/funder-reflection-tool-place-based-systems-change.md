# Funder reflection and action tool for place-based systems change investments

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces a tool intended for funders who have chosen place-based systems change strategies. As printed, "This tool is intended to help funders who have elected to invest in place-based systems change strategies assess the extent to which their strategic intent, culture, and capacity can support complementary dimensions of systems change." The tool operates as a structured self-assessment instrument through which funders examine their own organizations rather than external programs, and it is positioned as a step toward clarifying investment focus.
