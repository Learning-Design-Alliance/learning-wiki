Coursemojo: AI-powered teaching assistant platform for middle school ELA¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Coursemojo is an AI-powered teaching and learning platform for English Language Arts instruction. Its intelligent assistant, named Mojo, "works alongside classroom teachers to provide individualized support to every student while maintaining the collaborative, social nature of classroom learning." It integrates with existing curricula rather than replacing instruction. Implementation requires a device per student, pre-launch training, and no additional staffing.

Claims¶

  • Stronger Coursemojo outcomes were associated with more consistent use of 2.5 or more days per week [+M]
  • Coursemojo use narrowed achievement gaps for students with disabilities and economically disadvantaged students in Tennessee [+M]
  • Coursemojo qualifies for a Moderate evidence rating based on positive ELA impacts across two studies [+M]
  • Coursemojo use was associated with higher sixth-grade ELA performance in a Tennessee quasi-experiment [+M]
  • Coursemojo use was associated with higher STAAR reading and NWEA MAP scores in a Texas quasi-experiment [+M]
