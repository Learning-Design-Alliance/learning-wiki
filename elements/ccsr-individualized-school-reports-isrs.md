# Individualized school reports (ISRs) tracking schools on core research-linked indicators

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Individualized survey reports are customized data products that bring CCSR research findings to each school: 'individualized survey reports (isrs) allow educators to track over time their status on critical indicators of school environment that research has linked to student achievement.' Each participating school receives a report comparing its performance to similar schools on the five essential supports, allowing schools to chart progress over time, revisit key improvement concepts, and build their own coherent conceptual models. CCSR is developing real-time and topic-customized ISRs, such as around freshman year performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing biennial survey administration and validated measures that live on as core indicators across studies
#### Constraints
- The report positions ISRs as extending the window of time research stays relevant, not as standalone improvement tools

### Target Learners
- School-based educators—principals, teachers, and school communities—in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Tracking school status on indicators of school environment linked to student achievement and guiding improvement planning
