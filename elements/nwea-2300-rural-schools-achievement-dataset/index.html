National analysis dataset covering over 2,300 rural US schools' math and reading achievement¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief draws on a large national dataset of rural schools to describe student achievement. The announcement states it uses "data from over 2,300 rural schools across the US" to provide "unique insight into math and reading achievement of students in rural schools." The dataset supports descriptive analysis of rural achievement and growth patterns nationwide.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires achievement and growth data covering rural schools across the United States, as the analysis depends on "data from over 2,300 rural schools across the US"

Constraints¶

  • Scope is limited to rural schools; the brief's described purpose is understanding "students in rural schools" specifically

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in rural US schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Math achievement
  • Reading achievement
  • Student growth over time
