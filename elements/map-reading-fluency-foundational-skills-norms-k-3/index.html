MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills achievement and growth norms for Grades K-3 in three domains¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report documents achievement and growth user norms for English MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills, covering "Phonological Awareness, Phonics & Word Recognition, and Language Comprehension" for Grades K-3. Achievement norms give percentile rankings for fall, winter, and spring; growth norms cover fall-to-winter, winter-to-spring, and fall-to-spring gains. Snapshot tables (Tables 3.3-3.14) present these norms for each grade and domain, scheduled for release in Summer 2022.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Scores must come from the English version of the assessment; the report states the norms are only applicable to the English version

Constraints¶

  • Norms are based on 2018-2019 test administration data, chosen as the most recent school year unaffected by COVID-19

Target Learners¶

  • Students in Grades K-3 taking the English MAP Reading Fluency assessment

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Early reading foundational skills: phonological awareness, phonics and word recognition, and language comprehension

Claims¶

  • Cross-term Foundational Skills scores were moderately correlated, with correlations ranging from 0.48 to 0.82 depending on domain and grade [+W]
  • Foundational Skills test scores generally increased across terms and grades, with less growth observed at higher grades [+W]
  • The multivariate normality assumption of the norming model was judged met for all domains and grades, despite some tail deviations in Q-Q plots [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • MAP Reading Fluency Adaptive Oral Reading default form routing students to Oral Reading Fluency or Foundational Skills tracks
  • Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills assessment
