KIPP Leadership Design Fellowship (KLDF) program disseminating school leadership development strategies outside KIPP¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The third KIPP school leadership program examined in the evaluation, described as "the KIPP Leadership Design Fellowship (KLDF) program, which disseminates effective school leadership development strategies outside of KIPP". It extends KIPP's leadership preparation approaches to other schools and the broader principal preparation field.
