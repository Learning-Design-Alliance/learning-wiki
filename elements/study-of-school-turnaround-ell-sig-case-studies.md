# Study of School Turnaround: case studies of 11 SIG schools with high ELL enrollments

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Study of School Turnaround is a multi-year evaluation examining school improvement in SIG-funded schools. This brief draws on "a purposive sample of 35 case study schools receiving federal funds through the School Improvement Grants (SIG) program over a three-year period (2010–11 to 2012–13 school years)" and focuses on 11 of these schools with high proportions of ELL students, a median of 45 percent ELLs, describing staffing strategies and professional development efforts to build teacher capacity for serving ELLs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SIG-funded schools participating in the three-year case study (2010–11 to 2012–13)
#### Constraints
- Findings are from a purposive sample of 11 SIG schools with high ELL proportions and may not generalize to other schools

### Target Learners
- English Language Learners in K-12 SIG schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving teachers' capacity to serve English Language Learners
