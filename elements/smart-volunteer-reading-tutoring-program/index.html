SMART® volunteer one-on-one reading tutoring program with book giveaways for at-risk K-2 readers¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Start Making a Reader Today® is a low-cost volunteer tutoring program for students in grades K-2 at risk of reading failure, operating in Oregon schools where at least 40% of students are eligible for free or reduced-price lunch. Volunteer tutors "read one-on-one with students twice a week for half an hour," typically with one volunteer working with two children. The program also gives each student two new books a month to encourage family reading, and overall program cost runs approximately $300 a year per child.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A part-time school coordinator who recruits and trains volunteers, is present during program hours, schedules reading sessions, and serves as primary contact for school personnel
  • Volunteer tutors who undergo a 1–2 hour training and use a handbook outlining the four reading strategies
  • Schools where at least 40% of students are eligible for free or reduced-price lunch

Constraints¶

  • Delivered as a supplement to the regular reading curriculum, not a replacement for classroom instruction

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades K-2 at risk of reading failure in high-poverty schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Beginning reading: alphabetics, fluency, and comprehension

Claims¶

  • SMART® effects on comprehension were not statistically significant on either subtest, though the domain-average effect size was substantively important [+W]
  • The evidence base for SMART® is a single small-extent RCT, capping the intervention rating at potentially positive effects [+W]
  • After one year of SMART® intervention, only the fluency outcome was statistically significant, while alphabetics and comprehension effects were not [+W]
  • SMART® tutoring produced statistically significant positive effects on oral reading fluency on both first- and second-grade passages [+W]
  • SMART® tutoring produced a statistically significant positive effect on first-graders' word identification after two years of intervention [+W]
