Amira — an AI-based personal reading tutor that assesses, tutors, and reports on oral reading¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Amira is "a personal artificial intelligent tutor program" that provides students with "an always available, infinitely patient, and highly-trained personal reading tutor". It assesses oral reading fluency, analyzes errors to pinpoint strengths and weaknesses, listens as a student reads aloud and intervenes when helpful, recommends appropriately challenging stories, and generates diagnostic reports for teachers and parents. It is browser-based and works on Chromebooks, PCs, Macs, iPads, and tablets without special software.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Devices should have at least 4 GB of RAM and a working microphone; headphones with a mic are encouraged in noisier classrooms.
  • A current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari (required on iPads), or Firefox is recommended.

Constraints¶

  • Amira operates as a closed, secure system that never accesses the open internet.

Target Learners¶

  • kindergarten and first grade students
  • students in grades 1–4 who struggled with reading

Target Learning Goals¶

  • early reading outcomes
  • oral reading fluency

Claims¶

  • In a matched comparison study, Amira students outperformed matched controls on end-of-year DIBELS by +0.26 in kindergarten and +0.06 in first grade [+W]
  • Across two studies totaling 15,602 students, Amira shows consistent positive impacts on early reading, qualifying it for a Moderate rating with an average effect size of +0.15 [+W]
  • In a student-randomized trial, struggling readers in grades 1–4 who used Amira scored significantly higher on the Woodcock Reading Mastery Test Total Reading Composite (effect size = +0.64) than Sustained Silent Reading controls [+M]

Examples¶

  • Deploy Amira as a browser-based program integrated with district systems through Single Sign-On and automated rostering
  • Implement Amira with no special staff qualifications, roughly two hours of initial teacher training plus a one-hour follow-up data deep-dive
