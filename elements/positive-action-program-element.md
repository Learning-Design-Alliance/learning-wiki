# Positive Action whole-school program: structured lessons, teacher manuals, and parallel parent handbooks

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Positive Action is a whole-school reform strategy intended to improve social-emotional, attendance, and achievement outcomes by building school climate, self-control, goal-setting, problem-solving, persistence, and other skills. Students learn through "structured discussions and activities, games, and role plays." Teachers receive detailed manuals supporting 140 lessons of 15-20 minutes per year, and parents receive handbooks paralleling the school lessons. No additional staff or technology are required.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers receive detailed manuals and materials to support the 140 15-20 minute lessons per year
- Administrators, faculty and support staff receive at least 3 to 5 hours orientation training in the first year and 2-4 hour sessions for each successive year
#### Constraints
- No additional staff required
- Technology: none required

### Target Learners
- school students in whole-school implementation

### Target Learning Goals
- social-emotional skills
- self-control
- goal-setting
- problem-solving
- persistence
- attendance
- math and reading achievement
