# 5Essentials Survey Supportive Environment measures

## Description
The Supportive Environment essential of the 5Essentials Survey is defined based on students' and teachers' reports on six measures: peer support for academic work, academic personalism (teacher support), safety, student-teacher trust, school-wide future orientation, and expectations for post-secondary education. For example, students report whether "their teacher is willing to give extra help on schoolwork and notices if they have trouble learning something." The brief links this element to schools' ability to reduce fear of bullying.
