Talent Development High School whole-school reform model¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

TDHS is described as "a whole-school reform model that focuses on reading and math". It provides a double dose of reading and math to ninth graders in high-poverty schools, with the reading approach called Strategic Reading and Student Team Literature. The model is implemented at school level with dedicated staffing and extensive professional development support.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • One FTE English teacher for each cohort of 75-100 students, plus if possible one English Instructional Coach provided by the school or district
  • Suggested professional development of approximately 20 days of Johns Hopkins Instructional Facilitator support

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Ninth graders in high-poverty high schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading comprehension and fluency
  • Mathematics

Claims¶

  • TDHS Strategic Reading produced a positive effect of +0.32 on CTBS Terra Nova reading outcomes for ninth graders compared to a control group in one Baltimore study [+W]
  • Teachers implementing TDHS Strategic Reading reported positive experiences and greater impact on student reading than traditional curricula [+W]
