# DreamBox Learning adaptive online mathematics program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 3 against) · 7 studies (6 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 5 of 7 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
DreamBox Learning is "a supplemental online mathematics program that provides adaptive instruction for students in grades K–5 and focuses on number and operations, place value, and number sense." The curriculum comprises more than 720 online lessons split into primary (K–2) and intermediate (3–5) levels, delivered through math games and puzzles using virtual manipulatives. As students interact, "the lesson sequence, difficulty, pace, and number of hints are adapted in real time." It can be used in classrooms, small groups, or independently, with reporting tools for teachers and administrators, and the developer recommends at least 90 minutes per week.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need access to computers; instructional sessions in the reviewed study were conducted in a computer lab, and the developer recommends at least 90 minutes a week on the program.
#### Constraints
- The program is supplemental and focuses on number and operations, place value, and number sense; in the reviewed study, progress and use information from the software was not used to modify face-to-face mathematics instruction.

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students in grades K–5

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematics achievement, including number and operations, place value, and number sense
