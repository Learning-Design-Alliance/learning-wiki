# Communication Channels

## Description
Communication channels are the element in which learners use designated tools or spaces to coordinate, discuss, ask questions, and share updates during a learning activity. They are useful when collaboration depends on reliable communication pathways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A channel matched to the type of interaction needed**
- **Norms for how and when the channel should be used**
#### Constraints
- **Too many channels can fragment attention and coordination**

### Target Learning Goals
- Support coordination, discussion, and timely communication.
