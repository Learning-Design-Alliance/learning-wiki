Seven-item parent-reported AAC implementation experience score¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A parent-reported questionnaire score measuring experiences during AAC implementation, covering professional support, follow-up, confidence using AAC, perceived individual fit, team cooperation, and consideration of emotional and psychological needs. Items are rated on four ordered options (never, sometimes, mostly, always) scored 0–3, giving a total range of 0–21. The article reports that "The final AAC experience score was calculated from seven positively worded experience items and showed good internal consistency (Cronbach’s α = 0.841)"; the observed median was 16.0 (IQR 12.75–19.0).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administration to parents or caregivers with at least six months of AAC exposure; scoring excludes negatively worded acceptance-difficulty items, which are analyzed separately

Constraints¶

  • Developed for this study via literature review, expert panel review, and pilot testing with 10 parents; the article states it was not a formally validated psychometric instrument

Target Learners¶

  • Parents and primary caregivers of children with autism using AAC

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Assessing parent-reported quality of AAC implementation experiences, including professional support and family-centered service dimensions

Claims¶

  • Exploratory subgroup and correlation analyses show mostly no significant differences in benefit or experience scores by child and family characteristics [+W]
