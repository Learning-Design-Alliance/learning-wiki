# TurnNJ whole-school turnaround project

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
TurnNJ is a project of Uncommon Schools, "a nonprofit charter management organization that starts and manages charter schools," intended "to continue and expand Uncommon's whole-school turnaround efforts in six schools in Camden and Newark." It was supported by a 2016 i3 grant from the U.S. Department of Education and evaluated with propensity score matching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Operates as part of broader urban renewal initiatives and school turnaround efforts in Camden and Newark, New Jersey
#### Constraints
- Outcomes could be examined for only three of the six TurnNJ schools

### Target Learners
- Students in turnaround charter schools in Camden and Newark, New Jersey

### Target Learning Goals
- Math achievement
- English language arts achievement
