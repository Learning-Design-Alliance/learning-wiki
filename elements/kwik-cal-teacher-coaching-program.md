# KWiK: teacher coaching plus Computer Assisted Learning for mastery-based math practice

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The KWiK program encourages mastery learning through technology paired with proactive continuous teacher support. In elementary and middle school mathematics, "teachers receive weekly coaching in how to use Computer Assisted Learning (CAL) for helping students follow a customized roadmap of incremental progress." The program was implemented with hundreds of teachers across partner districts and evaluated in two field experiments.
