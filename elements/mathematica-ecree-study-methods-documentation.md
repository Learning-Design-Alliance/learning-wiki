# Mathematica Ecree study methods documentation

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
A methods summary accompanying a series of findings briefs about the Ecree study. It documents "the study design, sample, data sources, measures, and analytic methods used to produce the findings reported in those briefs." The study was designed and conducted by the Mathematica study team in collaboration with Ecree to examine tool use and impacts on student and teacher outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accompanies a series of briefs summarizing results of the Ecree study for different audiences
#### Constraints
- Documents methods only; the findings themselves are reported in the accompanying briefs
