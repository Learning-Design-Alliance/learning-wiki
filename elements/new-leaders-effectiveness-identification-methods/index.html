New Leaders school and teacher effectiveness identification methods¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A set of methods designed by Mathematica Policy Research under contract with New Leaders to identify effective schools and teachers. The approach used for each partner differs, depending on the priorities of the partner and the type of information available to measure school and teacher performance. The first-year application identified effective schools for a consortium of 99 charter schools in 18 states and the District of Columbia.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Performance data of a type sufficient to measure school and teacher performance must be available for the partner organization.

Constraints¶

  • The approach differs by partner, depending on partner priorities and the type of information available; the model was to be revised in future years as additional data became available.

Target Learners¶

  • Students in the 99 charter schools in the consortium

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Identifying effective schools and teachers to support school improvement
