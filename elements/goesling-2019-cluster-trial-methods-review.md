# Methodological review and application search underlying the practical guide

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The evidence base for the guide is a two-part review: the author "reviewed summaries of the methodological literature on cluster randomized trials" and then "conducted a search to identify practical applications of these methods in school health research." This synthesis produced the six-decision framework and the practical recommendations, positioning the guide as a bridge between methodological literature and applied school health evaluation practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to summaries of the methodological literature and a search strategy for applied school health studies
#### Constraints
- The review is based on summaries of the methodological literature rather than a systematic meta-analysis

### Target Learners
- school health researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- designing and conducting rigorous cluster randomized evaluations
