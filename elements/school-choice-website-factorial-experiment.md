# Randomized factorial experiment of school choice website display formats

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A randomized factorial experiment in which 3,500 low-income parents of school-aged children chose schools for their children in a hypothetical school district while school information was displayed in varying formats. The design manipulated default ordering, data representation (icons, graphs, or just numbers), and summary conciseness, and measured chosen schools, understanding, satisfaction, and ease of use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Parents of school-aged children as participants
- A hypothetical school district with displayable school information
#### Constraints
- Choices were made in a hypothetical school district rather than a live enrollment system

### Target Learners
- low-income parents of school-aged children

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding school performance information for choice
