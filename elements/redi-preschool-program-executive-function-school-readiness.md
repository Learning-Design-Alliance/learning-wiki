# REDI preschool program combining social-emotional learning with early literacy activities

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
REDI (Research Based, Developmentally Informed) is a preschool program "designed primarily to enhance executive functions and school readiness among disadvantaged students." It delivers the Preschool PATHS social-emotional learning curriculum alongside "an interactive storybook reading and retelling approach, along with phonological awareness training and alphabet center activities," and is taught by classroom teachers with inservice training and weekly mentoring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Taught by classroom teachers
- Teachers receive two to four days of inservice plus weekly mentoring from local consultants

### Target Learning Goals
