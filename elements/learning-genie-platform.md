# Learning Genie platform

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Learning Genie is an AI-supported instructional planning and documentation platform for preschool through grade 12 that helps educators design "research-based, standards-aligned instruction using Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles." It provides AI-assisted unit design and lesson-planning tools supporting differentiation, inclusion, and alignment with high-quality instructional materials, while letting teachers integrate student evidence, contextual learning needs, and learner profiles. Digital portfolios, DRDP-aligned assessment tools, and multimodal documentation capture student voice and support student agency; the platform also supports collaboration, bilingual family communication, and reporting through data-system integrations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom teachers use it as part of their regular instructional and assessment workflow; a site- or district-level implementation lead coordinates account setup, SIS integration, and technical support during initial rollout.
#### Constraints
- Schools require reliable internet access; it operates on standard classroom devices (tablets, Chromebooks, desktops) using modern browsers.

### Target Learners
- prekindergarten through grade 12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- standards-aligned instruction in language, literacy, math, and social-emotional development
