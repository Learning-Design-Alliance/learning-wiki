# SEERNet hub of five digital learning platforms enabling large-scale education research

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
SEERNet is a hub of five digital learning platforms (DLPs), in K-12 or higher education, that enable researchers to conduct research and collect data on large numbers of students. The article states the DLPs "bring the power of Randomized Control Trials (RCTs) and can potentially connect interested researchers to large numbers of students to perform experimental work." The platforms are working to align with the SEER Standards around equity, and an important concern they are actively working to solve is protecting the identity, privacy, and data of students involved.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Platforms must determine how to protect the identity, privacy, and data of the students involved
#### Constraints
- In the short-term, the DLPs have pressing development schedules and cannot immediately do more

### Target Learners
- K-12 and higher education students using the platforms
- third-party researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- supporting equity-relevant education research at scale
