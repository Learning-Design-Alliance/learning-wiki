Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition® (CIRC) reading and writing program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

CIRC is a reading and writing program for students in grades 2 through 6 with three principal elements: "story-related activities, direct instruction in reading comprehension, and integrated language arts/ writing". Daily 90-minute lessons have mixed-ability teams reading, discussing, rereading for fluency, and working through drafting, revising, and editing, with team-based rewards for motivation. A Spanish version is available for grades 2 through 5, and the program is distributed by the Success for All Foundation as part of the Success for All model or stand-alone.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Daily 90-minute lessons; teacher training including a two-day session plus phone or on-site technical support; materials and training at approximately $150 per student

Constraints¶

  • Evidence reviewed covers grades 2 through 6 in US elementary schools; no qualifying studies examined alphabetics or reading fluency domains

Target Learners¶

  • students in grades 2 through 6
  • adolescent learners in grades 4–12 samples

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading comprehension
  • writing and integrated language arts
  • vocabulary and decoding

Claims¶

  • Stevens and Slavin (1995) found statistically significant positive effects of CIRC on CAT Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary subtests, while Jewell (1994) found no statistically significant effects [+W]
  • CIRC showed a statistically significant positive effect on CAT Language Expression but not CAT Language Mechanics in Stevens and Slavin (1995), and no significant effect on the Bass Academic Skills Sample Reading Proficiency subtest in Jewell (1994) [+W]
  • WWC finds Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition® has potentially positive effects on comprehension for adolescent learners (average improvement index +7 percentile points) [+W]
  • WWC finds CIRC has potentially positive effects on general literacy achievement for adolescent learners (average improvement index +2 percentile points) [+W]
