# NWEA test-score dataset of over 2.5 million kindergarten through eighth-grade students across three cohorts (2015-2018)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A large-scale longitudinal test-score dataset used to examine seasonal patterns of racial/ethnic achievement gaps. The article states it draws on "test scores from over 2.5 million kindergarten to eighth-grade students" and follows "three different cohorts of students from 2015 to 2018," enabling comparison of gap growth when school is in versus out of session across grades K-8.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires seasonal test-score data allowing within-student growth comparisons across school and summer periods
#### Constraints
- Restricted to students in the NWEA assessment population; the abstract notes prior seasonal studies were restricted to early grades, which this dataset extends through eighth grade

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten through eighth-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic skills in reading and mathematics as measured by standardized achievement tests
