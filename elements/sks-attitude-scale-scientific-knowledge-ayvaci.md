# Attitude Scale towards Scientific Knowledge (SKS)

## Description
The attitude scale towards scientific knowledge, developed by Ayvacı (2007), is the study's second quantitative instrument, applied as pre-test, post-test and retention test. The article states "The scale contains 25 items about SK, which are in five-point Likert type." Items were developed from components of science and the nature of scientific knowledge in the literature, and after language-expert review the data analysis yielded factor load values of .515–.885, a total variance percent of 42%, and internal consistency of 0.73.
