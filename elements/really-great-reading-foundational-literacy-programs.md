# Really Great Reading foundational literacy programs (Countdown, Blast, HD Word)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Really Great Reading offers structured foundational literacy curricula grounded in the science of reading, providing "explicit instruction in phonological awareness, phonics, word recognition, decoding, encoding, and fluency." The programs span Countdown (Kindergarten), Blast (Grade 1), and HD Word (Grades 2–3), with print and online teacher tools. Instruction is "systematic and cumulative" and serves learners across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 support.
