# Readable English: adaptive conversion tool plus scripted phonics-to-comprehension lessons

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Readable English is a reading intervention program that "utilizes adaptive technology, including a conversion tool that aids in reading grade-level curriculum, and scripted lessons". The scripted lessons are sequenced: they "initially focus on phonics and phonemic skills, later incorporates word-level decoding and encoding, and ultimately combines fluency skills with reading comprehension, writing, and vocabulary building". It was evaluated with grade 6-8 MTSS/RTI Tier 2-3 and Special Education students in rural Indiana districts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Computer or tablet for student use, Smart Board for small group/whole class use
- Small group reading instructor/teacher
- Two days of training prior to implementation, plus weekly meetings with an assigned Readable English reading coach hired by the district
#### Constraints
- Received a Promising rather than Moderate rating because the study sample size is just shy of the 350-student requirement

### Target Learners
- Struggling readers in grades 6-8, including MTSS/RTI Tiers 2 and 3 and Special Education students

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading fluency
- Reading comprehension
- Decoding and encoding
- Vocabulary
