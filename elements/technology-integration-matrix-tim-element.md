# The Technology Integration Matrix (TIM) and the Rubric for Effective Teacher Technology Use as evaluation tools for technology coaching

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Technology Integration Matrix, from the Florida Center for Instructional Technology at the University of South Florida, "describes five characteristics of learning environments (active, collaborative, constructive, authentic, and goal-directed) that are associated with five levels of technology integration (entry, adoption, adaptation, infusion, and transformation)." The paper proposes combining the matrix with coach roles and goals to evaluate technology coaching, alongside the Rubric for Effective Teacher Technology Use, which describes three proficiency levels across four Danielson-framework domains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Concrete evaluation tools are to be created once goals have been determined in the context of a specific program; the standards and matrices are guidelines only
#### Constraints
- The paper states these standards are only guidelines to support the definition of goals

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers and technology coaches

### Target Learning Goals
- Evaluating progress of technology integration and the success of coaching programs
