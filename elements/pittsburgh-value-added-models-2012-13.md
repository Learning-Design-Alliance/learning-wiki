# Value-added models estimating teacher and school contributions to student achievement in Pittsburgh Public Schools

## Description
Mathematica developed value-added models for Pittsburgh Public Schools that "aim to estimate the contributions of individual teachers and schools to the achievement of their students." The models were created at the request of the district and the Pittsburgh Federation of Teachers, and this report summarizes their current use for assessing educational quality in the district, updating an earlier 2012 report on the same topic.
