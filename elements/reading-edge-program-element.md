# The Reading Edge whole-school cooperative learning reading program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Reading Edge is "a whole-school cooperative learning approach to reading instruction" adapted from Student Team Reading. Students work in "4-5 member teams to help each other build reading skills," engaging in partner reading, story retelling, story-related writing, word mastery, and story-structure activities to prepare for individual assessments. Instruction includes explicit teaching of metacognitive skills such as clarification, summarization, predication, and graphic organizers. It is delivered by classroom teachers during usual Language Arts/Reading time.
