Heggerty's Bridge to Reading foundational skills curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Bridge to Reading is a teacher-led foundational skills curriculum that pairs explicit phonics instruction with phonemic awareness lessons for daily instruction. The article states it "provides all the components teachers need for comprehensive foundational skills instruction in 30 minutes a day, within the literacy block." It was evaluated in two studies across kindergarten through second grade.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires an educator to deliver classroom instruction as a teacher-led curriculum

Constraints¶

  • Professional development for teachers implementing Bridge to Reading is recommended, but it is optional

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten students
  • First-grade students
  • Second-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Foundational reading skills including phonics, phonemic awareness, and decoding

Claims¶

  • First graders using Bridge to Reading significantly outperformed control-school peers on MAP Growth Reading (ES +0.16) after one semester [+W]
  • Kindergarteners using Bridge to Reading showed greater growth in phonological awareness and phonics/word recognition (ES +0.22 each) [+W]
  • Students receiving special education services showed significant gains (ES +0.36) in the Michigan Tier 2 study [+W]
  • As a Tier 2 intervention, Bridge to Reading schools significantly outperformed comparison peers on spring NWEA MAP Reading (ES +0.33), with significant effects at both grade levels [+W]
  • The weighted average effect size across both Bridge to Reading studies is +0.23, qualifying the program for a Moderate rating [+W]
