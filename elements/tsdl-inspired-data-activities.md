## Description
New activities undertaken by states and districts that grew out of the TSDL project. The brief reports that "New TSDL-inspired activities included enhancing roster verification systems, integrating and linking data systems, and implementing new ways to present and use data." These activities extend the project's aim of bolstering linked teacher-student data in pilot states and districts.
