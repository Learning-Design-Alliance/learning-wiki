# Reflection Activities

## Description
Reflection activities are structured tasks that ask learners to examine what they did, understood, or would change next. They are useful when reflection needs to be made visible and actionable rather than left implicit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A recent task, experience, or decision to reflect on**
- **Prompts that focus attention on evidence, process, or next steps**
- **A use for the reflection in later action or planning**
#### Constraints
- **Generic prompts often produce shallow responses**
- **Over-frequent reflection can become compliance rather than insight**

### Target Learning Goals
- Support metacognition, transfer, and self-regulation.
