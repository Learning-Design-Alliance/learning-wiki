Rotating materials kits supplying all hands-on math and science instructional materials¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A supporting component of AMSTI is the provision of complete instructional materials to teachers. The article states that teachers "are provided with all necessary materials, including teacher and student guides, student assessments, software, manipulatives, and hands-on activities, all organized in 'kits' that are rotated among schools." The kits bundle guides, assessments, software, manipulatives, and hands-on activities so that inquiry-based lessons can be enacted without teachers sourcing materials themselves.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Kits must be organized and rotated among participating schools.
