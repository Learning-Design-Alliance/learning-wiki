WAT-C (Writing Analytics Toolkit Classroom), an open-source web platform providing formative writing feedback¶

Description¶

WAT-C is the classroom-facing implementation of the Writing Analytics Toolkit, an open-source platform providing formative feedback on student writing using natural language processing. The alpha version evaluated here "generates analytics in three categories for persuasive and source-based essays: basic analytics (e.g., word count, sentence length), a holistic overall score (1–6 scale), and advanced analytics" developed via NLP and principal component analysis. It presents 13 advanced metrics (e.g., Sophisticated Wording, Word Variety, Sentence Cohesion) on five-level continua with clickable pop-up explanations, revision suggestions, and example use cases, and includes teacher features for assigning tasks, selecting metrics, uploading rubrics, and tracking submissions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires instructor configuration of tasks, selected metrics, and uploaded rubrics or mentor texts

Constraints¶

  • The alpha version was revised away from predictive scoring toward descriptive feedback after earlier instructors preferred to remain the primary evaluator of writing quality

Target Learners¶

  • College students in entry-level English writing courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Writing quality, revision, and metric-aligned writing behaviors such as lexical sophistication, sentence cohesion, and idea development

Claims¶

  • AWE feedback has limited depth and questionable accuracy for evaluating complex writing aspects [~M] — a general caution on automated writing feedback that applies to WAT-C's advanced analytics

Related Elements¶

  • Feedback — WAT-C gives formative feedback on student writing
  • Five-domain TAM survey of WAT-C instructors — the instrument used to evaluate instructors' acceptance of this platform
  • Balance human and automated feedback in writing instruction
