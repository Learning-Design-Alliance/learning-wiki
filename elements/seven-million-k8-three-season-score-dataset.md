# Multi-season math and reading score dataset for over seven million K-8 students (2016-17)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's empirical resource is a dataset of math and reading test scores collected at three points in the school year: "across the fall, winter, and spring of the 2016-17 school year." It covers over seven million students in kindergarten through 8th grade and serves as the basis for testing whether within-year growth is linear, with separate analyses of reading and math.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires test scores from multiple within-year time points (fall, winter, spring) to distinguish within-year from summer growth patterns
#### Constraints
- Scope is kindergarten through 8th grade students in the 2016-17 school year

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten through 8th grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate measurement of within-year achievement growth in math and reading
