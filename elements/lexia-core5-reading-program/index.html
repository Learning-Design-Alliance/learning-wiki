Lexia Core5 Reading blended literacy program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Lexia Core5 Reading is a blended learning program aiming to "accelerate the development of fundamental literacy skills for students of all abilities in grades pre-K-5". It provides "explicit, systematic instruction through personalized learning paths", adapts to target skill gaps as they emerge, and equips teachers with data and instructional resources for personalized offline instruction. An embedded assessment supplies ongoing actionable data, and an online student dashboard encourages students to take ownership of their learning. It runs on PC, Mac, Chromebook, iPad, and Android devices.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • No special qualifications or certification are required to implement Core5.

Constraints¶

  • The evidence rating is Promising, based on one study of students receiving special education support for reading difficulties in grades K-5.

  • Students in grades pre-K-5 of all abilities, including students receiving special education support for reading difficulties

Target Learning Goals¶

Claims¶

  • Core5 use for one year yields significantly higher MAP reading scores than control for K-5 special education students (ES = +0.23) [+W]
