# MAP Growth math assessment data corpus for accessibility research

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study draws on MAP Growth math assessment data from two populations: about 29.5 million students taking standard versions of the assessment and 48,845 students taking accessible versions. This large-scale corpus enabled the researchers to "identified high-quality items, those that measured achievement for both students with and without VI equally well, and low-quality items, which showed differences between the two groups of students." The corpus is the empirical foundation for the item-quality analysis and the machine-learning prediction work described in the brief.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to MAP Growth assessment response data for both standard and accessible test versions
#### Constraints
- The accessible-version sample (48,845 students) is much smaller than the standard-version sample (about 29.5 million students)

### Target Learners
- students with visual impairments
- students without visual impairments taking math assessments

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring math achievement equitably across students with and without visual impairments
