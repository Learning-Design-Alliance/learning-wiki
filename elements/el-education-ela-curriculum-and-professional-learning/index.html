Description¶

This element is the combined EL Education English Language Arts curriculum and professional learning program whose impact on teacher practices the publication examines. The page lists the associated project as "EL Education Schools and Teacher Professional Development" with the time frame "2013-2019", and the work was prepared for EL Education by Mathematica Policy Research. The page prints no program components or findings.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The program is studied in combination: curriculum and professional learning are delivered together rather than separately

Constraints¶

  • The publication page prints no details of the program's components or its measured impacts

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers using EL Education's English Language Arts curriculum

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Transforming teachers' instructional practices in English Language Arts to meet rigorous standards

Claims¶

  • One year of Teacher Potential Project participation improved teachers' overall English language arts instructional practices relative to district curriculum and professional development [+M]
  • A significantly greater proportion of TPP teachers demonstrated and sustained CCSS-aligned instructional practices compared with district-supported teachers [+M]
  • Two years of teacher participation in the Teacher Potential Project improved students' English language arts achievement by roughly 1.4 months of typical improvement [+M]
  • Significantly more TPP teachers asked students to cite evidence from texts in discussions and writing [+M]
  • Significantly more TPP teachers encouraged students' higher-order thinking skills than district-supported comparison teachers [+M]
  • Emerging research suggests a need to integrate teachers' knowledge and skill development with instructional materials, but further evidence on the effectiveness of this approach is necessary [+W]
