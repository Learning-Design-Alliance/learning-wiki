# READ 180® Student Application with six adaptive instructional zones

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The computerized READ 180® Student Application is one of three small-group rotations and "includes six components (called 'zones')": Explore (anchor videos with vocabulary activities), Reading (close reading of individualized texts at the student's instructional reading level), Language (vocabulary building and practice), Fluency (spelling and reading practice), Writing (argumentative, narrative, and informative essays), and Success (progressively more complex fluency and comprehension activities). "The goal of the READ 180® software is to continually adjust the level of instruction based on student performance."
