## Description
The Productive Engagement through Collaborative Action and Sociology (PECAS) Mediator is a remote instruction tool that assists instructors in identifying unproductive collaborations in online teamwork. Using AI-based techniques, it automatically identifies moments of unproductive collaboration, notifies an instructor, and provides hints about how to intervene effectively. The team theorized that "monitoring and mediation during online teamwork sessions will increase social presence, thus resulting in teamwork productive engagement." The project was in early phases at the time of the report.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI detection of emotional state and behavior without storing personal or identifiable data; a human mediator (instructor) performs the mediation step
#### Constraints
- The project is still in the early phases; the team had no feedback mechanism currently to track whether suggested interventions were successful

### Target Learners
- instructors of remote/online team-based courses

### Target Learning Goals
- productive engagement in online teamwork; social presence during virtual collaboration
