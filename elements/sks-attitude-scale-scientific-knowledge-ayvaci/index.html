Attitude Scale towards Scientific Knowledge (SKS)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The attitude scale towards scientific knowledge, developed by Ayvacı (2007), is the study's second quantitative instrument, applied as pre-test, post-test and retention test. The article states "The scale contains 25 items about SK, which are in five-point Likert type." Items were developed from components of science and the nature of scientific knowledge in the literature, and after language-expert review the data analysis yielded factor load values of .515–.885, a total variance percent of 42%, and internal consistency of 0.73.

Claims¶

  • Both instructional conditions produced statistically significant pre-test to post-test gains in NOS and SK views, with larger effect sizes in the experimental group [+W]
  • Formative assessment embedded with explicit-reflective instruction yields higher post-test NOS and SK scores than explicit-reflective instruction alone among pre-school prospective teachers [+W]
  • The formative-assessment-embedded group retained higher NOS and SK scores than the control group on a retention test four weeks after the intervention [+W]
  • Neither group showed a significant decline from post-test to retention test on NOS or SK measures [+W]
  • Experimental and control groups did not differ significantly on pre-test NOS or SK scores [+W]
