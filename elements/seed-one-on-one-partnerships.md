# SEED one-on-one grantee-organization partnerships

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Supporting Effective Educator Development (SEED) partnership model pairs SEED grantees with a variety of education organizations in one-on-one partnerships to support educator development. The article states the approach "was developed through a U.S. Department of Education (ED) project that developed one-on-one partnerships between ED's Supporting Effective Educator Development (SEED) grantees and a variety of education organizations." The blueprint's guidance and tools were designed around developing and supporting these partnerships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A SEED grantee partner and an education organization willing to form a one-on-one partnership
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
