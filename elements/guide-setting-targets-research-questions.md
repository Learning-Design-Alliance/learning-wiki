# Guide to Setting Targets for Research Questions: a tool for organizations and research partners to set and use targets

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This Mathematica publication is a practical guide designed for organizations implementing solutions with support from a research partner. Its stated purpose is that "Using this guide will help organizations and their research partner set and use targets in their evidence-building work." It was prepared for the Gates Foundation under the project Evaluating the Development of Middle Years Math Teaching and Learning Solutions (2018-2023).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An organization implementing a solution, with support from a research partner
#### Constraints
- Intended for organizations implementing solutions that have a research partner, which could be internal staff with evaluation expertise or an external provider

### Target Learners
- Organizations implementing educational solutions and their research partners

### Target Learning Goals
- Setting and using targets in evidence-building work for educational solutions
