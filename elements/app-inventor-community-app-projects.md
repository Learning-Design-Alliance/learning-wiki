# App Inventor community-problem app projects with storyboarding

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The deck presents App Inventor as a tool for students to build mobile apps, suggesting app content ideas such as "Build an App that solves a problem for your community", healthcare apps, tourist needs, quiz games, vocabulary practice, and math practice. It references video tutorials and includes a storyboarding step, "Create a storyboard for an App", as planning before building.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need access to App Inventor and the referenced video tutorials; storyboarding precedes app building
