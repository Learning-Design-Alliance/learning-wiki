# National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A residential education and training program for youth ages 16 to 18 who have dropped out of or been expelled from high school. Its 20-week residential phase is quasi-military — cadets "live in barracks, have their hair cut short, wear uniforms, experience military-style discipline" — and "focuses on eight components: leadership, responsible citizenship, service to community, life-coping skills, physical fitness, health and hygiene, job skills, and academic excellence." Most of the day is spent on GED preparation. It costs about $14,000 per individual and is free to participants.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be unemployed, drug free, legal residents of the offering state, and not heavily involved with the criminal justice system
#### Constraints
- No requirements for military service; sites accept an average of 100 trainees per cohort

### Target Learners
- At-risk youth ages 16 to 18 who have dropped out of or been expelled from high school

### Target Learning Goals
- GED or high school diploma attainment
- job skills
- leadership
- responsible citizenship
- physical fitness and life-coping skills
