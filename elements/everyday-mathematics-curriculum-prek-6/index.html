Everyday Mathematics® core curriculum for grades pre-K–6¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Everyday Mathematics® is "a comprehensive curriculum for students in grades pre-K–6 that exposes students to material over extended periods of time by repeatedly reinforcing material both within and across grade levels." The article states "The curriculum includes six key features": real-life problem solving, balanced instruction, multiple opportunities for basic skills practice, emphasis on communication, home/school partnerships, and use of technology. It is organized around Program Goals (the same at all grade levels, organized by content strand) refined through approximately 20–25 Grade-Level Goals per grade, with teacher guides, student materials, manipulatives, games, and online resources.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Implementation supported by 40 hours of initial teacher training as recommended by the publisher, plus the Teacher's Resource Package with Teacher's Manual and Lesson Guide, Resource Book, home-and-school links instructions, and a manipulatives materials kit

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • primary students in grades pre-K–6

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mathematics achievement, including concepts, operations, and problem solving

Claims¶

  • Everyday Mathematics® has potentially positive effects on mathematics achievement for primary students, with a substantively important but statistically non-significant effect size of 0.27 [+W]
  • Everyday Mathematics® showed positive effects on TAAS math subtests (Concepts, Operations, Problem Solving) with effect sizes of 0.25 to 0.31 that were not statistically significant after clustering correction [+W]
