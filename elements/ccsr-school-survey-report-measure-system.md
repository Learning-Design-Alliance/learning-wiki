# Consortium school survey report system with 53 measures, demographic comparison groups, and time trends

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 associational), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Consortium prepares individualized, confidential survey reports for every Chicago school meeting response-rate criteria; in 2001, 412 elementary and high schools qualified. Each school is described on 53 measures constructed from questionnaire items, displayed against the systemwide average, demographically similar schools, and the school's own trend since 1994. Measures combine conceptually related items for more reliable understanding, and at least seven respondents are needed for a valid reading of a measure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least 42 percent of teachers or 50 percent of students must respond for a school to receive a report; at least seven students or seven teachers must respond to all items comprising a measure for a valid reading
#### Constraints
- Some measures may be unreported if response thresholds are not met; a year's data may be missing on some profiles

### Target Learners
- Students, teachers, and principals in Chicago public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- School self-assessment and long-term improvement planning
