## Description
A network created to sustain collaboration among states involved in data-linking work after the TSDL project. The brief reports that "Continuing collaboration among states was accomplished through the development of a gathering of a new network called the Chief Information Officer network." It serves as a support network for education agencies involved in similar work on linked teacher-student data.
