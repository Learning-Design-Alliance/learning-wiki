## Description
The fact sheet is itself a synthesized guidance artifact: the authors state they "distilled crisis response guidance from federal agencies, national professional associations, and federally funded research collaboratives and technical assistance centers." Its purpose is to help schools and districts develop a trauma-informed plan as they prepare to reopen during the COVID-19 era.
