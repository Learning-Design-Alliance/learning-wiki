# Untimed grammaticality judgment test for measuring wh-question learning gains

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study measured learning gains with an untimed grammaticality judgment test administered as pretest, posttest, and delayed posttest. It consisted of "30 items targeti ng wh-question forms" plus 15 distracters targeting other structures, with half the questions grammatical and half ungrammatical; items were identical across the three tests but in different order, and each correct answer received one point.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Distracter items are incorporated to avoid making learners sensitive to the target structures in the pretest
#### Constraints
- The authors note that untimed tests allowing unlimited time may draw on learners' explicit L2 knowledge

### Target Learners
- Iranian EFL university students

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring acquisition of English wh-question forms
