District-Charter Collaboration Grant program evaluation survey¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A survey of district and charter school educators (teachers and principals) sampled from 21 cross-sector collaboration activities across the seven grantee cities in 2014-2015, conducted by Mathematica for the Gates Foundation. It examined "the extent to which cross-sector collaboration and the transfer of practices occurred and the contextual factors that helped facilitate or impede cross-sector collaboration."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires sampled district and charter educators participating in grant-funded cross-sector collaboration activities

Constraints¶

  • Covers the seven grantee cities and 21 activities of the 2013-2016 District-Charter Collaboration Grant program

Target Learners¶

  • District and charter school teachers and principals

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Cross-sector collaboration and transfer of practices between district and charter schools
