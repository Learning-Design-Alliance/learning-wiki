Read Naturally® supplemental fluency program: modeled reading, repeated timed reading, and progress monitoring¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Read Naturally® is a supplemental reading program using texts, audio CDs, and computer software to improve "reading fluency, accuracy, and comprehension" of students in elementary, middle, or high school or adults. Its common strategy includes "modeling of story reading, repeated reading of text for developing oral reading fluency, and systematic monitoring of student progress by teachers and the students themselves." Students work at their reading level, progress at their own rate, and pass a story only after meeting four teacher-judged criteria including goal rate, three or fewer errors, appropriate phrasing, and correct comprehension answers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivery requires audio CDs with hard-copy materials, computer-based software, or the web-based version, plus a teacher's manual with implementation instructions and lesson plans.

Constraints¶

  • The reviewed evidence covers only Read Naturally® Masters Edition and Read Naturally® Software Edition with beginning readers in grades 2–4 as a supplement to core language arts instruction.

Target Learners¶

  • Beginning readers in grades 2–4

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Oral reading fluency
  • Reading accuracy
  • Reading comprehension

Claims¶

  • Christ and Davie (2009) found positive, statistically significant effects of Read Naturally® Software Edition on three reading fluency measures [+W]
  • Heistad (2008) found a statistically significant positive effect of Read Naturally® on the NALT Reading measure in a matched quasi-experiment [+W]
  • Read Naturally® shows mixed effects on reading fluency for beginning readers (average improvement index +7 percentile points) [~W]
  • Read Naturally® shows no discernible effects on alphabetics for beginning readers [-W]
  • Read Naturally® shows no discernible effects on comprehension for beginning readers [-W]
  • Read Naturally® shows potentially positive effects on general reading achievement for beginning readers (average improvement index +10 percentile points) [+W]
