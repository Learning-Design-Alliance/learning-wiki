# Waterford Early Reading Level One™ computer-based emergent literacy curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 mixed, 3 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Waterford Early Reading Level One™ is "an emergent literacy curriculum that uses computer-based technology to prepare children for reading." It begins with a computer/mouse tutorial and a reading placement evaluation assigning children to Level One objectives (capital letters, lowercase letters, or beginning decoding skills). It provides individualized, year-long instruction in daily 15-minute sessions, supplemented by phonological and phonemic awareness activities, letter recognition, story and print concepts, books, videos, and take-home materials, with teacher reports for monitoring progress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Individualized daily 15-minute computer sessions with the Waterford software, assessment materials, books, and videos
- Teachers review class and individual reports at least once a month to monitor progress and reassign activities for mastery
- On-site or online training plus a detailed teacher guide
#### Constraints
- Evaluated here only as an overlay on the High/Scope curriculum in Head Start center-based settings with children aged three to five

### Target Learners
- preschool children aged three to five in center-based Head Start settings

### Target Learning Goals
- emergent literacy: letter recognition, phonological and phonemic awareness, beginning decoding, print and story concepts
