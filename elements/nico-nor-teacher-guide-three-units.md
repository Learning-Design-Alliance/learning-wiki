# Early Science with Nico and Nor Teacher Guide with three units (Plants, Ramps and Shadows) and a Family Guide

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Teacher Guide is the program's classroom curricular resource. It "includes 3 units (Plants, Ramps and Shadows)", and each unit includes "a series of circle time, small group activity and learning center ideas to promote science, math & engineering in the classroom", plus support material for teachers and a Family Guide that links home and school learning. A QR code gives access to the guide.
