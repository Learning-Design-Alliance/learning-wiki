# Cognitive Tutor® secondary mathematics curriculum (Carnegie Learning)

## Description
Cognitive Tutor® is a secondary mathematics curriculum covering Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, and Integrated Math I–III, developed by Carnegie Learning. It "focuses on how students think about and learn mathematics," with teachers facilitating as students acquire and apply new information and discuss their work. Problems "emphasize connections between verbal, numeric, graphic, and algebraic representations," and materials include textbooks, adaptive software, teacher implementation guides, assessments, and professional development resources.
