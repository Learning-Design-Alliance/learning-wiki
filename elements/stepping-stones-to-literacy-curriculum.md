# Stepping Stones to Literacy supplemental curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Stepping Stones to Literacy (SSL) is a supplemental early-literacy curriculum of "25 lessons, for a total of 9–15 hours of instructional time," delivered in 10- to 20-minute daily lessons in small groups or individually. It targets "listening, print conventions, phonological awareness, phonemic awareness, and serial processing/rapid naming" for kindergarten and older preschool students considered underachieving readers. The kit includes a lesson book with serial rapid automatic naming activities and instructional prompts in English and Spanish, distributed by Sopris West for $223.49.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered as a supplement to the regular reading curriculum by a teacher or tutor following the lesson book
#### Constraints
- Students with significant early literacy deficits are taught individually rather than in small groups

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten and older preschool students considered underachieving readers

### Target Learning Goals
- Early literacy skills: listening, print conventions, phonological awareness, phonemic awareness, and serial processing/rapid naming
