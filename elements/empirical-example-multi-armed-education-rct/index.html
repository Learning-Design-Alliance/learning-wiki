Empirical example using data from a multi-armed education RCT¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 theoretical), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article includes an empirical demonstration applying its design-based estimators to real data from a multi-armed education randomized controlled trial. As the abstract states, "An empirical example using data from a multi-armed education RCT demonstrates the issues." The example illustrates the variance adjustment and block-weighting issues the article identifies for multi-armed designs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Data from a multi-armed education RCT with multiple research groups

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • education researchers applying design-based estimators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • demonstrating variance and weighting adjustments in practice

Claims¶

  • The developed estimators apply to a wide range of education research designs, including clustered and blocked designs [+W]
  • In multi-armed trials seeking the most effective treatments, each pairwise contrast sample is representative of the full set of randomized units [+W]
  • Variance terms in multi-armed RCT estimators need slight adjustment under the finite-population framework, which can reduce precision [+W]
  • Design-based estimators for two-group designs require modification for multi-armed designs when comparing pairs of research groups [+W]
