# Eckersley's design helper computer programs

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Michael Eckersley created computer-based programs he called "the design helper", a natural evolution of his work translating computer-generated random number streams into graphical concepts. As the paper reports, "Students use these program to complete images and patterns that can be related to specific design questions." The editor of Micro Cad News connected such tools to chaos science's mode of exploring a solution and then asking what problem it has solved, suggesting the design helper embodies that reversed inquiry role for design learners.
