# Suite of Mathematica tools supporting grantees to develop, refine, and scale evidence-based solutions

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The 3Us guide belongs to "a suite of tools designed to support grantees as they develop, refine, and scale evidence-based solutions (programs, products, or practices)." The suite targets solutions "that demonstrate success in improving educational outcomes for students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty," and includes companion publications on measurement planning, target setting, and sample size.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users must be grantees working on evidence-based educational solutions within the funder's portfolio
#### Constraints
- The suite is oriented to solutions serving students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty

### Target Learners
- grantees of the Gates Foundation education portfolio

### Target Learning Goals
- developing, refining, and scaling evidence-based educational solutions
