# DC 2007 systemic school reform package (staff evaluation, charter sector growth, unified enrollment)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Beginning in 2007, the District of Columbia implemented a systemic reform of educational governance and processes. The article describes that "These reforms included implementation of a more rigorous staff evaluation system, steady growth of the public charter school sector, and the introduction of a unified enrollment system." The package was designed, in the article's words, "to produce dramatic improvements in student outcomes" and is evaluated cumulatively rather than component by component.
