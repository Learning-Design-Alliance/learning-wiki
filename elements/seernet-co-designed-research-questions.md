## Description
The presentation lists examples of co-designed research questions, including "How does personalizing learning by providing leveled readings and resources affect the learning process of acquiring new concepts?", questions on effects of learning environments, modalities, or instructional design components, on providing engagement metrics to students, and on what reward system a platform should implement to support persistence, good study habits, and grit. The team notes these future-oriented questions are thematically current.
