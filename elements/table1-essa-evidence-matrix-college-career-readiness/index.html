Table 1 evidence matrix mapping interventions to ESSA evidence tiers across eleven outcome domains¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Table 1 summarizes, for each of 15 interventions, the outcome domains for which WWC-reviewed evidence meets ESSA Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 requirements, spanning domains from high school attendance through "Employment and labor market." It also flags research contexts: a check for Rural indicates "research explicitly including rural sites," and a check for Facing Economic Disadvantages indicates samples at least 40 percent economically disadvantaged. The format is adapted from the REL AP College Access Evidence Matrix.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Information drawn from What Works Clearinghouse intervention reviews

Constraints¶

  • Covers only interventions with WWC-reviewed evidence of at least one positive, statistically significant effect

Target Learners¶

  • secondary students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • college and career readiness

Claims¶

  • Springboard Summer scholars show larger reading improvements than statistically equivalent non-participating peers, meeting ESSA Tier 2 moderate evidence standards [+W]
  • Literacy First shows strong ESSA evidence of positive effects in phonics and related alphabetics, reading fluency, and reading comprehension for grades K–2 [+W]
  • Achieve3000 shows moderate ESSA evidence of positive effects on reading fluency [+W]
  • None of the WWC-standards-meeting studies of Tier 2 literacy interventions were conducted in CNMI or other Pacific islands, limiting generalizability [~W]
