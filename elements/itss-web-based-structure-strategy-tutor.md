# ITSS: web-based intelligent tutoring system for the structure strategy

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (6 for, 2 mixed) · 6 studies (4 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 5 of 6 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
ITSS is a supplemental web-based program for students in grades K-8 that teaches students to follow the logical structure of factual text and use text structure to improve understanding and recall. "ITSS highlights five main text structures that are used to (1) make comparisons; (2) present problems and solutions; (3) link causes and effects; (4) present sequences; and (5) describe things, people, creatures, places, or events." An animated Intelligent Tutor models each structure, and students practice with exercises providing immediate feedback, at their own pace, with roughly 12 lessons per text structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students access the program on a personal computer with earphones; teachers and administrators must complete professional development (a 2-day session for teachers, 1-day for administrators) plus 4 to 6 days of in-school coaching.
#### Constraints
- The evidence base covers grades 4, 5, and 7 in suburban and rural districts; effects are not known for other adolescent literacy outcome domains.

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-8; studies included grades 4, 5, and 7 in suburban and rural districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Comprehension of factual (nonfiction) text using text structure

## Claims

- [The effects of ITSS are not known for adolescent literacy outcomes other than comprehension](../claims/itss-effects-unknown-other-outcomes.md) [~W]
- [In the grades 4-5 cluster RCT, ITSS produced statistically significant positive effects on all six comprehension outcomes (average effect size 0.24)](../claims/itss-grade45-rct-comprehension-gains.md) [+S]
- [In the grade 7 cluster RCT, ITSS produced statistically significant positive effects on all eight comprehension outcomes (average effect size 0.23)](../claims/itss-grade7-rct-comprehension-gains.md) [+M]
- [ITSS has positive effects on reading comprehension, based on two studies meeting WWC standards covering 6,724 students](../claims/itss-positive-effects-comprehension.md) [+M]
- [ITSS significantly improves Grade 7 reading comprehension in rural and suburban schools (effect size +0.18)](../claims/itss-grade7-significant-reading-gain.md) [+W]
- [ITSS significantly improves Grade 5 reading comprehension after 6 to 7 months of implementation (effect size +0.20)](../claims/itss-grade5-significant-reading-gain.md) [+W]
- [ITSS qualifies for a Strong evidence rating based on multiple randomized studies with average effect sizes of +0.15 (elementary) and +0.18 (secondary)](../claims/itss-strong-evidence-rating.md) [+W]
- [ITSS shows a positive but statistically non-significant effect on Grade 4 reading comprehension (effect size +0.10)](../claims/itss-grade4-positive-nonsignificant.md) [~W]
