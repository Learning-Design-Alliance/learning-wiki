Evidence Evaluation Rubric for edtech efficacy (Resource C)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Resource C is a rubric for scoring whether an edtech tool has valid and sufficient evidence of impact, organized around strength of the evidence base, applicability to the district's need and context, feasibility of implementation, and continuous improvement. It contrasts weak evidence—for example vendor materials showing satisfaction percentages with no evidence that learning improved—with promising evidence ranging from ESSA Tier 4 design grounding through Tier 1, which "involves a well-designed and implemented experimental study with at least 350 participants in more than one school or district that found a statistically significant positive effect." Validation sources in the rubric are searchable on the EdTech Index.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Districts should develop an evidence rubric that aligns with their values and priorities while giving school staff, families, and students transparent access to decision-making

Constraints¶

  • The rubric treats satisfaction-only vendor claims as weak evidence (score 0)

Target Learners¶

  • District leaders evaluating edtech efficacy evidence

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Assessing whether tools have rigorous evidence of improving student outcomes in the intended use case and context
