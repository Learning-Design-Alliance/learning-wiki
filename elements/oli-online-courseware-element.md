# Open Learning Initiative (OLI) online courseware with embedded feedback activities and instructor dashboard

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 8 studies (6 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 8 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
OLI is Carnegie Mellon University's online course platform providing "online courses and learning materials to instructors and learners at low or no cost" in blended or purely online formats. Its components include learning objectives on every course page, expository materials, two embedded activity types — "Learn by Doing activities" for practicing skills and "Did I Get This?" self-assessments — with immediate feedback addressing misconceptions, checkpoint quizzes, a Learning Dashboard giving instructors real-time student-level progress and accuracy data, and a gradebook. Instructors may optionally add one to two hours of weekly face-to-face reinforcement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses require internet access and an up-to-date web browser; some content cannot be accessed on mobile devices.
#### Constraints
- Face-to-face reinforcement is optional; hybrid delivery requires physical space for periodic in-person instruction.

### Target Learners
- postsecondary students in credit-bearing instructor-led courses
- independent learners

### Target Learning Goals
- course content mastery across 40 OLI courses in high schools and colleges
