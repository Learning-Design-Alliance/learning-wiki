Stress Awareness field test measures for Spanish suprasegmental phonology¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Beginning Fall 2024, NWEA field tested three new Stress Awareness measures: Syllable Segmentation, Stressed Syllable, and Word Classification, all untimed. The guide states "Stress awareness is the strongest predictor of reading success in Spanish and is directly linked to word recognition (Levis, 2018)," categorizing it under suprasegmental phonology, measuring abilities that Phonological Awareness measures do not.
