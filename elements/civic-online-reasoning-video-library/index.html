Civic Online Reasoning video library supports teaching accurate online data evaluation¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The resource list includes the Civic Online Reasoning (COR) Video Library, described as "Library of videos that explain the importance of finding accurate data and strategies for analyzing online data sources." The deck marks it as appropriate for students in grades 7-12 or teachers of any grade, supporting the data-evaluation side of data analysis instruction.
