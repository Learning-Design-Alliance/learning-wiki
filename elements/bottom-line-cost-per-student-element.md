# Bottom Line cost profile of approximately $6,000 per student over five to six years

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report provides a preliminary list of cost ingredients for implementing Bottom Line, covering personnel, facilities, equipment and materials, and direct assistance. The total cost per student receiving intensive advising through the Access and Success programs is approximately $6,000 over a five- to six-year period, paid by Bottom Line, with target colleges providing facilities access and students and families incurring no costs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standard IT equipment such as computers, phones, printers, and photocopiers for staff is required.
#### Constraints
- The cost list is explicitly not exhaustive and is based on information available as of August 2020.

### Target Learners
- Low-income and first-generation high school and college students

### Target Learning Goals
- College enrollment, persistence, and career readiness
