# NCTE Transcript dataset of elementary mathematics classrooms linked with MQI observation scores

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The NCTE dataset is described as "the largest publicly available dataset of U.S. classroom transcripts linked with classroom observation scores." It consists of 1,660 fourth- and fifth-grade elementary mathematics observations collected between 2010-2013 from 317 teachers across 4 school districts serving largely historically marginalized students. The authors aligned whole-class transcripts with 7.5-minute MQI observation segments, yielding 9,886 segments each rated on 11 MQI variables across four dimensions of math instruction quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- MQI observation variables apply to 7.5-minute segments, requiring alignment of whole-class transcripts with observation segments
#### Constraints
- Student utterances are often inaudible because collecting student talk is challenging in practice
- The dataset was collected over a decade ago and may not represent the current education field

### Target Learners
- 4th and 5th grade elementary mathematics students in K-12 schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematical instruction quality across richness of the mathematics, working with students and mathematics, errors and imprecision, and student participation in meaning-making and reasoning
