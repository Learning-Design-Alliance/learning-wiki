# Bridges in Mathematics: comprehensive preK-5 math curriculum blending direct instruction, investigation, and exploration

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Bridges in Mathematics by MLC is a comprehensive preK-5 math curriculum that "emphasizes conceptual understanding, fluency, discourse, and problem solving." It blends "direct instruction, structured investigation, and open exploration" and includes whole-class, small-group, and independent activities. Lessons use visual models and hands-on manipulatives, and the curriculum provides teacher guides and student materials in print and digital formats.
