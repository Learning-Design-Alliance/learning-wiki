# Sharing Study Data: A Guide for Education Researchers (NCEE 2022-004)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A practical guide published by the U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences (NCEE 2022-004, March 2022) for education researchers on sharing study data. It "describes key issues that education researchers should consider when deciding which study data to share, how to organize the data, what documentation to include, and where to share their final dataset," and "provides strategies for addressing related challenges."
