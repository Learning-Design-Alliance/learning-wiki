# ARC digital tools: content delivery and performance management system, e-libraries, and educational games

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ARC offers digital solutions supporting ARC Core, including "a content delivery and performance management system, e-libraries, and educational games". The tools integrate with all Learning Management Systems and support single sign on via Clever Instant Login and ClassLink One Click Login, and are accessible on any Internet-connected device with a browser supporting HTML5, JavaScript, CSS, and cookies.
