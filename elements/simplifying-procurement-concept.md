# Simplifying Procurement concept: user-centered tools and case studies for navigating district purchasing

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
One of five concepts developed at the workshop, Simplifying Procurement is a set of user-centered tools meant to demystify district procurement. It proposes "district-specific maps of the procurement process and a collection of case studies and stories that describe how a product or service successfully entered the school district," spurring dialogue among vendors, administrators, and teachers. Its inspiration is customer-service experiences at companies like Nordstrom and Southwest, imagining the procurement office as a support service for teachers and vendors.
