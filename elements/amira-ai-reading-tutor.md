## Description
Amira is an AI tutor that uses 'advanced speech recognition and natural language processing technology' to listen to students read aloud, identify reading challenges, and deliver micro-interventions based on reading and neuroscience research. The team developed a logic model with research partner Instructure to describe its foundational research and theory of change, and has led three ESSA-aligned studies of the tool's impact on learning.
