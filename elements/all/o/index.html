  • Observation - Observation is the short-form canonical target for instructional activities in which learners watch people, processes, or contexts carefully in order to notice patterns, behaviors, or constraints.
  • Observation Shadowing - Learners observe a live or recorded performance by an expert or peer, then immediately imitate or "shadow" that performance themselves.
