# New Jersey statewide principal evaluation system (2013/14)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
New Jersey implemented a new principal evaluation system to improve principal effectiveness, beginning with a 2012/13 pilot in 14 school districts and statewide implementation in 2013/14. In 2013/14, "half of a principal’s overall rating was composed of two measures of practice"—a district-selected or developed principal practice instrument and a state-developed evaluation leadership instrument—"and half was composed of measures of student achievement." The report analyzes this system's statistical properties.
