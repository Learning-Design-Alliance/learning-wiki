# BARR: a K–12 training and coaching model grounded in Relationships and Data

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
BARR is "a training and coaching model that serves K–12 schools with a structured system and tools to improve student outcomes." It is "Grounded in two core pillars – Relationships and Data," strengthening "staff-student, staff-staff, and student-student relationships while embedding the consistent use of academic and non-academic data into daily practice." A staff coordinator manages, coordinates, and integrates the model, and no specific technology is required.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A staff member must be selected to serve as the school's BARR coordinator, who can be an assistant principal, school counselor, teacher, or another staff member.
- Teams of teachers need to meet weekly using the BARR process.
