## Description
Reach Out and Read (ROR) is one of two family engagement providers the Heising-Simons Foundation supported to refine its early math project and scale it up in new sites. The brief states the Foundation "has since supported two providers—Reach Out and Read (ROR) and the YMCA of Silicon Valley (YMCA)—as they refined their early math projects and scaled them up in new sites." Mathematica's implementation study examined this scale-up effort.
