Edpuzzle Originals ready-made standards-aligned lessons¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Edpuzzle Originals are ready-made, professionally-developed lessons aligned with Common Core standards that teachers can still adapt to their classroom requirements. The article states they are "ready-made, professionally-developed lessons that align with Common Core standards while still allowing teachers to adapt the lessons", and they were the intervention content in the randomized math study.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher access to the Edpuzzle platform for assignment and customization

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary students (grades 3–5 in the evaluated math use)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Mathematics achievement aligned to Common Core standards

Claims¶

  • Edpuzzle Original math videos raise grades 3–5 mathematics achievement on the CAASPP (effect size +0.09) [+M]
  • Dosage relationship: more assigned Edpuzzle videos associated with larger gains [+W]
  • Edpuzzle qualifies for a Promising evidence rating based on one school-level randomized study [+W]
  • Edpuzzle Original math videos raise Renaissance Star Math scores and fall-to-spring growth (effect size +0.11) [+M]
