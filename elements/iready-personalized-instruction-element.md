# i-Ready Personalized Instruction (online individualized reading lessons for grades K-8)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
i-Ready Personalized Instruction uses information from the i-Ready Diagnostic to generate an individualized program of online lessons in reading for grades K-8, serving students whether their performance is below-grade, on-grade, or above-grade. The program monitors how students are progressing in reading over time, and decision-making at student, group, class, school, and district levels is driven by comprehensive, actionable reports. It is accessed through the i-Ready Connect digital platform.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the i-Ready Diagnostic to generate the individualized program
- Requires points of contact to assist with initial set-up and disseminate communications to other staff members
#### Constraints
- Accessed through i-Ready Connect on Windows, MacOS, and Chromebook machines with browsers enabling JavaScript, cookies, and pop-ups

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-8

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading achievement
