Google Cardboard and smartphone-based VR as an inexpensive classroom VR setup¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article describes Google Cardboard-style headsets, which resemble goggles and attach to a smartphone to become a VR headset enabling students to experience far-away places as if they were actually at the site. Although NMC figures list headsets starting around ten dollars and VR units from $300-$999, the article notes teachers would not have to spend hundreds of dollars: a VR field trip can be produced with a mobile phone and a Google Cardboard headset, and Google made Cardboard open source so other manufacturers can mass produce headsets at low prices.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A VR tour requires 360-degree viewing so the wearer can look around the scene; content must be compatible with the headset, such as YouTube VR video with Google Cardboard.

Constraints¶

  • The article cautions that inappropriate material may cause safety concerns if the product is not monitored.
