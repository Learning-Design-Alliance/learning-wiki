# STEMscopes Math comprehensive core mathematics curriculum (K–Algebra 1)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
STEMscopes Math is "a comprehensive, standards-aligned core mathematics curriculum for grades K through Algebra 1, developed by Accelerate Learning." It is built around the 5E+IA instructional model, supports whole-group instruction, small-group differentiation, and blended learning, follows a Concrete-Representational-Abstract progression, and integrates digital tools including virtual manipulatives and PhET simulations alongside print materials. All student resources are available in English and Spanish.
