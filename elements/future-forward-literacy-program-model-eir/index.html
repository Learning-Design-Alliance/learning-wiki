Future Forward literacy program model¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 10 claims (6 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 10 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Future Forward is an early elementary literacy program administered by Education Analytics that "leverages community agencies and combines one-on-one intensive tutoring with parent engagement to promote students’ literacy development both at school and at home." Within sites, a site manager (typically a certified teacher) oversees tutoring by paraprofessionals or volunteers, and a family engagement coordinator handles family outreach including monthly family events. Students participate for one school year with a minimum of two 30-minute tutoring sessions per week and at least two family contacts per month.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A minimum of two 30-minute tutoring sessions per week per student
  • Communication with participating families at least two times per month
  • A site manager, typically a certified teacher, and a family engagement coordinator, typically a community member or parent
  • Adequate implementation requires at least 80% of students within a program site meet the expected tutoring and family contact minimums.

Constraints¶

  • Adequate implementation is defined as at least 80% of students at a site meeting the tutoring and family contact minimums
  • Sites struggled to connect with participant families at the level that was expected; only 30.2% of families received family engagement as intended in the 2019-20 scale-up.

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten, first, and second grade students in high-need schools
  • Kindergarten, first, and second grade students in schools with low literacy performance

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Early literacy development
  • School attendance
  • Early elementary literacy development at school and at home

Claims¶

  • Prior randomized studies of FF/SPARK found positive impacts on literacy and school attendance meeting WWC standards Without Reservations [+W]
  • One year of Future Forward participation produced a statistically significant positive impact on school attendance of lower-primary students compared to BAU literacy instruction [+W]
  • The tutoring component of FF scaled up successfully to 14 schools, with 89.7% of participants receiving the intended tutoring dosage [+W]
  • The tutoring component of Future Forward scaled up successfully to 14 schools, with 89.7% of participants receiving the intended sessions [+W]
  • The family engagement component of FF did not fully scale up: only 30.2% of families were contacted at the intended frequency and no site met the fidelity threshold [~W]
  • The family engagement component of Future Forward did not fully scale up, with only about 30% of families receiving intended contacts [~W]
  • One year of Future Forward participation raises regular-school-day attendance of lower-primary students relative to business-as-usual literacy instruction [+W]
  • Prior randomized studies of Future Forward/SPARK met WWC standards Without Reservations and found positive impacts on literacy and attendance [+W]
  • The attendance impact of Future Forward is larger for Black students, male students, and students with low baseline attendance [~W]
  • Black male students with low baseline attendance show the largest Future Forward attendance gains, though not statistically significant [~W]
