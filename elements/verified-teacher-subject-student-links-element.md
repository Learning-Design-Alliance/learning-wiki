# Verified teacher-subject-student link data for examining value-added model assumptions

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A dataset of teacher-student links in which teachers of mathematics and/or English/language arts verified the subjects and students they taught. The article uses it as a benchmark against teacher-student links from original administrative data to detect departmentalization in grades 4-5, finding that "instruction is often departmentalized in these grades." It serves research on teacher effectiveness data quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers of mathematics and/or English/language arts must verify the subjects and students they taught
#### Constraints
- Covers elementary grades examined in the study; the article does not state availability beyond that setting

### Target Learners
- Elementary and middle school students in grades 4-8

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate measurement of teacher effectiveness in mathematics and English/language arts
