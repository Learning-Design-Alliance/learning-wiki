# Spanish MAP Growth Math calibration dataset of 3,908 items and over 147,000 monolingual and 33,000 bilingual students

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (3 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's empirical basis is item and person data pulled from the NWEA Growth Research Database (GRD) covering five years of Spanish math testing. The item side comprises "3,908 Spanish math items across different tests" in the selected sample (3,955 collected), spanning 24 Spanish math tests from K-2 through 6+. The person side comprises 147,094 collected monolingual students (147,044 selected) and 33,624 collected bilingual students (33,607 selected), about 75% Hispanic across grades. The dataset supports the fixed-person-score calibration designs and all item and person results in the report.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student Spanish math test responses from the NWEA Growth Research Database, with English scores available for the bilingual calibration design
#### Constraints
- Item selection filtered to an absolute old-new RIT difference of 20 RITs or less; person selection filtered to an absolute old-new score difference of 5 RITs or less

### Target Learners
- K-12 students taking Spanish MAP Growth Math tests

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematics achievement measurement in Spanish
