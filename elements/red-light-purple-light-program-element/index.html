Red Light, Purple Light: a preschool music-and-movement games program for self-regulation¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Red Light, Purple Light is a preschool program of music and movement games aimed at fostering self-regulation in young children, targeting remembering instructions, switching attention between rules, and inhibiting impulsive behaviors and emotions. Sessions open with a greeting song, center on five variations of Red Light, Green Light in which teachers increase complexity by asking children "to perform the action for the opposite cue or by adding more cues and actions," and close with a goodbye song. Children may also present the cues and actions themselves.

Context¶

Requirements¶

Target Learners¶

  • preschool children in Head Start centers, ages 38 to 66 months, from families below the poverty threshold

Target Learning Goals¶

  • self-regulation: remembering instructions, shifting attention between rules, and inhibiting impulsive behaviors and emotions

Claims¶

  • Red Light, Purple Light has potentially positive effects on preschool children's self-regulation, with moderate evidence [+M]
  • Red Light, Purple Light shows uncertain effects on preschool language outcomes [~W]
  • Red Light, Purple Light shows uncertain effects on reading and literacy related achievement [~W]
