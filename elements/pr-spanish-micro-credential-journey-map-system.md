# Spanish-language micro-credential system with Journey Map and TUPS assessment for personalized technology professional learning

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The project offered teachers a curated list of 22 Spanish-language micro-credentials on the Digital Promise Micro-credential Platform, earned by submitting evidence such as classroom recordings and annotated lesson plans. Micro-credentials are described as "competency-based, research-backed, personalized, on-demand, and shareable." Teachers took the TUPS assessment, which placed them on a Journey Map and suggested micro-credentials matched to their stage of technology integration, with assessor feedback supporting resubmission.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers submit required evidence of competence, such as video/audio recordings of classroom instruction or annotated lesson plans
- Feedback from assessors so unsuccessful applicants can reflect and resubmit
#### Constraints
- Most educators in Puerto Rico were not familiar with micro-credentials prior to the project, and some remained confused about requirements by the end of the case study

### Target Learners
- In-service primary, middle, and secondary teachers in Puerto Rico

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective technology integration into classroom practice
- Competency demonstration in skills such as digital citizenship, assessment, and differentiation
