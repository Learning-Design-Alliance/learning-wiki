## Description
A published framework document from the Regional Educational Laboratory (REL) Mid-Atlantic project, prepared for the U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. It offers states and districts "an improved measurement strategy" for answering three critical questions about school performance and organizing performance measure data into complementary groupings.
