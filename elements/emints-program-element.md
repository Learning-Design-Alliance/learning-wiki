# eMINTS professional development program for middle schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
eMINTS (enhancing Missouri's Instructional Networked Teaching Strategies) is a professional development approach "designed to promote inquiry-based learning, high-quality lesson design, and technology-rich learning environments, as well as to build community among teachers and students." Classrooms have interactive whiteboards, LCD projectors, and a 1:1 student-to-computer ratio. Professional development targets principals, technology coordinators, and classroom teachers in middle schools, with a strong emphasis on principals leading implementation and conducting teacher observations.

## Design Implications

### Context
