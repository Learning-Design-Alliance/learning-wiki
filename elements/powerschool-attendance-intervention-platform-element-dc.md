# PowerSchool Attendance Intervention platform

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
PowerSchool Attendance Intervention is a platform intended to "increase family engagement and support student attendance". It helps educators "monitor daily and period-level attendance as well as chronic absenteeism data for each student", gathers data from a school or district's administrative system to provide attendance dashboards and customized messaging templates, and supports two-way messaging with families via texts and email, including auto-translation features for diverse families.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators and administrators access the platform via web or mobile apps; parents, guardians, and students need only a cellular phone with basic text messaging or phone calling or an email address to participate.
- The solution gathers data from a school or district's administrative system.
#### Constraints
- No special staffing is required, per the review's staffing section.

### Target Learners
- 6th-12th grade students in public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Student attendance and family engagement
