# Guided Inquiry

## Description
Guided inquiry is the element in which learners investigate questions or problems with structured prompts, facilitator support, or staged resources that keep the inquiry productive. It is useful when the goal is inquiry with support rather than direct telling or unguided discovery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A question or problem worth investigating**
- **Prompts, scaffolds, or resources that shape the inquiry path**
#### Constraints
- **Too much support can collapse inquiry into direct instruction**
- **Too little support can leave learners lost**

### Target Learning Goals
- Support inquiry, explanation building, and evidence-based sensemaking.
