Technology Integration¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · 134 claims (61 for, 62 mixed, 11 against) · 116 studies (25 associational, 23 qualitative, 20 causal, 18 review, 14 design, 11 quant-synthesis, 5 theoretical), q1–q4 · 20 of 116 report an effect size · 131 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Technology integration is the element in which digital tools are used in direct service of learning goals, feedback, access, or collaboration.

Design Implications¶

Affordances¶

  • Digital Learning
  • Multimodal Instruction

The evidence by question¶

  • Does building AI tools into instruction raise achievement over teaching the same content without them, and in which settings? AI-supported instruction has a moderate positive effect on student achievement in STEM education (g = 0.670 under a random effects model) [+M]
  • When learners lean heavily on AI tools, do their own critical thinking and learning suffer? Higher reliance on AI tools is correlated with lower critical-thinking performance, mediated by cognitive offloading [~M]
  • Does professional development that shows teachers how to use digital tools in their own teaching go with more classroom use of those tools? Teachers receiving 11 or more hours of training on integrating digital content report almost double the reliance on software and the Internet compared with untrained teachers [+M]
