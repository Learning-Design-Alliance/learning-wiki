# Computing Pathways Research Practice Partnership of districts designing K-12 CS/CT pathways

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Computing Pathways Research Practice Partnership is a collaboration in which districts design and articulate system-wide K-12 learning pathways in computational thinking and computer science. The poster profiles three core districts, including one of 14,000 students described as Becoming Urban with an equity focus on greater inclusion of growing number of Black, Latinx and ELL students, and a rural district of 7,500 students focused on engaging students from low socio-economic households and female students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District leaders who can articulate a vision and equity focus for the pathway
#### Constraints
- Work described is at pilot stage within the partner districts

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in participating districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Computational thinking and computer science skills across K-12
