National K–8 fall and spring achievement dataset covering 840,000 students in 8,800 public schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study leverages achievement data measured at two points in each school year — "in the fall and spring of kindergarten through eighth grade" — for a national sample of "840,000 students attending 8,800 public schools." This seasonal structure allows the authors to separate school-year growth from summer loss and to compare rural and nonrural schools. It is the empirical basis for all findings in the article.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Achievement measured in both fall and spring of each grade so that school-year and summer learning can be separated

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten through eighth-grade students in U.S. public schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Academic achievement and growth in reading and mathematics as measured by standardized assessments

Related Elements¶

  • NWEA MAP Growth assessment dataset (3.4 million students) as the evidence base for seasonal learning analysis
  • NWEA test-score dataset of over 2.5 million kindergarten through eighth-grade students across three cohorts (2015-2018)
  • NWEA MAP Growth assessment as national data source for seasonal skill-growth analysis
