# SEET system: web-based equity analytics tool with six co-designed visualizations of student experience data

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Science Student Electronic Exit Ticket (SEET) system is a web-based application that supports middle and high school science teachers to collect short student experience surveys at the end of class and automatically tabulate and visualize responses. It provides three "whole class visualizations" and three "equity visualizations" that disaggregate responses "by gender and race/ethnicity," co-designed with secondary science teachers in a year-long iterative process. A four-step workflow runs from administering a survey through visualization-supported sensemaking to selecting new instructional strategies. The system protects student anonymity with unique identification codes and suppresses results for groups of 2 or less.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers receive training on the system and visualization interpretation; students are explained the purpose of the system and how their data will be used before first use
#### Constraints
- The system does not report results for groups of 2 or less, which limited usefulness in classrooms with little racial or gender diversity

### Target Learners
- middle school science students

### Target Learning Goals
- equitable participation in classroom knowledge-building activities
