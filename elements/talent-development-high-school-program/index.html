Talent Development High School program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Talent Development High School is a whole-school reform program "intended to improve achievement and attendance for 9th graders in high-poverty urban schools." It delivers professional development so schools can implement four components: ninth-grade small interdisciplinary learning communities; double-dose English and math curricula with freshman seminars on study skills and time management, career academies, and cooperative learning; extra help for struggling students; and parent and community involvement activities.

Claims¶

  • The Philadelphia evaluation compared five Talent Development schools to six matched control schools over a 3-year period [+W]
  • TDHS Strategic Reading produced a positive effect of +0.32 on CTBS Terra Nova reading outcomes for ninth graders compared to a control group in one Baltimore study [+W]
