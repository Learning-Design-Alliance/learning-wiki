# MAP Reading and Mathematics computerized adaptive tests (NWEA)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
MAP tests are computerized adaptive assessments published by NWEA since 2000 for grades 3-9, intended "to provide educators with information to inform teaching and learning in Reading, Mathematics, and Science". The Reading test has about 40 multiple-choice items and four goals; the Mathematics test has 50 items and seven goals. Tests are aligned to state content standards, and "the marginal reliabilities of tests across 50 states and grades are consistently in the low to mid 0.90's".
