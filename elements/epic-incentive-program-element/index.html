Effective Practice Incentive Community (EPIC) incentive program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

EPIC is a program run with New Leaders for New Schools in which, within each partner site, incentives are offered to educators in eligible schools with significant student achievement gains to help document and disseminate their practices. The 2008 principal/vice principal survey was conducted as part of the program's implementation and evaluation in the Memphis and Washington, DC districts and a charter school consortium.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Partner sites (Memphis and Washington, DC districts and a charter school consortium) partnered with New Leaders for New Schools

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Educators in eligible EPIC schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Documenting and disseminating effective educational practices tied to student achievement gains

Claims¶

  • Most principals in EPIC sites have positive attitudes toward EPIC and merit pay in general [+W]
  • Principals exhibit an incomplete understanding of how achievement growth is captured in EPIC performance measures [~W]
  • Principals prefer school-level performance and achievement growth over teacher-level performance alone or test-score levels for incentive awards [+W]
  • The EPIC evaluation baseline report compares student characteristics across the program's three partners and compares award-determination data with data on all students in the awarded schools [+W]

Examples¶
