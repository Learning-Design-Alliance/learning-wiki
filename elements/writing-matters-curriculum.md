# Writing Matters second-year ECED curriculum

## Description
Writing Matters is "The second year curriculum" of ECED. It "strengthens students' abilities to share and communicate information with other, and identify ways to express and personalize their knowledge". Like Reading Matters, it is taught as a stand-alone class by reallocated school staff and is supported by the same professional development structure, though second-year professional development is optional but recommended.
