Mathematica multi-site random assignment evaluation of TIF pay-for-performance bonuses¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Mathematica's congressionally mandated study, prepared for the U.S. Department of Education's National Center for Education Evaluation, is described as "one of the largest multi-site random assignment evaluations conducted on the impact of pay-for-performance bonuses." It examines TIF implementation for all 2010 grantees and bonus impacts in a subset of 12 districts, with a time frame of 2009-2017.
