## Description
A research project on "Middle School Math: Culturally Responsive Materials, Teacher Professional Learning, and Student Engagement," conducted from 2019 to 2024, prepared for the Gates Foundation, and carried out across multiple districts. The brief draws its lessons from "our experience engaging study participants in the research process for a study of middle school math curricula and instruction," positioning partner engagement as central to the study's research-to-action approach.
