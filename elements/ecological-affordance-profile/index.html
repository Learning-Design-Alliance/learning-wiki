Ecological Affordance Profile: integrating micro-level observations, formative records, and summative accounts¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 theoretical), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Ecological Affordance Profile is the paper's proposed reporting artifact, integrating micro-level observations, formative records, and summative accounts into a coherent developmental history. It is designed to document and report the six affordance foci, offering ecological validity and developmental sensitivity, and providing trace-back capacity when discrepancies arise between test results and classroom evidence. It complements rather than replaces existing outcome assessment.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Documentation mechanisms such as teacher observation logs, preserved digital artifacts, and learner reflections

Constraints¶

  • Intended to complement existing forms of outcome assessment, not to replace them

Target Learners¶

  • higher education language learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • documenting developmental trajectories of communicative competence

Claims¶

  • Affordance resonance can be documented at scale through AI-generated logs identifying seed contributions and cascading uptake [+W]
  • Analysis of 52 higher-education assessment plans found outcome-based orientation dominant, with dynamic elements subsumed under summative scoring [+W]
  • Material arrangement of artifacts (strips centered and ordered by length, worksheets held close) affords and stabilizes the initial pattern of activity; later decentralization enables new patterns [+W]
  • Contextual micro-features (worksheet wording, salient length differences, matching part-whole structure) support persistence of the less-distance-implies-less-time intuition [+W]
