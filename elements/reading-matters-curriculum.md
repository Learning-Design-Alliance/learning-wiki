# Reading Matters first-year ECED curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Reading Matters is "the first year curriculum" of ECED. It "strengthens students' abilities to comprehend and gather information and to identify early learning strategies". It is a structured literacy curriculum based on authentic expository texts, provided as a supplement to regular English, and is taught by reallocated existing school staff with professional development support.
