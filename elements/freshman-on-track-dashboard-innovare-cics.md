# Freshman On-Track dashboard aggregating attendance, grades, discipline, and momentum scores into a single student view

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 4 studies (2 design, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Freshman On-Track dashboard is a co-designed configuration solution that "aggregates attendance, grades, discipline, and momentum scores into a single student view," eliminating manual cross-platform data pulls and creating a holistic student profile. It provides an at-a-glance view of on-track status for individual students and groups, with weekly updates to identify students who need support. It was deployed at CICS Northtown Academy in January alongside end-of-quarter data review cycles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires integrated data infrastructure; the report notes data interoperability issues surfaced that must be resolved before scaling beyond pilot schools.
#### Constraints
- Initially scoped to ninth grade at one pilot school; principals and teachers requested expansion beyond the initial scope.

### Target Learners
- Ninth-grade high school students at risk of falling off track for graduation

### Target Learning Goals
- Early identification of students needing support and timely assignment of appropriate interventions
