School-specific summary and details reports generated from survey participation¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The UChicago Consortium prepares two kinds of school-specific reports: summary reports showing how teachers and students responded on survey measures over several years, and details reports showing how respondents answered specific survey questions. Reports are prepared only for schools meeting participation thresholds — the article states reports are prepared "for every school in which at least 42 percent of teachers or 50 percent of students participate in the survey."
