# Technology access support for participating families

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program specifies its technology needs and provides access support: "Families can communicate with Waterford Upstart staff to track their child's progress on a phone or tablet," and "Some families may qualify for a computer and Wi-Fi at no cost," reducing device and connectivity barriers for low-income households participating in the home-based program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer with a mouse or laptop with a trackpad, or a tablet
#### Constraints
- Families without qualifying devices or connectivity depend on the program's no-cost computer and Wi-Fi provision

### Target Learners
- PreK–2 children in low-income households

### Target Learning Goals
- Enabling consistent participation in daily online early learning lessons
