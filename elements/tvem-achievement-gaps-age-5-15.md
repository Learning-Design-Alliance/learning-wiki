# Time-varying effect modeling (TVEM) of achievement gaps across continuous age 5 to 15

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Time-varying effect modeling (TVEM) is the analytic approach the study uses to estimate "how the associations among race/ethnicity, poverty status, and math and reading achievement vary across continuous age from age 5 to age 15." Unlike estimates fixed to grade levels, TVEM yields gap trajectories as a continuous function of age, which is how the study locates the period around ages 7-8 when poverty-group gaps widen.
