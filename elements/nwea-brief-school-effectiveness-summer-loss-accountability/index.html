NWEA research brief on school effectiveness, summer loss and federal accountability¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 2019 NWEA research brief, listed under the topics Growth modeling, Seasonal learning patterns & summer loss, and Student growth & accountability policies. Its description invites readers to "Learn more about the effect of seasonality on estimates of school effectiveness and how ignoring summer loss can impact which schools are identified as low performers." It is associated with a working paper applying the compound polynomial model in a program evaluation context.
