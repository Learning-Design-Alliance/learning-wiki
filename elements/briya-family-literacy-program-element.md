# Briya family literacy program integrating adult English, digital literacy, and parenting education with children's early education

## Description
Briya Public Charter School is "a free family literacy program in Washington, D.C." where "Adults study English, digital literacy, and parenting, while children receive a high-quality early education." It operates four D.C. campuses with classrooms for parents and children, plus support services through a partnership with Mary's Center, a federally qualified health center. The program integrates parents' and children's educational experiences to improve families' educational, economic, and social opportunities.
