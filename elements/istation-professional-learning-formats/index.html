Istation professional learning for teachers, instructional coaches, and principals¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes an implementation-support component: "A variety of professional learning is available to train teachers, instructional coaches, principals, and others." Formats include virtual and in-person training, and sessions are conducted by qualified professionals with classroom experience. This element supports classroom implementation of the program by the adults who deliver it.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Training is delivered by qualified professionals with classroom experience.

Constraints¶

  • The article does not report effects of the professional development itself.

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers, instructional coaches, principals, and other school staff implementing Istation

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective implementation of the Istation Reading assessment and supplemental curriculum

Claims¶

  • Istation use is associated with significantly greater NWEA MAP Reading growth than comparison students in a South Carolina district (ES +0.06) [+W]
  • Istation users scored significantly higher on STAAR Reading than non-using schoolmates in a large urban Texas district (ES +0.10) [+W]
  • Across two quasi-experimental studies, Istation Reading shows a weighted mean effect size of +0.08, qualifying it for a Promising evidence rating [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Istation Reading: computer-adaptive assessment and supplemental reading curriculum for prekindergarten through Grade 8
  • NWEA professional learning modules supporting MAP Growth implementation and formative practice
