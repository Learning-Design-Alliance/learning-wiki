# Reading Apprenticeship (RA) family of secondary literacy programs

## Description
Reading Apprenticeship is "a family of high school programs designed to improve students' literacy by integrating metacognitive strategy instruction into content areas, especially science and social studies." All variations incorporate extensive reading, collaborative sense-making, and teaching of metacognitive skills such as prediction, summarization, graphic organizers, and clarification. The review rates the family Strong based on four studies.
