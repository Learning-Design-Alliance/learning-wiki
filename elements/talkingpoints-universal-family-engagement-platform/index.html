TalkingPoints Universal Family Engagement platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

TalkingPoints is an education technology nonprofit platform that "drives student outcomes by facilitating effective family-school partnerships". It removes barriers to engaging with families such as technology, language, capacity, mindsets, and time, providing educators with embedded research-based guidance, data-informed insights, and "universally designed auto-translated two-way communication in families' preferred languages".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Families need only a simple cellular phone with basic text messaging to get started

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • students in grades 3-8 in a large urban school district

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved math achievement
  • improved English language arts achievement

Claims¶

  • ELA score gains under TalkingPoints were positive but not statistically significant [+W]
  • TalkingPoints use is associated with significant math score improvements in grades 3-8 [+W]
  • TalkingPoints holds a Tier 3 (Promising Evidence) rating based on one study of about 30,000 students with an average effect size of +0.06 [+W]
