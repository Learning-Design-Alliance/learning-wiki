# Milpitas Early Learning Transitions Model (ELTM)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The ELTM is a district program funded by the Heising-Simons Foundation in 2012–2013 to "enhance supports for children and families as they prepare for elementary school." It has many components, one key feature being integration of a computerized adaptive assessment into preschool teachers' practice. Mathematica Policy Research conducted a formative evaluation of it in the district's two preschool child development centers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District preschool programs implementing the model with foundation funding and an external formative evaluation
#### Constraints
- Implemented in one district's two preschool child development centers

### Target Learners
- preschool children ages 3 to 5

### Target Learning Goals
- school readiness in literacy and mathematical skills for the transition to elementary school
