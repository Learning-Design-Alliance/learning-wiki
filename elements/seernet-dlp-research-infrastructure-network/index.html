SEERNet: five digital learning platforms, six research teams, and a network hub coordinated as research infrastructure¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 qualitative), q1–q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

SEERNet is an IES-funded initiative begun in late 2021 comprising five platform teams, six research teams, and one network hub. The platforms are ASSISTments/E-TRIALS, OpenStax Kinetic, Terracotta/Canvas, UpGrade/MATHia, and ASU Learning@Scale. The paper explains, "The network hub connects the platform and research teams, and also links to practitioners and other stakeholders," and the funded research studies explore existing platform data and test whether platform enhancements increase student learning outcomes. The hub also documents progress through working papers and maintains a public website.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • IES funding of platforms to extend research capabilities and of a hub to coordinate efforts among the teams

Constraints¶

  • Each research team uses one specific platform, so studies are scoped to that platform's affordances and limits

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 and higher-education students using the platforms

Target Learning Goals¶

  • advancing scalable, practice-grounded educational research on student learning

Claims¶

  • OpenStax Kinetic shifted from gift cards to shareable digital badges to incentivize adult learners to participate in research studies [+W]
  • Studies run on the E-TRIALS platform reach far larger samples than is typical in educational research, with nearly 20% enrolling over 1,000 participants [+W]
  • SEERNet platforms trade off open science against availability of individual student data, with eTrials and ASU L@S at opposite extremes [+W]

Examples¶

  • Use a two-phase feasibility process — a pre-submission platform feasibility letter and a post-award six-month feasibility phase — before research funds are committed
  • Plan an extended feasibility phase in which research and platform teams jointly refine design, implementation, and logistics
  • Run conversational office hours in which practitioners and researchers co-design research questions with platform representatives
