Checklist 4: reporting evidence of reliability and construct validity¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The fourth checklist prompts researchers to report "evidence of the reliability and construct validity of the measures." It directs authors to document the psychometric evidence supporting the measures used, so readers can judge whether scores are dependable and measure the intended constructs. It is the fourth component of the brief's five-checklist framework for complete reporting of education research measures.
