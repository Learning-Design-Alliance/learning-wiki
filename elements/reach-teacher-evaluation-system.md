# REACH teacher evaluation system

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
REACH (Recognizing Educators Advancing Chicago Students) is Chicago Public Schools' teacher evaluation system launched in fall 2012. It replaced a checklist system with multiple observations rated on a detailed rubric, and "Final evaluation scores are based on a combination of these observations and student growth metrics" including performance tasks and value-added scores. Professional practice scores are combined with student growth scores into an overall REACH score ranging from 100 to 400, translated into ratings of Unsatisfactory, Developing, Proficient, or Excellent.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be observed four times to be assigned a REACH rating, with formal observations lasting at least 45 minutes and including pre- and post-observation conferences.
#### Constraints
- Only principals and assistant principals can be certified evaluators in CPS.
- Weights for professional practice and student growth differ by teacher type, grade, and subject, and changed across Years 1-3.

### Target Learners
- CPS classroom teachers (tenured and non-tenured)

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving teacher effectiveness and instruction through evaluation
