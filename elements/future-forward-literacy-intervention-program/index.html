Future Forward: one-on-one early literacy tutoring integrated with family engagement¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 9 claims (8 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Future Forward is a freestanding early literacy program for kindergarten through third grade students struggling with reading, integrating one-on-one tutoring and family engagement. Each site has a site manager, typically a certified teacher, who manages paraprofessional tutors, and a family engagement coordinator responsible for "family outreach and communication, which involves monthly family events and ongoing contacts". Tutoring sessions cover letter sounds, phonological awareness, shared readings, tutor read aloud, leveled readings, and writing.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Sites must provide students with a minimum of three 30-minute tutoring sessions per week
  • Sites must communicate with families at least two times per month
  • Working with an average of 30 highest-need struggling readers per school requires 1 Program Manager, 1 Family Engagement Coordinator, and 6.5 FTE tutors

Constraints¶

  • Computers and internet are required for virtual implementation; no student technology is required for in-person implementation

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten through third grade students struggling with reading

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Foundational literacy skills
  • Reading at grade level by the end of third grade

Claims¶

  • A COVID-19-modified version of Future Forward yielded a non-significant but positive effect of +0.09 [+W]
  • A one-semester version of Future Forward showed significant reading gains on Star Reading and DIBELS [+W]
  • Future Forward produces statistically significant gains in foundational literacy skills in a two-year RCT [+W]
  • A regression discontinuity study found a positive but non-significant effect of +0.16 on standardized literacy growth [+W]
  • Future Forward holds 'Strong' evidence ratings for Family Engagement and Attendance in addition to literacy [+W]
  • The tutoring component of Future Forward scaled up successfully to 14 schools, with 89.7% of participants receiving the intended sessions [+W]
  • The family engagement component of Future Forward did not fully scale up, with only about 30% of families receiving intended contacts [-W]
  • One year of Future Forward participation raises regular-school-day attendance of lower-primary students relative to business-as-usual literacy instruction [+W]
  • Prior randomized studies of Future Forward/SPARK met WWC standards Without Reservations and found positive impacts on literacy and attendance [+W]
