# Spanish MAP Reading Fluency adaptive group-administered assessment with automatic speech scoring

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Spanish MAP Reading Fluency is "an adaptive assessment that supports students on their path to reading comprehension by assessing and helping to improve both oral reading ﬂuency and foundational reading skills." Students take a 20-minute benchmark up to three times a year in group setting, wearing headsets; audio "is recorded and scored automatically by the speech scoring engine, rather than relying on human judgment and individual administration." Reports give proficiency relative to grade-level expectations, an individualized literacy profile, and recommended next steps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students wear headsets with microphones
- grade-level expectations exist for Grades PK-3, with students above Grade 3 compared to Grade 3 expectations
#### Constraints
- Designed for students who do not yet read with solid fluency and understanding, in Pre-K to Grade 4

### Target Learners
- Pre-K to Grade 4 students
- emergent bilinguals

### Target Learning Goals
- oral reading fluency
- foundational reading skills
- reading comprehension
