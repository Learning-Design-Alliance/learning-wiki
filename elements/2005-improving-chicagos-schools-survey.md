# 2005 Improving Chicago's Schools survey of Chicago public school students, teachers, and principals

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A system-wide survey administered in spring 2005 across the Chicago public school system, in which "nearly 130,000 students, teachers, and principals" participated. Students reported on "their school experiences, attitudes, and activities," while teachers and principals reported on classroom instruction, professional development experiences, and their working conditions.
