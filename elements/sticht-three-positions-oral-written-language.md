# Sticht's three theoretical positions linking oral and written language

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The article cites three theoretical positions from Sticht's Reading for Working supporting the stage sequence: "1. Oral language skills develop to a fairly high level prior tothe development of written language skills. 2. Oral and written languages share much the samelexicon (vocabulary) and syntax (grammar). 3. Beginning readers draw upontheir knowledge of oral language in learning to read." These positions ground the claim that literacy builds on oral competency and are used to argue foreign language teaching must respect the sequence.
