# GADRRS: an openly licensed data sharing agreement template for educational data exchanges

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
GADRRS is a fillable Data Sharing Agreement developed by the University of Florida Lastinger Center for Learning and licensed CC BY-ND 4.0 for use by any entity. It documents rights and obligations for the transmission, storage, use, and destruction of protected or confidential information, with exhibits for definitions, services, the studies exception, data to be shared, additional restrictions, and a data narrative. Its stated purpose is a clear, concise, and comprehensive agreement around key issues related to ethical and legal data sharing and transparency for stakeholders including families, educators, administrators, and local school board members.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Parties must complete party identification, term, exhibits (services, studies exception, data, restrictions), and designated Data Compliance Official and Data Custodian before execution.
#### Constraints
- The agreement complies with United States Federal Laws and a specified State's laws; it is licensed under CC BY-ND 4.0, which the article notes restricts modification of the license terms.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students whose education records are shared

### Target Learning Goals
- Ethical and legal sharing of student data to support educational services and research that improve instruction and educational outcomes
