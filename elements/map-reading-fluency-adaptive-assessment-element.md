# MAP Reading Fluency adaptive oral reading fluency assessment with dyslexia screener and progress monitoring

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
MAP Reading Fluency is an online, adaptive assessment for pre-K to grade 5 readers measuring foundational skills, literal comprehension, language comprehension, and oral reading fluency, with automatic speech-scoring and group administration in one class period. It includes four benchmark tests, a 5- to 10-minute progress-monitoring tool, and an optional K-3 dyslexia screener, and is "Available in English and Spanish." The paper describes it as the entry point that places students into personalized coaching pathways.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Speech-recognition technology for automatic scoring of recorded oral reading; administration individually or to a whole class or large groups
#### Constraints
- The dyslexia screener and progress monitoring are available for MAP Reading Fluency English only

### Target Learners
- pre-K to grade 5 readers, including at-risk students and older students still working on essential literacy skills

### Target Learning Goals
- oral reading fluency
- foundational skills
- literal comprehension
- language comprehension
- dyslexia screening
