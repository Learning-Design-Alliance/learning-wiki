# Monitoring Report form for submitting self-monitoring results to the state JTPA office

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A summary reporting form mirroring the 28 instrument items, with spaces for the JTPA coordinator visited, superintendent, date, ratings, and comments per item. It operationalizes the final step of the process: "Following the program monitor, the school districtsubmits the completed Monitoring Report to the State JTPA office." The form also records the rating scale used for each compliance item.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completion after the program monitor and presentation of findings to district staff who participated in the review
#### Constraints
- Specific to JTPA programs monitored by North Carolina's Division of Support Programs

### Target Learners
- JTPA coordinators and district superintendents

### Target Learning Goals
- Documenting and communicating program compliance findings to the state office
