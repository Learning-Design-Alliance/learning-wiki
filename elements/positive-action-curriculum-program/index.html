Positive Action K-12 character education curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Positive Action is a K-12 program promoting "character development, academic achievement, and social-emotional skills" and reducing problem behavior, built on the philosophy that "you feel good about yourself when you think and do positive actions." The curriculum has six units (plus a review unit in some grades), with scripted lessons using "classroom discussion, role-play, games, songs, and activity sheets or text booklets." Elementary grades average 140 lessons taught four days a week; typical lessons last about 15 minutes. Optional components include site-wide climate development, drug education, conflict resolution, counselor/parent/family classes, and community components.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher/staff training was provided by the program developer prior to each school year in the reviewed trial, plus annual in-service sessions and a winter mini-conference on schoolwide components

Constraints¶

  • The WWC notes the program "may have changed since the studies were conducted" and that student demographics and school context may affect outcomes
  • In the Hawaii trial, even high-implementation schools were not implementing at the level the developer would expect; few schools implemented the family- or community-involvement programs

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students, with reviewed evidence from elementary school students in grades K-5

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Character development
  • social-emotional skills
  • academic achievement
  • reduction of disruptive and problem behavior

Claims¶

  • The WWC's extent of evidence for Positive Action is moderate to large for behavior and academic achievement, with no qualifying studies addressing knowledge, attitudes, and values [+M]
  • In the Hawaii randomized trial, Positive Action showed no statistically significant effects on state standardized test scores in reading and math [-M]
  • Positive Action has positive effects on elementary school students' academic achievement, with a WWC domain average effect size of 0.37 and an improvement index of +14 percentile points [+W]
  • Positive Action has positive effects on elementary school students' behavior, with a WWC domain average effect size of 0.51 and an improvement index of +19 percentile points [+W]
  • In the Hawaii randomized trial, Positive Action significantly reduced grade retention, with an effect size of 1.09 and an improvement index of +36 [+W]
  • In the Hawaii randomized trial, Positive Action reduced serious violence among boys but not among girls [+W]
