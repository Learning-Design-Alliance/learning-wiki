# Non-Examples

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Non-examples are intentionally chosen instances that do not meet the target concept, criterion, or quality standard. They are useful when learners need to sharpen category boundaries, detect common errors, or distinguish a strong model from a flawed one.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A clear target concept or quality criterion**
- **Contrast with examples or correct models**
- **Prompts that ask learners what makes the item a non-example**
#### Constraints
- **Absolute novices may encode the wrong feature if contrast is unclear**
- **Trivial non-examples do little instructional work**

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve discrimination, concept clarity, and error detection.
