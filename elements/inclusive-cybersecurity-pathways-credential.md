## Description
An inclusive cybersecurity pathways initiative offering workforce credentials, enrolling "Over 800 students... across 10 school districts reflecting a uniquely diverse student population — 25 percent female, 12 percent with learning differences, 43 percent free and reduced lunch, 54 percent students of color."
