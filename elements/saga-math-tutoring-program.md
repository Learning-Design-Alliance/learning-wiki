# Saga Math high-dosage tutoring program (traditional and technology-enhanced models)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (7 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 10 claims rest on one study

## Description
Saga Math is a supplemental in-school math tutoring program for 9th and 10th graders offered in a traditional model at 1:2 ("research-proven"), 1:3, or 1:4 tutor-to-student ratios, and a technology-enhanced model at up to 1:8 in which half the students work with the tutor while the other half use "a computer-assisted learning platform called ALEKS", switching every other session. Tutoring is embedded in the school schedule at least three times weekly for 90 minutes total, aligned to Common Core standards, with tutorial instruction informed by assessments, observations, and progress-monitoring data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutoring must be embedded in students' regular school schedules and occur a minimum of three times per week for 90 minutes
- An assigned staff member is required in the classroom to support students, supervise tutors, and act as teacher-of-record
- Tutor supervisors typically meet with primary math teachers weekly to align lessons
#### Constraints
- The program is supplemental to students' primary math class and must not take away from Tier 1 instruction, extracurricular activities, or other responsibilities

### Target Learners
- 9th and 10th grade students in economically disadvantaged high schools, largely Black and Latinx, including at-risk students and diverse learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Secondary mathematics achievement aligned with Common Core standards and state exams
