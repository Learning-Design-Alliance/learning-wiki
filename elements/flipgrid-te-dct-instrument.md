# Flipgrid-based technology-enhanced DCT (TE-DCT) for eliciting nonverbal pragmatic data

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A data-elicitation instrument in which each discourse completion scenario pairs a written description (in the participant's L1) with a 3–5 second video clip showing spatial elements such as "distance to interlocutor, bodily stance, and direction of gaze". Participants download the Flipgrid application, watch each prompt, and video-record their oral responses on their own phones, capturing nonverbal devices. Responses are coded in V-Note software using a feature-based gesture scheme. The article reports it was successful in capturing nonverbal attention-getters in the majority of responses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants need a smartphone with the Flipgrid application and video capability; instructions tell them to prop up the phone for hands-free recording and include a tip to use words, sounds, motions, or actions
#### Constraints
- Course placement was used as the proficiency indicator, acknowledged as a limitation; gaze was not coded because participants faced the camera and no eye tracking was used; recording could be restarted, unlike real summons

### Target Learners
- L1 Spanish speakers
- intermediate and advanced L2 Spanish learners at university level

### Target Learning Goals
- pragmatic ability in the speech act of attention-getting
- nonverbal communication devices in L2 Spanish
