MAP Growth assessment¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MAP Growth is an assessment product published by NWEA, listed as the product associated with NWEA research publications on achievement, growth, equity, and test engagement. In the supplied text it appears repeatedly as "Products: MAP Growth" across related technical reports, research briefs, and data tools, including national trend dashboards reporting "student academic achievement and growth for grades K-8." It is the assessment context within which NWEA research on test effort and subgroup score differences is situated.
