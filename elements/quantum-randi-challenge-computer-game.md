# Quantum Randi Challenge: a modifiable computer game teaching quantum mechanics

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The QRC is a computer game that anybody can modify, designed to reject hidden variable models by simply teaching quantum mechanics. Per the article, "there is no bet or interaction with challengers". The present version includes a simulation of true quantum behavior violating Bell 99% of the time, hidden variables violating Bell and CHSH with 50% probability, and ones violating Bell 85% of the time when missing 13% anti-correlation. The challenge is to modify the hidden variables so the predicted quantum behavior, including anti-correlation, arises.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Challengers modify only the construction of the hidden variables and the measurement parts of the code; the random choice of angles must stay unaltered
- Future downloadable programs shall be interactive tutorials that are also Alice and Bob role games
#### Constraints
- The QRC refuses interaction with challengers and rejects all statistical thresholds where a bet is lost by sheer luck
- It is addressed at those who can accept quantum mechanics as validated theory, not at detection-loophole or superdeterminism positions

### Target Learners
- educated lay audience
- online physics communities
- IT professionals and artists who can help perfecting it

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding why local hidden variable models cannot reproduce quantum correlations
- understanding randomness and Bell inequality violations
