# NIH Intramural Loan Repayment Programs

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The NIH Intramural Loan Repayment Programs are an incentive mechanism by which, for over a decade, the National Institutes of Health has been "using repayment of education loans as an incentive to attract and retain first-rate biomedical scientists in its labs and clinics." The evaluation assesses the programs' role in recruitment and retention outcomes using survey data on participants and nonparticipants.
