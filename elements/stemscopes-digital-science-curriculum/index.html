STEMscopes digital science curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

STEMscopes is a comprehensive digital science curriculum for elementary science instruction that "uses a hands-on, inquiry-based approach aligned to state standards." It combines "digital and print resources as well as hands-on materials" and embeds "interactive investigations, real-world problem solving, and embedded formative assessments" to promote student engagement across diverse classrooms.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need computer and/or touch screen devices (e.g., IPad) to access the digital curriculum resources

Constraints¶

  • No additional staffing requirements are needed

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 5 students in diverse classrooms

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Grade 5 science achievement aligned to state standards

Claims¶

  • A smaller student subset showed a similar STEMscopes advantage on the NWEA MAP Growth science assessment (effect size +0.17) [+W]
  • Grade 5 students using STEMscopes significantly outperformed comparison students on STAAR Science (effect size +0.17), though the school-level difference was not significant [+W]
