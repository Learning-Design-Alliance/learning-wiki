# Four elementary school math curricula compared in the evaluation

## Description
The evaluation compares four distinct elementary school math curricula named by the article: "Investigations in Number, Data, and Space®; Math Expressions; Saxon Math™; and Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics (SFAW)." These curricula are the treatments whose relative effectiveness on first-grade math achievement the experimental study estimates. The comparison produced the article's headline finding of a 9 to 12 percentile-point advantage for two of the four curricula.
