1997 Improving Chicago's Schools Individual School Report (survey-based school report instrument)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This artifact is an individual school report built from two surveys — Improving Chicago's Schools: The Student Survey, 1997 and Improving Chicago's Schools: The Teacher Survey, 1997 — administered to sixth, eighth, and tenth grade students and to elementary and high school teachers in spring 1997. Its stated purpose was "to provide individual schools with useful information about themselves to help in their planning and self-analysis, and to guide their improvement efforts." Part 1 gives ten profile graphs comparing the school to the citywide average and to schools with similar ITBS scores; Part 2 gives two pages per scale showing item-level responses and frequency distributions against bottom- and top-quartile schools.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A response rate high enough to receive an individual school report; schools not meeting this threshold do not receive one.

Constraints¶

  • Four negatively worded scales are scored in reverse, with a low score indicating a strength; about 15 percent of schools lack results on the Support for New Students scale because few new students completed the survey.

Target Learners¶

  • elementary and high school students in Chicago public schools
  • teachers in participating elementary and high schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • school improvement planning and self-analysis
  • academic achievement
