Evidence-Based Guide on Presenting School Choice Information to Parents (with technical appendix)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An evidence-based guide, published October 30, 2018 by the National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education, on presenting school choice information to parents. The page states the guide "was informed by a recent study" of how presentation formats affect parents' understanding, perception, and use of school information, and that a technical appendix "provides a full description of the study's design, methods (including the analytic approach), and findings." The guide was prepared for the U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences and The Walton Family Foundation under a 2015-2018 project on making shopping for schools easier.
