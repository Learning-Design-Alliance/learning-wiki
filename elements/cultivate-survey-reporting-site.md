# Cultivate student experience survey and reporting site

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Cultivate is a student experience survey developed by the UChicago Consortium's Learning & Development Group, administered districtwide in CPS since fall 2022 to students in grades 5-12 twice a school year about one of their core subject classes. It asks students about nine classroom learning conditions and their own learning strategies and beliefs. The reporting site provides school-level reports depicting responses to each learning condition, an ordering of weakest to strongest conditions, changes over time, and disaggregation by grade level, subject area, or student demographics. Data is schoolwide and shared first with school leaders, and does not identify individual teachers and classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration to students in grades 5-12 about a core subject class
- School leaders receive schoolwide reports first and may share data with others
#### Constraints
- Data is schoolwide and does not identify individual teachers and classrooms
- District did not intend the data for evaluation and accountability, but for improvement and learning

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 5-12 in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving classroom learning conditions
- Centering student perspectives and daily experiences in school improvement
