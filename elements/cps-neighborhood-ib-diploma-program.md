# International Baccalaureate Diploma Program in Chicago neighborhood high schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The IB DP is an internationally recognized academic program that CPS scaled from its flagship Lincoln Park High School site to 13 neighborhood high schools beginning in 1997, an expansion unmatched in scale by any other US district at the time. It served predominantly African American and Latino first-generation college students, with test scores above average but typically below selective-enrollment admission thresholds. The Consortium study examined the program in 12 neighborhood high schools.
