# Reading Horizons Discovery K-3 foundational reading curriculum

## Description
Reading Horizons Discovery is a K-3 foundational reading program built on "the instructional principles of Orton-Gillingham and Structured Literacy." At its center is the Reading Horizons method, in which instruction is "systematic and cumulative, organized in a sequence that appropriately scaffolds teaching and learning." Each English sound is explicitly taught with its representing letter(s), and students practice and apply, or "transfer," the skills learned through engaging activities. The program runs on Windows, Chrome, Edge, and Safari and requires no special staff qualifications or certifications.
