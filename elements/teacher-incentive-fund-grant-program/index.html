Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) federal grant program supporting performance-based compensation systems¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program that "provides federal grants to support performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools." The 2010 cohort was evaluated nationally over multiple years, with this snapshot covering the third of four years of implementation in the 2013–2014 school year, including in-depth analysis in ten evaluation districts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Districts implement performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals under TIF grant guidance

Constraints¶

  • The evaluation covers 2010 TIF grantees in high-need schools over a four-year implementation window

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 teachers and principals in high-need schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Educator effectiveness and student math and reading achievement through performance-based compensation

Claims¶

  • Few districts structured pay-for-performance bonuses to align well with TIF grant guidance [~W]
  • Many teachers misunderstood whether they were eligible for performance bonuses or the amount they could earn [~W]
  • After three years of Teacher Incentive Fund implementation, pay-for-performance bonuses had small positive impacts on student math and reading achievement in ten evaluation districts [+W]
