Value-added models (VAMs) developed and estimated for Phase 1 of the Pennsylvania Teacher and Principal Evaluation Pilot¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article describes a set of value-added models (VAMs) developed and estimated as technical assistance for Phase 1 of the Pennsylvania Teacher and Principal Evaluation Pilot. The page states that "Mathematica provided technical assistance in developing and estimating VAMs for use in Phase 1 of the pilot." The VAMs served as the measurement component of the pilot's educator evaluation system.
