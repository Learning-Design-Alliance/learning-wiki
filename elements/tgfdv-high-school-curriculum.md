# Too Good for Drugs and Violence high school curriculum: 14 core lessons plus 12 infusion lessons with role-play and cooperative learning

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Too Good for Drugs and Violence is a school-based character education curriculum designed to promote high school students' prosocial skills, positive character traits, and violence- and drug-free norms. It consists of "14 core lessons and an additional 12 lessons that can be infused into other subject areas". Lessons are scripted and combine normative information with life skills development, using "role-play and cooperative learning activities" to encourage applying skills to different contexts. Developer materials include grade-level kits, student workbooks, parent newsletters, and a staff development curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lessons are scripted and intended to be taught by trained teachers or Too Good instructors; teacher training workshops are available from the developer
#### Constraints
- The program includes optional family and community involvement components that may or may not have been implemented as part of the program evaluated

### Target Learners
- High school students in grades 9-12

### Target Learning Goals
- Prosocial skills
- positive character traits
- violence- and drug-free norms
- goal setting, decisionmaking, peer resistance, and interpersonal skills
