# M&E Checklist Phase 3 document: Assess for Early Evidence of Success

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Phase 3 document is one portion of the Measurement and Evaluation (M&E) Checklist, a resource for evidence-building with stakeholders from a priority community. It "guides users specifically through Phase 3 of the evidence-building process, which describes how to conduct a small pilot study" to show a solution is associated with improved student outcomes. A companion glossary is provided.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users work in collaboration with stakeholders from a priority community, as the checklist process prescribes
#### Constraints
- Covers only Phase 3 of the four-phase evidence-building process; other phases have separate documents

### Target Learners
- students in a priority community

### Target Learning Goals
- improved student outcomes in middle years math solutions
