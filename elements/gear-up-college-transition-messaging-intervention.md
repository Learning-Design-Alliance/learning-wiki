# GEAR UP college transition text-messaging intervention (37 customized messages with advisor contact option)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A low-cost behavioral nudge in which GEAR UP students were sent 37 text messages "customized to their college and including the option to communicate with an advisor." The U.S. Department of Education's IES commissioned the evaluation of this messaging strategy as a way of supporting college enrollment and completion for low-income and first-generation students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires customized message content tied to students' specific colleges and an advisor available to respond
#### Constraints
- Did not increase enrollment or persistence in this evaluation

### Target Learners
- Low-income and first-generation college-going high school students in GEAR UP

### Target Learning Goals
- College enrollment and persistence after high school
