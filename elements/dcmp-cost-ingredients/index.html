DCMP implementation costs and resource requirements¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report provides a preliminary list of cost ingredients for implementing DCMP, based on information available as of December 2020. As reported in Rutschow et al. (2019), "the average DCMP start-up cost for each college was about $140,450 over two years", with ongoing costs of $19,340 per year per college or $132 per student. Cost ingredients include personnel training, facilities, curricula with a homework platform fee of approximately $40 per student, and start-up activities such as pathway alignment and transfer agreements.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Faculty, advisors, and other support staff receive initial training and ongoing professional development on how to implement DCMP
  • standard faculty and advisor office space and classroom space

Constraints¶

  • The preliminary cost list is not designed to be exhaustive; the Dana Center curricula are offered for free but using them is not a requirement for implementing DCMP.

Target Learners¶

  • Community college students in developmental math

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Implementation of accelerated, aligned mathematics pathways
