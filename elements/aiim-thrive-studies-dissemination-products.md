# Studies of Arkansas AIIM and THRIVE with findings presentations and infographic products

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The logic model lists two studies as partnership activities: "Study of Arkansas AIIM" and "Study of Arkansas THRIVE." Each is paired with dissemination products — presenting findings and producing infographics summarizing them — intended to build ADE staff understanding of program progress, trends, successes, and challenges and of districts' implementation of AIIM. A virtual panel of district leaders implementing THRIVE is planned to increase education and awareness of the program.
