# Post-secondary Attainment Index (PAI): a composite projection of CPS ninth-graders who would complete a college credential within 10 years

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (3 associational), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The PAI represents "the proportion of current CPS ninth-graders who would complete any degree or certificate from a two-year or four-year college within 10 years, if current rates of attainment were to hold constant over the next decade." It combines four-year high school graduation, immediate college enrollment, and six-year college completion rates, synthesizing data from multiple recent cohorts rather than following one cohort. The 2022 PAI is 29.9%, up 2.4 percentage points from 27.5% in 2021.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires four-year high school graduation rates, immediate college enrollment rates, and six-year college completion rates, each from the most recent available cohort
#### Constraints
- The PAI is not a true cohort attainment rate and is not intended to be a prediction of what will happen; the report recommends focusing on long-term trends rather than year-to-year fluctuations

### Target Learners
- Chicago Public Schools ninth-graders

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding district patterns of high school graduation, college enrollment, and college completion
