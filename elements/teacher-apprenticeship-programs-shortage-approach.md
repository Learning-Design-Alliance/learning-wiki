# Teacher apprenticeship programs as an approach to mitigating teacher shortages

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Teacher apprenticeship programs are presented as a relatively new approach to mitigating teacher shortages. REL Central is supporting two partnerships using this approach: the Mitigating Teacher Shortages in Wyoming through a Teacher Apprenticeship Program partnership and the Strengthening the Teacher Pipeline in South Dakota to Alleviate Teacher Shortages partnership. The publication notes that states interested in implementing such programs might consider decisions captured in an accompanying infographic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State or regional systems seeking to address teacher shortages, as in the REL Central region where addressing teacher shortages is among systems' highest education priorities.
#### Constraints
- The publication states there is minimal rigorous research available on the development and implementation of teacher apprenticeship programs.

### Target Learners
- Teacher apprenticeship participants and prospective teachers in Wyoming and South Dakota partnerships

### Target Learning Goals
- Preparing and certifying teachers to enter and remain in the public school workforce
