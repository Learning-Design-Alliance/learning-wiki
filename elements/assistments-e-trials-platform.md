# ASSISTments/E-TRIALS: free A/B testing platform integrated with a K-12 math practice platform

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
E-TRIALS is described as "a free learning science A/B testing platform that allows researchers to conduct randomized controlled trials," integrated with ASSISTments, "a free online K-12 math learning platform supporting millions of students." Teachers assign problems from open educational resources; the platform randomly assigns students to study conditions unobtrusively and supplies datasets covering performance, hint requests, log data, and school demographics. The article uses it to illustrate a four-condition study of hints and explanations with and without SEL language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers using pre-existing content need only add custom hints or explanations; designing custom math problems requires the researchers to recruit their own participants
#### Constraints
- Studies must be submitted to and approved by the E-TRIALS administrator before deployment, and researchers are advised to pre-register on OSF before data are shared

### Target Learners
- K-12 students using ASSISTments

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics problem solving with hints and explanations
