# MAP Reading Fluency adaptive online early literacy assessment

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
MAP Reading Fluency is "an adaptive online assessment that supports students on their path to reading comprehension by assessing and helping to improve both oral reading fluency and foundational reading skills" for pre-K to Grade 5. "The test adapts in real-time and presents easier or more difficult passages and items depending on student performance," and all spoken-response tasks are scored via automated speech recognition. Students typically take 20–30 minutes, and results include proficiency levels, an individualized literacy profile, and recommended next steps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each student needs a computing device and an over-ear headset with a boom microphone
#### Constraints
- The report states a flag on dyslexia screening reports is not a diagnosis of dyslexia or of a reading disability

### Target Learners
- pre-K to Grade 5 students who do not yet read with solid fluency and understanding

### Target Learning Goals
- oral reading fluency
- foundational reading skills
- reading comprehension
