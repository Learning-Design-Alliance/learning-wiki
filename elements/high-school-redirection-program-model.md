# High School Redirection alternative high school model

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
High School Redirection is an alternative high school model for youth at high risk of not completing school, including former dropouts, teen parents, over-age students, and students with low test scores. It "emphasizes basic skill development and offers an intensive remedial reading program, Strategies and Techniques for Advancement in Reading (STAR), for students with serious literacy problems." Schools are small (typically no more than 500 students), offer on-site child care and independent study with accelerated credit accumulation, keep classes small for individualized attention, encourage teachers to act as mentors, issue standard diplomas, and operate with autonomy from the local district.

### Target Learning Goals
