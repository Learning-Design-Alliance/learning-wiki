# Once Early-Reading Program: school-embedded one-on-one structured literacy tutoring delivered by trained paraprofessionals

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Once is a school-embedded early literacy tutoring program serving PreK through first grade students, in which existing school staff, primarily paraprofessionals, deliver daily 15-minute one-on-one structured literacy sessions during the school day. The curriculum is "grounded in the science of reading" and addresses decoding and language comprehension through systematic phonics, phonemic awareness, sight word instruction, and vocabulary development. Lessons are scripted and delivered via a laptop interface, enabling staff without specialized reading instruction backgrounds to deliver sequenced lessons with fidelity. A staff member fully dedicated to the program can serve up to 25 students per day.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each school support staff member delivering Once requires a laptop computer connected to the internet with a front-facing camera; the program is cloud-based with no software to download and no files stored on the computer.
#### Constraints
- The program is designed as a supplemental tier to core classroom instruction, embedded within the school day, not as a replacement for core instruction.

### Target Learners
- PreK through first grade students scoring below grade-level literacy benchmarks

### Target Learning Goals
- Decoding and language comprehension, including phonics, phonemic awareness, sight words, and vocabulary
