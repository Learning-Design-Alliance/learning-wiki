OpenSciEd open science curriculum materials¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

OpenSciEd is a middle school science curriculum described by the article as "a set of Creative Commons licensed, freely available curriculum materials addressing the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)". The materials are adaptable, extensive and detailed, and certified through a peer-review process for NGSS design. The paper treats OpenSciEd both as an intervention and, because of its openness, as an ecosystem supporting research and innovation.
