The newsflash format: a succinct email announcement suited to tablets and smartphones¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The newsflash is a succinct email-blast format announcing new research findings that incorporates a compelling story, engaging visuals, and electronic tools. The guide notes that "The succinct newsflash format is also perfect for smaller devices like tablets and smart phones, which are becoming increasingly important for delivering information."
