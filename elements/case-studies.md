# Case Studies

## Description
Case studies are the element in which learners analyze a concrete scenario, incident, or example in order to reason about concepts, decisions, or consequences. They are useful when learning benefits from applying ideas to situated complexity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A case with meaningful details and decision points**
- **Prompts that ask for analysis rather than summary**
#### Constraints
- **Thin or overly tidy cases may not support rich reasoning**

### Target Learning Goals
- Support applied analysis, judgment, and discussion.
