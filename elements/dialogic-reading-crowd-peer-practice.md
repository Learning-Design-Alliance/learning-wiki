# Dialogic Reading practice with CROWD prompts and PEER technique

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Dialogic Reading is an interactive shared picture-book reading practice in which "the adult and the child switch roles so that the child learns to become the storyteller" while the adult acts as an active listener and questioner. The adult uses five prompt types (CROWD): completion, recall, open-ended, wh-, and distancing prompts, within the PEER sequence where the adult prompts, evaluates, expands, and repeats. As the child becomes familiar with a book, the adult reads less and uses more higher-level prompts connecting the book to the child's own experiences.
