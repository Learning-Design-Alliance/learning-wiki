# LEAD Partnership professional development program for early childhood leaders and teachers

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The LEAD Partnership is a program whose goal is "to support the Oklahoma State Department of Education (OSDE) to increase the use of research-based early learning practices through professional development and support of school leaders, prekindergarten (preK), and kindergarten teachers". Its activities include providing professional development on "play-based learning and high-quality interactions", developing implementation resources, and creating dissemination materials. The program targets improved literacy and math outcomes for early elementary students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participating school leaders must want to improve the use of research-based early learning practices in preK and kindergarten classrooms
#### Constraints
- OSDE's support of research-based early learning practices must continue at least long enough for the potential benefits of the LEAD intervention to be realized

### Target Learners
- prekindergarten students
- kindergarten students

### Target Learning Goals
- improved academic outcomes in literacy and math
