Eksploratoryòm: story-based, culturally relevant interactive STEM lessons in Haitian Creole integrating literacy and SEL¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Eksploratoryòm is the project's digital science content for early grade learners, delivered in Haitian Creole. The presentation describes it as "Story-based, engaging lessons" that are "Local language (Haitian Creole)" and "Culturally relevant," and notes it "Integrates literacy & SEL" with "Immersive, experiential learning for teacher too!" Sample lesson content shown includes a Five Senses (Senk Sans) lesson teaching "See, hear, touch, smell, taste" and counting one through five in Creole. The content is designed to flip the narrative to focus on the student.
