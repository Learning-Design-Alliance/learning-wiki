# Fast ForWord® adaptive computer-based reading program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
Fast ForWord® is "a computer-based reading program intended to help students develop and strengthen the cognitive skills necessary for successful reading and learning." It comprises a Language/Literacy series building memory, attention, processing, sequencing, listening accuracy, and phonological awareness, and a Reading series targeting sound-letter associations, word recognition, vocabulary, and comprehension. The software adapts content and difficulty to individual responses so students respond correctly approximately 80% of the time, with usage options from 30 minutes a day for 12 to 16 weeks to 90 to 100 minutes a day for 4 to 8 weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designed for use 30 to 100 minutes a day, five days a week, for 4 to 16 weeks; students listen through headphones and respond using the mouse
#### Constraints
- A single Fast ForWord® Language license costs $900 and each Reading license $500, with limited quantity discounts

### Target Learners
- adolescent learners in grades 4-12
- students struggling with reading, language, and learning problems

### Target Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- phonological awareness
- vocabulary
- reading fluency
- cognitive skills such as memory, attention, processing, and sequencing
