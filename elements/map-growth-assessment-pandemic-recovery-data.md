# MAP Growth assessments as the data source for pandemic learning-loss analysis

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MAP Growth is NWEA's assessment product whose scores supply the achievement and growth data used in this brief and related NWEA COVID-19 recovery research. The brief draws on "data from 6 million students in grades 3-8 who took MAP Growth assessments in reading and math" to compare pandemic-period outcomes with pre-pandemic trends. Related NWEA publications also use the MAP Growth National Dashboard for post-COVID trend analyses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires administration of MAP Growth assessments in reading and math to generate the score data used for growth modeling against pre-pandemic norms.
#### Constraints
- The brief's analysis covers students in grades 3-8; the available text does not describe results for other grade ranges.

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 3-8

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading achievement
- Mathematics achievement
