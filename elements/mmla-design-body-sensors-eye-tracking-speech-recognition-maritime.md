# Proposed MMLA design for maritime simulation combining body sensors, eye-tracking, and speech recognition

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The proposed multimodal learning analytics design for full-mission bridge simulators proposes sensors and technologies to capture collaborative navigation training: "Body sensors: Capturing the amount of movement, student position in the spatial workspace, distance between students, active posture, and head, hand, and wrist movements"; eye-tracking capturing areas of interest and joint visual attention; and speech recognition capturing linguistic coordination, verbal coherence, and contribution to decision-making. Simulator log data may also be encompassed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Effective implementation requires the correct technology and a focus on appropriate pedagogy
#### Constraints
- The authors encountered problems attempting to assess these data sources' impact on actual learning during simulation

### Target Learners
- maritime students in high-fidelity navigation simulator training

### Target Learning Goals
- teamwork, attention coordination, communication, and professional decision-making on the bridge
