# BARR program: a K–12 training and coaching model built on Relationships and Data pillars

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
BARR is "a training and coaching model that serves K–12 schools with a structured system and tools to improve student outcomes." It is "Grounded in two core pillars – Relationships and Data," strengthening staff-student, staff-staff, and student-student relationships while embedding consistent use of academic and non-academic data into daily practice. Core strategies include teacher teams meeting regularly to discuss each student's strengths and progress, Community Connect meetings linking students to out-of-school resources, extensive professional development and coaching, and family engagement.
