# Greenfield Learning's Comprehensive Literacy: Making the Connections professional learning series

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 10-session (15-hour) professional learning series delivered over 6-8 months for educators, literacy specialists, and curriculum developers. The course "offers a comprehensive exploration of reading research, phonological awareness, phonics, fluency, language comprehension, and writing," and equips educators to support multilingual and neurodivergent learners. It debunks common misconceptions and provides practical strategies for effective teaching, and is suitable for both new and experienced practitioners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants attend live professional learning sessions with a dedicated cohort and complete self-guided learning and application.
#### Constraints
- Delivered virtually, in-person, or in a hybrid model depending on each district's preference.

### Target Learners
- educators, literacy specialists, curriculum developers, and anyone involved in literacy instruction

### Target Learning Goals
- deepen understanding of literacy acquisition
- enhance instructional practices for reading and writing
