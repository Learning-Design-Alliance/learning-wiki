# Measurement and Evaluation Needs Inventory: a self-assessment tool for organizational M&E capacity

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Measurement and Evaluation Needs Inventory is a tool designed to assist an organization in conducting a self-assessment of its existing measurement and evaluation (M&E) capacity. It covers study setup, project monitoring, compliance with research requirements, solution implementation support, instrument development, data collection, data analysis, and data interpretation and reporting, with the goal of identifying priority areas for support from an external research partner.
