College Track comprehensive college preparation program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

College Track is described in the brief as "a comprehensive college preparation program for students from underserved communities." The program serves students from underserved communities and aims to support application to, enrollment in, and persistence in college. Mathematica analyzed existing data to benchmark the postsecondary outcomes of College Track students in the 9th grade cohort of 2009 relative to other students in the same cohort with similar backgrounds.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Serves students from underserved communities, as the brief states the program is a college preparation program for that population

Constraints¶

  • Impact results from the rigorous experimental evaluation of the program are not yet available; the brief reports benchmark comparisons only

Target Learners¶

  • High school students from underserved communities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • College application, college enrollment (especially four-year colleges), and college persistence

Claims¶

  • College Track completers were more likely than similar students to apply to multiple colleges [+W]
  • College Track completers enrolled in college at higher rates than similar students, and were much more likely to enroll in four-year colleges [+W]
  • College Track and Mathematica are conducting a rigorous experimental evaluation of the program, with results not available for several years [+W]
  • College persistence was similar among College Track completers and similar students who enrolled in college, despite College Track's stricter persistence definition [~W]
