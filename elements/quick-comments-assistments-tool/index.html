QUICK-Comments teacher-augmentation tool in ASSISTments¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

QUICK-Comments is a teacher-augmentation tool within the ASSISTments web-based K-12 mathematics platform that supports teachers in assessing open-ended student responses. The article describes that "this tool allows teachers to select from a repository of pre-authored comments, which has been tailored to address common student errors or misconceptions." By streamlining feedback selection, it lets teachers deliver personalized, timely feedback efficiently; the feedback messages analyzed in this study were drawn from its use.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must manually review student open responses, since open-ended problems in ASSISTments require human evaluation rather than automatic assessment

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 mathematics students, specifically middle school mathematics students in this dataset

Target Learning Goals¶

  • supporting teacher feedback on students' open-ended mathematical problem-solving responses

Affordances¶

  • Data Driven Ten Type Math Feedback Taxonomy

Claims¶

  • Perceptions of feedback characteristics vary between researcher and teacher coders, with agreement shaped by professional background [+W]
  • Exploratory factor analysis of coder-assigned feedback codes yields 12 latent factors, several combining multiple codes with inverse loadings [+W]
  • Some teacher feedback codes commonly co-occur, such as Confirmation with Positive Affect and Mathematical Suggestions with Hints [+W]
  • K-means clustering of factor scores identifies 10 distinct types of teacher feedback content, mostly scaffolding-oriented [+W]
