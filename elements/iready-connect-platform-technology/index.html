i-Ready Connect digital platform for teacher-facing content, with browser, SSO, and third-party integration requirements¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

No technology is required for iRCL itself, but related teacher-facing content is accessed through the digital platform i-Ready Connect, which runs on Windows, MacOS, and Chromebook machines with browsers enabling JavaScript, cookies, and pop-ups. The platform supports single sign on via SAML and WS-Federation and third parties such as Clever, ClassLink, and Google.
