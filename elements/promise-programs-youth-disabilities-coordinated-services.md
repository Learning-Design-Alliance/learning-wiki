# PROMISE programs: coordinated federal-state-local service initiatives for youth with disabilities on SSI

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 4 studies (4 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
PROMISE programs are state-level initiatives designed, in the article's words, to "improve connections among federal programs at the state and local levels" for "youth with disabilities and their families with accessing resources that can improve their educational and employment outcomes." The evaluation covers six PROMISE programs, assessed for impacts on service receipt, education, employment, expectations, health insurance coverage, income, self-determination, and public assistance participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination among federal programs at the state and local levels to connect youth with disabilities and their families to resources
#### Constraints
- Evaluated population is youth with disabilities receiving Supplemental Security Income and their families

### Target Learning Goals
