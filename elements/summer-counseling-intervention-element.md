# Summer counseling intervention for college-intending high school graduates

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Summer counseling helps college-intending high school graduates complete the steps needed to matriculate, such as "enrolling in courses, completing housing forms, registering for orientation, finalizing arrangements to secure financial aid". It involves outreach by peer mentors or college counselors via text messaging, email, phone, in-person meetings, instant messaging, or social media, typically during 1-2 months between graduation and enrollment. Across the five reviewed studies, an average of 58% of students met with a counselor at least once.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions target students who intend to enroll in college and have graduated high school; there is no standard list of services, and implementation varies across sites
#### Constraints
- Typically runs for only 1-2 months in the summer after high school; costs vary from about $2-$7 per student for texting to $100-$200 per student for one-on-one counseling

### Target Learners
- College-intending recent high school graduates, particularly disadvantaged students at greater risk of summer melt

### Target Learning Goals
- College enrollment
- College persistence
