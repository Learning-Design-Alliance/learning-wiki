## Description
Role-playing is the element in which learners adopt roles and act through a scenario, interaction, or decision process from that perspective. It is useful when learning depends on situational judgment, empathy, communication, or applied decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A role with meaningful constraints or goals**
- **A scenario that makes the role consequential**
#### Constraints
- **Shallow roles can turn the activity into performance without reasoning**

### Target Learning Goals
- Support perspective-taking, communication, and situated decision-making.
