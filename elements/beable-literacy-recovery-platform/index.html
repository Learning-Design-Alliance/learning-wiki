Beable K-12 digital platform for literacy recovery¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 associational, 1 qualitative), q2 · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Beable is a K-12 digital platform for literacy recovery that "customizes content to the student's reading level, interests, and career goals." It is available in English and Spanish with translation support to over 100 languages, and integrates literacy acceleration, intervention response, English language acquisition, core content mastery, and test preparation, connecting literacy with career and essential skills development.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Beable is web-based and requires supported browsers and devices such as laptops, Chromebooks, and iPads with the operating systems the page lists

Constraints¶

  • The reported evaluation covers students in grades 2-10 in one California district; the positive outcome is limited to grades 2-8

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students, including English learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • literacy acceleration
  • English language acquisition
  • core content mastery
  • test preparation
  • career and life readiness

Claims¶

  • Beable One was evaluated with 2nd-10th grade students across 27 schools in one California district, matching about 10,000 users with 1,470 non-users [+W]
  • After one year, Beable One users in grades 2-8 significantly outperformed matched non-users on the i-Ready Reading Diagnostic (effect size = +0.07) [+W]
  • Beable received a Promising evidence rating based on one study of about 11,065 students with an average effect size of +0.07 [+W]
  • Educators broadly view AI-generated text customization to student interests and reading levels as a desirable opportunity, particularly for ELs [+W]
  • Existing MLL-focused edtech overwhelmingly offers English and Spanish, with a third offering only those two languages [~W]
