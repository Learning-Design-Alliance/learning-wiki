# Exploratory research review of promising practices and approaches to support remote learning (REL Mid-Atlantic, 2020)

## Description
An exploratory research review published by Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic in July 2020 addressing remote learning in K-12 settings. It was produced because, per the publication, "When schools are forced to shutter their doors because of natural phenomena, remote learning takes on added importance." The review's evidence base is 110 studies on the effectiveness of remote learning practices. This page presents only the publication's abstract and metadata, not its findings.
