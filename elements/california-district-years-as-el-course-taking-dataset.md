# Years-as-EL student-level dataset from a California district (N = 41,343)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study analyzes student-level data from a single school district in California chosen for its large populations of Chinese- and Spanish-speaking English learners. The abstract reports the sample size as "N = 41,343" and describes the district as "a unique district in California with a large number of Chinese and Spanish ELs." The dataset supports by-subgroup and by-subject course-taking analyses comparing ELs by years spent classified as EL.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires student-level records linking EL classification duration to high school course-taking and eighth-grade test scores
#### Constraints
- Drawn from one district in California, so findings are scoped to that district's context

### Target Learners
- High school students classified as English learners (Chinese- and Spanish-speaking subgroups)

### Target Learning Goals
- Access to general and advanced academic coursework across subject areas
