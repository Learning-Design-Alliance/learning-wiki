# CT Pathways Toolkit for district design of K-12 CS/CT pathways

## Description
The CT Pathways Toolkit is a resource for school districts to guide them in the design and articulation of their own system-wide K-12 learning pathway in computational thinking (CT) and computer science (CS). Its stated purpose is to involve all students in a district in learning critical CT and CS skills throughout their K-12 education. The toolkit is organized into three steps: articulating the Why (vision, foundations, strategic alignment), defining and communicating the What (lead, identify, define competencies), and establishing the How (district leaders, grade-band progression, measurement of success).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A district team that can define competencies in language your team can understand and communicate effectively
#### Constraints
- Designed for district-level pathway design rather than individual classroom use

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in adopting districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Critical CT and CS skills throughout K-12 education
