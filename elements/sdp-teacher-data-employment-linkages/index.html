School District of Philadelphia administrative dataset on teacher employment and student–teacher linkages¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The report's appendixes draw on administrative data supplied by the School District of Philadelphia. As the publication page states, "The School District of Philadelphia (SDP) provided data on teacher employment, teacher characteristics, student–teacher linkages, and school characteristics." These four data streams underpin the report's analysis of teacher turnover and equity of access to effective teachers.
