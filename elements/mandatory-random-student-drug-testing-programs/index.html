Mandatory-random student drug testing programs for high school students¶

Description¶

Mandatory-random student drug testing is a school-based intervention approach whose effectiveness was evaluated in the study introduced by this executive summary, titled "The Effectiveness of Mandatory-Random Student Drug Testing." The summary frames the evaluation against concern that "the prevalence of illicit substance use among youth remains high and a cause of concern" despite a decade of decline in adolescent substance use. The supplied text does not describe the program's components or the evaluation's design or results.
