# 360 Filmmakers Challenge program model: 360-degree film production with prompts, mentors, and authentic audience

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The 360 Filmmakers Challenge, part of the Oculus VR for Good initiative, engages young people to produce 360-degree films that make an impact, aiming to inspire the next generation of VR creators and build student skills and confidence as producers with emerging technology. In Fall 2016 it reached over 1,200 students in 36 high schools across seven states and the District of Columbia, providing Samsung Gear VR headsets, Ricoh Theta S cameras, an online educator community, and professional filmmaker mentors. Documentation identified the challenge prompts, creating for an authentic audience and competition, and access to cutting-edge 360 technology as particularly valuable aspects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools need 360 film production tools, production resources, an educator community space, and filmmaker mentor feedback as provided in the program
#### Constraints
- Only one student can use a headset at a time, and limited camera access during production can become a bottleneck

### Target Learners
- high school students in public, charter, and private schools, many in Title I schools

### Target Learning Goals
- technical and creative production skills
- communication and collaboration
- perspective-taking
- career awareness in creative and technical fields
