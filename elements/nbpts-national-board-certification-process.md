# National Board for Professional Teaching Standards (NBPTS) certification process

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
NBPTS was "established to elevate the teaching profession by providing an advanced, rigorous level of certifica- tion," beginning in 1995. Certification involves an extensive portfolio with unedited classroom videotapes, analysis of student work, and evidence of work with families and colleagues, plus six assessment-center examinations scored by trained teachers; Educational Testing Service administers the assessments. The report evaluates Chicago NBCTs and candidates on professional development, commitment, leadership roles, and principal ratings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Candidates must complete an extensive portfolio and pass a series of six assessment-center examinations
#### Constraints
- About 11 percent of surveyed principals were unclear about the program and whether they had board-certified teachers

### Target Learners
- Experienced teachers seeking advanced certification in urban districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Advanced teaching competence and teacher leadership in schools
