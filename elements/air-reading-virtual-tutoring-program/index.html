Air Reading live virtual small-group tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Air Reading is an assessment-driven, live virtual tutoring program for foundational reading from Kindergarten through 6th grade. "Small groups of up to four students are paired with the same tutor for 30-minute sessions four times each week," with one-on-one diagnostics informing group placement and bi-weekly progress assessments. Sessions are tracked on the program's proprietary platform, and the model is described as scalable across geographies and staffing capabilities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Student devices and a reliable internet connection
  • Tutors provided by Air Reading plus on-site school staff to supervise tutoring sessions

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten through 6th grade students needing additional reading intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Foundational reading skills

Claims¶

  • Students receiving 40 or more Air Reading tutoring sessions showed stronger reading impacts (effect size +0.17) [+W]
  • A second randomized study in Louisiana and Texas found Air Reading students scored significantly higher on combined reading assessments (effect size +0.29) [+W]
  • Air Reading tutoring significantly improved NWEA MAP reading scores in a randomized study of grade 1-6 students (effect size +0.12) [+W]
  • Across two randomized studies, Air Reading shows a weighted average effect size of +0.20 on reading achievement, meeting the Strong standard [+W]
