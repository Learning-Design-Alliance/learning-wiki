## Description
The Pay Equity Fund (PEF) is a Washington, DC initiative to address pay gaps between child care and early education educators and K-12 teachers. The page describes it as "a pioneering initiative to address pay gaps between child care and early education educators and K-12 teachers" and is the subject of a benefit-cost analysis and a multi-year impact and cost-effectiveness study running from 2022 to 2026, prepared for a set of early childhood funders.
