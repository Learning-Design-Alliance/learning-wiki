# Act

## Description
`Act` is the execution phase of a challenge-based or inquiry cycle, where learners move from investigation and planning into producing, testing, sharing, or implementing a response in the real world.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A defined challenge or problem to respond to
- Enough prior investigation for learners to justify their action
- A concrete product, intervention, or decision that can be observed and discussed

#### Constraints
- Action can become performative if learners are rushed into output before they understand the problem
- Real-world implementation may require time, permissions, materials, or external partners
- The more public the action, the more carefully success criteria and support need to be structured

### Target Learners
- Learners working on authentic problems
- Learners who benefit from applying knowledge in visible ways
- Groups developing collaboration, decision-making, and follow-through

### Target Learning Goals
- Applying learning to a real or realistic context
- Producing a justified solution, artifact, or intervention
- Learning from implementation, feedback, and revision

### Affordances
- Turns planning into observable performance
- Increases authenticity and accountability
- Creates conditions for reflection, debrief, and iteration
