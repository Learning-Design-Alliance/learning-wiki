ASSISTments online math homework platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ASSISTments is an online homework tool coupled with teacher training that "provid[es] students with math assistance and teachers with assessments of student progress." It has two content types: problem sets linked to existing textbook homework, giving teachers flexibility in assignment, and mastery-oriented skill practice content. The web-based platform is available at no cost to schools, but students need computer access at home.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • 5 days of professional development plus three in-person or remote coaching visits accompany implementation
  • students need computer access at home

Constraints¶

  • the web-based platform is free to schools but assumes home computer access for students

Target Learners¶

  • middle school students, specifically grade 7 math classrooms

Target Learning Goals¶

  • middle school mathematics achievement

Claims¶

  • ASSISTments use for 7th-grade math homework significantly raises TerraNova mathematics scores in Maine (effect size +0.18) [+W]
  • Across two randomized studies, ASSISTments averages a mean effect size of +0.13, meeting Strong evidence standards [+W]
  • A North Carolina replication found ASSISTments 7th-grade use raised 8th-grade end-of-grade math scores (effect size +0.10) despite COVID-19 disruption [+W]
  • A possible mediating effect of teacher homework review practices was observed in the ASSISTments trial, but the planned mediation analysis failed to show an effect [~W]
