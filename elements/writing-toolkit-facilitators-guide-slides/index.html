Facilitator's Guide and PLC slides within the writing toolkit¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

One component of the toolkit: the Facilitator's Guide contains all of the information needed to lead a group of teachers in the PLC sessions, and the toolkit also includes slides for a facilitator to use during the PLC sessions. These materials let a facilitator run the eight one-hour professional learning community sessions without sourcing additional materials.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A facilitator to lead the group of teachers through the PLC sessions
