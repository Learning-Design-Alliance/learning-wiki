# GO Tutor Corps high-impact in-school math tutoring program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
GO Tutor Corps "provides high-impact tutoring during the school day that is designed to accelerate student learning through targeted academic support." It offers individualized or small-group tutoring in mathematics to students needing additional help, supplementing regular classroom instruction across the school year with consistent sessions and progress monitoring, delivered within participating schools with structured delivery and use of student performance data to guide instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partner schools must designate a non-teaching staff member to manage the tutors on-site during the hours tutors serve in the school.
#### Constraints
- No specialized technology is required.

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 6-10 identified as needing additional help in mathematics

### Target Learning Goals
- Accelerating mathematics achievement across the school year
