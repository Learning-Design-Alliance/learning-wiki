Math 180 targeted math intervention program with adaptive student application and teacher-facilitated instruction¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Math 180 is a targeted math intervention program that "builds algebra readiness in core or dedicated intervention classrooms, using research-based strategies for striving students." It combines an adaptive student application with teacher-facilitated instruction to accelerate students toward grade-level proficiency. Lessons target foundational computation and reasoning skills, including "operations with whole numbers, fractions, ratios, decimals, proportional reasoning, and functional thinking" to prepare students for on-grade-level coursework.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Implementation in the studied models used a blended model requiring a dedicated classroom with a teacher or paraprofessional for each section.

Constraints¶

  • The program is targeted at striving students eligible for intervention, in grades 5 through 8 in the studies reported.

Target Learners¶

  • striving middle-grades mathematics students (grades 5-8) eligible for intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • algebra readiness
  • foundational computation and reasoning skills
  • proportional reasoning and functional thinking

Claims¶

  • Math 180 matched comparison study: intervention-eligible students in grades 5-8 showed significantly greater NWEA MAP gains than matched comparison students (ES +0.23) [+W]
  • Across two studies (650 students), Math 180 shows positive impacts on mathematics achievement supporting a Moderate evidence rating with average effect size +0.26 [+W]
  • Math 180 randomized study: 6th graders receiving Math 180 showed greater growth on NWEA MAP mathematics than business-as-usual comparison students (ES +0.31) [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • READ 180® blended-learning reading intervention for struggling adolescent readers
  • I CAN Learn® self-paced, mastery-based computer math curriculum

Examples¶

  • Implement Math 180 as a blended rotation model with 1:1 computer use during rotation and roughly half the class on software at a time
