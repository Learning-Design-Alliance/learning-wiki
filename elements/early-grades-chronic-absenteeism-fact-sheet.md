## Description
This artifact is a fact sheet addressed to schools, published by Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic on Nov 20, 2019, under the title "Improving the School Attendance of New Jersey's Youngest Learners." It is described as a "Fact sheet for schools on the importance of attendance in pre-kindergarten and kindergarten and the risk factors," and it is tagged by the publisher under Human Services, Early Childhood, and Education.
