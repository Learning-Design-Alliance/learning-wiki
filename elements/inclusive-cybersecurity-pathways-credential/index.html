Inclusive Cybersecurity Pathways with Workforce Credentials¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

An inclusive cybersecurity pathways initiative offering workforce credentials, enrolling "Over 800 students... across 10 school districts reflecting a uniquely diverse student population — 25 percent female, 12 percent with learning differences, 43 percent free and reduced lunch, 54 percent students of color." The pathway is one of the differentiated solutions produced through Inclusive Innovation that reflects centering the leadership, voice and needs of educators, students and communities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Enrollment must reflect a diverse student population across participating districts

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • historically and systematically excluded learners including female students, students with learning differences, low-income students, and students of color

Target Learning Goals¶

  • cybersecurity career readiness
  • workforce credentials

Related Elements¶

  • Micro-credential stack pairing data analysis with algorithm creation
