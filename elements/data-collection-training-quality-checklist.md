# Checklist 2: reporting data collection training and quality

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The second checklist addresses "data collection training and quality," prompting researchers to report how data collectors were trained and what quality procedures were followed. As one of the brief's five checklists, it targets a distinct information need readers have when judging the credibility of measurement in an education study. It is applied when drafting or checking the completeness of a report's methods section.
