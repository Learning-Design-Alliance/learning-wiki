# PROMISE programs: state-level initiatives connecting federal programs for youth with disabilities and their families

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The PROMISE programs are state initiatives "designed to improve connections among federal programs at the state and local levels" to help youth with disabilities and their families access resources. The evaluation covers six PROMISE programs and examines their effects on education and employment outcomes. This appendix documents the data and methods used for the interim impact analysis of these programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Operates by improving connections among federal programs at the state and local levels, as the article states
#### Constraints
- Evaluated impacts were measured at 18 months after youth enrolled in the PROMISE evaluation, an interim time point

### Target Learners
- youth with disabilities receiving Supplemental Security Income
- their families

### Target Learning Goals
- improved educational outcomes
- improved employment outcomes
