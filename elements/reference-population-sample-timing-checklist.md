# Checklist 3: reporting reference population, study sample, and measurement timing

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The third checklist covers "the study's reference population, study sample, and measurement timing." It prompts researchers to state who the study's findings are meant to generalize to, who was actually measured, and when measurement occurred. Within the brief's five-checklist framework, this component addresses the sample and timing information readers need to interpret reported measures appropriately.
