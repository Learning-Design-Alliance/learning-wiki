# Digital Promise Product Certifications ecosystem

## Description
Product Certifications are "competency- and evidence-based signals" giving districts an independent overview of edtech quality. Product teams apply by submitting evidence; expert assessors at nonprofit organizations review submissions, and successful products earn an Open Badge. As of May 31, 2023, 162 certifications had been awarded across Research-Based Design (77), Learner Variability (34), Project Unicorn Interoperability (45), and others. Certifications expire every two years to confirm products still meet requirements as tools update.
