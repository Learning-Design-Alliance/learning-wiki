Equatio digital math tool with Mathspace¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (5 for, 2 against) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Equatio is Texthelp's advanced equation editor for digital mathematics environments. The report describes that "it includes features such as a Desmos graphing integration, handwriting recognition, language translation, and a screenshot reader with the ability to quickly and easily copy and paste math without losing formatting," plus Mathspace, "a digital space where learners can work freely with equations, shapes, and freehand drawings." The study examined how teachers use these features to support UDL principles in grades 4-12 mathematics, finding the tool versatile for both teacher authoring and student expression.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The report notes the power of such tools lies in how they are used; teachers needed professional learning to learn features beyond the basic equation editor

Constraints¶

  • In some teachers' settings only students requiring assistive technology had access to Equatio, limiting classroom-wide use

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 4-12 mathematics students and their teachers, including students requiring assistive technologies

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Communicating mathematical symbolism, tables, graphs, and diagrams; creating, using, and connecting multiple mathematical representations

Affordances¶

  • Udl Three Principles Framework Math

Claims¶

  • Teachers described Equatio as a time-saver for creating instructional materials, with 42% of survey respondents strongly agreeing it saves them time [+W]
  • Most Equatio-using teachers were not using the tool to support UDL-based practices, with only about 22% reporting use of a given UDL affordance on average [-W]
  • Mathspace dynamic representations, such as fraction smart shapes, let students see how changing the number of equal parts alters part size while the whole stays the same [+W]
  • After focus-group professional learning, all nine teacher partners committed to trying Mathspace and reported broader, more integrated uses of Equatio [+W]
  • When all of a teacher's students had access to Equatio, teachers leveraged it in more robust workflows such as assessments, digital submissions, and timely feedback [+W]
  • At the end of the pilots, three-quarters of Mathspace teachers and all Ratio Rancher teachers said they would recommend the tool to a colleague [+W]
  • Mathspace classroom use was limited, with 90% of teachers using it two hours or less weekly and two-thirds reporting technical challenges [-W]
