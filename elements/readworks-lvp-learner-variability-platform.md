# ReadWorks digital reading comprehension platform with LVP-informed learner-variability features

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
ReadWorks is a free digital platform providing K-12 teachers a library of curated nonfiction and literary articles, comprehension and vocabulary supports, assessments, and teacher resources. Through the LVP partnership it added student-facing scaffolds—audio, split screen, text magnification, guided reading strip, paragraph numbering, and article annotation—intended as scaffolds bolstering strengths and mitigating challenges tied to key learner factors such as attention, working memory, vocabulary, and vision. The platform's reach is large: 88 percent of all K-8 public and charter schools in the U.S. had a teacher who used ReadWorks in 2018-2019, potentially reaching over 15 million students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers assign articles to students to be read on the digital platform; features appear as options on the student-facing article interface, with audio requiring teacher enablement (later changed to default-on).
#### Constraints
- With the exception of the audio support, the described features are always available for all students; audio access depends on teacher assignment settings.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, especially elementary readers across the full range of reading abilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- Vocabulary development
- Student engagement with text

## Claims

- [In the district study, teachers assigned articles above students' STAR Lexile levels (about 375 points higher on average), and greater STAR growth related to completing more, harder, and more accurate ReadWorks assignments](../claims/readworks-assignment-difficulty-growth-association.md) [+W]
- [Changing the audio default from opt-in to opt-out raised audio availability from 72% to 99% of assignments and student audio use from 68% to 84%](../claims/readworks-audio-default-change-increased-use.md) [+W]
- [Feature use related to a slight increase in multiple choice accuracy, larger for lower-Lexile students and for easier articles](../claims/readworks-feature-use-accuracy-gains.md) [+W]
- [Feature use was widespread across student groups (92% of students used at least one feature; 75% of assignments included feature use), with no differences by STAR score, special education status, or income, but substantial variation across teachers](../claims/readworks-feature-use-broad-equitable-teacher-variation.md) [+W]
- [Using at least one feature raised students' multiple choice question attempt rate to 89 percent and eliminated the drop on hard assignments, with a significant feature-use × difficulty interaction](../claims/readworks-feature-use-engagement-attempts.md) [+W]
- [Teachers who tried ReadWorks features reported high intent to assign more and higher-level articles (89% and 82%)](../claims/readworks-features-raise-teacher-assignment-ambitions.md) [+W]
