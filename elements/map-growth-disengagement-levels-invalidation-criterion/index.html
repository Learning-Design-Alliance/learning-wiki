MAP Growth rapid-guessing invalidation criterion and four-level disengagement classification¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 4 studies (3 associational, 1 theoretical), q2–q3 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MAP Growth test events carry both performance data (RIT score plus standard error) and engagement data (percent of item responses that were rapid guesses). The study formed "four levels of test-taking disengagement": serious (30% or more rapid guesses), moderate (11 to 30%), mild (1 to 10%), and zero disengagement. The invalidation criterion holds that when students rapid-guess on 30% or more of items, scores are apt to be so distorted they are too untrustworthy to report.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Item-level response-time data used to identify rapid guesses, and RIT scores with standard errors

Constraints¶

  • The 30% invalidation threshold rests on the assumption that rapid guessing distorts scores downward and that disengaged students would score higher if engaged

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students taking MAP Growth adaptive assessments

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Identifying invalid or distorted test scores due to disengagement

Claims¶

  • Rapid guesses on MAP Growth reflect a lack of engagement rather than a lack of knowledge [+W]
  • Students who rapid-guess less on a MAP Growth retest tend to see score gains, while those who rapid-guess more tend to see declines [+W]
  • Seriously disengaged initial testers who stopped rapid-guessing on retest showed large RIT gains, averaging about 6 points in Reading and 12 in Math [+W]
  • E-M scoring partially accounts for observed RIT score differences between disengaged first tests and engaged retests [+W]
  • E-M scoring leaves residual score distortion, suggesting some disengaged test responses are nonrapid rather than rapid guesses [+W]
  • Rapid-guessing behavior can distort test scores and adversely affect measurement [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Same-day MAP Growth retest dataset spanning four test types and four testing terms
  • MAP Growth retest dataset for evaluating E-M scoring accuracy
