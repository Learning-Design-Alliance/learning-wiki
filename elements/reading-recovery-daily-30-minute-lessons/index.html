Reading Recovery daily 30-minute individual tutoring lessons¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Reading Recovery provides the lowest-achieving first-grade students "daily 30-minute lessons" delivered individually by specially trained teachers. Lessons combine "fluency instruction, letter and word identification skills, phonemic and orthographic awareness instruction," reading a new challenging book with scaffolded support, and daily progress monitoring of independent oral reading. Students either reach average reading and writing levels within 20 weeks or are referred for additional testing and possible more intensive placement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Specially trained teachers work with four students individually for two and one half hours each school day
  • Teachers must successfully complete a yearlong graduate level course taught by a teacher leader

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • first-grade students in the lowest 20-30% of their reading cohort

Target Learning Goals¶

  • beginning reading
  • writing

Claims¶

  • Reading Recovery shows an average effect size of +0.43 across four qualifying studies, earning a Strong evidence rating [+M]
  • Reading Recovery produces two positive outcomes: reaching average levels within 20 weeks or reliable referral for further testing [+W]
  • Reading Recovery targets first-graders in the lowest 20-30% of their cohort after one year of formal schooling [+W]
