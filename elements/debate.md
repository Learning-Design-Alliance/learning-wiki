## Description
Debate is the element in which learners take positions, justify them with evidence, and respond to alternatives in a structured exchange.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A contestable question**
- **Evidence standards and discussion norms**
#### Constraints
- **Without structure, debate can reward performance over reasoning**

### Target Learning Goals
- Build argumentation, evidence use, and perspective taking.
