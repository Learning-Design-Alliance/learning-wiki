# National Writing Project teacher-leader preparation for facilitating professional development

## Description
The National Writing Project's preparation of teachers to lead one of its professional development programs is the source of the brief's insights. The brief describes this as "the National Writing Project's experience preparing teachers to lead one of its professional development programs," positioning the preparation effort as a working example of building teacher leaders' capacity to facilitate colleagues' professional learning and to support teachers in learning how to apply instructional strategies.
