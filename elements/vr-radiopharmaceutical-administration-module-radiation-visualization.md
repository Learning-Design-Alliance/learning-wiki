# VR radiopharmaceutical administration training module with clinically measured radiation field visualization

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A VR training module for radiopharmaceutical administration built in Unity with a VIVE Pro Eye HMD, trackers, and VR gloves, integrating a radiation visualization system constructed from spatial dose measurements of Technetium-99m. Dose rates measured around vial and syringe were normalized and "implemented as transparent fixed objects in the VR environment," color-mapped on a logarithmic scale. Visualizations can be toggled so learners "directly compare shielding effects during task performance."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PC with NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080, VIVE Pro Eye HMD, VIVE Tracker 3.0 devices, SteamVR Base Station 2.0, and Vib+[L] VR gloves with the Mollisen SDK
#### Constraints
- The measured activity (68.6 MBq of ⁹⁹ᵐTc) was lower than clinical activities; the distribution was normalized to reproduce spatial distribution rather than absolute dose rates
- Spatial accuracy of the visualization was not quantitatively evaluated

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate radiological technology students

### Target Learning Goals
- radiopharmaceutical administration procedure
- radiation safety awareness
- understanding of spatial radiation dose distribution and shielding
