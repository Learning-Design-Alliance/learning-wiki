# Free online growth mindset intervention materials (PERTS and Mindset Kit)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report identifies freely accessible implementation resources for postsecondary institutions: "Postsecondary institutions can access existing intervention materials for free online at sites such as https://www.perts.net/orientation/cg and https://www.mindsetkit.org. Alternatively, college personnel can adapt intervention materials described or provided in the studies reviewed here." These reduce the equipment-and-materials cost ingredient, which the report assigns to the college as funding source.

## Claims

- [Aronson et al. (2002) pen-pal growth mindset intervention produced a statistically significant positive effect on quarter GPA (effect size 0.85)](../claims/aronson-2002-significant-gpa-effect.md) [+W]
- [Broda et al. (2018) found indeterminate effects on GPA, full-time enrollment, and credits completed in a large randomized trial](../claims/broda-2018-indeterminate-effects-large-trial.md) [~W]
- [Growth Mindset interventions have no discernible effects on college enrollment and progressing in college](../claims/growth-mindset-no-discernible-enrollment-progressing.md) [-M]
- [Growth Mindset interventions have potentially positive effects on postsecondary academic achievement (improvement index +13)](../claims/growth-mindset-potentially-positive-academic-achievement-postsecondary.md) [+M]
- [Suh et al. (2019) found a positive but non-significant effect on course passing rate (effect size 0.80) and a negative non-significant effect on final exam score](../claims/suh-2019-mixed-achievement-findings.md) [~W]
- [Yeager et al. (2016) and Fink et al. (2018) found indeterminate effects on enrollment, credit completion, and chemistry final exam scores](../claims/yeager-fink-indeterminate-effects.md) [~W]
