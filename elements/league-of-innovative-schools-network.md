# League of Innovative Schools membership network

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Digital Promise membership network of forward-thinking school districts, described as "a coalition of 86 districts across 33 states, serving more than 3 .3 million students", including urban, suburban, and rural districts. Members engage in cross-district collaboration, cross-sector partnerships with research institutions and education-focused companies, and sharing of best practices. Related Digital Promise offerings to member districts include Verizon Innovative Learning Schools (8.6 percent of districts), educator micro-credentials (50 percent of surveyed districts), ed-tech research pilots (16 percent), and Learning Studios (26 percent).
