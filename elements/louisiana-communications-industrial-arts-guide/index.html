Louisiana middle-school communications industrial arts curriculum guide (drafting, electricity-electronics, graphic arts, photography)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A tentative state curriculum guide for grades 6-9 implementing career education through industrial arts. Per the abstract, "the course is designed to help youth understand the basic concepts of the communications career cluster; including, drafting, electricity, graphic arts, and photography." It is organized as topic outlines pairing performance objectives with suggested activities and resources, each resource coded to a bibliography following each major section.

Requirements¶

  • Performance objectives with suggested activities and resources for the major topics and subtopics
  • A coded bibliography of educational resources following each major section

Constraints¶

  • Marked tentative; the guide notes there is more material provided than could normally be covered in the designed time

Target Learners¶

  • Middle school students in grades 6 through 9

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Career awareness and tentative career decision-making in the communications cluster
  • Technical abilities and interests exploration in drafting, electricity, graphic arts, and photography
