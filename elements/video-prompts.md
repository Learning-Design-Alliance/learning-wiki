# Video Prompts

## Description
Video prompts are short video clips used to launch attention, curiosity, discussion, or analysis. They are useful when a brief visual or narrative stimulus can focus learners on a question, problem, or phenomenon.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A clip clearly tied to the learning objective**
- **A prompt or follow-up task that directs attention to what matters**
#### Constraints
- **Video without a follow-up question often becomes passive viewing**

### Target Learning Goals
- Capture attention and initiate analysis or discussion.
