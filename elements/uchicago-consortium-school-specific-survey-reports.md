# School-specific survey reports with minimum participation thresholds

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The UChicago Consortium prepares school-specific reports of survey responses, but only for schools meeting participation thresholds: reports are prepared "for every school in which at least 42 percent of teachers or 50 percent of students participate in the survey." The reports describe the learning climate, quality of instructional program, student-teacher relationships, leadership, professional environment, and school relationships with parents and community.
