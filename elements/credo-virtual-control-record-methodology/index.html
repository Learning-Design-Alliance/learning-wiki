CREDO Virtual Control Record (VCR) methodology¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Virtual Control Record is a quasi-experimental counterfactual method in which a synthesized record combines the actual academic experiences of up to seven matched traditional public school students. As the report explains, a VCR, or a "virtual twin", is built from feeder-school students matched on grade level, gender, race/ethnicity, poverty status, ELL status, special education status, and prior test scores. In this study, virtual matches were created for 85 and 84 percent of tested charter observations in reading and math.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Matched TPS students must be drawn from feeder schools whose students transfer to the given charter school
  • Matching on grade level, gender, race/ethnicity, poverty status, ELL status, special education status, and prior test scores
  • Sufficient tested students for reliable inference at school level (at least 60 matched records over two growth periods or 30 for one period)

Constraints¶

  • This study matches students period by period per WWC Procedures Handbook Version 4.0, so caution is advised when comparing findings with previous CREDO reports
  • Current data limitations prevent the inclusion of non-academic outcomes in the analysis

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students enrolled in South Carolina charter schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Annual academic growth in reading and mathematics as measured by state accountability tests
