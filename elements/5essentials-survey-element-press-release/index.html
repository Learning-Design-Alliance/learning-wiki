5Essentials Survey (My Voice, My School survey)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The 5Essentials Survey, administered in Chicago Public Schools since the 1990s, "measures a school’s strength in five essential organizational conditions that influence student learning: Effective Leaders, Collaborative Teachers, Involved Families, Supportive Environment, and Ambitious Instruction." The two studies examined its predictiveness of school improvement and how schools use its results. It is also included in CPS's SQRP accountability system.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • District administration of the survey to students and teachers to generate school-level measures

Constraints¶

  • Its inclusion in the SQRP accountability system created incentives and pressures that some principals and teachers reported affected priorities and responses

Target Learners¶

  • Chicago Public Schools students and teachers whose responses generate school-level measures

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring and improving school climate to support student outcomes

Claims¶

  • Strength on most 5Essentials measures was similarly likely across poverty levels, but five measures were less prevalent in high-poverty schools [+W]
  • 5Essentials Survey measures predicted school improvement in both high- and low-poverty schools at elementary and high school levels [+W]
  • Accountability pressures overshadowed the survey's improvement potential without reducing its predictive power [~W]
  • Leadership coaching partnerships and instructional leadership team collaboration increased schools' capacity to use survey data [+W]
  • Few schools used 5Essentials data for improvement, citing opaque data, principal-focused measures, and lack of district-wide strategy [-W]
  • Among elementary schools with strong 5Essentials results, high-poverty schools improved more than low-poverty schools on more than one-half of measures [+W]
