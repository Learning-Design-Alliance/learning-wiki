# Virtual Control Record (VCR) matched-comparison methodology

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CREDO's quasi-experimental design constructs a "virtual twin" for each charter student from records of students in feeder traditional public schools, matching demographic and academic characteristics so the twin differs "only on a single factor: attending a charter school." The VCR produces the expected score the charter student would have realized in TPS. In this study, virtual matches were possible for 87 percent of tested charter observations in reading and 88 percent in math, and matches were newly developed for every growth period to conform to WWC Procedures Handbook Version 4.0 baseline equivalence criteria.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Feeder schools whose students transfer to a given charter school supply the potential matches
- Sufficient tested students per school for reliable school-level inference (at least 60 matched charter students per the report's stated criterion)
#### Constraints
- The report notes VCR performance is equated to zero, so the analysis cannot discern significant trends in TPS achievement over time
- Very few retained charter students had scores from two or more consecutive years, so no VCR match could be obtained for retained charter students in Washington during the years studied

### Target Learners
- K-12 students enrolled in Washington State charter schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Annual academic growth in reading and math on state standardized assessments
