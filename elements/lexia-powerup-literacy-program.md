# Lexia PowerUp Literacy: blended adaptive literacy program for struggling adolescent readers

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
PowerUp is a literacy program for struggling and non-proficient readers in grades 6-12 that blends "online student-driven explicit instruction with offline teacher-delivered lessons and activities". It targets Word Study, Grammar, and Comprehension, identifies skill gaps, and provides personalized, systematic instruction through adaptive learning paths, with embedded progress monitoring and scripted lessons for teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- No special qualifications or certification are required to implement PowerUp.
#### Constraints
- Designed for students with reading levels below the 35th percentile in grades 6-12; used as supplemental reading instruction rather than core instruction.

### Target Learners
- struggling and non-proficient readers in grades 6-12
- middle school students in Title 1 schools

### Target Learning Goals
- fundamental literacy skills
- higher-order thinking skills
- comprehension of complex texts across genres
