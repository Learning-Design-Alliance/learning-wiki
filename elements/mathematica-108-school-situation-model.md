# Simulation model of 108 school situations predicting infections and in-person attendance under opening, quarantining, and closing strategies

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A modeling tool developed by Mathematica to support school reopening decisions during the COVID-19 pandemic. It "ran complex models of 108 different school situations, including urban, suburban, and rural settings" and "modelled outcomes for various in-person, hybrid, and distance learning approaches." Outputs include likely infections in a school, total likely infections when the first infection is detected, and the percentage of school days a student can attend in person.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires community and school context information to select among the modeled school situations
#### Constraints
- Results are model-based predictions, not measured infection outcomes

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in urban, suburban, and rural schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining in-person learning opportunities while mitigating COVID-19 spread
