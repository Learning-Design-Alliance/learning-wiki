App Inventor community-problem app projects with storyboarding¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The deck presents App Inventor as a tool for students to build mobile apps, suggesting app content ideas such as "Build an App that solves a problem for your community", healthcare apps, tourist needs, quiz games, vocabulary practice, and math practice. It references video tutorials and includes a storyboarding step, "Create a storyboard for an App", as planning before building.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need access to App Inventor and the referenced video tutorials; storyboarding precedes app building

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 3-8 working on community- or content-oriented app projects

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Designing and planning an app that solves a real problem, applying algorithmic thinking to app development

Related Elements¶

  • Scratch coding activities expressing content as interactive illustrations, games, or stories
  • ScratchJR app for young children to build interactive stories and games
  • CT Boosters: 30-minute algorithmic-thinking professional learning sessions for grades 3-8
