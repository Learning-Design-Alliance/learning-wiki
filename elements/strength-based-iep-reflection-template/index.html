Strength-based Teacher Report/IEP Reflection Template with key information, academic/cognition, and SEL/behavior sections¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Section Two of the guide is a fillable template where teachers "capture the most important information you gathered from your reflection about your student." It is broken into three parts — Key Information, General Academic and Cognition, and Social-Emotional Learning and Behavior — each prompting present levels of performance, strengths and growth areas, goal planning, accommodations, and student input. Teachers add data such as grades, attendance, social/emotional skills, and behavior, and the output feeds directly into classroom supports and the IEP.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Involvement of the student and parent or caregiver in completing the template
  • Data such as grades, assessment scores, and behavior tracker data

Constraints¶

  • Intended for use within PreK-12 public education IEP processes

Target Learners¶

  • PreK-12 students with IEPs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Documenting present levels of academic and SEL performance
  • Planning goals and accommodations from strengths

Affordances¶

  • Learner Variability Whole Child Framework

Related Elements¶

  • Learner Variability Navigator (LVN) web app curating factors of learning and strategies

Examples¶

  • Leverage what students do well and enjoy to address their learning challenges
  • Interview the student one-on-one about strengths, interests, and growth areas before writing the IEP
