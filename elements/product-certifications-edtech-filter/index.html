Product certifications as a baseline filter for evidence-based edtech procurement¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The presentation positions product certifications as a "Baseline requirement to consider edtech options" that districts can use to filter vendor pitches, inform contract renewals, and structure Requests for Proposals. It names three quality dimensions — Evidence-Based Edtech, Practitioner-Informed Design, and CAST's UDL — organized under "Five Quality Indicators." Certifications such as the ISTE Seal and EdSurge Product Index validations are cited as examples of market validators districts can request from applicants.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Vendors must hold or provide alignments, badges, or certifications related to digital pedagogy, usability, and/or research and evidence for the certification route to apply

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in districts procuring edtech

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Selecting effective, evidence-based edtech tools aligned to learner goals

Related Elements¶

  • Outcome-based contracting (OBC) edtech rate cards tying payment to measured learner outcomes
  • EdTech Index with third-party validations
  • CCC product badges: Centering Students' Diverse Lived Experiences, Commitment to Continuous Improvement, and Faculty Agency and Connection

Examples¶

  • Use OBC RFP templates with market validators and ESSA evidence tiers as efficacy criteria
  • Discover & Decide: establish evidence-based selection criteria and instructional alignment in an RFP process to select edtech for priority learner populations
  • Require a brief certification questionnaire to filter vendor pitches
  • Embed certification-status questions with evidence requirements in RFPs and RFAs
