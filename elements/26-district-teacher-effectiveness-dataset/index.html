26-district teacher effectiveness dataset covering grades 4-8 over five school years (2008-2009 to 2012-2013)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study's evidence base is teacher effectiveness data for "fourth- to eighth-grade teachers over five school years (2008-2009 to 2012-2013) in 26 school districts across the country." It supports the study's comparisons of high- and low-income students' access to effective teachers and analyses of teacher hiring, transfer, and attrition patterns.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher effectiveness measures for fourth- to eighth-grade teachers across the included districts

Constraints¶

  • Limited to 26 study districts and to grades four through eight over the 2008-2009 to 2012-2013 school years

Target Learners¶

  • Fourth- to eighth-grade students in the 26 study districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Equitable access to effective teachers and its relation to the student achievement gap

Claims¶

  • Teacher hiring and transfer patterns are consistent with small effectiveness differences, while attrition does not contribute to inequitable access [+M]
  • A small subset of study districts shows meaningful inequity in low-income students' access to effective math teachers [+M]
  • High- and low-income students have similar chances of being taught by the most effective and the least effective teachers [+M]
  • In the average study district, differences in teacher effectiveness between high- and low-income students' teachers are small [+M]

Related Elements¶

  • Multi-district teacher effectiveness equity study of grades 4-8, 2008-09 to 2012-13
