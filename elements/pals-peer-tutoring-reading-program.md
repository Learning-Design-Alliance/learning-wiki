# PALS: structured peer-tutoring program supplementing the primary reading curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies and Peer-Assisted Literacy Strategies are "peer-tutoring programs that supplement the primary reading curriculum" in which students work in pairs, alternately acting as tutor and tutee, on partner reading, paragraph "shrinking," and prediction relay activities intended to improve reading accuracy, fluency, and comprehension. Sessions last 35 minutes and are implemented three to four times per week, with separate versions for kindergarten, grade 1, and grades 2–6. Teachers receive an all-day training workshop with modeling and role-playing, and a team point system motivates accurate reading and paragraph summarizing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers are trained to use the learning strategies through an all-day workshop plus a program manual
#### Constraints
- Student reading materials are not provided in the grades 2–6 version; teachers select appropriate reading materials

### Target Learners
- kindergarten students
- grade 1 students
- students in grades 2–3 (as part of a grades 2–6 set)

### Target Learning Goals
- reading accuracy
- reading fluency
- reading comprehension
