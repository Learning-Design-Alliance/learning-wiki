Duolingo asynchronous discussion boards¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Duolingo connects learners through asynchronous discussion boards attached to each lesson in each of over 38 language courses, where users "can come together to problem-solve vocabulary and grammar issues, troubleshoot technical aspects of the program, and discuss additional resources for language content." A single board may stay active for years, and users include average users, plus users, and volunteer moderators who answer questions more reliably. During the pandemic the boards also carried social-emotional exchanges such as gifting lingots to healthcare workers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A public forum with moderators who answer questions and lead discussions

Constraints¶

  • Discussions are not limited to language-related topics and venture into socio-emotional spaces; the platform does not offer COVID-19 lesson content itself

Target Learners¶

  • Self-directed online foreign language learners on Duolingo

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Foreign language vocabulary and phrase learning
  • Peer-to-peer problem solving about platform tools and curriculum

Claims¶

  • Duolingo discussion boards provided emotional support — social encouragement and community — beyond the instrumental and informational support previously documented [+W]
  • Social-emotional support was the most frequent code in COVID-19-related Duolingo discussion posts, followed by gratitude and pandemic [+W]
  • Duolingo users sought COVID-19 vocabulary in foreign languages and shared their own translations, with 17 users requesting pandemic-related lesson content [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Use online platform discussion boards as a window into learners' pandemic-related anxieties and needs

Key Sources¶
