# New Leaders principal training program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
New Leaders is a principal preparation program founded in 2000 that has trained more than 1000 school leaders serving high-need schools. The article describes that "New Leaders supports aspiring principals through a structured residency program and ongoing training and support after they assume leadership positions in schools." Its selection process is described as "highly competitive, with substantial outreach to identify high quality applicants." Its mission is "to ensure high achievement for all children, especially children of color growing up in poverty." This report evaluates its impacts in Oakland Unified School District.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Aspiring principals must be selected through the program's competitive selection process before entering the structured residency program.
#### Constraints
- The reported achievement impacts are specific to principals and students in Oakland Unified School District.

### Target Learners
- Aspiring school leaders (principals) in high-need schools

### Target Learning Goals
- School leadership capability to raise student achievement and graduation rates
