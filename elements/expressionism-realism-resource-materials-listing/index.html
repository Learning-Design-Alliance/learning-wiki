33-page annotated resource listing for students and teachers¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide includes an extensive compilation of resource materials supporting both students and teachers. The abstract states: "A 33-pagelisting of resource materials for students and teachersis included in this courseoutline." The listing backs the lecture strategies, which repeatedly point to named films, records, filmstrips, and reference works such as Becker's Documents of Literary Realism and Shipley's Dictionary of World Literature.
