# Verizon Innovative Learning Schools program with Always Available Access and Powerful Professional Learning pillars

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 6 studies (5 associational, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 6 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Verizon Innovative Learning Schools program, a Verizon-Digital Promise partnership begun in 2014, has reached 626 schools, over 620,000 students, and nearly 32,000 teachers across 11 cohorts. It rests on two named pillars: "Always Available Access," providing students 24/7 connectivity and devices, and "Powerful Professional Learning," including live and on-demand learning, customized support, and job-embedded coaching. District leads reported devices and connectivity were the hook for joining, but coaches are described as the linchpin of the transformation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructional technology coaches hired for and supported by the program, since without the coach "the program does not work" per one district lead
#### Constraints
- Access to devices and internet alone is described as "far from enough" without coaching support

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in program-supported schools
- teachers and instructional technology coaches

### Target Learning Goals
- technology integration into instruction
- digital equity and inclusion
- STEM and digital skills
