# Learning Studios: technology-equipped classrooms for design-centered, experiential learning

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Learning Studios are a global network of 60 classrooms under HP and Microsoft's Reinvent the Classroom initiative, each "equipped with technology, project guides, training and professional development to support teachers and students." The package includes a Sprout Pro by HP with integrated 3D scanner and interactive touch mat, a Dremel 3D printer, and Windows 10 notebook computers. Sites also received access to an online professional learning community and design challenges implemented at teacher discretion; the first implementation reached over 4,000 students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Advanced technology package, project guides, teacher training and professional development, and an online professional learning community for teachers
#### Constraints
- Teachers chose which project guides and design challenges to implement, producing marked variation across sites in projects, curriculum integration, and instruction

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, including high school students and students with learning difficulties and disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- Design-centered and maker learning, technology skills, engagement, agency, collaboration, and design thinking
