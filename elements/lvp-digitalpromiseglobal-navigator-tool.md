# Learner Variability Navigator (LVN) web app curating factors of learning and strategies

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Learner Variability Navigator is a free, open online tool that curates learning research: "The Learner Variability Navigator (LVN) is a free and open source web app that features Learner Models for grades Pre-K to 12 in math and literacy and an adult learning model." It provides factors of learning and strategies to help teachers customize learning experiences, and its whole child framework structures this guide's reflection template and strategy bank.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers visit the LVN to identify factors of learning and matched strategies for their student
#### Constraints
- Models cover grades Pre-K to 12 in math and literacy plus an adult learning model

### Target Learners
- PreK-12 students
- Adult learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Understanding learner variability
- Selecting strategies matched to student strengths and challenges
