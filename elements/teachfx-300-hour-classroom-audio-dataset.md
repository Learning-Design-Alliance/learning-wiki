# TeachFX 300-hour manually transcribed classroom audio dataset for equitable ASR research

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
TeachFX manually transcribed 300 hours of classroom audio recordings to create a dataset with dual purposes: refining transcription models within TeachFX to improve product accuracy, and providing external researchers with resources for more equitable AI and education research. The project aimed to enhance transcription for teachers and students who speak diverse cultural and regional English dialects, addressing known biases in automated speech recognition.
