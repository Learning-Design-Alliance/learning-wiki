ICCSD Inclusive CT Pathways competency map with five key competencies¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A district competency map identifying five key CT competencies: Algorithms, Computational Models, Systems, Data, and the Impact of Computing. It was developed by a District Development Team of four teachers, one part-time instructional design specialist, and a district instructional design coach, and aligned to the Iowa Computer Science Standards, CSTA Standards, and Iowa CORE Standards. Teachers reviewing it in Spring 2020 reacted positively to the map as a "single blueprint" pointing to grade-level integration points.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A District Development Team of teachers and instructional design specialists to identify competencies
  • Alignment to district and state standards (Iowa CS, CSTA, Iowa CORE)
  • Teacher feedback cycles to refine the map

Constraints¶

  • Developing modeling activities for students was a substantial lift and assessing them via rubrics and checklists took time

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in a public school district

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Computational thinking competencies: algorithms, computational models, systems, data, and the impact of computing

Related Elements¶

  • CT Pathways Toolkit for district design of K-12 CS/CT pathways
  • IPSD Inclusive CT Pathway competency map of six computational thinking competencies

Examples¶

  • Use NGSS-aligned K-8 science coursework as the starting point for district CT integration
  • Appoint a district-level CS/CT leadership position to center and prioritize computing as a curricular offering
  • Leverage school-based librarians to create and deliver K-6 CS/CT lessons when classroom integration slows
  • Commit at the district level to computing pathways that are cumulative, consistent, and competency-based across K-12 schools
