# Pre-K Mathematics supplemental preschool curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Pre-K Mathematics is "a supplemental curriculum designed to develop the informal mathematical knowledge and skills of preschool children with content organized into seven units": counting and numbers, arithmetic operations (fall and spring), spatial sense and geometry, patterns, measurement and data, and logical reasoning. Concepts are taught through teacher-guided small-group activities with concrete manipulatives, with parallel take-home activities designed to help parents support their children's mathematical development at home. It was developed by Alice Klein and Prentice Starkey with Alma Ramirez and distributed by Pearson Scott Foresman.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom implementation uses teacher-guided small-group lessons of four to six children, twice weekly for approximately 20 minutes, over the school year, supported by teacher workshops and on-site training in the reviewed studies.
#### Constraints
- The reviewed evidence addresses Pre-K Mathematics combined with DLM Early Childhood Express Math software; the core program package cost $152.47 and information on the cost of professional development is not available.

### Target Learners
- preschool children in Head Start and public preschool programs

### Target Learning Goals
- informal mathematical knowledge and skills: counting, arithmetic operations, spatial sense, geometry, patterns, measurement, data, and logical reasoning
