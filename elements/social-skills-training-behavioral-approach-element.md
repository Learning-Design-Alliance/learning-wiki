# Social skills training: a behavioral, practice-based approach to teaching preschool social skills

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Social skills training is "not a specific curriculum, but rather a collection of practices that use a behavioral approach for teaching preschool children age-appropriate social skills and competencies, including communication, problem solving, decision making, self-management, and peer relations." Teachers use modeling, role-playing, and specific instruction; children then practice the skills and receive positive reinforcement for appropriate social behavior. It can occur in regular or special education classrooms, with individual children, small groups, or whole classes, typically for up to one hour daily over a few days to several weeks.
