# Meta-analytic corpus of 20 pretest-posttest control-group studies on creativity applications (2000–2015)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article assembles a corpus of 20 studies meeting its inclusion criteria: pretest-posttest control-group designs comparing CBL, PBL, or DI against traditional lecture-based instruction, published between 2000 and 2015 in English or Turkish, reporting statistics sufficient to compute effect sizes. Of the 20 studies, "1 article, 9 PhD theses, 10 master's theses were retained in the analysis with a total sample size of 1089 students," split into 549 experimental and 540 control students. Analyses were run in Comprehensive Meta-Analysis and MetaWin with inter-coder reliability of 100%.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies must report sample sizes, means and standard deviations, or t-test, F-test, or p-values so effect sizes could be calculated
#### Constraints
- Only pretest-posttest control-group studies were included; no single-subject studies
- Reports had to be available in English or Turkish
- One outlier study (Bacak, 2008, d:15.7584) was excluded from the data set

### Target Learners
- Students across grade levels in the included experimental studies

### Target Learning Goals
- Academic achievement as the dependent variable, examined in relation to creativity-focused applications
