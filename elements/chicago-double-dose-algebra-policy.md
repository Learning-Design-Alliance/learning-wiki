# Chicago's double-dose algebra policy (two periods of algebra with teacher supports)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (4 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
An instructional support strategy in which "First-time ninth-grade students who tested below the national median on the eighth-grade math test enrolled in two periods of algebra coursework—a regular algebra class and an additional algebra support class, for a full academic year." The district provided professional development workshops three times a year and new curricular materials, plus three implementation guidelines: the same teacher for both courses, sequential scheduling, and the same classmates in both periods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Same teacher in algebra and algebra support; courses offered sequentially; support class taken with the same students as the regular algebra class; teacher professional development and new curricular materials
#### Constraints
- Did not specifically address accommodations for students with learning disabilities

### Target Learners
- Ninth-graders scoring below the national median on the eighth-grade math test

### Target Learning Goals
- Algebra skills, course passing, and preparation for advanced mathematics
