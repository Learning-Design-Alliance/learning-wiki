# Healthy Harlem two-tier program: universal Prevention component plus targeted Get Fit program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Healthy Harlem is a comprehensive program aimed at promoting healthy lifestyles among Harlem Children's Zone after-school students. It has two tiers: a universal Prevention component, which "includes physical activity, healthy snacks, nutrition education lessons, and parent workshops," and Get Fit, an intensive program for overweight or obese middle and high school students in which students "identify and work on specific goals for improving their eating and/or physical activity habits." The evaluation examined impacts on knowledge, attitudes, social support, fitness, and BMI.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery through HCZ after-school programs, with all students exposed to the Prevention component
- For Get Fit, students must be middle or high school students who are overweight or obese
#### Constraints
- Get Fit is a 12-week intensive program targeted only to students who are overweight or obese; the Prevention component is the universal tier

### Target Learners
- Students attending HCZ after-school programs in Central Harlem, including middle and high school students who are overweight or obese

### Target Learning Goals
- Nutrition knowledge
- Self-efficacy for healthy eating and physical activity
- Healthy eating and physical-activity habits
