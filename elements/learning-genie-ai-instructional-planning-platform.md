# Learning Genie: AI-supported instructional planning and documentation platform using UDL principles

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Learning Genie is "an AI-supported instructional planning and documentation platform for preschool through grade 12" that helps educators design standards-aligned instruction "using Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles." It provides AI-assisted unit design and lesson-planning tools supporting differentiation, inclusion, and alignment with high-quality instructional materials, while integrating student evidence, contextual learning needs, and learner profiles into planning. Digital portfolios, DRDP-aligned assessment tools, and multimodal documentation capture student voice and support student agency, and the platform facilitates collaboration, bilingual family communication, and reporting through data-system integrations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom teachers use it as part of regular instructional and assessment workflow; a site- or district-level implementation lead coordinates account setup, SIS integration, and technical support during initial rollout
- Schools require reliable internet access and standard classroom devices including tablets, Chromebooks, and desktop computers using modern browsers
#### Constraints
- Initial rollout requires coordination of account setup, SIS integration, and technical support by an implementation lead

### Target Learners
- Prekindergarten through grade 12 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Standards-aligned instruction
- Language and literacy development
- Mathematics development
- Social and emotional development
