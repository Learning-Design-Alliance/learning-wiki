# Wraparound support services: counseling and special education support alongside academic instruction

## Description
Beyond academic instruction, the Wraparound program offers counseling services to address students' social and emotional needs and special education support to accommodate diverse learning requirements. Counselors must hold counseling certification registered in compliance with state law and/or an MSW, and all staff complete pre-service onboarding training plus ongoing professional development for each service type.
