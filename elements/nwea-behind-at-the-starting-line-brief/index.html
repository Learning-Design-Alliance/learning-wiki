NWEA research brief on Kindergarten scores and third-grade proficiency¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (3 associational), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A research brief by NWEA's research team (Megan Kuhfeld, Karyn Lewis, Emily Morton) examining Kindergarten test scores from more than 400,000 U.S. students. Per its description, it notes "patterns that can predict academic success or identify students at risk of falling further behind," positioning the work as support for educators' early identification efforts ahead of third grade.
