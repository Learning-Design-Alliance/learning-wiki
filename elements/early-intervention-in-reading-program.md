# Early Intervention in Reading® (EIR®) supplemental beginning-reading program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
EIR® is a supplemental program providing extra instruction to groups of students at risk of failing to learn to read. The report states "The program uses picture books to stress instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, and contextual analysis, along with repeated reading and writing." In grades K–2 it is whole-class instruction with additional small-group work for struggling readers; in grades 3–4 it is small-group instruction 20 minutes, four days a week. Instruction involves 15–20 minutes of daily supplemental instruction to the whole class or groups of five to seven struggling readers, developed by Dr. Barbara Taylor and distributed by the EIR® program.
