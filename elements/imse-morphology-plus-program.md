# IMSE's Morphology+ structured literacy professional development and curriculum program for grades 3-5

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
IMSE's Morphology+ combines 30 hours of educator training with 148 sequenced lessons providing "explicit instruction in prefixes, suffixes, Greek and Latin roots, vocabulary development, and comprehension." It is designed for grades 3-5, builds on foundational phonics instruction, and supports students in analyzing complex academic vocabulary through systematic word study and cumulative review. It can be implemented by general education, special education, and intervention teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers should adhere to the instructional routines and lesson components.
- All teachers, including classroom assistants, who work with students during their literacy block should be trained in IMSE's Morphology+ program.
#### Constraints
- Designed for grades 3-5 and intended to build on foundational phonics instruction.

### Target Learners
- Third- through fifth-grade students in elementary literacy instruction

### Target Learning Goals
- Morphological analysis of complex academic vocabulary
- Vocabulary development
- Reading comprehension
