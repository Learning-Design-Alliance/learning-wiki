# CHSRI-supported small high schools in Chicago (11 schools in year two, including conversion and contract schools)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 11 claims (6 for, 2 mixed, 3 against) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 11 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Chicago High School Redesign Initiative created new small high schools within converted large high school buildings (Bowen, Orr, South Shore) plus two Big Picture Company contract schools, designed with "no more than 500 students, a cohesive and self-selected faculty, more ﬂexibility and autonomy than traditional high schools." By 2004, 11 schools participated. Students entered substantially below district averages: average eighth-grade ITBS of 229 at conversion schools versus 246 in non-CHSRI schools, and 23.3 percent of freshmen receiving special education services versus 17.7 percent district-wide.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District policies granting flexibility and autonomy; funding including Gates Foundation support
#### Constraints
- Host buildings had been on probation since 1997; staff worried about sustainability once Gates Foundation money ends

### Target Learners
- Predominantly African-American and Latino high school students with low incoming achievement

### Target Learning Goals
- Personalized schooling, thematic curricular focus, and improved instruction
