Intelligent Tutoring for the Structure Strategy (ITSS): web-based text-structure tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ITSS is a web-based reading comprehension program that "teaches students to use the organization of nonfiction texts to identify and remember important information." Students learn text structures including "comparison, problem and solution, cause and effect, sequence, and description" and use signaling words to identify main ideas and monitor comprehension. Students "work individually with an animated tutor that models strategies, provides practice and feedback," progressing at their own pace through lessons used as a partial substitute for regular language arts instruction for 30 to 45 minutes per week.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Web-based technology
  • A part-time system administrator

Constraints¶

  • Typically used as a partial substitute for regular language arts instruction for 30 to 45 minutes per week

Target Learners¶

  • Students in Grades 4, 5, and 7 in rural and suburban schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading comprehension of nonfiction texts, including identifying main ideas and text structures

Claims¶

  • ITSS shows a positive but statistically non-significant effect on Grade 4 reading comprehension (effect size +0.10) [+W]
  • ITSS significantly improves Grade 5 reading comprehension after 6 to 7 months of implementation (effect size +0.20) [+W]
  • ITSS significantly improves Grade 7 reading comprehension in rural and suburban schools (effect size +0.18) [+W]
  • ITSS qualifies for a Strong evidence rating based on multiple randomized studies with average effect sizes of +0.15 (elementary) and +0.18 (secondary) [+W]
