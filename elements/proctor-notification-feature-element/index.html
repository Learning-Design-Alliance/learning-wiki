Proctor notification feature for detecting and responding to student disengagement¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 4 studies (2 causal, 1 associational, 1 design), q2–q3 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A notification mechanism within a testing platform that alerts proctors when students disengage. The brief describes research on "student test engagement" showing "how proctor notification can make a significant impact," presenting the notification as a component of the assessment system that supports test-day monitoring of engagement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Integration with a testing platform capable of signaling disengagement events to proctors

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students taking standardized tests

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Valid assessment scores reflecting students' best effort

Claims¶

  • Proctor notification after rapid-guessing detection improved test performance and yielded higher convergent validation evidence [+W]
  • After a proctor notification triggered by rapid-guessing detection, test-taking engagement tended to increase [+W]
  • The assessment's engagement feature had a consistently positive impact under both in-person and remote proctoring [+W]
  • The test platform detects rapid guessing in real time and attempts to re-engage disengaged students to protect score validity [+W]
  • Proctor notification when students disengage makes a significant impact on student test engagement [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Effort monitoring with proctor notification feature for computer-based tests

Examples¶

  • Notify test proctors when students disengage so students give their best effort on testing day
  • Use automated disengagement detection with proctor notification during remote low-stakes testing
