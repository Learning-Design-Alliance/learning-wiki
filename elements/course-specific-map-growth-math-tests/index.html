Course-specific MAP Growth Mathematics tests (Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A suite of course-specific interim assessments released by NWEA in 2017 (NWEA- and CCSS-aligned) and 2018 (state-aligned versions for Florida, Missouri, Texas, and Virginia). The tests are "item-level computerized adaptive tests (CATs)" scaled with the Rasch model, use randomesque exposure control and content balancing, range from 41 to 43 items, and report scores on the RIT scale shared with regular MAP Growth Mathematics tests, though scores are not interchangeable across domains.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administered multiple times per year (typically fall, winter, spring) to support growth evaluation over a course
  • Items assessing course prerequisites to assess course readiness

Constraints¶

  • A score of 220 on a course-specific test should not be used interchangeably with a 220 on MAP Growth Mathematics because they test different subject domains

Target Learners¶

  • Students in Grades 6–12 enrolled in Algebra 1, Geometry, or Algebra 2 courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Course-specific mathematics achievement and growth in Algebra 1, Geometry, and Algebra 2

Related Elements¶

  • Course-specific MAP Growth tests (Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, Integrated Math 1–3, Biology/Life Science)
  • NWEA MAP Growth assessment and longitudinal achievement database
