# Schon's design-studio practicum as a reflective learning environment

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper describes Schon's ideal learning environment, the practicum, in which teacher and student confront real problems in a secure, almost-real setting and grow together through dialogue. In the design studio, 'The established challenging design problem here serves as a thematic organizer'; the teacher's design-review responses are modeling demonstrations of reflection-in-action that students may adopt, and students grasp the process through reflection-on-action.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A challenging, real design problem
- dialogue between teacher and student
- design reviews at the student's work bench
#### Constraints
- Described in the context of professional education, though the paper seeks ways it can fit professional education more broadly

### Target Learners
- professional students and novices
- design students

### Target Learning Goals
- reflection-in-action
- shared intuitive understanding of design process
