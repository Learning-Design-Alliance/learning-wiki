# Promotion power measure of Louisiana high schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Promotion power is defined in the report as "a school’s effect on the long-term success of its students" as indicated by high school graduation, college or career readiness, college enrollment and persistence, and job market success. Mathematica calculated it for each Louisiana public high school using a statistical model applied to statewide administrative data, in partnership with the Louisiana Department of Education and with Walton Family Foundation funding. The measure separates school contributions from factors such as family resources and students' learning before high school.
