Teach For America alternate-route teacher preparation program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 9 claims (6 for, 3 mixed) · 5 studies (4 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 5 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Teach For America is a nation-wide alternate route teacher preparation program designed to address the shortage of effective teachers in high-poverty rural and urban K–12 public schools in the USA. It "selectively recruits both college undergraduates and graduates—many from top colleges—and professionals to teach in low‐income schools". Roughly 25% of recruits become corps members through a selection process including a writing activity, telephone interview, sample teaching lesson plan, group discussion, and in-person interview. Since 1990 TFA has placed approximately 40,000 corps members, and in 2012 recruited approximately 5% of the graduating classes of 135 colleges and universities.
