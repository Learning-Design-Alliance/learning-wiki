# Outcome-based contracting (OBC) edtech rate cards tying payment to measured learner outcomes

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (4 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The presentation presents OBC edtech contracts structured as rate cards in which vendors are paid per student for achieving defined outcome metrics, alongside a base payment for services delivered. The worked example ties payments to FAST ELA results: Outcome 1 pays $6 per student for a percentile rank increase from PM1 to PM2 with a $12,250 cap, Outcome 4 pays $21 for Level 3 or above on 24-25 FAST PM3 ELA, and a base payment of $18 per student totals $65 per student and $122,500 overall. A second example rate card ties payments to TELPAS composite growth levels. Contracts also specify process outcomes such as usage of 45 min/week per module, progress-monitoring assessment windows, and Personalized Learning Plan progress tracked bi-weekly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must define payment outcome metrics, assessment timeframes, and process outcomes (usage, progress monitoring, personalized learning plan progress) with data collection timeframes
#### Constraints
- Outcome payments are capped per outcome (e.g., $12,250 for Outcome 1), and the TELPAS example excludes students in Appendix B, which has a cap of $5,000

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, including English learners (TELPAS example) and elementary ELA students (FAST example)

### Target Learning Goals
- ELA achievement growth and English language proficiency growth tied to vendor payment
