# i-Ready Classroom Mathematics (iRCL): a K-8 core mathematics curriculum combining print and digital materials

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
iRCL is described as "a core K–8 math curriculum designed to build conceptual understanding, problem-solving skills, and student engagement." It combines print and digital materials including teacher guides, student workbooks, practice tools, and hands-on manipulables, and integrates professional learning supports for teachers implementing the curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementations require points of contact to assist with initial set-up and disseminate communications to other staff members
#### Constraints
- No additional staff are required; set-up support is provided by i-Ready Partners

### Target Learners
- K-8 students, including those who need additional scaffolding

### Target Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding in mathematics
- Problem-solving skills
- Student engagement
