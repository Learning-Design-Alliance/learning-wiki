# Graphic Design for Researchers guide covering design elements for research publications and digital products

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A guide prepared for the Institute of Education Sciences' Regional Education Laboratories that 'covers key concepts behind good graphic design'. It discusses how researchers can use 'basic elements like photographs, images, color, tables, figures, and type' when creating publications and digital products for disseminating research.
