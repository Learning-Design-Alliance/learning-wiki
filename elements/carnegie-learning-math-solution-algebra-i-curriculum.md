# Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution Algebra I curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A first-year algebra course designed for students ranging in ability and grade levels. The curriculum "combines traditional textbook and workbook materials with self-paced individualized instruction via automated tutoring software." It was evaluated in three qualifying studies with 20,485 students and carries a strong evidence rating with a weighted effect size of +0.04.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers are expected to receive at minimum three days of initial training, plus four or more days per teacher of in-classroom support and instructional coaching in at least the first two years of implementation.
#### Constraints
- No additional staffing requirements are needed beyond existing staff.

### Target Learners
- High school students
- Eighth-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- First-year algebra proficiency
