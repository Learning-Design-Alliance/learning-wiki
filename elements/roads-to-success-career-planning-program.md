# Roads to Success education and career planning program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Roads to Success is a school and career planning program evaluated over a 2007-2009 time frame in high-poverty rural schools. The report assesses "the impact of two years of exposure to the school and career planning program, Roads to Success, on eighth graders from high-poverty rural schools." It targets career planning knowledge, confidence, and engagement with school staff around career and school plans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two years of exposure to the program, as evaluated in the report
#### Constraints
- Evaluated among eighth graders in high-poverty rural schools

### Target Learners
- Middle school students, specifically eighth graders in high-poverty rural schools

### Target Learning Goals
