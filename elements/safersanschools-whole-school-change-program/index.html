SaferSanerSchools™ Whole-School Change program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A whole-school change program disseminated through the International Institute for Restorative Practices and grounded in 11 essential elements centering on communication, responsibility, restoration, and separating the deed from the doer. All school staff learn to enact the key elements with the aim of increasing understanding of how one's actions affect others and creating a better school climate. Implementation requires a designated school team and two days of pre-launch training.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools designate a team of at least three members (administrator, interventionist, and teacher) who meet monthly
  • School-based teams attend two days of training prior to program start

Constraints¶

  • Implementation was evaluated over 2 years in the Pittsburgh study

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 school students
  • all school staff

Target Learning Goals¶

  • school climate improvement
  • restorative communication and responsibility
  • attendance

Claims¶

  • Whole-school restorative practices program significantly improves average daily attendance at the student level [+W]
  • Participating schools report more positive teacher and staff perceptions of teaching and learning conditions [+W]
  • Whole-school restorative practices program significantly reduces chronic absenteeism at the student level [+W]
  • School-level outcomes of the whole-school restorative practices program were not significant [-W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Establish a monthly school implementation team with two days of pre-launch training and ongoing technical support
