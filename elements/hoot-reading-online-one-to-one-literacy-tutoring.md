# Hoot Reading online one-to-one literacy tutoring program

## Description
Hoot Reading provides online, one-to-one literacy instruction delivered by trained teachers, with sessions taking place over video call through the company's proprietary platform. The platform was developed through an earlier research collaboration involving the Nokia Research Lab, Sesame Workshop, and the Joan Ganz Cooney Center. The program works with school districts across North America to advance student reading achievement, and tutors are provided by Hoot Reading while school staff supervise at each location.
