# Collaborative Decision-Making

## Description
Collaborative decision-making is the element in which learners evaluate options together and negotiate a shared choice or recommendation. It is useful when the instructional goal includes reasoning, tradeoff analysis, and group judgment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A decision with real alternatives**
- **Criteria or evidence to weigh collectively**
#### Constraints
- **Groups may converge too quickly without structured comparison of options**

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve shared reasoning, tradeoff analysis, and negotiated judgment.
