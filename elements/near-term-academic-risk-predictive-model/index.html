Predictive model of near-term academic risk combining school and child welfare data¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a predictive model that uses both school data and child welfare data to flag students at risk of near-term academic problems. The fact sheet states it "describes predictors related to near-term academic problems, meaning academic problems in the next quarter or semester," and that the companion report "details the approach used to develop a predictive model." The model's purpose is early identification of at-risk students so educators can intervene within the coming academic term.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to linked school and child welfare data sources to compute the predictors

  • The model targets near-term academic problems, defined as problems in the next quarter or semester, not longer-horizon outcomes

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students whose records appear in school and child welfare data systems

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Early identification of students at risk of near-term academic problems so support can be provided within the quarter or semester
