# Quinmester humanities course 'Ideas of Western Man: Expressionism and Realism' integrating literature, philosophy, art, and music

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This is an authorized course of instruction for the Dade County quinmester program in English and humanities. Its course description defines it as "An analysis of some predaninant schools of man's changing thought thragh the study of literature." The course focuses on philosophy and uses painting, sculpture, and music to illustrate each school of thought, treating Expressionism and Realism as they have affected contemporary American culture.
