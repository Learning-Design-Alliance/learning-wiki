# Teaching Fractions Toolkit (TFT) providing grade 6 teacher PD modules, reflection tool, and formative assessment probes

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The TFT is a professional development and support package for grade 6 math teachers on evidence-based fraction instruction. It includes "six PD modules, a teacher reflection tool, and formative assessment probes", plus resources for administrators and math leaders such as "videos, infographics, checklists, and teacher PD facilitator guides".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Co-development with participating districts and schools so the toolkit fits local grade 6 fraction instruction
#### Constraints
- Development uses "an iterative research and development model, across several cohorts", so components are still being refined

### Target Learners
- Grade 6 math students in Illinois school districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding of fractions
- fraction computation
- ratio and rate problem solving
