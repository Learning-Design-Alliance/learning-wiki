Linking Learning to Careers (LLC) program model for high school students with disabilities¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

LLC is a Vermont Division of Vocational Rehabilitation program offering high school students with disabilities "an individualized and targeted approach to strategically plan for their futures." It builds on usual DVR services by emphasizing unpaid and paid work-based learning experiences in integrated environments, college exploration and coursework at the Community College of Vermont, team-based guidance from DVR staff, dedicated assistive technology support, and additional transportation funding. It was evaluated with a randomized controlled trial recruiting 803 participants.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • DVR usual services as a base, plus work-based learning experiences, CCV coursework opportunities, DVR team-based guidance, assistive technology support, and transportation funding

Constraints¶

  • The report suggests the LLC model may not have been sufficiently different from usual services, which could explain the lack of consistent employment impacts

Target Learners¶

  • high school students with disabilities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • career readiness, postsecondary education enrollment, employment

Claims¶

  • LLC increased the likelihood of earnings among later enrollees by 11 percentage points [+M]
  • LLC increased postsecondary education enrollment: 31 percent of participants enrolled versus 22 percent of the control group [+M]
  • LLC produced large impacts on service use in the two years after enrollment, particularly services the program intended to offer [+M]
  • LLC did not affect employment for all participants within the first 24 months of enrollment [~M]
