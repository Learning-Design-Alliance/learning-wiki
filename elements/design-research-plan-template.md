# Design Research Plan Template: a planning tool for incorporating community needs into program, practice, or product design

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Design Research Plan Template is a tool for organizations that want to incorporate the needs and preferences of the communities they intend to reach into their program, practice, or product's design. It includes space to document a description of the program, practice, or product; a theory of change; learning objectives and approach; and a timeline to carry out the work. As organizations complete design research activities, they update the plan with results and adjustments. It is part of Mathematica's suite of measurement and evaluation tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizations must document their program, practice, or product description, theory of change, learning objectives and approach, and a timeline to carry out the work
#### Constraints
- Designed for organizations intending to reach specific communities whose needs and preferences should inform design

### Target Learners
- Organizations designing programs, practices, or products for the communities they intend to reach

### Target Learning Goals
- Incorporating community needs and preferences into program, practice, or product design
