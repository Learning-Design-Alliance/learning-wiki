K-12 Digital Equity Framework Self-Assessment tool¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Digital Promise designed a Digital Equity Framework Self-Assessment to facilitate adoption of the framework in schools and districts. Through its application, schools and districts can identify domains for which they have already addressed indicators and domains requiring substantial action. The tool asks for a holistic, cross-domain view while allowing flexible use: districts may focus on areas aligned with immediate needs and gradually expand efforts, revisiting the self-assessment to monitor progress in the spirit of continuous improvement. Figure 2 shows the tool for the Leadership for Digital Transformation domain with ratings of Emerging, Accelerating, or Innovating.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools and districts should operationalize all domains in ways that reflect their interdependence and incorporate the framework's three guiding principles.

Constraints¶

  • The objectives and tool are not one-size-fits-all; schools and districts are encouraged to use the tool flexibly.
