Pennsylvania Department of Education elementary school student school climate survey¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A school climate survey for elementary school students, available on a voluntary basis to any school in Pennsylvania, that "provides a way for schools to track their school climate and identify aspects of school climate that need additional support". It comprises three domains: social-emotional learning; safe and respectful school climate; and student support and academic engagement. PDE's Office for Safe Schools partnered with REL Mid-Atlantic to assess its validity and reliability.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Voluntary adoption by schools; validity and reliability established through the REL Mid-Atlantic analysis, with one domain recommended for revision

Constraints¶

  • The safe and respectful school climate domain fell short of the established reliability threshold

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Tracking school climate and identifying aspects needing additional support

Claims¶

  • The three domains of the PDE elementary school climate survey were distinct from one another [+W]
  • Items in each of the three domains of the PDE elementary school climate survey measured the constructs they intended to measure [+W]
  • The safe and respectful school climate domain of the elementary student survey fell short of the established reliability threshold [-W]
  • Students and teachers reported more positive school climate perceptions in 2020/21 during hybrid and remote learning than in 2018/19 and 2021/22 [~W]
  • School climate scores were steady across the years before COVID-19 in the Pennsylvania sample [+W]
  • No evidence of a significant decline in school climate scores between 2018/19 and 2021/22 in the Pennsylvania sample [+W]
