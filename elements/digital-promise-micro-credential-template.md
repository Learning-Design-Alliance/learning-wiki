# Digital Promise micro-credential template structuring competency, method, research rationale, and submission criteria

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Issuers draft micro-credentials using a Digital Promise template with fields for Title, Competency, Key Method, Method Components, Supporting Rationale and Research, Resources, and Submission Guidelines and Criteria covering overview questions, work examples/artifacts/evidence with a scoring guide, and optional reflection. The Refugee Educator Academy's Julie Kasper called it "constraining in a good way—and really rigorous," forcing clarity in language and openly available research citations.
