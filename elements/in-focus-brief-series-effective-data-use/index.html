Three-brief In Focus series on effective data use in education¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A Mathematica Policy Research In Focus brief series, prepared for the Gates Foundation under the Strategic Data Use in Education project (2011-2015), comprising three briefs. This brief "focuses on supporting effective data use. The other two briefs focus on developing a coherent plan for effectively using data and on considerations for developing or enhancing a data system."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The series is designed for state officials, district-level staff, and system developers seeking to promote effective data use.

Constraints¶

  • The series was prepared for the Gates Foundation under the Strategic Data Use in Education project, time frame 2011-2015.

Target Learners¶

  • State officials, district-level staff, and system developers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective data use by decision makers and educators at any level of the education system

Claims¶

  • In practice, data often leaves educators and policymakers unmoved or drowns them in extraneous information rather than driving decisions [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Strategic Data Use in Education initiative evaluation (2011-2015)
  • Strategic Data Project and Education Pioneers fellowship programs placing data-skilled fellows in education agencies
  • Strategic Data Project and Education Pioneers programs supporting data capacity in education agencies
  • Teacher-Student Data Link (TSDL) project

Examples¶

  • Target data-system guidance separately to state officials, district-level staff, and system developers
