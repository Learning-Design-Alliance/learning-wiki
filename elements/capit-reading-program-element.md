# CAPIT Reading blended supplemental phonics program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
CAPIT Reading is "a blended supplemental phonics program designed for all three tiers of instruction in PK-3 classrooms." It provides "explicit, systematic phonics instruction through a Sound-to-Print approach," beginning with decoding, encoding, and handwriting and advancing to vocabulary, fluency, and comprehension. Its "competency-based digital platform provides students with individualized instruction and immediate feedback, while equipping educators with actionable data."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implemented by current ELA classroom teachers, school coaches, and para-professionals
- Device-agnostic technology accessible on iOS and any device with a web browser
#### Constraints
- Designed as a supplemental program for PK-3 classrooms across all three tiers of instruction

### Target Learners
- Pre-kindergarten through grade 3 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational literacy skills including decoding, encoding, handwriting, vocabulary, fluency, and comprehension
