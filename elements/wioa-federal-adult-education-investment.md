# The Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) as the key federal investment in adult basic skills and high school equivalency

## Description
The Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) is described in the report as "the key federal investment helping adults acquire these and other important skills, as well as to earn a high school equivalency credential." It operates by encouraging adult education programs toward evidence-based service improvement. In the report, WIOA supplies the policy rationale for identifying and building evidence on adult education strategies.
