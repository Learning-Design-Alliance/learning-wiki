## Description
A 2019 NWEA research brief by Megan Kuhfeld and James Soland, categorized under Measurement & scaling, School & test engagement, and Student growth & accountability policies. Its stated purpose is to help readers "Learn more about our examination of student disengagement and how it may bias estimates of effectiveness based on observed test results." The brief page also links associated research, including a working paper and journal article on using assessment metadata to quantify disengagement's impact.
