NWEA MAP Growth interim assessments¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 4 studies (2 associational, 2 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

NWEA's MAP Growth assessments are interim assessments used by states and districts during the 2020-2021 school year. The article describes them as the data source for investigating school-composition change: researchers "examine data from NWEA's MAP Growth assessments, interim assessments used by states and districts during the 2020–2021 school year". School-aggregate scores from these assessments are used by school officials to monitor school performance and make policy decisions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administered as part of state or district assessment programs to produce school-aggregate scores

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in states and districts administering MAP Growth

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Monitoring teaching and learning through interim assessment of student achievement and growth

Claims¶

  • After the U.S. Department of Education offered assessment waivers to all 50 states in 2019-2020, many educators and policymakers advocated restarting assessment programs in 2020-2021 to evaluate teaching and learning and inform recovery policies [+W]
  • Student test-taking disengagement on remotely administered adaptive interim assessments differs from disengagement on the same assessment administered in school [~W]
  • The dashboard primarily focuses on grades 3 through 8 because remote and in-person MAP Growth results are largely comparable for grades 3 and up [~W]
  • The NWEA COVID-19 visualization provides state-level insights into 2020–2021 MAP Growth performance to inform academic recovery [+W]
