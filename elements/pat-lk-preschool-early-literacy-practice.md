# Phonological Awareness Training plus Letter Knowledge Training practice for preschool early literacy

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A general early childhood practice combining phonological awareness training (activities teaching children to "identify, detect, delete, segment, or blend segments of spoken words") with letter knowledge training that includes "teaching children the letters of the alphabet and making an explicit link between letters and sounds." It has no single developer; the reviewed interventions were developed by study authors. It is used as a supplement to the regular classroom curriculum, with individual children or in small or large group settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implemented in English in center-based settings with children ages 3 to 5 or in preschool, per the WWC eligibility criteria for the reviewed evidence
#### Constraints
- No single developer provides materials; the report points readers to publicly available resources that have not been reviewed or endorsed by the WWC
- Information is not available on the number or demographics of children or centers using these practices, nor about costs of teacher training and implementation

### Target Learners
- Preschool children ages 3 to 5, including economically disadvantaged children and children whose primary language is not English

### Target Learning Goals
- Phonological awareness
- Print knowledge and letter knowledge
- Early reading and writing skills
