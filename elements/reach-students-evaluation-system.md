# REACH Students teacher evaluation system

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 11 claims (6 for, 4 mixed, 1 against) · 5 studies (4 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 11 claims rest on one study

## Description
REACH (Recognizing Educators Advancing Chicago's Students) is CPS's teacher evaluation system launched in 2012-13, replacing a 1970s-era checklist. It combines "multiple classroom observations" using a modified Danielson Framework rubric with student growth measures from Performance Tasks and value-added or expected gains scores. All administrators must be certified as evaluators through training modules and assessments. In its first year it applied with stakes to non-tenured teachers, generating over 36,000 observations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certified administrator evaluators trained via modules and two assessments
- Pre- and post-observation conferences for each formal observation
- District data systems linking students and teachers for growth calculations
#### Constraints
- In 2012-13 only non-tenured teachers received a summative evaluation score
- Student growth capped at 25 percent of the evaluation score in the initial year

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Teacher instructional improvement
- Differentiation of strong and weak teacher practice for personnel decisions
