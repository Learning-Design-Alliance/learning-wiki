# Structured Discussion

## Description
Structured discussion is the element in which talk is guided by prompts, roles, turns, protocols, or evidence requirements rather than left entirely open. It is useful when the goal is to make discussion more equitable, rigorous, and productive.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A discussable question or problem**
- **Clear participation structure**
- **Prompts or norms that keep the talk evidence-based**
#### Constraints
- **Over-structuring can flatten spontaneity**
- **Weak prompts still produce shallow discussion**

### Target Learners
- Learners who benefit from support in participation, reasoning, and collaborative sensemaking.

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve quality of dialogue, reasoning, and equitable participation.
