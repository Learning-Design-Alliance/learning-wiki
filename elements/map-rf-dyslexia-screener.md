# MAP Reading Fluency Dyslexia Screener with multivariate predictive model and RAN

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Dyslexia Screener form assesses key reading skills "including those most often associated with dyslexia, without the need for a separate assessment." "The Sentence Reading Fluency score and domain scores in Phonological Awareness, Phonics & Word Recognition, and Language Comprehension are included in a multivariate predictive model flagging students at risk of dyslexia," with Rapid Automatized Naming (RAN) scores supplementing the model. The screener became available to all users in Fall 2021 and is group-administered and automatically scored.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rapid Automatized Naming (RAN) data supplement the multivariate predictive model
#### Constraints
- A flag on these reports is not a diagnosis of dyslexia or of a reading disability; it is an indicator that performance suggests possible risk factors

### Target Learners
- pre-K to Grade 5 students in English-speaking classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- early identification of students at risk of dyslexia or other reading difficulties
