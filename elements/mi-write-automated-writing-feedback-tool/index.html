MI Write automated writing feedback tool¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 2 against) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 review), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MI Write is an automated writing feedback tool used in classrooms. The article describes a study of the tool "which took place during the 2021–2022 school year," examining "teachers' and students' use of the tool during the study and the impact of MI Write on student and teacher outcomes." The tool is the focal artifact of the study and of the accompanying briefs for teachers, students and families, and school and district leaders.

  • Teachers found Ecree and MI Write useful for their writing instruction and for helping students improve their writing [+W]
