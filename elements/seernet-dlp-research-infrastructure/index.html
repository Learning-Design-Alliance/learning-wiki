SEERNet: a network of five digital learning platforms operating as research infrastructure¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

SEERNet connects platform developers, researchers, and practitioners to leverage existing digital learning platforms as research infrastructure. The five DLPs—OpenStax, Mathia, ASSISTments, Canvas, and ASU—pair learning applications with research infrastructures (Kinetic, UpGrade, E-TRIALS, Terracotta, ASU's Learning@Scale) that "enable varying methodological approaches (e.g., student-level random assignment), and provide built-in efficiencies to conduct studies on large scales." The network aims to reduce the chasm between research insights and improvements in learning experiences.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Researcher-DLP collaboration; alignment of planned comparisons with existing platform content; data privacy infrastructure

Constraints¶

  • Platforms have bounds on available data, supported experimental comparisons, and modifiable features; some studies require traditional recruitment for smaller samples

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 and adult learners using the platforms; education researchers studying them

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving mathematics teaching and learning and other platform-delivered learning through research embedded in authentic environments

Related Elements¶

  • ASSISTments/E-TRIALS: free A/B testing platform integrated with a K-12 math practice platform
  • Canvas/Terracotta: LMS-embedded tool for randomized experiments within regular instruction
  • MATHia/UpGrade: open-source field-trial platform integrated with Carnegie Learning's adaptive math tutoring system
  • SEERNet: five digital learning platforms, six research teams, and a network hub coordinated as research infrastructure
  • SEERNet shared digital learning platform infrastructure enabling a performer community model
  • SEERNet hub of five digital learning platforms enabling large-scale education research
  • Terracotta: a SEERNet platform enabling randomized research within the Canvas LMS
  • SEERNet network connecting platforms, researchers, and educators
  • UpGrade open-source A/B testing platform for classroom-embedded field experiments

Examples¶

  • Plan an extended feasibility phase in which research and platform teams jointly refine design, implementation, and logistics
  • Conduct a smaller sequence of rapid-cycle studies that build on one another instead of planning a single large confirmatory study
  • Run conversational office hours in which practitioners and researchers co-design research questions with platform representatives
  • Use a two-phase feasibility process — a pre-submission platform feasibility letter and a post-award six-month feasibility phase — before research funds are committed
  • Five recommendations for integrating Improvement Science with SEERNet
