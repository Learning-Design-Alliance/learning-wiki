# Chula Vista Promise Neighborhood (CVPromise): a place-based community program serving the Castle Park neighborhood

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
CVPromise is a Promise Neighborhood initiative serving the Castle Park neighborhood of Chula Vista, California. The profile describes it as serving "the neighborhood of Castle Park, a 33-census block area in the city of Chula Vista." The program is documented as part of the Promise Neighborhoods Case Studies project (2014-2015), prepared for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink by Mathematica Policy Research, covering areas including early childhood, child development, education, college and career readiness, and family support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Operates in collaboration with Chula Vista Elementary School District leadership, whose participation defined the program's catchment boundary
#### Constraints
- Serves a geographically bounded catchment: the Castle Park neighborhood, a 33-census block area in the city of Chula Vista

### Target Learners
- Children and families in the Castle Park neighborhood of Chula Vista

### Target Learning Goals
- Education and college and career readiness for children in the catchment area
