# The Evaluation Toolkit: a teacher-facing instrument for assessing technology-rich learning activities

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Evaluation Toolkit is a work-in-progress set of instruments developed by the Benton Foundation and EDC's Center for Children and Technology with teachers and administrators in Chicago and Milwaukee. It is "designed to aid teachers in assessing the impact of technology-rich activities on skills development, student learning and media literacy" and builds on ISTE, state, and local technology standards. It helps teachers determine students' sophistication with technology, define clear lesson objectives, and see concrete examples of student attainment.
