# Combined TRUST and Panorama Teacher and Staff Survey instrument for adult SEL

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's survey instrument combines elements of two existing instruments: the TRUST, which "evaluates respondents' emotion regulation (effectiveness for making themselves feel better) and relationship management skills (building/maintaining a positive teacher-student relationship)", and Panorama Education's Teacher and Staff Survey, which assesses professional well-being and school climate perceptions. Demographic items and school NSLP percentages were added. It was administered to K-8 educators from March to April 2024 with a 19% response rate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the TRUST and Panorama Teacher and Staff Survey source instruments, plus district-provided school NSLP percentages
#### Constraints
- The TRUST was developed using samples of pre-service and in-service teachers in Germany, so fit to U.S. K-12 contexts is uncertain

### Target Learners
- K-8 educators in a large U.S. public school district

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring educators' emotion regulation, relationship management, well-being, and school climate perceptions

## Claims

- [Educators' emotion regulation, relationship management, well-being, and school climate perceptions did not predict whether students met i-Ready math or reading growth goals](../claims/adult-sel-measures-not-predicting-student-growth.md) [~W]
- [Teachers identifying as women or non-binary/third gender reported higher relationship management scores than men, while men reported higher well-being](../claims/gender-identity-relationship-management-wellbeing-differences.md) [~W]
- [White teachers reported higher relationship management scores than teachers of Color, a finding the authors urge reading with caution](../claims/racial-identity-relationship-management-differences.md) [~W]
- [The sample's emotion regulation and relationship management scores were comparable to the original TRUST validation sample of in-service teachers](../claims/sample-sel-scores-comparable-original-trust-sample.md) [+W]
- [Many teacher background characteristics showed no association with SEL skills, well-being, or school climate perceptions](../claims/teacher-background-characteristics-null-associations-sel.md) [~W]
