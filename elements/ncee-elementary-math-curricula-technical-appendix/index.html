NCEE technical appendix for the elementary math curricula evaluation, organized in six analytic sections¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A technical appendix published by the U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, providing "the details that underlie the analyses reported in the evaluation brief." It is organized in six sections: Study Curricula and Design, Data Collection, Construction of the Analysis File, Curriculum Effects on Student Math Achievement, Curriculum Implementation, and Effects of Switching Curricula. It serves as the methodological and evidentiary record for the brief's comparative curriculum findings.
