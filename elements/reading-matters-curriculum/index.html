Reading Matters first-year ECED curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Reading Matters is "the first year curriculum" of ECED. It "strengthens students' abilities to comprehend and gather information and to identify early learning strategies". It is a structured literacy curriculum based on authentic expository texts, provided as a supplement to regular English, and is taught by reallocated existing school staff with professional development support.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Reallocation of existing school staff to teach the course
  • Three-day introductory institute for teachers plus site visits and monthly phone check-ins

  • comprehending expository text

Claims¶

  • ECED schools made somewhat greater gains on state tests than control schools (effect size +0.06) in a large two-year study [+W]
  • ECED schools made greater gains on state math tests than control schools (effect size +0.14) in one large two-year study [+W]
