# Eight-session CLT-based thermodynamics educational software

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A desktop-based, guided learning module of eight sessions covering the thermodynamics unit of General Chemistry II, developed for individual self-paced learning with exercises and feedback. "The content of educational software was arranged according to some principles of CLT, including the goal-free effect, worked example effect, completion problem effect, split-attention effect, modality effect, and redundancy effect." Texts paired with visuals were voiced, students used individual headphones, and learners controlled transitions between interfaces. The draft was reviewed by eight experts and piloted before the main study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Individual headphones and a computer lab are used so students are not distracted; the software is desktop-based to avoid internet interruptions.
#### Constraints
- The software was developed for a specific target audience — first-year chemistry department students taking General Chemistry II.

### Target Learners
