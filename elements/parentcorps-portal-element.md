# ParentCorps Portal

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ParentCorps Portal is a technology component provided to program facilitators. "Facilitators receive access to the ParentCorps Portal, where they can connect with ParentCorps staff, access all program materials, and receive additional resources" to strengthen social-emotional learning and family engagement and enhance cultural and racial awareness in daily practice.
