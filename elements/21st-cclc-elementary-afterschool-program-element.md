# 21st Century Community Learning Center afterschool programs (elementary component, national evaluation)

## Description
The 21st Century Community Learning Center afterschool programs keep elementary schools open late, providing supervised after-school settings. The article presents evidence from "the elementary school component of Mathematica's national evaluation" of these programs, "conducted in 12 school districts and 26 afterschool centers." The evaluation measured supervision arrangements, self-care, safety perceptions, academic outcomes, and behavior incidents.
