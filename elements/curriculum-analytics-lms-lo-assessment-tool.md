# Curriculum analytics tools integrated into institutional LMSs for LO assessment and visualization

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
CA tools are learning analytics systems integrated into institutional LMSs that capture, store, and visualize LO assessment data reported by instructors at the end of each academic period. They support "the alignment between courses, assessments, and intended LOs" by mapping LOs to courses, extracting rubric- or scale-based assessment results, and generating visual reports such as percentages of students meeting expectations (U1) or mean scores, standard deviations, and achievement rates (U2). At U1 the tool was developed via design-based research with about five managers and 160 teaching staff, and redesigned into the LMS in 2024; at U2 it serves approximately nine engineering programs with 9,906 students and 153 instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty must map LOs to courses and indicate relationships between program graduate competencies and course LOs
- Instructors select course assessment methods or student work as evidence and apply rubrics or standardized scales
- Academic advisory units support mapping, data interpretation, and communication with developers
#### Constraints
- Used primarily to support LO assessment within the context of accreditation
- Visualization formats and metrics differ by institution (four-level rubric vs five-point scale)

### Target Learners
- higher education engineering students

### Target Learning Goals
- 21st-century competencies such as communication, teamwork, ethics, problem-solving, and lifelong learning
