# Drawing laboratory with time-lapse photography of drawing evolution

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A purpose-built laboratory for collecting in-process records of drawing: a neutral white room with a fixed tilt-top drawing board, a front-surface mirror, and a camera outside the studio area. The report states that "Parallel with this is a small window in the wall, through which a camera records by means of time-lapse photography the evolution of each drawing." Artists work on 12"x18" white pads in black and white media, with materials adjusted to artist requests in a nurturant environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers do not interfere with, intervene in, or interrupt ongoing drawings
#### Constraints
- All artists were restricted to black and white media

### Target Learners
- non-art undergraduate college students volunteering at any skill level

### Target Learning Goals
- recording and understanding individual drawing processes
