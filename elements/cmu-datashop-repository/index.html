DataShop educational data repository and analysis platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

DataShop was created out of a 2004 NSF grant to the Pittsburgh Science of Learning Center as "an online repository and analysis platform designed to support research and analysis of educational data." After 20 years it holds over 700,000 hours of student learning data across over 1000 unique data sets, serving as a hub for storing, sharing, and analyzing data from varied educational technology systems under open science principles. Early data came from cognitive tutoring systems with novel logging, formatting, and tagging formats.
