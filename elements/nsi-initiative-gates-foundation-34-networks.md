## Description
The Networks for School Improvement (NSI) initiative was a Gates Foundation investment in network-based continuous improvement from 2018 to 2025. The foundation awarded "more than $300 million to intermediary organizations that led 34 Networks for School Improvement (NSI) across the country." Networks were organized around grade-level and college-readiness goals including 8th-grade on-track, 9th-grade on-track, college-ready on-track, and postsecondary enrollment. This report evaluates student outcomes through schools' fourth year of participation.
