# CE-S-DSP & SOS hybrid self-report measure of cognitive engagement

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study used a hybrid cognitive engagement measure, CE-S-DSP & SOS, built from previously used self-reporting tools. The article reports it "showed preferable reliability for the overall score of cognitive engagement," but the data could not be explored with a 5-factor confirmatory factor analysis "due to model fit limitations." It served as the outcome measure for the platform comparison in grades 6-8.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reliance on the overall cognitive engagement score, for which reliability was preferable; the intended 5-factor structure could not be validated due to model fit limitations
#### Constraints
- 5-factor confirmatory factor analysis was not possible due to model fit limitations

### Target Learners
- students in grades 6-8

### Target Learning Goals
- self-reported cognitive engagement in mathematics assessment
