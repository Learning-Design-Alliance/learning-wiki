## Description
The Glossary of Artificial Intelligence Terms for Educators, by Pati Ruiz and Judi Fusco, is a Digital Promise reference resource written for educators to reference when learning about and using artificial intelligence. It explains how AI systems work: AI systems use hardware, algorithms, and data to create intelligence to do things like make decisions, discover patterns, and perform some sort of action.
