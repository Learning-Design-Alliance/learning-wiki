## Description
The article is a fact sheet produced for districts and schools addressing chronic absenteeism in the early grades. As the page states, it is a "Fact sheet for districts and schools on the importance of attendance in pre-kindergarten and kindergarten and how to choose and implement and proven strategies." It was published by Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic in November 2019 as part of work on improving the school attendance of New Jersey's youngest learners.
