# MAP Growth assessment as a data source for gender-gap trend analysis

## Description
MAP Growth is NWEA's national assessment used in this brief as one of three data sources for estimating gender gaps in 8th-grade STEM skills. The brief analyzes "five years of data from three national assessments – TIMMS, NAEP, and NWEA's MAP Growth" and separately draws on "a sample of MAP Growth data from two million students" for subgroup analyses.
