KinderTEK: an iPad-delivered kindergarten mathematics intervention¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

KinderTEK is an iPad-delivered kindergarten mathematics intervention whose development is the setting for the two studies reported in the article. The article describes it as "an iPad delivered kindergarten mathematics intervention" and measures its implementation through student use, including within-class variability of use and the percentage of students meeting a duration-of-use target. Implementation depended on instructor facilitation in authentic school settings.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor facilitation in authentic school settings, since instructor facilitation is described as an essential element in successful implementation of technology-based interventions

Claims¶

  • Instructors' pre-intervention iPad experience varied widely and this range corresponded with variable student use of the intervention [+W]
  • Instructors who reported moderate iPad experience and/or no barriers to technology integration demonstrated the least desirable student use patterns [+W]
  • Second-order technology barriers may be most influential on technology-based intervention implementation, and the quantity of barriers reported was associated with the percentage of students meeting target durations of use [+W]
