# Correlational analysis linking student achievement growth to district and CMO policies and practices

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
As one of its three study activities, the report "conducted a correlational analysis to understand the relationship between growth in student achievement and district and CMO policies and practices." This analysis, together with the literature review and case studies, provided the evidence base from which the report's three themes about effective levers for improving student achievement were derived.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data on growth in student achievement and on district and CMO policies and practices
#### Constraints
- The analysis is correlational, addressing relationships between achievement growth and policies and practices

### Target Learners
- Students in school districts and charter management organization networks

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying which district and CMO levers relate to student achievement growth
