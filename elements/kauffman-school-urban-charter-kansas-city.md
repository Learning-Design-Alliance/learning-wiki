## Description
The Ewing Marion Kauffman School is an urban charter school in Kansas City established by the Kauffman Foundation after years of operating local education programs. The page states that "The success of these programs led Foundation leaders to consider the impact they might have on students in Kansas City if they established a charter school." A Mathematica evaluation report documents the school's founding and its first three years of academic growth and key school characteristics.
