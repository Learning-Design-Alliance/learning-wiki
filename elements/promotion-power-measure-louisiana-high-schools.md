# Promotion power measure of high school effects on long-term student success

## Description
Promotion power is defined as "a school's effect on the long-term success of its students as indicated by high school graduation, college or career readiness, college enrollment and persistence, and success in the job market." The measure was computed for each Louisiana public high school, and is intended to isolate school contributions "separately from other factors, such as family resources and students' learning before high school."
