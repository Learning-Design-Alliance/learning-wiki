# Kolibri: Learning Equality's open-source, offline-first digital platform for content access without internet connectivity

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Kolibri is Learning Equality's "open-source, offline-first digital platform," listed in the project's future technology plans and described in its own slide. Students and teachers "access digital content through mobile devices"; it "Does not require Internet connectivity"; the "User interface & content in Haitian Creole"; and "Data uploads occur when device is able to connect." It serves the project's goal of improving access to digital content in schools with poor infrastructure, including the 57 pilot schools using offline resources only.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mobile devices for access; occasional connectivity for data uploads
#### Constraints
- Designed for settings without reliable internet access; data uploads only occur when a device can connect

### Target Learners
- Early grade primary students and teachers in rural Haitian schools with limited connectivity

### Target Learning Goals
- Access to standards-aligned digital learning content in Haitian Creole
