# 2007 CPS AVID student and teacher survey with individualized school reports

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A district-wide survey and reporting tool developed by CCSR for Chicago Public Schools' AVID program. In spring 2007 over 2,800 ninth and tenth grade AVID students and over 1,000 teachers in AVID schools were surveyed; 32 of 39 AVID schools participated, with a 77 percent student response rate. The report summarizes six student measures and one teacher measure (program satisfaction, skills, teacher support, tutors, postsecondary discussions, postsecondary academic planning), standardized to a mean of 1 and standard deviation of 1, and delivers each school an "individualized report like this one" comparing it with other AVID schools, disaggregated by achievement, gender, and grade level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Enough teachers responding: schools with fewer than 11 teachers and fewer than 40% responding were not included in the teacher measure
#### Constraints
- Categories from lowest to highest are relative to other AVID schools; a lowest school may still have fairly positive responses
- School confidentiality is maintained; schools are assigned random IDs in citywide reporting

### Target Learners
- Ninth and tenth grade AVID elective students in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Program self-assessment and long-term planning around the AVID program
- Improving academic skills, teacher and tutor support, and postsecondary planning
