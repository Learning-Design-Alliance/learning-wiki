# eMINTS Comprehensive Program: technology-integration professional development and coaching for teachers

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The eMINTS Comprehensive Program is a teacher professional development intervention aiming to help teachers "integrate technology into their curriculum, implementing high-quality lesson plans, and building a learning community within their school." It supports inquiry-based learning in any subject area. Over 2 or more years, teachers receive approximately 140 hours of sequenced professional development, up to 10 in-classroom coaching sessions of 1–2 hours each, and access to an online portal with assessments, lesson plans, and teacher courses. Cost is $10,000 per teacher per year plus roughly $500 per student for devices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each student must have individual access to an Internet-connected computer or tablet
- Schools or districts supply space for in-person professional development sessions
#### Constraints
- The two reviewed studies each provided more than 200 hours of professional development, rather than the recommended 140 hours

### Target Learners
- Teachers of students in kindergarten through grade 12

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving teacher practice through technology-supported inquiry-based instruction
- Improving student achievement in mathematics and literacy

## Claims

- [Brandt et al. (2013) finds a statistically significant positive effect of eMINTS on seventh- and eighth-grade mathematics achievement](../claims/brandt-2013-significant-math-effect.md) [+M]
- [Both included studies report high-fidelity implementation of eMINTS](../claims/emints-high-fidelity-implementation.md) [+W]
- [eMINTS shows no discernible effects on general literacy achievement](../claims/emints-no-discernible-literacy-effects.md) [-W]
- [eMINTS has potentially positive effects on general mathematics achievement (improvement index +2 across two studies)](../claims/emints-potentially-positive-math-achievement.md) [+W]
- [Martin et al. (2009) finds indeterminate effects of eMINTS on both mathematics and literacy achievement in grades 4–5](../claims/martin-2009-indeterminate-effects.md) [~W]
