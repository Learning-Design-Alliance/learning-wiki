# Targeted Skills Instruction (TSI): live small-group virtual tutoring for K-12 reading and math

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Targeted Skills Instruction is "a live, small-group virtual tutoring program for K-12 reading and math, delivered in 30–45-minute sessions three or more times per week." In the studied implementation, students were assigned based on fall Exact Path Insights diagnostic scores at or below the 25th percentile and served in groups of 2-6 students in three 30-40 minute sessions per week across three 6-week cycles. Sessions follow a gradual release model with tutor support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by experienced, qualified K-12 educators with formal degrees in education
- Operates on commonly used district devices and browsers with no additional technology applications or devices required
#### Constraints
- In the studied implementation, students were assigned based on fall Exact Path Insights diagnostic scores at or below the 25th percentile

### Target Learners
- K-12 students reading below the 25th percentile
- economically disadvantaged students
- multilingual learners
- students with disabilities

### Target Learning Goals
- K-12 reading and math skills
- ELA achievement on state assessment
