# Estimated cost of Twelve Together implementation

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Independent researchers estimated the cost of Twelve Together in Chula Vista, California, at "$307 a student per month of program participation" (in 2006 dollars). The figure includes an estimate of the value of volunteer facilitator time, since the program relied heavily on volunteers to lead weekly discussion sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reliance on volunteer facilitators, whose time is costed into the estimate
#### Constraints
- The cost estimate applies to the Chula Vista, California implementation and is converted to 2006 dollars

### Target Learners
- middle school students in the Chula Vista implementation

### Target Learning Goals
- dropout prevention
