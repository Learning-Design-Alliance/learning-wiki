# Technical appendix documenting sample and methods for the 2021-22 COVID learning-recovery study

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A technical appendix published by NWEA Research in December 2021, authored by Megan Kuhfeld and Karyn Lewis, accompanying the brief on achievement and growth at the start of the 2021-22 school year. Its stated purpose is to "share more detailed results and to describe more fully the sample and methods used in the research" in the companion brief. It frames the work around two research questions on pandemic-era achievement and growth.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Serves as a companion document to the research brief Learning during COVID-19: An update on student achievement and growth at the start of the 2021-22 school year
#### Constraints
- The supplied text is a webpage description; it does not print the appendix's detailed results, sample sizes, or methods themselves

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in US schools assessed with MAP Growth

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematics achievement
- Reading achievement
- Academic growth relative to pre-pandemic norms
