# LPS website: a free, open-source digital resource translating Learner Factors research into design and curriculum strategies

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Learner Positioning Systems (LPS) website is a free, open-source digital resource presenting the research behind the Learner Factors that most impact learner variability, culminating two years of systematic literature review and framework development. It contains a description of each Factor and its influence on reading, shows which Factors interact with others, and links Factors to research-based instructional strategies that also provide guidance for product developers. The paper directs readers to explore Factors and strategies at lps.digitalpromiseglobal.org.
