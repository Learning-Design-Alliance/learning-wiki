# Mathematica random assignment study of alternative routes to teacher certification

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
A large-scale random assignment evaluation of alternative routes to teacher certification conducted by Mathematica for the U.S. Department of Education. It "tracked 2,600 students in 63 schools in 20 medium and large school districts in 7 states during the 2004-2005 and 2005-2006 school years" and "collected detailed data on coursework requirements and content from 80 teacher training programs." It focused on less selective alternative certification programs producing large numbers of teachers across rural to urban districts.
