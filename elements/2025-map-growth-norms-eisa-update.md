# 2025 MAP Growth norms update with Enhanced Item Selection Algorithm (EISA) alignment

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The 2025 MAP Growth norms update recalibrates the national reference group for RIT scores, achievement percentiles, conditional growth percentiles, and projected proficiency. It was prompted by post-COVID achievement and growth patterns falling "substantially below prepandemic trends" and incorporates technical enhancements, most notably that "the update aligns norms to the EISA, which strengthens alignment of MAP Growth to grade level content." NWEA updates its norms every three to five years; supporting documentation includes the 2025 Norms Technical Manual and Norms Toolkit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users interpreting norm-referenced metrics must account for the recalibrated national reference group when comparing results across norm sets or applying fixed thresholds.
#### Constraints
- Projected proficiency in math is affected in mixed directions: higher growth expectations can raise projections while EISA-related higher proficiency thresholds can make proficiency harder to reach.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students assessed with MAP Growth

### Target Learning Goals
- Norm-referenced measurement of achievement and growth for accountability, screening, and evaluation
