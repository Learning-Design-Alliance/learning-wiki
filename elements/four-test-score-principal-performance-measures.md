# Four principal performance measures based on student test scores

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study assessed four principal performance measures based on student test scores: average achievement, adjusted average achievement, school value-added, and adjusted school value-added. The first two "do not account for students' past achievement," while the latter two do. The brief evaluates how accurately each reflects principals' contributions to student achievement in future years using Pennsylvania statewide data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires statewide data linking students and principals over multiple years, as in Pennsylvania's 2007/08 to 2013/14 data
#### Constraints
- Measures not accounting for students' past achievement provide no predictive information for future principal contributions

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in Pennsylvania public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Student achievement as measured by test scores
