# Measurement and Evaluation Plan Template as a roadmap for developing an M&E plan with a research partner

## Description
The Measurement and Evaluation (M&E) Plan Template is described as "a road map organizations and funders can use to develop a detailed measurement and evaluation plan with support from a research partner." The research partner may be internal evaluation staff, an external organization, a technical assistance provider, or a consultant. The template structures planning for evaluating solutions, including education solutions such as middle years math teaching and learning.
