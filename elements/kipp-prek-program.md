# KIPP Pre-K program

## Description
KIPP Pre-K is a pre-kindergarten program offered by the KIPP charter school network, evaluated as an offer of admission in a study conducted as part of the associated project "KIPP: Preparing Youth for College" over the time frame 2007-2017. The study examined whether "any impacts persist as students advance beyond kindergarten," comparing students who won an offer of admission to KIPP Pre-K with peers who did not. The program is studied in combination with KIPP early elementary schooling.
