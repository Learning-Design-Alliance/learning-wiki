# Educator micro-credentials: five-step competency-based professional learning credential

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Digital Promise defines educator micro-credentials as a professional learning credential in which educators select a skill, develop evidence, submit it, and are evaluated by experts. 'There are five steps to earning a micro-credential,' beginning when 'Educators select the micro-credential they want to earn based on their students' needs and their own interests.' If evidence demonstrates competence, the educator earns the credential and may share a digital badge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must develop required evidence, such as classroom videos or student work, that experts evaluate against an associated rubric
#### Constraints
- Awards depend on evaluation against the associated rubric; the badge is shared at the educator's choice

### Target Learners
- classroom teachers, librarians, principals, and instructional coaches

### Target Learning Goals
- demonstrating competence in specific classroom practices
