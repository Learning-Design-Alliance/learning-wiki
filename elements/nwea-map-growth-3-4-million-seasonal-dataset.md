# NWEA MAP Growth assessment dataset (3.4 million students) as the evidence base for seasonal learning analysis

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's evidence base is score data from "the 3.4 million students who took the NWEA MAP Growth assessments," used to estimate school-year gains and summer loss across a very large student population. The scale of the dataset is what allows the article to identify that the students with the greatest summer loss were those who made the greatest prior school-year gains. The dataset is described only in the abstract of the available text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Seasonal (spring-to-fall) administration of MAP Growth assessments to the same students across school-year and summer periods.
#### Constraints
- The full article text was published outside NWEA; only the abstract describing the dataset is available here.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students taking NWEA MAP Growth assessments

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring seasonal patterns in academic achievement growth in reading and mathematics
