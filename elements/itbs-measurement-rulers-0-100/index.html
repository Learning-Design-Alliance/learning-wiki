Measurement Rulers linking ITBS scores on a new 0-100 scale to the difficulty of test content¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The guide encloses Measurement Rulers for reading and math that show how to relate specific test results to the content of the ITBS. Productivity profiles use a new vertical scale from 0 to 100 created for this study; the rulers let users "roughly translate a score into content material by looking at these measurement rulers." The right-hand side of the profile marks national norms for fourth, sixth, and eighth grades.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Users must consult the attached Measurement Rulers to interpret the 0-100 profile scale

Constraints¶

  • The scale translation is approximate: users can roughly translate a score into content material

Target Learners¶

  • Chicago public elementary school staff reading their school's productivity profiles

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Interpreting school reading and math test results in terms of the content students mastered

Claims¶

  • Less than half of Chicago elementary schools keep pace with ITBS grade-level content expectations during the last three years of elementary schooling [+W]
