# Digital Village Square: student-designed app for sharing writing ideas and feedback

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Digital Village Square is a concept for an app designed by Sunnyside student interns in which "students can share ideas on writing topics, get feedback on their writing, interact through comments and threads, and participate in polls and surveys, including those that elicit high interest topics for writing." It was one of three solutions the student interns developed to address adolescent writing engagement, alongside Compact Writing Assignments and the Student Curriculum Club.
