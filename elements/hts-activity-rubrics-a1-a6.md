# Historical Thinking Skills Activity Rubrics (A1-A6) for assessing learning opportunities in world history assignments

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of six rubric dimensions (A1-A6) meant to study the extent to which high school world history classroom activities provide opportunities for students to engage in historical thinking practices. Per the article, "EacK GLPHQVLrq KaV IRXU OHYHOV (0-3)", where 0 generally indicates absence of a historical thinking practice in the activity, 1 emergence, 2 partial presence, and 3 solid presence. The dimensions cover historical argumentation claims and evidence, causation, comparison, contextualization, continuity and change over time, and sourcing. The rubric was piloted with three expert world history teachers scoring 19 assignments.
