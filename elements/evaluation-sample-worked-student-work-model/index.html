Evaluation Sample: a worked model applying the Checklist to real student work¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Evaluation Sample is a worked example showing teachers how to apply the Checklist to an actual student product — a live presentation about the Bancroft-Arnesen Antarctic expedition. As the document explains, "the Evaluation Sample gives teachers a model of how to apply the Checklist to a piece of student work," pairing each student slide with a marked checklist and teacher comments outlining what the student mastered and where improvement is needed.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires the accompanying Checklists, since the sample consists of checklist items marked against a student's slides

Constraints¶

  • The sample evaluates one student's live presentation; the original was in color, and comments on color refer to the original work

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students producing live presentations

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Digital skills, media and meaning, point of view, and audience awareness in presentation work
