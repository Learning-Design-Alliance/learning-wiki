Reading Mastery direct instruction reading program (Reading Mastery Classic K-3 and Reading Mastery Plus K-6)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Reading Mastery is a direct instruction program providing 'explicit, systematic instruction in English language reading,' available as Reading Mastery Classic (levels I and II, grades K-3) and Reading Mastery Plus, an integrated reading-language program for grades K-6. It begins with phonemic awareness and sound-letter correspondence and moves into word and passage reading, vocabulary development, comprehension, and oral reading fluency. Developed by Dr. Siegfried Engelmann as Distar Reading for Project Follow Through, it is distributed by SRA/McGraw-Hill and used with more than 1 million students in one-third of the nation's schools.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students are grouped by similar reading level, based on program placement tests, and the program includes placement assessments and a continuous monitoring system.

Constraints¶

  • Although not designed exclusively for English language learners, Reading Mastery can be used with this group of students.

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades K-6 learning to read in English, including English language learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Phonemic awareness
  • Decoding and word recognition
  • Vocabulary development
  • Reading comprehension
  • Oral reading fluency

Claims¶

  • The WWC-computed average effect size for Reading Mastery on ELL reading achievement is 0.76, corresponding to an improvement index of +28 percentile points [+M]
  • One year after the intervention, Reading Mastery showed a statistically significant effect on word attack for English language learners, with positive but non-significant differences on other reading measures [+M]
  • Supplemental Reading Mastery instruction showed substantively important immediate effects on four of five reading measures for English language learners, none statistically significant [+M]
  • The WWC rates Reading Mastery as having potentially positive effects on the reading achievement of English language learners [+M]

Related Elements¶

  • Enhanced Proactive Reading curriculum: 120 daily small-group lessons across five content strands
  • Waterford Early Reading Program™: a software-based K–2 reading curriculum with three year-long levels and 15-minute individual computer sessions
  • Spelling Mastery Direct Instruction spelling curriculum with phonemic, morphemic, and whole-word strategies
  • Reading Mastery: a Direct Instruction reading curriculum for grades K–6 in three versions

Examples¶

  • Deliver supplemental reading instruction in small pull-out groups of two to three students with extra time for English vocabulary explanation
