# Lexia Reading: computerized supplementary phonics practice program with adaptive independent practice and web-based progress reporting

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Lexia Reading is a computerized reading program that "provides phonics instruction and gives students independent practice in basic reading skills," designed to supplement regular classroom instruction across the five National Reading Panel areas. It combines Early Reading, Primary Reading, and Strategies for Older Students under a single management system with Internet-based reporting and data hosting. Students work independently while the software tracks responses and automatically assigns additional practice when needed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular use of two to five weekly sessions of 20 to 30 minutes each in a lab or classroom setting, with usage frequency differentiated by student risk level
#### Constraints
- The WWC considers the extent of evidence for Lexia Reading to be small for alphabetics, fluency, comprehension, and general reading achievement

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten and first-grade students, including at-risk, ESL/ELL, Title I, and special education readers

### Target Learning Goals
- Phonics and basic reading skills across alphabetics, fluency, comprehension, and general reading achievement
