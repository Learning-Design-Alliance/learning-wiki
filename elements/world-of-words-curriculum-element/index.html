World of Words supplementary prekindergarten curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 7 studies (6 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 7 of 7 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

World of Words is a supplementary curriculum for prekindergarten children that, per the report, "aims to accelerate the development of children's vocabulary knowledge, concept knowledge, and content knowledge in science through topic-centered conversations and shared book readings." It consists of three recurring session components: "Talk together" (initial conversation building background knowledge with video clips, picture cards, and explicit definitions), "Read together" (shared book reading of predictable, narrative, and informational texts), and "Reflect together" (open-ended questions connecting learning across topics). Teachers implement 12- to 15-minute whole-group circle-time sessions over 12 to 24 weeks, covering 4 to 8 science topics with sessions 3-5 days a week across 2-3 weeks per topic.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers received initial training, ongoing coaching, and sets of materials provided by the developer
  • The developer provides six sets of materials, each including five texts, a scripted teacher's guide, 15 picture cards, and a topic poster

Constraints¶

  • The report provides evidence of effectiveness only in prekindergarten, though the program is designed for prekindergarten through grade 2
  • Effects on outcomes other than language are unknown

Target Learners¶

  • prekindergarten children in Head Start and state-funded preschools, predominantly from low-income families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • vocabulary knowledge
  • concept knowledge
  • science content knowledge

Claims¶

  • World of Words produced a statistically significant medium effect on a comprehension composite measure (effect size 0.54) after 12 weeks [+M]
  • World of Words produced a statistically significant medium effect on expressive vocabulary (Expressive One-Word Picture Vocabulary Test, 4th Edition; effect size 0.43) in a scaled-up 12-site study [+M]
  • World of Words produced a statistically significant medium-to-large effect on a researcher-developed vocabulary inductive reasoning test (effect size 0.66) [+M]
  • World of Words showed no statistically significant effects on the Woodcock-Johnson Picture Vocabulary Subtest or the PPVT-IV [-W]
  • World of Words has positive effects on the language outcome domain for prekindergarten children, with an average effect size of 0.43 across four studies [+M]
  • World of Words produced a large, statistically significant effect on taught-word sorting performance (effect size 0.94) in a two-site Head Start study [+M]

Examples¶

  • Implement World of Words with developer training, ongoing coaching, and structured text-set materials
