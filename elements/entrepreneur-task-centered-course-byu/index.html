Entrepreneur course: a task-centered online course teaching business startup to non-business majors¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Developed by Brigham Young University's Center for Instructional Technology and Outreach, the course teaches "the fundamental steps for starting a business to non-business majors in developing countries." "The whole task involves six steps: (1) Identify a business opportunity, (2) Define the idea, (3) Identify resources, (4) Acquire resources, (5) Start your business, and (6) Manage your business." It is organized around scenarios for five real-world businesses of increasing complexity, with information in a left window and portrayals in a right window, and learners apply properties to new businesses with audio feedback.
