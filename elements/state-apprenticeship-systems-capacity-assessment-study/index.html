State Apprenticeship Systems Capacity Assessment study¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A multi-publication evidence-building study examining how the public sector and its partners support and implement Registered Apprenticeship programs across US states. The article states the study "will conduct a deep dive into state-level Registered Apprenticeship systems in the United States". This report is the first in the series and is used to select states for subsequent data collection.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires scanning of available apprenticeship data, literature, and recent studies before primary data collection

Constraints¶

  • The report covers United States state-level Registered Apprenticeship systems only

Target Learners¶

  • apprentices in Registered Apprenticeship programs
  • pre-apprentices

Target Learning Goals¶

  • workforce preparation through Registered Apprenticeship

Claims¶

  • The study treats data infrastructure and performance measurement as a dimension of state apprenticeship expansion and success [+W]
  • The report provides an overview of federal support for state apprenticeship systems and variation across states in key characteristics [+W]
  • The study examines differences between State Apprenticeship Agency states and DOL Office of Apprenticeship states in registration of apprenticeship and pre-apprenticeship programs [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Two-stage evidence-building: document scan followed by targeted state interviews
