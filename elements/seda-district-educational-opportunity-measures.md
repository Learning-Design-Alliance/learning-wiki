# SEDA measures of district educational opportunity

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (4 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
SEDA is described by the brief as a data resource providing "a unique measure of educational opportunity across the United States". It produces both achievement scores and growth estimates at the district level, and this brief reports validation of those measures against a common assessment. The validation research supports the achievement scores while revealing differences in the growth estimates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validation against a common assessment is used to check the measures, per the brief's title
#### Constraints
- The brief notes differences in growth estimates, indicating the growth measures are less settled than the achievement scores

### Target Learners
