# Improving Chicago's Schools biannual survey of students, teachers, and principals

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Improving Chicago's Schools survey is a biannual Consortium on Chicago School Research data collection in which students report on school experiences, attitudes, and activities, while teachers and principals report on instruction, professional development, and working conditions. In 2005 'more than 132,000 students, teachers, and principals across the Chicago public school system participated,' and the Consortium produced individualized confidential reports for schools meeting response-rate criteria, which show change over the ten years of survey administration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least 42 percent of teachers or 50 percent of students must respond in order for a school to receive a report
#### Constraints
- Split survey versions mean roughly half of students have scores on math-class items and half on English-class items for a subset of measures

### Target Learners
- Chicago Public Schools students in grades 6-8 at the reported school

### Target Learning Goals
- learning climate, quality of instructional program, student-teacher relationships, and school leadership
