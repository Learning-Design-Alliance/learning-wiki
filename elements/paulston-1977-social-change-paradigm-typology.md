# 1977 fixed typology of social change paradigms and educational reform assumptions

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Figure 2 reproduces the author's 1977 Comparative Education Review typology, which the paper describes as "A fixed typology presentinga phenomological comparison of how the international literature may be seen to construct national educational and social change/.reform." It arrays paradigms — evolutionary, neo-evolutionary, structural-equilibrium, systems, Marxian, neo-Marxian conflict, cultural revitalization, anarchistic utopian — against linked assumptions about preconditions, rationales, scope and process, and outcomes of educational change. The paper reports it portrayed "how some 320 international texts constructed multiple educational reform realities."
