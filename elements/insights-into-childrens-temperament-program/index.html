INSIGHTS into Children's Temperament program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

INSIGHTS is "an evidence-based intervention that helps families, educators and young children identify how young children's personalities and temperaments affect their behavior." It has three components: a six-lesson self-paced professional development program for educators with remote facilitated sessions, ten weekly 30-minute classroom sessions for children using mitten puppets, videos and activities, and six self-paced one-hour lessons for parents in facilitated groups of eight. No additional staffing is required.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators and parents need a remote device to access lesson modules, videos, interactive activities and downloadable content

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • kindergarten and first-grade students
  • teachers, counselors, psychologists and administrators
  • parents of young children

Target Learning Goals¶

  • social-emotional development
  • self-regulation
  • academic learning in reading and math

Claims¶

  • INSIGHTS reduced behavior problems relative to control (effect size +0.54) [+M]
  • INSIGHTS students grew significantly faster than control students in math achievement (effect size +0.31) [+W]
  • INSIGHTS students grew significantly faster than attention-control students in reading achievement (effect size +0.55) [+M]
  • INSIGHTS improved sustained attention relative to control (effect size +0.39) [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Use temperament-based classroom sessions with puppets, videos and activities to build empathy and dilemma resolution
