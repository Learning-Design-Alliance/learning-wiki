Open Court Reading© K–6 comprehensive reading curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Open Court Reading© is a reading program for grades K–6 published by McGraw-Hill Education, designed to teach decoding, comprehension, inquiry, and writing. The article describes it as organized "in a three-part logical progression", with grade-specific materials including decodable texts, student anthologies, workbooks, and a Teacher's Edition providing "systematic, explicit skills instruction centered on literature selections". In 2007 it was revised and renamed Imagine It!©.

Claims¶

  • Open Court Reading© has potentially positive effects on comprehension for beginning readers in grades 1–3, with a small extent of evidence [+W]
  • Open Court Reading© has potentially positive effects on general reading achievement for beginning readers, with a small extent of evidence [+W]
  • The evidence base for Open Court Reading© is thin: of 185 identified studies, only two met WWC group design standards, and none met standards for alphabetics or reading fluency [~W]
