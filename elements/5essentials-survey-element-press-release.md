# 5Essentials Survey (My Voice, My School survey)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The 5Essentials Survey, administered in Chicago Public Schools since the 1990s, "measures a school’s strength in five essential organizational conditions that influence student learning: Effective Leaders, Collaborative Teachers, Involved Families, Supportive Environment, and Ambitious Instruction." The two studies examined its predictiveness of school improvement and how schools use its results. It is also included in CPS's SQRP accountability system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District administration of the survey to students and teachers to generate school-level measures
#### Constraints
- Its inclusion in the SQRP accountability system created incentives and pressures that some principals and teachers reported affected priorities and responses

### Target Learners
- Chicago Public Schools students and teachers whose responses generate school-level measures

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring and improving school climate to support student outcomes
