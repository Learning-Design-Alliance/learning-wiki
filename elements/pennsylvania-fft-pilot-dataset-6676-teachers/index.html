Pennsylvania teacher evaluation pilot dataset covering 6,676 teachers in 269 districts across two school years¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q1–q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report analyzed pilot data on two teacher performance measures: the observation-based FFT and value-added measures based on student test scores. The data cover "6,676 teachers from 269 districts in the state of Pennsylvania, including Pittsburgh public schools", spanning the 2012-2013 school year with comparison data from 2011-2012 from a previous pilot phase.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Observation-based FFT component ratings and merged value-added measure scores for the same teachers

Constraints¶

  • Coverage limited to participating Pennsylvania pilot districts; Pittsburgh contributed more teachers than any other district

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 teachers in Pennsylvania pilot districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Evaluating the measurement properties of observation-based teacher effectiveness ratings

Claims¶

  • Rating distributions and FFT-VAM relationships were broadly similar across the two pilot phases, though top-two-category ratings decreased somewhat in Pittsburgh [+W]
  • FFT scores showed acceptable-to-good internal consistency, with the overall PPR score more internally consistent than any single domain score [+W]
  • FFT-based teacher ratings in Pennsylvania's pilot were overwhelmingly concentrated in the top two performance categories, with less than 0.1 percent rated failing [+W]
  • FFT scores correlated positively with value-added measures, with domain correlations ranging from 0.19 to 0.22 and generally statistically significant [+W]
  • Mathematica developed and estimated VAMs for use in Phase 1 of the Pennsylvania evaluation pilot [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Framework for Teaching (FFT) observation instrument with 22 components scored into four domains and an overall Professional Practice Rating
