# Magma Math digital math platform with process-showing canvas, heatmaps, and data dashboards

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Magma Math is a digital math platform for grades 4–8 supporting student-centered mathematical learning and discourse. Students "solve problems on a digital canvas using keyboard or stylus input, showing their full problem-solving process rather than just a final answer." Teachers assign problems, view work in real time through heatmaps and data dashboards, and use student solutions to drive classroom discussion and differentiated instruction. It runs in any modern web browser and as an iPad app, with no downloads or plug-ins required.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet-connected devices; accessible through any modern web browser with no required downloads or plug-ins, or the dedicated iPad app
#### Constraints
- Evaluated so far in one quasi-experimental study in a single public Ohio district, grades 4–8

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 4–8, including English learners and students with Individualized Education Plans

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematics problem-solving and mathematical discourse

## Claims

- [In grades 4–5, students who used Magma Math scored significantly higher on spring i-Ready Mathematics than matched non-users (effect size +0.12)](../claims/magma-math-grades-4-5-significant-i-ready-gain.md) [+W]
- [In grades 6–8, Magma Math users outscored matched non-users but the difference was not statistically significant (effect size +0.07)](../claims/magma-math-grades-6-8-nonsignificant-advantage.md) [+W]
- [Across a matched quasi-experimental evaluation of 1,686 students in grades 4–8, Magma Math showed an average effect size of +0.09, earning a Promising evidence rating](../claims/magma-math-overall-promising-rating-es-009.md) [+W]
