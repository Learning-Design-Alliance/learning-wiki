NWEA research brief 'The COVID-19 slide' with associated working paper, presentation, and technical appendix¶

Description¶

This element is the NWEA Research brief of April 2020 by Megan Kuhfeld and Beth Tarasawa, which connects summer learning loss research to the potential achievement impacts of COVID-19 school closures. The page lists associated research artifacts: a working paper titled "Projecting the potential impacts of COVID-19 school closures on academic achievement," a presentation on COVID-19 closures and learning loss, and a technical appendix supporting the brief. Together these form a resource set for understanding and projecting closure-related learning loss.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The brief is intended to be read alongside its associated working paper and technical appendix for the underlying projections

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students affected by COVID-19 school closures

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding and projecting academic achievement impacts of extended school closures

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Use summer learning loss research to help educators, policy makers, and families plan for and address impacts of extended school closures
  • Convene educators, policymakers, and families to plan for the impacts of extended pauses in classroom instruction
