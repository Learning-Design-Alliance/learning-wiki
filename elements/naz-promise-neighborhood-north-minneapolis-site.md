# Northside Achievement Zone (NAZ) Promise Neighborhood site

## Description
The Northside Achievement Zone is a Promise Neighborhood site serving "a contiguous 13-by-18-block area in North Minneapolis". The profile documents it as a place-based initiative covering early childhood through education and family support, profiled as part of the Promise Neighborhoods Case Studies project for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink.
