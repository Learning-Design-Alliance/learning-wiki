# Wilson Reading System: supplemental word-level decoding and spelling curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Wilson Reading System® is "a supplemental reading and writing curriculum designed to promote reading accuracy (decoding) and spelling (encoding) skills for students with word-level deficits." It teaches phonemic awareness, alphabetic principles, word study, spelling, sight words, fluency, vocabulary, oral language, and comprehension, using activities such as hearing sounds, practicing with syllable and word cards, and reading aloud. Two vocabulary levels (A for younger or ESL students, B for older students) support use in grade 2 through adulthood.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training through a formal professional development process, including an instructors' manual, workshops, videos, online education, and ongoing support via the Wilson Academy
#### Constraints
- Designed for students in grade 2 and above; the related Fundations® program serves Kindergarten through third grade and was not reviewed

### Target Learners
- students in grade 2 and above with word-level reading deficits
- ESL students (Level A vocabulary)
- adult learners

### Target Learning Goals
- reading accuracy (decoding)
- spelling (encoding)
- phonemic awareness
- word study and fluency
