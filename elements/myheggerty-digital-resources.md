# myHeggerty optional digital resources for planning, professional learning, and classroom instruction

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
myHeggerty is the program's optional digital platform. The curriculum does not require classroom technology, but "the program does include digital resources via myHeggerty that teachers can use to support their planning and professional learning and enhance classroom instruction." Use of these materials may involve laptops, iPads, or interactive whiteboards or projectors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers may choose hardware such as a laptop, desktop computer, iPad, or interactive whiteboard or projector to use the optional digital materials
#### Constraints
- Technology is not required to implement the curriculum in the classroom

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten students
- First-grade students
- Second-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational reading skills including phonics and phonemic awareness
