# Financial incentives for teen parents (LEAP and Cal-Learn welfare programs)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Financial incentives for teen parents are components of state welfare programs in which "the incentives take the form of bonuses and sanctions to the welfare grant related to school enrollment, performance, and completion." Ohio's LEAP program (begun 1989) provides $62 monthly attendance bonuses and sanctions and a $200 completion bonus; California's Cal-Learn (begun 1994) adjusts family support by $50 or $100 based on report cards and pays a $500 completion award. Eight states operate such programs.
