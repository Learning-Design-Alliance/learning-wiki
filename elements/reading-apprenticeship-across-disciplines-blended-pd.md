# Reading Apprenticeship Across the Disciplines blended professional development program

## Description
A WestEd professional development program that "blends online and in-person professional development to support teachers in a variety of subject areas as they work to build students' literacy skills." The brief presents implementation insights from this program as a worked example of integrating online professional learning communities with face-to-face sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of online and in-person components so the two formats reinforce one another
#### Constraints
- The brief notes that little guidance exists on how to integrate online and in-person professional development

### Target Learners
- Teachers across a variety of subject areas

### Target Learning Goals
- Building students' literacy skills through teacher professional learning
