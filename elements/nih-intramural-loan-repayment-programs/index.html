NIH Intramural Loan Repayment Programs¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The NIH Intramural Loan Repayment Programs are an incentive mechanism by which, for over a decade, the National Institutes of Health has been "using repayment of education loans as an incentive to attract and retain first-rate biomedical scientists in its labs and clinics." The evaluation assesses the programs' role in recruitment and retention outcomes using survey data on participants and nonparticipants.

Context¶

Target Learners¶

  • biomedical scientists recruited to NIH labs and clinics

Target Learning Goals¶

  • recruitment into biomedical research careers
  • retention in research careers

Claims¶

  • Participation in NIH loan repayment programs is associated with longer stays at the NIH and in research careers [+W]
  • Eligibility for NIH loan repayment is associated with higher recruitment success of biomedical researchers [+W]
  • The NIH Intramural Research Loan Repayment Program evaluation combined an outcomes evaluation with a process analysis drawing on focus groups, staff interviews, and administrative data [+W]
