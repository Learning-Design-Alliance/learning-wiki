Bottom Line college advising program (Access and Success components)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Bottom Line is an intensive advising program for low-income students, most of whom are the first in their family to go to college. It includes Bottom Line Access for high school juniors and seniors, using the "LEAD Model (Lists, Essays, Applications, Decisions)" to guide application, financial aid, and college selection, and Bottom Line Success, which provides campus-based advising at target colleges for up to six years using the DEAL model covering Degree, Employability, Affordability, and Life supports.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students meet individually with advisors for about an hour every three to four weeks during the application process; college students meet three to four times per semester, with additional contact by texting, email, and video or phone calls.

Constraints¶

  • Approximately 50 percent of students choose to enroll in a target institution and continue into Bottom Line Success; effects are not known for outcomes outside college enrollment and progression.

Target Learners¶

  • Low-income high school students, most of whom are first-generation college students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • College enrollment and persistence toward a degree

Claims¶

  • Comparison-group students in the Bottom Line study accessed existing advising and nearly all applied to college and for financial aid [~M]
  • The effects of Bottom Line are not known for outcome domains other than college enrollment and progressing in college [~W]
  • The supporting evidence for Bottom Line comes from a randomized controlled trial that meets WWC group design standards without reservations [+M]
  • Bottom Line increases enrollment in a four-year college among low-income high school students [+W]
  • Bottom Line increases progression in college (continuous enrollment for three semesters after high school) [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Bottom Line cost profile of approximately $6,000 per student over five to six years
  • Talent Search program: federal TRIO services supporting high-school completion and college access for low-income, first-generation students

Examples¶

  • Use data on students' college application and enrollment choices to support college-going decisions
