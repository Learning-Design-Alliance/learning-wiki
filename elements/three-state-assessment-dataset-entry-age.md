# Multi-state longitudinal assessment dataset for early-grade growth analysis

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The study draws on what the authors call "rich assessment data from three states," covering students' achievement across the first three years of school. This longitudinal, multi-state data source enables the piecewise growth modeling of entry-age effects on both achievement levels and growth rates, and supports moderator analyses by gender and race. The available text does not print sample sizes, assessment names, or the states' identities.
