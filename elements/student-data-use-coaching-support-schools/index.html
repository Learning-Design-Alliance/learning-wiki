Coaching and support to schools for using student data to inform teachers' instruction¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

This element is the intervention evaluated in the study: providing coaching and support to schools to help them use student data to inform teachers' instruction. The executive summary frames it as the treatment whose effects on 'teachers’ practices and student achievement' were tested through random assignment. The associated project ran from 2012 to 2019 and was prepared for the U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences. The supplied text does not describe the coaching model's components, dosage, or delivery in detail.
