# MORE tasks for individual out-of-class work

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
In addition to classroom investigations, Core-Plus Mathematics provides sets of MORE tasks, which are designed to engage students in "Modeling with, Organizing, Reflecting on, and Extending their mathematical understanding" during individual work outside of class. The tasks extend the lesson cycle beyond the classroom, complementing the collaborative in-class investigations with independent practice that reinforces and deepens lesson objectives.
