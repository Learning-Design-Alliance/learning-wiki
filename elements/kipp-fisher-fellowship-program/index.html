KIPP Fisher Fellowship program preparing new principals to lead new KIPP schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

One of three KIPP school leadership programs examined in the evaluation, described as "the Fisher Fellowship program, which prepares new principals to lead new KIPP schools". The evaluation also examined the reliability of the instrument KIPP uses to select participants for this fellowship program.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Uses a selection instrument to identify candidates, whose reliability the evaluation examined

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • aspiring KIPP school principals

Target Learning Goals¶

  • school leadership effectiveness

Claims¶

  • The Fisher Fellowship selection instrument is reliable, though three of 34 items had relatively low levels of one type of reliability [~W]
  • Former participants desired more follow-up with their cohort and more support developing talent, managing resources, and cultivating organizational culture [~W]
  • Former KIPP school leadership program participants reported satisfaction and application of program lessons in their own settings [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • KIPP Leadership Design Fellowship (KLDF) program disseminating school leadership development strategies outside KIPP
  • KIPP Successor Prep program preparing new principals to lead existing schools

Examples¶

  • Use evaluation findings to guide replication of effective principal preparation tools and strategies across charter, traditional public, and preparation-field settings
