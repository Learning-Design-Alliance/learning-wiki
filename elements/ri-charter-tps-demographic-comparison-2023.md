# Rhode Island charter, feeder TPS, and statewide TPS demographic comparison dataset

## Description
The report tabulates 286 statewide TPS, 86 feeder TPS, and 31 charter schools, with total enrollments of 132,589, 42,204, and 8,859 students respectively. Charters serve higher-poverty populations (71% vs 67% in feeders), more Hispanic students (55% vs 38%), and fewer special education students (14% vs 20%). This demography table establishes the comparison context for the impact analysis.
