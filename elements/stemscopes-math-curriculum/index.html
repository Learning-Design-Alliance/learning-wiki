STEMscopes Math comprehensive core mathematics curriculum (K–Algebra 1)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

STEMscopes Math is "a comprehensive, standards-aligned core mathematics curriculum for grades K through Algebra 1, developed by Accelerate Learning." It is built around the 5E+IA instructional model, supports whole-group instruction, small-group differentiation, and blended learning, follows a Concrete-Representational-Abstract progression, and integrates digital tools including virtual manipulatives and PhET simulations alongside print materials. All student resources are available in English and Spanish.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need computer and/or touch screen devices to access the digital curriculum resources; students have a digital platform accessible on a computer or touchscreen device.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades K through Algebra 1

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Standards-aligned mathematics achievement

Claims¶

  • A significantly higher percentage of STEMscopes Math students met or exceeded grade-level benchmarks (77% vs. 69%) [+W]
  • STEMscopes Math students scored significantly higher on STAAR Math than comparison students in a Texas quasi-experiment (effect size +0.19) [+W]
  • Positive STEMscopes Math effects were found for Economically Disadvantaged (+0.51), Hispanic (+0.35) and Black (+0.74) students [+W]
