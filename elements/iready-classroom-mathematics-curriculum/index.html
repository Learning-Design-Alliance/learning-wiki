i-Ready Classroom Mathematics (iRCL): a K-8 core mathematics curriculum combining print and digital materials¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

iRCL is described as "a core K–8 math curriculum designed to build conceptual understanding, problem-solving skills, and student engagement." It combines print and digital materials including teacher guides, student workbooks, practice tools, and hands-on manipulables, and integrates professional learning supports for teachers implementing the curriculum.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Implementations require points of contact to assist with initial set-up and disseminate communications to other staff members

Constraints¶

  • No additional staff are required; set-up support is provided by i-Ready Partners

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 students, including those who need additional scaffolding

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Conceptual understanding in mathematics
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Student engagement

Claims¶

  • iRCL effects were consistent across grades, especially pronounced in Grades 4 and 5, with significant benefits for Hispanic, Black, economically disadvantaged, English learner, and disability subgroups [+W]
  • iRCL students were significantly more likely to meet Typical Growth (+0.17) and Stretch Growth (+0.16) targets [+W]
  • Students in schools implementing i-Ready Classroom Mathematics outperformed matched comparison peers on the spring i-Ready Diagnostic for Mathematics (average ES +0.10) [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • i-Ready Connect digital platform for teacher-facing content, with browser, SSO, and third-party integration requirements
  • Curriculum Associates professional development for iRCL: on-site development days and the Online Educator Learning platform
  • i-Ready digital learning platform with adaptive diagnostic and personalized instruction
