# Reading Go! (formerly Reading Rescue) one-on-one early literacy tutoring program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Reading Go! (formerly Reading Rescue) prepares school staff to "accelerate literacy among low performing first and second graders" through professional training that includes "a clinical experience in reading." Trained tutors work with individual students one on one for 30 minutes per day, typically for one semester, delivering a balanced literacy block of fluency building, daily assessments, phonological awareness and word study, sentence writing, and vocabulary development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools nominate at least three and no more than eight staff members to participate in training, with one serving as the school's Reading Rescue Program Coordinator
- Partnerships last for a minimum of two years
#### Constraints
- Students typically receive tutoring for one semester

### Target Learners
- low-performing first and second graders
- language-minority, low-SES first graders

### Target Learning Goals
- word attack and word reading
- reading comprehension
- phonological awareness and word study
- vocabulary development
- sentence writing
- fluency
