## Description
A research-review infographic from Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic that "provides an overview of blended learning, its impact on student achievement, and best practices for successful implementation." It is intended for school districts considering blended learning and distills the review's key findings on heterogeneous effects and the lack of identified implementation strategies.
