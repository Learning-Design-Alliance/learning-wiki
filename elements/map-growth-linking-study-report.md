# MAP Growth linking study report with cut score tables, classification accuracy statistics, and proficiency projections

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A state-specific report NWEA produces for each linking study that "explains the methodology, summarizes the study sample, presents the results, and provides evidence that shows how well the RIT scores can predict state summative performance." Each report presents sample demographics, descriptive statistics, fall/winter/spring RIT cut scores corresponding to state performance levels, classification accuracy statistics, and probabilities of achieving proficiency based on RIT scores. Results are also embedded in the NWEA reporting system and individual score reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A completed linking study meeting the 1,000-student minimum per grade and subject
- State summative data shared by partner districts
#### Constraints
- Report cut scores use default instructional weeks (Weeks 4, 20, and 32) and may differ slightly from score reports reflecting district-specific instructional weeks

### Target Learners
- K-12 students taking MAP Growth in participating states

### Target Learning Goals
- Early identification of students at risk of not meeting state proficiency standards
