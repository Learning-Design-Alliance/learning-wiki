# Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP): a national network of public charter schools with extended day/year and a Commitment to Excellence

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
KIPP is "a nonprofit network of more than 200 public charter schools" serving prekindergarten through high school students, launched in 1994 in Houston. Schools operate autonomously under charter authorizers, use admissions lotteries when oversubscribed, have "an extended school day and an extended school year compared with traditional public schools", and require students, parents, and teachers to sign the Commitment to Excellence pledge covering attendance, homework, behavior, parental support, and teacher preparation. Principals can hire and fire staff and allocate resources.
