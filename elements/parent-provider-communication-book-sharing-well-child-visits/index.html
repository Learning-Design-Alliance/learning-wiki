Parent-provider communication and book sharing during well-child primary care visits to support early math development¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The brief describes a promising approach to support early childhood math development that synthesizes the influence of parents, the home environment, and children's health care providers. It works by "leverag[ing] communication between parents and health care providers" and "book sharing during well-child primary care visits", embedding early math support into routine primary care. The brief presents this as a promising approach rather than one with printed evaluation results.
