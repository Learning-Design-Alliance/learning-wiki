# Method for isolating principals' persistent effects on student achievement growth from school value added

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The article describes an analytic approach that isolates principals' effects on student achievement growth and examines how much of principals' persistently demonstrated effectiveness is captured by school value added. It is applied to "longitudinal data on the math and reading outcomes of fourth- through eighth-grade students in Pennsylvania," covering both math and reading as outcome domains. The approach's purpose is evaluative: to assess whether accountability systems based on school value added convey accurate information about principal quality.
