# Five Illinois case study districts implementing PERA teacher evaluation systems

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
A set of five case study districts located in north, central, and southern Illinois, representing a variety of evaluation approaches and each at different stages of planning and implementation of new teacher evaluation systems required by the Performance Evaluation Reform Act (PERA). The brief studied these districts through qualitative interviews with members of each district's evaluation committee to capture challenges, strategies, and lessons learned.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
