Companion Mathematica publications supporting M&E planning: Prior Research Table, Targets Guide, Sample Size Guide, and Reporting Template¶

Description¶

The page lists companion publications released alongside the template in February 2023: a Prior Research Table, a "Guide to Setting Targets for Research Questions", a "Sample Size Guide", and a Measurement and Evaluation Reporting Template. Together with the plan template, these form a toolkit covering prior evidence, target setting, sample sizing, and reporting for organizations developing M&E plans.
