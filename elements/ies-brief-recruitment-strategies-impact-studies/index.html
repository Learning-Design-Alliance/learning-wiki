IES brief of practical recruitment strategies for education impact studies¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This element is the 2023 Institute of Education Sciences brief 'Practical Strategies for Recruiting Districts and Schools for Education Impact Studies' by Dana Robinson, Linda Rosenberg, Alejandro Espinoza, and Eric Zeidman. It offers practical strategies to help researchers recruit districts and schools to participate in impact studies, addressing factors such as urgent priorities, limited staff capacity, fairness and collaboration, and privacy.

Examples¶

  • Account for limited staff capacity when recruiting districts and schools for research
  • Address districts' and schools' urgent priorities and needs when recruiting them for impact studies
  • Demonstrate commitment to fairness and collaborative practices when recruiting districts and schools
  • Protect student and staff privacy when recruiting districts and schools for impact studies
