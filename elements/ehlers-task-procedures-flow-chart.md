## Description
The report presents, in Figure III, a systems flow chart of task procedures for developing individualized instructional materials, drawn from Walter H. Ehlers' work on computer-assisted instruction in social work education (Educational Technology, Vol. 18, No. 9, Sept 1969). The chart runs from instructional problem identification through behavioral objectives, entry behaviors, learning materials preparation, field implementation, formative and summative evaluation, and performance assessments.
