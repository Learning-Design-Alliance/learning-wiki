# Empirical Bayes shrinkage as implemented in teacher value-added accountability systems

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Empirical Bayes shrinkage is described as "an approach commonly used in implementing teacher accountability systems" that adjusts value-added estimates. The paper studies how it affects estimates for teachers of students with hard-to-predict achievement levels, finding it increases precision and reduces absolute estimate values without significantly changing extreme-rating probabilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Value-added estimates for teachers, and an accountability system that uses those estimates, must be available for the shrinkage approach to be applied as studied.
#### Constraints
- The studied effects concern teachers of students with hard-to-predict achievement levels, such as students with low prior achievement who receive free lunch.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students, including students with low prior achievement and students receiving free lunch

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate and precise measurement of teacher effectiveness for accountability decisions
