SEERNet researcher support resources: platform research guide and proposal-writing resource¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As network hub, Digital Promise with Empirical Education developed two resources for researchers. The first, "Conduct Research on the Digital Learning Platforms," provided "a comprehensive description of each platform's capabilities throughout the research process." The second, "5 Ways SEERNet Can Help You Write a Strong Proposal," helps researchers ensure proposals are feasible and aligned to the IES SEER principles. SEERNet and DLPs also hosted office hours for proposers.
