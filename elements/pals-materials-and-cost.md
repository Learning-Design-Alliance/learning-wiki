# PALS commercially distributed materials and cost structure

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
PALS materials "range from $15 to $35" and are distributed by the Vanderbilt Kennedy Center for Research on Human Development. Large-print lessons ($15) are recommended for kindergarten; 1st-grade materials consist of scripted lessons, teacher-directed decodable worksheets, and decoding lesson worksheets for student pairs; materials for 2nd grade and above consist of a teacher's manual with scripted lessons, with students using classroom reading material to implement the program. Kindergarten and first-grade materials were developed by Patricia G. Mathes and others under the Peer Assisted Literacy Strategies name, distributed by Sopris West.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom reading materials (e.g., anthology from a core reading program, children's books) for the partner-reading portion
#### Constraints
- Separate packaging and distributor for kindergarten and first-grade materials under the Peer Assisted Literacy Strategies name

### Target Learners
- Students from kindergarten through 5th grade and beyond, including English language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading instruction via peer tutoring
