## Description
The Teacher Toolbox is the educator-facing digital access point for Magnetic Reading Foundations (K-2), delivered through the i-Ready Connect platform. It provides digital versions of program materials to support remote or blended learning, plus Interactive Tutorials to further engage students, and runs on Windows, MacOS, and Chromebook machines with JavaScript, cookies, and pop-ups enabled.
