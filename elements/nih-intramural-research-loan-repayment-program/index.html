NIH Intramural Research Loan Repayment Program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The NIH Intramural Research Loan Repayment Program is a program evaluated by Mathematica Policy Research in 2005. The report describes gathering information from "participant focus groups, program staff, other NIH staff, and administrative data" to understand how the program has been implemented, especially in recent years. The program operates within NIH's Human Services and Education-related work as framed by the publisher.
