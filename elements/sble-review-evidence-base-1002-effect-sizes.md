# Evidence base of 1002 effect sizes from 32 quasi-experimental studies of school-based law enforcement

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's synthesized corpus comprises "a final sample of 1002 effect sizes from 32 studies," identified through searches of 64 databases (through 2009), then 18 and 35 databases in 2015 and 2020 updates. Of these, 520 effect sizes examined crime/behavior outcomes at the school level, 349 at the student level, 13 and 20 examined perceptions, and 41 and 56 examined learning outcomes at school and student levels respectively. All effect sizes involved secondary schools at least in part, and all but three studies were based in the United States.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies had to use randomized or quasi-experimental designs comparing units with SBLE to units without SBLE or with different SBLE dosages
#### Constraints
- Only 42.6% of effect sizes came from published studies; over half of effect sizes included zero control variables, and no true experiments existed in the corpus

### Target Learners
- Primary and secondary school students, teachers/staff, schools, and districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Crime and behavior outcomes, perceptions of safety and school climate, and learning outcomes
