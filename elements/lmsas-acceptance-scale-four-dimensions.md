# LMSAS: Learning Management System Acceptance Scale with four UTAUT dimensions

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Learning Management System Acceptance Scale (LMSAS) by Sezer and Yilmaz (2019) was the data collection instrument. It "has four dimensions of performance expectancy, effort expectancy, facilitating conditions and social influence". Reliability was high: "The α coefficient for the whole scale is .926", with subscale coefficients of .934 (PE, 8 items), .785 (EE, 5 items), .785 (FC, 5 items) and .838 (SI, 3 items), all above the 0.70 threshold the authors cite for reliability.
