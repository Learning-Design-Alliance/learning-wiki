# IPSD Inclusive CT Pathway competency map of six computational thinking competencies

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The IPSD Inclusive CT Pathway centers on a competency map identifying six CT competencies: "Decomposition, Pattern Recognition, Abstraction, Algorithms, Working with Data, and Creating Computational Artifacts." The district curriculum team aligned these competencies to the K-12 CS Framework, drafted the map, gathered teacher feedback in a focus group, and piloted it during 2019-2020 with school-level, after-school, and summer professional development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A district leadership team to develop a shared understanding of CT and identify relevant competencies
#### Constraints
- Illinois had no CS or CT standards at the outset of the project, so the district prioritized defining CT itself

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in a suburban district, with an equity focus on five Title I elementary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Computational thinking competencies integrated across disciplines
