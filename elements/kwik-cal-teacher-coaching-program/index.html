KWiK: teacher coaching plus Computer Assisted Learning for mastery-based math practice¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The KWiK program encourages mastery learning through technology paired with proactive continuous teacher support. In elementary and middle school mathematics, "teachers receive weekly coaching in how to use Computer Assisted Learning (CAL) for helping students follow a customized roadmap of incremental progress." The program was implemented with hundreds of teachers across partner districts and evaluated in two field experiments.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must receive weekly coaching in how to use Computer Assisted Learning for mastery-based practice

Constraints¶

  • Gains concentrate in classrooms achieving at least an average of 35 minutes of practice per week

Target Learners¶

  • elementary and middle school mathematics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mathematics proficiency through mastery learning and incremental progress

Claims¶

  • Math gains from the CAL program occur in classrooms with at least an average of 35 minutes of practice per week [~W]
  • A teacher-coaching CAL program produces significant intent-to-treat gains in elementary and middle school math performance of 0.12-0.22 standard deviations [+W]
  • Teachers who achieve high-dosage CAL practice show strong initial buy-in, a clear implementation strategy for practice timing, and close monitoring with follow-up [+W]
