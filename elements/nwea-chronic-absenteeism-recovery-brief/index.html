NWEA chronic absenteeism research brief linking absenteeism to slower academic recovery¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (4 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A research brief by Sofia Postell, Emily Morton, and Megan Kuhfeld examining how absenteeism relates to recovery from COVID-era learning disruptions. The page describes it as follows: "Using MAP Growth data from 2,000 US school districts, NWEA researchers analyzed whether persistently high absenteeism rates were associated with slower recovery in math and reading." It is listed with a companion technical appendix under the topics COVID-19 & schools and Growth.
