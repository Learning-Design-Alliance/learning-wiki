# Table 1 evidence matrix mapping interventions to ESSA evidence tiers across eleven outcome domains

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Table 1 summarizes, for each of 15 interventions, the outcome domains for which WWC-reviewed evidence meets ESSA Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 requirements, spanning domains from high school attendance through "Employment and labor market." It also flags research contexts: a check for Rural indicates "research explicitly including rural sites," and a check for Facing Economic Disadvantages indicates samples at least 40 percent economically disadvantaged. The format is adapted from the REL AP College Access Evidence Matrix.
