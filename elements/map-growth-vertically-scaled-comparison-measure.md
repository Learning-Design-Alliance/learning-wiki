# NWEA MAP Growth vertically scaled assessment as a common comparison measure

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
MAP Growth is NWEA's assessment used in this study as a common assessment against which SEDA's district growth estimates are compared. The article describes it as a "vertically scaled" assessment from which district growth estimates in mathematics and ELA can be derived, serving as an independent benchmark for the convergent validity analysis of SEDA. Its estimates show a high degree of congruence with SEDA's, with small systematic discrepancies in some contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires vertically scaled scores that support growth estimation across grades 3 to 8
#### Constraints
- Discrepancies with SEDA estimates are slightly more pronounced in ELA in high socioeconomic districts

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 3 to 8 across United States school districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring academic growth in mathematics and English Language Arts at the district level
