# CREDO Virtual Control Record (VCR) methodology

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Virtual Control Record method builds a counterfactual "virtual twin" for each charter student by averaging the subsequent-year test scores of up to seven matched traditional public school students from feeder schools. Matches are made on grade level, gender, race/ethnicity, poverty status, English language learner status, special education status, and prior test scores. In this study, virtual matches were created for 79 percent of tested charter observations in reading and 62 percent in math, and matching was done period by period to meet WWC Version 4.0 baseline equivalence criteria.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient feeder-school TPS students with identical traits and matching baseline test scores must be available to form each virtual twin
#### Constraints
- The report cautions that altering the match to period-by-period means caution is advised when comparing findings with previous reports
- Non-academic outcomes could not be included due to current data limitations

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 3-8 and end-of-course assessed New Mexico charter and traditional public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Annual academic growth in reading and mathematics on state achievement tests
