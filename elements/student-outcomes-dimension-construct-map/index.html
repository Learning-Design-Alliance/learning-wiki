Student outcomes dimension: engagement, mindsets, belonging, and grade-level proficiency¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Dimension 6 of the construct map defines student outcomes as "Students have positive learning experiences and master grade level content". Its four sub-constructs cover deep engagement with lesson work (self-monitoring, managing frustration, problem-solving strategies, explaining thought processes), feeling affirmed with positive mindsets, feeling they belong to the classroom community, and meeting grade-level proficiency standards. This dimension anchors the map's outcome side for ELA and math.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Must be assessed alongside the instructional dimensions the map positions upstream of student outcomes

Constraints¶

  • Sub-constructs are stated as measurement targets, not as tested empirical results

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in ELA and math classrooms

Target Learning Goals¶

  • deep engagement with content
  • positive academic mindsets
  • classroom belonging
  • grade-level proficiency

Affordances¶

Claims¶

  • Students with a stronger sense of belonging tend to show more academic self-confidence, motivation, engagement and achievement (review reports Pedler et al.) [+W]
