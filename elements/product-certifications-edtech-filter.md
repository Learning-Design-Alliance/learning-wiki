# Product certifications as a baseline filter for evidence-based edtech procurement

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The presentation positions product certifications as a "Baseline requirement to consider edtech options" that districts can use to filter vendor pitches, inform contract renewals, and structure Requests for Proposals. It names three quality dimensions — Evidence-Based Edtech, Practitioner-Informed Design, and CAST's UDL — organized under "Five Quality Indicators." Certifications such as the ISTE Seal and EdSurge Product Index validations are cited as examples of market validators districts can request from applicants.
