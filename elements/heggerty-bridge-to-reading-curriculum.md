# Heggerty's Bridge to Reading foundational skills curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Bridge to Reading is a teacher-led foundational skills curriculum that pairs explicit phonics instruction with phonemic awareness lessons for daily instruction. The article states it "provides all the components teachers need for comprehensive foundational skills instruction in 30 minutes a day, within the literacy block." It was evaluated in two studies across kindergarten through second grade.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an educator to deliver classroom instruction as a teacher-led curriculum
#### Constraints
- Professional development for teachers implementing Bridge to Reading is recommended, but it is optional

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten students
- First-grade students
- Second-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational reading skills including phonics, phonemic awareness, and decoding
