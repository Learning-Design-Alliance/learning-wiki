# NWEA 2020 MAP Growth Achievement Status and Growth Norms for Students and Schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The 2020 MAP Growth Norms are a norming study and released reference tables characterizing student and school achievement and growth in academic subjects on the MAP Growth scale. The report states that norms "characterize each score in terms of its re lative distance from other scores in a distri- bution of scores." The release covers mathematics, reading, language usage, and science, with appendices of status norms, growth norms, status percentiles, and conditional growth distributions and percentiles for students and schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- MAP Growth test event data scored on the stable MAP Growth (RIT) scale
- school calendar information to compute instructional exposure for each test event
#### Constraints
- Norms reflect the performance of students and are sensitive to changes in student populations, so status patterns differ from the prior 2015 norms

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in U.S. public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- achievement status and growth in mathematics, reading, language usage, and science
