# Lexia Core5 Reading blended literacy program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Lexia Core5 Reading is a blended learning program aiming to "accelerate the development of fundamental literacy skills for students of all abilities in grades pre-K-5". It provides "explicit, systematic instruction through personalized learning paths", adapts to target skill gaps as they emerge, and equips teachers with data and instructional resources for personalized offline instruction. An embedded assessment supplies ongoing actionable data, and an online student dashboard encourages students to take ownership of their learning. It runs on PC, Mac, Chromebook, iPad, and Android devices.
