# Headsprout Early Reading: adaptive Internet-based supplemental early literacy curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Headsprout Early Reading is an Internet-based supplemental early literacy curriculum of eighty 20-minute animated episodes, the first 40 appropriate for prekindergarten students, plus 30 stories, 100 printable flashcards, and six Headsprout Readers. The episodes are "designed to teach phonemic awareness, phonics, fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension". Animated cartoon characters guide children through interactive episodes, and the program "provides individualized, adaptive instruction, and children work through the lessons at their own pace".
