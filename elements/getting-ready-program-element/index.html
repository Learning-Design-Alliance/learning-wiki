Getting Ready family-partnership program for early childhood educators¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Getting Ready is a program developed at the University of Nebraska—Lincoln to increase school readiness among children five and younger in home visitation or center-based early childhood programs. It "trains educators in eight evidence-based strategies to enhance their partnerships with families" and provides coaching for meetings with families through home visits, conferences, and informal interactions in which educators and families "collaboratively set goals to position each other for success."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators need initial training plus ongoing coaching sessions to reflect on and support their approaches

Constraints¶

  • Evidence ratings for Family Engagement and Academic Performance are Promising rather than Strong because the supporting sample was below 350 children

Target Learners¶

  • children ages five and younger who are educationally at risk in home visitation or center-based early childhood programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • school readiness
  • social-emotional learning
  • family engagement
  • academic performance

Claims¶

  • In the first trial, Getting Ready produced significant positive impacts on teacher-reported language, reading, and writing outcomes after two years [+M]
  • A replication trial with 267 preschoolers found greater gains in social skills and stronger teacher-child and parent-teacher relationships than controls [+W]
  • Getting Ready shows a strong evidence rating for social-emotional learning, with an average effect size of +0.22 across two studies and 487 students [+W]
  • The first trial also found social-emotional gains including increased attachment and initiative and reduced anxiety/withdrawal [+M]

Related Elements¶

  • Teachers and Parents as Partners (TAPP / Conjoint Behavioral Consultation)
  • Head Start program

Examples¶

  • Deliver Getting Ready educator training and coaching through virtual formats
