# ParentPowered READY4K! family text-messaging program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
ParentPowered delivers "three text messages to family members per week for fifty-two weeks," tailored to the student's age or grade and available in more than ten languages. Messages contain age-differentiated insights, activities and learning-expansions aligned to state standards and federal frameworks, covering a whole-child curriculum. Schools can craft local messages and access engagement data, surveys and impact reports through the ParentPowered Dashboard.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Families need access to receiving text messages.
#### Constraints
- No additional staffing required.
- No additional PD/Training required.

### Target Learners
- preschool children and their families
- kindergarten students and their families

### Target Learning Goals
- early literacy
- family engagement in home learning
