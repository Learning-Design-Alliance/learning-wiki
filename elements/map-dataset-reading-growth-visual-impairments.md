# MAP assessment dataset on reading growth for students with visual impairments

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The study used data from Northwest Evaluation Association's Measures of Academic Progress (MAP) assessment to analyze reading achievement for "224 students with visual impairments in grades 3 - 10, in four states over an eight-year time period." Growth was compared with "a nationally normed group of students from the general population." The dataset supports longitudinal trajectory analysis of an understudied population.
