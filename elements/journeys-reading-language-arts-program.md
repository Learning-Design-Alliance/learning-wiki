# Houghton Mifflin Harcourt Journeys comprehensive reading and language arts program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Journeys is a comprehensive reading and language arts program designed for kindergarten to 6th grade, though it has only been evaluated in grades K-2. It provides teachers with materials and guidelines emphasizing whole group and small group activities built around a daily "big idea" and "essential question," a teacher read-aloud, and vocabulary, phonics, comprehension, and fluency activities. The review describes it as qualifying for the "Strong" category based on one K-2 study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by a grade-level classroom teacher with approximately 90 minutes daily of reading instruction and 30 minutes for language arts
#### Constraints
- Only evaluated in grades K-2 despite being designed for K-6

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten to 6th grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading and language arts: vocabulary, phonics, comprehension, and fluency
