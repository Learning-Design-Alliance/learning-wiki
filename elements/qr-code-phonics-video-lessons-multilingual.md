# QR-code phonics video lessons embedded in decodable books

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each decodable book in the packs includes a QR code linking to a 2-minute phonics video lesson. The videos reinforce phonics instruction in English and Spanish as well as 12 other languages, and are intended to support classroom learning, small-group instruction, and extended practice at home. The article states the videos "reinforce phonics instruction in both English and Spanish, as well as 12 other languages, supporting classroom learning, small-group instruction, and extending practice at home."
