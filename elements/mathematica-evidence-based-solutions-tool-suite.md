# Suite of Mathematica tools supporting grantees to develop, refine, and scale evidence-based educational solutions

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Prior Research Table is part of a suite of tools designed to support grantees as they develop, refine, and scale evidence-based solutions that demonstrate success in improving educational outcomes for students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty, and to promote adoption of such solutions more broadly. Companion publications listed include a Measurement and Evaluation Needs Inventory, a Measurement and Evaluation Plan Template, and a Design Research Plan Template.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intended for use by grantees working to build and scale evidence-based solutions in education
#### Constraints
- Focused on improving educational outcomes for students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty

### Target Learners
- Grantees and education organizations scaling evidence-based programs, products, or practices

### Target Learning Goals
- Building evaluation capacity and promoting adoption of evidence-based educational solutions
