My Math Academy: game-based adaptive learning system for early number sense¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

My Math Academy is a game-based adaptive learning system for pre-K through 2nd grade covering number sense and operations. It includes 30 games with over 130 game-based activities, and "built-in adaptivity provides scaffolding and adjusts difficulty based on the learner's performance." Assessment is embedded throughout play, with dashboards giving teachers real-time data and recommended groupings.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Devices meeting the program's minimum technical requirements; no extra staffing is required from the school or district side

Constraints¶

  • Tablet access in the randomized study was restricted to My Math Academy only

Target Learners¶

  • Pre-kindergarten through 2nd grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Number sense and operations, from counting to 10 to adding and subtracting three-digit numbers

Claims¶

  • Learning gains are greatest for students starting with moderate math knowledge, and increase with games played [+W]
  • My Math Academy raises kindergarten math scores in a randomized study (ES = +0.23, p = .03) [+W]
  • Teachers report the program supports student interest, confidence, and enjoyment of math [+W]
  • My Math Academy raises pre-K math scores in a matched Texas study (ES = +0.32) [+W]
