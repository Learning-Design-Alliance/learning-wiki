## Description
LAUNCH (Leadership and Urban Network for Chicago), begun in 1998, is a professional development program created to 'accelerate, intensify, and deepen the knowledge, skills, and experience of principal candidates' in Chicago Public Schools. It includes a four-week summer Leadership Academy at Northwestern University's Kellogg Graduate School of Management, a full-time yearlong internship with mentor principals covering both elementary and high school experience, monthly meetings with book studies and action research, and an ongoing Urban Network of retreats and workshops. CPS has invested $7,500,000 in LAUNCH since 1999.
