Mathematics mastery learning platform (Khan Academy MAP Accelerator) used by over half a million U.S. students in grades 3–8¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A technology-enhanced, mastery-based mathematics learning platform used at scale by U.S. students in grades 3–8. The article states it is "used by over half a million U.S. students in grades 3–8" and evaluates its impact on standardized math test scores. The reference list identifies the platform as Khan Academy MAP Accelerator in a cited prior proceedings paper by the same research team.

Claims¶
