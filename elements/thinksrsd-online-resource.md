# thinkSRSD: free online implementation resource with paid professional development options

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
thinkSRSD (www.thinksrsd.com) is an online resource supporting implementation of SRSD in classrooms. The report states that "thinkSRSD (www.thinksrsd.com), provides free materials designed to help school districts and educators implement SRSD in the classroom." As of November 2017, professional development through thinkSRSD "ranges in price from $100 for online courses to $495 for in-person courses." The report also notes lesson plans for many SRSD strategies are available in Harris, Graham, Mason, and Friedlander (2008), Powerful writing strategies for all students.
