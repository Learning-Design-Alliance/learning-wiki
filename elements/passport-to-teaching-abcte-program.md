## Description
Passport to Teaching is described as "the more recent Passport to Teaching, offered by the American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence (ABCTE)". It is presented as one example among several alternative certification programs, alongside Troops to Teachers and Transition to Teaching. The report it introduces studies the career choices and experiences of teachers certified through this route.
