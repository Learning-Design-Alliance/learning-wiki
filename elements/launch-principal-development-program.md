# LAUNCH principal development program (Leadership and Urban Network for Chicago)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
LAUNCH is "a professional development program created to accelerate, inten- sify, and deepen the knowledge, skills, and experience of principal candidates," designed to recruit, identify, prepare, and support aspiring principals in Chicago Public Schools. Begun in 1998, it had 160 completers by the time of the study, with 55 serving as principals. The report evaluates LAUNCH graduates' professional development activity, leadership ratings, and their schools' ITBS learning gains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designed for candidates aspiring to be principals in the Chicago Public Schools
#### Constraints
- The report notes LAUNCH principals averaged only two years in position, so it was too early to expect substantial impacts on teacher ratings or student outcomes

### Target Learners
- Aspiring and new principals in urban public school systems

### Target Learning Goals
- School leadership capacity to improve student learning
