# Self-Regulation Questionnaire (SRQ)

## Description
The Self-Regulation Questionnaire (SRQ; Brown, Miller, & Lawendowski, 1999) is a self-report assessment built directly on Miller and Brown's seven-step model of self-regulation (Miller & Brown, 1991; Brown, 1998), itself built on Kanfer's foundational work (Kanfer, 1970a, 1970b). Respondents rate each item on a five-point scale (Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree), covering behaviors such as goal-tracking, distractibility, follow-through, learning from mistakes, and resistance to peer conformity. Scores above 239 indicate high (intact) self-regulation capacity (top quartile); scores from 214-238 indicate moderate self-regulation capacity; scores below 213 indicate low (impaired) self-regulation capacity (bottom quartile).

Because the instrument's items map onto the seven-step model's stages (receiving information, evaluating it against norms, triggering change, searching for options, formulating a plan, implementing it, and assessing effectiveness), a low overall score can be paired with item-level review to identify *which* step in the self-regulation cycle is faltering for a given learner, rather than treating self-regulation as a single undifferentiated trait.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered as a self-report instrument; requires a respondent capable of introspecting on and honestly reporting their own habitual behavior
#### Constraints
- Self-report format means results reflect the respondent's self-perception, which may not fully match observed behavior
- Best used as a reflective or diagnostic starting point, not a definitive clinical or high-stakes measure

### Target Learners
- Learners capable of introspective self-report, from adolescence through adulthood

### Target Learning Goals
- Self-diagnosis of where in the self-regulation cycle (per the [Self-Regulated Learning](../theories/self-regulated-learning.md) Miller & Brown model) a learner's habitual difficulty lies, to target support accordingly

### Affordances
- [Self-Regulated Learning](../theories/self-regulated-learning.md) — the SRQ operationalizes the Miller & Brown seven-step self-regulation model into a scorable instrument

## Examples
- A learner scoring in the low range overall, but whose individual item responses cluster around "searching for options" and "formulating a plan," may benefit specifically from structured goal-planning support rather than generic motivational encouragement.
