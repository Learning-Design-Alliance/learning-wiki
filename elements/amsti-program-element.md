# AMSTI: professional development strategy with summer training, on-site coaching, and rotating materials kits

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
AMSTI is a professional development strategy for elementary and middle school teachers and principals. Participants attend a 2-week summer training session followed by on-site coaching during the school year, with a focus on hands-on, inquiry-based instruction in math and science. Teachers are also provided with all necessary materials organized in "kits" that are rotated among schools, including teacher and student guides, student assessments, software, manipulatives, and hands-on activities.
