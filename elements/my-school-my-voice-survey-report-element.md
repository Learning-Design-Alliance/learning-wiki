# My School, My Voice surveys and the 31-measure individual school survey report

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In spring 2007 CCSR surveyed all Chicago Public School teachers, principals, and students in grades six through twelve, "receiving almost 158,000 responses". The individualized report combines a 312-item teacher survey and a 172-item student survey into thirty-one measures (e.g., Program Coherence, Academic Engagement, Teacher-Principal Trust), each displayed with trend lines from 1999-2007 for the school, demographically similar schools, and the system. A companion Details report breaks measures down to individual survey items.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least 42 percent of teachers or students must respond for a school to receive a report; measures need enough respondents for a statistically reliable school average
#### Constraints
- Some measures may be unreported if too few teachers or students answered the specific questions; teacher response rates are approximate because exact teacher counts are unavailable

### Target Learners
- students in grades six through twelve and their teachers in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- diagnosing school conditions related to instruction, leadership, professional capacity, climate, and family involvement
