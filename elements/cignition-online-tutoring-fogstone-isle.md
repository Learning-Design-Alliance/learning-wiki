# Cignition online tutoring program with the FogStone Isle fractions game

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
An online 1:1 mathematics tutoring program in which experienced teacher-tutors met students twice weekly for roughly 25-minute sessions in an online environment where they could "talk and also each draw on a mutually-visible surface". Students also played the fractions game FogStone Isle, and game play and tutoring were "interwoven to target areas of fraction understanding in most need". The evaluation covered equivalence, comparing, and adding fractions with like and unlike denominators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors are experienced mathematics teachers selected by Cignition and given approximately 6 hours of training on the platform, number talks, and fraction content
- Tutors meet consistently with the same students
#### Constraints
- Fewer sessions occurred in practice than planned due to absenteeism

### Target Learners
- Fifth-grade students in low-income schools needing additional support in fractions

### Target Learning Goals
- Fraction equivalence
- Comparing fractions
- Adding fractions with like and unlike denominators
