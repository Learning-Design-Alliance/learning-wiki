Achieve3000® five-step differentiated online literacy routine¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Achieve3000® is a supplemental online literacy program providing nonfiction reading content for grades preK–12 that builds "phonemic awareness, phonics, fluency, reading comprehension, vocabulary, and writing skills". Teachers assign one article to the whole class while "the program then tailors the version of the article to each student by automatically increasing the difficulty of text when a student is ready for more challenging text". Lessons follow a five-step routine — Before Reading Poll, reading an article, activity questions, After Reading Poll, and a Thought Question — with placement via the LevelSet™ assessment and more than 15,000 nonfiction articles.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A LevelSet™ placement test identifies each student's reading level before differentiation begins
  • Teachers receive progress reports and student usage data to track whole-class and individual progress
  • Recommended use is 80 lessons over the school year

Constraints¶

  • Content covers only nonfiction reading in English and Spanish versions matched to reading level

Target Learners¶

  • general education students
  • struggling readers in need of intensive tutoring
  • English learners in grades preK–12

Target Learning Goals¶

  • nonfiction reading comprehension
  • vocabulary
  • writing skills

Claims¶

  • Comprehension domain: one study found a substantively important positive effect and one an indeterminate effect [+W]
  • General literacy achievement domain: one statistically significant positive effect and one indeterminate effect yield a potentially positive rating [+W]
  • WWC rates Achieve3000® as having potentially positive effects on comprehension and general literacy achievement for adolescent readers [+W]
  • Tracey and Young (2004): grade 5 students using differentiated KidBiz3000® outscored comparison students on the SRI (effect size 0.29) [+W]
  • Hill and Lenard (2016): LevelSet Lexile effects were negative in spring 2014 and positive in spring 2015 [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Achieve3000 grade-band program options

Examples¶

  • Differentiate one shared article to each student's reading level rather than assigning varied texts
