PATHS (Promoting Alternative THinking Strategies) emotional literacy curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

PATHS is an experimental-based emotional literacy curriculum initially designed for deaf children, developed by Greenberg, Kusché, and associates, and designed for kindergarten through grade 6. The article quotes its purpose: "to enhance social and emotional competence and understanding in children, as well as to develop a caring, prosocial context that facilitates educational processes in the classroom." It serves as both an intervention program for children with problems and a prevention program for regular children, employing methods such as dialoguing, role-playing, story-telling, simulation, modeling, and verbal mediation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • It is intended to be a separate course within the general curriculum, conducted mostly by teachers, with parents expected to participate in modeling outside school.

Constraints¶

  • The article's analysis covers only the Feelings and Relationships Unit, which the author selected because it represents the major portion of emotional literacy content.

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten through grade 6 children, including deaf children, children with behavioral or emotional problems, and regular or gifted children

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Self-control
  • Emotional awareness and management
  • Positive self-esteem
  • Interpersonal problem-solving skills

Affordances¶

  • Abcd Model Paths Foundation

Claims¶

  • PATHS spiral sequencing treats basic emotions as prerequisites for complex emotions across four levels of complexity [+W]
  • Within-lesson sequencing in the PATHS Feelings and Relationships Unit proceeds from cognitive to behavioral to affective and from external to internal [+W]
  • PATHS topical sequencing follows a general-to-detailed continuum, the Simplifying Conditions Method, and synonymous and antonymous relationships [+W]
  • PATHS macro-level sequencing combines spiral and topical sequencing patterns [+W]
