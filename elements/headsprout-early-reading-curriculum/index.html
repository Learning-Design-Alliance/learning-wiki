Headsprout Early Reading: adaptive Internet-based supplemental early literacy curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Headsprout Early Reading is an Internet-based supplemental early literacy curriculum of eighty 20-minute animated episodes, the first 40 appropriate for prekindergarten students, plus 30 stories, 100 printable flashcards, and six Headsprout Readers. The episodes are "designed to teach phonemic awareness, phonics, fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension". Animated cartoon characters guide children through interactive episodes, and the program "provides individualized, adaptive instruction, and children work through the lessons at their own pace".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Children must meet specific performance criteria in order to progress to the next lesson
  • A subscription must be purchased for each student

Constraints¶

  • The evidence presented in this report is applicable only to the first 40 episodes of Headsprout Early Reading

Target Learners¶

  • prekindergarten and K-2 children, including at-risk preschoolers in Head Start

Target Learning Goals¶

  • phonemic awareness
  • phonics
  • fluency
  • vocabulary
  • comprehension

Claims¶

  • The evidence base for Headsprout Early Reading is limited to one small RCT, supporting only a potentially positive rating [-W]
  • Headsprout Early Reading has a statistically significant positive effect on the oral language of at-risk preschool children [+W]
  • Headsprout Early Reading has a statistically significant positive effect on the print knowledge of at-risk preschool children [+W]
