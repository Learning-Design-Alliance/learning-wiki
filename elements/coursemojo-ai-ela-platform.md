# Coursemojo: AI-powered teaching assistant platform for middle school ELA

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Coursemojo is an AI-powered teaching and learning platform for English Language Arts instruction. Its intelligent assistant, named Mojo, "works alongside classroom teachers to provide individualized support to every student while maintaining the collaborative, social nature of classroom learning." It integrates with existing curricula rather than replacing instruction. Implementation requires a device per student, pre-launch training, and no additional staffing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each student needs their own device (iPad, Chromebook, laptop, desktop)
- Coursemojo provides pre-launch training and implementation support throughout the year as needed
#### Constraints
- Designed specifically for English Language Arts instruction

### Target Learners
- middle school students
- sixth-grade students

### Target Learning Goals
- English language arts achievement
