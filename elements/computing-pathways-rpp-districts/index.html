Computing Pathways Research Practice Partnership of districts designing K-12 CS/CT pathways¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q1 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Computing Pathways Research Practice Partnership is a collaboration in which districts design and articulate system-wide K-12 learning pathways in computational thinking and computer science. The poster profiles three core districts, including one of 14,000 students described as Becoming Urban with an equity focus on greater inclusion of growing number of Black, Latinx and ELL students, and a rural district of 7,500 students focused on engaging students from low socio-economic households and female students.

Claims¶

  • Talladega County Schools narrowed a broad CT framework to five core competencies embedded across the curriculum [+W]
  • Participating districts report large enrollment gaps in CS courses by income and gender [+W]
  • Pilot districts report teacher knowledge and integration, not student demand, as their principal equity challenges [+W]
  • The partnership's research plan studies pathway creation, grade-band competencies, and whether a competency-based PD framework assists instructors in demonstrating teaching proficiency [+W]
