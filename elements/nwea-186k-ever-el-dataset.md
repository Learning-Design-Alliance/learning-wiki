# NWEA dataset of 186,139 ever-ELs and never-EL peers in 1,520 schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study leverages what the author calls "unique data that include 186,139 ever-ELs and their never-EL peers in 1,520 schools." This large-scale dataset supports within-year (fall-to-spring) achievement and gain comparisons across grades K-8 for three EL status groups against the all-student average. Its scale permits grade-level pattern analysis of growth for relatively small subgroups such as dually-identified students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student-level assessment scores from fall and spring administrations
- Records of EL service eligibility and special education identification
#### Constraints
- Coverage is limited to the schools and grades contained in the NWEA data (grades K-8 in 1,520 schools)

### Target Learners
- English Learners in grades K-8

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring within-year achievement gains for EL subgroups
