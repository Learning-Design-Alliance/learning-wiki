# Way2Work Maryland program with three major components: early referral, multiple work experiences, and service collaboration

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (4 for, 2 mixed, 3 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
Way2Work Maryland is a transition program for high school students with disabilities developed and implemented by the Maryland Division of Rehabilitation Services and the University of Maryland. It includes "three major components: early referral to the vocational rehabilitation agency, multiple work experiences, and service collaboration with integrated training and technical assistance." Services were provided through high school graduation or for 16 to 24 months depending on enrollment date.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Enrolled students had either an individualized education program (IEP) or Section 504 plan and were two years from graduation
- Voluntary enrollment with a parent or guardian providing informed consent
#### Constraints
- Services were provided only through high school graduation or for 16 to 24 months, depending on enrollment date

### Target Learners
- High school students with disabilities (IEP or Section 504 plan) two years from graduation

### Target Learning Goals
- College and career readiness
- Employment and transition to adulthood for youth with disabilities
