## Description
The Digital Equity Competencies for K-12 School Systems define what teachers, instructional technology coaches, and administrators need to know, be able to do, and the mindsets they need to implement each of the five framework domains. Specific competencies are identified for each educator group and organized around components and subskills: eight broad categories shared by each educator group, with subskills as the specific abilities required to acquire a competency. For example, a teacher competency of Visionary Leadership includes subskills such as developing a shared vision for technology specific to grade or content area.
