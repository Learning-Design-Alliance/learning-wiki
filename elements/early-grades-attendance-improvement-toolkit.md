## Description
A companion artifact released Nov 21, 2019 by the same authors, titled "Strategies for Improving Attendance in Pre-Kindergarten and Kindergarten: Toolkit for Districts, Schools, and Early Childhood Providers." It is a toolkit aimed at districts, schools, and early childhood providers, published alongside the fact sheet as part of the same Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic effort on early-grade attendance. Its role is to give those providers concrete strategies for improving attendance among the youngest learners.
