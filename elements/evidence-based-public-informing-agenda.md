# Evidence-based public informing agenda on reform progress and challenges

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The book identifies a sustained public informing agenda as one of the levels at which Chicago's improvement occurred. The overview lists "sustaining an ambitious evidence-based public informing agenda on the progress of key reform initiatives and the challenges still ahead" among the changes that elevated the system. This element made evidence about reform progress publicly available, alongside enhancing classroom instruction, building more effective school communities, and strengthening teacher and leader preparation and support.
