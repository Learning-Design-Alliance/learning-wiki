# Rubric-based self-assessment instrument for OER competencies (instrument 4)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's central instrument is a set of four rubrics, one per competency module, used for self-assessment at the end of the seminar. As printed, "Four different rubrics were developed according to the defined competencies: 1) production, 2) search / selection, 3) research and 4) mobilization of OER", each broken into indicators, with "the scale used was apprentice, practitioner and expert". The instrument underwent content validation by consultant professors, whose proposed modifications were included in the final version.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content validation via expert consultant professors before application
#### Constraints
- The study presents only the features and results of instrument 4, not the other four instruments

### Target Learners
- teachers in higher education and distance education contexts

### Target Learning Goals
- production, search/selection, diffusion and mobilization of open educational resources
