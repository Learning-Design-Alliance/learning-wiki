# TALIS 2013 principal survey dataset used for cross-national control analysis

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study analyzes the TALIS 2013 dataset collected by the OECD, which "provides background information of the school, teaching and learning environment, professional development, and working conditions of teachers and principals from 38 countries." The authors used principal surveys at lower-secondary schools; "the final sample included 7,436 schools from 38 countries." Private schools were excluded, and four binary items measured external versus internal control over assessment, hiring, learning materials, and budget allocation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Country-level estimates require applying final school weights and sufficient survey response rates
#### Constraints
- Private schools were excluded because they have more independence regarding national policy implementation
- For a few countries response rates were not high enough to support reliable estimates, and five sub-national entities were included in country-level analyses

### Target Learners
- school principals
- education researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring school-level external and internal control across education systems
