## Description
Digital learning is the element in which instruction, resources, or interaction are mediated through digital tools, platforms, or networks. It is useful when learners need flexible access, interactive media, or non-linear exploration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **Accessible digital platform or medium**
- **A clear instructional purpose for the technology**
#### Constraints
- **Technology can add distraction or access inequity if poorly designed**

### Target Learning Goals
- Support flexible access, interactive learning, and networked exploration.
