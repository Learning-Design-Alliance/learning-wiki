NWEA state dashboard for exploring educational impacts of COVID-19¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The NWEA state dashboard summarizes achievement and growth trends for students who took MAP Growth assessments in public schools within selected states. The guide states it "provides insight into the educational impacts of COVID-related academic disruptions" and aims to explain "the analyses, statistics, terms, and data included in the dashboard." It serves state-level users examining unfinished learning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • States included are those where students took MAP Growth assessments in public schools; data only from schools or districts whose agreements with NWEA permit research use

Constraints¶

  • Not all students within a state take MAP Growth assessments, so trends may not generalize to all students in the state

Target Learners¶

  • kindergarten to eighth grade public school students in selected states

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading and mathematics achievement and growth relative to pre-COVID norms

Claims¶

  • The dashboard's cohort view restricts results to students who consistently tested across selected terms, and those outcomes may be positively biased [~W]
  • The dashboard's cross-sectional view compares same-grade students across terms using the full sample of testers, but attrition is not random [~W]
  • The dashboard reports median achievement percentiles normed to a nationally representative pre-COVID sample and projected proficiency against state cut scores [+W]
  • The dashboard primarily focuses on grades 3 through 8 because remote and in-person MAP Growth results are largely comparable for grades 3 and up [+W]
  • The dashboard supports comparisons of achievement trends between student groups, including by school poverty level, with a recommendation to compare similar groups [+W]
  • The NWEA COVID-19 visualization provides state-level insights into 2020–2021 MAP Growth performance to inform academic recovery [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • MAP Growth computer-adaptive assessment and its national norms and dashboard
  • NWEA state-level data visualization of MAP Growth performance during the 2020–2021 school year
