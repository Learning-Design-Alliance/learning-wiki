## Description
A six-member advisory board of educators formed by Digital Promise in January 2022 to give feedback to edtech startup Merlyn Mind on its AI classroom digital assistant. Members were recruited through Digital Promise's networks, applied, and were interviewed; all have extensive classroom experience, four in technology coach or administrative roles. From February 2022 the board met monthly, joined group discussions with Merlyn Mind staff on professional development, learning communities, and technical resource implementation, completed surveys, and collaborated on short articles on AI in the classroom.
