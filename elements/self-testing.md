# Self-Testing

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Self-testing is the element in which learners quiz themselves or otherwise attempt to retrieve knowledge without immediate external prompting. It is useful when the goal is to strengthen retention and help learners gauge what they actually know.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **Prompts that require recall**
- **A way to check or correct the answer**
#### Constraints
- **Self-testing without correction can reinforce errors**

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve retention and support self-monitoring of knowledge.
