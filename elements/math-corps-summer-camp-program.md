## Description
Math Corps is an intensive summer camp for middle school students, founded at Wayne State University by Steven Kahn and Leonard Boehm nearly 30 years ago. It "provides not only that emotional and social support, but also high-quality math instruction," leveraging its founders' backgrounds as math teachers. In 2015 NSF funded replication of the camp in three cities, with Mathematica conducting an independent study.
