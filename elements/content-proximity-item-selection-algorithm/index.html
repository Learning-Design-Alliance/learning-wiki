Content Proximity item-selection algorithm for MAP Growth assessments¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 causal), q2 · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Content Proximity is a new item-selection algorithm used by MAP Growth assessments, introduced in a spring 2022 pilot study reported by NWEA. The article describes it as "this new item-selection algorithm" leveraged by MAP Growth assessments, and the pilot evaluated the resulting assessments' validity, reliability, and test score comparability. The supplied text prints no further detail about how the algorithm selects items or how it differs from prior approaches.

Claims¶

  • The Content Proximity spring 2022 pilot study examined validity, reliability, and test score comparability of MAP Growth assessments using the new item-selection algorithm [+W]
  • Content Proximity pilot tests contained significantly more on-grade items and made better use of adjacent-grade items than traditional MAP Growth tests [+W]
  • Content Proximity math scores were consistently higher than traditional MAP Growth math scores, with small effects varying by grade [+W]
  • Growth-norms predictions showed larger RMSDs for pilot math scores but similar predictions for reading [~W]
  • The adaptive algorithm's item selection produces a significantly lower SEM than fixed-form tests [+W]
