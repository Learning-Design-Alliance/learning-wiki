# Large-scale experimental evaluation of charter middle schools (Gleason et al., 2010)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A federal evaluation described as "the largest charter school study of its kind to date to use an experimental design", conducted by Mathematica for the U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences over a 2003-2010 time frame. It compares lottery-based charter middle schools with traditional public schools on achievement, behavior, and school progress outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Relies on charter schools that hold lotteries, enabling an experimental comparison of applicants
#### Constraints
- Findings apply to charter middle schools that hold lotteries, not all charter schools

### Target Learners
- middle school students in lottery-based charter schools

### Target Learning Goals
- student achievement
- student behavior
- school progress
