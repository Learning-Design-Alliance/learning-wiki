## Description
A framework published by Digital Promise in December 2017 to support sustainable leadership in K-12 maker learning, responding to a lack of frameworks for planning and designing school-wide maker programs. It is "Organized around three goals – establish the vision, build the culture, and make the program" and provides cross-organizational leadership teams of administrators, educators, and support staff with tools for building maker learning programs. It was developed over a year with input from partners, researchers, and maker educators and administrators, and piloted with six districts from Digital Promise's League of Innovative Schools.
