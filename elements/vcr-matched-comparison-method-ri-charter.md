# Virtual Control Record (VCR) matched-comparison methodology for charter growth evaluation

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The analysis evaluates one-year academic growth using state assessment scores, where "1 growth period requires 2 years of data" and four growth periods are possible. Charter students are matched to feeder and TPS students "on all demographics and baseline achievement" via Virtual Control Records, with match rates of 70% in reading and 68% in math. Sample sizes per growth period range from 1,933 to 2,506 students per subject across 2014-15 to 2017-18.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two years of data per growth period and state assessment test scores for matched charter and feeder TPS students
#### Constraints
- Match rates limit the analytic sample to 70% of charter students in reading and 68% in math

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in Rhode Island charter and traditional public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- One-year academic growth in reading and mathematics
