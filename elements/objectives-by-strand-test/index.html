Objectives by Strand test: 78-item district-developed math achievement measure¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The outcome measure in the reviewed study was the Objectives by Strand test, "A 78-item multiple choice test of mathematical ability developed by a large urban school district in 1980 and administered at the end of the school year." It includes 61 concepts and applications items, and average internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha) across the intervention and comparison groups was greater than 0.90. No norming information was available.
