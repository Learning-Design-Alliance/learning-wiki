Imagine Math Facts: a digital 3D game environment for arithmetic fluency and automaticity¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Imagine Math Facts by Imagine Learning is a digital program delivered in a "3D game environment" that targets fluency and automaticity in addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. It can be used in class or as homework and is positioned as building "pre-algebra readiness" by providing a foundation for higher order thinking. The summary rates it "promising" based on one study of 4,522 students.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires networked computers or tablets, headsets, and access to the Imagine Math Facts instructional content

Constraints¶

  • The promising rating applies to the early elementary grade band; results were not statistically significant for upper elementary students

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary students in grades 1-5

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Fluency and automaticity in addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division
  • Pre-algebra readiness

Claims¶

  • Early elementary students using Imagine Math Facts demonstrated significantly more growth on Ren Star math assessments (effect size +0.11) [+W]
  • Across grades 1-5, the overall effect of Imagine Math Facts on Ren Star math growth was small (effect size +0.05) [+W]
  • Imagine Math Facts use was not associated with statistically significant growth for upper elementary students (grades 3-5) [-W]

Related Elements¶

  • The Nomads — a 3D math game training adaptive expertise in rational number arithmetic

Examples¶

  • Offer optional professional development for administrators and educators alongside program implementation
  • Implement Imagine Math Facts with existing math staff monitoring individualized learning paths and progress reports
