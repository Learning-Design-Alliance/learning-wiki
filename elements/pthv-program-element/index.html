Parent Teacher Home Visits (PTHV) program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

PTHV is a family engagement program co-created by parents and educators using community organizing principles of shared leadership. Participating teachers conduct "30-40 minute home visits in which educators listen, ask questions, and make observations that they can take back to their classrooms to improve instruction for the learner." The model was evaluated in District of Columbia Public Schools in grades 1–5.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers are trained and compensated for visits
  • Visits are voluntary and arranged in advance

Constraints¶

  • No additional staffing required; no professional development or technology beyond the model's own training is required

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school students in grades 1–5 and their families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Family engagement and improved instruction informed by home-visit observations

Claims¶

  • Teacher home visits slightly improved student attendance in the following school year (ES = +0.02) [+W]
  • Home visits showed slight improvements in math scores but no difference in ELA scores [~W]

Related Elements¶

  • Parent Teacher Home Visits (PTHV) family engagement model
  • Structured relationship-building teacher home visits as a family engagement program component

Examples¶

  • Two-visit structure: relationship-building visit first, academic-focused visit later
  • Conduct voluntary, pair-based teacher home visits focused on hopes and dreams across a cross-section of students
