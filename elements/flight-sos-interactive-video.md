# Flight SOS interactive video program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Flight SOS, a co-production with the BBC, is an interactive video program applying many features of Montevidisco to English as a foreign language, following a Japanese businessman visiting the United States through immigration and customs, encounters with a female executive, a hotel clerk, an outspoken Texan, and business invitations. Helps available to the student include backing up and repeating a scene, "instantaneous feedback with a surrogate or model who appears on the screen" after the student attempts a response, a dictionary or glossary tracking words occurring in a scene with their definitions, and an explanation file in Nehongo.
