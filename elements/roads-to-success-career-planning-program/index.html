Roads to Success education and career planning program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (5 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Roads to Success is a school and career planning program evaluated over a 2007-2009 time frame in high-poverty rural schools. The report assesses "the impact of two years of exposure to the school and career planning program, Roads to Success, on eighth graders from high-poverty rural schools." It targets career planning knowledge, confidence, and engagement with school staff around career and school plans.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Two years of exposure to the program, as evaluated in the report

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated among eighth graders in high-poverty rural schools

Target Learners¶

  • Middle school students, specifically eighth graders in high-poverty rural schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Education and career planning, including career choice confidence and understanding requirements for career success

Claims¶

  • Exploratory analyses suggest Roads to Success increased confidence in choosing careers and understanding career success requirements [+W]
  • Exploratory analyses suggest Roads to Success positively impacted eighth graders' talking to school staff about career and school plans [+W]
  • Estimated impacts of Roads to Success on student behaviors were mixed [~W]
  • Two years of Roads to Success exposure had no measurable effect on eighth graders' desire to learn job skills or their study and learning habits [-W]
  • Roads to Success was evaluated as part of an associated project running 2007-2009 [+W]
  • Funding cuts in 2008 ended the Roads to Success program and its evaluation in 2009 [+W]
  • The Roads to Success evaluation used a treatment-control design with baseline comparison of groups [+W]
