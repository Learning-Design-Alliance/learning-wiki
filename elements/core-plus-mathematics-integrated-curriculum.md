## Description
Core-Plus Mathematics is a problem-based, inquiry-oriented four-year integrated mathematics curriculum for all high school students in grades 9-12. Rather than separate Algebra, Algebra II, and Geometry courses, each course is organized around connected strands of algebra/functions, geometry/trigonometry, statistics/probability, and discrete mathematics, with instruction focused on problem solving and mathematical modeling and a balanced treatment of concepts and procedures.
