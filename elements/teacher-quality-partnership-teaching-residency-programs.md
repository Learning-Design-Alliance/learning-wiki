# Teaching residency programs (TRPs) funded through the Teacher Quality Partnership grants program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Teaching residency programs are teacher-preparation models in which, per the brief, TRPs "prepare new teachers primarily through a year-long residency in a high-need school and integrated coursework leading to a master's degree." They are funded through the U.S. Department of Education's Teacher Quality Partnership grants program. This brief examines retention outcomes for teachers trained through 12 such TRPs serving six districts.
