Holding onto the GREEN Zone curriculum: an Earth Science riparian education program for grades 5-8¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A riparian education curriculum for learners in grades 5-8 or youth group members ages 10-14, focused on science inquiry and experiential learning. It guides learners in "investigating, measuring, and documenting some characteristics of a riparian zone" across six key elements such as water quality and quantity, soils, plant characteristics, erosion and deposition, and surrounding land uses. The package includes an Action Guide for learners, this Leader Guide, and a poster, and is designed for both formal and nonformal settings.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A local natural resource partner is recommended to supply expertise, equipment, and local data
  • About 15 contact hours including the field trip to cover all material and build concepts sequentially

Constraints¶

  • Written for grades 5-8 with limited prior introduction to the subject; adaptation needed for younger or older audiences

Target Learners¶

  • youth in grades 5-8 (ages 10-14) in classrooms, day camps, after-school programs, and youth groups

Target Learning Goals¶

  • understanding riparian zone components, functions, and health
  • science inquiry and critical thinking skills
  • environmental stewardship of local watersheds

Claims¶

Related Elements¶

  • Zone Notebooks: learner journals for processing, assessment, and sharing
  • GEMS® Space Science Sequence curriculum for grades 3–5

Examples¶

  • Assess youth learning through journals, observation, discussion, and applied reflection products
  • Engage youth as partners in stewardship rather than as subjects to be taught
