## Description
MAP Growth is NWEA's assessment product whose district-level data underpins this research brief. The brief states it is "Using MAP Growth data from 2,000 US school districts" to study the link between absenteeism and academic recovery in math and reading. The dataset spans a nationwide sample of US districts, enabling district-level comparisons of recovery trajectories.
