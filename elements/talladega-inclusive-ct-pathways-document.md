# Talladega Inclusive CT Pathways document and competency map

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A K-12 pathway document developed by Talladega County Schools to expand computer science and computational thinking access, built on the Rigorous Curriculum Design template because it "was very similar to Talladega's content pacing guides used at all grade levels." The accompanying competency map aligns grade-level content to five CT competencies, maps to Alabama DLCS standards, and was refined through teacher focus-group feedback, classroom piloting, and professional development sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Development time with official release time from classroom duties; the planning team logged 65 hours of working sessions
#### Constraints
- The district acknowledged that disparities in computing participation and achievement persisted despite the pathway

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in a rural district, with an equity focus on students from low socioeconomic households and female students

### Target Learning Goals
- Computational thinking and computer science competencies across K-12 grade levels
