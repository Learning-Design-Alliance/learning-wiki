# I CAN Learn® self-paced, mastery-based computer math curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
I CAN Learn® is a full-curriculum mathematics software program developed by JRL Enterprises, Inc., delivered through "a series of computer-based interactive lessons that students work on individually at their own computers". Students typically begin with a placement test that customizes their lesson plan, and each lesson includes a warm-up, lesson presentation, guided practice, and a quiz to ensure mastery before progressing. Courses include Fundamentals of Math, Pre-Algebra, Algebra, and Geometry, containing 20 to 180 self-paced, mastery-based lessons with multimedia content, plus a Classroom Explorer system for tracking attendance, homework, grades, and individualized learning paths.
