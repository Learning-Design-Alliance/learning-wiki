Jackknife leave-one-year-out empirical Bayes estimation of teacher grade effects¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A two-step value-added estimation procedure, adapted from the teacher value-added literature, that produces teacher grade effects used to predict student outcomes. A teacher's grade effect in a given year is estimated from grades given in every other year, so effects 'are not estimated with the same group of students with which we measure student outcomes,' ensuring the measure reflects a teacher characteristic rather than a single year's class conditions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must have observations in at least two years
  • A large array of student-, course-, and school-level control variables to address student sorting into teachers

Constraints¶

  • Grade effects estimated for any individual teacher could be affected by course selection or other unmeasured patterns

Target Learners¶

  • Ninth-grade high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Valid measurement of teacher effects on course grades

Affordances¶

  • Teacher Grade Effects Three Component Decomposition

Claims¶

  • Teachers' persistent grade effects predict students' long-term outcomes [+M]
  • Teacher grade effects show spillovers to students' broader ninth-grade outcomes [+M]
  • Ninth-grade teachers' grade effects are stable across years [+M]
  • Ninth-grade teachers vary substantially and systematically in the grades they give [+M]

Related Elements¶

  • Empirical Bayes shrinkage as implemented in teacher value-added accountability systems
