# PowerSchool Attendance Intervention platform for attendance monitoring and two-way family messaging

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
PowerSchool Attendance Intervention is a school-family engagement tool that helps educators monitor daily and period-level attendance and chronic absenteeism data for each student. It supports two-way messaging with families via texts and email, gathers data from a school or district's administrative system to provide attendance dashboards, customized messaging templates, and options for administrators and teachers to communicate with families. Auto-translation features help reach parents from diverse backgrounds, and automatic population of names and real-time attendance data into templates reduces staff time burden.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators and administrators access the platform via web or mobile apps; parents, guardians, and students need only a simple cellular phone with basic text messaging or phone calling or an email address to participate.
- No special staffing is required; targeted training is provided for administrators, support staff, and teachers using the Train-the-Trainer methodology.
#### Constraints
- Participation by families depends on access to a cellular phone with basic text messaging, phone calling, or an email address.

### Target Learners
- 6th-12th grade students in public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Increase family engagement and support student attendance
- Reduce chronic absenteeism
