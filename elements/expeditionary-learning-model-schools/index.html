Expeditionary Learning (EL) model schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Expeditionary Learning (EL) model schools are whole-school designs evaluated in this report. The page describes "the first rigorous study of the impacts of Expeditionary Learning (EL) model schools," commissioned by EL Education and prepared by Mathematica Policy Research, focusing on five EL middle schools and their academic achievement impacts relative to other public schools.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Operates as a whole-school model whose impacts are evaluated at the middle school level in comparison with other public schools

Constraints¶

  • Evidence on this page covers five EL middle schools; the study's time frame is listed as 2013-2019 for the associated EL Education schools and teacher professional development project

Target Learners¶

  • middle school students in public schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading achievement
  • mathematics achievement

Claims¶

  • Students in Expeditionary Learning middle schools perform better in reading and math than counterparts in other public schools [+W]
  • EL middle school students perform better in reading and math than their public school counterparts [+W]
