# MAP Growth assessment dataset covering 5.5 million students in grades 3-8 in reading and math

## Description
The brief draws on the MAP Growth assessment program, NWEA's national assessment product, to examine pandemic-era learning. The analysis uses "data from 5.5 million students in grades 3-8 who took MAP Growth assessments in reading and math," comparing "gains across the 2020-21 school year and student achievement in spring 2021" to pre-pandemic trends. The dataset supports both within-year growth comparisons and spring achievement level comparisons.
