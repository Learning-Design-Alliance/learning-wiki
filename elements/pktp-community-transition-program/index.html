Pre-K to Kindergarten Transitions Project (PKTP): a teacher co-designed, community-wide transition approach with three core components¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

PKTP is a community approach in Chicago's Altgeld-Riverdale neighborhood that promotes intentional early educational transitions through classroom and community transition practices, staffed by BPI. As the report states, "Rather than offering a transitions curriculum, PKTP includes three co re components": a cross-grade, cross-school professional learning community for teachers; common classroom practices across pre-K and kindergarten; and family learning activities such as Family Fun Hours and field trips. The components are intended to promote smoother transitions for families and children, support teachers by building community, and strengthen connections across grades, schools, teachers, and families.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Local implementers adapt the three components to different community contexts and student needs

Constraints¶

  • The components are not a structured transitions curriculum

Target Learners¶

  • pre-K and kindergarten children and their families in the Altgeld-Riverdale community

Target Learning Goals¶

  • smoother academic and social-emotional transition from pre-K to kindergarten
  • strengthened connections across grades, schools, teachers, and families

Claims¶

  • PKTP family learning activities created opportunities for informal teacher-family relationships and a greater sense of community [+W]
  • Consistent participation in a cross-grade, cross-school professional learning community supported PKTP implementation and teacher learning [+W]
  • Pre-K teachers implemented PKTP's common classroom practices more consistently than kindergarten teachers, with dramatic play and trauma-sensitive practices least frequent among kindergarten teachers [~W]
  • At the close of the first full year, PKTP's communication about the importance of transition was unclear to many teacher and parent participants [-W]
  • Inclusive, family-focused co-curricular events deepened relationships between educators and parents [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Three-week structured summer kindergarten transition program
