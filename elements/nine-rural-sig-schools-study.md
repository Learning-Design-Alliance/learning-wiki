# Nine rural schools within a 35-school SIG school improvement process study

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
The studied element is a set of nine rural schools that were part of a study for the U.S. Department of Education on the school improvement process, drawn from "a purposive sample of 35 schools receiving federal funds through the School Improvement Grants (SIG) program." The article examines how respondents in these rural schools perceived that the rural setting influenced the schools' challenges and improvement actions. It serves as the evidence base for the article's findings on rural context and staffing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must be receiving federal funds through the School Improvement Grants (SIG) program
#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
