GroweLab all-in-one coaching and talent development platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

GroweLab is engage2learn's technology platform for delivering and documenting educator coaching. The summary describes it as "an all-in-one coaching and talent development platform" that gives leaders "actionable insights into effective initiatives in driving long-term change and improvements". Coaching was facilitated through this platform, which documented all interactions and tracked attendance, evidence of learning, and adult learning/growth data, integrating educator growth data with student and staff outcomes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Coaching interactions must be documented in the platform so attendance, evidence of learning, and adult learning/growth data can be tracked

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • teachers
  • instructional coaches
  • assistant principals
  • administrators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mastery of core instructional best practices
  • alignment of educator growth with learner outcomes

Claims¶

  • Students of e2L-coached teachers outperformed students in comparison schools on end-of-year STAAR math in a propensity-score-matched study [+W]
  • STAAR results in the first e2L study were conflicting: positive versus separate control schools but negative versus non-e2L teachers in the same schools [~W]
  • Students of engage2learn teachers scored significantly higher on NWEA MAP than students of non-e2L teachers in the same schools and in comparison schools [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Badge-based competency coaching in which teachers design customized mastery paths and earn badges for demonstrated evidence of practice
