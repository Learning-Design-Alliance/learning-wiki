## Description
A technical appendix published by NWEA Research in December 2022, authored by Megan Kuhfeld and Karyn Lewis, accompanying the research brief on pandemic recovery. Its stated purpose is to "share more detailed results and to describe more fully the sample and methods used in the research included in the brief" on rebounding achievement. It serves as the methodological documentation for the brief's analyses of summer learning and start-of-year scores.
