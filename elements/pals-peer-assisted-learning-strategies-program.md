# Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies (PALS) first-grade literacy program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
PALS is a peer-tutoring technique in which "children work in pairs, taking turns as teacher and learner, to learn a structured sequence of literacy skills". The sequence covers "phonemic awareness, phonics, sound blending, passage reading, and story retelling", and "children use a simple error correction strategy with each other, under guidance of a teacher". The program requires no additional classroom staff or technology but does require on-site coaches.
