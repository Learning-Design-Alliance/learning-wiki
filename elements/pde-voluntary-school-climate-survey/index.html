Pennsylvania Department of Education voluntary school climate survey¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (6 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A statewide survey administered by the Pennsylvania Department of Education's Office for Safe Schools in partnership with REL Mid-Atlantic. It is "available on a voluntary basis to any school in the state" and "provides a way to track school climate and identify schools that need additional support to improve school climate". The REL study used its scores across 2018/19 through 2021/22.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools must voluntarily opt in to administer the survey for their scores to be tracked.

Constraints¶

  • The study sample of schools that took the survey consistently was small and not representative of the rest of Pennsylvania.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in Pennsylvania public schools
  • teachers in Pennsylvania public schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • monitoring and improving school climate

Claims¶

  • No association found between 2021/22 school climate scores and the amount of virtual and hybrid instruction in 2020/21 [+W]
  • No evidence of a significant decline in school climate scores between 2018/19 and 2021/22 in the Pennsylvania sample [+W]
  • Students and teachers reported more positive school climate perceptions in 2020/21 during hybrid and remote learning than in 2018/19 and 2021/22 [+W]
  • School climate scores were steady across the years before COVID-19 in the Pennsylvania sample [+W]
  • Items in each of the three domains of the PDE elementary school climate survey measured the constructs they intended to measure [+W]
  • The safe and respectful school climate domain of the elementary student survey fell short of the established reliability threshold [-W]
  • The three domains of the PDE elementary school climate survey were distinct from one another [+W]
