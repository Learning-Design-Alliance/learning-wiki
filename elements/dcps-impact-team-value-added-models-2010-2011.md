## Description
This report presents the value-added models used in the District of Columbia Public Schools' IMPACT and TEAM effectiveness systems for the 2010-2011 school year. As the report states, it "presents the value-added models used to measure school and teacher effectiveness in the District of Columbia Public Schools in the 2010-2011 school year." It was prepared for DC Public Schools under the Value-Added Assessment System for DC Schools and Teachers project (time frame 2009-2015).
