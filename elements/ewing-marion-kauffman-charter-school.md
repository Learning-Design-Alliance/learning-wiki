## Description
The Ewing Marion Kauffman School is "a public charter school that serves students from low-income neighborhoods in Kansas City, Missouri". The evaluation examined its impacts on achievement, attendance, and suspensions, and described its hallmarks and operations through surveys, interviews, focus groups, and classroom observations to explore possible mechanisms for its effects, informing the literature on school effectiveness.

## Description
The Ewing Marion Kauffman School is a public, tuition-free charter school serving students in Kansas City, Missouri. In the 2017-18 school year it enrolled 994 students in grades 5 through 11, of whom the report notes "Most (87 percent) of the students were low-income, and 89 percent were black or Hispanic." The school is the subject of a Mathematica evaluation running from 2011 to 2024, prepared for the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation.
