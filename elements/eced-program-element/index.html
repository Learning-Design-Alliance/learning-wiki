Every Classroom, Every Day (ECED) two-year stand-alone literacy and math program for high schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ECED is "a program for literacy and math in high schools provided over two years as a stand-alone class". It supplements regular English with "a structured literacy curriculum based on authentic expository texts", doubling students' exposure to English language arts. Year one (Reading Matters) builds comprehension and information-gathering; year two (Writing Matters) builds sharing, communicating, and personalizing knowledge; both years use the Power 10 strategies. The program reached 8,250 students in the evaluating study.
