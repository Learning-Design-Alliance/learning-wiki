# Stanford Educational Data Archive (SEDA) district achievement and growth dataset

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
SEDA is described as "the first data set to allow comparisons of district academic achievement and growth from Grades 3 to 8 across the United States, shining a light on the distribution of educational opportunities." It provides district-level achievement and growth estimates in mathematics and ELA for grades 3 through 8, which this study validates against MAP Growth-based estimates. The dataset is used to study the distribution of educational opportunities across US districts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires district-level assessment data from which achievement and growth estimates in math and ELA can be derived for grades 3 to 8
#### Constraints
- Growth estimates may not generalize to estimates obtained through other assessments such as MAP Growth in some states

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 3 to 8 across United States school districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring district academic achievement and growth to illuminate the distribution of educational opportunities
