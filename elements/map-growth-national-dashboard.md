## Description
The MAP Growth National Dashboard is a NWEA data product that releases national trend data on student achievement and growth. This brief is the first edition of a Trend Snapshots series built on it: the brief "highlights trends seen in the latest data released in the NWEA Map Growth National Dashboard," with Spring 2025 as the first snapshot. Related products listed include a user guide and FAQ, a technical appendix, and specialty-school dashboards.
