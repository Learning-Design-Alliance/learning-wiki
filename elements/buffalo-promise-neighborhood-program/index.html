Buffalo Promise Neighborhood program element¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The Buffalo Promise Neighborhood is a Promise Neighborhoods initiative site in Buffalo, New York, profiled by Mathematica Policy Research for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink as part of the Promise Neighborhoods Case Studies (time frame 2014-2015). The profile describes it as serving "one of Buffalo's poorest communities," documenting the population and economic context in which the neighborhood's education, early childhood, and family-support programming operates.
