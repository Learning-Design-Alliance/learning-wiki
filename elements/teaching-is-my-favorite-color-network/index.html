Teaching is My Favorite Color: teacher-designed support network for teachers of color¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Teaching is My Favorite Color (TiMFC) is "a teacher-designed and -led support and professional development community" for teachers of color across three southwestern Pennsylvania districts. It grew from one teacher of color in a school to "a support network of 50+ teachers across southwestern Pennsylvania." At its heart, TiMFC builds community and addresses the sense of isolation that teachers of color experience, providing a space to mentor each other, celebrate, share experiences openly, foster belonging, navigate bias, and discuss unspoken rules in schools.

Context¶

Target Learners¶

  • professional belonging

Claims¶

  • Over 90 percent of Bristol teachers who participated in student-led mental health PD reported being more conscious of the experiences of students of color in school [+W]
  • Network learning spread beyond the network through a website, conference presentations, school and district sharing, and AIR-led professional learning communities [+W]
