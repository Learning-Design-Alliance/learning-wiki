Associated project assessing long-term impacts of KIPP middle schools on college enrollment and persistence¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Beyond the achievement brief, the page lists an associated project, "Assessing the Long-Term Impacts of KIPP Middle Schools on College Enrollment and Persistence," with a stated time frame of 2018-2019 and prepared for Arnold Ventures. This project extends the evaluation of KIPP middle schools from achievement outcomes to longer-term postsecondary outcomes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Studying long-term impacts requires following KIPP middle school students to college enrollment and persistence outcomes.

Constraints¶

  • The project's stated time frame is 2018-2019, later than the 2013 brief, and the page reports no findings from it.

Target Learners¶

  • KIPP middle school students followed into postsecondary education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • college enrollment and persistence

Claims¶

  • For continuing KIPP students, KIPP high schools have positive impacts on college preparation activities and the likelihood of applying to college [+W]
  • For students continuing from KIPP middle schools, KIPP high schools have positive impacts on a variety of college preparation activities and the likelihood of applying to college [+W]
