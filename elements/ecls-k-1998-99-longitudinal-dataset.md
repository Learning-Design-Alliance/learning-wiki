# Early Childhood Longitudinal Study, Kindergarten Class of 1998-1999 (ECLS-K)

## Description
The Early Childhood Longitudinal Study, Kindergarten Class of 1998-1999 is the national longitudinal dataset on which this report's analyses are based. The report states it is "Based on the Early Childhood Longitudinal Study, Kindergarten Class of 1998-1999", providing kindergarten enrollment status and first-grade reading and math achievement measures for a national cohort of children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longitudinal follow-up of a kindergarten cohort from fall 1998 through first grade, capturing enrollment status and achievement measures.
#### Constraints
- The available text describes only its use for examining associations between kindergarten enrollment status and first-grade reading and math achievement.

### Target Learners
- kindergarten and first-grade children in the United States

### Target Learning Goals
- early reading achievement
- early mathematics achievement
