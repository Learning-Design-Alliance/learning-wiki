# Early Science with Nico & Nor preschool science program for home and school

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 9 studies (5 causal, 4 design), `q2` · 0 of 9 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Early Science with Nico & Nor is a preschool science program spanning three curricular units—Plants, Ramps, and Shadows—designed to promote science, math, and engineering across home and school. The report states "The program includes a Teacher's Guide, a Family Science Fun Guide, three digital science journals, and eight digital games." The Teacher's Guide organizes activities into four weeks per unit matching preschool formats, and the Family Science Fun Guide offers brief adaptable activities in English and Spanish. Guides are freely available via WGBH's First 8 Studios website and iPad apps via the Apple store.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Digital tools require iPad tablets; the program was implemented with iPads delivered to classrooms and orientation sessions for teachers and parents.
#### Constraints
- Implementation required adjustments for COVID-19 restrictions, including a staggered start and remote administration options for assessments and family debrief sessions.

### Target Learners
- preschool children
- early childhood teachers
- families and caregivers

### Target Learning Goals
- early science learning (Plants, Ramps, Shadows core ideas)
- science practices
- math concepts (measurement, visual and spatial thinking, comparison of quantity)
- engineering practices
