Policy Analysis and Decision-Making Capacity project (Phases 1-3)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A project funded by the Office of Science and Data Policy within ASPE at the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, aimed at improving how evidence is used for decision-making within government, particularly HHS. "During Phase 1 of the project, we conducted a review to assess grey and peer-reviewed literature describing whether and how effectively decision-makers use program evaluation findings to inform their decisions." Phase 2 comprised interviews with thought leaders and experts; Phase 3 plans include "convening a technical expert panel to further discuss potential opportunities to create or improve a systematic approach to using evidence for decision-making within government."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Funding and sponsorship by ASPE's Office of Science and Data Policy
  • Engagement of thought leaders and experts in evaluation, dissemination, and evidence use

Constraints¶

  • Phase 3 may be conducted at a later date and had not yet been carried out when the report was published

Target Learners¶

  • Government decision-makers within the Department of Health and Human Services

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Using program evaluation findings and research evidence to inform program and policy decisions

Claims¶

  • Subject matter interviews reveal barriers to evidence use in government: untimely evidence, research not geared to decision-makers, limited understanding of evidence, and organizational influences [+W]
  • Interview respondents identify collaborative relationships, trust in evidence and its users, clarity on research types, and supportive leadership as facilitators of evidence use [+W]
  • A literature review identifies misalignment between conducted research and needed evidence, contextual factors, and rarely used bridging frameworks as challenges to evidence use in policy decisions [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Improve relationships between evidence producers and decision-makers and build staff capacity and government infrastructure for accessing relevant research
  • Interactive and alternative review practices: applicant response rounds, sandpits, bibliometric scoring, decision aids, lotteries, and crowdfunding
