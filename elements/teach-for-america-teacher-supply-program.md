# Teach For America (TFA) as a teacher-supply program for hard-to-staff subjects in high-poverty schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
Teach For America is a program that supplies teachers for hard-to-staff subjects in high-poverty U.S. schools. The article describes it as "an important but controversial source of teachers for hard-to-staff subjects in high-poverty U.S. schools" and evaluates its secondary math teachers experimentally. The program requires only a two-year commitment from its teachers, a feature the authors explicitly address in their analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Operates by placing TFA teachers in hard-to-staff subjects in high-poverty U.S. schools
#### Constraints
- TFA requires only a two-year commitment, which the authors note is a source of concern addressed in the study

### Target Learners
- Secondary students in high-poverty U.S. schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Secondary mathematics achievement
