Democracy Prep Public Schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Democracy Prep Public Schools is a charter school network whose civic-outcomes effects were evaluated in this study. The article describes it as "an education focused on preparing students for citizenship", and the evaluation measured its impact on "voter registration and participation in the 2016 election" among its former students.
