Mobl21 mobile-assisted learning platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Mobl21 is a mobile-assisted learning platform usable by teachers to create study materials and by students to create lesson notes. The article lists its functions: creating "study materials in the form of multimedia educational textbooks", access to materials at any convenient time and place, self-paced review, teacher management of content and users, and tests and quizzes. It was the platform used in the article's implementation study.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers must develop study materials and define exercise types; the platform supports distribution and self-paced access.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • senior-year university students learning foreign languages

Target Learning Goals¶

  • foreign language competence development

Claims¶

  • MALL implementation with Mobl21 was psychologically comfortable for students and facilitated actualization of their learning potential [+W]
  • Students showed a high level of interest and rated the MALL technology as significant in the implementation survey [+W]
  • Independent work on personal mobile devices mobilized attention, improved perception, and turned students into initiative actors and controllers of their own learning [+W]
