# VR-based learning environment for beginning driver instruction

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A desktop VR-based learning environment developed to teach traffic rules and traffic signs to beginning car drivers in Malaysia, addressing limitations of the existing lecture- and reading-based program. In the guided mode it "provides additional navigational aids in the form of a tracer that provides a real-time indicator of the virtual vehicle position on a map, and directional arrows"; the non-guided mode is identical except without these aids. Learning was assessed with a 15-question VR-based pre/post test showing three-dimensional road scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners receive training on navigation of the virtual environment before the treatment
#### Constraints
- The study's groups were intact classes without pre-experimental sampling equivalence

### Target Learners
- Form Four secondary students who had not undergone the driver instruction program

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying errors in traffic rules and traffic signs in three-dimensional road scenarios
