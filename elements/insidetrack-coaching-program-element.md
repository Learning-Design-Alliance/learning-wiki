# InsideTrack© Coaching program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 5 studies (3 causal, 2 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
InsideTrack© Coaching "provides proactive, personalized coaching to help students identify and overcome both academic and non-academic barriers to college persistence and graduation." It partners with universities to supply personnel and technology, delivering coaching through phone, video, email, text, and mobile apps. Coaches help students clarify goals, track deadlines, access resources, and build time management and study skills, focusing on students' lives outside school as well as academics. In the qualifying study, students generally worked with coaches over two semesters and met at least five times.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- InsideTrack© partners with universities to deliver its coaching program, supplying the personnel and technology
- Colleges provide information such as transcripts, course syllabi, or course performance data to customize coaches' interactions
#### Constraints
- The effects of InsideTrack© Coaching are not known for outcomes including college access and enrollment; college attendance; academic achievement; and labor market outcomes

### Target Learners
- college students including traditional undergraduates, first-generation, low income, minority, graduate, online, adult, and military-connected students

### Target Learning Goals
- college persistence
- degree completion
- self-advocacy and study skills
