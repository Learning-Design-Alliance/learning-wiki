Simulation study of Bayesian adaptive design conditions¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article includes a simulation study built around a randomized experiment of students in an online course as a motivating example. Its purpose is to identify the conditions under which the Bayesian adaptive design can produce better inference and ultimately smaller trials, and it reports conditions with more than a 90 percent chance of superior inference using less than one-third the sample size.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A motivating randomized experiment to parameterize the simulations

Constraints¶

  • Findings are simulation-based and conditional on the circumstances the authors describe

Target Learners¶

  • Students in online courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Evaluating which interventions work for which learners

Claims¶

  • The adaptive trial design reallocates more study subjects to more promising treatment arms as evidence accumulates [+W]
  • The Bayesian adaptive design can produce better inference and ultimately smaller trials under identifiable conditions [+W]
  • Under certain conditions the Bayesian adaptive design yields superior inference with less than one-third the sample size of a standard design [+W]
