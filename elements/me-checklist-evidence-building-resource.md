# Measurement and Evaluation (M&E) Checklist for evidence-building with priority-community stakeholders

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The M&E Checklist is a resource that guides users through an evidence-building process to design, refine, and test a solution in collaboration with stakeholders from a priority community. It was developed under the project Evaluating the Development of Middle Years Math Teaching and Learning Solutions (2018-2023), prepared for the Gates Foundation. The checklist structures evidence building so that solution teams work directly with community stakeholders rather than testing in isolation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users engage stakeholders from a priority community throughout the evidence-building process
#### Constraints
- Developed in the context of middle years math teaching and learning solutions

### Target Learners
- students in a priority community
- middle years math students

### Target Learning Goals
- improved student outcomes from designed learning solutions
