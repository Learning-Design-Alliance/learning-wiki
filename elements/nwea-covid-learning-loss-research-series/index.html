NWEA COVID-19 learning-loss research series on seasonal learning patterns and equity¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 4 studies (3 associational, 1 review), q1–q2 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A series of related NWEA Research publications organized around COVID-19 and schools, equity, and seasonal learning patterns and summer loss, including a working paper on projecting the potential impacts of COVID-19 school closures, the COVID-19 slide research brief, and its technical appendix. The page presents these as "Associated Research" items, positioning the appendix within a broader program of research on pandemic-related learning loss.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Functions as part of NWEA's published research communications, linked to the MAP Growth product line

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in US schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Estimating and monitoring academic achievement impacts of pandemic school closures

Claims¶

  • Summer learning loss patterns are used to project the potential impact of COVID-19 school closures on student academic achievement [+W]
  • COVID-19 school closures will likely impact student academic achievement [+W]
  • Projections indicate students may return in fall 2020 with approximately 63-68% of typical reading learning gains [+W]
  • Projections indicate students may return in fall 2020 with 37-50% of typical math learning gains [+W]
  • Projected COVID-19 learning loss was not universal, with the top third of students potentially making gains in reading [+W]

Examples¶
