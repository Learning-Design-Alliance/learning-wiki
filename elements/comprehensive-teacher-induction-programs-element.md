# Comprehensive teacher induction programs (full-time trained mentors plus structured support curriculum)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The article describes comprehensive teacher induction as a support model for novice teachers in which "comprehensive teacher induction provides novice teachers with carefully selected and trained full-time mentors, as well as a curriculum of intensive and structured support." Two such programs were evaluated in a large-scale randomized controlled study, with first-year impacts measured on teacher retention, classroom practices, and student achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Carefully selected and trained full-time mentors
- A curriculum of intensive and structured support
#### Constraints
- First-year impacts on retention, classroom practices, and student achievement were null in the evaluated randomized controlled study

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals

- Improving student achievement
- Increasing teacher retention
