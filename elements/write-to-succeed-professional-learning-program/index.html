Write to Succeed professional learning program for literacy instruction¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Write to Succeed is a professional learning program that the SWELL partnership will "iteratively enhance and test" to "help teachers improve their literacy instruction." The partnership adapts, implements, and refines the program across 2023/24 through 2025/27, adding enhancements for teacher collaboration and culturally and linguistically relevant instruction before statewide scale-up.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Recruitment sufficient to support implementation as planned
  • District- and NMPED-based coaches who are effective instructional coaches

Constraints¶

  • Key assumptions state that short-, medium-, and long-term outcomes must be realized within the REL Southwest contract period

Target Learners¶

  • New Mexico teachers of English learner students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Literacy instruction
  • Supporting English learner students
  • Culturally and linguistically relevant instruction

Claims¶

  • The SWELL logic model posits long-term positive impacts on English learner English proficiency and on literacy achievement, including writing, for all students served [+W]
  • The SWELL logic model posits medium-term improvements in teachers' literacy instruction and ability to accommodate language learning and embed CLRI [+W]
  • The SWELL logic model posits that participating teachers will increase self-efficacy in literacy instruction and in supporting English learner students [+W]
  • Teacher confidence in teaching literacy and English learner students increased over the pilot year, especially with a local coach [+W]
  • Teachers reported increased use of culturally and linguistically relevant instruction over the year, while teacher collaboration remained unchanged [+W]
  • Teachers with a local coach completed far more of the intended Write to Succeed professional learning activities than teachers without one [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Write to Succeed professional learning program for grade 4-8 teachers

Examples¶

  • Coach-the-coaches model for sustaining teacher professional learning implementation
  • Phased iterative testing: optimization testing before efficacy testing before statewide scale-up
