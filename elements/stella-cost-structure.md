# STeLLA® cost structure

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Implementing STeLLA® costs approximately $1,750 per teacher for the yearlong program, covering the 2-week summer institute and eight monthly study group sessions. A 2-year leadership development program for personnel to become STeLLA® professional development leaders costs approximately $10,000 per leader but is not required. Schools and districts pay BSCS Science Learning for the program and are responsible for facilities, transportation, and videorecording costs; program fees cover teacher materials.
