# Representative online survey of 200 Pennsylvania LEAs on pandemic-era instruction

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An online survey the Pennsylvania Department of Education and Mathematica distributed to a representative sample of 200 LEAs during the summer and fall of 2021. It asked LEAs (school districts and charter schools) about instructional approaches, special education services, technology barriers, and health and safety measures during the 2020–2021 school year. The brief states it "describes how Pennsylvania LEAs ... responded to the public health emergency and other challenges."
