## Description
A multi-year evaluation project associated with the PEF publication, listed on the page with the time frame "2022 - 2026". It anchors a suite of related outputs, including the final workforce impact study, a follow-up analysis of whether economic returns change over time, and a policy analysis brief, produced by Mathematica and Under 3 DC for a consortium of early childhood funders.
