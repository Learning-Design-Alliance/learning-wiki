# Adaptive learning systems that calibrate tasks to the zone of proximal development

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper names adaptive learning systems as technology supporting the level dimension of personalization. It states that "Adaptive learning systems (e.g., DreamBox, i-Ready, Istation, A2I) calibrate work automatically, keeping tasks in the zone of proximal development," defined as the difference between what a learner can do without help and what he or she cannot do. Feedback from adaptive assessments helps teachers quickly pinpoint learning level and adjust units of learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to appropriate and research-based technology; feedback loops from adaptive assessments to teacher decisions
#### Constraints
- The paper presents these systems as examples supporting differentiation, reporting no comparative effectiveness evidence in this document

### Target Learners
- K-12 students needing individually calibrated task difficulty

### Target Learning Goals
- Maintaining productive challenge, avoiding boredom and frustration, and pinpointing learning level
