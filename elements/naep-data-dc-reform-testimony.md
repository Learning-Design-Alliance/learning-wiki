# National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP) data, early 1990s to 2017, as the evidence base for the DC reform testimony

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The testimony rests on "nearly a quarter century of data (from the early 1990s to 2017)" from the National Assessment of Educational Progress, described on the page as "a national student assessment test that has been administered nationally every few years since the early 1990s". This long-run NAEP series supplied the outcome data for comparing DC with other areas before and after the 2007 reforms.
