## Description
A literature review produced by Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic that "summarizes the evidence on measures of student achievement growth used in teacher evaluation that do not rely on traditional annual state assessments." It was created because members of REL Mid-Atlantic's Teacher Evaluation Research Alliance "wanted to understand more about the alternatives" to state assessment growth measures.
