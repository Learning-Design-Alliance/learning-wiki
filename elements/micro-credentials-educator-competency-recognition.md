# Micro-credentials as competency-verified recognition of educator professional learning

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Micro-credentials are a mechanism for recognizing specific competencies that support a student-centered classroom, such as checking for understanding, using exit tickets, using student data for decision making, or facilitating wait time. To earn one, educators "submit authentic artifacts of learning, such as student work samples, classroom videos, or reflections" as evidence of the articulated competency; qualified assessors review the evidence and award the micro-credential if the competency is demonstrated. The report positions micro-credentials as bridging informal and formal professional learning while letting educators manage their own learning journey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators must submit authentic evidence artifacts demonstrating the articulated competency, and qualified assessors must review and award the credential
#### Constraints
- A major consideration the report names is how micro-credentials will be officially recognized as a benefit to the educator's professional trajectory

### Target Learners
- K-12 educators and teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- Specific classroom competencies supporting student-centered, personalized learning
