EveryDay Intervention Absence Reports program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The EveryDay Intervention (formerly InClassToday) Absence Reports program "partners with schools and districts to reduce absenteeism by delivering Absence Reports to parents and guardians with actionable, personalized information about their student's attendance." After a district partnership is established and attendance data securely received, the program runs analyses to identify students at risk of being chronically absent, generates personalized reports tailored to each student, and mails them directly to parents or guardians.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A district partnership with secure transfer of attendance data to EveryDay Intervention
  • One district staff member supporting implementation (approximately 30 min/week) and one district-level data staff member supporting data transfer (< 3 hrs total work)

Constraints¶

  • Targets students identified as at risk of being chronically absent and most likely to benefit

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students at risk of chronic absenteeism in elementary and secondary schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reducing student absenteeism to increase opportunities for learning

Claims¶

  • Mailing parents personalized Absence Reports significantly reduced absences among at-risk students in a Philadelphia randomized evaluation (ES=+0.19) [+W]
  • A California replication across urban, suburban, and rural elementary districts also significantly reduced absences (ES=+0.10) [+W]
  • The weighted mean effect size across the two Absence Reports studies is +0.16, qualifying the intervention for a Strong evidence rating [+W]
  • Mailing parents personalized Absence Reports significantly reduces student absences among at-risk students (ES=+0.19) in an urban randomized evaluation [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Mail parents personalized, actionable absence reports about their own student's attendance
