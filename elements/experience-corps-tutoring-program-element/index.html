Experience Corps (EC) program: older adult volunteers tutoring and mentoring at-risk elementary students¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Experience Corps is a program that "brings older adults ages 55 and up into public elementary schools to tutor and mentor children who are at risk of academic failure." EC members also "help teachers in the classroom and lead after-school enrichment activities." The program was evaluated for its effects on student reading outcomes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Older adult volunteers ages 55 and up serve in public elementary schools as tutors and mentors.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school children at risk of academic failure

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading skills
  • Reading comprehension

Claims¶

  • The Experience Corps program was found to be a low burden to teachers [+W]
  • Students receiving Experience Corps tutoring and mentorship showed significant improvements in reading and comprehension skills [+W]
  • Experience Corps had grown to 23 sites with nearly 2,000 tutors serving approximately 20,000 students [+W]
  • A funded evaluation of Experience Corps effects on student reading outcomes was conducted by Washington University's Center for Social Development [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • AARP Foundation Experience Corps volunteer tutoring program for struggling K-3 readers
  • Salesforce-based web database and online survey infrastructure for program management
  • Reading Partners one-to-one volunteer tutoring program

Examples¶

  • Provide at least 25 hours of targeted volunteer training, with at least half pre-service and the remainder throughout the year
