# Excellence in Teaching Project (EITP): Danielson Framework-based principal observation and conferencing system

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
EITP replaced Chicago's checklist-based teacher evaluation with a system in which principals conduct two formal observations per year for all teachers, each with a pre-observation conference, a 30–60 minute observation collecting evidence rather than opinions, ratings on ten areas of practice, and a post-observation conference focused on improving practice. Principals rated teachers on the Danielson Framework's Classroom Environment and Instruction domains across "four levels of performance (unsatisfactory, basic, proﬁcient, and distinguished)." The district aimed to improve teaching and learning, build a professional learning climate, and foster constructive evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Principals require district training in the Danielson Framework, evidence collection, and conferencing, plus central-office support such as deadline reminders and ratings calibration sessions
#### Constraints
- Because the district and union disagreed on impacts for nontenured teachers, the classroom observation ratings under EITP could not be used for teacher accountability such as tenure decisions

### Target Learners
- Elementary school teachers and principals in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving instructional practice and student achievement in reading and mathematics
