# Enhanced Core Reading Instruction professional development workbooks and Leadership Guide

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program's materials include "professional development workbooks for teachers and interventionists and a Leadership Guide for literacy leaders." The workbooks are "designed to be used in a study group, self-study or as part of a facilitated training," with a recommended 3 days of study for Tier 1 instructors and 2 days for Tier 2 interventionists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training is required for the instructor
- Tier 1 instructors must be classroom teachers; Tier 2 instructors must be paraprofessionals
#### Constraints
- The program assumes that the instructor has some experience with reading instruction

### Target Learners
- classroom teachers
- instructional assistants and paraprofessional interventionists
- literacy leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- practical routines for enhancing core reading instruction in foundational reading skills, vocabulary, and comprehension aligned with the Common Core State Standards
