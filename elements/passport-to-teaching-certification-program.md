## Description
Passport to Teaching is a teacher certification credential developed by the American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence, an alternative route into teaching. The article states that "Alternative routes to teaching have been promoted during the past decade because of concerns about teacher shortages and teacher quality." The credential is being evaluated for its effects on teacher performance as measured by student achievement.
