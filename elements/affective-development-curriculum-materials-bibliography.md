# Bibliography of 29 curriculum materials for affective development

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Chapter 4 of the report presents "a bibliography of 29 available curriculum materials for affective development," fulfilling the project objective to analyze available educational techniques and materials relating to affective development. The chapter describes the search for curriculum materials, types of curriculum materials, and the organization of a chart, followed by the bibliography itself. Materials are intended to support affective education for normal and handicapped preschool children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum materials must address affective development in preschool children
#### Constraints
- The bibliography covers only materials available at the time of the 1976 search

### Target Learners
- normal and handicapped preschool children

### Target Learning Goals
- affective education, including self-concept and socialization
