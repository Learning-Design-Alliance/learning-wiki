Research-Based Early Mathematics Assessment (REMA)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (7 for) · 5 studies (4 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q3–q4 · 5 of 5 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Research-Based Early Mathematics Assessment (REMA) (Clements & Sarama, 2008) is the one mathematics outcome measure used in all three studies meeting WWC standards, administered at the end of 26 or 30 weeks of implementation. The report notes "The developer chose to include the term “Research-Based” in the name of the measure. This term does not reflect the opinion of the WWC." Group means, standard deviations, effect sizes, and improvement indices are reported per study.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administered at the end of 26 or 30 weeks of implementation in the reviewed studies.

Constraints¶

  • The developer chose to include the term “Research-Based” in the name of the measure; this term does not reflect the opinion of the WWC.

Target Learners¶

  • preschool children

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring early mathematics achievement

Claims¶

  • In a 28-classroom cluster randomized trial, Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 1.07 (improvement index +36) [+M]
  • In a large-scale 106-classroom cluster randomized trial, Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 0.48 (improvement index +18) [+M]
  • In a 139-classroom scale-up cluster randomized trial (TRIAD), Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 0.55 (improvement index +21) [+M]
  • Building Blocks has a potentially positive effect on preschool mathematics achievement, with a pooled effect size of 0.58 across three studies meeting WWC standards [+M]
  • In a 2006 randomized trial, Building Blocks for Math significantly outperformed business-as-usual on the Early Mathematics Assessment [+M]
  • Building Blocks for Math outperformed Pre-K Mathematics with DLM Express in a head-to-head comparison on the Early Mathematics Assessment [+M]
  • In a 2007 randomized trial, Building Blocks for Math significantly improved geometry but not number outcomes [+M]
