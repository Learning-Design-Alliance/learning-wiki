# Nico and Nor digital games and digital journals for iPad supporting science observation, testing, and data practices

## Description
The program includes free digital apps for iPad: Nico and Nor games give children "opportunities to observe science phenomena and easily test and revise solutions", and Digital Journals "support engagement in science practices collaboratively", designed for "observation, data collection, analysis and reflection during science investigations". The games support learning across school and home.
