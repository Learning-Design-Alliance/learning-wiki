This element identifies the four elementary mathematics curricula compared in the Mathematica evaluation of math curricula for early elementary grades. The brief names them as "(1) Investigations in Number, Data, and Space; (2) Math Expressions; (3) Saxon Math; and (4) Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics."
