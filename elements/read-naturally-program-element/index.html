Read Naturally® supplemental reading fluency program (four product editions)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Read Naturally® is a supplemental reading program using "a combination of texts, audio CDs, and computer software" to improve reading fluency, accuracy, and comprehension of elementary, middle, high school, or adult students. It is delivered through four products sharing a common fluency-building strategy — Masters Edition, Encore, Software Edition, and Live — differing in delivery mode, sequenced texts, and whether phonics instruction is included. Students "work at their own reading level, progress through the program at their own rate, and work (for the most part) on an independent basis."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Supplement a school's core language arts instruction; delivery via audio CDs with hard-copy materials, computer-based software, or a web-based version

Constraints¶

  • The WWC evidence review covers only the Masters Edition variant, as implemented in the one qualifying study

Target Learners¶

  • students with learning disabilities in grades 4–6
  • special education, Title I, and English language learner students (per developer)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • oral reading fluency
  • reading accuracy
  • reading comprehension

Claims¶

  • Read Naturally® shows no discernible effects on reading fluency for students with learning disabilities, with small negative effect sizes on GORT-III accuracy and rate [-W]
  • Read Naturally® shows potentially positive effects on writing for students with learning disabilities, with a substantively important but non-significant effect size on the WIAT Written Expression subtest [+W]
  • The evidence base for Read Naturally® with students with learning disabilities is small: one qualifying RCT of 20 students, with 42 of 43 reviewed studies excluded [~W]
  • Christ and Davie (2009) found positive, statistically significant effects of Read Naturally® Software Edition on three reading fluency measures [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Read Naturally® supplemental fluency program: modeled reading, repeated timed reading, and progress monitoring
  • Strategic Adolescent Reading Intervention (STARI)
  • Reading interventions for grades 4–9 as defined by the WWC practice guide

Examples¶

  • Pass-timing routine with four mastery criteria and repeated practice until criteria are met
