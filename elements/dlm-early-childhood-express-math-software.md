# DLM Early Childhood Express Math software

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The DLM Early Childhood Express Math software, developed by Douglas H. Clements and Julie Sarama and distributed by McGraw-Hill School Education as part of the DLM Express Math Resource Package, "includes corresponding math-based activities to reinforce math concepts taught in the classroom." It reinforces classroom support in two math domains: geometric and spatial concepts and skills, and numerical concepts and skills. In the PCER study it included 26 numerical, quantitative, geometric, and spatial activities providing individualized mathematics instructional activities approximately twice a week; Klein et al. (2008) used only 27 of the math software activities on the CD.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- It can be purchased only as part of a bundle called the DLM Express Math Resource Package, which includes the software CD and the math resources guide booklet.
#### Constraints
- The studies reviewed for this report used only the math software, not the full DLM Early Childhood Express package.

### Target Learners
- preschool children in Head Start and public preschool programs

### Target Learning Goals
- numerical, quantitative, geometric, and spatial concepts and skills
