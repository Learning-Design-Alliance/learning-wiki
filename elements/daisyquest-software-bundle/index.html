DaisyQuest software bundle for phonological awareness instruction¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 9 claims (8 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

Description¶

DaisyQuest is a software bundle offering computer-assisted instruction in phonological awareness for children aged three to seven, framed in a fairy tale about searching for a friendly dragon named Daisy. It comprises two components (DaisyQuest and Daisy's Castle) plus the Undersea Challenge adaptive test, and "uses graphics and story lines to engage children in the learning process." Activities teach rhyming, matching beginning, middle, and ending sounds, blending phonemes, and counting sounds in words, with digitized and synthetic speech, tutorials, feedback, and statistical reports for parents and teachers.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires computer access; in reviewed studies students used the program for 15-32 sessions of 20-25 minutes each, conducted by experimenters rather than classroom teachers

Constraints¶

  • Information is not available on the number or demographics of students, schools, or districts using the software; classroom teachers did not direct use in the reviewed studies

Target Learners¶

  • Children aged three to seven, including at-risk first graders, kindergarteners, and low-progress readers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Phonological awareness (rhyming, sound matching, phoneme blending and segmenting) and phonics

Claims¶

  • DaisyQuest outperformed math-oriented control software on phonological awareness and phonics measures [+M]
  • DaisyQuest outperformed alternative reading software on phonological awareness in at-risk first graders [+M]
  • DaisyQuest improved phonological awareness over regular schooling in a child-care facility randomized trial [+M]
  • DaisyQuest improved phonological awareness over regular kindergarten curriculum on three of four measures [+M]
  • DaisyQuest has positive effects on alphabetics skills in beginning readers, per WWC synthesis of four randomized controlled trials [+M]
  • DaisyQuest matched teacher-delivered phonological awareness instruction but beat other instructional software in low-progress readers [+M]
  • In Lonigan et al. (2003), DaisyQuest effects were statistically significant on four of eight phonological processing outcomes and not significant on the others [~M]
  • The WWC rated DaisyQuest as having positive effects on phonological processing, its highest applicable rating [+W]
  • DaisyQuest has positive effects on phonological processing in preschool children, with a domain-average effect size of 0.68 across two randomized controlled trials [+M]
