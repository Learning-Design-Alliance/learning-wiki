# Power4Kids evaluation: impact study, implementation study, and functional neuroimaging study of four reading interventions

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Power4Kids evaluation is a multi-component assessment of four reading interventions for struggling elementary readers, conducted in the Allegheny Intermediate Unit near Pittsburgh. According to the report, "The evaluation consists of an impact study, an implementation study, and a functional neuroimaging study." The impact study used two-level random assignment with nearly 800 children, and the report presents impacts on reading achievement at the end of the intervention year. It was prepared for the U.S. Department of Education and The Smith Richardson Foundation as part of a congressionally mandated Title I report.
