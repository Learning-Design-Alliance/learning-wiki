ELLM/Plus curriculum: literacy-focused comprehensive curriculum with eleven monthly theme-based packets, children's books, support materials, and home/school activities¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ELLM/Plus is a literacy-focused comprehensive curriculum designed to help young children from low-income families enter kindergarten ready to read and learn. Its materials include "Eleven monthly theme-based instructional packets, high-quality children’s books, and a host of support materials", which let teachers plan activities, monitor progress, and address the needs of a culturally diverse classroom. It also strengthens the home/school connection through family activities done at home or in the community. No additional staff and no technology are required, though Family Tips and Take-Home Bracelets are available online in English and Spanish.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Eleven monthly theme-based instructional packets, high-quality children’s books, and support materials
  • Professional development for teachers and coaches to implement the curriculum with fidelity

Constraints¶

  • No technology requirements; monthly Family Tips and weekly Take-Home Bracelets are available online in both English and Spanish

Target Learners¶

  • Preschool children from low-income families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Emergent literacy skills
  • Kindergarten readiness in reading

Claims¶

  • Kindergarten follow-ups after ELLM/Plus found significant TERA-3 effects in the first study and significant PPVT (+0.34) and TOLD (+0.44) effects in the second [+W]
  • In the second PCER study, ELLM/Plus showed a positive but not statistically significant difference of +0.12 across TERA-3, Woodcock, PPVT and TOLD tests [+W]
  • ELLM/Plus produced statistically significant positive effects on four TERA-3 scales in the first randomized study, with an average effect size of +0.26 [+W]
