# Mathematica's design for the Teacher Preparation Models Impact Evaluation (2003-2009), prepared for the U.S. Department of Education

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This element is the evaluation design presented in the report, created for Mathematica's "evaluation of these issues" concerning the effectiveness of different teacher training strategies. It was "Prepared for: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences" and the National Center for Education Evaluation, under the associated project "Teacher Preparation Models Impact Evaluation" with a "Time frame: 2003-2009". The design report itself is the artifact, describing how the planned impact study of teacher preparation models would be carried out.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The design is intended to support an impact evaluation comparing the effectiveness of different teacher training strategies, as stated in the report's purpose
#### Constraints
- The document is a design report only; it presents the plan for the evaluation rather than evaluation findings

### Target Learners
- prospective and new teachers entering the profession through varied preparation routes

### Target Learning Goals
- effectiveness of different teacher preparation and training strategies in preparing teachers for the nation's schools
