# School Leader's Guide for supporting toolkit completion

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The School Leader's Guide contains guidance, tip sheets, and other tools for supporting teachers to complete the Toolkit and improve their writing instruction. It assigns school leaders an active support role in the professional development, rather than treating the PLC sessions as a teacher-only activity.
