# Spanish MAP Reading Fluency adaptive oral reading assessment

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Spanish MAP Reading Fluency is a 20-minute benchmark assessment administered up to three times a year that "can be administered in a group setting rather than one-on-one." Students wear headsets, read aloud, and "the audio is recorded and scored automatically by the speech scoring engine rather than relying on human judgment and individual administration." It adapts across pre-readers, early readers, and independent readers, and reports proficiency determinations, an individualized literacy profile, and recommended next steps, with most results appearing immediately.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Headsets with microphones for audio recording and automatic speech scoring
#### Constraints
- Grade-level expectations exist for Pre-K through grade 3; students above grade 3 are compared with grade 3 expectations

### Target Learners
- Pre-K to grade 4 students in biliteracy and emergent bilingual programs

### Target Learning Goals
- Oral reading fluency, foundational decoding skills, and language comprehension
