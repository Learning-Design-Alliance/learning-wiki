Ready, Set, Leap!® multisensory preschool early-reading curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (7 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Ready, Set, Leap!® is a preschool curriculum focusing on "early reading skills, such as phonemic awareness, letter knowledge, and letter–sound correspondence, using multisensory technology that incorporates touch, sight, and sound." Teachers may adopt a theme-based or literature-based approach, each with lesson plans, learning objectives, and assessment tools. Components include 19 interactive LeapPad® books, the LeapMat™ alphabet surface, and the LeapDesk™ workstation, which also assesses progress and offers customized lessons. The complete English edition cost $1,995 per class.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Implementation included teacher training: three days (RMC study) or four full days (PCER study) on the curriculum over the course of the year.

Constraints¶

  • According to the developer, the program was being phased out and replaced by The Literacy Center; in the RMC study the curriculum was not fully implemented in all classrooms.

Target Learners¶

  • preschool children aged three to five years in center-based settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • phonemic awareness
  • letter knowledge
  • letter–sound correspondence
  • oral language
  • print knowledge
  • early math

Claims¶

  • Ready, Set, Leap!® shows no discernible effects on oral language, print knowledge, phonological processing, early reading/writing, or math in preschoolers [-W]
  • Ready, Set, Leap!® has no significant effect on preschool early reading/writing (effect size 0.09) [-W]
  • Ready, Set, Leap!® effects do not persist to kindergarten follow-up on oral language, print knowledge, or math measures [-W]
  • Ready, Set, Leap!® shows no significant effect on preschool math; the study authors' negative finding was not significant in WWC calculations [-W]
  • Ready, Set, Leap!® has no significant effect on preschool oral language (domain average effect size 0.02) [-W]
  • Ready, Set, Leap!® has no significant effect on preschool phonological processing (domain average effect size 0.06) [-W]
  • Ready, Set, Leap!® has no significant effect on preschool print knowledge (domain average effect size 0.06) [-W]
