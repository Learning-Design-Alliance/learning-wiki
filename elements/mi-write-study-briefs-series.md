# MI Write study briefs series for multiple audiences

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study results are communicated through "a series of briefs summarizing results of the MI Write study for different audiences (teachers, students and families, school and district leaders, and tool designers and instructional support teams)." This methods summary accompanies the briefs "by providing more information on the study design, sample, data sources, measures, and analytic methods." The series is a dissemination artifact of the study.
