## Description
A research-practice partnership, supported by NSF Grant No. 1837386, in which Digital Promise works with three districts to develop inclusive K-12 computing pathways. The paper prints a table of the districts: one of 14,000 students described as becoming urban (37% low income, 12% Latinx, 19% Black) focused on greater inclusion of growing numbers of Black, Latinx and ELL students; one suburban district of 28,000 (17% low income) focused on Title I schools with large achievement gaps; and one rural district of 7,500 (71% low income, 33% Black) focused on engaging students from low socio-economic households and female students. Pathway documents focus on algorithms, data, and systems and computational models.
