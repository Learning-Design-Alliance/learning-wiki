ePSRL Management System¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The electronic Personalised Self-Regulated Learning (ePSRL) Management System is a purpose-built learning management system developed "to foster self-regulated learning throughout the whole learning process". It comprises four components structured according to Zimmerman's self-regulated learning phases: the Self-Regulated Learning Experience (a diagnostic mini course generating personalised study content), a calendar-structured Personalised Study Plan with goal-tagged tasks, Learning Monitoring and Appraisal for reflecting on value, difficulty, emotions and mastery, and a real-time Learning Analytics Dashboard tracking goal, time, procrastination, task-appreciation and emotional-regulation domains. It was integrated with the courses' existing learning management system so concurrent use and switching were seamless.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students use the system concurrently with their online course, as a mandatory course requirement in the study
  • The system must be integrated with the learning management system the courses reside in so switching between both systems is seamless

Constraints¶

  • The study did not investigate the effectiveness of each individual self-regulated learning component or how they interact
  • The extent of adoption after course end was not captured, as it was not possible to track continued practice

Target Learners¶

  • Postgraduate online students in healthcare-related continuing professional education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Practising the cognitive, metacognitive and motivational aspects of self-regulated learning across planning, monitoring and reflection phases

Claims¶

  • Students who use the ePSRL Management System meet the course completion deadline at a significantly higher rate than non-users [+W]
  • Students who use the ePSRL Management System spend more time learning per month and complete the course in fewer months than non-users [+W]
  • Students who use the ePSRL Management System post more scaffold reflections and self-reflections per month than non-users [+W]
  • Using the ePSRL Management System increases postgraduate online students' perceived awareness, understanding and application of self-regulated learning [+W]
  • Using the ePSRL Management System shows no significant difference in mean assessment results or mean attempts per quiz between users and non-users [-W]
