# Berea College Promise Neighborhood initiative serving three rural Appalachian Kentucky counties

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Berea College Promise Neighborhood is a place-based education initiative serving Clay, Jackson, and Owsley Counties in rural Appalachian Kentucky. The profile states the initiative "aims to reach all of them in some way," referring to the roughly 6,300 students residing in the three counties. It was documented as part of the Promise Neighborhoods Case Studies project (2014-2015), prepared for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Serves a defined geographic catchment: Clay, Jackson, and Owsley Counties, comprising 961 square miles of rural Appalachian Kentucky
#### Constraints
- Operates in a high-need context: in 2010, 81 percent of students qualified for free or reduced-price school meals, and all included middle and high schools are persistently low-achieving

### Target Learners
- Students in prekindergarten through grade 12 in Clay, Jackson, and Owsley Counties, Kentucky

### Target Learning Goals
- College and career readiness
- Child development
- Family support
