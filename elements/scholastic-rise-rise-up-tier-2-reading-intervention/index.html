Scholastic RISE and RISE Up Tier 2 small-group reading intervention program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Scholastic RISE and RISE Up is a Tier 2 intervention for students in grades 1-5 providing "targeted small-group instruction to address and prevent reading gaps using explicit, structured comprehension, word study, phonics instruction, and guided writing". RISE (levels C-N) has groups of four students rotate through four stations; RISE Up (levels O-Z) uses three stations focused on comprehension strategies on short texts. A kit provides teacher resources, books, short reads, and digital access.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher(s) or interventionists to staff the stations
  • A kit with teacher resources, books, short reads, and digital access

Constraints¶

  • No technology required for implementation; an online portal is included for teachers and students

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 1-5 needing Tier 2 reading intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Comprehension
  • word-solving
  • fluency
  • phonics
  • guided writing

Claims¶

  • Average impact of Scholastic RISE and RISE Up across grades and measures is +0.09 [+W]
  • RISE students made similar progress to matched controls on Star and FAST assessments (ES +0.02) [+W]
  • Teachers and interventionists gave mostly favorable implementation ratings, with over 90% comfortable implementing and finding it supplemented the core program well [+W]
  • RISE and RISE Up students significantly outperformed controls on FastBridge Words Read Correctly Per Minute (ES +0.40) [+W]
