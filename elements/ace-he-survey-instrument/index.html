ACE-HE survey instrument measuring ABC engagement and institutional support in online/blended higher education¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The ACE in Higher Education (ACE-HE) is a survey measuring indicators of affective, behavioral, and cognitive engagement (four items each) plus institutional support elements (nine affective, nine behavioral, six cognitive items) and eight external personal-environment barriers, on 6-point agreement scales. Items were "translated from English into Spanish (see Appendix A) and piloted for comprehension and clarity with students from the host university." It was completed by 1,295 students (1,165 undergraduate, 130 graduate), 14.2% of the university population, and both English and Spanish versions are publicly available.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administration to students with online course experience; stakeholder involvement in development, translation, and piloting as the authors describe

Constraints¶

  • Validation was conducted only with the Spanish version at a single Colombian university; the authors state validation "should not be applied to the English version of the instrument considering the imperfect nature of translation"

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate and graduate students in online and blended courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring student engagement and institutional support for engagement

Affordances¶

  • Ace Framework Expanded Institutional Community

Claims¶

  • The ACE-HE measurement model of affective, behavioral, and cognitive engagement fits online/blended survey data well [+W]
  • The ACE-HE measurement model of institutional support for ABC engagement also fits the data well [+W]
