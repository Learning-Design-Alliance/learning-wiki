# Pirate Math one-to-one tutoring program for third-grade calculation and word-problem difficulties

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Pirate Math is a one-to-one tutoring program for third graders struggling with math calculations and word problems. Students receive 48 sessions of 20-30 minutes, three per week over 16 weeks, delivered outside ordinary math periods by tutors who may be teachers or paraprofessionals. Sessions focus on "counting up" strategies and solving word problems by finding missing numbers, with a gold-coin reward system placed on a treasure map.
