# Strategic Adolescent Reading Intervention (STARI)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
STARI is a supplemental, multicomponent reading intervention for middle school students who struggle with reading. It "incorporates components essential for skilled reading (e.g., decoding, fluency) while also teaching meaning-making strategies important for comprehension," and is organized into thematic units of relevance to young adolescents. It was evaluated in a randomized study in 8 Title 1 schools, taught during an elective or whole-school intervention period three to five periods per week for the entire school year, and requires no student technology.

## Design Implications

### Context

#### Constraints
- Designed for middle school students who struggle with reading; no technology is required for students

### Target Learners
- middle school students who struggle with reading

### Target Learning Goals
- decoding
- fluency
