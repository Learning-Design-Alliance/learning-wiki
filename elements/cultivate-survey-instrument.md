# Cultivate Survey: twice-yearly student voice survey of classroom learning conditions and learning beliefs

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Cultivate is a student voice survey administered in Chicago Public Schools twice annually (fall and spring) since 2022-23, capturing students' experiences of nine dimensions of classroom instructional environments plus learning-related beliefs. It is "designed to enhance the work that is being done in schools to strengthen adult-student relationships and promote student learning and well-being," equipping "educators and students with a tool to help them work together to improve classroom learning conditions." The I am Valued belief measure composites Belonging and Identity Safety items rated on a five-point truth scale about a target class.
