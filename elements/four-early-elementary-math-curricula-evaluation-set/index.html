Four early elementary math curricula compared in the federal evaluation: Math Expressions, Saxon Math, Investigations in Number, Data, and Space, and Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics¶

Description¶

The evaluation compared four early elementary math programs, each a textbook-based curriculum with an associated instructional approach. The page frames the study as addressing the long-standing debate over "which textbooks and their associated instructional approaches have the greatest impact on student learning in key subjects." The four curricula were Math Expressions, Saxon Math, Investigations in Number, Data, and Space, and Scott Foresman-Addison Wesley Mathematics, assigned to schools in an experimental design.
