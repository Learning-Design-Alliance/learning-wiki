## Description
EPIC (the Effective Practice Incentive Community) is a school-level award program evaluated by Mathematica Policy Research. Schools receive EPIC awards based on data used to determine award eligibility, and the program operates through three partner organizations whose student populations the baseline report compares. The supplied text describes EPIC only at the level of the evaluation's baseline statistics, stating that the report "provides a general overview of EPIC" and examines "which schools receive EPIC awards."
