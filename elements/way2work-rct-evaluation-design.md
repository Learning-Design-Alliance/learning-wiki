# Way2Work Maryland impact evaluation: randomized controlled trial with 401 enrolled students and 24-month follow-up

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The impact evaluation used a randomized controlled trial design in which the program "randomly offered some participants enhanced Way2Work Maryland services (the treatment group)" while others received usual services (the control group). Between September 2017 and early January 2019 the program enrolled 401 high school students. Data came from follow-up surveys with enrollees and parents conducted 24 months after enrollment, administrative data, and program enrollment data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Random assignment of enrolled participants to enhanced services or usual services
- Follow-up surveys with enrollees and parents at 24 months, plus administrative and program enrollment data
#### Constraints
- Findings apply to high school students with disabilities in Maryland two years from graduation who voluntarily enrolled between September 2017 and early January 2019

### Target Learners
- High school students with disabilities two years from graduation

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring impacts on education, employment, and transition outcomes for youth with disabilities

## Claims

- [Way2Work Maryland did not affect postsecondary education and training outcomes, but more treatment group members completed high school within two years of enrollment](../claims/way2work-high-school-completion-gain-no-postsecondary-effect.md) [+M]
