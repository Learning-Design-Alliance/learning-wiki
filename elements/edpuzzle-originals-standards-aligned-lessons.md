## Description
Edpuzzle Originals are ready-made, professionally-developed lessons aligned with Common Core standards that teachers can still adapt to their classroom requirements. The article states they are "ready-made, professionally-developed lessons that align with Common Core standards while still allowing teachers to adapt the lessons", and they were the intervention content in the randomized math study.
