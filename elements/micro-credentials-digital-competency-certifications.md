# Micro-credentials as digital competency certifications for educator professional learning

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Micro-credentials are "digital certifcations that verify an individual's competence in a specifc skill or set of skills." The report describes them as "competency-based, researched-backed, on-demand, personalized, and shareable." In the pilot, school leaders, coaches, and teachers engaged with micro-credentials around systems change, coaching, and technology, and coaches supported teachers in earning micro-credentials aligned with the challenges and strategies they were working on.
