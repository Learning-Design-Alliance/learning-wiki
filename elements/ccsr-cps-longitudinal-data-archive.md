# CCSR longitudinal data archive on Chicago Public Schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CCSR data archive is a linked longitudinal database on Chicago Public Schools built under an ongoing data-sharing agreement, described as 'the most encompassing longitudinal data archive on a city's public system in the country.' It contains complete administrative records for every semester since 1991, high school course transcripts since 1992, achievement test scores since 1987, personnel files, National Student Clearinghouse college outcomes, and biennial surveys of students, teachers, and principals, all linked by student- and school-specific identification numbers. The archive lets researchers respond quickly to policy shifts, build coherence across studies, and serve as a technical resource for non-researchers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An ongoing data-sharing agreement with CPS and continued school participation in surveys
- Documented, consistent analytic conventions across analysts to maintain coherence
#### Constraints
- The report contrasts the archive with independent one-off studies whose data are designed for a particular problem and often do not generalize to new problems

### Target Learners
- Researchers, district administrators, reporters, external evaluators, and program administrators in Chicago

### Target Learning Goals
- Enabling rigorous study of district reform, tracking change over time, and building the city's analytic capacity
