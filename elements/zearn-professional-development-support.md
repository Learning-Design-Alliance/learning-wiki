# Zearn professional development and coaching supports for educators and school leaders

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Zearn offers professional development through live onsite and virtual sessions that help educators launch the program and use data to identify student needs, with foundational and advanced sessions tailored to implementation goals. It also provides on-demand professional development building pedagogical content knowledge for each K-5 math unit, a library of asynchronous resources including webinars and tutorials, and optional school-based coaching to help establish strong usage routines and support data-driven implementation. No additional personnel are required to use the program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designated points of contact at the school or district to work with Zearn's Partnerships Team for onboarding and rostering setup.
#### Constraints
- Coaching and professional learning are described as optional additions rather than required components.

### Target Learners
- K-8 educators and school leaders implementing Zearn Math

### Target Learning Goals
- Effective implementation of Zearn Math and data-informed math instruction
