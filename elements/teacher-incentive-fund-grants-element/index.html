Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) grants supporting performance-based compensation systems¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program established by Congress in 2006 that "provides grants to support performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools." The grants pursue two stated goals: reforming compensation to reward educators for improving student achievement, and increasing the number of high-performing teachers in high-need schools and hard-to-staff subjects. This report examines the 2010 grant cohort's first year of implementation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Districts must implement the required components of the TIF program, including pay-for-performance bonuses tied to performance measures

Constraints¶

  • The report examines implementation near the end of the first year, before the first pay-for-performance payouts to teachers and principals

Target Learners¶

  • Educators (teachers and principals) in high-need schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improve educator effectiveness and the quality of the teacher workforce to improve student achievement

Claims¶

  • Educators in schools offering pay-for-performance bonuses tended to be less satisfied than those in schools without bonuses [-W]
  • TIF districts expected most educators would receive a bonus, inconsistent with guidance for challenging pay-for-performance [-W]
  • Most teachers and principals reported satisfaction with professional opportunities, school environment, and the TIF program [+W]
  • Districts implemented most components of the performance-based compensation system throughout all four years, but fewer than half planned to keep offering performance bonuses once the grant ended [~W]
  • Traditional salary schedules may not reward effective teaching or give the most effective teachers incentives to work in high-need schools, motivating performance-based compensation [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) educator evaluation and compensation strategies
  • Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) performance-based compensation program

Examples¶

  • Design pay-for-performance bonuses that are substantial, differentiated, and challenging per TIF guidance
  • Design pay-for-performance bonus structures that align with grant guidance and clearly communicate eligibility to teachers
  • Explore differentiated preservice preparation, contextualized in-service learning, and incentives to retain principals in lower-retention environments
