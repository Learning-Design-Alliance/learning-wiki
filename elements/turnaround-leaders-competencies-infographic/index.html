Competencies and Strategies of Effective School Turnaround Leaders infographic¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

An infographic published January 29, 2019 by the Institute of Education Sciences through REL Mid-Atlantic administered by Mathematica Policy Research. It communicates research-based turnaround leadership competencies and strategies, drawing on the Center on School Turnaround's Four Domains framework, to assist states, districts, and schools in turnaround efforts.
