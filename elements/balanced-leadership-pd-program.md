# Balanced Leadership® professional development program for school leaders

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Balanced Leadership® is a professional development program for current and aspiring school leaders, developed by McREL International, aiming to improve student outcomes by helping leaders "understand and implement a set of actions and behaviors of school leaders that may be associated with improved student outcomes." It delivers in-person sessions with trained facilitators over one or two years, with optional one-on-one coaching and online support. In the reviewed study, principals attended 10 two-day sessions across three school years using a consortium training model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained facilitators; instructional specialists provide the professional development, and training consultants must complete practice training sessions with more experienced facilitators before facilitating in the field
#### Constraints
- The effects of Balanced Leadership® are not known for outcomes other than school leader retention at the school, per the WWC review

### Target Learners
- principals, assistant principals, teacher leaders, and others responsible for school-level leadership in K–12 schools

### Target Learning Goals
- implementing leadership actions and behaviors associated with improved student outcomes, including leading school change and fostering shared beliefs and a purposeful school community
