## Description
This is the research base behind the book: "Over the course of three years, Bryk and Schneider, together with a diverse team of other researchers and school practitioners, studied reform in 12 Chicago elementary schools." Each school "was undergoing extensive reorganization in response to the Chicago School Reform Act of 1988, which called for greater involvement of parents and local community leaders in their neighborhood schools." The study drew on "years longitudinal survey and achievement data, as well as in-depth interviews with principals, teachers, parents, and local community leaders." It is used in the wiki as the evidence base for claims about relational trust and school improvement.
