America's Teacher Corps (ATC) program element¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

America's Teacher Corps is a proposed federal program element for recognizing and rewarding teaching excellence. Membership would be open to "Highly effective K-12 public school teachers" documented through district or state evaluation systems complying with federal standards. Members would receive visible recognition, and those serving in high-poverty Title I schools would receive a salary supplement and a portable credential. The program is proposed, not implemented or evaluated, in this publication.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Federal legislation creating the ATC
  • District or state evaluation systems that comply with federal standards for documenting teacher effectiveness

Constraints¶

  • Rewards are conditional on service in high-poverty Title I schools

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 public school students, especially those in Title I schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Equitable access to highly effective teachers
