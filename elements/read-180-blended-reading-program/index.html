READ 180 blended learning program for struggling readers in grades 4-12¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

READ 180 is "a blended learning program designed for struggling readers who are reading 2 or more years below grade level." It combines online and direct instruction, student assessment, and teacher professional development, delivered in 45- to 90-minute sessions with whole-group instruction, three small-group rotations (adaptive computer application, teacher-led small group, independent reading), and whole-class wrap-up, for students in grades 4 through 12.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An additional READ 180 teacher is needed.
  • At least one device (desktop, laptop, or tablet with internet connections) is needed for every three students; an interactive whiteboard is also recommended.
  • Teachers receive at least one day of professional development, with additional days available for purchase.

Constraints¶

  • Designed for students reading 2 or more years below grade level in grades 4 through 12.

Target Learners¶

  • Struggling readers in grades 4 through 12 reading two or more years below grade level

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading achievement for struggling adolescent and elementary readers

Claims¶

  • READ 180 shows a modest average effect size of +0.08 across five studies, qualifying it for a Strong evidence rating [+M]
  • A Florida READ 180 study found positive outcomes for students at moderate risk but negative effects for students at high risk (average effect size +0.12) [~W]
  • Teachers and principals reported satisfaction with READ 180 implementation and positive perceptions of its impacts, while recommending additional professional development [+W]
  • READ 180 studies in Newark (NJ) and Memphis found no differences from control [-M]
  • Two READ 180 studies found statistically significant positive effects over control groups (Western Massachusetts +0.18; Milwaukee +0.14) [+W]
