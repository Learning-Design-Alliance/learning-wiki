Inventory of available instruments for assessing postsecondary students' noncognitive attributes¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review identifies that many instruments are available to postsecondary institutions for measuring students' noncognitive attributes, and reports convergence on a limited number of key attributes with "multiple instruments are available to measure these attributes." The supplied text does not name the individual instruments or their psychometric properties; it establishes their availability and the theoretical (rather than applied) character of most research reporting findings from them.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instruments must be available to postsecondary institutions for assessing noncognitive attributes

Constraints¶

  • Most research reporting findings from these instruments is theoretical in nature, not applied to college practice

Target Learners¶

  • college students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring noncognitive attributes relevant to student success

Claims¶

  • Literature and expert opinion converge on a limited number of key noncognitive attributes that matter for student success, with multiple available instruments [+W]
  • Most research on noncognitive assessment and segmentation in postsecondary education is theoretical rather than applied to college practice [+W]
