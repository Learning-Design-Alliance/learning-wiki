## Description
The Los Angeles Promise Neighborhood is a Promise Neighborhoods implementation site profiled by Mathematica Policy Research for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink. The profile states that "The Los Angeles (LA) Promise Neighborhood includes two separate communities, Pacoima and Hollywood, which are about 17 miles from each other." The site spans education, early childhood, child development, college and career readiness, and family support topic areas listed for the publication.
