Nico and Nor digital games and apps for school and home STEM learning¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The program includes free digital apps for iPad described as digital games that "support children’s learning accross school and home." The games "provide children opportunities to observe science phenomena and easily test and revise solutions." The overview names eight games: Wonder Farm, Puppy Park, Coconut Star, Coconut Canyon, Shadow Play, Berry Garden, Farmers Market, and Shadow Cave. The Spanish text adds that the games let children practice math and test and revise engineering solutions.

Claims¶

  • Digital tools complemented hands-on investigations by providing opportunities not possible in the classroom and letting children easily practice what they learned in hands-on activities. [+W]
  • Children in classrooms implementing the Early Science with Nico and Nor program made significantly more improvement in science learning than peers in comparison classrooms. [+M]
  • Children who engaged in the program at both home and school made significantly more improvement in science learning than children who implemented at school only [+M]
