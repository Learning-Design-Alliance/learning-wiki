# Observation

## Description
Observation is the short-form canonical target for instructional activities in which learners watch people, processes, or contexts carefully in order to notice patterns, behaviors, or constraints.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A clear purpose for what to notice**
- **Prompts or criteria that focus attention**
#### Constraints
- **Unguided observation may stay impressionistic**

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve noticing, evidence gathering, and situated understanding.
