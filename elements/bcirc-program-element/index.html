Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition (BCIRC) program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (5 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

BCIRC is an adaptation of the Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition (CIRC) program designed to help Spanish-speaking students succeed in reading Spanish and then transition to English reading. In the adaptation, "students complete tasks that focus on reading, writing, and language activities in Spanish and English, while working in small cooperative learning groups". Students are assigned to heterogeneous four-member teams; activities include partner reading, story-component recognition, vocabulary development, creative writing, and comprehension tasks, with teachers modeling strategies such as making and confirming predictions. It targets grades 2-5 and is now offered as Reading Wings on the Success for All website.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers received extensive staff development on using a constructivist framework to facilitate cooperative learning discussions

Constraints¶

  • Developed for use with students whose primary home language is Spanish

Target Learners¶

  • Spanish-speaking English language learners in grades 2-5

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading comprehension in Spanish and English
  • English language development
  • Transition from Spanish to English reading instruction

Claims¶

  • A larger percentage of BCIRC students met the bilingual education exit criterion in language (39% versus 21%), not statistically significant [+W]
  • A larger percentage of BCIRC students met the bilingual education exit criterion in reading (32% versus 10%) [+W]
  • BCIRC has potentially positive effects on English language development (effect size 0.29, not statistically significant) [+W]
  • BCIRC has potentially positive effects on reading achievement for Spanish-speaking English language learners (effect size 0.61, not statistically significant after clustering correction) [+W]
  • Two years of BCIRC exposure is associated with larger reading gains than one year (effect size 0.87 significant versus 0.33 non-significant) [+W]
  • Contrary to program guidelines, English acquisition was given priority over Spanish acquisition in practice [-W]
  • In a five-year quasi-experimental study, the intervention program showed a statistically significant positive effect on fifth-grade reading performance assessment but not on the CTBS Reading subtest [~W]
