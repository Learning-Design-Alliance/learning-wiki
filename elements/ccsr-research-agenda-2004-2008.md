## Description
This document is a research agenda laying out planned studies across five sections: student outcomes and school productivity, deeper examination of the essential supports, a closer look at students in CPS, high school follow-up research, and assisted studies. Tables I and II list all studies and proposed time lines, noting that "We have listed more studies than we can complete during the next five years," with several studies contingent on raising funds. It aims to provide results useful to CPS leadership, local schools, and the education reform community, and to bring evidence to bear on the theory of action behind major initiatives.
