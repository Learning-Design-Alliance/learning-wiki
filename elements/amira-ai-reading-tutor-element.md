# Amira — an AI-based personal reading tutor that assesses, tutors, and reports on oral reading

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Amira is "a personal artificial intelligent tutor program" that provides students with "an always available, infinitely patient, and highly-trained personal reading tutor". It assesses oral reading fluency, analyzes errors to pinpoint strengths and weaknesses, listens as a student reads aloud and intervenes when helpful, recommends appropriately challenging stories, and generates diagnostic reports for teachers and parents. It is browser-based and works on Chromebooks, PCs, Macs, iPads, and tablets without special software.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Devices should have at least 4 GB of RAM and a working microphone; headphones with a mic are encouraged in noisier classrooms.
- A current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari (required on iPads), or Firefox is recommended.
#### Constraints
- Amira operates as a closed, secure system that never accesses the open internet.

### Target Learners
- kindergarten and first grade students
- students in grades 1–4 who struggled with reading

### Target Learning Goals
- early reading outcomes
- oral reading fluency
