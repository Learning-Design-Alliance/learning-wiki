# SEERNet: five digital learning platforms, six research teams, and a network hub coordinated as research infrastructure

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
SEERNet is an IES-funded initiative begun in late 2021 comprising five platform teams, six research teams, and one network hub. The platforms are ASSISTments/E-TRIALS, OpenStax Kinetic, Terracotta/Canvas, UpGrade/MATHia, and ASU Learning@Scale. The paper explains, "The network hub connects the platform and research teams, and also links to practitioners and other stakeholders," and the funded research studies explore existing platform data and test whether platform enhancements increase student learning outcomes. The hub also documents progress through working papers and maintains a public website.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- IES funding of platforms to extend research capabilities and of a hub to coordinate efforts among the teams
#### Constraints
- Each research team uses one specific platform, so studies are scoped to that platform's affordances and limits

### Target Learners
- K-12 and higher-education students using the platforms

### Target Learning Goals
- advancing scalable, practice-grounded educational research on student learning
