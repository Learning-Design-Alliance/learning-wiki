# Fraction Face-Off! supplemental fractions program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q4` · 3 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Fraction Face-Off! is "a supplemental math program designed to support fourth-grade students who need assistance solving fraction problems." It comprises 36 lessons taught three times a week for 12 weeks, each with a warm-up problem, group work, a speed game to build fluency, and a worksheet. Materials include a teacher's guide, student worksheets, and an optional Implementation Ready Pack with flashcards and manipulatives; the current version is the 2015 Revised Edition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by classroom educators, including teachers and paraprofessionals, to individual students or small groups of up to three students, three times a week for 12 weeks for about 30 minutes per session
#### Constraints
- The effects of Fraction Face-Off! are not known for other Primary Mathematics outcomes, including data analysis, statistics, and probability, and algebra

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade students who have demonstrated difficulty in mathematics

### Target Learning Goals
- understanding the magnitude of fractions
- comparing two fractions
- ordering three fractions
- placing fractions on a number line
