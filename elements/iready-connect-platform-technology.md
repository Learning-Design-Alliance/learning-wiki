# i-Ready Connect digital platform for teacher-facing content, with browser, SSO, and third-party integration requirements

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
No technology is required for iRCL itself, but related teacher-facing content is accessed through the digital platform i-Ready Connect, which runs on Windows, MacOS, and Chromebook machines with browsers enabling JavaScript, cookies, and pop-ups. The platform supports single sign on via SAML and WS-Federation and third parties such as Clever, ClassLink, and Google.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Browsers that enable JavaScript, cookies, and pop-ups; SSO via SAML or WS-Federation for authentication and authorization
#### Constraints
- No technology is required for i-Ready Classroom Mathematics itself; the platform is for teacher-facing content

### Target Learners
- Teachers and school staff managing iRCL accounts

### Target Learning Goals
- Access to teacher-facing curriculum content and account management
