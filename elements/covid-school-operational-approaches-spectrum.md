# Spectrum of school operational approaches during COVID-19, from fully remote to fully in-person with hybrid approaches between

## Description
The report situates hybrid instruction on a spectrum of pandemic-era school operational approaches. It notes that "schools across the country used different operational approaches, ranging from fully remote instruction to fully in-person instruction with various “hybrid” approaches in between." Hybrid approaches occupy the middle of this range and were the focus of the districts examined for the Pennsylvania Department of Education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must choose and implement an operational approach along the remote-to-in-person spectrum during pandemic conditions
