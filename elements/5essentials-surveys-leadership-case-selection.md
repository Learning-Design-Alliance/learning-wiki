# 5Essentials annual teacher surveys as the school-selection instrument

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The 5Essentials surveys are annual teacher surveys used in this study to identify schools where teachers reported strong principal instructional leadership. The article states researchers "visited 12 schools in Chicago where teachers reported strong principal instructional leadership based on the annual 5Essentials surveys from 2011-13." The surveys thus served as the screening instrument for the contrasting case study, and the article also tags school climate as a publication theme.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Annual administration of the 5Essentials teacher surveys, as used from 2011-13 in this study, to provide teacher-reported measures of principal instructional leadership for school selection.
#### Constraints
- Selection rests on teacher-reported perceptions of leadership rather than directly observed leadership practice.

### Target Learners
- K-12 school leaders and teachers in Chicago public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying school conditions, especially principal instructional leadership, associated with improving instruction and student achievement
