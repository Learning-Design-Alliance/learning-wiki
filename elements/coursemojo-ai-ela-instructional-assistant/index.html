Coursemojo AI-powered ELA instructional assistant with data dashboards¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Coursemojo is an AI-powered, curriculum-aligned instructional assistant for fifth through eighth grade students that provides personalized Socratic feedback on student writing in real time, supports instruction in over 50 home languages, and generates actionable reports for teachers on student progress and engagement. Its assistant avatar Mojo integrates pre-programmed content from ELA curricula with generative AI (GPT-4o). The platform includes accessibility features such as read-aloud, speech-to-text, and graphic organizers aligned with UDL principles, plus engagement-dot dashboards and translation features.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Integrated within existing high-quality instructional materials to maintain pedagogical fidelity
  • a mediating layer of instructional coaching that translates raw data into pedagogically-driven classroom practice

Constraints¶

  • Automated feedback is not always precisely differentiated for students' developmental level

Target Learners¶

  • fifth through eighth grade students
  • multilingual learners
  • students with IEPs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • literacy, comprehension, and writing outcomes
  • close reading and literary analysis

Claims¶

  • Coaches cautioned that AI-generated feedback should be used formatively rather than for summative assessment because of AI's probabilistic nature [~W]
  • Instructional coaches co-constructed implementation plans rooted in Kotter's framework to create the climate for change and make the change happen for an AI literacy tool [+W]
  • Coaches reported that usage dashboards grounded coaching conversations in evidence rather than perception and prompted reality checks on implementation [+W]
  • Coursemojo's color-coded engagement dots served as a real-time diagnostic that coaches reported enabled in-the-moment scaffolding and small group pulls [+W]
  • In a landscape scan survey, 12 of 13 coaches agreed or strongly agreed that AI ELA products can help personalize learning [+W]
