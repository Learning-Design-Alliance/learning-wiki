# The educator micro-credentialing ecosystem: Digital Promise's PK-12 platform, 400+ micro-credentials, and the NEA partnership

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The micro-credentialing ecosystem is a set of platforms and issuers through which PK-12 teachers and administrators demonstrate and earn recognition for specific skills. Digital Promise launched the nation's first educator micro-credentials in 2014; by the brief's writing, more than 50 universities, non-profits, and school districts had developed more than 400 micro-credentials, and the NEA had partnered with Digital Promise to develop more than 150 micro-credentials with a platform for members. The brief uses this ecosystem as the context for its policy analysis, noting credentials cover topics from critical thinking strategies to supporting English language learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Issuing organizations must define the evidence required and score submitted demonstrations of skill
#### Constraints
- The brief calls the innovation an early, "wild west" stage of development, with organizations offering similar micro-credentials whose required evidence may differ

### Target Learners
- PK-12 teachers and administrators

### Target Learning Goals
- Demonstration and recognition of teaching and leadership skills through competency-based professional learning
