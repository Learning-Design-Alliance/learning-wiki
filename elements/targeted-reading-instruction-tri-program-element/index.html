Targeted Reading Instruction (TRI): one-to-one tutoring model for struggling K-1 readers¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

TRI is a one-to-one tutoring model "designed in particular for isolated rural schools." Teachers work individually with struggling kindergarten and first-grade readers for 15 minutes a day, using "re-reading, word work, and guided oral reading" during sessions. Instruction focuses on "oral language, decoding, writing, comprehension, vocabulary, and fluency skills." Webcam technology connects teachers with TRI coaches for training and follow-up.

Context¶

Claims¶

  • TRI shows an average effect size of +0.51 on Woodcock reading outcomes across two studies [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • TRI staffing structure: literacy coaches supervised by an intervention director

Examples¶

  • Layered professional development: summer institute plus weekly web conferencing follow-up
  • Weekly webcam coaching meetings with observation and feedback sustain teacher implementation
