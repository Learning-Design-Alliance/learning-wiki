Voyager Universal Literacy System® core reading program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A core reading program "designed to help students learn to read at or above grade level by the end of the third grade." It uses "individual reading instruction, higher-level comprehension activities, problem solving, and writing," plus computer-based practice in phonological skills, comprehension, fluency, language development, and writing, delivered in whole classroom, small group, and independent settings. First published in 2000, it has been used in 360 districts in 22 states, with almost 17,500 teachers and over 331,000 students.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Sequenced lessons provide teachers with tools and directions for instruction and assessment; implementation includes ongoing professional development and school-based reading coaches

Constraints¶

  • Cost is $244 per student for the first year and $160 for subsequent years, varying by selected elements

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten through third-grade students reading at all levels, including students receiving special education services

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Beginning reading: alphabetics, comprehension, fluency

Claims¶

  • No individual alphabetics outcome reached statistical significance in the WWC analysis [-W]
  • Evidence extent for Voyager is moderate to large for alphabetics and small for comprehension, with no qualifying studies of fluency or general reading achievement [~W]
  • The WWC found Voyager Universal Literacy System® had potentially negative effects on comprehension [-W]
  • The WWC found Voyager Universal Literacy System® had potentially positive effects on alphabetics for kindergarten students [+W]
  • The Frechtling, Zhang, and Silverstein (2006) study showed a substantively important positive average alphabetics effect, while Hecht (2003) did not [+W]
