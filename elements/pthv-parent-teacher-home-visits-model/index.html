Parent Teacher Home Visits (PTHV) family engagement model¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

PTHV is a family engagement program co-created by parents and educators using community organizing principles of shared leadership. Teachers conduct 30-40 minute home visits in which they listen, ask questions, and make observations to improve instruction. The model is structured by five non-negotiables, including that "Visits are voluntary and arranged in advance" and "Educators go in pairs and reflect."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers are trained and compensated for visits
  • Visits focus on hopes and dreams
  • Educators visit a cross-section of students (not a targeted intervention)

Constraints¶

  • No additional staffing required
  • None required for professional development or technology

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 1-5 and their families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Family engagement, student attendance, academic performance, and social-emotional outcomes

Claims¶

  • Teacher home visits slightly improved student attendance in the following school year (ES = +0.02) [+W]
  • Home visits improved standardized math scores (ES = +0.11) but showed no difference in ELA scores [+W]
  • A home visit before the school year reduced the likelihood of a disciplinary incident (effect size = +0.10) [+W]
  • PTHV is rated promising for social-emotional learning with an average effect size of +0.10 from one study [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Parent Teacher Home Visits (PTHV) program
  • Structured relationship-building teacher home visits as a family engagement program component

Examples¶

  • Conduct voluntary, pair-based teacher home visits focused on hopes and dreams across a cross-section of students
  • Two-visit structure: relationship-building visit first, academic-focused visit later
  • Home Visits
