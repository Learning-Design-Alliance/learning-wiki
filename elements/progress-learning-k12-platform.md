# Progress Learning: a standards-aligned K-12 instructional platform with individualized learning paths and progress monitoring

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Progress Learning is a comprehensive, state standards-aligned instructional resource for Grades K-12 in multiple subjects including ELA, Math, Science, and Social Studies. Each student receives "a learning path or plan featuring specific standards uniquely aligned to their individual learning needs," accessible from any Wi-Fi-enabled device. Teachers can create and share assessments and assignments, track progress, and use diagnostics and progress monitoring to identify students not performing at grade level; district and school dashboards support cross-subject monitoring, with integrations for Clever, Classlink, Canvas, and Google Classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom teachers as primary users; school and district administrator access included with subscriptions
- Wi-Fi-enabled devices and supported browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
#### Constraints
- Custom training packages beyond the free virtual and pre-recorded training start at $1000

### Target Learners
- K-12 students across ELA, math, science, and social studies

### Target Learning Goals
- Grade-level standards mastery in multiple subjects
- Remediation for students below grade level
