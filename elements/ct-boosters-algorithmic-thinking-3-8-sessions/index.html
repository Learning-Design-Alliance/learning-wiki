CT Boosters: 30-minute algorithmic-thinking professional learning sessions for grades 3-8¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The CT Boosters are short professional learning sessions on algorithmic thinking for teachers of grades 3-8. The deck states the format as "What: 30min CT Boosters(3-8)" with a plan of "5 minutes: Introduction & Vocabulary", "15 minutes: Lesson Ideas", and "5 minutes: Q & A". The session opens with guiding questions about why computers need detailed directions.
