# School data tools for family and LSC inquiry: CIWP, 5Essentials, CPS School Profiles, To&Through, and ISBE report card

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The playbook's Data Dive section assembles six data sources parents and LSC members can use to learn about their school: the Continuous Improvement Work Plan, 5Essentials Survey results, CPS School Profiles (covering academic progress, school climate, and community engagement), To&Through elementary and high school data tools, lived experiences of families, and the ISBE report card. It advises that "These data points are best used in sparking questions & conversations to understand what's happening at school, and what improvements can be made toward stronger student experiences & learning."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Public availability of the school's CIWP, 5Essentials reports, CPS School Profiles, To&Through tools, and the ISBE report card; outside Chicago, publicly available district and state data.
#### Constraints
- The playbook frames these data points as conversation-starters for questions and dialogue, not as standalone verdicts on a school.

### Target Learners
- parents and caregivers
- Local School Council members
- community leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- using school data to understand student experiences and guide school improvement conversations
