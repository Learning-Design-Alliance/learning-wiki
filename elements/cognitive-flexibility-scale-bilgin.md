# Cognitive Flexibility Scale (Bilgin, 2009a)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Cognitive Flexibility Scale is a 19-item adjective-pair self-report instrument developed by Bilgin (2009a), with scores ranging from 19 to 95 where higher points mean greater flexibility. The article reports that "19 items in CFS explain 51% of the variance", a criterion-validity correlation of .-44 with the Non-Functional Attitude Scale, Cronbach's Alpha of .92, and item-total correlations between .49 and .63. It served as the dependent measure in the study.
