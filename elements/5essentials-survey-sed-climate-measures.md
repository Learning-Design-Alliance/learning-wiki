# 5Essentials Survey measures of socioemotional development and school climate

## Description
The 5Essentials Survey provided the study's measures of student socioemotional development (SED) and school climate. The study used "5Essentials Survey measures of socioemotional development (SED) from 8th and 9th grade students who attended a CPS high school between 2011 and 2017 (160,148 students)" and 5Essentials Survey measures of school climate completed by high school students and teachers, alongside administrative records of demographics, attendance, and discipline.
