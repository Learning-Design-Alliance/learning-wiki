## Description
Teaching is My Favorite Color (TiMFC) is "a teacher-designed and -led support and professional development community" for teachers of color across three southwestern Pennsylvania districts. It grew from one teacher of color in a school to "a support network of 50+ teachers across southwestern Pennsylvania." At its heart, TiMFC builds community and addresses the sense of isolation that teachers of color experience, providing a space to mentor each other, celebrate, share experiences openly, foster belonging, navigate bias, and discuss unspoken rules in schools.
