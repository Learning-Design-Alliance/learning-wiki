## Description
A technical brief published by NWEA Research that documents the methods used to produce the achievement projections in the white paper 'The COVID-19 slide: What summer learning loss can tell us about the potential impact of school closures on student academic achievement.' The page states it "outlines the methodology behind the projections" and tags the work under COVID-19 and schools, equity, and seasonal learning patterns and summer loss.
