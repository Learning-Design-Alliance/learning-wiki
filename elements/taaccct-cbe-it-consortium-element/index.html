TAACCCT consortium implementing flexibly paced CBE programs in information technology¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 4 studies (3 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Under a Round 2 TAACCCT grant, "Sinclair Community College is leading a consortium with Austin Community College and Broward College to implement flexibly paced, competency-based education (CBE) programs in information technology." The consortium is the setting from which the brief's highlighted practices were drawn, with Mathematica serving as external evaluator of the grant.

Claims¶

  • A consortium led by Sinclair Community College implemented competency-based information technology programs in three community colleges, documented at or near baseline [+M]
  • A three-college consortium successfully implemented online competency-based programs as planned, meeting most implementation milestones and outcome targets [+M]
  • About 35 percent of consortium participants completed a grant-funded program of study, including certifications, certificates, and degrees [~M]
  • Consortium-wide, 35 percent of participants completed a grant-funded program of study, with high employment rates and wage growth above the national average [~M]
  • CBE participants completed programs quickly, averaging less than two terms to a first industry certification course and about four terms to certificates and degrees [+M]
