Teacher instructional practice dimension: implementing HQIM with integrity while adapting to students¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Dimension 5 of the construct map states that "Teachers implement HQIM with integrity while adapting to their students' needs and identities". Its three sub-constructs cover drawing on the underlying evidence base, content and pedagogy; adapting HQIM for student learning needs and identities while maintaining integrity to core components (grade-level rigor, standards-alignment, accurate content portrayal); and routinely collaborating with peers about HQIM implementation and adaptation outside formal professional learning structures.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Assessment requires observing both faithful implementation of core HQIM components and adaptation for student needs and identities

Constraints¶

  • The dimension names specific core components — grade-level rigor, standards-alignment, accurate content portrayal — as the integrity boundary for adaptation

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 ELA and math teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • implementing and adapting high-quality instructional materials with fidelity and responsiveness

Affordances¶

  • Shared Measures Construct Map Curriculum Shifts

Related Elements¶

  • Shared Measures Construct Map instrument for ELA and math curriculum shifts
  • Student outcomes dimension: engagement, mindsets, belonging, and grade-level proficiency

Examples¶

  • Use the construct map for progress monitoring of CBPL and HQIM implementation
