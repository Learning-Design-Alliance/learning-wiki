Description¶

As part of the same project, Mathematica assisted Pittsburgh Public Schools in developing performance-based pay plans for teachers and principals, building on the value-added estimates of educator effectiveness. The report describes this as work "assisting the Pittsburgh Public Schools in developing performance-based pay plans for teachers and principals."
