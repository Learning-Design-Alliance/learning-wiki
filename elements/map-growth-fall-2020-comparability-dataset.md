# Fall 2020 MAP Growth remote-versus-in-person comparability dataset (over 535,000 K-8 students in 147 districts)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The analysis draws on test scores from "over 535,000 K-8 students in 147 school districts", of which 92 operated fully remotely in fall 2020 and 55 offered in-person instruction to all students. This large observational dataset underpins the brief's reliability, engagement, and score-trend comparability findings for remotely and in-person administered MAP Growth assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires fall 2020 MAP Growth test score records tagged by administration mode (remote or in-person) and by district instructional mode.
#### Constraints
- Districts analyzed were those operating fully remotely or offering in-person instruction to all students; partially hybrid districts are not described in the abstract.

### Target Learners
- K-8 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Measuring achievement and growth in reading and mathematics via MAP Growth assessments
