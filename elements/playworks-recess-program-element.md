# Playworks school-based recess program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Playworks is a school-based program that guides students in organized activities during recess and makes improvements to the recess play yard. In the evaluation described here, it was delivered for one school year in schools randomly assigned to receive it, and its effects on boys' and girls' physical activity were measured with accelerometers and recess observations. The article describes it as one of the "School-based programs, such as Playworks" that might increase physical activity, especially for girls.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation during recess over a school year, with organized activities and play-yard improvements
#### Constraints
- Effects on accelerometer-measured activity were significant for girls but not for boys in the trial
