# Google voice typing in Google Docs as a free ASR-based L2 writing tool

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Google voice typing in Google Docs is an automatic speech recognition tool that converts speech to text and was used by participants to compose academic texts after a short training session. The article describes it as accessible and free, noting it is "available at no cost" and already on learners' devices, which they use to compose shorter L1 text messages. It served as the treatment technology in the study's two writing tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training on using Google voice typing in Google Docs before writing tasks
- Learners' speech must be intelligible enough for the technology to transcribe
#### Constraints
- Accuracy depends on learners' fluency and pronunciation; the pandemic context with few English-speaking opportunities may have diminished ASR output accuracy

### Target Learners
- adult ESL / English for Academic Purposes students

### Target Learning Goals
- academic L2 writing
- pronunciation practice
- focus on content and meaning over form
