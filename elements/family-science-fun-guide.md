# Family Science Fun Guide

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Family Science Fun Guide is a digital guide that "includes indoor and outdoor activity ideas spanning three topics: Plants, Ramps and Shadows." It "includes brief activities and fun projects to inspire families to engage in science, math & engineering in playful ways and material to help parents and caregivers feel prepared to engage in rich conversations with their children." A Spanish-language version, the Guía para Familias, covers the same three topics and notes some activities can be done at home and others outside during common outings.
