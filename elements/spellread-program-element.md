## Description
SpellRead™ (formerly SpellRead Phonological Auditory Training®) is described as "a literacy program for struggling readers in grades 2 or above, including special education students, English language learners, and students more than two years below grade level in reading". It integrates auditory and visual aspects of reading through systematic, explicit instruction in 140 lessons completed over five to nine months. Instruction occurs in small groups of five students with one instructor in 60–90 minute classes, and includes a web-based Instructor Support System for monitoring progress.
