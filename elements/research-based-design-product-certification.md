# Digital Promise Research-Based Design Product Certification

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A third-party certification, launched by Digital Promise in February 2020, that recognizes edtech products grounded in learning sciences research; nearly 60 certifications had been issued by the report's writing. The updated criteria state that certified products demonstrate that "Rigorous research about learning informs and drives significant design decisions," that the design philosophy is grounded in learning sciences research, and that practitioners, learners, and communities can easily access information about the product's research basis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Demonstrating that rigorous learning research drives significant design decisions
- Publicly accessible explanation of the product's research basis
#### Constraints
- The 2022 revision added a template for applicants to name learner communities in their studies versus their user base, to surface equity gaps in the research used

### Target Learners
- practitioners, education leaders, and learners selecting edtech tools

### Target Learning Goals
- identifying products designed to support meaningful learning
