FASTalk text-message family engagement program for at-home literacy activities in the home language¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

FASTalk (Families and Schools Talk) is a family engagement tool that "seeks to promote equity and build partnerships between teachers and historically underserved families by sharing engaging, at-home learning activities via text messages in each family's home language." Parents received three text messages per week for 8 weeks supporting literacy development at home. The program requires no additional staffing, offers optional professional development for administrators and educators, and requires only a text message-enabled phone.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Families need a text message-enabled phone
  • Parents sign up to receive the text messages

Constraints¶

  • Delivery is limited to text messages; the article describes no other channel
  • The evaluated dosage was three text messages per week for 8 weeks supporting literacy at home

Target Learners¶

  • historically underserved families and their children, including kindergarten students in the evaluated studies

Target Learning Goals¶

  • literacy development at home
  • family engagement and teacher-family partnerships

Claims¶

  • FASTalk students showed significantly higher spring iSTEEP literacy growth than matched non-FASTalk peers (effect size +0.39) [+W]
  • FASTalk kindergarten students in Oakland Unified gained 1.1 months of additional literacy compared with matched comparison students [+W]
  • FASTalk holds a Promising evidence rating based on two qualifying studies averaging +0.27 across 585 students [+W]
