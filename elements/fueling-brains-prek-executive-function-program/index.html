Fueling Brains pre-kindergarten executive function program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Fueling Brains is an early childhood program that "integrates executive function development into pre-kindergarten instruction," grounded in research on neuroplasticity and whole-child development. It "blends guided and open-ended play with hands-on, interactive learning" and targets working memory, cognitive flexibility, and inhibitory control. Instruction is designed to be ecologically authentic, letting children apply skills in real-world contexts, and the program emphasizes data-informed instruction using ongoing assessments.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Minimum of one instructional coach per school district

Constraints¶

  • In the studied implementation, the intervention occurred only in pre-kindergarten

Target Learners¶

  • pre-kindergarten students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • executive function skills such as working memory, cognitive flexibility, and inhibitory control; early literacy and math

Claims¶

  • Fueling Brains pre-kindergarten gains persist to end of kindergarten on six of seven TX-KEA literacy and math subtests [+M]
  • Fueling Brains students significantly outperform matched controls on CIRCLE phonological awareness at end of pre-kindergarten (ES +0.26) [+W]
  • Fueling Brains students significantly outperform matched controls on CIRCLE rapid vocabulary at end of pre-kindergarten (ES +0.18) [+W]
