# National K–8 fall and spring achievement dataset covering 840,000 students in 8,800 public schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study leverages achievement data measured at two points in each school year — "in the fall and spring of kindergarten through eighth grade" — for a national sample of "840,000 students attending 8,800 public schools." This seasonal structure allows the authors to separate school-year growth from summer loss and to compare rural and nonrural schools. It is the empirical basis for all findings in the article.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Achievement measured in both fall and spring of each grade so that school-year and summer learning can be separated
