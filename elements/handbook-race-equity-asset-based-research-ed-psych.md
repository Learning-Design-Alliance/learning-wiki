## Description
An edited scholarly volume, edited by Francesca A. López, Jessica T. DeCuir-Gunby, and DeLeon L. Gray, published by Routledge Press. The publisher describes it as offering "a profound vision of contemporary educational psychology scholarship, practice, policy, teaching, and research design that is both informed by and empowering of social justice." It charts "a new course in educational psychology that supports equity-focused research agendas, refined theoretical syntheses, and meaningful attention to the complexities of race, culture, and class." The mathematics-learning chapter reviewed here is chapter 7 of this volume.
