# SEERNet: a network of five digital learning platforms operating as research infrastructure

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SEERNet connects platform developers, researchers, and practitioners to leverage existing digital learning platforms as research infrastructure. The five DLPs—OpenStax, Mathia, ASSISTments, Canvas, and ASU—pair learning applications with research infrastructures (Kinetic, UpGrade, E-TRIALS, Terracotta, ASU's Learning@Scale) that "enable varying methodological approaches (e.g., student-level random assignment), and provide built-in efficiencies to conduct studies on large scales." The network aims to reduce the chasm between research insights and improvements in learning experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researcher-DLP collaboration; alignment of planned comparisons with existing platform content; data privacy infrastructure
#### Constraints
- Platforms have bounds on available data, supported experimental comparisons, and modifiable features; some studies require traditional recruitment for smaller samples

### Target Learners
- K-12 and adult learners using the platforms; education researchers studying them

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving mathematics teaching and learning and other platform-delivered learning through research embedded in authentic environments
