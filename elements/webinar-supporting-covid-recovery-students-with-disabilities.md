## Description
An on-demand June 2021 webinar by "the Alliance for Excellent Education, NWEA, and the National Center for Learning disabilities" on "recent research on academic growth for students in special education before the pandemic and implications for policies and practices designed to spur COVID-19 recovery." The panelists share research findings, policy recommendations for states and districts, and "the perspective of a district leader and what their district is doing to support students with disabilities."
