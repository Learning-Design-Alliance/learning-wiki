Digital Promise educator micro-credential ecosystem and online platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The ecosystem is Digital Promise's infrastructure for the design, development, and implementation of educator micro-credentials. It includes "more than 20 partners with content expertise" and, as of January 2016, "more than 120 micro-credentials are available through an online platform that facilitates selection, submission, assessment, and awarding of micro-credentials." Issuing partners include CTQ, the Friday Institute, Relay Graduate School of Education, and the University of San Diego's Mobile Technology Learning Center, covering competencies from data literacy to deeper learning practices. Digital Promise, CTQ, and partners also work with states, schools, and districts to provide formal recognition for teachers who earn micro-credentials.

Claims¶

  • As of mid-2017 the Digital Promise micro-credential ecosystem had drawn over 5,000 submissions from educators in approximately 550 districts, with about 55 percent of portfolio submitters awarded a micro-credential [+W]
  • Nine states offered CEUs for Digital Promise micro-credentials by 2017, but no coherent framework existed for valuing micro-credentials [+W]
  • Districts and universities increasingly attach concrete incentives to earning Digital Promise micro-credentials [+W]
