NWEA research brief: Trends in children's academic skills at school entry, 2010–2017¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A research brief published by NWEA Research in 2019, authored by Megan Kuhfeld, James Soland, Christine Pitts, and Margaret Burchinal. The page describes it as a study that "provides the first national insight into trends and disparities in the academic skills of children at school entry" for students who began kindergarten between 2010 and 2017. It is tagged under the topics Equity and Early learning, and companion versions exist as a journal article and a working paper.
