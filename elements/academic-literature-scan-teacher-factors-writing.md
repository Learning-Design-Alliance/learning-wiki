# Academic literature scan on teacher factors in secondary writing

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The evidence base for the brief is an academic literature scan that Mathematica conducted "in partnership with teacher development and writing instruction experts." The scan's findings and conclusions underpin the brief's summary of instructional skills and practices that support writing improvements in grades 6-12. The work was funded by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints

### Target Learning Goals
