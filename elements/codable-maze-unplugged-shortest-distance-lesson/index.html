Codable maze unplugged lesson for shortest-distance algorithms across content areas¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The deck describes an unplugged lesson in which "Students will use this unplugged lesson to code the shortest distance through a maze". It proposes applying the maze task to various content areas, including moving through a map, ordering a historical timeline, and ordering the parts of a story, making the same sequencing task reusable across subjects without computers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • No computers are required since the lesson is unplugged; teachers adapt the maze task to their content

Constraints¶

  • The deck presents applications as possibilities across content areas rather than tested implementations

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 3-8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sequencing steps to find an efficient path, applying algorithmic thinking to maps, timelines, and story order
