## Description
This element is the NWEA Research brief of April 2020 by Megan Kuhfeld and Beth Tarasawa, which connects summer learning loss research to the potential achievement impacts of COVID-19 school closures. The page lists associated research artifacts: a working paper titled "Projecting the potential impacts of COVID-19 school closures on academic achievement," a presentation on COVID-19 closures and learning loss, and a technical appendix supporting the brief. Together these form a resource set for understanding and projecting closure-related learning loss.
