# Building Blocks preschool mathematics curriculum

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (3 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 4 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building Blocks is a mathematics curriculum that "aims to develop preschool children’s knowledge of mathematics using activities that are intentionally sequenced based on the developmental progression of children’s mathematical learning." It includes whole- and small-group instruction, center activities, and computer activities, plus weekly family letters, with teacher support through professional development. Activities were implemented over 26 to 30 weeks in the reviewed studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers implementing Building Blocks in their classrooms received training and coaching.
- Teachers regularly assessed children’s learning and adapted activities to build on and extend the level of children’s mathematical thinking.
#### Constraints
- The Building Blocks curriculum is designed for use in prekindergarten through grade 8. However, this intervention report provides evidence of its effectiveness only in prekindergarten.

### Target Learners
- preschool children, especially those from lower-resource communities

### Target Learning Goals
- early mathematical knowledge and processes

## Claims

- [In a 28-classroom cluster randomized trial, Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 1.07 (improvement index +36)](../claims/building-blocks-clements-2008-large-effect.md) [+M]
- [In a large-scale 106-classroom cluster randomized trial, Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 0.48 (improvement index +18)](../claims/building-blocks-clements-2011-scale-up-effect.md) [+M]
- [In a 139-classroom scale-up cluster randomized trial (TRIAD), Building Blocks raised end-of-intervention REMA scores with an effect size of 0.55 (improvement index +21)](../claims/building-blocks-hofer-2013-scale-up-effect.md) [+M]
- [Building Blocks has a potentially positive effect on preschool mathematics achievement, with a pooled effect size of 0.58 across three studies meeting WWC standards](../claims/building-blocks-potentially-positive-preschool-math-effect.md) [+M]
