# Rasch rating-scale analysis methodology for deriving school survey measures

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors derive survey measures or scales through Rasch rating-scale analysis, an item response latent-trait model in which "Survey items are used to define a measure based on the relative probability of a respondent choosing each category on each item." Individuals are placed on the scale from their responses, and the logit units "constitute a linear measurement system and therefore are suitable for use in statistical procedures." Four statistics are reported for each measure: person reliability, school-level reliability, item difficulty, and item infit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Respondent choice probabilities across item categories must be modeled to place individuals on a logit scale
#### Constraints
- Some measures, such as Change in Human Resources and Cosmopolitan Experience, are not empirical Bayes estimates and have no school-level reliability coefficient; single-respondent measures have no school-level reliability

### Target Learners
- school researchers and analysts using the Chicago survey data

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring school organizational conditions on a linear scale for statistical analysis
