# MAP Reading Fluency Adaptive Oral Reading default form routing students to Oral Reading Fluency or Foundational Skills tracks

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
MAP Reading Fluency is "a multistage adaptive assessment" administered typically three times a year, presenting easier or more difficult tasks based on prior performance. Under the default Adaptive Oral Reading form, students are routed "to either the Oral Reading Fluency track if they are ready to read passages or to the Foundational Skills track if they are not"; teachers can also assign tracks directly. Foundational Skills items are scored dichotomously and calibrated with the Rasch model onto a scale with mean 500 and SD 10 using Winter 2020 estimates as reference.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners
- K-3 students taking MAP Reading Fluency, including those not yet ready to read passages

### Target Learning Goals
- Oral reading fluency and foundational reading skills supporting reading comprehension
