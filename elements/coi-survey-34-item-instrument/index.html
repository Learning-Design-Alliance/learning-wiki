34-item Community of Inquiry Survey instrument¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A validated 34-item survey instrument developed to assess the three CoI presences (teaching, social, and cognitive). Swan et al. (2008) collaboratively developed it and "tested its efficacy on a multi-institutional, multi-disciplinary sample of students taking CoI-designed courses," validating the three-dimension framework. In the present study it was administered electronically to MBA students, with additional items added to capture prior online experience and course delivery method.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administration with reference to a specific course the student is currently taking or has most recently completed

Constraints¶

  • The authors note the survey did not fully capture the nature of feedback MBA students sought, and that peer monitoring thoughts and actions seemed to go beyond what is measured by the CoI survey

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students in online and blended courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • assessment of teaching, social, and cognitive presence perceptions
