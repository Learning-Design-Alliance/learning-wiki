95 Phonics Screener for Intervention (progress-monitoring screener)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The 95 Phonics Screener for Intervention is an early literacy screening and progress-monitoring tool used to identify students Below or Well Below Benchmark and to target instruction. In the California implementation, students were tested with the screener every three weeks as part of the implementation, giving educators the results needed to target specific skill gaps with the Lesson Library.
