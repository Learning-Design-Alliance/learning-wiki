# IXL Math personalized learning program with Real-Time Diagnostic and IXL Analytics

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
IXL Math is "a comprehensive personalized teaching and learning program supporting math instruction from PreK through Calculus." Its curriculum includes "thousands of adaptive skills aligned to state standards, popular textbooks, and assessments, as well as video tutorials, classroom engagement and motivation tools, games, and more." The Real-Time Diagnostic identifies each student's grade-level proficiency in key strands and builds an action plan with next steps, while IXL Analytics provides reports on student learning and growth at district, school, classroom, and individual student levels. It runs on Windows, Mac OS, Chrome OS, iPad and iPhone, Android devices, and Fire tablets.
