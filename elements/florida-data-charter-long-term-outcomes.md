## Description
The study uses administrative data from Florida to link students' charter high school attendance with later outcomes: high school graduation, college enrollment, college persistence, and earnings in adulthood. The authors state, "Using data from Florida, we first confirm previous research (Booker et al., 2011)" before extending the analysis to outcomes "not previously studied in research on charter schools." The dataset enables estimating charter effects on attainment and earnings rather than test scores alone.
