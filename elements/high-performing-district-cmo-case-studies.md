# Case studies of three high-performing districts and one high-performing CMO

## Description
The report developed case studies of organizations identified as high-performing: "Metropolitan School District of Lawrence Township, St. Charles Parish Public Schools, and Chicago Public Schools" and one CMO, "DSST, previously known as Denver School of Science and Technology." These case studies were one of the study's three activities, alongside a literature review and a correlational analysis, and fed into the report's three cross-cutting themes for district and CMO leaders.
