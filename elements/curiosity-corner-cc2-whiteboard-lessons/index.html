Curiosity Corner 2nd Edition interactive whiteboard lessons delivered as HTML files on a flash drive¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Curiosity Corner 2nd Edition (CC2) provides interactive whiteboard lessons as HTML files on a flash drive, usable directly from the drive without transfer to a school network. Full utilization requires an interactive whiteboard/projector and a computer with speakers in each classroom; the review recommends a PC with Windows 7 or above or a Mac with Mac OS 10.6.8 or above and broadband of at least 1.5 Mbps for updates.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • For full utilization of the CC2 program, an interactive whiteboard/projector as well as a computer with speakers are needed in each classroom

Constraints¶

  • Up to 4GB of hard drive space may be needed to download updated lesson files
  • Curiosity Corner can also be used without technology

Target Learners¶

  • prekindergarten children in classrooms using CC2 lessons

Target Learning Goals¶

  • delivery of the program's literacy and language activities via interactive whiteboard
