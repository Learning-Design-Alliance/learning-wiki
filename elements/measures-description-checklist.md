# Checklist 1: describing the study's measures

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The first of the brief's five checklists helps researchers "provide complete information describing (1) their study's measures." It prompts authors to report what measures were used in a study so readers can understand and evaluate them. It is one component of the brief's overall five-checklist reporting framework and is intended for use when writing or reviewing the methods section of an education research report.
