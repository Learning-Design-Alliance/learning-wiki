# Reading Apprenticeship® teacher professional development program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Reading Apprenticeship® is a professional development program that trains teachers to model reading comprehension strategies and help students practice these strategies in their classrooms, with the goal of improving literacy and social-emotional outcomes. The teacher acts as the "master reader" who "models comprehension strategies and helps students (the 'reading apprentices') practice these strategies." Professional development is delivered through courses such as Reading Apprenticeship® Essentials I and II plus on-site or online coaching, in several packages (RAAL, RAISE, iRAISE, RAAD) implemented over one school year per cohort.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional development courses (Essentials I and II) plus follow-up coaching and supports such as professional learning community meetings or teacher leader meetings
#### Constraints
- The program is usually implemented along with a school-selected literacy curriculum

### Target Learners
- Middle and high school students in English, mathematics, science, and social studies classrooms

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension strategies
- Literacy skills across content areas
