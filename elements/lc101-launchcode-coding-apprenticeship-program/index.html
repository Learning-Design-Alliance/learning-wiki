LC101: LaunchCode's free coding course and paid apprenticeship program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 8 claims (8 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

Description¶

LC101 is LaunchCode's flagship alternative STEM pathway: a part-time, evening program combining "a free 20-week coding course" and "a paid apprenticeship at a local employer that can last 12-52 weeks," with a portfolio-building lift-off phase between them. Courses cover a JavaScript unit on foundational programming and a Java or C# unit on web applications. Apprenticeship pay subsidizes the program, making it free for all students.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Admitted students must express interest in a coding career, have time for an estimated 15 hours/week of coursework, and demonstrate proficiency on the HackerRank test

Constraints¶

  • Apprenticeship pay is lower than permanent full-time roles, which leads some skilled students to exit before completing the apprenticeship

Target Learners¶

  • Adults with nontraditional backgrounds seeking coding careers, including women and minoritized racial/ethnic groups

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Foundational programming concepts in JavaScript and web application development in Java or C#

Claims¶

  • Black students are more likely to complete the apprenticeship component of LC101 when accounting for all other factors, suggesting alternative STEM programs may represent vehicles for racial equity in STEM [+W]
  • HackerRank test scores and prior coding experience are significantly associated with increased odds of persistence across both the coursework and apprenticeship phases of the LC101 program [+W]
  • Qualitative interviews identify prior coding interest, time resources, course pacing, and financial constraints as key experiential factors shaping LC101 persistence [+W]
  • Educational attainment is not significantly associated with persistence in either the coursework or apprenticeship phase of the LC101 program [+W]
  • Finding another job (29.1%) and low apprenticeship pay (13.9%) are the largest barriers to completing the LC101 apprenticeship phase [+W]
  • Time constraints (47.3%) and course difficulty (27.7%) are the two largest barriers to completing the LC101 coursework phase [+W]
  • Mentorship timing has divergent effects on LC101 persistence: post-application mentorship increases course completion odds while pre-application mentorship decreases persistence across both phases [+W]
  • Interest alignment is highest among course completers while economic motivations (higher pay, better benefits, increased stability) are highest among non-completers [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Provide mentorship and outreach support after program entry to sustain persistence in alternative STEM programs
