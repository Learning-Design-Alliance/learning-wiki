Faculty-authored OER digital textbook and equitized syllabus template at BMCC¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

At Borough of Manhattan Community College, more than 20 instructors joined the core team to equitize syllabi and develop culturally responsive open educational resources for Speech 100. The report states "The faculty-authored OER digital textbook was developed for use in their gateway course, Speech 100," motivated by financial accessibility and support for trauma-informed pedagogy focused on equity, inclusion, and racial justice. The project leader also created an equitized syllabus template shared with all faculty via Microsoft Teams to standardize across sections.

Context¶

Requirements¶
