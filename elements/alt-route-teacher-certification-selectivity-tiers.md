# Alternative teacher certification routes compared in the IES studies: highly selective (Teach For America, Teaching Fellows) and less selective routes

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 4 studies (3 causal, 1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The brief organizes two IES studies around a selectivity distinction among alternative teacher routes. Highly selective routes are Teach For America and the Teaching Fellows programs, while "less selective alternative routes that accept nearly all applicants" form the other tier; both are compared against traditional routes on student test scores. The selectivity tier is the organizing axis for the reported findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers entering via the alternative routes being evaluated, compared with teachers from traditional routes
#### Constraints
- Findings summarized cover elementary teaching, secondary math, and the named programs only

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in disadvantaged schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Student achievement in elementary and secondary math subjects
