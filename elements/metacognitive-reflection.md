# Metacognitive Reflection

## Description
Metacognitive reflection is the element in which learners examine how they thought, what strategies they used, where they were confused, and what they should do next. It is useful when the goal is to make reflection more explicitly about thinking and strategy, not just about feelings or outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A recent task or reasoning process to analyze**
- **Prompts that focus on strategy, monitoring, or next steps**
#### Constraints
- **Generic reflection prompts can blur metacognition into vague self-report**

### Target Learning Goals
- Improve self-monitoring, strategy choice, and adaptive revision.
