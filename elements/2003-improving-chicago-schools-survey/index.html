2003 Improving Chicago's Schools survey¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A biannual survey administered in spring 2003 in which "more than 101,000 students, teachers, and principals across the Chicago public school system participated." Students reported on school experiences, attitudes, and activities, while teachers and principals reported on instruction, professional development, and working conditions. The survey contained 542 teacher items and 191 student items, and data from schools meeting response-rate criteria were compiled into 412 school-specific confidential reports for principals, teachers, and Local School Councils.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • At least 42 percent of teachers or 50 percent of students must respond for a school to receive a report
  • At least seven students or seven teachers must respond to a measure's items for a valid reading

Constraints¶

  • Reports show each school against demographically similar schools and the system as a whole, using 2002 indicators for comparability

Target Learners¶

  • Chicago Public Schools students in elementary and high schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • school climate, instruction, professional community, and technology conditions relevant to student learning

Affordances¶

  • Five Essential Supports Student Learning Framework

  • Consortium school survey report system with 53 measures, demographic comparison groups, and time trends
