Emerging Technology Adoption Framework question-and-criteria instrument¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A living-document instrument from Digital Promise and Educator CIRCLS that structures edtech evaluation as objectives, criteria, and stakeholder-specific questions across initial evaluation, adoption, and post-adoption stages, plus a guidelines and curated resources section (equity, research-based, privacy, and teaching-with-emerging-technologies resources). It is "a living document to help guide evaluation and procurement processes — it is intended to evolve and inspire discussions."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Feedback from users is sought to inform future iterations of the guidance

Constraints¶

  • Not all questions apply in every context

Target Learners¶

  • PK-12 education community: leaders, technology specialists, teachers, students, families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • informed evaluation and procurement decisions for emerging technologies

Related Elements¶

  • CIRCLS community hub for NSF emerging-technology teaching and learning research
  • K-12 Digital Equity Framework Self-Assessment tool

Examples¶

  • During adoption, determine professional learning needs and secure sustainable funding for training before proceeding
  • During initial evaluation, scrutinize what data is collected, how it is stored and shared, and whether it is extraneous
  • During initial evaluation, investigate legal compliance, equity, accessibility, and context-specific barriers
  • During post-adoption, monitor fidelity of use, examine equity patterns, and share strengths and challenges with developers
  • Prepare and test technology before launch, scaffold the learning curve, and leverage tech-savvy students as peer resources
