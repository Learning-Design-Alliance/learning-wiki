## Description
The empirical basis of the brief is a large NWEA dataset covering "162,000 eighth-grade students across 22 states." The dataset supports analysis of both school-level Algebra offerings and individual student placement into the course, enabling equity comparisons by poverty, geography, and race/ethnicity.
