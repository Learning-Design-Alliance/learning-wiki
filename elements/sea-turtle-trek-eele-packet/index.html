Sea Turtle Trek Environmental Education Learning Experience packet¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 124-page educator's activity packet developed by a park superintendent for Hammocks Beach State Park, providing "environmental education through a series of hands-on activities geared to Hammocks Beach State Park." It targets grades 6-8, meets North Carolina standard course of study objectives, and includes an introduction to reptiles, a vocabulary list, scheduling worksheet, parental permission form, evaluation forms, and appendices with sea turtle fact sheets and conservation information.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators should photocopy and prepare provided materials such as story sheets, worksheets, and picture cards before each activity

Constraints¶

  • Designed to be reproduced for use in North Carolina classrooms; other uses require crediting the N.C. Division of Parks and Recreation

Target Learners¶

  • Middle-grade students (grades 6-8) in North Carolina

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sea turtle life cycle and adaptations
  • Taxonomy and dichotomous keys
  • Endangered species and human impact
  • Resource management

Claims¶

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Cooperative adaptation-card sorting to compare two turtle species
  • Dichotomous key practice with sea turtle picture cards
  • Predator/prey simulation game on the beach for hatchling survival
  • Analyzing real park nesting data to teach resource management
