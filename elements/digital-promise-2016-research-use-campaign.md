# Digital Promise 2016 ed-tech research-use campaign and scoring rubric

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
In summer 2016 Digital Promise ran a campaign asking ed-tech companies to share how they use research to inform product development and evaluate efficacy. Companies submitted in three categories; 53 companies submitted in total. Researchers at Teachers College, Columbia University and Digital Promise scored responses with a rubric assessing three main areas: rigor and appropriateness of research methods, whether the company worked with or employed a researcher, and whether it shares research publicly, with multiple reviewers per application and averaged scores.
