Early Reader Skills Scaffold (ERSS): sequenced heavily scaffolded activities for early and pre-readers¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Early Reader Skills Scaffold is a series of practice activities given to students categorized as early readers or pre-readers. Each activity is relatively short, featuring 4-7 targeted items, builds on previous activities, and repeats words and sounds to help students feel successful. The sequence, designed with Dr. Katie Pace Miles and tested with kindergarten and grade 1 classes, moves from letter/sound introduction through Letterflies, Up and down, Elkonin box, Change one part, word lists, and easy decoding sentences.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Categorization of students as early readers or pre-readers by the system or a teacher; interventions range from repeating and modeling to full interventions or encouragement to try again

Constraints¶

  • Designed and student-tested in kindergarten and grade 1 classes

Target Learners¶

  • kindergarten and grade 1 early readers and pre-readers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • letter-sound correspondence
  • phonemic awareness
  • word analysis
  • decoding CVC words
  • sentence reading

Claims¶

  • Automated speech recognition tutoring supports early reading in three areas: word identification, attention to meaning, and motivation [+W]
  • An intelligent reading tutor used 20 minutes a day offered time efficiencies over conventional human tutoring of 30 or more minutes a day [+W]
  • Word recognition fluency significantly predicted reading comprehension in grades 1-3, with rate becoming the stronger predictor by third grade [+W]
