ECoach web-based coaching platform with Exam Playbook for large gateway courses¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ECoach is a web-based coaching platform that gives students tips about how to approach their course, personalized performance feedback, and success tools. The article states "ECoach helps students learn by prioritizing the most important tasks for each week, tracks their progress on grades and study habits, and provides tailored strategies to help them improve their grade." Student-facing features include the exam playbook, grade calculator, and 'Name that scenario'; faculty-facing features include tailored messages, texts, and emails plus classroom survey and research tools. It is used in introductory statistics, chemistry, biology, and physics, and the report presents it as technology mediating support for students' metacognitive and self-regulation practices.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Faculty-facing configuration, such as assigning grade cutoffs and choosing course resources listed in the exam playbook

Constraints¶

  • The exam playbook was tested as a stand-alone support before being incorporated into ECoach; a correlational study found significant positive relationships between use of specific ECoach features and final course grade, which is not causal evidence

Target Learners¶

  • students in high-enrollment first-year introductory courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • metacognition and self-regulated learning, including strategic resource use before exams

Claims¶

  • The exam playbook intervention showed no statistically significant treatment-effect differences by gender, race, class standing, or prior performance [+W]
  • A strategic resource-use exam playbook intervention raised final course grades in introductory statistics across two randomized cohorts, with a dosage effect for completing it twice [+W]
