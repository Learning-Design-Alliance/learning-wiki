# Building Decision Skills ethics curriculum for middle and high school students

## Description
Building Decision Skills is a character education curriculum developed by the Institute for Global Ethics, first published in 1995, that "aims to raise middle and high school students' awareness of ethics, help them gain practical experience in developing core values, and give them practical strategies for dealing with ethical dilemmas." It consists of 10 lessons using readings, handouts, and overheads, with small-group activities, class discussions, homework, and schoolwide components such as seminars and assemblies. The curriculum costs $100 and includes a teacher's guide, lesson plans, the book How Good People Make Choices, and an audiocassette overview.
