# Edpuzzle interactive video-learning platform

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Edpuzzle is an interactive learning platform that turns passive video viewing into active learning by letting teachers weave questions, commentary, and engaging elements into videos, slides, and quizzes at precise moments. The article describes it as a tool that "transforms how educators deliver video-based instruction by turning passive viewing into active learning experiences", usable across grade levels and subjects, with viewing analytics, LMS integrations, and compatibility with flipped, distance, and face-to-face instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need web-browser or iOS/Android app access; the platform supports Windows, Mac OS, Chromebooks, iPads/Tablets, and cellular devices
#### Constraints
- No additional staff is required; schools and districts manage their own usage

### Target Learners
- Elementary through high school students across subject areas

### Target Learning Goals
- Content presentation and comprehension checking in video-based instruction
