# Response redundancy index of relational congruence based on information theory

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A quantitative index operationalizing relational congruence as patterning in verbal interaction, drawing on Shannon and Weaver's mathematical theory of communication. Entropy (H), maximum entropy (Hmax), and relative entropy (RE = H/Hmax) are computed over joint therapist-client response pairs, and redundancy (R = 1 - RE) serves as the primary index: "the last index of response redundancy served as the primary index response patterning/structure and so of relational congruence/incongruence." In the study it was computed per session for three VRM role dimensions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Transcribed verbal utterances coded with a verbal response taxonomy (Stiles' VRM) to yield response transition probabilities
#### Constraints
- The authors state the measure is inherently a function of the response categories studied, and its lack of relation with the WAI suggests redundancy may be a poor measure of relationship congruence

### Target Learners
- adult psychotherapy clients in time-limited therapy

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring relational congruence and relational change in dyadic interaction
