eMINTS professional development program for middle schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

eMINTS (enhancing Missouri's Instructional Networked Teaching Strategies) is a professional development approach "designed to promote inquiry-based learning, high-quality lesson design, and technology-rich learning environments, as well as to build community among teachers and students." Classrooms have interactive whiteboards, LCD projectors, and a 1:1 student-to-computer ratio. Professional development targets principals, technology coordinators, and classroom teachers in middle schools, with a strong emphasis on principals leading implementation and conducting teacher observations.

Target Learning Goals¶

  • inquiry-based learning

Claims¶

  • eMINTS produces a statistically significant positive effect on state math measures (ES +0.15) in a 3-year rural Missouri study [+W]
  • eMINTS shows no differences in reading outcomes compared with traditional control schools [~W]
  • Brandt et al. (2013) finds a statistically significant positive effect of eMINTS on seventh- and eighth-grade mathematics achievement [+W]
  • Martin et al. (2009) finds indeterminate effects of eMINTS on both mathematics and literacy achievement in grades 4–5 [~W]
  • Both included studies report high-fidelity implementation of eMINTS [~W]
