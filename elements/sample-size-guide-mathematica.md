# Sample Size Guide: a phase-organized planning document for education solution developers

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Sample Size Guide is a published planning document from Mathematica, prepared for the Gates Foundation as part of a project on middle years math teaching and learning solutions. It "provides an overview of the sample size considerations for each of the evidence-building phases", covering Design the Solution, Refine the Solution, Assess for Early Evidence of Success, and Validate Effectiveness. It is one of a suite of tools supporting grantees developing, refining, and scaling evidence-based solutions for students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizations use the guide as they begin recruitment and planning for measurement and evaluation activities.
#### Constraints
- For Phase 3 and Phase 4 studies, the guide is a starting point and specific sample sizes should be determined with a research partner.

### Target Learners
- students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty

### Target Learning Goals
- improving educational outcomes through evidence-based programs, products, or practices
