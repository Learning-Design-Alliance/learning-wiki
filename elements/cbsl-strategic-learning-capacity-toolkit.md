# Toolkit of guides and templates for launching and sustaining an intermediary-led cohort of strategic learning grantees

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A toolkit published by Mathematica for facilitators of strategic learning initiatives. It is meant to help a facilitator "launch and sustain an intermediary-led cohort of strategic learning grantees" and "includes guides on designing and facilitating meetings and templates for theories of change, meeting design, data collection, and data synthesis." All resources are free to use and adaptable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator of a strategic learning initiative to use the document with a cohort of grantees
#### Constraints
- Resources are free to use and adaptable to meet the needs of users

### Target Learners
- K-12 education grantee organizations building strategic learning capacity

### Target Learning Goals
- Equity-centered strategic learning: goal setting, hypothesis testing, and using information to improve organizational models
