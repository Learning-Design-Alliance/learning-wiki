CRAFT: Classroom-Ready Resources About AI For Teaching¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

CRAFT stands for "Classroom-Ready Resources About AI For Teaching" and is described as "a co-design initiative from the Stanford Graduate School of Education." It provides AI literacy resources for educators, with resources currently being developed and more to be added, hosted at craft.stanford.edu. It serves as a concrete support for building the AI literacy the presentation frames as foundational.
