Reading Recovery® one-on-one tailored tutoring program for grade 1 students with low literacy achievement¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 6 studies (4 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), q2–q3 · 2 of 6 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Reading Recovery® is a supplemental intervention providing daily 30-minute one-on-one tutoring to grade 1 students with low literacy achievement over 12 to 20 weeks. The report describes it as "tailoring the content of each lesson to each student based on observations and analyses of the student strengths and weaknesses from prior lessons." Trained teachers incorporate instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, fluency, comprehension, writing, oral language, and motivation depending on student needs. No lesson plans are provided; teachers use Reading Recovery® forms to record observations and design individualized daily lessons.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Specially trained Reading Recovery® teachers deliver tutoring daily in 30-minute one-on-one sessions over 12 to 20 weeks
  • Teachers complete a full academic year of Reading Recovery® training with graduate credit under a registered teacher leader
  • Students are screened before the intervention using the Observation Survey of Early Literacy Achievement

Constraints¶

  • Reading Recovery® does not provide lesson plans; teachers must design individualized lessons themselves
  • Students are pulled out of their regular non-literacy instruction during lessons

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grade 1 (typically 6 or 7 years old) with low literacy achievement

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading skills
  • Writing skills
  • Early literacy foundations including phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, fluency, and comprehension

Claims¶

  • Reading Recovery positively impacts grade 1 literacy achievement immediately after the intervention, with moderate evidence from one randomized controlled trial [+M]
  • Reading Recovery improves receptive communication skills immediately after the intervention, with promising evidence from one small quasi-experimental study [+W]
  • Reading Recovery improves writing conventions three years after the intervention, but the effect is significant only against one of the two comparison groups [+W]
  • Reading Recovery improves writing productivity immediately after the intervention, with promising evidence from one small quasi-experimental study [+W]
  • Reading Recovery shows uncertain effects on general academic achievement ten years after the intervention [~W]
  • Reading Recovery shows uncertain effects on mathematics achievement three years after the intervention [~W]
