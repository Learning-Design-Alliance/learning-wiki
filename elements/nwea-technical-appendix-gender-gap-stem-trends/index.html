Technical appendix document accompanying the gender-gap STEM trends study¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A technical brief published by NWEA in May 2025, described as "the accompanying technical appendix to the research study" on gender achievement gaps in 8th grade STEM skills. It documents the data and methods behind the study, "leveraging data from three national assessments, as well as enrollment in Algebra". It is listed under the topics COVID-19 & schools, Equity, Growth, and Math & STEM.

Claims¶

  • The study examines post-COVID trends in gender gaps in 8th grade STEM skills using three national assessments and Algebra enrollment data [+W]
  • Gender gap trends in 8th-grade STEM were estimated from five years of TIMSS, NAEP, and MAP Growth data spanning the pandemic [+M]
  • A two-million-student MAP Growth sample was used to test whether gender gaps widened among low- and high-achieving students [+M]
  • Boys regain the advantage in middle school STEM skills in post-COVID gender achievement gap trends [+M]
