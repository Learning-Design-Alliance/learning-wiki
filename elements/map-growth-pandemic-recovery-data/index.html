MAP Growth assessment data as the evidence base for national pandemic-recovery monitoring¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MAP Growth is NWEA's assessment product whose nationwide score data underpins this brief's findings on pandemic recovery. The brief is described as sharing "several key findings" "Based on data from 2023-24," covering growth trends, pre-COVID gap widening, months-of-schooling catch-up estimates, and race/ethnicity comparisons. It functions here as the measurement instrument enabling longitudinal recovery monitoring.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires longitudinal MAP Growth test score data comparable to pre-COVID baselines to compute recovery gaps and catch-up estimates

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in U.S. schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading achievement
  • Mathematics achievement

Claims¶

  • The average student needs the equivalent of 4.8 additional months of schooling to catch up in reading and 4.3 months in math [+W]
  • Student growth during 2023-24 fell short of pre-pandemic trends in nearly all grades, continuing stalled pandemic recovery [+W]
  • The gap between pre-COVID and COVID test score averages widened in 2023-24 in nearly all grades, by an average of 36% in reading and 18% in math [+W]
