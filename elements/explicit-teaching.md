# Explicit Teaching

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Explicit teaching is the element in which instructors directly explain, model, and sequence key knowledge or procedures rather than leaving them to be inferred. It is useful when learners need clarity about what to attend to and how to proceed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A clear target concept or procedure**
- **Explanation or modeling that highlights the critical moves**
#### Constraints
- **Explicit teaching without practice or feedback remains incomplete**

### Target Learning Goals
- Accelerate early understanding and reduce ambiguity for novices.
