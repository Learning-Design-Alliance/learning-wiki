# Strategic Instruction Model (SIM) and its Learning Strategies Curriculum (Xtreme Reading, Fusion Reading)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
SIM is "a multi-tiered approach to adolescent literacy" created by the University of Kansas Center for Research on Learning. It emphasizes teaching routines that let teachers organize and present content clearly and give students tools to solve problems independently. All SIM approaches teach decoding, comprehension, and other reading objectives with step-by-step strategies such as word identification, visual imagery, self-questioning, paraphrasing, vocabulary learning, and writing, with mnemonics to help students remember the steps. Targeted versions provide an additional daily reading period through the Learning Strategies Curriculum, disseminated as Xtreme Reading and Fusion Reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content and support teachers willing and able to implement the routines and strategies
- Leadership willing to support implementation, provide coaching time, and create accommodating school schedules
- A Literacy Leadership Team of core content and support teachers and school leadership to guide implementation
#### Constraints
- Targeted versions focus on students reading two to five years below grade level and replace electives such as art, music, or study hall

### Target Learners
- adolescents in secondary schools
- struggling readers two to five years below grade level

### Target Learning Goals
- decoding
- reading comprehension
- vocabulary learning
- writing
- independent problem solving with learning strategies
