Free online growth mindset intervention materials (PERTS and Mindset Kit)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (2 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report identifies freely accessible implementation resources for postsecondary institutions: "Postsecondary institutions can access existing intervention materials for free online at sites such as https://www.perts.net/orientation/cg and https://www.mindsetkit.org. Alternatively, college personnel can adapt intervention materials described or provided in the studies reviewed here." These reduce the equipment-and-materials cost ingredient, which the report assigns to the college as funding source.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Information technology infrastructure and software if the intervention is delivered online

Constraints¶

  • The cost ingredient list is preliminary and not exhaustive, based on information available as of November 2020

Target Learners¶

  • Postsecondary students in intervention-delivering institutions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Growth mindset adoption supporting academic achievement and persistence

Claims¶

  • Aronson et al. (2002) pen-pal growth mindset intervention produced a statistically significant positive effect on quarter GPA (effect size 0.85) [+W]
  • Broda et al. (2018) found indeterminate effects on GPA, full-time enrollment, and credits completed in a large randomized trial [~W]
  • Growth Mindset interventions have no discernible effects on college enrollment and progressing in college [-M]
  • Growth Mindset interventions have potentially positive effects on postsecondary academic achievement (improvement index +13) [+M]
  • Suh et al. (2019) found a positive but non-significant effect on course passing rate (effect size 0.80) and a negative non-significant effect on final exam score [~W]
  • Yeager et al. (2016) and Fink et al. (2018) found indeterminate effects on enrollment, credit completion, and chemistry final exam scores [~W]

Examples¶

  • Growth Mindset Interventions
  • Deliver growth mindset messages once, near the start of college or around first exams, in roughly 30-minute sessions
