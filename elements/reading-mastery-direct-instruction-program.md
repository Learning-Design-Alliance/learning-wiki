# Reading Mastery direct instruction reading program (Reading Mastery Classic K-3 and Reading Mastery Plus K-6)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Reading Mastery is a direct instruction program providing 'explicit, systematic instruction in English language reading,' available as Reading Mastery Classic (levels I and II, grades K-3) and Reading Mastery Plus, an integrated reading-language program for grades K-6. It begins with phonemic awareness and sound-letter correspondence and moves into word and passage reading, vocabulary development, comprehension, and oral reading fluency. Developed by Dr. Siegfried Engelmann as Distar Reading for Project Follow Through, it is distributed by SRA/McGraw-Hill and used with more than 1 million students in one-third of the nation's schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students are grouped by similar reading level, based on program placement tests, and the program includes placement assessments and a continuous monitoring system.
#### Constraints
- Although not designed exclusively for English language learners, Reading Mastery can be used with this group of students.

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-6 learning to read in English, including English language learners

### Target Learning Goals
- Phonemic awareness
- Decoding and word recognition
- Vocabulary development
- Reading comprehension
- Oral reading fluency
