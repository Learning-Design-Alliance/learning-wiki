Technical appendix document detailing sample and methods for the COVID-19 achievement divide brief¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A technical brief published by NWEA Research in November 2022 accompanying the research brief 'The widening achievement divide during COVID-19.' Its stated purpose is to 'share more detailed results and to describe more fully the sample and methods used in the research included in the brief.' It is tagged under the topics COVID-19 & schools and Equity and is authored by Megan Kuhfeld, Meredith Langi, and Karyn Lewis.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Serves as a companion document to the research brief 'The widening achievement divide during COVID-19', providing the methodological detail behind that brief's findings

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in U.S. schools whose reading and math achievement was measured during the COVID-19 pandemic

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding pandemic-related change in the variability and distribution of student reading and math achievement

Claims¶

  • The brief compared pandemic achievement gains to pre-pandemic trends for students low- or high-achieving at the pandemic's start [+W]
  • The brief investigated the degree to which students' reading and math test scores became more variable as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic [+W]
