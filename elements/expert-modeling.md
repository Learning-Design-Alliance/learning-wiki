# Expert Modeling

## Description
Expert modeling is the element in which a skilled performer demonstrates not only what to do, but how an expert notices cues, makes decisions, and checks quality during performance. It is useful when learners need access to expert judgment that would otherwise remain tacit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A task with meaningful expert judgment**
- **Narration or annotation that reveals expert decision points**
#### Constraints
- **Experts may omit tacit moves unless prompted to make them explicit**

### Target Learning Goals
- Make expert reasoning visible and support transfer to learner performance.
