Teacher Team Method for co-taught value-added estimation¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Teacher Team Method is one of three methods the article proposes for estimating teacher value added when students are co-taught. Like the Full Roster Method, it presumes that co-teachers share joint responsibility for the achievement of their shared students. The article explores its properties and compares its empirical estimates with the Full Roster Method, finding the two produce similar results.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires identification of teams of teachers sharing students.

Constraints¶

  • Treats co-teachers as jointly responsible rather than apportioning credit by time spent with each teacher.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students co-taught by multiple teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Accurate estimation of individual teacher effectiveness in evaluated subjects

Claims¶

  • The Teacher Team Method and Full Roster Method produce similar estimates of teacher value added [+W]
  • Teacher-confirmed roster data show levels of co-teaching far beyond what appears in administrative data [~W]
