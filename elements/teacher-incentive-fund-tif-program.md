# Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) performance-based compensation program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program that "supports performance-based compensation systems in high-need schools." Its grant incentives "aim to raise student achievement by improving teacher effectiveness and workforce quality." The program required districts to implement four components, and Mathematica evaluated its implementation and bonus impacts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must implement four required TIF components as a condition of the grant
- Federal grants to districts implementing performance-based compensation systems
- Implementation over multiple school years to assess impacts
#### Constraints
- Applies to high-need schools served by TIF grantees
- Impacts assessed only in the ten evaluation districts after three years of implementation

### Target Learners
- Students in high-need schools in TIF evaluation districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Raise student achievement by improving teacher effectiveness and workforce quality
- Student math achievement
- Student reading achievement
- Educator performance

# Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) performance-based compensation program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program that "provides federal grants to support performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools." The 2010 cohort of grantees was evaluated over four years of implementation, with the third year (2013–2014) covered by this report. Ten evaluation districts implemented pay-for-performance bonuses for educators, and the evaluation measured impacts on educator and student outcomes.
