Maker Learning Self-assessment Rubric¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A self-evaluation instrument (Appendix B) that maker champions used to rate their district's maker learning program in six categories: Faculty and Staff Support; Administration Support; Resources and Budget; Tools, Materials, and Spaces; Assessment and Documentation; and Curricular Integration. In each category respondents rate their district as "beginning," "exploring," "integrating," or "embedded," where at the embedded level making is "a regular part of the day-to-day learning for all students in the school." The rubric was designed from leadership interview goals with curricular integration as the guiding principle.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Completed by the person most responsible for implementing maker learning in the school or district

Constraints¶

  • The report states the assumption that schools progress evenly across all six areas was not shown in the self-assessments, and future research will determine whether the rubric should be re-balanced

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in Maker Promise signer schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Integration of maker learning into regular academic subjects and programming

Claims¶

  • Maker champions rate their programs highest in Tools, Materials, and Spaces, while curricular integration lags behind independent spaces [+W]
  • School leaders developing maker learning programs cite lack of unity around goals, a small research base, lack of academic integration models, and no planning frameworks as key challenges [+W]
