CWPT Learning Management System (CWPT–LMS) software support¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The CWPT Learning Management System is a computer-based version of the peer tutoring program distributed by the Juniper Gardens Children's Project. It is "a system of peer tutoring instruction with computer software support designed to help teachers implement effective instruction, monitor progress, and sustain use of the program over time." Training materials are available on the developer web site and training is available from Juniper Gardens' staff; the materials, including a CD, training, and teacher's manual, cost $245.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher training is needed; training materials are available on the developer web site and training is available from Juniper Gardens' staff

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school students in beginning reading programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading skills practiced through peer tutoring with teacher progress monitoring

Claims¶

  • Greenwood et al. (1993) reported a statistically significant CWPT effect on CTBS-Reading, but the WWC-corrected analysis was not statistically significant [~W]
  • The evidence base for CWPT in beginning reading is small, with no standards-meeting studies of alphabetics, fluency, or comprehension [~W]
  • Severe and differential attrition at grade 4 (68.2% intervention vs. 44.2% comparison) led the WWC to exclude grade-4 outcomes from review [~W]

Examples¶

  • Implement CWPT through daily peer-tutoring sessions with points, teams, and weekly testing
