Fraction Face-Off! small-group tutoring program for at-risk fourth graders¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 4 studies (4 causal), q1–q4 · 4 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Fraction Face-Off! is "an approach to teaching fractions to at-risk fourth graders using small group tutoring." Tutors deliver supplementary lessons of 35 minutes daily for 36 days over a 12-week period, following structured manuals with lesson guides and materials. Content emphasizes "comparing, ordering, placing fractions on number lines, and equivalencies." No technology is required.

Context¶

  • at-risk fourth graders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • fraction comparison, ordering, number-line placement, and equivalencies

Claims¶

  • Fraction Face-Off! improves at-risk fourth graders' fractions performance on a NAEP-derived measure with an average effect size of +0.51 across two studies. [+M]
  • Fraction Face-Off! tutoring can be delivered by either teachers or paraprofessionals with no technology required. [+W]
  • Fraction Face-Off! shows potentially positive effects on fourth-grade students' geometry and measurement outcomes, with a WWC-calculated average effect size of 0.96 and improvement index of +33 [+M]
  • Fraction Face-Off! shows potentially positive effects on general mathematics achievement, with a WWC-calculated effect size of 0.64 and improvement index of +24 on NAEP selected items [+M]
  • Fraction Face-Off! shows potentially positive effects on number and operations outcomes, with a WWC-calculated average effect size of 0.89 and improvement index of +31 [+M]
