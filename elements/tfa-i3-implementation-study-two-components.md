# TFA i3 scale-up implementation study with two components: goal-attainment analysis and program-model fidelity analysis

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The implementation study of TFA's i3 scale-up comprised two components: an analysis of whether TFA met its scale-up goals during the early years of the grant, and an analysis of whether it maintained fidelity to key aspects of its program model as it scaled program activities. The study examined "TFA's scale-up efforts during the first two years of the grant" and produced the goal-attainment and fidelity findings reported in the publication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examines the first two years of the i3 scale-up grant period
#### Constraints
- Covers only the first two years of the grant, not the full scale-up period

### Target Learners
- TFA corps members teaching in partner schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Increasing the number of effective teachers in high-need schools
