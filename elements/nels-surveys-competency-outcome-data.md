# National Education Longitudinal Study (NELS) student and teacher surveys as the data source for competency-outcome analyses

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The National Education Longitudinal Study (NELS) surveys of students and teachers supply the competency indicators and outcome data for this report. The report states: "Drawing on the National Education Longitudinal Study (NELS) surveys of students and teachers, we analyze how postsecondary earnings and the likelihood of attending and completing a postsecondary education program are related to several competencies." Competency indicators include math skills, work habits, leadership skills, teamwork and other sports-related skills, and locus of control.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Competency indicators measured in the NELS student and teacher surveys
#### Constraints
- Findings are observational associations from NELS survey data, not experimental estimates

### Target Learners
- secondary school students in the NELS longitudinal panels

### Target Learning Goals
- academic competencies such as math skills
- nonacademic competencies including work habits, leadership, teamwork, and locus of control
