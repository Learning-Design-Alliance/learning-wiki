NCME 2020 presentation on effort-moderated IRT parameter estimation accuracy¶

Description¶

A presentation delivered at the 2020 National Council on Measurement in Education virtual conference by James Soland and Joseph Rios, listed by NWEA Research under the topics of "Measurement & scaling" and "School & test engagement". Its stated content is new research findings on understanding and managing test-taking disengagement, with the specific study examining parameter estimation accuracy of the effort-moderated IRT model under multiple assumption violations. The page links to the presentation but prints no slides, results, or methods detail.
