# DELICATE checklist for trusted learning analytics

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The DELICATE checklist, presented by Drachsler and Greller (2016) and reviewed in this article, is a practical tool for establishing trusted LA within any data-driven educational organization. The review cites it as an instance of privacy-as-right conceptualization and notes its authors also stressed contextual integrity as important in the age of Big Data. It appears in the review as one of the frameworks offering privacy principles without fully unpacking the privacy concept.

## Design Implications
