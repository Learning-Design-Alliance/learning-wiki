# 508 Compliance guide as an accessibility resource for education dissemination products

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A published guide from the U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences (May 30, 2014), authored by Joanne Pfleiderer, that "offers tips and tools for making materials, including presentations, websites, and multimedia products, accessible." It functions as a reference resource for staff preparing disseminable materials and was "prepared for the Institute of Education Sciences' Regional Education Laboratories."

## Design Implications

### Context
