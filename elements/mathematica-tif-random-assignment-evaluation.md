# Mathematica multi-site random assignment evaluation of TIF pay-for-performance bonuses

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Mathematica's congressionally mandated study, prepared for the U.S. Department of Education's National Center for Education Evaluation, is described as "one of the largest multi-site random assignment evaluations conducted on the impact of pay-for-performance bonuses." It examines TIF implementation for all 2010 grantees and bonus impacts in a subset of 12 districts, with a time frame of 2009-2017.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Congressional mandate; prepared for the U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Evaluation
#### Constraints
- Impact analysis limited to a subset of 12 districts, while implementation is examined for all 2010 grantees

### Target Learners
- Teachers and principals in high-need schools participating in TIF

### Target Learning Goals
- Estimate impacts of pay-for-performance bonuses on teacher effectiveness and student achievement
