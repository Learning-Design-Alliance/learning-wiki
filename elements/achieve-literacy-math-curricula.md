## Description
The Wraparound program delivers systematic, small-group instruction in literacy and math through the AchieveLiteracy and AchieveMath curricula, which the page describes as emphasizing "explicit, research-based strategies to improve students' skills and confidence." Instruction is delivered in a structured environment with a low student-to-teacher ratio, aiming to accelerate academic achievement while fostering a supportive learning atmosphere.
