Literacy Express preschool curriculum¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Literacy Express is a preschool curriculum for three- to five-year-old children "structured around units on oral language, emergent literacy, basic math, science, general knowledge, and socioemotional development." It includes daily individual, small-group, and large-group activities, with three brief daily small-group activities (dialogic reading, phonological awareness, and print knowledge) for groups of three to five children. The package includes a teacher's manual, 10 thematic unit guides, more than 80 thematically linked picture and alphabet books, and phonological awareness activity picture cards, at a cost of $2,300 per classroom package.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers participate in a two-day professional development workshop at the beginning of the school year and in either two additional half-day workshops or one additional full-day workshop.

Constraints¶

  • The research reviewed was based on an earlier version of the curriculum with 11 thematic units; the current version has 10.

Target Learners¶

  • three- to five-year-old preschool children, including typically developing children, children with special needs, and English language learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • oral language
  • emergent literacy
  • basic math
  • science
  • general knowledge
  • socioemotional development

Claims¶

  • Literacy Express shows no discernible effects on cognition and math for preschool children [-W]
  • Literacy Express has positive effects on oral language for preschool children, with significant effects in two of three studies [+W]
  • Literacy Express has positive effects on phonological processing for preschool children, with significant effects in two of three studies [+W]
  • Literacy Express has positive effects on print knowledge for preschool children, with significant effects in two of three studies [+W]
