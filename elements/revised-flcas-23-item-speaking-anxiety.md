# Revised 23-item Foreign Language Classroom Anxiety Scale (FLCAS) questionnaire

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's instrument is a revised version of Horwitz, Horwitz, and Cope's (1986) Foreign Language Classroom Anxiety Scale, described as a questionnaire that "had 23 items which included most of the possible causes of foreign language speaking anxiety." Students selected options on a Likert scale from strongly disagree to strongly agree, and response frequencies were counted to rank the main causes. The article reviews prior administrations reporting Cronbach's alpha values of 0.93 (Horwitz et al., 1986), 0.80 (Aida, 1994), and 0.84 (Hajizadeh, 2013, with 38 Iranian learners).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered as a close-ended questionnaire to learners of the target foreign language
- Scored on a Likert scale from strongly disagree to strongly agree
#### Constraints
- Validity in this study was consulted by three experts in the field rather than empirically re-established

### Target Learners
- EFL learners at English language institutes (ages 18-30)

### Target Learning Goals
- Foreign language speaking proficiency and affective factors in language learning
