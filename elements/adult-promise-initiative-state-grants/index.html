Adult Promise initiative: multiyear Lumina Foundation state grants to engage and support adult learners in postsecondary education¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Adult Promise initiative is a Lumina Foundation grant program, launched in 2017, that provided "multiyear state grants to engage and support adult learners in postsecondary education programs." Mathematica served as its learning and evaluation partner from 2017 to 2021, and this brief draws on the marketing, outreach, and enrollment strategies that grantee states used to engage adults ages 25 to 64.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • States received multiyear grants to engage and support adult learners in postsecondary education programs

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Adult learners ages 25 to 64 returning to postsecondary education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Post-high school credentials and college completion aligned with state workforce goals

Claims¶

  • The brief reports that adult learners' priorities differ from traditional-age students', making appropriate messages critical to engaging them [+W]
  • The brief reports that adult learners often need high-touch support such as navigators through enrollment and financial aid [+W]
  • The brief reports that online tools can engage prospective adult learners and make enrollment convenient given work and family demands [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Provide navigator support through enrollment and financial aid, plus continued support after enrollment
  • Use marketing, outreach, and enrollment strategies tailored to adult learners' work and family demands
