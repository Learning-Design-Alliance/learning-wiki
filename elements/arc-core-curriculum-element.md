# ARC Core K-12 literacy curriculum with knowledge-building units

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
ARC Core is a K-12 English and Spanish literacy curriculum in which students develop agency and expertise "through extensive reading, writing, research, and analysis". It is organized as knowledge-building units integrating science, social studies, and literature content while developing literacy skills and vocabulary, with "four 9-week units per grade". Teachers receive new topically aligned materials each unit while instructional structures stay consistent across the year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implemented by classroom teachers during an integrated literacy block; the evaluated kindergarten implementation replaced regular literacy instruction for 120 minutes per day for the full school year
#### Constraints
- Teachers reported that full implementation, including student learning centers and individual meetings, required additional classroom support

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten students (evaluated); K-12 students as designed scope

### Target Learning Goals
- Literacy skills and vocabulary development
- Knowledge building in science, social studies, and literature content

## Claims

- [The evidence base for ARC Core's kindergarten efficacy is one randomized controlled trial covering 792 students, with an average effect size of +0.14 and a strong evidence rating.](../claims/arc-core-evidence-base-one-rct-792-students.md) [+M]
- [In a multi-site randomized controlled trial, kindergarten students in ARC Core classrooms scored significantly higher on AIMSWeb Letter Naming Fluency than control students (ES = +0.28).](../claims/arc-core-letter-naming-fluency-es-028.md) [+M]
- [In the same randomized controlled trial, kindergarten students in ARC Core classrooms scored significantly higher on WMRT Passage Comprehension than control students (ES = +0.16).](../claims/arc-core-passage-comprehension-es-016.md) [+M]
- [Teachers implementing ARC Core in the Philadelphia study reported positive effects on student engagement, learning, and behavior, but noted full implementation required extra classroom support and occasional supplementing of materials.](../claims/arc-core-teacher-reported-engagement-and-implementation-challenges.md) [+W]
- [Meta-analyses consistently validate extensive reading benefits across languages and populations, with program length as a moderator](../claims/meta-analyses-validate-extensive-reading-benefits-length-moderates.md) [+M]
