# DaisyQuest software bundle for phonological awareness instruction

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 9 claims (8 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
DaisyQuest is a software bundle offering computer-assisted instruction in phonological awareness for children aged three to seven, framed in a fairy tale about searching for a friendly dragon named Daisy. It comprises two components (DaisyQuest and Daisy's Castle) plus the Undersea Challenge adaptive test, and "uses graphics and story lines to engage children in the learning process." Activities teach rhyming, matching beginning, middle, and ending sounds, blending phonemes, and counting sounds in words, with digitized and synthetic speech, tutorials, feedback, and statistical reports for parents and teachers.
