# Reading interventions for grades 4–9 as defined by the WWC practice guide

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The guide defines reading interventions for students in grades 4–9 as comprising two forms: "supplemental programs provided in addition to regular classroom English language arts instruction as part of a multi-tiered system of support (MTSS)" and "reading courses or electives that are provided to students in middle or high school." Elementary schools tend to provide supplemental interventions while middle and high schools tend to provide elective reading courses, though policies vary by state, district, and school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applies to students in grades 4–9 receiving reading support beyond regular classroom English language arts instruction
#### Constraints
- Delivery form varies by school level and by state, district, and school policies

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 4–9

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading proficiency
