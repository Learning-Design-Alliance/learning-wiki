AIASA student industrial arts clubs¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The American Industrial Arts Student Association (AIASA) is a national student club organization officially organized by the AIAA at its 27th annual conference in Tulsa, Oklahoma, in March 1965. Its motto is "Learning to Live in_a Technical World." The clubs familiarize students with industrial and technical fields through activities such as industrial tours, clubwork, and leadership conferences. The AIASA president's address describes the clubs as a feeder program for the profession, reporting membership figures from 1,715 in 1967 to a peak of 5,219 in 1969, and argues the clubs depend on teacher sponsorship by AIAA members.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Guidance and direction from teachers who serve as club leaders and sponsors

Constraints¶

  • The address states AIASA can never function without AIAA support and that growth historically fluctuated with the presence of a student club coordinator

Target Learners¶

  • High school students in industrial arts courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Familiarity with industrial and technical fields, leadership development, and improved attitudes toward industrial arts
