Splash and Bubbles for Parents app with three sections (Dive In, Ocean Guide, Activities) and a sync feature¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (4 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Splash and Bubbles for Parents app is a second-screen digital resource providing conversation starter ideas, video clips, ocean animal and environment information, and hands-on activities for families to explore ocean science together while watching the Splash and Bubbles show. It includes three main sections: Dive In (with Let's Talk About It conversation starters, We'll Learn About science themes, Characters, and Clips), Ocean Guide (Featured, Characters, Environments, Topics, and Clips), and Activities (hands-on learning with a Snap It photo component). A sync feature uses ACR technology to automatically sync to the episode being watched. The station study examined what supports families need to use it.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Families need orientation to the app's features and how to access them, as the field study highlighted parents' need for support around ways to engage with and use the app

Constraints¶

  • The sync feature requires using a second screen; families watching the show and using the app on the same device cannot use it

Target Learners¶

  • Parents and caregivers of children aged 4-7

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Ocean science concepts and science talk between parents and children

Claims¶

  • Most families used the Activities section and rated it highly helpful, with many completing two or more activities [+W]
  • Nearly all participating families reported using the Splash and Bubbles for Parents app and found it helpful for supporting children's science learning [+W]
  • Families used multiple sections of the Splash and Bubbles for Parents app, with Dive In the most used and Let's Talk About It conversation starters rated most helpful within it [+W]
  • Families showed widespread variability in which Ocean Guide and Activities components they found most helpful, often citing child age and interest [~W]
  • Attrition in the station study was higher than expected, and partial-data families rated app components slightly lower than complete-data families [~W]
  • Families who used the app's features more extensively internalized its purpose and reported stronger changes in joint media engagement and science attitudes [+W]
  • Sync feature uptake declined between survey and interview, with non-users citing unawareness, technical difficulties, or single-device viewing [~W]
