REDI preschool program combining social-emotional learning with early literacy activities¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

REDI (Research Based, Developmentally Informed) is a preschool program "designed primarily to enhance executive functions and school readiness among disadvantaged students." It delivers the Preschool PATHS social-emotional learning curriculum alongside "an interactive storybook reading and retelling approach, along with phonological awareness training and alphabet center activities," and is taught by classroom teachers with inservice training and weekly mentoring.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Taught by classroom teachers
  • Teachers receive two to four days of inservice plus weekly mentoring from local consultants

Constraints¶

  • Designed primarily for disadvantaged students in preschool settings

Target Learners¶

  • disadvantaged preschool students in Head Start centers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • executive functions
  • school readiness
  • social-emotional learning
  • early language and literacy

Claims¶

  • REDI improves preschool language and literacy outcomes with a mean end-of-preschool effect size of +0.23 [+W]
  • REDI effects largely fade by end of kindergarten, with significant positive effects only on TOWRE Phonetic Decoding Efficiency [-W]

Related Elements¶

  • Preschool PATHS Curriculum promoting self-regulation and emotional control

Examples¶

  • Engage parents through home-learning packets with videos, tips, and activity suggestions
  • Support REDI implementation with brief inservice training plus weekly coaching from local consultants
