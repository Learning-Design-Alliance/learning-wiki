# Self-Explanation

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 1 causal), `q2`–`q4` · 1 of 3 report an effect size

## Description
Self-explanation is the element in which learners explain to themselves why something is true, why a step works, or how a solution fits the problem. It is useful when the goal is to deepen understanding beyond surface completion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- **A prompt that asks learners to explain reasoning, not just give an answer**
- **Enough time to articulate and refine the explanation**
#### Constraints
- **Unguided self-explanations can stay superficial unless prompts are specific**

### Target Learning Goals
- Strengthen conceptual understanding, integration, and transfer.
