Matched-comparison evaluation design for the Kauffman School impact study¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The evaluation identified a group of students in other Kansas City district and charter schools who had similar demographic characteristics and achievement at the end of 4th grade, then compared their outcomes to Kauffman students in grades 5 through 10. The report describes this as the method "to measure the impact of the Kauffman School on its students." This matched-comparison design supports the reported achievement and attendance impact findings.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A comparison group of students in other Kansas City district and charter schools matched on demographic characteristics and end-of-4th-grade achievement

Constraints¶

  • Impacts are estimated relative to other Kansas City public schools, not to a randomly assigned control group

Target Learners¶

  • students in grades 5 through 10 in Kansas City, Missouri public and charter schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mathematics achievement growth
  • English language arts achievement growth
  • science achievement growth
  • student attendance
