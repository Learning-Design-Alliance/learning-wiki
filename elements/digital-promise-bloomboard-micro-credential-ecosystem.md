# Digital Promise micro-credential ecosystem on the BloomBoard platform

## Description
The Digital Promise micro-credentialing ecosystem is "currently housed on a platform powered by the ed tech startup BloomBoard." Micro-credentials are described as mini-certifications in which K-12 educators "present evidence of their ability to demonstrate competency in teaching or leading school improvement and receive quick feedback from trained reviewers." As of June 2017, DP and its 40 content partners had developed over 300 micro-credentials, and the platform hosted more than 5,000 submissions from educators in approximately 550 districts. The report also describes platform functions: navigation from selection to submission, assignment of submissions, reviewer notes, data gathering, and paywall transactions.
