# Goal-Based Scenarios (GBS)

## Description
A learning model that engages students in realistic, goal-driven scenarios where they apply knowledge and skills to achieve specific outcomes. GBS structures learning around a mission, where learners must make decisions, solve problems, and interact with simulated challenges. The model provides just-in-time information, reflection opportunities, and feedback loops to reinforce learning.

## Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires well-structured, interactive scenario design and facilitator support.
#### Constraints
- Time-intensive to create realistic, immersive scenarios; Requires good facilitator guidance.

## Design

### Sequence
<!-- Steps with links to elements -->
1. Learners are presented with a realistic mission or goal.
 2. They explore relevant resources and tools as needed.
 3. Learners engage in iterative problem-solving and decision-making.
 4. Feedback and reflection support deeper learning.
