# Exploratory laboratory program pattern: hands-on experiences leading to tentative career decisions

> **Pattern** · [All patterns](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A course-level pattern in which middle school students explore career fields through varied experiential modes before selecting secondary programs. The guide states that "Student exploration and tentative decisions will be made through experiences gained through discussion, research, field trips, role-playing situations, mock industries, and 'hands-on' activities." After exploring one or more industrial arts programs, students can better select a job family in the secondary program to further develop tentative career decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Laboratory and classroom experiences across the cluster's areas
- Programs designed for thirty-six (36) weeks with more material than can normally be covered
#### Constraints
- Career decisions at this level are explicitly tentative, to be further developed in the secondary industrial arts program

### Target Learners
- Middle school students in grades 6 through 9

### Target Goals
- Discovering technical abilities and interests
- Making tentative career decisions
