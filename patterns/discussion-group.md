# Discussion Group

## Description
A structured forum where learners discuss a topic, guided by clear goals and moderation strategies, to promote deep engagement with ideas.

## Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a discussion platform (asynchronous or synchronous) and a guiding question
#### Constraints
- Can become off-topic; May be dominated by a few voices; Needs facilitation for deep engagement
#### Grain Size
Lesson, Unit

### Target Goals
<!-- Link to claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Argumentation, critical thinking, co-construction of knowledge

### Target Learners
<!-- Link to claims: [[claims/claim-slug]] -->
- Students in online, blended, or hybrid courses; Adult learners in professional development settings
