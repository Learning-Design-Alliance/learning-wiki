# Practice with Simulated People

> **Pattern** · [All patterns](index.md)

## Description and scope

Practice in which the learner performs a professional or interpersonal task with someone who plays a role: a standardized patient (a trained actor), a virtual patient, a simulated client, customer or colleague, avatar students in a mixed-reality classroom simulation, or a role-play partner played by a large language model. The decisions it covers are which performance to rehearse, how realistic and how responsive the simulated person is, how many attempts a learner gets, how feedback and debriefing follow each attempt, and how practice transfers to real patients, clients, students or customers.
