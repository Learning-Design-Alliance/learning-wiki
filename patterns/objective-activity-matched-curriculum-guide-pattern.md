# Performance-objective-driven curriculum pattern pairing each stated objective with matched student activities

> **Pattern** · [All patterns](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide states six lettered performance objectives (A through F) and then organizes its entire activity section objective by objective, so each activity serves a stated objective. Objectives move from examining cultural movements given theoretical background, through identifying movement characteristics in literature, art, and music, to drawing inferences about influences on contemporary American culture and predicting future developments. For example, objective B reads: "Given selected definitions of Realism and Naturalism, the student will investigate characteristics of thesedefinitions in literature, art, and music."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each activity must be traceable to one of the stated performance objectives
#### Constraints
- Objectives are phrased as student performances given specified background input such as definitions or theories

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Target Goals
- identifying characteristics of artistic and literary movements
- drawing inferences about cultural influences
- predicting future developments in literature, art, and music
