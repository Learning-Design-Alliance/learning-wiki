# Interactive oral grammar exercises with five defining qualities (Comeau)

> **Pattern** · [All patterns](index.md)

## Description
The bibliography describes, citing Comeau (1992), an interactive oral grammar exercise defined as having five qualities: "be communicative, be meaningful, provide a limited choice, be expressive, and be integrated with other types of exercises". Grammar is viewed as a social activity taught through student-to-student and teacher-to-student participation, with communication on a par with correctness. Student-to-student activities include dialogues, poetry and drama, and information-gathering activities.
