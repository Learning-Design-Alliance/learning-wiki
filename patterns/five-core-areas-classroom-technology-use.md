# Five core areas of classroom technology use as organizing units for assessment

> **Pattern** · [All patterns](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Toolkit structures its materials around five recurring forms of student work with technology. As printed, "The Toolkit currently focuses on five core areas of technology use commonly encountered in classrooms": Internet Research, Data Collection and Representation (spreadsheets and graphing tools), Live Presentations, Web Design, and Illustrated Reports. Each area receives its own affordance analysis, conceptual framework, and checklist entries, so teachers can locate assessment guidance by the kind of digital product students produce.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must identify which of the five forms of technology work matches their planned lesson activity
#### Constraints
- The set of five areas reflects the forms of technology use 'commonly encountered in classrooms' at the time and is explicitly current rather than exhaustive

### Target Learners
- K-12 students

### Target Goals
- Internet research, data representation, presentation, web design, and illustrated reporting skills
