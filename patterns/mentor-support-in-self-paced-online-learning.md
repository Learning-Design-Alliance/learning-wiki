# Mentor Support in Self-Paced Online Learning

> **Pattern** · [All patterns](index.md)

## Description and scope

Support for learners who work through online courses on their own schedule, from mentors, coaches or instructors who are not lecturing but are available to help: answering questions, giving feedback, helping learners plan and keep pace, and reaching out when a learner stalls. The decisions it covers are how much contact is scheduled and how much is on demand, what triggers outreach (inactivity, missed milestones, failed assessments), what a contact does (content help, planning and self-regulation, encouragement, referral), and how human mentors and automated or AI support divide the work.
