# Quilt Project: Six-Session Integrated STEM Unit

> **Pattern** · [All patterns](index.md)

## Description
A six-session project developed for the GEMS afterschool club by expanding a quilt mathematics task. The designers "integrate art, cultural, real-life elements, and geometry, technology, and algebra into the project". Sessions run from knowing patterns, appreciating quilt designs and sharing stories, using Geogebra to draw a quilt design, listing materials and making the quilt, to an exhibition involving parents.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Six sessions: knowing patterns; appreciating quilt designs and sharing stories about quilts; using Geogebra to draw a quilt design; listing materials needed; making the quilt; an exhibition.
- Elements per session drawn from art, real life, mathematics, cultural, community and personal assets, technology, hands-on work and involving parents.
#### Constraints
- The article says the project is less focused on mathematics, since it does not connect to one specific subject.

### Target Learners
- Girls in an informal afterschool STEM club (GEMS)

### Target Goals
- Geometry, technology and algebra in an integrated STEM task
- Bringing cultural and life experiences into mathematics
