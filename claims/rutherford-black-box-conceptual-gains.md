M. Tuveri, E. Murgia, and V. Fanti (2025). Analogical models to introduce high school students to modern physics: an inquiry-based activity on Rutherford's gold foil experiment. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ae0386](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ae0386)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-activity Mentimeter quiz administered at the end of the half-day session with 15 first-year students. The article reports "69% correctly answered No" on atomic indivisibility; Table 3 shows 11 of 15 answers attributing a massive, positively charged nucleus to Rutherford's discovery. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "“Are atoms indivisible?” — 69% correctly answered No, indicating an increased awareness of subatomic structure compared to the initial word cloud results."
