### Chandralekha Singh and David Rosengrant 2016

Chandralekha Singh and David Rosengrant. (2016). Multiple-choice test of energy and momentum concepts. arXiv:1602.06497. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06497](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06497)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Administration of the 25-item test and accompanying individual interviews to introductory calculus- and algebra-based physics students showed that "most students have diﬃculty in qualitatively interpreting basic principles" of energy and momentum. The test results reveal students lack coherent understanding and are distracted by irrelevant details.
