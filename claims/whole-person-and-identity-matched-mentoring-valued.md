## Evidence

### M. K. Rodriguez Wimberly 2022

M. K. Rodriguez Wimberly, Alexander L. Rudolph, Carol Hood, Rachel E. Scherr, and Christine Pfund. (2022). A model of mentorship for students from historically underrepresented groups in STEM. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.03395](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.03395)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with 30 scholars across 2018-2020 (14 in 2018, 9 in 2019, 7 in 2020), coded for positive mentorship experiences, identity associations, and helpful mentorship behaviors. Scholars described mentors making "a concerted effort to mention that I’m more than just school."

> "when there is the opportunity for Cal-Bridge scholars to be paired with someone from their identity group, the relationship can be especially meaningful, even transformative:"
