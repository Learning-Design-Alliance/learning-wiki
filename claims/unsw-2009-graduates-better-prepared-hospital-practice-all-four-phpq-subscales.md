## Evidence

### Scicluna Helen A 2012

Scicluna Helen A, Grimm Michael C, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter, Pilotto Louis S, Jones Philip D, McNeil H Patrick. (2012). Clinical capabilities of graduates of an outcomes-based integrated medical program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-23

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test comparison of PHPQ Part 2 responses between the 2009 and 2007 UNSW cohorts three months into internship. New program graduates "rated themselves better prepared for hospital practice on all 4 subscales of the PHPQ", with the most substantial improvement in interpersonal skills and collaboration.

> "Graduates of the new UNSW program rated themselves better prepared for hospital practice on all 4 subscales of the PHPQ compared to the 2007 graduating cohort (Figure 2), (P < 0.001 for interpersonal and collaboration and P = 0.003 for confidence and prevention)."
