Cameron 1984¶

Cameron, Kim. (1984). The Paradox in Institutional Renewal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED257351

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The author's research on 335 four-year colleges and universities found that institutions improving effectiveness scores over time "possessed paradoxical characteristics" — being both clans and markets, or both hierarchies and adhocracies. No effect size is printed.

"the institutions that tendedto improve their effectiveness scores over time (from the mid-1970s to the mid-1980s) possessed paradoxical characteristics.That is, they were apt to be both clans and markets."
