`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis of burnout dimension in the multilevel meta-analysis; burnout dimension was significant, F(3,33) = 17.74, p < .0001. The article reports exhaustion and inefficacy showed significant negative effects versus total burnout; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "When total burnout was set as the reference level, exhaustion ( z = -.15, p = .02) and inefficacy ( z = -.16, p = .03) had significant negative effects."
