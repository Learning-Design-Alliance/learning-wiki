## Evidence

### Hulsbosch An-Katrien 2025

Hulsbosch An-Katrien, Van der Oord Saskia, Tripp Gail. (2025). Academic Achievement in Children with ADHD: the Role of Processing Speed and Working Memory. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01346-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 504 children with ADHD (serial mediation model with bootstrap CIs, 5000 draws). The article reports the reading serial pathway was "significant for both models" and the relation was "partially mediated through both PS and WM"; no standardized effect size is printed for the indirect effect.

> "Moreover, the serial mediation effect through both PS and WM was significant for both models. Given that the direct pathway between inattention symptom severity and reading performance was significant for both serial mediation model 1 and model 2, these results indicate this relation is partially mediated through both PS and WM"

### Hulsbosch An-Katrien 2025 (2)

Hulsbosch An-Katrien, Van der Oord Saskia, Tripp Gail. (2025). Academic Achievement in Children with ADHD: the Role of Processing Speed and Working Memory. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01346-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same SEM analysis of 504 children with ADHD: the serial PS–WM pathway to mathematics was significant in both mediation models (model 1 with parent ratings, model 2 without). Only bootstrap confidence intervals are printed, no standardized effect size.

> "The serial mediation effects through PS and WM were also significant for mathematics performance across both models."

### Hulsbosch An-Katrien 2025 (3)

Hulsbosch An-Katrien, Van der Oord Saskia, Tripp Gail. (2025). Academic Achievement in Children with ADHD: the Role of Processing Speed and Working Memory. Research on Child and Adolescent Psychopathology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10802-025-01346-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Serial mediation model 2 (spelling available only without parent ratings) in the same sample of 504 children with ADHD. The article reports partial mediation via PS and WM; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The serial mediation effect through PS and WM was also significant in the relation between inattention symptom severity and spelling performance. Thus, given the direct effect between inattention symptom severity and spelling performance was significant, these results indicate this relation is partially mediated by PS and WM"
