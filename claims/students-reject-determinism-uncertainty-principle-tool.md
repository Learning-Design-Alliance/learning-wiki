## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2010

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2010). Refined Characterization of Student Perspectives on Quantum Physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.6.020113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.6.020113)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview finding across the 19 students: most were unfamiliar with the word determinism in physics, yet nearly all held probabilistic or Uncertainty-Principle-based stances. The authors call for "a more detailed exploration of the Uncertainty Principle as an epistemological tool for students."

> "We also find it significant that most every student expressed distaste for deterministic ideas in the context of quantum physics, although it had been anticipated that Realist or Statistical students might favor such notions."
