## Evidence

### Teodóra Vékony 2022

Teodóra Vékony, Claire Pleche, Orsolya Pesthy, Karolina Janacsek and Dezso Nemeth. (2022). Speed and accuracy instructions affect two aspects of skill learning differently. npj Science of Learning 7:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00144-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of standardized probability-based learning scores in the Same Instruction Phase (equal speed-accuracy instructions) found "no difference was found between groups"; the printed rRB = −0.23 accompanied a non-significant test, so equivalence was not established.

> "Contrary to the results of the Different Instruction Phase, no difference was found between groups in the Same Instruction Phase, U = 221, p = 0.18, rRB = −0.23, BF01 = 2.20"
