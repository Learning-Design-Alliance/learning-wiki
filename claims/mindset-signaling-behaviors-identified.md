## Evidence

### Eunjin Seo 2025

Eunjin Seo, Margarett Clapper, Cameron A. Hecht, Robert Crosnoe, David S. Yeager. (2025). Peer Mindset Culture as a Developmental Context for Belonging. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10082-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reporting the behavior taxonomy from Muenks and Yan (2024)—competitiveness, self-deprecation, fixed-oriented verbal messages, withdrawing effort, disinterest in helping—plus boasting about success and reluctance to share struggles attributed to Seo et al. (2025). The review argues these behaviors are learnable and actionable intervention targets.

> "Some behaviors signal a mindset explicitly. Fixed mindset beliefs are most overt in verbal messages like, "I am just not a math person," which clearly communicate that intelligence is static"
