## Evidence

### Klusmann Dietrich 2023

Klusmann Dietrich, Knorr Mirjana, Hampe Wolfgang. (2023). Exploring the relationships between first impressions and MMI ratings: a pilot study. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 28(2), 519-536. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10151-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation of means across all 8 stations in the 2012 Hamburg MMI cohort; the correlation "was r = 0.49 in the total sample of all 192 applicants, and yielded the same value in the subsample of applicants admitted to medical school (n = 111)".

> "The correlation between the total scores for first impression and MMI-performance, both taken as the mean of two raters across all 8 stations, was r = 0.49 in the total sample of all 192 applicants, and yielded the same value in the subsample of applicants admitted to medical school (n = 111)."
