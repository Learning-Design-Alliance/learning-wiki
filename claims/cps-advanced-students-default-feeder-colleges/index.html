q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive finding from CPS postsecondary tracking data on enrollment destinations. Although these students apply to more colleges than the average CPS student, the report states they "only apply to a few colleges that match their qualifications."

"Many academically advanced students end up defaulting to colleges within CPS's traditional feeder patterns; of those who attend a four-year college, more than 40 percent attend one of the six most popular colleges for CPS students."
