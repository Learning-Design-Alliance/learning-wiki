Evidence¶

Moberg 2006¶

Moberg, E. (2006). Pedagogy is for Kids: Andragogy is for Adults. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED507785

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The essay attributes to Knowles (1998, p. 66) the point that adult learner groups are "more heterogeneous than groups of children," and supports it with the author's own ballroom dance class spanning learners aged 17 to 67, from a novice to a decades-experienced refresher student.

"Knowles also points out that groups of adult learners will be more heterogeneous than groups of children due to the varying age and experience levels amongst adult learners."
