Trend analysis of performance-versus-augmentation curves across the six augmentation methods and four classifiers, reported in the Discussion of RQ4. Llama models "continued to rise as the augmentation neared 100x".

> "In self and GPT-augmented models tested, performance peaked and degraded after 55x to 89x augmentation. For the Llama-augmented models, performance continued to rise as the augmentation neared 100x, suggesting more augmentation could be applied above 100x to achieve even higher performance."
