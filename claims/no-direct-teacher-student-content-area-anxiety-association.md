## Evidence

### McLean 2023

McLean, L., Janssen, J., Espinoza, P., Lindstrom Johnson, S., & Jimenez, M. (2023). Associations Between Teacher and Student Mathematics, Science, and Literacy Anxiety in Fourth Grade. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000790

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression models with cluster-robust standard errors in MPlus regressed 463 fourth-graders' mid-year self-reported anxiety on their teachers' beginning-of-year anxiety, controlling for students' fall anxiety. The article reports "did not reveal any significant relation" for mathematics; parallel null main effects are reported for science and ELA. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The main effects model for mathematics did not reveal any significant relation between teachers' Time 1 mathematics anxiety and students' Time 2 mathematics anxiety."
