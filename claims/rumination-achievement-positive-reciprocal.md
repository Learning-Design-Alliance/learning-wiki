A secondary CCLPM finding the authors had not hypothesized: "Rumination was a positive predictor of achievement" and achievement in turn positively predicted rumination, an unexpected direction the authors leave as an open question.

> "Rumination was a positive predictor of achievement (T2 and T4 rumination to T3 and T5 achievement, respectively). Achievement was, in turn, a positive predictor of rumination (T1 and T3 achievement to T2 and T4 rumination, respectively)."
