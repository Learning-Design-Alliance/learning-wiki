Zhou Yuanyuan, Sechelski Amber, Mowdy Brent, Johnson Tess, Vernardo Nikea, West Courtney A. (2026). Data driven student support: utilize the LASSI to improve academic performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09519-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlation analyses controlling for pre-matriculation variables showed MCAT was significantly correlated with all outcomes; the article reports "Pearson’s rs = 0.15–0.28". Controlling for USGPA strengthened some LASSI correlations and weakened others.

> "The MCAT scores were statistically significantly correlated with all four academic outcomes (Pearson’s rs = 0.15–0.28, ps = 0.000–0.026)."
