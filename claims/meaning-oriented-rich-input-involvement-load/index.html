Evidence¶

Murphy 2020¶

Murphy, V. A., Arndt, H., Briggs Baffoe-Djan, J., Chalmers, H., Macaro, E., Rose, H., Vanderplank, R., & Woore, R. (2020). Foreign language learning and its impact on wider academic outcomes: A rapid evidence assessment. University of Oxford Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED612981

q2 · i? · review · r3

General finding from the review's synthesis of seed reviews and updated RCT/QED studies on FL teaching approaches, reported in the executive summary. Defines involvement load as "how engaged the learner is with the task/language".

"In general, approaches that are largely meaning-oriented, providing rich, authentic, and stimulating FL input for students, which increases the involvement load (how engaged the learner is with the task/language) tends to be more successful."
