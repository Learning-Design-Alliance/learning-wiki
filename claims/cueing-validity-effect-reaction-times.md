# The spatial cueing task produced a small cueing validity effect on reaction times across stimulus categories

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Reaction times increased linearly from goal-relevant valid to goal-irrelevant to goal-relevant invalid cues (η2p = 0.072), indicating attention orienting. [→ Yoann Stussi 2019](#yoann-stussi-2019)

## Evidence

### Yoann Stussi 2019

Yoann Stussi, Aude Ferrero, Gilles Pourtois and David Sander. (2019). Achievement motivation modulates Pavlovian aversive conditioning to goal-relevant stimuli. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0043-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures GLM on spatial cueing reaction times in 72 participants showed a significant linear trend with printed effect size η2p = 0.072, with means rising from 496.52 ms (valid) to 505.21 ms (invalid cues), suggesting the task triggered attention orienting; the effect was small.

> "A polynomial contrast analysis showed a statistically signiﬁcant linear trend in the reaction times as a function of stimulus category, F(1, 70) = 5.41, p = 0.023, η2p = 0.072"
