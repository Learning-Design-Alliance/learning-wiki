### Fast ForWord® 2010

Fast ForWord®. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Fast ForWord®, Adolescent Literacy. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/173

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

One of two alphabetics-domain studies in the WWC synthesis: a randomized controlled trial of 5- to 14-year-olds in Western Australia comparing 68 Fast ForWord students with 69 controls. The report concludes "both studies showed indeterminate effects" in this domain.

> "Scientific Learning Corporation (2007a) did not find a statistically significant effect of Fast ForWord® on the Queensland University Inventory of Literacy (QUIL), nor was the effect large enough to be considered substantively important according to the WWC criteria (that is, an effect size of at least 0.25)."
