## Evidence

### ŞAKTANLI 2011

ŞAKTANLI, S. C., & ÖZDEMİR, G. (2011). THE COMPARISON OF THE EFFECT OF BLOCK FLUTE ACCOMPANIED SONG TEACHING WITH MULTI-SOUND NOTATION AND VOCALIZATION PROGRAM ACCOMPANIED SONG TEACHING ON THE SUCCESS OF STUDENTS’ SONG LEARNING BEHAVIOR. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ926566

`q3 · i?`

Independent-samples t-test on lay-rhythm behavior with 51 experimental and 51 pilot students; experimental mean 2.90 (S=0.25) vs pilot mean 1.79 (S=0.55). The article reports "a significant difference in favor of the experimental group".

> "Table 3 shows that in the analysis of behavior of “singing the lay of the song in accordance with its rhythm”, there is a significant difference in favor of the experimental group in comparison to the pilot group [t= 13.03; p< 0.05]."
