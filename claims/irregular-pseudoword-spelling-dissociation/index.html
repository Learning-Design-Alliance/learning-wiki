q3 · i3 · associational · r3

Partial correlations controlling for year group among 641 children's spelling scores; the printed correlation difference between irregular–regular (r = .76) and irregular–pseudoword (r = 0.54) was tested with z = 6.79, p = .001.

"irregular word spelling had stronger associations with regular word spelling (r = .76) than pseudoword spelling (r = 0.54) and this difference was significant (z = 6.79 p = .001)"
