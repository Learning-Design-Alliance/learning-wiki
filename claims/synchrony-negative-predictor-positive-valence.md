### Haataja 2021

Haataja, E., Malmberg, J., Dindar, M., & Järvelä, S. (2021). The pivotal role of monitoring for collaborative problem solving seen in interaction, performance, and interpersonal physiology. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 241–268. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09279-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

GEE model predicting positive valence monitoring duration from physiological synchrony measures in 19 groups. Both %REC and ADL were "significant negative predictors, meaning that higher physiological synchrony was related to less monitoring with positive valence."
