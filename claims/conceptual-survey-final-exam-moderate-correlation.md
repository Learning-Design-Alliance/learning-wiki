## Evidence

### Nafis I. Karim 2018

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Impact of evidence-based flipped or active-engagement non-flipped courses on student performance in introductory physics. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2017-0171](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2017-0171)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear regression analysis (Fig. 1 and Table VIII) of CSEM posttest versus final exam scores for courses of ten instructors who provided final exam data. The article reports there were no clear trends in the correlation coefficients based upon whether the instructor used EBAE strategies or whether the class was LB.

> "Linear regression of the CSEM posttest scores (conceptual) and final exam scores (heavy focus on quantitative problems) for four calculus -based introductory physics courses shows the correlation coefficients between 0.438 -0.598."
