### Hart Ruth I. 2026

Hart Ruth I., Sheehan Christina, Brewin Debbie, Ayling Kieran, Vedhara Kavita, Game Fran, Lawton Julia. (2026). Facilitating a complex behaviour-change intervention: healthcare professionals’ accounts of their journeys to competence and confidence. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-13676-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Abstract-level summary of the thematic findings from 18 interviews with 15 REDUCE facilitators, describing how supervision and group support underpinned reflective practice around challenging cases.

> "Interviewees emphasised the particular contributions of experiential learning and reflective practice, noting the key roles that supervision and group support played in the latter, and highlighting how such arrangements helped them to learn from, rather than be derailed by, challenging cases and interactions."

### Hart Ruth I. 2026 (2)

Hart Ruth I., Sheehan Christina, Brewin Debbie, Ayling Kieran, Vedhara Kavita, Game Fran, Lawton Julia. (2026). Facilitating a complex behaviour-change intervention: healthcare professionals’ accounts of their journeys to competence and confidence. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-13676-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results-section finding on group supervision. Interviewees described peer interactions providing reassurance, making them feel part of a community of learners, and helping them find ways forward when stuck.

> "group sessions (facilitated by an experienced and accredited CBT supervisor) were also highly valued by many. Interviewees highlighted learning from and with their peers, noting how whilst participants were unique, there were often commonalities in the challenges they presented."
