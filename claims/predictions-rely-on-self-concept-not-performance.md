Study 2 hierarchical regression on global predictions one week before the psychology exam. The article reports the judgment "relied mostly on domain-specific self-concept" and that "Test performance itself had no predictive power for predictions."

> "When students had not yet processed the task, their judgment relied mostly on domain-specific self-concept. Test performance itself had no predictive power for predictions."
