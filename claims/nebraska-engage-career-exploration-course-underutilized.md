Descriptive analysis of 2023/24 NDE course enrollment data. The report finds the NDE-developed course "accounted for just 0.4 percent of all enrollments statewide (n=779)". In conversations with local practitioners, the Engage! curriculum was rarely mentioned.

> "the NDE-developed course for career exploration— Middle School Engage! Career Exploration and Readiness— accounted for just 0.4 percent of all enrollments statewide (n=779), suggesting that this course is underutilized at the local level."
