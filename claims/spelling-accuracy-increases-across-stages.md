## Evidence

### Critten 2006

Critten, S., Pine, K., & Steffler, D. (2006). Spelling Development in Young Children: A Case of Representational-Redescription? Manuscript submitted for publication (Date of Submission: Thursday 3rd August 2006). https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.1.207

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA on correct spellings across the five Nunes et al. stages in the 51-child sample showed a "main effect for stage"; Scheffé tests located differences between stage 1 and stages 2-5 and between stage 2 and stages 4-5, suggesting two main ability steps.

> "A one-way ANOVA was computed on the number of correct spellings at each stage indicating a main effect for stage, F (4, 46) = 37.33, p < .05"
