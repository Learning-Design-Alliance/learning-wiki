q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review (PRISMA, PROSPERO-registered) of 12 primary studies with 430 participants in surgical and perioperative contexts, synthesised descriptively rather than pooled. The review reports that "Leadership performance improved, with reported gains of 2–4 points on behavioral scales." No pooled effect size was computed.
