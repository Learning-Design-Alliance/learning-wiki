## Evidence

### Asha 2016

Asha, I. K., & AL Hawi, A. M. (2016). The Impact of Cooperative Learning on Developing the Sixth Grade Students Decision-Making Skill and Academic Achievement. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(10). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099599

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experiment: 48 sixth-grade female students taught a 16-lesson geometry unit over 4 weeks, with a researcher-built achievement test based on Bloom's taxonomy (total 30 marks, Cronbach's alpha 0.83). ANCOVA showed statistically significant differences favoring the experimental group, whose adjusted mean was 17.80 versus 13.78 for the control group.
