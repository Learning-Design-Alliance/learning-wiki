# Each additional term of HRCF use is associated with 0.044–0.048-point increases in learning-related evaluation ratings for small/medium-enrollment (<250) offerings

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For small/medium-enrollment (<250) offerings from courses continuing HRCF, each additional prior use is associated with statistically significant increases on the GOAL (β=0.044) and LEARN (β=0.048) items. [→ Yunsung Kim 2026](#yunsung-kim-2026)

## Evidence

### Yunsung Kim 2026

Yunsung Kim, Hansol Lee, Candace Thille, and Chris Piech. 2026. A Large-Scale Observational Study on Obtaining Lightweight, Randomized Weekly Student Feedback: Associations with End-of-Term Course Evaluations. In Proceedings of the Thirteenth ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3774398.3811622

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression with course and year fixed effects on 310 offerings; the article reports "repeat HRCF use is positively associated with student evaluations" for GOAL and LEARN in <250 offerings, with unstandardized coefficients β=0.044 and β=0.048 (p<0.05).

> "repeat HRCF use is positively associated with student evaluations in small/medium-enrollment (< 250) course offerings with at least one prior HRCF use for “Learning goals” (𝛽=0.044 ∗) and “How much learned” (𝛽= 0.048∗) items."
