# Inconsistent messaging and supports, including coach modifications based on teacher buy-in or differing interpretations, created confusion about DLS implementation expectations

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Inconsistency in messaging and supports at the beginning of implementation created confusion around small group instruction for teachers in a few schools, partly because coaches modified expectations based on teacher buy-in or their own interpretations. [→ Mai Chou Vang 2020](#mai-chou-vang-2020)

## Evidence

### Mai Chou Vang 2020

Mai Chou Vang, Zareen Kasad, & Viki M. Young. (2020). Implementing and scaling Differentiated Literacy System: A case of evaluators' voices channeling outside-in and bottom-up perspectives for equity and continuous improvement. American Evaluation Association, Eval2020 Reimagined Conference. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/244ec2ff-be10-4063-aad1-f798a4a45369

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Finding from interviews and focus groups across 21 schools in 9 districts. The article reports that in some cases "DLS coaches modify expectations for DLS implementation based on the level of the teacher buy-in," and in others modifications resulted from coaches' different interpretations.

> "our findings demonstrate that inconsistency in messaging and supports, especially at the beginning of implementation, creates confusion around small group instruction for teachers in a few schools."
