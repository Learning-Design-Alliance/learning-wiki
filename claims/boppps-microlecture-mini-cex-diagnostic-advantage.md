# Orthopaedic postgraduates taught with a BOPPPS-based micro-lecture system outperformed ordinary-teaching peers on most Mini-CEX clinical diagnostic and decision-making domains (p < 0.007 after Bonferroni correction), except humanistic care

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After two months of rotation, the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group on medical history enquiry, physical examination, clinical judgement, communication skills, organisational skills, and overall performance on the Mini-CEX (p < 0.007 Bonferroni corrected), but not on humanistic care (p = 0.015). ## Evidence

### Yang Xu 2026

Yang Xu, Liu Tianyang, Li Ying, Gao Fuqiang. (2026). A BOPPPS-based micro-lecture teaching intervention for orthopaedic postgraduates in China. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2616194

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised comparison of 24 experimental vs 24 control first-year surgical postgraduates after a two-month orthopaedics rotation, assessed by simulated patient consultation with a unified Mini-CEX scale scored by two non-teaching preceptors. The article reports "the ESs outperformed CSs in almost all aspects, excluding humanistic care"; Table 2 prints means such as clinical judgement 7.33 ± 1.20 vs 5.54 ± 0.93 (Welch F = 33.250, p = 0.000).

> "After Bonferroni correction (α = 0.05/7), p < 0.007 was considered statistically significant. The results indicate that the ESs outperformed CSs in almost all aspects, excluding humanistic care. (p < 0.007)."
