## Evidence

### Rob J. Nadolski 2021

Rob J. Nadolski, Hans G. K. Hummel, Ellen Rusman, Kevin Ackermans. (2021). Rubric formats for the formative assessment of oral presentation skills acquisition in secondary education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10030-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A short six-item online questionnaire administered just before the first oral presentation (experimental groups only; the control group questionnaire was not administered) served as a control check. The "independent t-test comparing the similarity of participants in both experimental groups" showed no differences on any of the three factors. Teachers assessed the control group as similar on these factors.

> "An independent t-test comparing the similarity of participants in both experimental groups before their first presentation for anxiety, preparedness, motivation showed no difference, with t(1,98) = 1.32 and p = 0.19 for anxiety, t(1,98) = − 0.14 and p = 0.89 for preparedness, and t(1,98) = − 1.24 and p = 0.22 for motivation"
