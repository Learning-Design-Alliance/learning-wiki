## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an ITT analysis, the G-SS group attending six sessions scored significantly better than the control group on OSCE total (p = 0.003) and MC exam total (p = 0.003). [→ Rogan Slavko 2021](#rogan-slavko-2021)
`q2 i?` Students attending five G-SS sessions showed no significant difference from controls on OSCE total (p = 0.114) or MC total (p = 0.259). [→ Rogan Slavko 2021](#rogan-slavko-2021)

## Evidence

### Rogan Slavko 2021

Rogan Slavko, Taeymans Jan, Zuber Stefan, Zinzen Evert. (2021). Impact of guided self-study on learning success in undergraduate physiotherapy students in Switzerland – a feasibility study of a higher education intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02794-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis of first-semester exam scores for the randomized G-SS group (n = 26) versus control group (n = 25), reported in Table 2. With six attendances the between-group comparison was significant ("0.003*") for both OSCE total and MC total; with five attendances it was not (p = 0.114 and 0.259).

> "OSCE total	40.55 (± 0.44)	41.96 (± 0.19)	39.73 (±3.34)	0.114	0.003*	MC	62.35 (± 3.00)	66.10 (± 2.47)	60.74 (± 7.73)	0.259	0.003*"
