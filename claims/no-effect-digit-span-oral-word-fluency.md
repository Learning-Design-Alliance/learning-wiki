## Evidence

### Kawabata Masato 2021

Kawabata Masato, Lee Kerry, Choo Hui-Cheng, Burns Stephen F. (2021). Breakfast and Exercise Improve Academic and Cognitive Performance in Adolescents. Nutrients 13(4). https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13041278

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null findings from the randomized trial: the Digit-Span working memory task showed no group differences (all p > 0.05), and the article reports no differences in oral word fluency total valid words or retrieval speed (data not shown, all p > 0.05).

> "the maximum number of digits memorized in the forward and backwards direction was similar with B-E in all groups across the morning (all p > 0.05)."
