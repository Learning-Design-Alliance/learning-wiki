`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis (Meta-Essentials, random-effects model) of five studies evaluating educational programs for nurses, using standardized mean differences from pre-test/post-test data. The review reports the knowledge variable as statistically significant with "p < 0.001"; no effect size value is printed.

> "Considering statistical significance, the knowledge and attitude variables show statistically significant results (p < 0.001) but the perceptions variable does not present statically significant result (p > 0.005)."
