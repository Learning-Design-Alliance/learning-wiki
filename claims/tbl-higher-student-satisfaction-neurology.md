# Students in TBL groups report higher satisfaction than students in LBL groups

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

`q3 i3` Students in the TBL group were more satisfied than those in the LBL group, whether satisfaction was measured continuously or dichotomously. [→ Fu Jie 2026](#fu-jie-2026)

`q3 i2` The level of satisfaction among students in the TBL group was significantly higher than in the LBL group (SMD = 1.08). [→ Xie Zhi-Bo 2025](#xie-zhi-bo-2025)

## Evidence

### Fu Jie 2026

Fu Jie, Chen Xiu, Li Jinglun, Peng Lilei. (2026). TBL alone or combined with other teaching methods for neurology education in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09468-1

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of student satisfaction from 10 studies, analyzed separately by data type: 3 studies with continuous data (SMD = 1.17) and 7 studies with dichotomous data (OR = 6.31). Both favored TBL.

> "The pooled SMD of satisfaction with continuous data was 1.17 (95% CI: 0.85 to 1.48, p < 0.00001), and the overall OR for satisfaction with dichotomous data was 6.31 (95% CI: 3.58 to 11.12, p < 0.00001)."

### Xie Zhi-Bo 2025

Xie Zhi-Bo, Cheng Xin-Yu, Li Xiao-Yan, Zhang Yi-Fan. (2025). Team based learning pedagogy enhances the education quality: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07175-x

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Pooled analysis of two studies measuring student satisfaction, using a fixed-effects model with zero heterogeneity. The analysis found satisfaction among TBL students was "significantly higher" than among LBL students.

> "Pooled results [26, 34] also indicated that the level of satisfaction among students in the TBL group was significantly higher (SMD = 1.08, 95%CI 0.87 to 1.29, I2 = 0.0%, fix-effects model)"
