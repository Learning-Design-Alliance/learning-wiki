## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students in the TBL group were more satisfied than those in the LBL group, whether satisfaction was measured continuously or dichotomously. [→ Fu Jie 2026](#fu-jie-2026)

## Evidence

### Fu Jie 2026

Fu Jie, Chen Xiu, Li Jinglun, Peng Lilei. (2026). TBL alone or combined with other teaching methods for neurology education in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09468-1

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of student satisfaction from 10 studies, analyzed separately by data type: 3 studies with continuous data (SMD = 1.17) and 7 studies with dichotomous data (OR = 6.31). Both favored TBL.

> "The pooled SMD of satisfaction with continuous data was 1.17 (95% CI: 0.85 to 1.48, p < 0.00001), and the overall OR for satisfaction with dichotomous data was 6.31 (95% CI: 3.58 to 11.12, p < 0.00001)."
