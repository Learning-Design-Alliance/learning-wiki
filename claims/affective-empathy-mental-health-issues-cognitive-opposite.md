## Evidence

### Chen Hao 2024

Chen Hao, Xuan Hanwen, Cai Jinquan, Liu Meichen, Shi Lei. (2024). The impact of empathy on medical students: an integrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05448-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A cross-sectional study of 886 medical students (Carrard et al., Switzerland) measured empathy with JSPE, QCAE and IRI and mental health with depression, anxiety and burnout indicators; the review reports the dimensional contrast quoted here.

> "In this study, positive associations were found between affective empathy and more mental health issues such as depression, anxiety and burnout, while cognitive empathy was the opposite."

### Chen Hao 2024 (2)

Chen Hao, Xuan Hanwen, Cai Jinquan, Liu Meichen, Shi Lei. (2024). The impact of empathy on medical students: an integrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05448-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A longitudinal study of 353 medical students (von Harscher et al., USA) used the Maslach Burnout Inventory and IRI; the review reports the quoted dimensional contrast in burnout over time.

> "students with high levels of empathic concern had statistically lower scores of burnout over time while students with high levels of personal distress showed statistically higher scores of burnout during medical education"
