## Evidence

### Filippou 2018

Filippou, F., Rokka, S., Pitsi, A., Gargalianos, D., Bebetsos, E., & Filippou, D. A. (2018). Interdisciplinary Greek Traditional Dance Course: Impact on Student Satisfaction and Anxiety. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 363-374. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11325a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the somatic anxiety subscale of the Competitive State Anxiety Inventory-2; the experimental group's mean fell from 4.31 to 1.90 while the control group stayed at 4.32. The article reports a "statistically significant interaction" with F(1,258)=1274.44, p<.001.
