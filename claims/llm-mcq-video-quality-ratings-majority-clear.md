## Evidence

### Giacomo Cassano 2026

Giacomo Cassano, Daniel Ebbert, Negin Mirriahi, Nicoletta Di Blas. (2026). Instructors’ perceptions of a large language model framework for generating automated multiple-choice questions from video transcripts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10646-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert-judgment evaluation in which seven instructors from Politecnico di Milano assessed 257 MCQs generated from 45 MOOC videos using the 19-item IWF checklist plus four content criteria. The study reports "84.05% of the questions were rated as clear" and the distractor and answerability percentages shown. No effect size is printed.

> "Specifically, 84.05% of the questions were rated as clear, 86.38% contained plausible distractors, and 93.39% were directly answerable based on the video content."
