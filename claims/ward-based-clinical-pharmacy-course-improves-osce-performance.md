## Evidence

### Dircks Monika 2017

Dircks Monika, Mayr Andreas, Freidank Annette, Kornhuber Johannes, Dörje Frank, Friedland Kristina. (2017). Advances in clinical pharmacy education in Germany: a quasi-experimental single-blinded study to evaluate a patient-centred clinical pharmacy course in psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1092-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental single-blinded parallel-group study with 84 final-year pharmacy students; intervention group (n=42) completed the full ward-based course, control group (n=42) only the preparation lecture. The article reports a statistically significant difference in overall OSCE scores favoring the intervention group, with no standardized effect size printed.

> "The intervention group achieved significantly better overall results on the OSCE assessment (46.20 ± 10.01 vs. 26.58 ± 12.91 of a maximum of 90 points; p < 0.0001)."
