## Evidence

### Bylow Helene 2019

Bylow Helene, Karlsson Thomas, Lepp Margret, Claesson Andreas, Lindqvist Jonny, Herlitz Johan. (2019). Effectiveness of web-based education in addition to basic life support learning activities: A cluster randomised controlled trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0219341

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Primary outcome of a parallel cluster randomised controlled trial in which 2,623 laymen from 84 Swedish workplaces were randomised to BLS or BLS+WEB; the retention test six months after training was assessed with the modified Cardiff Test (19–70 points), analysed with mixed linear regression in 2,480 participants. The Results section reports a "significantly higher total score" for BLS+WEB; Table 2 prints mean 58.8 (SD 5.0) vs 58.0 (SD 5.0), p = 0.03.

> "The BLS+WEB education was more effective and the participants obtained a significantly higher total score for adherence to practical skills in the BLS algorithm six months after training compared with the BLS without the web-based education (Table 2)."
