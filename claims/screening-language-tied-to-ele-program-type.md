# Screening language practices are related to English language education program type: SEI students were mostly screened in English while DLBE students were more often screened in Spanish or alternately

## Evidence

### Soo Ping Chow 2025

Soo Ping Chow, A., Faulkner-Bond, M., Lemke, M., LeBeau, B., & Quesen, S. (2025). How English learners are screened for risk of reading difficulty: Use of English- and Spanish-language screeners in the 2023/24 school year. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677598

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cluster analysis of language-of-assessment patterns for 23,955 English learners shows program enrollment differences: clusters screened mostly in English had 92–98% SEI enrollment, while clusters screened more in Spanish had 93% and 51% DLBE enrollment. The brief reports SEI students were "mostly screened in English, sometimes with additional screening in Spanish."

> "English learners in structured English immersion (SEI) programs were mostly screened in English, sometimes with additional screening in Spanish."
