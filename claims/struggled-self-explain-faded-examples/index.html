Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025¶

Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld, Bernadette Gold. (2025). Complete or faded double-content examples of video-based analyses: How do they impact student teachers' professional vision of classroom management? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09738-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

IG2 student teachers submitted solutions to the faded double-content examples from time blocks 2 (n = 58) and 3 (n = 60); raters coded whether they identified the correct expertise-related aspects (κ = .89), and descriptive statistics indicated widespread struggle.

"Descriptive statistics indicated that student teachers struggled to self-explain the expert features from the double-content examples (see Fig. 6)."
