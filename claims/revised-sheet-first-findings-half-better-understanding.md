`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Small-scale first trial of the revised instruction sheet with 18 students (6 groups of 3) during one semester, accompanied by personal instructions. The author reports only first findings: "at least half of the students show a better understanding of the experimental procedure and of their measurements". No effect size or statistical test is reported.
