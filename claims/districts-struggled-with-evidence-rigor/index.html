Evidence¶

Bugler 2021¶

Bugler, D. (2021). School District Collaboration: Lessons From Districts Participating in California Education Partners' California Language & Learning Initiative (CALLI). WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616075

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Interview data show teams initially relied on state test scores, then interim and classroom assessments after COVID limited statewide testing, and acknowledged "the need to improve the quality of the evidence they use to assess progress." No effect size.

"Many districts found that their evidence was not as rigorous and reliable as it needed to be. The collaboration helped teams develop better evidence through consultation with content experts, other district teams, and Ed Partners staff."
