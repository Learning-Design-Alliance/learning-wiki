## Evidence

### Koedinger 2010

Koedinger, K. R., McLaughlin, E. A., & Heffernan, N. T. (2010). A quasi-experimental evaluation of an on-line formative assessment and tutoring system. Journal of Educational Computing Research, 43(4), 489-510. https://doi.org/10.2190/EC.43.4.d

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main-effect analyses for special education students only, excluded from the 3 × 2 because of small cell sizes (e.g., only six non-usage special education students with a high usage teacher); adjusted means were 31.5, 33.2, and 33.3. Equivalence was not tested.

> "A 3-level ANCOVA with pre-test as a covariate and special education student usage (no usage vs. low vs. high) as a between subjects factor did not reveal an effect of special education student usage,F(2, 195) = .481, p = .62"

### Koedinger 2010 (2)

Koedinger, K. R., McLaughlin, E. A., & Heffernan, N. T. (2010). A quasi-experimental evaluation of an on-line formative assessment and tutoring system. Journal of Educational Computing Research, 43(4), 489-510. https://doi.org/10.2190/EC.43.4.d

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted post-test scores of special education students were 30% with no usage teachers, 34% with low usage teachers, and 34% with high usage teachers; treatment non-users' means matched the control group's (30.2 vs. 30.1).

> "The difference between special education students of no usage teachers and some usage teachers (either low or high) is statistically reliable ( F(1, 196) = 3.76, p = .05)."
