## Evidence

### Hosseini 2014

Hosseini, S. M. H. (2014). Competitive Team-Based Learning versus Group Investigation with Reference to the Language Proficiency of Iranian EFL Intermediate Students. International Journal of Instruction, 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085235

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Matched t-test on the GI control group's pre-test (X1 = 17.76) and post-test (X2 = 21.16) means, described as "a little progress in this group."

> "The results showed significant difference between the control group performances on both the tests, because the observed t of -6.8 at a probability level of P 0.05 exceeded the critical t of 2.045."

### Hosseini 2014 (2)

Hosseini, S. M. H. (2014). Competitive Team-Based Learning versus Group Investigation with Reference to the Language Proficiency of Iranian EFL Intermediate Students. International Journal of Instruction, 7(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085235

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-test on the CTBL experimental group's pre-test (X1 = 17.6) and post-test (X2 = 25.5) means, which the author describes as "a remarkably high difference."

> "This time the t-observed (16.8) far exceeded the value of t -critical (2.045) at a probability level of P  0.05."
