### Kyle M. Whitcomb and Chandralekha Singh 2020

Kyle M. Whitcomb and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Not all disadvantages are equal: Racial/ethnic minority students have largest disadvantage of all demographic groups in both STEM and non-STEM GPA. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.04376](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.04376)

Observational analysis of ten years of institutional records for N = 24,567 undergraduates, plotted as mean yearly GPA by sixteen demographic groups (Fig. 1). The authors note "the four groups that contain URM students are consis- tently in the lowest four or ﬁve mean GPAs."
