### Joseph T. Lizier 2018

Joseph T. Lizier, Michael S. Harré, Melanie Mitchell, Simon DeDeo, Conor Finn, Kristian Lindgren, Amanda L. Lizier, and Hiroki Sayama. (2018). An interview based study of pioneering experiences in teaching and learning Complex Systems in Higher Education. [doi:10.1155/2018/7306871](https://doi.org/10.1155/2018/7306871)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with pioneering complex systems teachers and one student. The paper reports the theme was "constant across all interviews": wide variation in mathematical and computer programming capabilities follows from open enrolment across disciplines.

> "The major theme – constant across all interviews and echoed b y Porter [9] – was that the key challenge in teaching and learning of complex systems at the tertiary level is the diversity of student backgrounds."
