## Evidence

### Ajmi Soffien 2024

Ajmi Soffien, Kurz Martin, Lindner Thomas Werner, Dalen Ingvild, Ersdal Hege Langli. (2024). Does clinical experience influence the effects of team simulation training in stroke thrombolysis? A prospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-086413

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Dichotomised unadjusted mixed model analysis for illustrative purposes, with predicted geometric mean DNTs and 95% CIs; experienced registrars were defined as having 15 or more prior IVT treatments, based on the cohort median.

> "the unadjusted geometric mean DNT for patients treated by inexperienced registrars (ie, <15 prior IVT treatments) was 25.3 min (95% CI, 21.9 to 29.2) compared with 21.9 min (95% CI, 18.9 to 25.3) for experienced registrars."
