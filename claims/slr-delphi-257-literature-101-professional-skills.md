### Nicolai B. Plintz 2026

Nicolai B. Plintz, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2026). Empowering children online: a holistic skills framework for cybersecurity. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10565-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Systematic literature review following the PRISMA statement across six databases, plus round 1 of a two-round Delphi study. The review yielded "N = 257 relevant skills remained" and professionals contributed "N = 101 skills in the first round of the Delphi study"; no effect sizes are reported.

> "From the systematic literature review, we initially extracted over 500 skills from different scientific papers, games as well as the platforms mentioned in those. After excluding those that were not age-appropriate and removing duplicates, N = 257 relevant skills remained. In addition to the skills from the scientific literature, the professionals identified N = 101 skills in the first round of the Delphi study."
