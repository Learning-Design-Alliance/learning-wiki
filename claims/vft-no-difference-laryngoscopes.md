## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wang M 2025

Wang M, Ruan W, Li H, Yuan W, Song J, Wang J. (2025). Comparison study of LANJA, UE, and VDO video laryngoscopes for simulated endotracheal intubation in beginners. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000045803

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null finding from the same manikin study's timed-phase analysis: pairwise VFT comparisons were LANJA vs UE P = 1.000, LANJA vs VDO P = .551, UE vs VDO P = .495. Equivalence was not tested; the study only failed to detect a difference.

> "However, there was no significant difference in the VFT time among the 3 laryngoscopes (LANJA: 25.21 ± 7.35; UE: 25.04 ± 7.75; VDO: 27.66 ± 3.80, P = .31)."
