## Evidence

### Sergei Volodin 2025

Sergei Volodin, Hala Khodr, Pierre Dillenbourg, Wafa Johal. (2025). Going Down the Abstraction Stream with Augmented Reality and Tangible Robots: The Case of Vector Instruction. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.14562](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.14562)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Correlational analysis across the 14 teams comparing Year 10 and Year 12 scores. The article reports year class "correlated with the pre-test scores ρ = 0.62, p <10−4" but not with post-test scores, which the authors interpret as differentiation across age groups.

> "The team’s year class is correlated with the pre-test scores ρ = 0.62, p <10−4, but not with post-test scores. This shows that while there exists an initial difference between of knowledge between the two age groups (Year 10 and Year 12), it disappears after learning with our activity."
