## Evidence

### Dhlamini 2013

Dhlamini, J., & Mogari, D. (2013). The effect of a group approach on the performance of high school mathematics learners. Pythagoras, 34(2), Art. #198. https://doi.org/10.4102/pythagoras.v34i2.198

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The first author observed one lesson per control teacher (four teachers) to classify instructional mode as learner-centred or teacher-centred. Observations converged with interview data, with a reported correlation agreement of 0.87, supporting classification of control teaching as didactic.

> "Observations of teachers' lessons showed that lessons in most control schools were similar and embraced the following characteristics: the classrooms represented a traditional teaching and learning setting in which desks are arranged linearly; teachers solely explained problem-solving steps and provided solutions to learners; minimal learner participation and interaction took place during the lesson"
