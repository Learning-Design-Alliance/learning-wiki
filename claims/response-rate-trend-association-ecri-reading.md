## Evidence

### Doabler 2013

Doabler, C. T., Nelson-Walker, N., Kosty, D., Baker, S. K., Smolkowski, K., & Fien, H. (2013). Explicit Instructional Interactions: Observed Stability and Predictive Validity During Early Literacy and Beginning Mathematics Instruction. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563121

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel models nesting approximately 2,954 ECRI students within 138 classrooms, computing partial correlations controlling for pretest. Table 3 shows partial r values of .20~ for ORF and .20~ for WRC for Variable 4, marked at p < .10.

> "Table 3 provides results that indicate small trend-level associations in the ECRI data were obtained for the fourth instructional variable (rate of group or individual response sequences). These positive effects were found on DIBELS ORF and NWF (Words Recoded Completely and Correctly)."
