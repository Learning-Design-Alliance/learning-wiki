# In calculus-based FCI data, RT labs with traditional lecture significantly outperformed T, IL and RT-T labs with traditional lecture (p < 0.0001), a lab-style difference not seen under PERL lecture

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Under traditional lecture, RT labs produced much higher FCI normalized gain than RT-T and IL labs, with T, IL and RT-T labs not significantly different from each other but each significantly different from RT/TL at p < 0.0001. [→ Beth Thacker 2012](#beth-thacker-2012)

## Evidence

### Beth Thacker 2012

Beth Thacker, Hani Dulli, Dave Pattillo, and Keith West. (2012). Lessons from a Large-Scale Assessment: Results from Conceptual Inventories. Physics Department, Texas Tech University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post FCI assessment of calculus-based large lecture sections across four lab styles (T, RT-T, RT, IL) under traditional lecture; the article reports the p < 0.05 and p < 0.0001 Student's T-test comparisons quoted. No effect size is printed.

> "The T, IL and RT-T labs with TL instruction are not significantly different from each other by a Student's T-test at the p < 0.05 level, but they are each significantly different from the RT/TL labs at the p < 0.0001 level."
