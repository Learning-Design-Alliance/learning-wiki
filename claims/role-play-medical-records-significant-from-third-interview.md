## Evidence

### Huang et al. 2018

Simin Huang, Peng Xu, Lie Feng, Chunting Lu & Jing Yang. (2018). An Effective Teaching Method to Enhance History-Taking Skills for Chinese Medical Students. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n2p144

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled comparison of 52 students randomized into an observation group (n=25) and control group (n=27); seven graded medical case records per student. The article reports "no significant differences between these two groups in their first and second interviews" and significant differences from the third to seventh interviews (e.g., P = 0.026 at interview 3, P = 0.000 at interviews 6 and 7).

> "The scores for medical records in the observation group were higher than those in the control group. However, there were no significant differences between these two groups in their first and second interviews with real patients ( P = 0.083 and P = 0.158, respectively)."
