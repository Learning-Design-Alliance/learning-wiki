# Students in both the flipped classroom and jigsaw groups reported high satisfaction and preferred these active methods over lecture-based teaching

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in both experimental groups expressed similar satisfaction levels and substantially preferred the active methods over lecture-based teaching (FC mean 4.24, jigsaw mean 4.16 on the preference item). [→ Khodadadeh Alireza 2025](#khodadadeh-alireza-2025)

## Evidence

### Khodadadeh Alireza 2025

Khodadadeh Alireza, Rivaz Mozhgan, Torabizadeh Camellia. (2025). The effects of flipped classroom and jigsaw teaching strategies on learning, retention of course content, and satisfaction among nursing students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07662-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 12-item 5-point Likert satisfaction questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.93) was given to the flipped classroom (n = 35) and jigsaw (n = 43) groups; Mann-Whitney U tests found "similar levels of satisfaction with the FC and jigsaw educational approaches" across items.

> "Students in both the FC and jigsaw groups showed a substantial preference for these active learning approaches over LBT, as demonstrated by their answers to question 5 (mean scores: FC = 4.24 ± 0.90, Jigsaw = 4.16 ± 1.04)."
