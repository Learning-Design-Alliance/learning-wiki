`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 15 within-study comparisons in which RD and RCT estimates are made at the same cutoff point where they should not differ asymptotically. Both Bayesian and frequentist methods show "the RD bias is below 0.01 standard deviations on average."

> "Both Bayesian and frequentist meta-analysis methods show that the RD bias is below 0.01 standard deviations on average, indicating RD's high internal validity."
