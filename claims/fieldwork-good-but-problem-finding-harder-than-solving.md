### Ankit A. Ravankar 2018

Ankit A. Ravankar, Shotaro Imai, Abhijeet Ravankar, Tomomichi Kato, Taichi Takasuka, Teruyuki Tsuji, Kaori K. Shigetomi, Ken Saito. (2018). Towards Better Problem Finding and Creativity in Graduate School Education. [doi:10.1109/iiai-aai.2018.00088](https://doi.org/10.1109/iiai-aai.2018.00088)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Findings-section questionnaire item (N = 26) on the fieldwork component, noting "half of the students had previous experience of doing field-work"; rated a good experience at M = 4.65. Descriptive only.

> "While half of the students had previous experience of doing ﬁeld-work, most students stated that doing ﬁeld-work to understand problem ﬁnding process was a good experience (M = 4 .65, SD = 0 .48)."

### Ankit A. Ravankar 2018 (2)

Ankit A. Ravankar, Shotaro Imai, Abhijeet Ravankar, Tomomichi Kato, Taichi Takasuka, Teruyuki Tsuji, Kaori K. Shigetomi, Ken Saito. (2018). Towards Better Problem Finding and Creativity in Graduate School Education. [doi:10.1109/iiai-aai.2018.00088](https://doi.org/10.1109/iiai-aai.2018.00088)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Findings-section questionnaire item (N = 26): students agreed they found "the problem finding step difficult than the problem solving step", M = 4.11 with SD = 1.10, the largest spread among the reported items. Descriptive only.

> "Most student also agreed that they found the problem ﬁnding step diﬃcult than the problem solving step ( M = 4.11, SD = 1.10)."
