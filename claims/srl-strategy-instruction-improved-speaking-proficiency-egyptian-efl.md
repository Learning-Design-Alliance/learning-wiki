## Evidence

### El-Sakka 2016

El-Sakka, S. M. F. (2016). Self-Regulated Strategy Instruction for Developing Speaking Proficiency and Reducing Speaking Anxiety of Egyptian University Students. English Language Teaching, 9(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n12p22

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-group pre-posttest quasi-experiment with forty third-year English majors at Suez Faculty of Education, tested with the IELTS Speaking Test scored by two raters (inter rater reliability 0.89). The paired samples t-test was significant and "effect size for this difference was 1.920".

> "As shown in Table 1, the difference between the mean scores of the pre-post test of speaking was significant (t=29.668, p≤0.05). Additionally, using Cohen's (1988) formula, effect size for this difference was 1.920."
