# Both FSP-TCM and OSP-TCM groups scored higher than the traditionally taught group on formative mini-CEX physical examination and comprehensive ability, with no FSP–OSP difference

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the formative evaluation, FSP-TCM and OSP-TCM students scored significantly higher than TT students in physical examination (Pa = 0.01; Pb = 0.04) and comprehensive ability (Pa = 0.01; Pb = 0.006), while FSP-TCM and OSP-TCM did not differ (Pc = 0.93–0.96). ## Evidence

### Huang Meilan 2024

Huang Meilan, Yang Han, Guo Jing, Fu Xiaoxu, Chen Wangshu, Li Bin, Zhou Shan, Xia Ting, Peng Sihan, Wen Lijuan, Ma Xiao, Zhang Yi, Zeng Jinhao. (2024). Faculty standardized patients versus traditional teaching method to improve clinical competence among traditional Chinese medicine students: a prospective randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05779-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Formative evaluation using a refined mini-CEX on a nine-point scale at week 6 of the 12-week course, comparing three randomized groups (n = 30 each). The article reports the SP groups' physical examination and comprehensive ability scores "significantly exceeded that of the TT group" with the printed pairwise p-values.

> "Notably, their performance in “physical examination” (Pa = 0.01, Pb = 0.04, Pc = 0.93) and “comprehensive ability” (Pa = 0.01, Pb = 0.006, Pc = 0.96) significantly exceeded that of the TT group."
