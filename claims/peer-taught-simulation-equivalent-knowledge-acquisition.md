## Evidence

### House Joseph B. MD 2017

House Joseph B. MD, Choe Carol H. MD, Wourman Heather L. BA, Berg Kristin M. MD, Fischer Jonathan P. MPH, Santen Sally A. MD, PhD. (2017). Efficient and Effective Use of Peer Teaching for Medical Student Simulation. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2016.11.32753

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental clerkship study comparing 111 peer-taught and 65 physician-taught students on pre/post knowledge tests. The article reports "The mean for the post-test was 75% for both groups (p=0.6)", with pre-test means of 66% and 65%; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean for the pre-test was 66% for the peer-taught group and 65% for the physician-taught group. The mean for the post-test was 75% for both groups (p=0.6)."
