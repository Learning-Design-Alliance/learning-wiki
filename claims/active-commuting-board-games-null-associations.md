[doi:10.3389/fpubh.2024.1412634](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1412634)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted regression models (Table 4, Model 2) in the GUSTO cohort found the article reports active commuting was "not significantly associated with executive function and academic achievement" at both exposure ages. No effect sizes were printed for these null findings.

> "Engagement in active commuting to/from school at age 5.5 and 8 years were not significantly associated with executive function and academic achievement."

The same adjusted models (Table 5, Model 2) found no association of sitting and playing board/card games with either outcome at either exposure age. No effect sizes were printed for these null findings.

> "We also did not find associations of playing board/card games at ages 5.5 and 8 years with executive function and academic achievement."
