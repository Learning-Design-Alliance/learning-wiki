Kidd 2013¶

Kidd, J. K., Gadzichowski, K. M., Gallington, D. A., Lopez, C., & Pasnak, R. (2013). A Teacher-Friendly Method of Improving Reading and Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Teacher-Friendly+Method+of+Improving+Reading+and+Mathematics%22+SREE

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the randomized study, W-J Mathematics Concepts A and B showed patterning (M = 15.00, 16.48) and mathematics (13.90, 12.87) significantly better than reading and social studies; Table 3 superscripts denote p < .02 for a priori comparisons.

"The patterning and mathematics groups were both significantly better than the other groups on the W-J Mathematics Concepts Scales"

Kidd 2013 (2)¶

Kidd, J. K., Gadzichowski, K. M., Gallington, D. A., Lopez, C., & Pasnak, R. (2013). A Teacher-Friendly Method of Improving Reading and Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Teacher-Friendly+Method+of+Improving+Reading+and+Mathematics%22+SREE

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Key Math results in the same study: patterning means were highest on every scale, significant (p < .05) on Numeration, Addition, Algebra, Measurement, Foundations, Computation, Data Problems, and Applied Problems, but not Geometry or Multiplication.

"Mean scores for the patterning group were always higher than for the other groups on the Key Math scales, and the differences on all except Geometry and Multiplication were significant"
