The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22How+Does+the+Ability+to+Make+Rapid+Mental+Adjustments+Affect+Test+Scores+and+Pupil+Achievements%22+Zirbes

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Fourth-grade diagnostic study during 1917-1918: initial oral and silent rates were nearly equal for much of the class. After brief explanation about silent reading, pupils were re-tested; 'Comprehension was checked so that the pupils were really reading at a faster rate.'

> "there appeared variations which indicated that the mere consciousness of a need of change in mental set had an effect on rate of silent reading."
