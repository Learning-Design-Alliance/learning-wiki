# ENEM 2009 percent correct is lower on quantitative Physics items than on qualitative ones

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among ENEM 2009 examinees, mean percent correct on quantitative Physics items (17.2 concluintes, 18.0 all) was lower than on qualitative items (34.5 concluintes, 35.0 all). [→ Wanderley P. Gonçalves Jr and Marta F. Barroso 2013](#wanderley-p-goncalves-jr-and-marta-f-barroso-2013)

## Evidence

### Wanderley P. Gonçalves Jr and Marta F. Barroso 2013

Wanderley P. Gonçalves Jr and Marta F. Barroso. (2013). As questões de Física e o desempenho dos estudantes no ENEM. [doi:10.1590/s1806-11172014000100017](https://doi.org/10.1590/s1806-11172014000100017)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Descriptive analysis of ENEM 2009 microdata (2,555,594 all participants; 944,162 self-declared concluintes), blue test as reference. Table 2 reports mean percent correct of 34.5 (concluintes) on qualitative items versus 17.2 on quantitative items; the article states "O desempenho, em média, é inferior" for items requiring mathematical reasoning.

> "O desempenho, em média, é inferior nas questões que exigem algum tipo de raciocínio matemático."
