## Evidence

### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of the imputed ALSPAC sample (n = 7465) linking teacher expected achievement to national examination results. The article reports the association as "a 0.88 (95% CI 0.87, 0.89) and 0.92 (95% CI 0.91, 0.93) SD increase" and R2 values of 0.78 and 0.85 at Key Stages 2 and 3.

> "Each one standard deviation (SD) increase in teacher expectation was associated with a 0.88 (95% CI 0.87, 0.89) and 0.92 (95% CI 0.91, 0.93) SD increase in realised achievement at Key Stages 2 and 3 respectively in the imputed data"
