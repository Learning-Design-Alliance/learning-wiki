Observational analysis using cyclic momentary-time sampling across three experiments (Gas Laws, Heat Engine, Mapping Electric Potential); participation percentages averaged per student and compared by gender via independent-samples t-tests, showing "male and female students engaged comparably in lab activities". No effect size printed.

> "Independent samplest-tests indicated that male and female students engaged comparably in lab activities (t=−0.073, p= 0.471, α= 0.05)."
