Evidence¶

Espinoza 2018¶

Espinoza, D., Saunders, R., Kini, T., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2018). Taking the long view: State efforts to solve teacher shortages by strengthening the profession. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/long-view

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report cites a review spanning medicine and teaching finding these programs effective at recruiting and retaining high-quality professionals when they "cover a significant portion of tuition and/or living costs." No effect size is printed.

"A recent review of research on service scholarship and loan forgiveness programs in both medicine and teaching found that, when such strategies cover a significant portion of tuition and/or living costs, they are effective in recruiting and retaining high-quality professionals into the fields and communities in which they are most needed."

Espinoza 2018 (2)¶

Espinoza, D., Saunders, R., Kini, T., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2018). Taking the long view: State efforts to solve teacher shortages by strengthening the profession. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/long-view

q2 · i? · review · r2

A national U.S. Department of Education survey found "1 in 4 public school teachers who had left teaching" and would consider returning identified loan forgiveness as extremely or very important to that decision. Descriptive survey figure; no effect size.

"1 in 4 public school teachers who had left teaching and also said they would consider returning to the profession identified loan forgiveness as extremely or very important to their decision to return"
