# In preliminary acoustic testing, stereo position was interpreted as 3D rather than 2D, and loudness and pitch differences were hardly quantifiable

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Users correctly interpreted the spatial position of sounds as source position but thought the sources were distributed in a 3D space instead of the intended 2D map. [→ Varano 2023](#varano-2023)
`q2 i?` Sound loudness was a hardly quantifiable feature, difficult to associate to actual differences in source distance. [→ Varano 2023 (2)](#varano-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Varano 2023

Varano, S., and Zanella, A. (2023). Design and evaluation of a multi-sensory representation of scientific data. Frontiers. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.3389/feduc.2023.1082249](https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2023.1082249)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative testing of a stereo acoustic representation of four point-like sources with the same 27-user sample. Spatial position was correctly interpreted, but users "thought the sources were distributed in a 3D space, instead of the intended 2D one".

> "However the users thought the sources were distributed in a 3D space, instead of the intended 2D one, namely on an imaginary 2D map ”in front” of the user."

### Varano 2023 (2)

Varano, S., and Zanella, A. (2023). Design and evaluation of a multi-sensory representation of scientific data. Frontiers. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.3389/feduc.2023.1082249](https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2023.1082249)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Same acoustic preliminary test: all participants recognized different sound intensities and associated them with distance, but "the sound loudness was a hardly quantifiable feature", so intensity and distance were later mapped to separate features.

> "Nevertheless, the sound loudness was a hardly quantifiable feature, difficult to associate to actual differences in the distance."

### Varano 2023 (3)

Varano, S., and Zanella, A. (2023). Design and evaluation of a multi-sensory representation of scientific data. Frontiers. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.3389/feduc.2023.1082249](https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2023.1082249)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Same acoustic preliminary test: "All participants but three perceived the different pitches associated to sounds", but higher versus lower pitches and relative pitch-difference sizes could not be judged, motivating a change of mapping in later iterations.

> "All participants but three perceived the different pitches associated to sounds, but showed difficulties in distinguishing higher from lower pitches, while none of them could tell when the difference in pitch between two sources was bigger or smaller."
