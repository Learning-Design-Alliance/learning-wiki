## Evidence

### Wu Yajun 2025

Wu Yajun, Kang Xia, Li Yiyan. (2025). Linking EFL psychological capital and EFL achievement: the chain mediating effect of academic boredom and behavioural engagement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03685-x

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of survey data from 1460 Chinese secondary EFL learners (grades 7-8, stratified three-stage cluster sampling), controlling gender, age and SES. The direct path was significant: "β = 0.28, SE = 0.04, p <.001".

> "Third, the path from EFL PsyCap to EFL achievement (β = 0.28, SE = 0.04, p <.001) was also positive, but medium in magnitude."
