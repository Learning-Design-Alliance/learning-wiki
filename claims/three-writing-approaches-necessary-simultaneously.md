Jessica R. Hoehn and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Framework of goals for writing in physics lab classes. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010125)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Background section (type e, authors' interpretation of the literature). They contrast writing as communication, which foregrounds the final product, with WTL, which 'focuses on the process of writing as a tool for thinking and learning,' and the WAP approach, which emphasizes mastering disciplinary forms of reasoning and argumentation.

> "Our interpretation of the literature we have drawn from in this section is that each of the three approaches to writing—Communication, WTL, and WAP—are neces- sary for writing in science, and that we can attend to all three simultaneously, perhaps emphasizing one over the others depending on the context."
