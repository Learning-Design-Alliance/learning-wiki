## Evidence

### Nabizadeh Sakineh; Hajian Sepideh; Sheikhan Zohre; Rafiei Fatemeh 2019

Nabizadeh Sakineh; Hajian Sepideh; Sheikhan Zohre; Rafiei Fatemeh. (2019). Prediction of academic achievement based on learning strategies and outcome expectations among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1527-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling (maximum likelihood, AMOS) on the 380-student sample; Table 4 reports the learning strategies → average score path with standardized loading 0.167, T = 1.965, p = 0.049, while outcome expectancy (p = 0.817) and motivated strategies (p = 0.139) were not significant.

> "having examined the simultaneous effect of each of these variables as an independent variable on the CGPA, the standard coefficients of the model indicated that the variable of learning strategies has a greater effect on the increase in the CGPA and, in turn, on academic achievement of the students"
