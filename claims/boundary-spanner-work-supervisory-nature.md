## Evidence

### Burns 2015

Burns, R. W., & Baker, W. (2015). The Boundary Spanner in Professional Development Schools: In Search of Common Nomenclature. School-University Partnerships, 9(2), 28-40. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130688

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Meta-analysis coding of boundary-spanner responsibilities across thirteen studies using structural codes from supervision frameworks. The authors found "multiple tasks of preservice teacher supervision" were enacted, with Collaboration and Community the only universal task.

> "individuals in formalized boundary-spanning roles engaged in multiple tasks of preservice teacher supervision that included Teaching, Collaboration and Community, Individual Support, Equity, Curriculum Development and Support, and Research for Innovation."
