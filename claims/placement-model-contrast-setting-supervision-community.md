## Evidence

### Golos Anat 2019

Golos Anat, Tekuzener Esti. (2019). Perceptions, expectations and satisfaction levels of occupational therapy students prior to and after practice placement and comparison of practice placement models. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1762-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney tests comparing post-placement scores of 128 role-established and 27 role-emerging students across four categories. Setting and supervision: role-established M = 4.23 vs role-emerging M = 3.60, Z = −4.10, p < .001; community: role-established M = 3.81 vs role-emerging M = 4.08, Z = −2.35, p = .019. No effect sizes were printed for these comparisons.

> "the role-established placement scores were significantly (p < .001) higher than the role-emerging placement scores. However, in the categories of personal and professional skills, the results indicated nonsignificant differences (p > .05)"
