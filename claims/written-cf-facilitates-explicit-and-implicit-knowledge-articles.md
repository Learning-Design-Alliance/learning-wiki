## Evidence

### Jiang and Xiao 2014

Lin Jiang & Hailing Xiao. (2014). The Efficacy of Written Corrective Feedback and Language Analytic Ability on Chinese Learners’ Explicit and Implicit Knowledge of English Articles. English Language Teaching, 7(10). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n10p22

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-treatment-posttest-delayed posttest study with 92 low-intermediate Chinese EFL learners in three intact classes randomly assigned to DOC, DMC, or control. One-way ANOVAs revealed significant between-group differences at Posttest 1 and 2 on both tests, with both treatment groups outperforming control.

> "As for the error correction and picture-cued writing tests, both treatment groups performed much better than the control group in Posttest 1 and 2, suggesting that written CF can facilitate the acquisition of implicit as well as explicit knowledge."
