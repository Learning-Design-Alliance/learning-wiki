## Evidence

### Harris Cooper 2010

Harris Cooper, Ashley Batts Allen, Erika A. Patall, and Amy L. Dent. (2010). Effects of Full-Day Kindergarten on Academic Achievement and Social Development. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654309359185

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The meta-analysis reports that the kindergarten-year achievement advantage of "about one quarter standard deviation" was not maintained; the article states the association "disappeared by third grade" and discusses reasons for this fade-out.

> "a positive association with academic achievement (compared to half-day kindergarten) equal to about one quarter standard deviation at the end of the kindergarten year. But the association disappeared by third grade."
