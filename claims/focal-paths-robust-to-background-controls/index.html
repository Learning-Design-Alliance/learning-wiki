q2 · i? · associational · r2

Two-stage control analysis with standardized construct scores and 5,000 case-wise bootstrap samples; the quote reports that "the absolute changes in path coefficients did not exceed 0.008". Explained variance increased only marginally (incremental f² of 0.012–0.026).

"the direction and significance of every focal path remained unchanged after the controls were included, and the absolute changes in path coefficients did not exceed 0.008"

Zou J, Li N, Wang X and Du J (2026) From AI literacy to AI-integrated inquiry-based science teaching: the serial mediating roles of AI-TPACK and science teaching self-efficacy among Chinese pre-service science teachers. Front. Psychol. 17:1911909. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1911909

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Secondary control-model analysis of the same 548-teacher sample; the quote reports the sometimes/often versus rarely contrast with "β = 0.099, p = 0.032" and "β = 0.096, p = 0.033", and that very frequent use was not significant.

"compared with par- ticipants who rarely used AI, those who used AI sometimes (β = 0.099, p = 0.032) or often (β = 0.096, p = 0.033) reported higher STSE, whereas very frequent AI use was not significant"
