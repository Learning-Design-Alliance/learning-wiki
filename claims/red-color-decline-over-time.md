# Red color effects declined over time: initial studies found substantially larger effects than subsequent research

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cumulative meta-analyses showed a general decline of pooled effects over time; initial studies found Δ = -0.86 (p < .001) for reasoning tests, while subsequent findings identified Δ = -0.34. [→ Gnambs Timo 2020](#gnambs-timo-2020)

## Evidence

### Gnambs Timo 2020

Gnambs Timo. (2020). Limited evidence for the effect of red color on cognitive performance: A meta-analysis. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-020-01772-1

`q3 · i?`

Cumulative meta-analyses sequentially pooling studies by publication year (Fig. 2): for anagram and knowledge tests no time point showed a significant effect; for reasoning tests effects were significant but "gradually became smaller over time."

> "Whereas initial studies found substantial color effects for reasoning tests (Δ = -0.86, p < .001, 95% CI [-1.31, -0.41]), subsequent findings identified a more modest pooled effect of Δ = -0.34, p = .016, 95% CI [-0.61, -0.06]."
