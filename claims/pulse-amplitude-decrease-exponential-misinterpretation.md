## Evidence

### Richard N. Steinberg 1997

Richard N. Steinberg, Michael C. Wittmann, and Edward F. Redish. (1997). Mathematical Tutorials in Introductory Physics. AIP Conference Proceedings 399, 1075-1092. [doi:10.1063/1.53110](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.53110)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interview (N=9) and pretest (N=57) administration of the Fig. 1 pulse-sketch problem after traditional instruction. Correct unchanged-amplitude sketches were given by "44% of the students who were interviewed and 56% of the students who took the pretest"; most others drew decreased amplitude.

> "This answer was given by 44% of the students who were interviewed and 56% of the students who took the pretest. Most of the rest of the students (56% of the interview-students and 35% of the pretest-students) drew a pulse displaced an amount x0 but the amplitude decreased"
