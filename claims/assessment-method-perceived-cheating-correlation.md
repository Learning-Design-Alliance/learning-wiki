## Evidence

### Shadnaz Asgari 2021

Shadnaz Asgari, Jelena Trajkovic, Mehran Rahmani, Wenlu Zhang, Roger C. Lo, and Antonella Sciortino. (2021). An Observational Study of Engineering Online Education During the COVID-19 Pandemic. IEEE Access. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0250041](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250041)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of faculty assessment methods against trichotomized perception of online cheating relative to face-to-face (faculty survey question #8, 110 faculty respondents). The analysis found no statistically significant correlation except fully online exam with printed correlation of -0.40 (p-value<0.0001), semi-online asynchronous exam with 0.26 (p-value = 0.005), and open-book/closed-book with -0.30 (p-value = 0.002); semi-online asynchronous exams were associated with more perceived cheating, fully online and open-book with less.
