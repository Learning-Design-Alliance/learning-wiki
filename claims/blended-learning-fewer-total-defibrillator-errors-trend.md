## Evidence

### Siebert JN 2022

Siebert JN, Glangetas A, Grange M, Haddad K, Courvoisier DS, Lacroix L. (2022). Impact of blended learning on manual defibrillator's use: A simulation‐based randomized trial. Nurs Crit Care. 2022;27(4):501‐511. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.12713

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 101 nurses and residents randomized 1:1 to blended or face-to-face learning; primary outcome was total errors during three simulation-based scenarios. The article reports the pooled median errors and the P = .06 comparison, with no effect size printed.

> "Health care providers exposed to BL showed fewer median total errors per participant for the three electrical procedures pooled (2 [IQR: 1‐4]) than those exposed to FFL only (3 [IQR: 2‐5]), approaching statistical significance (P = .06)"
