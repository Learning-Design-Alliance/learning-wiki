### Zhang Xiangping 2024

Zhang Xiangping, Qian Wensheng. (2024). The effect of social support on academic performance among adolescents: The chain mediating roles of self-efficacy and learning engagement. PLoS One 19(12): e0311597. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0311597

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (2000 samples) in the same 265-student study treated learning engagement as a mediator; the printed point estimate was 0.092 with confidence intervals excluding zero, supporting partial mediation.

> "Lastly, the pathway of social support→learning engagement→academic performance had an indirect effect of 0.092 (95% bias-corrected CI [0.042, 0.171], percentile CI [0.036, 0.158], P<0.01)."
