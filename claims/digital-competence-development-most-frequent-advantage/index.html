Descriptive frequency analysis of primary advantages across the RQ1/RQ2 subset (N = 155). The quote shows digital competence development at "n = 118; 76.1%", with flexibility/access second at 8.4% and pedagogical innovation at 5.2%. The review interprets the distribution descriptively, not as evidence of relative effectiveness.

"Digital competence development was by far the most frequently reported advantage (n = 118; 76.1%), followed by flexibility, access and continuity (n = 13; 8.4%), pedagogical innovation and redesign (n = 8; 5.2%)"
