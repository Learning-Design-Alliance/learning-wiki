### Daniel Dziob 2015

Daniel Dziob, Łukasz Kwiatkowski, Dagmara Sokołowska. (2015). Studying the impact of the class tournament as an assessment method on student achievements in physics courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.08663](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.08663)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of the same n = 28 sample (Fig. 8(d)) relating the modified gain factor to former-test scores. The authors infer "the tournament provided equal opportunities of improvement to all students"; the regression for MGF likewise found FT not significant (p = 0.2778, R2 = 0.1841).

> "Some slight (r = 0.1259), yet statistically insignificant correlation is observed between MGF and FT, hinting at no dependence of a student gain upon his/her previous performance"
