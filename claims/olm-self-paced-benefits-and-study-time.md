Descriptive within-OLM-group questionnaire results (n = 39, Table 3). Students also reported using the pause function (76.9%) and repeat function (56.4%), and mean study time was 3.46 ± 2.36 h; these are self-reports with no inferential tests.

> "Self-paced learning was perceived as beneficial for retention and motivation (89.7% and 82.1%, respectively). In addition, many students considered the OLM format to allow more effective use of study time (82.1%) and to support better attention (74.4%)."
