Hierarchical linear modeling of the same survey data shows splitting hands-on roles all semester negatively predicts attitude post-scores, with significant negative effects for self-efficacy, perceived agency, and recognition; the quoted sentence prints no effect size value.

> "More time spent splitting roles corresponded to the strongest negative effect sizes (statistically significantly less than zero for self-efficacy, perceived agency, and recognition)."
