Descriptive analysis of ISAT cut scores set by a two-stage rating process and shown in Figure 1, on the 120-to-200 scale for grades three, five, and eight in reading and mathematics. In mathematics the cut scores "become progressively higher moving up the grades."

"In mathematics, the cut scores become progressively higher moving up the grades. In other words, a higher score is required to meet state standards. For third grade it is 152.5, for fifth 157.5, and for eighth 161.5."
