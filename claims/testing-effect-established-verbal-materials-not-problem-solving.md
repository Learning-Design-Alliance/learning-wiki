Background synthesis in the article's introduction, citing prior reviews and studies (e.g., Roediger and Karpicke 2006a) showing the testing effect across varied materials; the article notes problem-solving tasks "have received very little attention in research on the testing effect". This is second-hand attribution, not a new empirical result of this study.

> "The testing effect refers to the finding that after an initial study opportunity, testing is more effective for long-term retention than not testing and even than restudying (for a review, see Roediger and Karpicke 2006a)."
