`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

LC-CUSUM analysis of the prospective cohort using console time thresholds (<165/180 min) and absence of PSM ≥3 mm as success criteria. The study reports "Senior 1 met the pre-defined proficiency criteria after 23 procedures" and "Junior 1 ... achieved proficiency after 37 procedures"; Junior 2 had not met the threshold by study end.

> "The LC-CUSUM analysis demonstrated that Senior 1 met the pre-defined proficiency criteria after 23 procedures, following an initial phase of performance deviation. Junior 1 demonstrated performance deviations during the intermediate phase of the learning curve and achieved proficiency after 37 procedures."
