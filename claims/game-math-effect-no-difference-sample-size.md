# The effect of games on mathematics achievement does not differ significantly by sample size

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Between-group homogeneity for sample size was not significant (QB = 1.016, p = 0.602) across 21–40, 41–60 and 61-or-more person groups. [→ Sedat Turgut 2017](#sedat-turgut-2017)

## Evidence

### Sedat Turgut 2017

Sedat Turgut, Özlem Dogan Temur. (2017). The Effect of Game-Assisted Mathematics Education on Academic Achievement in Turkey: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2017236115

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

> "Therefore, the academic achievement attained by using games with different sample sizes does not show a statistically significant differentiation."
