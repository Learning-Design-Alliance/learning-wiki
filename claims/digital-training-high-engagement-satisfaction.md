### Tembo Tapiwa A 2026

Tembo Tapiwa A, Rosenberg Nora E, Mollan Katie, Kim Maria, Rutstein Sarah E, Mkandawire Angella M, Chitani Mike J, Kumbuyo Caroline, Phiri Duncan, Mphande Mtisunge, Kavuta Elijah, Chilala Samuel, Wang Jiayu, Ahmed Saeed, Simon Katherine, Bekker Linda-Gail. (2026). Digital Training for Lay Health Care Workers’ Knowledge and Skills in HIV Index Case Testing: Cluster Randomized Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/89942

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of monitored completion in the enhanced arm only. "100% of the lay HCWs completed 8 hours of the self-paced digital training portion within the allotted 3 weeks"; uptake of the small group face-to-face training sessions was 98%, and the virtual phone feedback session was 100%.

> "In the enhanced arm, 100% of the lay HCWs completed 8 hours of the self-paced digital training portion within the allotted 3 weeks. Uptake of the small group face-to-face training sessions was 98%, and the virtual phone feedback session was 100%. Quality improvement sessions ranged from 81% to 93%."

### Tembo Tapiwa A 2026 (2)

Tembo Tapiwa A, Rosenberg Nora E, Mollan Katie, Kim Maria, Rutstein Sarah E, Mkandawire Angella M, Chitani Mike J, Kumbuyo Caroline, Phiri Duncan, Mphande Mtisunge, Kavuta Elijah, Chilala Samuel, Wang Jiayu, Ahmed Saeed, Simon Katherine, Bekker Linda-Gail. (2026). Digital Training for Lay Health Care Workers’ Knowledge and Skills in HIV Index Case Testing: Cluster Randomized Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/89942

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Descriptive posttraining evaluation survey in the enhanced arm only. "Most participants expressed satisfaction with self-paced training sessions (107/125, 85%) and small-group face-to-face training sessions (112/125, 90%)"; overall helpfulness was rated with a mean of 4.3 (SD 0.6) and ease of use with a mean of 4.8 (SD 0.6).

> "Most participants expressed satisfaction with self-paced training sessions (107/125, 85%) and small-group face-to-face training sessions (112/125, 90%). Additionally, the majority of participants (108/125, 86%) agreed that the digital training and face-to-face training content technical levels were just right"
