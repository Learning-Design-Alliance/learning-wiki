### Williams Adrienne E. 2026

Williams Adrienne E., Denaro Kameryn. (2026). Studying with Friends in Introductory Biology Is Associated with Low Impact Study Strategies and No Improvement of Exam Scores. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-04-0074

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Inductive content analysis of open-ended Week 10 responses from students who studied with friends (n = 199), coded by two researchers with 92% agreement. Learning (38%) and collaboration (32%) were most frequent, but "none of the reasons why students valued studying with friends" predicted exam scores after model selection.

> "When we conducted model selection, none of the reasons why students valued studying with friends (learning, collaboration, metacognition, social, explaining, and other) remained in the final model, suggesting that the expressed reason for studying with friends provides an insignificant increase in predictive power on exam scores"
