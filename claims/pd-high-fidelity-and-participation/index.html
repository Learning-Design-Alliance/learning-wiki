q3 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive comparison of self-reported math-related PD during the study year; reported focus differences on K–8 math content and student thinking were statistically significant, ranging from 0.7 to 1.3 on a 4-point survey scale.

"Overall, treatment teachers participated in 95 more hours of math-related PD than did control teachers, which is close to the approximately 93 hours of math PD provided by the study"
