## Evidence

### Lazarides 2018

Lazarides, R., Gaspard, H., & Dicke, A.-L. (2018). Dynamics of classroom motivation: Teacher enthusiasm and the development of math interest and teacher support. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.012

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 1 multilevel latent change analysis of 47 classrooms; the latent correlation between the two change scores was Φ = .46, SE = .26, p = .07, non-significant; no equivalence test was reported.

> "Contrary to Hypothesis 1b, the latent correlations (Φ) in this model showed that changes in the students' mathematics interest were not significantly associated with changes in the student-perceived teacher support (Φ = .46, SE = .26, p = .07)."
