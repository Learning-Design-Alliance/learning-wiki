## Evidence

### Behjat 2013

Behjat, F. (2013). Joint Book Reading: How Do Blogs Foster Collaborative Comprehension? i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 3 No. 2, April-June. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068891

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of mean gain scores by group and gender in the same 62-student quasi-experiment. Female experimental students gained 4.4400, male experimental students ranked second, and control students gained least; the article reports the gender hypothesis "is rejected here" with no effect size printed.

> "According to Table 6, the highest gain score belonged to the female students who were in the experimental group (gain = 4.4400); the gain score for male students in the experimental group was ranked the second."
