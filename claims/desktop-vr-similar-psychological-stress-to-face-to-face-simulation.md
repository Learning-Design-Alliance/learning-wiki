## Evidence

### Liaw Sok Ying 2023

Liaw Sok Ying, Sutini, Chua Wei Ling, Tan Jian Zhi, Levett-Jones Tracy, Ashokka Balakrishnan, Te Pan Terry Ling, Lau Siew Tiang, Ignacio Jeanette. (2023). Desktop Virtual Reality Versus Face-to-Face Simulation for Team-Training on Stress Levels and Performance in Clinical Deterioration: a Randomised Controlled Trial. Journal of General Internal Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-022-07557-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this RCT, 120 medical and nursing students in interprofessional teams were randomly assigned to a 2-h desktop VR or face-to-face simulation. ANCOVA with baseline covariates showed no significant between-group difference in psychological stress ("F = 0.43, p = 0.51").

> "There were no significant differences in the psychological (F = 0.43, p = 0.51) and biophysiological stress responses for SBP (F = 2.73, p = 0.10) and HR (F = 3.15, p = 0.95) between participants in the VR and SP groups after the team training simulation"
