`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors argue, from the sound card's audio sampling capability, that its timing resolution is a fraction of a millisecond and "comparable to that of photogate timers". This is an analytical claim about the instrument rather than a controlled accuracy comparison.

> "a computer sound card records audio frequencies up to several kilohertz, which means it has a time resolution of a fraction of millisecond, comparable to that of photogate timers"
