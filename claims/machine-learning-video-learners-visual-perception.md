## Evidence

### Jarodzka 2020

Jarodzka, H., Skuballa, I., & Gruber, H. (2020). Eye-Tracking in Educational Practice: Investigating Visual Perception Underlying Teaching and Learning in the Classroom. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09565-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The review reports Goldberg, Sümer, Stürmer, Wagner, Göllner, Gerjets, Kasneci, and Trautwein's study of university students' visual perception within an authentic lecture using regular video recording rather than eye-tracking. The review notes its potential for educational practice where video recordings of large numbers of learners are more feasible.

> "The innovative aspect of this study is the use of machine learning to automatically analyze these video recordings allowing to draw conclusions on learners' visual perception."
