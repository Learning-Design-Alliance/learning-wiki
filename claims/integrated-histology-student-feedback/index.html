Lu et al. 2016¶

Xiaoye Lu, Xin Cheng, Ke Li, Kenneth Ka Ho Lee & Xuesong Yang. (2016). Integration of Histology Lectures and Practical Teaching in China. International Journal of Higher Education, 5(4). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n4p157

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Online questionnaire (Sojump) of all 24 integrated-course participants; descriptive percentages only, no inferential test. The article also reports 75.4% agreement on interest, 72.3% on time allotment, and 61.5% on maximizing learning time.
