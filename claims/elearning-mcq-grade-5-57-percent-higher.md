## Evidence

### Stevens Niall T. 2019

Stevens Niall T., Holmes Killian, Grainger Rachel J., Connolly Roisín, Prior Anna-Rose, Fitzpatrick Fidelma, O'Neill Eoghan, Boland Fiona, Pawlikowska Teresa, Humphreys Hilary. (2019). Can e-learning improve the performance of undergraduate medical students in Clinical Microbiology examinations? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1843-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparative cohort study: 313 students in 2016/2017 (no online material) and 309 in 2017/2018 (with online material) sat the same MCQ and SNQ papers. "For the MCQ examination, the average difference was 5.57%" in favour of the e-learning cohort, statistically significant.

> "For the MCQ examination, the average difference was 5.57% (95% CI: 3.92 to 7.24%; P < 0.001), and for the SNQ examination, 2.08% (95% CI: 0.74 to 3.41%; P = 0.02)."
