## Evidence

### Coller et al. 2011

B. D. Coller, D. J. Shernoff, and A. D. Strati. (2011). Measuring Engagement as Students Learn Dynamic Systems and Control with a Video Game. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076061

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Separate two-level models were conducted for each of six dependent variables using only the participants and self-reports in Year 3 in which students indicated that they were playing the video game. During Wave 2, students were designing balancing feedback control laws for the pendu-car using PD techniques.

> "The activity associated with the second wave of data collection had a positive influence on Engagement, Intrinsic Motivation, Positive Affect and Work and Play, and a negative effect on Negative Affect. Wave 3 did not produce a significant effect on any of the outcome variables, however."
