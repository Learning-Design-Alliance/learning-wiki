Kok Maryse C 2014¶

Kok Maryse C, Dieleman Marjolein, Taegtmeyer Miriam, Broerse Jacqueline EW, Kane Sumit S, Ormel Hermen, Tijm Mandy M, de Koning Korrie AM. (2014). Which intervention design factors influence performance of community health workers in low- and middle-income countries? A systematic review. Health Policy and Planning. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4597042/

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports nine studies referring to positive influence of community selection on CHW motivation or performance, but also that Kenyan CHWs selected by the community "did not show any difference in guideline adherence" compared with CHWs without community involvement in selection. No effect size is printed.

"However, CHWs in Kenya, who reported to be selected by the community, did not show any difference in guideline adherence when compared to CHWs who reported no community involvement in selection (Rowe et al. 2007a)."
