Evidence¶

Ayame Tamura 2022¶

Ayame Tamura, Kou Murayama, Ryo Ishii, Michiko Sakaki and Ayumi Tanaka. (2022). The effect of low-intensity exercise on emotional and cognitive engagement in the classroom. npj Science of Learning 7:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00125-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear mixed-effects final models for the remaining four dependent variables in the nine-lesson classroom intervention. The condition effects on "depression, interest, sleep frequency, and mind-wandering" were all non-significant (Table 3, unstandardised B with exercise coded 1 and control −1: depression −0.04, P = 0.08; interest 0.04, P = 0.11; sleep frequency −0.06, P = 0.20; mind-wandering −0.05, P = 0.43). No standardised effect size is printed, so impact is coded i?; non-significance here does not show the effects are zero, and equivalence was not tested.

"The condition effects were not signiﬁcant in the models predicting depression, interest, sleep frequency, and mind-wandering."
