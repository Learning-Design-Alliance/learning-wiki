Evidence¶

Harper 2011¶

Harper, L. J. (2011). Nursery rhyme knowledge and phonological awareness in preschool children. The Journal of Language and Literacy Education [Online], 7(1), 65-78. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097164

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group comparison of N=11 experimental children on the revised PALS-Pre-K tasks (beginning sound awareness, rhyme awareness, nursery rhyme awareness) before and after the ten-week training. The mean rose from 11.45 to 16.55, reported as "significantly different (Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, two-tailed, Z=-2.943, P=0.003)".

"The pre-test and post-test pre-K PALS scores for the experimental group were significantly different (Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, two-tailed, Z=-2.943, P=0.003)."
