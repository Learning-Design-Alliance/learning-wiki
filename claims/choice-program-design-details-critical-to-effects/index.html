The authors' interpretive conclusion from their synthesis of voucher and charter evidence, presented in the publication's own summary. No effect sizes are printed on the page.

"the details of policy design are likely to be critical to the effects produced by choice programs: not all voucher programs and charter laws are alike, and different programs will produce different effects"
