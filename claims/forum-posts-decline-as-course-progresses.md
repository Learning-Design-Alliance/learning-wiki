## Evidence

### Simon Fauvel 2015

Simon Fauvel, Han Yu. (2015). A Survey on Artificial Intelligence and Data Mining for MOOCs. Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The survey reports study [54], which split forum posts into common MOOC subcategories and statistically analysed post counts as a function of normalised course length, showing "an exponential decay of posts across all subcategories". A similar study [55] found a sharp decline across all subcategories.

> "They show an exponential decay of posts across all subcategories."

### Simon Fauvel 2015 (2)

Simon Fauvel, Han Yu. (2015). A Survey on Artificial Intelligence and Data Mining for MOOCs. Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The survey reports that study [55] used linear regression to identify factors explaining the decline in forum involvement, finding "active participation of the instructors is associated with an increase in discussion volume" without reducing the decline rate.

> "Interestingly, they find that active participation of the instructors is associated with an increase in discussion volume but does not reduce the participation decline rate."
