## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Women participants selected higher proportions of women than men participants did in all four nomination categories: study partners (59.4% vs. 32.9%), help sources (52.4% vs. 34.3%), most knowledgeable (49.7% vs. 33.7%), and best students (49.5% vs. 32.4%), all p < .001. ## Evidence

### Bloodhart 2020

Bloodhart, B., Balgopal, M. M., Casper, A. M. A., Sample McMeeking, L. B., & Fischer, E. V. (2020). Outperforming yet undervalued: Undergraduate women in STEM. PLoS ONE 15(6): e0234685. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0234685

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear multilevel models of 935 survey participants' peer nominations, controlling for the proportion of women enrolled in each class. The quote reports the study-with and help-seeking contrasts; the same section reports parallel significant contrasts for knowledgeable and best-student nominations (F(1, 553) = 18.71; F(1, 791.88) = 50.75, both p < .001). No standardized effect sizes are printed.

> "Women (M = 59.4%) were more likely than men (M = 32.9%) to select a higher proportion of women students with whom they chose to study, F (1, 541) = 61.31, p < .001, and women (M = 52.4%) were more likely than men (M = 34.3%) to select a higher proportion of women students from whom they sought help, F (1, 663) = 30.86, p < .001."

### Bloodhart 2020 (2)

Bloodhart, B., Balgopal, M. M., Casper, A. M. A., Sample McMeeking, L. B., & Fischer, E. V. (2020). Outperforming yet undervalued: Undergraduate women in STEM. PLoS ONE 15(6): e0234685. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0234685

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretive argument in the Discussion: because women outperformed men in both course types, even gender-equal nomination of best students constitutes underestimation of women's performance. This is the authors' reasoning, not a tested result.

> "However, since women outperformed men across both life and physical sciences, picking an equal number of men and women is still underestimating the performance of women in the class."
