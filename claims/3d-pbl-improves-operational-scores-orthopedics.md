### Li Ting 2025

Li Ting, Hu Yidan, Song Ruohong, Liu Hangyu, Gao Xin, Wang Fei, Xiao Chengwei, Liu Xilin. (2025). Unveiling the ultimate advantage: a meta-analysis of 3D visualization and problem-based learning in orthopedic education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06654-5

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of nine RCTs (N = 565) with a random-effects model found "3D + PBL teaching yielded higher operational scores compared to LBL teaching". GRADE certainty was very low; significant heterogeneity was detected (I2 = 94%).

> "The analysis indicated that 3D + PBL teaching yielded higher operational scores compared to LBL teaching (SMD = 2.29, 95% CI: 1.41–3.16, P < 0.00001; see Fig. 4)."
