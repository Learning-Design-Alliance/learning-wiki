Stieglitz Verena, Buggenhagen Holger, Strelow Kai-Uwe, Schmidtmann Irene, Skala Christine, Kurz Sandra. (2023). Action competence in obstetric emergencies—can this be achieved via e-learning? Interprofessional blended learning module on diagnostics and emergency treatment of shoulder dystocia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04335-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported e-learning usage collected from the 174 study participants before the simulation course. The article reports that "every participant had dealt with the e-learning in advance, at least in part," with 95 completing it fully and others reporting 20–90% completion.

> "The evaluation showed that every participant had dealt with the e-learning in advance, at least in part. 95 participants stated that they had completed the e-learning in full."
