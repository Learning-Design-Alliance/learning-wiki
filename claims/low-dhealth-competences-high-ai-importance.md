## Evidence

### Gerit Wagner 2025

Gerit Wagner, Mickaël Ringeval, Louis Raymond, Guy Paré. (2025). Digital health competences and AI beliefs as conditions for the practice of evidence-based medicine: a study of prospective physicians in Canada. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2459910

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the survey of 177 students: "the students had below-average dHealth knowledge competences ([1.9, 2.5], SD = 0.8)"; experiential competences were also low.

> "Generally, the students had below-average dHealth knowledge competences ([1.9, 2.5], SD = 0.8), being less familiar with advanced technologies, such as AI or wearables, than with traditional technologies."

### Gerit Wagner 2025 (2)

Gerit Wagner, Mickaël Ringeval, Louis Raymond, Guy Paré. (2025). Digital health competences and AI beliefs as conditions for the practice of evidence-based medicine: a study of prospective physicians in Canada. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2459910

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results: "our respondents considered this technology to play an important role in the future of medicine, particularly for their medical practice (4.8, SD = 2.9)" despite low AI knowledge and experience.

> "Despite having low knowledge (2.1, SD = 0.5) and hands-on experience with AI (1.5, SD = 0.6), our respondents considered this technology to play an important role in the future of medicine, particularly for their medical practice (4.8, SD = 2.9)."
