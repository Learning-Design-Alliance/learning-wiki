## Evidence

### Lee Haesu 2026

Lee Haesu, Woo Young Keun, Park Kyue-nam, Kim Sujin. (2026). Online versus offline Fugl-Meyer lower extremity training for physical therapy students: a randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09634-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learning-curve analysis across all four practice sessions and the final test in the randomized trial; the article reports a significant main effect of time (p < .01, partial η² = 0.08) and no significant session × education-type interaction (F(4, 172) = 0.45, p = .772).

> "Post-hoc analysis revealed that accuracy reached its peak at the third session (67.5% ± 2.55%, p = .009) and remained significantly higher at the final test compared to the first session (p = .007). No further significant improvements were noted between the third and subsequent sessions."
