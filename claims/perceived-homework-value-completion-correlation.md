## Evidence

### F. J. Kontur and N. B. Terry 2013

F. J. Kontur and N. B. Terry. (2013). How different incentives affect homework completion in introductory physics courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1305.2342](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1305.2342)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of anonymous questionnaires from ungraded-homework semesters (fall 2011, spring 2012), comparing section-average perceived value to section-average completion (Fig. 2). The article reports "p = 0.001 for the fall 2011 data, p < 0.0001 for the spring 2012 data"; no effect size is printed.

> "Based on the line fits, a 0.5 point increase in the perceived value of homework will, on average, lead to nearly 20% more homework being completed."
