`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Development-phase evaluation of the instructional video. The first version was shown to ten experienced dentists and evaluated with the Think-Aloud method; a second phase involved ten DC/TMD workshop participants and two gold standard examiners, whose suggested changes were added. The article reports "evaluated according to the Think-Aloud method, and modified accordingly" with no effect sizes.
