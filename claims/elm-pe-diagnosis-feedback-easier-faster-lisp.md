Evaluation study of the ELM-PE programming environment, reported as background to ELM-ART's roots. The article states learning was "easier and faster with the support of ELM-PE" than with the same environment lacking cognitive diagnosis and feedback; the results received a best paper award at ED-Media 1994. No effect size is printed.
