Evidence¶

Sopandi 2018¶

Sopandi, W., Kadarohman, A., Rosbiono, M., Latip, A., & Sukardi, R. R. (2018). The Courseware of Discontinuous Nature of Matter in Teaching the States of Matter and Their Changes. International Journal of Instruction, 11(1), 61-76. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1115a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

One-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 65 students (particle-concept sample) using structure-oriented teaching assisted by the courseware; N-gain computed per Hake (1999). The article reports the "effectiveness of the courseware" as 54% overall, a middle-category gain.

"The total N-gain average (male and female data were combined) is 0.54. This score suggests that the effectiveness of the courseware in increasing students' understanding on the particle concept as a whole is 54%."
