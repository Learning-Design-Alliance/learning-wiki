Jun Wu, Shuoli Qi and Yueshan Zhong. (2022). Intrinsic motivation, Need for cognition, Grit, Growth Mindset and Academic Achievement in High School Students: Latent Profiles and Its Predictive Effects. [doi:10.31234/osf.io/mheg7](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/mheg7)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal comparison of 9th- to 12th-grade scores across profiles showed low-grit students improved substantially. The article notes "the performance of students low in grit tends to increase substantially" and that for English "the low grits students show greater potential and the score tends to be higher than any other profiles."

> "In examining the predictive effect of three student profiles on their 12th grade academic performance, we found that the low grit students improved significantly in 12th grade on Math and English."
