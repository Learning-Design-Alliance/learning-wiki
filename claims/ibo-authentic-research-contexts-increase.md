## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The share of theoretical items addressing authentic pieces of research rose from 6% (1993) to 48% (2017; η² = 0.19). [→ Opitz Sebastian 2020](#opitz-sebastian-2020)

## Evidence

### Opitz Sebastian 2020

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r3`

Context-authenticity coding across six IBO cohorts analyzed by ANOVA; authentic-research items rose "1993: 6%; 2017: 48%" (F(5, 618) = 29.12, p < 0.001, η² = 0.19).

> "The trend of increasing numbers of theoretical items involving data analysis (see Figure 3) coincided with steadily more items that addressed authentic pieces of research (1993: 6%; 2017: 48%; F(5, 618) = 29.12, p < 0.001, η² = 0.19)."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

### Opitz Sebastian 2020 (3)

Opitz Sebastian, Harms Ute. (2020). Assessing High Performers in the Life Sciences: Characteristics of Exams Used at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO) and Their Implications for Life Science Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-10-0215

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`
