### Sebring 1995

Sebring, P. A., Bryk, A. S., Luppescu, S., & Thum, Y. M. (1995). Charting Reform in Prairie School: Results of Student and Teacher Surveys. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-prairie-school-results-student-and-teacher-surveys

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the ten-item Principal Leadership scale. The report contrasts these top quartile figures with bottom quartile schools, where almost 40 percent of teachers hold their principals in low regard and less than 20 percent hold the principal in high or very high regard.

> "Ninety-six percent agree or strongly agree with all the statements (scores of 3-5 and higher), with 40 percent registering very high regard (scores of 9-10). These schools have the kind of leadership which prior research has shown can galvanize restructuring efforts."
