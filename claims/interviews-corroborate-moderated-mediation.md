## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Staff interviewees reported that interactive teaching improves learning efficiency and evaluations, that network gains stopped yielding obvious improvements once stable, and that the efficiency–evaluation link weakens with grade level. [→ Zhang Wanting 2025](#zhang-wanting-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhang Wanting 2025

Zhang Wanting, Sun Junhua, Yao Haichang. (2025). The impact of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation in urban-rural interactive classrooms: a moderated mediation model. BMC Psychology, 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03313-8

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke's six-stage framework, NVivo 12, two independent coders) of semi-structured interviews with 9 staff and volunteers; the volunteer minister's quote illustrates the grade-dependent weakening of the efficiency–evaluation link.

> "We have noticed that for lower-grade students, when their learning efficiency improves, teachers' teaching evaluations often significantly improve. However, as the grade level increases, the impact of this learning efficiency on teaching evaluations becomes less obvious."
