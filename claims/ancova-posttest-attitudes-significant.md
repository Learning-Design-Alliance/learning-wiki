## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ANCOVA of post-test attitude scores, adjusting for KCSE Mathematics, English and Physics points, showed statistically significant differences among groups E, C1 and C2 (F = 3.115, df = 2/99, Sig. = 0.049). [→ Daniel M. Getuno 2015](#daniel-m-getuno-2015)

## Evidence

### Daniel M. Getuno 2015

Daniel M. Getuno, Joel K. Kiboss, Johnson M. Changeiywo, & Leo B. Ogola. (2015). Effects of an E-Learning Module on Students' Attitudes in an Electronics Class. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.36. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086475

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Omnibus ANCOVA on post-test attitude scores for the three groups, with students' average KCSE scores in Mathematics, English and Physics as covariates (evaluated at 7.0609 points). The contrast was significant, with "F  (.049, 2, 99)  = 3.115"; no effect size is printed.

> "The results reveal that there were statistically si gnificant differences in post-test scores for stude nts' attitudes where, F  (.049, 2, 99)  = 3.115 > Critical F  (.05, 2, 99)  = 3.0718."
