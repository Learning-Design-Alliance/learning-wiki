## Evidence

### Álvarez-García C. 2019

Álvarez-García C., Álvarez-Nieto C., Kelsey J., Carter R., Sanz-Martos S., López-Medina I.M. (2019). Effectiveness of the e-NurSus Children Intervention in the Training of Nursing Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16214288

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparisons between the Spanish (n = 110) and United Kingdom (n = 157) samples in the quasi-experimental study. The article reports that "the attitudes and skills were higher in British students before the intervention" and Spanish students after. The Spanish session was compulsory while the UK session was optional, which the authors offer as one explanation.

> "The attitudes and skills were higher in British students before the intervention but after the intervention they were higher in Spanish students. The knowledge was higher among Spanish students before and after the intervention."
