Kulakow 2023¶

Kulakow, S., Mahlau, K., & Kocaj, A. (2023). The longitudinal relationship between internalizing and externalizing behavioral problems with academic achievement in elementary school. https://osf.io/cyqak/?view_only=37eade9dd7c142e88fe9bef9451c125e

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the cross-lagged panel models (N = 461), autoregressive paths showed "stable autoregressive effects of standardized estimates ranging from β = 0.62 to β = 0.75" for written language and β = 0.38 to 0.45 for mathematics, all significant.

"we found stable autoregressive effects of standardized estimates ranging from β = 0.62 to β = 0.75. While the estimates were smaller for mathematical achievement (β = 0.38 to β = 0.45), they were throughout significant and indicated medium regression effects."
