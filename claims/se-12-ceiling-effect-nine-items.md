### Axboe Mette K. 2016

Axboe Mette K., Christensen Kaj S., Kofoed Poul-Erik, Ammentorp Jette. (2016). Development and validation of a self-efficacy questionnaire (SE-12) measuring the clinical communication skills of health care professionals. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0798-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of the 195-clinician test-retest sample against the criterion that effects are present if more than 15% of respondents achieve the highest score. Nine items exceeded the limit; among untrained respondents only, 7 of 12 items were not affected.

> "A ceiling effect was present in 9 of 12 self-efficacy questions, which exceeds the >15 % set as a limit. The distribution of respondents marking the highest possible score is shown in Table 2."
