### Jeffrey A. Phillips 2016

Jeffrey A. Phillips, Jeremy E. McCallum, Katharine W. Clemmer, Thomas M. Zachariah. (2016). A Problem-Solving Framework to Assist Students and Teachers in STEM Courses. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1607.07853](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1607.07853)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post survey comparing twelve Likert scale items on students' use of monitoring when solving problems between ACE-M and comparison sections. The article reports statistically significant gains (p< 0.05) in ACE-M courses only; no effect size is printed.

> "students in the ACE-M Calculus and Organic Chemistry courses reported gains in their value and use of monitoring that were statistically significant (p< 0.05), whereas those in comparison courses indicated no such gains."
