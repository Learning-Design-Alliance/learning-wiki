# College students taught with ABSI plus multi-modal instruction outscored ABSI-only peers on science achievement midterm and final exams

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students in the treatment group (ABSI with multi-modal instruction) scored significantly higher than the comparison group (ABSI only) on both midterm and final science achievement exams. [→ Demirbag 2014](#demirbag-2014)

## Evidence

### Demirbag 2014

Demirbag, M., & Gunel, M. (2014). Integrating Argument-Based Science Inquiry with Modal Representations: Impact on Science Achievement, Argumentation, and Writing Skills. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 14(1), 386-391. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2014.1.1632

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing midterm and final exam performance of treatment versus comparison groups in a quasi-experimental study of 119 third-year science education students; the article reports "the treatment group outscored the comparison group" on both measures.

> "ANOV A results yielded that there were statistical performance differences between the groups on midterm scores (F(1,115)=5.73, p< 0.05) and final exam scores (F(1,115)=5.34 p< 0.05). In both measurements, the treatment group outscored the comparison group."
