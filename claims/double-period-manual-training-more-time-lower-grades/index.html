Evidence¶

Turner 1912¶

Turner, E. A.; Markus, H. P. (1912). Rules versus drill in teaching spelling; Some results in manual training. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069736

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational comparison of time and grade records from 14 boys working single periods and 9 boys working double periods in manual training during one school year, using records made in the same semester under practically the same conditions. The printed table of averages gives grand averages of 6.10 vs. 7.98 periods and 79.8 vs. 72.9 grades. No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"seemed to indicate that in general boys who worked double periods needed more time to complete a given exercise than those who worked single periods, and a careful comparison was made of the time and grades on seven drawings and four bench exercises which were done by all of the pupils."
