## Evidence

### Tsukamoto Atsushi 2026

Tsukamoto Atsushi, Zan Thum Su, Nitta Makie, Yoshida Hiromitsu, Katahira Hirotaka, Fujita Yoshiharu, Takagi Satoshi, Nakamura Shinichiro. (2026). Educational efficacy of training videos and simulators for teaching basic mouse experimental skills to novice veterinary students. Experimental Animals. https://doi.org/10.1538/expanim.25-0093

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre-practice psychological state item in the post-practice questionnaire of 149 novice veterinary students. "In total, 84% of the students reported feeling “a little anxious” or “very anxious”" before handling live mice.

> "In total, 84% of the students reported feeling “a little anxious” or “very anxious”"

### Tsukamoto Atsushi 2026 (2)

Tsukamoto Atsushi, Zan Thum Su, Nitta Makie, Yoshida Hiromitsu, Katahira Hirotaka, Fujita Yoshiharu, Takagi Satoshi, Nakamura Shinichiro. (2026). Educational efficacy of training videos and simulators for teaching basic mouse experimental skills to novice veterinary students. Experimental Animals. https://doi.org/10.1538/expanim.25-0093

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Spearman correlations (Table 2) between anxiety and tool variables: necessity (videos) r=0.22 P<0.01; necessity (Mimicky) r=0.41 P<0.001; frequency (videos) r=0.30 P<0.001; frequency (Mimicky) r=0.45 P<0.001; frequency (fabric toy) r=0.26 P<0.001.

> "In addition, positive correlations were found between the students’ anxiety levels and both the frequency of use and perceived necessity of each tool (Table 2)."

### Tsukamoto Atsushi 2026 (3)

Tsukamoto Atsushi, Zan Thum Su, Nitta Makie, Yoshida Hiromitsu, Katahira Hirotaka, Fujita Yoshiharu, Takagi Satoshi, Nakamura Shinichiro. (2026). Educational efficacy of training videos and simulators for teaching basic mouse experimental skills to novice veterinary students. Experimental Animals. https://doi.org/10.1538/expanim.25-0093

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of students divided into anxiety and non-anxiety groups (Table 3): training videos 4.7±0.6 vs 4.2±0.9; Mimicky Mouse 4.0±0.9 vs 3.1±1.1; fabric toy 3.8±1.0 vs 3.2±1.1; all P<0.05.

> "The group with anxiety showed significantly higher scores for frequency of use (Table 3)."
