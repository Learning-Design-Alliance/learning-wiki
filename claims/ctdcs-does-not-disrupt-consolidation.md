## Evidence

### Taewon Kim 2024

Taewon Kim, Hakjoo Kim, Benjamin A. Philip & David L. Wright. (2024). M1 recruitment during interleaved practice is important for encoding, not just consolidation, of skill memory. npj Science of Learning 9, 77. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00290-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the 2 (Group: IP-Sham, IP-ctDCS) × 3 (Time Point) ANOVA on retention tests, the non-significant Group × Test Block interaction "veriﬁed that the nature of consolidation common ly observed following IP occurred irrespective of the presence or absence of ctDCS".

> "The absence of a Group × Test Block interaction,F (2, 68) = 0.44,p = 0.65, ηp 2 = 0.01) veriﬁed that the nature of consolidation common ly observed following IP occurred irrespective of the presence or absence of ctDCS during training."

### Taewon Kim 2024 (2)

Taewon Kim, Hakjoo Kim, Benjamin A. Philip & David L. Wright. (2024). M1 recruitment during interleaved practice is important for encoding, not just consolidation, of skill memory. npj Science of Learning 9, 77. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00290-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same ANOVA showed a significant main effect of Test Block, which the article reads as showing that "ctDCS had no impact on subsequent consolidation": both IP groups improved offline across test sessions.

> "ctDCS had no impact on subsequent consolidation, as indicated by the signiﬁcant main effect of Test Block,F(2, 68) = 35.51,p <0 . 0 0 1 ,ηp 2 = 0.51."
