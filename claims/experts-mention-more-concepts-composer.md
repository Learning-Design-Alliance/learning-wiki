### Stefan Küchemann 2023

Stefan Küchemann, Malte Ubben, David Dzsotjan, Sergey Mukhametov, Carrie A. Weidner, Carrie A. Weidner, Linda Qerimi, Jochen Kuhn, Stefan Heusler, and Jacob F. Sherson. (2023). The impact of an interactive visualization and simulation tool on learning quantum physics: Results of an eye-tracking study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Transcript coding of verbal answers in the eye-tracking study deduced ten concepts (e.g. amplitude/minima/maxima of wave functions at 83% occurrence); on average four concepts were mentioned per interview, and "the experts referred to a signiﬁcantly higher number of concepts to explain the answers (p = 0.007, d = 1.10)".

> "We found over all tasks, that the experts referred to a signiﬁcantly higher number of concepts to explain the answers (p = 0.007, d = 1.10)."
