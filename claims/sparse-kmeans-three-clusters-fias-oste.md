Clustering analysis of 38 clinical teachers' FIAS-coded behaviors in a 15-minute OSTE feedback station. The article reports that "Figure 1 presents the results of this clustering into three main groups (C1–C3)", with cluster sizes of 20, 10, and 8 teachers respectively.
