## Evidence

### Topacio 2018

Topacio, K. N. M. (2018). Exploring the use of online educational platform in teaching writing among ESL students. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(1), 86-101. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1175799

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group interview data from the experimental group. Students mentioned distractions at home, social media temptations, and desire for more teacher interaction. The author interprets this as "overreliance to teacher‟s lecture" rooted in lecture-based schooling habits.

> "This lack of focus and overreliance to teacher‟s lecture may suggest the lack of autonomous learning skills among students"
