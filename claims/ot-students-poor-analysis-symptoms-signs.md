## Evidence

### Moruno-Miralles P. 2020

Moruno-Miralles P., Reyes-Torres A., Talavera-Valverde M.-Á., Souto-Gómez A.-I., Márquez-Álvarez L.-J. (2020). Learning and Development of Diagnostic Reasoning in Occupational Therapy Undergraduate Students. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/6934579

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of case responses in the multicentre cross-sectional study (n = 247; Table 5): 232 of 247 (93.9%) associated zero symptoms/signs with performance problems. The article states "the ability of students to analyse information was poor."

> "Only 4.9% of the participants associated one symptom or sign, and only three participants in their fourth year associated two or more symptoms or signs."
