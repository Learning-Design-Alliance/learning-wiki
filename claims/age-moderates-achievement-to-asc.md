## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The effect of achievement on ASC decreased as students grew older, being stronger for children (β = 0.18) than for adolescents (β = 0.12). [→ Wu et al. 2021](#wu-et-al-2021)

## Evidence

### Wu et al. 2021

Huimin Wu, Yiqun Guo, Yingkai Yang, Le Zhao and Cheng Guo. (2021). A Meta-analysis of the Longitudinal Relationship Between Academic Self-Concept and Academic Achievement. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09600-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis of the achievement-to-ASC path in the meta-analysis, which showed significant heterogeneity (Q(60) = 874.78, I2 = 92.21%). Age was a significant moderator: "t (54.5) = - 2.26, p = 0.03", with childhood β = 0.18 versus adolescence β = 0.12.

> "student age significantly moderated the effect of achievement on ASC, t (54.5) = - 2.26, p = 0.03. The effect of achievement on ASC was stronger for children (β = 0.18, p \< 0.01) than for adolescents (β = 0.12, p \< 0.01)"
