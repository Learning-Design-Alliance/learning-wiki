### Pelakh 2025

Thematic analysis of student perceptions of resources and demands experienced in introductory physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.14692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.14692)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Pairwise co-occurrence analysis (Fig. 5a) of negative experience codes across the 546 coded segments; only 6 of 15 cells exceeded the even-spread criterion of 20.4. The authors state this shows "the intersection of classroom/course structure, the nature of the learning activity, and the instructors' actions are a major area of negative experiences."

> "The three most common pairwise co-occurrences are the ones between CLASSROOM, INSTRUCTOR, and LEARNING ACTIVITY. These three pairwise co-occurrences account for 42.8% of all pairwise co-occurrences (131 out of 306 pairwise co-occurrences)"
