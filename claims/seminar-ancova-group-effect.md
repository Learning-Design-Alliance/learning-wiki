`q3 i2` In an ANCOVA with pretest scores as covariate, seminar membership significantly predicted pretest-to-posttest improvement, F(1, 158) = 12.8, η²p = 0.075. [→ Simon Cullen 2018](#simon-cullen-2018)

## Evidence

### Simon Cullen 2018

Simon Cullen, Judith Fan, Eva van der Brugge and Adam Elga (2018). Improving analytical reasoning and argument understanding: a quasi-experimental field study of argument visualization. npj Science of Learning 3:21. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0038-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA using group membership as independent variable and pretest scores as covariate, controlling the pretest–improvement relationship (B = −0.40). Group membership "accounted for a signi ﬁcant proportion of the variance in scores", η²p = 0.075.

> "we found a signi ﬁcant effect of membership in the seminar group, B = 1.5, 95% CI: [0.67, 2.34], p < 0.001, which accounted for a signiﬁcant proportion of the variance in scores, F(1, 158) = 12.8, p < 0.001, η2 p = 0.075"
