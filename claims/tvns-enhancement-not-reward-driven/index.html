Evidence¶

Llanos 2020¶

Llanos, F., McHaney, J. R., Schuerman, W. L., Yi, H. G., Leonard, M. K. and Chandrasekaran, B. (2020). Non-invasive peripheral nerve stimulation selectively enhances speech category learning in adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0070-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Supplementary experimental condition (tVNS-feedback) in a shorter version of the training task, compared against tVNS-easy and Control via logit mixed-effects modeling. The tVNS-feedback interaction was non-significant (p = 0.55) while tVNS-easy was significant (β = 0.006, z = 2.8, p < 0.005).

"In contrast, the interaction of trial-by-group for the tVNS-feedback group was not signiﬁcant (β = 0.001, z = 0.59, p = 0.55). These results indicate that pairing stimulation with positive feedback (i.e., feedback following correct responses) did not enhance learning."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Brief tVNS paired with speech training produces no measurable change in the sensory representation of non-native pitch as indexed by frequency-following responses — related
  • Sub-perceptual-threshold tVNS evokes brainstem vagal potentials consistent with peripheral nerve engagement, and the pulse-evoked response follows an inverted-U function of stimulation intensity — related
  • Pairing sub-perceptual tVNS with easier-to-learn Mandarin tone categories improves trial-by-trial speech category learning faster than training alone, and the enhancement is specific to the stimulated categories — related
  • The tVNS-easy group's performance falls outside the normal range of individual learning variability observed across 678 comparable unstimulated learners — related
  • tVNS paired with easier-to-learn categories increases retention of correct stimulus-response associations between training blocks — related
  • tVNS-enhanced speech category learning generalizes to new talkers' exemplars without accompanying stimulation or feedback — related
  • Feedback-stage reward prediction errors, signed or unsigned, do not modulate memory in either learning condition — related
