### Salim Nabhan 2026

Salim Nabhan, Anita Habók. (2026). Language teachers' AI literacy: A psychometric study based on the ED-AI framework. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence 10 (2026) 100583. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100583

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

EFA result (N = 165, PCA with varimax). The Autonomy factor's eigenvalue of 11.458 (38.194% of variance) is substantially larger than other factors'; the authors interpret this as reflecting "the central role of autonomy as a foundational competence" and strong intercorrelations among autonomy items.

> "The fifth factor—Autonomy—included five items with loadings between 0.457 and 0.794, a notably high eigenvalue of 11.458, accounting for 38.194% of the variance, and a Cronbach's alpha of 0.829."
