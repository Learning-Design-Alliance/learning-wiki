# LRBs partially mediate the association between working memory and first-grade reading achievement

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The indirect path from working memory through LRBs to reading was significant and the direct effect of WM on reading remained significant, indicating partial mediation. [→ Rivella 2025](#rivella-2025)

## Evidence

### Rivella 2025

Rivella, C., & Viterbori, P. (2025). Executive functions and school achievement: The mediating role of learning‐related behaviour in primary school children. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70053

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation model (Model 1b) in the same correlational first-grade sample, WM factorial score as predictor, LRB as mediator, silent word-segmentation reading task as outcome, with sex, parents' education, and Raven's scores as covariates; the "significant indirect path" and significant direct effect indicate partial mediation.

> "The mediation hypothesis was confirmed, with a significant indirect path (β = .083, CI [.016, .177]). The direct effect of WM on Reading was also significant (p = .023, CI [.613, 8.158]), indicating partial mediation."
