Evidence¶

Zekun Wu 2026¶

Zekun Wu, Man Su, Huiyong Li, Tomohiro Nagashima, and Anna Maria Feit. (2026). Gaze-Informed Proactive AI Assistance for Children Exploring Picture Books. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00445

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Within-subject gaze analysis restricted to instances where children fixated the secondary AOI. Even the most attentive children did not exceed 3.5 s; "this difference was not statistically significant (𝑝>. 05)"; equivalence was not tested.

"For fixation duration on the secondary AOI (Figure 7a), children spent only a short time looking at the region in both conditions, with a mean fixation duration of 𝑀= 1.08s (𝑆𝐷= 0.90) in the gaze-based condition and 𝑀= 1.02s (𝑆𝐷= 1.13) in the baseline condition; this difference was not statistically significant (𝑝>. 05)."

Zekun Wu 2026 (2)¶

Zekun Wu, Man Su, Huiyong Li, Tomohiro Nagashima, and Anna Maria Feit. (2026). Gaze-Informed Proactive AI Assistance for Children Exploring Picture Books. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00445

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Same study, latency measure from the onset of the secondary AOI's verbal mention to the first fixation on it. Children generally needed substantial time to locate the referenced region; "no significant difference between conditions (𝑝>.05)".

"Time to first fixation was𝑀= 6.65s (𝑆𝐷= 2.26) in the gaze-based condition and 𝑀= 5.65s (𝑆𝐷= 4.26) in the baseline condition, with no significant difference between conditions (𝑝>.05)."
