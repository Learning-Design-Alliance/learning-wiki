The Impact of Group Discussion and Formation on Student Performance: An Experience Report in a Large CS1 Course. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3641554.3701973](https://doi.org/10.1145/3641554.3701973)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational analysis of chat and code-submission logs from 255 groups formed during 11 peer-instruction sessions in a large CS1 course, comparing active and inactive groups. Active groups improved slightly more often (66% vs 63%), but per-session: "In 4 sessions (36%), inactive groups outperformed active groups."

> "Among the active groups, 96 (66%) showed improvement in poor-performing students' coding skills, compared to 69 (63%) in inactive groups."
