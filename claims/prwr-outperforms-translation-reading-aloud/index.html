Azhar Aziz Lubis; Gunadi Harry Sulistyo 2018¶

Azhar Aziz Lubis; Gunadi Harry Sulistyo. (2018). PRWR: Evidence of Its Effectiveness in Teaching Academic Content-Area Reading across English Proficiency. Dinamika Ilmu, Vol. 18 No. 1. http://dx.doi.org/10.21093/di.v18i1.1070

q2 · i? · causal · r1

A 2x2 factorial quasi-experimental posttest-only study in which class C (35 students) was taught with PRWR and class E (23 students) with Translation and Reading Aloud; a Mann-Whitney U test on the 30-item posttest gave "the obtained p-value from the Mann -Whitney U Testing was .045".

"Table 6 demonstrated that the obtained p-value from the Mann -Whitney U Testing was .045, meaning that the obtained p-value was less than the level of significance .05 (Sig .045 < .05)."
