Longitudinal cohort analysis of Texas new teachers from 2012 to 2018, reported in the Teacher Attrition section and Table 2. The report notes the loss of a quarter of a cohort by year 5 challenges workforce stability, given over a third of Texas teachers have 5 years of experience or less.

> "A statewide analysis of new teacher cohorts, following them from 2012 to 2018, found that an average of 8% of new teachers left the classroom after year 1, an average of 26% by year 5, and an average of 46% by year 8 (Table 2)."
