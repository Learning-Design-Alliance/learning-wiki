## Evidence

### Singh 2022

Singh, C., Asfaw, A., & Levy, J. (2022). Preparing students to be leaders of the quantum information revolution. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1119/5.0027661](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0027661)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre/post survey at the 2020 IBM Qiskit Global Summer School, the authors report it was "the largest quantum summer school of its kind" with more than 4000 students; 745 attendees responded before and after the two-week school. The finding is self-reported skill, not a direct learning measure.

> "Hands -on QIST approaches result in deeper understanding and more rapid uptake of quantum concepts, according to a survey conducted after a 2020 remote global summer school hosted by IBM."
