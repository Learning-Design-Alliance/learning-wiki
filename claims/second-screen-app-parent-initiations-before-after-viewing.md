# Parents in the intervention condition tended to initiate more conversations before and after episode viewing than comparison parents, with no difference during viewing

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adults in the intervention condition tended to initiate more conversations before and after episode viewing than comparison adults, while initiations during viewing did not differ across conditions. [→ Dominguez 2021](#dominguez-2021)

## Evidence

### Dominguez 2021

Dominguez, X., Rood, E., Kamdar, D., Leones, T., & Huynh, K. (2021). Splash and Bubbles for Parents App: Field Study Report [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/119

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational coding of conversations during three in-person co-viewing sessions with Cohort 1 families (7 intervention, 9 comparison), documented by research assistants. The study found adults in the intervention condition "tended to initiate more conversations in those moments than parents in the comparison condition" before and after viewing; during-viewing patterns were not different across conditions.

> "even though initiations before and after episode viewing were less frequent than initiations during episodes, adults in the intervention condition tended to initiate more conversations in those moments than parents in the comparison condition"
