## Evidence

### Piumatti Giovanni 2021

Piumatti Giovanni, Cerutti Bernard, Perron Noëlle Junod. (2021). Assessing communication skills during OSCE: need for integrated psychometric approaches. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02552-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis (principal-component method) of the four-item Hodges and McIlroy scale applied separately at each of the study's OSCE stations, reported in Results. The article states the "one-factor solution appeared to be the most appropriate" with the printed loading and alpha ranges.

> "Results from EFA suggested that across all stations the one-factor solution appeared to be the most appropriate to synthesize the four global rating items, with factor loadings ranging from 0.56 to 0.94 and Cronbach's alphas ranging from 0.69 to 0.92"
