Artifact analysis finding from Table 2 ratings: game-like learning objects (Object 15) and 3D design environments (Object 18) enabled learners to choose interaction paths, manipulate media, or embed their own content; the purpose was "not to guide learners through a predetermined path but to let them engage with the content in ways that suited their goals and interests."

> "the table also highlights that receptivity frequently intersects with freedom of action, especially in tools that afford learner creativity and exploration."
