### Michael F. J. Fox 2020

Michael F. J. Fox, Benjamin M. Zwickl, and H. J. Lewandowski (2020). Preparing for the quantum revolution - what is the role of higher education?. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020131)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interview study of 21 quantum companies, employers reported that deep QIS theory is "not a necessary or suﬃcient requirement to work in the quantum industry" while classical skills are highly valued. This is the authors' synthesis of employer responses, not a measured comparison.

> "A deep knowledge of the theory behind quantum infor- mation science is not a necessary or suﬃcient requirement to work in the quantum industry. Indeed, ‘classical’ skills are highly valued."
