## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Fancsali 2016

Fancsali, C., Abe, Y., & Pyatigorsky, M. (2016). Developing Content-Area Literacy: The Reading Apprenticeship Improving Secondary Education (RAISE) project: A Randomized Controlled Trial. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566986

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Year-2 student survey impacts in the cluster RCT (Exhibit 4, N = 11,456–11,463): metacognitive conversations ES = 0.21 (p = .004) and integration of content and literacy activity ES = 0.18 (p = .009); reader identity ES = 0.04 and other domains were not significant.

> "RAISE produced positive and statistically significant impacts on the full sample of students in two student mediating outcome domains that are hallmarks of the Reading Apprenticeship framework"
