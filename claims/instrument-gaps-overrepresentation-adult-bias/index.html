Evidence¶

Kelsey E. Schenck 2024¶

Kelsey E. Schenck, Mitchell J. Nathan. (2024). Navigating Spatial Ability for Mathematics Education: a Review and Roadmap. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09935-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's own analysis of its compiled instrument table (Table 1), identifying gaps in coverage across sub-components and populations. The review states these disparities are essential for educational considerations. No effect size is printed.

"It shows a pressing need for more tasks explicitly designed for other broad sub-components, such as the extrinsic-static classifications. It also reveals that the slate of available instruments is dominated by tasks that have only been tested on adults and few measures that test more than one subcomponent."
