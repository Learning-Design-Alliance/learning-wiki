## Evidence

### Kalani Hettiarachchilage 2023

Kalani Hettiarachchilage, Neel Haldolaarachchige. (2023). Effective Model with Personalized Online Teaching and Learning Science in the Era of ChatGPT. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' qualitative investigation testing ChatGPT against their own ID-integrated assignment problems (Figure 5 example from PHY116). They found ChatGPT always considers given angles with respect to the +X axis, producing wrong force components when the incline angle is defined differently. This is a demonstration, not a systematic evaluation.

> "ChatGPT-generated answers for this question are misleading the students' process of answering such as a free body diagram, wrong X and y components, wrong directions or symbols for acceleration, not showing Newton's second law as discussed in the class, the difficulty of handling ID, misleading conclusions ...etc."
