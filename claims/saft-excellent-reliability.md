## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a reliability study with 21 occupational therapists completing the SAFT twice one week apart, test-retest reliability for total scores was excellent (ICC = 0.95) and inter-rater reliability was excellent at both time points (ICC = 0.995 and 0.99). [→ Buchanan H 2015](#buchanan-h-2015)

## Evidence

### Buchanan H 2015

Buchanan H, Jelsma J, Siegfried N. (2015). Measuring evidence-based practice knowledge and skills in occupational therapy—a brief instrument. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0475-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability study (Study 1) in which 21 of 26 recruited occupational therapists completed the SAFT twice, a median of 7.0 days apart, with two independent raters scoring all questionnaires. The article reports "excellent for total scores" for both test-retest and inter-rater reliability, and satisfactory internal consistency (α = 0.70).

> "Test-retest (ICC = 0.95, 95 % CI = 0.88–0.98) and inter-rater reliability (Time 1: ICC = 0.995, 95 % CI = 0.99–0.998; Time 2: ICC = 0.99, 95 % CI = 0.97–0.995) were excellent for total scores."
