Angelica Paz Ortiz 2019¶

Journal of General Education 67(1), 109-121. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/positionality-in-teaching-implications-for-advancing-social-justice/ doi:10.5325/jgeneeduc.67.1-2.0109

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

This is the authors' reflective assertion in the abstract of a practice-based article; no empirical study, data, or effect size is reported. The article states the practice "normalizes and values these moments" for students encountering a new endeavor.

"If talking about our own mistakes, struggles, and fears is central to the curriculum, that lessens the fear of making mistakes in a new endeavor and even normalizes and values these moments."
