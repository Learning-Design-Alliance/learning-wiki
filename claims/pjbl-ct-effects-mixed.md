## Evidence

### Sofie M. M. Loyens 2023

Sofie M. M. Loyens, Julianne E. van Meerten, Lydia Schaap, Lisette Wijnia. (2023). Situating Higher-Order, Critical, and Critical-Analytic Thinking in Problem- and Project-Based Learning Environments: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09757-x

`q2 · i3` · `review · r3`

Independent-groups pre-post study (Sasson et al., 2018) in a 2-year K-12 PjBL program, n = 38 PjBL vs 27 control, reported in the review's Table 3 with Cohen's d = 0.86 [0.34, 1.37].

> "CT score: No significant differences between the PjBL and the control group on the pretest (beginning 9th grade) and posttest (end 9th grade). PBL scored significantly higher on the second posttest (end of 10th grade; independent t-tests)"

### Sofie M. M. Loyens 2023 (2)

Sofie M. M. Loyens, Julianne E. van Meerten, Lydia Schaap, Lisette Wijnia. (2023). Situating Higher-Order, Critical, and Critical-Analytic Thinking in Problem- and Project-Based Learning Environments: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09757-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-groups pre-post study (Burris & Garton, 2007), n = 77 PBL vs 63 control, reported in Table 3 with Cohen's d = -0.02 [-0.36, 0.31]; the review codes the direction as negative.

> "CT total score: Control group (supervised study) scored significantly higher than the PBL group on the CT posttest (ANCOVA, pretest as covariate). Subscale scores: not reported"
