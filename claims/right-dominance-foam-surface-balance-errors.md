## Evidence

### Notarnicola Angela 2014

Notarnicola Angela, Maccagnano Giuseppe, Pesce Vito, Pierro Silvia Di, Tafuri Silvio, Moretti Biagio. (2014). Effect of teaching with or without mirror on balance in young female ballet students. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/1756-0500-7-426

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis from the same randomized study identified right dominance as a factor in foam-surface stance scores; the printed coefficients are unstandardised, so no effect size is coded.

> "The values of Foam Surface-double-leg-stance and of Foam Surface-single-leg-stance at T1 are influenced by the same value at T0 (respectively coef = 0.80, t = 6.59, p < 0.0001; coef = 0.98, t = 13.3; p < 0.0001) and by right dominance (respectively coef = 0.45, t = 3.12, p = 0.005; coef −0.93, t = 3.67, p = 0.001)."
