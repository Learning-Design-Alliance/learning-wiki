Descriptive cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 92 medical trainees conducted in May 2017 at the Faculty of Medicine in Nouakchott. The survey found that "Twelve participants had never performed lumbar puncture, most often because they had low self-esteem." Only percentages and counts are reported, no effect sizes.
