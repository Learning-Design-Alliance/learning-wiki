## Evidence

### Sunil Kumar D 2026

Sunil Kumar D, Sucheta Dandekar, G. Hari Prakash. (2026). Dynamic CBME in action: rule-based digital case-based learning to evaluate antibiotic-stewardship reasoning in MBBS learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09810-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental single-group pre-post study of 271 MBBS learners. Paired t-test on overall SCT scores showed a mean change of 0.40 with "95% CI: -0.41 to 1.21" and "p = 0.33", a negligible effect of "Cohen's d = 0.059".

> "Overall SCT scores changed from 5.65 +/- 4.73 to 6.05 +/- 4.48 (mean change = 0.40; 95% CI: -0.41 to 1.21; p = 0.33; Cohen's d = 0.059)."
