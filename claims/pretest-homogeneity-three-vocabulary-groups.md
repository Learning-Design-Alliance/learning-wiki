## Evidence

### Sirmandi and Sardareh 2016

Effat Heidari Sirmandi, Sedigheh Abbasnasab Sardareh. (2016). The Effect of BBC World Clips With and Without Subtitles on Intermediate EFL Learners' Vocabulary Development. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1116325

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A one-way ANOVA was run on pretest scores of all three groups (Table 1 means 18.7000, 18.5000, 18.6500); the article states "there is no significant difference between them". Equivalence was not formally tested beyond this; no effect size is printed.

> "The results show that all groups are almost the same in terms of their vocabulary knowledge, and there is no significant difference between them."
