q3 · i2 · causal · r2

WWC calculations from Lovett, Meyer, & Thille (2008), an RCT at Carnegie Mellon University with 61 students in an introductory statistics course. Intervention mean "68.00 (18.90)" versus comparison 53.00 (30.00), effect size 0.55, improvement index +21, p = .044, statistically significant.

"Comprehensive Assessment of Outcomes in Statistics (CAOS) (Lovett, Meyer, & Thille, 2008)b Postsecondary students 61 students 68.00 (18.90) 53.00 (30.00) 15.00 0.55 +21 .044"

schunn-final¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, January). Supporting Postsecondary Success intervention report: Open Learning Initiative (OLI). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/698

q2 · i? · causal · r2

WWC calculations from Schunn & Patchan (2009), a quasi-experimental study of a symbolic logic course at Carnegie Mellon with baseline equivalence established. Final exam means "62.35 (22.71)" versus 64.13 (25.68), effect size -0.07, improvement index -3, p = .547, not significant.

"Final exam score (Schunn & Patchan, 2009)c Postsecondary students 276 students 62.35 (22.71) 64.13 (25.68) -1.78 -0.07 -3 .547"

bowen-ach¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, January). Supporting Postsecondary Success intervention report: Open Learning Initiative (OLI). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/698

q3 · i? · causal · r3

WWC calculations from the Bowen et al. (2014) RCT: final exam effect size 0.11, improvement index +4, p = .248; the CAOS outcome in the same study showed effect size 0.08, +3, p = .420. Both characterized as indeterminate.

"Final exam score (Bowen et al., 2014)a Postsecondary students 431 students 0.57 (0.13) 0.55 (0.22) 0.02 0.11 +4 .248"
