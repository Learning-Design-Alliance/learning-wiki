## Evidence

### Christian Hayley E. 2020

Christian Hayley E., Cross Donna, Rosenberg Michael, Schipperijn Jasper, Shilton Trevor, Trapp Georgina, Trost Stewart G., Nathan Andrea, Maitland Clover, Thornton Ashleigh, Wenden Elizabeth J., George Phoebe (2020). Development of physical activity policy and implementation strategies for early childhood education and care settings using the Delphi process. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-020-01034-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In round three, experts ranked the top five barriers to implementing the policy strategies (80 complete responses). Barriers for management, supervisors and educators included "existing workloads and competing priorities, lack of training and/or professional development and costs in time, resources and money".

> "Key barriers to policy implementation strategies focused on Management/Supervisors/Educators were related to existing workloads and competing priorities, lack of training and/or professional development and costs in time, resources and money (Table 4)."

### Christian Hayley E. 2020 (2)

Christian Hayley E., Cross Donna, Rosenberg Michael, Schipperijn Jasper, Shilton Trevor, Trapp Georgina, Trost Stewart G., Nathan Andrea, Maitland Clover, Thornton Ashleigh, Wenden Elizabeth J., George Phoebe (2020). Development of physical activity policy and implementation strategies for early childhood education and care settings using the Delphi process. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-020-01034-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

In the round three survey, experts ranked the top five enablers alongside the barriers (80 complete responses). Enablers included "funding availability, a clear, concise and easy to follow policy document and legislative or National Quality Standard requirements".

> "Key enablers to policy implementation strategies focused on Management/Supervisors/Educators were related to funding availability, a clear, concise and easy to follow policy document and legislative or National Quality Standard requirements."
