# A class histogram of plantable cloves per bulb supported probabilistic predictions, with 14-17 cloves accounting for 80% of outcomes

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The class histogram showed 16 plantable cloves was the most common outcome, occurring 5 of 15 times (1/3 of the time). [→ Nathan Moore and John Deming 2008](#nathan-moore-and-john-deming-2008)

## Evidence

### Nathan Moore and John Deming 2008

Nathan Moore and John Deming. (2008). Using Garlic As A Far-Transfer Problem of Proportional And Probabilistic Reasoning. https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.2133 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0811.2133](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0811.2133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Assessment activity in which the class constructed a histogram (Figure 3) of plantable cloves per bulb from the 15-bulb dataset; the modal outcome is reported descriptively.

> "As the data shows, the most common (and we argued, most probable) outcome was 16 plantable cloves."

### Nathan Moore and John Deming 2008 (2)

Nathan Moore and John Deming. (2008). Using Garlic As A Far-Transfer Problem of Proportional And Probabilistic Reasoning. https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.2133 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0811.2133](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0811.2133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The histogram discussion introduced confidence intervals as the probability that a new bulb falls in a range; the article prints the 12-of-15 (80%) figure and the parallel 75% figure for 14-17 cloves in the Figure 3 caption.

> "Finally, the range of 14-17 plantable cloves accounts for 12 of the 15 outcomes, which is 80% of the total probability."

### Nathan Moore and John Deming 2008 (3)

Nathan Moore and John Deming. (2008). Using Garlic As A Far-Transfer Problem of Proportional And Probabilistic Reasoning. https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.2133 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0811.2133](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0811.2133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Informal check after the assessment: two leftover bulbs were opened and their clove counts matched the predicted range. The authors describe the effect on students' minds as an impression, not a measured outcome.

> "Fortunately for us, the two remaining bulbs contained respectively 16 and 15 plantable cloves, which seemed to further strengthen the power of the statistical argument in the our students’ minds."
