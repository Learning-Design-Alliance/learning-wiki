### Zhang Feng 2026

Zhang Feng, Chen Zhi Lan, Qi Li En, Wang Xiao Yan, Zhang Li, Zhang Jing, Wang Ping. (2026). Pre–post changes in operating room nurses’ knowledge and attitudes toward medical device–related pressure injuries after a problem-based and simulation-based training program: a single-group pretest–posttest study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09147-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-participant comparison across the five APuP dimensions (n = 80); Table 5 reports Cohen's dz values of 1.131, 0.847, 0.739, and 0.514 for the improved dimensions and dz = 0.09 for responsibility (P = 0.157). Wilcoxon sensitivity analyses were unchanged.

> "Significant improvements were found in personal prevention capability, prioritization of prevention, perceived impact of pressure injuries, and confidence in the effectiveness of prevention (all P < 0.05). No statistically significant change was observed in the responsibility dimension (P = 0.157)."
