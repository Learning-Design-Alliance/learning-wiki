### Mawyin-Muñoz CE 2025

Mawyin-Muñoz CE, Salmerón-Escobar FJ, Hidalgo-Acosta JA, Calderon-León MF. (2025). Medical simulation: an essential tool for training, diagnosis, and treatment in the 21st century. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07610-z

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized single-blind trial by Han et al. (2021) with 95 medical students divided into a standardized patient group (39) and a VRNET SP group (56). The review reports "the SP group with VRNET had higher NPE scores (3.81 ± 0.92) than the SP group (3.40 ± 1.01)" at p = 0.043.

> "Neurological physical examination (NPE) score (p = 0.043); the SP group with VRNET had higher NPE scores (3.81 ± 0.92) than the SP group (3.40 ± 1.01)."
