## Evidence

### Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein 2014

Charles Baily and Noah D. Finkelstein. (2014). Ontological Flexibility and the Learning of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.8499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.8499)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview and written-survey evidence: three students remained committed to a strictly particle description, with statements such as Student B's "I can't picture it not having a definite position, because it is a piece of mass, too." These quotes evidence how ontology may influence reasoning about light and matter.

> "What is important to notice about these statements is the difficulty students have with attributing mass or charge to an electron without also ascribing to it a localized position."
