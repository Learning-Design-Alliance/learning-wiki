## Evidence

### Rod Ellis 1987

Rod Ellis. (1987). Interlanguage Variability in Narrative Discourse: Style Shifting in the Use of the Past Tense. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 9, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263100006483

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Across the three planning conditions with 17 intermediate L2 learners, regular past tense accuracy followed the ordering planned writing > planned speech > unplanned speech, as the abstract states accuracy was "most evident in planned writing and least evident in unplanned speech".
