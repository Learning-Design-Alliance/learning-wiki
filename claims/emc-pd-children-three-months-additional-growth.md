## Evidence

### McCray 2013

McCray, J. & Chen, J.-Q. (2013). Innovation in Early Math Education: The Whole Teacher Approach to Professional Development. Proceedings of the 35th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. https://www.pmena.org/pmena-proceedings/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental evaluation with pre- and post-measures; 154 three- to five-year-olds, 91 intervention and 63 comparison children from matched classrooms. Two-level HLM controlling for pre-test scores and ethnicity showed "an average of 3 months additional growth"; the printed coefficient was γ01 = 3.0609, p = 0.031.

> "Compared to comparison classrooms, children in intervention classrooms showed an average of 3 months additional growth in WJ age estimate score over the intervention year (p<.03)."
