Pennings 2017¶

Pennings, H. J. M. (2017). Using a Complexity Approach to Study the Interpersonal Dynamics in Teacher-Student Interactions: A Case Study of Two Teachers. Complicity: An International Journal of Complexity and Education, 14(2). https://complicityjournal.org

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Based on visual inspection of the time-series and the Communion phase result, the discussion reports the refraining hypothesis was not confirmed, contrasting with a prior finding (Pennings, 2017) that teachers with desirable styles refrained more often.

"the hypothesis that both teachers can refrain from negative student behavior could not be confirmed. For Ethan, because he simply did not face hostile student behavior, while for Sam, it might be the case that he is struggling so much to get his students' attention that he cannot think of remaining friendly."
