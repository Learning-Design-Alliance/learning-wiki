Completion rates for each assignment (Table 7) were compared across sections as percentages. Hybrid students led on three of four journal entries but lagged on in-class assignments; for only half the time were hybrid students more compliant with online assignments than online students.

> "None of these three sections consistently maintained a higher completion rate than any of the other two sections.
