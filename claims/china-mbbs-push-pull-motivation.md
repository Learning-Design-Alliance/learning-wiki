## Evidence

### Jiang Qinxu 2022

Jiang Qinxu, Horta Hugo, Yuen Mantak. (2022). International medical students’ perspectives on factors affecting their academic success in China: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03597-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 40 international MBBS students from developing countries at two Chinese medical schools. The reported "The majority (52.5%)" cited cost as a push factor, alongside competitive admission and resource shortages at home.

> "The majority (52.5%) of participants mentioned that medical education was more expensive in their own countries than in China."

### Jiang Qinxu 2022 (2)

Jiang Qinxu, Horta Hugo, Yuen Mantak. (2022). International medical students’ perspectives on factors affecting their academic success in China: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03597-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same interview study, participants also cited perceived quality of medical education, use of technology in treatment, and safety as pull factors; "strong recommendations from their family members, relatives, friends, and seniors" was the most-cited pull factor (55%).

> "The majority (55%) of participants said that they had chosen China because of strong recommendations from their family members, relatives, friends, and seniors."
