Observational comparison across three instructors' classes at one US research university; I3 taught the second-semester course and framed the materials as research study participation. The article says framing "may have negatively influenced student engagement and performance".

> "the framing of instructor 3 (I3), who stated that the tutorial was not relevant for the course syllabus and was part of a research study, may have negatively influenced student engagement and performance in that course (even on the pretest)."
