## Evidence

### Bostic et al. 2016

Jonathan D. Bostic, Stephen J. Pape, Tim Jacobbe. (2016). Encouraging Sixth-Grade Students' Problem-Solving Performance by Teaching Through Problem Solving. Investigations in Mathematics Learning, Volume 8, Number 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1094896

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA (after the FCAT covariate proved non-significant) on the 25-point unit test of rates, ratios, and data analysis. The comparison mean was "approximately two points higher" than the intervention group's.

> "There was a significant difference in the groups' mean scores on the unit test favoring the comparison group, F(1, 55) = 8.27, p < .005, d = .79"
