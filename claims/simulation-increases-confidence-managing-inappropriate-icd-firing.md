# Residents' self-reported confidence in managing patients with inappropriately firing defibrillators increased after the simulation (Likert mean 2.69 to 4.06)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Self-reported confidence in managing a patient with an inappropriately firing defibrillator increased from a mean of 2.69 to 4.06 on a 5-point Likert scale (p < .001). [→ Thorpe Rachel Lauren 2019](#thorpe-rachel-lauren-2019)

## Evidence

### Thorpe Rachel Lauren 2019

Thorpe Rachel Lauren, Rohant Namit, Cryer Michael, Gainey Christopher. (2019). Inappropriately Firing Defibrillator: A Simulation Case for Emergency Medicine Residents. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10808

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U test comparing pre/post confidence ratings of the 16 residents. The mean rose "from 2.69 presimulation to 4.06 postsimulation (U = 23.0, z = −3.93, p < .001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "an increase in mean score on a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree) from 2.69 presimulation to 4.06 postsimulation (U = 23.0, z = −3.93, p < .001) for confidence managing a patient with an inappropriately firing defibrillator"
