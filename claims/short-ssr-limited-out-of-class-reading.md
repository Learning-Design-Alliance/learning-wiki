## Subclaims
`q2 i?` SSR and comparison groups slightly decreased free reading across periods; some SSR participants read their required book entirely within the 15-minute in-class session and did no outside reading. [→ McLean 2018](#mclean-2018)

## Evidence

### McLean 2018

McLean, S., & Poulshock, J. (2018). Increasing reading self-efficacy and reading amount in EFL learners with word-targets. Reading in a Foreign Language, 30(1), 76–91. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up interviews with SSR-group participants in this quasi-experiment revealed that some completed their required reading entirely within the 15-minute in-class SSR session and did no outside reading.

> "some SSR group participants reported that they did not read outside of class at all, as they were able to read at least one book per week as required within the 15-minute SSR period."
