q2 · i? · associational · r2

Partial correlation analysis in 37 healthy older adults testing whether sleep quality and Verrucomicrobia proportions were independently associated with Stroop Word performance. The article reports "Sleep quality (rs=−.24, p=.17) and Verrucomicrobia (rs=.18, p=.31) were not independently associated with Stroop Word performance."
