## Evidence

### Nygren 2025

Nygren, T., Samuelsson, M., Hansson, P.-O., Efimova, E., & Bachelder, S. (2025). AI Versus Human Feedback in Mixed Reality Simulations: Comparing LLM and Expert Mentoring in Preservice Teacher Education on Controversial Issues. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00484-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Across 60 generation attempts (five iterations, three models, four lessons), many expert feedback points appeared inconsistently, "pop up inconsistently across different generation attempts".

> "Small total frequencies for many points indicate that they pop up inconsistently across different generation attempts. In other words, the same model might highlight poor time management in the first attempt but then replace it with insufficient inclusion of quiet pupils in the next round, which reflects the nature of LLMs."
