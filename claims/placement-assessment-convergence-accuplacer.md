## Evidence

### Venezia 2010

Venezia, A., Bracco, K. R., & Nodine, T. (2010). One-shot deal? Students' perceptions of assessment and course placement in California's community colleges. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A telephone survey of matriculation officers sent to all community colleges, with 73 of the 110 responding in 2009, found variation across campuses but convergence around a few assessments; the report states "84 percent of colleges that responded indicated that they used one of two assessments in mathematics." ACCUPLACER was the most common assessment for both mathematics and English.

> "84 percent of colleges that responded indicated that they used one of two assessments in mathematics, and 70 percent said that they used one of two assessments in English"
