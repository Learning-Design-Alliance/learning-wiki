### Elissavet Papageorgiou · Jacqueline Wong · Qinyi Liu · Mohammad Khalil · Annoesjka J. Cabo 2025

Elissavet Papageorgiou · Jacqueline Wong · Qinyi Liu · Mohammad Khalil · Annoesjka J. Cabo. (2025). A Systematic Review on Student Engagement in Undergraduate Mathematics: Conceptualization, Measurement, and Learning Outcomes. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:66. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10046-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of self-report use across the corpus. The majority administered self-reports at a single time point, typically at the end of the course; one study used a pre–post design.

> "Self-reports, primarily using questionnaires (e.g., Büchele et al., 2021; Hong & Chien, 2023; Lavidas et al., 2020; Sidekerskienė & Damaševičius, 2023) and, less frequently, semi-structured interviews (Dibbs, 2019), were also commonly employed (n = 21) to assess SE."

### Elissavet Papageorgiou · Jacqueline Wong · Qinyi Liu · Mohammad Khalil · Annoesjka J. Cabo 2025 (2)

Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:66. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10046-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review finding on dimension coverage in process data. The review also notes temporal approaches were rare (n = 7 of 17 online-learning studies used aggregated measures) and few studies combined multiple data sources.

> "Cognitive engagement was far less frequently measured via process data and appeared in only two studies using textual analysis (Tucker et al., 2023; Wu, 2021)."
