### Gambari 2016

Gambari, A. I., Shittu, A. T., Daramola, F. O., & James, M. (2016). Effects Of Video-Based Cooperative, Competitive And Individualized Instructional Strategies On The Performance Of Senior Secondary Schools Students In Geometry. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 4(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1116321

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

t-test comparing 15 male and 15 female students in the competitive group; means were 76.40 (male) and 72.93 (female). The article reports a significant difference "in favour of male students" (t = 3.187, df = 28, p = 0.004); no effect size is printed.

> "This indicates that there wss significant difference between the male and female students taught with COMVIP, (t = 3.187, df  = 28, p = 0.004). Hence, HO 3 was rejected. Therefore, there is significant difference between male and female students taught with COMVIP in favour of male students."
