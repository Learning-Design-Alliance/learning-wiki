## Evidence

### Lei Jiedi 2021

Lei Jiedi, Jones Lauren, Brosnan Mark. (2021). Exploring an e-learning community's response to the language and terminology use in autism from two massive open online courses on autism education and technology use. Autism. https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361320987963

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 'Purpose of terminology' from the thematic analysis of forum comments. The article reports access to "support, resources, and accommodations in society" depends on a valid diagnosis, and families and professionals highlighted teaching children and young people to disclose to their advantage.

> "individual preferences for terminology needed to take into account how best to access support, resources, and accommodations in society, all of which depend on a valid diagnosis being in place."

### Lei Jiedi 2021 (2)

Lei Jiedi, Jones Lauren, Brosnan Mark. (2021). Exploring an e-learning community's response to the language and terminology use in autism from two massive open online courses on autism education and technology use. Autism. https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361320987963

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same 'Purpose of terminology' theme, on the process of disclosure. The article reports some self-advocates "perceived this conflict in identity as a barrier to disclosing their diagnosis", and notes diagnostic overshadowing could leave co-occurring mental health conditions overlooked.

> "autistic self-advocates commented on feeling uncomfortable that autism disclosure often fell under the umbrella of disability, as they do not perceive autism to be a disability."
