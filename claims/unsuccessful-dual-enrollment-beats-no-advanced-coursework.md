# Students who enrolled in dual enrollment and did not pass are about 6 percentage points more likely to enroll in college than comparable students who never took advanced coursework

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among matched students who took neither AP nor IB, those who enrolled in dual enrollment and did not pass had a +6.4 percentage point higher college enrollment probability than observationally similar students who never enrolled in any advanced coursework. [→ Terrell 2026](#terrell-2026)
`q2 i?` This advantage holds equally for students from low-income and first-generation backgrounds and their peers. [→ Terrell 2026](#terrell-2026)
