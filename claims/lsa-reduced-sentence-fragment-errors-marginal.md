## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The average number of sentence fragment errors per message dropped from 0.16 (pre-LSA, N=87) to 0.05 (post-LSA, N=85), a marginally significant reduction (p < 0.10). [→ Austin 2017](#austin-2017)

### Austin 2017
