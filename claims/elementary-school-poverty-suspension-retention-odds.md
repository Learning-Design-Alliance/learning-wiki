### Irsheid 2023

Irsheid, S. (2023). Beyond the Student: Structural Factors that Affect Grade Retention. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/beyond-the-student

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Elementary school findings (pp. 102-109 of the full study) from the observational analysis of CPS students: school-level poverty and suspension rates were associated with retention odds even among students with similar achievement. The brief reports these odds ratios directly.

> "Comparing students with similar test scores, grades, and attendance, the odds of being retained in grade were: o 32% higher in schools where more than 1% of students are suspended in a year vs. schools with few suspensions; and o 100% higher in schools with the highest poverty rates (top quartile) than schools with the lowest poverty rates (bottom quartile)."
