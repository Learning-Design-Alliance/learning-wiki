## Evidence

### Lamidi et al. 2015

Bashirat T. Lamidi, Oloyede S. Oyelekan and Adekunle S. Olorundare. (2015). Effects of Mastery Learning Instructional Strategy on Senior School Students’ Achievement in the Mole Concept. Electronic Journal of Science Education, 19(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1188287

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent sample t-test analysis (Table 1) in respect of research question 1, in a quasi-experimental non-randomized pre-test post-test control group design with two intact classes. The experimental group was taught the mole concept using mastery learning while the control group was taught conventionally, with a "mean gain score of 15.50" versus 7.04 favouring the experimental group.

> "mastery learning instructional strategy yielded a mean gain score of 15.50 for the experimental group while the control group for which mastery learning was not administered as a treatment had a mean gain score of 7.04. At 0.05 level of significance, a t-value of 21.363, and a p-value of 0.000 was obtained"
