Evidence¶

Cekiso 2012¶

Cekiso, M. (2012). Effects of strategy instruction on the reading comprehension and strategy awareness of Grade 11 English Second Language learners in the Eastern Cape. Reading & Writing 3(1), Art. #23. https://doi.org/10.4102/rw.v3i1.23

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Author's conclusion drawn from the quasi-experiment comparing strategy-use frequencies and comprehension scores across the experimental and control groups; the article prints no correlation coefficient or effect size for this relationship, so the stated association is reported without a statistic.

"As for the relationship between perceived strategy use and reading comprehension, this study revealed that strategy use did positively affect reading comprehension"
