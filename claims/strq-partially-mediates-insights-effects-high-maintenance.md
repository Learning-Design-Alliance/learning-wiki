# Improvements in student-teacher relationship quality partially mediated INSIGHTS effects on behavior problems and off-task behaviors, but not on engagement, for high-maintenance children

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Effects of INSIGHTS in reducing behavior problems and off-task behaviors were partially mediated through improvements in student-teacher relationship quality; no mediation evidence was found for behavioral engagement. [→ O'Connor 2014](#oconnor-2014)

## Evidence

### O'Connor 2014

O'Connor, E. E., McCormick, M. P., Cappella, E., & McClowry, S. G. (2014). Getting a Good Start in School: Differential Effects of INSIGHTS on the Behaviors and Engagement of Children with Challenging Temperaments. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562775

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel mediation analysis (Figure 3) using the Zhang et al. (2009) framework, testing path a (treatment to relationship quality, γ = .62, p < .05) and whether the significant interaction coefficients decreased when group-mean relationship quality was added as a predictor.

> "mediation analyses suggested that effects of INSIGHTS in reducing behavior problems and off-task behaviors were partially mediated through improvements in student-teacher relationship quality. However, there was no evidence to suggest that effects of INSIGHTS on behavioral engagement were mediated through improvements in student-teacher relationship quality."
