## Evidence

### Zohreh Shaghaghian 2022

Zohreh Shaghaghian, Heather Burte, Dezhen Song, and Wei Yan. (2022). An Augmented Reality Application and User Study for Understanding and Learning Spatial Transformation Matrices. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.00110](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.00110)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Motivation questionnaire based on MSLQ administered after the workshops in the AR vs non-AR user study. Descriptively, the AR group agreed more that the subject was interesting, useful, and helpful for self-learning; equivalence was not tested.

> "The result of the non-parametric Wilcoxon Rank Sum tests  on students’ answers to each category did not show any significant difference between AR vs. non-AR groups (pintrinsic-value = 0.31, ptask-anxiety = 0.55, pself-regulated-learning = 0.11)."
