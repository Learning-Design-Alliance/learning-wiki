# Upside-down mastery inversion occurs only as Na becomes greater than Nu, transitioning from bell-shaped to exponential-shaped curves

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When Na is approximately equal to Nu the mastery curve is bell-shaped as in traditional courses; as Na becomes greater than Nu the distribution inverts, and for Na = 19 with Nu = 9 the majority of students obtain mastery in all units. [→ Danilo T. Alves 2022](#danilo-t-alves-2022)

## Evidence

### Danilo T. Alves 2022

Danilo T. Alves, Nelson P. C. de Souza, Silvio C. F. Pereira Filho. (2022). A simple mathematical model underlying Keller's individualized teaching method. [doi:10.1590/1806-9126-rbef-2021-0344](https://doi.org/10.1590/1806-9126-rbef-2021-0344)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Numerical results from equation (4) plotted in Fig. 3 for (alpha, beta) = (1/2, 1/2) and Nu = 9, comparing Na = 9, 14 and 19. The model shows the inversion 'does not occur for an arbitrary value of Na' but is enhanced as Na increases relative to Nu.

> "For Na = 14 (triangles on the green line), we already observe a deformation of the “bell-shaped” curve. For Na = 19 (crosses on the purple line), we ﬁnally observe an inversion: a transition from a bell-shaped to an “exponential-shaped” curve, indic ating that the majority of students obtained mastery in all Nu = 9 units."
