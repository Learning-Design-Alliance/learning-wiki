## Evidence

### Tran 2019

Van Dat Tran. (2019). Does Cooperative Learning Increase Students' Motivation in Learning? International Journal of Higher Education, 8(5), 12. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v8n5p12

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test independent t-test (n = 36 per group) on the affective component: the cooperative learning group's mean test anxiety (2.71, SD .49) was lower than the control group's (3.12, SD .52), t = 3.65, p = .040. No effect size is printed.
