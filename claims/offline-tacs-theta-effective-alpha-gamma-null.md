## Evidence

### Tae Lee Lee 2023

Tae Lee Lee, Hanall Lee and Nyeonju Kang. (2023). A meta-analysis showing improved cognitive performance in healthy young adults with transcranial alternating current stimulation. npj Science of Learning 8:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00152-9

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis of offline-tACS comparisons by frequency band in the meta-analysis: "the moderator variable analysis on 12 theta frequency band comparisons from the 10 studies showed significant positive effects on cognitive performance" (SMD = 0.221); offline alpha (SMD = 0.044) and gamma (SMD = 0.060) were not significant.

> "the moderator variable analysis on 12 theta frequency band comparisons from the 10 studies showed significant positive effects on cognitive performance: SMD = 0.221; SE = 0.067; 95% CI = 0.090–0.352; Z = 3.298; P = 0.001"
