# School belonging shows small-to-moderate positive correlations with motivational, social-emotional and behavioural outcomes (r between .30 and .39)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` School belonging correlates medium to moderately large (r = .30 to .39) with motivational outcomes such as mastery goal orientation, social-emotional outcomes such as self-concept and self-efficacy, and behavioural outcomes such as behavioural, cognitive and agentic engagement. ## Evidence

### Korpershoek and Fokkens-Bruinsma 2020

H. Korpershoek, E. T. Canrinus, M. Fokkens-Bruinsma & H. de Boer (2020). The relationships between school belonging and students' motivational, social-emotional, behavioural, and academic outcomes in secondary education: a meta-analytic review. Research Papers in Education, 35:6, 641-680. https://doi.org/10.1080/02671522.2019.1615116

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis including 82 studies with 208,796 students. Average correlations across the motivational, social-emotional and behavioural domains ranged "(varying between r = .30 and .39"; the Q-statistics for these domains were significant, indicating real heterogeneity among effect sizes.

> "whereas the correlations with the other motivational, social-emotional, and behavioural outcomes were medium to moderately large (varying between r = .30 and .39)"
