## Evidence

### P. Spangenberger 2025

P. Spangenberger, N. Matthes, F. Kapp, L. Kruse, J. L. Plass. (2025). Orchestrating iVR technology in an authentic classroom setting and its effects on factual knowledge, comprehension and transfer. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10409-2

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on troubleshooting comprehension (image-ordering test) in the same between-subject classroom study. The time × condition interaction was significant, F(1, 27) = 4.36, p = .046, ηp2 = .139; post hoc analyses showed comprehension scores "were higher for the iVR group".

> "The analysis revealed a statistically significant interaction effect for time and condi-tion (F(1, 27) = 4.36, p = .046, ηp2 = .139), indicating that increases in comprehension differed for the two groups."
