## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Intervention students significantly outperformed at-risk controls on the Number Sets Test, a novel integrative transfer measure not taught during intervention. [→ Fuchs et al. 2019](#fuchs-et-al-2019)

## Evidence

### Fuchs et al. 2019

Lynn S. Fuchs, Douglas Fuchs, and Jennifer K. Gilbert. (2019). Does the Severity of Students' Pre-Intervention Math Deficits Affect Responsiveness to Generally Effective First-Grade Intervention? Exceptional Children, 85(2), 147–162. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918782628

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Main-effects model on the Number Sets Test with Huber-White corrected standard errors after outlier removal; the intervention-versus-control effect was significant (ES = 0.27), and the practice-variant contrast was not significant.

> "The main effects model without the interaction terms revealed a significant main effect for Contrast 1,β2 = 0.33, SE = 0.08, p < .001, ES = 0.27 (both intervention conditions vs. control)"
