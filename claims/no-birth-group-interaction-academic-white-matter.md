## Evidence

### Collins Simonne S.E. 2019

Collins Simonne S.E., Spencer-Smith M., Mürner-Lavanchy I., Kelly C.E., Pyman P., Pascoe L., Cheong J., Doyle L.W., Thompson D.K., Anderson P.J. (2019). White matter microstructure correlates with mathematics but not word reading performance in 13-year-old children born very preterm and full-term. NeuroImage: Clinical. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nicl.2019.101944

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Interaction model testing whether mathematics-score associations with diffusion parameters differed between birth groups found "no voxels that had an interaction at p ≤.05, FWE-corrected between birth group (VPT or FT) and mathematics score". The reading interaction was similarly null.

> "There were also no voxels that had an interaction at p ≤.05, FWE-corrected between birth group (VPT or FT) and mathematics score for the associations with the diffusion parameters."
