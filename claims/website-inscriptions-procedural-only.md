### Krange 2008

Krange, I., & Ludvigsen, S. (2008). What does it mean? Students' procedural and conceptual problem solving in a CSCL environment designed within the field of science education. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 3, 25-51. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-007-9030-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

From the interaction analysis of Extract 1: although the knowledge needed to answer Cornelia's conceptual question was available on the website, "the educational inscriptions on the website... only support procedural aspects". The authors call the codontable and website interdependent reinforced tendencies.

> "the educational inscriptions on the website, explaining for example, the codontable, only support procedural aspects of how to employ this table, while issues that could have been raised to support the students ’ conceptual knowledge of what this table actually contributes to suffer."

### Krange 2008 (2)

Krange, I., & Ludvigsen, S. (2008). What does it mean? Students' procedural and conceptual problem solving in a CSCL environment designed within the field of science education. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 3, 25-51. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-007-9030-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

From the analysis of Extract 2: the authors claim "the 3D model comes with certain taxations", naming weak conceptual representations and the helping-the-professor storyline, which together with students' eagerness to solve the problem took focus away from the knowledge domain.

> "the 3D model comes with certain taxations, like the weaknesses concerning the conceptual representations, and the story-line that partly steals the focus away from the disciplinary problem they are asked to solve"
