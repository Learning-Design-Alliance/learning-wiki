## Evidence

### Chen 2015

Chen, Y. (2015). Linking Learning Styles and Learning on Mobile Facebook. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 16(2). https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/1973

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Face-to-face interviews were conducted with 24 students (six per learning style group) after the nine-week treatment. "Students across all learning styles generally expressed the opinion that mobile Facebook provided an environment in which it was simple" for uploading and editing ideas.
