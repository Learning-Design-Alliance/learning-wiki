`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Team exercise outcomes reported in Section 2.6 (Student Presentations): 8 teams of 3-4 students each wrote CutLang ADL files for assigned ATLAS or CMS analyses after independent study. The article reports "5 were able to produce the ﬁnal discriminant histograms, and 2 were even able to do ﬁts"; no team extracted limits.

> "Out of the 8 teams, while none were able to extract limits (since not all backgrounds were available), all were able to obtain various object histograms, 5 were able to produce the ﬁnal discriminant histograms, and 2 were even able to do ﬁts to those histograms with ROOT."
