Evidence¶

Osman Tasdelen 2025¶

Osman Tasdelen, Daniel Bodemer. (2025). Generative AI in the Classroom: Effects of Context-Personalized Learning Material and Tasks on Motivation and Performance. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00491-9

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Classroom experiment comparing AI-generated context-personalized vs. standard learning material during regular math class. The personalized group scored higher (M = 3.78, SD = 1.03) than the standard group (M = 3.25, SD = 1.13), "d = 0.49, indicating a small effect size".

"A one-tailed t-test indicated a statistically signiﬁcant difference in intrinsic motivation between the two groups, t(112) = 2.63, p = .005, d = 0.49, indicating a small effect size"
