## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On a 5-point Likert scale, students rated usage enjoyment M = 3.75 (SD 1.79) and ease/intuitive use M = 3.44 (SD 1.65), with lowest ratings for design (M = 3.08, SD 1.58) and its realization (M = 2.93, SD 1.58). [→ Karrenbauer 2023](#karrenbauer-2023)

## Evidence

### Karrenbauer 2023

Karrenbauer, C., Brauner, T., König, C. M., & Breitner, M. H. (2023). Design, development, and evaluation of an individual digital study assistant for higher education students. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10255-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire evaluation within the field test, using six Likert-scale usability and user-interface items adapted from Bruner (2012); the first four questions drew 271 responses while the last two received only 19 answers and were excluded. The average feedback rating was 3.3 across categories, indicating "these characteristics require further improvement".

> "The students particularly enjoyed the usage (M = 3.75, /u1D70E = 1.79) and per - ceived using the IDSA as relatively easy and intuitive (M = 3.44, /u1D70E = 1.65). Students gave their lowest ratings to the design (M = 3.08, /u1D70E = 1.58) and its realization (M = 2.93, /u1D70E = 1.58)"
