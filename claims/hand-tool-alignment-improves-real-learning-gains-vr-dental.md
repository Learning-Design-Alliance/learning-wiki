## Evidence

### Kaluschke Maximilian 2023

Kaluschke Maximilian, Yin Myat Su, Haddawy Peter, Suebnukarn Siriwan, Zachmann Gabriel. (2023). The effect of 3D stereopsis and hand-tool alignment on learning effectiveness and skill transfer of a VR-based simulator for dental training. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0291389

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study with 40 fifth-year dental students; comparing pooled aligned groups (1 & 2) against pooled misaligned groups (3 & 4) on real plastic teeth pre/post error ratings, the "alignment" group improved by −0.5 with p = 0.0598, while the misaligned group's difference of 0.0278 was not significant (p = 0.531).

> "However, the “alignment” group improved from their pre-test (M = 2.71, SD = 1.1) by −0.5 from their post-test (M = 2.21, SD = 1.19). The t-test shows a statistical significance of p = 0.0598 (t(18) = 1.635)."
