## Evidence

### Shikino Kiyoshi 2021

Shikino Kiyoshi, Rosu Claudia A., Yokokawa Daiki, Suzuki Shingo, Hirota Yusuke, Nishiya Katsumi, Ikusaka Masatomi. (2021). Flexible e-learning video approach to improve fundus examination skills for medical students: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02857-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome measured by the same pre/post questionnaire in the randomized trial. The printed post-post comparison found "a significant difference (intervention group 4.4 vs. control group 3.9, p < 0.001)"; the within-group rise was significant in both groups (control p = 0.002, Table 2).

> "The motivation score increase in the post-test vs. pre-test was 3.5 ± 0.7 to 4.4 ± 0.6 in the flexible e-learning video group and 3.3 ± 1.0 to 3.9 ± 0.9 in the F2F lecture group."
