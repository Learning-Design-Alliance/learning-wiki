### Xingyu Zhao 2023

Xingyu Zhao. (2023). The Impact of Live Polling Quizzes on Student Engagement and Performance in Computer Science Lectures. arXiv preprint arXiv:2309.12335. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2309.12335](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2309.12335)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive result for attendance question Q5, comparing attendance in modules with and without LPQs among 28 valid responses. The article reports "the majority (64.3%) are neutral" and 32.1% suggested higher attendance with LPQs.

> "the majority (64.3%) are neutral in terms of if the use pf LPQs helps the attendance. A substantial proportion (32.1%) suggests the LPQs may have increased motivation and engagement, leading to higher lecture attendance rates."
