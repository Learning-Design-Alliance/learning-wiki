## Evidence

### Constance Steinkuehler 2008

Constance Steinkuehler, Sean Duncan. (2008). Scientific Habits of Mind in Virtual Worlds. J Sci Educ Technol. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10956-008-9120-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quantitative coding of a random sample of 1,984 posts across 85 threads from the official WoW priest forum (November 2006), coded by four raters with 92% four-way interrater reliability. The corpus showed "86% of the WoW discussion forums consisted of talk" that was social knowledge construction; only 8% was social banter and 6% uncodable.

> "We found that 86% of the WoW discussion forums consisted of talk that could be considered ''social knowledge construction''—meaning, the collective development of understanding, often through joint problems solving and argumentation."
