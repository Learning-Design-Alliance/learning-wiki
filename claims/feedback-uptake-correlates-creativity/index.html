Evidence¶

Junting Yin 2025¶

Junting Yin, Zheyu Jin, Yuxuan Zhang, Xuening Li, Yangzhuo Li, Guoping Zhang & Junlong Luo. (2025). Applying, not ignoring: how feedback uptake and neural synchrony drive creativity. npj Science of Learning 10:61. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00351-0

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Pearson correlation analyses (FDR-corrected) linking coded feedback uptake to creativity change in the IF group. Ignoring feedback correlated negatively with fluency acquisition (printed r = −0.641); applied feedback correlated positively with fluency (r = 0.473) and uniqueness (r = 0.504) acquisition.

"the amount of feedback ignored (r = −0.641; pcorr = 0.003) and the percentage of feedback ignored (r = −0.627; pcorr = 0.003) were signiﬁcantly negatively correlated withﬂuency acquisi- tion"
