## Evidence

### Polivka L 2024

Polivka L, Delcour C, Dufresne H, Bartoli S, Bataille P, Bekel L, Bonigen J, Deladrière E, Dimarcq S, Felix A, Havas C, Le Goff H, Levy M, Riback E, Welfringer-Morin A, Houdouin V, Hadj-Rabia S, Bodemer C, Faye A, Melki I. (2024). Breaking bad news: an active learning method for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05821-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison randomised by hospital unit (cluster allocation) of 31 fifth-year students (17 interventional, 14 control) from voluntary units in two hospitals, with students of one unit never split across groups and evaluators blinded to allocation. The workshop group's mean OSCE score was "10.49 out of 15" versus 7.80, p = 0.0007.

> "The mean [range] score in the OSCE was significantly higher in the interventional group than in the control group (10.49 out of 15 [7; 13] vs. 7.80 [4.75; 12.5]; p = 0.0007)"
