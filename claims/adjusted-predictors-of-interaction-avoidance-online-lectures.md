`q2 i?` In adjusted logistic regression, odds of avoiding interaction were at least twice as high among students unsatisfied with platform atmosphere (OR 6.27), with slow-responding instructors (OR 6.84), not feeling safe (OR 4.41), with opposite-sex students present (OR 2.05), poor internet (OR 2.24), and feeling threatened by negative comments (OR 2.05). ### Alghamdi Amal A. 2024

Alghamdi Amal A., Alyousif Ghada F., AlQarni Amani M., Amer Fatma H., Alfadhel Taghreed O., Almutairi Rawan N., Almutairi Shatha M., Almutairi Anwar D., Hakami Nouf A., Al Ghamdi Kholoud. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted logistic regression models (Table 4) in the national survey of 1371 students estimating odds of interaction avoidance, adjusted for sex, academic year, and GPA. The article reports "adjusted OR = 6.27, 95% confidence interval [CI] = 4.79–8.21" for platform atmosphere and further adjusted ORs for other predictors.

> "the odds of avoiding was at least two times higher amongst the participants who reported unsatisfaction with the provided platform atmosphere (adjusted OR = 6.27, 95% confidence interval [CI] = 4.79–8.21), the instructor’s slow response (adjusted OR = 6.84, 95% CI = 5.09–9.20), not feeling safe during interaction (adjusted OR = 4.41, 95% CI = 3.21–6.04)"
