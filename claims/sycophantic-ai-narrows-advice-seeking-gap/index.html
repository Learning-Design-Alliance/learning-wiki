Evidence¶

Lujain Ibrahim 2026¶

Lujain Ibrahim, Franziska Sofia Hafner, Myra Cheng, Cinoo Lee, Rebecca Anselmetti, Robb Willer, Luc Rocher, Diyi Yang. (2026). Sycophantic AI makes human interaction feel more effortful and less satisfying over time. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07912 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.07912

q3 · i0 · causal · r3

Three-week longitudinal experiment (N=1364, census-representative U.S. sample) with 12 sessions of conversation with sycophantic, neutral, or challenging AI or a no-AI control; weekly preference measured with mixed-effects regression. Mediation ran through "feeling understood by the AI (indirect = 0.22, 95% CI [0.06, 0.38], 59% mediated)" and helpfulness and positive affect, not certainty.

"compared to the neutral condition, participants in the sycophantic condition showed a smaller gap between their inclination to seek advice from AI and from close friends and family (0.37 pt on a 7-point scale;d= 0.19,p adj = 0.034). This shift emerged early and persisted across all three weeks."
