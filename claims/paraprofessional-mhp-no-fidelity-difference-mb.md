## Evidence

### Diebold 2020

Diebold, A., Ciolino, J. D., Johnson, J. K., Yeh, C., Gollan, J. K., & Tandon, S. D. (2020). Comparing Fidelity Outcomes of Paraprofessional and Professional Delivery of a Perinatal Depression Preventive Intervention. Administration and Policy in Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10488-020-01022-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity analysis of 160 audio-recorded MB sessions from a cluster-RCT, using linear mixed models with a random facilitator effect. Arm was not significantly associated with adherence (p = 0.090) and neither arm nor session was associated with competency (p = 0.512 and p = 0.533).

> "Neither arm nor session were significantly associated with mean competency (p = 0.512 and p = 0.533, respectively). Although arm was not significantly associated with adherence score (p = 0.090, see Fig. 1)"
