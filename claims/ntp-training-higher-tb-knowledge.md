## Evidence

### Naseer Maliha 2013

Naseer Maliha, Khawaja Ali, Pethani Amin S, Aleem Salik. (2013). How well can physicians manage Tuberculosis? A Public-Private sector comparison from Karachi, Pakistan. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-439

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 196 doctors in Karachi using five clinical vignettes and 20 multiple choice questions; the article reports "significantly higher mean knowledge scores" for NTP-trained doctors, with no effect size printed.

> "Doctors trained by NTP had significantly higher mean knowledge scores compared to those who were not trained (p-value < 0.001)."
