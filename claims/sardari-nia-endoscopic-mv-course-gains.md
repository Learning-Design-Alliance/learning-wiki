## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean operative speed decreased from 87 (SD 26) to 42 (SD 19) seconds (p < 0.001) among 103 participants in a 2-day endoscopic mitral valve surgery training course. [→ EL-Andari R 2023](#el-andari-r-2023)

## Evidence

### EL-Andari R 2023

EL-Andari R, Bozso SJ, Kang JJH, Fialka NM, Adams C, Nagendran J. (2023). Teaching heart valve surgery techniques using simulators: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.017921

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Course-based training study reported in the review: 103 participants took a 2-day endoscopic mitral valve surgery course with day 1 theoretical content on basic endoscopic skills and day 2 hands-on training on endoscopic mitral annuloplasty.

> "After the course, there was a significant decrease in mean operative speed, from 87 (SD 26) seconds to 42 (SD 19) seconds (p < 0.001)."

### EL-Andari R 2023 (2)

EL-Andari R, Bozso SJ, Kang JJH, Fialka NM, Adams C, Nagendran J. (2023). Teaching heart valve surgery techniques using simulators: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.017921

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Same 2-day course (103 participants): accuracy of posterior and anterior leaflet sutures and a standardized question assessment of theoretical knowledge (58% to 67%, p < 0.001) were measured after hands-on endoscopic mitral annuloplasty training.

> "There was also a significant improvement in accuracy with the posterior leaflet sutures improving from an initial 56% to 100% (p < 0.001) and the anterior leaflet sutures from 43% to 99% (p < 0.001)."
