## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2015

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2015). Student difficulties with the probability distribution for measuring energy and position in quantum mechanics. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1509.04081](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1509.04081)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the N = 210 survey, on questions 2 and 4 concerning energy measurement at t > 0, "77% of the students did not select statement (2)", which correctly states the E1 measurement probability is time-independent because energy is a constant of motion.

> "Table I shows that 77% of the students did not select  statement (2) in questions 2 and 4 as true indicat ing that they have difficulty with the fact that energy is conserved for a quantum system for which the Hamiltonian does not depend on time."
