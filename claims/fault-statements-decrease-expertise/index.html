Boshuizen 1995¶

Boshuizen, H. P. A., Schmidt, H. G., Custers, E. J. F. M., & van de Wiel, M. W. (1995). Knowledge Development and Restructuring in the Domain of Medicine: The Role of Theory and Practice. Learning and Instruction. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/learning-and-instruction

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 2 narrative descriptions coded for fault idea units; the article notes the steady decrease, unaffected by probe, "is a further confirmation of the hypothesis of knowlcdgc encapsulation", with experts using shorthand concepts like "virus".

"Figure 6 shows the proportion of fault statements that decreased monotonically (F(3, 81)=16.137; ~~0.001) with increasing expertise levels. No effects (neither main nor interaction) of the kind of probe applied could be found."
