## Evidence

### Jauregi 2015

Jauregi, K., Candel, S., De Vos, H., De Smet, E., Van Daele, S., & Hauck, M. (2015). Cross-cultural discussions in a 3D virtual environment and their affordances for learners' motivation and foreign language discussion skills. Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2015.000345

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental case study comparing pre- and post-test scores on discussion ability for 18 pupils in three groups. The article reports that "the three groups of pupils increased their discussion skills", with "average improvement 0.7" for the Finnish and Dutch control groups versus "0.3" for the Dutch ICC group.

> "Differences between pre- and post-test values show that the three groups of pupils increased their discussion skills. Growth was higher for the Finnish and the Dutch control group (average improvement 0.7) than for the Dutch ICC group (0.3)."
