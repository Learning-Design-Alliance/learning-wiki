Evidence¶

Walter L. Leite 2025¶

Walter L. Leite, Huibin Zhang, Shibani Rana, Yide Hao, Amber D. Hatch, Lingchen Kong, Huan Kuang. (2025). Do intelligent tutoring systems benefit K-12 students? A meta-analysis and evaluation of heterogeneity of treatment effects in the U.S. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04997

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup meta-regression across student disadvantage targeting levels. Low achievers (21 effect sizes) showed a significant effect of g=0.270; the general population (48 effect sizes) showed a smaller but significant g=0.216. The authors report these magnitudes as close.

"Also, the effect sizes were similar across studies targeting disadvantaged groups: English Learner students (g=0.266), students in poverty (g=0.303), and low achievers (g=0.270)."
