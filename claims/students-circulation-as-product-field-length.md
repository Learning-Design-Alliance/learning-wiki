## Evidence

### Álvaro Suárez 2025

Álvaro Suárez, Arturo C Marti, Kristina Zuza, Jenaro Guisasola. (2025). Understanding student challenges with circulation and Ampère-Maxwell law. arXiv. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ada1b1](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ada1b1)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phenomenographic analysis of 65 written responses; Table 1 assigns 15.4% to Category B. Interview data (Gustavo and Maite) show students reasoning that the straight section should contribute to the integral.

> "We also observed that a similar percentage disagrees with the calculation of the magnetic field circulation, because they do not recognise that the magnetic field does not contribute to the circulation in the straight section."
