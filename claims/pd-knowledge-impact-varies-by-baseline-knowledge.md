## Evidence

### Garet 2016

Garet, M. S., Heppen, J. B., Walters, K., Parkinson, J., Smith, T. M., Song, M., Garrett, R., Yang, R., & Borman, G. D. (2016). Focusing on mathematical knowledge: The impact of content-intensive teacher professional development (NCEE 2016-4010). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Differential-impact analysis of the randomized trial using baseline knowledge scores standardized with the control-group mean and SD. The report concludes the initial boost from Intel Math "was not sustained for teachers who began the PD with lower levels of knowledge."

> "the estimated impact of the PD on spring teacher knowledge was positive and statistically significant for teachers with a baseline knowledge score 1 standard deviation above average (improvement index of 34 percentile points) but was not statistically significant for teachers with a baseline knowledge score 1 standard deviation below average (improvement index of 8 percentile points)."
