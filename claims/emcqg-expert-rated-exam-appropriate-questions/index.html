Evidence¶

J. Leo 2019¶

J. Leo, G. Kurdi, N. Matentzoglu, B. Parsia, U. Sattler, S. Forge, G. Donato, W. Dowling. (2019). Ontology-Based Generation of Medical, Multi-term MCQs. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00172-w

q2 · i? · design · r2

Evaluation of EMCQG's generated questions: a stratified random sample of 435 questions, of which "316 were rated by two experts", was judged by 15 medical experts for exam appropriateness. The article reports the counts and percentages above; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

"We find that using a stratified random sample of 435 questions out of which 316 were rated by two experts, 129 (30%) are considered appropriate to be used in exams by both experts and a further 216 (50%) by at least one expert."
