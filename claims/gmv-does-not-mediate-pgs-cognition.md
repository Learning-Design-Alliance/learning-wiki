# Total grey matter volume does not mediate the association between an intelligence polygenic score and cognitive ability

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to the authors' hypothesis, the indirect effect of IQ-PGS on cognitive ability via total grey matter volume was not significant. [→ Corley E 2023](#corley-e-2023)

## Evidence

### Corley E 2023

Corley E, Fahey L, Fitzgerald J, Holleran L, Walton E, Morris DW, Donohoe G. (2023). The impact of early adversity and education on genetic and brain morphological predictors of cognitive ability. Genes, Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1111/gbb.12850

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM mediation model (Model 1) in the UK Biobank sample covarying age, sex and total intracranial volume; the PGS→GMV path and the indirect effect were both nonsignificant, so "the relationship between IQ‐PGS and cognitive ability was not mediated by total GMV".

> "the association between the IQ‐PGS and total GMV was nonsignificant (β = 0.032, SE = 0.020, p = 0.100), and the relationship between IQ‐PGS and cognitive ability was not mediated by total GMV (indirect effect β = 0.023, 95% CIs = −0.002; 0.023, SE = 0.006, p = 0.096)"
