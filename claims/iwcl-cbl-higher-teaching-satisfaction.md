## Evidence

### Su Xiaoqun 2026

Su Xiaoqun, Hu Bing, Liu Chengcheng, Ren Hongmiao. (2026). Application of integrated whole-course clinical learning combined with case-based learning in undergraduate otolaryngology clerkships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09871-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous satisfaction questionnaire completed by all 79 students (100% response rate) at clerkship end, using a 4-point Likert scale across 15 items; IC median 3.49 vs LC 3.13, adjusted p < 0.01.

> "The students in the IC group reported higher overall satisfaction with the teaching method than those in the LC group did (median [IQR]: 3.49 [3.44–3.59] vs. 3.13 [3.08–3.15]); Mann–Whitney U = 10, adjusted p < 0.01)."
