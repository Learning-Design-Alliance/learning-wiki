## Evidence

### Ðurić Saša 2021

Ðurić Saša, Bogataj Špela, Zovko Vinko, Sember Vedrana. (2021). Associations Between Physical Fitness, Objectively Measured Physical Activity and Academic Performance. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.778837

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis of SLOfit fitness tests against school-year grades in 20 girls found the PE grade most strongly related to fitness, with "r = 0.53–0.95" at significance level 0.01; the authors expected this because school PE programs exercise the relevant muscles.

> "the PE grade shows the highest overall correlations with the results of the PF test battery (r = 0.53–0.95) at the significance level 0.01."
