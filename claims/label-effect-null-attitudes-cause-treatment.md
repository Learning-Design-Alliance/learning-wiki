## Evidence

### Franz 2023

Franz, D. J., Richter, T., Lenhard, W., Marx, P., Stein, R., & Ratz, C. (2023). The Influence of Diagnostic Labels on the Evaluation of Students: a Multilevel Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09716-6

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Subgroup estimates from the evaluation-type moderator analysis in the multilevel meta-analysis of 284 effects. Three evaluation categories showed small, non-significant label effects; equivalence was not tested.

> "Non-significant effects were found for attitudes toward the child (g = 0.21), evaluations of the cause of the student's problems (g = −0.23), and evaluations of treatment strategies (g = −0.20, Table 1 and Fig. 2)."
