# Even with relaxed monotonic response assumptions, generalized SST cannot produce nonmonotonic lag functions

## Evidence

### Benjamin 2010

Benjamin, Aaron S., & Tullis, Jonathan. (2010). What makes distributed practice effective? http://www.psych.uiuc.edu/~asbenjam/SuperadditivityReferences.htm

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation (Appendices A and B): SST is generalized to variable retention intervals, the derivative of the response function with respect to lag is solved for zero, and a solution occurs only when the retention interval is zero. No inflection point exists within meaningful bounds.

> "This analysis demonstrates that, even under the most liberally relaxed response assumptions, SST can not accommodate lag functions that are nonmonotonic."
