## Evidence

### Houssa and Nader-Grosbois 2016

Marine Houssa, Nathalie Nader-Grosbois. (2016). Experimental Study of Middle-term Training in Social Cognition in Preschoolers. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i1.972

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression on post-test scores: Model 3 explained "24% of the variance" of post-test RES, with "pre -test result in RES and group condition" as the only contributing variables.

> "After accounting for the pre -test result in RES (Model 1), the RES in post -test was predicted by Model 3 (24% of the variance). It is important to note that pre -test result in RES and group condition are the only variables which made a contribution."

### Houssa and Nader-Grosbois 2016 (2)

Marine Houssa, Nathalie Nader-Grosbois. (2016). Experimental Study of Middle-term Training in Social Cognition in Preschoolers. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i1.972

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same hierarchical regression showed that for ToM Task Battery and ToMI, once pre-test results were controlled in Model 1, "predictors entered in Models 2 and 3 were non-significant".

> "After controlling for pre-test result in ToM Task Battery and ToMI (Model 1), predictors entered in Models 2 and 3 were non-significant."
