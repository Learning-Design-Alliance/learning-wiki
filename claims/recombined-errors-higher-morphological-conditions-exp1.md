Error analysis in Experiment 1 categorised errors into recombined, partial, unrelated and spelling types; Table 1 reports recombined errors of 12.04% (affix-centric) and 11.92% (base-centric) versus 3.87% (control) in Test 1.

> "As is clear from Table 1, the rate of recombined errors was much greater in the two morphological study conditions, leading to somewhat higher error rates in the two morphological conditions."
