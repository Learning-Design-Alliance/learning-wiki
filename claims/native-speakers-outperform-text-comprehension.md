### Buehler 2021

Buehler, F. J., van Loon, M. H., Bayard, N. S., Steiner, M., & Roebers, C. M. (2021). Comparing metacognitive monitoring between native and non-native speaking primary school students. Metacognition and Learning, 16, 749–768. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09261-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

In Study 2's text comprehension task (six expository German texts, 12 open-ended questions), the univariate follow-up to a significant MANOVA showed "native speakers answered significantly more open-ended questions correctly than non-native speakers" (η2p = 0.14). Descriptive means were 54.40% vs. 38.43% correct.

> "Univariate tests revealed that native speakers answered significantly more open-ended questions correctly than non-native speakers, F(1, 70) = 11.36; p < 0.01; η2p = 0.14."
