# Alumni survey responsiveness tends to peak at two milestones: the first year out and the fourth or fifth year out

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Response rates do not necessarily decline linearly over time; an experienced survey operator reports responsiveness peaks in the 1st year out and again in the 4th or 5th year out. [→ ev-peaks](#ev-peaks)

An expert interview reported in an Insight from the Field box: the interviewee identifies the 1st year out, when graduates still feel a fresh connection to high school, and the 4th or 5th year out, during a reflective phase, as the two peaks. No rates or effect sizes are printed.
