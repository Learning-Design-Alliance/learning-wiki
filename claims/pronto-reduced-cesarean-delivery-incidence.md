## Evidence

### Walker Dilys M. MD 2016

Walker Dilys M. MD, Cohen Susanna R. CNM, DNP, Fritz Jimena MD, MSc, Olvera-García Marisela MSc, Zelek Sarah T. MPH, Fahey Jenifer O. CNM, MSN, MPH, Romero-Martínez Martín PhD, Montoya-Rodríguez Alejandra MSc, Lamadrid-Figueroa Héctor MD, ScD. (2016). Impact Evaluation of PRONTO Mexico: A Simulation-Based Program in Obstetric and Neonatal Emergencies and Team Training. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000106

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pair-matched hospital-based controlled trial of 24 Mexican public hospitals (12 intervention, 12 control), analyzed with mixed-effects negative binomial regression using a difference-in-differences approach. The reported "21% decrease, P = 0.005" for cesarean sections was significant over 12 cumulative months; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "There was a significant estimated impact of PRONTO on the incidence of cesarean sections in intervention hospitals relative to controls adjusting for baseline differences during all 12 months cumulative of follow-up (21% decrease, P = 0.005)"
