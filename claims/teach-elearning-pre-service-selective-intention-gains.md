# Pre-service ECEs' behavioural intention and perceived behavioural control increased significantly only for select behaviours, with no significant differences for the remaining behaviours

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-service ECEs' behavioural intention for promoting outdoor play and avoiding screen use, and perceived behavioural control for engaging children in at least 120 min/day of physical activity and avoiding screen use, increased significantly; remaining behaviours showed no significant differences (p > .007). ## Evidence

### Bruijns BA 2022

Bruijns BA, Vanderloo LM, Johnson AM, Adamo KB, Burke SM, Carson V, Heydon R, Irwin JD, Naylor P-J, Timmons BW, Tucker P. (2022). Change in pre- and in-service early childhood educators’ knowledge, self-efficacy, and intentions following an e-learning course in physical activity and sedentary behaviour: a pilot study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-12591-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed Ranks tests of pre-service ECEs' (n = 32 retained) ECE-MBIPC composites in the pre-post pilot. Significant pre-post gains were printed for two intention and two perceived-control composites (e.g., outdoor play intention Z = −3.227, p = .001); the remaining behaviours showed no significant differences and no effect sizes are printed.

> "However, there were no significant differences in behavioural intention or perceived control for any of the remaining behaviours (p > .007)."
