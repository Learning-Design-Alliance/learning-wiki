### Ji Pengyu 2026

Ji Pengyu, Li Qianqian, Li Na, Zhang Lin. (2026). The efficacy of bilingual immunology teaching on undergraduates in China: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08725-7

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

The review reports, from one included study spanning multiple academic years (300 first-year and 180 second-year undergraduates), that sophomores did not improve relative to freshmen, prompting separate cohort meta-analyses. The authors attribute this partly to sophomores having reached the required English proficiency threshold.

> "The findings revealed that, following bilingual instruction, second-year students did not exhibit improved performance compared to their first-year counterparts. Consequently, separate meta-analysis was conducted for each cohort."
