Competing-risks regression (Fine and Gray subhazards model, death and emigration as competing events) in the Finnish register cohort. The article reports cumulative incidences "from 39.6% to 28.7%" below average versus 15.7% at the highest level; these are descriptive percentages, not standardized effect sizes.

> "The cumulative incidences of any subsequent mental disorder between the ages 16 and 38 were from 39.6% to 28.7% among those with below-average school achievement (−2SD to −1SD), 20.3% with average school achievement, and 16.1% to 15.7% with above-average school achievement (+1SD to +2SD)."
