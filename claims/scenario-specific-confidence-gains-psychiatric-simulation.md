## Evidence

### Matthew Tennant 2025

Matthew Tennant, Maggie Meeks, Ben Beaglehole, Chris Frampton, Jane Foley, Emily Ide, Dylan Hill, Carol Dean. (2025). Preparing House Officers for Emergencies in a Psychiatric Setting with Simulation-Based Learning. Academic Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40596-025-02217-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quantitative arm of the same mixed-methods evaluation: 23 house officers rated confidence for each of the three trained scenarios before and directly after the 2-hour simulation session, with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. The article reports they felt "significantly more confident managing post-injection syndrome, deliberate self-harm, and a non-fatal hanging (p < 0.001)". No effect sizes are printed.
