# Near transfer after working memory training is greatest on tasks structurally similar to the training tasks, and metacognitive strategy training extends transfer to the least similar task

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Both training groups improved on the Dot Matrix and Backwards Digit Recall tasks, which are structurally similar to Cogmed training tasks, and on forwards Digit Recall. [→ Jones JS 2020](#jones-js-2020)
`q3 i?` Only the MetaCogmed group improved on the Spatial Span task, the least similar to the training tasks, outperforming both control and Cogmed at immediate outcome. [→ Jones JS 2020 (2)](#jones-js-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Jones JS 2020

Jones JS, Milton F, Mostazir M, Adlam ALR. (2020). The academic outcomes of working memory and metacognitive strategy training in children: A double‐blind randomized controlled trial. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.12870

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Task-level ANCOVAs at immediate outcome in the RCT showed MetaCogmed transferred to all four AWMA tasks versus control, with the largest gains on tasks sharing structure and material with the Cogmed training tasks.

> "At the immediate outcome, the MetaCogmed group had significantly higher scores on the Dot Matrix (p = 0.009), Digit Recall (p = 0.001), Backwards Digit Recall (p < 0.001), and Spatial Span tasks (p = 0.006) compared to the Control group."

### Jones JS 2020 (2)

Jones JS, Milton F, Mostazir M, Adlam ALR. (2020). The academic outcomes of working memory and metacognitive strategy training in children: A double‐blind randomized controlled trial. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.12870

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA comparison of the two training groups on the task least similar to Cogmed showed a MetaCogmed advantage: "performed significantly higher on the Spatial Span task compared to the Cogmed group at the immediate outcome (p = 0.027)"; the mixed model version was non-significant (p = 0.103).

> "The MetaCogmed group also performed significantly higher on the Spatial Span task compared to the Cogmed group at the immediate outcome (p = 0.027)."
