## Evidence

### Sensibaugh Cheryl A. 2017

Sensibaugh Cheryl A., Madrid Nathaniel J., Choi Hye-Jeong, Anderson William L., Osgood Marcy P. (2017). Undergraduate Performance in Solving Ill-Defined Biochemistry Problems. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar63. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-04-0106

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of IPSA domain scores at biochemistry program graduation (Table 4, N = 47). The Investigate domain correlated moderately with Evaluate (r = 0.31, p = 0.032) and Integrate (r = 0.41, p = 0.004), which the article notes contrasts with prior findings.

> "However, in contrast to prior findings (Mitchell et al., 2011), the Investigate domain scores moderately correlated with Evaluate (r = 0.31, p = 0.032) and Integrate (r = 0.41, p = 0.004)."

### Sensibaugh Cheryl A. 2017 (2)

Sensibaugh Cheryl A., Madrid Nathaniel J., Choi Hye-Jeong, Anderson William L., Osgood Marcy P. (2017). Undergraduate Performance in Solving Ill-Defined Biochemistry Problems. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar63. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-04-0106

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

The same correlation analysis (Table 4, N = 47) found a moderate correlation between Evaluate and Integrate domain scores (r = 0.33, p = 0.025), which the article reports as expected based on previous work.

> "As expected based on previous work, a moderate correlation was also demonstrated between Evaluate and Integrate domain scores (r = 0.33, p = 0.025)."

### Sensibaugh Cheryl A. 2017 (3)

Sensibaugh Cheryl A., Madrid Nathaniel J., Choi Hye-Jeong, Anderson William L., Osgood Marcy P. (2017). Undergraduate Performance in Solving Ill-Defined Biochemistry Problems. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar63. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-04-0106

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Correlation of IPSA domain scores with ACS Biochemistry Exam scores (Table 4, N = 47): only the Evaluate domain correlated moderately with content exam scores (r = 0.36, p = 0.014), supporting the claim that domain scores are somewhat related to yet distinct from content knowledge.

> "Only the Evaluate domain scores moderately correlated with content exam scores (r = 0.36, p = 0.014)."

### Sensibaugh Cheryl A. 2017 (4)

Sensibaugh Cheryl A., Madrid Nathaniel J., Choi Hye-Jeong, Anderson William L., Osgood Marcy P. (2017). Undergraduate Performance in Solving Ill-Defined Biochemistry Problems. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar63. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-04-0106

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlation of IPSA domain scores with honors research experience (Table 4, N = 47): only the Investigate domain correlated moderately (r = 0.31, p = 0.032); students with two semesters of honors research and a thesis earned higher Investigate scores.

> "Our claim that domain scores are somewhat related to—yet distinct from—research experience was demonstrated by a moderate correlation with only the Investigate domain scores (r = 0.31, p = 0.032)."
