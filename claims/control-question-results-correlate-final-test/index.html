Evidence¶

Király 2016¶

Sándor Király. (2016). How to Implement an E-learning Curriculum to Streamline Teaching Digital Image Processing. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 9, Number 2. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/2065-1430

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Observational analysis of Moodle logs comparing average control-question results at the end of lectures with final-test results. The article reports r = 0.5530, a "near and positive relation", and that only two students achieved more than 80% in control tests but fewer than 80% in the final test, concluding the evaluation system showed students precisely how deeply they learned.

"The correlation coefficient was 0.5530 which means that there was near and positive relation between the achievements in the semester and in the final test."
