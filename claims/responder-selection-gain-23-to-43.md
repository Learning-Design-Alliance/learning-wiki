## Evidence

### Benjamin Gagl & Klara Gregorová 2024

Benjamin Gagl & Klara Gregorová. (2024). Investigating lexical categorization in reading based on joint diagnostic and training approaches for language learners. npj Science of Learning 9:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00237-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Categorization diagnostics on leave-one-out cross-validation used an optimized decision boundary of 13.5% predicted reading-speed increase, with sensitivity 0.73, specificity 0.74, accuracy 0.73, and precision 0.86 (N = 44 of 75 selected; 38 true positives, 6 false positives, 17 true negatives, 14 false negatives). The selected group's mean gain was significantly higher, t = 2.188.

> "Therefore, the selection of responders increased the mean reading speed from 23% to 43% on the group level (FE = 0.199, SE = 0.091, t = 2.188, see Fig. 5C)."
