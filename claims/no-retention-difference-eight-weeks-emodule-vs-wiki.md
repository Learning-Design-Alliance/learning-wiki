# Knowledge retention after eight weeks does not differ between eModule and Wikipedia-like webpage learning

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Second-quiz scores after a mean of 59 days did not differ significantly between the eModule group (71%) and Wiki group (68%). [→ Rajan Kiran 2022](#rajan-kiran-2022)

## Evidence

### Rajan Kiran 2022

Rajan Kiran, Pandit Anand S. (2022). Comparing computer-assisted learning activities for learning clinical neuroscience: a randomized control trial. BMC Medical Education 22:522. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03578-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fourteen participants per group returned the second quiz after a mean of 59 days; the retention quiz covered the same topics with changed question details and was compared with an unpaired t-test, showing "non-significant (p = 0.7)" difference.

> "Differences between the mean score of the second quiz for the e-learning group (71%, 95% CI 61% to 80%) and the control-group (68%, 95% CI 60% to 77%) were non-significant (p = 0.7)."
