Evidence¶

Jen DeNeal 2025¶

Jen DeNeal, Charlotte Pierce, and the EdTrust-Midwest team. (2025). Addressing Michigan's Teacher Shortage Problem for Students Most in Need: Closing the Opportunity Divide. EdTrust-Midwest. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED671388

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of Michigan educator workforce data (MI School Data 23-24 Annual Education Report and Staff and Student Headcount Reports) found "more than 16.5% of teachers in high-poverty school districts were teaching out-of-field" versus under 4% in low-poverty districts, against a state average of 8.1%.

"In 2022-23, more than 16.5% of teachers in high-poverty school districts were teaching out-of-field – twice the state average. On the other hand, the out-of-field teacher rate for low-poverty school districts was less than 4%"
