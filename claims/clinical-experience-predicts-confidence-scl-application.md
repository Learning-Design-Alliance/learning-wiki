> "Ordinal logistic regression adjusting for gender and academic degree revealed that greater clinical experience was independently associated with higher odds of reporting greater confidence in applying SCL-acquired knowledge to clinical practice (adjusted OR = 1.78 per experience category increase, 95% CI: 1.05–3.01, P = 0.031)."
