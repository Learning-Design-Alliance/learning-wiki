## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The relationship between stress and performance is not linear: low or high stress impairs vigilance and working memory performance while moderate stress improves it. [→ Bensouda Brahim 2018](#bensouda-brahim-2018)

## Evidence

### Bensouda Brahim 2018

Bensouda Brahim, Mandel Romain, Mejri Abdelwaheb, Lachapelle Jean, St-Hilaire Marie, Ali Nabeel. (2018). Effect of an audience on trainee stress and performance during simulated neonatal intubation: a randomized crossover trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1338-4

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Theoretical argument offered in the Discussion, citing prior literature, to explain why the audience raised heart rate without lengthening intubation time. The article offers this as interpretation rather than a tested result of the present trial.

> "Studies have demonstrated that the effects of stress intensity on behavior are characterized by an inverted-U-shaped function while low or high levels of stress lead to performance impairment on tests of vigilance and working memory, a moderate level of stress leads to performance improvement"
