# Curiosity Corner shows significant positive kindergarten follow-up effects on early reading and letter-word identification, averaging +0.27

## Evidence

### Curiosity Corner 2016

Curiosity Corner. (2016). Curiosity Corner. Evidence for the ESSA program review. https://evidenceforessp.org.uk/curiosity-corner

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Kindergarten follow-up of the PCER cluster randomized study (fewer than 350 students). The review reports "significant positive effects were found on TERA (effect size=+0.43), and Letter-Word ID (effect size=+0.43)" and an average follow-up effect of +0.27.

> "However, at kindergarten follow-up, significant positive effects were found on TERA (effect size=+0.43), and Letter-Word ID (effect size=+0.43), and non-significant positive effects were seen on spelling, CTOPP, PPVT, and TOLD, for an average of +0.27."
