## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Applying the same rule to all students is fair only if the rules themselves are fair; it is therefore of no use except in the context of grading. [→ Mathieu Bouville 2008](#mathieu-bouville-2008)

## Evidence

### Mathieu Bouville 2008

Mathieu Bouville. (2008). Exam fairness. https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4235 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in the Fairness of Treatment section: because a rule such as crediting names starting with B is applied identically yet favors some, treatment fairness cannot generate exam-design objectives. The author states it "is not applicable to exam design as a whole."

> "Fairness of treatment is no fairness at all when it means applying rules that are themselves unfair: it merely propagates the fairness or unfairness of pre-existing rules. It may be relevant to grading but it is not applicable to exam design as a whole."
