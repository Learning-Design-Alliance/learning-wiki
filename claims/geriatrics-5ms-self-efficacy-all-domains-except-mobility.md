## Evidence

### Phillips SC 2019

Phillips SC, Hawley CE, Triantafylidis LK, Schwartz AW. (2019). Geriatrics 5Ms for Primary Care Workshop. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10814

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre- versus postworkshop comparison of self-efficacy ratings on a 5-point Likert scale among the 33 workshop learners across three pilot sessions. The article reports "self-efficacy in all five domains increased" but describes these as trends that were not statistically significant; no effect size was printed.

> "We asked learners to rate self-efficacy in the Geriatrics 5Ms domains on a 5-point Likert scale. Among learners who participated in the workshop, self-efficacy in all five domains increased (Figure 1)."

### Phillips SC 2019 (2)

Phillips SC, Hawley CE, Triantafylidis LK, Schwartz AW. (2019). Geriatrics 5Ms for Primary Care Workshop. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10814

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Workshop learners (N = 27) versus control learners (N = 10) comparison of self-efficacy ratings on a 5-point Likert scale. Higher ratings favored workshop participants in all domains except mobility; the article states these trends were not statistically significant and prints no effect size.

> "Compared to control learners, higher self-efficacy ratings were seen among workshop participants in all domains except mobility (Figure 2)."
