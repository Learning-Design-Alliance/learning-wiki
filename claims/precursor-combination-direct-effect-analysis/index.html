Evidence¶

Jessica Massonnié 2018¶

Jessica Massonnié, Maryse Bianco, Laurent Lima, Pascal Bressoux. (2018). Longitudinal predictors of reading comprehension in French at first grade: Unpacking the oral comprehension component of the simple view. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.005

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Alternative SEMs on the same 556 French first-graders computed every two-precursor combination (Table 3); with phonemic awareness and letter knowledge but no RAN, prediction of T2 decoding skills was weaker (β = .49) than with RAN included (β = .9 or .91).

"First, the unique combination of phonemic awareness and letter knowledge seemed to explain the direct effect from T1 decoding precursors to T2 reading comprehension. Secondly, the inclusion of RAN in the decoding precursor factor strengthened the prediction of T2 decoding skills."
