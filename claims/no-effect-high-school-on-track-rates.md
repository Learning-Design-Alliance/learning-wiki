Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago Urban Education Institute. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/when-schools-close-effects-displaced-students-chicago-public-schools

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

High school outcomes analysis using the freshman on-track indicator (Figure 8): on-track rate was 48.1 percent for displaced students versus 47.1 percent for the comparison group, with no significant difference; earlier-grade displaced students entered high school at expected achievement levels.

> "Despite entering high school significantly behind in reading and in math, students whose schools closed at the end of their eighth-grade year were just as likely to be on-track at the end of their freshmen year as students in our comparison group."
