`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Practical test of a modified RUSH protocol on healthy subjects at T3, scored with a DOPS-based form structured by the iPOCUS mnemonic (max. 315 AP, 10 min). The article reports the total score quoted and per-view means, with best results in "Lung" (91.6 ± 7.0) and lowest in the suprapubic view (83.0 ± 12.5).

> "In the practical testing of the RUSH protocol (P-RUSHT3), paramedics achieved total score results in high percentage ranges (87.0 ± 5.6)."
