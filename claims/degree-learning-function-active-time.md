`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the Future Research section. The author notes psychologists have paid little attention to pure time in human learning and cites Bugelski's paired-associate experiment as supporting "more parsimonious descriptions of learning may be obtained by use of time as a variable" rather than number of trials.

> "One of the bolder hypotheses implicit in the model is that the degree of learning, other things being equal, is a simple function of the amount of time during which the pupil engages actively in learning."
