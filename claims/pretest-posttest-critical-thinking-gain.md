## Evidence

### Chaipichit et al. 2015

Dudduan Chaipichit, Nirat Jantharajit and Sumalee Chookhampaeng. (2015). Development of learning management model based on constructivist theory and reasoning strategies for enhancing the critical thinking of secondary students. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2193

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-samples t-test comparison of pre-test and post-test critical thinking scores (maximum 60) for the experimental class taught with the model. The article reports t = 29.536, p = .000 for the difference between means 27.26 and 46.48.

> "the students taught by the instructional model had average scores on the test in critical thinking (maximum score was 60) of 27.26, and the average post-test score was 46.48. This difference was at the .05 level of significant."
