`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomised controlled trial comparing a weekly EBP-structured case conference with a weekly didactic lecture for final-year medical students (n=94) in Taiwan. The review reports "significantly higher post-intervention scores of EBP knowledge: 21.2 (3.5) vs. 19.0 (4.6), P<0.01".

> "When compared to students in the didactic lecture group, students in the EBP conference group had significantly higher post-intervention scores of EBP knowledge: 21.2 (3.5) vs. 19.0 (4.6), P<0.01."
