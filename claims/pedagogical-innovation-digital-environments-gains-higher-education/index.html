Evidence¶

Casimiro Urcos CN 2026¶

Casimiro Urcos CN, Casimiro Urcos WH, Alberca Pintado NE, Ostos Miraval RM, Carrasco Muñoz MA, Piñas Rivera LC. (2026). Pedagogical Innovation in Digital Environments: A Systematic Review Focused on Higher Education. F1000Research 15:522. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.176682.1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's thematic synthesis of 39 included studies (total 9927 participants) reported "improvements in motivation, self-regulation, academic performance, and digital skills". No pooled effect sizes were printed, so magnitude is not stated.

"Improvements were reported in motivation, self-regulation, academic performance, and digital skills, although gaps in connectivity, teacher training, and institutional sustainability persisted."
