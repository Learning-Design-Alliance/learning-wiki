q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative thematic analysis of participant responses on identity. Participants cited lack of Black representation in recruitment and management, frequent microaggressions, and compounding effects of intersectional identities on perceived visibility and validation.

"a recurring themeof some participants who identified as Black women shared that in tech spaces they are often 1) notseen to be at the same level of intellect as men, 2) being overlooked when it comes to discussions,advice, and opinions"
