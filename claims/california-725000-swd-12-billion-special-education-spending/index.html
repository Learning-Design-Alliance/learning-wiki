Jason Willis 2020¶

Jason Willis, Sara Menlove Doutre, Kelsey Krausen, Tyson Barrett, Tye Ripma, and Ruthie Caparas. (2020). Study of California's Special Education Funding System: Phase I Report — A Descriptive Analysis of Special Education Funding in California. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616102

Descriptive analysis of 2018/19 publicly available California data in the executive summary reports the scale of the system: "more than 725,000 students with disabilities" and roughly $12 billion invested annually. A companion figure reports state funding at approximately 28 percent and LEA unrestricted funds at 61 percent of spending.
