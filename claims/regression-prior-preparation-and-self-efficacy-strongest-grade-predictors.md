# In regression models, working alone predicted higher grades before COVID but not during COVID, while prior preparation (HS GPA, SAT Math) and physics self-efficacy were the strongest grade predictors in both periods

## Evidence

### Ghimire A and Singh C 2025

Ghimire A and Singh C. (2025). Peer Influence on Physics Self-Efficacy and Grades: A comparative study of students in an introductory calculus-based course who typically worked alone or in groups before and during the pandemic. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression models predicting course grade (Table III) using gender, working mode, HS GPA, SAT Math, and physics self-efficacy. The article reports that before COVID "those students who TWA tended to score higher than those who TWG, as shown by the negative coefficient for the Alone/Group variable"; during COVID neither gender nor working mode was significant.

> "Across all models, those students who TWA tended to score higher than those who TWG, as shown by the negative coefficient for the Alone/Group variable."

### Ghimire A and Singh C 2025 (2)

Ghimire A and Singh C. (2025). Peer Influence on Physics Self-Efficacy and Grades: A comparative study of students in an introductory calculus-based course who typically worked alone or in groups before and during the pandemic. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression Model 4 (Table III) combining prior preparation and self-efficacy. The article reports Model 4 "does the best job of explaining grade differences" with adjusted R² of 0.40 before COVID and 0.31 during COVID, with HS GPA, SAT Math, and SE significant (p < 0.001) in both periods.

> "Finally, Model 4, which includes both prior preparation and self-efficacy, does the best job of explaining grade differences. This suggests that a mix of solid earlier preparation and confidence in physics is the best predictor of success in the course."
