# Embedding test-taking strategies and ACT practice items in algebra improved practice ACT mathematics scores (McMann, 1994; effect size 0.34)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a randomized controlled trial of 196 students, students whose algebra course embedded general test-taking strategies and ACT practice items scored statistically significantly higher on a practice ACT mathematics exam than comparison students (effect size 0.34, improvement index +13). [→ ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016](#actsat-test-preparation-and-coaching-programs-2016)
`q3 i1` Supplemental findings for the same trial show small positive effects for male students (effect size 0.35, p .09) and female students (effect size 0.38, p .06) that were not statistically significant in either gender subgroup. [→ ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016 (2)](#actsat-test-preparation-and-coaching-programs-2016-2)
