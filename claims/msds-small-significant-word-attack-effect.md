# The MSDS structured decoding curriculum produced a small but significant effect on adult learners' pseudoword decoding (WJR Word Attack) relative to control instruction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a randomized control field trial, the treatment group made significantly greater gains than the control group on the WJR Word Attack decoding test with a small effect size of 0.19. [→ Alamprese 2011](#alamprese-2011)

## Evidence

### Alamprese 2011

Alamprese, J. A., MacArthur, C. A., Price, C., & Knight, D. (2011). Effects of a Structured Decoding Curriculum on Adult Literacy Learners' Reading Development. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2011.555294

`q3 · i1`

Randomized control field trial with 16 ABE programs randomly assigned at the program level; 349 learners with pre- and posttests analyzed in two-level hierarchical linear models. The treatment effect on WJR Word Attack was "a small effect size of 0.19" (p = .047 in Table 2).

> "The treatment group made significantly greater gains than the control group on the Woodcock-Johnson-R Word Attack test with a small effect size of 0.19."
