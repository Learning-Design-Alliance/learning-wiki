Deborah Lowe Vandell 2021¶

Deborah Lowe Vandell, Megan Kuhfeld, Elizabeth Gershoff, Robert Crosnoe. (2021). Predictors and consequences of school mobility in middle childhood. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology, 76. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/predictors-and-consequences-of-school-mobility-in-middle-childhood/

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Design statement of the study: a prospective longitudinal design with N = 1094 children followed from a mean age of 5.6 years to 11.1 years, distinguishing "a move during the previous year" from "cumulative moves across elementary school" as predictors of child functioning.

"In this prospective longitudinal study (N = 1094, M age = 5.6 years to M age = 11.1 years), the authors examined family factors associated with school mobility and then asked if either a move during the previous year or cumulative moves across elementary school were related to child functioning."
