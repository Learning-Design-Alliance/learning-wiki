## Evidence

### Loosveld LM 2020

Loosveld LM, Van Gerven PWM, Vanassche E, Driessen EW. (2020). Mentors’ Beliefs About Their Roles in Health Care Education: A Qualitative Study of Mentors’ Personal Interpretative Framework. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000003159

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretation from the cross-case analysis of 18 mentor interviews: positions are "not... fixed and stable innate traits" and may "dynamically vary across contexts and over time," modified by factors such as type of mentee, formal or informal mentoring, and whether assessment is required.

> "These positions should not be regarded as fixed and stable innate traits but rather as reflecting the different conceptualizations and combinations of mentors’ PSU and SET"
