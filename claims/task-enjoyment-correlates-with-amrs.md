# Average task enjoyment correlates with trait-based reading motivation (AMRS) but not with reading engagement

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Average enjoyment in the task correlated with AMRS trait motivation (r = 0.27, p = 0.019) but not with reading engagement (r = 0.15, p = 0.21). [→ Amrita Bains 2023](#amrita-bains-2023)

## Evidence

### Amrita Bains 2023

Amrita Bains, Carina Spaulding, Jessie Ricketts and Saloni Krishnan. (2023). Using a willingness to wait design to assess how readers value text. npj Science of Learning 8:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00160-3

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pooled correlational analysis across both experiments comparing the paradigm's average enjoyment metric with traditional measures; the small positive AMRS correlation (r = 0.27, a medium-range correlation) supports that the task taps liking.

> "As expected, average enjoyment in our task was correlated with trait-based motivation as assessed by the AMRS, r = 0.27,p = 0.019. We did not find a correlation between average enjoyment in our task and reading engagement,r = 0.15, p = 0.21."
