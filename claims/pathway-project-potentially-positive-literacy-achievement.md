# The Pathway to Academic Success Project has potentially positive effects on literacy achievement for English learners in grades 6-12

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2021

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021). Pathway to Academic Success Project: Intervention Report | English Learners Topic Area. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/724

Findings from the Kim et al. (2011) randomized controlled trial with low attrition. On the California Standards Test English language arts composite for 2,726 English learners, the intervention mean was 328.48 versus 325.60, effect size "0.07", improvement index "+3", p <.05.
