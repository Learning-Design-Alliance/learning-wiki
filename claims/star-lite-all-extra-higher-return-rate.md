## Evidence

### Liu 2022

Liu, C., Bruner, J., & Ammigan, R. (2022). Success Training for Academic Resiliency: An Advising Intervention Program for Undergraduate Students on Probation. Journal of Interdisciplinary Studies in Education, 11(2). https://ojed.org/jise

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of return-to-good-standing rates disaggregated by program completion level (none, some, all, extra) across four cohorts of STAR Lite participants and a 41-student comparison group. The article reports "Participants that completed all the STAR Lite program or extra components of the program returned to good academic standing at a higher rate than the comparison group." No inferential test or effect size accompanies this contrast.

> "Participants that completed all the STAR Lite program or extra components of the program returned to good academic standing at a higher rate than the comparison group while students that completed none or only some of the program returned to academic standing at a lower rate."
