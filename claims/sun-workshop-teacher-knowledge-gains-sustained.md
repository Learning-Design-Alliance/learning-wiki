### Batiza Ann Finney 2013

Batiza Ann Finney, Gruhl Mary, Zhang Bo, Harrington Tom, Roberts Marisa, LaFlamme Donna, Haasch Mary Anne, Knopp Jonathan, Vogt Gina, Goodsell David, Hagedorn Eric, Marcey David, Hoelzer Mark, Nelson Dave. (2013). The Effects of the SUN Project on Teacher Knowledge and Self-Efficacy Regarding Biological Energy Transfer Are Significant and Long-Lasting: Results of a Randomized Controlled Trial. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-09-0155

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which equivalent school pairs were assigned to SUN treatment or control; knowledge was measured by a 25-item multiple-choice exam and a drawing with written explanation. The article reports a "maintained a significant increase even 1 yr later" for the treatment group; no effect size is printed in this section.

> "While the treatment and control groups were initially similar, teachers significantly increased their understanding of biological energy transfer as a result of the SUN workshop and maintained a significant increase even 1 yr later compared with their preworkshop score and compared with the preworkshop 2009 scores of randomly assigned controls."
