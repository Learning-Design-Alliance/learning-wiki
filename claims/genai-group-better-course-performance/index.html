Evidence¶

Haifang Liu 2026¶

Haifang Liu. (2026). AI-enhanced legal pedagogy: a case study of generative AI integration in property law courses. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10698-9

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Between-group comparison of course assessment components in the quasi-experiment (Table 6); the case-analysis contrast is quoted, and the article prints d values of 0.65–0.88 across components, all medium-to-large.

"the experimental group earned a significantly higher average grade on course assignments with a focus on case analysis (experimental group, M = 82.4, SD = 6.8, vs. Control Group, M = 76.1, SD = 7.4), t(178) = 5.93, p < .001, Cohen's d = 0.88"
