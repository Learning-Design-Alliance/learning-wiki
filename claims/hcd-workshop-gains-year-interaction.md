# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, self-reported creative self-efficacy and psychological empowerment responses fell overall from pretest to posttest but rose significantly from pretest to posttest in 2023

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In one uncontrolled pre-post study, self-reported CSE and PE responses fell overall from pretest to posttest but rose significantly from pretest to posttest in 2023 (P < .001 for each). [→ Holt JM 2025](#holt-jm-2025)
`q3 i?` In one uncontrolled pre-post study, self-reported TDT subconstruct responses rose significantly from pretest to posttest in 2023 for all subconstructs except Integrative Thinking (P = .864) and Collaboration (P = .507). [→ Holt JM 2025 (2)](#holt-jm-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Holt JM 2025

Holt JM, Talsma A, Lloren JIC, Eljarrah I, Woehrle L, Avdeev I. (2025). Human-Centered Design in Graduate Nursing Education: Outcomes From a Cohort Study. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001778

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear mixed-effects model results in Table 2: the Posttest*2023 interaction estimates were 1.425 (95% CI 1.122-1.729) for CSE and .626 (95% CI .203-.774) for PE, both P < .001. The article calls these "significantly higher in the CSE (P < .001) and PE (P < .001) instrument constructs".

> "Though, in general, there is a significant decrease in mean posttest responses, this is not the case in the year 2023, where the posttest responses are significantly higher in the CSE (P < .001) and PE (P < .001) instrument constructs."

### Holt JM 2025 (2)

Holt JM, Talsma A, Lloren JIC, Eljarrah I, Woehrle L, Avdeev I. (2025). Human-Centered Design in Graduate Nursing Education: Outcomes From a Cohort Study. Nurse Educator. https://doi.org/10.1097/NNE.0000000000001778

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Supplemental Table 3 subconstruct-level mean change results, reported as "significantly higher in all the subconstructs (P = .032 to P < .001)" except two TDT subscales. This is a secondary analysis of the same year-adjusted models.

> "this is not the case in the year 2023, where the posttest responses are significantly higher in all the subconstructs (P = .032 to P < .001) except for 2 of the subscales in the TDT instrument (Integrative Thinking, P = .864 and Collaboration, P = .507)."
