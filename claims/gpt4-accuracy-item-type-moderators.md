## Evidence

### Bodke Parag 2026

Bodke Parag, Jumle Rahul. (2026). Large language models in undergraduate medical education: a systematic review of examination performance, knowledge acquisition, and learner perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09999-7

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of performance moderators across 19 examination studies in the review's Results. The review reports the named accuracy ranges and notes a systematic overconfidence pattern in five studies, with high confidence assigned to incorrect responses.

> "GPT-4 accuracy on single-fact recall and pattern recognition items (83%–88%) substantially exceeded its accuracy on multi-step integration vignettes requiring sequential clinical decision-making (61%–71%). Performance on image-dependent questions (radiology, histopathology, dermatology) was disproportionately low (48%–63%)"
