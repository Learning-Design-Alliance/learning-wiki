## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students who practiced PVC placement in the Skills Lab scored significantly higher on the PVC OSCE station than matched non-practicing peers (ATT 9.98 percentage points). [→ Tsikas Stefanos A. 2024](#tsikas-stefanos-a-2024)

## Evidence

### Tsikas Stefanos A. 2024

Tsikas Stefanos A., Afshar Kambiz, Fischer Volkhard. (2024). Does voluntary practice improve the outcome of an OSCE in undergraduate medical studies? A Propensity Score Matching approach. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0312387

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity Score Matching analysis (kernel matching with replacement, common support enforced) comparing OSCE scores of students who reported free practice of PVC with matched non-practicing controls; the ATT was "almost ten percentage points" (Table 2 reports 9.98%, T-statistic 2.01).

> "The placement of a peripheral venous catheter (PVC) appears to be the most challenging medical skill, with FP demonstrating a substantial ATT of almost ten percentage points in the OSCE."
