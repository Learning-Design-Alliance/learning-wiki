# An analytic criterion fsT² < c²a/C0² (C0 = 3.073490) determines when an accelerating source produces an unambiguous Doppler-shifted spectral peak

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The article derives that the dominant spectral term G0 has a well-defined peak only when fsT² < c²a/C0², with C0 = 3.073490 determined numerically. [→ Kenichiro Aoki 2009](#kenichiro-aoki-2009)

Analytical derivation of the power spectrum of a signal from a constantly accelerating source (Section IV), expressed via Fresnel integrals; the derivation shows "G0 has a well deﬁned peak" only under the stated condition on the dimensionless parameter yo.
