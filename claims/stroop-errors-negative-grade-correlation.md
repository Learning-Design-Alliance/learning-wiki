## Evidence

### Dvorak Martin 2024

Dvorak Martin. (2024). Inhibitory control and academic achievement – a study of the relationship between Stroop Effect and university students’ academic performance. BMC Psychology 12:498. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01984-3

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation of Stroop Test error counts with grade averages in the 107-student sample yielded the study's only significant relationship, "r(107) = − 0.21, p = .03"; the printed effect size r = −0.21 is a small-to-medium correlation, indicating academically weaker students made more incorrect color identifications.

> "The only statistically significant relationship detected appears to be the one between the students’ grade averages and the number of mistakes (incorrect color identifications) made during the Stroop Test with a weak negative correlation, r(107) = − 0.21, p = .03"
