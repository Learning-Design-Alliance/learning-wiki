## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One-sample t-tests showed significant activation in the mid frontal cortex, right parietal cortex and right putamen during proactive inhibition, and bilateral striatum activation during reactive inhibition. [→ Pas P. 2021](#pas-p-2021)

## Evidence

### Pas P. 2021

Pas P., Hulshoff Pol H.E., Raemaekers M., Vink M. (2021). Self-regulation in the pre-adolescent brain. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101012

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ROI one-sample t-tests in the cohort found significant proactive-inhibition activation surviving Bonferroni correction (p < 0.05/3 = 0.017) in "the mid frontal cortex, t(639) = 9.11; p < 0.001, the right parietal cortex, t(639) = 6.90; p < 0.001, and the right putamen t(639) = 4.42". Reactive inhibition showed significant bilateral striatal activation (left t(639) = 10.67, right t(639) = 10.65, both p < 0.001).

> "For proactive inhibition, there was significant activation in the network associated with proactive inhibition: the mid frontal cortex, t(639) = 9.11; p < 0.001, the right parietal cortex, t(639) = 6.90; p < 0.001, and the right putamen t(639) = 4.42; p < 0.001."
