Hidaka BH, Asghar A, Aktipis CA, Nesse RM, Wolpaw TM, Skursky NK, Bennett KJ, Beyrouty MW, Schwartz MD. (2015). The status of evolutionary medicine education in North American medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0322-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 51 responding curriculum deans rating importance and coverage of 12 evolutionary medicine topics; Spearman correlation of school-level mean ratings reported in Results. The article reports "rs = 0.76, P < 0.01"; no standardized effect size beyond the correlation is printed.

> "The overall mean rating of all 12 topics’ importance at a medical school was highly correlated with the school’s mean reported coverage (rs = 0.76, P < 0.01)."
