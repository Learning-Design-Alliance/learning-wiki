### Mastour Haniye 2023

Mastour Haniye, Emadzadeh Ali, Hamidi Haji Abadi Omid, Niroumand Shabnam. (2023). Are students performing the same in E-learning and In-person education? An introspective look at learning environments from an Iranian medical school standpoint. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04159-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparison of knowledge test scores for the same students across two consecutive semesters of preclinical courses, first in-person then e-learning. The article reports the overall average scores "increased significantly during e-learning versus in-person education (P < 0.001)".

> "Furthermore, the overall students’ average scores increased significantly during e-learning versus in-person education (P < 0.001)."

### Mastour Haniye 2023 (2)

An introspective look at learning environments from an Iranian medical school standpoint. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04159-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional comparative analytical study using census sampling; the same 126 undergraduate medical students were repeatedly measured under both teaching methods in the preclinical physiopathology phase at Mashhad University of Medical Sciences, Iran.

> "a group of 126 undergraduate medical students’ intended learning outcomes under two different teaching methods, including e-learning and in-person, were repeatedly measured based on the census sampling method."
