Descriptive analysis of post-PD Likert survey responses from the 50 participants covering four modules. The quote reports the overall positive pattern; Table 3 shows the module 3 usefulness item at M = 3.86, the lowest rating. Inductive coding of open-ended comments yielded themes of awareness increase, knowledge expansion, empowering teachers, and protecting students.

> "All items except for one item "the information is useful for your current position" in Module 3 were rated above 4 on the five-point Likert scale showing positive perception."
