`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Individual-level LMM on Scenario B data controlling for pre-simulation responses. PSind significantly predicted post-stress; adding PAind or the interaction did not further improve model fit. Only a p-value is printed.

> "For post-stress levels, the effect of PSind was signiﬁcant in Model 1.1 ( AIC = 262.26, BIC = 271.78, /Delta1χ2 = 4.23, p = .040)."

### Lixiang Yan · Dragan Gašević · Vanessa Echeverria · Linxuan Zhao · Yueqiao Jin · Xinyu Li · Roberto Martinez-Maldonado 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Individual-level LMM on Scenario B data. PAind significantly predicted task performance satisfaction; the interaction between PSind and PAind did not improve fit significantly (p = .154). Only a p-value is printed.

> "The effect of PA ind on task performance satisfaction was signiﬁcant in Model 1.2 (AIC = 235.82, BIC = 247.73, /Delta1χ2 = 5.89, p = .015), indicating that higher PAind scores were associated with greater task satisfaction."
