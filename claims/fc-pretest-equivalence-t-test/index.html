Cabi 2018¶

Emine Cabi. (2018). The Impact of the Flipped Classroom Model on Students' Academic Achievement. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 19(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1185114

q2 · i? · causal · r2

An unrelated t-test compared pre-test scores of the experimental (n=28) and control (n=31) groups before treatment; the article reports t(57)=0.71, p>0.05, and concludes both groups could participate in the experimental process. No effect size is printed.

"Table 4 displays that there were no statistically significant differences between the mean scores of the students (t(57)=0.71, p> 0.05)."
