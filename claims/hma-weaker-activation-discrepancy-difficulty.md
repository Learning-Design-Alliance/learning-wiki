## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the task difficulty × math anxiety group interaction contrast, the HMA group showed weaker activation discrepancy between high and low difficulty than LMA peers in ventral valuation and cognitive control regions; the reward × group interaction showed no significant effect on activation. ## Evidence

### Jie Liu 2025

Jie Liu, Zhifeng Li, Jiawang Yang, Hao He & Fang Cui. (2025). The neurocognitive mechanism underlying math avoidance among math anxious people. npj Science of Learning 10:50. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00343-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

fMRI GLM in the same study localized activation for the difficulty × group interaction in regions including cuneus, SMA, right TPJ, MCC, Rolandic operculum, left PI, right HPC, NAc, and superior temporal gyrus; the HMA group's high-low difficulty activation discrepancy was weaker than LMA peers'.

> "The HMA group showed weaker brain activation discrepancy between high and low task difﬁculty among these areas than their LMA peers. In contrast, the interaction effect between reward magnitude and math anxiety group showed no signiﬁcant effect on the brain activation, which is consistent with the behavioral observations."
