## Evidence

### Novicoff 2024

Novicoff, S., Reardon, S. F., & Johnson, R. C. (2024). California's English learners and their long-term learning outcomes. Learning Policy Institute.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cumulative percentage of K-cohort ELs meeting state guidance for English proficiency on the CELDT (Early Advanced overall with Intermediate domain scores), shown in Figure 4. The report notes that "for kindergarteners in 2015, 40.0% had reached this level (a two thirds increase)" over the 2006 cohort.

> "for kindergarteners in 2006, only 23.4% of K-cohort EL students were at this level by the end of 1st grade. for kindergarteners in 2015, 40.0% had reached this level (a two thirds increase)."

### Novicoff 2024 (2)

Novicoff, S., Reardon, S. F., & Johnson, R. C. (2024). California's English learners and their long-term learning outcomes. Learning Policy Institute.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of CELDT domain-level scores (Figure B6) shows improvements across listening, speaking, and reading, "with the largest cohort changes in the rate of acquisition in reading attainment," while writing proficiency by end of 1st grade was relatively flat between the 2009 and 2015 cohorts.

> "the percentage scoring Intermediate for reading improved by 23.0%, the percentage scoring Intermediate for speaking improved by 19.7%, and the percentage scoring Intermediate for listening improved by 16.7%."
