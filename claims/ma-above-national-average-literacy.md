## Evidence

### Mariann Lemke 2024

Mariann Lemke, Dan Murphy, Aaron Soo Ping Chow, and Angela Acuña. (2024). Early Literacy Performance in Massachusetts: Results of Ongoing Analysis of Literacy Screening Assessments. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED663754

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of Massachusetts students' screening performance to national norms provided by assessment publishers, reported overall and by student group. Hispanic students performed less well than the national sample.

> "At EOY, the median national percentile of performance for Massachusetts students was 58 (with 50 the median national percentile). Black students in Massachusetts perform about the same as all other students around the country, White students perform slightly better, Asian students perform significantly better"

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Growth analysis using publisher-provided growth norms compares the share of Massachusetts students growing at average or above-average rates to the national share.

> "Growth of students using publisher-provided growth norms shows 66 percent of students growing at average or above average rates compared to about 60 percent nationally."
