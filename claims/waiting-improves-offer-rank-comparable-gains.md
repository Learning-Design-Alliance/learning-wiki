## Evidence

### Boon-Falleur 2025

Boon-Falleur, M., Huillery, E., & Chevallier, C. (2025). Waiting time during admission procedures increases social inequalities in higher education. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 122(47), e2426604122. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2426604122

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Regression of preference gain (rank improvement of final offer vs. initial offer) on waiting behavior with financial aid interactions, year fixed effects, and high-school-clustered SE; the study found waiting "increased the rank of their final offer" with interaction coefficients of 0.01 and −0.01, both non-significant.

> "students who waited once increased the rank of their final offer by an average of 0.64 (SE = 0.01, P < 0.001) while those who always waited increased the rank of their final offer by an average of 0.51 (SE = 0.005, P < 0.001)"
