Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year One 2017¶

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year One. (2017). Digital Promise and Maker Education Initiative. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/dfc366e2-83b1-4b7b-8899-c7519dc364d8

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey question on grade levels involved in maker learning, reported in Figure 1 with total responses of 105 (K-2), 126 (3-5), 120 (6-8), 83 (9-10), and 81 (11-12). The text states the finding "echoes that of Remold et al. (2016)" on maker educators focusing on learners aged 5-13.

"Maker champions reported the ages of students participating in maker learning activities in their schools (Figure 1), revealing upper elementary and middle grades have much more access to this type of learning than younger students (early elementary) and older students (high school) ."
