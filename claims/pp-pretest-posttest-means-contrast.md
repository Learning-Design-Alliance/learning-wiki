### Amiripour 2017

Amiripour, P., Dossey, J.A., & Shahvarani, A. (2017). Using a new schema approach with primary at-risk students in word problem solving. REDIMAT, 6(3), 228-255. http://dx.doi.org/10.17583/redimat.2017.2612

Descriptive comparison of total-population pretest and posttest statistics (N = 50 per group) in Table 3: control means 6.56 to 6.40, experimental means 5.84 to 15.68. These are descriptive means; the article reports no effect size for this contrast.

> "Striking in this data are the similarities of the two groups on the pretests, but their vast differences in their performance on the posttests."
