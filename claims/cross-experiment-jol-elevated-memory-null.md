# Across both experiments, richer-format groups reported elevated judgments of learning versus static diagrams, but memory did not differ among any of the four groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Both Experiment 2 richer-material groups gave higher judgments of learning than the Experiment 1 static condition (video: d = 0.376; VR passive: d = 0.83). [→ Benjamin A. Clegg 2024](#benjamin-a-clegg-2024)

## Evidence

### Benjamin A. Clegg 2024

Benjamin A. Clegg, Alex Karduna, Ethan Holen, Jason Garcia, Matthew G. Rhodes, & Francisco R. Ortega. (2024). Multimedia and Immersive Training Materials Influence Impressions of Learning But Not Learning Outcomes. 2022 Interservice/Industry Training, Simulation, and Education Conference (I/ITSEC), Paper No. 22459. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.05504](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.05504)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cross-experiment comparison combining the two experiments' ratings, with a significant omnibus difference in ratings of learning, "F(3,160) = 6.46, p < .001, h2p = 0.11", and both richer groups exceeding the static condition (printed effect sizes d = 0.376 and d = 0.83).

> "both Experiment 2 groups with richer materials also differed in their judgments of the learning that had occurred following study compared to the previous static image condition from Experiment 1 (Exp. 1 static versus Exp. 2 video: t(81) = 2.78, p < .01, d = 0.376; Exp. 1 static versus Exp 2 VR passive: t(81) = 3.03, p < .005, d = 0.83)"

### Benjamin A. Clegg 2024 (2)

Benjamin A. Clegg, Alex Karduna, Ethan Holen, Jason Garcia, Matthew G. Rhodes, & Francisco R. Ortega. (2024). Multimedia and Immersive Training Materials Influence Impressions of Learning But Not Learning Outcomes. 2022 Interservice/Industry Training, Simulation, and Education Conference (I/ITSEC), Paper No. 22459. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.05504](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.05504)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-experiment memory analysis across all four conditions found no differences, "F(3,160) < 1, h2p = 0.02"; equivalence was not tested.

> "an analysis of memory for the information showed no significant differences in the learning between any of the four groups (F(3,160) < 1, h2p = 0.02), including for the passive VR condition compared to the previous static image condition from Experiment 1 (Exp. 1 static versus Exp 2 VR passive: t(81) = 1.30, p > .05, d = 0.35)"
