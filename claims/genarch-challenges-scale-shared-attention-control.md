# Students reported challenges with generated-model scale fidelity, shared attention in multi-user XR, motion comfort, and control over generation

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` GenAI-generated models did not reliably encode scale, simultaneous immersion did not always produce shared attention, and students reported motion sickness and limited control or reproducibility of outputs. [→ Xiao 2026](#xiao-2026)

## Evidence

### Xiao 2026

Xiao, Y., Chen, M., Li, Y., Powers, N., Wiesenfeld, M., Smith, G., Farzin, S., Liu, S. (2026). Generative AI and Extended Reality in Collaborative Architectural Design Education: An Exploratory Studio Study. arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.13494. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13494

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interview-based thematic finding on implementation frictions. One team reported communication "didn't really work out" because members "were looking at different areas, and we weren't looking at the same thing"; students also reported motion sickness, unintentional actions during early use, and prompt history that did not allow predictable reproduction.

> "Students noted that GenAI-generated models did not reliably encode scale, making it difficult to use them directly for spatial reasoning. One student stated, "I asked it to make a 10 feet wall of 3D model, it wasn't 10 feet" (T3)."
