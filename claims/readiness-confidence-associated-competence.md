## Evidence

### Aksu Miray 2026

Aksu Miray, Varol Belgin, Özer Küçük Emine. (2026). Determination of Managerial Competence Levels and Training Requirements of New Nurse Managers for Role Transition: Empirical Research Quantitative. Nursing Open, 13(10). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70715

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis comparisons across three readiness-perception groups in the survey of 185 new nurse managers; Table 3 reports leadership KW = 30.638, p = 0.000, η² = 0.167, a large effect.

> "Statistically significant differences were found in the areas of job-related knowledge and nursing practices (p = 0.000), human resources management (p = 0.000), quality management (p = 0.000) and leadership (p = 0.000)"
