## Evidence

### O. I. González-Peña 2024

O. I. González-Peña, G. Morán-Soto, and B. M. Rodríguez-Lara. (2024). Decoding physics identity: A Spanish-language adaptation on an instrument and its correlation with STEM achievement. [doi:10.1057/s41599-024-04112-z](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-024-04112-z)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis on the second half of the Fall 2020 dataset (n=167), maximum likelihood with Satorra-Bentler correction for non-normal data. The model "showed good overall fit indices" after nine additional low-loading items were removed.

> "The CF A conducted with these 14 items showed good overall fit indices, with a Comparative Fit Index of 0.967, a Tucker Lewis Index of 0.958, and a root mean square error of approximation value of 0.056."

### O. I. González-Peña 2024 (2)

O. I. González-Peña, G. Morán-Soto, and B. M. Rodríguez-Lara. (2024). Decoding physics identity: A Spanish-language adaptation on an instrument and its correlation with STEM achievement. [doi:10.1057/s41599-024-04112-z](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-024-04112-z)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Internal-consistency testing of the 14 retained items from the validated instrument, exceeding the authors' stated 0.80 threshold for "strong internal reliability" in all four subconstructs.

> "The Cronbach's alpha values for the four subconstructs showed strong internal reliability with coefficients of α = 0.88 for recognition; α = 0.86 for competence/performance; α = 0.89 for physics interest; and α = 0.93 for science interest."
