## Evidence

### Youngwoo Bryan Yoon & Wi Hoon Jung 2025

Youngwoo Bryan Yoon & Wi Hoon Jung. (2025). Impact of impulsivity on the relationship of the brain structures with school performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00344-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain voxel-level regression of VBM gray matter volume on GPA in 153 college students (age 22.57 ± 2.77; GPA 3.93 ± 0.48), controlling for age, sex, handedness, education duration, and global GMV, with FWE-corrected p < 0.05 at cluster level. The study reports that "the GMVs of three clusters in the cerebellum were positively associated" with school performance while left lateral orbitofrontal cortex and bilateral caudate nucleus were negatively associated (Table 2, Fig. 1).

> "The GMVs of three clusters in the cerebellum were positively associated with an individual ’s level of school performance, whereas the GMVs of the left lateral orbitofrontal cortex and bilateral CN were negatively associated with an indivi- dual’s level of school performance"
