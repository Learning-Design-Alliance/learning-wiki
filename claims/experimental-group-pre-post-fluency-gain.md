## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Within the experimental group, mean fluency scores increased significantly from pre-test (9.980) to post-test (12.020). [→ Mohammadi 2018](#mohammadi-2018)

## Evidence

### Mohammadi 2018

Mohammadi, M., & Enayati, B. (2018). The Effects of Lexical Chunks Teaching on EFL Intermediate Learners’ Speaking Fluency. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 179-192. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11313a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples analysis of the experimental group (N = 30): mean rose from 9.980 to 12.020; paired difference mean -2.0400, t = -7.721, df = 29, Sig. (2-tailed) = .000, which the article notes is "less than 0.05".

> "The results of applying paired samples t -test for comparing the means of experimental group learners’ pre -test and post -test scores indicated that there were significant differences between the performance of the learners in pre -test and post -test."
