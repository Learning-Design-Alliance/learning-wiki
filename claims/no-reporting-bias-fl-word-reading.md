# No evidence of reporting bias was found for the foreign language word reading SMD meta-analysis

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Funnel plot analysis with trim and fill showed no evidence of reporting bias for the word reading SMD (p = 0.25 right side, p = 0.50 left side). [→ von Hagen 2021](#von-hagen-2021)

## Evidence

### von Hagen 2021

von Hagen, A., Kohnen, S., & Stadie, N. (2021). Foreign Language Attainment of Children/Adolescents with Poor Literacy Skills: a Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09566-6

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Reporting bias could only be investigated for the word reading SMD due to limited data, using the trim and fill method with estimator R0 in the metafor package; the test was non-significant on both sides of the funnel plot.

> "showed no evidence of reporting bias. The null hypothesis that the number of missing studies of the funnel plot was zero could not be rejected for the right (p = 0.25) or for the left side of the funnel plot (p = 0.50; Viechtbauer 2010)."
