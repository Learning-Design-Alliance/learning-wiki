## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learning flow significantly predicts learning outcomes among nursing students in distance e-learning (B = 0.72, p < .001). [→ Kim Sin-Hyang 2021](#kim-sin-hyang-2021)
`q3 i?` The indirect effect of learning flow on learning outcomes through distance e-learning satisfaction is significant (effect .51, 95% CI [.36 ~ .65]). [→ Kim Sin-Hyang 2021](#kim-sin-hyang-2021)

## Evidence

### Kim Sin-Hyang 2021

Kim Sin-Hyang, Park Sihyun. (2021). Influence of learning flow and distance e-learning satisfaction on learning outcomes and the moderated mediation effect of social-evaluative anxiety in nursing college students during the COVID-19 pandemic: A cross-sectional study. Nurse Education in Practice. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nepr.2021.103197

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 310 nursing students at two South Korean colleges, analyzed with PROCESS Macro Models 4 and 7 and bootstrapped confidence intervals. The article reports the indirect effect was ".51 (95% CI [.36 ~.65])", supporting mediation; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The bias-corrected bootstrap indicated that the mediating effect of DE-LS on the relationship between learning flow and learning outcome was.51 (95% CI [.36 ~.65]), which indicates that DE-LS significantly mediated the relationship between learning flow and learning outcome."
