## Evidence

### Rocha de Macedo Bruno 2025

Rocha de Macedo Bruno, Lima Carolina Saldanha, Haydar Ahmed, Holanda Marcelo Alcantara, Hayashi Fatima Kiyoko, Ferreira Juliana Carvalho. (2025). Impact of a Competency-based Mechanical Ventilation Course Using Virtual Simulation. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2024-0083OC

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-center cohort study of internal medicine residents; 81 completers were analyzed. A 24-item multiple-choice examination was administered at baseline and at the end of the 8-month course and compared with a paired t test, showing "1.19 ± 1.26 points (95% confidence interval [CI], 0.91–1.47; P < 0.001)". The increase is about 10% on a baseline mean of 7.26 ± 1.38 as reported in the discussion; no effect size is printed.

> "The increase in score from baseline to final test for all residents was 1.19 ± 1.26 points (95% confidence interval [CI], 0.91–1.47; P < 0.001)."
