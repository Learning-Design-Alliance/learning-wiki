Killingsworth 2015¶

Killingsworth, S. S., Clark, D. B., & Adams, D. M. (2015). Self-Explanation and Explanatory Feedback in Games: Individual Differences, Gameplay, and Learning. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology, 3(3), 162-186. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1066360

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Regression analysis with mean actions per trial as dependent variable and ANT network scores, ANT neutral RT, physics pre-test score, and game condition as predictors. A separate regression showed mean actions per trial predicted more level completions, b = .710, SEb = .103, p < .0001, with overall model R² = .423.

"Regression analyses showed a significant effect of physics pre-test score, with higher pre-test scores predicting more actions per trial, b = .096, SEb = .023, p < .001, 95% CI (.049, .142). None of the other predictors explained significant variance in the number of actions students took per trial. The overall model was significant, F(5, 76) = 3.387, p = .008, R2 = .182."
