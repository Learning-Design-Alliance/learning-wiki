Univariate and multivariate linear regression of workplace and individual factors on the three exam scores at follow-up (Table 4), with case and physical exam models adjusted by HTI. The discussion notes revised-group TMGs and those with access to diagnostic equipment scored higher on clinical cases.

> "Those students whose workplace had TB smear microscopy available still scored higher than their peers (6.27; CI: 1.35, 11.19) while those placed in Health Centre II (−3.42; CI: −6.85, 0.01) still scored lower than those placed in Health Centre I."
