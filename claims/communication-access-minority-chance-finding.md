## Evidence

### Braun 2017

Braun, D. C., Gormally, C., & Clark, M. D. (2017). The Deaf Mentoring Survey: A Community Cultural Wealth Framework for Measuring Mentoring Effectiveness with Underrepresented Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0155

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney test comparing white/European mentees (n = 51) with combined underrepresented minorities (n = 10). With 32 comparisons, the authors note about 1.6 would be expected to reach p ≤ 0.05 by chance and regard this least-significant result as disregarded.

> "We found a significant difference in Communication Access (p = 0.788, 0.269, 0.044, and 0.788) scores reported of the mentoring relationship. Figure 2 shows that Communication Access scores were higher in the minority group. We are unable to explain this significant difference."
