Regression on a composite of self-reported influence on colleagues' teaching beliefs, knowledge, and practices; Type III ANOVA confirmed effects of DBER (F(1,135)=6.54, p=0.012), mentoring (F(1,135)=6.62, p=0.011), and publications (F(1,135)=10.07, p=0.002).

> "This model (S4 Table) revealed significant positive associations between overall teaching influence and engaging in DBER (β = 0.44, p = 0.03) and generating peer-reviewed publications (β = 0.36, p = 0.01)."
