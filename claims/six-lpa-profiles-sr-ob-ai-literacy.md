# Latent profile analysis of combined SR and OB factors identifies six distinct teacher profiles spanning overestimation, underestimation, and alignment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the full sample (N = 288), a six-profile solution fit best, including an overestimation profile (high SR, lower OB; n = 43), an alignment profile (n = 40), an underestimation profile (low SR, near-average OB; n = 59), and profiles near the overall mean. [→ lpa-full](#lpa-full)

## Evidence

### lpa-full

Shan Zhang, Ruiwei Xiao, Anthony F. Botelho, Guanze Liao, Thomas K. F. Chiu, John Stamper, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2026. How to Assess AI Literacy: Misalignment Between Self-Reported and Objective-Based Measures. In LAK26: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2026). ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785088

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis on combined SR and OB factors in the full sample (N = 288) found "the six-profile solution provided the best fit". Classification quality was high (mean confidence .935, mean entropy .25), with profile sizes n = 37, 62, 40, 43, 59, and 47.

> "Model selection using both AIC and BIC indicated that the six-profile solution provided the best fit. The emergent profiles varied meaningfully in terms of both self-reported and objective-based AI literacy"
