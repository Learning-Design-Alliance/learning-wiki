q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in a comment article, citing research on desirable difficulties. The authors argue that "when AI removes the struggle and supplies ready-made solutions,it short-circuits these processes" of encoding, retrieval, and reorganization. No new empirical data are reported here.

"Research on desirable difficulties shows that struggling to encode, retrieve, and reorganize information— whether through active engagement, persistence, or adjustment— produces deeper comprehension and retention. In contrast, when AI removes the struggle and supplies ready-made solutions,it short-circuits these processes"
