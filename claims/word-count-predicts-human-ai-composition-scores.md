## Evidence

### Woo 2025

Woo, D. J., Susanto, H., Yeung, C. H., & Guo, K. (2025). Approaching the Limits to EFL Writing Enhancement with AI-generated Text and Diverse Learners. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.00367](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.00367)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple linear regression with partial correlations on all 59 student compositions, relating content, language, organization and total scores to AI and human chunk and word counts. The article reports "a value around 𝐶̃ ≈ 0.4" for word counts with p-values below 0.01, while chunk-count correlations were smaller and not statistically significant.

> "the partial correlations between all the scores, including the total scores, and the number of AI and human words were positive with a value around 𝐶̃ ≈ 0.4 and were highly statistically significant with a p-value less than 0.01."
