Evidence¶

Mutlu-Bayraktar 2018¶

Mutlu-Bayraktar, D. & Bayram, S. (2018). Effects of cueing and signaling on change blindness in multimedia learning environment. World Journal on Educational Technology: Current Issues, 11(1), 128–139. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1205386

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive eye-tracking finding from the multimedia animation study: on the scene with fewer objects, 8 of 12 participants (66.66%) detected the change, and eye movements showed participants could focus on the change more easily there.

"Eight participants (6 6.66%) more easily managed to detect the change in the screen where there were fewer objects (See Table 1)."
