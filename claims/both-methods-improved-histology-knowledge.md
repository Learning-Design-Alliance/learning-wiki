## Evidence

### Poorhassan M 2026

Poorhassan M, Hooshmandja M, Karimian Z, Seifali E. (2026). Enhancing histology education: Iranian medical students’ experience with eHistoLab web-application virtual microscopy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09829-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests within each of the two groups (50 students each) in the quasi-experimental study showed significant pre-to-post gains; no effect size is printed for the within-group gains. Pre-test scores did not differ between groups (P = 0.62).

> "The results indicated that post-test scores were significantly higher than pre-test scores in both the e-Lecturing and eHistoLab groups (P < 0.001), demonstrating that both instructional methods improved students’ knowledge."
