## Evidence

### Yu S 2026

Yu S, Lin Y and Chen C (2026) Designing for AI literacy: psychological mechanisms and self-determination. Front. Psychol. 17:1937845. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1937845

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Two-way ANOVA in a randomized 2×2 between-subjects experiment with 320 undergraduates (80 per cell). The main effect of instructional approach was significant, F(1, 316) = 50.79, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.138, with TBI (M = 9.21) higher than SBI (M = 7.69).

> "A two-way ANOV A (Ståhle and Wold, 1989) revealed a signiﬁcant main eﬀect of instructional approach on AI literacy performance, F(1, 316) = 50.79, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.138. Participants receiving TBI (M = 9.21, SD = 1.99) achieved higher performance than those receiving SBI (M = 7.69, SD = 2.05)."
