The review reports Carretero et al. (1997), in which novices and experts ranked six types of causes (political, economic, ideological, personalistic, remote, international policy) for four historical events; "non-experts attributed greater importance to personalistic causes". No effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that non-experts attributed greater importance to personalistic causes. This is in line with findings of Rivière et al. (1998) that show that personal factors are better recalled, especially in lower levels of education."
