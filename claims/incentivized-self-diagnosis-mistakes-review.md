`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Yerushalmi et al.'s studies on self-diagnosis of mistakes in physics problem-solving; the most effective intervention "incentivized them to correct their mistakes" with partial credit before solutions were provided. No effect size printed.

> "the intervention that best helped students learn from their mistakes and improve their problem-solving incentivized them to correct their mistakes. They were required to use their book and lecture notes to correct their mistakes for 50% points back on the problems before they were provided the correct solutions."
