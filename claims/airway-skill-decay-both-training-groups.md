## Evidence

### Sun Yanxia 2017

Sun Yanxia, Pan Chuxiong, Li Tianzuo, Gan Tong J. (2017). Airway management education: simulation based training versus non-simulation based training-A systematic review and meta-analyses. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-017-0313-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Skill-retention analysis of three studies evaluating BMV, DL and cricoid pressure with repeated testing 4 weeks or 8 months later. The review reports "the skills decay significantly in both SBT and NSBT groups" with no between-group difference at follow-up.

> "All of these studies showed that the skills decay significantly in both SBT and NSBT groups and the between-group differences were no longer evident at follow-up assessment."
