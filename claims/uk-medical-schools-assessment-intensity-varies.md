## Evidence

### Devine OP 2015

Devine OP, Harborne AC, McManus IC. (2015). Assessment at UK medical schools varies substantially in volume, type and intensity and correlates with postgraduate attainment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0428-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey-derived intensity index (minutes per raw mark) across the 25 programmes; the article reports "range = 0.85–2.08" and notes notable exceptions outside the 1–2 min per mark band.

> "The median intensity of assessment in our analysis was 1.24 min per mark (mean = 1.28, SD = 0.30, LQ = 1.11, UQ = 1.37, range = 0.85–2.08). The majority of schools prescribe between 1 and 2 min per mark"
