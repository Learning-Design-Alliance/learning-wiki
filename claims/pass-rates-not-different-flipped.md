# Pass rates and grade distributions in the general biology course itself are not significantly different between flipped and non-flipped sections

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Overall pass rates did not differ significantly between flipped (63.5%) and non-flipped (60.2%) sections, and grade distributions were also very similar. [→ Riedl Ann 2021](#riedl-ann-2021)

## Evidence

### Riedl Ann 2021

Riedl Ann, Yeung Fan, Burke Tina. (2021). Implementation of a Flipped Active-Learning Approach in a Community College General Biology Course Improves Student Performance in Subsequent Biology Courses and Increases Graduation Rate. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-07-0156

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of registrar-recorded pass rates (C or better) for 378 flipped and 410 non-flipped students over two semesters. The article reports "63.5% of students from flipped sections passed" versus 60.2%, p = 0.170; equivalence was not tested.

> "The overall pass rates for flipped and non-flipped sections were not significantly different: 63.5% of students from flipped sections passed the course with an “A”, “B”, or “C” grade, compared with 60.2% of students from non-flipped sections (p = 0.170; Supplemental Table S2)."
