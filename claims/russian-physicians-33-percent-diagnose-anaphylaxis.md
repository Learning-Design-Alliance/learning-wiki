## Evidence

### Munblit Daniel 2017

Munblit Daniel, Treneva Marina, Korsunskiy Ilya, Asmanov Alan, Pampura Alexander, Warner John O. (2017). A national survey of Russian physicians' knowledge of diagnosis and management of food-induced anaphylaxis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-015901

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey (VrachiRF.ru, February–March 2016) of 299 Russian physicians after excluding 16 allergy-immunology specialists; the case-based scenario described cutaneous, cardiorespiratory, gastrointestinal and generalised symptoms. The article reports "Thirty-three per cent of surveyed physicians diagnosed the presented patient with anaphylaxis" (table 2 prints 99 of 299).
