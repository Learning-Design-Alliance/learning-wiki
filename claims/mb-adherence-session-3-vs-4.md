## Evidence

### Diebold 2020

Diebold, A., Ciolino, J. D., Johnson, J. K., Yeh, C., Gollan, J. K., & Tandon, S. D. (2020). Comparing Fidelity Outcomes of Paraprofessional and Professional Delivery of a Perinatal Depression Preventive Intervention. Administration and Policy in Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10488-020-01022-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model analysis of mean adherence across the six MB sessions in the 160-session fidelity dataset. The session effect was significant (p = 0.009); the Tukey-adjusted Session #3 vs. Session #4 comparison had a mean difference of 10.0 (95% CI 0.3–19.7; adjusted p = 0.040).

> "there was a significant session effect on adherence (p = 0.009, see Fig. 2). After adjusting for multiple hypothesis tests, just one of the pairwise comparisons remained significant: Session #3"
