# Knowledge tracing (KT) is a sequential student model technique, whereas NIDA is a non-sequential technique, and the technique used indicates the model's type.

## Evidence

### Valdés Aguirre 2016

Valdés Aguirre, B., Ramírez Uresti, J. A., & du Boulay, B. (2016). An Analysis of Student Model Portability. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0113-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the article's synthesis of prior classifications, the authors assembled a table of skill-modelling techniques and state they "use this table to infer the type of model from the technique used in an ITS", giving KT (sequential) versus NIDA (non-sequential) as the example contrast.

> "an ITS that uses knowledge tracing (KT) will use a sequential model, whereas an ITS that uses "noise input deterministic and" (NIDA) will use a non-sequential model"
