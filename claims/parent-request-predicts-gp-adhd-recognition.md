## Evidence

### Tatlow-Golden M 2016

Tatlow-Golden M, Prihodova L, Gavin B, Cullen W, McNicholas F. (2016). What do general practitioners know about ADHD? Attitudes and knowledge among first-contact gatekeepers: systematic narrative review. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-016-0516-x

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Community-based study in which parents and teachers of children aged 5–11 registered with 10 London GP practices completed the SDQ; predictors of GP recognition were analysed. The review reports these odds ratios; where a GP recognised hyperactivity, this always led to onward referral. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Factors that increased the likelihood of GP recognition, and subsequent referral, included parental recognition or referral request (OR 20.83, 95 % CI 3.05–142.08), and comorbid behaviour problems (OR 1.48, 95 % CI 1.04–2.12)."
