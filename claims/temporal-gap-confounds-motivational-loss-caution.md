Impact of Cognitive-based Instructional Intervention on Learning Motivation: The Implementation of Student-made Glossary in a Programming-oriented Engineering Problem-solving Course and Its Impact on Learning Motivation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED485015

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion: the pre-motivational survey was administered in the first quarter of the semester while the glossary was not in place until the last quarter, and the study ran simultaneously with course progression, confounding the one-group pre/post result.
