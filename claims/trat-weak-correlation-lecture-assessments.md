## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Team readiness assurance test scores exhibited predominantly weak correlations with lecture assessment scores, with statistical significance observed only for TRAT 1 and TRAT 5 with the final exam and TRAT 1 with Exam 1. [→ Bell 2022](#bell-2022)

## Evidence

### Bell 2022

Bell, E. C. (2022). Individual Vs. team based readiness assurance testing in pharmacy calculations. International Journal of Instruction, 15(1), 733-746. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2022.15142a

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis of the same 69-student cohort's TRAT scores against lecture assessments. The study reports "predominantly weak ( -.2 < r < .3) correlations"; Table 1 shows significance only for TRAT 1 with Exam 1 (p < .05) and Final Exam (p < .005), and TRAT 5 with Final Exam (p < .05). No single effect size is printed for the overall pattern.

> "In contrast, team readiness assurance tests exhibited predominantly weak ( -.2 < r < .3) correlations with lecture assessment scores."
