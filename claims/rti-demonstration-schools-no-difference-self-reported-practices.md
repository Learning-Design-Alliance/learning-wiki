### Hall and Mahoney 2013

Carol Hall, Jamie Mahoney. (2013). Response To Intervention: Research And Practice. Contemporary Issues In Education Research, 6(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1073194

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental survey study using ANOVA with 54 general education elementary teachers (32 demonstration, 22 comparable schools) in a large Florida district. Descriptives for RQ 1 show demonstration schools mean 59.59 (SD 18.12) versus comparable schools mean 63.55 (SD 11.96); the article reports "no differences" between groups.

> "Teachers surveyed at the selected demonstration schools and teachers surveyed at comparable sites showed no differences in their self -reported practices in implementing the RTI process."
