Recent Studies in Handwriting 1912¶

Recent studies in handwriting [Unsigned review]. (1912). Journal of Educational Psychology, 3(1), 41–46. doi:10.1037/h0068789

q2 · i? · review · r2

Downey's preliminary study of family resemblance in handwriting, summarized by the reviewer: resemblances among relatives' handwriting are attributed to family relationship since training and environment may differ, with strong indication of definite handwriting types appearing in families. No effect size is printed.

"These resemblances can be explained only on the basis of family relationship, for all other factors, as training, environment, etc.. may be different. The author says further that there is strong indication of the apjteavance in families of definite handwriting types"
