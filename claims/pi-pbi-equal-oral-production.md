## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Adem Soruç 2017

Adem Soruç, Jingjing Qin, YouJin Kim. (2017). Comparing the Effectiveness of Processing Instruction and Production-Based Instruction on L2 Grammar Learning: The Role of Explicit Information. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(2), 49–70. http://dx.doi.org/1018806/tesl.v34i2.1262

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons following a 5 × 3 repeated measures ANOVA on oral production scores. The article reports all four instructional groups beat control (e.g., PI-EI vs control Mdiff = 3.40, p = .001) and "no statistically significant differences among the instructional groups".

> "There were no statistically significant differences among the instructional groups on the oral production task."
