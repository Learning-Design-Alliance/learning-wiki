### Innes SI 2018

Innes SI, Leboeuf-Yde C, Walker BF. (2018). How frequent are non-evidence-based health care beliefs in chiropractic students and do they vary across the pre-professional educational years. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-018-0178-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey analysis by year of study (Table 2). The article reports "This gradual increase was generally linear as the proportions tended to be incrementally higher in each successive year, the only exception being for musculoskeletal conditions."

> "There was no overlapping of the 95% CI for the ‘often, quite often’ response when comparing the estimates for the 1st year students to the combined estimates for 4th and 5th year students for ‘prevention of stress’, ‘cardiovascular disease’ and ‘diabetes’."
