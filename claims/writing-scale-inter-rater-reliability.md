### Khatib and Meihami 2015

Mohammad Khatib, Hussein Meihami. (2015). Languaging and Writing Skill: The Effect of Collaborative Writing on EFL Students' Writing Performance. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.1p.203

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Scoring phase of the same study: two assessors rated pretest and posttest essays with the Jacobs et al. rating scale after training on two practice essays. The article prints inter-assessor correlations of .84 (pretest) and .88 (posttest).

> "Again, the two instructors were in charge of assessing the essays with the help of the described writing scale. Once again and due to the purpose of being sure about the inter-reliability of the scores another correlation between the scores gave by the both teachers was run. The correlation between the scores was .88."
