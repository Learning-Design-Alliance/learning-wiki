## Evidence

### Gürgil 2018

Fitnat Gürgil. (2018). The Effect of Authentic Learning Approach in Social Studies Teaching on the Academic Success. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2061-2068. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061002

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed between-within ANOVA on the 92 experimental-group students across low, medium and high SES schools, with alpha set to .01 because the Levene test for posttest variances was significant (p = .032). The article concludes authentic learning "has a similar effect on the academic success of students at all SES levels".

> "showed that there was no significant interaction between socioeconomic status (SES) variable and time, Wilks' Lambda = .97, F (2,89) = 1.49, p = .231, η2 =.032."

### Gürgil 2018 (2)

Fitnat Gürgil. (2018). The Effect of Authentic Learning Approach in Social Studies Teaching on the Academic Success. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2061-2068. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061002

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The SES main effect in the same ANOVA was non-significant; the article notes only 2% of achievement-score variance is explained by SES. Equivalence between SES groups was not directly tested.

> "F (2,89)  = .89, p = .415, η2 = .020."
