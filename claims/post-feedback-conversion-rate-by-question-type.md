Attempt-level analysis of the 79-student field study logs computing the proportion of initially incorrect responses corrected on the subsequent attempt. The overall rate was "36.7% (=260/708)"; question-type rates (Table 7) were 55.1% closed-ended, 35.6% CGT, 33.9% open-ended.
