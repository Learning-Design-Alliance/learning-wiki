## Evidence

### Çifci İ. 2025

Çifci İ., Gök U., Yorgancıoğlu A. (2025). A current assessment of family physicians’ knowledge and attitudes toward asthma management in primary care in Turkey according to the GINA strategy report. NPJ Primary Care Respiratory Medicine 36, 5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41533-025-00468-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Knowledge items in the cross-sectional survey of 596 physicians: "94.1%" knew the first-step non-pharmacological interventions; 82.6% answered comorbidity items correctly; the discussion also cites 91.4% correct on patient-device compatibility, a slight inconsistency with the 94.1% printed in results.

> "The non-pharmacological interventions that should be performed as the first step in asthma treatment, such as patient education, management of comorbidities, smoking cessation, exercise recommendations, weight loss, and vaccination, were known by 94.1% of physicians."
