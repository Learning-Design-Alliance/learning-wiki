The article reports, citing Fabiano et al. (2015), a review of 12 meta-analyses of ADHD psychosocial treatment that found low overlap in included studies; the review attributes the low overlap figures to Zwi et al. (2011) and Fabiano et al. (2009). The review's design is not described in detail here.

> "when all the studies were recorded across the meta-analyses, overlap was low for the inclusion of all possible studies, ranging from 2% (Zwi et al., 2011) to 46% (Fabiano et al., 2009)."
