### Bruce Thompson 2006

Bruce Thompson. (2006). Evaluating Three Programs Using a School Effectiveness Model: Direct Instruction, Target Teach, and Class Size Reduction. Third Education Group Review, Vol. 2, No. 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ874476

Simulation study (more than 100 runs) modeling reported school scores as varying randomly about true scores, estimating that five of the 34 lowest-rated schools were there only by chance, inflating apparent annual improvement by .03. The author calls this a worst-case scenario.
