### Senf B  2019

Senf B , Fettel J , Demmerle C , Maiwurm P . (2019). Physicians' attitudes towards psycho‐oncology, perceived barriers, and psychosocial competencies: Indicators of successful implementation of adjunctive psycho‐oncological care? Psycho‐Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1002/pon.4962

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive self-ratings from the survey of 102 physicians. The article reports physicians estimated "their psychosocial education and knowledge as being moderate (M = 3.62, SD = 1.02)" while agreeing with skills and abilities items. No effect sizes are printed.

> "On average, physicians estimated their psychosocial education and knowledge as being moderate (M = 3.62, SD = 1.02). On the other hand, participants mostly agreed with items concerning their psychosocial skills and abilities (M = 4.40, SD = 0.70)."
