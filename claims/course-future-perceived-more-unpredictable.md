## Evidence

### Tapio Rasa 2022

Tapio Rasa, Elina Palmgren, Antti Laherto. (2022). Futurising science education: students' experiences from a course on futures thinking and quantum computing. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09572-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Self-reported change quoted from student Nora in the phenomenographic interview analysis; the theme 'Future perceived as more unpredictable' was identified in 24% of the 21 interviews. The authors attribute the feeling to the course's multiple-scenario activities.

> "It's like I feel that I know even less, I mean in a good way, not like I felt before like I really know, but now I'm even more like, anything can happen."
