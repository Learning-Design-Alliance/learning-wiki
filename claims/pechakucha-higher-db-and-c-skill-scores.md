## Evidence

### Cevheroğlu Seda 2025

Cevheroğlu Seda, Fırat Kılıç Hülya, Budak Vildan. (2025). The effect of the PechaKucha presentation method on nursing students’ deep breathing and coughing exercise skills and their satisfaction with the educational method: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08258-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which 96 second-year nursing students were simple randomly assigned to PechaKucha (n=48) or traditional PowerPoint (n=48) DB&C training, with blinded checklist scoring. The intervention group scored "47.25 ± 10.11" versus "36.48 ± 8.62" (p = 0.000, t = 3.850, Cohen's d = 1.320).

> "The mean scores on the checklist, measured two days after the intervention, were 47.25 ± 10.11 for the intervention group and 36.48 ± 8.62 for the control group (p = 0.000, t = 3.850, Cohen’s d = 1.320, 95% confidence interval)."
