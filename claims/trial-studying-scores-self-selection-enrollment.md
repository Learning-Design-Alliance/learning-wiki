## Evidence

### Niessen A. Susan M. 2016

Niessen A. Susan M., Meijer Rob R., Tendeiro Jorge N. (2016). Predicting Performance in Higher Education Using Proximal Predictors. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0153663

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of all 851 applicants predicting enrollment from the three test scores, controlling for receiving a discouraging phone call. The model was significant (Model X2, p < .01, Table 5). The odds ratio is an unstandardized logistic coefficient, so no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The trial-studying score was the only significant predictor in the model, showing a small effect. A one-unit increase in the trial-studying score increases the odds of enrolling by a factor of 1.02 to 1.09"
