## Evidence

### Chrispeels H. E. 2014

Chrispeels H. E., Klosterman M. L., Martin J. B., Lundy S. R., Watkins J. M., Gibson C. L., Muday G. K. (2014). Undergraduates Achieve Learning Gains in Plant Genetics through Peer Teaching of Secondary Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-01-0007

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control analysis on the 2012 final exam comparing four questions on topics learned in both lecture and non-SLP labs (hypothesis generation, cell structure, ecology) with the 70 lecture-only questions. The article reports no significant differences, suggesting higher SLP-related scores were a result of SLP-related activities and not just repeated exposure.

> "As shown in Figure 4C, we found no significant differences, as determined by an exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test, between scores of lab and lecture questions compared with scores on lecture-only questions for both MS undergraduates and HS undergraduates."
