Rosenshine's tribute attributes the origin of the phrase to Brophy's reading of raw correlational results from the process-outcome studies; Rosenshine says he helped spread the idea but that the original idea came from Brophy.

> "It was Jere who looked at the raw correlational results and then developed the phrase "teaching in small steps " to describe the instructional behaviors of those teachers whose students had a high proportion of correct responses."
