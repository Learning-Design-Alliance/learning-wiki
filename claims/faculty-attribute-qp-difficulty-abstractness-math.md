### Bayram Akarsu 2010

Bayram Akarsu. (2010). An extensive Study of Teaching / Learning Quantum Mechanics in College. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1002.4975](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1002.4975)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured hour-long audiotaped interviews with five physics faculty members at Midwestern universities, analyzed by the constant comparative method. The article reports this attribution pattern from the interview themes; no effect size is printed.

> "All of the faculty members, except David, supported the idea of abstract nature of quantum mechanics as one of the reasons that makes it more difficult and less understandable by the students."

### Bayram Akarsu 2010 (2)

Bayram Akarsu. (2010). An extensive Study of Teaching / Learning Quantum Mechanics in College. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1002.4975](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1002.4975)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

From the same faculty interview analysis, the article reports that only three of the five professors named heavy mathematical demands as a cause of difficulty, showing the attribution was not unanimous across the interviewed faculty.

> "For the second part, only three of them, Albert, Brian, and David, believed that heavy mathematical thinking and tools make quantum physics much harder for the students."
