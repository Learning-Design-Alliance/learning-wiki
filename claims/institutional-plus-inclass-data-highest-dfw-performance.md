## Evidence

### Jie Yang 2020

Jie Yang, Seth DeVore, Dona Hewagallage, Paul Miller, Qing X. Ryan, and John Stewart. (2020). Using Machine Learning to Identify the Most At-Risk Students in Physics Classes. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weekly classification models on Sample 2 (N = 1683, fall 2016–spring 2019, single stable instructional design) comparing combined versus institutional-only variable sets. The article reports the combined models "gave the highest model performance" with the stated weekly improvements.

> "The combination of institutional and in-class variables gave the highest model performance with an improvement of 3% in week 1, 6% in week 2, 9% in week 5 (when test 1 grades were available), and 18% in week 8 (when test 2 grades were available) compared to the model containing only institutional variables."
