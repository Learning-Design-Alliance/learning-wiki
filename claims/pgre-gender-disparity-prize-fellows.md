### Emily M. Levesque 2015

Emily M. Levesque, Rachel Bezanson, & Grant R. Tremblay. (2015). Physics GRE Scores of Prize Postdoctoral Fellows in Astronomy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 149 respondents with reported PGRE scores (75 male, 53 female, 21 other/not specified). The actual score histogram by gender is compared against an expected distribution assuming no gender disparity; the observed distributions differ notably.

> "The distribution of scores is also notably diﬀerent for male and female fellows; ∼42% of female fellows fall below the 50th percentile. The results show a clear disparity in gender performance on the Physics GRE among prize fellows."
