## Evidence

### Stanovich 1997

Keith E. Stanovich and Richard F. West. (1997). Reasoning Independently of Prior Belief and Individual Differences in Actively Open-Minded Thinking. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.89.2.342

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 1 group comparisons in the AET study (df = 346 for the Thinking Dispositions Scales). The article reports the AOT composite difference was "highly significant (p \< .001)" and that the HIARG group "consistently displayed more openmindedness, cognitive flexibility, and skepticism, and less dogmatism and cognitive rigidity."

> "The HIARG group scored significantly higher on the Flexible Thinking scale, Openmindedness- Ideas, and Openmindedness-Values scales and significantly lower on the Absolutism, Dogmatism, and Categorical Thinking scales."

### Stanovich 1997 (2)

Keith E. Stanovich and Richard F. West. (1997). Reasoning Independently of Prior Belief and Individual Differences in Actively Open-Minded Thinking. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.89.2.342

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary disposition measures in the same Table 1 comparison. The article notes the groups did not differ on social desirability, so the response patterns were "not due to a tendency for the HIARG group to give more socially desirable responses."

> "The HIARG group also displayed significantly lower scores on the Superstitious Thinking scale, significantly higher scores on the two-item Counterfactual Thinking scale, and showed significantly less out- come bias."
