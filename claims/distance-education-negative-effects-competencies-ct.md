## Evidence

### Görgülü Deniz 2026

Görgülü Deniz, Cömert Melike, Demir Mustafa, Şahin Çiğdem. (2026). The role of the 2023 earthquakes in the competencies and critical thinking skills of teacher candidates in Türkiye. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05006-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews with 37 teacher candidates on distance education's impact on competencies. The article reports Negative Effects (f = 34) led by inefficiency (f = 15) and inadequacies in practice (f = 5); within Positive Effects, learning educational technologies was most cited (f = 4).

> "Figure 2 shows that the most prevalent theme in teacher candidates' perspectives on the impact of distance education on personal and professional competencies was Negative Effects (f = 34), followed by Positive Effects (f = 16)."

### Görgülü Deniz 2026 (2)

Görgülü Deniz, Cömert Melike, Demir Mustafa, Şahin Çiğdem. (2026). The role of the 2023 earthquakes in the competencies and critical thinking skills of teacher candidates in Türkiye. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05006-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of interviews on distance education's impact on critical thinking. The article reports predominantly negative perception (f = 24), with ineffective implementation most cited (f = 7), followed by lack of communication, regression, and being less effective than face-to-face education (f = 3 each).

> "Figure 4 shows that the impact of distance education on critical thinking skills was perceived predominantly negatively (f = 24), followed by Neutral Views (f = 8) and Positive Effects (f = 11)."
