Evidence¶

Liborius 2019¶

Liborius, P., Bellhäuser, H., & Schmitz, B. (2019). What makes a good study day? An intraindividual study on university students' time investment by means of time-series analyses. Learning and Instruction, 60, 310-321. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.10.006

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel analysis (days within persons) of the 105-student diary study testing Hypothesis 1, controlling for intended time and student-level variables. The article reports both time variables "positively predicted students' daily study satisfaction" with the printed coefficients, and a random-slope model fit better (χ²(5) = 499.00, p < .05).

"Both positively predicted students' daily study satisfaction (b lecture time = 0.11; b independent study time = 0.19)."
