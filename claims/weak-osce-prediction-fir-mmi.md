## Evidence

### Klusmann Dietrich 2023

Klusmann Dietrich, Knorr Mirjana, Hampe Wolfgang. (2023). Exploring the relationships between first impressions and MMI ratings: a pilot study. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 28(2), 519-536. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10151-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression using mean ratings from two communication-skill OSCE stations taken 18 months after admission as criterion, with FIR and MMI as predictors; the model "yielded a significant prediction effect (p(F) = 0.009) of small size (8.4% explained variance)". Coefficients were representative of eight permutation runs.

> "A regression model (Table 3, model 3) with OSCE-performance as the criterion yielded a significant prediction effect (p(F) = 0.009) of small size (8.4% explained variance)."
