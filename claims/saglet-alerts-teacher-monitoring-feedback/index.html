Nancy Law 2018¶

Nancy Law, Carolyn P. Rose, Ulrike Cress & Sten Ludvigsen. (2018). Different technologies, methodologies and epistemologies—is CSCL a community or communities? Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9280-3

Study by Schwarz, Prusak, Swidan, Livny, Gal and Segal of the SAGLET orchestration environment, which uses fine-grained, near real-time data and computational analysis to support collaboration in small-group mathematics learning.

"a technology system that can detect "critical moments" in a group 's progress to provide alerts to the teacher. Schwarz et al. 's study shows that the teacher partner in the study was able to use the system to monitor and give appropriate feedback"
