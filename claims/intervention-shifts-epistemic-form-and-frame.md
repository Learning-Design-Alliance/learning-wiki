# Interviewer intervention targeting epistemic form can shift a student's epistemological frame

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` An interviewer's prompt about comparing positions shifted Luke's epistemic form from Reasoning to Number, producing a shift from a Conceptual Physics to an Algorithmic Physics frame. [→ Kelli Shar 2020](#kelli-shar-2020)

## Evidence

### Kelli Shar 2020

Kelli Shar, Rosemary S. Russ, James T. Laverty. (2020). Student Epistemological Framing on Paper-Based Assessments. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020101)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Single case study of student Luke on the Car problem during a think-aloud interview. After the interviewer noted the question asked for validation of positions, Luke shifted to substituting givens into a physics equation. The authors interpret "the intervention at the level of Epistemic Form is what caused Luke to shift."

> "We argue that the intervention at the level of Epistemic Form is what caused Luke to shift from a Conceptual Physics to an Algorithmic Physics frame, and thus a new set of resources."
