## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Introductory life-science students with a calculus prerequisite and good grades had serious trouble knowing how to start on a two-drag-equation problem, while handling analogous pure-math problems easily. [→ Edward F. Redish 2015](#edward-f-redish-2015)

## Evidence

### Edward F. Redish 2015

Edward F. Redish. (2015). Analysing the Competency of Mathematical Modelling in Physics. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-44887-9_3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-44887-9_3)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative observation from a "work together" problem in a large lecture for life scientists, where the author facilitated and recorded a stuck group's dialogue; one student realized writing Av = Bv2 made it easy. The article reports no effect size.

> "I have seen many introductory students having serious trouble with the multi-parameter equations in physics"
