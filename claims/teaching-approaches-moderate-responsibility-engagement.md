# Teaching approaches moderate the responsibility–engagement link: CCSF amplifies it while ITTF dampens it

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The cross-level interaction between responsibility and CCSF is positive and significant, and the interaction between responsibility and ITTF is negative and significant. [→ Hao Xia-ying 2025](#hao-xia-ying-2025)

## Evidence

### Hao Xia-ying 2025

Hao Xia-ying, He Yun, Zhang Dong-xu, Li Xian-yin. (2025). Teacher-focused approach to foster student engagement — a hierarchical linear model based on Chinese college students’ psychological capital. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03683-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Full HLM with cross-level interaction terms; the article reports the printed γ51 and γ52 coefficients and concludes "CCSF approach has positive moderating effect between responsibility and student engagement" while ITTF negatively moderates it. Interactions for SEF, RS, and OP were not significant.

> "the coefficient for the interaction between responsibility and the CCSF approach(γ51) is 0.07 (SE = 0.03, t = 2.02, p < 0.05), while the coefficient for the interaction between responsibility and the ITTF approach(γ52) is −0.08 (SE = 0.03, t=−2.36, p < 0.05)"
