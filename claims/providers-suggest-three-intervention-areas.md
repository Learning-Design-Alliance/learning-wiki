### Lambert R. Frederick 2020

Lambert R. Frederick, Yu Amy, Orrell Catherine, Haberer Jessica E. (2020). Perceived oral health interventions by medical providers in Gugulethu, South Africa. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233437

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of the 20 interviews identified intervention suggestions as a third content area alongside oral health knowledge and patient care; participants "independently suggested interventions in three broad areas" without prompting. Thematic saturation was reported for the sample.

> "Finally, participants independently suggested interventions in three broad areas: (1) education, (2) expanded provider roles, and (3) colocation of services."
