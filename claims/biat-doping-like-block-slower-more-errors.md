# The BIAT experimental effect was induced: reaction times and error rates were higher in the doping-like (incongruent) block than the doping-dislike block

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Mean reaction times (η2 = 0.36) and error rates (η2 = 0.34) in the doping-like block were significantly higher than in the doping-dislike block. [→ Deng Zhangyan 2022](#deng-zhangyan-2022)

## Evidence

### Deng Zhangyan 2022

Deng Zhangyan, Guo Jinyang, Wang Dong, Huang Tao, Chen Zuosong. (2022). Effectiveness of the world anti-doping agency's e-learning programme for anti-doping education on knowledge of, explicit and implicit attitudes towards, and likelihood of doping among Chinese college athletes and non-athletes. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13011-022-00459-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on behavioural BIAT performance in the 64-participant study, confirming the experimental effect induced by different semantic associations between concepts and attitude (doping + like vs. doping + dislike); reaction time η2 = 0.36 and error rate η2 = 0.34 as printed.

> "the mean reaction time (F(1, 60) = 33.30, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.36) and error rate (F(1, 60) = 30.82, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.34) in the doping-like block were higher than those in the doping-dislike block."
