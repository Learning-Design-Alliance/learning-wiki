### Haggerty 1918

Haggerty, M. E. (1918). Tests of applicants for admission to University of Minnesota Medical School. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 9*(5), 278–286. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0071405

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of test correlations with pre-medical honor-point averages (S. L. A. marks, 68 cases) against first-semester medical marks. The article states "either test alone is a better prognostic measure than were the S. L. A. marks," with the printed correlations .62, .60, .65 versus .49.

> "In fact either test alone is a better prognostic measure than were the S. L. A. marks. The "quality of reading" correlates with medical marks to the extent of .62, the omnibus test to .60 and the two combined to .65, while the S. L. A. marks show but .49."
