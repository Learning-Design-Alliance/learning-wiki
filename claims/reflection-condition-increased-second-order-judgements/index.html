Evidence¶

Nederhand 2020¶

Nederhand, M. L., Tabbers, H. K., Jongerling, J., & Rikers, R. M. J. P. (2020). Reflection on exam grades to improve calibration of secondary school students: a longitudinal study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09233-9

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel analysis of second-order judgements (confidence in the estimate, 1–5 scale) over eight French exams. The article reports a statistically detectable group × time interaction ("b = 0.06, t(212) = 2.12, p = .035") favouring increasing confidence only in the Reflection group.

"students in the Reflection group differed statistically detectable from students in the Practice and Grade comparison groups, b = 0.06, t(212) = 2.12, p = .035. Students in the reflection group became more confident during the school year, whereas this change was not found for the other two groups."
