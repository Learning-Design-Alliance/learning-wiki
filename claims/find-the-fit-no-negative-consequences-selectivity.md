# Find the Fit's selectivity shift came with no higher out-of-pocket cost and no increase in dropping out or transferring down

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2`–`r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no significant difference in likely out-of-pocket net price ($10,183 Find the Fit vs. $9,587 regular advising), though sticker price was higher ($25,925 vs. $24,109). [→ Linkow 2021](#linkow-2021)
`q3 i?` Find the Fit students were no more likely than regular advising students to transfer to a less selective college or drop out by the start of the second year. [→ Linkow 2021 (2)](#linkow-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Linkow 2021

Linkow, T., Parsad, A., Martinez, A., & Miller, H. (2021). Study of Enhanced College Advising in Upward Bound: Impacts on Where and How Long Students Attend College (NCEE 2021-2022-002r). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RCT cost analysis (Exhibit 10) using IPEDS-based sticker and net prices for students from households with incomes below $30,000. Sticker price was significantly higher for Find the Fit colleges, but net price did not differ significantly.

> "there was no significant difference in the likely out-of-pocket cost for students (net price for students from low-income families): the average net price was $10,183 for students in Find the Fit projects versus $9,587 for students in regular UB advising projects"

### Linkow 2021 (2)

Linkow, T., Parsad, A., Martinez, A., & Miller, H. (2021). Study of Enhanced College Advising in Upward Bound: Impacts on Where and How Long Students Attend College (NCEE 2021-2022-002r). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

RCT analysis of dropout and downward transfer by October 1, 2017 (Exhibit 11; 2,336 vs. 2,107 students), addressing advisors' prior overmatch concerns. No significant differences were found.

> "Students in Find the Fit projects were no more likely than their counterparts in regular UB advising projects to transfer to a less selective college or drop out of college by the start of the second year (Exhibit 11;"
