## Evidence

### Vinesh Rajpaul 2014

Vinesh Rajpaul, Saalih Allie, Sarah-Louise Blyth. (2014). The introductory astronomy course at the University of Cape Town: probing student perspectives. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Free-association task administered pre-course to 79 and post-course to 91 AST1000F students; responses were spelling-corrected and equivalent words grouped before rank comparison. The article reports that words rising in prevalence, such as "spectroscopy", "dark energy", "radiation", were technical course concepts.

> "The words which feature more prominently post-course than pre-course (“spectroscopy”, “dark energy”, “radiation”, and so on) are largely more technical concepts which were taught in the course, suggesting a shift from a popular view of astronomy to a more technical one."
