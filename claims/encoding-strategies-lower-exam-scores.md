`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Final OLS regression (Table 4) of total midterm scores on Week 10 strategy-time percentages with demographic and GPA controls. "increased use of four study strategies were associated with slightly lower exam scores", with flashcards showing the largest negative coefficient (b = −0.55, p < 0.001).

> "we found that increased use of four study strategies were associated with slightly lower exam scores: summarizing notes (bsummarizing = −0.13, p = 0.001), listening to friends explain the content (blistening = −0.18, p = 0.001), using flashcards (bflashcards = −0.55, p < 0.001), and re-writing notes from memory (bre-writing notes = −0.18, p = 0.032)."
