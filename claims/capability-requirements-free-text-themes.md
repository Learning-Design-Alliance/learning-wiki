`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive qualitative content analysis of 57 free-text comments from the cross-sectional survey, coded into main and sub-categories and two overall themes. The article reports that "The NEWS and proACT training were perceived to provide structure and confidence" and that overcrowding, insufficient staffing, and insufficient physician access sometimes hindered optimal monitoring.

> "The NEWS and proACT training were perceived to provide structure and confidence, facilitate clear oral and written communication, and increase patient safety"
