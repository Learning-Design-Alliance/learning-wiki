`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two microgenetic case studies of students with cognitive disabilities (Fred and Ali) were analyzed through collaborative microgenetic analysis of audio-video data, showing "conceptual advantages enjoyed by participants in both groups" who coordinated perspectives.

> "Microgenetic case studies indicate conceptual advantages enjoyed by participants in both groups who successfully coordinated egocentric proximal actions (walking a floor-based number line) with perception of distal semiotic enactment (of an avatar mirror-walking their action on its own number line)"
