## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Overall video-assessor questionnaire ratings favored physicians (p = .043), driven mainly by technical-implementation and competence items. [→ Krautter Markus 2017](#krautter-markus-2017)

## Evidence

### Krautter Markus 2017

Krautter Markus, Diefenbacher Katja, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Maatouk Imad, Herrmann-Werner Anne, Koehl-Hackert Nadja, Herzog Wolfgang, Nikendei Christoph. (2017). Physical examination skills training: Faculty staff vs. patient instructor feedback—A controlled trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0180308

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire assessment by two independent video raters (12 items, Likert 1–6) in the same RCT; overall means 2.51 (physicians) vs 3.18 (PIs), p = .043. Individual item p-values as printed in Table 2.

> "Concerning overall ratings by video assessors, physician feedback was significantly better rated than PI feedback (p = .043). There was no statistically significant difference concerning four items (see Table 2)."
