# A general-purpose LLM used as a tutor without pedagogical prompting overwhelmingly provides high assistance, with more than 95% of responses classified as Explaining or Solving

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In an introductory AI course, more than 95% of the LLM's 14,637 responses were classified as Explaining (40.3%) or Solving (55.2%), with Prompting, Hinting and Minimal together under 5%. [→ Suhyeon Lee 2026](#suhyeon-lee-2026)

## Evidence

### Suhyeon Lee 2026

Suhyeon Lee, Juneha Baek, Jaehyeong Park, Donghyuk Shin. (2026). LLM Pedagogical Behavior in AI Tutoring Interactions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22993 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.22993](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.22993)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 14,637 paired turns from 203 students using a GPT-4o-mini assistant with the system prompt "You are a helpful assistant." Responses were LLM-classified on the five-level scaffolding scale; the mean level was 3.43 (SD = 0.84).

> "More than half of responses provide a complete, task-specific solution, and another 40.3% provide detailed explanations. Prompting, Hinting, and Minimal responses together account for fewer than 5% of responses."
