Evidence¶

Amy E. Barth 2016¶

Amy E. Barth, Sharon Vaughn, Philip Capin, Eunsoo Cho, Stephanie Stillman-Spisak, Leticia Martinez, and Heather Kincaid. (2016). Effects of a Text-processing Comprehension Intervention on Struggling Middle School Readers. Top Lang Disord. 2016; 36(4): 368–389. https://doi.org/10.1097/TLD.0000000000000101

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Secondary regression analysis of dosage, with mean total instruction of 17.3 hours (SD = 2.6, Range = 8 - 21 hours) recorded via attendance records. The article reports dosage was not associated with end of treatment performance; no effect size is printed.

"regression models examining whether the number of treatment sessions predicted outcomes controlling for pretest indicated that dosage was not associated with end of treatment performance"
