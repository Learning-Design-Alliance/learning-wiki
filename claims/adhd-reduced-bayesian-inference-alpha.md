## Evidence

### Gabay 2025

Gabay, Y., Jacob, L., Mansour, A. & Hertz, U. (2025). Computational markers show specific deficits for dyslexia and ADHD in complex learning settings. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00323-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Computational modeling of trial-by-trial choices with a hybrid reinforcement learning/Bayes model in the ADHD comparison; the alpha parameter was lower in the ADHD group on the No-Explicit-Assist task. The article reports the alpha contrast as significant at p = 0.008 in Table 1 (control 0.23 vs ADHD 0.12); no standardized effect size is printed, so no magnitude is asserted.

> "On the Explicit-No-Assisttask, the difference between the two groups was in the best-ﬁt value of the free parameter alpha, which was lower for the ADHD group. This parameter reﬂects the balance between the contributions of reinforcemen t learning and Bayesian learning processes, with lower values indic ating greater reliance on the rein forcement learning mechanism."
