## Evidence

### Acosta Tatiana 2020

Acosta Tatiana, Sutton Jill Marie, Dotters-Katz Sarah. (2020). Improving Learners' Comfort With Cesarean Sections Through the Use of High-Fidelity, Low-Cost Simulation. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10878

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey of 21 learners: model rating improved 0.6 (0.1 to 1.0), p = .01 for all participants, and 0.7 (0.0 to 1.4), p = .02 for third-year medical students. The article states the final rating "statistically significantly surpassed their expectation."

> "As noted in Tables 1 and 2, all participants had a very good expectation of the CS model, and their final rating statistically significantly surpassed their expectation."

### Acosta Tatiana 2020 (2)

Acosta Tatiana, Sutton Jill Marie, Dotters-Katz Sarah. (2020). Improving Learners' Comfort With Cesarean Sections Through the Use of High-Fidelity, Low-Cost Simulation. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10878

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of participant comments (N = 21): 41% usefulness/positive reaction, 13% more participation, 14% more teaching, 18% better audiovisual, 14% greater fidelity; only one comment wished for a shorter simulation.

> "Notably, the majority of comments (41%) were positive and included “I loved this simulation” and “Very helpful!”"
