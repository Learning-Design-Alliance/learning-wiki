## Evidence

### Majuri M 2024

Majuri M, Malin M, Antila A, Luukkonen R, Palosaari M, Reijula K. (2024). Perceived mental health skills and competence among occupational health physicians. Occupational Medicine (Oxford, England). https://doi.org/10.1093/occmed/kqae080

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey (June–August 2022) of the Finnish OH specialist physician training programme; 59 trainers and 42 trainees responded (9% response rate). The results section reports trainers' mean was higher: "seems to be better than for the trainees'". No effect size was printed for this contrast.

> "The trainers’ mental health competence (mean 3.97, SD 0.72) seems to be better than for the trainees’ (mean 3.66, SD 1.08)."
