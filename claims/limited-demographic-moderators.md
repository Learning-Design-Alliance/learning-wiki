## Evidence

### Tianyi Ma 2024

Tianyi Ma, Cassandra L. Tellegen, Julie Hodges, Matthew R. Sanders. (2024). The Associations Between Parenting Self-Efficacy and Parents' Contributions to the Home-School Partnership Among Parents of Primary School Students: a Multilevel Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09937-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-regression and subgroup analyses on M1 tested child gender, parent gender, ethnicity, sample type, and SES separately. Child gender was non-significant (p = .183); parent gender (p = .053), ethnicity (p = .719), targeted vs general sample (p = .064), and SES (p = .160) were also non-significant.

> "The results suggested that child gender (% of male) was not associated with the strength of the overall association, β = .086, F(1) = 1.77, p = .183."
