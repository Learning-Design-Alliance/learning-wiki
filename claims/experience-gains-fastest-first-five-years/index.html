Podolsky 2025¶

Podolsky, Anne, and Linda Darling-Hammond. (2025). Think Again: Do the Returns to Teacher Experience Fizzle Out? Washington, D.C.: Thomas B. Fordham Institute. https://fordhaminstitute.org/national/research/think-again-do-returns-teacher-experience-fizzle-out

q2 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of the 23 fixed-effects studies in Table 1: 22 of 23 (96%) suggest improvement in years 0-5. A cited North Carolina study "found that they improved about two to four times faster in their first five years than they did in the next five years."

"Of these studies, the overwhelming majority found a positive and statistically significant relationship between experience and student achievement in a teacher’s first five years."
