## Evidence

### Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario 2014

Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario. (2014). Involving Users to Improve the Collaborative Logical Framework. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/893525

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Layer 3 check of the 2009 study comparing system-computed indicators with those written down by observing aDeNu researchers. The article reports differences "especially" for with-initiative (observed 24; computed 3) and gossip (observed 27; computed 15), and more system-found cases for insightful, thorough, and useful.

> "we found some differences between both the observed and the computed ones, especially in those indicators with a behavior highly recognizable from the observation point of view but roughly modeled by the metrics as "with-initiative" (observed 24; computed 3) or "gossip" (observed 27; computed 15)."

### Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario 2014 (2)

Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario. (2014). Involving Users to Improve the Collaborative Logical Framework. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/893525

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Deeper analysis of the communicative indicator in the same 2009 study: of 24 computed cases, only 15 matched the 29 observer-detected cases, leaving 9 system-only and 14 observer-only identifications.

> "a deeper analysis showed that only 15 of the computed ones matched up to the 29 detected by aDeNu researchers. Therefore there were 9 (24–15) who were identified as "communicative" by the CLF but not so by the instructors and 14 (29–15) who were identified by the instructors but not so by the CLF."
