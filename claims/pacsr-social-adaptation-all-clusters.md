## Evidence

### Kvintová Jana 2025

Kvintová Jana, Lacková Lucia, Liu Hongyang, Váchová Lucie, Potmesil Milon, Růžičková Veronika, Kopecký Kamil, Regec Vojtech. (2025). Resilience displays similar associative patterns with academic achievement regardless of the personality and mental health profile of future teachers. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03697-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cluster-wise regressions on the AAQ social adaptation subscale in the 793-student survey; overall models yielded the highest explanatory power (adjusted R² 0.14–0.24). PACSR was significantly positive in all three clusters, while Control was significant only in some clusters.

> "Across clusters, Positive Acceptance of Change and Secure Relationships (PACSR) had significant positive effects (Cluster 1: β = 0.10, p < 0.001; Cluster 2: β = 0.11, p < 0.001; Cluster 3: β = 0.07, p = 0.011), demonstrating its importance in fostering adaptability and social connections"
