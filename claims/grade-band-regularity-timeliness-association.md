## Evidence

### Kates Meghan 2026

Kates Meghan, Fitzpatrick Alexa, Zhang Yupeng, Gibbs Alison, Liu Qin, da Silveira Fleck Alan, Freedman Leora, Andreopoulos Stavroula. (2026). Effectiveness of Scientific Literacy and Communication E‐Modules in a Molecular Biology Course: A Qualitative and Quantitative Analysis. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70043

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reverse correlation associating final grade bands (A > 80%, B–C 79%–60%, D–F < 59%) with average regularity of e-module access in the pilot cohort. A-grade students "accessed the e-modules on more days" than both B–C (p = 0.026) and D–F (p < 0.001) groups.

> "Students who achieved an A grade accessed the e-modules on more days compared to both the B–C group (p-value = 0.026) and the D–F group (p-value < 0.001). In addition, the B–C group accessed modules on more days than the D–F group (p-value = 0.022)."

### Kates Meghan 2026 (2)

Kates Meghan, Fitzpatrick Alexa, Zhang Yupeng, Gibbs Alison, Liu Qin, da Silveira Fleck Alan, Freedman Leora, Andreopoulos Stavroula. (2026). Effectiveness of Scientific Literacy and Communication E‐Modules in a Molecular Biology Course: A Qualitative and Quantitative Analysis. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.70043

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same pilot study, timeliness metric by final grade band. A-band students "accessed e-modules significantly earlier than those in the B–C group (p = 0.049)"; A vs D–F (p = 0.236) and B–C vs D–F (p = 0.779) were not statistically significant.

> "In terms of timeliness, students in the A grade band accessed e-modules significantly earlier than those in the B–C group (p = 0.049). However, differences between A and D–F students (p-value = 0.236) and between B–C and D–F students (p-value = 0.779) were not statistically significant."
