## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Srdjan Verbić 2010

Srdjan Verbić. (2010). Does it matter if you answer slowly?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1005.0714](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1005.0714)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analysis of response-time logs from the PD09 low-stakes computer-based test for fourth-graders. The article reports "median value 38 s" for correct answers versus 45 s for wrong answers, with t(21833)=-24.3, p<2e-16.

> "average item RT of correct answers (median value 38 s) was much lower than RT of wrong answers (median value 45 s). Using t-test for log RT, we can quantify difference between log RT of correct answers and wrong answers, on the item level, and clearly demonstrate statistical significance of the difference: t(21833)=-24.3, p<2e-16."
