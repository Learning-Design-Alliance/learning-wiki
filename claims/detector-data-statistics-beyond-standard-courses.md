### T. Wibig 2006

T. Wibig, K. Ko/lodziejczak, R. Pierzyński, R. Sobczak. (2006). Educational studies of cosmic rays with telescope of Geiger-Müller counters. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/41/6/009](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/41/6/009)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors' account of the analysis phase: the scientist introduced the Poisson variation of counting rate and the barometric effect, then students wrote their first C programs; the article states this gives "a perfect possibility for students to be introduced to statistical methods at a level not available in standard courses".

> "Then, after a short lesson on statistics (straight line ﬁtting, χ2 methods, e.t.c.), the students wrote their ﬁrst programs in C and ran them to get th e result presented above."
