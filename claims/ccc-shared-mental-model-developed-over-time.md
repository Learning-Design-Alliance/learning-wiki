`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Observation of two CCC meetings (October 2016 and February 2017) using a semi-structured observation guide. After the first meeting the group leader re-explained EPA and CanMEDS criteria; the second meeting showed "less than in the first meeting" disagreement.

> "In the second meeting there were still discussions about how to assess the performance of residents, however, less than in the first meeting, showing that the CCC started to acquire a shared understanding."
