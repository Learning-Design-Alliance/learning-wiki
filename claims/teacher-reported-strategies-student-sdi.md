## Evidence

### Taylor 2007

Taylor, I. M., & Ntoumanis, N. (2007). Teacher motivational strategies and student self-determination in physical education. Journal of Educational Psychology, 99, 747-760. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.747

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression using teacher reports of the three strategies (51 teachers, 787 students) predicting student SDI. The article reports involvement positive (ß = .23), autonomy support negative (ß = -.13), and structure non-significant (ß = .02).

> "Teacher reports of involvement positively predicted student SDI (ß = .23, p < .001), autonomy support negatively predicted student SDI (ß = -.13, p < .05), however, structure did not predict student SDI (ß = .02, p > .05)."

### Taylor 2007 (2)

Taylor, I. M., & Ntoumanis, N. (2007). Teacher motivational strategies and student self-determination in physical education. Journal of Educational Psychology, 99, 747-760. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.747

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-mediator multilevel analysis of teacher-reported strategies (Table 4.4). Teacher-reported involvement predicted relatedness (ß = .23) and relatedness predicted student SDI (ß = .20), so "relatedness mediated the involvement-student SDI relationship"; teacher-reported autonomy support predicted no need satisfaction.

> "with student perceptions of relatedness as the mediator, all mediation steps were significant indicating that relatedness mediated the involvement-student SDI relationship."
