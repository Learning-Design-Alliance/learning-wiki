Evidence¶

Derman et al. 2016¶

Aysegul Derman, Elvan Sahin, Esme Hacieminoglu. (2016). Does Outdoor Education Make any Difference in Environmental Literacy of Pre-service Classroom Teachers? INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL OF ENVIRONMENTAL & SCIENCE EDUCATION, 11(15), 8491-8506. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Content analysis of answers to the third open-ended EEAES question. The article reports control participants "superficially mentioned which teaching methods they would use", whereas experimental participants said they would use student-centered methods and activities such as "field trips, tree plantations, walk in nature, observing the birds".

"All the teacher candidates in the experimental and the control group are planning to integrate the environmental education into their teachings when they become teachers."
