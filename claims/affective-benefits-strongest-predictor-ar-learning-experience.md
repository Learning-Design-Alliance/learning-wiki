`q2 i?` Affective benefits (pleasure, joy, entertainment) attained from using AR learning applications positively and most strongly influence student e-learning experience. [→ Baabdullah 2021](#baabdullah-2021)

## Evidence

### Baabdullah 2021

Baabdullah, Alsulaimani, Allamnakhrah, Alalwan, Dwivedi & Rana. (2021). Usage of Augmented Reality (AR) and Development of e-Learning Outcomes: An Empirical Evaluation of Students' e-Learning Experience. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Usage+of+Augmented+Reality+AR+and+Development+of+e-Learning+Outcomes+An+Empirical+Evaluation+of+Students+e-Learning+Experience

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling of survey responses from 325 undergraduates at four Saudi universities. In the structural model, the path from affective benefits to experience was the largest: "γ=0.488, p<0.000", supporting H4.

> "AB was observed to be the most significant factor predicting the student experience with AR.LRP (γ=0.488, p<0.000) followed by CGB (γ=0.341, p<0.007)"
