## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In the 24-hour periods of the intermediate and final sessions, LF/HF ratio evolution showed a significant group × time interaction, with higher LF/HF (more stress) in the control group, in diurnal (p = 0.04, g = 0.61) and nocturnal (p = 0.01, g = 0.82) periods. ## Evidence

### Ghazali Daniel Aiham 2019

Ghazali Daniel Aiham, Breque Cyril, Sosner Philippe, Lesbordes Mathieu, Chavagnat Jean-Jacques, Ragot Stéphanie, Oriot Denis. (2019). Stress response in the daily lives of simulation repeaters. A randomized controlled trial assessing stress evolution over one year of repetitive immersive simulations. PLoS ONE 14(7): e0220111. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0220111

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

24-hour Holter RM-ANOVA comparing the 9-session experimental group (n = 24) and 3-session control group (n = 24) across the three common sessions. The article reports the interaction "in diurnal period (p = 0.04, g = 0.61) and in nocturnal period (p = 0.01, g = 0.82)" and states "LF/HF ratio was higher in the control group for the intermediate and final sessions."

> "There existed an interaction between group and time in LF/HF ratio evolution between initial and intermediate sessions, and also between intermediate and final sessions in diurnal period (p = 0.04, g = 0.61) and in nocturnal period (p = 0.01, g = 0.82)."
