Schrijvers 2019¶

Schrijvers, M., Janssen, T., Fialho, O., & Rijlaarsdam, G. (2019). Gaining insight into human nature: A review of literature classroom intervention studies. Review of Educational Research, 89(1), 3-45. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654318812914

The review’s analysis of empirical support across the 13 included experimental and quasi-experimental intervention studies found that "One intervention affected students’ insight into themselves". No effect size is printed for this count-based synthesis.

"One intervention affected students’ insight into themselves, two affected their understanding of fictional others, and six affected their understanding of, views on, or intended behavior toward real-world others."
