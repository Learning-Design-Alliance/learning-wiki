Khodadady and Elahi 2012¶

ANOVA results on the 120-item schema-based cloze MCIT: pretest means 35.20 (control) and 33.50 (experimental) showed homogeneity; posttest means were 34.90 versus 77.70, a "superior performance" for SBI.

"the control and experimental groups were at the same level of language proficiency when the experiment started.
