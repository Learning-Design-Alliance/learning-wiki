Surman Craig B. H. 2013¶

Surman Craig B. H., Biederman Joseph, Spencer Thomas, Miller Carolyn A., McDermott Katie M., Faraone Stephen V. (2013). Understanding deficient emotional self-regulation in adults with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: a controlled study. Attention deficit and hyperactivity disorders. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4009378/

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Correlational analysis within the 206 adults with ADHD using Pearson's correlation between the DESR total score and current ADHD symptom severity. The study reports "a correlation of 0.70 (p<0.001)"; r = 0.70 is a large printed effect size.

"Among the ADHD subjects, the DESR score was associated with severity of current ADHD symptoms with a correlation of 0.70 (p<0.001)."
