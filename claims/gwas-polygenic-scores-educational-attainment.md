> "Most recently, a GWAS study with 1.1 million individuals identi ﬁed 1271 independent SNPs associated with educational attainment. 88 These SNPS were used to compute a polygenic prediction score that explained 11– 13% of the variance in educational attainment and 7 – 10% of the variance in cognitive performance in independent samples."

### H. Moriah Sokolowski and Daniel Ansari 2018 (2)

H. Moriah Sokolowski and Daniel Ansari. (2018). Understanding the effects of education through the lens of biology. npj Science of Learning 3:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0032-y

`q2 · i0` · `review · r2`

The review reports that the three SNPs from the original years-of-education GWAS have "coef ﬁcient of determination R(2) ≈0.02%", about 1 month of schooling per allele, replicated in a study with a threefold larger sample; this per-SNP effect is negligible.

> "Critically, the estimated effects sizes of these three SNPs are small (coef ﬁcient of determination R(2) ≈0.02%; this equals approximately 1 month of schooling per allele)."
