Sidorkin 2026¶

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of an end-of-course survey completed by 24 consenting students in LEAD 503, a purpose-built instrument treated as descriptive rather than latent-construct measurement. The finding reports perceived learning value: "Seventy-five percent agreed that they learned more than they would have in a comparable course without an AI companion."
