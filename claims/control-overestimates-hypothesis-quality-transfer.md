## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The three groups differed in monitoring accuracy (bias) for hypothesis quality in the transfer task (η2 = 0.09), with control participants overestimating their hypothesis quality relative to VMP and VM participants. [→ Omarchevska 2022](#omarchevska-2022)

## Evidence

### Omarchevska 2022

Omarchevska, Y., Lachner, A., Richter, J., & Scheiter, K. (2022). Do Video Modeling and Metacognitive Prompts Improve Self-Regulated Scientific Inquiry? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09652-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory one-way ANOVA on monitoring accuracy (bias) for hypothesis quality in the transfer task: "The three groups differed significantly in monitoring accuracy regarding hypothesis quality" (η2 = 0.09). Bias scores were computed from retrospective confidence judgments minus performance scores.

> "The three groups differed significantly in monitoring accuracy regarding hypothesis quality, F(2, 122) = 5.68, p = 0.004, η2 = 0.09."
