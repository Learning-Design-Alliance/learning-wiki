Content analysis found documentation themes concentrated in specific courses. The study reports "these findings suggest a longitudinal progression in documentation skills, although this theme was not represented in some areas, such as Knowledge Management and Research."

> "Theme 3 “Prepares the clinical history and other medical records in a clear, truthful, and concrete manner” was reflected in 16.27% of all quotes (n = 76) and particularly emphasized in Semiology I (C2-1), Psychiatry (C2-6), and Urinary and Renal System (C6-1)."
