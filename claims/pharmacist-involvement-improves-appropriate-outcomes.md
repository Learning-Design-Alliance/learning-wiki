## Evidence

### Collins Jack Charles 2017

Collins Jack Charles, Schneider Carl Richard, Naughtin Clare Louise, Wilson Frances, de Almeida Neto Abilio Cesar, Moles Rebekah Jane. (2017). Mystery shopping and coaching as a form of audit and feedback to improve community pharmacy management of non-prescription medicine requests: an intervention study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-019462

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pearson’s χ2 analysis of pharmacist involvement (directly serving or consulted) against appropriate outcome across 520 of the 521 eligible visits. "interactions with a pharmacist resulted in the appropriate outcome in 62% of cases" versus 33% without (χ2=35.04; P<0.001); median questioning score 50% vs 25%. No effect size printed for this finding.

> "Interactions without the involvement of a pharmacist resulted in the appropriate outcome being achieved in 33% of cases, whereas interactions with a pharmacist resulted in the appropriate outcome in 62% of cases. Pearson’s χ2 analyses found this to be a significant difference (χ2=35.04; P<0.001)."
