Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 132 South Korean prelicensure nursing students using paired t-tests and IPA. The article reports the total need-experience gap as 1.64 ± 0.91 with paired t = 20.72 (p < 0.001), "a gap between needs and experience in all 21 categories."
