Ahmad D. Suleiman 2026¶

Ahmad D. Suleiman, Daqing Hou, and Maliha Noushin Raida. (2026). LLM-Generated Design Problems for Assessing Higher-Order Thinking in Project-Based Learning. arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.11032. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11032 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.11032

q2 · i? · design · r2

One-way ANOVA of Task A grades across the three courses found F=15.519, p<0.001; Tukey HSD showed DT3 (paper-based, N=40) significantly lower than the two web-based courses, which did not differ significantly.

"student DP performance differed significantly across the three deployments (𝐹= 15.519,𝑝< 0.001). Tukey HSD showed thatDT3 grades were significantly lower thanTouchalyticsandBreached! (𝑝< 0.001)"

Ahmad D. Suleiman 2026 (2)¶

Ahmad D. Suleiman, Daqing Hou, and Maliha Noushin Raida. (2026). LLM-Generated Design Problems for Assessing Higher-Order Thinking in Project-Based Learning. arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.11032. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11032

q2 · i2 · design · r2

Spearman correlation analysis of performance data found word count strongly correlated with DP grades (ρ=0.67, p<0.001), while time on task correlated negligibly (ρ=−0.05, p=0.76), suggesting longer typed responses better articulated design justifications.

"word count showed a strong positive correlation with DP grades (𝜌= 0.67,𝑝< 0.001), suggesting that longer responses enabled students to better articulate and justify design choices."
