`q2 i?` In mathematics in the full national sample, there is a tendency of the intraclass correlations to be larger at lower grades, with a negative regression slope on grade (b = -.004) and a correlation between grade and ICC of -.443. [→ Hedges 2007](#hedges-2007)

## Evidence

### Hedges 2007

Hedges, L. V., & Hedberg, E. C. (2007). Intraclass Correlation Values for Planning Group-Randomized Trials in Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 29(1), 60-87. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373707299706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

The mixed-model analysis of mathematics ICCs across Grades K-12 in the full national sample found "a tendency of the intraclass correlations to be larger at lower grades", though adjacent-grade changes were generally not large; the table's summary rows print b = -.004 and r = -.443.

> "Although there is a tendency of the intraclass correlations to be larger at lower grades, in general, there are not large changes across adjacent grade levels."
