## Evidence

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer and Jérôme Prado 2023

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer and Jérôme Prado. (2023). Arithmetic skills are associated with left fronto-temporal gray matter volume in 536 children and adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00201-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bayesian mixed-effect models on the same n = 536 VBM sample quantified evidence for the null hypothesis of no GMV-arithmetic relation in each ROI. The article reports "strong in the left AG (BF 01 = 11.58) and hippocampus" and "substantial in the left IPS (BF 01 = 7.10)".

> "Evidence for a lack of relation between GMV and arithmetic score was strong in the left AG (BF 01 = 11.58) and hippocampus (left: BF 01 = 10.38, right: BF10 = 12.43), substantial in the left IPS (BF 01 = 7.10), and anecdotal in the right IPS (BF 01 = 2.82)."
