## Evidence

### Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet 2025

Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet, Tefera Aklile Semu. (2025). Impact of Nutrition on Academic Performance of First-cycle Primary School Children in Amhara Region, Ethiopia: A Multicenter Cross-Sectional Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13077153/

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 514 first-cycle primary students in North Shoa Zone, Ethiopia; multivariable logistic regression on previous-semester results. Breakfast eaters showed "about 4.2 times good academic performance" with adjusted odds ratio AOR = 4.253101 (95% CI: 2.160–8.371, p=0.0001).

> "Students who ate their breakfast were found to have about 4.2 times good academic performance compared to those students who did not take their breakfast (AOR=4.253101, 95 % CI:2.160-8.371)."
