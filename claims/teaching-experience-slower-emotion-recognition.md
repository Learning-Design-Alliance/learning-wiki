## Evidence

### Samuel Fynes-Clinton 2022

Samuel Fynes-Clinton, Chase Sherwell, Maryam Ziaei, Ashley York, Emma Sanders O'Connor, Kylee Forrest, Libby Flynn, Julie Bower, David Reutens and Annemaree Carroll. (2022). Neural activation during emotional interference corresponds to emotion dysregulation in stressed teachers. npj Science of Learning 7:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00123-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Partial correlation analysis controlling for age in 49 stressed teachers, reported alongside Table 1. "years of teaching experience and participants ' median overall reaction time on the Emotion Recognition Task (ERT) were positively correlated (r = 0.31,p < 0.05)", i.e. more experience meant slower emotion recognition.

> "Controlling for the effect of age, partial correlation analysis revealed that years of teaching experience and participants ' median overall reaction time on the Emotion Recognition Task (ERT) were positively correlated (r = 0.31,p < 0.05)."
