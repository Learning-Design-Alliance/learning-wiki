Quantitative 17-item usability survey (0-10 scale) completed by 60 nursing students after the VR intervention. The ease-of-use item "The initial education regarding devices and usage was appropriate" received the highest mean (9.25±1.00), with interest in other VR programs at 8.90±1.42.
