`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview findings from the theme 'Development of self-confidence'. "Trainees from the VP group also reported feeling confident for future practice, but this was not reported by any trainees who used the NI cases"; trainees attributed this to the safe environment for practice.

> "All trainees in the VP group and the majority of trainees in the NI group reported that using the cases improved their confidence for the pre-registration examination. Trainees from the VP group also reported feeling confident for future practice, but this was not reported by any trainees who used the NI cases."
