## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Teaching must be targeted to a diagnosed learner need; giving information to a learner who lacks skills rather than knowledge is ineffective. [→ Glick SB 2010](#glick-sb-2010)

## Evidence

### Glick SB 2010

Glick SB, Fernandez L, Irby DM, Harleman E, Fernandez A. (2010). Teaching About Health Care Disparities in the Clinical Setting. J Gen Intern Med 25(Suppl 2):95–101. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-009-1203-6

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analytical argument in the diagnose-the-learner section, drawing on Irby and Wilkerson's work on teaching when time is limited. The article states the purpose of inquiry is "to identify at least one area in which the learner would benefit from instruction," not to diagnose all needs.

> "Failing to diagnose the learner may cause the teacher to provide information that the learner already knows, while ignoring areas that are needed."
