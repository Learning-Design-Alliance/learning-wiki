Authors' interpretation in the Discussion: concepts like recognising celestial objects and Earth's axial movement "cannot be properly understood by just watching videos, studying from books etc." The article reports no comparative measurement supporting this claim.

> "using a telescope, organising the alignment of the telescope in the field and then the help of various substitute instruments/technologies like compass, gps location can significantly boost the acquisition of the knowledge of the sky and the astronomy in general."
