Rohaeti 2019¶

Rohaeti, E.E., Bernard, M., & Primandhika, R.B. (2019). Developing interactive learning media for school level mathematics through open-ended approach aided by Visual Basic application for Excel. Journal on Mathematics Education, 10(1), 59-68. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1204890

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive pre-learning mastery test of 68 prospective-teacher students. "73. 91% the students understand about arithmetical operation"; the article also reports 60.86% following the example picture and 8.69% forming other content (geometry, fractions, roots, exponents).

"From the essential mathematical concept mastery test obtained from 68 students, the result shows 73. 91% the students understand about arithmetical operation such as addition, subtraction, division, and multiplication"

Related Claims¶

  • After open-ended VBA-based learning, students showed gains in independent creation: 31.43% created new ideas without following examples and 20% made animation-based props, up from 8.69% and 60.86% example-dependence — related
  • Post-learning media products spanned multiple mathematics topics: 42.8% geometry-related props, 28.57% arithmetic-related props, 51.43% could explain prop creation, and 60% could use shapes to run their code — related
