## Subclaims
`q3 i2` There is a weak positive relationship between spatial intelligence score and visual memory score in both disciplines (r = 0.294 medical, r = 0.280 dental, P<0.001). [→ Amin Aspanani 2023](#amin-aspanani-2023)

## Evidence

### Amin Aspanani 2023

Amin Aspanani, Hosein Sadeqhi, Omid Athar. (2023). The relationship between visual memory and spatial intelligence with students’ academic achievement in anatomy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04327-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation between the two measures in each discipline (Table 3): r=0.294 in medical students (N=148) and r=0.280 in dental students (N=85), both P<0.001, described by the authors as weak positive.

> "there was a weak positive relationship between spatial intelligence score and visual memory score in students of both disciplines (P-value<0/001 & rdentist=0.280rmedical=0.294). In other words, as the visual memory score increased, so did the spatial intelligence score"
