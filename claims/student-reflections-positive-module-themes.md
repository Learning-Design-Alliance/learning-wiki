`q2 i?` Thematic analysis of 160 ePortfolio reflections identified positive themes including promoting understanding, motivation, efficiency, flexibility, navigation, feedback, and individualisation. [→ Herbert C 2017](#herbert-c-2017)

## Evidence

### Herbert C 2017

Herbert C, Velan GM, Pryor WM, Kumar RK. (2017). A model for the use of blended learning in large group teaching sessions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1057-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive phenomenological thematic analysis (NVivo) of ePortfolio reflections from 160 students asked to reflect critically on the online modules. "The capacity of the modules to build understanding was strongly borne out"; students also valued feedback, flexibility, motivation, and efficiency.

> "The capacity of the modules to build understanding was strongly borne out in the reflective comments."
