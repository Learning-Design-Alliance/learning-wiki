## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mandinach 2012

Mandinach, E. B., & Gummer, E. S. (2012). Navigating the Landscape of Data Literacy: It IS Complex. White paper. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED582807

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Materials-analysis finding on the inferences/interpretations category. The authors note the "paucity of description" and conjecture that inference processes may be explained in supplementary materials and during professional development activities rather than in the texts themselves.

> "Few texts addressed the knowledge and skills needed to make inferences and interpretations in great detail, and this paucity of description may point to another limitation of the analysis of texts as a way to describe professional development related to data literacy knowledge and skills."
