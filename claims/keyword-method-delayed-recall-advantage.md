`q2 i?` Three weeks after instruction, the keyword group scored significantly higher than the traditional group on a surprise delayed recall posttest, which the authors interpret as a long-term retention effect. [→ Davoudi 2016](#davoudi-2016)

## Evidence

### Davoudi 2016

Davoudi, M., & Yousefi, D. (2016). The Effect of Keyword Method on Vocabulary Retension of Senior High School EFL Learners in Iran. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(11). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099563

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Surprise delayed recall posttest given three weeks after the eight-session treatment to the same two groups (20 keyword, 18 traditional). Keyword group mean was M= 17.1500 versus M= 8.44 for the traditional group; the article concludes keyword method had long-term effects on vocabulary retention.

> "The results of  t-test in Table 4.8 indicated that there was a significant difference between two grou ps in delayed recall posttest (t=  4.36 , Sig= 0.000)."
