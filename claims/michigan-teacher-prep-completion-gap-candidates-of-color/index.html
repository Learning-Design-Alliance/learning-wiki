Charlotte Pierce 2026¶

Charlotte Pierce. (2026). Strong Start, Strong Teachers: Supporting Teacher Preparation Programs for a Brighter Future. EdTrust-Midwest. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682554

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The report's introduction, citing MDE Educator Workforce Data Report data analyzed by EdTrust-Midwest, states the enrollment-to-completion gap among candidates of color: "nearly 20%" of enrollees versus "only 12%" of completers in 2022-23.

"while nearly 20% of those enrolled in teacher prep programs were people of color in 2022-23, only 12% of those who completed a teacher preparation program were people of color"
