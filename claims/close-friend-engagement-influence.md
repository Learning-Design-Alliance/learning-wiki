### Huiyoung Shin 2022

Huiyoung Shin. (2022). Social contagion of academic behavior: Comparing social networks of close friends and admired peers. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0265385

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SAOM analysis of friend networks across two waves found a significant positive average similarity influence effect on behavioral engagement, reported as "OR = 26.05, p < .001"; no standardized effect size printed.

> "Regarding social influence, the positive influence effects were reflected in how adolescents’ academic engagement became more similar to those of their close friends (OR = 26.05, p < .001)"
