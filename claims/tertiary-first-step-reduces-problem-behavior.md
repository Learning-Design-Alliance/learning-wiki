## Evidence

### Frey 2015

Frey, A. J., Small, J. W., Lee, J., Walker, H. M., Seeley, J. R., Feil, E. G., & Golly, A. (2015). Expanding the range of the First Step to Success intervention: Tertiary-level support for children, teachers, and families. Early Childhood Research Quarterly 30 (2015) 1–11. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.05.002

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Covariate-adjusted regression models on three problem-behavior outcomes (SSBD-MBI, SSiS-PB teacher and parent) comparing 33 intervention with 22 comparison students. The article reports "statistically significant reductions in maladaptive and problem behaviors across both school and home settings" with Hedges' g of −.77 to −1.17.

> "Students who participated in the intervention also had statistically significant reductions in maladaptive and problem behaviors across both school and home settings. The Hedges’ g effect sizes for the teacher- and parent-reported problem behaviors ranged from −.77 to −1.17"
