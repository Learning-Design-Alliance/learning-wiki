Evidence¶

Michaela Minárechová 2016¶

Michaela Minárechová. (2016). Using a concept cartoon© method to address elementary school students' ideas about natural phenomena. European Journal of Science and Mathematics Education, Vol. 4, No. 2, 214-228. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1107824

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Direct structured observation of one student group per lesson (three constant groups, each observed three times over nine themes). "the communication of students depended especially on teaching topics"; answers in familiar topics were spontaneous and unprovoked by the teacher.

"Based on the observation of the three groups we can say that the communication of students depended especially on teaching topics. It was obvious that they had no problems with presenting their views in topics which they knew (due to them having experience) for example in topic of inclined plane."
