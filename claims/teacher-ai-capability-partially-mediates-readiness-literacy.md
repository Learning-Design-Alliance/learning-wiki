# Aggregated teacher-perceived AI capability partially mediates the association between school AI readiness and student AI literacy

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` School AI readiness was positively associated with aggregated teacher-perceived AI capability (β = 0.165, p < 0.001), establishing the a-path of the mediation model. [→ Guan 2026](#guan-2026)
`q3 i0` Teacher-perceived AI capability was positively associated with student AI literacy in the cross-level b-path (β = 0.009, p < 0.001), and the indirect effect was significant (ab = 0.0015, p = 0.034) with the direct effect remaining significant, indicating partial mediation. [→ Guan 2026 (2)](#guan-2026-2)
`q3 i0` Other teacher perception measures showed weaker or statistically inconsistent cross-level associations, and general attitudinal acceptance measures did not demonstrate stable transmission effects. [→ Guan 2026 (3)](#guan-2026-3)

## Evidence

### Guan 2026

Guan, X., Zheng, M., Gašević, D., Guo, W., Liu, Y., Han, X., Gasevic, D., Ma, R., Wu, Q., & Yan, L. (2026). From School AI Readiness to Student AI Literacy: A National Multilevel Mediation Analysis of Institutional Capacity and Teacher Capability. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056)

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

First-stage school-level a-path from the 2-2-1 mediation model relating school readiness to aggregated teacher mechanisms. Readiness was also positively associated with perceived positive effects (β = 0.176), difficulty of use (β = 0.208) and social support (β = 0.185), and negatively with satisfaction.

> "overall school AI readiness was positively associated with teachers’ perceived AI capability (𝛽= 0.165,𝑆𝐸= 0.033,𝑡(𝑑𝑓= 999) = 5.003,𝑝 <0.001)"

### Guan 2026 (2)

Guan, X., Zheng, M., Gašević, D., Guo, W., Liu, Y., Han, X., Gasevic, D., Ma, R., Wu, Q., & Yan, L. (2026). From School AI Readiness to Student AI Literacy: A National Multilevel Mediation Analysis of Institutional Capacity and Teacher Capability. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Cross-level b-path estimated on a stratified random subsample of up to 300 students per school. The article reports the indirect effect was significant by the Delta method (ab = 0.0015, z = 2.114, p = 0.034; CI [0.00011, 0.00300]) and Monte Carlo CI [0.00024, 0.00318], with the direct effect remaining significant, indicating partial mediation.

> "teachers’ perceived AI capability was positively associated with student AI literacy after controlling for school AI readiness and structural covariates (𝛽= 0.009,𝑆𝐸= 0.004,𝑡(𝑑𝑓= 286,418) = 2.333,𝑝 <0.001)"

### Guan 2026 (3)

Guan, X., Zheng, M., Gašević, D., Guo, W., Liu, Y., Han, X., Gasevic, D., Ma, R., Wu, Q., & Yan, L. (2026). From School AI Readiness to Student AI Literacy: A National Multilevel Mediation Analysis of Institutional Capacity and Teacher Capability. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

In the simultaneous mediation model only perceived AI capability showed a stable cross-level b-path. The article concludes that collective professional capability, rather than general evaluative attitudes or satisfaction, was the most stable teacher-level predictor.

> "Other perception-based indicators exhibited weaker or statistically inconsistent associations in the full model."
