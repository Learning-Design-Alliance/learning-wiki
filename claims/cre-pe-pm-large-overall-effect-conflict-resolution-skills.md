## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across 23 Turkish studies, the pooled random-effects Hedge's g for the effect of CRE, PE and PM programs on students' conflict resolution skills is 1.256, classified as a quite large effect. [→ Fulya Turk 2017](#fulya-turk-2017)

## Evidence

### Fulya Turk 2017

Fulya Turk. (2017). Evaluation of the Effects of Conflict Resolution, Peace Education and Peer Mediation: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Education Studies; Vol. 11, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p25

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 23 Turkish experimental and semi-experimental studies meeting inclusion criteria, using Hedge's g with a random-effects model. The pooled effect size (Hedge's g = 1.256) was "determined as 1,256", which the article classifies as "quite large" under the Thalheimer and Cook (2002) scheme.

> "The general effect size for the effect sizes of individual studies has been determined as 1,256. According to the effect size classification by Thalheimer and Cook (2002), this interval is an indication that the effect is quite large."
