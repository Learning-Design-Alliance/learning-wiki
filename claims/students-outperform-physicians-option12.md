### Herschbach L 2026

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Group comparison in the preregistered feasibility study using a Welch 2-tailed 2-sample t test after the equal-variance assumption was violated. The article reports students "performed significantly better than physicians" on the OPTION-12 total score.

> "Results showed that students (n=22; mean 28.41, SD 4.38) performed significantly better than physicians (n=34; mean 22.79, SD 8.14; t52.60=3.49; P=.002; 95% CI 2.24-8.99)."

### Herschbach L 2026 (2)

Herschbach L, Festl-Wietek T, Sonanini A, Holderried F, Albrecht T, Herrmann B, Herrmann-Werner A. (2026). AI-Simulated Patients for Training Shared Decision-Making: Feasibility Study in Medical Education. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100467

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent-samples 2-tailed t test on IcanSDM attitude scores in the same study. The article reports "no significant difference in SDM attitudes" between the two groups; equivalence was not tested.

> "The results indicated no significant difference in SDM attitudes between students (n=22; mean 3.96, SD 1.05) and physicians (n=34; mean 4.13, SD 1.31; t54=–0.507; P=.61; 95% CI –0.835 to 0.498)."
