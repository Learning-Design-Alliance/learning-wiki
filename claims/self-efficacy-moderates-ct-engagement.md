## Evidence

### Su Weilin 2026

Su Weilin, Chen Chen. (2026). The mediating role of academic engagement and the moderating role of academic self-efficacy in the relationship between critical thinking and academic competence: evidence from Chinese college students. BMC Psychology, 14, 700. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04446-0

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analysis (Model 3, Table 3) with subgroups formed at mean ± 1 SD of self-efficacy (Fig. 2). The article grades the moderation: "the moderating role of academic self-efficacy (β = 0.158, p < 0.001) qualifies as a small effect".

> "the results of Model 3 show that the interaction term of critical thinking and academic self-efficacy is significant in predicting academic engagement (β = 0.158, p < 0.001), which indicates that academic self-efficacy of Chinese college students play a significant positive moderating role in the influence of critical thinking on their academic engagement."
