## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The intervention group scored significantly higher than the comparison group on ethical perspective (effect size 0.84, improvement index +30), as calculated by the WWC. [→ Building Decision Skills 2006](#building-decision-skills-2006)

## Evidence

### Building Decision Skills 2006

Building Decision Skills. (2006). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse. https://www.globalethics.org/services/edu/bds.htm

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r?`

WWC recalculation of the Leming (2001) quasi-experiment (283 Grade 12 students, six classrooms). Appendix A3 reports ethical perspective means of 1.84 (intervention) versus 1.31 (comparison), effect size 0.84, statistically significant at α = 0.05 after corrections for clustering and multiple comparisons.

> "Leming reported statistically sig- nificant differences favoring the Building Decision Skills group on four student outcomes (ethical awareness, ethical responsibility, ethical perspective, and social responsibility-school), and one of these outcomes, ethical perspective, was found to be statistically significant (as calculated by the WWC)."
