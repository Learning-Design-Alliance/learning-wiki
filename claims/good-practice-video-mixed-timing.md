## Evidence

### Demedts 2024

Demedts, D., Magerman, J., Goossens, E., Tricas-Sauras, S., Bilsen, J., De Smet, S., & Fobelets, M. (2024). Using simulation to teach nursing students how to deal with a euthanasia request. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0299049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of group interviews after the full module. The authors report that more inexperienced students found the video useful guidance while more experienced students feared referring back to it too much and preferred seeing it after the simulation.

> "There was no unanimity on whether the video should be shown before, or after the simulation exercise. Some students felt the video would guide them into a too narrow direction, while others needed that guidance."
