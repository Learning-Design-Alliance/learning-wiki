In consultations with experienced online supervisors and their former students, the most frequently cited desirable supervisor characteristics were a supportive attitude (61.54% total), mastery of the research field (57.69%), being directive (53.85%), and being accessible, friendly and empathetic (50% total). → Darder 2015

Darder 2015¶

Darder, A.; Pérez Garcias, A. (2015). Online tutoring procedure for research project supervision: management, organization and key elements. New Approaches in Educational Research 4(2). https://doi.org/10.7821/naer.2015.4.110

q2 · i? · design · r2

Consultation with 14 experienced online supervisors (from 23 invited across 16 PhD programs) and 12 of their former students, using semi-structured interviews and closed questions. Table 1 reports percentages, e.g. "Showing a supportive attitude 71.43 50 61.54" and "Mastering the research field 57.14 58.33 57.69".

"The results obtained from the consultation with trainee researchers and tutors with regard to the characteristics that define a research project supervisor are: accessibility, empathy, friendliness, support, organization, an open mind, mastery of the field, mastery of methodology, direction, etc."
