## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One-way ANOVA found no significant difference in gain scores among computer science, engineering, physics, science, and high school background groups. [→ Özlem Salehi 2022](#ozlem-salehi-2022)

## Evidence

### Özlem Salehi 2022

Özlem Salehi, Zeki Seskir, and Ilknur Tepe. (2022). A Computer Science-Oriented Approach to Introduce Quantum Computing to a New Audience. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1109/te.2021.3078552](https://doi.org/10.1109/te.2021.3078552)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Among 258 participants who provided department information, grouped into CS, physics, engineering, science, and high school categories, gain scores were normally distributed by Shapiro-Wilk test and one-way ANOVA returned "F (4, 246) = .66, p = .62" — no significant difference. Equivalence was not tested.

> "one-way ANOV A test is conducting suggesting that there is no signiﬁcant difference between the gain scores of the participants with different educational background s, F (4, 246) = .66, p = .62."
