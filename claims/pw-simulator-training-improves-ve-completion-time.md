## Evidence

### Bigras Catherine 2019

Bigras Catherine, Kairy Dahlia, Archambault Philippe S. (2019). Augmented feedback for powered wheelchair training in a virtual environment. Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-019-0482-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 40 healthy young adults trained in the miWe simulator; mixed-effects ANOVA of VE test-task completion time at baseline, 20-min post-training and two-day retention. Participants were "seven seconds faster than at baseline" at acquisition and eight seconds faster at retention.

> "The mixed effects model found a significant effect of time from baseline to acquisition (p = .002) and from baseline to retention (p < .001) for all participants. No significant group differences were found."
