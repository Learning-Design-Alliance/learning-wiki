### Ashiqul Islam Dip 2016

Ashiqul Islam Dip, Mohammad Tomal Hossain, Md Forman Ullah, Md Salah Uddin. (2016). Gender Differences in Undergraduate Physics Courses: A Comparative Study of Persistence. Science Journal of Education. [doi:10.11648/j.sjedu.20170501.13](https://doi.org/10.11648/j.sjedu.20170501.13)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Attendance analysis of the six-day 1st Workshop on Vector Calculus, where 66 male and 17 female undergraduates attended at least one day; exponential fits gave decrease rates per group. The authors report the female index is "100.6% higher than the persistence index of the male participants".

> "The obtained decrease rate is 𝜆 = 0.320, and the persistence index ℘ = 3.125‡. This persistence index of the female participants is 100.6% higher than the persistence index of the male participants of the same workshop."
