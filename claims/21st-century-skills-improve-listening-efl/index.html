Evidence¶

Ashraf 2017¶

Ashraf, H., Ahmadi, F., & Hosseinnia, M. (2017). Integrating 21st Century Skills into Teaching English: Investigating its Effect on Listening and Speaking Skills. i-manager's Journal on English Language Teaching, 7(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1173769

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test on listening post-test scores of 55 female second-grade high school students in two intact classes in Birjand, Iran (convenience sampling; the methods give 25 experimental and 30 control, the abstract the reverse) after 12 sessions by the same teacher; the article reports "p-value = 0.021 < α = 0.05". Descriptives: control mean 10.70, experimental mean 12.25. One study in one school and population; it does not establish the effect for EFL instruction generally.

"results of the t-test indicated that there was a significant difference between the mean scores of the control and experimental groups in post-test because: p-value = 0.021 < α = 0.05."
