## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2019

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Validation and Administration of a Conceptual Survey on the Formalism and Postulates of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Large-scale administration of the validated QMFPS to 464 students from 6 institutions (350 undergraduate, 114 graduate). The authors report "There is a significant difference between the graduate and undergraduate students' average scores (p-value of t-test<0.001)" and an overall average of 41% (SD 20%).

> "The average score of undergraduate students is 37% and the average score of graduate students is 52%."
