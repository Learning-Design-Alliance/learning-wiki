## Evidence

### Bender 2026

Bender, L., Renkl, A., & Endres, T. (2026). Punchline with(out) purpose: Integrating research on instructional humour and seductive details. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70080

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Planned post-hoc comparisons from the MANCOVA in Study 1, a randomized laboratory experiment with 115 adults learning about lightning from a narrated slideshow. The irrelevant-humour group scored worse on transfer than control (M diff = −8.63) and relevant humour (M diff = −7.40).

> "post-hoc comparisons revealed that the irrelevant-humour group's transfer performance was significantly worse than both the control group's (M diff = −8.63, p = .018, 95% CI [−15.76; −1.51]) and the relevant-humour group's (M diff = −7.40, p = .036, 95% CI [−13.82; −.50])."
