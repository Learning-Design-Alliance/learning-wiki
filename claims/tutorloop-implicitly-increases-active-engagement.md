## Evidence

### Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang 2026

Songlin Xu and Xinyu Zhang. (2026). TutorLoop: Regulating Student Learning Behaviors via Sensor-in-the-Loop Generative Feedback. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.09400](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.09400)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Slide switch ratio (button clicks over the 600-second session) measured active engagement in the N=187 study; Full's reference mean was 0.092. TutorUp showed a significant decrease relative to Control (p=.040). The printed correlation with semantic similarity was r=0.173, p=0.018, a small effect.

> "the significant improvement in active engagement (slide switching) in ourFullgroup, combined with the positive correlation between active engagement and learning outcomes (𝑟= 0.173,𝑝= 0.018), suggests that our system can enhance learning by fostering active engagement implicitly"
