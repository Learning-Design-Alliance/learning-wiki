Post hoc Tukey comparisons following the three-group ANOVA on the non-cognitive skills scale. Notably, the traditional lecture group scored significantly below both the interactive group and the untreated control group; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The results indicated a statistically significance difference between methods used in Group A and B, in favor of Group A; in addition to a statistically significant difference between methods used in Group B and Group C (the control group), in favor of Group C."
