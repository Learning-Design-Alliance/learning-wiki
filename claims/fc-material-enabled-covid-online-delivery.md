## Evidence

### Binnie Rebecca S. L. 2021

Binnie Rebecca S. L., Bonsor Stephen J. (2021). The implementation of a flipped classroom approach at a UK dental school. British Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41415-021-3125-6

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretive account (type e) of how the flipped format supported pandemic-era delivery; students saw no interruption of theoretical teaching as online live tutorials replaced face-to-face sessions. No comparative data are reported for this observation.

> "At the time of writing in the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic, this approach enabled the course to be delivered online and facilitated the move for staff to work from home as the core of the teaching was in place."
