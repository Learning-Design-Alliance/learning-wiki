## Evidence

### Josephine C. Meyer 2022

Josephine C. Meyer, Gina Passante, Steven J. Pollock, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2022). The interdisciplinary quantum information classroom: Themes from a survey of quantum information science instructors. arXiv:2202.05944. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2202.05944](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2202.05944)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Follow-up interview with David, a physics-department instructor at a public R1 institution teaching a hybrid physics/CS course; this is an instructor perception, not a tested statistical result. Edwin similarly reported no benefit from a wave-functions-first QM prerequisite.

> "We found that ... how much quantum mechanics the students had previously been exposed to was not a very good indicator [of] how much trouble, or how well they did, in the course."
