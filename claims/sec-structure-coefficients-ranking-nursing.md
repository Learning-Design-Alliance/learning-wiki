Structure coefficients from the same canonical correlation analysis of 195 nursing students. The article reports critical thinking disposition (Rs = 0.89), problem-solving (Rs = 0.86), and collaboration (Rs = 0.80) as the top SEC contributors, with clinical performance (Rs = 0.95) versus subjective AA (Rs = 0.57) on the AA side.

> "The variables with the highest correlation with SEC were critical thinking disposition (Rs = 0.89), problem-solving (Rs = 0.86), and collaboration (Rs = 0.80), while AA had the highest correlation with clinical performance (Rs = 0.95)."
