Evidence¶

Yilmaz A 2026¶

Yilmaz A (2026) The psychological mechanisms and behavioral determinants of academic integrity in the age of artificial intelligence. Front. Psychol. 17:1853790. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1853790

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Multiple regression analyses in the survey of 1,045 undergraduates tested social and contextual factors measured with a researcher-developed instrument. The article reports "ethical and moral education emerged as a negative predictor" of AI-assisted academic dishonesty. No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"In addition, social norms, peer behaviors, family attitudes, insuﬃcient sanctions, teacher attitude, high expectations, and adverse conditions signiﬁcantly predicted AI-assisted academic dishonesty, whereas ethical and moral education emerged as a negative predictor."
