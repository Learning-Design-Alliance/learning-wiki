# Adolescents with pure ADHD symptoms progress to institutions of higher education less often than controls by age 32

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Males with ADHD symptoms reached higher education at 14.0% versus 41.8% of male controls (adjusted OR 0.25, 95% CI 0.17–0.38). [→ Seppä S 2023](#seppa-s-2023)
`q3 i?` Females with ADHD symptoms reached higher education at 30.0% versus 60.4% of female controls (adjusted OR 0.37, 95% CI 0.24–0.57). [→ Seppä S 2023 (2)](#seppa-s-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Seppä S 2023

Seppä S, Halt A-H, Nordström T, Hurtig T. (2023). Effects of Symptoms of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) on Academic Performance and Educational Attainment. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-023-01598-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multinomial logistic regression of male educational attainment at 32 in NFBC1986, controls as reference, adjusted for parental education, family type, and other psychiatric disorders. Only odds ratios and confidence intervals were printed, no standardized effect size.

> "41.8% of the participants in the control group had an institution of higher education as their ultimate educational attainment, whereas this was the case for 14.0% of those in the ADHD group (adjusted OR 0.25, CI 0.17–0.38)"

### Seppä S 2023 (2)

Seppä S, Halt A-H, Nordström T, Hurtig T. (2023). Effects of Symptoms of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) on Academic Performance and Educational Attainment. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-023-01598-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multinomial logistic regression of female educational attainment at 32 in NFBC1986, controls as reference. The female ADHD group's adjusted OR for higher education was 0.37 (95% CI 0.24–0.57); no standardized effect size was printed.

> "60.4% of the female participants in the control group had an institution of higher education as their ultimate educational attainment, as opposed to 30.0% of those in the ADHD group (adjusted OR 0.37 with a 95% CI of 0.24–0.57)"
