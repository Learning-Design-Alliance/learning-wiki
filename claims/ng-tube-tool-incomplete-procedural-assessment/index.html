Morris Marie C. 2012¶

Morris Marie C., Gallagher Tom K., Ridgway Paul F. (2012). Tools used to assess medical students competence in procedural skills at the end of a primary medical degree: a systematic review. Medical Education Online. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3427596/

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of Morse's descriptive study, in which of the total sample of 32 participants only four were medical students. The review states the tool "does not comprehensively assess procedural skills in nasogastric tube insertion".

"Morse's (17) study on NG tube insertion focused specifically on interpersonal skills. The tool includes four items on tube preparation and does not comprehensively assess procedural skills in nasogastric tube insertion. No data is provided on the reliability or validity of this tool or the competence of the participants."
