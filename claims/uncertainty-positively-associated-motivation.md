### Ozcelik 2013

Ozcelik, E., Ercil Cagiltay, N., & Sahin Ozcelik, N. (2013). The effect of uncertainty on learning in game-like environments. Computers & Education, 67, 12–20. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2013.02.009

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A path analysis using LISREL 8.8 on data from the 140-participant experiment tested the hypothesized causal model; it showed "uncertainty was positively associated with motivation", supporting H1. The model fit indices were GFI = 0.95, CFI = 0.92, SRMR = 0.05.

> "As shown, uncertainty was positively associated with motivation ( t ¼ 1.29). Thus, H1 was supported."
