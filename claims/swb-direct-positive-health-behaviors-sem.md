## Evidence

### Karimi Mehrdad 2026

Karimi Mehrdad, Zareipour MoradAli, Zeynali Esmayil, Sharafkhani Rahim. (2026). Investigating spiritual well-being and its relationship with health behaviors, depression, and academic achievement in medical students: a structural equation modeling study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04902-x

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 362 medical sciences students (census sampling, 72.4% participation) using the Paloutzian SWB scale and Ahmadi health behavior questionnaire; the structural model "showed significant positive direct associations with all components of health-promoting behaviors" with the standardized coefficients reported above.

> "Spiritual Well-Being (SWB) showed significant positive direct associations with all components of health-promoting behaviors: Physical Activity (β = 0.35, p < 0.001), Healthy Diet (β = 0.48, p < 0.001), and No Smoking (β = 0.58, p < 0.001)."
