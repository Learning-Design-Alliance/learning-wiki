## Evidence

### Jorm Anthony F 2010

Jorm Anthony F, Kitchener Betty A, Sawyer Michael G, Scales Helen, Cvetkovski Stefan. (2010). Mental health first aid training for high school teachers: a cluster randomized trial. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-244X-10-51

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized trial with 7 intervention and 7 control schools; 327 teachers analyzed with mixed-effects models. Knowledge quiz mean difference was "2.08, P < 0.001" at post-test and 1.79 at follow-up; no standardized effect size printed.

> "At post-test, teachers who received training had greater gains in knowledge (mean difference = 2.08, P < 0.001) and these gains were maintained at follow-up (mean difference = 1.79, P < 0.001)."
