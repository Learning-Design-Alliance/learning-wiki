### Taneja 2026

Taneja, K., Singh, A., & Goel, A.K. (2026). Impact of Multimodal and Conversational AI on Learning Outcomes and Experience. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_60](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_60)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In-depth user-interaction analysis comparing MuDoC and TexDoC groups in the RCT. All comparisons were non-significant (e.g., time with AI "p=.31, d=−0.11"; notes "p=.80, d=−0.06"; response length "p= 0.10, d=−0.36"), so the article concludes the visuals in MuDoC responses likely drove the learning gains.
