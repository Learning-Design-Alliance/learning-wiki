## Evidence

### Bolat 2016

Bolat, M. (2016). The Development and Implementation of a Model for Teaching Astronomy to Deaf Students. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i7.1506

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interview findings with students S7, S12 and S20 across three observation dates; the article reports these as students' initial mental models, since students had received no schooling about seasons. The authors note these align with Roald & Mikalsen (2001) and other literature.

> "the students in our study explained the formation of seasons as “ the shadow of the Moon ” and “ the clouds preventing the rays of the Sun ”."
