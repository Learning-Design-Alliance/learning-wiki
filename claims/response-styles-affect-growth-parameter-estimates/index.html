Evidence¶

James Soland 2020¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld. (2020). Do response styles affect estimates of growth on social-emotional constructs? Evidence from four years of longitudinal survey scores. Multivariate Behavioral Research. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/do-response-styles-affect-estimates-of-growth-on-social-emotional-constructs-evidence-from-four-years-of-longitudinal-survey-scores-journal/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Empirical and simulation analyses scored four years of longitudinal survey data with IRT models that do and do not account for response styles, then compared growth-model results. The authors report that "response styles can affect estimates of growth parameters including the slope."

"Generally, we found that response styles can affect estimates of growth parameters including the slope, but that the effects vary by psychological construct, response style, and IRT model used."
