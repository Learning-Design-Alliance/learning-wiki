## Evidence

### Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage 2016

Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage. (2016). Developing an Action Concept Inventory. arXiv:1605.01786. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Misconception analysis during ACI development found some students, applying classical conceptions to the explore all paths concept, believed "there is only one path that particles can take"; the article notes this deeply quantum mechanical idea conflicts with everyday experience.

> "In applying classical conceptions some students believed that there is only one path that particles can take."

### Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage 2016 (2)

Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage. (2016). Developing an Action Concept Inventory. arXiv:1605.01786. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Student interview evidence found some students assumed the stationary path's amplitude magnitude exceeds other paths', although "the magnitude of the amplitude of all paths is the same".

> "We also found that some stu- dents assumed that the magnitude of the amplitude of the stationary path is greater than that of other paths, even though the magnitude of the amplitude of all paths is the same."

### Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage 2016 (3)

Lachlan P. McGinness and C. M. Savage. (2016). Developing an Action Concept Inventory. arXiv:1605.01786. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010133)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interview and assessment-task analysis identified the most common probability misconception as echoing Max Born's famous 1926 error: that probability is "the magnitude of the complex amplitude, rather than its magnitude squared", which Born corrected in a footnote in proof.

> "This misconception is that the proba- bility is the magnitude of the complex amplitude, rather than its magnitude squared."
