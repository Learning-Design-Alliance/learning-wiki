## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Among children without reading difficulties, the group-by-time interaction in the oddity task showed larger effect sizes in the two rhythm-trained groups than in the phonological awareness-only group. [→ Gutiérrez-Palma 2026](#gutierrez-palma-2026)

## Evidence

### Gutiérrez-Palma 2026

Gutiérrez-Palma, N., Calet, N., Robles-Bello, M.A., & Valencia-Naranjo, N. (2026). The role of rhythm in learning to read in children with and without reading difficulties: an intervention study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102273

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Non-parametric longitudinal analysis of children without reading difficulties; Table 1 prints simple-effect effect sizes of Cliff's delta = .53 (PAR group) and Cliff's delta = .48 (RM group) on the oddity task, with the interaction significant at p < .01 (ATS = 4.76).

> "However, an interaction was obtained in the oddity task, and the effect size was larger in the two groups in which rhythm was trained."
