## Evidence

### David W Chadwick 1999

David W Chadwick, Rana Tassabehji and Andrew Young. (1999). Experiences of Using a Public Key Infrastructure for the Preparation of Examination Papers. IT Institute, University of Salford. http://www.salford.ac.uk/its024/X500.htm

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

User-difficulty analysis from the pilot: Entrust passwords required a minimum of 8 characters including one numerical and one uppercase, and three of six ITI originators forgot theirs. The authors note users who remembered theirs reused a password they used elsewhere.

> "Given that 50% of our users forgot their passwords in the short time between installation and training and actual use, a way needs to be identified to ensure that users do not forget their passwords, or do not need them."
