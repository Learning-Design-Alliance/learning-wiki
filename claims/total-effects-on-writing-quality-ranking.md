## Evidence

### Kim Young-Suk Grace 2017

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Schatschneider Christopher. (2017). Expanding the developmental models of writing: A direct and indirect effects model of developmental writing (DIEW). Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000129

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 3 of the SEM study with 193 Grade 1 children displays direct, indirect, and total effects of component skills on writing based on the final complete mediation model. Discourse-level oral language, spelling, and handwriting had direct effects; the other skills' effects were indirect.

> "The largest effects were found for discourse-level oral language (.46), working memory (.43), and spelling (.37), followed by vocabulary (.19), handwriting (.17), theory of mind (.12), inference (.10), and grammatical knowledge (.10)."
