## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors interpret the generally high counseling skills reported by respondents in both countries as possibly due to the history of SCI-specific physical activity promotion projects conducted in both countries. [→ Kuipers L 2024](#kuipers-l-2024)

## Evidence

### Kuipers L 2024

Kuipers L, Hoekstra T, SCI Physical Activity Counseling Panel, Hoekstra F. (2024). Professionals' knowledge, skills and confidence on using the best practices for spinal cord injury physical activity counseling in Canada and the Netherlands. The Journal of Spinal Cord Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/10790268.2024.2391595

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, not a tested result: community-based PA programs exist in Canada including tele-health, peer-delivered and group-based programs, and Dutch SCI counseling support is structurally integrated into rehabilitation care via PA counseling centers.

> "The generally high scores of counselors' knowledge, skills, and confidence found in this study may be explained by the many SCI-specific PA-promoting activities developed, studied, and implemented in Canada (9,11,26) and the Netherlands (9)."
