Final regression model (Table 2, N = 828) of the chain mediation analysis. The article reports general self-efficacy with a coefficient near zero ("b = ‐0.01") and a confidence interval including zero; the four non-significant indirect paths all involved self-efficacy, and moderator models were also non-significant.
