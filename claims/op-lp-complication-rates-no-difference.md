### Oomen M. W. N. 2010

Oomen M. W. N., Hoekstra L. T., Bakx R., Heij H. A. (2010). Learning curves for pediatric laparoscopy: how many operations are enough? The Amsterdam experience with laparoscopic pyloromyotomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-010-0880-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective cohort of 229 infants (158 OP, 71 LP) operated between January 2002 and September 2008. The study reports "not significantly different" complication rates of 21.5% versus 21.1% with p = .947; no effect size is printed, and equivalence was not tested.
