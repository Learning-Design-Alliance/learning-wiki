## Evidence

### S. G. J. Mochrie 2015

S. G. J. Mochrie. (2015). Vision and change in introductory physics for the life sciences. [doi:10.1119/1.4947003](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4947003)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

The same nine-year SET dataset shows "the work load of PHYS 170 is about one standard deviation greater than those of these three classes" (PHYS 180, PHYS 200, PHYS 260; Cohen's d = 1.6, 0.97, 0.95), while PHYS 150's work load is more than two standard deviations below the others (d = 2.6, 2.8, 1.8). This workload difference contextualizes the comparable overall rating.

> "the work load of PHYS 170 is about one standard deviation greater than those of these three classes. (Cohen's d = 2.6, 2.8, and 1.8 for the difference between the work loads of PHYS 180, PHYS 200 and PHYS 260, respectively, and PHYS 150, while Cohen's d = 1.6, 0.97, and 0.95 for difference in the work loads of PHYS 180, PHYS 200, and PHYS 260, respectively, and PHYS 170)."
