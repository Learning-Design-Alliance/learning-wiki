Wang Yuanmei, Chen Gang, Chen Jie, Jiang Fan. (2024). Application of Mini-CEX combined with DOPS in standardized training of community outpatient residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05739-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures comparison of Mini-CEX scores for 20 general-practice residents at enrollment, 2 weeks and 4 weeks, analyzed with ANOVA in SPSS 30.0. The article reports the improvement was "statistically significant difference of p < 0.05"; Table 1 shows all items at p < 0.001. No effect size is printed.
