The review reports adherence figures from the 2019 Australian Stroke Foundation national stroke audit: 48% for fever treatment, 30% for insulin within one hour, and swallow screening before food or drink in only half of patients. The review attributes similar deficiencies in other settings to nursing oversights.

> "the 2019 Australian Stroke Foundation’s national stroke audit revealed that only 48% of patients with fever received acetaminophen/paracetamol within the recommended one-hour window, and just 30% of those needing insulin were treated within the same timeframe. Even more concerning, only half of acute stroke patients underwent a swallow screen before consuming food or drink"
