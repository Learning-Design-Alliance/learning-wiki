### Pan Wenwen 2025

Pan Wenwen, Zhang Hongbao, Xu Long. (2025). Association between physical activity and academic achievement in adolescents mediated by self-concept and physical and mental health. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-18559-w

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis (5000 resamples) in the 458-adolescent survey; Table 5 reports the PA→PMH→AA pathway as "Supported" with a Bootstrap 95% CI of 0.025 to 0.090.

> "Specifically, the indirect effect of PA on AA through PMH was β = 0.052, accounting for 14.86% of the total effect. These findings are in agreement with those reported by Wang and Guo29"
