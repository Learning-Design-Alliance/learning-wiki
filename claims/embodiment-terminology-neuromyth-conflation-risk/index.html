Toward a unified vocabulary for embodiment research: challenges and solutions. npj Science of Learning 11:36. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00413-x

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The authors argue that when movement-based learning is used loosely, educators may dismiss effective interventions such as encouraging students to gesture when explaining mathematical ideas. The reverse error—investing in ineffective practices borrowing embodiment rhetoric—is also described. This is an interpretive argument, not a tested result.

"Crucially, terminological ambiguity also risks con ﬂating evidence- based embodied learning with debunked neuromyths such as“kinesthetic learning styles”36."
