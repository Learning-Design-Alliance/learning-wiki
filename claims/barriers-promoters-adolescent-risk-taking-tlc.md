# Participants identified performance concerns, classroom distractions, unclear purpose of learning, and time/coverage pressures as barriers to adolescent healthy risk-taking, with relevance and feeling heard as promoters

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Barriers to exploration included performance concerns, behavioral distractions, disconnect regarding the purpose of learning, and perceived societal restrictions around time and coverage. [→ Iwatani 2023](#iwatani-2023)
`q1 i?` Promoters of exploration included interactive student-driven assignments, relevance to students, students feeling heard and seen, and envisioning a desirable future goal. [→ Iwatani 2023 (2)](#iwatani-2023-2)
