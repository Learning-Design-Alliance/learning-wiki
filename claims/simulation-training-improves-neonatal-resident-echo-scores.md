## Evidence

### Singh Yogen 2025

Singh Yogen, Ramanathan Rangasamy, Ebrahimi Mahmood, Noori Shahab. (2025). A narrative review of Neonatal Echocardiography Simulator Training (NEST): role of simulators in acquiring neonatal echocardiography skills effectively. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-025-04321-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a multicentered randomized controlled trial by Blanchetière and colleagues comparing a control group (theoretical and bedside training) with a simulation group (theoretical training, a 3-hour simulation session and bedside training), among "17 controls and 35 simulation group participants". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "at 3 months residents in the simulation group exhibited a higher mean score for both the reference score (11.5 ± 2.3 points versus 7.4 ± 3.4 points, P < 0.001) and the custom-made score (25.8 ± 5.3 points versus 16.9 ± 7.8 points, P < 0.001)"
