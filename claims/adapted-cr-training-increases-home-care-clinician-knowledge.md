## Evidence

### Feinberg JL 2017

Feinberg JL, Russell D, Mola A, Bowles KH, Lipman TH. (2017). Developing an Adapted Cardiac Rehabilitation Training for Home Care Clinicians. Journal of Cardiopulmonary Rehabilitation and Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1097/HCR.0000000000000228

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post paired sample t test on a 15-item knowledge test completed by 41 clinicians (RNs, PTs, OTs) at one certified home health agency. Mean difference 1.81 ± 1.42, 95% CI 1.37-2.27, "P < .001"; the design was a single-group pretest/posttest evaluation, so causal strength is limited.

> "Posttraining knowledge test scores improved an average of 1.8 points out of a total possible score of 15 points (pretraining and posttraining mean scores = 12.81 and 14.63, respectively; P < .001)."
