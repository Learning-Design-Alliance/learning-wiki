Evidence¶

Dunn 2019¶

Dunn, L., & Ambroso, E. (2019). Balancing act: State and district roles in school improvement under ESSA. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED602966

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Document analysis by CST staff of 23 state ESSA plans approved in 2017–18 and 2018–19. The authors report that roles "fell into four main categories" covering autonomy, oversight, resource provision, and collaboration.

"The SEA and LEA roles described in the state ESSA plans fell into four main categories: LEA Independence, SEA Oversight, SEA Resource, and SEA–LEA Collaboration."
