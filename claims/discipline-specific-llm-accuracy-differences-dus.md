## Evidence

### Dundar Sari Makbule Buse 2026

Dundar Sari Makbule Buse, Sezer Berkant. (2026). Comparative performance evaluation of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini Advanced in the Turkish Dentistry Specialization Exam. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08621-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square comparisons across the eight clinical dentistry disciplines in the DUS dataset, with Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc interpretation. The authors attribute the pattern to uneven distribution of specialty-specific knowledge in training corpora and varying reasoning complexity, not inherent superiority of one model.

> "Statistically significant results were found in favor of ChatGPT-4o in Prosthetic Dentistry (p = 0.013), where its accuracy was 10.2% points higher, and in Dentomaxillofacial Radiology (p = 0.001), with a 15.1% advantage. Conversely, Gemini Advanced had significantly higher accuracy in Pediatric Dentistry (p = 0.008)"
