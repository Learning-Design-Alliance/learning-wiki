q2 · i? · theoretical · r?

The review reports Byrd's (2017) race-focusing study, which used interracial interactions and school racial socialization to develop a multidimensional school racial climate scale and examined its relations to student motivational outcomes.

"Findings suggest positive relationships between the subscales of her school racial climate measure and key motivational constructs such as interest, belonging, and utility value"
