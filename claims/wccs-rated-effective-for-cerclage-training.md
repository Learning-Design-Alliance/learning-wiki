## Evidence

### Buechel J 2024

Buechel J, Kalisz A, Herbert S-L, Scherer-Quenzer A, Blau-Schneider B, Starrach T, Kraft K, Wöckel A, Pecks U, Kiesel M. (2024). Development and validation of a cost-effective DIY simulation model for McDonald cerclage training. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-024-07812-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire evaluation by 13 obstetricians with prior McDonald cerclage experience, using a 7-point Likert scale. The article reports the effectiveness rating was "Mean 6.92, standard deviation 0.29" and notes the material does not meet all criteria of real tissue.

> "All participants noted that the simulator was effective for training stitches for McDonald cerclage (rating Mean 6.92, standard deviation 0.29), providing a high degree of realistic challenges, despite the simulator material not meeting all criteria of real tissue."
