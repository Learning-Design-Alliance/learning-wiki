`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-skill OSCE comparison at 12 months post-training in the cluster randomized controlled trial (arm 1, n1 = 172; arm 2, n2 = 127), described in the Results text accompanying Figure 5. The text reports "better post-training assessment scores" for the named skills, p < 0.05.

> "Figure 5 shows that overall for BEmONC skills, the LDHF/m-mentoring arms had better post-training assessment scores at 12 months post-training for assisting normal birth (including care of the newborn), AMTSL, manual removal of placenta, bimanual compression of the uterus, abdominal aortic compression, PEE management (p < 0.05)."
