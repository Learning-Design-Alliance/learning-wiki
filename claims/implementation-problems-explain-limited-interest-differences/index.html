Laine et al. 2017¶

Erkka Laine, Marjaana Veermans, Aleksi Lahti, Koen Veermans. (2017). Generation of student interest in an inquiry-based mobile learning environment. Frontline Learning Research Vol.5 No. 4 (2017) 42 - 60. https://doi.org/10.14786/flr.v5i4.306

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Focus group interview data from the experimental group; students also criticized lessons where the teacher had merely replicated ordinary classes with books, notebooks and handouts, using the tablet minimally.

"In relation to pedagogy, students noted that teachers were not always up to speed with the new technology sometimes the students even needed to guide them on technical issues."

Erkka Laine, Marjaana Veermans, Aleksi Lahti, Koen Veermans. (2017). Generation of student interest in an inquiry-based mobile learning environment. Frontline Learning Research Vol.5 No. 4 (2017) 42 - 60. https://doi.org/10.14786/flr.v5i4.306

q1 · i? · causal · r1
