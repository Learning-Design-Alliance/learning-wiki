### Kruger N 2025

Kruger N, Plinsinga M, Window P, Hayes S, Bunzli S. (2025). Comfortable knowing little about lymphoedema? A qualitative study of medical interns in Australia. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-089789

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Reflexive thematic analysis of 12 semistructured interviews with first-postgraduate-year interns in Australia identified ambivalence as the overarching theme, with the participant asking "Do I know enough to be providing good patient care?" Four themes captured it.

> "The overarching theme was one ambivalence: Do I know enough to be providing good patient care? Maybe I don’t, maybe I do (Participant 5). Ambivalence was captured in four themes: ‘gaps in knowledge’, ‘low expectation to know about lymphoedema’, ‘unclear relevance to the intern role’ and ‘low sense of responsibility towards lymphoedema care’"
