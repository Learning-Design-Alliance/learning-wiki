Evidence¶

Mostafavi and Vahdany 2016¶

Fatemeh Mostafavi, Fereidoon Vahdany. (2016). The Effect of Explicit Affective Strategy Training on Iranian EFL learners' Oral Language Proficiency and Anxiety Reduction. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.4p.197

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on second FLCAS administration: experimental mean 3.73 (SD .72) vs control mean 2.55 (SD .97), n=30 each; t=5.30, df=58, p=.00, mean difference 1.17. No effect size was printed.

"The level of anxiety control for the experimental group is statistically different and higher than that of the control group. Thus, the second null hypothesis was rejected"
