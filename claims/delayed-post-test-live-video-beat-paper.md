## Evidence

### Weidenbusch M 2019

Weidenbusch M, Lenzer B, Sailer M, Strobel C, Kunisch R, Kiesewetter J, Fischer MR, Zottmann JM. (2019). Can clinical case discussions foster clinical reasoning skills in undergraduate medical education? A randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025973

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Delayed post-test 2 weeks after the three weekly sessions in the same RCT. The format effect persisted, "F(2,87)=30.91, p<0.001, partial η2=0.415"; Live-CCD vs Video-CCD was not significant (p=0.146).

> "Two weeks after course completion, the effect of the teaching format was still found in a delayed knowledge application post-test, F(2,87)=30.91, p<0.001, partial η2=0.415. Both Live-CCD (M=13.36; SD=3.23) and Video-CCD (M=11.84; SD=2.92) outperformed the Paper-Cases group (M=7.89; SD=2.41)."
