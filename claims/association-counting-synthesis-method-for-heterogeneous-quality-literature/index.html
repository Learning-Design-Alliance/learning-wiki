Steven J. Howard, Kate L. Lewis, Emma Walter, Irina Verenikina, & Lisa K. Kervin. (2024). Measuring the Quality of Adult–Child Interactions in the Context of ECEC: a Systematic Review on the Relationship with Developmental and Educational Outcomes. q2 · i? · review · r3

Methods statement: the review determined "the number of positive, negative and null associations" for each interaction/outcome category, since aggregating effect sizes across disparate measures "would have unclear meaning and utility".

"Yet, in fields where diversity in meth- ods and measures is high, as in the current review, meta-analysis often is not rec- ommended (indeed, prior meta-analyses have typically been confined to research on particular quality measures)."
