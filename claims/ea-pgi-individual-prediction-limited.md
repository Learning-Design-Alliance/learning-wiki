`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of European-ancestry Add Health and HRS prediction samples plotted by PGI decile (Fig. 2b). The article reports the decile prevalence contrast and states that "the PGI cannot be used to meaningfully predict an individual's EA."

> "only 7.3% and 6.8% of individuals in the lowest PGI decile have a college degree in Add Health and HRS, respectively, compared to 70.7% and 53.0% in the highest PGI decile."
