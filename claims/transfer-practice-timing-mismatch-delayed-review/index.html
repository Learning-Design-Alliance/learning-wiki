transfer-funnel¶

Harry Feng, Yuan Tian, and Erica Zhao. 2026. From Answer Generators to Reasoning Facilitators: Designing AI Tutors for Mathematical Reasoning in High-Stakes Environments. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01692

q2 · i? · design · r2

Telemetry transfer funnel from the field deployment. The article reports "93 transfer-card exposures" with 8 cards opened and 4 practice questions launched, interpreted as a timing mismatch rather than lack of perceived value.

"We observed 93 transfer-card exposures; students opened 8 cards (8.6% of exposures) and launched 4 practice questions (4.3% of exposures)."

transfer-funnel (2)¶

Harry Feng, Yuan Tian, and Erica Zhao. 2026. From Answer Generators to Reasoning Facilitators: Designing AI Tutors for Mathematical Reasoning in High-Stakes Environments. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01692

q2 · i? · design · r3

Interview finding from the same field study: participants P09 and P10 wanted saved problems organized into a knowledge-map wrong-book for weekend spaced retrieval, which telemetry on selective saving corroborated.

"Students like KX (P09) and TYK (P10) still wanted AITutor to organize these problems into a knowledge-map "wrong-book" for delayed retrieval practice during weekend reviews."
