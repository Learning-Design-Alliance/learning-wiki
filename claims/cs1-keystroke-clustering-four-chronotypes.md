## Subclaims
`q2 i?` K-means clustering of four-dimensional keystroke-timing vectors identifies morning, napper, afternoon and evening clusters in both a US and a European CS1 context, similar to Putilov et al.'s chronotypes. [→ Albina Zavgorodniaia 2021](#albina-zavgorodniaia-2021)

## Evidence

### Albina Zavgorodniaia 2021

Albina Zavgorodniaia, Raj Shrestha, Juho Leinonen, Arto Hellas, John Edwards. (2021). Morning or Evening? An Examination of Circadian Rhythms of CS1 Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Unsupervised k-means clustering (k=4, chosen by elbow method) of keystroke-percentage feature vectors from 519 US and 318 European students. The authors note the clusters' "high similarity to the Putilov's et al. [23] chronotypes" and assigned the same terms; cluster centroids were similar across contexts (Table II).

> "Due to distinctive differences between the discovered clusters and their high similarity to the Putilov's et al. [23] chronotypes, we assigned the same terms to the clusters"
