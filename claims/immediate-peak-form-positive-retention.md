## Subclaims
`q2 i2` A higher proportion of immediate peak curves was significantly positively associated with the problem-solving retention test (r = .35) and marginally with the transfer test (r = .21, q = .098). [→ Baker et al. 2013](#baker-et-al-2013)

## Evidence

### Baker et al. 2013

Ryan S. Baker, Arnon Hershkovitz, Lisa M. Rossi, Adam B. Goldstein & Sujith M. Gowda (2013). Predicting Robust Learning With the Visual Form of the Moment-by-Moment Learning Curve. Journal of the Learning Sciences, 22:4, 639-666. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2013.836653

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of curve-form prevalence against four posttests in the 72-student genetics tutor dataset with false discovery rate control. The immediate peak form was significantly associated with retention (r = .35) and only marginally with transfer (r = .21), suggesting relationships between quick learning and high achievement per the authors.

> "The immediate peak curve form was statistically significantly positively associated with the problem-solving retention test, r = .35, F(1, 71) = 9.73, q = .01. It was also marginally statistically significantly associated with the transfer test, r = .21, F(1, 71) = 3.40, q = .098."
