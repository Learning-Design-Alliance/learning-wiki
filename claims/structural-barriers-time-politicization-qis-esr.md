### Josephine Meyer 2022

Josephine Meyer, Noah Finkelstein, and Bethany Wilcox. (2022). Ethics education in the quantum information science classroom: Exploring attitudes, barriers, and opportunities. Submitted to the 2022 ASEE Annual Conference & Exposition. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2202.01849](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2202.01849)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the six-faculty interview study, as many as 5 interviewees expressed consternation that their typically 1-semester elective courses lacked time for technical content; one instructor also avoided contentious issues given political polarization. The paper reports the time concern quoted above as a common structural barrier.

> "A common concern was that incorporating issues of ESR into the QIS classroom would further reduce the already limited amount o f time available in the course for core technical content."
