## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across twelve majors, SAT correlates with upper division in-major GPA at values ranging from about 0.35 to 0.5, with an all-majors correlation of 0.40. [→ Stephen D.H. Hsu 2010](#stephen-dh-hsu-2010)

## Evidence

### Stephen D.H. Hsu 2010

Stephen D.H. Hsu, James Schombert. (2010). Data Mining the University: College GPA Predictions from SAT Scores. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.1589792](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.1589792)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 4,420 University of Oregon students' records plotted as a density distribution of combined SAT versus upper division GPA (Fig. 5), showing "a clear trend for increasing SAT score with increasing GPA (R=0.40)"; per-major correlations in Table 2 range from 0.36 to 0.49 (CIS 0.00).
