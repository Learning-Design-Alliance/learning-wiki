# The EmMa course significantly reduced early childhood teachers' static orientation towards mathematics

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The EmMa course produced a significant interaction effect between time of measurement and treatment group on static orientation towards mathematics, with the intervention group decreasing more than the control group. [→ Bruns et al. 2017](#bruns-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Bruns et al. 2017

Julia Bruns, Lars Eichen, Hedwig Gasteiger. (2017). Mathematics-related Competence of Early Childhood Teachers Visiting a Continuous Professional Development Course: An Intervention Study. Mathematics Teacher Education and Development, 19(3), 76-93. MERGA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1163878

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measure ANOVA on static orientation beliefs (6-point Likert questionnaire) in the same quasi-experimental study found a significant interaction effect between time of measurement and treatment group (F(1, 39) = 7.99, p = 0.007, ηp2 = .17), with intervention group means decreasing from t1 (M = 4.30, SD = 0.75) to t2 (M = 3.91, SD = 0.83).

> "We found, however, a significant interaction effect between the time of measurement of the static orientation and the treatment group (F(1, 39) = 7.99, p = 0.007, ηp2 = .17)."
