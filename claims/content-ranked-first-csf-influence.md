Ranking derived from aggregated means in Table 5 of the meta-analysis of nine studies. The article states "content is considered to have the most influence, followed by technical competence of learners, user friendly design, learner community development, and ownership"; net means were 3.958, 3.848, 3.646, 3.564, and 3.499 respectively.
