### Chytiris S 2024

Chytiris S, Teliti M, Croce L, Coperchini F, Grillini B, Cerutti M, Fonte R, Magri F, Rotondi M. (2024). Proficiency in performing radiofrequency ablation procedure for non-functioning benign thyroid nodules: a qualitative rather than quantitative matter. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2024.1399912

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective single-operator cohort analysis of the first 179 consecutive RFA procedures, with CUSUM charts targeting VRR 50% and control limits at ±20 SD. The analysis "changes in the slope could be observed after 39 and 145 procedures" yielding three phases: initial learning, consolidation, and an experienced period from case 146.

> "Based on the CUSUM analysis of the 179 consecutive procedures, changes in the slope could be observed after 39 and 145 procedures, allowing to divide the LC into 3 distinct phases."
