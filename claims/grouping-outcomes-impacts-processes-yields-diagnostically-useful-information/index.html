The report's framing statement asserts that organizing performance measure data into the three complementary groupings "provides rich, fair, and diagnostically useful information." No empirical test or data analysis is reported in the available text; this is the authors' design argument.

"Organizing valid, reliable, and robust performance measure data into complementary groupings of student outcomes, school impacts on outcomes, and processes that drive those impacts provides rich, fair, and diagnostically useful information."
