## Evidence

### Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan 2015

Arezoo Shirazi and Amir H. Behzadan. (2015). Content Delivery Using Augmented Reality to Enhance Students' Performance in a Building Design and Assembly Project. Advances in Engineering Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1076141

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary-measure results from the same two-session experiment: control-group "building cost and embodied carbon is significantly less" than the test group's, while fire resistance showed no statistical difference considering CV values. The authors attribute the secondary-measure disadvantage to repetitive device scanning under time pressure.

> "the average building cost and embodied carbon is significantly less for the control group (session 1) than the test group (session 2). However, no statistical difference was observed between the average fire resistance factors for both groups, considering the CV values."
