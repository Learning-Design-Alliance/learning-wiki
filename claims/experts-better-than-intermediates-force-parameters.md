## Evidence

### Rahimi A. Masie 2023

Rahimi A. Masie, Hardon Sem F., Willuth E., Lang F., Haney Caelan M., Felinska Eleni A., Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Müller-Stich Beat P., Horeman Tim, Nickel F., Daams Freek. (2023). Force-based assessment of tissue handling skills in simulation training for robot-assisted surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-09905-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparisons in the same 60-participant multicenter suturing study showed "the experts showed better results in mean non-zero force" and two further force parameters compared to intermediates; only p-values and descriptive medians are printed, no effect sizes.

> "Furthermore, the experts showed better results in mean non-zero force (22 N vs 13 N p = 0.015), maximum impulse (24 Ns vs 17 Ns p = 0.043) and force volume (25 N3 vs 16 N3 p = 0.025) compared to the intermediates (p ≤ 0.05)"
