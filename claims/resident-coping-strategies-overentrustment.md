Thematic analysis of the resident interviews identified instrumental coping for situations where residents felt overentrusted with clinical tasks; one resident described using experienced peer residents and a psychologist as substitute "attendings" when the attending was unavailable.

> "Specifically, they mentioned instrumental coping strategies, including planning differently, reaching out to other attending physicians, or discussing cases with different health professionals:"
