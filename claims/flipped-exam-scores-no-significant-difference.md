## Evidence

### Galway Lindsay P 2014

Galway Lindsay P, Corbett Kitty K, Takaro Timothy K, Tairyan Kate, Frank Erica. (2014). A novel integration of online and flipped classroom instructional models in public health higher education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-181

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-year comparison of an 11-question exam marked by the same individual: 2013 flipped students (n=11) vs 2011 lecture-based students (n=22), analyzed with a Mann–Whitney U test. The article prints no effect size, so the null is reported as no statistically significant difference.

> "The mean test score was 88.8% for the 2013 students and 86.4% for the 2011 students. A Mann–Whitney U test revealed no statistically significant difference (p = 0.72) between examination scores across the two groups ."
