`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 3 of the interview study reports extensive upfront costs including capital equipment purchases, frustration during cocreation with technology companies, and ongoing costs to maintain software through updates or licensing.

> "Implementation of a VR simulation curriculum required extensive resources, particularly upfront costs (funding and time commitments)."

### Jordan Talan 2025 (2)

Jordan Talan, MHPE, MD; Forster Molly, MD; Joseph Leian, MD; Pradhan Deepak, MHPE, MD. (2025). Exploring the Role of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation in Health Professions Education: Thematic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/62803

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 3 participant quote on barriers to sharing software programs; others felt restricted by marketplace incentives and compatibility concerns between different software or hardware companies.

> "There’s no great way to share content yet. So a lot of stuff is just getting reinvented over and over again, which is a really expensive way to do things."
