# NBA Math Hoops can be implemented at low marginal cost: $17.85 per pupil (analytic sample) in materials and training beyond typical summer programming, yielding 0.19 SD math gain

## Evidence

### McKinney 2026

McKinney, D., Walters, K., Powell, K., Sabalburo, E., & Morton, C. (2026). The impact of NBA Math Hoops on students' math achievement: A randomized controlled trial. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681343

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cost analysis based on program records and study data: total per-pupil cost $184.41 (analytic) or $98.57 (total sample); marginal cost of materials and training $17.85 or $11.37 per pupil respectively. Virtual training option would cost under 35 cents per student.

> "Assuming that NBA Math Hoops is integrated into typical summer programming, the cost-effectiveness is $92.99 per pupil (for the analytic sample) and $59.23 per pupil (for the study sample) to produce an increase of 1 standard deviation in student math learning."
