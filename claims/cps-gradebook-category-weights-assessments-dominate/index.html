Diaz 2021¶

Diaz, B., & Easton, J.Q. (2021). CPS Gradebook Technical Report. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/CPS-gradebook-technical-report

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of section/category family/student records (N=86,533, Table 3). The report states "our median assessment category family weighting of 60.0 percent exceeded the CPS/CTU recommendation" and that mastery-based or weekly families determined nearly all the final grade where used.

"our median assessment category family weighting of 60.0 percent exceeded the CPS/CTU recommendation that no single category should exceed 50 percent of the total weighting"
