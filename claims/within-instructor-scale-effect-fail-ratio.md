## Evidence

### Webb 2020

Webb, D. J., Paul, C. A., & Chessey, M. K. (2020). Relative impacts of diﬀerent grade-scales on student success in introductory physics. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020114](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020114)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-instructor comparison of the seven of 60 instructors who used both grade scales at different times, holding the instructor constant. Ratios changed only slightly when incoming GPA was added as a covariate, and the authors read this as "evidence against either instructor or students as causes of the effects."

> "Table III shows that these seven instructors gave between 4 and 12 times more course grades less than C − under percent grading than under 4-point grading and between 2 and 11 times as many non-zero F’s on individual exam items."
