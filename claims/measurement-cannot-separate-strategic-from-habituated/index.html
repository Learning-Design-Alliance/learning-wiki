Evidence¶

Patricia A. Alexander 2017¶

Patricia A. Alexander. (2017). Issues of Constructs, Contexts, and Continuity: Commentary on Learning in Higher Education. Educational Psychology Review, 29, 345–351. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9409-3

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the commentary about measurement limits: relying on self-report "cannot illuminate those differences in compelling or convincing ways," and "Even data-capture techniques like eye tracking cannot reliably tell" whether behavior was habituated or planned. Researchers are left to presume strategic behavior. No empirical test is offered.

"Even data-capture techniques like eye tracking cannot reliably tell whether the documented behavior represented the learners'habituated actions or some planned maneuver."
