Evidence¶

Kim Stienstra 2024¶

Kim Stienstra, Antonie Knigge & Ineke Maas. (2024). Gene-environment interaction analysis of school quality and educational inequality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00225-x

q2 · i? · associational · r3

ACE-moderation analysis (Model 4, Table 2) of Cito scores for 18,384 same-sex and 11,050 opposite-sex twin pairs, with parental SES added as moderator. The previously significant genetic moderation by school quality disappears: "we ﬁnd no evidence for moderation effects of school quality anymore".

"When this is considered, we ﬁnd no evidence for moderation effects of school quality anymore (Fig. 1c)."
