`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review's challenges section, citing Poon (2013) and Vaughan (2007), states that "Unrealistic expectations and feelings of isolation are the challenges to students" and that universities are challenged by time and support issues. Students in cited studies assumed fewer classes meant less work and reported feeling isolated.

> "The use of blended learning can pose challenges for students and universities. Unrealistic expectations and feelings of isolation are the challenges to students, where Universities are challenged by time and support issues. Both students and institutions encounter challenges presented by technology issues (Poon, 2013)."
