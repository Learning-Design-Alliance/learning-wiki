## Evidence

### Borman 2013

Borman, K., Murphy, S. P., LoSasso, J., & Schlanger, L. (2013). Preliminary Findings from an Efficacy Study of a Distributed Leadership Model of School Organization. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564091

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive FLDOE achievement trends from the same six-school study. Science proficiency rose from 35% to 38% in control schools versus 39% to 40% in treatment schools; the abstract also reports control schools' writing proficiency "dropped from 93% to 77%" while treatment schools' writing increased from 39% to 40%.

> "Where the mean percent of students proficient in science in control schools increased from 35% to 38%, the mean percent of students proficient in science in treatment schools increased from 39% to 40%."
