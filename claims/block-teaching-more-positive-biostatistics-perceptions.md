## Evidence

### Li Chen 2018

Li Chen, Wang Ling, Zhang Yuhai, Li Chanjuan, Xu Yongyong, Shang Lei, Xia Jielai. (2018). Assessment of a block curriculum design on medical postgraduates’ perception towards biostatistics: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1232-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cohort comparison of 499 block-taught versus 465 traditionally taught first-year postgraduates surveyed after the course with a 24-item Likert questionnaire; the block group's comment-section mean was "3.66 vs. 2.97, P < 0.01" versus the traditional group.

> "With respect to the course comment section, students’ perceptions significantly transformed from negative to positive after the block teaching reform (mean score of the Likert 5-point question for this section: 3.66 vs. 2.97, P < 0.01)."

### Li Chen 2018 (2)

Li Chen, Wang Ling, Zhang Yuhai, Li Chanjuan, Xu Yongyong, Shang Lei, Xia Jielai. (2018). Assessment of a block curriculum design on medical postgraduates’ perception towards biostatistics: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1232-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariable linear regression on the two cohorts adjusted for age, specialty, computer basics and research experience imbalances; the adjusted course-value mean score change for the block group was 0.14 (95% CI 0.07, 0.20, P < 0.01).

> "Regarding the course value section, more number of block teaching students were interested in biostatistics course than the traditional teaching students (3.68 vs. 3.48, P < 0.01) and the course value mean score increased to 0.14 due to the block teaching approach after adjusting the imbalance characteristics between two groups."
