Mihindo W. Jane 2017¶

Mihindo W. Jane, Wachanga S.W. & Anditi Z. O. (2017). Effects of Computer-Based Simulations Teaching Approach on Students’ Achievement in the Learning of Chemistry among Secondary School Students in Nakuru Sub County, Kenya. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133108

q2 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on post-test CAT scores of 175 students in the Solomon Four Non-Equivalent Control Group Design; experimental groups E1 (mean .6870) and E2 (.6200) exceeded control groups C1 (.5135) and C2 (.5480). The study reports "statistically significant F (3,171) = 9.305".
