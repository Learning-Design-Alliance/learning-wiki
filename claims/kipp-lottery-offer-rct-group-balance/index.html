Evidence¶

Thomas Coen 2019¶

Thomas Coen, Ira Nichols-Barrer, Philip Gleason. (2019). An Impact that Lasts: KIPP Middle Schools Boost College Enrollment. Cambridge, MA: Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/an-impact-that-lasts-kipp-middle-schools-boost-college-enrollment

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Design statement about the study's randomized controlled trial, in which admission offers were determined by 5th- or 6th-grade admissions lotteries. The design ensures the treatment and control groups 'are similar on average', covering observable characteristics such as prior test scores and unobservable ones such as motivation and parental support.

"students who were offered admission to a KIPP middle school (the treatment group) are similar on average to students who did not receive an offer of admission (the control group)"
