# Phonological awareness has no direct effect on higher-order QNC (Level II and above) or Grade 3 math achievement once QNC skills are modeled, confirming the isolated number-words hypothesis

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After controlling for its impact on basic numerical skills, phonological awareness did not directly predict higher-level QNC or Grade 3 mathematics achievement. [→ Krajewski 2009](#krajewski-2009)

## Evidence

### Krajewski 2009

Krajewski, K., & Schneider, W. (2009). Exploring the impact of phonological awareness, visual-spatial working memory, and preschool quantity-number competencies on mathematics achievement in elementary school: Findings from a 3-year longitudinal study. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology, 103(4), 516-531. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2009.03.009

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the initial structural equation model of the 3-year longitudinal study, phonological awareness's path to Grade 3 math (DEMAT 2+) was non-significant ( = -.10, p > .05) once QNC skills were included; its path to QNC Level II/III was also non-significant ( = .13, p > .05).

> "Neither visual-spatial sketch pad ( = -.00, p > .05), nor phonological awareness ( = -.10, p > .05) contributed significantly to the prediction of Grade 3 math performance when QNC skills were included in the model."
