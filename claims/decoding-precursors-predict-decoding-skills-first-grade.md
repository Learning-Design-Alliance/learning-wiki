## Evidence

### Jessica Massonnié 2018

Jessica Massonnié, Maryse Bianco, Laurent Lima, Pascal Bressoux. (2018). Longitudinal predictors of reading comprehension in French at first grade: Unpacking the oral comprehension component of the simple view. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.005

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal SEM on 556 French first-graders tested the continuity from decoding precursors at T1 (September–November 2014) to decoding skills at T2 (May–June 2015); the authors note the "strong predictive power, compared to Moll et al. (2014) and Ziegler et al. (2010)".

> "T1 decoding precursors strongly predicted T2 decoding skills, explaining 75% of their variance (β = .85)."
