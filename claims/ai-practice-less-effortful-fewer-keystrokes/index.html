Evidence¶

Benjamin Lira 2025¶

Benjamin Lira, Todd Rogers, Daniel G. Goldstein, Lyle Ungar, and Angela L. Duckworth. (2025). Coach not crutch: Evidence that AI can improve writing skill despite reducing effort. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02880

q4 · i0 · causal · r3

Same pre-registered experiment (N=2,238). Participants practicing with AI "spent 0.44 fewer minutes" during the practice phase (d=-.12, p=.025); the time difference was small. Self-reported effort was also lower.

"participants who practiced with the AI tool spent 0.44 fewer minutes during the practice phase (3.73 vs. 4.17;d= −.12,p= .025), logged roughly a quarter as many total keystrokes (26%d= −.44,p<.001)"
