## Evidence

### Kiuru 2020

Kiuru, N., Wang, M.-T., Salmela-Aro, K., Kannas, L., Ahonen, T., & Hirvonen, R. (2020). Associations between Adolescents' Interpersonal Relationships, School Well-being, and Academic Achievement during Educational Transitions. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-019-01184-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-lagged SEM in a longitudinal study of 848 Finnish adolescents across the primary-to-lower-secondary transition. Positive cross-lagged paths from "closeness to parents (β = 0.07, SE = 0.03, p = 0.049)" and closeness to friends to later school satisfaction, controlling for construct stabilities.

> "the cross-lagged paths from closeness to parents (β = 0.07, SE = 0.03, p = 0.049) and to school friends (β = 0.10, SE = 0.04, p = 0.006) in the fall of the sixth grade to school satisfaction in the fall of the seventh grade"

### Kiuru 2020 (2)

Kiuru, N., Wang, M.-T., Salmela-Aro, K., Kannas, L., Ahonen, T., & Hirvonen, R. (2020). Associations between Adolescents' Interpersonal Relationships, School Well-being, and Academic Achievement during Educational Transitions. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-019-01184-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same cross-lagged SEM for closeness. Negative paths from parent closeness to later school stress, as the article reports: paths "to school stress in the fall of the seventh grade" and to spring stress "were negative".

> "the cross-lagged paths from closeness to parents in the fall of the sixth grade (β = −0.12, SE = 0.05, p = 0.011) to school stress in the fall of the seventh grade, and from closeness to parents in the spring of the seventh grade (β = −0.09, SE = 0.04, p = 0.021) to school stress in the spring of the seventh grade were negative"
