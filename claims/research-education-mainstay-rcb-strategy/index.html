Evidence¶

King 2022¶

King, O., West, E., Lee, S., Glenister, K., Quilliam, C., Wong, S. A., & Beks, H. (2022). Research education and training for nurses and allied health professionals: a systematic scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03406-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

Background synthesis in the review's introduction: RCB strategies combine education and training, funding for embedded researchers, strategic collaborations and infrastructure, and research education is a sustaining feature of many. The authors link this to insufficient research coverage in undergraduate health curricula.

"RCB strategies often comprise a combination of the aforementioned approaches [8] and notably, research education and training programs are a sustaining feature of many [2, 3, 6, 8–11]."
