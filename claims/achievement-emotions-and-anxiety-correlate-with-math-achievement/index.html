Enjoyment correlates positively (r = .31), and boredom, mathematics anxiety and test anxiety negatively (r = −.23 to −.28), with mathematics achievement¶

Claim · All claims Evidence · 1 study · review r3 · q3 · i2 medium · n=30 meta-analyses, 66 correlations from 13,853 effect sizes (~4.66 million participants)

Enjoyment correlates r = .31 with mathematics achievement and boredom −.28 (Camacho-Morles et al. 2021); mathematics anxiety −.28 (Barroso et al. 2021), test anxiety −.23 and general anxiety −.19 to −.09. These are correlations compiled across meta-analyses: they describe how strongly the characteristic goes with mathematics achievement, not what causes it.

Evidence¶

Breit et al. 2025¶

Breit, M., Schneider, M., & Preckel, F. (2025). Mathematics achievement and learner characteristics: A systematic review of meta-analyses. Learning and Individual Differences, 118, 102621. doi:10.1016/j.lindif.2024.102621

q3 · systematic review of meta-analyses · i2 · medium effect, r = .31 to −.28 · n=30 meta-analyses, 66 correlations from 13,853 effect sizes (~4.66 million participants) · review · r3

The review compiles zero-order correlations from published meta-analyses (each with at least ten effect sizes, the largest one kept where several covered a variable), found by a systematic PsycINFO search in July 2024. It pools nothing further and controls for nothing: many of these characteristics correlate with one another, so a correlation here says how strongly the characteristic goes with mathematics achievement, not that it causes it. Enjoyment r = .31 (k = 20, n = 19,055) and boredom −.28 (k = 19, n = 13,534), both corrected for unreliability (Camacho-Morles et al. 2021, school and university). Mathematics anxiety −.28 (Barroso et al. 2021, k = 747, n = 385,441), weaker in older samples; test anxiety −.23 (Caviola et al. 2022, k = 88); general anxiety −.19 (Caviola et al. 2022) and −.09 (Brumariu et al. 2022).

Discussion¶

Correlations only, and the authors expect the relationship to run both ways: achievement shapes emotions as well as the reverse. See Math anxiety and math achievement for the anxiety meta-analysis itself.
