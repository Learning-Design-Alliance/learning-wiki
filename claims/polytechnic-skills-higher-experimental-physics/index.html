Imashev 2016¶

Imashev, G., Abykanova, B. T., Rakhmetova, M. T., Tumysheva, A. A., Moldasheva, R. N., Ilyasova, S. S. & Shahimova, A. A. (2016). Development of Polytechnic Knowledge and Abilities in the Course of Studying Physics. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(10), 3595-3606. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Analysis of 260 written works from experimental classes and 250 from control classes in Atyrau secondary schools, reported in Table 1. In experimental classes "31% of experimental classes’ students formed high level measuring skills, while 53% formed average level skills"; Table 1 shows higher correct-answer percentages than controls across skill categories.

"It was determined that during the performance of polytechnic tasks, 31% of experimental classes’ students formed high level measuring skills, while 53% formed average level skills."
