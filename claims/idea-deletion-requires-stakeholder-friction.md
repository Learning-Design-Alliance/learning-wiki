# Novice designers deleted ideas only when confronted with stakeholder friction; unsupported self-reflection produced no deletions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No deletions occurred in the Self Reflection group despite explicit permission, while a few participants in the guided conditions deleted ideas after encountering pushback. [→ Howard Ziyu Han 2026](#howard-ziyu-han-2026)

## Evidence

### Howard Ziyu Han 2026

Howard Ziyu Han, Nikolas Martelaro. (2026). Enacting Constructive Conflicts with AI Agents to Enhance Reconsideration among Novice Interaction Designers. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04166

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Idea-deletion coding across the 45-participant experiment: three Interactive Engagement participants deleted four ideas and two Stepwise Guidance participants deleted three; the article reports deletions appeared to occur only when participants realized friction from potential disagreement.

> "No deletions occurred in the Self Reflection group, even though participants were explicitly told that deletion was allowed."
