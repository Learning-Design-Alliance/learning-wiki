A matched comparison study during the 2023-2024 school year in a large Florida district compared 397 CAPIT pre-kindergarten students to 397 similar peers. CAPIT students "scored an average of 17.2 points higher on the FAST Star Early Literacy assessment (effect size = +0.19)".

> "The analysis compared 397 pre-kindergarten students who received CAPIT instruction to 397 demographically and academically similar peers. CAPIT students scored an average of 17.2 points higher on the FAST Star Early Literacy assessment (effect size = +0.19)"
