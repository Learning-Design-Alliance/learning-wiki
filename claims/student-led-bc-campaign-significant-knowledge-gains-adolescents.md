## Evidence

### Abbas Maram O 2026

Abbas Maram O, Beshir Semira, AlKhalidi Doaa Kamal, Rahma Azhar T, Elbarazi Iffat, AL-Zeer Rahaf, Yahya Hana, AL-Maskari Fatima. (2026). Evaluation of a student-led breast cancer awareness campaign as a co-curricular model in pharmacy education: a mixed-methods study in the UAE. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09356-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre–post study of 87 female secondary school students in Dubai using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test. The mean total knowledge score increased from 41.7 ± 15.2 to 67.6 ± 15.7, with "a large effect size (r = 0.84)".

> "The analysis showed a significant increase in total knowledge scores (Z = 7.88, p < 0.001) with a large effect size (r = 0.84)."
