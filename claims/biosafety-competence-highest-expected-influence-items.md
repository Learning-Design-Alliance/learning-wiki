### Wu Chao 2025

Wu Chao, Zha Ning, Zhang Kai, He Shuai, He Jing, Fu Mimi, Zhang Xinyan, Lang Hongjuan. (2025). The relationship between components of the biosafety incident response competence for clinical nursing staff: a network analysis. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-025-07438-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Network analysis of a cross-sectional survey of clinical nurses using a self-designed 29-item competence scale, fitted with a Gaussian graphical model via LASSO and EBIC. The results section reports that P4, M3, D5, and K5 are "the most strongly associated nodes in the current network."

> "P4, M3, D5, and K5 exhibit the highest expected influence, which statistically indicates that these variables are the most strongly associated nodes in the current network."
