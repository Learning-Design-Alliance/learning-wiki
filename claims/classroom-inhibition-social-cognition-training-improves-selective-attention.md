# A combined inhibition and social cognition classroom training improves selective visual attention in 5- and 6-year-olds

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Children who received the classroom training showed a significantly greater improvement in the Face Cancelation Task efficiency score than control children (interaction η² = 0.017). [→ Honoré N 2020](#honore-n-2020)

## Evidence

### Honoré N 2020

Honoré N, Houssa M, Volckaert A, Noël M-P and Nader-Grosbois N (2020) Training Inhibition and Social Cognition in the Classrooms. Front. Psychol. 11:1974. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01974

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA (time × group) on 121 experimental and 120 control children in a 6-month classroom intervention; the group×time interaction was significant (F = 4.01, p = 0.046, η² = 0.017), with a "greater in the experimental group" gain in efficiency score.

> "For the Face Cancelation Task (Figure 1), the improvement in ES was greater in the experimental group (0.038 ± 0.046) than in the control group (0.026 ± 0.048)."
