`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of mentee identity responses (N=76 coded segments) in the longitudinal remote REU interview study; the authors grouped responses into lower-, mid-, and higher-level identity categories.

> "During the sixth week of interviews, mentees were asked to describe their physics and researcher identity. Five mentees expressed a low-level of researcher identity."

### Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2021 (2)

Dina Zohrabi Alaee, Micah K. Campbell, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2021). Impact of virtual REU experiences on students' psychosocial gains during the COVID-19 pandemic. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Joshua's final-interview quote from the identity analysis; the authors note growing evidence that his relationship with his mentor, e.g. speaking the same language, impacted how he recognized himself as a physicist.

> "I think REU has taught me something about confidence in doing research. I think the REU has made me think of myself as a physicist."
