# Self-efficacy and the combined self-efficacy/self-esteem score rose significantly from pre-test to post-test, but self-esteem alone did not change significantly, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Average self-efficacy rose significantly from pre-test to post-test (median 3.75 to 4.13, p < 0.001) and the total combined score rose significantly from pre-test to post-test (median 3.56 to 3.75, p = 0.001), in one group with no comparison group. [→ Moustafa HAM 2024](#moustafa-ham-2024)
`q3 i?` Self-esteem scores rose from pre-test to post-test, but this change was not statistically significant (p = 0.203), in one group with no comparison group. [→ Moustafa HAM 2024 (2)](#moustafa-ham-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Moustafa HAM 2024

Moustafa HAM, Hamid AE, Hassoub G, Kassem AB. (2024). Assessing the impact of critical care training on pharmacy students in Egypt: a pre-post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison in Table 3 (n = 72): self-efficacy median 3.75 (3.31–4.19) pre vs 4.13 (3.75–4.63) post, p < 0.001; total score median 3.56 (3.28–3.78) vs 3.75 (3.50–3.94), p = 0.001, Wilcoxon signed ranks test.

> "Following the training course, the average self-efficacy students’ scores improved significantly (p < 0.05) (Table 3). There was also a significant increase in the overall average mean score of both self-efficacy and self-esteem after finishing the training course (p < 0.05)"

### Moustafa HAM 2024 (2)

Moustafa HAM, Hamid AE, Hassoub G, Kassem AB. (2024). Assessing the impact of critical care training on pharmacy students in Egypt: a pre-post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06427-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null result from Table 3 of the same pre-post study: self-esteem median 3.30 (3.05–3.70) pre vs 3.40 (3.10–3.70) post, p = 0.203; no effect size printed, equivalence not tested.

> "On the other hand, self-esteem scores displayed a notable increase, though this change was not statistically significant (Fig. 2)"
