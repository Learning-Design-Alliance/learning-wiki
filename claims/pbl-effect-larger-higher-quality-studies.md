## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Stratified by quality score, pooled SMDs were 0.70 (score 6), 0.53 (score 5) and 0.38 (score 4), with higher-quality studies yielding larger effects. [→ Ding Xiaojie 2014](#ding-xiaojie-2014)

## Evidence

### Ding Xiaojie 2014

Ding Xiaojie, Zhao Liping, Chu Haiyan, Tong Na, Ni Chunhui, Hu Zhibin, Zhang Zhengdong, Wang Meilin. (2014). Assessing the Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning of Preventive Medicine Education in China. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep05126

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of examination-score effects stratified by Newcastle-Ottawa-adapted quality scores. The article reports that "studies with high quality scores would yield significantly larger effect sizes than those with low quality scores."

> "For quality scores = 6, SMD = 0.70, 95% CI = 0.37–1.04, Pheterogeneity < 0.001; scores = 5, SMD = 0.53, 95% CI = 0.37–0.70, Pheterogeneity = 0.153; scores = 4, SMD = 0.38, 95% CI = 0.03–0.73, Pheterogeneity = 0.924"
