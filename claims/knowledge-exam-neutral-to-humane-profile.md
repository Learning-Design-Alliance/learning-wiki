## Evidence

### Abbiati Milena 2016

Abbiati Milena, Baroffio Anne, Gerbase Margaret W. (2016). Personal profile of medical students selected through a knowledge-based exam only: are we missing suitable students? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29705

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same multivariate ANOVA comparison of 311 students' PCA-derived profiles, the self-determined, sociable, and empathy-related facets showed no statistically significant differences between selected and non-selected students. Equivalence was not tested; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Other personal characteristics such as motivation, sociability, and empathy did not significantly differ, comparing selected and non-selected students."
