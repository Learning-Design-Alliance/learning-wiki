Evidence¶

Hooshang Khoshsima & Forouzan Rezaeiantiyar 2014¶

Hooshang Khoshsima & Forouzan Rezaeiantiyar. (2014). The Effect of Presentation Strategy on Reading Comprehension of Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.3p.101

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Test of homogeneity of variances (Levene statistic .171, df 82, sig .826) on the reading comprehension pre-test of the 61 participants; the article concludes the groups "were comparable" at the outset. No effect size is printed.

"As it can be seen in Table3, the reported sig. is ( P>.05) 0.826 > .05 which is larger than 0.05. So there was no significant difference between these groups at the beginning of the experiment and they were comparable."
