Feedback Without the Wait: Piloting a Generative AI Practice Platform in a Large Maths Class. 37th Annual Conference of the Australasian Association for Engineering Education, UNSW, Sydney. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of an end-of-semester survey of 53 responses, with the nine experience-based items reported for the 19 users only. The authors report means for all five constructs, e.g. "usability and efficiency (3.82)"; responses were summarised descriptively with no significance testing.

"Engagement and active learning were rated most highly (mean 3.97; 79% agreement), followed by usability and efficiency (3.82), conceptual understanding (3.68), and feedback effectiveness (3.54); while motivation and persistence was ranked the lowest (3.39; 55% agreement)."
