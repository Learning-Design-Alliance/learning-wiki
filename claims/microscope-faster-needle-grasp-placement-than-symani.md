## Evidence

### Wieker H 2025

Wieker H, Brandenburg N, Spille D, Wagner J, Weitkamp J-T, Wiltfang J, Spille J. (2025). Simulation and comparison of objectively graded microsurgery steps by using a microscope and Symani robotic system by young professionals. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02490-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject comparison of operation times in the study's first exercise, in which 40 students (18 men, 22 women) with no prior surgical experience grasped and placed a 9.0 needle with each system; the microscope mean was "00:24 min" versus 00:36 min for the Symani, a significant difference (p = 0.003). No effect size is printed.

> "The average exercise time was 00:24 min (minimum 00:09 min; maximum 01:12 min) for the operation step by using the microscope and 00:36 min (minimum 00:15 min; maximum 02:08 min) using the Symani. There was a significant difference in the operation time (p = 0.003)."
