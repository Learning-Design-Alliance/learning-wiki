## Evidence

### M.A. Kraus 2022

M.A. Kraus, R. Rust, M. Rietschel, D. Hall. (2022). Improved Perception of AEC Construction Details via Immersive Teaching in Virtual Reality. Preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.10617](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.10617)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Welch's t-tests on the same 41-participant experiment, restricted to the professional subgroup. The article concludes the VRE "did not improve learning for them", while noting their control-group scores were more widely distributed.

> "However, for professionals, thep values are 0.9161 and 0.775 and therefore the null hypothesis can not be rejected in that case, so we have to assume that the VRE did not improve learning for them."
