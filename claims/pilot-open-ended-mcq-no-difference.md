## Evidence

### Buljan Ivan 2021

Buljan Ivan, Marušić Matko, Tokalić Ružica, Viđak Marin, Peričić Tina Poklepović, Hren Darko, Marušić Ana. (2021). Cognitive levels in testing knowledge in evidence-based medicine: a cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02449-y

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot with second-year medical students randomized to open-ended versus multiple-choice versions of the same test questions; median scores were 19 versus 20 with no significant difference (Mann-Whitney, P = 0.722). The authors chose the multiple-choice format for simpler, more objective scoring. Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "In the pilot study, no difference was found between the groups that took the test with open- ended answers (Md = 19, 95% CI = 16 to 19, n = 46) and those that took the test with multiple choice answers (Md = 20, 95% CI = 18 to 21, n = 42; P = 0.722)."
