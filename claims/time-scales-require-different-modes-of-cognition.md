## Evidence

### H. P. A. Boshuizen 2016

H. P. A. Boshuizen. (2016). Teaching as regulation and dealing with complexity. Instructional Science, 44, 311–314. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9377-x

`q1 · i?`

Theoretical argument in the editorial, drawing on Eraut's (1994) four processes of reading the situation, decision making, overt action, and metacognitive awareness. The article offers this as an analytical account, not a tested result: "instant events require reﬂexive action".

> "The different time scales of the events taking place within one situation require different modes of cognition: instant events require reﬂexive action (if any), rapidly evolving events ask for intuitive decision making and actions, while ‘slow’ events allow a deliberative and analytic approach."
