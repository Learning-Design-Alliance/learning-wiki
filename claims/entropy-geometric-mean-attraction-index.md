## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The geometric mean of 2^H(Xs) and relative viewing time measures attraction to a learner, profiling diminishing marginal utility of repeated viewing and increasing information from new videos. [→ Zheng Xie 2018](#zheng-xie-2018)

## Evidence

### Zheng Xie 2018

Zheng Xie, Xiao Xiao, Jianping Li, Jinying Su. (2018). Profiling MOOCs from viewing perspective. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01698)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Definitional and schematic illustration (Section 4) of the index I(Xs), the geometric mean of entropy-based video count and relative viewing time; the article states it "profiles the diminishing marginal utility in learning" and the increasing information of viewing new videos.

> "As above schematic examples showed, the geometric mean I(Xs) profiles the diminishing marginal utility in learning, because ∂2I(Xs)/∂(tsi )2 < 0."

### Zheng Xie 2018 (2)

Zheng Xie, Xiao Xiao, Jianping Li, Jinying Su. (2018). Profiling MOOCs from viewing perspective. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01698)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Numerical results over all learners of the eight courses shown in Fig. 3: the average geometric mean "positively correlated to the number of videos viewed by per learner and to the viewing time length per learner".

> "Fig. 3 showed that this average positively correlated to the number of videos viewed by per learner and to the viewing time length per learner, which fits the common sense: view more and longer, be attracted deeper."
