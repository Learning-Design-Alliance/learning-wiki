## Evidence

### Bowman K 2023

Bowman K, Cadman T, Goncalves Soares A, Robinson O, Hughes A, Heron J, Segal AB, Huerta MC, Howe LD. (2023). Mediators of the association between childhood body mass index and educational attainment: Analysis of a UK prospective cohort study. Pediatric Obesity. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijpo.13014

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cohort mediation analysis of 6949 ALSPAC participants with clinic-measured BMI at 11.7 years and linked National Pupil Database GCSE scores, analysed separately by sex with multiple imputation. The total causal effect was "β for a one standard deviation higher BMI = −3.47" in females and −4.33 in males, both CIs excluding the null.

> "Higher BMI z‐scores at age 11.7 years were associated with lower GCSE scores (TCE: β for a one standard deviation higher BMI = −3.47 95% CI −5.54, −1.41 in females; males β = −4.33 95% CI −6.73, −1.94)"
