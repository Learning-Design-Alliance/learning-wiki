The review reports, citing Misgano et al., an association between active interest groups and a 78.6% increase in student interest, with a printed relative risk of "RR 1.3" (p < 0.05). Observational association reported second-hand, not a causal claim; no standardized effect size printed.

### Khosla Lakshay 2025 (2)

Khosla Lakshay, Keenan Kayla M., Raman Jay D. (2025). Models and Metrics for Medical Education in Urology: Medical Student Education, Resident Education, and Future Directions. Current Urology Reports. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11934-025-01308-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review describes a didactic-plus-hands-on skills session study (Mohaghegh et al.) reporting pre-post confidence gains from under 10% to 98–100%, and a separate third-year simulation curriculum reporting procedural accuracy gains from 66% to 81% (p < 0.0001). Pre-post designs; no standardized effect sizes.

> "confidence in performing bedside urologic procedures, including catheter placement and physical exams, increased from under 10% to 98–100% [21]"
