## Evidence

### Zhang Yang 2025

Zhang Yang, Chen Jie, Zhong Xiao, Shao Yongcong, Fu Weiwei. (2025). Evaluating the effectiveness of flight simulator training on developing perceptual-motor skills among flight cadets: a pilot study. Scientific Reports 15, 28062. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-12929-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of takeoff scores in a randomized two-group experiment (TTG n = 20, ETG n = 20) over baseline plus seven post-training tests. The article reports the interaction "η² = 0.06" and concludes the ETG "is able to improve takeoff performance more rapidly and overcome the training plateau".

> "a significant interaction between training method and measurement time (F (3.01, 114.44) = 2.59, p = 0.05, η² = 0.06, 95% CI [0.007, 0.095]). Post-hoc comparisons indicated that, The ETG showed significant differences at all time"
