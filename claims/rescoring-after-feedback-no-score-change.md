# Rescoring after providing feedback does not change supervisors' diagnostic performance scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no significant difference in scores before versus after providing feedback, and no significant interaction between vignette outcome and moment of scoring. [→ van Sassen C 2025](#van-sassen-c-2025)

## Evidence

### van Sassen C 2025

van Sassen C, Mamede S, van den Broek W, Bindels P, Zwaan L. (2025). Does Knowledge of Clinical Case Outcome Influence Supervisor Evaluation of Resident Clinical Reasoning? Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same within-subjects experiment, supervisors rescored residents after delivering TIPS/TOPS feedback. The repeated-measures ANOVA found no significant effect of moment of scoring (F1,61 = 0.04, P = .85); equivalence was not tested.

> "However, there was no significant difference based on the moment of scoring (before or after providing feedback), suggesting that reflecting on feedback did not change the scores (F1,61 = 0.04, P = .85, η2 = 0.001)."
