## Evidence

### Hehl 2026

Hehl, M., Toyonaga, T., Carson, R. E., Dupont, P., Van Laere, K., Swinnen, S. P. & Cuypers, K. (2026). No significant changes in synaptic density and gray matter volume following motor learning—a pilot study. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00451-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple linear regression per VOI in the 22-participant pilot. No association was found in DLPFC, hMT/V5+, PMd and SM1 (all p > 0.05), nor between baseline GMV and baseline SV2A binding (all p > 0.15), nor between changes in GM metrics and changes in SV2A uptake (all p > 0.37 for GMV; p > 0.22 for aCT).

> "baseline aCT (corrected for eTIV) was positively associated with baseline SV2A tracer uptake in (pre)SMA (model: F = 4.340, p = 0.0280, adj. R2 = 0.2413; main effect of SUVR at baseline: β = 0.1446, p = 0.0169)"
