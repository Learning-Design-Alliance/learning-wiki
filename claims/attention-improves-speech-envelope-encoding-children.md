## Evidence

### Jacqueline Phelps 2022

Jacqueline Phelps, Adam Attaheri & Mirjana Bozic. (2022). How bilingualism modulates selective attention in children. Scientific Reports 12:6381. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-09989-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects analysis of mTRF reconstruction accuracy (Pearson's r) from 48 children's EEG in the three dual-stream conditions. The article reports the attended advantage as "significant overall" with d = 0.10, and significant in each condition separately.

> "Pairwise comparisons confirmed that the attended stream EEG data showed on average higher stimulus reconstruction accuracy than the unattended ones, with the difference between them significant overall [rattd = 0.057, runattd = 0.040, t = 8.79, p < 0.001, d = 0.10]"
