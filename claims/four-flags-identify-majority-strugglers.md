### Yates Janet 2011

Yates Janet. (2011). Development of a 'toolkit' to identify medical students at risk of failure to thrive on the course: an exploratory retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-95

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective case study of progress files from five Nottingham cohorts (1188 admitted; 87 completers with difficulties). The combined Year 1 + Year 2 flag total including the Part I <50% marker "selected 14/25 (56%) of the strugglers", with lower detection in other outcome categories.

> "A total of four or more flags then selected 14/25 (56%) of the strugglers, but only 4/18 (22%) of the pre-clinical group, 2/19 (11%) of the borderlines, 4/17 (23%) of the Health-related group, and again none of the clinical problems group."
