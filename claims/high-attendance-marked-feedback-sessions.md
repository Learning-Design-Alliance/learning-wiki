## Evidence

### Herbert C 2017

Herbert C, Velan GM, Pryor WM, Kumar RK. (2017). A model for the use of blended learning in large group teaching sessions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1057-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational attendance data from 264 Pathology students. Average attendance at the 4 integration/feedback sessions was "88.4 ± 1.6% of enrolled students", while only 54.2% attended an end-of-semester revision session not linked to marks. The authors suggest marks strongly influenced attendance decisions.

> "Average attendance for the 4 large-group sessions was 88.4 ± 1.6% of enrolled students (n = 264). In contrast, only 54.2% of the cohort attended an end-of-semester revision session for which engagement was not linked with the course marks"
