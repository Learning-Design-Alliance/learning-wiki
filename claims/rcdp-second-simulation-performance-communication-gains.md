## Evidence

### Kang Kyungja; Yu Mi 2025

Kang Kyungja; Yu Mi. (2025). Rapid cycle deliberate practice simulation with standardized prebriefing and video based formative feedback in advanced cardiac life support. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97222-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison within the experimental group (N = 30) between first and second simulations, scored by checklist from a simulation operator and evaluator. Performance skill rose from 1.62 ± 0.49 to 2.59 ± 0.16. No effect size printed.

> "The experimental group showed significant improvements in both ACLS performance skill (z = − 4.56, p < 0.001) and team communication (z = − 4.82, p < 0.001) after the second (vs. first) simulation"

### Kang Kyungja; Yu Mi 2025 (2)

Kang Kyungja; Yu Mi. (2025). Rapid cycle deliberate practice simulation with standardized prebriefing and video based formative feedback in advanced cardiac life support. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97222-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same within-group Wilcoxon comparison for team communication (5-item checklist, Cronbach's alpha 0.74), rising from 1.33 ± 0.28 to 2.71 ± 0.40 between the first and second simulations. No effect size printed.

> "The experimental group showed significant improvements in both ACLS performance skill (z = − 4.56, p < 0.001) and team communication (z = − 4.82, p < 0.001) after the second (vs. first) simulation"
