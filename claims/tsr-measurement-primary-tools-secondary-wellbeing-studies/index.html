Evidence¶

Rhoda Lai 2026¶

Rhoda Lai, Jennifer Southam, Luella Mageean, Sue Roffey, Kelly-Ann Allen. (2026). Associations Between Teacher-Student Relationship Quality and Middle and Secondary School Teachers' Wellbeing: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10107-2

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's analysis of relationship measures across the 55 studies found the STRS most frequent, with language usually adapted for older students "though the precise changes made were rarely reported". The authors note such adaptations likely affect reliability and validity.

"the Student-Teacher Relationship Scale (STRS), particularly its Closeness and Conflict subscales or in its short form, was the most frequently used (n = 9) despite being originally designed for use with primary school students"

Rhoda Lai 2026 (2)¶

Rhoda Lai, Jennifer Southam, Luella Mageean, Sue Roffey, Kelly-Ann Allen. (2026). Associations Between Teacher-Student Relationship Quality and Middle and Secondary School Teachers' Wellbeing: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10107-2

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports "wide variation in how teacher-student relationships were measured and defined" across the included literature, with most studies using primary-school-formulated tools and definitions in middle/secondary settings.

"There was wide variation in how teacher-student relationships were measured and defined, with majority of the studies adopting tools and definitions that were formulated for primary school teacher-student relationships"
