## Evidence

### Quílez-Robres A. 2021

Quílez-Robres A., Moyano N., Cortés-Pascual A. (2021). Task Monitoring and Working Memory as Executive Components Predictive of General and Specific Academic Achievements in 6–9-Year-Old Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18136681

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Preliminary correlational analyses in the Results section of the 133-child sample, followed by confirmatory ANOVA comparisons that found no significance (p > 0.05), leading the authors to discard sex and age from further analyses. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Neither sex nor age yielded significant results in the correlational analyses with respect to general executive functions (p = 0.629 and p = 0.498, respectively)."
