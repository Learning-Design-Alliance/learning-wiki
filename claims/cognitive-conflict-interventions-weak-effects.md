Narrative review evidence: the article reports Limón's (2001) review of the cognitive conflict strategy, quoting her conclusion that "the most outstanding result of the studies using the cognitive conflict strategy is the lack of efficacy for students to achieve a strong restructuring". The review's studies were not read directly.

> "She found that although there are cases in which inducing a cognitive conflict produces positive benefits, those benefits are typically smaller than expected or desired, and in the majority of studies there is no measurable benefit from the cognitive conflict intervention."
