# Students who perceive teaching as good, and perceive clear goals, supportiveness and relevant courses, are more inclined towards a deep approach

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Perceiving teaching as good is associated with a deeper approach and less use of a surface approach. [→ Baeten 2010](#baeten-2010)
`q2 i?` Perceived clarity of goals and standards, perceived supportiveness, perceived independent study, and perceived relevance to professional practice are positively related to a deep approach. [→ Baeten 2010 (2)](#baeten-2010-2)

## Evidence

### Baeten 2010

Baeten, M., Kyndt, E., Struyven, K., & Dochy, F. (2010). Using student-centred learning environments to stimulate deep approaches to learning: Factors encouraging or discouraging their effectiveness. Educational Research Review, 5, 243–260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2010.06.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review synthesis of studies on perceived teaching quality. The review also reports one study found positive correlations between perceptions of teaching and both deep and surface approaches, and another found an indirect effect via perceived effect of the teaching.

> "If they per- ceive the teaching as ‘good’, students are more inclined towards using a deep approach and less towards using a surface approach"

### Baeten 2010 (2)

Baeten, M., Kyndt, E., Struyven, K., & Dochy, F. (2010). Using student-centred learning environments to stimulate deep approaches to learning: Factors encouraging or discouraging their effectiveness. Educational Research Review, 5, 243–260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2010.06.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis across multiple cited studies covering perceived clarity of goals, supportiveness, independent study, course book usefulness and perceived relevance to professional practice, all related to a deep approach.

> "the degree to which the teacher was perceived to have made goals and standards clear throughout the course was positively related to a deep approach and negatively to a surface approach"
