## Evidence

### Lei Fan and Fangxue Liu 2026

Lei Fan and Fangxue Liu. (2026). Impacts of generative AI skill development on academic adaptation and learning outcomes among university students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.30599](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.30599)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Tie-corrected Kruskal–Wallis tests across five trajectory groups (n = 311) with Benjamini–Hochberg correction; responsible use H = 26.305, ε² = 0.073, BH-adjusted p < 0.001, while academic gains H = 7.142, BH-adjusted p = 0.185. The three largest groups had similar mean academic-gain scores (3.82–3.91).

> "with the largest group difference observed for responsible academic use (ε² = 0.073). In contrast, perceived academic gains and assessment alignment showed no clear differences across trajectories, nor did time since first use or use frequency."
