### Emily M. Stump 2023

Emily M. Stump, Matthew Dew, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Context affects student thinking about sources of uncertainty in classical and quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157)

Scenario survey results for the deterministic classical experiment: "Students rarely mentioned principles as sources of uncertainty in the projectile motion experiment (6% of students)." When they did, they alluded to the statistical nature of measurement rather than classical physics principles.
