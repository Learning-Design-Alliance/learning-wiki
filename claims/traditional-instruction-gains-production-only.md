# Traditional form-focused instruction produces significant gains in production but not in comprehension

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learners experiencing traditional instruction (explanation and output practice) made significant gains on the production task only, with no reported comprehension gains. [→ Bill VanPatten and Teresa Cadierno 1993](#bill-vanpatten-and-teresa-cadierno-1993)

## Evidence

### Bill VanPatten and Teresa Cadierno 1993

Bill VanPatten and Teresa Cadierno. (1993). Explicit Instruction and Input Processing. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 15, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263100011979

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the same experiment analyzed by ANOVA, the traditional instruction group, whose treatment "involves explanation and output practice of a grammatical point," showed significant gains on the production measure only. No effect size is printed, and equivalence on comprehension was not tested.

> "For those experiencing traditional instruction, significant gains were made in production only."
