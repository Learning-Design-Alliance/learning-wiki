Amy C. Crosson & Margaret G. McKeown 2016¶

Amy C. Crosson & Margaret G. McKeown. (2016). Middle School Learners' Use of Latin Roots to Infer the Meaning of Unfamiliar Words. Cognition and Instruction, 34(2), 148-171. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1145121

Qualitative coding of seventh-grade dynamic assessment episodes found conflation—difficulty separating the meaning of instructed RAVE words from the Latin root's meaning—was the second most common approach for the RAVE groups; Table 4 shows conflation in 15.09% of RAVE and 15.39% of Honors RAVE episodes versus 2.88% of control episodes.
