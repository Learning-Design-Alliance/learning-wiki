Masum Hasan 2026¶

Masum Hasan, Ron Epstein, Thomas Carroll, Ehsan Hoque. (2026). Scalable AI-based clinical communication training and automated assessment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22517

q2 · i? · design · r2

Within-participant comparison of LLM-judged 3E scores across two counterbalanced AI-patient encounters among 59 participants. The "overall score increased from 0.403 to 0.471"; Be Explicit showed no significant change (∆ = 0.014; p=0.1204).

"The overall score increased from 0.403 to 0.471 (∆ = 0.068; 95% CI, 0.038–0.098; one-sidedp <0.001). Higher scores were also observed for Empower (∆ = 0.083; 95% CI, 0.046–0.121; one-sidedp <0.001) and Empathize (∆ = 0.107; 95% CI, 0.055–0.159;p= 0.001), whereas the difference for Be Explicit was not significant"
