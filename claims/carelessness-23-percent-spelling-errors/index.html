Foster 1911¶

Foster, W. T. (1911). The Spelling of College Students. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070538

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Error-classification analysis of 10,000 themes covering all the work of 300 Bowdoin College students, coded by five clerks with no stake in the results. The article reports "467, or 23 per cent., were due to carelessness" and excludes them from causal analysis.

"Of the 2005 errors, 467, or 23 per cent., were due to carelessness."
