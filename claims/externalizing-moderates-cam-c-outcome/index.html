Lopata 2020¶

Lopata, C., Donnelly, J. P., Thomeer, M. L., Rodgers, J. D., Lodi-Smith, J., Booth, A. J., & Volker, M. A. (2020). Moderators of School Intervention Outcomes for Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder. Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology, 48, 1105-1114. https://link.springer.com/journal/10802

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Moderator analysis of the cluster RCT using BASC-2 Externalizing composite as moderator of the CAM-C outcome; Table 2 prints the interaction fixed effect b=-.48, SE=.21, p=.01, 95% CI -.90 to -.07, and Fig. 1 plots predicted means with 95% CI.

"Externalizing behaviors appear to have impacted the treatment such that children in the treatment group who were lower in externalizing behaviors at baseline experienced a larger improvement in social-cognitive understanding/skills (p=.01)."
