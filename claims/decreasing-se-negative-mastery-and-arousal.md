## Evidence

### Peura 2026

Peura, P., Koponen, T., Aro, T., Viholainen, H., & Aro, M. (2026). Dynamics of change in math self-efficacy, sources of self-efficacy, and math skills. Journal of Educational Psychology, 118(2), 209-223. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001006

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Latent growth models of the four sources of self-efficacy were compared across trajectories with BCH; the four trajectories differed in initial level and rate of change for each source (LR tests p < .01). The Decreasing trajectory uniquely showed rising negative arousal and declining mastery experiences.

> "In addition, the Decreasing SE trajectory was the only one that showed increasing levels of negative emotional and physiological arousal over time. The ES ranged from intermediate to large (d = 0.46–4.87)."
