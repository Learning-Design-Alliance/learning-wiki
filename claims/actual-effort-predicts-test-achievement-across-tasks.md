## Evidence

### Kehle 2025

Kehle, L., & Urhahne, D. (2025). Interest and effort in learning and performance. *British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96*(2), 593–611. [doi:10.1111/bjep.70040](https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70040)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of Study 1 (N = 152 university students) with latent interest and effort variables predicting test achievement in two learning tasks. The article reports that "only actual effort emerges as a significant positive predictor of test achievement" for the MPT, alongside triggered interest for the CPT.

> "They indicate that for the CPT, both triggered interest and actual effort significantly and positively predict test achievement. For the MPT, only actual effort emerges as a significant positive predictor of test achievement"
