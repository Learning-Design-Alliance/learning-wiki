### Chen Ziqi 2024

Chen Ziqi, Zhu Sennan, Tao Jiayu, Chen Qiuyu, Du Wenzhuo, Sun Jindan, Yu Mengqi, Zhou Yi, Zhao Yu, Zhang Qiong. (2024). Application of spaghetti knotting technology in single-hole laparoscopic suturing instructional program on the basis of multi-directional stitching technology: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06266-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 40 first-year gynecology residents randomized to spaghetti vs conventional knotting training; post-training assessment on a multi-directional stitching pad showed "the time of knotting in the experimental group was significantly shorter" (Table 4: 7.85 ± 0.81 vs 9.10 ± 1.33 min, p = 0.001).
