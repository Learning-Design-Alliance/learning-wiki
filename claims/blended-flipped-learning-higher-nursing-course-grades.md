## Evidence

### Halasa S  2020

Halasa S , Abusalim N , Rayyan M , et al. (2020). Comparing student achievement in traditional learning with a combination of blended and flipped learning. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.492

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test-only study of 125 second-year nursing students in the Foundation of Growth and Development course, spring 2018; experimental group n = 59, control n = 66. Means were 77.77 (SD 7.985) versus 72.33 (SD 11.892), t = 2.968, p = .004; the computed effect size was "d = (77.77 − 72.33)/10.23717568 = 0.531", described as a medium effect per Cohen (1988).

> "Table 5 shows that the value of "T" of the achievement of students between the two groups according to the teaching method applied was 2.968, which is statistically significant."
