# Including out-of-school learning environments in 6th-grade science instruction produces significantly higher attitudes toward the science course than curriculum-only instruction at posttest

## Evidence

### Yildirim 2018

Halil Ibrahim Yildirim. (2018). The Impact of Out-of-School Learning Environments on 6th Grade Secondary School Students Attitude Towards Science Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 6(12). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v6i12.3624

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control-group study of 60 Turkish 6th graders (30 per group) over 18 weeks; independent samples t-test on posttest attitude scores gave t(58)=-4.93, p<.05, favoring the experimental group, "The calculated effect size is η2=0.30".

> "When the posttest scores towards the science lesson of students in experimental (x̄ =3.87) and control (x̄ =3.24) group students are compared, it can be stated that the averages of the students in the experimental group are higher than the averages of the students in the control group at a significant level. The calculated effect size is η2=0.30."
