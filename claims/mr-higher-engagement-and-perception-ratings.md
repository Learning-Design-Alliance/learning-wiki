## Evidence

### Wang Chris 2020

Wang Chris, Daniel Ben Kei, Asil Mustafa, Khwaounjoo Prashanna, Cakmak Yusuf Ozgur. (2020). A Randomised Control Trial and Comparative Analysis of Multi-Dimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62855-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey ratings from the three-group experiment (Table 2) show the MR group rated user perception (M = 4.30, SD = 0.85), long-term retention effectiveness (M = 3.26, SD = 1.19), and memorability (M = 3.74, SD = 1.15) significantly higher than the text-only group; no effect sizes printed.

> "The participants in the MR group had self-reported significantly higher ratings compared to the text-only group in the domains of user perception, long-term retention, and memorability."

### Wang Chris 2020 (2)

Wang Chris, Daniel Ben Kei, Asil Mustafa, Khwaounjoo Prashanna, Cakmak Yusuf Ozgur. (2020). A Randomised Control Trial and Comparative Analysis of Multi-Dimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62855-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 2 ANOVA results show 3DM group ratings exceeded text-only for user perception (M = 3.72 vs 2.75) and usability (M = 4.42 vs 3.72, F = 2.17, p = 0.027); no effect sizes printed for these comparisons.

> "The 3DM group responses were significantly higher than the text-only group in the domains of user perception and usability."
