# Teachers rated the TCLEBL class significantly higher than the LBL class on all five feedback items

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers judged the TCLEBL model more effective at enhancing student understanding, more enjoyable, and more conducive to learning than the LBL class (p < 0.001 on all items). [→ Huang Tianlong 2024](#huang-tianlong-2024)

## Evidence

### Huang Tianlong 2024

Huang Tianlong, Zhou Shun, Wei Qiaoyan, Ding Chun. (2024). Team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning in medical postgraduates training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05650-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Teacher questionnaire comparing the same teacher's TCLEBL and LBL classes, analyzed by paired t-test. All five items favored TCLEBL at p < 0.001, including "enhancing students’ understanding of the topic (7.6 ± 0.699 vs. 6.1 ± 0.568, p < 0.001)". No effect sizes printed.

> "teachers felt that the TCLEBL model was more effective in enhancing students’ understanding of the topic (7.6 ± 0.699 vs. 6.1 ± 0.568, p < 0.001) and that the class met their expectations (8.2 ± 0.789 vs. 5.3 ± 0.483, p < 0.001)"
