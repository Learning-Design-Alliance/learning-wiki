## Evidence

### Poulton T 2014

Poulton T, Ellaway RH, Round J, Jivram T, Kavia S, Hilton S. (2014). Exploring the Efficacy of Replacing Linear Paper-Based Patient Cases in Problem-Based Learning With Dynamic Web-Based Virtual Patients: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3748

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same crossover randomized controlled trial (80 examined), Mann-Whitney U tests on the comparison question set showed "no statistically significant differences in performance between the 2 cohorts." The Results also state no consistent mean-score pattern between cohorts for these questions. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Questions 6-9 were not related to D-PBL and showed no statistically significant differences in performance between the 2 cohorts."
