## Evidence

### Schreiber Benjamin E 2010

Schreiber Benjamin E, Fukuta Junaid, Gordon Fabiana. (2010). Live lecture versus video podcast in undergraduate medical education: A randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-68

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative free-text analysis of comments from the 66 questionnaire respondents. Control of playback was the second most cited advantage, supporting self-pacing such as "understand content before proceeding to the next section". Descriptive counts only.

> "17 students commented on the ability to control the playing of podcasts, which facilitates pausing to "write things down", "look things up on the internet", replay sections and to repeat the whole podcast."
