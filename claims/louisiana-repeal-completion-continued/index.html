Victoria Jackson 2026¶

Victoria Jackson, Kyla Hurns, and Paulina Fraser. (2026). Understanding the Implementation of Universal FAFSA Completion Policies in Colorado, Illinois, Indiana, and Louisiana. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682438

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The report describes publicly stated reasons for Louisiana's March 2024 repeal of the six-year policy, which had "resulted in substantial increases in FAFSA completion." Interviews with three state officials also surfaced political ideology and privacy lobbying as factors.

"The reasons included concerns about privacy, countering the narrative that college is the best post-high school pathway, and reducing the burden on students and parents."

Victoria Jackson 2026 (2)¶

Victoria Jackson, Kyla Hurns, and Paulina Fraser. (2026). Understanding the Implementation of Universal FAFSA Completion Policies in Colorado, Illinois, Indiana, and Louisiana. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682438

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

A Louisiana state official reported that workshops, FAFSA Frenzy Week, and assistance continued post-repeal, with the state "1,300 FAFSAs ahead of where we were last year" as of 9/12 — a descriptive count, not a tested comparison.

"As of 9/12, we're actually 1,300 FAFSAs ahead of where we were last year. So, students and families are continuing to fill out the FAFSA in spite of the mandate being repealed"
