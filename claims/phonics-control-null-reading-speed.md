### Benjamin Gagl & Klara Gregorová 2024

Benjamin Gagl & Klara Gregorová. (2024). Investigating lexical categorization in reading based on joint diagnostic and training approaches for language learners. npj Science of Learning 9:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00237-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the Experiment 2 randomized controlled comparison of training procedures with the same letter strings, the phonics training did not significantly increase reading speed (Table 1: M = 2.5%, t(26) = 0.43, p = .670), and the LC-vs-phonics contrast was unreliable, t(26) = 1.73, p = 0.096; equivalence was not tested.

> "Comparing lexical categorization and phonics in Experiment 2, we found no signiﬁcant difference, as the phonics had a small but unreliable positive effect (t(26) = 1.73, p = 0.096)."
