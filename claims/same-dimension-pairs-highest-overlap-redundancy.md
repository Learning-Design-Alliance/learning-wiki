### Qerimi 2025

Qerimi, L., Malone, S., Rexigel, E., Mehlhase, S., Kuhn, J., Küchemann, S. (2025). Exploring the mechanisms of qubit representations and introducing a new category system for visual representations: Results from expert ratings. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert rating of representation pairs A–F on whether they contain the same information content; pairs "with the same dimensions were assigned the highest rating values for overlap/redundancy", namely the two three-dimensional and the two two-dimensional representations combined.

> "Representations with the same dimensions were assigned the highest rating values for overlap/redundancy (B: 3.45 ± 0.89; E: 3 .95 ± 1.05)."
