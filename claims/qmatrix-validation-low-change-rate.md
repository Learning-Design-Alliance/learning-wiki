# Expert Q-matrix coding closely matched DINA-recovered structure, with only 2.7% of item-LO codings changed during validation

## Evidence

### Vy Le 2026

Vy Le, Jayson M. Nissen, Jason W. Morphew, Hua Hua Chang, and Ben Van Dusen. (2026). Mechanics Cognitive Diagnostic: Testing Fine-Grained Learning Objectives in Introductory Physics. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09584 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.09584](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.09584)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Three researchers independently coded items, and DINA-based analysis of posttest responses suggested modifications that coders reviewed by consensus. "The coders reviewed all 104 suggestions and adopted 20 changes," an overall adoption rate of 19%.

> "In total, this validation step changed only 2.7% of all possible item-LO codings (20 of 754). This low overall change rate indicates that the initial expert coding closely matched the structure the DINA model recovered from student response data, and it supports the reliability of the final Q-matrix used in the subsequent CD modeling analyses reported below."
