Comparison of final grades (Kf) for 173 students across both contexts (Fig. 1 and Fig. 3a). The article states HA students "had on average a better performance in virtual context" while the opposite held for LA students; Table 3 shows relative Kf changes of 1.8% (HA), -1.5% (AA), -5.1% (LA).
