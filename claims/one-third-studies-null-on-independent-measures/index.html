Descriptive within-study comparison of 50 studies containing at least one researcher or developer measure and one broad or narrow measure in the same outcome domain. The article reports "in 32% of studies" effects were significant only on researcher or developer measures.

"Descriptive findings suggest that in 32% of studies, positive and statistically significant effects were identified on a researcher or developer measure, yet null effects were identified on all independent (broad or narrow) measures in the same study and outcome domain."
