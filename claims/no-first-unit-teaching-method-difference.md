## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Following the first two-week instructional period, the difference in posttest scores between the inquiry-based and subject matter groups was not statistically significant. [→ Thoron 2011](#thoron-2011)

## Evidence

### Thoron 2011

Thoron, A. C., & Myers, B. E. (2011). Effects of Inquiry–based Agriscience Instruction on Student Achievement. Journal of Agricultural Education, 52(4). https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2011.04175

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of the first instructional period's posttest with pretest covariate: inquiry-based mean 63.49 (SD = 17.86) versus subject matter mean 60.43 (SD = 17.44); the article reports the contrast as "not be statistically significant, F(4,334) = 2.82, p = .09, r2 = .09". Equivalence was not tested.
