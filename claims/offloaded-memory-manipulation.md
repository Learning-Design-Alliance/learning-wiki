An experiment in which participants offloaded information to a digital external store and the experimenters manipulated it in certain trials (Risko et al., 2019, as reviewed here): "Participants rarely took note of this change and even replaced their internal memory with this altered information." The review frames this as memory corruption with societal consequences.
