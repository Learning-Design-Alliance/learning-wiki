Evidence¶

McNally et al. 2016¶

Sandra McNally, Jenifer Ruiz-Valenzuela and Heather Rolfe. (2016). ABRA: Online Reading Support — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN18254678

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Comparison between the two control groups built into the trial design (within-treatment-school controls versus pure control schools). The report calls this "preliminary evidence" and states it will be further investigated with TA survey and log data.

"Pupils that received normal literacy provision in the schools where the interventions took place did better than students in schools that only received normal literacy provision. This is consistent with the existence of spillover (or peer) effects."
