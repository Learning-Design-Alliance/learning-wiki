S. Dovis 2015¶

S. Dovis, S. van der Oord, H.M. Huizenga, R.W. Wiers, P.J.M. Prins. (2015). Prevalence and diagnostic validity of motivational impairments and deficits in visuospatial short-term memory and working memory in ADHD subtypes. European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry, 24(5), 575-590. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-014-0612-1

Analysis of the relationship between memory (WM/STM) impairment and motivational (reinforcement-sensitivity) impairment in 86 children with ADHD-C and 27 with ADHD-I. The abstract states "Memory and motivation were found to represent independent neuropsychological domains."

  • Motivational impairments in reinforcement sensitivity are uncommon (22% impaired) and equally prevalent in both ADHD subtypes — related

  • STM and WM performance is worse in ADHD-C than in ADHD-I, while CE-related performance does not differ between subtypes — related
  • Visuospatial working memory and short-term memory impairments are most prevalent in children with ADHD-C relative to ADHD-I and typically developing controls — related
