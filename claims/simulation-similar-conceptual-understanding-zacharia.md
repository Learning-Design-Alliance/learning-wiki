The review reports this second-hand from Zacharia and Olympiou's (2011) study of heat and temperature conceptual understanding, comparing simulation and hands-on lab conditions; "similar conceptual understanding" was found. The review attributes the key factor to opportunity to manipulate variables and engage in inquiry, not physical interaction.

> "Zacharia and Olympiou (2011) found students who used a simulation had similar conceptual understanding as students who conducted a hands-on lab."
