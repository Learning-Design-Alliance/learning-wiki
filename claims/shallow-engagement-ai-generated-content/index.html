q1 · i? · design · r2

Observation surfaced in Session 4, where faculty worked through four faculty-sourced AI-integrated homework tasks (explanation generation, Python code, diagrams, simulations) and reviewed student solutions to identify prompting strategies. The group concluded productive use "has to be modeled rather than assuming students' expertise in AI use."

"students do not necessarily engage deeply with AI-generated content, and without structure they tend to shallow prompting and shallow modifications of AI generated output"
