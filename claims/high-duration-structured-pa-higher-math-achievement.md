## Evidence

### Dapp Laura C. 2019

Dapp Laura C., Roebers Claudia M. (2019). The Mediating Role of Self-Concept between Sports-Related Physical Activity and Mathematical Achievement in Fourth Graders. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16152658

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group comparison within the N = 138 cross-sectional sample: children in sports clubs were split into low-duration (up to two hours/week) and high-duration (two hours or more) groups. The printed means and t-test show the high-duration group scored significantly higher; no effect size is printed.

> "the high-duration children (M = 25.62, SD = 4.57) achieved significantly higher scores on the mathematical achievement test than the low-duration children (M = 23.27, SD = 4.52), t(101) = −2.51, p < 0.05."

### Dapp Laura C. 2019 (2)

Dapp Laura C., Roebers Claudia M. (2019). The Mediating Role of Self-Concept between Sports-Related Physical Activity and Mathematical Achievement in Fourth Graders. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16152658

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null comparison in the same sample: low-duration sports-club children did not differ from non-participants in mathematical achievement, suggesting a minimum duration threshold; equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast, the low-duration children did not significantly differ from children who did not engage in structured PA at all, t(70) = 0.83, p = 0.41."
