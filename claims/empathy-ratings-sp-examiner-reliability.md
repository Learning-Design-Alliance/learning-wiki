## Evidence

### Wright Barry 2014

Wright Barry, McKendree Jean, Morgan Lewys, Allgar Victoria L, Brown Andrew. (2014). Examiner and simulated patient ratings of empathy in medical student final year clinical examination: are they useful? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-199

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis of empathy ratings in the OSCE stations, comparing clinical examiners with actor simulated patients rating independently: "the reliability as measured by the ICC is 0.645 (95% CI 0.593-0.692) indicating substantial agreement".

> "Overall the reliability as measured by the ICC is 0.645 (95% CI 0.593-0.692) indicating substantial agreement [33]."

### Wright Barry 2014 (2)

Wright Barry, McKendree Jean, Morgan Lewys, Allgar Victoria L, Brown Andrew. (2014). Examiner and simulated patient ratings of empathy in medical student final year clinical examination: are they useful? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-199

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Internal consistency analysis of the empathy scale scores across the four stations: "The reliability of the Empathy scale scores measured by Cronbach's alpha was .74", with total scores ranging 20–38 against a possible 8–40.

> "The range of total scores given across all students was 20–38 with a possible range of 8–40. The reliability of the Empathy scale scores measured by Cronbach's alpha was .74."
