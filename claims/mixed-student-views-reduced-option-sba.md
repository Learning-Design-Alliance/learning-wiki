Online survey of 108 students (33% response rate) using Likert and free-text questions distributed via SurveyMonkey. Positive responses cited increased efficiency and better use of time; negative responses cited increased guessing probability and perceived unfairness.

> "Student appraisal of the change in SBA format was mixed; 49.4% of responses contained elements of positivity towards the proposed new format, whilst 78.2% of responses contained negativity."
