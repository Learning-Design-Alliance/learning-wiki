## Evidence

### Jenny Mirjam Annina 2018

Jenny Mirjam Annina, Keller Niklas, Gigerenzer Gerd. (2018). Assessing minimal medical statistical literacy using the Quick Risk Test: a prospective observational study in Germany. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-020847

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective observational pretest of the Quick Risk Test administered to 169 final-year medical students (~60% of the semester cohort) at Charité University Medicine; the pretest median percentage of correct responses across all 10 questions was 53.8% (IQR=44.4%–68.5%), "For comparison, chance performance is 25%."
