# Systematic implementation of Energizers improves on-task behavior during academic instruction in third- and fourth-grade students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` On-task behavior improved by 8% between pre-Energizers and post-Energizers observations, a statistically significant moderate effect (ES = 0.60). [→ Mahar 2006](#mahar-2006)

## Evidence

### Mahar 2006

Mahar, M. T., Murphy, S. K., Rowe, D. A., Golden, J., Shields, A. T., & Raedeke, T. D. (2006). Effects of a Classroom-Based Program on Physical Activity and On-Task Behavior. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise, 38(12), 2086-2094. https://doi.org/10.1249/01.mss.0000235359.16685.a3

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A multiple-baseline across-classrooms design with 62 third- and fourth-grade students (37 and 25) evaluated on-task behavior before and after Energizers activities; a repeated-measures ANOVA showed "the improvement in on-task behavior of 8%" was significant with ES = 0.60.

> "The improvement in on-task behavior of 8% between the pre-Energizers and post-Energizers observations was statistically significant (P < 0.017), and the difference was moderate (ES = 0.60)."
