# In Study 1's extended five-domain DCT model, near-comparison cross-paths were not significantly different from zero while far-comparison cross-paths were significantly negative

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the extended SEM with 25 achievement-to-self-concept paths across five domains, the mean of the near coefficients was not significantly different from zero (Mn = −.014) while the mean of the far coefficients was significantly negative (Mn = −.080), and the near–far difference was highly significant (Mn = .066). ## Evidence

### Marsh 2015

Marsh, Lüdtke, Nagengast, Trautwein, Abduljabbar, Abdelfattah, & Jansen. (2015). Dimensional Comparison Theory: Paradoxical relations between self-beliefs and achievements in multiple domains. Learning and Instruction, 35, 16-32. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2014.08.005

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Extended DCT SEM with five domains (25 paths classified a priori as matching, near, or far) in the same 1140-student sample: near paths "not significantly different from zero (Mn = −.014, SE = .017)", far paths significantly negative (Mn = −.080, SE = .010); difference Mn = .066, SE = .017. No standardised effect size is printed for this finding.

> "the mean of the 8 near coefficients is not significantly different from zero (Mn = −.014, SE = .017), whilst the mean of the 12 “far” coefficients is significantly negative (Mn = −.080, SE = .010); the difference between the two is highly significant (Mn = .066, SE = .017)"
