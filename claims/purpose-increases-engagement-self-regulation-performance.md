### purpose-1

Anton, G., Mills, K., Jacob, S., Chou, N., McNeil, K., & Wegemer, C. (2026, July). Powerful learning: Fostering agency, purpose, and curiosity through connection. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/307

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

A framework report (the Powerful Learning Framework, a synthesis of theories of how people learn, with vignettes) asserts, citing Priniski et al. (2018), that purpose fosters meaning in learning activities, "consequently increasing engage- ment, improving self-regulation, and boosting academic performance." The report presents no study of its own testing this; no effect sizes are printed.

> "When learners are able to find purpose while learning, they instill a sense of meaning for the learning activities, consequently increasing engage- ment, improving self-regulation, and boosting academic performance (Priniski et al., 2018)."
