### Ma 2023 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' summary of the gender comparison results in the Discussion, highlighting "distinct roles of executive function subcomponents in predicting English academic performance across genders".

> "working memory span had a stronger negative bridging connection with listening in males, while cognitive flexibility had a greater bridging influence on overall performance in males. Conversely, working memory span's bridging influence was strongerinfemales."
