## Evidence

### Mihci 2017

Mihci, C. & Ozdener Donmez, N. (2017). Teaching GUI-programming concepts to prospective K12 teachers: MIT App Inventor as an alternative to text based languages. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 3(2), 543-559. https://doi.org/10.21890/ijres.327912

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative Phase 2 survey one year after the course: open-ended answers coded in NVivo and compared by relational survey method; Experimental Group 2 (n=101) versus Control Group 2 (n=76) showed the printed percentage differences across primary, middle school, and high school categories.

> "students who were educated with both the TBL and the BBL displ ayed a slight tendency in their answers towards earlier ages for beginning computer programming education (29% primary, 56% middle school), as compared to students who were educated solely with the TBL (25% primary, 50% middle school)."
