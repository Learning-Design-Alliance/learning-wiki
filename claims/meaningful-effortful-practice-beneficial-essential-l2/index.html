Robert DeKeyser 2010¶

Robert DeKeyser. (2010). Practice for Second Language Learning: Don't Throw out the Baby with the Bathwater. International Journal of English Studies, 10(1). https://www.um.es/ijes

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in a position paper. The author endorses Lightbown's formulation that "the role of practice is clearly beneficial and even essential" when practice means meaningful use plus effortful work on difficult features, while conceding practice is necessary but not sufficient.

""[w]hen practice is defined as opportunities for meaningful language use (both receptive and productive) and for thoughtful, effortful practice of difficult linguistic features, then the role of practice is clearly beneficial and even essential" (Lightbown, 2000, p. 443)."
