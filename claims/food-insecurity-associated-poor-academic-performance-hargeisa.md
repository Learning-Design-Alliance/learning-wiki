## Evidence

### Adam Sagal Mohamed 2024

Adam Sagal Mohamed, Teshoma Melese Sinaga, Ahmed Awale Sh. Dahir, Tamiru Dessalegn. (2024). Household food insecurity and its association with academic performance among primary school adolescents in Hargeisa City, Somaliland. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0303034

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 630 primary school adolescents in governmental schools in Hargeisa, with academic performance dichotomized at the 50% promoted mark and food insecurity measured with an adapted HFIAS. The crude contrast was significant (P<0.001), with 71% poor performance among food-insecure versus 32.7% among food-secure households; multivariable analysis gave "AOR = 5.24, 95%CI = 3.17–8.65".

> "The frequency of poor academic performance is significantly higher in adolescents from food-insecure households than in adolescents from food-secure households (P<0.001)."
