### Ines Zeitlhofer 2026

Ines Zeitlhofer, Joerg Zumbach. (2026). Germane processes moderate the effectiveness of complex desirable difficulty tasks. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09807-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A regression analysis with ICL as independent variable and MAI-complexity as dependent variable, computed with 131 cases after one outlier was removed, found "a strong effect of ICL on MAI-complexity" with R2 = 0.50; R² is not treated as a standardized effect size.

> "Results revealed a strong effect of ICL on MAI-complexity ( F (1, 129) = 126.40, p < .001, R2 = 0.50; Hypothesis 2.3). The model leads to the regression equation MAI = 1.22 + 0.77 x ICL (t (129) = 11.24; p < .001)."
