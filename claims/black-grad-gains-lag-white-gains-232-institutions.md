### Nichols 2016

Nichols, A. H., Eberle-Sudré, K., & Welch, M. (2016). Rising Tide II: Do Black Students Benefit as Grad Rates Increase? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566672

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of IPEDS graduation rate data for 232 four-year public institutions that improved overall graduation rates and had at least 30 first-time, full-time black and white students. The report states "graduation rates for black students at these institutions improved 4.4 percentage points compared with 5.6 points for white students."

> "In the last 10 years, graduation rates for black students at these institutions improved 4.4 percentage points compared with 5.6 points for white students (Figure 2)."
