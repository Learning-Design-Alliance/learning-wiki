Jaya Shivangani Kashyap and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Strategies educators can use to counter misinformation related to the quantum information revolution. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/adbeb1](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/adbeb1)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

In the interview study, Educator A reflected on misinformation that entanglement enables faster-than-light communication, stating "it doesn't allow us to send signals faster than light". He also suggested emphasizing that "teleportation is to move a quantum state from one place to another", not material transport as a fax machine would do.

> "one has to emphasize that whatever else you may think about how the universe works, it doesn't allow us to send signals faster than light"
