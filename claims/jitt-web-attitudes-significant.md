### Rewadee Chantoem & Saowalak Rattanavich 2016

Rewadee Chantoem & Saowalak Rattanavich. (2016). Just-In-Time Teaching Techniques through Web Technologies for Vocational Students' Reading and Writing Abilities. English Language Teaching, 9(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n1p65

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Attitudes measured with a five-choice Likert questionnaire (reliability 0.99) in the randomized pretest-posttest experiment. The article reports "a significant difference in attitudes towards English learning" between groups, with post-test means 3.91 (JiTT) vs 3.58 (control).

> "there was a significant difference in attitudes towards English learning at the .01 confidence level between the experimental and control groups ( t2 = 2.45**, p-value = .019)"
