### Celentano C 2026

Evaluating the effect of two different training interventions on improving vaginal twin birth rates and provider confidence and knowledge levels: A pre‐ and post‐intervention study. International Journal of Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70947

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective multicenter pre/post study of 278 twin pregnancies (131 pre, 147 post) at two Italian hospitals. The overall vaginal birth rate rose from 9.2% to 40.8%, with site-specific increases from 6.4% to 31.5% in Pescara and 16.2% to 56.4% in Florence, all reported as statistically significant.

> "The incidence of vaginal birth increased significantly in the second period, whether considering both hospitals together or each hospital individually (P < 0.0001, P < 0.0001, P = 0.0001; overall, Pescara, and Florence respectively)."
