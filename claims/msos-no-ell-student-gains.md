# English language learner students showed no statistically significant gains on either force and motion or California Standards Test outcomes

## Subclaims
`q4 i?` ELL subsample students did not score significantly higher on the ATLAST Test of Force and Motion (effect size = 0.31) or on the physical science reporting clusters of the California Standards Test (effect size = 0.09). [→ Heller 2012](#heller-2012)

## Evidence

### Heller 2012

Heller, J.I. (2012). Effects of Making Sense of SCIENCE™ professional development on the achievement of middle school students, including English language learners. (NCEE 2012-4002). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED530414

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Confirmatory analysis for the English language learner subsample of the randomized trial, on both the ATLAST force and motion test (effect size = 0.31, reported in the preceding summary sentence) and the California Standards Test physical science clusters (effect size = 0.09); neither was statistically significant after multiple-comparison adjustment.

> "Similarly, intervention group students in neither the full sample (effect size = 0.03) nor the English language learner subsample (effect size =0 .09) scored higher on the physical science reporting clusters of the California Standards Test than did their control group counterparts."
