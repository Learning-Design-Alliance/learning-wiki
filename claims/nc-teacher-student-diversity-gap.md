# North Carolina's teacher workforce is far less racially diverse than its student population

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In North Carolina in 2018-19, 51.7% of students were people of color while only 23% of teachers were, with especially large gaps for Black and Latino teachers. [→ EdTrust 2022](#edtrust-2022)

### EdTrust 2022

EdTrust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: NORTH CAROLINA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED622852

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive demographic analysis using federal CCD (2017-18) student data and NTPS (2017-18) teacher data for K-12 public and charter schools. The table prints "Total Percentage of People of Color" at 51.7% for students versus 23% for teachers; Black students are 25.3% versus 14.7% of teachers, Latino students 17.5% versus 3.9%.
