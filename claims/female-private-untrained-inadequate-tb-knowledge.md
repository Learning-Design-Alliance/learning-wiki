## Evidence

### Naseer Maliha 2013

Naseer Maliha, Khawaja Ali, Pethani Amin S, Aleem Salik. (2013). How well can physicians manage Tuberculosis? A Public-Private sector comparison from Karachi, Pakistan. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-439

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression on the 196-doctor survey using a dichotomous knowledge variable (adequate >75%, inadequate ≤75%); odds ratios are printed but the article prints no standardized effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Factors associated with inadequate knowledge scores were being female gender [OR: 2.76 (95% CI: 1.418-5.384)], private employment status [OR: 1.50 (95% CI: 1.258-2.439)], and not trained by NTP [OR: 2.98 (95% CI: 1.286-3.225)] on multivariate logistic regression analysis."
