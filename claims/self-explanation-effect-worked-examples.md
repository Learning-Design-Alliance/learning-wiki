# Students who self-explain worked examples learn more effectively than those who do not, and the quality of self-explanations relates to explaining deep structure

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Physics-naive college students who generated self-explanations while studying examples showed greater success at subsequent problem solving than students who did not. [→ Atkinson 2000](#atkinson-2000)
`q2 i?` The quality of effective learners' self-explanations is attributable to how often they attempt to self-explain the deep structure of problems. [→ Atkinson 2000 (2)](#atkinson-2000-2)

## Evidence

### Atkinson 2000

Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181-214. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Chi and colleagues, a protocol study of physics-naive college students studying an elementary physics lesson; students 'who self-explained while studying examples appeared to learn more effectively' than those who did not.

> "They found that the students in their study who self-explained while studying examples appeared to learn more effectively (as demonstrated by their greater success at subsequent problem solving) than those who did not exhibit this behavior."

### Atkinson 2000 (2)

Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181-214. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Renkl, a study of qualitative differences in college students' self-explanations; 'the quality of the effective learners' self-explanations could be attributed to how often they attempted to self-explain the deep structure'.

> "Renkl (1997b) discovered that the quality of the effective learners' self-explanations could be attributed to how often they attempted to self-explain the deep structure of the problems."
