# Higher academic self-efficacy profiles report greater overall academic AI use, with a large effect (η² = .36)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Suriá-Martínez R 2026

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`
