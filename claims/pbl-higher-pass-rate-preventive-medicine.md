## Evidence

### Ding Xiaojie 2014

Ding Xiaojie, Zhao Liping, Chu Haiyan, Tong Na, Ni Chunhui, Hu Zhibin, Zhang Zhengdong, Wang Meilin. (2014). Assessing the Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning of Preventive Medicine Education in China. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep05126

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Pooled analysis of the four included studies (495 subjects) that reported pass rate of the theoretical test. The pooled odds ratio was 2.88 (95% CI 1.44–5.75) with "a lack of heterogeneity" (P = 0.83, I² = 0.0%).

> "Four studies reported pass rate of the theoretical test, when they were pooled together, there was a lack of heterogeneity (P = 0.83) and the PBL effect was significant (OR = 2.88, 95% CI = 1.44–5.75)"
