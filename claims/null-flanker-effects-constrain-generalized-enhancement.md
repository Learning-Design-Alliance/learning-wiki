`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary's reading of the null Flanker findings in Zhang et al. (2026): brief, light-to-moderate exercise "implemented in a manner that is both feasible and realistic for classroom settings" did not robustly alter canonical domain-general inhibition. Frequentist nulls are not affirmative evidence of invariance.

> "the results suggest that any benefits of brief exercise are selective and contingent, thereby placing principled limits on how far such effects can be expected to generalize."
