## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Even multimodal LLMs that can process images performed worse on image-based tasks than on text-based questions, a limitation consequential for dental education where radiographs and clinical visual imagery are essential. [→ Kong Mengjie 2025](#kong-mengjie-2025)

## Evidence

### Kong Mengjie 2025

Kong Mengjie, Fok Elise Hoi Wan, Yiu Cynthia Kar Yung. (2025). A Scoping Review of Large Language Models in Dental Education: Applications, Challenges, and Prospects. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.103854

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the review's discussion of technical challenges, it states that "their performance on image-based tasks remains inferior to that on text-based questions", citing multiple included studies on image-based examination performance.

> "Although some multimodal models have recently emerged, empirical studies indicate that their performance on image-based tasks remains inferior to that on text-based questions."
