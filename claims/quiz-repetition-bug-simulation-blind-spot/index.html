q2 · i? · design · r2

Pilot survey technical-reliability findings from the eight-participant CMP511 study. The authors state the defect is one that "simulation-based evaluation is structurally unable to identify", making it a deployment-only failure mode.

"However, a question-repetition bug was reported by 2/8 participants; one classified it as "severe" and reported that it prevented meaningful use. This defect is one that simulation-based evaluation is structurally unable to identify."
