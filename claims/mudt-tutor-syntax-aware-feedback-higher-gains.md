## Evidence

### Jiaxin Zuo 2025

Jiaxin Zuo, Yiquan Wang, Yuan Pan, and Xiadiya Yibulayin. (2025). Construction and educational application of a linguistically grounded dependency treebank for Uyghur. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.21536](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.21536)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Controlled classroom experiment with 35 first-year undergraduate native Chinese speakers studying Uyghur, quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test design with a four-day procedure and a cooling-off day; post-cleaning N=15 experimental, N=16 control. The t-test showed "a significant difference between the two groups (t(29) =2.51,p=0.018)"; the article reports the effect size was calculated as Cohen’sd=0.90, i.e. d = 0.90, a large effect.

> "The analysis revealed a significant difference between the two groups (t(29) =2.51,p=0.018). Furthermore, the effect size was calculated as Cohen’sd=0.90, indicating a large effect."
