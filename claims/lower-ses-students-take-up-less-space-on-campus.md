## Evidence

### Bart-Plange 2025

Bart-Plange, D.-J., Henderson, K., Hoffman, K., & Trawalter, S. (2025). Critiquing and Reimagining Belonging in Public Spaces in Higher Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10061-z

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

In an observational study, eight research assistants coded demographics and space use (7-point scale) of 388 individuals in iconic campus spaces including the Lawn, amphitheater, and library. Perceived SES predicted space use, η2 = .08; perceived race and gender did not predict use of space, |ts| < 1, ps > .60.

> "Results revealed a main effect of perceived SES on use of space, B = .33, t (382) = 5.77, p < .0001, η2 = .08, 95% CI [.04, .14], such that students perceived as lower-SES students were rated as using less space than students perceived as higher-SES."
