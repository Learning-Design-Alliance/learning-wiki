# Individual choice provision showed low reliability and was merged with class-directed choice provision into one perceived choice provision factor

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The individual choice provision factor had too low reliability (ωL1 = .402) with non-significant item loadings, so it was merged with class-directed choice provision. [→ Flunger 2024](#flunger-2024)

## Evidence

### Flunger 2024

Flunger, B., Verdonschot, A., Zitzmann, S., Hornstra, L., & van Gog, T. (2024). A Bayesian approach to students' perceptions of teachers' autonomy support. Learning and Instruction, 91, 101873. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101873

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Level-specific reliability check following Geldhof et al. (2014) in the Bayesian ML-CFA. The merged L1 factor's reliability improved but remained low (ωL1 = .602); overall L1 reliabilities ranged from ωL1 = .553 to .861.

> "the reliability of the factor individual choice provision was too low (ωL1 = .402), and the items did not load significantly on this factor. Therefore, we merged the two factors representing perceptions of individual and class-directed choice provision into one factor"
