Grant, K. E., Farahmand, F., Meyerson, D. A., Dubois, D. L., Tolan, P. H., Gaylord-Harden, N. K., Barnett, A., Horwath, J., Doxie, J., Tyler, D., Harrison, A., Johnson, S., & Duffy, S. (2014). `q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A truncated three-month pilot of the first year of the intervention with 17 intervention youth and 18 controls in two partner schools; coping sessions were video-taped and fidelity, usability, and satisfaction measures were collected. Volunteer rates rose from approximately 50% in the first pilot to more than 98% in the second pilot of 34 intervention and 33 control students.

> "Observation of intervention component implementation and analysis of fidelity, usability, feasibility, and satisfaction measures suggest that the intervention is feasible and usable and appreciated by participants"
