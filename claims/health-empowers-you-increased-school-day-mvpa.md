## Evidence

### Elish Paul N. 2022

Elish Paul N., Bryan Cassandra S., Boedeker Peter J., Calvert Hannah G., Kay Christi M., Meyer Adria M., Gazmararian Julie A. (2022). The longitudinal association between objectively-measured school-day physical activity and academic achievement in US elementary school students. International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-022-01328-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of accelerometer-measured mean daily MVPA by randomization status across 40 schools (Table 2): intervention 22.6 vs control 19.6 minutes in T1, 24.1 vs 19.6 in T2, 21.5 vs 16.3 in T3, all p < 0.001. The article states "The intervention increased school-day MVPA among intervention students relative to control students" and that the gap widened to over 5 minutes in T3.
