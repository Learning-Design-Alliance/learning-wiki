## Evidence

### Fielding Shona 2018

Fielding Shona, Tiffin Paul Alexander, Greatrix Rachel, Lee Amanda J, Patterson Fiona, Nicholson Sandra, Cleland Jennifer. (2018). Do changing medical admissions practices in the UK impact on who is admitted? An interrupted time series analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023274

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Segmented regression with 2013 as interruption for case study C, which raised the UKCAT factor weighting to 50%, added 500 points to the threshold and reduced the personal statement weighting; no trends or interruption effects were statistically significant and no relative change estimates were significant.

> "The interrupted time series (table 2) did not yield any statistically significant results for the trends before or after the interruption and at the 2013 interruption"
