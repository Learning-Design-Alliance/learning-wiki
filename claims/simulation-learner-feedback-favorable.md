### DerGarabedian B 2025

DerGarabedian B, Lacovara L, Delic J, Rajagopalan S. (2025). BRAIN-SIM: Leveraging Simulation for Neurocritical Care Education with an Innovative Multidisciplinary Approach. Journal of Intensive Care Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1177/08850666251327156

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous post-simulation Likert and open-ended feedback from thirty-eight surveys. Learners "found the multidisciplinary simulation experiences to be realistic and relevant to real-world patient encounters" and identified areas for improvement.

> "All learners agreed that faculty were knowledgeable, and sessions were well-informed and thorough. Moreover, all learners agreed the course objectives were met and the time-allotted for the case and debrief were appropriate."
