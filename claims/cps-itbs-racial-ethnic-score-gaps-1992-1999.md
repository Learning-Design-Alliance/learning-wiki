# Asian and white CPS students score consistently higher than Latino and African-American students in reading and math, and Asian students score significantly higher in math than white students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In both reading and math, Asian and white students score consistently higher than Latino and African-American students, with Asian and white students tending to be above national average grade level and Latino and African-American students below. [→ John Q. Easton 2000](#john-q-easton-2000)
`q2 i?` Asian students score significantly higher in math than white students, while white and Asian trends are similar in reading. [→ John Q. Easton 2000 (2)](#john-q-easton-2000-2)
