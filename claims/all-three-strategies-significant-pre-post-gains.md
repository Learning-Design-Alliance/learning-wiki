## Evidence

### Hassan Soleimani & Fatemeh Nabizadeh 2012

Hassan Soleimani & Fatemeh Nabizadeh. (2012). The Effect of Learner Constructed, Fill in the Map Concept Map Technique, and Summarizing Strategy on Iranian Pre-university Students' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 5(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n9p78

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t tests within each of the three groups (N=30 per group as printed in Table 4) comparing pretest and posttest reading comprehension. Table 4 prints t=9.62, 10.86, and 7.58 with df=29 and mean differences of 3.30, 4.06, and 2.23; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "As Table 4 displays in all three groups there was significant difference between the performance of the subjects in reading comprehension pre and post tests of each group (sig 1=.001, sig2 =.001 and sig3 =.007). In other words all strategies (learner constructed and fill in the map CM technique and summarizing strategy) had positive effect on subjects' reading comprehension."
