## Evidence

### Sawatsky Adam P. 2015

Sawatsky Adam P., Zickmund Susan L., Berlacher Kathryn, Lesky Dan, Granieri Rosanne. (2015). Understanding the challenges to facilitating active learning in the resident conferences: a qualitative study of internal medicine faculty and resident perspectives. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.27289

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Faculty focus group theme on the appropriate amount of information in conferences. A faculty member explained feeling "pressed to provide all the key information even if you know it's not the most effective way". The authors state this perceived need directly opposed the stated learning preferences of both groups.

> "You don't give a lot of talks, so you feel pressed to provide all the key information even if you know it's not the most effective way, because you don't want them to never have heard it"
