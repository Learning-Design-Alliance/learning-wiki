Theoretical argument of this Perspective: the author distinguishes definitions of role modeling as demonstration of skills and feedback from definitions as unselective imitation, arguing that under the latter, "the benefits of role modeling should be weighed against its unintended harm."

> "However, if it is defined as a learner’s unselective imitation of role models and uncritical adoption of the messages of the learning environment, then the benefits of role modeling should be weighed against its unintended harm."
