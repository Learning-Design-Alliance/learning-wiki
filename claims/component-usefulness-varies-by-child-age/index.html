Digital Promise 2021¶

Digital Promise, The Jim Henson Company, Concord Evaluation Group, & Curious Media. (2021). Designing an app to support families' joint engagement with media: Design principles and lessons from research. [White Paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/121

q2 · i? · design · r3

Station study (38 families). The report states "The usefulness of the different app components varied and depended on the child's interest or age." Families with younger children still used clips, characters, environments, and activities. Qualitative pattern; no effect size printed.

"The usefulness of the different app components varied and depended on the child's interest or age. Certain features may have been more conducive for younger children, while others tended to work well with older children."
