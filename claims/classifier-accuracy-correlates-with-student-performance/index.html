Evidence¶

Rebecka Weegar · Peter Idestam-Almquist 2024¶

Rebecka Weegar · Peter Idestam-Almquist. (2024). Reducing Workload in Short Answer Grading Using Machine Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00322-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational finding reported in the Other Results section of the exam data set: classification performance correlated with student performance across the 64 questions. The authors suggest this was perhaps partly due to more variation among incorrect answers compared to correct answers.

"The classification performance correlated with the student performance. The classifier was more accurate with relatively more high-quality answers to learn from and less accurate for questions where the students struggled."
