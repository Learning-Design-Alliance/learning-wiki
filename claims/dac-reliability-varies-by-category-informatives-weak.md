## Evidence

### Erkens 2008

Erkens, G., & Janssen, J. (2008). Automatic coding of dialogue acts in collaboration protocols. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 3, 447-470. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-008-9052-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability analysis on the same 1,000-message Study 2 protocol (Table 4). The article notes "Especially the negative evaluations (InfEvlNeg) and nonsense statements (InfStmNon) are considerably below the 0.70 criterion" and that students' chat abbreviations (e.g., "luvU") were often unrecognized.

> "the category Kappa 's range from an unacceptable 0.04 (0.12 without InfStm?) to an excellent 1.00. When judging these kappa 's against Krippendorff 's standard of a minimum agreement of 0.70, 5 of the 29 coding categories fall short of this criterion"
