# Systematically designed E&M instruction based on the First Principles of Instruction model produced significantly higher domain-specific CT scores than regular instruction

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The experimental condition scored significantly higher than the control condition on the CTEM domain-specific CT test (d = 1.55). [→ Tiruneh 2016](#tiruneh-2016)

## Evidence

### Tiruneh 2016

Tiruneh, D. T., Weldeslassie, A. G., Kassa, A., Tefera, Z., De Cock, M., & Elen, J. (2016). Systematic design of a learning environment for domain-specific and domain-general critical thinking skills. Education Tech Research Dev, 64, 481–505. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9417-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A pretest–posttest quasi-experiment with 45 experimental and 44 control first-year physics students. An independent samples t test showed the "CTEM mean score for the experimental group was significantly higher than that of the control group", d = 1.55, exceeding Cohen's convention for a large effect.

> "The results indicated that the CTEM mean score for the experimental group was significantly higher than that of the control group, t(87) = 7.15, p < .001, d = 1.55."
