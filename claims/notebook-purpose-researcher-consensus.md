### Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski 2017

Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Recommendations for the use of notebooks in upper-division physics lab courses. [doi:10.1119/1.5001933](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5001933)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative synthesis of interviews with 13 physics graduate student researchers at the University of Colorado Boulder, all in table-top experimental subfields. The authors report researchers described the notebook as 'the memory of the experiment' and agreed on its purposes.

> "In essence, the purpose of the notebook was to help make sense of the experiment, think through future directions of the project, create a foundation for publications, and communicate progress with others."
