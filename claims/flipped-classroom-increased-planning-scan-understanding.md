### Schuller BW 2022

Schuller BW, Burch C, Casterton T, Crowther C, Fowler J, Stenmark MH. (2022). Precision patient education using a “flipped classroom” approach. J Appl Clin Med Phys. https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13601

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post survey comparison in the pilot cohort, analyzed with a one-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank test for matched pairs (α = 0.05). Mean self-assessed understanding rose from "Avg, 3.7" to "Avg, 5.0" on a five-point Likert scale; the article reports p = 0.031 and no effect size.

> "Active learning increased patient understanding regarding the purpose of the treatment planning scan (before vs. after intervention [Figure 1a]: Avg, 3.7; 95% CI, 2.5–4.9 vs. Avg, 5.0; 95% CI, 5.0–5.0; p = 0.031)"
