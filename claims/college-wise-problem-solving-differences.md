`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

College-wise analysis of platform logs (Figure 8) comparing total users, chat messages, solved and unsolved problems. The largest group (957 users) did not specify a college, contributing 1,440 chat messages and 2,060 solved problems.

> "Among specific colleges, IIITD had the highest problem-solving efficiency (249 solved, 22 unsolved), while BITS (155 solved, 29 unsolved) and UPES (144 solved, only 3 unsolved) also showed strong performance. SVCET users engaged the most in chat (851 messages) but struggled with the highest failure rate (203 unsolved problems out of 769 attempts), suggesting more reliance on support."
