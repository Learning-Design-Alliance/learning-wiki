## Evidence

### Zeng H 2025

Zeng H, Zhu Z, Hu J, Cui Y. (2025). Application of ChatGPT-assisted problem-based learning teaching method in clinical medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06321-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report survey with six questions completed only by students in the PBL-ChatGPT-assisted group (n = 21); Table 3 shows most responses in the agree/strongly agree categories, e.g. 16 agree and 5 strongly agree that ChatGPT effectively assists with answering questions. The authors note self-reported data may be prone to bias.
