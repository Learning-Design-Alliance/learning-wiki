Evidence¶

Rowe 1975¶

Rowe, Wayne; Murphy, Harry B.; De Csipkes, Robert A. (1975). The Relationship of Counselor Characteristics and Counseling Effectiveness. Review of Educational Research, 45(2). https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543045002231

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's assessment of studies using global ratings of effectiveness as the sole criterion, citing Whiteley (1969) that such work is "popular, though unproductive". This is the review's own evaluative synthesis, not a pooled result.

"The lack of clarity concerning the basis for determining "effectiveness" makes replication fruitless and has brought forth the characterization of such work as popular, though unproductive (Whiteley, 1969)."
