de Jong Peter F.; Zee Marjolein. (2026). Help! I Have to Assist My Child With Reading and Spelling: Mothers' Teaching Self‐Efficacy and Mother–Child Conflict During Homework. Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.70048

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Path analysis in a cross-sectional survey of 153 mothers of children with dyslexia. The article reports that "the relations of Household Chaos and Support provided by the Clinic with MC‐Conflict were fully mediated by TSE" and that perceived stress was only partially mediated, leaving a small direct effect.

> "the relations of Household Chaos and Support provided by the Clinic with MC‐Conflict were fully mediated by TSE. The relation between Perceived Stress and MC‐Conflict was only partially mediated by TSE"
