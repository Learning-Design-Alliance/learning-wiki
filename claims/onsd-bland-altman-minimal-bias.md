## Evidence

### Betcher Joseph 2018

Betcher Joseph, Becker Torben K., Stoyanoff Peter, Cranford Jim, Theyyunni Nik. (2018). Military trainees can accurately measure optic nerve sheath diameter after a brief training session. Military Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40779-018-0189-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bland-Altman agreement analysis of right-eye ONSD measures by trainee vs. physician users in the same proof-of-concept study; the article reports "minimal bias" with trainee measures slightly lower on average. No standardised effect size was printed.

> "Using the Bland-Altman approach, the results showed that the mean difference between the measures of right eye optic sheath by trainee vs. physician users was − 0.004 mm (SE = 0.007, 95% CI = − 0.02 to 0.01, Fig. 3)."

### Betcher Joseph 2018 (2)

Betcher Joseph, Becker Torben K., Stoyanoff Peter, Cranford Jim, Theyyunni Nik. (2018). Military trainees can accurately measure optic nerve sheath diameter after a brief training session. Military Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40779-018-0189-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bland-Altman agreement analysis of left-eye ONSD measures in the same study; the article again reports "minimal bias" for trainee users. No standardised effect size was printed.

> "Also using the Bland-Altman approach, the results showed that the mean difference between the measures of left eye optic sheath by trainee vs. physician users was − 0.007 mm (SE = 0.01, 95% CI = − 0.03 to 0.01, Fig. 4)."
