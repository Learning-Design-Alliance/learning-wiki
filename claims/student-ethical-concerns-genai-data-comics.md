`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of Study Part 5 ethical-implication responses from 60 participants (Cohen's kappa 0.83). Four themes emerged: reinforcing misinformation, perpetuating biases, threatening ownership and intellectual property, and reducing trustworthiness; N=37 raised ownership and N=23 trustworthiness concerns.

> "A majority of participants (N=45) raised concerns about misinformation in using GenAI to support data comics creation, with two-thirds (N=40) highlighting bias and inequity issues."
