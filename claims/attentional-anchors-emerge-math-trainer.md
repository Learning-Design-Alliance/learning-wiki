## Evidence

### Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner 2022

Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner. (2022). Toward Synergizing Educational Research and Movement Sciences: a Dialogue on Learning as Developing Perception for Action. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09668-3

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Qualitative micro-genetic case analysis of Lars, a 14-year-old Dutch student, working on a Trainer variant task, with eye-gaze overlays showing his foveal gaze near the center of the imagined diagonals. The article notes "Lars's diagonal solution was quite typical". No effect size printed.

> "When Lars achieved fluent movement in green, he was asked to explain his method. Lars said he was attending to an imaginary diagonal line connecting the cursors"

### Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner 2022 (2)

Dor Abrahamson · Franz Mechsner. (2022). Toward Synergizing Educational Research and Movement Sciences: a Dialogue on Learning as Developing Perception for Action. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09668-3

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Qualitative analysis across the design-research program: the article reports evidence for a variety of attentional anchors across participants and, in Lars's case, the emergence of a positive-slope diagonal as a secondary attentional anchor from the primary negative-slope one. No effect size printed.

> "Already, though, we have witnessed the emergence of secondary attentional anchors from primary attentional anchors, as demonstrated, above, in the case study of Lars."
