## Evidence

### Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo 2021

Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo, Morán-García Javier, Abián Pablo, Abián-Vicén Javier. (2021). Association of the Use of the Mobile Phone with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18031042

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Simple linear regression with Pearson correlation across the 501-student cross-sectional sample yielded "r = 0.359; p < 0.001" between fitness and academic variables; the correlation is reported, not a causal effect.

> "A positive correlation was found between physical fitness and academic performance (r = 0.359; p < 0.001)."

### Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo 2021 (2)

International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18031042

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`
