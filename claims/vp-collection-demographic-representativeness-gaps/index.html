Evidence¶

Urresti-Gundlach M. 2017¶

Urresti-Gundlach M., Tolks D., Kiessling C., Wagner-Menghin M., Härtl A., Hege I. (2017). Do virtual patients prepare medical students for the real world? Development and application of a framework to compare a virtual patient collection with population data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1013-1

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive comparison of 66 coded VPs against German Federal Statistical Office data. The article reports "One VP (1.5%) was described as intellectual disabled" versus 12.7% in the population, and similarly reports 1.5% migration coverage versus 20.3%, and 0% unemployment versus 6.7%.

"One VP (1.5%) was described as intellectual disabled; in all other cases no physical or mental disability was mentioned. In 2013 the percentage of disabled persons in the German population was 12.7% [22]."

Urresti-Gundlach M. 2017 (2)¶

Urresti-Gundlach M., Tolks D., Kiessling C., Wagner-Menghin M., Härtl A., Hege I. (2017). Do virtual patients prepare medical students for the real world? Development and application of a framework to compare a virtual patient collection with population data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1013-1

q2 · i? · design · r2

Author interpretation of the age distribution comparison shown in Fig. 1, noting that "this age peak coincides with the age of the VP authors, who were mainly residents in that age range."

"The age distribution showed an unrealistic peak of VPs aged 30–39. Interestingly, this age peak coincides with the age of the VP authors, who were mainly residents in that age range."
