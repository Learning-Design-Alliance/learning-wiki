# Pre-reading expert referent networks and post-reading network-based writing feedback do not improve semantic quality of integration

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither the pre-reading expert network (Study 1) nor network-based writing feedback (Study 2) produced significant main effects or interactions on semantic quality of integration. [→ Ziqian Wei 2025](#ziqian-wei-2025)

## Evidence

### Ziqian Wei 2025

Ziqian Wei, Shuting Yin, Roy B. Clariana, Xuqian Chen. (2025). The influence of pre-reading purpose and extra-textual networks with summary writing on multiple document concept network integration: a replication of Wei et al. (2024). Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10508-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In Study 1, a 2×2×3 repeated measures ANOVA on the similarity of integrative links found no significant main effects of prompts, referent network, or writing stage, and no interactions.

> "Second, regarding the similarity of integrative links (semantic quality), results showed no significant main effects or interactions, all p-values >.05. Therefore, H2a and H2b were not supported."
