## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Learners who watched an instructional video with a visible instructor reported higher social presence than learners who only heard the instructor's voice (d = 0.85). [→ Steffi Heidig 2024](#steffi-heidig-2024)

## Evidence

### Steffi Heidig 2024

Steffi Heidig, Maik Beege, Günter Daniel Rey, Sascha Schneider. (2024). Instructor presence in instructional videos in higher education: three field experiments in university courses. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10391-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory field study with 18 college students randomly assigned to visible or no visible instructor conditions. The t-test showed a significant difference for social presence with "a large effect size (d  = 0.85)" favoring the visible instructor condition.

> "They showed a significant difference for social presence ( t (16) = 1.80, p = .045) with a large effect size (d  = 0.85) indicating that learners who watched an instructional video with a visible instructor experienced higher social presence than learners who only heard the instructor's voice."
