## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Singaravelu 2012

Singaravelu, G. (2012). Discovery Learning Strategies in English. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 2 No. 1, January - March 2012. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070208

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Rotational group experimental study with 30 experimental-group pupils of standard V; pre-test mean 57.43 (S.D. 5.07) and post-test mean 86.76 (S.D. 6.72), with the calculated 't' value (19.08) greater than table value (2.00).

> "In Table 2, the calculated 't' value is (19.08) greater than table value (2.00). Hence null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 levels."

### Singaravelu 2012 (2)

Singaravelu, G. (2012). Discovery Learning Strategies in English. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 2 No. 1, January - March 2012. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070208

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the control group's post-test (mean 57.43, S.D. 5.07) with its second post-test after the discovery learning treatment (mean 79.86, S.D. 5.87), with 30 pupils per stage and 't' value (15.83).

> "In Table 3, the calculated 't' value is (15.83) greater than table value (2.00). Hence null hypothesis is rejected at 0.05 levels."
