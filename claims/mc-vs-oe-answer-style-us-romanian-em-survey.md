Written E&M survey (2002-2003) given to 74 American students at The Ohio State University and 52 Romanian students at Bucharest University; results summarized in Tables 5.2-5.6. The author reports "in average slightly better" for American students but calls the comparison irrelevant due to unequal sizes.

> "American students did in average slightly better than Romani an students did (see Tables 5.2, 5.3, 5.4, 5.5), even though a comparison of this ty pe has no relevance, the number of Romanian students being smaller than the number of American students."

### C. M. Răduță 2013 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

The same written survey's qualitative observation: the presented American answer uses "the minimum number of formulas, words or symbols necessary", while Romanian students try "to explain in words each step needed for solving the problem, in the reasoning process".

> "the American student is very brief in his answers, writing mostly formulas (and this held true for almost all Romanian and American students who gave reasonable good answers for the problems as one can see also from Table 5.6)"
