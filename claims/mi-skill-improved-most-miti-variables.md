## Evidence

### Forsberg Lisa 2010

Forsberg Lisa, Forsberg Lars G, Lindqvist Helena, Helgason Asgeir R. (2010). Clinician acquisition and retention of Motivational Interviewing skills: a two-and-a-half-year exploratory study. Substance Abuse Treatment, Prevention, and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1186/1747-597X-5-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational coding study in which three Swedish National Tobacco Quitline counsellors had three sessions MITI 3.0-coded at each of 11 assessment periods over two-and-a-half years; the authors report counsellors "improved their MI skill compared to baseline level of skill in most of the MITI variables".

> "Moreover, our findings show that counsellors improved their MI skill compared to baseline level of skill in most of the MITI variables, though some variables took longer to improve."
