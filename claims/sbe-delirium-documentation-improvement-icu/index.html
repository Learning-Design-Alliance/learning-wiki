Evidence¶

EL Hussein Mohamed Toufic 2026¶

EL Hussein Mohamed Toufic, Sheehan Dawson S., Hirst Sandra. (2026). Simulation‐Based Education to Support Nurses' Critical Thinking in Delirium Recognition: A Scoping Review. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70640

q2 · i? · design · r2

This was a quality-improvement project using Lean Six Sigma with PDSA cycles in an urban tertiary-care ICU, tracking nursing documentation of delirium screening per 12-h shift via the EHR. Documentation rose "from 40% to 93% on day shifts and 27% to 71% on night shifts, following SBE and workflow redesign"; the review reports these as descriptive percentages, with no effect size.

"Aparanji et al. (2018) recorded substantial improvements in ICU delirium‐assessment documentation, from 40% to 93% on day shifts and 27% to 71% on night shifts, following SBE and workflow redesign."
