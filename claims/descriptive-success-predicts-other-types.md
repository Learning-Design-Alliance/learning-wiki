## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Abid H. Mujtaba 2016

Abid H. Mujtaba. (2016). Analyzing student conceptual understanding of resistor networks using binary, descriptive, and computational questions. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1611.06583](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1611.06583)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Conditional-probability analysis of the 28 students' responses projected onto a Venn diagram: 18 succeeded on binary, 16 on computational, 5 on descriptive. The article reports "P (B|D) = P (C|D) = 1" and "P (D|C) = 5 /16 ≈ 0.31".

> "The zeros in the D portion of the Venn Diagram led to P (B|D) = P (C|D) = 1, namely every student who answered the descriptive question successfully also managed to answer both the binary and computational questions correctly. However the converse was not true, P (D|C) = 5 /16 ≈ 0.31"
