## Evidence

### Diego Rojo 2024

Diego Rojo, Houda Lamqaddam, Lucija Gosak, and Katrien Verbert. (2024). Petal-X: Human-Centered Visual Explanations to Improve Cardiovascular Risk Communication. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.18690](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.18690)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled between-subject experiment (38 GSC, 50 Petal-X) with healthcare students performing four trials of tasks aligned to three communication challenges. The article reports a statistically significant speed advantage for Petal-X on T1 and T2, "by 10.2 and 4.1 seconds, respectively"; no effect size is printed.

> "The use of Petal-X led to a significantly shorter median duration of tasks T1 and T2 compared to GSC, by 10.2 and 4.1 seconds, respectively."
