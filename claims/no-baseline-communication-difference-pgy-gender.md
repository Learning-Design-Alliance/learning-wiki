## Evidence

### Beaudoin Pier-Luc 2019

Beaudoin Pier-Luc, Labbé Mathilde, Fanous Amanda, Young Meredith, Rappaport Jamie, Park Yoon Soo, Manoukian John, Nguyen Lily H. P. (2019). Teaching communication skills to OTL-HNS residents: multisource feedback and simulated scenarios. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-019-0329-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

ANOVA comparison of entering ratings across PGY levels in the three-year cohort. The initial score, "regardless of PGY level, was not statistically different" (p = 0.201); equivalence was not tested.

> "The initial score of all participants on entering the curriculum, regardless of PGY level, was not statistically different (p = 0.201)."

### Beaudoin Pier-Luc 2019 (2)

Beaudoin Pier-Luc, Labbé Mathilde, Fanous Amanda, Young Meredith, Rappaport Jamie, Park Yoon Soo, Manoukian John, Nguyen Lily H. P. (2019). Teaching communication skills to OTL-HNS residents: multisource feedback and simulated scenarios. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-019-0329-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent t-tests compared mean assessment ratings across reported genders. There was "no significant difference in mean communication score across genders" (p = 0.119); equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in mean communication score across genders (p = 0.119)."
