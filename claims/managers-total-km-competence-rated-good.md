## Evidence

### Karsikas 2025

Karsikas, E., Meriläinen, M., Koivunen, K., & Kanste, O. (2025). Health and social care managers' self‐assessed competence in knowledge management: A descriptive cross‐sectional study. Journal of Advanced Nursing, 81, 787–797. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16237

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive cross‐sectional survey of 116 managers in six Finnish public organizations, analysed with descriptive statistics; the KM total competence sum variable had median 3.029 (range 3.80), described as "good," with 54.3% rating themselves good and 21.6% very good.

> "Table 2 shows that the health and social care managers' total self‐assessed competence level in KM was reported as “good,” with a median of 3.029 and a range of 3.80."
