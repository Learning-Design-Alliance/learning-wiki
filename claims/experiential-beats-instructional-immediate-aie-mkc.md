### Pau Benazet i Montobbio 2026

Pau Benazet i Montobbio, Janne Rotter, Davinia Hernández-Leo. (2026). Experiential Versus Instructional Approaches for Eliciting Metacognitive Awareness in AI-Assisted Learning: A Short-Term Longitudinal Study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20047 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.20047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2607.20047)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison of pre-post immediate change scores in a quasi-experiment with 126 first-year engineering students assigned by class schedule. The experiential group's AIE gain (+0.41) was more than twice the instructional group's (+0.18), and MKC favoured experiential (r = .34).

> "The intervention favoured AIE (U= 1242,p<.001,r= .37), with the experiential group (G2) exhibiting a change more than twice that of the instructional group (∆M= +0.41 vs. +0.18). MKC was also significantly increased by the intervention (U= 1315.5,p= .001,r= .34), with a substantive gain (+0.38) against a near-flat instructional group trajectory (+0.05)."
