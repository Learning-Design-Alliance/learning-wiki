## Evidence

### Wei 2016

Xia Wei. (2016). Research on the Boost of Development on Young Children's Fine Motor by Folk Games. International Education Studies, 9(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n9p111

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-factor quasi-experiment in one public urban kindergarten: a lower-scoring middle class received folk games activities while a parallel class served as control, with pre-, intermediate and post surveys of fine motor. The post-test general average was 1.23 versus 1.14, and the article reports the intervention class "0.09 higher than the control class".

> "the post-test result (Table 3) indicated that fine motor skills level of the intervention class has gained obvious improvement with 0.09 higher than the control class, which was showed by the result of fine motor test."
