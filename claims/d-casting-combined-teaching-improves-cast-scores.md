# Combining D-Casting with traditional teaching produces significantly higher cast scores in a physical environment than traditional teaching alone

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The D-Casting group's scores of the casts were statistically significantly higher than the control group's (P = 0.042). [→ Wang YiZhen 2025](#wang-yizhen-2025)
`q3 i?` The D-Casting group's D-Casting module test scores were also statistically significantly higher than the control group's (P < 0.001). [→ Wang YiZhen 2025 (2)](#wang-yizhen-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Wang YiZhen 2025

Wang YiZhen, Li Yi, Ma ChaoYi, Zhang WeiYi, Yuan ZiYa, Zhu Qian, Zhao XiaoHan, Song Jinlin, Yang Sheng, Ji Ping, Pang MengWei. (2025). Effectiveness evaluation of a serious game for dental lost wax casting training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06643-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized skill transfer assessment with 78 third-year dental technology students assigned by random number table to traditional instruction or traditional instruction combined with D-Casting; an independent instructor evaluated casts produced in a physical environment. The article reports no standardized effect size.

> "The scores of the casts of the D-Casting group were statistically significantly higher than that of the Control Group (82.26[13.711] vs. 89.64[6.796]; Z = -2.033; P = 0.042)."

### Wang YiZhen 2025 (2)

Wang YiZhen, Li Yi, Ma ChaoYi, Zhang WeiYi, Yuan ZiYa, Zhu Qian, Zhao XiaoHan, Song Jinlin, Yang Sheng, Ji Ping, Pang MengWei. (2025). Effectiveness evaluation of a serious game for dental lost wax casting training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06643-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U test on the virtual D-Casting module test from the same randomized assessment with 39 students per group; the article reports no standardized effect size.

> "The D-Casting module test scores of the D-Casting group were statistically significantly higher than that of the Control Group (84.21[13.694] vs. 94.26[8.629]; Z = -3.482; P < 0.001)."
