# Bayesian multilevel ordinal models confirm the upward GPA trend, with cumulative odds of a higher GPA category 43% greater for 2020 than 1999 admits

## Evidence

### Lee V 2026

Lee V, Kuncel NR, Sackett PR. (2026). Graduate grade inflation at a U.S. research-intensive university: A 22-year longitudinal analysis. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0341315

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis fitting Bayesian multilevel ordinal models with GPA grouped into six categories; the printed odds ratio OR = 1.43, 95% Crl = [1.28, 1.60] confirms the upward trend under an alternative model choice. Degree-specific models showed the same trend.

> "the cumulative odds of being in a higher GPA category were 43% greater for students admitted in 2020 compared to those admitted in 1999 (OR = 1.43, 95% Crl = [1.28, 1.60])."
