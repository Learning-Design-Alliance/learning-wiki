## Evidence

### Torkani F 2025

Torkani F, Ahmadi S-H, Binazir A, Fath MM, Shafigh N, Asadiparvar-Masouleh H, Vasli P. (2025). The relationship between health-related quality of life and academic success in nursing students: the mediating role of academic burnout and academic motivation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06661-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct-path coefficients (Table 4, paths c1–c3) from the path analysis of 261 Iranian nursing students, with GPA as the outcome. The article prints significant unstandardized effects for both motivation types and a null amotivation path.

> "The results indicated a significant direct effect of intrinsic motivation on AS (c1, p ≤ 0.05) and of extrinsic motivation on AS (c2, p ≤ 0.05). This implies that a one-unit increase in intrinsic motivation and extrinsic motivation is associated with a 0.02 and 0.05 increase in AS, respectively. In contrast, the direct path from amotivation to AS was not significant (c3, B = -0.002, p = 0.88)."
