## Evidence

### Patience E. Idoga 2022

Patience E. Idoga, Dokun Iwalewa Oluwajana, Ibrahim Adeshola. (2022). Educational quality and technological complexity on recognition of enhanced learning platform in developing countries using PLS-SEM in a post COVID era. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10150-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM structural model analysis of 299 instructor questionnaires collected from three private Nigerian schools, tested with ADANCO 2.0.1. The path from self-efficacy to perceived ease of use was supported: "(β = 0.210, p < 0.01)". No standardized effect size beyond the path coefficient is printed.

> "the study result indicates instructors’ self-efficacy shows a positive impact on perceived ease of use of the e-learning platform (β = 0.210, p < 0.01)"
