### Azzurra Ruggeri 2022

The review reports, citing Ruggeri and Lombrozo (2015), that children aged 7 to 10 generate different question types depending on the likelihood distribution, and 'they do this as promptly as adults (Ruggeri & Lombrozo, 2015).' No effect size is printed.

> "Seven- to 10-year-olds generate different types of questions—hypothesis scanning and constraint seeking—depending on the likelihood distribution of the hypotheses under consideration."
