## Evidence

### Kurz 2026

Kurz, T. L., Jayasuriya, S., Swisher, K., Mativo, J., Pidaparti, R., & Robinson, D. T. (2026). Computer vision versus human vision: analyzing middle school teachers' construct restructuring following computer vision professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10594-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of the human vision dendrograms in the same pre/post repertory grid study shows only one construct shift: Construct 17 (using data) moved from cluster 2 to cluster 1. The authors state "This was the only construct shift in relation to human vision," supporting the stability of human vision perceptions.

> "In the pre-PD dendrogram, Human vision can be described as…using data (Construct 17) moved from cluster 2 to cluster 1. Participants viewed this as more descriptive of human vision after PD. This was the only construct shift in relation to human vision."
