## Evidence

### Zhang Wanting 2025

Zhang Wanting, Sun Junhua, Yao Haichang. (2025). The impact of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation in urban-rural interactive classrooms: a moderated mediation model. BMC Psychology, 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03313-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (PROCESS Model 4, 10,000 resamples, 95% CI) on the 449-student sample; the indirect effect via classroom atmosphere was 0.156 (26.35% of total effect) with a confidence interval excluding zero, supporting H2.

> "the Bootstrap 95% confidence interval for the mediating effect of classroom atmosphere on the relationship between learning efficiency and teaching evaluation does not include zero in either the lower or upper limits. The direct effect (0.437) and mediating effect (0.156) account for 73.65% and 26.35% of the total effect, respectively."
