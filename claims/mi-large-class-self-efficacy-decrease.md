# Students in large Modeling Instruction introductory physics courses showed a statistically significant decrease in overall physics self-efficacy

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Mean SOSESC-P total scores dropped significantly from pre to post in the Fall 2014 and 2015 MI courses, with a small to medium effect size (Cohen's d = 0.40). [→ Remy Dou 2016](#remy-dou-2016)

## Evidence

### Remy Dou 2016

Remy Dou, Eric Brewe, Justyna P. Zwolak, Geoff Potvin, Eric A. Williams, and Laird H. Kramer. (2016). Beyond performance metrics: Examining a decrease in students' physics self-efficacy through a social networks lens. Physical Review Physics Education Research 12, 020124. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.12.020124

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Dependent samples t test comparing pre and post SOSESC-P total scores for all students enrolled in the Fall 2014 and 2015 MI courses, with multiple imputation yielding N = 147, found a significant drop "with a small to medium effect size (Cohen 's d ¼ 0.40)."

> "The outcome revealed a statistically significant drop in physics-related self-efficacy from the beginning of the semester to the end of the semester [ tð146Þ¼ −4.75, p< 0.001] with a small to medium effect size (Cohen 's d ¼ 0.40)."

### Remy Dou 2016 (2)

Remy Dou, Eric Brewe, Justyna P. Zwolak, Geoff Potvin, Eric A. Williams, and Laird H. Kramer. (2016). Beyond performance metrics: Examining a decrease in students' physics self-efficacy through a social networks lens. Physical Review Physics Education Research 12, 020124. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.12.020124

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article reports the 3.79% average drop masks wide individual variation: "students ranged from a 29% decrease from prescore to a 46% increase from prescore," which the authors call a living example of contrary affective outcomes in the same course.

> "Though we report a somewhat minor 3.79% overall drop in students ' physics self-efficacy, this is an average measure. Individually, students ranged from a 29% decrease from prescore to a 46% increase from prescore."
