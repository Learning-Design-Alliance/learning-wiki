Marcus Hasselhorn & Kathrin Linke-Hasselhorn. (2013). Fostering Early Numerical Skills at School Start in Children at Risk for Mathematical Achievement Problems: A Small Sample Size Training Study. International Education Studies, 6(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n3p213

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same factorial ANOVA on the eight children's EMBI scores: the Group main effect was not significant, F(1,6) = 5.55, p > .05. The article attributes this to "extremely low test power"; no effect size is printed for this null result.

> "A 2 (group) x 2 (measurement point) factorial analysis of variance with repeated measurement of the measurement point (pre- vs. posttest) did not produce any main effect at the previously determined level of significance ( = .05) for Group (F(1,6) = 5.55, p > .05)."
