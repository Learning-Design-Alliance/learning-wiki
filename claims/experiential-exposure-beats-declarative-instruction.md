Theoretical argument, not an empirical test in the AI domain: the article cites prior findings that declarative knowledge about cognitive biases does not reliably produce behavioral debiasing, and that supportive defenses are weaker than refutational ones. The author presents the application as empirically untested.

> "The key claim, grounded in inoculation theory's six decades of evidence, is that Phase 2 cannot be replaced by instruction alone."
