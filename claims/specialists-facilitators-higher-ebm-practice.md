`q3 i?` Specialists (mean 86.6) scored higher than non-specialists (83.7) on EBM practice (P = 0.029), and doctors with high facilitating factors (92.8) scored higher than moderate (82.7) and low (80.0) levels (both P < 0.001). [→ Albart Stephenie Ann 2025](#albart-stephenie-ann-2025)

MANCOVA practice-score results from the same survey, adjusted for years of service. The quote gives the facilitating-factor contrast; the text also reports "The mean practice score for specialists (mean = 86.6, SE = 1.25) was statistically significantly higher than non-specialists (mean = 83.7, SE = 0.86)" at P = 0.029.

> "The mean practice score for the high level of facilitating factors (mean = 92.8, SE = 1.17) was statistically significantly higher than the moderate (mean = 82.7, SE = 0.85) and low level of facilitating factors (mean = 80.0, SE = 1.49), with both P < 0.001."
