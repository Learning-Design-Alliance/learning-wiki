## Evidence

### Gu Zheyao 2026

Gu Zheyao, Xi Ting, Zhang Chunyuan. (2026). DeepSeek-assisted problem-based learning for glaucoma education in an undergraduate ophthalmology clerkship: a randomized educational pilot study. Scientific Reports 16, 24184. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-58447-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mini-CEX rated independently by three faculty raters (mean Cohen's κ = 0.74) for both groups (n = 37 each). Table 2 reports all seven domains significant, e.g. medical history interview skills 5.86 ± 1.32 vs 4.92 ± 1.28 (p = 0.002, d = 0.72).

> "the PBL-DeepSeek group showed statistically significant improvements in counseling skills, professional clinical competence, clinical judgment and overall clinical competence (all P < 0.05). Effect sizes were moderate to large, with Cohen’s d values ranging from 0.49 to 0.77"
