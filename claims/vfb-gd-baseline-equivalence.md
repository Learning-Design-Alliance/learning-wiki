`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison in the RCT (GD n = 37, VFB n = 39) used Mann-Whitney U and chi-square tests; the article reports no significant differences in age (p = 0.183), sex (p = 0.242), internship duration (p = 0.188), or baseline OSLER scores. No effect size is printed.

> "Baseline OSLER scores (OSLER1) were also comparable between the groups (GD: 4.38 ± 1.04; VFB: 5.10 ± 2.09; p = 0.213)."
