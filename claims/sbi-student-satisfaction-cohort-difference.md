## Evidence

### Jitendra 2016

Jitendra, A. K., Harwell, M. R., Karl, S. R., Dupuis, D. N., Simonson, G., Slater, S. C., & Lein, A. E. (2016). Schema-based instruction: The effects of experienced and novice teacher implementers on seventh-grade students' proportional problem solving. Learning and Instruction, 44, 53-64. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.03.001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-sample t-tests of student Treatment Acceptability Rating Scale-Revised (TARF-R) scores comparing Cohort 1 and Cohort 2 students. The article reports "student attitudes regarding SBI were significantly different (t = 4.01, df = 1791, p < .001) across cohorts," with Cohort 1 students rating SBI higher on a 1-to-4 scale.

> "The analyses (two-sample t-tests) of the TARF-R identified that student attitudes regarding SBI were significantly different (t = 4.01, df = 1791, p < .001) across cohorts. On a scale of 1 to 4, students in Cohort 1 classrooms had a mean score of 2.92 (SD = 0.58) and students in Cohort 2 classrooms had a mean score of 2.81 (SD = 0.61)."
