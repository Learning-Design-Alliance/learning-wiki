## Evidence

### Walsh Jason L 2018

Walsh Jason L, Harris Benjamin H L, Denny Paul, Smith Phil. (2018). Formative student-authored question bank: perceptions, question quality and association with summative performance. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2017-135018

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Item difficulty analysis of the top 100 most answered questions per academic year found year 2 mean difficulty p values of 0.438 versus 0.370 and 0.362 for the two year 1 groups; "The year 2 questions were significantly easier compared with both year 1 academic years (p=0.001)".

> "The mean difficulty (p value) in the three groups was 0.370 (2013–2014, year 1) 0.438 (2014–2015, year 2) and 0.362 (2014–2015, year 1). The year 2 questions were significantly easier compared with both year 1 academic years (p=0.001)."
