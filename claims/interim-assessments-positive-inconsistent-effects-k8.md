## Evidence

### Konstantopoulos 2015

Konstantopoulos, S., Li, W., Miller, S., & van der Ploeg, A. (2015). Effects of Interim Assessments on the Achievement Gap: Evidence from an Experiment. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562166

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-level cluster randomized trial in Indiana (2009-2010) with 57 schools (35 treatment, 22 control) and nearly 20,000 students. Quantile regression with cluster-corrected standard errors estimated treatment effects on ISTEP+ and Terra Nova scores. The article reports estimates "on average around one-tenth of a SD in mathematics, but they were smaller in reading."

> "Across quantiles and across grades all treatment effect estimates both in mathematics and reading scores were positive. The estimates produced by grade K-8 analysis were on average around one-tenth of a SD in mathematics, but they were smaller in reading."
