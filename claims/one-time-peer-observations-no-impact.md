## Evidence

### Gormally Cara 2014

Gormally Cara, Evans Mara, Brickman Peggy. (2014). Feedback about Teaching in Higher Ed: Neglected Opportunities to Promote Change. CBE—Life Sciences Education 13(2). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.13-12-0235

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The essay's review, citing Spencer and Flyr (1992), reports that "one-time observations have been shown to have virtually no impact on faculty teaching, aside from influencing textbook selection." It also lists problems including observer bias toward similar teaching style and reluctance to give negative feedback.

> "one-time observations have been shown to have virtually no impact on faculty teaching, aside from influencing textbook selection (Spencer and Flyr, 1992), and may even lead to erroneous inferences (Weimer, 2002)."
