# The interaction effect between students' learning approaches and the three treatment modes on posttest scores was not significant.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The effect of treatment modes on posttest scores did not depend on students' learning approaches (surface or deep). [→ Hii 2013](#hii-2013)

## Evidence

### Hii 2013

Hii, S. C., & Fong, S. F. (2013). Effects of Multimedia-Based Graphic Novel Presentation on Critical Thinking among Students of Different Learning Approaches. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1018032

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANCOVA with pretest as covariate tested the interaction between treatment mode (GT, GN, GTN) and learning approach (surface, deep) on posttest scores. The 2-way interaction was not significant, F(2, 284) = 0.488, p = 0.614, indicating "no significant interaction effect between the treatment modes and the learning approaches".

> "It was found that there was no significant interaction effect between the treatment modes and the learning approaches, F(2, 284) = 0.488, p = 0.614."
