# Self-evaluation of group dynamics favoured tutored sessions in 2007 but trends were inconsistent in 2008

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In 2007, self-evaluated group dynamics were significantly higher in tutored sessions (p = 0.02 session 1; p = 0.03 session 2), but 2008 trends were inconsistent and combined-year comparisons showed no significant differences. [→ Hayashi Shogo 2013](#hayashi-shogo-2013)

## Evidence

### Hayashi Shogo 2013

Hayashi Shogo, Tsunekawa Koji, Inoue Chikako, Fukuzawa Yoshitaka. (2013). Comparison of tutored group with tutorless group in problem-based mixed learning sessions: a randomized cross-matched study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-158

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Session-level self-evaluation analysis (Table 1) in the cross-matched PBL1 study. 2007 group dynamics averages were 4.31 vs 3.88 (session 1, p = 0.02) and 4.57 vs 4.27 (session 2, p = 0.03); when sessions were combined, "no significant differences were found between the tutored and tutorless groups".

> "In particular, self-evaluation of group dynamics were significantly different. However, inconsistent trends were recognised in 2008."
