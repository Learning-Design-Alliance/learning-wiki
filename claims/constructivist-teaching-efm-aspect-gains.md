## Evidence

### Chen Zhuli 2025

Chen Zhuli, Yao Qiang, Huang Ming, Wang Guoyu. (2025). Effect of constructivist teaching in the improvement of midwives’ interpretation ability in fetal monitor graphs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07890-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons of pre- and post-training scores on the seven fetal monitoring key points in Table 4, with printed P values including < 0.001 for uterine contractions and variability, 0.002 for baseline rate, 0.005 for decelerations, and 0.001 for comprehensive evaluation.

> "statistically significant differences were found in the scores for identification of high-risk factors, interpretation of uterine contractions, interpretation of baseline fetal heart rate, interpretation of fetal heart rate variability, interpretation of fetal heart rate decelerations, and comprehensive evaluation for fetal monitoring training between pre- and post-training (P < 0.05)"

### Chen Zhuli 2025 (2)

Chen Zhuli, Yao Qiang, Huang Ming, Wang Guoyu. (2025). Effect of constructivist teaching in the improvement of midwives’ interpretation ability in fetal monitor graphs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07890-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Table 4 shows the accelerations item with median 0 (0, 1.0) both before and after training and P value 1.000. The study did not detect an improvement on this aspect; equivalence was not tested.

> "No statistically significant differences were found in the scores for interpretation of fetal heart rate accelerations (P > 0.05)."
