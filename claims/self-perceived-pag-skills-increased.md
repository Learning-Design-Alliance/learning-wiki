## Evidence

### Torres Anna 2020

Torres Anna, Horodeńska Magdalena, Witkowski Grzegorz, Torres Kamil. (2020). Hybrid simulation of pediatric gynecologic examination: a mix-methods study of learners' attitudes and factors affecting learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02076-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post self-assessment questionnaire (15 items, 0-10 scale) completed by 16 residents before and after the workshop; Table 1 shows e.g. pelvic exam of a girl below 12 years median 0 to 5 (p < 0.001) and communication with the child 4 to 7 (p < 0.001). The article reports p-values only, no effect sizes.

> "Self-perceived skills in PAG exam increased significantly after the workshop, with no change noted in regards to an adult exam (Table 1)."
