### Stolze Franka 2026

Stolze Franka, Lehm Manuel, Behme Daniel, Fuchs Erelle, Schwab Roland. (2026). Comparison of video-based and presentation-based self-directed instruction for learning basic neuroangiography. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10418-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 20 participants (VG n = 9, PG n = 11) performing neuroangiography on a silicone flow model; Mann-Whitney-U test showed the presentation group's median fluoroscopy time of "237.00 s" was significantly lower than the video group's 369.00 s (p = 0.031). No effect size printed.
