Evidence¶

Steffen P. Schmidgall 2020¶

Steffen P. Schmidgall, Katharina Scheiter, Alexander Eitel. (2020). Can we further improve tablet-based drawing to enhance learning? An empirical test of two types of support. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09513-6

q3 · i3 · causal · r3

ANOVA on drawing quality across the three drawing conditions, F(2,114) = 31.26, partial η2 = 0.35. The global support condition (M = 33.05%, SD = 13.83) also significantly outperformed local support; no support and global support did not differ (p = 0.77).

"It revealed that drawing quality differed significantly between conditions, F(2,114) = 31.26, MSE = 131.49, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.35. Bonferroni adjusted multiple comparisons revealed that the no support condition (M = 36.00%, SD = 11.80) constructed statistically significantly more accurate drawings than the local support condition (M = 16.93%, SD = 8.00, SE = 2.60 p < 0.001)."
