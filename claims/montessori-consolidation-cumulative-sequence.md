Marshall, C., Livingston, L., & Van Herwegen, J. (2026). Mapping the Montessori Mathematics Curriculum to Dehaene's Four Pillars of Learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00436-4

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' mapping argument for the consolidation pillar: sequences such as Number Rods, Spindle Box and Numbers and Counters offer "Repetition so that knowledge that is not yet fully acquired can be consolidated", with representations becoming progressively less concrete.

> "In the Montessori maths curriculum, Consolidation is achieved through the materials forming a cumulative, learning sequence; there is emphasis on preparing secure foundations for later, more advanced, learning and automatisation of knowledge."
