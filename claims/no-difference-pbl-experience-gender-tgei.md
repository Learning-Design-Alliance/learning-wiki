## Subclaims
`q3 i?` No differences in cognitive or motivational TGEI scores or overall group effectiveness were found between students with and without previous PBL experience, nor between men and women. [→ Edelbring Samuel 2020](#edelbring-samuel-2020)

## Evidence

### Edelbring Samuel 2020

Edelbring Samuel, Alehagen Siw, Mörelius Evalotte, Johansson AnnaKarin, Rytterström Patrik. (2020). Should the PBL tutor be present? A cross-sectional study of group effectiveness in synchronous and asynchronous settings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02018-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison across the full sample of 221 participating students (126 with and 94 without prior PBL experience; 192 women, 29 men). No effect sizes are printed, so the null findings indicate non-significant tests only, not equivalence.

> "There was no difference in scores for any of the cognitive or motivational aspects or in the overall rating of group effectiveness between women and men or between students with and without previous PBL experience."
