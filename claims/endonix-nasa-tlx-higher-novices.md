## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Median unweighted NASA-TLX total scores were higher in novices (285) than beginners (210) and experienced endoscopists (225), with novices higher on all six subdomains. [→ Hohlstein Philipp 2026](#hohlstein-philipp-2026)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Unweighted NASA-TLX (0–600 range) administered after simulator use in the congress sample. Novices scored "median 285 vs. 210 points" versus beginners and "285 vs. 225 points" versus experienced endoscopists; only test statistics and medians are printed, no effect size.

> "The total task load was higher in novices compared to beginners (median 285 vs. 210 points, P = 0.007), as well as compared to experienced endoscopists (median 285 vs. 225 points, P < 0.001, Fig. 5B)."
