Lataifeh, M. (2018). Attitude, Aptitude, and Amplitude (AAA): A framework for design driven innovation. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1808.00544](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1808.00544)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reviews attitude-behaviour research (citing Ajzen, 2005), reporting that intentions 'are found to forge an accurate predictor for a variety of actions' when people have control over performance; this is the base for the theory of planned behaviour.

> "Intentions on the other hand, are found to forge an accurate predictor for a variety of actions, particularly when people have control over performance like skills, emotion, and the opportunity to act upon intentions (Ajzen, 2005, pp. 99–115)."
