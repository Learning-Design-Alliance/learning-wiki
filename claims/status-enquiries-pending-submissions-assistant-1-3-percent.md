# At least 20.4% of queries in an online internship programme are status enquiries about pending submissions, which a corpus-grounded AI assistant served only 1.3% of the time

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Status enquiries about the progress of a pending submission account for at least 20.4% of queries, and the retrieval-constrained AI assistant served only 1.3% of them because a stored answer cannot report an individual's current status. [→ Gupta 2026](#gupta-2026)

## Evidence

### Gupta 2026

Gupta, J., Ayinampudi, P., Aditya B.M.V., Hegade, P., Sharma, R., Sharma, S., Meenakshi, V., Iyengar, S.R.S. (2026). What Students Actually Ask: Demand Structure and Automation Potential in a Hybrid Support System. arXiv:2609.38967. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38967 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.38967](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.38967)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective deployment study classifying all 4,093 operational query records by rule-based text classification (validated precision 75.0%). Of 148 AI-assistant resolutions, only 11 were status enquiries, "The last figure corresponds to 1.3% of all status enquiries"; status enquiries closed at a median of 40.8 hours.

> "Of the 148 queries resolved by the AI assistant, 60 were procedural information, 42 enrolment intent and 11 status enquiry. The last figure corresponds to 1.3% of all status enquiries. Status enquiries closed at a median of 40.8 hours."
