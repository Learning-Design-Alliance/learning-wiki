Liu 2025¶

Mental effort ratings (Paas-based 7-point scale) collected after each event in the 24-participant case study; Table 1 shows medians of 2 or 3. The article reports cognitive load was 'generally low' while participants 'did not achieve satisfactory performance in terms of perception and interpretation.'

"The measurement revealed that the cognitive load of perceiving and interpreting the seven classroom events was generally low."
