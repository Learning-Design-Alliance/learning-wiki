Evidence¶

Burns 2021¶

Burns, D., Espinoza, D., Ondrasek, N., & Yang, M. (2021). Students experiencing homelessness: The conditions and outcomes of homelessness among California students. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/students-experiencing-homelessness

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of 2015–16 CAASPP assessment results for California students in grades 3–8 and 11 compares proficiency rates. The report states that for students experiencing homelessness, outcomes "were 29% and 19%, respectively", against 48% ELA and 37% mathematics statewide. No effect size is printed.

"Statewide, 48% of all students met or exceeded state standards in English language arts in 2015–16, and 37% did so in mathematics. For students experiencing homelessness, those outcomes were 29% and 19%, respectively."

Burns 2021 (2)¶

Burns, D., Espinoza, D., Ondrasek, N., & Yang, M. (2021). Students experiencing homelessness: The conditions and outcomes of homelessness among California students. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/students-experiencing-homelessness

q2 · i? · associational · r3

The same CAASPP descriptive analysis reports subgroup proficiency among students experiencing homelessness. The report finds "fewer than 9%" of English learners experiencing homelessness met mathematics standards and "just 4%" of students with disabilities experiencing homelessness did so. No effect size is printed.

"For English learners experiencing homelessness, fewer than 9% met or achieved state standards in mathematics; for students with disabilities experiencing homelessness, this proportion was just 4%."
