## Evidence

### Mutluhan Yilmaz 2026

Mutluhan Yilmaz, Andrea Gauthier, Mutlu Cukurova. (2026). Supporting online learners' regulation skills with the help of learning analytics and generative artificial intelligence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10595-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 21 low-SRL students per condition in the MOODLE statistics course; Mann–Whitney U on post-scores found the AI-low condition outperformed Tutor-low on goal setting, "with a moderate effect size as indicated by the Rank-Biserial Correlation (r = 0.388)".

> "there were significant differences between the Tutor-low control condition (16.83) and the AI-low experimental condition (26.17), with the AI-based low cluster performing better (U = 122.500, p = 0.012, r = 0.388)"
