### Ahlborg Liv 2015

Ahlborg Liv, Weurlander Maria, Hedman Leif, Nisell Henry, Lindqvist Pelle G., Felländer-Tsai Li, Enochsson Lars. (2015). Individualized feedback during simulated laparoscopic training: a mixed methods study. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.55a2.218b

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative theme from the focus group interviews: students stated the authenticity of the situation, i.e. face validity, was important, and introducing the session in the context of a patient case seemed an important factor.

> "Both groups perceived the session in the simulator as authentic, particularly the feedback group. The connection to the patient case was important for both groups."
