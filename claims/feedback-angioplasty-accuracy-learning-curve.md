### Trehan Abhishek 2015

Trehan Abhishek, Barnett-Vanes Ashton, Carty Matthew J, McCulloch Peter, Maruthappu Mahiben. (2015). The impact of feedback of intraoperative technical performance in surgery: a systematic review. BMJ Open. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4480038/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Three-armed RCT by Boyle et al in simulated renal artery angioplasty with 18 surgeons, six cases per surgeon, assessing procedure-specific outcomes including contrast volume, fluoroscopic time, balloon placement accuracy, residual stenosis and lesion coverage. Balloon placement accuracy improved significantly in the feedback group.

> "balloon placement accuracy was shown to be significantly improved in those receiving feedback (p=0.041)"
