## Evidence

### Zhang Wanting 2025

Zhang Wanting, Sun Junhua, Yao Haichang. (2025). The impact of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation in urban-rural interactive classrooms: a moderated mediation model. BMC Psychology, 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03313-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation regression (PROCESS Model 5) on the 449-student sample with gender and participation frequency as controls; the interaction term was β = -0.363, t = -2.788, significant at p < 0.01, supporting H3.

> "the interaction term between Network environment and study efficiency significantly and negatively predicted teaching evaluations (β= -0.363, P < 0.01), indicating that Network environment moderated the predictive effect of study efficiency on teaching evaluations"

### Zhang Wanting 2025 (2)

Zhang Wanting, Sun Junhua, Yao Haichang. (2025). The impact of learning efficiency on teaching evaluation in urban-rural interactive classrooms: a moderated mediation model. BMC Psychology, 13. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03313-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slope analysis (Fig. 3) splitting participants by M ± 1 SD network environment; the learning efficiency effect declined from β = 0.4781 to β = 0.2264 as network availability improved, with all three slopes significant.

> "Under low Network environment levels (M − 1 SD = -0.3463), study efficiency exerted the strongest effect on teaching evaluations (β = 0.4781, p < 0.001). At moderate Network environment levels (Mean = 0), the effect remained significant but diminished (β = 0.3522, p < 0.001). Under high Network environment levels (M + 1 SD = 0.3463), the effect further declined to β = 0.2264 (p = 0.011)."
