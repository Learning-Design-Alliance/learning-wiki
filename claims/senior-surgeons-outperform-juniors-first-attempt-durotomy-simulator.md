`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Validation study of the simulator with 21 participants across training levels; success was judged by a third-party observer checking for leak at maximum saline flow. First-attempt success rates "0% for PGY-1s" versus "50% for attending surgeons".

> "On the first attempt, success rate of durotomy repair as determined by absence of leak at attempt completion was 0% for PGY-1s, 29% for PGY-2s, 25% for senior residents and fellows, and 50% for attending surgeons."
