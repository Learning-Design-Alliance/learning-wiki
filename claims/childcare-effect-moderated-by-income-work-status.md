## Evidence

### Garon-Carrier 2022

Garon-Carrier, G., Ansari, A., Letarte, M.-J., & Fitzpatrick, C. (2022). Early childcare enrollment and the pursuit of higher education: A Canadian longitudinal study. Learning and Instruction, 80. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101615

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Interaction terms tested one at a time in weighted logit models on matched data with Bonferroni-corrected alpha of <0.006. Simple slopes showed informal care favored middle-to-high income children (b= 0.83) while formal care was associated with enrollment for low-income and non-working families' children.

> "Of the eight interactions estimated (see Table 3), household income (see Figure S1a) and the mother work status (see Figure S1b) were found to be significant moderators of the pursuit in higher education."
