### Fritúz Gábor 2026

Fritúz Gábor, Kovács Gergely, Kállai András, Nagy Tamás, Maár Csaba, Szvath Petra, Berczi Máté, Fábry Szabolcs, Skultéti Dalma, Iványi Zsolt, Gál János, Kovács Enikő. (2026). The efficacy of BLS training among fifth-year medical students—a randomized, assessor-blinded, parallel group trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08606-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized assessor-blinded parallel-group trial in 32 fifth-year medical students randomized to a practical BLS examination (n=17) or additional practice with continuous assessment (n=15). The study reports "no significant difference in overall BLS skill retention between the two groups" at all three assessment time points.

> "There was no significant difference in overall BLS skill retention between the two groups (total score after two weeks: p = 0.764; after two months: p = 0.542; after one year: p = 0.791)."
