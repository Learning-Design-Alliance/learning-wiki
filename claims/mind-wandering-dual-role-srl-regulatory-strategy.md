The commentary summarizes Bühler et al.'s (2025) systematic review of 134 studies mapping mind wandering onto the SMA grid. Cognitive and metacognitive processes were frequently examined while affective and motivational processes were underrepresented; assessment was typically via self-report probes, eye-tracking, or both.

> "They suggest that mind wandering can both prompt SRL (e.g., refocusing attention) and function as a regulatory strategy (e.g., managing frustration)."
