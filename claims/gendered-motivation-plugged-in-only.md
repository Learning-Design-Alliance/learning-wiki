## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Girls showed lower post-test motivation and willingness to pursue programming than boys, but only in the plugged-in condition (motivation d = 1.02; item 20 d = 0.88). [→ Sigayret 2022](#sigayret-2022)

## Evidence

### Sigayret 2022

Sigayret, K., Tricot, A., & Blanc, N. (2022). Unplugged or plugged-in programming learning: A comparative experimental study. Computers and Education, 184, 104505. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104505

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Gender-by-condition analysis of motivation items found strong effects favouring boys "in the plugged-in condition" (d = 1.02; item 20 d = 0.88); these effects were not observable in the unplugged condition.

> "On the other hand, in the plugged-in condition, these effects are strong both for motivation post-test without items 19 & 20 (F(1, 74) = 19.92; p < .001; d = 1.02) and for item 20 (F(1, 77) = 15.42; p < .001; d = 0.88)."
