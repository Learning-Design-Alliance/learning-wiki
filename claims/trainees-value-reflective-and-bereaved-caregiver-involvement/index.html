q2 · i? · review · r2

Thematic synthesis of self-reflection exercises and debriefing across the reviewed curricula. The review reports peer reflection activities "had a positive impact on trainees" and that debriefing opportunities "were also rated positively".

"Activities designed to reflect on and discuss grief with peers had a positive impact on trainees, encouraging them to delve more deeply into their experiences, feelings and concerns [39,59]. Opportunities to debrief in group sessions were also rated positively [59]."
