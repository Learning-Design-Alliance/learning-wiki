An analytical result in Section 8.1 deriving the 2PL Fisher information and the information-maximizing selection condition under mild smoothness of the timing transform g(τ).

> "For 2PL items, the Fisher information is Iq(θ) = a2qσ(aq(θ − bq))σ(−aq(θ − bq)). Under mild smoothness of g(τ ), selecting items near θ ≈ bq maximizes information; EDGE respects this while adding corrective gains from misconception resolution."
