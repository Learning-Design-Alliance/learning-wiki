# Simple slopes show the negative usage–attention effect is stronger when parental usage duration is high

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The predictive effect of short video usage on attention is significant at both low and high parental usage, with a steeper slope at high parental usage. [→ Gong Qiong 2024](#gong-qiong-2024)

## Evidence

### Gong Qiong 2024

Gong Qiong, Tao Ting. (2024). The relationship between short video usage and academic achievement among elementary school students: The mediating effect of attention and the moderating effect of parental short video usage. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309899

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slope analyses grouping parental usage at ±1 SD showed significant negative slopes at both levels, with the high-parental-usage slope steeper (-.163 vs -.071). The authors note "The slope values show an increasing trend".

> "when parental short video usage duration is low, the predictive effect of short video usage on attention is significant (simple slope = -.071, t = -3.811, p < .001). Similarly, when parental short video usage duration is high, the predictive effect of short video usage on attention remains significant (simple slope = -.163, t = -8.719, p < .001)."
