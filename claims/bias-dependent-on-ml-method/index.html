Bias analysis (research question 2) evaluated separation—whether models make correct and incorrect predictions at similar rates for different groups—on the best-performing models from research question 1, focusing on the technical aspect of bias.

"To investigate to what extent the predictions of students' learning outcomes exhibited bias with respect to gender or parental educational background, we focused on the best performing models from research question 1. For those models, we evaluated the predictive accuracy for each Gender and parental educational background separately."
