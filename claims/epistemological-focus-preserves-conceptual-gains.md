Pre-post conceptual testing across four offerings of the reformed first-semester course using FCI or FMCE, with fractional gain <g> computed as the standard figure of merit. The fall 2007 FMCE pre-post averages were 15% and 53%, yielding "the fractional gain was <g> = 0.44" (Fig. 4).

> "During the project, we taught the first semester course four times. A sample of our results are shown in figure 4). The average gains in the class ranged from 0.44-0.47."
