q2 · i? · associational · r1

Delphi survey ratings of 15 themes by 70 international experts. The article reports automated assessment shared the second-highest Impact rating and showed "moderate challenge (M = 7.13)", yielding a positive priority indicator.

"The second-highest Impact rating was shared by Automated formative assessment and feedback with AI agents (M = 8.13) and Evolved roles of teachers while teaching with AI agents (M = 8.13). However, their challenge profiles diverged: automated assessment showed moderate challenge (M = 7.13)"
