## Evidence

### Kimmo Eriksson 2019

Kimmo Eriksson, Ola Helenius, Andreas Ryve. (2019). Using TIMSS items to evaluate the effectiveness of different instructional practices. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9473-1

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-wave comparison of mean class-level instruction variables for the 36 countries participating in both TIMSS 2003 and 2007, illustrated in Fig. 1 dotplots. The article reports the correlations "r = .70" and "r = .91", indicating the measures reflect genuine differences in how math is taught across countries.

> "there were highly consistent country differences across waves with respect to mean levels of reported use of instructional practices, both for ClassListen, r = .70, p < .001, and ClassDaily, r = .91, p < .001."
