## Evidence

### Navarro Juan-José 2015

Navarro Juan-José, García-Rubio Javier, Olivares Pedro R. (2015). The Relative Age Effect and Its Influence on Academic Performance. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0141895

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

One-way ANOVA on the SIMCE global score of 15,234 Chilean eighth graders across five birthdate groups; Table 2 reports F = 23.24, p < .001, with G2 (Mean = 267.09) significantly higher than all other groups and G5 (Mean = 257.24) lowest. No effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Regarding the RAE, the data show that G1 has lower score than G2; however, from G2 to G5 it shows a declining score, in line with the minor age of the students."
