## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Joint presentation without instructions beat sequential presentation only for alignable differences on the heat flow scenarios, and did not differ from JP+I on that measure; for change of state it did not differentiate from the other conditions. [→ Mason 2016](#mason-2016)

## Evidence

### Mason 2016

Lucia Mason & Maria Caterina Tornatora (2016). Analogical encoding with and without instructions for case comparison of scientific phenomena. Educational Psychology, 36(2), 391-412. https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410.2014.953038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' summary of the pairwise comparisons in the Results: the un-instructed joint presentation condition outperformed sequential presentation on only one measure for one phenomenon, indicating limited effectiveness of merely lining up cases.

> "It is only for the scenarios of heat ﬂow phenomenon, when listing the aligned differences, that learners in the condition of joint presentation without instructions performed signiﬁcantly better than those in the condition of sequential presentation, without differing from participants in the condition with instructions."
