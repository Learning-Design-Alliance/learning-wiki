## Evidence

### Kuo 2015

Kuo, E., & Wieman, C. E. (2015). Seeking instructional specificity: an example from analogical instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020133)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test clicker questions in lecture, after direct instruction and textbook reading, for the same N = 231 sample. The combined direction-and-magnitude difference remained significant; individually the direction difference was 9% (p = .065) and magnitude 11% (p = .10), both marginal. No effect size printed.

> "Using the Mantel-Haenszel test to consider the direction and magnitude post-test questions together, there is still a significant difference at post-test between conditions, with GR students outperforming CC students, χ2MH (1, N = 462) = 5.35, p = .021."
