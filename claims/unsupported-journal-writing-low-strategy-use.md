## Evidence

### Nückles 2020

Nückles, M., Roelle, J., Glogger-Frey, I., Waldeyer, J., & Renkl, A. (2020). The Self-Regulation-View in Writing-to-Learn: Using Journal Writing to Optimize Cognitive Load in Self-Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09541-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Observational analysis of university students' learning journal entries written as follow-up coursework after weekly seminars with only a brief informal introduction. The review reports "journal writing without instructional support resulted in almost absent metacognitive strategies", and that realized knowledge construction strongly correlated with end-of-term test outcomes.

> "journal writing without instructional support resulted in almost absent metacognitive strategies and clear deficits in the use of cognitive strategies"
