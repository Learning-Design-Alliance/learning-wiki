`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results from the mixed-effects polynomial regression on quiz scores for the two randomly assigned instruction groups (25 lab, 25 textbook). Table 2 prints a main effect estimate of 0.04 (p = 0.10) and group-by-timepoint interaction p values of 0.64 and 0.23; no effect size is printed, so none is asserted.

> "There was no significant effect of instruction group (lab vs. textbook) on quiz performance, neither in the form of an interaction with the timepoint effect nor in the form of a main effect of instruction group (Table 2, Figure 2)."
