Evidence¶

Smagorinsky 1992¶

Smagorinsky, P., & Smith, M. W. (1992). The Nature of Knowledge in Composition and Literary Understanding: The Question of Specificity. Review of Educational Research, 62(3), 279-305. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543062003279

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Langer's finding that readers employed the same four stances in both literary and nonliterary reading, which she interprets as evidence the stances underlie meaning-making regardless of text type. No effect size is printed.

"the similarities in the process involved in responding across the different text types indicate that the four stances represent a range of meaning-making options that underlie developing understandings in general—regardless of text type"
