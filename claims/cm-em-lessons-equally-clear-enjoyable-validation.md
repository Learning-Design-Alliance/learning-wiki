Rachel Sortino, Christina Kim, Thalia Guettler, Katie McClyman, Lorna Quandt, Alicia Wooten & Rachel Pizzie. (2026). Producing concept-motivated signs supports learning of STEM content in American Sign Language. npj Science of Learning 11:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00418-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Online validation survey of teachers of the D/HH, ASL teachers, interpreters, and linguists (N = 30; n = 7-9 per video pair) judging video pairs; enjoyability similarly fit an equal-distribution model, and most participants (n = 19) found neither video frustrating.

> "We then compared our results with a predicted model of an equal distribution for preferences for "CM,"" EM," and "both" with a limited number of responses for"neither" and found no signiﬁcant difference between our data and this hypothesized model (X2(3) = 0.51,p = 0.918)."
