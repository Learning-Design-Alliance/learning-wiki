## Evidence

### Hazlett 2025

Hazlett, Z. S., Jimenez, P. C., & Knight, J. K. (2025). Self-Testing and Follow-Through of Learning Strategies Supports Student Success. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-04-0128

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-quiz survey responses (quizzes 1, 3, and 5) from students in three semesters of an introductory genetics course, combined across semesters (n = 1270 responses for options offered in all three semesters), show the article reports "self-testing", the most common choice at 79%, ahead of rereading notes at 68%. No effect size is printed.

> "Across all semesters, students overwhelmingly reported using the active cognitive strategy of self-testing ("Did Prep questions and/or Practice tests", 79%). The second most common strategy reported was the passive cognitive strategy of rereading notes (68%)."
