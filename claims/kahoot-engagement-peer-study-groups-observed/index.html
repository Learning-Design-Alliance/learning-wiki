Evidence¶

Iwamoto 2017¶

Iwamoto, D. H., Hargis, J., Taitano, E. J., & Vuong, K. (2017). Analyzing the efficacy of the testing effect using Kahoot on student performance. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 18(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1145220

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' observational interpretation from the discussion section, not a tested result: energy, engagement, and class dialogue were observed to be higher in the experimental group, alongside increased use of peer study groups.

"This level of enthusiasm was not observed in the control group. The energy level, engagement, and even relevant class dialogue was observed to be higher in the experimental group. An interesting side effect to this was an increase use of peer study groups."
