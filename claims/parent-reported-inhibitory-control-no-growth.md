## Evidence

### Fosco 2019

Fosco, W. D., Hawk, L. W. Jr., Colder, C. R., Meisel, S. N., & Lengua, L. J. (2019). The development of inhibitory control in adolescence and prospective relations with delinquency. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adolescence.2019.08.008

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent growth curve modeling of parent-reported EATQ-R inhibitory control across three annual waves in the same 387-family community sample. The article reports "no detectable changes in parent-reported inhibitory control over time"; significant intercept variance was observed, with average score at age 11 of 3.66 (range 1–5).

> "including a linear slope did not improve model fit, Δχ2 (3)= 4.9, p>.10, and the slope was non-significant, b=0.02, p=.22, suggesting no detectable changes in parent-reported inhibitory control over time"
