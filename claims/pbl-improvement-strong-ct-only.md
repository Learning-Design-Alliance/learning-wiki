## Evidence

### Pu Dan 2019

Pu Dan, Ni Juhua, Song Demao, Zhang Weiguang, Wang Yuedan, Wu Liling, Wang Xian, Wang Yun. (2019). Influence of critical thinking disposition on the learning efficiency of problem-based learning in undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1418-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Independent t-test on improvement scores (Strong-CT n = 28, Weak-CT n = 30). Table 6 prints PBL improvement scores of 0.875 ± 1.96 versus −0.508 ± 1.74, P = 0.006; preparation, communication skills and critical discussion dimensions also differed significantly.

> "The average improvement score of the CT-Strong group significantly increased; conversely, the score decreased in students with weak CT dispositions, particularly in the dimensions of preparation, communication skills and critical discussion dimensions"
