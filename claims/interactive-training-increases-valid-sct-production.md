### Gaillet Antoine 2026

Gaillet Antoine, Cottereau Anne-Ségolène, Cariou Alain, Lebeaux David, Lemogne Cédric, de Prost Nicolas, Vodovar Dominique, Roux Damien. (2026). Script concordance tests production optimization using enhanced interactive training: randomized, single-blind, bicentric trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09251-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized single-blind bicentric trial of 115 junior faculty producing SCTs before and after training. The trained arm produced significantly more valid SCTs 2 than the control arm, "n = 17/107 (16%) versus n = 40/98 (41%), p < 0.001".

> "there were significantly more valid SCTs 2 in the trained arm than in the control arm (n = 17/107 (16%) versus n = 40/98 (41%), p < 0.001, Table 2)"
