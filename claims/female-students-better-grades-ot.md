## Evidence

### Bonsaksen T. 2021

Bonsaksen T., Magne T. A., Stigen L., Gramstad A., Åsli L., Mørk G., Johnson S. G., Carstensen T. (2021). Associations between occupational therapy students’ academic performance and their study approaches and perceptions of the learning environment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02940-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate comparison in the sample of 174 (33 men, 141 women): mean exam grade 4.0 (SD 1.0) for women vs 3.3 (SD 1.2) for men, Cohen's d = -0.63. In the adjusted regression, "being female was associated with obtaining better exam results (β = 0.22, p < 0.01)".

> "The female students had obtained significantly better exam grades, compared to the male students (p < 0.001)."
