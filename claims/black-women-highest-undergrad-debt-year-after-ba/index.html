Education Trust analysis of NCES National Postsecondary Student Aid Study: 2016 Graduate Students and Baccalaureate and Beyond: 2016/2017 data, presented in Figure 1. The analysis finds "Black women hold more student debt than any other group" with the printed averages of $38,800 (undergraduate) and $58,252 (graduate). No effect size is reported.

"A year after completing a bachelor's degree, Black women hold more student debt than any other group — with an average of $38,800 in federal undergraduate loans. Black female borrowers who attended graduate school hold an average of $58,252 in graduate loans"
