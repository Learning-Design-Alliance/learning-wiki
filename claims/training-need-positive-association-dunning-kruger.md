## Evidence

### Xu Binbin 2026

Xu Binbin, Wang Yan, Mo Yun, Zhang Rong, Chen Hongyan, Zhou Xuezhi, Hu Li. (2026). Factors influencing nurses’ knowledge on peripherally inserted central catheter care in Guizhou, China: A web-based cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-55786-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Finding from the multiple linear regression on 3528 nurses (Table 3 reports B = 1.836, 95% CI: 0.878 to 2.794, p < 0.001). The authors propose reverse causation via awareness bias, conceptually consistent with the Dunning–Kruger effect, and caution that causality cannot be established.

> "Interestingly, nurses who expressed a need for further training scored higher on PICC care knowledge. This finding contrasts with our initial assumption that reporting unmet training needs might be associated with lower knowledge levels."
