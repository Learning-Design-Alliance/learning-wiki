### Cassandra Paul 2026

Cassandra Paul, David J. Webb, and Mary Kate Chessey. (2026). Missing Data on Physics Exams: Demographic Patterns, Course-Level Predictions, and Implications for Equity. [doi:10.1103/b4q5-dkn5](https://doi.org/10.1103/b4q5-dkn5)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subset analysis limited to students whose average of completed test items was a middle B or higher (a little over 40% of the dataset), shown in Fig. 3. The authors found that "the average number of uncollected test items from students who are in both group HM and 1stG is about twice the average number uncollected from students who are in neither of those two groups," holding for both the subset and the full dataset.
