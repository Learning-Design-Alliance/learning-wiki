### Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann 2021

Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann. (2021). Students' Motivational Trajectories and Academic Success in Math-Intensive Study Programs: Why Short-Term Motivational Assessments Matter. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000708

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Latent change score analysis of N = 1,004 first-semester students across three math-intensive programs, assessed at beginning (T1), midpoint (T5), and end (T6) of semester. Found "significant decreases in students' expectancy, intrinsic value, and utility value" and increases in costs, all ps < .001.

> "This motivational decline was characterized by significant decreases in students' expectancy, intrinsic value, and utility value (ΔM = −0.36 to −0.30, ps < .001) and corresponding increases in perceived psychological and effort costs (ΔM = 0.40 and ΔM = 0.26, ps < .001; see Table 2)."
