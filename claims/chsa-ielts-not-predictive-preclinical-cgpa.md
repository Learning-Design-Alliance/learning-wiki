### Dabaliz Al-Awwab 2017

Dabaliz Al-Awwab, Kaadan Samy, Dabbagh M. Marwan, Barakat Abdulaziz, Shareef Mohammad Abrar, Al-Tannir Mohamad, Obeidat Akef, Mohamed Ayman. (2017). Predictive validity of pre-admission assessments on medical student performance. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5a10.04e1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within the preclinical multiple linear regression of five pre-admission variables on cGPA, CHSA and IELTS were non-significant. The authors note the high school average was also not predictive at either phase, contrary to most prior studies, attributing this to Arabic-medium schooling and differing assessment styles.

> "Cumulative high school average and IELTS were not significant predictors of preclinical cGPA (p=0.17, p=0.691 respectively)"
