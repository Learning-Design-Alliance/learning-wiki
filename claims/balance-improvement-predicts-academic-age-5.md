# At age 5, improvement in balance is strongly associated with improvement in self-awareness, language, and knowledge of the environment

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Among 5-year-olds, children who improve in balance are 16.41 times more likely to improve in self-awareness (OR = 16.41, p < 0.001), 7.52 times more likely to improve in language (OR = 7.52, p = 0.002), and, with the other dimensions, between 1.29 and 7.88 times more likely to improve in knowledge of the environment.
