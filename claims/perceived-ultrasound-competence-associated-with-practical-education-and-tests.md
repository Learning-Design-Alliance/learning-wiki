# Perceived ultrasound competence was significantly associated with practical (RR: 4.08) or combined theoretical-and-practical education (RR: 4.34) in medical school and with being tested in ultrasound (adjusted RR: 1.55)

> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In modified Poisson regression adjusted for age, gender and university, practical education (RR 4.08) and both theoretical and practical education (RR 4.34) in medical school, and being tested in ultrasound (adjusted RR 1.55), were significantly associated with perceived competence acquisition; theoretical education alone was not significant. [→ Gram P 2024](#gram-p-2024)
`q2 i?` During internship, perceived competence was significantly associated with practical training on patients (adjusted RR 1.09), required number of scans (RR 1.06), competence tests (RR 1.06) and consulting books/encyclopedia (RR 1.07), but not with the number of educational components received. [→ Gram P 2024 (2)](#gram-p-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Gram P 2024

Gram P, Purup T, Eriksen EM, Søbye S, Wielandt SK, Astorp K, Jensen MB, Andersen CA. (2024). Ultrasound education, competencies and expected future use among medical interns in Denmark: a national cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06510-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Modified Poisson regression (adjusted for age, gender, university) in the results section reports adjusted RRs for practical education 4.08 (1.45–11.43, P = 0.008) and both types 4.34 (1.57–12.02, P = 0.005); theoretical alone had P = 0.148. The article also reports "having been tested in ultrasound correlated significantly with competency acquisition in medical school" (adjusted RR 1.55).

> "Competency acquisition in medical school correlated significantly with receiving practical or both theoretical and practical education, but not with theoretical education alone"

### Gram P 2024 (2)

Gram P, Purup T, Eriksen EM, Søbye S, Wielandt SK, Astorp K, Jensen MB, Andersen CA. (2024). Ultrasound education, competencies and expected future use among medical interns in Denmark: a national cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06510-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same regression models for internship components report adjusted RRs: practical training on patients 1.09 (1.00–1.19, P = 0.038), required number of scans 1.06 (P = 0.035), competence tests 1.06 (P = 0.020), books/encyclopedia 1.07 (P = 0.033). RRs are relative risks, not standardized effect sizes.

> "Among recipients of formal ultrasound education during internships, significant associations were found between obtained competencies and practical training on patients, required scan numbers, evaluated scanning competency, and consulting books/encyclopedia"
