# Engagement-based metrics are more robust predictors of students' perceived feedback helpfulness than pedagogical dimensions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In logistic regressions predicting high perceived helpfulness, RelScore (β = 0.420, p < 0.001) and SuccScore (β = 0.187, p < 0.001) are positively and significantly associated with helpfulness, while most pedagogical dimensions show weak or inconsistent associations. [→ Rose Niousha 2026](#rose-niousha-2026)

Three binary logistic regression models on student helpfulness ratings (954 ratings from 274 students under BaselineTutor; 2,915 ratings from 639 students under MisconceptionTutor), with a semester control. The quote reports "β= 0.420,p<0.001" for RelScore.

> "In the engagement-only model, bothRelScore(β= 0.420,p<0.001) andSuccScore(β= 0.187,p <0.001) are positively and significantly associated with higher perceived helpfulness."
