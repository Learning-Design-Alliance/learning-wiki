## Evidence

### Al-Madani 2015

Feras Mohammed Al-Madani. (2015). The Effect Of Blended Learning Approach On Fifth Grade Students' Academic Achievement In My Beautiful Language Textbook And The Development Of Their Verbal Creative Thinking In Saudi Arabia. Journal of International Education Research, 11(4). https://clutejournals.com/index.php/JIER

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA on post verbal creative thinking scores (Torrance test, empirically tested in the Arab environment from fourth grade through university level) for the same 49 students: F = 94.18, Sig. 0.000, favoring the blended learning group. Posttest means: 94.64 vs 92.

> "Table 4 shows the value of F statistics (94.18), its significance level (0.000) which indicates a statistical significant difference (α ≤ 0.05 ) between the students' mean scores in both groups in the post verbal creative thinking test in favor of the students in Experimental Group who studied using blended learning."
