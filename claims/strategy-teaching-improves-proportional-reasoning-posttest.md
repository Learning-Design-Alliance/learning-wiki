## Evidence

### Şen 2017

Şen, C., & Güler, G. (2017). Effect of Strategy Teaching For the Solution of Ratio Problems on Students' Proportional Reasoning Skills. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 5(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1142503

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of posttest Proportional Reasoning Test scores in a quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study (n=16 per group). The average rank was 21.59 for the experimental group and 11.41 for the control group, "in favor of the experimental group (U=46.500, p=.002<.05)".

> "This result shows that there was a statistically significant difference between the PRT posttest scores of the experimental and control groups in favor of the experimental group (U=46.500, p=.002<.05)."
