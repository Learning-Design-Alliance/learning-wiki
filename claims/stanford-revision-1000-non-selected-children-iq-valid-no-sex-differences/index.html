Entry 390 summarizes Terman, Lyman, Ordahl, Galbreath and Talbert's 1916 Journal of Educational Psychology article reporting the Stanford Revision applied to "1000 non-selected children," with the intelligence quotient "found a valid expression of intelligence" and practically no sex differences reported.

"Discuss tests upon 1000 non-selected children. Explain the basis and development of the revision. The intelligence quotient found a valid expression of intelligence. Practically no sex differences. Differences in social status not a disturbing influence in estimating mental age."
