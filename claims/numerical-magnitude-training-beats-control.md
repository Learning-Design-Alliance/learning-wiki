## Evidence

### Honoré N 2016

Honoré N, Noël M-P (2016) Improving Preschoolers' Arithmetic through Number Magnitude Training: The Impact of Non-Symbolic and Symbolic Training. PLoS ONE 11(11): e0166685. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0166685

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the collection comparison task in the randomized training study showed a significant time × group interaction, "F(2,50) = 4.09; p = .023, η2 = 0.140"; the discussion notes no performance increase was observed in the control group.

> "we found a significant interaction between time and group,F(2,50) = 4.09; p = .023, η2 = 0.140"

### Honoré Nastasya 2016

Honoré Nastasya, Noël Marie-Pascale. (2016). Improving Preschoolers' Arithmetic through Number Magnitude Training: The Impact of Non-Symbolic and Symbolic Training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0166685

`q3 · i?`

In the randomized training study, gains appeared in the trained tasks: collection comparison and large-numerosity non-symbolic number line after non-symbolic training, and Arabic-number number line after symbolic training. The authors add that "no performance increase was observed in the control group, demonstrating the efficiency of our training program compared to spontaneous development."

> "First, the trained process was enhanced. Indeed, gains were observed after non-symbolic training in collection comparison and in the positioning of large non-symbolic numerosities on the number line and after symbolic training in the positioning of Arabic numbers on a number line."
