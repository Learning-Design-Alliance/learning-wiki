## Evidence

### Pavlo D. Antonenko 2007

Pavlo D. Antonenko, John Jackman, Piyamart Kumsaikaew, Rahul R. Marathe, Dale S. Niederhauser, C. A. Ogilvie, and Sarah M. Ryan. (2007). Understanding Student Pathways in Context-rich Problems. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0701284](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0701284)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PSLP tracking data on task completion times (Table 2). The article reports the qualitative analysis mean completion time fell from 41 to 29 minutes and the normalized ratio ( tQualAnal/tsolution) fell from 0.78 to 0.61, a 20% earlier completion.

> "It is notable that the average completion time of the qualitative analysis task decreased from 41 minutes in problem 1, to 29 minutes in problem 5. This persists even when normalizing by the time each group submitted their problem solution:  ( tQualAnal/tsolution) = 0.78 for problem 1 and ( tQualAnal/tsolution) = 0.61 for problem 5, i.e., the qualitative analysis is completed 20% earlier in problem 5 than in problem 1."
