## Evidence

### Xaverius Pamela K. 2014

Xaverius Pamela K., Grady Mary Alice. (2014). Centering Pregnancy in Missouri: A System Level Analysis. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/285386

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

McNemar tests matching posttest expectations that patient-level indicators would improve under CP with pretest ratings of usual care, among direct-service-site respondents. The article reports the five significant contrasts and the non-significant safety contrast quoted; no effect sizes are printed.

> "significantly different proportions were found for equitable (46.5% versus 65.0%, P > 0.001), effectiveness (61.5% versus 65.0%, P > 0.001), efficiency (66.7% versus 79.7%, P > 0.001), patient-centeredness (73.2% versus 77.8, P > 0.001), and timeliness (73.3% versus 80.8%, P > 0.001) but not for safety (32.4% versus 43.2%, P = 0.063)"
