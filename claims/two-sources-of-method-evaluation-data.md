### Stone 1913

Stone, C. W. (1913). Problems in the Scientific Study of the Teaching of Arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology, IV(1). https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Methodological argument in a 1913 agenda-setting paper; no data reported. The article illustrates with subtraction taught by the Austrian or making change method versus the common or taking away method, asking why not measure and evaluate these methods, and with rationalizing operations such as carrying, borrowing and placing partial products, proposing that pupils definitely taught each way be measured and the methods evaluated.

> "There are two main sources from which data may be secured for the evaluation of different methods, viz.: measurements of current practices and measurements of specifically controlled practices or procedures."
