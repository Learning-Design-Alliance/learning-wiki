## Evidence

### Zhao Xiaojuan 2026

Zhao Xiaojuan. (2026). Psychological characteristics of metacognitive strategy use and vocabulary development paths among vocational university students in a personalized english teaching environment. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04595-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise multiple regression predicting posttest VLT with pretest scores as covariates (N = 120). Neither motivation nor planning reached significance once other variables were held constant; anxiety approached significance (β = -0.15, p = .078) but did not cross the threshold.

> "the breadth model accounted for 47% of variance in posttest VLT scores. Self-efficacy (β = 0.34, p < .001) and monitoring strategy (β = 0.31, p = .001) emerged as the only significant independent predictors"

### Zhao Xiaojuan 2026 (2)

Zhao Xiaojuan. (2026). Psychological characteristics of metacognitive strategy use and vocabulary development paths among vocational university students in a personalized english teaching environment. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04595-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Stepwise multiple regression predicting posttest VKS with pretest scores as covariates. Self-efficacy, dominant in the breadth model, fell short of significance for depth (β = 0.18, p = .062), showing divergent predictor profiles across dimensions.

> "Intrinsic motivation (β = 0.27, p = .004) and evaluation strategy (β = 0.25, p = .009) were the significant predictors, jointly explaining a substantial share of the 42% total variance"
