# Practical training alone produced a smaller, statistically significant intragroup OSATS checklist gain but did not have a significant between-group effect on surgical performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The practical training group achieved a ΔOSATS of 2.5 ± 4.3 (p = 0.028) on the task-specific checklist, and in the factorial comparison practical training did not have a significant effect (p = 0.38). [→ Pape-Koehler Carolina 2013](#pape-koehler-carolina-2013)

## Evidence

### Pape-Koehler Carolina 2013

Pape-Koehler Carolina, Immenroth Marc, Sauerland Stefan, Lefering Rolf, Lindlohr Cornelia, Toaspern Jens, Heiss Markus. (2013). Multimedia-based training on Internet platforms improves surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-012-2672-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT, 17 participants received 2 h of practical Pelvi-Trainer training (an average of two laparoscopic cholecystectomies). The intragroup gain was statistically significant, but the article separately reports that "Practical training did not have a significant effect on surgical performance (p = 0.38)" in the factorial between-group comparison; no effect size is printed.

> "The practical training group achieved 2.5 ± 4.3 (p = 0.028), whereas the combination training group achieved 4.6 ± 3.5 (p < 0.001), and the control group achieved 0.8 ± 2.9 (p = 0.294)."
