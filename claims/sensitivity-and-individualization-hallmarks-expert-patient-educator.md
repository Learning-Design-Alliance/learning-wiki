## Evidence

### Svavarsdóttir MH 2015

Svavarsdóttir MH, Sigurðardóttir ÁK, Steinsbekk A. (2015). How to become an expert educator: a qualitative study on the view of health professionals with experience in patient education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0370-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of 19 Icelandic and Norwegian nurses, physiotherapists and cardiologists with a mean of 12 years in cardiac care, analyzed with systematic text condensation. Participants "being sensitive to the patients’ interests and learning needs" and adjusting education to each patient were named the most prominent expert signs. No effect size is reported.

> "However, the most prominent signs of an expert educator were considered the ability to know when a patient is ready to receive information, being sensitive to the patients’ interests and learning needs, and being able to adjust the education to each patient’s needs and the context of the situation."
