Classroom evaluation of three Ecolab versions (VIS, WIS, NIS) with pre and post tests identifying learning gains. The article notes one must bear in mind the small number of participants.

> "The VIS version of the Ecolab produced the greatest average improvement in test scores, but was not the most successful system with learners of all abilities. The mean improvement amongst WIS users who were in the high ability group was greater than those for VIS and NIS."
