## Evidence

### Qin Z 2026

Qin Z, Shao X, Wu X, Guo J, Yang L, Ye J, Feng X, Yang D-Y, Liang Q, Taylor DCM. (2026). Impact of a tripartite framework on perioperative clinical reasoning in junior anaesthesiology residents in China: a longitudinal quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08706-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal quasi-experimental comparison of 45 experimental versus 37 control first-year residents at a tertiary academic hospital, reported in Results. The experimental group showed "comprehensive superiority across all domains (p < 0.01)" with the printed effect sizes above; Table 1 reports means of 108.9 ± 8.8 vs 99.8 ± 8.9 (MCQs), 78.7 ± 9.1 vs 70.5 ± 8.2 (planning), 80.5 ± 10.7 vs 67.7 ± 9.1 (oral).

> "However, in the medium-term assessment (approximately 16 months), the experimental group demonstrated comprehensive superiority across all domains (p < 0.01), with notably substantial effect sizes for MCQs (d = 1.02), anaesthesia planning (d = 0.94), and oral examination (d = 0.99)"
