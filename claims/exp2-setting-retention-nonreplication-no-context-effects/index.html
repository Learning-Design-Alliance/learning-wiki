Merkt 2020¶

Merkt, M., Lux, S., Hoogerheide, V., van Gog, T., & Schwan, S. (2020). A Change of Scenery: Does the Setting of an Instructional Video Affect Learning? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000414

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Experiment 2 repeated the authentic-versus-neutral setting comparison and added setting screenshots as backgrounds during the retention and application test with a comparable sample. Contrary to expectations, "the effect of setting on retention did not replicate and there was no evidence for context effects."

"Contrary to our expectations, the effect of setting on retention did not replicate and there was no evidence for context effects, despite using the same learning materials and a comparable sample as in Experiment 1."
