Evidence¶

Xu Wei 2021¶

Xu Wei, Ye Tingjie, Wang Xiaoling, Hayashi. Shogo. (2021). The effectiveness of the problem-based learning in medical cell biology education: A systematic meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000027402

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Pooling of the 2 included studies that measured student satisfaction; the pooled RR was 1.18 (0.96, 1.46). Because the lower confidence bound was below 1, the authors conclude "there was no difference between the satisfaction of students to PBL and LBL curriculum"; no standardized effect size is printed.

"The RRs were risk rate between PBL and LBL curriculum and calculated to be 1.18 (0.96, 1.46) (Fig. 4). However, the low CI was less than 1, which indicated there was no significance for the pooled RRs. Therefore, the result indicated that there was no difference between the satisfaction of students to PBL and LBL curriculum."
