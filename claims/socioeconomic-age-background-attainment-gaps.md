### Jones Rebecca L. 2024

Jones Rebecca L., Prusmetikul Suwimol, Whitehorn Sarah. (2024). Differential attainment in assessment of postgraduate surgical trainees: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05580-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Vinnicombe et al., that trainees from the most deprived deprivation quintiles (defined by area at medical school application) had significantly more non-satisfactory ARCP outcomes (24.4% vs 14.2%). Percentages are descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "Trainees from DQ1 and DQ2 received significantly more non-satisfactory ARCP outcomes (24.4%) than DQ4 and DQ5 (14.2%) [14]."

### Jones Rebecca L. 2024 (2)

Jones Rebecca L., Prusmetikul Suwimol, Whitehorn Sarah. (2024). Differential attainment in assessment of postgraduate surgical trainees: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05580-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the single included disability study found significantly lower MRCS Part A pass rates for disabled trainees but no significant Part B difference; older trainees received more non-satisfactory ARCP outcomes in three cited studies. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Trainees with disability had significantly lower pass rates in MRCS part A compared to candidates without disability. However, the difference was not significant for part B [59]."
