## Evidence

### Akmam Akmam 2025

Akmam Akmam, Serli Ahzari, Emiliannur Emiliannur, Rio Anshari, David Setiawan. (2025). Enhancing Science Literacy through Cognitive Conflict-Based Generative Learning Model: An Experimental Study in Physics Learning. Social Science and Humanities Journal. https://doi.org/10.18535/sshj.v9i07.1934

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 167 Grade XI students across three schools (83 GLBCC, 84 expository learning), analyzed with ANOVA and Tukey HSD. The "Post School A GLBCC group showed a mean difference of 20.892" versus the EL group (p < 0.001). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Notably, the Post School A GLBCC group showed a mean difference of 20.892 compared to the Post School A EL group (p < 0.001), indicating substantial improvement in science literacy abilities."
