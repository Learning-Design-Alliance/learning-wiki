## Evidence

### Sophie von Stumm 2022

Sophie von Stumm, Sophie Nicole Cave and Paul Wakeling. (2022). Persistent association between family socioeconomic status and primary school performance in Britain over 95 years. npj Science of Learning (2022) 7:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00120-3

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Meta-regression of Fisher's z-transformed correlations from 16 British birth cohorts born 1921–2011, each adjusted for cohort-specific confounders including assessment type, indicator counts, age, geographical scope, and missingness. The pooled correlation effect size was 0.28 [0.22–0.34], described in the article as "reflecting a medium effect size".

> "Across all 16 population cohorts, we estimated the average association between family SES and children’s school performance to be 0.28 [0.22 –0.34], reflecting a medium effect size (see Table S2 in the SI for complete model results)."
