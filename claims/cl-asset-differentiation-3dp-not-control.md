## Evidence

### Massey Patrick A. 2025

Massey Patrick A., Scalisi Wayne, Barton Richard Shane, Hansen Austin, Montgomery Carver, Solitro Giovanni F. (2025). Validity Assessment of a Three-Dimensional Printed Arthroscopic Shoulder Simulator: An Experimental Evaluation of Construct Validity and Educational Value. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-25-00225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial arm; five medical students and five expert surgeons on the 3D-printed simulator, with blinded video review using the ASSET Global Rating Scale. The "ASSET rating was markedly different between the medical students and the expert surgeons" on the 3DP (P = 0.017).

> "The ASSET rating was markedly different between the medical students and the expert surgeons when they were tested on the 3DP (P = 0.017, Figure 4)."

### Massey Patrick A. 2025 (2)

Validity Assessment of a Three-Dimensional Printed Arthroscopic Shoulder Simulator: An Experimental Evaluation of Construct Validity and Educational Value. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial arm; five medical students and five expert surgeons on the commercial control simulator, videos graded with the ASSET Global Rating Scale. > "No notable difference was found in ASSET rating between the medical student and expert surgeon on the control (P = 0.533)."
