`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive preference ratings from the online survey (n = 233) on presentation of practical examples per topic. The article reports "case vignettes about patients with different diagnoses (81.1%)" among the preferred example formats; percentages are descriptive with no inferential testing.

> "Participants would highly appreciate a presentation of practical examples of integration of PROs into the clinical workflow (92%), clinical actions based on PRO data (91%), successful implementations in different clinical settings (90.6%) and self-management recommendations (88%) as well as case vignettes about patients with different diagnoses (81.1%)."
