Downey 1914¶

Downey, J. E., and Anderson, J. E. (1914). Form and Position in Handwriting Interpretation. Part I. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Form+and+position+in+handwriting+interpretation%22+Downey+Anderson

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of the six reading tests (Tables I and II) with 16 reagents: equated per-100-word times ranged from 84 seconds for B 1 to 270.4 seconds for A 2. The authors interpret the relative retardation on mutilated samples as again showing B's comparative ease.

"The time and error range as well as the average time (equated) indicates that samples A and C are much more difficult to read than is sample B."
