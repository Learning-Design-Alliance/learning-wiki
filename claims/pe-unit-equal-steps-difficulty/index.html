Evidence¶

Herring 1919¶

Herring, J. P. (1919). Derivation of a scale to measure abilities in scientific thinking. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q2 · i? · design · r2

Definitional statement in the section on the probability curve: the P.E. represents equal steps of difficulty within a group, with the median as the zero reference point and 50% of cases between -1 and +1 P.E. No effect size is printed.

"The P. E. of a normal group of unselected measures is that distance below or above the median of its surface of distribution whose ordinate cuts off just 25% of the measures between itself and the median."
