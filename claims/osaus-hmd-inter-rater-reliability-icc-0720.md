## Evidence

### Gomes Sara Hora 2023

Gomes Sara Hora, Trindade Marta, Petrisor Cristina, Costa Dinis, Correia-Pinto Jorge, Costa Patrício S., Pêgo José M. (2023). Objective structured assessment ultrasound skill scale for hyomental distance competence – psychometric study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04146-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-rater reliability computed with a two-way random effects ICC (model 2,5) on the mean OSAUS score of three measurements per participant, rated by five assessors. The study reports "The ICC was 0.720" with a wide 95% confidence interval.

> "The ICC was 0.720 (95%CI 0.408 to 0.893), with a significance level inferior to 0.001 (p < 0.001)."
