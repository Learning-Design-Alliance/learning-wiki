## Evidence

### Liu Keqiao 2023

Liu Keqiao. (2023). Middle school students’ mental unwellness and academic performance in China: The effects of parental involvement. PLoS One 18(11): e0294172. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294172

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

HLM Model 2 for mathematics in the CEPS panel; the article reports "β = 1.298, p < 0.05" for mother-child communication and non-significant coefficients for the other two involvement variables. Chinese and English models show the same pattern (β = 0.657 and 1.147).

> "only children’s perception of mother-child communication was positively related to academic performance in mathematics (β = 1.298, p < 0.05), while neither parental non-academic activity involvement (β = -0.414, p > 0.05) nor father-child communication (β = -0.268, p > 0.05) were found to be associated"
