Frequency analysis of the 283 prompt labels across the 1,238-publication corpus. The article reports the printed frequencies of the four most common labels and that "47 prompt labels appeared in five or more records," showing convergence on a few focal prompt types despite widespread inconsistency.

> "A small subset of prompts appeared with notably high frequency: metacognitive prompt (89), self-explanation prompt (88), question prompt (71), and reflection prompt (64)."
