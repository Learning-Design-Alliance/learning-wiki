# Item parameter differences between chain and simultaneous equating were small (at most 12 items, 7%, flagged by RMSD), while comparisons involving concurrent calibration flagged up to 11% of items

> "With two exceptions of the tests 2005S and 2006S, a maximum of 12 items (7%) were flagged when comparing CH and SM. Between CH and CR and between SM and CR, more items were flagged in general (up to 11% of items)."
