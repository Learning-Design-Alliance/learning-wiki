## Evidence

### J. Mercier 2023

J. Mercier, N. Chabloz, G. Dozot, C. Audrin, O. Ertz, E. Bocher, D. Rappo. (2023). Impact of Geolocation Data on Augmented Reality Usability: A Comparative User Test. [doi:10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-xlviii-4-w7-2023-133-2023)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Focus measured via eye-tracking glasses (41 usable recordings of 54 participants), with gaze frames classified by a fine-tuned ViT model at 95% validity. No significant group difference in screen interaction time.

> "The GNSS group spend an average M = 73.3% (SD = 9.81) of the time looking at the tablet screen. The RTK group spend an average M = 69.2% (SD = 12.4) of the time looking at the tablet screen. The results do not show any significant difference (t(51) = 1.16, p = 0.251)."
