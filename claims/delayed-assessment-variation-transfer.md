## Evidence

### Lyn D English and Jane M Watson 2015

Lyn D English and Jane M Watson. (2015). Exploring variation in measurement as a foundation for statistical thinking in the elementary school. International Journal of STEM Education (2015) 2:3. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-015-0016-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Written assessment administered 1 to 3 months after the activity to four year 4 classes (two classes after 1 month, two streamed high-achieving classes after 3 months). 26 marks possible; "the median score is seen to be 18"; the mean was 16.7, becoming 17.5 without three very low scores of students who spoke virtually no English.

> "The distribution of scores for the 66 students who completed the questions is shown in Figure 4, where the median score is seen to be 18."

### Lyn D English and Jane M Watson 2015 (2)

Lyn D English and Jane M Watson. (2015). Exploring variation in measurement as a foundation for statistical thinking in the elementary school. International Journal of STEM Education (2015) 2:3. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-015-0016-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Authors' summary of assessment outcomes for research question 3, covering items Q10 to Q15 including a request for a definition of variation. "Many students had developed the ability to transfer their appreciation of variation to anothercontext and could clearly explain the meaning of variation."

> "the results of the assessment indicated that many students had developed the ability to transfer their appreciation of variation to anothercontext and could clearly explain the meaning of variation"
