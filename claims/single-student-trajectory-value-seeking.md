John M. Aiken, Shih-Yin Lin, Scott S. Douglas, Edwin F. Greco, Brian D. Thoms, Marcos D. Caballero and Michael F. Schatz. (2013). Student Use of a Single Lecture Video in a Flipped Introductory Mechanics Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.2620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.2620)

Detailed case analysis of a single student's viewing trajectory (Figure 1), the third view and first complete view. The student sought to pause at 528 s where initial conditions were discussed and paused 64 s at 610 s where the iterative time step was discussed; "This student spent a total of 46.8 s on the conceptual final three interaction point questions".
