Evidence¶

Jana Gonnermann-Müller 2026¶

Jana Gonnermann-Müller, Jennifer Haase, Nicolas Leins, Thomas Kosch, Sebastian Pokutta. (2026). LLM-Based Educational Simulation: Evaluating Temporal Student Persona Stability Across ADHD-Related Persona Specifications. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06307 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Experiment II education scenario comparing scripted versus unscripted conditions across 9-turn conversations. The scripted observer model yielded a non-significant turn effect (β = −0.02, SE = 0.05, t = −0.36, p = .722) with all post-hoc comparisons p = 1.000; the 97% figure is descriptive. No standardized effect size is printed.

"The contrast between unscripted (∆ =−4.0,11 .1%) and scripted (∆ = −0.1,0 .3%) high-intensity education observer ratings is a97%reduction in behavioral drift from conversation structure alone."
