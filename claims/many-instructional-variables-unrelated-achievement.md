### Doabler 2013

Doabler, C. T., Nelson-Walker, N., Kosty, D., Baker, S. K., Smolkowski, K., & Fien, H. (2013). Explicit Instructional Interactions: Observed Stability and Predictive Validity During Early Literacy and Beginning Mathematics Instruction. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563121

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Concurrent analysis across both ECRI and ELM datasets using multilevel models with students nested within classrooms. The paper reports this as a preliminary finding from the two randomized controlled trials.

> "Preliminary analysis of the ELM and ECRI data reveals that many of the studied instructional variables were unrelated to student achievement. One explanation for these results is that the low stability of the instructional variables attenuated the relationships."
