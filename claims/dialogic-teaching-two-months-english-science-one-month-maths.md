## Evidence

### Jay et al. 2017

Tim Jay, Ben Willis, Peter Thomas, Roberta Taylor, Nick Moore, Cathy Burnett, Guy Merchant, Anna Stevens. (2017). Dialogic Teaching: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581114

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Efficacy cluster trial comparing 38 intervention schools (2,492 pupils) with 38 control schools (2,466 pupils), analysed by multilevel linear regression of GL Assessment Progress Test scores. Table 1 reports effect sizes of "0.09" for maths (p = 0.19), "0.12" for science (p = 0.04), and "0.15" for English (p = 0.05).
