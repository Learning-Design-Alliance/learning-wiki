Evidence¶

Ha 2017¶

Do Thi Ha. (2017). The Impact of Different Instructions on Vietnamese EFL Students' Acquisition of Formulaic Sequences. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p18

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group Mann-Whitney comparisons of Part A vs Part B for all three groups on both post-tests; the article reports recognition outperformance as significant for each group (e.g. CT W=209, p=0.001677). The author links the gap to Bloom's revised taxonomy, with Creating harder than Remembering.

"On all the tests, the scores of form detection (Part A) are higher than the corresponding scores of active recall (Part B)."
