## Evidence

### Li 2022

Li, B., Yu, Q., & Yang, F. (2022). The effect of blended instruction on student performance: A meta-analysis of 106 empirical studies from China and abroad. Best Evidence in Chinese Education, 10(2):1395-1403. https://doi.org/10.15354/bece.22.ar018

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis grouping experiments into three period bands. The between-group test (p = 0.017 < 0.05) is significant, which the article says "reveals that teaching periods can moderate the effects of blended instruction".

> "the experimental period of one to three months yields the most significant effect (SMD = 0.845), followed by the periods of less than one month (SMD = 0.705) and more than three months (SMD = 0.530)"
