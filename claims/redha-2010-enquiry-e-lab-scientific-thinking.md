The review reports, citing Redha (2010), a quasi-experimental study of 91 students in three groups (two experimental, one control) using a scientific-thinking test, e-lab, and enquiry and experimentation worksheets; "the effectiveness of the e-lab varied according to its type in favor of the enquiry-based e-labs".
