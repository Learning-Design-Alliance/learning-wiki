## Evidence

### Lilot Marc 2019

Lilot Marc, Evain Jean-Noel, Vincent Alban, Gaillard Guillain, Chassard Dominique, Mattatia Laurent, Ripart Jacques, Denoyel Lucas, Bauer Christian, Robinson Philip, Duclos Antoine, Lehot Jean-Jacques, Rimmelé Thomas. (2019). Simulation of difficult airway management for residents: prospective comparative study. Brazilian Journal of Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjane.2019.03.004

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group comparison of self-assessment survey forms (0–10 scales) completed before simulation and 6 months later by 44 intervention residents, analyzed with Wilcoxon tests; the article reports "a significant increase in both theoretical knowledge and practical skills" for each device (e.g., p < 0.001 for most devices in Tables 2–3).

> "In the intervention group, there was a significant increase in both theoretical knowledge and practical skills between the initial and final survey forms for each airway management device individually."
