`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Across the five-year follow-up of the 4,228-student cohort, the study reports the disparity between the two groups "grow from under 0.4 standard deviations in kindergarten to 0.9 standard deviations in 4th grade", driven by differential summer loss. The article prints these gap magnitudes as descriptive values, not as an effect size for a treatment contrast, so no standardized effect size is coded.
