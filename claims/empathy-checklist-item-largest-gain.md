## Evidence

### Yang 2026

Yang, L., Liu, H., Li, S., Jia, R., Xiao, Y., Chen, G., & Lu, L. (2026). Evaluating Scaffolding-Oriented Multi-Agent Large Language Model System for Clinical Interview Training. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10939

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level checklist comparison in the randomized exam phase found the "item expressing empathy" 31 percentage points higher in MA than CT, significant after Holm correction (P = 8.30 × 10−4). The article interprets this as improved observable empathic communication behavior within the AI-SP setting, not a stable attribute change.

> "The largest absolute difference was observed for the item expressing empathy, which was 31 percentage points higher in the multi-agent group than in the control group (Figure 2f). This difference remained statistically significant after Holm correction(P = 8.30 × 10−4)."

### Yang 2026 (2)

Yang, L., Liu, H., Li, S., Jia, R., Xiao, Y., Chen, G., & Lu, L. (2026). Evaluating Scaffolding-Oriented Multi-Agent Large Language Model System for Clinical Interview Training. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10939

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Further item-level comparisons in the same exam-phase analysis showed the medication allergy history item significant after Holm correction, and duration of illness and summarizing/confirming information significant under FDR correction; other items (onset of symptoms, bowel habit changes, urinary changes, chief-complaint inquiry) showed positive descriptive differences only.

> "Themedication allergy historyitem remained statistically significant after Holm correction, whereasduration of illnessandsummarizing and confirming informationreached significance under false discovery rate(FDR)correction."
