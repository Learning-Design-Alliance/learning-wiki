Evidence¶

Hazzam 2023¶

Hazzam, J., & Wilkins, S. (2023). The influences of lecturer charismatic leadership and technology use on student online engagement, learning performance, and satisfaction. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104809

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Moderation analysis (Process model 1, 5000 bootstrap samples); interaction significant (β = -0.09, t = -3.89, p < .001, ΔR² = 0.014). Unlike charismatic leadership, the technology effect remains significant at +1 SD.

"The results suggest that for -1 standard deviation from the mean on self-efficacy the effect (β = 0.33, t = 8.48, p < .001) is higher than the effect on the mean level of self-efficacy (β = 0.24, t = 7.26, p < .001) and the effect of +1 standard deviation (β = 0.16, t = 3.84, p < .001) from the mean on student self-efficacy."
