## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Muhidin Mohamed 2025

Muhidin Mohamed, Shubhadeep Mukherjee and Bhavana Baad. (2025). Impact of UK Postgraduate Student Experiences on Academic Performance in Blended Learning: A Data Analytics Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation on the 255-student survey found stress level negatively and significantly associated with academic performance, with printed effect size (Spearman coefficient) r = -0.382 and p value 2.6705×10 −10; the article states this increase undermines academic achievement.

> "Stress level vs Academic performance -0.382 2.6705×10 −10"

### Muhidin Mohamed 2025 (2)

Muhidin Mohamed, Shubhadeep Mukherjee and Bhavana Baad. (2025). Impact of UK Postgraduate Student Experiences on Academic Performance in Blended Learning: A Data Analytics Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

The same Spearman analysis found study workload negatively and significantly associated with academic performance, with printed effect size (Spearman coefficient) r = -0.281 and p value 5.011×10 −6, consistent with the descriptive finding that over 80% of students whose performance worsened reported increased workload and stress.

> "Study workload vs Academic performance -0.281 5.011×10 −6"
