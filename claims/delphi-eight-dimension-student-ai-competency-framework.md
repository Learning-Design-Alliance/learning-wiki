A three-round Delphi study with 29 K-12 AI education professionals in Hong Kong, using a 75% agreement level, revised the initial materials. The article reports that "Eight dimensions were revised and finalized from 10 dimensions in the initial lists," with three dimensions integrated into one and each dimension holding four items.
