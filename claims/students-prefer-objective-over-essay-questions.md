Islam, K., Ahmadi, P., & Yousaf, S. (2017). Assessment Formats and Student Learning Performance: What is the Relation? George Mason University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1711.10396](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1711.10396)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Demographics survey analysis reported in the authors' previous study (Ahmadi et al., 2017), which found students "tend to lean toward objective questions over subjective essay formats". The authors attribute this to MCQ answer options, since "students are able to see the answer options with the question".

> "students tend to lean toward objective questions over subjective essay formats. This is perhaps because with MCQ format, students are able to see the answer options with the question"
