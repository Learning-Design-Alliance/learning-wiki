Kirkpatrick Level 1 satisfaction item on the anonymous Pre-Clerkship End of Year Student Evaluation Form completed by 182 students (82.7% response rate); the article reports descriptive percentages only, with no inferential test or effect size.

> "At the end of the first-year medical school curriculum, 63.2% of responding students indicated that they agreed or strongly agreed that the CRE course contributed to their ability to demonstrate initiated and self-direction in learning, while 25.8% were neutral and 10.99% said they disagreed or strongly disagreed"
