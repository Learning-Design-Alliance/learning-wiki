## Evidence

### Lazarides 2025

Lazarides, R., Frenkel, J., Petković, U., Göllner, R., & Hellwich, O. (2025). 'No words'—Machine‐learning classified nonverbal immediacy and its role in connecting teacher self‐efficacy with perceived teaching and student interest. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S15–S31. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12732

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel path analysis of TALIS video study data from 50 teachers (46 classrooms after exclusions) with student reports nested in classrooms. In Model 1 the standardized coefficient was β = .38 (p = .034) and in Model 2 β = .40 (p = .048), described as 'positively and significantly related'.

> "TSESE before the lesson (Time 1) was positively and significantly related to teachers' NVI during the lesson (Time 2). Teachers' NVI during the lesson (Time 2) was positively related to class‐level enthusiastic teaching behaviours, reported after the lesson (Time 3)."
