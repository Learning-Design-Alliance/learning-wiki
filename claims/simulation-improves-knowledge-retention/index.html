Related Claims¶

  • Simulation training in critical care during COVID-19 enhanced knowledge and confidence more than theoretical and clinical training alone — related
  • VR tools enhance acquisition and retention of clinical and procedural skills but face barriers including cybersickness, limited tactile feedback and variable long-term skill transfer — related
  • Several included studies found haptic VR training equivalent rather than superior to conventional dental training — related
  • Across the reviewed studies, simulation-based interventions consistently improved paediatric undergraduate learning outcomes, including clinical reasoning, procedural skills and confidence — a broader claim this one bears on
  • High-fidelity simulation methods (simulators, 3D models, cadaver-based practice) are associated with superior Acquisition retention, though some studies report contradictory findings — related
  • Simulation Based Education With Deliberate Practice Improves Clinical Outcomes — related
  • Active methodologies show no definitive superiority over traditional teaching in undergraduate dentistry — related
  • Long-term retention of high-fidelity simulation benefits is controversial: one RCT found no difference at 3 months while another found improvement after a median of 107 days — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • VR and conventional teaching produce equivalent medium-term learning success after one month — reports the opposite
