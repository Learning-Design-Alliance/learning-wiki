## Evidence

### Noël M-P and Rousselle L 2011

Noël M-P and Rousselle L (2011) Developmental changes in the profiles of dyscalculia: an explanation based on a double exact-and-approximate number representation model. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 5:165. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2011.00165

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports developmental change in ANS acuity, citing Halberda and Feigenson (2008) for the w values at ages 3, 6, and adulthood. The article offers two candidate explanations — natural brain maturation or stimulation by exact-number processing — without adjudicating between them.

> "change in number acuity are seen throughout the child's development (going from a w = 0.525 at age 3 to a w = 0.179 at age 6) up to adulthood ( w = 0.108, Halberda and Feigenson, 2008)"
