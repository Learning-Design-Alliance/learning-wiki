### Condelli 2010

Condelli, L., Cronen, S., Bos, J., Tseng, F., and Altuna, J. (2010). The Impact of a Reading Intervention for Low-Literate Adult ESL Learners (NCEE 2011-4003). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Classroom observation study of Sam and Pat implementation across the study sites. "About two-thirds (65 percent) of the Sam and Pat classes observed met the study’s instructional fidelity criteria"; criteria required at least 1 hour of Sam and Pat materials and 1 hour of reading development instruction per class day, in at least three reading development areas.

> "About two-thirds (65 percent) of the Sam and Pat classes observed met the study’s instructional fidelity criteria regarding the use of Sam and Pat materials and engagement in reading instruction."
