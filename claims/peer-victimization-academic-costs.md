## Evidence

### Juvonen 2019

Juvonen, J., Lessard, L.M., Rastogi, R., Schacter, H.L., & Smith, D.S. (2019). Promoting social inclusion in educational settings: Challenges and opportunities. Educational Psychologist, 54, 250-270. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2019.1655645

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review summarizing prior studies of peer victimization and school adjustment. The review reports that bullied students "are absent from school more frequently and receive lower grades" in both elementary and secondary school, and cites proposed pathways through emotional distress, somatic complaints, and reduced classroom participation. No effect sizes are printed.

> "students who are bullied by their peers are absent from school more frequently and receive lower grades in elementary (van Lier et al., 2012) and secondary (Ladd, Ettekal, & Kochenderfer-Ladd, 2017) school."
