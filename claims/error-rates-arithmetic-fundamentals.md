Phillips, F. M. (1916). Comparison of the work done in the successive minutes of a ten minute practice period in the fundamentals of arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072031

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Error-rate analysis computed from attempts and counted errors for each fundamental, grade, and minute across the test sheets. The article reports first-minute versus remaining-minute error averages per fundamental, with multiplication highest.

> "The average amount of error in the first minute in addition was .80, in the remaining minutes it was .81. In subtraction it was 1.11 and 1.26 respectively. In multiplication it was 5.32 and 5.49, and in division 2.80 and 2.34."
