## Evidence

### Kozikowski A 2026

Kozikowski A, Bruza-Augatis M, Ritsema T, Sasek C, Jiang Y, Dallas A, Goodman J. (2026). Factors associated with PAs' choice of the traditional PANRE over the PANRE-LA. JAAPA. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.JAA.0000000000000411

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate logistic regression (Figure 5) in the full cohort; the article prints "aOR 0.54; P < .001" for dermatology and "aOR 0.63; P < .001" for surgery subspecialties versus primary care, plus aOR 1.24 for sub-master's degrees and aOR 1.18 for rural/isolated residence. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Conversely, the lowest odds of selecting the traditional PANRE were observed among PAs practicing in dermatology (aOR 0.54; P < .001) and surgery subspecialties (aOR 0.63; P < .001), relative to those in primary care, and among PAs with a doctorate compared with those with a master's degree (aOR 0.69; P < .001)."
