## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The percentage of inflations with pressures above the 35 cm H2O pressure limit by inexperienced caregivers decreased from 94 (46-100)% before training to 2 (0-70)% after training (p<0.01). [→ van Vonderen Jeroen J. 2014](#van-vonderen-jeroen-j-2014)

## Evidence

### van Vonderen Jeroen J. 2014

van Vonderen Jeroen J., Witlox Ruben S., Kraaij Sascha, te Pas Arjan B. (2014). Two-Minute Training for Improving Neonatal Bag and Mask Ventilation. PLoS ONE 9(10): e109049. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0109049

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Manikin experiment with 25 inexperienced caregivers using a self-inflating bag with a 35 cm H2O pop-off valve; pressures were measured with a respiratory function monitor before and after the two-minute training. The article reports "94 (46-100)% vs. 2 (0-70)%; p<0.01"; no effect size is printed.

> "Nearly all the inflations of the inexperienced caregivers before training were delivered with pressures above the pressure limit, but this significantly decreased after training (94 (46-100)% vs. 2 (0-70)%; p<0.01)"
