### Benjamin Brown and Chandralekha Singh 2021

Benjamin Brown and Chandralekha Singh. (2021). Development and Validation of a conceptual survey instrument to evaluate introductory physics students' understanding of thermodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

A reliability/validity investigation in a calculus-based introductory course after instruction: 226 students were randomly assigned one of four versions differing only in answer-choice order. The article reports the Kruskal-Wallis test "found no difference" with "p-value ... 0.994"; equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "Performing a Kruskal-Wallis test for statistically signiﬁcant difference between any of the four sets found no difference. In particular, the p-value that differences between the four groups were due to chance alone was found to be 0.994 [36]. This study was performed with a total of 226 students who scored an average of 36.9%"
