## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across Experiments 3 and 4, micro-offline learning accounted for the totality of early learning even when practice duration was substantially reduced or unpredictable, inconsistent with latent motor slowing as the driver. [→ Marlene Bönstrup 2020](#marlene-bonstrup-2020)

## Evidence

### Marlene Bönstrup 2020

Marlene Bönstrup, Iñaki Iturrate, Martin N. Hebart, Nitzan Censor and Leonardo G. Cohen. (2020). Mechanisms of offline motor learning at a microscale of seconds in large-scale crowdsourced data. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0066-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Convergent behavioral tests across Experiments 3 (reduced practice duration) and 4 (unpredictable practice termination): micro-offline gains persisted unchanged under both manipulations, which would not be expected if they reflected dissipation of latent motor slowing.

> "Together, the results of Experiments 3 and 4 are inconsistent with a dominant contribution of latent motor slowing to micro-of ﬂine gains and thus support consolidation as driving micro- ofﬂine gains."
