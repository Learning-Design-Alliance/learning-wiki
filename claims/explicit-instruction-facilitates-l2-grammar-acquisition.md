### Rick de Graaff 1997

Rick de Graaff. (1997). The Experanto Experiment: Effects of Explicit Instruction on Second Language Acquisition. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, 19(2). https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263197002064

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A controlled experiment in which two groups of 27 university students, differing in exposure to explicit instruction, followed a computer-controlled self-study course in an artificial language; posttest results "confirm the general hypothesis that explicit instruction facilitates the acquisition of L2grammar."
