## Evidence

### Zousel 2013

Zousel, M. L., Rule, A. C., & Logan, S. R. (2013). Teaching Primary Grade Students Perfectionism through Cartoons Compared to Bibliotherapy. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(2), 199-218. www.iejee.com. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070469

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative teacher observation of both conditions across four 30-minute lessons. Control students' remarks included 'This is boring,' and discussion prompts seemed ineffective at times, whereas cartoon students smiled, laughed, and shared personal examples during elbow partner time.

> "Students taught about perfectionism through the use of cartoons were engaged in the lessons from beginning to end. There was an aura of excitement in the classrooms when the teacher said, ‘Time to pass out the cartoons we will be discussing.’"
