Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska. (2022). Effects of Nature (Greenspace) on Cognitive Functioning in School Children and Adolescents: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1217–1254. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09658-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review contrasts Wallner et al. (2018), positive D2-R effect in Vienna high school students, with Mygind et al. (2018), no effect in Danish children aged 10–12 exposed to a grassed area with trees while reading quietly for 60 min. The review concludes this domain "may not necessarily benefit from exposure to greenspace, irrespective of the duration of exposure".

> "reported a significant positive effect after a 60-min break in a green park or forest relative to a small urban park in high school students in Vienna, but no effect was found in younger affluent Danish children"
