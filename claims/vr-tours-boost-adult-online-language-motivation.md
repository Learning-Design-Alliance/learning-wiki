## Evidence

### Figueroa 2025

Figueroa, R. B., Jr., & Jung, I. (2025). Integrating VR tours in online language learning: A design-based research study. Australasian Journal of Educational Technology, 41(1). [doi:10.14742/ajet.9498](https://doi.org/10.14742/ajet.9498)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reflexive thematic analysis of nine surveys, observation notes and three interviews from a DBR study of nine adult learners in 10 VR tour lessons. Learners' statements tied interest to spatial presence, "supported by 36 instances"; no effect size was printed.

> "As expected, learners reported that spatial presence significantly influenced their interest. This was supported by 36 instances, where statements such as "I went to the place that was interesting" and "it was enjoyable because [it] was a realistic experience"."
