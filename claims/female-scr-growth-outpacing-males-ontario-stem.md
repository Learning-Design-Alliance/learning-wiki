### Eamonn Corrigan 2023

Eamonn Corrigan, Martin Williams, and Mary A. Wells. (2023). High School Enrolment Choices — Understanding the STEM Gender Gap. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42330-023-00285-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed effects linear regression of yearly change in Student Continuation Rate on eleven years of Ontario administrative enrolment data, with sex-by-year interaction and a school random effect. The article reports female students had greater ∆SCR estimates "in almost all STEM courses"; only 12U Physics showed the reverse, and its interaction was not significant (p = 0.39).

> "When comparing between sexes, female students were found to have greater estimates for ∆SCR than male students in almost all STEM courses."
