# After the PBL improvement intervention, student post-PBL formative assessment scores improved significantly, with an overall Cohen's d of -0.54

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Post-intervention formative assessment scores across PBL blocks improved significantly versus the previous year (P <.001), with a mean paired-t Cohen's d of -0.54. [→ Ullah Himayat 2025](#ullah-himayat-2025)

## Evidence

### Ullah Himayat 2025

Ullah Himayat, Huma Sarwat, Naeem Lubna, Yunus Mohammed, Sarfraz Junaid. (2025). Bridging the gaps in problem-based learning: an evidence-based intervention in bachelor of medicine and surgery (MBBS) program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07003-2

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of post-PBL formative assessment scores (20 multiple-choice questions) between the post-intervention year and previous years across 12 blocks. The mean score rose from 3.8 (0.52) to 4.1 (0.59), a "30% overall improvement as compared to the previous year".

> "Cohen’s “d” for paired t-test showed the mean effect size of -0.54, with several blocks showing a d value above − 0.80 (P <.001), which is a huge improvement."
