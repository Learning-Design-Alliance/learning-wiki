Evidence¶

Riandi Riandi 2026¶

Riandi Riandi, Ismail Ismail, Ida Kaniawati, Wahyu Sopandi, Diana Ayu Rostikawati, Defrizal Hamka & Suhendar Suhendar. (2026). Teacher involvement in developing sustainable education materials for AI integration in green energy education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34422-4

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

PLS-SEM analysis of survey data from 122 in-service teachers in a professional development program, using dichotomous questionnaire indicators. The structural path from Use of AI in Science and Green Energy to AI Integration Ability was significant, "with a moderate effect size (f 2 = 0.180)".

"Hypothesis testing revealed that the Use of AI in Science and Green Energy had a significant influence on AI Integration Ability (β = 0.391, t = 3.063, p < 0.01), with a moderate effect size (f 2 = 0.180)."
