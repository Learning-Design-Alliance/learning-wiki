## Evidence

### Symes 2025

Symes, W., Lichtenfeld, S., Wood, P., & Putwain, D. W. (2025). Profiles of control, value and achievement emotions in primary school mathematics lessons. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 888–902. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12768

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis of T2 self-reports from 883 year-5 students in 23 English primary schools identified three profiles; the entropy score of .92 indicated high classification accuracy. The quote reports the profile sizes and mean-level patterns as printed.

> "Students in the Positive profile (n = 459) had the highest levels of control, values, and enjoyment, and the lowest levels of boredom and anxiety. Conversely, students in the Negative profile (n = 108) had the lowest levels of control, values (particularly intrinsic value), and enjoyment, and the highest levels of boredom and anxiety."
