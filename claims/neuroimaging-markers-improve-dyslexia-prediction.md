## Evidence

### Chyl 2021

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The review reports, citing a prospective study, that "a study used phonology-related ERP responses from newborns to classify children into dyslexic, poor, and typical readers" eight years later, achieving 81.25% correct classification; related infant auditory ERPs predicted 44% of reading-speed variability at 14 years.

> "a study used phonology-related ERP responses from newborns to classify children into dyslexic, poor, and typical readers based on their reading skills eight years later and achieved 81.25 % correct classification"

### Chyl 2021 (2)

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that "Children having familial risk diagnosed with dyslexia at the end of the second grade already had underactived left vOT for letters and false fonts in kindergarten (age 5.5)" compared with typically developing children without familial risk.
