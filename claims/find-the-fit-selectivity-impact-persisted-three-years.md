# The selectivity impact of Find the Fit persisted through the third fall after high school graduation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Through the fall of the third year after graduation, Find the Fit students remained about 3 percentage points more likely than regular advising students to attend colleges that were at least very competitive, a statistically significant difference. [→ Linkow 2021](#linkow-2021)
