# LLM adoption varies substantially across CS subfield assignments under a shared instructional intervention, with highest use in Algorithms (89.6%) and Web Development (80.6%) and lowest in Software Engineering (15.2%)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` LLM adoption differed significantly across the seven assignments, ranging from 15.2% (Software Engineering) to 89.6% (Algorithms). [→ Nizamani 2026](#nizamani-2026)

## Evidence

### Nizamani 2026

Nizamani, S. B., Lee, Y., Donekal Chandrashekar, N., Ellis, M., & Ramakrishnan, N. (2026). Understanding Student Use of Large Language Models Across Computer Science Subfields. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01158 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2610.01158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.01158)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of post-assignment reflection surveys from 211 students who completed all seven assignments in a within-subject course design. The results section reports that "Algorithms (89.6%) and Web Development (80.6%) showing the highest usage, and Software Engineering the lowest (15.2%)"; Networking and Databases showed moderate adoption (58.3%).

> "LLM adoption varied substantially by assignment, with Algorithms (89.6%) and Web Development (80.6%) showing the highest usage, and Software Engineering the lowest (15.2%)."
