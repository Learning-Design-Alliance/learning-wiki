## Evidence

### Waseem J 2026

Waseem J, Ritter Nicola L, McMordie J, Su P, Hu M, Chen C, Xu S. (2026). Generative AI–Assisted Progressive-Disclosure Case-Based Learning for Clinical Reasoning in Occupational Medicine: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/103584

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report questionnaire on a 0-10 scale across 5 domains. All comparisons remained significant after Holm-Bonferroni adjustment (adjusted P<.001).

> "the intervention group reported significantly higher scores for perceived improvement in clinical reasoning (mean 8.55, SD 0.50 vs mean 5.96, SD 0.79; P<.001) and course engagement (mean 8.46, SD 0.50 vs mean 6.11, SD 0.81; P<.001)"
