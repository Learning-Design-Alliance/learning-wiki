## Evidence

### Chytiris S 2024

Chytiris S, Teliti M, Croce L, Coperchini F, Grillini B, Cerutti M, Fonte R, Magri F, Rotondi M. (2024). Proficiency in performing radiofrequency ablation procedure for non-functioning benign thyroid nodules: a qualitative rather than quantitative matter. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2024.1399912

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CUSUM analysis of the 35 procedures on large nodules (>25 ml) against the VRR 50% target: "There is no positive or negative shift in the VRR values", so proficiency was not detected. The discussion adds that even an operator with ∼180 procedures may fail to reach a 6-month VRR >50% for large nodules.

> "Figures 4A, B show the CUSUM analysis of the LC of G3. There is no positive or negative shift in the VRR values."
