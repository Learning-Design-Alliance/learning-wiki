Kuhfeld 2022¶

Observational comparison of spring 2022 MAP Growth achievement against a similarly sized pre-pandemic comparison sample (2015-16 to 2018-19 testers). The brief reports achievement "remains lower than in a typical year," with math declines of 5 to 10 percentile points exceeding reading declines of 2 to 4 percentile points.
