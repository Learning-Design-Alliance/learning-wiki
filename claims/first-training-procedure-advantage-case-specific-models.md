`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM analysis of training performance in the RCT found the case-specific group scored higher on the first model, while "In the second procedure, the groups had reached almost identical performance" (CM 15.8, GM 15.7; P = 0.86).

> "In their first training performance on 3D-printed TB models, the mean final product performance score was higher for the CM group with 13.1 points (95% CI [11.7–14.6]) compared with the GM group with 10.9 points (95% CI [9.5–12.4]) (LMM, P = 0.004)."
