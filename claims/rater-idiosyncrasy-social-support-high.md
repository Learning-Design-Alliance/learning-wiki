## Evidence

### Ferguson E 2014

Ferguson E, Buttery A, Miles G, Tatalia C, Clarke DD, Lonsdale AJ, Baxendale B, Lawrence C. (2014). The Temporal Rating of Emergency Non-Technical skills (TRENT) index for self and others: psychometric properties and emotional responses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0240-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Main-study reliability decomposition using the Viswesvaran et al. method (difference between inter-rater reliability and stability coefficient). The article notes that in general occupational settings idiosyncrasy accounts for 20-30% of rating variance and that TRENT effects were "much lower than this for all categories except social support".

> "The rater idiosyncrasy variance (%) for each dimension was: (1) 18% for 'Focuses on colleagues rather than self', (2) 0% for 'Attends and reacts to the environment', (3) 3% for 'Avoids taking the lead' and (4) 40% for 'Offers social support'."
