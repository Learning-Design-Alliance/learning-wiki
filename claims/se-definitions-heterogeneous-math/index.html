Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:66. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10046-y

q3 · i? · review · r3

Review coding of whether papers defined SE, distinguishing "explicit" definitions (a clear statement by the researchers) from "implicit" ones. The review concludes this indicates "a lack of explicit definition of SE."

"Of the 48 papers reviewed, 48% (n = 23) provided an explicit definition of SE."

Elissavet Papageorgiou · Jacqueline Wong · Qinyi Liu · Mohammad Khalil · Annoesjka J. Cabo 2025 (2)¶

Elissavet Papageorgiou · Jacqueline Wong · Qinyi Liu · Mohammad Khalil · Annoesjka J. Cabo. (2025). A Systematic Review on Student Engagement in Undergraduate Mathematics: Conceptualization, Measurement, and Learning Outcomes. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:66. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10046-y

q3 · i? · review · r3

Content analysis of the 23 definitions (Table 2), coding keywords denoting engagement-related aspects. Thirteen papers used multidimensionality, drawing on Fredricks et al.'s (2004) framework; the review notes studies using multiple elements offered more concise definitions.

"In addition to multidimensionality, our analysis identified two recurring elements in SE definitions: psychological investment, emphasizing non-observable affective, conative, and cognitive aspects (n = 16), and observable behavior (n = 20)."
