## Evidence

### Wijaya Tommy Tanu 2022

Wijaya Tommy Tanu, Rahmadi Imam Fitri, Chotimah Siti, Jailani Jailani, Wutsqa Dhoriva Urwatul. (2022). A Case Study of Factors That Affect Secondary School Mathematics Achievement: Teacher-Parent Support, Stress Levels, and Students’ Well-Being. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192316247

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural model (PLS-SEM bootstrapping, 5000 resamples) of the 531-student survey; the hypothesis-testing results show teachers' academic support "significantly increased well-being" and achievement and interest, with a significant decrease in stress.

> "Teachers’ academic support in this study significantly increased well-being (β = 0.225, t = 4.519, p = 0.000), mathematics achievement (β = 0.132, t = 3.151, p = 0.002), and interest in learning (β = 0.180, t = 2.633, p = 0.008) with decrease in stress (β = −0.129, t = 2.066, p = 0.039)."
