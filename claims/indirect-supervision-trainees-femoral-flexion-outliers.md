Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery 145(1):362. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-025-05973-y

Blinded two-assessor radiographic analysis of consultant- and trainee-performed Oxford UKRs, with multivariable logistic regression adjusting for year of surgery, found a greater proportion of flexion/extension outliers 'in the subgroup of trainees who received indirect consultant supervision'.

> "However, a greater proportion of outliers was found for the flexion/extension angle of the femoral component in the subgroup of trainees who received indirect consultant supervision."
