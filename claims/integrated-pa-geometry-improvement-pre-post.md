## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hraste Mladen 2018

Hraste Mladen, De Giorgio Andrea, Jelaska Petra Mandić, Padulo Johnny, Granić Ivan. (2018). When mathematics meets physical activity in the school-aged child: The effect of an integrated motor and cognitive approach to learning geometry. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0196024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-design 2×2 ANOVA on geometry test scores of 36 fourth-graders (experimental n1 = 19, control n2 = 17) with between-subjects factor Group and within-subjects factor Treatment. Treatment was significant: "F1,36 = 7.760; P = 0.008; ŋp2 = 0.177". The article prints no standardised effect size (d, g, r) for this finding.

> "the results of factorial mixed design between/within the 2×2 ANOVA show a statistically significant impact for the factor Group (F1,36 = 5.051; P = 0.031; ŋp2 = 0.123), and the factor Treatment (F1,36 = 7.760; P = 0.008; ŋp2 = 0.177)"

### Hraste Mladen 2018 (2)

Hraste Mladen, De Giorgio Andrea, Jelaska Petra Mandić, Padulo Johnny, Granić Ivan. (2018). When mathematics meets physical activity in the school-aged child: The effect of an integrated motor and cognitive approach to learning geometry. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0196024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fisher's LSD post hoc analysis of interaction effects in the same 2×2 ANOVA found no significant pre-post change in the control group; the article prints no effect size for this null finding.

> "there is no statistically significant difference (P<0.05) within the control group between the initial and final measurements, which leads to rejection of the third hypothesis"
