Evidence¶

Tan 2026¶

Tan, A.S.-C., & Ali, F. (2026). Uncovering subgroup heterogeneity in dyslexia intervention outcomes using explainable machine learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00429-3

q2 · i? · associational · r1

K-Means clustering of LIME coefficients from a Gaussian Naive Bayes model (F1 = 0.72) applied to 132 dyslexia intervention participants. Lowest DBI solutions were chosen, meaning "the clusters were most distinct overall", supported by visual assessment. Subgroup sizes were 48/17 (responders) and 27/18/15/7 (non-responders).

"Two subgroups were identified under the responder group (DBI = 0.81), and four subgroups were identified under the non-responder group (DBI = 0.48)."
