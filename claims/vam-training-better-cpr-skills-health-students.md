## Evidence

### García-Suárez 2019

García-Suárez, M., Méndez-Martínez, C., Martínez-Isasi, S., Gómez-Salgado, J., & Fernández-García, D. (2019). Basic Life Support Training Methods for Health Science Students: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16050768

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of RCTs comparing VAM-based training with other methods. The review reports that groups "acquired knowledge through a VAM obtained better results with statistically significant differences relative to the rest of the groups".

> "As for the acquisition of technical CPR skills, the groups that acquired knowledge through a VAM obtained better results with statistically significant differences relative to the rest of the groups [9,12]."

### García-Suárez 2019 (2)

García-Suárez, M., Méndez-Martínez, C., Martínez-Isasi, S., Gómez-Salgado, J., & Fernández-García, D. (2019). Basic Life Support Training Methods for Health Science Students: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16050768

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review reports a counter-result from one included trial: the "instructor-led group obtained better results than the self-directed groups that used a VAM" immediately after intervention; the discussion adds that ventilations were not improved at first in VAM studies per Isbye et al.

> "Finally, in the study by Isbye et al. [22], the instructor-led group obtained better results than the self-directed groups that used a VAM."
