Evidence¶

Wu SS 2012¶

Wu SS, Barth M, Amin H, Malcarne V and Menon V (2012) Math anxiety in second and third graders and its relation to mathematics achievement. Front. Psychology 3:162. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00162

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Correlational analyses in the 162-child sample against WIAT-II reading measures. The article also reports non-significant results for Reading Comprehension (β = −0.08, p = 0.23) and Word Reading (β = −0.11, p = 0.22), and states neither SEMA subscale correlated with the reading measures.

"After controlling for trait anxiety and FSIQ, math anxiety was not significantly correlated with the Reading Composite score [ β =− 0.09, t (126) =− 1.45, p = 0.16, R2 = 0.14]."
