### Nagin Perry M.D. 2019

Nagin Perry M.D., Salandy Anthony Ph.D., MSc., Diaz Angela M.D., Ph.D. (2019). Unpacking the Influence of Abuse and Depression on Grades among Urban Ethnic Minority Adolescents and Young Adults. Annals of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.5334/aogh.2478

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on self-reported past-year average grades in a cross-sectional sample of 476 urban African American and Latino youth ages 14–24 recruited at a primary care clinic, with depression measured by the BDI-FS. The article reports "a statistically significant main effect for depression" with partial η2 = .03; no standardized effect size beyond η² is printed.

> "There was a statistically significant main effect for depression, F(1, 472) = 15.97, p < .01, partial η2 = .03."
