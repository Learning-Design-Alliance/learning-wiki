## Evidence

### Liu Shengjun 2025

Liu Shengjun, Zhao Zhangyi, Chen Xiangyu, Chi Yi, Yuan Siyi, Cai Fuhong, Song Zhangwei, Ma Yue, He Huaiwu, Su Longxiang, Long Yun. (2025). Evaluation of health care providers’ ability to identify patient-ventilator triggering asynchrony in intensive care unit: a translational observational study in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06638-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the 109 qualified professionals by number of published articles in clinical critical respiratory medicine. The article reports the accuracy contrasts and p values quoted here; the ineffective-trigger and double-trigger contrasts were not significant (p = 0.142 and 0.075).

> "Qualified professionals who had published more than 2 articles related to the field of clinical critical respiratory medicine performed better on auto-triggers [41.7% vs. 15.9% (p = 0.001)] and reverse triggers [38.9% vs. 19.2% (p = 0.018)]"

### Liu Shengjun 2025 (2)

Liu Shengjun, Zhao Zhangyi, Chen Xiangyu, Chi Yi, Yuan Siyi, Cai Fuhong, Song Zhangwei, Ma Yue, He Huaiwu, Su Longxiang, Long Yun. (2025). Evaluation of health care providers’ ability to identify patient-ventilator triggering asynchrony in intensive care unit: a translational observational study in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06638-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary multiple logistic regression on identifying ≥4 of 8 asynchronies. The article reports OR 14.70 (95% CI 2.55–84.64, p = 0.003) for having ≥2 published articles in the field.

> "Professionals who had published no less than 2 articles related to the field of clinical critical respiratory medicine had nearly 15-fold greater odds of identifying ≥ 4 patient-ventilator asynchrony"
