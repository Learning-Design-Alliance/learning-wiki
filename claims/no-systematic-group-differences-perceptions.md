### Balzer Katrin 2016

Balzer Katrin, Schröder Rhian, Junghans Anne, Stahl Ute, Träder Jens-Martin, Köpke Sascha. (2016). Improving competencies in evidence-based dementia care: Results from a pilot study on a novel inter-professional training course (the KOMPIDEM project). GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001034](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001034)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of survey data from 10 medical and 8 nursing students in the revised course, analyzed for the total sample and stratified by sub-sample. The article reports slight but inconsistent differences, with unlimited positive judgements of inter-professional learning slightly more frequent among nursing students.

> "The descriptive results of this pilot study does not provide any indication of systematic differences between the medical and the nursing students in the perception of the newly developed inter-professional training course, apart from slightly divergent judgements on some aspects."
