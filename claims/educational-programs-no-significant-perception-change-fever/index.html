Evidence¶

Vicens-Blanes 2021¶

Vicens-Blanes, F., Miró-Bonet, R., & Molina-Mula, J. (2021). Analysis of Nurses' and Physicians' Attitudes, Knowledge, and Perceptions toward Fever in Children: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182312444

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Meta-analysis of five educational-program studies for nurses. The review reports the perceptions variable as not statistically significant; perceptions had the least heterogeneity (I2: 60.75%). No effect size is printed, so the null is not an equivalence finding.

"Considering statistical significance, the knowledge and attitude variables show statistically significant results (p < 0.001) but the perceptions variable does not present statically significant result (p > 0.005)."
