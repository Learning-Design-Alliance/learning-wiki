### Aparicio M. 2026

Aparicio M., López-Herce J., Montalvo N., Solana M. J., Vázquez P., Mencía S., López-Herce Jesus. (2026). Simulation based learning as a strategy for patient education training in medical students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-57950-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Inter-rater reliability analysis comparing scores given by two primary-care and two PICU tutors to all students across the four assessment blocks. All correlations were significant at p < 0.001; the lowest was r = 0.415 for the single-item emotional connection domain.

> "A strong positive correlation and agreement (p < 0.001) were found between the PCP and PICU tutor scores for each of the four performance blocks, with Spearman correlation coefficients of + 0.68 for the I APN and + 0.531 for the II. PCI, + 0.869 for III. APL and + 0.415 for IV.ECP."
