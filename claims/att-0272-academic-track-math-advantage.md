## Evidence

### Herrmann 2025

Herrmann, S., & Bach, K. M. (2025). Ability grouping in German secondary schools: The effect of non‐academic track schools on the development of Math competencies. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 578–602. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12741

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Propensity score weighting analysis (IPTW with logistic propensity scores, multiple imputation with chained equations) of the NEPS SC2 panel comparing academic‐track with non‐academic‐track attendees on seventh‐grade math WLEs. The analysis "revealed a significant average treatment effect on the treated (ATT) of 0.272", which the article "categorized as moderate".

> "The propensity score analysis (see Table 2) revealed a significant average treatment effect on the treated (ATT) of 0.272. Given the distribution of the WLE (see also Table 1 and Figure A1), this effect can be categorized as moderate."
