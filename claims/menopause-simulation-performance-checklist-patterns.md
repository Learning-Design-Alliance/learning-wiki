### Collazo Madison MD 2026

Collazo Madison MD, Meade Aaliyah, Marko Emily MD, Slater Amanda, Hu Inchi PhD, Davis Carolyn MD. (2026). The Effectiveness of a Simulation-Based Menopause Education Program. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11625

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Faculty experts completed a standardized performance checklist for each learner during the standardized patient encounters; scores were compared by standardized patient case. The authors attribute the bone density case's lower scores to lower relevance of certain checklist items rather than to learner deficiency.

> "In general, residents participating in the case related to abnormal bone density had lower performance scores. This is attributed to the lower relevance in discussing certain checklist items in a case related to bone density (vaginal bleeding pattern, counseling on bioidentical hormones, etc.)."
