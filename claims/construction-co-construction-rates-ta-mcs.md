## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When one TA answered correctly individually, the group answered correctly 93% of the time (construction). [→ Ghimire 2025](#ghimire-2025)
`q2 i?` When neither TA answered correctly individually, the group still answered correctly 72% of the time (co-construction). [→ Ghimire 2025 (2)](#ghimire-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Ghimire 2025

Ghimire, A. and Singh, C. (2025). Using Unguided Peer Collaboration to Facilitate Early Educators' Pedagogical Development: An Example from Physics TA Training. [doi:10.3390/educsci15081038](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15081038)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of the same 19 TA groups' individual and group responses on the MCS, with construction rates computed per item from binary individual/group scores. The article reports construction "in 93% of eligible cases"; no effect size is printed for this rate.

> "We found that construction occurred in 93% of eligible cases across all MCS questions, indicating that when one TA knew the correct answer, the group response was correct."

### Ghimire 2025 (2)

Ghimire, A. and Singh, C. (2025). Using Unguided Peer Collaboration to Facilitate Early Educators' Pedagogical Development: An Example from Physics TA Training. [doi:10.3390/educsci15081038](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15081038)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Co-construction rates were computed for groups where both members answered incorrectly individually (binary score 001). The article reports co-construction "in 72% of eligible cases"; no effect size is printed for this rate.

> "We found that co-construction occurred in 72% of eligible cases across all questions, meaning that when none of the TAs knew the correct answer, the group response was correct."
