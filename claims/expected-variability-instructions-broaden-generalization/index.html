Evidence¶

Hadar Ram 2024¶

Hadar Ram, Guy Grinfeld & Nira Liberman. (2024). Anticipated variability increases generalization of predictive learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00269-z

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Preregistered online experiment (final N = 96, random assignment) using a predictive learning task with bacteria-colony rings; 4 × 2 mixed ANOVA showed "broader generalization in the high-expected-variability condition (M = 7.03, SD = 1.90)"; Table 1 reports expected variability F(1, 94) = 5.99, p = 0.016, η2p = 0.060 (first block) and η2p = 0.044 (all blocks).

"As predicted, we found broader generalization in the high-expected-variability condition (M = 7.03, SD = 1.90) than in the low-expected-variability condition (M = 5.91,SD = 2.53; Fig.1d, Table1)."
