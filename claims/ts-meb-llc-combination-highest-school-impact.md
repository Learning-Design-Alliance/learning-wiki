## Evidence

### Claussen AH 2018

Claussen AH, Bitsko RH, Holbrook JR, Bloomfield J, Giordano K. (2018). Impact of Tourette Syndrome on School Measures in a Nationally Representative Sample. Journal of Developmental & Behavioral Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1097/DBP.0000000000000550

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subset analysis comparing children with TS+MEB+LLC to children with TS+MEB without LLC and children without TS/LLC/MEB. IEP prevalence was 87.4% (TS+MEB+LLC) versus 2.7% (no conditions); the authors note almost 90% of children with TS and both disorder categories were receiving IEP services.

> "Children who had combined TS, MEB, and LLC had the highest rates of IEP and school problems and were also significantly more likely to not complete homework."
