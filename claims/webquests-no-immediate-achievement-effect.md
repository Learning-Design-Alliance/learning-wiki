### Yenmez 2017

Yenmez, A. A., Özpinar, İ., & Gökçe, S. (2017). Use of WebQuests in Mathematics Instruction: Academic Achievement, Teacher and Student Opinions. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050913

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test on posttest scores of the 39-student experimental and 39-student control groups after the four-week treatment. The experimental mean (70.9) was descriptively higher than the control mean (67.7), but "no statistically significant mean difference" was found (t(76)=0.884, p=.379).
