## Evidence

### Charles Baily 2014

Charles Baily, Laurens Bollen, Andrew Pattie, Paul van Kampen and Mieke De Cock. (2014). Student thinking about the divergence and curl in mathematics and physics contexts. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.008](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.008)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis splitting post-instruction vector calculus responses into pure math majors (N=79) and students concurrently taking a second-year physics course (N=36); differences were "not statistically significant".

> "There were apparent differences between these two subgroups on the same statements as for the pre-E&M students and post-Math students in aggregate, but in this case the differences were not statistically significant (𝑝=0.42 for the divergence statements, and also for the curl statements)."
