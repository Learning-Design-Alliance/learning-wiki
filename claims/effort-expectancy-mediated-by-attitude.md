## Evidence

### Vo 2020

Vo, H. N. (2020). Understanding higher education learners' acceptance and use of mobile devices for language learning: A Rasch-based path modeling approach. Computers and Education, 146, Article 103761.

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the path analysis of 293 learners, effort expectancy showed no direct effect on behavioral intention; its influence operated fully through attitude, consistent with some prior studies but diverging from others.

> "Effort expectancy was found to have no direct effect on behavior intention. Rather, its effect on behavior intention was fully mediated by attitude."
