## Evidence

### Kilp-Kabel 2025

Triinu Kilp-Kabel, Kaja Mädamürk. (2025). Expectancies, values, and task persistence can alleviate the negative effects of math anxiety on math performance. European Journal of Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10212-024-00928-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analyses (Table 4) found non-significant indirect paths for attainment value (grade five p = 0.22, grade eight p = 0.17) and utility value (grade five p = 0.19, grade eight p = 0.27); no statistically significant difference was detected, equivalence was not tested.

> "As expected, since there were either no correlations or the smallest correlations of only below 0.18 for attainment value and utility value with math anxiety and math achievement in both grades, neither attainment value nor utility value mediated the relationship between math anxiety and math achievement."
