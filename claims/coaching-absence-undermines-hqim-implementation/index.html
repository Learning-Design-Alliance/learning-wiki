Spotlight interview finding: Birch's coaching roles existed but were limited by teacher refusal and weak instructional leadership; the district director and superintendent were working to shift these dynamics.

"Four years into IM adoption, the Birch interviewee described middle school teachers refusing instructional coaching and a building principal who struggled to see herself as an instructional leader."
