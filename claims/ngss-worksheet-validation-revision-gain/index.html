Evidence¶

Rachmawati 2019¶

Rachmawati, E., Prodjosantoso, A. K., & Wilujeng, I. (2019). Next Generation Science Standard in Science Learning to Improve Student's Practice Skill. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 299-310. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12120a

q2 · i? · design · r3

Validator assessment of the NGSS-oriented worksheet by expert lecturers and science teachers in two stages. Stage 1 scored 2.6, below the >3.0 feasibility threshold; the article reports the stage-2 result "obtained a score of 3.36 which was very good". Descriptive scores only.

"Based on Table 4, the validation score of stage 1 of the developed worksheet obtained a score of 2.6, which is good. This means that the developed worksheet is not yet feasible for use."
