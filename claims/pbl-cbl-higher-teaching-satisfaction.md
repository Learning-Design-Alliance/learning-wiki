## Evidence

### Xiong Xianghua 2025

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 7 studies (468 students) comparing satisfaction with teaching methods. Pooled risk ratio favored PBL-CBL: "RR = 1.34, 95% CI: 1.23–1.45, P < 0.0001" with no heterogeneity (I2 = 0%); no standardized effect size was printed.

> "The PBL-CBL group showed higher satisfaction with the teaching methods than the LBL group (RR = 1.34, 95% CI: 1.23–1.45, P < 0.0001, I2 = 0%)"
