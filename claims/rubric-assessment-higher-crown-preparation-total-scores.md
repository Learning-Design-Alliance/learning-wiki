## Evidence

### Chen Marie Fung Ying 2026

Chen Marie Fung Ying, Mundathaje Mahesh, Natarajan Srikant, Bajantri Prashant, Mukherjee Sandipan. (2026). Effectiveness of a structured rubric-based assessment in preclinical crown preparation training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08667-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 90 fourth-year dental students randomized 1:1 to rubric-based or conventional assessment. The abstract's results report the total-score contrast: "30.53 ± 4.99 in the test group versus 10.56 ± 4.34 in the control group" with Cohen's d = 4.21.

> "Mean total score was 30.53 ± 4.99 in the test group versus 10.56 ± 4.34 in the control group (p < 0.001; Cohen’s d = 4.21)."
