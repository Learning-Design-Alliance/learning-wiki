## Evidence

### Somm Irene 2024

Somm Irene, Hajart Marco, Fehr Folkert, Weiß-Becker Christoph. (2024). Perceptions of supervision and feedback in PaedCompenda, the competency-based, post-graduate curriculum in pediatrics (www.paedcompenda.de). GMS Journal for Medical Education, 41(5). https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001710.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative accompanying research on the three-year implementation (2019–2023) at 19 pediatric practices in two model regions, analyzed with reconstructive grounded theory. The authors report "a distinctly more positive perception of the benefits of supervision and feedback" among trainees at PaedCompenda practices and distinguish unreservedly positive from less positive perceptions.

> "A distinctly more positive perception of the benefits of supervision and feedback as a teaching and learning method was seen in the physicians who received specialist training at the practices following the PaedCompenda curriculum."
