Krausen 2020¶

Krausen, K., Caparas, R., Ripma, T., & Willis, J. (2020). Strategic resource allocation for the 21st century: How state leaders can address the silent recession. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/strategic-resource-allocation/

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's special education section states that because LEAs are obligated to meet FAPE and LRE requirements irrespective of cost, educating a student with disabilities is "approximately twice the cost, on average, of educating a student without a disability," citing Chambers, Shkolnik, & Perez (2003) and other sources.

"The education of a student with disabilities can therefore be very expensive — approximately twice the cost, on average, of educating a student without a disability"
