## Evidence

### McCarthy Nora 2023

McCarthy Nora, Neville Karen, Pope Andrew, Barry Lee, Livingstone Vicki. (2023). Effectiveness of a proficiency-based progression e-learning approach to training in communication in the context of clinically deteriorating patients: a multi-arm randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open 13(8):e072488. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072488

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Pairwise Fisher's exact tests in the same RCT comparing the self-directed arm, which practised the same four scenarios without predefined metrics or benchmarks, against the standard national HSE e-learning arm. No significant difference in proficiency; equivalence was not tested.

> "Post hoc pairwise comparisons revealed statistically significant differences between the (PBP) group and both the (S) group (p<0.001) and the (HSE) group (p<0.001). A statistically significant difference was not found between the (S) group and the (HSE) group (p=0.479)."
