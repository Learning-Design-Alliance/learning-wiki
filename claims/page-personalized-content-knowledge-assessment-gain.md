## Evidence

### Joy Jia Yin Lim 2025

Joy Jia Yin Lim, Daniel Zhang-Li, Jifan Yu, Xin Cong, Ye He, Zhiyuan Liu, Huiqin Liu, Lei Hou, Juanzi Li, and Bin Xu. (2025). Learning in Context: Personalizing Educational Content with Large Language Models to Enhance Student Learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.15068](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.15068)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In-situ user study: 40 university students randomly assigned to Personalized (n=20) or Standardized (n=20) conditions across two introductory courses, completing a 15-minute instructor-designed knowledge test with multiple-choice and short-answer questions. The article reports participants in the Personalized condition "scored higher on the knowledge assessment"; no effect size is printed.

> "Participants inPersonalizedcondition scored higher on the knowledge assessment compared toStandardizedcondition (Figure 5), confirming a direct benefit to knowledge acquisition."
