Dupéré, V., Leventhal, T., Dion, E., Crosnoe, R., Archambault, I., & Janosz, M. (2014). Stressors and Turning Points in High School and Dropout: A Stress Process, Life Course Framework. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Stressors+and+Turning+Points+in+High+School+and+Dropout

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Lessard et al. and Bridgeland et al., that a majority of surveyed dropouts "had passing grades" and left within two years of graduation, and that interview narratives document events such as hospitalization, conflicts, pregnancies, and bullying preceding dropout.

> "Such pivotal events might be present in almost 50% of cases (Lessard et al., 2008)."
