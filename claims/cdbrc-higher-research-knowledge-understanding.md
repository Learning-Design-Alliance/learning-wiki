## Evidence

### Gan Xuejing 2024

Gan Xuejing, Ou Qianmin, Gong Zhuohong, Cai Gengbin, Liu Xinyu, Yang Ruihan, Lin Yuhang, Li Lin, Li Xiaolan, Hong Yun, Chen Zetao. (2024). A “flexible” compulsory dental basic research course for undergraduate students: a three-year retrospective study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04874-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Likert-scale questionnaire comparing ten knowledge topics between Group A and Group B. Group A scores "ranged from 3.300 to 3.667" versus 2.834 to 3.253 in Group B, with printed P values from < 0.0001 to 0.012 for significant items. No effect sizes are printed.

> "the average scores for self-assessment of the level of understanding of basic dental research in Group A ranged from 3.300 to 3.667, which was generally higher than the scores in Group B (2.834 to 3.253; Table 3)."

### Gan Xuejing 2024 (2)

Gan Xuejing, Ou Qianmin, Gong Zhuohong, Cai Gengbin, Liu Xinyu, Yang Ruihan, Lin Yuhang, Li Lin, Li Xiaolan, Hong Yun, Chen Zetao. (2024). A “flexible” compulsory dental basic research course for undergraduate students: a three-year retrospective study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-024-04874-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null finding within the same questionnaire comparison: the topic management and analysis of research results showed no significant difference (printed P = 0.2849 in Table 3), while other topics favored Group A.

> "Although there was no significant difference in the “management and analysis of research results” between the two groups, the understanding level of other options in Group A was higher than that in Group B."
