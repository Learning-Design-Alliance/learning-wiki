## Evidence

### Luthra 2025

Luthra, S., Luor, A., Tierney, A. T., Dick, F., & Holt, L. L. (2025). Statistical learning dynamically shapes auditory perception. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00328-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In Exp 6b, after a mid-study shift to equiprobability, decision RTs for 1000 Hz remained as fast as when it was 80% probable (d = 0.025), and overall frequency modulation persisted (p = 8.306e-5, η² = 0.068). In Exp 5b, 1000 Hz detection declined after the shift (d = 0.496) but formerly low-probability tones showed no detection change despite a more than 3-fold probability increase (p = 1).

> "Even though 1000 Hz is now 20% probable, RTs are not signiﬁcantly different than in theﬁrst experiment half when it was 80% probable (linear contrast, p = 0.720, Cohen's d = 0.025)"
