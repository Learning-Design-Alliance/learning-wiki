Nader Asadi Aidinlou & Alireza Moradinejad 2016¶

Nader Asadi Aidinlou & Alireza Moradinejad. (2016). Short-term and Long-term Retention of Vocabulary through Authentic Subtitled Videos. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.5p.14

Independent t test on delayed post-test scores of the same 18-per-group sample; experimental mean 13.11 vs control mean 11.83, mean difference -1.278. The article reports "the difference is statistically significant, t (34) = -2.148".

"It shows that the difference is statistically significant, t (34) = -2.148, at p < .05, 2-tailed. However, this time the experimental group that had a slightly better performance."
