# Aesthetic pictures selected by research-based criteria are rated significantly better than classroom experiment pictures in physics tasks on the same content, with large effect sizes for three of four topics

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large
> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Junior high school students rated aesthetic pictures (AP) significantly higher than classroom experiment pictures (CEP) on aesthetic and affective perception for light propagation/shadows, reflection and refraction, with Cohen's d between 0.85 and 1.56. [→ Zähringer 2024](#zahringer-2024)
`q2 i?` A robust two-way mixed ANOVA showed a significant main effect of picture category (AP vs CEP) on both aesthetic and affective perception ratings. [→ Zähringer 2024 (2)](#zahringer-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Zähringer 2024

Zähringer, T., Girwidz, R., & Müller, A. (2024). Conceptualization and Quantitative study of Aesthetic and Affective Perception of Pictures in Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Per-topic Welch Two Sample t-tests in the crossover classroom study (six Bavarian lower secondary classes) compared AP and CEP ratings, finding large effects, e.g. "d = 1.05 – 1.56" for aesthetic perception, with spectrum the exception.

> "Large values were found for both aesthetic (d = 1.05 – 1.56) and affective picture perception (d = 0.85 – 1.48) for the topics of light propagation /shadows, reflection and refraction."

### Zähringer 2024 (2)

Zähringer, T., Girwidz, R., & Müller, A. (2024). Conceptualization and Quantitative study of Aesthetic and Affective Perception of Pictures in Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04779)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Robust two-way mixed ANOVA over the same crossover study; the printed main effect of picture category was "F(1, 108) = 65.55" for IaePP and "F(1, 108) = 62.71" for IafPP, both p < 0.05; no effect size printed.

> "The main effects was the picture category with IaePP: F(1, 108) = 65.55, p < 0.05; and IafPP: F(1, 108) = 62.71, p < 0.05."
