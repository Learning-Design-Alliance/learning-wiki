## Evidence

### Natto Zuhair S 2026

Natto Zuhair S. (2026). Blended peer-led research curriculum with AI integration improves postgraduate students’ academic performance and satisfaction: a quasi-experimental mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08576-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test of CASP-rubric critique scores, double-rated (Cohen's kappa above 0.70), in the quasi-experimental section comparison. Descriptive means favored the intervention but the test was non-significant; no effect size was printed.

> "Critique performance did not differ significantly between the intervention group (M = 9.23, SD = 1.12) and the control group (M = 8.77, SD = 0.96), t(48.86) = 1.59, p = 0.12, 95% CI [− 0.12, 1.04]"
