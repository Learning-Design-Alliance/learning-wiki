# Students' perceived understanding of microscopic morphology rose from before to after annotation activities in one uncontrolled pre-post study, in both junior and senior cohorts

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Median self-rated understanding (1–10 scale) improved significantly after annotation for junior students (4 to 6) and senior students (3.5 to 7). [→ Sahota Michael 2016](#sahota-michael-2016)

## Evidence

### Sahota Michael 2016

Sahota Michael, Leung Betty, Dowdell Stephanie, Velan Gary M. (2016). Learning pathology using collaborative vs. individual annotation of whole slide images: a mixed methods trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0831-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Online questionnaire administered before the timed quiz in both cohorts; Mann-Whitney U test on before/after perceived understanding. Medians rose from 4 to 6 (juniors) and 3.5 to 7 (seniors), both "statistically significant improvement".

> "There was a statistically significant improvement between students’ median perceptions of understanding on a scale from 1 to 10 before and after annotation for junior (median before annotation = 4 out of 10, interquartile range 2–5; median after annotation = 6 out of 10, interquartile range 3–7; P < 0.0001; n = 119) and senior students (median before annotation = 3.5 out of 10, interquartile range 2.25–5; median after annotation = 7 out of 10, interquartile range 5.25–8; P = 0.005; n = 12)"
