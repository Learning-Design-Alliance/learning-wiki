## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Upper-division student difficulties with Separation of Variables. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020131)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of exam solutions to Cartesian SoV questions with implicit prompts. The article reports "only one of the 166 solutions to implicit prompts used a method other than SoV", suggesting students consistently activate SoV resources on these canonical questions.

> "Alternatively, prompts consistent with A2 or A3 require students’ to identify SoV as the appropriate technique, and only one of the 166 solutions to implicit prompts used a method other than SoV."
