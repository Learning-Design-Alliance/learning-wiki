## Evidence

### Mileder LP 2021

Mileder LP, Bereiter M, Wegscheider T. (2021). Telesimulation as a modality for neonatal resuscitation training. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1892017

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective observational pilot study (July 2020 – February 2021) with 18 participants assessed with a 20-question single-choice questionnaire before and after a one-hour telesimulation session; scores rose from "a median of 16/20" to "20/20 (IQR 18.75-20) correct answers" (p = 0.001).

> "For the whole cohort of 18 participants, neonatal resuscitation knowledge scores increased significantly from a median of 16/20 (IQR 14.5–18) before to 20/20 (IQR 18.75-20) correct answers after the educational intervention (p = 0.001; Figure 1)."
