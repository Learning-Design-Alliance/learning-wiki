# CA dimensions add only small explained variance beyond demographics and game play factors

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The additional explained variance provided by including CA in the explanatory models was quite low, with demographic variables such as gender and age having much stronger predictive validity. [→ Nicholas David Bowman 2012](#nicholas-david-bowman-2012)

## Evidence

### Nicholas David Bowman 2012

Nicholas David Bowman, Daniel Schultheiss, and Christina Schumann. (2012). 'I'm Attached, and I'm a Good Guy/Gal!': How Character Attachment Influences Pro- and Anti-Social Motivations to Play Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Games. CyberPsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking. https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2011.0311

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' stated limitation of their own regression models: CA subdimensions added delta adjusted R-squared of 0.019 (pro-social) and 0.034 (anti-social) in Step 3, while demographics dominated Step 1.

> "we should note that the additional explained variance provided by the inclusion of CA in our explanatory model is quite low. This can be partially explained by the fact that demographic variables such as gender and age had much stronger predictive validity"
