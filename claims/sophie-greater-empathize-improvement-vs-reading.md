## Evidence

### Haut KG 2026

Haut KG, Hasan M, Carroll T, Epstein RM, Sen T, Hoque E. (2026). Effects of a Generative AI Virtual Patient on Serious Illness Communication Skills: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/93034

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed effects models on 51 randomized participants (102 SP encounters) under intention-to-treat showed a significant study arm-by-time interaction for Empathize, "β=.07, 95% CI .01-.13; P=.03", representing additional pre-to-post improvement in the SOPHIE arm relative to control.

> "The SOPHIE arm showed greater pre-to-post improvement than the control arm for Empower (β=.11, 95% CI .04-.17; P=.002), Be Explicit (β=.08, 95% CI .03-.13; P=.002), Empathize (β=.07, 95% CI .01-.13; P=.03), and overall communication score (β=.09, 95% CI .04-.14; P=.001)."
