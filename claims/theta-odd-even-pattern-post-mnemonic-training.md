## Evidence

### Yafeng Pan 2023

Yafeng Pan, Ning Hao, Ning Liu, Yijie Zhao, Xiaojun Cheng, Yixuan Ku and Yi Hu. (2023). Mnemonic-trained brain tuning to a regular odd-even pattern subserves digit memory in children. npj Science of Learning 8:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00177-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Planned three-way repeated-measures ANOVAs on frontal theta power (4–8 Hz, 200–1000 ms) in the MT group revealed a significant three-way interaction; the post-training session showed "a signi ﬁcant increase in theta power from the odd- (10 ± 5 %, percent signal change) to even-position (24 ± 6 %) condition in the left anterior area"; the NC group's ANOVA found no significant results (all ps > 0.09).

> "Importantly, there was a signi ﬁcant three-way interac- tion, F (2, 30) = 6.92, p < 0.05, η2 partial = 0.37. Breaking down the ANOVA based on the factor Session revealed an interaction"
