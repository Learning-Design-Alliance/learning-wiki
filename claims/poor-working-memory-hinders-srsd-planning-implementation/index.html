Moderation finding from the same quasi-experimental study, in which working memory capacity, graphomotor skills, self-efficacy, and writing motivation were assessed as potential moderators. The article reports that "some students under the SRSD condition struggled to implement the planning strategies they were taught, particularly students with poor working memory".
