## Evidence

### Cao 2012

Linying Cao. (2012). A Feasibility Study of Task-based Teaching of College English Writing in Chinese EFL Context. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p80

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test of posttest scores in a six-week two-class quasi-experiment (40 students per class) comparing the task-based approach with the common teaching method; the article reports a significant difference with the experimental class "higher than that of the control class by about 1.1 points". No effect size is printed.

> "Similarly, Table 8 indicates that the posttest mean score of  the experiment is also higher than that of the control class by about 1.1 points. What’s more, in regard to eith er the former or the latter, such a difference is very significant in terms of statistics for the reason that the value of P (Sig.) is constantly less than α (α = 0.05)."
