Convergent validity analysis comparing automated formative scores with independent blinded expert ratings (3 attending physicians) across 26 sessions. "strong rank-order consistency for AP (ρ=0.88, P<.001; MAE=0.40), SR (ρ=0.93, P<.001)"; CC agreement was weakest (ρ=0.71, MAE=0.68).

> "Agreement analyses showed strong rank-order consistency for AP (ρ=0.88, P<.001; MAE=0.40), SR (ρ=0.93, P<.001; MAE=0.50), and CR (ρ=0.78, P=.007; MAE=0.45)."
