## Evidence

### Cho 2019

Cho, E., Capin, P., Roberts, G., Roberts, G. J., & Vaughn, S. (2019). Examining Sources and Mechanisms of Reading Comprehension Difficulties: Comparing English Learners and Non-English Learners within the Simple View of Reading. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000332

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent mean comparison after establishing partial scalar measurement invariance in the MG-CFA of 446 struggling fourth graders; ELs' reading comprehension factor scores were also lower (α = −.78, SE = .05, p < .05).

> "ELs had on average lower factor scores for vocabulary (α = −.63, SE = .09, p < .05) and listening comprehension (α = −.69, SE = .13, p < .05) but higher factor scores for word reading (α = .45, SE = .07, p < .05) in comparison to non-ELs"
