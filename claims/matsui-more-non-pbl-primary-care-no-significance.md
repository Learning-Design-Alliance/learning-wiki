### Tsigarides Jordan 2017

Tsigarides Jordan, Wingfield Laura R., Kulendran Myutan. (2017). Does a PBL-based medical curriculum predispose training in specific career paths? A systematic review of the literature. BMC Research Notes. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5219658/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Small study of 468 participants in Japan comparing graduates from two tracts (PBL and non-PBL curriculum) at Tokyo Women's Medical University, an all-female cohort aged 25–53 graduating 1989–2003. The review reports "more non-PBL graduates were working in primary care (9%)" with no significance mentioned.
