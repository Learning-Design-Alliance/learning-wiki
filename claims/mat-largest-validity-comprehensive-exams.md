## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The corrected true-score correlation between MAT scores and comprehensive examination scores is ρ = .58 with SDρ = .03, the largest validity among the academic criteria examined. [→ Kuncel et al. 2004](#kuncel-et-al-2004)

## Evidence

### Kuncel et al. 2004

Nathan R. Kuncel, Sarah A. Hezlett, and Deniz S. Ones. (2004). Academic Performance, Career Potential, Creativity, and Job Performance: Can One Construct Predict Them All? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.86.1.148

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of MAT validity for comprehensive/preliminary examination scores (Table 2). The printed correlation effect size is ρ = .58, SDρ = .03, k = 10, N = 987; the article notes the sample size is more modest than for GPA and faculty-rating criteria.

> "The validity of the MAT was largest for this criterion, with a ρ of .58 and a small (.03) SDρ , and was based on a more modest sample size (k ⫽ 10, N ⫽ 987) than the GGPA, 1st-year GPA, and faculty rating criteria."
