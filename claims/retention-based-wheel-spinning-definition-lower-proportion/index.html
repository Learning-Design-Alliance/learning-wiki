Evidence¶

Kai 2018¶

Kai, S., Heffernan, C., Almeda, M. V., Heffernan, N., & Baker, R. S. (2018). Decision Tree Modeling of Wheel-Spinning and Productive Persistence in Skill Builders. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 10, No 1. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/210

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive comparison of operational definitions over the same 75,481 student-problem set pairs with ARRS information; the article argues Beck and Gong's definition "contains a great deal of productive persistence" because many such students later pass retention tests.

"According to our definition, then, 9.1% of the total 75,481 student-problem set pairs that had ARRS information involved wheel -spinning, a much lower proportion of wheel -spinning than reported in earlier papers defining wheel-spinning as whether the student took a large number of problems to learn a skill"
