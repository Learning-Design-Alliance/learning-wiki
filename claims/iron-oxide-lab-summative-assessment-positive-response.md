### Armando D. Urbina 2024

Armando D. Urbina, Hari Sridhara, Alexis Scholtz, Andrea M. Armani. (2024). Synthesis and Characterization of Superparamagnetic Iron Oxide Nanoparticles: A Series of Laboratory Experiments. Journal of Chemical Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.19970](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.19970)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Summative assessment with qualitative and quantitative components administered immediately after the three-day lab to a volunteer cohort of first- through fourth-year USC engineering undergraduates. The article reports that "100% of the students felt that the lab experiment should be integrated as a required element". No effect size is printed.

> "Notably, 100% of the students felt that the lab experiment should be integrated as a required element into the undergraduate curriculum, and 100% felt that they gained valuable skills and/or knowledge applicable outside of this lab session."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same summative assessment of the pilot cohort: the article reports a "mixed response when asked about the level of the rigor of the lab", attributed to the cohort's diverse academic backgrounds. No effect size is printed.

> "However, the cohort had a mixed response when asked about the level of the rigor of the lab. Given the wide range in course preparation and academic levels, this response is not surprising."
