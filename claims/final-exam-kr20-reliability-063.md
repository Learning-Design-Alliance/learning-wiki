### Carl Wieman and N.G. Holmes 2015

Carl Wieman and N.G. Holmes. (2015). Measuring the Impact of Introductory Physics Labs on Learning. [doi:10.1119/1.4931717](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4931717)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post hoc psychometric analysis of the 20 final exam items: "KR-20 = 0.63 for the exam" and item-total "correlations between individual questions and the total score ranged from 0.15 to 0.51". The exam was not evaluated for validity or reliability a priori.

> "This was found to be KR-20 = 0.63 for the exam, which is an acceptable range for an internally consistent (reliable) test, measuring multiple constructs. Correlations between individual questions and the total score ranged from 0.15 to 0.51, with only two questions having r < 0.2."
