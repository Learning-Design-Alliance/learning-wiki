`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The author's interpretation of the Chart I data: the sample was dominated by children referred by schools and the Bureau of Attendance for unsatisfactory progress or truancy, skewing ratings below chronological age.

> "Bearing this in mind one will understand why so many cases fall below their chronological age in both of the tests and also why so few cases measure up to normal."
