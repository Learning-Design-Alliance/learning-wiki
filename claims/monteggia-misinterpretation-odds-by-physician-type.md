`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Univariate logistic regression with pediatric radiologists as reference, from the Results section of the 120-case diagnostic accuracy study. The article reports "OR = 17.50, 95% CI: 5.88–52.10, p < 0.001" for first-year residents on Monteggia fractures; for other fractures significance was limited to first-year residents (OR = 3.71) and general radiologists (OR = 2.81).

> "First-year orthopedic residents had the highest odds of misinterpretation for Monteggia fractures (OR = 17.50, 95% CI: 5.88–52.10, p < 0.001), followed by general radiologists (OR = 13.78, 95% CI: 4.71–40.28, p < 0.001)."
