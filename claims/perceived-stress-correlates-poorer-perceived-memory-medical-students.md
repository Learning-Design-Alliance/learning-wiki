## Evidence

### Pathmavarthini G 2026

Pathmavarthini G, Ughanthinii K, Shalini SB, Rubini SS, Trisha P, Devaashiny SV, Nursiati MT, Fatiah RMRZ, Dewi RR, Anwar ZK. (2026). Prediction of academic outcomes by serum cortisol, perceived academic stress, perceived memory performance, and physical activity among preclinical undergraduate medical students: a short-term observational study. BMC Psychology 14:557. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04327-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis of baseline questionnaire data from 106 preclinical medical students. The study reports a "fair-strength and significantly positive correlation" between MSSQ stress and PRMQ memory-problem scores, with a printed effect size of r = 0.445 (printed as rS=0.445), p < 0.001.

> "a fair-strength and significantly positive correlation was observed between perceived stress (overall MSSQ score) and perceived memory problems (overall PRMQ score) (rS=0.445, p < 0.001)"
