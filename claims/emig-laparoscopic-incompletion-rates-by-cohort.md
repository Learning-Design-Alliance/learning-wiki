`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of did-not-complete rates from the same 227-participant cohort trial, reported in Figure 4 and accompanying text. Participants who timed out were assigned the maximum allowable time for time calculations; the novice cohort had high failure rates in all but L-3, and only experts had negligible rates for all five exercises.

> "The novice cohort had high failure to complete rates in all but the extracorporeal knotting exercise (L-3), and for the mid-level and proficient groups, failure to complete rates were greater than 15% for the Circle Cut (L-2) and intracorporeal knot tying (L-4) exercises. Only the expert group had negligible failure to complete rates for all five laparoscopic exercises."
