## Evidence

### Darling-Hammond 2022

Darling-Hammond, L., Wechsler, M. E., Levin, S., Leung-Gagné, M., & Tozer, S. (2022). Developing effective principals: What kind of learning matters? [Report]. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/641.201

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National and California principal survey analyses found principals in low-poverty schools reported more learning opportunities and more problem-based and cohort-based preparation; "principals serving high-poverty schools were less than half as likely as principals serving low-poverty schools" to have a mentor or coach.
