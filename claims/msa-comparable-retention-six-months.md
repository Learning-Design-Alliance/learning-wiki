## Evidence

### Sopka Saša 2012

Sopka Saša, Biermann Henning, Rossaint Rolf, Knott Sebastian, Skorning Max, Brokmann Jörg C, Heussen Nicole, Beckers Stefan K. (2012). Evaluation of a newly developed media-supported 4-step approach for basic life support training. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-20-37

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Six-month follow-up of the controlled parallel-group trial, with n=30 standard and n=32 MSA participants re-evaluated on the same manikin scenario. The article reports no difference detected in initial assessment quality or CPR initiation delay; retention of skills was comparable between groups.

> "Even after six months, there was no difference detected in the quality of the initial assessment algorithm or delay concerning initiation of CPR."
