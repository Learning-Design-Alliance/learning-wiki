> "In the mandible, significant differences were found in favour of the analogue measurement method, with an average deviation of 1.15 ± 1.23 mm in group A compared with 1.56 ± 2.89 mm in group D (p = 0.0137) and an average deviation of 1.36 ± 1.95 mm in group A and 2.36 ± 2.05 mm in group D (p < 0.0001) (Fig. 3; Table 3)."
