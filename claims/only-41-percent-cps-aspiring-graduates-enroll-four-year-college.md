## Evidence

### Roderick 2011

Roderick, M., Coca, V., & Nagaoka, J. (2011). Potholes on the Road to College: High School Effects in Shaping Urban Students' Participation in College Application, Four-year College Enrollment, and College Match. Sociology of Education, 84(3), 178-211. https://doi.org/10.1177/0038040711411280

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of 2005 CPS graduates (N=5,194) who aspired to a four-year degree, tracking sequential steps: planning, applying, acceptance, and enrollment. The sample was more qualified than the full cohort, so this likely overestimates the broader rate.

> "Even with this more qualified sample, only 41 percent met each of these benchmarks and enrolled in a four-year college the fall after graduation."
