`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Modified Delphi consensus recommendation from a 19-member UK and Ireland expert panel, graded strong with moderate-quality evidence and reaching 100% agreement. The supporting systematic review it cites encompassed 28 trainees and 7051 EUS procedures, and reported that "Only 4 of 17 trainees reached competency by 225 to 295 EUS procedures."

> "All trainees should have evidence of a lifetime ‘hands-on’ experience of a minimum of 250 EUS cases prior to assessment for certification. (Strong recommendation, moderate-quality evidence) Consensus: 100%"
