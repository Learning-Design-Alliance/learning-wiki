Julia Chatain 2025¶

Julia Chatain, Charlotte Müller, Keny Chatain, Leon Calabrese, Manu Kapur. (2025). Concreteness and Abstraction in Mathematics Education: A Taxonomy of the Semantic Landscape. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10073-9

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Narrative review attributing this to cited studies: the review reports that Kaminski et al. (2008) found concrete real-world examples may impair performance on abstract transfer tasks, while follow-up studies by Trninic et al. (2020) and De Bock et al. (2011) canceled this effect. The review narrates these studies without re-analyzing them.

"the effectiveness of concrete representations may lie more in how relatable they are rather than in the sole fact that they exist in the real world"
