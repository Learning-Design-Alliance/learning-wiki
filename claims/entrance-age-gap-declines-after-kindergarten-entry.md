`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The article reports the developmental trajectory of the entrance-age association: it “appears during the first months of kindergarten” and then “declines sharply in subsequent years.” This timing pattern is presented as evidence against an in-school learning-ability advantage. No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "The association between achievement test scores and entrance age appears during the first months of kindergarten, declines sharply in subsequent years, and is especially pronounced among children from upper-income families, a group likely to have accumulated the most skills prior to school entry."
