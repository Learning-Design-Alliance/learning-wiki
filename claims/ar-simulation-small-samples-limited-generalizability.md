The review's synthesis of AR studies found that "a notable limitation across most studies was that the AR simulators were tested on a small subset of neurosurgical patients", which the authors state restricts generalizability.

> "Nonetheless, a notable limitation across most studies was that the AR simulators were tested on a small subset of neurosurgical patients, which restricts the generalizability of their reported utility and effectiveness [6, 8, 10, 15, 19, 28, 29, 50, 60, 66, 67, 69]."
