# All 48 reviewed spacing studies obtained the spacing effect using rest-from-deliberate-learning periods

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ouhao Chen & Fred Paas & John Sweller 2021

Ouhao Chen & Fred Paas & John Sweller. (2021). Spacing and Interleaving Effects Require Distinct Theoretical Bases: a Systematic Review Testing the Cognitive Load and Discriminative-Contrast Hypotheses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09613-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review analysis of 48 studies coded as spacing studies, all of which included rest-from-deliberate-learning. The review reports that "All of the 48 spaced practice studies (see Table1) obtained the spacing effect" with rest periods from 30 s to days. Effect sizes in Table 1 ranged widely, e.g. d = .26 to 2.98.

> "All of the 48 spaced practice studies (see Table1) obtained the spacing effect and used a rest-from-deliberate-learning period either by distributing learning sessions across multiple days or months or by inserting rest-from-deliberate-learning activities between learning sessions for 30 s to hours or days."
