# Effects on affirming motivations and reading comprehension are trivial at delayed post-tests and do not significantly differ from immediate-posttest effects

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r?` · `q4` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q4 i0` For affirming motivations, the delayed-posttest effect was trivial (d = 0.19) and did not significantly differ from the immediate effect. [→ van der Sande 2023](#van-der-sande-2023)
`q4 i0` For reading comprehension, the delayed-posttest effect was trivial (d = 0.16) and did not significantly differ from the immediate effect. [→ van der Sande 2023 (2)](#van-der-sande-2023-2)

## Evidence

### van der Sande 2023

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

`q4 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Delayed-posttest synthesis (k = 5 for affirming motivations, delay 2 to 28 weeks). The immediate effect was small (d = 0.40) and the delayed effect trivial (d = 0.19); the difference was not significant (Q(1) = 2.00, p = .16).

> "For affirming motivations, a small effect was found on immediate post-tests (Cohen's d = 0.40; SE = 0.07) and a trivial effect on delayed post-tests (Cohen's d = 0.19; SE = 0.13)."

### van der Sande 2023 (2)

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

`q4 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Delayed-posttest synthesis (k = 7 for reading comprehension). The immediate effect was small (d = 0.29) and the delayed effect trivial (d = 0.16); the difference was not significant (Q(1) = 1.99, p = 0.16).

> "For reading comprehension, a small effect was found on immediate post-tests (Cohen's d = 0.29; SE = 0.06) and a trivial effect on delayed post-tests (Cohen's d = 0.16; SE = 0.07)."
