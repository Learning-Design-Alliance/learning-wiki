### Alali Faisal Mehsen 2025

BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07698-3

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate binary logistic regression of the cross-sectional survey data (Table 4) with study level, gender and university type as predictors. Table 4 prints intern OR = 1.55 (1.01–2.37), p = 0.045 for complications knowledge and female OR = 1.65 (1.15–2.36), p = 0.007 for care-management knowledge.

> "Being a dental intern was a significant predictor of good knowledge regarding oral complications from cancer therapy, while being female was a significant predictor of good knowledge in oral care/health management for cancer patients (p < 0.05)."
