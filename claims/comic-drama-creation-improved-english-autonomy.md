## Evidence

### Yu-Ju Lan 2018

Yu-Ju Lan. (2018). Technology enhanced learner ownership and learner autonomy through creation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9608-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The editorial reports, citing Chen, Fan, Chang, Chang, and Chen, a study of a situational comic play (SCP) learning system with four fifth-grade classes, one conventional and three using SCP, with knowledge application tests administered. The SCP students "outperformed those without such a learning experience"; no effect size is printed.

> "The finding was that the students learning with comic drama creation outperformed those without such a learning experience"

### Yu-Ju Lan 2018 (2)

Yu-Ju Lan. (2018). Technology enhanced learner ownership and learner autonomy through creation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9608-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the same SCP study, a questionnaire on learner autonomy and ownership was administered; students self-reported that SCP use "improved their learning autonomy." This is self-reported, with no effect size printed.

> "Besides, the students also expressed in the question- naire that by using SCP for learning English improved their learning autonomy"
