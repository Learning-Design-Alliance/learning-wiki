## Evidence

### Gianotto-Oliveira R 2026

Gianotto-Oliveira R, Rojas M, Queiroz M, Zanchetta F, Ferrari A, Kojima L, Paula A, Campos B, Cecilio-Fernandes D, Caruso TJ. (2026). Remote Augmented Reality Versus Traditional Simulation for Team Leader Assessment in a Cardiac Arrest Scenario: Noninferiority Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/84367

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Parallel noninferiority randomized trial at UNICAMP, Brazil, with 42 residents (remote AR n=22; traditional n=20) leading an identical VF cardiac arrest scenario. Mean checklist scores were "41.6 (SD 6.2)" (AR) and "42.6 (SD 5.8)" (traditional); the AR 95% CI (38.9-44.4) exceeded the 34.1 margin. No effect sizes were reported.

> "The remote AR group demonstrated comparable performance to the traditional group in cognitive leadership tasks, in the objective and subjective scores (Table 2). In all cases, the lower bound of the 95% CI for the AR group exceeded the predefined noninferiority margin"
