## Evidence

### Dubosh NM 2020

Dubosh NM, Hall MM, Novack V, Shafat T, Shapiro NI, Ullman EA. (2020). A Multimodal Curriculum With Patient Feedback to Improve Medical Student Communication: Pilot Study. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2018.11.44318

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized pilot trial of 64 fourth-year students (27 intervention, 37 control) in an EM clerkship, analyzed with a generalized estimating equation logistic regression model. Descriptive percentages were 62.3% vs 51.1% maximal scores in weeks 3–4; the printed effect is the odds ratio "OR [1.65], 95% CI, 1.14–2.41, p = 0.008".

> "During the second half of the clerkship (weeks 3–4), the intervention group students achieved a maximum score more often than the control group (OR [1.65], 95% CI, 1.14–2.41, p = 0.008, Table 3)."
