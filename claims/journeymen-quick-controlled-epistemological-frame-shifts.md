# Journeyman physics students can shift their epistemological framings quickly yet in a controlled manner during problem solving

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Case study of two quantum mechanics students shows rapid, controlled shifting among framings in a homework episode. [→ Bing 2011](#bing-2011)

## Evidence

### Bing 2011

Bing, T. J., & Redish, E. F. (2011). Epistemic Complexity and the Journeyman-Expert Transition. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.8.010105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.8.010105)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case study drawn from approximately 150 hours of video data of upper-level undergraduates solving physics problems; two students working Griffiths problem 6.32b shifted framings. The article reports they shifted "very quickly, yet in a con- trolled manner."

> "the students in these examples  were seen to shift their epistemological framings very quickly, yet in a con- trolled manner (that is, their fellow students and the interviewer had little trouble following their line of thinking)"
