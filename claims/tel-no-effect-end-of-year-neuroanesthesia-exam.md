### Raksakietisak M 2024

Raksakietisak M, Lertsiripatarajit V, Aroonpruksakul N, Plailaharn N, Raksamani K. (2024). Test-enhanced learning in Neuroanesthesia for the First Year anesthetic residents: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 24:905. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05887-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

At the conclusion of the first year, all residents undertook the program's final neuroanesthesia exam of 20 questions; TEL-group scores of "13.7 ± 1.72" did not differ from controls' 13.1 ± 2.05, "(p = 0.322)". No effect size was printed.

> "Furthermore, there were no significant differences between the end-of-year exam scores of the participants in Group A (13.7 ± 1.72) and Group B (13.1 ± 2.05) (p = 0.322), as illustrated in Table 2."
