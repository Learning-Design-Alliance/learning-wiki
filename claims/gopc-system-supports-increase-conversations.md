## Evidence

### Brown 2025

Brown, Janie; Hu-Collins, Phoebe. (2025). Communication training for effective Goals of Patient Care conversations in acute care: An integrative review of the literature. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951525000264

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental study across six general medicine teams pairing a 10-minute didactic and pocket card with text alerts within 48 hours of admission. The review reports significantly more documented goals-of-care discussions after implementation versus the control period, scored from admission charts.

> "Significantly more patients had goals of care discussion documented in the medical record after tool was implemented, compared to the control period."

### Brown 2025 (2)

Brown, Janie; Hu-Collins, Phoebe. (2025). Communication training for effective Goals of Patient Care conversations in acute care: An integrative review of the literature. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951525000264

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study during COVID-19 with 143 patients and 72 emergency clinicians, combining a 60-minute virtual training with templated EHR Smart Phrases for ACP documentation. The review reports the 25.4% increase in ED-based ACP activities descriptively, with no effect size printed.

> "Observed a clinically meaningful increase in ED-based ACP activities in 25.4% of subjects across the pre- and post-intervention groups"
