`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of CBI-30 sub-dimension scores among 249 newly graduated nurses. The CBI-30 total median was 164.0 (103.0–180.0). The authors interpret this as new graduates prioritising task-oriented care over individualised patient care.

> "The highest median score was recorded in the 'Professional Knowledge and Attitude' subdimension, with a median of 56.0 (range: 30.0–60.0)."
