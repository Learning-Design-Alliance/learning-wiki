Evidence¶

Julian Roelle 2023¶

Julian Roelle, Tino Endres, Roman Abel, Niklas Obergassel, Matthias Nückles, Alexander Renkl. (2023). Happy Together? On the Relationship Between Research on Retrieval Practice and Generative Learning Using the Case of Follow-Up Learning Tasks. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09810-9

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of indirect metacognitive effects states that "generative learning activities mainly influence metacomprehension, whereas retrieval practice primarily influences metamemory," attributing the difference to which implicit cues become salient during each activity type. Both activity types can foster monitoring accuracy.

"However, generative learning activities mainly influence metacomprehension, whereas retrieval practice primarily influences metamemory."
