## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Alsadi 2025

Alsadi, M., Alosta, M. R., Othman, E. H., Obiedallah, H., & Nashwan, A. J. (2025). Optimising Educational Practices in Simulation‐Based Nursing Education: Results of A National Survey. Nursing Open, 12(4), e70172. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70172

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ANOVA comparison of SDS and EPQ scores across three self-rated computer skill levels (excellent, medium, weak) in the same survey; Table 4 reports p < 0.001 for both scales. No effect size is printed.

> "students with higher computer skills have statistically higher SDS scores (M = 3.37 ± 1.00) and EPQ scores (M = 3.54 ± 0.83) compared to students who have a lower‐rated computer skill."
