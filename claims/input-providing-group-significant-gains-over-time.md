## Evidence

### Mousavi 2018

Mousavi, K., Alavinia, P., & Gholami, J. (2018). Input Providing vs. Output-Prompting Negotiation Strategies in Learning Grammar among Young EFL Learners. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 497-512. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11234a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the input-providing group's pretest, immediate posttest and delayed posttest scores (N=18, means 6.88, 15.55, 15.66). Sphericity was violated, so the Greenhouse-Geisser row was used. Bonferroni comparisons showed significant pretest-to-posttest gains but no significant immediate-to-delayed difference; no standardized effect size is printed in the quoted finding.

> "for ANOVA with repeated measures with a Greenhouse -Geisser correction, the mean scores for grammar learning concentration were statistically significantly different ( F (1.33, 22.67) = 152.72,  p < 0.0001)."
