### Harendza Sigrid 2019

Harendza Sigrid, Soll Henning, Prediger Sarah, Kadmon Martina, Berberat Pascal O., Oubaid Viktor. (2019). Assessing core competences of medical students with a test for flight school applicants. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1438-1

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup comparison within the 67 medical students (S10 n = 28, final year n = 39 per Fig. 3): t-tests showed higher awareness in semester-10 students, "p < .02; d = .58", with both groups below the flight school entrance level.

> "Students in semester 10 (S10) showed significantly (p < .02; d = .58) higher scores in the domain awareness (AW) compared with students in their final year (PY). On average, neither group reached the flight school entrance level in this domain"
