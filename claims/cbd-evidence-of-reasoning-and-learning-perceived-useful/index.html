Evidence¶

Vundum Jonathon 2025¶

Vundum Jonathon, Day Fiona. (2025). Evaluating the Efficacy of Case-Based Learning for Medical Oncology Trainees: A Systematic Review. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-025-02651-w

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative study (Jyothirmayi, 2012) recording and transcribing CBDs for conversational analysis plus semi-structured interviews with 12 participants (6 registrars, 6 consultants). The review reports the study "concluded that reasoning and learning were taking place during CBDs"; knowledge retention was not examined.

"The study concluded that reasoning and learning were taking place during CBDs and that they were perceived as useful."
