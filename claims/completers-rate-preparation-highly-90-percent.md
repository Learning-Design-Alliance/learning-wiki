### Patrick 2023

Patrick, S. K., Darling-Hammond, L., & Kini, T. (2023). Educating teachers in California: What matters for teacher preparedness? Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/956.678

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of CTC program completer survey responses from almost 60,000 completers (2016–17 to 2020–21), with estimated annual response rates of 84–90%. The report states "Ninety percent of responding completers rated their TPP as effective or very effective." More than 80% felt well or very prepared for each TPE except working with families (72%) and self-assessment (79%).

> "Ninety percent of responding completers rated their TPP as effective or very effective, and 90% or more felt at least adequately prepared in all areas represented by the state’s teaching performance expectations."
