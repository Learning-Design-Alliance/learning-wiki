## Evidence

### Benhamou Dan 2023

Benhamou Dan, Tant Sarah, Gille Benoit, Bornemann Yannis, Ruscio Laura, Kamel Karl, Dunyach Chloé, Jeannin Bénédicte, Bouilliant-linet Maxime, Blanié Antonia. (2023). An observer tool to enhance learning of incoming anesthesia residents' skills during simulation training of central venous catheter insertion: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04915-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 91 incoming anesthesia residents randomized to observer tool (OT+, n = 49) or no tool (OT-, n = 42) during a simulation bootcamp; the composite score showed "no significant difference between groups (p = 0.323)". Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "Immediately after training, the composite score was similar between the two groups: 45.3 ± 4.2 (OT+, n = 49) and 44.4 ± 4.8 (OT-, n = 42) (p = 0.323)."
