q2 · i? · associational · r2

EdTrust analysis of DESE/NCES data on the percentage of Massachusetts high schools offering any AP course by majority race/ethnicity in SY 2021-22; the accompanying figure shows 65% for majority-Black, 63% for majority-Latino, and 91% for majority-White schools.

"Looking at AP classes specifically, schools that are more than 50% Black or Latino are much less likely to offer AP courses than majority-White schools. This statistic stayed fairly constant from 2018-19 to 2021-22."
