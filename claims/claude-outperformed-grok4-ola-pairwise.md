> "In pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons, Claude Sonnet 4.5 significantly outperformed Grok 4 (Z=3.18; r=0.53; adjusted P=.009). No other pairwise comparisons were significant (adjusted P≥.08 in all cases)."
