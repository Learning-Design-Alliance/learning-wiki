## Evidence

### Domarecki Patryk 2025

Domarecki Patryk, Plata-Nazar Katarzyna, Sohl Kristin. (2025). An Assessment of the Knowledge of Autism Spectrum Disorder Among Polish Primary Care Physicians. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina61040761

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

U-Mann–Whitney comparison within the 166-physician survey (23 male, 143 female). Female physicians scored higher on all categories; general knowledge means were "7.87 vs. 10.60; p < 0.001" for males versus females.

> "We found statistically significant differences in the level of ASD general knowledge (7.87 vs. 10.60; p < 0.001), the knowledge of etiology (1.7 vs. 2.36; p < 0.039), diagnosis (2 vs. 3.16 p < 0.001), and support (4 vs. 5.07; p < 0.001) between male and female physicians."
