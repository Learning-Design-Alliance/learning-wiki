### Bismilla Zia 2015

Bismilla Zia, Dubrowski Adam, Amin Harish J. (2015). Program directors’ perceptions of importance of pediatric procedural skills and resident preparedness. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1499-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of mean importance and preparedness ratings from the same 16-respondent survey; four of the ten gap procedures are FITER skills. Means are descriptive; no effect size or test statistic is printed for this contrast.

> "Ten procedures with a mean importance rating >3 had a mean preparedness rating of <3: IV access/blood drawing, OG/NG tube placement, immunizations, bladder catheterization, limb immobilization, tracheostomy tube care, ear curettage, GU/pelvic exam/specimens, ENT foreign body removal, and chest tube/thoracentesis"
