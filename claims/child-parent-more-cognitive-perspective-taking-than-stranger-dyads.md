# Child-parent dyads manifested more cognitive perspective taking than child-stranger dyads, but no group difference was found in social-emotional perspective taking

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Child-parent dyads manifested more Listen/understand and Extend/revise behaviors than child-stranger dyads without control variables, but this effect became insignificant when Utterance number and Inviting behavior were controlled. [→ Bai 2026](#bai-2026)
`q3 i?` No group difference between child-parent and child-stranger dyads was found on social-emotional perspective taking measures, with or without control variables. [→ Bai 2026 (2)](#bai-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Bai 2026

Bai, H.; Chan, L.; Luo, A.; Kroesbergen, E.H.; Christie, S. (2026). Creativity in dialogues: How do children interact with parents vs. strangers for generating creative ideas? Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102286

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

MANOVA on dyad-level perspective-taking measures showed a significant dyad-type effect without controls (Pillai's Trace = 0.08), "showing that the child-parent dyads manifested more Listen/ understand as well as more Extend/revise behaviors than the child-stranger dyads". No effect size beyond Pillai's Trace was printed.

> "showing that the child-parent dyads manifested more Listen/ understand as well as more Extend/revise behaviors than the child-stranger dyads (see Table 3 for the ANOVA test results)"

### Bai 2026 (2)

Bai, H.; Chan, L.; Luo, A.; Kroesbergen, E.H.; Christie, S. (2026). Creativity in dialogues: How do children interact with parents vs. strangers for generating creative ideas? Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102286

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

MANOVA/MANCOVA comparing the four social-emotional perspective taking codes between the 60 child-parent and 43 child-stranger dyads found "no group difference was found, whether the control variables were included or not". This is a non-significant result with no printed effect size; equivalence was not tested.

> "Regarding the social-emotional perspective taking measures, no group difference was found, whether the control variables were included or not."
