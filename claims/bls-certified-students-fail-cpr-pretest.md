## Evidence

### Oermann Marilyn H. 2020

Oermann Marilyn H., Krusmark Michael A., Kardong-Edgren Suzan, Jastrzembski Tiffany S., Gluck Kevin A. (2020). Training interval in cardiopulmonary resuscitation. PLoS ONE 15(1): e0226786. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0226786

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest assessment before feedback in each training session showed certified students below the 75% pass criterion; the article states they "were not able to provide quality compressions". Ventilation pretest means ranged from 15.9 to 22.8 across intervals. No effect size printed.

> "At session 1 pretest, even though all of the participants were certified in BLS, they were not able to provide quality compressions, scoring less than the 75% criterion (Fig 2)."
