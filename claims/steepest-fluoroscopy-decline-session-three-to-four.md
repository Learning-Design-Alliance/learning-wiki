`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section analysis of virtual fluoroscopy times across the five sessions for ten students, with box-and-whisker plots and Wilcoxon Signed Rank testing in SPSS. The authors report the session-by-session mean decreases as printed.

> "Notably, the steepest mean reduction in virtual fluoroscopy time, 3.8 min, occurred between the third and fourth session. Between the other exercises, a consistent decrease in virtual fluoroscopy time, averaging 2.49 min per session, was recorded."
