Descriptive analysis of program records for 56 school-based tutoring sites (32 rural, 24 nonrural) on timing of tutoring in 2022/23. Interviewees attributed the rural preference to transportation barriers and difficulty reaching students after school.

"Ninety-two percent of rural schools offered tutoring during school only, compared with 47 percent of nonrural schools."
