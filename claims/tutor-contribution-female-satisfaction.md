## Evidence

### Francisco González-Gómez 2011

Francisco González-Gómez, Jorge Guardiola, Óscar Martín Rodríguez, Miguel Ángel Montero Alonso (University of Granada, Spain). (2011). Gender differences in e-learning satisfaction. FEG Working Paper Series. http://www.ugr.es/~biblio/biblioteca_ugr/bibliotecas_centros/economicas/index.html

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regressions by gender (Table 3) show tutor contribution significant for female students (0.10***, p 0.0001) but not males (0.04, p 0.2381). The article notes past literature (Sun et al., 2008) indicating female students value tutor proximity more.

> "The results indicate that the contribution of the tutor is important for female students when they form their overall view of the course, while for male students it is irrelevant."
