## Evidence

### Maciej Jakubowski 2025

Maciej Jakubowski, Tomasz Gajderowicz & Harry Anthony Patrinos. (2025). COVID-19, school closures, and student learning outcomes. New global evidence from PISA. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00297-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Numerical regression estimates shown in Fig. 1 compare predicted losses at the 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles of four closure measures (UNESCO full and full-plus-partial; PISA student and principal reports). Countries with the longest closures "experienced losses of 17–23% SD, or 9–12 months of learning".

> "First, the learning losses increase with the length of school closures. This shows that the achievement decline is associated with school closures and not only the overall impact of the pandemic on students."
