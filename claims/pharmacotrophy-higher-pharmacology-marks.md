# Participation in the Pharmacotrophy tournament was associated with 20.1% higher pharmacology exam marks than non-participants, with comparable overall academic levels

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Over 2021 and 2022 combined, tournament participants scored on average 20.1% higher on pharmacology questions in the PNEDI exam than non-participants (p = 0.02). [→ Delage Clément 2024](#delage-clement-2024)

## Evidence

### Delage Clément 2024

Delage Clément, Palayer Maeva, Lerouet Dominique, Besson Valérie C. (2024). “Pharmacotrophy”: a playful tournament for game- and team-based learning in pharmacology education - assessing its impact on students’ performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05157-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective comparison of 4th-year exam marks between tournament participants (n2021 = 19, n2022 = 20) and the rest of the class (n2021 = 315–320, n2022 = 279–281), using the ratio of each student’s mark to the class mean. The article reports “20.1% higher marks on pharmacology questions” for participants (p = 0.02 in the abstract).

> "Thus, on average and over both years, “Pharmacotrophy” participants had 20.1% higher marks on pharmacology questions than non-participants."
