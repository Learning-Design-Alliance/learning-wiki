Process-level analysis addressing RQ2 in the same within-subjects study: JVA aggregated two minutes before versus two minutes after feedback showed a significant increase (t[df = 25] = 12.76, p<.0001). No effect size printed.

> "a test between the JVA values before (average for the two minutes before) and after (averaged for two minutes after) the feedback was provided showed a significant increase in the JVA of the dyads (t[df = 25] = 12.76, p<.0001, figure 4a)."
