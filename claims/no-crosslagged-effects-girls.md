## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The prospective cross-lagged effects observed among boys were not significant in girls, and the gender difference was significant for SW 10 → AA 12, AA 6 → SW 8, and AA 12 → SW 14. [→ Stenseng 2022](#stenseng-2022)

## Evidence

### Stenseng 2022

Stenseng, F., Tingstad, E. B., Wichstrøm, L., & Skalicka, V. (2022). Social withdrawal and academic achievement, intertwined over years? Bidirectional effects from primary to upper secondary school. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 92, 1354–1365. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12504

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group RI-CLPM analyses by sex, evaluated with the Satorra–Bentler chi-square test, in the five-wave TESS sample. The article states "None of these effects was significant in girls" and reports the significant gender-difference tests for three specific paths.

> "None of these effects was significant in girls (Figures 2 and 3). The gender difference proved significant in SW 10 → AA 12 (Δχ2 = 7.24 [1], p = .007), AA 6 → SW 8 (Δχ2 = 18.70 [1], p < .001) and AA 12 → SW 14 (Δχ2 = 4.86 [1], p = .028)."
