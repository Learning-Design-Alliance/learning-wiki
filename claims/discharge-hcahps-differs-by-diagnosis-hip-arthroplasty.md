# Discharge HCAHPS scores differ by diagnosis: total hip arthroplasty patients score higher than knee arthroplasty, cervical and lumbar disc herniation patients, driven by question 19

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Patients with total hip arthroplasty presented a higher discharge HCAHPS score than those with total knee arthroplasty, cervical disc herniation, and lumbar disc herniation (p=0.024, p=0.027, and p=0.05, respectively), with no difference between the Intervention and Control Groups within each diagnosis. ## Evidence

### Barbosa Luciana Gardin 2020

Barbosa Luciana Gardin, Frazão Cinthya da Silva. (2020). Impact of demonstration in a realistic simulation environment as a postoperative education in patients’ experience. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2020AO4831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis comparison of discharge HCAHPS scores across diagnoses in the randomized trial. Total hip arthroplasty patients "presented a higher score than those with total knee arthroplasty, cervical and lumbar disc herniation"; no group difference existed within each diagnosis, and no effect size is printed.

> "There was a difference in the evaluation of the discharge HCAHPS between the diagnoses. Patients with total hip arthroplasty presented a higher score than those with total knee arthroplasty, cervical and lumbar disc herniation (p=0.024, p=0.027, and p=0.05, respectively)."

### Barbosa Luciana Gardin 2020 (2)

Barbosa Luciana Gardin, Frazão Cinthya da Silva. (2020). Impact of demonstration in a realistic simulation environment as a postoperative education in patients’ experience. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2020AO4831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up analysis attributing the diagnosis difference to question 19, which asks whether staff discussed whether the patient would have the help needed when leaving the hospital. "No" answers occurred mainly in the hip arthroplasty group; the authors see this as difficulty measuring the need for help in this population.

> "This difference in the discharge HCAHPS score was due to a better performance by patients with a diagnosis of total hip arthroplasty on question 19 (p=0.001)."
