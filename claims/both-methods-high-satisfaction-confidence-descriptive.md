## Evidence

### Tanrıkulu F 2025

Tanrıkulu F, Gündoğdu H, Erol F, Dikmen Y. (2025). A comparison of 3D game-based simulation versus traditional methods in vital signs education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07980-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive result from the randomized controlled trial: intervention total 3.73 ± 0.68 and control total 3.97 ± 0.43 on the 12-item Student Satisfaction and Self-Confidence in Learning Scale; these are untested descriptive means.

> "it was determined that the overall satisfaction and self-confidence scores of students in both groups were considered high. This finding shows that both traditional teaching methods and 3D animation-supported simulation applications can positively affect students’ satisfaction and confidence levels in learning."
