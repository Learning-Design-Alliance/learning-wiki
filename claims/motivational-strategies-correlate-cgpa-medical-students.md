## Evidence

### Nabizadeh Sakineh; Hajian Sepideh; Sheikhan Zohre; Rafiei Fatemeh 2019

Nabizadeh Sakineh; Hajian Sepideh; Sheikhan Zohre; Rafiei Fatemeh. (2019). Prediction of academic achievement based on learning strategies and outcome expectations among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1527-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in the 380-student cross-sectional study; the motivational strategies structure correlated with CGPA at R = 0.193 (p < 0.001), alongside the learning strategies correlation, while outcome expectations showed no relationship.

> "There was a direct and significant relationship between the motivational strategies’ structures (R = 0.193, p < 0.001) as well as learning strategies (R = 0.243, p < 0.001) and the CGPA, while there was no relationship between outcome expectations and CGPA."
