# Both individual and organizational factors are perceived to influence competency enactment, with individual factors seen as easier to modify

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · qualitative `r3` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Focus group participants perceived both individual factors (knowledge/skills, motivation, emotions, behavioural regulation, beliefs about consequences and capabilities) and organizational factors (context/resources, social influences/team, climate/culture, stressors/pressure) as influencing enactment, maintenance and development of competencies. [→ Rochette Annie 2020](#rochette-annie-2020)
`q2 i?` Participants perceived individual factors as easier to modify than organizational factors. [→ Rochette Annie 2020 (2)](#rochette-annie-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Rochette Annie 2020

Rochette Annie, Brousseau Martine, Vachon Brigitte, Engels Cynthia, Amari Fatima, Thomas Aliki. (2020). What occupational therapists' say about their competencies' enactment, maintenance and development in practice? A two-phase mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02087-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Phase 2 focus groups with 16 occupational therapists, analyzed with the Framework Approach using the Theoretical Domains Framework as an initial coding grid. The article reports "both individual and organizational factors were perceived as influencing" competency.

> "Qualitatively, both individual and organizational factors were perceived as influencing the enactment, maintenance and development of competencies."

### Rochette Annie 2020 (2)

Rochette Annie, Brousseau Martine, Vachon Brigitte, Engels Cynthia, Amari Fatima, Thomas Aliki. (2020). What occupational therapists' say about their competencies' enactment, maintenance and development in practice? A two-phase mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02087-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participant quote from the focus groups illustrating that individual factors were "perceived as easier to modify"; a contrasting participant quote states extrinsic factors "may take longer" to change.

> "I think that in an intrinsic way there are still many that can be modified, such as interpersonal skills, abilities, knowledge, professional identity, our emotions."
