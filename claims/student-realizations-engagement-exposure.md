Stanton, J. D., Neider, X. N., Gallegos, I. J., & Clark, N. C. (2015). Differences in Metacognitive Regulation in Introductory Biology Students: When Prompts Are Not Enough. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0135

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of E1-SE responses (n = 245) identified two themes related to understanding the task. The article reports 33 students (13.5%) recognized the need for active approaches, and "More than half of the students (55.5%) saw the need to spend more time with the material, yet many still focused on retention of information."
