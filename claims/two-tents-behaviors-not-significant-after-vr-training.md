## Evidence

### Mazur L 2025

Mazur L, Butler L, Mitchell C, Lashani S, Buchanan S, Fenison C, Adapa K, Tan X, An S, Ra J. (2025). Effect of Immersive Virtual Reality Teamwork Training on Safety Behaviors During Surgical Cases: Nonrandomized Intervention Versus Controlled Pilot Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/66186

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same pilot comparison of 10 intervention and 10 control surgeons, Table 1 shows P values of .14 for behavior 1d and .15 for behavior 4b. The authors note very few patient interactions were observed, limiting assessment of 1d, and that 4b was the highest-scoring behavior at baseline. No effect size was printed.

> "The 2 behaviors that did not reach statistical significance, despite trending positively, were 1d (communicating and receiving information with patients), and 4b (willingness to support others across different roles) (Table 1)."
