## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Walck-Shannon 2021

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Rowell, S. F., & Frey, R. F. (2021). To What Extent Do Study Habits Relate to Performance? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-05-0091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression (exam 2 n = 447; posttest n = 392; final n = 450) controlling for the base model including total study time. On average students began studying 5.842 days in advance (SD = 4.377). No effect size was printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "the number of days in advance that studying began was not a significant predictor of in-term exam 2, the posttest, or the cumulative final (Table 5; see Supplemental Table 7A for base model)."

### Walck-Shannon 2021 (2)

Walck-Shannon, E. M., Rowell, S. F., & Frey, R. F. (2021). To What Extent Do Study Habits Relate to Performance? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-05-0091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same regression set measuring consistency; students studied on average 5.082 of the 8 days before exam 2 (SD = 1.810). The article reports no significant relationship and no effect size.

> "the number of days studied in the week leading up to the exam was not a significant predictor of in-term exam 2, the posttest, or the cumulative final (Table 5; see Supplemental Table 7A for base model)."
