Evidence¶

Robabeh Puran 2017¶

Robabeh Puran, Mohamad Hasan Behzadi, Ahmad Shahvarani, Farhad Hosseinzadeh Lotfi. (2017). The Effects of Training and Other Factors on Problem Solving in Students. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 6(3): 448-460. https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2017.3.448

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANOVA (Hypothesis 3) on the 450-student sample: between-groups p = 0.219, non-significant. The article notes most parents were self-employed and suggests they elaborate less on the creativity of their own jobs. Equivalence was not tested.

"The results of table 6 show that the p-value (0.219) was more than 0.05, so the mean of parents' job and students' creati ve thinking are the same and there weren’t any meaningful differences between parents' job and the students' creative thinking."
