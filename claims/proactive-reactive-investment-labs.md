A definitional claim from the report's Section 3 framework. Proactive investment is defined as unprompted changes made to increase accessibility before a course begins; the report defines its counterpart as changes "after being prompted by an individual or a group seeking accommodations" and states neither type eliminates the need for the other.

> "Reactive investment, by contrast, is defined as changes made to increase accessibility after being prompted by an individual or a group seeking accommodations."
