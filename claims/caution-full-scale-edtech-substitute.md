## Evidence

### Robert Fairlie and Prashant Loyalka 2020

Robert Fairlie and Prashant Loyalka. (2020). Schooling and Covid-19: lessons from recent research on EdTech. npj Science of Learning 5:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00072-6

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

This is the article's overall conclusion drawn from its two large-scale randomized experiments (~10,000 primary school students in China and Russia) evaluating EdTech as a substitute for traditional schooling while controlling for time on task. The authors state the trials show "we need to be careful about using EdTech as a full-scale substitute"; no pooled effect size is printed.

> "The findings from the large-scale trials indicate that we need to be careful about using EdTech as a full-scale substitute for the traditional instruction received by schoolchildren."
