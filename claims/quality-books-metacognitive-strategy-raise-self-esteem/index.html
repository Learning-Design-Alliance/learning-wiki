Evidence¶

Erkan Cer & Ertugrul Sahin 2017¶

Erkan Cer & Ertugrul Sahin. (2017). The Effects of Quality Books for Children and the Metacognitive Strategy on Students’ Self-Esteem Levels. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n1p72

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group study of 93 eighth graders in Turkey; experimental group (n=48) received metacognitive strategy instruction with quality books for 10 weeks, control group (n=45) read 100 Classic Works. ANCOVA controlling for pre-test showed post-test means of 35.19 versus 18.42, with the article reporting "The intervention has a large effect size (d=9.67)".

"The one-way ANCOVA results were significant ( F (1, 90)=2130.86, p<.001, d=9.67) indicating that self-esteem scores ( M=35.19, SD=1.63) of the students in the experimental group were significantly higher than those of the students in the control group ( M=18.42, SD=1.89) controlling for their pre-test scores."
