Gider Neslihan Yağmur, Tosun Betül, Dirgar Ezgi (2025). Evaluation of the Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices of Intensive Care Unit Nurses Regarding Eye Care: A Descriptive Study. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70114

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive barrier frequencies from the 207-nurse Turkish survey (Table 3). The article reports "excessive paperwork (25%)" as the top barrier, then low prioritization (24.2%), time constraints (21.4%), staffing (20.7%), and knowledge/skills gaps (8.5%). Percentages are descriptive self-report frequencies, not effect sizes.

> "The most frequently cited barrier to providing adequate eye care was excessive paperwork (25%), followed closely by the perception that eye care is less important than other critical issues (24.2%) and time constraints (21.4%). Additionally, 20.7% of nurses attributed insufficient staffing as a limiting factor, while 8.5% identified a lack of knowledge and skills in eye care as a barrier."
