## Evidence

### Blanchetière Amélie 2025

Blanchetière Amélie, Guillouët Erwan, Favrais Géraldine, Lardennois Caroline, Bellot Anne, Savey Baptiste. (2025). Simulation‐Based Echocardiography Training Enhanced Competence and Self‐Confidence in Neonatal Residents. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.70003

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary endpoint of the multicentre randomised controlled trial: TTE score at 3 months on the simulator with a normal heart scenario, groups compared with a Mann–Whitney U test. The simulation group scored "11.5 ± 2.3 points, while the control group scored 7.4 ± 3.4 points (p < 0.001)".

> "When it came to the reference score, the simulation group scored 11.5 ± 2.3 points, while the control group scored 7.4 ± 3.4 points (p < 0.001)"

### Blanchetière Amélie 2025 (2)

Blanchetière Amélie, Guillouët Erwan, Favrais Géraldine, Lardennois Caroline, Bellot Anne, Savey Baptiste. (2025). Simulation‐Based Echocardiography Training Enhanced Competence and Self‐Confidence in Neonatal Residents. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.70003

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same trial, secondary custom-made 33-point score for recognising anatomical structures at the 3-month evaluation. "The custom‐made scores were 25.8 ± 5.3 points for the simulation group and 16.9 ± 7.8 points for the control group (p < 0.001)".

> "The custom‐made scores were 25.8 ± 5.3 points for the simulation group and 16.9 ± 7.8 points for the control group (p < 0.001)"
