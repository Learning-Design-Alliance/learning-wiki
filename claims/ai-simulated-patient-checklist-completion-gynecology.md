## Evidence

### Gao Lingling 2026

Gao Lingling, Zhang Li, Zhang Yanxin, Wu Min, Xu Jianbo, Lu Dan. (2026). Utilizing generative artificial intelligence to create simulated patient for history taking in gynecology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09219-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 40 fourth-year medical students in a gynecology clerkship, 20 per group, assessed with a 44-item Calgary-Cambridge-based checklist by two independent examiners (κ = 0.83). The trial reports "a significantly higher checklist completion rate" with Cohen's d = 1.03, and missed key items of 3.05 vs 4.85 (d = 1.03).

> "The AI group demonstrated a significantly higher checklist completion rate compared to the control group (93.07 ± 3.93 vs. 88.98 ± 4.05, p = 0.0025, Cohen’s d = 1.03)."
