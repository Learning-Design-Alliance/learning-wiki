## Evidence

### Ali 2025

Ali, F., Wang, Y., Wang, S. J.-W. & Zhu, G. (2025). Triggers of curiosity in social constructivist classroom discourse. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00330-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian DAG network analysis of transcribed discourse from 28 Knowledge Building lessons (1752 second-grade utterances) revealed the chain S:CUR4→S:CUR2→S:CUR, showing that "curiosity in two utterances back strongly inﬂuenced curiosity in the current utterance."

> "We first observed a clear chain of curiosity amongst students (S:CUR4→S:CUR2→S:CUR). The DAG model found that curiosity in two utterances back strongly inﬂuenced curiosity in the current utterance."

### Ali 2025 (2)

Ali, F., Wang, Y., Wang, S. J.-W. & Zhu, G. (2025). Triggers of curiosity in social constructivist classroom discourse. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00330-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A supplementary DAG analysis restricted to curiosity codes, with prior curiosity utterances sub-coded as same- or different-student, found that "curiosity chains persisted in the same student as well as exhibiting a contagion effect" across both grades.

> "For both second and third grade, curiosity chains persisted in the same student as well as exhibiting a contagion effect where curiosity spread to different students (Supplementary Figs. 1 and 2)."
