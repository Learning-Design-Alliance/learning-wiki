### Yang 2026

Yang, L., Liu, H., Li, S., Jia, R., Xiao, Y., Chen, G., & Lu, L. (2026). Evaluating Scaffolding-Oriented Multi-Agent Large Language Model System for Clinical Interview Training. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10939

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Turn-level expert annotation of the released consultation corpus (2,350 student messages) found "approximately 24.1% of student utterances required instructional scaffolding", rising from about 16.1% early to about 34.8% late in the encounter, suggesting learners needed more support during integration, diagnostic reasoning, and management decisions.

> "It increased from approximately 16.1% in the early phase of the consultation to approximately 34.8% in the late phase, suggesting that learners more often required support when the encounter moved from initial information gathering toward integration, diagnostic reasoning, and management decisions"
