## Evidence

### Walker L 2017

Walker L, Warfa A-R M. (2017). Process oriented guided inquiry learning (POGIL®) marginally effects student achievement measures but substantially increases the odds of passing a course. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0186203

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by discipline. The authors note this is consistent with prior findings of lower effect sizes in chemistry, possibly due to "the nature and culture of assessment in this subfield"; heterogeneity was significant in both datasets.

> "The summary effect size for achievement from chemistry studies [n = 15 studies, g = 0.22, 95% CI = - 0.024–0.414, Fig 3A] was significant (z = 2.20, p = 0.028) and roughly 2 times lower than that of other disciplines (n = 11)"
