Kate Gordon 1916¶

Kate Gordon. (1916). A Class Experiment with the Hillegas Scale. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

q2 · i? · causal · r1

In the intercorrelation analysis of forty pairs of judges from the class experiment, Pearson coefficients were computed before and after scale use; "The average correlation is 48 without the scale and .46 with the scale." Ranges printed were .11 to .86 without and .00 to .78 with.
