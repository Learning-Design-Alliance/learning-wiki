## Evidence

### Meng Cao & Paulo F. Carvalho 2026

Meng Cao & Paulo F. Carvalho. (2026). Striking the Balance: How Variability Shapes Retrieval Practice and Worked Examples for Transfer Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10169-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a mixed-design online experiment (2 variability × 2 strategy, strategy within-subjects, 39 participants per variability group after exclusions), tested transfer on 25 novel items. Retrieval practice beat worked examples with a printed effect size of ηp² = .10 ("F(1,76) = 8.08, p = .006, ηp 2 = .10").

> "worked examples, F(1,76) = 8.08, p = .006, ηp 2 = .10 (see Fig. 2). The interaction between variability and learning strategy was not significant, F(1,76) = 0.02, p = .877"
