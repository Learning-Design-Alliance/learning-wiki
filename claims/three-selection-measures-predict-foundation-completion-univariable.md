## Evidence

### Smith Daniel T 2018

Smith Daniel T, Tiffin Paul A. (2018). Evaluating the validity of the selection measures used for the UK’s foundation medical training programme: a national cohort study. BMJ Open 2018;8:e021918. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-021918

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariable logistic regressions in the national UKMED cohort of 14 131 UK applicants starting foundation training in 2013 and 2014, with ARCP outcome 6 as the outcome. The article reports all three selection measures "predict obtaining an outcome 6", with table 2 printing ORs and 95% CIs for each predictor.

> "The results from univariable logistic regressions (table 2) show that all three measures, the SJT z-score (mean=0.10, SD=0.86), EPM (mean=5.62, SD=2.83) and educational achievements (mean=2.47, SD=2.01) predict obtaining an outcome 6."
