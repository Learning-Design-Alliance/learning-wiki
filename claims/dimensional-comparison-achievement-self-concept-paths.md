# Achievement shows positive within-domain and negative cross-domain effects on academic self-concept and value, replicating dimensional comparison theory

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Grades showed strong positive within-domain paths to self-concept (math β = 0.85; German β = 0.75) and negative cross-domain effects on self-concept and value in the other domain. [→ Jansen 2020](#jansen-2020)

## Evidence

### Jansen 2020

Jansen, M., Becker, M., & Neumann, M. (2020). Dimensional Comparison Effects on (Gendered) Educational Choices. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(2), 330-350. https://nbn-resolving.org/urn:nbn:de:0111-pedocs-220459

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

SEM path analysis of the longitudinal sample (N = 519) testing the I/E pattern. The article reports "strong positive within-domain paths from grades to self-concept" with math β = 0.85 and German β = 0.75, plus negative cross-domain effects on self-concept and value for three of four paths.

> "As expected, we found strong positive within-domain paths from grades to self-concept (math: β = 0.85, p < .01; German: β = 0.75, p < .01; see Table 3, Model 1) and value."
