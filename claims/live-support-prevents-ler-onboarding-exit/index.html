q2 · i? · design · r2

Self-reported finding from the two co-design workshops, where participants onboarded with facilitators and vendor representatives present. The report notes participants were "hesitant to adopt the tool or give the application numerous attempts independently" after completing onboarding with tech glitches.

"A significant portion of participants in each workshop admitted they would have exited the onboarding process had they not had live support in the workshop to persist."
