Eugenio Tufino, Stefano Oss, and Micol Alemani. (2024). Integrating Python data analysis in an existing introductory laboratory course. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad4fcc](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad4fcc)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post matched questionnaires (42 matched answers). Pre-survey also found 62% of students had some prior programming experience, 38% of those familiar with Python, and only 7% with extensive JN experience. The authors link positive epistemology to reduced resistance to curricular change.

> "It is remarkable that students' already positive views on the importance of computational skills not only remained consistent but even improved between the pre-survey and the post -survey (see figure 2)."
