### Manal Mohamed Khodary 2017

Manal Mohamed Khodary. (2017). Using the Vocabulary Self-Collection Strategy Plus to Develop University EFL Students' Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p135

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pre-post comparison for the VSSPlus experimental group (n = 40) over the 11-week treatment; the difference was "statistically significant (t = 20.03, p < 0.05)" with an effect size of 0.911, considered large.

> "the mean score of the experimental group on the pre VAT was 23.92 with a standard deviation of 3.40, while its mean score on the post VAT was 37.92 with a standard deviation of 2.38"
