### Brambila-Tapia Aniel Jessica Leticia 2022

Brambila-Tapia Aniel Jessica Leticia, Miranda-Lavastida Aris Judit, Vázquez-Sánchez Nancy Araceli, Franco-López Nancy Lizbeth, Pérez-González Martha Catalina, Nava-Bustos Gonzalo, Gutiérrez-Rodríguez Francisco José, Mora-Moreno Francisco Fabián. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19084804

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis in the cross-sectional sample of 124 students, stratified by sex. The authors call the somatization association an "unexpected finding"; the overall correlation was r = 0.194, and in women both correlations reached significance.

> "As an unexpected finding, we observed a correlation between non-verbal intelligence and somatization (r = 0.194), which was higher and significant in women (r = 0.312), as well as a tendency with the number of diseases (r = 0.165, p = 0.07) that reached significance in women (r = 0.331)"
