## Evidence

### Zohreh Shaghaghian 2022

Zohreh Shaghaghian, Heather Burte, Dezhen Song, and Wei Yan. (2022). An Augmented Reality Application and User Study for Understanding and Learning Spatial Transformation Matrices. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.00110](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.00110)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Screen-recording data from the AR vs non-AR workshops compared play time via Wilcoxon Rank Sum tests. Table 8 reports mean play time of 25.33 minutes (AR) vs 15.99 minutes (non-AR). The authors interpret this as the AR app being more engaging.

> "The result of the non-parametric Wilcoxon Rank Sum tests on students’  play time with the apps showed that students played more with the AR app during the workshops than the non-AR app, with a significant difference (p < 0.001)."
