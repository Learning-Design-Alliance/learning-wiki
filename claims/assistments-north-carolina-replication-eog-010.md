# A North Carolina replication found ASSISTments 7th-grade use raised 8th-grade end-of-grade math scores (effect size +0.10) despite COVID-19 disruption

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a school-level randomized replication across 63 North Carolina schools with nearly 6,000 students, intervention-school students scored significantly higher on the 8th-grade EOG mathematics test one year after implementation (effect size = +0.10). [→ ASSISTments evidence summary 2020](#assistments-evidence-summary-2020)

## Evidence

### ASSISTments evidence summary 2020

ASSISTments evidence summary. (2020). https://www.assistments.org

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

School-level randomized replication in 63 diverse schools; nearly 6,000 students (2961 treatment, 3,030 control), 61% economically disadvantaged. COVID-19 closures interrupted usage and ended EOG test collection in year one, so impacts were measured at the end of 8th grade.

> "Students in the intervention schools assigned to use ASSISTments in 7th grade scored significantly higher than comparison students on North Carolina's 8th grade EOG mathematics test (effect size = +0.10)."
