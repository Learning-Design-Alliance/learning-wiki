Evidence¶

Avia Ben-Ari 2025¶

Avia Ben-Ari, Tess Gavrielle Levinson, Jessica Blake-West, Marina Umaschi Bers, Rinat B. Rosenberg-Kima. (2025). From avoidance to ownership: preschool teachers learn to teach code—a case study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10548-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Pre-post administration of the Coding Stages Assessment (CSA) to the 26 preschool children in the case study, one-on-one, before and after the 24-session curriculum. The article reports "significant growth in children’s coding knowledge" with the printed means and t statistic; no effect size is printed.

"Repeated ANOVA measures showed significant growth in children’s coding knowledge throughout the curriculum (t = 15.25, p < .0001). Prior to the intervention, children’s average CSA score was 2.64 (SD = 1.16), while following the program completion, it rose to 7.38 (SD = 2.30)"
