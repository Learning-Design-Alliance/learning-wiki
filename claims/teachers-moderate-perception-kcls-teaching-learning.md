## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers rated their perceptions of KCLS on teaching and learning processes at a moderate level (mean 3.23, SD 0.10). [→ Karma Wangda 2020](#karma-wangda-2020)

## Evidence

### Karma Wangda 2020

Karma Wangda, Kinzang Dorji, Kinley. (2020). Teachers and Students Perception on the Impact of Kagan Cooperative Learning Structures at Higher Secondary School. ASEAN Journal of Open and Distance Learning, 12(2). https://rcl.sce.rub.edu.bt/ajod/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 36 teachers from six Bhutanese higher secondary schools using 5-point Likert questionnaires adapted from Hinson (2015); descriptive statistics in SPSS showed the "average mean value of 3.23, SD 0.10 for the teachers", interpreted as a Moderate perception level. No effect size or inferential test is reported.

> "Analysis based on theme one on teachers and students' perception of KCLS towards teaching and learning processes particularly drawn from survey questionnaires indicated an average mean value of 3.23, SD 0.10 for the teachers"
