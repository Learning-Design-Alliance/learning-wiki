Ade-Yusuf Oyinlola Rodhiat et al. (2026). From classrooms to practice: does pre-service training for one health professions provide readiness for antimicrobial stewardship? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09843-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Curriculum-mapping analysis of two medicine pre-service curricula (ABU Zaria, AAU) against WHO AMS competencies using a five-point traffic-light scale. The article reports "AMS alignment with WHO AMR competency framework was 42%", with ABU Zaria at 51% and AAU at 33%. No effect size is printed.

> "Overall, AMS alignment with WHO AMR competency framework was 42% (Table 3). Medicine curricula tended to align better with foundational pharmacology (mechanisms, spectrum concepts) and less with system-level components (formulary development; research and regulation) and prescribing behaviours tied to guidelines. By university, ABU Zaria recorded stronger overall alignment (51%) than AAU (33%)"
