## Evidence

### Simpson Paul L 2014

Simpson Paul L, Scicluna Helen A, Jones Philip D, Cole Andrew MD, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter G, Velan Gary, McNeil H Patrick. (2014). Predictive validity of a new integrated selection process for medical school admission. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-86

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression including education group and rurality as predictors across six performance criteria (N = 240–271). Rurality was marginally non-significant for WAM phase 1 (beta -0.132, p = 0.051); education group was non-significant throughout.

> "Although measures of educational background and rurality are commonly unexamined within selection method evaluation studies, this study found no effect of these demographics."
