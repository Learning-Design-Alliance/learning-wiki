### Jacob T. Stanley 2017

Jacob T. Stanley, Weifeng Su, and H. J. Lewandowski (2017). Using lab notebooks to examine students' engagement in modeling in an upper-division electronics lab course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Notebook coding of the voltage divider activity found that despite high scaffolding, students found more than one modeling pathway: "Of the 43 students, 10 of them took this alternative path", starting from the revised model with finite input resistance carried over from the 1 MΩ circuit.
