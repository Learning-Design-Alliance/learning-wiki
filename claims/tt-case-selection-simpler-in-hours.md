## Evidence

### Henderson L 2025

Henderson L, Patwardhan N, Dagash H. (2025). Trainee as trainer in paediatric laparoscopic appendicectomy. Annals of The Royal College of Surgeons of England. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2024.0032

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Histology-based complexity comparison between the 53 TT cases and non-TT cases in the same two-year audit. The review reports "The rate of complex appendicitis was significantly lower in the TT group (p=0.020)".

> "Of the 53 TT cases, 8 (15.1%) were normal appendices, 28 (52.8%) were simple and 17 (32.1%) were complex appendicitis (either gangrenous or perforated), based on histology reports. The rate of complex appendicitis was significantly lower in the TT group (p=0.020)."

### Henderson L 2025 (2)

Henderson L, Patwardhan N, Dagash H. (2025). Trainee as trainer in paediatric laparoscopic appendicectomy. Annals of The Royal College of Surgeons of England. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2024.0032

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Age comparison between TT and non-TT groups in the same retrospective audit. The review reports 34 patients (16.9%) under five in the non-TT group "compared with three patients (5.6%) in the TT group (p=0.047)".

> "In the non-TT group, there were 34 patients (16.9%) under five years of age, compared with three patients (5.6%) in the TT group (p=0.047)."

### Henderson L 2025 (3)

Henderson L, Patwardhan N, Dagash H. (2025). Trainee as trainer in paediatric laparoscopic appendicectomy. Annals of The Royal College of Surgeons of England. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2024.0032

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Timing analysis of the 53 TT cases versus non-TT cases in the audit. TT cases overwhelmingly occurred "in normal working hours (8am–5pm)" and Monday to Friday, both differences significant at p<0.001.

> "Of the 53 TT cases, 47 (88.7%) took place in normal working hours (8am–5pm), and 6 (11.3%) were out of hours (p<0.001). A total of 50 (94.3%) cases were performed Monday to Friday, and 3 (5.7%) at the weekend (p<0.001)."
