### Xue Gang 2025

Xue Gang, Chen Siting, Xie JiaBing, Yang Min, Wang Zhengyu. (2025). Application of problem-based learning combined with three-dimensional visualization reconstruction technology in trauma orthopedics teaching and its impact on teaching satisfaction.
