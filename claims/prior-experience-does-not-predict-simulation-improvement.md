Cross-sectional analysis of the 10-delegate 2019 cohort, splitting delegates by number of neurosurgical cases observed/assisted. The article reports "no statistically significant difference" in absolute mean improvement across the three case-number groups, implying benefit regardless of prior theatre exposure.
