## Evidence

### H. Sabbagh 2025

H. Sabbagh, T. Ribnishki, L. Hötzel, L. von Bronk, Y. Khazaei, and A. Wichelhaus. (2025). An Open‐Source, AI‐Supported Teaching Tool in Orthodontic Education—Assessment of Acceptance and Effectiveness. Journal of Dental Education 89(12): 1753–1764. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13916

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison across three consecutive semesters (S1 control, N = 47; S2, N = 62; S3, N = 41) at one institution; the intervention groups had 2 weeks of MCAT access plus a 2-h training session. Boxplots (Figure 4) showed "a higher interquartile range compared to the intervention groups" for the control. No effect size is printed for the variability difference.

> "The control group (S1) exhibited a higher interquartile range compared to the intervention groups (S2 and S3)."
