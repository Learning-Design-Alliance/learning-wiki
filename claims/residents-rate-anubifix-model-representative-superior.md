## Evidence

### Slieker Juliette C. 2012

Slieker Juliette C., Theeuwes Hilco P., van Rooijen Göran L., Lange Johan F., Kleinrensink Gert-Jan. (2012). Training in laparoscopic colorectal surgery: a new educational model using specially embalmed human anatomical specimen. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-012-2158-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course questionnaire study: eleven surgical residents with box-trainer and virtual reality experience but no laparoscopic colectomies completed an eight-item five-point Likert questionnaire; "Data are presented as mean ± standard deviation". Only descriptive ratings were reported, with no inferential statistics or effect sizes.

> "The results of the questionnaire, given in Fig. 6, show that the majority of residents believed that (1) the quality of the tissue is representative of the real situation, (2) the model is superior to animal models or virtual reality simulators, and (3) the model helps improve knowledge of three-dimensional anatomy and laparoscopic skills."
