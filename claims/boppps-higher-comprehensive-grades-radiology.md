## Evidence

### Cheng Wei-Qin 2025

Cheng Wei-Qin, He Ling, Cai Jin-Hua, Fan Xiao. (2025). Hybrid BOPPPS model in radiology education: a case study of pediatric undergraduates. BMC Medical Education, 25. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07279-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective comparative cohort study of 418 undergraduates (205 BOPPPS, 213 LBL). Mann-Whitney U tests on non-normal score distributions showed the BOPPPS group higher on all three measures, e.g. comprehensive scores of 86.60 (82.95, 89.80) vs 85.00 (79.90, 88.85). The article calls the gains "statistically significant yet modest improvements".

> "Compared to the LBL group, the hybrid BOPPPS group demonstrated statistically significant yet modest improvements in formative assessment (median difference: + 1.25 points), final exam (+ 2.0 points) and comprehensive grades (+ 1.6 points) (all p < 0.05)."
