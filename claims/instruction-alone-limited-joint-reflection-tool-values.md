## Evidence

### Carolyn P. Rosé 2019

Carolyn P. Rosé, Nancy Law, Ulrike Cress, Sten Ludvigsen. (2019). Highlighting tools and technologies for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. (2019) 14:1–6. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09297-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The editorial describes the Eshuis et al. study of vocational students across three conditions: instruction only about RIDE, instruction plus a joint reflection tool, or neither. It reports "the limited utility of training by itself" and highlights the synergistic effect of implicit and explicit support combined.

> "The surprising finding is the limited utility of training by itself. The current study contributes to a line of research supporting the value of joint reflection in fostering development of collaborative skills during collaborative encounters"
