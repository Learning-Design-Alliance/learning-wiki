## Evidence

### Lee Young Hwan 2018

Lee Young Hwan, Kim Kyong-Jee. (2018). Enhancement of student perceptions of learner-centeredness and community of inquiry in flipped classrooms. BMC Medical Education, 18, 242. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1347-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Within-group paired t-tests from Table 3 (high performers n = 26, low performers n = 29). Low performers' teaching-presence mean rose from 3.29 (.67) to 3.76 (.47); high performers' from 3.51 (.57) to 3.69 (.66), not significant. No effect sizes printed.

> "No significant change in the sense of teaching presence was observed among higher performing students (t = 1.01, p = 0.32), but it increased significantly among lower performing students (t = 4.79, p < 0.001)."
