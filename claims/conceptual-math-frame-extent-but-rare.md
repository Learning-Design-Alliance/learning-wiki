## Evidence

### Deepa N. Chari 2019

Deepa N. Chari, Hai D. Nguyen, Dean A. Zollman, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2019). Student and instructor framing in upper-division physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5120392](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5120392)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Quantitative coding of all 50 episodes shows students entered CM only 6 times (average 70 seconds each) and the instructor 4 times (average 30 seconds each). The authors count this rarity "as a success for theory-driven research" since prior literature commonly reserved conceptual thinking for physics alone, but their theory predicts a conceptual math frame distinct from an algorithmic one.

> "Both students and instructors spend the smallest fraction of their time in the conceptual math (CM) frame ( 2%), meaning that it is extant, but rare."
