## Evidence

### Lehmann Ronny 2019

Lehmann Ronny, Seitz Anke, Meyburg Jochen, Hoppe Bettina, Hoffmann Georg Friedrich, Tönshoff Burkhard, Huwendiek Sören. (2019). Pediatric in-hospital emergencies: real life experiences, previous training and the need for training among physicians and nurses. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-019-4051-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-factor ANOVA with between-subject factor profession and qualification level on 5-point Likert self-ratings from 128 survey respondents; LSD post hoc tests with Bonferroni corrections were used. Both professions rated themselves as having moderate knowledge and skills.

> "Agreements to a certain level of knowledge and skills differed significantly concerning pediatric emergencies, see Fig. 2 (knowledge F(3,123) = 6.647, p < 0.001; skills F(3,123) = 5.832, p = 0.001)."
