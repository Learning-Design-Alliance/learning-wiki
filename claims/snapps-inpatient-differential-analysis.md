## Evidence

### Jain Vishakha 2019

Jain Vishakha, Rao Siddharth, Jinadani Mariya. (2019). Effectiveness of SNAPPS for improving clinical reasoning in postgraduates: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 19: 224. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1670-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Open-label randomized controlled trial with case presentations as the unit of analysis. SNAPPS residents "discussed approx twice more basic attributes in support of the diagnosis in their differential diagnosis" (2.04 vs. 1.07, p value < 0.01).

> "the students in the SNAPPS group on an average discussed approx twice more basic attributes in support of the diagnosis in their differential diagnosis as compared to the students in the control group (2.04 vs. 1.07, p value < 0.01)."

### Jain Vishakha 2019 (2)

Jain Vishakha, Rao Siddharth, Jinadani Mariya. (2019). Effectiveness of SNAPPS for improving clinical reasoning in postgraduates: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 19: 224. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1670-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same trial, same unit of analysis. SNAPPS students "justified their diagnostic possibilities twice more than the control group (median 2 vs.0, p value < 0.01)" using the case summary, literature, or previous experience.

> "Students in the SNAPPS group justified their diagnostic possibilities twice more than the control group (median 2 vs.0, p value < 0.01) by providing supporting evidence from the case summary, literature, or their previous experience."
