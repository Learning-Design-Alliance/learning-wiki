de la Torre 2023¶

de la Torre, M. (2023). Exploring Chicago Connected: Participation patterns. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/exploring-chicago-connected

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive participation rates by eligibility index score among eligible students, shown in Figure 2. The report notes the eligibility index was still related, with statistical significance, to participation even when taking into account other student, neighborhood, and school characteristics.

"36% of students with an index of 6 or higher participated in 2020–21, vs. 23% if the eligibility index was 4 or 5, and 12% among eligible students with an index of less than 3."
