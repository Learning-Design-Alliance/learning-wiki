Jun J 2014¶

Jun J, Bridges SM. (2014). Educational Technologies in Problem-Based Learning in Health Sciences Education: A Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3240

q2 · i? · review · r3

Across the 28 reviewed studies, the review identifies implementation failures attributable to inadequate support and infrastructure. The conclusions state that "insufficient technical support, infrastructure, and resources were seen as impacting negatively on uptake and learning outcomes."
