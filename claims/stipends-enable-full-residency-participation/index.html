Evidence¶

Wojcikiewicz 2025¶

Wojcikiewicz, S. K., Ralph, K., Snyder, J., Bland, J. A., & Wechsler, M. E. (2025). Teacher residencies in Texas: Supporting successful implementation. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/630.675

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative interview study of residents, mentor teachers, site coordinators, and program leaders at three EPP–district partnerships, plus document analysis. The report finds stipends supported engagement "by allowing them to forego outside employment"; no effect size is printed.

"Stipends facilitated residents’ engagement in their clinical placements and concurrent EPP courses and requirements by allowing them to forego outside employment while they completed their residencies"
