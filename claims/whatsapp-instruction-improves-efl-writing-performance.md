# WhatsApp-based instruction improved Jordanian eleventh-graders' EFL writing performance on all four writing aspects after an eight-week treatment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Writing performance developed significantly on all writing aspects (content and ideas, organization and mechanics, vocabulary, language use) following the WhatsApp-based treatment. [→ Bataineh et al. 2017](#bataineh-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Bataineh et al. 2017

Ruba Fahmi Bataineh, Raghda Fayez Al-Hamad and Dina Abdulhameed Al-Jamal. (2017). GENDER AND EFL WRITING: DOES WHATSAPP MAKE A DIFFERENCE? Teaching English with Technology, 18(2), 21-33. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1177754

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of two intact eleventh-grade sections taught through a WhatsApp-based treatment for eight weeks; post-test scores were compared with pre-test scores. The authors report that "writing seems to have developed significantly on all writing aspects." No effect size is printed.

> "Even though male and female participants scored significantly differently across the four aspects of the test, writing seems to have developed significantly on all writing aspects."
