## Evidence

### Snyder Julia J. 2015

Snyder Julia J., Wiles Jason R. (2015). Peer Led Team Learning in Introductory Biology: Effects on Peer Leader Critical Thinking Skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115084

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Open-ended questionnaire administered with the CCTST post-test to the 37 peer leaders. "only 43% of the peer leaders actually improved" despite 62% perceiving improvement; the authors suggest leaders may have defined critical thinking in terms of communication, collaboration, or content knowledge rather than CCTST skills.

> "62% of the peer leader participants thought they improved their critical thinking skills over the course of the Spring 2011 semester, while only 43% of the peer leaders actually improved their skills from the pre-test to the post-test."
