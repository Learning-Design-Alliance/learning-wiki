Evidence¶

Midgett 2016¶

Midgett, A., Doumas, D., Trull, R., & Johnston, A. (2016). A Randomized Controlled Study Evaluating a Brief, Bystander Bullying Intervention with Junior High School Students. Boise State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1146195

q3 · i? · causal · r2

GLM repeated measures ANOVA using the BASC-3 SRP-A Depression scale (sample α = .88) in the randomized controlled study of junior high students. No statistically significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested.

"results indicated the interaction effect Time x Group was not significant for depression, Wilks' Lambda = .99, F(1, 51) = 0.34, p = .56, η 2 p = .01."
