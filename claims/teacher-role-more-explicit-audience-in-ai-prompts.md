# Pre-service elementary teachers explicitly specify the target audience in AI prompts more often when acting as teachers than as students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In prompts to AI about representations, 17 of 20 participants specified the target audience in the teacher role versus 11 in the student role. [→ Razan Hamed 2025](#razan-hamed-2025)

## Evidence

### Razan Hamed 2025

Razan Hamed, Amogh Sirnoorkar, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Dual-Role Dynamics in Prompting: Elementary Pre-service Teachers' AI Prompting Strategies for Representational Choices. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.hamed](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.hamed)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Emergent coding of prompts from 20 pre-service elementary teachers in two lab sessions (teacher role: wave velocity; student role: Ideal Gas Law) found "17 participants specified the target audience in the teacher role, only 11 participants did so in student role"; eight specified it only as teachers, two only as students.

> "While 17 participants specified the target audience in the teacher role, only 11 participants did so in student role."
