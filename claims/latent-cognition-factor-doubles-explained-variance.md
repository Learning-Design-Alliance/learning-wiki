## Evidence

### Corley E 2023

Corley E, Fahey L, Fitzgerald J, Holleran L, Walton E, Morris DW, Donohoe G. (2023). The impact of early adversity and education on genetic and brain morphological predictors of cognitive ability. Genes, Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1111/gbb.12850

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison within the same SEM framework in the UK Biobank sample: the latent factor built from four tests (reasoning, processing speed, working memory, executive function) explained 14% of variance versus 7% for the VNR test alone.

> "the percentage of variance explained by IQ‐PGS and total GMV on the latent variable of cognitive ability was 14%. By comparison, the percentage of variance explained in the model when the latent variable of cognitive ability was replaced with the VNR test alone was 7%."
