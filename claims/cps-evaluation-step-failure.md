# Students succeeded in early CPS steps but largely failed the final self-evaluation step

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` More than 80% of students successfully completed the focusing and description steps and up to 70% the planning and execution steps. [→ Carlo Maria Carbonaro 2020](#carlo-maria-carbonaro-2020)
`q2 i?` Percentages were reversed in the evaluation step: most groups did not understand the request to evaluate their results and at most answered yes or no. [→ Carlo Maria Carbonaro 2020 (2)](#carlo-maria-carbonaro-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Carlo Maria Carbonaro 2020

Carlo Maria Carbonaro, Alessia Zurru, Viviana Fanti, Matteo Tuveri, Gianluca Usai. (2020). Cooperative Problem Solving: an experience of high-school teaching updating. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ex-post ranking analysis of the student elaborates, scoring each step of the solution scheme on a 0-1 rank grouped as insufficient, sufficient and good. "more than 80% of them were able to successfully complete the first two steps".

> "As reported in Fig. 7 students experimented increasing difficulties in the different steps of the solution scheme but more than 80% of them were able to successfully complete the first two steps (focusing and description) and up to 70% the plan and execution steps."

### Carlo Maria Carbonaro 2020 (2)

Carlo Maria Carbonaro, Alessia Zurru, Viviana Fanti, Matteo Tuveri, Gianluca Usai. (2020). Cooperative Problem Solving: an experience of high-school teaching updating. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.07731)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same ranking analysis of student elaborates, focusing on the fifth (evaluation) step of the scheme. "The most part of the groups did not understand the request of evaluation" and gave easy yes or no answers despite being advised to examine results rationally.

> "The percentages are totally reversed in the last step (evaluation) where the students should evaluate their results and give reasons if they found them reasonable or not. The most part of the groups did not understand the request of evaluation and at most gave the easy answer as yes or no"
