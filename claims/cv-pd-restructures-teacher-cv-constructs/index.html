Evidence¶

Kurz 2026¶

Kurz, T. L., Jayasuriya, S., Swisher, K., Mativo, J., Pidaparti, R., & Robinson, D. T. (2026). Computer vision versus human vision: analyzing middle school teachers' construct restructuring following computer vision professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10594-2

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre/post repertory grid study (no comparison group) of 12 in-service middle school teachers (six in Arizona, six in Georgia) using hierarchical cluster analysis (Ward's Method) of construct ratings. The results section reports that computer vision constructs shifted clusters far more often than human vision constructs, with "72% (n = 13) of the constructs shifted clusters" for computer vision versus 6% (n = 1) for human vision. The counts are of the 18 constructs in the repertory grid, not of teachers.

"Regarding human vision, only 6% (n = 1) of the constructs shifted clusters; regarding computer vision, 72% (n = 13) of the constructs shifted clusters."
