Evidence¶

Kardaş 2016¶

Kardaş, D. (2016). The use of personalized texts for teaching Turkish as a second language. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2538

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest comparison of the two groups (12 students each) using the Turkish test form; the article reports means of 70.8 (control) and 76.0 (experimental) with the t-test above, concluding students "were distributed homogenously". The test was prepared in English and Arabic for students with no prior Turkish.

"No difference was found out between students belonging to experimental and control groups before any application was made [t (24) = -1.423, p>0.01]."
