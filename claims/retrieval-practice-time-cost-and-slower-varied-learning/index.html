Evidence¶

Meng Cao & Paulo F. Carvalho 2026¶

Meng Cao & Paulo F. Carvalho. (2026). Striking the Balance: How Variability Shapes Retrieval Practice and Worked Examples for Transfer Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10169-w

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Supplementary learning-time analyses in Experiment 1 (with a similar pattern in Experiment 2) showed retrieval practice's time cost grew under varied items; the article reports no printed time statistics in the main text.

"Retrieval practice required more learning time than studying worked examples, and this time difference was larger when practice items were varied than when they were repeated"

Meng Cao & Paulo F. Carvalho 2026 (2)¶

Meng Cao & Paulo F. Carvalho. (2026). Striking the Balance: How Variability Shapes Retrieval Practice and Worked Examples for Transfer Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10169-w

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Trial-level accuracy analyses within the retrieval practice condition of Experiment 1 showed slower improvement with varied items; the authors say this aligns with research suggesting variability increases a task's cognitive demands.

"participants improved more slowly when practicing varied items than when practicing repeated items"
