Evidence¶

Yousefi 2021¶

Yousefi, M., & Nassaji, H. (2021). Corrective Feedback in Second Language Pragmatics: A Review of Research. Teaching English as a Second Language Electronic Journal (TESL-EJ), 25(1). https://tesl-ej.org/pdf/ej97/a6.pdf

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Nipaspong and Chinokul's (2010) three-group study (explicit feedback, prompt, control) on refusals, using parallel pretest/posttest and interview protocols over 10 weeks; the prompts group won on pragmatic awareness. No effect size is printed.

"results from a pragmatic awareness multiple-choice test and qualitative data revealed a significant increase in pragmatic awareness, especially with regard to unconventional refusal expressions, for the prompts group over the explicit feedback and the control groups."
