# A standardized clinical examination shifts emergency physicians' fracture probability ratings toward the eventual radiological diagnosis in maxillofacial injury patients

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the standardized clinical examination, median fracture probability ratings moved toward the radiological diagnosis in both midfacial fracture patients (6 to 7) and midfacial non-fracture patients (3 to 2), each p < 0.001. [→ van der Zaag P 2025](#van-der-zaag-p-2025)
`q2 i?` For mandibular fracture patients, the median fracture probability rating increased from 7 to 9 after the clinical examination (p < 0.001). [→ van der Zaag P 2025 (2)](#van-der-zaag-p-2025-2)
