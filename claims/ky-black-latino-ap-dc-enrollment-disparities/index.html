Kristen Hengtgen 2023¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of state report card enrollment data disaggregated by race/ethnicity for 2018-19 and 2021-22. The report finds "Black and Latino students much less likely to take both AP and DC than their White peers."

"And disparities persist, with Black and Latino students much less likely to take both AP and DC than their White peers."
