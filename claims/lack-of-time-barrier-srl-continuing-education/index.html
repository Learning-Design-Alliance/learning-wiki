Evidence¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler 2024¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2024). Self-regulated learning strategies in continuing education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100629

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Synthesis of included primary studies in the review's results on learning process-related factors. The review reports that "Lack of time available for learning was identified as a barrier" to cognitive, metacognitive, and peer learning strategies, attributing this to the cited primary studies.

"Lack of time available for learning was identified as a barrier to learners' use of cognitive (Hosseini et al., 2020; Rabin et al., 2020), metacognitive (Hosseini et al., 2020; Milligan & Littlejohn, 2016; Rabin et al., 2020), and peer learning (Milligan & Littlejohn, 2016) strategies."
