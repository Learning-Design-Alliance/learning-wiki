Julian Rudisch, Luis K. H. Holzhauer, Karmen Kravanja, Fred H. Hamker & Claudia Voelcker-Rehage. (2024). A systematic review of observational practice for adaptation of reaching movements. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00271-5

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review narrative of four mixed-schedule studies (Larssen et al., Lim et al., Larssen et al., Ong et al.). Lim et al. showed a mixed group had larger direct effects than pure physical practice in retention; Larssen et al. showed largest direct effects in an interspersed mixed group. No effect sizes printed here.

> "While Ong, et al. 53 have shown no differences between two groups that received either physical or observational practice as well as a third group, receiving a mixed schedule, all other three studies have indeed shown differential effects of mixed schedules."
