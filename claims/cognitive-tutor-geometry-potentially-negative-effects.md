## Evidence

### Cognitive Tutor® 2016

Cognitive Tutor®. (2016). WWC Intervention Report: Cognitive Tutor®, Secondary Mathematics. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.carnegielearning.com

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

WWC review of one randomized study in eight high schools in Baltimore County, with 669 students in 60 classrooms (329 intervention, 340 comparison) over three school years. The review confirmed "a statistically significant negative difference," rated potentially negative effects with small extent of evidence; the study met standards with reservations due to high attrition.

> "Pane et al. (2010) reported, and the WWC confirmed, a statistically significant negative difference between the Cognitive Tutor® Geometry group and the comparison group on the Baltimore County Public School District geometry assessment."
