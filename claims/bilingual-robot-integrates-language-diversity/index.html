Evidence¶

Yanghee Kim 2021¶

Yanghee Kim, Sherry Marx, Hung Viet Pham, Tung Nguyen. (2021). Designing for robot-mediated interaction among culturally and linguistically diverse children. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10051-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative findings describe a recent immigrant child fluent in Spanish who showed he had a lot to say in his first robot session and later disclosed his difficult experience with schooling; the authors report the opportunity to engage in Spanish was a chance for him to be "a fully integrated member of the classroom community."
