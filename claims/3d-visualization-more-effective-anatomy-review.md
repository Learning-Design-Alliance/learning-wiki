# Across 21 reviewed studies, 3D visualization methods were significantly more effective than traditional methods for learning anatomy in twelve studies, while nine found no significant advantage

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a systematic review of 21 comparison studies, twelve found 3D visualization significantly more effective than traditional methods and nine did not. [→ Triepels CPR 2019](#triepels-cpr-2019)

## Evidence

### Triepels CPR 2019

Triepels CPR, Smeets CFA, Notten KJB, Kruitwagen RFPM, Futterer JJ, Vergeldt TFM, Van Kuijk SMJ. (2019). Does three‐dimensional anatomy improve student understanding? Three‐Dimensional Methods for Learning Anatomy. Clinical Anatomy 32:25–33. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.23405

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review (PRISMA) of articles published 2002 to mid-2017 comparing digital 3D anatomy visualizations with traditional methods on test results or student feedback; the review reports "Twelve articles found that three‐dimensional visualization is a significantly more effective learning method". No pooled effect size was computed.

> "Twelve articles found that three‐dimensional visualization is a significantly more effective learning method compared to traditional methods, whereas nine articles did not find that three‐dimensional visualization was a significantly more effective method."
