## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Upper-division student difficulties with the Dirac delta function. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.010108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.010108)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interviews probing the units of the constant in a delta-function charge density. "9 of 11 participants" argued the delta function is unitless; four had earlier articulated a correct physical argument, and only two independently convinced themselves the delta function has units.

> "When prompted to comment on units, 9 of 11 participants explicitly argued (incorrectly) that the δ-function is unitless and thus, regardless of the geome- try of the charge distribution, the units of the constant must be C/m3."
