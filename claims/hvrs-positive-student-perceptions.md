## Evidence

### Şimşek Emine 2026

Şimşek Emine, Satar Selimcan. (2026). The effect of haptic virtual reality simulation training before clinical endodontic practice on student performance and perception. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09577-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 10-item 5-point Likert questionnaire developed by the authors and administered only to the 15 experimental-group students after training and clinical application showed generally positive views, with Cronbach's alpha of 0.98. Only 40% agreed HVRS helped them understand anatomical difficulties.

> "the majority of participants indicated that they would like HVRS to be used not only for access cavity preparation but also for other endodontic procedures such as canal shaping, irrigation, and obturation (strongly agree, 46.7%), and stated that this training should be incorporated into the endodontics curriculum before clinical procedures (strongly agree, 46.7%)"
