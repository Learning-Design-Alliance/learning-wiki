Evidence¶

Lorenz Weise 2022¶

Lorenz Weise, Saskia D. Forster, Siegfried Gauggel. (2022). Reverse-correlation reveals internal error-corrections during information-seeking. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:321–335. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09286-4

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Control analysis reducing the dataset to a third, with condition as a between-participant factor. The article reports the three-way interaction again significant, F(2, 24) = 5.28, effect size generalized eta squared (ɳG2) = 0.1, confirming the reversed low-confidence info-seeking weights, though only the positive non-selected weight reached significance.

"The three way interaction between condition, selection and confidence was again significant ( F(2, 24) = 5.28, p = 0.013, ɳG2 = 0.1)."
