## Evidence

### Ruiz-Martín Héctor 2024

Ruiz-Martín Héctor, Blanco Fernando, Ferrero Marta. (2024). Which learning techniques supported by cognitive research do students use at secondary school? Prevalence and associations with students’ beliefs and achievement. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-024-00567-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis among study-technique scales (n = 2,414, corrected P < 0.001). The article reports the elaborative–non-elaborative association at “r = 0.495” and the highlighting-copying by processing correlation at r = 0.503.

> "Remarkably, the association between elaborative and non-elaborative techniques was quite relevant (r = 0.495, P-value < 0.001). When the subscales were compared, they all yielded significant correlations (P-value < 0.001) with Pearson’s coefficients between 0.137 and 0.503. The higher correlation was between highlighting-copying and information-processing techniques, such as summarization or conceptual mapping (r = 0.503)."
