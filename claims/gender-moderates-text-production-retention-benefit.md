## Evidence

### Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani 2018

Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani. (2018). On the Efficiency of Text Production in Vocabulary Learning: An Empirical Study on Iranian GFL-Learners. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n3p176

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA (2 with/without text production × 2 gender) on the women's group (CG n = 14, EC n = 14); the article reports "F (1, 26) = 8.37, p = .008" with η2 = .24 for the 24-hour retention of female participants.

> "a significant influence could be observed on the medium-term retention performance of female participants ( F (1, 26) = 8.37, p = .008 ( p ≤ 0.01), η2 = .24). This is the only significant difference in the women’s group."

### Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani 2018 (2)

Nader Haghani & Samira Kiani. (2018). On the Efficiency of Text Production in Vocabulary Learning: An Empirical Study on Iranian GFL-Learners. Journal of Education and Learning, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v7n3p176

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA on the male group (CG n = 11, EC n = 14) found no statistically significant effects at any interval; the article reports "F (1, 23) = 1.56, p = .224" for short-term and F (1, 23) = 1.08, p = .310 for medium-term retention.

> "The F-value for short-term retention is not statistically significant (F (1, 23) = 1.56, p = .224). This also applies to the F-value for medium-term retention (F (1, 23) = 1.08, p = .310)"
