`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Chuang et al., a cheating-detection system using time delay and head pose estimation with the CLM-GAVAM model that "achieved an accuracy of 75.6%". Other reported figures include 87% for the Attentive system and 99.31% for NGDNet on the Pointing'04 dataset under yaw angle variation.

> "To decrease the complexity of the data, they introduced FCBF and achieved an accuracy of 75.6%."
