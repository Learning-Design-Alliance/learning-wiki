Learning Policy Institute & Turnaround for Children 2021¶

Learning Policy Institute & Turnaround for Children. (2021). Design principles for schools: Putting the science of learning and development into action. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis section of the playbook ("Why Positive Developmental Relationships Are Important") asserts, citing the science of learning and development, that relationships are "the active ingredient in any effective child-serving system". No effect sizes are printed; this is the report's research synthesis, not a new experiment.

"Positive relationships enable children and adolescents to manage stress, ignite their brains, and fuel the connections that support the development of the complex skills and competencies necessary for learning success and engagement."
