Shihui Feng 2025¶

Shihui Feng, Ziyi Wei. (2025). The CoMPAS Framework for Modeling Individual Participation in Collaborative Learning Processes: a Systematic Review. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09450-x

The review's RQ1 synthesis of 66 studies found "A total of nine articles provided a clear theoretical basis" (Table 2), otherwise studies employed a data-driven approach. The review attributes this pattern to the field and argues it highlights the need for a unified framework; no effect size applies.

"A total of nine articles provided a clear theoretical basis for understanding individual participation in collaborative learning, with seven studies drawing on regulation of learning theory and two studies on Community of Inquiry (CoI) theory"
