Preliminary analyses of the longitudinal sample (59.7% women) examined gender differences in goal affordances, endorsements, and incongruity (Supplemental Table 1). The article reports no significant differences; no effect sizes are printed and equivalence was not tested.

> "Moreover, there were no significant gender differences in communal/agentic affordances and endorsements, suggesting that men and women did not endorse different goals or perceive the opportunities afforded by STEM roles dissimilarly"
