Evidence¶

Moedjito 2017¶

Moedjito. (2017). Improving EFL Learners' Pronunciation of English through Quiz-Demonstration-Practice-Revision (QDPR). English Language Teaching, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n1p10

q1 · i? · causal · r1

The article reports this attribution to Moedjito and Ito (2008) and illustrates it with Sasak-speaking participants confusing /f/, /v/ and /p/ (e.g., saying /paɪp/ for five) and treating /iː/ and /ɪ/ as allophones of one L1 phoneme. No new data on this is collected in the present study.

"two main reasons for these common and serious mispronunciations, namely (1) the absence of English phoneme in EFL learners' mother tongue, and (2) different categorization of the same sounds in English and EFL learners' mother tongue"
