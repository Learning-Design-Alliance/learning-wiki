Thematic synthesis of qualitative extracts. Teachers "sensitised typically developing learners to maintain low levels of noise", created "safety zones" for learners with autism, established clear routines, introduced "disability issues" in the curriculum, and gave children with DD leadership roles to foster peers' acceptance and self-esteem.

> "Teachers adopted a whole-class approach when promoting an inclusive environment, by organizing time and space and fostering peer inclusion."
