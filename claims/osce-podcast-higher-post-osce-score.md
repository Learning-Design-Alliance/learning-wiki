# Fourth-year medical students assigned to an OSCE preparation podcast scored significantly higher on post-intervention nephrology OSCE stations than controls

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After a four-week podcast intervention, the podcast group's cumulated post-intervention OSCE score on the AI platform was significantly higher than the control group's (p = 0.002). [→ Dupont V 2025](#dupont-v-2025)

## Evidence

### Dupont V 2025

Dupont V, Guerrot D, Bouazzi L, Figueres L, Pers Y-M, Guenou E, Barbe C, Yauy K. (2025). Evaluating podcasts as a tool for OSCE training: a randomized trial using generative AI-powered simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06675-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 50 fourth-year medical students randomized 1:1 to podcast or control; post-intervention OSCE scores were assessed on three novel stations of the DocSimulator generative AI platform. The trial reports the podcast group scored "27.6 ± 3.6 vs. 23.6 ± 5.0; p = 0.002".

> "The cumulated note after the intervention was significantly higher in the podcast group compared to controls (27.6 ± 3.6 vs. 23.6 ± 5.0; p = 0.002)."
