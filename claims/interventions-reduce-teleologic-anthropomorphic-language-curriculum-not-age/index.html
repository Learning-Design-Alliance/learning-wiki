Evidence¶

Bautista 2015¶

Bautista, R. G. (2015). Students' affordance of teleologic explanations and anthropomorphic language in eliciting concepts in physics. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 5(1), 31-40.

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA of between-subjects effects of classroom pedagogical interventions on 48 students' affordance of teleologic and anthropomorphic language; the corrected model was significant (F = 17.305, p = .000) with adjusted R Squared = .675.

"It was found out that there is a signifcant diference on the students’ afordance of teleologic and anthropomorphic languages afer various classroom pedagogical interventons had been introduced: F-value of 9.407 and a p-value of .004, at .05 level of signifcance."

Bautista 2015 (2)¶

Bautista, R. G. (2015). Students' affordance of teleologic explanations and anthropomorphic language in eliciting concepts in physics. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 5(1), 31-40.

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA grouping factor tests in the same study: age F = .395, p = .676 (not significant); curriculum F = 5.087, p = .030 (significant). The author attributes this to students exposed to English eliciting better ideas over counterparts exposed to Arabic instruction.

"The interactons of the classroom pedagogical interventons to the respondents’ afordance of teleologic and anthropomorphic languages were also analyzed when the respondents were grouped according to their age and curricular undertakings: F-values of .395 and 5.087, and p-values of .676 and 0.030, at .05 level of signifcance, respectvely."
