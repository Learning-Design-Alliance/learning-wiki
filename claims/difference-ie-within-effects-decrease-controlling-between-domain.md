Revisiting the Role of Social and Dimensional Comparisons in the Internal/External Frame of Reference Model: A Systematic Comparison of the Classic and the Difference I/E Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10212-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Reanalysis of PISA 2000 total sample (N = 55,582) comparing changes in within-domain effects across the two I/E model specifications. The authors report "a mean increase in the within-domain effects in Model 2 of ∆B = .059 (∆β = .073), whereas the mean decrease in the within-domain effects in Model 3 was only ∆ B = –.026 (∆β = –.033)."
