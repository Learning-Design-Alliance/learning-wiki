Zachery Quince and Emily Faulconer 2026¶

Zachery Quince and Emily Faulconer. (2026). Student GenAI use under changing institutional policies: a mixed-methods case study in undergraduate physics. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00651-w

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Descriptive analysis of declared tool use in the case study cohort (31, 31, and 36 submissions for A1–A3); average reported tools per assessment were 1.33, 1.27, and 1.23. Other tools were used by fewer than 10% of students.

"The results show that ChatGPT was the most used tool across all three assessments (A1 n = 16, A2 n = 13, A3 n = 21). Copilot was the next most frequently used tool, although its uptake was notably lower (A1 n = 5, A2 n = 5, A3 n = 5)."
