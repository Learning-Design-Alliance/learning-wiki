### Cahill Helen 2015

Cahill Helen, Coffey Julia, Sanci Lena. (2015). ‘I wouldn’t get that feedback from anywhere else’: learning partnerships and the use of high school students as simulated patients to enhance medical students’ communication skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0315-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Pre-workshop focus groups with 38 school students aged 15–16 asked which issue was most personal among sexual health, mental health, or drugs and alcohol; one student said "it’s more something you’d tell friends…you wouldn’t trust them enough". Qualitative, no effect sizes.

> "Most of the school students said they thought mental health was the ‘most personal’ issue and that it would be more difficult to talk about than sexual health [29]."
