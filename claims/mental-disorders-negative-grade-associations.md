## Evidence

### Bortes Cristian 2022

Bortes Cristian, Nilsson Karina, Strandh Mattias. (2022). Associations between children’s diagnosed mental disorders and educational achievements in Sweden. Scandinavian Journal of Public Health, 50(8), 1140–1147. https://doi.org/10.1177/14034948221089056

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear regression models on register data comparing exposed and unexposed individuals on standardised grade sums. Table I reports, for example, a female eating-disorder grade difference of "0.19 (0.16 to 0.23)" and negative differences for the other disorders; no standardised effect size summary is printed for the overall pattern.

> "The results revealed negative associations between all the examined mental disorders and educational achievements, except for positive associations between eating disorders and grades among female students."
