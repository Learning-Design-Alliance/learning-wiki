### Jasmine Singh 2022

Using Machine Learning to Predict Engineering Technology Students' Success with Computer Aided Design. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1002/cae.22489](https://doi.org/10.1002/cae.22489)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Preliminary test shown in Figure 3 comparing actual and predicted final net energies for 11 randomly selected students (20% of 55 students) from the 128-student cohort. The article reports the linear model "is not capable of making accurate predictions based solely on tallied designed actions."

> "The figure demonstrates that a linear regression model is not capable of making accurate predictions based solely on tallied designed actions, which is not surprising since the model is attempting to determine the precise final net energy values for each student."
