Aldawood Fatima Dawood, Shamas Nour, Alshamrani Majid. (2026). Designing an antimicrobial stewardship curriculum for nurses to strengthen knowledge and clinical practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09314-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A mandatory electronic course evaluation survey administered at the end of the course assessed participants' overall satisfaction, meeting of objectives, educational materials and teaching strategies, logistics, and recommendations. The article reports that "Most nurses expressed satisfaction with the course content and its relevance to their everyday practice." No effect size or statistical test is reported.

> "Most nurses expressed satisfaction with the course content and its relevance to their everyday practice. However, some nurses found some lectures difficult, including "basic microbiology" and "basic pharmacology and antibiotic classes.""
