## Evidence

### Kunpalin Yada 2025

Kunpalin Yada, Kik Charlotte C., Lebouthillier Francis, Abbasi Nimrah, Ryan Greg, Spoor Jochem, Looi Thomas, Kulkarni Abhaya V., Van Mieghem Tim. (2025). Fetoscopic Robotic Open Spina Bifida Treatment (FROST): A Preclinical Feasibility and Learning Curve Study. BJOG: An International Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.18161

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Structured questionnaire administered to a panel of six MFM specialists, three paediatric neurosurgeons and one paediatric plastic surgeon with extensive OSB surgery experience, rated on a 5-point Likert scale. Overall realism scored "4.20 ± 0.79"; content-validity recommendation scores were "4.70 ± 0.48". All subcategories exceeded 3.5 except skin undermining (3.10 ± 1.10), attributed to lack of tissue resistance and bleeding.

> "Considering face validity, the participants considered our simulator realistic as the mean of the overall realism was 4.20 ± 0.79."
