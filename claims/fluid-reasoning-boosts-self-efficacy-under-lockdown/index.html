Evidence¶

Martin Andrew J. 2023¶

Martin Andrew J., Ginns Paul, Collie Rebecca J. (2023). University students in COVID-19 lockdown: The role of adaptability and fluid reasoning in supporting their academic motivation and engagement. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101712

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Auxiliary moderation test in the SEM of 500 students, assessing lockdown status × fluid reasoning interaction terms; detailed results are in Supplementary Materials. The authors interpret it as a booster effect consistent with JD-R theory.

"moderation tests revealed a significant lockdown x fluid reasoning interaction effect for self-efficacy such that fluid reasoning yielded a significant positive role for the self-efficacy of students who were in lockdown."
