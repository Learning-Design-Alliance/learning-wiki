## Evidence

### Modrau B 2025

Modrau B, Kirk KF, Said SMA, Bjarkam CR, Sunde L, Bodilsen J, Dal J, Kristensen JK, Emmersen J, Astorp MB, Andersen S. (2025). Pass/Fail Versus Tiered Grades and Academic Performance in Undergraduate Medical Education: Crossover Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/74975

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the fourth-year exams that alternated grading format before the 2018 reform: "1.8% (9/497)" Pass/Fail versus "0.8% (4/491)" Tiered, χ2 P=.20; the same directional trend as the main comparison but not significant.

> "In the subgroup of 988 examinations alternating between Tiered grade and Pass/Fail (upper left box in Figure 1), the frequency of students failing exams was 1.8% (9/497) with Pass/Fail and 0.8% (4/491) with Tiered grade exams (χ2 test, P=.20)."
