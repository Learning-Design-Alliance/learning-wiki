## Evidence

### Xu Bingjie 2024

Xu Bingjie. (2024). Mediating role of academic self-efficacy and academic emotions in the relationship between teacher support and academic achievement. Scientific Reports 14, 24705. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-75768-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 721 valid responses from university students in Nanjing; a structural equation model with teacher support as predictor and GPA-based achievement as outcome. The direct effect path carried a standardized effect size of 0.816 with a bootstrap 95% CI of "[0.776, 0.854]" excluding zero.
