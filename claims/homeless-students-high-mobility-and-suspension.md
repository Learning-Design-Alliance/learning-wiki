# Students experiencing homelessness change schools at triple the rate of other students and are twice as likely to receive an out-of-school suspension

## Evidence

### Burns 2021

Burns, D., Espinoza, D., Ondrasek, N., & Yang, M. (2021). Students experiencing homelessness: The conditions and outcomes of homelessness among California students. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/students-experiencing-homelessness

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Analysis of 2015–16 California Department of Education data for grades 3–8 and 11 examines school mobility. The report finds mobility "triple the rate of other students", with approximately 11% of African American students experiencing homelessness changing schools two or more times, the highest rate among racial/ethnic groups. No effect size is printed.

> "One in 5 students experiencing homelessness in the tested grades changed schools at least once during the school year—triple the rate of other students—and a quarter of these changed schools twice or more."

### Burns 2021 (2)

Burns, D., Espinoza, D., Ondrasek, N., & Yang, M. (2021). Students experiencing homelessness: The conditions and outcomes of homelessness among California students. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/students-experiencing-homelessness

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same 2015–16 administrative data analysis examines exclusionary discipline. The report states students experiencing homelessness "were also twice as likely as all students to receive an out-of-school suspension", with the executive summary noting rates especially high among students of color. No effect size is printed.

> "Students experiencing homelessness in the tested grades were also twice as likely as all students to receive an out-of-school suspension."
