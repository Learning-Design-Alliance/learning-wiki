## Evidence

### Yamamoto R 2026

Yamamoto R, Nojiri T, Yoshida D, Oyamada Y, Fukutoku A, Kon K. (2026). Accuracy of dynamic computer-assisted implant surgery among novice operators using three different patient registration methods: an in-vitro study. International Journal of Implant Dentistry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40729-026-00666-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Implant-level analysis (n = 25 per group) of vertical apex deviation in the in vitro study; means were 0.60 mm (XC), 0.51 mm (XM), and 0.62 mm (ND), with "no significant differences among groups". Equivalence was not tested; only non-significance reported.

> "The Kruskal–Wallis test indicated no significant differences among groups (H = 1.76, p = 0.42), and this result remained non-significant after Bonferroni correction (adjusted p = 1.00)."
