# Self-reported confidence and preparedness rose significantly from pre-test to post-test among newly appointed ENT ST3 registrars after a two-day ENT bootcamp, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ENT ST3 registrars self-reported a statistically significant increase in both confidence and preparedness after completing a two-day bootcamp. [→ Rai Amar 2024](#rai-amar-2024)

## Evidence

### Rai Amar 2024

Rai Amar, Shukla Shivani, Mehtani Nikita, Acharya Vikas, Tolley Neil. (2024). Does a junior doctor focused 'Bootcamp' improve the confidence and preparedness of newly appointed ENT registrars to perform their job roles? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05691-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of paired questionnaires from 29 UK ENT ST3 trainees who completed a two-day bootcamp, scored on 10-point Likert scales and tested with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Median confidence rose from 5.0 to 8.0 and preparedness from 6.0 to 9.0, both "p < 0.001". No effect size is printed.

> "Participants self-reported a significant increase in confidence (p < 0.001) and preparedness (p < 0.001) following the bootcamp course."
