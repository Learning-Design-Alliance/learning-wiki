Dejan Vinković, Dunja Potočnik. (2010). Educational asymmetries in the making: Science Fair Competitions as Proxies of the Economic Development. Sociologija i prostor, 48 (2010) 186 (1): 51–75. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1007.2443](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1007.2443)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis comparing county-level Science Fair Participation Index (SFPI) with two development indexes (RCI and SEI) for competitions in 2001–2009. The article reports that as is revealed, there is a growing trend of interconnection between development index and participation rates.

> "As is revealed, there is a growing trend of interconnection of the development index and participation rates at science competitions in all disciplines."
