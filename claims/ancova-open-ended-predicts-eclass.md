## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Open-ended versus guided laboratory activities: Impact on students' beliefs about experimental physics. arXiv:1608.01621. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

5-way ANCOVA on N = 4759 matched responses with major and gender data, controlling for preinstruction score, course level, major, and gender; "Type of activities (open-ended vs. guided only) accounted for the largest diﬀerence in adjusted postinstruction means", with all four categorical variables significant predictors (F-test, p < 0.01).
