## Evidence

### Lawn Sharon 2017

Lawn Sharon, Zhi Xiaojuan, Morello Andrea. (2017). An integrative review of e-learning in the delivery of self-management support training for health professionals. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1022-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's concluding recommendation, based on synthesis of the ten studies. The discussion reports that for some chronic conditions, some aspects of learning still require face-to-face delivery to demonstrate and practice SMS skills.

> "Blended learning that combines e-learning and face-to-face methods is suggested to support SMS skills development for health professionals."

### Lawn Sharon 2017 (2)

Lawn Sharon, Zhi Xiaojuan, Morello Andrea. (2017). An integrative review of e-learning in the delivery of self-management support training for health professionals. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1022-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Newton et al.'s study of 13 GP supervisors, 13 medical educators and 40 GP registrars, that a hybrid online-plus-face-to-face approach was preferred for motivational interviewing training.

> "Newton et al... found that a hybrid approach was preferred, using a combination of online and face-to-face learning for motivational interviewing – the former providing conceptual knowledge of the required skills and the latter providing the opportunity to practice these skills"
