### Sierksma 2023

Sierksma, J. (2023). Children perpetuate competence-based inequality when they help peers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00192-9

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 niceness measure (5-point scale): no difference between targets (d = 0.11, p = .34), and niceness ratings did not interact with competency in predicting help type (p = .70 and p = .11). Study 3 replicated the null (d = −0.10, p = .52).

> "Children thought that competent and incompetent targets were equally nice (respectively, M = 4.00, SD = 0.99, M = 3.88, SD = 1.12), t(79)= 0.96, p = .34, d = 0.11, 95% CI = [−0.11, 0.33]."
