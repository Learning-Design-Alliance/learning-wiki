`q3 i1` In the 286-participant effectiveness trial conducted by an independent research group with school-employed coaches, effect sizes ranged from 0.21 (small) to 0.67 (medium), with all differences significant. [→ Andy J. Frey 2023](#andy-j-frey-2023)

## Evidence

### Andy J. Frey 2023

Andy J. Frey, Jason W. Small, Hill M. Walker, Brandon Mitchell, John R. Seeley, Edward G. Feil, Jon Lee, and Steven R. Forness. (2023). First Step Next: A Best-Evidence Synthesis of Replication Randomized Controlled Trials From 2009 to 2021. Remedial and Special Education, 44(1), 60-72. https://doi.org/10.1177/07419325211068145

`q3 · i1` · `review · r2`

Four-year national effectiveness trial across 48 elementary schools in five U.S. states, with 24 schools randomized to each condition and 286 teacher-student-parent triads; coaches were school employees. The review reports a small ES of 0.21 on parent-reported SSiS-PB and a medium ES of 0.67 on teacher-reported SSiS-SS, with all differences significant.

> "ESs across prosocial, PB, and academic engagement domains ranged from 0.21 (small; improvement index = +8.4) on the parent-reported SSiS-PB subscale to 0.67 (medium; improvement index = +24.7) on the teacher-reported SSiS-SS subscale."
