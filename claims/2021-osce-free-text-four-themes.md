## Evidence

### O'Malley A 2025

O'Malley A, Fitzgerald N, Moylett E, Gaffney G, McCarthy G, Murphy A.W., Geoghegan R, Hallahan B. (2025). A comparison of objective structured clinical examinations (OSCEs) for medical students, modified during the COVID-19 pandemic. Irish Journal of Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-025-03976-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Consensus open-coding of free-text questionnaire responses from the 2021 OSCE cohort by two researchers. Unlike the 2020 feedback, there was no written complaint about COVID-19 restrictions, and 2020 respondents had preferred more OSCE stations, in contrast to 2021.

> "In total, four themes emerged relating to the 2021 OSCE: (1) experienced as too long in duration (n = 9), (2) a lack of examination feedback (n = 9), (3) a preference for examiners to provide more support to students during the OSCE (n = 6), and (4) that the OSCE was well organised (n = 3)"
