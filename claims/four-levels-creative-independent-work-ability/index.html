Evidence¶

Shangaraeva et al. 2016¶

Liya F. Shangaraeva, Alfiya A. Yarkhamova, Zubayda A. Biktagirova, Dorice Agol. (2016). The Formation of Students' Creative Independence at the English Language Classes. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(6), 1267-1274. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1114326

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Definitional/descriptive statement of the assessment rubric: the first level is characterized through "ignorance of the theory, inability to analyze, inability to put the theory into practice"; the second lacks theory knowledge but applies the first type of analysis in tasks of medium difficulty; the third knows the theory and works with insignificant teacher help on difficult questions; the fourth is independent problem posing and solving.

"the Fourth level of formation abilities of creative independent work assumes that students put a problem and solve it."
