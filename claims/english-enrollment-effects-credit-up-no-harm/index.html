Evidence¶

Elaine Allensworth 2008¶

Elaine Allensworth, Takako Nomi, Nicholas Montgomery, & Valerie E. Lee. (2008). College Preparatory Curriculum for All: Consequences of Ninth-Grade Course Taking in Algebra and English on Academic Outcomes in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://ccsr.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

RQ2 enrollment-effect models for ninth-grade English outcomes; course absence was very slightly less (about a third of a day a year, p<.05) for the two lower ability groups.

"Students at all ability levels were much more likely to earn English I credit (p<.01), and their failure rates were unaffected."

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Same RQ2 models; the authors note the English test contained literary material more typical of English I, but about half the questions were based on short and simple passages.

"Neither English GPA nor reading test scores were affected."
