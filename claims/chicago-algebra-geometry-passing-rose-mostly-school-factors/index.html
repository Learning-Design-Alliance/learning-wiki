q2 · i? · associational · r3

Two-year cohort trend analysis of math course-taking among high school eligible Chicago students, adjusted for student characteristics, covering the period after the sequence became a graduation requirement in 1997–98. The report attributes "the bulk of the improvement (82.2 percent)" to changes other than incoming student composition.

"For the 1998–00 cohort, 50 percent of students passed the sequence, compared with 37 in 1993–95. This suggests that changes in incoming students account for only 17.8 percent of improvement, while other changes, possibly curricular reforms in the high schools, account for the bulk of the improvement (82.2 percent)."
