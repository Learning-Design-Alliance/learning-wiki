## Evidence

### Rhodes Katherine T. 2017

Rhodes Katherine T., Branum-Martin Lee, Washington Julie A., Fuchs Lynn S. (2017). Measuring arithmetic: A psychometric approach to understanding formatting effects and domain specificity. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000189

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the Encoding Complex full measurement model of the N=1314 confirmatory factor analysis, the three Arabic numeral, small-problem-size outcomes retained high loadings on the seemingly modular encoding complex factor. The article describes this as evidence that something beyond executive attention and language predicts these outcomes.

> "Three outcomes in particular (Basic Facts Addition, Basic Facts Subtraction, and Computational Fluency, all of which were formatted with Arabic numerals and involved relatively small problem sizes) had high encoding complex factor loadings despite the addition of executive attention as a predictor."
