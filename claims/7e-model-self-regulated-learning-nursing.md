## Evidence

### Cui Liping 2024

Cui Liping, Dong Yuting, Zhang Shan, Shi Xiaoxia. (2024). The value of the ‘7E’ instructional model in the teaching of nursing students in nursing clinical probation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06157-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison on the self-learning ability tool developed by Zhang Xi Yan et al. (30 entries, 5-point Likert, Cronbach's α 0.822) after intervention; total score t = 2.332, P = 0.020 per Table 3.

> "After the intervention, the experimental group had greater learning motivation (30.57 ± 5.24 vs. 29.41 ± 4.77), learning cooperation ability (20.35 ± 4.41 vs. 19.02 ± 4.25), information literacy (23.14 ± 4.36 vs. 21.12 ± 3.95) and total scores (114.00 ± 19.72 vs. 109.07 ± 18.61) than the control group, with a statistically significant difference (p < 0.05)."
