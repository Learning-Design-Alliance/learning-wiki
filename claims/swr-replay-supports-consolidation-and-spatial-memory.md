## Evidence

### Drew B. Headley and Denis Paré 2017

Drew B. Headley and Denis Paré. (2017). Common oscillatory mechanisms across multiple memory systems. npj Science of Learning 2:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-016-0001-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that SWS SWRs occur at cortical upstate onset and reactivate prior experience patterns, with cortical and striatal replay events partly initiated by SWRs. The role is characterized as supported by multiple converging lines of evidence.

> "SWRs tend to occur at the onset of upstates, 27,33,34 and are thought to play an important role in memory consolidation by reactivating hippocampal and cortical activity patterns that occurred during past experiences."

### Drew B. Headley and Denis Paré 2017 (2)

Drew B. Headley and Denis Paré. (2017). Common oscillatory mechanisms across multiple memory systems. npj Science of Learning 2:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-016-0001-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review cites rodent experiments in which "Disrupting these online SWRs impairs performance on a task requiring spatial working memory", providing interventional support for awake-SWR replay's behavioral role during consummatory pauses.

> "Disrupting these online SWRs impairs performance on a task requiring spatial working memory (Fig. 2).39"
