## Evidence

### Şükrü Gönen E 2025

Şükrü Gönen E, Taşkın Egici M, Gönen E. (2025). Knowledge and Awareness of Family Physicians as Key Stakeholders Regarding Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip and the Nationwide Screening Program. Acta Orthop Traumatol Turc. https://doi.org/10.5152/j.aott.2025.24034

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional face-to-face survey of 401 Istanbul family physicians. The overall score was "66.59 ± 8.4%" with significant positive correlations including specialty training (r = 0.156) and in-service/online education (r = 0.167).

> "Overall knowledge and awareness score was 66.59 ± 8.4% and positively correlated with following patients with DDH, reporting to have attended congress and symposium, choose specialty training as a source of information, receive a lot of education and information about DDH, and reported to have screened numerous babies in their unit in the last year (P = .042; P = .008; P = .022; r = 0.156 and P = .002; r = 0.167 and P < .001, respectively)."
