Höbaus Clemens, Schmidmaier Ralf. (2025). Individual and group reflection in lecture-based large groups lead to comparable learning success. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-025-02610-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`
