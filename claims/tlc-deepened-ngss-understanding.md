`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-TLC teacher survey data reported in the TLC Early Implementers' Reaction section: 84, 80, and 81 percent respectively for DCIs, SEPs, and CCCs, with 78 percent for 3D approach understanding. Self-reported perceptions; no effect size printed.

> "Specifically, over 80 percent of teachers felt their TLC experiences deepened their understanding of the disciplinary core ideas (DCIs), how to use science and engineering practices (SEPs) to teach science and engineering, and how to use the crosscutting concepts (CCCs) to teach science and engineering (84 percent, 80 percent, and 81 percent, respectively)."
