# Students report feeling less safe and more likely to perceive violence and disorder in schools with metal detectors

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schildkraut 2019

Schildkraut, J., & Grogan, K. (2019). Are metal detectors effective at making schools safer? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/are-metal-detectors-effective-at-making-schools-safer

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The brief's synthesis of prior studies (Cornell 2015; Gastic 2011; Perumean-Chaney & Sutton 2013) reports that students "report feeling less safe in schools where metal detectors are present."

> "a number of researchers have found that students report feeling less safe in schools where metal detectors are present, compared to students in schools without such devices"

### Schildkraut 2019 (2)

Schildkraut, J., & Grogan, K. (2019). Are metal detectors effective at making schools safer? San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/are-metal-detectors-effective-at-making-schools-safer

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The brief reports, citing Mayer and Leone (1999), that "students are more likely to perceive violence and disorder" when metal detectors are used, an unintended negative consequence for the learning environment.

> "Similarly, students are more likely to perceive violence and disorder at their schools when such devices are used"
