## Evidence

### Wei 2025

Wei, Y., Peng, X., Zhong, Y., Pi, F., Zhai, Y., & Bao, L. (2025). Can Contextualized Physics Problems Enhance Student Motivation? Uncovering a Student-Teacher Perception Gap through a Large-Scale Survey. [doi:10.1103/2g1b-hmhq](https://doi.org/10.1103/2g1b-hmhq)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional analysis of student motivation-and-interest scores plotted against years of physics learning across grades 8–11 (Figure 4). The article reports "a slightly positive attitude toward CPP" only for 8th graders, with a plateau after 9th grade; no effect size is printed.

> "students who have studied physics for only 0.3 years (8th grade) tend to have a slightly positive attitude toward CPP. However, for 9th graders with over a year of physics learning experience, CPP is perceived as having no effect on promoting motivation or interest"
