## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Recency discrimination accuracy was significantly better when participants stayed within a mission boundary than when they crossed it (p = 0.04). [→ Yue Li 2025](#yue-li-2025)

## Evidence

### Yue Li 2025

Yue Li, Mikael Johansson & Andrey R. Nikolaev. (2025). Hierarchical event segmentation of episodic memory in virtual reality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00321-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GLMM analysis (binomial logistic) of recency discrimination accuracy after a VR encoding session in which 26 adults acted as salespeople delivering objects to customers. Mean accuracy was 68.4% (SD = 10.2%); the model "indicat[ed] better memory when participants were within the mission than when they crossed the mission boundary".

> "We found a signiﬁcant main effect of crossing the mission boundary ( p = 0.04), indicating better memory when participants were within the mission than when they crossed the mission boundary."
