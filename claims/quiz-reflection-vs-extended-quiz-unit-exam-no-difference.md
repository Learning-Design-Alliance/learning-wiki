## Evidence

### Clinton 2018

Virginia Clinton. (2018). Reflections versus Extended Quizzes: Which is Better for Student Learning and Self-Regulation? Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v18i1.22508

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 218 introductory psychology students in two courses, one completing weekly 5-item quizzes plus reflections (n = 114) and one completing weekly 10-item quizzes (n = 104). The ANOVA on summed unit exam scores found "There was no difference between conditions, F(1, 217) = 1.07, p = .30".

> "a one-way ANOVA was conducted with condition as the independent variable and the number of items answered correctly on the three unit exams as the dependent variable. There was no difference between conditions, F(1, 217) = 1.07, p = .30"
