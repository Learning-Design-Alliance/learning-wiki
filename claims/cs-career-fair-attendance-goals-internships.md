Survey of 86 valid responses from students attending the Fall 2024 career fair at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa; question #4 asked the primary goal for attending. The survey found "45 participants (52.33%) indicated that they were looking for internship opportunities" and 25 (29.07%) sought full-time jobs.
