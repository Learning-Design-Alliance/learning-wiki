The review reports from Kim and Yang's comparison of Yes/No versus Percentage rating methods within the Modified Angoff that "the ICC ranging from 0.48 to 0.65" for percentage ratings versus a maximum ICC of 0.41 for Yes/No, making the percentage method preferable.

> "However, using percentage ratings in the Modified Angoff method produced higher reliability, with the ICC ranging from 0.48 to 0.65, whereas the ‘Yes/No’ option in the Angoff method had a maximum ICC of 0.41 [34]."
