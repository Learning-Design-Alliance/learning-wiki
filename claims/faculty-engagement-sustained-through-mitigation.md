James T. Laverty, Stuart H. Tessmer, Melanie M. Cooper, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2014). Engaging Physics Faculty in Course Transformation. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.033](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.033)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' retrospective account of their own implementation in the MSU Physics and Astronomy department, reporting that challenges "were mitigated by the facilita- tor, the faculty, or both" so that faculty engagement was maintained. This is the authors' interpretation of their case, not a tested result.

> "Throughout this work, we have encountered a number of challenges that could have disengaged faculty from the process. These challenges were mitigated by the facilita- tor, the faculty, or both and, thus, we have kept faculty engaged."
