Qualitative component of the mixed-methods study: semi-structured interviews with 18 purposively sampled students, analysed with Graneheim and Lundman's conventional content analysis until saturation. Students reported the courses "helped them take responsibility for their learning" and enhanced self-regulation and reflection.

> "The qualitative results were classified into a theme “a learner as a director in the learning process”. The categories included “self-regulation in learning pathway” and “dynamic formation of applied knowledge”."
