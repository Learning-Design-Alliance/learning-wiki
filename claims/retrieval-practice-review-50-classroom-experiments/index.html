Evidence¶

Dillon H. Murphy 2023¶

Dillon H. Murphy, Jeri L. Little, Elizabeth L. Bjork. (2023). The Value of Using Tests in Education as Tools for Learning—Not Just for Assessment. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09808-3

q3 · i? · theoretical · r3

The commentary cites Agarwal et al.'s (2021) systematic review of 50 classroom experiments, reporting that "retrieval practice yields medium to large benefits in most cases (57%)," with positive impacts across education levels, content areas, designs, test delays, and formats.

"a recent review of 50 classroom experiments by Agarwal et al. (2021) demonstrated that retrieval practice yields medium to large benefits in most cases (57%)"

The commentary notes the same review found "only a small fraction of experiments (6%) were conducted in non–Western, educated, industrialized, rich, and democratic (non-WEIRD) countries," so whether benefits accrue across more diverse contexts remains undetermined.
