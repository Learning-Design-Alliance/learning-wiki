## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2025

Meagan Sundstrom, Justin Gambrell, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Eric Brewe. (2025). Evaluating recognition and recall formats of social network surveys in physics education research. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sundstrom](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sundstrom)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of 65 students who completed both a recognition and a recall survey in one introductory calculus-based mechanics course; the authors state they did not conduct statistical tests because response rates were low, calling it "preliminary results". 33 students reported more peers on recognition, 11 more on recall, 21 the same.

> "Students, on average, reported interacting with 2.7 (stan- dard deviation, SD=2.3) and 1.6 (SD=1.6) peers on the recog- nition and recall surveys, respectively (Fig. 1)."
