## Evidence

### Jamal Mohammad H 2014

Jamal Mohammad H, Wong Stephanie, Whalen Thomas V. (2014). Effects of the reduction of surgical residents' work hours and implications for surgical residency programs: a narrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-S1-S14

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of 10 included studies assessing operating room experience before and after duty hour restrictions, reported in its Results section: "4 reported negative or mixed outcomes and 6 reported neutral outcomes".

> "Of the 10 studies examining effects on operating room experience, 4 reported negative or mixed outcomes and 6 reported neutral outcomes, although non-compliance was demonstrated in 2 of these studies."

### Jamal Mohammad H 2014 (2)

Jamal Mohammad H, Wong Stephanie, Whalen Thomas V. (2014). Effects of the reduction of surgical residents' work hours and implications for surgical residency programs: a narrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-S1-S14

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Hope and colleagues, a survey of residents and program directors showing case coverage dropped from "85% of cases" before restrictions to "only 60% of cases" after.

> "Hope and colleagues [9] surveyed residents and program directors and found that, before the work hour restrictions, 85% of cases were covered by residents, in contrast with only 60% of cases after the work hour restrictions."
