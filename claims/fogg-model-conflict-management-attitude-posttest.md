## Evidence

### Mohseni Fatemeh 2024

Mohseni Fatemeh, Mohammadi Aeen, Khabaz Mafinejad Mahboobeh, Khajavirad Nasim, Basiri Kamal, Gruppen Larry D. (2024). Teaching conflict management to medical students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06514-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison from the RCT of 96 second-year medical students at TUMS (93 completing), analyzed with independent t-test; means were 51.78 (4.23) intervention vs 48.51 (6.06) control on total attitude, "significantly higher in the intervention group compared to the control group". No effect size was printed.

> "The mean total attitude score, as well as the mean scores for subscales 2 and 3 in the post-test, were significantly higher in the intervention group compared to the control group (P = 0.003, P = 0.001, and P = 0.001, respectively)."
