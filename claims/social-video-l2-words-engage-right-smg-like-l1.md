## Evidence

### Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong 2020

Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong. (2020). The social brain of language: grounding second language learning in social interaction. npj Science of Learning 5:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0068-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review reports an fMRI study by Jeong et al. in which Japanese speakers learned Korean words under two conditions: L1 translation or videos of joint activities in real-life situations. Retrieval of socially learned words showed "more activation in the right SMG". The review interprets this as socially learned L2 words being processed similarly to L1 words.

> "the words learned through videos with social interactions produced more activation in the right SMG whereas the words learned from translation produced more activity in the left middle frontal gyrus (MFG)"
