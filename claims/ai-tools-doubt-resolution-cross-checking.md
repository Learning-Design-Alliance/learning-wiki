### Raj Gupta 2026

Sakshm AI: Advancing AI-Assisted Coding Education for Engineering Students in India Through Socratic Tutoring and Comprehensive Feedback. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1145/3788679](https://doi.org/10.1145/3788679)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of the 25 semi-structured interviews on doubt-resolution practices. Participants reported using ChatGPT "to resolve doubts" and valued instant, concise responses; no effect size is reported.

> "Twenty out of twenty-five participants mentioned using AI-based tools, particularly ChatGPT, to resolve doubts. Participants appreciated the instant and concise responses provided by AI, especially for debugging, code suggestions, and explaining complex logic."

### Raj Gupta 2026 (2)

Raj Gupta, Harshita Goyal, Dhruv Kumar, Apurv mehra, Sanchit Sharma, Kashish Mittal, and Jagat Sesh Challa. (2026). Sakshm AI: Advancing AI-Assisted Coding Education for Engineering Students in India Through Socratic Tutoring and Comprehensive Feedback. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1145/3788679](https://doi.org/10.1145/3788679)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interview participant P8's account within the thematic analysis of doubt-resolution resources. Four participants still valued human support from professors, peers, and lab instructors for personalized, context-specific explanations.

> "ChatGPT helps with straightforward answers but sometimes provides wrong ones, so I cross-check with verified platforms."
