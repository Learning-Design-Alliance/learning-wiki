Evidence¶

Hassan Soleimani & Fatemeh Nabizadeh 2012¶

Hassan Soleimani & Fatemeh Nabizadeh. (2012). The Effect of Learner Constructed, Fill in the Map Concept Map Technique, and Summarizing Strategy on Iranian Pre-university Students' Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 5(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n9p78

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post hoc multiple comparison of posttest scores among three intact groups of 30 intermediate female pre-university students after a five-session treatment. The article reports the fill in the map CM group "performed significantly better than the summarizing group" with MD=1.93 and Sig.=.007; no effect size is printed.

"only in the case of the comparison of group two and three, the difference was significant ( MD=1.93; Sig.=.007). In other words, the fill in the map CM group performed significantly better than the summarizing group."

Related Claims¶

  • All three strategies (learner-constructed CM, fill in the map CM, and summarizing) produced significant pre-to-post gains in reading comprehension — related
  • Differences between the learner-constructed CM group and the other two groups were not statistically significant — related
  • ANOVA showed a significant difference in reading comprehension posttest performance among the three strategy groups — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Summary writing produced no significant comprehension improvement and the most disputational talk, and did not significantly differ from completing a map in learning outcomes — related
  • Scheffe post-hoc comparisons ranked concept mapping highest, followed by cooperative learning, then conventional teaching, with all pairwise differences significant — related
