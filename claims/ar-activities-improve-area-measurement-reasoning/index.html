Eunhye Flavin 2025¶

Eunhye Flavin, Moon Chung, Sunghwa Flavin, Matthew T. Flavin. (2025). Augmented reality for area measurement reasoning of elementary students. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10502-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Exploratory multiple-case study of three elementary students in a 7-week program; accuracy and trajectory-phase coding of paper-and-pencil tests showed all students correctly solved basic and composite shape problems after AR Activities 3 and 4, reaching "developmental phase 8 (array structurer)".

"In Test 3, which took place after AR Activities 3 and 4, all students were able to correctly solve basic and composite shape problems."
