## Evidence

### Sharma 2016

Sharma, Y. (2016). Alleviating mathematics anxiety of elementary school students: A situated perspective. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 2(2), 509-517. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1110268

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The interaction term of the 2×2 ANCOVA (n=99) testing whether the situated learning effect depended on students' effortful control level was non-significant: "F (1, 94) = .454, p>.05". No effect size was printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "From Table 2, it can be seen that F (1, 94) = .454, p>.05 is not significant. It indicates that mathematics anxiety is found to be independent of the interaction between treatment and effortful control when pre -mathematics anxiety is taken as a covariate."
