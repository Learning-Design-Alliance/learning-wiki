## Evidence

### Bozavlı 2017

Bozavlı, E. (2017). Vocabulary Teaching in Foreign Language via Audiovisual Method Technique of Listening and Following Writing Scripts. International Education Studies, 10(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n5p129

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experimental study with two randomly established groups of 14 at the Department of French Language Teaching, Ataturk University. Descriptive percentage analysis of test results showed "the experimental group attained a success rate between 56.66% and 86.66% in week one", while the control group's most successful participant attained only 50% and the least successful 26.66%. No inferential statistics were reported.
