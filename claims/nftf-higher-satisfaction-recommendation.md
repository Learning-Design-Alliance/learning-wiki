## Evidence

### Lin Li and Shek Daniel T. L 2021

Lin Li and Shek Daniel T. L. (2021). Serving Children and Adolescents in Need during the COVID-19 Pandemic: Evaluation of Service-Learning Subjects with and without Face-to-Face Interaction. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18042114

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subjective outcome evaluation with 345 students (125 FTF, 220 nFTF). ANOVA results show "students who used the nFTF mode were more satisfied with the subject mode" and "more willing to recommend this subject to their friends than those who used the FTF mode." The printed ηp2 values (0.01 and 0.02) fall below the threshold for a small effect.

> "The results showed students who used the nFTF mode were more satisfied with the subject mode (F(1342) = 3.87, p < 0.05, ηp2 = 0.01) and were more willing to recommend this subject to their friends than those who used the FTF mode (F(1342) = 8.18, p < 0.01, ηp2 = 0.02)."
