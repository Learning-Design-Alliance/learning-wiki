### Bautista 2026

Bautista, G. Jr., Cacuyong, R., Lavicza, Z., Sabitzer, B., & Emara, M. (2026). Function Art: Understanding the Artistic Transformation of Mathematical Functions through Learning Analytics. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9037

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post hoc pairwise comparisons using adjusted standardized residuals (>1.96, p < .05) following the significant chi-square test for grade level. Results were visualized using heat maps (Figure 4).

> "the Repetitivist group included a significantly higher proportion of lower-grade students—specifically Grade s 8 and 9 —compared to the Simplist and Multifunctionist groups (adjusted residuals > 1.96, p <.05). In contrast, the Multifunctionist group showed a significantly greater representation of upper-grade students—namely Grades 10 and 11—relative to the other two groups."
