## Evidence

### niromand Elham 2024

niromand Elham, Mansoory Meysam Siyah, Ramezani Ghobad, Khazaei Mohammad Rasool. (2024). Design, implementation and evaluation of e-learning program for common diseases to smartphone-based medical students: at a developing university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-05023-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the evaluation phase, 50 internship students were divided into two groups of 25 each (traditional vs mobile application). Exam scores came from 60 questions drawn from previous pre-internship exams across three topics; the mobile group's averages were higher and "statistically significant across all three topics" (Table 1 prints p values of 0.008, 0.012 and 0.01).

> "The results revealed that the average scores of students who received traditional teaching were lower than those who received education through the mobile application. This discrepancy in averages was statistically significant across all three topics"
