# Faster word recognition at 24 months is associated with larger vocabularies and greater vocabulary gains in Spanish-learning children

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hurtado Nereyda 2008

Hurtado Nereyda, Marchman Virginia A., Fernald Anne. (2008). Does input influence uptake? Links between maternal talk, processing speed and vocabulary size in Spanish-learning children. Developmental Science. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2898277/

`q2 · i?`

In the same longitudinal sample, children's RT was measured with the looking-while-listening procedure at 24 months and vocabulary via parent report. The article reports "those with faster RTs knew more words at 24 months (M = 270, SD = 153) than did those with slower RTs (M = 141, SD = 100), t(25) = 2.6, p < .01", and faster children also showed larger vocabulary gains (M = 204 vs. M = 106, t(25) = 2.3, p < .05).

> "Grouping children by median mean RT, those with faster RTs knew more words at 24 months (M = 270, SD = 153) than did those with slower RTs (M = 141, SD = 100), t(25) = 2.6, p < .01."
