## Evidence

### Havard C 2025

Havard C, Jouffroy V, Le Gouez A, Bruyère M, Figueiredo S, Roulleau P, Benhamou D, Margat A, Blanié A. (2025). Use of an observer tool to enhance learning of anaesthesia resident's non-technical skills during high-fidelity simulation: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education 25:97. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06608-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomised trial (OT+ n = 33, OT- n = 30), the decision-making sub-item (/4) was the only ANTS sub-category with a significant between-group difference on the Mann-Whitney test; no effect size is printed. The other three sub-items showed no significant differences (p = 0.48, 0.66, 0.63).

> "Among sub-items of the ANTS score, decision-making was significantly better in the OT + group (3 [2–3] versus 2 [2–3], p = 0.01)."
