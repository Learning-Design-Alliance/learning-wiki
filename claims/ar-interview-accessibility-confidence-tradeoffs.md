## Evidence

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Deductive coding of 452 minutes of semi-structured interviews from 24 participants in NVivo. Ten participants reported AR's structured guidance supported task attempts; twelve said videos were less helpful; thirteen described the AR interface as intuitive once familiar.

> "In AR sessions, ten participants reported that the structured guidance supported them in attempting the task:"It guided me precisely on what I should be doing, which made it feel less overwhelming and helped me stay calmer"(P12)."

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026 (2)

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interview coding on perceived training support: seventeen participants said video provided a model but did not help them evaluate their own performance, while nine reported anxiety from red AR signals and twelve wanted the speed indicator repositioned within their field of view.

> "Nine participants said the red signals created anxiety. One described,". . . whenever I felt something was turning red, I just stopped, [myself] panicked. I don't know if that had to be done or not. Initially, that happened a lot"(P23)."
