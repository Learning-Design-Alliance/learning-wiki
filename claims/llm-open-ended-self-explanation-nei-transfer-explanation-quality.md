`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA analysis of the N=92 between-subjects experiment (Outcome ~ Pre-test + Condition + Counterbalance) on NEI transfer open-ended explanation quality. The open-ended condition outperformed control with "β=+11.9%, SE=5.8%, t=2.06, p=.030" and d=0.44; menu-based showed no significant difference (β=+4.7%, p=.343).

> "ANCOVA revealed a significant effect: the open-ended condition scored 11.9 percentage points higher than control (β=+11.9%, SE=5.8%, t=2.06, p=.030, 95% CI [1.2%, 22.6%], d=0.44). Menu-based did not differ from control (β=+4.7%, p=.343)."
