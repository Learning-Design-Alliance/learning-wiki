## Evidence

### Alshareef Ali M. 2026

Alshareef Ali M., Alhasani Mona, Qamar Nabeel, Alhazmi Aiman. (2026). Tailoring hybrid flipped learning course design: exploring motivation, personality, and experiences of students and faculty. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09570-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-sample t-tests on IMI subscales from 149 valid student responses in a hybrid flipped medical terminology course. Interest/Enjoyment M = 3.78 (d = 0.84), Value/Usefulness M = 4.27 (d = 1.74), Effort/Importance M = 3.81 (d = 1.07), Perceived Tension M = 2.55 (d = -0.47), Perceived Competence M = 3.76 (d = 0.90), all p < 0.001, indicating "scores significantly differed from the neutral midpoint".

> "all scores exceeded the neutral midpoint of 3 on the 5-point scale, except for perceived tension (M = 2.55, SD = 0.93). A one-sample t-test confirmed that these scores significantly differed from the neutral midpoint"
