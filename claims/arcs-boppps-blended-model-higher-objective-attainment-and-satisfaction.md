## Evidence

### Hou Yepo 2026

Hou Yepo, Xiao Chan, Yang Hui, Juan Mei, Zhao Guangye, Wang Chunfeng. (2026). Development and evaluation of a blended learning model integrating ARCS and BOPPPS for Endodontics education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08564-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A multi-dimensional evaluation system assessed three-dimensional teaching objective attainment and overall teaching satisfaction via questionnaires. Chi-square tests showed the experimental group achieved "significantly higher attainment rates" across all three objectives and "overall teaching satisfaction rate in the experimental group reached 96.4%, markedly higher than the 71.7%" in controls (P < 0.01).
