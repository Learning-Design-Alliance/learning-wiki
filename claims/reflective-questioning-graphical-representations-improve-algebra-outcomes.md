`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The guide's summary of the two of six studies that taught reflective questioning, one method of focusing on structure. Both comparisons favored reflective questioning; no effect sizes are printed in the guide.

> "One study showed positive effects on procedural and conceptual knowledge for reflective questioning compared to having students study solved problems in small groups, and the other study showed positive effects on procedural knowledge, for reflective questioning compared to regular instruction without reflective questioning."
