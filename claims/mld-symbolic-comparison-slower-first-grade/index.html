Evidence¶

Bert De Smedt 2011¶

Bert De Smedt, Camilla K. Gilmore. (2011). Defective number module or impaired access? Numerical magnitude processing in first graders with mathematical difficulties. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology, 108, 278–292. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2010.09.003

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on reaction times of 82 first graders (20 MLD, 21 LA, 41 TA) on symbolic and non-symbolic comparison. The "significant group × task interaction" (ηp² = .11) showed MLD slower only on the symbolic task; non-symbolic task showed no group differences (t < 1).

"a significant group × task interaction emerged (F(2,79) = 5.12, p < .01, ηp² = .11) (Figure 1). Post-hoc t-tests revealed that on the symbolic task children with MLD were significantly slower than TA children (t(59) = 3.33, p < .01, d = 0.88)."
