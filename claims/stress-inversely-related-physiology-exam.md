## Evidence

### Lampe Luisa Charlotte 2021

Lampe Luisa Charlotte, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2021). Mindfulness-based intervention helps preclinical medical students to contain stress, maintain mindfulness and improve academic success. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02578-y

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis at T3 linking PSS-10 scores to exam results. Physiology was the exam with the lowest pass rate (32.8%) and was considered the most challenging; the stress correlation was r = −0.3364, p = 0.0004.

> "there was no correlation between perceived stress and the exam results in biochemistry. However, stress was inversely related to exam results in physiology, resulting in a correlation coefficient of r = − 0.3364, a 95% CI of − 0.4997 to − 0.1501 and a corresponding p-value of 0.0004."
