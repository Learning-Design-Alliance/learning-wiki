## Evidence

### Ulrich Trautwein 2026

Ulrich Trautwein, Janina Eberhart, Charlotte Dignath, Cavan V. Bonner, Brent W. Roberts. (2026). Self-Regulation Competence as a Superpower: An Integrated Framework for K-12. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10190-z

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

The review reports four meta-analyses it attributes to Meyer et al. (2023), Mammadov (2022), Poropat (2009), and Richardson et al. (2012), each printing "r = .20", "r = .20", "r = .19", and "r = .19" respectively for the conscientiousness–achievement association.

> "Multiple meta-analyses have demonstrated that conscientiousness is a reliable pre- dictor of academic achievement (Meyer et al., 2023: r = .20; Mammadov, 2022: r = .20; Poropat, 2009: r = .19; Richardson et al., 2012: r = .19) across a broad spectrum of different performance indicators"
