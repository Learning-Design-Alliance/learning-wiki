## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a moderated-moderation model predicting GPA, the main effect of depressive symptoms was significant and negative. [→ Kilgore Jenna 2023](#kilgore-jenna-2023)

## Evidence

### Kilgore Jenna 2023

Kilgore Jenna, Collins Amanda C., Miller Julie Anne M., Winer E. Samuel. (2023). Grit, depression, social desirability, and GPA. PLoS One 18(7): e0288270. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0288270

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional moderated-moderation regression in a sample of 520 undergraduates, with GPA drawn from university records. The article reports a significant negative main effect of depressive symptoms ("b = -.15, 95% CI [-.251, -.058], t = -3.14, p = .002"). No standardized effect size was printed.

> "The main effects for depressive symptoms, b = -.15, 95% CI [-.251, -.058], t = -3.14, p = .002, and social desirability, b = -.11, 95% CI [-.208, -.020], t = -2.38, p = .02, were significant."
