## Evidence

### Mocellin D 2026

Mocellin D, Maglio S, Nanyaro M-W, Belardi P, Borellini M, Shayo EH, Ferrari A, Mfaume B, Ntinginya N, Tognarelli S, Menciassi A. (2026). Neonatal Ventilation Training Using a Low-Cost, High-Fidelity Simulator in a Low-Resource Setting: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.33261

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon paired-sample test of pre- vs posttraining knowledge questionnaire scores among 42 medical students with no prior neonatal ventilation experience, reported in the Results section. The article reports "a significant improvement was observed in theoretical knowledge following training" with the printed means and P value.

> "Among medical students, a significant improvement was observed in theoretical knowledge following training, with higher posttraining scores compared with pretraining scores (mean [SE], 8.88 [0.13] vs 8.29 [0.17] of a maximum 10 score; P < .001)."

### Mocellin D 2026 (2)

Mocellin D, Maglio S, Nanyaro M-W, Belardi P, Borellini M, Shayo EH, Ferrari A, Mfaume B, Ntinginya N, Tognarelli S, Menciassi A. (2026). Neonatal Ventilation Training Using a Low-Cost, High-Fidelity Simulator in a Low-Resource Setting: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.33261

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon paired-sample test of pre- vs posttraining knowledge questionnaire scores among 37 health professionals (mostly midwives) at Tosamaganga Regional Referral Hospital, reported in the Results section. The article reports significantly higher posttraining scores with the printed means and P value.

> "Among experienced health professionals, posttraining theoretical test scores were significantly higher than pretraining scores (mean [SD] on 10-point scale, 9.14 [0.13] vs 8.65 [0.17]; P = .03)."
