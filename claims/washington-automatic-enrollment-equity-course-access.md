## Evidence

### Leung 2020

Leung, M., Cardichon, J., Scott, C., & Darling-Hammond, L. (2020). Inequitable opportunity to learn: Access to advanced mathematics and science courses. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/crdc-course-access

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

State-level CRDC analysis identifying Washington as an outlier. The report attributes the pattern to a policy in which "students meeting set academic standards are automatically enrolled in advanced courses" rather than requiring opt-in, begun in the Federal Way district in 2010 and enacted statewide as HB 1599 in 2019.

> "For example, we found that 59% of schools serving larger percentages of students of color offered Calculus, compared to only 49% of schools serving smaller percentages of these students."
