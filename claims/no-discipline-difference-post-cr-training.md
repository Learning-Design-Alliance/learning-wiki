## Evidence

### Feinberg JL 2017

Feinberg JL, Russell D, Mola A, Bowles KH, Lipman TH. (2017). Developing an Adapted Cardiac Rehabilitation Training for Home Care Clinicians. Journal of Cardiopulmonary Rehabilitation and Prevention. https://doi.org/10.1097/HCR.0000000000000228

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Discipline-level paired t tests in the same pre/post evaluation: separate tests showed significantly greater knowledge for "RNs and PTs/OTs both P < .001", but posttraining means of 14.72 (RNs, n = 16) and 14.56 (PTs/OTs, n = 25) did not differ significantly. Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "There was no significant difference observed in posttraining knowledge scores by discipline (posttraining mean scores for RNs and PTs/OTs were 14.72 and 14.56, respectively)."
