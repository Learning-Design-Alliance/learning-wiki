Cognition-inclusive ordinal regression models on the ABCD sample (N = 5001). Tables 4–5 print ADI OR 0.94 (0.88, 1.02), p = 0.12 at 2-year and OR 0.93 (0.87, 1.00), p = 0.05 at 3-year follow-up; ITN remained significant at OR 1.27 at both timepoints.
