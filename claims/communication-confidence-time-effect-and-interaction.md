## Evidence

### Huang Hsiang-Ping 2025

Huang Hsiang-Ping, Tien Yun, Lin Yung-Chang, Yu I-Chen, Chien Nai-Hui. (2025). Effects of empathy mapping and mini-simulation on second-year nursing students’ empathy and communication self-confidence: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06686-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on communication confidence in the quasi-experimental study (n = 98) showed a "significant and positive time effect (F = 7.950, p = 0.006)", while the between-group effect was nonsignificant (F = 2.140, p = 0.147).

> "but a significant and positive time effect (F = 7.950, p = 0.006), meaning that students’ self-confidence in communication improved significantly over time."

### Huang Hsiang-Ping 2025 (2)

Huang Hsiang-Ping, Tien Yun, Lin Yung-Chang, Yu I-Chen, Chien Nai-Hui. (2025). Effects of empathy mapping and mini-simulation on second-year nursing students’ empathy and communication self-confidence: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06686-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same repeated measures ANOVA on communication confidence reported a "significant group-by-time interaction" (F = 6.320, p = 0.014); Fig. 1B shows the experimental group surpassing the control group by semester end.

> "There was also a significant group-by-time interaction in the between-group effect (F = 6.320, p = 0.014), meaning that the differences in self-confidence in communication between groups varied significantly over time."
