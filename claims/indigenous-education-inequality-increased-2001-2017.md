## Evidence

### David Gunawan 2022

David Gunawan, William Griffiths, Duangkamon Chotikapanich. (2022). Inequality in Education: A Comparison of Australian Indigenous and Nonindigenous Populations. Working paper, University of Wollongong. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1515/spp-2021-0026](https://doi.org/10.1515/spp-2021-0026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Posterior generalized Lorenz dominance probabilities for each pair of indigenous distribution years (2001, 2006, 2014, 2017). Probabilities "are all greater than 0.6, providing evidence that each of the later years is dominated by each of the earlier years", i.e. inequality increased.

> "The GLD probabilities in the first row of Table 6 are all greater than 0.6, providing evidence that each of the later years is dominated by each of the earlier years. Inequality has increased."

### David Gunawan 2022 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Posterior densities for the J and CF(0.1) inequality indices for the indigenous population (Figures 4 and 5) show "considerable overlap in these distributions, but there is nevertheless a distinct movement to the right from 2001 to 2017", confirming the GLD outcome.

> "There is considerable overlap in these distributions, but there is nevertheless a distinct movement to the right from 2001 to 2017."
