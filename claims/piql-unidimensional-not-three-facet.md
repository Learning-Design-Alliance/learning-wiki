# Student response patterns on the PIQL fit a unidimensional model rather than the expert-proposed three-facet factor structure

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` CFA of all PIQL versions rejected the facet-driven three-factor model: CFI and TLI were below 0.8 against a target above 0.9. [→ White Brahmia 2021](#white-brahmia-2021)
`q2 i?` For v2.0, v2.1 and v2.2, a unidimensional CFA model fits well, with CFI and TLI above 0.93 and RMSR and RMSEA both below 0.04. [→ White Brahmia 2021 (2)](#white-brahmia-2021-2)

## Evidence

### White Brahmia 2021

White Brahmia, S. W., Olsho, A., Smith, T. I., Boudreaux, A., Eaton, P., and Zimmerman, C. (2021). The Physics Inventory of Quantitative Literacy: A tool for assessing mathematical reasoning in introductory physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Confirmatory factor analysis testing the expert-proposed three-facet model (ratios, covariation, signed quantities) against response data from all PIQL versions; CFI and TLI fell below 0.8, below the 0.9 target threshold.

> "For all versions of the PIQL the CFI and TLI were below 0.8 when using the facet-driven factor model; therefore, students’ response patterns did not match our expectations of reasoning developing differently in the areas of ratio and proportion, covariation, and signed quantities/negativity."

### White Brahmia 2021 (2)

White Brahmia, S. W., Olsho, A., Smith, T. I., Boudreaux, A., Eaton, P., and Zimmerman, C. (2021). The Physics Inventory of Quantitative Literacy: A tool for assessing mathematical reasoning in introductory physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

EFA and CFA of the most recent versions (v2.0, v2.1, v2.2) suggest unidimensionality: the article reports CFI and TLI above 0.93 under a unidimensional model, with RMSR and RMSEA both below 0.04.

> "Goodness-of-ﬁt analyses show that the CFI and TLI were above 0.93 for both versions under CFA using a uni- dimensional model. Additionally, the standardized root mean square of the residuals (RMSR) and the root mean square of the error of approximation (RMSEA) were both below 0.04"
