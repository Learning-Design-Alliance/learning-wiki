## Subclaims

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group paired comparisons after standardized training in the randomized trial. The article also reports the control group "exhibited a significant decrease solely in their ‘Resilience’ score after the training (20.11 ± 2.20 vs. 17.22 ± 3.35, p = 0.004)". Interviews suggested the CPOAL group became more cautious in questioning supervising pharmacists.

> "It is noteworthy that the CPOAL group’s score on ‘Insight and independent thinking’ was the only item that showed a significant decrease after standardized training (21.11 ± 1.27 vs. 19.89 ± 1.76, p = 0.047)"
