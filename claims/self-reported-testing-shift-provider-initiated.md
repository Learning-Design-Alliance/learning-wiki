## Evidence

### Joore Ivo Kim 2018

Joore Ivo Kim, van Bergen Jan E. A. M. J, ter Riet Gerben, van der Maat A., van Dijk N. (2018). Development and evaluation of a blended educational programme for general practitioners' trainers to stimulate proactive HIV testing. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-018-0723-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired questionnaires from 74 GP trainers before and three months after the programme, analysed with Wilcoxon signed rank tests. The article reports a "significant decrease after the training programme (p = 0.004)" and a shift toward symptom-based initiation (p = 0.017); no effect sizes are printed.

> "Self-reported HIV testing behaviour showed a significant decrease after the training programme (p = 0.004). Before the programme, the HIV test was significantly more often initiated by the patient than after the programme (p = 0.001)"
