Tedeschi MN, Hose TM, Mehlman EK, Franklin S, Wong TE. (2023). Improving Models for Student Retention and Graduation using Markov Chains. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Positive-control benchmarking of institutional records from RIT (Fall 2013-2015 cohorts) compares the reduced-form Markov model against the traditional cohort calculation. The model "faithfully match[es] the traditional graduation rate estimates, GRtr", and the article concludes these "results provide strong evidence that the Markov model is a valid approach to estimate SYGR."

> "When only cohorts with a full six years of data are used to compute SYGR using the Markov method, estimates of GRma from the reduced form Markov model faithfully match the traditional graduation rate estimates, GRtr"
