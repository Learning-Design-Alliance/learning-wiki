Evidence¶

Li Zhao 2024¶

Li Zhao, Junjie Peng, Shiqi Ke, Kang Lee. (2024). Effectiveness of Unproctored vs. Teacher-Proctored Exams in Reducing Students’ Cheating: A Double-Blind Randomized Controlled Field Experimental Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09965-z

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Hierarchical meta-regression across the four studies entered reminders after exam type; the reminders step was significant (Q(df = 1) = 28.71, p < 0.001) with very strong Bayes evidence (BF10 = 37.94). Reminders had no significant effect on cheating extent (b = 0.04, p = 0.904).

"The academic integrity reminders significantly affected cheating rate, b = –0.06, SE = 0.02, 95% CI = –0.11 to –0.02, p = 0.008, BF10 = 37.94. It accounted for 25.19% of the variance."
