## Evidence

### Katherine D. Rainey and Bethany R. Wilcox 2020

Katherine D. Rainey and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2020). Faculty survey on upper-division thermal physics content coverage. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.rainey](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.rainey)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis of topic selections from 59 respondents at 52 institutions, comparing three topic groups from Table I. The authors report "9/9 (100%) of assumed core topics" selected by all, versus 16% of supporting topics and 0% of other core topics.

> "We found that 9/9 (100%) of assumed core topics, 5/32 (16%) of assumed supporting topics, and 0/20 (0%) of other core topics were selected by all respondents."
