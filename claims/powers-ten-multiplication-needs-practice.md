`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post results for Q5 (multiplication of powers of ten) in trials 2 and 3, after Activity 11 (Lazy Maths: Powers is faster) was added. The article reports post-test scores "reaching 74% and 90% in trials 2 and 3", indicating sufficient practice was key to this outcome.

> "Consequently, the post-test scores showed a significant improvement, reaching 74% and 90% in trials 2 and 3, compared to the initial pre-test scores of 16% and 10%, respectively (Fig.6b)."
