## Evidence

### Knobel Roxana 2020

Knobel Roxana, Menezes Mariane de Oliveira, Santos Débora de Souza, Takemoto Maíra Libertad Soligo (2020). Planning, construction and use of handmade simulators to enhance the teaching and learning in Obstetrics. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.3684.3302

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional pilot study using a structured questionnaire based on the Kirkpatrick model, answered by 31 apprentices (10 resident doctors, 12 undergraduate medical students, 9 nurse midwives) with an "81.58% response rate". Data were analyzed descriptively with no hypothesis testing, as stated for this pilot study.

> "the respondents (31 apprentices) positively evaluated the simulators, perceiving significant gains in theoretical knowledge, ability to solve clinical problems and decreased anxiety to deal with situations similar to those simulated."
