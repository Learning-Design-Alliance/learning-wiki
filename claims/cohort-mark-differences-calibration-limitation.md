## Evidence

### Andreani Y 2025

Andreani Y, Gunaratne BC, Sadr A, Martin FE, Divnic‐Resnik T, Sukumar S. (2025). Do Pre‐Clinical Summative Assessments Predict a Student's Clinical Performance? A Retrospective Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13098

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective cohort analysis across six cohorts (2013–2019) with calibrated rubric‐driven assessment. The article reports significant between‐cohort differences in marks, while the Endo delta score showed no significant difference between cohorts A to D (p = 0.11 one way ANOVA).

> "Despite the sustained effort made to standardise assessment, there were statistically significant differences in marks between the cohorts across all disciplines except for Endo when using extracted teeth in simulation"
