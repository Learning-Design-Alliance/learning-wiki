Muhidin Mohamed, Shubhadeep Mukherjee and Bhavana Baad. (2025). Impact of UK Postgraduate Student Experiences on Academic Performance in Blended Learning: A Data Analytics Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-tabulation of survey responses (Tables 2–3, n = 255) showed younger students more negatively affected; the article also reports male (53.64%) students slightly more negatively affected than female (42.73%) cohort, and speculates older students' employment experience helped them adapt.

> "the academic performance of young students (18 - 34) were more negatively affected compared to their relatively older peers (35-54)."

### Muhidin Mohamed 2025 (2)

Muhidin Mohamed, Shubhadeep Mukherjee and Bhavana Baad. (2025). Impact of UK Postgraduate Student Experiences on Academic Performance in Blended Learning: A Data Analytics Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A t-test comparing study-time samples across gender groups (Figure 5) found male students' average study hours significantly lower than female learners'; the test also found significant differences in mean study times across the three age groups (p < 0.05).

> "we found that the average study hours by male students is significantly lower than similar time by the fellow female learners (p <0.01, t-test)."
