### Linares M 2026

Linares M, Grimaldi C, Palma N, Vintimilla B, Candal S, Estrella D, Saps M. (2026). Knowledge Does Not Translate Into Diagnostic Restraint: Application of Rome‐Based IBS Diagnosis Among Medical Students in Latin America. Neurogastroenterology and Motility. https://doi.org/10.1111/nmo.70335

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Alarm-feature classification task in the cross-sectional survey (n = 238), with features selected a priori from Rome IV bowel disorder criteria and IBS guidance. Correct identification ranged from 44.5% (nocturnal diarrhea) to 89.9% (unintentional weight loss).

> "Abdominal pain relieved by defecation was incorrectly labeled as an alarm feature by 30% of respondents. Mild fatigue (12.2%) and normal laboratory results (9.2%) were also incorrectly classified as red flags by subsets of participants."
