`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The editorial reports, citing Mullins, Rummel and Spada, a study of collaborative mathematics. It explains the finding via Vygotsky's zone of proximal development: collaboration can lead to long-term conceptual gains when the task is just beyond individual mastery level.

> "By differentiating math tasks involving reasoning from those stressing practice, they rigorously showed that collaboration aids in the learning of elaboration skills but not in the learning of procedural skills."
