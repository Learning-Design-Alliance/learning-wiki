## Evidence

### Mariam S 2025

Mariam S, Heba I, Mohamed FHN. (2025). Diabe-teach: a randomized controlled trial of a gamified approach to enhance medical undergraduates’ knowledge and comprehension of diabetes mellitus. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06602-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 56 preclinical medical students randomized via Randomizer.org to a Diabe-teach board game session or a structured self-study hour with identical content. Post-test correct-answer means were “M = 81.8,SD = 9.8” versus M = 68.9, SD = 14.2; the between-group post-test independent t test was significant, p < 0.001, t = −3.93 (Table 2).

> "In the post-test, both the “Diabe-teach” game-based learning group (M = 81.8,SD = 9.8) and the self-study group (M = 68.9,SD = 14.2) showed a statistically significant increase in correct responses compared to their pretest scores"
