Palmisano Francesco, Santuari Nadia, Moletta Cristina, Ambrosi Elisa, Rizzoli Andrea. (2024). The effect of a team-based blended simulation training program on cardiopulmonary resuscitation on healthcare professionals' perception, performance, and costs: a mixed-method study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06543-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective cohort comparison of certification outcomes: 1,052 of 1,167 riAniMO participants certified versus 839 of 845 BLSDr participants. Of the 9.8% not certified in riAniMO, "79.1% of them repeated the team-based BLS/AED simulation" and 20.9% repeated all BLSD training. No effect size or test statistic is printed.

> "However, the percentage of HCPs certified decreased by 9% in the riAniMo group (90.2% of HCPs certified) compared to the BLSD group (99.2% of HCPs certified)."
