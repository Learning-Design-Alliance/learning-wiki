## Subclaims
`q2 i?` High-school students with higher mastery approach goals reach out to more peers (out-degree), are closer to peers (closeness), and more often connect unconnected classmates (betweenness). [→ Hemi 2025](#hemi-2025)

## Evidence

### Hemi 2025

Hemi, A., Madjar, N., Rich, Y., & Daumiller, M. (2025). Relationships between students' achievement goals and social positioning in the classroom. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1047–1062. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12762

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional multilevel SNA study of 472 high-school students from four Israeli public schools; four two-level random intercept-slope models tested the four goals against each centrality measure, with unstandardized coefficients reported (out-degree 3.62**, closeness .04***, betweenness 3.92*). "mastery approach goals were positively associated with out‐degree, closeness and betweenness centrality measures"; no standardized effect size printed.

> "Support for this hypothesis was manifest in three centrality measures; mastery approach goals were positively associated with out‐degree, closeness and betweenness centrality measures."
