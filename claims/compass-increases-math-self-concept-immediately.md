## Evidence

### Hörsch 2026

Hörsch, H., Schumacher, J., & Wolff, F. (2026). Long-Term Effects of an Intervention Targeting Dimensional and Temporal Comparisons on Students' Math and English Academic Self-Concepts: A Waiting-List Replication and Extension Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10137-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Baseline change models on math-intervention students (Group A n = 219, Group B n = 117) from 33 academic-track classes showed "students' math self-concept was significantly increased directly after participation in the COMPASS intervention in both groups", with d = 1.05 in Group A and d = 0.91 in Group B.

> "students' math self-concept was significantly increased directly after participation in the COMPASS intervention in both groups (Group A: MDiff2_1 = 0.39, p <.001, d = 1.05, 95% CI [0.86, 1.24]; Group B: MDiff3_2 = 0.37, p <.001, d = 0.91, 95% CI [0.65, 1.17])"
