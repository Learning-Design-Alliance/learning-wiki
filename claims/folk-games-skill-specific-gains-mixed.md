Xia Wei. (2016). Research on the Boost of Development on Young Children's Fine Motor by Folk Games. International Education Studies, 9(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n9p111

Subscale comparison from the same single-factor experiment's post-test (Table 3): intervention pinch 2.24 vs 1.79, touch 3.7 vs 3.62, draw 0.10 vs 0.09, cut 0.07 vs 0.06, but fold and string showed "no obvious difference from the control class".

> "It is found in post-test result that the intervention class’s average scores of all fine motors were higher than the control class except folding and stringing, which did not have obvious difference from the control class."
