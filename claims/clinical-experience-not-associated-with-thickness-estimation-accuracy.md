## Evidence

### Fong Raymond 2026

Fong Raymond, Kwan Connie C. Y. (2026). Accuracy of Visual Inspection of Thickened Liquids: Implications for Multidisciplinary Dysphagia Management and Clinical Nutrition. Journal of Human Nutrition and Dietetics. https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.70294

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same cross-sectional survey, students were assigned 0 years of experience and merged with practitioners to form an experience continuum. The analysis found "the absence of any linear trend between experience and accuracy (ρ = −0.084, p = 0.38)"; no effect size is printed. The authors state this corroborates Glassburn and Deem's earlier finding.

> "A Kruskal–Wallis H test confirmed that years of clinical experience did not significantly affect the reliability of visual inspection (H = 6.56, p = 0.16). A Spearman's correlation further confirmed the absence of any linear trend between experience and accuracy (ρ = −0.084, p = 0.38)."
