## Evidence

### Zinski Anne 2017

Zinski Anne, Blackwell Kristina T.C. Panizzi Woodley, Belue F. Mike, Brooks William S. (2017). Is lecture dead? A preliminary study of medical students' evaluation of teaching methods in the preclinical curriculum. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.59b9.5f40

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 55 MS1 and 65 MS2 students at one medical school; Likert ratings of eight methods. The article reports team-based learning "yielded the highest mean scores for preferred instructional methods" (M=4.2, SD=1.0; M=4.0, SD=1.0).
