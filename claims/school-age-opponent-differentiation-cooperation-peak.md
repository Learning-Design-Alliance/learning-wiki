## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Across grades 1-12, children cooperated more with the high-competitive than the low-competitive opponent after repeated interaction. [→ Zhang 2026](#zhang-2026)
`q3 i3` Cooperation rates were lowest in grades 1-2 and highest in grades 4-5, declining toward adolescence. [→ Zhang 2026 (2)](#zhang-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, R., Zhang, L., Hao, X.-M., Dreher, J.-C., & Qu, C. (2026). Learning to adaptively cooperate through social interactions during childhood and adolescence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00423-9

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on cooperation rates for 146 participants aged 6-17: Opponent × Block interaction was significant (ηp² = 0.02), with higher cooperation toward HCO in blocks 2-5 but not block 1. There was also a significant Opponent main effect (d = -0.35, p < .001).

> "We found a significant Opponent × Block interaction, F (3.75, 532.48) = 2.84, p = .027, 𝜂𝑝2 = 0.02."

### Zhang 2026 (2)

Zhang, R., Zhang, L., Hao, X.-M., Dreher, J.-C., & Qu, C. (2026). Learning to adaptively cooperate through social interactions during childhood and adolescence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00423-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Grade main effect (ηp² = 0.17): grades 1-2 showed lower cooperation than grades 4-5 (d = -0.81, p < .001), grades 7-9 (d = -0.67) and grades 10-12 (d = -0.46, p = .037). No Grade × Opponent × Block interaction (p = .807), so opponent differentiation was similar across grades.

> "There were significant main effects of Opponent F (1, 568) = 32.39, p < .001, 𝜂𝑝 2 = 0.18, and Grade F (3, 142) = 10.19, p < .001, 𝜂𝑝 2 = 0.17."
