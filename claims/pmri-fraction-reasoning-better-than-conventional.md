## Evidence

### Saleh 2018

Saleh, M., Prahmana, R.C.I., Isa, M., & Murni. (2018). Improving the Reasoning Ability of Elementary School Student through the Indonesian Realistic Mathematics Education. Journal on Mathematics Education, 9(1), 41-54. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1173650

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment at two Banda Aceh elementary schools: 51 students taught with PMRI and 45 with conventional learning, each classified into low, intermediate, and high initial-ability levels. The t-test of N-Gain gave "the value of 2.977 and Significant (P) value of 0.004 for all categories", so the null hypothesis was rejected.
