# Metacognitive monitoring bias during competitive Kahoots did not differ by competitive preference, though bias shifted across time

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Daniell DiFrancesca 2025

Daniell DiFrancesca, Dan Spencer. (2025). The effects of competitive classroom activities on undergraduates with varied levels of competitive preference. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09741-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A 2x8 mixed ANOVA on bias scores from prediction judgments across eight Kahoots (LC n = 24, HC n = 28) found group effects "non-significant" and a within-subject time effect (η2 = 0.07); post-hoc tests showed the eighth Kahoot bias was lower than the first, third, and seventh.

> "Both the interaction effect, F(7, 350) = 0.45, p =.87, and between group effect, F(1, 50) = 3.84, p =.06, were non-significant. There was a significant effect of time, F(7, 350) = 3.79, p <.001, η2 = 0.07."
