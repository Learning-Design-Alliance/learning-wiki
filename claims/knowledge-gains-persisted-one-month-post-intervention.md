## Evidence

### Hossein Meysam Pour 2025

Hossein Meysam Pour, Ansari Mojgan, Fekri Nazanin, Yazdimoghaddam Hamideh. (2025). The effects of e-learning vs. gamification-based training on ICU nurses’ knowledge and attitudes toward organ donation candidates: a study based on the psychological security and empowerment model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07299-0

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-month follow-up in the 45-nurse trial showed between-group differences remained significant (P < 0.001, generalized Eta-Squared 0.79); group means were 13.60 (e-learning), 15.93 (gamification), and 11.26 (control).

> "Although a slight decline in knowledge retention was noted one month post-intervention—likely attributable to the natural process of forgetting—the knowledge levels remained higher than the pre-intervention stage, demonstrating a lasting effect."
