Thorndike 1922¶

Thorndike, E. L., & Upton, C. B. (1922). An Experiment in Learning an Abstract Subject. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 13(6). https://doi.org/10.1037/h0065229

q1 · i? · causal · r1

The authors' estimate, not a measured comparison: had the text clearly stated the meaning of gauss and its relation to unit-pole force, there would have been "a saving per person of 30 minutes time, much irritation and at least ten per cent in errors."

"We estimate that if it had stated it clearly and emphatically there would have been a saving per person of 30 minutes time, much irritation and at least ten per cent in errors."
