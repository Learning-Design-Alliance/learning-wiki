`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of procedure requirements across the 42 retrieved national curricula. The study reports that "Training in diagnostic endoscopy was incorporated into all curricula" with median requirements of 300 gastroscopies and 200 colonoscopies, and separately reports EUS in 40.5% (17/42), ERCP in 69.0% (29/42), and simulation training in 16% (7/42) of curricula.

> "Training in diagnostic endoscopy was incorporated into all curricula. 69.0% (29/42) of the curricula specified required numbers of performed gastroscopies and colonoscopies, with a median of 300 gastroscopies (IQR: 200–300) and 200 colonoscopies (IQR: 100–300) required"
