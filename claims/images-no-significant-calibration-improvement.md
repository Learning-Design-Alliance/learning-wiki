`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of calibration scores (EOL minus performance index) controlling for age, gender, and native language found no significant group difference; Table 1 reports F(1, df) = 1.12, P = .291, partial η² = 0.010.

> "In contrast, calibration did not differ significantly between groups. These findings suggest that visual images improved test performance and perceived EOL, but not calibration accuracy."
