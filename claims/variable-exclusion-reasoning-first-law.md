### Mary Jane Brundage 2024

Mary Jane Brundage, David E. Meltzer, and Chandralekha Singh. (2024). Investigating introductory and advanced students’ difficulties with change in internal energy, work and heat transfer using a validated instrument. Originally published 1991. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.03795](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.03795)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Think-aloud interview data from 11 introductory and 6 upper-level students, reported in Section III.A. The paper notes this “variable-exclusion” thinking pattern was previously described by Rozier and Viennot in 1991, and gives interview examples of students ignoring heat transfer when reasoning about internal energy.

> "students invoked relationships between only two of the three variables (Q, E, and W) that are incorporated in the first law of thermodynamics, leading them to make incorrect inferences."
