# Self-regulated vs. externally regulated prompt presentation shows no significant effect on posttest performance and no interaction with prompt format

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no significant difference between self-regulated and externally regulated presentation on overall posttest total, F(1,47) = 3.43, p = .07. [→ Reisslein 2004](#reisslein-2004)
`q2 i?` There was no significant interaction between the presentation and format factors. [→ Reisslein 2004 (2)](#reisslein-2004-2)

## Evidence

### Reisslein 2004

Reisslein, J., Atkinson, R. K., & Reisslein, M. (2004). Exploring the Presentation and Format of Help in a Computer-Based Electrical Engineering Learning Environment. Arizona State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the 51-participant 2x2 factorial ANOVA, the presentation factor showed "no significant difference between the two levels of the presentation factor" on overall posttest total; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference between the two levels of the presentation factor (self vs. external regulation) on the overall posttest total, F ratio F(1,47) = 3.43, mean square error MSE  = 11.20, significance level p = .07."

### Reisslein 2004 (2)

Reisslein, J., Atkinson, R. K., & Reisslein, M. (2004). Exploring the Presentation and Format of Help in a Computer-Based Electrical Engineering Learning Environment. Arizona State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The posttest ANOVA in the same experiment reported that "there was  no significant interaction between the two factors" of prompt format and prompt presentation.

> "Finally, there was  no significant interaction between the two factors."
