## Evidence

### Shin 2019

Narrative review argument: successful resolution enhances satisfaction and expected future satisfaction, encouraging further curiosity and deeper information seeking. The review contrasts this with the externally supported four-phase path from situational to individual interest.

> "Through this iterative curiosity –resolution cycle, individuals may construct a more elaborative knowledge network and build a foundation for individual interest"
