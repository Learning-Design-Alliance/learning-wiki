### Stephen D.H. Hsu and James Schombert 2010

Stephen D.H. Hsu and James Schombert. (2010). Nonlinear Psychometric Thresholds for Physics and Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1011.0663](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1011.0663)

Observational analysis of 2000-2004 University of Oregon student records, plotted in figure 1 as upper GPAs vs SAT-M for Physics and Mathematics graduates. The article reports "no student below approximately SAT-M = 600" reached the 3.5 upper GPA.

> "no student below approximately SAT-M = 600 was able to attain the 3.5 upp er GPA (i.e., roughly equal numbers of A and B grades) typically required for admission t o a Ph.D. program."
