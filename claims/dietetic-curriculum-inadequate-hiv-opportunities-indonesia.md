## Evidence

### Mutiara Tirta Prabandari Lintang Kusuma 2020

Mutiara Tirta Prabandari Lintang Kusuma, Kidd Tandalayo, Muturi Nancy, Procter Sandra Butin, Yarrow Linda, Hsu Wei-Wen. (2020). The Symbolic Meaning of HIV: Understanding Lecturers’ Ambivalence over Teaching HIV to Dietetic Students. Journal of the International Association of Providers of AIDS Care. https://doi.org/10.1177/2325958220939755

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Curriculum mapping via document analysis (syllabi, handbooks, lecture notes) plus interviews with 13 lecturers in 3 Indonesian dietetics schools. The review "showed the limited opportunities provided to lecturers to discuss HIV with their students"; no dedicated HIV class or assigned lecturer existed in any participating school.

> "A thorough review of the syllabus, academic documents, and lecture notes from each participant showed the limited opportunities provided to lecturers to discuss HIV with their students. There was no class entirely designed to discuss HIV and no lecturer specifically assigned to teach HIV in all participating dietetic schools."
