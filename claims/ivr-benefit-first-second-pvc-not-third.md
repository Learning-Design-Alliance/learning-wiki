## Evidence

### Andersen NL 2021

Andersen NL, Jensen RO, Posth S, Laursen CB, Jørgensen R, Graumann O. (2021). Teaching ultrasound-guided peripheral venous catheter placement through immersive virtual reality: An explorative pilot study. Medicine. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8270624/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fisher exact tests on per-cannula success in the same RCT (IVR n=10, control n=9). IVR success rates were 100%, 80%, and 40% for PVCs 1-3 versus 44%, 22%, and 0% in controls; "significantly higher in the IVR group compared to the control group for the 1st and 2nd PVC (P = .011, P = .023), but not for the 3rd PVC (P = .087)."

> "Furthermore, the proportion of successful cannulations was significantly higher in the IVR group compared to the control group for the 1st and 2nd PVC (P = .011, P = .023), but not for the 3rd PVC (P = .087)."
