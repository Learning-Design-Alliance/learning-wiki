Evidence¶

Philip M. Gleason 2014¶

Philip M. Gleason, Christina Clark Tuttle, Brian Gill, Ira Nichols-Barrer, Bing-ru Teh. (2014). Do KIPP Schools Boost Student Achievement? Education Finance and Policy, vol. 9, no. 1. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/do-kipp-schools-boost-student-achievement

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The publication's own summary of the evaluation states that the study measured achievement impacts of "41 Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP) charter middle schools nationwide" and found "consistently positive and statistically significant test-score effects" in all four subjects. No effect sizes or sample sizes are printed on the page.

"This article measures the achievement impacts of 41 Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP) charter middle schools nationwide and found consistently positive and statistically significant test-score effects in reading, math, science, and social studies."
