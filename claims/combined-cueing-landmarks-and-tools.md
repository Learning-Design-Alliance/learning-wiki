`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative synthesis of the 28 combined verbal-nonverbal cueing excerpts in the review's results. Examples include surgeons pointing while naming anatomy ('Fibroid over there') and a dentistry expert physically guiding scaling strokes while verbally directing the novice to key kinesthetic features.

> "drawing attention to landmarks and ‘where to look’ was sometimes found in excerpts with verbal cues only, but most often found in excerpts with combined verbal and nonverbal cues."
