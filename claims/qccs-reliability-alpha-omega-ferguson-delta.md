Test-level reliability analysis of the v2.2 pilot responses reports both coefficients; the authors calculate 'α and ω to both be 0.83', exceeding the advised 0.7 for group measurements and 0.8 for potential individual use.

> "Within PER, researchers have advised a reliability of at least 0.7 for group measurements, and at least 0.8 to be potentially used for individual measurements [11]. We calculate α and ω to both be 0.83, which is above the discussed threshold."
