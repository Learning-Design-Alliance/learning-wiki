## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Random-effects Bayesian model selection identified a literal-learner utility model (informational value + movement cost + speaker preferences) as the most likely model of teachers' choices, with protected exceedance probability > 0.999. [→ Vélez 2023](#velez-2023)

## Evidence

### Vélez 2023

Vélez, N., Chen, A. M., Burke, T., Cushman, F. A., & Gershman, S. J. (2023). Teachers recruit mentalizing regions to represent learners’ beliefs. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2215015120

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model comparison of 12 models fit to the scanner teaching choices of 28 participants, using random-effects Bayesian model selection on model evidences (−0.5*BIC). The winning literal-learner model had "protected exceedance probability > 0.999"; no effect size is printed.

> "The most likely model in the population is one that selects examples to teach by considering the teacher’s preferences, the teacher’s movement costs, and the informational value to a learner who will interpret the examples literally (protected exceedance probability > 0.999; Fig. 3A)."
