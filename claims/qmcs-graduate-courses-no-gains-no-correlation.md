### McKagan 2010

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reporting of QMCS results from graduate quantum courses, summarized in the article's goals section. The authors state "there is no diﬀerence between pretest and posttest scores" and no correlation with final exams; equivalence was not tested with a printed effect size.

> "In graduate courses, there is no diﬀerence between pretest and posttest scores, even for students who score low on the pretest, and there is no correlation between QMCS scores and ﬁnal exam scores."
