## Evidence

### Luttenberger Katharina 2014

Luttenberger Katharina, Graessel Elmar, Simon Cosima, Donath Carolin. (2014). From board to bedside – training the communication competences of medical students with role plays. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-135

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sex-group comparisons within the evaluation of 182 students using t tests and Mann-Whitney U tests with Bonferroni correction. Table 4 prints, for the competence item, male mean 2.17 (SD 1.01) versus female mean 1.69 (SD 0.73), t test p = .001.

> "Women admitted more often that they were nervous before the role play. Women were also less likely to agree that they preferred more feedback from their fellow students than the male students. In the end, women gave higher ratings than men to the competence they gained in difficult communication situations (Table 4)."
