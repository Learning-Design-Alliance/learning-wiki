## Evidence

### Nachiket Kotalwar 2026

Nachiket Kotalwar, Conrad Borchers, Shubham Gandhi, Partha Sarathi Purkayastha, David Yaron, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2026). Exploring Visual Access and Proactive AI Tutoring for Handwritten Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content analysis of 2,140 tutor messages labeled by a validated LLM annotation model (model-human agreement α=.655–.693 for moves), compared across conditions with Holm-corrected Kruskal–Wallis tests of participant shares; marked as differing across conditions (Holm p < .05) in Figure 4b.

> "Correctness feedback affirms or rejects existing work. Its share rose from 40% of tutor messages inText-Reactive to 63% inVisual-Reactiveand 69% inVisual-Proactive(59% amongVisual-Proactive’s requested replies, Table 13; Figure 4b)."
