Walrond 2025¶

Walrond, N., Serwanga, R., & Farrell, G. (2025). Systems alignment and coherence: Opportunities for Alameda County's early care and education system. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673491

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

The report cites a 2024 memo by Pryor and Saucedo reporting subsidized childcare access rates: "in the same year, only one in seven of Alameda's children who were eligible for subsidized child-care received services," compared with one in nine statewide in 2022. The report attributes the disparity in access to families of color and families with low incomes. Figures are reported second-hand; no effect size is printed.
