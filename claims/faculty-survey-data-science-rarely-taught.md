Faculty survey run in March 2022 through APS listservs with 100 physics faculty responses from 78 US institutions, 7 international institutions, and 10 unknown. The survey found that "The majority of respondents ( N = 70) have not taught data science in their physics courses in the past 5 years."

> "The majority of respondents ( N = 70) have not taught data science in their physics courses in the past 5 years."
