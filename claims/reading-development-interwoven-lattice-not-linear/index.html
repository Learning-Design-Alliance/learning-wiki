Sheppard 2015¶

Sheppard, S. M. (2015). Supporting Research-Based Personalization for Reading Success. Digital Promise Global. https://global.digitalpromise.org

q2 · i? · review · r2

The paper's synthesis of current literacy and learning science research describes the developmental path students take to become confident readers. It states the process "can be conceptualized more as an interwoven lattice" in which literacy skills and social-cognitive skills support each other's growth, with emphasis shifting from decoding toward comprehension.

"While this pathway from developing decoding abilities to comprehending complex texts has skills that follow one upon the other, it is not a simple linear process. Rather, this process can be conceptualized more as an interwoven lattice where language, literacy, cognitive, social, and other environmental factors all interact and develop in a reciprocal manner."
