## Evidence

### Kramer 2024

Kramer, A.-W., Huizenga, H. M., Van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K., & Krabbendam, L. (2024). Do I want to learn today? Day-to-day variations in adolescents' academic motivation and effort. Learning and Motivation, 85. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lmot.2023.101957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian hierarchical diary model on daily Cog-ED indifference points (N = 39, 342 diaries) found a positive within-person competence effect, "β= 0.08, 95% CI = [0.02, 0.13]"; the 95% CI excluded zero.

> "For the model fit on the indifference point, we found a positive within-person effect of daily experienced feelings of competence (β= 0.08, 95% CI = [0.02, 0.13])."

### Kramer 2024 (2)

Kramer, A.-W., Huizenga, H. M., Van Duijvenvoorde, A. C. K., & Krabbendam, L. (2024). Do I want to learn today? Day-to-day variations in adolescents' academic motivation and effort. Learning and Motivation, 85. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lmot.2023.101957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The parallel Bayesian hierarchical model on daily self-reported academic motivation showed a positive within-person competence trend, "β= 0.07, 90% CI = [0.01, 0.13]", significant only at the 90% CI.

> "For the model fit on self-reported academic motivation, we found a positive within-person trend effect of daily experienced feelings of competence (β= 0.07, 90% CI = [0.01, 0.13])"
