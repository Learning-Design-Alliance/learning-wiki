## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The percentage of total URM applicants reviewed rose significantly from 15% (2007–2012) to 23% (2013–2017) after holistic review was implemented (Fisher's exact test, p < 0.0001). [→ Wilson Marenda A. 2019](#wilson-marenda-a-2019)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre–post comparison of reviewed applicant proportions in Table 1 using Fisher's exact tests: "Total URM applicants, %" rose from 15 (128/848) to 23 (301/1331), p < 0.0001; female URM applicants rose from 15 (63/425) to 25 (194/768), p < 0.0001; male URM applicants (p = 0.1497) and international applicants (p = 0.9054) did not change significantly.
