## Evidence

### Mizuno K 2011

Mizuno K, Tanaka M, Fukuda S, Imai-Matsumura K, Watanabe Y. (2011). Relationship between cognitive function and prevalence of decrease in intrinsic academic motivation in adolescents. Behavioral and Brain Functions 7:4. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-7-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 267 Japanese students in grades 4-9. The ANOVA across six grade groups showed a main effect of grade ("F = 3.70, p = .003"), and post-hoc tests showed 7th- and 8th-grade scores significantly lower than 4th-grade scores.
