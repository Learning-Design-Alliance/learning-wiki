### Dünya Baradari 2025

Dünya Baradari, Nataliya Kosmyna, Oscar Petrov, Rebecah Kaplun, and Pattie Maes. 2025. NeuroChat: A Neuroadaptive AI Chatbot for Customizing Learning Experiences. In Proceedings of the 7th ACM Conference on Conversational User Interfaces (CUI '25). https://doi.org/10.1145/3719160.3736623

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear Mixed Model on normalized EEG engagement scores from 24 participants in a counterbalanced within-subjects study comparing NeuroChat to a control GPT chatbot. The article reports "the effect of Condition reached statistical significance (𝛽= 0.216,𝑝= 0.029)"; no standardized effect size is printed.
