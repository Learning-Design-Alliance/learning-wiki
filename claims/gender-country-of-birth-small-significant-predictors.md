## Evidence

### Simpson Paul L 2014

Simpson Paul L, Scicluna Helen A, Jones Philip D, Cole Andrew MD, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter G, Velan Gary, McNeil H Patrick. (2014). Predictive validity of a new integrated selection process for medical school admission. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-86

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression on 2004–2005 cohorts. Gender beta was 0.136 (p = 0.012) for WAM phase 1 and 0.180 (p = 0.002) for program final; East Asian COB had negative betas for clinical skills (e.g. -0.230, p = 0.001, phase 3). Unique variances around 2.7–4.1%.

> "Gender (female, p < 0.01) and COB (p < 0.01, European descent) also significantly predicted clinical skills for phase 1 and 3."
