## Evidence

### Fang J 2018

Fang J, Huang X, Zhang M, Huang F, Li Z and Yuan Q (2018) The Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect on Academic Self-Concept: A Meta-Analysis. Front. Psychol. 9:1569. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01569

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Mixed-effects moderator analysis (main effect Z = −14.56, p < 0.001; Q = 11.07, df = 4, p < 0.05) across 56 effect sizes coded by region, with β = −0.35 for Asia and β = −0.20 for North America. The article attributes the Asian pattern to cultures that highly value academic achievement and emphasize peer competition.

> "Asian students indicate the strongest eﬀect (βAsia = −0.35), while North American students show the weakest eﬀect ( βNorthAmerica = −0.20)"
