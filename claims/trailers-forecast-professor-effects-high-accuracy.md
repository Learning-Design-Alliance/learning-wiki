# Teaching trailers forecast which professors elicit better live-lecture evaluations with very high accuracy (76% of variance, R = .87)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Professors who elicited consensus favourable evaluations and positive affect in trailers also elicited consensus favourable evaluations of live lectures. [→ Gross Jennifer 2015](#gross-jennifer-2015)

## Evidence

### Gross Jennifer 2015

Gross Jennifer, Lakey Brian, Lucas Jessica L, LaCross Ryan, R Plotkowski Andrea, Winegard Bo. (2015). Forecasting the student–professor matches that result in unusually effective teaching. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12049

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Prediction of professor effects (N = 10 professors) from trailer-based consensus ratings; the printed multiple correlation was R = .87. The article reports the two predictors were nearly perfectly correlated, so unique predictive power was not estimated given the limited professor sample.

> "Multiple regression indicated that evaluations and positive affect from trailers predicted 76% of the variance in evaluations of live lectures (R = .87)."
