Evidence¶

Madia 2025¶

Madia, J. E., Gruijters, R. J., Raabe, I. J. & Hübner, N. (2025). Growth mindset and socioeconomic inequality in academic achievement across seventy-three PISA countries. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00365-8

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Total effect from the four-way decomposition of PISA 2022 math scores across 73 countries, averaged over countries with country fixed effects; Table 1 reports TE = -0.587 (SE 0.003).

"Students from lower SES backgrounds score 0.587 standard deviations lower in mathematics compared to their higher SES peers ( p < 0.01)."

Madia 2025 (2)¶

Madia, J. E., Gruijters, R. J., Raabe, I. J. & Hübner, N. (2025). Growth mindset and socioeconomic inequality in academic achievement across seventy-three PISA countries. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00365-8

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Results section comparison across the three PISA subjects from the same decomposition, showing the SES effect operates overwhelmingly through direct pathways rather than through growth mindset.

"The controlled direct effects (CDE) remain dominant across all subjects, accounting for 98% of the total effect in mathematics, 97.7% in science, and 97.8% in reading when closing the sample gap in growth mindset."
