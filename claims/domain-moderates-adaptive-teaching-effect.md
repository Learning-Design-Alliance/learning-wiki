# Subject domain moderates the adaptive teaching effect: benefits in languages, none in STEM or humanities

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Adaptive teaching units outperformed control units in language subjects, with a medium effect in the delayed posttest (β = 0.58), while no differences emerged in STEM or humanities. [→ Sibley 2025](#sibley-2025)
`q3 i2` The condition × domain interaction was significant at both posttests (β = 0.29 immediate; β = 0.30 delayed). [→ Sibley 2025 (2)](#sibley-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Sibley 2025

Sibley, L., Fabian, A., Plicht, C., Pagano, L., Ehrhardt, N., Wellert, L., Bohl, T., & Lachner, A. (2025). Adaptive teaching with technology enhances lasting learning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102141

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Simple-comparison follow-ups to the significant condition × domain interaction in the same cluster-robust models. Languages showed "β = 0.58, p < .001, medium effect" at delay.

> "Additional simple comparisons revealed that the adaptive teaching units were more effective than the control units in languages (immediate posttest: β = 0.41, p = .001, small effect). This effect was even more pronounced in the delayed posttest (β = 0.58, p < .001, medium effect)."

### Sibley 2025 (2)

Sibley, L., Fabian, A., Plicht, C., Pagano, L., Ehrhardt, N., Wellert, L., Bohl, T., & Lachner, A. (2025). Adaptive teaching with technology enhances lasting learning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102141

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus moderation analysis of the same study. The article also reports "no differences among conditions in STEM subjects and humanities (p ≥ .301)."

> "results revealed a significant interaction between condition and domain in both the immediate posttest (β = 0.29, p = .002, small effect) and the delayed posttest (β = 0.30, p = .001, small effect)."
