Evidence¶

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year Two 2018¶

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year Two. (2018). Digital Promise and Maker Education Initiative. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/f800e8da-813a-4de3-a10d-fd50b74646d0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Coding of an open-ended survey question on how maker learning is implemented in schools or districts. The five themes were Space, In Class, Extracurricular, Professional Development, and Beginning, mirroring the previous year's report.

"Mirroring results from last year’s report, the same five maker learning program implementation areas emerged as common themes"

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year Two 2018 (2)¶

Fulfilling the Maker Promise: Year Two. (2018). Digital Promise and Maker Education Initiative. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/f800e8da-813a-4de3-a10d-fd50b74646d0

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Comparison of coded implementation themes across the 2016 and 2017 surveys (Figures 5 and 6). The authors interpret "the emerging balance of making happening in dedicated makerspaces and in classrooms" as suggesting educators are more comfortable integrating making using classroom resources.

"An interesting development since the previous year is the emerging balance of making happening in dedicated makerspaces and in classrooms"
