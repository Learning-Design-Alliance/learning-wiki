Evidence¶

Ngo 2011¶

Ngo, C. M. & Trinh, L. Q. (2011). Lagging Behind Writing Pedagogical Developments: The Impact of Implementing Process-Based Approach on Learners' Writing in a Vietnamese Secondary Education Context. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 1(3), July-September 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071038

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Fifteen-item questionnaire on perceptions of pre-writing, drafting, peer-evaluating and revising, administered pre and post to both groups (reliability .75 and .83). The article reports a significant within-group change with no effect size.

"there is a significant change in participants' perceptions in the experimental condition (F (1, 26) = 9.803, p = .004); the post level (M post = 4.12) is higher than the initial level (M pre = 3.78)"

Ngo 2011 (2)¶

Ngo, C. M. & Trinh, L. Q. (2011). Lagging Behind Writing Pedagogical Developments: The Impact of Implementing Process-Based Approach on Learners' Writing in a Vietnamese Secondary Education Context. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 1(3), July-September 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071038

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Between-group ANOVA on post-questionnaire scores; the article reports significance with the F statistic only, no effect size.

"The post level of participants' perceptions in the experimental condition is significantly higher than that in the control condition (F (1, 55) = 18.73, p = .00)."
