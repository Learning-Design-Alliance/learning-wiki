Evidence¶

Duan 2026¶

Duan, N., Li, L., Lin, G. & Chen, H. (2026). The impact of AI anxiety on career decisions of college students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-37648-y

q3 · i? · associational · r2

SEM path analysis of survey data from 315 Chinese college students. AI anxiety directly predicted career decisions: "β = −0.275, p < 0.01". Career decision was measured with 15 adapted CTI items covering confidence, goal clarity, knowledge satisfaction, and preparedness.

"AI anxiety was negatively associated with Career decisions ( β = −0.275, p < 0.01), indicating that higher AI anxiety levels corresponded to more adverse Career decisions among students."
