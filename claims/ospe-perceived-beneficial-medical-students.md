### Alrashed FA 2026

Alrashed FA, Ahmad T, Alsubiheen AM, Alhammad SA, Aldaihan MM, Albishi AM, Hasan Z. (2026). Medical students’ perspectives on the role of OSPE and OSCE in the educational journey and contribution to career development: A cross-sectional study. Medicine 2026;105:3(e47233). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047233

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 451 first- to fifth-year medical students at King Saud University using a self-administered questionnaire (pilot Cronbach alpha 0.794). The article reports "a chi-square value of 43.5 (P = .000)" for the OSPE benefit rating; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "students believed that OSPE and OSCE were found to be beneficial for medical education and contribution to career development with mean scores (OSPE: very beneficial: mean = 3.55, standard deviation = 0.56) and a chi-square value of 43.5 (P = .000)"
