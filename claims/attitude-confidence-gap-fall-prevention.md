`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of two-item attitude and confidence domains in the cross-sectional survey (N = 384). Attitudes were 'very high' (M = 4.23, Cohen's d = 1.23) while confidence was 'high' (M = 3.60, Cohen's d = 0.64); confidence in intervention selection (M = 3.56) was lower than assessment confidence (M = 3.63).

> "Analysis revealed a significant attitude-confidence gap of 0.63 points (95% CI difference: 0.51–0.75) among physical therapists regarding fall prevention."
