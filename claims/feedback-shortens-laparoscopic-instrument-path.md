## Evidence

### Ahlborg Liv 2015

Ahlborg Liv, Weurlander Maria, Hedman Leif, Nisell Henry, Lindqvist Pelle G., Felländer-Tsai Li, Enochsson Lars. (2015). Individualized feedback during simulated laparoscopic training: a mixed methods study. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.55a2.218b

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized experiment with 16 medical students performing three simulated salpingectomies on the LapSimGyn VR simulator; Mann-Whitney U compared groups. The feedback group's "Right instrument path length was significantly shorter" with medians as printed, p<0.05.

> "Right instrument path length was significantly shorter in the feedback group, in the first (median 5.2 m; IQR: 4.5-6.5) and last (median 3.9 m; IQR: 3.3-4.9) sessions as compared to the control group (median 7.7 m; IQR: 5.8-12.3) and (median 5.9 m; IQR: 5.0-8.1), p<0.05"
