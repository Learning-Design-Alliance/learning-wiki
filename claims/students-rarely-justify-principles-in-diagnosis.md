A. Mason, E. Cohen, E. Yerushalmi, and C. Singh. (2007). Identifying Differences In Diagnostic Skills Between Physics Students: Developing A Rubric. [doi:10.1063/1.3021239](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3021239)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Scoring decision reported in the scoring section of the rubric development study: because justification "was found to occur very rarely" in students' work, the authors concluded students did not think of justification as part of the solution or self-diagnosis procedure. No effect size or frequency count is printed.

> "Eventually, we did not score the "justification" part (row 4), since students’ justification of chosen principles was found to occur very rarely ."
