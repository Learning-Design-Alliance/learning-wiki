# Fifth-year students permanently switched from Tiered to Pass/Fail grading failed more often (3.6% vs 1.1%)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After the 2018 reform switched all fifth-year exams from Tiered grades to Pass/Fail, the fail rate rose from 1.1% to 3.6% (P<.001). [→ Modrau B 2025](#modrau-b-2025)

## Evidence

### Modrau B 2025

Modrau B, Kirk KF, Said SMA, Bjarkam CR, Sunde L, Bodilsen J, Dal J, Kristensen JK, Emmersen J, Astorp MB, Andersen S. (2025). Pass/Fail Versus Tiered Grades and Academic Performance in Undergraduate Medical Education: Crossover Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/74975

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Before-after comparison within the same fifth-year modules after the 2018 reform changed grading from Tiered to Pass/Fail while keeping learning objectives and teaching unaltered: "3.6% (92/2550)" versus "1.1% (13/1227)", χ2 P<.001.

> "In the subgroup of 5th year students switching permanently from Tiered grade to Pass/Fail (lower boxes in Figure 1), the frequency of students failing exams was 3.6% (92/2550) with Pass/Fail and 1.1% (13/1227) with Tiered grade assessments (χ2 test, P<.001)."
