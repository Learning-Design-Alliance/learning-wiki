## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Five years after the intervention, the intervention group significantly outperformed the control group on word decoding (d = 0.37), with no significant differences on the other measures. [→ Wolff 2016](#wolff-2016)

## Evidence

### Wolff 2016

Ulrika Wolff. (2016). Effects of a Randomized Reading Intervention Study Aimed at 9-Year-Olds: A 5-Year Follow-up. Dyslexia, 22, 85–100. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1529

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Grade 8 follow-up (intervention n = 47; control n = 37) of the randomized grade-3 intervention, analyzed with ANCOVA controlling for grade-3 scores. Word decoding showed a significant group difference (F = 7.35, p = .01, d = 0.37); the article calls this "the only difference ... that reached signiﬁcance".

> "The only difference between the intervention group and the control group that reached signiﬁcance was on word decoding."
