This article reports, citing Fox et al. (reference 17), results from in-depth interviews with 26 people at 21 quantum companies. The article attributes these percentages to that study; its design is not described here beyond the qualitative interviews.

> "The authors found that most companies had job roles categorised as “engineer” (95% of companies) and “ex- perimental physicist” (86%), and about half had em- ployees working as “theorists” (57%) and “technicians” (43%)."
