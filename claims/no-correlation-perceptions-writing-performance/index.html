Ngo 2011¶

Ngo, C. M. & Trinh, L. Q. (2011). Lagging Behind Writing Pedagogical Developments: The Impact of Implementing Process-Based Approach on Learners' Writing in a Vietnamese Secondary Education Context. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 1(3), July-September 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071038

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pearson correlation tests on post-study questionnaire and writing-test scores for each group; neither correlation was significant. The article concludes the constructs were "not compatibly developing".

"after the study, there is no correlation between participants' perceptions and their writing performance for both control and experimental groups (N = 30, r = .076; p = .689 and N = 27, r = .246; p = .217 respectively)"
