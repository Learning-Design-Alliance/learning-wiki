# Collaborative disimpaction with vaginal push-up assistance changes the force profile: the abdominal operator applies higher forces while the assisting vaginal operator applies lower forces

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The abdominal operator receiving push-up assistance applied higher maximum and time-averaged forces than the independent-operator medians on multiple sensors. [→ Parris Dawn 2025](#parris-dawn-2025)

## Evidence

### Parris Dawn 2025

Parris Dawn, Xue Yang, Navaseelan Thurga, Salvadores Fernandez Carmen, Javidan Maryam, Gupta Rohit, Salaris Nikolaos, Chen Jeremy, Tiwari Manish K., Siassakos Dimitrios. (2025). Characterising Forces Applied During the Simulated Management of Impacted Fetal Head: Pre‐Clinical Methods Study. BJOG: An International Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.70107

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single assisted disimpaction (participant 3 assisted by participant 4's vaginal push-up), analysed separately from group statistics because the configuration differed; forces were "higher... compared to the group median maximum forces" on the sensors listed.

> "Participant 3 applied higher forces on sensors 4 (15.94 N vs. 12.04 N), 5 (21.99 N vs. 21.70 N), 7 (5.38 N vs. 4.41 N), 11 (22 N vs. 17.56 N) and 12 (21.98 N vs. 7.35 N) compared to the group median maximum forces recorded by participants who completed the procedure independently."

### Parris Dawn 2025 (2)

Parris Dawn, Xue Yang, Navaseelan Thurga, Salvadores Fernandez Carmen, Javidan Maryam, Gupta Rohit, Salaris Nikolaos, Chen Jeremy, Tiwari Manish K., Siassakos Dimitrios. (2025). Characterising Forces Applied During the Simulated Management of Impacted Fetal Head: Pre‐Clinical Methods Study. BJOG: An International Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.70107

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The assisting participant's glove data showed generally lower forces than independent-operator medians; the article notes exceptions at sensors 3, 5, 9 and 12, and that the assisting participant's time-averaged forces at all sensors were below 5.2 N.

> "In contrast, participant 4 who performed the vaginal push-up generally applied lower maximum and time-averaged forces than the median values recorded from those performing disimpaction independently."
