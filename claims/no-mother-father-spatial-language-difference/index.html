Farran 2025¶

Farran, E. K., Fink, E., Hughes, C., Gilligan-Lee, K. A., & The NewFAMS team. (2025). Limited sex differences in spatial language in parent-child dyads. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102006. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102006

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Within the MANOVA (N = 104), the significant omnibus parent-sex effect was not supported by follow-up ANOVAs: quantity "F (1,102) = 1.25, p = .266, ηp2 = .012"; quality "F (1, 102) = 2.38, p = .13, ηp2 = .023". The authors did not consider it further.

"follow up ANOVAs demonstrated nonsignificant effects of parent sex for each dependent variable: quantity: F (1,102) = 1.25, p = .266, ηp 2 = .012; quality: F (1, 102) = 2.38, p = .13, ηp 2 = .023, and so this main effect is not considered further."
