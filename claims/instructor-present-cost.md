## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wickens 2013

Wickens, C. D., Hutchins, S., Carolan, T., & Cumming, J. (2013). Effectiveness of Part-Task Training and Increasing-Difficulty Training Strategies: A Meta-Analysis Approach. Human Factors, 55(2), 461-470. https://doi.org/10.1177/0018720812451994

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Delivery-environment moderator analysis within the ID meta-analysis. ID "produced a benefit when there was no instructor present (TR = 1.38, k† = 23, p < .10; g = +0.42*, k† = 10)" and a cost with an instructor; printed effect sizes g = +0.42 and g = –0.48 are medium.

> "ID produced a benefit when there was no instructor present (TR = 1.38, k† = 23, p < .10; g = +0.42*, k† = 10) and, generally, a cost when the instructor was present (TR = 0.89, k† = 7; g = –0.48*, k† = 5)."

### Wickens 2013 (2)
