## Evidence

### Isaac Ali Kwasu; Ema Ema 2015

Isaac Ali Kwasu; Ema Ema. (2015). Effectiveness of Animated Instructional Resource for Learning Facilitation among Secondary School Student in Bauchi Nigeria. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.21. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079103

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment in three Bauchi schools: 189 questionnaires each to experimental (AIR DVD) and control (oral teaching) groups, 63 per location, analysed with means, standard deviations and Z-tests. The summary table prints Z-values of -4.95, -8.286 and -6.76 with p-values 0.001, 0.000 and 0.0001, all "less tha n a significant level of 0.05".

> "From the above table, it can be seen that the P-Val ues in each of the three locations are all less tha n a significant level of 0.05, implying that students’ performance in each case differ significantly between the three zones."
