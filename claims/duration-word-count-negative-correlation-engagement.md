## Evidence

### Tolba 2025

Tolba, Kendall, Tudball Smith, Gregg, Vo, Wordley. (2025). An Open Workflow Model for Improving Educational Video Design: Tools, Data, and Insights. Proceedings of AAEE 2025, The University of Queensland, Australia. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.16254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.16254)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis in the exploratory data analysis stage of the case study of lecture videos from two engineering courses, correlating each video feature with average percentage viewed. Printed effect sizes: duration r = −0.23 and word count r = −0.22, described as "the strongest negative correlations with engagement", with all |r| < 0.25.

> "video duration (𝑟 = −0.23) and word count (𝑟 = −0.22) showed the strongest negative correlations with engagement, suggesting that longer and more verbose videos are generally associated with lower viewer engagement."
