### Pers Michał 2026

Pers Michał, Kupis Robert, Kononowicz Andrzej A., Cebula Grzegorz, Szopa Magdalena. (2026). Linear virtual patients versus student role-plays in teaching patient-centeredness: a mixed-method study. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.6a0d.902f

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Spearman rank-order correlations between satisfaction and PPOS scores among 106 students; all associations were non-significant, indicating satisfaction with the teaching method is independent of patient-centered attitudes.

> "Correlation analyses did not reveal statistically significant associations between satisfaction and any of the Patient–Practitioner Orientation Scale (PPOS) scores, including Sharing (r = .15, p = .12), Caring (r = .12, p = .20), and Total PPOS (r = .14, p = .14)."
