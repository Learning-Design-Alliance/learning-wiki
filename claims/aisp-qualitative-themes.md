Baraza W., Staples G., Wells C., Barbier L., Tonga I., Singh P., Puttick M., Webster C. (2026). Artificially Intelligent Simulated Patients in Undergraduate Surgical Teaching: A Single Blinded Randomized Controlled Study. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70486

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis of two free-text questions from the 14 intervention participants, following Thomas's general inductive approach. The article reports "Three themes emerged from the free text responses, these being performance, learning and interaction." Participants described the interaction as "less stressful" and the AISP as "realistic" and "non-judgmental".
