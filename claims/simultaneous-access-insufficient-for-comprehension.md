## Subclaims
`q3 i?` LiquidText Access and Non-Guidance conditions did not significantly outperform the Adobe Reader control on comprehension. [→ Jordan Lombard 2021](#jordan-lombard-2021)

## Evidence

### Jordan Lombard 2021

Jordan Lombard, Ivar Bråten, Cécile van de Leemput, Franck Amadieu. (2021). Performance and acceptance when using tablets as a multiple document learning tool: do application and guidance matter? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09537-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc REGWF comparisons after a significant omnibus ANOVA, F(3, 84) = 7.46, p < .001, showed no significant comprehension differences among LiquidText Access, LiquidText Non-Guidance, and the control condition. Functions available without guidance gave no advantage over sequential reading.

> "whereas there were no statistically significant differences (p > .05) between the three last mentioned conditions"
