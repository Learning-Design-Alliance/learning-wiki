## Evidence

### Mårtensson S 2026

Mårtensson S, Eckerström J, Knez R, Sörman K, Larsson M. (2026). Tell us what you really think: undergraduate nursing students validation of the Questionnaire Virtual Patient - a qualitative think-aloud study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09738-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the think-aloud cognitive interviews, students expressed uncertainty about terms like "digital competence, navigation and instructions" and the abbreviation VP. The authors revised affected questions, e.g. writing out "virtual patient" in full and specifying technological contexts in RQ1 through RQ4.

> "the result reflects nuanced perception of inconsistency in the interpretation of technological terminology. For example, the terminology related to digital competence, navigation and instructions elicited uncertainties, mainly due to lack of specificity regarding the technological context."
