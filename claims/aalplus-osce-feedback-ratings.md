## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Hundertmark Jan, Fahrbach-Veeser Johanna, Eicher Christiane, Tonchev Pencho, Kurczyk Sonia, Szecsenyi Joachim, Loukanova Svetla. (2020). The AaLplus near-peer teaching program in Family Medicine strengthens basic medical skills—A five-year retrospective study. PLoS ONE 15(5): e0233748. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233748

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-OSCE evaluation on 5-point Likert scales administered directly after the OSCE across the study years; the study prints station means of 4.69–4.76 for feedback helpfulness and mean 4.90 (SD = 0.37) for tutors as assessors.

> "They rated the feedback they received after the OSCE stations as “very helpful” (anamnesis mean 4.76, SD = 0.50, n = 1231; venepuncture mean 4.74, SD = 0.60, n = 1233; physical examination mean 4.69, SD = 0.70, n = 1204)."
