Interview finding on financial support: the most cited enrollment reason was affordability, but "Multiple apprentices found the pay so insufficient that they or their peers had to work second or third jobs," and some experienced pay cuts when transitioning from paraprofessional roles.

> "Multiple apprentices found the pay so insufficient that they or their peers had to work second or third jobs, underscoring the difficulty of maintaining financial stability while managing the intense demands of their job-embedded apprenticeship."
