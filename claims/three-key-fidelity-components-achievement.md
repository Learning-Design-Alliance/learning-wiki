Barbara Means, Julie Neisler, Daniel Parker, Vanessa Peters Hinton, and Emily Pressler. (2026). Lessons From Implementing Courseware in Gateway Statistics Courses. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/12bb84f5-0073-40e6-85bb-41561222891b

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of implementation rates for individual fidelity elements between classes with positive vs. negative grade residuals. Three components showed "moderate-to-large differences"; consistent enactment of all three was associated with "grades about 0.35 points higher than expected."

> "Students of instructors who consistently enacted all three of these key implementation components had course-level grades about 0.35 points higher than expected based on their prior achievement."
