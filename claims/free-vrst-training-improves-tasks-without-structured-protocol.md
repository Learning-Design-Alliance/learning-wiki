## Evidence

### Sparn Moritz B. 2024

Sparn Moritz B., Teixeira Hugo, Chatziisaak Dimitrios, Schmied Bruno, Hahnloser Dieter, Bischofberger Stephan. (2024). Virtual reality simulation training in laparoscopic surgery – does it really matter, what simulator to use? Results of a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 24, 589. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05574-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of their before-after comparison of 135 entry and 127 exit examinations. Training consisted of entry exam, two hours of free training, and return visits; the study did not evaluate proficiency-based progression due to limited time.

> "In this study, we show that free training can indeed lead to relevant improvements in basic surgical tasks and in more complex exercises, regardless of the simulator used."
