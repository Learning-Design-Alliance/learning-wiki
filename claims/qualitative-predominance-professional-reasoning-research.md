# Qualitative studies predominate among empirical research on professional reasoning in occupational therapy (48.3% of empirical articles)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among the 149 empirical indexed-journal articles, qualitative articles (n = 72; 48.3%) exceed quantitative, mixed, and review articles. [→ Márquez-Álvarez L.-J. 2019](#márquez-álvarez-l-j-2019)
`q2 i?` Fisher's exact test shows a significant relationship between qualitative methodology and publications in 1993–2003 (p < 0.01) and between quantitative methodology and 2004–2014 (p < 0.05). [→ Márquez-Álvarez L.-J. 2019 (2)](#márquez-álvarez-l-j-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Márquez-Álvarez L.-J. 2019

Márquez-Álvarez L.-J., Calvo-Arenillas J.-I., Talavera-Valverde M.-Á., Moruno-Millares P. (2019). Professional Reasoning in Occupational Therapy: A Scoping Review. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/6238245

`q2 · i?`

Descriptive methodological analysis of the 149 empirical articles published in indexed journals, summarised in Table 4. Phenomenological designs are the largest qualitative category (n = 26; 17.7%), followed by ethnographic (n = 20; 13.6%).

> "Overall, the percentage of qualitative articles published n = 72 (48.3%) exceeds the percentage of quantitative articles, mixed articles, and reviews."

### Márquez-Álvarez L.-J. 2019 (2)

Márquez-Álvarez L.-J., Calvo-Arenillas J.-I., Talavera-Valverde M.-Á., Moruno-Millares P. (2019). Professional Reasoning in Occupational Therapy: A Scoping Review. Occupational Therapy International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/6238245

`q2 · i?`

Inferential analysis (Fisher's exact test, applied because some categories had n < 5) of major design approaches across the first three periods (n = 127). The test associates qualitative methodology with 1993–2003 and quantitative methodology with 2004–2014.

> "We can confirm that there is a significant relationship between qualitative methodology and publications during the years 1993 to 2003 (p < 0.01) and between quantitative methodology and publications with respect to the period 2004-2014 (p < 0.05)."
