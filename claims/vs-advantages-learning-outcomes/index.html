Evidence¶

Zhang Guoyong 2026¶

Zhang Guoyong, Sun Xianghong, Yang Dan, Shao Qinwen. (2026). Nursing students’ experiences in virtual simulation teaching: a meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1863553

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Synthesized finding across the 17 qualitative studies; students described learning more from mistakes than from correct answers and rethinking which aspects they had not fulfilled after seeing feedback marks.

"The repetitive practice and timely error-correction feedback mechanisms of VS systems enhance students’ learning outcomes and accelerate the skill acquisition process."

Zhang Guoyong 2026 (2)¶

Zhang Guoyong, Sun Xianghong, Yang Dan, Shao Qinwen. (2026). Nursing students’ experiences in virtual simulation teaching: a meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1863553

q2 · i? · review · r3

Synthesized professional-cognition finding: students approached virtual patients from the standpoint of prospective nurses, deliberating on intervention strategies rather than executing tasks mechanically, as reflected in student quotes about feeling professional responsibility for the virtual patient.

"This process deepens their understanding of nursing practice and enhances their professional accountability."
