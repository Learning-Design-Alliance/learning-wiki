### James Soland 2021

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld, Joseph Rios. (2021). Comparing different response time threshold setting methods to detect low effort on a large-scale assessment. Large-scale Assessments in Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40536-021-00100-w

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The abstract characterizes the research gap: prior comparisons "typically do so using simulated or small-scale data", and large-scale studies often were not from a CAT, used only a handful of items, or examined methods incomprehensively.

> "While other studies have compared the efficacy of different threshold-setting methods, they typically do so using simulated or small-scale data."
