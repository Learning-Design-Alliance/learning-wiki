Hammerstein Svenja 2021¶

Hammerstein Svenja, König Christoph, Dreisörner Thomas, Frey Andreas. (2021). Effects of COVID-19-Related School Closures on Student Achievement-A Systematic Review. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.746289

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis of the three positive-effect studies (Meeter, +0.20 SD mathematics; Spitzer and Musslick, −2.43% error rate; van der Velde, +0.25 SD French). No standardized effect size is printed for the pooled positive pattern, so impact is null.

"Three studies reported positive effects of COVID-19 related school closures on student achievement."

Hammerstein Svenja 2021 (2)¶

Hammerstein Svenja, König Christoph, Dreisörner Thomas, Frey Andreas. (2021). Effects of COVID-19-Related School Closures on Student Achievement-A Systematic Review. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.746289

q2 · i? · review · r3

Authors' interpretation (type e) of the three positive-effect studies: students had used the software before closures, increased its use at home, were less distracted, and received individualized assignments. This is the review's proposed explanation, not a tested result.

"Thus, the positive effects may be explained by the students under investigation being familiar working with the corresponding online-learning software prior to school closures."
