## Evidence

### Sivaram and Ramar 2014

R.T. Sivaram, R. Ramar. (2014). Effect of CAI on Achievement of LD Students in English. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 4, No. 1, January-March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068387

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test comparison of 25 control-group LD students (mean 23.2) and 25 normal-group students (mean 51.6); Table 4 reports a t-value of 14.29, significant at the 0.01 level. The normal group was also taught by lecture.

> "There is significant difference in the post-test performance between the control group LD students and the normal group students."
