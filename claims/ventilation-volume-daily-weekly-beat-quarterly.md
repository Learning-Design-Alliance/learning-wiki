### Oermann Marilyn H. 2020

Oermann Marilyn H., Krusmark Michael A., Kardong-Edgren Suzan, Jastrzembski Tiffany S., Gluck Kevin A. (2020). Training interval in cardiopulmonary resuscitation. PLoS ONE 15(1): e0226786. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0226786

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary ventilation outcome in the randomized trial: adequate-volume improvement was not significant for the quarterly interval, and session 4 contrasts favored daily and weekly over quarterly ("t = 2.90... p = .020"; "t = 3.06... p = .012"). No effect size printed.

> "By session 4 there were significant differences between the daily and quarterly (t = 2.90, df = 1440, p = .020), weekly and quarterly (t = 3.06, df = 1447, p = .012), but not among the others."
