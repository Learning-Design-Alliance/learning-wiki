`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Workshop discussion finding on professional development. Participants noted many new programs are funded for three years and tied to one location, serving cohorts of "no more than 10–100 educators," with difficulty scaling beyond the initial funding period.

> "There was clear consensus that individual or small programs most likely cannot deploy PD either at scale or for multiple years without the sustained scaffolding a center could provide."

Workshop discussion on PD incentives: in K-12, compensated PD is standard practice and can cost "anywhere between a few hundred dollars and several thousand per participant," while undergraduate educators more commonly receive conference travel support.

> "In all cases, workshop attendees noted the need for incentives, including financial and travel support, for educators participating in professional development."
