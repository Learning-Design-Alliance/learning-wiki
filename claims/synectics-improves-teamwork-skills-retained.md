## Evidence

### de Villiers Scheepers 2015

de Villiers Scheepers, M. J. & Maree, L. (2015). Fostering Team Creativity in Higher Education Settings. e-Journal of Business Education & Scholarship of Teaching, 9(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1167371

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA in a quasi-experimental pre-, post-, post-post-test design with 54 final-year students who completed all questionnaires. Teamwork means "differed significantly over the stickiness stages" with multivariate partial eta squared=.70. After the workshop the mean rose to 4.337a and three months later was 4.157a, a non-significant decline.

> "Teamwork means, shown in Table 3 differed significantly over the stickiness stages [Wilks Lambda=0.30, F(3, 44) =36.580, p<0.0005, multivariate partial eta squared=.70]"
