Daniel Starch and Edward C. Elliott. (1911). Reliability of the Grading of High-School Work in English. The School Review. http://www.journals.uchicago.edu/t-and-c

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of marks from schools with 150 pupils or less (Figs. 12 and 14, medians 89.5 and 82.0) with schools of 151 or more (Figs. 13 and 15, medians 86.8 and 80.3). The article concludes smaller schools "probably indicate a real tendency" toward more liberal grading.

> "The smaller schools graded paper A 2.7 points higher and paper B 1.7 points higher than the larger schools. Although this difference is small, it appears in both papers, and probably indicates a real tendency among smaller schools to grade more liberally."
