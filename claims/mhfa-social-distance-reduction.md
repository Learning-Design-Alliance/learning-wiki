## Evidence

### Reavley Nicola J. 2018

Reavley Nicola J., Morgan Amy J., Fischer Julie-Anne, Kitchener Betty, Bovopoulos Nataly, Jorm Anthony F. (2018). Effectiveness of eLearning and blended modes of delivery of Mental Health First Aid training in the workplace: randomised controlled trial. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-018-1888-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Primary outcome in the 608-participant RCT, measured with the Social Distance Scale on depression and PTSD vignettes and analysed with mixed-effects models; odds ratios favouring eLearning MHFA over control were 5.30 (depression) and 4.99 (PTSD), while blended-vs-control CIs crossed 1.

> "Those in the eLearning MHFA group were significantly more likely to show a reduced desire for social distance from a person with depression or PTSD than those in the PFA eLearning group. The reduction in social distance in the blended group was not significantly greater than the PFA eLearning group."
