# MFC under blended teaching decodes to the original global knowledge structure but with larger individual variance than TPJ under lecturing alone

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Brain decoding during LH lecturing recovered the original knowledge structure from MFC activity, and predicted structures showed significantly larger individual variance (lower pISC) in MFC-LH than TPJ-H-only. [→ X. Feng 2025](#x-feng-2025)

## Evidence

### X. Feng 2025

X. Feng, X. Xu, Z. Meng, J. Jiang, M. Pei, Y. Zheng, C. Lu. (2025). A Rapid Cortical Learning Process Supporting Students’ Knowledge Construction During Real Classroom Teaching. Advanced Science. https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202416610

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Leave-one-dyad-out decoding plus inter-subject pattern correlation (pISC) analysis during lecturing. Decoding succeeded in MFC-LH (d = 1.024) and TPJ-H-only (d = 0.806) with no difference between them; the pISC comparison showed "Cohen's d = 0.636".

> "the results showed significantly larger individual variance in the MFC of the LH condition than in the TPJ of the H‐only condition (paired sample t‐test: t (20) = 2.914, p = 0.009, Cohen's d = 0.636"
