## Evidence

### Austin 2017

Austin, C. R., Vaughn, S., & McClelland, A. M. (2017). Intensive Reading Interventions for Inadequate Responders in Grades K–3: A Synthesis. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(4), 191–210. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714446

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

Study in Table 6 of the synthesis: grade 1 inadequate responders received an intensified one-on-one Tier 3 intervention (30 min, 5×/week, 7 weeks) targeting letter–sound correspondence, sight words, phonemic awareness, decoding, and fluency; the comparison group "continued to receive Tier 2 intervention without intensification". All printed effects favored control.

> "Treatment < Control: Sight Word Efficiency: d = −0.49; Phonemic Decoding Efficiency: d = −0.57; WRMT-R Word Attack: d = −0.35; WRMT-R Word Identification: d = −0.60"
