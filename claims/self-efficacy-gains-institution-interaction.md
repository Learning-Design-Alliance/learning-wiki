## Evidence

### Reeves Todd D. 2018

Reeves Todd D., Hake Laura E., Chen Xinnian, Frederick Jennifer, Rudenga Kristin, Ludlow Larry H., O'Connor Clare M. (2018). Does Context Matter? Convergent and Divergent Findings in the Cross-Institutional Evaluation of Graduate Teaching Assistant Professional Development Programs. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0044

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of GTA self-efficacy across three institutions found a significant time-by-institution interaction, F(2, 55) = 9.92, p < 0.01, ηp2 = 0.27; there was no main effect of institution.

> "However, the interaction between time and institution was significant, F(2, 55) = 9.92, p < 0.01, ηp2 = 0.27, which implies differential growth by institution."

### Reeves Todd D. 2018 (2)

Reeves Todd D., Hake Laura E., Chen Xinnian, Frederick Jennifer, Rudenga Kristin, Ludlow Larry H., O'Connor Clare M. (2018). Does Context Matter? Convergent and Divergent Findings in the Cross-Institutional Evaluation of Graduate Teaching Assistant Professional Development Programs. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-03-0044

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Site-level dependent-means effect sizes from Table 3: University C d = 0.68, University A d = 0.11, University B d = 0.02. University C GTAs were incoming first-year students with no prior GTA teaching experience.

> "However, GTAs at University C reported 0.68 of a SD change in self-efficacy from immediately before to immediately after the workshop, whereas GTAs at University B reported almost no change."
