Jordan Gutterman 2026¶

Jordan Gutterman, Ashish Gurung, Lee Branstetter, Kenneth Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Cross-Subject Predictive Validity for Learning Outcomes of Delayed Start Behavior. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25308

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Mixture modeling of the delayed start distribution (mean 8.96 minutes, tail to 27.6): the "two-cluster model (BIC = 4076.1) performs best"; clusters held n=426 (60%, centered 6.0 min, SD 2.1) and n=285 (40%, mean 12.1 min, SD 4.4), intersecting at 9.0 minutes.

"A two-cluster model (BIC = 4076.1) performs best, outperforming both three-cluster (BIC = 4090.0), and unimodal (BIC = 4196.6) models."
