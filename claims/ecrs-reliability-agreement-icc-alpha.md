## Evidence

### Terregino Carol A. 2019

Terregino Carol A., Copeland H. Liesel, Sarfaty Suzanne C., Lantz-Gefroh Valeri, Hoffmann-Longtin Krista. (2019). Development of an empathy and clarity rating scale to measure the effect of medical improv on end-of-first-year OCSE performance: a pilot study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2019.1666537

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability study using archived videos of third-year students in a capstone OSCE station; two clinician educators and one actor rated 20 videos, with agreement counted within one scale point.

> "We determined that the percent agreement for ECRS between the three raters across the 140 agreement points (seven items for 20 videos) was 84%."

### Terregino Carol A. 2019 (2)

Terregino Carol A., Copeland H. Liesel, Sarfaty Suzanne C., Lantz-Gefroh Valeri, Hoffmann-Longtin Krista. (2019). Development of an empathy and clarity rating scale to measure the effect of medical improv on end-of-first-year OCSE performance: a pilot study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2019.1666537

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same 20-video reliability dataset: ICC on mean ratings across all seven items under a two-way random effects model was .527; Cronbach alpha for internal consistency was .948.

> "The intraclass correlation coefficient estimating reliability of raters' summed score was .527. The Cronbach alpha estimating internal consistency of ECRS was .948."
