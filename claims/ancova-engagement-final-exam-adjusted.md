### Atharva Dange 2026

Atharva Dange, Ramon E. Lopez, Nimish Shah, Louis Deslauriers. (2026). aiPlato: A Novel AI Tutoring and Stepwise Feedback System for Physics Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

ANCOVA on final exam scores adjusting for Exam 1 score, pre-aiPlato homework average (Homework 0-4), and pre-aiPlato quiz average (Quizzes 1-2). The article reports engagement "remained a statistically significant predictor of final exam scores" after adjustment.

> "Taken together, these results indicate that higher levels of engagement with aiPlato were associated with higher final exam performance, even after adjusting for measured prior academic performance."

### Atharva Dange 2026 (2)

Atharva Dange, Ramon E. Lopez, Nimish Shah, Louis Deslauriers. (2026). aiPlato: A Novel AI Tutoring and Stepwise Feedback System for Physics Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r?`

The article converts the covariate-adjusted high-versus-low difference into a printed effect size of Cohen's d of approximately 0.81 standard deviations over four assignments, quoting that "an effect size (Cohen'sd) of approximately 0.81 standard deviations" was observed, contextualized against benchmark intervention ranges in Fig. 9.

> "The observed difference of 13.86 percent- age points corresponds to an effect size (Cohen'sd) of approximately 0.81 standard deviations over four assignments"

### Atharva Dange 2026 (3)

Atharva Dange, Ramon E. Lopez, Nimish Shah, Louis Deslauriers. (2026). aiPlato: A Novel AI Tutoring and Stepwise Feedback System for Physics Homework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.09965)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r?`

Same ANCOVA model: Exam 1 score "emerged as a strong predictor of end-of-semester performance" while "pre-aiPlato homework and quiz averages were not statistically significant predictors." The authors attribute this to the measures' restricted scope and timing.

> "Among the covariates, Exam 1 score emerged as a strong predictor of end-of-semester performance, consistent with established findings in physics education research (Jensen & Barron, 2014; Meghji et al., 2023; Pittman, Titus, & Williams, 2021). In contrast, pre-aiPlato homework and quiz averages were not statistically significant predictors."
