## Evidence

### Zou Ya 2026

Zou Ya, Xie Wenzhao, Huang Hui, Liu Dan, Zhou Qing, Yi Qifeng, Tong Xiaoliang, Mao Ping. (2026). The effects of case-based, problem-based, and team-based learning on nursing students’ problem-solving, self-directed learning, and communication skills: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08602-3

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by teaching method for communication skills (Table 6). TBL's effect is significant while PBL's is not; the authors attribute TBL's advantage to "its emphasis on real-time team interaction and feedback".

> "Significant intervention effects were observed in TBL (SMD = 1.94, 95% CI: 0.26–3.62, I2 = 98%, P = 0.02) but not in PBL (SMD = 1.69, 95% CI: -0.45-3.83, I2 = 96%, P = 0.12)"
