## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Scores on Pre-test Question 2 (whether changing basis affects measurement probabilities) rose significantly from the tutorial pre-test to the exam in both semesters within each semester's group and without a comparison group (p < 0.01; V = 0.39 and V = 0.21), with pre-test scores already fairly high (65% and 78%).
