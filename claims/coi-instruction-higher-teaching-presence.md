# CoI-based online instruction produced significantly higher teaching presence perceptions than direct instruction

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Students in the CoI-based experimental group reported significantly higher teaching presence than control students (η2 = .121, a medium effect). [→ Aktı-Aslan 2021](#akt-aslan-2021)

## Evidence

### Aktı-Aslan 2021

Aktı-Aslan, S. & Turgut, Y. E. (2021). Effectiveness of community of inquiry based online course: Cognitive, social and teaching presence. Journal of Pedagogical Research, 5(3), 187-197. https://doi.org/10.33902/JPR.2021371365

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test in the quasi-experiment showed the experimental group's teaching presence mean (47.66) exceeded the control's (44.49), with η2 = .121, which the article interprets as a medium effect of the CoI-based activities.

> "A significant difference was observed between the groups ( 𝑡𝑡(79) = 3.294;  𝑝𝑝 < .05). According to the results, the students in the experimental group had higher scores  (𝑋𝑋 = 47.66) than the students in the control group (𝑋𝑋 = 44.49). The eta square value (η2) was calculated as .121."
