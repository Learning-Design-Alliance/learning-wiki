# Non-selective acceptance of all eight AI suggestions was less frequent in the justification condition (15.6%) than in the selection-only (30.8%) and batch (32.3%) conditions

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yan Tao 2026

Yan Tao, Jennifer Meyer & René F. Kizilcec. (2026). Think Before You Accept: Can Written Justification Reduce Uncritical Uptake of AI Writing Suggestions? Preprint. Under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square tests on non-selective acceptance (accepting all eight suggestions) across conditions in the embedded randomized experiment: 10 of 64 (15.6%) in justification, 20 of 65 (30.8%) in selection-only, and 20 of 62 (32.3%) in the batch baseline.

> "Non-selective acceptance was less frequent in the justification condition, with evidence of a significant difference compared to the batch condition (χ2(1) = 3.842,p=.050) and a similar directional pattern compared to the selection-only condition (χ2(1) = 3.339,p=.068)."
