## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within the intervention group, psychological empowerment impact (p = 0.005), total structural empowerment (p = 0.008), formal power (p = 0.022) and informal power (p = 0.011) improved over time. [→ Nilsson Annika 2015](#nilsson-annika-2015)

## Evidence

### Nilsson Annika 2015

Nilsson Annika, Engström Maria. (2015). E-assessment and an e-training program among elderly care staff lacking formal competence: results of a mixed-methods intervention study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-0843-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group comparisons in the intervention study using Spreitzer's Empowerment Scale and the CWEQ II; the impact factor rose from 2010 to 2011 at "p = 0.005" (n = 26), and structural empowerment total, formal power and informal power also improved significantly. No effect sizes were printed.

> "Staff in the intervention group gave significantly higher scores, over time, for the factor "impact" on the psychological empowerment scale (2010 Mean = 4.4, SD = 1.3 vs. 2011 Mean = 5.1 SD = 0.8; p = 0.005; n = 26)."

### Nilsson Annika 2015 (2)

Nilsson Annika, Engström Maria. (2015). E-assessment and an e-training program among elderly care staff lacking formal competence: results of a mixed-methods intervention study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-0843-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparisons of change scores between intervention (n = 26) and comparison groups; psychological empowerment total, self-determination and impact all favoured the intervention group significantly, while "Regarding Structural empowerment, no significant differences were found over time between the groups." No effect sizes were printed.

> "When differences over time were compared between the groups, there were significant differences for the scale Psychological empowerment (intervention group Mean differences = 0.3, SD = 0.6; n = 26 vs. comparison group Mean differences = −0.2, SD = 0.7; p = 0.033) as well as for the factors "self-determination" (intervention group Mean differences = 0.3, SD = 1.0; n = 26 vs. comparison group Mean differences = −0.4, SD = 1.4; p = 0.042) and "impact" (intervention group Mean differences = 0.7, SD = 1.0; n = 26 vs. comparison group Mean differences = −0.4, SD = 0.8; p = 0.003)."
