q2 · i? · causal · r?

The white paper's own description of its quasi-experimental design: a state-policy analysis drawing on IPEDS data in which the effects of articulation agreements on the specified student outcomes are estimated with a Difference-in-Difference (DiD) approach. No results are printed on this page.

"This study measures the effect of adopting statewide articulation agreement policies on college enrollment, associate degree attainment, and bachelor’s degree attainment using data from the Integrated Postsecondary Educational Data Systems (IPEDS). The effects of articulation agreements on the specified student outcomes are estimated using a Difference-in-Difference (DiD) approach."
