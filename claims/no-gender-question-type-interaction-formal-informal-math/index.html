Evidence¶

Kersey 2018¶

Kersey, A. J., Braham, E. J., Csumitta, K. D., Libertus, M. E., & Cantlon, J. F. (2018). No intrinsic gender differences in children's earliest numerical abilities. npj Science of Learning, 3, 12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0028-7

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA on a subset of 275 TEMA-3-tested children who answered at least four formal and four informal questions. "no interaction between gender and question type nor did we ﬁnd a main effect of gender"; question type had a strong main effect (F = 235.98, p < 0.0001).

"We found no interaction between gender and question type nor did we ﬁnd a main effect of gender (Fig. 6; Gender: F(1, 207) = 0.56, p = 0.46; Question Type: F(1, 207) = 235.98, p < 0.0001; Gender × Question Type: F(1, 207) = 0.30, p = 0.58)"
