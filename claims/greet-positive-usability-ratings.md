## Evidence

### Phillips Anna C. 2016

Phillips Anna C., Lewis Lucy K., McEvoy Maureen P., Galipeau James, Glasziou Paul, Moher David, Tilson Julie K., Williams Marie T. (2016). Development and validation of the guideline for reporting evidence-based practice educational interventions and teaching (GREET). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0759-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive usability ratings from 30 of 31 participants (97%) in the validation study. Six items (Incentives, Instructors, Environment, Schedule, Face to face time, Modifications) were rated easy to use by all participants; item 1 Intervention achieved the least "yes" ratings (70%).

> "The ratings, on a 5-point scale from poor to excellent, were positive, with participants rating the overall layout and ease of use as Poor (n = 0), Fair (n = 2, 7 %), Good (n = 12, 40 %), Very Good (n = 12, 40 %), and Excellent (n = 4, 13 %)."
