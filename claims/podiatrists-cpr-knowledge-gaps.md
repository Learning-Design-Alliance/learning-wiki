### Brenton-Rule 2021

Brenton-Rule, A., Harvey, D., Moran, K., O'Brien, D., & Webber, J. (2021). Knowledge and perceptions of cardiopulmonary resuscitation amongst New Zealand podiatrists: a web-based survey. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research, 14, 40. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-021-00481-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey knowledge section: 11 true/false statements on ANZCOR CPR protocols. The article reports "percentage scores of less than 50 % were reported for four of the 11 statements"; the lowest-scoring statement (reassessing every two minutes) drew 20% correct. No effect size printed.

> "The percentage of correct answers for the CPR protocol statements ranged from 20 to 94 %, and percentage scores of less than 50 % were reported for four of the 11 statements."
