Workflow comparison reported in Results Section B: the baseline was approximately two hours of Fusion 360 modeling per chart, observed in the authors' engagement with one statistics course; the pipeline runs in under 60 ms on-device, excluding the vision API round-trip.

"The baseline workflow required approximately two hours of Fusion 360 modeling per chart. The pipeline reduces this to under 60 ms on-device (excluding vision API round-trip), a reduction of approximately four orders of magnitude in software-side labor."
