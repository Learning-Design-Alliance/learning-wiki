`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive learning-gain analysis in the randomized trial, calculated by the formula the article attributes to Barwood et al. with a maximum score of 560. VP advantages reversed from immediate (SP higher) to one-month follow-up (VP higher), per "38.01% in the SP group and 40.12% in the VP group".

> "In the SP group, there was a net learning gain of 51.46% immediately after clinical reasoning exercise compared to that in the VP group, in which it was 49.1%. After one month, it was 38.01% in the SP group and 40.12% in the VP group (Graph 1)."
