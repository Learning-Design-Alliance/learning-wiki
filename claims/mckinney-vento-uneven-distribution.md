### Espinoza 2023

Espinoza, D., Griffith, M., Burns, D., & Shields, P. M. (2023). Federal and state resources for students experiencing homelessness. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/546.264

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Report's analysis of the competitive subgrant process; state allocations per identified student ranged from $38 in Utah to $287 in Vermont because the formula is based on Title I proportions rather than homelessness counts. The average grant of $21,455 covers about one third of one teacher's salary.

> "Because the limited amount of funds is distributed through a competitive process, most districts do not receive funds—in fact, fewer than 24% of LEAs (4,497 out of 18,844 in 2019–20) do."
