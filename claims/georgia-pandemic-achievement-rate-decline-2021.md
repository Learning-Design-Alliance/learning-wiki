`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparative analysis of Georgia Department of Education achievement rates across 2019, 2021, and 2022 school years. The printed means show the decline and partial recovery: "decreased from 63.16% in 2019 to 46.20% in 2021, before increasing to 57.31% in 2022"; English similarly fell and partly recovered. IQR narrowing is reported descriptively.

> "For Math, the mean achievement rate decreased from 63.16% in 2019 to 46.20% in 2021, before increasing to 57.31% in 2022 (Figure 2)."
