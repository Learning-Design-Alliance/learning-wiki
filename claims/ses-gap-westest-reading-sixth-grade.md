## Evidence

### Securro 2010

Securro, S., Jr., Jones, J. D., & Cantrell, D. R. (2010). Effects of Extensive Engagement with Computer-Based Reading and Language Arts Instructional Software on Reading Achievement for Sixth Graders. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102789

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent Samples t-test on Westest scaled scores by socio-economic status, operationally defined as receiving (58) or not receiving (28) Free/Reduced Lunch. The article reports the printed effect size of .48 favoring students not receiving lunch funding, described as "a moderate effect size of .48".

> "there was a significance for Not Free Reduced/L (M =663.6, Sd. 29.3) compared to Free-Reduced/L (M =648.1, Sd.35.2), t ( 83)= 1.998, p .049, with a moderate effect size of .48."
