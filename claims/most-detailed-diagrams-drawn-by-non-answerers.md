# Across several problems, the most detailed unprompted diagrams were drawn by students who did not select an answer

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For the Center of Mass and Torque problems, diagrams drawn by students who did not select an answer contained the largest number of details, a pattern the article also reports for Mirrors, Stage Ramps, and Circular Track. [→ Vignal 2022](#vignal-2022)

## Evidence

### Vignal 2022

Vignal, M., & Wilcox, B. R. (2022). Investigating Unprompted and Prompted Diagrams Generated by Physics Majors During Problem Solving. arXiv. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010104)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative comparison of unprompted diagrams for the Center of Mass and Torque problems found diagrams by students who did not select an answer "contained the largest number of details," which the authors suggest indicates students tried to use diagrams to reach answers unobtainable by other means.

> "Every student drew an unprompted diagram for both of these problems. As with the mirrors problem, the diagrams drawn by students who did not select an answer contained the largest number of details."
