## Evidence

### Vadla MS 2023

Vadla MS, Mduma ER, Kvaløy JT, Mdoe P, Hhoki BH, Sarangu S, Michael P, Oftedal B, Ersdal H. (2023). Increase in Newborns Ventilated Within the First Minute of Life and Reduced Mortality After Clinical Data-Guided Simulation Training. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000740

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary outcome analysis using logistic regression adjusted for source of admission, antenatal problem, prematurity, mode of delivery, and fetal heart rate. The article reports "no statistically significant changes in perinatal mortality for all births" in either analysis.

> "There were no statistically significant changes in perinatal mortality for all births (FSBs and 24-hour newborn deaths), in unadjusted (P = 0.468) or adjusted analyses (same variables as above, P = 0.182)."

### Vadla MS 2023 (2)

Vadla MS, Mduma ER, Kvaløy JT, Mdoe P, Hhoki BH, Sarangu S, Michael P, Oftedal B, Ersdal H. (2023). Increase in Newborns Ventilated Within the First Minute of Life and Reduced Mortality After Clinical Data-Guided Simulation Training. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000740

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis of the 1320 nonbreathing newborns receiving bag-mask ventilation. The unadjusted reduction from 12.6% to 7.5% (P = 0.03) was "no longer statistically significant (P = 0.111) after adjustment" for the listed risk factors.

> "Comparing the baseline period with the intervention period, a significant reduction in perinatal mortality (FSB and 24-hour newborn deaths, 12.6%–7.5%, P = 0.03) was found. However, the results were no longer statistically significant (P = 0.111) after adjustment"
