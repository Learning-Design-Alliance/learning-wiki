### Lisk Kristina 2024

Lisk Kristina, Cheung Jeffrey JH. (2024). A case study: exploring the impact of 3D printed models on cognitive integration during clinical skills training. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.78564

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative case study combining in-class observation field notes, a six-student focus group, and an instructor interview; thematic analysis. Observers documented that students intuitively leveraged the models to address questions and clarify how to identify anatomical landmarks, e.g., pointing to the lesser tubercle on the model.

> "The availability of the 3D printed models created several opportunities for peer teaching. Students intuitively leveraged the models to address questions and clarify how to identify anatomical landmarks."
