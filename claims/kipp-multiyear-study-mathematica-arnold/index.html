Related Claims¶

  • The evaluation covers 43 KIPP middle schools across 13 states and the District of Columbia and is the most rigorous large-scale evaluation of KIPP charters to date — related
  • KIPP middle schools show significant and substantial positive impacts on achievement in reading, math, science, and social studies — related
  • For students continuing from KIPP middle schools, KIPP high schools have positive impacts on a variety of college preparation activities and the likelihood of applying to college — related
  • For continuing KIPP students, KIPP high schools have positive impacts on college preparation activities and the likelihood of applying to college — related
  • KIPP high schools have positive, statistically significant achievement impacts for students new to the KIPP network, but not for students continuing from KIPP middle schools — related
  • For students continuing from KIPP middle schools to KIPP high schools, achievement impacts are not statistically significant — related
  • In the vast majority of 22 KIPP middle schools, impacts on students' state assessment scores in math and reading are positive, statistically significant, and educationally substantial — related
  • KIPP Pre-K combined with KIPP early elementary school may also have a positive impact on students' executive function — related
  • The second phase of the national KIPP evaluation nearly doubled the sample to 43 KIPP middle schools and estimated achievement impacts in four subjects — related
  • KIPP middle and high schools had a large, statistically significant combined effect on college graduation rates — related
