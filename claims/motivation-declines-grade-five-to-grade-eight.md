### Kilp-Kabel 2025

Triinu Kilp-Kabel, Kaja Mädamürk. (2025). Expectancies, values, and task persistence can alleviate the negative effects of math anxiety on math performance. European Journal of Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10212-024-00928-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney U tests compared motivation variables between grades five (N = 335) and eight (N = 251); all variables "differed significantly" with small rank-biserial correlations reported in Table 2 (e.g., rrb = 0.108 to 0.246).

> "all variables differed significantly with students in grade five having higher scores in the positive aspects of motivation (self-efficacy, attainment value, utility value, interest value, and task persistence) and lower scores in the negative aspects of motivation (effort cost, and math anxiety) than their grade eight counterparts"
