## Evidence

### Wrzesien 2010

Wrzesien, M., & Alcañiz Raya, M. (2010). Learning in serious virtual worlds: Evaluation of learning effectiveness and appeal to students in the E-Junior project. In Computers & Education (Vol. 55, Issue 1, pp. 178–187). Elsevier BV. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2010.01.003

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled comparison reported in the study abstract: a class in a serious virtual world was compared with a traditional class "that contained identical learning objectives and contents but lacked a gaming aspect," on a sample of 48 children. No statistically significant difference in learning effectiveness was found; no effect size is printed.

> "With regards to learning effectiveness, the results showed that the serious virtual world does not present statistically significant differences with the traditional type of class."
