## Evidence

### Cooper Natalia 2021

Cooper Natalia, Millela Ferdinando, Cant Iain, White Mark D., Meyer Georg. (2021). Transfer of training—Virtual reality training with augmented multisensory cues improves user experience during training and task performance in the real world. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248225

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on Presence Questionnaire ratings collected after the first and last virtual trials from the two VR groups (14 each). The article reports a "significant interaction between groups and the time of the presence responses" with η² = 0.19, and a higher end-of-task presence score for the ATV group (t28 = 1.747, p = 0.04, one-tailed).

> "A mixed ANOVA with 'training group' as the between-subject factor and PQ time-responses (first time, second time) as a within-subject factor revealed a significant interaction between groups and the time of the presence responses (F(1,26) = 6.139, p < 0.05, n2 = 0.19)."

### Cooper Natalia 2021 (2)

Cooper Natalia, Millela Ferdinando, Cant Iain, White Mark D., Meyer Georg. (2021). Transfer of training—Virtual reality training with augmented multisensory cues improves user experience during training and task performance in the real world. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248225

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Subscale-level mixed ANOVA on final Presence Questionnaire scores for the two VR groups. The article reports a significant subscale×group interaction with η² = 0.29 and a significant sensory-subscale difference between groups (t28 = -4.053, p < 0.001), which the authors attribute to the ATV group's higher presence.

> "The analysis revealed a main effect of subscale (F(3,78) = 4.891, p < 0.01, n2 = 0.16) and significant interaction between subscales and group (F(3,78) = 10.509, p < 0.001, n2 = 0.29)."
