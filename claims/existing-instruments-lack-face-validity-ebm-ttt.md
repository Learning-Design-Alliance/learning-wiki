`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's discussion of known assessment tools states the RSES "has been designed to assess research self-confidence" and thus "lacks face validity for teaching the teachers courses"; the SOC model is said to suffer the same deficiency, examining attitudes towards research rather than EBM teaching.

> "RSES scale has good internal consistency but it has been designed to assess research self-confidence. Thus it lacks face validity for teaching the teachers courses which focus on helping with EBM teaching not on conducting or teaching how to research."
