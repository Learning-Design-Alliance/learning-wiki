## Evidence

### Kellman 2009

Kellman, P. J., & Garrigan, P. (2009). Perceptual Learning and Human Expertise. Physics of Life Reviews, 6(2), 53-84. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2008.12.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Saarinen & Levi (1995), a 6-fold threshold decrease after 8000 trials. The review notes Vernier acuity is a hyperacuity, with untrained subjects often detecting "misalignments of less than 10 arc secs".

> "Despite the neural complexity already involved in achieving hyperacuity, with training over thousands of trials, Vernier thresholds improve substantially. Saarinen & Levi (1995), for example, found as much as a 6-fold decrease in threshold after 8000 trials of training."
