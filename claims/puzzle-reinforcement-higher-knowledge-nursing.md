## Evidence

### Eren Nadiye Barış; Çiftçi Bahar 2026

Eren Nadiye Barış; Çiftçi Bahar. (2026). Active learning–based reinforcement in nursing education: a cluster-randomized controlled trial using Roper, Logan and Tierney's life model. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2026-0022en

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized pretest–posttest trial with 70 first-year nursing students (35 per group); between-group independent-samples t-test on the Knowledge Test. Table 2 reports post-test means of 79.09 ± 17.80 (intervention) vs 37.14 ± 13.83 (control), t = 11.008, p = 0.001, ES = 2.631; the quote fragment "higher than the control group’s on the Knowledge Test" states the direction.

> "It was determined that the experimental group’s mean post-test score was higher than the control group’s on the Knowledge Test, and this difference was statistically significant (p < 0.05)."
