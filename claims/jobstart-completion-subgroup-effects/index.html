Appendix A4.1 of the Cave et al. (1993) randomized controlled trial reports subgroup findings for men (n=900), women living with own children (n=508), and women not living with own children (n=533); all three subgroup effects were statistically significant with Cox Index effect sizes of 0.37, 0.42, and 0.27.
