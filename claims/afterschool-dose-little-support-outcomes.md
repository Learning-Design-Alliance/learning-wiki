`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review's synthesis of the literature on afterschool participation amount and developmental outcomes reports that the authors "found little support for the general notion" that greater participation relates to academic, behavioral, or socio-emotional outcomes, contradicting earlier reviews.

> "Contrary to the findings from previous reviews we found little support for the general notion that greater amounts of participation in afterschool programs was related to academic, behavioral, or socio-emotional outcomes."
