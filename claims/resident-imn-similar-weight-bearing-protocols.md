The matched cohort analysis reports "Weight-bearing protocols did not differ significantly between groups after matching" with detailed distributions showing 40.0% full weight bearing in resident cases versus 29.3% in attending cases.

> "Weight-bearing protocols did not differ significantly between groups after matching (p = 0.218). Among resident-led cases, 30 patients (40.0%) were allowed full weight bearing, 13 (17.3%) partial weight bearing, and 31 (41.3%) remained non-weight bearing."
