## Evidence

### Weatherton M. and Aikens M 2026

Weatherton M. and Aikens M. (2026). Resource Use in Introductory Biology: Evaluating Latent Patterns, Demographic Covariates, and Effects on Final Course Grade. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-09-0212

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pairwise Wald χ2 tests with Benjamini–Yekutieli correction on class-specific distal means of standardized course grade. High Overall Frequency profile mean was −0.24 (SE = 0.06), the lowest of the four profiles; Independent Navigator mean was 0.13 (SE = 0.05).

> "Specifically, students in the High Overall Frequency latent profile had significantly lower standardized final grades than those of any of the other profiles (for all pairwise comparisons, p < 0.001)."
