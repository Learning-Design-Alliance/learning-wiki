## Evidence

### Thacker 2007

Thacker, B., Diaz, A., & Eligon, A. (2007). The Development of an Inquiry-based Curriculum Specifically for the Introductory Algebra-based Physics Course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702247](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0702247)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post FCI administration in the inquiry-based course at TTU, compared by the authors to traditionally taught TTU classes. The comparison is reported as significantly better, but no test statistic or effect size is printed.

> "The FCI scores at TTU have an absolute gain of about 30% and an averaged normalized gain of about 0.4, falling in the “interactive engagement region” and in the “medium–g” range as defined by Hake8 and significantly better than the FCI scores in the TTU classes taught traditionally."
