Evidence¶

McElhaney 2024¶

McElhaney, K., Urban, R., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., Tsai, K., and Roschelle, J. (2024). Practitioner-reported Needs for Enacting, Implementing, and Adopting OpenSciEd Curriculum Materials [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/215

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey analysis of 155 practitioners who rated 22 challenges and selected three high-priority ones. The report states that "All these challenges identified as highest priority except for one (adapting materials) were also among the seven challenges respondents reported as experiencing or observing most often."
