### Feil 2014

Feil, E. G., Frey, A., Walker, H. M., Small, J. W., Seeley, J. R., Golly, A., & Forness, S. R. (2014). The Efficacy of a Home-School Intervention for Preschoolers With Challenging Behaviors: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Preschool First Step to Success. Journal of Early Intervention. https://doi.org/10.1177/1053815114566090

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' own stated limitation in the Discussion section of the RCT. They note reliance on participant ratings of child behavior limits conclusions and creates potential for participant bias, possibly inflating achieved effects.

> "Although we were able to detect intervention results in parent and teacher ratings, we did not show effects on direct observations of child behavior; and this should be a priority for further study and documentation."
