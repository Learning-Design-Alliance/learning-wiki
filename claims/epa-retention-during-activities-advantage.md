## Evidence

### Celal Boyraz 2017

Celal Boyraz, Gökhan Serin. (2017). Science Instruction through the Game and Physical Activities Course: An Interdisciplinary Teaching Practice. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(11). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051119

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on the EPA retention test administered six months after instruction to all groups; the article reports F(3,78)=4.093, p<.05, partial eta-squared ƞ2=.136, described as close to large, with power above .80.

> "there was a significant difference between the groups in terms of their retention levels, F (3,78)=4.093, p<.05, ƞ2= .136."

### Celal Boyraz 2017 (2)

Celal Boyraz, Gökhan Serin. (2017). Science Instruction through the Game and Physical Activities Course: An Interdisciplinary Teaching Practice. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(11). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051119

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni comparisons on the EPA retention test found only Experiment 2 significantly higher than the control (p=.005, Cohen's d=1.05); Experiment 1 (p=.176) and Experiment 3 (p=.503) did not differ significantly from the control.

> "it was found as d=1.05 for the control and Experiment 2 groups."
