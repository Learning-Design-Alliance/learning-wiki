## Evidence

### Ramani Soumya; Pradeep Thanuja Gopal; Sundaresh Divya D 2018

Ramani Soumya; Pradeep Thanuja Gopal; Sundaresh Divya D. (2018). Effect of wet-laboratory training on resident performed manual small-incision cataract surgery. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/ijo.IJO_1041_17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective comparative observational study of 464 resident-performed MSICS cases (232 per group, before vs after March 2013 introduction of wet-laboratory training), abstracted from surgical records and compared with the Chi-squared test. The article reports "a higher rate of complications with 23.7% of cases having complications as opposed to only 15.08% in Group B".

> "The frequency of intraoperative complications was higher in Group A (23.7%) than in Group B (15.08%) (P = 0.019)."
