Evaluation of a Novel e-Learning Program for Physiotherapists to Manage Knee Osteoarthritis via Telehealth: Qualitative Study Nested in the PEAK (Physiotherapy Exercise and Physical Activity for Knee Osteoarthritis) Randomized Controlled Trial. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2 (practice makes perfect) from the thematic analysis. The mock consultation with a simulated patient and 4 practice video consultations with pilot patients were valued as "hugely beneficial" and consolidating learning ahead of real consultations.

> "Upon completion of the practical components of the e-learning program, physiotherapists expressed a high level of confidence and readiness to go into their first patient consultations using their new skills and knowledge"
