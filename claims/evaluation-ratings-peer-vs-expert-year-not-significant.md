## Evidence

### Hughes Thomas C 2010

Hughes Thomas C, Jiwaji Zoeb, Lally Kamaldeep, Lloyd-Lavery Antonia, Lota Amrit, Dale Andrea, Janas Robert, Bulstrode Christopher JK. (2010). Advanced Cardiac Resuscitation Evaluation (ACRE): A randomised single-blind controlled trial of peer-led vs. expert-led advanced resuscitation training. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-18-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational comparison of anonymised evaluation forms (one-to-five scale) between the trial year and the preceding expert-taught year; group-specific evaluation data was not available. The t test found no significant difference. No effect size printed.

> "For the year of the study (2007-8) the mean and SD were 3.92 and 0.41 compared with a 3.83 and 0.59 for the previous year. This difference was not significant (T test for independent samples p = 0.91)."
