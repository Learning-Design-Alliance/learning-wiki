Evidence¶

McLeod et al. 2019¶

Ragan H. McLeod, Ann P. Kaiser, and Jessica K. Hardy. (2019). The Relation Between Teacher Vocabulary Use in Play and Child Vocabulary Outcomes. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121418812675

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Mixed model analysis of the 53 EMT sessions; supports included physical supports (point, show, give) and verbal supports (word association, definition, expansion, prompt), combined into one category for analysis due to minimal examples of each. The article reports child target tokens "increased by 0.25 (SE = 0.06; p < .001)" per support.
