## Evidence

### Schlett Christopher L 2010

Job requirements compared to medical school education: differences between graduates from problem-based learning and conventional curricula. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-1

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Survey ratings of competencies as taught in medical school, from the same 4,821-graduate comparison. The article reports the difference for these two competencies demonstrated only a small effect size (Hedges' g: 0.3 and 0.4).

> "PBL graduates rated "Medical knowledge" and "Research competence" inferior compared to graduates from conventional curricula (4.38 ± 1.0 vs. 4.69 ± 0.9, p = 0.002, and 2.57 ± 1.3 vs. 3.10 ± 1.3, p < 0.001 respectively)"
