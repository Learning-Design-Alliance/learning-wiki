### Li Gengchun 2025

Li Gengchun. (2025). Understanding the roles of grit and well-being in L2 achievement among Chinese university english learners. BMC Psychology, 13, 576. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-02933-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap bias-corrected mediation analysis with 5,000 iterations on the SEM model found that "the confidence intervals of all mediation paths included 0", indicating non-significant mediation. No effect size was printed for this null finding.

> "Using the Bootstrap bias-corrected method (with 5,000 iterations), this study analyzed the mediating effects of the four elements of well-being between the two dimensions of grit and L2 achievement. The results showed that the confidence intervals of all mediation paths included 0"

### Li Gengchun 2025 (2)

Li Gengchun. (2025). Understanding the roles of grit and well-being in L2 achievement among Chinese university english learners. BMC Psychology, 13, 576. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-02933-4
