### S. B. McKagan 2007

S. B. McKagan, K. K. Perkins, and C. E. Wieman. (2007). Why we should teach the Bohr model and how to teach it effectively. https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.1541 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0707.1541](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0707.1541)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Based on the authors' interviews and observations of students doing homework during the course; students seemed to view each model as a list of characteristics to be memorized rather than having a clear mental picture. The authors cite Etkina et al. on the difficulty of developing scientific reasoning skills without targeted curricula.

> "Most students could describe the models, but had trouble giving satisfactory reasoning for why each model surpassed the previous one."
