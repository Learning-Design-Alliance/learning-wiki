## Evidence

### Mubeen Raffa 2026

Mubeen Raffa, Khan Asghar, Kiyani Humaira Shamim, Butt Ayesha Kamal, Bogaardt Hans, Noveen Sadaf. (2026). Development and validation of dysphagia knowledge and self-reported competence questionnaire for nurses. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0358250

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Test-retest reliability was assessed with 46 nurses across two administrations separated by a two-week interval. Internal consistency was evaluated on the full validation sample of 537 nurses.

> "The ICC value of 0.829 (95% CI:0.711–0.902, p<0.001) indicated excellent stability of the tool over a two-week interval. Internal consistency was evaluated using Cronbach's Alpha. The final 21-item scale yielded a Cronbach's alpha of 0.906."
