## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hass 2020

Hass, C. A. F., Ryan, Q. X., & Sayre, E. C. (2020). Teacher Noticing and Shifting of Student Epistemic Framing. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2002.12879](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2002.12879)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video analysis of the oral exam episode shows that after the instructor's question the student made statements such as reasoning that components cancel, which the authors read as conceptual understanding of how geometry affects physical behavior; they conclude her framing is now Conceptual Physics.

> "Rather than following steps, these are statements about Abbey's conceptual understanding of how the geometry of a system affects its physical behavior. We conclude that Abbey's framing is now Conceptual Physics."
