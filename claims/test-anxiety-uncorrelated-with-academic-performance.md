## Evidence

### Hahn Henry 2017

Hahn Henry, Kropp Peter, Kirschstein Timo, Rücker Gernot, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2017). Test anxiety in medical school is unrelated to academic performance but correlates with an effort/reward imbalance. PLoS ONE 12(2): e0171220. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171220

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis using self-reported previous term grades and the written physiology test score in the 48-student cohort; the study reports no correlation between test anxiety and academic performance in either direction. The oral exam was pass/fail only, allowing no performance discrimination.

> "we did not observe any correlation between test anxiety and subsequent or previous academic performance or test anxiety and depressiveness"
