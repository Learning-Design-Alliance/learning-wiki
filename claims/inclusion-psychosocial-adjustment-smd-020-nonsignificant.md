Dalgaard, N. T., Bondebjerg, A., Viinholt, B. C. A., & Filges, T. (2022). The effects of inclusion on academic achievement, socioemotional development and wellbeing of children with special educational needs. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 18, e1291. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1291

`q2 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of non-randomised studies comparing inclusive versus segregated placement, using inverse variance weighted random effects models. The pooled effect size was SMD = 0.20, printed as "0.20 (95% confidence interval [CI]: −0.01 to 0.42)", a small effect favouring inclusion that was not statistically significant.
