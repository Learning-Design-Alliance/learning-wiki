Cargnelutti, E., Tomasetto, C., Passolunghi, M.C. (2017). How is anxiety related to math performance in young students? A longitudinal study of Grade 2 to Grade 3 children. COGNITION & EMOTION, 31(4), 755-764. https://doi.org/10.1080/02699931.2016.1147421

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Longitudinal analyses following the same children from Grade 2 to Grade 3 tested whether earlier math anxiety predicted later math performance. The article reports "an indirect effect of math anxiety in Grade 2 on subsequent math performance in Grade 3"; no effect size is printed in the available text.
