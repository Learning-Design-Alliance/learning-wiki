`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

In the review's summary, drawing on the WFUMB position paper and a comparison table of assessment formats, the authors state OSCE and DOPS "represent the gold standard according to the current state of research" for assessing ultrasound learning success.

> "Following the WFUMB recommendations of structured assessment tools that also consider technical aspects [3], OSCE and DOPS represent the gold standard according to the current state of research and allow a precise examination of learning success while offering high educational value"
