## Evidence

### Day Alison J. 2021

Day Alison J., Fenn Kimberly M., Ravizza Susan M. (2021). Is it worth it? The costs and benefits of bringing a laptop to a university class. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251792

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of RescueTime-tracked laptop activity across 19 lecture periods in an introductory psychology course; students spent 28% of class time off-task. The article reports the correlation as "negatively correlated with exam score, r(101) = -0.23, p = 0.02".

> "Average duration of off-task activity was negatively correlated with exam score, r(101) = -0.23, p = 0.02"

### Day Alison J. 2021 (2)

Day Alison J., Fenn Kimberly M., Ravizza Susan M. (2021). Is it worth it? The costs and benefits of bringing a laptop to a university class. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0251792

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression entering standardized test scores in step one and off-task use in step two; ACT significantly predicted performance (F(1, 87) = 23.07, p < 0.001, ΔR2 = 0.21), and "off-task activity accounted for an additional 4.8% of the variance in exam score, p = 0.02".

> "off-task activity accounted for an additional 4.8% of the variance in exam score, p = 0.02"
