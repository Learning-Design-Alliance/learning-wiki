## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants randomized to LLM-assisted self-study scored significantly higher on the post-intervention exam than those using traditional resources, controlling for baseline score, GPA, gender, and study preferences. [→ Bharat Anand 2026](#bharat-anand-2026)

## Evidence

### Bharat Anand 2026

Bharat Anand, Lihi Idan. (2026). Generative AI and the Productivity Divide: Human–AI Complementarities in Education and Knowledge Work (Working Paper). https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18143 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.18143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.18143)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled experiment at Texas A&M University with 179 participants, mostly engineering undergraduates, randomized to LLM or baseline self-study for three days. The OLS treatment coefficient remained positive and statistically significant (p<0.05) with controls; descriptive means show "M=.56,SD=.26, compared toM=.48,SD=.23".

> "The average post-intervention exam score among LLM participants wasM=.56,SD=.26, compared toM=.48,SD=.23 in the baseline group."
