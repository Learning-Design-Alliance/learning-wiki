# Students with the same HSGPA or the same ACT score graduate from college at different rates depending on which high school they attended

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2`–`r3` · `q2`–`q3`

## Subclaims

`q3 i?` There is significant high school variance in graduation rates within every HSGPA bin and considerable variance within ACT bins. [→ Allensworth 2019](#allensworth-2019)

`q2 i?` College graduation rates six years after initial enrollment vary substantially across high schools among students with identical HSGPAs or ACT scores. [→ Allensworth 2020](#allensworth-2020)

## Evidence

### Allensworth 2019

Allensworth, E. M., & Clark, K. (2019). High School GPAs and ACT Scores as Predictors of College Completion: Examining Assumptions about Consistency across High Schools. University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-gpas-and-act-scores-as-predictors-of-college-completion

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Binned nonparametric HLM models (Table 3) estimating school-level random effects for CPS graduates of 2006-2009 cohorts. Students with a 3.25-3.5 HSGPA at schools one SD below the mean graduate at rates similar to students with 2.75-3.0 HSGPAs at typical schools.

> "There is significant high school variance in college graduation rates for students in each HSGPA bin. For example, among students with HSGPAs between 3.25-3.5, a two-standard deviation range of high school effects is 0.144 ± 0.575 in log-odds in the conditional model."

### Allensworth 2020

Allensworth, E. (2020). High school GPAs and ACT scores as predictors of college graduation. Educational Researcher, 49(3), 198-211. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-GPAs-and-ACT-scores-as-predictors-of-college-completion-er-article

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The study's overview reports its central finding: "students with the same HSGPA or the same ACT score graduate at very different rates based on which high school they attended," based on graduation six years after initial enrollment.

> "We found students with the same HSGPA or the same ACT score graduate at very different rates based on which high school they attended."
