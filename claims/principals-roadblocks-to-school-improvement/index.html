Principals' self-reports on the CCSR 2003 principal survey (Table 9) about factors preventing school improvement; the article reports that "principals identified six as the most common." Internal factors included lack of planning time and difficulty removing poor teachers; external factors included community social problems and apathetic parents.

"From a list of 25 possible roadblocks, principals identified six as the most common. T wo- thirds to three-quarters of them considered these road- blocks as a serious factor or somewhat of a factor."
