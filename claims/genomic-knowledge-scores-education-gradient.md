## Evidence

### Calzone 2026

Calzone, K., Stokes, L., Peterson, C., Yee, L. M., Liewehr, D., and Badzek, L. (2026). Evaluation of the Integration of Genetics and Genomics Into Nursing Practice. Journal of Nursing Scholarship 58, no. 1: e70056. https://doi.org/10.1111/jnu.70056

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive subgroup analysis of 2023 GGNPS knowledge scores by demographic variables. The article reports "increasing scores as the level of education increased" with medians of 8, 8.5, 9, and 10 across degree levels.

> "Median 2023 knowledge scores varied based on level of academic preparation with lower levels of education having lower median knowledge scores and increasing scores as the level of education increased. Specifically, Diploma prepared nurses had a median score of 8, Associate or Baccalaureate degree nurses 8.5, Master's degree 9, and Doctorate degree 10."

### Calzone 2026 (2)

Calzone, K., Stokes, L., Peterson, C., Yee, L. M., Liewehr, D., and Badzek, L. (2026). Evaluation of the Integration of Genetics and Genomics Into Nursing Practice. Journal of Nursing Scholarship 58, no. 1: e70056. https://doi.org/10.1111/jnu.70056

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same descriptive subgroup analysis of the 2023 survey by primary practice role, reporting median knowledge scores of 8 for staff and head nurses versus 10 for researchers and clinical nurse specialists.

> "Role also showed differences in knowledge scores with staff nurses and head nurses both having a median of 8, and higher scores in researchers and clinical nurse specialists, median 10."
