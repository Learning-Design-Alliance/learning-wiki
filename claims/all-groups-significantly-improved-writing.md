## Evidence

### Nicholes 2016

Nicholes, J. (2016). Measuring the Impact of Language-Learning Software on Test Performance of Chinese Learners of English. TESL-EJ, 20(2). https://tesl-ej.org/pdf/ej76/a3.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests with Wilcoxon signed rank confirmation on pre/post writing scores for all four groups. For example, RS improved from M = 54.21 to M = 63.26, t(18) = -4.67, p < 0.001; Wilcoxon confirmed all groups (RS p = 0.001, MEM p = 0.001, TMM p = 0.006, WOW p = 0.022).

> "Analysis of pre- and posttest means revealed that all groups significantly improved at the p < 0.05 level in writing. RS pre-writing (M = 54.21, SD = 11.04) differed significantly from RS post-writing (M = 63.26, SD = 11.42); t(18)= -4.67, p < 0.001."
