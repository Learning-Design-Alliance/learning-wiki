# Full-day kindergarten shows a positive association with children's self-confidence (d ≈ 0.55–0.56)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` FDK was associated with higher parent- or teacher-reported self-confidence, with weighted mean d of 0.56 (fixed) and 0.55 (random), and an adjusted effect of d = 1.36. [→ Harris Cooper 2010](#harris-cooper-2010)

## Evidence

### Harris Cooper 2010

Harris Cooper, Ashley Batts Allen, Erika A. Patall, and Amy L. Dent. (2010). Effects of Full-Day Kindergarten on Academic Achievement and Social Development. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654309359185

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of six samples (eight effect sizes, reports 1980–2005, sample sizes 29–250) using single questionnaire items filled out by parents or teachers. One adjusted comparison showed a significant positive effect favoring FDK (d = 1.36, 95% CI = 0.91/1.82).

> "Under fixed-effect assumptions, the weighted mean d-index was 0.56 (95% CI = 0.41/0.72). Under random-effects assumptions, the weighted mean d-index was 0.55 (95% CI = 0.32/0.79)."
