# DHH students report using redundant multimedia features for support, conflicting with the CTML redundancy principle

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` DHH students perceived benefits from using two or more multimedia features in redundant, interchangeable ways, which conflicts with the CTML principle that redundant on-screen text should be avoided. [→ Gehret 2025](#gehret-2025)

## Evidence

### Gehret 2025

Gehret, A. U., & Elliot, L. B. (2025). Perceptions of e-learning by deaf and hard of hearing students using asynchronous multimedia tutorials. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10476-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Content analysis of open-ended survey responses from DHH students identified a 'redundant support' theme, where the connecting thread was 'two or more features being used in an interchangeable manner', such as using captions to recover missed spoken information.

> "The emergent theme, redundant support, encompassed a variety of scenarios with the connecting thread of two or more features being used in an interchangeable manner."
