# Offspring exposed to gestational diabetes have a marginally lower GPA at compulsory school graduation than background-population offspring (adjusted mean difference –0.36, Cohen's D 0.14)

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` In a national Danish register cohort, O-GDM scored lower on GPA at age 15–16 than O-BP, with a fully adjusted mean difference of –0.36 (95% CI –0.44 to –0.29) and an effect size of Cohen's D 0.14. [→ Heldarskard GF 2021](#heldarskard-gf-2021)

## Evidence

### Heldarskard GF 2021

Heldarskard GF, Spangmose AL, Henningsen A-KA, Wiingreen R, Mortensen EL, Gundersen TW, Jensen RB, Knorr S, Damm P, Forman JL, Pinborg A, Clausen TD. (2021). Academic Performance in Adolescents Born to Mothers With Gestational Diabetes—A National Danish Cohort Study. The Journal of Clinical Endocrinology and Metabolism. https://doi.org/10.1210/clinem/dgab451

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Primary-outcome analysis of the national register-based cohort (4286 O-GDM, 501 045 O-BP; GPA registered in 3777 O-GDM and 459 055 O-BP), using linear mixed models accounting for sibling correlation. The "fully adjusted mean difference (model 2) was –0.36 (95% CI, –0.44 to –0.29)"; the article reports Cohen's D of 0.14, considered small.

> "When adjusting according to model 1 the mean difference increased to -0.57 (95% CI, –0.64 to–0.50), while the fully adjusted mean difference (model 2) was –0.36 (95% CI, –0.44 to –0.29)"
