`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Crossover trial comparing a 3D online learning module with gross anatomy lab resources. The study reports "significant positive relationships" between spatial ability and test performance (Table 5 notes r≈0.25); the printed correlation values in the text are garbled, so no effect size is asserted.

> "There were significant positive relationships between participants' spatial ability scores and their performance on the pretest, Quiz 1, and Quiz 2 assessments"
