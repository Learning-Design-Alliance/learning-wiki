Schaller Michael D., Gencheva Marieta, Gunther Michael R., Weed Scott A. (2023). Training doctoral students in critical thinking and experimental design using problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04569-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' report on a curricular change between course iterations: statistics exercises were added after a biostatistics workshop entered the first-semester curriculum. The finding rests on student evaluations; no test statistic or effect size is printed.

> "Student evaluations indicated that the introduction of these additional exercises was not effective. Improved coordination between the biostatistics workshop and the PBL course is required to align expectations, better equipping students for the statistical analysis of experimental results encountered later in this course."
