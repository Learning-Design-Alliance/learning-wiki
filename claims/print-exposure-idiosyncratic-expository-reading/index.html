Evidence¶

Song 2025¶

Song, M., Li, L., He, D. & Cai, Q. (2025). Reading experience reveals shared and idiosyncratic neural patterns during text comprehension. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00390-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

AnnaK model analysis over 153 ROIs on fMRI data from 39 participants during expository reading, with education regressed out and FDR correction (q < .05). Negative model effects indicate "negative effects" of exposure dissimilarity on neural similarity in FPN-related regions.

"However, during expository reading, print exposure showed negative effects in the left inferior frontal gyrus (IFG) and inferior parietal lobule (IPL), as well as posterior regions of the right inferior temporal gyrus (ITG)."
