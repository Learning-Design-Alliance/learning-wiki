Relationships between psychological characteristics, academic fit and engagement with academic performance in veterinary medical students. BMC Veterinary Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-023-03695-0

The model showed "both internal locus of control and self-efficacy had a positive relationship with academic fit". No standardized path coefficients are printed in the text.
