### Fallon 2018

Fallon, T. L., Aylett, R., Minnis, H. and Rajendran, G. (2018). Investigating social vulnerability in children using computer mediated role-play. Computers and Education, 125, pp. 458-464. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.06.027

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc comparisons following the ANOVA in the Results section showed 6-year-olds scored higher than both older groups (both p < 0.001); the 8- vs 10-year-old contrast was not significant (p = .287). No effect size was printed for these contrasts.

> "Post hoc tests revealed that 6-year-olds displayed more DSE than 8-year-old s (p < 0.001) and 10 year-olds (p < 0.001). There was no significant difference between 10 & 8-year olds, p = .287."
