The review reports this second-hand from Jung et al. (2020), a pre- and post-program comparison including pharmacy students; the review attributes the null to scenarios "primarily focused on medication errors caused by nurses or doctors".

"However, pharmacy students did not experience an increase in self-efficacy for interprofessional experiential learning, in contrast to medical and nursing students."
