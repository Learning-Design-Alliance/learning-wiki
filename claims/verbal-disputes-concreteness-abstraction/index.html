Evidence¶

Julia Chatain 2025¶

Julia Chatain, Charlotte Müller, Keny Chatain, Leon Calabrese, Manu Kapur. (2025). Concreteness and Abstraction in Mathematics Education: A Taxonomy of the Semantic Landscape. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10073-9

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

Theoretical argument applying philosophy of language: the article quotes Chalmers' definition of a broadly verbal dispute and illustrates it with statements such as math being difficult to learn because it is abstract, where parties meaning generalizable versus unfamiliar would disagree. The argument is analytical, not empirical.

"A dispute over S is (broadly) verbal when, for some expression T in S,t h e parties disagree about the meaning of T , and the dispute over S arises wholly in virtue of this disagreement regarding T ."
