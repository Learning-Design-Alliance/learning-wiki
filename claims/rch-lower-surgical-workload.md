Secondary outcome measured with the validated SURG-TLX questionnaire after the final skills session, completed by 10 control and 13 intervention participants with one intervention outlier removed; "Total workload score for the RCH group was 170.7, whereas the control group scored 195.8 (P = 0.014)".

### Amin Mohammad S. A. 2020 (2)

Shamim, Dasgupta Prokar, Makanjuoala Jonathan, Ahmed Kamran. (2020). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SURG-TLX domain analysis found "the RCH group scored 10.8 whilst the HCA group scored 29 achieving borderline significance (P = 0.047)" in the distraction domain; other domains showed no significant differences.
