## Evidence

### Shin TH 2025

Shin TH, Fanta A, Gokcal F, Shields M, Benlice C, Kudsi OY. (2025). Decoding surgical proficiency and complexity: a machine learning framework for robotic herniorrhaphy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12412-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal observational analysis of an eight-year single-surgeon case series; t-SNE dimensional reduction of OPIs with time- and volume-weighted Euclidean distances and Savitzky–Golay smoothing. The text reports "a rapid reduction by the first 10 months of the series". No effect size printed.

> "Euclidean distances for OPIs were much greater in the beginning of the surgeon's cases with a rapid reduction by the first 10 months of the series. Following this, the absolute value and variance of distances remain tightly bound despite introduction of increasingly complex cases."
