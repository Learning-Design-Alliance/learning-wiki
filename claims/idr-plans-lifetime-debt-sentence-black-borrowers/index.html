Mustaffa 2021¶

Mustaffa, J. B., & Davis, J. C. W. (2021). Jim Crow Debt: How Black Borrowers Experience Student Loans. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/black-student-debt-study/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey and interview findings from Finding 2 of the study. Of repaying borrowers, "72% were enrolled in an IDR plan," and interviewees described plans as a "trap" or "scam" and compared them to historic racial oppression.

"Of the Black borrowers in our study who were in repayment, 72% were enrolled in an IDR plan. In interviews, many of them described their student loans as a “trap” or “scam” or drew comparisons between their experiences in these plans and historic examples of racial oppression."
