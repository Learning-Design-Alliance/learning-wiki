### Luiz Rodrigues 2026

Luiz Rodrigues, Guilherme Guerino, Thomaz Edson Silva, Luana Bianchini, Mariana Alves, Geiser Chalco Challco, Thales Vieira, Marcelo Marinho, Valmir Macario, Diego Dermeval, Ig Ibert Bittencourt, Seiji Isotani. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Robust mediation analysis tested whether the experimental condition (relative to training) affected each workload subscale, which in turn affected learning gains; the Effort path yielded AB = 3.649, CI [1.097, 6.398], p-adj = 0.007, the only subscale significant after adjustment.

> "The results suggest a statistically significant mediation effect for Mental Demand and Effort, although only the latter remains significant after p-values adjustment. Overall, this finding suggests that the experimental condition reduces teachers' effort, which in turn is negatively associated with students' learning gains."
