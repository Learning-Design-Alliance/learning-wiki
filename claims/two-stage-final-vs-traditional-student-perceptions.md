### Callaghan 2026

Two-Stage Final Exams: An Assessment Strategy for Enhanced Collaborative Learning and Reduced Student Stress. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2026.2620442](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2026.2620442)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-final survey in which students compared their two-stage final experience with what they would likely have experienced in a traditional individual-only final (Figure 4); all students had experience with traditional finals and none with a two-stage final. Perceptions are self-reported comparisons, not measured learning or retention.

> "students at both institutions indicated that they felt completing the final group exam increased how much they learned and were likely to remember three months later. The students also reported no decrease in motivation among their group members and had less anxiety about the exam overall."
