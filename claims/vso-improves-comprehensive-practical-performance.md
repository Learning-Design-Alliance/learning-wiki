## Evidence

### Wang Jiaxue 2025

Wang Jiaxue, Jiang Yunshui, Yang Jun, Zhou Qian, Hu Hui, Chen Yan, Jiang Jinqin. (2025). Application of virtual simulations in the practical teaching of clinical microbiology laboratory courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07603-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of three 2021-cohort classes (n = 86, VSO) against three 2020-cohort classes (n = 88, video), baseline-matched on sex, age, CNCEE score and prior course scores. The experimental group's median comprehensive score was 90.30 versus 86.00, with "a significant difference in score distribution between the groups (Z = − 7.21, P < 0.001)".

> "The median practical comprehensive performance scores in the experimental group and control group were 90.30 (88.92, 92.16) and 86.00 (82.75, 88.50), respectively. Statistical analysis revealed a significant difference in score distribution between the groups (Z = − 7.21, P < 0.001)."
