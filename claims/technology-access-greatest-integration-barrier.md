## Evidence

### Brasiel 2016

Brasiel, S., Jeong, S., Ames, C., Lawanto, K., Yuan, M., & Martin, T. (2016). Effects of educational technology on mathematics achievement for K-12 students in Utah. Journal of Online Learning Research, 2(3), 205-226. https://www.learntechlib.org/j/JOLR/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey of 2,933 teachers in the grant program administered via Qualtrics, with open-ended responses coded. Table 11 reports 32% citing not enough computers and 34% reporting no barriers; the authors note this confirms prior research identifying infrastructure as a challenge to technology integration.

> "Thirty-two percent of teachers reported lack of technology access to be the greatest barrier (Table 11). The next most common challenge was in setting up student accounts, with the largest percent of teachers with concerns reported for MathXL (12%) and ST Math (10%)."
