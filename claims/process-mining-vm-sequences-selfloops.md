## Evidence

### Omarchevska 2022

Omarchevska, Y., Lachner, A., Richter, J., & Scheiter, K. (2022). Do Video Modeling and Metacognitive Prompts Improve Self-Regulated Scientific Inquiry? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09652-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory process mining (HeuristicsMiner in ProM 5.2) of coded process data from the training task. In the VM conditions, Monitoring and Control showed "strong self-loops"; in the control condition, Problem Identification, Hypothesis Generation, and Monitoring were disconnected from other processes and no self-loops were observed.

> "Monitoring and Control were not related to specific scientific reasoning processes and both processes had strong self-loops, which indicates that these processes were performed sequentially several times."
