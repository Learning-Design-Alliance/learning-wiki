# Secondary math teachers from Teach For America and the Teaching Fellows programs are as effective as teachers receiving traditional certification

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a large-scale random assignment study, secondary math teachers from Teach For America and the Teaching Fellows programs were as effective as traditionally certified math teachers. [→ Melissa A. Clark 2013](#melissa-a-clark-2013)

## Evidence

### Melissa A. Clark 2013

Melissa A. Clark, Hanley S. Chiang, Tim Silva, Sheena McConnell, Kathy Sonnenfeld, Anastasia Erbe, Michael Puma. (2013). The Effectiveness of Secondary Math Teachers from Teach For America and the Teaching Fellows Programs. Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-effectiveness-of-secondary-math-teachers-from-teach-for-america-and-the-teaching-fellows-programs

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A large-scale random assignment study of secondary school math teachers compared Teach For America and Teaching Fellows teachers with traditionally certified teachers and found they were "as effective as, and in some cases more effective than" the comparison teachers.

> "The first large-scale, random assignment study of the effects of secondary school math teachers from Teach For America and the Teaching Fellows programs found they were as effective as, and in some cases more effective than, teachers receiving traditional certification."
