Dubois Fatéméh, Ambrosetti Damien, Tosca Lucie. (2026). Educational Impact of an Instagram Account on Histology Teaching: Mixed Methods Approach in Medical Education. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/89044

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instagram Insights analytics over a 90-day period (August 27 to November 27, 2025) for the Histo.Embryo account. The authors caution that very few Reels were published, so the format contrast partly reflects editorial strategy rather than pure preference.

> "Analysis of content format showed a strong preference for static, image-based posts, which accounted for 98.1% (203/207) of all views. In contrast, Reels represented only 1.9% (4/207) of total content consumption."
