The essay's evaluation section reports observational studies attributing this to Grunspan et al., Bloodhart et al., and Stark et al. The essay states "Peer assessment is vulnerable to implicit bias" and that in mixed-gender groups women received lower evaluations despite substantive contributions. Biases based on race/ethnicity also influenced peer evaluations, with white students receiving higher ratings compared with students of other racial identities. No effect size is printed.

> "Peer assessment is vulnerable to implicit bias (Stonewall et al., 2018), influenced by status perceptions and concerns about the distribution of effort (Anderson and Kilduff, 2009; Hall and Buzwell, 2013). For example, in mixed-gender groups, women were perceived as less competent (Grunspan et al., 2016; Bloodhart et al., 2020) and received lower evaluations despite substantive contributions"
