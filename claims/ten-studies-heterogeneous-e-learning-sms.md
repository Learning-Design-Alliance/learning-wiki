The review's search and screening process, reported in the Results section: 1505 articles returned, 52 full texts checked, 42 excluded, and ten peer-reviewed articles included. The authors state these themes "revealed substantial heterogeneity in instructional design and other elements of e-learning applied to SMS".

> "The search returned 1505 articles, with most subsequently excluded based on their title or abstract. Fifty-two full text articles were obtained and checked, with 42 excluded because they did not meet the full criteria. Ten peer-reviewed articles were included in this review."
