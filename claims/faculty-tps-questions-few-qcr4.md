Systematic coding of 353 multiple-choice Think-Pair-Share questions authored by faculty in professional development workshops, using the authors' curriculum characterization framework. The distribution shows "only 15.9% are QCR = 4", which the authors call pedagogically problematic.

> "Out of the entire set of 353 faculty-produced questions spanning 15 broad topical areas, 1.7% are QCR = 1, 25.5% are QCR = 2, 56.9% are QCR = 3, and only 15.9% are QCR = 4."
