`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-sample t-test comparing 101 pre-clinical POM students with 54 clinical EM students on the 21-item checklist found "no significant differences in mean baseline scores"; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "There were no significant differences in mean baseline scores for pre-clinical students (POM, M = 12.64, SD = 3.15) and clinical students (EM, M = 12.80, SD = 2.76) t(153) = 0.30, P = 0.77)"
