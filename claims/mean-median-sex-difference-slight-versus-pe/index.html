Evidence¶

Frasier 1915¶

Frasier, George W. (1915). An Inquiry Into the Statistical Basis of a Conclusion Concerning Sex Differences. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Comparative computation on the same data: arithmetic means of 63.9 (boys) and 60.8 (girls) with P.E. of 1.24 and 1.43 (difference 3.1); medians of 65.8 and 59.7 with P.E. of 1.55 and 1.79 (difference 6.1); the Yerkes mode difference was 10.

"It appears from the above that no pronounced sex difference is shown. On the basis of the arithmetic mean or the refined median this difference is slight when the P.E. is taken into consideration."
