## Evidence

### Xu J 2018

Xu J, Campisi P, Forte V, Carrillo B, Vescan A, Brydges R. (2018). Effectiveness of discovery learning using a mobile otoscopy simulator on knowledge acquisition and retention in medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-018-0317-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Usage tracked via built-in analytics over one week of access. The study reports "Overall device usage was low" with means of 21.47 and 13.84 minutes respectively, and an independent samples t-test showed no statistical difference (t36 = 1.03, p = 0.31).

> "Overall device usage was low, as the discovery then instruction group spent 21.47 ± 26.28 min, while the instruction then discovery group spent 13.84 ± 18.71 min."
