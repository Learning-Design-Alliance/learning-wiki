Evidence¶

Magma Math: Evidence Rating Promising 2025¶

Magma Math: Evidence Rating Promising. (2025). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/magma-math/

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study in the 2024–25 school year matching 843 Magma Math users with 843 non-users on baseline math scores and demographics across 13 Ohio schools. The matched comparison found users "scored significantly higher on the spring i-Ready Mathematics assessment" with effect size +0.12.

"Students who used Magma Math in grades 4–5 scored significantly higher on the spring i-Ready Mathematics assessment than their matched peers who did not use the program (effect size = +0.12)"
