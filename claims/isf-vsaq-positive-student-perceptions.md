## Evidence

### Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong 2024

Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong, Kantiwong Anupong. (2024). Development and validation of immediate self-feedback very short answer questions for medical students: practical implementation of generalizability theory to estimate reliability in formative examination designs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05569-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Five 5-point Likert perception questions administered after the exam to the participating students, analyzed descriptively. "More than 80% of students strongly agreed or agreed" on all five dimensions; overall mean 21.56 ± 3.72 of 25, α = 0.90 (95% CI: 0.88 to 0.93). No effect size is printed.

> "More than 80% of students strongly agreed or agreed that VSAQs effectively improve knowledge review and problem analysis (81.91%), align with real situation and critical thinking skills (80.85%), find areas for improvement (84.05%), help in comprehensive knowledge development (84.25%), give useful assessment feedback (84.04%), and make it easy to practice solving problems on electronic platform (87.15%) (Fig. 5)."
