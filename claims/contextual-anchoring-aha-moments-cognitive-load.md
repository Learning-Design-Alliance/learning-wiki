Semi-structured interviews conducted with each of the 40 participants immediately after the learning session (RQ3 qualitative analysis). Participants reported that "the tailored explanations anticipated their potential points of confusion"; a Computer Science major described a video-game quest-log analogy for neural networks that made the concept click instantly.
