## Evidence

### van de Steeg L 2015

van de Steeg L, IJkema R, Wagner C, Langelaan M. (2015). The effect of an e-learning course on nursing staff's knowledge of delirium: a before-and-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0289-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline scores by demographic category from multivariable multilevel linear regression (Table 3); the article also states "No significant differences in change score were found between subgroups" (Table 4). Only p-values and means are printed.

> "Those aged 50 years or older scored significantly lower than those under the age of 30 (77.0 vs. 79.7, p < 0.01) (Table 3) and those aged between 30 and 50 (79.7, p < 0.01). Furthermore, nursing staff with a bachelor's or master's degree scored significantly higher than those with a vocational education (81.2 vs. 78.7, p < 0.01)."
