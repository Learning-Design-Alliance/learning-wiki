## Subclaims
`q2 i?` High school GPA correlated only .18 and SAT only .19 with marketing grades; even 1st-year college GPA (.44) outpredicted them. [→ Bacon 2006](#bacon-2006)

## Evidence

### Bacon 2006

Donald R. Bacon and Beth Bean. (2006). GPA in Research Studies: An Invaluable but Neglected Opportunity. Journal of Marketing Education, Vol. 28 No. 1, April 2006 35-42. https://doi.org/10.1177/0273475305284638

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis within the 157-student undergraduate sample comparing admissions variables with course grades. The article notes self-reports of SAT are less reliable than self-reports of GPA (.88 versus .97, Cassady 2001), further diminishing SAT's usefulness.

> "The correlation between high school GPA and all mar- keting grades (regardless of year) was only .18, and the corre- lation between SAT score and marketing grades was only .19."
