## Evidence

### Abdelhalim 2015

Safaa M. Abdelhalim. (2015). Children Literature Based Program for Developing EFL Primary Pupils’ Life Skills and Language Learning Strategies. English Language Teaching, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n2p178

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the personal and social skills questionnaire post-test showed a large effect (η² = .85) favoring experimental groups over control. Tukey comparisons confirmed both experimental groups significantly exceeded control.

> "there were statistically significant differences between the first experimental group and the second experimental group pupils’ mean scores on post administrations of the language learning strategies questionnaires and two of the life skills measures, namely , the personal and social questionnaire and the oral interpersonal communication test in favor of the second experimental group"
