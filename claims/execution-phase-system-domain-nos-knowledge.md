## Evidence

### Lassen 2021

Lassen, I. C., Arielle-Evans, A. A., Ríos, L., Lewandowski, H. J., & Dounas-Frazer, D. R. (2021). Student ownership and understanding of multi-week final projects. [doi:10.1119/perc.2021.pr.lassen](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2021.pr.lassen)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Emergent thematic analysis of the subset of about 220 excerpts coded as new understanding, drawn from the same 15 life-grid interviews; the execution phase was the most discussed. Nine students "described gaining DOMAIN knowledge" and, most often, students described gaining system knowledge, e.g. troubleshooting laser performance; no effect size is reported.

> "In total, all 15 participants each discussed NEW UNDER - STANDING during the EXECUTION phase several times dur- ing their interviews. Of these, 8 described gaining knowl- edge about the NATURE OF SCIENCE , 9 described gaining DOMAIN knowledge, and 15 students described gaining SYS - TEM knowledge."
