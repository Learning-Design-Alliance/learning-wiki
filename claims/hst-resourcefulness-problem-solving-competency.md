Roche Adam F., Condron Claire M., Eppich Walter J., O'Connor Paul E. (2023). A Mixed Methods Study Identifying the Competencies of Health Care Simulation Technicians. Simulation in Healthcare 18(5). https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000682

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' thematic analysis across both data sources identified resourcefulness as a skills domain; Table 1 shows problem solving in 12 (67%) of job descriptions and 21 (70%) of interviews. The article states resourcefulness builds on other skill-based competencies such as problem solving and technical ability.

> "In our study, the ability to solve problems stood out as a competency in the resourcefulness domain, which corresponds to a key requirement for the SSH Healthcare Simulation Operations Specialist Certification."
