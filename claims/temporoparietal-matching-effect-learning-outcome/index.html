Cao 2026¶

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Block 4 learning-outcome analysis in 62 adults showed "a significant difference between matching and non-matching pairs only in the temporoparietal (TP) cluster in S3" (η²P = .148); no effects in S2 or occipitoparietal cluster, and no group interactions.

"a significant difference between matching and non-matching pairs only in the temporoparietal (TP) cluster in S3 (Matching effect: F(1, 60) = 10.397, p = .002, η²P = .148), with reduced negative ERP responses to matching compared to non-matching pairs"
