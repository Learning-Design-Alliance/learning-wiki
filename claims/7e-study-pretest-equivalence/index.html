Evidence¶

Samaresh Adak 2017¶

Samaresh Adak. (2017). Effectiveness of constructivist approach on academic achievement in science at secondary level. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3298

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-test comparison in the quasi-experiment before intervention: experimental mean 7.1 slightly higher than control mean 6.1, but t = 1.042 with significance value 0.306, so the groups were statistically comparable at baseline. Equivalence was not formally tested beyond this t-test.
