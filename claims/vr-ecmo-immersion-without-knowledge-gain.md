## Evidence

### Rafi Alireza 2025

Rafi Alireza, Habibi Amin, Kalantarion Masomeh. (2025). Traditional medical education under siege: the revolutionary integration of virtual reality and gamification in medical escape rooms. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07367-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports a quasi-experimental study of 66 fourth-year nursing students from three South Korean universities using a VR ECMO simulation program; "no significant differences were found in knowledge, confidence, or clinical reasoning" but immersion and satisfaction were significantly higher. Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "While no significant differences were found in knowledge, confidence, or clinical reasoning, the experimental group reported significantly higher learning immersion and satisfaction."
