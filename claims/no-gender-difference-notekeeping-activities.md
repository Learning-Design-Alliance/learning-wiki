## Evidence

### Matthew Dew 2024

Matthew Dew, Emma Hunt, Viranga Perera, Jonathan Perry, Gregorio Ponti, and Andrew Loveridge. (2024). Group Dynamics in Inquiry-based Labs: Gender Inequities and the Efficacy of Partner Agreements. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010121](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010121)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical linear modeling of the combined laptop, calculator, and paper group fraction in both recorded courses. The article reports no statistically significant gender difference, and separately no significant effects from partner agreements or preferences; equivalence was not tested.

> "In both Physics I Lab and Physics II Lab without partner agreements, we do not see any statistically significant difference in ‘Laptop,' ‘Calculator,' and ‘Paper' group fraction between men and women."
