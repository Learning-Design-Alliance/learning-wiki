# Inserting an interference sequence disrupted and then recovered joint-context performance: RT rose from Block 6 to 7 and fell from Block 7 to 8 only in the joint context

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` RTs increased significantly from Block 6 to the interference Block 7 in the joint context (M = 412 ms to M = 425 ms) but not in the baseline context. [→ Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang 2024](#zheng-zheng-jun-wang-2024)

## Evidence

### Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang 2024

Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang. (2024). Interbrain neural correlates of self and other integration in joint statistical learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00280-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interference-effect LMM on modulating-phase RTs: the phase × context interaction was significant (β = −19.50, P = 0.001), and simple effects showed RT rose significantly in the joint context from Block 6 to Block 7 (β = 13.14, P < 0.001) but not in baseline; no printed effect size.

> "A follow-up simple effect analysis showed that the RTs was signi ﬁcantly larger in Block 7 (M = 425 ms, SE = 6.95) than in Block 6 ( M = 412 ms, SE = 6.95) for the joint context (β = 13.14,SE = 3.38,t = 3.89,P < 0.001)"

### Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang 2024 (2)

Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang. (2024). Interbrain neural correlates of self and other integration in joint statistical learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00280-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Recovery-effect LMM: the phase × context interaction was significant (β = −11.94, P = 0.045); simple effects showed RT fell significantly from Block 7 to Block 8 in the joint context (β = 12.28, P < 0.001) with no difference in baseline; no printed effect size.

> "The follow-up simple effect analysis showed that the RTs was signi ﬁcantly larger in Block 7 (M = 425 ms, SE = 6.95) than in Block 8 ( M = 413 ms, SE = 6.95) for the joint context (β = 12.28, SE = 3.39, t = 3.63, P < 0.001), but no signi ﬁcant differences in RTs were observed between Block 7"
