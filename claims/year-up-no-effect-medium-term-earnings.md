# Year Up may result in little to no change in medium-term earnings

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Based on one RCT meeting WWC standards, Year Up showed no discernible effects on medium-term earnings three years after program completion. [→ medium-term-earnings](#medium-term-earnings)

## Evidence

### medium-term-earnings

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021, November). Year Up. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/722.

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The WWC review of Roder & Elliott (2014), based on 135 students, rated medium-term earnings as no discernible effects; average annual earnings three years after program completion were $19,191 for the intervention group versus $17,257 for the comparison group, an effect size of 0.14, improvement index +5, not statistically significant (p = 0.48).

> "The WWC determined that one study that meets WWC group design standards without reservations (Roder & Elliott, 2014) showed evidence of an indeterminate effect of Year Up on medium-term earnings."
