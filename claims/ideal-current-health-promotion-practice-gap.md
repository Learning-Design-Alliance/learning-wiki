## Evidence

### Pati S. 2017

Pati S., Chauhan A.S., Mahapatra S., Sinha R., Pati S. (2017). Practicing health promotion in primary care –a reflective enquiry. Journal of Preventive Medicine and Hygiene. [doi:10.15167/2421-4248/jpmh2017.58.4.749](https://doi.org/10.15167/2421-4248/jpmh2017.58.4.749)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory two-step self-administered questionnaire study of thirty government primary care physicians at Community Health Centres in Odisha, January–February 2013. Elements were rated on a 10-point Likert scale for ideal and current practice and compared with t-test statistics; "the difference between the mean of current and ideal is highly significant" for all sixteen elements (p < 0.001).

> "It is evident that for each of the 16 elements listed, the difference between the mean of current and ideal is highly significant."
