## Evidence

### New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers 2020

New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers. (2020). Survey slide report of New York State charter schools, May 2020. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/report-3/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey of instructional-mode changes during remote instruction in New York charter schools. The report states that among schools using self-directed learning "most of the day" before closure, "92.3% of them reported even more self-directed learning post-closure," and that the shift did not depend on prior learning models.

> "Of the schools describing themselves as engaging in self-directed learning “most of the day” before building closure, 92.3% of them reported even more self-directed learning post-closure;"

### New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers 2020 (2)

New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers. (2020). Survey slide report of New York State charter schools, May 2020. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/report-3/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same survey section on the pivot to new modes of instruction: schools that previously spent none of the day on self-directed learning still reported increases after closure, with 76% of them calling the increases significant. The report also notes less than 5% of schools spent more than "some" time on self-directed or blended learning before closure.

> "Similarly, 88% of schools that were conducting self-directed learning “none of the day” in the prior period reported increases in self-directed learning after closure, with over three quarters (76%) of them labeling the increases “significant”."
