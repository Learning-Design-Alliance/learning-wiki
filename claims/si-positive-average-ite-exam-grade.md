## Evidence

### Beemer 2018

Beemer, J., Spoon, K., He, L., Fan, J., & Levine, R.A. (2018). Ensemble Learning for Estimating Individualized Treatment Effects in Student Success Studies. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 28, 315–335. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0148-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ITE analysis of the Fall 2013 STAT 119 offering, where 17% of students voluntarily enrolled in the supplemental instruction section (STAT 119A). Both "average ITE are both significantly greater than zero (p< 0.0001)", reflecting an increase of almost half a grade point on the four-point scale. No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is not coded.

> "the average individualized treatment effect for final exam score was 9.3 with a standard error of 1.48. The average individualized treatment effect for final course grade was 0.45 with a standard error of 0.03. These average ITE are both significantly greater than zero (p< 0.0001)."
