## Evidence

### Yıldız 2021

Yıldız, M. (2021). Language choices of language teachers and learners: Meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 17(Special Issue 1), 472-492. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1285162

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesis finding citing Kang (2013) and Owu-Ewie & Eshun (2015); the article reports that teachers "who were not proficient in English preferred to use Ghanaian language (Fante) as a compensatory strategy". Qualitative evidence; no effect size printed.

> "Since many language teachers are not native speaker s of the target language, they may rely more on their first language in which they are more proficient and thus feel more self-confident to express themselves."
