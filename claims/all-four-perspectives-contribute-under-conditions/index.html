Slavin 2014¶

Slavin, R. E. (2014). Cooperative Learning and Academic Achievement: Why Does Groupwork Work?. Anales De Psicología, 30(3), 785–791. https://doi.org/10.6018/analesps.30.3.201201

The review states that despite the primacy of the motivational perspective, "there are conditions under which" methods from all four perspectives contribute to achievement gain. The specific conditions are discussed in the chapter body; no effect sizes are printed in the available text.
