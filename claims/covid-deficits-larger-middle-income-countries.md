`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Bivariate comparison grouping estimates from Brazil, Colombia, Mexico and South Africa against high-income countries (Fig. 6c; medians high −0.12, middle −0.37). The printed mean difference "δ = −0.29" is statistically significant (P = 0.008); no study from a low-income country met the inclusion criteria.

> "The learning deficit is appreciably larger in middle-income countries than in high-income countries (mean difference δ = −0.29, t(41) = −2.78, two-tailed P = 0.008, 95% CI −0.50 to −0.08). In fact, the three largest estimates of learning deficits in our sample are from middle-income countries"
