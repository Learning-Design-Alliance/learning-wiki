## Evidence

### W. K. Black 2023

W. K. Black, Rebecca L. Matz, Mark Mills, and A. E. Evrard. (2023). Practice Makes Better: Quantifying Grade Beneﬁts of Study. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010139](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010139)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

KLLR deviation analysis of PHYSICS 140 and 240 students using GPAO as the baseline for grade expectations (Table VII), analogous to the T-based analysis; the measured gain is less than half the T-based gain, which the article attributes to GPAO's correlation with study volume subsuming much of the study signal.

> "Those with highest study volume tended to do better than expected by 0 .28± 0.07 grade points (averaging over the three study indicators) as compared to those with low study volume."
