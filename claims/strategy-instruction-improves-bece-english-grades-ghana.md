## Evidence

### Agor 2014

John T. Agor. (2014). Language-Learner Strategy Instruction and English Achievement: Voices from Ghana. English Language Teaching, 7(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n6p175

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact classes (57 experimental, 58 control) at a public JHS in Madina, Ghana. The experimental class received explicit strategy instruction embedded in English lessons; the control class received the same content without it. Table 5 reports that 55 of 57 experimental candidates obtained Grade One versus 14 of 58 control candidates.

> "At the end of the academic year, the English la nguage results of the two groups were compared: 96.5% of the experimental class obtained Grade “O ne” while 24% of the control group obtained that Grade."
