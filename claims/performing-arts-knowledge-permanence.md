## Evidence

### Kenan BAŞ 2019

Kenan BAŞ, Esen DURMUŞ. (2019). Pre-test the Effect of Teaching Social Studies Course through Performing Arts on the Students’ Academic Achievement and Permanence of their Knowledge. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies 7(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.7n.2p.107

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Permanence test administered to the experimental group (n = 125) four weeks after the posttest; mean was 74.74 versus posttest 74.22, with no statistically significant difference reported. No effect size is printed for this within-group comparison.

> "In the experimental group, the students’ level of knowledge was checked again by the permanence test performed four weeks after the last test. When we look at the permanence test results, it was seen that there was no statistically significant difference according to the post-test."

### Kenan BAŞ 2019 (2)

Kenan BAŞ, Esen DURMUŞ. (2019). Pre-test the Effect of Teaching Social Studies Course through Performing Arts on the Students’ Academic Achievement and Permanence of their Knowledge. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies 7(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.7n.2p.107

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison of permanence-test means (experimental 74.74, control 60.96) reported as statistically different in favor of the experimental group; only a p-threshold significance statement is printed, with no effect size for this contrast.

> "when the results of permanence test applied to the students 4 weeks after the end of the applications were examined, it was seen that the mean scores of the students in the experimental group and control group were statistically different in favor of the experimental group"
