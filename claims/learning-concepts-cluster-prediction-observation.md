## Evidence

### Amin Bayat Barooni 2018

Amin Bayat Barooni, Joshua Von Korff, Brian D. Thoms, Zeynep Topdemir, Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2018). Three research-based approaches to the design of physics activities. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.098](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.098)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cluster-level frequency pattern from the k-means analysis. The authors connect these features to conceptual change theory, noting prediction and observation "bring out students' pre-conceptions, require comparison with results of experiments, and confirm or refute their understanding".

> "Compared with the other clusters, the most frequent features observed were Prediction and Observation (Table I)."

### Amin Bayat Barooni 2018 (2)

Amin Bayat Barooni, Joshua Von Korff, Brian D. Thoms, Zeynep Topdemir, Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2018). Three research-based approaches to the design of physics activities. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.098](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.098)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cluster membership from Results and Table I; the cluster also includes five SDI Labs, three each from ISLE and TIP, two UMI, and one each from SCL and OST. Designers report a prediction-observation-comparison learning cycle.

> "The Learning Concepts cluster is the largest of the three clusters and includes all six analyzed activities from RTP, WP, Physics Department, Texas Tech University (TTU), and The University of Illinois labs (UI)."
