### Magdin 2016

Magdin, M., & Turčáni, M. (2016). Modeling the Behavior of Students in E-Learning Courses on the Basis of Use Interactive Animations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086224

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interpretation of the transition-matrix data from the five-year log analysis. The authors state that "High figures ranging between 3000 and 5000" correspond to revisits before and after announced written tests.

> "High figures ranging between 3000 and 5000 presented in interactive matrices represent places to which the students returned based on the written test announced in advance and also the places, to which the students returned after completing the test in order to verify the correctness of what they had written into the test."
