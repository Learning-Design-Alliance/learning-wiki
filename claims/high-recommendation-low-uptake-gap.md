## Evidence

### Satman Ilhan 2013

Satman Ilhan, Akalin Sema, Cakir Bekir, Altinel Serdar, Study Group The diaVAX. (2013). The effect of physicians' awareness on influenza and pneumococcal vaccination rates and correlates of vaccination in patients with diabetes in Turkey. Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics 9(12). https://doi.org/10.4161/hv.25826

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective phase of the diaVAX registry: vaccination histories for the past five years obtained from physicians' records and/or patient report. The article reports the recommendation-uptake gap with the printed percentages 87.9%, 83.4%, 27% and 9.8%; no effect size is printed.

> "The data revealed that the participating physicians had recommended influenza and pneumococcal vaccinations to 87.9% and 83.4% of their diabetes patients, respectively during the last five years, but 27% of these patients had received influenza and only 9.8% had received pneumococcal vaccines."
