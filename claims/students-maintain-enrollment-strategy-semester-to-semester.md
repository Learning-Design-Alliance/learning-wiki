## Evidence

### Shahab Boumi 2020

Shahab Boumi, Adan Vela. (2020). Quantifying the relationship between student enrollment patterns and student performance. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2003.10874)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Numerical estimation of HMM transition matrix parameters from approximately 170,000 undergraduate records at UCF (2008–2017). The diagonal of the transition matrix shows "with 0.898 probability, a student employing a FES will continue employing a FES" and similarly high probabilities for PES (0.74) and MES (0.873).

> "Based on the estimated transition matrix A, most students maintain their enrollment strategy with high probabilities from one semester to the next."
