Related Claims¶

  • DysLexLens achieves a mean Answer Relevancy of 0.75 across 30 responses to research questions and follow-ups on dyslexia-AI Reddit data — related
  • DysLexLens is moderately robust to paraphrased queries (Answer Relevancy 0.58) but sensitive to keyword-perturbed queries (0.34) — related
  • Of 100 human-audited claims, 39 are fully verifiable, 55 partially verifiable, and 6 not verifiable, with main responses showing stronger provenance than follow-ups — related
  • Generated answers sometimes added correct background knowledge not explicitly supported by retrieved evidence, an unsupported-answer behavior the authors flag as requiring faithfulness evaluation — related
  • The three specialized chatbot modes achieved their distinct pedagogical goals on RAGAS metrics: Quick Mode highest faithfulness (0.92), Deep Understanding near-perfect context recall (0.98) with intentionally low answer relevancy, Exam Coach highest answer relevancy (0.82) — related
  • KITE's non-procedural responses show strong retrieval grounding (faithfulness 0.85, context relevance 0.94) and answer similarity 0.76, with lower factual correctness 0.45 — related
  • The RAG-enhanced assistant achieves context recall above 0.93 and answer relevance of 0.87 on an expert-built 52-question evaluation set — related
  • AlgoRAG answered all 179 TCS exam-style questions successfully with a mean response time of 38.0 seconds and a pedagogical quality score of 0.7620 — related
  • Artifact-grounded agent responses are rated significantly higher than transcript-only responses on groundedness, analytical depth, helpfulness, relevance, and overall quality — related
