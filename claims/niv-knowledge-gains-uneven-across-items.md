`q2 i?` Item-level McNemar comparisons pre- to postintervention were significant for the contraindications item (68.2% to 90.9% correct, p < 0.001) and ventilator modes item (75.8% to 89.4%, p = 0.049), but not for items on interface choice, complications, programmable parameters, or feeding. ## Evidence

### Pla‐Canalda Elsa 2025

Pla‐Canalda Elsa, Jiménez‐Herrera María F., Fernández‐Sáez José, Concha‐Martínez Pablo, and Martínez‐Segura Estrella. (2025). Impact of Educational Intervention on Noninvasive Ventilation (NIV) in Nurses and Students. The Clinical Teacher 22, no. 5. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level analysis of the 12-item test (Table 3, n = 66 per item, McNemar test). Improvements were "not uniformly across all knowledge domains": question 9 rose from 68.2% to 90.9% correct (p < 0.001) and question 3 from 75.8% to 89.4% (p = 0.049), while questions 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11 and 12 showed non-significant changes.

> "Initial comparisons of preintervention knowledge test results revealed significant improvements in certain areas, though not uniformly across all knowledge domains"
