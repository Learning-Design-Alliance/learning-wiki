Evidence¶

Mokhtari 2015¶

Mokhtari, K., Neel, J. L., Kaiser, F., & Le, H.-H. (2015). Assessing the Promise of a Supplemental Reading Intervention for At-Risk First Grade Students in a Public School Setting. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 7(3), 281-300. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068056

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Analysis of National Curve Equivalent (NCE) scores (mean 50, SD 21.06) across three groups showed tutored at-risk students gained nearly 20 NCEs while "the scores of at-risk non-tutored students actually declined by nearly two points from 30.1 to 28.6". Typically developing peers gained nearly 18 points.
