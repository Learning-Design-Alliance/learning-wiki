## Evidence

### Molitor Vincent 2026

Molitor Vincent, Seiters Johanna Christina, Hoch Jonas, Thürmann Petra, Holle Bernhard, Vollmar Horst Christian, Palm Rebecca. (2026). Improving delirium knowledge and recognition confidence in nursing homes through an e-learning program: a pre–post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09297-2

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Same pre–post pilot sample of 80 nurses; confidence was measured with a single 10-point Likert item (0 = not confident at all, 10 = very confident) before and after the e-learning program. The article reports "the confidence increased to a median of 7 (IQR = 1)", p < 0.001, r = 0.92. Subjective self-report only; no behavioral recognition test.

> "The median subjective confidence in recognizing delirium among nursing home residents on a 10-point Likert scale in the pretest was 5 (IQR = 2.25). In the posttest, the confidence increased to a median of 7 (IQR = 1). The Wilcoxon signed-rank test was used (V = 84.5, p < 0.001), indicating statistical significance. The calculated effect size of the total sample was r = 0.92."
