### Kavas Mustafa Volkan; Demirören Meral; Koşan Ayşen Melek Aytuğ; Karahan Süleyman Tuna; Yalim Neyyire Yasemin 2015

Kavas Mustafa Volkan; Demirören Meral; Koşan Ayşen Melek Aytuğ; Karahan Süleyman Tuna; Yalim Neyyire Yasemin. (2015). Turkish students' perceptions of professionalism at the beginning and at the end of medical education: a cross-sectional qualitative study. Medical Education Online. [doi:10.3402/meo.v20.26614](https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.26614)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of six focus groups with 59 AUFM students (31 Grade-1, 28 Grade-6) found three interrelated features in connotations of 'being a physician': 'students addressed the issue primarily on the individual level', no distinction between good person and good professional, and predominantly positive connotations with no negative G1 views.

> "students addressed the issue primarily on the individual level, while rarely touching upon its societal aspects. Second, students often drew no distinction between traits attributed to 'being a good person' and 'being a good professional'. Last, connotations of 'being a physician' were predominantly positive; in fact, G1 students had no negative views in this regard."
