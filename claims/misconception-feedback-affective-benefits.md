## Evidence

### Anselm Strohmaier 2026

Anselm Strohmaier, Fien Depaepe, Michael Nickl, Andreas Obersteiner. (2026). A domain-specific perspective on adaptivity. Learning and Instruction 104. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102364

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The editorial reports, citing Demedts et al. (2024), a study in which each incorrect number line fraction placement was automatically matched to a known misconception category and explained; "this domain-specific adaptation did not lead to differences in cognitive outcomes".

> "Whereas this domain-specific adaptation did not lead to differences in cognitive outcomes, the study revealed that domain-specific feedback can be beneficial in terms of reducing mathematics anxiety and supporting students' mathematics motivation"
