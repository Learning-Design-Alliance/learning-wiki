## Evidence

### Nir Shalev 2024

Nir Shalev, Sage Boettcher & Anna C. Nobre. (2024). Spatiotemporal predictions guide attention throughout the adult lifespan. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00281-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM on correct-response reaction times from the online dynamic search task (N = 300). The article reports "main effects of Predictability ( β = 0.015; SE = 0.006; t(270.5) = −2.239; p = 0.025)", and states reaction times were faster for predictable targets.

> "Analysis of the reaction times in the dynamic visual-search task revealed main effects of Predictability ( β = 0.015; SE = 0.006; t(270.5) = −2.239; p = 0.025)"

### Nir Shalev 2024 (2)

Nir Shalev, Sage Boettcher & Anna C. Nobre. (2024). Spatiotemporal predictions guide attention throughout the adult lifespan. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00281-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same LMM on reaction times. The Age × Response-Onset Interval interaction "(β = 0.02; SE = 0.007; t(440.5) = 2.788;p = 0.005)" is reported; the authors note the benefit of the conditional probability was attenuated for older adults, though a similar trend appeared across all ages.

> "alongside a two-way interaction of Response-Onset Interval and Age (β = 0.02; SE = 0.007; t(440.5) = 2.788;p = 0.005)"
