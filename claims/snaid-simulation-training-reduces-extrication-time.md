## Evidence

### Segura-Fornieles 2026

Segura-Fornieles, A. J., Márquez-Hernández, V. V., García-Viola, A., Rodríguez-García, M. C., & Garrido-Molina, J. M. (2026). Improving operational preparedness for armored vehicle casualty rescue: effectiveness of simulation-based training with a novel extrication device. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1910210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest study (N = 156); time measured by stopwatch from device contact with the casualty's neck to extraction through the top hatch. Statistically significant reduction reported with no printed effect size.

> "Statistically significant differences were found between the pre- and post-intervention times (p < 0.001). Specifically, the average time in the pre-test was 3.41 min (SD = 1.28), while in the post-test it was 3.06 min (SD = 1.06)."
