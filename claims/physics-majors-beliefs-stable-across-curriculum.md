## Evidence

### Kyle M. Whitcomb 2023

Kyle M. Whitcomb, Alexandru Maries, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Progression in self-efficacy, interest, identity, sense of belonging, perceived recognition and effectiveness of peer interaction of physics majors and comparison with non-majors and Ph.D. students. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.06192](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.06192)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of matched pre/post survey responses plotted in Fig. 1 across four undergraduate years. Interest and self-efficacy "remain fairly consistent"; self-efficacy ranges from 3.17 (year 1 pre) to 2.88 (year 3 post). The authors interpret stability as students re-anchoring item interpretation to current course expectations.

> "We observe that interest (Fig. 1b) and self-efficacy (Fig. 1a) remain fairly consistent, although self-efficacy shows a slight drop from year 1 pre (at 3.17) to year 1 post (3.00), but after that point it remains relatively consistent through year 4"
