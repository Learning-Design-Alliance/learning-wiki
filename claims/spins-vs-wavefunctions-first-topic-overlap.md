Survey-based comparison of topic-importance ratings between 13 spins-first and 41 wavefunctions-first QM1/single-semester courses, using the same 50% threshold. The article reports "notable overlap between the spins-ﬁrst and wavefunctions-ﬁrst courses" with eight topics significant in only one group (Fig. 7).
