Finding synthesized from an included literature review of 48 papers on involvement of people with intellectual disabilities in health and social care education. The review reports "only a small proportion (16%) featured individuals with Disorders of Intellectual Development as co-teachers," with most involvement as patients, simulated patients, or recipients.

> "Review data indicated that approximately half of interventions featured direct interaction between students and people with Disorders of Intellectual Development, however, only a small proportion (16%) featured individuals with Disorders of Intellectual Development as co-teachers [31]."
