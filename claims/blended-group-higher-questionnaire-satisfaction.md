## Evidence

### Huang Yinxue 2025

Huang Yinxue, Yin Xintong, She Rui, Tian Linqing, Cai Ming, Han QunChao, Huo Lijun. (2025). A PBL-, CBL-, and seminar-based blended teaching model for chairside instruction of undergraduate dental students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08214-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous 20-item validated questionnaire (100 points) completed by all 58 students within one week after teaching, with a 100% response rate; "the score of the experimental group was significantly higher than the control group" on all three subscales and total scores (93.17 ± 6.88 vs. 86.10 ± 12.81, p < 0.05).

> "the score of the experimental group was significantly higher than the control group in teaching mode evaluation (38.28 ± 2.67 vs. 35.34 ± 6.13, p < 0.05), self-evaluation of internship effect (25.55 ± 4.13 vs. 23.14 ± 4.17, p < 0.05)"
