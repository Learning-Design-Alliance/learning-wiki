# Interventions lasting ≥24 weeks show larger effects on mathematics and overall academic achievement than shorter interventions

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` For mathematics, interventions ≥24 weeks show a favorable association (SMD = 0.13, 95% CI: 0.03–0.23, p = 0.010; I² = 66%) compared with <24 weeks (SMD = 0.11, 95% CI: 0.01–0.21, p = 0.030; I² = 49%). [→ He Hongxue 2025](#he-hongxue-2025)
`q3 i2` For overall academic achievement, interventions ≥24 weeks show a positive association (SMD = 0.47, 95% CI: 0.25–0.68, p < 0.001; I² = 14%) while <24 weeks shows no association (SMD = 0.13, 95% CI: −0.11 to 0.37, p = 0.300). [→ He Hongxue 2025 (2)](#he-hongxue-2025-2)

## Evidence

### He Hongxue 2025

He Hongxue, Yang Yahui, Sun Jiaojiao, Wang Fang, Zhang Wei and Zhu Fengshu. (2025). Effects of school-based physical activity on academic achievement in children and adolescents: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1651883

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis by intervention duration for mathematics outcomes. The review reports "SMD = 0.13, 95% CI: 0.03–0.23, p = 0.010" for interventions ≥24 weeks.

> "However, a favorable association was found between physical activity and mathematics achievement after an intervention duration ≥24 weeks (SMD = 0.13, 95% CI: 0.03–0.23, p = 0.010; I2 = 66%)."

### He Hongxue 2025 (2)

He Hongxue, Yang Yahui, Sun Jiaojiao, Wang Fang, Zhang Wei and Zhu Fengshu. (2025). Effects of school-based physical activity on academic achievement in children and adolescents: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1651883

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis by intervention duration for overall academic achievement. The review reports "SMD = 0.47, 95% CI: 0.25–0.68, p < 0.001" for interventions ≥24 weeks, with lower heterogeneity (I² = 14%).

> "However, when the intervention duration was ≥24 weeks, a positive association was found between physical activity and overall academic achievement (SMD = 0.47, 95% CI: 0.25–0.68, p < 0.001; I2 = 14%)."
