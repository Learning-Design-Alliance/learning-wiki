## Evidence

### Zhao L 2026

Zhao L, He J, Han Z, Zhang Y, Li L. (2026). Impact of problem-based learning on the development of clinical reasoning skills in esophageal cancer teaching. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047451

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney comparison in the 132-intern retrospective cohort. The PBL group's differential diagnosis lists were longer, "Mann–Whitney U = 968, z = 5.50, P < .001", with a Hodges–Lehmann median difference of 2 items.

> "The PBL group demonstrated a greater number of correctly formulated differential diagnosis items (median 8, IQR 7–9) than the control group (median 6, IQR 5–7); Mann–Whitney U = 968, z = 5.50, P < .001, with a Hodges–Lehmann median difference of 2 items."

### Zhao L 2026 (2)

Zhao L, He J, Han Z, Zhang Y, Li L. (2026). Impact of problem-based learning on the development of clinical reasoning skills in esophageal cancer teaching. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047451

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blinded-assessor case presentation scores in the same cohort favored PBL interns, "Welch t = 7.883, P < .001", a mean difference of 8.4 points on a 0–100 scale.

> "In addition, structured case presentation quality scores were higher in the PBL group (88.6 ± 5.4) compared with the control group (80.2 ± 6.8), Welch t = 7.883, P < .001."
