## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ito Takamichi 2022

Ito Takamichi, Umemoto Takatoyo. (2022). Examining the causal relationships between interpersonal motivation, engagement, and academic performance among university students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0274229

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling of data from 363 Japanese undergraduates in psychology lecture courses with pair work; Table 3 reports standardized β = .22 (behavioral), .22 (emotional), .25 (cognitive), each p < .01, described as "weak positive paths from identified regulation".

> "First, the weak positive paths from identified regulation toward all three aspects of engagement were substantial."
