# No statistically significant differences between SNAPPS and OMP groups in meaning units related to DD, MP, or LI

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Meaning units related to differential diagnoses (DD), management plans (MP), and learning issues (LI) did not differ significantly between residents taught with SNAPPS and those taught with the OMP. [→ Seki Masayasu 2016](#seki-masayasu-2016)

## Evidence

### Seki Masayasu 2016

Seki Masayasu, Otaki Junji, Breugelmans Raoul, Komoda Takayuki, Nagata-Kobayashi Shizuko, Akaishi Yu, Hiramoto Jun, Ohno Iwao, Harada Yoshimi, Hirayama Yoji, Izumi Miki (2016). How do case presentation teaching methods affect learning outcomes?-SNAPPS and the One-Minute preceptor. BMC Medical Education 16. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0531-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same randomized comparative study (n = 71); Mann–Whitney U tests of the counted meaning units found "no statistically significant differences in the number of meaning units related to DD, MP, or LI" between the two teaching-method groups.

> "In our study, we observed no statistically significant differences in the number of meaning units related to DD, MP, or LI that were elicited from residents who presented and discussed the case by either SNAPPS or the OMP"
