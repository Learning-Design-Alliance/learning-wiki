Evidence¶

M. Danish Shakeel · Paul E. Peterson 2022¶

M. Danish Shakeel · Paul E. Peterson. (2022). A Half Century of Progress in US Student Achievement: Agency and Flynn Effects, Ethnic and SES Differences. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09657-y

q3 · i? · associational · r3

The abstract and introduction report period-specific trends, noting "a flattening in the upward math trend for the most recent period"; the article gives special attention to the recent period because intelligence studies find slowed IQ growth.

"Consistent with Flynn effects, median trends show larger gains in m (0.19sd) than in rd (0.04sd), though rates of progress for cohorts born since 1990 have increased in rd but slowed in m."
