Evidence¶

Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser 2023¶

Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser. (2023). Generative artificial intelligence enhances creativity but reduces the diversity of novel content. https://aspredicted.org/136723

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Post-disclosure ownership ratings in the evaluator phase of the experiment; the discount was 31.0% (b = –2.40, p<0.001) for writers with access to up to five GenAI ideas.

"evaluators ascribed 25.4% less ownership to authors who had access to one GenAI idea, relative to writers in the Human only condition (b = –1.96, p<0.001, see Table S13; compared to the Human only mean of 7.74)."

Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser 2023 (2)¶

Anil R. Doshi and Oliver P. Hauser. (2023). Generative artificial intelligence enhances creativity but reduces the diversity of novel content. https://aspredicted.org/136723

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Hypothetical profit-split elicitation, asked only for GenAI-condition stories whose writers requested at least one idea; the relationship was no longer statistically significant once the ownership index was controlled (a one-SD ownership increase added 16.2% of profits to writers, b=7.70, p<0.001).

"evaluators only impose a marginally significant penalty of 2.3% to writers who had access to 5 GenAI ideas (b= –2.30, p=0.072, see Table S14), relative to having access to 1 GenAI idea."
