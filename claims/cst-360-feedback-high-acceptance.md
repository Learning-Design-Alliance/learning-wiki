### Engerer Cosima 2016

Engerer Cosima, Berberat Pascal O., Dinkel Andreas, Rudolph Baerbel, Sattel Heribert, Wuensch Alexander. (2016). Integrating 360° behavior-orientated feedback in communication skills training for medical undergraduates: concept, acceptance and students’ self-ratings of communication competence. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0792-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation questionnaire study of the new CST with 34 first-clinical-year medical students (82% female), rated on a 6-point scale from 1 'very good' to 6 'insufficient' after the workshop; response rate was 100%. The article reports "overall impression (M = 1.56)" and "constructive learning atmosphere (M = 1.18)".

> "The concept achieved high acceptance from good to very good: overall impression (M = 1.56), sufficient interaction for discussion (M = 1.15), and constructive learning atmosphere (M = 1.18)."
