# Students proposed analyzing task allocation between humans and AI in PBL reports because tasks differ in the skills they nurture

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In designing AI usage reports, participants wished to differentiate tasks handled by humans versus AI, since different tasks carry different weights in nurturing and showcasing skills, and because humans and AI have advantages in different tasks. [→ Chengbo Zheng 2024](#chengbo-zheng-2024)

## Evidence

### Chengbo Zheng 2024

Chengbo Zheng, Kangyu Yuan, Bingcan Guo, Reza Hadi Mogavi, Zhenhui Peng, Shuai Ma, and Xiaojuan Ma. (2024). Charting the Future of AI in Project-Based Learning: A Co-Design Exploration with Students. [doi:10.1145/3613904.3642807](https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642807)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In report design, participants proposed differentiating human versus AI tasks because "different tasks within the project have different weights in nurturing and showcasing various skills"; one participant visualized the division with annotated task blocks, time spent, and required creativity.

> "An important design consideration stems from the students' view that different tasks within the project have different weights in nurturing and showcasing various skills."
