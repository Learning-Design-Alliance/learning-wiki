## Evidence

### Dionyssopoulos A 2014

Dionyssopoulos A, Karalis T, Panitsides EA. (2014). Continuing medical education revisited: theoretical assumptions and practical implications: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0278-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of 12 EWAPS participants; this parameter was outlined by the majority of interviewees, who considered the short-presentation format to have "considerably reduced the lecture-centered approach" in favour of dialogue and reflection.

> "each presentation should not normally exceed three minutes, followed by a twelve minute discussion. The specific practice is considered to have considerably reduced the lecture-centered approach, allowing sufficient time for prompting dialogue and promoting critical reflection and interaction"
