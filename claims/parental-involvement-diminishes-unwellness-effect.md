## Evidence

### Liu Keqiao 2023

Liu Keqiao. (2023). Middle school students’ mental unwellness and academic performance in China: The effects of parental involvement. PLoS One 18(11): e0294172. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294172

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

HLM Model 2 on the same CEPS panel adds parental non-academic activity involvement, mother-child and father-child communication; the Chinese unwellness coefficient drops to "β = -0.891, p < 0.05". Math and English coefficients become -1.264 and -1.316, still significant.

> "After controlling for parental involvement variables, this negative relationship remained, though to a slightly lesser extent, β = -0.891, p < 0.05 (Table 3, Model 2)."
