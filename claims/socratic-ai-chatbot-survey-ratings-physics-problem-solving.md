### Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi 2025

Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Analyzing Undergraduate Problem-Solving in Physics Through Interaction With an AI Chatbot. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14778

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-activity Qualtrics survey (20 Likert items) completed by 150 first-year STEM majors after a 50-minute chatbot session in a first-semester calculus-based mechanics course. Composite scores per survey section showed "Knowledge-Based Skills had the high- est overall ratings (median ≈ 4.0)" and positive overall effectiveness ratings.

> "We observe that Knowledge-Based Skills had the high- est overall ratings (median ≈ 4.0). The Overall Effective- ness section was also rated positively (median ≈ 3.4), indi- cating that many students would recommend the chatbot"
