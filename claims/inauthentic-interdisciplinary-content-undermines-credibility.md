### Ian Descamps 2020

Ian Descamps, Thomas Moore, Benjamin Pollard. (2020). What is interdisciplinarity? Views from students and profe ssors in a non-major intro physics course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2005.05360](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2005.05360)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In an interview with physics professor PCS (pseudonym), the professor recounted that previously included biology content "often looks to be pandering and wasn’t genuine," that students saw through it, and that this loss of credibility made the class suffer.

> "There was deﬁnitely a sense that the biology that was included in it previously often looks to be pandering and wasn’t genuine. And they saw through that. And when students feel that they’re being pandered to, I think you lose a lot of the credibility."
