## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the Summer 2023 and Winter 2023/2024 terms, students scored significantly lower on LLM-generated debugging tasks than on instructor-designed tasks in both Operations Research and Cryptography courses. [→ Kohen-Vacs 2025](#kohen-vacs-2025)

Kohen-Vacs, D., Usher, M., & Jansen, M. (2025). Integrating Generative AI into Programming Education: Student Perceptions and the Challenge of Correcting AI Errors. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00496-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired-samples t-test from the Summer 2023 Operations Research exam comparing LLM-task with instructor-designed task scores; only test statistics are printed (t(23) = 2.61, p =.01), no effect size, so no magnitude is reported.

> "students scored considerably lower on LLM-generated tasks ( M = 0.57, SD = 0.70) than on instructor-designed tasks ( M = 7.00, SD = 6.48), with statistically significant ( t(23) = 2.61, p =.01)"
