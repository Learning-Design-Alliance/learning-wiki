# Self-efficacy for medical emergency management is lower in 4th-year students, interns, females, higher-GPA trainees, and with longer time since BLS certification

### Alawaji YN 2025

Alawaji YN, Alqahtani HM, Alfadley A, Alshehri A, Aboalela A, Altuwaijri SH, Albalawi F, Kalagi SA, Alghamdi A, Alshibani A, Alharbi M, Binhotan M, Alharthy NA. (2025). Development and validation of scales measuring self-efficacy and behavioral capability in managing common medical emergencies in dental clinics. BMC Medical Education 25:1059. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07663-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Univariate linear regression on the summed self-efficacy scores of 140 dental trainees, reported in the Results section (Table 2). Adjusted R2 values of 8.9% and 8.3% are printed for these models.

> "Self-efficacy was significantly lower in 4th year students and interns compared to 3rd year students, with a mean reduction of 17.6-point (95% CI: -27.4, -7.7) and 18.2-point (95% CI: -32.6, -3.8), respectively"

### Alawaji YN 2025 (2)

Alawaji YN, Alqahtani HM, Alfadley A, Alshehri A, Aboalela A, Altuwaijri SH, Albalawi F, Kalagi SA, Alghamdi A, Alshibani A, Alharbi M, Binhotan M, Alharthy NA. (2025). BMC Medical Education 25:1059. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07663-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same univariate regression analysis of the 140-trainee cross-sectional data; gender (p = 0.007) and GPA (p = 0.037) each predicted lower self-efficacy in separate models.

> "Female students showed a 12.5-point lower self-efficacy (95% CI: -21.5, -3.4) compared to males. Additionally, higher GPAs were associated with slightly lower self-efficacy scores (9.9 points lower, 95% CI: -19.1, -0.6)"

### Alawaji YN 2025 (3)

Alawaji YN, Alqahtani HM, Alfadley A, Alshehri A, Aboalela A, Altuwaijri SH, Albalawi F, Kalagi SA, Alghamdi A, Alshibani A, Alharbi M, Binhotan M, Alharthy NA. (2025). BMC Medical Education 25:1059. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07663-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression models on time since last BLS certification for the same sample, printed in Table 2 with p-values of 0.049 and < 0.001 and adjusted R2 of 5.8% and 10.2% respectively.

> "Participants certified within 6 to < 12 months had a 14.6-point reduction (95% CI: -29.2, -0.1), and those certified ≥ 12 months ago experienced a 19.9-point reduction (95% CI: -30.7, -9.1), compared to those certified within the last 6 months"
