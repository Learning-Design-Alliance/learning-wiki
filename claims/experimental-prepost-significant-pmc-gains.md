## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired-samples t-tests showed significant pre-to-post gains in the experimental groups on NWRP (t = -13.69, p < .05) and NWRC (t = -12.96, p < .05). [→ Farvardin et al. 2014](#farvardin-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Farvardin et al. 2014

Mohammad Taghi Farvardin, Akbar Afghari & Mansour Koosha. (2014). The Effect of Dual N-Back Task Training on Phonological Memory Expansion in Adult EFL Learners at the Beginner Level. English Language Teaching, 7(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n9p137

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests comparing Time 1 and Time 2 within the experimental group (n = 57): NWRP rose from M = 14.65 to 16.98 and NWRC from M = 9.89 to 12.30, both significant. The article attributes the gains to the dual n-back treatment.

> "Table 5 depicts that there is a significant difference be tween the mean scores of th e experimental groups in pre-test and post-test (NWRP test: t = -13.69, p < .05; NWRC: t = -12.96, p < .05)."
