## Evidence

### Doo 2023

Doo, M. Y., Bonk, C. J., & Heo, H. (2023). Examinations of the relationships between self-efficacy, self-regulation, teaching, cognitive presences, and learning engagement during COVID-19. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10187-3

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

SEM results from 1435 undergraduates supported H9 with "β = 0.592, t = 12.705, p < 0.001". The authors report full mediation for teaching presence and partial mediation for self-regulation through cognitive presence.

> "Finally, the positive relationship between cognitive presence and learning engagement was found (β = 0.592, t = 12.705, p < 0.001); thus, H9 was supported."
