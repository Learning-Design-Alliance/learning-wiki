## Evidence

### Delgado 2026

Delgado, W., & Sartain, L. (2026). Classroom composition affects teacher performance ratings. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 48(2). https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/classroom-composition-affects-teacher-performance-ratings

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' exploratory mechanism analysis in the Chicago Public Schools data found the rating effects "were not reflected in outcome-based measures of teacher productivity", which they interpret as evidence of evaluator bias.

> "Furthermore, our exploration of mechanisms indicated that these effects were not reflected in outcome-based measures of teacher productivity (i.e., student growth measures), pointing to evaluator bias rather than genuine changes in teacher effectiveness as the likely driver."
