# Gaming, sewing, or knitting experience speeds novices' needle grasping on both systems, but not more complex exercises

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students reporting gaming, sewing, or knitting had significantly shorter times for grasping and placing a needle on both the microscope and the Symani (p < 0.001). [→ Wieker H 2025](#wieker-h-2025)
`q3 i?` No significant tech-savvy advantage appeared for needle threading (microscope p = 0.711; Symani p = 0.493) or the anastomosis suture (microscope p = 0.197; Symani p = 0.089). [→ Wieker H 2025 (2)](#wieker-h-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Wieker H 2025

Wieker H, Brandenburg N, Spille D, Wagner J, Weitkamp J-T, Wiltfang J, Spille J. (2025). Simulation and comparison of objectively graded microsurgery steps by using a microscope and Symani robotic system by young professionals. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02490-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of exercise 1 times between students reporting gaming, sewing, or knitting experience and those without, in the 40-student study; the tech-savvy group was significantly faster on both systems (p < 0.001). No effect size is printed.

> "The average time for the tech-savvy group was significantly shorter for both the microscope and the Symani (p < 0.001)."

### Wieker H 2025 (2)

Wieker H, Brandenburg N, Spille D, Wagner J, Weitkamp J-T, Wiltfang J, Spille J. (2025). Simulation and comparison of objectively graded microsurgery steps by using a microscope and Symani robotic system by young professionals. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02490-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same Mann–Whitney comparisons for exercise 3 (anastomosis suture): no significant difference for the tech-savvy group on either system (p = 0.197 microscope; p = 0.089 Symani). Exercise 2 likewise showed no difference (p = 0.711; p = 0.493). No effect sizes are printed.

> "There was no significant difference in the average time for the tech-savvy group for the microscope (p = 0.197) or the Symani (p = 0.089)."
