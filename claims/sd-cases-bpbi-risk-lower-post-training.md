## Evidence

### Kaijomaa M 2023

Kaijomaa M, Gissler M, Äyräs O, Sten A, Grahn P. (2023). Impact of simulation training on the management of shoulder dystocia and incidence of permanent brachial plexus birth injury: An observational study. BJOG. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.17278

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational analysis of 248 register-identified SD cases (62 pre-training, 184 post-training) with maternal documentation review. The study reports BPBI risk "among cases of SD was significantly lower (43.5% vs 6.0%, p < 0.001)" after training began.

> "After the implementation of systematic simulation training, the risk for permanent BPBI among cases of SD was significantly lower (43.5% vs 6.0%, p < 0.001)."
