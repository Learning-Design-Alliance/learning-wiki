## Evidence

### Starch and Elliott 1913

Daniel Starch and Edward C. Elliott. (1913). Reliability of Grading Work in Mathematics. The School Review, 21(4), 254–259. https://doi.org/10.1086/436086

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational study in which one pupil's geometry final examination was reproduced and graded independently by mathematics teachers in North Central Association high schools; 138 usable grades were returned. The authors report the distribution is a "normal distribution" with extremely wide range; Fig. 3 gives median 70 and probable error 7.5.

> "The investigation shows the extremely wide variation of the grades even more forcibly than our study of English marks. The distribution considered purely from the statistical standpoint is a normal distribution just like that of any set of mental or physical measurements."
