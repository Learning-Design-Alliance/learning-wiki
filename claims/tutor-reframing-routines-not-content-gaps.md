### Yoo Dong Mi 2026

Yoo Dong Mi, Cho A Ra, Kim Sun. (2026). Enacted practices and developmental experiences of senior medical student tutors in a structured peer tutoring program. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.111

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic finding from analysis of 24 tutor activity reports in the diagnostic-assessment domain; Table 2 quotes a tutor: "I thought I'd be explaining concepts, but I was stabilizing daily routines."

> "Tutors reported that tutees' difficulties often stemmed from unstable routines and ineffective study habits rather than specific content gaps. By reviewing schedules, assessment results, and study processes, tutors identified behavioral, cognitive, and environmental barriers requiring targeted intervention."
