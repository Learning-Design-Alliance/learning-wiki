# Repeated measures ANOVA confirms significant within-group improvement over time in both intervention groups but not control

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Repeated measures ANOVA showed significant differences over time in the face-to-face (p < 0.001, η² = 0.972) and m-learning (p < 0.001, η² = 0.951) groups, but no notable difference in the control group. [→ Sabeti Fahimeh 2026](#sabeti-fahimeh-2026)

## Evidence

### Sabeti Fahimeh 2026

Sabeti Fahimeh, Ranjbar Azadeh, Kashaki Mandana, Khanjari Sedigheh, Banazadeh Marjan. (2026). Comparing between face-to-face and mobile learning for improving nurses’ performance in premature infant positioning: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09855-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with 90 NICU nurses. Repeated measures ANOVA across three timepoints yielded η² = 0.972 (face-to-face) and η² = 0.951 (m-learning). The quote confirms "significant differences were observed in both the face-to-face training (p < 0.001) and m-learning (p < 0.001) groups."

> "The findings from the repeated measures analysis of variance indicated no notable difference in the positioning performance of participants within the control group throughout the study. In contrast, significant differences were observed in both the face-to-face training (p < 0.001) and m-learning (p < 0.001) groups."
