## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Dean William H. 2021

Dean William H., Gichuhi Stephen, Buchan John C., Makupa William, Mukome Agrippa, Otiti-Sengeri Juliet, Arunga Simon, Mukherjee Subhashis, Kim Min J., Harrison-Williams Lloyd, MacLeod David, Cook Colin, Burton Matthew J. JAMA Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaophthalmol.2020.4718

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Masked simulation assessments at 1 year and 15 months from the same multicenter RCT. Table 2 shows the 15-month difference of 1.9 points was not significant (P = .26), after the control group received the intervention training. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "Scores at 1 year were 8.5 points (95% CI, 6.7-10.9; P < .001) higher in the intervention group compared with the controls, adjusting for baseline scores, supporting a continued benefit from the training intervention."
