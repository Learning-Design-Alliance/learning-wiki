The same subgroup analysis reports that on-track rates "among Black young men increased by 28 percentage points" to over 71 percent, and among Latinx young men climbed from 52 to 77 percent, between 2005 and 2013.

"Between 2005 and 2013, Freshman OnTrack rates among Black young men increased by 28 percentage points so that by 2013, over 71 percent of Black young men in CPS were on-track for graduation."
