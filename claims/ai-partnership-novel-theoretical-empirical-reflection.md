# Cognitive partnerships with AI enable students to engage in theoretical and empirical reflection that the authors characterize as genuinely novel epistemic practices

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · qualitative `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students use AI to generate alternative perspectives and evaluate their work against subject-specific concepts, engaging in theoretical reflection on their own work. [→ Dalsgaard 2026](#dalsgaard-2026)
`q2 i?` Students use AI to identify and explain weaknesses in their work, demonstrating empirical reflection that is difficult without external input. [→ Dalsgaard 2026 (2)](#dalsgaard-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Dalsgaard 2026

Dalsgaard, C., & Prilop, C. N. (2026). AI as a cognitive partner in learning: Emerging epistemic practices of reflective thought. Journal of Research on Technology in Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2695013

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interpretive finding from the interview analysis: students' inspiration and improvement practices prompt theoretical reflection. The authors state these reflection processes are "difficult, if not impossible, to perform without external input"; no effect size is reported.

> "Theoretical reflection occurs when students use AI to generate ideas for alternative perspectives to incorporate into their task solutions, prompting them to engage in theoretical reflection on their own work."

### Dalsgaard 2026 (2)

Dalsgaard, C., & Prilop, C. N. (2026). AI as a cognitive partner in learning: Emerging epistemic practices of reflective thought. Journal of Research on Technology in Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2695013

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interpretive finding from the same thematic analysis: students' shortcomings-identification practice demonstrates empirical reflection. The article reports this qualitatively, with no effect size.

> "Students also demonstrate empirical reflection when they use AI to identify and explain weaknesses in their work or to suggest adaptations to elements of their task solutions."
