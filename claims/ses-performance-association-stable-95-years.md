## Evidence

### Sophie von Stumm 2022

Sophie von Stumm, Sophie Nicole Cave and Paul Wakeling. (2022). Persistent association between family socioeconomic status and primary school performance in Britain over 95 years. npj Science of Learning (2022) 7:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00120-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Trend examination of the 16 cohort estimates plotted against birth years 1921–2011 in Fig. 1 and in linear-trend models (Fig. 3); the association "varied little and inconsistently", contradicting the assumption that family-background inequality increased over time. No effect size is printed for the trend itself.

> "By and large, the association between family SES and children’s school performance varied little and inconsistently across cohorts’ birth years. No systematic increasing or decreasing trends in the strength of the association could be observed across the past 95 years."
