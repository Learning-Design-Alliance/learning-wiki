# Charter high school students had comparable high school graduation but much higher four-year college enrollment, selective-college enrollment, and four-semester college completion than comparable non-charter students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among typical students entering high school 2008-10, the four-year college enrollment rate was 45.1 percent for charter students versus 26.2 percent for non-charter students, and very selective enrollment was 7.2 versus 2.2 percent. [→ Gwynne 2017](#gwynne-2017)
`q3 i?` Among high school graduates, 21.4 percent of charter students completed at least four semesters of college versus 13.0 percent of non-charter graduates; among college enrollees, however, completion rates were similar at around 53 percent for both groups. [→ Gwynne 2017 (2)](#gwynne-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Gwynne 2017

Gwynne, J.A., & Moore, P.T. (2017). Chicago's charter high schools: Organizational features, enrollment, school transfers, and student performance. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://cps.edu/NewSchools/Pages/RFP2017.aspx

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chapter 5 educational attainment analysis using the second sample of 81,257 first-time ninth-graders (2008-10 cohorts) in 133 high schools, 36 charters, with National Student Clearinghouse data and models controlling for incoming skills and background; charter students showed much higher college enrollment.

> "the four- year college enrollment rate was 45.1 percent for those who attended a charter high school, compared to 26.2 percent for students who attended a non-charter high school. Enrollment rates in very selective colleges and universities were also higher for charter school stu- dents—7.2 percent compared to 2.2 percent"

### Gwynne 2017 (2)

Gwynne, J.A., & Moore, P.T. (2017). Chicago's charter high schools: Organizational features, enrollment, school transfers, and student performance. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://cps.edu/NewSchools/Pages/RFP2017.aspx

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chapter 5 college persistence analysis of the 2008-10 cohorts among high school graduates, showing higher four-semester completion for charter graduates (21.4 vs 13.0 percent); among college enrollees, completion rates were similar at around 53 percent for both groups.

> "Among high school graduates, charter school students were more likely to complete at least four semesters of college than students who attended a non-charter high school, after controlling for differences in incoming skills, experiences, and background characteristics— 21.4 percent compared to 13.0 percent."
