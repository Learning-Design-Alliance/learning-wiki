Kuemmerli Christoph, Linke Katja, Daume Diana, Germann Nicolas, Peterli Ralph, Müller-Stich Beat, Klasen Jennifer M. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures analysis in a randomized trial of 24 surgical residents (nine completers) at a Swiss tertiary hospital; the hospital-based group's objective and subjective ratings improved significantly ("p = .006 and 0.003"). No effect size value is printed.

> "Both the objective and subjective assessment ratings improved statistically significantly between the baseline and the 12-week time point of the hospital-based training group over all five exercises (p = .006 and 0.003)."
