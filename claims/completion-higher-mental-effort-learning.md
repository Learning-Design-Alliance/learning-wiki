# Concept map completion imposed higher mental effort during the guided-learning activity than concept map study

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` There was a main effect of learning condition (reported by the article as a large effect size) on mental effort invested in the guided-learning activity, with higher effort in concept map completion than in concept map study. [→ Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017](#montpetit-tourangeau-k-2017)

## Evidence

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

2×2 ANOVA on Paas nine-point mental effort ratings after the guided-learning session; Table 3 reports means of 7.1 (completion) vs 6.3 (study), F(1,57) = 9.048, p = .004, ηp² = .137. No prior ability effect or interaction.

> "There was a main effect of the learning condition (large effect size) on the mental effort invested by learners in the guided learning activity, with higher mental effort invested in concept map completion than in concept map study"
