Case-study analysis of Ken's think-aloud attempt at the Gravitron task. After engaging the Denotative Function (drawing a representation) and beginning the Demonstration component, Ken acknowledged being stuck, left to solve other problems, and on returning calculated centripetal acceleration before finally abandoning his pursuit without a claim.

> "Unable to relate the forces, Ken discontinues his attempt, and proceeds to solve other problems from the set."
