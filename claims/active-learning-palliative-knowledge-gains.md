## Evidence

### Thammasin I 2025

Thammasin I, Orapan F, Pawita L. (2025). Enhancing thai fifth-year medical students’ knowledge and attitudes toward palliative care through active learning methods. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07659-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest analysis of the n = 62 subgroup with complete pre- and post-course data, using paired-samples t-tests on knowledge scores. The article reports "Significant improvements" in principles, assessment, and pain management, with advance care planning also significant (p < 0.001). No effect sizes were printed.

> "Significant improvements were observed in the knowledge of palliative care principles (pre: 1.94 ± 0.76, post: 2.54 ± 0.64, p < 0.001), assessment (pre: 1.89 ± 0.94, post: 3.31 ± 1.20, p < 0.001), pain management (pre: 1.56 ± 0.72, post: 2.06 ± 0.67, p < 0.001)"
