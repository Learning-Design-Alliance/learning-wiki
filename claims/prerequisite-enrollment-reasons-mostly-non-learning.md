## Evidence

### Sato Brian K. 2017

Sato Brian K., Lee Amanda K., Alam Usman, Dang Jennifer V., Dacanay Samantha J., Morgado Pedro, Pirino Giorgia, Brunner Jo Ellen, Castillo Leanne A., Chan Valerie W., Sandholtz Judith H. (2017). What’s in a Prerequisite? A Mixed-Methods Approach to Identifying the Impact of a Prerequisite Course. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0260

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Semistructured interviews with 29 of 47 volunteering MLab students, coded by two researchers (92% agreement, Cohen’s κ = 0.88 on survey free responses; interview coding κ = 0.80 for enrollment reasons). Reasons for taking included background knowledge (46.7%), scheduling (40.0%), and graduate-school requirements (26.7%); reasons for not taking were led by its non-required status (50.0%) and scheduling (35.7%).

> "The most common responses for why students did not enroll in MLec before MLab include the fact that it was not required, highlighting that students are guided strongly by the program rules laid out for them, or scheduling issues prevented them from doing so"
