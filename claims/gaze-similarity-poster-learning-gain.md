## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Gaze similarity during the poster phase correlates positively with the pair's average first posttest score (r = 0.51). [→ Sharma Kshitij 2020](#sharma-kshitij-2020)

## Evidence

### Sharma Kshitij 2020

Sharma Kshitij, Leftheriotis Ioannis, Giannakos Michail. (2020). Utilizing Interactive Surfaces to Enhance Learning, Collaboration and Engagement: Insights from Learners’ Gaze and Speech. Sensors (Basel). https://doi.org/10.3390/s20071964

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of dual eye-tracking data from 18 dyads in a museum-like lab; gaze similarity was computed as cosine similarity of proportionality gaze vectors in 10 s windows. The printed effect size is "r (16) = 0.51, p = .03".

> "we observe a significant correlation between the gaze similarity during the poster phase and the first posttest score (r (16) = 0.51, p = .03). The pairs having high gaze similarity have high average first posttest score"
