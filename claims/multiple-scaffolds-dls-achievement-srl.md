## Evidence

### Rebecka Rundquist 2024

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Lin and Yang's (2021) experimental design comparing three classes: multiple scaffolds (SRL, computer supported collaborative learning, group presentation, teacher scaffolding), flipped classroom, and traditional teaching.

> "The results show that using a DLS supported with multiple scaffolds can lead to better learning achievements and more student activity. Students also had a more positive attitude towards SRL as they scored higher on planning, self-monitoring, evaluation, reflection, and effort (Lin & Yang, 2021)."

### Rebecka Rundquist 2024 (2)

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports the subgroup contrast within Lin and Yang (2021): the scaffolds condition beat traditional teaching among high-achieving students but did not differ significantly from the flipped classroom condition.

> "High-achieving students who received multiple scaffolds performed significantly better than the high-achieving students who received traditional teaching, but there were no significant differences between the high-achieving students receiving multiple scaffolds compared to the flipped classroom."
