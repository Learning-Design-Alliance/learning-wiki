Review synthesis of four RCTs comparing cadaveric with low-fidelity simulation. The review reports "a mixed picture": two favoured cadaveric training, one found equivalence, and one found cadaveric training inferior to the bench model.

> "The four RCTs48, 49, 50, 51 in this area revealed a mixed picture: two49, 51 showed superiority of cadaveric simulation training, one48 showed equivalence with low‐fidelity bench‐model training, and one50 showed that cadaveric simulation training was inferior to the bench‐model alternative."
