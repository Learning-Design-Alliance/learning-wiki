`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted mixed-effects regression from the KaziAfya trial (n = 559): no intervention arm significantly affected mathematics scores; baseline mathematics scores were a strong predictor (p < 0.001). No effect size was printed.

> "None of the intervention groups showed significant effects on the performance in mathematics. However, sex was a significant predictor, with boys scoring higher than girls (p = 0.04), and age was negatively associated with performance in mathematics (p = 0.03)."
