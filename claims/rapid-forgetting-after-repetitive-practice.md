## Evidence

### Taewon Kim 2021

Taewon Kim, John J. Buchanan, Jessica A. Bernard and David L. Wright. (2021). Improving online and offline gain from repetitive practice using anodal tDCS at dorsal premotor cortex. npj Science of Learning 6:31. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00109-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational finding from the post-immediate test, which occurred after about 3–5 min used to remove tDCS electrodes and complete a questionnaire; the authors note a similar increase in total time across this interval in two other recent papers including the RP-S condition.

> "The fact that performance deteriorates in this manner suggests that the forgetting commonly observed following RP formats is incredibly rapid, further supporting the common claim that exposure to this practice scheduling condition is particularly ineffective for retention."
