## Evidence

### Ranellucci 2017

Ranellucci, J., Hall, N., Muis, K., Lajoie, S., & Robinson, K. (2017). Mastery, Maladaptive Learning Behaviour, and Academic Achievement: An Intervention Approach. Canadian Journal of Education, 40(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1166656

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical regression on Time 2 interest-based studying. Interest-based studying was lower for poor-performing students in the intervention condition (M = 2.74) than controls (M = 3.06), while high-achieving students reported higher levels (M = 3.56 vs. M = 3.25).

> "Regression findings for the social desirability intervention revealed a significant interaction between the social desirability intervention and prior GPA on interest-based studying, β = -.24, p = .051"
