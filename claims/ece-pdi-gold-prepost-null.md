## Evidence

### Whalen 2016

Whalen, Samuel P. PhD; Horsley, Heather L. PhD; Parkinson, Kathleen K. MEd; and Pacchiano, Debra PhD (2016) "A Development Evaluation Study of a Professional Development Initiative to Strengthen Organizational Conditions in Early Education Settings," Journal of Applied Research on Children: Informing Policy for Children at Risk: Vol. 7 : Iss. 2 , Article 9. http://digitalcommons.library.tmc.edu/childrenatrisk/vol7/iss2/9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre/post design using hierarchical linear models on 1-year and 2-year child cohorts (N = 1,162; 208 intervention, 924 comparison), adjusting for age-standardized baseline measures, child-level, and center-level characteristics. The article reports no significant effect; equivalence was not tested.

> "The results for the GOLD domain measures and the GOLD English Language Acquisition composite scale indicated that the ECE PDI model did not have a significant effect on the children's learning and development."

### Whalen 2016 (2)

Whalen, Samuel P. PhD; Horsley, Heather L. PhD; Parkinson, Kathleen K. MEd; and Pacchiano, Debra PhD (2016) "A Development Evaluation Study of a Professional Development Initiative to Strengthen Organizational Conditions in Early Education Settings," Journal of Applied Research on Children: Informing Policy for Children at Risk: Vol. 7 : Iss. 2 , Article 9. http://digitalcommons.library.tmc.edu/childrenatrisk/vol7/iss2/9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same HLM pre/post analysis testing enrollment duration as a moderator of intervention effects on post-intervention GOLD scores. No significant interaction was detected between exposure length (2 years versus 1 year) and condition.

> "We did not detect any significant interaction effects of the ECE PDI on post -intervention scores for children enrolled in ECE PDI centers for 2 years versus 1 year."
