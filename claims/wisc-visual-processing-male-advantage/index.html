Evidence¶

D. Giofrè · K. Allen · E. Toffalini · S. Caviola 2022¶

D. Giofrè · K. Allen · E. Toffalini · S. Caviola. (2022). The Impasse on Gender Differences in Intelligence: a Meta-Analysis on WISC Batteries. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09705-1

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of 26 studies (39 samples) for the gV factor; all gV subtests showed effects close to d ≈ 0.20 favouring males, and the standardization-sample estimate was similar (d = 0.17).

"The estimated mean effect size was significant but small, with higher scores in males than in females, d = 0.21 [0.16, 0.25], p < 0.001."

D. Giofrè · K. Allen · E. Toffalini · S. Caviola 2022 (2)¶

D. Giofrè · K. Allen · E. Toffalini · S. Caviola. (2022). The Impasse on Gender Differences in Intelligence: a Meta-Analysis on WISC Batteries. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09705-1

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression moderator test on the gV factor; the article notes Block design in particular showed a male advantage that seems large and relatively unchanged between WISC versions.

"The old/new version of the battery did not appear to be a moderator of the effect size, χ2(1) = 0.55, p = 0.46, Δd = 0.04."
