## Evidence

### Monteiro S 2018

Monteiro S, Sibbald D, Coetzee K. (2018). i-Assess: Evaluating the impact of electronic data capture for OSCE. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0410-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA of the May IENCAP administration (n=154), with tablet tracks compared against scan-sheet tracks in a between-subjects design. The article reports a "main effect of modality (F (1,152) = 25.06, p < 0.001)" and an average tablet total score of 3.39 versus 3.00 on scan sheets, a 10% increase.

> "Study design 1 (the May exam) revealed a main effect of modality (F (1,152) = 25.06, p < 0.001)."
