# Program intensity is a significant moderator: high-duration gloss interventions yield larger effects (ES = 1.48) than low-duration ones (ES = 0.57)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The treatment had a moderate, positive effect at low treatment duration (ES = 0.57) and a large, positive effect at high duration (ES = 1.48), and the difference between the two subcategories was statistically significant (Q = 3.89, p < 0.05). ## Evidence

### Vahideh Sadat Vahedi 2014

Vahideh Sadat Vahedi, Behzad Ghonsooly, and Reza Pishghadam. (2014). Vocabulary Glossing: A Meta-Analysis of the Relative Effectiveness of Different Gloss Types on L2 Vocabulary Acquisition. Teaching English with Technology, 16(1), 3-25. http://www.tewtjournal.org

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis comparing high- and low-intensity treatment duration subgroups in the meta-analysis. Both subgroup effects were statistically significant, and the between-subgroup Q-test was significant. The article suggests more exposure to glosses can lead to better learning outcomes.

> "Based on Table 2, the test of heterogeneity did show a statistically significant difference between the effect sizes of two treatment duration subcategories (Q= 3.89, p<0.05), suggesting that more technology use can probably lead to better outcomes."
