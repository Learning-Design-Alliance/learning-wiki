## Evidence

### Kalyva 2026

Kalyva, M., Kavvadia, K., Louca, L., Televantou, I., & Michaelides, M. P. (2026). The “Testing Effect” in Undergraduate Endodontic Education: The Impact of Regular Low Stakes Testing on Learning Outcomes and Student Engagement. International Endodontic Journal, 59(6), 1227–1235. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.70022

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-participants experiment in which 81 third-year dental students were tested after half the course lectures. Descriptive results show higher performance on tested material ("Mean = 25.284, SD = 4.202") than non-tested material (mean = 21.111, SD = 3.847); no effect size is printed for the descriptive contrast.

> "On average, students scored higher on tested material (Mean = 25.284, SD = 4.202) compared to non‐tested material (mean = 21.111, SD = 3.847)."

### Kalyva 2026 (2)

Kalyva, M., Kavvadia, K., Louca, L., Televantou, I., & Michaelides, M. P. (2026). The “Testing Effect” in Undergraduate Endodontic Education: The Impact of Regular Low Stakes Testing on Learning Outcomes and Student Engagement. International Endodontic Journal, 59(6), 1227–1235. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.70022

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the two final exam subscores of the same 81 students confirmed the difference was "statistically significant with a very large effect size" (F 1,80 = 108.329, p < 0.001), effect size η² = 0.575, a large effect.

> "Repeated measures ANOVA between the scores on the tested and non‐tested material showed that the mean difference between the two scores was statistically significant with a very large effect size (F 1,80 = 108.329, p < 0.001, η p 2 = 0.575)"
