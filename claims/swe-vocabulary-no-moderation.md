## Evidence

### Nathan H. Clemens 2019

Nathan H. Clemens, Eric Oslund, Oi-man Kwok, Melissa Fogarty, Deborah Simmons, and John L. Davis. (2019). Skill Moderators of the Effects of a Reading Comprehension Intervention. Exceptional Children, 85(2), 197–211. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918787339

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Separate moderation models were run for each pretest moderator with the same base model and covariates. Table 2 reports the Condition × Pretest SWE interaction at p = .45 and Condition × Pretest Vocabulary at p = .65; no effect sizes were printed, so the null findings are untested for equivalence.

> "Pretest SWE and vocabulary were not statistically significant predictors of posttest comprehension and did not demonstrate statistically significant interaction effects with intervention condition"
