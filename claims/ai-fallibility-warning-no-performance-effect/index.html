Tomohiro Nagashima 2026¶

Tomohiro Nagashima, Mirella Hladký, and Vera Rief. (2026). Warning About AI Fallibility Increases Help-Seeking in an Intelligent Tutoring System. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03822

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same classroom regression analysis of problem-solving log data: "The groups did not differ significantly on the error rate and average time spent" (error rate β=−0.02, p=.61; time spent β=−0.23, p=.39). Table 1 reports Mistake means of 0.49 error rate and 1.60 time per step versus NoMistake 0.46 and 1.36.

"The groups did not differ significantly on the error rate and average time spent (error rate:β=−0.02,t(235) =−0.51,p=.61; time spent:β=−0.23,t(235) =−0.86,p=.39)."
