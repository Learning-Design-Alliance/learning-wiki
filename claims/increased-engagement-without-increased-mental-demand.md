Kumar, H., Reza, M., Mitchell, J., Musabirov, I., Zhang, L., & Liut, M. (2025). Understanding Help-Seeking Behavior of Students Using LLMs vs. Web Search for Writing SQL Queries. Preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3735091.3737569](https://doi.org/10.1145/3735091.3737569)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

This is the authors' interpretation (type e) from the Discussion section of the randomized interview study, synthesizing the interaction and mental demand findings. The article states "students who used Instructor-tuned LLM reported levels of mental demand that were equal to or lower than those using ChatGPT and Web Search."

> "Interestingly, the higher number of interactions with the Instructor-tuned LLM did not result in higher reported mental demand. In fact, students who used Instructor-tuned LLM reported levels of mental demand that were equal to or lower than those using ChatGPT and Web Search."
