## Evidence

### Aragón 2018

Aragón, O. R., Eddy, S. L., & Graham, M. J. (2018). Faculty Beliefs about Intelligence Are Related to the Adoption of Active-Learning Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 17(3). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-05-0084

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same SEM of 620 Summer Institutes participants, mindset showed "an additional 0.11 SD decrease in the implementation of active-learning practices" per 1 SD increase in fixed belief; the full predictor set explained 53% of implementation variance. No standardised effect size for the path itself is printed.

> "Similarly, a 1 SD increase in the belief that student intelligence is fixed was associated with an additional 0.11 SD decrease in the implementation of active-learning practices. As a whole, the predictors in this model explained 53% of the variation in implementation of active learning."
