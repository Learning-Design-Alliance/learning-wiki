## Evidence

### Stocker M 2017

Stocker M, Laine K, Ulmer F. (2017). Use of simulation-based medical training in Swiss pediatric hospitals: a national survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0940-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fisher exact comparison of 5 high-involvement versus 8 low-involvement units (Table 1). Significant differences: geography (p = 0.001), high-technology equipment (p = 0.034), structured evaluation (p = 0.02), instructor protected time (p < 0.001), instructor overtime exceeding 5 h (p = 0.001).

> "All units with high (defined as >66%) involvement of their staff were located in the French part of Switzerland, whereas 7 out of 8 units with low (defined as <33%) involvement were located in the German part of Switzerland."
