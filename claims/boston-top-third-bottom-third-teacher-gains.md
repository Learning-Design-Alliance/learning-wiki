A Bain and Company study for the Boston Public Schools charting the progress of tenth-grade classrooms with approximately equal average scores across teachers over the year. In reading, top-third teachers' gains (5.6) were slightly below the national median of 8.0 while bottom-third teachers showed 0.3; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The top third teachers produced gains on average that exceeded the national median (14.6 to 11.0 nationally), whereas the bottom third again showed virtually no growth (-0.6)."
