Singh Yogen, Ramanathan Rangasamy, Ebrahimi Mahmood, Noori Shahab. (2025). A narrative review of Neonatal Echocardiography Simulator Training (NEST): role of simulators in acquiring neonatal echocardiography skills effectively. Pediatric Research. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-025-04321-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that Neelankavil and colleagues randomized 61 anesthesiology residents to control (30) and simulation training (31) in basic transthoracic echocardiography skills. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "the simulation group scored higher on all assessment criteria after the first training session: written post-test (57.9% ± 8.8% vs 68.2% ± 10.1%; P < 0.001), volunteer subject post-test image quality scores (0 to 25 scale) (6.4 ± 3.5 vs 12.4 ± 4.2; P = 0.003)"
