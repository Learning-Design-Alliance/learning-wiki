## Evidence

### Michael J. Baker 2015

Michael J. Baker. (2015). The Negotiation of Meaning in Epistemic Situations. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0050-3

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The author reports, across multiple corpora of student argumentative interactions in collaborative problem-solving and pedagogical debate, results that were "clearly differentiated with respect to scientific domains" and socio-scientific domains. In scientific domains attitudes tended to weaken; in socio-scientific domains viewpoints could polarise. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The results that emerged (see e.g., Baker 2003, 2009) were clearly differentiated with respect to scientific domains, such as mathematics and physics, and socio-scientific domains such as history or geography (involving questions such as the acceptability of nuclear energy, or genetically-modified organisms)."
