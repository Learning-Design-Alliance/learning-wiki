## Evidence

### Francesco Pupillo 2023

Francesco Pupillo, Javier Ortiz-Tudela, Rasmus Bruckner and Yee Lee Shing. (2023). The effect of prediction error on episodic memory encoding is modulated by the outcome of the predictions. npj Science of Learning 8:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00166-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Supplemental analysis in which PE incorporated participants' prediction outcomes, described as similar to the signed PE of previous studies. No effect size is printed in the text, so no magnitude is asserted; the article says it is in line with the interaction result.

> "The analysis of the eﬀects of this kind of PE on recognition memory (included in Supplemental Material, see Fig. S9) showed a signiﬁcant positive relationship between PE and recognition memory, with stronger positive PE related to better memory and stronger negative PE related to worse memory"
