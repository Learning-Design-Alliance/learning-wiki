Eugenio Tufino, Stefano Oss, and Micol Alemani. (2024). Integrating Python data analysis in an existing introductory laboratory course. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad4fcc](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad4fcc)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course paper questionnaire (42 matched pre-post answers from 48 voluntary participants). The printed percentages are descriptive; the authors note the statistical analysis is only descriptive, not inferential.

> "Students’ evaluation on the course (see figure 4), revealed that the majority of students (65%) found the introduction to Python feasible, 17.5% believed it was ‘Easy’ or ‘Very easy’ and 7.5% of students that it was ‘Difficult’. Notably, no students chose 'Very difficult’."
