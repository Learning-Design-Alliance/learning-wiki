## Evidence

### Hannah Emily M. 2025

Hannah Emily M., Bloom Matthew B., Schwaitzberg Steven D. (2025). Project interpret cholangiogram at the SAGES 2024 Education & Innovation Center: skill deficiency in bile duct injury recognition among surgical residents. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12145-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation of self-reported IOC utilization during laparoscopic cholecystectomy over the past year against quiz scores in the 88-participant sample; the article reports "More frequent use of IOC was positively correlated (rs: + 0.394) with better quiz performance (p < 0.001)", an effect size of rs = +0.394.

> "More frequent use of IOC was positively correlated (rs: + 0.394) with better quiz performance (p < 0.001)."

### Hannah Emily M. 2025 (2)

Hannah Emily M., Bloom Matthew B., Schwaitzberg Steven D. (2025). Project interpret cholangiogram at the SAGES 2024 Education & Innovation Center: skill deficiency in bile duct injury recognition among surgical residents. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12145-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null secondary correlation in the same sample: operative volume alone did not predict quiz performance, quoted verbatim as "Increased frequency of performing LC had a weak, positive correlation (rs: + 0.070) with overall quiz performance."

> "Increased frequency of performing LC had a weak, positive correlation (rs: + 0.070) with overall quiz performance, this relationship did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.515)."
