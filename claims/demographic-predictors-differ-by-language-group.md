## Evidence

### McWayne 2016

McWayne, C. M., Melzi, G., Limlingan, M. C., & Schick, A. (2016). Ecocultural Patterns of Family Engagement among Low-income Latino Families of Preschool Children. Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0040343

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression covariate analyses within the LPA for the English-speaking subsample. Low Engagement parents were less likely to work full-time than the high profile group; no distinctions emerged versus the average group.

> "Employment was the only significant demographic predictor of profile membership for the English-speaking parents, where parents forming the Low Engagement profile were less likely to work full-time than those in the High Supplemental/High Foundational Education profile"
