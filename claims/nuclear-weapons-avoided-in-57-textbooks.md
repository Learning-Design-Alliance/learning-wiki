The author reports his prior textual analysis of 57 senior-level nuclear physics textbooks (The Shadow of the Bomb), finding narratives that omit nuclear weapons, evoked as 'Hamlet without the Prince'. He adds the study showed remarkably little has changed since 1945.

> "The phrase 'Hamlet without the Prince' accurately evokes the nature of the narratives presented in these textbooks, a feature that has remained notably constant over the sixty year span of publication dates of the texts surveyed, apart from a small temporary shift in the late 1980s."
