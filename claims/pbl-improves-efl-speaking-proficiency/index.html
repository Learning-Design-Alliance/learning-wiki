Guo Qian 2024¶

Guo Qian, Jamil Halimah, Ismail Lilliati, Luo Shujie, Sun Zhubin. (2024). Effects of problem-based learning on EFL learning: A systematic review. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0307819

Narrative synthesis of five studies (Studies 7, 16, 20, 22, 26) on speaking fluency, spanning mixed, quasi-experimental, and case-study designs. The review reports improvements in "fluency, grammar, comprehension, vocabulary, confidence level, intonation skills, and pronunciation".

"All three studies reported positive impacts of PBL on speaking abilities, with students demonstrating improvements in fluency, grammar, comprehension, vocabulary, confidence level, intonation skills, and pronunciation."
