## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox 2020

Bethany R. Wilcox, Steven J. Pollock, and Daniel R. Bolton. (2020). Retention of conceptual learning after an interactive introductory physics course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010140)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of FMCE performance between the matched (N = 1068) and unmatched datasets of PHYS II students. The unmatched group scored 13.5% lower on average, significant by Mann-Whitney U test (p < 0.001), suggesting a preparation mismatch.

> "Students in the un-matched dataset scored lower, on average, on the FMCE in PHYS II than students in the matched dataset by 13.5%, and this diﬀer-ence was statistically signiﬁcant (Mann-Whitney U test, p < 0.001)."

### Bethany R. Wilcox 2020 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox, Steven J. Pollock, and Daniel R. Bolton. (2020). Retention of conceptual learning after an interactive introductory physics course. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010140)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within the 387 unmatched students, roster checks showed 181 appeared in a prior PHYS I roster and 206 did not. FMCE scores were 63.8% for non-CU PHYS I students versus 67.2% for those who took PHYS I at CU, not statistically significant (p = 0.2).

> "we saw slightly lower performance in the group who did not take PHYS 1 at CU (an average score of 63.8% compared to 67.2%), but this diﬀerence was not statistically signiﬁcant (Mann-Whitney U test, p = 0.2)"
