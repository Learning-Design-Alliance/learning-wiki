## Evidence

### Danielle R. Thomas 2025

Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2025). Beyond Agreement: Rethinking Ground Truth in Educational AI. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.00143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.00143)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The paper reports, citing Henkel and Hills (2023), two experiments comparing categorical with comparative judgment on middle school open responses; "Krippendorff’s α improved from 0.66 to 0.80" and accuracy rose 13%, gains reported as statistically significant. No effect size is printed.

> "Results show that comparative judgment substantially improved both accuracy and inter-rater reliability. For short-answer tasks, Krippendorff’s α improved from 0.66 to 0.80, and accuracy increased by 13%. For oral fluency, α improved from 0.70 to 0.78. These gains were statistically significant."
