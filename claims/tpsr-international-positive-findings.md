The article's introduction summarizes international TPSR studies: a Turkish systematic review found improved efficacy, interpersonal interactions, and emotional regulation; Taiwanese research found improved self-efficacy in middle school students; a US study found improved socially responsible behaviors; and Spanish research found more positive perceptions of athleticism plus leadership and decision-making improvements.
