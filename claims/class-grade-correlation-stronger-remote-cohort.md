## Evidence

### Teemu Hynninen 2023

Teemu Hynninen, Henna Pesonen, Olli Lintu, and Petriina Paturi. (2023). Ongoing eﬀects of pandemic-imposed learning disruption on student attitudes. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010101)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation (R) between CLASS category scores and the final grade, given on an integer scale of 0–5, for the no-contact teaching cohort; the printed correlation coefficient was R = 0.65 for the post-survey overall CLASS score, the highest found.

> "The highest R is found for post-survey overall CLASS score, for which R = 0.65."

### Teemu Hynninen 2023 (2)

> "For classes in full or limited contact teaching, all R-values are about 0.35 or less."
