## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GPA quota students outperformed selection, WT, ex-ante and foreign students on both written and oral M1 grades. [→ Mommert A 2020](#mommert-a-2020)

## Evidence

### Mommert A 2020

Mommert A, Wagner J, Jünger J, Westermann J. (2020). Exam performance of different admission quotas in the first part of the state examination in medicine: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02069-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of 655 students' linked quota and IMPP exam records; one-way ANOVA with admission quota as independent variable. Table 2 reports written grade means of 2.2 (SD 0.9) for the GPA quota versus 2.9 (SD 0.8) for the selection quota, F(4, 636) = 22.24, p < .001.

> "Regarding the performance on the written part of the M1 exam, students in the GPA quota performed better than students from the selection quota (see Table 2); followed by students in the ex-ante and WT quota as well as foreign students."
