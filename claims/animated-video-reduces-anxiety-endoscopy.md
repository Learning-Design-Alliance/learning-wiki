`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale scores in the retrospective cohort (n = 77 conventional vs n = 89 video) showed "anxiety" was significantly lower in the video group after education and at follow-up; only t and P values were printed, no effect size.

> "anxiety (post-education: 19.88 ± 3.78 vs. 18.75 ± 3.42, P = 0.046; follow-up: 18.59 ± 3.4 vs. 17.54 ± 3.25, P = 0.046), and improvement in sleep quality"
