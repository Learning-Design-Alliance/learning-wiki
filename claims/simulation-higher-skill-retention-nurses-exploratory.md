Farah T, Besbes H, Ajmi H, Zayani S, Chemli J, Chouchane S. (2026). Comparing video-based and four-step simulation training in paediatric basic life support: a pilot randomised controlled study. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2025-003986

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory subgroup analysis of 3-month skill retention within the pilot RCT. The article reports "80.0% vs 72.0%, p=0.003" for nurses trained with simulation versus video-based instruction, and notes no differences in knowledge retention between groups.

> "No differences in knowledge retention were observed between groups, whereas exploratory analyses indicated higher skill retention among nurses trained with simulation (80.0% vs 72.0%, p=0.003) (figure 1)."
