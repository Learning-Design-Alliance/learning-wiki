## Evidence

### Moore Malcolm 2017

Moore Malcolm, Roberts Chris, Newbury Jonathan, Crossley Jim. (2017). Am I getting an accurate picture: a tool to assess clinical handover in remote settings? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1067-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Generalisability (G-study/D-study) analysis of the checklist sub-total across 132 assessments of 55 recorded remote telephone handovers. The D-study gave "a G-coefficient of 0.73 with four assessors, increasing to 0.80 with six assessors"; the global score alone was slightly lower (G = 0.70 with 6).

> "The D-study gave a G-coefficient of 0.73 with four assessors, increasing to 0.80 with six assessors. The reliability of the global score alone was slightly lower (G = 0.70 with 6 assessors)."
