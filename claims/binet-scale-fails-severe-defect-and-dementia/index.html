Evidence¶

De Sanctis 1911¶

De Sanctis, S. (1911). Mental development and the measurement of the level of intelligence. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 2, 498-507. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069835

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The author's own empirical clinical experience applying the metric scale across clinical groups. He explains the failure: applying the scale to idiots or dements would assume the laws of intellectual regression mirror those of progression, which he believes untrue.

"Empirically I have found that the scale, which works well with slightly atypical children, proves less satisfactory with imbeciles and idiots, and is almost worthless for dements."
