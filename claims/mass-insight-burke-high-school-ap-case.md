# A school-level partnership with Mass Insight Education & Research expanded AP access and passing at Jeremiah E. Burke High School, with an 8-point gain for Black and 13-point gain for Latino test takers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` After reintroducing supports (Saturday and virtual study sessions, a summer bridge program, AP faculty professional development, and specialist check-ins), Black students' AP test-taking rose 8 percentage points and Latino students' rose 13 percentage points from 2018-19 to 2021-22, and passing rose from 0% to 4.3% (Black) and 4.9% (Latino). Kristen Hengtgen and Hector Biaggi. (2024). Increasing Access to Advanced Coursework. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641974

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

A single-school promising-practice case: Jeremiah E. Burke High School, a Boston Public School, partnered with Mass Insight Education & Research starting in 2013 and recently reintroduced access-expansion strategies; passing rates rose from 0% in 2018-19 to 4.3% for Black and 4.9% for Latino students in 2021-22.

> "In three years, access to AP tests for Black and Latino students dramatically expanded at the school, which saw an 8-percentage point increase in Black students taking at least one AP test, and a 13-percentage point increase in Latino students taking AP tests from 2018-19 to 2021-22."
