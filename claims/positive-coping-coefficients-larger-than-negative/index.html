Ball 2025¶

Ball, I., Banerjee, M., Holliman, A., & Tyndall, I. (2025). Investigating Success in the Transition to University: A Systematic Review of Personal Risk and Protective Factors Influencing Psychosocial Success. https://osf.io/vxu5q?view_only=ee23e20f57c54891b842c92b7a4858de

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of the personal disposition domain (Table A1) across the retained longitudinal studies; the review compares coefficients for positive versus negative coping styles and finds the positive larger. No specific coefficients are printed for this comparison.

"the coefficients for the positive coping styles were larger their negative counterparts, suggesting that it would be more beneficial to improve positive coping strategies rather than focus on minimising negative ones"

q2 · i? · review · r3

Within the personal disposition synthesis, the review reports that engaging in maladaptive cognitive and coping strategies is a risk factor while adaptive self-regulation skills are protective; detached coping is singled out as adaptive. No effect size is printed.

"Interestingly, detached coping was identified as an adaptive strategy and a protective factor to success"
