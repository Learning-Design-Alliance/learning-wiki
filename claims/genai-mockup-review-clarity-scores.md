Three APW mockup projects (Map, Bud, Sin) were reviewed by PCI students via shared Google Drive documents; comments from twelve reviewers were interpreted by the AI assistant using "a Likert scale (1 to 5 values". Table 6 prints global Clarity of 3.83 (Map) and 2.75 (Bud).

> "Average scores highlighted the disparity of the interpretation results with low level scores in aspects related to the Clarity of ideas expressed in Map or Bud projects."
