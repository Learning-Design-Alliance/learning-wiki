## Evidence

### Fielding Shona 2018

Fielding Shona, Tiffin Paul Alexander, Greatrix Rachel, Lee Amanda J, Patterson Fiona, Nicholson Sandra, Cleland Jennifer. (2018). Do changing medical admissions practices in the UK impact on who is admitted? An interrupted time series analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023274

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Segmented regression with 2012 as interruption year for case study A, which raised academic requirements and doubled the UKCAT weighting from 7% to 14%; the male relative change was −0.33 (95% CI −0.73 to −0.07), while estimates for the other three groups were not statistically significant.

> "The relative change for proportion male was −0.33 (95% CI −0.73 to −0.07) indicating the proportion of males per year decreased by this amount compared with what it would have been if the selection policy had not changed"
