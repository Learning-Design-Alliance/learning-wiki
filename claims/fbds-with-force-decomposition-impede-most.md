# The negative effect of provided free-body diagrams was largest and only statistically significant for problems whose diagrams showed resolution of forces into components

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Only for Problem 1 and Problem 3, the two problems whose provided FBDs showed resolution of forces into multiple components, were between-group differences statistically significant. [→ Vanes Mešić 2017](#vanes-mesic-2017)

## Evidence

### Vanes Mešić 2017

Vanes Mešić, Sabaheta Mahmutović, Elvedin Hasović, Nataša Erceg. (2017). Free-Body Diagrams and Problem Solving in Mechanics: An Example of The Effectiveness of Self-Constructed Representations. European J of Physics Education, Volume 7 Issue 3. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1309-7202

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-problem analysis of the same randomized two-group experiment (n=18 per group, 0-20 scale per problem) found the largest between-group differences for Problem 3 and Problem 1, the only two with component-resolution FBDs; significance survived the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure at FDR 0.05.

> "Furthermore, it was found that only for these two problems the between -group differences were statistically significant (for Problem 1: t  (34) = 2.46, p=0.019; for Problem 3: t  (31.3) = 2.94, p=0.006)."
