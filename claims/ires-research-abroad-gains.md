`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Evaluation of the NANOGrav IRES program, a 10-week REU-style research-abroad internship (30 participants 2017–2019; 43% female, 26% URM), reporting "statistically signiﬁcant increases in skills and conﬁdence in international collaboration". No effect sizes are printed.

> "Students who have participated in the program demonstrate increased knowledge in multiple research areas and statistically signiﬁcant increases in skills and conﬁdence in international collaboration. They also demonstrated an increased desire to work with scientists from other countries."
