### Gerd Kortemeyer 2025

Gerd Kortemeyer, Marina Babayeva, Giulia Polverini, Ralf Widenhorn, Bor Gregorcic. (2025). Multilingual Performance of a Multimodal Artificial Intelligence System on Multisubject Physics Concept Inventories. [doi:10.1103/98hg-rkrf](https://doi.org/10.1103/98hg-rkrf)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Brief item-level investigation comparing GPT-4o's three independent responses on English items with their translated counterparts; the article reports the printed response-rate progression and states the trend holds for most languages in the dataset. No effect size is reported.

> "When all three English responses were incorrect, the correct response rate for the non-English versions was only 18%. This increased to 33% when one English response was correct, 48% for two, and 82% for three."
