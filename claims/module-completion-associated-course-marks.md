## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students completing at least 90% of modules had a significantly higher average course mark (72.4% vs 56.5%; p < 0.0001) and lower failure rate (4.5% vs 21.1%). [→ Herbert C 2017](#herbert-c-2017)

## Evidence

### Herbert C 2017

Herbert C, Velan GM, Pryor WM, Kumar RK. (2017). A model for the use of blended learning in large group teaching sessions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1057-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison of course outcomes among 264 Pathology students grouped by module completion. Students completing at least 90% of modules averaged "72.4% compared to 56.5%" (p < 0.0001); 21.1% of low-completion students failed versus 4.5% of high-completion students. No effect size is printed.

> "The average course mark achieved by students who completed at least 90% of the modules was significantly higher than by students who completed less than 90% of the modules (72.4% compared to 56.5%; p < 0.0001)."
