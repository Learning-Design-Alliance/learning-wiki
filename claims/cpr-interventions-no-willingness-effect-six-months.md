## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Herlitz Johan, Svensson Leif, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). Effect of two additional interventions, test and reflection, added to standard cardiopulmonary resuscitation training on seventh grade students' practical skills and willingness to act: a cluster randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014230

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Survey measure in the cluster randomised trial at 6 months found "no significant differences between the three intervention groups" in how students would act in OHCA situations; equivalence was not tested.

> "At 6-month follow-up, there were no significant differences between the three intervention groups with regard to how they would act in OHCA situations; 76% (O), 73% (T) and 78% (RT) would do both compressions and ventilations if a friend suffered a cardiac arrest."
