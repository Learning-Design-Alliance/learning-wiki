# Both experimental and control groups improved significantly on all questions and conceptual questions from pre- to post-test after using PyKinetic

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants from both groups improved their scores significantly between pre- and post-test on all questions and on conceptual questions, providing evidence that PyKinetic supported learning. [→ Fabic 2019](#fabic-2019)
`q3 i?` There was no significant improvement on procedural questions only. [→ Fabic 2019](#fabic-2019)

## Evidence

### Fabic 2019

Fabic, G. V. F., Mitrovic, A., & Neshatian, K. (2019). Evaluation of Parsons Problems with Menu-Based Self-Explanation Prompts in a Mobile Python Tutor. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 29, 507–535. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00184-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled study with 63 participants from two universities using PyKinetic for about an hour. Wilcoxon Signed Ranks tests showed significant pre/post improvement on all questions (experimental W = 379, p = .000; control W = 483, p = .002) and conceptual questions, but not procedural questions only.

> "Participants from both groups improved their scores significantly between the pre- and post-test (the Improvement on All Questions row), and on conceptual questions (the Improvement Conceptual row), but there was no significant improvement on procedural questions only."

### Fabic 2019 (2)

Fabic, G. V. F., Mitrovic, A., & Neshatian, K. (2019). Evaluation of Parsons Problems with Menu-Based Self-Explanation Prompts in a Mobile Python Tutor. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 29, 507–535. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00184-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports positive Cohen's d effect sizes for both groups on all questions, noting that "the effect size was higher for the experimental group" (d = .89 experimental vs. d = .55 control as printed in Table 6).

> "Both groups had a positive Cohen's d effect size, but the effect size was higher for the experimental group."
