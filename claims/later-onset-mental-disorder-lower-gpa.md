## Evidence

### Yan Li 2026

Yan Li, Niemi Ripsa, Gutvilig Mai, Elovainio Marko, Flykt Marjo, Hakulinen Christian. (2026). Mental disorders in childhood or adolescence and subsequent comprehensive school achievement. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 67(11), 1796-1806. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70181

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear regression with exposure categorized by age at first diagnosis in the nationwide cohort. The printed standardized differences ranged from −0.17 (ages 1–4) to −0.53 (ages 13–17); "the later the onset of a mental disorder, the lower the overall school achievement".

> "the later the onset of a mental disorder, the lower the overall school achievement, with the greatest association observed in those diagnosed at ages 13–17, −0.53 [95% CI, −0.54 to −0.52], followed by ages 9–12, −0.43 [95% CI, −0.44 to −0.42], ages 5–8, −0.26 [95% CI, −0.27 to −0.25], and ages 1–4, −0.17 [95% CI, −0.18 to −0.15]."
