### Mellor 2021

Mellor, D. (2021). Improving norms in research culture to incentivize transparency and rigor. Educational Psychologist, 56(2). https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3125-5888

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article reports, citing Silberzahn et al. (2018), that 29 teams of data analysts given the same dataset on soccer referees and red cards produced "29 different answers", with statistical significance and uncertainty varying widely. No effect size is printed.

> "With the same research question and the same data, one might expect that there would be one answer, perhaps with a reasonable band of uncertainty. There were, however, 29 different answers."
