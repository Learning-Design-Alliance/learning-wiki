## Evidence

### Eka Woldeab Daniel 2025

Eka Woldeab Daniel, Tola Tiruwork Tamiru, Negasi Reda Darge. (2025). Effects of students’ mathematics learning strategy and perceived task difficulty on their achievement of mathematics. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0337115

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive comparison of teacher-made test scores (Fig 2, N = 351) reported grade-10 means of 59.73 for males versus 57.89 for females and, for grade 11, 58.01 versus 56.65, so "males achieve better than females in average in both classes". No inferential test is reported.

> "for grade 10, the mean, minimum and maximum scores were 59.73, 27 and 82 respectively for males, whereas, 57.89, 38 and 81 respectively for females"
