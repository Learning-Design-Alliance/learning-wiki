Evidence¶

Kashika Khurana and Ally Liew 2026¶

Kashika Khurana and Ally Liew. (2026). An exploratory behavioral and electroencephalographic study of artificial intelligence-assisted learning modes in high school students. https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/allyliew/participant-data

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Wilcoxon signed-rank post-hoc comparisons of Stress scores (fidgeting, sighing, micro-movements) in the within-subject behavioral study of 48 participants. The authors report Tutor mode "demonstrated significantly higher stress-related behaviors" than both other modes, with r = 0.564 and r = 0.449.

"Tutor mode demonstrated significantly higher stress-related behaviors than Collaborator mode ( = 𝑍 -3.909, p < 0.001, r = 0.564) and Solver mode ( = -3.113, 𝑍 p = 0.0019, r = 0.449)."

Kashika Khurana and Ally Liew 2026 (2)¶

Kashika Khurana and Ally Liew. (2026). An exploratory behavioral and electroencephalographic study of artificial intelligence-assisted learning modes in high school students. https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/allyliew/participant-data

q2 · i0 · causal · r2

Post-hoc comparison of Stress between Collaborator and Solver modes in the same pairwise analysis. The printed effect size r = 0.031 is negligible; the authors report no statistically significant difference, and equivalence was not tested.

"No statistically significant difference was identified between Collaborator and Solver modes ( = -0.215, p = 0.829, r = 0.031)."
