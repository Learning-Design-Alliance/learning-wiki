Qualitative arm of the mixed-methods sequential explanatory design: semi-structured interviews with 12 intervention-group interns, coded independently in NVivo 12.0 with member checking. The analysis identified "a total of 9 core themes" across the four named clusters; no effect sizes apply.

> "Data saturation was reached after semi-structured interviews with 12 participants (mean duration 4 ± 2 min). The findings are presented as four theme clusters were identified, including social interactive construction of knowledge (2 sub-themes), contextualized construction of knowledge (2 sub-themes), active construction of knowledge (2 sub-themes), and cultivation of clinical core competencies and professional qualities (3 sub-themes), with a total of 9 core themes"
