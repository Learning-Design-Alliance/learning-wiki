## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz 2015

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz. (2015). Attending to lifelong learning skills through guided reflection in a physics class. [doi:10.1119/1.4930083](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4930083)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Double coding of 71 reflections by both authors during reliability calibration; neither rater identified any achievement statements during calibration. The article reports "both raters agreed that there were no achievement statements in 62 (87%) of the 71 reﬂections", and these statements were dropped from further analyses.

> "Moreover, both raters agreed that there were no achievement statements in 62 (87%) of the 71 reﬂections that were coded separately."
