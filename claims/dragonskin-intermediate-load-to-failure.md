### Western L 2025

Western L, Roberts PG, Rees J, Howgate D. (2025). Validation of a novel simulated tendon model for core suture tendon repair. Annals of The Royal College of Surgeons of England. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2024.0064

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mechanical load to failure testing of ten samples per material using a universal testing device; Kruskal–Wallis test. The article reports "Significant differences in load to failure" with means of 11.1N, 31.7N and 56.2N and p < 0.001; post-hoc Dunn's tests p < 0.02 for all.

> "Significant differences in load to failure were observed among bathroom sealant, DragonSkin® and porcine tendon (Mean value of 11.1N, 31.7N and 56.2N respectively; p < 0.001) (Figure 3)."
