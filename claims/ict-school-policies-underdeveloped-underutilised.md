## Evidence

### Tondeur 2008

Tondeur, J., van Keer, H., van Braak, J., & Valcke, M. (2008). ICT integration in the classroom: Challenging the potential of a school policy. Computers & Education, 51, 212-223. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2007.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of structured interviews with 53 principals. A second interview finding: "17 of the 53 principals claimed that the ICT policy has been evaluated occasionally and only nine principals reported a systematic evaluation." Plans focused mainly on technical skills rather than integrated instructional use.

> "only 12 of the 53 principals reported the availability of a comprehensive ICT plan including clear goals and determining the means to realise these goals."
