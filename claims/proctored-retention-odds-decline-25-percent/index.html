Rismanchian 2026¶

Rismanchian, S., Uzun, H., Matayoshi, J., Cosyn, E., & Kurd-Misto, E. (2026). Faster Completion, Less Learning: Generative AI Reduced Study Time on Math Problems and the Knowledge They Build. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21629

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Logistic fixed-effects model on 6,698 randomly assigned extra retention item observations across 66 topics and 22 quarters, proctored conditions. Estimate "β=− 0.0260, p= 0.037"; randomization inference within difficulty quartiles gave p= 0.027.

"a 2.61% per-quarter decline in the log-odds of correctly answering AI-susceptible items relative to AI-resistant items in proctored conditions ( β=− 0.0260, p= 0.037), accumulating to a 25.0% cumulative reduction in the odds of correct response"
