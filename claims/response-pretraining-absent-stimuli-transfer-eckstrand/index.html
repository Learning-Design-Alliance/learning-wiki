Evidence¶

Eckstrand 1950¶

Eckstrand, G. A. (1950). Response Practice as a Factor in Transfer of Training (AF Technical Report No. 6017). Wright Air Development Center. doi:10.1037/e478582004-001 (read through Faison's 1954 research abstract, doi:10.1007/bf02713278)

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Controlled laboratory experiment: four groups of approximately 30 men each responded to colors on a vertical display panel; pretraining groups practiced key presses to letters instead of colors. The article reports the pretraining groups were "superior in performance of the actual training task"; no effect size is printed.

"All experimental groups receiving response pretraining in absence of the task stimulus were superior in performance of the actual training task than the group receiving no pretraining."
