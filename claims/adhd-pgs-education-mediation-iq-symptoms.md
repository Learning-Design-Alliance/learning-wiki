## Evidence

### Stergiakouli Evie 2017

Stergiakouli Evie, Martin Joanna, Hamshere Marian L., Heron Jon, St Pourcain Beate, Timpson Nicholas J., Thapar Anita, Davey Smith George. (2017). Association between polygenic risk scores for attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder and educational and cognitive outcomes in the general population. International Journal of Epidemiology. [doi:10.1093/ije/dyw216](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyw216)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modelling (Sobel-Goodman mediation test) in ALSPAC children with GCSE results as outcome and IQ as mediator, sex as covariate; bootstrapping (1000 replications) showed a consistent pattern. The quote reports "49% of the effect ... mediated through IQ".

> "In the first model, including GCSEs results as the outcome and IQ as the mediator, 49% of the effect of ADHD polygenic scores on educational outcomes in adolescents was mediated through IQ (Figure 1)."

### Stergiakouli Evie 2017 (2)

Stergiakouli Evie, Martin Joanna, Hamshere Marian L., Heron Jon, St Pourcain Beate, Timpson Nicholas J., Thapar Anita, Davey Smith George. (2017). Association between polygenic risk scores for attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder and educational and cognitive outcomes in the general population. International Journal of Epidemiology. [doi:10.1093/ije/dyw216](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyw216)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second SEM in ALSPAC children using parent-completed DAWBA ADHD symptoms at age 7 years 7 months as mediator of the polygenic-score effect on GCSE outcomes. The quote reports "16% of the total effect ... mediated through ADHD symptoms".

> "There was evidence of mediation with 16% of the total effect of ADHD polygenic scores on GCSE educational outcomes mediated through ADHD symptoms (Figure 2)."
