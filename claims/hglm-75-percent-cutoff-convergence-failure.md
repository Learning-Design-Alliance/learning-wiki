## Evidence

### Jayson M. Nissen 2021

Jayson M. Nissen, Ian Her Many Horses, and Ben Van Dusen. (2021). Investigating society's educational debts due to racism and sexism in student attitudes about physics using quantitative critical race theory. Physical Review Physics Education Research 17, 010116. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.17.010116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical generalized linear models predicted the probability of CLASS scores at or above a 75% expertlike cutoff, fit with extended quasilikelihood. The article reports algebra-based post-test "models failed to converge" because so few marginalized-group students reached the cutoff.

> "the post-test scores in algebra-based physics courses had so few students from marginalized groups score greater than or equal to 75% that the models failed to converge."
