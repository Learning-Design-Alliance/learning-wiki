## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Malone et al. 2019

Amelia S. Malone, Lynn S. Fuchs, Sonya K. Sterba, Douglas Fuchs, and Lindsay Foreman-Murray. (2019). Does an Integrated Focus on Fractions and Decimals Improve At-Risk Students' Rational Number Magnitude Performance? Contemporary Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED595127

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cross-classified partially nested multilevel model analysis of the 0-1 Decimal Number Line outcome. The article reports both intervention conditions outperformed control, with the full fraction intervention showing far transfer to decimal magnitude understanding at ES = 0.50.

> "That is, although integrated fraction-decimal intervention students outperformed the control group (ES = 0.50), so did full fraction intervention students, for whom the decimal number line task required far transfer."
