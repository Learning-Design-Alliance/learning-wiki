### Jorge Fábrega 2026

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of standardized assignment scores for 134 of 150 conversations with exact score linkage, across minimum-turn thresholds (Table 4). The article reports the contrasts "do not reveal a stable performance difference"; no significance test or effect size is printed, so this is a descriptive null, not an equivalence finding.

> "Across the alternative opportunity thresholds, the score comparisons do not reveal a stable performance difference between conversations in which all three dimensions are observed and those in which at least one is not."
