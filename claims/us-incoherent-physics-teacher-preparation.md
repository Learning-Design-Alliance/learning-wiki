Ben Van Dusen, Christoph Vogelsang, Joseph Taylor, and Eva Cauet. (2021). How to Teach a Teacher: Challenges and Opportunities in Physics Teacher Education in Germany and the USA. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-87391-2_3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-87391-2_3)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Descriptive synthesis of US physics teacher supply, citing Meltzer et al. (2012). The chapter reports that of roughly 3,100 new high-school physics teachers yearly, about 1,700 are in-service teachers transitioning into physics, reflecting a severe national shortage. The quote states "over 90% of physics teachers graduate from programs that do not offer any specialized instruction".

> "There is no coherent system for preparing physics teachers (Meltzer et al., 2012). The majority of physics teachers have neither a major or minor in physics. Furthermore, over 90% of physics teachers graduate from programs that do not offer any specialized instruction to prepare them for teaching physics."
