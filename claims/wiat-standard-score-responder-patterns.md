### Ritchey et al. 2017

Kristen D. Ritchey, Kimberly Palombo, Rebecca D. Silverman, and Deborah L. Speece. (2017). Effects of an Informational Text Reading Comprehension Intervention for Fifth-Grade Students. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948716682689

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive responder analysis using norm-referenced standard scores; no inferential test is reported. Seven students with pre-test scores below 90 also remained below 90 at post-test, with changes from a 7-point decrease to a 5-point increase.

> "Of the students with a standard score below 90 at pre-test on WIAT Reading Comprehension, five students had post-test standard scores above 90. Standard score increases ranged from 3 to 19 points, suggesting strong response to instruction for several students."
