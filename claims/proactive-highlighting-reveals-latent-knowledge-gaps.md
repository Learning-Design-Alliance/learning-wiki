### Ruqi Sun 2026

Ruqi Sun, Jiaping Li, Wenhui Tao, Ximing Zheng, Yuefeng Tan, Jiahao Wei, and Yuxin Ma. (2026). VeriForge: Mitigating Latent Knowledge Gaps in Narrative Drafting via Mixed-Initiative Scaffolding. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09698 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.09698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.09698)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects user study (N=12; 8 hobbyists, 4 online novelists) comparing VeriForge against a strengthened baseline sharing the same retrieval backend. The blind-spot alerting difference survived Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction, with a large printed effect size r=.885, described as "the most pronounced finding" of RQ1.

> "Participants rated VeriForge's blind-spot alerting significantly higher than the baseline (𝑀= 6.33vs. 𝑀= 3.00;𝑊= 0.0,𝑝=. 001,𝑞=. 016,𝑟=. 885), with 11 of 12 scoring VeriForge higher."

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
