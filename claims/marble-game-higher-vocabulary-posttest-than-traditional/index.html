Evidence¶

Coşkun 2013¶

Coşkun, H. (2013). The importance of educational marble games in teaching German. Egitim Arastirmalari – Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 53, 151-174. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ejer

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 64 sophomore students (34 experimental, 30 control) electing German at Hacettepe University; pre-tests used as covariates. The article reports "a significant difference between the post-test means" with F(1,61) = 25.503, p < .001; adjusted means were 22.27 versus 18.65. No effect size is printed.

"According to the ANCOVA results, when the pre -test scores are used as a covariate variable, there is a significant difference between the post-test means of the students in different groups [(F 1, 61) = 25,503, p< .001 ]."
