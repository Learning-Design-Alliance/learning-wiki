Subgroup random-effects meta-analysis of four studies on alcohol consumption and academic achievement; the pooled SMD of -0.24 was "not significant" (p = 0.073), so no effect size magnitude is established as significant.

> "we had 2,479 subjects in the control group and 7,134 alcohol consumers. The finding reveals that alcohol consumption is negatively associated with positive academic achievement, but the association is weak as the pooled SMD (− 0.24) was not significant (95% CI − 0.51 to 0.04; p = 0.073)."
