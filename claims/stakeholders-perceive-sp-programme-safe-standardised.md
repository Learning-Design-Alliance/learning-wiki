`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews and focus groups with 25 stakeholders (leaders, trainers, students, SPs) identified the SP programme as a comparable educational strategy. Participants valued "a safe yet realistic environment" and standardised exposure to rare or sensitive cases unavailable with real patients.

> "Participants consistently described SP training as a safe yet realistic environment that supports repeated practice without the risk of harming actual patients."
