## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In multivariable logistic regression, stunted children had significantly lower odds of high academic performance than normal children, i.e. 79% less likely to score high. [→ Asmare Biachew 2018](#asmare-biachew-2018)

## Evidence

### Asmare Biachew 2018

Asmare Biachew, Taddele Mekuanint, Berihun Sileshi, Wagnew Fasil. (2018). Nutritional status and correlation with academic performance among primary school children, northwest Ethiopia. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3909-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on the 436-child cross-sectional sample, with academic performance dichotomised at the 50% average-mark pass point set by the Ethiopian ministry of education. Stunting (HAZ) was significant at p = 0.001 with AOR = 0.21 (95% CI 0.10–0.43); the article states stunted students were "79% less likely to score high academic performance as compared with normal". No standardised effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "Students those nutritional status had stunted were 79% less likely to score high academic performance as compared with normal."
