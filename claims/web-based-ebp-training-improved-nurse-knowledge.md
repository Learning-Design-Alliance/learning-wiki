## Evidence

### Mohamed Rasha A. 2024

Mohamed Rasha A., Alhujaily Muhanad, Ahmed Faransa A., Nouh Wael G., Almowafy Abeer A. (2024). Exploring the potential impact of applying web-based training program on nurses’ knowledge, skills, and attitudes regarding evidence-based practice: A quasi-experimental study. PLOS ONE 19(2): e0297071. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0297071

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental one-group pretest-posttest study of 64 professional nurses in Bisha, Saudi Arabia. Table 2 reports total knowledge rising from 11.47±8.17 to 53.08±15.9, paired t = 28.91, p = 0.000, Cohen's d = 3.29, described as "highly significant difference in the total mean knowledge scores".

> "there was a highly significant difference in the total mean knowledge scores between the post-intervention and pre-intervention (P≤005)"
