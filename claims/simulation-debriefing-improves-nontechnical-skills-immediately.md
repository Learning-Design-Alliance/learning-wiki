## Evidence

### Friedlaender Rafaella Fadel 2025

Friedlaender Rafaella Fadel, Gubert Eduardo Maranhão, Fernandes Cláudia Maria Baroni, Mello Rosiane Guetter, Coelho Izabel Cristina Meister Martins. (2025). Evaluation of acquisition and retention of non-technical skills of residents submitted to interprofessional simulation-based training in pediatric cardiopulmonary resuscitation. Jornal de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jped.2024.12.003

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-group cohort study of 85 pediatric medicine and nursing residents in 16 interprofessional teams; comparison of TEAM tool means between the first (M0) and second (M1) same-day simulated cardiopulmonary arrest scenarios with debriefing showed "a significant difference between all dimensions evaluated" (leadership, teamwork, task management, global), each with p < 0.001 in Table 1.

> "It is observed that in M0 and M1, there is a significant difference between all dimensions evaluated (Table 1)."
