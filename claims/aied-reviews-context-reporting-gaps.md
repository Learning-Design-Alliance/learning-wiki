## Evidence

### Yao Fu 2024

Yao Fu, Zhenjie Weng, Jiaxi Wang. (2024). Examining AI Use in Educational Contexts: A Scoping Meta-Review and Bibliometric Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00442-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Analysis of learning environment and modality reporting across the 126 reviews. The article reports 110 reviews omitted learning environment information and 83 omitted modality; online learning was the most widely reported modality (about 28%) and none focused solely on face-to-face learning.

> "Out of the 126 reviews, 110 studies did not provide information about the learning environment. In detail, only six included reviews addressed on formal classroom learning, one on experimental informal learning, nine on both formal and informal learning environments"

### Yao Fu 2024 (2)

Yao Fu, Zhenjie Weng, Jiaxi Wang. (2024). Examining AI Use in Educational Contexts: A Scoping Meta-Review and Bibliometric Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00442-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Analysis of educational levels (Fig. 7) and subject areas (Fig. 8) across the included reviews. Among reviews specifying levels, 31% covered multiple levels, 16% higher education, and 11% PreK-12; almost half did not specify learning content areas.

> "42% of the reviews centered on general AIED without providing detailed information about the type of education examined in individual studies"
