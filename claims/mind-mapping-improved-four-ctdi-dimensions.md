## Evidence

### Wu Hang-Zhou; Wu Qiu-Ting 2020

Wu Hang-Zhou; Wu Qiu-Ting. (2020). Impact of mind mapping on the critical thinking ability of clinical nursing students and teaching application. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060519893225

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests on the seven CTDI-CV dimensions in the same 64-student pre/post study; the quoted sentence reports the four significant dimensions. Table 1 prints e.g. open-mindedness 35.25 ± 5.83 to 39.02 ± 5.62 (P = 0.00). No effect sizes printed.
