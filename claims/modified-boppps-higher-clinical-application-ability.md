## Evidence

### Wei Mingchao 2026

Wei Mingchao, Tang Jian, Wang Jun, Song Zhengkai, Ji Piyou, Wang Yumin. (2026). Effectiveness of a modified BOPPPS teaching model in theoretical education for thoracic surgery: an empirical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08909-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Prespecified primary endpoint in the quasi-experiment (48 vs. 48 students); post-course scores were 15.4 ± 1.8 vs. 13.2 ± 2.0, with the group "significantly higher primary endpoint (comprehensive clinical application ability) than the control group (P = 0.032, Cohen's d = 0.89)".

> "Unadjusted analysis confirmed the experimental group had a significantly higher primary endpoint (comprehensive clinical application ability) than the control group (P = 0.032, Cohen's d = 0.89), with a large effect size."

### Wei Mingchao 2026 (2)

Wei Mingchao, Tang Jian, Wang Jun, Song Zhengkai, Ji Piyou, Wang Yumin. (2026). Effectiveness of a modified BOPPPS teaching model in theoretical education for thoracic surgery: an empirical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08909-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression on the primary endpoint controlling for three covariates; the adjusted model "still showed the modified model was independently associated with improved primary endpoint scores (β = 2.35, 95% CI: [0.38, 4.32], P = 0.020)"; covariates were non-significant (all P > 0.05).

> "After adjusting for potential confounding factors (surgical internship experience, English proficiency, pre-course self-efficacy), multiple linear regression still showed the modified model was independently associated with improved primary endpoint scores (β = 2.35, 95% CI: [0.38, 4.32], P = 0.020; Table 4)."
