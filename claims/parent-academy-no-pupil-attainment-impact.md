## Evidence

### Husain et al. 2016

Fatima Husain, Nico Jabin, Sarah Haywood, Adetayo Kasim, Jonathan Paylor. (2016). Parent Academy: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN42518213

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Three-arm randomised controlled efficacy trial in 16 English primary schools, individual-level randomisation of pupils, analysed on an intention-to-treat basis with multilevel models. The report states the "estimated impact on each outcome was close to zero" for reading and general mathematics (InCAS age-standardised scores).

> "The evaluation did not find evidence that offering parents either incentivised or non-incentivised Parent Academy classes improved maths or reading outcomes for pupils in the trial."

### Husain et al. 2016 (2)

Fatima Husain, Nico Jabin, Sarah Haywood, Adetayo Kasim, Jonathan Paylor. (2016). Parent Academy: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN42518213

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

The same trial's reported negative point estimates for children of parents attending non-incentivised sessions were not interpreted as a negative effect; the evaluators state the results "may have been due to chan ce".

> "attended the non-incentivised sessions, the estimat ed impacts on maths and English were actually negative, but these results may have been due to chan ce, so we would not conclude that Parent Academy has a negative impact."
