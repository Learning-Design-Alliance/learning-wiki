`q3 i?` Clinical scenario-based questions showed higher discrimination indices (0.28) than knowledge-recall questions (0.14), and integration-type questions showed the highest discrimination (0.34). [→ Başaranoğlu Mert 2025](#basaranoglu-mert-2025)

## Evidence

### Başaranoğlu Mert 2025

Başaranoğlu Mert, Akbay Erdem, Erdem Erim. (2025). AI-generated questions for urological competency assessment: a prospective educational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07202-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric analysis of the 100 validated AI-generated questions classified by type. The article reports "t = 6.82, p < 0.001" for the scenario-versus-recall discrimination contrast; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "clinical scenario-based questions (n = 55) demonstrated higher discrimination indices (mean: 0.28, 95% CI: 0.24–0.32) compared to knowledge-recall questions (n = 45, mean: 0.14, 95% CI: 0.10–0.18; t = 6.82, p < 0.001). Integration-type questions (n = 25) exhibited the highest discrimination indices (mean: 0.34, 95% CI: 0.29–0.39)"
