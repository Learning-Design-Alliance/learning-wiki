Moeller 2017¶

Moeller, J., Brackett, M. A., Ivcevic, Z., & White, A. E. (2017). High school students' feelings: Discoveries from a large national survey and an experience sampling study. Preprint / self-archived. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=High+school+students%E2%80%99+feelings+Moeller+Brackett+Ivcevic+White

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Validity check in the national survey comparing t-tests of scale ratings between students who mentioned a feeling (happy, stressed, sad, lonely, anxious, bored, nervous, depressed) and those who did not. Effects were "moderate to large (0.44 ≤ |d| ≤ 1.50)", i.e., d = 0.44 at the low end.

"All differences in ratings between students who mentioned a given feeling and students who did not mention that feeling were significant and all effect sizes were moderate to large (0.44 ≤ |d| ≤ 1.50)."
