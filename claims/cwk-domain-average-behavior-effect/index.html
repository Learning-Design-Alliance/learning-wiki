Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Connect with Kids. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/104

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

WWC calculations across the four student survey outcomes in the quasi-experimental study. Appendix A3 reports a domain average effect size of 0.40, statistically significant, and an average improvement index for behavior of +16 percentile points.

"The average effect across all four outcome measures was positive and statistically significant."
