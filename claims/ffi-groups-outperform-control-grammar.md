## Evidence

### Rooholamin et al. 2016

Amir Rooholamin, Reza Biria & Hamid Reza Haghverdi. (2016). Effectiveness of Proactive Intensive FFI in Cultivating Grammatical Knowledge of Iranian EFL Learners at Different Proficiency Levels. English Language Teaching, 9(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n3p197

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on posttest grammar scores of 110 learners in FFI (EG, n = 57) versus no-FFI (CG, n = 53) groups. The Groups effect was significant, p = .000, partial η² = .201; descriptive means were 16.14 (EG) versus 14.50 (CG).

> "the p value corresponding to Groups was smaller than the significance level (i.e. .000 < .05), which gives rise to the conclusion that EG learners significantly outperformed CG  learners."
