## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Total scores of the two groups did not differ significantly (p=0.265), indicating no carry-over effect; when feedback scores were excluded, group D-scores also showed no significant difference (p=0.700). [→ Atakan Coban 2025](#atakan-coban-2025)

## Evidence

### Atakan Coban 2025

Atakan Coban, David Dzsotjan, Stefan Küchemann, Jürgen Durst, Jochen Kuhn & Christoph Hoyer. (2025). AI Support Meets AR Visualization for Alice and Bob: Personalized Learning Based on Individual ChatGPT Feedback in an AR Quantum Cryptography Experiment for Physics Lab Courses. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00310-z](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00310-z)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Control analysis in the crossover study: independent sample t-tests on total scores and on D-scores computed from initial answers only; the latter "resulted in a p-value of 0.700", supporting that observed differences were due to feedback.

> "The p-value was determined as 0.265 in this test. Therefore, the difference between the total scores of the two groups was not statistically significant (p>.05). This indicates that there was no carry-over effect."
