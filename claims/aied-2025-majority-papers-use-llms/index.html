q2 · i? · review · r2

A full-text literature review of all 396 AIED 2025 conference proceedings papers, coded by two coders with consensus resolution, found "257 have used an LLM and 139 have not". The review also reports 176 papers used LLMs as tutors or interactive-system components, 100 as subjects of experiments, 108 for content design, and 79 for data analysis.
