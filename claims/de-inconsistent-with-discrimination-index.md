# Distractor efficiency does not correlate in a consistent pattern with the discrimination index of Best Answer Questions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across 7 examinations, the group of items with the highest or lowest distractor efficiency did not consistently match the item-quality classes based on discrimination index, leading the authors to reject their null hypothesis. [→ Puthiaparampil Thomas 2021](#puthiaparampil-thomas-2021)

## Evidence

### Puthiaparampil Thomas 2021

Puthiaparampil Thomas, Rahman Mizanur. (2021). How important is distractor efficiency for grading Best Answer Questions? BMC Medical Education, 21:29. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02463-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective item analysis of 350 BAQ items from 7 final professional medical examinations, with items sorted into five classes by discrimination index and mean distractor efficiency compared test-wise. The authors found "no consistent pattern, as to which group had the highest or the lowest DE".

> "The outstanding finding of our study was that there was no consistent pattern, as to which group had the highest or the lowest DE."
