# Students generally attempted fewer items under ego-involving than task-involving feedback, but this gap disappeared among praised students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Ego-involving feedback lowered trials relative to task-involving feedback (b = −1.5, t = −5.27, p <.001). [→ Cheng-Hong Liu 2026](#cheng-hong-liu-2026)
`q3 i?` Among students receiving person praise, trials did not differ between feedback conditions (b = −0.2, t = −0.5, p =.618). [→ Cheng-Hong Liu 2026 (2)](#cheng-hong-liu-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Cheng-Hong Liu 2026

Cheng-Hong Liu, Xian-Rui Yin. (2026). Effects of person praise on students' motivation and performance under ego-involving and task-involving feedback conditions. Instructional Science (2026) 54:34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09789-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main effect from the OLS regression in the 146-participant experiment: ego-involving feedback predicted fewer attempted items ("b = −1.5, t = −5.27, p <.001"), model R² = 0.27 for the motivation outcome.

> "participants under the ego-involving feedback condition attempted to resolve fewer items relative to those under the task-involving feedback condition, b = −1.5, t = −5.27, p <.001."

### Cheng-Hong Liu 2026 (2)

Cheng-Hong Liu, Xian-Rui Yin. (2026). Effects of person praise on students' motivation and performance under ego-involving and task-involving feedback conditions. Instructional Science (2026) 54:34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09789-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple slopes in the same regression showed the feedback-manner gap was largest without praise (b = −2.78, t = −6.96, p <.001) but vanished among praised students (b = −0.2, t = −0.5, p =.618): person praise raised engagement in the low-motivation ego-involving condition.

> "for participants who did not receive person praise, those under the ego-involving feedback condition answered fewer items relative to those under the task-involving feedback condition, b = −2.78, t = −6.96, p <.001."
