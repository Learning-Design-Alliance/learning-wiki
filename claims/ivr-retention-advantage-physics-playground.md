## Evidence

### Paolo Boffi 2026

Paolo Boffi, Alberto Gallace, Pier Luca Lanzi. (2026). An Investigation of the Relation Between Immersion and Learning Across Three Domains. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01644)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the four-week high-school Physics Playground deployment (74 students, class-level randomization), retention analysis showed a significant decay for DVR and no change for IVR; "IVR and control participants scored significantly higher than DVR ones" at two weeks, with no IVR-control difference (p=.521).

> "post-hoc analyses revealed that IVR and control participants scored significantly higher than DVR ones (IVR vs. DVR:p=.012,∆mean = 1.24; control vs. DVR:p < .001,∆ mean = 1.71)"
