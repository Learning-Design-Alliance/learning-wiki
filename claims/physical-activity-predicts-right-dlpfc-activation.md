`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple mediation model (Fig. 7) from the adolescent fNIRS study, controlling for MSIT % correct responses; fNIRS channels aggregated into four ROIs. The same significant R-dlPFC effect appeared in the English-grades model.
