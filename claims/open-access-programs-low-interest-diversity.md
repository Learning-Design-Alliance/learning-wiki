## Evidence

### Schelfhout Stijn 2019

Schelfhout Stijn, Wille Bart, Fonteyne Lot, Roels Elisabeth, De Fruyt Filip, Duyck Wouter. (2019). The effects of vocational interest on study results: Student person – environment fit and program interest diversity. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0214618

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Program-level comparison of interest diversity (AD COR) across 39 open-access programs against a pooled Medicine/Dentistry entry-exam control group, reported in the Results section on research question 1. The t-test showed "a significant difference, t(38) = -4.27, p < .001" with "Cohen's d = 0.69"; average diversity was M = .15 (SD = .05) with an average PE fit correlation of .70.

> "A two-sided one sample t-test indeed revealed a significant difference, t(38) = -4.27, p < .001, with a somewhat large effect size of Cohen's d = 0.69."
