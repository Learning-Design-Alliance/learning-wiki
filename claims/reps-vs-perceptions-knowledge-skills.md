## Evidence

### Hu Jia 2025

Hu Jia, Wang Xiuqing, Chen Ruonan, Lu Guoying, Zhang Ming, Huang Xiaojing. (2025). Use of virtual simulation for regenerative endodontic training: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06808-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire study of the randomized groups using a 12-item five-point Likert scale collected via SurveyStar; Group A Cronbach's alpha exceeded 0.7. The learning-needs item averaged "4.38 ± 0.80", "markedly higher than group B (p = 0.008)"; no effect size is printed.

> "In group A, 98.3% of students concurred that the REPs-VS teaching platform enhanced their understanding by providing an emulation of the operational requirements and learning needs. The average score of the questionnaire was 4.38 ± 0.80, which was markedly higher than group B (p = 0.008)"

### Hu Jia 2025 (2)

Hu Jia, Wang Xiuqing, Chen Ruonan, Lu Guoying, Zhang Ming, Huang Xiaojing. (2025). Use of virtual simulation for regenerative endodontic training: randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06808-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same questionnaire, knowledge-and-skills items: the clinical reasoning item averaged 4.20 ± 0.88 (between-group p = 0.058) and the communication item 4.13 ± 0.81 (p = 0.569), the latter not significantly different between groups.

> "98.4% of students agreed that their clinical reasoning, knowledge, and decision-making skills have been improved with an average score of 4.20 ± 0.88. In addition, 98.3% of students agreed that the platform improved their doctor-patient communication abilities and humanistic literacy (score = 4.13 ± 0.81)"
