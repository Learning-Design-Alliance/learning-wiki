# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, student motivation rose from pre-test to post-test after self-assessment implementation, while cognitive strategies, resource management and total MSLQ scores did not change significantly

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The motivation subscale of the MSLQ was significantly higher at post-test than at pre-test after self-assessment implementation in one uncontrolled pre-post study, with a higher post mean (169.4) than pre mean (124.6). [→ Khoiriyah Umatul 2025](#khoiriyah-umatul-2025)
`q3 i?` Cognitive strategies, resource management and total MSLQ scores were not significantly different before and after self-assessment implementation. [→ Khoiriyah Umatul 2025 (2)](#khoiriyah-umatul-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Khoiriyah Umatul 2025

Khoiriyah Umatul, Roberts Chris. (2025). Investigating the role of self-assessment in enhancing self-regulated learning amongst medical students in problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07359-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test of pre- and post-course MSLQ scores in a single-group interventional study (n = 65). The pre/post motivation means were 124.6 and 169.4, with the article reporting "significantly increased"; no effect size is printed.

> "The students’ motivation after implementation of self-assessment was significantly increased. However, students’ cognitive strategies, resource management and total score of the MSLQ before and after the test were not significantly different (p > 0.05)."

### Khoiriyah Umatul 2025 (2)

Khoiriyah Umatul, Roberts Chris. (2025). Investigating the role of self-assessment in enhancing self-regulated learning amongst medical students in problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07359-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same paired t-test analysis (Table 2, n = 65) shows the motivation post mean of 169.4 exceeded the pre mean; cognitive strategies (163.1 to 158.7), resource management (93.1 to 93.0) and total score means changed little. No effect size printed.

> "Table 2 indicates that the mean of students’ motivation after the implementation (169.4) was higher than before it."
