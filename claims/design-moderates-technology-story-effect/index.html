Evidence¶

Takacs 2015¶

Takacs, Z. K., Swart, E. K., & Bus, A. G. (2015). Benefits and Pitfalls of Multimedia and Interactive Features in Technology-Enhanced Storybooks: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research, 85(4), 698-739. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654314566989

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analytic moderator analysis of study design, with printed subgroup effect size g+ = 0.33 for between-subject studies versus g+ = -0.02 for within-subject studies. Publication status was not a significant moderator, Qbetween(1) = 0.26, p = .61, and the fail-safe N was 344.

"On average, studies with a between-subject design yielded an average effect of 0.33, k = 40, SE = 0.08, 95% CI = [0.17, 0.48], p < .01, which was significantly higher than studies incorporating a within-subject design, g+ = −0.02, k = 17, SE = 0.11, 95% CI = [−0.24, 0.21], p = .89), Qbetween(2) = 6.25, p = .01."
