q2 · i? · review · r2

Frequency analysis of measure types across the 41 plans with a postsecondary readiness indicator, reported in Table 2. Table 2 shows any academic measure in 40 states (98%), any career measure in 34 states (83%), and "only 27 percent of plans explicitly had a military-focused measure." Pennsylvania was the only state with no academic measure.

"However, only 27 percent of plans explicitly had a military-focused measure, such as achieving a threshold score on the ASVAB exam or enlisting in a military branch (see Table 2)."
