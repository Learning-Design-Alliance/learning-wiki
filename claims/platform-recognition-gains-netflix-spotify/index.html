Jake Renzella 2026¶

Jake Renzella, Christian Bergh, Natasha Banks, and Alexandra Vassar. (2026). Gender Differences in AI Literacy Workshop Outcomes and Deepfake Engagement. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14718

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pre–post percentage-point changes in platform identification from the post-intervention survey (npost = 136). The article prints percentage changes with p-values but no effect sizes for these items; TikTok gain (+12.8%, p=.016) was female-only.

"Both genders showed an increase in recognition of AI’s role within enter- tainment platforms when identifying platforms that use AI, specifically Netflix (Male:+27.2%,p=<.001; Female:+28.2%,p=<.001) and Spotify (Male: +25.3%,p=.001; Female:+17.8%,p=.011)."
