Evidence¶

Steven Wise 2022¶

Steven Wise, Megan Kuhfeld, John Cronin. (2022). Assessment in the time of COVID-19: Understanding patterns of student disengagement during remote low-stakes testing. Educational Assessment, 27(2), 136-151. https://doi.org/10.1080/10627197.2022.2087621

q3 · i? · associational · r?

The study evaluated the assessment's engagement feature, which "automatically paused the test event of a disengaged student and notified the test proctor," and found a consistently positive impact under both in-room and remote proctoring. No effect size is printed in the available text.
