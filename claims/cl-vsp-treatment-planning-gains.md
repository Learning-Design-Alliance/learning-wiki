## Evidence

### Xu Liyuan 2025

Xu Liyuan, Xu Qinrong, Liu Chunya, Chen Baozhen, Wang Chunxia. (2025). Virtual Standardized Patients for Improving Clinical Thinking Ability Training in Residents: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/73196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Treatment plan assessment from the randomized controlled trial after 4 weeks of training, reported in Figure 3J-L. The VSP group showed "significantly greater improvement in medication management ability" and in nonpharmacological treatment ability.

> "the medical students in the experimental group showed significantly greater improvement in medication management ability (mean 5.69, SD 2.27 vs mean 4.51, SD 2.14; F1, 59=4.29; Cohen d=0.62; 95% CI 4.51-5.69; P=.04) and in nonpharmacological treatment ability (mean 1.54, SD 0.47 vs mean 1.21, SD 0.43; F1, 59=7.85; Cohen d=0.68; 95% CI 1.25-1.50; P=.007) compared to the control group."

### Xu Liyuan 2025 (2)

Xu Liyuan, Xu Qinrong, Liu Chunya, Chen Baozhen, Wang Chunxia. (2025). Virtual Standardized Patients for Improving Clinical Thinking Ability Training in Residents: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/73196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Overall treatment outcome from the same trial's treatment plan assessment: improvement was "significantly higher in the experimental group than in the control group" (Cohen d=0.63, P=.01).

> "Additionally, the improvement in the overall ability to provide treatment to patients was significantly higher in the experimental group than in the control group (mean 7.23, SD 2.41 vs mean 5.72, SD 2.19; F1, 59=6.42; Cohen d=0.63; 95% CI 5.85-7.01; P=.01)."
