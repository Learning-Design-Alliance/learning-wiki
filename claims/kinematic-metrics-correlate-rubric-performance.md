`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that path length, the total distance traveled by instrument tips during a task, shows "reductions in path length have been correlated with improved performance on structured rubrics" across several studies.

> "In several studies, reductions in path length have been correlated with improved performance on structured rubrics, suggesting construct validity as a measure of surgical expertise."

### Laughlin Jacob L. BS 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Under Robotic Control, the review reports expert-novice contrasts: experts show "lower instrument vibration magnitudes, fewer collisions, and reduced contact forces compared to novices", correlating with higher GRS and GEARS scores.

> "Studies have shown that experts tend to exhibit lower instrument vibration magnitudes, fewer collisions, and reduced contact forces compared to novices, all of which correlate with higher GRS and GEARS scores."
