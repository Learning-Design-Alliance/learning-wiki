The essay's review of the student-evaluation literature, citing Cohen and McKeachie (1980) and others, concludes that "Student evaluations provide few concrete ideas for improving instructional effectiveness or learning outcomes." It adds that evaluations typically measure satisfaction and didactic teaching rather than learning.

> "Student evaluations provide few concrete ideas for improving instructional effectiveness or learning outcomes (Cohen and McKeachie, 1980; Abrami et al., 1990) or changing curriculum or course objectives (Neal 1988; Abrami 1989)."
