## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors hypothesize that analysis of DGBL on a micro level (learning mechanics and game mechanics) is needed to understand what makes a digital game achieve its educational and playfulness objectives. [→ Proulx 2017](#proulx-2017)

## Evidence

### Proulx 2017

Proulx, J. N., Romero, M., & Arnab, S. (2017). Learning Mechanics and Game Mechanics Under the Perspective of Self-Determination Theory to Foster Motivation in Digital Game Based Learning. Simulation & Gaming. https://doi.org/10.1177/1046878116674399

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Authors' hypothesis, grounded in their interpretation of inconsistent DGBL-motivation findings: mechanics like competition may motivate some students while other mechanics "could even hinder motivation". This is a theoretical position, not a tested result.

> "we make the hypothesis that we should consider the analysis of DGBL on a micro-level (considering the learning mechanics  and game mechanics ) rather than on a macro level  (considering the game as a whole)."
