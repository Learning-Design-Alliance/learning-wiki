Weaver K. F., Morales V., Nelson M., Weaver P. F., Toledo A., Godde K. (2016). The Benefits of Peer Review and a Multisemester Capstone Writing Series on Inquiry and Analysis Skills in an Undergraduate Thesis. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0072

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA testing change over time of composite scores across the intervention years (2009–2013); the null result is reported as stability of the gains, not as evidence of equivalence between years.

> "the one-way ANOVA was not significant (F value = 0.1211, df = 4, p value = 0.974), demonstrating the composite score gains were stable within the intervention group from implementation to the end of the data-collection time frame."
