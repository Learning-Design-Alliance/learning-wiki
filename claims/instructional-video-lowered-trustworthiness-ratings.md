## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students who watched the instructional video found the interviewees less trustworthy than students who had not (η²p=.046). [→ Colin Lescarret 2022](#colin-lescarret-2022)

## Evidence

### Colin Lescarret 2022

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on perceived trustworthiness ratings with effect size "η²p=.046" (F(1,250)=12.144, p<.001); the video group rated interviewees less trustworthy, 95% CI [4.30:4.57] vs [4.64:4.91], M difference=0.339, t(254)=-3.48, pbonferroni<.001. The article labels this effect unexpected.

> "An unexpected main effect of the factor Instructional Video was observed, F(1,250)=12.144, p<.001, η²p=.046, as students who had watched the instructional video in pretest found the interviewees to be less trustworthy than the other students"
