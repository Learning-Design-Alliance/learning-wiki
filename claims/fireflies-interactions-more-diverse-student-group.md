# FireFlies enabled interactions with a more diverse group of students, countering a distorted picture of student readiness

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` By letting all students signal answers or needs, FireFlies surfaced students outside the usual small group that interacts with the teacher, revealing needs teachers would otherwise miss. [→ Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An 2020](#nine-sellier-and-pengcheng-an-2020)

## Evidence

### Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An 2020

Nine Sellier and Pengcheng An. (2020). How Peripheral Interactive Systems Can Support Teachers with Differentiated Instruction: Using FireFlies as a Probe. To appear in DIS '20, July 6–10, 2020, Eindhoven, Netherlands. https://doi.org/10.1145/3357236.3395497

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation and teacher report during a quiz using answering teachers' questions: P5 was surprised how many students did not know the answer; without FireFlies the same group always answered, which she felt gave a "distorted picture of the readiness of the other students".

> "in lessons without the FireFlies, there would always be the same group of students who would give answers to the questions and that this might have given her a distorted picture of the readiness of the other students"
