`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Secondary finding from the mixed-model analyses, reported in the Discussion: GPA was included as a demographic covariate and "student GPA did not predict whether or not students placed more value on the group activity."

> "Interestingly, student GPA did not predict whether or not students placed more value on the group activity, suggesting that, all else being equal, students in the top and bottom of the class do not have different perceptions of the activities."
