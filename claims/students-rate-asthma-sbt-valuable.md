## Evidence

### Liu Zhenhua 2022

Liu Zhenhua, Chen Qiong, Wu Jing, Li Xinhua, He Yuchen, Yu Qiao. (2022). Simulation-based training in asthma exacerbation for medical students: effect of prior exposure to simulation training on performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03300-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Voluntary post-course evaluation survey based on the Kirkpatrick scale, completed by 106 of 203 students (52.21%) on https://www.wjx.cn. The article reports the printed percentages of strongly agree and agree responses.

> "Large percentages of students, 73.21% and 26.13% strongly agreed and agreed, respectively, that SBT improved their skills in problem-solving, decision-making and effective communication with patients, and resulted in a comprehensive understanding of their strengths and weaknesses in the management of asthma exacerbation."

### Liu Zhenhua 2022 (2)

Liu Zhenhua, Chen Qiong, Wu Jing, Li Xinhua, He Yuchen, Yu Qiao. (2022). Simulation-based training in asthma exacerbation for medical students: effect of prior exposure to simulation training on performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03300-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same post-course survey. The article reports that 0.66% of participants found the training stressful, indicating the experience undermined confidence for a small minority.

> "However, 0.66% of participants indicated that the training undermined their confidence and increased the tension and fear in facing clinical problems."
