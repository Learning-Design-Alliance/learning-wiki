`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review tabulated pedagogical tools and learning theories for each of the 68 programs (Table 2). Named theories in the table include self-efficacy theory, Roger's Diffusion of Innovation theory, social cognitive theory, the transtheoretical model of change, Ely's Conditions for Change Model, and the ARCC model based on cognitive-behavioural theory and control theory; many programs list only pedagogical tools such as didactic, collaborative and experiential learning.
