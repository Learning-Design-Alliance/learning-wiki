## Subclaims
`q3 i?` RTE scores, computed from dichotomous item solution-behavior indices, demonstrated adequate reliability with coefficient alpha far above the .80 criterion. [→ Wise 2005](#wise-2005)

## Evidence

### Wise 2005

Wise, S. L., & Kong, X. (2005). Response Time Effort: A New Measure of Examinee Motivation in Computer-Based Tests. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, Montreal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED490203. Published version, Applied Measurement in Education: [doi:10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric evaluation of RTE scores for 472 freshmen taking an 80-item computer-based Information Literacy Test in a low-stakes assessment session. Because solution behavior was dichotomously scored per item, coefficient alpha was used, and the scores showed "a very high degree of internal consistency."

> "The observed value of alpha was .97, indicating a very high degree of internal consistency."
