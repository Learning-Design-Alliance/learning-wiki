## Evidence

### Wang Yun 2025

Wang Yun, Tian Hong, Li Jianyun, Zhou Chongzhi, Feng Yiwen, Li Jinbao, Huang Lina. (2025). Effect of flipped classroom to anesthesia crisis management simulation education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07011-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results-section comparison of ANTS subcategory ratings between the FC (n = 20) and CL (n = 20) groups, each subcategory scored 1–4 by blinded video raters. Three of four subcategories favored FC as printed; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The ANTS scores of the FC group in ‘Decision making’, ‘Team-working and leadership’, and ‘Task management’ were all higher than those in the CL group (FC vs. CL: 2.90 ± 0.97 vs. 2.10 ± 1.07, p = 0.018; 3.20 ± 0.83 vs 2.40 ± 0.94, p = 0.007; 2.75 ± 1.12 vs 2.10 ± 0.79, p = 0.040)."

### Wang Yun 2025 (2)

Wang Yun, Tian Hong, Li Jianyun, Zhou Chongzhi, Feng Yiwen, Li Jinbao, Huang Lina. (2025). Effect of flipped classroom to anesthesia crisis management simulation education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07011-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result for the Situation awareness subcategory in the same ANTS rating of the 40 residents; the test showed "no significant difference between the two groups" (p = 0.632). Equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference between the two groups in ‘Situation awareness’ ANTS scores (FC vs. CL: 3.10 ± 0.97 vs. 2.95 ± 1.00, p = 0.632)."
