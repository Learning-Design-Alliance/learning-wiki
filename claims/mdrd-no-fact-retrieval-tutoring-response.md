## Evidence

### Powell 2009

Powell, S. R., Fuchs, L. S., Fuchs, D., Cirino, P. T., & Fletcher, J. M. (2009). Effects of Fact Retrieval Tutoring on Third-Grade Students with Math Difficulties with and without Reading Difficulties. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice, 24(1), 1-11. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5826.2008.01272.x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized trial (n = 139), Tukey follow-up comparisons found no differences among the four tutoring conditions for MDRD students; Table 3 effect sizes for MDRD contrasts ranged from -0.34 to 0.17 with confidence intervals spanning zero.

> "In contrast, for MDRD students, there were no differences among tutoring conditions."
