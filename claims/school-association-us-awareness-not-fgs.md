# School attended was associated with urinary schistosomiasis awareness (p = 0.002) but not with FGS awareness (p = 0.458)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Chi-square analysis found a statistically significant association between school attended and having heard of urinary schistosomiasis (Keta 47.2% vs UHAS 30.0% vs Hohoe 22.8%; p = 0.002), while no such association existed for FGS (p = 0.458). [→ Azanu 2024](#azanu-2024)
