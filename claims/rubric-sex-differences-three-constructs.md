## Evidence

### Young 2022

Young, N. T., Tollefson, K., Zegers, R. G. T., and Caballero, M. D. (2022). Rubric-based holistic review: a promising route to equitable graduate admissions in physics. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020140)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of faculty rubric ratings by applicant sex (the application system recorded only male or female) among the 321 domestic applicants. "While males tended to score higher on the physics GRE score, females tended to score higher on community contributions and diversity contributions"; these were the only three constructs with statistically significant differences.
