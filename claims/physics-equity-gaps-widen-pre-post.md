## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson M. Nissen 2018

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson M. Nissen. (2018). Systemic inequities in introductory physics courses: the impacts of learning assistants. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.095](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.095)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from pre-post concept inventory data (FCI or FMCE) collected through the LASSO platform, after cleaning and hierarchical multiple imputation of the dataset of 4,365 students in 93 courses. The article reports the pretest gaps of "11.9% for underserved gender and 8.1% for underserved race/ethnicity" widening to 12.3% and 12.0% at posttest. No inferential test or effect size is printed for this descriptive comparison.

> "The gaps that students began the course with (11.9% for underserved gender and 8.1% for underserved race/ethnicity) are even wider by the posttest (12.3% for underserved gender and 12.0% for underserved race/ethnicity)."
