`q2 i1` Underclassmen in the module group scored significantly higher (d = 0.33, p < 0.001), while upperclassmen showed no significant difference between treatments (p = 0.73). [→ Goff Eric E. 2017](#goff-eric-e-2017)

## Evidence

### Goff Eric E. 2017

Goff Eric E., Reindl Katie M., Johnson Christina, McClean Phillip, Offerdahl Erika G., Schroeder Noah L., White Alan R. (2017). Efficacy of a Meiosis Learning Module Developed for the Virtual Cell Animation Collection. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(1):ar9. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0141

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Year-in-school stratification of the quasi-experiment grouped students into underclassmen and upperclassmen; "significantly higher normalized gain scores (t(281.33) = 4.51, p < 0.001, d = 0.33)" favored the module for underclassmen only.

> "Analysis of student performance shows significantly higher normalized gain scores (t(281.33) = 4.51, p < 0.001, d = 0.33) for underclassmen who interacted with the online-learning module compared with those whose received instruction in the traditional lecture treatment"
