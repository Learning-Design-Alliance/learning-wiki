### Asfaw 2022

Asfaw, A., Blais, A., Brown, K. R., et al. (2022). Building a Quantum Engineering Undergraduate Program. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1109/te.2022.3144943](https://doi.org/10.1109/te.2022.3144943)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article addresses, in its Quantum 101 section, the concern that a course without sophisticated math would "require sacriﬁcing rigor or accuracy", and cites references 77-79 and 50 for the arithmetic-based formalism. The article states some authors have used it for high-school outreach and freshman courses, but reports no outcome data and notes more research on efficacy is needed.

> "Surprisingly, that does not have to be the case, and there now exists a formalism that can explain quantum states, the concept of superposition, entanglement, and unitary transformations, as well as quantum algorithms rigorously without the need for linear algebra [77], [78], [79], [50]. The only requirement is knowledge of basic arithmetic."
