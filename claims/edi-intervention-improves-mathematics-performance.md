## Evidence

### Cantero María-José 2020

Cantero María-José, Bañuls Raquel, Viguer Paz. (2020). Effectiveness of an Emotional Intelligence Intervention and Its Impact on Academic Performance in Spanish Pre-Adolescent Elementary Students: Results from the EDI Program. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17207621

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures analysis of mathematics performance in the intervention group. The article reports "a significant increase in scores from T1 to T2" with d = 0.52, maintained through T4 (d = 0.42); the comparison group's time effect was not significant (p = 0.14).

> "In the intervention group, there was a significant increase in scores from T1 to T2 (mean difference = 0.75, SE = 0.09, p < 0.001, d = 0.52, 95% CI [0.52, 0.98]), from T1 to T3 (mean difference = 0.43, SE = 0.14, p = 0.01, d = 0.28, 95% CI [0.07, 0.80]), and from T1 to T4 (mean difference = 0.66, SE = 0.15, p < 0.001, d = 0.42, 95% CI [0.27, 1.06])."
