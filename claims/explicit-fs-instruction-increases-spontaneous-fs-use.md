### Liou 2018

Liou, H.-C., & Chen, W.-F. (2018). Effects of explicit instruction on learning academic formulaic sequences for EFL college learners' writing. Taiwan Journal of TESOL, 15(1), 61-100. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1176537

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Token and type counts of the 50 taught FSs were tallied across pretest summaries, posttest summaries, book reports, and research reports. Mean tokens per student rose from 0.13 to 0.87, a "significant difference."

> "The mean number, token, of target FSs used per student in the pretest writing was 0.13, which was calculated as the total used target FSs divided by 15 st udents and in the posttest writing was 0.87. Their comparison shows a significant difference ( p = 7.86e-05 < .05; z = -3.95)."
