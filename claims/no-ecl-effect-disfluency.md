# Disfluency and WMC × disfluency did not affect extraneous cognitive load, contrary to hypotheses

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Contrary to Hypothesis 6, neither disfluency nor the disfluency × WMC interaction significantly predicted extraneous cognitive load. [→ Janina Lehmann 2015](#janina-lehmann-2015)

## Evidence

### Janina Lehmann 2015

Janina Lehmann, Christina Goussios, Tina Seufert. (2015). Working memory capacity and disfluency effect: an aptitude-treatment-interaction study. Metacognition Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-015-9149-z

`q2 · i?`

Regression analyses of subjective ECL ratings after learning and after testing in the 47-student experiment found no significant effects; the regression models were only marginally significant (p=.06).

> "In contrast to our hypotheses, neither disfluency nor the interaction term disfluency × WMC were significant predictors for ECL."
