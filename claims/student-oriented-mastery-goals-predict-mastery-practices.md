## Evidence

### Daumiller 2025

Daumiller, M., Gaspard, H., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2025). Bridging teacher motivation and instruction: Relevance of student‐oriented goals for teaching alongside personal achievement goals and self‐efficacy. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S98–S114. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12776

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-level path modelling on 70 teachers with 345 lesson diary entries, analysing between-teacher differences in teaching practices at the teacher level. The article reports that "student‐oriented mastery goals positively predicted interestingness, cognitive stimulation, individualization and collaboration".

> "Specifically, student‐oriented mastery goals positively predicted interestingness, cognitive stimulation, individualization and collaboration, as expected and were also (but only in the combined model) linked to increased reports of competition."
