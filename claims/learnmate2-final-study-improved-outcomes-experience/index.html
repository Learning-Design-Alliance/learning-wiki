final-study¶

In Designing Interactive Systems Conference (DIS '26), June 13–17, 2026, Singapore, Singapore. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3800645.3812972

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The authors conducted a final user study (n=16) comparing LearnMate2 against a workflow combining a state-of-the-art online learning platform and an LLM used for learning support. They report that "LearnMate2 advances AI pedagogy by improving both learning outcomes and user experience compared to existing online learning and support tools."

"Results indicate that LearnMate2 advances AI pedagogy by improving both learning outcomes and user experience compared to existing online learning and support tools."
