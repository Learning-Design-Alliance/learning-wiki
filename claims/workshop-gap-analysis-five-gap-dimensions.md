`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative gap analysis of presentation slides and notes from a two-day workshop with five heads of science of learning labs from three countries, thematically analysed using an adapted Miles framework. The analysis "Gaps were identiﬁed in relation to" five dimensions and reported consensus that the challenges are shared internationally.

> "Gaps were identiﬁed in relation to: (1) existing theory and knowledge; (2) the research-practice divide; (3) research methodologies; (4) empirical testing and evidence veriﬁcation; and (5) research with speciﬁc populations."
