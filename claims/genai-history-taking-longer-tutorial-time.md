Descriptive observation of audio recordings from the four tutorial groups (two per condition) found genAI tutorials ran 55–65 min versus 36–39 min for ePBLM, with history-taking at "24–30 min vs. 16–19 min". Only genAI groups spent 10–12 min reporting diagnosis to the patient.

> "GenAI groups spent approximately 10 min longer taking the patient history than ePBLM groups (24–30 min vs. 16–19 min for genAI and ePBLM groups, respectively)."
