## Evidence

### Talan Jordan 2024

Talan Jordan, Mangalick Keshav, Pradhan Deepak, Sauthoff Harald. (2024). Accuracy of Echocardiographic Cardiac Output Assessment by Critical Care Fellows. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2024-0030OC

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pilot study of seven fellows performing 35 echocardiograms on 32 ICU patients after a mastery learning intervention, compared with formal echocardiography. Fellow VTI averaged "17.0 ± 4.37 cm" versus cardiologist 17.3 ± 5.19 cm; the printed correlation effect size was "0.73 (P < 0.001)" with 19.5 ± 12.0% mean percent difference.

> "The average fellow-reported LVOT VTI was 17.0 ± 4.37 cm, whereas the average cardiologist-reported VTI was 17.3 ± 5.19 cm. The mean percent difference between fellow-reported VTI and cardiologist-reported VTI was 19.5 ± 12.0. The correlation between fellow- and cardiologist-reported VTI was 0.73 (P < 0.001)"
