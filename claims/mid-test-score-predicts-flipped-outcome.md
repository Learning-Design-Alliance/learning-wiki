## Evidence

### Chen Kuo-Su 2021

Chen Kuo-Su, Hsieh Ming-Ju, Huang Min-Ping, Chen Chih-Ken, Hung Ming-Jui. (2021). Academic outcome and moderator of flipped classroom learning program “Teaching on the Run”. Biomedical Journal, 44(5), 636–643. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bj.2020.05.009

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

General linear model on post-test scores of the 115 TOTR participants; the printed mid-test coefficient was β = 0.761 (0.631, 0.890), p < 0.001, and session remained significant (p = 0.046). No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Multivariate analysis by GLM with post-test scores as the independent variable and significant factors in univariate analysis as covariates showed that only mid-test scores and session of TOTR were significant predictors of final outcome [Table 3]."
