`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-year comparison of cluster-level pass rates from platform logs (Results §3.2, Figure 3b). The article reports 2024 pass rates of "33.6% and 10.2%" for System Level and Temporal and a 2025 System Level rate of 64.5%. No effect size or significance test is printed.

> "In 2024, System Level and Temporal topics emerged as the most significant hurdles, characterized by low pass rates (33.6% and 10.2%, respectively) that did not improve with repeated attempts. The 2025 data shows a remarkable recovery in these areas, with System Level pass rates climbing to 64.5%, suggesting improved alignment of question difficulty."
