`q3 i2` Students randomized to a peer-led mock OSCE had significantly lower state anxiety (STAI) than controls, with a moderate effect size (Cohen's d = 0.692). [→ Lima Leonardo Mateus de 2026](#lima-leonardo-mateus-de-2026)

## Evidence

### Lima Leonardo Mateus de 2026

Lima Leonardo Mateus de, Favarato Maria Helena, Tibério Iolanda Fátima Lopes Calvo. (2026). Empowering medical students: Peer-Led OSCE reduces anxiety and may enhance test performance. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0340407

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial: 28 intervention vs 39 control fifth-semester medical students; STAI state subscale completed immediately after the summative OSCE. The intervention group scored "significantly lower state anxiety levels" with mean difference 8.79 points, p = 0.009, Cohen's d = 0.692.

> "students in the intervention group had significantly lower state anxiety levels (54.8 ± 13.40, mean ± standard deviation) compared to the control group (63.56 ± 12.20, p = 0.009; mean difference = 8.79, 95% CI [2.25, 15.3], Cohen's d = 0.692)"
