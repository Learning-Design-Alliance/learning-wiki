The review's research applications theme reports that AI-powered tools accelerate literature review, data analysis and evidence synthesis, with orthopaedic residents reporting faster writing and literature review.

"Generative AI models (e.g., ChatGPT/GPT-4) have been used to refine search strategies, summarize large bodies of papers, and even draft coherent narrative syntheses"
