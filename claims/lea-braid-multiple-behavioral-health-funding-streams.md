# California LEAs cobble together school-based behavioral health funding from short-term funds, ongoing education funds, and Medi-Cal reimbursement, with no ongoing dedicated source

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` LEAs and local partners braid and blend funding from three categories—short-term funding, ongoing education funding, and ongoing health care funding via Medi-Cal reimbursement—because no ongoing funding source covers the full breadth of needed supports. [→ Caparas 2024](#caparas-2024)

## Evidence

### Caparas 2024

Caparas, R., Eisenberg, L., Krausen, K., & Lias, C. (2024). Schools can't do it alone: Developing sustainable systems of care for school-based behavioral health in California. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655746

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Analysis across expert interviews, SBHIP needs assessments and project plans, and literature review found LEAs lack an ongoing dedicated behavioral health funding source and instead "braid and blend a number of different funding sources" across the three categories the brief names.

> "LEAs and local partners braid and blend a number of different funding sources, which generally fall into three categories: short-term funding, ongoing education funding, and ongoing health care funding via Medi-Cal reimbursement."
