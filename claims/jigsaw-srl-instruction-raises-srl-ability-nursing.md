## Evidence

### Chen Yiwen 2025

Chen Yiwen, Song Jiali, Li Mengying, He Huijuan, Wang Xiangrong, Zhou Siyu, Wang Ling. (2025). Integrating jigsaw teaching into self-regulated learning instruction: an instructional design to improve nursing students’ self-regulated learning. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1437265

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with two intact classes (intervention n=40, control n=44) in a Basic Nursing course; independent and paired t-tests on the Self-regulated Learning Ability Scale. The between-group post-test difference was "statistically significant (p < 0.05)", with printed Cohen's d = 0.524 (between groups) and d = 0.428 (within intervention group).

> "After the intervention, the difference between the two groups in nursing students’ self-regulated ability was statistically significant (p < 0.05)."
