## Evidence

### David Menendez 2023

David Menendez. (2023). Cues to generality: Integrating linguistic and visual information when generalizing biological information. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000812

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content analysis of 611 accompanying texts found "47.8% (n = 655) of the sentences that accompanied visualizations used generics"; 62.1% of accompanying texts (n = 427) included at least one generic sentence, and 2.0% (n = 28) were exemplified generics.

> "About 47.8% (n = 655) of the sentences that accompanied visualizations used generics and about 40.0% (n = 548) used non-generics (12.1% of sentences used other linguistic structures, such as questions)."
