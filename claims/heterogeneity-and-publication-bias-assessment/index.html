Evidence¶

Turgut 2018¶

Turgut, S., & Gülşen Turgut, İ. (2018). The Effects of Cooperative Learning on Mathematics Achievement in Turkey: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 663-680. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11345a

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Heterogeneity assessment in Table 3: fixed-effect Q=339,497 exceeded the chi-square critical value of 76,778 for df=58, and I² of 82.916% indicated high heterogeneity, so the random-effects model was used for the pooled estimate.

"it can be said that the studies meta-analyzed show a heterogeneous distribution. Additionally, I2 value with 82.916% addresses high level of heterogeneity. Random effect model was preferred for calculating the average effect sizes"

Turgut 2018 (2)¶

Turgut, S., & Gülşen Turgut, İ. (2018). The Effects of Cooperative Learning on Mathematics Achievement in Turkey: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 663-680. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11345a

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Publication-bias assessment using the funnel plot (shown in Figure 2 as largely symmetrical but not absolutely so) and Rosenthal's fail-safe N (FSN=8910 in Table 2, discussed as N=891). The Mullen et al. (2001) ratio exceeded 1, so the findings were deemed resistant to publication bias.

"According to N/(5k+10) formula (Mullen et al., 2001) 8910/(5*59+10) the calculati on is 29,213. According to this calculation, it can be identified the studies included in meta -analysis are resistant to publication bias."
