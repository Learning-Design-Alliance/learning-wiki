Linus Brunnström & Lisa Palmqvist 2026¶

Linus Brunnström & Lisa Palmqvist. (2026). AI-interaction literacy: reflections on how generative AI might be used to support self-regulated learning in higher education. Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2026.2731261

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Reflective demonstration: the authors, role-playing a naive student, prompted Claude Opus 4.6 with the exam question 'Do machines think?' with course-literature and word-limit restrictions. The polished essay-style answer "would most likely receive a pass" yet listed replies to Searle's Chinese Room argument without explaining them.

"In SOLO terms, the response remained largely at the quantitative phase: it organised relevant ideas but did not support the learner in building their own qualitative, relational understanding."
