## Evidence

### Liang Zhanming 2018

Liang Zhanming, Blackstock Felicity C., Howard Peter F, Briggs David S, Leggat Sandra G, Wollersheim Dennis, Edvardsson David, Rahman Aziz. (2018). An evidence-based approach to understanding the competency development needs of the health service management workforce in Australia. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-018-3760-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired t-tests comparing self-assessment with individual colleague assessments in the MCAP 360° study (Table 6); the article reports "means for all the six competencies assessed by peers were statistically significantly higher than the mean self-assessment score", and likewise for reports, while for supervisors only C3 (Knowledge) reached significance (p = 0.002). No effect sizes printed.
