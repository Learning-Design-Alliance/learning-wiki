`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective longitudinal study (N = 1094, ages 5.6 to 11.1) examining family factors associated with school mobility. The article reports that "changes in family income and household structure did predict higher odds of two or more moves" while no family factors predicted a recent or single move; no odds ratios are printed.

> "Family factors were not linked to a recent move or a single move, but changes in family income and household structure did predict higher odds of two or more moves in elementary school."
