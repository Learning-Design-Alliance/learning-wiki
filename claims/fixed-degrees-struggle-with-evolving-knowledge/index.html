Smadar Szekely 2018¶

Smadar Szekely, Judith Gal-Ezer, and David Harel. (2018). Rethinking Higher Education: From Fixed Curricula to Learnity Graphs. J. ACM 37, 4, Article 111. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08543 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.08543

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the introduction, citing higher-education research: rigid structures evolve slowly relative to rapidly changing knowledge domains, and the authors argue this "leads to program proliferation with only marginal differentiation". No empirical data are reported.

"When new fields or techniques emerge (e.g., data science and machine learning), institutions typically respond by creating new degree programs, often ones that overlap significantly with existing ones."
