## Evidence

### Töpfer J 2026

Töpfer J, Fritsch S, Wollny A, Romeike B, Endlicher D, Schrötter S, Schmitz S, Ciężka A. (2026). Pilot of a blended learning model on medical students’ communication skills in the context of digital innovations. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09662-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test descriptive results from the pilot questionnaire (n = 6); the article notes that "competencies related to digital innovations in physician–patient communication showed the lowest baseline confidence levels but the strongest subjective learning gains following the intervention."

> "In contrast, confidence ratings below this threshold were observed for knowledge of narrative communication, narrative elicitation, questioning techniques and information gathering, agenda setting, and all scales related to managing digital innovations."
