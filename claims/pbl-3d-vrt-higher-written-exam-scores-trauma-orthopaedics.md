## Evidence

### Xue Gang 2025

Xue Gang, Chen Siting, Xie JiaBing, Yang Min, Wang Zhengyu. (2025). Application of problem-based learning combined with three-dimensional visualization reconstruction technology in trauma orthopedics teaching and its impact on teaching satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08364-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Single-blinded randomized controlled trial of 50 clinical medical students randomized to PBL+3D-VRT (n=25) or traditional teaching (n=25). The Results section reports the post-intervention written examination contrast: "mean difference = 5.20, 95% CI: 3.62–6.78, P < 0.01".

> "The written examination scores were 84.72 ± 2.39 for the experimental group versus 79.52 ± 2.21 for the control group (mean difference = 5.20, 95% CI: 3.62–6.78, P < 0.01)."

### Xue Gang 2025 (2)

Xue Gang, Chen Siting, Xie JiaBing, Yang Min, Wang Zhengyu. (2025). Application of problem-based learning combined with three-dimensional visualization reconstruction technology in trauma orthopedics teaching and its impact on teaching satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08364-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison in the same RCT before teaching: written examination scores were 68.84 ± 2.40 versus 69.16 ± 1.80 with P = 0.603, indicating comparable baseline performance; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "Before the intervention, there were no significant differences in written examination scores (experimental group: 68.84 ± 2.40; control group: 69.16 ± 1.80; P = 0.603)"

### Xue Gang 2025 (3)

Xue Gang, Chen Siting, Xie JiaBing, Yang Min, Wang Zhengyu. (2025). Application of problem-based learning combined with three-dimensional visualization reconstruction technology in trauma orthopedics teaching and its impact on teaching satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08364-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Statistical analysis section of the same trial reports a post hoc effect size for the written examination between-group difference: "Cohen's d = 1.02", with calculated power of 0.91 at α = 0.05 for 25 students per group.

> "A post hoc power analysis was conducted based on the difference in written examination scores between the two groups (Cohen's d = 1.02). The calculated statistical power was 0.91 at α = 0.05"
