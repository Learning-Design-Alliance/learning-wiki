## Evidence

### Loo Kang WEE and Giam Hwee GOH 2012

Loo Kang WEE and Giam Hwee GOH. (2012). Geostationary Earth Orbit Satellite Model using Easy Java Simulation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1212.3863](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1212.3863)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative survey and interview themes from the laboratory lessons. Students noted "Not enough group activities. The questions are not interesting enough" and suggested "more diverse options", which inspired the authors to add non-geostationary orbit options at 3*RE and 10.5*RE. No effect sizes are reported.

> "We suspect that the students are requesting for more open-ended inquiry activities to be designed for learning instead of embedding the simulation to existing tutorial questions that tend to be more theoretical"
