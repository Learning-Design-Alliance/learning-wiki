Survey administration after the first semester of a two-semester graduate quantum mechanics course to 33 first-year graduate students, who averaged 51.6%. This interpretation comes from discussions with the two graduate course instructors after they saw the results.

> "the graduate instructors agreed that many of the graduate students lacked conceptual understanding necessary for performing well on the survey even though they do well on the quantitative exams typically given in the graduate level courses"
