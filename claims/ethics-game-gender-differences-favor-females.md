## Evidence

### Michalis Xenos 2019

Michalis Xenos, Vasiliki Velli. (2019). A Serious Game for Introducing Software Engineering Ethics to University Students. 21st International Conference on Interactive Collaborative Learning, ICL2018, Kos, Greece. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Group comparison (RQ4) within the same 144-student study: 29 female and 115 male students. Because normality was violated (Kolmogorov-Smirnov), two-tailed Mann-Whitney U tests were used. The article reports the gain "statistically significant higher for the female students".

> "The Normalized learning gain was higher for female students (M=53.60, SD=45.26) than male students (M=20.42, SD=53.83) and using a two-tailed Man-Whitney U test, we have found that the normalized learning gain was statistically significant higher for the female students (U=1172, Z=-2.477, p=0.013)"

### Michalis Xenos 2019 (2)

Michalis Xenos, Vasiliki Velli. (2019). A Serious Game for Introducing Software Engineering Ethics to University Students. 21st International Conference on Interactive Collaborative Learning, ICL2018, Kos, Greece. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same Mann-Whitney comparisons on the adjective rating and the 3-item educational experience scale; the article concludes female students "enjoyed the Ethics Game more than male students" and found it a more valuable educational tool.

> "For the usability adjective rating the female students evaluated the Ethics Game higher (M=5.34, SD=0.72) compared to male students (M=4.24, SD=0.99) and the two-tailed Man-Whitney U test revealed that the adjective evaluation was statistically significant higher for the female students (U=1016.5, Z=-3.566, p=0.000)"

### Michalis Xenos 2019 (3)

Michalis Xenos, Vasiliki Velli. (2019). A Serious Game for Introducing Software Engineering Ethics to University Students. 21st International Conference on Interactive Collaborative Learning, ICL2018, Kos, Greece. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

SUS comparison between the same gender groups; the article reports the difference as not statistically significant but marginal (p=0.054). Equivalence was not tested.

> "Regarding the SUS, although the female students evaluated the Ethics Game higher (M=85.34, SD=7.70) compared to male students (M=79.05, SD=8.00), the two-tailed Man-Whitney U test showed that these differences were not statistically significant (U=1282.5, Z=-1.929, p=0.054), although the result was marginal"
