`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Structural equation modeling on 238 households; model fit was acceptable (CFI = 0.95, TLI = 0.93, NFI = 0.93, SRMR = 0.08). Both direct paths were "significant" with the printed standardized coefficients β = 0.21 and β = 0.39; the 0.21 coefficient is described as moderately low.

> "the direct effects of the model showed that parent–child interaction using newspapers had a significant effect on child’s reading motivation (β = 0.21, p < 0.01), and child’s reading motivation had a significant effect on academic achievement (β = 0.39, p < 0.001)."
