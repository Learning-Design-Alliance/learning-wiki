## Evidence

### Dallı Öznur Erbay 2025

Dallı Öznur Erbay (2025). Spiritual Care Practices and Competence Among Critical Care Nurses in Türkiye: A Structural Equation Modeling Approach. Journal of Religion and Health, 64(5), 4074–4087. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-025-02353-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the cross-sectional survey of 323 CCNs (Table 2). Mean competence was "77.31 ± 12.46 points", with subscale means of 16.46 ± 3.50 (AISC), 40.06 ± 7.11 (PSCPC), and 20.63 ± 5.42 (ATPSC); the authors interpret this as moderate competence. No effect size is printed.

> "The mean total SCCS score among CCNs was 77.31 ± 12.46 points. Among its subscales, the mean scores were 16.46 ± 3.50 points for AISC, 40.06 ± 7.11 for PSCPC, and 20.63 ± 5.42 points for ATPSC."
