## Evidence

### Rotthoff Thomas 2014

Rotthoff Thomas, Ostapczuk Martin S., Kröncke Klaus D., Zimmerhofer Alexander, Decking Ulrich, Schneider Matthias, Ritz-Timme Stefanie. (2014). Criterion validity of a competency-based assessment center in medical education – a 4-year follow-up study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v19.25254

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of n=26 students completing the mandatory third-year OSCE of nine stations, mostly assessing practical medical skills. Neither the OSCE total score nor the history-taking station correlated significantly with any AC score; the authors interpret this as discriminant validity.

> "We found predominantly low and insignificant correlations between the AC and the OSCE on basic clinical skills (r=−0.33 to 0.30, all p's>0.05)."
