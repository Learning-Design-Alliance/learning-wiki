# Adolescents with ADHD respond more slowly than non-ADHD peers across all conditions, and color-shape binding slows responses in both groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The ADHD group showed slower reaction times than the non-ADHD group in all conditions. [→ Ortega 2020](#ortega-2020)
`q3 i3` The Color-Shape binding condition produced slower reaction times than the Shape-Only condition in both groups. [→ Ortega 2020 (2)](#ortega-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Ortega 2020

Ortega, R., López, V., Carrasco, X., Escobar, M. J., García, A. M., Parra, M. A. & Aboitiz, F. (2020). Neurocognitive mechanisms underlying working memory encoding and retrieval in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Scientific Reports 10:7771. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-64678-x

`q3 · i3`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of reaction times in the same 18-vs-18 adolescent sample: "the ADHD group showed slower responses than the non-ADHD group in all conditions", F(1,34) = 13.035, p = 0.00097, η2 = 0.28.

> "Regarding reaction times (RTs), the ADHD group showed slower responses than the non-ADHD group in all conditions (F(1,34) = 13.035, p = 0.00097, η2 = 0.28)."

### Ortega 2020 (2)

Ortega, R., López, V., Carrasco, X., Escobar, M. J., García, A. M., Parra, M. A. & Aboitiz, F. (2020). Neurocognitive mechanisms underlying working memory encoding and retrieval in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Scientific Reports 10:7771. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-64678-x

`q3 · i3`

Same RT ANOVA: a main effect of condition, with "The Color-shape condition showed slower RTs than the Only-Shape condition", F(1,34) = 6.2918, p = 0.01706, η2 = 0.16; no Group x Condition interaction was reported.

> "The Color-shape condition showed slower RTs than the Only-Shape condition (F(1,34) = 6.2918, p = 0.01706, η2 = 0.16). No other effects were observed."
