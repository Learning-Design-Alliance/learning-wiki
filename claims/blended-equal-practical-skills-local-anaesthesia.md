## Evidence

### Bock Anna 2021

Bock Anna, Kniha Kristian, Goloborodko Evgeny, Lemos Martin, Rittich Anne Barbara, Möhlhenrich Stephan Christian, Rafai Nicole, Hölzle Frank, Modabber Ali. (2021). Effectiveness of face-to-face, blended and e-learning in teaching the application of local anaesthesia: a randomised study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02569-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One week after the theory phase, students performed a block anaesthesia on a peer in small-group seminars, rated by a lecturer blinded to group on a 9-point scale per act (maximum 180 points). Means were 161 (lecture), 154 (e-learning), 152 (blended); "There were no significant differences between all three groups".

> "There were no significant differences between all three groups (p > 0.017). Also, the results of the subitems preparation, skill in block anaesthesia of the inferior alveolar nerve and block anaesthesia of the infraorbital nerve did not show significant differences (p > 0.017)."
