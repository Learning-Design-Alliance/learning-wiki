`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Secondary exploratory ANCOVA in the randomized trial including HEXACO openness and Technology Readiness Score as covariates; "no modifying gender effect on the training effect was found as tested by the interaction term gender*group (p = 0.996)". No effect sizes were printed.

> "The study did not reveal any differences between gender (p = 0.684), personality structure (p = 0.412) or technology readiness (p = 0.199) with respect to the primary outcome. Furthermore, no modifying gender effect on the training effect was found as tested by the interaction term gender*group (p = 0.996)."
