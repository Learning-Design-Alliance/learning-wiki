The review's synthesis of Table 3 modality data across the 30 included studies. It reports that "High-fidelity manikins were frequently utilized" and that "Virtual reality emerged as a prominent modality", alongside virtual patients, serious games, simulation games, and telesimulation.

> "High-fidelity manikins were frequently utilized for teaching paediatric emergencies [31, 32], respiratory diseases [33], therapeutics [50], and OSCE preparation [49]. Virtual reality emerged as a prominent modality for emergency response training [47], congenital heart disease education [22], clinical assessment of respiratory distress [23, 29], airway management [27], and user experience evaluation [24]."
