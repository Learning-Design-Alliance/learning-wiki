## Evidence

### Qian He 2025

Qian He, Jiahao Li, Ye Xiao, Jingjing Tian, Ningbo Mao, Sheng Lu, Yu Zhang, Yu Zhao. (2025). An immersive mixed reality surgical self-training system for precise pedicle screw placement: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07978-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 32 undergraduate medical students randomized 16 per group; Mann–Whitney U comparison of node-level completion rates in the unaided assessment session found the IMR group's "completion rate (median (IQR))" of 1.000 significantly higher than the control group's 0.88 (p < 0.01).

> "During the assessment session, the completion rate (median (IQR)) in the IMR group was 1.000 (1.0–1.0), significantly higher than 0.88 [0.80,1.00] in the control group (p < 0.01)"
