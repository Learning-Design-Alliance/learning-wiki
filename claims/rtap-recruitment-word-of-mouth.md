Qualitative interview study by EdTrust and TECA with 32 apprentices of color (19 Tennessee, 13 Texas); the recruitment theme reports that "Participants primarily learned of their program through word-of-mouth," usually after a school leader or close colleague encouraged them while already working at a school.
