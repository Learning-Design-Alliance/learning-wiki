## Evidence

### Walsh Jessica A. 2026

Walsh Jessica A., Dale Marita T., McKeough Zoe J., Alison Jennifer A., Dennis Sarah M. (2026). Changes in knowledge, skills, and confidence following a training program for primary care physiotherapists and exercise physiologists in pulmonary rehabilitation: a pre-post study. Primary Health Care Research & Development, 27, e74. https://doi.org/10.1017/S146342362610139X

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-arm pre-post study of 17 primary care clinicians (8 physiotherapists, 9 AEPs) completing the LFA online program and practical workshop. The abstract reports the knowledge gain with a large effect size: "Hedges' g = 1.52, 95% CI 0.81 to 2.21". No control group.

> "Median (IQR) knowledge scores increased from 7 (6,8) at baseline to 10 (9,11) post-training (p < 0.001; Hedges' g = 1.52, 95% CI 0.81 to 2.21)."
