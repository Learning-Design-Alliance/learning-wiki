q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

The WWC's review of supplemental findings reports that the strongest study's positive full-sample literacy effect did not hold for the English learner subsample, and similar insignificant findings appeared for English learners on science and mathematics. These supplemental findings do not factor into the effectiveness rating.

"While Steele et al., (2017) found positive effects of dual language programs on literacy achievement for the full student sample, the study found insignificant findings on literacy achievement for English learners."
