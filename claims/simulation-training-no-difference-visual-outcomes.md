## Evidence

### Dean William H. 2021

Dean William H., Gichuhi Stephen, Buchan John C., Makupa William, Mukome Agrippa, Otiti-Sengeri Juliet, Arunga Simon, Mukherjee Subhashis, Kim Min J., Harrison-Williams Lloyd, MacLeod David, Cook Colin, Burton Matthew J. (2021). Intense Simulation-Based Surgical Education for Manual Small-Incision Cataract Surgery: The Ophthalmic Learning and Improvement Initiative in Cataract Surgery Randomized Clinical Trial in Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, and Zimbabwe. JAMA Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaophthalmol.2020.4718

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Patient-specific visual acuity outcomes for all procedures during the 12-month period, analyzed using the Wilcoxon rank-sum test. Good outcomes were 36.8% (138 of 375) intervention vs 25.6% (30 of 117) control; poor outcomes 10.1% vs 12.8%. Equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in the proportion of good or poor outcomes between groups (Wilcoxon rank-sum P = .90 for the intervention group and P = .95 for the control group)."
