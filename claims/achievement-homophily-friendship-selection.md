## Evidence

### Qin Xingna 2026

Qin Xingna, Li Simeng, Sjögren Björn, Ren Ping, Thornberg Robert. (2026). The Role of Parental Influences in Chinese Adolescents’ Academic Achievement through Shaping Friendship Network Dynamics. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-025-02306-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RSiena Model 1 selection effects across 20 converged classroom networks. The article reports achievement alter β = 0.04, achievement ego β = −0.03 (both p < .001), and achievement ego × alter β = 0.04 (p < .001), indicating homophilous selection.

> "the achievement alter effect was positively significant (β = 0.04, p < .001), indicating that higher-achieving adolescents received more friendship nominations. In contrast, the achievement ego was negatively significant (β = − 0.03, p < .001)"
