Observational megacode checklist assessment (19 items from 6 NRP lessons, maximum 38) scored by an NRP-certified attending neonatologist for 21 senior residents; 18 passed the advanced standard megacode on the first session and three passed after a booster session. Table 1 reports per-task percentages, e.g. 100% correct team briefing question order.
