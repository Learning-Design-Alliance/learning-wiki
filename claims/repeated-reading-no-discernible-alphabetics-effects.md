## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2014

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2014). Repeated Reading: WWC Intervention Report, Students with Learning Disabilities. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/576

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

WWC review of the Wexler et al. (2010) randomized controlled trial, the only study reporting alphabetics outcomes, using the Letter-Word Identification subtest of the WJ III Tests of Achievement with 62 students in 11 classrooms. The WWC-calculated effect size was "not large enough to be considered substantively important," yielding a rating of no discernible effects.

> "Wexler et al. (2010) found no statistically significant effects of repeated reading on one measure of alphabetics: the Letter-Word Identification subtest of the WJ III Tests of Achievement."
