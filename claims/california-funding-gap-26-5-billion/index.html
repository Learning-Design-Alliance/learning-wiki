Second-hand estimate: the report cites a GDTFII study (J. Levin et al., 2018) finding California "would need to spend about $4,000 more per pupil—an additional $26.5 billion annually." The report notes the $26.5 billion figure includes recommended preK investments. The underlying study's method is not described.
