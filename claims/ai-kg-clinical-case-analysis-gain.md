Score-composition analysis within the final examination of the cluster-randomized trial. The experimental group outperformed the control group by "33.8%" on subjective analysis questions but "only increased by 15.2%" on objective memory questions.

> "the average score of the experimental group in subjective analysis questions (e.g., analyzing the pathological changes and clinical significance of a patient’s liver puncture specimen ) reached (28.5 ± 3.2) points (full score: 40 points), 33.8% higher than that of the control group (21.3 ± 4.1) points; while the objective memory questions (e.g., types of necrosis ) only increased by 15.2%"
