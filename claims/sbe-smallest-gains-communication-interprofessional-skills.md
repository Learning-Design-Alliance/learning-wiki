## Evidence

### Hough J 2019

Hough J, Levan D, Steele M, Kelly K, Dalton M. (2019). Simulation-based education improves student self-efficacy in physiotherapy assessment and management of paediatric patients. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1894-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive item-level comparison in the combined cohort; communication showed "the lowest mean improvement overall (0.77)". Improvements were still statistically significant, and 64.9% of students scored 3 or higher post-SBE on communication items.

> "question 4 (maintaining communication with nurse, carer and child) had the lowest mean improvement in 2 out of the 3 scenarios (musculoskeletal and cardiorespiratory) as well as the lowest mean improvement overall (0.77)"
