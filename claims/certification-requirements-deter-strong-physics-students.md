Samina S. Masood. (2014). K-12 Teaching and Physics Enrollment. Department of Physics, University of Houston Clear Lake. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.5501](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.5501)

The author's observation about teacher certification programs, stated without supporting data. The article reports that entry requirements match school minimums and only passing grades in any subject are needed, so students with good grades pursue other careers and teaching becomes an alternative for those who cannot.

> "in most of the teacher's certification programs the minimum requirement to get into the program is the same as the minimum requirement of getting in the school . Afterwards it just requires passing grades (C or above) in any subject."
