`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Programme participation counts reported in Table 2 and running text, alongside a comparison with other programmes (Higgs Hunters IRIS: 14 students/5 schools; ORBYTS: 17 schools; HiSPARC: 140 schools across 13 universities, ~11 per institution). An academic staff member acting as project lead and researcher can support 4 schools (~50 students) in about 8 hours over 6 months.
