## Evidence

### Kawabata Masato 2021

Kawabata Masato, Lee Kerry, Choo Hui-Cheng, Burns Stephen F. (2021). Breakfast and Exercise Improve Academic and Cognitive Performance in Adolescents. Nutrients 13(4). https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13041278

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subjective ratings in the randomized trial: B-E feeling improved versus all groups mid-morning, mental effort dropped versus F-E (β13 = 16.63, p = 0.042), while motivation and arousal were similar across groups.

> "feeling (good/pleasure) improved in B-E from the start to mid-morning compared with the other three groups (B-S: β12 = −0.59, p = 0.040; F-S: β11 = −0.59, p = 0.038; F-E: β13 = −0.64, p = 0.036) but were again similar until morning end (all p > 0.05)."
