What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Twelve Together. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · review · r?

The WWC reviewed two studies of Twelve Together. The Dynarski et al. (1998) RCT met standards with reservations; survey response rates were 92% for the intervention group and 86% for the control, exceeding the 5% differential attrition threshold. The other study (Orr, 1987) failed screens for baseline equivalence.

"One study (Dynarski, Gleason, Rangarajan, & Wood, 1998) was a randomized controlled trial that met WWC evidence- standards with reservations because of differential attrition between the intervention and control groups."
