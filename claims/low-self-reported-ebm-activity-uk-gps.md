Galbraith Kevin, Ward Alison, Heneghan Carl. (2017). A real-world approach to Evidence-Based Medicine in general practice: a competency framework derived from a systematic review and Delphi process. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5415750/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 302 UK GPs reported within the systematic review. Self-reported frequent engagement with EBM steps was low: "39% claimed to identify gaps in knowledge frequently; 28% to frequently track down evidence; and only 8% to frequently appraise literature critically." The authors note self-report may be susceptible to response bias.
