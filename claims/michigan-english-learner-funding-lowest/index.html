Evidence¶

The Education Trust–Midwest 2021¶

The Education Trust–Midwest. (2021). Engine of Inequality: Michigan's Education System. https://midwest.edtrust.org/advocacy/

q2 · i? · review · r1

EdBuild data cited in the report show Michigan's English Learner allocations are "the lowest in the country among states that apply a weight or multiplier." The report states research recommends English Learners receive at least twice as much funding as native English speakers for language acquisition supports.

"Michigan allocates between about 1% and 11% more funding for English Learners (depending on their English language proficiency levels) than for students with English as their native language."
