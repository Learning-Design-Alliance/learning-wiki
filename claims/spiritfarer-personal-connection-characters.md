`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Findings from the LED thematic analysis of player responses; the article identifies two main factors in emotional bonds, including resemblance to real-life individuals, with participants quoting examples such as seeing their grandma in the spirits Summer, Alice, and Beverly.
