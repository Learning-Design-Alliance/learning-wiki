## Evidence

### Ulrich Trautwein 2026

Ulrich Trautwein, Janina Eberhart, Charlotte Dignath, Cavan V. Bonner, Brent W. Roberts. (2026). Self-Regulation Competence as a Superpower: An Integrated Framework for K-12. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10190-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional observational study of 313 college students administering nine MSLQ subscales and six Chernyshenko Conscientiousness Scales facets; the canonical model was significant (Wilk's 𝜆 = 0.426, F(54, 1534.3) = 5.17, p < .001) and "57% of the variance is shared".

> "The full model across all six functions was statistically significant, Wilk's 𝜆 = 0.426, F(54, 1534.3) = 5.17, p < .001. The full model effect size was r2 = .57, which indicates that 57% of the variance is shared between the CSS and MSLQ variable sets."
