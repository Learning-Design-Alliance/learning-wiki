### Cho Daniel 2017

Cho Daniel, Cosimini Michael, Espinoza Juan. (2017). Podcasting in medical education: a review of the literature. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2017.69

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Vasilopoulos et al., a moderator effect of prior podcast experience on gains after an educational podcast module; Table 3 notes the podcast group had a small but significant score increase (mean change 6.21 vs. 5.08).

> "One study detailed that experienced educational podcast users (defined as having used four or more educational podcasts previously) benefited more from a new podcast intervention when compared to those with less podcast experience [35]."
