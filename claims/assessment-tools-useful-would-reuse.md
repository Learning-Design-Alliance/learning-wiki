## Evidence

### García de Diego Laura 2015

García de Diego Laura, Cuervo Marta, Martínez J. Alfredo. (2015). Development of a Learning-Oriented Computer Assisted Instruction Designed to Improve Skills in the Clinical Assessment of the Nutritional Status: A Pilot Evaluation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0126345

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Second-phase questionnaire data from 30 participants after a ~30-minute simulation of a nutritional study using the CAI. The authors suggest that performing a simulation of a clinical case with the software teaches users to manage nutritional assessment tools; the remaining 10% would not use the tools because they do not fit their work field.

> "46.7% of the survey respondents had never used them, but 100% considered them useful for the nutritional assessment of the patient. At the end of the second session 90% of participants stated that they would use these tools again"
