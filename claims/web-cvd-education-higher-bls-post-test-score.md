## Evidence

### Bylow Helene 2019

Bylow Helene, Karlsson Thomas, Lepp Margret, Claesson Andreas, Lindqvist Jonny, Herlitz Johan. (2019). Effectiveness of web-based education in addition to basic life support learning activities: A cluster randomised controlled trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0219341

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section of the cluster randomised controlled trial of laymen from Swedish workplaces, comparing the BLS+WEB and BLS groups on the modified Cardiff Test total score at post-test and retention test. The article reports the difference as "statistically significant" in favour of BLS+WEB at both occasions; Table 2 prints post-test means of 59.6 vs 58.7 (p = 0.004) and retention means of 58.8 vs 58.0 (p = 0.03), with no effect size reported.

> "Notably, both directly and six months after training, there was a statistically significant difference in favour of the BLS+WEB group, in addition to the BLS training."
