## Evidence

### Michlmayr N. 2025

Michlmayr N., Mitofsky J., Haverkamp N., Wittek A., Plöger R., Walter A., Strizek B., Recker F. (2025). Design and implementation of a microlearning-based OSCE preparatory curriculum in obstetrics: a pilot study. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-025-08157-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot evaluation of a beta app version with a cohort of 13 students using an anonymous online questionnaire with 5-point Likert items and one open-ended question, analyzed descriptively. The article reports "predominantly positive" feedback with no effect sizes or test statistics.

> "The feedback obtained from the pilot cohort following the beta testing phase of the app was predominantly positive. Students reported that the app served as a valuable complement to the obstetrics lecture series and supported their preparation for examinations."
