Lea Waters 2015¶

Lea Waters, Adam Barsky, Amanda Ridd, Kelly Allen. (2015). Contemplative Education: A Systematic, Evidence-Based Review of the effect of Meditation Interventions in Schools. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-014-9258-2

q2 · i3 · review · r3

The review reports Mendelson et al.'s (2010) RCT (intervention=51, control=46) of 12 weeks of mindfulness. Table 3 prints involuntary engagement d=.83, rumination d=.70, intrusive thoughts d=.51, emotional arousal d=.64 (dIGPP, SDpost).

"Significant improvements in self-reported involuntary responses to stress were found in the meditation group compared to controls (as assessed by the RSQ). Specifically, significant differences were found on rumination, intrusive thoughts and emotional arousal subscales"

Lea Waters 2015 (2)¶

Lea Waters, Adam Barsky, Amanda Ridd, Kelly Allen. (2015). Contemplative Education: A Systematic, Evidence-Based Review of the effect of Meditation Interventions in Schools. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-014-9258-2

q2 · i2 · review · r2

The review reports Schonert-Reichl and Lawlor's (2010) non-random experiment trial with teacher-rated TRSC outcomes. Table 3 prints aggressive behaviour d=.52 and oppositional behaviour d=.43 (dIG, SDpost).

"Significant teacher-reported improvements in aggressive and oppositional behaviour/ dysregulation in intervention group compared to controls"
