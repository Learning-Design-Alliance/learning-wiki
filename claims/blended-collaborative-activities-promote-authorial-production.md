## Evidence

### Beraldo et al. 2022

Rossana Beraldo, Silviane Barbato, M. Beatrice Ligorio. (2022). Dialogic approach to the analysis of the meaning-making process in a blended setting. Dialogic Pedagogy: An International Online Journal. https://doi.org/10.5195/dpj.2022.499

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case study of one dyad (Agnes and Deca, two female students aged 17 and 18) in a Brazilian third-year state secondary school, analysed within a larger set of 14 dyads. The article reports that "the paired and grouped collaborative activities in blended learning promoted authorial production" and that the dyad achieved reflective meta-analysis through comparison with colleagues' perspectives.

> "Altogether, the paired and grouped collaborative activities in blended learning promoted authorial production. Our dyad achieved reflective meta-analysis when they compared their viewpoints with the perspective of their colleagues by using justifications and explanations grounded in their production, generating reflexivity and agency in dialogue."
