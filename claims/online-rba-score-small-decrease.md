## Subclaims
`q2 i1` First-quarter students scored lower online (8.4 ± 3.2, N = 397) than in person (9.3 ± 3.2, N = 326) on the 17 common items, a difference the authors call fairly small with Cohen's d ≈ 0.3. [→ Alexis Olsho 2020](#alexis-olsho-2020)

## Evidence

### Alexis Olsho 2020

Alexis Olsho, Suzanne White Brahmia, Charlotte Zimmerman, Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, Andrew Boudreaux. (2020). Online administration of a reasoning inventory in development. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.olsho](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.olsho)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of average scores on 17 items common to both versions, limited to C(I) students seeing the instrument for the first time. The article reports "a percent difference of about 10%" and "Cohen’s d ≈ 0.3".

> "Average overall score and standard deviation on the subset of 17 items for Online was 8.4 ± 3.2 (N = 397 ); In-person, it was 9.3 ± 3.2 (N = 326 ), a percent difference of about 10%. While this difference is slightly larger than expected from past quarters’ data, the effect is fairly small, with Cohen’s d ≈ 0.3."
