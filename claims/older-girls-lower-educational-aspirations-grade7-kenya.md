## Evidence

### Kangwana Beth 2020

Kangwana Beth, Muthengi Eunice, Austrian Karen. (2020). Intra-grade variability in educational and psychosocial competencies of school going adolescent girls, in the coastal region of Kenya: implications for school based interventions. BMC Public Health 20:1097. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-020-09097-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate GEE analysis of baseline survey data from 3489 grade 7 girls in 140 public primary schools in Kilifi County, Kenya, comparing age groups. The adjusted analysis reported OR 0.63 (95%CI 0.53, 0.74) for "ambitions of furthering their education beyond secondary school".

> "those in the higher age group were less likely to have ambitions of furthering their education beyond secondary school compared to the younger group (odds ratio (OR) 0.59 (95% Confidence Intervals (CI): 0.50, 0.69))"
