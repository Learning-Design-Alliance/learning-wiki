## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jordan Talan 2025

Jordan Talan, MHPE, MD; Forster Molly, MD; Joseph Leian, MD; Pradhan Deepak, MHPE, MD. (2025). Exploring the Role of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation in Health Professions Education: Thematic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/62803

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 16 semistructured interviews with VR early adopters; Theme 1 reports that participants “focused on VR simulation for the development of cognitive skills,” with psychomotor skills limited to procedural knowledge.

> "Study participants focused on VR simulation for the development of cognitive skills, including communication, teamwork, clinical reasoning, situational awareness, and interdisciplinary skills, occasionally referred to as “soft skills.”"

### Jordan Talan 2025 (2)

Jordan Talan, MHPE, MD; Forster Molly, MD; Joseph Leian, MD; Pradhan Deepak, MHPE, MD. (2025). Exploring the Role of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation in Health Professions Education: Thematic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/62803

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 1 of the interview study reports that haptic technology was the unanimous barrier to teaching psychomotor skills; participants turned to mixed reality or mannequin simulation for those skills, sometimes offering a blended experience.

> "The most common barrier to teaching psychomotor skills in the VR environment was haptic technology, noted unanimously by every interview participant."
