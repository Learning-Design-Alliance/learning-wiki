## Evidence

### Son Daisuke 2018

Son Daisuke, Shimizu Ikuo, Ishikawa Hirono, Aomatsu Muneyoshi, Leppink Jimmie. (2018). Communication skills training and the conceptual structure of empathy among medical students. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0431-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative descriptive analysis of one open-ended question administered to the student cohort after the OSCE, coded by the first author and reviewed by three co-authors to consensus. Students reported acquiring cognitive interviewing skills while struggling to empathize emotionally.

> "Recurring difficulties were the 'difficulty of empathizing with patients' feelings' and the 'difficulty of feeling compassion despite showing empathic behaviour'."
