Pearson's chi-square tests of independence between WASSCE core subject grades and last-semester GPA (ordinal categories). Effect sizes were small (Cramer's V ranging from 0.12 to 0.15). The article notes a substantial proportion of cells had expected counts below five.

> "Overall, no statistically significant associations were observed between last-semester GPA and grades in Core Mathematics (χ²(20) = 18.90, p = .528), Integrated Science (χ²(20) = 23.29, p = .275), or Social Studies (χ²(28) = 32.57, p = .252)."
