Evidence¶

Ibrahim 2018¶

Mahmoud Ibrahim. (2018). Explicit Versus Implicit Modes of EFL Reading Literacy Instruction: Using Phonological Awareness With Adult Arab Learners. English Language Teaching, 11(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n9p144

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-sample t-test on the treatment group (n=36 completers) in a quasi-experimental study at the University College of Umluj; means rose from 4.33 (SD 3.59) to 42.64 (SD 17.06), t(35)=13.911, p=0.001, "in favor of the Posttest".

"Table 3 illustrates that t value was 13.9 with a significant p-value of 0.001 (which is less than 0.05). in other words, there is a significant difference between the mean score of the Posttest and the mean of the Pretest in favor of the Posttest."
