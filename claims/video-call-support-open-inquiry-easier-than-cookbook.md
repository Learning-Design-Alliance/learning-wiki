# Home-based Arduino experiments with video-call support were found easier for supporting open inquiry than structured inquiry would have been, though some support types were less efficient remotely

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The instructors found supporting open inquiries over video call easier than guaranteeing correct pre-defined steps of cookbook inquiries, and code troubleshooting easier without crowding around one laptop. [→ Bradbury 2020](#bradbury-2020)
`q1 i?` Some instructor support types, such as troubleshooting electrical wiring or conveying ideas through a quick sketch, were less efficient via video call. [→ Bradbury 2020 (2)](#bradbury-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Bradbury 2020

Bradbury, F. R., & Pols, C. F. J. (2020). A Pandemic-Resilient Open-Inquiry Physical Science Lab Course Which Leverages the Maker Movement. Electronic Journal for Research in Science & Mathematics Education, 24(3), 60-67. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.06881](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.06881)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructors' qualitative impressions from conducting individual team meetings over video call during the pandemic transition. They also report that code snippets could be written on their own keyboards and shared via the video call's chat interface. No measures are printed.

> "Supporting open inquiries was thus found to be easier than attempting over video call to guarantee that students were correctly taking all the pre-defined steps and reaching the end-goals of more structured or “cookbook” style inquiries."

### Bradbury 2020 (2)

Bradbury, F. R., & Pols, C. F. J. (2020). A Pandemic-Resilient Open-Inquiry Physical Science Lab Course Which Leverages the Maker Movement. Electronic Journal for Research in Science & Mathematics Education, 24(3), 60-67. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.06881](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.06881)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same instructors' counterbalancing observation about remote support limits, naming wiring troubleshooting and quick sketches as less efficient by video. This qualifies the positive impressions and is an unquantified interpretation.

> "On the other hand, some types of instructor support were definitely less efficient via video call (e.g. helping to trouble-shoot a problem with electrical wiring or conveying ideas through a quick sketch)."
