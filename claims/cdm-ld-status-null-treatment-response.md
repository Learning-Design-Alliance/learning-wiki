# C/DM LD status likewise does not predict posttest reading outcomes beyond pretest performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` C/DM LD status was not a significant predictor of posttest decoding, fluency, or comprehension, with eta squared statistics of .00 to .002. [→ Miciak Jeremy 2016](#miciak-jeremy-2016)

## Evidence

### Miciak Jeremy 2016

Miciak Jeremy, Williams Jacob L., Taylor W. Pat, Cirino Paul T., Fletcher Jack M., Vaughn Sharon. (2016). Do Processing Patterns of Strengths and Weaknesses Predict Differential Treatment Response? Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(6), 898-909. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000096

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVAs parallel to the XBA analyses using C/DM LD status as predictor for the same 206 treatment-group fourth graders. Pretest eta squared ranged .52-.69; C/DM status added no significant prediction on any outcome.

> "C/DM LD status was not a significant predictor of posttest performance in decoding, F (1, 205) = .96, p > .05, η2 = .00, reading fluency, F (1, 205) = .24, p > .05, η2 = .00, or reading comprehension, F (1, 205) = .47, p > .05, η2 = .00."
