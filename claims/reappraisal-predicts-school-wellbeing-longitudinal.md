## Evidence

### Beaumont 2022

Beaumont, J., Putwain, D. W., Gallard, D., Malone, E., Marsh, H. W., & Pekrun, R. (2022, in press). Students' emotion regulation and school-related wellbeing: Longitudinal models juxtaposing between- and within-person perspectives. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://osf.io/W5CPE

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Three-wave longitudinal SEM (CLPM) of 2,365 students in four English secondary schools, controlling gender, age, and FSM, with the lag 2 autoregressive model showing good fit (CFI = .969, RMSEA = .016). The article reports "reappraisal was a positive predictor of school-related wellbeing". No standardized path coefficient for this path is printed in the provided text, so no effect size is reported here.

> "As shown in Figure 2, reappraisal was a positive predictor of school-related wellbeing, and wellbeing was a positive predictor of reappraisal."
