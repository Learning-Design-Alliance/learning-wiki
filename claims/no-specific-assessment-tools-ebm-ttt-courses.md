`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review searching PubMed, BioMed, EmBase, Cochrane and Eric from database inception to November 2008, with independent study selection and data extraction by two reviewers. The review reports that "There were no specific assessment tools for evaluating effectiveness of EBM TTT courses" among the five included papers.

> "Of 1230 potentially relevant studies, five papers met the selection criteria. There were no specific assessment tools for evaluating effectiveness of EBM TTT courses."
