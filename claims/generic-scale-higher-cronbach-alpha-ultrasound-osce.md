## Evidence

### Schneider Simon 2026

Schneider Simon, Stricker Daniel, Berendonk Christoph, Baron-Stefaniak Joanna, Walter Robin. (2026). Abdominal ultrasound performance assessment: a comparison of generic and extended OSCE rating scales. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08994-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Single-centre cohort study in which 80 medical students' OSCE stations were rated in parallel with both scales; the generic scale's mean Cronbach's α of 0.803 exceeded the extended scale's 0.699, a difference the authors report as significant (p = 0.011).

> "Our results indicate that internal consistency was higher for the generic scale than for the extended rating scale (Cronbach's α 0.803 vs. 0.699; p = 0.011, Table 1)."

### Schneider Simon 2026 (2)

Schneider Simon, Stricker Daniel, Berendonk Christoph, Baron-Stefaniak Joanna, Walter Robin. (2026). Abdominal ultrasound performance assessment: a comparison of generic and extended OSCE rating scales. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08994-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-station analysis across the OSCE stations showed the extended scale's Cronbach's α ranging from "0.50 to 0.83" while the generic scale's ranged 0.74–0.87, indicating the generic scale was more consistent at every station.

> "Across stations, Cronbach's α ranged from 0.50 to 0.83 for the extended and 0.74–0.87 for the generic scale (Appendix 4)."
