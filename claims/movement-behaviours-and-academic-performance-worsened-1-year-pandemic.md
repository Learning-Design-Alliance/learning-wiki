(2022). 24-h movement behaviours in Spanish youth before and after 1-year into the covid-19 pandemic and its relationship to academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-21096-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Descriptive comparison (Student t-tests and Chi-squared tests) of the two subsamples on movement behaviours and academic performance. The article reports that adolescents before the pandemic showed "higher physical activity levels, sleep duration, and higher academic performance, as well as lower recreational screen time" (all p < 0.05); no effect sizes are printed.

> "Overall, adolescents evaluated before Covid-19 pandemic reported higher physical activity levels, sleep duration, and higher academic performance, as well as lower recreational screen time, compared to adolescents assessed after 1-year into the Covid-19 pandemic (all, p < 0.05)."
