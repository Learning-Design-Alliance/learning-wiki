## Evidence

### G. Peter Lepage 2021

G. Peter Lepage. (2021). Active Learning in a Graduate Quantum Field Theory Course. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/10.0002349](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0002349)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Instructor-reported outcomes across four consecutive years of teaching the course. Evaluations rose from 3.7/5 to 5/5; attendance was nearly perfect (95%) despite the 8:30 am start time, which the author partly attributes to participation credit. No statistical analysis is reported.

> "End-of-semester student evaluations of the course were mediocre (3.7/5) in the first year, but improved substantially as the course evolved, until they were as positive as they could be (5/5) in the last year."
