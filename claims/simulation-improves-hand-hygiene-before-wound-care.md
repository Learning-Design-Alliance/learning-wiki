Borges Francisca Edinária de Sousa; Pereira Francisco Gilberto Fernandes; de Aguiar Maria Isis Freire; Fernandes Bruna Karen Cavalcante; Machado Ana Larissa Gomes; Beserra Eveline Pinheiro; Caetano Joselany Áfio. (2025). Nursing students' skills in surgical wound care improve after clinical simulation: a quasi-experiment. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0171

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental before-and-after study with 50 nursing students; skills observed with a validated checklist pre and post simulation and analyzed by McNemar's test at 5% with Cohen's D. Hand hygiene reached p<0.001 with effect size 0.71 (pre 40.0% vs post 90.0% in Table 1), a "significant differences" result.

> "Significant differences were obtained in all items assessed, from hand hygiene (<0.001), procedure guidance (0.016), all domains of clinical anamnesis, such as patient identification (0.016)"
