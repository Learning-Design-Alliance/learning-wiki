## Evidence

### Chen J 2026

Chen J, Cui J, Li X, Yang J, Yang X, Zhong H, Wang F, Gu Q, Duan C. (2026). Application of a SPOC-based blended teaching system in full-cycle training for medical imaging interns: A randomized controlled educational intervention study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000049908

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Self-report questionnaire completed by the same participants at the immediate post-intervention time point in the randomized study. The "unadjusted mean difference of 91.87 points" favored the experimental group; Table 3 reports r = 0.859 for the total score, and the authors note the experimental mean represented approximately 93.9% of the maximum possible scale score, indicating a potential ceiling effect.

> "The mean self-reported self-directed learning ability score was 286.37 ± 4.90 in the experimental group and 194.50 ± 34.65 in the control group, corresponding to an unadjusted mean difference of 91.87 points (95% CI, 78.82–104.91; P < .001)"
