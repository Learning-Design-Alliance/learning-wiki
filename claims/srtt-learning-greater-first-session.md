## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Z-scores were significantly greater (more negative) before than after the intervention period, with a time main effect of η2 = 0.262. [→ McGlashan H.L. 2017](#mcglashan-hl-2017)

## Evidence

### McGlashan H.L. 2017

McGlashan H.L., Blanchard C.C.V., Sycamore N.J., Lee R., French B., Holmes N.P. (2017). Improvement in children's fine motor skills following a computerized typing intervention. Human Movement Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.humov.2017.10.013

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA on tapping-task Z-scores for 21 children showed a main effect of time, F(1,20) = 7.11, p = .015, η2 = 0.262, and of condition, F(1,20) = 13.0, p = .002, η2 = 0.395. The article reports "significant main effects of time" with no group interaction.

> "Z-scores were significantly greater (more negative) before (−0.145 ± 0.049) than after (0.025 ± 0.038) the intervention period, and were significantly greater for sequence (−0.185 ± 0.047) compared to random (0.066 ± 0.045) conditions."
