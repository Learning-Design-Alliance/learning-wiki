## Evidence

### Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S. 2025

Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S., Ju William, Alamri Yassar, Wilkinson Tim J. (2025). Association of completing an intercalated year-long research degree with post-graduation academic success. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07133-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Matched-cohort analysis of higher-degree completion (Masters and above) for 94 cases vs 94 controls via conditional logistic regression. Cases "significantly more likely to obtain higher academic degrees", OR 3.2, p = 0.023.

> "BMedSc(Hons) graduates were significantly more likely to obtain higher academic degrees following graduation compared with the controls (18.1% (n = 17) vs. 6.4% (n = 6), OR 3.2, 95% CI 1.17–8.74, p = 0.023)"

### Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S. 2025 (2)

Al-Busaidi Ibrahim S., Ju William, Alamri Yassar, Wilkinson Tim J. (2025). Association of completing an intercalated year-long research degree with post-graduation academic success. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07133-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PhD-or-equivalent completion within the same matched cohort: 13 cases (13.8%) vs 1 control (1.1%), OR 13, p = 0.013. The wide confidence interval reflects the small number of PhD completions.

> "including PhD degree (13.8% vs. 1.1%, OR 13, 95% CI 1.70–99.38, p = 0.013)"
