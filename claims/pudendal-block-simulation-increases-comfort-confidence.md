## Evidence

### Thiyagarajan D 2025

Thiyagarajan D, Wheatley C, Salva CR, Lerner V, Saab SS. (2025). Development and validation of a pudendal nerve block simulation‐based educational intervention. Int J Gynecol Obstet. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.70012

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post intervention study at four academic institutions with 56 enrolled subjects (37 residents, 19 practicing clinicians) analyzed with paired t-tests; the article reports the increases were significant with "P value for all <0.001". Table 3 shows, for example, residents' confidence identifying candidates rising from 1.30 to 3.19.

> "Comfort and confidence with identifying appropriate candidates, discussing benefits and risks; offering and performing; and identifying and managing complications of the procedure all increased with the simulation intervention for both residents and practicing clinicians (Table 3)."
