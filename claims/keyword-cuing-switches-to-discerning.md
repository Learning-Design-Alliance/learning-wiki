Interview observation of five students who defaulted to class keywords when questions used familiar terminology; one called the initial questions "just plug and chug stuff" and later noted they "didn't think about [doing] that" as questions became unfamiliar and their thinking became more discerning.

> "All students who were unfamiliar with the material later in the interview exhibited the switch from cuing to discerning behavior, further discussed in Section V."
