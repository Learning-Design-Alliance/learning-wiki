## Evidence

### Richard Océane 2023

Richard Océane, Jollant Fabrice, Billon Grégoire, Attoe Chris, Vodovar Dominique, Piot Marie-Aude. (2023). Simulation training in suicide risk assessment and intervention: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2023.2199469

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Narrative analysis of the review's non-RCT and PPT studies. For attitudes, "42 out of 48 PPT studies reporting statistical significance" improved; the review notes natural learning effects cannot be excluded in single-group pre/post designs.

> "In addition to RCTs, 10 out of 13 non-RCTs (including attitude toward suicidal patients, self-confidence, and self-efficacy) and 42 out of 48 PPT studies reporting statistical significance on attitudes provide further support on the effectiveness of simulation-based education, even if we cannot exclude natural learning effect for PPTs."
