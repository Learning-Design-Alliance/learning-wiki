## Evidence

### Yifan Liu 2026

Yifan Liu, Jaime Arguello, Orland Hoeber, Chang Liu, Soo Young Rieh, Luanne Sinnamon, et al. (2026). Report on CHIIR 2026 Workshop on Generative AI and Academic Search (GAI&AS). ACM SIGIR Forum. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08936

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A within-subjects user study in a social science academic search engine compared interfaces with and without a single SERP-level summary synthesized from the five top-ranked results, combined with an accuracy assessment across expert-sourced queries. The report states "the overall effects were mixed"; no effect size is printed.

> "Results suggested that summaries may reduce mental demand and frustration for some users, while participants descriptively performed slightly fewer clicks and query reformulations. At the same time, the overall effects were mixed."
