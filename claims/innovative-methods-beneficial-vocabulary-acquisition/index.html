Related Claims¶

  • Digital game-based learning supports long-term retention of second-language vocabulary — related
  • The authors propose, without testing it here, that competitive environments may provide clear goals, structures, and rewards that foster engagement, particularly for students less familiar with such environments — related
  • Creative drama method has a very large positive effect on Social Studies course achievement in favor of experimental groups (random-effects d = 1.171) — related
  • Creative drama-based learning has a large positive effect on academic success in Turkish language instruction (REM ES = 1.712) — related
  • The very large effect found here is consistent with prior meta-analyses of creative drama, which report medium-to-huge positive effects on achievement — related
  • Practice defined as meaningful language use plus effortful practice of difficult features is beneficial and even essential for second language acquisition — related
