## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental group's higher mean score (8.62 vs 7.31) was not statistically significant; the two-sample t-test (t = 1.901, df = 24, Sig. 2-tailed = 0.069) did not exceed the tabled critical value, so the difference could not be attributed to the new method.
