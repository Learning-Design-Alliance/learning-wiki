# Pretest analysis showed the control and treatment groups of 7th graders were parallel in previous algebra knowledge

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The difference between control and treatment groups' pretest mean scores was not statistically significant, so the intact groups were parallel in previous knowledge. [→ Bhutto Muhammad Ilyas 2013](#bhutto-muhammad-ilyas-2013)

## Evidence

### Bhutto Muhammad Ilyas 2013

Bhutto Muhammad Ilyas, Khalid Jamil Rawat, Muhammad Tariq Bhatti, & Najeeb Malik. (2013). Effect of Teaching of Algebra through Social Constructivist Approach on 7th Graders' Learning Outcomes in Sindh (Pakistan). International Journal of Instruction, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085370

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest t-test analysis of the two intact grade-7 sections (control N 26, treatment N 28) using GraphPad QuickCalcs. The two-tailed p-value was 0.3319 with observed t-value 0.9896 at df 25, a "not statistically significant result"; means were 2.35 (control) and 2.93 (treatment).

> "The t-test analysis of pretest scores of both groups revealed that the difference of mean scores was not statistically significant thus the null hypothesis could not be rejected (but accepted)."
