## Subclaims
`q3 i1` On the difficult far-transfer construction task, the Label group was more likely than the Control group to draw a diagonal brace, while HA and LA groups performed similarly. [→ Zheng 2025](#zheng-2025)

## Evidence

### Zheng 2025

Zheng, Y. S., Goldwater, M., & Gentner, D. (2025). Structural Alignment and Linguistic Contrast Help Children Learn a Key Principle of Spatial Construction. Cognitive Science, 49(12), e70149. https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.70149

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 Day 2 far-transfer construction task on a bunkbed picture; the language effect was significant (odds ratio = 1.60, p = .027) but alignment was not (odds ratio = 1.36, p = .15). Overall performance was low, as the article expected.

> "The logistic regression revealed a significant effect of language: the Label group was more likely to draw a brace than was the Control group ( M Label = 0.40, SD Label = 0.49; M Control = 0.21, SD Control = 0.41)."
