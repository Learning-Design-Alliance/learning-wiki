## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski (2016). Developing skills vs reinforcing concepts in physics labs: Insight from E-CLASS. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Raw pre/post overall E-CLASS scores (Table III, Fig. 1) for the aggregate data set (N = 4915) split by course level; the skills-focused shift was "positive" and statistically significant, while the FY concepts-focused shift was negative. FY both-focused shift was d = −0.2.

> "In both the FY and BFY courses, skills-focused courses showed a small (d = 0 .1) but statistically signiﬁcant (p ≤ 0.01) positive shift in overall score. Concepts-focused courses, on the other hand, showed no shift in BFY courses, and a moderately sized (d = −0.4) and statistically signiﬁ- cant (p ≪ 0.01) negative shift in FY courses."
