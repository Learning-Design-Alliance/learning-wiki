## Evidence

### Komasawa Nobuyasu 2018

Komasawa Nobuyasu, Berg Benjamin W., Minami Toshiaki. (2018). Problem-based learning for anesthesia resident operating room crisis management training. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0207594

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same pre/post chi-square analysis of the 35-resident cohort. The article reports no significant difference for these three airway items; equivalence was not tested. The authors hypothesize the null reflects "the relative greater clinical experience reported for these areas".

> "Impossible intubation, impossible oxygenation, and awake intubation did not show significant differences between pre- and post–PBL (P = 0.694, P = 0.887, and P = 0.765)."
