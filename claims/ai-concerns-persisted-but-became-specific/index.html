q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive comparison of pre- and post-session surveys (approximately 22–25 post-survey respondents depending on question). The article reports the session "did not fundamentally alter community members’ underlying concerns about AI" but supported "a shift from abstract unease to more articulated, specific, and actionable forms of understanding". No effect sizes or tests were printed.

"These patterns suggested that while the session did not fundamentally alter community members’ underlying concerns about AI, it supported a shift from abstract unease to more articulated, specific, and actionable forms of understanding."
