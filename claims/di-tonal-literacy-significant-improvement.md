## Evidence

### Lowe 2012

Lowe, G., & Belcher, S. (2012). Direct Instruction and music literacy: one approach to augmenting the diminishing? Australian Journal of Music Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1000240

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests on the tonal subtest of the Iowa Tests of Music Literacy showed the combined DI mean difference of 12.47 was significant (p < .000), while the combined non-DI mean difference of 3.68 was not significant (p = .217).

> "Again, the t-tests indicated a statistically significant improvement in test results for the DI classes, and a smaller standard deviation."
