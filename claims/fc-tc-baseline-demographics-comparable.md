### Zhang Weihao 2021

Zhang Weihao, Gu Jiaoyang, Li Fan, Feng Feifei, Chen Huiqiang, Xing Xiaowei, Liu Lan. (2021). The effect of flipped classroom in multiple clinical skills training for clinical interns on Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCE). Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.2013405

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison of the randomized groups (A n = 37, B n = 42): mean age 27.62 ± 2.95 vs. 27.57 ± 3.42 years; "There was no significant difference in sex", marital status, or average age. All interns denied prior FC or work experience.

> "There was no significant difference in sex (p = 0.945), marital status (P = 0.867) and average age (p = 0.379) between the two groups."
