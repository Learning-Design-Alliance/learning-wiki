`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

One-sample Wilcoxon signed-rank tests against the neutral midpoint in a post-project survey of VGC participants (N = 36). All mediator effects were large: "all effects were large (r > .82, allp < .001)"; outcome constructs also exceeded neutral significantly (all p < .001, r = .835-.872).

> "One-sample Wilcoxon signed-rank tests confirmed that all three mediator constructs were significantly above neutral, supporting H1, H2, and H3. As shown in Table IV, all effects were large (r > .82, allp < .001)"
