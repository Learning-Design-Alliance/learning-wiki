This is interview data from Miles, a first-generation biochemistry major in his first physics course (Interview 2, mid-semester). He attributes diffusion's connection to physiology and biochemistry to physics: "it has to be physics, it has to be." The article notes his connections are more general than Maria's or Beverly's.
