User-level performance analysis categorizing 1,170 logged users into four quartiles by problems attempted. The article reports Q3 users' 30.8% chat usage and 91% success rate; Q4 super users (261 users) averaged 10.5 attempts with 85% success.

> "The third quartile (Q3) represents highly engaged users (68 users), who attempted three problems on average, with a strong success rate (91%) and a significant increase in chat usage (30.8%)."
