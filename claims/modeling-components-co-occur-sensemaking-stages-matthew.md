# In a completed physics problem-solving attempt, engagement with the Denotative Function and Demonstration components of modeling co-occurs with the explanation-generation stage of sensemaking, and the Inferential Function co-occurs with resolution

### Amogh Sirnoorkar 2023

Amogh Sirnoorkar, James T. Laverty, and Paul D. O. Bergeron (2023). Sensemaking and Scientific Modeling: Intertwined processes analyzed in the context of physics problem solving. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010118)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study analysis of Matthew's think-aloud interview on the Gravitron task, segmented into modeling components and epistemic-game stages. The analysis identifies his force diagram as the Denotative Function and his equating of denoted forces as the Demonstration, the same moves marking his navigation through the explanation generation stage (Stage 2).

> "Matthew's moves to represent the target system through a force diagram marks the modeling's Denotative Function in his approach."

### Amogh Sirnoorkar 2023 (2)

Amogh Sirnoorkar, James T. Laverty, and Paul D. O. Bergeron (2023). Sensemaking and Scientific Modeling: Intertwined processes analyzed in the context of physics problem solving. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010118)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the same case study, Matthew's final interpretation of the inequality through the rider-Gravitron system is identified as the Inferential Function, and his decisive claim that riders would slide off the wall marks his exit condition from the Sensemaking Epistemic Game.

> "Matthew's concluding statement also marks his exit from the sensemaking process and thus the Resolution stage of the Sensemaking Epistemic Game."
