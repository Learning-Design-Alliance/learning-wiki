`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/posttest evaluation of predicting-input questions, which ask students to determine characteristics of the input signal from a provided output signal and the lock-in settings. Performance was 'roughly 40%' before the tutorial and 'improved to over 80% on all posttest material'.
