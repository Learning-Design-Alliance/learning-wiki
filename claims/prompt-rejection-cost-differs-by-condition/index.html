q3 · i? · causal · r?

Within-student reject-minus-accept contrasts in answer quality for well-specified prompts, compared between groups in the classroom study. The article reports the contrast "differed significantly between groups", with d = 0.91, suggesting unguided prompt revision was harmful for control students.

"The reject-minus-accept contrast differed significantly between groups (𝑀=−4.29 in control vs. 𝑀= 1.61 in intervention; Welch 𝑡= 2.83 ,𝑝=.009 ,𝑑= 0.91 ; Fig. 2A)."
