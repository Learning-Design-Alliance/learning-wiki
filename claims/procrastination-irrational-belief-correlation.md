## Evidence

### Amoke CV 2025

Amoke CV, Ede MO, Mawila-Chauke D, Obeagu IE. (2025). Disputing and challenging academic procrastination behaviors in students using REBT approach. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000041725

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis across the three assessment waves of the same 129-participant study, using PASS and ABS-2-AV scores; the article reports "a positive relationship between academic procrastination and irrational belief across all 3 levels of assessment" (r = .559, .416, .639, each significant at the 0.01 level).

> "Furthermore, the relationship between academic procrastination and irrational belief at the pretest, posttest, and follow-up stages were 0.559, 0.416, and 0.639 respectively (see Tables 5–7)."
