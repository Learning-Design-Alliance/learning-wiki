### Hossain Md. Ismail 2026

Hossain Md. Ismail, Ahmad Iftakhar. (2026). Education in Haor areas of Bangladesh: A mixed-methods study on academic performance, school attendance, and school intervention. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0348890

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression in the same 189-student survey found peer help for study significantly predicted good academic performance, "OR = 3.89, 95% CI [1.424, 10.632]". A guardian interview described a son studying at friends' homes before examinations.

> "Peer support for studies was also a significant positive factor (B = 1.359, p = .008, OR = 3.89, 95% CI [1.424, 10.632])"
