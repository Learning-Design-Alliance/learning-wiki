Klatte M 2013¶

The review reports that the RANCH cross-sectional study, often cited only for the aircraft-noise reading effect, also showed "enhanced performance in episodic memory with increasing exposure to road trafﬁc noise", a finding the review states remains unexplained.

"there was another exposure–effect relationship, revealing enhanced performance in episodic memory with increasing exposure to road trafﬁc noise. This counter-intuitive ﬁnding remains unexplained."
