`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The author's theoretical argument opening the essay: students rarely encounter science history, and what they encounter portrays scientists as people with the right answers; wrong-answer scientists such as Riccioli correct this. This is an argued position, with the author's own classroom experience described later.

> "But those who got the wrong answers can teach students that in science answers are often elusive -- not found in the back of a book or discovered in a bold stroke of genius."
