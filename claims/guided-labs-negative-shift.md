## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Open-ended versus guided laboratory activities: Impact on students' beliefs about experimental physics. arXiv:1608.01621. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020132)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

In the aggregate data set (N = 4915), guided-only students' overall E-CLASS scores declined from 16.2 pre to 14.4 post, a statistically significant negative shift, while open-ended students shifted positively.

> "students in courses using only traditional guided labs showed a small ( d = −0.2) but statistically signiﬁcant negative shift ( p ≪ 0.01)."
