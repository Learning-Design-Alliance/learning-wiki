## Evidence

### Giesbrecht Ed M. 2022

Giesbrecht Ed M., Rushton Paula W., Dubé Evemie. (2022). Wheelchair service provision education in Canadian occupational therapy programs. PLoS ONE 17(2): e0262165. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0262165

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive cross-sectional online survey of educators from all 14 accredited Canadian OT programs (29 educators enrolled). Results report that "Only 5 programs covered all the WHO recommended 8 steps" and that Assessment and Prescription were universal, with Product Preparation and Follow-up least covered.

> "Only 5 programs covered all the WHO recommended 8 steps of wheelchair service provision, with all programs covering at least four of the eight steps. Step 2 (Assessment) and 3 (Prescription) were covered in all programs."
