## Evidence

### Mustafa Sat 2026

Mustafa Sat, Kursat Cagiltay. (2026). Weaving STEAM with threads: teacher professional development with e-textile projects. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10597-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-program survey of PD camp participants (N = 31 complete responses) using a 7-point Likert questionnaire. The article reports "average scores ranging from 5.74 to 6.77" and 83.9% strongly agreeing on interdisciplinary learning support; qualitative interviews corroborated creativity gains.

> "The findings indicated highly positive perceptions across all items measured, with average scores ranging from 5.74 to 6.77."

### Mustafa Sat 2026 (2)

Mustafa Sat, Kursat Cagiltay. (2026). Weaving STEAM with threads: teacher professional development with e-textile projects. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10597-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same post-survey (N = 31). The Arduino item was the weakest: "32.3% strongly agreed that they learned Arduino coding skills, while 9.7% reported a neutral stance" (M = 5.74, SD = 1.21).

> "Arduino programming instruction received the lowest endorsement and showed the greatest variability among all measured items. 32.3% strongly agreed that they learned Arduino coding skills, while 9.7% reported a neutral stance."
