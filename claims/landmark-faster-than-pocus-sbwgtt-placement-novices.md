## Evidence

### Gillett Katrine E. 2026

Gillett Katrine E., Boysen Søren R., Harding Cole N., Finch Ashley J., Hillstead Madison E., Menard Julie. (2026). Teaching Clinical Competency: Evaluating POCUS‐Guided Versus Landmark Techniques for Thoracostomy Tube Placement in Canine Cadavers by Veterinary Students. Journal of Veterinary Emergency and Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/vec.70118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover randomized study of 30 veterinary students placing SBWGTTs in canine cadavers. The results section reports that "Landmark‐guided insertions of SBWGTTs were faster than POCUS‐guided insertions" with specific group means and p-values as printed.

> "Landmark‐guided insertions of SBWGTTs were faster than POCUS‐guided insertions (Figure 5), with LPE placements (mean ± SD: 5.2 ± 1.1 min) faster than UPE (7.9 ± 2.8 min, p = 0.0083) and UPN (9.0 ± 2.7 min, p = 0.0001) and LPN (4.6 ± 1.6 min) placements faster than UPN (p < 0.0001) and UPE (p = 0.0009)."
