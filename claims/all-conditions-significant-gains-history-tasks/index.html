Evidence¶

Maaike E. Prangsma 2008¶

Maaike E. Prangsma, Carla A. M. Van Boxtel, Gellof Kanselaar. (2008). Developing a 'big picture': Effects of collaborative construction of multimodal representations in history. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-007-9026-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Preliminary check in the same experiment confirming pupils learned from the collaborative tasks in all three conditions; only p-values printed, no effect sizes.

"The paired samples T-tests showed that in each condition the pupils' score increased significantly from pre-test to post-test (p = .00) and from pre-test to retention test (p = .00)."

Maaike E. Prangsma 2008 (2)¶

Maaike E. Prangsma, Carla A. M. Van Boxtel, Gellof Kanselaar. (2008). Developing a 'big picture': Effects of collaborative construction of multimodal representations in history. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-007-9026-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Prior-knowledge check: the three conditions were statistically comparable at pre-test, though the authors note the pre-test had low reliability (Cronbach's alpha .31) because prior knowledge was low.

"An ANOVA indicated that the three conditions did not differ signiﬁcantly from each other in their mean pre-test scores (F(2, 82) = .32, p = .72)."
