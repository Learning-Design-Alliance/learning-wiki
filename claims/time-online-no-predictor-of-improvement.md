### Palmer Edward 2014

Palmer Edward, Devitt Peter. (2014). The assessment of a structured online formative assessment program: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear regression on Year 2 improvement scores with Group, total time online, support cases completed and incident cases completed as predictors. The article reports group significant at p = 0.046 and no evidence for the other predictors; no effect size is printed.

> "Only the student group was found to be a significant predictor of improvement scores (p = 0.046). There was no evidence that the number of incident cases completed, the number of support cases completed or time spent online had an influence on improvement scores."
