Bland 2023¶

Bland, J. A., Wojcikiewicz, S. K., Darling-Hammond, L., & Wei, W. (2023). Strengthening pathways into the teaching profession in Texas: Challenges and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/957.902

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cohort retention comparison of the 2016–17 first-licensure cohort by preparation route, reported in the Executive Summary. Both named routes are compared: traditional preservice (69%) versus alternative certification (60%) remaining in Texas classrooms after five years.

"Five years after their first year of full licensure in 2016–17, 69% of teachers from traditional preservice programs remain in Texas classrooms, versus 60% of teachers who received a standard certificate after completing alternative certification programs."
