## Evidence

### Selbing 2025

Selbing, I., Becker, N., Pan, Y., Lindström, B., & Olsson, A. (2025). Effects of described demonstrator ability on brain and behavior when learning from others. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00292-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Whole-brain and ROI fMRI analyses in 37 participants (one excluded for incomplete neuroimaging) found no significant activity differences as a function of described ability during either the observation or action phase.

> "In the analyses, of both the observation phase and the action phase, we also performed Best > Worst and Worst > Best contrasts. These did not yield any signiﬁcant results."

### Selbing 2025 (2)

Selbing, I., Becker, N., Pan, Y., Lindström, B., & Olsson, A. (2025). Effects of described demonstrator ability on brain and behavior when learning from others. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00292-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model-based fMRI analysis modulating choice-stimulus presentation by the RL copying parameter κ. Modulation by κ for Best demonstrators appeared in regions the authors associate with action observation, imitation, and decision-making.

> "the copying rate signi ﬁcantly modulated participants' brain activity during the presentation of the choice stimuli when the demonstrators were described as Best, but not Worst. These effects were seen in the bilateral supramarginal gyrus, left middle temporal cortex, and right inferior temporal cortex"
