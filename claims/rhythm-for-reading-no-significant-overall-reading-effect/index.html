Evidence¶

Styles et al. 2014¶

Dr Ben Styles, Rebecca Clarkson, Katherine Fowler. (2014). Rhythm for Reading: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN10404460/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Intention-to-treat analysis of a pupil-randomised waitlist controlled trial (419 pupils randomised, 175 intervention and 180 control in the final model). The primary outcome effect size was 0.03 with confidence interval passing through zero, so the difference was not statistically significant.

"Rhythm for Reading demonstrated an effect size for all pupils of 0.03 and an effect size for children on free school meals (FSM) of 0.11."

Styles et al. 2014 (2)¶

Dr Ben Styles, Rebecca Clarkson, Katherine Fowler. (2014). Rhythm for Reading: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN10404460/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANOVA on post-test reading scores in the same trial reported no significant impact of randomised group (F=0.56, p=0.45, n=366). Equivalence was not tested; the report states the difference may have occurred by chance.

"An ANOVA of post-test reading score by randomised group showed no significant impact of the intervention (F=0.56, p=0.45, n=366)."
