# The first impression of complexity best predicts the overall post-learning cognitive load assessment, indicating a primacy effect in learning contexts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r?` · `q3` · `i2`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In a multiple regression, the first ICL assessment was the strongest predictor of the delayed ICL assessment (β = 0.70), exceeding ICL2 (β = 0.34) and ICL3 (β = 0.47). [→ Krieglstein 2025](#krieglstein-2025)

## Evidence

### Krieglstein 2025

Krieglstein, F., Beege, M., Wesenberg, L., Rey, G. D., & Schneider, S. (2025). The Distorting Influence of Primacy Effects on Reporting Cognitive Load in Learning Materials of Varying Complexity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09980-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Multiple linear regression in the main study with ICL1, ICL2, ICL3 as predictors of the delayed ICL assessment; adjusted R² = 0.67. The first assessment was the strongest predictor: "β = 0.70, 95% CI [0.60, 0.80], p < 0.001", with ICL2 and ICL3 significant but to a significantly lesser extent given the confidence intervals.

> "it could be shown that the first ICL assessment (ICL1) best predicted the delayed ICL assessment, β = 0.70, 95% CI [0.60, 0.80], p < 0.001."

### Krieglstein 2025 (2)

Krieglstein, F., Beege, M., Wesenberg, L., Rey, G. D., & Schneider, S. (2025). The Distorting Influence of Primacy Effects on Reporting Cognitive Load in Learning Materials of Varying Complexity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09980-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Paired samples t-tests in the main study comparing distances of the overall assessment from the first versus last impression. In the increasing condition d = 0.49; in the decreasing condition d = 0.35 (t(46) = 2.40, p < 0.001). Both conditions showed the overall assessment closer to the first impression.

> "the difference between the first and the overall assessment (M = 1.69, SD = 1.12) was significantly smaller than the difference between the last and the overall assessment (M = 2.47, SD = 1.23), t(52) = 3.53, p < 0.001, d = 0.49."
