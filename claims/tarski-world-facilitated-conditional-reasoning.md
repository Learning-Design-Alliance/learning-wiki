## Evidence

### Jelke van Der Pal 1999

Jelke van Der Pal, Tessa Eysink. (1999). Balancing situativity and formality: the importance of relating a formal language to interactive graphics in logic instruction. Learning and Instruction, 9 (4), pp.327-341. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00197363

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Empirical evaluation reported in the abstract comparing Tarski's World instruction with only-situated and only-formal instruction. The article states "Tarski's World was found to facilitate conditional reasoning", supporting the developmental situativity theory; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "The developmental situativity theory was supported empirically: Tarski's World was found to facilitate conditional reasoning, in contrast to instruction that is based on only-situated or only-formal tasks."
