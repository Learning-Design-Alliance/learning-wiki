Evidence¶

McInnes 2026¶

McInnes, R., Airey, L., Moss, P., & Rathnappulige, S. (2026). Efficiency at what cost? Salvaging constructive alignment from the GenAI hype. Australasian Journal of Educational Technology, 42(4), 80–95. https://doi.org/10.14742/ajet.11466

q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

The article's central argument from its critical discourse analysis of grey literature on GenAI in constructive alignment, framed by critical and post-digital literature. The authors contend that "the encroachment of automation into pedagogical design risks devaluing the educational process". This is a theoretical argument, not an empirical measurement; no effect size is reported.

"We argue that while the current GenAI discourse promises efficiencies, the encroachment of automation into pedagogical design risks devaluing the educational process, undermining educator expertise and shifting constructive alignment towards an instrument of compliance."
