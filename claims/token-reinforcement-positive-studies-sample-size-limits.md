Per-study effect size comparison across the 31 aggregated studies (Table 1, forest plot). The article reports "19 out of 31 studies (61.29%)" positive and 12 negative; Yager (2008) had the largest gain (g = 18.505) with low precision, and only two studies (6.45%) had adequate sample sizes.
