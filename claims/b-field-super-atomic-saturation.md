### Savannah L. Mitchem 2016

Savannah L. Mitchem, Dina Zohrabi Alaee, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2016). Student understanding of electric and magnetic fields in materials. [doi:10.1119/1.4991376](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4991376)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of final-exam responses on the magnetization part, which no student had seen before. The article reports the "vast majority of student responses included only super-atomic resources" consisting mainly of alignment, and that only two students did not reach saturation; those two used sub-atomic resources and concluded breakdown of the material.

> "The vast majority of student responses included only super-atomic resources to describe and explain magneti- zation and its eﬀect on the material. These resources con- sisted mainly of alignment of atoms in some sense."
