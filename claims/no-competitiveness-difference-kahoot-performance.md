## Evidence

### Daniell DiFrancesca 2025

Daniell DiFrancesca, Dan Spencer. (2025). The effects of competitive classroom activities on undergraduates with varied levels of competitive preference. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09741-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A 2 (Competitiveness Group) X 8 (Time) mixed ANOVA of Kahoot scores for HC (n = 29) and LC (n = 25) students showed the interaction and group effects were "non-significant" while a within-group time effect emerged (η2 = 0.09).

> "Both the interaction effect, F(5.52, 287.18) = 0.16, p =.98, and between group effect, F(1, 52) = 2.80, p =.10, were non-significant. The within-group analyses showed a significant effect of time, F(5.52, 287.18) = 5.33, p <.001, η2 = 0.09."
