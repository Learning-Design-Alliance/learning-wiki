## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi P 2019

Moll-Khosrawi P, Kamphausen A, Hampe W, Schulte-Uentrop L, Zimmermann S, Kubitz JC. (2019). Anaesthesiology students’ Non-Technical skills: development and evaluation of a behavioural marker system for students (AS-NTS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1609-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Second step of the two-step interrater analysis: ratings from the 67 emergency scenarios were aggregated across raters grouped by year of anaesthesiology training (e.g. attending vs 2nd-year resident pairs) to test whether expertise influenced agreement. ICCs across training-year comparisons remained mostly good to excellent.

> "Data aggregation in the full sample, supports the result that the rating agreement is detached from anaesthesiology training and experience in medical education, fostering the usability of AS-NTS."
