Evidence¶

Garet 2016¶

Garet, M. S., Heppen, J. B., Walters, K., Parkinson, J., Smith, T. M., Song, M., Garrett, R., Yang, R., & Borman, G. D. (2016). Focusing on teachers' mathematical knowledge: The impact of content-intensive professional development (NCEE 2016-4010). Washington, D.C.: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/20164010

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Implementation analysis using PD attendance records and a spring 2014 teacher survey. Treatment teachers participated in 98 percent of Intel Math, 90 percent of the Mathematics Learning Community, and 97 percent of the Video Feedback Cycles, and reported 95 more hours of math-related PD than control teachers.

"On average, 96 per- cent of the expected 80 hours of Intel Math and 100 percent of the expected 13 hours of the Mathematics Learning Community and Video Feedback Cycle s were delivered."
