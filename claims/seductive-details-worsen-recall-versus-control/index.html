Evidence¶

Kienitz 2023¶

Kienitz, A., Krebs, M.-C., & Eitel, A. (2023). Seductive details hamper learning even when they do not disrupt. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09632-w

q3 · i2 · causal · r3

Online experiment with N = 248 randomly assigned to four conditions; ANCOVA comparing control (n = 60) vs. no-prompt (n = 58) on recall showed "a significant difference for recall, F(1,112) = 16.430, p < .001, ηp2 = 0.074". The control group "significantly outperformed participants in the no-prompt condition".

"For recall, participants in the control group significantly outperformed participants in the no-prompt condition, revealing a seductive details effect (see Fig. 2)."
