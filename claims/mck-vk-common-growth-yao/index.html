Evidence¶

Mark Feng Teng 2024¶

Mark Feng Teng, Lawrence Jun Zhang. (2024). Ethnic minority multilingual young learners' longitudinal development of metacognitive knowledge and breadth of vocabulary knowledge. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09360-z

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Parallel development LGC model for Yao students (both variables linear), fitted with bootstrapped GLS fit indices; the model fit the data well. The article reports "a significant correlation between the linear slope of MCK and the slope of VK breadth", showing a common growth trend; no standardized effect size is printed.

"there is a significant positive correlation between the intercept of MCK and the intercept of VK breadth, indicating that the higher the starting point of MCK is, the higher the starting point of VK breadth. There is a significant correlation between the linear slope of MCK and the slope of VK breadth."
