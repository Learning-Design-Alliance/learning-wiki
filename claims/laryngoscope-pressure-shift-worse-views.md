`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Empirical calibration data collected with a modified C-MAC video laryngoscope from seven expert physicians across five C-L views under deflated and inflated tongue conditions; mean pressure profiles (Figs. 12-13, Table 4) show the "significant reduction in the pressure exerted on the tip of the laryngoscope blade (sensor 5)".

> "As the C-L views move from grade 1 to 4 (i.e., better to worse), there is a significant reduction in the pressure exerted on the tip of the laryngoscope blade (sensor 5) (Figs. 12 and 13)."
