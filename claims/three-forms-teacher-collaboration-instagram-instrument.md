## Evidence

### Eric Richter 2022

Eric Richter, Jeffrey P. Carpenter, André Meyer, Dirk Richter. (2022). Instagram as a platform for teacher collaboration and digital social support. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104624

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of a 10-item Instagram collaboration instrument with 249 German teachers. The three-factor model showed good fit ("CFI = 0.94, RMSEA = 0.07, SRMR = 0.05"), while a one-factor model fit poorly (CFI = 0.55), supporting differentiation of the collaboration types.

> "The estimated model meets the criteria specified and therefore indicates a good fit to the data (χ2 = 67.39, df = 32, p = .01, CFI = 0.94, RMSEA = 0.07, SRMR = 0.05)."
