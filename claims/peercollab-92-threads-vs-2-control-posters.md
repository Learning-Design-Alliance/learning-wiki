## Evidence

### Dilrukshi Gamage and Mark E Whiting 2021

Dilrukshi Gamage and Mark E Whiting. 2021. Together we learn better: leveraging communities of practice for MOOC learners. In Asian CHI Symposium 2021, May 8–13, 2021, Yokohama, Japan. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3429360.3468176

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled experiment (n=22): social presence was evaluated by analyzing interactions coded as forum messages, likes, and online meeting conversations; control data were self-reports and PeerCollab data came from the platform. The article calls the contrast "stark" while flagging self-report limits.

> "Only 2 participants in the control condition reported posting a comment in the forum, while the PeerCollab condition had 92 threads in the community space. The challenges of self-report notwithstanding, this difference is stark"
