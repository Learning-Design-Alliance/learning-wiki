Conditional indirect-effect estimates from the CEPS moderated mediation analysis at the low-activity level; both gender groups' confidence intervals "did not include “0”", so the indirect effect was significant for each.

> "male students with low physical activity showed an indirect effect of −0.002 (95% CI (confidence interval) [−0.0041~−0.0004]) and female students showed −0.002 (95% CI [−0.0053~−0.0006]), thereby statistically significant since 95% CI did not include “0”."
