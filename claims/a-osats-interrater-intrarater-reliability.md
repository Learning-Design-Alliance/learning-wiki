`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Blinded video-rating study in a live porcine model (Results): two blinded raters scored recorded anastomoses twice each; interrater reliability was excellent, with "unweighted A-OSATS: ICC = 0.923, p < 0.001; weighted A-OSATS: ICC = 0.924, p < 0.001".

> "In addition, an excellent interrater reliability was seen for both A-OSATS (unweighted A-OSATS: ICC = 0.923, p < 0.001; weighted A-OSATS: ICC = 0.924, p < 0.001)."
