## Evidence

### Badr F. Albanna 2012

Badr F. Albanna, Joel C. Corbo, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Angela Little and Anna M. Zaniewski. (2012). Building Classroom and Organizational Structure Around Positive Cultural Values. [doi:10.1063/1.4789638](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789638)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multiple-choice items from the same fall 2011 survey report frequencies of peer interaction. The authors interpret these counts as evidence of "strong, multifaceted relationships among Compass students"; no inferential statistics are printed.

> "When asked about their interactions with other students from their cohort, 13 students said they saw each other at least once per week, 9 of whom saw each other daily. In addition, 8 studied together and 9 lived with, or planned to live with, another Compass student."
