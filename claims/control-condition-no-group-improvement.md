## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Control participants revising without social information improved individual error by only 3%, and showed no significant change in group mean (P >0.94) or median (P >0.64) accuracy. [→ Becker et al. 2017](#becker-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Becker et al. 2017

Joshua Becker, Devon Brackbill, and Damon Centola. (2017). Network dynamics of social influence in the wisdom of crowds. PNAS. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1615978114

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Across 8 control trials, repeated revision without social information yielded only a 3% individual-error decrease, significantly smaller than the 23% in decentralized networks (n = 21 trials, P <0.001, Wilcoxon rank sum test); the article reports no significant changes in group mean (P >0.94) or median (P >0.64).

> "the opportunity for revision produced a small (3%) decrease in average individual error even in the absence of social information (n = 8 control trials, P <0.001, Wilcoxon signed rank test)"
