`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of Spring 2025 interaction logs from the ten institutions and 200 classes with the highest adoption rates. Adoption was 60.8% with a median of 5 sessions; the distribution was "highly skewed, with a small number of students highly active" (25th percentile = 1; 75th percentile = 19).

> "Among the 11,406 students, 6,932 (60.8%) engaged with the GenAI Tutor at least once. Among those who used the GenAI Tutor, students generated a median of 5 conversation sessions."
