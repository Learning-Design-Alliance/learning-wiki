# Eighth-grade graduates fared better in the transition to high school over time, but Freshman OnTrack rates were not consistently higher than at similar schools

## Evidence

### Elaine M. Allensworth and Andrew Zou 2020

Elaine M. Allensworth and Andrew Zou. (2020). Supporting Neighborhood Schools from Pre-K through High School: Successes and Challenges of the North Lawndale Cluster Initiative. UChicago Consortium Research Report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supporting-neighborhood-schools-from-pre-k-through-high-school

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cohort analysis of first-time ninth-graders at Collins Academy and of NLCI eighth-grade graduates' ninth-grade GPAs, compared with district averages and matched schools. The report finds improving ninth-grade GPAs and OnTrack rates but states they were "not consistently higher" than similar schools; no effect size printed.

> "The NLCI Freshman OnTrack rates were not consistently higher than those at other schools serving similar populations of students, suggesting that the improvements largely resulted from other district efforts, rather than the initiative itself."
