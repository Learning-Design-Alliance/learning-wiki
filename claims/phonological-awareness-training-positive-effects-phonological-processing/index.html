What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Phonological Awareness Training. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

The WWC's synthesis of six studies (four RCTs meeting standards, two RCTs meeting standards with reservations due to high attrition) of more than 100 preschool children. The report states "The average improvement index for phonological processing is +27 percentile points across the six studies."

"The average improvement index for phonological processing is +27 percentile points across the six studies, with a range of –27 to +50 percentile points across findings."
