## Evidence

### Jiao Chen and Chunhui Liu 2023

Jiao Chen and Chunhui Liu. (2023). The longitudinal association between children’s growth mindset in senior primary school and their parents’ growth mindset. Front. Psychol. 14:1110944. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1110944

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parallel process latent growth curve model (conditional model fit CFI = 0.986, RMSEA = 0.017). The slope-to-slope path from maternal to child growth mindset was significant in both models, indicating coupled decline trajectories; the slope-to-intercept path was also significant (β = 0.215, p = 0.008).

> "The results also showed a significant and positive link from the slope of maternal growth mindset to children’s slope in the conditional (β = 0.362, p = 0.006) and unconditional models (β = 0.369, p = 0.007); that is, when the mothers’ growth mindset declined more rapidly, the children’s growth mindset declined more rapidly"
