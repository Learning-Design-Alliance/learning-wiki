## Evidence

### Nabizadeh Sakineh; Hajian Sepideh; Sheikhan Zohre; Rafiei Fatemeh 2019

Nabizadeh Sakineh; Hajian Sepideh; Sheikhan Zohre; Rafiei Fatemeh. (2019). Prediction of academic achievement based on learning strategies and outcome expectations among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1527-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 380 undergraduate medical sciences students sampled multi-stage from nine faculties; Pearson correlations with past-semester CGPA. Learning strategies correlated positively, "confirming the first hypothesis of the study"; Table 2 reports R = 0.243 (p < 0.001) for the learning strategies scale.

> "The calculation of Pearson correlation coefficients between study variables and the CGPA of students (P < 0.05) revealed a direct linear and positive correlation between the motivational strategies and learning strategies plus most of their sub-structures and CGPA students, confirming the first hypothesis of the study."
