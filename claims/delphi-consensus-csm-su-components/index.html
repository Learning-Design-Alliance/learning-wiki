Two-round Delphi validation with 10 Cypriot field experts (six senior principals, two inspectors, two academics). Round 2 re-evaluation reported "consensus established at 80% or higher" on all major model elements; no effect size is printed.

"Experts indicated their level of agreement with key components of the model, with consensus established at 80% or higher. All major elements met this criterion, indicating both conceptual robustness and practical feasibility within educational settings."
