# Hands-on CPR, endotracheal intubation, and defibrillation operational scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in paediatric postgraduates after integrated simulation with TBL, in a single-group pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After integrated simulation with TBL, operational scores for hands-on CPR, endotracheal intubation, and defibrillation all rose significantly (P < 0.05) in a pre-post self-comparison of 24 paediatric postgraduates. [→ Zhang Li-dan 2026](#zhang-li-dan-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhang Li-dan 2026

Zhang Li-dan, Wang Li, Li Ya-ting, Guo Yao-yu, Lin Hong-xin, Ye Xiao-ting, Chen Hua-bao, Tang Yan-lai, Chen Chun, Xu Ling-ling. (2026). Effectiveness of integrated simulation and team-based learning in paediatric postgraduates for cardiopulmonary resuscitation training: a multiple methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08694-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post self-comparison of 24 paediatric postgraduates at one hospital; skills scored on a 100-point Skills Evaluation Scale. Operational scores rose for hands-on CPR, intubation and defibrillation with paired t-tests at "P < 0.05"; no effect sizes printed.

> "The integrated simulation with TBL approach produced significant improvements in clinical operation scores: hands-on CPR (80.95 ± 5.81 vs. 88.46 ± 7.35), endotracheal intubation (76.03 ± 6.51 vs. 81.89 ± 9.72), defibrillation (79.96 ± 4.37 vs. 89.84 ± 3.98), and teamwork (28.33 ± 2.46 vs. 32.99 ± 2.85) (P < 0.05)."
