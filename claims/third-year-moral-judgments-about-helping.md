### Dahl A. 2019

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Dahl (2018a), that in simple situations "most preschoolers judge that people should help." It adds that children, like adults, are less likely to judge helping as okay if the recipient is trying to steal something.

> "In simple situations, for instance helping someone who is hurt at little cost to oneself, most preschoolers judge that people should help (Dahl, 2018a)."
