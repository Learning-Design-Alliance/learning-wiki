## Evidence

### Fred M. Newmann 2000

Fred M. Newmann, Karin Sconzert. (2000). School Improvement with External Partners. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://ccsr.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/publications/ExternalPartners.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Finding from the comparative case study of 18 schools across nine networks, where each network included one school selected for high potential to benefit and one with more uncertain potential. Qualitative evidence across schools within the same network showed differential uptake of partner programs.

> "Moreover, partners’ success varies substantially between schools, according to teachers’ and administrators’ willingness to work with them and imple- ment their ideas."
