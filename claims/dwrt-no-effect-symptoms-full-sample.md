## Evidence

### Mahaffey Brittain L. 2020

Mahaffey Brittain L., Mackin Daniel M., Rosen Jonathan, Schwartz Rebecca M., Taioli Emanuela, Gonzalez Adam. (2020). The disaster worker resiliency training program: a randomized clinical trial. International Archives of Occupational and Environmental Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00420-020-01552-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null mixed ANOVA results for the PSS, PCL-5, and PHQ-9 in the complete randomized sample at T1 and T2. No statistically significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested, and the article attributes this partly to low symptom base rates.

> "Results suggest that there were not any significant effects of condition, time, or the condition by time interactions on Perceived Stress, PTSD symptoms, or depression symptoms."
