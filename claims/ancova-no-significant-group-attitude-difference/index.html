Evidence¶

Aykut Emre Bozdogan 2011¶

Aykut Emre Bozdogan. (2011). The Effects of Instruction with Visual Materials on the Development of Preservice Elementary Teachers' Knowledge and Attitude Towards Global Warming. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932241

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA on posttest GWAS scores of both groups (Table 7, R Squared = .572): the group effect was not significant after controlling for pretest; the experimental mean (3.83) was descriptively higher than the control mean (3.70).

"Analysis of covariance results showed that when pretest total scores were controlled, it was not meaningful with respect to posttest adjusted mean scores [F (1-44)=3.48, p>.05]."
