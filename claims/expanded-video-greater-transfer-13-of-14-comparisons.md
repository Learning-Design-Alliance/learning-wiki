## Evidence

### Ana Saavedra · Daniel L. Schwartz 2026

Ana Saavedra · Daniel L. Schwartz. (2026). Improving the transfer of inquiry strategies by using short instructional videos. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10648-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results across five experiments with eighth- and ninth-grade students in Bogota and Medellin, each comparing subsets of video treatments against Expanded. The article reports the descriptive superiority was "highly unlikely to occur by chance (p = .0009)" but never significant within a single experiment.

> "Within a given experiment, the Expanded condition was descriptively superior to the other conditions in 13 out of 14 head-to-head treatment comparisons, which is highly unlikely to occur by chance (p = .0009). However, the advantage within a single experiment never reached significance."
