In the Priority 4 section on SEL, the report argues that restorative practices are needed because 'SEL programs cannot enable meaningful long-term growth' in punitive, exclusionary environments. This is the report's own assertion; no study or effect size is printed.

"SEL programs cannot enable meaningful long-term growth for students in environments that are otherwise authoritarian, punitive, and exclusionary rather than educative and inclusive."
