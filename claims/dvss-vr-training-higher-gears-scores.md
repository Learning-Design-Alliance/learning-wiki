## Evidence

### Wang Rong 2025

Wang Rong, Zheng Nan, Liang Yujing, Cui Haoxin, Ren Tong, Xing Wenhui, Li Yanghui. (2025). Multi-perspective analysis of daVinci surgical virtual reality training: a prospective randomized controlled study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02309-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled comparison on the dVSS simulator after proficiency-based VR training versus no training. The study reports "a significant superiority over the control group in the total score of the GEARS (24.91 ± 3.36 vs. 19.68 ± 3.07; p < 0.01)". GEARS was rated blind by 10 non-medical graduate students.

> "The Residents and the Fellows had no significant difference in the overall score of the GEARS (21.08 ± 4.31 vs. 22.74 ± 3.92; p = 0.31), whereas the VR-training group had a significant superiority over the control group in the total score of the GEARS (24.91 ± 3.36 vs. 19.68 ± 3.07; p < 0.01)"
