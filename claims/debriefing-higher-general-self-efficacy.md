## Evidence

### Cao Xi 2026

Cao Xi, Wang Min, Huang Ye, Hu Qiu-Min, Tang Rong. (2026). Research on the application of debriefing in simulation-based education of surgical nursing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09133-7

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Self-controlled before-and-after study of 340 nursing students using GSES. Paired t-test showed a significant increase with "The magnitude of this difference was medium (Cohen's ds = 0.47)".

> "General self-efficacy scores were significantly higher in the experimental phase compared to the control phase (P < 0.001), as shown in Table 4. The mean score was 0.38 points higher (95% CI: 0.29 to 0.46), representing a 13.48% difference relative to the control phase. The magnitude of this difference was medium (Cohen's ds = 0.47)."
