Soo Ping Chow 2025¶

Soo Ping Chow, A., Faulkner-Bond, M., Lemke, M., LeBeau, B., & Quesen, S. (2025). How English learners are screened for risk of reading difficulty: Use of English- and Spanish-language screeners in the 2023/24 school year. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677598

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive finding from the cluster analysis of 2023/24 screening practices: clusters 4 and 5 (185 and 79 students) lacked English screening at all three time periods. The brief states "these students’ schools appear to be out of compliance" with the twice-yearly approved-assessment requirement.

"these students’ schools appear to be out of compliance with Massachusetts’s regulation that requires screening with an approved assessment (currently available only in English) at least twice per year."
