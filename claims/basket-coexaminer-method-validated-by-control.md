### McManus I C 2013

McManus I C, Elder Andrew T, Dacre Jane. (2013). Investigating possible ethnicity and sex bias in clinical examiners: an analysis of data from the MRCP(UK) PACES and nPACES examinations. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-103

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Statistical analysis of 26 PACES diets and six nPACES diets using a control condition assumed to have no true effect on behaviour. The article reports the control "suggested that about 5% of examiners were significant at an (uncorrected) 5% level".

> "The control (odd-number) condition suggested that about 5% of examiners were significant at an (uncorrected) 5% level, and that the method therefore worked as expected."
