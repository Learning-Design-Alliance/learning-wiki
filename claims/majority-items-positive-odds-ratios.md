Marc Harper, Alison Ahlgren Reddy. (2013). Detecting Concepts Crucial for Success in Mathematics Courses from Knowledge State-based Placement Data. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.1127](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.1127)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Contingency-table odds ratios computed for every item in every Fall 2010 class, plotted as z-score counts in Figure 6; items universal or absent within a course and items with empty cells are excluded. The standard deviation drops to about 0.9 when small-count items are excluded.
