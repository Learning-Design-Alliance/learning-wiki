## Evidence

### Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020

Stuij Sebastiaan M., Drossaert Constance H. C., Labrie Nanon H. M., Hulsman Robert L., Kersten Marie José, van Dulmen Sandra, Smets Ellen M. A. (2020). Developing a digital training tool to support oncologists in the skill of information-provision: a user centred approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1985-0

`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative individual user-testing study (five junior oncologists, think-aloud protocol). Participants valued annotating "video-recorded consultations", and all five valued uploading and responding to their own and peers' recordings, considering this novel, motivating, and insightful.

> "the functionality of annotating fragments of video-recorded consultations was evaluated positively."

### Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020 (2)

Stuij Sebastiaan M., Drossaert Constance H. C., Labrie Nanon H. M., Hulsman Robert L., Kersten Marie José, van Dulmen Sandra, Smets Ellen M. A. (2020). Developing a digital training tool to support oncologists in the skill of information-provision: a user centred approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1985-0

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the same user-testing analysis, critical comments concerned "emotional concerns or practical barriers" for recordings; participants suggested involving experts, having several feedback providers, and guaranteeing privacy through patient permission and access restrictions.

> "Critical comments concerned emotional concerns or practical barriers (time consuming, technical problems) for making a recording."
