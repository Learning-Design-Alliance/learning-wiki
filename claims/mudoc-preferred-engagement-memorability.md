## Evidence

### Karan Taneja 2025

Karan Taneja, Anjali Singh, and Ashok K. Goel. (2025). Towards a Multimodal Document-grounded Conversational AI System for Education. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98462-4_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98462-4_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects survey with 30 graduate students comparing MuDoC and text-only TexDoC on a 7-point Likert scale (Fig. 3). For memorability, "23 participantsfelt more confident about problem-solvingwith MuDoC" was reported separately, and overall 22 preferred MuDoC versus 4 for TexDoC.

> "Regarding interest and engagement, 26 participants preferred MuDoC while only 3 preferred TexDoC. Many characterized images in MuDoC responses as helpful in learning and effective in capturing attention."
