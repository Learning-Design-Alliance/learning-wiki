## Evidence

### Souiki Tarik 2021

Souiki Tarik, Benzagmout Mohammed, Alami Badreeddine, Ibn Majdoub Karim, Toughrai Imane, Mazaz Khalid, Boujraf Saïd. (2021). Impact of mental imagery on enhancing surgical skills learning in novice's surgeons: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02987-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot prospective randomized comparative study in which 17 first-semester surgical residents and interns performed a hand-sewn intestinal anastomosis on bovine intestine after either a 45-min mental imagery session or free document review, scored on 14 criteria (max 28) by a blinded examiner. The article reports "These two means are statistically different (p = 0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The group (a) that used MI technique recorded a mean score of 17.78 out of 28 (with n = 8, standard deviation = 2.42), while the control group (b) recorded a score of 10.63 (with n = 9, standard deviation = 2.85). These two means are statistically different (p = 0.001)."
