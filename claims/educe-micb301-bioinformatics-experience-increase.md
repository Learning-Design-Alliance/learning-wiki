## Evidence

### Dill-McFarland KA 2021

Dill-McFarland KA, König SG, Mazel F, Oliver DC, McEwen LM, Hong KY, Hallam SJ. (2021). An integrated, modular approach to data science education in microbiology. PLoS Computational Biology. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008661

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Matched pre/post survey analysis from the same MICB 301 preliminary evaluation. The article reports "student self-reported experience in “bioinformatics” significantly increased" across three transitions (FDR P = 1.84E-7, 2.74E-5, 2.69E-2); no effect sizes are printed. The authors link this to attracting students to 400-level EDUCE electives.

> "Similarly, student self-reported experience in “bioinformatics” significantly increased from none to low (FDR P = 1.84E-7) or medium (FDR P = 2.74E-5) as well as from low to medium (FDR P = 2.69E-2)."
