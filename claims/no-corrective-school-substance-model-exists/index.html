q2 · i? · review · r2

Results statement of the scoping review (Abstract/Results synthesis) covering the 43 included studies identified through SCOPUS and Web of Science searches to December 2025. The review reports "there was no school-based intervention model that was corrective in its approach." This is a descriptive gap finding, not an effect estimate.

"All school-based interventions examined were preventive or protective in their approaches. There was no school-based intervention model that was corrective in its approach."
