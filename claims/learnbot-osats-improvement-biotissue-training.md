Video-assessed analysis of consecutive robotic pancreaticojejunostomy and hepaticojejunostomy attempts during the LEARNBOT biotissue training, scored by blinded graders using OSATS. The article reports "0.350 OSATS points per attempt (ρ=0.350), P<0.001" and a 21-minute per-attempt decrease in reconstruction time.

> "During simulation training, OSATS scores significantly improved [0.350 OSATS points per attempt (ρ=0.350), P<0.001], and the duration of reconstruction decreased significantly (21 min per attempt, P<0.001)."
