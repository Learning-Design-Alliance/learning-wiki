Analysis of the linked statewide dataset in the Invisible Achievement Gap report compared test outcomes of students in foster care with other subgroups. The data "revealed a previously unconfirmed achievement gap between students in foster care and other students in the state."

"on the statewide mathematics test administered in grades 2-7, students in foster care had the lowest proficiency of any group, including other at-risk subgroups such as students with low socioeconomic status ( SES ), English language learners ( ELL students ), and students with disabilities."
