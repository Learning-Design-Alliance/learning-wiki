Evidence¶

Mokhtari 2015¶

Mokhtari, K., Neel, J. L., Kaiser, F., & Le, H.-H. (2015). Assessing the Promise of a Supplemental Reading Intervention for At-Risk First Grade Students in a Public School Setting. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 7(3), 281-300. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068056

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pilot study comparing three groups (at-risk tutored, at-risk non-tutored, typically developing) using ANCOVA with fall Gates-MacGinitie scores as covariate. The article reports "an overall significant group effect of our reading intervention after controlling for initial group differences in reading ability".

"Using a test of Between-Subject effects, we found an overall significant group effect of our reading intervention after controlling for initial group differences in reading ability, F(2,26) = 8.002, p = .002"

Mokhtari 2015 (2)¶

Mokhtari, K., Neel, J. L., Kaiser, F., & Le, H.-H. (2015). Assessing the Promise of a Supplemental Reading Intervention for At-Risk First Grade Students in a Public School Setting. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 7(3), 281-300. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068056

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Sidak-corrected post hoc pairwise comparisons on adjusted means showed "the at-risk tutored students outperformed the at-risk non-tutored students" (p = .04). Tutored students' adjusted mean was 393.83 (SD = 12.6) versus 351.09 (SD = 11.1) for non-tutored peers.

"We verified these results using the Sidak Corrected post hoc pairwise comparisons among the three group means and found that the at-risk tutored students outperformed the at-risk non-tutored students, (p = .04)"
