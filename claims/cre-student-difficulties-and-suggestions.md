## Evidence

### Si Jihyun 2020

Si Jihyun. (2020). Course-based research experience of undergraduate medical students through project-based learning. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2020.152

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Open-coded responses to two end-of-course survey questions. Asked about the most difficult aspects, students named conducting research ("a difficult aspect of the course"), understanding scientific journals, selecting topics, being active learners, free-riding in collaborative work and excessive preparation; the study does not rank these. Asked how the course could be improved, they suggested clearer guidelines, topic options and more class time.

> "As for suggestions to improve the learning experience, they called for clearer guidelines about the research process, options for research topics, and more class time, saying "clearer guidelines about the research process are necessary,""
