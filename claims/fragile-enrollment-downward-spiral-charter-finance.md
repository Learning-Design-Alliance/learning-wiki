## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In interviews, under-enrolled charter schools lost finances needed to cover costs, and enrollment loss shrank budgets in ways that made further enrollment decline steeper. [→ National Charter School Resource Center 2021](#national-charter-school-resource-center-2021)

## Evidence

### National Charter School Resource Center 2021

National Charter School Resource Center, Evan, A., & Sullivan, H. (2021). Identifying Indicators of Distress in Charter Schools, Part 2: The Roles and Perspectives of Charter School Leaders and Board Members. Bethesda, MD: Manhattan Strategy Group. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616211

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Leader and board interviews about finance and operations distress. The report describes "downward spiraling enrollment, where schools that lost students had an increasingly smaller budget," making it hard to invest in operations and recruitment, resulting in steeper enrollment and financial decline.

> "Schools with patterns of under enrollment lacked the finances needed to cover the school's debts and ongoing costs."
