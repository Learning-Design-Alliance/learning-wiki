## Evidence

### Nummijoki 2018

Nummijoki, J., Engeström, Y., & Sannino, A. (2018). Defensive and expansive cycles of learning: A study of home care encounters. Journal of the Learning Sciences, 27(2), 224-264. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2017.1412970

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Detailed analysis of Case D, a 50-minute visit with 766 turns of talk. The authors report that "the cycle consists mainly of the learning actions of implementing (6 actions), reflecting (7actions) and consolidating (4 actions)" and that questioning, modeling and examining were missing.

> "both the client and the home care worker took an expansive stance in all the 19 learning actions of the encounter"
