`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Tasks were repeated half an hour after the first set, with differences analyzed using a paired t-test. The study reports the mean time reduction "for the S group (p < 0.001)" was greater than for the R group, and the error reduction was significant for students (p = 0.001) but not residents (p = 0.229), suggesting LABOT may be particularly useful in the initial phase of laparoscopic learning.

> "The mean difference for time taken (±SD) between the first and the second attempt was −99.5 (±70.4) s for the S group (p < 0.001) and −31.3 (±43.1) s for the R group (p = 0.014). The mean differential number of errors was −5.7 (±5.1) for the S group (p = 0.001) and −0.5 (±1.6) for the R group (p = 0.229)."
