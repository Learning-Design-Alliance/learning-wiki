## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yang H 2023

Yang H, Xiao X, Wu X, Fu X, Du Q, Luo Y, Li B, Zeng J, Zhang Y. (2023). Virtual Standardized Patients Versus Traditional Academic Training for Improving Clinical Competence Among Traditional Chinese Medicine Students: Prospective Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/43763

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Summative assessment of the randomized trial after the 12-week course: online case-based knowledge test, offline clinical skill checklists, and real-time Arizona Clinical Interviewing Rating scores from trained occupational standardized patients, all favoring the VSP-TCM group.

> "Similarly, in the summative evaluation, the VSP-TCM group performed better in the online systematic knowledge test (OSKT; mean 86.62, SD 2.71, vs mean 85.38, SD 2.62; P=.046), application of TCM technology (mean 87.86, SD 3.04, vs mean 86.19, SD 3.08; P=.02), TCM syndrome differentiation and therapeutic regimen (mean 90.93, SD 2.42, vs mean 89.60, SD 2.86; P=.03), and communication skills (mean 90.67, SD 4.52, vs mean 88.24, SD 4.56; P=.02) than the control group."

### Yang H 2023 (2)

Yang H, Xiao X, Wu X, Fu X, Du Q, Luo Y, Li B, Zeng J, Zhang Y. (2023). Virtual Standardized Patients Versus Traditional Academic Training for Improving Clinical Competence Among Traditional Chinese Medicine Students: Prospective Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/43763

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null summative result: written medical record scores did not differ significantly between the VSP-TCM group and the control group; the article reports no effect size for this contrast.

> "There was no significant difference in medical writing between both groups (mean 75.07, SD 3.61, vs mean 75.71, SD 2.86; P=.37)."
