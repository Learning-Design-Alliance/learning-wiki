Longitudinal regression analysis of 50 dyads predicting PPVT at 30 months. "When parent tokens was added as a predictor with the controls (Model 2), it was a marginally significant positive predictor of PPVT scores at 30 months (p < 0.10)" with R-square increasing by 5 percent over the control model.
