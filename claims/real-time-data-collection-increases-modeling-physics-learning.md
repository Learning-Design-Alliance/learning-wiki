# Students in a technology-enhanced modeling physics class learned significantly more than students in an otherwise identical no-technology class

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The 2005 technology-enhanced class scored significantly higher on FCI posttest (t(87) = 2.80, p < .01, d = 2.13) and Hake gain (t(87) = 2.90, p < .01, d = 4.06) than the 2004 no-technology class. [→ Brewe 2007](#brewe-2007)

## Evidence

### Brewe 2007

Brewe, E., Sawtelle, V., and Pamela, P. (2007). Impacts of real-time data collection on introductory algebra-based physics. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Impacts+of+real-time+data+collection+on+introductory+algebra-based+physics

`q3 · i3`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact Fall sections (2004 no-tech, 2005 tech) of the same algebra-based mechanics course with the same instructor. The article reports "the two classes differed significantly on Posttest score t(87) = 2.80, p < .01" with effect sizes d = 2.13 (post) and d = 4.06 (Hake gain).
