### Akçay Ahmet Oğuz 2026

Akçay Ahmet Oğuz, Karahan Engin, Kara Ahmet. (2026). The role of problem-solving and decision-making skills in linking critical thinking dispositions to perceptions of success. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04918-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Path analysis of survey data from 285 Turkish primary students; the CTD→DMS path was the largest, with critical thinking disposition explaining 42% of decision-making variance. The article reports "increases their decision-making skills by 0.65 (t = 14.350, p < 0.001)".

> "Figure 2 shows that a one-unit increase in primary school students’ critical thinking dispositions increases their decision-making skills by 0.65 (t = 14.350, p < 0.001)"
