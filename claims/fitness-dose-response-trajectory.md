## Evidence

### Hsieh Shu-Shih 2018

Hsieh Shu-Shih, Tsai Jia-Ren, Chang Shao-Hsi, Cheng Chih-Fu, Sung Yao-Ting, Hung Tsung-Min (2018). The Relations between 3-year Changes in Physical Fitness and Academic Performance in Nationally Representative Sample of Junior High School Students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-34370-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Authors' interpretation of the group ordering in the nationwide observational data; the article reports the H-H group "had the highest exam scores, followed by the N-H, the H-N, and the N-N groups". This is an interpretive claim, not a tested contrast.

> "Results of the present study further suggest a dose-response relationship during the junior high school period, as indicated by the fact that the H-H group, who presumably were the most aerobically active, had the highest exam scores, followed by the N-H, the H-N, and the N-N groups."
