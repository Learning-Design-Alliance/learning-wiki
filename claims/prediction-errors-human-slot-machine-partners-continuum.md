### Jessica Joiner 2017

Jessica Joiner, Matthew Piva, Courtney Turrin and Steve W. C. Chang. (2017). Social learning through prediction error in the brain. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0009-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review of a human neuroimaging game study comparing social and nonsocial interactive partners. The review cites it as evidence that social information processing "may not be categorically distinct from other types of information, but rather lie on a continuum".

> "when human participants play a game with other individuals or slot machine partners programmed to display varying levels of generosity, activations in TPJ, PCC, PrCu, vmPFC, and several other regions reflected the prediction errors for generosity similarly for both human and slot machine partners."
