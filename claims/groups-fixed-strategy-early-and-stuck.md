## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Groups decided on their combination of platforms early and did not change part way through the year, so groups not using Scoop.it early never began using it later. [→ Duncan C 2017](#duncan-c-2017)

## Evidence

### Duncan C 2017

Duncan C, Rengasamy ER, Batchelor S, Pope C, Riley S, Cunningham AM. (2017). Using social media to support small group learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1060-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interim (after case 3) and end-of-year (after case 6) metrics plus facilitator feedback showed the pattern of platform use did not change between analyses: "the groups decided on a strategy early on and stuck with it". Groups using Scoop.it heavily early continued; non-users never began.

> "This suggested that the groups decided on a strategy early on and stuck with it for the remainder of the academic year."
