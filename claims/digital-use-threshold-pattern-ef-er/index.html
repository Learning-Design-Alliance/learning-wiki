Evidence¶

Shi Guochun 2026¶

Shi Guochun, Shi Zimo, Wang Qianqian. (2026). Cross-sectional bidirectional associations between executive function and emotion regulation in adolescents: the moderating role of digital engagement intensity. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05038-8

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Group comparisons stratifying the 368 adolescents into low, moderate, and high tertiles of objectively tracked screen time. Post-hoc Bonferroni tests showed "the high-use group scored significantly lower than both moderate and low-use groups on EF and reappraisal, whereas moderate and low groups did not differ".

"One-way ANOVA revealed significant main effects for both the EF composite (F(2, 365) = 14.87, p < .001, ηp² = 0.08) and cognitive reappraisal (F(2, 365) = 11.23, p < .001, ηp² = 0.06)"
