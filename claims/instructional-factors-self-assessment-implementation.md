## Evidence

### Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan 2023

Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan. (2023). A Systematic Review on Students' Perceptions of Self-Assessment: Usefulness and Factors Influencing Implementation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09799-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of eight studies on practice and training. The review reports, citing Hill (2016), that "the percentage of students planning to per - form the self-assessment increased from 23.1% to 91%" after a self-assessment assignment; Perera et al. (2010) found over 90% self-assessed consistently or frequently after training.

> "After com- pleting the self-assessment assignment, the percentage of students planning to per - form the self-assessment increased from 23.1% to 91% (16)."

### Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan 2023 (2)

Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan. (2023). A Systematic Review on Students' Perceptions of Self-Assessment: Usefulness and Factors Influencing Implementation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09799-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of four studies on environmental support: the review reports, citing Hill (2016), that students were "more willing to self-assess in an environment with incentives for self-assessment"; peer emotional support and competition also encouraged implementation.

> "South African stu- dents reported being more willing to self-assess in an environment with incentives for self-assessment (i.e., 5% of the final marks was allocated for the self-assessment accuracy and the quality of the reflection) (16)."
