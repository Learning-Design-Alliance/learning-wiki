## Evidence

### Harries P 2014

Harries P, Yang H, Davies M, Gilhooly M, Gilhooly K, Thompson C. (2014). Identifying and enhancing risk thresholds in the detection of elder financial abuse: a signal detection analysis of professionals' decision making. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0268-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of 154 novices with 33 experienced professionals judging the same 43 scenarios in the RCT's pre-intervention phase; experts' mean certainty was 70.61 versus novices' 58.04 (Table 3), described as "a lower threshold for abuse identification than the novices".

> "The experts’ mean certainty (of abuse) was higher than the novices’ mean across the first (pre intervention) 28 scenarios, indicating that they had a lower threshold for abuse identification than the novices."
