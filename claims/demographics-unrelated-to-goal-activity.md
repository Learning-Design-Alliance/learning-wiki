# Family demographics were largely unrelated to goal setting, with child gender the only associated variable

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Chi-square analyses found no significant demographic differences between goal and no-goal families except gender, where a higher proportion of girls appeared in the goal subgroup. [→ Patricia H. Manz 2013](#patricia-h-manz-2013)

## Evidence

### Patricia H. Manz 2013

Patricia H. Manz, Jaana Lehtinen, and Catherine Bracaliello. (2013). A Case for Increasing Empirical Attention to Head Start's Home-Based Program: An Exploration of Routine Collaborative Goal Setting. School Community Journal, 23(1). http://eclkc.ohs.acf.hhs.gov/hslc/standards/Head%20Start%20Requirements

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Bifurcated-sample comparisons (n = 42 with goals, n = 31 without) using chi-square and discriminant analysis found no significant differences on caregiver relationship, home language, ethnicity, education, employment, family type, siblings, or child age; only child gender differed, χ2(1) = 9.95, p = 0.002.

> "Gender differences emerged between the two groups; a higher proportion of girls were in the subsample of families who had identified goals (χ2(1) = 9.95, p = 0.002)."
