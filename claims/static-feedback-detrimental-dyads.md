## Evidence

### Sailer 2022

Sailer, Michael; Bauer, Elisabeth; Hofmann, Riikka; Kiesewetter, Jan; Glas, Julia. (2022). Adaptive Feedback from Artificial Neural Networks Facilitates Pre-Service Teachers' Diagnostic Reasoning in Simulation-based Learning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101620

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Within the N = 178 experiment, which manipulated whether learners worked individually or in dyads on computer lab-based simulations, the article reports that "static feedback even had detrimental effects on the learning process in dyads". No effect size is printed in the available text.
