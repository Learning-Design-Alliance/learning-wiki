## Evidence

### Xin et al. 2015

Huaibo Xin, Monica Kempland, Faustina H. Blankson. (2015). ADAPTABILITY AND REPLICABILITY OF WEB-FACILITATED, HYBRID, AND ONLINE LEARNING IN AN UNDERGRADUATE EXERCISE PSYCHOLOGY COURSE. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology – January 2015, volume 14 issue 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057333

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t-tests compared shared activities (two online activities for hybrid/online; two in-class activities for web-facilitated/hybrid). No statistically significant differences were found; equivalence was not tested.

> "Moreover, comparisons were also made between the means of the common activities applied to the web-facilitated and hybrid sections or hybrid and online sections only. No statistically significant differences were indicated."
