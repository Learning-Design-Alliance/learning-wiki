## Evidence

### Wan Chong Choi & Iek Chong Choi 2022

Wan Chong Choi & Iek Chong Choi. (2022). The Influence and Relationship between Computational Thinking, Learning Motivation, Attitude, and Achievement of Code.org in K-12 Programming Education. [doi:10.1145/3578837.3578871](https://doi.org/10.1145/3578837.3578871)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired samples t-test on the PCTS for 20 third-grade students in a one-group pretest-posttest design, reported in the Results section. The article reports a statistically significant increase, with means rising from "3.37 (SD = 0.59)" to "3.88 (SD = 0.51)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, the total scores also reflected significant enhancement, with the pre -test score averaging 3.37 (SD = 0.59) and the post-test score at 3.88 (SD = 0.51). The t-value of -5.77, along with a p-value of less than 0.05"

### Wan Chong Choi & Iek Chong Choi 2022 (2)

Wan Chong Choi & Iek Chong Choi. (2022). The Influence and Relationship between Computational Thinking, Learning Motivation, Attitude, and Achievement of Code.org in K-12 Programming Education. [doi:10.1145/3578837.3578871](https://doi.org/10.1145/3578837.3578871)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same paired t-test analysis, per-dimension results. Computational Perspectives improved significantly but, as the article states, "less pronounced" than the other dimensions (Concepts t = -6.64, Practices t = -4.44); no effect sizes are printed.

> "The Computational Perspectives dimension, although less pronounced, still showed a significant improvement. The pre-test mean score of 3.42 (SD = 0.64) improved to 3.70 (SD = 0.59) in the post -test, with a t-value of -2.44 and a p-value less than 0.05"
