Hempel G, Neef M, Rotzoll D, Heinke W. (2013). Study of medicine 2.0 due to Web 2.0?! - Risks and opportunities for the curriculum in Leipzig. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000854](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000854)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article's observational account of StudiVZ/MeinVZ usage trends among German-speaking students. It draws the lesson that "they can disappear just as rapidly" as they became successful, so meticulously planned Web 2.0 educational projects may come to nothing if students stop using the platform.

> "Since then, however, its significance has declined sharply. The networks have been clearly overtaken by Facebook regarding both the numbers of users and visit duration."
