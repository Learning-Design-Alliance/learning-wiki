## Evidence

### Sarıköse Seda 2026

Sarıköse Seda, Sevim Fatma, Alık Şebnem, Karaçay Pelin, Yaşar Özgen. (2026). Integrating Insights: A Mixed‐Methods Approach to Nurses' Competencies in Evidence‐Based Practice and Clinical Decision‐Making. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70525

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate t-tests and one-way ANOVA across 387 nurses (Table 3). Only test statistics are printed for these contrasts, so no effect size is reported and, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The competence and CDM levels towards EBPs of nurses who work in shift system [(EBP‐COQ, t = 5.221; p < 0.001) and (CDMNS, t = 3.895; p < 0.001)] or who work 48 h or more per week [(EBP‐COQ, F = 16.979; p < 0.001) and (CDMNS, F = 4.430; p = 0.013)] are statistically significantly lower."
