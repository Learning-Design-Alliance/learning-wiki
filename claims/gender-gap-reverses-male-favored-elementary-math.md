## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean i-Ready scale score differences initially favor girls in kindergarten and Grade 1 fall, then reverse and increasingly favor boys from Grade 2 onward, widening in magnitude in later grades. [→ Kadir Bahar 2026](#kadir-bahar-2026)

## Evidence

### Kadir Bahar 2026

Kadir Bahar. (2026). Longitudinal Analysis of Gender Disparities in Mathematics Achievement among Elementary Students. E4 Center. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2026/02/18/longitudinal-analysis-of-gender-disparities-in-mathematics-achievement-among-elementary-students/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal analysis of two cohorts (8,361 and 9,325 students) tracked 2017-2018 to 2021-2022 using i-Ready math scale scores. Kindergarten spring showed a small non-significant female advantage (DM = 0.38, p = .353); by Grade 4 spring Cohort 1 showed "DM= -3.47, p <.001".

> "From Grade 2 onward, all recorded mean differences in i-Ready mathematics scale scores are negative, indicating that mean scores increasingly favor boys at each observed time point."
