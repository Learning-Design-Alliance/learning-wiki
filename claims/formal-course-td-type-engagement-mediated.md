## Evidence

### Connolly 2018

Connolly, M. R., Lee, Y.-G., & Savoy, J. N. (2018). The Effects of Doctoral Teaching Development on Early-Career STEM Scholars' College Teaching Self-efficacy. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

OLS regression on TD type (Table 2A, y-standardized coefficients with Bonferroni correction). "Participants in formal courses were significantly more confident than nonparticipants" in two dimensions; coefficients and p-values are printed, no standardized effect size.

> "Participants in formal courses were significantly more confident than nonparticipants in course planning (0.317, p < 0.001 in M1) and teaching methods (0.367, p < 0.001 in M2)."

### Connolly 2018 (2)

Connolly, M. R., Lee, Y.-G., & Savoy, J. N. (2018). The Effects of Doctoral Teaching Development on Early-Career STEM Scholars' College Teaching Self-efficacy. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-02-0039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Simultaneous estimation of engagement and type (Table 2B) shows type coefficients shrink to non-significance while engagement remains significant in five of six dimensions, indicating "the effects of TD type are mostly mediated by TD engagement."

> "The significant effects of TD type on course planning and teaching methods in Table 2A were mostly explained by the inclusion of TD engagement in Table 2B. This finding suggests that the effects of TD type are mostly mediated by TD engagement."
