Correlational analysis (Table 2) of teacher ratings against PSP-1 results for 412 children. The article reports the strongest correlations in "teachers' ratings of students' language skills (0.305, p ≤ 0.01) and reading ability (0.239, p ≤ 0.01)" with the PSP-1 total score.
