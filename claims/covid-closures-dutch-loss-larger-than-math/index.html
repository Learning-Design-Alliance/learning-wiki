Evidence¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte. (2020). The Effect of School Closures on Standardised Student Test Outcomes. KU Leuven Faculty of Economics and Business Discussion Paper Series DPS20.17. https://www.kuleuven.be/dps20.17

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Difference-in-differences estimation on the same Flemish school-level panel (2015-2020) with the full control set; the printed effect size is 0.26 standard deviations (d = 0.26, and 0.29 SD against 2019 alone). Dutch losses were "slightly larger, that is more negative, for Dutch scores than for the mathematics scores" in all specifications.

"In the speciﬁcation with all control variables and the sample of all six years, the 2020 cohort has a decrease in Dutch scores with 0.26 standard deviations, signiﬁcant at the 1%-level."
