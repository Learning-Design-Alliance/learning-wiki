### J. C. B 1917

J. C. B. (1917). Recent Publications in History. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The final chapter of Bagley and Rugg's monograph, which the reviewer calls "The Hall of Fame," counts how frequently public men are mentioned in school textbooks. Printed frequencies use Lincoln as base 100: "Washington comes next with 84, Jefferson 82, Jackson 49."

> "Of those most prominent ia civil life, Lincoln heads the list and if we take his frequency as 100, Washington comes next with 84, Jefferson 82, Jackson 49, John Adams 46, and Madison 40."

> "In military affairs Washington heads the list, and with him as the basis of comparison, R. E. Lee comes next with 83, Grant 76, W. T. Sherman 48, Cornwallis 42, McClellan 41."
