Dallavis 2021¶

Dallavis, J., Kuhfeld, M., Tarasawa, B., & Ponisciak, S. (2021). Achievement growth in K-8 Catholic schools using NWEA data. Journal of Catholic Education, 24(2). http://dx.doi.org/10.15365/joce.2402012021

In the national K-8 MAP Growth sector comparison, the study finds a grade-moderated growth pattern: in earlier grades, Catholic school students "show less growth in both reading and math during the academic year" than public school peers. No effect sizes are printed in the available text.
