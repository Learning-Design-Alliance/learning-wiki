## Subclaims
`q2 i?` SES does not significantly predict participation in privately organized visual art activities (β = 0.04, p = .067) but does predict parent-supported participation (β = 0.19, p < .001). [→ Deer Genman 2024](#deer-genman-2024)

## Evidence

### Deer Genman 2024

Deer Genman, Wu Hao, Zhang Li, Tadesse Endale, Khalid Sabika, Duan Congyu, Tian Wang, Gao Chunhai. (2024). Effect of out-of-school visual art activities on academic performance. The mediating role of socioeconomic status. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0298901

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM path analysis on the 1624-student Guizhou survey found SES did not predict VAP1 (β = 0.04, p = .067) while it did predict VAP2 (β = 0.19, p < .001), a finding the authors call unexpected against prior Chinese literature.

> "the results determined that SES does not influence the likelihood of children participating in OOSVAP1 (β = 0.04, p = .067). Nevertheless, SES predicts the participation of children in OOSVAP2 (β = 0.19, p < .001)."
