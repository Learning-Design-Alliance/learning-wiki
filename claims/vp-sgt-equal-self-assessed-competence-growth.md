## Evidence

### Seifert Lukas B. 2019

Seifert Lukas B., Socolan Octavian, Sader Robert, Rüsseler Miriam, Sterz Jasmina. (2019). Virtual patients versus small-group teaching in the training of oral and maxillofacial surgery: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1887-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-assessment questionnaire of 13 items on a 6-point Likert scale administered before and directly after the intervention in the randomized trial. Both groups improved significantly; "No significant differences in self-assessed competence could be found after the intervention". No effect size printed.

> "In the self-assessment questionnaire both the VP group and the SGT group rated their competence regarding the predetermined learning objective significantly higher than before the intervention (See Table 1). No significant differences in self-assessed competence could be found after the intervention."
