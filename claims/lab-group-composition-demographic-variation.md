## Evidence

### Kontro 2020

Kontro, I.; Génois, M. (2020). Combining surveys and sensors to explore student behaviour. [doi:10.3390/educsci10030068](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10030068)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative analysis of strongly connected groups in sections 2 and 5 during week 3 (Figure 9), with demographics coded by major, year and gender. Section 2 was "a particularly homogeneous section which consisted mainly of physics majors in their first year"; section 5's other strongly connected groups were heterogeneous in major, year and gender.

> "In week 3, the students formed three strongly connected single-gender groups (two female, one male group) and only one mixed-gender group (Figure 9)."
