Physical argument (Section II.B): a hypothetical monopole wave would be emitted by a pulsating spherical charge distribution; time-reversal symmetry implies such a wave is necessarily longitudinal, but the monopole piece of the field is known to be non-radiative. Students meet the underlying shell argument in Newtonian gravity.
