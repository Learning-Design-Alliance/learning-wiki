Analysis of coded adult talk moves (asks, calls attention, invites prediction, extends, checks, connects, other) from Cohort 1 observations. The report states "intervention families tended to use two talk moves, asks a question and checks for understanding , more frequently than comparison families," with the difference more pronounced after viewing.
