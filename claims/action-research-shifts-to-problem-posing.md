## Evidence

### Razfar 2011

Razfar, A. (2011). Action Research in Urban Schools: Empowerment, Transformation, and Challenges. Teacher Education Quarterly, 38(4), 25-44. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ960617

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of focus group discussions with seven urban educators after completion of their action research projects. The article reports that a 'problem posing' orientation emerged as opposed to a 'problem-solving' approach, quoting one teacher who said her project 'helped me to think critically about how the students actually make sense of things rather than following the scripted curriculum.'

> "TRs recognized the limitations of trying to 'fix' and 'solve' problems with myopic remedies and 'one-size fits all solutions.'"
