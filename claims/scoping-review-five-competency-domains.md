`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review following Joanna Briggs Institute guidelines, searching PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science and Google Scholar plus manual searches of nine universities' programme pages; 623 documents identified, 44 full-text reviewed, six articles and nine curricula included. Descriptive qualitative content analysis produced the domains.
