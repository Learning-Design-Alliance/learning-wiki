## Evidence

### Hoffman M 2014

Hoffman M, Wilkinson JE, Xu J, Wiecha J. (2014). The perceived effects of faculty presence vs. absence on small-group learning and group dynamics: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0258-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired quasi-experimental survey comparison of the same students' 'in' and 'out' case ratings (n = 114 pairs): students were 'more likely to express a preference for having the faculty member present after “in” case vs. “out” case discussions' (p < 0.001; preference item mean 3.5 vs 3.1).
