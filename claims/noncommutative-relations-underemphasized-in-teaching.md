## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Commutative quantities are taught extensively and elementarily, whereas non-commutative quantities are mostly treated at an abstract, cognitively demanding and non-illustrative level. [→ Martin Erik Horn 2006](#martin-erik-horn-2006)

## Evidence

### Martin Erik Horn 2006

Martin Erik Horn. (2006). The Didactical Relevance of the Pauli Pascal Triangle. GDCP Annual Conference, Berne. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0611277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0611277)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical-didactical argument in the paper's section on introduction modes: basic arithmetic instruction simultaneously teaches commutative relations, while "the presentation of non-commutative quantities mostly has abstract, cognitively demanding and very often not illustrative standards". No empirical data are reported.

> "While commutative quantities are introduced at a very basic and elementary level, the presentation of non-commutative quantities mostly has abstract, cognitively demanding and very often not illustrative standards."
