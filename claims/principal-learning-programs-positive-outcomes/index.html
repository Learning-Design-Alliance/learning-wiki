Evidence¶

Darling-Hammond 2022¶

Darling-Hammond, L., Wechsler, M. E., Levin, S., Leung-Gagné, M., & Tozer, S. (2022). Developing effective principals: What kind of learning matters? [Report]. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/641.201

q2 · i? · review · r3

The report's synthesis of 104 peer-reviewed studies published 2000–2021, coded for methods, design, population, program details, and findings, concludes that "high-quality principal preparation and professional development programs are associated with positive principal, teacher, and student outcomes". Not all studies found correlations; many had design limitations.

"A growing body of literature indicates that high-quality principal preparation and professional development programs are associated with positive principal, teacher, and student outcomes, ranging from principals' feelings of preparedness and their engagement in more effective practices to stronger teacher retention and improved student achievement."
