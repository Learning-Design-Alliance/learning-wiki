Word-frequency analysis of 103 articles on educational digital games for children (2007–2017), tallied with Python scripts. The analysis found "different descrip tions were applied for the concept of educational digital games" across the corpus.

"The results reve aled that different descrip tions were applied for the concept of educational digital games (e.g., digital game s, computer games, game-based learning, video games, and serious games)."
