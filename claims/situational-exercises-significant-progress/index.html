Evidence¶

Gallagher 1976¶

Gallagher, W. K. (1976). Facilitating the Immigrant's Learning a Second Language in the Classroom. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED136593

q2 · i? · causal · r1

This is the article's first stated conclusion from the limited experimental research at Brigham Young University Hawaii, where thirty adult students spent fifteen weeks in a planned informal immersion environment. The article reports significance without printing an effect size, so no magnitude is reported.

"Situational communication exercises such as language games, interviews, dialogues, and skits were shown to be sufficient in making statistically significant progress in both structural and communication skills."
