## Evidence

### Huang Pin-Hsiang 2024

Huang Pin-Hsiang, Arianpoor Arash, Taylor Silas, Gonzales Jenzel, Shulruf Boaz. (2024). Insights into undergraduate medical student selection tools: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.22

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 67 included studies yielding 237 effect sizes, with statistics converted to Cohen's d. The printed pooled effect sizes were d = 0.697 (early program academic), d = 0.619 (end of program academic), and d = 0.545 (end of program clinical exams), as the article reports that "prior academic achievement best predicts academic results".

> "Findings show that prior academic achievement best predicts academic results for early program and end of program time points with effect sizes of 0.697 (95% CI, 0.533 to 0.861) and 0.619 (95% CI, 0.533 to 0.705), respectively, as well as performance on end of program OSCE/OSLER/clinical exams (0.545; 95% CI, 0.235 to 0.855)."
