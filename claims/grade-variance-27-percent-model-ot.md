## Evidence

### Bonsaksen T. 2021

Bonsaksen T., Magne T. A., Stigen L., Gramstad A., Åsli L., Mørk G., Johnson S. G., Carstensen T. (2021). Associations between occupational therapy students’ academic performance and their study approaches and perceptions of the learning environment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02940-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression variance decomposition (n = 174): Block 1 explained 11.5%, Block 2 R² change 4.7% (p = 0.06), Block 3 R² change 11.2% (p < 0.001), total 27.5%. The authors note a substantial variance proportion remains unexplained.

> "The full model explained 27.5 % of the variance in exam grades, with the sociodemographic variables and the study approach scales accounting for similar proportions (11.5 % and 11.2 %, respectively)."
