# In Experiment 2, norm inference remained consistent with Bayesian updating in the presence of deviant behaviors, though 25% norm-consistent behavior pushed posteriors below 0.5

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Posterior probabilities increased with the proportion of norm-consistent behaviors even with deviant behaviors present (ηp2 = 0.90). [→ Duan 2026](#duan-2026)
`q3 i?` When only 25% of agents exhibited norm-consistent behaviors, posteriors were significantly below 0.5; at 75% and 100% they were significantly above 0.5. [→ Duan 2026](#duan-2026)

## Evidence

### Duan 2026

Duan, J., Guo, X., Zheng, L. & Yin, J. (2026). A Bayesian perspective on observers' inference of group norms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00405-x

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 (N = 32) introduced deviant behaviors as negative evidence; the reported ANOVA gave "ηp2 = 0.90" with adjacent-condition ds > 1.79. At 25% norm-consistent behavior posteriors fell below 0.5 (t (31) = -8.06, d = -1.42); at 50% they did not differ from 0.5; at 75% and 100% they exceeded 0.5 (ds > 2.61).

> "One-way repeated measures ANOVA revealed a significant main effect for the proportion of norm-consistent behaviors, F (1.40, 43.39) = 287.92, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.90."
