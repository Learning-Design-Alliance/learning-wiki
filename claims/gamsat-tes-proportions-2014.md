Mercer Annette, Crotty Brendan, Alldridge Louise, Le Luc, Vele Veronica. (2015). GAMSAT: A 10-year retrospective overview, with detailed analysis of candidates’ performance in 2014. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0316-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Analysis of high-achieving 2014 candidates (Table 4), where a TES is an overall score of at least 60 with at least 50 on each section. Observed/expected ratios: human biosciences 1.24, Honours 1.19, Masters 0.62, all health-related 0.69.

> "Overall, 36.9% of candidates (3434 of 9307) achieved a TES in 2014; 31.2% of females and 43.6% of males. The proportion of candidates achieving a TES decreased with increasing age."
