## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The language used in teaching and the amount of L1 depend on students' proficiency in the target language; switching to L1 for low-proficient learners is considered a strategy to reduce anxiety and provide a comfortable environment. [→ Yıldız 2021](#yldz-2021)

## Evidence

### Yıldız 2021

Yıldız, M. (2021). Language choices of language teachers and learners: Meta-synthesis of qualitative research. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 17(Special Issue 1), 472-492. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1285162

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis finding drawing on studies including Cho & Kim (2016), Kurata (2014), Moore (2013), Promnath & Tayjasanant (2016), and Willans (2011). The article quotes a teacher: "How much you switch to Thai depends on the students' level of proficiency". Qualitative evidence; no effect size printed.

> "Therefore, for low -proficient learners who may have trouble understanding the instructions of their teacher and producing language to respond to those instructions, switching to the first language is considered a n excellent strategy to reduce their anxiety and thus provide them a comfortable environment."
