### Jesper Bruun 2019

Jesper Bruun, Pia J. Ray, Linda Udby. (2019). Network analyses of student engagement with online textbook problems. Preprint submitted to Computers & Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1903.11390](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1903.11390)

Interpretation of cluster mean scores and standard errors on each component. Group A clusters are "very linear"; Group B shows linearity with detours interpreted as exploration; Group C scores highly on Complexity and is quite diverse on other components.

> "Based on cluster scores on Complexity, we divided the ten clusters into three groups, each of which represent a different level of complexity."
