Between-group comparisons in the quasi-experiment used the Student's T test for initial achievement and the Mann-Whitney U test for post-test achievement. The article reports "no significant differences were found in the achievement percentages when comparing CG and EG"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Figure 6 shows that no significant differences were found in the achievement percentages when comparing CG and EG. In this sense, the levels initially obtained by each group are not different, nor are the results at the end of the course."
