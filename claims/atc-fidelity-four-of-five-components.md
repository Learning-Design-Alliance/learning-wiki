## Evidence

### Wolf 2016

Wolf, B., Latham, G., Laurenzano, M., Ross, S. M., Tam, W., & Cheung, A. C. K. (2016). Evaluation of Around the Corner: Funded by the Investing in Innovation Fund (i3) Grant #112209. Center for Research and Reform in Education, Johns Hopkins University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED574582

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Implementation study using school ratings, snapshots, and visit reports collected by SFA across the six treatment schools; component-level fidelity scores were 100% for the four measured components in both years.

> "Regarding fidelity of ATC implementation, we found that in both implementation school years (2014-15 and 2015-16), the program was implemented with fidelity in four out of five program components: training of SFA coaches, teacher professional development, communication meetings for principals and program facilitators, and distribution of ATC materials to teachers."
