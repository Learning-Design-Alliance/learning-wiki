# Bodily activation with gestures and poses deepened student teachers' understanding of physics concepts conveyed in a digitized dance-physics performance

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In a seminar-type classroom experiment, activation of bodies with gestures and poses deepened the students' and teacher's understandings of what took place in the digitized performance video. [→ Carol Marie Webster 2022](#carol-marie-webster-2022)

## Evidence

### Carol Marie Webster 2022

Carol Marie Webster, Panagiotis Pantidos, DeNapoli Clarke and Jiannis K. Pachos. (2022). Break-in' Point: Somatic Narratives - the convergence of arts and science in the transformation of temporal communities. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2208.08441](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2208.08441)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Case experiment with one teacher (A4) and three students in a science-education seminar; they paused the video at physics-relevant moments and explained with their bodies. The article reports "activation of bodies, with gestures and poses" deepened understandings; no effect size given.

> "Through activation of bodies, with gestures and poses, the students and teacher (A4) deepened understandings of what took place in the video"

### Carol Marie Webster 2022 (2)

Carol Marie Webster, Panagiotis Pantidos, DeNapoli Clarke and Jiannis K. Pachos. (2022). Break-in' Point: Somatic Narratives - the convergence of arts and science in the transformation of temporal communities. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2208.08441](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2208.08441)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

In the transferability stage, students pulled a sheet of paper in both directions to experience equilibrium as equal but opposite forces, having "transferred it to the physical space of the classroom producing a brand new bodily form"; no test reported.

> "grasped the idea of mechanical equilibrium introduced in the digitized space, transferred it to the physical space of the classroom producing a brand new bodily form (i.e., pulling the paper)"
