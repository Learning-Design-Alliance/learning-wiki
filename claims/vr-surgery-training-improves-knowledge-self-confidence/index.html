q3 · i? · review · r3

Multisite, single-blind, parallel randomized controlled trial (study [98]) in which the study group used a virtual reality surgery application with the LMC and Oculus Rift while the control group received similar content in a standard presentation; 95 residents from 7 dental schools. The review reports that "Immersive virtual reality experiences improve the knowledge and self-confidence of the surgical residents".
