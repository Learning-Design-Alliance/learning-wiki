## Evidence

### Jimmie Leppink & Patricia Pérez-Fuster 2019

Jimmie Leppink & Patricia Pérez-Fuster. (2019). Mental Effort, Workload, Time on Task, and Certainty: Beyond Linear Models. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-09460-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Five competing fixed-effects logistic regression models on the Blissett et al. (2018) task-level data (binary correct/incorrect performance); Model L1 (certainty only) was preferred, and mental effort in Model L3 was not significant: "B = − 0.073, p = 0.395".

> "Both AIC and BIC prefer Model L1, and from the differences in R2McF it is easy to understand why. Model L1 explains considerably more than Model L2 (difference in R2McF of about 0.069), and next to certainty— going from Model L1 to Model L3— mental effort adds almost nothing (difference in R2McF of about 0.002). In Model L3, we find for mental effort: B = − 0.073, p = 0.395, and 95% CI = [− 0.243; 0.096]."
