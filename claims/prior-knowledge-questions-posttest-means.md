## Evidence

### Hanna Onyi Yusuf & Sadiq Mohammed 2013

Hanna Onyi Yusuf & Sadiq Mohammed. (2013). Influence of Prior Knowledge Questions on Pupils' Performance in Reading Comprehension in Primary Schools in Kaduna, Nigeria. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 4(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.4n.1p.134

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive post-test comparison in the quasi-experiment: experimental mean 78.50 (SD 12.12) versus control mean 51.50 (SD 7.80), 30 pupils per group. The authors attribute the difference "probably" to the prior knowledge questions, which the control group was not exposed to.

> "Table 2 shows that the mean and standard deviation of the experimental group appears to be higher than that of the control group. This is probably because of the prior knowledge questions."
