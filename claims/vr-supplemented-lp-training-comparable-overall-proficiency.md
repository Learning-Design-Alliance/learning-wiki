`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 88 medical students randomly assigned to CT (N=45, 30 min conventional) or VRT (N=43, 20 min conventional plus 10 min VR); blinded neurologists scored an independent simulator-based LP. "The total number of key points completed did not differ significantly" with a mean difference (VRT − CT) of −0.50 points (95% CI: −1.41 to 0.42).

> "The total number of key points completed did not differ significantly between the VRT and CT groups (VRT group vs CT group: 7.79 ± 2.23 vs 8.29 ± 2.08, P = 0.282)"
