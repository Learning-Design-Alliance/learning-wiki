# Learning Partnerships workshops were rated by medical students as highly useful for improving skills and confidence in communicating with adolescents about sensitive health issues

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-workshop survey ratings for skills and understanding items were high, with the mean 7.72 or higher and top items such as applying the HEADSS tool rated 8.41. [→ Cahill Helen 2015](#cahill-helen-2015)

## Evidence

### Cahill Helen 2015

Cahill Helen, Coffey Julia, Sanci Lena. (2015). ‘I wouldn’t get that feedback from anywhere else’: learning partnerships and the use of high school students as simulated patients to enhance medical students’ communication skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0315-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-workshop paper surveys completed by 164 medical students ranked usefulness out of 10; descriptive means with standard deviations were computed in SPSS. The article reports "The mean was 7.72 or higher" across skills items, with the HEADSS item and the confidentiality item both at mean 8.41. No inferential tests are reported.

> "The mean was 7.72 or higher for all ‘skills and understanding’ items on the survey, with ‘Understand importance of informing young people about confidentiality’ (mean 8.41), ‘Knowledge of how to apply the HEADSS psycho-social screening tool’ (mean 8.41)"

### Cahill Helen 2015 (2)

Cahill Helen, Coffey Julia, Sanci Lena. (2015). ‘I wouldn’t get that feedback from anywhere else’: learning partnerships and the use of high school students as simulated patients to enhance medical students’ communication skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0315-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of five post-workshop focus groups with 39 medical students; students said "it was ok to get it wrong because we were in such a supportive environment" and valued feedback such as "I got told ‘you probably need to smile more!’". Qualitative, no effect sizes.

> "Respondents explained that they particularly valued the supportive and experimental learning space which provided opportunity to try out different communication strategies, and to re-play after receiving coaching."
