## Evidence

### Andersen NL 2021

Andersen NL, Jensen RO, Posth S, Laursen CB, Jørgensen R, Graumann O. (2021). Teaching ultrasound-guided peripheral venous catheter placement through immersive virtual reality: An explorative pilot study. Medicine. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8270624/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The review reports, citing Yoganathan et al, a randomized controlled trial in which 40 surgical trainees' reef knot tying improved significantly more after a 15-minute IVR lesson than after video demonstration, with subsequent face-to-face teaching not compensating for the difference.

> "This study found that 40 surgical trainees' ability to tie a single-handed reef knot improved significantly after a 15-minute IVR lesson compared to after watching a video demonstration and again after subsequent face-to-face teaching with a blinded instructor."
