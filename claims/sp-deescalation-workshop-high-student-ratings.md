## Evidence

### Shenai Neeta MD 2024

Shenai Neeta MD, Fulmer Valerie, Gowl Catherine MFA, See Jordan MD, Peterson Ryan MD, Van Deusen Reed MD. (2024). A Verbal De-escalation Standardized Patient Workshop for Third- and Fourth-Year Medical Students. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11417

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation-form data from 139 participating clerkship students across 12 sessions, with 125 submitting feedback. The article reports "102 of 125 students (82%) strongly agreed" objectives were met and "86% assigned the letter grade A"; no effect size is printed.

> "On a 5-item Likert scale ranging from strongly agree to strongly disagree, 102 of 125 students (82%) strongly agreed, 20 (16%) agreed, two (2%) were neutral, and one (1%) disagreed that the workshop met the stated learning objectives. One hundred eight students (86%) assigned the letter grade A for educational quality"
