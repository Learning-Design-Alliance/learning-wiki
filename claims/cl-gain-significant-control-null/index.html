Evidence¶

Min Han 2015¶

Min Han. (2015). An Empirical Study on the Application of Cooperative Learning to English Listening Classes. English Language Teaching, 8(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n3p177

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-test within the experiment class (N=52): pretest mean 59.58, posttest mean 64.54, t reported as -5.909 with Sig. 0.000. The article reports "improved by nearly 5 points"; no effect size is printed.

"Compared with the scores in the pretest, the mean score in the postte st is improved by nearly 5 points for the experiment class. The significance level is 0.000"

Min Han 2015 (2)¶

Min Han. (2015). An Empirical Study on the Application of Cooperative Learning to English Listening Classes. English Language Teaching, 8(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n3p177

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-test within the control class (N=53): pretest mean 58.68, posttest mean 59.09, Sig. 0.449. The article concludes students under traditional teaching "make no progress in their listening skills" in this research; no effect size is printed.

"For the control class, the mean score in the posttest is hi gher than that of the pretest, yet the significance level is 0.449 (p>0.05), consequently, these two scores have no obvious difference."
