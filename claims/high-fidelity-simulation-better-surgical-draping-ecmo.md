Exploratory sub-item analysis (Fisher's exact test) in the randomized trial found a significant difference in surgical draping success favoring the high-fidelity group (86.7% vs 33.3%, "p = 0.01").

> "the high-fidelity simulation group demonstrated significantly better performance on surgical draping of the two scarpas, with 86.7% of participants achieving success compared to only 33.3% in the procedural simulation workshop group (p = 0.01)"
