### Mutmainna 2026

Exploring Students’ perceptions of their learning experience and self-efficacy in physics online class with project-based learning: An interpretative phenomenological analysis. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.13251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.13251)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Participant interview statement from the IPA findings on teacher monitoring of assignments. Indi (self-described impatient coordinator) believed individual checking of per-progress assignments reduces plagiarism; "the possibility of plagiarism is small" is her own perception, not a measured outcome.

> "if it is per progress or there is a schedule, the possibility of plagiarism is small because it is checked one by one by the teacher."

### Mutmainna 2026 (2)

Mutmainna, Edi Istiyono, Haryanto, Beta Wulan Febriana. (2026). Exploring Students’ perceptions of their learning experience and self-efficacy in physics online class with project-based learning: An interpretative phenomenological analysis. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.13251](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.13251)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Participant interview statement from the IPA findings on instructor commenting in synchronous online sessions. Cinta, who described herself as socially reserved, reported "both a pleasant and an unpleasant feeling" and described feeling cornered and embarrassed before classmates when her work was commented on publicly.

> "When the instructor comments on my work, for me there is both a pleasant and an unpleasant feeling"
