### González-Abós C 2026

González-Abós C, Reyes LG, Lorenzo C, Ausania F. (2026). MyIntuitive telemetry identifies proficiency thresholds in robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12591-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Supportive descriptive telemetry from a video-defined resection-phase window (Kocherisation to mesopancreas dissection completion) in the single-surgeon series. The article reports the decrease as "323 vs. 215 min; P = 0.002"; the resection time proportion rose from 32 to 37% without significance.

> "The assessment of Resection-phase telemetry (Landmark-R) showed that time to complete mesopancreas dissection decreased across the curve (323 vs. 215 min; P = 0.002)"
