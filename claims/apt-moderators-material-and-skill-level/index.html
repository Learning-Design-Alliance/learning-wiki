McManus 2015¶

McManus, M. M., & Aiken, R. M. (2015). Supporting Effective Collaboration: Using a Rearview Mirror to Look Forward. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 26, 365–377. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0068-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of Adamson et al.'s experiments on the Bazaar platform, where an automated Academically Productive Talk system gave positive feedback in collaborative chat. The article reports the system's effectiveness 'depends on several factors, such as the type of the material and the skill level of each student', and that 'Review material and a higher student skill level normally yielded better results in learning.'
