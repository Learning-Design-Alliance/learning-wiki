## Evidence

### Duncan C 2017

Duncan C, Rengasamy ER, Batchelor S, Pope C, Riley S, Cunningham AM. (2017). Using social media to support small group learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1060-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey and facilitator feedback on wiki use during year 1 CBL found "Use of the wiki was less widespread, largely because many found it difficult to use". Individuals found it useful for revision because group work was available in a directory-like format, unlike the chronological layout of Facebook.

> "Use of the wiki was less widespread, largely because many found it difficult to use. Some groups adopted it enthusiastically, and found it a useful way to record their agreed learning outcomes and share photos taken of flipcharts in the face-to-face sessions."
