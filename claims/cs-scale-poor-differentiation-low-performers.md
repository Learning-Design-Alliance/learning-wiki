Piumatti Giovanni, Cerutti Bernard, Perron Noëlle Junod. (2021). Assessing communication skills during OSCE: need for integrated psychometric approaches. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02552-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Conclusion drawn from the combined EFA, MGCFA and IRT results on 296 students' OSCE communication scores. The discussion states every student tends to score high on communication and that new or modified items should tap low levels of CS to avoid ceiling effects.

> "Using this four-item set in its current form it may be difficult to adequately differentiate between students who are poor in CS from those who perform better."
