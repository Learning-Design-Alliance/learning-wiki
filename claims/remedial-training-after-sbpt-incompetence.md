## Evidence

### Kodikara Kaumudee 2023

Kodikara Kaumudee, Seneviratne Thilanka, Premaratna Ranjan. (2023). Pre-clerkship procedural training in venipuncture: a prospective cohort study on skills acquisition and durability. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04722-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the immediate post-training assessment, four students refrained from performing after three attempts and were rated incompetent. The article reports that after remedial training, "three were rated borderline, and one was rated competent when assessed by the same independent assessor".

> "Interestingly, four students (7.27%) were rated incompetent to perform venipuncture following simulation-based training."
