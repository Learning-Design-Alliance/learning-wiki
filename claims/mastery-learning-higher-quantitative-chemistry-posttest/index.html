Evidence¶

Telimoye Leesi Mitee and Georgina N. Obaitan 2015¶

Telimoye Leesi Mitee and Georgina N. Obaitan. (2015). Effect of Mastery Learning on Senior Secondary School Students' Cognitive Learning Outcome in Quantitative Chemistry. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.5. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083639

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental control group study: four schools randomly assigned, 207 mastery learning students and 194 controls, two weeks of treatment, independent sample t-test on a 25-item chemistry achievement test. The article reports "The effect size is 0.60" and t(399) = 14.92, P<0.05.
