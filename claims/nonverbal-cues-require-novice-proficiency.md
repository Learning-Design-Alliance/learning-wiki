Qualitative synthesis of the 20 active nonverbal cueing excerpts, drawing on medical-domain articles and Leff et al. (2015), where trainee fixations under gaze guidance were more localised to the nodule and GL was significantly shorter in Collaborative Gaze Control mode. Ong et al. document a trainee missing an expert's subtle independence message.

> "It appears that some novice proficiency is required for active nonverbal cues, without verbal support, to be effective."
