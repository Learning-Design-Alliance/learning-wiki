## Evidence

### Hertanti Nuzul Sri 2026

Hertanti Nuzul Sri, Nguyen Nga Thi Thuy, Chi Do Thi Kim, Chuang Yeu-Hui. (2026). Psychometric evaluation of the Indonesian version of the confidence scale among primary healthcare nurses in dengue-related prevention practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09471-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group CFA across education levels (n = 321 Diploma; n = 137 Bachelor's or higher) tested configural, metric, and scalar invariance; ΔCFI values of 0.000 and 0.001 and ΔSRMR values of 0.009 and 0.002 met the thresholds, so "ΔCFI and ΔSRMR remained within the recommended thresholds."

> "In the present study, ΔCFI and ΔSRMR remained within the recommended thresholds, indicating acceptable scalar invariance."
