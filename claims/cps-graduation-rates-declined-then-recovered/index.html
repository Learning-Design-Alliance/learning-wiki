Montgomery 2010¶

Montgomery, N., & Allensworth, E. M., with Correa, M. (2010). Passing Through Science: The Effects of Raising Graduation Requirements in Science on Course-Taking and Academic Achievement in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/passing-through-science-effects-raising-graduation-requirements-science-course-taking

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Cohort comparison of administrative graduation records across successive ninth-grade entry cohorts, with statistical controls for incoming student characteristics. The decline was temporary, recovering almost to pre-policy levels within five years.

"They declined by four percentage points in the first year of the policy and another percentage point in the next year, after accounting for changes in the backgrounds and prior achievement of students entering CPS high schools. Three years after the policy change, however, graduation rates began recovering; the rates were almost at pre-policy levels after five years."
