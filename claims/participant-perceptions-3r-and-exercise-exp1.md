## Evidence

### Loprinzi Paul D. 2019

Loprinzi Paul D., Harris Faith, McRaney Kyle, Chism Morgan, Deming Raymond, Jones Timothy, Zou Liye, Tan Miaoqing. (2019). Effects of Acute Exercise and Learning Strategy Implementation on Memory Function. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina55090568

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive survey data from Experiment 1: 3-R participants rated ease of implementation at mean 4.23 (0.86) and usefulness at mean 4.25 (0.70); exercise groups rated exercise usefulness at mean 3.76 (0.83), just under the agree threshold. No effect sizes for these descriptive ratings beyond printed d values for group comparisons.

> "The mean (SD) score was 4.23 (0.86), suggesting that participants who were randomized into the groups that implemented this technique "agreed" that this technique was easy to implement."
