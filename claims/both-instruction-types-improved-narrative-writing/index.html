Evidence¶

Balaman 2018¶

Balaman, S. (2018). Digital storytelling: A multimodal narrative writing genre. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(3), 202-212. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1193109

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Follow-up GLM ANOVA within the control group taught through traditional writing practices. The article reports the group "significantly scored higher at the post-test compared to the pretest" with partial η2 = .802 and MD = 7.60.

"Follow-up ANOVAs indicated a significant difference between the control group’s pre-and post-test writing scores, F (1,19) = 77.067, p < .001, partial η2 = .802, meaning that the control group significantly scored higher at the post-test compared to the pretest"

Balaman 2018 (2)¶

Balaman, S. (2018). Digital storytelling: A multimodal narrative writing genre. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(3), 202-212. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1193109

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Follow-up GLM ANOVA within the experimental group receiving DS-integrated narrative writing instruction. The article reports the group "increased their scores from the pre-test to the post-test significantly" with partial η2 = .891 and MD = 14.48.

"A significant difference in experimental group’s pre -and posttest results was also elicited, F (1, 22) = 179.247, p < .001, partial η2 = .891."
