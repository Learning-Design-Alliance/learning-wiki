Webb, N. M. (1993). Collaborative Group Versus Individual Assessment in Mathematics: Processes and Outcomes. Educational Assessment, 7(2), 131-152. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326977ea0102_3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparisons of group versus individual performance on nine scored components of decimal-cost problems for 53 seventh-graders, reported in Table 1. The article reports the difference as "significantly lower in the individual setting than in the group setting (p < .01)" with group means near ceiling (e.g., .94 and .97 on total problems).

> "On every component of the problem, as well as for the total problem, mean performance was significantly lower in the individual setting than in the group setting (p < .01). Performance in group work was close to the ceiling of 1.00."
