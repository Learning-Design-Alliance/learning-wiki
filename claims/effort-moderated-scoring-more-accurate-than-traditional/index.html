Modeling student test-taking motivation in the context of an adaptive achievement test 2016¶

Modeling student test-taking motivation in the context of an adaptive achievement test. (2016). Journal of Educational Measurement, 53(1), 86–105. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/modeling-student-test-taking-motivation-in-the-context-of-an-adaptive-achievement-test/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The study's comparison of scoring approaches on adaptive achievement test data found effort-moderated scoring "yield scores that were more accurate than those found under traditional scoring." No effect size or accuracy statistic is printed in the available text.

"Effort‐moderated scoring was found to both yield scores that were more accurate than those found under traditional scoring, and exhibit improved person fit statistics."
