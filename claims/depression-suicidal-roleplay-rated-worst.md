# SP roleplay of depression/suicidal tendencies is rated significantly worse than anxiety disorder and somatoform disorder, with small effects

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Pairwise comparisons show the depression/suicidal tendencies roleplay rated significantly worse than anxiety disorder (adjusted p = .0096, d = .202) and somatoform disorder (adjusted p = .012, d = .225); all other combinations showed no significant difference. [→ Himmelbauer M 2018](#himmelbauer-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Himmelbauer M 2018

Himmelbauer M, Seitz T, Seidman C, Löffler-Stastka H. (2018). Standardized patients in psychiatry – the best way to learn clinical skills? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1184-4

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of students' roleplay ratings by psychiatric disorder, with each disorder acted out an equal number of times. The article reports the two significant pairwise contrasts and notes "the effects were small".

> "Pair-by-pair comparisons showed a significant difference between the evaluation of the played psychiatric disease “anxiety disorder” and “depression/suicidal tendencies” (adjusted p = .0096, Cohen’s d = .202), as well as between “depression/ suicidal tendencies” and “somatoform disorder” (adjusted p = .012, Cohen’s d = .225)."
