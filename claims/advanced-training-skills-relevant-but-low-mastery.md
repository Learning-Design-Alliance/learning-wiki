## Evidence

### Dannenberg Katja Anne 2016

Dannenberg Katja Anne, Stroben Fabian, Schröder Therese, Thomas Anke, Hautz Wolf E. (2016). The future of practical skills in undergraduate medical education – an explorative Delphi-Study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001061](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001061)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of Delphi ratings cross-tabulated with the Consensus Statement's three-tier mastery scale (seen demonstrated, performed under supervision, performed repeatedly) for the advanced-training stage. The article reports 8% irrelevant and 42% at the lowest level.

> "Only 8% of the learning goals and skills which should be mastered upon completing advanced training were assessed as irrelevant for the future, yet 42% of skills were assessed at the lowest level of skill ("seen demonstrated")."
