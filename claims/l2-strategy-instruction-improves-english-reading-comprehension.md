### Talebi 2012

Seyed Hassan Talebi. (2012). Reading in L2 (English) and L1 (Persian): An Investigation into Reveres Transfer of Reading Strategies. English Language Teaching, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n3p217

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measure ANOVA on pre/post English reading test scores of control and experimental groups (each 30 students per proficiency cell as printed in Table 1); the group-by-change interaction was significant (F=286.318; P<.000), with the experimental group gaining "a gain of 2.60 scores" versus .05 for controls.
