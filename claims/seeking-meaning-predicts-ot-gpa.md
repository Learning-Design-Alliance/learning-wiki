Bonsaksen T, Brown T, Lim HB, Fong K. (2017). Approaches to studying predict academic performance in undergraduate occupational therapy students: a cross-cultural study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0914-3

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression block 2 (deep approach subscales) in the cross-sectional study of n = 712 students; Table 3 reports std. β = 0.12, p = 0.01 for 'seeking meaning', while 'relating ideas', 'use of evidence' and 'interest in ideas' were not significant.
