Descriptive percentages of average scores per indicator from the Instant DCA administration in the one-group study, shown in Figure 1. The article reports "a sufficient expertise" on interface identification and technical problem solving and "a very low value (26%)" for technological understanding. These are descriptive percentages, not effect sizes.
