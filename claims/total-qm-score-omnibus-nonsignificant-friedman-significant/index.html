Human expert evaluation with 20 teachers rating all three systems; the article reports the omnibus ANOVA "did not reach con- ventional significance" while the Friedman test suggested an overall difference, and post-hoc Wilcoxon tests with Holm correction revealed no significant pairwise comparisons.

"For the total score, the omnibus ANOV A did not reach con- ventional significance,F (2, 38) = 2.66,p =.083, although the non-parametric Friedman test suggested an overall dif- ference across systems,χ2(2) = 6.24,p =.044."
