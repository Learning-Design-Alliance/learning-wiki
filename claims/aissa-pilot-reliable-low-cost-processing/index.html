Alvaro Becerra 2026¶

Alvaro Becerra, Diego Gomez, Ruth Cobos. (2026). AISSA: Implementation and Deployment of an AI-based Student Slides Analysis tool for Academic Presentations. LASI Spain 26: Learning Analytics Summer Institute Spain 2026. https://orcid.org/0009-0003-7793-2682

q2 · i? · design · r2

Pilot deployment at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid with 46 final-year undergraduates during the second trimester 2026. The article reports "no processing errors or data extraction failures were detected, with the model consistently returning the exact required JSON structure" and an estimated average cost of $0.06 to $0.07 USD per evaluation based on about 18,000 input tokens and 2,500–2,600 output tokens.

"AISSA successfully processed a total of 90 presentations over the course without observable service degradation. The end-to-end processing time ranged from approximately 1 to 3 minutes per submission."
