## Evidence

### Pomerantz et al. 2007

Eva M. Pomerantz, Elizabeth A. Moorman and Scott D. Litwack. (2007). The How, Whom, and Why of Parents' Involvement in Children's Academic Lives: More Is Not Always Better. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/003465430305567

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of research on naturally occurring school-based involvement (e.g., attending parent-teacher conferences, volunteering), including longitudinal studies adjusting for children's earlier achievement, concludes such involvement 'benefits children in terms of their achievement.' No effect size is printed for the synthesis.

> "In sum, the research on parents' involvement on the school front is fairly consistent in suggesting that such involvement benefits children in terms of their achievement."
