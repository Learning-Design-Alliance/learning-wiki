## Evidence

### Södersved Källestedt 2025

Södersved Källestedt, M.-L., & Hidefjäll, P. (2025). A mixed methods study of self-directed learning in clinical practice using a mobile skills training system. BMC Medical Education, 25, 1515. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08127-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview data from healthcare professionals and clinical educators. Online learning with certificates allowed assessment of theoretical knowledge before hands-on SDL skills training, though participants noted a temptation to click through answers without absorbing content.

> "It became apparent that online learning with certificates and SDL mutually reinforce each other."
