# After AI-generated-image teaching, post-teaching bacterial keratitis accuracy was significantly lower than in the real-image group, though accuracy improvement did not differ

## Evidence

### Xie Wenjia 2025

Xie Wenjia, Yuan Zhouhang, Si Yuxuan, Huang Zhengxing, Li Yingming, Wu Fei, Yao Yu-Feng. (2025). Enhancing medical students’ diagnostic accuracy of infectious keratitis with AI-generated images. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07592-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post hoc comparison of bacterial keratitis accuracy between the AI-generated-image group (G2, n=37) and real-medical-image group (G3, n=30) after teaching. G2 (65.68% ± 18.79%) was significantly lower than G3 (79.33% ± 18.93%), P = 0.002; improvement (23.51% vs 27.33%) was not significantly different.

> "After teaching, the mean accuracy of bacterial keratitis in the G2 group (65.68% ± 18.79%) was significantly lower than the G3 group (79.33% ± 18.93%) (P = 0.002, post hoc test), but the improvement of accuracy was not significantly different"
