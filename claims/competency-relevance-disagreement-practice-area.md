## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For six of the ten behaviours showing disagreement, disagreement on relevance was associated with area of pharmacy practice (P < 0.05). [→ Udoh Arit 2018](#udoh-arit-2018)
`q2 i?` Respondents in non-patient-facing areas ranked pharmaceutical care and organisation-and-management behaviours as less relevant, while the converse held for research-related and quality control behaviours. [→ Udoh Arit 2018 (2)](#udoh-arit-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Udoh Arit 2018

Udoh Arit, Bruno Andreia, Bates Ian. (2018). A survey of pharmacists’ perception of foundation level competencies in African countries. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-018-0280-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of the 10 behaviours with more than 10% 'not relevant' ratings in the survey of 469 African pharmacists; the article reports "associated with area of pharmacy practice for six of the ‘disagreed’ behaviours" with P < 0.05.

> "This observed disagreement was associated with area of pharmacy practice for six of the ‘disagreed’ behaviours (Table 9; P < 0.05)."

### Udoh Arit 2018 (2)

Udoh Arit, Bruno Andreia, Bates Ian. (2018). A survey of pharmacists’ perception of foundation level competencies in African countries. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-018-0280-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of 'not relevant' ratings between patient-facing and non-patient-facing practice sectors for the ten disagreed behaviours, reported in Table 10 with p values including 0.04, 0.003, and 0.05; non-patient-facing respondents rated care and management behaviours "as ‘not relevant’" more often, with the converse for research behaviours.

> "A higher percentage of the respondents in the ‘non-patient’ facing areas of practice (this means area of pharmacy practice with little or no daily patient interactions such as industrial and academic pharmacy) ranked the behaviours that showed disagreement in the ‘pharmaceutical care’ (B6, B13, B14, B20) and ‘organisation and management’ cluster (B32, B34, B58) as ‘not relevant’ compared to respondents in the ‘patient-facing’ practice areas (such as hospital and community pharmacy) (Table 10)."
