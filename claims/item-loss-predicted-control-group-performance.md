## Evidence

### Bahrick 1991

Bahrick, H. P., & Hall, L. K. (1991). Lifetime maintenance of high school mathematics content. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 120(1), 20-33. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.120.1.20

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression on individual Algebra 1 items using initial difficulty, student control performance, adult control performance, format, and content as predictors. High student-control performance marks content relearned in the algebra course after earlier exposure, and adult-control performance marks content acquired outside formal training, both of which are less likely to be forgotten.

> "The analysis yielded a final multiple correlation of .71, and the two control group scores were the best predictors."
