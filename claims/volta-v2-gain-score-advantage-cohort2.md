## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In the Spring 2015 cohort, ANOVA on gain scores showed a significant effect of VOLTA on student performance, with a large effect size. [→ Saleheen 2018](#saleheen-2018)

## Evidence

### Saleheen 2018

Saleheen, F., Wang, Z., Picone, J., Butz, B. P., and Won, C.-H. (2018). Efficacy of a Virtual Teaching Assistant in an Open Laboratory Environment for Electric Circuits. Advances in Engineering Education. http://www.temple.edu/csnap/volta.html

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA on gain scores of 34 students across 11 lab assignments in the Spring 2015 TA vs. VOLTA LAB 2 experiment, where the experimental group received closed-lab TA assistance plus VOLTA v2. The article reports "d was found to be 2.13" and p < .001, a large effect favoring the VOLTA group.

> "For Cohort 2, d was found to be 2.13 for F(1,27) = 27 .145. The effect size (d = 2.13) indicates that this is a large effect."
