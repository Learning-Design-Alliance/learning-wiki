## Evidence

### Muntaner-Mas Adrià 2025

Muntaner-Mas Adrià, Valenzuela Pedro L., Pinto-Escalona Tania, Erickson Kirk I., Martínez-de-Quel Óscar. (2025). Mental health mediates the association between cardiorespiratory fitness and academic performance in European schoolchildren. Jornal de Pediatria. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jped.2024.10.013

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PROCESS macro mediation analyses (Hayes procedure, 10,000 bootstrap samples; valid sample 288) with academic performance as outcome, CRF as predictor and SDQ mental health indicators as mediators, adjusted for sex and socioeconomic status. The study reports mediation "ranged from 8 % to 25 %"; no standardized effect size is printed for the indirect effects, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Mediation analyses (path a*b) revealed that the association between CRF and academic performance indicators was partially mediated via mental health difficulties (except for conduct and peer problems), and this mediation ranged from 8 % to 25 %, (β ranging, from −0.025 to −0.080, p > 0.05)."
