# Mindfulness is inversely associated with depression and stress but not with GPA in Saudi medical students

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Mindfulness shows a strong inverse correlation with depression (r = -0.619) and stress (r = -0.640), and no correlation with last-semester GPA (r = -0.021). [→ Alzahrani AM 2020](#alzahrani-am-2020)

## Evidence

### Alzahrani AM 2020

Alzahrani AM, Hakami A, AlHadi A, Batais MA, Alrasheed AA, Almigbal TH. (2020). The interplay between mindfulness, depression, stress and academic performance in medical students: A Saudi perspective. PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231088. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231088

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 289 KSU medical students using Pearson correlations; Table 2 reports r = -0.619 for depression and r = -0.640 for stress (both P < 0.001), versus r = -0.021 (P = 0.725) for GPA, an "inverse relationship between mindfulness and both stress and depression".

> "The correlation analysis revealed a statistically significant inverse relationship between mindfulness and both stress and depression (Fig 1, Table 2). However, last-semester GPA did not."
