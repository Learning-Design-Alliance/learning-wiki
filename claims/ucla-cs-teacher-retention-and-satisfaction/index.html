Karen Hunter Quartz 2023¶

Karen Hunter Quartz. (2023). UCLA Community School: Celebrating Language, Culture, and Community. Learning Policy Institute. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED642538

q2 · i? · design · r2

Survey and administrative reporting cited in this case study, drawing on LAUSD School Experience Survey results and the school's annual report. The report states "90% of teachers retained" across 2019-20 to 2020-21 and that students and parents report above-district satisfaction; no effect sizes are printed.

"Teacher retention rates continue to be higher than the national average for urban schools with high levels of poverty, with 90% of teachers retained between the 2019–20 and 2020–21 school years."
