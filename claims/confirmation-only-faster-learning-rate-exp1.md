## Evidence

### Ramsburg 2015

Ramsburg, J. T., & Ohlsson, S. (2015). Category Change in the Absence of Cognitive Conflict. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Category+Change+in+the+Absence+of+Cognitive+Conflict%22

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Repeated-measures ANOVA with Greenhouse-Geisser correction on percentage correct for blocks 6–10 in Experiment 1. The condition main effect showed "the Confirmatory-Only condition performed better than the Complete condition, F(1, 72) = 7.60, p < .01, ηp2 = .096".

> "The analysis revealed a main effect for blocks showing that participants improved with training, F(2.646, 190.477) = 75.01, p < .001, ηp2 = .671, and a main effect for condition showing that the Confirmatory-Only condition performed better than the Complete condition,  F(1, 72) = 7.60, p < .01, ηp2 = .096."

### Ramsburg 2015 (2)

Ramsburg, J. T., & Ohlsson, S. (2015). Category Change in the Absence of Cognitive Conflict. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Category+Change+in+the+Absence+of+Cognitive+Conflict%22

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The condition × blocks interaction in the same Experiment 1 repeated-measures ANOVA was significant: "The rate of learning was faster for the Confirmatory-Only condition than the Complete condition, F(2.646, 190.477) = 5.21, p < .01, ηp2 = .146".

> "The rate of learning was faster for the Confirmatory-Only condition than the Complete condition, F(2.646, 190.477) = 5.21, p < .01, ηp2 = .146."
