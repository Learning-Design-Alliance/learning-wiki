Evidence¶

Belland 2017¶

Belland, B. R., Walker, A. E., Kim, N. J., & Lefler, M. (2017). Synthesizing Results From Empirical Research on Computer-Based Scaffolding in STEM Education: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research, 87(2), 309-344. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654316670999

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 144 experimental studies (333 outcomes) of computer-based scaffolding in STEM education, published 1993–2014, each with a control condition. The article reports "z = 18.19, p < .01" for the overall effect, with heterogeneity of Q = 1096.96, I2 = 69.7%.

"The overall mean effect size (see Figure 3) is greater than 0 at a statistically significant level, z = 18.19, p < .01, suggesting that students who receive computer-based scaffolding do better on cognitive tests than students who do not receive scaffolds."
