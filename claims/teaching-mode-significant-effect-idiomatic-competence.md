## Evidence

### Zainab Abolfazli Khonbi & Karim Sadeghi 2017

Zainab Abolfazli Khonbi & Karim Sadeghi. (2017). Improving English Language Learners’ Idiomatic Competence: Does Mode of Teaching Play a Role? Iranian Journal of Language Teaching Research 5(3), 61-79. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1156202

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 47 Iranian language learners at two institutes randomly assigned to four teaching-mode groups; one-way between-groups ANOVA on 45-item posttest scores found a statistically significant effect for teaching mode, F (3, 43) = 18.50, P = .00. Levene’s test showed the assumption of homogeneity of variances was not violated.
