### Megan Kuhfeld 2018

Megan Kuhfeld, Elizabeth Gershoff, Katherine Paschall. (2018). The development of racial/ethnic and socioeconomic achievement gaps during the school years. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology 57, 62-73. https://www.journals.elsevier.com/journal-of-applied-developmental-psychology

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

From the same TVEM analysis of two large longitudinal data sets covering ages 5 to 15, the study reports that "within-group variation increased across time," which the authors interpret as later-grade standardized differences corresponding to larger absolute knowledge differences.

> "Furthermore, we found that within-group variation increased across time, which indicated that a standardized difference in later grades translates to a larger difference in knowledge in later grades."
