## Evidence

### Yao Yitong 2024

Yao Yitong, Yang Guihong, Li Xueling, Li Yanshan, Zhou Jing, Hong Yun, Yang Ling, Zhao Yu. (2024). An assessment of clinical reception training using standard patient and dental simulator in prosthodontic dentistry for dental undergraduates: a historical control trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06563-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Historical control trial of 65 fifth-year dental undergraduates (SP group n=36, control n=29) assessed with Mini-CEX score sheets on real patients. The article reports the "SP group was superior to that of the control group" with P < 0.05; no effect size is printed.

> "The total performance of interns in the SP group was superior to that of the control group at both 2-week and 8-week intervals after training, particularly in history taking, physical examination, and doctor-patient communication (P < 0.05), as depicted in Fig. 3."
