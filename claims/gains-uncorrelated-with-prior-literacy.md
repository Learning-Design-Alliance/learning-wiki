### Hosseini 2026

Hosseini, H. (2026). The Pedagogy of AI Mistakes: Fostering Higher-Order Thinking. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05472 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.05472](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.05472)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary correlational analysis comparing baseline self-reported proficiency across DBMS domains (SQL design, database types, ER modeling, system use) with learning gains in the 13-student course. The authors conclude that "initial confidence in AI or database skills did not predict who benefited most from the course".

> "Pearson correlations revealed no meaningful associations: gains were uncorrelated with AI literacy (r= 0.05,p=.88) and weakly, non-significantly related to database literacy (r=−0.09,p=.77)."
