Fang Eric Kin Meng, Boon Yuru, Ho Jin Yang, Tey Natasha, Low Matthew Jian Wen. (2026). Artificial intelligence generated virtual patients in health professions education: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10028-w

q3 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of use cases charted from 97 included studies in a scoping review following the Arksey and O'Malley framework, searched across four databases. The review reports "85 (87.6%) focused primarily on teaching and learning", with assessment a minority focus.

"Of 97 studies, 85 (87.6%) focused primarily on teaching and learning, 8 (8.2%) focused primarily on assessment, and 4 (4.1%) combined learning and assessment."
