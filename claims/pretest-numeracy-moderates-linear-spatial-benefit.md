## Evidence

### Schiffman J 2018

Schiffman J, Laski EV. (2018). Linear-spatial materials improve young children's addition strategies and accuracy, irregular arrays don't. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208832

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Multiple regression in the randomized experiment predicting number of problems accurately answered via advanced strategy at posttest, partialing out pretest accurate use. The "Numeracy Screener by condition interaction was significant, b = .15, SE = .07, β = .29, t = 2.13, p = .04"; the positive relation between pretest numeracy and posttest accurate advanced strategy use held only in the linear-spatial condition (p = .03), not the irregular condition.
