## Evidence

### Bearman Margaret 2019

Bearman Margaret, Greenhill Jennene, Nestel Debra. (2019). The power of simulation: a large‐scale narrative analysis of learners’ experiences. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13747

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the 327 narratives identified these five mechanism themes; the article reports they were "notable in the progression and transformation narratives, but generally absent from practice and humiliation narratives". The discussion aligns these with prior systematic reviews on debriefing, role-play and observation. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Themes regarding mechanisms that supported learning were as follows: verisimilitude; feedback, debriefing and facilitation; observation of self and others; repetition of activities; and role‐playing the patient."
