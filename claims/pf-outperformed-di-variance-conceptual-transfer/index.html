Evidence¶

Manu Kapur 2012¶

Manu Kapur, Nikol Rummel. (2012). Productive failure in learning from generation and invention activities. Instr Sci 40:645-650. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-012-9235-4

q3 · i? · review · r2

Classroom-based study with 9th-grade students learning the concept of variance; PF students first attempted problems in small groups, then the teacher compared student-generated solutions and taught the canonical concept, while DI students received direct instruction first. The editorial reports PF students "signiﬁcantly outperformed DI students" on the posttest.

"On the posttest however, PF students signiﬁcantly outperformed DI students on conceptual understanding and transfer without compromising procedural ﬂuency."
