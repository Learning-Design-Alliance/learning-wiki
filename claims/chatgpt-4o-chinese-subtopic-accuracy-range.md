## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the Chinese question bank, ChatGPT-4o performed particularly well in Nerve Disorders (90%), Implants (80%), Salivary Gland Disease (80%), and Cysts/Tumors/Lesions (76%), with the lowest accuracy in Dentofacial Deformities (38%), the only subtopic below 60%. `q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subtopic-level analysis of the Chinese question bank results (visualized in Fig. 2 dumbbell plots) identified Dentofacial Deformities as the weakest area at 38% accuracy, while Nerve Disorders (90%), Implants (80%), Salivary Gland Disease (80%), and Cysts/Tumors/Lesions (76%) were the strongest.

> "The lowest accuracy was observed in Dentofacial Deformities (38%), indicating that it is the only subtopic with an accuracy below 60%."
