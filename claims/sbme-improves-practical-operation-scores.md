## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students in the SBME group scored significantly higher on final practical operation performance than control-group students in both the 2018 and 2019 junior cohorts. [→ Wang S 2021](#wang-s-2021)

## Evidence

### Wang S 2021

Wang S, Ren X, Ye J, Wang W, Huang H, Qin C. (2021). Exploration of simulation-based medical education for undergraduate students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000025982

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section of a randomized controlled study of 1178 junior surgery students (844 control, 334 SBME) at Nanjing Medical University, 2018–2019. The SBME group outperformed the control group on final practical operation in both cohorts, with the printed means and P values reported here.

> "final performance of practical operation (for the 2018 junior students, control group: 28.38 ± 0.83 vs SBME group: 31.98 ± 0.88, P = .001, and for the 2019 junior students, control group: 29.12 ± 0.87 vs SBME group: 31.12 ± 0.69, P = .001)"
