## Evidence

### Hengtao Tang 2025

Ellison, J. P., & Tang, H. (2025). Self-regulated learning and video annotation in a high school acting classroom. Instructional Science, 53, 457–477. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09699-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of seven of ten participants who completed both assessments in a small mixed-methods study; a Wilcoxon Signed Rank test was used. The mean score of participants' ITS-AR "increased from 4.95 (Fair) to 7.57 (Good)". No effect size was printed.

> "The result showed that the increase in participants' scores from pre-assessment to post-assessment was statistically significant, U = 9.5, z=-2.44, p =.015, indicating interventions significantly improved the participants' acting skills."
