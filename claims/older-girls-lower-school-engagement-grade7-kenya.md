Bivariate GEE analysis of school engagement items in the baseline survey of 3489 grade 7 girls; adjusted estimates were OR 1.18 (95%CI 1.02, 1.36) for confidence and OR 1.37 (95%CI 1.18, 1.60) for understanding lessons.

> "more likely to report not feeling confident enough to answer questions in class (OR:1.24 (95% CI:1.07, 1.43)), and more likely to report often not understanding what is being taught in the lessons (OR:1.47 (95% CI:1.27,1,71))"
