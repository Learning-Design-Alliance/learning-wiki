# Each readiness dimension shows a positive independent association with student literacy, but coefficients attenuate to non-significance when dimensions are modelled simultaneously, indicating readiness operates as an integrated configuration

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` All six readiness dimensions (strategy, structure, process, technical, data, ethics) showed positive, statistically significant associations with student AI literacy when examined separately. [→ Guan 2026](#guan-2026)
`q3 i0` When the six dimensions were entered simultaneously, coefficients were substantially attenuated and no longer reached conventional significance; data readiness retained the numerically largest coefficient (β = 0.0087, p = 0.10). [→ Guan 2026 (2)](#guan-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Guan 2026

Guan, X., Zheng, M., Gašević, D., Guo, W., Liu, Y., Han, X., Gasevic, D., Ma, R., Wu, Q., & Yan, L. (2026). From School AI Readiness to Student AI Literacy: A National Multilevel Mediation Analysis of Institutional Capacity and Teacher Capability. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r?`

Separate covariate-adjusted multilevel models for each readiness dimension in the linked national sample of 1,007 schools. The article reports that "all six readiness dimensions showed positive and statistically sig- nificant associations with student AI literacy" when modelled one at a time, with data readiness showing the numerically largest standardized coefficient.

> "When examined separately, all six readiness dimensions showed positive and statistically sig- nificant associations with student AI literacy."

### Guan 2026 (2)

Guan, X., Zheng, M., Gašević, D., Guo, W., Liu, Y., Han, X., Gasevic, D., Ma, R., Wu, Q., & Yan, L. (2026). From School AI Readiness to Student AI Literacy: A National Multilevel Mediation Analysis of Institutional Capacity and Teacher Capability. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.20056)

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Simultaneous six-dimension model with adjusted generalised variance inflation factors from 2.16 to 3.96, below severe-multicollinearity thresholds. The article attributes the attenuation to "overlapping institutional capacities rather than the absence of meaningful associations".

> "data readiness retained the numerically largest coefficient (𝛽= 0.0087,𝑆𝐸=0.0053,𝑡(𝑑𝑓=933.334)=1.645,𝑝=0.10), although the effect was not statistically significant."
