## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Biology and wildlife students reported significantly lower enjoyment than chemical science students (P = 0.020); all other subject-group comparisons were non-significant. [→ Rafiq R 2025](#rafiq-r-2025)

## Evidence

### Rafiq R 2025

Rafiq R, Matthews H, Abedalreza D, Yahya F, Jones MA. (2025). Card games are effective tools to enhance foundation year health and safety inductions. FEBS Open Bio 15(12). https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.70140

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Participants stratified into biomedicine, chemical sciences and biology/wildlife programme groups (one-way ANOVA, Tukey post hoc) showed "a significantly lower response score from biology and wildlife students compared to those studying chemical science‐based foundation years" on enjoyment only.

> "there was a significantly lower response score from biology and wildlife students compared to those studying chemical science‐based foundation years (Chemical Science: 4.83 ± 0.38, n = 18, Biology and Wildlife: 4.27 ± 0.74, n = 30, P = 0.020, Fig. 8A)"
