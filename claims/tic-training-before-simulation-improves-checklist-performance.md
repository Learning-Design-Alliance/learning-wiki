## Evidence

### Brown Taylor 2026

Brown Taylor, Ashista Henry, Sudario Gabriel, Clery Michael, Dubosh Nicole M., Harries Aaron, Misa Nana Yaa. (2026). Trauma-informed procedural training in emergency medicine: a multi-site randomized control pilot study. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-026-01535-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled pilot (19 intervention, 22 control students at two sites), observed simulation performance on a 16-item TIC clinical skills checklist favored the intervention group, "a statistically significant difference (p = 0.0036)". No effect size is printed.

> "Participants receiving TIC training prior to simulation achieved a higher average completion rate on the 16-item clinical skills checklist (67%) compared to the control group (44%), representing a statistically significant difference (p = 0.0036)."
