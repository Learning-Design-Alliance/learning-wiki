Evidence¶

Harkins 2020¶

Harkins, D. A., Grenier, L. I., Irizarry, C., Robinson, E., Ray, S., & Shea, L.-M. (2020). Building relationships for critical service-learning. Michigan Journal of Community Service Learning, 26(2), 21–38. https://doi.org/10.3998/mjcsloa.3239521.0026.202

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Correlational analyses across all 125 participants examined professor-student NRI-SPV relationship variables against CASQ civic outcomes. The article also reports negative correlations: conflict with social justice (r = -.27, p < .05) and antagonism with social justice (r = -.42, p < .001).

"Positive correlations were found between the CASQ variable civic action and the NRI professor relationship variables guidance (r = .39, p < .05); satisfaction (r = .46, p < .001); nurturance (r = .28, p < .05); reassurance of competence (r = .41, p < .01); and feelings of power (r = .38, p < .01)"
