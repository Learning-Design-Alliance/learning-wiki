# In an out-of-school lab for social sciences, the authenticity level of the learning activity (Productive Failure vs. Direct Instruction) did not affect students' perceived authenticity or situational interest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` PF students did not report higher perceived authenticity or situational interest than DI students at the end of the OSL visit, contrary to hypotheses H1 and H2. [→ Valentina Nachtigall 2021](#valentina-nachtigall-2021)

## Evidence

### Valentina Nachtigall 2021

Valentina Nachtigall, Nikol Rummel. (2021). Investigating students' perceived authenticity of learning activities in an out-of-school lab for social sciences: a replication study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09556-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 152 10th graders (PF n=80, DI n=72, whole classes assigned) in an out-of-school lab; a MANCOVA with individual interest as covariate found no significant condition effects on perceived authenticity or situational interest (catch or hold). The authors state the study was "against our H1 and H2, but in line with the findings of our first study".

> "Thus, against our H1 and H2, but in line with the findings of our first study, the authenticity level of the learning activity did not affect students' perceived authenticity nor their situational interest."
