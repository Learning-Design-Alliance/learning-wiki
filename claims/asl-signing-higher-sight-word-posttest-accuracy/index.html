Evidence¶

Sherman 2011¶

Sherman, J. (2011). Signing for Success: Using American Sign Language to Learn Sight Vocabulary. SRATE Journal, 20(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ959527

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Small modified inquiry comparison in a Professional Development School: eleven struggling first graders randomly divided into Group A (five students, no ASL) and Group B (six students, with ASL), one word per week for five weeks. The article reports "76% accuracy rate" for Group A and 96% for Group B; no effect size or significance test is printed.

"Group A students learned their words using just the flashcards and reading the words in context with a 76% accuracy rate."
