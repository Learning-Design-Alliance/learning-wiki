A scoping review following Arksey and O'Malley's framework, synthesizing 30 included studies to map facilitator responsibilities. The review states these aspects "include providing an optimal learning environment, describing the process of clinical reasoning, assessing students' abilities, and implementing effective learning techniques and methods."
