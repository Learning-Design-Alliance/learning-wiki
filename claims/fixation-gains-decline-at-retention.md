## Evidence

### Choi Kai Yip 2026

Choi Kai Yip, Chan Ka Wing, Yau Hiu Ching, Lau Yuet Yan, Leung Tsz Wing. (2026). Perceptual learning enhances diabetic retinopathy detection skills in novice optometry students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08691-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures analysis of fixation hits in the PL group across baseline, post-training, and three-month retention (Fig. 6b; F(2,36) = 19.78, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.52). Post-hoc tests showed "a statistically significant decrease from the post-training assessment to the retention assessment" while fixation hits "remained significantly higher than at baseline."

> "there was a statistically significant decrease from the post-training assessment to the retention assessment (t(18) = −3.66, p = 0.002, Cohen's d = −1.06). Despite this decline, the fixation hit percentage at three months remained significantly higher than at baseline (t(18) = 2.60, p = 0.04, Cohen's d = 0.75)."
