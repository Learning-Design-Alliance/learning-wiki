q2 · i? · causal · r2

Inspection of delayed writing GCsim across Study 1 and Study 2 (Fig. 8) suggests an interaction between network structure and presentation timing, consistent across both prompt conditions.

"When the network was presented before reading (Study 1), the integrative (inter-text) network form was more like the expert than the intra-text network. This pattern was reversed when the network was presented as summary writing feedback (Study 2), the intra-text network form (GCsim) was more like the expert than was the integrative (inter-text) network."
