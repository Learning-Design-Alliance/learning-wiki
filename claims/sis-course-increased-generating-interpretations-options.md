## Evidence

### Schutte et al. 2018

Ingrid Schutte, Elanor Kamans, Marca Wolfensberger and Wiel Veugelers. (2018). Effects of an International Undergraduate Honors Course on Awareness of Global Justice. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 82. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8020082

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-posttest factorial repeated-measures analysis with control group (honors students not taking the course); N participants = 18, N control = 18 for ESS6. The simple effect showed "a signiﬁcant increase of generating interpretations and options within the participant group F(1,34) = 5.95, p = 0.02, N2p = 0.15", with no increase in the control condition (F < 1). The printed effect size is η²p = 0.15 (printed as N2p = 0.15), a large partial eta-squared.

> "Results revealed a signiﬁcant interaction, with respect to the subscale generating interpretations and options (ESS6) F(1,34) = 5.91, p = 0.02, N2p = 0.15."
