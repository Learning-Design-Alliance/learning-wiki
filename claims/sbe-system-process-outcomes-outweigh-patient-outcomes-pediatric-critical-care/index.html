Zanin A 2026¶

Zanin A, Poletto E, Mencia S, Gawronski O, Jourdain G. (2026). Evaluating the impact of simulation on patient outcomes in pediatric and neonatal critical care: a systematic review and critical appraisal of the evidence. European Journal of Pediatrics 185(5):302. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-06925-3

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of 16 included studies mapped onto the Kirkpatrick framework. The review reports that five studies targeted system-level processes, with improvements in ECMO deployment time and reductions in unplanned PICU admissions and bed days.

"Kirkpatrick Level 4a (system-level processes): Five studies specifically addressed organizational efficiency and healthcare delivery processes. Significant improvements were demonstrated by Su et al. [10] through faster ECMO deployment and by Theilen et al. [31] through reductions in PICU bed days and unplanned admissions."

The review's narrative synthesis of included studies reports that direct patient-level (4b) impacts were described in the majority of the 16 studies, spanning mortality, complication and infection outcomes.

"Kirkpatrick Level 4b (actual patient outcomes): Direct impacts on patient health were reported in the majority of studies [11, 24–30, 32, 34–36]."
