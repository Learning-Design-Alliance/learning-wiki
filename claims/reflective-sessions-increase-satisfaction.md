## Evidence

### Hassani Nia Nasser 2025

Hassani Nia Nasser, Karami Sahar, Shahraki Farimah, Kohan Mahmoud. (2025). Structured reflective sessions enhance knowledge and clinical skills in operating room technology students: a Solomon four-group study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08431-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Satisfaction questionnaire (20-item Likert, five domains, Cronbach's α = 0.87) administered at week 14; the reflection group scored higher than controls with t = 16.19, p < 0.001; Table 1 reports "Cohen's d = 1.93".

> "Analysis of student satisfaction showed that the mean score in the reflection group (65.26 ± 4.23) was significantly higher than in the control group (37.46 ± 5.12) (t = 16.19, p < 0.001)."
