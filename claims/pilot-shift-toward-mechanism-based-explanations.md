`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory pilot with 20 second-year biomedical device technology students in a supervised 40-minute session; analysis was descriptive with no inferential statistics. The authors report that "some students began to move from output-oriented descriptions toward more mechanism-based explanations."

> "Instructor observations, student scenario responses, and short post-session feedback suggested that some students began to move from output-oriented descriptions toward more mechanism-based explanations of device behavior."
