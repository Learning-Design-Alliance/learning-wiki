Technical findings across the cohort on using AI to evaluate AI outputs, reported in Table 5. The report attributes the difficulty to models reinforcing stated content and to humans struggling to agree on rubrics for automated evaluation.

"This was often challenging, particularly because AI are "yes people" and reinforce what has been stated. Further, cohort members found it to be difficult for humans to agree on rubrics to automate evaluation."
