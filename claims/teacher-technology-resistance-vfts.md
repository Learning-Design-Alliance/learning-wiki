## Evidence

### Norris E. 2015

Norris E., Shelton N., Dunsmuir S., Duke-Williams O., Stamatakis E. (2015). Teacher and pupil perspectives on the use of Virtual Field Trips as physically active lessons. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1698-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of 12 teacher interviews found 7 teachers (58.3%) anticipated varied colleague reactions to interactive whiteboard use, from heavy reliance to avoidance; no interviewed teacher expected personal difficulty. The article links this to the Technology Acceptance Model.

> "Some interviewees (n = 7, 58.3 %) highlighted that VFTs could receive different reactions from teachers depending on their ICT competencies."
