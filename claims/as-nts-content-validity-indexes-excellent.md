## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi P 2019

Moll-Khosrawi P, Kamphausen A, Hampe W, Schulte-Uentrop L, Zimmermann S, Kubitz JC. (2019). Anaesthesiology students’ Non-Technical skills: development and evaluation of a behavioural marker system for students (AS-NTS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1609-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Evaluation questionnaire completed by 21 anaesthetists who had used the rating tool at least three times in undergraduate education; the content validity index reflected "the proportion of relevance" per dimension, with all three dimensions exceeding the excellent threshold.

> "The calculated content validity index for the first dimension of AS-NTS was 0.9, for the second dimension 0.95 and for the third dimension 0.8. A content validity index of 0.75 or higher is considered as “excellent”."
