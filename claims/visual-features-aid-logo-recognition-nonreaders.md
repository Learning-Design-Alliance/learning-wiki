## Evidence

### Kurvers 2009

Kurvers, J., Van Hout, R., & Vallen, T. (2009). Print awareness of adult illiterates: a comparison with young pre-readers and low-educated adult readers. Read Writ 22, 863-887. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-008-9129-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test comparing logo item types among non-readers, supported by participants' explanations: Fatma pointed at the yellow arches when asked how she knew it said McDonald's.

> "logos with additional visual features, such as the yellow arches of McDonald's, or the colours and curls of the C&A logo, were signiﬁcantly better recognized by the two groups of non-readers than logos with only color and type face as distinctive features, such as Hema and Edah (paired t-test, t(39) = 6.83, p \ 0.00)."
