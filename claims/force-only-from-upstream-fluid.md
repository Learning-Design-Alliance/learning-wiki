## Evidence

### Alvaro Suarez 2017

Alvaro Suarez, Sandra Kahan, Genaro Zavala, and Arturo C. Marti. (2017). Student Conceptual Difficulties in Hydrodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview problem 2, question C asked students to describe forces on confined and unconfined fluid elements. "Half of the students made reference to a weight force and a downward force exerted by the ﬂuid on the volume element"; when asked the force's origin the most common argument was the weight of the water column above.

> "Half of the students made reference to a weight force and a downward force exerted by the ﬂuid on the volume element (E2.4), regardless of whether they assumed that the ﬂuid was moving at a constant or accelerated veloc- ity."
