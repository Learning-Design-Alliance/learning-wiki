The report asserts, citing Priniski et al., that purpose fosters meaning in learning activities, "consequently increasing engage- ment, improving self-regulation, and boosting academic performance." No effect sizes are printed; this is a narrative synthesis claim.

"When learners are able to find purpose while learning, they instill a sense of meaning for the learning activities, consequently increasing engage- ment, improving self-regulation, and boosting academic performance (Priniski et al., 2018)."
