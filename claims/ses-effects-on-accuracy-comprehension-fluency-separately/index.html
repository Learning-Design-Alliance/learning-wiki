Evidence¶

Li 2021¶

Li, J., Peng, P., Ding, N., Ma, X., & Zhao, J. (2021). How does family socioeconomic status influence children's reading ability? Evidence from meta-analytic structural equation modeling. https://www.frontiersin.org

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Second-stage TSSEM on three just-identified observable reading models. The comprehension model's reported coefficients show the VK indirect path (β = 0.10) exceeding the PA indirect path (β = 0.05); accuracy and fluency models also had significant direct and indirect effects per the article.

"SES exerted a significant direct effect on reading comprehension, β = 0.12, 95 % LBCI [0.07, 0.16], as well as two parallel significant indirect effects on reading comprehension through PA (β = 0.05, 95 % LBCI [0.04, 0.06]) and VK (β = 0.10, 95 % LBCI [0.08, 0.11])"
