Peter Jamieson, Suman Bhunia, and Dhananjai M. Rao. (2023). With ChatGPT, do we have to rewrite our learning objectives - CASE study in Cybersecurity. [doi:10.1109/fie58773.2023.10343052](https://doi.org/10.1109/fie58773.2023.10343052)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument, citing Lang's caution that professors assume lower skills can simply be looked up with technology: the authors argue higher-order performance "requires that a human can keep and think about ideas and concepts" built on remember and understand.

> "The reality is to achieve skills such as "create" and "plan" in Bloom's taxonomy requires that a human can keep and think about ideas and concepts within a field of study that requires the "remember" and "understanding"."
