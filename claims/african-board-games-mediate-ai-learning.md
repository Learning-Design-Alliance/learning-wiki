### Rebecca Y. Bayeck 2026

Rebecca Y. Bayeck. (2026). Exploring learning in classic African board games: a review of recent literature. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10733-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Ali et al. (2020), a non-empirical study applying AI methods to Ntxuva of Mozambique, where minimax, alpha-beta pruning, and random agents were developed and put to play.

> "Findings indicate that applying a minimax algorithm create an AI agent intelligent enough to play Ntxuva like a professional human player."

### Rebecca Y. Bayeck 2026 (2)
