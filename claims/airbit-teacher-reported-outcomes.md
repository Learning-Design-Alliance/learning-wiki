## Evidence

### Bjørn Fjukstad 2019

Bjørn Fjukstad, Nina Angelvik, Morten Grønnesby, Maria Wulff Hauglann, Hedinn Gunhildrud, Fredrik Høisæther Rasch, Julianne Iversen, Margaret Dalseng, Lars Ailo Bongo. (2019). Teaching Electronics and Programming in Norwegian Schools Using the air:bit Sensor Kit. arXiv. [doi:10.1145/3304221.3325527](https://doi.org/10.1145/3304221.3325527)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Teacher survey with 8 of 11 participating teachers replying, using multiple choice questions and a free text field. More than half reported much or very much knowledge of electrical circuits, soldering, programming and scientific project planning, though some reported very little or little experience in all these areas.

> "Of the 8 replies, all teachers responded that their students had signiﬁcant or very signiﬁcant learning outcomes. All teachers also responded that they would recommend other classes to participate in the project."
