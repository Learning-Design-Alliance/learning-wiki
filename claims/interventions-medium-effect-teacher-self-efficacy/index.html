Evidence¶

Mok 2022¶

Mok, S. Y., Rupp, D., & Holzberger, D. (2022). What kind of individual support activities in interventions foster pre-service and beginning teachers' self-efficacy? A meta-analysis. Preprint submitted for peer-review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100552

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

Random-effects meta-analysis with robust variance estimation of 28 intervention studies (k = 49 effect sizes) targeting PSTs and BTs. The article reports "Hedges' g = 0.678" (p < .001, CI [0.547, 0.809]), a medium effect, with high heterogeneity (I2 = 90.133).

"we showed a significant and medium-sized overall effect on teacher self-efficacy across all 28 studies (k = 49 effect sizes; Hedges' g = 0.678 (SE = 0.064, p < .001, CI [0.547, 0.809], I2 = 90.133, Figure 1)."
