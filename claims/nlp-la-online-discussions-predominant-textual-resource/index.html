q3 · i? · review · r3

Frequency analysis of textual resources across the 156 selected papers in the review's results. Online discussions led with 55 papers and essays 26; questions, short answers, feedback messages, and reflective statements each appeared in 9–10 papers, with speech transcription, syllabi, tutorial dialogue, and learning objects also used.

"The predominant resource is online discussions (55), followed by essays (26). Additionally, questions (10), short answers (10), feedback messages (9), and reflective statements (9) are also extensively adopted."
