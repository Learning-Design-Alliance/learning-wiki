The review attributes this finding to the National Reading Panel meta-analysis by Ehri and colleagues; the reviewed meta-analysis itself was not read here, so the claim is second-hand and reported as the review states it.

> "research showing that instruction that includes systematic phonics leads to better spelling and reading outcomes than instruction that does not (Ehri, Nunes, Stahl, & Willows, 2001) suggests that children benefit from the power of generics in learning about alphabetic writing systems."
