Lucile Favero, Juan Antonio Pérez-Ortiz, Tanja Käser, Nuria Oliver. (2026). From Substitution to Scaffolding: Breaking the Self-Reinforcing Harm Cycle of AI in Education (and Beyond). arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17451 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.17451](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.17451)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Exploratory deductive coding of 49 first, unaided IB argumentative essays (median age 17, three German-speaking Swiss schools, n = 19, 11, 19) responding to the prompt on whether AI changes what it means to learn. The authors report that "39 (80%) explicitly linked reliance on AI to reduced thinking". Findings are descriptive only; no statistical generalizability is claimed.
