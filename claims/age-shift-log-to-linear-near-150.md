## Evidence

### Opfer 2007

Opfer, J. E., & Siegler, R. S. (2007). Representational change and children's numerical estimation. Cognitive Psychology 55 (2007) 169–195. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2006.09.002

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of absolute error change per number between age groups in Experiment 1, with improvement correlated with distance from 150 at r = -.80. Improvement around 150 (mean 266) exceeded that around 725 (18, t[3] = 37.76, p < .01) and around 5 (101, t[3] = 3.84, p < .05), as the log discrepancy hypothesis predicted.

> "Improvement in estimation accuracy proved to be highly correlated with distance from 150: r(21) D ¡.80, p < .001; the closer the number to 150, the greater the improvement with age"
