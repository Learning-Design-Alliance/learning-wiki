## Evidence

### Silander 2016

Silander, M., Moorthy, S., Dominguez, X., Pasnik, S., & Llorente, C. (2016). Using Digital Media at Home to Promote Young Children's Mathematics Learning: Results of a Randomized Controlled Trial. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567485

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial, two conditions, 197 four- and five-year-olds analyzed with multilevel models. Table 3 reports for Factor 1 (n=172) a coefficient of 5.26, "Hedges g (Effect Size)" 0.51, SE 1.12, p 0.000, favoring the intervention over BAU.

> "Children who participated in the intervention exhibited improvements in ordinal numbers, spatial relationships, and 3-D shapes compared to children in the BAU condition (ES=0.51, p<.05; see Table 3)."
