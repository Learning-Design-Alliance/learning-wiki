Steinhardt Laura C., Onikpo Faustin, Kouamé Julien, Piercefield Emily, Lama Marcel, Deming Michael S., Rowe Alexander K. (2015). Predictors of health worker performance after Integrated Management of Childhood Illness training in Benin: a cohort study. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-015-0910-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Register-review cohort analysis of 9,330 initial consultations by the first 32 IMCI-trained health workers in Benin, with IMCI gold-standard classifications generated by computer algorithm. The study reports "63.6 % were treated exactly according to IMCI guidelines, and 77.8 % were treated adequately".

> "For children with at least one PLTI, 63.6 % were treated exactly according to IMCI guidelines, and 77.8 % were treated adequately (i.e., effective treatment even if it did not exactly match IMCI guidelines)"
