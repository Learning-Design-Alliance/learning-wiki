Fumie Watanabe 2026¶

Fumie Watanabe, Tota Suko, Takashi Ishida, Yuko Kuma, Manabu Kobayashi, Shigeichi Hirasawa, and Gendo Kumoi. (2026). Interaction Effects Between Learner Characteristics and Dialogue Format in TTS Dialogue-Based Lessons. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20822

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Linear mixed-effects model for self-reported learning outcomes (two items) from the same 222 students; fixed effects accounted for Marginal R2 = .129. No interaction reached conventional significance; the article reports these as trend-level findings only.

"For the RCE factor, both the SS and TT formats showed a trend toward significance (b=−0.195, bothp < .10), and for the CE factor, the interaction with the SS format showed a trend toward significance (b= 0.121,p=.057)"
