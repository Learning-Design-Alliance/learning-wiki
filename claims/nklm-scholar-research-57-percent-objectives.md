## Evidence

### Hautz SC 2015

Hautz SC, Hautz WE, Keller N, Feufel MA, Spies C. (2015). The scholar role in the National Competence Based Catalogues of Learning Objectives for Undergraduate Medical Education (NKLM) compared to other international frameworks. GMS German Medical Science. [doi:10.3205/000224](https://doi.org/10.3205/000224)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Assignment of NKLM learning objectives to the five components by two independent raters; 93 of 95 assignable objectives concern research at 57%, with inter-rater agreement "ICC(2,1) = 0.907". The article also states research competencies are qualitatively more differentiated than in other frameworks.

> "The remaining 93 objectives concern research in 57% (see Table 2 (Tab. 2)). Inter-rater agreement in selecting and assigning learning objectives was good to excellent (ICC(2,1) = 0.907)."
