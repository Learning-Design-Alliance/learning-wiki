## Evidence

### Booth 2015

Booth, J., Oyer, M., Paré-Blagoev, E. J., Elliot, A. J., Barbieri, C., Augustine, A., & Koedinger, K. R. (2015). Learning Algebra by Example in Real-World Classrooms. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2015.1055636

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study in three Algebra I classrooms: 26 treatment and 25 control students randomly assigned within class to worked-example or problem-only assignments during a 3-4 week linear equations unit. The 2x2 ANOVA on posttest scores showed "a significant main effect of condition" with ηp2 = .10.

> "The analysis yielded a significant main effect of condition (F[1,47] = 5.34, p = .03, ηp2 = .10); students in the treatment group outscored students in the control group."
