## Evidence

### Cizmic Amila 2025

Cizmic Amila, Reichert Paulina, Häberle Frida, Preukschas Anas A., Pianka Frank, Mehrabi Arianeb, Nießen Anna, Müller-Stich Beat P., Hackert Thilo, Grotelüschen Rainer, Nickel Felix. (2025). Effects of training in pairs versus training alone on reaching proficiency in minimally invasive Roux-en-Y-gastric bypass on a virtual reality trainer in medical students: a randomized-controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11701-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Automatic VR-trainer assessments (instrument movements, pathway in cm, speed in cm/s) and performance time in the randomized-controlled trial. Printed p-values show the movement (0.033) and pathway (0.041) differences significant only at the fourth repetition; average instrument speed never differed significantly.

> "There was no difference in performance time between the two groups (Table 5). Attempts 7 and 8 were not calculated because they missed the majority (attempt 7) or completely (attempt 8) of the intervention group. Additionally, the difference between the number of instrument movements and the total instrument pathway in centimeters favored the intervention group only during the observation of the fourth MIS RYGB (Table 5)."
