Program evaluation of the pandemic-adapted sonoBYstudents courses: 45 abdominal ultrasound and 30 echocardiography students completed the compact format. The article reports "OSCE results were comparable to the previous semesters" and uniformly positive feedback; no test statistics or effect sizes are printed.

> "45 and 30 students attended the abdominal and echocardiography courses, respectively. Teaching goal was achieved and the acceptance for the adapted course format was high as feedback was throughout positive. OSCE results were comparable to the previous semesters despite the adapted course format."
