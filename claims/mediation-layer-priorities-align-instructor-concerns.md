`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive validation check in one graduate CS course offering: five semi-structured instructor interviews yielded 41 coded segments mapped to topics via a theme–topic table. The article reports "Spearman 𝜌= 0.80" agreement and interprets this as descriptive evidence, not predictive performance.

> "Among the top five mediated topics, three overlapped with instructor-identified concern topics (e.g., analogical reasoning, planning, knowledge-based AI). Rank agreement between mediated priorities and instructor concern levels was high (Spearman 𝜌= 0.80 ,𝑝 < .001,𝑛= 19 topics with survey data)."
