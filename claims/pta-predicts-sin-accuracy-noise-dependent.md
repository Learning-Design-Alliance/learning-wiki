`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc GLMM adding a PTA-by-noise-level interaction to the SIN comprehension model of the RCT. Comprehension declined alongside increasing PTA with the effect more pronounced under higher noise (as shown in Fig. 5); the session-by-group interaction remained significant (OR = 2.10, p = 0.002), and age remained a significant predictor (OR = 0.83, p = 0.040).
