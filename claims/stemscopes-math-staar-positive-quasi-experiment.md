### STEMscopes Math Evidence Summary 2024

STEMscopes Math Evidence Summary. (2024). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/stemscopes-math-2/

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A quasi-experimental study of 4th and 5th grade Texas public school students during the 2023-2024 school year compared STEMscopes Math students (n = 1,807) with comparison students (n = 2,209) on STAAR Math, controlling for baseline demographics and achievement; "effect size = +0.19" favoring STEMscopes Math.

> "Controlling for baseline demographics and achievement, STEMscopes Math students (n = 1,807) scored significantly higher on the Spring 2024 STAAR Math assessment than comparison students (n = 2,209) across the combined grade sample (effect size = +0.19)."
