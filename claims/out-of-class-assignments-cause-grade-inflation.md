## Evidence

### Benjamin O. Tayo and Ananda A. Jayawardhana 2018

Benjamin O. Tayo and Ananda A. Jayawardhana (2018). Grade inflation in introductory physics – the influence of out-of-class assignments. Pittsburg State University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1804.05084](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1804.05084)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square goodness-of-fit tests over 8 semesters of PHYS 104 grades (186 proctored plus out-of-class scores vs 213 proctored reference scores), with expected counts from a Bayesian multinomial fit and from simple reference proportions. The article reports "p-values of 0.023 and 0.008" and that "approximately 25% of all grades are shifted towards higher grades." No effect size is printed.

> "Using the expected counts from the two different methods, we found p-values of 0.023 and 0.008. Both p-values support the hypothesis that there is significant difference in the proctored plus out-of-class grade distribution compared to the reference."
