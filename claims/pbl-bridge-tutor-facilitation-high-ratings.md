`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Tutor evaluation questionnaire using a 5-point Likert scale across seven cohorts (N = 1,072). The article reports "The highest-rated item was 'Fully prepared, punctual, and manages session time effectively' (Item 1, M = 4.73, SD = 0.45)", with all ten items above 4.2 and the lowest being inclusive climate (M = 4.23, SD = 0.59).

> "The highest-rated item was "Fully prepared, punctual, and manages session time effectively" (Item 1, M = 4.73, SD = 0.45), followed by "Asks open-ended, probing questions that deepen inquiry" (Item 3, M = 4.68, SD = 0.47)."
