## Evidence

### Stuart Luppescu 1995

Stuart Luppescu, Anthony S. Bryk, Paul Deabster, John Q. Easton, and Yeow Meng Thum. (1995). School Reform, Retention Policy, and Student Achievement Gains. University of Chicago draft report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-reform-retention-policy-and-student-achievement-gains

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of equated ITBS gain scores (1987-1992, ~one million records) matching retained and promoted students on a logistic-regression propensity-for-retention measure. The article reports that retained students "made smaller gains than students who were promoted" in almost all cases; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Comparing gains made by the students who were retained with those who were not retained shows that in almost all cases, students who were retained made smaller gains than students who were promoted."
