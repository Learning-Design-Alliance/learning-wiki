Ji Ying 2026¶

Ji Ying. (2026). Academic Integrity in the Age of Generative AI: a Scoping Review of Research on Higher Education Student Voices. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09752-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Huang et al.'s (2025) study of 522 Chinese university students using an Ethical Dissonance Index, that "the study identifies a pervasive/illegitimate cluster of students who frequently engage in behaviours they view as unethical" among four dissonance-based clusters.

"the study identifies a pervasive/illegitimate cluster of students who frequently engage in behaviours they view as unethical, highlighting a clear gap or collision between students' awareness of ethical issues and their continued engagement in unethical practices"

Ji Ying 2026 (2)¶

Ji Ying. (2026). Academic Integrity in the Age of Generative AI: a Scoping Review of Research on Higher Education Student Voices. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09752-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Ofem et al.'s (2025) structural equation modelling of 4,679 Nigerian university students found "students with positive perceptions of ChatGPT were more prone to use it for dishonest academic purposes", while positive attitudes toward academic integrity served as a significant negative mediator. No effect size is printed.

"students with positive perceptions of ChatGPT were more prone to use it for dishonest academic purposes"

Related Claims¶

  • The article interprets the ethics gap as students viewing ethics through coursework practices and instructors treating it as an integrity question shaped by lack of institutional clarity — related
  • University students' engagement with AI tools tends to cluster at two extremes: avoidance and uncritical use — related
  • Research on student voices on academic integrity in GenAI use is nascent and methodologically immature, with most instruments self-developed and all reviewed studies cross-sectional — related
