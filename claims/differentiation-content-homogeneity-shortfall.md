Cosine-similarity analysis of program supply profiles against occupation-family demand profiles (Figure 6). Own-family cells average 0.88 against 0.722 off-diagonal, though Computer Science and Information Technology sit nearest the Computer Engineering family, an artifact of the shared computing core.

> "On the full realized curriculum their pairwise domain-profile similarity is high (cos = 0.835), reflecting the large shared core, but on the major blocks alone it falls to 0.535, confirming that the programs diverge in their specializations."
