Observational analysis of institutional data from 109 teacher education students at PCC across academic years 2019/20–2021/22, with graduation rates calculated by the study authors. The report states "Thirty-three of the 109 teacher education students" graduated within two years, and notes the result is similar to PCC's IPEDS 2019 overall graduation rate of 34 percent.

"Thirty-three of the 109 teacher education students enrolled at PCC seeking associate’s degrees during 2019/20–2021/22 graduated within two years6 (six terms), which PCC considers on time for full-time students."
