# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, prospective physics teachers’ self-rated perceptions of ChatGPT’s answer quality shifted slightly toward more negative ratings after criterion-based evaluation; the shift was not statistically significant

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After engaging in criterion-based evaluation of ChatGPT-generated didactic content, students’ mean rating of answer quality decreased from 3.16 to 2.66 on a 5-point scale; the change was not reported as statistically significant. ## Evidence

### Farahnaz Sadidi and Thomas Prestel 2025

Farahnaz Sadidi and Thomas Prestel. (2025). Impact of Criterion-Based Reflection on Prospective Physics Teachers’ Perceptions of ChatGPT-Generated Content. TU Dresden. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/s44217-025-01071-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-025-01071-8)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post questionnaire ratings from the exploratory study of 39 prospective physics teachers evaluating ChatGPT3.5 answers to didactical tasks; the mean quality rating "decreased from 3.16 to 2.66" (Table II reports SDs of .76 and .85). No significance test value is printed for this shift.

> "The results show a slight shift towards a more negative perception of its answer quality. This shift is also reflected in the mean value, which decreased from 3.16 to 2.66"
