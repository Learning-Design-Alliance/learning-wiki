## Evidence

### Owens Melinda T. 2017

Owens Melinda T., Tanner Kimberly D. (2017). Teaching as Brain Changing: Exploring Connections between Neuroscience and Innovative Teaching. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-01-0005

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review of laboratory neuroscience studies cited in the article's section on factors affecting neural change. Dopamine is associated with reward or anticipation of reward and acetylcholine with novelty or surprise; the article reports that "blocking either neurotransmitter impairs synaptic plasticity, while the presence of either neurotransmitter enhances it." No effect sizes are printed.

> "Unsurprisingly, blocking either neurotransmitter impairs synaptic plasticity, while the presence of either neurotransmitter enhances it (Conner et al., 2003; Reed et al., 2011; Froemke et al., 2013; Takeuchi et al., 2014)."
