## Evidence

### Cardile Davide 2023

Cardile Davide, Ielo Augusto, Corallo Francesco, Cappadona Irene, D'Aleo Giangaetano, De Cola Maria Cristina, Bramanti Placido, Ciurleo Rosella. (2023). Communication Training: Significance and Effects of a Preliminary Psychological Intervention upon an Audit Team. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054173

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post assessment of the 10-member audit team using the 54-item Interpersonal Communication Inventory before and after training. Scores rose from a T0 median of 80.1 to a T1 median of 90.5; the statistical analysis revealed significant differences between T0 and T1 for each questionnaire administered, with no effect size printed.

> "The ICI test at T0 resulted in a score between 75 and 87, with a median value of 80.1. As shown in Figure 3, the ICI median scores obtained by males and females at T0 were 79.5 and 80.5, respectively. The ICI score at T1 was in the range of 83 to 95, with a median value of 90.5"
