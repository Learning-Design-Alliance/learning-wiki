## Evidence

### Philip Parker 2021

Philip Parker, Kelly-Ann Allen, Rhiannon Parker, Jiesi Guo, Herbert W Marsh, Geetanjali Basarkod, and Theresa Dicke. (2021). School Belonging Predicts whether an Emerging Adult will be Not in Education, Employment, or Training (NEET) after School. https://www.pwc.com.au/government/government-matters/youth-unemployment.html

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Exploratory multilevel model predicting belonging (Table 2): SES beta 0.12, Gender 0.15, Urban 0.07, Indigenous 0.05, Immigrant 0.06, Achievement 0.01; all weak effects. Cohort (2015) beta was -0.20.

> "Girls, urban youth, Immigrants, and high SES children had higher levels of belonging. Academic achievement was also a signiﬁcant predictor but the eﬀect size was not practically signiﬁcant. Indeed, the demographic and achievement predictors all had relatively weak eﬀect sizes."
