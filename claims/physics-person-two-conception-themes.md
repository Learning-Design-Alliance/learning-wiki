`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of 120 open-ended survey responses from students at 88 institutions attending CUWiP 2023, coded independently by four authors through descriptive, in vivo, and provisional cycles. The analysis identified "two predominant themes: (1) someone who enjoys and is passionate about physics" and academic/research ability.

> "Our analysis of student responses identified two predominant themes: (1) someone who enjoys and is passionate about physics, at times to the exclusion of other interests; and (2) someone who is good at physics and research, to whom concepts come naturally."
