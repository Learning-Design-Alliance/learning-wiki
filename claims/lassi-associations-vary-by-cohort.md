### Zhou Yuanyuan 2026

Zhou Yuanyuan, Sechelski Amber, Mowdy Brent, Johnson Tess, Vernardo Nikea, West Courtney A. (2026). Data driven student support: utilize the LASSI to improve academic performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09519-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory cohort-level correlational analyses found that, for example, Information Processing showed consistent associations in Cohort 2024 while "no LASSI subscale demonstrated a consistent association with any of the four academic outcomes" in Cohort 2025.

> "The results revealed substantial variability across cohorts, suggesting that the strength and consistency of the associations were highly dependent on the samples included in the correlational analyses."
