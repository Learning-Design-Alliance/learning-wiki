## Evidence

### Luke McCrone & Martyn Kingsbury 2024

Luke McCrone & Martyn Kingsbury. (2024). An ecological approach to understanding transitions and tensions in complex learning contexts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00267-1

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Ethnographic observation of a bridge period in which two 1st year students discussed fluid mechanics content in the breakout space, confirmed in field interviews where they explained "Yes we were discussing concepts from the lecture" and cited the space's convenient, quiet location. A separate group of five students showed similar behaviour remaining in the lecture theatre.

> "These findings suggest that the type 2 configuration may have the potential for more meaningful transition engagement than the type 1 configuration by providing 1st year students (who planned to attend both lectures) with a'bridge'11:00–12:00 containing 1 h of self-directed pedagogic potential sandwiched between 2 h of formal teaching contact."
