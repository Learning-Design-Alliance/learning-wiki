### P G Kubendran Amos 2023

P G Kubendran Amos. (2023). Befriending ChatGPT and other superchatbots: An AI-integrated take-home assessment preserving integrity. Preprint. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.4484188](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4484188)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational outcomes discussion of a take-home assignment given to 78 second-year undergraduate students in thirteen self-formed groups, all answering the same two questions. The author reports "no noticeable similarities" across groups and attributes the varied answers to different approaches in utilising ChatGPT.

> "A cursory view of the responses from the thirteen groups revealed no noticeable similarities. Stated otherwise, the required involvement of ChatGPT seemingly improved the integrity of the take-home assessment, considering same questions were assigned to every group."
