(Meta) Competences for Digital Practice: Educational Scenarios for the Workplace of the Future Exemplified by Building Information Modeling Work Processes. [doi:10.3390/knowledge2030027](https://doi.org/10.3390/knowledge2030027)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Theme from the semi-structured interviews (11 students, video chat, 10–15 minutes each); printed statement counts (17, 3, 2) ground the claim. Qualitative analysis only.

> "In a total of 17 statements, the enormous importance of competences for handling digital tools was emphasized. In three statements, domain competences, i.e., knowledge of BIM, were considered to be very valuable. In two statements, on the other hand, domain competences were valued less strongly, but rather knowledge of the concrete software was emphasized as relevant."
