## Evidence

### Störmann S 2016

Störmann S, Stankiewicz M, Raes P, Berchtold C, Kosanke Y, Illes G, Loose P, Angstwurm MW. (2016). How well do final year undergraduate medical students master practical clinical skills? GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001057](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001057)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire-based self-assessment (170 of 214 participants, 79.4%) compared with converted OSCE scores on a 5-point scale; the article prints r=0.26, with 30.0% over-estimating by one and 18.8% by two marks.

> "Self-assessed performance and total examination score correlated positively and significantly (r=0.26; p<0.001). On average, students over-estimated their performance by half an examination mark. 60 participants (35.3%) correctly assessed their performance."
