Evidence¶

Hu 2024¶

Hu, Y., Wouters, P., van der Schaaf, M., & Kester, L. (2024). The effects of achievement goal instructions in game-based learning on students' achievement goals, performance, and achievement emotions. Learning and Instruction, 93, 101943. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101943

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the randomized experiment (N = 450), robust regression found no significant main effects of mastery-approach goal instructions on mastery-approach goals after controlling for teacher and prior achievement goals; the article also reports no significant main effects on achievement emotions.

"There were no statistically significant main effects on mastery-approach goals after controlling for teacher and prior achievement goals. As a result, mastery-approach goal instructions were not included in the further analysis."
