# Students who formulated their own rules for when to use AI performed better on assignments in both conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in either condition who had set themselves rules for AI use (e.g., building a solution first, delegating syntax only) scored better on assignments. [→ Azimi 2026](#azimi-2026)

## Evidence

### Azimi 2026

Azimi, S. (2026). AI Literacy over Tool Design: A Mixed-Methods Study of Scaffolded versus Unrestricted Generative AI in Programming Education. Delft University of Technology. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16784

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interview and reflection data from the pilot identified students in both conditions who had formulated rules such as "building a solution first and using AI only to check it"; these students "performed better on the assignments regardless of the condition". No effect size is printed.

> "These students performed better on the assignments regardless of the condition to which they had been assigned."
