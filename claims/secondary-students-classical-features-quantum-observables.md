Interpretive review of empirical literature at the quasi-qualitative representational level, citing interview data from Ravaioli and Levrini (2017) in which students stated that "The object itself does own a well-defined property". The review attributes this difficulty to the trajectory of the concept of physical quantity in theory change.

> "Secondary students tend to ascribe classical-like features to quantum observables, and even struggle to accept the possibility of quantities having no definite value."
