## Evidence

### Ricky Ellis 2022

Ricky Ellis, Peter A Brennan, Amanda J Lee, Duncan SG Scrimgeour, Jennifer Cleland. (2022). Differential attainment at MRCS according to gender, ethnicity, age and socioeconomic factors: a retrospective cohort study. Journal of the Royal Society of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1177/01410768221079018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate logistic regression in the Part B cohort (final model N = 1125) adjusting for EPM decile and all sociodemographic factors. The article reports "having attended a fee-paying school (OR 1.51, 95% CI 1.08–2.10)" as an independent predictor of Part B success; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "being a non-graduate (OR 1.77, 95% CI 1.15–2.71) and having attended a fee-paying school (OR 1.51, 95% CI 1.08–2.10) were found to be independent predictors of Part B success, with gender no longer being a significant predictor (p > 0.05)."
