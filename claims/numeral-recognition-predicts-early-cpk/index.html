Evidence¶

Geary David C. 2019¶

Geary David C., vanMarle Kristy, Chu Felicia W., Hoard Mary K., Nugent Lara. (2019). Predicting Age of Becoming a Cardinal Principle Knower. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000277

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Binomial logistic regression contrasting early CPKs (already CPK at preschool start) with all other children in the 197-child sample. The model was highly significant, χ2(4) = 98.91, p < .0001, and correctly classified 88% of children.

"a 1 SD increase in numeral recognition resulted in a 3.5-fold increase in the odds of being an early cardinal principle knower [χ2(1) = 24.19, p < .0001, 95% confidence interval 2.1 to 5.8]."
