## Evidence

### Polly K. Lai 2026

Polly K. Lai. (2026). Enhancing learning of nanoscale phenomena: the role of agent-based models and embodied reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09776-x

Repeated-measures ANOVA in the same 27-participant randomized experiment found a significant main effect of Time on declarative knowledge, "F(1, 25) = 9.24, p =.005, η2 =.27"; the article reports no Group × Time interaction for this outcome.

> "For declarative knowledge, there was a significant main effect of Time, F(1, 25) = 9.24, p =.005, η2 =.27, indicating overall learning gains across groups (see Fig. 3)."
