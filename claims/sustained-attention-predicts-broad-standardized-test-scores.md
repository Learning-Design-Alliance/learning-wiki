## Evidence

### Gallen 2023

Gallen, C. L., Schaerlaeken, S., Younger, J. W., Project iLEAD Consortium, Anguera, J. A., & Gazzaley, A. (2023). Contribution of sustained attention abilities to real-world academic skills in children. Scientific Reports 13, 2673. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-29427-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear mixed models predicting Smarter Balanced (SBAC) statewide standardized test scores in math and English Language Arts in the same school-based sample. The article reports "positive main effects" for both subjects with the printed F and p values; no standardized effect size is reported.

> "attention showed positive main effects on measures of math (F(1, 621) = 9.65, p = 0.002, R2 m (model) = 0.29, R2 c (model) = 0.35) and ELA (F(1, 621) = 22.70, p < 0.001, R2 m (model) = 0.25, R2 c (model) = 0.30)"
