Evidence¶

Skagerlund K 2019¶

Skagerlund K, Östergren R, Västfjäll D, Träff U (2019) How does mathematics anxiety impair mathematical abilities? Investigating the link between math anxiety, working memory, and number processing. PLoS ONE 14(1): e0211283. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0211283

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Authors' interpretation of the fitted arithmetic SEM (N = 170 adults): beyond the two significant indirect paths, a direct MA→arithmetic path remained in the model. The direct effect is a model estimate, not an isolated experimental manipulation.

"For arithmetic calculation ability, we find that MA has an indirect effect through both WM and basic number processing as well as a direct effect above and beyond the indirect effects."
