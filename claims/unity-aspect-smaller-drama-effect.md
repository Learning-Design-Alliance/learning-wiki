## Evidence

### Bataineh 2017

Bataineh, R. F., & Salah, N. A. (2017). The Effectiveness of Drama-Based Instruction in Jordanian EFL Students' Writing Performance. TESOL International Journal, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1247797

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test unity scores (Table 4): experimental mean 1.63 (SD 0.54) versus control 1.35 (SD 0.48), t = 2.40, df = 78, Sig. 0.019 — the smallest t of the five components. The researchers suggest more teacher emphasis on unity might have improved scores further.

> "The  results  reveal  a  statistically  signifcant,  albeit  smaller,  difference  in  students' performance  on  the unity component."
