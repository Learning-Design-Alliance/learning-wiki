Does students' awareness of school-track-related stereotypes exacerbate inequalities in education? npj Science of Learning 8:59. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00203-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional (RQ3) paths from the multigroup growth curve models for engagement, controlling for SES, gender, and migration background, were significant and negative in all three tracks; the article reports this as limited evidence for the stereotype-awareness-as-harmful-for-all hypothesis.

> "Initial levels of school-track-related stereotype awareness in Grade 5 were significantly and negatively related to initial levels of engagement in the lowest track (b = −0.360, p < 0.001), the higher track (b = −0.594, p < 0.001), and the combined track (b = −0.469, p < 0.001"
