## Evidence

### Shakibaei 2013

Shakibaei, Z., Rahimaghaee, F., & Arish, M. (2013). Effect of Educational Games on Social Skills in Iranian Mentally Retarded Children. li-manager's Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1101771

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second-hypothesis ANCOVA on the self-help dressing subscale in the same pre-test/post-test design with 10 test and 10 control girls; moderated means were 8.033 (test) versus 5.017 (control). The article reports a significant factor effect with no effect size printed.

> "Moderated mean scores suggest that the test group (based on performance of the educational games) significantly dominates the control group (conventional method) in terms of their scores, such that the educational games have a positive effect on the self-help for wearing clothes."
