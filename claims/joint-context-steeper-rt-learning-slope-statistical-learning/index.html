Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang. (2024). Interbrain neural correlates of self and other integration in joint statistical learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00280-4

Behavioural LMM analysis of actors' RTs across Blocks 1–6 in dyads randomly assigned to a joint (both respond) or baseline (actor plus passive observer) context. The phase × context interaction was significant (β = −3.50, P = 0.007), with "the joint context exhibited a facilitation effect on sequence learning"; no printed effect size.
