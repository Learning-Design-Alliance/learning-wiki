# All computer vision cluster means increased post-PD and the most descriptive cluster became more detailed, indicating teachers viewed computer vision constructs as more descriptive after PD

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The means for all computer vision clusters increased post-PD, and cluster 1 (the highest-mean cluster) grew to contain more constructs after PD. [→ Kurz 2026](#kurz-2026)

## Evidence

### Kurz 2026

Kurz, T. L., Jayasuriya, S., Swisher, K., Mativo, J., Pidaparti, R., & Robinson, D. T. (2026). Computer vision versus human vision: analyzing middle school teachers' construct restructuring following computer vision professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10594-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive statistics from the computer vision repertory grids (Tables 1 and 2) show that every cluster's mean rose post-PD and that cluster 1, the most descriptive cluster, expanded from four to twelve constructs. The authors report "The means for all clusters increased post-PD," indicating teachers rated computer vision constructs as more descriptive after the intervention.

> "Cluster 1 had the highest mean for both pre-PD and post-PD, as well as the lowest standard deviation out of all the clusters both pre-PD and post-PD. A high mean indicates that the participants thought of the cluster as very descriptive of computer vision. The means for all clusters increased post-PD."
