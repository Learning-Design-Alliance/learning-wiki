## Evidence

### Fuhrmann Delia 2019

Fuhrmann Delia, Schweizer Susanne, Leung Jovita, Griffin Cait, Blakemore Sarah-Jayne. (2019). The neurocognitive correlates of academic diligence in adolescent girls. Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1080/17588928.2018.1504762

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ROI analysis of fMRI data from 40 girls aged 14–15 during an emotional go-no-go task, using linear mixed models with diligence as a z-scored fixed effect. The diligence effect on IFG activation was significant (χ2 = 4.03, p = .045), with "higher diligence showed higher activation of the IFG" (β = 0.20); no effect size beyond β was printed.

> "Diligence correlated significantly with IFG activation in the task overall, but not with activation of the DS or VS (Table 3). Participants with higher diligence showed higher activation of the IFG during the go-no-go task (β = 0.20; Figure 3)."
