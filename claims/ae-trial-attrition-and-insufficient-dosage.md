# Retention and delivered instructional minutes did not differ significantly between experimental and control conditions, and completers received well under planned dosage

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participant retention rates (51% experimental vs. 59% control) and average minutes of instruction delivered to completers did not differ significantly between conditions. [→ Hock Michael F. 2011](#hock-michael-f-2011)
`q3 i?` Completers received only 68% to 86% of planned instructional hours depending on strategy, with only Prediction approaching the prescribed dosage. [→ Hock Michael F. 2011 (2)](#hock-michael-f-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Hock Michael F. 2011

Hock Michael F., Mellard Daryl F. (2011). Efficacy of learning strategies instruction in adult basic education. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, 4(2), 134-153. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2011.555291

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retention and attendance analysis in the RCT results section. T-tests also showed no significant differences in average minutes of instruction delivered to completers by strategy (Bridging p = .805, Fluency p = .839, Prediction p = .145, Summarization p = .463).

> "The data indicate no significant difference in the overall participant retention rates in experimental (51%) and control (59%) classes (p = .147)"

### Hock Michael F. 2011 (2)

Hock Michael F., Mellard Daryl F. (2011). Efficacy of learning strategies instruction in adult basic education. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, 4(2), 134-153. https://doi.org/10.1080/19345747.2011.555291

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dosage analysis from Table 2 of the RCT. Planned instruction ranged from 19 to 34 hours across strategies; the article states "Prediction was the only course that approached the prescribed dosage."

> "instruction time for Bridging completers averaged 69% of planned hours, that is, 19 out of a possible 27 hours; Fluency completers averaged 13 out of 19 hours (68%); Prediction completers averaged 30 out of 34 hours (86%); and Summarization completers averaged 17 out of 23 hours of instruction (74%)."
