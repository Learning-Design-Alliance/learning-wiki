# Most pilot teachers used the Write to Succeed practices and found them easy to use and helpful for students, with ease of use increasing across semesters

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · design `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 85% of teachers reported using each WTS practice (academic language/vocabulary development, text jumble and cohesion analysis, jointly constructing text) at least once in classroom instruction (N = 20). [→ Patrick Rich 2025](#patrick-rich-2025)
`q2 i?` Perceived ease of use increased from first to second semester for all three practices (e.g., jointly constructing text from 60% to 89% somewhat or very easy). [→ Patrick Rich 2025 (2)](#patrick-rich-2025-2)
`q2 i?` Among teachers who used the practices, 94-100% rated each practice helpful or very helpful for students (N = 17). [→ Patrick Rich 2025 (3)](#patrick-rich-2025-3)

### Patrick Rich 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Survey of teachers who used a practice each semester (N = 18 first semester, N = 17 second) shows ease-of-use percentages rising for all three practices, e.g., jointly constructing text from 60% to 89%.

> "Teachers who used the practices indicated that they were easy to use, and the perceived ease of use increased across semesters."
