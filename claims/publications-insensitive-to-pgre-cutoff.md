### Emily M. Levesque 2015

Emily M. Levesque, Rachel Bezanson, & Grant R. Tremblay. (2015). Physics GRE Scores of Prize Postdoctoral Fellows in Astronomy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Figure 5 compares histograms of first-author papers written during and exiting graduate school for fellows below PGRE thresholds from 20-60%, with means and medians indicated per threshold; Table 2 gives totals for thresholds from 10-90%. Means and medians do not vary meaningfully with cutoff.

> "The general shape of the histogram and the average number of publications are both insensitive to PGRE cutoﬀ score."
