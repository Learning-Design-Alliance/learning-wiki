Pontefract S. K., Wilson K. (2019). Using electronic patient records: defining learning outcomes for undergraduate education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1466-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consensus-development study: a National Working Group of academics across 12 institutions, two students, and NHS and EPR supplier representatives discussed and documented competencies, with "A competency was defined to the group as “an observable behaviour in the context of the role of the healthcare professional”". The group produced six domains and 29 learning outcomes before external review.

> "The working group agreed upon six domains of competence and 29 learning outcomes related to the training of undergraduate healthcare students in the context of EPRs (Additional file 1)."
