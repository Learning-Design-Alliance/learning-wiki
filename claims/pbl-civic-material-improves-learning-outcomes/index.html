Evidence¶

Wulandari 2018¶

Wulandari, T., Suharno, & Triyanto. (2018). Field trial analysis of teaching material civic education based on problem based learning (PBL) to improve student’s outcome. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 4(4), 259-265. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.4.4.259

q2 · i? · design · r3

Extensive test using a quasi-experimental Only Pretest-Posttest Control Group Design with fourth-grade classes; non-independent t-test in SPSS 21.0 at 0.05 significance level after normality and homogeneity checks. The article reports "sig (2 -tailed) 0.000 < 0.05"; no effect size is printed.

"T-test performed shows that sig (2 -tailed) 0.000 < 0.05 indicates H0 is rejected. It can be concluded that the teaching material Civic education based PBL can improve the student learning outcomes."
