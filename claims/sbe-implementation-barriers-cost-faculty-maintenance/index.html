Eid Farida 2026¶

Eid Farida, Maryum Rida, Waugh Lucy, Kilian Paddy, Atiomo William, Al-Hammadi Suleiman. (2026). Simulation-Based Education (SBE) in paediatric undergraduate medical education: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09730-6

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of implementation barriers reported in Table 3 across the 30 studies. It reports that "Resource limitations represented the primary challenge", alongside technical hurdles (equipment maintenance, software requirements, platform stability, internet reliability, motion sickness) and time constraints on scenario development and faculty preparation.

"Resource limitations represented the primary challenge, with cost constraints affecting high-fidelity manikins [31, 33], virtual reality [22, 45, 47], surgical simulators [28], and comprehensive curricula [30]."
