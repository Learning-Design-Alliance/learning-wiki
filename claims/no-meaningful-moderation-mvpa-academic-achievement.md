# Student- and school-level characteristics do not meaningfully moderate the school-day MVPA–academic achievement relationship in elementary students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cross-sectional and longitudinal multilevel analyses found no significant moderation of the MVPA–academic achievement relationship by student gender, FRL status, race/ethnicity, or school-level SES, physical activity environment, or opportunities, after Bonferroni correction. ## Evidence

### Behringer Hannah K. 2022

Behringer Hannah K., Saksvig Emilie R., Boedeker Peter J., Elish Paul N., Kay Christi M., Calvert Hannah G., Meyer Adria M., Gazmararian Julie A. (2022). Physical activity and academic achievement: an analysis of potential student- and school-level moderators. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-022-01348-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel moderation analysis of 4,936 Grade 4 students in 40 schools, with accelerometer-measured school-day MVPA across three semesters and multiple imputation. The authors conclude that "statistically significant results were observed for certain outcomes, practical differences were negligible." No effect sizes printed; i is null.

> "Overall, our results did not suggest that student- or school-level characteristics moderate the MVPA-academic achievement relationship. While statistically significant results were observed for certain outcomes, practical differences were negligible."
