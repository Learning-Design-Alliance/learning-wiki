## Evidence

### Doğan Yunus 2026

Doğan Yunus, Kılıç Zeynep, Kalınkara Yusuf, Talan Tarık. (2026). The Impact of Artificial Intelligence-Supported Instruction on Student Learning in STEM: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence14060109

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis across the 35 studies compared effect sizes by educational level using the Q test. High school showed g = 1.099, elementary g = 0.465, middle school g = 0.392, university g = 0.578; "The test of between-group heterogeneity was significant (Q = 30.13, df = 3, p < 0.001)."

> "Based on student level, the highest effect size was observed at the high school level (g = 1.099, 95% CI [0.89, 1.30], Z = 10.53, p < 0.001). In contrast, the effects were lower at the elementary school (g = 0.465) and middle school (g = 0.392) levels."
