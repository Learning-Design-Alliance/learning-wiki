`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Standardized parameter evaluation across CFA, ESEM, Bifactor-CFA and Bifactor-ESEM (Table 7). All principal loads were "greater than 0.50" and cross-loadings below 0.30, supporting discriminant validity; items X1–X12 each loaded highest on their intended dimension.

> "all the principal loads of the above items are greater than 0.50, which is reflected as strong item -factor consistency and ideal convergence validity 63. If the cross-loading remains below 0.30, it indicates that there are typical differences between each index and the non-target factor"
