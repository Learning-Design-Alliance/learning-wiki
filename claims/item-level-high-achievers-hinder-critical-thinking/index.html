q2 · i1 · associational · r1

Item-level Spearman correlations with student-reported GPA band (Table 2), conducted because the grouped indices had weak internal consistency. Strongest item associations ranged from ρ = −0.285 (autonomy) to ρ = 0.252 (reliance hinders critical thinking), all p < 0.001.

"High-achieving students were less likely to report that AI supported autonomy, helped them learn more effectively, or formed part of active academic engagement. They were also more likely to agree that reliance on AI hinders critical thinking and independent problem solving."
