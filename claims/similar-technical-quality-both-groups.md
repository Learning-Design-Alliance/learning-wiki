## Evidence

### Biagioni Gabriela 2026

Biagioni Gabriela, Comodo Fernanda, Coelho Marcelo Santos. (2026). Technical Quality and Students' Perception of Endodontic Preclinical Training Using Natural or LikeReal Artificial Teeth. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Assessment of 408 teeth (204 per group) found no coronal perforation in either group. Remaining gutta-percha was similar between groups (22.06% NTG, 17.64% ATG), leading authors to conclude both teeth types were similar in this aspect.

> "The results of the errors in the crowns found no perforation during the access of the teeth in both groups. This fact can be attributed to the good training the students received."
