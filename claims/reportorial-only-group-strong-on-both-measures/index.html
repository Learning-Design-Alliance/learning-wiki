Katsioloudis et al. 2012¶

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretive observation (type e) from the Data Analysis section of the N = 43 quasi-experiment, following the non-significant quiz ANOVA; the reportorial group "was the only group to score well with both hands-on demonstration and written comprehension".

"However, it should be noted that the group who received instruction with reportorial views was the only group to score well with both hands-on demonstration and written comprehension."
