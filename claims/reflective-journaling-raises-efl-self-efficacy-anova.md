## Evidence

### Baleghizadeh 2014

Baleghizadeh, S., & Mortazavi, M. (2014). The impact of different types of journaling techniques on EFL learners’ self-efficacy. PROFILE Issues in Teachers’ Professional Development, 16(1), 77-88. https://doi.org/10.15446/profile.v16n1.37184

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experimental study with 60 upper-intermediate Iranian EFL learners randomly assigned to four conditions (NFC, TFC, PFC, NJC), 15 per condition. Posttest SGSES scores differed significantly across conditions: "the one-way ANOVA yielded a significant main effect of the experimental conditions F (3, 56) = 46.94" with ω² = .061 described as a medium effect.

> "the one-way ANOVA yielded a significant main effect of the experimental conditions F (3, 56) = 46.94. The effect size, calculated using omega squared (ω²), was .061, indicating a medium effect."
