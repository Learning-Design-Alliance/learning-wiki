## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Video podcasts were rated lower than live lectures on presentation (mean 1.5 vs 1.1, p < 0.001) and content (mean 1.5 vs 1.2, p = 0.002) on a 5-point scale. [→ Schreiber Benjamin E 2010](#schreiber-benjamin-e-2010)

## Evidence

### Schreiber Benjamin E 2010

Schreiber Benjamin E, Fukuta Junaid, Gordon Fabiana. (2010). Live lecture versus video podcast in undergraduate medical education: A randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-68

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire ratings from the 66 responding students in the crossover trial. Podcasts rated lower on presentation ("mean 1.5 for video podcasts compared to mean 1.1 for live lecture", p < 0.001) and content (mean 1.5 vs 1.2, p = 0.002). Only means and p-values printed; no effect size.

> "Comparison of the video podcast to the live lecture on presentation shows that the video podcast was rated lower than the lecture both on quality of presentation (mean 1.5 for video podcasts compared to mean 1.1 for live lecture on a 5 point scale, where 1 is the best and 5 is the worst, p < 0.001)."
