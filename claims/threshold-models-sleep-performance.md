## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, M., Chen, Z., Han, N., & Yao, H. (2025). Sleep duration and subject-specific academic performance among adolescents in China. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00361-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Double-threshold regression for Chinese, mathematics, and science detected two significant thresholds between 7–8 h and 8–9 h; coefficients rose markedly in the 7–9 h window before turning negative above it.

> "once sleep exceeded the second threshold (8-9 h), the coefﬁcients became signiﬁcantly negative, implying that excessive sleep duration may be associated with lower academic outcomes."
