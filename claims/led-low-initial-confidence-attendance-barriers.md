`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Targeted needs assessment: semi-structured interviews with 30 LEDs (8 UK graduates, 24 IMGs; median 3 years since graduation). The thematic analysis found "Initial confidence levels were generally low", a qualitative finding with no effect size.

> "Initial confidence levels were generally low, but exposure to clinical practice significantly improved confidence."
