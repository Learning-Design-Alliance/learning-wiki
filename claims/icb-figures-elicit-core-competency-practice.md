## Evidence

### Finby B 2021

Finby B, Heyer LJ, Malcolm Campbell A. (2021). Data‐rich textbook figures promote core competencies: Comparison of two textbooks. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21488

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretation of the think-aloud transcripts: students describing ICB figures in their own words (e.g., comparing unlabeled graph lines, reading axes) were judged to practice core competencies, even though the interviewed students had never read either textbook and approached figures out of context.

> "Specifically, these students employed the process of science when they evaluated experimental evidence and developing problem‐solving strategies. The students also revealed quantitative reasoning when they interpreted graphs and used statistical measures to evaluate diverse data."
