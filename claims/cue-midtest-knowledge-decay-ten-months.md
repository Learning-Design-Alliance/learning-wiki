## Evidence

### Zwolak 2015

Zwolak, J. P. and Manogue, C. A. (2015). Assessing student reasoning in upper-division electricity and magnetism at Oregon State University. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020125)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of first and second CUE midtest scores for N = 57 students tested about 10 months apart, spanning a gap with no E&M-related courses. The authors report an average relative loss of "17.3± 4.2%" and retention over 80%.

> "When compared to the ﬁrst midtest, students lost on average 9.7± 2.4%, wherein N = 18 improved their scores by 10% on average and N = 39 had scores lower than on the ﬁrst midtest by 19% on average."
