Chen 2023¶

Chen, T.-I., Lin, S.-K., & Chung, H.-C. (2023). Gamified educational robots lead an increase in motivation and creativity in STEM education. Journal of Baltic Science Education, 22(3), 427-438. https://doi.org/10.33225/jbse/23.22.427

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent t-test on the 50-item Creativity Assessment Packet in the same quasi-experiment; experimental group mean 4.61 vs control 4.06, t = 3.417, p = .001. The article prints no effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
