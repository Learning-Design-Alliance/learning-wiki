# Tertiary First Step improves positive peer interactions and reduces negative peer interactions within the intervention group

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Positive interactions with peers increased from baseline (M = 27.3, SD = 16.5) to post-intervention (M = 35.2, SD = 17.1), and negative interactions decreased from 4.3 (SD = 4.1) to 1.7 (SD = 1.9), with medium-to-large within-subject effects. [→ Frey 2015](#frey-2015)

## Evidence

### Frey 2015

Frey, A. J., Small, J. W., Lee, J., Walker, H. M., Seeley, J. R., Feil, E. G., & Golly, A. (2015). Expanding the range of the First Step to Success intervention: Tertiary-level support for children, teachers, and families. Early Childhood Research Quarterly 30 (2015) 1–11. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2014.05.002

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject ANOVA on Peer Social Behavior partial-interval observation data for the intervention group. The article reports positive interactions "increased from baseline (M[SD] = 27.3[16.5]) to post intervention (M[SD] = 35.2[17.1])" and negative interactions decreased, with partial r of .42 and .53.

> "The effect size for positive interactions (F[1,30] = 6.04, p = .020) was .42 and .53 for negative interactions (F[1,30] = 11.59, p = .002). Positive interactions with peers increased from baseline (M[SD] = 27.3[16.5]) to post intervention (M[SD] = 35.2[17.1]) and negative interactions with peers decreased from 4.3 (SD = 4.1) to 1.7 (SD = 1.9)"
