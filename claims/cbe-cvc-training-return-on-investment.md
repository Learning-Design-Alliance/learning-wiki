Narrative review synthesis of the three level-5 studies (Burden, Cohen, Sherertz), all concerning central venous catheterization. The review reports "return on investment in the range of a minimum of $63,000 over 18 months up to $700,000 per year"; no effect size is calculated.

> "The results of all three studies investigating Kirkpatrick Level 5 show cost-saving potential because of prevention of complications and improved patient outcomes. The studies report return on investment in the range of a minimum of $63,000 over 18 months up to $700,000 per year"
