q3 · i? · causal · r2

Cluster-randomised controlled trial in 45 Greater Manchester primary schools analysing Year 5 InCAS and Year 6 KS2 outcomes via hierarchical linear modelling. The report states "the overall trend is that PATHS failed to impact upon pupils' attainment", with Year 6 English g = -0.106 significant favouring control.

"The only exceptions are an effect on KS2 English scores at intention to treat favouring the control group and a marginal , non-significant effect (p = 0.06) on KS2 English scores for FSM eligible pupils favouring the PAT HS group."
