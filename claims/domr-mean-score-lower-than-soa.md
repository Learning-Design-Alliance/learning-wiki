### Somya Swarnkar 2023

Somya Swarnkar, Rittick Roy, Tejinder Kaur, and David Blair. (2023). On the effectiveness of the early introduction of modern physics in school curriculum: the case of the structure of atom versus wave-particle duality. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.16525](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.16525)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire study of 20 volunteered first- and second-year Indian undergraduates; 10 multiple-choice and 2 subjective questions graded manually. Mean scores were "5 .16/6.00" for SoA and "3 .70/6.00" for DoMR, about 28% lower; no statistical test is reported.

> "The mean score of the students from the questionnaire in the section of structure of atom was found to be 5 .16/6.00. The mean score for the section of dual nature of matter and radia- tion was found to be 3 .70/6.00, ∼ 28% lower than that seen in case of structure of atom."
