`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview responses about the fictitious spectrum histogram. The article reports explanations "were coded across all four categories", with more than half citing physical principles (multiple peaks as intrinsic properties) and more than half citing physical limitations, emphasizing background light.

> "For the spectrum experiment ( quantum), participants' re- sponses did not match their reasoning about either the ball- drop or single-slit experiments, and explanations were coded across all four categories."
