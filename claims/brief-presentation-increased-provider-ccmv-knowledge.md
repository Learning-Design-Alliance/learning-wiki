## Evidence

### Pesch Megan H. 2020

Pesch Megan H., Anderson Carter, Mowers Erika. (2020). Improving Obstetric Provider Congenital Cytomegalovirus Knowledge and Practices. Infectious Diseases in Obstetrics and Gynecology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/8875494

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test comparing pre- and post-intervention knowledge scores for the 24 dual survey completers after a 20-minute PowerPoint grand-rounds presentation on cCMV. Post-intervention mean rose to 29.33 of 36 (81.5% correct). The article prints no standardized effect size for this gain.

> "For the individuals who completed both surveys (n = 24), there was an improvement in their Overall cCMV Knowledge of 9.67 points (SE = 0.97) or 26.9% more answers correct (p < .0001)."
