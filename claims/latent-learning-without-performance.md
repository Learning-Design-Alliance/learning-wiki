## Evidence

### Soderstrom 2015

Nicholas C. Soderstrom and Robert A. Bjork. (2015). Learning Versus Performance: An Integrative Review. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615569000

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review of the classic Tolman and Honzik three-group T-maze experiment, in which a delayed-reinforcement group dropped to the level of the regularly reinforced group once food was introduced on Day 11. The review reports this as evidence that "learning occurred when performance was stagnant."

> "Thus, delaying reinforcement revealed that the rats did, indeed, learn the maze while no reinforcement was provided and their behavior seemed rather aimless. In other words, learning occurred when performance was stagnant."
