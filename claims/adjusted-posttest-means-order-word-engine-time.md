## Evidence

### McLean 2013

McLean, S., Hogg, N., & Rush, T. W. (2013). Vocabulary learning through an online computerized flashcard site. The JALT CALL Journal, 9(1), 79-98. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1108008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA adjusted posttest means from the same 12-class quasi-experiment, reported in Table 5 with 95% confidence intervals. The article reports the means were "in the order of the time spent using the word card program"; no standardized effect size is printed for this ordering.

> "The means of the posttest VST score adjusted for initial differences were in the order of the time spent using the word card program (Table 5). The control group had the smallest adjusted mean (M = 2719.00), vocabulary and ER group had a larger adjusted mean (M = 3680.00), and the vocabulary group had the largest adjusted mean (M = 4092.00)."
