q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of two-year enrollment by institution for 2006–2015 CPS graduates shown in Figure 2. The report finds most immediate two-year enrollees "attended a City College" and growth was largely there.

"Most CPS graduates who made an immediate transition to a two-year college attended a City College ( see Figure 2 ). Moreover, the growth in two-year enrollment over the last decade has been largely at City Colleges."

"it appears that much of the growth in rates of immediate enrollment at City Colleges for CPS graduates occurred prior to City Colleges' recent reform efforts"
