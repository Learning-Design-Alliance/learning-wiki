## Evidence

### Ulupınar Fadime 2025

Ulupınar Fadime, Karasu Şeyda. (2025). Perceived vs. experienced care: a comparison between nursing students’ self-assessed competence and patients’ reported care. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12880-w

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of the 167 matched student–patient dyads. The entry reports the printed coefficients "with Assurance (r = 0.412, p ≤ 0.05)" and r = 0.411; the link to trust is the authors' suggested interpretation, not a tested effect.

> "The Ethics and Professional Responsibility subscale showed moderate correlations with Assurance (r = 0.412, p ≤ 0.05) and Interpersonal Relationships (r = 0.411, p ≤ 0.05), suggesting a potential link between ethical conduct and patients’ trust or engagement."
