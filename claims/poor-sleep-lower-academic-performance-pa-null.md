## Evidence

### Sanyaolu Olutosin 2026

Sanyaolu Olutosin, Reeves-Doyle Brandy, Olaniyan Afolakemi C., Max Tarenina, Asala Adetoun, Osime Esther. (2026). Relationships Between Physical Activity, Sleep, Psychological Well-Being, and Academic Performance Among Native American College Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph23040491

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression (proportional odds model) on the ACHA-NCHA secondary analysis predicting self-reported cumulative grade averages; the final model was significant (χ2(6) = 113.62, p < 0.001, Nagelkerke R2 = 0.068).

> "Students who reported poor sleep had lower odds of reporting higher academic performance compared to those with good sleep (B = −0.33, SE = 0.09, Wald = 12.80, p < 0.001, 95% CI: −0.50–−0.15)."

### Sanyaolu Olutosin 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same ordinal logistic regression model: PA (PAGUIDE binary variable) showed no statistically significant association with academic performance; equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast, PA was not a significant predictor (B = −0.09, SE = 0.09, Wald = 0.87, p = 0.350, 95% CI: −0.27–0.10)."
