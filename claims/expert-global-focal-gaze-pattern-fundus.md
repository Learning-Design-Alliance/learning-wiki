Qualitative gaze-pattern analysis of a representative ocular toxoplasmosis image with composite heat maps: junior residents showed "initial sporadicity without consistent fixation on the area of interest"; attendings showed initial concentration on inflammatory regions followed by systematic peripheral analysis.

> "The junior PGY2s' and PGY3s' gaze patterns have an initial sporadicity without consistent fixation on the area of interest until after repetitive scanning throughout the image."
