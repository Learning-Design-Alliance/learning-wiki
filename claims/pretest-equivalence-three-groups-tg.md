# The three groups were statistically equivalent in transformational geometry achievement before instruction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pre-test ANOVAs found no significant differences among groups on recognition, features, construction, or the total test (all p > .05). [→ Gül Kaleli Yılmaz 2015](#gul-kaleli-ylmaz-2015)

## Evidence

### Gül Kaleli Yılmaz 2015

Gül Kaleli Yılmaz. (2015). The Effect of Dynamic Geometry Software and Physical Manipulatives on Candidate Teachers’ Transformational Geometry Success. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.2610

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test ANOVA across the Computer (n=43), Manipulatives (n=36), and Traditional (n=38) Groups on all TGAT sections; no statistically significant differences, establishing baseline equivalence. No effect sizes printed.

> "the ANOV A results showed no significant difference in the mean scores among the groups for the recognition section of the test [ F(2,114) = .16,  p  > .05], the features section of the test [F(2,114) = .46, p > .05], the construction section of the test [ F(2,114) =.61, p >  .05] or the overall test [F(2,114) = .08, p > .05]"
