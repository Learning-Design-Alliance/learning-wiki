`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on 178 respondents; the article reports "AOR = 6.12, 95% CI: [2.45, 15.28], p < 0.001" for formal in-service training, controlling for profession, experience, and qualification.

> "Multivariable regression revealed formal in‐service training as the sole independent predictor of good knowledge (AOR = 6.12, 95% CI: [2.45, 15.28], p < 0.001)."
