## Evidence

### Sun Jui-Chiung 2026

Sun Jui-Chiung, Chang Min-Yu, Tsai Yi-Chun. (2026). Changes in Nursing Students' Clinical Handover Performance Following ISBAR-Structured Handover Simulation Teaching: A Mixed-Methods Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70955

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A single-group pretest-posttest study of 57 nursing students in an obstetric simulation course. Paired-samples t-test showed a significant increase in Communication Clarity Scale total score, with a large within-subject effect size (Cohen's dz = 1.04). There was no control group: the authors state that the change "cannot be attributed exclusively to the intervention" and that maturation and Hawthorne effects cannot be excluded, so this is a pre-post gain, not a between-group effect of ISBAR teaching.

> "Following the ISBAR-structured handover simulation intervention, students' overall post-test scores on the Communication Clarity Scale (mean = 63.47, SD = 6.32) significantly exceeded pre-test scores (mean = 51.24, SD = 11.43) (t = 7.87, p < 0.001; mean difference 12.23, 95% CI [9.12, 15.34], Cohen's dz = 1.04)."
