## Evidence

### Andrea Roxana Bellot 2025

Andrea Roxana Bellot, Mar Gutiérrez-Colón Plana, Katarzyna Agnieszka Baran. (2025). Redefining Literature Education: The Role of ChatGPT in Undergraduate Courses. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00497-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey comparison of the experimental group (ChatGPT texts) against the control group (student texts) across Questions 3–10 using Likert-scale means analyzed in JASP. Setting means 4.381 vs 3.773; "ChatGPT excelled in maintaining a consistent and appropriate tone (mean of 4), which was significantly higher than the control group's mean (3.273)". Only descriptive means are reported.

> "The setting was significantly better appreciated in the texts generated by ChatGPT (mean of 4.381) as opposed to those written by students (mean of 3.773), showcasing a stronger ability of ChatGPT to vividly convey place and time."
