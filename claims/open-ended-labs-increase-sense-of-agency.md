## Evidence

### Z. Yasemin Kalender 2020

Z. Yasemin Kalender, Martin Stein, and N. G. Holmes. (2020). Sense of agency, gender, and students' perception in open-ended physics labs. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.kalender](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.kalender)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post t-test on Sense of Agency survey scores in first-semester calculus-based honors physics lab courses at a large research university; 88 pre-test and 66 post-test respondents. The article reports the increase was significant, "p < 0.001", with effect size 0.76, considered between medium and large.

> "students' average SoA scores increased from M = 2.96, SD = 0.98 at pre-test to post-test M = 3.73, SD = 0.84 with t(56) = 5.29, p < 0.001"
