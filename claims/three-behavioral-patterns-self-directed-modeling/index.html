Evidence¶

Sungeun An 2025¶

Sungeun An, Jennifer Hammock, Ashok Goel. (2025). How Online Learners Engage in Self-Directed Modeling: A Behavioral Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00506-5

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Behavioral clustering of activity sequences from 822 models created by 315 learners in VERA, grouped by sequence length and clustered via Levenshtein distance and agglomerative hierarchical clustering. The article reports "Type 1 (N = 382): Observation", Type 2 Construction (N=338, mean 16 actions), and Type 3 Exploration (N=69, mean 154.73 actions).

"Type 1 (N = 382): Observation. This pattern is characterized by frequent sequences of parameterization followed by simulation"

Related Claims¶

  • Markov chain analysis yields nine finer-grained behavioral clusters that map predominantly onto the three broader behavior types — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Observation behavior is predominantly associated with copied rather than original models, while Construction and Exploration draw more on original models — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Markov transition analysis shows the three clusters differ in workflow dynamics: iterative optimization loops, rapid prototyping flows, and browsing-anchored exploration — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Exploration behavior is associated with the most complex and most varied conceptual models, with statistically significant differences across behavior types — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
