# Total research competence scores rose from pretest to posttest among graduate nursing students in a single-group quasi-experiment after an FC-TBL-based literature seminar course

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Total research competence scores increased significantly from pretest to posttest (p < 0.001) in a single-group quasi-experiment with 124 master's nursing students. [→ Qi Hong 2026](#qi-hong-2026)

## Evidence

### Qi Hong 2026

Qi Hong, Ban Jingjing, Wang Nana, Guo Huimin, Yang Yang, Tang Shan. (2026). Development and evaluation of a literature seminar course based on flipped classroom and team-based learning in the graduate nursing curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08823-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental single-group pretest-posttest study of 124 master's nursing students. Paired-samples t-tests showed the total research competence score rose from 1.78 ± 0.42 to 2.47 ± 0.56 (t = -11.94, p < 0.001), a "significant" increase with no effect size reported.

> "Following the intervention, both the total score and the scores for all dimensions of research competence increased significantly compared to those before the intervention (p < 0.001)."
