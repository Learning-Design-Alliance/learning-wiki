Review reports the Cowan, Donnell, and Saults incidental word-list study with a surprise same-list pair test; the small difference (about 59% vs. about 53%) is described as unintentional learning of associations between items together in the focus of attention just once.

> "performance was better for words from short lists of 3 items (about 59%) than for words from lists of 6 or 9 items (about 53%)."
