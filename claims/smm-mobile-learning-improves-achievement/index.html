Evidence¶

Chang 2018¶

Chang, Chiu, and Huang. (2018). A Sharing Mind Map-oriented Approach to Enhance Collaborative Mobile Learning With Digital Archiving Systems. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 19(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1174044

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with 31 experimental and 30 control sixth graders in two classes; independent sample t-test on post-test scores showed a significant difference (t=3.32, p=.002 <.01), with the experimental group "learning performance of the experimental group was better".

"The statistical analysis found that the average post-test score of the experimental group was 83.26 points, while that of the control group was 75.33 points."
