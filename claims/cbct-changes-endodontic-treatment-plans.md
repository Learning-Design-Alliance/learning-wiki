## Evidence

### Fonseca 2025

Fonseca, Braz, Vieira, de Souza Ferreira, Damian. (2025). Do CBCT and Clinical Experience Impact Decision‐Making in Endodontic Diagnosis and Treatment Planning? A Before‐After Study. Australian Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.12966

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Same before-after design with five students and 28 cases; treatment plan options ranged from no treatment to extraction. The article reports "36.4% of treatment plans" altered, significant (p = 0.000); Table 1 gives OR = 13.33.

> "Among undergraduate students, 32.9% of diagnostic decisions and 36.4% of treatment plans were altered during the second assessment compared to the first. In both outcomes, the proportions of change were statistically significant (p = 0.000)."
