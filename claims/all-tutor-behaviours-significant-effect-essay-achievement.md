# Social congruence, cognitive congruence and subject-matter expertise each have a significant effect on student achievement in PBL

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All three tutor-related behaviours significantly affected essay-test achievement at the end of the PBL day. [→ Chng Esther 2011](#chng-esther-2011)

## Evidence

### Chng Esther 2011

Chng Esther, Yew Elaine H. J., Schmidt Henk G. (2011). Effects of tutor-related behaviours on the process of problem-based learning. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9282-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA (GPA covariate) of essay-test scores from the same 223 students, scored by idea units with inter-rater correlation r = 0.77. The article reports significant effects for all three tutor behaviours on achievement; only F statistics are printed, so no effect size is available.

> "Social congruence also had a significant effect on student achievement as measured by the essay, F(2, 219) = 4.914, p < 0.01. Similar effects were found for the subject-matter expertise, F(2, 219) = 7.74, p < 0.01, and cognitive congruence, F(2, 219) = 7.74, p < 0.01."
