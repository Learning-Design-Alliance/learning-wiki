Evidence¶

Yijun Ruan 2024¶

Yijun Ruan, Urs Maurer, Catherine McBride. (2024). Effectiveness of Reading Interventions on Literacy Skills for Chinese Children with and Without Dyslexia: a Meta-analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09915-9

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Three-level meta-analysis of 49 standardized mean-change effect sizes from 28 randomized controlled trials (37 word reading, 12 spelling) following PRISMA guidelines, conducted in R with the metafor package. The pooled estimate was g = 0.68, described as a "significant" impact on literacy achievement.

"Overall, the existing reading intervention significantly impacted Chinese children's literacy achievement (g = 0.68)."
