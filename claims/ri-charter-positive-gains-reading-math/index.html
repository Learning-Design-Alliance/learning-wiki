Charter School Performance in Rhode Island 2023¶

Charter School Performance in Rhode Island. (2023). https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Charter+School+Performance+in+Rhode+Island

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental matched-comparison analysis using Virtual Control Records matching charter and feeder TPS students on demographics and baseline achievement; match rates were 70% in reading and 68% in math. Impact tables print "All Students1 0.16 94" in reading and "0.15 89" in math, i.e. an effect size of 0.16 SD in reading and 0.15 SD in math.

"In the aggregate, we find positive and significant gains associated with charter attendance in both reading and math."
