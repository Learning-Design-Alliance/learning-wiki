Baxmann Martin 2025¶

The review reports that "virtual learning environments and distance education modules have been reported to enhance knowledge retention and flexibility". Studies have shown that students engaging in digital learning platforms exhibit improved retention of orthodontic principles, particularly when multimedia elements are incorporated.
