### Dargue 2020

Dargue, N; Sweller, N. (2020). Learning Stories Through Gesture: Gesture's Effects on Child and Adult Narrative Comprehension. Educational Psychology Review, 32 (1). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09505-0
