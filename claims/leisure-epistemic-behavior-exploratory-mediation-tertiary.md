# Exploratory: leisure epistemic behavior mediates the relation of cognitive ability with tertiary academic performance, with a very small effect

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In exploratory indicator-specific models, the indirect effect of cognitive ability on tertiary GPA was mediated by leisure epistemic behavior (β = 0.02, p < 0.001), a very small post hoc effect. [→ Mussel 2022](#mussel-2022)

## Evidence

### Mussel 2022

Mussel, Patrick. (2022). Processes Underlying the Relation between Cognitive Ability and Curiosity with Academic Performance: A Mediation Analysis for Epistemic Behavior in a Five-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Intelligence 10: 23. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence10020023

`q2 · i?`

Exploratory post hoc mediation analyses repeated separately for each of the four epistemic behavior indicators, with Bonferroni-adjusted p = 0.0125, reported in the Results section. The article stresses the effect size was very small, occurred without a direct effect, and requires replication.

> "it turned out that the indirect effect of predicting tertiary academic performance according to levels in cognitive ability was mediated by leisure epistemic behavior (β = 0.02; p < 0.001)."
