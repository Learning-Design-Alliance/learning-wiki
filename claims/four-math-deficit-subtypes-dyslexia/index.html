Evidence¶

B. Pedemonte 2024¶

B. Pedemonte, C. W. Pereira, V. Borghesani, M. Ebbert, I. E. Allen, P. Pinheiro-Chagas, J. De Leon, Z. Miller, B. L. Tee & M. L. Gorno-Tempini. (2024). Profiles of mathematical deficits in children with dyslexia. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00217-x

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Behavioral-neurology classification of 75 children with dyslexia by a professional clinician team, followed by MCB evaluation of concordance. The article states "there is heterogeneity in the nature of these de ﬁcits" across the four domains assessed by the battery.

"Our study reveals that within a cohort of children with dyslexia, 66% exhibit mathematical deﬁcits, and among those with mathematical deﬁcits, there is heterogeneity in the nature of these de ﬁcits."

Related Claims¶

  • Fact retrieval deficits in LA-Severe children occur alongside intact addition procedural competencies and modest, improving number-task performance — related
  • Adults with DD rely on time-consuming back-up strategies like finger usage for single-digit arithmetic, with prolonged response latencies that appear number-specific — related
  • Counting-string intrusion errors are as common in LA-Severe Fact Retrieval children as in children with MLD, but decline across grades only for the MLD group — related
  • LA-Severe Fact Retrieval children commit retrieval errors on forced retrieval at rates equal to children with MLD, with no across-grade improvement — related

  • Adults with dyslexia without dyscalculia show no deficit in nonsymbolic numerical magnitude processing — related
