The authors describe the design intent of the MetaLight application in the Results and discussion section. The interface design targets three specific learning goals: "the relationship between effect sizes and their appearance on the forest plot", model selection impact, and funnel plot construction.

> "Its interface has been designed to develop understanding of: the relationship between effect sizes and their appearance on the forest plot; the impact on results of selecting a fixed or random effects model; and how funnel plots are constructed."
