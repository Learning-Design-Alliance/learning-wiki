# Under 2D visualization, novices perform better with straight than with complex curved instruments in PEG Transfer and Intracorporeal Suture

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In 2D laparoscopy, novices using straight instruments scored significantly higher than those using curved instruments in PEG Transfer (T2, T3) and Intracorporeal Suture (including T5). [→ Hidding Johanna 2024](#hidding-johanna-2024)

## Evidence

### Hidding Johanna 2024

Hidding Johanna, Bucher Julian, Heiliger Christian, Andrade Dorian, Trupka Lukas, Halmen Matthias, Werner Jens, Karcz Konrad, Frank Alexander. (2024). Laparoscopy training of novices with complex curved instruments using 2D- and 3D-visualization. Langenbeck's Archives of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-024-03297-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 48 laparoscopy-naive medical students stratified into four groups; comparison of straight vs. curved instruments under 2D visualization on FLS tasks. "The subjects in the group with straight instruments performed better" at T2 and T3 in PEG Transfer, and the Intracorporeal Suture showed better straight-instrument performance at T5 (305 ± 100.5 vs. 147.3 ± 191.5, p = 0.012).

> "The subjects in the group with straight instruments performed better (224.1 ± 8.6 vs. 190.1 ± 54.1, p = 0.024 and 233.4 ± 21.1 vs. 208.3 ± 28.6, p = 0.028)."
