## Evidence

### Gallego-Romero 2020

Gallego-Romero, J.M., Alario-Hoyos, C., Estévez-Ayres, I. et al. (2020). Analyzing learners' engagement and behavior in MOOCs on programming with the Codeboard IDE. Education Tech Research Dev 68, 2505–2528 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09773-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

t-test over 1,036 work sessions (820 anonymous, 216 registered) from Codeboard activity logs in three Java MOOCs, with normality and Levene's variance checks. The article reports means of 6.35 vs 4.57 actions per session, t=-3.78, p=0.0001 (Table 3).

> "Interestingly, registered learners performed 6.35 actions per session on average, while anonymous learners performed only 4.57 actions, with a significant difference."

### Gallego-Romero 2020 (2)

Gallego-Romero, J.M., Alario-Hoyos, C., Estévez-Ayres, I. et al. (2020). Analyzing learners' engagement and behavior in MOOCs on programming with the Codeboard IDE. Education Tech Research Dev 68, 2505–2528 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09773-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same session-level t-test analysis of Codeboard logs. The article reports mean session time of 250.88 seconds for registered versus 148.58 seconds for anonymous learners, t=-3.95, p<0.0001 (Table 3).

> "The data collected also shows that registered learners spent more time per session than anonymous learners: 250.88 seconds for registered learners versus 148.58 seconds for anonymous learners, also with a significant difference."
