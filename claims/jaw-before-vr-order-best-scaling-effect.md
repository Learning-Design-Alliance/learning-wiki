## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Supragingival scaling effect was significantly higher in combined groups than in single-method groups, and J-V exceeded V-J (P = 0.035), with J-V showing the highest efficacy. [→ Zhang Jie 2021](#zhang-jie-2021)

## Evidence

### Zhang Jie 2021

Zhang Jie, Xing Jiawei, Zheng Min, Sheng Jie, Zhang Kailiang, Zhang Baoping. (2021). Effectiveness of virtual simulation and jaw model for undergraduate periodontal teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-03064-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Plaque-index outcome in the randomized experiment using the Turesky modification of the Quigley–Hein index at teeth #21 and #46 before and after scaling. Combined groups scored higher, and "Group J-V indicated the highest efficacy" (V-J vs J-V, P = 0.035).

> "The supragingival scaling effect was significantly higher in Group V-J and Group J-V (V-J: 53.00 ± 3.05; J-V: 63.40 ± 4.39) compared with the supragingival scaling effect in Group J and Group V (V: 41.90 ± 5.23; J: 47.40 ± 4.31; P < 0.01)"
