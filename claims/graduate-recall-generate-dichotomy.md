## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2015

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2015). Student difficulties with quantum states while translating state vectors in Dirac notation to wave functions in position and momentum representations. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.048](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.048)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 45 first-year graduate students in a core quantum mechanics course compared with 46 undergraduates (Table III). On the same survey, 42% correctly recalled ⟨p|p′⟩ = δ(p−p′) but could not generate a momentum eigenstate in momentum representation.

> "Table III shows that when  45 graduate students were asked to write , e.g., ⟨𝑝|𝑝′⟩ or ⟨𝑥|𝑝′⟩ without Dirac notation, 91% correctly wrote , e.g., that  ⟨𝑝|𝑝′⟩ = 𝛿(𝑝 − 𝑝′) which is significantly higher than 35%, the corresponding average undergraduate percentage."
