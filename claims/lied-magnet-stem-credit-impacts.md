## Evidence

### Nakamoto 2022

Nakamoto, J., Grant, A., Snipes, J., Allender, S., & Figueroa, T. (2022). Clark County School District Magnet Schools Assistance Program (MSAP): Spring 2022 interim evaluation summary report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621271

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

STEM credit outcomes from the Lied QED, comparing matched treatment and comparison students in grades 6-8 on 2019-20 credits attempted and earned. Exhibit 11 prints effect sizes of 1.32, 3.13, and 2.70 for credits attempted, all significant at p < .001 after correction.

> "the treatment students in Grade 6 at Lied attempted and completed just over one third more STEM credits than the comparison students, and the treatment students in Grades 7 and 8 attempted and completed approximately one more STEM credit than the comparison students, all of which were statistically significant differences."
