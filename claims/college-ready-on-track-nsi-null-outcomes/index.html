Quasi-experimental comparison of college-ready on-track NSI students to similar students in comparable non-participating schools across six outcome measures. No statistically significant impact was detected on any prioritized outcome through the fourth year of participation.

"The college-ready on-track NSI did not impact the student outcomes prioritized by the foundation: GPA, advanced course taking, math and ELA test scores, high school graduation, or college enrollment."
