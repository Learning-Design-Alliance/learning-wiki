### Cristian Raduta 2005

Cristian Raduta, Gordon Aubrecht. (2005). A comparison between the styles Romanian and American students use to approach an E&M physics problem. Originally published 2002. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503123](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503123)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Written survey trajectory results (Table 2.5 shows 10 Romanian students correct, 42 incorrect); the hardest part of the problem for both populations.

> "None of the American students gave a correct answer for the trajectory (a spiral) when the charged particle is coming under an angle ≠ 90 degrees in a magnetic field; only 10 Romanian students (19.2%) identified correctly the trajectory as being a spiral."
