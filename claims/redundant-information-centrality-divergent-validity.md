### Cristian Candia 2022 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robustness analysis using Burt's constraint centrality in the same regression framework; the article reports no significant effect on final GPA; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "We provide more evidence on this point by replicating these results using Burt’s network constraint, which quantiﬁes access to redundant information (Table 2). We ﬁnd no signiﬁcant eﬀect of Burt’s constraint on ﬁnal GPA (Fig. S14)."
