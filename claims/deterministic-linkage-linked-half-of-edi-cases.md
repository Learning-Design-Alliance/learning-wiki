Reported linkage outcome from the abstract and Results of this data-creation study. The linking process began with EDI and Grade 3 EQAO datasets using school board ID, gender, and birth date; "The proportion of EDI participants linked to the four EQAO datapoints ranged from 42–50%, depending on the cohort year."

> "We successfully linked 50% of the EDI's 374,239 cases, 86,778 of which contained all five datapoints, creating a database tracking achievement for multiple cohorts from kindergarten through grade 10"
