Narrative background attribution: the article reports, citing Kaliyadan et al. (2012), a comparison of tutor-less with tutor-led PBL finding no significant difference in effects on student learning; the reviewed study's design is not described here. Relatedly, the article cites Woods (1996) on improved grades and Pastirik (2006) on perceived learning in tutor-less groups.

> "Kaliyadan et al. (2012) compared tutor-less PBL with tutor-led PBL and found no significant difference in effects on student learning, which indicates that tutor-less PBL could be implemented in instances in which funding and time constraints had previously prohibited use of small PBL groups requiring use of additional tutors."
