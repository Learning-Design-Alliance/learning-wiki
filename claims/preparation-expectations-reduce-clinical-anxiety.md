## Evidence

### Nabeela C 2025

Nabeela C, Shykhon M, Milward M, Yonel Z. (2025). The use of 360-videos to bridge the gap between pre-clinical and clinical dental teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06946-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group theme on preparation: students valued advance resources for planning sessions and reported that not knowing session content caused anxiety. Handbooks were found overwhelming due to information overload, with preference for "bite-sized", week-by-week delivery via email or WhatsApp.

> "Students felt that preparation prior to clinical sessions was important, alongside an idea of clear expectations to reduce feelings of anxiety."
