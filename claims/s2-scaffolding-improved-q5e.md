## Evidence

### Hu 2022

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2022). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with measurement uncertainty of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac9ba3)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

CQS 3.4-3.5 were added for the in-person implementation after online students demonstrated difficulties with outcomes of measurement of S². The authors state the additional scaffolding "appears to have been effective," though differences could be attributable to more than one factor; only descriptive percentages are printed, no effect size for this contrast.

> "Across the two years, students’ performance rose from 48% percent in the online implementation to 80% on the in-person implementation (see Tables II-III)."
