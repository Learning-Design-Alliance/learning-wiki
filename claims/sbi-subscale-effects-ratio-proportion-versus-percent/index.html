Evidence¶

Jitendra 2017¶

Jitendra, A. K., Harwell, M. R., Dupuis, D. N., & Karl, S. R. (2017). A randomized trial of the effects of schema-based instruction on proportional problem solving for students with mathematics problem-solving difficulties. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219416629646

q3 · i?

Subscale analyses of the PPS measure (ratio/proportion and percent subscores analyzed separately via HLM) in the randomized MD-PS sample. The ratio/proportion effect sustained to delayed posttest while the percent effect did not; the delayed percent treatment effect was not significant. No standardized effect size (d or g) was printed for these subscale findings.

"Although the SBI group outperformed the control group on the ratio and proportion subscale at both posttest and delayed posttest (.23 SDs and .30 SDs higher than the control group), we found treatment effects only at immediate posttest (.32 SDs higher than the control group) for the percent subscale."
