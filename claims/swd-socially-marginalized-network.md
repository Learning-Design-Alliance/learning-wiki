### Chen et al. 2015

Chin-Chih Chen, Jill V. Hamm, & Thomas W. Farmer. (2015). Exceptionality and Peer Victimization Involvement in Late Childhood: Subtypes, Stability, and Social Marginalization. Remedial and Special Education, 36(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED588091

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive distribution of social-cognitive-mapping centrality categories by exceptionality status in fall of sixth grade. "Students with disabilities were predominantly socially marginalized"; fewer than 7% were nuclear, while well over a third of academically gifted students were nuclear and very few were marginalized.

> "Students with disabilities were predominantly socially marginalized (i.e., socially isolated or peripheral) in the social network. Among students with a disability, fewer than 7% were classified as nuclear (i.e., highly prominent or well-integrated) in the sixth-grade social network system."
