Candace Walkington and Katie Bainbridge 2025¶

Candace Walkington and Katie Bainbridge. (2025). Mathematics Teachers' Use of Generative AI to Create Active Learning Experiences. https://socialinnovationsjournal.com/index.php/sij/article/view/9994

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative reflexive thematic analysis of written responses and ChatGPT logs from 11 teachers in an Algebraic Reasoning course who asked ChatGPT to modify a classroom task; positive themes are summarized in Table 1, including that activities were "often hands-on and collaborative".

"Teachers appreciated that the activities proposed by ChatGPT were often hands-on and collaborative: “It is a good way to learn with something touchable, and has a time to work with peers.”"
