The review reports, citing Singh et al., that across the majority of high-quality randomized control trials a "positive beneficial effect of physical activity interventions on mathematics outcomes" was observed in the pediatric population; it also cites an earlier meta-analysis by Alvarez-Bueno et al. finding improved academic achievement. No effect sizes are printed here.
