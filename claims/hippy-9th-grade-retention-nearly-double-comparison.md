### Brown 2014

Brown, A., & Lee, J. (2014). School Performance in Elementary, Middle, and High School: A Comparison of Children Based on HIPPY Participation During the Preschool Years. School Community Journal, 24(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048627

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analysis of 9th-grade retention data showed 23.5% of HIPPY children had one-year retentions compared to 46.6% of comparison children. The authors note this finding implies critical long-term impacts of HIPPY participation.

> "However, the grade retention rate of the comparison group was nearly double that of the HIPPY group (HIPPY 23.5% vs. comparison 46.6%) in the 9th grade."
