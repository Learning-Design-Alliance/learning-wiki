### Marinova–Takorova M 2026

Marinova–Takorova M, Mihaylova Z. (2026). Bridging the gap: student and staff views on teaching, learning, and assessment in dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09772-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study at the Faculty of Dental Medicine, Medical University – Sofia, with 397 Bulgarian students, 122 international students, and 57 academic staff; Table 3 reports 193 (48.6%) Bulgarian students and 41 (33.6%) international students attending all lectures, versus 175 (44.1%) and 68 (55.7%) attending only dental-related lectures. Overall comparison between respondent groups: χ² = 12.3, p < 0.001.

> "Approximately half of the students reported attending all lectures, while attendance related primarily to dental disciplines was more common among international students."
