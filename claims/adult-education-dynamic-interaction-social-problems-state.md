# Adult education development in the Philippines resulted from dynamic interaction among social problems, popular demands, state response and external constraints

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The historical development of adult education in the Philippines has been shaped by a dynamic interaction among social problems (non-literacy, language, inequitable land distribution), popular demands (people's movements), state responses (initially repression, then accommodation through legislation), adult education provision and restructuring, and external constraints (World Bank structural adjustment policies).
