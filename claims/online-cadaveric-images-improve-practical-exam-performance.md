## Evidence

### Gimeno A. 2026

Gimeno A., Alberola‐Zorrilla P., Blasco‐Serra A., Sánchez‐Zuriaga D., Zaragozá R. (2026). Online anatomical images are an effective resource to improve knowledge acquisition and practical exam performance in gross anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70254

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of two intervention groups (n=180) with image access versus two control groups (n=182) in a first-year medical visceral anatomy course. Mann–Whitney test showed "a significantly higher performance was observed in the intervention groups" for head, neck, and thorax blocks (p < 0.0001).

> "The practical exam scores of the intervention groups were compared with those of the control groups, and a significantly higher performance was observed in the intervention groups (Figure 4A) for the blocks of head, neck, and thorax."
