## Subclaims
`q2 i?` No statistically significant differences between conditions in baseline scores (t(475) = -0.17, p = 0.87), gender distribution (χ² = 0.93, p = 0.33), or mean age (t(475) = 1.23, p = 0.22). [→ Owen Henkel 2024](#owen-henkel-2024)

## Evidence

### Owen Henkel 2024

Owen Henkel, Hannah Horne-Robinson, Nessie Kozhakhmetova, Amanda Lee. (2024). Effective and Scalable Math Support: Experimental Evidence on the Impact of an AI- Math Tutor in Ghana. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-64315-6_34](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64315-6_34)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline equivalence analyses comparing the 241 control and 236 treatment completers on baseline scores, gender, and age. The article reports the t-test above plus a non-significant gender chi-square (χ² = 0.93, p = 0.33) and age t-test (t(475) = 1.23, p = 0.22); no effect sizes are printed.

> "No statistically significant difference was found in the baseline scores of the two groups (t(475) = -0.17, p = 0.87), suggesting that the two groups had equivalent levels of math knowledge at the start of the evaluation."
