## Evidence

### Ibarra Santacruz 2018

Ibarra Santacruz, D., & Martínez Ortega, D. (2018). Can working memory strategies enhance English vocabulary learning? HOW, 25(2), 29-47. https://doi.org/10.19183/how.25.2.410

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test administered to both intact groups two weeks into the course, scored with the same four performance criteria and analyzed by independent-samples t-test. The article reports the difference "was not statistically significant", indicating similar initial conditions; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "the difference in terms of performance was not statistically significant between the control (M = 1.955, SD =1.253) and experimental (M = 2.143, SD = 1.145) groups, t(48) = -0.5538, p = 0.58, implying that the initial conditions were fairy similar as measured with the pre-test."
