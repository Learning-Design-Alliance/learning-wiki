## Evidence

### Ratekin Carly Schade 2025

Ratekin Carly Schade, Auko Erick, Wafula Meshack Malenya, Omwanda Wycliffe Okoth, Wamae Jane, Mudhune Sandra A, Rogers Ash, Mbeya Julius, Ouma Frederick Ochieng, Njoki Lillian, Young William, Starnes Joseph R. (2025). Evaluation of the Helping Babies Breathe programme in Migori County, Kenya. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2025-003887

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Adjusted logistic regression of 553 recorded births (47 in-facility deaths, 57 transferred neonates with recorded outcomes included) comparing survival with versus without an HBB trained provider; the article prints OR 1.13 with "95% CI (0.66 to 1.93)" and p=0.64. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Having an HBB trained provider attend the delivery was associated with a 13% higher odds of neonates living following delivery, but this change did not reach a significance level of 0.05 (95% CI (0.66 to 1.93))"
