Hahnel 2020¶

Hahnel, C., Hough, H., & Willis, J. (2020). Securing and Protecting Education Funding in California. Policy Analysis for California Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED609211

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview finding within Finding 2 on balancing equity and local revenue-raising. The report states "the parcel tax...is the only real option available to school districts that want to raise additional revenues" and that many interviewees called it inequitable and regressive.

"many interviewees were concerned that parcel taxes are inequitable—since high-wealth communities are more likely to pass them—and regressive—since a flat tax is generally levied for each property, regardless of property value."
