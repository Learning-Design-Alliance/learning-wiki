# Collaborative problem-solving improves attitudinal tendency (ES = 1.17) more than cognitive skills (ES = 0.70)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2`–`r3` · `q3` · `i2`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Collaborative problem-solving significantly enhances students' attitudinal tendency toward critical thinking (ES = 1.17), with significant intergroup differences between the two dimensions (chi2 = 7.95, P < 0.01). [→ Xu 2023](#xu-2023)
`q3 i2` Collaborative problem-solving improves cognitive skills less strongly (ES = 0.70), described as only an upper-middle impact. [→ Xu 2023 (2)](#xu-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Xu 2023

Xu, E., Wang, W., & Wang, Q. (2023). The effectiveness of collaborative problem solving in promoting students' critical thinking: A meta-analysis based on empirical literature. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-023-01508-1

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis of 27 effect quantities for the attitudinal tendency dimension (Table 3). The pooled effect was "significant comprehensive effect (ES = 1.17, z = 7.62, P < 0.01, 95% CI [0.87, 1.47])" with significant intergroup difference between dimensions (chi2 = 7.95, P < 0.01).

> "the improvements in students' attitudinal tendency are much more pronounced and have a significant comprehensive effect (ES = 1.17, z = 7.62, P < 0.01, 95% CI [0.87, 1.47])"

### Xu 2023 (2)

Xu, E., Wang, W., & Wang, Q. (2023). The effectiveness of collaborative problem solving in promoting students' critical thinking: A meta-analysis based on empirical literature. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-023-01508-1

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of 52 effect quantities for the cognitive skill dimension (Table 3). The pooled effect was "(ES = 0.70, z = 11.55, P < 0.01, 95% CI [0.58, 0.82])", smaller than the attitudinal-tendency effect.

> "gains in learners' cognitive skill are slightly improved and are just above average. (ES = 0.70, z = 11.55, P < 0.01, 95% CI [0.58, 0.82])."
