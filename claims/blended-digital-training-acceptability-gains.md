### Tembo Tapiwa A 2026

Tembo Tapiwa A, Rosenberg Nora E, Mollan Katie, Kim Maria, Rutstein Sarah E, Mkandawire Angella M, Chitani Mike J, Kumbuyo Caroline, Phiri Duncan, Mphande Mtisunge, Kavuta Elijah, Chilala Samuel, Wang Jiayu, Ahmed Saeed, Simon Katherine, Bekker Linda-Gail. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretraining and posttraining Likert-scale surveys adapted from Chao, analyzed with generalized estimating equations in the cluster randomized controlled trial. Acceptability increased from 67.0% to 68.9% in the enhanced arm while declining from 67.9% to 65.2% in the standard arm; the between-arm difference was "4.5% (95% CI 0.3%, 7.0%; P=.03)".

> "On average, digital technology acceptability scores increased by 1.9%, from 67.0% pretraining to 68.9% posttraining in the enhanced arm, while in the standard arm, there was a 2.6% reduction in acceptability scores, from 67.9% pretraining to 65.2% posttraining. The between-arm difference in pre-to-post differences comparing the enhanced vs standard arms was 4.5% (95% CI 0.3%, 7.0%; P=.03)."
