He Hongxue, Yang Yahui, Sun Jiaojiao, Wang Fang, Zhang Wei and Zhu Fengshu. (2025). Effects of school-based physical activity on academic achievement in children and adolescents: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1651883

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis pooling six RCTs on reading comprehension. The review reports "no significant correlation" with SMD = 0.07 (95% CI: −0.03 to 0.18, p = 0.170) and high heterogeneity (I² = 74%).

> "Figure 5 shows the combined effect size of six studies (27–30, 37, 40), indicating no significant correlation between physical activity and reading comprehension achievement (SMD = 0.07, 95% CI: −0.03 to 0.18, p = 0.170, I2 = 74%)."
