## Evidence

### Haas D 2026

Haas D, Fredette J, Murphy KA, Deitchman A, Anderson J, Blodgett M. (2026). Novel Simulation-based Awake Fiberoptic Intubation Curriculum: Pilot Study. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.50778

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-center pilot study of 46 emergency physicians completing a simulation curriculum with rapid-cycle deliberate practice; GRS scores were compared between pre-test and three-month post-test, showing a significant increase described as "improved from a mean of 22.5 (4.5) to 28.8 (6.3)" with P < .001. No effect size was printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "The primary outcome—observed performance using the GRS—improved from a mean of 22.5 (4.5) to 28.8 (6.3), P < .001."
