## Evidence

### Guill Karin 2019

Guill Karin, Lüdtke Oliver, Köller Olaf. (2019). Assessing the instructional quality of private tutoring and its effects on student outcomes: Analyses from the German National Educational Panel Study. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12281

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Regressions of the latent quality factors on tutor qualification category (mathematics N = 1,719, German N = 148, English N = 491). Effects were scattered across dimensions and subjects rather than uniform, so the article reports "only partial evidence for an effect of tutors' formal qualifications".

> "There was only partial evidence for an effect of tutors' formal qualifications (see Table 3; Hypothesis 2b). Regarding mathematics tutoring, students stated that they found tutoring more challenging if the tutor was a teacher or had an unknown qualification rather than being a university student."
