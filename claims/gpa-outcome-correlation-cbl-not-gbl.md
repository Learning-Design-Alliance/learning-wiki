## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In the CBL group, GPA correlated positively with Test 3 (r = 0.372, p = 0.047) and with Test 1–Test 3 progress (r = 0.474, p = 0.009); in the GBL group no significant GPA–outcome correlations were found. [→ Amini Mahtab 2026](#amini-mahtab-2026)

## Evidence

### Amini Mahtab 2026

Amini Mahtab, Amini Shahideh, Alizadeh Maryam. (2026). Flipped classroom showdown: game-based vs. case-based learning in pharmacy education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09754-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory Pearson correlation analysis within each randomized group; the CBL group showed the printed moderate positive correlations r = 0.372 and r = 0.474, while in the GBL group no significant relationship between test results, progress rates and cumulative GPA was detected.

> "there is a positive significant relationship between the cumulative GPA and Test 3 by Pearson correlation [27] = 0.372; p = 0.047 and a positive significant relationship between the cumulative GPA and the progress rate from Test 1 to Test 3 by Pearson correlation [27] = 0.474; p = 0.009."
