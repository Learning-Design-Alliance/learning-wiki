## Evidence

### Rachel Love 2025

Rachel Love, Edith Law, Philip R. Cohen, Dana Kulić. (2025). Teaching a Conversational Agent using Natural Language: Effect on Learning and Engagement. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00461-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

User study with 46 adult participants teaching two virtual agents, one per modality. The article reports that "teaching by paraphrasing in natural language is perceived as being more helpful for learning" while objective gain scores did not differ significantly between conditions.

> "The results indicate that teaching by paraphrasing in natural language is perceived as being more helpful for learning, but that this perception of improvement is not reﬂected in actual learning gain over a ﬁxed interaction."
