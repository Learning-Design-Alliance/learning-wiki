## Evidence

### Tom Zhang 2022

Tom Zhang, Michelle Taub, and Zhongzhou Chen. 2022. A Multi-level Trace Clustering Analysis Scheme for Measuring Students' Self-Regulated Learning Behavior in a Mastery-Based Online Learning Environment. In LAK22: 12th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK22). ACM, New York, NY, USA. [doi:10.1145/3506860.3506887](https://doi.org/10.1145/3506860.3506887)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of m-cluster 2 (AYAn), the second-largest cluster passing after study, found 74.3% of study events took place immediately after the first attempt; the authors interpret this as reflection on performance or planned learning per the instructor's recommendation.

> "The majority (74.3%) of study events in this cluster took place immediately after the first attempt, showing that students either actively reflected on their problem solving-performance, or planned their learning strategy according to the instructor's recommendation."
