Evidence¶

Patterson 1909¶

Patterson, T. L. (1909). Pedagogical Suggestions from Memory Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://books.google.com

q2 · i? · causal · r1

In the Oxford, Freeland and Woodland Street Schools, four digit tests were given with the same digits reordered or repeated across tests. The article reports improvement with each test and attributes the Woodland Street pattern to unconscious memory of the previous day's digits. Descriptive only.

"The results from all the schools point to the conclusion that the pupils improve with practice. The great uniformity and large increases with each test in the Woodland Street School seem to show that the pupils unconsciously remember the digits that have been dictated one day previous."

Patterson 1909 (2)¶

Patterson, T. L. (1909). Pedagogical Suggestions from Memory Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://books.google.com

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Morning-versus-afternoon correct-judgment totals in the Freeland and Woodland Street Schools. The pattern matched test order rather than time of day, and the article concluded "the pupils suffered no fatigue from their school work" as discoverable by such tests. Descriptive totals only.

"The total number of correct judgments for the morning tests in the Freeland Street School was 2,690 and for the afternoon tests 2,640; for the morning tests in the Woodland Street School 6,609, and for the afternoon tests, 7,179."
