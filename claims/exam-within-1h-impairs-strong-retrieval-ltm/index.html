Evidence¶

P. Lopes da Cunha 2018¶

P. Lopes da Cunha, D. Ramirez Butavand, L. B. Chisari, F. Ballarini and H. Viola (2018). Exams at classroom have bidirectional effects on the long-term memory of an unrelated graphical task. npj Science of Learning 3:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0036-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Strong-retrieval control groups (CTRs) had a Memory Index of about 0.6. Exams within 1 h of copying significantly decreased retention (e.g., +0 h: t = 3.98, p < 0.001; -1 h: t = 2.33, p < 0.05), while exams at -4 h or +4 h did not (p > 0.05).

"the presence of an exam either before ( −1o r −0.5 h) or after ( +0, +0.5, or +1 h) the copying of the ﬁgure, signiﬁcantly decreased the memory index of the EXM groups with respect to their corresponding CTRs groups"
