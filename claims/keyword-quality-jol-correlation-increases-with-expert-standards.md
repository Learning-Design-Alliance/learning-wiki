# The keyword-quality–JOL correlation increases after comparison with expert keywords, indicating learners exploit keyword quality as a cue only when given external standards

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Waldeyer 2021

Waldeyer, J., & Roelle, J. (2021). The keyword effect: A conceptual replication, effects on bias, and an optimization. Metacognition and Learning, 16, 37–56. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09235-7

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Planned contrast (weights 1 vs. −1) on intraindividual Goodman-Kruskal gamma correlations between rated keyword quality (scored against expert keywords by two raters, all ICCs > .85) and JOLs. The correlation increased only with external standards, t(63) = 2.24, p = .014 (one-tailed), ηp2 = .077.

> "intraindividual correlations between participants ’ keyword quality and JOLs increased statistically significantly from the first to the second measurement time point for the keyword+comparison group but did not increase for the keyword group, t(63) = 2.24, p = .014 (one-tailed), ηp2 =. 0 77"
