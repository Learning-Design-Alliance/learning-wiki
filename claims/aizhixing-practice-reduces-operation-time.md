## Evidence

### Zhang Yuejiao 2025

Zhang Yuejiao, Li Xiaolan, Wei Xi, Xiao Jiexuan, Zhang Qi, Mai Sui. (2025). A pilot study on the application of a digital dental teaching evaluation system in Class II cavity preparation teaching. BMC Oral Health 25, 372. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05695-y

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed linear model analysis of real-time Aizhixing assessment data from 18 clinicians practicing Class II cavity preparation, with time effects for the second, third and fourth tests of -213.57, -229.43 and -244.71. The article reports "a decreasing trend as the number of examinations increased" with statistical significance. No effect size is printed.

> "Compared with the first examination, the overall operation time in the four examinations showed a decreasing trend as the number of examinations increased, and the difference was statistically significant (P-values were 0.003, 0.002 and 0.001, < 0.05)."
