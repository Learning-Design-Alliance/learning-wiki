## Evidence

### Nixon 2026

Nixon, N., Samadi, M. A., Choi, J., Park, S., De Bastos, P. M., & JaQuay, S. (2026). The Social Cost of an AI Teammate: How an Artificial Teammate Reshapes Human-Human Communication in Small-Team Decision-Making. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.27179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.27179)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects GCA models on 75 analyzed students (30 treatment, 45 control) nested in teams. Differences appeared on "the dimensions that index relational integration", while newness and density "did not differ after FDR adjustment"; only the test statistics are printed.

> "Treatment students had lower participation (b=−0.077,SE= 0.023, 95% CI[−0.123,−0.032], q=.005), overall responsivity (b=−0.034, SE= 0.013, 95% CI[−0.059,−0.009],q=.021), and social impact (b=−0.034,SE= 0.013, 95% CI[−0.060,−0.008],q=.021) than control students."
