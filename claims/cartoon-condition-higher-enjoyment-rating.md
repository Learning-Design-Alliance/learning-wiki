## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students in the cartoon condition rated their liking of the lessons higher (mean 8.3, SD 2.4) than the bibliotherapy group (mean 7.0, SD 3.5) on a 10-point scale, a difference significant only at alpha = 0.10 (p = 0.08). [→ Zousel 2013](#zousel-2013)

## Evidence

### Zousel 2013

Zousel, M. L., Rule, A. C., & Logan, S. R. (2013). Teaching Primary Grade Students Perfectionism through Cartoons Compared to Bibliotherapy. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(2), 199-218. www.iejee.com. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070469

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Student ratings of instructional condition were tabulated with means and standard deviations for the same 46-student two-condition experiment. The cartoon group's mean rating of 8.3 exceeded the control group's 7.0, but the t-test reached significance only at the alpha = 0.10 level (p = 0.08).

> "Students in the control group responded with an average rating of 7.0 (Standard deviation = 3.5) out of 10, while students in the experimental group rated the lessons as 8.3 (s. d. = 2.4) out of 10. A t -test showed that the experimental group’s higher rating was statistically significant only at the alpha = 0.10 level ( p = 0.08)."
