Gorard et al. 2014¶

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See. (2014). Future Foundations: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581128

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup gain-score analyses of the randomised trial: Table 15 prints boys-only English effect +0.22 (treatment mean 3.03 vs control 0.16) and Table 16 prints boys-only maths effect +0.01. The report also prints girls-only English at +0.09 in its summary table.
