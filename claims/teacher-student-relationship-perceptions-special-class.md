# Special physics class students rate teacher-student relationships statistically significantly higher than students in other classes, and report more research, project and experimental opportunities

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Special physics class students gave statistically significantly higher grades on all investigated dimensions of the teacher-student relationship (4.7-4.8 of 5.0 vs 3.0-3.8 in other classes). [→ G.S. Djordjevic 2011](#gs-djordjevic-2011)
`q2 i?` They report significantly more opportunities for research, project design and experimental work, and feel better prepared for further education. [→ G.S. Djordjevic 2011 (2)](#gs-djordjevic-2011-2)

## Evidence

### G.S. Djordjevic 2011

G.S. Djordjevic, D. Pavlovic-Babic, J. Stankovic. (2011). Evaluation of High School Programme for Gifted Pupils in Physics and Sciences in Serbia - Experience in Regional Cooperation - SEENET-MTP Network. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1110.5072](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1110.5072)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaires with parallel thematic structure administered to students of all programmes and special physics class teachers in the external evaluation; students rated support of independence, encouragement of active participation, respect for creativity and respectful treatment, giving "statistically significantly higher grades for all investigated dimensions".

> "Special physics class students have given statistically significantly higher grades for all investigated dimensions of this relationship  (between 4.7 and 4.8 of 5.0( !) in comparison with 3.0 to 3.8 of 5.0 in other classes in this testing)"

### G.S. Djordjevic 2011 (2)

G.S. Djordjevic, D. Pavlovic-Babic, J. Stankovic. (2011). Evaluation of High School Programme for Gifted Pupils in Physics and Sciences in Serbia - Experience in Regional Cooperation - SEENET-MTP Network. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1110.5072](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1110.5072)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same perception questionnaires, special physics class students gave more positive programme-quality marks than both control groups and "claim to have significantly more opportunities for research, project designing and conducting"; students and teachers agreed the curriculum content is relevant but too large.

> "They claim to have significantly more opportunities for research, project designing and conducting, as well as for the experimental work."
