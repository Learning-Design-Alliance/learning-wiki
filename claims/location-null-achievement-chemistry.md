## Evidence

### Jacobson Barineka Nbina & Obomanu 2011

Jacobson Barineka Nbina & Obomanu, B. Joseph. (2011). Assessment of the Effects of Problem Solving Instructional Strategies On Students' Achievement and Retention in Chemistry With Respect to Location in Rivers State. World Journal of Education, 1(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v1n2p74

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANCOVA of achievement in CAT with pretest as covariate, testing 428 SS II students taught by PF, PM and PC. The location source in Table 2 is marked NS (F = 0.017); "The null hypothesis was therefore accepted as stated". Equivalence was not tested beyond this non-significant result.

> "Results in Table 2 showed that there was no significant difference in the post-test mean scores of urban and rural subjects in the Chemistry achievement tests. The null hypothesis was therefore accepted as stated."
