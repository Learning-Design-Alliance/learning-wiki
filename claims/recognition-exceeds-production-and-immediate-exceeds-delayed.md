## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across all groups, learners scored higher on recognition than production tests and higher on immediate than delayed posttests. [→ Lin 2012](#lin-2012)

## Evidence

### Lin 2012

Lin, C.-C., & Tseng, Y.-F. (2012). Videos and animations for vocabulary learning: A study on difficult words. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 11(4), 346-356. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ989299

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive results from the quasi-experiment's two posttests showed that "the participants scored higher in the recognition tests than in the production tests" and that performance declined from immediate to delayed posttest.

> "Across the groups, the participants scored higher in the recognition tests than in the production tests; also, they performed better in the immediate posttest than in the delayed posttest."
