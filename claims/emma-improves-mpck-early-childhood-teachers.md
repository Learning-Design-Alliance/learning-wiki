## Evidence

### Bruns et al. 2017

Julia Bruns, Lars Eichen, Hedwig Gasteiger. (2017). Mathematics-related Competence of Early Childhood Teachers Visiting a Continuous Professional Development Course: An Intervention Study. Mathematics Teacher Education and Development, 19(3), 76-93. MERGA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1163878

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with a control group of in-service early childhood teachers. Repeated measure ANOVA on Rasch-scaled MPCK test scores (35 items) revealed a significant interaction effect between time of measurement and treatment group (F(1, 44) = 5.62, p = 0.02, ηp2 = .113), indicating the intervention group improved more than the control group.

> "There was, however, a significant interaction effect between the time of measurement of mathematical pedagogical content knowledge and the treatment group (F(1, 44) = 5.62, p = 0.02, ηp2 = .113)."
