## Evidence

### Grossi Sarah 2023

Grossi Sarah, Cattoni Maria, Rotolo Nicola, Imperatori Andrea. (2023). Video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery simulation and training: a comprehensive literature review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04482-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Haidari and collaborators' validity-evidence study of the new LapSim VATS lobectomy modules with haptic feedback covering all five lobectomies; significance is reported without an effect size.

> "Forty-one participants (novices n = 22, intermediates n = 10, experts n = 9) performed three consecutive simulated VATS lobectomies of randomly selected lobes, for a total of 123 lobectomies. In this study, 3 metrics (time, blood loss, and total instrument path length) showed a significant difference between experienced surgeons and novices"
