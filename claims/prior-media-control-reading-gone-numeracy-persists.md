## Evidence

### Mundy LK 2020

Mundy LK, Canterford L, Hoq M, Olds T, Moreno-Betancur M, Sawyer S, Kosola S, Patton GC. (2020). Electronic media use and academic performance in late childhood: A longitudinal study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0237908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sensitivity analysis adding grade 3 media use to the cross-sectional models (Table 4) in the same cohort; in the adjusted numeracy models TV >2 hours gave β = -12.3 (p = 0.02) and computer >1 hour gave β = -11.3 (p = 0.04).

> "All of the associations observed in Table 3 with regards to NAPLAN reading scores were no longer apparent after adjusting for grade 3 media use. However, the association between grade 5 TV watching and grade 5 NAPLAN numeracy score, and the association between grade 5 computer use and grade 5 NAPLAN numeracy score were still evident, suggesting these are proximal; effects."
