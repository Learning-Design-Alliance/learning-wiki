### Bowens 2016

Bowens, B. D., & Warren, S. R. (2016). Toward Social Justice: The Characteristics of an Effective Mathematics Intervention Program for Urban Middle School Students. Journal of Urban Learning Teaching and Research, 12, 35-46. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1119158

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal comparison of N = 248 students with CST scores for grades 6, 7, and 8: n = 27 participated in JEMP for two years and n = 221 served as control. Treatment means on the 2013 Algebra CST were 455.26 versus 394.20 for control (Table 7).

> "students who participated in JEMP for two consecutive summers had significantly higher mean scores F(1, 242) = 9.99, p = .002, η2 = .039 on the 2013 grade eight Algebra CST scores than students who received no treatment"
