### Hedman Leif Rune 2020

Hedman Leif Rune, Felländer-Tsai Li. (2020). Simulation-based skills training in non-performing orthopedic surgeons: skills acquisition, motivation, and flow during the COVID-19 pandemic. Acta Orthopaedica. https://doi.org/10.1080/17453674.2020.1781413

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null finding from the same pilot experiment with 30 surgical novices trained to a pre-set criterion on MIST-VR manipulative diathermy, with motivation self-assessed by the Situational Motivation Scale. The article prints no effect size or test statistic.

> "We found no statistically significant correlations between types of situational motivation and MIST-VR performance. Presumably, the manipulation task in this low-fidelity simulator was too simple."
