## Evidence

### Beck A.K. 2020

Beck A.K., Baker A.L., Carter G., Wratten C., Bauer J., Wolfenden L., McCarter K., Britton B. (2020). Assessing Adherence, Competence and Differentiation in a Stepped-Wedge Randomised Clinical Trial of a Complex Behaviour Change Intervention. Nutrients 12(8), 2332. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12082332

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Differentiation analyses of the coded consultation sample plus intention-to-treat linear mixed models of ARM-5 alliance scores. Mean interpersonal effectiveness fell in the "expert" range in both conditions (M = 5.51 control; M = 5.69 intervention); the spirit comparison "did not significantly differ" (p = 0.762). Alliance (dietitian p = 0.528; patient p = 0.542) and dose were also non-significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "The "spirit" with which consultations were delivered did not significantly differ between control and intervention consultations (95% CI = –0.246, 0.180; p = 0.762)."
