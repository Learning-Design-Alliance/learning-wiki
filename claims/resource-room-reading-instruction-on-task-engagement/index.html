Evidence¶

Swanson 2010¶

Swanson, E. A., & Vaughn, S. (2010). An Observation Study of Reading Instruction Provided to Elementary Students With Learning Disabilities in the Resource Room. Psychology in the Schools, 47(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/pits.20484

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational study of 10 special education resource-room teachers, with 2,178 minutes of reading instruction coded using the ICE-R over three consecutive observation days. The discussion reports "students remained on task, with an overall student engagement rating across all observations of 2.76 on a 3-point scale" and less than 10% of time on logistical tasks.

"students remained on task, with an overall student engagement rating across all observations of 2.76 on a 3-point scale (range = 2.43–3.0)"
