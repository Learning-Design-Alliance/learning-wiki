## Evidence

### Melesse Girma Tufa 2024

Melesse Girma Tufa, Amde Tewodros, Tezera Robel. (2024). Competency in evidence‐based medicine and associated factors among medical radiology technologists in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.777

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable binary logistic regression on the 392-respondent survey data (model predicting 82.4% of observed respondents, X² = 71.726, df = 7, p < 0.001); the study reports AOR = 1.949 (95% CI 1.155–3.291) for moderate versus poor knowledge.

> "Thus, MRT who had moderate EBM knowledge was 1.95 times (AOR = 1.949, 95% CI = 1.155, 3.291) higher odds to have a good EBM practice compared to those who had poor EBM knowledge."

### Melesse Girma Tufa 2024 (2)

Melesse Girma Tufa, Amde Tewodros, Tezera Robel. (2024). Competency in evidence‐based medicine and associated factors among medical radiology technologists in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.777

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multivariable logistic regression; the study reports AOR = 1.824 (95% CI 1.135–2.930) for good versus poor understanding of statistical terms, with 57.9% of MRTs overall having poor understanding of EBM statistical terms.

> "The odds of having a good EBM practice were 1.82 times (AOR = 1.824, 95% CI = 1.135, 2.930) higher for MRTs who had a good understanding of statistical terms when compared with those individuals with a low understanding of statistical terms."
