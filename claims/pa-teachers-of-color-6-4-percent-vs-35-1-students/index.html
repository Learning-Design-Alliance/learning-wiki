The Education Trust 2022¶

The Education Trust. (2022). Educator Diversity State Profile: Pennsylvania. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The Education Trust's analysis of 2018-19 Common Core of Data student records and state teacher data for K-12 public and charter schools reports the demographic mismatch. The table prints "Total Percentage of People of Color" at 35.1% for students and 6.4% for teachers; White teachers are 93.6%.
