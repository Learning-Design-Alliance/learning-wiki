### Madsen 2013

Madsen, McKagan, and Sayre (2013). Gender gap on concept inventories in physics: What is consistent, what is inconsistent, and what factors influence the gap? Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 9, 020121. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.9.020121

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

The review's synthesis of published FCI and FMCE pretest studies across institutions, instructors, and student populations reports the gap 'ranges between 8.2% and 18.7%' with a weighted average of 13.0%, always favoring men.

> "Studies that compare male and female students' scores on the FCI and FMCE contain a remarkably consistent finding: there is always a gender gap favoring men on the pretest [8-19]. This gap ranges between 8.2% and 18.7% (see Fig. 1), with a weighted average value of 13.0%."
