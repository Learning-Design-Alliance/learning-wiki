Pei-Hua Chen 2026¶

Pei-Hua Chen, Hsin-Yu Lee, Yueh-Min Huang and Ting-Ting Wu. (2026). Unpacking the heterogeneity of pre-service teachers' ChatGPT acceptance: a latent profile analysis across STEM and non-STEM disciplines. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00634-x

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Chi-square test of a contingency table crossing STEM/non-STEM background with the four LPA profiles among 128 pre-service teachers (68 STEM, 60 non-STEM). The article reports "Cramer's V = 0.532, corresponds to a medium-to-large association"; Fisher's exact test was also significant.

"The chi-square test of independence showed a highly significant association between disciplinary background and latent profile membership, χ²(3) = 36.20, p < .001. Fisher's exact test also indicated a robust association, p < .001. The effect size, Cramer's V = 0.532, corresponds to a medium-to-large association."
