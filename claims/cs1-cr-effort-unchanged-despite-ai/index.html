Evidence¶

Peter Fowles 2026¶

Peter Fowles, Erik Falor, Sulove Bhattarai, John Edwards, and Seth Poulsen. (2026). Combating Harms of Generative AI in CS1 with Code Review Interviews and a Flipped Classroom. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21374

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Keystroke-log analysis comparing effort measures per submission across Fall 2024 and CS1-CR; time-on-task summed elapsed keystroke time with gaps over 5 minutes discarded. The study found "no evidence" of decreased effort; no effect size is printed.

"Surprisingly, we found no evidence of this with(𝑈= 4336, 𝑝= 0.53) for time-on-task and(𝑈= 4372, 𝑝= 0.60) for number of keystrokes (Figures 3c and 3d, respectively). It appears that students engaged with the code just as much when AI use was allowed as when it was not."
