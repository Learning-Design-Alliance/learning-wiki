Grion 2026¶

Grion, Valentina; Doria, Beatrice; Agostini, Daniele; Slaviero, Giorgia. (2026). Artificial intelligence and feedback in university education: effectiveness and student perceptions. Assessment and Evaluation in Higher Education. https://dx.doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2026.2697962

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Non-inferiority and equivalence analyses within the same quasi-experimental design (47 groups, N = 238) comparing two LLM feedback conditions against expert teacher feedback. The article reports "practical comparability between GPT-o4-mini and teacher feedback" and non-inferiority for DeepSeek R1.
