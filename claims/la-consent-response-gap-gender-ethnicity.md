# Response rates to a learning analytics consent email differ by gender and ethnicity, with Black students underrepresented and White students overrepresented among respondents

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Gender is associated with email engagement (click rate) but not with consent rate among those who responded, with a moderate association (Cramer's V = 0.22). [→ Warren Li 2022](#warren-li-2022)
`q3 i1` Ethnicity is associated with click rate (Cramer's V = 0.13): Black students participate less than non-Black students and White students participate more than non-White students. [→ Warren Li 2022 (2)](#warren-li-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Warren Li 2022

Warren Li, Kaiwen Sun, Florian Schaub, Christopher Brooks. (2022). Disparities in Students' Propensity to Consent to Learning Analytics. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 32:564–608. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00254-2

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared analysis of email-prompt responses from 272 of 4,000 stratified students found "gender is independent of the consent rate" but a moderate association with click rate, with printed effect size Cramer's V = 0.22 (χ2 = 13.24, p = 0.0003).

> "A Chi-Squared test indicates that gender is independent of the consent rate, but this is not the case for number of clicks ( χ2 = 13.24, p = 0.0003, Cramer’s V = 0.22); there is a moderate association."

### Warren Li 2022 (2)

Warren Li, Kaiwen Sun, Florian Schaub, Christopher Brooks. (2022). Disparities in Students' Propensity to Consent to Learning Analytics. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 32:564–608. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00254-2

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared test of email clicks by ethnicity found ethnicity "is not independent from the click rate" with printed effect size Cramer's V = 0.13 (χ2 = 14.32, p = 0.002); proportion tests additionally showed Black students underrepresented (−40%) and White overrepresented (31%).

> "Once again, we find that ethnicity is not independent from the click rate ( χ2 = 14.32, p = 0.002, Cramer’s V = 0.13) with a medium effect size, whereas there is no such relationship with consent."
