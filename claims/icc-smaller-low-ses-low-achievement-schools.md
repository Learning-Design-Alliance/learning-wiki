# Unconditional intraclass correlations are smaller in low-SES schools and in low-achievement schools than in the full national sample.

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In mathematics, unconditional ICCs in low-SES schools (bottom half of the school SES distribution) are a bit smaller than those in the entire national population. [→ Hedges 2007](#hedges-2007)
`q2 i?` In low-achievement schools, unconditional mathematics ICCs range from about .09 to .13 in Grades K-4, about .05 to .08 in Grades 5-7, and .075 to .085 in Grades 8-12. [→ Hedges 2007 (2)](#hedges-2007-2)

## Evidence

### Hedges 2007

Hedges, L. V., & Hedberg, E. C. (2007). Intraclass Correlation Values for Planning Group-Randomized Trials in Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 29(1), 60-87. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373707299706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mixed-model analysis of schools in the bottom half of the SES distribution (proportion eligible for free or reduced-price lunch) found unconditional mathematics ICCs "a bit smaller than those reported in Table 2 for the entire national population"; the tendency did not hold for adjusted ICCs.

> "There is a tendency for the intraclass correlation values in this sample to be a bit smaller than those reported in Table 2 for the entire national population, a tendency that does not hold for the conditional (adjusted) intraclass correlations."

### Hedges 2007 (2)

Hedges, L. V., & Hedberg, E. C. (2007). Intraclass Correlation Values for Planning Group-Randomized Trials in Education. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 29(1), 60-87. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373707299706

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In schools in the bottom half of the school mean mathematics achievement distribution, unconditional ICCs ranged from about .09 to .13 (Grades K-4), .05 to .08 (Grades 5-7), and .075 to .085 (Grades 8-12).

> "In general, the intraclass correlations at kindergarten through Grade 4 range from about .09 to .13, in Grades 5-7 from about .05 to .08, and in Grades 8-12 from .075 to .085."
