## Evidence

### Kılıç Gülşen 2026

Kılıç Gülşen, Işık Sevcan Avcı, Çevik Banu. (2026). Nursing students’ first clinical encounters with delirium: a qualitative study of experiential learning and professional development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09980-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Students described using "reality orientation, reassurance, calm verbal guidance, distraction, and emotionally supportive communication," acquired largely through trial-and-error and observation rather than structured preparation.

> "Orienting patients to reality, maintaining a calm and patient tone, and utilizing reassuring explanations proved more effective than reacting to noncompliant behaviors."
