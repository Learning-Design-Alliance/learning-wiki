## Evidence

### McCaughan E.M. 2021

McCaughan E.M., Flannagan C., Parahoo K., Bingham S.L., Brady N., Connaghan J., Maguire R., Thompson S., Jain S., Kirby M., O'Connor S.R. (2021). Effects of a Brief E-Learning Resource on Sexual Attitudes and Beliefs of Healthcare Professionals Working in Prostate Cancer Care: A Pilot Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph181910045

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pairwise comparisons in the 44-participant pilot (overall F = 27.013; P = 0.001). The authors call the 1-month maintenance of the language improvement an important finding and postulate the EASSi framework contributed to it.

> "For the question on having the right language to use when discussing sexual concerns, significant differences were found between both T0 and T1 and T0 and T2 (mean score/4 = 2.52 vs. 2.98, respectively: P = 0.001 and mean score/4 = 2.52 vs. 2.85, respectively: P = 0.001)"
