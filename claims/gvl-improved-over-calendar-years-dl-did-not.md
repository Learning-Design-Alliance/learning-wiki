## Evidence

### Sakles John C MD 2014

Sakles John C MD, Mosier Jarrod MD, Patanwala Asad E. PharmD, Dicken John BS. (2014). Learning Curves for Direct Laryngoscopy and GlideScope® Video Laryngoscopy in an Emergency Medicine Residency. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2014.9.23691

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same logistic mixed-effects analyses of the seven-year dataset, calendar year was included as a possible confounder. DL odds did not increase (aOR 1.0), while "GVL performance improved over time" (aOR 1.2, p-value 0.009).

> "for each calendar year increment, the odds of first pass success did not increase with DL (aOR 1.0, 95% CI 0.9–1.1, p-value 0.878). However, GVL performance improved over time with continued use in the ED (aOR 1.2, 95% CI 1.1–1.4, p-value 0.009)"
