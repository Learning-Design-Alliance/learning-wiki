### Malik Behlol & Hukam Dad 2010

Malik Behlol & Hukam Dad. (2010). Effectiveness of Structural Method of Teaching Vocabulary. English Language Teaching, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/elt

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group experiment with 24 students per group, equated on pretest scores by matching. The posttest t test gave t = 3.79, df = 46, p = .000, so the null hypothesis was rejected; means were "66.83 and that of control group was 55.33".

> "The mean of the experimental group was found 66.83 and that of control group was 55.33. The standard deviations were 11.09 and 9.86 respectively."
