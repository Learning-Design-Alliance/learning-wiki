## Evidence

### Rojas 2016

Natalia Rojas. (2016). The Association of Peer Behavioral Regulation with School Readiness Skills in Preschool. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567502

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null findings from the same two-level HLM analysis of the ParentCorps RCT preschool sample. The article reports "d = -0.29, p = 0.192" for class-level attention-impulse control and no significant language associations for either peer measure; no effect size is printed for the language nulls.

> "Attention-impulse control was not a significant predictor of children's school readiness skills (d = -0.29, p = 0.192). Neither peer behavioral regulation measure (engagement nor attention-impulse control) were significantly associated with language growth."
