Survey section on unintentionally active bonded retainers among the 236 Dutch GDPs. The printed percentages are 76.8% familiar, 73.1% had observed it, and 83.8% referred or would refer; 55.1% also informed the patient about the problem.

> "The greater part of GDPs was familiar with unintentionally active BRs, had observed it in a patient, and referred or would refer their patients to the orthodontic practitioner, 76.8% (n = 166), 73.1% (n = 158) and 83.8% (n = 181) respectively"
