## Evidence

### Torab-Miandoab A 2026

Torab-Miandoab A, Ghafourifard M, Habibi-Chenaran S, Ghaffarifar S. (2026). Structured approaches to improving outcomes of oral exam in medical and paramedical education: A systematic review and evidence-based framework. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0355461

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of proposed solutions across the 102 included studies. The review reports examiner training recommended in 62 studies (60.8%) and standardization in 58 studies (56.9%), often "achieving inter-rater reliability coefficients exceeding 0.80 in multi-case assessments"; multiple examiners were advocated in 45 studies (44.1%).

> "Examiner training was the most frequently recommended intervention (62 studies; 60.8%), typically involving orientations, workshops, or peer review sessions to mitigate bias and improve scoring consistency."

### Torab-Miandoab A 2026 (2)

Torab-Miandoab A, Ghafourifard M, Habibi-Chenaran S, Ghaffarifar S. (2026). Structured approaches to improving outcomes of oral exam in medical and paramedical education: A systematic review and evidence-based framework. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0355461

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of solution outcomes. The review reports that "structured and mock examination formats demonstrated superior effectiveness in addressing identified challenges", with kappa coefficients >0.70, and that examiner training and scoring standardization addressed examiner variability in 85% of relevant studies.

> "Overall, structured and mock examination formats demonstrated superior effectiveness in addressing identified challenges, with reported improvements in inter-rater reliability (kappa coefficients >0.70) and enhanced learner satisfaction."
