Descriptive synthesis of assessment instruments across the 20 included studies. The review reports "All but seven studies reported using valid and reliable instruments" and lists Fresno test, Berlin questionnaire, Taylor questionnaire, Scale of efficacy toward EBP, and EBP competence questionnaire among them.

"All but seven studies reported using valid and reliable instruments [4,6,9,14,16,21]. Two studies used the Fresno test [6,21], one the Fresno test and Berlin questionnaire [9], one the Taylor questionnaire [14], one the Scale of efficacy toward EBP [16], one the EBP competence questionnaire [4], and one a validated instrument for assessment of EBP related knowledge [20]."
