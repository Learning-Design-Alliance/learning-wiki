## Evidence

### Surcouf JW 2018

Surcouf JW, Mumphrey CG, Barkemeyer BM, Buis M, Gupta RW, Olister S, Patrick-Esteve J, Rivera D, Zeringue A, Chauvin SW. (2018). Neonatal Intensive Care Unit Boot Camp: A Preparatory Curriculum for Pediatric Residents. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10720

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same post-rotation questionnaire (N = 46 respondents). The article reports these as descriptive frequencies with no inferential test; of 10 residents who repeated the camp, eight (80.00%) found the repeat useful.

> "Responses to Item 8 revealed that 80.40% agreed that holding the boot camp prior to each NICU rotation would be useful. Residents were asked if they would find it useful to repeat the boot camp prior to future NICU rotations (Item 9), and 80.43% responded yes."
