## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Some students with low pre-test grades increased their post-test achievement, so the study did not confirm the earlier finding that high course readiness is needed to succeed with LOs. [→ Çakiroğlu 2012](#cakiroglu-2012)

## Evidence

### Çakiroğlu 2012

Çakiroğlu, Ü., Baki, A., & Akkan, Y. (2012). The effects of using learning objects in two different settings. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 11(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ976582

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observation of individual students' pre-test to post-test change in the quasi-experiment. The authors state the research of Chalk et al. (2003), which linked readiness to LO success, "has not been confirmed with this study".

> "it is seen that some of the students who had low grades at pre-tests have increased their achievements at post tests."
