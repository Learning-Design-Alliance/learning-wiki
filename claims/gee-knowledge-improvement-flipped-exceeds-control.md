# GEE analysis shows the flipped classroom group's posttest knowledge improvement exceeds the control group's by 11.98 points

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The posttest-minus-pretest knowledge difference of the experimental group was significantly higher than the control group's, with a group-by-time interaction coefficient of 11.98 (p < 0.001). [→ Chu Tsung-Lan 2019](#chu-tsung-lan-2019)

## Evidence

### Chu Tsung-Lan 2019

Chu Tsung-Lan, Wang Jeng, Monrouxe Lynn, Sung Yu-Chih, Kuo Chen-li, Ho Lun-Hui, Lin Yueh-E. (2019). The effects of the flipped classroom in teaching evidence based nursing: A quasi-experimental study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210606

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Generalized Estimating Equation analysis of individual improvement over time in the 151-nurse study. The Group × Learning effect interaction for posttest vs. pretest yielded β = 11.98, SE = 2.58, 95% CI [6.91, 17.04], Wald χ² = 21.47, p < 0.001.

> "In terms of the learning effect between groups, the posttest-pretest knowledge difference of the experimental group was higher than that of the control group, with the difference of 11.98 (p < 0.001)."
