### Mark H. Ashcraft and Jeremy A. Krause 2007 (2)

Mark H. Ashcraft and Jeremy A. Krause. (2007). Working memory, math performance, and math anxiety. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 14(2), 243-248. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03194059

> "When college majors are given a math anxiety test, those who average the highest are individuals preparing to be elementary school teachers (Hembree, 1990). Compounding this, students earning such degrees are typically required to take very few math courses"
