# Human coding of other-oriented transacts in the debate corpus reached high inter-coder reliability (kappa 0.72 for reasoning, 0.7 for transactivity, 0.95 for self/other distinction)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Two trained coders achieved kappa agreement of 0.72 for reasoning coding, 0.7 for transactivity versus externalization, and 0.95 for self-oriented versus other-oriented transacts. [→ Gweon 2013](#gweon-2013)

## Evidence

### Gweon 2013

Gweon, G., Jain, M., McDonough, J., Raj, B., & Rosé, C. P. (2013). Measuring prevalence of other-oriented transactive contributions using an automated measure of speech style accommodation. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning (2013). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-013-9172-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability evaluation of the manual three-step transactivity coding over the full corpus. The article separately reports that "For the distinction between self-oriented and other-oriented transacts, the kappa value was 0.95" and 0.7 for transactivity versus externalization.

> "In a final evaluation of reliability for reasoning coding, the kappa agreement was 0.72 between two coders over all of the data."
