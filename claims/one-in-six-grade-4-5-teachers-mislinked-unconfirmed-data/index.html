The paper's comparison of roster-confirmed versus unconfirmed teacher/student links in DC Public Schools administrative data finds that departmentalized grade 4-5 instruction produces mislinks: "about one in six teachers in these grades and subjects is linked in the unconfirmed data to a subject that he or she does not teach."

"Due to the departmentalization of instruction in math and reading/English language arts in grades 4 and 5, about one in six teachers in these grades and subjects is linked in the unconfirmed data to a subject that he or she does not teach."
