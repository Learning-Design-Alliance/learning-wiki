## Evidence

### Egenberg 2017

Egenberg, S., Masenga, G., Bru, L. E., Eggebø, T. M., Mushi, C., Massay, D., & Øian, P. (2017). Impact of multi-professional, scenario-based training on postpartum hemorrhage in Tanzania: a quasi-experimental, pre- vs. post-intervention study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1478-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same random-sample comparison found no significant change in diagnosed PPH; estimated blood loss means were 237 ml (SD 244) pre vs 243 ml (SD 246) post, p = 0.19. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Data extracted from the random samples at KCMC, showed incidence of PPH of 0.9% before training and 1.3% after training (p = 0.23)."
