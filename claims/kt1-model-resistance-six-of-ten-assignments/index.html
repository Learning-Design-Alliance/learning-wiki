M. Mikhasenko, M. Stahl, I. Segal, D. Parmar, A. Zimmer, and A. Kazatsky. (2026). AI in Particle Physics Education: Research Problems and Foundational Skills. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05719 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.05719

q1 · i? · design · r2

Instructor observation reported without a systematic benchmark: "Codex 5.6-Sol can produce solutions to HW1, HW4, HW5, HW6, HW7, and HW8" while struggling with HW2, HW3, HW9, and HW10. The article states no systematic benchmark of named models, prompts, or access tiers was conducted.

"At the time of publica- tion, Codex 5.6-Sol can produce solutions to HW1, HW4, HW5, HW6, HW7, and HW8. It struggles with HW2 (a complex multistep workflow), HW3 (a visual interface), HW9 (human input for visualization is needed), and HW10 (no multi-speaker podcast capability)."
