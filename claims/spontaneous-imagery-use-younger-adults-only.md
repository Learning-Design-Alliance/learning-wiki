# Instructed visual-imagery use was near-universal across age groups, whereas spontaneous imagery use occurred almost only in younger adults

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Strategy-instructed participants used mental imagery far more often than controls (.95 vs. .28), η² = .63. [→ von der Linden et al. 2015](#von-der-linden-et-al-2015)
`q3 i3` In the control group, spontaneous imagery use was high only in younger adults (.65); none of the children and only 5% of adolescents used it. [→ von der Linden et al. 2015 (2)](#von-der-linden-et-al-2015-2)

## Evidence

### von der Linden et al. 2015

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on self-reported strategy use (Strategy Use Questionnaire, ratings kappa = .95) in the life-span experiment (N = 160). Instructed participants used imagery far more often (.95 vs. .28), η2 = .63.

> "we found a significant main effect of experimental condition (F(1,154) = 221.46; p < .001; η2 = .63): Participants who received the strategy instruction used mental imagery much more often than participants in the control condition (.28 vs. .95)"

### von der Linden et al. 2015 (2)

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up ANOVA within the control group on spontaneous imagery use. A main effect of age group, η2 = .35: younger adults (.65) used imagery spontaneously far more than children (.00), adolescents (.05), or older adults (.25).

> "For subjects in the control group, the ANOVA revealed a main effect of age group (F(3,74) = 12.30; p < .001; η2 = .35).  A post-hoc analysis showed that the percentage of younger adults (.65) spontaneously applying a mental imagery strategy was significantly higher than that of the other age groups"
