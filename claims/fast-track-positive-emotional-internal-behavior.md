### Fast Track: Elementary School 2014

Fast Track: Elementary School. (2014). WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/628

Findings from the Conduct Problems Prevention Research Group (1999a) randomized controlled trial, which met WWC standards without reservations, after one year of implementation.
