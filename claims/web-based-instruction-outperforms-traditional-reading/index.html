Evidence¶

Maryam Soleimani & Shima Gahhari 2012¶

Maryam Soleimani & Shima Gahhari. (2012). Computer Assisted Language Testing: On the Efficacy of Web-based Approach in the Instruction of Elementary Learners of English. English Language Teaching, 5(9). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n9p66

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with 20 web-based and 20 traditional-group elementary learners in a language institute, compared on gained scores after six months. The t-test on reading showed "a significant difference" favoring the web-based group, t(38)=4.39.

"The result of the t-test indicates that there is a significant difference (M web-based =28.20, M traditional =17.90) between students in web-based group and in traditional group (t(df=38)=4.39; p.05)."
