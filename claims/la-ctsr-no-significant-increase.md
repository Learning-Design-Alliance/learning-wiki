## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Andrew Ferstl 2010

Andrew Ferstl. (2010). Teaching and Learning Science with Learning Assistants. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1007.3789](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1007.3789)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post CTSR administered to the eight recruited LAs during 2009-2010. The article reports no significant increase and offers the authors' explanation that LAs scored very high at the beginning. No test statistic or effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "First, the CTSR was given pre-post to the LAs.  There was not a significant increase in the scores.  This, in hindsight, is not surprising since they all scored very high on the CTSR at the beginning (i.e. we purposely recruited "the brightest and best") and thus had little room for improvement."
