## Evidence

### Teresa Swist 2024

Teresa Swist, Simon Buckingham Shum, Kalervo N. Gulson. (2024). Co-producing AIED Ethics Under Lockdown: an Empirical Study of Deliberative Democracy in Action. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 34:670–705. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00380-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of the UTS DD consultation: 13 stakeholders (students n=5, educators n=4, leaders n=4) interviewed after the process. Theme 1 reports a "structured and contextual process built upon a culture of trust" scaffolding elicitation of stakeholder opinions.

> "This project's particular application of DD offered students, educators, and leaders a structured and contextual process built upon a culture of trust that scaffolded the conscious/careful elicitation of different stakeholder opinions and experiences."

### Teresa Swist 2024 (2)

Teresa Swist, Simon Buckingham Shum, Kalervo N. Gulson. (2024). Co-producing AIED Ethics Under Lockdown: an Empirical Study of Deliberative Democracy in Action. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 34:670–705. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00380-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Theme 2 of the same interview study reports an "interactive information exchange" empowering participants to generate new knowledge; students and educators reported learning from experts and from diverse stakeholder perspectives.

> "This DD project involved students, educators, and leaders in an interactive information exchange which relied upon contextually testing what is reasonable by empowering participants to transgress preconceived notions and generate new knowledge."
