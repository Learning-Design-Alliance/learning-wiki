## Evidence

### Vignoles et al. 2015

Anna Vignoles, John Jerrim, Richard Cowan. (2015). Mathematics Mastery: Primary Evaluation Report. Education Endowment Foundation. http://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/library/maths-mastery-primary

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory analysis reported in Figure 4: a series of quantile regression models estimated at every 5th percentile between p15 and p85, with standard errors bootstrapped by cluster using 100 replications. The report states there is little evidence of heterogeneous treatment effects across the post-test distribution.

> "There is some, but relatively little, evidence that the intervention was less effective for the bottom half of the test distribution. The quantile regression estimates all sit relatively closely to the dashed horizontal line (OLS estimate), with all those below p40 or above p65 not statistically different from zero at even the 10% level."
