Theoretical argument in the Discussion section of this position paper: students who stress-test prompts to find where a model fails become "expert" observers of AI behavior rather than passive consumers. The article offers no empirical data for this claim; it rests on the authors' conceptual argument.

> "Furthermore, cultivating AI intuition serves as a vital safeguard against the risks of technological overreliance and the erosion of critical thinking. By engaging in inductive experimentation and phenomenological mapping, such as stress-testing prompts to identify where a model fails, students move beyond passive consumers to become "expert" observers of AI behavior."
