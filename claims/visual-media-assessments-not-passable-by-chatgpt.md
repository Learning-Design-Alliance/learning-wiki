## Evidence

### Ivory 2026

Ivory, M., Finnerty, S., Dunn, K., Philpot, R., & Warmelink, L. (2026). The Integrity of Psychology Assessments in the AI Age: A Critical Examination. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09729-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Within the case study, poster and presentation assessments could not pass because the model did not generate the slides needed; the authors note the offered content was sufficient for a student to copy and paste into slides, and that newer tools can generate downloadable files.

> "For assignments that required visual media output, such as posters and presentation slides, the Gen-AI was incapable of producing sufficient content as the model could not produce complete presentation slides nor poster content."
