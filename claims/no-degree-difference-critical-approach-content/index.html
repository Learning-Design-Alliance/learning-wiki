Mahmoodarabi 2015¶

Mahmoodarabi, M., & Khodabakhsh, M. R. (2015). Critical Pedagogy: EFL Teachers' Views, Experience and Academic Degrees. English Language Teaching, 8(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n6p100

q3 · i0 · associational · r1

Null result from the between-subjects ANOVA on Factor 3 among the 403 respondents: "F (2, 400) = 1.40, P > .05". Descriptive means were PhD 4.92, BA 4.72, MA 4.68, but no test reached significance; equivalence was not tested.

"Based on the results displayed in table 3 (F (2, 400) = 1.40, P > .05, Partial η2 = .007 representing a weak effect size), it can be concluded that there were not any significant differences between the BA, MA and PhD teachers' awareness of the third factor of critical pedagogy."
