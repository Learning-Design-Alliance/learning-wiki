### M.M. Taddei 2009

M.M. Taddei, T.N.C. Mendes, C. Farina. (2009). Subtleties on energy calculations in the image method. https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4497 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0903.4497)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Proved theorem in Section 4.1, stated as a theorem and demonstrated analytically from the general energy expression U = (1/2)∫ρΦ dV. Because each conductor surface is equipotential, the surface term vanishes for grounded or neutral conductors, leaving U = (1/2)qΦ̃(x0), the potential due to the image charges.

> "The electrostatic energy of a point charge q near N perfect conductors of arbitrary shapes, each conductor being either neutral or gr ounded, is half the Coulombic energy between the charge q and each image charge."
