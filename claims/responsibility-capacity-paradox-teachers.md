Reflexive thematic analysis of four initial teacher workshops (n = 10) identified this theme in Stage 1 co-development. The analysis was inductive, following Braun and Clarke's reflexive thematic analysis, with the theme and verbatim examples reported in Table 5.

> "a theme emerged from the tension experienced by teachers who actively expanded the boundaries of their professional identity by viewing emotional understanding and support as a core part of their daily job, yet experienced a responsibility-capacity paradox in which they are structurally limited by low levels of mental health literacy, overwhelming workloads, and a lack of training, tools, and structured support"
