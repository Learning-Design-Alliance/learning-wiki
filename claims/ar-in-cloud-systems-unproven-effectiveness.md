The article's own analysis of scientific publications (its stated research method) concludes that "the experience of the augmented reality using in the systems based on cloud technologies already exists" while effectiveness remains unproven. The article adds that laboratory tests are known but no natural-condition experiment in control and experimental groups has been carried out.
