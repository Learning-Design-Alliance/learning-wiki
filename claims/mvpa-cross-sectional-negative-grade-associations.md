# Within-semester school-day MVPA shows small negative cross-sectional associations with Grade 4 course grades, strongest in Grade 4 Fall

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Grade 4 Fall MVPA was significantly negatively associated with all four course grades (standardized effect sizes about −0.04 to −0.06), while Grade 4 Spring MVPA was significantly associated only with math grades and Grade 5 Fall MVPA with none. [→ Elish Paul N. 2022](#elish-paul-n-2022)

## Evidence

### Elish Paul N. 2022

Elish Paul N., Bryan Cassandra S., Boedeker Peter J., Calvert Hannah G., Kay Christi M., Meyer Adria M., Gazmararian Julie A. (2022). The longitudinal association between objectively-measured school-day physical activity and academic achievement in US elementary school students. International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-022-01328-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional multilevel analyses of 4936 Grade 4 students in 40 schools, adjusting for demographics, school characteristics and prior achievement. The article reports "T1 MVPA was significantly associated with all course grades" with standardized effect sizes from −0.042 to −0.061; T2 only math (β = −0.053) and T3 none were significant after Bonferroni correction.

> "T1 MVPA was significantly associated with all course grades (βMath = − 0.055, βReading = − 0.052, βSpelling = − 0.069, βWriting = − 0.059, all p-values = <.0001), but T2 MVPA was only significantly associated with math course grades"
