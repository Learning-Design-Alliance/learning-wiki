## Evidence

### Hope D 2018

Hope D, Adamson KA, McManus IC, Chis L, Elder A. (2018). Using differential item functioning to evaluate potential bias in a high stakes postgraduate knowledge based assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1143-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of the impact of the eight removed DIF questions on the 13,694 candidate sittings of the MRCP(UK) Part 1 examination. The article reports the questions "did not significantly impact the overall performance of the cohort" and that group-level differences cannot be explained by unfair questions.

> "These questions did not significantly impact the overall performance of the cohort. Group-level differences in performance between the groups we studied in this examination cannot be explained by a subset of unfair questions."
