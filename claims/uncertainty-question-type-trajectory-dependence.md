## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Chi-square tests revealed little dependence of child question types on parent question types outside uncertainty periods, but dependence emerged with statistically significant Chi-square values for exam preparation and university topics during uncertainty periods. [→ Ali 2026](#ali-2026)
`q2 i?` Open-possibility and closed-possibility questions in child comments were more likely to be dependent on the same question types in parent comments during the uncertainty period. [→ Ali 2026](#ali-2026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests of parent-child question-type dependence against a null model of no dependence (Table 5), with residual examination in Table 6. The authors call the possibility-thinking transmission finding "preliminary evidence" and note lexical mimicry cannot be ruled out.
