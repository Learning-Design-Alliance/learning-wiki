## Evidence

### Kipp Markus 2021

Kipp Markus. (2021). Impact of the COVID-19 Pandemic on the Acceptance and Use of an E-Learning Platform. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182111372

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Small voluntary online surveys at the end of each study period (12 of 68 non-pandemic respondents; 109 of 201 pandemic respondents). The quote gives the non-pandemic function preferences; the pandemic survey found book collection at "72%" and downloads at "53%".

> "although various ELP functions were used by the students, the possibility to download items (75%) and search for specific items (i.e., book collection; 67%) received the most appeal."
