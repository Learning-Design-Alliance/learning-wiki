## Evidence

### Lim Jaeseo 2022

Lim Jaeseo, Ko Hyunwoong, Park Jooyong, Ihm Jungjoon. (2022). Effect of active learning and online discussions on the academic performances of dental students. BMC Medical Education, 22, 312. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03377-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on the online experiment's transfer item scores with study condition (lecture vs self-study) and discussion condition (worksheet vs free discussion) as factors. The article reports the interaction "partial eta-squared = 0.104", a medium-large omnibus effect, alongside a significant main effect of study condition.

> "the interaction effect of these two factors was significant; F (1, 78) = 9.04, P < 0.001, partial eta-squared = 0.104 (Fig. 1)."
