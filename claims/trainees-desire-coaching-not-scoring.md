## Evidence

### Burm Sarah 2020

Burm Sarah, Sebok-Syer Stefanie S., Van Koughnett Julie Ann, Watling Christopher J. (2020). Are we generating more assessments without added value? Surgical trainees’ perceptions of and receptiveness to cross-specialty assessment. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-020-00594-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group findings from the study of cross-specialty formative assessment in the Surgical Foundations program. Trainees described observational feedback as vague or generic, with one noting "there's no expert opinion", and said assessors could tick boxes but not provide improvement strategies.

> "What trainees wanted were strategies for improvement, not merely an assessment of performance."
