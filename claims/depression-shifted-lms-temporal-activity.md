## Evidence

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026

Arya VarastehNezhad, Fattaneh Taghiyareh. (2026). Behavioral and Performance Indicators of Depression and Anxiety in Electronic Learning Systems. Department of Computer Engineering, University of Tehran. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04254)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison from the temporal engagement analysis of Group 2 (37 students). Depressed students' night log counts averaged 59.73 versus 26.23 for non-depressed students, "corresponding to a mean difference of 33.51" with d=1.075. Spearman correlations with BDI-II score were also positive.

> "Depressed students had higher night log counts than non-depressed students, with mean values of 59.73 versus 26.23, corresponding to a mean difference of 33.51 and a percentage difference of 127.75% (p=0.0028, d=1.075)."

Group 2 weekend-activity comparison in the temporal engagement section: depressed students "had higher weekend log counts" (190.13 vs 136.91, d=0.787) and a higher weekend log ratio (0.481 vs 0.395, d=0.766), with supporting Spearman correlations.

### Arya VarastehNezhad 2026 (3)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`
