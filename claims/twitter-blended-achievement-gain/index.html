Evidence¶

Amry 2018¶

Aicha Amry. (2018). The Effect of Twitter Activities in a Blended Learning Classroom Guided by Activity Theory on Students' Achievement and Attitudes. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1176184

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact groups of female students (36 analyzed per group per Table 2) in the course Computers in instruction during the 2016 academic year; the experimental group's blended process included Twitter learning activities. The article reports the experimental mean as greater, with t = 4.578, df = 70 in Table 3, exceeding the table value of 2.00.

"The results of comparison between the means of the experimental group and the control group show that the mean of the experimental group (3.79) is greater than the mean of the control group (2.58)"
