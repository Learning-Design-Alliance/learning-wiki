## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2010

Michael C. Wittmann. (2010). Using conceptual blending to describe emergent meaning in wave propagation. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1008.0216](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1008.0216)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview analysis (data from the author's 1998 dissertation) of students asked how to shorten a wavepulse's travel time on a long, taut spring. The author reports that explanations were "strongly connected to the hand motions they made", with gestures such as a harder hand jerk or quicker wrist flick accompanying answers.

> "Many students' explanations were strongly connected to the hand motions they made to indicate the creation of the original wavepulse. The “faster flick” or the “greater effort” or the “smaller wave” were all shown through gesture"
