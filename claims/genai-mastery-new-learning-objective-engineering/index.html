Tiina Leino Lindell 2026¶

Tiina Leino Lindell, Christian Stöhr. (2026). The AI disruption in engineering education: an analysis of changing student norms through cultural historical activity theory. Journal of Computing in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12528-025-09488-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic finding from the interview study at the technical university. One student likened GenAI to "just another skill, like being good at programming or math", expecting to use it for almost everything at work to get the job done efficiently.

"These findings suggest that the participating students' access to GenAI might transform the goals of learning within the engineering education context studied. These engineering students now view mastering GenAI as crucial as acquiring other engineering skills."

Tiina Leino Lindell 2026 (2)¶

Tiina Leino Lindell, Christian Stöhr. (2026). The AI disruption in engineering education: an analysis of changing student norms through cultural historical activity theory. Journal of Computing in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12528-025-09488-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Second finding within the objectives-of-learning theme, reporting "widespread uncertainty and concern" about competence expectations and over-reliance. An architecture student asked "How dependent will I become on AI?" and worried about being expected to design six buildings instead of one in the same time.

"We also identify widespread uncertainty and concern regarding changing expecta- tions on competences and about a potential over-reliance on AI."
