## Evidence

### Akpinar Havva 2025

Akpinar Havva, Ozcan Mircali Fatmanur, Bulut Ugurlu Nezihe. (2025). The Relationship Between Therapeutic Communication Skills and Spiritual Care Competencies of Nurses: The Example of Türkiye. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-025-02348-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

t-tests, one-way ANOVA and Tukey post hoc comparisons of TCSSN total scores across descriptive characteristics in the 215-nurse survey. Age and working-time ANOVAs were significant (F = 3.982, p = .020; F = 5.925, p = .003) with post hoc 3 > 1 contrasts; no effect sizes are printed.

> "nurses aged 38 years and older (78.00 ± 16.95) scored higher on the TCSSN than those aged 18–27 years (70.68 ± 12.97). Similarly, those with more than 11 years of working experience (78.37 ± 16.26) scored higher than those with 1–5 years of experience (71.44 ± 13.65)."

### Akpinar Havva 2025 (2)

Akpinar Havva, Ozcan Mircali Fatmanur, Bulut Ugurlu Nezihe. (2025). The Relationship Between Therapeutic Communication Skills and Spiritual Care Competencies of Nurses: The Example of Türkiye. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-025-02348-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests on TCSSN total scores in the same survey: work unit t = −3.476, p = .001; familiarity with communication concepts t = 3.045, p = .003; belief that spiritual care is necessary t = 3.005, p = .001. Only test statistics and means are printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Nurses working in medical or surgical units (78.02 ± 16.50), those familiar with therapeutic and non-therapeutic communication concepts (76.52 ± 16.57), and those who considered spiritual care essential (77.04 ± 15.66) also had higher TCSSN scores."
