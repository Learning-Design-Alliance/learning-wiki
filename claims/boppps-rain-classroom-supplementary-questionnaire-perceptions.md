### Wang Yujia 2026

Wang Yujia, Chen Zhimin, Huang Kaiqi, Zhang Xiaohong, Chen Caiming, Xu Yanfang. (2026). Comparison of the BOPPPS model combined with rain classroom teaching and traditional method on nephrology education for fifth-year undergraduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08714-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Investigator-developed supplementary questionnaire of eight items on five-point Likert scales, distributed to all 36 experimental-group students with a 100% response rate. Reported percentages are descriptive self-receptions, not tested comparisons.

> "the majority of students concurred that the BOPPPS model, when integrated with Rain Classroom, notably enhanced their overall learning experience in the nephrology course, with a significant majority strongly agreeing (83.33%) and a smaller group agreeing (13.89%), while only a few were neutral (2.78%)."
