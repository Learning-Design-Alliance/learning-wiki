`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retest of 83 eligible students (of 172 invited, 112 responded) approximately one month after first response, excluding students who reported changed learning or functioning. The article reports the "limits-of-agreement of -27.12 to 34.23"; five outliers showed high disagreement.

> "Using a Bland-Altman comparison of test and retest scores, the mean difference on GRAS-DK scores was found to be 3.55 (CI: [0.21; 6.90]) with limits-of-agreement of -27.12 to 34.23"
