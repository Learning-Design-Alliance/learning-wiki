Liu Y 2026¶

Liu Y, Meng M and Zhang Y (2026) The competence paradox: negotiating ease, risk, and creative identity in text-to-image generative AI use among art and design students. Front. Psychol. 17:1858187. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1858187

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Structural model (latent-variable SEM) of survey responses from 400 students with prior/current T2I experience, estimated by maximum likelihood. The direct-effects table shows PE→BI β = 0.292, p = 0.014; SI→BI β = 0.265, p = 0.012; NV→BI β = 0.279, p = 0.024; CC→BI β = 0.526, p = 0.044, so that "PE, SI, NV, and CC positively predicted BI".
