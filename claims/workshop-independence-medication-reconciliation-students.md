## Evidence

### Ramjaun Aliya 2015

Ramjaun Aliya, Sudarshan Monisha, Patakfalvi Laura, Tamblyn Robyn, Meguerditchian Ari N. (2015). Educating medical trainees on medication reconciliation: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4373246/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Pre- and post-workshop survey (n=78) of third-year students attending a 3-hour workshop with five interactive small-group case-based sessions on safe healthcare transitions; "a mean score of 2.7 out of 4" rose to 3.2 (p < 0.01) for independence in medication reconciliation.

> "Where students previously reported, according to a 4-point scale, a mean score of 2.7 out of 4, after the workshop the mean score was 3.2 (p < 0.01) [28]"
