  • Behavioral attitude, subjective norm, perceived behavioral control, and behavioral intention are each negatively associated with GenAI-assisted learning procrastination — related
  • Teachers' attitudes toward GenAI tools significantly predict their behavioural intention to integrate them — related
  • Ghanaian pre-service science teachers show positive attitudes, perceived behavioural control, and behavioural intentions toward AI — related
  • Performance expectancy, social influence, novelty value, and creative competence positively predict art and design students' behavioral intention to use T2I GenAI tools — related
  • Learning GenAI anxiety moderates the intention–procrastination association, which is negative and significant at high anxiety but absent at low anxiety — related
  • TPB constructs indirectly link GenAI use to lower GenAI-assisted learning procrastination, accounting for about 30.9% of the total association — related
