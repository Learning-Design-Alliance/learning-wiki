### Rudd 2017

Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.isrctn.com/search?q=&filters=intervention%3AMathsFlip%2CfunderName%3AEducation+Endowment+Foundation&searchType=advanced-search

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity assessment via training attendance (16 teachers from 11 treatment schools at two-day sessions) and hour-long lesson observations in four randomly chosen treatment schools in March–April 2015, using an observation sheet based on Bloom's taxonomy cognitive dimensions to detect Higher Order Thinking Skills. The report notes limited fidelity data means it cannot be sure implementation failure was not responsible for the lack of impact.

> "In summary, the data from the su rveys, focus groups, and observati ons suggests that MathsFlip was delivered well and behaviour in the class reflected the key components of a flipped learning approach."
