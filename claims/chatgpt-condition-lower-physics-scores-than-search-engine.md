### Lars Krupp 2024

Unreflected Acceptance - Investigating the Negative Consequences of ChatGPT-Assisted Problem Solving in Physics Education. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.3233/faia240195](https://doi.org/10.3233/faia240195)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Rank-aligned one-way ANOVA comparing main-test scores between the ChatGPT condition (N=27, mean 1.04/12) and the search engine condition (N=12, mean 1.83/12) in a between-subject study with STEM students. The article reports "(F (1, 37)=5.5, p=.02, η2=.13)", a medium effect size by the article's own thresholds.
