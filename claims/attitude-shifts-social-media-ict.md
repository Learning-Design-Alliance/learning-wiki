# Students' attitudes toward learning via ICT shifted after the activities in the first study and toward learning via social media in the second, with no shifts in the third study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the first study, attitude toward learning via ICT improved post-test (mean 3.60 to 3.89, p=0.045); in the second study, attitude toward learning via social media improved (mean 3.28 to 3.50, p=0.006). [→ Alexia Katrimpouza 2017](#alexia-katrimpouza-2017)
`q2 i?` No attitude shifts were significant for self-reported attraction towards technology in any study, nor in the third study on any variable. [→ Alexia Katrimpouza 2017 (2)](#alexia-katrimpouza-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Alexia Katrimpouza 2017

Alexia Katrimpouza, Nikolaos Tselios and Maria-Christina Kasimati. (2017). Twitter adoption, students' perceptions, Big Five personality traits and learning outcome: Lessons learned from 3 case studies. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t-tests on pre-post questionnaire data (first study N=10) showed the learning-via-ICT attitude rose from 3.60 to 3.89 (p=0.045); in the second study (N=73) the learning-via-social-media attitude rose from 3.28 to 3.50 (p=0.006). No effect sizes are printed, so impact is unquantified.

### Alexia Katrimpouza 2017 (2)

Alexia Katrimpouza, Nikolaos Tselios and Maria-Christina Kasimati. (2017). Twitter adoption, students' perceptions, Big Five personality traits and learning outcome: Lessons learned from 3 case studies. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the third study (N=46), paired comparisons of learning via ICT, learning via social media, and self-reported technology attraction showed no statistically significant pre-post differences (Table 4 p values .387-.995); the first study's social media and attraction comparisons were likewise non-significant.

> "In the third study, no statistically significant differences were observed."
