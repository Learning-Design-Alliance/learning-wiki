Evidence¶

Bach 2025¶

Bach, K. M., Reinhold, F., & Hofer, S. I. (2025). Unlocking math potential in students from lower SES backgrounds – using instructional scaffolds to improve performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00358-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Latent profile analysis of N = 321 sixth-graders compared two- to six-profile solutions on AIC, BIC, aBIC and entropy. The authors chose the three-profile model, noting "the value was highest for the three-pro ﬁle model" and using parsimony and interpretability; no effect size is printed for the classification itself.

"Entropy was above the required value of 0.8 for all pro ﬁle solutions; the value was highest for the three-pro ﬁle model."
