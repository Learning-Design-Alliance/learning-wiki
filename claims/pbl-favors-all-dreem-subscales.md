## Evidence

### Qin Yongjie 2016

Qin Yongjie, Wang Yungui, Floden Robert E. (2016). The Effect of Problem-Based Learning on Improvement of the Medical Educational Environment: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Medical Principles and Practice. https://doi.org/10.1159/000449036

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of subscale scores reported in all six included studies (335/338 per subscale). The quote lists three of five subscales; the article also reports atmosphere (WMD 1.76) and social self-perceptions (WMD 1.19), all favoring PBL. The authors note prior individual studies were inconsistent on four subscales, whereas "our meta-analysis found that the PBL method was superior to the LBL method in all subscales of DREEM".

> "The pooled effect sizes for students' perceptions of learning (WMD 2.84, 95% CI 2.23–3.45, p < 0.0001), students' perceptions of teachers (WMD 1.77, 95% CI 0.92–2.61, p < 0.0001), students' academic self-perceptions (WMD 1.06, 95% CI 0.61–1.50, p < 0.00001)"
