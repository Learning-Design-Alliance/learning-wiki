## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Residents appreciated active engagement but emphasized creating a safe environment, disliking being called on or pimped; faculty described difficulty getting residents to respond during interactive lectures. [→ Sawatsky Adam P. 2015](#sawatsky-adam-p-2015)

## Evidence

### Sawatsky Adam P. 2015

Sawatsky Adam P., Zickmund Susan L., Berlacher Kathryn, Lesky Dan, Granieri Rosanne. (2015). Understanding the challenges to facilitating active learning in the resident conferences: a qualitative study of internal medicine faculty and resident perspectives. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.27289

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Faculty focus group theme on engagement. Both groups acknowledged active participation improves investment and learning, but a faculty member noted "you don't get any response from the residents". The authors state this perceived difficulty contradicts residents' stated preference for active participation.

> "You can make the lecture interactive, but then what happens a lot of times you don't get any response from the residents"

### Sawatsky Adam P. 2015 (2)

Sawatsky Adam P., Zickmund Susan L., Berlacher Kathryn, Lesky Dan, Granieri Rosanne. (2015). Understanding the challenges to facilitating active learning in the resident conferences: a qualitative study of internal medicine faculty and resident perspectives. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.27289

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Resident focus group theme (from the previously collected seven resident groups) on engagement. Residents disliked being called on or pimped, with one saying "picking on someone is the way to go about it" is not effective, and instead listed safe-environment strategies.

> "Residents discussed effective strategies to create a safe environment for participation, including setting expectations ahead of time, asking questions in a nonthreatening way, not putting residents on the spot to answer questions, and creating a culture where large group discussion is expected."
