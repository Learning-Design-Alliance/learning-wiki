Related Claims¶

  • Chronic cardio exercise programs improve executive functions, attention, and behavior in children with ADHD
  • Acute cardio exercise improves executive functions, response time, and academic performance in children with ADHD — related
  • Aerobic exercise improves executive functioning, most notably inhibitory control, in typically developing children, though evidence is tentative and one week-long study found no main effect — related
  • In adults with ADHD, acute cardio exercise does not improve sustained attention but enhances mood and motivation — related
  • Small pilot studies provide preliminary evidence that exercise interventions improve behavioral symptoms and neuropsychological functioning in children with ADHD — related
  • Acute exercise produces an overall small positive effect on cognition, with executive functions benefiting most and effects lasting up to two hours post-exercise — related
  • Daily moderate-to-vigorous physical activity is associated with better executive functioning in boys with ADHD — related
  • Reported benefits of physical activity programs on executive functions depend on program duration and the executive domain analyzed — related
  • In university students, walks or sitting in natural settings after cognitive fatigue improve some attention and working memory measures, with null results for others — related
