# Using recycle materials for science practicum has a statistically significant effect on Open University students' basic science process skills

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2017 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Experimental-class students who did practicum with recycle materials scored significantly higher on a basic science process skills test than control-class students using the university practicum kit (Kruskal-Wallis significance 0.000 < 0.05). [→ Setiyo Prajoko 2017](#setiyo-prajoko-2017)

## Evidence

### Setiyo Prajoko 2017

Setiyo Prajoko, Mohamad Amin, Fatchur Rohman, Muhana Gipayana. (2017). The Usage of Recycle Materials for Science Practicum: Is There Any Effect on Science Process Skills? International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.6, No.1. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized posttest-only control group experiment with 83 Open University Surakarta students divided into experimental and control classes; hypothesis testing used the Kruskal-Wallis nonparametric test in SPSS 18 after normality failed. The article reports "Kruskal Wallis obtained significance of 0.000 <0.05". No effect size is printed.

> "Based on Table 7, Kruskal Wallis obtained significance of 0.000 <0.05. The results showed that the working hypothesis was accepted, while the null hypothesis was rejected."
