`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Backwards stepwise multivariable logistic regression on UKMED data (N = 770 with conduct-related declarations) from students starting medical school in 2007 and 2008; the article reports "male gender (OR 3.07)" as the sole demographic predictor retained. No effect size beyond the OR is printed.
