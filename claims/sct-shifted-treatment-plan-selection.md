## Evidence

### Jia Xiaoshi 2025

Jia Xiaoshi, Guo Jiawen, Wu Maodan, Wang Xiaodong, Fan Le, Yao Yichen, Deng Xiaoyi, Yang Ling. (2025). Study on the effect of simulation-based case teaching method on the preclinical teaching of tooth defects restoration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07010-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square comparison of Case 1 treatment plan answers before and after SCT in the same 96-student self-controlled study. Table 2 prints post and core crown 39.80% to 96.94% (p < 0.001), tooth bleaching 32.65% to 55.10% (p = 0.001), resin filling 78.57% to 57.14% (p = 0.001), full crown 83.67% to 68.37% (p = 0.009); veneer did not change significantly (p = 0.237).

> "The percentage of the students chose D and E significantly increased after SCT, while the percentage of the students chose A and C significantly decreased (Table 2)."

### Jia Xiaoshi 2025 (2)

Jia Xiaoshi, Guo Jiawen, Wu Maodan, Wang Xiaodong, Fan Le, Yao Yichen, Deng Xiaoyi, Yang Ling. (2025). Study on the effect of simulation-based case teaching method on the preclinical teaching of tooth defects restoration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07010-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive combination analysis of open-answer treatment plan responses: "no students thought tooth bleaching combined with post-core crown was feasible before SCT", rising to 21.43% after SCT; the number of answer combinations rose from 15 to 17.

> "no students thought tooth bleaching combined with post-core crown was feasible before SCT, while that percentage increased to 21.43% (ADE: 19.39% + DE: 2.04%) after SCT."
