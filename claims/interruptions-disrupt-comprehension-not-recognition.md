## Evidence

### Foroughi 2015

Foroughi, C. K., Werner, N. E., Barragán, D., & Boehm-Davis, D. A. (2015). Interruptions Disrupt Reading Comprehension. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/xge0000074

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2, a 2 x 2 repeated-measures ANOVA with 24 students comparing comprehension and recognition questions under interruption. Simple effects showed poorer comprehension when interrupted (d = 1.90) but no significant difference for recognition questions (p > .249).

> "a significant interaction, F(1,23) = 46.00, p < .001, η2∂ = .67 (see Figure 2). Tests of simple main effects using a Bonferroni correction (α = .05) within the comprehension questions revealed significantly poorer performance under the interruption condition"
