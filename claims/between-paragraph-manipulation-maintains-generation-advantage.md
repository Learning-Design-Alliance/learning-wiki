## Evidence

### deWinstanley 2004

deWinstanley, P. A., & Bjork, E. L. (2004). Processing strategies and the generation effect: Implications for making a better reader. Memory & Cognition, 32(6), 945-955. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03196872

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design experiment with 68 students in which encoding task switched between paragraphs, denying participants the experience of both item types on the same test. The ANOVA showed "a gener- ation advantage [F (1,66) /H110056.38" with no order effect [F(1,66) = 1.01, p < .30].

> "And indeed, this apparent result was confirmed by the outcome of a mixed analy- sis of variance (ANOV A), which revealed both a gener- ation advantage [F (1,66) /H110056.38, MSe /H11005520.03, p /H11021 .01], and that the advantage for generated items occurred in the absence of an order effect"
