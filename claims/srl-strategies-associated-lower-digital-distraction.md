# Self-regulated learning strategies (goal setting, environment structuring, time management) are the strategies most prominently associated with lower digital distraction among college students in online courses

## Evidence

### Shi 2025

Shi, H., Bi, R., Lin, X., & Dai, Y. (2025). Decreasing Digital Distraction in College Students: Associated Online Learning Strategies Identified by Unsupervised Data Mining Approaches. https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/P85S3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Association rule mining (ARM) on survey data from 530 students at a public university in southeastern America, using rules with lift < 0.95 and phi < 0. The Step 2 analysis of 42 two-antecedent rules found SRL strategies most prominent: "emerged as the most prominent factor associated with lower digital distraction scores".

> "Overall, SRL strategies (i.e., goal setting, environment structuring, and time management) emerged as the most prominent factor associated with lower digital distraction scores, followed by certain aspects of learner-content and learner-instructor engagement."
