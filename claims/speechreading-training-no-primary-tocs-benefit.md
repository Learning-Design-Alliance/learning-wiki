## Evidence

### Pimperton 2019

Pimperton, H., Kyle, F., Hulme, C., Harris, M., Beedie, I., Ralph-Lewis, A., Worster, E., Rees, R., Donlan, C., & MacSweeney, M. (2019). Computerized Speechreading Training for Deaf Children: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research. https://doi.org/10.1044/2019_JSLHR-H-19-0073

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Intention-to-treat ANCOVAs in the RCT compared the speechreading and maths groups on the ToCS while adjusting for baseline and three randomization stratifiers; the printed effect sizes were negligible (d = 0.04 at T2, d = 0.10 at T3, d = 0.14 at T4). The article reports "no significant differences" on the "prespecified primary outcome variable".

> "There were no significant differences between the intervention and control groups on the prespecified primary outcome variable, total ToCS standard score, at either T2 (d = 0.04) or T3 (d = 0.10) nor on the ToCS Word subtest at T4 (d = 0.14)."
