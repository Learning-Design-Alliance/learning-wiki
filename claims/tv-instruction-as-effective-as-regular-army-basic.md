## Evidence

### Kanner 1955

Kanner, J. H., Runyon, R. P., & Desiderato, O. (1955). Television in Army Training: Evaluation of Television in Army Basic Training (Technical Report No. 14). Human Resources Research Office, The George Washington University. Read through Twyford's 1955 research abstract, [doi:10.1007/bf02719927](https://doi.org/10.1007/bf02719927)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled comparison within the Army basic training evaluation: 14 hours of instruction were given by television and by regular instruction with the same instructors, with achievement tests administered immediately and after one month. The report states "television instruc- tion was at least as effective as regular instruction".

> "Experimental comparisons indicated that: (1) television instruc- tion was at least as effective as regular instruction; (2) television instruction was remembered at least as well as regular instruction;"
