Thematic and word-frequency analysis of questionnaire responses using an online text analyzer. The experimental group (n=24) mentioned Kahoot 17 times; the control group (n=23) named PowerPoint 11 times, notes 9, and study guide 8.

> "In response to the first question, “What helped yo u in class prepare for the exam,” participants in the control group (n=23) mentioned “PowerPoint” 11 times followed by “notes” (9) and “study guide” (8). Participants in the experimental group (n=24) mentioned “Kahoot™” 17 times"
