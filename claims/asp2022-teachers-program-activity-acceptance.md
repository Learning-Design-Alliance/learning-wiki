## Evidence

### Kenneth Cecire and Shane Wood 2023

Kenneth Cecire and Shane Wood. (2023). Report of the Notre Dame Contribution to the African School of Fundamental Physics and Applications 2022 in Gqeberha, South Africa. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.03095](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.03095)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Informal email survey sent January 30, 2023 to Teachers Program participants; only eight teachers responded. The authors attribute low response to the survey's lateness and report that the spreadsheet-based mass calculation was "the least favored activity according to 50% of the respondents".

> "Tracking this, the least favored activity according to 50% of the respondents was Higgs boson mass calculation."

### Kenneth Cecire and Shane Wood 2023 (2)

Kenneth Cecire and Shane Wood. (2023). Report of the Notre Dame Contribution to the African School of Fundamental Physics and Applications 2022 in Gqeberha, South Africa. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.03095](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.03095)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Participation count reported for the first-week Teachers Program (November 28 to December 2, 2022); 51 filled a supplementary registration form, of whom 44 reported being high school teachers.

> "A group of 76 South African teachers, most from the Eastern Cape province of South Africa, participated in the Teachers Program."
