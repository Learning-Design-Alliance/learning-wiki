## Evidence

### Keselman 1998

Keselman, H. J., Huberty, C. J., Lix, L. M., Olejnik, S., Cribbie, R. A., Donahue, B., Kowalchuk, R. K., Lowman, L. L., Petoskey, M. D., Keselman, J. C., & Levin, J. R. (1998). Statistical Practises of Educational Researchers: An Analysis of Their ANOVA, MANOVA and ANCOVA Analyses. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Statistical+Practises+of+Educational+Researchers

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Content analysis of 79 between-subjects multivariate articles. The review found that in "an overwhelming 84% (66/79) of the studies, researchers never used the results of the MANOVA(s) to explain effects of the grouping variable(s)," drawing substantive conclusions from univariate results instead.

> "In an overwhelming 84% (66/79) of the studies, researchers never used the results of the MANOVA(s) to explain effects of the grouping variable(s). Instead, they interpreted the results of multiple univariate analyses"
