Content analysis of teacher-reported reasons for participant dropout from online MBP courses (n = 71 responses). The article reports "The time factor was most frequently occurring (n = 29)", with sub-themes of general lack of time (n = 16), work commitments (n = 11), and family commitments (n = 2); change of circumstances was reported by 17 teachers.
