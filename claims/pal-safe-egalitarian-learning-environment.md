## Evidence

### Tamachi Shameena 2018

Tamachi Shameena, Giles James A., Dornan Tim, Hill Elspeth J. R. (2018). “You understand that whole big situation they’re in”: interpretative phenomenological analysis of peer-assisted learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1291-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Participant account from semi-structured interviews with three peer tutors and five students in the SRFT near-peer PAL scheme; this student linked comfort directly to learning, saying “you’re not going to be taking as much in”.

> "If you don’t have a safe learning environment, if you’re not comfortable, you’re not going to be taking as much in."

### Tamachi Shameena 2018 (2)

Tamachi Shameena, Giles James A., Dornan Tim, Hill Elspeth J. R. (2018). “You understand that whole big situation they’re in”: interpretative phenomenological analysis of peer-assisted learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1291-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the interview transcripts found egalitarianism central to the PAL environment: participants “felt legitimacy in their roles as session participants”, which the article links to participating more fully in sessions.

> "Egalitarianism was prominent in students’ experience of the environment; they felt legitimacy in their roles as session participants, which led to feeling free to participate more fully in sessions."
