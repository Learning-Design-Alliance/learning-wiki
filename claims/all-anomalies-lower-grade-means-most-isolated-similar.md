## Evidence

### Roustaei Z 2023

Roustaei Z, Heino A, Kiuru-Kuhlefelt S, Morris J K, Glinianaia S V, Garne E, Loane M, Rankin J, Gissler M. (2023). Educational achievement of children with selected major congenital anomalies and associated factors: a Finnish registry-based study. The European Journal of Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/ckad149

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Generalized linear regression of grade means for 12,122 children with anomalies versus 389,422 reference children, adjusted for birth year, sex, gestational age, maternal age and education; table 3 prints the adjusted all-subjects difference as −0.05 (−0.06 to −0.03). The article lists thirteen isolated anomalies not associated with lower grade means in the majority of subjects.

> "Nevertheless, for the 'All anomalies' subgroup, children with congenital anomalies had lower grade means for all school subjects in the adjusted analysis, except for native language, than the reference group (table 3)."
