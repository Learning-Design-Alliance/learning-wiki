`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

2016 survey preparedness items. "Around 75 percent of elementary teachers and 65 percent of high school teachers" in the ELA group felt very familiar and very prepared, up from about 60 percent of elementary teachers in 2015; reports of sufficient materials and PD also rose.

> "Around 75 percent of elementary teachers and 65 percent of high school teachers in the ELA response group reported feeling "very familiar" and "very prepared" to teach the ELA standards"

### Julia A. Gwynne and Jennifer R. Cowhy with R. Steven Quispe 2018 (2)

Julia A. Gwynne and Jennifer R. Cowhy with R. Steven Quispe. (2018). Getting Ready for the Common Core State Standards: A 2016 Update. UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/getting-ready-common-core-state-standards-experiences-cps-teachers-and-administrators

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the math response group, "nearly 70 percent of elementary teachers and around one-half of high school teachers" felt very familiar and very prepared; just over 60 percent of elementary but just under 40 percent of high school teachers very much agreed they had needed curricular materials.

> "Slightly lower proportions of teachers in the math sample—nearly 70 percent of elementary teachers and around one-half of high school teachers—reported feeling "very familiar" and "very prepared" to teach the new math standards"
