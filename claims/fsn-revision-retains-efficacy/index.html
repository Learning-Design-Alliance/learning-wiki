Evidence¶

Feil 2020¶

Feil, E. G., Walker, H. M., Frey, A. J., Seeley, J. R., Small, J. W., Golly, A., Lee, J., & Forness, S. R. (2020). Efficacy Validation of the Revised First Step Program: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Exceptional Children. https://journals.sagepub.com/home/ecx

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' interpretation comparing this RCT's effect sizes with prior First Step RCTs (e.g., Feil et al., 2014, where teacher-measure effect sizes ranged approximately 0.7 to 0.9 and parent measures 0.3 to 0.4). This is an interpretive claim, not a direct statistical comparison within the study.

"this study adds to its accumulated literature base by providing empirical support that the revised program, which unified the preschool and elementary versions and streamlined components to improve usability and satisfaction, retains its effectiveness in the preschool population"
