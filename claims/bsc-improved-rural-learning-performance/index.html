Yang 2019¶

Yang, J; Yu, H; Chen, NS. (2019). Using blended synchronous classroom approach to promote learning performance in rural area. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103619

q2 · i? · design · r?

Two-year study applying BSC in two primary schools in Ningbo China, using comparative analysis, questionnaire survey and classroom video analysis; the study reports "students' learning performance in rural school was improved significantly after the two-year practice of BSC". No effect size is printed in the available text.
