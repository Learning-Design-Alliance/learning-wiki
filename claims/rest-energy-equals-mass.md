Analytical result derived in section 5 from the mass formula at zero momentum: setting p = 0 gives e0 = m, or "E0 = mc 2" in units where c ≠ 1. The author describes this as Einstein's discovery that matter at rest stores energy in its mass.

> "The realization that ordinary matter at rest stores an enorm ous amount of energy in its mass was Einstein’s great discovery."
