`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same comparative study with historical controls, analyzed with the Mann-Whitney U test (Z = -3.299). The reported difference in objective-question scoring rates was statistically significant (P = 0.001).

> "students in the control group achieved a median scoring rate of 100.00 in the objective section of the dental hard tissue non-carious diseases test. Conversely, during the final examination of the first semester of the 2022–2023 academic year, students in the experimental group achieved a median scoring rate of 75.00"
