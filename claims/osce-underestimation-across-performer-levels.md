## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2014

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Myers Gil, Khatib Yasmin, Dacre Jane. (2014). How well do doctors think they perform on the General Medical Council's Tests of Competence pilot examinations? A cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2013-004131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Tertile analysis of estimated versus actual OSCE scores in the same 524-doctor cross-sectional study. The article reports both high and lower performers significantly underestimated OSCE performance (t (180)=−26.28 and t (172)=−16.20, both p<0.01), with the top percentile underestimating most.

> "Both high and lower performers significantly underestimated their OSCE performance; t (180)=−26.28, p<0.01 and t (172)=−16.20, p<0.01, respectively. Those in the top percentile underestimated their OSCE performance to the greatest extent."
