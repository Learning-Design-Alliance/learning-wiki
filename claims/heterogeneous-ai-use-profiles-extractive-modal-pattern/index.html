The chapter reports, citing Stojanov et al., a latent profile analysis of 490 university students' self-reported reliance on ChatGPT that "identified five distinct profiles" including versatile low reliers, assignment delegators, and all-rounders. The chapter notes the authors did not investigate process traces of interactions.
