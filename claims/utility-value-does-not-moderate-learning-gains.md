### Witti MJ 2026

Witti MJ, Coleman J-C, Richters C, Schönberger J, de Ruijter M-A, Stortz A, Bauer M, Kiriakidou R, Stadler M. (2026). Simulation-Based Radiology Training for Medical Technical Assistant Trainees: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/97489

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-design analysis of covariance with time as the within-subject factor and utility value as a continuous covariate. The time × utility value interaction was not significant, "indicating that perceived utility value did not moderate the degree of learning improvement."

> "However, the time × utility value interaction was not significant (F1,46=0.92; P=.34; η2=0.005), indicating that perceived utility value did not moderate the degree of learning improvement."
