## Evidence

### Seika Philippa 2025

Seika Philippa, Martin Friederike, Serwah Armanda, Maurer Max Magnus, Winter Axel, Ossami-Saidy Ramin Raul, Ritschl Paul V., Dobrindt Eva, Kurreck Annika, Raakow Jonas, Pratschke Johann, Biebl Matthias, Denecke Christian. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11539-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survival analysis of 97 patients (66 trainer, 31 trainee) with at least 2 years of follow-up; the article reports 1-year OS of 97.84% vs 98.80% and 3-year OS of 82.88% vs 82.71% (p = 0.436), and DFS p = 0.305, with no significant distinctions.

> "The 1-year overall survival rates stood at 97.84% for the Trainer group and 98.80% for the Trainee group, while the 3-year survival rates were 82.88% and 82.71%, respectively (p = 0.436)."
