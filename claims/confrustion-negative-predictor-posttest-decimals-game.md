## Evidence

### Mogessie et al. 2020

Mogessie, M., Richey, J. E., McLaren, B. M., Andres-Bray, J. M. L., & Baker, R. S. (2020). Confrustion and Gaming While Learning with Erroneous Examples in a Decimals Game. Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_38

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of gameplay and test data from 191 middle-school students using the Decimal Point game. Correlations show "significantly, negatively correlated with performance on the pretest (r = −.62, p < .001) and posttest (r = −.68, p < .001)"; the regression controlling for pretest was also significant (β = −.32, p < .001 for confrustion).
