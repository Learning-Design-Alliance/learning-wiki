## Evidence

### Wang Zi-Yue 2021

Wang Zi-Yue, Zhang Li-Jie, Liu Yu-Hong, Jiang Wei-Xi, Jia Jing-Yun, Tang Sheng-Lan, Liu Xiao-Yun. (2021). The effectiveness of E-learning in continuing medical education for tuberculosis health workers: a quasi-experiment from China. Infectious Diseases of Poverty. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40249-021-00855-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results across the regression models in the quasi-experiment for the public health physician sample (sample size 168/169); the article reports no statistically significant improvement "no matter what format of training they have taken". Qualitative interviews attributed this to content irrelevance to daily work.

> "We didn't find any statistically significant improvement for the public health physicians in terms of their TB knowledge level, no matter what format of training they have taken"
