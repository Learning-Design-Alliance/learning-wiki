## Evidence

### Gero 2014

Gero, A., Zoabi, W., & Sabag, N. (2014). Animation Based Learning of Electronic Devices. Advances in Engineering Education, 4(1), Winter 2014. https://journals.gmu.edu/aaa/issue/winter-2014

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent t tests on the same randomized study's final achievement test, comparing scores on problems classified by two experts as requiring strategy and interpretation skills versus routine and diagnosis skills. The animation group's posttest mean of 82.23 on higher-order problems was significantly higher than the control group's 68.65, with p < 0.0005 and an effect size of 1.24, versus 0.74 on lower-order problems.

> "The effect size relating to higher-order thinking skills (1.24) was considerably higher than the effect size relating to lower-order thinking skills (0.74)."
