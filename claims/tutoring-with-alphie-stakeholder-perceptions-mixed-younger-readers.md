## Evidence

### Centre for Effective Education 2015

Centre for Effective Education, Queen's University Belfast. (2015). Tutoring with Alphie: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581542

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Process evaluation finding from tutor surveys and interviews: perceptions were mixed overall, with negative comments centred on technical issues, but persistent schools developed "a more positive experience of Tutoring with Alphie" over time.

> "Many of the difficulties with the software were  resolved over time and a number of schools who persisted with the programme perceived it as beneficial to the children’s reading."

### Centre for Effective Education 2015 (2)

Centre for Effective Education, Queen's University Belfast. (2015). Tutoring with Alphie: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581542

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pupil survey returns from 11 of 21 schools; pupils also reported frustration with screen freezes and a missing 'done' button, and felt the graphics were too 'babyish' for their age group.

> "A number of pupils highlighted how they enjoyed spending time on the computers and working collaboratively in pairs. Others suggested that they felt it had helped them with their reading and spelling and had boosted their confidence in reading."
