## Evidence

### Carneiro Vera Lúcia Alves 2020

Carneiro Vera Lúcia Alves, Jorge Jorge (2020). Competencies and training needs of the Portuguese optometrists - a national inquiry. Journal of Optometry. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optom.2019.12.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of procedure frequency across the four printed intervals of professional practice years in the national survey (n = 444). Comparing up to 4 years with 14 or more years, "statistically significant differences in the order of ρ < 0.001 were observed for each of the procedures".

> "statistically significant differences in the order of ρ < 0.001 were observed for each of the procedures, which indicates that individuals with more years of professional practice perform Basic Binocular Vision procedures more frequently"
