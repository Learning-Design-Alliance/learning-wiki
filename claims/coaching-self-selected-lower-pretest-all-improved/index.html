Evidence¶

Richman 2014¶

Richman, E. L., Rademacher, K. N., & Maitland, T. L. (2014). Coaching and college success. Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability, 27(1), 33-52. https://www.ahead.org/publications/jped

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive pre/post comparison from the quasi-experimental coaching study (16 treatment, 8 comparison students). The article reports these patterns descriptively; the t-tests were not significant and no effect size is printed.

"First, every student who self-selected to be in the intervention group began the study with lower pretest scores than comparison group students in all three measures. Second, the posttests revealed that all intervention group students improved in every post-test measure and except in one case, to a greater extent than the comparison group."
