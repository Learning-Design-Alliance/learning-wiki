### Szumski Grzegorz 2022

Szumski Grzegorz, Smogorzewska Joanna, Grygiel Paweł. (2022). Academic achievement of students without special educational needs and disabilities in inclusive education–Does the type of inclusion matter? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0270124

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal Polish study of students without disabilities in 108 classrooms, analysed with latent growth curve models over three waves. The comparison of groups' changes found "the growth of results in mathematics did not differ between groups"; the printed general-versus-inclusive contrast was Est = 0.137, p = .225.

> "the growth of results in mathematics did not differ between groups; general education versus inclusive non-co-teach classrooms: Est = 0.137, SE = 0.113, p = .225"
