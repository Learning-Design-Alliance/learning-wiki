## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Variability decreased from pretest to posttest for both total strategies and gesture-speech mismatches, but the test × condition interaction did not reach significance for either measure. [→ Alibali 1999](#alibali-1999)

## Evidence

### Alibali 1999

Alibali, M. W. (1999). How children change their minds: Strategy change can be gradual or abrupt. Developmental Psychology, 35(1), 127-145. https://doi.org/10.1037/0012-1649.35.1.127

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA with 143 children across five instruction groups. Main effect of test was significant for both variability measures; interaction with instruction group was not significant.

> "Overall, variability decreased from the explanation pretest to the explanation posttest, F(l, 138) = 4.60, p < .05, for total number of strategies, and F(l, 138) = 4.51, p < .05, for gesture-speech mismatches."
