## Evidence

### Eugene T. Torigoe 2015

Eugene T. Torigoe. (2015). Emphasizing Expert Practice with Spaced Recall. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.082](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.082)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative finding from student work and interview remarks across the recall sessions, including on alternate problems. The article reports students "strategically memorize portions of the solution, but then used the diagrams work through the solutions." No effect size printed.

> "It was clear from their work as well as their remarks that neither memorized the entire solution. Both were able to strategically memorize portions of the solution, but then used the diagrams work through the solutions."
