# FastPerson video summarization halves viewing time with no significant quiz-score loss versus normal playback

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In experiments with 40 participants watching educational videos, FastPerson achieved an average 53% reduction in viewing time with no statistically significant difference in quiz scores compared with normal playback. [→ Kazuki Kawamura 2025](#kazuki-kawamura-2025)
`q2 i?` 78% of FastPerson participants rated the chapter-by-chapter summary/full-video switching functionality as useful or very useful. [→ Kazuki Kawamura 2025 (2)](#kazuki-kawamura-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Kazuki Kawamura 2025

Kazuki Kawamura. (2025). AI-Guided Learning: Research on Knowledge and Skill Acquisition Support Methods Using Deep Learning Audio-Video Processing Techniques. Doctoral dissertation, The University of Tokyo. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08990

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Lecture-video viewing experiment with 40 participants comparing FastPerson summarized viewing with normal playback. Viewing time fell 53% on average; quiz-measured comprehension showed "no statistically significant difference" from normal playback; equivalence was not tested. No effect size printed.

> "FastPerson achieved an average 53% reduction in viewing time, with no statistically significant difference in quiz scores compared with normal playback; 78% of participants rated the switching functionality as useful or very useful."

### Kazuki Kawamura 2025 (2)

Kazuki Kawamura. (2025). AI-Guided Learning: Research on Knowledge and Skill Acquisition Support Methods Using Deep Learning Audio-Video Processing Techniques. Doctoral dissertation, The University of Tokyo. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08990

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

User-experience ratings from the same 40-participant experiment: a large majority rated the chapter-switching interface positively. This is a descriptive percentage, not an effect size.

> "78% of participants rated the switching functionality as useful or very useful."
