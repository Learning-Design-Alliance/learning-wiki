## Evidence

### Markus Sebastian Feser and Dietmar Höttecke 2023

Markus Sebastian Feser and Dietmar Höttecke. (2023). Development of a test in German language to assess middle school students’ physics proficiency. Preprint, The Physics Educator. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1142/s2661339523200020](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339523200020)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Rasch analysis (R-package eRm) of test responses from N = 177 eighth grade students from Hamburg, Germany, collected December 2018 to January 2019 at two middle schools. The authors report "a coeﬃcient of 0.78" for person separation and 0.97 for item separation; no effect size is printed for these reliability coefficients.

> "The person-separation reliability reached a coeﬃcient of 0.78 and is thus suﬃcient.27 For the item-separation reliability, a coeﬃcient of 0.97 was obtained."
