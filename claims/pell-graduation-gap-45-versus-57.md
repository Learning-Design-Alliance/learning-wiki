Early results from the Access to Success Initiative, in which 20 systems of higher education comprising about 300 institutions annually submit graduation rates disaggregated by income status to The Education Trust. The report cites the baseline-year figures of "45 percent, compared with 57 percent" as a glimpse of what national income-disaggregated reporting may show.

> "In the baseline year of the initiative, a substantial gap separated low-income students from their higher income peers: The average six-year graduation rate for Pell Grant recipients was 45 percent, compared with 57 percent for those from higher income families."
