## Evidence

### James Kim 2016

James Kim, Lowry Hemphill, Margaret Troyer, Stephanie Jones, Maria LaRusso, Ha-Yeon Kim, Suzanne Donovan, Catherine Snow. (2016). The Experimental Effects of the Strategic Adolescent Reading Intervention (STARI) on a Scenarios-Based Reading Comprehension Assessment. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567033

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat OLS regression with fall GISA pretest, grade, and school fixed effects in a lottery design with 562 students in the core analytic sample. The article reports "d = .11" and p = .057, which did not reach conventional significance thresholds.

> "Overall, the intention-to-treat analysis indicates that STARI students outperformed control students by 5.69 scaled score points (t = 1.91, p = .057).  Expressed in standard deviation units, the effect size was d = .11."
