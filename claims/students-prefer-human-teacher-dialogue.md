After the dialogue session, both groups completed a 5-point Likert-scale form assessing how well the teacher/ChatGPT addressed questions and their satisfaction with the dialogue. Ratings in Tables III and IV show higher evaluations for the teacher group on both indicators.

> "Teacher demonstrates a higher level of proficiency in solving students' queries, and students prefer learning through dialogue with teachers."
