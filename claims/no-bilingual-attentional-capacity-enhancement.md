# The study found no evidence for enhanced attentional capacity in bilingual children, behaviourally or neurally

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Instead of overall enhancement, bilinguals showed a trend toward weaker neural encoding overall and significantly weaker reconstruction in low- or no-interference conditions. [→ Jacqueline Phelps 2022](#jacqueline-phelps-2022)

## Evidence

### Jacqueline Phelps 2022

Jacqueline Phelps, Adam Attaheri & Mirjana Bozic. (2022). How bilingualism modulates selective attention in children. Scientific Reports 12:6381. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-09989-x

`q3 · i?`

The authors' summary of the EEG and behavioural findings in the Discussion: overall attended reconstruction was r = 0.054 for bilinguals versus r = 0.066 for monolinguals, described as a trend, with no behavioural advantage either.

> "Our results showed no evidence for the enhanced attentional capacity, behaviourally or neurally, in the bilingual group. In contrast there was a trend for weaker neural encoding in bilinguals overall (rattd = 0.054 for bilinguals vs rattd = 0.066 for monolinguals)"
