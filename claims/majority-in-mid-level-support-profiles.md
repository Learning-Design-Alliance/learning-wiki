## Evidence

### Hernández 2024

Hernández, M. M., Kornienko, O., Figueroa, J. M., Coker, M., Paredes, K., Toth, C., Carrillo, J. P., Rogers, A. A., & Ha, T. (2024). Identifying Culturally Relevant School Support Profiles and Links to Academic Functioning in Adolescents. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02098-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Distributional finding from the four-profile LPA of 717 students. "The majority of adolescents (83%)" were in the moderate general/devoid critical and high general/moderate cultural & critical profiles, indicating few students experienced very low or very high support.

> "The majority of adolescents (83%) were in the moderate general, moderate cultural, & devoid critical (#2) and high general, moderate cultural & critical (#3) support profiles, whereas fewer were in the low general, devoid cultural & critical (#1) and high general, cultural, & critical (#4) support profiles, suggesting that few adolescents in the study's sample experienced very low or high levels of support in school."
