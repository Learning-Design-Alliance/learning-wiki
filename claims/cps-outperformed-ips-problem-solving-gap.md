`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The editorial reports, citing Guo, She, Chen and Tsai, a study contrasting online electromagnetism problem-solving programs with simulations, CPS versus IPS, for 8th grade students over five class sessions; the CPS group "significantly outperformed" the IPS group.

> "The results show that students in the CPS group significantly outperformed those in the IPS group on their performance of physics problem solving and online problem-solving."
