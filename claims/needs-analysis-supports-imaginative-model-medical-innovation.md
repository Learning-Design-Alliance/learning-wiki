Needs-analysis phase combining interviews with six experienced innovation educators and a survey of 150 students from three Chinese medical institutes analyzed in SPSS. The article reports that "6 experts' interviews" confirmed the need, and the discussion adds that "92% of expert participants acknowledged the necessity of integrating imaginative, student-centered approaches."
