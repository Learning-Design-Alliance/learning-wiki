Analysis of tutorial drop-off for 1190 players registered between July 2013 and March 2014 (Figure 4). Drop-off mainly happens in the first six levels (M=98%); the 7th level, the first including zones of death, shows M=81% completion, "most likely proving too diffic ult or disrupting competence motivation".
