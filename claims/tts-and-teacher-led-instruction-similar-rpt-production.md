# TTS-based instruction and teacher-led instruction produce similar RPT production outcomes in EFL learners

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no effect for Time or Group on production of regular past tense allomorphs: TTS and Non-TTS groups remained statistically similar from pretest to posttest. [→ Cardoso 2018](#cardoso-2018)

## Evidence

### Cardoso 2018

Cardoso, W. (2018). Learning L2 pronunciation with a text-to-speech synthesizer. In P. Taalas, J. Jalkanen, L. Bradley & S. Thouësny (Eds), Future-proof CALL: language learning as exploration and encounters – short papers from EUROCALL 2018. Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2018.26.806

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed between-within subjects ANOVA with pairwise comparisons in a pretest-posttest study of 18 intermediate EFL students stratified into a TTS group and a Non-TTS group over four weeks. The article reports "no effect for Time or Group" on RPT allomorphy production.

> "For both Word Reading and Guided Task, there was no effect for Time or Group, indicating no significant improvement: the RPT allomorphs remained the same from pretest to posttest, across the two groups and tasks."
