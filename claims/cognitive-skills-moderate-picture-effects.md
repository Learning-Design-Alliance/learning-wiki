# Cognitive skills are the most probable student characteristic to modify picture effects: low math performers do better without representational pictures, low reading-comprehension students better with them

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students low in general mathematics performed better on text-only versions than with representational pictures, while students low in reading comprehension and visuospatial executive functioning performed better with representational pictures. [→ Terhi Vessonen 2026](#terhi-vessonen-2026)
`q2 i?` Arithmetic skills did not relate to the association between representational pictures and performance. [→ Terhi Vessonen 2026 (2)](#terhi-vessonen-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Terhi Vessonen 2026

Terhi Vessonen, Heidi Hellstrand, Pirjo Aunio, Anu Laine. (2026). Pictures in Mathematical Word Problems: A Systematic Review on the Role of Task and Student Characteristics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10112-5

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review synthesis of eight studies measuring student characteristics. The review reports this low-mathematics finding and, separately, that "low-performing students in reading comprehension and visuospatial executive functioning showed better performance on the tasks with representational pictures".

> "Booth and Koedinger (2012) found that students who were low-performing in general mathematics performed better on text-only versions of the word problems when these were contrasted to representational pictures."

### Terhi Vessonen 2026 (2)

Terhi Vessonen, Heidi Hellstrand, Pirjo Aunio, Anu Laine. (2026). Pictures in Mathematical Word Problems: A Systematic Review on the Role of Task and Student Characteristics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10112-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis reporting that representational pictures "had similar effects for students with high and low arithmetic skills" across two studies. No effect sizes are printed for this moderator finding.

> "arithmetic skills were not shown to relate to the association between of representational pictures and students' performance (Berens & Van Lieshout, 2009; Van Lieshout & Xenidou-Dervou, 2018)."
