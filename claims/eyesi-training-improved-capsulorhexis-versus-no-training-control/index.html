Lee Roxanne 2020¶

Lee Roxanne, Raison Nicholas, Lau Wai Yan, Aydin Abdullatif, Dasgupta Prokar, Ahmed Kamran, Haldar Shreya. (2020). A systematic review of simulation-based training tools for technical and non-technical skills in ophthalmology. Eye. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41433-020-0832-1

The review reports, citing Feudner et al., an RCT (n = 63: 32 residents, 31 medical students, per Table 2) in which "those who trained with the Eyesi achieved significant improvements in their capsulorhexis performance in the wet lab" versus no-training controls. No effect size is printed.
