### Jacobs Fabian 2014

Jacobs Fabian, Stegmann Karsten, Siebeck Matthias. (2014). Promoting medical competencies through international exchange programs: benefits on communication and effective doctor-patient relationships. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-43

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental online survey comparing 29 exchange participants with 23 non-selected applicants on Tuning Level 1 outcomes. The doctor-patient relationship outcome showed "t(47) = 3..96, p < .001, d = 1.09"; Table 2 gives means of 4.46 (participants) vs 3.97 (controls).

> "The t-tests revealed significant effects of participation in the exchange program in the area of doctor-patient relationships, t(47) = 3..96, p < .001, d = 1.09, two-tailed"
