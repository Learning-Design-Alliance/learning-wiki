## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mann-Whitney U tests showed median satisfaction with live classes, recorded lectures and lecture materials was significantly higher among students whose performance did not change or improved versus those whose performance worsened (p < 0.05), though the Same–Improved contrast was not significant for recorded lectures and lecture materials. [→ Muhidin Mohamed 2025](#muhidin-mohamed-2025)
`q2 i?` For tutor feedback on assignments and exams, only the Worsened–Improved median difference was significant; agreement on staff responses to student questions differed significantly across performance groups. [→ Muhidin Mohamed 2025 (2)](#muhidin-mohamed-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Muhidin Mohamed 2025

Muhidin Mohamed, Shubhadeep Mukherjee and Bhavana Baad. (2025). Impact of UK Postgraduate Student Experiences on Academic Performance in Blended Learning: A Data Analytics Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U tests on median satisfaction ratings (Tables 8–10) across academic performance groups; all contrasts significant (p < 0.05) except Same–Improved for pre-recorded lectures (p = 8.646×10 −2) and lecture materials (p = 0.438). Students were least satisfied with lecture materials.

> "the median satisfaction rating of the students whose academic performancedid not change orimprovedis statistically significantly different (i.e, higher) from those withworsenedperformance."
