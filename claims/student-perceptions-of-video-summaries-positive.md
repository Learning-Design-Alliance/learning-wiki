## Evidence

### Fanguy 2017

Fanguy, M., Costley, J., & Baldwin, M. (2017). Pinch Hitter: The Effectiveness of Content Summaries Delivered by a Guest Lecturer in Online Course Videos. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 18(7). http://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/3210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc email survey of 19 respondents after grades were allocated; students described summaries as complementing note-taking, clarifying content for weaker students, and providing a rest point. Qualitative self-report, no effect size.

> "Students from the two treatment groups generally perceived the summaries positively, noting they were helpful for reviewing and emphasizing the salient points of the lecture."
