## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants reported that medical school social events, often associated with alcohol, were settings in which they did not feel comfortable, reducing socialisation with peers and adding to isolation. [→ Morrison Nariell 2019](#morrison-nariell-2019)

## Evidence

### Morrison Nariell 2019

Morrison Nariell, Machado Michelle, Blackburn Clare. (2019). Student perspectives on barriers to performance for black and minority ethnic graduate-entry medical students: a qualitative study in a West Midlands medical school. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032493

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group narrative on cultural differences; the participant wished more events were more inclusive, and another described feeling isolated when unable to join peer activities fully.

> "I often find that the social events are often held in settings that I don't feel comfortable in because I don't drink alcohol and the majority of the medical school culture is often associated with alcohol."
