# Anxiety negatively predicts subsequent mathematics test performance in primary school students when emotions are modeled simultaneously

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` T1 anxiety negatively predicted T2 mathematics test performance, and T3 anxiety negatively predicted T4 performance, after controlling for covariates and autoregressive effects. [→ Putwain 2021](#putwain-2021)

## Evidence

### Putwain 2021

Putwain, D. W., Wood, P., & Pekrun, R. (2021). Achievement emotions and academic achievement: Reciprocal relations and the moderating influence of academic buoyancy. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000637

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation modeling of four-wave longitudinal data from 1,242 primary school students showed that "T1 anxiety negatively predicted T2 mathematics test performance" while enjoyment and boredom did not significantly relate to subsequent performance.

> "T1 anxiety negatively predicted T2 mathematics test performance. T1 enjoyment and T1 boredom were not significantly related to T2 performance."
