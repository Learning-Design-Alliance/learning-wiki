Fargol Seifollahi, Christian D. Schunn, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Questioning Your Brilliance in Physics: Differential Shifts in Fixed Mindsets by Grade and Gender. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of mindset-category percentages by gender in the N = 508 sample, with Chi-Square tests reported in Figure 2. The article found "almost twice as many women represented in that group" (Hesitant) and men in the Confident group approximately one-third more often than women; only test statistics, no standardized effect sizes, are printed.

> "Here, there were large differences by gender in representation in the Hesitant group, with almost twice as many women represented in that group at the beginning of both Physics 1 and 2."
