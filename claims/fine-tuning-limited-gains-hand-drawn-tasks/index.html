Related Claims¶

  • Fine-tuning primarily improves alignment between code generation and visual reasoning rather than visual understanding or spatial reasoning — related
  • Fine-tuning VLMs on synthetic data from TURTLEAI-Datagen improves performance on TURTLEAI, raising symbolic success rates by over 10% over base counterparts — related
  • Current vision-language models struggle on education-oriented Turtle Graphics tasks, with even top models far below reliable performance on real-world tasks for grades 3-6 — related
  • The v2 synthetic generator was tuned for stricter trace-data realism with more incorrect code, retries, and longer dialogues — related
  • All evaluated models struggle with spatial reasoning and precise visual replication on Turtle Graphics tasks — related
  • Fine-tuning is associated with more compact generated code, with the fine-tuned Qwen2-VL-72B maintaining a length ratio near 1.0 while the base model's failed outputs become substantially longer — related
