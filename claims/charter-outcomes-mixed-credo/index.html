Evidence¶

Darling-Hammond 2017¶

Darling-Hammond, L., Rothman, R. & Cookson, P. W., Jr. (2017). Expanding high-quality educational options for all students: How states can create a system of schools worth choosing. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/expanding-high-quality-options

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report describes the CREDO 16-state comparative study of charter versus traditional public school achievement; "the largest share showed no difference." The report also notes newer CREDO feeder-school comparisons find somewhat more positive, though still mixed, results.

"A large-scale study of student data from 16 states, from the Center for Research on Education Outcomes (CREDO) at Stanford University, found that 17% of charter schools produced greater academic gains than traditional public schools, while 37% performed worse than their traditional public school counterparts serving similar students."
