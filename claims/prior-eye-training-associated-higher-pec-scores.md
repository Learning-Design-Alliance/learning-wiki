## Evidence

### Burn H 2020

Burn H, Puri L, Roshan A, Singh SK, Burton MJ. (2020). Primary Eye Care in Eastern Nepal. Ophthalmic Epidemiology 27(3):165-176. https://doi.org/10.1080/09286586.2019.1702217

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional analysis of total skills scores by explanatory factors (Table 4): mean total 9.22 (SD 2.41) with previous eye training vs 7.32 (SD 2.49) without, p=0.03 by independent t-test. Table 6 shows non-significant odds ratios for correct diagnosis (e.g. cataract OR 4.38, 95% CI 0.65–∞, p=0.14).

> "Having received eye care training during their professional career was associated with achieving a slightly higher PEC knowledge and skill score. This finding highlights an important gap in eye care training within this region"
