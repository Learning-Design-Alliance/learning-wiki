### Zhang Juan 2024

Zhang Juan, Chen Hong, Wang Xie, Huang Xiaofeng, Xie Daojun. (2024). Application of flipped classroom teaching method based on ADDIE concept in clinical teaching for neurology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05343-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Assessed at the end of week four with a seven-dimension Critical Thinking Skills Assessment Scale (Cronbach's alpha 0.90). Table 2 reports 231.49 ± 10.73 (control) vs 239.38 ± 8.52 (observation), t = 4.14, "statistically significant differences (P < 0.001)".
