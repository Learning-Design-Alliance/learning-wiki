### Choi Min Hyeok 2025

Choi Min Hyeok, Moon Min Hui, Kang Suk Woong, Kim Kyeong Baek, Ahn Tae Young, Bae Jung Yun. (2025). Challenges in Diagnosing Pediatric Monteggia Fractures: Role of Clinical Experience and Imaging. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina61081457

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' observational interpretation from the Discussion of the radiograph-review study, in which participants diagnosed folders without clinical information. The article attributes the effect to awareness rather than a tested variable, and links it to the cognitive bias "satisfaction of search" distracting from radial head dislocation.

> "In our study, participants who considered this diagnosis to be informed by their prior training or clinical experience were more likely to correctly identify the injury. This supports the notion that a high index of suspicion plays a critical role in recognizing Monteggia fractures."
