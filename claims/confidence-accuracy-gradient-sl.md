## Evidence

### Liang 2026

Liang, F., Zhu, Y., Li, R. & Fu, Q. (2026). Foil Type Modulates Developmental Changes in Statistical Learning Across Childhood to Adulthood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00434-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Group × Confidence GLMM (M3b) on partword foil trials within childhood; the main effect of Confidence was significant (𝜒2(2) = 8.01, p = 0.018), with Familiar also exceeding Guess (B = 0.411, pHolm = 0.017), indicating all children showed some metacognitive sensitivity for partword foils.

> "Remember responses (𝐸𝑀𝑀 = 0.545, 95% CI [0.493, 0.596]) were more accurate than Guess responses (𝐸𝑀𝑀 = 0.461, 95% CI [0.394, 0.529])"

### Liang 2026 (2)

Liang, F., Zhu, Y., Li, R. & Fu, Q. (2026). Foil Type Modulates Developmental Changes in Statistical Learning Across Childhood to Adulthood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00434-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Group × Confidence GLMM (M3b) on partword foil trials for Grade 5 versus adults; the Confidence effect was significant (𝜒2(2) = 17.958, p < 0.001), and the main effect of Group was significant (𝜒2(1) = 5.857, p = 0.016) with an adult EMM of 0.626 versus 0.520 for children.

> "Remember responses (𝐸𝑀𝑀 = 0.640, 95% CI [0.567, 0.708]) were more accurate than Guess responses (𝐸𝑀𝑀 = 0.445, 95% CI [0.351, 0.544])"
