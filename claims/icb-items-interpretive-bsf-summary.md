## Evidence

### Finby B 2021

Finby B, Heyer LJ, Malcolm Campbell A. (2021). Data‐rich textbook figures promote core competencies: Comparison of two textbooks. Biochem Mol Biol Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/bmb.21488

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Full-census classification of all 1824 visual elements as summary or interpretive per the printed definitions. The census found "80% of its figures and 90% of its tables were interpretive" in ICB, versus 23% and 3% respectively in BSF, whose items often supplied the take-home message.

> "In ICB, 80% of its figures and 90% of its tables were interpretive. Figures in BSF were interpretive 23% of the time, and tables were interpretive 3% of the time."
