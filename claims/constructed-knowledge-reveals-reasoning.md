Qualitative analysis of phase 2 of the interview, in which Sarah's epistemological approach shifted to "knowledge as invented stuff" after the interviewer prompted her to consider "any explanation you find." She then developed the electron pull description and committed to it, saying "that's what [she's] going with."

> "By showing how she puts together elements of her thinking into new ideas, Sarah gives the interviewer insight into her reasoning."
