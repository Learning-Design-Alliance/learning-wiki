# Student engagement increased across all performance levels over three DBR cycles, with low-performing students moving from 7–10 teacher prompts per lesson to 95–100% sustained attention by Cycle 3

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q1` argument or single case

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Low-performing students' engagement improved from needing 7–10 teacher prompts per lesson before the intervention to 95–100% sustained attention by Cycle 3. [→ Lin 2026](#lin-2026)
`q1 i?` The majority group achieved stable 95–100% on-task behaviour by the second cycle, and top performers required no further teacher intervention beyond the second cycle. [→ Lin 2026](#lin-2026)

## Evidence

### Lin 2026

Lin, T., Mullick, J., & Campbell, M. (2026). Inclusive pedagogy in practice within the multi-tiered systems of support framework: a design-based research in a Chinese EFL classroom. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09754-3

`q1 · i?`

Teacher-reported engagement observations across the three DBR cycles in one Chinese EFL class of 20 students. Low performers' prompts fell from "7–10 teacher prompts per lesson" to 95–100% sustained attention; no effect size is reported.

> "prior to the intervention, low-performing students required 7–10 teacher prompts per lesson to remain focused. This number decreased to 3–5 after the first cycle, then to 1–2 in the second cycle, ultimately resulting in 95–100% sustained attention by Cycle 3."
