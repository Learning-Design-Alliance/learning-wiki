Evidence¶

Zhang Song 2018¶

Zhang Song, Xu Jiancheng, Wang Hongwei, Zhang Dong, Zhang Qichuan, Zou Liguang. (2018). Effects of problem-based learning in Chinese radiology education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000010069

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of 13 studies involving 1090 medical students (PBL group = 545, control group = 545), pooled with a random-effects model due to significant heterogeneity (I2 = 95%, P < .00001). Skill tests assessed "clinical practice for film reading."

"The result showed a significant difference in skill scores (SMD = 2.10, 95% CI [1.38, 2.83]) in favor of PBL, compared with the traditional teaching"
