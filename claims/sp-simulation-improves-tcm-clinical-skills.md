## Evidence

### Zeng Jinhao 2023

Zeng Jinhao, Liang Shuang, Fu Xiaoxu, Guo Jing, Bai Yaolin, Zhou Shan, Du Quanyu, Wang Zhenxing, Zhang Xiyu, Peng Sihan, Wen Lijuan, Li Wenyuan, Li Bin, Yang Han, Zhang Yi. (2023). Student standardized patients versus occupational standardized patients for improving clinical competency among TCM medical students: a 3-year prospective randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04198-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Clinical performance examination scored on TCM-specific skill dimensions (inspection, tongue manifestation, inquiry, palpation). ANOVA was significant in all three years; the authors state the results "suggested that performing case scenario simulation by OSP-TCMs or SSP-TCMs offered an advantage over traditional didactic training."

> "results of one-way ANOVA for comparing the differences among the three groups (2018/19/20, F = 4.836/5.434/4.310, P = 0.013/0.008/0.019) and post-hoc analysis showed that trainees in both SSP-TCM and OSP-TCM groups exhibited significantly greater improvements relative to those in the TM group"
