## Evidence

### Zurc Joca 2022

Zurc Joca, Planinšec Jurij. (2022). Associations between Physical Activity and Academic Competence: A Cross-Sectional Study among Slovenian Primary School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19020623

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 1 of the multiple regression analysis in the cross-sectional study of 1520 students, run separately by sex. The article reports "participation in sports clubs among boys (p = 0.002)" and girls' PA frequency (p = 0.024) as significant predictors; only p-values are printed, no effect size.

> "The frequency of PA among girls (p = 0.024) and participation in sports clubs among boys (p = 0.002) were statistically significant predictors of academic achievements among Slovenian 9- to 11-year-olds."
