The review's cross-study synthesis of 10 teaching-and-learning-method studies categorizes reported methods into three pedagogical approaches and characterizes experiential clinical learning as most impactful yet most demanding to implement.

"experiential learning in authentic clinical environments, such as community engagement, home visits, and outreach rotations, which were consistently reported to produce the greatest positive impact on students’ attitudes, confidence, and perceived competence, but remain the most resource-intensive and logistically challenging to implement"
