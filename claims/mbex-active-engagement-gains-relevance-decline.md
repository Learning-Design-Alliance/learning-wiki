Chatzikyriakidou, K., Hall, K. L., Redish, E. F., & Cooke, T. J. (2025). A critical review of pre-post surveys designed to measure student epistemology in undergraduate science courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.15575](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.15575)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Hall (2013), that in the introductory organismal biology course the MBEX showed active-engagement pedagogy, not conventional lectures, tended to significantly improve principles and interdisciplinary reasoning expectations. No effect sizes are printed.

> "the MBEX revealed that active-engagement pedagogy, but not conventional lectures, tended to significantly improve their expectations for the themes of principles and interdisciplinary reasoning (Hall, 2013)"

### Chatzikyriakidou 2025 (2)

Chatzikyriakidou, K., Hall, K. L., Redish, E. F., & Cooke, T. J. (2025). A critical review of pre-post surveys designed to measure student epistemology in undergraduate science courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.15575](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.15575)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that all course versions produced significant deterioration of favorable pre-class attitudes toward the relevance theme, per post-course MBEX responses. No effect size is printed.

> "Regardless of the pedagogy used, all versions of this course resulted in significant deterioration of favorable pre-class attitudes toward the relevance theme."

### Chatzikyriakidou 2025 (3)

Chatzikyriakidou, K., Hall, K. L., Redish, E. F., & Cooke, T. J. (2025). A critical review of pre-post surveys designed to measure student epistemology in undergraduate science courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.15575](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.15575)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Chatzikyriakidou and McCartney (2020), that pre-post MBEX in a large general biology course showed slight, insignificant declines in all four themes; fine-grained analysis revealed favorable general-science responses alongside unfavorable biology-specific ones.

> "These authors observed that students expressed slight, albeit insignificant, declines in all 4 epistemic themes"
