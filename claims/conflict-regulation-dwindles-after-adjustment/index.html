Evidence¶

Schnaubert 2021¶

Schnaubert, L., Krukowski, S., & Bodemer, D. (2021). Assumptions and confidence of others: the impact of socio-cognitive information on metacognitive self-regulation. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09269-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Within-subject experiment testing hypothesis 3b: point-biserial rank correlations between post-adjustment conflict (absolute distance between disagreeing answers) and information requests. The mean coefficient was "at 0.04 ( SD = 0.20)", and equivalency testing (Lakens, 2017) with two one-sided t-tests against a − 0.20 to 0.20 Cohen's d interval was applied.

"The mean correlation coefficient across the sample was at 0.04 ( SD = 0.20). Because we hypothesized the absence of a correlation, we used equivalency testing as proposed by Lakens ( 2017) to test its equivalence to zero."
