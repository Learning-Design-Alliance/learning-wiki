## Evidence

### Luke Alexander Maniangat 2021

Luke Alexander Maniangat, Mathew Simy, Kuriadom Sam Thomas, George Jeny Mary, Karobari Mohmed Isaqali, Marya Anand, Pawar Ajinkya Mansing. (2021). Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning versus Traditional Teaching Methods in Improving Acquisition of Radiographic Interpretation Skills among Dental Students—A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/9630285

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis in RevMan 5.3 of twelve studies of PBL-type interventions versus controls for radiographic interpretation skills, using a random-effects model; nine of the twelve studies showed positive mean difference values. The pooled "positive difference of 0.54 (0.18, 0.90)" was statistically significant favouring the intervention group.

> "the mean difference in the proficiency score showed a positive difference of 0.54 (0.18, 0.90) with the random effect model based on the heterogeneity value of I2 which was statistically significant favouring the intervention group"
