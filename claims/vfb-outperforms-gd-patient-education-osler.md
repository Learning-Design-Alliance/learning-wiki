Double-blind randomized controlled trial of 78 medical interns randomized to VFB or GD workshops; between-group comparison used the Mann-Whitney U test. The article reports "The VFB group significantly outperformed the GD group in post-intervention scores (p < 0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The VFB group significantly outperformed the GD group in post-intervention scores (p < 0.001), with a mean score improvement of 12.33 ± 2.53 compared to 4.05 ± 2.05 in the GD group (p < 0.001)."
