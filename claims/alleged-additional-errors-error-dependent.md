### Plata Christopher 2021

Plata Christopher, Nellessen Martin, Roth Rebecca, Ecker Hannes, Böttiger Bernd W., Löser Johannes, Wetsch Wolfgang A. (2021). Impact of video quality when evaluating video-assisted cardiopulmonary resuscitation: a randomized, controlled simulation trial. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-021-00486-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of alleged additional errors in the simulation trial. The paper reports "allegedly errors did not significantly depend on video quality levels (p = 0.422)" while the presented error was significant (p < 0.001), highest in wrong-hand-position videos (46.2%) versus correct CPR (7.6%).

> "in 184 of 650 video sequences (28.3%), 262 additional errors, that were not shown in the video sequence, were indicated by the evaluators"
