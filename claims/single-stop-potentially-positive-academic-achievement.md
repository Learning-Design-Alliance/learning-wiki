# Single Stop has potentially positive effects on academic achievement of first-time community college students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 causal `r2` · `q2` · `i0`–`i1`

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` In Zhu et al. (2018), the percentage of attempted college credits completed was 52.80% for Single Stop students versus 46.50% for comparison students (effect size 0.18, p = .03), and cumulative GPA was 2.59 versus 2.41 (effect size 0.16, p = .05). [→ zhu-achievement](#zhu-achievement)
`q2 i0` In Daugherty et al. (2016), the percentage of attempted college credits completed was identical in both groups (78.70%), yielding an effect size of 0 and no statistically significant difference in academic achievement. [→ daugherty-credit-null](#daugherty-credit-null)
