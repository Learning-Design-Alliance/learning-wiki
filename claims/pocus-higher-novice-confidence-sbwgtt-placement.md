### Gillett Katrine E. 2026

Gillett Katrine E., Boysen Søren R., Harding Cole N., Finch Ashley J., Hillstead Madison E., Menard Julie. (2026). Teaching Clinical Competency: Evaluating POCUS‐Guided Versus Landmark Techniques for Thoracostomy Tube Placement in Canine Cadavers by Veterinary Students. Journal of Veterinary Emergency and Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/vec.70118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Visual analog scale data from 30 veterinary students in a crossover randomized cadaver study. The results section reports that "confidence scores were higher for UPN (mean: 7.2 ± 2.4) compared with LPN (4.4 ± 2.6, p = 0.0232)" and LPE.

> "Students felt more confident that the SBWGTT was correctly placed within the pleural space when using POCUS‐guided techniques compared with the landmark technique (Figure 8A). Specifically, confidence scores were higher for UPN (mean: 7.2 ± 2.4) compared with LPN (4.4 ± 2.6, p = 0.0232) and LPE (4.6 ± 2.7, p = 0.0422)."
