Descriptive rubric-wise analysis of scores for Rubrics 1–5 extracted from the learning reports of 42 field-study students. Rubrics 1 and 2 recorded mean scores "close to 80 points", Rubric 3 about 65, and Rubric 5 near 50; these are descriptive means with no test statistics.

> "The lowest performance was observed in Rubric 5, with an average score near 50 points and a comparatively wide standard deviation."
