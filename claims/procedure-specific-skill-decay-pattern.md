## Evidence

### Marschalek Julian 2021

Marschalek Julian, Kuessel Lorenz, Stammler-Safar Maria, Kiss Herbert, Ott Johannes, Husslein Heinrich. (2021). Comparison of a practice-based versus theory-based training program for conducting vacuum-assisted deliveries: a randomized-controlled trial. Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00404-021-06159-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level comparison of procedure-specific rating scores between first and second post-training tests in the RCT. Theory-group decreases reached p = 0.002, 0.022, 0.002, 0.010; the practice group showed "only a significant worsening in 'protection of the perineum'" (p = 0.006).

> "In the theory-based group, there was a significant decrease with respect to "vaginal examination for the presenting part, rotation and station" (First post-training test: 4 (IQR 3–5) vs. Second post-training test: 3 (IQR 2–4), p = 0.002), "assessment of the need for oxytocin""
