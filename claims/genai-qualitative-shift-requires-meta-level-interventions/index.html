Linus Brunnström & Lisa Palmqvist 2026¶

Linus Brunnström & Lisa Palmqvist. (2026). AI-interaction literacy: reflections on how generative AI might be used to support self-regulated learning in higher education. Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2026.2731261

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Reflective demonstration across eight prompt–response exchanges (Table 1). Simplification of the argumentation-practice objection and condensing of an overwhelming SRL-based plan occurred only after explicit learner requests, illustrating demand on self-regulation.

"In other words, the learner needed fairly advanced self-regulatory skills to 'tune' the GenAI into a genuinely helpful partner."

The authors' summary of the conversational trajectory: the three-step loop (set one small goal, learn and practise, reflect) was rated the most pedagogically effective response, coded relational/extended abstract, and required multiple prior rounds of learner-driven calibration.

"The condensed three-step learning loop, as shown in Step 8 in Table 1, was the most actionable and accessible response in the entire conversation, yet it took seven prior rounds of calibration to get there."
