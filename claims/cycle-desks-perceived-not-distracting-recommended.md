Post-intervention researcher-developed Likert questionnaire with pooled chi-square analysis (neutral responses omitted); only twelve students responded, a small sample the authors themselves flag. Survey found cycling "not intrusive or distracting" (p = 0.004); no effect size printed.

> "The combined post-intervention survey results revealed cycling was not intrusive or distracting (CYC, n = 6; SIT, n = 6) (p = 0.004) and incorporating activity into a classroom setting was recommended (CYC, n = 5; SIT, n = 5) (p = 0.011)."
