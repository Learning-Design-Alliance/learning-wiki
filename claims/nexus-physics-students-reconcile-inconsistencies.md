## Evidence

### E. F. Redish 2014

E. F. Redish, C. Bauer, K. L. Carleton, T. J. Cooke, M. Cooper, C. H. Crouch, B. W. Dreyfus, B. Geller, J. Giannini, J. S. Gouvea, M. W. Klymkowsky, W. Losert, K. Moore, J. Presson, V. Sawtelle, K. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' observational conclusion from ethnographic observations, classroom videos, interviews, and analysis of artifacts in the small development classes delivered in 2011-12 and 2012-13 with N~50; e.g., a student (Gavin) interviewed about diffusion described when "things started to click." Conclusions are preliminary per the authors.

> "In this context, students bring sophisticated reasoning to physics from their other classes, identify inconsistencies in their knowledge that had previously gone unnoticed, and (with help) find ways to reconcile those inconsistencies."
