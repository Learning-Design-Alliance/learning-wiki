### Imundo 2024

Imundo, M. N., Zung, I., Whatley, M. C., & Pan, S. C. (2024). When Two Learners Are Better Than One: Using Flashcards with a Partner Improves Metacognitive Accuracy. Metacognition and Learning. https://link.springer.com/journal/11409/articles

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 3 (405 participants, preregistered) instructed Individual-condition participants to test themselves aloud, enforced with an audio monitor. The abstract reports that with overt retrieval required, the two conditions' global and item-level judgments were similarly accurate, supporting overt retrieval as the driver of the paired-condition calibration benefit.

> "With this change, participants in the Individual and Paired conditions reported similarly accurate gJOLs and iJOLs."
