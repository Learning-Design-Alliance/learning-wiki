Kahn P 2026¶

Kahn P, Carrigan M, Wyman I, Liu R, Smith P, Andonegui AR, Murtagh L and Song F (2026) Still emerging: understanding Generative AI use in Higher Education. Front. Educ. 11:1885253. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885253

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Factual item from the staff survey in the case study. The article reports 63% had used at least one GAI tool in the six months to May 2024, with only 14% holding a paid subscription; reported student engagement with subscriptions was slightly higher at 21%.

"Responses indicated that 63% of staff respondents had used at least one GAI tool at some point in the 6 months to May 2024, although only 14% of respondents had taken out a paid subscription."

Kahn P 2026 (2)¶

Kahn P, Carrigan M, Wyman I, Liu R, Smith P, Andonegui AR, Murtagh L and Song F (2026) Still emerging: understanding Generative AI use in Higher Education. Front. Educ. 11:1885253. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885253

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Authors' interpretation (type e) in the Discussion: because free versions are rate limited and restricted to older models, the article reasons that a subscription proxies sustained engagement, and cites Mollick (2024) that practical experimentation is needed to develop a working understanding of GAI tools.

"a subscription can be taken as a meaningful proxy for sustained engagement, suggesting that a substantial majority of our respondents were working with affordances that were substantively imagined to varying degrees"
