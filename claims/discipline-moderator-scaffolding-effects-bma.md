# Scaffolding effects in PBL are higher in Engineering, Technology, and Mathematics than in Science, and relatively low in integrated STEM education

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i1`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Scaffolding had higher effect sizes in Engineering (g = 0.528), Technology (g = 0.379), and Mathematics (g = 0.425) than in Science (g = 0.146). [→ Nam Ju Kim 2018](#nam-ju-kim-2018)
`q3 i1` In integrated STEM education the effect size was relatively lower (g = 0.201, 95% CrI 0.005 to 0.428). [→ Nam Ju Kim 2018 (2)](#nam-ju-kim-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Nam Ju Kim 2018

Nam Ju Kim, Brian R. Belland, & Andrew E. Walker. (2018). Effectiveness of Computer-Based Scaffolding in the Context of Problem-Based Learning for STEM Education: Bayesian Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9419-1

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the discipline moderator in the Bayesian meta-analysis. Engineering showed the highest effect at "g = 0.528", with Science the lowest at g = 0.146.

> "Scaffolding had higher effect sizes when it was used in Engineering ( g = 0.528, 95% CrI = 0.025 to 0.983), Technology ( g = 0.379, 95% CrI = 0.011 to 0.782), and Mathematics education (g = 0.425, 95% CrI = 0.024 to 0.883) than when it was utilized in Science (g = 0.146, 95% CrI = 0.003 to 0.295)"

### Nam Ju Kim 2018 (2)

Nam Ju Kim, Brian R. Belland, & Andrew E. Walker. (2018). Effectiveness of Computer-Based Scaffolding in the Context of Problem-Based Learning for STEM Education: Bayesian Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9419-1

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup result for integrated STEM within the discipline moderator of the Bayesian meta-analysis. The effect was "g = 0.201, 95% CrI = 0.005 to 0.428", which the discussion notes was contrary to prior meta-analyses on integrated STEM education.

> "In the case of integrated STEM education, which integrated the content and process of disciplines, the effect size was relatively lower (g = 0.201, 95% CrI = 0.005 to 0.428)."
