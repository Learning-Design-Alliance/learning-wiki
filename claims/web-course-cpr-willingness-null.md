## Evidence

### Nord Anette 2017

Nord Anette, Svensson Leif, Claesson Andreas, Herlitz Johan, Hult Håkan, Kreitz-Sandberg Susanne, Nilsson Lennart. (2017). The effect of a national web course “Help-Brain-Heart” as a supplemental learning tool before CPR training: a cluster randomised trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-017-0439-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire in a cluster randomised trial of school students at 6-month follow-up. The friend scenario reached P = 0.05 favouring the intervention; the stranger scenario showed "no significant difference in the situation involving a stranger".

> "At 6 months, 80% (intervention) and 73% (control, P = 0.05) of the students responded that they would do both compressions and ventilation if a friend suffered OHCA; there was no significant difference in the situation involving a stranger (34 and 31%, respectively; NS)."
