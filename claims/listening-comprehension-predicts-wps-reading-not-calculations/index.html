Early listening comprehension directly predicts later word-problem solving and word reading but not calculations, and numerical cognition does not mediate this relation

In a longitudinal path model of 370 first graders, early listening comprehension had direct associations with later word-problem solving (0.167) and word reading (0.103) but not calculations, not mediated by numerical cognition; the design is observational and cannot show cause.
