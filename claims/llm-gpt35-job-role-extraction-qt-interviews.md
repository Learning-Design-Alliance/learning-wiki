### Franziska Greinert 2025

Extending the European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies: new proficiency triangle and qualification profiles. arXiv. Originally published 2024. `q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

LLM-assisted analysis of anonymized interview transcripts using two prompts, yielding descriptions of 10 roles. The article states the LLM "improves the objectivity of the qualitative content analysis by having a kind of second coder"; the extracted personas influenced the version 2.1 qualification profile descriptions.

> "30 anonymized interview transcripts were analyzed using a large language model (LLM, namely GPT3.5) to identify frequently mentioned job roles representing categories or clusters."
