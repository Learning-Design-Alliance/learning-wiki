Sarı et al. 2017¶

İrfan Sarı, Nadir Çeliköz, Süleyman Ünal. (2017). The Effect of Peer Support on University Level Students’ English Language Achievements. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.8, No.1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131776

Independent T-test comparing posttest scores of the successful experimental group (Assistant, M = 87.78) and successful control group (Not Assistant, M = 87.61), each n = 200, t = .367, df = 398, p = .714. The article concludes "Being in the role of teaching did not affec t the ultimate test scores of the assistants".
