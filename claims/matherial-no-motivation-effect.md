Motivation measured with a 17-item four-point Likert scale adapted from the Dutch 'Attitude towards Mathematics' scale (Cronbach's alpha of 0.73); no significant effect found.

> "With regard to motivation to learn math, an ANOVA revealed no effect of the intervention (F(1, 226) = 0.006, p > 0.93 (experimental condition, M = 38.75, SD = 8.18, control condition, M = 38.65, SD = 9.76)), and the effect size was very small (partial eta squared = 0.0001)."
