`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Skills were assessed with a 13-item validated checklist during the simulated scenario (5-minute briefing, 12 minutes per student). "Inferential analysis found no statistically significant difference in skills scores between the EG and CG (p = 0.853; small effect size, d = 0.076)"; means were 10.91 (CG) and 10.79 (EG).

> "Inferential analysis found no statistically significant difference in skills scores between the EG and CG (p = 0.853; small effect size, d = 0.076). Mean scores were similar: 10.91 (SD±1.64; 95% CI 9.96-11.86) in the CG and 10.79 (SD±1.63; 95% CI 9.88-11.71) in the EG."
