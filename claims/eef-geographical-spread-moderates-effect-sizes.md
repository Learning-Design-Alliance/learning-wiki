## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Primary ITT and FSM effect sizes were higher for interventions located in one or up to three geographical areas than for interventions in a greater number of areas; the pattern did not hold for secondary ITT effects. [→ Demack 2021](#demack-2021)

## Evidence

### Demack 2021

Demack, S., Maxwell, B., Coldwell, M., Stevens, A., Wolstenholme, C., Reaney-Wood, S., Stiell, B., & Lortie-Forgues, H. (2021). Review of EEF projects: Evaluation report. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED625480

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis of effect sizes by geographical context across the EEF trials. The review reports "higher effect sizes for interventions located in one or up to three geographical areas" and notes a similar pattern for FSM but not secondary ITT effects.

> "The meta-analyses found an association between geographical context and primary ITT effect size that showed higher effect sizes for interventions located in one or up to three geographical areas  compared to interventions located in a greater number of geographical areas."
