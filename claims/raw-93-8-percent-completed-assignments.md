### Armbrust Wineke 2015

Armbrust Wineke, Bos Joyce J. F. J., Cappon Jeannette, van Rossum Marion A.J., Sauer Pieter J. J., Wulffraat Nico, van Wijnen Veera K., Lelieveld Otto T. H. M. (2015). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multicenter acceptance evaluation of R@W in 64 children with JIA aged 8-13 across three Dutch clinics; commitment was monitored weekly. The article reports that "60 participants (93.8 %) had completely fulfilled all the assignments for all 14 weeks".

> "At the end of the program, 60 participants (93.8 %) had completely fulfilled all the assignments for all 14 weeks on a proper way, also showing that they understood the meaning of the assignments."
