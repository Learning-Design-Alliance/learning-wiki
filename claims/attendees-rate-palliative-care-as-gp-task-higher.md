## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Reith Dorothee, Walter Tobias, Engeser Peter, Wensing Michel, Flum Elisabeth, Szecsenyi Joachim, Krug Katja. (2020). How to ensure basic competencies in end of life care – a mixed methods study with post-graduate trainees in primary care in Germany. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-020-00540-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of three role-rating items between intervention-group attendees and 75 non-attendee controls surveyed once in March 2018. Group I also rated "palliative care is a fundamental part of general practice" 4.37 (SD 0.84) versus 3.92 (SD 1.0) in group C. No significance test printed for these items.

> "Group I rated the statement “Palliative care is one of my tasks as a GP” with a mean of 4.47 (SD 0.75) on a 5-point Likert scale in contrast to group C who rated it with 4.11 (SD 0.83)."
