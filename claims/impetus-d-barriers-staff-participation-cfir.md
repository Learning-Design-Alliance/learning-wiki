`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative process evaluation coding interview and survey data in NVivo 12 against CFIR domains (inner setting, intervention characteristics, characteristics of individuals). Reported reasons for non-participation included 25% unable to attend the session and 16% having technical issues accessing online training.

> "One of the main barriers to staff participation was having competing priorities that occurred at the same time as the IMPETUS-D program was implemented, specifically the introduction of the National Aged Care Standards and the Royal Commission into Aged Care Quality and Safety."
