Steiss 2023¶

Steiss, Jacob; Tate, Tamara; Graham, Steve; Cruz, Jazmin; Hebert, Michael; Wang, Jiali; Moon, Youngsun; Tseng, Waverly; Warschauer, Mark. (2023). Comparing the Quality of Human and ChatGPT Feedback on Students' Writing. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Comparing+the+Quality+of+Human+and+ChatGPT+Feedback+on+Students+Writing

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive and ANOVA analysis of coded feedback ratings (n = 198) found "all small" partial eta-squared values from .01 to .12; ChatGPT means ranged from 3.09 to 4.02 across dimensions.

"Partial eta-squared effect sizes for variance explained by the difference in rater were all small, ranging from .01 (for criteria-based feedback) to .12 (for prioritization of essential features), indicating that the difference in raters explained very little of the differences in feedback ratings."
