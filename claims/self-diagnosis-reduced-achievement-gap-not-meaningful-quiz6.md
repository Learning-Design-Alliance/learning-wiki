## Evidence

### Andrew Mason 2009

Andrew Mason, Elisheva Cohen, Chandralekha Singh, and Edit Yerushalmi. (2009). Self-Diagnosis, Scaffolding and Transfer: A Tale of Two Problems. [doi:10.1063/1.3266736](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266736)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intra-group correlation analysis for the difficult quiz 6 (Table 3): intervention groups D, C and B all showed non-significant pre–post and SD–post correlations, while the control group showed a positive pre–post correlation. The authors conclude they "cannot simply declare any of the three interventions as completely superficial o r meaningful".

> "on one hand in all intervention groups there are no significant correlations  between the pre vs. post whereas there is a positive correlation between these two variables in the control group, suggesting that the intervention indeed has reduced the gaps bet ween low and high achievers."
