### Freisberg L 2026

Expected Competencies and Personal Attributes of Digital Health Navigators to Support Digital Mental Health Care: Focus Group and Interview Study With Patients and Health Care Professionals. JMIR Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.2196/83073

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of the hard skills domain (35 participants). "10/35, 28.57%" emphasized basic mental health knowledge; separately, "6/35, 17.14%" considered it important that DHNs recognize early warning signs such as progressing social isolation and escalate to clinicians.

> "the interviewees (10/35, 28.57%) emphasized the importance of equipping DHNs with basic knowledge of mental health conditions and the ability to manage mental health crises."

### Freisberg L 2026 (2)

Expected Competencies and Personal Attributes of Digital Health Navigators to Support Digital Mental Health Care: Focus Group and Interview Study With Patients and Health Care Professionals. JMIR Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.2196/83073

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same qualitative corpus, crisis management category. "4/35, 11.43%" stressed clear role boundaries; participants also questioned whether DHNs could realistically be trained to manage crises, noting psychiatric warning signs emerge more subtly than somatic ones.

> "Other participants (4/35, 11.43%) stressed the need for clear role boundaries and solid training for DHNs in managing such situations, emphasizing that clinical decisions and crisis interventions should remain the responsibility of qualified clinicians."
