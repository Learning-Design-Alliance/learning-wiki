### Janssen 2025

Janssen, L., Schick, K., Pittroff, S. I. D., Reiser, S., Bauer, J., Berberat, P. O., Gartmeier, M. (2025). “It turns out I was wrong”: a thematic analysis of medical students’ reflective writing in an e-learning course on communication skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08172-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of 495 reflective texts from 55 first-year clinical medical students in a three-module e-learning course. The analysis found that “students addressed all key topics conveyed in the course”, with Themes 1–3 mapping onto the three modules.

> "Regarding the first research Question 1 “What learning content do the students address in their reflections?” the findings revealed that students addressed all key topics conveyed in the course."
