`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design rationale reported by the builders for scaling down the 2014 Duke University lightboard, which they relied on heavily via Chip Bobbert's description and documentation. Scaling was done by reducing long transverse T-slotted pieces by 12 inches and adjusting support locations for the 3.5-foot glass height.

> "The reduced width and weight of our scaled-down board makes it easier for a single person to control, and makes it easier to move into and out of – and maneuver within – relatively small office spaces or stockrooms."
