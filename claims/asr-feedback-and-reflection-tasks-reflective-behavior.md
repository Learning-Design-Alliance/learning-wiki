## Evidence

### Chen D 2026

Chen D, Zeng L, Ou W and Zhong S (2026) ASR technology in college English speaking instruction: the role of feedback internalization and metacognitive strategies. Front. Psychol. 17:1847238. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1847238

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis within a structural equation model of 325 valid questionnaire responses tested ASR features as predictors of reflective behavior. The article reports corrective feedback significantly predicted reflective behavior, "with a path coefficient of 0.398 (SE = 0.074, CR = 5.416)"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "In contrast, timely Corrective Feedback had a significant positive effect on reflective behavior, with a path coefficient of 0.398 (SE = 0.074, CR = 5.416)."
