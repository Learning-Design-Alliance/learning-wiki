Supplemental subgroup analysis from the 2013 Denver RCT; only four grade-by-demographic subgroups met WWC group design standards, and the two largest female subgroups were combined. The report states this difference "was large enough to be considered substantively important" despite non-significance.

> "For the combined (female students in grades 1 and 2) sample, the WWC found a positive but not statistically significant difference; this difference was large enough to be considered substantively important."
