## Evidence

### Zou Ya 2026

Zou Ya, Xie Wenzhao, Huang Hui, Liu Dan, Zhou Qing, Yi Qifeng, Tong Xiaoliang, Mao Ping. (2026). The effects of case-based, problem-based, and team-based learning on nursing students’ problem-solving, self-directed learning, and communication skills: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08602-3

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by teaching method for self-directed learning (Table 5). CBL was adopted in only one study and was excluded from the subgroup analysis, so no CBL estimate is pooled here.

> "PBL (SMD = 0.53, 95% CI: 0.35–0.71, I2 = 49%, P<0.001), and TBL (SMD = 0.78, 95% CI: 0.46–1.10, I2 = 0%, P<0.001) all significantly improved nursing students’ self-directed learning"
