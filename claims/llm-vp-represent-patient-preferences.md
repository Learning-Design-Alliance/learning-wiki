## Evidence

### Coristine A 2025

Coristine A, Li D, Foroutan DB, Ojo TF, Menon NP, Aditya I, Cook DA, Overgaard J, Pankratz VS, Del Fiol G, Aakre CA. (2025). Virtual Patients Using Large Language Models: Scalable, Contextualized Simulation of Clinician-Patient Dialogue With Feedback. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68486

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Blinded final ratings of perceived versus planned preferences (testing/treatment, cost, illness-control prioritization) across 48 GPT-4.0 dialogues; comparable results for GPT-3.5-Turbo. Preference-rating ICCs were 0.59, 0.75, and 0.39.

> "For dialogues created using GPT-4.0-Turbo, 5 of 6 nonneutral planned preferences were recognized as such in ≥54% of dialogues, and all 3 neutral planned preferences were rated as “no opinion” in ≥90% of the dialogues."
