# CPS ninth-grade math category families typically contained many tasks per semester—about 11 in assessments and 28 in assignments—except weekly categories, which typically contained only four tasks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On average there were about 11 tasks per semester in the assessments family and about 28 in assignments; teachers using weekly grading tended to consistently use four tasks for final semester grades. [→ Diaz 2021](#diaz-2021)

## Evidence

### Diaz 2021

Diaz, B., & Easton, J.Q. (2021). CPS Gradebook Technical Report. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/CPS-gradebook-technical-report

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of task counts per category family (Table 4, N=86,533). The report states "There were about 28 tasks on average in the assignments category family" and that weekly schemes tended to use four tasks, so no single task played an outsized weight.

> "On average, there were about 11 tasks per semester in the assessments category family—approximately one assessment task every three weeks over the course of a full semester. There were about 28 tasks on average in the assignments category family."
