## Evidence

### D. Sonntag 2021

D. Sonntag, O. Bodensiek. (2021). EYE-TRACKING-BASED DESIGN OF MIXED REALITY LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS IN STEM. Technische Universität Braunschweig. [doi:10.21125/inted.2021.1990](https://doi.org/10.21125/inted.2021.1990)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Minor usability study with participants of the preliminary study using the thinking-aloud method while wearing an MR-HMD, plus Brooke's 10-item system usability scale extended with 5 items on visualization helpfulness: "an average score of 83.60 %".

> "In terms of the system usability scale we had an average score of 83.60 % while the lowest value was 78 %."

### D. Sonntag 2021 (2)

D. Sonntag, O. Bodensiek. (2021). EYE-TRACKING-BASED DESIGN OF MIXED REALITY LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS IN STEM. Technische Universität Braunschweig. [doi:10.21125/inted.2021.1990](https://doi.org/10.21125/inted.2021.1990)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-report from the same usability study: "all of the participants reported that the visualizations in the MR application are helpful to understand the experiment", and the group enjoyed working in the MR environment despite the technology being new to them.

> "Furthermore, all of the participants reported that the visualizations in the MR application are helpful to understand the experiment."
