Evidence¶

Sarah Haywood 2015¶

Sarah Haywood, Julia Griggs, Cheryl Lloyd, Stephen Morris, Zsolt Kiss, Amy Skipp. (2015). Creative Futures: Act, Sing, Play Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581247

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Separate analysis of the two treatment arms (ASP-strings and ASP-singing) against the ASP-drama control in the three-arm RCT. The report states "neither strings nor singing workshops showed evidence of out-performing drama workshops"; no separate effect sizes are printed in the text provided.

"Each of the two different types of music workshop (strings and singing) was also analysed separately. As was the case with the main analysis, neither strings nor singing workshops showed evidence of out-performing drama workshops in advancing maths or literacy attainment."
