Phenomenographic analysis of mentee interviews across the REU program identified three objective categories: "gaining research experience and career clarity, gaining information about diﬀerent physics subﬁelds, and making connections with a broader community," with career clarity most frequent (N = 9) and community connections stated by N = 6.
