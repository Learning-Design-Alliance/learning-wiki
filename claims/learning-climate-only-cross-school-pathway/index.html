Evidence¶

Sebastian 2013¶

Sebastian, J., & Allensworth, E. (2013). How Do Secondary Principals Influence Teaching and Learning? Principal's Research Review, 8(4). https://www.nassp.org/prr

q2 · i? · associational · r2

In the same Chicago high school analysis comparing schools, the learning climate pathway uniquely accounted for cross-school differences in instructional quality and achievement; other pathways did not. The study examined pathways both within schools (comparing teachers) and across schools.

"In fact, school learning climate was the only process through which high school leadership accounted for differences across schools in instructional quality and student achievement."
