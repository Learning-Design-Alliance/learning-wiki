# Excluding a middle 'normal' school group raises the association between school retardation/advancement and median-mentality to Q = +.85

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When 7-year-olds in the normal grades (2B and 2A, and correspondingly at other ages) are eliminated, the four-class association between school retarded/advanced and below/above median mentality reaches Q = +.85 across all three schools combined. [→ Toops 1918](#toops-1918)

## Evidence

### Toops 1918

Toops, H. A., and Pintker, R. (1918). Mentality and School Progress. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Mentality+and+School+Progress+Toops+Pintner

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of group mental-test and grade data from three schools (children of superior, medium, and inferior social status), ages 6 to 16. Eliminating the normal school group yields "QFV = +.85", which the authors call "a very high degree of relationship between mentality and school grade"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "We, therefore, proceeded to calculate Q with the school "Normal" children eliminated-- that is, with all 7-year-old children in grades 2B and 2A eliminated, and correspondingly for the other ages. We thus secure QFV = +.85."
