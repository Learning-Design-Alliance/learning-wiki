### Scherff Aline D. 2025

Scherff Aline D., Kääb Stefan, Fischer Martin R., Berndt Markus. (2025). EYE-ECG2: Addressing medical student feedback in an RCT with eye-tracking videos featuring cued retrospective reporting and modified learning sequences for ECG interpretation skills training. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001771

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manual categorisation of free-text feedback from the INT group showed "sentiment was mostly positive (45% positive, 29% neutral, 26% negative)". Positive comments praised following the expert's gaze; negative comments cited pacing and material density.

> "Overall, 35 of 47 students (74%) of the INT group followed the request for written feedback on the EYE-ECG2 video. Of those who did provide feedback, sentiment was mostly positive (45% positive, 29% neutral, 26% negative)."
