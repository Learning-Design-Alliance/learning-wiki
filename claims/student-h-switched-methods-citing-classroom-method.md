## Evidence

### Jeffrey M. Hawkins 2009

Jeffrey M. Hawkins, John R. Thompson, and Michael C. Wittmann. (2009). Students’ Consistency of Graphical Vector Addition Method on 2-D Vector Addition Tasks. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings (venue not printed in text). [doi:10.1063/1.3266704](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266704)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview excerpt from Student H, a calculus-based course student, after the distracter task, explaining his continued use of head-to-tail. The authors report that "The student’s stated reason for switching was because of recall of the classroom method."

> "I’m not doing it the whole eyeballing way that I did at the beginning because, in doing these problems I’ve realized that this [the head-to-tail method] is probably a more sound way, considering that we actually went over it in class."
