### Yu Jingjing 2025

Yu Jingjing, Liu Chang, Yu Jian. (2025). Chinese dental students’ knowledge, attitudes, and practice regarding traumatic dental injuries in immature permanent teeth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07584-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Attitude items in the questionnaire survey of 502 students (Table 4). Large majorities endorsed vital pulp preservation (95.0%), additional examination (91.0%), and the need for more training (96.4%); only 34.3% were confident treating traumatized immature teeth.

> "Most participants agreed that vital pulp preservation (95.0%) and additional examination (91.0%) are essential for TDI management."
