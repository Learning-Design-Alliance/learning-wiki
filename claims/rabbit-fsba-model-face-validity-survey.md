## Evidence

### Joyeux Luc 2021

Joyeux Luc, Javaux Allan, Eastwood Mary P., De Bie Felix R., Van den Bergh Gert, Degliuomini Rebecca S., Vergote Simen, Micheletti Talita, Callewaert Geertje, Ourselin Sebastien, De Coppi Paolo, Van Calenbergh Frank, Vander Poorten Emmanuel, Deprest Jan. (2021). Validation of a high-fidelity training model for fetoscopic spina bifida surgery. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-85607-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous online survey of fetal surgeons worldwide (response rate 53%, 26/49) assessing realism on 7-point Likert scales. Good to exceptional scores (63 to 100% of scores ≥ 4/7) concerned items requiring a living and breathing animal, such as dissection, resection, suturing and depth perception.

> "The three subspecialties of respondents considered our live model realistic since all survey questions reached scores ≥ 4/7 in ≥ 60% of cases and recommended it for its realism (≥ 84% of scores ≥ 4/7; Table 2)."
