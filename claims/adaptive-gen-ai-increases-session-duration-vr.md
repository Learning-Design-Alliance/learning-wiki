## Evidence

### Ka Hei Carrie Lau 2025

Ka Hei Carrie Lau, Sema Sen, Philipp Stark, Efe Bozkir, and Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Adaptive Gen-AI Guidance in Virtual Reality: A Multimodal Exploration of Engagement in Neapolitan Pizza-Making. arXiv. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3716553.3750760](https://doi.org/10.1145/3716553.3750760)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects experiment with 54 participants randomly assigned to No (M = 300.00, SD = 65.05), Moderate (M = 507.62, SD = 182.97) and High (M = 492.24, SD = 94.41) adaptivity; Welch one-way ANOVA after Levene's test found "a significant effect of adaptivity condition, 𝐹(2, 30.5) = 29.91,𝑝 <.001,𝜂 2𝑝 =.38". Games–Howell post-hoc tests showed significant differences of No vs Moderate (mean difference = 207.62 s, p < .001) and No vs High (mean difference = 192.24 s, p < .001); Moderate vs High was not significant (p = .927).
