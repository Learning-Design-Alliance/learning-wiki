## Evidence

### Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al 2022

Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al. (2022). eLearning significantly improves maternity professionals' knowledge of the congenital cytomegalovirus prevention guidelines. Australian & New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics & Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13500

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported practice over the previous month, asked at T1 and T3 and analysed with an ordinal regression model with random intercept (Supplemental Figure S1). The proportion not discussing CMV fell from 70% to 22%.

> "At T1, 70% (188/270) of practicing midwives indicated they did not discuss CMV with women in their care. Analysis of available data at T3 showed that this had dropped to 22% (5/22) (P < 0.001)"
