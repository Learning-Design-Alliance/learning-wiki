The authors' argument that AI-assisted scholarship heightens the urgency of shared vocabulary: retrieval itself becomes biased because term-dependent queries favor the home discipline. The authors also hypothesize, without evidence, that LLMs may overinterpret superficial naming differences as spurious oppositions.

> "term-dependent queries (whether typed by humans or generated by AI agents) are more likely to surface work from the user's home discipline, while omitting adjacent literatures that use different keywords for the same construct."
