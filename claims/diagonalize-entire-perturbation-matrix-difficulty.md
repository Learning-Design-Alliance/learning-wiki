# Many students do not realize the perturbation matrix must be diagonalized only within each degenerate subspace and instead seek simultaneous eigenstates of the two non-commuting Hamiltonians

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q1`
> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Students incorrectly attempt to diagonalize the entire perturbation matrix or find simultaneous eigenstates of H0 and H′ when they do not commute. [→ Emily Marshman 2019](#emily-marshman-2019)

## Evidence

### Emily Marshman 2019

Emily Marshman, Christof Keebaugh, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student difficulties with the corrections to the energy spectrum of the hydrogen atom for the intermediate field Zeeman effect. arXiv:2006.05000. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.marshman](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.marshman)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview findings (13 individual interviews, 45 hours total) following completion of quantum mechanics courses. One interviewed student "justiﬁed his reasoning by incorrectly stating, "We must ﬁnd the simultaneous eigenstates of ˆH 0 and ˆH ′.""

> "Many students did not realize that when the initially chosen basis is not a good basis and the unperturbed Hamiltonian ˆH 0 and the perturbing Hamiltonian ˆH ′ = ˆH ′f s + ˆH ′Z do not commute, they must diagonalize the ˆH ′ = ˆH ′f s + ˆH ′Z matrix only in each degenerate subspace of ˆH 0."
