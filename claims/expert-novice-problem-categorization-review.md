### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2023

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Helping students become proficient problem solvers Part I: A brief review. [doi:10.3390/educsci13020156](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13020156)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review reports Chi et al.'s think-aloud categorization study: "novices categorize problems based on surface features while experts categorize them based on deep features related to problem solution", tied to hierarchical versus loosely connected knowledge organization.

> "Chi et al., [6] found that novices categorize problems based on surface features while experts categorize them based on deep features related to problem solution"

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2023 (2)

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Helping students become proficient problem solvers Part I: A brief review. [doi:10.3390/educsci13020156](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13020156)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports later uses of the categorization task in large enrollment introductory courses finding a continuum of categorization ability, and notes the study "has been replicated with students in an upper-level quantum mechanics course".

> "More recent research has used the categorization task in large enrollment introductory courses [86, 87] and found that students’ ability to categorize the problems fell in a continuum with some students categorizing the problem very similarly to experts, others much closer to novices, and everything in between."
