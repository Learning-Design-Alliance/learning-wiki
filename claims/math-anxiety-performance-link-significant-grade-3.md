# The association between math anxiety and math performance is significant in Grade 3 after accounting for general anxiety

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In Grade 3 children, math anxiety is significantly associated with math performance once general anxiety is accounted for. [→ Cargnelutti 2017](#cargnelutti-2017)

## Evidence

### Cargnelutti 2017

Cargnelutti, E., Tomasetto, C., Passolunghi, M.C. (2017). How is anxiety related to math performance in young students? A longitudinal study of Grade 2 to Grade 3 children. COGNITION & EMOTION, 31(4), 755-764. https://doi.org/10.1080/02699931.2016.1147421

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Longitudinal study of Grade 2 to Grade 3 children measuring math anxiety, general anxiety and math performance across two school years. The article reports that "this link was significant in Grade 3"; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Results revealed that this link was significant in Grade 3, with a prevalent direction from math anxiety to performance, rather than the reverse."
