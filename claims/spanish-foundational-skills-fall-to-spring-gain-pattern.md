`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics of test scores in the norming sample by school year, grade, and term (Table 3.1, Section 3.1). Means generally rose fall to spring with the largest gains in kindergarten and grade 1; the authors suggest older students had already developed stronger foundational skills or were approaching the upper end of the scale.

> "Within grades, mean scores generally increased from fall to spring, with the largest gains observed in kindergarten and grade 1. Mean scores also tended to increase across grade levels. However, both within-grade and cross-grade growth became less pronounced in the higher grades, particularly from grade 2 to grade 3."
