### Behmanesh Fereshteh 2025

Behmanesh Fereshteh, Gholamnia-Shirvani Zeinab, Ghaffarifar Saeideh, Nikbakht Hossein-Ali, Nazmi Sana. (2025). Designing, implementing, and evaluating a gamification approach in childbirth training for midwifery students using computer and mobile application. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07370-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Satisfaction was measured four months after the intervention with a 16-question scale scored 16–48 (Cronbach's alpha 0.8). Gamified satisfaction averaged "41.32 ± 5.54" versus 32.35 ± 6.86 for traditional education, an independent-samples t-test comparison.

> "The data indicate that satisfaction with the gamified approach was significantly greater than that with the traditional method (P < 0.001), with an average satisfaction difference of 8.97 (12.36 for gamified versus 5.58 for traditional, CI = 95%)."
