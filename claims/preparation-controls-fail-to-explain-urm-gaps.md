## Subclaims

## Evidence

### David J. Webb and Cassandra A. Paul 2023

David J. Webb and Cassandra A. Paul. (2023). Attributing equity gaps to course structure in introductory physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

HLM analysis of N = 633 students controlling for Calc and PreFCI yielded URM gaps of -0.388 ± 0.089 for the chapter-by-chapter classes and 0.36 ± 0.14 for the concepts-first class; the interaction term b_URM*ConcptFrst = 0.73 had z = 4.07, P < 10^-3. The authors conclude the Student Deficit model does not explain the gap differences.

> "Neither gap is con-sistent with zero after using this Student Deficit model and the estimated gap for the concepts-first class has increased instead of decreasing"
