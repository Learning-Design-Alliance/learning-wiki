Hanna Dumont and Douglas D. Ready 2023¶

Hanna Dumont and Douglas D. Ready. (2023). On the promise of personalized learning for educational equity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00174-x

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports conflicting prior findings on equity effects, stating the evidence "is much more mixed". Other cited studies found initially low-performing students and students with lower working memory capacity learned less than their more competent peers with technology-enabled personalized learning systems.
