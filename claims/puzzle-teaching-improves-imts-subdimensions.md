`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparisons in Table 2 of the RCT: Attention t = 2.216, p = 0.030, ES = 0.550; Compliance t = 2.623, p = 0.011, ES = 0.651; Trust t = 2.009, p = 0.049, ES = 0.499; Satisfaction t = 2.792, p = 0.007, ES = 0.693 — all favoring the experimental group.

> "However, the posttest mean scores for the experimental group were significantly higher than those of the control group across all these measures (p < 0.05) (Table 2)."
