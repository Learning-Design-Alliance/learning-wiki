### Bart-Plange 2025

Bart-Plange, D.-J., Henderson, K., Hoffman, K., & Trawalter, S. (2025). Critiquing and Reimagining Belonging in Public Spaces in Higher Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10061-z

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

In an experiment (Trawalter et al., 2021, Study 4), students were randomly assigned to visit various campus places while piloting a GPS app or to go about their day normally, then completed a belonging survey that evening. Assigned public-space use 'significantly reduced' the SES belonging gap; no effect size is printed.

> "However, in the "use of public space" condition, this difference was significantly reduced, suggesting that use of public space on campus can reduce gaps in belonging at the university."
