### Zsifkovits Monika 2025

Randomized controlled trial of asynchronous vs. synchronous online teaching formats: equal knowledge after training, greater acceptance and lower intrinsic motivation through asynchronous online learning. `q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Stepwise multiple linear regression across the whole sample identified "pressure/tension (β = − 0.274, p <.001)" and perceived competence (β = − 0.094, p =.005) as significant predictors of the multiple-choice test results; both coefficients were negative.

> "Multiple linear regression analysis of the entire sample revealed the following factors (independent variables) as predictors of the test results (dependent variable): perceived competence (β = − 0.094, p =.005), pressure/tension (β = − 0.274, p <.001)"
