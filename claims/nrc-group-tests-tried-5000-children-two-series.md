`q1 · i?` · `design · r?`

The editorial reports the committee's development procedure: selection of "some twenty tests," a careful trial "on five thousand children," and re-trial "on several thousand more children" to perfect the procedure. It describes the vetting process as printed, without statistical detail.

> "After detailed study the committee selected some twenty tests and gave these a careful trial on five thousand children. From these tests two series were selected which seemed to give the most satisfaction, and in order to perfect the procedure and to still further check up on the results the tests were tried on several thousand more children."
