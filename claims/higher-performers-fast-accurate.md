## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Higher performers (Cluster 1, 39% of sample) had the highest proportion correct across all levels and shorter response times than other clusters on situation model questions. [→ Fang 2021](#fang-2021)

## Evidence

### Fang 2021

Fang, Y., Lippert, A., Cai, Z., Chen, S., Frijters, J. C., Greenberg, D., & Graesser, A. C. (2021). Patterns of Adults with Low Literacy Skills Interacting with an Intelligent Tutoring System. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00266-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects models comparing clusters at four theoretical levels; pairwise contrasts (Table 7) show Cluster 1 accuracy exceeded Cluster 3 and Cluster 4 at all levels (e.g., 0.16*** to 0.25***). Cluster 1 comprised 39% (n = 97) of the sample.

> "Meanwhile, Cluster 1 achieved the highest proportion correct scores across all theoretical levels. Because of the students ’ high accuracy and short response time, we named this cluster “higher performers.” The accuracy of higher performers did not seem to be affected by theoretical level, since they did equally well in lessons across different levels."
