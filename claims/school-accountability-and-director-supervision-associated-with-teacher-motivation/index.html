Evidence¶

Wolf 2014¶

Wolf, S., Aber, J. L., Torrente, C., Rasheed, D., & McCoy, M. (2014). Cluster randomized trial of a large-scale education initiative in the Democratic Republic of Congo: Pilot year impacts on teacher development. SREE Spring 2014 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562697

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Baseline analyses from the OPEQ evaluation in Katanga reported by the authors as school-level correlates of teacher attitudes. The article states that "factors at the school level, such as accountability and the quality of supervision provided by school directors" were associated with teacher motivation and job satisfaction; no effect sizes are printed.

"our analyses show that there were also factors at the school level, such as accountability and the quality of supervision provided by school directors that were associated with teachers' motivation and level of job satisfaction."
