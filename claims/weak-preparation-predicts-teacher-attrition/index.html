Saunders 2024¶

Saunders, R., Fitz, J., DiNapoli, M. A., Jr., & Kini, T. (2024). Teacher residencies: State and federal policy to support comprehensive teacher preparation. Learning Policy Institute & EdPrepLab. https://doi.org/10.54300/358.825

q2 · i? · review · r2

Cited in the report's introduction as background on turnover: "teachers with little to no pedagogical preparation were 2 to 3 times more likely to leave the profession than those with the most comprehensive preparation." The report cites several studies finding higher turnover for entrants without adequate preparation.

"One study found that teachers with little to no pedagogical preparation were 2 to 3 times more likely to leave the profession than those with the most comprehensive preparation, which includes student teaching, formal feedback on their teaching, and multiple courses in student learning and teaching."
