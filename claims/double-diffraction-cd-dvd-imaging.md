# A CD-DVD double-diffraction pair forms an inverted virtual image of an object in white light, with about 1% of the light usable

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When a first diffractive element has double the line density of a second (DVD vs CD, using the CD's second diffraction order), an observer near the second element sees a virtual, inverted image of an object placed before the first element. [→ José J. Lunazzi 2008](#jose-j-lunazzi-2008)
`q2 i?` Because each element's diffraction efficiency is about 10%, only about 1% of the light survives double diffraction, so the object must be strongly illuminated. [→ José J. Lunazzi 2008 (2)](#jose-j-lunazzi-2008-2)

## Evidence

### José J. Lunazzi 2008

José J. Lunazzi, Daniel S. F. Magalhães, Maria C. I. Amon, Noemi I. R. Rivera. (2008). Experimentos simples demostrando algunas propiedades de lentes difractivas y redes espirales. https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.0818 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0805.0818](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0805.0818)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Demonstration in section 5: two discs separated by 8,5 cm, object at 24,5 ±0,1 cm from the first disc, illuminated by a 50 W dichroic halogen lamp, produce an image that is "invertida y con alguna distorsión". The authors explain the inversion by opposite deviation directions of the two diffractions, yielding a virtual image.

> "La figura 13 nos muestr a la foto del objeto en form a de flecha y a su lado la imagen del mismo, invertida y con alguna distorsión."

### José J. Lunazzi 2008 (2)

José J. Lunazzi, Daniel S. F. Magalhães, Maria C. I. Amon, Noemi I. R. Rivera. (2008). Experimentos simples demostrando algunas propiedades de lentes difractivas y redes espirales. https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.0818 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0805.0818](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0805.0818)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Brightness constraint stated in section 5 for the double-diffraction imaging setup: with each element's efficiency "del orden del 10%", only 1% of the light is usable, requiring a strongly illuminated object (a 50 W halogen lamp) and a high-contrast white arrow on black background.

> "El fenómeno [17] utiliza luz doblemente difractada, como la eficiencia de cada elemento es del orden del 10%, solo 1% puede ser aprovechado y es necesario tener un objeto fuertemente iluminado."
