### Ozbey Furkan 2026

Ozbey Furkan, Yasa Yasin. (2026). Impact of self-confidence and personality on dental students’ radiographic interpretation skills. BMC Medical Education, 26, 207. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08481-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hayes' PROCESS Model 2 moderation analysis with 83 students. The article reports both interaction terms "were not statistically significant"; the gender interaction approached significance (p = 0.0531) but the data do not support systematic gender influence on confidence calibration.

> "Similarly, interaction terms for self-confidence × personality traits (B = − 0.01, p = 0.6040) and self-confidence × gender (B = − 0.15, p = 0.0531) were not statistically significant."
