Evidence¶

Genç Osman Ilhan and Şahin Oruç 2016¶

Genç Osman Ilhan and Şahin Oruç. (2016). Effect of the use of multimedia on students’ performance: A case study of social studies class. Educational Research and Reviews. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099996

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre/post comparison of the experimental group (N=35 per Table 1) taught the unit with multimedia. The article reports t(68) = -4.323, p=.000<.05, with means rising from 71.57 to 84.87; no effect size is printed.

"When the pre and post test results of the experimental group were compared, a significant difference is observed between the two tests [t(68) = -4.323, p=.000<.05]."
