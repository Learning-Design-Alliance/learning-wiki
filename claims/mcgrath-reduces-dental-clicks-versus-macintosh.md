# Dental clicks were less frequent with McGrath VL than Macintosh in both scenarios, suggesting reduced dental injury risk during novice training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the cervical immobilized manikin, dental click incidence was 22% (McGrath), 34% (Pentax) and 46% (Macintosh), p = 0.001, with Macintosh significantly higher than McGrath (p < 0.001). [→ Kyong YI 2020](#kyong-yi-2020)
`q3 i?` In the normal manikin, dental clicks were 19%, 30% and 36% (p = 0.022), with Macintosh significantly higher than McGrath (p = 0.005). [→ Kyong YI 2020](#kyong-yi-2020)
