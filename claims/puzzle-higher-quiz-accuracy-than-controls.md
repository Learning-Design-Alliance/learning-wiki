## Evidence

### Morén Constanza 2026

Morén Constanza, Mariscal Emi Ruiz, Negredo Mónica, Preciado María, Martínez Gemma, Llaurado-Serra Mireia, Rosa Antonio, González Melinda, Rodríguez Natàlia, Martínez Laura. (2026). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09484-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Educational intervention study with three intervention and two control groups among 330 second-year nursing students. Quiz administered within a two-week window in October 2024. Intervention groups showed "significantly higher accuracy percentages compared to the control groups".

> "Overall, the average accuracy among the intervention groups was calculated at 87.87 ± 11.39%, while the control groups displayed a lower average accuracy of 71.27% ± 11.24%. The results revealed a significant difference (t(32) = 4.01, p < 0.001)"
