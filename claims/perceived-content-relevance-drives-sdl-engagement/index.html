Review synthesis attributing this pattern to cited source studies (refs 22–26, 32); the review also reports that SDL activities demonstrating improved medical practice encouraged physician engagement.

"Physicians were more favorable toward the SDL program if they perceived the content to be useful and relevant to their clinical practice,22,26,32 but they were less likely to engage in the SDL program if they judged the content to be irrelevant to them.23,25"
