Evidence¶

Arulselvi 2013¶

Arulselvi, E. (2013). Effectiveness of Mutual Learning Approach in the Academic Achievement of B.Ed Students in Learning Optional II English. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 3(3), 25-31. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068866

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group comparison of the 25-member experimental group's pre-test and Post Test I means (45.7 vs. 68 as printed in Table 2). The article attributes the gain to "the effectiveness of Mutual learning approach."

"The experimental group shows significant difference between its pre- test and post-test -1 mean achievement scores ( 't' = 7.87, significant at 0.01 level)."

Arulselvi 2013 (2)¶

Arulselvi, E. (2013). Effectiveness of Mutual Learning Approach in the Academic Achievement of B.Ed Students in Learning Optional II English. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 3(3), 25-31. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068866

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group comparison of the experimental group's pre-test and Post Test II scores (45.7 vs. 70 per Table 4). The article states the group "shows better performance in Post test II than in its Pre test performance."

"Achievement scores of the Pre test and Post test II of the experimental group were compared and there exists significant difference between these two scores by the 't' value 8.83 which is significant at 0.01 level of significance."

Arulselvi 2013 (3)¶

Arulselvi, E. (2013). Effectiveness of Mutual Learning Approach in the Academic Achievement of B.Ed Students in Learning Optional II English. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 3(3), 25-31. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068866

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Comparison of the experimental group's two post-tests (68 vs. 70 as printed). The article observes performance was "equally better" across both tests, with Post Test II slightly higher descriptively.

"Achievement scores of Post test 1 and Post test II of the experimental group were compared and there exists no significant difference between these two scores by the 't' value 0.38 which is not significant at 0.01 level of significance."
