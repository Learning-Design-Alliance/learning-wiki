Evidence¶

Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan 2023¶

Zi Yan · Ernesto Panadero · Xiang Wang · Ying Zhan. (2023). A Systematic Review on Students' Perceptions of Self-Assessment: Usefulness and Factors Influencing Implementation. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09799-1

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of five studies on external feedback for implementation: students saw missing supervisor feedback as a barrier, and feedback from teachers "also increased the accuracy of self-assessment" per the review, citing Rees and Shepherd (2005).

"From the students' perspective, it is a barrier for them to complete a self-assessment with- out formative feedback from their supervisors."

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Orsmond and Merry (2013), that "high-achieving students preferred tutor feedback and were more likely to use it during self-assessment" than low achievers; peer feedback and e-feedback were also reported as helpful.

"Compared to low achiev - ers, high-achieving students preferred tutor feedback and were more likely to use it during self-assessment (23)."
