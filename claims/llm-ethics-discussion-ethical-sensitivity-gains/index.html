Pre-post paired t-tests in a user study with 45 undergraduate STEM students across 15 groups showed statistically significant gains in both ethical sensitivity sub-constructs, with "large effect sizes (Cohen’s𝑑 = 1.43 for awareness, Cohen’s𝑑 = 1.28 for reasoning)". Wilcoxon signed-rank tests corroborated the results.
