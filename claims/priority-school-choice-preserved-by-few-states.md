Daria Hall. (2013). A Step Forward or a Step Back? State Accountability in the Waiver Era. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543222

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The report's comparison of state waiver plans against the former NCLB choice requirement finds that "only a handful of states chose to preserve public school choice," naming Arizona, Colorado, Michigan, New York, Oklahoma, and South Carolina as offering Priority school choice.

> "Beyond states that already have broad choice laws (including Minnesota, Indiana, Florida, and Georgia) only a handful of states chose to preserve public school choice, even for students in their lowest performing schools."
