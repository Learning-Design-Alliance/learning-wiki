Examination study in Arithmetic, Geography, Grammar, History, and Spelling given to eighth-grade B Class pupils, with returns from 211 grade children and 177 country children analyzed separately by sex. Rural children scored higher in every branch; no effect size is printed.

> "When the results were compared it was found that the children from the country had received a higher average per cent, in every branch, not only in the "reasoning," but also in the "mechanical" group."
