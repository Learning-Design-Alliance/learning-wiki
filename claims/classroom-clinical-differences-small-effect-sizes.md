## Evidence

### Doyle Patricia 2015

Doyle Patricia, VanDenKerkhof Elizabeth G, Edge Dana S, Ginsburg Liane, Goldstein David H. (2015). Self-reported patient safety competence among Canadian medical students and postgraduate trainees: a cross-sectional survey. BMJ Quality & Safety. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjqs-2014-003142

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unpaired t tests comparing classroom and clinical H-PEPSS dimension scores among medical students; printed effect sizes range from 0.00 (culture of safety, p=0.58) to 0.26 (managing safety risks, p<0.01).

> "While there were statistically significant differences in mean patient safety dimension scores between the classroom and clinical setting, in most cases, the effect size was very small and, therefore, of low clinical significance."
