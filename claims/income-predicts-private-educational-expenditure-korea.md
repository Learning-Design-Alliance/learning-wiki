### Kang Ran 2026

Kang Ran. (2026). Household income and academic performance in Korean adolescents: A longitudinal test of dual investment pathways. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0353476

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal path model on the KCYPS 2018 Grade 4 cohort (N = 2,387) estimating the income-to-material-investment path with baseline expenditure, gender, age, and parental education controlled. The article reports "β = .08, p < .001" for this path.

> "Consistent with Hypothesis 1, household income at Wave 1 significantly predicted higher levels of material investment at Wave 2 (β = .08, p < .001, 95% CI [.04, .11])."
