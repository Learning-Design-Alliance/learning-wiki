Descriptive results from the same cross-sectional survey of 363 trainees on perceived challenges. "limited workspace at the computer laboratory" was the most cited challenge (33%), followed by internet/intranet speed (29%) and content relevance and faculty training (25% each).

> "The results show that 33 % of respondents perceived limited workspace at the computer laboratory as a challenge; 29 % perceived speed of internet and intranet (29 %); 25 % each perceived relevance of e-learning content and limited training of faculty (25 %) as challenges."
