## Evidence

### Krage Ralf 2017

Krage Ralf, Zwaan Laura, Tjon Soei Len Lian, Kolenbrander Mark W, van Groeningen Dick, Loer Stephan A, Wagner Cordula, Schober Patrick. (2017). Relationship between non-technical skills and technical performance during cardiopulmonary resuscitation: does stress have an influence? Emergency Medicine Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/emermed-2016-205754

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised cross-over simulator study of 30 anaesthesiologists and residents leading two CPR scenarios, one with radio noise and a distracting scripted family member. The article reports "significantly lower ANTS scores" with stressors, means 41.7±4.4 vs 45.6±4.0, adjusted mean difference 3.9 points.

> "Overall, significantly lower ANTS scores were observed in scenarios where external stressors where added, with and without adjustment for period effects (41.7±4.4 (mean±SD) vs 45.6±4.0, mean difference 3.9, unadjusted 95% CI 2.3 to 5.6, adjusted 95% CI 2.4 to 5.5"
