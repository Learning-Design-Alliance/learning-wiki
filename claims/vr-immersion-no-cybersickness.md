## Evidence

### Angélique Remacle 2023

Angélique Remacle, Stéphane Bouchard, Dominique Morsomme. (2023). Can teaching simulations in a virtual classroom help trainee teachers to develop oral communication skills and self-efficacy? A randomized controlled trial. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104808

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the experimental group, the Simulator Sickness Questionnaire was completed before and after the first VR immersion. Paired t-tests found no significant differences for the Total score, Nausea, or Oculo-motor subscales, indicating that "unwanted negative side effects were not induced".

> "Paired t-tests showed no significant differences for the SSQ Total score (t =1.33, p = .199), the Nausea subscale (t = .417, p = .681), and the Oculu-motor subscale (t = -1.4, p = .177) before and after the immersion in session 1, indicating that unwanted negative side effects were not induced."
