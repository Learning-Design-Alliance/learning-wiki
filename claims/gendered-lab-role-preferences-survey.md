# Women and men report different lab activity role preferences at semester start, with women preferring notes and managing and men preferring equipment and analysis

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Women more often prefer notes and managing roles while men more often prefer equipment, analysis, or no preferred role, controlling for course and lecture track. [→ Matthew Dew 2024](#matthew-dew-2024)

## Evidence

### Matthew Dew 2024

Matthew Dew, Emma Hunt, Viranga Perera, Jonathan Perry, Gregorio Ponti, and Andrew Loveridge. (2024). Group Dynamics in Inquiry-based Labs: Gender Inequities and the Efficacy of Partner Agreements. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010121](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010121)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preferences survey of 1,871 completed responses analyzed with logistic regressions controlling for course, track, and their interaction. The article reports "Women more often prefer notes" and managing, while men prefer equipment or analysis, with the printed p-values listed.

> "Women more often prefer notes ( p <0.001) and managing (p <0.001), while men more often prefer equipment (p = 0.024), analysis (p = 0.001) or have no preferred role (p = 0.004)."
