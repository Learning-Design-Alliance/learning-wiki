## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a cross-sectional study of 691 Chinese middle school students, growth mindset was significantly negatively associated with mathematics achievement (β = -0.0929, p < 0.05), while the total effect was not significant. [→ Dong Jia 2026](#dong-jia-2026)

## Evidence

### Dong Jia 2026

Dong Jia, Yang Xiqing, Yang Xi, Tan Dingliang, Wang Kexin. (2026). The relationship between growth mindset and mathematics achievement in Chinese middle school students: The role of achievement goals and grit. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05153-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 691 Grade 7 students from one Chinese public middle school using PROCESS Model 80 chain mediation analysis; the regression reported a significant negative direct association with mathematics achievement (β = -0.0929, p < 0.05).

> "growth mindset showed a significant positive association with grit (β = 0.1996, p < 0.001), but a significant negative association with mathematics achievement (β = -0.0929, p < 0.05)."

### Dong Jia 2026 (2)

Dong Jia, Yang Xiqing, Yang Xi, Tan Dingliang, Wang Kexin. (2026). The relationship between growth mindset and mathematics achievement in Chinese middle school students: The role of achievement goals and grit. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05153-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap chain mediation analysis (5000 resamples, 95% CI) in the same sample reported a non-significant total effect of growth mindset on mathematics achievement, consistent with the authors' competitive mediation interpretation.

> "Consequently, the total effect of growth mindset on mathematics achievement was not significant (Standardized β = -0.0100, SE = 0.3025, 95% CI [-0.6733, 0.5146])."
