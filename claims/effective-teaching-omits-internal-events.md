## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Within the MOT, effective teaching ignores internal events such as wandering thoughts, aversion, boredom, insecurity, and distrust, even though student behavior hinges on them. [→ Stanisław D. Głażek 2008](#stanisaw-d-gazek-2008)

## Evidence

### Stanisław D. Głażek 2008

Stanisław D. Głażek. (2008). Heuristic model of teaching. https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.4796 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0804.4796](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0804.4796)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Model-based analysis (Section IV.B): the article classifies events as external, internal, or engaging, and argues that effective teaching treats attitudes and engagement as given while omitting internal events such as boredom, feeling insecure, or not trusting the teacher. A theoretical claim of the model, not an empirical measurement.

> "the kinematical concept of eﬀective teaching in the model does not explicitly include a host of feelings, thoughts, attitudes, wants, and expectations that are important for the course and outcome of the process of teaching."
