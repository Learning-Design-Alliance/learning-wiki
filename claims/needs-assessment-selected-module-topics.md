`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Needs assessment survey conducted in December 2020 via Qualtrics, asking residents which topics they wanted covered in depth; requested topics included asthma management, cystic fibrosis, chronic cough, tracheostomy basics, OSA, and pulmonary function testing. The article states the four chosen topics were "based on these results".

> "The survey was distributed to pediatric residents at University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) and Oakland campus via the Qualtrics platform, with 66 responses collected."
