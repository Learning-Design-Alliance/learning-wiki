Hazlett, Z. S., Jimenez, P. C., & Knight, J. K. (2025). Self-Testing and Follow-Through of Learning Strategies Supports Student Success. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-04-0128

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square test of post-quiz intent responses categorized by quiz grade (Figure 1A) shows intent-to-change distributions differ significantly by grade; a companion test on intended new strategies found none (χ2 (9, N = 567) = 0.7, p > 0.05). No effect size is printed.
