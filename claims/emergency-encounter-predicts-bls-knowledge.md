## Evidence

### Khattak Osama 2026

Khattak Osama, Ehsan Aliya, Khalid Ayesha Akbar, Iqbal Azhar, Chaudhary Farooq Ahmad, Baig Muhammad Nadeem, Alsharari Thani, Memon Muhammad Rizwan. (2026). Assessment of clinical readiness, knowledge and attitude, regarding Basic Life Support (BLS) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) skill among dentists practicing in Saudi Arabia. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21098

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same adjusted binary logistic regression model on the 400-dentist survey, with emergency encounter entered as a predictor (ref: No). The printed effect size is an adjusted odds ratio, aOR = 12.97 (95% CI [6.99–24.06], p < 0.001), a very large association. The authors note prior emergency encounters were uniquely associated with knowledge, not confidence.

> "Similarly, dentists who had previously encountered an emergency were almost 13 times more likely to have good knowledge (aOR = 12.97; 95% CI [6.99–24.06]; p < 0.001)."
