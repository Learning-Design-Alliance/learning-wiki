## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors argue that dividing skill into three classes instead of two yields more precise, informative feedback for trainees, at the cost of lower classification metrics due to finer-grained variability. [→ Hoffmann Hanna 2024](#hoffmann-hanna-2024)
