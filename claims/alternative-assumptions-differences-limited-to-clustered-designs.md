## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the sensitivity analysis using RCT-YES default assumptions, model- and design-based results were similar but differences were slightly bigger than in the matched analysis, and these differences were limited to clustered designs. [→ Kautz 2017](#kautz-2017)

## Evidence

### Kautz 2017

Kautz, T., Schochet, P., & Tilley, C. (2017). Comparing Impact Findings from Design-Based and Model-Based Methods: An Empirical Investigation (NCEE 2017–4026). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED575021

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sensitivity analysis across the nine RCTs comparing the reference model-based method to the design-based method under RCT-YES default assumptions; the report attributes the additional differences to "weighting schemes and standard error calculations" rather than to the design-based framework itself.

> "These differences are limited to clustered designs and are consistent with differences in weighting schemes and standard error calculations between the default and alternative settings analyses."
