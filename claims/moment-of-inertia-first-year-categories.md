## Evidence

### J Guisasola 2024

J Guisasola, J Ametller, D Baccino, M Carli, S Kapon, A C Marti, M Monteiro, O Pantano, T Peer, P Sarriugarte, M Schvartzer, A Suárez and K Zuza. (2024). Connecting research in physics education, curriculum decisions and teaching practices. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.10674](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.10674)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Open-ended questionnaire (7 questions) given to 97 first-year engineering students at UPV/EHU after studying rigid-body rotation around a fixed axis; answers were categorized with inter-rater reliability Cohen's kappa κ = 0.92. Table 1 reports A1 34%, A2 11%, B 22%, C 24%, D 4%, E 4% for question Q1.

> "Around 25% of the answers (see Table 1) were explanations that focus their analysis on one of the two variables (mass and distance to the axis of rotation) that influence the moment of inertia and were classified as category C."
