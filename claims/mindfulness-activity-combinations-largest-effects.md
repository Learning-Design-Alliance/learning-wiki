`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analytic moderator analysis of program characteristics across the 24 included studies. The article reports that programs "consist[ing] of combinations of various mindfulness activities" had the largest effects; no separate effect size is printed in the available text.
