### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Regression of teacher expectation accuracy on pupil sex and SEN status (statemented vs not statemented) in the ALSPAC imputed sample at both Key Stages. The article reports "little evidence" for either factor, with printed coefficients such as male sex 0.010 (p = 0.710) at KS2.

> "There was little evidence children of differing sex or SEN status under/overperformed their teacher’s expectations."
