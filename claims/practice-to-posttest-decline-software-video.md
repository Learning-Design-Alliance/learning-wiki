## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2016

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij. (2016). Demonstration-based training (DBT) in the design of a video tutorial for software training. Instr Sci 44:527–542. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9394-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise comparisons within the repeated measures ANOVA on test scores in the quasi-experiment with 55 students. The article states "a considerable part of what was learned was also quickly forgotten."

> "there was a signiﬁcant decrease from practice to immediate post-test ( F(1, 53) = 38.37, p \ 0.001, d = 1.02), and that scores remained the same for the two post-tests ( F \ 1)."
