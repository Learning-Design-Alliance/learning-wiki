`q3 i3` The mean number of prompts given per scenario-protocol changed differently over the five series of problems (interaction partial η² = .83). [→ Bosma et al. 2017](#bosma-et-al-2017)

## Evidence

### Bosma et al. 2017

Tirza Bosma, Claire E. Stevenson, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2017). Differences in Need for Instruction: Dynamic Testing in Children with Arithmetic Difficulties. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2326

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures analysis with Series (1-5) and Scenario-protocol (A-F) as within-subjects variables in the trained group; the scenario × series interaction was significant with printed effect size partial η² = .83, and the change patterns are shown in Figure 2.

> "A significant interaction effect between scenario-protocols and series was found: F (20, 40) = 10.01, p < .001, partial η² = .83."
