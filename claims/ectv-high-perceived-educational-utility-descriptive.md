Educational utility of observational workplace-based assessment modalities in Australian vocational general practice training: a cross-sectional study. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of cross-sectional questionnaire responses collected soon after each ECTV (741 registrar and 696 ECT visitor observations after removing delayed responses). Percentages are printed as descriptive proportions; no effect size applies, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Most registrars (64.1%, n = 475/741) rated the overall educational utility of ECTVs as 'very useful', and 58.5% (n = 429/734) and 47.9% (n = 352/735) of registrars rated their likelihood to change practice and their approach to learning/training as 'very likely', respectively. Fewer ECT visitors rated overall educational utility as 'very useful' (33.8%, n = 235/696)."
