## Evidence

### Yedigöz-Kara 2022

Yedigöz-Kara, Z., & Bümen, N. T. (2022). The search for an effective curricular change adoption in foreign language education: A meta-synthesis. International Journal of Curriculum and Instructional Studies, 12(2), 337-366. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1373082

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The meta-synthesis found teachers complained about overloaded curricula regarding students' age and minority students, and needed space to adapt the curriculum according to their students' needs.

> "Since a demanding curriculum required more time, teachers had difficulty in allocating time for its implementation process, which was illustrated in the following: "…we don't have enough time to do so with so many teaching tasks to accomplish and a set of exercises to mark.""
