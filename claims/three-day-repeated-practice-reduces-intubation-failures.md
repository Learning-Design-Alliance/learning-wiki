# Intensive repeated practice over 3 days reduced Macintosh intubation failures to zero by day 3 on both manikins, indicating skill improvement with repeated performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Intubation failures with the Macintosh decreased progressively over 3 days (2 to 1 to 0 on AirSim; 3 to 2 to 0 on AMT), and the article states intensive repeated performance over 3 days facilitates skill improvement. [→ Murakami Y 2021](#murakami-y-2021)

## Evidence

### Murakami Y 2021

Murakami Y, Ueki R, Niki M, Hirose M, Shimode N. (2021). Three-day tracheal intubation manikin training for novice doctors using Macintosh laryngoscope, McGRATH MAC videolaryngoscope and Pentax AirwayScope. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000023886

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Author interpretation of the failure counts across the 3-day study with 15 novice doctors: "the number of intubation failures decreased from 3 on day 1, to 2 on day 2 and zero failures on day 3" (AMT, Mac group), with a parallel trend on AirSim. No effect size printed.

> "Our study also reveals that intensive training by repeated performance of intubation procedures over a 3-day training period facilitates skill improvement and enhances acquisition of the skills required for endotracheal intubation."
