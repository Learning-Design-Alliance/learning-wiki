## Evidence

### Luc Paquette 2015

Luc Paquette, Jean-François Lebeau, Gabriel Beaulieu & André Mayers. (2015). Designing a Knowledge Representation Approach for the Generation of Pedagogical Interventions by MTTs. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 25:118–156. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0030-z

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design-level analysis of MTT frameworks (TDK, CTAT) versus Astus presented in the article's introduction. The authoring-process comparison shows that CTAT-style tutors embed hint templates in task-model production rules, whereas Astus generates hint content from the task model, as stated: "those frameworks require that their pedagogical content" be authored while Astus generates it.

> "those frameworks require that their pedagogical content (hint messages and common errors) be included in the model of the task whereas Astus ’s pedagogical module automatically generates it"

### Luc Paquette 2015 (2)

Luc Paquette, Jean-François Lebeau, Gabriel Beaulieu & André Mayers. (2015). Designing a Knowledge Representation Approach for the Generation of Pedagogical Interventions by MTTs. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 25:118–156. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0030-z

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analytical description of Astus's hint generation mechanism in the Model Tracing in Astus section. The episodic tree, instantiated from the procedural graph according to the state of the knowledge base, lets generated hints reflect completed and waiting sub-goals, so that "Astus to contextualize the hints it generates according to the current state of the task case."

> "Using the content of the episodic tree allows Astus to contextualize the hints it generates according to the current state of the task case."
