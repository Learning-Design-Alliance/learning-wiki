# A generalized linear model of status-exam performance shows a highly significant effect of the drillset in which the question is seen

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a generalized linear model of status exam grades, there is a highly significant effect of where the student sees the question, i.e. which drillset it appears in, alongside significant student, repetition and question-difficulty effects. [→ G. Stefansson 2022](#g-stefansson-2022)

## Evidence

### G. Stefansson 2022

G. Stefansson, J. Lentin, A. H. Jonsdottir, E. Eiríksson, A. Kristjánsson. (2022). Mathematics and Incentives in the Slums. [doi:10.21125/inted.2022.0584](https://doi.org/10.21125/inted.2022.0584)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Generalized linear model analysis of almost 200 thousand status exam allocations out of some 3 million drill item allocations, from the experiment start through January 2022. The article reports "a highly significant effect of where the student sees the question" with no printed effect size.

> "Incorporating all these effects into a generalised linear model demonstrates highly significant student effects as well as the importance of repetition and the difference in the difficulty of the status exam questions. But most importantly, there is a highly significant effect of where the student sees the question"
