Evidence¶

Hsin-Yun Wang & Jerry Chih-Yuan Sun 2026¶

Hsin-Yun Wang & Jerry Chih-Yuan Sun. (2026). Understanding online knowledge communities via social networks and self-regulation in synchronous VR co-creation for distance learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev, 74, 1037–1061. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10588-0

q2 · i? · causal · r2

One-mode SNA of aggregate networks over four weeks for 22 students per group, computed in UCINET. The study reports "the number of ties (3D VR: 110; 2D: 71)" and the other printed measures; no effect size was printed for this network comparison.

"The interactivity and cohesiveness of the 3D VR network were higher than those of the 2D digital network, as shown by the number of ties (3D VR: 110; 2D: 71), the degrees of the average linkage (3D VR: 5; 2D: 3.227), the centralization (3D VR: 0.052; 2D: 0.038), and the density (3D VR: 0.238; 2D: 0.154)."
