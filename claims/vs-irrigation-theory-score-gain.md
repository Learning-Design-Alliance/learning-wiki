## Evidence

### Yue Xin 2025

Yue Xin, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Sun Jianping, Xu Li, Shen Jing. (2025). Evaluation of a virtual simulation system for root canal irrigation training in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08387-x

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Single-group pre-post study of 34 dental students; paired-sample t-test comparing theoretical quiz scores before and after virtual simulation training. The article reports "a mean increase of 9.3%" with t(33) = 3.82, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 0.65.

> "Post-training theoretical scores (S2) were significantly higher than pre-training scores (S1), with a mean increase of 9.3% (t(33) = 3.82, p < 0.001; Cohen's d = 0.65)."
