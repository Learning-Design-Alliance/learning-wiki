## Evidence

### Ebrahim Sumayyah 2024

Ebrahim Sumayyah, Kinoo Suman Mewa, Naidoo Maheshwar, Van Wyk Jacqueline Marina. (2024). The use of household items to support online surgical knot-tying skills training: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05549-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional online questionnaire of 71 final-year medical students who completed the remote knot-tying exercise. Most "expressed confidence in their ability to perform the skill" and could follow the demonstration; median practice repetitions before submission were 7.0 (IQR 5.0–10.0).

> "83.1% (59/71) of students were able to follow the steps in the video demonstration, and 91.5% (65/71) expressed confidence in their ability to perform the skill. Median number of times needed to practice before video submission was 7.0 (5.0–10.0)."
