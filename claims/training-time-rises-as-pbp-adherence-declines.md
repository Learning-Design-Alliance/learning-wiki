`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the OSSET randomised trial, mean hands-on training time to proficiency rose monotonically with declining PBP adherence: Group 1 2.96 h, Group 2 3.38 h (+14%), Group 3 6.00 h (+103%), Group 4 7.75 h (+162%).

> "Group 2 required a mean of 3.38 h (+14%) to reach the benchmark, which translated into a median of 1.25 full days in the skills laboratory (mean 1.29 days)"
