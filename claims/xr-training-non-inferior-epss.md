## Evidence

### Mulder Tessa A. 2026

Mulder Tessa A., Jacobs Robin C., Zuurmond Manon S., Qaddoumi Adel, Regeer Madelien V., Dekker Friedo W., Bauer Martijn P., Srámek Alexandr, Langers Alexandra M.J., Hierck Beerend P. (2026). Augmedicine: Ultrasound: Training in ultrasound using XR – a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08897-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this RCT, 56 medical students were randomized to XR-based or standard POCUS training. The EPSS non-inferiority analysis showed a "mean difference 0.3 mm; 95% CI -0.8 to 1.3 mm", remaining within the predefined 3 mm margin.

> "The mean difference from the expert value for the EPSS measurement was 2.7 mm (SD 1.7) in the XR group and 2.5 mm (SD 2.2) in the standard training group (mean difference 0.3 mm; 95% CI -0.8 to 1.3 mm)"
