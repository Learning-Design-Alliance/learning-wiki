## Evidence

### Gyawali Rajendra 2019

Gyawali Rajendra, Bhayal Bharat Kumar. (2019). Practice scope and job confidence of two-year trained optometry technicians in Eritrea. BMC Medical Education, 19, 303. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1738-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-administered cross-sectional questionnaire administered in January 2017 to 70 government-employed optometry technicians identified through the Ministry of Health database. The results report percentages of OTs routinely involved in each practice area, including "dispensing (62.9%)" and "clinical examination of patients (ocular health examination) (35.7%)".

> "The OTs reported being routinely involved in refraction (100%), dispensing (62.9%); clinical examination of patients (ocular health examination) (35.7%); and diagnostic services including visual field examination (22.9%) among other areas."
