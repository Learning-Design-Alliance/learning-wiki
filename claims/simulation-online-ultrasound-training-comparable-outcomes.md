Fernández-Carnero Samuel, Diaz-Pulido Belen, Méndez-Rodriguez Jorge, Pecos-Martin Daniel, Garcia-Miguel Santiago, Achalandabaso-Ochoa Alexander, Cuenca-Zaldívar Nicolas, Naranjo-Cinto Sr Fermín. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized blinded trial with 68 physiotherapy students (n=34 per group) assigned to the WAZO simulation-based online platform or traditional on-site instruction. The study reports "No significant differences were found between the online and on-site groups in theoretical scores" and practical performance variables, supporting equivalence of outcomes.

> "No significant differences were found between the online and on-site groups in theoretical scores (mean 4.94, SD 1.47 vs mean 5.08, SD 1.14; P=.65) or practical performance variables"
