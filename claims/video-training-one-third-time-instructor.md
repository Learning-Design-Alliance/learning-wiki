## Evidence

### Christensen L 2020

Christensen L, Rasmussen CS, Benfield T, Franc JM. (2020). A Randomized Trial of Instructor-Led Training Versus Video Lesson in Training Health Care Providers in Proper Donning and Doffing of Personal Protective Equipment. Disaster Medicine and Public Health Preparedness. https://doi.org/10.1017/dmp.2020.56

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Time-use data from the randomized pilot trial: video group viewing was logged on calendars, averaging 55 minutes total, while instructor sessions of 1–4 participants took 120–180 minutes. The discussion states video training took "only around one-third of the time taken for a training session with an instructor."

> "The average amounts of time spent watching the videos during 1 month were 23 minutes for the donning video and 32 minutes for the doffing video, yielding an average total video training time of 55 minutes. For the instructor (control) group, the training sessions with 1–4 participants took 120–180 minutes."
