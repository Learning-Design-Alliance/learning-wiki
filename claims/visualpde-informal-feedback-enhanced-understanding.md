Teaching-use report from §5.1: the authors used VisualPDE demonstrations in lectures and via the course's content management system. They report that "Initial informal feedback from students suggests that VisualPDE has significantly enhanced students' understanding of a range of topics in the course." No assessment data, comparison group, or effect size is reported.

> "Two of the authors of this manuscript have begun making extensive use of VisualPDE in teaching a third year undergraduate mathematical biology course at Durham University, providing links and interactive demonstrations of VisualPDE simulations in lectures and via the course's content management system."
