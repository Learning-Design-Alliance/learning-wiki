## Evidence

### Zhang Dandan 2024

Zhang Dandan, Wang Xiaoxue, Chen Xueting, Lou Yingze, Zhou Xin, Zhang Kai. (2024). Optimizing resident training in obstetrics and gynecology: a new perspective on the refined Peyton four-step teaching method. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05851-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous online satisfaction survey within one week after training, all 116 residents responding, on a 5-point Likert questionnaire with Cronbach's alpha 0.852. The article reports the RP group "scored higher than the TTM group in all nine items" with P < 0.05; no effect size is printed.

> "The RP group scored higher than the TTM group in all nine items, including stimulating learning interest and enhancing self-learning ability, with all differences being statistically significant (P < 0.05), as shown in Table 4."
