## Evidence

### Andersen Nina Bjerre 2014

Andersen Nina Bjerre, O'Neill Lotte, Gormsen Lise Kirstine, Hvidberg Line, Morcke Anne Mette. (2014). A validation study of the psychometric properties of the Groningen Reflection Ability Scale. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-214

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of GRAS-DK scores from 361 respondents (mean 88, SD = 11.42), which were approximately normally distributed. The article reports "Cronbach's alpha was 0.87"; some items had more than 40% of answers in the lowest or highest categories.

> "Cronbach's alpha was 0.87 and the average inter-item covariance was 0.22. The distribution of GRAS-DK scores showed no overall floor or ceiling effect."
