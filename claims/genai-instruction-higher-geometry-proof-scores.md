# Senior high school students taught with generative AI-supported instruction scored significantly higher on geometric reasoning and proof construction than students taught conventionally

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Post-test geometric reasoning and proof scores were significantly higher for the generative AI-supported group than the conventional group, with a large effect size. [→ Davor I 2026](#davor-i-2026)

## Evidence

### Davor I 2026

Davor I (2026) Generative AI-supported instruction as a cognitive scaffold: effects on senior high school students' geometric reasoning and proof construction. Front. Educ. 11:1869961. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1869961

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on post-test scores in a quasi-experimental pre-test-post-test non-equivalent control group study of 86 students (43 per group) in a public senior high school in Ghana. The experimental group mean was "21.74" versus 16.79 for control, t (84) = −4.999, p < .001; the article reports Cohen's d ≈ 1.05 as large.

> "The average score for the experimental group was 21.74, and the standard deviation was 4.42. On the other hand, the average score for the control group was 16.79, and the standard deviation was 4.76. The t-test was used, and the results were t (84) = −4.999, p < .001."
