# Interactive tutoring from a human tutor or supervised LearnLM outperformed static hints on immediate mistake remediation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students who received interactive support from a human tutor solved the problem on their second attempt at a rate of 91.2%, versus 65.4% for students receiving a static hint. [→ LearnLM Team 2025](#learnlm-team-2025)

## Evidence

### LearnLM Team 2025

LearnLM Team, Google & Eedi. (2025). AI tutoring can safely and effectively support students: An exploratory RCT in UK classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.23633](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.23633)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian RCT analysis of Eedi platform data from the trial (N=165 students, five UK secondary schools) comparing second-attempt success after an intervention. Human-tutored students succeeded at 91.2%, "compared to only 65.4% [63.8%, 66.9%] of students who received a static hint."

> "91.2% of students who received interactive support from a human tutor solved the problem correctly on their second attempt (with a 95% credible interval of [88.5%, 93.6%]), compared to only 65.4% [63.8%, 66.9%] of students who received a static hint."

### LearnLM Team 2025 (2)

LearnLM Team, Google & Eedi. (2025). AI tutoring can safely and effectively support students: An exploratory RCT in UK classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.23633](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.23633)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same RCT, in which tutoring sessions were randomized between a human tutor alone and a supervised LearnLM session. LearnLM-supervised students answered correctly "93.0% [90.4%, 95.3%] of the time" on their second attempt.

> "Students receiving guidance from LearnLM answered their second attempt correctly 93.0% [90.4%, 95.3%] of the time."
