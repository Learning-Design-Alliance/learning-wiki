## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Removing the five studies outside the Galbraith plot boundaries reduced heterogeneity to non-obvious levels (I2 = 26.807%, p = 0.251) while the PBL advantage remained significant (Hedges' g = 0.592, 95% CI 0.415–0.770, p<0.001, fixed effect model). [→ Gao Junwei 2020](#gao-junwei-2020)

## Evidence

### Gao Junwei 2020

Gao Junwei, Yang Ling, Zhao Jinghui, Wang Lian, Zou Jiao, Wang Chunxiang, Fan Xiaotang. (2020). Comparison of problem-based learning and traditional teaching methods in medical psychology education in China: A systematic review and meta-analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0243897

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Sensitivity-oriented re-analysis of four remaining studies (250 cases, 256 controls) after a Galbraith plot identified five studies affecting heterogeneity. The pooled fixed-effect estimate was "Hedges’ g = 0.592", still significantly favoring PBL.

> "After removing them, no obvious heterogeneity was found across studies (I2 = 26.807%, p = 0.251). A forest plot indicated that the total examination scores of students in the PBL group were still significantly higher than those of controls (Hedges’ g = 0.592, 95% CI 0.415–0.770, p<0.001)"
