Across the three qualitative studies compared via the Domains of Power framework, the disciplinary domain differed starkly. Study 1 faculty were "reprimanding students who failed to work equitably in groups"; a Study 2 student urged instructors to confront microaggressions; Study 3 women said faculty seemed oblivious to issues between students.
