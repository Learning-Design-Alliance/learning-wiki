## Evidence

### McCaffrey 2011

McCaffrey, D. F. and Lockwood, J. R. (2011). Missing data in value-added modeling of teacher effects. The Annals of Applied Statistics, 5(2A), 773-797. https://doi.org/10.1214/10-AOAS405

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian analysis of vertically linked grade 1-5 mathematics scores (1998-2002) from a large urban district, 9,295 students with 31 response patterns. Comparing SEL (random effects selection) and MAR posterior mean teacher effects: "0.99, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00 and 1.00 for teacher s from grade 1 to 5"; no effect size is printed.

> "The correlation between estima ted eﬀects from the two models was 0.99, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00 and 1.00 for teacher s from grade 1 to 5, respectively."

### McCaffrey 2011 (2)

McCaffrey, D. F. and Lockwood, J. R. (2011). Missing data in value-added modeling of teacher effects. The Annals of Applied Statistics, 5(2A), 773-797. https://doi.org/10.1214/10-AOAS405

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same district data analysis, comparing pattern mixture (PMIX) and MAR posterior mean teacher effects across grades; correlations "range from 0. 98 to 1.00 across grades". No effect size printed.

> "The correlations between the two sets of estimates range from 0. 98 to 1.00 across grades."
