`q1 · i?` · `review · r?`

An authors' historical note about the Cognitive Tutor Algebra deployment: instructors discouraged worked examples in both text and tutor, fearing shallow processing would impede deeper conceptual understanding expected from classroom discussion and collaborative projects.

> "Use of worked examples in the Cognitive Tutor Algebra course, both in text materials and in the tutor, were discouraged by our collaborating instructors (cf., Koedinger et al. 1997) because it was thought that urban students, if they processed the examples at all, would do so shallowly, which would impede deeper conceptual understanding"
