## Evidence

### Rotgans 2011

Rotgans, J. I., & Schmidt, H. G. (2011). Cognitive engagement in the problem-based learning classroom. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 16(4), 465–479. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9272-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pairwise LSD comparisons following the repeated measures ANOVA in the 208-student Study 2 sample. The article reports M(1) = 3.33 vs. M(3) = 3.59, p < .01 and M(2) = 3.37 vs. M(3) = 3.59, p < .01, contradicting the expectation that self-study would drive the first increase. Only means and p-values are printed, no effect size.

> "The results suggest that students' level of cognitive engagement increases not so much during the initial self-study phase, as we had expected, but when they come together and share their findings with each other and the tutor."
