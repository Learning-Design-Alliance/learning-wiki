### Dundar Sari Makbule Buse 2026

Dundar Sari Makbule Buse, Sezer Berkant. (2026). Comparative performance evaluation of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini Advanced in the Turkish Dentistry Specialization Exam. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08621-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square comparison of the two chatbots on the clinical dental sciences portion of the DUS dataset (eight clinical disciplines). Accuracy was lower than in fundamental sciences, and discipline-specific differences were more pronounced.

> "ChatGPT-4o achieved a significantly higher total accuracy in clinical dental sciences at 79.5% (95% CI: 76.9–81.9) compared to Gemini Advanced at 75.8% (95% CI: 73–78.3) (p = 0.046), corresponding to an absolute difference of 3.7%."
