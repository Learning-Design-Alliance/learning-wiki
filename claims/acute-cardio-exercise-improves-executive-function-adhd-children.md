### Den Heijer 2017 (2)

Den Heijer, A. E., Groen, Y., Tucha, L., Fuermaier, A. B. M., Koerts, J., Lange, K. W., Thome, J., & Tucha, O. (2017). Sweat it out? The effects of physical exercise on cognition and behavior in children and adults with ADHD: a systematic literature review. J Neural Transm 124(Suppl 1):S3–S26. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00702-016-1593-7

`q2 · i?`

The review reports improved academic performance "in both children with ADHD and healthy children following physical exercise, including enhanced levels of reading comprehension and arithmetics". It also notes unchanged performance in verbal short-term and working memory, spelling, reading, and color naming.

> "Academic performance was also found to be improved in both children with ADHD and healthy children following physical exercise, including enhanced levels of reading comprehension and arithmetics (Pontifex et al. 2013)"
