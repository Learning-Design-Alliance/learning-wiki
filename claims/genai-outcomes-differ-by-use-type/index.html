Evidence¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Descriptive between-group comparisons from the randomized experiment, classifying treated AI users' ChatGPT conversation logs as automation, augmentation, mixed, or other. The article reports these patterns are "consistent with the model’s prediction that what students learn from AI depends on how they use it."

"Automation and mixed users submit higher-rated essays in Session One, but automation users’ advantage is absent from their unaided essays one week later. Augmentation and mixed users show positive test-score differences relative to the control group in both sessions"
