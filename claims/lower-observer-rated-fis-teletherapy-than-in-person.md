## Evidence

### Lin 2025

Lin, T., Aafjes‐van Doorn, K., Heckman, T. G., Antebi‐Lerman, E., & Anderson, T. (2025). Are Therapists Less Skilful in Teletherapy Than In‐Person Therapy Scenarios? A Latent Profile Analysis of Facilitative Interpersonal Skills. Clinical Psychology & Psychotherapy. https://doi.org/10.1002/cpp.70047

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Paired comparison of trained coders' ratings of 223 therapists' videotaped responses to standardised challenging-scenario clips in tele‐FIS versus in-person FIS tasks. The study reports "p < 0.01, d = 0.12" for the total FIS difference.

> "Therapists had lower levels of observer‐rated FIS in teletherapy scenarios than in‐person therapy scenarios (p < 0.01, d = 0.12)."
