## Evidence

### Parchment Amelia 2025

Parchment Amelia, Lawrence Wendy, Rahman Em, Townsend Nick, Wainwright Elaine, Wainwright David. (2025). Increasing confidence and competence in supporting behaviour change in physiotherapy practice using Making Every Contact Count Healthy Conversation Skills: a before and after evaluation. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12513-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U test comparing trained (n participants = 11, n responses = 44) versus treatment-as-usual (n participants = 12, n responses = 48) physiotherapists' typed responses to patient statements at 6-month follow-up (U = 402, p < 0.001). The reference group completed measures only at 6 months.

> "At this time point, 91% of responses of MECC HCS trained physiotherapists were open discovery questions, compared to 31% of responses of those who had not been trained. Reference group physiotherapists responded with mostly telling/suggesting and closed questions."
