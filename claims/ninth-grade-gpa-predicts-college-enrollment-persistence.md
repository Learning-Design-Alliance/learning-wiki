# Ninth-grade GPA predicts college enrollment and one-year college persistence in linear, incremental patterns

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adjusted freshman GPA predicts college enrollment (about 18 percent of F students vs 70 percent of A students enrolling) and, conditional on enrolling, one-year persistence. [→ Easton 2017](#easton-2017)

## Evidence

### Easton 2017

Easton, J.Q., Johnson, E., & Sartain, L. (2017). The predictive power of ninth-grade GPA. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/predictive-power-ninth-grade-gpa

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Analysis of CPS records linked to National Student Clearinghouse data (2008-10 cohorts), with fixed effects and controls; enrollment coded unconditional on graduation. Quote reports the printed enrollment rates by GPA category; persistence (2007-09 cohorts, conditional on enrollment) followed the same linear pattern.

> "about 18 percent of students who had an F freshman GPA went on to college. About 35 percent of students with Ds went to college; about 50 percent of students with Cs did. B and A students fared better: 60 percent of B students went to college and 70 percent of A students did."
