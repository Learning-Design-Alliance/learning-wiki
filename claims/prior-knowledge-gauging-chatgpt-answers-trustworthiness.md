### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2025

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, and Jonas Enger. (2025). Generative AI as a lab partner: a case study. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/ggy1-3kjk](https://doi.org/10.1103/ggy1-3kjk)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case study discussion of seven Swedish high school lab groups (n=19); authors report that "students require knowledge about the underlying physics" to gauge ChatGPT answer trustworthiness, exemplified by Group 6 spotting a faulty gas constant.

> "our findings find that, in agreement with previous research [6], students require knowledge about the underlying physics in order for them to gauge whether answers provided by ChatGPT were trustworthy."
