# SBS-trained students outperform VBT-trained students on technical skills in a simulated perioperative anaphylaxis scenario one week after training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the simulation scenario test 7 days after training, the SBS group's median checklist technical skills score (33.5, IQR 31.0–36.0) was significantly higher than the VBT group's (28.0, IQR 26.0–30.0), p<0.001. [→ Yang Xiuli 2026](#yang-xiuli-2026)

## Evidence

### Yang Xiuli 2026

Yang Xiuli, Jiang Hui, Wang Huan, Wang Lixia, Chen Lijian. (2026). Effect of scenario-based simulation on students’ learning outcomes and satisfaction in perioperative anaphylaxis: a pilot randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2025-003713

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Seven days after training, all students managed a novel perioperative anaphylaxis scenario on a full-body simulator, assessed blind with a checklist technical skills score (range 10–40) by two calibrated tutors. The SBS group scored "33.5 (31.0–36.0) vs 28.0 (26.0–30.0), p<0.001".

> "The median checklist technical score was significantly higher in the SBS group compared with the VBT group (33.5 (31.0–36.0) vs 28.0 (26.0–30.0), p<0.001)."
