# Teacher ratings favored control for independent learning but SDTM for doctor–patient communication

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Clinical teachers rated SDTM residents higher on doctor–patient communication (d = 0.90) but lower on independent learning capability than controls (d = 0.53). [→ Yang Liu 2022](#yang-liu-2022)

## Evidence

### Yang Liu 2022

Yang Liu, Li Wen, Zou Jian, An Junnan, Zeng Bin, Zheng Yitao, Yang Jiming, Ren Jia. (2022). The application of the spot the difference teaching method in clinical skills training for residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03612-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Blinded clinical teachers' subjective ratings after rotation: communication 28.00 vs 27.00 (max 30), Z = -3.685, d = 0.90; independent learning 27.00 vs 28.00, Z = -2.308, P = 0.021, d = 0.53; total scores did not differ (P = 0.415, d = 0.18).

> "the comparison of independent learning capability showed that the control group’s performance was better than that of the SDTM group (P < 0.005) with a medium effect size (d= 0.53). Notably, the SDTM group’s doctor–patient communication capability received a higher rating than the control group’s (P < 0.005) with a large effect size (d = 0.90)."
