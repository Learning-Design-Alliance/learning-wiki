## Evidence

### Erdem Onan 2024

Erdem Onan, Felicitas Biwer, Roman Abel, Wisnu Wiradhany & Anique de Bruin. (2024). Optimizing self-organized study orders: combining refutations and metacognitive prompts improves the use of interleaved practice. npj Science of Learning 9:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00245-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Within the intervention phase, all participants studied 12 artists' painting styles, six via blocked and six via interleaved practice (counterbalanced), then took a 24-trial classification test. The paired t-test replicated the interleaving effect with d = 1.05.

> "A paired sample t-test revealed that interleaved practice ( M = 6.56, SD = 2.97) resulted in better classi ﬁcation performance than blocked practice (M = 3.57, SD = 2.40), t(90) = 10.00, p < 0.001, d = 1.05, replicating the well-established interleaving effect in learning painting styles."
