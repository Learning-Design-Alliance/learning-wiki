### Sibieta 2016

Luke Sibieta. (2016). REACH: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581275

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary composite reading comprehension outcome (YARC-RC and WIAT II) in the randomised controlled trial. The report states the comprehension effect "was much smaller" and more likely due to chance, and that "It is therefore not possible to say with confidence that the REACH interventions improve reading comprehension." No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "However, the impact of the interventions on pupils’ reading comprehension in particular, measured using a combination of reading comprehension tests, was much smaller. These effects are also more likely to have occurred by chance."
