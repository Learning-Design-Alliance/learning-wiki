## Evidence

### Woodbury J 2024

Woodbury J, Offerdahl E. (2024). Mind the Gap: Investigating Disaggregated Student Performance in an Upper-Division Biochemistry Course Following Team-Based Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education 23(3):ar39. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-02-0090

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Models of BCI normalized change (pre/post Biochemistry Concept Inventory, −1 to 1) and final course grades across three TBL semesters. The article states the adjusted R2 values for these models were very low; exploratory modeling found BCI prescores did not explain grades.

> "Students’ average iRAT scores across the course significantly explained a small amount of the BCI normalized change outcome (β = 0.006, Supplemental Materials). None of the selected factors significantly explained course grades."
