## Evidence

### Anishan V 2024

Anishan V, Konge L, Bjerrum F. (2024). Variable practice is superior to self-directed training for laparoscopic simulator training: a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10688-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

SIM-TLX questionnaires were completed after the first and final intervention sessions by all 36 participants. First-session scores included Mental Demands 75 vs 58, Frustration 81 vs 60, Task Complexity 79 vs 57; the variable group "experienced a significantly higher amount of mental demand, frustration, and perceived task complexity" (all p < 0.001).
