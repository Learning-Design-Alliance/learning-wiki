### Laurens Bollen 2015

Laurens Bollen, Paul van Kampen, and Mieke De Cock. (2015). Students’ difficulties with vector calculus in electrodynamics. arXiv preprint arXiv:1502.02830v2 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pretest graphical-interpretation question (N=30) showing four 2D vector fields; results plotted in Fig. 1. The authors note many students did not answer (about 20-30% for divergence, 30-40% for curl), suggesting they did not know how to solve these problems.

> "For Field 4, half of the students gave a correct answer, but for the other three vector ﬁelds less than one out of four ﬁgured out correctly where the divergence and curl are non-zero."
