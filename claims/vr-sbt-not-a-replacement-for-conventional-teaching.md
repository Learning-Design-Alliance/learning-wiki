In its synthesis of the RCT evidence, the review states there is "no strong evidence to suggest the use of SBT as a replacement for conventional training", citing the simulator's limits in mimicking real-life variables and complexities.

> "There is, however, no strong evidence to suggest the use of SBT as a replacement for conventional training, giving the limitation of a model in mimicking real life variables and complexities."
