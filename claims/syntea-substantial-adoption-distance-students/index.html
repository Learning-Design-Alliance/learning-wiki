Kristina Schaaff 2026¶

Kristina Schaaff, Quintus Stierstorfer and Valerie Hekkel. (2026). Using AI-based Learning Assistants in Higher Education: A Large-Scale Descriptive Analysis. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08748

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive log-data analysis of all Bachelor's and Master's distance students active in February 2025 at IU, after cleaning removed 78 incomplete records. The article reports "44,035 out of the 76,485 students in our sample used Syntea", with 2,509 first-time and 41,526 returning users; no effect size is printed.

"During our observation period, a total of 44,035 out of the 76,485 students in our sample used Syntea. 2,509 students used Syntea for the first time during this period, while 41,526 students were returning users."
