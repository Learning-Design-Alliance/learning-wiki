`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey of 89 trainee nurses and 80 clinical instructors using a 20-item, 5-point effectiveness scale (Cronbach's α = 0.941). Ratings were interpreted against the article's printed mean-value bands, with 'very effective' spanning 4.21–5.00.

> "Quantitative data show that trainee nurses and clinical instructors evaluated training objectives, clinical instructors and training schedule as 'very effective'. Trainee nurses' evaluation of training content was 'effective', whereas clinical instructors' evaluation of training content was 'very effective'."
