## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The four successful teams pursued and refined a shared epistemic object through sketching, prototyping and experimentation, while the Plant team never formed a comprehensive epistemic object and its efforts remained scattered. [→ Riikonen 2020](#riikonen-2020)

### Riikonen 2020

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-case comparison of the five teams at the intermediate analysis level, which combined Process-Rug results, field notes and artifact documentation; "both success and collaboration within teams resulted when the team members shared the same envisioned epistemic object."

> "In contrast with the successful teams, the Plant team did not work out a comprehensive epistemic object and, consequently, their efforts remained scattered and the invention did not advance."
