`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the multilevel models on political knowledge, the direct path from open classroom climate was not significant. The article notes the literature also contains null findings (Geboers et al., 2013) and that a prior 38-country study found the relation significant for only 25 of 38 countries.

> "Surprisingly, we did not find an association between an open classroom climate and political knowledge."
