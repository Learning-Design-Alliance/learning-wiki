## Evidence

### Mason Ron 2020

Mason Ron, Doherty Kathleen, Eccleston Claire, Winbolt Margaret, Long Marita, Robinson Andrew. (2020). Effect of a dementia education intervention on the confidence and attitudes of general practitioners in Australia: a pretest post-test study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033218

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples comparison between the 332 registrars and 114 supervisors in the pretest post-test study. The authors report that "GPRs exhibited a significantly greater improvement in score than supervisors"; no effect size is printed for this between-group difference.

> "GPRs exhibited a significantly greater improvement in score than supervisors (t(414)=3.797; p<0.001). The effect size of the change in Confidence in Clinical Abilities was strong for both groups and greatest for GPRs."
