## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Discrimination coefficients calculated against domain-specific sub-scores rose to above 0.2 for most items, while overall-test discrimination coefficients were low. [→ Buljan Ivan 2021](#buljan-ivan-2021)

## Evidence

### Buljan Ivan 2021

Buljan Ivan, Marušić Matko, Tokalić Ružica, Viđak Marin, Peričić Tina Poklepović, Hren Darko, Marušić Ana. (2021). Cognitive levels in testing knowledge in evidence-based medicine: a cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02449-y

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item analysis comparing top-performing 25% and bottom-performing 25% of the 203 participants. Overall-test discrimination median was 0.20 (95% CI 0.15 to 0.25), whereas domain-specific medians reached 0.52 (learning domain) and 0.50 (EBM topic), an acceptable level above 0.2.

> "The test items had low discrimination coefficients when they were calculated for the overall test scores. However, when the discrimination coefficients for each question were calculated based on the scores on an individual EBM topic or cognitive level, the discrimination coefficients increased to an acceptable level (over 0.2) [19] (Table 2)."
