Thematic analysis of a 90-minute semi-structured focus group with four of six first-year Physics Ph.D. students. A participant described the department as "shockingly healthy for a physics department" with "a really good air of cooperation," comparing favorably to prior programs.

> "I think the department is shockingly healthy for a physics department. From a lot of my experiences and from what my friends at [other] grad schools have said, it just seems like there's a really good air of cooperation"
