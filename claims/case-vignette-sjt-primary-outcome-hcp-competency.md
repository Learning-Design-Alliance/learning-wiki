## Evidence

### Inhestern L 2019

Inhestern L, Frerichs W, Johannsen LM, Bergelt C. (2019). Process-evaluation and outcome-evaluation of a training programme for healthcare professionals in oncology to enhance their competencies in caring for patients with minor children: a study protocol for a randomised controlled pilot study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032778

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Protocol description of the primary outcome instrument in the Primary outcome section. Two vignettes are developed per measurement point, pilot tested by 2-3 HCPs, and reviewed by the research team for comprehensibility, relevance, fairness, difficulty and authenticity.

> "The primary outcome competency to approach child-related and family-related topics in patients with cancer will be measured using comprehensive case vignettes developed with elements of situational judgement test (SJT) and knowledge assessment."

### Inhestern L 2019 (2)

Inhestern L, Frerichs W, Johannsen LM, Bergelt C. (2019). Process-evaluation and outcome-evaluation of a training programme for healthcare professionals in oncology to enhance their competencies in caring for patients with minor children: a study protocol for a randomised controlled pilot study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032778

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Protocol description of the vignette scoring structure. Participants answer open-ended, multiple choice and forced choice questions per vignette, and receive a sum score per domain based on the two vignettes of each measurement point.

> "Each vignette captures four domains: (1) transfer of knowledge into clinical practice, (2) empathic behaviour towards affected parents, (3) integration of child-related and family-related topics into clinical practice, (4) perceived relevance of integrating child-related and family-related topics into clinical practice."
