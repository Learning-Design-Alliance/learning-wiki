# Including patient ethnicity in SBA questions does not significantly affect medical student performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students scored a mean of 0.5 pp lower on questions that included ethnicity compared to those that did not, a non-significant difference. [→ Mohan Jenanan 2025](#mohan-jenanan-2025)

## Evidence

### Mohan Jenanan 2025

Mohan Jenanan, Pishia Michaela, Alim Duaa, McGown Patrick J., Amin Anjali, Pinder Richard J., Halse Omid, Brown Celia, Sam Amir H. (2025). The impact of ethnicity information in single best answer questions on student performance, item response time and psychometric properties. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-025-10489-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised parallel-forms study of 267 final-year medical students at Imperial College London; paired t-test on percentage scores for questions with versus without ethnicity. The article reports "0.5 pp lower ... not statistically significant (t = 1.56, p = 0.12)".

> "Students scored a mean of 0.5 pp lower on questions that included ethnicity compared to those that did not, but this difference was not statistically significant (t = 1.56, p = 0.12)"
