### Polanin 2024 (2)

Sensitivity analysis comparing studies with and without missing confirmatory moderator information in the math domain: g with missing = 1.09 versus g without missing = 0.38, a nearly threefold difference.

> "For math, however, we found that studies with at least some missing information had effects nearly three times as large (g with missing = 1.09 vs. g without missing = 0.38)."
