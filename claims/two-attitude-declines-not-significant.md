## Evidence

### David Wittman 2010

David Wittman. (2010). Shaping Attitudes Toward Science in an Introductory Astronomy Class. https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3321 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0902.3321](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0902.3321)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Matched pre/post attitude survey (n = 63) in the author's astronomy course. The printed results list "I want my tax money to help support science" at 0% (3.68, 3.68), "Science is fun" at -3% (3.70, 3.65), and the experiments forced-choice item at -9% (3.43, 3.29); the author reports "Thus the two declines are not statistically signiﬁcant".

> "The typical per-question statistical uncertainty in gain ( posttest-pretest) is 0.12 points. Thus the two declines are not statistically signiﬁcant."
