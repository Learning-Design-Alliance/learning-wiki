## Evidence

### Eiring Lisa 2026

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of peer-teaching-based exam training. The abstract reports that stress levels decreased between the first and second training rounds, with "more students experiencing no or only mild stress".

> "Stress levels decreased in the second round, with more students experiencing no or only mild stress."

### Eiring Lisa 2026 (2)

Eiring Lisa, Benson Sven, Fandrey Joachim, Prost-Fingerle Katrin, Winning Sandra. (2026). M1ündlich – S(t)imulation of competency-oriented learning in physiology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09661-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis within the same quasi-experimental study. Students who rated their exam performance as poorer reported higher stress levels (p < 0.001 at all three time points), with Kendall's τ ranging from 0.28 to 0.31.

> "There was a positive (Kendall´s τ = 0.28–0.31) and significant correlation between stress levels and self-assessment at all three time points."
