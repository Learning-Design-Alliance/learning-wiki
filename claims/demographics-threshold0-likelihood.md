Logistic regression analysis of College Scorecard, PVC ROI framework, and IPUMS data estimating likelihood of meeting Threshold 0. The article reports these groups "are less likely to meet Threshold 0" and that colleges with greater Asian proportions are more likely to meet it; patterns persist or strengthen after SES control. No effect sizes printed.
