## Evidence

### Núñez 2017

Casañ Núñez, J. C. (2017). Testing audiovisual comprehension tasks with questions embedded in videos as subtitles: a pilot multimethod study. The EUROCALL Review, 25(1). https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.7062

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of a reliable (Cronbach's alpha = .778) nine-item helpfulness Likert scale completed by the 19 experimental-group test-takers; higher scores indicate greater agreement that embedded questions helped complete the test.

> "The mean of the scale with outliers ( M = 20.42) suggests that, overall, participants considered that imprinted questions were useful. The mean of the scale without outliers ( M = 21.47) revealed the same fact even more clearly."
