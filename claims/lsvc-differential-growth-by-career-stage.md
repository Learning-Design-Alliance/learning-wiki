## Evidence

### Yuzhu Xia 2025

Yuzhu Xia, Adria Patthoff, Liana Balloffet, Marco A. Bravo, Kip Téllez. (2025). Beyond boundaries: leveraging technology for differentiated professional development with lesson study video club. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10524-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey analysis of the 57 survey completers grouped by experience (n = 15 under five years, n = 16 five-to-ten years, n = 26 over ten years); Table 3 reports by-group paired t-test results such as Q19 gains of 0.6**, 0.53*, and 0.73***. The article reports "consistent and statistically significant growth across all measured areas" for the over-ten-years group.

> "Notably, teachers with over 10 years of experience demonstrated consistent and statistically significant growth across all measured areas, showcasing a deepening of their instructional practices and mentoring abilities."

### Yuzhu Xia 2025 (2)

Yuzhu Xia, Adria Patthoff, Liana Balloffet, Marco A. Bravo, Kip Téllez. (2025). Beyond boundaries: leveraging technology for differentiated professional development with lesson study video club. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10524-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired-sample t-test analysis of the novice group (n = 15, under five years of experience) found no statistically significant practice change; the article attributes this possibly to "the small sample size for novice teachers" or needing more time for pedagogical changes to manifest. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The different results in growth trajectories suggest that while novice teachers perceived the LSVC PD as highly beneficial, statistically significant evidence of changes in their classroom practices was not identified."
