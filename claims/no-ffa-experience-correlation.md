# Right FFA activation level does not correlate significantly with radiological experience in radiologists in training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Spearman correlations between radiological experience and right FFA beta values were non-significant for short (Rho = 0.18) and long (Rho = 0.20) presentation trials. [→ Kok Ellen M. 2021](#kok-ellen-m-2021)

## Evidence

### Kok Ellen M. 2021

Kok Ellen M., Sorger Bettina, van Geel Koos, Gegenfurtner Andreas, van Merriënboer Jeroen J. G., Robben Simon G. F., de Bruin Anique B. H. (2021). Holistic processing only? The role of the right fusiform face area in radiological expertise. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256849

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis within the radiologists-in-training group using the ordinal experience measure (years), testing hypothesis 4. The article reports "Rho = 0.18, p = .49" and "Rho = 0.20, p = .43", both non-significant.

> "Within the radiologists-in-training group, no correlation between radiological experience and beta values were found for the short- (Rho = 0.18, p = .49) and the long-presentation trials (Rho = 0.20, p = .43)."
