### Stubbs 2026

Stubbs, C. W., Huang, D., Koh, J., Woods, M., & Plazas Malagón, A. A. (2026). Lessons Learned from Integrating Generative AI into an Introductory Undergraduate Astronomy Course at Harvard. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.04389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.04389)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of end-of-term course evaluations: 11 of 14 students in the GAI-integrated 2025 offering completed evaluations, compared against the 2024 non-GAI version (Figure 6). The 1 sigma figure is an uncertainty estimate, not an effect size.

> "If we use the standard deviation to compute an uncertainty in the mean scores, there is a 1 sigma increase in student satisfaction for the 2025 version."
