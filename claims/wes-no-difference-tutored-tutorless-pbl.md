# Written examination scores did not significantly differ between tutored and tutorless PBL groups in either year

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` First-year medical students in tutorless PBL groups achieved written examination scores that did not significantly differ from tutored groups in 2007 or 2008. [→ Hayashi Shogo 2013](#hayashi-shogo-2013)

## Evidence

### Hayashi Shogo 2013

Hayashi Shogo, Tsunekawa Koji, Inoue Chikako, Fukuzawa Yoshitaka. (2013). Comparison of tutored group with tutorless group in problem-based mixed learning sessions: a randomized cross-matched study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-158

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized cross-matched study of PBL1 sessions (2007, N = 102; 2008, N = 100): tutored and tutorless groups were formed by combining sessions across Groups A and B. WES averages were 67.83 vs 65.82 (2007) and 77.85 vs 74.33 (2008); "this difference was not significant".

> "In the tutorless groups, a tendency towards higher average scores during both years was observed, but this difference was not significant (2007, p = 0.25; 2008, p = 0.20)."
