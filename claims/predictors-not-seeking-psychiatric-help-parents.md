## Evidence

### Yoshii Hatsumi 2012

Yoshii Hatsumi, Watanabe Yuichiro, Kitamura Hideaki, Nan Zhang, Akazawa Kouhei. (2012). Effect of an Education Program on Improving Help-Seeking among Parents of Junior and Senior High School Students in Japan. Global Journal of Health Science. https://doi.org/10.5539/gjhs.v4n1p33

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of parental characteristics against non-help-seeking (Table 2) in the parent cohort; the printed predictors were "having children with all 3 symptoms, age, and family income" at p<0.05.

> "Characteristics that predicted a decision not to seek psychiatric medical help (excepting consultation at a department of internal medicine) were having children with all 3 symptoms, age, and family income (p<0.05) (Table 2)."

### Yoshii Hatsumi 2012 (2)

Yoshii Hatsumi, Watanabe Yuichiro, Kitamura Hideaki, Nan Zhang, Akazawa Kouhei. (2012). Effect of an Education Program on Improving Help-Seeking among Parents of Junior and Senior High School Students in Japan. Global Journal of Health Science. https://doi.org/10.5539/gjhs.v4n1p33

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons of age groups in the same cohort; 43.9% of parents aged 30-39 did not seek psychiatric help, differing significantly from the 40-49 (p=0.003) and 50-59 (p=0.001) groups.

> "Among parents aged 30 to 39 years, 43.9% did not seek psychiatric medical help. The Bonferroni multiple comparison procedure showed significant differences in the rate between parents aged 30 to 39 years and both those aged 40 to 49 years (p=0.003) and those aged 50 to 59 years (p=0.001)."
