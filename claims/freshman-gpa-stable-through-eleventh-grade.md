# Freshman GPA is highly stable, correlating 0.87 with eleventh-grade GPA

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The correlation between ninth- and eleventh-grade GPA is r = 0.87, and most students retain the same GPA category two years later (e.g., 75 percent of B students kept a B average). [→ Easton 2017](#easton-2017)

## Evidence

### Easton 2017

Easton, J.Q., Johnson, E., & Sartain, L. (2017). The predictive power of ninth-grade GPA. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/predictive-power-ninth-grade-gpa

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of CPS transcript data (2010-12 cohorts) relating ninth- to eleventh-grade GPA; Table 3 shows category stability, e.g. 99 percent of A students earned As or Bs two years later. The printed correlation is "a coefficient of 0.87" (r = 0.87), which the article calls quite large.

> "Using correlation to measure the relationship between freshman GPA and GPA two years later, we found a coefficient of 0.87. This correlation coefficient is quite large by most standards"
