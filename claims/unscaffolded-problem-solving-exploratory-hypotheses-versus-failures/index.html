Sinha 2021¶

Sinha, T., Kapur, M., West, R., Catasta, M., Hauswirth, M., & Trninic, D. (2021). Differential Benefits of Explicit Failure-Driven and Success-Driven Scaffolding in Problem-Solving Prior to Instruction. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 530–555. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000483

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Framing statement from the article's introduction of the problem space: unscaffolded preparatory problem-solving affords exploratory hypothesis generation but may not deliver the initial failures that prior literature links to preparatory benefits. The article presents this as motivation, not as a tested contrast.

"Unscaffolded problem-solving before receiving instruction can give students opportunities to entertain their exploratory hypotheses at the expense of experiencing initial failures."
