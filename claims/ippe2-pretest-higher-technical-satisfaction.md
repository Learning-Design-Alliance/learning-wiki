# In IPPE2, pre-test satisfaction was significantly higher for all five technical-domain items, while exercise, instructor and communication domains showed no significant pre-post differences

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fourth-year IPPE2 students rated all five technical-domain items significantly higher (p < 0.05) in the pre-test than post-test; the other three domains showed no significant difference. [→ Palanisamy A 2024](#palanisamy-a-2024)

## Evidence

### Palanisamy A 2024

Palanisamy A, Hameed PMSH, Prabhu VV, Mohideen NTM, Falah AM, Muralikrishnan D, Vasudevan M, Sayed AMA. (2024). The effectiveness of a structured validated questionnaire to assess student perception with virtual pharmacy simulation in pharmacy practice experiential education. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0314117

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional pre/post survey of 121 IPPE2 students using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests; e.g., labeling and barcode scanning fell from "4.43 (0.80)" to "4.21 (0.76)" (p = 0.004). Only p-values are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "In IPPE 2, the students’ perception of pre-and post-tests had no significant difference regarding exercise, instructor, and communication domains. However, in the pre-test response, the students had significantly higher satisfaction (p < 0.05) with all five items related to the technical domain."
