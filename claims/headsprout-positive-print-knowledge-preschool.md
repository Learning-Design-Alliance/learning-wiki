# Headsprout Early Reading has a statistically significant positive effect on the print knowledge of at-risk preschool children

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In the same randomized controlled trial, Headsprout Early Reading produced a statistically significant positive effect on print knowledge (TERA-3), with an effect size of 0.57 and an improvement index of +22 percentile points. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2009](#what-works-clearinghouse-2009)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2009

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2009). Headsprout® Early Reading (WWC Intervention Report). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/211

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (Huffstetter, 2005) reviewed by the WWC: 62 four-year-old Head Start children randomly assigned to Headsprout Reading Basics or Millie's Math House comparison. On the TERA-3 the mean gain difference was 8.71, effect size 0.57, "Statistically significant", improvement index +22.

> "The authors report, and the WWC confirms, a statistically significant positive effect of Headsprout®  Early Reading on print knowledge of children in this study."
