## Evidence

### Zheng 2025

Zheng, Y. S., Goldwater, M., & Gentner, D. (2025). Structural Alignment and Linguistic Contrast Help Children Learn a Key Principle of Spatial Construction. Cognitive Science, 49(12), e70149. https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.70149

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preliminary logistic regressions in Experiment 1 on age and gender; age did not predict performance (ps > .81), and gender did not interact with alignment or language (ps > .54), so data were collapsed across age and gender. No effect size was printed.

> "Gender marginally predicted Repair performance ( M boys = .72; M girls = .49), p = .056, and significantly predicted Transfer performance ( M boys = .83; M girls = .57), p = .034."
