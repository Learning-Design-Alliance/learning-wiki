Email analysis of the Streamline group's messages over the program year, reported in the Findings section. The total number of emails in a given month ranged from eight to fifty four, and "the percentage of the emails originating from teachers increased" except in the final month.

> "Initially, the researchers sent the majority of the emails, but over time the teachers began to send a larger share of the total number of emails."
