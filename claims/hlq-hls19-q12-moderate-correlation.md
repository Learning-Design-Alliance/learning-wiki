The review reports a convergence validity study by Liu et al. comparing the HLS19-Q12 with the HLQ: the score correlates "positiv im moderaten Bereich (zwischen r = 0,24 und r = 0,42; Pearson-Korrelation)" with the 9 HLQ scales. The article cautions the instruments overlap in content only partially.
