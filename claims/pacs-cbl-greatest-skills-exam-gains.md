## Evidence

### Gu Peng 2026

Gu Peng, Xu Zhendong, Zhang Zheng, Wen Yonghao, Jia Yanqing, Du Ying, Wen Tianlin, Man Chao, Jia Zhiwei, Wu Yaohong, Li Wei. (2026). Comparative effectiveness of orthopedic teaching methods in China: a Bayesian network meta-analysis of randomized trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08582-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Network meta-analysis of practical skills exam scores from 39 studies, covering 9 teaching designs and 36 comparisons; the printed mean differences versus LBL are reported, with PACS-CBL rated low quality while 3D-PBL and PBL-CBL were rated high quality.

> "Compared to LBL, PACS-CBL (MD 18.81, 95% CI 17.46 to 20.16) had the greatest improvement in skills exam scores, followed by 3D-PBL (MD 7.84, 95% CI 7.34 to 8.35) and PBL-CBL (MD 7.42, 95% CI 6.35 to 8.50)."
