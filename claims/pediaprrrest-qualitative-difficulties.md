`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative feedback collected through open-ended postscenario questions and debriefing in the pilot study; participant comments were categorized into common themes. The article reports the "most frequently highlighted difficulties", including unclear screen-flow prompting (n=8 team leaders), dense reversible-causes screen (n=5), icon-timing ambiguity (n=5), and divided attention while leading (n=4).

> "Based on the qualitative feedback provided by participants, the most frequently highlighted difficulties were (1) interacting with the screens flow because information delivery was unclear about recommendation on performance of an action versus suggestion to perform an action based on the team skillset"
