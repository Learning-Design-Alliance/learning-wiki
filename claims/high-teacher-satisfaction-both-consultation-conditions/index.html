Evidence¶

Owens et al. 2017¶

Julie Sarno Owens, Erika K. Coles, Steven W. Evans, Lina K. Himawan, Erin Girio-Herrera, Alex S. Holdaway, Allison K. Zoromski, Terah Schamberg, and Ann Schulte. (2017). Using Multi-Component Consultation to Increase the Integrity with which Teachers Implement Behavioral Classroom Interventions: A Pilot Study. School Mental Health, 9, 218-234. https://doi.org/0.1007/s12310-017-9217-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

End-of-program satisfaction items (Table 7) completed by teachers in both conditions of the pilot. The article reports ratings were high and the between-condition differences "are not statistically significant"; no effect size is printed.

"In most cases, the satisfaction ratings for teachers in the multi-component condition (regardless of latent classes and baseline group) are higher than that of their counterparts in the comparison condition, yet these differences are not statistically significant."
