## Evidence

### Brueckmann M 2025

Brueckmann M, Wild E, Wolff F. (2025). Planting the seed: how parental negative conditional regard boosts vulnerability to excessive study behaviour and burnout. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03354-z

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Serial mediation SEM with retrospective reports of parental regard at school age in 624 university students. The exhaustion pathway included a "significant serial mediation effect via academic self-esteem contingency and excessive study behaviour (β = 0.06, 95% CI [0.03, 0.09])".

> "A significant total effect was observed (β = 0.13, 95% CI [0.04, 0.21]) regarding the relationship between parental negative conditional regard and exhaustion. This consisted of a significant serial mediation effect via academic self-esteem contingency and excessive study behaviour (β = 0.06, 95% CI [0.03, 0.09]), and an additional simple mediation effect, mediated only by academic self-esteem contingency (β = 0.03, 95% CI [0.01, 0.07])."
