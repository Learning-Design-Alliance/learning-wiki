`q2 i?` On a computer-based multiple-choice interpretation quiz with normal and pathological images, senior residents averaged 76% (range 68–89%). [→ Schmidt Jessica N. MD 2015](#schmidt-jessica-n-md-2015)

## Evidence

### Schmidt Jessica N. MD 2015

Schmidt Jessica N. MD, MPH; Kendall John MD; Smalley Courtney MD. (2015). Competency Assessment in Senior Emergency Medicine Residents for Core Ultrasound Skills. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2015.9.28587

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Assessment of senior residents with a 38-question computer-based quiz containing live cine-clips and still images of normal and abnormal pathology, scored by an external server. The "average score was 76% with a range from 68 to 89%"; one resident was excluded for an incomplete quiz. No effect size is printed.

> "For image interpretation, the average score was 76% with a range from 68 to 89%. One resident data point was excluded due to an incomplete on-line quiz due to technical errors."
