## Evidence

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2023

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Moeller Korbinian, Musslick Sebastian. (2023). Assignment strategies modulate students’ academic performance in an online learning environment during the first and second COVID-19 related school closures. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0284868

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Main effect from the first-closure logistic mixed model on completion (single versus books contrast). The dataset comprised 16,646 students from 1,908 classes who calculated 170,522 problem sets.

> "The main effect of time window was significant (b = 0.14; z = 3.94; p < .001), with more completed problem sets during school closure as compared to the same time window in the previous year."

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2023 (2)

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Moeller Korbinian, Musslick Sebastian. (2023). Assignment strategies modulate students’ academic performance in an online learning environment during the first and second COVID-19 related school closures. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0284868

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Main effect from the first-closure coins model. The article notes control analyses indicated the mere degree of software usage did not explain observed differences between time windows.

> "The main effect of time window was significant (b = .09; z = 2.85; p = .0043), with overall more coins gained during school closure as compared to the same time window in the previous year."
