## Evidence

### Junga A 2026

Junga A, Kockwelp P, Dabel J, Scherzer SH, Risse B, Holling M, Marschall B, Friederichs H. (2026). Olfactory Cues and Patient Safety Behavior in Virtual Reality Medical Simulation: Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/93148

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory end point in the same controlled trial: disinfection immediately when leaving the patient environment. The wide CI "reflects the low event rate in the control group and warrants cautious interpretation of this exploratory finding".

> "Only 1 of 43 control participants (2.3%) exhibited this behavior, compared with 10 of 52 participants (19.2%) in the intervention group. Fisher exact test yielded an OR of 9.82 (95% CI 1.29-443.13; P=.01)."
