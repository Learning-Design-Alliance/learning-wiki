## Evidence

### Gonzalez Quiroz 2018

Gonzalez Quiroz, A., & Garza, N. R. (2018). Focus on Student Success: Components for Effective Summer Bridge Programs. Journal of Hispanic Higher Education, 17(2), 101-111. https://doi.org/10.1177/1538192717753988

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of self-selected Summer Bridge participants against randomly identified controls receiving standard services at Laredo Community College. The article reports participants "outperformed the regular semester pass rates by 21%, 13%, and 8%" in the three gatekeeper courses.

> "Students who participated in the 2013 Summer Bridge outperformed the regular semester pass rates by 21%, 13%, and 8% in History 1301, Math 1314, and English 1301, respectively."

### Gonzalez Quiroz 2018 (2)

Gonzalez Quiroz, A., & Garza, N. R. (2018). Focus on Student Success: Components for Effective Summer Bridge Programs. Journal of Hispanic Higher Education, 17(2), 101-111. https://doi.org/10.1177/1538192717753988

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison across program years reported in the Results section and Table 2; the 2015 cohort's pass rates reached 90% (History), 100% (Math), and 100% (English) versus control rates of 66%, 70%, and 71%. The article states students "increased 26%, 32%, and 25%" over 2013 FOSS rates.

> "Compared with fall 2013 FOSS pass rates, students in Cohort 2015 increased 26%, 32%, and 25% in History 1301, Math 1314, and English 1301, respectively (see Table 2)."
