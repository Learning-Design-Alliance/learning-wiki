## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Meeting other international students with the same problems was reassuring, and tutors with similar backgrounds were seen as role models who understood students' anxieties. [→ Huhn D 2015](#huhn-d-2015)

## Evidence

### Huhn D 2015

Huhn D, Eckart W, Karimian-Jazi K, Amr A, Herzog W, Nikendei C. (2015). Voluntary peer-led exam preparation course for international first year students: Tutees' perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0391-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme C.1 (26 quotations) from the qualitative content analysis of 10 interviews: peer contact with students facing the same language and adaptation difficulties fostered friendly relationships where nobody feared being mocked, contributing to well-being and clearer learning experiences.

> "Students found it very reassuring that they were able to meet other international students who 'have the same problems' and, for example, 'have to look up whole words, too'."
