## Evidence

### Broc Lucie 2025

Broc Lucie, Goudeau Sébastien, Colliot Tiphaine. (2025). Comparing the effects of typing and handwriting on spelling performance in school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-03369-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Replication copying study omnibus medium × error-type interaction (η² = 0.07). Post-hocs showed more orthographic errors per word typed (M = 0.06) than handwritten (M = 0.01), and more phonological errors typed in Study 1 (M = 0.06 vs 0.02).

> "The analyses also revealed a significant interaction between the medium used and the type of spelling errors made per word [F(2, 332) = 51.2, p < .001, η² = 0.07], as shown in Fig. 2."
