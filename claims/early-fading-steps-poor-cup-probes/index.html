Groff 2011¶

Groff, R. A., Piazza, C. C., Zeleny, J. R., & Dempsey, J. R. (2011). Spoon-to-cup fading as treatment for cup drinking in a child with intestinal failure. Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 44, 949–954. https://doi.org/10.1901/jaba.2011.44-949

q2 · i1 · causal · r1

Multiple-probe assessment during fading in the single-participant study. The article reports "Acceptance was variable ( M 5 75%), and mouth clean was low ( M 5 20%) during the first four cup probes," in contrast to high performance (99% and 100%) during the first five fading steps.
