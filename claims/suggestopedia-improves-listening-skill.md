## Evidence

### Muhammad Ali Mustapha 2018

Muhammad Ali Mustapha. (2018). Use of Suggestopedia to Improve Students’ Listening Skill in English Language. i-manager’s Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 8 No. 4. https://www.i-manager.com/journals/journal_of_english_language_teaching

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 80 SS II students in two schools, 40 experimental and 40 control, analyzed with independent t-test in SPSS. Posttest means show the experimental group at "(M=66.65, SD=6.221)" versus control at 30.90; the article reports the two-tailed P-value as less than 0.0001 and calls the difference extremely statistically significant.

> "Thus, the mean score of the experimental group was (M=66.65, SD=6.221) in posttest. While mean score of the control group was (M=30.90, SD=7.146)."
