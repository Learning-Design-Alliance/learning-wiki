## Evidence

### Nilsson C. 2017

Nilsson C., Sorensen J. L., Konge L., Westen M., Stadeager M., Ottesen B., Bjerrum F. (2017). Simulation-based camera navigation training in laparoscopy—a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-016-5210-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Motor-skills parameter on the LASTT model in the randomized trial: the camera group showed significantly shorter angular path length than the control group (p = 0.016); the discussion notes procedure training was superior to no training only in total time.

> "The total angular path length was 3686 degrees (95% CI, 2943–4429) for the camera group versus 5300 degrees (95% CI, 4161-6441) for the control group (p = 0.016)."
