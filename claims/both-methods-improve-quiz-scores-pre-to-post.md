### Hu Xiaolei 2019

Hu Xiaolei, Zhang Hengyan, Song Yuan, Wu Chenchen, Yang Qingqing, Shi Zhaoming, Zhang Xiaomei, Chen Weidong. (2019). Implementation of flipped classroom combined with problem-based learning: an approach to promote learning about hyperthyroidism in the endocrinology internship. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample t-tests in the same randomized trial showed significant pre-to-post improvement in both quiz domains in both groups. Only p-values are printed; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Further analysis found that in both basic theoretical knowledge and clinical case analysis, compared with the pre-quiz, the post-quiz scores were significantly improved, regardless of which teaching method was adopted (p < 0.001) (Fig. 2b)."
