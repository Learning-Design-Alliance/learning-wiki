The review's synthesis of four RCTs measuring teamwork performance. Thomas et al. found improved teamwork event behaviors in high-fidelity groups (p = 0.004), while three studies found no significant difference between groups; no meta-analysis was possible.

> "In our study, four RCTs analyzed the teamwork efficacy of high-fidelity simulation-based training, but a positive result was shown in only one study."
