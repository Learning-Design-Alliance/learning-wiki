## Evidence

### Kullberg 2023

Kullberg, N., Kiili, C., Bråten, I., González-Ibáñez, R., & Leppänen, P. H. T. (2023). Sixth graders' selection and integration when writing from multiple online texts. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09613-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In this study of 162 sixth graders completing an online inquiry task, snippet selections were scored for relevance on a 0-3 scale per snippet, with a maximum of 18 points. The mean was "16.00 (SD = 2.41) out of 18 points", though five students scored below 12.

> "their mean score for the snippet selections was 16.00 (SD = 2.41) out of 18 points. Still, students' scores ranged from 4 to 18, and five students scored below 12 points."
