Victoria Jackson 2026¶

Victoria Jackson, Kyla Hurns, and Paulina Fraser. (2026). Understanding the Implementation of Universal FAFSA Completion Policies in Colorado, Illinois, Indiana, and Louisiana. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED682438

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative case study of 15 interviews and focus groups with state officials, counselors, and principals in four states. Participants "consistently described the policy as an unfunded mandate"; one Illinois counselor said the state has "added way too much to the plate of school counselors" without lowering ratios or hiring staff.

"Participants in interviews consistently described the policy as an unfunded mandate, emphasizing that neither schools nor state agencies received additional resources to manage the increased workload."
