Breitwieser, J., Neubauer, A. B., Schmiedek, F. & Brod, G. (2024). Realizing the potential of mobile interventions for education. npj Science of Learning, 9, 76. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00289-9

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in this Perspective, drawing on mHealth examples such as the HeartSteps program. The authors argue algorithms can send "different types and/or amounts of prompts based on decisions made in real time," e.g. study reminders based on location or breathing prompts based on electrodermal activity.

> "Mobile devices are ideally suited toimplement and adapt interventions just in time, especially when teachers or other human support is not readily available."
