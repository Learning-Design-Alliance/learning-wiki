# The LMS inactive period is about an hour shorter on days with 08:00 classes than on non-school days, driven by earlier login onset

## Evidence

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Aggregated analysis of 17.4 million time-stamped LMS logins from 39,458 students over five semesters, sorted by first class of the day. Linear mixed-effects models with Tukey's tests showed login onset advanced with earlier classes while login offset varied little (−0.43 h at 08:00); no effect size printed.

> "the LMS login onset tracked closely the start time of morning classes, with activity starting more than an hour earlier for classes at 08:00 relative to non-school days (difference in LMS login onset relative to days with no classes, Tukey's test: 08:00, −1.37 h, 95% CI = −1.46 h to −1.27 h, P < 0.001"

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023 (2)

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same LMS dataset (39,458 students, five semesters); the inactive period between login offset and onset was used as an estimate of nocturnal sleep opportunity. The 08:00 inactive period was also significantly shorter than for all other start times (e.g. −1.33 h vs 16:00); no effect size printed.

> "The LMS inactive period was about 1 h shorter when classes started at 08:00 compared with non-school days (difference in LMS inactive period relative to days with no classes, Tukey's test: 08:00, −0.93 h, 95% CI = −1.05 h to −0.82 h, P < 0.001"
