### Leon I. 2023

Leon I., Tautolo E.-S., Plank L.D., Rush E. (2023). Pacific Islands Families Study: Household Food Security during Pregnancy and Secondary School Educational Achievement. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15194131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared comparisons of food insecurity prevalence by offspring sex in the 649-member analysis sample at the six-week post-partum interview, using both the seven-question PIF definition (score ≥4 of 14) and the single-question MOH definition; neither differed by sex.

> "In this analysis, at birth, 32% of households with female offspring and 27% of households with male offspring were rated food insecure (p = 0.168) using previously published PIF criteria [16], while 43% of households with female offspring and 40% of households with male offspring were categorised as food insecure using MOH criteria (p = 0.438) (Table 1)."
