## Evidence

### Taylor 2007

Taylor, I. M., & Ntoumanis, N. (2007). Teacher motivational strategies and student self-determination in physical education. Journal of Educational Psychology, 99, 747-760. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.747

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Baron and Kenny mediation analysis with class-average perceived student self-determination as predictor and teacher SDI as mediator (N = 51). Table 4.2 reports Sobel z scores of 2.49 for autonomy support and 2.96 for involvement; "for both autonomy support and involvement all the paths prescribed by the mediation steps were significant."

> "However, for both autonomy support and involvement all the paths prescribed by the mediation steps were significant, confirming the hypothesized mediation."
