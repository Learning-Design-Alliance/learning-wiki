# Multimedia-assisted physics teaching significantly reduced students' negative attitudes to physics across all five measured aspects

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the pedagogical intervention with multimedia materials, students' negative attitude to physics significantly decreased in all observed aspects (popularity, content attractiveness, difficulty, comprehensibility, importance). [→ Záhorec 2010](#zahorec-2010)

## Evidence

### Záhorec 2010

Záhorec, J., Hašková, A., & Munk, M. (2010). Impact of Electronic Teaching Materials on Process of Education – Results of an Experiment. Informatics in Education, 9(2), 261–281. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1064255

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pedagogical intervention with pre/post questionnaire in a Slovak grammar school; three groups (ESA n=26, ESB n=24, KS n=23). Kruskal–Wallis tests rejected null hypotheses for items 1POST–5POST; e.g. item 1POST H(2, N = 73) = 11.80656, p = 0.0027, "so we rejected the null hypothesis".

> "They proved that due to the use of the multimedia materials in teaching process the degree of the students’ negative attitude to the subject physics signiﬁcantly decreased in all the observed aspects"
