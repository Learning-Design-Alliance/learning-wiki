### Carrier Annie 2024

Carrier Annie, Éthier Alexandra, Beaudoin Michaël, Hudon Anne, Bédard Denis, Jasmin Emmanuelle, Lavoie-Trudeau Étienne, Verville France. (2024). Agency: occupational therapists’ perspectives on their sense of competence and their skills following training. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.78346

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Deductive content analysis of two open-ended pre-training questions answered by 103 occupational therapists, categorized against Picotin et al.'s nine personal factors; frequencies computed in SPSS 26. Descriptive.

> "les ergothérapeutes rapportent comme compétence la plus importante la « communication efficace » (n = 110; 35,6 %), suivie de la « collaboration intentionnelle » (n = 68; 22,0 %) et des habiletés « d’observation et d’analyse » (n = 67; 21,7 %)."
