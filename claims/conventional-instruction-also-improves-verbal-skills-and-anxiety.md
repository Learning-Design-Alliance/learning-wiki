## Evidence

### Mehmet Nuri Kardaş & Raşit Koç 2017

Mehmet Nuri Kardaş & Raşit Koç. (2017). Effect of Drama Instruction Method on Students' Turkish Verbal Skills and Speech Anxiety. International Journal of Progressive Education, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131916

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Related samples t-test on the 19 control-group students' TRSS scores; pretest mean 66.89, posttest mean 78.26, "statistically significant as well (p: .002 ˂ 0.05; t: - 3.682)."

> "It was determined that this numerical difference was statistically significant as well (p: .002 ˂ 0.05; t: - 3.682). The difference demonstrated that conventional instruction applied in the control group was effective in the development of students' Turkish verbal skills."

### Mehmet Nuri Kardaş & Raşit Koç 2017 (2)

Mehmet Nuri Kardaş & Raşit Koç. (2017). Effect of Drama Instruction Method on Students' Turkish Verbal Skills and Speech Anxiety. International Journal of Progressive Education, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1131916

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Related samples t-test on the 19 control-group students' SAS scores; pretest mean 56.36 (intermediate), posttest mean 47.68 (low anxiety), with "a significant difference between pretest and posttest scores favoring posttest results (p: .004 ˂ 0.05; t: 3.263)."

> "Statistical analysis reflected that there was a significant difference between pretest and posttest scores favoring posttest results (p: .004 ˂ 0.05; t: 3.263). The finding demonstrates that the conventional instruction method had a significant effect on students' speech anxiety."
