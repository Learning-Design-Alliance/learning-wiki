## Evidence

### Zafra-Tanaka JH 2018

Zafra-Tanaka JH, Hurtado-Villanueva ME, Saenz-Naranjo MdP, Taype-Rondan A. (2018). Self-perceived competence in early diagnosis of cervical cancer among recently graduated physicians from Lima, Peru. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203778

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional self-administered survey of 432 recently graduated Lima physicians attending the VI Convención Nacional SERUMS in April 2017; competence rated on a five-point scale, with "Agree" or "Strongly agree" coded as adequately competent. Reported proportions: "367 (86.2%) perceived themselves as adequately competent to perform PAP".

> "Regarding the proportion of physicians who perceived themselves as adequately competent to perform cervical cancer screening tests, 367 (86.2%) perceived themselves as adequately competent to perform PAP, 257 (60.5%) to perform VIA, and 247 (58.1%) to perform VILI"
