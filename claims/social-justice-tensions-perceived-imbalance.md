Across-case analysis of the four cases reports this assertion. Cases 2 and 4 experienced tension between Western academic culture and community values; Cases 1 and 3 experienced "a perceived imbalance of power between the learning designer and faculty members or subject matter experts."

> "Overall, tension arose when there was a perceived imbalance between the social justice goals and the needs and perspectives of the designer, other team members, and the project / project stakeholders."
