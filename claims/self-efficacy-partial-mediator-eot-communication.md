## Evidence

### Yu Jiaying 2026

Yu Jiaying, Zhu Chengyu, Ren Yuwen, Fan Mingna & Yang Lili (2026). Alexithymia and clinical communication competence in nursing interns in China with the mediating role of self-efficacy. Scientific Reports 16, 10615. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44952-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM mediation analysis (Amos 23.0, bias-corrected Bootstrap) on the 208-intern survey, testing EOT → GSES → communication paths across four subdomain models; bootstrap 95% CIs excluded zero. Proportions of total effect are as printed; no standardised effect size reported.

> "The mediating effect of establishing a harmonious relationship model accounted for 16.58% of the total effect, the listening sensitively model for 23.49%, the recognizing patient problems model for 25.42%, and the verifying feelings model for 34.62% of the total effect."

### Yu Jiaying 2026 (2)

Yu Jiaying, Zhu Chengyu, Ren Yuwen, Fan Mingna & Yang Lili (2026). Alexithymia and clinical communication competence in nursing interns in China with the mediating role of self-efficacy. Scientific Reports 16, 10615. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44952-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model fit assessment of the four SEM models in the same analysis, reporting χ2/df, RMSEA, and CFI values as printed. Fit statistics are not effect sizes.

> "The model for establishing harmonious relationships and identifying patient problems demonstrated good fit (χ2/df = 2.518, RMSEA = 0.086), as did the model for listening sensitively and verifying feelings (χ2/df = 2.073, RMSEA = 0.072). The CFI for all four models were above 0.900, suggesting a good fit for the models."
