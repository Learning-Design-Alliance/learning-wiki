## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After a four-week rotation, residents taught with integrated clinical pathway and case-based teaching achieved significantly higher theoretical examination scores than residents taught traditionally (90.7 ± 1.4 vs. 82.4 ± 0.9). [→ Chen Xiaolin 2026](#chen-xiaolin-2026)

## Evidence

### Chen Xiaolin 2026

Chen Xiaolin, Huang Yan, Cai Yuli, Chen Qiong. (2026). The integration of clinical pathway and case-based teaching in standardized residency training in the department of endocrinology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09074-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 87 residents (43 control, 44 experimental) over a four-week endocrinology rotation; post-training theoretical scores favored the experimental group, "90.7 ± 1.4 vs. 82.4 ± 0.9, p < 0.00". Pre-training scores did not differ (74.8 ± 2.4 vs. 75.0 ± 1.8, p = 0.94).

> "However, after the training, participants in the experimental group achieved significantly higher scores compared to those in the control group (90.7 ± 1.4 vs. 82.4 ± 0.9, p < 0.00; 27.2 ± 0.4 vs. 24.1 ± 0.3, p < 0.00)"
