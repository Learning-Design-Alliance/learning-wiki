q2 · i? · design · r2

Longitudinal item exposure counts across administrations (Tables 4.16–4.19) were compared between engines. On the CBE the longitudinal exposure control was changed "from a constraint to a guideline" to prevent test crashes from item starvation.

"an item can be administered again to the same student within the 14-month timespan if and only if the item bank cannot fulfill the test requirements. This is not allowed on COLO but is on the CBE due to the longitudinal item exposure control being changed from a constraint to a guideline to prevent the test crash as a result of item starvation."
