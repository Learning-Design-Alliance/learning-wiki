### Nim Casper et al 2024

Nim Casper et al. (2024). The association between students’ confidence and ability to modulate spinal manipulation force–time characteristics of specific target forces: a cross-sectional study. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-024-00557-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional measurement of 900 valid SMT trials from 149 students showing wide ranges of performed forces for all targets. The article concludes "substantial variability in SMT force–time characteristics" exists and suggests "some of this variability could be attributed to students' confidence".

> "There was substantial variability in SMT force–time characteristics, which may have implications for adverse events and patient satisfaction. Some of this variability could be attributed to students’ confidence."
