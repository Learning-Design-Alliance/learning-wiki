## Evidence

### Qu Bo 2012

Qu Bo, Zhao Yu-hong, Sun Bao-zhi. (2012). Assessment of Resident Physicians in Professionalism, Interpersonal and Communication Skills: a Multisource Feedback. International Journal of Medical Sciences. https://doi.org/10.7150/ijms.3353

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of questionnaires completed by 258 first-year residents and their evaluators at 19 Chinese hospitals; reliability assessed with Cronbach's alpha per evaluator group. Alphas ranged "0.901 and 0.933" across the six surveys, all exceeding 0.90.

> "There was an overall alpha of 0.913, 0.924, 0.930, 0.921, 0.901 and 0.933 respectively on attending doctor, self-evaluation, resident peer, nurse, office staff and patient surveys."
