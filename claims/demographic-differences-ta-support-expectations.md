## Evidence

### Wilson 2025

Wilson, D., & Kardam, N. (2025). What do students expect from teaching assistants (TAs)? Considerations of gender, race, and international status. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0334904

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparisons of primary-theme percentages across demographic groups in the 1,678-student dataset; race/ethnicity was analyzed for domestic students only because 92.8% of international students reporting race were Asian.

> "Across demographics, interactions appeared to be top priority for fewer Black (35.3%) and Latino (34.8%) students than for Asian (44.6%), White (43.8%), and Asian/White (45.5%) students. Domestic students also favored interactions in their responses (43.9%) more than international students (37.5%)."

### Wilson 2025 (2)

Wilson, D., & Kardam, N. (2025). What do students expect from teaching assistants (TAs)? Considerations of gender, race, and international status. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0334904

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square tests of independence on primary-theme assignments across gender (male, female, other), five race/ethnicities, and two statuses across four periods; non-binary students were excluded from gender tests because their inclusion appeared to mask male/female differences.

> "Chi-square tests indicated that this null hypothesis could be rejected when considering the entire student sample and the ERT sample, but when considering the pre-COVID and post-COVID sample, the null hypothesis could not be rejected."
