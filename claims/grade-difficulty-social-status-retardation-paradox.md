## Evidence

### Toops 1918

Toops, H. A., and Pintker, R. (1918). Mentality and School Progress. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Mentality+and+School+Progress+Toops+Pintner

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Graph I plots, for Schools A and C, the ratio of median mental age at each grade to median chronological age. The article interprets the greater rate of increase in School A as rising grade difficulty, and offers as "A possible explanation, although one on which we have no data" that School A eliminates more laggards.

> "We see that School A, of highest status, has a consistently greater rate of increase than School C, the lowest in social status, and which is taken by way of affording a good contrast. Accordingly, since it is harder in School A for a child of normal intelligence to pass each successive grade than for a similar child in School C, we should expect School A to have much the larger percentage of retardation. Actually, however, School A has a smaller percentage, 33.5 for the entire school as compared with 38.4 similarly for School C."
