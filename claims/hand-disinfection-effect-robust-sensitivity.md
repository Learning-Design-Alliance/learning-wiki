## Evidence

### Junga A 2026

Junga A, Kockwelp P, Dabel J, Scherzer SH, Risse B, Holling M, Marschall B, Friederichs H. (2026). Olfactory Cues and Patient Safety Behavior in Virtual Reality Medical Simulation: Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/93148

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prespecified sensitivity analyses in the same trial, excluding six intervention participants with missing demographic data (complete-case n=89). Fisher exact test (P=.04) and chi-square (χ²1=4.47; P=.04) further supported the finding.

> "In the complete-case sample (n=89), the unadjusted OR was 2.81 (95% CI 1.09‐7.75; P=.04). Adjustment for sex did not materially change the estimate (adjusted OR=2.58, 95% CI 0.99‐7.20; P=.06)."
