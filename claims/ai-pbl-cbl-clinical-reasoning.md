## Evidence

### Wei H 2025

Wei H, Dai Y, Yuan K, Li KY, Hung KF, Hu EM, Lee AHC, Chang JWW, Zhang C, Li X. (2025). AI-Powered Problem- and Case-based Learning in Medical and Dental Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.100858

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Descriptive synthesis of one RCT (Wang et al., 101 medical undergraduates and postgraduates) measuring clinical reasoning with CCTT Level Z; the review reports "SMD = 1.34" versus traditional PBL, noting the measure relied on subjective self-assessment.

> "Compared with traditional PBL, the integration of LearnGuide, a customised tool based on ChatGPT, showed higher effectiveness in enhancing clinical reasoning capability (SMD = 1.34, 95% CIs [0.91,1.77])."

### Wei H 2025 (2)

Wei H, Dai Y, Yuan K, Li KY, Hung KF, Hu EM, Lee AHC, Chang JWW, Zhang C, Li X. (2025). AI-Powered Problem- and Case-based Learning in Medical and Dental Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.100858

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Descriptive synthesis of one RCT (Zeng et al., 42 fifth-year medical interns) using mini-CEX assessment; ChatGPT-assisted PBL showed "SMD = 0.90" versus traditional teaching on clinical judgment and overall clinical competence.

> "A weaker favourable effect was observed in ChatGPT-assisted PBL when compared to traditional teaching (SMD = 0.90, 95% CIs [0.26,1.54])."

### Wei H 2025 (3)

Wei H, Dai Y, Yuan K, Li KY, Hung KF, Hu EM, Lee AHC, Chang JWW, Zhang C, Li X. (2025). AI-Powered Problem- and Case-based Learning in Medical and Dental Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.100858

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Descriptive synthesis of one RCT (Suebnukarn, 36 second-year dental students) using the clinical reasoning problem approach; COMET-tutored and human-tutored groups did not differ significantly (P = .058); normality test was not fulfilled.

> "Previous studies using the CRP approach showed that the clinical reasoning gains for AI-tutored students were similar to those for human-tutored students, with no significant difference in the average post-test score observed for the 2 groups (Mann-Whitney, P = .058)."
