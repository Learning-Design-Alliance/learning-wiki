# Role-script support (TAU) during generation led university relearning students to outperform direct instruction in all weeks but the first

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2` · `q2`–`q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a four-week in vivo experiment with 76 university students in a relearning situation, Think Ask Understand (TAU) role-script students outperformed DI students in all weeks but the first. [→ Manu Kapur 2012](#manu-kapur-2012)
`q3 i?` Students collaborated in accordance with the role script and increasingly internalized it; the more students were familiarized with TAU, the better their learning outcomes. [→ Manu Kapur 2012 (2)](#manu-kapur-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Manu Kapur 2012

Manu Kapur, Nikol Rummel. (2012). Productive failure in learning from generation and invention activities. Instr Sci 40:645-650. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-012-9235-4

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Four-week, in vivo experiment with 76 university students relearning four topics of mathematical analysis for upcoming examinations, comparing TAU role-script support to direct instruction. No effect size is printed.

> "Westermann and Rummel found that students in the TAU condition outperformed students in the DI condition in all weeks but the ﬁrst."

### Manu Kapur 2012 (2)

Manu Kapur, Nikol Rummel. (2012). Productive failure in learning from generation and invention activities. Instr Sci 40:645-650. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-012-9235-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Process data from the same relearning experiment indicated fruitful, script-consistent collaboration and increasing internalization of the script over the four weeks.
