Evidence¶

Mark Feng Teng 2026¶

Mark Feng Teng. (2026). Effectiveness of GenAI in enhancing writing performance: a meta-analysis. Asian-Pacific Journal of Second and Foreign Language Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40862-026-00444-2

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup moderator analysis in the expanded main synthesis (k = 11). The article reports that "sample size category (21–40 vs. >40 participants) did not significantly moderate effect size", with both subgroups showing positive pooled effects; Table 6 also shows total sample size non-significant in meta-regression (p = 0.171).

"sample size category (21–40 vs. >40 participants) did not significantly moderate effect size (QB = 0.50, p = 0.479), although both subgroups showed positive pooled effects."
