### Paul 2026

Paul, B. (2026). P-A.I.R.: A Structured AI-Replication Framework for Active Learning in Introductory Physics. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.18587](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.18587)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

### Paul 2026 (2)

Paul, B. (2026). P-A.I.R.: A Structured AI-Replication Framework for Active Learning in Introductory Physics. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.18587](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.18587)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation between prior self-rated confidence in using AI effectively and post-session problem-solving confidence (C1), with printed effect size rs = 0.375 (p = .034, n = 32). The article suggests students more capable with AI reported higher confidence solving problems independently.

> "prior AI confidence was significantly associated with post-session self-confidence scores (rs = 0.375,p=.034,n= 32; Fig. 4)"
