# Alpha neurofeedback training produces no change in alpha ERSP or pre-stimulus alpha in the encoding and maintenance phases of the modified Sternberg task

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Repeated-measures ANOVA showed no significant Group × Time interaction in alpha ERSP in the encoding stage, the maintenance stage, or pre-stimulus alpha power. [→ Zhou 2024](#zhou-2024)

## Evidence

### Zhou 2024

Zhou, W., Nan, W., Xiong, K., & Ku, Y. (2024). Alpha neurofeedback training improves visual working memory in healthy individuals. npj Science of Learning 9, 32. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00242-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

EEG results (64-channel recording during the modified Sternberg task) from pretest and posttest 1 in the sham-controlled experiment; Table 1 reports descriptive ERSP and pre-stimulus alpha means for both groups.

> "The repeated-measures ANOVA showed no signiﬁcant Group× Time interaction in the alpha ERSP in the encoding stage (F (1,38) = 0.03, p = 0.861) and in the maintenance stage (F (1,38) = 0.05, p = 0.833). Similarly, the pre-stimulus alpha activity had no signiﬁcant Group × Time interaction (F (1,38) = 0.49, p = 0.486)"
