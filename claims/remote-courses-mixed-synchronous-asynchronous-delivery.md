## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Means 2021

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., Wiley, K., & Grifths, R. (2021). Lessons from remote learning during COVID-19. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 1 of the national student survey (n = 1,008) reports delivery mechanisms: "Live sessions (synchronous instruction) 67%", recorded lectures 65%, pre-recorded videos 55%. The report notes these percentages would be mathematically impossible if many courses did not include both synchronous and asynchronous delivery.

> "Live sessions (synchronous instruction) 67% Recorded lectures by the instructor (asynchronous) 65% Pre-recorded videos, not of instructor 55%"
