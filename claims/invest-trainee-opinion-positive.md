## Subclaims

## Evidence

### van Det M. J. 2011

van Det M. J., Meijerink W. J. H. J., Hoff C., Middel L. J., Koopal S. A., Pierie J. P. E. N. (2011). The learning effect of intraoperative video-enhanced surgical procedure training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-010-1545-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-curriculum questionnaire with seven statements on a 5-point Likert scale completed by the five INVEST trainees; they "totally agreed with the statements that intraoperative video training is fun" and supports the learning curve. No effect size printed.

> "The trainees in the INVEST group totally agreed with the statements that intraoperative video training is fun, it has a positive effect on the learning curve, and it is a uniform means of learning laparoscopic cholecystectomy."

### van Det M. J. 2011 (2)

van Det M. J., Meijerink W. J. H. J., Hoff C., Middel L. J., Koopal S. A., Pierie J. P. E. N. (2011). The learning effect of intraoperative video-enhanced surgical procedure training. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-010-1545-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The MAM trainees, shown the video after their curriculum, gave similar ratings; the article reports the answers were "similar and not statistically different". Equivalence was not formally tested; no effect size printed.

> "The answers in the MAM group were similar and not statistically different (Table 2)."
