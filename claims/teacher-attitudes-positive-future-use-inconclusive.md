## Evidence

### Quintas-Hijós Alejandro 2020

Quintas-Hijós Alejandro, Peñarrubia-Lozano Carlos, Bustamante Juan Carlos. (2020). Analysis of the applicability and utility of a gamified didactics with exergames at primary schools: Qualitative findings from a natural experiment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231269

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interviews with all eight participating teachers in the natural experiment. Initial skepticism gave way to positive attitudes once teachers could see and understand the class dynamics and the intervention's compatibility with the curriculum.

> "The first response with which the male researcher-teacher came across when considering the experiment in all the participating schools was teachers’ skepticism, along with their doubts about the intervention’s applicability."

### Quintas-Hijós Alejandro 2020 (2)

Quintas-Hijós Alejandro, Peñarrubia-Lozano Carlos, Bustamante Juan Carlos. (2020). Analysis of the applicability and utility of a gamified didactics with exergames at primary schools: Qualitative findings from a natural experiment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231269

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Teacher interviews in the same natural experiment showed that a previous condition for future application was sufficient training and knowledge, but teachers did not prioritize training in exergames and gamification, leading to low expectations of use.

> "Therefore, this intervention would be more applicable if teachers had more specific training in exergames and gamification. However, the required training was not placed as a priority because teachers carry a heavy training load in other new trends."
