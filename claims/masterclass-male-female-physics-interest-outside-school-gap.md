Questionnaire analysis of about 500 students attending the Pisa Masterclass from 2010 to 2016 (300 male, 200 female); the conclusion reports "almost 80% of male students declare to be interested in physics outside school, compared to 46% of female students". No effect size or significance test is printed.

> "Looking for differences in answers from male and female students, we notice that almost 80% of male students declare to be interested in physics outside school, compared to 46% of female students."
