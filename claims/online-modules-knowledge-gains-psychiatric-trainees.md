`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that across studies of brief online modules on topics such as catatonia and tobacco use disorder, "there were significant increases in knowledge [39,40] or improved levels of stigma [41] between pre- and posttests, with some of the improvements being retained at the 3-month or 6-month follow-up." Most included studies used 1-group pretest-posttest designs with subjective measures.
