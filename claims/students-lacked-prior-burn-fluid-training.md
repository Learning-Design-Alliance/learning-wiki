Demographic section of the cross-sectional survey (n = 356): 248 of 356 (69.7%) reported no fluid resuscitation training and IV fluid follow-up during the acute phase for burn patients, and 230 (64.6%) had no practical training in surgery or burn ICU. The article states "more than two‐thirds of the students had not received any prior training".
