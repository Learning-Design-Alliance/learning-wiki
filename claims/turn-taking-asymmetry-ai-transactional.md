### Morris 2026

Morris, C., & Maes, P. (2026). When Peers Outperform AI (and When They Don't): Interaction Quality Over Modality. [doi:10.22318/cscl2026.989526](https://doi.org/10.22318/cscl2026.989526)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive turn-taking analysis of recorded peer and AI sessions; means and ratios are descriptive, no inferential test was reported for this contrast.

> "HQ-PP dyads demonstrated more balanced contributions (turn ratio ≈ 0.45-0.55), while LQ-PP students showed greater imbalance (turn ratios ranging 0.20-0.80). AI sessions showed extreme asymmetry: students provided minimal responses (M=5 words per turn) compared to AI's lengthier explanations (M=27 words)."
