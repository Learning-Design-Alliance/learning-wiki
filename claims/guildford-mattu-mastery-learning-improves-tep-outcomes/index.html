Evidence¶

Pelly T. 2021¶

Pelly T., Vance-Daniel J., Linder C. (2021). Characteristics of laparoscopic and open hernia repair simulation models: a systematic review. Hernia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8251686/

q3 · i? · review · r2

Randomised controlled trial of 50 surgical residents comparing a simulation-based mastery-learning curriculum using the Guildford MATTU TEP model against standard apprenticeship training; the review reports the mastery-learning group "perform TEP faster, with higher operative performance scores". The article prints no effect size.

"Of the 50 surgical residents who participated, the 26 enrolled in the “mastery-learning” curriculum were found on assessment to perform TEP faster, with higher operative performance scores assessed via the Global Operative Assessment of Laparoscopic Skills (GOALS) tool."
