`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Descriptive analysis of cognitive ability covariate means across profiles in six samples (Fig. 4). Absolutistic profiles showed average or slightly below-average abilities; the sophisticated profile showed an inconsistent pattern across samples.

> "Even though these results were not consistent across profiles, they point to above-average cognitive abilities in (highly) sophisticated profiles."
