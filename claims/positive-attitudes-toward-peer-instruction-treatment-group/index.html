Al-Hebaishi 2017¶

Safaa Mohammad Al-Hebaishi. (2017). The Effect of Peer Instruction Method on Pre-Service Teachers' Conceptual Comprehension of Methodology Course. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n3p70

26-item five-point Likert questionnaire administered to the treatment group (N = 41) at semester's end; Cronbach's alpha was .950. Item means ranged "between 4.5500- and 3.4000" with a total mean of 4.258; 92% agreed peer instruction created a supportive environment.
