## Evidence

### Lore 2016

Lore, M. D., Wang, A. H., & Buckley, M. (2016). Effectiveness of a Parent-Child Home Numeracy Intervention on Urban Catholic School First Grade Students. Journal of Catholic Education, 19(3). http://dx.doi.org/10.15365/joce.1903082016

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Second ANCOVA comparing the treatment group (n = 12) with a matched control group (n = 21) created to control for parental education differences. Posttest Total means were 45.67 (treatment) vs 33.05 (matched control). The article reports "a large effect size (partial eta-squared = .12)".

> "Results also indicated a statistical significant difference between the experimental group and the matched control group on the GMADE Total Test score (F(1, 30) = 4.26, p < .05) with a large effect size (partial eta-squared = .12)"
