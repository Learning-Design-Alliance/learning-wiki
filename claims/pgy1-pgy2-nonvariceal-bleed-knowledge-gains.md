### Wilkoff MH 2025

Wilkoff MH, Seltzer ES, Piniella NR, Seepersaud H, Loanzon P, Kurtz S, Salonia J, Jodorkovsky D. (2025). Acute Upper Gastrointestinal Bleeding: A Hands-On Simulation Case for Internal Medicine Residents Improves Knowledge and Confidence. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11541

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post single-group assessment within a hands-on high-fidelity simulation curriculum; 129 presimulation and 118 postsimulation respondents. The article reports statistically significant knowledge gains for "treatment of nonvariceal GI bleeds" with no effect size printed.

> "PGY1 and PGY2 residents had a statistically significant increase in their knowledge postsimulation with regards to treatment of nonvariceal GI bleeds (PGY 1: p < .001, PGY 2: p = .001; Table 1)."
