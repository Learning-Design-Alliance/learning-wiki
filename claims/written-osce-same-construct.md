## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The observed written-OSCE correlation of 0.849 rose to 0.998 after disattenuation for unreliability, indicating the two formats assess largely identical constructs. [→ McManus IC 2013](#mcmanus-ic-2013)

## Evidence

### McManus IC 2013

McManus IC, Woolf Katherine, Dacre Jane, Paice Elisabeth, Dewberry Chris. (2013). The Academic Backbone: longitudinal continuities in educational achievement from secondary school and medical school to MRCP(UK) and the specialist register in UK medical students and doctors. BMC Medicine 11:242. https://doi.org/10.1186/1741-7015-11-242

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

Reliability and disattenuation analysis in the UCLMS Cohort Study using Cronbach's alpha composites (written 0.909, OSCE/practical 0.796). The printed correlation r = 0.849 (P<0.001, n = 726) is a large effect size, and the article reports the disattenuated value "0.849/√(0.909 × 0.796) = 0.998".

> "the disattenuated correlation between written and OSCE examinations was 0.849/√(0.909 × 0.796) = 0.998. Written and OSCE examinations can therefore be construed as largely assessing identical constructs"
