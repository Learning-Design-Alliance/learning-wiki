The article argues, citing prior work (reference 5, Hunter, Laursen and Seymour 2007), that UGR fosters professional identity development because "the research experience focuses on asking as well as answering questions", unlike the classroom. This is an interpretive claim drawing on cited literature.

> "An UGR is where a student realises for the ﬁrst time that the classroom setting provides answer s while the research experience focuses on asking as well as answering questions."
