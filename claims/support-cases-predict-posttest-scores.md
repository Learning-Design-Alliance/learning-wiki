## Evidence

### Palmer Edward 2014

Palmer Edward, Devitt Peter. (2014). The assessment of a structured online formative assessment program: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear regression on Year 2 post-test scores with Group, time online, and support and incident cases completed as predictors. The article reports an unstandardised increase of 0.13 units per support case; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "Both group (p = 0.0012) and the number of support cases completed (p = 0.013) were found to be predictive of post-test scores. For every single unit increase in the number of support cases completed, the post-test score increased by an average of 0.13 units (p = 0.013)."
