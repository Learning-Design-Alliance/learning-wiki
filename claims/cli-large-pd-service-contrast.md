# Treatment teachers received substantially more literacy-focused professional development than control teachers (total PD difference 61.39 hours, ES = 0.77)

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Treatment teachers reported substantially more literacy-related PD than control teachers: 61.39 more total PD hours (ES = 0.77, p<.0001), including 30.28 more institute/seminar hours (ES = 0.99) and 27.66 more coaching hours (ES = 1.17). [→ Parkinson 2015](#parkinson-2015)

## Evidence

### Parkinson 2015

Parkinson, J., Meakin, J., & Salinger, T. (2015). Longitudinal Impacts of the Children's Literacy Initiative Professional Development, Coaching, and Model Classroom Intervention on Early Literacy Achievement. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562357

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Spring teacher survey contrast analysis of 446 teachers (239 treatment, 207 control) reported in Table 3. Total PD difference was 61.39 hours with effect size 0.77 (p<.0001); coaching hours differed by 27.66 with effect size 1.17, a large effect.

> "As designed, a large service contrast was observed in the amount and type of professional development and coaching received by treatment and comparison teachers, with treatment teachers reporting that they had received substantially more literacy-focused professional development."
