Angela Johnson 2020¶

Angela Johnson. (2020). Dual language education and academic growth. NWEA Research Working Paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/dual-language-education-and-academic-growth/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The piecewise multilevel growth model applied to administrative data from a large school district found that "dual language participants grew faster than nonparticipants during each school year in grades 2 to 5" while losing more over summers. No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"In math, dual language participants grew faster than nonparticipants during each school year in grades 2 to 5 but lost more learning during subsequent summers."
