### Bruun 2014

Bruun, J. and Christiansen, F. V. (2014). Kinesthetic activities in physics instruction: Image schematic justification and design based on didactic situations. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1410.4602](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1410.4602)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative analysis of student-generated written accounts collected after a teaching unit in which upper secondary students performed the kinesthetic activities while discussing and writing descriptions, concepts, and explanations. The authors note the explanation is not wrong: force needed to sustain constant velocity is independent of velocity, but increasing velocity requires a larger force.

> "The example in Table 2 clearly reflects to topology of effort­resistance­flow. More effort begot more movement. The physical concept of (G­)forces acting through the string is imprecise, and the explanation also follows the effort­resistance­flow topology"
