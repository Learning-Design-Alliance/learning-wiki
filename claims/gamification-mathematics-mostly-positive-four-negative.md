`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative synthesis of 45 quantitative studies (4,345 students, ages 13–38, 23 countries) comparing gamified and traditional mathematics instruction. The review reports "Only four studies reported negative effects", two in secondary and two in higher education, across general mathematics, calculus, and algebra.

> "Overall, the majority of the 45 studies included in this review reported positive effects of gamification on motivation and engagement in mathematics.
