Interpretation of marginally significant interaction trends (p < .10) across the two phase transitions in the HLM analyses. The authors conclude that "even within a single ALT, different forms of feedback can play distinct, phase-specific roles in SRL," consistent with prior work contrasting dashboards with strategy prompts.

"Thus, our findings highlight that, even within a single ALT, different forms of feedback can play distinct, phase-specific roles in SRL."
