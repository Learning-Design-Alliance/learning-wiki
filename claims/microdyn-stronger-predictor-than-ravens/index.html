Evidence¶

Shute 2016¶

Shute, V. J., Wang, L., Greiff, S., Zhao, W., & Moore, G. (2016). Measuring problem solving skills via stealth assessment in an engaging video game. Computers in Human Behavior, 63, 106-117. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2016.05.047

q3 · i3 · design · r2

Correlational analysis in the validation study comparing the stealth assessment estimate's relation to the two MicroDYN components. The article reports rule application at r = 0.43 (p < 0.01) versus rule acquisition at r = 0.24, not significant, and attributes the difference to conceptual similarity between MicroDYN and the game.

"our problem solving estimate was more strongly related to MicroDYN 's rule-application facet ( r ¼ 0.43, p < 0.01) than rule-acquisition (r ¼ 0.24, NS)."
