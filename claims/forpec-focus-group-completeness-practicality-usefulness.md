Kaneko Regina Mayumi Utiyama, Monteiro Inês, Lopes Maria Helena Baena de Moraes. (2022). Form for planning and elaborating high fidelity simulation scenarios: A validation study. PLoS ONE 17(9): e0274239. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0274239

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative analysis of two focus groups (eight participants, seven nurses and one doctor, in groups of four), lasting 48 and 74 minutes, held after the scenario test. Comments were categorized into "completeness, practicality and usefulness".

> "The footage was transcribed in full, analyzed and categorized as to completeness (15 comments), practicality (nine comments) and usefulness (four comments), according to textual statements identified by the number assigned to each professional"
