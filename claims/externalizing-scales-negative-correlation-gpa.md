## Evidence

### Flores Jerome 2022

Flores Jerome, Caqueo-Urízar Alejandra, López Verónica, Acevedo Daniel. (2022). Symptomatology of attention deficit, hyperactivity and defiant behavior as predictors of academic achievement. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-022-03714-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Correlational analysis of the primary school sample (n = 1754) reported in Results, Table 2. Attention problems showed the largest correlation with GPA (r = −.359, p < .01), hyperactivity r = −.254, defiant behavior r = −.229. In secondary school all three correlations were negative and significant but lower in magnitude, with attention problems again highest (r = −.285).

> "The most significant association was the inverse correlation between hyperactivity and general academic achievement."
