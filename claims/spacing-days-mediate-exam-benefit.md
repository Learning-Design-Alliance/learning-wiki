### Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick 2025

GSEM mediation analysis of the within-class experiment (n = 143) with controls for gender, ethnicity, academic level, and native language. The printed path coefficient for practice days on exam scores is b = 0.088, controlling for total questions; no significant question-count effect was found.
