Evidence¶

Lasha Labadze 2023¶

Lasha Labadze, Maya Grigolia and Lela Machaidze. (2023). Role of AI chatbots in education: systematic literature review. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00426-1

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Deng and Yu (2023), that "Chatbot technology demonstrated a substantial impact on overall learning outcomes" and significant enhancements in knowledge retention, with nonsignificant effects on critical thinking, learning engagement, and motivation. No effect sizes are printed in the review.

"Chatbot technology demonstrated a substantial impact on overall learning outcomes, regardless of fac- tors such as intervention duration and learning content"
