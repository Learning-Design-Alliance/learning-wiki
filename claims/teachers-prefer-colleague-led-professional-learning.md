# Teachers prefer professional learning led by their colleagues over training from outside consultants

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When ILC workshops are contrasted with traditional professional development offered by outside consultants, teachers prefer learning from and with their colleagues, trusting colleagues' knowledge of local needs and context. [→ Lotan 2019](#lotan-2019)

## Evidence

### Lotan 2019

Lotan, R. A., & Burns, D. (2019). The Instructional Leadership Corps: Teachers leading sustainable professional learning in their communities. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/instructional-leadership-corps-teachers-leading

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative casebook finding from the Lessons Learned chapter, based on interviews and observations across four ILC sites; the report states that "teachers prefer learning from and with their colleagues" and that local leaders can demonstrate, not just describe, instructional shifts.

> "When ILC workshops are contrasted with traditional professional development offered by outside consultants, teachers prefer learning from and with their colleagues."
