### Ibukunoluwa Bukola 2026 (2)

Ibukunoluwa Bukola, Eric Brewe, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Meagan Sundstrom. (2026). Characterizing adopters of named active learning methods in physics: An interview study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.14311](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.14311)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview coding of supports by method (Fig. 6). The article reports "Community support and instructor agency" were most common overall; three of four SCALE-UP instructors acknowledged administrative support for transforming their physical classroom, and all ISLE instructors reported community support.

> "Community support and instructor agency to choose how they teach their physics courses were the most commonly reported forms of support across all methods (first panel of Fig. 6)"
