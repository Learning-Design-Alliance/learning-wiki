## Evidence

### Zhandire 2026

Zhandire, T. T., Bakare-Fatungase, O. D., & Mkhize, N. (2026). Mental health, resilience, and academic outcomes among university students: a global scoping review with particular attention to African and Global South contexts. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05242-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's descriptive synthesis of 53 included studies from 25 countries reports that "none of the three-construct studies were conducted in sub-Saharan Africa", with nine African studies (17.0%) total.

> "Twenty-six studies (49.1%) examined all three simultaneously. The most common two-construct pairing was mental health and resilience (n = 12; 22.6%), followed by mental health and academic outcomes (n = 11; 20.8%). Notably, none of the three-construct studies were conducted in sub-Saharan Africa."
