Evidence¶

James 2010¶

James, K. H. (2010). Sensori-motor experience leads to changes in visual processing in the developing brain. Developmental Science, 13(2), 279-288. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2009.00883.x

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Four-alternative forced-choice letter identification administered each training session in the same twelve children. The interaction fell short of significance ("p < .07, partial eta squared = .50"); means were descriptive only (experimental 7.5 to 8.0; control 7.8 unchanged). Equivalence was not tested.

"Behavioural results measured before and after training revealed a trend towards an interaction among training group and pre-and post-training letter recognition (F(1,5) = 5.0, p < .07, partial eta squared = .50)."
