### Kuhfeld 2022

Kuhfeld, M., & Lewis, K. (2022). Student achievement in 2021-22: Cause for hope and continued urgency. NWEA Research brief. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/student-achievement-in-2021-22-cause-for-hope-and-continued-urgency/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Large-scale observational analysis of MAP Growth assessment data from 8.3 million students in grades 3-8 tested between 2018-19 and 2021-22, compared with a similarly sized pre-pandemic sample. The brief reports "encouraging early signs of academic rebounding in 2021–22" with gains paralleling pre-pandemic trends in many grades.

> "this new research reveals some encouraging early signs of academic rebounding in 2021–22: reading and math achievement gains paralleled pre-pandemic trends in many grades, with stronger rebounding in math and among younger students."
