# Standardized benchmarks are often too generic to reflect the needs of innovative educational applications

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Standardized benchmarks conduct model-to-model comparisons on closed datasets but often fail to capture educationally relevant capabilities. [→ Li 2026](#li-2026)

## Evidence

### Li 2026

Li, W., Kurimchak, M., & Whitmer, J. (2026). ATS Field Note: AI Model Evaluation. ATS Hub Field Note Series. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/300

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The field note's synthesis of evaluation methods characterizes standardized benchmarks, noting they are "often too generic to reflect the needs of innovative educational applications" and illustrating with the algebra hint example. This is the authors' framework argument, not a measured comparison.

> "However, these methods are often too generic to reflect the needs of innovative educational applications.For instance, a benchmark might show that a model answers algebra questions correctly, yet say nothing about whether it can give a struggling student a helpful hint without giving away the answer."
