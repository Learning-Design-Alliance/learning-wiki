## Evidence

### Pečjak and Pirc 2018

Sonja Pečjak, Tina Pirc. (2018). Developing Summarizing Skills in 4th Grade Students: Intervention Programme Effects. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, June 2018, Volume 10, Issue 5, 571-581. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2018541306

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Univariate ANOVA comparing 110 EG and 74 CG students on Summ_2 (a longer-text summary, max 11 points) after the 5-month programme; EG mean 5.85 vs CG 4.82, F = 6.77, p = .010, "the effect size was small" (η² = .036).

> "The results in Table 2 show that EG had significantly higher achievement in summarizing than CG had after the intervention (Summ_2), but the effect size was small (Cohen, Miles, & Shevlin, 2001)."
