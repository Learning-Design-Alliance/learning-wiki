von Bechtolsheim Felix, Franz Andreas, Schmidt Sofia, Schneider Alfred, La Rosée Felicitas, Radulova-Mauersberger Olga, Krause-Jüttler Grit, Hümpel Anja, Bodenstedt Sebastian, Speidel Stefanie, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repetition counts until proficiency (min. 90 points twice) were compared between the randomized groups for the six FRS training tasks; the printed means and p-values above show the VR group needed more repetitions in three tasks. No effect size is printed.

> "the VR group needed significantly more repetitions in the FRS Ring tower (Robot:2.48 vs. VR: 5.45; p < 0.001), Knot Tying (Robot: 5.34 vs. VR: 8.13; p = 0.006), and Vessel Energy Dissection (Robot: 2 vs. VR: 2.38; p = 0.001) tasks until reaching proficiency."
