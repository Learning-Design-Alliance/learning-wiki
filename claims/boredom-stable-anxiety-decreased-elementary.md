## Evidence

### Lichtenfeld 2022

Lichtenfeld, S., Pekrun, R., Marsh, H. W., Nett, U., & Reiss, K. (2022). Achievement Emotions and Elementary School Children’s Academic Performance: Longitudinal Models of Developmental Ordering. Journal of Educational Psychology, in press. https://osf.io/ywt8q/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent neighbor change models across the three annual waves found boredom stable, learning-related anxiety decreasing from Year 2 to 3, and test anxiety stable — counter to the hypothesis that negative emotions would increase. No effect sizes for these changes are printed.

> "Boredom remained low and stable over the three years. Counter to our expectations, anxiety related to learning decreased from Year 2 to 3 and remained stable thereafter. Similarly, test anxiety remained relatively stable from Year 3 to 4."
