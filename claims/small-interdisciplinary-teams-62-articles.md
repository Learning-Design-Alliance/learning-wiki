Review synthesis of specific teaching and learning strategies in Part 3. The review reports small interdisciplinary teamwork in 62 articles, with team sizes ranging from pairs to groups of three to seven students.

> "Having students intentionally work together in small interdisciplinary teams was explicitly reported in 62 of the articles. Teams varied in size, for example, from pairs [49, 56, 58, 59, 63, 65, 68, 108, 128, 137] to groups of three to seven students [70, 76, 78, 79, 85–87, 100, 102, 106, 115, 116, 121, 129, 140, 148, 149]."
