### Hinzmann Dominik 2023

Hinzmann Dominik, Wijnen-Meijer Marjo, Corazza Laura, Becker Veronika, Kagerbauer Simone, Haseneder Rainer, Berberat Pascal O., Jedlicska Nana. (2023). Experiences of medical students and nursing trainees from unexpected death through simulation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04638-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group finding on characteristics determining the simulation's success. Prebriefing deliberately omitted any warning of the manikin's possible death so participants experienced the unexpected death authentically; this is a qualitative participant-perception result with no effect size.

> "Our interviewees considered the unexpected character as the key to the simulation's success."
