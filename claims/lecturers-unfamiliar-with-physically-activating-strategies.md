## Evidence

### Rupp Robert 2025

Rupp Robert, Wallmann-Sperlich Birgit, Bucksch Jens. (2025). Strategies and barriers to implementing physically active teaching in universities from the perspective of lecturers: a qualitative study. BMC Public Health 25, Article 855. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22075-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with 16 lecturers at Heidelberg University of Education, analysed by structured content analysis. The article reports that "just under half (7 out of 16)" could not name any measures, and that 11 of 16 (68.75%) knew and used no or only 1–3 methods.

> "just under half (7 out of 16) of the lecturers were initially unable to name any physically activating measures or teaching methods that they could spontaneously think of to their own teaching."
