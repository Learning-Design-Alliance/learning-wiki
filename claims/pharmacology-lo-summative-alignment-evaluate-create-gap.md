## Evidence

### Kelly‐Laubscher Róisín 2025

Kelly‐Laubscher Róisín, Flaherty Caoimhe C., Herzel Lea, and Barry Órla P. (2025). Deciphering the Language of Assessment in Pharmacology Summative Written Assessments. Pharmacology Research & Perspectives 13, no. 6: e70116. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.70116

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Mapping of learning-outcome verbs from modules across four programmes onto summative assessment verbs, categorised by Bloom's revised taxonomy; the analysis "revealed good alignment" for the four lower tiers with the printed proportions.

> "Our analysis revealed good alignment between the module LOs and summative assessment action verbs, particularly within the four tiers of “Remember,” “Understand,” “Apply,” and “Analyze” (100%, 82%, 70%, and 78%, respectively) (Figure 6C)."

### Kelly‐Laubscher Róisín 2025 (2)

Kelly‐Laubscher Róisín, Flaherty Caoimhe C., Herzel Lea, and Barry Órla P. (2025). Deciphering the Language of Assessment in Pharmacology Summative Written Assessments. Pharmacology Research & Perspectives 13, no. 6: e70116. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.70116

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same learning-outcome-to-assessment mapping; "less alignment" was found for Evaluate and especially Create, with the printed proportions 68% and 17%.

> "However, there was less alignment for action verbs associated with “Evaluate,” and in particular “Create” (68% and 17%, respectively)."
