## Evidence

### Jiang N 2024

Jiang N, Zhang Y, Liang S, Lyu X, Chen S, Huang X, Pan H. (2024). Effectiveness of Virtual Simulations Versus Mannequins and Real Persons in Medical and Nursing Education: Meta-Analysis and Trial Sequential Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/56195

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis by discipline within the procedural-skills meta-analysis of 10 RCTs: 7 medicine studies and 3 nursing studies. The nursing subgroup favored mannequins or RPs (SMD=–0.55; 95% CI –1.07 to –0.03; P=.04); the medicine subgroup showed no significant difference. Subgroup difference was not significant at P=.07.

> "Only the nursing group showed a significant difference between VSs and mannequins or RPs (SMD=0.06; 95% CI –0.33 to 0.46; I2=64%; P<.001 for medicine and SMD=–0.55; 95% CI –1.07 to –0.03, I2=69%; P=.04 for nursing)"
