## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participating in profession-role exchange further increased RIPLS role understanding scores for medical, pharmacy and nursing students (p < 0.05), with gains of 21.4%, 18.1% and 19.0% versus control gains of 10.9%, 11.8% and 8.5%. [→ Wang J 2020](#wang-j-2020)
