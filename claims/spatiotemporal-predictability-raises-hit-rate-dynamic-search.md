## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Hit rates were higher for predictable than variable targets (main effect of Predictability in a GLMM over responses). [→ Nir Shalev 2024](#nir-shalev-2024)

## Evidence

### Nir Shalev 2024

Nir Shalev, Sage Boettcher & Anna C. Nobre. (2024). Spatiotemporal predictions guide attention throughout the adult lifespan. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00281-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Online dynamic visual-search experiment with 300 participants aged 20-80; GLMM on hit rate with Predictability, Interval and Age as fixed effects. The article reports "main effects of Predictability ( β = 0.22; SE = 0.06; z = 3.36; p < 0.001)", indicating higher detection of predictable targets.

> "Analysis of the hit rate forﬁnding targets in the dynamic visual-search task revealed main effects of Predictability ( β = 0.22; SE = 0.06; z = 3.36; p < 0.001)"
