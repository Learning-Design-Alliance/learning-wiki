Rob J. Nadolski, Hans G. K. Hummel, Ellen Rusman, Kevin Ackermans. (2021). Rubric formats for the formative assessment of oral presentation skills acquisition in secondary education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10030-7

In the mixed ANOVA comparing the video-enhanced (n = 51) and text-based (n = 57) rubric conditions, "no significant differences between experimental groups were found" at both measures, so the hypothesis that video-enhanced rubrics would yield better performance growth was rejected.
