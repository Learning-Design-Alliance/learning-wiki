### Cassandra A. Paul 2025

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

NU transcript analysis of the never-reformed PhysicsB course, graded against a never-enrolled standard cohort (2.71 ± 0.06 vs 2.68 ± 0.06) to rule out grading drift; CLASP students' grades differ by only these "small effects" despite 22% more HM students advancing.

> "We suggest that these small effects (Cohen's d of 0.08 ± 0.06 for NonHM and 0 .03 ± 0.17 for HM) between the CLASP and NonCLASP students in PhysicsB"

### Cassandra A. Paul 2025 (2)

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of final-2.5-year GPAs (N = 487, 19 majors, ≥4 terms and ≥43 units after PhysicsA) found an unadjusted apparent CLASP advantage (3.16 vs 3.02) that the article attributes to a time-to-degree confound; unadjusted results not tested statistically.

> "the difference in upper-division GPA between the CLASP and NonCLASP courses becomes 0.022 ± 0.043 so the GPA effect is removed and we conclude that the two groups are roughly equally prepared for upper-division work."
