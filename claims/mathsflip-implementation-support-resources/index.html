Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.isrctn.com/search?q=&filters=intervention%3AMathsFlip%2CfunderName%3AEducation+Endowment+Foundation&searchType=advanced-search

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation drawing on five instruments (staff interviews, online surveys, telephone interviews, school visits with lesson observation, and teacher/pupil focus groups), analysed by triangulation. Teachers valued prompt technical fixes and the project manager's coaching; pupils enjoyed the online resources and their immediate feedback.

"Two key elements were identified which indicated the likelihood of successful implementation: ongoing support for teachers and ample mathematics resources."
