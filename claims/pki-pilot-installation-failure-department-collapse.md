## Evidence

### David W Chadwick 1999

David W Chadwick, Rana Tassabehji and Andrew Young. (1999). Experiences of Using a Public Key Infrastructure for the Preparation of Examination Papers. IT Institute, University of Salford. http://www.salford.ac.uk/its024/X500.htm

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational usage statistics from the live pilot in two departments and the Examinations office. The authors report that the CMS administrator's installation failed (Windows 3.1 with Lotus Notes suspected), so "half of the external examiners could no longer participate in the pilot project" and CMS produced no electronic result.

> "Because of the failure to install the security software on the PC of one departmental administrator, this meant that half of the external examiners could no longer participate in the pilot project."
