## Evidence

### Alqahtani SS 2025

Alqahtani SS. (2025). Predictive validity of early and mid-year literacy assessments for end-of-year word reading fluency. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327242

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression with initial (mid-year) WRF entered first to control for prior reading skill, followed by LNF, LSF, and PSF blocks. The grade 1 mid-year model reached "explained variance to 88.9%" with PSF2 significant at β = 0.094, a negligible standardized effect; kindergarten LNF2 added 5% variance (β = 0.325).

> "Adding PSF2 increased the explained variance to 88.9% (R2 = 0.889, F = 127.525, p < .001), with PSF2 showing a small but significant contribution (β = 0.094, p = .032)."
