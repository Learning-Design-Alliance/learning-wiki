## Evidence

### Bomholt Katrine Bjørnshave 2019

Bomholt Katrine Bjørnshave, Krogh Lise Qvirin, Bomholt Svend Rosendahl, Nebsbjerg Mette Amalie, Thim Troels, Løfgren Bo. (2019). Three-Month Retention of Basic Life Support with an Automated External Defibrillator Using a Two-Stage versus Four-Stage Teaching Technique. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/1394972

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective follow-up of the same randomised layperson cohorts (n=65 two-stage, n=64 four-stage) tested in a simulated cardiac arrest scenario; mean number of 17 adequately performed actions fell from roughly 16 immediately after training to roughly 13.4-13.7 at three months in both groups. The article reports no significance test for this within-group decay.

> "Total average skills adequately performed (of 17) decayed from 16.1 versus 16.2 to 13.3 versus 13.7 among laypersons trained with the two-stage (n=65) and the four-stage (n=64) technique, respectively."
