## Evidence

### Jason Paul Siko 2011

Jason Paul Siko. (2011). Homemade PowerPoint Games. http://it.coe.uga.edu/wwild/pptgames

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The article's narrative review of prior studies (Parker 2004; Barbour, Clesson, & Adams 2011; Barbour, Kinsella, & Rieber 2011; Siko et al. 2011) reports no significant differences; the Siko et al. study used approximately 150 students across two unit tests.

> "However, all of the published research to date on homemade PowerPoint games has shown no significant difference in performance between control (i.e., students that neither constructed nor played games) and treatment groups."
