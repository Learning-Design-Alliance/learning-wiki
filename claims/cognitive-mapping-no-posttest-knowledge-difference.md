# No significant difference between cognitive-mapping and verbal-text groups on subject-matter knowledge pretest or posttest scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Independent t-tests found no significant group difference in pretest or posttest scores. [→ Wu Bian 2016](#wu-bian-2016)

## Evidence

### Wu Bian 2016

Wu Bian, Wang Minhong, Grotzer Tina A., Liu Jun, Johnson Janice M. (2016). Visualizing complex processes using a cognitive-mapping tool to support the learning of clinical reasoning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0734-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest/posttest subject-matter knowledge tests (three multiple-choice, ten extended-matching, four essay questions; scores rescaled 0 to 1), marked by two blind domain experts with inter-rater kappa 0.93. No group difference; the article reports no effect size, so equivalence was not tested.

> "The independent t-tests showed no significant difference between the two groups in the pretest scores (experimental group: M = 0.51, SD = 0.12; control group: M = 0.52, SD = 0.19) and posttest scores (experimental group: M = 0.56, SD = 0.21; control group: M = 0.55, SD = 0.21)."
