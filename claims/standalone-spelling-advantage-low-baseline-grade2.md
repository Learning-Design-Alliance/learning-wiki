## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Among grade 2 students with a low FRA Spelling baseline score, the standalone intervention outperformed the embedded intervention by 25 points, equivalent to 0.27 standard deviation and 11 percentile rank points. [→ Foorman 2017](#foorman-2017)

## Evidence

### Foorman 2017

Foorman, B., Herrera, S., Dombek, J., Schatschneider, C., & Petscher, Y. (2017). The relative effectiveness of two approaches to early literacy intervention in grades K–2 (REL 2017–251). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Southeast. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Subgroup analysis from the grade 2 hierarchical linear model probing a significant baseline-by-treatment interaction; adjusted means were "404 in standalone intervention schools and 379 in embedded intervention schools". The printed effect size is 0.27 standard deviation (25 points, 11 percentile rank points), significant after the Benjamini–Hochberg correction.

> "The average FRA Spelling outcome among a subgroup of grade 2 students with a low FRA Spelling baseline score was 404 in standalone intervention schools and 379 in embedded intervention schools."
