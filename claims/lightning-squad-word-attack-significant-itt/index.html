Evidence¶

Tutoring with the Lightning Squad 2020¶

Tutoring with the Lightning Squad. (2020). Success for All Foundation. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/tutoring-with-the-lightning-squad/

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Cluster randomized study across 23 schools of 390 grade 2-3 students performing in the lowest 33rd percentile, comparing intent-to-treat tutored students to controls. The article reports "significantly higher scores on the Word Attack scale" with a printed effect size of ES = +0.18, qualifying the program for a Strong rating.

"Students randomly assigned to the group of students to be tutored using Tutoring with the Lightning Squad (the Intent to Treat sample) had significantly higher scores on the Word Attack scale scores from the Woodcock Reading Mastery Test compared to the control group (ES = +0.18)"
