### Vadla MS 2023

Vadla MS, Mduma ER, Kvaløy JT, Mdoe P, Hhoki BH, Sarangu S, Michael P, Oftedal B, Ersdal H. (2023). Increase in Newborns Ventilated Within the First Minute of Life and Reduced Mortality After Clinical Data-Guided Simulation Training. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000740

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of resuscitation characteristics among the 1320 ventilated newborns across the four study periods (chi-square, P < 0.001). The article reports midwife-provided resuscitation "increasing throughout the study period" and recent simulator training decreasing sharply postintervention.

> "Most resuscitations were provided by midwives, increasing throughout the study period (83.5%–92.7%–98.2%–97.1%, P < 0.001) (Table 4). Healthcare workers who had trained using NeoNatalie Live in the last 7 days before conducting a real resuscitation decreased throughout the study (64.7%–44.6%–6.7%–5.5%) (Table 4)."
