## Evidence

### Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska 2022

Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska. (2022). Effects of Nature (Greenspace) on Cognitive Functioning in School Children and Adolescents: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1217–1254. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09658-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports two quasi-experiments: Johnson et al. found "a small positive effect" for children aged 8–15 after a forested-trail walk, and Stevenson et al. found positive effects for speed and stability of responses in children aged 10–14, with "no improvement in executive attention".

> "Two studies (Johnson et al., 2019 and Stevenson et al., 2019) using similar computer attention tests based on the fish flanker task, reported a significant positive effect on selective attention performance after a 30-min walk in a natural environment with trees."
