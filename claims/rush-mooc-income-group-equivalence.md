## Evidence

### Cevik Arif Alper; Abu-Zidan Fikri M 2025

Cevik Arif Alper; Abu-Zidan Fikri M. (2025). Open-access RUSH protocol course improves knowledge and perceived confidence among healthcare learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07984-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of quiz gains between LIC-LMIC and HIC-UMIC participants; the descriptive gain difference (26.7 vs 20) was not statistically significant (p = 0.274). Both groups' within-group improvements were significant (Wilcoxon, effect sizes −0.971 and −0.957).

> "While there was no statistically significant difference, the post-course quiz median (IQR) scores of participants from LIC-LMIC improved more than those from HIC-UMIC, with scores of 26.7 (6.7–40) and 20 (13.3–33.3), respectively (p = 0.274, Mann-Whitney U)."
