# Students in alternative schools experience extraordinarily high lost-instruction rates, with Black boys losing 235 days per 100 enrolled

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the nation's alternative schools (excluding juvenile justice schools), Black boys lost 235 days per 100 students and boys with disabilities lost 170 days per 100 at the secondary level. [→ Losen 2020](#losen-2020)

## Evidence

### Losen 2020

Losen, D. J., & Martinez, P. (2020). Lost opportunities: How disparate school discipline continues to drive differences in the opportunity to learn. Learning Policy Institute; Center for Civil Rights Remedies at the Civil Rights Project, UCLA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608537

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

First-of-its-kind descriptive analysis of 2015-16 CRDC data for alternative schools, excluding juvenile justice system schools, quoting the printed per-group rates. The report calls this the first documentation of alternative schools' lost-instruction rates.

> "Black boys lost 235 days per 100 students. Black girls lost 156 days per 100 students. Boys with disabilities lost 170 days per 100 students."
