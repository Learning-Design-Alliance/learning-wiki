## Evidence

### Özdas and Batdi 2017

Faysal Özdas & Veli Batdi. (2017). A Thematic-based Meta Analytic Study Regarding the Effect of Creativity on Academic Success and Learning Retention. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i3.2043

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Thematic meta-analysis of seven CT studies using pretest-posttest control group designs, analyzed via random effects model in CMA/MetaWin. The REM effect size was "ES= 1.336" with 95% CI [0.825, 1.847], large by Cohen's classification.

> "the standard error is 0.26 and the upper limit for a confidence interval of 95% is 1.847, while the lower limit is 0.825 and the effect size is ES= 1.336"
