# Children shift from counting to memory-based retrieval strategies in single-digit addition over a 1.2-year interval in middle childhood

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Over 1.2 years, children's use of memory-based retrieval strategies increased and counting strategies decreased during arithmetic problem solving. [→ Qin Shaozheng 2014](#qin-shaozheng-2014)

## Evidence

### Qin Shaozheng 2014

Qin Shaozheng, Cho Soohyun, Chen Tianwen, Rosenberg-Lee Miriam, Geary David C., Menon Vinod. (2014). Hippocampal-neocortical functional reorganization underlies children's cognitive development. Nature Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3788

`q3 · i?`

Longitudinal behavioral strategy assessment in 28 children ages 7-9 at two time points 1.2 years apart, using a validated trial-by-trial classification of counting versus direct retrieval. The article reports "increased use of memory-based strategies (t(27)=2.43, P=0.02)" and decreased counting; no effect size is printed.

> "Longitudinal changes in children's problem solving between ages 7–9 involved increased use of memory-based strategies (t(27)=2.43, P=0.02) and decreased use of counting strategies (t(27)=– 2.16, P=0.04)."
