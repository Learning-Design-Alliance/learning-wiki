`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

IRR was calculated as agreements over total ratings for all 24 scored performances by two independent reviewers blinded to participant identity and expertise level, after a structured training phase requiring agreement of at least 0.8 on practice videos.

> "The calculated overall IRR was 0.96 (0.92 for novices and 1 for experts)."
