Sandra Schulz · Bruce M. McLaren · Niels Pinkwart. (2023). Towards a Tutoring System to Support Robotics Activities in Classrooms – Two Wizard-of-Oz Studies. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 33. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00305-2

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Descriptive pre- and post-test comparison in WOZ I using a validated psychometric test of control-structure use (0–7 points). Post-intervention, students “answered 65% of all tasks correctly” with M = 5 (SD = 4.6); the authors describe a slight improvement and report that no statistical significance was calculated due to the small number of participants.

> "The students achieved scores between one and seven points, and answered 65% of all tasks correctly. With M = 5 ( SD = 4.6 ), the mean score also shows a positive trend."
