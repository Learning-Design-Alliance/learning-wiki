# Montessori-schooled students show higher temporal stability than traditionally schooled students in dorsal attention, ventral attention, somatomotor, frontoparietal, and cerebellar networks, but not in visual, limbic, or default mode networks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` STD differed significantly in the DA, VA, SM, FP, and CBL systems, with Montessori-schooled participants consistently lower (more stable). [→ Zanchi 2024](#zanchi-2024)
`q3 i?` No between-group STD differences were observed in the visual, limbic, and default mode systems. [→ Zanchi 2024 (2)](#zanchi-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Zanchi 2024

Zanchi, P., Mullier, E., Fornari, E., Guerrier de Dumast, P., Alemán-Gómez, Y., Ledoux, J.-B., Beaty, R., Hagmann, P., & Denervaud, S. (2024). Differences in spatiotemporal brain network dynamics of Montessori and traditionally schooled students. npj Science of Learning, 9, 45. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00254-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

FDR-corrected system-level STD comparisons across the eight functional systems; five systems showed significant group differences with the printed pFDR values reported here and no effect sizes.

> "signiﬁcant differences emerged between Montessori- and traditionally schooled participants in the DA ( pFDR = 0.020), VA ( pFDR < 0.001), somatomotor (pFDR < 0.001), FP (pFDR = 0.020), and CBL ( pFDR = 0.020) functional systems (Fig. 4b, c)."

### Zanchi 2024 (2)

Zanchi, P., Mullier, E., Fornari, E., Guerrier de Dumast, P., Alemán-Gómez, Y., Ledoux, J.-B., Beaty, R., Hagmann, P., & Denervaud, S. (2024). Differences in spatiotemporal brain network dynamics of Montessori and traditionally schooled students. npj Science of Learning, 9, 45. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00254-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null results for STD in three of the eight functional systems (visual, limbic, default mode), reported with pFDR > 0.110 and no effect sizes; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, no between-group differences were observed for the visual, limbic, and default mode functional systems (pFDR > 0.110)."
