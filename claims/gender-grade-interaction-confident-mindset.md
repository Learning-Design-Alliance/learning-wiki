## Evidence

### Fargol Seifollahi 2026

Fargol Seifollahi, Christian D. Schunn, Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Questioning Your Brilliance in Physics: Differential Shifts in Fixed Mindsets by Grade and Gender. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.11659)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression interaction terms (Table 2): Men with B (OR = 3.38, p = 0.034) and Men with C (OR = 5.15, p = 0.019) for the Confident outcome. Marginal means (Figure 6) show "women with a C grade are nearly three times less likely than men with the same grade to fall into the Confident category."

> "In particular, there was a large additional effect of being a man with a B or a C grade with odds ratios of approximately 3 and 5, respectively, significantly increasing the likelihood of being categorized as Confident compared to women with similar grades."
