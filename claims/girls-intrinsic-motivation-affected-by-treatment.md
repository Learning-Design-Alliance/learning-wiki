## Evidence

### Conradty 2016

Conradty, C. & Bogner, F. X. (2016). Hypertext or Textbook: Effects on Motivation and Gain in Knowledge. Education Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci6030029

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U test on girls' IMI interest/enjoyment subscale scores comparing the CALC and CALT treatment groups, administered via the German IMI immediately after the intervention.

> "Although girls are more interested in the subject with CALT than with TAL (not significant) they are significantly less interested with CALC (MWU: CALC CALT: Z = −2.130, p = 0.033)."

### Conradty 2016 (2)

Conradty, C. & Bogner, F. X. (2016). Hypertext or Textbook: Effects on Motivation and Gain in Knowledge. Education Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci6030029

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports no treatment differences in IMI subscales for boys across the CALC, CALT, and TAL conditions.

> "The boys reported similar intrinsic motivation in all treatments."
