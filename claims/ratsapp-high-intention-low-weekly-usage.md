`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive results from the anonymous end-of-semester usability survey (36 users, 28 non-users). The article reports "Around 70% of students rated their intention to use higher than the neutral value of 4"; these are descriptive percentages, not effect sizes.

> "Around 70% of students rated their intention to use higher than the neutral value of 4. Only about 25% rated it neutral or lower and about 5% did not answer."

Descriptive frequency-of-use results from the usability survey, shown in Fig. 3. The article states the median usage value corresponds to "Twice a month"; percentages are descriptive counts of responses.

> "The most frequent responses to the usage question were “Twice a month” and “Once per semester,” each accounting for approximately 25% of total responses. Only around 5% of respondents reported daily usage."
