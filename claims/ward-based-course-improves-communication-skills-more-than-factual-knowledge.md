## Evidence

### Dircks Monika 2017

Dircks Monika, Mayr Andreas, Freidank Annette, Kornhuber Johannes, Dörje Frank, Friedland Kristina. (2017). Advances in clinical pharmacy education in Germany: a quasi-experimental single-blinded study to evaluate a patient-centred clinical pharmacy course in psychiatry. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1092-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc division of the practical OSCE tasks into hard skills (factual knowledge) and soft skills (communication), assessed by a blinded observer using an evaluation checklist. The article reports both differences as significant, with the soft-skills difference larger; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The intervention group achieved significantly higher scores than the control group in the latter (27.28 ± 8.34 vs. 16.00 ± 9.07 of a maximum of 40 points; p < 0.0001), while the effect in hard skills was smaller (6.73 ± 3.95 vs. 3.83 ± 2.79 of a maximum of 20 points; p < 0.0001)."
