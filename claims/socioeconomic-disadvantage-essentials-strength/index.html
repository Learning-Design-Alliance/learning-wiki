Klugman 2015¶

Klugman, J., Gordon, M. F., Sebring, P. B., & Sporte, S. E. (2015). A First Look at the 5Essentials in Illinois Schools. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://illinois.5-essentials.org/2014_public/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

This analysis used a standardized scale combining school-level free/reduced-price lunch eligibility and area-level poverty and joblessness measures to examine how socioeconomic disadvantage relates to essential support strength. The report states that "The effect of socioeconomic disadvantage is stronger in Chicago than the rest of Illinois."

"Schools located in socioeconomically disadvantaged communities are less likely to be strong in the essentials, compared to schools located in the least disadvantaged communities. The effect of socioeconomic disadvantage is stronger in Chicago than the rest of Illinois."
