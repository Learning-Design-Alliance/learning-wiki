## Evidence

### Laaksonen M 2025

Laaksonen M, Paavilainen E, Koivisto A-M, Halkoaho A. (2025). Genomic competence among nurses: A spotlight on ethics. Nursing Ethics, 32(8). https://doi.org/10.1177/09697330251366594

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-Square analysis in the same cross-sectional survey: among nurses rating ethics understanding excellent/good, 56.1% also rated equity at that level; 98.3% of nurses who rated ethics poorly also rated equity as weak.

> "A Chi-Square test indicated the statistically significant association (p < .001) between understanding ethical issues and understanding equity. 56.1% of those who classified their understanding of ethics related to genomics as excellent/good also felt their understanding of equity in genomics to be at the same level."
