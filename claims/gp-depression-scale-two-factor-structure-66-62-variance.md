## Evidence

### Zhang Yuqin 2025

Zhang Yuqin, Sun Xuemei, Zhong Wen, Sun Dingkui, Gao Yingyan, Chen Xiyang, Zhang Weiru. (2025). Development and validation of a Chinese general practitioners' ability to recognise and manage depression assessment scale: a cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-094404

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Phase 3 exploratory factor analysis of survey responses from 170 valid GP responses (98.84% response rate); KMO was 0.96 with p<0.01, and the analysis found "Two common factors were identified, contributing a cumulative variance of 66.62%". Principal axis factoring with maximum oblique rotation was used.

> "Two common factors were identified, contributing a cumulative variance of 66.62%. The factor loadings of the assessment scale ranged from 0.46 to 0.61, while the communalities were between 0.60 and 0.83"
