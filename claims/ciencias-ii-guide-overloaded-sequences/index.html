q2 · i? · theoretical · r1

Analysis of Table V of the SEP Ciencias II work guide: Block 1 lists 5 expected learnings in 6 hours and Block 2 lists 8 in 5 hours; the article states that for Blocks 3 and 4 "la guía de trabajo pretende alcanzar 7 y 6 aprendizajes esperados respectivamente en un tiempo de 6 horas", and Block 5 has no stated total time.

"En los ejemplos de secuencia para los bloques 3 y 4, la guía de trabajo pretende alcanzar 7 y 6 aprendizajes esperados respectivamente en un tiempo de 6 horas."

Related Claims¶

  • The new Ciencias II curriculum shifts the entry point to physics from measurement of physical properties (1993 plan) to the perception of movement, integrating light and sound from the start of the course — related
  • SEP national monitoring reports indicate insufficient teacher training for the reform: 35% of surveyed teachers requested training in planning and design of didactic activities, and about 30% of directors rated induction-workshop information as poorly adequate or inadequate — related
  • The 2006 reform concentrated physics, previously taught across three secondary years, into a single second-grade course (Ciencias II) with 6 hours per week, raising teacher concern about fostering interest in one school cycle — related
  • Students receive very little climate change instruction, typically only 1 or 2 hours per year — related
  • Students value knowing class routines and report that rushed pacing, especially in Advanced Placement courses, is a barrier to mastering content — related
