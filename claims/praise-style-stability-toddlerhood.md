## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gunderson Elizabeth A. 2013

Gunderson Elizabeth A., Gripshover Sarah J., Romero Carissa, Dweck Carol S., Goldin-Meadow Susan, Levine Susan C. (2013). Parent Praise to 1-3 Year-Olds Predicts Children's Motivational Frameworks 5 Years Later. Child development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal change analysis within the same sample; process praise as a percent of total praise did not significantly differ between 14 and 38 months, t(52) = 1.37, p = .18, and overall praise as a percent of utterances stayed near 3%. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "parents' use of person praise as a percent of total praise was significantly lower at 38 months than at 14 months, t(52) = −5.56, p < .001, whereas parents' use of other praise as a percent of total praise was significantly higher at 38 months than at 14 months, t(52) = 4.70, p < .001"
