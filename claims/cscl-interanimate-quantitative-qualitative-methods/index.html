Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse 2007¶

Gerry Stahl & Friedrich Hesse. (2007). Welcome to the future: ijCSCL volume 2. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-007-9010-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial's interpretive conclusion (type e) drawn from three methodology articles it reviews: Meier, Spada and Rummel's nine-dimension rating scheme, De Laat and colleagues' combined SNA and content analysis, and Rourke and Kanuka's naturalistic-paradigm case study. The editorial states this as its own implication, not a tested result.

"Perhaps the implication of the papers in this issue is that CSCL needs to promote the inter-animation of complementary quantitative and qualitative perspectives rather than hoping to converge on a single ideal method."
