Berkeley J. Dietvorst 2014¶

Berkeley J. Dietvorst, Joseph P. Simmons, and Cade Massey. (2014). Algorithm Aversion: People Erroneously Avoid Algorithms After Seeing Them Err. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000033

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Belief measures in Study 3b (N = 1,036) showed the perfection account failed to support the alternative hypothesis; the aversion effect among perfection-believers was tested with χ²(1, N = 795) = 4.92, p = .027.

"Moreover, in Study 3b, the effect of seeing the model err on the likelihood of betting on the model persisted even among those who thought the model was more perfect than themselves (63% vs. 55%)."
