`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes to Klahr and Robinson a finding that children had considerably more difficulty with all-pegs-occupied Tower of Hanoi problems "despite the number of moves being the same in both problems". Reported as a cited second-hand finding, no effect size printed.

> "Klahr and Robinson (1981), for example, found that children had considerably more difﬁculty solving Tower of Hanoi problems in which they had to end up with all pegs occupied compared to the traditional Tower of Hanoi problem where all disks must end up on one peg."
