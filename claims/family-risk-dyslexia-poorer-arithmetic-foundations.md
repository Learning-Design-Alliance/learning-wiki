## Evidence

### Moll K. 2015

Moll K., Snowling M. J., Göbel S. M., Hulme C. (2015). Early language and executive skills predict variations in number and arithmetic skills in children at family-risk of dyslexia and typically developing controls. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Group comparisons in Table 1 showed the FR group (N = 93) performing below the TD group (N = 76) on measures including one-minute addition (FR 7.99 vs TD 10.26, p < .01) and one-minute subtraction (FR 5.71 vs TD 7.32, p < .01), with Cohen's d values of .47 and .45.

> "There was a small but fairly consistent trend for the FR group to do more poorly than the TD group."
