# At the end of French first grade, T2 decoding skills and T2 lower level language skills predicted reading comprehension, but T2 discourse skills did not

## Evidence

### Jessica Massonnié 2018

Jessica Massonnié, Maryse Bianco, Laurent Lima, Pascal Bressoux. (2018). Longitudinal predictors of reading comprehension in French at first grade: Unpacking the oral comprehension component of the simple view. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.005

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Structural equation model (SEM 1) on 556 French first-graders predicted end-of-year reading comprehension from T2 decoding skills, T2 LLL skills and T2 discourse skills, with father's SES and home language as covariates; the model explained 81% of reading comprehension variance, and "T2 discourse skills" was not a significant predictor.

> "T2 decoding skills and T2 lower level language skills, but not T2 discourse skills predicted T2 reading comprehension (β = .58 and β = .46, respectively). The model explained 81% of its variance."
