`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Trio SEM in the 83,627-trio MoBa sample testing hypothesis 1 for positive parenting (Alabama Parenting Questionnaire subscale, α = .78). The null contrasts with the significant negative bivariate association; equivalence was not tested. Authors note this conflicts with Wertz et al. (2020).

> "in the trio model controlling for parent PGSedu (Figure 1C) children's PGSedu were not significantly associated with maternal positive parenting (β = 0.01, 95% CI [−0.02, 0.05], p = .410)"
