## Evidence

### Jiang Dingyuan 2025

Jiang Dingyuan, Huang Danpei, Wan Hua, Fu Wuliang, Shi Weidong, Li Jin, Zou Huan, Hou Niannan, Li Qing, Li Nan. (2025). Effect of integrated case-based and problem-based learning on clinical thinking skills of assistant general practitioner trainees: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06634-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial of 70 AGP trainees assigned to CBL-PBL or LBL; post-course total on the CTSES, a Likert self-rating scale rather than a performance assessment, compared between groups (post-course p = 0.000 in Table 2). The article reports the CBL-PBL group "increasing from 30.833 to 72.5" versus LBL "33.232 to 54.697". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "Total clinical thinking skills in the CBL-PBL group showed significant improvement, with scores increasing from 30.833 to 72.5. In comparison, the LBL group showed modest improvement, with scores increasing from 33.232 to 54.697."
