## Evidence

### University of Chicago School Mathematics Project 2016

University of Chicago School Mathematics Project. (2016). University of Chicago School Mathematics Project (UCSMP) — WWC Intervention Report, Secondary Mathematics. What Works Clearinghouse. https://ucsmp.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Longitudinal 4-year quasi-experiment (Hirschhorn, 1993) matching UCSMP students to comparison students on sixth-grade scores in two suburban sites (62 students in the analytic pooled sample). Effects on the Mathematics Level 1 Achievement test differed by site; pooled results showed no statistically significant difference. No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "Hirschhorn (1993) found no statistically significant effects in one site, and a positive and statistically significant effect in another, on the Mathematics Level 1 Achievement test. The WWC pooled the results from both sites and found no statistically significant differences between multiple UCSMP courses and comparison groups on the test."

### University of Chicago School Mathematics Project 2016 (2)

University of Chicago School Mathematics Project. (2016). University of Chicago School Mathematics Project (UCSMP) — WWC Intervention Report, Secondary Mathematics. What Works Clearinghouse. https://ucsmp.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the same longitudinal quasi-experiment, both sites showed statistically significant positive effects on the UCSMP Applications Test, which the WWC confirmed after pooling; taking both outcomes together and "after adjusting for multiple comparisons", the WWC rated the finding a statistically significant positive effect. No effect size is printed for this finding.

> "In addition, Hirschhorn (1993) found statistically significant positive effects in each site on the UCSMP-developed Applications Test, and the WWC confirmed this finding, pooling the results from both sites."
