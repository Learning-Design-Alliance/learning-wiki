## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Validation and analysis of the coupled multiple response Colorado upper-division electrostatics (CUE) diagnostic. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Consistency coding of student responses to each question with a consistency check (excluding Q8, Q11, Q14, & Q15). Students were coded consistent, inconsistent, or neither based on whether reasoning elements supported their method choice. The authors take this as indicating students connect answers and reasoning in meaningful ways rather than randomly guessing.

> "On all ques-
tions but one, the fraction of consistent students is ≥0.5,
and the fraction of inconsistent students is ≤0.32."
