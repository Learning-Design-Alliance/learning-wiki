Evidence¶

Fatima Zehra Allahverdi 2025¶

Fatima Zehra Allahverdi, Lynn Gelzheiser. (2025). Comparison of more effective and typical teachers' instructional quality and implementation of engaged reader processes. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09725-8

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive statistics for the 85-student quantitative sample show CLASSIC percentage mean 5.0 (SD 3.0) and fidelity percentage mean 45.4 (SD 26.0). The authors suggest low values may reflect teachers addressing other comprehension topics and sessions devoted to reading and discussion.

"On average, five percent of the 50 intervention sessions focused on the engaged reader processes of predicting and purpose setting (see Table 4). Moreover, the instruction on the engaged reader processes was not always of high fidelity, with an average of only 45.4%."
