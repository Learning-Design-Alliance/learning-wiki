# Replicating White's 1982 procedures shows a slight decrease in the average SES-achievement correlation (.299 vs. .343)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The average correlation in the 1990-2000 replication sample (M = .299, k = 207) was lower than in White's 1918-1975 review (M = .343, k = 219). [→ Sirin 2005](#sirin-2005)

## Evidence

### Sirin 2005

Sirin, S. R. (2005). Socioeconomic Status and Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analytic Review of Research. Review of Educational Research, 75(3), 417-453. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543075003417

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Replication meta-analysis following White's (1982) procedures on 207 correlations from 58 journal articles (1990-2000), comparable to White's 219 correlations from 59 articles (1918-1975). The printed means were M = .299 (SD = .169) versus M = .343 (SD = .204); no effect size for the change was printed.

> "The average correlation in the present review was .299, as compared with White's average correlation of .343."
