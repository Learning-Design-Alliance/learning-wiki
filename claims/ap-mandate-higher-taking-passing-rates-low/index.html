Descriptive comparative analysis of student AP exam taking and passing rates in DCPS high schools that mandate AP course enrollment versus schools that do not. The report states "passing rates were generally still low in schools mandating AP course enrollment"; no effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

"Three of the four high schools that adopted a mandate on AP course enrollment during the study period had higher AP exam taking and passing rates after the mandate went into place. However, passing rates were generally still low in schools mandating AP course enrollment."
