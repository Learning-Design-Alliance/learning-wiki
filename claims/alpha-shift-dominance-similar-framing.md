Hai D. Nguyen, Deepa N. Chari, Eleanor C. Sayre. (2016). Dynamics of students' epistemological framing in group problem solving. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/6/065706](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/6/065706)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the alpha-shift majority in the video-corpus analysis; they attribute it to students having "approximately the same level of physics knowledge and skills" and being "generally collegial".

> "it implies that individuals in a group have similar framing of the problems, thus they can easily reach a concensus about what is taking place and what should occur next while solving problems."
