## Evidence

### Popa-Velea Ovidiu 2021

Popa-Velea Ovidiu, Pârvan Irina, Diaconescu Liliana Veronica. (2021). The Impact of Self-Efficacy, Optimism, Resilience and Perceived Stress on Academic Performance and Its Subjective Evaluation: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178911

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison by academic-cycle position in 118 medical students; the negative stress–GPA relationship held "only in clinical students (p < 0.004)". No correlation coefficient or effect size is printed.

> "However, GPA and perceived stress correlated negatively only in clinical students (p < 0.004), suggesting a possible cumulative effect of the academic stress perceived in preclinical years."

### Popa-Velea Ovidiu 2021 (2)

Popa-Velea Ovidiu, Pârvan Irina, Diaconescu Liliana Veronica. (2021). The Impact of Self-Efficacy, Optimism, Resilience and Perceived Stress on Academic Performance and Its Subjective Evaluation: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18178911

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression for clinical students (N = 97, Table 5): neither model significant overall, but "a certain importance of perceived stress was evident in model 2 (t = −2.641, p < 0.010)". Only the t statistic is printed.

> "For clinical students, although none of the models was statistically significant, a certain importance of perceived stress was evident in model 2 (t = −2.641, p < 0.010)."
