### Bingham 1916

Bingham, W. V. (1916). Some Norms of Dartmouth Freshmen. The Journal of Educational Psychology, VII(3), 129-142. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Some+Norms+of+Dartmouth+Freshmen%22+Bingham+1916

Group memory test (auditory digits), administered to groups of from eight to twelve subjects, with two successes out of three trials counted as passing. The result is a descriptive frequency: "six per cent, of the class did fail on numbers of six digits".

> "As a matter ot fact, six per cent, of the class did fail on numbers of six digits; consequently we have no record of the lower limit in this ability."
