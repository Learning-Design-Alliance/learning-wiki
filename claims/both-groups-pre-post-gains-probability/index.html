Ömer Sinan Can 2016 (2)¶

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test on the control group's (n=21) pre-test and post-test scores after traditional-method instruction. No effect size printed.

"statistical significant differences were found between the Probability Achievement pre-test and post-test scores of the control group (z=- 4.021, p=.000<.050).
