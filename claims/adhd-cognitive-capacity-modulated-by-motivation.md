### Sebastian Skalski 2021

Sebastian Skalski, Grzegorz Pochwatko, Robert Balas. (2021). Impact of Motivation on Selected Aspects of Attention in Children with ADHD. Child Psychiatry & Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-020-01042-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' summary conclusion drawn from the quasi-experiment with 30 ADHD and 30 TD children; no effect size is printed in this concluding statement, so the claim records the authors' interpretation of their own findings.

> "Cognitive deficits in children with ADHD are not permanent neuropsychological impairments. Cognitive capacity may be modulated by means of motivation. Evoking interest and more frequent strengthening help reduce cognitive deficits in children with ADHD."
