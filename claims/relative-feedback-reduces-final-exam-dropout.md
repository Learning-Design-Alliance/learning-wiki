## Evidence

### Kajitani Shinya 2020

Kajitani Shinya, Morimoto Keiichi, Suzuki Shiba. (2020). Information feedback in relative grading: Evidence from a field experiment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231548

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear probability model on 254 students (Table 7): the dropout coefficient for D was −0.050 (significant at 10%). Nine control students versus three treatment students scored zero on the final, suggesting feedback prevents low midterm scorers from dropping out.

> "relative performance information feedback on the midterm examination has a significantly negative impact on dropout of the final examination."
