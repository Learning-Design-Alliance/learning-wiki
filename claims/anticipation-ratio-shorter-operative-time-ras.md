### Mahendran Vimaladhithan 2024

Mahendran Vimaladhithan, Turpin Laura, Boal Matthew, Francis Nader K. (2024). Assessment and application of non-technical skills in robotic-assisted surgery: a systematic review. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10713-1

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Cohort study by Sexton et al. in the live operating room, reported within the review. The review reports the anticipation ratio "showed a significant correlation with shorter operative time (r = -0.44, p = 0.01)" and that more requests were associated with longer operative time (r = 0.79, p < 0.001).

> "Higher anticipation ratio, was defined as "the ratio of anticipated requests to the total number of requests during a given operation" and showed a significant correlation with shorter operative time (r = -0.44, p = 0.01)"
