# IT entrepreneurs' desired mentoring support changes across the conception, start-up and growth phases of their entrepreneurial career

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Conception-phase entrepreneurs seek entrepreneurial (especially information) support, start-up entrepreneurs seek psychological support such as decision-making help, and growth-phase entrepreneurs increasingly seek networking and role model support. [→ Jamshed Memon 2018](#jamshed-memon-2018)

## Evidence

### Jamshed Memon 2018

Jamshed Memon, Mohd Zaidi Abd Rozan, Kamariah Ismail, Agha Jahanzeb. (2018). Mentor Protégé Matchmaking Framework for IT Entrepreneurs: A Qualitative Study. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1808.04724](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1808.04724)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of Malaysian IT entrepreneurs interviewed in three career phases (17 nascent, 8 start-up, 5 growth). Directed content analysis found phase-specific support preferences; the article notes "Entrepreneurs at start-up phase were more interested in psychological support" while conception-phase entrepreneurs favoured entrepreneurial support.

> "Entrepreneurs at start-up phase were more interested in psychological support as compared to nascent entrepreneurs at conception phase who were more inclined to get entrepreneurial support."
