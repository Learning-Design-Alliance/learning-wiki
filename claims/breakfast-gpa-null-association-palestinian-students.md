## Evidence

### Nawajah Inad 2025

Nawajah Inad. (2025). The statistical relationship between sweet consumption and academic achievement among Palestinian university students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-025-07425-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the fully adjusted regression model (Model 3) among the 220 students, breakfast (regular vs. irregular) carried β = 0.10 (95% CI −0.02, 0.23; p = 0.104) per Table 2, described as "marginally associated with higher GPA but not significant". Equivalence was not tested.

> "Breakfast consumption was marginally associated with higher GPA but not significant at the level needed in the final model."
