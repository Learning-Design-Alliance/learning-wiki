## Evidence

### Helen Crompton 2026

Helen Crompton, Diane Burke and Christine Nickel. (2026). Designing faculty standards for technology integration in higher education institutions: a design-based research study. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00584-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Macro cycle two retrospective analysis of coded feedback. Faculty and accrediting associations requested named technology lists and an AI focus; the authors responded that the standards are overarching and do not focus on specific technologies, citing prior evidence on technological change.

> "Extant empirical evidence across time (viz., Baron, 2010) show that the technological context is highly changing and naming technologies can quickly leave documents dated and irrelevant as technologies are replaced, renamed, or obsolete."
