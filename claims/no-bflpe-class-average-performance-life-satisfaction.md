# Class-level average school performance shows no significant association with students' life satisfaction, independent of individual performance (no BFLPE for life satisfaction)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In multilevel models of German seventh graders, class-level average performance was not significantly associated with life satisfaction, so the big-fish-little-pond effect was not supported for life satisfaction. [→ Rathmann Katharina 2018](#rathmann-katharina-2018)

## Evidence

### Rathmann Katharina 2018

Rathmann Katharina, Herke Max, Bilz Ludwig, Rimpelä Arja, Hurrelmann Klaus, Richter Matthias. (2018). Class-Level School Performance and Life Satisfaction: Differential Sensitivity for Low- and High-Performing School-Aged Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15122750

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-level random-intercept multilevel analysis (students in classes in schools) of NEPS SC3 wave 3 data, n = 5196 students in 478 classes in 250 schools. Model 3 added class-level average performance; the printed coefficient was −0.10 (SE 0.09), not significant. The study reports "no significant association with life satisfaction".

> "The class-level performance shows no significant association with life satisfaction, while other coefficients and results are virtually unchanged, when compared to Model 2."
