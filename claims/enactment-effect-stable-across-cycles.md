## Evidence

### Kubik 2022

Kubik, V., Jemstedt, A., Mahjub Eshratabadi, H., Schwartz, B. L., & Jönsson, F. U. (2022). The underconfidence-with-practice effect in action memory: The contribution of retrieval practice to metacognitive monitoring. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09288-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the interim-test recall analysis of the experiment, enactment outperformed verbal encoding: "Learners recalled significantly more nouns after enactment than verbal encoding across all tests"; recall also increased across cycles with no reliable Encoding Type × Cycle interaction.

> "Learners recalled significantly more nouns after enactment than verbal encoding across all tests, F(1, 57)  = 12.66, p < 0.001, ϖ 2 = 0.091"
