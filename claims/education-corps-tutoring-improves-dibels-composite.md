# Education Corps high-dosage literacy tutoring raises end-of-year DIBELS Composite scores more than business-as-usual supports for below-benchmark K-3 students

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Education Corps 2025

Education Corps. (2025). Education Corps program evaluation summary. https://www.evidenceforesight.org

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A randomized study across 9 schools in a single large North Carolina district during the 2024-2025 school year assigned 867 K-3 students below grade-level benchmarks to Ed Corps tutoring or business-as-usual supports. The tutored group scored "significantly higher than control group students" with effect size +0.15.

> "After one school year, students assigned to Ed Corps tutoring scored significantly higher than control group students on the end-of-year DIBELS Composite assessment (effect size = +0.15)."
