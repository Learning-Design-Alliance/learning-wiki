## Evidence

### Ashish Gurung 2025

Ashish Gurung, Jionghao Lin, Zhongtian Huang, Conrad Borchers, Ryan S. Baker, Vincent Aleven, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2025). Starting Seatwork Earlier as a Valid Measure of Student Engagement. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.13341](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.13341)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

BIC-based forward and backward stepwise regression on the Cognitive Tutor dataset (222 students), controlling for prior final score. The forward model (R2 = 0.53, BIC = 373) retained relative delayed start (beta = -0.25) and gaming tendency (beta = -0.12).

> "Relative delayed start (β = −0.21, p <0.001), early stop (β = −0.13, p= 0 .003), and idle time (β = −0.16, p= 0 .002) in the final model and all three measures had a significant negative correlation with final math score."
