`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap SEM analysis in the 931-student sample. The serial pathway's "indirect effect of 0.075" had a CI excluding zero, supporting hypothesis 4; the article describes the effect size as relatively modest.

> "The partial mediation pathway of digital technology use → emotional intelligence → learning engagement → English academic performance had an indirect effect of 0.075 with a 95% confidence interval of [0.011, 0.231]"

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' discussion of the same model notes the distal path "exhibited marginally significant t-value (1.389)", calling for cautious interpretation and replication.
