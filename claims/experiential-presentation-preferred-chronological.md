Descriptive survey results on preferred methods of organizing skills for potential employers: "experientially (32.9%)" was the top preference over chronological presentation. Percentages are descriptive with no significance test. Relatedly, 31% reported difficulty translating skills from one job into language for another.

> "Respondents indicated that they would most prefer to present their information experientially (32.9%) rather than the standard practice of relaying employment history chronologically (29%), while a significant number indicated that they would rather present their skills based on technical/job-specific skills (24.5%)."
