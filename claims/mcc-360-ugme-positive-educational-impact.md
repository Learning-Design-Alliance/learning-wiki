### Bartman I 2025

Bartman I, St-Onge C, Roy M, Gingerich A, Katsoulas E, Chahine S, Gagnon N. (2025). Multi-source feedback in undergraduate medical education: a pilot study. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.79283

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-evaluation survey of the pilot at one Canadian school: eleven students participated, seven completed the survey. Students reported a 'positive, while moderate, educational impact' and credited facilitated discussions with helping them identify strengths, points for improvement, and set learning objectives. In Table 1, six of seven rated the report's impact as moderate and seven of seven said working with the facilitator helped them better understand their weaknesses. No effect size is printed.

> "Students reported the MCC 360 UGME program had a positive, while moderate, educational impact (Table 1). They stated the discussion with a facilitator allowed them to ‘identify their strengths and points for improvement’"
