`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Dignath et al. (2008), a meta-analysis of classroom interventions for Grades 1–6 (average d = 0.73 for cognitive and metacognitive strategies). It states "researcher-led programs produced stronger effects than teacher-led ones" and math-focused interventions were especially impactful.

> "As in earlier work, researcher-led programs produced stronger effects than teacher-led ones, and mathematics-focused interventions tended to be especially impactful."

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Same meta-analysis (Dignath et al., 2008) as reported by the review: "the duration of the intervention was unrelated to effect sizes in this analysis, and older students showed smaller effects", whereas the earlier Dignath & Büttner (2008) synthesis found longer interventions showed larger effects.

> "Interestingly, the duration of the intervention was unrelated to effect sizes in this analysis, and older students showed smaller effects."
