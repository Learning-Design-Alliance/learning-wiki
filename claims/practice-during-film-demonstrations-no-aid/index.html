RESEARCH ABSTRACTS 163 1955¶

RESEARCH ABSTRACTS 163. (1955). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=NONE

q3 · i? · causal · r?

From the film repetition experiments; the abstract reports neither practice between demonstrations nor concurrent practice aided learning of the perceptual-motor knot-tying task.

"Permitting individuals to prac- tice a perceptual motor task between learning experiences did not prove to be an aid to learning. Concurrent practice was not found to be an aid to learning."
