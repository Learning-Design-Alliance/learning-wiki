`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive report of the course's practical exam, in which assessment covers both practical skills, with students presenting and justifying their methods, and recorded results. No score distributions or statistics are printed.

> "During this practical exam, students do experiments and measurements similar to what they practiced during the practica l sessions, and record their findings in a notebook. The assessment is based both on the practical skills of the student who is asked to present and justify the methods employed, and on the recorded results. The scores of students at this practical exam are usually good."
