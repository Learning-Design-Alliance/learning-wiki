## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Intervention-group self-assessment and physician-assessment improvements were significantly correlated for skills (R=0.872, p<0.01), framework (R=0.813, p<0.001) and steps (R=0.914, p<0.001); regular-group correlations were not significant. [→ Chiang Dung-Hung 2022](#chiang-dung-hung-2022)

## Evidence

### Chiang Dung-Hung 2022

Chiang Dung-Hung, Chen Chung-Ting, Wang Tse-Yao, Yang Ying-Ying, Huang Chia-Chang, Li Tzu-Hao, Huang Shiau-Shian, Kao Shou-Yen, Chen Chen-Huan, Hou Ming-Chih, Sheu Wayne Huey-Herng. (2022). Enhancing the learning and transfer of preprocedural communication skills during clerkship using audio-visual material: a prospective case-controlled study over 2 years. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-055953

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in the intervention group relating self-assessed to physician-assessed per cent improvement; correlations were "R=0.872, p<0.01; R=0.813, p<0.001; R=0.914, p<0.001" across skills, framework and steps.

> "correlation analysis revealed that, in the intervention group, the degree of improvement of the end-of-rotation data of trainees' self-assessment from baseline was significantly correlated with the degree of the improvement in physicians' assessment data in the aspects of skills, framework, and steps in framework (R=0.872, p<0.01; R=0.813, p<0.001; R=0.914, p<0.001, figure 6)."
