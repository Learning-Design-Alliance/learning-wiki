## Evidence

### Rudolph 2013

Rudolph, A. L. (2013). Do You Always Need a Textbook to Teach Astro 101?. Astronomy Education Review. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.3635](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.3635)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Clicker surveys of student attitudes across multiple terms: at the end of Spring 2009 (textbook required) 56% said "yes" to preferring no required purchase; across the four optional terms, "somewhat less than half (44%) of the students bought the textbook", and 67% were glad not to have bought it or sorry they had.

> "When the students were asked if they would have preferred the book not be a required purchase, just over half (56%) of those surveyed said “yes”."
