## Evidence

### Gider Neslihan Yağmur 2025

Gider Neslihan Yağmur, Tosun Betül, Dirgar Ezgi (2025). Evaluation of the Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices of Intensive Care Unit Nurses Regarding Eye Care: A Descriptive Study. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70114

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 207 ICU nurses in three Turkish public hospitals using the ECCCQ. The article reports a significant education-knowledge association (p = 0.035) with post hoc Bonferroni comparisons; "nurses with a postgraduate degree had the highest knowledge scores (7.65 ± 2.08)" versus 5.61 ± 3.41 for associate degrees. No effect size is printed.

> "A statistically significant difference was found between nurses' educational status and the knowledge sub‐dimension of the ECCCQ (p = 0.035). To determine the source of this difference, a post hoc Bonferroni test was conducted, revealing that nurses with a postgraduate degree had the highest knowledge scores (7.65 ± 2.08), while associate degree holders had the lowest (5.61 ± 3.41)."
