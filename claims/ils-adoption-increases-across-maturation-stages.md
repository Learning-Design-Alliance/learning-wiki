# ILS adoption rate increases across knowledge maturation stages, from 3.38% for individually created ILSs to 43.94% for published ILSs

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Knowledge maturation level is strongly associated with later classroom implementation of an ILS, with adoption rising sharply from sharing to co-creating to formalizing. [→ María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana 2020](#maría-jesús-rodríguez-triana-2020)
`q2 i?` A minority of Graasp teachers (35.13% of 20,064) shared, co-created or formalized ILSs, and increasing percentages of teachers at higher maturation levels were involved in implemented ILSs. [→ María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana 2020 (2)](#maría-jesús-rodríguez-triana-2020-2)

## Evidence

### María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana 2020

María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley, Ton de Jong, Denis Gillet. (2020). Social practices in teacher knowledge creation and innovation adoption: a large-scale study in an online instructional design community for inquiry learning. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-020-09331-5

`q2 · i?`

Observational analysis of 40,235 ILSs in Graasp, each assigned one exclusive maturation category. The chi-squared test showed "a strong association between ILS maturation levels and adoption," with adoption rates of 3.38%, 18.38%, 32.19% and 43.94% across levels.

> "a chi-squared test of independence also indicated a strong association between ILS maturation levels and adoption: χ2 (3, N = 40,235) = 4276.6,p < 0.001."

International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-020-09331-5

`q2 · i?`

Teacher-level descriptive analysis of the 20,064 Graasp teachers who (co)created ILSs. Overall, "in total 7049 or 35.13% of teachers either shared, co-created or formalized ILSs," and percentages involved in implemented ILSs increased with maturation level (Table 3).
