## Evidence

### Dange 2026

Dange, A. and Lopez, R.E. (2026). Chatbot Conversations in Physics Education: Using Artificial Intelligence to Analyze Student Reasoning through Computational Grounded Theory. arXiv:2603.04616. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.04616](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.04616)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Topic-modeling analysis of the September dataset: 344 high-information student messages were embedded, reduced, and clustered with BERTopic, and the article reports that BERTopic generated a total of 9 coherent topic clusters, with the largest (n = 130) being student chatbot socialization.

> "BERTopic generated a total of 9 coherent topic clusters from the 344 clean sentences collected during September."

### Dange 2026 (2)

Dange, A. and Lopez, R.E. (2026). Chatbot Conversations in Physics Education: Using Artificial Intelligence to Analyze Student Reasoning through Computational Grounded Theory. arXiv:2603.04616. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.04616](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.04616)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Human interpretive reading of Topic 1 (n = 43), Relativistic Energy Confusion, found formula-based reasoning with conceptual mix-ups; the authors note students frequently misapplied the energy-momentum relation or confused rest energy with kinetic energy, citing prior PER studies.

> "Students frequently misapplied the energy-momentum relation or confused rest energy with kinetic energy, similar to other studies ( Bryce and MacMillan 2009; Jewett 2008)."
