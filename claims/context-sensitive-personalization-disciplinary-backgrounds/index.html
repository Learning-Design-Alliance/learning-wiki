Khalifeh 2026¶

Khalifeh, F., Santiago, R., & Palau, R. (2026). Redefining personalized learning in the artificial intelligence era: an updated systematic review from 2019 to 2025. Smart Learning Environments. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40561-026-00440-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Gao et al. (2025), comparative analyses of CS and Mathematics students in introductory programming courses; the contrast is attributed to goal congruity, with CS students perceiving programming as central to their trajectories. The review attributes these findings to the cited study, which was not independently verified.

"CS students tend to develop more efficient learning strategies over time, showing reduced surface engagement alongside increased productivity and conceptual mastery."
