## Evidence

### Margina Ruiter & Sofie Loyens & Fred Paas 2015

Margina Ruiter & Sofie Loyens & Fred Paas. (2015). Watch Your Step Children! Learning Two-Digit Numbers Through Mirror-Based Observation of Self-Initiated Body Movements. Educ Psychol Rev (2015) 27:457–474. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9324-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Final math test analysis in the randomized experiment: post hoc Gabriel test comparing move-and-mirror (N=27) with move-only (N=28) found no significant difference; equivalence was not tested. Practice-phase Games-Howell test was likewise non-significant (p=0.93).
