### Meng Jing 2025

Meng Jing, Wang Li, Jin Danni, Li Zihan, Tao Qingqing, Ying Lan. (2025). Situational simulation teaching can effectively enhance the clinical thinking ability of residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08248-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stratified between-group comparison in the same RCT (Table 6). Intervention-group post-course scores exceeded controls for undergraduate (90.02 vs 80.23), postgraduate (91.71 vs 80.50), and emergency specialty (92.00 vs 80.20) strata, all P < 0.001; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Furthermore, in the intervention group, residents at different stratification levels had significantly higher post-course clinical thinking scores compared to the control group, with statistically significant differences (P < 0.001), as shown in Table 6."
