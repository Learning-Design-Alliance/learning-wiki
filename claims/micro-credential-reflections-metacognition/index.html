Interpretation from Lauren Acree, Policy and Personalized Learning Lead at the Friday Institute, in the case study of the Learning Differences MOOC-Ed micro-credential stacks. This is practitioner attribution, not a tested result.

"more structure through reflections, since they ask educators to share what they learned from the resources provided, and also what made them effective at the time of implementation. This push toward metacognition and self evaluation facilitates more rigorous learning,"
