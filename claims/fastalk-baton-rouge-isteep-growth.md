## Evidence

### FASTalk program description and evidence summary 2019

FASTalk program description and evidence summary. (2019). Family Engagement Lab. https://www.familyengagementlab.org/fastalk

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r?`

A retrospective quasi-experimental evaluation in two Baton Rouge, Louisiana schools (2018-2019) matched students of parents who signed up for FASTalk texts with non-enrolled students; 231 students (69 FASTalk, 162 control), 99% Black and 93% free/reduced-price lunch. The article reports "significantly higher growth than their non-FASTalk peers" with effect size = +0.39.

> "After accounting for pretest differences, FASTalk students demonstrated significantly higher growth than their non-FASTalk peers on the spring iSTEEP literacy assessment (effect size = +0.39)."
