Evidence¶

G. M. W 1910¶

G. M. W. (1910). Abstracts and Reviews: Some Recent Literature upon Fatigue; Studies in Arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069029

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review summarizes Offner's analysis of factors other than fatigue that condition efficiency (Leistungsfahigkeit), listing "practice, both concomitant and persistent," warming up, spurts, and diurnal rhythms; no quantitative findings are printed.

"the psychologist unearths, in addition to fatigue proper, practice, both concomitant and persistent (glcichlaufcnde and nachdauernde), adaptation, preparedness, warm- ing up (Anlauf, Anregung), spurts (Antricbe) of several types—initial, terminal, etc.—together with voluntary effort, ennui, diurnal rhythms"
