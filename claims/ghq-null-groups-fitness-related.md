## Evidence

### Reigal 2020

Reigal, R. E., Hernández-Mendo, A., Juárez-Ruiz de Mier, R., and Morales-Sánchez, V. (2020). Physical Exercise and Fitness Level Are Related to Cognitive and Psychosocial Functioning in Adolescents. Front. Psychol. 11:1777. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01777

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the 208-adolescent sample, ANOVA found no group differences on these GHQ dimensions, but the authors report that "physical ﬁtness was shown to be signiﬁcantly related to these factors" once correlation and regression analyses were performed; no effect sizes are printed for these specific relations.

> "some dimensions of the GHQ, such as social dysfunction or serious depression, showed no diﬀerences between the groups divided according to hours of physical exercise per week. However, when correlation and linear regression analyses were performed, physical ﬁtness was shown to be signiﬁcantly related to these factors."
