Emin Cener 2015¶

Emin Cener, Ismail Acun & Gokhan Demirhan. (2015). The Impact of ICT on Pupils' Achievement and Attitudes in Social Studies. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 6(1), 190-207. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105333

q2 · i? · causal · r2

In the same regression, ICT attitude pre-test (beta = -.106, p = .329), social studies attitude pre-test (beta = .229, p = .049), ICT attitude post-test (beta = .033, p = .768) and social studies attitude post-test (beta = -.077, p = .496) were reported as not affecting achievement; the second block did not contribute (R² change = .028, p = .310).
