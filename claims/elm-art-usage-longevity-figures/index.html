Evidence¶

Gerhard Weber & Peter Brusilovsky 2016¶

Gerhard Weber & Peter Brusilovsky. (2016). ELM-ART – An Interactive and Intelligent Web-Based Electronic Textbook. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0066-8

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive usage accounting for the running ELM-ART system, not an experimental comparison. The article reports "more than 20,000 people" used the system and "More than 1000 of them completed the whole course", with many classes still using parts of the course. No effect sizes apply.

"Over many years of its service, more than 20,000 people from all over the world used ELM-ART. More than 1000 of them completed the whole course and gave interesting feedback."
