# The effects of LDI structure on recall and recognition of linkages are fully mediated by fixation times on the referred-to picture elements

## Subclaims

`q3 i?` The effect of audio explanation format on recognition hits was fully mediated by fixation times. [→ Manuela Glaser 2022 (2)](#manuela-glaser-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Manuela Glaser 2022

Manuela Glaser, Manuel Knoos, Stephan Schwan. (2022). Localizing, describing, interpreting: effects of different audio text structures on attributing meaning to digital pictures. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09593-6

`q3 · i?`

Exploratory mediation analysis (PROCESS, 5000 bootstrap resamples) with format as predictor, fixation time as mediator, interest in history as covariate: model F(3, 51) = 4.73, p = .006, R² = 0.22; direct effect not significant, mediator effect significant.

> "the indirect effect of audio explanation format on cued recall of linkages by the mediator fixation times was significantly different from zero, partially standardized indirect effect ab
ps = 0.2382"

### Manuela Glaser 2022 (2)

Manuela Glaser, Manuel Knoos, Stephan Schwan. (2022). Localizing, describing, interpreting: effects of different audio text structures on attributing meaning to digital pictures. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09593-6

`q3 · i?`

Second mediation analysis for recognition hits: model F(3, 51) = 4.34, p = .009, R² = 0.20; direct effect not significant, mediator effect significant, indirect effect abps = 0.254 via 5000 bootstrap resamples.

> "the indirect effect of audio explanation format on the proportion of hits in the recognition test by the mediator fixation times was significantly different from zero, partially standard ized indirect effect ab
ps = 0.254"
