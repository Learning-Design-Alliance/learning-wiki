> "This plot clearly demonstrates close to four orders of magnitude improvement in the oscillator performance, from 9 ×10−8 over a duration of 10 s in the free-running regime, to 8 × 10−12 over 100 s in the regime of the coherent population trapping-based atomic clocks."
