Patti Constantakis and Sierra Noakes 2019¶

Patti Constantakis and Sierra Noakes. (2019). Tapping Data for Frontline Talent Development. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/44b193d2-b037-4ded-8322-61e8fb00f89b

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Report's ecosystem diagnosis based on the authors' stakeholder analysis: data collection is 'a very manual process', data 'are rarely shared between providers', and data 'are not shared back with the frontline workers'.

"The current learning ecosystem that serves frontline workers is complex, siloed, and removes agency from the worker. Although many data types are collected, much of it is duplicative and data are rarely used to inform impact and long-term outcomes."
