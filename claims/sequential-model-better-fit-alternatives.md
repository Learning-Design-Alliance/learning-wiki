## Evidence

### Gai Guangyan 2026

Gai Guangyan, Zhu Xueke, Ahmad Nor Shafrin. (2026). Perceived peer support and academic achievement among university students: the chain mediating roles of emotion regulation and behavioral engagement. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-45417-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-fit comparison of five competing SEMs (Table 5), with the hypothesized model at χ²/df = 1.601, GFI = 0.944, TLI = 0.974, SRMR = 0.030, RMSEA = 0.033. The article states the hypothesized model "demonstrated the best overall fit compared with all alternative models" and the reverse model (AA → PPS) fit worst (χ²/df = 2.720, SRMR = 0.177).

> "the hypothesized sequential mediation model demonstrated the best overall fit compared with all alternative models, whereas the reverse-causality model showed substantially poorer fit."
