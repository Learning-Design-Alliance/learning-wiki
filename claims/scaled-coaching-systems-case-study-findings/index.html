Evidence¶

Schachner 2024¶

Schachner, A., Yun, C., Melnick, H., & Barajas, J. (2024). Coaching at scale: A strategy for strengthening the early learning workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/984.909

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Cross-case synthesis of the five case studies (interviews, focus groups, observations) reports the average coach-to-site ratio as "approximately 1 coach for every 22 sites," with individual systems ranging from 1:17 to 1:29 in Table 2.

"The average ratio of coaches to sites was similar across the five systems, regardless of primary recipient, at approximately 1 coach for every 22 sites."

Schachner 2024 (2)¶

Schachner, A., Yun, C., Melnick, H., & Barajas, J. (2024). Coaching at scale: A strategy for strengthening the early learning workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/984.909

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The cross-case synthesis of coaching approaches found "no standardized model for the frequency and duration of coaching," with frequency based on perceived need and varying from multiple sessions per week to two sessions per year.

"There was no standardized model for the frequency and duration of coaching. Frequency was based on the perceived level of need and varied from multiple sessions per week to two sessions per year."

Schachner 2024 (3)¶

Schachner, A., Yun, C., Melnick, H., & Barajas, J. (2024). Coaching at scale: A strategy for strengthening the early learning workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/984.909

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative case-study findings from interviews and focus groups: participants "described a perceived improvement in educator practice and, in turn, children's learning." Administrators also connected improvements to higher ratings on tools such as the Classroom Assessment Scoring System and Environment Rating Scales, attributing high ratings to coaching.

"Study participants across all the coaching systems described a perceived improvement in educator practice and, in turn, children’s learning."
