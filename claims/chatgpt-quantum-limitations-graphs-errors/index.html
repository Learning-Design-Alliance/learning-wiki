Saif Alneyadi and Yousef Wardat 2024¶

Saif Alneyadi and Yousef Wardat. (2024). Integrating ChatGPT in Grade 12 Quantum Theory Education: An Exploratory Study at Emirate School (UAE). International Journal of Information and Education Technology, Vol. 14, No. 3. https://doi.org/10.18178/ijiet.2024.14.3.2061

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Theme 4 of the qualitative analysis: students found ChatGPT cannot read graphic equations or draw graphs between two variables (Fig. 3) and "occasionally makes unexplainable mathematical errors", prompting reliance on other resources.

"Although ChatGPT can have superficial discussions about equations, it lacks a proper understanding of Quantum Physics graphs. As a result, it falls short of correcting physics misunderstandings and, in some cases, may even introduce errors ."

Related Claims¶

  • ChatGPT-enhanced instruction produced significantly higher overall post-test achievement in Grade 12 Quantum Theory than traditional teaching, with a large effect size (η² > 0.14) — related
  • Differences between AI-generated and teacher assessments of laboratory reports may stem from difficulties retrieving and interpreting equations, graphs, tables, and units, not only from model reasoning capabilities. — related
  • Students report negative experiences with ChatGPT including inaccurate outputs, lack of emotional connection, and risk of overreliance, which constrain competence — related
  • Reviewed literature reports risks of overreliance, AI errors in complex tasks, and students' uncritical acceptance of AI-generated outputs — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Students found ChatGPT responses useful but insufficient in depth and detail compared with TA responses — related
