## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2012

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2012). Using an Isomorphic Problem Pair to Learn Introductory Physics: Transferring from a Two-step Problem to a Three-step Problem. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.020114](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.020114)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Think-aloud interviews with six introductory students who learned from the solved snowboard problem and attempted the putty problem under varied interventions; students invoked principles superficially without understanding the governing conditions underlying each principle, and did not systematically plan before implementing.

> "many students first wrote down the principle they “believed” should be used (because the same principle was shown in the given solved problem ) and then tried to plug in some variables from the new situation  in order to solve for the target variable ."
