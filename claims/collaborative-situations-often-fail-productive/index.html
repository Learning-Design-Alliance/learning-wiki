Related Claims¶

  • Groups of high-performing students often fail problem-solving tasks when they do not collaborate, showing why collaborative skills need assessment and feedback — related
  • Past peer-collaboration school improvement initiatives repeatedly failed, and Joyce attributes failures to poor documentation, exclusive focus on student learning, and unclear leadership — related
  • Collaborative tasks should be complex enough that joint effort is worthwhile, engaging the collective working memory effect — related
  • Productive failure students significantly outperformed lecture-practice and facilitated complex problem solving students on well-structured and higher-order application post-test problems — related
  • Groups outperform individuals when devising creative solutions to real-world complex problems — related
  • In this inquiry, student pairs work better than groups of three because larger groups risk disengaging members — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Different group compositions adopted different collaboration strategies: homogeneous groups split interface and programming work, while the heterogeneous CS/non-CS pair showed the most divergent behavior patterns — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Evidence for teacher-side effects of AI-based instruction is mostly perceptual or indirect, and teacher orchestration was not directly associated with student achievement in the largest quantitative study — related
  • Cooperative learning has drawbacks including slow learners depending on fast achievers, so teachers should stress both cooperative and individual work — related
  • Collaborative group failure on ill-structured physics problems confers later individual problem-solving advantages over success on well-structured problems (productive failure) — related
  • Well-defined tasks and accessible materials support shared creative influence in group work — related
  • Seeding student groups with alternative ideas improves learning more than randomly assigning students to groups — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Well-structured collaborative learning is extremely effective, especially for learning concepts and complex problem solving — reports the opposite
  • GenAI students collaboratively phrased and troubleshooted questions, with collaborative problem-solving varying substantially between the two genAI groups — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The collective working memory effect: group learning is more efficient than individual learning for high-complexity tasks, but individual learning is more efficient for low-complexity tasks — related
  • Stereotype threat in collaborative contexts can interfere with working memory and reduce meaningful contribution — related
