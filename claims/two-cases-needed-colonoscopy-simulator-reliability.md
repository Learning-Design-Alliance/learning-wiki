## Evidence

### Preisler Louise MD 2015

Preisler Louise MD, Svendsen Morten Bo Søndergaard MScEng, Nerup Nikolaj MD, Svendsen Lars Bo MD, DMSc, Konge Lars MD, PhD. (2015). Simulation-Based Training for Colonoscopy: Establishing Criteria for Competency. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000000440

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the virtual-reality simulator test's aggregated score. Reliability of both cases combined (ICC average measures) reached 0.80, meeting the 0.80 benchmark the authors cite; a single case was unreliable at 0.67.

> "We found that testing in a single task did not provide a reliable test result—ICC single measure = 0.67. To ensure a reliability above 0.80,21 it was necessary to use 2 tasks—ICC average measures = 0.80."

### Preisler Louise MD 2015 (2)

Preisler Louise MD, Svendsen Morten Bo Søndergaard MScEng, Nerup Nikolaj MD, Svendsen Lars Bo MD, DMSc, Konge Lars MD, PhD. (2015). Simulation-Based Training for Colonoscopy: Establishing Criteria for Competency. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000000440

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the phantom-model test's aggregated score, assessed with two cases of differing difficulty. Average-measure ICC was 0.87 versus a single-measure ICC of 0.77, supporting the authors' recommendation to assess at least two procedures.

> "Testing in 2 cases was necessary to ensure reliability—ICC average measures = 0.87, single measure = 0.77. At least 2 procedures should be assessed when using the phantom model for certification."
