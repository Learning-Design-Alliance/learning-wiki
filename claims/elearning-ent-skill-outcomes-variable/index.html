Narrative synthesis of six studies (five RCTs, one cohort) assessing clinical examination skills, therapeutic maneuvers, and otoscopic image interpretation. The review reports "higher skill gains following e‐learning compared to traditional methods" in two studies, e.g. Chin et al. mean exam scores of 78.5/99 versus 55.8/99.

"Two studies found higher skill gains following e‐learning compared to traditional methods [28, 29]. For example, Chin et al. [28] reported mean exam scores of 78.5/99 for e‐learning versus 55.8/99 for traditional learning."
