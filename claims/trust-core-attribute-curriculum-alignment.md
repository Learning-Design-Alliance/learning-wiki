Editorial summary of a paper on curriculum renewal in the digital society, based on a sociocultural framework conceptualizing alignments between policy and practice at national and school levels. The conclusion about trust is the authors' own synthesis, not a tested result.

> "The paper explores and exemplifies three issues that are key in realizing alignment: (1) stakeholder engagement; (2) teacher professionalism, and (3) summative assessment. The authors conclude that trust between policy and practice, and between the national and school level is a core attribute of alignment."
