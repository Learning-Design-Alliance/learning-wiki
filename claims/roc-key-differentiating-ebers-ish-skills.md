Subgroup ROC curve analysis of the practical assessment criteria (Fig. 4B) evaluated each technical skill's power to discriminate PBL from LBL performance, with AUC as the primary metric; the article reports these AUCs as indicating the primary drivers of the performance gap.

> "The highest discriminatory power was observed for minimizing “Scratches, Damage, or Section Loss” (AUC = 0.815), preventing “False Negatives in Controls” (AUC = 0.801), ensuring “Adequate Proteinase Digestion” (AUC = 0.786), and avoiding “Non-specific Staining” (AUC = 0.765)"
