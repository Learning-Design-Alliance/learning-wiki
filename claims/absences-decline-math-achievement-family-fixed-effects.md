## Evidence

### Michael A. Gottfried 2011

Michael A. Gottfried. (2011). The Detrimental Effects of Missing School: Evidence from Urban Siblings. American Journal of Education 117(2). https://doi.org/10.1086/657886

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Family fixed effects models for SAT9 math on the sibling sample. The article states this held true in the final model, "which incorporates all covariates for observable characteristics pertaining to the student, neighborhood, teacher, and classroom and which contains controls for grade, year, school, and family."

> "the findings first and foremost demonstrate that increasing missed school days is consistently related to declining math achievement."
