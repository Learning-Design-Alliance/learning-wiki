Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Sanjit Kakarla, Jionghao Lin, Shambhavi Bhushan, Boyuan Guo, Erin Gatz, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2025. Does Multiple Choice Have a Future in the Age of Generative AI? A Posttest-only RCT. In LAK25: The 15th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2025), March 03–07, 2025, Dublin, Ireland. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706468.3706530

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on log-transformed instruction time (RQ2) found a significant main effect of condition, F(2, 716) = 12.56, p < .001; MCQ Only averaged M = 3.83 minutes versus M = 5.87 for Both and M = 5.38 for Open-response Only. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "the MCQ Only condition took learners significantly shorter than the Both condition, estimate = –0.37, SE = 0.07, p < .001, and the Open-response Only condition, estimate = -0.30, SE = 0.07, p < .001."
