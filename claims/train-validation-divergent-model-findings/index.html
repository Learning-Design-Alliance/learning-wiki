Evidence¶

Kimmo Sorjonen 2025¶

Kimmo Sorjonen, Bo Melin, Gustav Nilsonne. (2025). Inconclusive Evidence for a Prospective Effect of Academic Self-Concept on Achievement: A Simulated Reanalysis and Comment on Marsh et al. (2024). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10008-4

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Reanalysis of simulated data matching the TRAIN study (N = 3757, four annual waves, grades 5–8). RI-CLPM effects b = 0.114 and b = 0.135 (p < 0.001); LCSM effects b = −0.106 and b = −0.128 (p < 0.001); ESDM fit χ2 = 821, CFI = 0.934; modified LCSM b = 0.130, p < 0.001.

"The RI-CLPM suggested an increasing prospective effect of achievement on subsequent self-concept (b = 0.114 [0.085; 0.142], p < 0.001) and vice versa (b = 0.135 [0.109; 0.160], p < 0.001) (Fig. 3A). The LCSM, on the other hand, suggested decreasing prospective effects"
