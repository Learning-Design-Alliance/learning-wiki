## Evidence

### Zhou Quan 2024

Zhou Quan, Gidziela Agnieszka, Allegrini Andrea G., Cheesman Rosa, Wertz Jasmin, Maxwell Jessye, Plomin Robert, Rimfeld Kaili, Malanchini Margherita. (2024). Gene-environment correlation: the role of family environment in academic development. Molecular Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-024-02716-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mediation analyses comparing cognitive and noncognitive polygenic score pathways to achievement in the TEDS sample. The study reports effects "stronger for the NonCog PGS prediction", with mediating effects approaching or exceeding half of the total NonCog PGS effect for family SES at age 7.

> "Although a similar pattern of results emerged for both Cog and NonCog PGSs, effects were stronger for the NonCog PGS prediction (e.g. the indirect effect of family SES was ß = 0.06 [95% CI, 0.04–0.09] for Cog and ß = 0.09 [95% CI, 0.07–0.12] for NonCog; Supplementary Tables 10a and 10b)"
