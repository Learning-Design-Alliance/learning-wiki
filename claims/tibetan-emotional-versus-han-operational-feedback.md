## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu 2024

Guangtian Zhu, Jing Hu, Chun Du. (2024). Assisting Tibetan Students in Learning Quantum Mechanics via Mathematica. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.18810](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.18810)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

After-class feedback collected from all students following each Mathematica-assisted lesson, with 32 effective feedback pieces after the first two lessons categorized as emotional feelings or operational techniques. The article reports Tibetan students' operational expressions were "often vague" while Han students precisely recorded hints.

> "Among the 15 pieces of feedbacks on emotional feelings, 11 pieces were provided by Tibetan students. Also, only 6 out of 17 pieces of feedbacks on operational techniques were provided by Tibetan students."
