### Luschin-Ebengreuth M 2015

Luschin-Ebengreuth M, Dimai HP, Ithaler D, Neges HM, Reibnegger G. (2015). Situational judgment test as an additional tool in a medical admission test: an observational investigation. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1033-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Aggregation of per-item indices of discrimination (correlation of item with total test) across the three admission years, reported in Table 3. Printed SJT means were 0.196, 0.311 and 0.176 versus knowledge-test means of 0.306, 0.342 and 0.349.

> "the highest mean indices of discrimination were found for the knowledge test part (consisting of biology, chemistry, physics and mathematics), followed by text comprehension, and the least discriminatory test part was, with the exception of 2011, the SJT."
