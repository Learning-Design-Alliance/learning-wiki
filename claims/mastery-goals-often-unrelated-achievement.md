## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Senko 2011

Senko, C., Hulleman, C. S., & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2011). Achievement Goal Theory at the Crossroads: Old Controversies, Current Challenges, and New Directions. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2011.538646

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing the Hulleman et al. (2010) meta-analysis, that mastery goals show consistent favorable links with interest, persistence, and deep strategies, but "mastery goals are often unrelated to academic achievement". No effect size is printed here.

> "But there is a noteworthy omission from this list of beneficial outcomes: mastery goals are often unrelated to academic achievement (for a meta-analytic review, see Hulleman, Schrager, Bodmann, & Harackiewicz, 2010)."
