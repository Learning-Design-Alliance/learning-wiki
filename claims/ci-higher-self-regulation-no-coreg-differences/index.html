Zhanxin Hao 2026¶

Zhanxin Hao, Xiaobo Liu, Jiaxin Fan, Yun Long, Jifan Yu, Wenli Chen, Yu Zhang. (2026). Unpacking Interaction Profiles and Strategies in Human-AI Collaborative Problem Solving: A Cognitive Distribution and Regulation Perspective. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21288 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2603.21288

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Post-task questionnaire (six adapted items, Cronbach's alpha 0.83) comparing reported self-regulation across the three clusters. The article reports H = 7.06, p = .029, with "the CI group reported significantly higher ratings for SRL compared to the DR group"; no effect size is printed.

"The results indicated significant differences among the three groups in terms of reported SRL strategies, H = 7.06, p =.029. The post hoc tests revealed that the CI group reported significantly higher ratings for SRL compared to the DR group."
