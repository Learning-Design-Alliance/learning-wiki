Protocol description of intervention delivery. Each of the two in-person sessions lasted about four hours and combined individual online didactic modules, online IATs, face-to-face simulation with standardized patients, and group-based facilitated debriefing sessions. The article cites a spaced learning rationale for the five-week interval.

> "The program is delivered over two in-person sessions, scheduled five weeks apart, to leverage a spaced learning design.[29] The sessions were designed to minimize the time burden of the intervention on trainees outside of the classroom."
