## Evidence

### Felix Gorny 2026

Felix Gorny, Anika Grohmann, Mareike Müller-Cleve, Daniela Bavaresco, Saskia Wirtz, Christan Brandt, Tanja Sappok. (2026). Fostering inclusion in healthcare: a systematic review of collaborative teaching with individuals with disorders of intellectual development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09930-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Pre–post study of a communication skills training in which tutors with intellectual disability engaged in direct interaction with students after a structured teaching session. The review reports "mean IDP scores decreased from 73.7 (SD 10.6) pre‑session to 70.41 (SD 10.2) post‑session" with a significant one-tailed repeated-measures t-test; no effect size was explicitly reported.

> "mean IDP scores decreased from 73.7 (SD 10.6) pre‑session to 70.41 (SD 10.2) post‑session, indicating reduced discomfort. A one‑tailed repeated‑measures t‑test yielded a statistically significant result, t(127) = 5.07, p < .001"
