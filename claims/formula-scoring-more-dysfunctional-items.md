## Evidence

### Cecilio-Fernandes D 2017

Cecilio-Fernandes D, Medema H, Collares CF, Schuwirth L, Cohen-Schotanus J, Tio RA. (2017). Comparison of formula and number-right scoring in undergraduate medical training: a Rasch model analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1051-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Response-option analysis within the Rasch model of the same crossover experiment. Table 3 shows totals of 16 and 20 dysfunctional items under formula scoring versus 6 under number-right in each test. The article states the highest number emerged when "students in the penalty category had a higher average ability".

> "Second, the response options analysis showed clear differences between number-right scoring and formula scoring. The formula-scoring tests produced around three times more dysfunctional items."
