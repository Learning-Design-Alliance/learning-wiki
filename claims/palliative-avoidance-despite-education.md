## Evidence

### Bassah Nahyeni 2016

Bassah Nahyeni, Cox Karen, Seymour Jane. (2016). A qualitative evaluation of the impact of a palliative care course on preregistration nursing students’ practice in Cameroon. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-016-0106-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of focus group and critical incident interview data; one interviewee reported speaking to a patient "without mentioning death, death, death, or making her to know that she had an incurable disease". Avoidance was one of six subthemes of students' reported implementation of learning.

> "The students also talked about some ‘uncomfortable/difficult’ incidences that they avoided. Most particularly, they avoided talking about dying to patients and/or their families. It shows some students still experienced anxiety and lacked confidence in talking about dying, despite participation in the palliative care course"
