Evidence¶

Jung Sun Sung · Wenhao David Huang 2026¶

Jung Sun Sung · Wenhao David Huang. (2026). Digital learning innovation: engineering students’ learning motivation for AI scaffolding. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10576-w

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Thematic analysis of interview data revealed that AI-generated content lacking references led participants to perceive it as not requiring further investigation, reducing their intellectual curiosity and attention to the content.

"participants were not likely to autonomously assess the credibility or accuracy of the AI-generated information. They perceived AI-generated content as not requiring reassessment, memorization, or further investigation, mostly because it often lacks references so that they felt they had expertise to assess the credibility."
