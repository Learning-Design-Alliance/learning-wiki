SHERPA criticality ratings from the Results section, rated by consensus of the five subject matter experts on a three-level scale in which high means permanent clinical effect. The three named subtasks are printed verbatim; no effect sizes are reported.

> "Three subtasks were identified as high criticality, i.e. having the potential to cause permanent clinical effect. These included failure to expel air from the syringe before LA administration, failure to aspirate the syringe before infiltrating the local anaesthesia and failure to complete the WHO time-out checklist before skin incision."
