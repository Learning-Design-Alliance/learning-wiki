## Evidence

### Brymer 2022

Brymer, E., & Schweitzer, R. D. (2022). Learning clinical skills: an ecological perspective. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 27(3), 691-707. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10115-9

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument (authors' exposition) about emergence and self-organisation in clinical learning. The article states goal-directed learning is "a nonlinear process in the messy, noisy real world, characterised by progressions, regressions, jumps and skips".

> "the ecological perspective proposes that learners' responses are emergent, based on the interaction of various subcomponents (e.g. past social experiences, emotional readiness, ability to relate with a range of people from diverse backgrounds) and the ability of these subcomponents to self-organise or naturally adjust and adapt"
