`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Prespecified exploratory subgroup analysis stratified by baseline OSCE score using a 60% cut-off. The low-performer subgroup (n = 5) improved from 53.4 to 90.2 while high performers (n = 20) improved from 71.5 to 90.5.

> "The mean gain was greater in the low-performer subgroup, with a between-group difference of 17.9 points (95% CI 8.2 to 27.5; p < 0.001; Cohen's d = 1.92)."
