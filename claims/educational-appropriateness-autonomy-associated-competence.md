SEM direct-effects results for the competence knowledge model; the quote reports the printed coefficients. In the application model, educational appropriateness (β = 0.441) and work autonomy (β = 0.380, p = 0.005) were significant; role clarity, training intentions, and complexity were not.

> "Educational Appropriateness (β = 0.428, p < 0.001), Autonomy at work (β = 0.318, p = 0.003), and Role Clarity (β = −0.260, p = 0.039, inverse score) presented significant positive associations."
