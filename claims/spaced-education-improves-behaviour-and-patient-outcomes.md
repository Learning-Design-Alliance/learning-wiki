## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The spaced education RCT improved behaviour (RR 0.75) sustained at 18 months, and the spaced education game modestly improved patient outcomes (HR 1.043, 95% CI 1.007–1.081) and knowledge (SMD 0.81). [→ Tudor Car Lorainne 2019](#tudor-car-lorainne-2019)

## Evidence

### Tudor Car Lorainne 2019

Tudor Car Lorainne, Soong Aijia, Kyaw Bhone Myint, Chua Kee Leng, Low-Beer Naomi, Majeed Azeem. (2019). Health professions digital education on clinical practice guidelines: a systematic review by Digital Health Education collaboration. BMC Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-019-1370-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Narrative synthesis of patient outcomes across three RCTs. Only the email-delivered spaced education game versus non-interactive online resources (blood pressure control) showed modest improvement; the review's conclusion names spaced education as the only intervention improving behaviour and modestly patient outcomes.

> "Of three studies reporting patient outcomes, only one study comparing email-delivered, spaced education games to non-interactive online resources reported modest improvement in the intervention group."
