### Arthur I. Gates 1923

Arthur I. Gates. (1923). The Correlations of Achievement in School Subjects with Intelligence Tests and Other Variables (Concluded). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0066477

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Regression and multiple correlation analysis for grades IV-VI. The printed effect sizes are r = 0.54 rising to multiple r = 0.605: "the multiple r becomes 0.605" when the mean verbal group test is added to Stanford MA.

> "In Grades IV, V, VI, taking mean results, the Stanford MA gives a correlation with achievement of 0.54. Adding the independent elements of the mean verbal group test, the multiple r becomes 0.605."
