# Boredom and academic interest fit two separate correlated factors better than one common factor in mathematics and German at every wave

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Confirmatory factor analyses at all four waves showed superior fit for two-factor models of boredom and interest versus single-factor models in both domains. [→ Katharina Luisa Boehme 2025](#katharina-luisa-boehme-2025)

## Evidence

### Katharina Luisa Boehme 2025

Katharina Luisa Boehme, Thomas Goetz, Markus Feuchter, Franzis Preckel. (2025). Is Boredom the Opposite of Interest? A Longitudinal Reciprocal Effect Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09991-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

CFA model comparisons across four waves in a sample of German secondary students (per-wave n around 1000) using CFI, BIC, and Satorra-Bentler scaled chi-square difference tests, all significant at p < 0.01. Two-factor models showed "superior to the respective single-factor models" fit; ΔBIC ranged from −51.970 to −207.758. No standardised effect size was printed for this model-comparison finding.

> "At each wave of measurement, model fit for two-factor models with boredom and interest as separate but correlated factors was superior to the respective single-factor models for both mathematics and German"
