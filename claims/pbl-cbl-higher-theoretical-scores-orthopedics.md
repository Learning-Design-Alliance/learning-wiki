## Evidence

### Xiong Xianghua 2025

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 14 studies (920 students) comparing PBL-CBL with LBL in orthopedic teaching. The pooled estimate favored PBL-CBL: "SMD = 1.46, 95% CI: 1.15–1.78, P < 0.0001". Heterogeneity was high (I2 = 77%).

> "The PBL-CBL group demonstrated significantly higher theoretical scores than the LBL group (SMD = 1.46, 95% CI: 1.15–1.78, P < 0.0001, I2 = 77%)"
