## Evidence

### Hossain Md. Ismail 2026

Hossain Md. Ismail, Ahmad Iftakhar. (2026). Education in Haor areas of Bangladesh: A mixed-methods study on academic performance, school attendance, and school intervention. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0348890

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on the 189-student Haor survey found punitive teacher behavior strongly reduced odds of good performance, "OR = 0.065, 95% CI [0.015, 0.281]". Interviews described routine corporal punishment diminishing interest in learning.

> "punitive teacher behavior significantly reduces academic performance (B = –2.740, p < .001, OR = 0.065, 95% CI [0.015, 0.281])"

### Hossain Md. Ismail 2026 (2)

Hossain Md. Ismail, Ahmad Iftakhar. (2026). Education in Haor areas of Bangladesh: A mixed-methods study on academic performance, school attendance, and school intervention. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0348890

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

In the same regression model, bullying (verbal or physical harassment going to or from school) reduced odds of good performance, "OR = 0.08, 95% CI [0.029, 0.220]". The discussion emphasizes community-level rather than school-based bullying in Haor areas.

> "whereas experiences of bullying negatively affected academic outcomes (B = –2.529, p < .001, OR = 0.08, 95% CI [0.029, 0.220])"
