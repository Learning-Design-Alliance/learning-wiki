Williams Adrienne E., Denaro Kameryn. (2026). Studying with Friends in Introductory Biology Is Associated with Low Impact Study Strategies and No Improvement of Exam Scores. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-04-0074

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression of regular Week 10 study-with-friends on demographics, high school GPA, and high school study-group frequency. After backward elimination only high school behavior remained; "4.8 times more likely" for regular high-school studiers, but the authors note "the overall predictive power of the model is weak".

> "Although students who regularly study with friends in high school are 4.8 times more likely to use peer study groups by the end of their first quarter in college compared with students who rarely or never studied with friends during high school, the overall predictive power of the model is weak"
