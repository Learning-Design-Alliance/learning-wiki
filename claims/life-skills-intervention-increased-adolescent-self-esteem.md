## Evidence

### Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X. (2013). Drug Prevention by Increasing Self-Esteem: Influence of Teaching Approaches and Gender on Different Consumption Groups. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental intervention study of 282 low-achieving eighth graders with self-esteem measured at pre-test, post-test and retention. The article reports "Chi-Square = 46.55; df = 2; p < .001" for the total group, with only small effect-size changes between T1 and T3 and T2 and T3.

> "Our educational intervention was associated with students’ self-esteem over all test dates (Friedman Test, Chi-Square = 46.55; df = 2; p < .001)."

### Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013 (2)

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X. (2013). Drug Prevention by Increasing Self-Esteem: Influence of Teaching Approaches and Gender on Different Consumption Groups. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A control group (n = 46) served for test assessment and exclusion of external influences; it was measured only at the end of the study (T3). The article reports "Z = -1.568; p = .117", i.e. no detected change.

> "Control group scores did not differ at all (Wilcoxon, Z = -1.568; p = .117)."
