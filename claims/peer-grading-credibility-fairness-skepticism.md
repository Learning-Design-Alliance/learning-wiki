## Evidence

### Kristanto 2018

Kristanto, Y. D. (2018). Technology-enhanced pre-instructional peer assessment: Exploring students' perceptions in a Statistical Methods course. REiD (Research and Evaluation in Education), 4(2), 105-116. [doi:10.21831/reid.v4i2.20951](https://doi.org/10.21831/reid.v4i2.20951)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of reflections and interviews categorized into Wen and Tsai's four attitude categories; the negative-attitude category was dominated by credibility concerns, and the discussion reports 'negative attitudes toward the fairness of peer grading' despite the provided rubric.

> "However, the results of this study also reveal the resistance of peer assessment. Many students in this study have negative attitudes toward the fairness of peer grading."

### Kristanto 2018 (2)

Kristanto, Y. D. (2018). Technology-enhanced pre-instructional peer assessment: Exploring students' perceptions in a Statistical Methods course. REiD (Research and Evaluation in Education), 4(2), 105-116. [doi:10.21831/reid.v4i2.20951](https://doi.org/10.21831/reid.v4i2.20951)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Students' reflections on the yes-or-no per-criterion grading strategy: it 'provided not many options in marking peers' works', and students preferred a scale-rating strategy to feel more confident in assessing peers.

> "With regard to the number of errors grading strategy, they perceived that it provided not many options in marking peers' works."
