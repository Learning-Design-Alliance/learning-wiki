## Evidence

### Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X. (2013). Drug Prevention by Increasing Self-Esteem: Influence of Teaching Approaches and Gender on Different Consumption Groups. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cluster analysis of CATI questionnaire response patterns on consumption behaviour and attitude (N = 282). The article reports a cross-tabulation of the two procedures showing "a high level of agreement of the four-cluster solution (adjusted coefficient of contingency Ckorr = .93, N = 282".

> "We extracted a four-group classification using the agglomerative hierarchical Ward’s method (Norusis, 1993)."

### Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X 2013 (2)

Heyne Thomas & Bogner Franz X. (2013). Drug Prevention by Increasing Self-Esteem: Influence of Teaching Approaches and Gender on Different Consumption Groups. World Journal of Education, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v3n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive cluster characterization prior to the intervention: PC made up "77.5% of the total sample"; the article further reports CC at 14.4% (n = 34), EP at 6.4% (n = 15) and CO at 1.7% (n = 4).

> "The potentially curious cluster (PC; n = 183) made up 77.5% of the total sample and, thus, comprised the largest fraction of the sample which had never tried any drugs"
