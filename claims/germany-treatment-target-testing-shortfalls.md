## Evidence

### Murray Suzanne 2011

Murray Suzanne, Lazure Patrice, Schroter Sara, Leuschner Philipp J, Posel Peter, Kellner Thomas, Jenkins Richard D. (2011). International challenges without borders: a descriptive study of family physicians' educational needs in the field of diabetes. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-12-27

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review-attributed German utilization data cited in the Testing section of the article. The review reports that "HbA1c is tested in only 68% of patients" and that only 7.6% reached target blood pressure and 15.8% reached target LDL-C, citing the underlying studies.

> "Studies report that in Germany, HbA1c is tested in only 68% of patients [30]. Furthermore, only 7.6% of diabetic persons reached target blood pressure values of < 130/80 mm Hg, and target LDL-C < 100 mg/dL (< 2.6 mmol/L) was achieved in only 15.8% of patients [31]."
