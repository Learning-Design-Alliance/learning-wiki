## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Lund Frederike 2012

Lund Frederike, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Maatouk Imad, Krautter Markus, Möltner Andreas, Werner Anne, Weyrich Peter, Jünger Jana, Nikendei Christoph. (2012). Effectiveness of IV Cannulation Skills Laboratory Training and Its Transfer into Clinical Practice: A Randomized, Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0032831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Sub-analysis of the video-rated IPPI, with the eleven items divided into technical skills items (4–6; 9–11) and communication skills items (1–3; 7; 8). Both sub-categories favoured the skills laboratory group, "p = 0.031 for technical skills; IG: 2.4; CG: 3.0; p = 0.047 for communication skills".

> "Students of the IG also received significantly better ratings in these sub-categories (median scores: IG: 3.6; CG: 3.9; p = 0.031 for technical skills; IG: 2.4; CG: 3.0; p = 0.047 for communication skills) as shown in Table 2 and Figure 2."
