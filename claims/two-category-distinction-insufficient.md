## Evidence

### Abhilash Nair 2018

Abhilash Nair, Paul Irving, and Vashti Sawtelle. (2018). Using disciplinary perspectives to refine conceptions of the "real world". arXiv:1808.10429. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.062](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.062)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

This is the article's theoretical-framework argument, grounded in the distinction Adams et al. report between "whether students think that physics describes the real world and whether they actually care or think about the physics they experience in everyday life." The paper contends the categories miss nuance for life-science students.

> "We argue that for our life-science students with rich disciplinary experiences, this dis-tinction into two categories may be insufﬁcient to capture t he diverse ways in which students are able to connect physics to their lives."
