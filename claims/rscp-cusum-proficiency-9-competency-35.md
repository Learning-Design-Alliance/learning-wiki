## Evidence

### Park Kena 2024

Park Kena, Kwon Ji Young, Yoo Eun-Hee, Lee Seon Hwa, Song Jeong Min, Pyeon Seung Yeon. (2024). Application of CUSUM analysis in assessing learning curves in robot-assisted sacrocolpopexy performed by experienced gynecologist. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-024-02691-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective single-surgeon series of 50 consecutive robot-assisted sacrocolpopexies (June 2018 to June 2023); piecewise linear regression of CUSUM operative time gave breakpoints "at case 8.47 (95% CI 8.0, 9.0) and case 34.41 (95% CI 32.7, 36.1)" with R2 = 0.87, rounded to cases 9 and 35, yielding phases of 9, 26, and 15 cases.

> "The regression identified breakpoints at case 8.47 (95% CI 8.0, 9.0) and case 34.41 (95% CI 32.7, 36.1), with an R2 value of 0.87, which agrees with that of the second-order polynomial equation."
