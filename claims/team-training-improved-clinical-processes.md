## Evidence

### Costar 2020

Costar, D. M., & Hall, K. K. (2020). Improving Team Performance and Patient Safety on the Job Through Team Training and Performance Support Tools: A Systematic Review. Journal of Patient Safety. https://doi.org/10.1097/PTS.0000000000000746

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review reports four studies of clinical process improvements after team training; for example, time-out compliance rose from "47%–86%, P < 0.05" six months after CRM training, and surgical case delays were significantly reduced at 12 months (P < 0.0001).

> "Another study reported that staff compliance with all elements of time-outs had significantly improved 6 months after CRM training (47%–86%, P < 0.05)."
