Evidence¶

Schachner 2024¶

Schachner, A., Yun, C., Melnick, H., & Barajas, J. (2024). Coaching at scale: A strategy for strengthening the early learning workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/984.909

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The report's review of the features-of-effective-coaching literature states there is "promising evidence that coaching can be effective in all modes," while noting most studies assess in-person sessions and far fewer examine exclusively distance coaching.

"However, based on the available research, there is promising evidence that coaching can be effective in all modes: face-to-face, distance, and a combination of face-to-face and distance."
