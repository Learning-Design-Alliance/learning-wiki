## Evidence

### Yi Yang 2025

Yi Yang. (2025). Reciprocal Relationships Between Parental Involvement and Academic Performance in Early Adolescence: A Two-Year Longitudinal Study in China. Journal of Youth and Adolescence, 54(4), 876–889. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02102-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-lagged SEM comparison of four nested models (auto-regressive only, parent-driven, child-driven, reciprocal) on CEPS two-wave data from 9,449 students; the reciprocal model M4 fit best. The article reports "the hypothesized reciprocal relationship" was supported; no effect size is printed for the model comparison.

> "As the Chi-square differences among M4 and other models were all significant (p < 0.01), indicating M4 was the best-fitting model, it supported the hypothesized reciprocal relationship."
