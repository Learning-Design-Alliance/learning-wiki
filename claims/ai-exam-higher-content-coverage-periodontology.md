`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content validity analysis of the two 90-question MCQ papers, comparing keyword coverage of the Periodontology teaching syllabus extracted with Python's NLTK. The AI paper achieved "significantly higher content coverage" at 81.3% (95% CI 75.4–87.2%) versus the human paper's 72.4% (95% CI 65.8–79.0%).

> "The AI examination achieved significantly higher content coverage (81.3% [95% CI: 75.4–87.2%]) compared to the human examination (72.4% [95% CI: 65.8–79.0%]) (Fig. 1)."
