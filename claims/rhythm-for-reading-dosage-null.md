## Evidence

### Styles et al. 2014

Dr Ben Styles, Rebecca Clarkson, Katherine Fowler. (2014). Rhythm for Reading: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN10404460/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up analysis in which total delivery time per school divided by intervention pupils gave a dosage measure used in place of dichotomous allocation. Intended dosage of 10 was achieved in three schools; the remaining schools had dosage of 1, 17 and 19. The report notes this analysis may be biased due to self-selection to differing levels of exposure.

> "The 'on-treatment' analysis revealed no significant effect of dosage (p = 0.669)."
