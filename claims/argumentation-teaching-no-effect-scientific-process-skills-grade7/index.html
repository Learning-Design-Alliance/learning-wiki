Evidence¶

Mehmet Erkol & Mehmet Tamer Kaya; Tuğba Yurdakul (Kül) 2023¶

Mehmet Erkol & Mehmet Tamer Kaya; Tuğba Yurdakul (Kül). (2023). The Effect of Argumentation-Based Teaching on Scientific Process Skills of 7th Grade Students. Open Journal for Educational Research, 7(2), 79-92. https://doi.org/10.32591/coas.ojer.0702.02079e

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest paired control group study with 36 7th graders (18 per group) over about 12 weeks. ANCOVA controlling for pretest found the adjusted post-test means did not differ significantly; the article reports F(1-33)=4.211, p>0.05 in Table 4. The authors conclude argumentation-based teaching "did not have any effect on students' scientific process skills".

"In the case that the SPS pre -test scores are kept under control, the PPS post -test mean score of the experimental group (=15 .877) does not statistically differ significantly from the PSS post -test mean score of the control group (=14 .956)."
