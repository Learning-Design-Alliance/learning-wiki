Evidence¶

Dietrichson J 2020¶

Dietrichson J, Filges T, Klokker RH, Viinholt BCA, Bøg M, Jensen UH. (2020). Targeted school‐based interventions for improving reading and mathematics for students with, or at risk of, academic difficulties in Grades 7–12: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1081

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis with robust-variance estimation of 71 studies (76% RCTs), 99 interventions and 214 effect sizes, around 105,700 student observations. The review reports "weighted average effect size [ES] = 0.22", corresponding to a 56% chance that an intervention student's score exceeds a control student's score.

"The average effect size for short‐run outcomes was positive and statistically significant (weighted average effect size [ES] = 0.22, 95% confidence interval [CI] = [0.148, 0.284])."
