## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The mean number of correct answers on the 45-item Natural Sciences exam is about one third of the total, and it slowly decreases across the years 2009–2014. [→ Marta F. Barroso 2017](#marta-f-barroso-2017)

## Evidence

### Marta F. Barroso 2017

Marta F. Barroso, M. S. O. Massunaga and G. Rubini. (2017). Brazilian National High School Examination: assessing student's learning in Physics. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1707.04181](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1707.04181)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Large-scale observational analysis of Enem microdata for participants concluding high school, 2009–2014. Classical Test Theory grades normalized to 10 show means from 3.4 (2009) down to 2.6 (2013), described as "only one third of th e total number of questions".

> "It can be ob served that the mean value of correct answers in the exam is only one third of th e total number of questions, and that this number is slowly decreasing along the years."
