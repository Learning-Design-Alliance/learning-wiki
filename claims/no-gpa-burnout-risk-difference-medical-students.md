## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among 760 medical students, the prevalence of high burnout risk was 17.0% in the GPA ≤8 group and 14.0% in the GPA >8 group, a non-significant difference. [→ Ilić IM 2023](#ilic-im-2023)

## Evidence

### Ilić IM 2023

Ilić IM, Ilić MD. (2023). The relationship between the burnout syndrome and academic success of medical students: a cross-sectional study. Archives of Industrial Hygiene and Toxicology. https://doi.org/10.2478/aiht-2023-74-3719

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 760 medical students in Kragujevac, Serbia (90.9% response rate) using the MBI-SS. Table 3 reports high burnout risk in 47/276 (17.0%) of the GPA ≤8 group versus 67/484 (14.0%) of the GPA >8 group, chi-squared P=0.270; "the two GPA groups do not significantly differ". Equivalence was not tested.

> "However, the two GPA groups do not significantly differ in the prevalence of high burnout risk (Table 3)."
