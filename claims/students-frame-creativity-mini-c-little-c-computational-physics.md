## Evidence

### Pachi Her and Patti Hamerski 2024

Pachi Her and Patti Hamerski. (2024). Investigating student perceptions of creativity and generative ai in computational physics. 2024 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2024.pr.Her

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative coding of narrative essays from six students in the PH 36X computational physics sequence, coded with the Four C Model. One student described “creating a solution that is new to us,” illustrating mini-c; another emphasized collaboration with classmates, illustrating little-c.

> "Students framed their creativity around mini-c and little-c. Several participants expressed the idea that creativity stemmed from the individual’s prior experience, reflecting mini-c’s emphasis on the individual as well as the learning involved in the creative process."
