Evidence¶

Daumiller 2019¶

Daumiller, M., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2019). University Instructors' Achievement Goals for Teaching. Journal of Educational Psychology, 111(1), 131–148. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000271

q3 · i? · associational · r2

CFA model comparisons for Hypothesis 1a in the instructor survey found that only the four-factor performance model "resulted in an acceptable model fit"; the four classes were moderately to highly correlated (ρ = .54–.81), implying unique variance in each.

"splitting the items into approach and avoidance, or appearance and normative, factors described the data better than not differentiating between them (i.e., one factor model); however, only an incorporation of both aspects (i.e., a four-factor model) resulted in an acceptable model fit."
