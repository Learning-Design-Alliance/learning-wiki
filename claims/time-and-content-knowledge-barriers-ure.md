### N.G. Holmes and Carl E. Wieman 2016

N.G. Holmes and Carl E. Wieman. (2016). Examining and contrasting the cognitive activities engaged in undergraduate research experiences and lab courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020103](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020103)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Discussion of focus group findings: building and testing equipment took over other EPCTA aspects, and many tasks require extensive understanding of the current state of knowledge in the field as well as technical expertise. Both barriers are described as logistically impractical to overcome.

> "The primary barrier to engaging in cognitive tasks in their URE, extracted from student comments, was time. The limited term of the URE restricted the scope of the project."
