# DIBELS subtests share a significant multivariate relationship with TERA early reading dimensions (Early Literacy Competence variate)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Canonical analysis found one significant variate pair, Early Literacy Competence (Rc = .34), linking TERA alphabet knowledge, conventions of print, and meaning from print with the three DIBELS subtests. [→ Rouse 2006](#rouse-2006)

## Evidence

### Rouse 2006

Rouse, H. L., & Fantuzzo, J. W. (2006). Validity of the Dynamic Indicators for Basic Early Literacy Skills as an Indicator of Early Literacy for Urban Kindergarten Children. School Psychology Review, 35(3), 341-355. https://doi.org/10.1080/02796015.2006.12087971

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Canonical correlation analysis of kindergarten DIBELS and TERA scores (N = 326) yielded one significant variate pair, "Early Literacy Competence {Re = .34)"; redundancy analysis showed the TERA explained 31% of variance in DIBELS and DIBELS 25% in TERA.

> "This bimultivariate relationship was defined by one significant canonical variate pair. Early Literacy Competence {Re = .34)."
