## Evidence

### Cao Zhen-Yu 2024

Cao Zhen-Yu, Lin Feng, Feng Chun. (2024). Interpretation of course conceptual structure and student self-efficacy: an integrated strategy of knowledge graphs with item response modeling. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05401-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of 3PLM-estimated theta against final exam scores and average GPA in 208 students. The article reports no significant full-range correlation; equivalence was not tested.

> "the estimated SEL was not significantly correlated with exam scores (p = 0.81) or GPA (p = 0.81). However, within the range of θ > 0, a weak positive correlation was observed between GPA and θ (p = 0.02, r = 0.22)."

### Cao Zhen-Yu 2024 (2)

Cao Zhen-Yu, Lin Feng, Feng Chun. (2024). Interpretation of course conceptual structure and student self-efficacy: an integrated strategy of knowledge graphs with item response modeling. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05401-6

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`
