Bruzgulis 2019¶

Bruzgulis, A., Hart, H., & Young, C. (2019). The challenges facing CPS administrators: Roadblocks to school improvement. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/challenges-facing-cps-administrators

Descriptive comparison of 2017 survey responses against 2009 responses on staffing roadblock items; the brief reports the top-category proportion for difficulty removing poor teachers rose from "39 percent" to "48 percent" despite the REACH teacher evaluation reform introduced in 2012.
