## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Si Chen 2026

Si Chen, Haocong Cheng, Suzy Su, Lu Ming, Sarah Masud, Qi Wang, and Yun Huang. (2026). Redesigning Educational Videos for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Learners. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1145/3772318.3791470](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791470)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Within-subject user study with 16 DHH participants comparing original and revised clips of a mainstream Coursera AR video; linear mixed models with estimated marginal means showed significant reductions in "mental demand", "physical demand", and "temporal pressure" and a significant satisfaction increase (EMM = 0.74, p < .01). No standardized effect size printed.

> "there is a significant reduction in mental demand (EMM = –0.51, t(22.3) = -2.56, p < .05), significant reduction for physical demand (EMM = –0.52, t(22.3) = -2.62, p < .05), significant reduction in temporal pressure (EMM = –0.49, t(22.3) = -2.47, p < .05)"
