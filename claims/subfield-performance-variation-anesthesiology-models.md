`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of performance across 21 subfields following the KSA taxonomy; the article states the number of questions per field was too limited for statistical comparison. "The highest-scoring subfield was geriatric anesthesia" for all three models.

> "The highest-scoring subfield was geriatric anesthesia in GPT-3.5 (58.8%), GPT-4 (88.2%), and CLOVA X (64.7%). The lowest scoring subfield was “neuromuscular blocking agents” for GPT-3.5 and CLOVA X (17.6%), and “anesthesia equipment and monitoring” for GPT-4 (37.5%)."
