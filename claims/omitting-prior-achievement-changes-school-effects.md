`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Refitting the five models without prior achievement on the 2019 English census, mimicking systems yet to reach a cohort with matchable prior achievement. The article reports correlations with the original models' effects "are also substantially reduced (1.000, .815, .778, .584, .794)".

> "omitting student prior achievement from each model (dashed line in Figure 3) dramatically increases the percentages of schools classified as having moderate or large effects (61.9%, 61.9%, 47.1%, 47.1%, 36.7%)."
