### Shayan Majidy 2024

Shayan Majidy. (2024). Addressing misconceptions in university physics: A review and experiences from quantum physics educators. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.20923)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Miller et al. studied predict-observe-explain across 22 mechanics and electricity-and-magnetism demonstrations in introductory courses at two universities, collecting reports within one day and at semester end. Among predictors, approximately 18% of one-day reports mismatched the observed outcome.

> "Students who made predictions reported the outcomes more accurately, whether or not their predictions were correct. Prediction-making was also associated with better retention, but because students chose whether to make predictions, the study cannot isolate its causal effect."
