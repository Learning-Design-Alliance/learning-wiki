Xiaolu Zhang, Nancy Holincheck, Jessica L. Rosenberg, Stephanie Dodman, Ben Dreyfus, Jennifer Simons. (2024). Why Teach Quantum?: Elementary Teachers Initial Beliefs about Quantum. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1109/qce60285.2024.20471](https://doi.org/10.1109/qce60285.2024.20471)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of baseline interviews and surveys found most participants' quantum knowledge came from popular culture; one teacher said, "I know quantum and physics go together in some contexts, but that's about it."

> "Most participants had limited exposure to quantum concepts prior to their work on the project. For many, their knowledge of quantum mechanics was derived from cultural references such as the TV shows “Big Bang Theory” and “Quantum Leap,” or through Marvel movies rather than formal educational or scientific sources."
