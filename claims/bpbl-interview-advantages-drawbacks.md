## Evidence

### Servos Ulrike 2022

Servos Ulrike, Reiß Birger, Stosch Christoph, Karay Yassin, Matthes Jan. (2022). A simple approach of applying blended learning to problem-based learning is feasible, accepted and does not affect evaluation and exam results—a just pre-pandemic randomised controlled mixed-method study. Naunyn-Schmiedeberg's Archives of Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00210-022-02306-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Inductive qualitative content analysis (Mayring) of semi-structured group interviews with 7 bPbL and 7 cPbL groups; category assignment was independently verified by two other persons. "The low effort with regard to travel and the free choice of location" dominated pro arguments.

> "The categories relating to advantages of bPbL that were most frequently touched upon overall, but also by cPbL and bPbL groups, respectively, were the low effort with regard to travel and the free choice of location."

### Servos Ulrike 2022 (2)

Servos Ulrike, Reiß Birger, Stosch Christoph, Karay Yassin, Matthes Jan. (2022). A simple approach of applying blended learning to problem-based learning is feasible, accepted and does not affect evaluation and exam results—a just pre-pandemic randomised controlled mixed-method study. Naunyn-Schmiedeberg's Archives of Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00210-022-02306-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

From the same interview content analysis, chat-communication difficulties (duplications, typing delay, linguistic reduction) generated the most sceptical statements; 27 bPbL-group statements fell in this category versus 15 from cPbL groups.

> "The category to which by far the most bPbL-sceptical statements could be assigned was “Communication: implementation”. Compared to cPbL groups, almost twice as many statements from bPbL groups referred to this category (n = 15 vs. n = 27)."
