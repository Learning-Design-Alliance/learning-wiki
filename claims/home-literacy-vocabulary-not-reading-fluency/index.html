Nurturing the reading brain: home literacy practices are associated with children's neural response to printed words through vocabulary skills. npj Science of Learning 6:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00112-9

Girard 2021 (2)¶

Girard, C., Bastelica, T., Léone, J., Epinat-Duclos, J., Longo, L. & Prado, J. (2021). Nurturing the reading brain: home literacy practices are associated with children's neural response to printed words through vocabulary skills. npj Science of Learning 6:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00112-9

q2 · i? · associational · r2

In the same behavioral sample, the Alouette-R reading fluency test showed no significant relation with home literacy practices for either accuracy or speed. The authors suggest the 3-min dyslexia-screening test may have lacked sensitivity in typical readers.

"there was no positive relation between overall frequency of home literacy practices and either child reading accuracy (partial r(64) = 0.148, p = 0.126) or child reading speed (partial r(64) = 0.121, p = 0.174)"
