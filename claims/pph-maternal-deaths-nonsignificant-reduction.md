## Evidence

### Egenberg 2017

Egenberg, S., Masenga, G., Bru, L. E., Eggebø, T. M., Mushi, C., Massay, D., & Øian, P. (2017). Impact of multi-professional, scenario-based training on postpartum hemorrhage in Tanzania: a quasi-experimental, pre- vs. post-intervention study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1478-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Departmental mortality reports (conception to 42 days postpartum) across the two birth cohorts (4095 and 4031 deliveries); total maternal deaths were similar between periods, and the authors say this finding should be interpreted with caution due to low power.

> "However, we observed a non-significant reduction in maternal deaths related to PPH associated with training (7 deaths before vs. 4 deaths after training; p = 0.35)."
