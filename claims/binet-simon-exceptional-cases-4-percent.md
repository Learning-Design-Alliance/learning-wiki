### Carl C. Brigham 1914

Carl C. Brigham. (1914). An Experimental Critique of the Binet-Simon Scale. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0071183

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Individual-case analysis of the 194 teacher-judged cases: thirty exceptional cases were identified, of which nineteen were re-graded by the principal in the direction the tests indicated, three were under-age for grade, and the remaining eight — "4 per cent, of all the 194 cases" — had no obvious explanation and were attributed to judgment error.

> "They constitute 4 per cent, of all the 194 cases treated above. Since the judgments of the teachers and the principal did not coin- cide within two groups in 9 per cent, of the cases, and since their judgments actually coincided in only 41 per cent, of the eases, the assumption is legitimate that the error of 4 per cent. is on the part of the judgment of the teacher and principal, and not the Binet-Simon scale."
