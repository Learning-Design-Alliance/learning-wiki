Ho Martin Patrick, Heskin Leonie, Dablouk Lena, Richa Yasmina, Nolan Joshua, Brien Andrew O’, Abrar Samin, Hennessey Derek. (2026). Near-peer teaching of laparoscopic skills among medical students: a randomised feasibility study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08461-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed rank tests in this randomised feasibility study (42 students) showed significant pre-to-post improvement in both groups; the article reports the same for Circle Cutting (W = 34.5), String (W = 27.5), and Peg Transfer (W = 29), all "p < 0.001". No effect size is printed.

> "Using Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, there was a significant improvement in both groups’ post-intervention objective scores in “Ball Transfer” station (W = 0, p < 0.001)."
