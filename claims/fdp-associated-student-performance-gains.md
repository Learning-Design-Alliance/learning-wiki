Descriptive comparison of student performance in the same three courses before and after the intervention (Table 1). The article reports mean score improvement from "18.29 6 0.77" to "20.33 6 0.47" and passing rate rising from 49.2 to 56.3%; no significance test is reported for these gains.

> "The improvement of students average mean score of the three courses before workshop training (mean score 18.29 6 0.77) and after workshop training (mean score 20.33 6 0.47) was observed (Table 1). Similarly, overall passing rate of students increased from 49.2 to 56.3% after workshop training."
