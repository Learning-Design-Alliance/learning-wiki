Miranda 2026¶

Miranda, J. P. P., Parreño, E. B., & Rivera, J. G. (2026). Defining AI Fatigue in Academic Contexts: Dimensions, Indicators, and a Stage-Based Model Using Grounded Theory. International Journal of Learning, Teaching and Educational Research, 25(5), 91–107. https://doi.org/10.26803/ijlter.25.5.5

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Frequency counts from grounded theory coding of 1,054 student responses. The Output Overwhelm and Verification Strain indicators captured difficulty handling AI response volume and exhaustion from repeated checking; e.g., "I feel mentally exhausted after checking so many outputs" (n = 72). No effect sizes are printed.
