Evidence¶

Edward L. Thorndike 1911¶

Edward L. Thorndike. (1911). Repetition versus recall in memorizing vocabularies; The effect of continuous exercise and of rest upon difficult mental multiplication. Journal of Educational Psychology, Communications and Discussions. https://archive.org/search?query=Journal+of+Educational+Psychology+Thorndike

q2 · i? · causal · r1

In the same 72-subject mental multiplication experiment, rows done after a 30-minute rest and after a night's sleep showed further gains: "The rest of 30 minutes increased the prod- uct produced per unit of time by about 5%" and the night's rest about 7% more, with accuracy essentially unchanged.

"The rest of 30 minutes increased the prod- uct produced per unit of time by about 5%; the rest of a night in- creased the product by about 7% more. The accuracy of the work remained almost exactly the same."
