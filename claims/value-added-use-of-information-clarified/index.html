Steven Glazerman 2010¶

Washington, DC: Brown Center on Education Policy at Brookings. https://www.brookings.edu/articles/evaluating-teachers-the-important-role-of-value-added-evidence/

The paper's own abstract states its purpose is to clarify confusion about value-added methodology, listing "use of value-added information" as the first area addressed. This is the authors' statement of scope, not an empirical test.

"This paper clarifies four areas of confusion about value-added methodology and its role in teacher evaluation: (1) use of value-added information;"
