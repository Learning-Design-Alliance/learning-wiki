`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A student quote (Subject 32) from the open-ended survey illustrating the 7.5% of students who disliked pre-assigned permanent groups. The article concludes that 'this period of time might cause students to lose their motivation to learn effectively' and recommends that further studies rotate groups and change membership after each lesson.

> "One thing I did not like was formation of permanent group over the duration of the course. This made me lose motivation for each lesson in my group"
