## Evidence

### Soares 2023

Soares, S., Boyes, M. E., Parrila, R., & Badcock, N. A. (2023). Does reading anxiety impact on academic achievement in higher education students? Dyslexia, 29(3), 179–198. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1738

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In this study of 169 university students, an exploratory factor analysis of the RAT-A suggested two factors, labelled social and non-social reading anxiety; both factors had high internal reliability (Factor 1 α = 0.950, Factor 2 α = 0.943). The authors state reading anxiety is "a distinct sub‐type of anxiety" distinct from trait anxiety.

> "We found that amongst university students reading anxiety is a distinct sub‐type of anxiety that is comprised of both social and non‐social anxiety and both of these anxieties are distinct from trait anxiety."
