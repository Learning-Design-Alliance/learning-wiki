# Both the heterogeneity simulation and the heterogeneity text increased pre-service teachers' beliefs about responding to university students' heterogeneity in both studies

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In Study 1, the time × group interaction on beliefs about responding to university students' heterogeneity was significant, with significant time effects in the simulation and text conditions but not the control. [→ Baier-Mosch 2026](#baier-mosch-2026)
`q3 i2` Study 2 replicated this exploratory result for university-student heterogeneity beliefs. [→ Baier-Mosch 2026 (2)](#baier-mosch-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Baier-Mosch 2026

Baier-Mosch, Franziska; Dignath, Charlotte; Kunter, Mareike (2026). Effects of heterogeneity simulations on beliefs. Learning and Instruction 101, Art. 102258. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102258

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 exploratory ANOVA on beliefs about responding to university students' heterogeneity: "a significant interaction between time and group, F(2, 151) =3.214, p =.043, partial η2 =.041"; simple effects were significant for the simulation (partial η2 =.278) and text (partial η2 =.118) conditions, not the control.

> "The results of a mixed ANOVA showed that there was a significant interaction between time and group, F(2, 151) =3.214, p =.043, partial η2 =.041."

### Baier-Mosch 2026 (2)

Baier-Mosch, Franziska; Dignath, Charlotte; Kunter, Mareike (2026). Effects of heterogeneity simulations on beliefs. Learning and Instruction 101, Art. 102258. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102258

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 replicated the exploratory finding: "a significant interaction between time and group, F(2, 149) =3.427, p =.035, partial η2 =.044", with significant time effects in the simulation (partial η2 =.155) and text (partial η2 =.110) conditions and none in the control.

> "The results of a mixed ANOVA showed that there was a significant interaction between time and group, F(2, 149) =3.427, p =.035, partial η2 =.044."
