`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational summary of 2016 social media analytics for DES EPO posts, obtained from native Facebook Insights and Twitter Analytics. The article reports the average post "reached ∼2, 500 users" and that reach was "highly variable," with the lowest and highest posts reaching 258 and 14,450 users.

> "In 2016, the average DES EPO social media post reached ∼2, 500 users. The reach of social media products both within and between projects was highly variable, with the lowest- and highest-reaching posts reaching 258 and 14,450 users, respectively."
