Descriptive analysis of test scores by domain, term, and grade in the norming sample (Table 3.1), reporting mean, SD, and N of test events. The report states scores "increased across terms and grades" with diminishing growth at higher grades; no effect size is printed.

"For each domain, test scores in general increased across terms and grades. As students advanced to a higher grade (e.g., from Grades 2 to Grade 3), less cross-grade and within-grade growth was observed."
