## Evidence

### Knight 1985

Knight, James A. (1985). Building a Positive School Climate. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the Midwestern Educational Research Association, Chicago, IL. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED265663

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The paper reports research conducted by Purkey in North Carolina in which students were identified as invited or disinvited by how teachers greeted them at the classroom door. The two groups "were really not different in their academic ability," yet received different wait times.

> "the invited students were given an average of 3 seconds to respond before the teacher gave clues, restated the question, redirectedthe question, or answeredthequestion themselves.On the other hand, the disinvitedstudentswho were just as capable,were given an average of .9 second"
