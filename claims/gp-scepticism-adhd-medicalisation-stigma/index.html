Evidence¶

Tatlow-Golden M 2016¶

Tatlow-Golden M, Prihodova L, Gavin B, Cullen W, McNicholas F. (2016). What do general practitioners know about ADHD? Attitudes and knowledge among first-contact gatekeepers: systematic narrative review. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-016-0516-x

q2 · i? · review · r3

Thematic narrative synthesis across the review's included quantitative and qualitative studies. The review reports that in Iran (2010), 1 in 5 GPs believed ADHD was not a serious problem, and in the UK ADHD was considered a fashionable diagnosis and referrals a risk avoidance strategy. No effect size is printed.

"In surveys and focus groups in the UK, Australia and Iran (2000–2010), GPs typically expressed mixed feelings and scepticism about ADHD and about medicalising childhood behaviour, as well as concern about stigma."
