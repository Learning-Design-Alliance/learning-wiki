Evidence¶

Sunny Yu 2026¶

Sunny Yu, Myra Cheng, Ahmad Jabbar, Ilia Sucholutsky, Katherine M. Collins, Dan Jurafsky, Robert D. Hawkins. (2026). Cognitive offloading and the speedup illusion in human-AI interaction. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23177

q3 · i? · causal · r3

In the completion-sample analysis, "task completion with AI assistance was faster by 26.14 seconds" on difficult tasks only. Per-task comparisons (Figure 3) showed significant time savings for just three tasks: generating ten synonyms for a word, summarizing a long article, and editing a long text.

"AI assistance only made difficult tasks faster, and not easy ones: task completion with AI assistance was faster by 26.14 seconds (SE=11.9,z=2.19,p<0.05) on difficult tasks, but no difference between independent completion time and AI-assisted completion time was found for easy tasks."
