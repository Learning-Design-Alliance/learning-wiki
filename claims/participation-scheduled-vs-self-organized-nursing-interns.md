## Evidence

### Zhang Hao 2026

Zhang Hao, Mei Yuan, Zhang Chun‐ling, Liu Jie, Ma Xu‐jue. (2026). Nursing Students' Participation and Perceptions of Teaching Effectiveness at Clinical Teaching Bases: A Cross‐Sectional Survey. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70824

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional descriptive survey of 318 final-year nursing interns at two Chinese clinical teaching bases, using three dichotomous participation items; descriptive proportions with Wilson 95% CIs are reported, no effect size for the scheduled-vs-unscheduled contrast.

> "Participation was highest for timetabled classroom discussions (204/318, 64.2%, 95% CI 58.7–69.2) and about half that level for extracurricular academic activities (99/318, 31.1%, 95% CI 26.3–36.4) and self‐organized study groups (89/318, 28.0%, 95% CI 23.3–33.2)."
