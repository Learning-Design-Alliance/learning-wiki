## Evidence

### Mostafavi Marzieh 2025

Mostafavi Marzieh, Shokrpour Nasrin, Afraz Shahram. (2025). Beliefs, practices, and awareness of written corrective feedback among EFL teachers: a mixed-methods study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03861-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 10 purposively sampled teachers analyzed in MAXQDA. Teachers reported "no specific training" and experiential shaping of practice; findings are qualitative with no effect size.

> "Most said they had no specific training beyond general teacher training courses at language institutes or theory-based university classes. Their feedback practices were largely shaped by personal experience, past teachers, articles, and discussions with colleagues."
