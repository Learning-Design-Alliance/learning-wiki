Evidence¶

Grace Kim Young-Suk & Graham Steve 2022¶

Grace Kim Young-Suk & Graham Steve. (2022). Expanding the Direct and Indirect Effects Model of Writing (DIEW): Reading-Writing Relations, and Dynamic Relations As a Function of Measurement/Dimensions of Written Composition. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000564

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation models comparing complete versus partial mediation of discourse oral language and lexical literacy were fitted for each dimension in the Grade 2 sample; the article reports that reading comprehension "differentially mediated the relations of discourse oral language and lexical literacy to writing quality." No mediation effect sizes or test statistics are printed in the available text.

"Reading comprehension was related only to writing quality, but not to writing productivity or correctness in writing; and reading comprehension differentially mediated the relations of discourse oral language and lexical literacy to writing quality."
