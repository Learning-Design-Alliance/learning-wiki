# Explanations of quantum phenomena in a news article lower perceived comprehension of the article relative to no explanation

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a pre-registered randomized online experiment, explanation condition showed a statistically significant omnibus effect on perceived comprehension of the news article (η2= 0.014). [→ Meinsma 2026](#meinsma-2026)
`q3 i?` Control participants reported significantly higher perceived comprehension than both the non-metaphorical and the metaphorical explanation groups; no effect size was printed for these pairwise contrasts. [→ Meinsma 2026 (2)](#meinsma-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Meinsma 2026

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Omnibus ANOVA in the pre-registered randomized online experiment, with explanation condition as independent variable and perceived comprehension as dependent variable; the condition difference was statistically significant with η2= 0.014.

> "Statistically significant differences between the conditions were found for perceived comprehension (F(2,1164) = 8.49, p <.001, η2= 0.014) and actual comprehension (F(2,1131) = 32.6, p <.001, η2= 0.055)."

### Meinsma 2026 (2)

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Bonferroni-corrected pairwise comparisons from the same experiment: the "control group scored significantly higher than both the non-metaphorical group" and the metaphorical group on perceived comprehension (mean difference = 0.32 and 0.29); only unstandardized mean differences are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "Results of the pairwise comparisons with a Bonferroni correction showed that for perceived comprehension, the control group scored significantly higher than both the non-metaphorical group (mean difference = 0.32, SE = 0.09, p <.001) and the metaphorical group (mean difference = 0.29, SE = 0.08, p = 0.002)."
