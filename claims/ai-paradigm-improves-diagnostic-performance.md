## Evidence

### An Jie 2026

An Jie, Leng Xi, Liu Yujie, Zhou Jie, Liang Yi, Chen Yuna, Qiu Shijun (2026). Rebuilding ability acquisition in medical imaging teaching: a multi-level AI-driven teaching paradigm based on cognitive-information theory. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09002-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Course-level RCT with 60 postgraduate imaging students randomized 1:1 to AI paradigm or traditional teaching over 8–12 weeks, analyzed using linear mixed-effects models. The AI group showed "AUC: β = 0.072, P < 0.001" and "accuracy: +5.8%, P = 0.004". No standardized effect size was printed for this finding.

> "The AI paradigm significantly improved diagnostic performance (AUC: β = 0.072, P < 0.001; accuracy: +5.8%, P = 0.004)"
