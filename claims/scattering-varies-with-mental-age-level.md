`q2 i?` The smallest amount of scattering occurs at the lowest mental age levels, and the largest possibly in the middle range of ages. [→ Wallin 1921](#wallin-1921)

## Evidence

### Wallin 1921

Wallin, J. E. W. (1921). Intelligence Irregularity as Measured by Scattering in the Binet Scale. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of scattering by intelligence-age level across the three scales, with the author noting results are discrepant across scales and that upper-age data are unreliable because of scale limits. The author states these age-level differences do not entirely harmonize with Pressey's and Mathews' findings.

> "it would be that the smallest amount of scattering occurs in the lowest mental age levels, and possibly that the largest amount tends to occur in the middle range of ages."
