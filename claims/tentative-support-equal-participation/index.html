Evidence¶

Strauß 2021¶

Strauß, S., & Rummel, N. (2021). Promoting regulation of equal participation in online collaboration by combining a group awareness tool and adaptive prompts. But does it even matter? International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning (2021) 16:67–104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09340-y

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of group-level gini coefficients at the end of the two two-week collaborative tasks in the university online course, reporting printed group counts and percentages: "12 out of all 16 groups (75%)" and later "38,5% (5 out of 13 groups)".

"At the end of topic #3, 12 out of all 16 groups (75%) achieved a gini coefficient of 0.5 or greater, at the end of topic #4, the percentage dropped to 38,5% (5 out of 13 groups)."

Strauß 2021 (2)¶

Strauß, S., & Rummel, N. (2021). Promoting regulation of equal participation in online collaboration by combining a group awareness tool and adaptive prompts. But does it even matter? International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning (2021) 16:67–104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09340-y

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Condition comparison of groups with gini ≥ 0.5 across the field experiment; the authors state the analysis "revealed that out of all groups that struggled to meet the criterion, more than half belonged to the control condition." No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"Comparing the experimental conditions (see Table 4) revealed that out of all groups that struggled to meet the criterion, more than half belonged to the control condition."
