Evidence¶

Purificacion Toledo-Morales & Jose Manuel Sanchez-Garcia 2018¶

Purificacion Toledo-Morales & Jose Manuel Sanchez-Garcia. (2018). Use of Augmented Reality in Social Sciences as Educational Resource. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 19(3), Article 4. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1183349

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Open in-depth interview with the classroom teacher about incentives, integration possibilities and problems of AR use. She regretted the use being limited to a few sessions and one subject, and considered AR unsuited to some materials such as language, maths or physical education.

"Although it welcomed this initiative was not it considered formed to create the content related to the AR, process that was complex, the same being so desirable that these contents are provided by publishers and out of easy implementation in the classroom"
