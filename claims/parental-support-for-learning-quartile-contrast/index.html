Sebring 1995¶

Sebring, P. A., Bryk, A. S., Luppescu, S., & Thum, Y. M. (1995). Charting Reform in Prairie School: Results of Student and Teacher Surveys. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-prairie-school-results-student-and-teacher-surveys

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive results from the eleven-item student scale on parents' involvement in students' learning, covering encouragement, homework help, and talk about school and grades. In bottom quartile schools the largest group, about 45 percent, report limited support from parents.

"More than one in every six students in the top quartile schools report a great deal of support from parents (scores of 9-10). Nearly 50 percent of the students in these schools report moderate support (scores of 6-8). Thus, two-thirds of the students fall in these top two categories."
