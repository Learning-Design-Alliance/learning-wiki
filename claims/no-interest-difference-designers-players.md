## Evidence

### Slussareff 2016

Slussareff, M. and Boháčková, P. (2016). Students as Game Designers vs. 'Just' Players: Comparison of Two Different Approaches to Location-Based Games Implementation into School Curricula. Digital Education Review, 29, 284-297. http://greav.ub.edu/der

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same quasi-experiment; interest measured by one questionnaire item scored from -1 to 1 (stronger/no change/weaker), distributed one week after the game-play session. Descriptive means were 0.36 (experimental) vs 0.12 (comparison), but the ANOVA found "no significant difference" [F(1, 25) = 1.12, p = 0.300]; equivalence was not tested.

> "One-way between subjects ANOVA was conducted, its results for dependent variable interest in a given area revealed no significant difference between active ('designers') or passive ('players') approach to the use of educational location-based games in school curricula; at the p<.05 level [F(1, 25) = 1.12, p = 0.300]."
