BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08714-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational engagement counts recorded during sessions: Rain Classroom response logs for the experimental group and instructor-observed, manually counted student-initiated questions for the control group. Counts are descriptive; no statistical test or effect size is reported.

> "In the experimental group, a greater number of responses were recorded during question-and-answer sessions (58 responses across five questions) compared to the control group (11 responses for the same questions). Additionally, student-initiated questions were more frequently observed in the experimental group (19 vs. 7 in the control group)."
