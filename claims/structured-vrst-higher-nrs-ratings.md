## Evidence

### Sparn MB 2026

Effect of a structured virtual reality simulation curriculum on laparoscopic performance in a porcine cholecystectomy model: a prospective comparative study. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blinded raters assigned a numerical rating scale score (0–10) as an amendment to GOALS in the porcine cholecystectomy post-test; the intervention group's "Median = 6.7" exceeded the control's "Median = 4.8" (U = 419.0, p = 0.036).

> "Furthermore, subjective evaluations using the NRS revealed significantly better ratings for the intervention group (Median = 6.7) versus control (Median = 4.8; U = 419.0, p = 0.036)"
