## Evidence

### Jinann Bitar 2022

Jinann Bitar, Gabriel Montague, Lauren Ilano. (2022). Faculty Diversity and Student Success Go Hand in Hand, So Why Are University Faculties So White? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED626440

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ed Trust analysis of IPEDS faculty demographics from Fall 2005 to Spring 2020 across 543 public institutions. The report found "many of the highest percentage increases in Black and Latino faculty were at institutions that had no (that is, zero) Black or Latino faculty in 2005". Top increases ranged from 5.6% to 13.8% (Black) and 6.3% to 13.5% (Latino). No effect size is printed.

> "many of the highest percentage increases in Black and Latino faculty were at institutions that had no (that is, zero) Black or Latino faculty in 2005, so any increase amounted to a large percentage point increase."
