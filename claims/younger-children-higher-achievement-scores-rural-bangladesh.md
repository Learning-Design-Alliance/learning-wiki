## Evidence

### Hossain Sheikh Jamal 2021

Hossain Sheikh Jamal, Tofail Fahmida, Sujan Hasan Mahmud, Arifeen Shams El, Hamadani Jena. (2021). Factors associated with school achievement of children aged 8–10 years in rural Bangladesh: Findings from a post hoc analysis of a community-based study. PLoS ONE 16(7): e0254693. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254693

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted regression (Table 5) of the 372-child rural Bangladeshi cohort: the age coefficients are negative with confidence intervals excluding zero for all three subscales, so younger children scored higher.

> "Older children had lower scores of reading -0.48 (-0.94, -0.02), spelling -0.41 (-0.88, -0.02) and math computation -0.47 (-0.80, -0.14)."

### Hossain Sheikh Jamal 2021 (2)

Hossain Sheikh Jamal, Tofail Fahmida, Sujan Hasan Mahmud, Arifeen Shams El, Hamadani Jena. (2021). Factors associated with school achievement of children aged 8–10 years in rural Bangladesh: Findings from a post hoc analysis of a community-based study. PLoS ONE 16(7): e0254693. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254693

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted background-characteristics regression (Table 2): girls had scored significantly higher than boys unadjusted in reading (B 4.98, CI 0.27–9.70) and spelling (B 6.32, CI 1.90–10.75), but the adjusted coefficients were not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "After adjustment of related background characteristics in the model, sex of the children remained no longer significant."
