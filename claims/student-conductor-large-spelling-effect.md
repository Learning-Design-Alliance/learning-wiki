## Evidence

### Galuschka Katharina 2014

Galuschka Katharina, Ise Elena, Krick Kathrin, Schulte-Körne Gerd. (2014). Effectiveness of Treatment Approaches for Children and Adolescents with Reading Disabilities: A Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0089900

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of spelling performance by intervention conductor across randomized controlled trials. The Hedges g effect size for student-led interventions was g = 0.945 while teacher-led (g = 0.099) and therapist-led (g = 0.148) interventions led to "negligible mean effect sizes"; the difference reached statistical significance (p = .021).

> "Interventions that were conducted by teachers (g' = 0.099; 95% CI [-0.412, 0.610]; I 2 = 0%) or special education therapists (g' = 0.148; 95% CI [-0.082, 0.378]; I 2 = 23%) led to negligible mean effect sizes. Interventions that were conducted by students reached a large mean effect size (g' = 0.945; 95% CI [0.417, 1.474]; I 2 = 0%). This difference reached statistical significance (p = .021)."
