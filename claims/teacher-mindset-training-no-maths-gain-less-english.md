## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pupils whose teachers received the professional development intervention made no additional progress in maths compared to control pupils, and made less progress in English, though the English difference was not statistically significant. [→ Rienzo et al. 2015](#rienzo-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Rienzo et al. 2015

Cinzia Rienzo, Heather Rolfe and David Wilkinson (NIESR). (2015). Changing Mindsets: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581132

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

School-level randomised controlled trial of the teacher training (INSET) intervention involving 30 schools and 1,505 Year 5 pupils, compared with a business-as-usual control. The trial found "no additional progress in maths" and non-significantly less progress in English for intervention pupils.

> "made no additional progress in maths compared to pupils in the control group. These pupils made less progress in English than pupils in the control group, but this finding was not  statistically significant"
