## Evidence

### Joswig H. 2026

Joswig H., Nestle S. H., Funk J. M., Sultanova S., Doenitz C., Seyfried T., Augustin J., Grassmann F., Träger U., Blecha S. (2026). Effect of training on navigated frameless and frame-based stereotactic brain biopsies: a retrospective comparison of staff neurosurgeon and trainee perioperative performance and complications. Acta Neurochirurgica. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-026-06948-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective comparison of operation times by surgeon type and technique. Frame-based anesthesia time was also longer for residents (164.5 ± 98 vs 111.8 ± 28.4 min; p < 0.01). "frame-based biopsies took significantly longer for supervised trainees".

> "Navigated biopsies required similar operation times in both groups, but frame-based biopsies took significantly longer for supervised trainees (47.5 ± 32 vs. 32.9 ± 9.9 min; p = 0.047)."

### Joswig H. 2026 (2)

Joswig H., Nestle S. H., Funk J. M., Sultanova S., Doenitz C., Seyfried T., Augustin J., Grassmann F., Träger U., Blecha S. (2026). Effect of training on navigated frameless and frame-based stereotactic brain biopsies: a retrospective comparison of staff neurosurgeon and trainee perioperative performance and complications. Acta Neurochirurgica. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-026-06948-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective two-center cohort. Operation time for navigated frameless biopsies did not differ significantly between residents and staff (p = 0.133), though anesthesia time was slightly shorter for residents (p = 0.049).

> "Mean operation time for navigated stereotactic biopsies was 38.1 ± 14.8 min for residents and 44.7 ± 22.6 min for BCFNs (p = 0.133)."
