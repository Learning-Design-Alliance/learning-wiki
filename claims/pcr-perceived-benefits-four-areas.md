## Evidence

### Noonkong et al. 2017

Uraiwan Rattanapan Noonkong, Anamai Damnet, Kanjana Charttrakul. (2017). Enhancing Thai Engineering Students’ Complaints and Apologies through Pragmatic Consciousness-Raising Approach (PCR). Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.6p.92

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis using Grounded Theory's color coding of semi-structured interviews with twelve participants; the article reports the four benefit areas in the participants' perceptions of the innovative class.

> "Furthermore, participants perceived the PCR class to be beneficial in four areas: having more awareness of the impacts of social factors in language use, realizing the favor of indirect strategies, more understanding of nonverbal communication, and provision of motivating class atmosphere."
