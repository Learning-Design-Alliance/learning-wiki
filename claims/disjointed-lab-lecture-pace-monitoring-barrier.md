# Lab sections covering content ahead of lecture hindered students' ability to monitor their learning pace, leading one participant to withdraw

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A participant with executive-function challenges could not determine whether they were on pace when the lab covered content ahead of the lecture, and the difficulty was significant enough that they withdrew from the STEM course. [→ Westley James 2019](#westley-james-2019)

## Evidence

### Westley James 2019

Westley James, Kamryn Lamons, Roberto Spilka, Caroline Bustamante, Erin Scanlon and Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2019). Hidden walls: STEM course barriers identified by students with disabilities. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1909.02905](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1909.02905)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Single-participant finding from the IPA analysis: the participant said "the- the lab and lecture were so disjointed, it always felt like I was behind, even though I wasn't," and their usual pace-monitoring strategies did not translate to this STEM course.

> "they shared how the lab section, which occurred separate from the lecture, covered content ahead of the lecture, resulting in the participant not being able to determine if they were on pace with the course"
