# The association between sleep problems and poor school performance is partly mediated by children's internalizing and externalizing mental health problems, with a direct effect remaining only for persistent DIMS

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mediation through internalizing and externalizing problems was significant for all DIMS groups, but a significant relative direct effect on poor school performance remained only in the Persistent DIMS group (log odds ratio 0.7076, p < .001). [→ Stormark KM 2019](#stormark-km-2019)

## Evidence

### Stormark KM 2019

Stormark KM, Fosse HE, Pallesen S, Hysing M (2019) The association between sleep problems and academic performance in primary school-aged children: Findings from a Norwegian longitudinal population-based study. PLoS ONE 14(11): e0224139. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0224139

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parallel multiple mediation analysis (PROCESS macro, 5,000 bootstrap samples) with internalizing and externalizing SDQ subscales as mediators. The "significant relative direct effect in Persistent DIMS group (log odds ratio of 0.7076, p< .001)" was absent in the Concurrent group.

> "there was still an overall significant direct relative effect of DIMS on poor school performance (χ(3) = 14,26 p< .001), due to a significant relative direct effect in Persistent DIMS group (log odds ratio of 0.7076, p< .001), while not in the Concurrent DIMS group (log odds ratio of 0.2240, p = .23)"
