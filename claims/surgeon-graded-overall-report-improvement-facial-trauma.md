## Evidence

### Malouf William T. 2024

Malouf William T., Kondaveeti Geeth, Phillips Jacline G., Patel Kunjan, Hall Justin A., Fourrey Torrey L., May Nelson, Meegalla Nuwan T., Reger Kevin J., Runyan Christopher M., Hiatt Kevin D. (2024). Facial trauma education in radiology: using surgeon feedback as the benchmark for success. Emergency Radiology 31(6):807-814. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10140-024-02288-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post assessment study in which 26 radiology participants interpreted paired complex facial trauma CT cases before and after an educational session; blinded otolaryngology evaluators graded reports on 5-point Likert scales, and "significant improvements were noted" in overall accuracy and clarity.

> "Comparing pooled post-assessments to pre-assessments, significant improvements were noted in the reporting of the Le Fort, nasoseptal, and NOE subunits as well as in the overall report scores for both report accuracy (Fig. 4) and report clarity."

### Malouf William T. 2024 (2)

Malouf William T., Kondaveeti Geeth, Phillips Jacline G., Patel Kunjan, Hall Justin A., Fourrey Torrey L., May Nelson, Meegalla Nuwan T., Reger Kevin J., Runyan Christopher M., Hiatt Kevin D. (2024). Facial trauma education in radiology: using surgeon feedback as the benchmark for success. Emergency Radiology 31(6):807-814. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10140-024-02288-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of pooled pre/post assessment grades found organization rose from 3.16 to 3.48 (p = 0.036) while actionability rose non-significantly from 3.16 to 3.37 (p = 0.088); no effect sizes were printed.

> "The overall report organization metric significantly improved from a mean Likert score of 3.16 on the pre-assessment to 3.48 on the post-assessment (p = 0.036). The overall report actionability metric improved, but not significantly from a mean Likert score of 3.16 on the pre-assessment to 3.37 on the post-assessment (p = 0.088)."
