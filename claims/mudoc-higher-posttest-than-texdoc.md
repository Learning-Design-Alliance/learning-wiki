# Document-grounded conversational AI with interleaved text-and-image responses (MuDoC) yields significantly higher post-test scores than a text-only conversational AI (TexDoC) in a visually rich STEM topic

## Evidence

### Taneja 2026

Taneja, K., Singh, A., & Goel, A.K. (2026). Impact of Multimodal and Conversational AI on Learning Outcomes and Experience. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_60](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29760-0_60)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled online experiment on Prolific (N=124) learning meiosis cell division, with pre-test screening and random assignment to MuDoC, TexDoC, or DocSearch. The ANOVA on post-test scores gave "F(2,121) = 4.60, p= .012, η2 = 0.071", with MuDoC significantly above TexDoC ("p=.010,dif f= 0.69"); MuDoC vs DocSearch (diff=0.47) was not significant (p=.11).

> "A one-way ANOVA on post-test scores revealed a statistically significant effect of the AI system type with a moderate effect size,F(2,121) = 4.60, p= .012, η2 = 0.071. Post-hoc comparisons using Tukey's honestly significant difference (HSD) test showed thatMuDoCparticipants achieved significantly higher scores thanTexDoC(p=.010,dif f= 0.69)."
