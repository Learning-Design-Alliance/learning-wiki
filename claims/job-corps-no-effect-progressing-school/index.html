Evidence¶

Job Corps 2008¶

Job Corps. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Job Corps. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://jobcorps.dol.gov/about.htm

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial of Job Corps applicants randomly assigned to program (9,409) or control (5,977), analyzed at 48-month follow-up. The WWC table reports a mean difference of –0.1 years, effect size –0.06, not statistically significant, improvement index –3 percentile points for 11,313 students.

"At the end of the 48-month follow-up period, Schochet et al. (2001) found no statistically significant difference between Job Corps and control group youth in their self-reported years of regular schooling completed."
