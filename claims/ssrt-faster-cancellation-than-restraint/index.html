Evidence¶

Sinopoli 2011¶

Sinopoli, K.J., Schachar, R., & Dennis, M. (2011). Reward Improves Cancellation and Restraint Inhibition Across Childhood and Adolescence. Developmental Psychology, 47(5), 1479-89. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0024440

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Repeated measures ANOVA comparing SSRT across the cancellation and restraint versions of the SST. The article reports a large task effect with partial η2=0.871, which the authors say supports the idea that IC is not a unitary construct.

"SSRT was faster in the cancellation task relative to the restraint task (linear effect, F(1, 39)=263.686, p<0.001, partial η2=0.871)."
