Evidence¶

Ansari 2018¶

Ansari, A. (2018). The persistence of preschool effects from early childhood through adolescence. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000255

q2 · i? · associational · r3

In the same mediational decomposition models of the matched ECLS-K sample, children who attended preschool demonstrated lower social skills during kindergarten, which in turn resulted in them making fewer academic gains; the printed share of convergence attributed to social skills was roughly 10–20%, with no standardized effect size printed.

"the difference in children’s social skills throughout middle childhood that was attributed to preschool enrollment accounted for roughly 10–20% of the convergence in their academic test scores from kindergarten through eighth grade"
