### John M. Aiken 2019

John M. Aiken, Rachel Henderson, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2019). Modeling student pathways in a physics bachelor's degree program. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010128)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Contingency table analysis of all features for 1422 students (Table I): all features except modern physics showed negligible effect sizes (φ of 0.03–0.05 for grades and timing, below d = 0.2 equivalent). This is in tension with prior work by Aiken and Caballero.

> "however, through this analysis it was found that high grades in both courses had negligible eﬀect sizes on earning a degree from the Department of Physics and Astronomy."
