### Roderick 2011

Roderick, M., Coca, V., & Nagaoka, J. (2011). Potholes on the Road to College: High School Effects in Shaping Urban Students' Participation in College Application, Four-year College Enrollment, and College Match. Sociology of Education, 84(3), 178-211. https://doi.org/10.1177/0038040711411280

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of the sequential enrollment steps among 2005 CPS graduates aspiring to four-year degrees, showing attrition even after acceptance. Among those who applied and were accepted, only 80 percent (0.41/0.51) enrolled in college the following fall.

> "Approximately 10 percent of graduates report that they were accepted into a four-year college but were not enrolled in a four-year college the following fall."
