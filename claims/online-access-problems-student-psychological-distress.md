### Zarei Soraya 2022

Zarei Soraya, Mohammadi Shahriar. (2022). Challenges of higher education related to e-learning in developing countries during COVID-19 spread: a review of the perspectives of students, instructors, policymakers, and ICT experts. Environmental Science and Pollution Research International. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11356-021-14647-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the Kapasia et al. study of undergraduates and postgraduates in West Bengal, India during lockdown: over 30% complained of poor internet, and "self-mentioned depression, stress, and anxiety in 42% of the students". Percentages are as printed; no effect sizes reported.

> "More than 30% of the students complained about poor internet connection. All those problems led to self-mentioned depression, stress, and anxiety in 42% of the students"

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Subedi et al., the Nepal nursing-student study in which 63% felt anxiety due to online-class problems. The review offers no effect size for this finding.

> "63% of nursing students in Nepal felt anxiety because of the problems that occurred during online classes"
