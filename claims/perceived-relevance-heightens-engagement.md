`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 4 of the thematic analysis of postcourse open-ended survey responses; students characterized the curriculum as relatable and applicable rather than theoretical or distant, and described familiar struggles reframed through new actionable lenses. Qualitative; the authors frame the motivation link as a suggested pattern, not a tested one.

> "Across comments, the prevailing tone suggested that applicability heightened motivation; when strategies resonated with real‐world contexts, learners became more willing to test them and, by extension, sustain engagement with the course."
