## Evidence

### Carpentier Philippe 2022

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. (2022). A sequential model of the contribution of preschool fluid and crystallized cognitive abilities to later school achievement. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0276532

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

QNTS path analysis: Block Design, DCCS and VCR at 63 months predicted the latent crystallized factor, "significantly predicted (R2 = .45) by the three fluid skills". R² is variance explained; no standardized path coefficient is printed in this sentence.

> "Indeed, the three measures of crystallized abilities clearly converged to create a reliable general latent crystallized abilities factor, which was significantly predicted (R2 = .45) by the three fluid skills, and successfully predicted later school achievement."

### Carpentier Philippe 2022 (2)

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. (2022). A sequential model of the contribution of preschool fluid and crystallized cognitive abilities to later school achievement. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0276532

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

QLSCD path analysis with fluid measures at two preschool ages. Visual-spatial skills and short-term memory at 41 months had β = .17 and .21, and at 73 months β = .33 and .09, predicting the crystallized factor (R2 = .44).

> "the two fluid skills, already at 41 months (β = .17 and Beta = .21, p < .001, respectively) and then at 73 months (β = .33 and Beta = .09, p < .001, respectively) each predicted crystallized abilities (latent factor; R2 = .44)"
