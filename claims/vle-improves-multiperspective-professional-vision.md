Promoting preservice teachers' multiperspective professional vision in virtual learning environments – On the effects of modeling videos and prompts during video-based lesson analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10546-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-post-follow-up study with 145 preservice teachers in elementary science education. Hierarchical regression analyses controlling for pretest levels showed the VLE group outperformed the control group on all facets of multiperspective professional vision at posttest and follow-up.

> "A quasi-experimental pre-post-follow-up study with 145 preservice teachers showed that a basic virtual learning environment improved participants' multiperspective professional vision compared to an untreated control group in the short and long term."
