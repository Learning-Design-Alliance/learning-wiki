## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2007

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Waterford Early Reading Program™. July 16, 2007. http://www.pearsondigital.com/pdfs/werp/researchsummary-werp.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study characteristics reported in WWC Appendix A1: a quasi-experimental design in six inner-city or rural Ohio public schools. Students were pretested in the fall and posttested in the spring; because of mobility and absences, 64 students attrited and the final analysis sample was 76 students (mean age five years seven months), mostly low-SES and African-American and eligible for free/reduced lunch. Baseline equivalence was demonstrated by the authors.

> "The study began with 140 full-day, at-risk Kindergarten students who were randomly selected from six schools. Students from four schools who received the Waterford Early Reading Program™ were matched to students in two schools who did not receive the program."
