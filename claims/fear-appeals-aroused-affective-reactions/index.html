Related Claims¶

  • Strong, moderate, and minimal fear appeals were equally effective in teaching the factual content of a dental hygiene communication and modifying beliefs about the proper kind of toothbrush — related
  • The strong fear appeal produced a more mixed or ambivalent attitude toward the communication: rated more interesting and of high quality, yet with the greatest subjective dislike and the most complaints about content — related
  • The minimal fear appeal produced the greatest conformity to the communication's hygiene recommendations, while the strong appeal produced no significant change in dental practices — related
