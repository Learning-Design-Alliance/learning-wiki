Mead, C. D. (1916). Results in Silent Versus Oral Reading. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0067774

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study of 112 sixth-grade pupils of the Cincinnati schools tested in May, 1914, using the "Alice in Wonderland" material with "Six two-minute tests were given in each of the methods of reading". All five classes reproduced a greater percentage of possible points silently; "Seventy-one per cent, of the children taken separately did better by this method".

> "Six two-minute tests were given in each of the methods of reading. Without exception, each class (five) reproduced a greater percentage of possible points by the silent method. Seventy-one per cent, of the children taken separately did better by this method."
