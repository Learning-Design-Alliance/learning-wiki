## Evidence

### Baker Courtney N. 2015

Baker Courtney N., Tichovolsky Marianne H., Kupersmidt Janis B., Voegler-Lee Mary Ellen, Arnold David H. (2015). Teacher (Mis)Perceptions of Preschoolers' Academic Skills: Predictors and Associations With Longitudinal Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000008

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of discrepancy scores in a sample of 760 preschoolers from 70 low-income centers; discrepancy scores were normally distributed and classified children by standard deviations over- and underestimated. "99 (13%) having discrepancy scores between 1 and 2 standard deviations above the mean."

> "With regard to language and pre-literacy, 124 preschoolers were considerably overestimated by their teachers, with 99 (13%) having discrepancy scores between 1 and 2 standard deviations above the mean and 25 (3%) having discrepancy scores over 2 standard deviations above the mean."
