## Evidence

### Abdulkadir Kırbas 2018

Abdulkadir Kırbas. (2018). The Effect of Interactive Whiteboard Applications Supported by Visual Materials on Middle School Students' Listening Comprehension and Persistence of Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(11): 2552-2561. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061120

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test post-test control group experiment with two intact 8th-grade classes (n=36 each) formed through random assignment at one Erzurum middle school. Independent samples t test on post-test LAT scores yielded t = 7.991, p = .000, with means of 76.94 versus 62.92; the article reports "a significant difference between the groups at .05 significance level".

> "the LAT score of the students in the experimental group, who were taught using interactive whiteboards supported by visual materials, was 76.94, whereas the LAT score of the students in the control group, who were taught according to the 2018 Turkish Course Curriculum, was 62.92. The t value, which was calculated based on the experimental and control group scores, was 7.991."
