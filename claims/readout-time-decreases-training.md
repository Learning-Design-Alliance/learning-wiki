### Stegarescu Pavel 2025

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Readout time learning curves (cubic spline GEE, four internal knots) with automatically recorded times in the 200-case study. Mean readout times for urology trainees were consistently higher but not statistically significant at any point (final segment difference 53.26 s [−9.42, 95.88]); prior radiological training had no significant effect on readout time reduction.

> "By case 200, urology trainees had reduced their readout times by 215.75 s (71.74, 349.23) and radiology trainees by 174.01 s per case (113.08, 232.80)."
