## Evidence

### Orawiwatnakul 2011

Orawiwatnakul, W., & Wichadee, S. (2011). A Comparison Of Students' Outcomes In Two Classes: Business Administration Students VS Communication Arts Students Based On Self-Directed Learning Activities. Contemporary Issues In Education Research, 4(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1072831

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-samples t-test comparing pre- and post-test English proficiency for 40 BA students in a 12-week quasi-experimental study. The article reports "significantly higher than the pre-test mean score t(39) = 12.54, p<0.05"; only the test statistic is printed, so no effect size is available.

> "the post-test mean score of students from the School of Business Administration was significantly higher than the pre-test mean score t(39) = 12.54, p<0.05"

### Orawiwatnakul 2011 (2)

Orawiwatnakul, W., & Wichadee, S. (2011). A Comparison Of Students' Outcomes In Two Classes: Business Administration Students VS Communication Arts Students Based On Self-Directed Learning Activities. Contemporary Issues In Education Research, 4(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1072831

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dependent-samples t-test for the 40-student CA group in the same 12-week intervention. The article reports "a significant improvement, t(39) = 15.56, p<0.05"; no effect size is printed.

> "Communication Arts students who had been exposed to self-directed learning made a significant improvement, t(39) = 15.56, p<0.05 , on their English pre- and post-tests after 12 weeks of the treatment"
