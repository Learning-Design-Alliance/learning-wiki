## Evidence

### Hampton et al. 2020

Lauren H Hampton, Ann P Kaiser and Elizabeth A Fuller. (2020). Multi-component communication intervention for children with autism: A randomized controlled trial. Autism, 24(8), 2104–2116. https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361320934558

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-intervention regressions from the RCT controlling for autism severity and pretest. The article notes PLS expressive differences were significant at the 0.1 level, and that SGD utterances averaged less than one occurrence per sample, so SCU changes were primarily spoken language.

> "On average, group assignment predicted increases in SCU-CCX (p = 0.076), SCU-NLS (p = 0.612), and PLS–Expressive/Receptive raw scores (p = 0.056 and p = 0.150, respectively), but these increases were not significant."
