### Kyriakoulis Konstantinos 2016

Kyriakoulis Konstantinos, Patelarou Athina, Laliotis Aggelos, Wan Andrew C, Matalliotakis Michail, Tsiou Chrysoula, Patelarou Evridiki. (2016). Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5066070/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Pre-post evaluation of information-seeking behaviors among final-year medical students (n=65) in Malaysia after multifaceted EBM training. The proportion searching PubMed or Medline more than three times per week rose from 9.7% to 31.7%, P<0.001.

> "The second study involved final-year medical students who received MI (lectures and small-group clinical sessions) and findings showed that students who searched PubMed or Medline for more than three times per week increased from 9.7% to 31.7% (P<0.001) [18]."
