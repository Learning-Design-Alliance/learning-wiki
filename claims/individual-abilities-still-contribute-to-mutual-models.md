# Symmetry of partner models does not rule out a contribution of individual modelling abilities to mutual modelling quality

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The finding that partner models are symmetrical does not rule out the fact that individual abilities also contribute to the quality of the modelling process. [→ Dillenbourg 2016](#dillenbourg-2016)

## Evidence

### Dillenbourg 2016

Dillenbourg, P., Lemaignan, S., Sangin, M., Nova, N., & Molinari, G. (2016). The symmetry of partner modelling. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-016-9235-5

`q3 · i?`

Authors' qualification, stated in the article's abstract, of the symmetry results reported across five collaborative experiments: correlated mutual-model accuracies are interpreted carefully, and individual modeler/modelée abilities remain a possible contributor. This is the article's own interpretive caveat, not an additional statistical result.

> "However, these findings do not rule out the fact that individual abilities also contribute to the quality of modelling process."
