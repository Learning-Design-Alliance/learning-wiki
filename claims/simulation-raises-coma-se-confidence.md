## Evidence

### DerGarabedian B 2025

DerGarabedian B, Lacovara L, Delic J, Rajagopalan S. (2025). BRAIN-SIM: Leveraging Simulation for Neurocritical Care Education with an Innovative Multidisciplinary Approach. Journal of Intensive Care Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1177/08850666251327156

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of twenty-two surveys from four coma and status epilepticus case-based simulation sessions, using paired two-tailed t-tests. Mean confidence rose "just under 1 point on the Likert scale" for both conditions, with P < .001 for each comparison.

> "Mean learner confidence levels in managing neurocritical patients improved by just under 1 point on the Likert scale from pre- to post-simulation with coma [3.18 ± 0.51 versus 4.32 ± 0.25 (P < .001)] and status epilepticus [3.23 ± 0.55 versus 4.36 ± 0.29 (P < .001)]"
