Evidence¶

Ketcheson et al. 2017¶

Leah Ketcheson, Janet Hauck and Dale Ulrich. (2017). The effects of an early motor skill intervention on motor skills, levels of physical activity, and socialization in young children with autism spectrum disorder: A pilot study. Autism 21(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/1362361316650611

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Second repeated-measures ANCOVA on 7-day accelerometer wear at pre, post, and 4-week follow-up. The article reports "non-significant group differences" for all PA levels; equivalence was not tested. Authors note small sample limited statistical power.

"For PA outcomes, non-significant group differences were observed for all levels of PA including SPA, LPA, MPA, MVPA, and VPA."
