Evidence¶

Inga Glogger-Frey 2022¶

Inga Glogger-Frey, Anne-Katrin Treier, Alexander Renkl. (2022). How preparation-for-learning with a worked versus an open inventing problem affect subsequent learning processes in pre-service teachers. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09577-6

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Exploratory Bayesian test of the inverse of Hypothesis 3b on think-aloud data from the N = 45 experiment; Table 2 reports regulation means of 0.75 (inventing) versus 0.30 (worked example), d = −.70, and the mediation analysis found regulation did not significantly mediate transfer.

"It was close to 4 times more likely that this data would occur under the hypothesis that the inventing group showed more metacognitive regulation than the worked example group"
