`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative reflexive-monitoring analysis across the six cases. Low forum activity was attributed partly to the small population of relatives using the site, so no-one wanted to be the first, and partly to lack of staff promotion; positive feedback from relatives was a strong motivator where it occurred.

> "As staff became aware of the lack of forum activity, they became less motivated to invite relatives, creating a vicious cycle."
