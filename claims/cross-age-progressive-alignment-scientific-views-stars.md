## Evidence

### Philipp Bitzenbauer 2023

Philipp Bitzenbauer, Sarah Navarrete, Fabian Hennig, Malte S. Ubben, and Joaquin M. Veith. (2023). A cross-age study on secondary school students' views of stars. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020165](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020165)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' summary in the discussion of their cross-age ANOVA study of N = 366 German secondary students in three cohorts, rating statements on stars; the study reports that "The statistically significant increase in the percentage of responses aligned with current scientific views from lower to upper secondary school applies to all aspects of stars examined in this study."

> "The statistically significant increase in the percentage of responses aligned with current scientific views from lower to upper secondary school applies to all aspects of stars examined in this study, as demonstrated through ANOVAs and post-hoc pairwise comparisons."
