## Evidence

### Sapkaroski Daniel 2021

Sapkaroski Daniel, Mundy Matthew, Dimmock Matthew Richard. (2021). Immersive virtual reality simulated learning environment versus role‐play for empathic clinical communication training. Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1002/jmrs.555

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired pre-post analysis of the SE-12 self-efficacy questionnaire completed before and after the assigned training session; participants lacking both questionnaires were excluded. The study reports "a significant improvement in self‐assessed communication scores" for all groups except CRP, with the printed percentage gains quoted above.

> "Analysis of intragroup (pre‐ vs. post‐) training scores demonstrated that all groups except the CRP group reported a significant improvement in self‐assessed communication scores following training: 11% for TVR (P < 0.05), 4.3% for TRP (P < 0.05), and 7.2% for CVR (P < 0.05)."
