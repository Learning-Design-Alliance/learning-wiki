## Evidence

### Gider Neslihan Yağmur 2025

Gider Neslihan Yağmur, Tosun Betül, Dirgar Ezgi (2025). Evaluation of the Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices of Intensive Care Unit Nurses Regarding Eye Care: A Descriptive Study. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70114

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null comparison in the 207-nurse cross-sectional survey between 29 nurses reporting formal eye care training and 177 without, across all ECCCQ sub-dimensions. The article reports no significant differences and no effect sizes; the authors suggest existing training programs may lack comprehensiveness or practical reinforcement.

> "Receiving formal training on eye care did not yield statistically significant differences in knowledge (p = 0.308), attitude (p = 0.897), implementation (p = 0.456), or total scores (p = 0.364)."
