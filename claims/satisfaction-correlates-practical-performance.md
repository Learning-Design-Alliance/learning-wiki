## Evidence

### Hsu Ming-Sung 2025

Hsu Ming-Sung, Yeh Chun-Liang, Cheng Shih-Jung, Lin Chun-Pin. (2025). Transforming Dental Morphology Education: The Impact of Three-Dimensional Digital Simulation on Practical Skill Development. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.103904

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation in the RCT between satisfaction questionnaire scores and practical test scores; the printed coefficient is "r = 0.453". The authors frame it as a potential influence, not a tested causal effect.

> "A moderate correlation was also observed between satisfaction with the digital course and practical test scores (r = 0.453), suggesting a potential influence of digital engagement on skill acquisition"

### Hsu Ming-Sung 2025 (2)

Hsu Ming-Sung, Yeh Chun-Liang, Cheng Shih-Jung, Lin Chun-Pin. (2025). Transforming Dental Morphology Education: The Impact of Three-Dimensional Digital Simulation on Practical Skill Development. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.103904

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple linear regression on experimental-group data predicting practical test scores from semester grades and satisfaction. Both predictors were significant; satisfaction's "smaller effect" was β = 0.28 (95% CI: 0.08-0.48); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Multiple linear regression analysis (Table 4) confirmed that semester grades were a significant predictor of practical test performance (β = 0.72, 95% CI: 0.53-0.91, P < .001). Satisfaction with the digital course also had a statistically significant but smaller effect (β = 0.28, 95% CI: 0.08-0.48, P < .05)"
