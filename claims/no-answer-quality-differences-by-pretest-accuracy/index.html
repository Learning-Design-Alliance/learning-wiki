Evidence¶

Clinton 2013¶

Clinton, V., Alibali, M. W., & Nathan, M. J. (2013). The effects of diagrams and questioning-while-reading on learning from a statistics lesson. In Martinez, M. & Castro Superfine, A. (Eds.), Proceedings of the 35th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. Chicago, IL: University of Illinois at Chicago. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584483

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Null one-way ANOVA on embedded-question answer accuracy by pretest accuracy; no effect size is printed, so no magnitude is asserted. The article also reports no associations between embedded-question answer quality and posttest scores for either subgroup.

"There were no differences in answer quality between participants who answered the pretest correctly (M = 3.00, SD = 1.70) and participants who answered the pretest incorrectly (M = 3.11, SD = 1.72)."
