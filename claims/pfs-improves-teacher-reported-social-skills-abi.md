# Preschool First Step intervention improves teacher-reported social skills (ESP-ABI) in preschoolers with challenging behaviors

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Children assigned to PFS had significantly higher teacher-reported adaptive behavior (ESP-ABI) at postintervention than controls, Hedges's g = .88. [→ Feil 2014](#feil-2014)

## Evidence

### Feil 2014

Feil, E. G., Frey, A., Walker, H. M., Small, J. W., Seeley, J. R., Golly, A., & Forness, S. R. (2014). The Efficacy of a Home-School Intervention for Preschoolers With Challenging Behaviors: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Preschool First Step to Success. Journal of Early Intervention. https://doi.org/10.1177/1053815114566090

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cross-site RCT randomizing 126 classrooms (one target child each) to PFS or usual-care control. Covariate-adjusted regression on ESP-ABI postintervention scores yielded t = 5.59, p < .001, Hedges's g = .88, a large effect favoring the intervention.

> "For the prosocial behavior domain, the intervention group differed from the control group on the three parent- and teacher-reported outcomes, with informants reporting statistically significant improvement at posttest in the prosocial functioning of children receiving PFS as compared with children in the control condition."
