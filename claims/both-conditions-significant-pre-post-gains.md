# Both instructional conditions showed significant immediate learning gains from pre-test to post-test, measured within each condition

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` MetaRoundWorm participants showed significant pre-to-post gains (d = 1.81) and Interactive Slides participants also showed significant gains (d = 1.22). [→ Xuanru Cheng 2025](#xuanru-cheng-2025)

## Evidence

### Xuanru Cheng 2025

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test/post-test comparison in the between-subjects study; both conditions showed significant gains, with "MetaRoundWorm: t(15) =7.24, p < .001, d = 1.81" and "Interactive Slides: t(14) =4.72, p < .001, d = 1.22".

> "Both groups exhibited significant gains from their respective pre-test scores (MetaRoundWorm: t(15) =7.24, p < .001, d = 1.81; Interactive Slides: t(14) =4.72, p < .001, d = 1.22), confirming that both interventions were effective in facilitating short-term learning."
