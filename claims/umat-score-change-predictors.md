## Evidence

### Puddey Ian B 2014

Puddey Ian B, Mercer Annette, Andrich David, Styles Irene. (2014). Practice effects in medical school entrance testing with the undergraduate medicine and health sciences admission test (UMAT). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-48

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariate linear regression of change in percentile score from first to second sit (N = 17,089), adjusting for initial section scores and years between tests. "The significant predictors of an increase in overall performance were being male or being a candidate from New Zealand rather than from Australia." Socio-demographic predictors raised variance explained to 12.2% (total), 15.3%, 22.7% and 27%.

> "The significant predictors of an increase in overall performance were being male or being a candidate from New Zealand rather than from Australia, while performance on re-sit was lower for older candidates and for those from Asian language, European language or Other language backgrounds compared to an English language background."
