Bruce S. Rawlings & Sarah J. Cutting. (2024). Linking Disparate Strands: A Critical Review of the Relationship Between Creativity and Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09973-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports growing evidence that "children who attend alternative schools (e.g. Montessori, Steiner) outperform those attending mainstream ones on figural (and to a lesser extent verbal) DT measures", citing multiple studies including Swiss 4–12-year-olds controlling for intelligence.

> "There is growing evidence that children who attend alternative schools (e.g. Montessori, Steiner) outperform those attending mainstream ones on figural (and to a lesser extent verbal) DT measures (Denervaud et al., 2021; Eon Duval et al., 2022)."
