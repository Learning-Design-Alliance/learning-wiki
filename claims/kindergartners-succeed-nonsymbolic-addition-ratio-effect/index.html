Evidence¶

Gilmore Camilla K. 2010¶

Gilmore Camilla K., McCarthy Shannon E., Spelke Elizabeth S. (2010). Non-symbolic arithmetic abilities and achievement in the first year of formal schooling in mathematics. Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2010.02.002

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Experiment 1's task required approximate addition of two dot arrays and comparison of the sum to a third array across ratios 4:5, 4:6, 4:7, with dot size, array size and density correlated with number positively on half the trials and negatively on half. Appendix analyses ruled out alternative strategies.

"Children in the main experiment succeeded at the non-symbolic addition task (62.0%, chance = 50%, t(40) = 7.20, p < .001), showing the characteristic ratio effect (F(1, 40) = 15.37, p < .001; Figure 2a) and without resort to guessing strategies or reliance on non-numerical properties of the displays"
