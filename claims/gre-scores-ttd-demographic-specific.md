# GRE scores correlate with time to degree in opposite directions for international and URM students

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Quantitative GRE score was negatively correlated with time to degree for international students (r = -0.2081, p = 0.0349). [→ Walters T 2022](#walters-t-2022)
`q2 i2` Verbal GRE score was positively correlated with time to degree for URMs (r = 0.3299, p = 0.0329), and a significant slope difference by representation was found for verbal scores (p = 0.0197). [→ Walters T 2022 (2)](#walters-t-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Walters T 2022

Walters T, Abeyta A, Bean AJ, Wilson MA. (2022). The impact of holistic review on correlations between doctoral student outcomes, and GPA and GRE scores in the biomedical sciences. PLOS ONE 17(12): e0279258. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0279258

`q2 · i2`

Scatterplot and Pearson correlation of GRE scores with time to degree in the retrospective GSBS cohort; the international-student quantitative correlation (Table 4, p = 0.0349, r = -0.2081) was one of two significant findings.

> "First, there was a negative correlation between quantitative GRE score and TTD for international students. These data suggest that the higher an international student scores on the quantitative portion of the GRE, the less time it will take for that student to complete the program."

### Walters T 2022 (2)

Walters T, Abeyta A, Bean AJ, Wilson MA. (2022). The impact of holistic review on correlations between doctoral student outcomes, and GPA and GRE scores in the biomedical sciences. PLOS ONE 17(12): e0279258. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0279258

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same correlational analysis (Table 4): URM verbal GRE score correlated positively with time to degree (p = 0.0329, r = 0.3299), and linear regression showed a significant verbal-score slope difference between well-represented students and URMs (p = 0.0197).

> "Second, there was a positive correlation between the verbal GRE score and TTD for URMs. This correlation indicates that the higher a URM student scores on the verbal assessment of the GRE, the longer it will take for this student to earn their PhD."
