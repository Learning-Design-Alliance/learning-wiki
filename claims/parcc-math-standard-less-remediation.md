# In math, students meeting the PARCC college-ready standard are less likely to need remediation than students meeting the MCAS proficient standard

## Evidence

### Ira Nichols-Barrer 2015

Ira Nichols-Barrer, Kate Place, Erin Dillon, Brian Gill. (2015). For Massachusetts Students, PARCC and MCAS Exams Comparable in Predicting College Outcomes (In Focus Brief). Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/in-focus-for-massachusetts-students-parcc-and-mcas-exams-comparable-in-predicting-college-outcomes

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The study's remediation analysis found that in math, students achieving the PARCC college-ready standard are "less likely to need remediation than students who achieve the proficient standard on MCAS," while in ELA the standards were not statistically distinguishable. No effect size is printed.

> "In math, students who achieve the college-ready standard on PARCC are also less likely to need remediation than students who achieve the proficient standard on MCAS, while in English language arts the two standards are not statistically distinguishable."
