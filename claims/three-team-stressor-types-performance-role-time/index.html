Dijkstra Femke S. 2021¶

Dijkstra Femke S., Renden Peter G., Meeter Martijn, Schoonmade Linda J., Krage Ralf, van Schuppen Hans, de la Croix Anne. (2021). Learning about stress from building, drilling and flying: a scoping review on team performance and stress in non-medical fields. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-021-00865-7

q2 · i? · review · r3

Qualitative content analysis of the scoping review's 15 included articles, synthesized by two researchers into three main themes. The review reports that stress sources "could be categorized as at least three non-mutually exclusive types" spanning performance, role and time pressure.

"The sources of stress found in the data could be categorized as at least three non-mutually exclusive types: 1) performance pressure; 2) role pressure; and 3) time pressure."
