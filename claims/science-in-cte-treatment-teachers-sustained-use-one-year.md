### Wendi Mizer Stachler 2013

Wendi Mizer Stachler, R. Brent Young, Mari Borr. (2013). Sustainability of professional development to enhance student achievement: A shift in the professional development paradigm. Journal of Agricultural Education, 54(4), 13-30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122391

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Follow-up questionnaire and personal interview data from the 15 experimental agricultural education teachers (N = 15 responding) one year after the Science-in-CTE pilot. Thirteen (86.7%) taught explicit science and were eligible for a personal interview, which one declined, so 12 were interviewed; the paper reports that of those 13, "12 (92%) used a combination of Science-in-CTE materials". Across all 15 experimental teachers that is 12 of 15 (80%); the other two did not teach agricultural education courses that year.

> "Therefore, of the 13 experimental teachers who taught explicit science and received a personal follow -up interview, 12 (92%) used a combination of Science-in-CTE materials that included the science-enhanced lessons and the pedagogical model."
