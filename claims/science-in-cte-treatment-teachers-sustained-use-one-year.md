Wendi Mizer Stachler, R. Brent Young, Mari Borr. (2013). Sustainability of professional development to enhance student achievement: A shift in the professional development paradigm. Journal of Agricultural Education, 54(4), 13-30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122391

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Follow-up questionnaire and personal interview data from the 15 experimental agricultural education teachers (N = 15 responding) one year after the Science-in-CTE pilot. Thirteen (86.7%) taught explicit science; of the 13 interviewed, "12 (92%) used a combination of Science-in-CTE materials".

> "Therefore, of the 13 experimental teachers who taught explicit science and received a personal follow -up interview, 12 (92%) used a combination of Science-in-CTE materials that included the science-enhanced lessons and the pedagogical model."
