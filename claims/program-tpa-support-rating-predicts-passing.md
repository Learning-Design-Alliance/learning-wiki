# Candidates from programs rated highest on TPA support have 1.7 times higher odds of passing than those from lowest-rated programs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Program-level completer ratings of TPA support were related to the likelihood of passing; odds of passing across all attempts were 1.7 times higher for candidates from the highest-rated versus lowest-rated programs. [→ Patrick 2024](#patrick-2024)

## Evidence

### Patrick 2024

Patrick, S. K. (2024). How preparation predicts teaching performance assessment results in California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/544.849

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r?`

Observational analysis linking program-level averages of the CTC program completer survey (66% of 14,709 completers reported being well or very well prepared for a TPA) to candidate TPA outcomes. The printed odds ratio of 1.7 is a small effect.

> "The odds of passing a TPA across all attempts were 1.7 times higher for candidates from programs with the highest rating on TPA support compared to candidates from the lowest-rated programs."
