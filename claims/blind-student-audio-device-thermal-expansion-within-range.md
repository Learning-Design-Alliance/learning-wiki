## Evidence

### O. Negrete 2020

O. Negrete, A. Lisboa, F. J. Peña, C.O. Dib, and P. Vargas. (2020). Teaching labs for blind students: equipment to measure the thermal expansion coeﬃcient of a metal. A case of study. arXiv. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab7c6b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab7c6b)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-case laboratory session: the student with impaired vision took the length readings, his partner read temperatures, and the student performed the linear regression himself; the article reports the slope value "αS SAl ∼ 22.86× 10−6 1◦C", which it states is "clearly within the expected range according to Eq. (2)". No effect size is printed.

> "Once SS ﬁnished collecting the data, he per- formed a linear regression where the slope indicates the value of the coeﬃcient of linear expansion. In this particular lab experience, the value obtained was: αS SAl ∼ 22.86× 10−6 1◦C."
