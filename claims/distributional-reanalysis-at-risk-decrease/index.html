Evidence¶

Wiedermann 2025¶

Wiedermann, W., Reinke, W. M., & Herman, K. C. (2025). Distributional moderation analysis: Unpacking moderation effects in intervention research. Journal of School Psychology, 108. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsp.2024.101399

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Re-analysis of data from a large-scale cluster-randomized trial of the Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management program, modeling students' post-treatment disruptive behavior with inflated GAMLSS. The article reports that distributional treatment effects reveal "a general decrease in the average disruptive behavior for at-risk students", which the traditional mean-focused analysis did not show. No effect size is printed for this finding.

"Although a traditional mean-focused analysis suggests that the intervention reduced students’ average disruptive behavior only for students receiving special education, an evaluation of distributional treatment effects reveals a general decrease in the average disruptive behavior for at-risk students."
