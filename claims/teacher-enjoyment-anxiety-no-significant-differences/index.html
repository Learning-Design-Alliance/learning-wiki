Evidence¶

Hughes 2026¶

Hughes, A., Gerson, S. A., & van Schaik, J. E. (2026). Teacher-led robot intervention in early primary school classrooms improves pupil and teacher outcomes. Computers & Education, 242: 105510. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105510

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Multilevel analysis of teacher anxiety and enjoyment subscales across three timepoints found no significant condition × time interactions; the article notes "positive descriptive trends" in the Intervention+ group without significant effects.

"Despite positive descriptive trends, the model did not show a significant interaction effect of condition and time on teachers’ anxiety scores (see Table 3). Neither Intervention + nor Intervention significantly differed from Control."
