Lord et al. 2017¶

Pippa Lord, Adam Rabiasz, Palak Roy, Jennie Harland, Ben Styles, Katherine Fowler. (2017). Evidence-based literacy support: the 'Literacy Octopus' trial — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN84508118

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Authors' interpretive conclusion drawn from the null impact results across all nine arms together with the implementation and process evaluation findings. The report recommends further research assess whether interventions can transform evidence into practical action and develop supportive implementation conditions in schools.

"The lack of impact across the different interventions suggests that simply communicating research evidence to schools is not enough to improve outcomes. How easily the presented evidence can be used in practice —and the conditions in schools for implementing evidence -based change —might be just as important ."
