## Evidence

### Angela Y. He 2017

Angela Y. He. (2017). Overcoming Barriers to Self-Directed Student Engagement with Educational Games: A Mixed-Methods Case Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.04491](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.04491)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Within-group pretest-posttest analysis of ten students using the same 10-item multiple-choice test before and after game play. The article reports the effect size (d=1.90/1.85=-1.03) as "between 'large' and 'very large'" and a positive pretest-posttest correlation (r=0.59, n=10, p=0.07).

> "A dependent t-test indicated a statistically significant improvement in test scores following the game treatment across groups from 5.10±2.08 to 7.00±2.06 ( t (9)=-3.24, p=0.01); an improvement of 1.90±1.85."
