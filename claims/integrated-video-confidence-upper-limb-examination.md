### Dwyer Shannon 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison within the Upper Limb module analyzed by Mann-Whitney U. Confidence after p = 0.0063, comfort with special tests p = 0.0008, special test explanation p = 0.0007, pathology explanation p < 0.0001, and differential diagnosis confidence p = 0.0322, showing "significantly higher confidence after viewing the assigned preparatory material" in the intervention group.

> "Compared with the control group, students in the intervention group reported significantly higher confidence after viewing the assigned preparatory material and gave higher ratings for anatomy explanation, comfort with special tests, special test explanation, and pathology explanation. The intervention group also reported greater confidence using physical examination findings to guide the differential diagnosis."
