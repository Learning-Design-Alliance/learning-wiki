## Evidence

### Yousef Nadya 2022

Yousef Nadya, Moreau Romain, Soghier Lamia. (2022). Simulation in neonatal care: towards a change in traditional training? European Journal of Pediatrics 181:1429–1436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-022-04373-3

`q3 · i2` · `review · r2`

The review reports a Bayesian meta-analysis (Wagner et al.) of simulation training for shoulder dystocia management by obstetrical providers, with the injury rate falling "from 12.1 to 5.7%" across 3121 deliveries, with the printed effect size "RR, 0.37; 95% Cr, 0.26–0.57".

> "A meta-analysis of 10 studies (non-randomized and 1 cluster randomized trial) showed that the percentage of neonatal brachial plexus injuries per shoulder dystocia case fell from 12.1 to 5.7% (RR, 0.37; 95% Cr, 0.26–0.57; probability of reduction 100%) in 3121 shoulder dystocia deliveries"

### Yousef Nadya 2022 (2)

Yousef Nadya, Moreau Romain, Soghier Lamia. (2022). Simulation in neonatal care: towards a change in traditional training? European Journal of Pediatrics 181:1429–1436. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-022-04373-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review cites a post-hoc analysis of a multicentre cluster randomised trial (van de Ven et al.) reporting that the effect of one-day obstetric simulation team training declined within a year; no effect size is printed.

> "The effect of simulation training was most noticeable immediately after introduction of these programs, but the effect on patient outcomes declined thereafter"
