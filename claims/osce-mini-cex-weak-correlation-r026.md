### Rogausch A 2015

Rogausch A, Beyeler C, Montagne S, Jucker-Kupper P, Berendonk C, Huwendiek S, Gemperli A, Himmel W. (2015). The influence of students' prior clinical skills and context characteristics on mini-CEX scores in clerkships – a multilevel analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0490-3

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective analysis of routinely collected clerkship assessment data at the University of Bern; OSCE data were available for 144 of 165 students. The study reports "only weak correlations" between OSCE and mini-CEX scores, with r = 0.26 for 'overall' and r = 0.27 for 'domain' scores.

> "OSCE scores showed only weak correlations with the mean 'overall' mini-CEX scores (r = 0.26) and the trainers' mean 'domain' mini-CEX scores (r = 0.27; see Fig. 3)."
