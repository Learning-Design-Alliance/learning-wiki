# Problem positions are averaged across grades with a weighting scheme that distrusts per cents near 0% and 100% and omits entries 3 P.E. or more from the median

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In averaging each problem's position across grades, entries within 1 P.E. of the median receive weight 2, entries from 1 to less than 3 P.E. receive weight 1, and entries 3 P.E. or more are omitted; only three measures were omitted. [→ Herring 1919](#herring-1919)

## Evidence

### Herring 1919

Herring, J. P. (1919). Derivation of a scale to measure abilities in scientific thinking. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical weighting procedure in the section referring all problems to zero: per cents near 0% and 100% are distrusted and weighted low, e.g. problem 32 in grade VIII determined by 4.5% of children is less reliable than problem 21 at 47.6%. No effect size is printed.

> "those entries in Table VIII within I P. E. of the median are given a weight of 2, those distant I P. E. or more, but less than 3 P. E., a weight of I, while those distant 3 P. E. or more, are omitted."
