## Evidence

### Marina Babayeva 2025

Marina Babayeva. (2025). Enhancing Physics Hand-on Lab through Online Educational Tools. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.16193](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.16193)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Written feedback from two educator workshops (approximately 20 and 6 participants, four completed surveys) plus a teacher interview after the pilot. The article reports these valuations and that "several teachers also highlighted the lack of inclusive materials for learners with special needs". Nearly all respondents reported high likelihood of future use.

> "Teachers across all levels found collaboration, content sharing, and tool accessibility to be the most valuable aspects. Reported challenges included internet connectivity issues, steep learning curves for some tools, and the need for clearer student instructions."
