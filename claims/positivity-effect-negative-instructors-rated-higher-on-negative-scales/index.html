Evidence¶

Lawson 2022¶

Lawson, A. P., & Mayer, R. E. (2022). The Power of Voice to Convey Emotion in Multimedia Instructional Messages. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00282-y

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

The 2×2×2 ANOVA on bored-instructor ratings in the Mturk within-subjects experiment showed a main effect for positive vs. negative emotion, which the authors read as differentiating positive from negative instructors regardless of agent presence.

"participants rated the bored instructor significantly higher on negative scales (M = 3.51, SD = 0.79) than on positive scales (M = 2.13, SD = 1.25), F(1, 99) = 83.55, p < 0.001, d = 0.91"

Lawson 2022 (2)¶

Lawson, A. P., & Mayer, R. E. (2022). The Power of Voice to Convey Emotion in Multimedia Instructional Messages. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00282-y

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

The 2×2×2 ANOVA on frustrated-instructor ratings in the same experiment showed a main effect for positive vs. negative emotion, consistent with the positivity hypothesis but only weakly sized as printed.

"the frustrated instructor significantly higher on negative scales (M = 3.04, SD = 0.96) than on positive scales ( M = 2.58, SD = 1.17), F(1, 99) = 9.07, p < 0.003, d = 0.31"

Lawson 2022 (3)¶

Lawson, A. P., & Mayer, R. E. (2022). The Power of Voice to Convey Emotion in Multimedia Instructional Messages. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00282-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Follow-up ANOVA on frustrated-instructor ratings showed the valence effect was moderated by agent presence, with the effect stronger for the agent present condition; the authors state the positivity result holds "but only for the agent present condition".

"however, this main effect was mediated by a significant interaction between agent type and positive–negative valence, F(1, 99) = 15.18, p < 0.001, in which the effect was stronger for the agent present condition that the agent absent condition."
