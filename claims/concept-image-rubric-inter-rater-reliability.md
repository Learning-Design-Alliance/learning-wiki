### Uysal 2026

Uysal, F. (2026). The role of gamification in sustainable teacher education: Examining concept images of curriculum elements using a solomon four-group design. PLOS One, 21(4). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0346892

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Inter-rater reliability assessment of the concept image rubric, with all protocols independently scored by two trained raters for pretest and posttest administrations. The article reports that 'multiple complementary reliability indices consistently exceeded acceptable thresholds' (Fleiss Kappa 0.60–0.88; Krippendorff's Alpha 0.60–0.88; ICC 0.89–0.98).

> "multiple complementary reliability indices consistently exceeded acceptable thresholds across all curriculum elements and both pretest and posttest administrations (Fleiss Kappa: 0.60–0.88; Krippendorff's Alpha: 0.60–0.88; ICC: 0.89–0.98)"
