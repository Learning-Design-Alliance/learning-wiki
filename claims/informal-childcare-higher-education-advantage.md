## Evidence

### Garon-Carrier 2022

Garon-Carrier, G., Ansari, A., Letarte, M.-J., & Fitzpatrick, C. (2022). Early childcare enrollment and the pursuit of higher education: A Canadian longitudinal study. Learning and Instruction, 80. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101615

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity score matching analysis (ATT, kernel matching) of 550 NLSCY children linked to tax records; 79% of formal-childcare and 89% of informal-childcare children enrolled in higher education. The article reports "a significant difference between formal and informal childcare" favoring informal care. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "results revealed a significant difference between formal and informal childcare (ATT= -2.89; = -0.100 [CI: -0.165; -0.035], p= .003), such that attending formal childcare in 1994-1995 decreased the probability of pursuing higher education 20 years later relative to informal childcare attendance."
