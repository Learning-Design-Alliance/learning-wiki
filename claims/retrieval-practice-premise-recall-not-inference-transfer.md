The review narrates Tran et al.'s experiments in which students studied premise scenarios, then restudied or practiced retrieval, before multiple-choice transfer questions requiring deductive inferences: "they found no evidence that having engaged in retrieval practice improved the ability to make inferences", though premises showed a testing effect.
