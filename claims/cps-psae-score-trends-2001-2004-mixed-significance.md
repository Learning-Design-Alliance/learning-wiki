Descriptive trend analysis of CPS average PSAE scores 2001-2004 (Figure 8). The report states "the changes in math and writing over this period were not statistically signiﬁcant," while science, social science, and reading differences were statistically significant small improvements.

> "There were small improvements in average PSAE scores in CPS from 2001 to 2004 (as shown in Figure 8) in all ﬁve subject areas, yet the changes in math and writing over this period were not statistically signiﬁcant. The differences in science, social science, and reading were statistically signiﬁcant and indicate a small improvement from 2001 to 2004."
