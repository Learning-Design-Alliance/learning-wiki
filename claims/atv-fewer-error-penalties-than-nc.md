## Evidence

### Cooper Natalia 2021

Cooper Natalia, Millela Ferdinando, Cant Iain, White Mark D., Meyer Georg. (2021). Transfer of training—Virtual reality training with augmented multisensory cues improves user experience during training and task performance in the real world. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248225

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Error-penalty comparison during the real task: the article reports 9 RO participants receiving twenty-one penalties, 12 NC participants forty-six penalties, and 10 ATV participants nineteen penalties; the ATV–NC difference was significant (p = 0.036, one-tailed). No effect size is printed.

> "Statistical comparison tests (Mann-Whitney U) were conducted and the results revealed that the difference in the amount of penalties received was statistically significantly different between the two VR training groups, ATV and NC groups (t(26) = 1.873, p = 0.036, one tailed)."
