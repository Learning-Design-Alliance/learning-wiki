## Evidence

### Kochmar 2020

Kochmar, E., Vu, D.D., Belfer, R., Gupta, V., Serban, I.V., Pineau, J. (2020). Automated Personalized Feedback Improves Learning Gains in an Intelligent Tutoring System. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_26](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-52240-7_26)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Platform experiment on Korbit in which Wikipedia-based explanations, extracted as the first sentence of crawled Wikipedia articles and ranked by a Decision Tree classifier, were delivered to students; "Students rated either or both types of explanations as helpful 83.33% of the time."

> "Experiments on the Korbit platform confirm that extracted and generated Wikipedia-based explanations lead to comparable student learning gains. Students rated either or both types of explanations as helpful 83.33% of the time."
