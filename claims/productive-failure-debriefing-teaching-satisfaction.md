## Evidence

### Yue Zhiying 2025

Yue Zhiying, Wu Haokun, Zhong Linjun, Li Mingxia, Zeng Mingli, Zeng Yujiao, Feng Hexiang, Zheng Rujun, Sun Yu. (2025). Application of productive failure theory combined with debriefing teaching model in peripherally inserted central catheter skill training for oncology specialist nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07863-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square/Fisher comparison of Ho's 20-item teaching satisfaction questionnaire (n = 73 per group). Table 12 shows teaching effect 73 (100%) vs 64 (87.7%), Fisher P = 0.003; teaching method 69 (94.5%) vs 60 (82.2%), P = 0.020; teaching attitude P = 0.010.

> "The differences between the two groups in satisfaction with teaching level, method, and effect were significant (a score of 90 or above is considered satisfactory) which indicated the merit of teaching mode based on the productive failure theory combined with debriefing to improve teaching effect satisfaction (P < 0.001), whereas no significant difference was observed in satisfaction with teaching attitude (P > 0.05)"
