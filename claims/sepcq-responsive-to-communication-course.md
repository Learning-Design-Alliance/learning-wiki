## Evidence

### Zachariae R 2015

Zachariae R, O'Connor M, Lassesen B, Olesen M, Kjær LB, Thygesen M, Mørcke AM. (2015). The self-efficacy in patient-centeredness questionnaire – a new measure of medical student and physician confidence in exhibiting patient-centered behaviors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0427-x

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Pre-post evaluation of students in Sample 2B who completed the course in communication and peer-supervision; a single-group pre-post design without a control group. The article reports Cohen's d of "0.21, 0.38, 0.45, and 0.73" and significance for Factors 2, 3, and Total but not Factor 1 (p = 0.053).

> "SEPCQ-scores increased for students who also completed the SEPCQ after completing the course, with changes corresponding to small-to-medium effect sizes (Cohen's d) [55] of 0.21, 0.38, 0.45, and 0.73 for Factor 1, 2, 3, and Total scores respectively"
