`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The paper reports, citing Ellis and LaPorte (1997), that "it has been demonstrated repeatedly that there is little or no effect of instructionon route of acquisition", which the author takes as evidence that instruction cannot influence underlying linguistic competence.

> "Importantly (as noted in Ellis and Ldporte 1997) it has been demonstrated repeatedly that there is little or no effect of instructionon route of acquisition.These results are indicative of the powerlessness of instruction to influence the learner's underlying linguistic competence."
