### Kilgour 2016

Kilgour, J. M., & Saadia, T. (2016). An investigation into the optimal number of distractors in single-best answer exams. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 21(3), 571-585. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9652-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA tests comparing mean scores across low, average and high performance ability groups for each year group and each of the three exam paper models. Performance remained discriminative despite the reduction in difficulty.

> "Furthermore, ANOVA testing demonstrated that the difference in mean total score between different ability groups, for each paper, remained statistically significant (p < 0.001) (with substantially large f values and 95% CI), regardless of which exam model was used."
