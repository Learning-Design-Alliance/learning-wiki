## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Protocol timings showed markedly lower time per simulation than per experiment for voltage-source and resistor circuits, while for capacitor circuits the difference did not matter and standard deviations were very high. [→ Björn S. Schmekel 2010](#bjorn-s-schmekel-2010)
