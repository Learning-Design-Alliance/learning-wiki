## Evidence

### D. J. Webb 2012

D. J. Webb. (2012). Improving student's problem-solving ability as well as conceptual understanding without sacrificing the physics content of a class. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1210.3385](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1210.3385)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression comparison of sections I and II, taught by the same instructor with identical homework, discussion, lab, and lecture-question materials, differing only in topic ordering. The treatment coefficient was "β4 = 6 .2 ± 2.3 (p = 0.007)", indicating higher than 99% confidence that treatment students earned higher scores.
