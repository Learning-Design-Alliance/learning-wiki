## Evidence

### Herodotou 2019

Herodotou, C., Rienties, B., Boroowa, A., Zdrahal, Z., & Hlosta, M. (2019). A large-scale implementation of predictive learning analytics in higher education: the teachers' role and perspective. Education Tech Research Dev, 67, 1273–1306. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09685-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression on 1325 students across nine courses found OUA weekly usage significantly predicted completion (p = .003). The article reports "the odds ratio is 7,4 times as large" per unit increase in usage; Nagelkerke's R2 was .181.

> "Exp (B) value indicates that when OUA usage is raised by one unit, that is a teacher is accessing OUA .1 or 10% more of the duration of the course weeks, the odds ratio is 7,4 times as large and therefore students are 7,4 times more likely to complete the course"
