`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Question 5 gave students an MZI with an additional detector in the L path; the article states "After working on the QuILT, the difficulty with the effect of an additional detector placed in the L path of the MZI was eliminated (see Table 1)." Table 1 reports 28/0 and Table 2 shows Q5 rising from 56 to 100.

> "On the pretest, 28% of the students incorrectly claimed that adding a detector in the L path would not change anything or would only cause fewer photons arrive at detectors D1 and D2 because some photons are absorbed."
