Evidence¶

Nguyen Van Han & Henriette van Rensburg 2014¶

Nguyen Van Han & Henriette van Rensburg. (2014). The Effect of Computer Assisted Language Learning (CALL) on Performance in the Test of English for International Communication (TOEIC) Listening Module. English Language Teaching, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n2p30

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment with two intact classes of 25 students each at the College of Finance and Customs, same teacher and major. The two-way mixed ANOVA revealed a significant group effect with printed effect size partial eta-squared = .138: "group (treatment vs. control) (F = 7.681, p = .008, partial eta-squared = .138)".

"group (treatment vs. control) (F = 7.681, p = .008, partial eta-squared = .138)"

Nguyen Van Han & Henriette van Rensburg 2014 (2)¶

Nguyen Van Han & Henriette van Rensburg. (2014). The Effect of Computer Assisted Language Learning (CALL) on Performance in the Test of English for International Communication (TOEIC) Listening Module. English Language Teaching, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n2p30

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Same quasi-experimental ANOVA analysis. The interaction between test type and group was significant with printed effect size partial eta-squared = .163: "the interaction between these two variables (F = 9.318, p = .004, partial eta-squared = .163)", supporting greater improvement in the CALL group.

"the interaction between these two variables (F = 9.318, p = .004, partial eta-squared = .163)"
