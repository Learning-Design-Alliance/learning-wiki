Evidence¶

Tseng 2026¶

Tseng, T., Seoror, L.H., Adda, J., Factor, M., Darabi, R., Matschke, K.R., Fu, T., Lin, A., Maram, A., Sinha, A. (2026). Flowcode: An AI-Powered Programming Environment for Scaffolding Iteration in Creative Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06721

q2 · i? · design · r3

Lab study with 9 novice creative coders remixing CodePen Anime.js animations over 40-minute think-aloud sessions. The article reports 8/9 used the flowchart, 5/9 for orientation, and "Users clicked on nodes in the flowchart an average of 10.8 times (SD=6.8)."

"All but one user (8/9) utilized the flowchart during the study (by clicking on nodes or panning/zooming into the flowchart)."

Tseng 2026 (2)¶

Tseng, T., Seoror, L.H., Adda, J., Factor, M., Darabi, R., Matschke, K.R., Fu, T., Lin, A., Maram, A., Sinha, A. (2026). Flowcode: An AI-Powered Programming Environment for Scaffolding Iteration in Creative Computing Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06721

q2 · i? · design · r2

Pilot workshop with 7 beginners in a 3-hour session building Anime.js animations. Thematic analysis of observations, Miro feedback, and logs showed the flowchart assisted "orientation" and that participants clicked nodes (4/7) or zoomed/panned (7/7) while working.

"Three of the seven participants began by clicking on nodes in the flowchart before prompting the LLM or editing the code."
