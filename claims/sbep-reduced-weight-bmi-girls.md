## Evidence

### Nevzat Demirci 2017

Nevzat Demirci, Pervin Toptaş Demirci, Erdal Demirci. (2017). The Effect of School-based Exercise Practices of 9-11 Year Old Girls Students on Obesity and Health-related Quality of Life. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(8): 1323-1331. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050805

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 85 intervention and 85 control girls in two Kars state schools, with Mann Whitney U tests on anthropometric measures (Table 1). The article reports that "the body height, weight and BMI of intervention group had a significant difference at the level of p<.001"; baseline physical properties showed no significant difference.

> "after 4 months from intervention, the body height, weight and BMI of intervention group had a significant difference at the level of p<.001 compared to those of control group"
