Descriptive and regression results from the cross-sectional survey of 97 residents. A total of 49 (51%) scored below the median; the article reports "Year 1 residents had the highest proportion (77%) scoring below the threshold" and Year 2/3 associations were only marginally significant (P = 0.078 and 0.054).
