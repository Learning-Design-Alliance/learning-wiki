## Evidence

### Santesson Ia 2024

Santesson Ia, Schell Carl Otto, Bjurling-Sjöberg Petronella. (2024). Capability to identify and manage critical conditions: effects of an interprofessional training intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05567-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal cohort of 99 proACT course participants answering an 18-question knowledge test before, one week after, and six months after training. The article reports a significant increase from "a mean score of 12.72 before to 15.31 the week after" with sustained improvement at six months.

> "In the longitudinal cohort, knowledge significantly increased after the training, from a mean score of 12.72 before to 15.31 the week after (p < 0.001) and sustained improvement six months later (mean score 14.37, p < 0.001)"

### Santesson Ia 2024 (2)

Santesson Ia, Schell Carl Otto, Bjurling-Sjöberg Petronella. (2024). Capability to identify and manage critical conditions: effects of an interprofessional training intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05567-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 538 in-hospital staff comparing trained versus untrained respondents on the 18-question test. The article reports "mean score 14.30 vs. 13.27, p < 0.001" and that all professions significantly benefited from the training.

> "In the cross-sectional survey, knowledge was higher in the group that had proACT training than in the group that did not (mean score 14.30 vs. 13.27, p < 0.001)"
