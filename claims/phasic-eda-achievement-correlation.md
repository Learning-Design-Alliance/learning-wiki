## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In a physics classroom arousal study with 24 students, phasic EDA extracted via Ledalab's curve matching correlated strongly with achievement (r = .66). [→ Kennett 2025](#kennett-2025)

## Evidence

### Kennett 2025

Kennett, R., Martin, A. J., Pearson, J., Burns, E. C., & Collie, R. (2025). Challenge and Threat Appraisal of Tests in High School: Integrating Educational Psychology and Biopsychology Improves Understanding of How Students Take Tests. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000964.supp

`q2 · i3` · `review · r?`

The supplementary materials report, citing Pijeira-Díaz et al. (2018), a small classroom study (N = 24) using Ledalab's deconvolution to extract phasic EDA, which showed a "strong correlation (r = .66)" with achievement. The review attributes this to that study.

> "Despite involving relatively few students (N = 24), this study illustrated the trend of moving from tonic to phasic EDA, finding a strong correlation (r = .66) between phasic EDA and achievement."
