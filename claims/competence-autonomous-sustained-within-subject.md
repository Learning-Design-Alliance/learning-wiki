## Evidence

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Joint MSEM (Model 3) comparing within-day and within-subject autoregressive parameters. Competence belief was "sustained more strongly within school-subjects" with a significant difference between lag types.

> "Competence belief at time T was sustained more strongly within school-subjects (ρ = .25; p < .001) than within days (ρ = .08; p = .118), a difference that was significant (∆B = -.18; p < .001)."

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019 (2)

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same joint MSEM. Autonomous motivation's within-subject autoregressive effect exceeded its within-day effect, with a significant difference in the printed parameters.

> "Autonomous motivation at time T was more strongly sustained within school-subjects (ρ = .33; p < .001) than within days (ρ = .19; p < .001), a difference that was significant (∆B = -.22; p = 0.045)."
