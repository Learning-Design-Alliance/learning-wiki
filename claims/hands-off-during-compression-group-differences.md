## Evidence

### Metelmann Camilla 2021

Metelmann Camilla, Metelmann Bibiana, Schuffert Louisa, Hahnenkamp Klaus, Vollmer Marcus, Brinkrolf Peter. (2021). Smartphone apps to support laypersons in bystander CPR are of ambivalent benefit: a controlled trial using medical simulation. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-021-00893-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression-adjusted result of the controlled simulation trial on pauses during compression. Mandatory app users averaged 0.5 s hands-off time during compression, not significantly different from the control group's 1.5 s; the facultative group's 11.0 s was significantly higher (β = 9.73 s; p < .001).

> "Participants with mandatory use of app had a hands-off time of 0.5 s during compression, which was not significantly different to the control group (β=-1.06 s; 95 %-CI: [-5.29; 3.17]; p = .62)."
