Appendix analysis comparing unaggregated peer grades with staff grades in the online class. The paper reports "26.3 % of grades were within 5 % of staff grades, and 46.7 % within 10 %", versus median agreement of 42.0% and 65.5%, demonstrating the value of aggregating peer grades.
