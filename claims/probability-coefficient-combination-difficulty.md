## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Asked for the probability of obtaining ℏ/2 for Sz for a single spin-1/2 state with grouped coefficients, 12% of 17 students incorrectly answered |a|² + |b|² instead of |a+b|². [→ Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016](#guangtian-zhu-and-chandralekha-singh-2016)

## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Guangtian Zhu and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving student understanding of addition of angular momentum in quantum mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06165)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single spin-1/2 probability question given to 17 students. "12% of the students incorrectly responded that it was |a|2 + |b|2" rather than combining the coefficients of the same basis vector first to get |a+b|².

> "When we asked 17 students to calculate the probability of obtaining ℏ/2 for measuring the observable Sz (corresponding to ms = 1/2), 12% of the students incorrectly responded that it was |a|2 + |b|2."
