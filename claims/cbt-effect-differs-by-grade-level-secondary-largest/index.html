Moderator (sub-group) analysis dividing the 78 studies into four grade-level categories. Table 3 reports primary .66 (medium), secondary 1.31, high school 1.04, university 1.02, total .95; QB = 19.60, Z = 14.44, df = 3, significant at p < .05.
