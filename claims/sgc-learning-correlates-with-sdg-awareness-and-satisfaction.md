## Evidence

### Blanco 2024

Blanco, J. M., Domínguez, C., Jaime, A., & Usandizaga, I. (2024). Connecting sustainability and computer science curricula through website learning projects embedding different types of student-generated content. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10376-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis across questionnaire items for 301 students. The study reports "significant correlations" between SGC activities and SDG awareness items, and notes that "satisfaction with SDGs correlates with all SGC activities". No correlation coefficients are printed in the available text.

> "Significant correlations are observed between all SGC activities and questions 2 and 3 on SDG awareness (ICTs and SDGs and inclusion in studies), indicating that SGC activities are related to SDG awareness."
