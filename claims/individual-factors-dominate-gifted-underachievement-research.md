## Evidence

### White 2018

White, Sonia, Graham, Linda, & Blaas, Sabrina (2018). Why do we know so little about the factors associated with gifted underachievement? A systematic literature review. Educational Research Review, 24, pp. 55-66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2018.03.001

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of factor coverage (§4.3, Table 5). The review reports "the majority of studies (n=8) focused mainly on individual factors"; only two examined home factors and only one study (Reis et al., 2005) included structural school variables such as program participation and support availability.

> "The majority of studies (n=8) focused mainly on individual factors. The most common factors investigated across the nine studies were motivation, emotion, and students’ perceptions of school."
