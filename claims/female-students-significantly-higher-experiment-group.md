Group comparison of post-experiment questionnaire scores in the experiment versus control sections. The article reports the 5%-level gender difference in the experiment group; the Model 2 Female Experiment interaction coefficient (-0.07) is not significant, so no differential experiment gain is indicated.

> "Female students score significantly higher than their male counterparts in the experiment group, at the 5% level of significance whereas nonwhite students score significantly less than white students in both control and experiment groups at the 5% level of significance."
