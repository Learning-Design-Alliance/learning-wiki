# Auditory input drives feed-forward inhibition onto LA interneurons, with cortical input producing a smaller E/I ratio than thalamic input

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` AT or AC stimulation elicited biphasic responses (disynaptic inhibition, picrotoxin-sensitive) in about half of LA interneurons, and AC input drove feed-forward inhibition with a smaller E/I ratio than AT input (5.9 ± 1.1 vs 10.6 ± 1.9; p = 0.0453). [→ Polepalli 2020](#polepalli-2020)

## Evidence

### Polepalli 2020

Polepalli, J. S., Gooch, H. & Sah, P. (2020). Diversity of interneurons in the lateral and basal amygdala. npj Science of Learning 5:10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0071-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Slice electrophysiology with interneurons voltage-clamped at −40 mV; outward component blocked by the GABAA antagonist picrotoxin, indicating disynaptic inhibition in LA interneurons. "a smaller E/I ratio as compared to AT input"; only test statistics printed.

> "We found that AC input drove feed-forward inhibition with a smaller E/I ratio as compared to AT input (AT-LA E/I ratio = 10.6 ± 1.9; AC-LA E/I ratio = 5.9 ± 1.1; p = 0.0453), consistent with the stronger AT input"
