# Symphony Math students made greater math achievement gains than district peers (effect size +0.30)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Students in grades 1-4 using Symphony Math as their sole technology intervention made greater gains in math achievement and outperformed peers using the standard district math curriculum, with an effect size of +0.30. [→ Symphony Math 2021](#symphony-math-2021)

## Evidence

### Symphony Math 2021

Symphony Math. (2021). Symphony Math program description and evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/symphony-math/

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

One study of 1,203 students in grades 1-4 across 7 schools in a rural Kentucky district compared Symphony Math as the sole technology intervention against the standard district math curriculum combining online and offline resources. On STAR Math, Symphony Math students "outperformed their peers in the district who did not use the program (effect size = +0.30)".

> "Results on the STAR® Math assessment showed Symphony Math students made greater gains in math achievement and, on average, outperformed their peers in the district who did not use the program (effect size = +0.30)."
