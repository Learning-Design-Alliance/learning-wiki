## Evidence

### Jansen et al. 2020

Malte Jansen, Oliver Lüdtke, and Alexander Robitzsch. (2020). Disentangling Different Sources of Stability and Change in Students' Academic Self-Concepts: An Integrative Data Analysis Using the STARTS Model. Journal of Educational Psychology. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000448

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Pooled STARTS estimates across the 11 studies show the autoregressive trait is the largest component; the article reports an average autoregressive stability of β = .65 for mathematics and .64 for the verbal domain across one school year.

> "The largest variance component was the slowly changing autoregressive trait component. It explained slightly more than 50% of the variance in both domains (pooled estimate for mathematics: 54%; pooled estimate for German: 50%)."
