## Evidence

### Päuler-Kuppinger and Jucks 2018

Lena Päuler-Kuppinger & Regina Jucks. (2018). Out-of-Class Instruction in Higher Education – Impact of Approaches to Teaching and Discipline. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n2p199

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression on content-analyzed reflection texts from the salience manipulation (N = 70). Only the teacher/content-oriented extracts differed; the article suggests the student/learning approach may be a more complete approach retrievable only by developed academics.

> "academics in the teacher-focused condition mentioned a greater proportion of teacher/content -oriented extracts than academics who received the instruction to reflect on the students’ learning, b* = -1.16, z = -4.00, p < .001. No differences were found for student/learning -oriented extracts."
