## Evidence

### Ward Stephen Thomas 2014

Ward Stephen Thomas, Mohammed Mohammed A, Walt Robert, Valori Roland, Ismail Tariq, Dunckley Paul. (2014). An analysis of the learning curve to achieve competency at colonoscopy using the JETS database. Gut. https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2013-305973

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mixed effects logistic regression of colonoscopy completion on 36,730 procedures, with trainee and hospital as random intercepts and patient, trainee and training factors as fixed effects. The study reports these five training factors "were independently associated with colonoscopy completion."

> "an increasing number of procedures performed, a high training intensity, previous experience of more than 100 flexible sigmoidoscopies, a baseline level of colonoscopy experience of >20 procedures and being a surgical trainee were independently associated with colonoscopy completion."
