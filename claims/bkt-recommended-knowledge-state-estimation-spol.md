## Evidence

### Hongxin Yan 2021

Hongxin Yan, Fuhua Lin & Kinshuk. (2021). Including Learning Analytics in the Loop of Self-Paced Online Course Learning Design. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education (2021) 31:878–895. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00225-z

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' reasoned recommendation, not a tested result: they argue that although DKT has been reported to have more predicting accuracy in some cases, BKT is a more explanatory model whose linkage to learning theory provides insights for teachers to act on.

> "Hence, when balancing between the explanatory power for insights and the predicting accuracy, BKT is a knowledge estimation option that acutely servers the purposes of learning awareness and academic intervention."
