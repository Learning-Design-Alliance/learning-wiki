## Evidence

### Eric Brewe 2012

Eric Brewe, Laird Kramer, and Vashti Sawtelle. (2012). Investigating student communities with network analysis of interactions in a physics learning center. Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 8, 010101. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.8.010101

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the hierarchical regression results for the 99-student PLC network at FIU, a Hispanic-Serving Institution with a majority minority population. This is an inference from null regression results, not a direct test of equity.

> "The absence of predictive power from variables such as gender and ethnicity indicates that participation in the learning community is equitably distributed. In other words, students do not appear to be excluded due to gender or ethnic background."
