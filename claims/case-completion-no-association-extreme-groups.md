## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In group 1 (GP score below 60), there were no differences in GP score or number of cases done between students who achieved the passing endpoint and those who did not (P = 0.377 for cases done). [→ Fernández Ros Nerea 2021](#fernandez-ros-nerea-2021)
`q2 i?` In group 3 (GP score above 71), there were no differences in GP score or number of cases done between those who achieved the endpoint and those who did not; nearly all group 3 students passed. [→ Fernández Ros Nerea 2021 (2)](#fernandez-ros-nerea-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Fernández Ros Nerea 2021

Fernández Ros Nerea, Lucena Felipe, Iñarrairaegui Mercedes, Landecho Manuel F., Sunsundegui Patricia, Jordán-Iborra Carlota, Pineda Iñigo, Quiroga Jorge, Herrero Jose Ignacio. (2021). Web-based formative assessment through clinical cases: role in pathophysiology teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02691-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis of group 1 (N = 29) by previous GP score; Table 2 reports the number of cases done comparison at p = 0.377 and GP score at p = 0.512, with no effect size printed.

> "In group 1, there were not differences either in the score obtained in GP or in the number of cases done between those who achieved the endpoint or not."

### Fernández Ros Nerea 2021 (2)

Fernández Ros Nerea, Lucena Felipe, Iñarrairaegui Mercedes, Landecho Manuel F., Sunsundegui Patricia, Jordán-Iborra Carlota, Pineda Iñigo, Quiroga Jorge, Herrero Jose Ignacio. (2021). Web-based formative assessment through clinical cases: role in pathophysiology teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02691-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis of group 3 (N = 32); a follow-up comparison against the class's highest quartile found students in the highest quartile "had better scores in the GP exam in the previous year, but the number of cases they did was similar".

> "In the group 3, there were also no differences in the GP score or in the number of cases done between those who achieved the endpoint and those who did not, but only one of them failed the exam."
