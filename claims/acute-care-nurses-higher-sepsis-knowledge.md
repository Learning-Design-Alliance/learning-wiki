## Evidence

### Pei Wang 2026

Pei Wang, Xueting Ju, Jiali Liu, Qingyuan Hou & Guomei Jiang. (2026). Assessing nurses' knowledge and confidence in adult sepsis: evidence from two hospitals in eastern China. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53878-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across clinical areas in the 355-nurse survey found significant differences (F = 15.787, p < 0.001 per Table 3), with ED/UCC nurses achieving "the highest mean scores" and ward nurses the lowest.

> "Among clinical areas, ED/UCC nurses achieved the highest mean scores (mean = 14.23, SD = 2.12), followed by those in ICU/HDU (mean = 12.89, SD = 1.82), while nurses in general wards had the lowest scores (mean = 10.05, SD = 1.55)."
