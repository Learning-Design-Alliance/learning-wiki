### Marcus Kubsch 2026

Marcus Kubsch, Natasha G. Holmes, Antti Lehtinen. (2026). Multidimensional Profiles of Critical Thinking in Physics Labs: Latent Structure, Instructional Change, and Connections to Physics Identity. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.11598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.11598)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged panel model result for the agency-to-profile path. The article reports β=−.084, p< .001, and that suppression checks confirmed the effect was not an artifact of collinearity with belonging; no separate effect size is printed.

> "Pre-agency negatively predicted post-Profile 2 membership (β=−.084, p< .001). Because this result was counterintuitive, we conducted explicit suppression checks: the negative coefficient persisted both with and without belonging in the model"
