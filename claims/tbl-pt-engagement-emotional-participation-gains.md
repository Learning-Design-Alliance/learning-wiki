`q2 i?` Significant improvements were observed in emotional engagement (20.6 ± 2.86 vs. 17.7 ± 3.76, P < 0.001) and participation/interaction engagement (25.5 ± 3.38 vs. 22.4 ± 4.12, P < 0.001) in the TBL+PT cohort. [→ Wang Leshi 2025](#wang-leshi-2025)

### Wang Leshi 2025

Wang Leshi, Chen Peiwen, Wang Xiao, Wei Sijia, Lin Jiayi, Jing Xi. (2025). Integrating team-based and peer-teaching strategies for standardized dental residency: a path to active learning and professional growth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07023-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

SCEQ results via independent t-tests reported in the Results section; the TBL+PT cohort showed significantly higher emotional and participation/interaction engagement ("fostered stronger emotional connections and greater teamwork"). Only t statistics and P values printed.

> "significant improvements were observed in emotional engagement (20.6 ± 2.86 vs. 17.7 ± 3.76, P < 0.001) and participation/interaction engagement (25.5 ± 3.38 vs. 22.4 ± 4.12, P < 0.001), suggesting that the active and collaborative teaching strategies fostered stronger emotional connections and greater teamwork among students"

### Wang Leshi 2025 (2)

Wang Leshi, Chen Peiwen, Wang Xiao, Wei Sijia, Lin Jiayi, Jing Xi. (2025). Integrating team-based and peer-teaching strategies for standardized dental residency: a path to active learning and professional growth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07023-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
