Evidence¶

Tsai 2017¶

Tsai, Min-hsiu. (2017). Reusing Comprehensive Charts of Tense Forms to Teach EFL Students in a University of Science and Technology. World Journal of Education, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v7n3p115

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANOVA of recognition scores by achievement quartile (228 participants). Whole group: F = 9.120, p = .003; advanced achievers: F = 5.934, p = .018; low achievers p = .762, intermediate p = .286, high p = .175. The article concludes the instruction "was most effective on the advanced achievers".

"Table 11 verifies again that the instruction with the Comprehensive Charts was a factor to the recognition task performance for the whole group at the significant level of .01. Regarding the individual groups, only the advanced achiever group reached the significant level (p ＜.05)."
