Observational analysis of units earned by race/ethnicity within QR placement (Figure 4), using SAS GLM Least Squares Means with Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons. The report finds White and Asian students earned "significantly higher than the average numbers of units earned by Black/African American students and Hispanic/Latino students."
