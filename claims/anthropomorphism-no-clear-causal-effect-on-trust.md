# Manipulating anthropomorphism shows no clear causal effect on trust in controlled post-generative-AI studies, though long-term use and cue type condition its effects

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Factorial experiments manipulating anthropomorphic qualities produced mixed results, with several finding no significant or only conditional effects on trust. [→ Abramson SL 2026](#abramson-sl-2026)
`q2 i?` A long-term use study found users who perceived their virtual personal assistant as more anthropomorphic reported higher integrity-based trust, and cue type moderated effects (human-like warmth beat competence in human-like chatbots; the reverse in machine-like chatbots). [→ Abramson SL 2026 (2)](#abramson-sl-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Abramson SL 2026

Abramson SL, Sora-Cardenas J, Nandakumar P, Bruce JE, Gopinath N, Zangari LG and Feijóo-García PG (2026). Trust and interaction design in AI-enabled systems: a systematic literature review. Front. Psychol. 17:1932655. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1932655

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that four of five anthropomorphism studies used factorial experimental designs (average sample size n=197) and found no significant or only conditional trust effects; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The factorial experimental designs manipulating anthropomorphic qualities produced mixed results, with several ﬁnding no signiﬁcant or only conditional eﬀects on trust."

### Abramson SL 2026 (2)

Abramson SL, Sora-Cardenas J, Nandakumar P, Bruce JE, Gopinath N, Zangari LG and Feijóo-García PG (2026). Trust and interaction design in AI-enabled systems: a systematic literature review. Front. Psychol. 17:1932655. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1932655

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes this long-term-use finding to Huang et al. (2024), and separately reports Huang and Ki (2023) found "human-like warmth generated greater trust than competence in human-like chatbots" with the reverse in machine-like chatbots.
