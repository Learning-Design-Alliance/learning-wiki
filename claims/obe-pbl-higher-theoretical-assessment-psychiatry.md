## Evidence

### Shi Wenjie 2025

Shi Wenjie, Shi Xiangsong, Zhuang Lirong, Zhu Tingting, Li Gongyin, Huang Chengbing. (2025). Integrating Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and Problem-Based Learning (PBL) in psychiatric clinical practice teaching: an empirical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08212-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled comparison of 43 fifth-year psychiatry interns, 22 in the OBE-PBL Study Group and 21 lecture-based controls, on a 100-point standardized closed-book exam. The Study Group scored "significantly higher (p = 0.004)"; only means and a p-value are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "The theoretical assessment score of the Study Group (88.45 ± 4.76) was significantly higher (p = 0.004) when compared with the Control Group (83.81 ± 5.30) (Fig. 1)."
