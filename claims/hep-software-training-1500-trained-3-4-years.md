### Michel H. Villanueva 2023

Michel H. Villanueva, Sudhir Malik, Meirin Oan Evans. (2023). Software Training in High Energy Physics. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/2438/1/012063](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2438/1/012063)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article's own report of its training activity: events organized over the last 3-4 years "trained about 1500 people", with virtual training events held in 2020 because of COVID-19. No participation effect size is printed.

> "We have organized training events [11] in the last 3-4 years and trained about 1500 people. Due to COVID-19, virtual training events were held in 2020."

### Sudhir Malik 2022

Lewis, Kyle Cranmer and Gordon Watts. (2022). Broadening the scope of Education, Career and Open Science in HEP. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.08809](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.08809)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The article's review of current HEP training programs (§5.1) reports this participation count for the IRIS-HEP and HSF software training efforts, noting they ran "even in times of COVID-19 challenges". No effect size or evaluation outcome is printed.

> "The software training programs of IRIS-HEP and HSF have trained over 1500 undergrads, Masters, PhDs, postdocs and senior physicists with software skills over the last 3 years, even in times of COVID-19 challenges."
