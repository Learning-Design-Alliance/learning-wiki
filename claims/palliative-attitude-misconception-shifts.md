## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Perceived difficulty decreased (3.2 to 1.6, p < 0.001), recognition of necessity increased (4.6 to 4.8, p < 0.001), and misconceptions about giving up on treatment (p = 0.002) and shortening life (p = 0.005) declined significantly. [→ Thammasin I 2025](#thammasin-i-2025)

## Evidence

### Thammasin I 2025

Thammasin I, Orapan F, Pawita L. (2025). Enhancing thai fifth-year medical students’ knowledge and attitudes toward palliative care through active learning methods. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07659-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fisher's exact tests on ordinal Likert attitude items in the n = 62 pre-post subgroup. The article reports a "notable decrease in the perceived difficulty" and increased recognition of necessity; misconception items also declined significantly (p = 0.002, p = 0.005). No effect sizes were printed.

> "participants reported a notable decrease in the perceived difficulty (mean score: 3.2 to 1.6, p < 0.001) and an increased recognition of the necessity of palliative care (mean score: 4.6 to 4.8, p < 0.001)"
