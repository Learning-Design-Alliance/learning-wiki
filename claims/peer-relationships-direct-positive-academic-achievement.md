`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 717 junior high students from two middle schools in eastern China, using standardized final-exam scores in Chinese, math, and English. The structural model reported "a significant and positive association between peer relationships and academic achievement (β = 0.178, P < 0.001)".
