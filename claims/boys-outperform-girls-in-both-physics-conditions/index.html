Evidence¶

Musasia 2016¶

Musasia, A. M., Ocholla, A. A., & Sakwa, T. W. (2016). Physics Practical Work and Its Influence on Students' Academic Achievement. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(28). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1118591

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Gender comparison of post-test means and standard deviations (Table 4): experimental boys 55.9, girls 48.8; control boys 28.6, girls 29.5. The article states "the mean scores for boys were higher than that of the girls" in both groups.

"In both groups the mean scores for boys were higher than that of the girls. This shows that boys grasp physics concepts better than their counterpart girls."

Musasia, A. M., Ocholla, A. A., & Sakwa, T. W. (2016). Physics Practical Work and Its Influence on Students' Academic Achievement. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(28). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1118591
