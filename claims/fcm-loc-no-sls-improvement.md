## Evidence

### Aslan Seda Aktı 2025

Aslan Seda Aktı, Turgut Yigit Emrah, Ünlü Gökhan, Allison Jordan. (2025). Effectiveness of flipped classroom model in distance education: An experimental study in Turkish language course. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335721

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on the 21-item SLS scale post-test in the quasi-experiment found no significant difference; the article reports the means were "close to each other" and "The effect size, as measured by Cohen's d, was 0.15, indicating a small effect".

> "According to Table 5, there is no significant difference between the groups according to the independent samples t-test results of the groups' SLS post-test scores (t(57)=−0.574, p > .05)."
