## Evidence

### Orban 2017

Orban, C., Porter, C. D, Smith, J. R. H., and Brecht, N. K., Britt, C. A., Teeling-Smith, R. M., & Harper, K. A. (2017). A Game-Centered, Interactive Approach for Using Programming Exercises in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.1119/1.5058449](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5058449)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cumulative online survey (Fig. 2) of OSU Marion students who completed the first exercise, Spring 2015–Fall 2016 (Ntot = 81 for the difficulty question). The authors report that "the diﬃculty level seems to be appropriate for the population of students"; responses are self-reported and qualitative.

> "The upper right plot in Fig. 2 shows that the diﬃculty level seems to be appropriate for the population of students, with a signiﬁcant number of students selecting “Easy!”."
