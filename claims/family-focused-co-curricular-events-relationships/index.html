Evidence¶

Orta 2022¶

Orta, D., & Gutiérrez, V. (2022). Improving school-family communication and engagement: Lessons from remote schooling during the pandemic. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/improving-school-family-communication-and-engagement

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Focus-group finding with middle school teachers discussing family night and literacy night events conducted virtually, described as "informal, no penalty" gatherings. Home visits and routine wellness checks were also cited as communication-facilitating programs.

"these teachers believed that programs which did not reprimand students for their or their parent's lack of participation would lead to positive communication."
