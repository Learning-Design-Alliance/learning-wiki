# Greater proficiency in scientific reasoning, debating, and English skills, greater perceived importance of soft skills, and more preparation time each predict better performance in YPT research tasks

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Expert-evaluation regressions found that greater post-preparation proficiency in scientific reasoning, debating, and English skills, and greater attributed importance to soft skills, were associated with higher juror-assigned performance, while presentation proficiency showed a negative post-preparation coefficient. [→ Aneva 2022](#aneva-2022)
`q2 i?` Students achieved greater performance in YPT tournaments the more time they spent on preparation, and students also reported that preparation contributed to soft-skill development (mean 2.39 on a 1–3 scale). [→ Aneva 2022 (2)](#aneva-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Aneva 2022

Aneva, B., Faletič, S., Hömöstrei, M., Jenei, P., Kundracik, F., Kyuldjiev, A., Lindner, T., Němec, H., Puhr, H., & Plesch, M. (2022). Development of Soft Skills via IYPT. Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of 794 expert (juror) assessments of student performance at YPT competitions, conducted as part of two master theses; printed coefficients include prof_post_reasoning 0.47 (p = 0.037) and prof_post_english 0.58 (p < 0.001), with prof_post_presentation -1.00 (p = 0.002).

> "we found that greate r proficiency of scientific reasoning skills (prof_post_reasoning), debating skills (prof_post_debating), and English skills (prof_post_english) enhanced performance in inquiry-based learning."

### Aneva 2022 (2)

Aneva, B., Faletič, S., Hömöstrei, M., Jenei, P., Kundracik, F., Kyuldjiev, A., Lindner, T., Němec, H., Puhr, H., & Plesch, M. (2022). Development of Soft Skills via IYPT. Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression on hours spent preparing (h_prep_AYPT coefficient 0.01, p = 1.39E-14) using the expert-assessment performance data; students' self-reported contribution of preparation to soft-skill development averaged 2.39 on a 1–3 scale, with English skills an outlier at 1.70.

> "Our findings indicate that students achieve the greater performance in YPT tournaments the more time they spend on preparation."
