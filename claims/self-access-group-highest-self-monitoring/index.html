Tsai Pi-hua 2015¶

Tsai Pi-hua. (2015). Computer-Assisted Pronunciation Learning in a Collaborative Context: A Case Study in Taiwan. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077659

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative content analysis of the Self-Access CAPT Group's weekly learning logs (30 students practicing MyET independently); the frequency count is descriptive, and the author offers the interpretation that more time on individual on-tasks facilitated awareness of learning progress.

"As for the Self-Access Group, it was discovered that they had produced the highest frequency of self-monitoring of language learning (N= 58)."
