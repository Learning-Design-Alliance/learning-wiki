Arsalidou 2016¶

Arsalidou, M. & Pascual-Leone, J. (2016). Constructivist developmental theory is needed in developmental neuroscience. npj Science of Learning 1, 16016. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.16

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The review reports, citing an fMRI study of children with biogenetic variants of the dopamine D4 receptor gene tested with cognitive tasks, this genotype-activation association. No effect size is printed; the review attributes the finding to the cited researchers.

"Although no behavioural differences were observed across groups, children with the seven-repeat allele had lower neural activation than children without the seven-repeat allele in the left middle and inferior frontal gyri"
