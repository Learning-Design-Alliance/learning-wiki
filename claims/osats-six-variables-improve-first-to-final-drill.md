## Evidence

### Yoshioka Ryuji 2021

Yoshioka Ryuji, Imamura Hiroshi, Ichida Hirofumi, Gyoda Yu, Mizuno Tomoya, Mise Yoshihiro, Saiura Akio. (2021). Simulation training in pancreatico-jejunostomy using an inanimate biotissue model improves the technical skills of hepatobiliary-pancreatic surgical fellows. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244915

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests comparing the six OSATS variables between first and final attempts (Table 1) in the eight-fellow drill study. For example, grader 1 rated gentleness 2.4 (SD 1.1) first versus 3.6 (SD 0.7) final, P = 0.002; all six variables improved significantly.

> "The scores for each of the six variables in the final drill were significantly higher than those in the first drill according to the assessments by both graders."
