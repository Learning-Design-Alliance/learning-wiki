## Evidence

### Liu Xiao-Yu 2022

Liu Xiao-Yu, Lu Chunmei, Zhu Hui, Wang Xiaoran, Jia Shuwei, Zhang Ying, Wen Haixia, Wang Yu-Feng. (2022). Assessment of the effectiveness of BOPPPS-based hybrid teaching model in physiology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03269-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A questionnaire survey of 506 valid responses from Class 2019 at the end of the 2020 semester assessing satisfaction and perceived learning ability. "HBOPPPS satisfied 86.2% students"; percentages are descriptive with no effect size printed.

> "HBOPPPS satisfied 86.2% students and did not satisfy 1.6% of them because of several reasons that were analyzed later. There were also 79.4% of the students who perceived that HBOPPPS improved their learning ability."

### Liu Xiao-Yu 2022 (2)

Liu Xiao-Yu, Lu Chunmei, Zhu Hui, Wang Xiaoran, Jia Shuwei, Zhang Ying, Wen Haixia, Wang Yu-Feng. (2022). Assessment of the effectiveness of BOPPPS-based hybrid teaching model in physiology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03269-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The final questionnaire item asked Class 2019 students about their future teaching model preference. "61.7% of them showed a preference to the HBOPPPS", with the remainder split between offline and online-only teaching; descriptive percentages only.

> "Among 506 respondents, 61.7% of them showed a preference to the HBOPPPS (Fig. 7) while 30.2% still preferred to receive the traditional offline teaching which was used over the whole process before college."
