# Children/adolescents with poor literacy skills show similar average foreign language receptive vocabulary knowledge to peers with typical literacy skills

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### von Hagen 2021

von Hagen, A., Kohnen, S., & Stadie, N. (2021). Foreign Language Attainment of Children/Adolescents with Poor Literacy Skills: a Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09566-6

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of five studies (119 poor readers/spellers, 126 controls) with non-significant heterogeneity (Q = 7.40, I² = 45.98%). The pooled SMD was −0.47 [−0.82, −0.11] with a non-significant Z of −2.59, described by the authors as "a similar performance".

> "For foreign language receptive vocabulary knowledge, children/adolescents with poor literacy skills on average showed a similar performance to their peers with typical literacy skills."
