# Professional learning experiences shift teachers' beliefs away from traditional views toward practice-based science and increase implementation confidence, with gains leveling off after initial workshops

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 1 review `r2`, 1 associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers' beliefs shifted significantly from the beginning to the end of PD, changing most dramatically after the first workshop. [→ belief-shift](#belief-shift)

## Evidence

### belief-shift

McElhaney, K. W., Mills, K., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., & Roschelle, J. (2023, March). A summary and synthesis of initial OpenSciEd research: Updated version [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/171

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal pre-post survey analysis of 322 teacher participants in OpenSciEd PD workshops over two years (Lowell, 2022), as summarized by the review; significance is reported without a printed effect size.

> "Analyses of responses from 322 teacher participants who engaged in PD workshops found that teachers' beliefs shifted significantly from the beginning to the end of PD (Lowell, 2022; OpenSciEd, 2019) and that teachers' beliefs changed most dramatically after the first workshop they participated in."

### confidence-shift

McElhaney, K. W., Mills, K., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., & Roschelle, J. (2023, March). A summary and synthesis of initial OpenSciEd research: Updated version [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/171

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Same longitudinal survey analysis (Lowell, 2022); the review notes confidence increases occurred over multiple rounds of enactment and PD, so shifts cannot be attributed entirely to professional learning.

> "As with beliefs, implementation confidence increased over the course of initial PD experiences and leveled off over time."
