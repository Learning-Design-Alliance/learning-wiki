# Knowledge gained in Newton's Race transfers only to situations closely resembling the game environment, not to different situations

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants scored significantly lower on transfer questions about situations different from the game than on paired questions about game-like situations, d = .523. [→ Anne van der Linden 2022](#anne-van-der-linden-2022)

## Evidence

### Anne van der Linden 2022

Anne van der Linden, Ralph F. G. Meulenbroeks and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2022). Learning Newtonian mechanics with an intrinsically integrated educational game. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2206.11627)

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Posttest paired comparison of two game-situation questions versus two different-situation transfer questions in the same 223-participant study. Participants scored significantly lower on the transfer questions with printed effect size d = 0.523 (printed as d = .523), indicating transfer stayed close to the game context.

> "participants scored significantly lower on the transfer questions (M = .700, SD = .611) than on the similar questions on game situations (M = 1.099, SD = .569); t(222) = 7.865, p < .001; d = .523."
