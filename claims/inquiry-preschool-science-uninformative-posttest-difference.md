## Evidence

### Dejonckheere 2016

Dejonckheere, P. J. N., De Wit, N., Van de Keere, K., & Vervaet, S. (2016). Exploring the classroom: Teaching science in early childhood. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 8(4), 537-558. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1109852.

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The ANOVA on percent uninformative explorations found "no significant difference between the experimentals and the controls in the pre-test, F<1, ns, in contrast to the post-test (Mcontr= 10.79; SD= 16.10; Mexp= 34.63; SD= 34.54), F(1, 52)= 10.51; p<0.002".

> "A further one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) revealed no significant difference between the experimentals and the controls in the pre-test, F<1, ns, in contrast to the post-test (Mcontr= 10.79; SD= 16.10; Mexp= 34.63; SD= 34.54), F(1, 52)= 10.51; p<0.002."
