## Evidence

### Shimono 2018

Shimono, T. R. (2018). L2 reading fluency progression using timed reading and repeated oral reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 30(1). http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t tests on comprehension percentages for the TROR group's four scoring-method passages; pretest-to-posttest comprehension rose about 12% (56.25% to 68.13%), while the extreme method showed a 9% drop that was not significant (p = .054).

> "Contrasting the pre- and posttest comprehension scores, a significant gain of about 12% was observed, t(19) = -2.97, p = .008. Cohen’s d was -0.66, which also indicates a small effect."
