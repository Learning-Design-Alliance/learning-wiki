## Evidence

### Park Yeji 2026

Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel regression analysis using county-level teacher bias estimates. Similarly, teacher explicit racial bias was generally not associated with exam taking or passing disparities, with negative coefficients in the two significant instances.

> "County-level teacher implicit racial bias was generally not associated with Black–White disparities in AP exam taking or passing (Fig. 1, Middle and Right). In two of five instances where the association was significant (2015–16 exam taking, 2013–14 exam passing), the coefficients were negative, indicating that greater teacher implicit racial bias was associated with smaller Black–White disparities."
