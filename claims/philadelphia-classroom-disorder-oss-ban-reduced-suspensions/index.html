q3 · i? · causal · r?

Difference-in-differences analysis of a Philadelphia district reform prohibiting out-of-school suspensions for classroom disorder infractions. The study reports that "classroom disorder OSS decreased" for previously suspended students following the reform; no effect size is printed.

"For students suspended before the reform, classroom disorder OSS decreased and attendance (but not academic achievement) improved following the reform."
