## Evidence

### Mathis 2014

Mathis, E. T., & Bierman, K. L. (2014). The Transactional Influence of Parents and Children in a Parent-Administered School Readiness Program. 2014 SREE Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562845

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-lag model path comparison in the randomized trial. The second freely varying path showed "parental support at time 1 was more strongly associated with child emergent literacy skills at time 2" for intervention families; no effect size printed.

> "parental support at time 1 was more strongly associated with child emergent literacy skills at time 2 for families in the intervention group than in the control group, suggesting that initial preschool levels of parental support had a greater impact on growth in child emergent literacy skills in families receiving intervention"
