NASA Task Load Index questionnaire administered after the simulated procedure. The article reports "no statistically significant differences were observed between novices and experienced participants across any NASA-TLX domains".

> "no statistically significant differences were observed between novices and experienced participants across any NASA-TLX domains, including mental (5.5 vs. 6.0), physical (5.5 vs. 5.0), temporal (5 vs. 5), performance (5.0 vs. 4.5), effort (5.5 vs. 6.0), frustration (4.5 vs. 6.0), or overall workload (32 vs. 34; all p > 0.05)."
