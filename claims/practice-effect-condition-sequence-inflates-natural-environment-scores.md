## Evidence

### Mygind Lærke 2018

Mygind Lærke, Stevenson Matt P., Liebst Lasse S., Konvalinka Ivana, Bentsen Peter. (2018). Stress Response and Cognitive Performance Modulation in Classroom versus Natural Environments: A Quasi-Experimental Pilot Study with Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15061098

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

GEE analysis of d2 scores in the pilot detected a practice effect: "Condition sequence appeared to predict all the variation (p < 0.000)" for TN-E and TN. The authors state this inflated observed cognitive scores in the natural environment.

> "Condition sequence appeared to predict all the variation (p < 0.000) for both TN-E and TN in favor of the second time the children performed the attention task."
