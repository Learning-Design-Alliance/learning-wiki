## Evidence

### Huiyoung Shin 2022

Huiyoung Shin. (2022). Social contagion of academic behavior: Comparing social networks of close friends and admired peers. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0265385

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SAOM analysis of admire networks found a significant positive influence effect, reported as "OR = 10.18, p < .001". Adolescents who received many admire nominations also showed higher engagement over time (OR = 1.11, p < .05).

> "Regarding social influence, the positive influence effects were reflected in how adolescents’ academic engagement became more similar to those of their close friends (OR = 26.05, p < .001) and their admired peers (OR = 10.18, p < .001) over time."
