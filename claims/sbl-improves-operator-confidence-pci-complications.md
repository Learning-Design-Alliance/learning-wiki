## Evidence

### Fukamizu Junichi et al 2025

Fukamizu Junichi et al. (2025). Prevention and Management of Percutaneous Coronary Intervention‐Related Complications: Assessing the Impact of Simulation‐Based Learning and Human Factors Training. Catheterization and Cardiovascular Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1002/ccd.70418

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-participant pre/post comparison using the same 5-point confidence scale immediately after 90-min SBL sessions at EuroPCR 2023–2024; the article reports a "striking improvement in confidence" in managing distal coronary perforation and stent loss. Only self-reported confidence was measured; no effect size is printed.

> "confidence levels in operators surveyed before and after undertaking SBL, for the management of distal coronary perforation and stent loss, demonstrated a striking improvement in confidence, with the vast majority expressing positive confidence levels following training"
