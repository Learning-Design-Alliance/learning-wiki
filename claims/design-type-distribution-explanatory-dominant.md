Matthew T. McCrudden · Macy Bowman · Gala Sofia Campos Oaxaca. (2025). Reporting of Methodological Rigor in Empirical Mixed Methods Research in Educational Psychology. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10090-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Design-type classification of the 57 articles to evaluate prevalence of designs. The review reports "30 (52.6%; 30/57) used an explanatory sequential design", 23 convergent, and four exploratory sequential; all high- and medium-rated articles used a core Creswell and Plano Clark design.

> "30 (52.6%; 30/57) used an explanatory sequential design and indicated the quantitative strand preceded the qualitative strand (Quan → Qual); 23 (40.4%; 22/57) used a convergent design and indicated that the quantitative and qualitative strands were implemented at the same (or approximately at) the same time (Quan + Qual); and four (7.0%; 4/57) used an exploratory sequential design"
