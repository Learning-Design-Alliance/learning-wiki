# Formative peer evaluation cycles improve student performance on final project components and course grades compared to summative-only peer evaluation.

## Evidence

### Mentzer 2017

Mentzer, N., Laux, D., Zissimopoulos, A., & Richards, K. A. R. (2017). Peer evaluation of team member effectiveness as a formative educational intervention. Journal of Technology Education, 28(2), 53-77. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164703

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA comparing fall 2012 (summative-only) and fall 2013 (formative + summative) semesters of a design-thinking course, controlling for SAT composite and writing. The formative semester scored higher on the final project planning component (M = .95, SD = .11 vs. M = .88, SD = .15), with a small effect (η2 = .050).

> "There was a significant difference between fall 2012 and fall 2013 for the final project planning component after controlling for SAT composite and SAT writing scores, F(1,297) = 15.76, p < .001, η2 = .050."

### Mentzer 2017 (2)

Mentzer, N., Laux, D., Zissimopoulos, A., & Richards, K. A. R. (2017). Peer evaluation of team member effectiveness as a formative educational intervention. Journal of Technology Education, 28(2), 53-77. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164703

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on the technical document component of the final project, controlling for SAT scores. The formative peer evaluation semester scored higher (M = .94, SD = .11 vs. M = .89, SD = .15), with a small effect (η2 = .024).

> "There was also a significant difference between semesters for the final project technical document component after controlling for SAT composite and SAT writing scores, F(1,297) = 7.16, p = .008, η2 = .024."

### Mentzer 2017 (3)

Mentzer, N., Laux, D., Zissimopoulos, A., & Richards, K. A. R. (2017). Peer evaluation of team member effectiveness as a formative educational intervention. Journal of Technology Education, 28(2), 53-77. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1164703

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on overall course grade controlling for SAT composite and writing. Students in the formative peer evaluation semester earned higher grades (M = .90, SD = .11 vs. M = .86, SD = .12), with a small effect (η2 = .020). SAT composite was a significant covariate.

> "Related to course grade, there was a significant difference between the fall 2012 and fall 2013 semesters, F(1,297) = 6.17, p = .014, η2 = .020."
