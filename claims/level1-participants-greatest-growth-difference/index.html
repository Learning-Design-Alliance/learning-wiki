Burlison 2014¶

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation in the Conclusions section, not a tested result: it explains the descriptive growth pattern (treatment mean 3.20 versus control .70) by pointing to Level 1 treatment participants' lower starting proficiency. No statistical test of this subgroup explanation is reported.
