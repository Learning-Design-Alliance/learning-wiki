## Evidence

### Liu Xiaonan L. 2014

Liu Xiaonan L., Liang Peipeng, Li Kuncheng, Reder Lynne M. (2014). Brain Correlates of Testing Effect. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0092025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Behavioral results from the fMRI session: 20 participants (5 excluded for head motion, leaving 15; contrasts used 10–15 depending on trial counts) studied 45 Chinese word pairs per session across two sessions. The article reports "correctly recalled 37% of the pairs on Test 1 and 57% on Test 2" and the 36% loss rate.

> "Participants, on average, correctly recalled 37% of the pairs on Test 1 and 57% on Test 2. Of the correctly recalled items on Test 1 (33 pairs), 36% (12 pairs) were not successfully recalled on Test 2"
