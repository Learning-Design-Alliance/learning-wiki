## Evidence

### Taylor 2007

Taylor, I. M., & Ntoumanis, N. (2007). Teacher motivational strategies and student self-determination in physical education. Journal of Educational Psychology, 99, 747-760. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.747

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression with 787 students nested in classes tested student perceptions of the three strategies as predictors of student SDI. The article reports "autonomy support (ß = .19, p < .001), structure (ß = .22, p < .001), and involvement (ß = .22, p < .001)" as significant.

> "student SDI was significantly predicted by student perceptions of autonomy support (ß = .19, p < .001), structure (ß = .22, p < .001), and involvement (ß = .22, p < .001)."

### Taylor 2007 (2)

Taylor, I. M., & Ntoumanis, N. (2007). Teacher motivational strategies and student self-determination in physical education. Journal of Educational Psychology, 99, 747-760. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.747

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-mediator multilevel analysis (Krull & MacKinnon procedure, N = 787) tested indirect effects through each need. The article reports autonomy mediated autonomy support, competence mediated structure, and both mediated involvement, while "student perceptions of relatedness did not significantly predict student SDI."

> "autonomy support's relationship with student SDI was found to be mediated by student perceptions of autonomy. Also, the relationship between structure and student SDI was found to be mediated by student perceptions of competence."
