## Evidence

### Molabe Manoko 2026

Molabe Manoko, Rasesemola Richard, Matlala Sidwell, Matshaka Lerato. (2026). Virtual simulation as a catalyst for equitable and authentic interprofessional learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09773-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group participants contrasted pre-programme variability with uniform core scenarios across the main campus and regional centres, saying "every student goes through the same core scenarios". Recorded playback of team communication was also described as removing subjectivity from debriefing.

> "Before this virtual program, your IPE experience totally depended on which clinical site you were placed at. Now, whether you're at the main campus or a regional centre, every student goes through the same core scenarios"
