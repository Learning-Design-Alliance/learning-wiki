Evidence¶

Megan Kuhfeld 2020¶

Megan Kuhfeld, Beth Tarasawa, Angela Johnson, Erik Ruzek, Karyn Lewis. (2020). Learning during COVID-19: Initial findings on students' reading and math achievement and growth. NWEA Research brief. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/learning-during-covid-19-initial-findings-on-students-reading-and-math-achievement-and-growth/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Growth analysis of fall 2020 MAP Growth data from nearly 4.4 million students in grades 3-8, benchmarked against the prior typical year. The brief reports "lower growth in math across grades 3 to 8" and more students falling behind relative to prior standing; no effect size is printed.
