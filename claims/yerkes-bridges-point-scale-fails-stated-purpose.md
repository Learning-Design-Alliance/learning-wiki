`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in the article's introduction. Otis contrasts the Point Scale's avowed purpose, that it "brings out the full value of the test material without increasing the expenditure of time and energy," with his judgment that its scoring method fails entirely to accomplish that purpose. The article offers a reasoned critique rather than a data test.

> "In the opinion of the writer, however, the method used in scoring with the Point Scale fails entirely to accomplish the purpose sought."
