Evidence¶

Ortega 2020¶

Ortega, R., López, V., Carrasco, X., Escobar, M. J., García, A. M., Parra, M. A. & Aboitiz, F. (2020). Neurocognitive mechanisms underlying working memory encoding and retrieval in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Scientific Reports 10:7771. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-64678-x

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA of reaction times in the same 18-vs-18 adolescent sample: "the ADHD group showed slower responses than the non-ADHD group in all conditions", F(1,34) = 13.035, p = 0.00097, η2 = 0.28.

"Regarding reaction times (RTs), the ADHD group showed slower responses than the non-ADHD group in all conditions (F(1,34) = 13.035, p = 0.00097, η2 = 0.28)."

Ortega 2020 (2)¶

Ortega, R., López, V., Carrasco, X., Escobar, M. J., García, A. M., Parra, M. A. & Aboitiz, F. (2020). Neurocognitive mechanisms underlying working memory encoding and retrieval in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Scientific Reports 10:7771. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-64678-x

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Same RT ANOVA: a main effect of condition, with "The Color-shape condition showed slower RTs than the Only-Shape condition", F(1,34) = 6.2918, p = 0.01706, η2 = 0.16; no Group x Condition interaction was reported.

"The Color-shape condition showed slower RTs than the Only-Shape condition (F(1,34) = 6.2918, p = 0.01706, η2 = 0.16). No other effects were observed."

Related Claims¶

  • Visual WM binding of color and shape is intact in ADHD: no group differences behaviorally or electrophysiologically, with Bayesian support for null Group x Condition interactions
  • Adults with dyscalculia perform the number comparison task as accurately as controls but respond significantly more slowly
  • Having three or more comorbid psychiatric conditions increases risk for slow processing speed, but a single comorbid condition does not
  • Retrieval P3 discriminates Same from Different trials only in non-ADHD adolescents, indicating a failure of post-selection prioritization in ADHD
  • Adolescents with ADHD show poorer accuracy than non-ADHD peers on all conditions of a visual short-term memory task, with the largest drop on trials requiring WM updating — related
  • Go-trial response times are slower during the stop-signal task than the no-tone task, with no diagnostic group difference — related
  • Typical readers respond faster than dyslexic readers overall, and responses slow as presentation rates increase, without a group-by-rate interaction for reaction time — related
  • Encoding P3 amplitude is reduced in ADHD adolescents during visual WM encoding, in both early and late time windows — related
  • Children with cochlear implants are generally slower than hearing peers in multiplication and subtraction but show comparable error profiles — related
  • Fourth graders with poor arithmetic-fact abilities (PoorAF) and matched controls did not differ on a perceptual shape-matching speed task — related
  • The dual-task condition is more difficult than the single-task condition, with a condition-by-modality interaction in reaction time — related
