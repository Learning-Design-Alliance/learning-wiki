### Abdelghani 2026

Abdelghani, R., Kaiser, P., Murayama, K. (2026). Changes in Help-Seeking Strategies Predict unaided Performance during AI-based Mathematical Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28472 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.28472](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.28472)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Descriptive analysis of the conversational corpus from 112 Grade-9 students chatting with Mistral Large during 40-minute open-ended mathematical-modelling practice; turns were coded with a validated turn-level codebook. The authors report "82% were classified asREQUEST" with plan, monitor, and evaluate rare.

> "Of the 880 substantive student turns, 82% were classified asREQUEST, compared with 10%PLAN, 6%MONITOR, and 2%EVALUATE."
