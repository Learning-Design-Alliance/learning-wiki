Evidence¶

Debra Kamps 2015¶

Debra Kamps, Howard Wills, Harriett Dawson-Bannister, Linda Heitzman-Powell, Esther Kottwitz, Blake Hansen, and Kandace Fleming (2015). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams "CW-FIT" Efficacy Trial Outcomes. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300714565244

q4 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized trial across 17 elementary schools and 4 years with 86 CW-FIT and 73 comparison classrooms. GLMM analysis of 30-s momentary time sample observations showed on-task rose from "51.95% in baseline to 82.99%".

"Class-wide on-task behavior during CW-FIT increased from 51.95% in baseline to 82.99%; this was a significant increase F(1,1765) = 1,600.12, p < .0001 (see Table 2)."

Debra Kamps 2015 (2)¶

Debra Kamps, Howard Wills, Harriett Dawson-Bannister, Linda Heitzman-Powell, Esther Kottwitz, Blake Hansen, and Kandace Fleming (2015). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams "CW-FIT" Efficacy Trial Outcomes. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300714565244

q4 · i? · causal · r2

GLMM analysis with teacher as unit of analysis and repeated observations modeled via SAS PROC MIXED. The significant "phase by condition interaction" confirms the treatment effect exceeded comparison-class change.

"Data analysis indicated there is a signifi- cant phase by condition interaction F(1, 1804) = 406.77, p < .0001."
