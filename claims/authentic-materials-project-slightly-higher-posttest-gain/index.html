Evidence¶

Castillo Losada 2017¶

Castillo Losada, C. A., Insuasty, E. A., & Jaime Osorio, M. F. (2017). The impact of authentic materials and tasks on students’ communicative competence at a Colombian language school. PROFILE Issues in Teachers’ Professional Development, 19(1), 89-104. http://dx.doi.org/10.15446/profile.v19n1.56763

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two A2 courses (experimental n = 14, control n = 9) taking the KET pre- and post-test. The article reports the experimental group "achieved an average score of 3.9" versus 3.7 for controls; no statistical test or effect size is printed.

"Whereas the experimental group achieved an average score of 3.9, the control group’s final average score was 3.7. As can be seen, the progress of the experimental group can be rated at 0.4 and the control group at 0.3."
