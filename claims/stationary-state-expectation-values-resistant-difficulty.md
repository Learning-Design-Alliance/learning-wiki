## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On CQS 4.2, which asked about expectation values in a stationary state, only 54% of students selected the correct answer on the first attempt and 57% on the second, despite an instructor hint. [→ Peter Hu 2022](#peter-hu-2022)

## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2022

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with time-development of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in virtual and in-person classes. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ac49f4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ac49f4)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-level polling data from the 2020 virtual CQS administration (class of N = 29) on CQS 4.2, where most incorrect responses omitted Sx, whose operator does not commute with the Hamiltonian. No effect size is printed for this item.

> "The instructor gave students two chances to answer this question, but the distribution of selected answers remained very similar across both chances. 54% of students selected the correct answer on the first attempt, and 57% did so on the second."
