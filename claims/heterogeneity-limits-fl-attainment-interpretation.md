# Significant between-study heterogeneity limits interpretation of foreign language attainment differences in poor readers/spellers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Significant heterogeneity (Q p < 0.05) made six of ten SMDs and eight of ten CVRs uninterpretable, including word reading, spelling, and orthographic knowledge. [→ von Hagen 2021](#von-hagen-2021)

## Evidence

### von Hagen 2021

von Hagen, A., Kohnen, S., & Stadie, N. (2021). Foreign Language Attainment of Children/Adolescents with Poor Literacy Skills: a Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09566-6

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Across the 10 random-effects meta-analyses, heterogeneity was significant for word reading (Q = 155.30, I² = 92.27%), spelling, orthographic knowledge, nonword reading, spoken word production, and letter knowledge SMDs, and for most CVRs, so those overall effects were not interpreted.

> "Meta-analytic results revealed overall SMDs and CVRs for 10 foreign language outcome measures. However, only four out of 10 overall SMDs could be interpreted, due to significant heterogeneity between study effects (Q statistic p < 0.05)."
