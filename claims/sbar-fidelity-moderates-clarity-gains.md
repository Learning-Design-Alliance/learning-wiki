Yun Jungmi, Lee Yun Ji, Kang Kyoungrim, Park Jongmin. (2023). Effectiveness of SBAR-based simulation programs for nursing students: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04495-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review discussion citing prior studies of SBAR fidelity. The review reports "classroom-based studies achieved levels of fidelity to SBAR ranging from 71–87%" with moderate to considerable clarity improvements, while clinical-setting studies showed "no or only moderate improvements in clarity, with fidelity ranging from 53–83%". The figures come from cited prior work (including Lo et al. 2021's review of SBAR fidelity), not from the review's own analysis; the two fidelity ranges overlap, and the review says only that the smaller clinical gains "suggest the need to establish higher fidelity". It ran no moderation analysis, so it does not show that fidelity moderates or causes the difference.
