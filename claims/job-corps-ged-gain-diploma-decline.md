# Job Corps' completion effect came entirely from GED receipt, with a small statistically significant negative effect on earning a high school diploma

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Job Corps significantly increased the likelihood of earning a GED certificate (effect size 0.41, improvement index +16). [→ Job Corps 2008](#job-corps-2008)
`q3 i?` Job Corps had a small, statistically significant negative effect on the likelihood of earning a high school diploma (5.3% vs 7.5%; effect size –0.22, improvement index –9). [→ Job Corps 2008 (2)](#job-corps-2008-2)

## Evidence

### Job Corps 2008

Job Corps. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Job Corps. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://jobcorps.dol.gov/about.htm

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial, Appendix A4.2: GED receipt among those without a credential at entry was 41.6% for Job Corps versus 26.6% for control, mean difference 15.0, Cox effect size 0.41, statistically significant, improvement index +16 (n = 8,597).

> "Job Corps' effect on completion came entirely from its positive and statistically significant effect on the likelihood of receiving a GED certificate."

### Job Corps 2008 (2)

Job Corps. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Job Corps. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://jobcorps.dol.gov/about.htm

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial, Appendix A4.2: diploma receipt was 5.3% for Job Corps versus 7.5% for control, mean difference –2.2, effect size –0.22, statistically significant, improvement index –9. The report notes 5.3% and 7.5% are both small percentages.

> "Job Corps was found to have a small, but statistically significant, negative effect on the likelihood of earning a high school diploma."
