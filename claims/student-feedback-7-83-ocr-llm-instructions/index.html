Patel 2025¶

Patel, K., Lin, Y.-Z., Raul, G., Shih, B. P.-J., Redondo, M. W., Latibari, B. S., Pacheco, J., Salehi, S., & Satam, P. (2025). Integrating Generative AI into Cybersecurity Education: A Study of OCR and Multimodal LLM-assisted Instruction. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02998

q2 · i? · design · r2

Live deployment of the OCR-to-LLM instructional assistant in a university cybersecurity course; 42 feedback ratings were collected from undergraduate and graduate students on a 1-10 scale. The article reports the average rating was "7.83, indicating a generally positive reception"; no effect size or comparison test is printed.

"The average rating across all submissions was 7.83, indicating a generally positive reception of the generative AI agent's simplified instructions."
