## Evidence

### Schiffman J 2018

Schiffman J, Laski EV. (2018). Linear-spatial materials improve young children's addition strategies and accuracy, irregular arrays don't. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208832

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analysis using the SPSS macro PROCESS with 10,000 bootstrap samples in the randomized experiment, with pretest absolute error as covariate. The indirect path "by way of frequency of using an advanced strategy was significant (point estimate = -.46" with a 95% CI excluding zero; the direct effect was reduced relative to the uncontrolled model.

> "The product of the coefficients (ab) for the indirect path from training condition to absolute error by way of frequency of using an advanced strategy was significant (point estimate = -.46; 95% bias-corrected; confidence interval (CI): [-1.47 to -.05]"
