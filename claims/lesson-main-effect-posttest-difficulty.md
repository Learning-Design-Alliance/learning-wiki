Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Sanjit Kakarla, Jionghao Lin, Shambhavi Bhushan, Boyuan Guo, Erin Gatz, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. 2025. Does Multiple Choice Have a Future in the Age of Generative AI? A Posttest-only RCT. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on posttest accuracy across the six scenario-based lessons reports a significant lesson main effect, F(5, 717) = 10.18, p < .001; no effect size is printed. Human-graded open-response correctness ranged from 45% to 89% on predict and 51% to 68% on explain questions.

> "a significant main effect of the lesson suggested substantial accuracy differences by lesson, which means that some lessons were harder than others, on average, F(5, 717) = 10.18, p < .001."
