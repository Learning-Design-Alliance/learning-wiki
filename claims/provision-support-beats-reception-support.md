# Feedback provision support has a positive medium-sized effect (g = 0.51) whereas feedback reception support shows no significant effect

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Support presented during the feedback provision phase has a significantly positive, medium-sized effect compared to peer-feedback without instructional support (g = 0.51). [→ Hornstein 2025](#hornstein-2025)
`q4 i?` Support presented during the feedback reception phase shows no significant effect (g = 0.19, p = 0.50), though this result is based on very few studies and is not interpretable. [→ Hornstein 2025 (2)](#hornstein-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Hornstein 2025

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of 29 studies (k = 178 effect sizes) for feedback provision support: g = 0.51, 95% CI [0.24, 0.78], p < 0.001 (Table 3).

> "Regarding RQ3a, we found a significantly positive, medium-sized effect of feedback provision support compared to peer-feedback without instructional support."

### Hornstein 2025 (2)

Hornstein, J., Keller, M. V., Greisel, M., Dresel, M., & Kollar, I. (2025). Enhancing the Peer-Feedback Process Through Instructional Support: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10017-3

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of only 3 studies (k = 14 effect sizes) for feedback reception support: g = 0.19, 95% CI [-0.83, 1.22], p = 0.50; degrees of freedom below 4 make the result non-interpretable per the authors.

> "For feedback reception support, we found no significant effect. Yet, as only very few studies exist that investigated the effects of instructional support for feedback reception (as indicated by the low degrees of freedom), this result might not be robust once the evidence basis increases"
