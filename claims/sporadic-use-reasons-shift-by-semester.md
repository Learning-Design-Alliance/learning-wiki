## Evidence

### Müller Lina R. 2019

Müller Lina R., Tipold Andrea, Ehlers Jan P., Schaper Elisabeth. (2019). TiHoVideos: veterinary students' utilization of instructional videos on clinical skills. BMC Veterinary Research 15, 326. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-019-2079-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey analysis of viewing frequency and motives (frequency question answered by n = 641). The article reports students "reported viewing the videos mainly sporadically" at all levels, and that from the sixth semester the main reason is "to prepare for exams", followed by "out of interest" and "to prepare for the CSL".

> "all semester levels reported viewing the videos mainly sporadically: 52.7% in the second, 51.1% in the fourth, 74.1% in the sixth, 73.1% in the eighth, 77.8% in the tenth and 82.6% above the tenth semester."
