The review reports a dry lab cohort study of medical students by Angell et al., in which the task involved incising a spiral structure; "intensive training in basic laparoscopic skills reduced the time taken" robotically and reduced errors. No effect sizes are printed.

> "The study showed that in a laparoscopic and robotic naive cohort, intensive training in basic laparoscopic skills reduced the time taken to perform the task robotically as well as reduced the number of errors."
