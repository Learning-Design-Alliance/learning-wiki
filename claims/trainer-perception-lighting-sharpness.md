# Trainee ratings of lighting favor the SBT while image sharpness favors the TBT, with no differences in overall satisfaction

> **Currency** · newest study 2016 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Lighting conditions were rated better in the SBT group (median 9 vs 7, P = 0.015) and image sharpness better in the TBT group (median 8.5 vs 6.5, P = 0.005), with no significant differences in visibility, posture comfort, effectiveness, or overall satisfaction. [→ Montanari Eliana MD 2016](#montanari-eliana-md-2016)

## Evidence

### Montanari Eliana MD 2016

Montanari Eliana MD, Schwameis Richard MD, Louridas Marisa MD, Göbl Christian MD, PhD, MSc, Kuessel Lorenz MD, Polterauer Stephan MD, Husslein Heinrich MD, PLLM, Torquati. Alfonso. (2016). Training on an inexpensive tablet-based device is equally effective as on a standard laparoscopic box trainer: A randomized controlled trial. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000004826

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire ratings on a 10-point visual analog scale after the training period; the article reports "The lighting conditions were perceived to be better in the SBT group than in the TBT group" and sharpness better in the TBT.

> "The lighting conditions were perceived to be better in the SBT group than in the TBT group (SBT group: median 9, IQR 8–9.75; TBT group: median 7, IQR 6–8.75; P = 0.015, Mann–Whitney U test), whereas the image sharpness was rated better in the TBT than in the SBT group (SBT group: median 6.5, IQR 4.5–7; TBT group: median 8.5, IQR 8–9; P = 0.005, Mann–Whitney U test)."
