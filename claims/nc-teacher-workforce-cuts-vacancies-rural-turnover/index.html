Findings from the educator supply, demand, and quality study prepared for the project, reported in the Increased Challenges section: a 5% reduction in employed teachers from 2009 to 2018 against a 2% enrollment increase. No effect size is printed.

Analysis of district-level teacher turnover data (Exhibit 5) and state vacancy reports in the Increased Challenges section: rural district losses above 20% in a single year and 1,621 unfilled vacancies in 2017-18. No effect size is printed.

"with some of the rural districts losing more than 20% of their teachers in a single year, as shown in Exhibit 5. The state reported 1,621 teacher vacancies — a consequence of declining supply and high turnover — that could not be filled by qualified teachers during 2017–18"
