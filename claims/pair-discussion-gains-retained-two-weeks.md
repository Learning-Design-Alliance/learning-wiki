## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2002

Chandralekha Singh. (2002). Effectiveness of group interaction on conceptual standardized test performance. [doi:10.1119/perc.2002.pr.017](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2002.pr.017)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Unannounced retest two weeks after the treatments: all students individually retook the same CSEM. The article reports the 74% average, the 0.42 gain, and that 81% of individual responses matched group responses; corresponding GII numbers were virtually the same.

> "In the IGI treatment, the average score for the second individual testing was 74%, a gain of 0 .42 compared to the initial individual score of 55%. A detailed comparison of the group vs. the second individual test scores shows that 81% of the overall indi- vidual responses chosen by the members of a particular group were the same as the group responses."
