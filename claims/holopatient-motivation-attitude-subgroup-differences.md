## Evidence

### Kang Yun 2025

Kang Yun, Lee Insook. (2025). HoloPatient-based simulation education to improve nursing students’ learning motivation and attitude: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07987-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent t-tests and one-way ANOVA on the post-test survey (n = 100) comparing motivation and attitude across general characteristics; only test statistics printed, no effect sizes.

> "There were significant differences in motivation towards HoloPatient-based simulation learning by age (F = 7.11, p =.001), major satisfaction (t=−6.81, p <.001), and grade point average (GPA) (F = 5.12, p =.017)"

### Kang Yun 2025 (2)

Kang Yun, Lee Insook. (2025). HoloPatient-based simulation education to improve nursing students’ learning motivation and attitude: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07987-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same survey sample; the gender contrast names male versus female participants with printed means. The article cautions the skewed gender distribution (16 males, 84 females) may explain the difference rather than true gender effects.

> "There were significant differences in attitudes towards HoloPatient-based simulation learning by gender (t = 2.28, p =.035), age (F = 12.94, p <.001), and major satisfaction (t=−4.66, p <.001). Male participants demonstrated more positive learning attitudes (M = 4.14, SD = 0.46) than female participants (M = 3.77, SD = 0.62)"
