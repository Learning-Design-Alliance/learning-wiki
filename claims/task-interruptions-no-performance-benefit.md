de Sousa Sónia Ferreira, Marques Maria do Céu Mendes Pinto, da Silva Amaral Guida Maria Marques. (2026). Factors Influencing the Maintenance of Advanced Life Support Competencies Among Critical Care Professionals: A Scoping Review. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70545

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports this null result from a randomised multi-centre controlled simulation study by Truchot et al., where interruptions included "repetitive phone calls and interruptions from relatives or other clinicians". No statistically significant between-group differences were observed; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not established.

> "Introducing task interruptions to increase realism in ALS simulation did not improve technical or non‐technical performance compared with standard simulation. In Truchot et al. [29], these interruptions included repetitive phone calls and interruptions from relatives or other clinicians, and no between‐group differences were observed, including at 3‐month retention."
