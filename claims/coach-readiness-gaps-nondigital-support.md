`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 148 edtech coaches (Figure 11): "more than 70 percent" were prepared for technology tool support, while more than one-third were unprepared for social-emotional support, home-environment guidance, connectivity solutions, digital deep coaching, remote schedules, and equitable lessons.

> "while more than 70 percent of respondents were moderately or very prepared to help teachers select and use appropriate technology tools and strategies for diferent purposes, on average more than one-third of respondents reported that at the onset of school closures, they were "slightly or not prepared""
