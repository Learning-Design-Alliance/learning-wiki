A matched comparison study in Arizona during 2022-2023 examined over 400 K-1 students, comparing PCP users with matched peers on the aimswebPlus assessment. The study "found significantly more growth on the aimswebPlus assessment among PCP users compared to peers (effect size = +0.29)", particularly in early literacy and foundational skills.
