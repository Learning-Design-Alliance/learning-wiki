## Subclaims

## Evidence

### P. Fraundorf 1996

P. Fraundorf. (1996). A one-map two-clock approach to teaching relativity in introductory physics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/9611011](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/9611011)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analytical argument in Section II.B: time dilation follows directly from the two-clock definitions, which the paper calls an "item A" benefit allowing relativity concepts at an earlier stage. The paper states "A practical awareness of the non-global nature of time thus does not require readiness for the abstraction of Lorentz transforms."

> "From the very deﬁnition of γ as a “traveler’s speed of map-time”, and the velocity relations which show that γ ≥ 1, it is easy for a student to see that the traveler’s clock will always run slower than map time."
