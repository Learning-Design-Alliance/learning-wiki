## Subclaims
`q3 i?` PGY-3 EM residents scored higher than novices on pericardiocentesis global rating (5 (5) vs 3 (3–4), p = 0.0002) and required fewer puncture attempts (1 (1) vs 2 (1–2), p = 0.002), while puncture time was comparable (p = 0.616). [→ Huang Sih-Shiang 2024](#huang-sih-shiang-2024)

## Evidence

### Huang Sih-Shiang 2024

Huang Sih-Shiang, Lin Chih-Hsien, Lin Shao-Yung, Huang Chien-Tai, Lien Wan-Ching. (2024). How to enhance the novices' learning in ultrasound-guided procedures utilizing handmade phantoms? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06458-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Concurrent comparison on the high-fidelity phantom in the same prospective study (novices n = 36, PGY-3 n = 12). Table 3 prints global rating p = 0.0002, puncture attempts p = 0.002, visualization p = 0.038, needle steadiness p = 0.016; puncture time did not differ.

> "The PGY-3 EM residents demonstrated better performance in visualizing the heart, pericardium, and effusion, showed greater needle steadiness during aspiration, received higher global rating scores, and required fewer puncture attempts (Table 3)."
