## Evidence

### Ferreira 2024

Ferreira, A., Lima, D. A., Oliveira, W., Bittencourt, I. I. B., Dermeval, D., Reimers, F., Isotani, S. (2024). Exploring Brazilian Teachers’ Perceptions and a priori Needs to Design Smart Classrooms. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00410-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of responses from 410 Brazilian teachers; School Bureaucracy code. One participant wrote, "I would like student attendance to go to the academic system by magic". No effect size is reported.

> "the bureaucracy mentioned comprises not only those performed in the classroom, such as ﬁlling out class diaries, recording school attendance, and registering student grades, amongst others, but the whole set of control activities."
