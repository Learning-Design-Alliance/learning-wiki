## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer 2021

Martin O. Archer, Natt Day, and Sarah Barnes. (2021). Demonstrating change from a drop-in space soundscape exhibit by using graffiti walls both before and after. [doi:10.5194/gc-4-57-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/gc-4-57-2021)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational tally at the space soundscape exhibit at the Science Museum, experienced by 1,003 people. The pre- and post-soundscape graffiti walls captured "rates of 53± 2% and 44± 2%", which the authors attribute to integrating the walls into the activity itself.

> "There were 535 and 446 responses (predominantly textual) on the pre- and post-soundscape grafﬁti walls respectively, rates of 53± 2% and 44± 2%. This is some 3–10 times greater than reported for typical grafﬁti walls (Public Engagement with Research team, 2019) likely due to their integration into the overall activity here."
