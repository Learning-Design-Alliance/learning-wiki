## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The logic model proposes direct effects via the transferred teacher, indirect effects via collaboration and resource allocation within the teaching team, and retention effects via phased incentive payments. [→ Glazerman 2012](#glazerman-2012)

## Evidence

### Glazerman 2012

Glazerman, S., Protik, A., Teh, B., Bruch, J., & Seftor, N. (2012). Moving Teachers: Implementation of Transfer Incentives in Seven Districts (NCEE 2012-4051). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical logic model (Figure I.1) presented as authors' hypothesis, not a tested result. It proposes that TTI improves "the performance of the teaching team" through direct, collaboration, and resource-allocation pathways, plus retention effects from installment payments.

> "The principle way that TTI would have an impact on final outcomes such as student achievement is through intermediate outcomes, specifically, by improving the performance of the teaching team that is targeted by the intervention"
