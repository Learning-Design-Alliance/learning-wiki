Building a Bridge between the Two Schools: Realizing a Practical Path to Include Literacy-based Skills within the STEM Curricula. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.17447](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.17447)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Experience report from computer systems courses at a technical and research university in the Netherlands, comparing editions with and without social gamification. The comparison is reported descriptively without statistics.

> "Using a social gamification approach, we report that more students completed the course and engaged with advanced topics than in previous editions without gamification."

### Jorge Torres Gómez 2026 (2)

Jorge Torres Gómez, Erika Gericke, Anton Rassõlkin, Mikołaj Leszczuk, Alexandru Iosup, Marcin Niemiec, Carmen Peláez-Moreno. (2026). Building a Bridge between the Two Schools: Realizing a Practical Path to Include Literacy-based Skills within the STEM Curricula. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.17447](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.17447)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of new teaching methods with the gamification technique at AGH University across six categories (adequacy, effectiveness, usefulness, efficiency, permanence, impact); in each case students indicated preference for the new methods. The authors confirmed increased interest and participation with high attendance rates.

> "an evaluation was carried out on a group of 53 students, of whom 18 responded to the surveys"
