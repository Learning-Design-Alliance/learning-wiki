## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Emotional tone in LLM interview responses is influenced by priming, with positive scores under the Math Lover condition and reversal under the Math Hater condition for some models. [→ Esposito 2026](#esposito-2026)

## Evidence

### Esposito 2026

Esposito, N., Tricarico, A., Porzio, L., Aghazadeh Ardebili, A., & Stella, M. (2026). Math Education Digital Shadows for Investigating Learning with GenAI: Mathematics Performance, Anxiety, and Confidence in LLMs. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27618 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.27618](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2604.27618)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sentiment analysis of Task 1 interview responses using VADER compound scores, averaged over Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q7 and pooled by model within priming groups. The article reports that "Scores are always positive under the “Math Lover” condition" while Grok 4.1 Fast and Magistral Small receive negative scores under the Math Hater condition.

> "Fig. 2b shows that emotional tone is influenced by priming. Scores are always positive under the “Math Lover” condition. Mistral Small 4 and Mistral Small 3.2 achieve the highest scores, while Granite 4 Tiny achieves the lowest."
