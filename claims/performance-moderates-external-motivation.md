`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory continuous-moderator analysis correlating performance (errors per assessment) with the four SIMS constructs under each design at 90% bootstrap CIs (Table 6). Performance was the single continuous moderator found; the correlations printed are CIs only, with no p-values or standardized coefficients.

> "Results indicate that higher performance was associated with higher external motivation for OSFA users [0.4;0.7], but not for personalized users [-0.24;0.16], and that such correlations did not overlap."
