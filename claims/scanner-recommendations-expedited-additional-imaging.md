> "The residents recommended 12 additional studies while at the scanner for 11 of 62 patients (18%). Of these additional studies, seven (58%) were CT angiograms of the head and neck, four (33%) were CT cystograms, and one (8%) was for CT angiogram of the lower extremities. All recommended studies were performed immediately at the scanner."
