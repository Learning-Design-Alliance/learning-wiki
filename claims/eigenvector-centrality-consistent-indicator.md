## Evidence

### Saqr and López-Pernas 2022

Mohammed Saqr, Sonsoles López-Pernas. (2022). The Curious Case of Centrality Measures: A Large-Scale Empirical Investigation. Journal of Learning Analytics. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7415

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 69 course offerings found eigenvector centrality at "r=0.41 [CI 0.36:0.45]" with I²=54%. The authors state "the eigenvector centrality is a consistent indicator of student success."

> "The eigenvector centrality had a combined correlation coefficient slightly higher than that of betweenness centrality (r=0.41 [CI 0.36:0.45]), with a comparable predictive interval [0.07;0.54] and a slightly higher heterogeneity (I²=54%)."
