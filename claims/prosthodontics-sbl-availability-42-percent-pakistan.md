`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 90 prosthodontics postgraduate trainees across Pakistan, analysed with SPSS 23.0. In the Results, "Thirty-eight participants (42.2% of the population) reported the availability of simulation-based learning in their training institutes", and coverage was "almost half the respondents from each province except Khyber Pakhtunkhwa", where only 8% had simulation learning.
