## Evidence

### Tutal Özgür 2026

Tutal Özgür. (2026). Concretizing the abstract: embodied learning as a pedagogical strategy for teaching measurement and evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10321-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Prespecified baseline-adjusted one-way ANCOVA with Bonferroni correction (α=0.017) on the measurement and evaluation self-efficacy perception scale (α=0.94 in this sample), yielding ηp² = 0.251. The embodied learning group's post-survey mean was 81.80 (SD=8.51) versus 71.63 (SD=9.58) for the comparison group.

> "For self-efficacy perceptions toward measurement and evaluation, a significant group effect was also found, F (1, 28) = 9.372, p = 0.005, ηp2 = 0.251, revealing that the treatment group outperformed the comparison group."
