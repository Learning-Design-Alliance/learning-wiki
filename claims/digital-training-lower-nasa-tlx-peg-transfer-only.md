## Evidence

### Geissler Mark Enrik 2024

Geissler Mark Enrik, Bereuter Jean-Paul, Geissler Rona Berit, Bökkerink Guus Mattheus Johannes, Egen Luisa, Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Haney Caelan. (2024). Comparison of distance versus in-person laparoscopy training using a low-cost laparoscopy simulator—a randomized controlled multi-center trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11069-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

NASA-TLX cognitive workload questionnaire completed after each exercise in the randomized trial's final assessment (in-person n=21, digital n=19). The workload advantage for digital training was task-specific, limited to Peg transfer (p = 0.0395); the remaining three tasks were non-significant.

> "While participants of the in-person group showed significantly higher NASA-TLX scores compared to the digital group during the Peg transfer task (control: 55.3 vs. intervention: 47, p value = 0.0395), no significant differences between both groups were observed during the other three tasks"
