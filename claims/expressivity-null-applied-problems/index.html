Hernández 2018¶

Hernández, M. M., Eisenberg, N., Valiente, C., Thompson, M. S., Spinrad, T. L., Grimm, K. J., VanSchyndel, S. K., Berger, R. H., Silva, K. M., Pina, A. A., Southworth, J., & Gal, D. E. (2018). Supplementary analysis reported in the limitations section: a standardized mathematics measure (applied problems) was examined and neither levels nor trajectories of negative expressivity significantly predicted G1 scores. No test statistics were printed.

"In supplementary analyses, we examined a standardized mathematics measure (i.e., applied problems) and the results were not significant; that is, negative emotional expressivity levels and trajectories both did not significantly predict applied problem scores in G1"
