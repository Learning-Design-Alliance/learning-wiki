## Evidence

### Banerjee Snigdha 2015

Banerjee Snigdha, Frey Hans-Peter, Molholm Sophie, Foxe John J. (2015). Interests shape how adolescents pay attention: The interaction of motivation and top-down attentional processes in biasing sensory activations to anticipated events. The European Journal of Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.12810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ERP results from the cued spatial attention task: ADAN cue × ROI interaction F(1,23) = 10.87, p = .003; LDAP F(1,23) = 12.07, p = .002; BRN F(1,23) = 21.93, p = .000, each driven by greater amplitude contralateral to the attended hemifield.

> "We observed top-down attentional modulation of all of the aforementioned components in adolescents contralateral to the attended location, in a manner that resembled what is classically observed in adults, pointing to the fact that these anticipatory spatial attentional mechanisms are already quite mature by mid-adolescence."
