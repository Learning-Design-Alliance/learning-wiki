## Evidence

### Yasuda Yukinori 2026

Yasuda Yukinori, Takehara Shoichiro, Inoue Soichiro. (2026). Quantitative analysis of gaze and body movement differences by proficiency in direct and video laryngoscope intubation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44432-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mannequin-based eye-tracking experiment with six experts and eight novices (one expert excluded for missing gaze data) using a direct laryngoscope; experts gazed 0.465 ± 0.122 m versus novices 0.325 ± 0.052 m, and the t-test showed "a significant difference between the two groups".

> "The result showed a significant difference between the two groups [t (12) = 2.690, p = 0.020]."
