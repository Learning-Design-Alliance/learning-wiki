## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A large UK cluster-RCT of school gardening did not show a statistically significant adjusted overall effect on fruit and vegetable intake (-40 g; 95% CI -88, 1; p = 0.06), but a three-level increase in implementation was associated with a mean increase of +81 g/day (95% CI 0, 163; p = 0.05) irrespective of group allocation. `q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

UK cluster-RCT (Christian et al., mean age 8.1 years, 18-month follow-up) with no significant adjusted overall intervention effect (-40 g; 95% CI -88, 1; p = 0.06); process measures linked a three-level implementation increase to +81 g/day intake. Implementation dose is observational within the trial, not a tested causal contrast.

> "process measures indicated that, irrespective of group allocation, a three-level increase in school gardening implementation was associated with a mean increase of +81 g/day in fruit and vegetable intake (95% CI 0, 163; p = 0.05)"
