Evidence¶

Kuhfeld 2020¶

Kuhfeld, Megan, and James Soland. (2020). Estimating Student Growth on Psychological and Social-emotional Constructs: A Comparison of Multiple Scoring Approaches. EdWorkingPaper No. 20-193. https://www.edworkingpapers.com/ai20-193

q2 · i? · design · r2

Table 4 of Simulation Study 1 reports correlations of estimated scores with true scores within each timepoint. The article attributes the timepoint-three diminution to ceiling effects: with a slope of .5, simulees "were already at the high end of the observed response scale in Time 1."

"This diminution mainly occurred with easy items, and was especially pronounced with a true slope of .5 and only four items. The relatively poor recovery of true scores at timepoint three under these conditions may have occurred due to ceiling effects."
