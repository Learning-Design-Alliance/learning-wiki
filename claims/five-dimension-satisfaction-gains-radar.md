### Ba Ruikai 2026

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Radar-plot analysis aggregating the 19 items into five pedagogical dimensions for both cohorts and the 2023 subgroups. Clinical Competence Acquisition gained Δ +0.58, Self-Regulated Learning Support Δ +0.49, Course Content & Structure Δ +0.45, and Teacher–Student Interaction Δ +0.23 (p = 0.012).

> "overall mean Likert score increased from 4.25 ± 0.31 in the 2022 traditional cohort to 4.78 ± 0.22 in the 2023 flipped cohort (Δ + 0.53, p < 0.001); the greatest gain was in Instructional Implementation (4.19 → 4.81, Δ + 0.62, p < 0.001)"
