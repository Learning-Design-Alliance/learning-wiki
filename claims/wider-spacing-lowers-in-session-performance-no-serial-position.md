## Evidence

### Philip I. Pavlik Jr. 2005

Philip I. Pavlik Jr., John R. Anderson. (2005). Practice and Forgetting Effects on Vocabulary Memory: An Activation-Based Model of the Spacing Effect. Cognitive Science 29. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog0000_14

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the first four S1 trials (practice condition × S1 spacing × retention group) in the authors' experiment; the article also reports no significant differences in first-two-trial means across practice conditions and no serial-position effects.

> "performance improved across the first four trials of S1, F(3, 114) = 412, p < .001, and that there was significantly lower performance with wider spacing, F(2, 76) = 240, p < .001."
