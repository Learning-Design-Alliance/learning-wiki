`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison within the 63-resident test-retest sample using non-parametric tests. The article reports medians of 2.0 (2.0-3.0) versus 2.0 (1.0-2.0) with "p=0.03"; no effect size is printed.

> "Regarding the year of training, residents with ≥3 years of training (n=22) performed better than those in the first 2 years of training (n=41) in the domain "Opioid Side Effects" (2.0 [2.0-3.0] versus 2.0 [1.0-2.0]; p=0.03)."
