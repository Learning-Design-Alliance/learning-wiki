## Evidence

### Jurkovič 2010

Jurkovič, V. (2010). Effect of Explicit Language Learning Strategy Instruction on Language-test and Self-assessment Scores. English Language Teaching, 3(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v3n1p16

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Sub-group comparisons (Tables 5–8) using independent samples t tests after merging CEFR levels into A1/A2, B1, and B2/C1 groups; all reported t values were non-significant (e.g., A1/A2 test difference t = 0.41, p = 0.69).

> "The results have not revealed any statistically significant differences between the treatment and contrast groups if students were divided into sub-groups based on their initial language competence as measured by both instruments"
