# Introducing a mid-term check on day 8 of online ligation practice did not significantly increase examination scores

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Welch's t tests showed no significant differences in scores before versus after the mid-term check for either instructor or the combined score (p = 0.178, 0.167, 0.113), though the lowest score rose from 9 to 10. [→ Okada Takuhisa 2022](#okada-takuhisa-2022)

## Evidence

### Okada Takuhisa 2022

Okada Takuhisa, Asao Takayuki, Inoue Hitoshi, Harimoto Norifumi, Tanaka Kazumi, Shiraishi Takuya, Sano Akihiko, Ogawa Hiroomi, Sohda Makoto, Shirabe Ken, Saeki Hiroshi. (2022). Retrospective study of online ligation student practice incorporating flipped learning into e-learning. Surgery Today. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00595-022-02490-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective comparison of examination scores before and after the mid-term check was introduced in April 2021, tested with Welch's t test at p < 0.05. None of the three comparisons reached significance; the article also notes "the lowest score was 10 after the introduction of the mid-term check".

> "The mean scores and significant differences (Welch’s t test) before and after the introduction of the mid-term check were as follows: Instructor 1: before check, 12.3 (9–15); after check, 12.8 (10–15), p = 0.178; Instructor 2: before check, 12.0 (9–15); after check, 12.3 (10–15), p = 0.167; Combined score: before check, 12.1 (9–15); after check, 12.5 (10.5–14.5), p = 0.113."
