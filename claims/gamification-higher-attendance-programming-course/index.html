Evidence¶

Fotaris P 2016¶

Fotaris P, Mastoras T, Leinfellner R and Rosunally Y. (2016). Climbing Up the Leaderboard: An Empirical Study of Applying Gamification Techniques to a Computer Programming Class. The Electronic Journal of e-Learning Volume 14 Issue 2, pp94-110. www.ejel.org. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1101229

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Administrative data comparison of attendance and late arrivals between the control class (N=54, first year) and the gamified experimental class (N=52, second year) over 12 weeks. The authors state both findings "suggest that gamification motivated EC students to be more punctual and attend classes more often".

"Average class attendance for CC was 65% ( ≈35 students), while EC had an average class attendance of 78% (≈42 students). Additionally, an average of 4 to 5 CC students and 1 to 2 EC students arrived to class late every week, respectively."
