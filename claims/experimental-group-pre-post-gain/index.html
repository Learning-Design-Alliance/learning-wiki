Evidence¶

Hamit Yokus 2015¶

Hamit Yokus, Tuba Yokus. (2015). Polyrhythmic Tapping: Examining the Effectiveness of the Strategy of Organizing Rhythmic Structures through Synthesis. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.1.1917

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group Wilcoxon signed-rank analysis for the experimental group (n = 9). Table 9 reports z = -2.66, p = .008 for the test total, with all sub-dimensions significant (p = .005-.007); the experimental group's mean rose from X = 45.00 to X = 85.67.

"The Wilcoxon test on the experimental group's pre-test and post-test results on the polyrhythmic tapping test reveals a significant difference ( p < .05). This confirms the zero hypothesis and refutes the alternative hypothesis."
