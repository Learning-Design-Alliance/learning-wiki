## Evidence

### Conway 2025

Conway, C. M., Jenkins, H. E., Milne, A. E., Singh, S. & Wilson, B. (2025). Addressing the theory crisis in statistical learning research. npj Science of Learning 10, 68. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00359-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The authors' narrative evaluation of age-effect studies, citing conflicting findings such as Raviv and Arnon's report of visual but not auditory improvements between 5- and 12-year-olds, versus a similar study finding improvements in both modalities. The article concludes the nature of "developmental changes across age is not a robust phenomenon".

> "In sum, the nature of developmental changes across age is not a robust phenomenon, further complicated by the apparent inﬂuence of modality- and stimulus-speci ﬁc effects on the development of statistical learning."
