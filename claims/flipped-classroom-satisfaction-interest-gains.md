## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Satisfaction with the teaching model (11.1%), course duration (14.3%) and learning interest (17.1%) were each significantly higher in the flipped group. [→ Wei Yaru 2025](#wei-yaru-2025)

## Evidence

### Wei Yaru 2025

Wei Yaru, Peng Zhengjun. (2025). Application of the flipped classroom model based on Bloom’s Taxonomy of Educational Objectives in endodontics education for undergraduate dental students. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.18843

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Five-point Likert questionnaire items comparing the two groups of 16 students; Table 2 shows interest 3.5 ± 0.5 vs 4.1 ± 0.6, teaching-mode satisfaction 3.6 ± 0.5 vs 4.0 ± 0.4, duration satisfaction 3.5 ± 0.5 vs 4.0 ± 0.6. No effect size printed.

> "Students’ satisfaction with the teaching model and class duration increased significantly, by 11.1% (t = 2.782, p = 0.009) and 14.3% (t = 2.449, p < 0.020), respectively. Students’ learning interest increased by 17.1% (t = 3.101, p = 0.004)."
