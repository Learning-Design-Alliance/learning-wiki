## Evidence

Olde Bekkink M, Donders ART, Kooloos JG, de Waal RMW, Ruiter DJ. (2015). Challenging students to formulate written questions: a randomized controlled trial to assess learning effects. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0336-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Gender comparison within each arm of the randomized controlled trial. Control-group females scored "7.1 versus 6.6" versus males (p = 0.016); in the intervention arm the gap narrowed to "7.2 versus 7.1" (p = 0.55). No effect size printed for these contrasts.

> "Female students’ subscore on tumour pathology in the control group was significantly higher compared with male students: 7.1 versus 6.6 (p = 0.016). In the intervention group, the difference between female and male students was much smaller: 7.2 versus 7.1 (p = 0.55)."
