# In stepwise regression, item type predicted 25% of eighth-grade item difficulty variance, rising to 28% with cognitive level added

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For eighth-grade items, type entered first (R2=.25) and cognitive level added 3% (R2=.28); the model was significant. [→ İlhan et al. 2020](#ilhan-et-al-2020)

## Evidence

### İlhan et al. 2020

Mustafa İlhan, Nagihan Boztunç Öztürk, Melek Gülşah Şahin. (2020). The Effect of the Item’s Type and Cognitive Level on Its Difficulty Index: The Sample of TIMSS 2015. Participatory Educational Research, 7(2), 47-59. http://dx.doi.org/10.17275/per.20.19.7.2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple regression on eighth-grade TIMSS 2015 item difficulty indices with type as a dummy variable; the full model was significant, F=42.45, p<.01, with R2=.28 as printed.

> "the variable of the item type took part and it could predict 25% of the variance regarding the item difficulty [R2=.25]. At the second step of the analysis, the variance that could be predicted increased to 28% as the variable of the cognitive level was included in the model [R2=.28]"
