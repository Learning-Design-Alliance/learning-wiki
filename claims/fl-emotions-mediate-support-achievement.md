## Evidence

### Chen Shengdong 2025

Chen, S., Xu, H., Jin, C., Yin, K., Wang, X., & Wang, T. (2025). Social support and english academic achievement: the mediating role of foreign language emotions and the moderating effect of academic self-concept. BMC Psychology, 13, 1286. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03617-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap moderated mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 7, 5,000 resamples) on the survey data. The study reports "statistically significant mediating effects" for all three emotions, with printed indirect effects ab = 1.412, 0.575, and 0.232.

> "Mediation analysis showed that foreign language enjoyment (ab = 1.412, 95% CI [0.880, 1.957]), foreign language boredom (ab = 0.575, 95% CI [0.120, 1.048]), and foreign language anxiety (ab = 0.232, 95% CI [0.041, 0.443]) all exhibited statistically significant mediating effects between social support and English academic achievement."
