### Kalyuga 2004

Slava Kalyuga and John Sweller. (2004). Measuring Knowledge to Optimize Cognitive Load Factors During Instruction. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.96.3.558

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 replicated Experiment 1 with one class of 20 Year 9 advanced mathematics students using coordinate geometry tasks structured as partially worked examples with increasing detail. The article reports the correlation above and that "test time for the rapid method was reduced by a factor of 2.5"; alphas were .68 and .57.

> "A Pearson product–moment correlation, r(19) ⫽ .85, p ⬍ .01, was obtained between scores for the tradi- tional and rapid tests, with a 95% confidence interval extending from .65 to .94."
