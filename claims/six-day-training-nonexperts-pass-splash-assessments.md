`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Training outcomes reported in the Results section (Echocardiography Training) of this prospective cross-sectional screening study. The training was delivered as "a combination of online modules, lectures, and face-to-face practical training delivered over 6 days"; completion required a minimum of 50 supervised SPLASH studies plus written and practical assessments.

> "Of 18 nonexpert practitioners who undertook the training, 15 successfully completed training and passed the assessments, including 6 nonspecialist doctors, 4 nurses, and 5 health workers, allowing them to participate in screening."
