### Choi S 2018

Choi S, Yuen HM, Annan R, Pickup T, Pulman A, Monroy-Valle M, Aduku NEL, Kyei-Boateng S, Velásquez Monzón CI, Portillo Sermeño CE, Penn A, Ashworth A, Jackson AA. (2018). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-reported application data from in-service professionals in the cross-country study. The article reports "85.9% of in-service professionals who took the course (128/149)" applied knowledge, with nutritionists (100%) and nurses (88%, 37/42) highest.

> "Participants were asked to report whether they had applied the knowledge, and 85.9% of in-service professionals who took the course (128/149) reported to have applied the gained knowledge in practice."

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Twelve-month follow-up of in-service and graduate preservice participants on clinical practice changes. The article reports 115 of 130 respondents "changed their practice in line with the WHO guidelines."

> "Of 130 respondents, 115 (88.5%, 115/130) stated that they had changed their practice in line with the WHO guidelines."
