# P2P-teaching qualification research shows weak grounding in learning and teaching theories and low levels of evidence

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The reviewed studies rarely use learning or teaching theories and mostly evaluate at low evidence levels (satisfaction and self-assessed learning). [→ Bernhard-Skala Christian 2026](#bernhard-skala-christian-2026)
`q2 i?` Most studies evaluate learning effects only at Kirkpatrick levels 1–2, mainly via subjective self-assessment, and about half involve fewer than 20 tutors. [→ Bernhard-Skala Christian 2026 (2)](#bernhard-skala-christian-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Bernhard-Skala Christian 2026

Bernhard-Skala Christian, Steinweg Bernhard. (2026). Towards a standardized competency-profile for peer-to-peer-teachers in medical education - a systematic mapping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09997-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of the 18 included studies' theoretical approaches: only two use social and cognitive congruence theoretically; scattered references include stress models, experience-based learning theory, the Van de Ridder feedback model, and Vygotsky's zone of proximal development.

> "Generally, the role of theory is rather weak, and the foundation of the studies in (learning) theories or theoretical models is limited."

### Bernhard-Skala Christian 2026 (2)

Bernhard-Skala Christian, Steinweg Bernhard. (2026). Towards a standardized competency-profile for peer-to-peer-teachers in medical education - a systematic mapping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09997-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review of research designs: ten single case studies dominate; ten studies are quantitative, five mixed-method, two qualitative focus-group designs. "In more than half of the empirical studies [11, 23, 29, 32, 34, 36] the number of tutors involved is lower than n = 20."

> "The designs of the sample twice [28, 29] exceed Kirkpatrick’s levels of evaluation [38] satisfaction (level 1) and learning (level 2)."
