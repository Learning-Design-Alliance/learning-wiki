## Evidence

### W. H. Winch 1910

W. H. Winch. (1910). Mental Fatigue in Day School Children as Measured by Immediate Memory. Part I. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://books.google.com

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled two-group comparison after equal practice: two initially equal groups (average preliminary mark 240.9 each) worked the same tests, Group A mornings only and Group B afternoons only, over three weeks. Improvement was 10.7 per cent for the morning group versus 5.0 per cent for the afternoon group.

> "The "morning group" improves 10.7 per cent, from the preliminary to the iinal tests; whilst the "afternoon group" improves 5.0 per cent, from the preliminary to the final tests. For a class of boys of this age and mental level therefore, the fatigue effect thus calculated appears to be between 5 and 6 per cent."
