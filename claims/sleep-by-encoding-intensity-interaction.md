### Drosopoulos 2007

Drosopoulos, S., Schulze, C., Fischer, S., & Born, J. (2007). Sleep's Function in the Spontaneous Recovery and Consolidation of Memories. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-3445.136.2.169

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 global ANOVA over sleep/wake and intense/weak encoding groups: the "Sleep versus Wake ⫻ Learning Intensity interaction" was significant, ηp2 = .11, while List × Learning Intensity and List × Sleep versus Wake interactions were not, F(1, 36) < 1.

> "This pattern was confirmed by significance for the Sleep versus Wake ⫻ Learning Intensity interaction, F(1, 36) ⫽ 4.17, p ⫽ .049, ␩p2 ⫽ .11 (Figure 3)"
