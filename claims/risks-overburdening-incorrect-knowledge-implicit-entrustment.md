### Gehrke-Beck Sabine 2025

Gehrke-Beck Sabine, Holzhausen Ylva. (2025). Entrustment of student supervision to GP trainees - a qualitative interview study of factors influencing entrustment decisions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07870-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on perceived risks. A GP worried a trainee with a student for a week would be "completely exhausted by the end of the week"; GP trainees also feared passing on false knowledge. The article reports no respondent described direct observation of supervision, as suggested by Iqbal.

> "The GPs see two main risks: overburdening the trainees and students learning incorrect facts - with the first risk being mentioned more frequently."
