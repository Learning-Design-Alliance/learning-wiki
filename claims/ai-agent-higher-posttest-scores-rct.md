## Evidence

### Fei Qin 2025

Fei Qin, Zhanxin Hao, Jifan Yu, Zhiyuan Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). AI instructional agent improves student's perceived learner control and learning outcome: Empirical evidence from a randomized controlled trial. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same RCT (n = 125 analyzed), post-test scores from 16 retained multiple-choice items were compared across the three conditions via ANOVA and Tukey HSD; the AI group scored significantly higher than both comparison groups. No effect size printed.

> "In addition, significant group differences were also found in post-test scores, F(2, 122) = 7.673, p < .01. Students in the AI group achieved significantly higher post-test scores than those in the human teacher group (M difference =
0.128, p < .01), and also outperformed the MOOC group (M difference = 0.112, p < .01)."
