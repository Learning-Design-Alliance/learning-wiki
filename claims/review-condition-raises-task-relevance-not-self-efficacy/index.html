Evidence¶

Hans van der Meij 2016¶

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij. (2016). Demonstration-based training (DBT) in the design of a video tutorial for software training. Instr Sci 44:527–542. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9394-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Post-training comparison of task relevance between conditions in the quasi-experiment. The authors call this finding unexpected because "the reviews did not present new information that could have piqued user interest in the formatting tasks."

"However, after training, participants viewing the review tutorial gave signiﬁcantly higher appraisals, F(1, 49) = 9.96, p = 0.003, d = 1.70."

Hans van der Meij 2016 (2)¶

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij. (2016). Demonstration-based training (DBT) in the design of a video tutorial for software training. Instr Sci 44:527–542. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9394-9

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on self-efficacy showed no condition effect; no effect size is printed for this null finding, so equivalence was not tested. The authors suggest the lack of user control may have prevented an effect of reviews on self-efficacy.

"There was no main effect for condition, F(1, 47) = 2.98, p = 0.091. Scores for self-efﬁcacy did not differ before training, F(1, 53) = 2.77, ns, or after training, F(1, 49) \ 1."
