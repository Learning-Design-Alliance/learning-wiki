Yu et al. 2015¶

Ka Chun Yu, Kamran Sahami, Victoria Sahami, Larry C. Sessions. (2015). Using A Digital Planetarium For Teaching Seasons To Undergraduates. Journal of Astronomy & Earth Sciences Education, 2(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1101071

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Figure 4 analysis of Post-Pre gains: with only n=39 Group I students present for both quizzes (CI of ±10.7%) versus n=260 Group III students (CI of ±4.6%), the Group III advantage over the control group is not statistically separable.

"The greater Group III gain is robust when compared with Group II since their 95% CIs do not overlap. However the Group I CI is wide enough to encompass the Group III CI because the number of Group I students was lower."
