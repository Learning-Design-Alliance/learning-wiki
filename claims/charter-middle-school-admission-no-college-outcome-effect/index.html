Evidence¶

Kate Place 2019¶

Kate Place, Philip Gleason. (2019). Do Charter Middle Schools Improve Students' College Outcomes? (Evaluation Brief). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Key finding of the brief, a longer-term follow-up of the National Evaluation of Charter Middle Schools examining admission to about 30 charter middle schools. The brief reports no significant effect on either college enrollment or college completion; no effect size is printed.

"Being admitted to a charter middle school in the study did not affect students’ chances of enrolling in or completing college."
