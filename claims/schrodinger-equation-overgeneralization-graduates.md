### Singh 2013

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Findings from prior work cited in this article, reported for the same 39 graduate students. In interviews many incorrectly claimed the equation holds for a superposition of ground and first excited states of an infinite square well, arguing verbally that adding the two eigenstate equations yields the general equation, and many had difficulty believing it false until they wrote the equations on paper.
