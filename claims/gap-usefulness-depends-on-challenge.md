## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The GAP's usefulness appears limited to players who faced sufficient difficulty, since persistence is elicited only under challenge. [→ Ventura 2013](#ventura-2013)

## Evidence

### Ventura 2013

Ventura, M., Shute, V., & Small, M. (2013). Assessing Persistence in Educational Games. Design Recommendations for Intelligent Tutoring Systems - Volume 2: Adaptive Instructional Management. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Assessing+Persistence+in+Educational+Games

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' interpretation of the Ventura and Shute (2013) results: players who had more difficulty in the game were operating under the required conditions to elicit persistence, since to persist one must be challenged.

> "The usefulness of the GAP does appear to depend on whether kids were being sufficiently challenged in the game."
