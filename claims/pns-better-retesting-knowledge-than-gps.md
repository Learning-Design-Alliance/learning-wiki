The chlamydia knowledge, awareness and testing practices of Australian general practitioners and practice nurses: survey findings from the Australian Chlamydia Control Effectiveness Pilot (ACCEPt). BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-14-169

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey comparison via conditional logistic regression; the Results section prints for the repeat-test item an odds ratio of OR 2.7 (95% CI 1.1, 6.4) and for the three-month timeframe OR 3.8 (95% CI 1.8, 8.11), all p<0.05.

> "More PNs than GPs knew that retesting was recommended after chlamydia treatment (93% vs 87%, P=0.027); and the recommended timeframe was 3 months (66% vs 26%, P<0.01)."
