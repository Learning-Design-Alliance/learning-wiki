### Carver-Thomas 2022

Carver-Thomas, D., Burns, D., Leung, M., & Ondrasek, N. (2022). Teacher shortages during the pandemic: How California districts are responding. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/899.809

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Open-ended survey of 12 California districts (eight largest plus four small rural) conducted August–September 2021 via email, phone, and video call. The report states "8 of 12" districts faced increased vacancies and six still had to fill "10% or more" of total vacancies. No effect size is printed.

> "Most districts surveyed for this study (8 of 12) faced an increased number of vacancies over pre-COVID-19 years and experienced greater challenges in filling these positions. At the time of this study, six districts indicated they still had to fill 10% or more of their total vacancies."
