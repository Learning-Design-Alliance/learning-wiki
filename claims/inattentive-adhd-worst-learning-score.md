## Evidence

### Mohamed A. Ahmed 2014

Mohamed A. Ahmed, Khaled A. El-Beh, Talal A. Mohammed, Dina F. Mansour, Mohammed Y. Ezz-Eldine. (2014). Learning Disabilities in Different Types of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorders and its Relation to Cortical and Brainstem Function. J Neurol Res. 2014;4(1):22-30. https://doi.org/10.14740/jnr268w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way comparison of learning disability scores across ADHD subtypes (28 combined, 9 inattentive, 3 hyperactive-impulsive). Means were ADHD-C 2.92, ADHD-I 2.40, ADHD-HI 2.97; the subtype difference reached P = 0.019.

> "The inattentive type had the worst score in comparison to combined and hyperactive-impulsive type (P = 0.019) (Table 2, Fig. 1)."
