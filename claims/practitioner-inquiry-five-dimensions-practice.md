## Evidence

### Koomen 2016

Michele Hollingsworth Koomen. (2016). Practitioner Inquiry with Early Program Teacher Candidates. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i11.1915

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Meta-analysis of practitioner inquiry projects completed by 42 pairs and 12 trios of early program teacher candidates. The author's Tier 2 grounded theory analysis identified these five dimensions as the top areas of enhanced practice knowledge, cited by almost 85% of candidates.

> "The findings suggest that the teacher candidates enhanced their knowledge of practice within practice across 5 dimensions of practice: inclusion, classroom discussion, classroom engagement, understanding student learning and grouping."
