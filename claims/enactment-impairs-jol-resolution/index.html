Evidence¶

Kubik 2022¶

Kubik, V., Jemstedt, A., Mahjub Eshratabadi, H., Schwartz, B. L., & Jönsson, F. U. (2022). The underconfidence-with-practice effect in action memory: The contribution of retrieval practice to metacognitive monitoring. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09288-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the same lab experiment, mean resolution gamma for study–test items was lower after enactive than verbal encoding: "enactment (M = 0.46, SE = 0.06) significantly hampered the resolution of JOLs compared to verbal encoding (M = 0.65, SE = 0.06)".

"enactment (M = 0.46, SE = 0.06) significantly hampered the resolution of JOLs compared to verbal encoding (M = 0.65, SE = 0.06), F(1, 47) = 4.68, p = 0.036, ω2 = 0.037"

Related Claims¶

  • Resolution of JOLs increases across study–test cycles, improving between Cycles 1 and 2 but not from Cycle 2 to Cycle 3 — related
  • Learners' JOLs are sensitive to the enactment benefit even when encoding type is manipulated between subjects — related
  • Past-test correlations exceed concurrent resolution scores, and JOLs rise for previously recalled items while falling for nonrecalled items — related
  • Enactive encoding yields higher cued recall than verbal encoding, and the enactment effect remains stable across learning cycles — related
  • Calibration of JOLs shifts toward underconfidence across learning cycles for action phrases under both verbal and enactive encoding, without first-cycle overconfidence — related
