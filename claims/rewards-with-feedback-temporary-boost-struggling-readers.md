### Grønli 2024

Grønli, K. M., Walgermo, B. R., McTigue, E. M., & Uppstad, P. H. (2024). Teachers' Feedback on Oral Reading: A Critical Review of its Effects and the use of Theory in Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09957-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of nine studies of supporting elements (goal setting and rewards) enhancing feedback during oral reading. It reports that "rewards combined with feedback temporarily boosted reading performance, especially for struggling readers", with variable long-term effects; two further studies adding rewards or goals reported no significant effects.

> "five studies found that rewards combined with feedback temporarily boosted reading performance, especially for struggling readers, but had variable long-term effects (Carroll, 2008; Chafouleas et al., 2004; Eckert et al., 2000, 2002; Henze & Williams, 2013)."
