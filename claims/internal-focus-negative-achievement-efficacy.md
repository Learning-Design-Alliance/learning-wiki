### Lee Madison Mason 2025

Lee Madison Mason, Vatral Caleb, Cohn Clayton, Davalos Eduardo, Jessee Mary Ann, Biswas Gautam, Levin Daniel T. (2025). Eye movements as predictors of student experiences during nursing simulation learning events. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-025-00640-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 of the pre-registered multilevel analysis; internal focus was the only predictor retained when clustering by student. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Increased internal focus was negatively associated with achievement scores (b = -0.20 t(306) = − 3.59, p < 0.001). The model accounted for approximately 14% of variance in achievement (Marginal R2 = 0.144)."
