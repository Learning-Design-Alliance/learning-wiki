## Evidence

### He Lidan 2026

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

OSCE-based skills assessment of five core procedures in 28 residents before and after 12 months of training. The authors report "the greatest relative improvement (25.7%)" for vaginal delivery assistance; manual placental separation improved 24.8% (d = 2.48). All procedures improved significantly (P < 0.001).

> "Vaginal delivery assistance demonstrated the greatest relative improvement (25.7%) with a mean difference of 17.3 points (95% CI [14.7 - 19.9], Cohen's d = 2.50, t = 13.26, p < 0.001)."

### He Lidan 2026 (2)

He Lidan, Wu Jianbo, Yang Huijuan, Wang Jinhua, Li Xuemei, Chen Lihong. (2026). Construction and implementation of an AI-enhanced progressive training model for clinical competency development in obstetrics and gynecology residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08751-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Direct paired comparison of improvement magnitudes between the two named procedures in the same 28-resident cohort. The test showed "significantly greater than that observed for IUD removal" with a 4.6-point difference in improvement; the article prints no effect size for this contrast.

> "Paired samples t-tests revealed that the magnitude of improvement in vaginal delivery skills was significantly greater than that observed for IUD removal (mean difference in improvement 4.6 points, 95% CI [2.1 - 7.1], p = 0.001), suggesting differential training effects across procedure types."
