## Evidence

### Rogers 2025

Rogers, L. K., Nguyen, T. D., Timmer, M., & Samson, J. (2025). Principal Burnout: A Systematic Review. Working paper. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED666666

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Federici & Skaalvik (2012), that SEM identified burnout as the strongest predictor of motivation to quit and a mediator. Only three studies examined motivation to quit; definitions and measurements varied. No study directly linked burnout to actual turnover.

> "Federici & Skaalvik (2012) used structural equation modeling to identify predictors of principals' expressed motivation to quit, with burnout being both the strongest predictor of motivation to quit and a mediator between other constructs (self-efficacy, job satisfaction) and motivation to quit."
