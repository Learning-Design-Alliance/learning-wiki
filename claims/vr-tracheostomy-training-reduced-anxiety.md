Pérez de los Cobos Cintas C, Vuillerme N, Thomann G, Di Marco L. (2025). Effect of an Extended Reality Simulation Intervention on Midwifery Students' Anxiety: Systematic Review. JMIR Nursing. https://doi.org/10.2196/68984

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports this prospective pre-post study of 60 health care providers, where the VR group showed "reduced anxiety (93% vs 75%, P=.002)" compared with regular training at follow-up.

> "at follow-up, the intervention group showed significantly higher agreement with statements about increased familiarity (83% vs 76%, P=.04), enhanced confidence (92% vs 74%, P=.001), and reduced anxiety (93% vs 75%, P=.002)."
