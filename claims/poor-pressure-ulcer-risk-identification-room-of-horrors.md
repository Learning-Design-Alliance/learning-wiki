`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, from Farnan et al.'s 30-minute mandatory 'room of horrors' simulation with a mannequin patient and nine safety hazards, that pressure ulcer risk "was the hazard that was recognised the least," identified by only 5% of medical students and 0% of interns.

> "Out of nine patient safety hazards the pressure ulcer risk was the hazard that was recognised the least. Only 5% of the medical students and 0% of the interns properly identified pressure ulcer risk."
