## Evidence

### Heller Sebastian 2024

Heller Sebastian, Reichel Jennifer L., Mülder Lina M., Schäfer Markus, Schwab Lisa, Werner Antonia M., Letzel Stephan, Rigotti Thomas, Dietz Pavel. (2024). The association between health behaviours and academic performance moderated by trait mindfulness amongst university students: an observational study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1340235

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA (N = 1,049) showed significantly lower academic performance amongst neuroenhancement users (p = 0.010, η2 = 0.007); the association was also significant in Step 3 of regression Model 1 (β = 0.09, p = 0.019) but not in the final step. The authors frame this as adding evidence to the debate on whether neuroenhancement improves grades.

> "A valuable ancillary finding was the significant association of pharmacological neuroenhancement with lower academic performance (in Step 3 of regression Model 1 and ANOVA results)."
