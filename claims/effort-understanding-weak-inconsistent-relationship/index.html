Katrin Schuessler 2026¶

Katrin Schuessler, Jenna Koenen, Elke Sumfleth, Marvin Rost. (2026). Variations in Repeated Measures of Cognitive Load and Interest During Complex Learning Tasks. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10105-4

q3 · i0 · associational · r2

Pairwise raw-data correlations across 21 measurement points in the N = 360 sample. The article reports 16 negative coefficients (r = −.04 to −.19, eight significant) and five positive ones (r = .00 to .18), only one significant (MP2.5).

"For invested mental effort and self-assessed understanding (Fig. 10, upper left, light grey line), 57% (i.e. 12 measuring points) of the correlations were not significant, indicating a weak and inconsistent linear relationship."

Related Claims¶

  • Perceived task difficulty correlates negatively with self-assessed understanding and with interest at essentially all measurement points, while interest and self-assessed understanding correlate positively throughout — related
  • Pairwise relationships between invested mental effort, perceived task difficulty, interest, and self-assessed understanding fluctuate across measurement points, with most correlations stronger when more content knowledge is presented — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Invested mental effort and interest are only weakly and inconsistently positively correlated across measurement points, with some correlations non-significant — related
  • Repeated measures of invested mental effort, perceived task difficulty, interest, and self-assessed understanding differ significantly across measurement points within complex learning tasks and are not adequately represented by their mean — related
  • Greater time and effort invested in the task correlate with better integration and smaller calibration error — related
  • Mental effort correlates negatively with self-assessments regardless of whether learners receive up-front cue information — related
  • Mental effort is more suitably modeled as a non-linear than linear predictor of learning outcomes in most re-analyzed studies — related
  • Effort's relation to achievement differs by operationalization: quality-of-learning effort shows a positive link, quantity-of-learning effort is weak, conditional, or negative — related
  • Students use invested mental effort as a cue when judging their learning, and self-rated mental effort is negatively related to judgments of learning — related
