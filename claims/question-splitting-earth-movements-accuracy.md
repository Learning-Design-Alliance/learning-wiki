## Evidence

### Alejandro Gangui 2010

Alejandro Gangui, María Iglesias & Cynthia Quinteros. (2010). Some remarks on a current study involving preservice elementary teachers and some basic astronomical phenomena. Proceedings IAU Symposium No. 260. [doi:10.1017/s1743921311003164](https://doi.org/10.1017/s1743921311003164)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison between pilot tests and the final version of the instrument on Earth's movements (Q9): in pilots "just 10% of the inquired students got it right", while after splitting the question into two, roughly 40 of 51 answers agreed with the scientific model. The authors call this a simple modification that changed the amount of correct answers completely.

> "This simple modiﬁcation of the question changed completely the amount of correct answers. Out of 51 peo ple we got roughly 40 whose answers agreed with the present scientiﬁc model (approxim ately 80%)"
