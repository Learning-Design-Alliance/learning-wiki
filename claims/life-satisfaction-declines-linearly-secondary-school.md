## Evidence

### Yi-Jhen Wu; Michael Becker 2023

Yi-Jhen Wu; Michael Becker. (2023). Association between School Contexts and the Development of Subjective Well-Being during Adolescence: A Context-Sensitive Longitudinal Study of Life Satisfaction and School Satisfaction. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-022-01727-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent growth model (Model 0) on the overall longitudinal sample (N = 1,841) with four measurement points from grade 6 to 10; the linear model fit best for life satisfaction. The article reports "the linear slope was significantly negative" (parameter −0.072, SE 0.006, Table 4).

> "For life satisfaction, the results demonstrated that the linear slope was significantly negative, suggesting that life satisfaction declined linearly over time."
