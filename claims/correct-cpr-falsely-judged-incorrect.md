### Plata Christopher 2021

Plata Christopher, Nellessen Martin, Roth Rebecca, Ecker Hannes, Böttiger Bernd W., Löser Johannes, Wetsch Wolfgang A. (2021). Impact of video quality when evaluating video-assisted cardiopulmonary resuscitation: a randomized, controlled simulation trial. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-021-00486-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of false classifications of correct CPR performance in the simulation trial; the paper lists misattributions such as "low compression rate (21.6%, [n = 8])" and "superficial compression depth and incomplete thorax release (each 27%, [n = 10])". No effect size is printed.

> "Correct CPR was falsely evaluated as “incorrect” in 41.3% of all videos and thus evaluators attributed errors to the correct CPR performance."
