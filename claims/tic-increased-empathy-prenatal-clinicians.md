### Itani Mohammad S. 2026

Itani Mohammad S., Shankar Megha, Koenig Mary Dawn, Johnson Sara, Goldstein Ellen. (2026). Prenatal clinicians’ perspectives on trauma-informed care competency and training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09347-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 23 prenatal clinicians found that "understanding TIC principles enables clinicians to develop greater empathy for patient perspectives". One participant noted they now understand "where they come from and have a little more empathy towards why they’re doing what they’re doing".

> "Our study participants indicated that understanding TIC principles enables clinicians to develop greater empathy for patient perspectives; recognizing that a patient’s resistance often stems from past experiences rather than defiance."
