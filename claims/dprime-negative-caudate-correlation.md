## Evidence

### Adamson Maheen M. 2014

Adamson Maheen M., Taylor Joy L., Heraldez Daniel, Khorasani Allen, Noda Art, Hernandez Beatriz, Yesavage Jerome A. (2014). Higher Landing Accuracy in Expert Pilots is Associated with Lower Activity in the Caudate Nucleus. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112607

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Post hoc correlational analysis in the same fMRI study, examining d′ against caudate activation separately for correct, incorrect, and all decisions; the printed "r = −0.61" is a large negative correlation.

> "D prime was specifically and negatively correlated with activity in the bilateral caudate region during correct decisions (r = −0.61, p<.01), but not during incorrect or even all decisions."
