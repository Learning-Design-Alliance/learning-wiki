## Evidence

### C. D. Porter 2020

C. D. Porter, J. R. H. Smith, E. M. Stagar, A. Simmons, M. Nieberding, C. M. Orban, J. Brown, A. Ayers. (2020). Using Virtual Reality in Electrostatics Instruction: The Impact of Training. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020119)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc analysis of the randomized study comparing classification by gaming frequency alone versus by game type (2D, 3D, or both, with examples such as Candy Crush and Minecraft). No effect size printed; the authors note the study contains no further 3D-gamer comparison.

> "However, a post-hoc analysis showed no signiﬁcant diﬀerences in our results when students are classiﬁed solely on frequency of gaming, with no consideration of 3-dimensionality. This brings into question whether familiarity with 3D visuospatial rotation in an electronic context is truly an explanation of the interaction eﬀect"
