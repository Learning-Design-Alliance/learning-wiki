## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The game's sequence of play reproduces the physical time evolution of Quark Matter: neutrinos leave first undetected, dileptons escape next, and hadronization concludes the game. [→ J. Csörgő 2013](#j-csorgo-2013)

## Evidence

### J. Csörgő 2013

J. Csörgő, Cs. Török and T. Csörgő. (2013). Memory of Quark Matter Card Game. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1303.2798](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1303.2798)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Descriptive account of the game's designed course in the rules section: the stock of cards represents the formation of Quark Matter, and play proceeds through neutrino removal, dilepton detection, and a final phase of hadronization. The article offers this as design description, not a tested result.

> "Initially, neutrinos leave the plasma undetected, so the players pick out them first, but they do not get scores for it and can take neutrinos one by one . Then we detect the dilepton enhancement, generated by the enourmous initial temperature."
