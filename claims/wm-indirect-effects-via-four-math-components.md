## Evidence

### Spiller J 2023

Spiller J, Clayton S, Cragg L, Johnson S, Simms V, Gilmore C. (2023). Higher level domain specific skills in mathematics; The relationship between algebra, geometry, executive function skills and mathematics achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0291796

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Parallel mediation analyses (PROCESS macro, bootstrapped CIs, n = 94) with number fact knowledge, calculation skills, algebra and geometry as mediators. Both WM measures showed significant indirect effects via all four mediators; total indirect effect was .34 for verbal WM and .38 for visuospatial WM.

> "For verbal WM, there were significant indirect effects on mathematics achievement through all four specific mathematical skills. The indirect path via algebra was again the strongest. A significant direct effect of verbal WM on mathematics achievement remained after controlling for the mediators"
