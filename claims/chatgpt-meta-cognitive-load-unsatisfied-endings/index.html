Evidence¶

Shravika Mittal 2026¶

Shravika Mittal, Su Lin Blodgett, Q. Vera Liao. (2026). Learning by Chatting? Investigating the Impact of Generative AI on Information Seeking and Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11669 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.11669

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of diary endings. The article describes ChatGPT unsatisfactory endings as rooted in "meta-cognitive load and frictions": unpredictable prompting (the "instruction gap"), confusing output formats, distracting follow-up prompts, and in some cases abandonment of information seeking.

"The code was observed in12.24%of entries from participants in the ChatGPT group (nC = 12/98), compared to10.71%of entries from those in the Google group (nG = 15/140)."
