### Morén Constanza 2026

Morén Constanza, Mariscal Emi Ruiz, Negredo Mónica, Preciado María, Martínez Gemma, Llaurado-Serra Mireia, Rosa Antonio, González Melinda, Rodríguez Natàlia, Martínez Laura. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Overall participation across intervention groups was 40.29%. Authors attribute higher Group A participation to integration within the routine academic framework rather than additional time dedication required for ad hoc sessions.

> "Participation was higher in the group where the activity was integrated into the routine framework of a scheduled academic session, as outlined in the standard teaching plan (Group A, 54.17%). In contrast, Groups B and C, where the activity was conducted outside regular academic timeframes, showed significantly lower participation rates, with 26.67% and 40.68%, respectively (χ2 (1) = 16.07, p ± < 0.001)"
