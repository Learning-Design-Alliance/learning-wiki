Systematic review of 19 studies mapping interventions to Redman and Wiek's competency framework; the review reports the frequency counts of addressed competencies, including that "systems‐thinking (n = 16), strategies‐thinking (n = 15), implementation competence (n = 12) and values‐thinking (n = 11) were addressed most frequently."
