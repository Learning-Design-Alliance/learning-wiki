# The reverse math-to-motivation genetic pathway is not moderated by SES, supporting a directional motivation-to-achievement relation

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the refit Cholesky model with math as predictor and motivation as outcome, SES moderated genetic influences on math but did not moderate the math-to-motivation relation, consistent with motivation driving achievement rather than the reverse. [→ Tucker-Drob 2011](#tucker-drob-2011)

## Evidence

### Tucker-Drob 2011

Tucker-Drob, Elliot M., & Harden, K. Paige. (2011). Learning Motivation Mediates Gene-by-Socioeconomic Status Interaction on Mathematics Achievement in Early Childhood. Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2011.11.015

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Follow-up validity check refitting the bivariate Cholesky model with motivation as outcome and mathematics as predictor in the same ECLS-B twin sample; the ab′ parameter for math → motivation was not significant in this direction.

> "In addition, SES positively moderated the genetic influences on math but did not moderate the math → motivation relation. These results are consistent with the hypothesis that it is the directional relation from motivation to math, rather than a reverse pathway from math achievement to increase motivation that is positively moderated by SES."
