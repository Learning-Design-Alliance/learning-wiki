## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi P 2019

Moll-Khosrawi P, Kamphausen A, Hampe W, Schulte-Uentrop L, Zimmermann S, Kubitz JC. (2019). Anaesthesiology students’ Non-Technical skills: development and evaluation of a behavioural marker system for students (AS-NTS). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1609-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interrater-reliability analysis of ratings from 98 simulation scenarios in undergraduate emergency and anaesthesiology training, using ICC for ordinal and Cohen's kappa for nominal data. Agreement was excellent except dimension two for "3rd vs. 5th year residents (fair correlation)"; the abstract reports a mean of 0.89.

> "The interrater reliability reached high levels of agreement (Table 6), except for dimension two, in the group of 3rd vs. 5th year residents (fair correlation)."
