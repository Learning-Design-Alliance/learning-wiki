Evidence¶

Johnson 2022¶

Johnson, A. (2022). Achievement and growth for English Learners. Educational Policy, 37(4), 1044–1069. https://doi.org/10.1177/08959048211049419

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Piecewise multilevel growth models (Model 2) estimated monthly learning rates for each group from MAP Growth scores, shown in Figure 3. The analysis found EL groups "tended to grow more during the year than never-ELs" in math, with summer loss patterns reversed by subgroup.

"In math, the three EL groups tended to grow more during the year than never-ELs, and always-ELs had the highest growth rates. In the summers, ever-ELs had the lowest math learning loss rates; always-ELs and dually-identified students had the highest."
