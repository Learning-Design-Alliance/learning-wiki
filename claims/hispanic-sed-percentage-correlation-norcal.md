Observational analysis of California Department of Education School Accountability Report Card data for 2016-17 for the regional school sample. The authors report that "the percentage of Hispanic students and the percentage of SED students at a high school are highly correlated (r2= 0.60)" and that these factors predict physics offerings.

> "the data revealed that the percentage of Hispanic students and the percentage of SED students at a high school are highly correlated (r2= 0.60). Additionally, these factors could be used as predictors of a school's physics offerings."
