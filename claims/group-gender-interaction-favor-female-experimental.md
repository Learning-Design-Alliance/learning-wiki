## Evidence

### Ziad Mohammed Mahmoud Qabaja 2016

Ziad Mohammed Mahmoud Qabaja, Jamal Subhi Ismail Nafi’, Maisa’ Issa Khalil Abu-Nimah. (2016). The Effect of Using the “SQP2RS via WTL” Strategy through Science Context to 10th Graders’ Reading Comprehension in English in Palestine. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.26. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1115849

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

3-way ANCOVA interaction result from the quasi-experiment; adjusted means show experimental females at 42.120 versus experimental males at 38.617, and the differences are in favor of the female in the experimental group.

> "F value for the interaction between the group  and gender was (6.157), and the significant level was ( 0.014), and this is less than the ( α≤  0.05), so there are significant differences for the interaction between groups and gender"
