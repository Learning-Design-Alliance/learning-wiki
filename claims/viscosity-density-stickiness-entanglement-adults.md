### Markus Sebastian Feser and Ingrid Krumphals 2023

Markus Sebastian Feser and Ingrid Krumphals. (2023). Viscous behavior of fluids in the eyes of adults: A global survey. Preprint of a paper accepted for publication in The Physics Educator. [doi:10.1142/s2661339523500178](https://doi.org/10.1142/s2661339523500178)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Percentage frequencies with bootstrapped 95% confidence intervals from the same 406-participant online survey (RQ1 analysis, Table 2). The authors call the entanglement of viscosity with other fluid properties "the most compelling trend" in the results; 19.0% equated viscosity and density, 22.9% equated viscosity and stickiness.

> "67.0% (95% CI [62.4%, 71.5%]) of our participants in- correctly associated viscosity with density, assum- ing that the more viscous a fluid is, the denser it is"
