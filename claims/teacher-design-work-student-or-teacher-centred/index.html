Evidence¶

Connolly 2021¶

Connolly, C., & Hall, T. (2021). Designing for emergency remote blended and online education: a response to Bennett et al. (2017). Education Tech Research Dev, 69, 281–284. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09892-0

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reports, based on its reading of Bennett et al. (2017), that "university teachers’ design work is generally focused in one of two ways: student-centred or teacher-centred". This is the authors' interpretive summary of the reviewed study, not a new empirical result.

"The paper and review of literature illustrate how design work is characterised indifferent countries and cultures, and how university teachers’ design work is generally focused in one of two ways: student-centred or teacher-centred."
