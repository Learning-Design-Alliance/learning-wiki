## Evidence

### Matteo Luca Ruggiero and Lorenzo Galante 2020

Matteo Luca Ruggiero and Lorenzo Galante. (2020). Online Tutoring in Introductory Physics Courses: a Lockdown Experience. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2011.13315](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2011.13315)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion section, not a tested result: they suppose tutored students "were more diligent than the others" and that tutoring gave continuous practice and revision, citing the accepted influence of time-on-task on learning outcomes. No analysis of diligence or selection is reported.

> "Obviously, it is not possible to attribute these positive results entirely to the tutoring intervention. However, we suppose that students who took part in the tutoring activities were more diligent than the others."
