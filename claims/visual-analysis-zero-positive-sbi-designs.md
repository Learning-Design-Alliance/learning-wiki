## Evidence

### Zimmerman 2018

Zimmerman, K. N., Pustejovsky, J. E., Ledford, J. R., Barton, E. E., Severini, K. E., & Lloyd, B. P. (2018). Single-Case Synthesis Tools II: Comparing Quantitative Outcome Measures. Research in Developmental Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ridd.2018.02.001

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The reviewers applied the SCARF systematic visual analysis guidelines to all included SCD designs of antecedent sensory-based interventions, with reliability coders reaching 100% exact agreement on the presence or absence of a functional relation. The result: "visual analysis indicated functional relations were not present between antecedent SBI and targeted behaviors."

> "Among the included designs, visual analysis using the SCARF (Authors, 2016) yielded zero designs with positive outcomes. In short, visual analysis indicated functional relations were not present between antecedent SBI and targeted behaviors."
