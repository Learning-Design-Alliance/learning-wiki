`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of state alignment among the nine selected states, visualized in Figure 5, which shows states strongly aligned with baseline models also align closely with one another. The article reports models were "not systematically more aligned with either left-leaning or right-leaning states." No effect size is printed.

> "Overall, the models were not systematically more aligned with either left-leaning or right-leaning states, largely because states with similar political orientations are not consistently aligned with each other."
