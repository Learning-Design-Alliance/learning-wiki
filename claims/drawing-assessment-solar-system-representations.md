## Subclaims
`q1 i?` At the end of the activity, students drew the Solar System without visual support, correctly ordering the planets from the Sun and representing size and shape differences between inner and outer planets. [→ Vereș 2020](#veres-2020)

## Evidence

### Vereș 2020

Vereș, S. & Magdaș, I. (2020). The Use of Animation Film in Forming Representations about the Planet Earth and the Solar System. Romanian Review of Geographical Education, 9(1), 38-59. https://doi.org/10.23741/RRGE120183

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative observation of EG students' end-of-activity drawings made without visual support, reported in the analysis of learning activity no. 2. Students used colours correctly and represented relative planet sizes.

> "They correctly or dered the planets from the Sun. They represented the size and shape of each planet: the smaller inner planets, Jupiter the largest of the planets, and the outer planets larger than the inner planets."
