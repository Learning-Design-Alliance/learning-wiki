## Evidence

### Bol L and Hacker DJ 2012

Bol L and Hacker DJ (2012) Calibration research: where do we go from here? Front. Psychology 3:229. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00229

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Quasi-experiment in college courses (Hacker et al., 2008a) with a fully crossed incentives-by-reflection design; extrinsic reward students could earn one to four additional points per exam for calibration accuracy. The article reports "incentives signiﬁcantly improved calibration accuracy but only among lower-achieving students"; no effect size is printed.

> "In another more ecologically valid study, we manipulated incentives and reﬂection in a fully crossed quasi-experiment conducted in college course (Hacker et al., 2008a). Students in an extrinsic reward condition were told they would receive one to four additional points on each of three exams, depending on their calibration accuracy, with more points given for greater accuracy. We found that incentives signiﬁcantly improved calibration accuracy but only among lower-achieving students"
