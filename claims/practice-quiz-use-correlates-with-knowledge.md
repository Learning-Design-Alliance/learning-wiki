### Wallerstedt SM 2026

Wallerstedt SM, Jood K, Steingrimsson S, Wentz E, Zelano J, Bergquist F. (2026). Helpful if “Medication took up more space in the course!” – a mixed methods study about pharmacotherapeutic knowledge and digital quizzes for learning and assessment in the medical programme. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09292-7

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rank correlations among step II respondents (n=93) linking self-reported quiz use and summative attempts to anonymous test scores. The article reports "r = 0.33; P = 0.002" for practice use, r = –0.44 for attempts, and r = –0.22 between attempts and practice use.

> "After step II, the extent of practice quiz use was positively correlated with scores in the knowledge evaluation test (r = 0.33; P = 0.002)."
