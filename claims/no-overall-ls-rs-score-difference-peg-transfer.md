## Evidence

### Kanitra John J. 2021

Kanitra John J., Khogali-Jakary Nashwa, Gambhir Sahil B., Davis Alan T., Hollis Michael, Moon Caroline, Gupta Rama, Haan Pamela S., Anderson Cheryl, Collier Deborah, Henry David, Kavuturu Srinivas. (2021). Transference of skills in robotic vs. laparoscopic simulation: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01385-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Combining all groups in the RCT, overall laparoscopic and robotic FLS peg transfer scores did not differ significantly (219 ± 19 vs 227 ± 23; p = 0.065). The authors report a post-hoc power analysis indicating 7 additional students would be needed for significance.

> "There was no significant difference between overall LS scores (219 ± 19) and robotic scores (227 ± 23) (p = 0.065)."
