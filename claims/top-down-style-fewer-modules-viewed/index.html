Stanton 1990¶

Stanton, N. A. and Stammers, R. B. (1990). A comparison of structured and unstructured navigation through a CBT package. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ414518

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within the unstructured condition, trainees classified by self-reported learning style showed a significant difference between the top down group (N = 7) and the bottom up group (N = 7) on modules viewed (Mann-Whitney U, P < 0.01), with the top down group exploring fewer modules.

"Statistical significance was demonstrated between a top down style (N = 7) and a bottom up style (N = 7) on the number of modules viewed (Mann-Whitney U, P < 0.01)."
