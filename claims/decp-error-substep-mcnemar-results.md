## Evidence

### Özgüven Öztornaci B 2025

Özgüven Öztornaci B, Karakul A, Ardahan Akgül E, Doğan P, Yildirim Sari H, Başbakkal Z. (2025). The effect of web-based double-eye control program on the prevention of pediatric medication errors of nursing students: A quasi-experimental study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000046222

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

McNemar test analysis of error sub-steps in the same 44-student study (Table 3). For example, expiration date control errors fell from 88.6% to 22.7% and registration errors from 84.1% to 11.4%.

> "the decrease in the pre-intervention and post-intervention error rates was statistically significant in correct medication control (P < .001), EXP date control (P < .001), dosage control (P < .001), safe dosage control (P < .001), diluent choice (P < .001), determining the concentration of the medication (P < .001), and labeling the medication (P < .001)"
