### Carl C. Brigham 1914

Carl C. Brigham. (1914). An Experimental Critique of the Binet-Simon Scale. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0071183

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article reports that examiner effects could not be accurately determined because one experimenter tested more older children and another more minus-grade cases; after eliminating such disturbing cases too few remained, but in every treatment the influence "never amounted to more than one-third of the average mean variation."

> "However, in every method by which the results were treated to bring out the influence of the personal equation, that influence never amounted to more than one-third of the average mean variation, a value too small to be considered significant."
