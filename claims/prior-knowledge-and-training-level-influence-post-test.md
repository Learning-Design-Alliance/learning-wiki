## Evidence

### Löffler Sabine 2011

Löffler Sabine, Feja Christine, Widmann Jan, Claus Ilona, von Lindeman Katharina, Eisnach Kristina. (2011). Interactive versus Reproductive Learning, a Comparison of Medical School Graduates with Participants of a Postgraduate CPD Session. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000769](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000769)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate trifactorial variance analysis (Table 4) with post-test as dependent variable and factors intervention group, training group and pre-test result. Training group (p=0.005) and pre-test result (p<0.0005) were significant; intervention group showed a trend (p=0.089).

> "The training group (p=0.005) and the pre-test result (p<0.0005) have a significant influence on the results of the post-test."
