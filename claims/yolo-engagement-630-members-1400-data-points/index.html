Acknowledgments section of the report describing participation across the Roadmap to the Future initiative. Round 1 sessions drew nearly 370 community members and Round 2 nearly 260, with youth focus groups and key informant interviews supplementing the sessions.

"Nearly 630 community members attended these sessions and provided over 1,400 points of collected data. Additionally, the initiative convened three community engagement sessions that involved leaders of over 90 organizations in Yolo County, known as "Champions.""
