Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2010¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2010). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report: Lindamood Phoneme Sequencing® (LiPS®). Students with Learning Disabilities. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/279

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial (Torgesen et al., 2001) of 50 fourth-grade students with learning disabilities randomly assigned to LiPS or Embedded Phonics. WWC analyses corrected for multiple comparisons confirmed the significant findings; the printed effect sizes were 0.93 (LAC) and 0.99 (Word Attack), with "statistically significant effects favoring the LiPS® group".

"The study authors found statistically significant effects favoring the LiPS® group for two of the eight measures, including the Lindamood Auditory Conceptualization Test and the Word Attack subtest from the Woodcock Reading Mastery Test–Revised."

  • LiPS shows potentially positive effects on reading fluency: authors found significant effects on GORT–III Reading Accuracy and Reading Rate, but WWC analyses did not confirm significance — related
