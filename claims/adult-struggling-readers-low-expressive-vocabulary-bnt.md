## Evidence

### Hall 2012

Hall, R., Greenberg, D., Laures-Gore, J., & Pae, H. K. (2012). The Relationship Between Expressive Vocabulary Knowledge and Reading Skills for Adult Struggling Readers. Journal of Research in Reading. [doi:10.1111/j.1467-9817.2012.01537.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9817.2012.01537.x)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional assessment of 232 adult literacy students reading at third- to fifth-grade levels on the Boston Naming Test. The mean total correct score of 36 (SD = 7.29) was "much lower than scores reported with other non-college educated adult samples" (normative means of 54.5 and 51.91 cited in discussion).

> "Our participants' mean correct score for the entire task (spontaneous correct responses plus correct responses after stimulus cues) of 36 (SD = 7.29, Range = 14–53) is much lower than scores reported with other non-college educated adult samples"
