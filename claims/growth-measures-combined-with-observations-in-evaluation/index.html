The fact sheet states growth measures "can be combined with other educator performance measures, such as scores from classroom observations" for identifying high- and low-performing teachers. This is a stated use, not a tested result.

"These measures can be combined with other educator performance measures, such as scores from classroom observations, to help states, districts, and schools identify the highest- and lowest-performing teachers."
