Evidence¶

Kelley 1916¶

Kelley, Truman Lee. (1916). A constructive ability test. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. VII, No. 1. https://www.google.com/search?q=Kelley+1916+A+Constructive+Ability+Test+Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational comparison within the norming sample of boys tested with the constructive ability test. The author reports the manual-training group "nearly two scale units above other high school boys of the same age", but argues the difference is due to selection of a natively more capable group.

"Considering alone the group who had had or were taking manual training it is evident from the data at hand that they stand nearly two scale units above other high school boys of the same age."
