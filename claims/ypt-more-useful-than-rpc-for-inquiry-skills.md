# Students perceive YPT-related activities as more useful than regular physics classes for inquiry-oriented hard skills, while RPC is more useful for solving close-ended problems

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students rate YPT-related activities more useful than RPC for designing experiments, interpreting data, developing theoretical models, numerical simulations, and critical assessment, but rate RPC more useful for solving close-ended problems. [→ Boyka Aneva 2022](#boyka-aneva-2022)

## Evidence

### Boyka Aneva 2022

Boyka Aneva, Sergej Faletič, Mihály Hömöstrei, Péter Jenei, Isza Éva, František Kundracik, Assen Kyuldjiev, Thomas Lindner, Hynek Němec, Harald Puhr, and Martin Plesch. (2022). Development of Scientific Skills via IYPT: How does YPT participation lead to hard-skill development? ELTE University Budapest. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.09076](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.09076)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 308 students (responses per hard skill between 140 and 280) with t-tests comparing perceived usefulness of RPC and YPT activities on a 5-point scale; e.g. solving close-ended problems favoured RPC (t = 4.409, p = 0.000) while designing experiments favoured YPT (t = -3.157, p = 0.002).

> "While regular physics classes seem to be more useful for “Solving close-ended problems” (p = 0,000) than YPT -related activities, we find that YPT-related activities and other activities are more useful than regular physics classes"
