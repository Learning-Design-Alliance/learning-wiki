The article reports Boulton & Cobb's (2017) meta-analytic conclusions on the conditions under which DDL effects held, quoting the four situations directly. No effect size is printed for this specific conclusion.

> "Third, DDL showed consistent significant effects in situations where (1) the presence of native English instructors was limited; (2) courses targeted undergraduate and graduate learners as well as those of intermediate and advanced English levels; (3) computer - and paper-based concordancing were used; and (4) corpora were used either for a reference resource or learning vocabulary and lexicogrammar"
