`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

At a 1-hour MIE 2025 workshop in Glasgow, most participants favored understanding AI as belonging to a whole group of competency areas rather than a single one. The authors add that this "includes practical experience handling AI-enabled tools and applications as part of the education."

> "Supported by the post-survey workshop discussions, these recommendations strongly suggest AI topics should be addressed in all domains not only in computer, data, and information science and for all roles of nurses."
