### Emily M. Stump 2020

Emily M. Stump, Courtney L. White, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2020). Student reasoning about sources of experimental measurement uncertainty in quantum versus classical mechanics. arXiv:2007.06675v2 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.stump](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.stump)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretation in the conclusion of the interview study. They note participants' ideas were "more polarized when considering uncertainty generically than in response to hypothetical data sets" and call for future work on framing and resources; no effect size is printed.

> "In addition to indicating that students think about measurement uncertainty quite differently across physics contexts and types of uncertainty, the results suggest that student thinking about uncertainty may be sensitive to cueing from questions."
