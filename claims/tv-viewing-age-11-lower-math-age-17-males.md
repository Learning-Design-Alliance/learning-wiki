## Evidence

### Guan 2026

Guan, K., Zhang, Z., Liu, Z. et al. (2026). Longitudinal associations between sedentary behavior types and mathematics ability mediated by externalizing and internalizing problems. npj Sci. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00424-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Negative binomial regression of Millennium Cohort Study data, stratified by sex, associated male TV viewing duration at age 11 with mathematics ability at 17; 2 to <3 hours showed "IRR = 0.921, 95%CI: 0.865, 0.981"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "For males, TV viewing for 1 hour to < 2 hours (IRR = 0.939, 95%CI: 0.889, 0.993) and for 2 hours to < 3 hours (IRR = 0.921, 95%CI: 0.865, 0.981) corresponded with 6.1% and 7.9% decreases in mathematics ability score at 17 years, respectively."
