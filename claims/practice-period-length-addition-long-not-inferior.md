`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of long-period A groups with short-period B groups in each grade, using Tables 2 and 3 with pupils of equal initial ability selected by dropping the best adders from A groups and the worst from B groups. Advantages of the A group by the first scoring method were 8.2, 0, 1.4 and 9.8 for grades 7, 6, 5 and 4.

> "On the whole we may safely say that in this experiment the short periods were certainly not much more favorable than the longer periods, the evidence, though not secure, being in favor of the longer periods."
