Systematic review frequency analysis of customization methods across 23 empirical studies (2020–2025), reported in the RQ2 findings section. The review states "Prompt engineering was the most prevalent approach", with fine-tuning (N = 7) and hybrid architectures (N = 3) less common.

"Prompt engineering was the most prevalent approach. In these studies, pedagogical alignment was achieved by designing task- or role-specific instructions that constrained model outputs without altering internal parameters."
