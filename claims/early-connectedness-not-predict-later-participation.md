## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Neither early attendance nor early strongly-connected fraction predicted participation during the last laboratory exercise; the article reports only that the calculated correlations were small. [→ Kontro 2020](#kontro-2020)

## Evidence

### Kontro 2020

Kontro, I.; Génois, M. (2020). Combining surveys and sensors to explore student behaviour. [doi:10.3390/educsci10030068](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10030068)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis comparing first-four-weeks participation metrics to remaining weeks (Table 5; e.g., SC beg. vs Present end r = -0.10). The article prints only the narrative that "the calculated correlations were small"; no standardised effect size is given for this finding. Results were similar when only the first two weeks were included.

> "Again, the calculated correlations were small, meaning neither the times the student was present early in the course nor the fraction of times they were strongly connected to other students predicted participation during the last laboratory exercise."
