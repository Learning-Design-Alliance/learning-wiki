## Evidence

### McGarr 2020

McGarr, O., & Gallchóir, C. Ó. (2020). Examining supervising field instructors' reporting and assessment of technology use by pre-service teachers on school placement. Computers & Education, 146, 103753. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103753

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with a purposive sample of 10 supervising field instructors, analysed using Braun and Clarke's six-step thematic analysis. The article reports the presentation-aid theme "was emphasised by all but one of the participants."

> "The most prominent theme to emerge from the interviews related to the use of technology as a presentation aid and aligned closely with the nature of comments made by the wider cohort of field instructors in the lesson reports.  This was emphasised by all but one of the participants."
