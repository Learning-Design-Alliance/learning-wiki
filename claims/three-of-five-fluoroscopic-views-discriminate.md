## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sønderup M 2024

Sønderup M, Gustafsson A, Konge L, Jacobsen M E. (2024). Intraoperative fluoroscopy skills in distal radius fracture surgery: valid and reliable assessment on a novel immersive virtual reality simulator. Acta Orthopaedica 95: 477–484. https://doi.org/10.2340/17453674.2024.41345

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Two-way mixed ANOVA with Holm–Bonferroni correction on simulator metrics from 11 novices and 9 experienced surgeons. Table 2 prints partial eta squared of 0.41 (lateral), 0.36 (facet), and 0.32 (PA view), all with adjusted significance levels reached.

> "There was a statistically significant difference between the 2 groups for total PA image z-score, total lateral image z-score, and total facet image z-score, respectively, indicating discriminatory ability of the simulator test for these projections (Table 2)."

### Sønderup M 2024 (2)

Sønderup M, Gustafsson A, Konge L, Jacobsen M E. (2024). Intraoperative fluoroscopy skills in distal radius fracture surgery: valid and reliable assessment on a novel immersive virtual reality simulator. Acta Orthopaedica 95: 477–484. https://doi.org/10.2340/17453674.2024.41345

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the same ANOVA analysis, the tangential view (P = 0.07) and AP view (P = 0.7) did not discriminate between groups. The authors note these "2 non-discriminatory views were not routinely used by the experienced surgeons in our sample".

> "Unexpectedly, 2 views failed to differentiate between experience levels, despite being identified as essential in a global Delphi consensus study by 87 orthopedic experts for assessing DRF fixation with a volar locking plate [13]."
