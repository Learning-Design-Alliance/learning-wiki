## Evidence

### Amali Priyanka Jambuge and James T. Laverty 2021

Amali Priyanka Jambuge and James T. Laverty. (2021). Assessing Scientific Practices in Physics Paper-based Assessments. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2106.13028](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2106.13028)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Think-aloud interview results for the Ferris wheel task: Table III shows 1 of 7 responses elicited evidence. The authors report "the task that includes symbolic variables, the Ferris wheel problem (1 task) did not prompt students to elicit the expected evidence"; 6 of 7 students started with conceptual analysis.

> "Unlike the assessment tasks that include numerical quantities in their problem statement (6 tasks), the task that includes symbolic variables, the Ferris wheel problem (1 task) did not prompt students to elicit the expected evidence"
