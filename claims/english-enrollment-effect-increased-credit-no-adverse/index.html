Evidence¶

Allensworth 2009¶

Allensworth, E., Nomi, T., Montgomery, N., & Lee, V. E. (2009). College Preparatory Curriculum for All: Academic Consequences of Requiring Algebra and English I for Ninth Graders in Chicago. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 31(4), 367-391. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373709343471

q2 · i? · causal · r2

From the enrollment-effect models addressing Research Question 2: English I enrollment effects on ninth-grade outcomes. The article reports students were "much more likely to earn English I credit (p < .01), and their failure rates were unaffected," with absence slightly lower for the two lower-ability groups.

"Students at all ability levels were much more likely to earn English I credit (p < .01), and their failure rates were unaffected. Course absence was very slightly less for the two lower-ability groups by about a third of a day a year (p < .05)."
