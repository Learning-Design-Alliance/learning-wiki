### Sanjoy Mahajan 2005

Sanjoy Mahajan. (2005). Observations on teaching first-year physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0512158](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0512158)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Tutorial observation: asked to estimate √1.2, most students "had no idea"; the only correct answer came from a student schooled in Singapore without early calculator use. The author also reports A-level students at Villiers Park unable to estimate √1.2.

> "All the students except the on e who answered correctly had been using calculators since age 11. This other student, who went to school in Singapore, had not been permitted to use a calculat or until the last two years of high school."
