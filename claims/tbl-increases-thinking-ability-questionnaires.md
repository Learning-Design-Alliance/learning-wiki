# TBL develops students' thinking ability more than LBL, though with high heterogeneity driven by one study

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A larger proportion of TBL students reported the pedagogy greatly developed their thinking ability (93.69% TBL vs 58.79% LBL; RR = 1.45, 95% CI [1.04, 2.02]); after removing one study the estimate was RR = 1.24. [→ Lang Bingchen 2019](#lang-bingchen-2019)

## Evidence

### Lang Bingchen 2019

Lang Bingchen, Zhang Lingli, Lin Yunzhu, Han Lu, Zhang Chuan, Liu Yantao. (2019). Team-based learning pedagogy enhances the quality of Chinese pharmacy education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1724-6

`q3 · i?`

Meta-analysis of three studies with 405 students (206 receiving TBL) pooling questionnaire responses on thinking ability. Heterogeneity was high (I2 = 88%) and resolved after removing the Luo study (I2 = 0%), with the summary estimate essentially unchanged ("RR = 1.24"). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The responses of questionnaires revealed that the application of TBL significantly developed students' thinking ability compared to LBL (the ratio of students thinking the pedagogy method had great influence on developing thinking ability: 93.69% in TBL group vs 58.79% in LBL group, RR = 1.45 with 95% CI [1.04,2.02], p < 0.0001, I2 = 88%)"
