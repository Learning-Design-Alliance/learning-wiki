## Evidence

### Gerhard Weber & Peter Brusilovsky 2016

Gerhard Weber & Peter Brusilovsky. (2016). ELM-ART – An Interactive and Intelligent Web-Based Electronic Textbook. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0066-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Longitudinal log monitoring of ELM-ART usage rather than a controlled study; the authors state they continued to monitor user data and were interested in drop-outs. Page-level drop-out spikes were used to revise text, after which drop-out rates at those pages returned to normal. No effect size is printed.

> "However, we discovered that some higher drop out rates at specific pages offer a hint to difficulties in the text. This information was used to revise the text at some pages, which, in turn, led to lower (normal) drop-out rates at these pages."
