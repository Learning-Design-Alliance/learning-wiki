## Evidence

### de Bruin 2017

de Bruin, A. B. H., Kok, E. M., Lobbestael, J., & de Grip, A. (2017). The impact of an online tool for monitoring and regulating learning at university: overconfidence, learning strategy, and personality. Metacognition and Learning, 12, 21–43. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-016-9159-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

2x4 repeated measures ANOVA on absolute accuracy at t=0 and t=2 with performance quartile as between-subjects factor. The quartile main effect was F(3, 125) = 49.06, p < .001, ηp2 = .54; the interaction was not significant, F(3, 125) = 1.79, p = .15.
