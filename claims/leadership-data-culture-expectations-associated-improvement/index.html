Across retrospective and descriptive studies in the review, "strong leadership, use of data to guide instruction, positive school culture characterized by trust, and increased expectations for students" were identified as factors associated with positive student outcomes. These findings come from designs that cannot establish causation.

"The studies summarized in this report identified strong leadership, use of data to guide instruction, positive school culture characterized by trust, and increased expectations for students as factors that seem to be associated with positive student outcomes in schools experiencing state intervention."
