Deductive thematic analysis of interviews with 11 medical students after VR simulation training, coded under the ITEM system usability domain. Participants reported headset discomfort, cost barriers, glitches, and desire for scenario variability and familiarization time; "the wearability of the headsets, which reduced the accessibility of the training."

> "Participants raised some concerns regarding the usability of the VR system. One key issue was the wearability of the headsets, which reduced the accessibility of the training."
