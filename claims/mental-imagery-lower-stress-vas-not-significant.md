### Levy Manon 2025

Levy Manon, Dopff Cécile, Audibert Gerard, Bouquet Antoine, Gurda Thomas, Sy Mohammed, Bleuze Pauline, Michelet Daphné. (2025). Effect of mental imagery using cognitive aids on the performance of novice anesthesiology residents during a simulated cardiopulmonary resuscitation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07782-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary outcome measured on the day of the final evaluation, just prior to the high-fidelity simulation, using a stress visual analogue scale. The article reports lower median scores in the mental imagery group with "p = 0.06", which the authors describe as not reaching the limit of significance.

> "In fact, the mental imagery group had lower median stress VAS scores than the control group (5.5 vs. 6.5, p = 0.06)."
