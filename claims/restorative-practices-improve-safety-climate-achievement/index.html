dePaoli 2023¶

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

q3 · i? · review · r?

The review reports this consistent pattern across studies of restorative practice programs, noting 60% of schools reported using some form of restorative practices in 2019–20.

"Studies of restorative practice programs consistently find that they reduce the use of exclusionary discipline, decrease rates of student misbehavior, and improve school climate."

dePaoli 2023 (2)¶

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

q3 · i? · review · r?

The review reports a 2023 study across California middle schools finding schoolwide improvements to climate, safety, wellness, behavior, and achievement where restorative practices increased, with "Black students benefited the most."

"for students, increased exposure to restorative practices was associated with decreased probability and duration of suspension and higher English language arts and mathematics assessment scores. While all students benefited from restorative practices, Black students benefited the most."
