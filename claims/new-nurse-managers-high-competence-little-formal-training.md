## Evidence

### Aksu Miray 2026

Aksu Miray, Varol Belgin, Özer Küçük Emine. (2026). Determination of Managerial Competence Levels and Training Requirements of New Nurse Managers for Role Transition: Empirical Research Quantitative. Nursing Open, 13(10). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70715

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive cross-sectional online survey of 185 new nurse managers in Türkiye using the 45-item Responsible Nurse Managerial Competence Assessment Scale (α = 0.98 in this study). The article reports "the mean score of the charge nurse managerial competence score of the new nurse managers is 4.44" with sub-dimension means near 4.4–4.5.

> "the mean score of the charge nurse managerial competence score of the new nurse managers is 4.44 (SD = 0.592; 95% CI: 4.35–4.53), the mean score of general unit management is 4.46 (±0.571), the mean score of personnel management is 4.41 (±0.651) and the mean score of care management is 4.45 (±0.688)"

### Aksu Miray 2026 (2)

Aksu Miray, Varol Belgin, Özer Küçük Emine. (2026). Determination of Managerial Competence Levels and Training Requirements of New Nurse Managers for Role Transition: Empirical Research Quantitative. Nursing Open, 13(10). https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70715

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey of 185 new nurse managers; Table 1 characteristics show 63.8% had no prior formal managerial training, and the sample was heavily weighted to public hospitals (78.9%).

> "Most were employed in public hospitals (78.9%) and reported having no prior participation in a formal managerial training programme (63.8%)."
