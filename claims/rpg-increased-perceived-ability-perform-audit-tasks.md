## Evidence

### Wongpinunwatana 2013

Nitaya Wongpinunwatana. (2013). Enhancing Information Systems Auditing Knowledge with Role-Playing Game: An Experimental Investigation. International Education Studies, 6(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n9p72

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-test on the same 75-participant one-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment. Mean perceived ability to perform actual tasks rose from 2.646 to 4.040; the article reports "Statistical significant support" with the printed t value. No effect size is printed.

> "Statistical significant support was obtained for the effects of level of teaching-methods-activity-fit on perceived ability to perform actual tasks (t=16.485, p < 0.001, see table 2)"
