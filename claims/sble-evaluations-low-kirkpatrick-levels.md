# Evaluations of palliative/EOL communication SBLEs cluster at low Kirkpatrick levels, with no level 4 (RESULTS) evaluations reported

## Subclaims

`q2 · i?`

The review's Evaluation section classifies each of the 30 articles by Kirkpatrick level. It reports 16 level 1 assessments, 12 level 2 assessments (all with pre- and postevaluations), 2 level 3, and states "There were no level 4 (RESULTS) reported in the 30 articles reviewed."

> "In 16 of the articles, a level 1 (REACTION) post-training assessment was conducted and typically focused on the user's satisfaction with, perceived value of, or perceived learning from the training."
