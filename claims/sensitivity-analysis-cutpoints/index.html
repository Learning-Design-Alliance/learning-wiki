Evidence¶

Jordan Gutterman 2026¶

Jordan Gutterman, Ashish Gurung, Lee Branstetter, Kenneth Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Cross-Subject Predictive Validity for Learning Outcomes of Delayed Start Behavior. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25308

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Sensitivity analysis (Equation 2) testing dummy cut-points from 4 to 16 minutes: gains were "significant in both subject areas" below 5 minutes and at 13/15 minutes; ELA effects varied less (range .13-.17 SD for 7-14 min cut-points). Benjamini-Hochberg corrections retained significance.

"The cut-points with statistical significance in both subject areas were average delayed starts of less than 5 minutes (Mathβ= .14, p = .02; ELAβ= .18, p =<.01), and at less than 13 or 15 minutes (Mathβ= .16, .20, p = .02, .03; ELAβ= .15, .22, p = .03,<.01)."
