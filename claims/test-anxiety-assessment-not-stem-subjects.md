Massimo Stella. (2020). Forma mentis networks reconstruct how Italian high schoolers and international STEM experts perceive teachers, students, scientists, and school. [doi:10.3390/educsci10010017](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10010017)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of the students' forma mentis network shows anxiety, a target word not among the cues, is a negative concept with a negative aura strongly linked to personal-assessment concepts; the article reports the missing links to physics and maths quoted here.

> "Notice that no associations are made between “anxiety” and “physics” or “maths”, so that the anxiety surrounding these STEM disciplines found in [16] should have a diﬀerent nature in comparison to the test anxiety detected here."
