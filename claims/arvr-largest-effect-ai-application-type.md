# AR/VR tools show the largest effect among AI application types (g = 1.765), while ITS and personalized educational games show small effects

## Subclaims
`q4 i3` AI application type significantly moderated effect sizes (QB = 16.776, p = 0.001); AR/VR had a large effect size (g = 1.765, p < 0.001), exceeding ITS (g = 0.347) and games (g = 0.292). [→ Li 2025](#li-2025)

## Evidence

### Li 2025

Li, S., Zeng, C., Liu, H., Jia, J., Liang, M., Cha, Y., Lim, C. P., & Wu, X. (2025). A meta-analysis of AI-enabled personalized STEM education in schools. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00566-y

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis of AI application types (AR/VR k = 8, ITS k = 61, games k = 23, others k = 7) in the meta-analysis, with AR/VR at "g = 1.765". The "other technologies" category was not statistically significant (g = 0.893, p = 0.078).

> "Among all the studies, AR/VR technology had a large effect size (g = 1.765, p < 0.001). ITS (g = 0.347, p < 0.001) and personalized educational games (g = 0.292, p < 0.001) applied in STEM education for K-12 both had small effect sizes."
