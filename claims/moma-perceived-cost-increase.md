# Both intervention conditions unexpectedly increased students' perceived cost relative to control

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students in both intervention conditions reported higher perceived cost than control students after the intervention, an unexpected effect. [→ Gaspard 2021](#gaspard-2021)

## Evidence

### Gaspard 2021

Gaspard, H., Parrisius, C., Piesch, H., Kleinhansl, M., Wille, E., Nagengast, B., Trautwein, U., & Hulleman, C. S. (2021). The potential of relevance interventions for scaling up: A cluster-randomized trial testing the effectiveness of a relevance intervention in math classrooms. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000663

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the cluster-randomized trial (78 classrooms, N = 1,744), perceived cost was assessed with nine items tapping emotional cost, effort required, and opportunity cost at pretest, posttest, and follow-up. The summary statement reports "higher perceived cost compared with the control group" in both intervention groups and that "other measures were unaffected."

> "At the same time, however, students in both intervention groups reported higher perceived cost compared with the control group, and other measures were unaffected."
