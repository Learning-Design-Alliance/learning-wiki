Ignite Reading program description 2024¶

Ignite Reading program description. (2024). Evidence rating: promising. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/ignite-reading/

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Retrospective matched-comparison study across Massachusetts districts in 2023-24 with over 1,000 first-grade students; tutored students received daily 15-minute virtual sessions while "control groups continued with standard classroom instruction"; the printed effect size is ES = +0.21.

"After one year, tutored students outperformed matched controls on the DIBELS assessment (ES = +0.21), equivalent to 0.9 additional months of learning."
