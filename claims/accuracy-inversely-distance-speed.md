## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski 2023

Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski. (2023). Moving-Target Intelligent Tutoring System for Marksmanship Training. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00308-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post mixed-factor ANOVA in the virtual range experiment. The text reports accuracy at ceiling for 100m-slow targets and around 60% even with eight rounds for 250m-fast targets, and states direction of movement had no effect, so left/right data were collapsed.

> "Shooting accuracy was inversely related to the distance to the target and the speed of the target; that is closer and slower targets were hit with higher accuracy."
