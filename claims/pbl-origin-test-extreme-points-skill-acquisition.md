## Evidence

### Nakhanu and Musasia 2015

Shikuku Beatrice Nakhanu, Amadalo Maurice Musasia. (2015). Problem Based Learning Technique and its effect on Acquisition of Linear Programming Skills by Secondary School Students in Kenya. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.20. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079054

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item 4 of MAT 2 tested forming linear inequalities, getting a linear equation from the inequality, drawing the graph and shading out the unwanted region, with a maximum score of 10 marks. Means were 6.3333 (experimental, N 745) versus 3.8733 (control, N 757); "a signif icance of 0.000 < 0.05" led to rejection of HO2. No effect size is printed.

> "With a t-value of 28.015 which is far greater than 2.37, and a signif icance of 0.000 < 0.05, the results confirm that in this study, the experimental group acquired the linear programm ing skills better than the control group."
