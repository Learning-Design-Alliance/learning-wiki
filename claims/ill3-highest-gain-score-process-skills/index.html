Hardianti 2017¶

Descriptive gain calculation from pretest-posttest mean scores (Table 5: ILL-2 47.42→77.78; ILL-3 42.89→78.44; ILL-4 51.85→69.68). The article reports "the process skill gain score of ILL-3 (which is 0.62)" as highest. Note the article's own inconsistency: Table 6 lists ILL-4 gain as 0.35 while the text says 0.58 for ILL-4 in one comparison.

"Table 6 indicates that the process skill gain score of ILL-3 (which is 0.62) is greater than that of ILL-4 (which is 0.58) and that of ILL-2 (which is 0.58) is greater than that of ILL-4 (which is 0.345)."
