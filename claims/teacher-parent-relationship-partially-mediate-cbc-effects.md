# The teacher-parent relationship improves under CBC and partially mediates CBC effects on several student outcomes

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Teachers reported significantly different rates of improvement in their relationship with parents favoring CBC, and this relationship partially mediated effects of CBC on several student outcomes. [→ Sheridan 2017](#sheridan-2017)

## Evidence

### Sheridan 2017

Sheridan, S. M., Witte, A. L., Holmes, S. R., Coutts, M. J., Dent, A. L., Kunza, G. M., & Wu, C. (2017). A randomized trial examining the effects of conjoint behavioral consultation in rural schools: Student outcomes and the mediating role of the teacher-parent relationship. Journal of School Psychology, 61, 33-53. https://www.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-school-psychology/0022-4405/guide-for-authors

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The teacher-parent relationship was measured with the PTRS-II (α = .94) and mediation was tested with multilevel structural equation modeling including only outcomes with significant Time x Condition effects. No effect size is printed in the available text for the mediation findings.

> "In addition, teacher responses indicated significantly different rates of improvement in their relationship with parents in favor of the CBC group. Finally, the teacher-parent relationship was found to partially mediate effects of CBC on several student outcomes."
