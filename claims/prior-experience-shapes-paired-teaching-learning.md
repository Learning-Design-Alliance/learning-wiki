## Evidence

### Jared B. Stang and Linda E. Strubbe 2015

Jared B. Stang and Linda E. Strubbe. (2015). Paired teaching for faculty professional development in teaching. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1507.05948](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1507.05948)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Cross-case interview analysis in this qualitative study. Instructor A had less than one year of teaching experience and no prior RBIS experience, while Instructors C and D had 10 years of teaching experience; the authors also note experience "could also affect instructors' openness to coaching."

> "Overall, the relatively less experienced novice instructors (A and B) reported learning more skills than the relatively more experienced novice instructors (C and D)."
