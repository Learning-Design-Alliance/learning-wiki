`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A/B test from August 19th to September 11th randomly allocated 9,442 users to page design A and 9,722 users to page design B. The article reports the printed percentage increases for design B over design A; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Compared to the page design A, the page design B is better at promoting all factors of user engagement by increasing 11.07%, 10.29%, 12.57% and 7.19% of the conversion rate, ARPU, total profit and the average number of free questions a user consumed, respectively."
