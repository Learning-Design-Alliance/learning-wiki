## Evidence

### Weisleder Adriana 2013

Weisleder Adriana, Fernald Anne. (2013). Talking to children matters: Early language experience strengthens processing and builds vocabulary. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797613488145

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same correlational analysis of the 29-infant sample: coders classified 5-min recording segments as child-directed or overheard, and overheard adult word rates did not predict later vocabulary. No effect size beyond the printed r is reported; equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast, differences in exposure to overheard speech directed to other adults and children were not related to vocabulary size (r = .25, p = .2)"
