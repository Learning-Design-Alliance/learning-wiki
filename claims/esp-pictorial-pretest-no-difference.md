## Evidence

### Seyyed Mahdi Erfani 2012

Seyyed Mahdi Erfani. (2012). Pictures Speak Louder than Words in ESP, Too! English Language Teaching, 5(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n8p164

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent t-test on pretest scores of the two groups (experimental M=18.04, SD=2.06; control M=17.91, SD=2.18; t-observed=1.16 below t-critical=2.00), confirming the groups "were not significantly different at the outset of the study".

> "While the two groups were not significantly different at the outset of the study, they scored differently on the posttest and the difference was statistically significant."
