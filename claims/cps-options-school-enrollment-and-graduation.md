Trend analysis of six-year graduation rates for ever-Options students (Figure 13, cohort Ns 4,081–5,052), using a six-year frame because Options schools serve students significantly behind on credits; rates rose from 43.2 percent (2007–08 cohort) to 48.9 percent (2014–15 cohort).

> "The six-year high school graduation rate for students who were ever enrolled in an Options school improved by about 5 percentage points between the 2007 and 2014 ninth-grade cohorts. The most significant improvement happened between the 2013 and 2014 cohorts, when the rate increased by more than 3 percentage points."
