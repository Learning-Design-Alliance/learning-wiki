Christina A. Bauer, Aashna Poddar, Eddie Brummelman, & Andrei Cimpian. (2025). The Brilliance–Belonging Model: How Cultural Beliefs About Intellectual Ability Undermine Educational Equity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10034-2

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Jenifer et al. (2023), a correlational gender difference in young children's math self-efficacy tied to FAB endorsement. It also cites Bauer et al. (2023): low-SES university students showed less self-efficacy (preference for easier problems) in a brilliance-oriented but not effort-oriented context.

> "A sample of US girls from the first and second grades reported lower self-efficacy than boys in math to the extent that they endorsed brilliance-oriented FABs about this domain"
