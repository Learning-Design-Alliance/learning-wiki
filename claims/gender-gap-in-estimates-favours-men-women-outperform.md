## Evidence

### Mehdizadeh Leila 2014

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Myers Gil, Khatib Yasmin, Dacre Jane. (2014). How well do doctors think they perform on the General Medical Council's Tests of Competence pilot examinations? A cross-sectional study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2013-004131

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Gender comparison of estimated and actual scores in the cross-sectional study of 524 doctors. The article reports men predicted higher OSCE performance (means 329, SD 59 vs 300, SD 81; t (516)=4.65, p<0.01) while women outperformed men on actual OSCE performance (t (482)=−2.82, p<0.01).

> "Men also predicted a higher overall OSCE performance than did women with mean estimates of 329 (SD 59) and 300 (SD 81), respectively. This was a significant difference; t (516)=4.65, p<0.01. Women outperformed men on OSCE performance and the difference was significant; t (482)=−2.82, p<0.01."
