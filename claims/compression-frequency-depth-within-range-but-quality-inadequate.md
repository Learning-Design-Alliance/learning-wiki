## Evidence

### Pereira RI 2024

Pereira RI, da Silva EMK. (2024). Interdisciplinary training program for pediatric cardiorespiratory arrest using rapid cycle deliberate practice: A descriptive cross-sectional study. São Paulo Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1590/1516-3180.2023.0271.16022024

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Software-recorded analysis of baseline compressions in the training program. Table 2 prints pre-training frequency 109.62 (DP 14.36) and depth 4.41 (DP 0.83) against AHA ranges of 100–120/min and 4–5 cm; the article reports the averages were within range but quality inadequate.

> "The information extracted from the software showed that the average frequency and depth of the chest compressions executed before the intervention, despite being within the ranges determined by the AHA protocols, were of inadequate quality. Therefore, in most resuscitation movements, these two variables are not present simultaneously in the pattern."
