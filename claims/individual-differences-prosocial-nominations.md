## Evidence

### Krijnen 2024

Krijnen, M. A., Wansink, B. G. J., van den Berg, Y. H. M., van Tartwijk, J., & Mainhard, T. (2024). Citizenship in the Elementary Classroom Through the Lens of Peer Relations. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09870-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Boor-Klip et al. (2017), a study of 59 upper elementary classes of approximately 25 children in which "the reported standard deviation was 4 peers" around a mean of 7 prosocial nominations, with some children receiving none and others 20 or more.

> "children were, on average, nominated by 7 classmates as being prosocial; however, the reported standard deviation was 4 peers, indicating clear differences between children."
