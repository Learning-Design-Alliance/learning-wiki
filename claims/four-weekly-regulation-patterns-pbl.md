Model-based clustering (Gaussian mixture models via MoEClust) of each group's weekly tied durations, evaluated across "nine regulation patterns ranging from two to ten". The "solution with four regulation patterns solution was optimal" per the article's fit criteria; Table 2 reports BIC of −6070.49 for the four-cluster solution.
