### Uysal 2026

Uysal, F. (2026). The role of gamification in sustainable teacher education: Examining concept images of curriculum elements using a solomon four-group design. PLOS One, 21(4). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0346892

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null finding from the Solomon four-group experiment, in which one experimental and one control group were pretested and the others were not, testing pretest sensitization. The study reports that 'No significant pretest effects were observed, confirming methodological adequacy'; no effect size was printed for this null result.

> "The findings revealed that gamification-supported teaching had a substantive effect on concept images of curriculum elements, with large effect sizes for content (d = 1.59) and total score (d = 2.04). No significant pretest effects were observed, confirming methodological adequacy."
