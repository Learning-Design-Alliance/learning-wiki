District-level regression accounting for differences between states, with district size measured as total schools (NCES). The report found "kindergarten students scored lower on the i-Ready math assessment in Fall 2023 (B = -.009, SE = .004, p = .039)" and lower improvement odds. No standardized effect size is printed.

"In districts with more schools, kindergarten students scored lower on the i-Ready math assessment in Fall 2023 (B = -.009, SE = .004, p = .039) and had lower odds of improvement across the kindergarten year (B = -.00, SE = .00, p = .029)."
