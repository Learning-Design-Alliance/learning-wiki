Evidence¶

Xiaoqi Wang 2026¶

Xiaoqi Wang, Lawrence Jun Zhang, Yanan Zhang. (2026). Generative artificial intelligence-supported self-regulated learning (GenAI-SRL) in L2 writing: scale development, validation, and short-form construction. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-026-09481-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Exploratory factor analysis (principal axis factoring with varimax rotation) on Sample one (N = 305 Chinese EFL learners) after item analysis retained 34 items. The extracted factors "aligned with the theoretical model" of six regulation dimensions; loadings ranged 0.542–0.786 and six factors explained 60.740% of variance.

"Six dimensions with eigenvalues above one were extracted, accounting for 60.740% of the total variance. Factor loadings ranged from 0.542 to 0.786."

Xiaoqi Wang 2026 (2)¶

Xiaoqi Wang, Lawrence Jun Zhang, Yanan Zhang. (2026). Generative artificial intelligence-supported self-regulated learning (GenAI-SRL) in L2 writing: scale development, validation, and short-form construction. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-026-09481-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Confirmatory factor analysis on the independent Sample two (N = 342) tested the EFA-derived structure. The article reports the six-factor model "fit the data well" with CFI = 0.934, TLI = 0.928, RMSEA = 0.053, and SRMR = 0.042, meeting the stated thresholds.

"The six-factor model fit the data well: χ²(512) = 1008.486, χ²/df = 1.970, CFI = 0.934, TLI = 0.928, RMSEA = 0.053, and SRMR = 0.042."
