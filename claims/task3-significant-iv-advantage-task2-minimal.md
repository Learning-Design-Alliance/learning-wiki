Enhancing Orthopedic Surgical Training With Interactive Photorealistic 3D Visualization. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-task comparison of learning effects (Figure 2) in the RCT; the table reports task 3 β = 15.200, p = 0.047, and the text states "task 3 revealed a significant advantage for IV". No standardized effect size printed.

> "Notably, task 3 revealed a significant advantage for IV, emphasizing the importance of full femur visualization for accurate judgment of 3D position."

### Roni Lekar 2025 (2)

Roni Lekar, Tatiana Gerth, Sergey Prokudin, Matthias Seibold, Reto Bürgin, Benjamin Vella, Armando Hoch, Siyu Tang, Philipp Fürnstahl, and Helmut Grabner. (2025). Enhancing Orthopedic Surgical Training With Interactive Photorealistic 3D Visualization. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same per-task analysis found "minimal differences between groups" for task 2, which the authors attribute to high pre and post scores and the task's clear geometric structure. Task 1 showed only a trend (β = 8.600, p = 0.083).

> "Task 2 exhibited minimal differences between groups, reflecting the task's simplicity (highpre and post scores) and clear geometric structure."
