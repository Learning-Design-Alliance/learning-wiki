## Evidence

### PERKINS 1989

D. N. Perkins and Gavriel Salomon. (1989). Are Cognitive Skills Context-Bound? Educational Researcher, 16-23. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1176259

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Clement's protocol studies of physicists on atypical problems, giving "distinct evidence of seeming use" and "clear evidence of an important role" for general heuristics, but no evidence of transfer or common absence.

> "the experts faced with an unfamiliar prob- lem will often: (a) resort to analogies with systems they understand better; (b) search for potential misanalogies in the analogy; (c) refer to intuitive mental models based on visual and kinesthetic intuition"
