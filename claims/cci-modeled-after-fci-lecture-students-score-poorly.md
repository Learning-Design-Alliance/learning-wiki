### Adrian Madsen 2017

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Eleanor C. Sayre, and Cassandra A. Paul. (2017). Resource Letter RBAI-2: Research-based assessment instruments: Beyond physics topics. American Association of Physics Teachers. [doi:10.1119/1.4977416](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4977416)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review describing the Calculus Concept Inventory, a multiple-choice pre/post assessment of basic calculus principles (functions, derivatives, limits, ratios, the continuum) with no computation. The review reports its design lineage and the documented pattern of poor lecture-course performance, attributed to its developers.

> "The CCI was modeled closely after the Force Concept Inventory11 (FCI), where the questions look trivial to experts, but students in lecture courses score quite poorly on the test."
