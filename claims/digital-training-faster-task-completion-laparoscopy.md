## Evidence

### Geissler Mark Enrik 2024

Geissler Mark Enrik, Bereuter Jean-Paul, Geissler Rona Berit, Bökkerink Guus Mattheus Johannes, Egen Luisa, Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Haney Caelan. (2024). Comparison of distance versus in-person laparoscopy training using a low-cost laparoscopy simulator—a randomized controlled multi-center trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11069-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Final proficiency assessment of the randomized controlled trial (in-person n=21, digital n=19) on the ForceSense system. The digital group's overall time advantage is reported as "control: 1058.1 s vs. intervention: 877.5 s, p value = 0.02". No effect size is printed.

> "The overall task completion time was significantly different between the in-person and the digital training group (control: 1058.1 s vs. intervention: 877.5 s, p value = 0.02) (Fig. 3B)."

### Geissler Mark Enrik 2024 (2)

Geissler Mark Enrik, Bereuter Jean-Paul, Geissler Rona Berit, Bökkerink Guus Mattheus Johannes, Egen Luisa, Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Haney Caelan. (2024). Comparison of distance versus in-person laparoscopy training using a low-cost laparoscopy simulator—a randomized controlled multi-center trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11069-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Task-wise analysis of the same final assessment: only the suture-and-knot task reached significance (p = 0.015), while peg transfer (p = 0.695), circle cutting (p = 0.176), and balloon resection (p = 0.208) did not. No effect size is printed.

> "When analyzing each task independently, the task completion time only differed significantly for the suture and knot task (control: 419 s vs. intervention: 320 s; p value = 0.015) between both measurements."
