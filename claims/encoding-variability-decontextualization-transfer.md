### Shavelson 1986

The review reports, citing DiVesta and Peverly (1984), an experiment manipulating the context in which novel information was learned (e.g., antique dealers) and measuring far transfer as recognizing concept instances in a new context (e.g., magic shows). The review attributes the hypothesis-and-finding to that study.
