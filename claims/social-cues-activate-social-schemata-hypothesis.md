# CASTLE's activation hypothesis states that social cues activate social schemata and trigger social processes

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Social cues in digital materials activate social schemata in learners, which trigger social and parasocial processes. [→ Sascha Schneider 2021](#sascha-schneider-2021)

## Evidence

### Sascha Schneider 2021

Sascha Schneider, Maik Beege, Steve Nebel, Lenka Schnaubert, Günter Daniel Rey. (2021). The Cognitive-Affective-Social Theory of Learning in digital Environments (CASTLE). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09626-5

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical derivation in the review, drawn from the CASA paradigm and media equation theory. The article states the hypothesis as its own formulation, noting that "media equation effects depend on individual differences" and anthropomorphic features of the environment. No effect size is offered.

> "Thus, individual learning can be influenced by social cues in digital technologies leading to the following hypothesis: Activation Hypothesis: Social cues activate social schemata and trigger social processes."
