## Evidence

### Sun 2026

Sun, C., Major, L., Daltry, R., Obradovic, L., & Friedberg, A. (2026). Enhancing literacy and numeracy through teacher dashboards and digital personalised learning: an A/B/C test in Kenyan early years education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10703-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square tests with Bonferroni corrections compared teacher accuracy ratings across the two dashboard partitions, based on 25,112 ratings from 5,576 rated learners in 1,184 schools. The Grouped Dashboard received significantly fewer 'yes' and more 'no' ratings.

> "teachers in the Grouped Dashboard partition provided significantly fewer “yes” ratings (χ 2 (1, N = 15,609) = 69.65, p <.001) and more “no” ratings (χ 2 (1, N = 6501) = 90.17, p <.001), compared to List Dashboard."
