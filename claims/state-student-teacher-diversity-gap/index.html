q2 · i? · associational · r1

A descriptive bar chart compares percentages of students of color versus teachers of color in seven states (e.g., California 77% students of color vs. 34% teachers of color; Texas 73% vs. 39%), drawn from the 2017-18 and 2018-19 state profiles. The brief states these data "paint a clear picture of the need to diversify the teacher workforce."
