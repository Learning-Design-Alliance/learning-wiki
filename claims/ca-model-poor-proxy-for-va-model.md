### George Leckie and Lucy Prior 2022

George Leckie and Lucy Prior. (2022). A Comparison of Value-Added Models for School Accountability. [doi:10.1080/09243453.2022.2032763](https://doi.org/10.1080/09243453.2022.2032763)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Specific comparison within the 2019 English census refits, relevant to cohorts whose start-of-phase tests were cancelled. The article adds that CA effects correlate more strongly with Raw effects (.902) and that even early achievement plus sociodemographics correlates with VA at just .811.

> "This model has a considerably higher percentage of moderate or large school effects than that for the original VA model including prior achievement (47.1 vs. 40.4) and the correlation between the CA and VA school effects is just .739 (not shown)."
