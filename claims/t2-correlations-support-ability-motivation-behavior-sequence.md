The impact of the flipped classroom model on college students' physical exercise behavior: a chain mediation effect of self-directed learning ability and exercise motivation. `q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis using T2 data in the quasi-experimental sample supported the suggested ability-motivation-behavior progressive process. Correlations of r = 0.48 (SDL-AM) and r = 0.41 (AM-PA) were both significant; the SDL-PA association was marginally weaker than ability-motivation.

> "at T2, SDL ability exhibited a substantial positive correlation with AM (r = 0.48, p < 0.001). AM had a substantial positive correlation with physical exercise behaviors (Total PA, MVPA) (r = 0.41, p < 0.001)."
