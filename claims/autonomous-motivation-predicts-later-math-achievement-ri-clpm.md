## Evidence

### Santana‐Monagas 2025

Santana‐Monagas, E., Núñez‐Regueiro, F., & Núñez, J. L. (2025). Does motivation lead to academic success, or conversely? Reciprocal relations between autonomous and controlled motivation, and mathematics achievement. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 513–529. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12736

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results of a three-wave random intercept cross-lagged panel model (Model 1) in a longitudinal study of 1042 secondary mathematics students across three trimesters. The article reports that "RI‐CLPM results showed a cross‐lagged effect of autonomous motivation to achievement" with β2 = .116 (T1–T2) and .140 (T2–T3), both p = .002; no effect size beyond these standardized coefficients is printed.

> "Regarding cross‐lagged effects, RI‐CLPM results showed a cross‐lagged effect of autonomous motivation to achievement from T1 to T2 (β2 = .116, p = .002) and from T2 to T3 (β2 = .140, p = .002)."
