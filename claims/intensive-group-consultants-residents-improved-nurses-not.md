`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon test comparisons from 0 to 12 months in the eleven intensive-education hospitals (Section 3.2, Table 3). The article reports significance for consultants and residents only; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The scores on the management of asthma attack and IT through facemask and mouthpiece improved significantly from the beginning (0 months) till the end of intensive intervention (12 months) in the subgroups of consultants (p:0.003, p:0.006, and p:0.021, respectively) and residents (p:0.008, p:0.011, and p:0.027, respectively) but not the nurses (p > 0.005 in all cases)."
