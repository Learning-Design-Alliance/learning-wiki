## Evidence

### Karen Masters 2016

Karen Masters, Eun Young Oh, Joe Cox, Brooke Simmons, Chris Lintott, Gary Graham, Anita Greenhill, Kate Holmes. (2016). Science Learning via Participation in Online Citizen Science. Journal of Science Communication (JCOM), Special Issue: Citizen Science, Part II. [doi:10.22323/2.15030207](https://doi.org/10.22323/2.15030207)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression of the VOLCROWE survey (N=1921 volunteers across five Zooniverse projects) with project-specific quiz score as dependent variable, controlling for demographics and attitudes. The article reports "a positive and significant (at 99% level) association" for three engagement measures; doubling active days associates with an 8% increase in score.

> "We find that three out of the four measures of engagement show a positive and significant (at 99% level) association with this score"
