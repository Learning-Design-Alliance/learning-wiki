## Evidence

### Vrillon Agathe 2022

Vrillon Agathe, Gonzales-Marabal Laurent, Ceccaldi Pierre-François, Plaisance Patrick, Desrentes Eric, Paquet Claire, Dumurgier Julien. (2022). Using virtual reality in lumbar puncture training improves students learning experience. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03317-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-session survey of all 168 participants assessing comfort and adverse symptoms. The article reports "Cybersickness was experienced by 5% of subjects" and "No user had to interrupt the viewing due to adverse symptoms"; no effect size is printed.

> "Comfort score during video exposure was overall high, rated M = 4.5 ± 0.8 on 5 (Table 2). Scores of dizziness, headache and eye discomfort remained low"
