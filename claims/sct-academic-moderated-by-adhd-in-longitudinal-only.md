## Evidence

### Hossain Bushra 2022

Hossain Bushra, Bent Stephen, Parenteau China, Widjaja Felicia, Davis Matthew, Hendren Robert L. (2022). The Associations Between Sluggish Cognitive Tempo, Internalizing Symptoms, and Academic Performance in Children With Reading Disorder: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547221085493

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted longitudinal mixed-effects model (N = 138) of within-individual change in teacher-rated academic performance in children with RD. The article reports significant moderation by ADHD-IN (p = .03), with the negative association present only at low ADHD-IN levels.

> "ADHD-IN showed a small but significant effect modification on the longitudinal relationship (p = .03), whereby the negative association between SCT and academic performance was seen in individuals with lower levels of ADHD-IN but not in individuals with higher levels of ADHD-IN"

### Hossain Bushra 2022 (2)

Hossain Bushra, Bent Stephen, Parenteau China, Widjaja Felicia, Davis Matthew, Hendren Robert L. (2022). The Associations Between Sluggish Cognitive Tempo, Internalizing Symptoms, and Academic Performance in Children With Reading Disorder: A Longitudinal Cohort Study. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/10870547221085493

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted cross-sectional regression at baseline (moderation term N = 121). The article reports no significant interaction of ADHD-IN with teacher-reported SCT for academic performance at baseline (moderation p = .06 in Table 2).

> "There was no significant interaction effect of ADHD-IN on the relationship, suggesting that ADHD-IN does not moderate the relationship between teacher-reported SCT and academic performance at baseline"
