Evidence¶

Gandolfi 2021¶

Gandolfi, E., Traverso, L., Zanobini, M., Usai, M. C., & Viterbori, P. (2021). The longitudinal relationship between early inhibitory control skills and emergent literacy in preschool children. Reading and Writing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-021-10131-y

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Zero-order Pearson correlations between Time 1 measures and Time 2 emergent literacy measures for both subsamples (Tables 2 and 3). At 4–5 years the Fish task correlated with the phonological awareness score (r = .554, p = .005), minimal pairs, rhyme detection and orthographic knowledge (r = .486, p = .014); at 5–6 years with phonological awareness (r = .530, p = .029) and orthographic knowledge (r = .549, p = .023).

"the early interference suppression task was significantly associated with most of the emergent literacy outcomes, while the response inhibition task was not."
