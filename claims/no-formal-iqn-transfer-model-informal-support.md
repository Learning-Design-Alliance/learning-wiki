## Evidence

### Kurup Chanchal 2026

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2026). Optimising the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Insights From Recruiting Managers' Perspectives. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70496

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Hospital transfer support analysis (n = 26 codes) found 'no transfer model' coded 21 times (81%); resource constraints and absent funding were cited as barriers to change.

> "Participants consistently emphasised that IQNs enter the workforce without dedicated transition programs or tailored induction."

### Kurup Chanchal 2026 (2)

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2026). Optimising the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Insights From Recruiting Managers' Perspectives. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70496

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Informal/ad hoc support was coded 13 times (50% of n = 26); examples included temporarily shielding IQNs from night shifts or complex tasks, which the article says were not sufficient substitutes for structured support.

> "Colleagues and managers would provide guidance as needed, but this assistance was inconsistent and dependent on individual goodwill rather than institutional policy."
