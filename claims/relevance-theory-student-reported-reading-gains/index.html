He 2018¶

Ping He. (2018). On Reading Comprehension Teaching for English Majors under Relevance Theory. English Language Teaching, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n1p46

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Post-experiment questionnaire survey of the two classes, reported alongside examination results and interviews. The experimental class attributed faster, more adequate reading to background information and clearer reading objectives; the control class reported confusion with unfamiliar themes. Self-reported, no statistics printed.
