## Evidence

### Burn H 2020

Burn H, Puri L, Roshan A, Singh SK, Burton MJ. (2020). Primary Eye Care in Eastern Nepal. Ophthalmic Epidemiology 27(3):165-176. https://doi.org/10.1080/09286586.2019.1702217

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative assessment of 107 PHCWs and direct equipment audit of 35 health posts. Table 3 prints 15/107 (14.0%) trained in visual acuity testing; Table 5 shows distance vision charts in 2 of 16 Siraha posts and none in the other districts. Among trained staff, many could still measure acuity.

> "The number of PHCWs who had been trained to measure visual acuity was very low (14%) and the number of HPs equipped with a distance vision chart was even lower (5.7%). This basic skill is an important component of PEC and is largely missing from PEC provision in this region"
