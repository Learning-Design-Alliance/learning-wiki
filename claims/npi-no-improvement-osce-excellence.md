## Evidence

### Shaddeau Angela 2015

Shaddeau Angela, Nimz Abigail, Sheeder Jeanelle, Tocce Kristina. (2015). Effect of novel patient interaction on students’ performance of pregnancy options counseling. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v20.29401

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 135 third-year medical students at one school, randomized by block to NPI plus standard curriculum or standard curriculum only, assessed by blinded standardized patients on a validated OSCE. The article reports "24% of the NPI group vs. 28% of controls (p=0.57)" for the primary outcome.
