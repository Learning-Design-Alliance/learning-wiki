Evidence¶

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal 2012¶

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal. (2012). Comparative Effects of Multisensory and Metacognitive Instructional Approaches on English Vocabulary Achievement of Underachieving Nigerian Secondary School Students. International Education Studies, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n1p18

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA of pronunciation subtest scores in the 120-student quasi-experiment, pretest as covariate. The article reports "the calculated f-value 26.84 was significant"; no effect size is named in the reporting text.

"Table 3 shows that the calculated f-value 26.84 was significant at p>o.o5. The hypothesis is therefore rejected. This result indicates that all the instructional approaches had significant effect on students’ pronunciation achievement."

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal 2012 (2)¶

Folakemi.O. Adeniyi; R. Adebayo Lawal. (2012). Comparative Effects of Multisensory and Metacognitive Instructional Approaches on English Vocabulary Achievement of Underachieving Nigerian Secondary School Students. International Education Studies, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v5n1p18

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Duncan Multiple Range Test on pronunciation means (Table 4): MSIA 12.733 (A), MCIA 11.200 (B), MSIA+MCIA 11.300 (B), CIA 8.733 (C), 30 cases per group.

"Table 4 shows that MSIA which is rated ‘A’ is the most effective instructional ap proach for teaching English pronunciation to underachieving students."
