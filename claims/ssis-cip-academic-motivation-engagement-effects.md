## Evidence

### DiPerna 2014

DiPerna, J. C., Lei, P., Bellinger, J., & Cheng, W. (2014). Efficacy of the Social Skills Improvement System – Classwide Intervention Program (SSIS – CIP) in the Primary Grades. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562704

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Teacher ratings in the CRT (ACES Motivation subscale), analyzed with multilevel models adjusting for pretest and covariates. Table 4 prints the effect size .34 for academic motivation (adjusted standardized difference), which the article characterizes as small-medium per Cohen's criterion; the article concludes "SSIS-CIP positively impacted academic engagement and motivation".

> "Academic Motivation  3.564 (.428) 3.351 (.497) 3.797 (.320) 3.364 (.454) .34"

### DiPerna 2014 (2)

DiPerna, J. C., Lei, P., Bellinger, J., & Cheng, W. (2014). Efficacy of the Social Skills Improvement System – Classwide Intervention Program (SSIS – CIP) in the Primary Grades. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562704

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Teacher ratings in the CRT (ACES Engagement subscale), analyzed with multilevel models adjusting for pretest and covariates. Table 4 prints the effect size .29 for academic engagement (adjusted standardized difference), a small effect by the article's Cohen-criterion framing.

> "Academic Engagement  3.901 (.444) 3.589 (.481) 4.148 (.350) 3.674 (.529) .29"

### DiPerna 2014 (3)

DiPerna, J. C., Lei, P., Bellinger, J., & Cheng, W. (2014). Efficacy of the Social Skills Improvement System – Classwide Intervention Program (SSIS – CIP) in the Primary Grades. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562704

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel models included product terms between SSIS-CIP and student-level pretest and demographics; the student pretest by condition interaction on academic motivation was significant and retained in the final model (coefficient -.179, p < .01 in Table 5).

> "Moreover, there was a statistically significant interaction between SSIS-CIP and student-level pretest on teacher ratings of academic motivation."
