# High-fidelity simulation outperforms traditional training in skill performance (SMD 1.63) and knowledge (SMD 0.69) immediately after training

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Compared with traditional training, high-fidelity simulation shows a large benefit in skill performance (SMD 1.63; 95% CI 0.49–2.77) with high heterogeneity (I2 = 90%). [→ Huang Jichong 2019](#huang-jichong-2019)
`q3 i2` Compared with traditional training, high-fidelity simulation shows a moderate benefit in neonatal resuscitation knowledge (SMD 0.69; 95% CI 0.42–0.96). [→ Huang Jichong 2019 (2)](#huang-jichong-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Huang Jichong 2019

Huang Jichong, Tang Ying, Tang Jun, Shi Jing, Wang Hua, Xiong Tao, Xia Bin, Zhang Li, Qu Yi, Mu Dezhi. (2019). Educational efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in neonatal resuscitation training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1763-z

`q3 · i3`

Meta-analysis of three RCTs comparing high-fidelity simulation with traditional training. The pooled skill-performance effect size was SMD = 1.63 (95% CI 0.49–2.77), a large benefit favoring high-fidelity simulation, with heterogeneity I2 = 90%.

> "The pooled results by meta-analysis showed a large benefit with high-fidelity simulation when compared with traditional training in skill performance (SMD 1.63; 95% CI 0.49–2.77)"

### Huang Jichong 2019 (2)

Huang Jichong, Tang Ying, Tang Jun, Shi Jing, Wang Hua, Xiong Tao, Xia Bin, Zhang Li, Qu Yi, Mu Dezhi. (2019). Educational efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in neonatal resuscitation training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1763-z

`q3 · i2`

Meta-analysis of the same three RCTs comparing high-fidelity simulation with traditional training on neonatal resuscitation knowledge. The pooled effect size was SMD = 0.69 (95% CI 0.42–0.96), a moderate benefit.

> "a moderate benefit in neonatal resuscitation knowledge (SMD 0.69; 95% CI 0.42–0.96) with high-fidelity simulation when compared with traditional training"
