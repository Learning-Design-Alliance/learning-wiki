## Evidence

### Young-Suk Grace Kim 2024

Young-Suk Grace Kim, Dandan Yang, Jinkyung Hwang. (2024). Are Mathematics and Writing Skills Related? Evidence from Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09960-4

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis (Robust Variance Estimation) of 211 studies with 564 effect sizes and 1,207,983 participants, using Fisher's z-transformed Pearson correlations. The pooled effect size was "moderate: r = 0.48" with significant heterogeneity (Q = 24,126.39, I-square = 98%).

> "The random-effects model showed that the overall correlation between mathematics and writing skills was moderate: r = 0.48 (t = 23.87, 95% CI [0.45, 0.52], p < 0.001)."
