Multiple regression analysis (Table 5) controlling for demographics and academic year in the 210-student sample. Significant predictors included age 21–23 (B = 0.35, p = 0.004), age 24+ (B = 0.50, p = 0.007), males (B = 0.40, p = 0.001), and fourth/fifth year (B = 0.40/0.50, p = 0.001). "Male students had a notable positive effect on academic achievement (B = 0.40, 95% CI = [0.20, 0.60], p = 0.001)" while the female coefficient was non-significant.

> "Male students had a notable positive effect on academic achievement (B = 0.40, 95% CI = [0.20, 0.60], p = 0.001), while female students showed a weaker and non-significant relationship (B = 0.20, 95% CI = [−0.05, 0.45], p = 0.10)."
