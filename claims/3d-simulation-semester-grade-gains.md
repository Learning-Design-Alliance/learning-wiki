## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group's semester grades improved significantly from pretest to post-test (67.4 ± 6.2 to 71.9 ± 7.9, P < .001) while the control group's declined (62.8 ± 2.8 to 60.5 ± 7.6, P = .037). [→ Hsu Ming-Sung 2025](#hsu-ming-sung-2025)

## Evidence

### Hsu Ming-Sung 2025

Hsu Ming-Sung, Yeh Chun-Liang, Cheng Shih-Jung, Lin Chun-Pin. (2025). Transforming Dental Morphology Education: The Impact of Three-Dimensional Digital Simulation on Practical Skill Development. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.103904

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired-sample t tests on semester grades (pretest = previous-semester grade, post-test = current-semester grade) in the RCT. The experimental group "significantly improved" while the control group declined; only p-values and means are printed, no effect size.

> "In the experimental group, mean scores significantly improved from 67.4 ± 6.2 to 71.9 ± 7.9 (P < .001). In contrast, the control group showed a decline from 62.8 ± 2.8 to 60.5 ± 7.6 (P = .037)"

### Hsu Ming-Sung 2025 (2)

Hsu Ming-Sung, Yeh Chun-Liang, Cheng Shih-Jung, Lin Chun-Pin. (2025). Transforming Dental Morphology Education: The Impact of Three-Dimensional Digital Simulation on Practical Skill Development. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2025.103904

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t test comparing post-test semester grades between the experimental (n = 43) and control (n = 43) groups in the RCT; the between-group test was significant at P < .001 with no printed effect size.

> "Post-test grades between the two groups differed significantly (P < .001), indicating a positive effect of the digital course on academic performance."
