## Evidence

### Dziadzko Mikhail 2024

Dziadzko Mikhail, Varvinskiy Andrey, Di Loreto Rodolphe, Scipioni Hugues, Ateleanu Bazil, Klimek Markus, Berger-Estilita Joana. (2024). Examiner workload comparison: three structured oral examination formats for the European diploma in anaesthesiology and intensive care. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2364990

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective observational study of EDAIC Part 2 examiners in 2023; NASA-TLX collected at the end of each examination day via an electronic survey. The article reports "215 workload measurements in 142 examiners" with a high mean score of 59.61 ± 14.13.

> "The overall NASA TLX score from 215 workload measurements in 142 examiners was high at 59.61 ± 14.13."

### Dziadzko Mikhail 2024 (2)

Dziadzko Mikhail, Varvinskiy Andrey, Di Loreto Rodolphe, Scipioni Hugues, Ateleanu Bazil, Klimek Markus, Berger-Estilita Joana. (2024). Examiner workload comparison: three structured oral examination formats for the European diploma in anaesthesiology and intensive care. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2364990

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-format comparison from the Results section using ANOVA/Kruskal-Wallis with post-hoc tests across F2F (n=38), hybrid (n=73) and online (n=104) measurements. The online format differed from hybrid by "5.3 points difference" (p = 0.0363) but was comparable to F2F.

> "The mean overall taskload was high at 59,61 ± 14.13, more important for online examination type, significant comparing to hybrid (5.3 points difference, 95%CI [0.27 to 10.36], p = 0.0363), and comparable to the F2F type."
