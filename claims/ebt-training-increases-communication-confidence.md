## Evidence

### Shortlidge Erin E. 2018

Shortlidge Erin E., Eddy Sarah L. (2018). The trade-off between graduate student research and teaching: A myth? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0199576

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey regression analysis of the same 338-student sample (best-fit linear regression: research communication index ~ training in EBT index). The article reports the positive EBT coefficient and that GTA financial support "was not selected to be included in the best models".

> "Student training in EBT increased student confidence in communicating their research (0.10 ± 0.029, t = 3.45, p = 0.0006, Fig 2). Interestingly, the amount of financial support from teaching was not selected to be included in the best models and even in the models it was in the term was not significant."
