## Evidence

### Alghadir Ahmad H. 2020

Alghadir Ahmad H., Gabr Sami A., Iqbal Zaheen A. (2020). Effect of Gender, Physical Activity and Stress-Related Hormones on Adolescent’s Academic Achievements. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17114143

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Hormone comparison across the three PA groups in the same 150-participant cross-sectional study; cortisol measured in saliva and serotonin in serum by competitive ELISA 2–3 months before examinations. Table 2 shows cortisol falling from 58.6 ± 6.9 pg/mL (mild boys) to 29.1 ± 8.0 (active boys) and serotonin rising from 38.44 ± 9.8 to 59.25 ± 6.9 ng/mL.

> "The participants with moderate to active PA showed a significant (p < 0.001) reduction in the levels of salivary cortisol and a release in the levels of serum serotonin compared to subjects with lower or mild PA, who represent abnormal levels of adrenal hormones (Table 2)."
