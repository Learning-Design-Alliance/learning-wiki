`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The dissertation reports, citing a large-scale log analysis of 6.9 million MOOC video-viewing sessions, that the fraction viewed decreased as videos became longer and median engagement was less than half the duration for videos longer than nine minutes. The review attributes this to the cited prior work; the underlying study was not read here.
