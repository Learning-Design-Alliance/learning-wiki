The guidelines narrate a randomized controlled study of multisource feedback on communication skills and professionalism in which paediatrics residents performed self-assessments, received reports of parent and nurse evaluations, and underwent tailored coaching. Nurse ratings "increased for residents receiving multisource feedback and decreased for the control group".

> "Nurse ratings of residents’ communication skills, timeliness, and demonstration of responsibility and accountability increased for residents receiving multisource feedback and decreased for the control group [55]."
