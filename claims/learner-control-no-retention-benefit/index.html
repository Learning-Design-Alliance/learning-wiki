Evidence¶

Huib K. Tabbers 2010¶

Huib K. Tabbers, Bastiaan de Koeijer. (2010). Learner control in animated multimedia instructions. Instructional Science, 38, 441–453. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-009-9119-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the same randomized experiment, retention of the eight lightning-formation steps did not differ between groups; the article reports "not significant" with t(50) = 0.79, p = .44, and d = 0.22 (d = 0.15 when prior knowledge was subtracted).

"The difference between the two experimental groups on retention was not significant ( t(50) = 0.79, p = .44, Cohen's effect size d = 0.22), not even if the prior knowledge score was subtracted from the retention score (t(50) = 0.55; p = .58, d = 0.15)."
