Evidence¶

Sunny Yu 2026¶

Sunny Yu, Myra Cheng, Ahmad Jabbar, Ilia Sucholutsky, Katherine M. Collins, Dan Jurafsky, Robert D. Hawkins. (2026). Cognitive offloading and the speedup illusion in human-AI interaction. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23177

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Completion-sample participants answered the 5-question NASA-TLX after each task. The article reports "it reduced mental effort for all tasks", a 0.61-point reduction on the 7-point scale, significant for 15 of 24 tasks, while completion time and NASA-TLX showed only a weak positive correlation (r=0.26).

"while AI assistance sped up completion of only a few tasks, it reduced mental effort for all tasks — on average, the NASA-TLX average (higher means more effortful) lowered by 0.61 points on a 7-point scale (SE=0.059,t=−10.38,p<0.001)."
