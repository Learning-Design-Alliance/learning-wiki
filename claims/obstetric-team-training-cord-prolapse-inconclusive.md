### Brogaard L 2022

Brogaard L, Glerup Lauridsen K, Løfgren B, et al. (2022). The effects of obstetric emergency team training on patient outcome: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14263

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of two observational studies evaluating training for umbilical cord prolapse using low Apgar scores at 5 minutes as endpoint. Certainty was downgraded to very low for risk of bias, inconsistency, and imprecision.

> "The studies had a combined OR of 1.31 (95% CI 0.11–15.96) with a substantial risk of heterogeneity (I 2 = 62%)."
