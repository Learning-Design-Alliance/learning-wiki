Evidence¶

Fan Yang · Jill E. Stefaniak 2025¶

Fan Yang · Jill E. Stefaniak. (2025). An exploration of instructional designers' prioritizations for integrating ChatGPT in design practice. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10509-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Inductive thematic analysis of challenge responses (N = 26) identified three themes; the article states "Table 4 presents a total of three types of challenges, including the quality of the output, tool limitations, and faculty unpreparedness." Output quality concerns included hallucinations, bias, and nonexistent references; tool limitations included prompt misunderstanding and tone problems.
