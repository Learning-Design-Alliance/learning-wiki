## Evidence

### Steenstra I 2026

Steenstra I, Tseng L-W, Miller KH, Diallo M, Moulis L, Badreddine M, Carbonnel F, Tostain J-B. (2026). Efficacy of an e-Learning Module on Endocrine Disruptors for Family Medicine Residents: Matched Before-And-After Cohort Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/89880

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Posttraining satisfaction questionnaire on a 5-point Likert scale administered to the 78 matched participants. The study reports "a mean of 4.0/5 (SD 0.9)" overall satisfaction, with 77% (60/78) satisfied with module length.

> "Overall satisfaction with the 4 modules (kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and living room, and outdoors) was rated at a mean of 4.0/5 (SD 0.9). The suitability of the e-learning format was rated at a mean of 4.1/5 (SD 1.0), and the extent to which the training achieved its objectives was rated at a mean of 4.3/5 (SD 0.8)."
