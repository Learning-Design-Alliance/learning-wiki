Qualitative strand findings on self-regulated teaching and reflection from teacher reflections and artifacts in the professional development study. One participant explained, "After lessons, I began asking AI reflective questions about student engagement and learning difficulties." The recurring planning, monitoring, evaluation, and revision cycle was interpreted as teacher-level self-regulation.

"After lessons, I began asking AI reflective questions about student engagement and learning difficulties. It helped me think more critically about my teaching."
