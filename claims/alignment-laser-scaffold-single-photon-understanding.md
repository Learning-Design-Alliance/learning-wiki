Post-interview reflection from a student in the think-aloud lab study. Luce explained that seeing wave behavior in the alignment laser and then the same behavior with single photons "really helped me understand that there's a dual nature."

> "having seen— set it up myself and seen that what I internalized as like wave behavior... was also observed on something that should just be a single particle really helped me understand that there's a dual nature."
