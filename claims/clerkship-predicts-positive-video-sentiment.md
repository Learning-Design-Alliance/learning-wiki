## Evidence

### Scherff Aline D. 2025

Scherff Aline D., Kääb Stefan, Fischer Martin R., Berndt Markus. (2025). EYE-ECG2: Addressing medical student feedback in an RCT with eye-tracking videos featuring cued retrospective reporting and modified learning sequences for ECG interpretation skills training. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001771

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square tests of participant characteristics against manually categorised feedback sentiment (negative/neutral/positive) from 35 of 47 INT students found only prior cardiological clerkship significant; "all of the negative sentiments were given by students without" a clerkship. No effect size was printed.

> "Singularly prior cardiological clerkship was a distinguishing factor in how the EYE-ECG2 video was received by participants (χ2(2)=7.57, p=.03). Notably, all of the negative sentiments were given by students without the experience of a prior cardiological clerkship."
