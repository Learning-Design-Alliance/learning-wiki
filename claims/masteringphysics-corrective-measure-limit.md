### Allan L. Alinea and Wade Naylor 2014

Allan L. Alinea and Wade Naylor. (2014). Polarisation of physics on global courses. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/50/2/210](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/50/2/210)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Authors' interpretation of the contrast between high homework averages (88%) and persistent polarisation on FCI force-identification items. The article notes MasteringPhysics provides hints and a limited number of tries, and the authors explicitly flag that more data are needed.

> "Although we may need more data, our preliminary study here suggests that the “corrective measure” of the system of MasteringPhysics may not be able to correct deeply ingrained student misconceptions/preconceptions about force."
