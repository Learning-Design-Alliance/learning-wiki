## Evidence

### David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter 2021

David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter. (2021). Gender-grade-gap zeroed out under a specific intro-physics assessment regime. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

HLM analysis of URM grade gaps (3,173 URM and 13,729 nonURM students): Usual N = 15,313 gap -0.375, M1Q N = 603 gap -0.328, M2Q N = 293 gap -0.56, all with P < 10^-3. The article reports the gaps as statistically indistinguishable.

> "These URM grade gaps are statistically indistinguishable so these changes in the assessment scheme toward mastery learning have negligible effect on URM grade gaps"
