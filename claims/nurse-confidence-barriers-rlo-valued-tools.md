## Evidence

### Singh‐Weldon J. 2022

Singh‐Weldon J., Tsianakas V., Murrells T., Grealish A. (2022). Preparing children's nurses for working with children and adolescents who self‐harm: Evaluating the ‘our care through our eyes’ e‐learning training package. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.13041

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thematic analysis (Ritchie framework) of open comments from 19 of the 22 post-training participants. All but four cited lack of confidence and knowledge as barriers; "Tools that demonstrated techniques in initiating conversations and listening were highly rated".

> "All participants found elements of the training beneficial to their practice, particularly the resources that could be accessed online. Tools that demonstrated techniques in initiating conversations and listening were highly rated by many participants."
