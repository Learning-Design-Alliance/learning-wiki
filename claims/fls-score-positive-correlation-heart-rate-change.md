## Evidence

### Iwabuchi Serena 2025

Iwabuchi Serena, Okata Yuichi, Kameoka Yasuyuki, Miyauchi Harunori, Yoshimura Shohei, Komyo Yuki, Kajihara Keisuke, Nakai Yumiko, Bitoh Yuko. (2025). Initial adaptation and surgical performance of the hinotori™ robotic system among pediatric surgeons with minimal robotic exposure. Pediatric Surgery International. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-025-06212-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory correlation analysis across all 20 trials of the ten-surgeon simulation study, with no adjustment for multiple comparisons. The article reports "r = 0.676, 95% CI 0.33–0.86, p = 0.0011" and states sensitivity analyses addressing SAA outliers did not alter these findings.

> "Across all 20 trials (Robo + Lap), the FLS score was positively correlated with ΔHR (r = 0.676, 95% CI 0.33–0.86, p = 0.0011; Spearman's ρ = 0.769, p = 7.3 × 10⁻5). Similar correlations were observed within each modality (Robo r = 0.732, p = 0.016; Lap r = 0.643, p = 0.045)."
