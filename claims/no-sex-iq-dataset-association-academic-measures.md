## Evidence

### Roell M. 2022

Roell M., Bellon E., Polspoel B., Declercq M. & De Smedt B. (2022). Investigating the association between variability in sulcal pattern and academic achievement. Scientific Reports 12, 12323. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-15335-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary analyses in the combined grade 4 dataset: the article concludes "neither sex nor IQ were associated with the academic skills measured in this study" — sex not associated with arithmetic (t(87) = - 1.65, p = .10), symbolic number (p = .46) or reading (p = .98); IQ correlations r(87) = 0.10, r(84) = - 0.05, r(62) = 0.03, all non-significant; no dataset differences (all p ≥ .52).

> "To conclude, neither sex nor IQ were associated with the academic skills measured in this study."
