## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The intensive intervention group (≥3 BIM sessions) scored higher than the control group in summarization, self-directed learning, and objective evaluation, dimensions not significantly improved by a single session. [→ Liu Qiqi 2025](#liu-qiqi-2025)

## Evidence

### Liu Qiqi 2025

Liu Qiqi, Ma Jixuan, Wu Chuansha, Du Yang, Chen Ting, Sun Xiaojie, Zhao Qin, Liu Yunhao, Shi Yuqin, Gu Yingying, Zhou Min, Zhang Ling, Zhou Ting. (2025). The effects of single and multiple BOPPPS-integrated model sessions on public health students' competences: a two-stage study combining quasi-experimental and cross-sectional analyses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07508-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 224 valid responses grouped by BIM frequency (control n=96, basic n=66, intensive n=62); one-way ANOVA with LSD post-hoc tests. Self-directed learning F = 8.56, P < 0.01; objective evaluation F = 10.46, P < 0.01; summarization F = 4.78, P = 0.01. No effect sizes printed.

> "The intensive intervention group surpassed the control group in these dimensions as well as in summarization, self-directed learning, and objective evaluation (P < 0.05)."
