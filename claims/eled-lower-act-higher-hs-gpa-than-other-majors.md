## Evidence

### Nixon Ryan S. 2025

Nixon Ryan S., Bailey Elizabeth Gibbons. (2025). Comparing Academic Performance of Elementary Education Majors in General Education Science Courses. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320137

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA on ACT scores by MAJOR TYPE in the archival sample was significant, F(5, 1183215) = 730.20, p < 0.001; Tukey post-hoc comparisons showed the ELED mean of 26.56 was significantly lower than Business, Humanities, and STEM means.

> "the mean score for ELED majors (M =  26.56, SD =  3.26, N =  7296) was significantly lower than the mean score for Business (M =  27.47, SD =  3.49, N =  26631)"

### Nixon Ryan S. 2025 (2)

Nixon Ryan S., Bailey Elizabeth Gibbons. (2025). Comparing Academic Performance of Elementary Education Majors in General Education Science Courses. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320137

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA on high school GPA by MAJOR TYPE was significant, F(5, 170121) = 340.01, p < 0.001; Tukey post-hoc comparisons showed the ELED mean GPA of 3.81 was "significantly higher than all other major types".

> "post-hoc Tukey comparisons between ELED and the other majors showed that the mean GPA for ELED majors (M =  3.81, SD =  0.21, N =  6903) was significantly higher than all other major types"
