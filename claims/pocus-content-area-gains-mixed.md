# In one single-group pre-post study, knowledge scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in five of six content areas after the POCUS course, but did not change significantly for the suprapubic/pelvic view

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In one single-group pre-post study, learners' scores rose significantly from pre-test to post-test in five content areas (e.g., POCUS fundamentals d = 1.09; pleural interface d = 0.94), while scores for the suprapubic/pelvic view did not change significantly (p = 0.072, d = 0.24). ## Evidence

### Weber Ariana 2025

Weber Ariana, Misra Asit, Rodriguez Richard D., Brotons Angel "Al", Mosetti Maria Antonietta, Lewiss Resa E., Tiozzo Eduard, Motola Ivette. (2025). Effectiveness of a simulation-based point-of-care ultrasound course for prehospital providers - a single group quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07675-w

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Item analysis of pre- and post-course assessments for learners who took the course between 2023 and 2024 (n = 122), comparing performance in six content areas. Table 2 prints the suprapubic/pelvic result as p = 0.072 with Cohen's d = 0.24, and e.g. POCUS fundamentals d = 1.09.

> "All the content areas showed statistically significant pre-to-post improvements except for the suprapubic/pelvic view. (Table 2)."
