`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Case study by Simmhan and Kulkarni (2025) at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, deploying an AI Instructor Agent within Microsoft Teams Copilot and Moodle as primary instructor, with the human instructor facilitating. Engagement analytics were generated from student-submitted chat transcripts; no effect size is printed.

> "The study reported a shift in engagement patterns across successive modules, from broad conceptual exploration in early weeks to deeper, more focused inquiry in later weeks, suggesting that agentic instruction can sustain and deepen student engagement over time."
