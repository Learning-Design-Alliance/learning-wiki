`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pairwise correlation analysis of total subjective assessment and objective theory test scores in the prospective observational study (n = 141). The printed correlation at T2 is R = 0.35 (p = 0.0031); at T1 R = 0.083 (p = 0.33), i.e. no relationship.

> "while no linear relationship was found at T1 (R = 0.083; p = 0.33), a significant positive linear relationship was observed at T2 (R = 0.35; p = 0.0031)."
