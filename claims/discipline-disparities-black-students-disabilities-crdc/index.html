Evidence¶

Blair Wriston 2023¶

Blair Wriston, Nancy Duchesneau. (2023). How School Discipline Impacts Students' Social, Emotional, and Academic Development (SEAD). The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED628035

q2 · i? · review · r1

The brief reports 2017-18 Civil Rights Data Collection statistics on exclusionary discipline shares versus enrollment shares, listing Black students at "15%" of enrollment yet "31% of in-school suspensions" and 38% of out-of-school suspensions, with parallel over-representation for students with disabilities.

"TOTAL ENROLLMENT (PERCENTAGE OF STUDENT POPULATION) BLACK STUDENTS: 15% ACCOUNT FOR… 31% of in-school suspensions 38% of out-of-school suspensions"

Blair Wriston 2023 (2)¶

Blair Wriston, Nancy Duchesneau. (2023). How School Discipline Impacts Students' Social, Emotional, and Academic Development (SEAD). The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED628035

q2 · i? · review · r1

The brief reports CRDC restraint and seclusion figures, stating that "78% of students who are secluded or restrained in the classroom are students with disabilities," and adds that Black students with disabilities account for 26% of physical restraint and 34% of mechanical restraint.

"Notably, 78% of students who are secluded or restrained in the classroom are students with disabilities."
