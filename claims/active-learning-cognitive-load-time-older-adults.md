## Evidence

### Zi-Wei Liang 2025

Zi-Wei Liang, Zhi-Jun Zhan, Ying-Chen Liu, Hong-Zhou Xu & Jing Yu. (2025). Interpersonal neural synchronization underlies interactive concept learning in older adults. npj Science of Learning 10:75. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00368-5

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Cognitive-load analysis in Study 1 using task completion time and task-related HbO increases as indices in 31 older learners. The paired t-test showed "older adults required more time to complete active learning compared to passive learning", and passive learning elicited focal frontopolar activation (CH11: d = 0.38) while the active condition showed a more distributed neural response.

> "Older adults required more time to complete active learning compared to passive learning ( t(30) = −2.11, p = 0.04, Cohen’s d = −0.38)."
