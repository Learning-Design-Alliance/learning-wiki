## Evidence

### Ludo W. van Meeuwen 2018

Ludo W. van Meeuwen, Saskia Brand-Gruwel, Paul A. Kirschner, Jeano J. P. R. de Bock, Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2018). Fostering self-regulation in training complex cognitive tasks. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 53–73. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9539-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Cued retrospective report utterances coded with a scheme based on Zimmerman's five SRL components (interrater kappa 0.73) were compared between conditions; the integrated condition's increase in total regulative utterances was significantly greater, with "r =- 0.480".

> "The increase in total reported regulative utterances in the integrated condition ( Mdn = 19, range 5–28) was signiﬁcantly greater compared to the original condition ( Mdn = 8, range 1–27, U = 39.50, z =- 2.542, p = 0.006, r =- 0.480)."

### Ludo W. van Meeuwen 2018 (2)

Ludo W. van Meeuwen, Saskia Brand-Gruwel, Paul A. Kirschner, Jeano J. P. R. de Bock, Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2018). Fostering self-regulation in training complex cognitive tasks. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 53–73. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9539-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney comparison of monitoring-activity increases between conditions; the integrated condition increased significantly more, with "r =- 0.525". Reflective evaluation showed a significant increase with r = -0.610 in the same analysis set.

> "An effect was found on the increase of monitoring activities: The integrated condition ( Mdn = 19, range 6–28) increased signiﬁcantly, compared to the original condition ( Mdn = 7, range 1–25, U = 34.50, z =- 2.780, p = 0.003, r =- 0.525)."

### Ludo W. van Meeuwen 2018 (3)

Ludo W. van Meeuwen, Saskia Brand-Gruwel, Paul A. Kirschner, Jeano J. P. R. de Bock, Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2018). Fostering self-regulation in training complex cognitive tasks. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 53–73. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9539-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same coded CRR analysis found no significant between-condition differences for positive evaluation, evaluation of learning, or evaluation of performance; orientation and adjusting also showed no effects (p = 0.268 and p = 0.110). Equivalence was not tested.

> "The results showed no effects between the two conditions on the increase of positive evaluation (U = 76.50, z =- 0.892, p = 0.187, r =- 0.169), the increase of evaluation of learning (U = 87.50, z =- 0.291, p = 0.386, r =- 0.055), and the increase of evaluation of performance ( U = 79.00, z =- 0.684, p = 0.247, r =- 0.129)."
