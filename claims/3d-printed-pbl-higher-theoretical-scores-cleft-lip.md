## Evidence

### Wang Xiao-Le 2025

Wang Xiao-Le, Xie Ying, Yue Ye-Xin, Yang Xue-Wen, Li Jian. (2025). A novel paradigm in cleft lip education: integration of 3D-printed simulator and problem-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07719-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized two-group teaching study of 40 senior stomatology undergraduates (n = 20 per group) at Wuhan University; in-class theoretical scores compared by Mann-Whitney U test. The 3D-printed PBL group scored 80 (70, 87.5) versus 70 (42.5, 80) points, a "statistically significant (P < 0.05)" difference.

> "However, the students in the 3D-printed PBL group demonstrated superior performance in the classroom assessment of theoretical knowledge compared to the traditional PBL group (Table 1). This difference was statistically significant (P < 0.05)."
