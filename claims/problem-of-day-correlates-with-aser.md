## Evidence

### Angrist N 2020

Angrist N, Bergman P, Evans DK, Hares S, Jukes MCH, Letsomo T. (2020). Practical lessons for phone-based assessments of learning. BMJ Global Health. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2020-003030

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Face-to-face validity analysis in Botswana (February–March 2020, 1080 students in grades 3–5; figure 2 averages 40 classes) correlating the final problem-of-the-day with the ASER test. The article prints "a correlation of 69%" (r = 0.69) and "a high R-squared value of 0.74" controlling for school-level variation, described as a first step towards construct validity.

> "We correlated the difficulty level of the final problem-of-the-day with the comprehensive assessment taken shortly after and found a correlation of 69%. We further find a high R-squared value of 0.74 and an average relationship estimated by a multivariate regression of 0.70 when we control for school-level variation (figure 2)."
