### Korda Mykhaylo 2025

Korda Mykhaylo, Shulhai Arkadii, Shevchuk Oksana, Shulhai Oleksandra, Shulhai Anna-Mariia. (2025). Psychological well-being and academic performance of Ukrainian medical students under the burden of war: a cross-sectional study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1457026

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IES-R survey of 491 students; "44.2% (р = 0.001) of all medical students had high scores (>37 points) and significant symptoms of PTSD". Subscale means were intrusion 1.48 ± 0.26, avoidance 1.45 ± 0.28, hyperarousal 1.61 ± 0.37, with hyperarousal highest.

> "Analysis of the post-traumatic stress disorder prevalence according to the results of the IES-R questionnaire (Table 3) showed that 44.2% (р = 0.001) of all medical students had high scores (>37 points) and significant symptoms of PTSD."
