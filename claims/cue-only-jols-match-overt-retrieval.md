# Cue-only JOLs performed similarly to overt retrieval, and cue–target JOLs similarly to restudy, on Phase B memory and transfer (Experiment 1)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Memory and transfer did not differ significantly between overt retrieval and cue-only JOLs, nor between cue–target JOLs and restudy. [→ Ritter 2025](#ritter-2025)

## Evidence

### Ritter 2025

Ritter, C., Hausman, H., Gaschler, R., Tiffin-Richards, S. P., & Kubik, V. (2025). The Forward Effect of Judgements of Learning on Memory and Transfer in Inductive Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10094-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered within-set contrasts in Experiment 1's GLMMs on Phase B posttests. The cue–target JOLs vs. restudy contrasts were also nonsignificant (memory OR = 1.12, p = .758; transfer OR = 1.09, p = .788). No effect sizes beyond ORs near 1 are printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "Memory did not differ significantly between overt retrieval (M = .64, SD = .27) and cue-only JOLs (M = .65, SD = .23, OR = 0.93, p = .833). Transfer also did not differ following overt retrieval (M = .61, SD = .26) and cue-only JOLs ( M = .60, SD = .22, OR = 1.03, p = .938)."
