## Evidence

### Warshafsky C 2026

Warshafsky C, Singh SS, Garber AB. (2026). In-Situ Simulation for Enhancing Safety in Outpatient Hysteroscopy: Development and Evaluation of a Crisis Resource Management-Based Training Package. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11604

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t test analysis of pre-post self-efficacy surveys in a 12-participant in-situ simulation study. The article reports "Mean self-efficacy scores increased for comfort in managing emergencies (3.23 vs 4.08, P = .002)" among other significant domains.

> "Mean self-efficacy scores increased for comfort in managing emergencies (3.23 vs 4.08, P = .002), resource knowledge (3.38 vs 4.46, P < .001), resource utilization (3.58 vs 4.15, P = .009), communication (3.92 vs 4.31, P = .033), oversedation management (3.00 vs 3.56, P = .021), and management of LAST (3.00 vs 4.25, P < .001)."
