## Evidence

### Rodriguez 2003

Michael C. Rodriguez. (2003). Construct Equivalence of Multiple-Choice and Constructed-Response Items: A Random Effects Synthesis of Correlations. Journal of Educational Measurement, 40(2). https://www.jstor.org/stable/1435344

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's synthesis of nine factor analytic studies. It reports four confirmatory studies found separate MC and CR factors with "Seven correlations between the two factors were reported, ranging from 0.71 to 0.97" and a median and mean of 0.86, none with stem-equivalent items.

> "Four of the factor analytic studies reported confirmatory analyses where MC items loaded on one factor and CR items loaded on a separate factor (Bennett et al., 1991; Bridgeman & Rock, 1993; Ercikan & Schwarz, 1995; Pollack, 1997). Seven correlations between the two factors were reported, ranging from 0.71 to 0.97 with a median and mean value of 0.86."
