## Evidence

### Kiyoshi Shikino 2025

Kiyoshi Shikino, Kazuyo Yamauchi, Nobuyuki Araki, Ikuo Shimizu, Hajime Kasai, Tomoko Tsukamoto, Hiroshi Tajima, Yu Li, Misaki Onodera, Shoichi Ito. (2025). Understanding Community Health Care Through Problem-Based Learning With Real-Patient Videos: Single-Arm Pre-Post Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/68743

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-administered 5-point Likert-scale questionnaire administered before and after PBL; only the test statistic (U=4446.5) and medians are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "For the statement "I am interested in community health care," the preintervention median score was 3 (IQR 3-4), which increased to 4 (IQR 3-4) post intervention, with a statistical significance of P<.001 (U=4446.5)."

### Kiyoshi Shikino 2025 (2)

Kiyoshi Shikino, Kazuyo Yamauchi, Nobuyuki Araki, Ikuo Shimizu, Hajime Kasai, Tomoko Tsukamoto, Hiroshi Tajima, Yu Li, Misaki Onodera, Shoichi Ito. (2025). Understanding Community Health Care Through Problem-Based Learning With Real-Patient Videos: Single-Arm Pre-Post Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/68743

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second self-assessment item from the pre-post questionnaire; the article prints only medians, IQRs, and the test statistic (U=2589.5), with no effect size.

> "Similarly, the median score for the statement "I can envision a community health care setting" improved from 3 (IQR 3-4) preintervention to 4 (IQR 3-4) postintervention, also showing a significant difference, with P<.001 (U=2589.5)."
