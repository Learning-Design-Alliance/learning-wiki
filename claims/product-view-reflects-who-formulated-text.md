## Evidence

### Divyansh Chandarana 2026

Divyansh Chandarana, Sandipan De, Vivek Gupta. (2026). DRAFTTRACE: A Multi-View Analytics Environment for AI-Integrated Writing. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36544

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case study comparing 81 classroom, 81 automated, and 11 copy-typed writing sessions in a graduate NLP course. Descriptive medians show classroom responses were easier to read (Flesch 52.8) than LLM-formulated responses (38.4 automated, 39.9 copy-typed); "median word length rose from 4.67 to 5.18 and 5.40 characters." No inferential test is reported.

> "Median Flesch Reading Ease was 52.8 for classroom responses, compared with 38.4 for automated and 39.9 for copy-typed responses, and me-dian word length rose from 4.67 to 5.18 and 5.40 characters."
