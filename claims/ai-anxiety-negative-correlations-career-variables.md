### Duan 2026

Duan, N., Li, L., Lin, G. & Chen, H. (2026). The impact of AI anxiety on career decisions of college students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-37648-y

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 2) in the 315-student sample, where mean AI anxiety (M = 2.322, SD = 0.893) was significantly below the scale midpoint of 3. The quoted "r = −0.811" and adjacent coefficients are large correlations.

> "AI anxiety demonstrated significant negative correlations with career adaptability ( r = −0.811, p < 0.001), self-efficacy (r = −0.672, p < 0.001), and career decisions (r = −0.718, p < 0.001)."
