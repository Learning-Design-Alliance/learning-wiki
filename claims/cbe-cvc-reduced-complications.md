## Evidence

### Bisgaard Claus Hedebo 2018

Bisgaard Claus Hedebo, Rubak Sune Leisgaard Mørck, Rodt Svein Aage, Petersen Jens Aage Kølsen, Musaeus Peter. (2018). The effects of graduate competency-based education and mastery learning on patient care and return on investment: a narrative review of basic anesthetic procedures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1262-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of level-4 patient-outcome studies, predominantly CVC. The review reports six studies "significantly diminishing the complication rate in CVC placement" and two showing significantly fewer needle passes; no effect sizes are printed.

> "six studies show that CBE-training courses benefit patient care by significantly diminishing the complication rate in CVC placement [66–69, 71–75]. Furthermore, two studies demonstrate significantly fewer needle passes as a strong measure for decreased complications risk"

### Bisgaard Claus Hedebo 2018 (2)

Bisgaard Claus Hedebo, Rubak Sune Leisgaard Mørck, Rodt Svein Aage, Petersen Jens Aage Kølsen, Musaeus Peter. (2018). The effects of graduate competency-based education and mastery learning on patient care and return on investment: a narrative review of basic anesthetic procedures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1262-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's own countervailing results: four studies (Britt, Smith, Miranda, Peltan) show no difference and Udani's spinal anesthesia study shows a negative effect on success rate, yet the review reads the overall pattern as positive.

> "Although four studies show no difference [70, 81, 93, 94] and one a negative effect on success rate [77], these results indicate a positive effect on patient care from CBE-trained CVC insertion."
