# Explicit pre-reading annotations correlate positively with achievement while inexplicit annotations do not

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Learning achievement correlated positively with the quantity of explicit annotations made in pre-reading in both phases (r=.582 and r=.615, p<.01). [→ Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011](#wu-yuin-hwang-guo-liang-hsu-2011)
`q3 i?` No positive relationship was found between achievement and the quantity of inexplicit annotations in pre-reading in either phase. [→ Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011 (2)](#wu-yuin-hwang-guo-liang-hsu-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011

Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu. (2011). The Effects of Pre-reading and Sharing Mechanisms on Learning with the Use of Annotations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932242

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlations within the pre-reading group using concept-based coding of annotation quantity; both printed correlations (r=.582, r=.615) exceed .37, and the article reports students making more explicit annotations "experienced greater learning achievement".

> "All phases exist as a positive correlation between learning achievement and explicit annotation in pre-reading (phase1: r=.582, p<.01; phase2: r=.615, p<.01)"

### Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011 (2)

Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu. (2011). The Effects of Pre-reading and Sharing Mechanisms on Learning with the Use of Annotations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932242

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations for inexplicit annotations (highlights, underlines) in the pre-reading group were non-significant in both phases (r=.033 and r=.086); a student interviewee reported forgetting what underlines meant.

> "The results of two phases indicate that there were no positive relationships between learning achievement and the quantity of inexplicit annotation in pre-reading (phase1: r=.033, p>.05; phase2: r=.086, p>.05)."
