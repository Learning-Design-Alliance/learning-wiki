# A student's shift from an extrinsic to an intrinsic, agent-based interpretation of a distance-time graph followed negotiation with his partner and facilitator prompting

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Arnav initially interpreted the distance graph extrinsically, wanting the turtle to reproduce the line's shape, ignoring the role of time; Liam offered an intrinsic interpretation of gradual change over time. [→ Farris 2014](#farris-2014)

## Evidence

### Farris 2014

Farris, A. V., & Sengupta, P. (2014). Perspectival Computational Thinking for Learning Physics: A Case Study of Collaborative Agent-based Modeling. Proceedings of the 11th International Conference of the Learning Sciences (ICLS 2014). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.3790](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.3790)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Episode 1 discourse segment [2:29-2:48]: Arnav wanted to program the ViMAP turtle to reproduce the graph's shape, a common novice approach; Liam argued the distance was "gradually going up" to 6, an intrinsic graph perspective taking the actors' viewpoint.

> "Arnav's interpretation of Graph 1 ignores the role of time over which the change in distance takes place. We call this an extrinsic graph perspective, as he focuses on the shape of the line in Graph 1"

### Farris 2014 (2)

Farris, A. V., & Sengupta, P. (2014). Perspectival Computational Thinking for Learning Physics: A Case Study of Collaborative Agent-based Modeling. Proceedings of the 11th International Conference of the Learning Sciences (ICLS 2014). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1403.3790](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1403.3790)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Episode 2 [18:19-18:30], after the facilitator asked about parts of the graph that are "the same" and left: Arnav explained distance from the starting point with a loop example, and the dyad's ViMAP model represented changes in displacement over time.

> "His embodied definition and gestural enactment evidence his shift to thinking about the motion from the perspective of the agent in motion, which is at the same position in the beginning (time = 3) and end (time = 7) of the loop."
