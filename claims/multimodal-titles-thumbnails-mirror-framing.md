`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Binary coding of title text and thumbnail features across 51 of 52 videos (one thumbnail unavailable), with group-level percentages. Titles and thumbnails showed parallel differences: G3 led in urgency (41.2%) and productivity language (76.5%), G1 in learning imagery (64.0%) and study context (48.0%).
