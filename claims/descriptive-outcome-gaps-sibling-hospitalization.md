# Children with ill siblings score lower on descriptive educational outcomes than children without hospitalized siblings

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Descriptively, children with a hospitalized sibling had lower mean grade points (189.2 vs 206.5) and a higher count of upper secondary ineligibility (1099 vs 11,185) than children without hospitalized siblings. [→ Bortes C 2020](#bortes-c-2020)

## Evidence

### Bortes C 2020

Bortes C, Strandh M, Nilsson K. (2020). Sibling Ill Health and Children's Educational Outcomes. Journal of School Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/josh.12887

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Table 2 of the register cohort study reports descriptive means and counts: children with a hospitalized sibling averaged 189.2 grade points (SD 69.8) versus 206.5 (SD 64.2) for children without, with unadjusted ineligibility counts of 1099 versus 11,185. These are unadjusted descriptives; no test statistics are printed for this contrast.

> "Sibling hospitalized	189.2 (69.8)	1099 (1.0)	
No sibling hospitalized	206.5 (64.2)	11,185 (9.7)"
