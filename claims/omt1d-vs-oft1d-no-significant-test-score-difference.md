`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Wald test for equality of regression coefficients between O-mT1D and O-fT1D in the pooled multiple regression models (Model 1 and Model 3) in the nationwide cohort. The article reports the contrast was "insignificant in both Model 1 and 3 (p = 0.60 and p = 0.16, respectively)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "The mean difference in test score for O-mT1D versus O-fT1D was insignificant in both Model 1 and 3 (p = 0.60 and p = 0.16, respectively) (Table 2)."
