Seeskin 2020¶

Seeskin, A., Mahaffie, S., & Usher, A. (2020). The forgotten year: Applying lessons from Freshman success to Sophomore year. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-forgotten-year

Analysis of 2014-15 freshmen by graduation status (Figure 13) shows sophomore warning indicators (off-track status, attendance below 85 percent, any course failure) captured "84 percent" of eventual non-graduates, with 63 percent off-track and 21 percent flagged only by attendance or failure.

"In total, 84 percent of these students had a warning flag as sophomores: 63 percent were off-track, and an additional 21 percent had either an attendance rate below 85 percent"
