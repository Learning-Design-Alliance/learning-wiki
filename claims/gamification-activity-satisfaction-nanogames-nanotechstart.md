## Evidence

### Ríos-Muñoz 2025

Ríos-Muñoz, G. R., Fuster-Barceló, C., & Muñoz-Barrutia, A. (2025). The impact of gamification on learning outcomes: experiences from a Biomedical Engineering course. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.06126](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.06126)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Online Google Forms surveys (five-point Likert scale) deployed after each activity during the 2022-23 academic year; internal consistency was high (Cronbach's alpha of 0.925). The satisfaction difference between activities was not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "The aggregated data from the surveys indicate a high level of overall satisfaction among participants, with 93.7% of Nanogames and 76.2% of NanoTechStart participants expressing overall satisfaction with their respective activities (p-value=0.1499)."
