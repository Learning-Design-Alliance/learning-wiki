# Improvement-based relative performance feedback translates into improved math learning outcomes for low-achievers, closing about 80% of the initial gap in Math Multiplication/Division

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Low-achievers in the treatment group scored 0.43 SD (p = .065) higher than low-achievers in the control group on Math Multiplication/Division, closing about 80% of the initial gap to middle-achievers. [→ Hermes et al. 2021](#hermes-et-al-2021)

## Evidence

### Hermes et al. 2021

Henning Hermes, Martin Huschens, Franz Rothlauf, Daniel Schunk. (2021). Motivating low-achievers—Relative performance feedback in primary schools. Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jebo.2021.04.004

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Transfer learning outcomes measured with math task sets conceptually different from training tasks, administered post-treatment. The printed effect sizes were a heterogeneous treatment effect of 0.74 SD (p = .011) and a treatment-control difference with an effect size of 0.43 SD (p = .065) for low-achievers on Math Multiplication/Division.

> "The subgroup of low-achievers displays a strong and signiﬁcant heterogeneous treatment effect on Math Multiplication/Division (0.74 SD, p = .011). The difference between low-achievers in treatment and control group for this learning outcome is large and significant at the 10-percent level (0.43 SD, p = .065)."
