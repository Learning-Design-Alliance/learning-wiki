Evidence¶

Craigo L. 2017¶

Craigo L., Ehri, L. C., & Hart, M. (2017). Teaching community college students strategies for learning unknown words as they read expository text. Higher Learning Research Communications, 7(1). https://doi.org/10.18870/hlrc.v7i1.350

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

ANCOVA comparing definition use in the two definition-trained groups (Table 4: F = 5.68, p = .03, partial η² = .24, S+D > D); the interaction arose because the S+D mean rose to 75% on the second passage while the Definitions group mean declined to 39%, and the article attributes the early boost to prior context-cue analysis reducing practice needed.

"In contrast, the Strategies plus Definitions group significantly outperformed the Definitions group in applying explicit definitions of the target words to explain their meanings in sentences."
