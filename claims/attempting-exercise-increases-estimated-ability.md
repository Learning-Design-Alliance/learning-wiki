## Evidence

### Letteri 2025

Letteri, I. & Vittorini, P. (2025). Enhancing Student Feedback in Data Science Education: Harnessing the Power of AI-Generated Approaches. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00492-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of system-logged Rasch ability estimates after every exercise for the 2023/24 cohort; the article calls the finding intuitive: "attempting a new exercise enhances ability, which leads to a better grade".

> "On average, we observed an increase of 0.0055 (with 95% conﬁdence intervals[0.0035, 0.0075]). This corresponds to an increase in grade of 0 .028 (with 95% conﬁdence intervals [0.018, 0.039]), cal- culated by applying a linear transformation of the ability (which usually ranges from −3 to 3) to the grade (which instead ranges from 0 to 31, i.e., 30 cum laude)."
