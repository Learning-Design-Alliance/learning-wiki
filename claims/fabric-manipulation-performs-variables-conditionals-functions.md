### Anna Keune 2022

Anna Keune (2022). Material syntonicity: Examining computational performance and its materiality through weaving and sewing crafts. Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2022.2100704

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the fabric manipulation unit, in which students sewed twisted square units on a checkered matrix. Pseudocode translation represented matrix dots as variables in dot_list and coordinate_grid lists, dot availability as conditionals, and repeated unit production as a function with a for-loop, showing "the performance of variables, conditionals, and functions."

> "Therefore, students had to identify where a dot was located on the fabric, which unique dots were part of a unit, and which dots had been previously used. As with weaving, this process also drove the performance of variables, conditionals, and functions."
