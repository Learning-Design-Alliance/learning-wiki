## Evidence

### Zwolak 2015

Zwolak, J. P. and Manogue, C. A. (2015). Assessing student reasoning in upper-division electricity and magnetism at Oregon State University. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020125](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020125)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of CUE post-test scores against PH431 Electromagnetism Capstone grades (N = 36). The printed correlation is "r = 0.18, p> 0.05"; equivalence was not tested. The authors take this as evidence the Capstone's additional material does not influence CUE performance.

> "We found a strong correlation between the ﬁrst CUE midtest scores and ﬁnal grades for PH422 (r = 0.53, p< 0.001, N = 85) and no statistically signiﬁcant relationship between the CUE post-test scores and ﬁnal grades in PH431 (r = 0.18, p> 0.05, N = 36)"
