## Evidence

### Stefan A. Schreder 2025

Stefan A. Schreder, Helen Jossberger, Hans Gruber. (2025). Mountain rescuers' experiences with video-assisted and verbal debriefings: a qualitative study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09716-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus group interviews after three one-day simulation trainings in the Bavarian mountain rescue organisation; "the majority (20 participants) preferred high-tech V AD" for audio and video quality, two camera angles, and timestamps making debriefings more focused. Remaining preferences are reported descriptively with no effect size.

> "Of all 42 participants, the majority (20 participants) preferred high-tech V AD. In their opinion, high-tech V AD provided better audio and video quality compared to lower-tech V AD."
