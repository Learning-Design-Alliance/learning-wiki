### Rouis Sana 2025

Rouis Sana, Chouchene Imed, Gaddas Meriem, Naija Walid, Ben Saad Helmi, Lassoued Latifa. (2025). Residents' contribution to teaching at a Tunisian faculty of medicine. The Libyan Journal of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/19932820.2025.2597563

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 105 FMSo residents out of 1,060 (9.9% response rate), conducted January–February 2024. Descriptive results show "92.4% (n = 97) of residents reported receiving no prior training on how to teach before entering residency"; 26.7% reported one to five hours of teaching training.

> "92.4% (n = 97) of residents reported receiving no prior training on how to teach before entering residency. 68.6% (n = 72) of residents reported receiving RaT training during their residency."
