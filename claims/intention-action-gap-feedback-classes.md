`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Thematic analysis of Q2 motivations across classes. The article reports the discrepancy between commitment and "actual behaviours concerning taking action, at only 6.25% and 9.03% respectively" for the two classes.

> "Although both classes exhibited considerable commitments to feedback  as a tool for improvement – with Inconsistent Action Takers with Low Challenge Awareness at 33.33% and Feedback Appreciators with Action Struggles at 43.75% – there was a notable discrepancy in their actual behaviours concerning taking action, at only 6.25% and 9.03% respectively."
