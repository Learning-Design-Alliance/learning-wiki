# Struggling English learners in TRI classrooms significantly outperformed control struggling ELs on spring Letter-Word Identification (g = .43)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Controlling for fall performance, struggling ELs in TRI treatment classrooms scored significantly higher on spring Letter-Word Identification than struggling ELs in control classrooms, with Hedges's g = .43. [→ Steven J. Amendum 2018](#steven-j-amendum-2018)

## Evidence

### Steven J. Amendum 2018

Steven J. Amendum, Mary Bratsch-Hines, Lynne Vernon-Feagans. (2018). Investigating the Efficacy of a Web-Based Early Reading and Professional Development Intervention for Young English Learners. Reading Research Quarterly, 53(2). https://doi.org/10.1002/rrq.188

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA (two-level HLM, intent-to-treat) from an RCT in which 108 ELs in 47 classrooms were randomized at the classroom level; the model controlled fall performance, grade, gender, and SES. The treatment advantage was "gaining 11.71 points across the year (p = .02, g = .43)".

> "treatment struggling ELs had significantly higher spring Letter- Word Identification scores (conditioned on fall scores) than control struggling ELs did, gaining 11.71 points across the year (p = .02, g = .43)"
