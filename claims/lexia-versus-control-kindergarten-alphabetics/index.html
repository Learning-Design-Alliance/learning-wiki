What Works Clearinghouse 2009¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2009). WWC Intervention Report: Lexia Reading, Beginning Reading. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/274

q2 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Randomized comparison in Gale (2006) of Lexia Early Reading versus control among at-risk students receiving 20 minutes daily computer-lab practice for 25 days. Kindergarten effect sizes were 1.14 (Initial Sounds Fluency) and 2.85 (Phoneme Segmentation Fluency); the WWC's multiple-comparisons correction made them not statistically significant but substantively important.

"The author reported, and the WWC confirmed, positive and statistically significant effects of Lexia Early Reading for two DIBELS subtests: Initial Sounds Fluency (kindergarten) and Phoneme Segmentation Fluency (kindergarten)."
