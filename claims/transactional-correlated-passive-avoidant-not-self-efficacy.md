## Evidence

### Sohrabi Zohreh 2016

Sohrabi Zohreh, Kheirkhah Masoomeh, Sahebzad Elahe Sadegi, Rasoulighasemlouei Seyedehsahel, Khavandi Siamak. (2016). Correlation between Students’ Self-Efficacy and Teachers’ Educational Leadership Style in Iranian Midwifery Students. Global Journal of Health Science. https://doi.org/10.5539/gjhs.v8n7p260

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Same cross-sectional correlational study of 97 midwifery students; Pearson/Spearman coefficients after Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality testing showed "a significant association between transactional and self-efficacy (P=0.001, r=0.584)" and no significant association for passive-avoidant leadership.

> "There was a significant association between transactional and self-efficacy (P=0.001, r=0.584), but there was no significant association between passive - avoidant leadership and self-efficacy (P=0.27)."
