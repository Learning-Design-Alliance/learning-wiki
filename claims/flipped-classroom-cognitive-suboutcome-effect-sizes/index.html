Hwan Young Jang and Hye Jeong Kim 2020¶

Hwan Young Jang and Hye Jeong Kim. (2020). A Meta-Analysis of the Cognitive, Affective, and Interpersonal Outcomes of Flipped Classrooms in Higher Education. Educ. Sci. 2020, 10, 115. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10040115

Random effects subgroup analysis by cognitive outcome across the 43 included studies, printing "understanding (ES= 0.60), achievement (ES= 0.59)" and other sub-outcome estimates. Evaluation rests on a single effect size (k = 1); knowledge is the largest category (k = 109, ES = 0.15).

"In the cognitive domain, understanding (ES= 0.60), achievement (ES= 0.59), synthesis (ES= 0.49), analysis (ES= 0.46), meta-cognition (ES = 0.26), application (ES = 0.25), knowledge (ES = 0.15), and evaluation (ES = −0.93) were in descending order of effect sizes."
