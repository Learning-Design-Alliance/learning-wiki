The review reports, from longitudinal cohort data, that childhood mental-ability scores correlated with later IQ: the correlation "was highly signiﬁcant and approached r = .70". The printed r = .70 is a large correlation, so impact is 3. The review attributes the finding to the Lothian Birth Cohorts of people born in 1921 and 1936.
