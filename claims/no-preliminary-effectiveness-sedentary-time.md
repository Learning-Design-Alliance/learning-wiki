## Evidence

### Gammon Catherine 2019

Gammon Catherine, Morton Katie, Atkin Andrew, Corder Kirsten, Daly-Smith Andy, Quarmby Thomas, Suhrcke Marc, Turner David, van Sluijs Esther. (2019). Introducing physically active lessons in UK secondary schools: feasibility study and pilot cluster-randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025080

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot cluster-randomised trial comparing intervention and control schools with accelerometer-assessed activity at baseline and ~8 weeks; the article reports "no evidence of preliminary effectiveness on sedentary time or light activity" or well-being indicators.

> "There was no evidence of preliminary effectiveness on sedentary time or light activity, or on indicators of mental health and well-being (including academic efficacy, positive and negative affect and disruptive behaviour)."

### Gammon Catherine 2019 (2)

Gammon Catherine, Morton Katie, Atkin Andrew, Corder Kirsten, Daly-Smith Andy, Quarmby Thomas, Suhrcke Marc, Turner David, van Sluijs Esther. (2019). Introducing physically active lessons in UK secondary schools: feasibility study and pilot cluster-randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025080

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post feasibility study (N=76 with valid accelerometer data) without a control group; the article reports sedentary time "increased by 8.7 min" and light-intensity activity "decreased by 8.1 min". No control group, so intervention contribution cannot be inferred.

> "Sedentary time increased by 8.7 min and time spent in light-intensity activity decreased by 8.1 min."
