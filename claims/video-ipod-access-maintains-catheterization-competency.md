## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Skill competency declined over 3 months in the control group for both male and female catheterizations, whereas competency remained stable in the intervention group that received the videos via video iPod. [→ Hansen M 2011](#hansen-m-2011)

## Evidence

### Hansen M 2011

Hansen M, Oosthuizen G, Windsor J, Doherty I, Greig S, McHardy K, McCann L. (2011). Enhancement of Medical Interns' Levels of Clinical Skills Competence and Self-Confidence Levels via Video iPods: Pilot Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1596

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot randomized controlled trial of 21 final-year medical trainee interns (intervention n=11, control n=10) at the University of Auckland; competency scored against a 16- and 15-item checklist. The results indicate "a decline in competency over time in the control group" while "the competency level was stable in the intervention (video iPod) group."

> "The results indicate a decline in competency over time in the control group for both female and male catheterizations. However, the competency level was stable in the intervention (video iPod) group for both procedures."
