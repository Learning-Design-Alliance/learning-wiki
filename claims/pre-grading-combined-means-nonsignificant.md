## Evidence

### Janicki et al. 2011

Thomas N. Janicki, Judith Gebauer, Ulku Yaylacicegi. (2011). Impact of Pre-Grading / Resubmission of Projects on Test Grades in an Introductory Computer Literacy Course. Information Systems Education Journal 9(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1145436

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of test score means across the whole section, combining all assignments (Table 3): control averaged 82.04 multiple choice and 86.27 hands-on; the experiment group averaged 86.06 and 91.8. The article states the difference was again not statistically significant at the 5% level.

> "For the experiment group, the difference between the multiple-choice and the hands-on components of the test is larger (5.72) than for the control group (4.22). Students who took advantage of pre-grading performed particularly well in the hands-on component of the test when compared with the multiple-choice component."
