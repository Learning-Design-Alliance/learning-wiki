## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Chi-square tests showed rating distributions were associated with country group for only 11 behaviours, implying homogeneity for 89% of behaviours; the authors attributed the 11 to sparse cells and analysed replies as one group. [→ Udoh Arit 2018](#udoh-arit-2018)

## Evidence

### Udoh Arit 2018

Udoh Arit, Bruno Andreia, Bates Ian. (2018). A survey of pharmacists’ perception of foundation level competencies in African countries. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-018-0280-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Test of homogeneity on survey replies grouped by high-response (Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa) and low-response countries; 11 behaviours showed association with country group (p < 0.01), but low cell counts led authors to assume "homogeneity in responses between the country groups for 89%" and pool the data.

> "The outcome of the χ2 test implied homogeneity in responses between the country groups for 89% of the GbCF v1 behaviours."
