Dong Xue, Chen Yihong, Tan Xin, Zeng Xi, Guo Tao. (2026). A feasibility and preliminary effectiveness study of a tiered gynecological laparoscopic training curriculum in southwestern China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09652-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of satisfaction by prior laparoscopic training experience among the 112 trainees; mean productivity satisfaction was 4.92 ± 0.34 with prior experience versus 4.71 ± 0.60 without. No effect size was printed.

> "Specifically, for Productivity, there was a significant difference in satisfaction with the training between those who had previously enrolled in laparoscopic training and those who had not (Z = −2.1, P = 0.03)."
