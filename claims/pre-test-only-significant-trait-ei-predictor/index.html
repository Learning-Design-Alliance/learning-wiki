Evidence¶

Cotler 2017¶

Cotler, J. L., DiTursi, D., Goldstein, I., Yates, J., & DelBelso, D. (2017). A Mindful Approach to Teaching Emotional Intelligence to Undergraduate Students Online and in Person. Information Systems Education Journal, 15(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135358

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Multiple regression analysis of Trait EI scores from the 3x2 factorial quasi-experiment. The article reports that most post-test variance was explained by pre-test scores and that low variability in scores limited the detectable intervention effects. No effect size is printed for this finding.

"Examining the regressions using the Trait EI measure, the only independent variable that was statistically significant was the pre -test score"
