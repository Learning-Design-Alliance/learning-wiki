## Evidence

### Lim Jonathan ZM 2020

Lim Jonathan ZM, Chew Shi Hao, Chin Benjamin ZB, Siew Raymond CH. (2020). Proficiencies of military medical officers in intubating difficult airways. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-020-00375-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover manikin study of 133 Singapore Armed Forces medical officer cadets intubating a SimMan 3G difficult airway (tongue swelling, cervical collar). The Results section reports "higher intubation success rates" for KVC and MG versus DL; Table 2 prints p = 0.0026 for KVC and p = 0.0073 for MG against DL.

> "Use of KVC and MG demonstrated higher intubation success rates for the difficult airway in a simulated trauma patient compared to DL."
