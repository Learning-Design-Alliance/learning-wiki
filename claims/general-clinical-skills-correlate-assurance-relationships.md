Ulupınar Fadime, Karasu Şeyda. (2025). Perceived vs. experienced care: a comparison between nursing students’ self-assessed competence and patients’ reported care. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12880-w

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same 167-dyad cross-sectional correlation analysis. The entry reports the printed coefficients "with Assurance (r = 0.520, p ≤ 0.05)" and r = 0.533; the article hedges that practical skills "might contribute" to trust, an interpretation rather than a tested mechanism.

> "Students’ perceived competence in General Clinical Skills demonstrated moderate to strong positive correlations with patients’ ratings of Assurance (r = 0.520, p ≤ 0.05) and Interpersonal Relationships (r = 0.533, p ≤ 0.05), suggesting that practical skills might contribute to patients’ sense of trust and relational quality."
