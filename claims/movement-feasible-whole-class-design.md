### Stewart 2026

Stewart, T. M., McCluskey, G., Johnson, H., Fry, D., McAra, L., & Booth, J. N. (2026). Co-developing the Child and Adolescent Mentally Healthy Classroom (CAMH Class) intervention for use in routine teaching in secondary schools. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359563

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative finding from co-development workshops with teachers (n = 10) and young people (n = 7). Participants converged on replacing passive note-taking with curriculum-embedded movement tasks, while diverging on competition and self-consciousness concerns.

> "Overall, embedding movement was perceived as feasible, acceptable and of recognised benefit when implemented to maximize efficiency through whole-class, creative and curriculum-based design."
