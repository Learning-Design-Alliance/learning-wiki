Evidence¶

Curtis Northcutt 2026¶

Curtis Northcutt, Inaara Hasmani, Kevin Feng, Trevor Khangi, Andreas Plesner, Jonas Mueller. (2026). StudentBench: AI and human tutoring yield equivalent GRE learning gains. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28470 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.28470

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized experiment comparing AI tutoring, human tutoring, and control across 2,469 sessions from 2,383 students with pre/post GRE assessments, ANCOVA-adjusted. Equivalence held with "combined AI− human difference was−0.58percentage points"; no standardized effect size printed.

"Human tutoring and AI tutoring pooled across all AI tutors produced equivalent mean learning gains across the combined Quantitative and Verbal sessions (p=.015,±0.25-SD bounds; also significant under the tighter ±0.20-SD bounds,p=.023; 1.59 d.f.). The combined AI− human difference was−0.58percentage points (90% CI[−2.18,1.03])."
