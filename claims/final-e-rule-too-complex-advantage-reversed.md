Cook, W. A. (1917). Shall we teach spelling by rule? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22Shall+we+teach+spelling+by+rule%22

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of rule-adherence scores for the final-e word group across the university and academy samples; the author reports the rule was given in general outline without qualifications or as special applications only, and about a dozen academy students who had it nearly exact scored no better than the mean.

> "By virtue of the higher average of those unconsciously using the rule, it obtained a slender advantage in the university, which it more than lost in the academy."
