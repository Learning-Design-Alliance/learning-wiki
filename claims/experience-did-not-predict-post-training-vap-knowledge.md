`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

> "The overall model was not statistically significant (F(3, 52) = 0.94, p = 0.428), accounting for only 5.2% of the variance in post‐training scores (R 2 = 0.052, Adjusted R 2 = −0.003)."
