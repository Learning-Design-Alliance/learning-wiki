### Mareike Ehlert 2025

Mareike Ehlert, Carola Grunschel, Friederike Koehler. (2025). The Role of Teachers' Emotions and Their Assessment in Professional Development Research: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10048-w

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of 25 included studies coded by two independent raters (Cohen's kappa M = 0.89). The review reports that "n = 13 studies did not refer to any specific emotion theory" while four used control-value theory, two stages of concern, and two Vygotsky's cognition-emotion dialectic.

> "Regarding the question of which emotion theories studies are based on (research question 1), n = 13 studies did not refer to any specific emotion theory (see Table 3). In contrast, four studies drew on the control-value theory (Pekrun, 2006)."
