## Evidence

### Bursztyn Leonardo 2015

Bursztyn Leonardo, Jensen Robert. (2015). How Does Peer Pressure Affect Educational Investments? The Quarterly Journal of Economics 130(3). https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjv021

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A natural experiment in over 100 high schools using a computer-based learning system, comparing daily correct answers before and after the September 20, 2011 leaderboard introduction with student fixed effects, in a sample of over 5,000 students. The article reports students "answer 0.63 fewer questions correctly per day (significant at the 1 percent level)".

> "Column (1) shows that the effect of the program across all students was negative. After the system is introduced, on average students answer 0.63 fewer questions correctly per day (significant at the 1 percent level). This is a 24 percent decline from the baseline of 2.6."
