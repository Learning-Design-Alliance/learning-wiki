### Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini 2011

Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini. (2011). Comparative Effectiveness of Contextual and Structural Method of Teaching Vocabulary. English Language Teaching, 4(1). http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group experiment with 56 tenth-grade students in two matched sections; group A was taught with the contextual method and group B with the structural method. The whole-group posttest means were "55.75 and that of group B was 66.82", with p = .002 reported as significant at the 0.05 level.
