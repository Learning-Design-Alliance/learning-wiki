### Wagener S 2015

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of pilot results (n=469) by academic year, shown in Figures 4-6. Correct answers rose from "22.02% in the first academic year to 66.67% in the sixth academic year", while 'don't know' responses decreased with advancing semester level.

> "This is demonstrated by the overall analysis of the right answers (22.02% in the first academic year to 66.67% in the sixth academic year) and also by the differentiated analysis of the subject groups and competency areas"
