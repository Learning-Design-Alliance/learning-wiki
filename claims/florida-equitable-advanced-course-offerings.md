State-level CRDC analysis identifying Florida as bucking the national trend. The report notes similar equity in science (61% vs. 62% Physics; 88% vs. 84% Chemistry) and that "others examining Florida's educational data have attributed this pattern of course offerings to the statewide efforts to increase educational opportunities."

> "For example, in Florida 80% of schools serving large proportions of students from low- income families offer Calculus, while 71% of schools serving small proportions of students from low- income families offer Calculus."
