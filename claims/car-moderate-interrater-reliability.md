`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interrater reliability was measured using a one-way, random ICC with three raters scoring 17 student research papers. Raters represented a spectrum of educator experience from undergraduate and graduate teaching assistants to PhD instructors with >15 years of experience.

> "The intraclass correlation coefficients (ICC) for key components 1–5 were 0.672, 0.713, 0.615, 0.553, and 0.692, respectively. The cumulative total CAR score had an ICC of 0.692 (k = 3)."
