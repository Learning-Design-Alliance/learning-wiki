## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants answered comprehension questions better for the control text than for the transfer text containing the trained words after isolated word training. [→ Oliver and Young 2016](#oliver-and-young-2016)

## Evidence

### Oliver and Young 2016

Rhonda Oliver, Shahreen Young. (2016). Improving reading fluency and comprehension in adult ESL learners using bottom-up and top-down vocabulary training. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 6(1), 111-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2016.6.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 1 posttest comprehension (score out of 4, four questions per text) in the same 20-participant classroom study. The control-text mean of 1.75 exceeded the transfer-text mean of 1.10; the article reports this as significant, printing "p > .05" as its significance notation. No effect size is printed.

> "the mean score of the responses for the con- trol text (M = 1.75, SD = 1.29) was also significantly higher (p > .05) than for the transfer text (M = 1.10, SD = 0.55)"
