## Evidence

### Lazaros Stavrinou 2025

Lazaros Stavrinou, Argyris Constantinides, Marios Belk, Vasos Vassiliou, Fotis Liarokapis, and Marios Constantinides. 2025. The Reel Deal: Designing and Evaluating LLM-Generated Short-Form Educational Videos. CHIGreece 2025. https://doi.org/10.1145/3749012.3749048

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Trust questionnaire (16 items adapted from Jian et al.'s trust in automation scale) completed only by the LLM condition. Participants rated the reels "trustworthy (6.29 ± 0.78) and accurate (6.32 ± 0.79)"; negatively framed items such as deceptiveness received low agreement (e.g., 2.29 ± 1.46). Descriptive means only; no effect sizes.

> "participants found the AI-generated reels to be trustworthy (6.29 ± 0.78) and accurate (6.32 ± 0.79), and expressed confidence in using the system for future learning (5.90 ± 0.98)"
