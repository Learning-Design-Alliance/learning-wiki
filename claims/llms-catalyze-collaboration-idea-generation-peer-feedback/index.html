Martínez-Peláez 2025¶

Martínez-Peláez, R.; Mena, L.J.; Toral-Cruz, H.; Ochoa-Brust, A.; Potes, A.G.; Flores, V.; Ostos, R.; Ramírez Pacheco, J.C.; Félix, R.A.; Félix, V.G. (2025). q2 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis in the RQ1 findings section of the review: LLMs can "simulate evaluative processes such as rubric-based assessments or expert reviews", providing structured input that accelerates iterative improvement, and by "reducing participation barriers" promote equitable collaborative learning in large-class settings. No effect sizes are printed.
