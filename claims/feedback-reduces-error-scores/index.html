Evidence¶

Trehan Abhishek 2015¶

Trehan Abhishek, Barnett-Vanes Ashton, Carty Matthew J, McCulloch Peter, Maruthappu Mahiben. (2015). The impact of feedback of intraoperative technical performance in surgery: a systematic review. BMJ Open. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4480038/

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Across the three included RCTs, error scores improved in the feedback groups versus no-feedback controls in all three procedures assessed, with the printed p values for each procedure reported in the results and summarised in table 3.

"Feedback also reduced error scores in cholecystectomy (p=0.003), simulated laparoscopic colectomy (p<0.001) and simulated renal artery angioplasty (p=0.004)."
