The editorial reports, citing the Kajamaa and Kumpulainen sociocultural discourse analysis of video records from two gender-homogeneous groups of students aged 9 to 12, that each practice includes "special forms of embodied actions, typical positioning in the physical space, special forms of talk". The editorial attributes this to the study.

> "The authors identified four intertwined multimodal knowledge practices: Orientation to knowledge, interpreting knowledge, concretizing knowledge and expanding knowledge."
