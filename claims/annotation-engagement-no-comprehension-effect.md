## Evidence

### Annette Kujawski Taylor 2011

Annette Kujawski Taylor. (2011). Students Learn Equally Well From Digital as From Paperbound Texts. Teaching of Psychology, 38(4), 278-281. https://doi.org/10.1177/0098628311421330

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Participants were randomly assigned to clean or annotated engagement conditions within each medium; annotated readers could underline, highlight, make margin notes, or use digital highlighting. The difference was nonsignificant (59.1% vs. 62.8%).

> "For the engagement variable, the mean quiz scores were 59.1 % for clean reading and 62.8 % for annotated reading. This difference was also nonsignificant."
