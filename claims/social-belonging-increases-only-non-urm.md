## Evidence

### Ballen C. J. 2017

Ballen C. J., Wieman C., Salehi S., Searle J. B., Zamudio K. R. (2017). Enhancing Diversity in Undergraduate Science: Self-Efficacy Drives Performance Gains with Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-12-0344

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-semester social belonging survey (three classroom-specific Likert items combined additively, Cronbach's alpha > 0.8) analyzed with general linear models including semester × URM status interaction. The article reports significance only for non-URM students; no effect size is printed.

> "Classroom social belonging (Supplemental Tables S3 and S4) also increased significantly with ALP (F(1, 2.47) = 4.20, p = 0.041), but only for non-URM students (Supplemental Tables S3 and S6)."
