## Evidence

### Chen Yuanshuai 2026

Chen Yuanshuai, Jiang Leyi, Wu Lingyan, Gong Wenxin, Wu Jingni, Zhen Junhai, Tong Yuling, Guo Yi, Xu Zhijie. (2026). A virtual patient-based training program to enhance clinical decision-making competencies in general practice residents: mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09607-8

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post intervention analysis of the 50-resident single-group cohort using paired t-tests on OSCE station scores rated by two certified assessors. The article reports "statistically significant improvements in history-taking" and clinical reasoning, with Cohen's d = 1.11 and d = 1.32 respectively.

> "residents demonstrated statistically significant improvements in history-taking (mean difference = 10.2, 95% CI [7.6, 12.8], Cohen's d = 1.11) and clinical reasoning (mean difference = 11.2, 95% CI [8.8, 13.6], Cohen's d = 1.32)"
