## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Character-matching accuracy increased with training (main effect of Time) but was not affected by training type; response time likewise improved with no group interaction. [→ Zhao Pei 2015](#zhao-pei-2015)

## Evidence

### Zhao Pei 2015

Zhao Pei, Li Su, Zhao Jing, Gaspar Carl M., Weng Xuchu. (2015). Training by visual identification and writing leads to different visual word expertise N170 effects in preliterate Chinese children. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2015.09.002

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the two-alternative character-matching task (24 trials) in 29 children: significant Time main effect on accuracy (F(1,27) = 34.62, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.56); the Training group effect and Time × Group interaction were not significant. Response time showed the same pattern (Time η2 = 0.42, interaction p = 0.11).

> "The analysis of ANOVA with repeated factor Time (Pre-test, Post-test) and between-participants factor Training group (VL vs. FW) only revealed a significant main effect of Time (F(1,27) = 34.62, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.56) with increasing accuracy in the post-training test (p < 0.01)"
