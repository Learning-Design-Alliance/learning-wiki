Levin 2020¶

Levin, S., Leung, M., Edgerton, A. K., & Scott, C. (2020). Elementary school principals’ professional learning: Current status and future needs. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. http://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/professional-learning-principals

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive statistics from the NAESP-LPI national survey of 407 elementary principals (41% response rate), reported in the study findings section. The article states there was “little variation in principals’ responses by school characteristics or principal experience.”

"the three topics most likely to be available to principals were using student or school data for continuous school improvement (98%), leading schools to support students’ social-emotional development (95%), and helping teachers improve through cycles of observation and feedback (95%)."
