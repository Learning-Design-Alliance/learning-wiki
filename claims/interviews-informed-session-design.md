## Evidence

### Ali Sana 2025

Ali Sana, McMillan Kim, Kelly Freya. (2025). Improving Palliative Care Knowledge of nurses caring for heart failure patients. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-025-01669-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative needs assessment (Step 3 of the Knowledge-to-Action cycle): semi-structured interviews with three staff members and a nurse educator. The article reports these themes "were similar to those in the literature".

> "The main themes identified included PC concepts, time to PC initiation, symptom management, ACP/GOC communication, emotional support, multidisciplinary team coordination, lack of well-documented GOC in the patient’s chart, and inpatient/outpatient PC service options."
