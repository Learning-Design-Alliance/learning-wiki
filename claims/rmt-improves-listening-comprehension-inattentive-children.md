## Evidence

### Shiels 2026

Shiels, L., Tomlin, D., Payne, J.M. et al. (2026). Benefits of classroom remote microphone technology for inattentive children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00427-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Phase one crossover RCT (n=35) in a simulated noisy classroom. Table 1 reports TAPS-4 scaled scores of 13.42 (5.69) without RMT versus 18.50 (6.94) with RMT, difference 5.08 (95% CI 2.98, 7.18), Cohen's d 0.79, p < 0.001. The trial tested comprehension with "Test of Auditory Processing Skills Processing Oral Directions sub-test".

> "Both speech recognition and listening comprehension scores improved with device use (P < .001)."
