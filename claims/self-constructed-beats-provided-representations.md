### Vanes Mešić 2017

Vanes Mešić, Sabaheta Mahmutović, Elvedin Hasović, Nataša Erceg. (2017). Free-Body Diagrams and Problem Solving in Mechanics: An Example of The Effectiveness of Self-Constructed Representations. European J of Physics Education, Volume 7 Issue 3. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1309-7202

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the randomized experiment in which the group solving without provided FBDs significantly outperformed the group given FBDs; the article frames this as evidence that "evaluating can be more difficult than creating".

> "More precisely, sometimes for the students it is easier to solve a physics problem by relying on self -constructed repres entations, than to rely on representations provided by the teacher."
