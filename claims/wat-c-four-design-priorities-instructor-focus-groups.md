`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Convergent mixed-methods analysis of two Zoom focus groups with nine writing instructors (average 9.4 years of college writing instruction) evaluating the alpha WAT-C, analyzed via LLM-assisted inductive coding with human review. The article reports "four key design priorities" but no effect sizes.

> "Findings from both qualitative and complementary survey data identified four key design priorities: (1) clearer, more concise feedback, (2) increased instructor customization, (3) reduced administrative burden, and (4) a simplified user interface."
