# Temporal process analysis reveals group- and task-related differences that the frequency accounts did not show, in the interchange of CPS elements over time

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For Group B, social state changes were 35.2% lower in the physical-objects task than the technology-enhanced task, indicating longer continued social moments despite slightly more social states. [→ Johanna Pöysä-Tarhonen 2022](#johanna-poysa-tarhonen-2022)
`q2 i?` For Group C, average social states increased (0.135 to 0.164) and social state changes rose 10.7% in Task 2, indicating slightly shorter social moments. [→ Johanna Pöysä-Tarhonen 2022 (2)](#johanna-poysa-tarhonen-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Johanna Pöysä-Tarhonen 2022

Johanna Pöysä-Tarhonen, Päivi Häkkinen, Pasi Tarhonen, Piia Näykki, Sanna Järvelä. (2022). “Anything taking shape?” Capturing various layers of small group collaborative problem solving in an experiential geometry course in initial teacher education. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09562-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Process analysis (Phase 2) of timestamped social and cognitive states for Group B across Tasks 1 and 2. "The calculated proportional difference between the tasks is 35.2% less" for social state changes.

> "It seems that at the group performance level, more social states appeared in the task with the physical objects than with GeoGebra, but the continued “social moments” in the group interaction, on average, were longer."

### Johanna Pöysä-Tarhonen 2022 (2)

Johanna Pöysä-Tarhonen, Päivi Häkkinen, Pasi Tarhonen, Piia Näykki, Sanna Järvelä. (2022). “Anything taking shape?” Capturing various layers of small group collaborative problem solving in an experiential geometry course in initial teacher education. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09562-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phase 2 group comparison for Group C; visualized in CPS circles (Figs. 8–9). The article states "the social moments were slightly shorter in Task 2 compared with Task 1" with 10.7% more state changes.

> "there was an increase in the average occurrence of social states in Task 2 compared with Task 1 (Task 1: 0.135, Task 2: 0.164)."

### Johanna Pöysä-Tarhonen 2022 (3)

Johanna Pöysä-Tarhonen, Päivi Häkkinen, Pasi Tarhonen, Piia Näykki, Sanna Järvelä. (2022). “Anything taking shape?” Capturing various layers of small group collaborative problem solving in an experiential geometry course in initial teacher education. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09562-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phase 2 group comparison for Group D, proportional difference 24.6% less. The article concludes that in Task 2 "fewer “cognitive moments” occurred, but on average, these moments lasted longer".

> "there was a decrease in the averages of the cognitive states (Task 1: 0.246, Task 2: 0.208), as well as a decrease in the average of the cognitive state changes in Task 2."
