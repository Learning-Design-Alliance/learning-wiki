# In a Danish RCT, trained physicians identified more cases of complicated grief and prescribed fewer psychotropic drugs than untrained control physicians

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Physicians who received grief support training identified more cases of complicated grief and prescribed fewer psychotropic drugs than physicians in the control group who received no training. [→ Sikstrom L 2019](#sikstrom-l-2019)

## Evidence

### Sikstrom L 2019

Sikstrom L, Saikaly R, Ferguson G, Mosher PJ, Bonato S, Soklaridis S. (2019). Being there: A scoping review of grief support training in medical education. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0224325

`q3 · i?`

The review reports a randomized controlled trial of grief support training in Denmark (cited as reference 57, Guldin et al., bereavement care in general practice). Trained physicians "identified more cases of complicated grief and prescribed fewer psychotropic drugs" than untrained controls; no effect size is printed.

> "A randomized controlled trial (RCT) of grief support training in Denmark found that physicians identified more cases of complicated grief and prescribed fewer psychotropic drugs than those in the control group who did not receive any training [57]."
