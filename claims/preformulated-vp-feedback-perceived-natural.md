## Evidence

### Helena Salminen 2014

Helena Salminen, Nabil Zary, Karin Björklund, Eva Toth-Pal, Charlotte Leanderson. (2014). Virtual Patients in Primary Care: Developing a Reusable Model That Fosters Reflective Practice and Clinical Reasoning. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2616

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' report from the pilot evaluation with 14 students in the Limitations section. The prototype VP system has no synchronous student-tutor interaction, so all feedback was preformulated, yet students reported it felt like feedback "as from a real tutor."

> "Surprisingly, all the students participating in the pilot test reported that they perceived the feedback as natural, as from a real tutor."
