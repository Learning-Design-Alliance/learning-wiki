### van Eeden 2017

van Eeden, R. & van Vuuren, J., 2017, 'The cognitive processing potential of infants: Exploring the impact of an early childhood development programme', South African Journal of Childhood Education 7(1), a499. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajce.v7i1.499

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of the intervention (N = 29) and control (N = 34) groups after the intervention. The article reports the intervention group "scored significantly higher after the intervention on the Cognitive Scale"; no effect size is printed.

> "However, Table 4 shows that the intervention group scored significantly higher after the intervention on the Cognitive Scale ( U = 732, Z = 3.32, p < 0.01)."

### van Eeden 2017 (2)

van Eeden, R. & van Vuuren, J., 2017, 'The cognitive processing potential of infants: Exploring the impact of an early childhood development programme', South African Journal of Childhood Education 7(1), a499. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajce.v7i1.499

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline Mann–Whitney U comparisons on all three cognitive subscales for the full sample of 63 infants confirmed the groups were matched in terms of ability before the programme began; all p-values were non-significant.

> "As seen in Table 3, no significant differ ences were found before the intervention on any of the subscales (Cognitive Scale U = 482, Z = -0.16; p = 0.83; Language Scale U = 507, Z = 0.19, p = 0.85; Motor Scale U = 582, Z = 1.23, p = 0.22)."
