Yang, L., Liu, H., Li, S., Jia, R., Xiao, Y., Chen, G., & Lu, L. (2026). Evaluating Scaffolding-Oriented Multi-Agent Large Language Model System for Clinical Interview Training. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10939 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.10939

Exam-phase analysis of the randomized comparison found no between-group difference in binary diagnostic accuracy (86% CT vs 84% MA, P = 1.000) and no significant difference in worksheet completion (P = 0.729). No statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

"Correct initial diagnoses were made in86%of control cases and84%of multi-agent cases( P = 1.000)(Figure 2c). Worksheet completion also did not differ significantly between groups(P= 0.729)."
