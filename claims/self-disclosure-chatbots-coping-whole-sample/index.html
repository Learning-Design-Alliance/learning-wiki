Evidence¶

Huinan Hu 2026¶

Huinan Hu, Mojtaba Habibi Asgarabad, Enyi Jen, H. N. Cheung. (2026). Turn to chatbots for sharing feelings or seeking solutions? Differential associations of preferences for chatbot-mediated self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking with adolescent interpersonal behaviors in the United States and Hong Kong. https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/qm6bg_v1

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Whole-sample SEM (N = 2023) with self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking dummies entered simultaneously against the nothing-to-say reference group. Both coping paths from self-disclosure were significant as printed.

"both self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking were positively correlated with adaptive coping (β = 0.22, p < 0.001; β = 0.16, p < 0.001), and self-disclosure was negatively correlated with maladaptive coping (β = -0.09, p < 0.01)"

Huinan Hu 2026 (2)¶

Huinan Hu, Mojtaba Habibi Asgarabad, Enyi Jen, H. N. Cheung. (2026). Turn to chatbots for sharing feelings or seeking solutions? Differential associations of preferences for chatbot-mediated self-disclosure and instrumental help-seeking with adolescent interpersonal behaviors in the United States and Hong Kong. https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/qm6bg_v1

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Path coefficients from the same whole-sample SEM (Figure 1) show coping styles relate to interpersonal behaviors in the expected directions, with the adaptive-coping-to-prosocial-behaviors coefficient the largest printed (β = 0.55, p < 0.001).

"adaptive coping was negatively correlated with bullying and positively correlated with prosocial behaviors (β = -0.12, p < 0.001; β = 0.55, p < 0.001), while maladaptive coping was positively correlated with bullying and negatively correlated with prosocial behaviors (β = 0.34, p < 0.001; β = -0.10, p < 0.001)"

Related Claims¶

  • Adolescents with self-disclosure or instrumental help-seeking preferences show higher adaptive coping and prosocial behaviors, and the self-disclosure group shows the lowest maladaptive coping and bullying, in ANOVA comparisons — related
  • US and HK adolescents differ in conversation-topic preferences: HK adolescents more often prefer instrumental help-seeking and self-disclosure, while US adolescents more often report nothing to say to chatbots — related
  • Adolescents' preference for instrumental help-seeking from AI chatbots is positively associated with adaptive coping, which relates to more prosocial behavior and less bullying, consistently across US and HK samples — related
  • The correlates of self-disclosure preference with AI chatbots diverge culturally: positive with adaptive coping in HK but not the US, and positively associated with maladaptive coping in the US but negatively in HK — reports the opposite
  • Mediation effects of self-disclosure preference on interpersonal behaviors are significant in HK adolescents but largely non-significant in US adolescents, where maladaptive coping mediates toward more bullying — related
