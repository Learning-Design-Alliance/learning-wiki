## Subclaims
`q3 i?` All questionnaire dimensions and total scores rose significantly within each of the three groups (P < 0.001 for most; Table 3 gives P < 0.01 for movement collaboration in all groups and for social-rule adaptation in the traditional group), with significant time × group interaction effects for both outcome families. The study had no non-swimming control, so the within-group gains alone do not show what any one model added. [→ Feng Wanyun 2026](#feng-wanyun-2026)

## Evidence

### Feng Wanyun 2026

Feng Wanyun. (2026). Effects of different pedagogical swimming teaching modes on teenagers' emotional adjustment ability and social adaptability. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1829856

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Repeated measures ANOVA across T0, T1, and T2 in the RCT (n = 120). Emotion regulation F = 42.365, P < 0.001 and social adaptability F = 38.562, P < 0.001 for the cooperative group versus others; "the scores of the three groups were significantly improved and there was a very significant time × group interaction effect" (Figure 1).
