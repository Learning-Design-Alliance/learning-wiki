## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Experts noticed whether and how instructors monitored student thinking and used it to inform instructional decisions 4.4 times more often than novices (p < 0.01, adjusted). [→ Auerbach A. J. 2018](#auerbach-a-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Auerbach A. J. 2018

Auerbach A. J., Higgins M., Brickman P., Andrews T. C. (2018). Teacher Knowledge for Active-Learning Instruction: Expert–Novice Comparison Reveals Differences. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-07-0149

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Generalized linear model comparison of counts from the lesson-analysis survey (14 experts, 29 novices); estimate 4.4 (95% CI 2.0–10.8), significant after Holm adjustment. Participants discussed approaches such as polling, collecting student work, and circulating to talk with groups.

> "Monitoring and responding to student thinking	whether and how instructors monitored student thinking while students were working, and if they used this knowledge to inform instructional decisions."
