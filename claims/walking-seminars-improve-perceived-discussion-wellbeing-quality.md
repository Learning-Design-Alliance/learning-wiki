## Evidence

### Bälter O. 2018

Bälter O., Hedin B., Tobiasson H., Toivanen S. (2018). Walking Outdoors during Seminars Improved Perceived Seminar Quality and Sense of Well-Being among Participants. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15020303

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire sent to 176 students enrolled in the programme-integrating courses, answered by 131 (74.4%); seven-item Likert items compared walking seminars to previous indoor seminars with the same group. Respondents reported that "78% answered that the discussions improved, 82% felt better after the seminar". No effect sizes are printed.

> "78% answered that the discussions improved, 82% felt better after the seminar, 71% thought that the quality of the seminars had improved, 72% thought the possibilities to speak had improved."
