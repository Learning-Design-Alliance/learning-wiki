McElhaney 2024¶

McElhaney, K., Urban, R., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., Tsai, K., and Roschelle, J. (2024). Practitioner-reported Needs for Enacting, Implementing, and Adopting OpenSciEd Curriculum Materials [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/215

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Synthesis of the focus-group needs maps (Figures 2–4) across 28 interviewed practitioners. The report states "Practitioners identify professional support for teachers to be foundational to meeting needs across the system levels," and that PL and other supports "appear to address root causes of many challenges experienced by practitioners."
