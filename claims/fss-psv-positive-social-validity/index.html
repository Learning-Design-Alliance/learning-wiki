Çelik 2016¶

Çelik, S., Diken, İ. H., Çolak, A., Arıkan, A., Bozkurt, F., & Tomris, G. (2016). Effectiveness of the preschool version of the first step to success early intervention program for preventing antisocial behaviors. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 16, 511-535. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2016.2.0317

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Social validity was measured with five-point Likert FSS-PSV Teacher and Parent Satisfaction Forms completed after implementation by 11 experimental-group teachers and 11 parents; responses were analyzed with frequencies and percentages.

"10 of 11 parents (91%) stated that the program met their needs at home and it made positive contributions to their children in terms of their relationships with their friends."
