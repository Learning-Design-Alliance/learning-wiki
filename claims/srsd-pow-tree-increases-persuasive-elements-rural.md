## Evidence

### Mason et al. 2017

Linda H. Mason, Anne Mong Cramer, Justin D. Garwood, Cheryl Varghese, Jill Hamm, and Allen Murray. (2017). Efficacy of Self-Regulated Strategy Development Instruction for Developing Writers With and Without Disabilities in Rural Schools: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Rural Special Education Quarterly, 36(4), 168–179. https://doi.org/10.1177/8756870517723617

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel model analysis (N = 592) of a matched-pair randomized controlled trial of 16 rural schools, controlling for pretest, gender, grade, and ability, showed intervention students "scored significantly higher at posttest on total elements written (b = 2.17, p < .001, d = 0.90)."

> "In the presence of all covariates, our results demonstrated that students in the intervention group scored significantly higher at posttest on total elements written (b = 2.17, p < .001, d = 0.90)."

### Mason et al. 2017 (2)

Linda H. Mason, Anne Mong Cramer, Justin D. Garwood, Cheryl Varghese, Jill Hamm, and Allen Murray. (2017). Efficacy of Self-Regulated Strategy Development Instruction for Developing Writers With and Without Disabilities in Rural Schools: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Rural Special Education Quarterly, 36(4), 168–179. https://doi.org/10.1177/8756870517723617

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The same multilevel model analysis at the late-spring maintenance time point found intervention students "scored significantly higher at mainte- nance on total elements written (b = 1.75, p < .001, d = 0.77)."

> "In the presence of all covariates, our results demonstrated that stu- dents in the intervention group scored significantly higher at mainte- nance on total elements written (b = 1.75, p < .001, d = 0.77)."
