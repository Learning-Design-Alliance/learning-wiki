## Evidence

### Hazzam 2024

Hazzam, J., Wilkins, S., Southall, C., & Ibrahim, B. (2024). The influence of LinkedIn group community on postgraduate student experience, satisfaction and grades. Computers & Education, 216, 105052. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105052

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent sample t-test comparing 40 experimental and 42 control postgraduate students on the PTES engagement dimension after a 12-week LinkedIn group intervention. The "experiment group (M = 4.525, SD = 0.488)" scored significantly higher than controls.

> "the results highlight that the experiment group (M = 4.525, SD = 0.488), t(80) =-2.062, p = 0.042 scored significantly higher than the control group (M = 4.190, SD = 0.908) on engagement"
