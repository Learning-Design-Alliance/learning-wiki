Evidence¶

Öztürk 2023¶

Öztürk, B. (2023). The Effect of Cooperative Learning Models on Learning Outcomes: A Second-Order Meta-Analysis. Educational Policy Analysis and Strategic Research, V18, N3. https://doi.org/10.29329/epasr.2023.600.13

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis grouping the 23 meta-analyses by research location. The between-groups test was significant, "Q (3) = 24.18 p<.01"; mixed-country meta-analyses averaged ES=.61, lower than single-country studies.

"The effect of cooperative learning on students’ learning outcomes differs according to the locations of meta-analysis studies (Q (3) = 24.18 p<.01). The average effect sizes of the meta-analysis studies with Chine locus were relatively high (ES=2.03). The average effect sizes of meta-analysis studies covering Türkiye and other countries (Indonesia and Iran) are high (ES=.93 ES=.80, respectively)."
