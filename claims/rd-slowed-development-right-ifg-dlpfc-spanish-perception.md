## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Adults with RD showed greater hemodynamic response than adults without RD during perception of Spanish syllables with high similarity to Chinese in right IFG and right DLPFC, with no child-group difference, indicating a smaller activation decrease from childhood to adulthood in RD. [→ Yang Fu 2024](#yang-fu-2024)

## Evidence

### Yang Fu 2024

Yang Fu, Xiaohui Yan, Jiaqi Mao, Haibin Su & Fan Cao. (2024). Abnormal brain activation during speech perception and production in children and adults with reading difficulty. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00266-2

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

fNIRS GLM ANCOVA (group × age × similarity × syllable consistency, Raven covariate) in 87 Chinese participants during Spanish syllable perception. The quote reports the three-way interaction with printed effect sizes "ηp 2 = 0.11" in right IFG and "ηp 2 = 0.17" in right DLPFC; simple effects showed adults with RD more activated than control adults for high-similarity syllables.

> "A signi ﬁcant three-way (age × group × similarity) interaction was found in CH31 (right IFG, F(1,80) = 10.07, pFDR = 0.048, ηp 2 = 0.11) and CH37 (right DLPFC, F(1,80) = 16.16, pFDR = 0.006, ηp 2 = 0.17)."
