## Evidence

### R.‐L. Chang 2025

R.‐L. Chang, S.‐F. Siao, S.‐C. Ku, Y.‐C. Yeh, Y.‐C. Chang, and C. C.‐H. Chen (2025). Improving Intensive Care Unit Nurses' Delirium Assessment Performance Through a Multimodal Educational Intervention. Nursing in Critical Care 30(5): e70168. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multivariable logistic regression of patient-related factors on disagreement (Table 3), run separately for pre- and post-intervention phases; "pre‐intervention OR = 4.25" and post-intervention OR = 4.91 for restraint use, with age > 85 (OR = 2.22, p = 0.01) the only age category significant post-intervention.

> "The use of physical restraints was consistently associated with rating disagreement in both phases: pre‐intervention OR = 4.25 (95% CI: 2.79–8.28, p < 0.001) and post‐intervention OR = 4.91 (95% CI: 1.92–12.54, p < 0.001)"

### R.‐L. Chang 2025 (2)

R.‐L. Chang, S.‐F. Siao, S.‐C. Ku, Y.‐C. Yeh, Y.‐C. Chang, and C. C.‐H. Chen (2025). Improving Intensive Care Unit Nurses' Delirium Assessment Performance Through a Multimodal Educational Intervention. Nursing in Critical Care 30(5): e70168. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-intervention logistic regression model (Table 3) on the 420 pre-intervention paired assessments; "odds ratios (ORs) of 1.70 for age > 65 (p = 0.022)" and higher ORs at older thresholds, with no significant associations post-intervention for RASS change, mechanical ventilation or younger age thresholds.

> "Before the intervention, older age was significantly associated with inaccurate nurse assessments, with odds ratios (ORs) of 1.70 for age > 65 (p = 0.022), 2.24 for age > 75 (p = 0.002) and 3.20 for age > 85 (p = 0.001)"
