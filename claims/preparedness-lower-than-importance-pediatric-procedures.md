## Evidence

### Bismilla Zia 2015

Bismilla Zia, Dubrowski Adam, Amin Harish J. (2015). Program directors’ perceptions of importance of pediatric procedural skills and resident preparedness. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-015-1499-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired-sample t-tests comparing mean importance and mean preparedness ratings for each of 31 skills in a survey of 16 of 21 Canadian pediatric program directors and Specialty Committee members. The article reports "significantly lower than importance" with p < 0.05; no effect size is printed.
