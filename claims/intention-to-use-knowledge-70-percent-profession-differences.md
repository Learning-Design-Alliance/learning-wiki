# 70% of participants planned to "definitely use" the knowledge in their work, with profession differences (public health 75.8% vs other 62.3%, P < 0.001), and relevance also differed by profession (P < 0.001)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In post-session surveys, 70.1% reported they would "definitely use" what was learned; public health professionals were most likely to report this (75.8%) compared to other professions (62.3%, P < 0.001), and relevance to work also differed by profession (P < 0.001). ## Evidence

### Goldin S 2022

Goldin S, Hood N, Pascutto A, Bennett C, Barbosa de Lima AC, Devereaux N, Caric A, Rai K, Desai S, Lindstrand A, Struminger B. (2022). Building global capacity for COVID-19 vaccination through interactive virtual learning. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-022-00710-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square comparison across professions (health worker, public health, other) in Table 2 of the post-session surveys (n = 3023): 70.1% overall planned to definitely use what was learned, public health 75.8%, other 62.3%, P < 0.001. The relevance item also differed significantly by profession (P < 0.001, 85.6% public health vs 74.8% other).
