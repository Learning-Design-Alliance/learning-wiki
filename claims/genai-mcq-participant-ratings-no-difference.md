# Participant ratings of perceived difficulty, clarity, and relevance did not differ by item origin, and subjective difficulty ratings tracked objective item difficulty

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sze Kai Ping 2026

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). Human-Edited Generative AI-Assisted Multiple-Choice Questions in Postgraduate Family Medicine: Blinded Cross-Sectional Comparative Psychometric Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Response-process analysis of embedded 5-point Likert postitem ratings from the 73 participants, analyzed as a family of 3 comparisons at Bonferroni-corrected α=.017. No comparison met the corrected threshold; no effect size is printed.

> "Mean ratings showed no significant difference by item origin for perceived difficulty (mean difference 0.06, 95% CI −0.12 to 0.23; P=.51), clarity (mean difference −0.03, 95% CI −0.08 to 0.03; P=.38), or relevance to day-to-day FM practice (mean difference 0.08, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.22; P=.20)"

### Sze Kai Ping 2026 (2)

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). Human-Edited Generative AI-Assisted Multiple-Choice Questions in Postgraduate Family Medicine: Blinded Cross-Sectional Comparative Psychometric Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlation of subjective difficulty ratings with objective difficulty index, reported separately per item origin (r=−0.72 for GenAI-assisted; r=−0.54 for educator-crafted). Both correlations are printed; the larger magnitude is r=−0.72.

> "mean perceived difficulty ratings were negatively correlated with difficulty index (r=−0.72, 95% CI −0.86 to −0.49; P<.001 and r=−0.54, 95% CI −0.76 to −0.23; P=.002, respectively), indicating that items rated as harder were answered correctly by fewer participants"
