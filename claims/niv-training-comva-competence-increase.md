## Evidence

### Pla‐Canalda Elsa 2025

Pla‐Canalda Elsa, Jiménez‐Herrera María F., Fernández‐Sáez José, Concha‐Martínez Pablo, and Martínez‐Segura Estrella. (2025). Impact of Educational Intervention on Noninvasive Ventilation (NIV) in Nurses and Students. The Clinical Teacher 22, no. 5. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70168

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nonparametric analysis (Friedman and Wilcoxon tests) of validated COM-VA questionnaire scores (30 items, six domains) at three time points in the same 66-participant quasi-experimental study. The median rose from "6.82 with an interquartile range (IQR) of 1.33" to 7.30 (p < 0.001) and 7.38 (p < 0.001), an 8.21% overall improvement.
