# Script&Shift users reported significantly greater perceived control, satisfaction, and publication comfort than both chat-based and standard conditions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` One-way ANOVAs revealed significant differences between conditions for perceived control (Q1: F(2, 85) = 101.90, p < .001), content satisfaction (Q2: F(2, 85) = 72.91, p < .001), and publication comfort (Q3: F(2, 84) = 26.15, p < .001), with Script&Shift highest on all three. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test survey agency items on a 7-point Likert scale in the RCT; Welch's t-tests showed Script&Shift "significantly higher ratings" than both comparison conditions for Q1, with similar patterns for Q2 (µ = 6.34 vs 2.83 and 5.50) and Q3 (µ = 5.90 vs 3.21 and 4.90).

> "For Q1, Script&Shift (µ = 6.59, σ = 0.87) showed significantly higher ratings compared to both chat-based (µ = 3.34, σ = 0.72; t(54.20) = 15.48, p < .001) and Standard conditions (µ = 5.83, σ = 1.09; t(55.06) = 2.95, p < .01)."
