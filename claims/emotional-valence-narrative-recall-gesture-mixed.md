### de Koning 2025

de Koning, B., Zhang, S., & Sepp, S. (2025). Integrating Human Movement in Learning: Advancements in Language Instruction, Multimedia, and Theory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10027-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports two experiments by Thakore et al. (2024) in which university students recalled positive, negative, and neutral narratives read aloud under instructed or spontaneous gesture conditions. The review states "emotional valence consistently influenced recall, with negative narratives being the most memorable." No effect size is printed.

> "Across both studies, emotional valence consistently influenced recall, with negative narratives being the most memorable."

### de Koning 2025 (2)

de Koning, B., Zhang, S., & Sepp, S. (2025). Integrating Human Movement in Learning: Advancements in Language Instruction, Multimedia, and Theory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10027-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that the effectiveness of gesturing as an instructional strategy was mixed in the two experiments by Thakore et al. (2024): "instructed gestures sometimes hindered recall" for high verbal memory individuals in both studies and for low propensity-to-gesture individuals in Study 2. No effect size is printed.

> "instructed gestures sometimes hindered recall, particularly for individuals with high VM (Studies 1 and 2) or low natural propensity to gesture (Study 2)."
