Descriptive survey results from the post-exam questionnaire (n = 191 respondents for these items), reported with the printed counts and percentages. No inferential statistics or effect sizes were reported.

> "Collaborative problem-solving was described as enjoyable (n = 78/191, 40.8%) or very enjoyable (n = 86/191, 45.0%). Nearly all respondents (99%) perceived teamwork during the exam as constructive (n = 50/191, 26.2%) to highly constructive (n = 139/191; 72.8%)."
