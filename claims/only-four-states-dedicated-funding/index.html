Espinoza 2023¶

Espinoza, D., Griffith, M., Burns, D., & Shields, P. M. (2023). Federal and state resources for students experiencing homelessness. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/546.264

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's own review of all 50 state K–12 education funding formulas and budgets (Table 3); annual state funding ranges from $87 per identified student in Washington to approximately $1,015 in Massachusetts. A 2019 Washington audit found districts spent $28.6 million on essentials but received only $2.5 million.

"Only four states have specific funding sources intended for students experiencing homelessness: California, Massachusetts, New York, and Washington."
