## Evidence

### Susac A 2014

Susac A, Bubic A, Vrbanc A and Planinic M (2014) Development of abstract mathematical reasoning: the case of algebra. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 8:679. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00679

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Two-way mixed-design ANOVA, Age × Block (first vs. second), on accuracy across the experiment's blocks; printed effect size η2 p = 0.015 for the Block main effect, a small effect.

> "The results showed a statistically signiﬁcant main effect of both factors, Age [F(3,327) = 8.37, p < 0.001, η2 p = 0.071] and Block [F(1,327) = 5.11, p < 0.05, η2 p = 0.015]"

### Susac A 2014 (2)

Same ANOVA design on RTs; printed effect size η2 p = 0.489 for the Block main effect, a large effect. "Participants of all ages became faster in equa­tion solving in the second block," even those with stable high accuracy.
