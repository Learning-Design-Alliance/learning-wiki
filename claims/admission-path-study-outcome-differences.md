## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students admitted by entrance test or the excellence quota achieved markedly better first-three-term outcomes than waiting-list and foreign-quota students (e.g. d = −0.95 and −1.39 vs the entrance test group). [→ Meyer Hubertus 2019](#meyer-hubertus-2019)

## Evidence

### Meyer Hubertus 2019

Meyer Hubertus, Zimmermann Stefan, Hissbach Johanna, Klusmann Dietrich, Hampe Wolfgang. (2019). Selection and academic success of medical students in Hamburg, Germany. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1443-4

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of outcome across admission paths for all enrolled students (Table 4, Fig. 6). Waiting-list students scored d = −0.95 and foreign-quota students d = −0.994 relative to the HAM-Nat group; about one third of these groups failed or lagged.

> "Applicants admitted by entrance test and by quota for excellent PEA achieved the best outcome (Fig. 6), only about 5% of this group failed or lagged, and nearly three quarters completed the first three terms without resitting a single exam."
