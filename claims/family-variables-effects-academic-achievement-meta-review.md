# Family variables relate to academic achievement, with parents' attitudes and behaviors (ES = .873) and parental expectations (ES = .865) showing large effects, and father's education level relating more strongly than mother's

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Koçak 2021

Koçak, Ö., Göksu, İ., & Göktaş, Y. (2021). The factors affecting academic achievement: a systematic review of meta analyses. International Online Journal of Education and Teaching, 8(1), 454-484. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1286675

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of meta-analyses: 34 family variables and 79 effect sizes from 20 meta-analyses (Table 4).
