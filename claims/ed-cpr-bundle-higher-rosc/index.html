q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this from a pre- and post-evaluation by Rolston et al. of an organisation-level ED redesign. The review notes in its discussion that "causal attribution was limited" for the improved resuscitation process measures and ROSC; no effect size is printed.

"Rolston et al. [40] reported higher return of spontaneous circulation (ROSC) after implementation of an ED bundle combining mechanical chest compression device training, in situ simulation for standardised roles, and multidisciplinary video review with feedback."
