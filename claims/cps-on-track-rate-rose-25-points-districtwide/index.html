Roderick 2014¶

UChicago CCSR Research Summary. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/preventable-failure-improvements-long-term-outcomes-when-high-schools-focused-ninth

Districtwide trend analysis of first-time freshmen not in special education, charter, or alternative schools, shown in Figure 1. The summary reports the rate "rose 25 percentage points, from 57 to 82 percent," with gains across all racial/ethnic groups, genders, and achievement levels.

"between 2007 and 2013, the CPS on-track rate rose 25 percentage points, from 57 to 82 percent ( see Figure 1 ). This represents an estimated 6,900 additional students who finish ninth grade each year without significant course failures"
