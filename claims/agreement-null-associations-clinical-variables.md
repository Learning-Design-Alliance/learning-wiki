`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fisher's exact tests on 41 physiotherapists found no significant association between agreement level and five demographic/clinical variables; the article prints p-values only, with no effect sizes, so equivalence was not tested.

> "However, the levels of agreement were not significantly associated with the age group (p = 0.098), work setting (p = 0.171), year of clinical practice (p = 0.666), preferred PFR views (p = 0.088) and the number of CPD attended (p = 0.069)"
