Evidence¶

Mehmet Alper Ardıç; Tevfik İşleyen 2018¶

Mehmet Alper Ardıç; Tevfik İşleyen (2018). The Effect of Mathematics Instruction through Computer Algebra Systems on the Academic Achievements of Secondary Education Students: Turkey Example. Journal of Education and e-Learning Research, 5(3): 165-173. https://doi.org/10.20448/journal.509.2018.53.165.173

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study of 145 10th-grade students in three Turkish high schools; experimental group (n=70) taught with Mathematica CAS, control group (n=75) with traditional teaching. Mann-Whitney U on posttest scores showed a significant difference favoring the experimental group, "U=930.5, p<.001, r= -0.56".

"According to Mann -Whitney U test results (U=930.5, p<.001, r= -0.56), it is understood that there is a statistically significant difference between the posttest scores of the control group and experimental group."
