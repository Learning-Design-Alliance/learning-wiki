Evidence¶

Dahl-Leonard 2025¶

Dahl-Leonard, K., Hall, C., Cho, E., Capin, P., Roberts, G. J., Kehoe, K. F., Haring, C., Peacott, D., & Demchak, A. (2025). Examining the Effects of Family-Implemented Literacy Interventions for School-Aged Children: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09985-3

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Outcome-type analysis within the SCED meta-analysis (11 BC-SMD effect sizes from 10 studies). Text reading effects were significant across 8 studies; decoding/word reading effects were not, which the authors attribute to only two studies examining that outcome. No standardized effect-size benchmark printed.

"The mean effect for decoding/word reading outcomes was positive, but non-significant (g = 0.86; p = 0.45; k = 3; m = 2), which is not unexpected given that only two SCED studies examined decoding/word reading outcomes. The mean effect for text reading outcomes was positive and significant (g = 1.80; p = 0.01; k = 8; m = 8)."
