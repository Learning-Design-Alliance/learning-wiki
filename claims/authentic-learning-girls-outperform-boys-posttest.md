# Under authentic learning, girls outperformed boys at posttest although both genders improved

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Posttest scores differed significantly between girls and boys in the experimental group (p = .007, η² = .08), with no pretest difference. [→ Gürgil 2018](#gurgil-2018)
`q2 i2` The time-gender interaction was significant (η² = .068), which the article calls a moderate effect. [→ Gürgil 2018 (2)](#gurgil-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Gürgil 2018

Fitnat Gürgil. (2018). The Effect of Authentic Learning Approach in Social Studies Teaching on the Academic Success. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2061-2068. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061002

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests on the 92 experimental-group students (46 girls, 46 boys). Posttest means were 24.43 for girls and 22.65 for boys; the article states "girls were found to be more successful than boys" despite both improving.

> "Findings (Table 8) obtained show that there is a statistically significant difference in posttest scores between girls and boys (p = .007, η2 = .08 ), while there is no statistically significant difference in pre -test scores between girls and boys (p = .347, η2 = .010)."

### Gürgil 2018 (2)

Fitnat Gürgil. (2018). The Effect of Authentic Learning Approach in Social Studies Teaching on the Academic Success. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2061-2068. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061002

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed between-within ANOVA on experimental-group scores with time and gender as factors. The gender main effect was also significant (F = 6.48, p = .013, η² = .067); the article states "both effect sizes are moderate" by Cohen's criteria.

> "Findings obtained (Table 7) show that there is a statistically significant interaction between gender and time, Wilks' Lambda = .93, F (1,90) = 6.57, p = .000, η2 = .068."
