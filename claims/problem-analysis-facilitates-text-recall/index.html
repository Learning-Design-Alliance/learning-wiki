Schmidt 1989¶

Schmidt, H. G., De Volder, M. L., De Grave, W. S., Moust, J. H. C., & Patel, V. L. (1989). Explanatory Models in the Processing of Science Text: The Role of Prior Knowledge Activation Through Small-Group Discussion. Journal of Educational Psychology, 81(4), 610-619. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.81.4.610

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 2 assigned 88 Dutch secondary students (46 ninth-grade novices, 42 tenth-grade experts) to analyze the blood cell or control problem in small groups before studying a 2,200-word text on osmosis and diffusion, then free recall. Experts recalled more overall, F(1, 82) = 82.47, p < .001, but both expertise levels benefited from problem analysis.
