## Evidence

### Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo 2021

Bravo-Sánchez Alfredo, Morán-García Javier, Abián Pablo, Abián-Vicén Javier. (2021). Association of the Use of the Mobile Phone with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance: A Cross-Sectional Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18031042

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc pairwise gender comparisons in the cross-sectional study: girls' Spanish was "17.7% higher" in MMP and "46.22% higher" in HMP; English advantages of 10.7% and 25.5% in MMP and HMP. Percentages are not standardised effect sizes.

> "Girls showed 17.7% higher values in Spanish than boys in the MMP group (p = 0.001) and 46.22% higher in the HMP group (p = 0.006). English marks for girls were 10.7% higher than for boys in the MMP group (p = 0.019) and 25.5% greater in the HMP group (p < 0.001)."
