`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory Mann-Whitney U subgroup comparison of participants with versus without prior helicopter boarding experience; baseline differences on five items converged post-course except impact-protection positioning.

> "After completing the course, only impact-protection positioning remained significantly different between the groups (4.70 ± 0.69 vs. 4.90 ± 0.30, p = 0.03) (Supplemental Fig. 1a). No significant differences were noted in the overall evaluation of the MeTra simulator or the course, regardless of onboarding experience"
