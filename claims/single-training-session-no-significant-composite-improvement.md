`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-and-after comparison using Fisher's exact test on 138 pre-intervention and 18 post-intervention cases. The article reports the composite OR, CI, and p-value, and attributes the null result to critical under-powering (estimated power <8%).

> "adequate quality care improved from 40.6% (56/138) pre-intervention to 44.4% (8/18) post-intervention, representing a 3.9% point increase (OR 1.17, 95% CI 0.44–3.15, p = 0.802)"
