`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Model-averaged weight-function selection analysis within RoBMA-PSMA across the 1,786 moderator effect sizes from 89 meta-analyses; the weight function showed "no down-weighting for results with p < .025 (ω = 1.00)" but progressively lower weights for larger p-values.

> "moderator effect sizes with p-values between 0.05 and 0.50 were estimated to be only 43% as likely to appear in the published record, corresponding to roughly 57% lower relative visibility."
