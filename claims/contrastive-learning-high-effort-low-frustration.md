## Evidence

### Elena Eleftheriou 2026

Elena Eleftheriou, George Pallis, and Marios Constantinides. 2026. Confidence Without Competence in AI-Assisted Knowledge Work. CHIWORK '26. [doi:10.1145/3808045.3808055](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808045.3808055)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Between-subjects experiment, subjective measures: Condition B balanced "the highest mental demand in the study (𝑀= 4.85)" with high trust and lowest frustration; the authors interpret this as meaningful deliberation. Only descriptive means are printed, no effect size.

> "This condition was a distinct outlier, since it had the highest mental demand in the study (𝑀= 4.85), yet participants maintained a high trust (𝑀= 5.24) and the lowest reported frustration (𝑀= 2.00)"

### Elena Eleftheriou 2026 (2)

Elena Eleftheriou, George Pallis, and Marios Constantinides. 2026. Confidence Without Competence in AI-Assisted Knowledge Work. CHIWORK '26. [doi:10.1145/3808045.3808055](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808045.3808055)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Interaction log analysis across both tasks: contrastive-learning participants engaged selectively, "presented with roughly 2.5 quizzes per task but chose to answer only about 0.8".

> "System logs support this through a pattern of selective engagement, showing that participants were presented with roughly 2.5 quizzes per task but chose to answer only about 0.8"
