## Evidence

### Hanna Onyi Yusuf & Sadiq Mohammed 2013

Hanna Onyi Yusuf & Sadiq Mohammed. (2013). Influence of Prior Knowledge Questions on Pupils' Performance in Reading Comprehension in Primary Schools in Kaduna, Nigeria. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 4(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.4n.1p.134

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of pre-test scores in the quasi-experiment: experimental mean 42.5 (SD 10.12) and control mean 41.3 (SD 9.91), N = 30 per group. The authors state the difference in standard deviations was very little and pupils were "of equivalent reading ability at the point of entry."

> "One can therefore say that pupils in the two groups are of equivalent reading ability at the point of entry."
