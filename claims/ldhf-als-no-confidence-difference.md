## Evidence

### Moll Lisa 2026

Moll Lisa, Riessen Reimer, Dahlmann Philipp, Häske David. (2026). Effect of low-dose, high-frequency advanced life support training versus annual full-day training on simulation-based resuscitation performance: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09717-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-developed questionnaire on subjective safety rated on a four-point Likert scale, administered before and after the trial (Table 4); all item p-values exceeded 0.05, with no printed effect size. The article reports "no statistically significant differences in subjective confidence were observed before or after the intervention."

> "Both groups demonstrated increased self-reported confidence following the intervention. However, no statistically significant differences in subjective confidence were observed before or after the intervention. Notably, the control group reported slightly higher post-intervention confidence levels than the intervention group."
