## Evidence

### Wei Mingchao 2026

Wei Mingchao, Tang Jian, Wang Jun, Song Zhengkai, Ji Piyou, Wang Yumin. (2026). Effectiveness of a modified BOPPPS teaching model in theoretical education for thoracic surgery: an empirical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08909-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison of post-course basic theoretical knowledge (39.8 ± 3.9 vs. 38.5 ± 4.0) in the quasi-experiment; "no significant difference in basic theoretical knowledge scores compared with the control group (P = 0.263)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "the experimental group showed no significant difference in basic theoretical knowledge scores compared with the control group (P = 0.263), but achieved a statistically significant greater score improvement (P = 0.049, Cohen's d = 0.32)"

### Wei Mingchao 2026 (2)

Wei Mingchao, Tang Jian, Wang Jun, Song Zhengkai, Ji Piyou, Wang Yumin. (2026). Effectiveness of a modified BOPPPS teaching model in theoretical education for thoracic surgery: an empirical study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08909-1

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pre-/post-course paired comparison (Table 7) showed within-group gains of 2.3 ± 1.5 vs. 1.3 ± 1.2; "the experimental group showed a statistically significant improvement in score gain (Δ = 2.3 vs. 1.3, P = 0.049)"; Table 7 reports Cohen's d = 0.71 for the between-group Δ contrast.

> "the experimental group showed a statistically significant improvement in score gain (Δ = 2.3 vs. 1.3, P = 0.049), indicating better knowledge retention driven by clinical practice linkage"
