## Evidence

### Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh 2026

Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Hafshejani Nazanin Damsaz. (2026). Locus of control and sense of coherence as predictors of academic achievement among medical and nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09941-x

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Independent t-test comparison by major in the 348-student cross-sectional sample (227 medical, 121 nursing). The article attributes the difference to nursing's earlier clinical exposure fostering a deeper perception of professional purpose.

> "Comparisons by academic major indicated that nursing students had significantly higher scores in the meaningfulness dimension compared to medical students (p = 0.006, Cohen’s d ≈ 0.30, small-to-medium effect)."

### Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh 2026 (2)

Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Hafshejani Nazanin Damsaz. (2026). Locus of control and sense of coherence as predictors of academic achievement among medical and nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09941-x

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Independent t-test comparison by gender in the same sample (154 male, 194 female). The authors note regional evidence links female gender to higher perceived academic stress among Iranian healthcare students, which might affect baseline SOC dimension scores.

> "However, a significant gender difference was observed in the meaningfulness dimension of SOC, with male students reporting higher scores than female students (p = 0.042, Cohen’s d ≈ 0.22, small effect)."
