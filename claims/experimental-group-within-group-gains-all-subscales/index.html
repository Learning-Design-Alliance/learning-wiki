Evidence¶

Demir 2025¶

Demir, R., & Akar, C. (2025). Transforming Clicks into Critical Thinking: An AI-Based Media Literacy Program for Children. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-6786882/v1

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group paired-samples t-test analysis (Table 4) of the experimental group (n = 18); the article reports significant gains on all subscales (p < .01) with descriptive mean gains of "+3.50", "+1.67", and "+5.17"; no standardized effect size is printed for the within-group tests.

"The magnitude of improvement observed in media reading (+3.50), media writing (+1.67), and overall media literacy (+5.17) indicates the multidimensional effectiveness of the AI-integrated curriculum."
