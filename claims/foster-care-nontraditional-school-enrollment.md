Barrat, V. X., Berliner, B., & Felida, N. J. (2015). Arizona's Invisible Achievement Gap: Education Outcomes of Students in Foster Care in the State's Public Schools. San Francisco: WestEd. http://www.azed.gov/research-evaluation/files/2013/11/2013-a-f-technical-manual.pdf

Observational analysis of enrollment in nontraditional schools (alternative, juvenile justice, and non-public schools) by school level in 2012/13 (figure 7). Across K–12, "some 11 percent of students in foster care were enrolled in nontraditional schools, compared with about 3 percent each for the other student population groups."

> "In high school, nearly one third of the students in foster care attended a nontraditional school compared to 13 percent for low-SES students and 9 percent for all students statewide."
