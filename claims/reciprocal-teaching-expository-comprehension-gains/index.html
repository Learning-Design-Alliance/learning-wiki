Evidence¶

Pilten 2016¶

Gulhiz Pilten. (2016). The Evaluation of Effectiveness of Reciprocal Teaching Strategies on Comprehension of Expository Texts. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i10.1791

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-test-post-test control group experiment with 26 experiment and 28 control fourth graders. Independent samples t-tests on post-test RCES scores favored the experiment group on all dimensions; the article reports the t and p values but no effect size.

"the differences in the increases in the post -test scores of experiment and control groups are statistically s ignificant in terms of all dimensions and total score in favour of experiment group [t=3.709, p<.05; t=4.691, p<.05; t=25.11, p<.05; t=15.89, p<.05]"
