# Giving teachers access to students' diagrams improves teachers' regulation accuracy but not their monitoring accuracy

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Janneke van de Pol 2020

Janneke van de Pol, et al. (2020). Mapping and Drawing to Improve Students’ and Teachers’ Monitoring and Regulation of Students’ Learning from Text: Current Findings and Future Directions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09560-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Generation-paradigm study with secondary education teachers judging student comprehension under name-only, name+completed diagram, and name+drawn diagram conditions. The review reports regulation gammas of −0.38 and −0.21 in diagram conditions versus 0.00 in control, with monitoring accuracy low to moderate in all conditions.

> "Thus, the accuracy of teachers’ judgments of students ’ understanding was not affected by condition, whereas the accuracy of their regulation decisions was."
