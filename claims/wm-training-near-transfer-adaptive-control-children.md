## Evidence

### Jones JS 2020

Jones JS, Milton F, Mostazir M, Adlam ALR. (2020). The academic outcomes of working memory and metacognitive strategy training in children: A double‐blind randomized controlled trial. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.12870

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVAs on baseline-adjusted AWMA composite scores from the double-blind RCT (N = 77 completers) showed both training groups outperformed the adaptive control, with adjusted mean differences of 10.86 and 9.69 points; "scores on the AWMA were significantly higher in the MetaCogmed (p < 0.001) and Cogmed groups (p < 0.001)".

> "Compared to the Control group, scores on the AWMA were significantly higher in the MetaCogmed (p < 0.001) and Cogmed groups (p < 0.001) at the immediate outcome."

### Jones JS 2020 (2)

Jones JS, Milton F, Mostazir M, Adlam ALR. (2020). The academic outcomes of working memory and metacognitive strategy training in children: A double‐blind randomized controlled trial. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.12870

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multi-level mixed models of change from baseline to 3-month follow-up in the same RCT found maintained working memory gains: "scores on the AWMA increased significantly more in the MetaCogmed (p < 0.001) and Cogmed groups (p = 0.040)" relative to control.

> "The mixed models indicated that scores on the AWMA increased significantly more in the MetaCogmed (p < 0.001) and Cogmed groups (p = 0.040) compared to the Control group at the 3‐month outcome."
