## Evidence

### Bolatli 2026

Bolatli, G., S. Birisci, and Z. Bolatli. 2026. An Alternative Approach in Anatomy Education: Design of a Learning Environment Based on Artificial Intelligence‐Supported Virtual Manipulatives and Investigation of Its Effectiveness. Clinical Anatomy 39, no. 1: 30–44. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.70008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on the single-item Paas cognitive load scale administered pre and post to the three groups; group effect not significant, experiment effect significant. Descriptive post means: C 5.38, SML 5.05, SML+AI 4.56.

> "Although there was no statistically significant difference in cognitive load scores for the three groups (F = 2.24; p > 0.05; η 2 = 0.017), it was determined that the experimental design caused a significant difference in students' cognitive load structures (F = 14.12; p < 0.05; η 2 = 0.089)."

### Bolatli 2026 (2)

Bolatli, G., S. Birisci, and Z. Bolatli. 2026. An Alternative Approach in Anatomy Education: Design of a Learning Environment Based on Artificial Intelligence‐Supported Virtual Manipulatives and Investigation of Its Effectiveness. Clinical Anatomy 39, no. 1: 30–44. https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.70008

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LCD post hoc tests (Table 4) found no significant pairwise cognitive load differences between C, SML, and SML+AI; the SML+AI decrease is descriptive only.

> "a post hoc LCD test for multiple comparisons showed that the mean values of cognitive load scores of all groups are not significant (p > 0.05)."
