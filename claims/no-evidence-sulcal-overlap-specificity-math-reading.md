## Evidence

### Roell M. 2022

Roell M., Bellon E., Polspoel B., Declercq M. & De Smedt B. (2022). Investigating the association between variability in sulcal pattern and academic achievement. Scientific Reports 12, 12323. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-15335-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The pre-registered Aim 3 analyses in the combined grade 4 dataset were deemed ill-guided after the primary Aim 1 and Aim 2 effects failed to replicate clearly: the study "failed to find a clear effect of the IPS sulcal pattern" on arithmetic and symbolic number comparison. No specificity or overlap result was reported in the main text.

> "However, this study failed to find a clear effect of the IPS sulcal pattern on arithmetic and symbolic number comparison abilities, nor did the study find a clear effect of the OTS sulcal pattern on reading abilities. As such, it seemed ill-guided to study the specificity of both these effects"
