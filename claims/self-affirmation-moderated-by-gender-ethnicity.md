## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Borman 2013

Borman, G. D., & Grigg, J. (2013). An Effort to Close Achievement Gaps at Scale through Self-Affirmation. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563077

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The multilevel model included interaction terms estimating differential treatment impacts by ethnicity and gender; the article reports that "gender and ethnic group moderated the impact of self-affirmation." Figures 2 and 3 report estimates for the intersection of gender and ethnicity, showing slighter achievement variation in the self-affirmation condition.

> "We did find, however, that gender and ethnic group moderated the impact of self-affirmation."
