## Evidence

### Poquet 2021

Poquet, O., Nguyen, Q., Kovanovic, V., Brooks, C., Dawson, S., & Biotteau, A. (2021). Social Architectures of University Forums: Grade-Based Similarity in Online Interactions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09675

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Descriptive network analysis of forum interactions among 14,643 students in three universities found that "students interact online with those in similar performance-bands", with average GPA differences within one standard deviation.

> "The results indicate that students interact online with those in similar performance-bands."
