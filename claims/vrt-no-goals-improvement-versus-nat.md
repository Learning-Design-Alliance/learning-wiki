The impact of virtual reality simulation training on operative performance in laparoscopic cholecystectomy: meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrac086

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of three RCTs on total GOALS, 75 participants randomized to VRT and 38 to NAT, low heterogeneity (I2 = 0 per cent), fixed-effects model favouring neither group, "MD 0.91, 95 per cent c.i. −0.29 to 2.11, P = 0.14". Equivalence was not tested.

> "Total GOALS scores were analysed including in three studies8,46,50, where 75 participants were randomized to VRT and 38 participants to NAT. Statistical heterogeneity was low (I2 = 0 per cent) using a fixed-effects model, the combined weighted effected favoured neither VRT nor NAT (MD 0.91, 95 per cent c.i. −0.29 to 2.11, P = 0.14)"

### Humm Gemma; Mohan Helen Peter; Fleming Christina; Harries Rhiannon; Wood Christopher; Dawas Khaled; Stoyanov Danail; Lovat Laurence B 2022 (2)

Humm Gemma; Mohan Helen Peter; Fleming Christina; Harries Rhiannon; Wood Christopher; Dawas Khaled; Stoyanov Danail; Lovat Laurence B. (2022). The impact of virtual reality simulation training on operative performance in laparoscopic cholecystectomy: meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrac086

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of five RCTs on GOALS domain scores, 47 participants randomized to VRT and 46 to NAT; depth perception favoured neither group (MD = 0.24, P = 0.51), as did bimanual dexterity, efficiency and tissue handling. Heterogeneity was substantial for some domains.

> "For the GOALS domain of depth perception, statistical heterogeneity was substantial (I2 = 82%). Using a random-effects model, the combined weighted effect favoured neither VRT nor NAT (MD = 0.24, 95%CI.−0.48 to 0.97, P = 0.51)"
