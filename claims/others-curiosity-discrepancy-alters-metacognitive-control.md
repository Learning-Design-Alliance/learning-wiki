## Evidence

### Sunae Kim 2025

Sunae Kim, Paul L Harris, Benjamin Néher. (2025). Curiosity can influence metacognitive processes. Metacognition and Learning (2025) 20:39. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09444-y

`q3 · i2`

Direct comparison of Experiments 3A and 3B (each final N = 40; 3B showed manipulated discrepant others' curiosity ratings before the answer decision). Mixed-effects regression found the "interaction between opt-out and Experiment was significant", with β = −0.22; the curiosity discrepancy between opted-out and non-opted-out trials was reduced in 3B; overall curiosity did not differ between experiments (p =.09).

> "The interaction between opt-out and Experiment was significant F (1, 3196) = 6.07, p =.01 (b = −5.83, SE = 2.37, β =, − 0.22), confirming our expectation."
