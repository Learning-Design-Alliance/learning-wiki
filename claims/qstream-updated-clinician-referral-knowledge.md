## Evidence

### Robinson Tracy 2017

Robinson Tracy, Janssen Anna, Kirk Judy, DeFazio Anna, Goodwin Annabel, Tucker Kathy, Shaw Timothy. (2017). New Approaches to Continuing Medical Education: a QStream (spaced education) Program for Research Translation in Ovarian Cancer. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-015-0944-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative semi-structured interviews at completion of the three-case QStream pilot, thematically analysed. Ten participants agreed the program increased their knowledge; one noted there 'were several genetic things that I thought that we shouldn't do and it turned out that we should do'. No knowledge test was administered, so no effect size is available.

> "A majority of participants agreed that the program had helped them update their knowledge about referring women for assessment and consideration of genetic testing. Ten participants agreed that the program increased their knowledge"
