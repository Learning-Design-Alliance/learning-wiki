## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Both 3rd-year and 4th-year medical students scored lower in the DV station (child abuse and elder abuse respectively) than in the other five SP stations of the same examination. [→ Kong Hyun-Hee 2018](#kong-hyun-hee-2018)

## Evidence

### Kong Hyun-Hee 2018

Kong Hyun-Hee, Im Sunju, Seo Ji-Hyun, Kim Do-Kyong, Roh HyeRin. (2018). Medical students’ clinical performance of dealing with patients in the context of domestic violence. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2018.79

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of consortium SP examination scores from five medical schools; 3rd-year students (n=111, 2014) scored 50.6±13.3 in the child abuse station, "which was the 2nd lowest among six stations". 4th-year students (n=143, 2016) scored 57.1±12.4 in the elder abuse station, third lowest of six.

> "Third year students scored an average of 50.6±13.3 points in the child abuse station, which was the 2nd lowest among six stations."
