### Keil 2001

Keil, C. T., & Cortina, J. M. (2001). Degradation of validity over time: A test and extension of Ackerman's model. Psychological Bulletin, 127(5), 673-697. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.127.5.673

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression reanalysis of short-time-span longitudinal studies of the perceptual speed-consistent task correlation reports a "significant cubic trend" with ΔR2 = .029, p < .05, and that "The quadratic trend was not significant," contrary to the predicted inverted U.

> "Analysis of short-time-span studies yielded a significant cubic trend (AJ?2 = .029, p \< .05) between time and the perceptual speed ability-consistent task performance correlation. The quadratic trend was not significant."
