## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zhou Yuanyuan 2016

Zhou Yuanyuan, Graham Lori, West Courtney. (2016). The relationship between study strategies and academic performance. Int J Med Educ 7:324-332. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.57dc.fe0f

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis in the same 168-student cohort: SDLRS-only models yielded R2 of .000 (Year I) and .015 (Year II), and adding SDLRS to MCAT/UGPA models did not significantly increase explained variance (Model 3 p = .556; Model 4 p = .430). The article reports no standardized effect size for the SDLRS-performance relationship.

> "The first SDLRS, nor the first LASSI, were predictive of academic performance."
