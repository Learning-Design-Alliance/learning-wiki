# Ethnic minority students received higher final ratings than majority students for ascending performances, but only when rated by residents

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` For ascending performances, ethnic majority students were rated significantly lower than ethnic minority students on final ratings, and this effect was moderated by assessor function, appearing only for residents (d = 0.56) and not specialists. [→ Steinmann JJ 2025](#steinmann-jj-2025)

## Evidence

### Steinmann JJ 2025

Steinmann JJ, Otto I, Born MP, Derous E, Woltman AM, van den Broek WW, Stegers-Jager KM. (2025). Adjustment of Assessors’ First Impressions Differs by Student Ethnicity. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.2196

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

LMM analysis of final ratings after full 5-minute videos in the same 81-physician experiment. Post hoc analyses showed a significant ethnicity-by-function interaction, F(1,78) = 6.81, p = .01, with residents rating minority students higher (Mmaj = 6.95; Mmin = 7.56, d = 0.56) but specialists not (Mmaj = 7.25; Mmin = 7.21).

> "ethnic majority students were rated significantly lower (M = 7.09, SD = .12) than ethnic minority students (M = 7.40, SD = .12), F(1,79) = 5.99, p = .02"
