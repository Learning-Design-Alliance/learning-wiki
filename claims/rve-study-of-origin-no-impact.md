`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

New robust variance estimation (RVE) analysis of the Leary et al. (2013) data, which span 113 studies with 1 to 24 outcomes per study (average 3.12). The article reports the rho sensitivity result quoted here as a preliminary analysis addressing the nested nature of the data.

> "At the two extremes of no correlation (rho = 0) and almost perfect correlation (rho = 0.99), Hedges' g was identical to four decimal places, suggesting that the study of origin does not make an impact on the final effect size."
