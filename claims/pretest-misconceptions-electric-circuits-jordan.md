### Ali Khalid Ali Bawaneh 2010

Ali Khalid Ali Bawaneh, Ahmad Nurulazam Md Zain, & Salmiza Saleh. (2010). The Effect of Herrmann Whole Brain Teaching Method on Students' Understanding of Simple Electric Circuits. European Journal of Physics Education, Vol. 2 No. 2, ISSN 1309 7202. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/13097202

Independent-samples t-test on pretest scores of the two groups (HWBTM n=135, CTM n=138); the t-test was non-significant (Sig. .283), and the article reports both groups "performed low on the pre test" (means 4.63 and 4.86 out of 14).

> "Table 3 demonstrates that the mean difference between the study groups is insignificant, implying that both groups are equivalent. The results show that both groups performed low on the pre test."
