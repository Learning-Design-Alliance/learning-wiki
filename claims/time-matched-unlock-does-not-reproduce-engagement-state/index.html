Xiaotian Su 2026¶

Xiaotian Su, Laura Rimell, Jiazheng Li, Amal Rannen-Triki, Ulrich Paquet, Lisa Anne Hendricks, Rida Qadri, Daphne Ippolito and Piotr Mirowski. (2026). Who Thinks First? Designing Productive Friction with Engage-to-Unlock GenAI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01518 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2610.01518

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Unlocking-status analysis in Results §4.1.1 of the N=398 experiment: 98 of 107 Engage-to-Unlock participants (91.6%) unlocked Tell-me; in Time-Matched Unlock, 34 of 94 never received an unlock before submission, partly due to a client-side timer stopping at 20 minutes.

"Thus, although Time-Matched Unlock reproduced the timing of unlocks from the Engage-to-Unlock condition, it did not reproduce the engagement state that triggered those unlocks."
