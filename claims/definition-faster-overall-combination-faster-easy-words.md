The author's comparative observation, based on several administrations of the vocabulary test both by definition and by cued combination: "the advantage in time lies with procedure by definition", while easier words go somewhat faster by combination.

> "For the whole Terman list, the advantage in time lies with procedure by definition. The easier words are somewhat more quickly disposed of by combination, but the harder words much more quickly by definition."
