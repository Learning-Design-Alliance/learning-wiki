# Group-by-time interaction shows large significant knowledge gains for the DVD group on both positioning-and-attachment and hand-expression subscales

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Mixed ANOVA found significant group × time interactions for total, P & A and HE knowledge scores favouring the intervention group, with η² = .33, .20 and .21 respectively. [→ Ma Yuan Ying 2018](#ma-yuan-ying-2018)

## Evidence

### Ma Yuan Ying 2018

Ma Yuan Ying, Wallace Louise L., Qiu Li Qian, Kosmala-Anderson Joanna, Bartle Naomi. (2018). A randomised controlled trial of the effectiveness of a breastfeeding training DVD on improving breastfeeding knowledge and confidence among healthcare professionals in China. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-018-1709-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed two-way ANOVA on the RCT's knowledge outcomes. All three interaction tests were significant, with η² = .33 (total), η² = .20 (P & A) and η² = .21 (HE); the article states the η² values indicate "large increases in knowledge in the IG group".

> "Using ANOVA (Group x Time) there was significant interaction between group and time in total knowledge score [F(1,188) = 92.11, p < 0.001, η2 = .33.], P & A knowledge score [F(1,189) = 48.02, p < 0.001, η2 = .20] and HE knowledge score [F(1,189) = 48.65, p < 0.001, η2 = .21]"
