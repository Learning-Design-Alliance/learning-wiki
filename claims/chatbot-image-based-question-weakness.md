Haberal Merve, Hançerlioğulları Dilek. (2026). Can artificial intelligence chatbots think like dentists? A comparative analysis based on dental specialty examination questions in restorative dentistry. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07612-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Format-type subgroup analysis (Table 5) of the DUS benchmark: four image-based items versus 184 text-based items. DeepSeek was excluded from visual evaluation for lacking image analysis. The authors conclude "the visual analytic capacity of currently available chatbots remains limited."

> "Although the number of image-based questions was relatively small (n = 4), a notable decline in overall accuracy was observed. Most models remained within the 50–75% accuracy range, whereas only Gemini Advanced and ChatGPT-3.5 achieved 100% accuracy in image-based questions."
