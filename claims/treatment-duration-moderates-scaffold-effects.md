# Scaffold effects peak at 2–4 weeks of treatment and decrease dramatically for treatments lasting 9–24 weeks

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Treatments of 2–4 weeks had the highest effect size (ES = 0.776) while 9–24 week treatments had a negative effect size (ES = -0.032); the overall duration moderator test was not significant (Q = 9.83, df = 5, p = 0.080). [→ Lanqin Zheng 2016](#lanqin-zheng-2016)

## Evidence

### Lanqin Zheng 2016

Lanqin Zheng. (2016). The effectiveness of self-regulated learning scaffolds on academic performance in computer-based learning environments: a meta-analysis. Asia Pacific Education Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12564-016-9426-9

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis of treatment duration across six coded bands in the 29 studies. The 2–4 week band (698 subjects) showed the highest effect and the 9–24 week band (66 subjects) a negative effect, but the between-group test was not significant.

> "Seven studies (24.1 %) were coded as 2–4 weeks, associated with the highest effect size (ES = 0.776). One study (3.4 %) was coded as 9–24 weeks, associated with the lowest and negative effect size (ES =- 0.032). There was no signiﬁcant difference among different kinds of duration ( Q = 9.83, df = 5, p = 0.080)"
