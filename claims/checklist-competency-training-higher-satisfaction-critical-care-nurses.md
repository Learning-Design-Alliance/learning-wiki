## Evidence

### Wang Liuliu 2026

Wang Liuliu, Liu Zeyu, Tong Minghui, Guo Junyan, Zhu Hongmei. (2026). Effect of a checklist-based core competency training and evaluation system on work-related alienation among critical care nurses. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1708734

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental before-after study of 150 critical care nurses comparing pre- and post-intervention phases using the McCloskey/Mueller Satisfaction Scale. The paired comparison showed "increased from 13.21 ± 1.54 to 16.39 ± 1.28" for effectiveness evaluation and similar gains on the other two dimensions, all p < 0.001.

> "Scores for effectiveness evaluation, content design, and participation willingness increased from 13.21 ± 1.54 to 16.39 ± 1.28, from 9.59 ± 1.53 to 12.16 ± 1.04, and from 8.59 ± 1.68 to 10.12 ± 1.31, respectively (all p < 0.001)."
