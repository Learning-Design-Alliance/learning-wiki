Systematic search of Pubmed/Medline, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus and ERIC in July 2025 without year or language restrictions; from 1,511 records, 887 duplicates were excluded and 11 studies were included. The review reports studies were "published between 2010 and 2025" with the United States contributing n = 4.

> "11 studies met the eligibility criteria and were included in this scoping review. They were published between 2010 and 2025, in the United States (n = 4), Malaysia (n = 2; 18.18%), Netherlands, Australia, United Kingdom, Kuwait, and Canada (n = 1; 9.09%)."
