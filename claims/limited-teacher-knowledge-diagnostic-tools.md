Mutmainna, Istiyono, E., Haryanto, Retnawati, & Setiawan, C. (2025). Physics Teachers' Perceptions about Diagnostic Assessment of Students' Physics Misconceptions: A Phenomenological Study. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2501.10422](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.10422)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview finding (RQ2): secondary teacher Dodi said of the four-tier term, "I haven't heard that term before, but I have used questions like that. I just didn't know the name for it"; higher-education participant Mita had four-tier training but had not applied such questions in class.

> "it appears that knowledge of diagnostic tools for Physics misconception is still limited among most teachers, except for those in higher education."
