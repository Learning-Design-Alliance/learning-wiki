Paired pre/post Likert analysis of 52 students; Table II reports Communication p 0.035, BF10 0.6 (anecdotal for H0), power 0.36436; Belonging p 0.075, BF10 0.3 (anecdotal for H0), power 0.29360. No statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "BF10 provided evidence in favor of change for all groups except 'Communication' and 'Belonging'. Note that these two categories have low power and thus true effects may not be detected."
