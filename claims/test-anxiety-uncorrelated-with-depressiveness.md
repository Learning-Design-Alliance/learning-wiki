## Evidence

### Hahn Henry 2017

Hahn Henry, Kropp Peter, Kirschstein Timo, Rücker Gernot, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2017). Test anxiety in medical school is unrelated to academic performance but correlates with an effort/reward imbalance. PLoS ONE 12(2): e0171220. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171220

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spearman rank correlation analysis (Bonferroni-corrected) of questionnaire and test data from the 48-student cohort; the results table shows test anxiety at both exam time points was not significant against depressiveness, whereas "anxiety as a trait significantly correlated with depressiveness".

> "while test anxiety neither correlated with depressiveness nor with any of the cognitive performances, anxiety as a trait significantly correlated with depressiveness"
