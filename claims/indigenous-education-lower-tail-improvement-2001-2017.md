## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Restricted first-order stochastic dominance over the two lowest categories shows strong evidence of improvement in the indigenous education distribution, with 2017 dominating 2001 and 2006 at probabilities greater than 0.96. [→ David Gunawan 2022](#david-gunawan-2022)
`q2 i?` The indigenous headcount index (proportion in the two lowest education categories) fell from 0.70 to 0.59 between 2006 and 2017, implying a greater proportion obtaining tertiary education. [→ David Gunawan 2022 (2)](#david-gunawan-2022-2)

## Evidence

### David Gunawan 2022

David Gunawan, William Griffiths, Duangkamon Chotikapanich. (2022). Inequality in Education: A Comparison of Australian Indigenous and Nonindigenous Populations. Working paper, University of Wollongong. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1515/spp-2021-0026](https://doi.org/10.1515/spp-2021-0026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian restricted first-order stochastic dominance analysis of HILDA highest-education categories for the indigenous population, comparing pairs of years over the two lowest categories. The article reports "strong evidence of improvement" with dominance probabilities for 2017 over 2001 and 2006 "both greater than 0.96".

> "we find strong evidence of improvement. The probabilities for 2017 dominating both 2001 and 2006 are both greater than 0.96."

### David Gunawan 2022 (2)

David Gunawan, William Griffiths, Duangkamon Chotikapanich. (2022). Inequality in Education: A Comparison of Australian Indigenous and Nonindigenous Populations. Working paper, University of Wollongong. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1515/spp-2021-0026](https://doi.org/10.1515/spp-2021-0026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Posterior densities for the headcount index H, the proportion of the indigenous population in the two lowest education categories, estimated for each HILDA year. The point estimate shows "a reduction in the point estimate from 0.70 to 0.59 from 2006 to 2017" after little change from 2001 to 2006.

> "We observe little change from 2001 to 2006, but a reduction in the point estimate from 0.70 to 0.59 from 2006 to 2017, implying from 2006 onwards a greater proportion of the population was obtaining a tertiary education."

### David Gunawan 2022 (3)

David Gunawan, William Griffiths, Duangkamon Chotikapanich. (2022). Inequality in Education: A Comparison of Australian Indigenous and Nonindigenous Populations. Working paper, University of Wollongong. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1515/spp-2021-0026](https://doi.org/10.1515/spp-2021-0026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

FSD probability curves plotted for each category (Figure 2) show which categories limit dominance: for 2006 versus 2001 "the minimum value in the probability curves occurs at the third category", and for 2017 versus 2014 the minimum occurs at category 4, so restricting to lower categories raises the dominance probability.

> "The probability Pr[2006 2001]restFSD> is bigger than Pr[2006 2001]FSD> because the minimum value in the probability curves occurs at the third category."
