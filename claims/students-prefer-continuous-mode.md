`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Behavioral observation and qualitative interviews during the three-month MAIC pilot. Students reported preferring 'the "continuous mode"' because it preserved their train of thought; interviewees described pausing to review slides instead of interacting, and one abandoned interactive mode after an unresponsive exchange.

> "when it comes to choosing the class mode, students tend to prefer the "continuous mode," believing that this mode allows them to maintain their train of thought without interruption"
