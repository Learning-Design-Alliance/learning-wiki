## Evidence

### Ito Takamichi 2022

Ito Takamichi, Umemoto Takatoyo. (2022). Examining the causal relationships between interpersonal motivation, engagement, and academic performance among university students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0274229

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

SEM verification of the hypothesized causal model in the 363-student sample, with missing values handled by full information maximum likelihood; the article reports the fit indices as satisfactory and presents significant paths in Table 3 and Fig 3.

> "The fit indices of the model were satisfactory, with χ2(18) = 20.139, p = .325, χ2/df = 1.119, RMSEA = .018, 90% CI [.000, .052], SRMR = .028, CFI = .998, and TLI = .996."
