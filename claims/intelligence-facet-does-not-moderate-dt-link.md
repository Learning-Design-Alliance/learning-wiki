## Evidence

### Gerwig Anne 2021

Gerwig Anne, Miroshnik Kirill, Forthmann Boris, Benedek Mathias, Karwowski Maciej, Holling Heinz. (2021). The Relationship between Intelligence and Divergent Thinking—A Meta-Analytic Update. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence9020023

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator meta-regression coding intelligence facet (g, gf, gc) per coefficient. The moderator test was borderline (p = .056) and no pairwise facet contrast was significant (all ps > .161), contradicting expectations from Silvia (2015) that gf would correlate more strongly.

> "The estimated correlations between DT and intelligence facets were found to be small to moderate and in the range from .23 to .28 across g, gc, and gf (see Table 2). The moderator test was non-significant (QM(2) = 5.77, p = .056)."
