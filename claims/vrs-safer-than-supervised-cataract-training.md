### Dormegny Lea 2024

Dormegny Lea, Lansingh Van Charles, Lejay Anne, Chakfe Nabil, Yaici Rémi, Sauer Arnaud, Gaucher David, Henderson Bonnie An, Thomsen Ann Sofia Skou, Bourcier Tristan. (2024). Virtual reality simulation and real-life training programs for cataract surgery: a scoping review of the literature. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06245-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Scoping review synthesis comparing supervised surgery studies with VRS studies. The review reports complication rates were relatively high compared to VRS studies, with a "median PCR rate of 8% vs. 2.6%". No pooled effect size was computed.

> "The supervised surgery studies reported intensive cataract surgery training with high number of surgeries performed by the trainees. Intra- and post-operative complications were frequently reported (75%): complication rates were relatively high compared to VRS studies (median PCR rate of 8% vs. 2.6%, respectively)"
