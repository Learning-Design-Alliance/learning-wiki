## Evidence

### Phillips SC 2019

Phillips SC, Hawley CE, Triantafylidis LK, Schwartz AW. (2019). Geriatrics 5Ms for Primary Care Workshop. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10814

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot workshop evaluation with 33 resident learners across three internal medicine residency programs and 10 control learners completing the delayed postworkshop evaluation. The comparison of high self-efficacy ratings ("75% had high self-efficacy ratings (score > 3.5)") was not statistically significant; no effect size was printed.

> "Of the learners who completed the workshop, 75% had high self-efficacy ratings (score > 3.5) compared to only 40% of control learners. These trends in self-efficacy ratings were not statistically significant."
