Poquet 2026¶

Poquet, O., Nanduri, M. S., Salinas Loyer, M. X., Stadler, M., Sailer, M., Jovanovic, J. (2026). Profiling Cognitive Offloading in LLM-mediated Synthesis Writing: Volume vs. Content. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10434 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.10434

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

In the volume-based profile analysis, connective density (discourse connectives per sentence in student-written segments) varied significantly across the four profiles. The correlation with prior knowledge was "negatively correlated with prior knowledge and not the number of prompts (r= -.30,p= 0.007)"; |r|=.30 is a medium correlation.

"Connective density also varied significantly across profiles. This was negatively correlated with prior knowledge and not the number of prompts (r= -.30,p= 0.007)."
