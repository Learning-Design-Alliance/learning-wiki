## Evidence

### Emery 2021

Emery, Nathan; Maher, Jessica Middlemis; Ebert-May, Diane. (2021). Environmental influences and individual characteristics that affect learner-centered teaching practices. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250760

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regressions of SCII factor scores on institution size across 32 institutions (N = 352 complete survey responses). Leadership: P = 0.044, r2 = 0.1; Evaluation of Effective Teaching: P = 0.001, r2 = 0.27; both "negatively correlated with institution size".

> "When SCII factor scores were regressed by size of institution [86], both “Leadership” and “Evaluation of Effective Teaching” were negatively correlated with institution size (Fig 4; linear regression: A, Leadership, P = 0.044, r2 = 0.1; B, Evaluation of Effective Teaching, P = 0.001, r2 = 0.27)."

### Emery 2021 (2)

Emery, Nathan; Maher, Jessica Middlemis; Ebert-May, Diane. (2021). Environmental influences and individual characteristics that affect learner-centered teaching practices. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250760

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA and Tukey HSD comparison of SCII factor scores across all participating institutions (Fig 3, N = 32). Other factors (Collegiality, Evaluation of Effective Teaching, Leadership, Support for Teaching Improvement) were relatively consistent.

> "Differences emerged with “Flexibility in Teaching” being significantly higher (P <0.001) and “Mentoring and Material Support” being significantly lower than all other factors (P <0.001)."
