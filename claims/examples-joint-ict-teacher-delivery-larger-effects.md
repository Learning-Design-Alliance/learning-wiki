## Evidence

### López 2026

López, P., Fidalgo, R., Álvarez-Moreno, C., Díaz-Tejedor, R., van den Bergh, H., & Rijlaarsdam, G. (2026). Compositional Analysis of Writing Instructional Practices Performed with Digital Tools: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10153-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Delivery-mode meta-regression (Teacher-only, ICT-only, ICT+Teacher) for the 'provide examples' activity showed a significant omnibus moderation (Fm(2, 11.19) = 12.32, p = .00149); post-hoc tests showed "joint delivery by ICT+Teacher was associated with larger effects than teacher-only delivery". ICT-only did not differ significantly from teacher-only (p = .131).

> "joint delivery by ICT+Teacher was associated with larger effects than teacher-only delivery (b = 0.700, 95% CI [0.072, 1.329], p = .0322)."
