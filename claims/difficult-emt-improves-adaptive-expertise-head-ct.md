# Difficult error management training improves adaptive expertise on novel head CT cases among emergency medicine residents, outperforming both easy EMT and error avoidance training

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Difficult EMT outperformed easy EMT and EAT on adaptive expertise posttest cases (60.6% vs 45.2% vs 40.9%), with a large omnibus effect size (η2 = 0.19). [→ Aliaga L 2024](#aliaga-l-2024)

## Evidence

### Aliaga L 2024

Aliaga L, Bavolek RA, Cooper B, Mariorenzi A, Ahn J, Kraut A, Duong D, Burger C, Gisondi MA. (2024). Error Management Training and Adaptive Expertise in Learning Computed Tomography Interpretation: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.31600

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Multicenter 3-arm randomized clinical trial of 150 posttest-completing emergency medicine residents (of 212 randomized), PGY 1-4 at 7 programs, stratified permuted-block randomization. One-way ANOVA on adaptive expertise posttest scores was "statistically significant, with a large effect size (η2 = 0.19; P < .001)".

> "The 1-way ANOVA results were statistically significant, with a large effect size (η2 = 0.19; P < .001)."

### Aliaga L 2024 (2)

Aliaga L, Bavolek RA, Cooper B, Mariorenzi A, Ahn J, Kraut A, Duong D, Burger C, Gisondi MA. (2024). Error Management Training and Adaptive Expertise in Learning Computed Tomography Interpretation: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.31600

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Posttest-only randomized trial with Bonferroni-adjusted planned contrasts (prespecified α at P < .017). Contrast results: "difficult EMT and easy EMT cohorts, with a medium effect size (η2 = 0.11; P < .001)" and difficult EMT vs EAT η2 = 0.18. No difference was found between easy EMT and EAT.

> "Planned contrasts showed significant differences between the difficult EMT and easy EMT cohorts, with a medium effect size (η2 = 0.11; P < .001), and between the difficult EMT and EAT control cohorts, with a large effect size (η2 = 0.18; P < .001)."
