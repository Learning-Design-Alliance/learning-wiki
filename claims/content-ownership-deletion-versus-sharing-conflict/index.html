Donghwa Lee 2023¶

Donghwa Lee, Hong-hyeon Kim, Seok-Hyun Sung. (2023). Development research on an AI English learning support system to facilitate learner-generated-context-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10172-2

q1 · i? · design · r2

Interview-based case finding: Haru stated he had finished studying the planned vocabulary and deleted all content, never considering sharing his lists with other learners. The authors treat this as an open design problem rather than a measured effect.

"after completing his learning plans, he saw that the content (i.e., his English vocabulary lists) was no longer useful and eventually deleted the content he had made up to that point."
