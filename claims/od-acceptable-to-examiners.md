`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Semi-structured interview findings from the sixteen-examiner pilot. The majority (11/16) found the rubric useful and 14 agreed the OD was a direct assessment of CR skills; two examiners felt it reflected presentation ability rather than reasoning.
