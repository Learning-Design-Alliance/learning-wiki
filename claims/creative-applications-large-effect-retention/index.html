Evidence¶

Özdas and Batdi 2017¶

Faysal Özdas & Veli Batdi. (2017). A Thematic-based Meta Analytic Study Regarding the Effect of Creativity on Academic Success and Learning Retention. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i3.2043

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Meta-analysis of three studies reporting retention scores, analyzed under FEM (ES = 1.244) and REM (ES = 1.311, 95% CI [0.723, 1.900]). The article states "the effect of CTAps is recorded to be positive on retention scores."

"The effect of CTAps on retention scores according to FEM is calculated and the effect size is found to be as ES=1.244. This value is in wide interval according to Cohen’s (1992) classification. Thus, the effect of CTAps is recorded to be positive on retention scores."
