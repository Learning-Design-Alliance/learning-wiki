In the discussion's secondary effect-size analysis of the same trial, the authors report "Cohen’s d = 0.43" and "Cohen’s d = 0.42" for Sessions 2 and 3, noting these did not reach statistical significance but suggest a potentially meaningful contribution of digital training.
