Archibald D, Macdonald Colla J, Plante J, Hogue RJ, Fiallos J. (2014). Residents' and preceptors' perceptions of the use of the iPad for clinical teaching in a family medicine residency program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-174

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey results summarized in Figure 1 on usefulness of the iPad for teaching/learning activities. The article reports output activities were "judged slightly more useful compared to input of information activities", supported by a preceptor quote that the keyboard was too small to type on easily.

> "Activities where the iPad generated an output of information (i.e. listening to audio, reading texts and viewing videos) were judged slightly more useful compared to input of information activities (i.e. collecting data, typing text)."
