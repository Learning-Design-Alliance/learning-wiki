## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Researcher observation during the study indicated that communication of introvert or communication-difficult students increased, responsibility developed, and children were motivated to fulfill tasks. [→ Akçay 2016](#akcay-2016)

## Evidence

### Akçay 2016

Nilüfer Okur Akçay. (2016). Implementation of Cooperative Learning Model in Preschool. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n3p83

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Unstructured researcher observation during the implementation, not a measured comparison; no instrument, ratings or statistics are printed. The author supports it by citing Avcıoğlu (2003) and Tarım and Artut (2004).

> "It was determined during the study with the observation of the researcher that a number of social skills were developed in the students on whom the cooperative learning model was implemented, the communication of the students who had difficulty in terms of communication and even introvert students with their friends increased, the sense of responsibility of the students was developed, the children were motivated to actively fulfill their tasks."
