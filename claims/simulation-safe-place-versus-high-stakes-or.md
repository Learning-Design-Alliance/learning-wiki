## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Focus group participants described simulation-based robotic training outside the OR as a safe environment for learning where mistakes carry no patient risk. [→ Greenberg AL 2022](#greenberg-al-2022)

## Evidence

### Greenberg AL 2022

Greenberg AL, Syed SM, Alseidi A, O'Sullivan PS, Chern H. (2022). Robotic training for medical students: feasibility of a pilot simulation curriculum. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01508-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Finding from inductive thematic analysis of three 30-min focus groups with 13 of the 14 students, facilitated by a non-surgeon surgical educator. The contrast is students' own: "the OR because mistakes are high-stakes" versus the safe simulated setting. Qualitative finding; no effect size.

> "Outside-OR robotic training with simulation, on the other hand, was felt by students to offer a safe place to learn and make mistakes."
