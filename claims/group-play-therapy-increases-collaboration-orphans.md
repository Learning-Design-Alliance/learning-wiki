### Bahareh Mousavi & Sahar Safarzadeh 2016

Bahareh Mousavi & Sahar Safarzadeh. (2016). Effectiveness of the Group Play Therapy on the Insecure Attachment and Social Skills of Orphans in Ahvaz City. International Education Studies, 9(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n9p42

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

MANCOVA-context analysis of the randomized two-group experiment; treatment collaboration means rose from 7.60 (pretest) to 14.53 (posttest) and 15.86 (follow-up), while control stayed near 8. The "collaboration variable in posttest stage" was significant.

> "The collaboration variable in posttest stage (F = 20.18 & p< 0.001) and in the follow-up stage (F = 18.03 & p < 0.001) is significant. Therefore, the third hypothesis is confirmed."
