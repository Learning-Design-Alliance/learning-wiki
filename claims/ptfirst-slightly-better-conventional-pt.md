# Students who took the conventional PT first performed slightly better on the conventional PT (Cohen's d = 0.16)

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` The PTfirst group outperformed the CA-PTfirst group on the conventional PT (d = 0.16), but no order difference appeared on the CA-PT. [→ Van Wijk EV 2024](#van-wijk-ev-2024)

## Evidence

### Van Wijk EV 2024

Van Wijk EV, Donkers J, De Laat PCJ, Meiboom AA, Jacobs B, Ravesloot JH, Tio RA, Van Der Vleuten CPM, Langers AMJ, Bremers AJA. (2024). Computer Adaptive vs. Non-adaptive Medical Progress Testing: Feasibility, Test Performance, and Student Experiences. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/pme.1345

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Order-group comparison in the cross-over study: PTfirst (n = 797) versus CA-PTfirst (n = 635) on the conventional PT, t(1373) = 3.08, p = 0.002, "Cohen's d = 0.16" — a printed effect size below d = 0.2; no order difference on the CA-PT.

> "Students in the PTfirst-group (n = 797; mean (M) = 0.406, SD = 1.06) performed slightly better on the conventional PT compared to students in the CA-Ptfirst-group (n = 635; M = 0.24, SD = 1.03; t(1373) = 3.08; p = 0.002; Cohen's d = 0.16). No difference was found in performance on the CA-PT between both groups (t(1345) = –1.0324, p = 0.302)."
