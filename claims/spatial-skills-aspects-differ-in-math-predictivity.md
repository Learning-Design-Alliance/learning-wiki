## Evidence

### Véronique Cornu 2017

Véronique Cornu, Caroline Hornung, Christine Schiltz, Romain Martin. (2017). The predictive role of spatial skills for arithmetic and number line estimation in kindergarten children. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogdev.2017.09.002

`q3 · i?`

Authors' interpretation of the longitudinal regression findings in the kindergarten sample (N = 125). The article states that "some aspects of spatial skills might be more predictive of mathematical development than others", hedged with "might", and draws the differentiation conclusion from the differing predictor patterns.

> "Our results further show that some aspects of spatial skills might be more predictive of mathematical development than others, underlining the importance to differentiate within the construct of spatial skills when it comes to understanding numerical development."
