### Harendza Sigrid 2019

Harendza Sigrid, Soll Henning, Prediger Sarah, Kadmon Martina, Berberat Pascal O., Oubaid Viktor. (2019). Assessing core competences of medical students with a test for flight school applicants. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1438-1

Results-section comparison of GAP-test ratings: medical students outscored selected flight school applicants on adherence to procedures and communication, with printed statistics "p < .001 (d = .63)" and "p < .01 (d = .62)" from t-tests.

> "Advanced medical students (N = 67) showed significantly higher ratings than selected flight school applicants (N = 117) for the competence domains adherence to procedures (AP), p < .001 (d = .63), and communication (CO), p < .01 (d = .62)"
