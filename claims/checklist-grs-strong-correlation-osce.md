`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of paired checklist and GRS scores from 36 students across 15 OSCE stations, with Pearson and intraclass correlations computed in SPSS. The study reports an overall Pearson correlation of r = 0.924, printed as "overall correlations of 0.924 between the checklist and GRS", and an overall ICC of 0.943, p < 0.001.

> "Pearson correlation also showed overall correlations of 0.924 between the checklist and GRS."
