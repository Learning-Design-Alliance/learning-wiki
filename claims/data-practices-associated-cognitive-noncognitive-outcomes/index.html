Synthesis statement from the review's abstract summarizing RQ3 across the 42 studies. The review defines cognitive outcomes as data practice skills, domain-general skills, and domain-specific knowledge, and non-cognitive outcomes as affective states and attitudes towards data sciences.

"The reviewed studies suggest that engagement in data practices is associated with both cognitive and non-cognitive learning outcomes."
