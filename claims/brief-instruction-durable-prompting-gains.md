# All four instructional approaches, including a two-sentence reminder, produced durable learning-oriented prompting gains that persisted six weeks later

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Xiao 2026

Xiao, R., Ye, R., Hou, X., Wen, J., Kumar, H., Liut, M., & Stamper, J. (2026). Bridging GenAI policy and practice: Brief instruction improves learning-oriented prompting in an introductory programming course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Semester-long randomized controlled trial in CSC108 (analytic n=431 across four conditions). Within-condition paired tests showed significant immediate and delayed gains; the brief reminder condition persisted at six weeks (paired t(101)=-4.00, p<.001, d=0.38).

> "All four conditions produced statistically significant gains in learning-oriented prompting at the immediate post-test, with mean gains ranging from 8 percentage points (SD=15 ) in the brief passive reminder condition (Condition 1) to 51 (SD=28 ) in select-then-write (Condition 4)"

### Xiao 2026 (2)

Xiao, R., Ye, R., Hou, X., Wen, J., Kumar, H., Liut, M., & Stamper, J. (2026). Bridging GenAI policy and practice: Brief instruction improves learning-oriented prompting in an introductory programming course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r?`

Delayed post-test administered as an extra-credit final-examination item without AI access. The reminder condition's gain of 6 percentage points persisted with d=0.38, described as "particularly notable" given no reinforcement.

> "students who read only a two-sentence reminder reframing GenAI as a tutor rather than an answer source produced prompts whose structure remained significantly altered six weeks later at the end of the semester, in the absence of booster sessions or in-course reinforcement (pairedt(101) =−4.00,p< .001,d=0.38)"
