# The task-based cognitive-approach programme produced a significantly higher PET speaking post-test score than regular instruction, with a large effect size

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Experimental-group students exposed to the proposed task-based programme scored significantly higher than control-group students receiving regular instruction on the PET speaking post-test (eta squared = 0.64). [→ Mohammad Mohammadipour & Sabariah MD Rashid 2015](#mohammad-mohammadipour-sabariah-md-rashid-2015)

## Evidence

### Mohammad Mohammadipour & Sabariah MD Rashid 2015

Mohammad Mohammadipour & Sabariah MD Rashid. (2015). The Impact of Task-Based Instruction Program on Fostering ESL Learners' Speaking Ability: A Cognitive Approach. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.2p.113

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on PET speaking post-test scores of 36 experimental and 36 control students after an eleven-week treatment. The article reports t (70) = -11.34, p= 0.00 and the printed effect size eta squared = 0.64, described as a very large difference favouring the experimental group.

> "There was significance difference in scores for control (  M =  13.52 , SD = 2.0) and experimental group, M = 19.19, SD = 2.22; t (70) = -11.34, p= 0.00 (two-tailed). The magnitude of difference in the means was very large (eta squared = 0.64)."
