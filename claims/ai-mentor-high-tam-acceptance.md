Cross-sectional usability and acceptance evaluation of 43 students using a 15-item TAM questionnaire on a 5-point Likert scale. Ratings were high, with "perceived usefulness (Mean = 4.14/5)" and "perceived ease of use (Mean = 4.15/5)".

> "TAM evaluations (N = 43) revealed exceptionally high perceived usefulness (Mean = 4.14/5) and perceived ease of use (Mean = 4.15/5), indicating strong learner acceptance of the cognitive core"
