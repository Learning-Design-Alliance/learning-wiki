### van Rijsewijk 2018

van Rijsewijk, L.G.M., Oldenburg, B., Snijders, T.A.B., Dijkstra, J.K., & Veenstra, R. (2018). A description of classroom help networks, individual network position, and their associations with academic achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208173

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

From the intercept-only multilevel model on 54 classrooms, "14% of the variance in academic achievement could be attributed to differences between classrooms, and 86% could be attributed to differences between students". The full model improved fit (χ2 = 56.3, df = 9, p < .001).

> "Results suggested that 14% of the variance in academic achievement could be attributed to differences between classrooms, and 86% could be attributed to differences between students."
