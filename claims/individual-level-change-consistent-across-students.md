## Evidence

### Stokstad JM 2026

Stokstad JM, Nedrehagen ES, Hufthammer KO. (2026). The Jigsaw teaching method compared to traditional teaching on anatomy and physiology knowledge in higher education – a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26:252. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08608-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Individual-level analysis of MCT score directions for all 47 students (41 at follow-up), reported in Table 3 and Fig. 3; the authors state this pattern "is generally consistent across students" in both groups.

> "Most students (91%) had a (usually large) score improvement from baseline to post-intervention, but from post-intervention to the 3-month follow-up, most students had a (usually smaller) decline (63%) or no change (17%)."
