## Evidence

### Bhuvaneswari S 2026

Bhuvaneswari S, Bamini Devi N, Helen Shaji J C, Lakshmi L. (2026). Voices of learners: a qualitative exploration of nursing students’ satisfaction with simulation-based basic life support training. BMC Medical Education, 26(1). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09260-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic finding from focus group discussions with 40 undergraduate nursing students who completed simulation-based BLS training. Participants reported that manikins, real-time feedback devices, and emergency-like environments made scenarios feel like real clinical situations, with one stating "The scenario felt very real, like what we might face in the ward."

> "Participants consistently highlighted the realism of simulation scenarios as a key factor contributing to their satisfaction. The use of manikins, real-time feedback devices, and emergency-like environments helped students perceive the scenarios as closely resembling real clinical situations."
