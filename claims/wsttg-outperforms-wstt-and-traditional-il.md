## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among first-year Malaysian degree students, the three instructional methods (WSTTG, WSTT, T) produced statistically significant differences in information literacy gain scores, with WSTTG significantly outperforming both comparison methods. [→ Ismail et al. 2018](#ismail-et-al-2018)

## Evidence

### Ismail et al. 2018

Mohd Nasir Ismail, Nurfaezah Mamat, Adnan Jamaludin. (2018). The Effects of Webopac Self Training Tool with Guided Exploration on Information Literacy Skills among First Year Degree Students. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165734

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVA across the three instructional method groups in the quasi-experimental posttest-only nonequivalent groups design (n=150). The article reports "F-value is 13.409 and p-value is 0.000 (< 0.05), effect size is 0.414 and the power is 99.80%" on the WTAILS dependent variable.

> "The ANOVA results of comparing the three groups were statistically significant for WebOPAC Training Assessment Information Literacy Skill (WTAILS ): F-value is 13.409 and p-value is 0.000 (< 0.05), effect size is 0.414 and the power is 99.80%."
