## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin 2011

Shih-Yin Lin, Chandralekha Singh, William Mamudi, Charles Henderson, Edit Yerushalmi. (2011). TA-Designed vs. Research-Oriented Problem Solutions. 2011 PERC Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3680043](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680043)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Findings on the framework-within-choices features from the artifact-comparison study. The authors report that most TAs did not notice F12 as an important consideration, and that the one TA who mentioned it presented a forward solution while 8 TAs wrote backward solutions without mentioning it.

> "Only one TA mentioned this feature. However, this TA presented his/her solutio n in a forward manner. On the other hand, there were 8 TAs who originally presented a backward solution, even though they did not mention F12 in the worksheets."
