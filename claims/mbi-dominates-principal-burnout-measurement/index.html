Evidence¶

Rogers 2025¶

Rogers, L. K., Nguyen, T. D., Timmer, M., & Samson, J. (2025). Principal Burnout: A Systematic Review. Working paper. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED666666

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of 99 empirical studies of principal burnout (1983–early 2022, 18 countries). Among the 84 quantitative or mixed-methods studies, 64% used the MBI and 36% used other instruments. The review notes some studies "relied only on one dimension of the MBI (typically emotional exhaustion), assessed each factor with a single item, or separated principals into arbitrary 'high' or 'low' burnout groups."

"Of the 84 quantitative or mixed methods studies, a little more than half, 64%, used the Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI), and 36% used other instruments other than MBI."
