## Evidence

### Li Lan 2026

Li Lan, Yeonhee Seo, Fan Qianqian. (2026). Enhancing psychological well-being through embodied bilingual teaching: the mediating role of basic psychological needs and learning motivation. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04185-2

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of questionnaire data from 518 university students in bilingual Tai Chi elective courses, tested in AMOS 26.0. All three direct paths were significant; the article reports "β = 0.416, p < 0.001" for basic psychological needs, with model fit χ2/df = 1.210, CFI = 0.985, RMSEA = 0.020.

> "Teacher support had a significantly positive effect on basic psychological needs (β = 0.416, p < 0.001, supporting H1), learning motivation (β = 0.235, p < 0.001, supporting H2), and psychological well-being (β = 0.317, p < 0.001, supporting H3)"
