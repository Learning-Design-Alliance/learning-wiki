## Evidence

### Anderson Steven Mark 2025

Anderson Steven Mark, Gillanders Sarahlouise, Hickey Anne, Offiah Gozie, Davis Niall. (2025). Video Killed the Radio Star—A Meta‐Analysis on Video‐Based Coaching to Improve Surgical Skill. World Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1002/wjs.12494

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of ten RCTs reporting change scores, with 137 VBC and 132 control participants. The pooled effect size was SMD 0.98 (95% CI 0.61, 1.35); change "was significantly higher in the VBC group compared to the control".

> "The overall change in performance from the baseline to final assessment was significantly higher in the VBC group compared to the control, with a SMD of 0.98 (95% CI 0.61, 1.35, Z = 5.19, p < 0.0001, and Figure 3)."
