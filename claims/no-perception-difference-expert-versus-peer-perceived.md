### Jacobsen 2025

Jacobsen, L. J., Mertens, U., Jansen, T., & Weber, K. E. (2025). AI, Expert or Peer? Provider Biases and Feedback Uptake among Pre-Service Teachers. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.16013](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.16013)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Perceived-source models within the 85-participant LLM condition found no expert-versus-peer perceived difference; the article states "Across both sets of models, the difference was between the human and the LLM categories."

> "The coefficient for perceived expert was comparable in size (usefulness: b = 1.00, SE = 0.57, p = .085), and feedback perceived to come from experts and from peers did not differ (all ps ≥ .57; see supplementary Table S2)."
