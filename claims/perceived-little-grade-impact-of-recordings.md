### Luminica Topale 2016

Luminica Topale. (2016). The strategic use of lecture recordings to facilitate an active and self-directed learning approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0723-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-reported perceived grade impact (Table 8) from the survey of 281 medical students; grades were not formally analyzed in the study, so these figures are perceptions only.

> "While over 28 % of the group felt that use of recordings significantly improved their performance, 58 % felt there was little or no positive impact on their grades. Few participants felt their grades had dropped as a result of using recordings (1.1 %)."
