## Evidence

### Securro 2010

Securro, S., Jr., Jones, J. D., & Cantrell, D. R. (2010). Effects of Extensive Engagement with Computer-Based Reading and Language Arts Instructional Software on Reading Achievement for Sixth Graders. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology, 6(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102789

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Independent Samples t-test on Westest reading/language arts scaled scores for 86 randomly assigned sixth graders (43 per grouping) in a two-group quasi-experimental post-test-only design. The article reports the printed effect size Cohen's d = .92, stating "an effect size measure of .92 (Cohen's d, with pooled s)" favoring the computer-based grouping.

> "a significant difference in scores resulted between Computer-based  (M= 667.4, Sd., 28) and Control M= 638.8, Sd., 35) t (83) = 4.254, p .000, CI 95% - 41.99 to -15.23. These data resulted in an effect size measure of .92 (Cohen's d, with pooled s)."
