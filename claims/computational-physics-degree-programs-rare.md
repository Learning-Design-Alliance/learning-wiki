Marcos D. Caballero and Laura Merner. (2018). On the Prevalence and Nature of Computational Instruction in Undergraduate Physics Programs across the United States. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.020129)

Survey results on formal programs (Figure 5): percentages of departments offering computational physics majors and minors are "quite low, 7% and 3% respectively", increasing to 18% and 8% when a single faculty member's report counts for the department.

> "The percentages of such departments are quite low, 7% and 3% respectively. These percentages increase slightly for both majors (18%) and minors (8%) when considering the report of a single faculty member (263 departments)."
