## Evidence

### Tarkowski Radoslaw 2015

Tarkowski Radoslaw, Vetto John T. (2015). Reflections: Surgical Education-the Times they are a-Changin': Lessons Learned from the 3rd MAYMET-ESO Joint Meeting. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-015-0831-2

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The commentary reports, citing Klampfer and a systematic review by Hull et al., that "non-technical skills... are now known to have the same magnitude of impact on a surgical outcome as surgical technique", while remaining "rather underestimated and all too missing in training programs".

> "Non-technical skills, what Klampfer has called "observable, non-technical behaviors that contribute to superior or substandard performance" [1], are now known to have the same magnitude of impact on a surgical outcome as surgical technique"
