`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group discussions with LHWs, reported in the Results section on maternal health responsibilities, describing current practice when hypertension is suspected. The FGDs found that LHWs "do not carry any antihypertensive agents for pregnant women" and refer suspected or confirmed hypertension cases to the nearest public facility.
