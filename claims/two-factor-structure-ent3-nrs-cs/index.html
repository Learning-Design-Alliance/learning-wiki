Evidence¶

Verkerk 2026¶

Verkerk, J. C., van de Rijt, B., & van Viersen, S. (2026). Development of numerical relational and counting skills as components of early numeracy. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102313

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Confirmatory factor analyses on ENT-3 data at each time point showed the two-factor NRS/CS model had excellent fit and "outperformed the one-factor model at T1, T3, and T4" (all p < .001); at T2, T5, and T6 differences were not significant, though the two-factor structure was retained.

"The two-factor model outperformed the one-factor model at T1, T3, and T4 as confirmed by chi-square difference tests (T1: Δχ2 (1) = 7.76, p < .001; T3: Δχ2 (1) = 7.59, p < .001; T4: Δχ2 (1) = 6.21, p < .001)."
