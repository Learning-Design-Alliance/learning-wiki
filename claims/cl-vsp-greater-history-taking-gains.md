## Evidence

### Xu Liyuan 2025

Xu Liyuan, Xu Qinrong, Liu Chunya, Chen Baozhen, Wang Chunxia. (2025). Virtual Standardized Patients for Improving Clinical Thinking Ability Training in Residents: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/73196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Medical interview assessment from the randomized controlled trial after 4 weeks of training. History-taking improvement was "significantly higher than those in the control group" (Cohen d=1.17, P<.001); post-training scores also favored the VSP group (Cohen d=1.35, P<.001).

> "the improvement in medical history-taking ability scores (mean 12.30, SD 3.43 vs mean 7.77, SD 2.84; F1, 59=31.09; Cohen d=1.17, 95% CI 9.04-11.04; P<.001) were significantly higher than those in the control group."
