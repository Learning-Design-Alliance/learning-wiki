Proficiency projection analysis (Table 3.4) from the Minnesota MCA-III Mathematics linking study, computing probabilities via the standard normal cumulative distribution function using expected growth and the conditional standard deviation of growth. The article prints that a fall RIT of 202 carries "a 97% chance of reaching Meets proficiency or higher."

"For example, a Grade 3 student who obtained a MAP Growth Mathematics score of 202 in the fall has a 97% chance of reaching Meets proficiency or higher on the MCA-III test."

Related Claims¶

  • The published norms allow normative interpretation of both status and fall-to-spring growth, e.g., an Algebra 1 student at the 55th fall percentile has an expected gain of 8 RIT with growth SD of 9, and an 11-RIT gain places the student at the 62nd growth percentile. — related
  • Norm shifts can raise achievement percentiles for schools serving struggling students even when RIT gains and projected proficiency do not improve — related
  • MAP Growth RIT scores correlate strongly (r = 0.92–0.93) with state summative mathematics scores in the Minnesota MCA-III linking study — related
  • The conditional growth index serves as a descriptive indicator of student learning progress — related
