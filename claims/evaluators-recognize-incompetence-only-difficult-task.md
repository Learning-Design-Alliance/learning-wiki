## Evidence

### Mohtashami Fariba MD 2014

Mohtashami Fariba MD, Thiele Allison MD, PhD, Karreman Erwin PhD, Thiel John MD. (2014). Comparing Technical Dexterity of Sleep-Deprived Versus Intoxicated Surgeons. JSLS : Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2014.00142

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Competency ratings by three blinded reviewers of anonymous video clips (Tables 2 and 3): intoxicated clips were rated competent 83% (task 1) and 72% (task 2) versus 59% and 50% for rested and postcall combined; correct incompetence prediction reached 77% only for task 3.

> "Thus, evaluators were unable to recognize the lack of competence for the simple tasks, and the correct recognition of incompetence was significant only for the difficult task (Table 3)."
