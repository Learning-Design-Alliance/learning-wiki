### Singas Effie 2025

Singas Effie, Schwartzman-Morris Julie, Whitson Matthew J., Bashir Humza, Jacome Sonia, Friedman Karen A. (2025). Procedural training for internal medicine residents pursuing subspecialty training: a national survey of fellowship program directors. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2499050

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre-planned qualitative analysis of open-ended comments by two independent reviewers with third-author adjudication; of 100 responses, 62 were assigned to themes. The largest category was uninterpretable, and some directors were unaware of the ABIM change.

> "Major themes surrounding fellowship director concerns identified included the following: Core Competency Skills, Practical Concerns, Fellow Readiness, Culture Shift, Subspeciality Specific Competency, and Fellowship Responsibility."
