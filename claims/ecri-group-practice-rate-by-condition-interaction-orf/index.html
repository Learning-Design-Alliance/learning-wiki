Fien 2015¶

Fien, H., Smith, J. L. M., Smolkowski, K., Baker, S. K., Nelson, N. J., & Chaparro, E. (2015). An Examination of the Efficacy of a Multitiered Intervention on Early Reading Outcomes for First Grade Students at-Risk for Reading Difficulties. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 48, 602-621. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED601958

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Interaction analysis of condition by rate of independent group practice opportunities on ORF in the cluster-randomized trial, depicted in Figure 1. The article reports the significant interaction (t = 2.55, df = 14, p = .0230) and that "the lower of the 95% confidence bounds crosses the zero" at 1.8 attempts per minute.

"Intervention schools performed statistically significantly better than comparison schools when their teachers offered group practice at a rate of a rate of approximately 1.8 opportunities per minute or better, as shown on Figure 1."
