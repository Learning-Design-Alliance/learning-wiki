## Evidence

### Mommert A 2020

Mommert A, Wagner J, Jünger J, Westermann J. (2020). Exam performance of different admission quotas in the first part of the state examination in medicine: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02069-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive pattern from the same ANOVA on 655 students' M1 grades; Table 2 reports oral means of 2.0 (GPA), 2.3 (selection), 2.6 (WT), 2.8 (ex-ante) and 3.0 (foreign), F(4, 636) = 13.19, p < .001. The narrower GPA-selection gap is the authors' interpretation.

> "A similar pattern was observed for the oral part of the exam, with the difference that students in the WT quota performed slightly better than students in the ex-ante quota."
