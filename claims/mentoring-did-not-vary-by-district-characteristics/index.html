Descriptive subgroup comparisons with statistical tests across district subgroups defined by National School Lunch Program eligibility, Missouri Assessment Program proficiency, and locale (tables C8–C10). The report found "no clear patterns" in direction of differences.

"The mentoring that first-year teachers reported receiving did not differ in any statistically significant way across districts with different characteristics."
