## Evidence

### Zhan Xinju 2026

Zhan Xinju, Yu Weihua, Cai Jianshu, Chen Jionghuang. (2026). From knowledge to judgment: A three-year longitudinal analysis of artificial intelligence large language model performance on the Chinese national nurse licensing examination. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0353059

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal observational evaluation of 15 LLMs on 9,800 NNLE multiple-choice questions (2022–2025) using standardized zero-shot prompting. The article reports a "63% relative improvement among top-performing models" and significant linear improvement (R² = 0.847, p < 0.001) with an average annual accuracy increase of 10.2 percentage points among top-quartile models.

> "Overall examination accuracy increased from a baseline range of 42–49% in 2022–72–79% in 2025, representing a 63% relative improvement among top-performing models."
