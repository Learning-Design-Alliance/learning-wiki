## Evidence

### Larkins Kirsten M. 2022

Larkins Kirsten M., Mohan Helen M., Gray Matthew, Costello Daniel M., Costello Anthony J., Heriot Alexander G., Warrier Satish K. (2022). Transferability of robotic console skills by early robotic surgeons: a multi-platform crossover trial of simulation training. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01475-w

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative feedback collected via unstructured interviews and a questionnaire from all ten participants; all also rated skills as absolutely (n = 5) or somewhat (n = 5) transferrable, with no responses suggesting trouble transferring skills.

> "All ten participants felt that multiplatform access was beneficial when completing basic robotic simulation training (very useful n = 7, somewhat useful n = 3)."
