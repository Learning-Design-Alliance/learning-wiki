## Evidence

### N. Mitchell 2025

N. Mitchell, M. Rashid, J. Foulds, and K. Forbes. (2025). Thinking About the Why: A Qualitative Study on Students' Perspectives of Paediatric Team‐Based Learning Discussions. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70161

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus-group theme analysis found TBL gave students, given their limited clinical experience, the opportunity to identify knowledge gaps and learn from others, and that "gaining experience in establishing consensus" was described as important for future clinical practice.

> "Co‐learning reflects the learning that takes place by working through cases alongside their peers and encompasses the social dynamics of the group learning process."
