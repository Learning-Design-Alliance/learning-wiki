Eugenio Tufino 2026¶

Eugenio Tufino. (2026). A multi-agent AI classroom based on dual-process reasoning hazards: a pilot with prospective physics teachers. https://huggingface.co/spaces

q3 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive coding of eight simulation session logs at the level of the teacher turn. Strategy use was "strongly uniform", and the hazard-specific strategies were "rarely matched to the hazard of the agent addressed".

"Of the 71 substantive teacher turns, 49 (69.0%) were guiding questions, 10 (14.1%) cognitive -conflict questions, 10 (14.1%) falling -back questions and 2 re -explanations."

Eugenio Tufino 2026 (2)¶

Eugenio Tufino. (2026). A multi-agent AI classroom based on dual-process reasoning hazards: a pilot with prospective physics teachers. https://huggingface.co/spaces

q3 · i? · design · r2

Keyword-based count over session logs, contrasted with written sheets: 5 of 12 PRE sheets and 7 of 13 POST sheets contained DPT terms. The contrast tracks distance from the live exchange, not before versus after.

"The vocabulary of the framework the participants had been taught — hazard, process 1/2, bias, intuition — appeared in only 2 of the 71 substantive teacher turns."
