## Evidence

### Gravity 2020

Gravity, Quantum Fields and Information research group, Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute). (2020). On the efficacy of virtual seminars. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.09922](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.09922)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

First-hand cost accounting from the group's own virtual seminar series, not a controlled comparison. The authors state that "eighty virtual talks save costs amounting to more than one year of a Ph.D. student salary in Germany" and note additional savings from curbing long-haul travel.

> "If we assume that organizing an average speakers visit within Europe costs 500 EUR, a rather modest estimate, then eighty virtual talks save costs amounting to more than one year of a Ph.D. student salary in Germany"
