### Alexia Katrimpouza 2017

Alexia Katrimpouza, Nikolaos Tselios and Maria-Christina Kasimati. (2017). Twitter adoption, students' perceptions, Big Five personality traits and learning outcome: Lessons learned from 3 case studies. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.04047)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of tweet counts in the first study (N=19) showed comments on other students' tweets (mean=21.42) exceeded weekly-activity tweets (mean=8.9) and course questions (mean=1.7); the same ordering was reported in the second and third studies. No inferential test is printed.

> "The greatest activity was observed to the comments to other students' tweets (mean=21.42, SD=34.04), in comparison to the tweets made fo r the educational weekly activities (mean=8.9, SD=4.8) or tweets-questions related to the course ( mean=1.7, SD=1.9)."
