## Evidence

### Weckström Tarja 2023

Weckström Tarja, Elovainio Marko, Pulkki-Råback Laura, Suokas Kimmo, Komulainen Kaisla, Mullola Sari, Böckerman Petri, Hakulinen Christian. (2023). School achievement in adolescence and the risk of mental disorders in early adulthood: a Finnish nationwide register study. Molecular Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-023-02081-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Nationwide Finnish register cohort of 1,070,880 individuals followed from age 15–16 to diagnosis, emigration, death, or end of 2017, analyzed with Cox proportional hazards models adjusted for covariates (Model 2). The printed effect size is HR = 2.45 (95% CI 2.38–2.51) at −2SD, described as "a 2.4-fold risk (HR = 2.45, 95% CI 2.38–2.51)" versus average achievers.

> "Compared with the average level of school achievement, the poorest level of school achievement (−2SD) was associated with a 2.4-fold risk (HR = 2.45, 95% CI 2.38–2.51) of any subsequent mental disorder, and the highest level of school achievement (+2SD) with almost half the risk (HR = 0.64, 95% CI = 0.60–0.68)."
