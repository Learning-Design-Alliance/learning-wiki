Damşa 2014¶

Damşa, C. I. (2014). The multi-layered nature of small-group learning: Productive interactions in object-oriented collaboration. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. (2014) 9:247–281. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-014-9193-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

In-depth qualitative analysis and descriptive statistics of interaction data and developing knowledge objects (research reports) collected during a 20-week project period with five student groups; the study reports that "productive interactions can take different forms" with discourse-based and object-oriented the most relevant patterns.
