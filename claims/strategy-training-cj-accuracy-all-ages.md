## Evidence

### von der Linden et al. 2015

Nicole von der Linden, Elisabeth Löffler, Wolfgang Schneider. (2015). Effects of a Short Strategy Training on Metacognitive Monitoring across the Life-span. Frontline Learning Research, 3(4), 37-55. http://dx.doi.org/10.14786/flr.v3i4.196

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on CJ accuracy (gamma correlations) in the life-span experiment (N = 160). The strategy-instruction condition showed higher mean gamma correlations (.41 vs. .33), a small but significant effect, η2 = .03.

> "Furthermore, a main effect of strategy instruction was found (F(1,151) = 4.75; p < .05; η2 = .03). Subjects in the strategy instruction condition had higher mean gamma correlations (.41) than participants without such instruction (.33)"
