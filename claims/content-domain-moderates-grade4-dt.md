## Evidence

### Said-Metwaly 2019

Said-Metwaly, S., Fernández-Castilla, B., Kyndt, E., Van den Noortgate, W., & Barbot, B. (2019). Does the Fourth-Grade Slump in Creativity Actually Exist? A Meta-Analysis of the Development of Divergent Thinking in School-Age Children and Adolescents. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Does+the+Fourth-Grade+Slump+in+Creativity+Actually+Exist

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis in Results of the Grade 3–4 overall DT difference found task content domain significant; the SMDs for figural (0.10) and verbal (-0.02) tasks were each non-significant, both indicating a plateau. The SMDs are descriptive moderator estimates without a printed effect size for the moderation itself.

> "The obtained SMD was positive when using figural tasks (0.10) and negative when using verbal tasks (-0.02). However, both SMDs were not significantly different from zero (p = .36 and .84 for figural and verbal content domains, respectively), suggesting a plateau from Grade 3 to Grade 4."
