Evidence¶

Gehlbach 2010¶

Gehlbach, Hunter. (2010). The social side of school: Why teachers need social psychology. Educational Psychology Review 22, no. 3: 349-362. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9138-3

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the review's framing section, citing Fine (1991) and Juvonen (2006) for the belonging-dropout link and noting Latino dropout rates over 20% per NCES (2009). The review offers this as an asserted cost, not a tested result of its own.

"students who experience a diminished sense of belonging at school are less likely to remain motivated and engaged in school; consequently, they are more likely to drop out"
