### Satoshi Takahashi 2021

Satoshi Takahashi, Hiroki Kuno, Atsushi Yoshikawa. (2021). Extracting candidate factors affecting long-term trends of student abilities across subjects. A preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.06446](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.06446)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multivariate logistic regression in evaluation experiment 1 comparing "stay high stably" versus "decrease from high", using fifth-grade national language item scores as explanatory variables. Group 1's R-squared was .15 (p-value .18); group 2's was .29 (p-value .02).

> "The results demonstrate that the common variation factor between groups 1 and 2 was “interpret the information of the text and make a supplementary statement.”"
