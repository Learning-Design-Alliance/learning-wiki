## Evidence

### Filipe Arantes Fernandes 2023

Filipe Arantes Fernandes, Claudia Susie Camargo Rodrigues, Eldanae Nogueira Teixeira and Claudia Werner. (2023). Immersive Learning Frameworks: A Systematic Literature Review. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1109/tlt.2023.3242553](https://doi.org/10.1109/tlt.2023.3242553)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 15 selected primary studies (from 1721 results) categorizing frameworks by purpose. The review reports that theoretical frameworks model relationships between factors influencing learning outcomes, while practical frameworks establish "guidelines or development models that support the production of immersive educational environments."

> "Theoretical frameworks are models that establish the relationship between factors that inﬂuence learning outcomes or the adoption of immersive technologies, as well as elements that support the design of learning activities in immersive educational environments."
