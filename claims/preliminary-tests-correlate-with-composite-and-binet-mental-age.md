`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A preliminary investigation in which 29 pupils in grades 4 to 8 of a small school were tested with fifteen tests. Table I reports each test's correlation with a composite and with mental age from the Stanford Revision of the Binet Scale, with the printed result "Correlation of Mental Age with Composite Score: .94". The author cautions the coefficients are of suggestive value only.
