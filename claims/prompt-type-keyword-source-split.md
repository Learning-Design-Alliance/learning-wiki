## Evidence

### Pablo Flores Romero 2025

Pablo Flores Romero, Kin Nok Nicholas Fung, Guang Rong & Benjamin Ultan Cowley. (2025). Structured human-LLM interaction design reveals exploration and exploitation dynamics in higher education content generation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00332-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of 76 prompts across 25 structured GPT interactions. Table 3 shows exploration prompts used guide keywords 100% and GPT keywords 0%; exploitation prompts used guide keywords 42.5% and GPT keywords 60%. 14 of 19 exploitation-prompt users included at least one GPT keyword.

> "exploration prompts exclusively incorporated guide's keywords, whereas exploitation prompts mainly employed GPT keywords identiﬁed within the generated scenarios (Table 3). Notably, 14 out of 19 participants that used exploitation prompts used at least one GPT keyword with them."
