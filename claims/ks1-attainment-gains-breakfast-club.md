# Providing schools with support to run a free universal before-school breakfast club raised Year 2 (KS1) attainment by around two months' additional progress in maths, reading and writing

## Evidence

### Crawford 2016

Crawford, C., Edwards, A., Farquharson, C., Greaves, E., Trevelyan, G., Wallace, E., & White, C. (2016). Magic Breakfast: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN14385822

`q4 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Results table from a cluster-randomised controlled trial (106 schools, around 8,600 pupils) comparing standardised KS1 outcomes of intervention and 'business as usual' control schools. All three KS1 effect sizes were positive and statistically significant: maths effect size 0.149, reading 0.104, writing 0.138, each rated as about two months' progress.
