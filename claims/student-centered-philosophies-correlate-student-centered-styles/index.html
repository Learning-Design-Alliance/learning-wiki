Evidence¶

Emel Saritaş 2016¶

Emel Saritaş. (2016). Relationship between philosophical preferences of classroom teachers and their teaching styles. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2787

q2 · i1 · associational · r1

Pearson correlation analysis (N=301) on the same survey data; Table 4 shows experimentalism with personal r=.571, facilitator r=.532, delegator r=.479, and existentialism with facilitator r=.579 and delegator r=.579, all significant at 0.01. The text describes these as "a medium and positive relationship".

"Considering the teaching styles of teachers who adopt experimentalist philosophy, the highest level of relationship is with personal teaching style (r=0.571)."
