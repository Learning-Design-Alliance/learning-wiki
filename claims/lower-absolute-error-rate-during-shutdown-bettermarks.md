## Evidence

### Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann 2021

Spitzer Markus Wolfgang Hermann, Musslick Sebastian (2021). Academic performance of K-12 students in an online-learning environment for mathematics increased during the shutdown of schools in wake of the COVID-19 pandemic. PLoS ONE 16(8): e0255629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed model on a within-group cohort of 2,556 German students in grades 4-10 who used Bettermarks both before and during the shutdown, controlling for repetitions, assignments, and cumulative assignments. The analysis found "lower absolute error rates during the shutdown than before the shutdown", contrary to the authors' hypothesis.

> "The absolute error rate of students did significantly differ between the two time windows (b = -2.37e-02; t = -8.39; p < .001), with lower absolute error rates during the shutdown than before the shutdown."
