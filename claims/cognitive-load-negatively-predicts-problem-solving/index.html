Sabrina Ludwig 2026¶

Sabrina Ludwig, Andreas Rausch, Michelle Taub. (2026). Effects of instructional design, instructional preferences, and cognitive load on problem solving and knowledge acquisition in a computer-based office simulation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102255

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA assessing instructional design effects on problem-solving performance with intrinsic cognitive load during the scenario as covariate (Table 5; n = 73). The cognitive load term was significant at p = .01 with beta-coefficient of -.29; the instructional design main effect was not significant (p = .09).
