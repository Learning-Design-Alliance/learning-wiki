Correlational analysis of the 1990 Cohort Study (Table 3), covering aAH5, GCSE/O-level, A-level, BMS, Finals, MRCP(UK) parts and Specialist Register for entrants. The article reports the exceptions to significance were "for the aAH5 test of aptitude"; the abstract adds the AH5's significant correlations were inconsistent.
