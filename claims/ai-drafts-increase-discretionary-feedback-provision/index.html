Evidence¶

Mahinpei 2026¶

Mahinpei, R., Dean, V., Fong, R., Liu, L. T., & Ribeiro, M. H. (2026). AI Assistance for Discretionary Work: Increasing Feedback Provision in Higher Education. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03095 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.03095

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-registered semester-long randomized field experiment in a 300-level ML course with 11 TAs and 88 students; question-level randomization with rotating assignment across four homework assignments. Within-question linear probability model with homework-question fixed effects estimated a "10.81 percentage points" increase in feedback provision (p < 0.001).

"We find that treatment assignment increased feedback provision by 10.81 percentage points (SE = 1.10, 95% CI [8.60, 13.00], 𝑝< 0.001)"
