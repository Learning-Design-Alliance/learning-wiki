## Evidence

### Charumanee 2014

Charumanee, N. (2014). IDEA SHARING: THE USE OF READ-SHARE-ACT TO PROMOTE EXTENSIVE READING. PASAA, 47. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1077997

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Findings on the A (Act) stage: despite limited rehearsal time and nervousness, students found rehearsal "good fun", shows went well, and "most students said the acting encouraged them to be more confident in speaking English".

> "Play rehearsal and play performance were the stages perceived as the most exciting and enjoyable as far as the RSA was concerned."
