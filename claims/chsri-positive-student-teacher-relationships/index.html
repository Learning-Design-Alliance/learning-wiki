Evidence¶

David Stevens 2008¶

David Stevens, Sue Sporte, Sara Ray Stoelinga, Alissa Bolz. (2008). Lessons from High Performing Small High Schools in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/lessons-high-performing-small-high-schools-chicago

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative observation of student-teacher interactions in 23 classes. Researchers recorded teachers expressing concern about well-being and academic progress, praising students, offering concrete encouragement, and inviting students to seek help outside class hours.

"Almost all of the classrooms contained concrete evidence of generally positive and mutually respectful student-teacher relationships. For example, many of the teachers we observed expressed concern about students’ well-being."
