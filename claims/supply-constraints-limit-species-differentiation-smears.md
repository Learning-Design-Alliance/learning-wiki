### Moura Sofia 2014

Moura Sofia, Fançony Cláudia, Mirante Clara, Neves Marcela, Bernardino Luís, Fortes Filomeno, Sambo Maria do Rosário, Brito Miguel. (2014). Impact of a training course on the quality of malaria diagnosis by microscopy in Angola. Malaria Journal, Volume 13, Article 437. https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2875-13-437

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' Discussion statement about supply constraints. Bengo delivered 200 pre-course slides of which 116 (58.0%) lacked the thin smear, decreasing to 20.8% post-course.

> "In the present study, Bengo laboratory performed a thin smear in only 60.5% of the samples mainly due to the lack of methanol."
