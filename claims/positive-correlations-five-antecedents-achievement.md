## Evidence

### Tang Chaoyong 2026

Tang Chaoyong, Shi Yongzhi, Jiang Fan, Chen Wanming. (2026). The combined effects of teachers’ caring behavior and academic self-efficacy on the academic achievement of Chinese undergraduates: a fsQCA approach. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05227-5

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis (diagnostic, not hypothesis-testing) in the survey of 276 undergraduates: all five bivariate correlations with achievement were positive and significant, with printed effect sizes γ = 0.541, γ = 0.471, γ = 0.558, γ = 0.308, and γ = 0.504; 95% confidence intervals excluded zero and all magnitudes were below 0.6.

> "academic achievement was significantly and positively correlated with conscientiousness (γ = 0.541, p < 0.05), supportiveness (γ = 0.471, p < 0.05), academic ability self-efficacy (γ = 0.558, p < 0.05), inclusiveness (γ = 0.308, p < 0.05), and academic behavior self-efficacy (γ = 0.504, p < 0.05)"
