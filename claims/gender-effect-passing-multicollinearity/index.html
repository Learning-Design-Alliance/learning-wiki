Zhang et al. 2014¶

Yi Zhang, Qiang Fei, Munir Quddus, Carolyn Davis. (2014). An examination of the impact of early intervention on learning outcomes of at-risk students. Research in Higher Education Journal, Volume 26. http://www.aabri.com/copyright.html

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Univariate logistic regression in the matched-sample study of 128 at-risk students, where "The odds of passing the class are 2.44 higher for female students" (Male=0, Female=1); no standardised effect size was printed.

"Column (3) shows that female students have a higher chance to pass the class than male students. The odds of passing the class are 2.44 higher for female students."

Zhang et al. 2014 (2)¶

Yi Zhang, Qiang Fei, Munir Quddus, Carolyn Davis. (2014). An examination of the impact of early intervention on learning outcomes of at-risk students. Research in Higher Education Journal, Volume 26. http://www.aabri.com/copyright.html

q2 · i? · associational · r1

In the combined regression the gender coefficient was insignificant; the authors attribute this to multicollinearity, since gender and advising correlated at 0.296 and more female students sought advising.

"Results of regression without advising are shown in Column (6) and gender becomes significant (at 5% level) when advising is excluded in the regression."
