International Journal of Colorectal Disease. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-05078-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Short-term clinical outcomes in the same 36-case cohort stratified at case 17, categorical comparisons by Fisher's exact test. "Two severe complications" occurred in the late phase; the study was not powered for rare adverse events, and no effect size is printed.

> "Two severe complications (Clavien–Dindo grade ≥ III) occurred in the late phase and none in the early phase (p = 0.487); however, the limited sample size precludes drawing any conclusions regarding safety differences between phases"

### Huang Shih-Feng 2026 (2)

Huang Shih-Feng, Tan Yung-Lin, Hsu Chao-Wen, Tseng Hsin-Ping, Miskovic Danilo, Wu Chih-Chien. (2026). Technical proficiency assessment of robotic intracorporeal single-stapling colorectal anastomosis using video-based RA-CUSUM. International Journal of Colorectal Disease. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-05078-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same cohort, same phase stratification. The authors note the 5.3% late-phase leak rate is consistent with literature rates for low pelvic colorectal anastomosis (0.7%–24.5%, median 7.3%). No effect size is printed.

> "One case of anastomotic leakage was observed in the late phase (5.3%, 1/19), with no cases reported in the early phase (0/17); this difference was not statistically significant (p = 1.000, Fisher’s exact test)"
