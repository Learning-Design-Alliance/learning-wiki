q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

The review describes the most detailed assessment method, by Scherrer et al: a collaboration between librarians and medical faculty to train librarians and health professionals to teach EBM principles, evaluated via a questionnaire administered by telephone interview. The review reports its "components of this questionnaire may constitute a basis for the development of an assessment tool".

"The components of this questionnaire may constitute a basis for the development of an assessment tool to evaluate teaching the teachers courses for EBM [16]."
