## Evidence

### Potkin KT 2012

Potkin KT, Bunney WE Jr (2012) Sleep Improves Memory: The Effect of Sleep on Long Term Memory in Early Adolescence. PLoS ONE 7(8): e42191. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0042191

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sensitivity analysis of the same randomized adolescent experiment re-including the three excluded outliers (n = 40). The sleep group's advantage grew to "a 32.7% increase" with t = 2.76 and p<0.009, confirming the primary finding.

> "When the three outliers are included, the number correct for recall of the paired-associates was statistically greater for the sleep group (7.5) compared to the no sleep group (5.9, t = (2.76), df (1,37), p,0.009), a 32.7% increase."
