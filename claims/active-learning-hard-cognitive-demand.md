## Evidence

### Wyse 2018

Wyse, S. A., & Soneral, P. A. G. (2018). “Is This Class Hard?” Defining and Analyzing Academic Rigor from a Learner’s Perspective. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0278

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Hard-attribution responses from students in the active-learning course (100-level n = 45, 300-level n = 5); chi-square comparison differed (χ2 = 21.61, df = 6, p = 0.0014). Both groups cited cognitive demand, workload, and low preparation; only 100-level students cited pace and unclear importance.

> "Patterns in responses for 100- and 300-level students differed, largely due to the absence of responses in the last four categories (χ2 = 21.61, df = 6, p = 0.0014)."
