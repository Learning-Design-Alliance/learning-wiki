## Evidence

### Zhang Lina 2026

Zhang Lina, Yan Mengling, Qiu Yiwen, Cai Haolei, Yu Yunxian. (2026). Effectiveness of the “Level-Points” online training model and influencing factors in enhancing scoring accuracy among surgical examiners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10033-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-tests across the five dimensions in the same 670-examiner cohort. "Statistically significant reductions were consistently observed across all five assessment dimensions"; the patient encounter dimension improved most (6.83 points, d = -1.15) and communication skills showed the lowest post deviation (14.03 points).

> "Statistically significant reductions were consistently observed across all five assessment dimensions (all P < 0.001, all adjusted P < 0.001), with effect sizes ranging from small-to-medium to large (Cohen’s d: -0.41 to -1.15)."
