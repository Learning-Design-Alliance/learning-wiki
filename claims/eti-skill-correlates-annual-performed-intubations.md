## Evidence

### Gillett Brian MD; Saloum David MD 2019

Gillett Brian MD; Saloum David MD, MACM; Aghera Amish MD; Marshall John P. MD, MBA. (2019). Skill Proficiency is Predicted by Intubation Frequency of Emergency Medicine Attending Physicians. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2019.6.42946

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional study of 32 attending physicians (24 EM, 8 PEM) assessed on a TruCorp Airsim Advance simulator; two-year EHR look-back supplied intubation counts. The article reports "r = 0.6, p < 0.001" for performed intubations per year.

> "Scores correlated well with the average number of intubations performed per year (r = 0.6, p < 0.001)"
