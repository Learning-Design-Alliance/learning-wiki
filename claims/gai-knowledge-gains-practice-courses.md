## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Learning with GAI in practice courses resulted in higher knowledge scores (SMD 0.89, 95% CI 0.25–1.54; p < 0.01), whereas it had little effect on theory courses (SMD −0.25). [→ Li Juan 2025](#li-juan-2025)

## Evidence

### Li Juan 2025

Li Juan, Yin Kaiyu, Wang Yida, Jiang Xiaoqin, Chen Dongxu. (2025). Effectiveness of generative artificial intelligence-based teaching versus traditional teaching methods in medical education: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07750-2

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis by course classification across the included RCTs. Practice courses (5 studies, 154 GAI vs 151 traditional) showed a pooled effect size of SMD = 0.89 (95% CI 0.25–1.54, p < 0.01); theory courses (6 studies) showed SMD −0.25 (95% CI −1.00 to 0.50); subgroup-difference p = 0.02.
