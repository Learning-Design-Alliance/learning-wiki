## Evidence

### Moon 2026

Moon, Eunkyung and Shin, Won Sug. (2026). Exploring synchronous, asynchronous, and conventional online courses in higher education. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0345955

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Latent mean analysis of survey data from 795 undergraduates in South Korea, with COC as reference group and rotated references for all pairwise comparisons. The study reports "higher mean values for all factors than Syn", with printed Cohen's d values including IQ d = −0.628 and PU d = −2.574 for COC vs Syn.

> "Overall, COC had higher mean values for all factors than Syn and higher mean values for six variables than Asyn except for system quality."
