## Evidence

### Soares 2023

Soares, S., Boyes, M. E., Parrila, R., & Badcock, N. A. (2023). Does reading anxiety impact on academic achievement in higher education students? Dyslexia, 29(3), 179–198. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1738

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate Pearson correlations in the results section (N = 169) show reading history correlated with social reading anxiety r = 0.420 and non-social reading anxiety r = 0.501; partial correlations controlling for trait anxiety remained large (0.343 and 0.485). The section reports "a history of reading difficulties was associated with greater reading anxiety".

> "Better reading comprehension was associated with lower reading anxiety, and a history of reading difficulties was associated with greater reading anxiety. Better reading comprehension moderately correlated with greater WAM outcomes."

### Soares 2023 (2)

Soares, S., Boyes, M. E., Parrila, R., & Badcock, N. A. (2023). Does reading anxiety impact on academic achievement in higher education students? Dyslexia, 29(3), 179–198. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1738

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the correlation table, reading comprehension correlated with non-social reading anxiety r = −0.302 (p ≤ 0.001) on one side of the diagonal and −0.314 on the other (Table 6 combines Pearson correlations with partial correlations controlling for trait anxiety and does not say which half is which) and with social reading anxiety r = −0.110 (not significant); the discussion notes the relationship held "with non‐social reading anxiety but not with social reading anxiety".

> "We found this relationship with non‐social reading anxiety but not with social reading anxiety."
