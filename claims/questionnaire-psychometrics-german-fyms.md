## Evidence

### Heckmann Markus B. 2025

Heckmann Markus B., Mages Christine, Finke Daniel, Reich Christoph, Haney Ailís Ceara, Riedel Caroline, Bugaj Till Johannes, Frey Norbert, Rahm Ann-Kathrin. (2025). Effects of mentoring on self-reflection and competence in Final year medical students’ internal medicine rotation. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331057

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Psychometric evaluation of translated instruments in 42 baseline respondents, including exploratory factor analyses. "the SRIS questionnaire showed a good internal reliability"; the SRQ-L-PJ controlled scale reached only 0.62 and item-drop analysis raised it to 0.73 after dropping two items.

> "With a Cronbach’s alpha of 0.87 (0.81;0.93 95% CI, n = 42) and 0.86 (0.80;0.92 95% CI, n = 42) for SRIS-IN and SRIS-SR at baseline, respectively, the SRIS questionnaire showed a good internal reliability (see S1 Table)."
