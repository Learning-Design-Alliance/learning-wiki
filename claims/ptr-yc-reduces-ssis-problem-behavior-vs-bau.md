## Subclaims
`q4 i2` In an RCT, the PTR-YC group showed a significantly greater pre-to-post reduction in SSIS problem behavior scores than the BAU group, with a small interaction effect. [→ Glen Dunlap 2018](#glen-dunlap-2018)

## Evidence

### Glen Dunlap 2018

Glen Dunlap, Phillip Strain, Janice K. Lee, Jaclyn Joseph, and Nancy Leech. (2018). A Randomized Controlled Evaluation of Prevent-Teach-Reinforce for Young Children. Topics in Early Childhood Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0271121417724874

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with classroom-level randomization; 169 children in pre-K and Head Start classrooms in two western US states. Mixed ANOVA on SSIS problem behavior showed a significant time-by-group interaction, "F(1, 156) = 10.40, p = .002", with ηp² = .062; posttest means were 27.80 (PTR-YC) vs 29.37 (BAU).

> "Comparing study groups on the SSIS problem behavior index revealed a statistically significant interaction effect between time and group, F(1, 156) = 10.40, p = .002. The statistically significant interaction represented a small effect size ( ηp 2 = .062)."
