Manjeet Singh Usma, Shaun Bangay and Atul Sajjanhar. (2025). Augmented Reality Enhanced Analytics for Education: A Systematic Review. Journal of Learning Analytics 12(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8447

Systematic review synthesis of limitation codes (Table 11) across the reviewed studies. The review attributes the cognitive overload point to Olea-Ibarra et al. (2025) and the constraints/inconsistency point to Senthil Pandi et al. (2024).

"A key limitation is the increased cognitive load and complexity associated with immersive AR environments, potentially leading to learner distraction or affective overload (Olea-Ibarra et al., 2025). Additionally, technological constraints, usability challenges, and methodological inconsistencies in analytics approaches limit broader implementation and interpretation across diverse educational contexts (Senthil Pandi et al., 2024)."
