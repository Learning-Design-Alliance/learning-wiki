### Ryugo Morita 2025

Ryugo Morita, Ko Watanabe, Jinjia Zhou, Andreas Dengel, and Shoya Ishimaru. (2025). GenAIReading: Augmenting Human Cognition with Interactive Digital Textbooks Using Large Language Models and Image Generation Models. [doi:10.1145/3745900.3746066](https://doi.org/10.1145/3745900.3746066)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Fixation-based AOI-ratio analysis across conditions C2–C4 (Fig. 9) using a 90 Hz Tobii Pro eye-tracker; individual participants showed distinct patterns (e.g., one participant largely ignored visuals, one minimally engaged with any GenAI material). No effect size is printed.

> "GenAI summaries were generally more engaged than single images, possibly due to their comprehensive nature."
