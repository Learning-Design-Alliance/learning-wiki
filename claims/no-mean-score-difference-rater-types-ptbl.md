## Evidence

### Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong; Kantiwong Anupong 2025

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing mean total scores across the three rater types in the PtBL course; the article reports "F = 0.75, p = 0.472", with domain-level F statistics from 0.86 to 0.99 and p-values 0.374–0.423, all non-significant.

> "The average scores as rated by students, MDs, and health professors are 54.00 ± 4.03, 53.24 ± 4.18, and 54.16 ± 4.16, respectively (F = 0.75, p = 0.472)."
