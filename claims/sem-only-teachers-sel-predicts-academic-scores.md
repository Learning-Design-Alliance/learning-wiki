## Evidence

### Asadi 2026

Asadi, I. (2026). Evaluating social–emotional skills among Arabic‐speaking students: A multi‐informant study of students, parents and teachers. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70044

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Maximum-likelihood SEM across grades with three latent SEL predictors (five CASEL subscales each) predicting a latent academic-scores variable; fit was RMSEA = .06, CFI = .95, TLI = .94, and the model explained 78% of variance. The β coefficients are relative contributions within one model, not standalone effect sizes, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "the relative contribution of teachers' SEL report to academic scores was significant (β = .84, p < .001), the relative contributions of both students (β = .05, p > .05) and parents (β = .03, p > .05) were not significant."
