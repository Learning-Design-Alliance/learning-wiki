# Students' skill mastery for all five attributes improved during gameplay in The Nomads, with all quantiles differing significantly between halves

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` All quantiles of attribute mastery distributions differed significantly at the .05 nominal level between first and second halves of gameplay, with second-half values higher. [→ Jiaqi Yu 2022](#jiaqi-yu-2022)
`q3 i?` For attributes 2 and 3, more participants had higher mastery in the second half; for attributes 1, 4, and 5, most students still fell between 0 and 0.6. [→ Jiaqi Yu 2022 (2)](#jiaqi-yu-2022-2)
