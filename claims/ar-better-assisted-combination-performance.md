## Evidence

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ART-ANOVA on assisted combination-session composite deviation (24 novices, within-subjects crossover). Main effect of condition (F 1,22 = 51.58, p < .001, η² = .70) and condition*group interaction (F 1,22 = 34.80, p < .001, η² = .61). Participants starting with AR performed significantly better than those starting with video (p < .001).

> "Results indicate a main effect ofCONDITION(F 1,22 = 51.58, p < .001, η2 =.70) and aCONDITION*GROUPinteraction (F 1,22 = 34.80, p < .001, η2 =.61)."

### Chuhan (Franklin) Xu 2026 (2)

Chuhan (Franklin) Xu, Lia Sparingga Purnamasari, Zhenfang Chen, Daragh Byrne, Dina El-Zanfaly. (2026). WeldAR: Augmenting Live Hands-On Training with In-Situ Guidance for Novice Learners. CHI 2026. https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791913

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Skill-level analysis of the assisted combination session identified Travel Speed and Work Angle as primary drivers. Travel speed showed significant effects for condition (F 1,22 = 76.63, p < .001, η² = .78), group (F 1,22 = 9.89, p = .005, η² = .31), and interaction (F 1,22 = 45.87, p < .001, η² = .68). Work angle showed similar significance.

> "Travel Speed exhibited the strongest results, with significant effects forCONDITION(F 1,22 = 76.63, p < .001, η2 =.78),GROUP(F 1,22 = 9.89, p=.005, η 2 =.31), and interaction (F 1,22 = 45.87, p < .001, η2 =.68)."
