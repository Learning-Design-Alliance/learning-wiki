## Evidence

### Fink Maximilian C. 2021

Fink Maximilian C., Heitzmann Nicole, Siebeck Matthias, Fischer Frank, Fischer Martin R. (2021). Learning to diagnose accurately through virtual patients: do reflection phases have an added benefit? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02937-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-test in the control group (n = 40) of the three-group experiment, corroborating H3.1: "diagnostic accuracy after working with the virtual patients was significantly higher than their current hypothesis at the start". A categorical follow-up found not changing the hypothesis (71.6%) more frequent than improvement (22.0%) or deterioration (6.4%).

> "participants' diagnostic accuracy after working with the virtual patients was significantly higher than their current hypothesis at the start of the virtual patient encounters (t(39) = 3.08, p = .002)."
