### K. Nikolopoulos 2020

K. Nikolopoulos, M. Pardalaki. (2020). Particle Dance: Particle physics in the dance studio. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ab6952](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ab6952)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative round-table feedback at the end of each of the seven pilot workshops, with student and accompanying-teacher comments. The article reports students indicated that they "learnt what is inside the atom"; no quantitative learning measure is reported.

> "Regarding the learning outcomes, students indicated that they learnt what is inside the atom. Most of them thought that the atom is indeed the smallest division of matter, and they were particularly excited to find out how many diﬀerent particles there are"
