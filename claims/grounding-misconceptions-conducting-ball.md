### Joshua Bilak and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Joshua Bilak and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Improving Students’ Conceptual Understanding of Conductors and Insulators. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07390](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07390)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results for multiple-choice question 2, administered to the same 235 introductory students and 19 graduate students. The correct answer is that grounding increases the attraction; the article also reports free-response grounding difficulties on pre-/post-tests.

> "Approximately half of the students from both groups did not provide the correct response. The incorrect responses suggest that one-third of the graduate students believed that the grounding wire will not affect the force between the aluminum ball and the charged plastic rod whereas 16% of them believed that a grounded object cannot be charged."
