## Evidence

### Jäncke L 2010

Jäncke L, Sandmann P. (2010). Music listening while you learn: No influence of background music on verbal learning. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-6-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MEQ rating data from the main experiment's 75 subjects, analysed with 2-way ANOVAs, showed a significant Group main effect on pleasantness with printed effect size η² = 0.42; a Background × Group trend reflected reduced pleasantness for in-tune melodies while learning.

> "There was a significant main effect for Group with respect to pleasantness, with the in-tune melodies receiving the highest pleasantness ratings and the noise stimulus the lowest (F(4,70) = 12.5, p < 0.001 eta2 = 0.42)"

### Jäncke L 2010 (2)

Jäncke L, Sandmann P. (2010). Music listening while you learn: No influence of background music on verbal learning. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-6-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of arousal ratings in the main experiment found "no significant difference between the five conditions"; no effect size was printed for this null finding.

> "For the arousal rating we obtained no significant difference between the five conditions (F(4,70) = 1.07, p = 0.378)."
