## Evidence

### Al-Najafi Dheyaa 2026

Al-Najafi Dheyaa, Krause Katherine D., Wang Yundi, Zuo Qi Kang, Koblanski Maya E, Leong Cameron J., Schmidt Emma, Faran Muhammad, Verma Vanay, Vyas Ravi, Campbell Matthew, Hwang Jaehyun, Deng Jiawen, Palepu Anita. (2026). Psychometric performance and student perceptions of AI- versus student-generated multiple-choice questions: a single-center randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09671-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Feasibility measurement in the RCT recorded generation and proofreading time for both protocols; the mean per-item efficiency ratio was 5.6 ± 3.5, with 2,195 min total for Student-gen versus 467 min for AI-gen across 112 items. The article reports "19.6 ± 7.5 min for the Student-gen protocol and 4.2 ± 1.9 min for the AI-gen protocol". No standardized effect size was printed for this comparison.

> "The mean time per MCQ was 19.6 ± 7.5 min for the Student-gen protocol and 4.2 ± 1.9 min for the AI-gen protocol (p < 0.0001; Supplementary Figure S2)."
