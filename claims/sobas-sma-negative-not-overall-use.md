Mediator-path estimates from the parallel mediation model on 698 adolescents controlling for age and gender. The article reports a negative association with self-reported SMA "but not with their overall social media use" (B = −0.554, CI including zero).

> "First, we observed that students’ SOBAS had a negative association with their self-reported SMA (B = − 0.235, 95% CI [− 0.319, − 0.152], β = − 0.223), but not with their overall social media use"
