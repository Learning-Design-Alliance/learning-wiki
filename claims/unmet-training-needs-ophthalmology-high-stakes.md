## Evidence

### Jones Lee 2019

Jones Lee, Taylor Deanna J, Sii Freda, Masood Imran, Crabb David P, Shah Peter. (2019). The Only Eye Study (OnES): a qualitative study of surgeon experiences of only eye surgery and recommendations for patient safety. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-030068

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on unmet training needs: participant P8 stated "The trainees are not getting the training" as case numbers drop and training years shorten. P1 reported not being able to think of a single trainee in five years with the necessary technical ability, bravery and surgical resilience for these cases.

> "The trainees are not getting the training. The number of cases are dropping, they are shortening the number of training years, so trainees are being compressed in both ways."

### Jones Lee 2019 (2)

Jones Lee, Taylor Deanna J, Sii Freda, Masood Imran, Crabb David P, Shah Peter. (2019). The Only Eye Study (OnES): a qualitative study of surgeon experiences of only eye surgery and recommendations for patient safety. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-030068

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on delegation: participant P10 called trainee performance of only eye surgery "inappropriate". P3 said the head of department or Medical Director would do all the only eyes themselves. Participants recommended focused training and mentoring by senior surgeons who perform that kind of surgery.

> "You wouldn't want a trainee doing an operation on an only eye, that would be inappropriate and I think we'd be doing the wrong thing by the patient if that was the case."
