In the opportunity theme of the interview study, participants described healthcare leaders, other practitioners, and patients as important influences; some reported managers who "supports my efforts in implementing EBP" while others reported dismissive leadership and physician rejection.

> "Many noted that despite receiving EBN training, a lack of support by stakeholders was a key barrier to their competency development."
