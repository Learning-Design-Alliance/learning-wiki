## Evidence

### Yang Yi 2025

Yang Yi, Yang Yao. (2025). Assessment of neonatologists' competency in managing gestational diabetes complications: a cross-sectional analysis from China. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2025.1574480

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression of good versus bad practice (Table 7). The results narrative reports knowledge OR "7·52 [5·90-9·60]" and attitude OR "0·14 [0·09-0·22]" (negative-attitude reference coding), both p<0·001.

> "Knowledge competency (7·52 [5·90-9·60]; p<0·001) and positive attitudes (0·14 [0·09-0·22]; p<0·001) were strongly associated with practice patterns"

### Yang Yi 2025 (2)

Yang Yi, Yang Yao. (2025). Assessment of neonatologists' competency in managing gestational diabetes complications: a cross-sectional analysis from China. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2025.1574480

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same practice model, education contrast with MD as reference. The printed ORs are "1·32 [1·03-1·71]" for MD-PhD and "0·69 [0·51-0·92]" for fellowship training.

> "varied by education level, with MD-PhD holders showing higher odds (1·32 [1·03-1·71]; p=0·032) compared to fellowship-trained practitioners (0·69 [0·51-0·92]; p=0·009)"

### Yang Yi 2025 (3)

Yang Yi, Yang Yao. (2025). Assessment of neonatologists' competency in managing gestational diabetes complications: a cross-sectional analysis from China. Frontiers in Endocrinology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2025.1574480

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same practice model, practice-setting contrast with academic hospital as reference. Printed ORs are "3·42 [1·31-4·22]" for private practice and "0·35 [0·02-0·93]" for community hospitals; tertiary centers did not differ significantly (OR 0.87, p=0.35).

> "with private practices showing higher odds of good practice (3·42 [1·31-4·22]; p<0·001) compared to community hospitals (0·35 [0·02-0·93]; p<0·001)"
