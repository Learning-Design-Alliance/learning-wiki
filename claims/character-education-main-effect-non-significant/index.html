Tannir 2013¶

Tannir, A., & Al-Hroub, A. (2013). Effects of character education on the self-esteem of intellectually able and less able elementary students in Kuwait. International Journal of Special Education, 28(1), 47-59. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1023237

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Two-way ANOVA main effect test. The character education group's overall self-esteem gain was "slightly higher but it was non-significant," indicating no reliable program-level difference independent of ability group.

"The main effect of a character building teaching approach, as compared to the traditional teaching group, was slightly higher but it was non-significant, F(.822) = .371, p> .05."
