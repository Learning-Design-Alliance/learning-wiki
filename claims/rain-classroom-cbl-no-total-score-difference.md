`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative study with historical controls analyzed using the Mann-Whitney U test. No statistically significant difference in total scoring rate was found between the two groups (P = 0.517).

> "the median total scoring rate for students in the control group during the first semester of the 2019–2020 academic year in the dental hard tissue non-carious diseases test was 85.70. In comparison, students in the experimental group in the first semester of the 2022–2023 academic year achieved a median total scoring rate of 90.00"
