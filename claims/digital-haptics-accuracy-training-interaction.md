### Ruxandra I. Tivadar 2023

Ruxandra I. Tivadar, Benedetta Franceschiello, Astrid Minier and Micah M. Murray. (2023). Learning and navigating digitally rendered haptic spatial layouts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00208-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Behavioural analysis of the trajectory navigation phase using repeated-measures permutation ANOVAs with factors Training and Group. Post-hoc permutation t-tests showed for Group 2 (trained on the easier trajectory) "higher accuracy for trained trajectories than untrained trajectories (0.88 vs. 0.57, respectively)" with d = −0.95; the article also reports the omnibus interaction p = 0.004, pω2 = 0.14.
