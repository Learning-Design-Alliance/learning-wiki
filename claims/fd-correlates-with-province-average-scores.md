## Evidence

### Min Xia 2024

Min Xia, Zhu Su, Wei-Bing Deng, Xiu-Mei Feng, Ben-Wei Zhang. (2024). How to quantify an examination? Evidence from physics examinations via complex networks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A pretest validation against provincial average scores in four provinces (SX, HN, HB, FJ), comparing same-province data over several years. The authors report "a direct correlation within each province" between average score and Fd; no effect size is printed.

> "we compared the relationship between the average scores on the physics NCEE over different years in four provinces and changes in Fd, finding a direct correlation within each province: the higher the average score is, the lower the comprehensive difficulty coefficient."
