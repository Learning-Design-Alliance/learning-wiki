## Evidence

### Bearman Margaret 2019

Bearman Margaret, Greenhill Jennene, Nestel Debra. (2019). The power of simulation: a large‐scale narrative analysis of learners’ experiences. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13747

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Narrative and thematic analysis of 327 online reflections from the NHET-Sim programme, coded by the authors into four narrative types with a quantitative content count. The article reports "progress (267/327 = 81%)" as the dominant category, with humiliation the smallest at 2%. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Narratives were categorised into the following categories: progress (267/327 = 81%); transformation (25/327 = 8%); practice (27/328 = 8%); and humiliation (8/327 = 2%)."
