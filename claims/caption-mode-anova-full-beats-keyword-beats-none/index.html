Evidence¶

Behroozizad and Majidi 2015¶

Sorayya Behroozizad, Sudabeh Majidi. (2015). The Effect of Different Modes of English Captioning on EFL learners' General Listening Comprehension: Full text Vs. Keyword Captions. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.115

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on the three groups' listening post-test scores (n=45 total). Post-test means were 45.86 (full), 38.13 (keyword), and 33.86 (no captions); the omnibus test was significant, F (2, 42) = 103.51, p<.01.

"Table 4, clearly indicated that the difference between the means of the three groups was statistically significant. F (2, 42) = 103.51, p<.01."

Behroozizad and Majidi 2015 (2)¶

Sorayya Behroozizad, Sudabeh Majidi. (2015). The Effect of Different Modes of English Captioning on EFL learners' General Listening Comprehension: Full text Vs. Keyword Captions. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.4p.115

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Scheffé post hoc multiple range test on post-test scores. The full-text group exceeded the keyword group by a mean difference of 7.73333 and the no-text group by 12.00000, both significant at the 0.05 level.

"According to Table5, the full captions group performed better than the keyword captions group and both of them significantly higher than the no-captions group."
