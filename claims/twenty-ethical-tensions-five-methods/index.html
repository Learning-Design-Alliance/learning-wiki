Baoyi W and Khan FU 2026¶

Baoyi W and Khan FU (2026). AI and higher education: teachers' perceptions of students' ethical tensions in online pedagogical methods. Front. Psychol. 17:1881032. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1881032

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 25 university language teachers in China (average interview time about 51.52 min). The authors report that "in five teaching methods, we identified a total of 20 key ethical issues", with tension names generated inductively from coded data.
