## Evidence

### Namkung 2016

Namkung, J. M., & Fuchs, L. S. (2016). Cognitive Predictors of Calculations and Number Line Estimation with Whole Numbers and Fractions among At-Risk Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(2), 214-228. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000055

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same structural equation model as the whole-number outcome: latent fraction calculations (Fraction Addition and Fraction Subtraction) predicted from seven cognitive predictors and incoming calculation skill in 139 at-risk fourth graders. The model "accounted for 51% and 32% of the variance" in whole-number and fraction calculations respectively; language emerged as a significant predictor only for the fraction outcome.

> "For fraction calculations, significant predictors were language, processing speed, and attentive behavior."
