## Evidence

### Abdekhoda M 2016

Abdekhoda M, Dehnad A, Yousefi M. (2016). Effectiveness of training intervention to improve medical student's information literacy skills. Korean J Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2016.44

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post skill assessment of the 90-student cohort showed search strategy competency rising from "2.04 (0.7) before intervention" to "3.07 (0.8) after intervention," a significant increase (p=0.03) by t-test.

> "The data showed a significant increase (p=0.03), in students' competency score concerning search strategy design and conducting a search. The mean (SD) was 2.04 (0.7) before intervention versus the mean (SD) 3.07 (0.8) after intervention."
