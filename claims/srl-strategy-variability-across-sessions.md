### Gloria Fernández-Nieto 2026

Gloria Fernández-Nieto, Kiyoshige Garcés, Mladen Raković, Tongguang Li, Xinyu Li, Linxuan Zhao, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Self-Regulated Learning in Essay Writing: Consistency of Strategies and Impact on Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.14228](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.14228)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sankey-diagram analysis of the 87 students who attended both sessions of the two-week essay-writing study. The printed transition percentages show within-strategy stability for Read first, write next and shifts away from the other strategies.

> "most students who began with theRead first, write nextstrategy ( 82.61%) kept using it across sessions. In contrast, most who started withRead and write simultaneouslyswitched, mainly toRead first, write next(80%)."

### Gloria Fernández-Nieto 2026 (2)

Gloria Fernández-Nieto, Kiyoshige Garcés, Mladen Raković, Tongguang Li, Xinyu Li, Linxuan Zhao, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Self-Regulated Learning in Essay Writing: Consistency of Strategies and Impact on Outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.14228](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.14228)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Repeated-measures test on paired strategy assignments of the same students across the two sessions. The test statistic (χ2 = 3.0, p= 0.392) indicated balanced switching between strategies; no effect size is printed.

> "The McNemar–Bowker's symmetry test on paired SRL strategies of each student across sessions (χ2 = 3.0, p= 0.392) suggested that changes between strategies were balanced, with no evidence of a directional preference"
