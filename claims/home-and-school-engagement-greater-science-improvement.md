### home-gains-1

Dominguez, X., Vidiksis, R., Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Presser, A., Bueno, M., & Orr, J. (2023, April). Integrating science, mathematics, and engineering: Linking home and school learning for young learners. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/180

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear regressions comparing the randomly assigned home-component subsample of intervention children with a stratified random sample receiving the classroom portion only. The report states the "classroom + home condition" made significantly more science improvement (β = 1.50, p < .05; β = 1.93, p < .004).

> "Results from the analyses of science data show a significant difference, with children in the classroom + home condition making significantly more improvement in science learning relative to children in the classroom only condition (β = 1.50, p = < .05 for the Score Gain model and β = 1.93, p = < .004 for the post-test model)."
