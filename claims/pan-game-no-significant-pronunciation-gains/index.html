Evidence¶

Cardoso 2017¶

Cardoso, W., Rueb, A., & Grimshaw, J. (2017). Can an interactive digital game help French learners improve their pronunciation? In K. Borthwick, L. Bradley & S. Thouësny (Eds), CALL in a climate of change: adapting to turbulent global conditions – short papers from EUROCALL 2017 (pp. 67-72). Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2017.eurocall2017.691

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon Signed-Rank tests on judges' mean rater scores for comprehensibility, fluency, and overall pronunciation found no significant within-group gains; e.g. treatment comprehensibility pre-to-post "Z=-1.39, p=0.16". The article reports a trend toward significance for fluency (p=.055) only.

"As the data are non-parametric, a Wilcoxon Signed-Rank test was conducted based on a student's mean rater score for each of the three measures between two given tests."
