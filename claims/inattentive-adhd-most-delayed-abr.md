## Evidence

### Mohamed A. Ahmed 2014

Mohamed A. Ahmed, Khaled A. El-Beh, Talal A. Mohammed, Dina F. Mansour, Mohammed Y. Ezz-Eldine. (2014). Learning Disabilities in Different Types of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorders and its Relation to Cortical and Brainstem Function. J Neurol Res. 2014;4(1):22-30. https://doi.org/10.14740/jnr268w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparing ABR means across ADHD subtypes; e.g. wave III mean was 3.78 in ADHD-I versus 3.53 (ADHD-C) and 3.43 (ADHD-HI). Waves I, II and interval III-V did not differ significantly across subtypes.

> "The delay was significantly high in inattentive type in wave III (P = 0.000), wave V (P = 0.002), I-III interval (P = 0.001) and I-V interval (P = 0.002) in comparison to the combined and the hyperactive impulsive type (Table 4)."
