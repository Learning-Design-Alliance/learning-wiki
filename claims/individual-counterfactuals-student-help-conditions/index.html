For a student who solved Q5 at the 2D level with scheme help, the counterfactual probability of solving Q5 at 2D without help is one; for Q12 solved at 1D with scheme help, the counterfactual probability of reaching 2D with feedback help is between 0 and 0.76, indicating uncertainty. → Francesca Mangili 2026

q2 · i? · design · r2

Individualised counterfactual inference for a single student from the CAT assessment (109-student dataset), conditioning on the student's full set of observed answers and hint usage. For Q12 the counterfactual probabilities were [0,0], [0.24,1] and [0,0.76] for 0D, 1D and 2D, indicating uncertainty about performance with more assistance.

"The former yields probability one forQ 5 = 2D, i.e., we are certain that the student would have been able to use a 2D algorithm even without help."
