TecCoBot: Technology-aided support for self-regulated learning — Automatic feedback on writing tasks with chatbots. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2111.11881](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2111.11881)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Qualitative needs analysis using the Teaching Analysis Poll in an educational science seminar in teacher education at Leipzig University; "In 2019 296 of 628 enrolled students were surveyed". Evaluation used qualitative content analysis, and statements were condensed via Think-Pair-Share. The quote reports the perceived obstacles.

> "In addition to the large number of texts to be read in the seminars, lack of focus on the content and its structure, which is diﬃcult to recognise, were mentioned as obstacles to learning."
