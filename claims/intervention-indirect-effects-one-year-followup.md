## Evidence

### Wolff 2011

Ulrika Wolff. (2011). Effects of a Randomised Reading Intervention Study: An Application of Structural Equation Modelling. Dyslexia 17: 295–311. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.438

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

SEM estimation of indirect effects through post-test in the randomised trial; all four total indirect effects at one-year follow-up were significant, with small effect sizes d = .33, d = .34, d = .13 and d = .32. A second model step found direct intervention paths at follow-up all non-significant, so effects were mediated via post-test performance.

> "Finally, indirect effects from intervention to the latent variables at follow-up were estimated. The total indirect effect was significant for all outcomes: reading comprehension (t = 3.34; p < .001; d = .33), spelling (t = 3.77; p < .001; d = .34), speed (t = 1.98; p < .05; d = .13), and phoneme awareness (t = 2.27; p < .05; d = .32)."
