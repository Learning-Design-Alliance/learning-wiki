## Evidence

### Lahme 2024

Lahme, S. Z., Cirkel, J. O., Hahn, L., Hofmann, J., Neuhaus, J., Schneider, S., & Klein, P. (2024). From enrollment to exams: Perceived stress dynamics among first-year physics students. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020127)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal panel analysis of weekly PSQ measurements in three groups of first- and second-semester physics students, plotted in Fig. 5. The trajectory shows a rise to "a level of around fifty-five" sustained until exams, then a post-exam decrease "even below the starting level". No effect size is printed for this descriptive trajectory.

> "In the first four to five lecture weeks, the stress level increased in all groups to a level of around fifty-five, a level sustained with minor fluctuations until the exam weeks. After the exams, the stress level decreased even below the starting level, down to around twenty to thirty."

### Lahme 2024 (2)

Lahme, S. Z., Cirkel, J. O., Hahn, L., Hofmann, J., Neuhaus, J., Schneider, S., & Klein, P. (2024). From enrollment to exams: Perceived stress dynamics among first-year physics students. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020127)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on the processed matched sample of thirty-nine students from groups A1 and A2 across eleven measurement periods. The article reports "a significant temporal evolution of perceived stress" with the printed effect size η2P = 0.51, which is large (η² ≥ .14).

> "a repeated measures ANOV A shows a significant temporal evolution of perceived stress within the two semesters ( F (5.7, 215.6) = 40.0, p <0.001, η2P = 0.51)"
