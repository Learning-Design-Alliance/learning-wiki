## Evidence

### Babaei Keivan 2023

Babaei Keivan, Sadeghian Efat, Khodaveisi Masoud. (2023). The Predictors of Clinical Competence among Hospital Nurses: A Cross-Sectional Study in Hamadan, West Iran. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ejhs.v33i1.5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional correlation analysis among 270 nurses; Table 1 prints Spearman/Pearson coefficients including total CC r=0.256 (P=0.01) and work role r=0.396. Helping role, teaching-coaching, therapeutic interventions, and ensuring quality were non-significant.

> "Participants' age also had significant positive correlation with the mean scores of CC and its situation management, diagnostic functions, and work role dimensions (P<0.05)"
