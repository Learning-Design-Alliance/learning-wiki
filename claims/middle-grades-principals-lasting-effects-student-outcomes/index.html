Evidence¶

Rivkin 2023¶

Rivkin, S.G., Sartain, L., & Schiman, J.C. (2023). Training and retaining effective school leaders: Results from efforts to improve principal effectiveness in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/training-retaining-effective-school-leaders

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Observational analysis of CPS administrative data 1994–2018 estimating principal effects with multiple isolation methods. The authors report that "middle grade principals had lasting impacts on their students’ outcomes into young adulthood" alongside strong contemporaneous achievement and climate effects. No effect size is printed in the brief.

"middle grade principals had lasting impacts on their students’ outcomes into young adulthood (e.g., college going, employment), as well as a strong influence on contemporaneous student achievement and school climate"
