Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition 2007¶

Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED405428

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

WWC calculations for the Calderón, Hertz-Lazarowitz, and Slavin (1998) quasi-experimental study: adjusted mean NAPT reading scores of 33.16 for the BCIRC group versus 23.83 for comparison among 85 posttested third graders, with a printed effect size of 0.61 ("0.61 ns +23"). The WWC rated the effect substantively important but could not confirm statistical significance after correcting for clustering.
