## Evidence

### Lee 2025

Lee, J., Nam, K., Suh, Y., Lee, Y., and Lee, D. 2025. "Factors Associated With Patient Safety Activities of Clinical Nurses: A Cross‐Sectional Secondary Data Analysis." International Nursing Review 72, no. 4: e70127. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70127

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate analysis (Table 1) of 291 nurses: age correlated with activities (r = 0.12, p = 0.04). In the regression (Table 3), age was not significant (β = 0.15, p = 0.146); the article attributes this to age proxying accumulated experience and competence.

> "Patient safety activity scores increased with age (r = 0.12, p = 0.04), and hospitals with fewer than 1000 beds reported higher patient safety activity scores than those with 1000 beds or more (F = 5.32, p = 0.01)."
