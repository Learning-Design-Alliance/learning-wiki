### Osama Swidan 2026

Osama Swidan. (2026). Responding to AI-generated emotional alerts: teachers' intervention and students' engagement in the mathematics classroom. ZDM-MathematicsEducation. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01810-7

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of one episode beginning at minute 33:20 with a red-framed alert; the first intervention took control of the software and demonstrated steps, while continued alerts prompted a return and a shift from explanation to inquiry. Rusol's answer that she sought the intersection of angle bisectors revealed an intention the procedural guidance had not addressed.

> "In this episode, the continuity alerts determine the in- structional move, prompting the teacher to return to the student and reconsider his initial strategy. The reorganiza- tion of engagement occurred only after this second, respon- sive intervention."
