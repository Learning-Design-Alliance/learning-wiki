## Evidence

### Joss Ives 2014

Joss Ives. (2014). Measuring the Learning from Two-Stage Collaborative Group Exams. Dept. of Physics & Astronomy, University of British Columbia. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.027](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.027)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects logistic regression on diagnostic-test responses for the second midterm (n = 673); the model "correctly predicted 77% of the cases". The treatment condition outperformed control with an odds ratio of 1.22 (95% CI [1.04, 1.42]).

> "It was found that treatment predicted diagnostic test success (β3 = .198, SE = .079, p = .012). Expressed as an odds ratio, the odds of answering a question correctly on the diagnostic test (versus not answering it correctly) increased by a factor 1.22 (95% CI [1.04, 1.42])"
