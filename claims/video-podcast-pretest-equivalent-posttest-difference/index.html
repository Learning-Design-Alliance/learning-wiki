Evidence¶

Thiyagu 2014¶

K. Thiyagu. (2014). Effectiveness of Video Podcast in Learning Psychology among Bachelor of Education Students. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097634

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-test/post-test comparison in the 48-student equivalent group design. Pre-test means were nearly equal (20.00 vs 20.29), confirming group equivalence. Post-test means differed significantly (30.29 vs 39.12), with "the experimental group performed better than the control group in their post test."

"the obtained 't' value 0.27 is less than the critical value at 0.05 level of significance and hence, the null hypothesis is accepted"
