`q1 i?` Faculty reported that categorization of introductory physics problems involves identifying fundamental principles, while categorization of upper-level quantum mechanics problems mainly involves identifying concepts and procedures required for solving. ## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Categorization of Quantum Mechanics Problems by Professors and Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.02947](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.02947)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Theme from individual discussions with faculty participants, not a tested result. Faculty noted fundamental principles such as conservation of energy and momentum are important for quantum processes but are not typically the focus of an upper-level undergraduate course.

> "Another common theme that emerged is that categorization of intr oductory physics problems involves identifying fundamental principles relevant for the problem s, whereas in the upper-level undergraduate quantum mechanics problems, it mainly involves ident ifying concepts and proce dures, because problem solving in such a course is tied to conceptual and procedural knowledge."
