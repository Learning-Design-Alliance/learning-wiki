Ukpai Patricia Ogonnaya 2016¶

Ukpai Patricia Ogonnaya, Gabriel Okafor, Okechukwu S. Abonyi, J.O Ugama. (2016). Effects of Concept Mapping Instruction Approach on Students’ Achievement in Basic Science. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.8. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095361

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest non-equivalent control group study; 70 treatment and 52 control students taught the same content for four weeks. ANCOVA on adjusted posttest means (47.07 vs 37.00) showed "the calculated F-ratio (F-cal) is 25.287" exceeding the critical value, so the null hypothesis was rejected.

"For hypothesis 1, result in table 7 shows that the calculated F-ratio (F-cal) is 25.287 while the critical value (F-critical) at alpha level of 0.05 is 3.84."
