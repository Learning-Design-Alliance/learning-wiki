## Evidence

### Reijers Stefanie N.H. 2025

Reijers Stefanie N.H., Vroegop Jantien L., Jansen Pauline W., Kremer Bernd, van der Schroeff Marc P. (2025). The Impact of Slight to Mild Hearing Loss on Academic Performance and Behavior of 9–15‐Year‐Olds. The Laryngoscope. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.31908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

A one-way ANOVA in the cross-sectional sample of 4688 children tested the effect of hearing thresholds on school level; the omnibus LPTA effect was "F(2, 31) = 10.57, p < 0.001".

> "There was a statistically significant effect of the HL in the LPTA for the 3 levels: F(2, 31) = 10.57, p < 0.001."

### Reijers Stefanie N.H. 2025 (2)

Reijers Stefanie N.H., Vroegop Jantien L., Jansen Pauline W., Kremer Bernd, van der Schroeff Marc P. (2025). The Impact of Slight to Mild Hearing Loss on Academic Performance and Behavior of 9–15‐Year‐Olds. The Laryngoscope. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.31908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Tukey HSD post hoc comparisons found significant VMBO–HAVO (M = 0.72) and VMBO–VWO (M = 0.75) differences, but "no significant difference between HAVO and VWO"; the HPTA omnibus effect was not significant.

> "However, there was no significant difference between HAVO and VWO (M = 0.03, SE = 0.18, 95% CI [−0.40, 0.45]). For the HPTA, the results showed no statistically significant effect and no post hoc tests were performed."
