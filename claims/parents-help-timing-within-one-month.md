## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yoshii Hatsumi 2012

Yoshii Hatsumi, Watanabe Yuichiro, Kitamura Hideaki, Nan Zhang, Akazawa Kouhei. (2012). Effect of an Education Program on Improving Help-Seeking among Parents of Junior and Senior High School Students in Japan. Global Journal of Health Science. https://doi.org/10.5539/gjhs.v4n1p33

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline timing results from the first questionnaire in the parent cohort (Table 1); the article prints that about 80% sought help within approximately 1 month and only 1.4% to 2.2% waited longer than 1 year.

> "For almost all symptom categories, approximately half (43.7%-55.5%) of participants sought help approximately 1 month after symptom onset (Table 1). About 80% of participants sought medical help within approximately 1 month for children with any symptom."

### Yoshii Hatsumi 2012 (2)

Yoshii Hatsumi, Watanabe Yuichiro, Kitamura Hideaki, Nan Zhang, Akazawa Kouhei. (2012). Effect of an Education Program on Improving Help-Seeking among Parents of Junior and Senior High School Students in Japan. Global Journal of Health Science. https://doi.org/10.5539/gjhs.v4n1p33

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired before/after comparison (McNemar's test, Table 1) after the education program; half-year delay rates decreased for all symptom categories at p<0.05.

> "Those who reported waiting approximately half a year to seek help decreased for all symptom categories (p<0.05)."
