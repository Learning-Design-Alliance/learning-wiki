## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Marcovitch S. 2014

Marcovitch S., O'Brien M., Calkins S. D., Leerkes E. M., Weaver J. M., Levine D. W. (2014). A Longitudinal Assessment of the Relation between Executive Function and Theory of Mind at 3, 4, and 5 Years. Cognitive Development. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogdev.2014.07.001

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Latent variable model fit to data from 226 children tested at 3, 4, and 5 years on multiple EF and ToM tasks, controlling for gender, income-to-needs, and PPVT. The model found "earlier EF abilities predicted later ToM abilities (from age 3 to age 4: β = .52, p = .001"; no effect size was printed.

> "After accounting for the effects of gender, income-to-needs, PPVT, and the stability within each construct, earlier EF abilities predicted later ToM abilities (from age 3 to age 4: β = .52, p = .001; from age 4 to age 5: β = .41, p = .009)."
