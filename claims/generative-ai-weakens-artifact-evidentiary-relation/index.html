Kai Yao 2026¶

Kai Yao. (2026). When AI Does the Work, What Is Learning For? Post-Instrumental Learning and the Risk of Capacity Dissolution. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28041 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.28041

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Conceptual argument using formal educational assessment as the principal worked case. The article argues artifacts such as essays and proofs have long served as evidence of capacity, and that continuing to treat the product as proof is "output preservation for capacity preservation".

"Generative AI loosens this evidentiary relation. The institution can still receive the artifact, but the artifact no longer shows what it used to show."
