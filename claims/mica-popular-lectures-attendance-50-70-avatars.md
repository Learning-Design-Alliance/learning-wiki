Program description from the Education and Public Outreach section: public lectures ran initially bi-weekly, then weekly during academic terms, with external speakers and team members. The authors report "a typical attendance of ~ 50 – 70 avatars" and that slides and recordings were posted freely on the MICA website.

> "They were very popular, with a typical attendance of ~ 50 – 70 avatars. The slides shown, and in most cases also the audio and/or video (machinima) recordings are posted on the MICA website, and are freely available."
