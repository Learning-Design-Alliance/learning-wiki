# Novice surgeons completed temporal bone anatomy tasks significantly faster in VR than in cross-sectional PACS viewing

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Novices used significantly less time per temporal bone task in the VR environment than in cross-sectional viewing (p = 0.003, linear mixed model). [→ Timonen Tomi 2022](#timonen-tomi-2022)

## Evidence

### Timonen Tomi 2022

Timonen Tomi, Dietz Aarno, Linder Pia, Lehtimäki Antti, Löppönen Heikki, Elomaa Antti-Pekka, Iso-Mustajärvi Matti. (2022). The effect of virtual reality on temporal bone anatomy evaluation and performance. European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology, 279(9), 4303–4312. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-021-07183-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled within-participant comparison of five novice surgeons performing landmark identification and distance measurements on five cadaver temporal bones in both methods. Table 3 reports mean task time of 43.89 min in PACS versus 30.04 min in VR, a mean difference of 13.85 min with "0.003*" by linear mixed model.

> "Novices	PACS	43.89 (21.86)	37.04, 50.74	13.85	4.96, 22.74	0.003*	VR	30.04 (15.17)	6.68, 53.39"
