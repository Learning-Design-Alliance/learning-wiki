# Idaho charter reading advantage appears only in the 2016-2017 growth period, not in 2015-2016

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the 2015-2016 growth period, Idaho charter students' gains do not differ statistically from TPS peers in either reading or math. [→ CREDO 2019](#credo-2019)
`q2 i?` In the 2016-2017 growth period, Idaho charter students' reading gains are significantly higher than TPS peers, about 24 more days of learning. [→ CREDO 2019 (2)](#credo-2019-2)

## Evidence

### CREDO 2019

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in Idaho. https://credo.stanford.edu

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Growth-period disaggregation of the statewide VCR analysis. The 2015-2016 period shows no statistically significant difference in either subject; equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "The gains of Idaho charter school students in the 2015 -2016 growth period do not differ statistically from the performance of their TPS peers in either reading or math."

### CREDO 2019 (2)

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in Idaho. https://credo.stanford.edu

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

In the 2016-2017 growth period of the same VCR analysis, charter students' reading gains are significantly higher, with growth of approximately 24 more days of learning in reading compared to TPS counterparts; math gains show no statistically significant difference.

> "the gains of Idaho charter school students in the 2016-2017 growth period are sign ificantly higher than the growth of their TPS peers in reading"
