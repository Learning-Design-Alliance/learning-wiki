## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the post-session five-point Likert self-evaluation, the SNAPPS group scored significantly higher than the OMP group on "It was easy to express questions and uncertainties" (P = 0.046). [→ Seki Masayasu 2016](#seki-masayasu-2016)

## Evidence

### Seki Masayasu 2016

Seki Masayasu, Otaki Junji, Breugelmans Raoul, Komoda Takayuki, Nagata-Kobayashi Shizuko, Akaishi Yu, Hiramoto Jun, Ohno Iwao, Harada Yoshimi, Hirayama Yoji, Izumi Miki (2016). How do case presentation teaching methods affect learning outcomes?-SNAPPS and the One-Minute preceptor. BMC Medical Education 16. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0531-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-point Likert self-evaluation (0 = strongly disagree to 4 = strongly agree) completed after the simulated-case session and compared with the Mann–Whitney U test. SNAPPS residents rated "It was easy to express questions and uncertainties" higher than OMP residents (P = 0.046).

> "The self-evaluation sheets revealed significant differences between the SNAPPS and OMP groups in terms of the following evaluations: "It was easy to express questions and uncertainties" (P = 0.046)"
