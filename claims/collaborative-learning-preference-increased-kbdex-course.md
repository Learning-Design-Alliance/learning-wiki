## Evidence

### Yoshiaki Matsuzawa 2013

Yoshiaki Matsuzawa, Sayaka Tohyama, and Sanshiro Sakai. (2013). THE COURSE DESIGN TO DEVELOP META-COGNITIVE SKILLS FOR COLLABORATIVE LEARNING THROUGH TOOL-ASSISTED DISCOURSE ANALYSIS. PROCEEDINGS, COINs13. [doi:10.1504/ijode.2014.065098](https://doi.org/10.1504/ijode.2014.065098)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post questionnaire in the 2012 class on 5-level Likert items, applied before and after the 15-week course. The paired t-test for collaborative learning preference was significant (t(44) = -2.95, p<.01); no effect size was printed.

> "The mean of preference of the collaborative learning was 2.82 before taking the class and 3.50 after taking the class."

### Yoshiaki Matsuzawa 2013 (2)

Yoshiaki Matsuzawa, Sayaka Tohyama, and Sanshiro Sakai. (2013). THE COURSE DESIGN TO DEVELOP META-COGNITIVE SKILLS FOR COLLABORATIVE LEARNING THROUGH TOOL-ASSISTED DISCOURSE ANALYSIS. PROCEEDINGS, COINs13. [doi:10.1504/ijode.2014.065098](https://doi.org/10.1504/ijode.2014.065098)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same 2012 pre/post Likert questionnaire. For general learning preference the paired t-test found no significant difference (t(44) = 0.66, p>.10); equivalence was not tested and no effect size was printed.

> "There were no significant differences between the mean scores of before and after class for the preference of general learning (t(44) = 0.66, p>.10)."
