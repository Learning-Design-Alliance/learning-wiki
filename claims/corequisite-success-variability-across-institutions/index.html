Strother 2019¶

Strother, S., & Klipple, K. (2019). Corequisite remediation in math: A review of first-year implementation and outcomes of Quantway and Statway. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://carnegiemathpathways.org/reports/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of section-level success rates at Institutions A and D, the only institutions with multiple sections, based on the faculty-reported grade data for all enrolled students. The report prints the standard deviations of "13 percent and 19 percent" within institutions and "20 percent" between institutions. No inferential test is printed.

"the variability within each institution (standard deviations of 13 percent and 19 percent, respectively) was slightly less than the variability between institutions (standard deviation of 20 percent)."
