## Evidence

### Johnels Jakob Åsberg 2025

Johnels Jakob Åsberg, Kuja‐Halkola Ralf, Larsson Henrik, Chang Zheng, Brikell Isabel, Lundström Sebastian. (2025). Does the family situation impact academic achievement differently in students with versus without neurodevelopmental disorders? The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70050

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null interaction findings from the same four GEE regression models in the 12,029-twin sample, with the p-value threshold set at .005 following Benjamin et al. The article reports "the interaction effects were not statistically significant"; sensitivity analyses using higher cut-offs, dimensional symptom scores, and unadjusted models did not change the conclusions.

> "By contrast, the interaction effects were not statistically significant (the lowest p‐value was .04 for ADHD and autism respectively and the highest strata of parental education), meaning that the association between conflict/closeness and Total grade score/secondary school non‐eligibility was similar among those with and without ADHD or autism."
