Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2418616122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

One of 27 preregistered subgroup interaction tests (nine subgroups across Models 1–3) with Benjamini–Yekutieli correction; this and the social-comparison-by-account-type interaction were the only two significant.
