# At a 1-day test delay, interleaved practice yields a moderate benefit over blocked practice (d = 0.42)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students tested 1 day after the review scored 80% vs. 64%, a statistically significant moderate interleaving benefit (d = 0.42). [→ Rohrer 2015](#rohrer-2015)

## Evidence

### Rohrer 2015

Rohrer, D., Dedrick, R. F., & Stershic, S. (2015). Interleaved practice improves mathematics learning. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(3), 900-908. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000001

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Classroom test administered 1 day after the review session, comparing students assigned to the 1-day delay (n = 63 per delay group as printed). Interleaved mean was 80% versus blocked 64%, with Cohen's d = 0.42 and 95% CI [0.07, 0.77].

> "Students tested 1 day after the review showed a moderate benefit of interleaving, 80% (SD = 33%) vs. 64% (SD = 42%), t(62) = 2.39, p = .02, d = 0.42, 95% confidence interval (CI) [0.07, 0.77]."
