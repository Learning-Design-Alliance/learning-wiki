## Evidence

### Cristi-Montero 2022

Cristi-Montero, C., Solis-Urra, P., Sanchez-Martinez, J., et al. as printed in full byline. (2022). Which one? A comparative study of traditional and sports uniforms on academic achievement, cognitive performance, playtime, bullying, and discrimination in adolescents: The Cogni-Action Project. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2022.917970

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of the same 988-adolescent sample; cognitive performance was a global score from eight NeuroCognitive Performance Test tasks. The article reports "no significant differences between uniform types in cognitive performance"; descriptive means were TU 99.0 ± 8.8 vs SU 101.4 ± 7.6. No effect size printed.

> "Figure 1B displays no significant differences between uniform types in cognitive performance (random effect LTR was 21.7; p < 0.001; ICC = 0.080)."
