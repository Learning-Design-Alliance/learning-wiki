Narrative subgroup synthesis of the four prespecified AI modalities (GenAI/LLM n=7, virtual patients/mannequins n=5, AI-VR/MR n=5, chatbots n=2) concluded "GenAI/LLM modalities provide the strongest evidence for cognitive and communication outcomes" and underscored aligning modality with learning objectives.

"GenAI/LLM modalities provide the strongest evidence for cognitive and communication outcomes; AI-driven mannequins show mixed effects with concerns about psychomotor skills; AI-VR/MR offers immersive potential limited by technical challenges; and chatbots serve primarily as adjunctive supports with weak independent effects"
