## Evidence

### Wendi Mizer Stachler 2013

Wendi Mizer Stachler, R. Brent Young, Mari Borr. (2013). Sustainability of professional development to enhance student achievement: A shift in the professional development paradigm. Journal of Agricultural Education, 54(4), 13-30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122391

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire data from 10 of 14 control agricultural education teachers, who received only one debriefing session rather than the seven-day professional development. Seven of the 10 respondents had taught explicit science a year after the pilot, and "only two (29%) of those teachers indicated that they had used the Science -in-CTE method and lessons". The 29% is of those seven: two of the 10 respondents (20%), and two of the 14 control teachers originally enrolled.

> "The remaining seven (70%) control agricultural education teachers indicated they had taught explicit science a year after the pilot study, but only two (29%) of those teachers indicated that they had used the Science -in-CTE method and lessons in their curricula."
