# In many large districts, secondary students lost more than a year of instruction per 100 enrolled, up to 416 days in Grand Rapids, MI

## Evidence

### Losen 2020

Losen, D. J., & Martinez, P. (2020). Lost opportunities: How disparate school discipline continues to drive differences in the opportunity to learn. Learning Policy Institute; Center for Civil Rights Remedies at the Civil Rights Project, UCLA. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608537

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive district-level analysis of 2015-16 CRDC data identifying large districts where secondary students lost over 182 days per 100 enrolled, with Grand Rapids at 416 days. Rates for Black students and students with disabilities were higher still.

> "Each of the following large districts had rates of well more than a year' s worth of school, over 182 days per 100 students."
