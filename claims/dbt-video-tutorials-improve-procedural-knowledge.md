## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2016

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij. (2016). Demonstration-based training (DBT) in the design of a video tutorial for software training. Instr Sci 44:527–542. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9394-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 55 German school students learning Word formatting via video tutorials. A repeated measures ANOVA showed a significant main effect of time; "compared with pre-test scores, participants' scores after working with the tutorials were higher at all three subsequent testing points."

> "Even the lowest of these three scores (i.e., delayed post-test) was signiﬁcantly higher than the pre-test score, F(1, 53) = 41.72, p \ 0.001, d = 1.15."
