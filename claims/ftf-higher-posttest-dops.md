`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Postintervention comparison in the randomized controlled trial: FTF mean 86.4 versus LS mean 78.9, reported as significantly better for FTF (P=.02). The authors note the data variation in the LS group was wider, suggesting significant variability in LS results.

> "However, the posttest scores of the FTF participants were significantly better than those of the LS participants (FTF: mean 86.4, SD 1 vs LS: mean 78.9, SD 15.5; P=.02)."
