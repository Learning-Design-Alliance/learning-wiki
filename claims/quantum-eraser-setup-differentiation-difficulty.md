## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2014

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2014). Developing an Interactive Tutorial on a Quantum Eraser. 2014 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.040

`q2 · i?`

Pretest finding on Question 3, which assessed understanding of the quantum eraser: students "incorrectly claimed that the quantum eraser setup is not different from the setup with two orthogonal polarizers in the paths of the MZI." In the correct analysis, polarizer 3 erases WPI at D1.

> "28% of students incorrectly claimed that the quantum eraser setup is not different from the setup with two orthogonal polarizers in the paths of the MZI or that fewer photons would reach the detectors"
