One-way ANOVA on hours of software use across the four groups was significant, F(1, 76) = 11.43, p = 0.001. Tukey post hoc tests showed TMM and MEM exceeded WOW by 19 and 16 hours respectively; no group met the 45-hour target.

> "Tukey post hoc tests showed where significant variation occurred: Both TMM (+19 hours, p = 0.002) and MEM (+16 hours, p = 0.008) used the technology significantly more than WOW."
