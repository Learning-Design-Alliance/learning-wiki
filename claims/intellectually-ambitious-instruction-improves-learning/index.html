Smylie 2006¶

q3 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report synthesizes an expanding body of evidence on human learning and cognition and classroom-based studies, finding that "Students who are exposed to teaching that demands complex, higher-order intellectual work are likely to perform as well or better academically." Outcomes span subject areas and school settings.

"Students who are exposed to teaching that demands complex, higher-order intellectual work are likely to perform as well or better academically than students who are exposed primarily to basic skills instruction."
