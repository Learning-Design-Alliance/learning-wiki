### Eu 2006

Leong Kwan Eu. (2006). Impact Of Geometer's Sketchpad On Students Achievement In Graph Functions. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology. http://www.mojet.net

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 22 experimental and 21 control students in intact classes over ten weeks; experimental post-test mean 65.90 (SD 23.54) versus control 29.14 (SD 18.41), and the post-test difference was "significant at p < 0.01". No effect size is printed.
