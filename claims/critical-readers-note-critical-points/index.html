Karabay 2015¶

Aysegul Karabay. (2015). The guiding effects of a critical reading program on the use of external reading strategies when confronting an ironical text. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2419

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation of the coded essays and notes, stated in the Discussion. Summarizers "rewrote the author’s expressions"; reviewers took notes and underlined with less summarization. No effect size is printed.
