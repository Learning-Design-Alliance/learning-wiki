## Evidence

### Rollinde 2019

Rollinde, E. (2019). Enacting Planets to Understand Occultation Phenomena. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1910.09403](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1910.09403)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' observation during the September 2019 classroom sequence with 12-year-old pupils: after a single instruction to raise a hand as soon as one planet passes another, "almost all individuals tried to spot overtaking", including initially uninterested learners. No quantitative engagement measure is reported.

> "The gamification of the activity was obvious. Consequently, almost all individuals tried to spot overtaking, even those who were not interested in the activity at first."
