## Evidence

### Sørensen Jette Led 2015

Sørensen Jette Led, Navne Laura Emdal, Martin Helle Max, Ottesen Bent, Albrecthsen Charlotte Krebs, Pedersen Berit Woetmann, Kjærgaard Hanne, van der Vleuten Cees. (2015). Clarifying the learning experiences of healthcare professionals with in situ and off-site simulation-based medical education: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. [doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2015-008345](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-008345)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of the four focus groups (Theme 4 and subthemes 4a–4e) found positive and negative factors "were not related to the ISS or OSS setting". Factors named include practical organisation, cases, reversed hierarchies, involvement and debriefing.

> "The statements from participants about positive and negative factors were not related to the ISS or OSS setting but rather to other factors, which are presented in subthemes 4a–4e."
