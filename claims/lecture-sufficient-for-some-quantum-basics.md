### Hu 2024

Hu, P., Li, Y., & Singh, C. (2024). Investigating and improving student understanding of the basics of quantum computing. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020108)

Pre-test data from both courses showing that certain foundational concepts, including identifying possible states of two-bit/qubit systems and applying the Born rule for probabilities, were already well understood after traditional lecture-based instruction.

> "Students earned high scores of 85% or greater across the board for Questions 1a, 1b, 1d, 2a, 2b, and 3b, and 9c, suggesting that these lecture-based instruction was sufficient for these concepts."
