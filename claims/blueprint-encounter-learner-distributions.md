> "The learner role is in 46% of the scenarios a resident, in 30% a consultant, and in 18% an intern (for more details see Appendix B, Table A1). About one-third (34%) of the encounters occur in a medical practice, 23% in a hospital, 21% in a university hospital, 12% in an outpatient clinic, and 11% in a rural hospital."
