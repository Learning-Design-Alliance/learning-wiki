### Terzimehić 2025

Terzimehić, N., Bühler, B., & Kasneci, E. (2025). Conversational AI as a Catalyst for Informal Learning: An Empirical Large-Scale Study on LLM Use in Everyday Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11789 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2506.11789](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2506.11789)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 678 LLM learners using a 24-item inquiry taxonomy. "The most frequently performed tasks were Explanatory Inquiries (𝑛 = 471, 69.5%), Factual Queries (𝑛 = 429, 63.3%), and Tutorial Requests (𝑛 = 414, 61%)." Participants reported an average of 7.53 different inquiries (SD = 4.40).
