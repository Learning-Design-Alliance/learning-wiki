## Evidence

### Iulia Salaoru 2020

Iulia Salaoru. (2020). Engaged pedagogy: An Innovative method to Teach Physics. Emerging Technologies Research Centre, De Montfort University. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.02190](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.02190)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing Freeman and collaborators, a study on 225 students investigating the impact of lecture type (traditional vs. active learning) on learning and course performance, finding "a reduction in the failure rates by 1.5 times" and about 6% higher average examination grades. No effect size is printed.

> "This study demonstrated that the active learning lecture could be linked to a reduction in the failure rates by 1.5 times when compared to the traditional approach and as well as an improvement in average examination grades by about 6%."
