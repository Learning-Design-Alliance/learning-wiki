### Bagheri Majid 2025

Bagheri Majid, Shiryani Morteza, Mehralizade Sara, Basirinezhad Mohammad Hasan, Bagheri Hossein, Abbasi Ali. (2025). The effect of educational intervention using surgery preference cards on anxiety and clinical competence among operating room nursing students in general surgeries: a randomized controlled trial study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07930-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Parallel-group randomized controlled trial of 60 operating room nursing students (n = 30 per group, four-block randomization). Multiple linear regression adjusting for pre-intervention state anxiety showed the intervention group scored "0.27 units lower than the control group" (P = 0.019); the unadjusted post-intervention between-group test gave p = 0.031. No effect size printed.

> "after the intervention, the mean state anxiety score in the intervention group was 0.27 units lower than the control group, which was significant after controlling for the pre-intervention score (P = 0.019)."
