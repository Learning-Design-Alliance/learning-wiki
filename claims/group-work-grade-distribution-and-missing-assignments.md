Comparison of lecture-portion grade distributions and percentage of missing assignments for PHY 156, PHY 116 and PHY 114 (Figure 4); the laboratory portion was excluded because other instructors ran it. The authors report little distribution change, improvement in the "range of 70 -92", and significant improvement in submitted work; no effect size is printed.

> "Although the grade distribution does not show much difference in both semesters, students in the range of 70 -92 have been improved in each class. There is significant improvement of submitting work as shown on the right panels in Figure 4."
