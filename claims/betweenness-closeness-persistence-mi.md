## Evidence

### Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe 2015

Justyna P. Zwolak and Eric Brewe. (2015). The impact of social integration on student persistence in introductory Modeling Instruction courses. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.094)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate logistic regression of persistence in the electricity and magnetism Modeling Instruction course on centrality measures; Table I prints betweenness estimate −26.22 (p = 0.043) and closeness 94.82 (p = 0.032) as significant, while total degree (p = 0.106), indegree (p = 0.135), outdegree (p = 0.188) and eigenvector (p = 0.697) are not. The article reports "statistically signiﬁcant correlations only for measures considering the entire social network."

> "For MI we found statistically signiﬁcant correlations only for measures considering the entire social network, that is betweenness and closeness, and no statistically signiﬁcant correlations for measures aimed at the students ego network."
