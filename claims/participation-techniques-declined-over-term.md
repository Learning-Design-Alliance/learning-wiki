## Evidence

### Becker Erin A. 2017

Becker Erin A., Easlon Erin J., Potter Sarah C., Guzman-Alvarez Alberto, Spear Jensen M., Facciotti Marc T., Igo Michele M., Singer Mitchell, Pagliarulo Christopher. (2017). The Effects of Practice-Based Training on Graduate Teaching Assistants' Classroom Practices. CBE—Life Sciences Education 16(4):ar58. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-05-0162

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Longitudinal Wilcoxon rank-sum comparison of weeks 2 and 10 across 15 GTAs; "Frequency of cold call, and stretch it: explain logic significantly decreased over the course of the term" while circulate (moderate) and group volunteer events increased. No effect sizes are printed in this sentence.

> "Frequency of cold call, and stretch it: explain logic significantly decreased over the course of the term, as did overall participation rate and cold-call levels. In contrast, the frequency of student-initiated contact with the GTA during small-group work (circulate [moderate]) increased, as did the frequency of group volunteer events"
