Cross-sectional survey of 395 Ukrainian education managers (September 2025) using single-item 5-point Likert measures of personal and system readiness. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test confirmed the "0.68-point mean difference ( 𝑑= 0.73 , large effect)", i.e. an effect size of d = 0.73, between the two readiness dimensions.
