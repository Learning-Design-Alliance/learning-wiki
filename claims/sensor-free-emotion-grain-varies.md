## Evidence

### de Morais 2024

de Morais, F., Goldoni, D. D'Á., Kautzmann, T., Silva, R. R. da, Jaques, P. A. (2024). Automatic Sensor-free Affect Detection: A Systematic Literature Review. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.5753/rbie.2024.4362](https://doi.org/10.5753/rbie.2024.4362)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's analysis of grain level across selected papers reports observation windows "can vary from seconds to weeks, depending on the approach used to collect emotions," citing 20-second BROMP windows, 17-second average self-report intervals, and biweekly pop-ups.

> "Based on the examined papers, this time period can vary from seconds to weeks, depending on the approach used to collect emotions."
