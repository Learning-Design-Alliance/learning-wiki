## Evidence

### Xiaomeng Xu 2026

Xiaomeng Xu, Dan Zhang & Yu Zhang. (2026). Student teacher inter brain coupling forecast cross semester academic fluctuation in real world classrooms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00421-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup analysis categorizing students into four baseline performance groups and comparing model fit indices of dynamic and static models per subject. Predictive effects of student-teacher coupling "varied across performance groups," confirming modulation by students' prior academic standing.

> "For Chinese, the dynamic model yielded the most significant estimates within the top 25% of students . In contrast, for math, the best model fit in the dynamic analysis was observed among middle-performing students (25%-75%), while the static model showed the strongest fit for the bottom -performing group (bottom 25%)."
