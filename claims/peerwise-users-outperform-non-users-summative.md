Walsh Jason L, Harris Benjamin H L, Denny Paul, Smith Phil. (2018). Formative student-authored question bank: perceptions, question quality and association with summative performance. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2017-135018

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Across all three academic years of the two Cardiff medical cohorts, t-tests compared summative exam percentages of PeerWise users versus non-users. The article reports users "performed significantly better (p<0.001)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Comparison of summative performance between PeerWise users and non-users showed that users performed significantly better (p<0.001; figure 2A)."
