## Evidence

### J Woithe 2021

J Woithe, M Kersting. (2021). Bend it like dark matter!. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abe09c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abe09c)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The article reports, citing Coble et al., that undergraduates entering a general education astronomy course were 'mostly unaware of the existence and origins of dark matter'; it adds that even after instruction students had difficulties distinguishing dark matter from dark energy.

> "Coble et al. (13) reported that even undergraduate students were mostly unaware of the existence and origins of dark matter when entering a general education astronomy course."

### J Woithe 2021 (2)

J Woithe, M Kersting. (2021). Bend it like dark matter!. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abe09c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abe09c)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' own observation, based on numerous informal discussions with students and teachers in their role as physics educators, that students 'often struggle to distinguish dark matter from dark energy' and also from antimatter and black holes.

> "Students often struggle to distinguish dark matter from dark energy  but also from other "mysterious" concepts such as antimatter and black holes (14)."
