## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The authors conclude that supervised biopsy performance by less experienced nephrologists including fellows maintains safety and adequacy, so biopsy should become integral to fellowship training. [→ Esposito Vittoria 2018](#esposito-vittoria-2018)

## Evidence

### Esposito Vittoria 2018

Esposito Vittoria, Mazzon Giulia, Baiardi Paola, Torreggiani Massimo, Semeraro Luca, Catucci Davide, Colucci Marco, Mariotto Alice, Grosjean Fabrizio, Bovio Giacomo, Esposito Ciro. (2018). Safety and adequacy of percutaneous kidney biopsy performed by nephrology trainees. BMC Nephrology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12882-017-0796-y

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretive conclusion drawn from their retrospective comparison showing comparable complication rates (19% vs 19%, p = 0.993) and diagnostic power before and after trainee involvement. This is the article's recommendation, not a separately tested result.

> "Thus the kidney biopsy should be an integral part of nephrology fellows training allowing a more widespread diffusion of the technique to smaller and more peripheral centers."
