## Evidence

### Świdwa 2025

Świdwa, Jacek; Klatt, Stefanie; Kantanista, Adam. (2025). Gaze behavior and decision-making among handball referees: exploring gender and expertise differences. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.19401

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Laboratory video-based decision task with 51 Polish Handball Federation referees (31 higher-level, 20 lower-level) watching 31 five-second match clips and responding after each; an independent panel scored accuracy. The results section reports "Cohen's d = 0.678" for the overall group difference.

> "On average, higher-level referees made a significantly higher number of correct decisions (M = 18.03, SD = 3.68) compared to lower-level referees (M = 15.75, SD = 2.79, p < 0.05; Cohen's d = 0.678)."
