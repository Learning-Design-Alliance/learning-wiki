## Evidence

### Shahbazi Sighaldeh S 2026

Shahbazi Sighaldeh S, Norollahpour Z, Nekoolaltak M, Khosravi S, Saberian P, Rahimi Foroushani A, Shateranni F. (2026). Bridging the gap in pre-hospital maternity care: emergency personnel’s knowledge and clinical skills in managing obstetric emergencies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09636-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis among 97 Tehran Emergency Center personnel; the article reports a "significant relationship between clinical skill in OSCE and all three variables" of self-reported knowledge, self-reported clinical skill, and knowledge.

> "Additionally, there was a significant relationship between clinical skill in OSCE and all three variables of self-reported knowledge, self-reported clinical skill, and knowledge (p < 0.05)."
