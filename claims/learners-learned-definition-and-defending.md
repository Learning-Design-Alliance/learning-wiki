## Evidence

### Peadar Donohoe and Carmel O’Sullivan 2015

Peadar Donohoe and Carmel O’Sullivan. (2015). The Bullying Prevention Pack: Fostering Vocabulary and Knowledge on the Topic of Bullying and Prevention using Role-Play and Discussion to Reduce Primary School Bullying. Scenario, Volume 2015 · Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.33178/scenario.9.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Learner focus groups with six learners from each participating third, fourth, fifth and sixth class, transcribed and analysed against three objectives including topic knowledge. The article states this supports that the repetitive-aggression definition "had been successfully communicated to them".

> "By the end of the study , all focus groups reported that they understood bullying as repeated aggression."

### Peadar Donohoe and Carmel O’Sullivan 2015 (2)

Peadar Donohoe and Carmel O’Sullivan. (2015). The Bullying Prevention Pack: Fostering Vocabulary and Knowledge on the Topic of Bullying and Prevention using Role-Play and Discussion to Reduce Primary School Bullying. Scenario, Volume 2015 · Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.33178/scenario.9.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Focus-group finding on the second objective, ability to defend, from learners' own end-of-study reports; no earlier focus group, comparison group or observed defending is reported. Learners commonly cited reporting to a teacher, with strengthened belief in teacher efficacy, e.g. "I know I can talk to the teacher who will do something about it".

> "Learners reported that overall they were conﬁdent defenders whether it was by direct peer-to- peer intervention or reporting bullying incidents to a trusted adult."
