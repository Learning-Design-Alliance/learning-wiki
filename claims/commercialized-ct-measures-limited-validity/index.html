Lee JuHee 2016¶

Lee JuHee, Lee Yoonju, Gong SaeLom, Bae Juyeon, Choi Moonki. (2016). A meta-analysis of the effects of non-traditional teaching methods on the critical thinking abilities of nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5025580/

Authors' critique citing Simpson and Courtney and other prior work. These tools "measured the limited aspect of critical thinking" and "are not able to measure the students' performance for example, patient specific situation driven critical thinking." Self-report method may cause socially desirable responding.

"However, these commercialized tools measured the limited aspect of critical thinking, such as analyzing and interpreting the suggested patient written data [24, 25]. These tools are not able to measure the students' performance for example, patient specific situation driven critical thinking."
