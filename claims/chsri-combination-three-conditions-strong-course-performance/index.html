W. David Stevens 2008¶

Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/if-small-not-enough-characteristics-successful-small-high-schools-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparative analysis of ten CHSRI small schools using survey measures, qualitative fieldwork coding, and freshman GPA and on-track rates; schools were coded strong on each condition and outcomes were compared to demographically similar district schools. The analysis found "two combinations of attributes associated with strong course performance."

"Formal analysis of the distribution of characteristics and outcomes finds that there are two combinations of attributes associated with strong course performance. The first, found in three schools, combines developmental practices, deep principal leadership, teacher influence, and strong student supports."
