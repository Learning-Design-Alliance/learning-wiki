# Family-implemented literacy interventions produce a positive, significant average effect on combined literacy outcomes in single-case experimental design studies (g = 1.50), specific to students with or at risk for reading difficulties

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Dahl-Leonard 2025

Dahl-Leonard, K., Hall, C., Cho, E., Capin, P., Roberts, G. J., Kehoe, K. F., Haring, C., Peacott, D., & Demchak, A. (2025). Examining the Effects of Family-Implemented Literacy Interventions for School-Aged Children: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09985-3

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 10 single-case experimental design studies using between-case standardized mean difference effect sizes estimated with the scdhlm package. The pooled BC-SMD was "g = 1.50; SE = 0.38; df = 7.46; p < 0.01". No standardized effect-size benchmark applies; impact is null as only g and test statistics are printed.

> "The weighted mean effect on combined literacy outcomes was also positive and significant at an alpha of 0.05 (g = 1.50; SE = 0.38; df = 7.46; p < 0.01; 95% CI [0.62–2.38])."
