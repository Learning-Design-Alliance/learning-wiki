### Laurence Viennot 2020

Laurence Viennot. (2020). How to choose which explanation to use with students? Discussing the tensiometer with beginning teachers. International Journal of Science Education. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2020.1843082](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2020.1843082)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phase 2 benefit assessment in the seven-participant interview study. Participants said the approach "enables me to clarify my way of thinking and my preferences"; others stressed it "is not a decision-making algorithm" and one noted it might facilitate discussions between colleagues. No effect size reported.

> "When asked to assess the proposed multi-criteria diagnosis approach to choosing an explanation, STs were unanimously very positive, with some nuances."
