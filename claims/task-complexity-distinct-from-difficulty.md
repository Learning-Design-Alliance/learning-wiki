# Task complexity and task difficulty are distinct concepts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Tasks can be difficult because of many non-interacting elements without being complex, or difficult because few interacting elements must be processed simultaneously. [→ Ouhao Chen · Fred Paas · John Sweller 2023](#ouhao-chen-fred-paas-john-sweller-2023)

## Evidence

### Ouhao Chen · Fred Paas · John Sweller 2023

Ouhao Chen · Fred Paas · John Sweller. (2023). A Cognitive Load Theory Approach to Defining and Measuring Task Complexity Through Element Interactivity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09782-w

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the review distinguishing difficulty from complexity: memorising periodic table symbols is "a difficult, but not a complex task" because elements can be studied individually, whereas interacting elements impose heavy working memory load even when few.

> "First, many elements may need to be learned even though they do not interact. For instance, learning the symbols of the periodic table in chemistry is a difficult, but not a complex task."
