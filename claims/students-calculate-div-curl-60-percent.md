## Evidence

### Laurens Bollen 2015

Laurens Bollen, Paul van Kampen, and Mieke De Cock. (2015). Students’ difficulties with vector calculus in electrodynamics. arXiv preprint arXiv:1502.02830v2 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of six pretest calculations (N=30) in Cartesian and cylindrical coordinates, categorized as correct, minor error, major error, or no answer. The authors report that "approximately 60% of the students are able to calculate" correctly. No effect size is printed.

> "approximately 60% of the students are able to calculate the divergence and the curl of given vector ﬁelds, independent of the level of diﬃculty and the coordinate system used, if we allow minor errors."
