## Evidence

### Schwill Simon 2020

Schwill Simon, Reith Dorothee, Walter Tobias, Engeser Peter, Wensing Michel, Flum Elisabeth, Szecsenyi Joachim, Krug Katja. (2020). How to ensure basic competencies in end of life care – a mixed methods study with post-graduate trainees in primary care in Germany. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-020-00540-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-after comparison within the KWBW vocational training programme: 219 attendees surveyed pre-intervention (T1) and 153 at 6–8 month follow-up (T2), rating 20 competency items on a 5-point Likert scale. Overall competency rose from 2.9 (±0.8) to 3.1 (±0.7), "significantly higher than before (p < 0.01)". No effect size printed.

> "In T2, participants ranked their overall expertise in end-of-life care significantly higher than before (p < 0.01)."
