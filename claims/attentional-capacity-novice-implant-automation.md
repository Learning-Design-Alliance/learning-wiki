## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Because human attentional capacity is limited, novices allocating more resources to monitoring their hands, fluoroscopic navigation and decision making quickly reach their attentional capacity threshold; deliberate practice in simulation automates psychomotor skills and spatial judgements, liberating capacity. [→ Mascheroni Jorio 2021](#mascheroni-jorio-2021)
`q1 i?` Automation speed varies with trainees' innate visiospatial, perceptual and psychomotor abilities, so a fixed training time or procedure count does not guarantee proficiency for everybody. [→ Mascheroni Jorio 2021](#mascheroni-jorio-2021)
