## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The deliberately uncomfortable simulation exercise was reported by students to support learning both via the stress it induced and by creating a realistic but low-stakes practice setting. [→ Greenberg AL 2022](#greenberg-al-2022)

## Evidence

### Greenberg AL 2022

Greenberg AL, Syed SM, Alseidi A, O'Sullivan PS, Chern H. (2022). Robotic training for medical students: feasibility of a pilot simulation curriculum. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-022-01508-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of focus groups identified "simulation of stress" as one of four themes. Students reported the stress "cements a memory" and enabled debriefing of mistakes without harm to a real patient. Qualitative student-perception finding with no quantitative measure.

> "students conveyed the value of the “uncomfortable” simulation exercise, which they felt facilitated learning not only directly through the stress it induced but also through creating a realistic (but low stakes) setting for practicing critical skills"
