### J.R. Persson 2015

J.R. Persson. (2015). Evaluating the Force Concept Inventory for diﬀerent student groups at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1504.06099](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1504.06099)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item analysis of FCI post-test data from the three NTNU groups. The article reports the number of questions with difficulty above 0.9 "rises to 14 and 7", and average difficulty indexes range from 0.70-0.78 in the pre-test to 0.72-0.86 in the post-test.

> "In the post-test the nu mber of questions with a diﬃculty above 0.9 rises to 14 and 7, for the TFY4145 and TFY41 15 groups, respectively."

### J.R. Persson 2015 (2)

J.R. Persson. (2015). Evaluating the Force Concept Inventory for diﬀerent student groups at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1504.06099](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1504.06099)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' discussion of the post-test results: for the TFY4145 group the average difficulty index of 86% indicates "ceiling eﬀects will caus e problems"; the average discrimination index, though still above 0.3, is not a good indicator as half the questions fall below that level.

> "The post-test for Physics majors (TFY414 5) can not be considered as applicable in the present form for the full group, the average diﬃculty index (86%) has reached a level where ceiling eﬀects will caus e problems."

### J.R. Persson 2015 (3)

J.R. Persson. (2015). Evaluating the Force Concept Inventory for diﬀerent student groups at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1504.06099](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1504.06099)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item discrimination analysis of FCI post-tests: for TFY4145 and TFY4115 the number of items with D <0.3 "rises to 15 and 11, out of 30", while the average discrimination index decreases to 0.36 and 0.42. In the TFY4104 group the discrimination index remains almost the same (0.48).

> "In the post-test the number of questions with the discrimination index D <0.3, rises to 15 and 11, out of 30, for the TFY4145 and TFY4115 groups, respectively , while the averaged discrimination index decreases to 0.36 and 0.42, respectiv ely."
