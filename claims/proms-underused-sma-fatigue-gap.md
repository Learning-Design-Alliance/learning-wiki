Theme from thematic content analysis of the needs assessment and workshop data: evaluators lacked awareness of PROMs; fatigue reduction was the most frequently reported treatment-related change not captured by traditional COAs, and the 6MWT assesses fatiguability only in ambulatory individuals.

> "While there is a growing recognition for PROMs, their integration into routine clinical assessments remains limited, despite their potential to capture meaningful functional and quality-of-life changes that are not reflected in standard COAs."
