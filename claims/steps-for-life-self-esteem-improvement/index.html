Students’ Psychosocial Empowerment With The ‘Steps For Life’ Personal and Social Skills Greek Elementary Programme. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 10(5), 535-549. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2018541303

Teacher-rated BASE scale scores in the cluster-randomized study showed self-esteem change of 0.31 (intervention) vs 0.08 (control), with repeated-measures ANOVA group-effect p < 0.001; the discussion states "the experimental-group students exhibited significantly higher improvement in it post intervention".
