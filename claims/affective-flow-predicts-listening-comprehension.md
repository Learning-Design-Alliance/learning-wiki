## Evidence

### Liang 2026

Liang, Y., & Hu, G. (2026). Investigating L2 listening comprehension and experience in immersive and interactive virtual reality: An experimental study. Computers & Education, 248, 105593. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105593

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical regression on RCT data (N = 84) tested flow components as predictors of listening comprehension. The article reports the affective component showed "a borderline significant yet medium-sized effect" while the cognitive component was non-significant.

> "In Model 2, the affective component of state flow showed a borderline significant yet medium-sized effect on listening comprehension (B = 0.27, β = .20, p = .051), accounting for a unique 5% of the variance (r2 partial = .05), whereas its cognitive component was not a significant predictor (B = 0.04, β = .08, p = .456)."
