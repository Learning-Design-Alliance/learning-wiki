## Evidence

### Yu Lu 2019

Yu Lu, Zhu Xiaoqin, Shek Daniel T. L., Zou Xiao-Bing, Deng Hong-Zhu, Au Yeung Peter W. H. (2019). Validation of the Simplified Chinese Psychoeducational Profile Third Edition in Mainland China. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders, 49(4), 1599-1612. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-018-3827-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA comparing 554 children with ASD and 311 typically developing controls (matched on age and gender ratios), with Bonferroni-adjusted alpha of 0.005. The omnibus test printed is "F (10, 781) = 107.75, Wilks’ Lambda = 0.42, p < .001"; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "The multivariate effect of group was significant for the ten Performance subtests as a group: F (10, 781) = 107.75, Wilks’ Lambda = 0.42, p < .001. The result of the univariate analyses showed that the mean scores of the children in the control group were significantly higher than those of the children in the ASD group."
