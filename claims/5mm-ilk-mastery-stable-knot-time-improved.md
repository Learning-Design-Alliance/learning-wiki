# With 5-mm instruments, ILK mastery and OSATS scores were stable between the two IAD_PLSC sessions while knot completion time improved significantly

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` With 5-mm instruments, the proportion of trainees mastering the ILK (86.0% vs 76.7%, p = 0.41) and median OSATS scores (32 vs 32, p = 0.19) did not significantly change between sessions, but median knot completion time improved significantly (184 s vs 166 s, p = 0.002). ## Evidence

### Bidault-Jourdainne Valeska 2024

Bidault-Jourdainne Valeska, Breaud Jean, Ali Liza, Talon Isabelle, Becmeur François, Ghoneimi Alaa El, Peycelon Matthieu. (2024). Contribution of simulation in paediatric laparoscopic surgery: improving suturing skills before performing a laparoscopic procedure in children. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective observational study of 43 trainees in the IAD_PLSC program (2021–2022), assessed at the end of each of two 12-hour sessions. The article reports "no significant improvement in the median [interquartile range, IQR] OSATS score" but a significant knot-time improvement (p = 0.002).

> "There was no significant improvement in the median [interquartile range, IQR] OSATS score (32 [30–36] vs. 32 [28–34], p = 0.19), but the median [IQR] knot completion time improved significantly (184 s [161–268] vs. 166 s [128–218], p = 0.002)."
