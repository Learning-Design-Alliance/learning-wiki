Theoretical and historiographical argument in the section on teaching QM at high school: the authors argue the historical approach "is not QM at all" and note that the usual postulates and key consequences are not even mentioned in Italian and other European guidelines for secondary education. No empirical data are reported.

> "the historical approach to QM is not QM at all, since it focuses mostly on concepts and models developed in the period from 1900-1925 and stops at the very threshold of what we call QM today."
