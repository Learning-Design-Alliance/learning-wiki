Urbańska-Grosz J 2023¶

Urbańska-Grosz J, Walkiewicz M, Sitek EJ. (2023). Is there sufficient evidence for the association between executive dysfunction and academic performance in adolescents with major depressive disorder?: a systematic review. European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-023-02275-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's overall conclusion, based on its systematic synthesis of five studies none of which reported educational attainment data. The review states the relationship "cannot be established on the basis of the existing literature".

"Summing up, the relationship between educational attainment and problem-solving and inhibitory control in adolescents with MDD cannot be established on the basis of the existing literature."
