Salazar 2015¶

Salazar, D. A. (2015). Razalas' Grouping Method and Mathematics Achievement. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.8. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1082724

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest analysis of the two intact classes (n=9 control, n=21 experimental after excluding 13 female students for cultural reasons) showed nearly all students scored zero; the author attributes this to weak algebra and differential calculus background.

"both groups (control and experimental) have a mean score in the pretest is 0.33 and 0.29 respectively with a grade equivalent of 5.0, a failure grade. 100% of the students in the control group failed."
