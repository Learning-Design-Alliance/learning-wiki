LSD comparison of combination groups in the quasi-experiment: conventional learning combinations held notation c or d, with the lowest corrected mean 43.443 for conventional lower ability. The article states conventional learning also had adverse effects on upper ability pre-service teachers.

> "the pre-service teachers (the upper, middle, and lower academic ability) obtain the lowest critical thinking skills scores. Moreover, due to the implementation of conventional learning, the critical thinking skills of the upper academic ability pre-service teachers are not significantly different from those of the middle academic ability pre-service teachers, or even equal"
