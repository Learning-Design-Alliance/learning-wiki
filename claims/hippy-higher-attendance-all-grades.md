## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children who participated in HIPPY as preschoolers had significantly higher school attendance rates than demographically matched comparison children in 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th grades. [→ Brown 2014](#brown-2014)

## Evidence

### Brown 2014

Brown, A., & Lee, J. (2014). School Performance in Elementary, Middle, and High School: A Comparison of Children Based on HIPPY Participation During the Preschool Years. School Community Journal, 24(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1048627

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-tests with Bonferroni adjustment compared mean attendance rates of 516 HIPPY and 516 matched comparison children across four grade levels. The study reports "significantly higher school attendance rates" for HIPPY children at every grade, with the largest gap in 9th grade (94% vs. 88%).

> "At all four grade levels, children in the HIPPY group had significantly higher school attendance rates during the academic year than children in the comparison group."
