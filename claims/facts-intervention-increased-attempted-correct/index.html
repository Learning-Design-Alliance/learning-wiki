Evidence¶

Barbara Allen-Lyall 2018¶

Barbara Allen-Lyall. (2018). Helping Students to Automatize Multiplication Facts: A Pilot Study. IEJEE, Volume 10, Issue 4, 391-396. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2018438128

q3 · i2 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of fall Grade 4 timed facts inventory scores between a no-treatment cohort (N=160) and a treatment cohort (N=133) taught by the same teachers. Independent-samples t-tests showed a significant difference in “attempted cor- rect” scores, with d= 0.52.

"There was a significant difference in the “attempted cor- rect” scores for no treatment (M= .90, SD= .12) and treat- ment (M= .95, SD= .06); t(250)= 4.53, p< .001, d= 0.52."
