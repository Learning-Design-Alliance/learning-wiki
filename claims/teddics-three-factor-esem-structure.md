## Evidence

### Siddiq F. 2015

Siddiq F., Scherer R. & Tondeur J. (2015). Teachers’ Emphasis on Developing Students’ Digital Information and Communication Skills (TEDDICS): A New Construct in 21st Century Education. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.10.006

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling analysis of the ICILS 2013 Norwegian teacher sample (N = 1,072) comparing CFA and ESEM measurement models of TEDDICS. The article reports that "ESEM significantly outperformed the CFA model" on the printed fit-change indices.

> "Moreover, ESEM significantly outperformed the CFA model (∆RMSEA = -.025, ∆CFI = +.061, ∆TLI = +.062) and was thus accepted."
