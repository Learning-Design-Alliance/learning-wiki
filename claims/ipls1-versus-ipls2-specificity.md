## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Geller 2022

Benjamin D. Geller, Jack Rubien, Sara M. Hiebert, and Catherine H. Crouch. (2022). The impact of introductory physics for the life sciences in a senior biology capstone course. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Wilcoxon comparison between students who had taken IPLS 1 and students who had taken only IPLS 2 on both code categories, testing whether IPLS 1-specific performance depends on IPLS 1 content; the article reports the significant difference on IPLS 1-specific elements and the non-significant quantitative comparison, giving the authors confidence their code categorization is reasonable.

> "Performance on the IPLS 1-specific code elements differed significantly between these groups (Wilcoxon p < 0.001, Fig. 3a), while performance on the general quantitative skills elements did not (Wilcoxon p = 0.65, Fig. 3b)."
