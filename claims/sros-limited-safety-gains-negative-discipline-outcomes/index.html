Evidence¶

dePaoli 2023¶

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

q3 · i? · review · r?

The review reports a 2023 national study including 3,443 schools using "a rigorous research design that included a strong comparison group" to estimate SRO impacts on safety indicators. SROs increased weapons detected and reduced fights and threats, but "did not lower rates of gun-related incidents."

"The national study found that school resource officers led to an increase in the number of weapons detected in a school and a reduction in the number of fights and threats. however, they did not lower rates of gun-related incidents."

dePaoli 2023 (2)¶

dePaoli, J., & McCombs, J. (2023). Safe schools, thriving students: Evidence-based strategies for creating safe and supportive schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/701.445

q3 · i? · review · r?

The same national study found negative effects: increased suspensions, expulsions, police referrals, and arrests. The review reports these effects were "consistently and significantly larger for Black students" and students with disabilities, and largest for Black students with disabilities.

"The presence of school resource officers led to an increase in the rate of suspensions, expulsions, police referrals, and student arrests—all of which are associated with long-term negative impacts on students."
