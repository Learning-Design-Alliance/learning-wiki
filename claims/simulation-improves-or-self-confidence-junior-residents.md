## Evidence

### Geoffrion R 2024

Geoffrion R, Koenig NA, Cundiff GW, Flood C, Hyakutake MT, Schulz J, Brennand EA, Lee T, Singer J, Todd NJ. (2024). Procedure-specific simulation for vaginal surgery training: A randomized controlled trial. Acta Obstetricia et Gynecologica Scandinavica. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Resident-rated validated self-confidence scale in the randomized trial, measured during real OR performance of index procedures. Adjusted analysis showed significant improvement "overall and for VH and PR (p < 0.05 for all)", with the PGY-1 subgroup showing "mean difference overall 22.4%, 95% CI: 8.8–36.0, p = 0.002".

> "In the adjusted analysis, it improved significantly overall and for VH and PR (p < 0.05 for all), with greatest improvement in the most junior PGY‐1 residents (mean difference overall 22.4%, 95% CI: 8.8–36.0, p = 0.002; Figures 2 and 3)."
