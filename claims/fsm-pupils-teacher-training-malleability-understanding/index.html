Rienzo et al. 2015¶

Cinzia Rienzo, Heather Rolfe and David Wilkinson (NIESR). (2015). Changing Mindsets: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581132

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis within the INSET randomised controlled trial measured pupils' theories of intelligence using Dweck's (1999) entity theory statements. The report states the intervention "led to higher scores on the growth mindset measurements for pupils eligible for FSM" and that this finding is secure.
