# VMI-3R scores decline significantly with age in Korean adults, dropping significantly from the 50s compared to the 20s

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Mean VMI-3R scores differed statistically across 10-yr age groups and were significantly lower from the 50s than in the 20s. [→ Kim E 2014](#kim-e-2014)

## Evidence

### Kim E 2014

Kim E, Park Y-K, Byun Y-H, Park M-S, Kim H. (2014). Influence of aging on visual perception and visual motor integration in Korean adults. Journal of Exercise Rehabilitation 10(4):245-250. https://doi.org/10.12965/jer.140147

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey comparing VMI-3R scores across six 10-yr age groups (n=192 for VMI-3R) with ANOVA and Scheffe post-hoc; means ranged from 43.47±6.54 in the 20s to 29.40±9.47 in the 70s, a "statistical differences among the groups" result.

> "The present results showed that there were statistical differences among the groups (F=16.583, P<0.001) and the mean score of VMI-3R was significantly decreased from 50s compared to that in 20s (Fig. 1B)."
