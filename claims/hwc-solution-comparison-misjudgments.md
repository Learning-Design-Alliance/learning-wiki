### Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox 2025

Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2025). Motivating reflection in problem solving: homework corrections in upper-division physics courses. [doi:10.1103/ntvp-spkc](https://doi.org/10.1103/ntvp-spkc)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interview data from Ari completing HWCs in Instructor A's QM2 course, on a question about energy level splitting values. The article reports he decided to correct a question although "Ari's initial work was correct and this difference was merely a different algebraic approach."

> "While comparing his work to the solutions, he states that he got all the correct values but "was not getting the 16 times smaller or 5 times smaller" and that he will have to correct this question. However, Ari's initial work was correct and this difference was merely a different algebraic approach."
