### Exact Path Proficiency + Exact Path Growth 2025

Exact Path Proficiency + Exact Path Growth. (2025). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/exact-path-proficiency-exact-path-growth-reading/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

A quasi-experimental study of middle school students in a large Florida district compared users of the combined program with non-users, matching students on prior achievement and demographics and accounting for school-level clustering. The study found a "positive effect size of +0.14 on Spring 2025 FAST ELA", described as statistically significant.
