Steffen Zitzmann 2025¶

Steffen Zitzmann, Gabe A. Orona. (2025). Why We Might Still be Concerned About Low Cronbach's Alphas in Domain-specific Knowledge Tests. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10015-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Analytical derivation in the commentary: assuming standardized parallel items, alpha is algebraically reduced to the true-score-variance-to-scale-score-variance ratio, which "is exactly what a reliability coefficient is expected to do (Cho, 2016)" under classical test theory, domain sampling, and generalizability theory.
