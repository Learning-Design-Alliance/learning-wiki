## Evidence

### AMSTI (Alabama Math 2012

AMSTI (Alabama Math, Science, and Technology Initiative) Evidence Summary. (2012). https://eric.ed.gov

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Teacher surveys administered across AMSTI treatment and comparison groups using Likert-based items (Newman et al., 2012) found AMSTI teachers "were more likely to report their students as engaged" and reported increased active learning use.

> "Findings from the teacher survey found that AMSTI teachers were more likely to report their students as engaged, had greater content knowledge in math or science, and increased their use of active learning streategies with students (by almost 25 minutes per week in math and 20 minutes per week in science)."
