## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Loturco 2022

Loturco, I.; Montoya, N.P.; Ferraz, M.B.; Berbat, V.; Pereira, L.A. (2022). A Systematic Review of the Effects of Physical Activity on Specific Academic Skills of School Students. Educ. Sci. 2022, 12, 134. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci12020134

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Narrative synthesis of moderator evidence, citing Quinto Romani et al.'s finding of limited benefit tending higher for high-intensity programs and Ardoy et al.'s comparison of standard, increased-volume, and increased-volume-plus-intensity PE conditions. The review reports increased frequency without intensity was insufficient.

> "the simple increase in frequency of physical education sessions (e.g., from 2 to 4 times/week) is not sufﬁcient to promote positive effects on academic achievement (at least when these increases are not accompanied by concomitant changes in exercise intensity)"
