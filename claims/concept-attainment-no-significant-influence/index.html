Wenno et al. 2016¶

Izaak Hendrik Wenno, Pieter Wattimena, Luky Maspaitela. (2016). Comparative Study between Drill Skill and Concept Attainment Model towards Physics Learning Achievement. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.5, No.3. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q2 · i? · causal · r1

In the quasi-experimental comparison, the concept attainment group's post-test results showed 46.7% of students still in the fail category. The authors state the model "do not show a significant influence on student learning achievement, when compared to the drill skill learning model".
