Quasi-experimental difference-in-differences analysis of 625 public two-year colleges (53 adopters) observed 2008–2014 using IPEDS and College Scorecard data, with college and year fixed effects and covariates. The article reports "no statistically significant impact" on enrollment or average amount borrowed.
