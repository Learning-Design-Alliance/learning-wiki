## Evidence

### Latifa Jackson 2020

Latifa Jackson, Heriberto Acosta Maestre. (2020). The Data Science Fire Next Time: Innovative strategies for mentoring in data science. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3394486.3411077](https://doi.org/10.1145/3394486.3411077)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post online survey administered before and after the 2019 BPDM workshop, in collaboration with Google's Explore Computer Science program. Pretest mean was 3.841 (N=28) and posttest mean 3.9821 (N=16); the authors report "a significant change over the course of the workshop" with no effect size printed.

> "When participants were asked about their sense of self efficacy prior (N=28, x=3.841, SD=0.74721, SE Mean=0.14121) to and after the workshop (N=16, x=3.9821, SD=0.87268, SEMean=0.21817), we saw a significant change over the course of the workshop."
