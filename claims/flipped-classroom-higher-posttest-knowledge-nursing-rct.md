### Yousefi Mahboubeh Sadat 2026

Yousefi Mahboubeh Sadat, Fooladzadeh Dehghan Mohsen, Ilkhani Mahnaz, Khajoei Rahimeh, Heidari Saeide, Jokar Mozhgan. (2026). The effects of flipped classroom versus lecture-based learning on academic performance and perceptions of classroom environment among nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09746-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

A single-blind parallel-group pretest-posttest RCT with 58 nursing students (29 per arm) at Qom University of Medical Sciences, analyzed by intention-to-treat. ANCOVA on post-test knowledge controlling for pretest showed "partial η² = 0.65"; adjusted mean difference was 3.59 points (95% CI 2.89–4.30) favoring the flipped group.

> "After controlling for pretest scores, ANCOVA revealed a significant and large effect of the flipped classroom on post-test knowledge (F = 104.719, P < 0.001, partial η² = 0.65)."
