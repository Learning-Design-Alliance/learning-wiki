# In Michigan districts where most students are Black, students are far more likely than peers in majority-white districts to have inexperienced or out-of-field teachers

## Evidence

### Jen DeNeal 2025

Jen DeNeal, Charlotte Pierce, and the EdTrust-Midwest team. (2025). Addressing Michigan's Teacher Shortage Problem for Students Most in Need: Closing the Opportunity Divide. EdTrust-Midwest. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED671388

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of Michigan workforce data comparing districts by student racial composition found that in districts where the majority of students are Black, "students are nearly four times more likely to learn from an out-of-field teacher" and face similarly elevated odds for emergency-credentialed and beginning teachers.

> "In these districts, students are nearly four times more likely to learn from an out-of-field teacher, four times more likely to learn from a teacher with emergency credentials, and nearly twice as likely to learn from a beginning teacher than in districts serving primarily white students."
