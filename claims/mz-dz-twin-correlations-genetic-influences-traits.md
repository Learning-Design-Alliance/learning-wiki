# Twin correlations indicate genetic influences on self-control, grit, and school performance, with cross-trait cross-twin correlations higher in MZ than DZ twins

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` MZ twin correlations for school performance, self-control, and grit (0.809, 0.715, 0.751) exceed DZ correlations (0.419, 0.276, 0.176), suggesting an ADE model for self-control and grit. [→ Kevenaar 2023](#kevenaar-2023)
`q3 i?` In the ADE model, most phenotypic variance is genetic: broad-sense heritability of school performance is 0.793 (76.8% A, 2.5% D), and about 73% of grit variance is genetic. [→ Kevenaar 2023 (2)](#kevenaar-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Kevenaar 2023

Kevenaar, S. T., van Bergen, E., Oldehinkel, A. J., Boomsma, D. I. & Dolan, C. V. (2023). The relationship of school performance with self-control and grit is strongly genetic and weakly causal. npj Science of Learning, 8:53. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00198-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Saturated-model MZ and DZ correlation matrices (Table 1), conditional on covariates sex, SES, and rater and corrected for censoring. The article notes cross-trait cross-twin correlations are "higher in MZ than DZ, suggesting genetic correlations between the traits".

> "The MZ twin correlations in Table 1 for school performance, self-control, and grit equal 0.809 (95% CIs: 0.788 –0.928), 0.715 (95% CIs: 0.685 –0.716), and 0.751 (95% CIs: 0.722 –0.778), respectively"

### Kevenaar 2023 (2)

Kevenaar, S. T., van Bergen, E., Oldehinkel, A. J., Boomsma, D. I. & Dolan, C. V. (2023). The relationship of school performance with self-control and grit is strongly genetic and weakly causal. npj Science of Learning, 8:53. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00198-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

ADE twin model (Model 2) variance decomposition (Table 2). For school performance, "76.8% is attributable to additive genetic factors" and 2.5% to dominance factors (broad-sense heritability 0.793); rater effects ranged from 21.2% (grit) to 0.2% (self-control).

> "the standardized variance of grit, conditional on the covariates (SES, sex, and rater), is expressed in proportions as follows 0.179 (A), 0.556 (D), and 0.265 (E)"
