`q2 i?` After a 20-hour simulation-based ultrasound training program, final-year medical students' mean theoretical exam score increased from 7.9 ± 2.2 to 17.1 ± 1.6 (p < .0001). [→ Eroglu Oguz & Coskun Figen 2018](#eroglu-oguz-coskun-figen-2018)

## Evidence

### Eroglu Oguz & Coskun Figen 2018

Eroglu Oguz & Coskun Figen. (2018). Medical students’ knowledge of ultrasonography: effects of a simulation-based ultrasound training program. The Pan African Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.11604/pamj.2018.30.122.14820

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pre-post study of 96 final-year medical students on an emergency department rotation, using a paired sample t-test. The article reports the mean theoretical exam score rose from "7.9 ± 2.2" to "17.14 ± 1.61" (p < .0001).

> "The mean test score of the students before training was 7.9 ± 2.2, while that after training was 17.14 ± 1.61 (p < .0001; Table 1)."
