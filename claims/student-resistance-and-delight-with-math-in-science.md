## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Life science students may express discomfort with equations beyond calculating while also responding enthusiastically when mathematical relationships explain familiar phenomena. [→ Edward F. Redish 2021](#edward-f-redish-2021)

## Evidence

### Edward F. Redish 2021

Edward F. Redish. (2021). Using math in physics: Overview. Preprint. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/5.0021129](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0021129)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Interview evidence from Watkins and Elby's study of life science students in introductory physics: the student Ashlyn memorizes equations for exams but resists thinking with them, yet later reports that learning the mathematical relationship behind scaling "was really, like, it blew my mind."

> "I don't like to think of biology in terms of numbers and variables. I feel like that's what physics and calculus are for."
