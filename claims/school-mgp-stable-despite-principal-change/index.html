Evidence¶

Christine Ross 2015¶

Christine Ross, Mariesa Herrmann, Megan Hague Angus. (2015). Measuring Principals' Effectiveness: Results from New Jersey's Principal Evaluation Pilot. Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs/regions/midatlantic

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational analysis of pilot data on school median student growth percentiles. The report finds "year-to-year stability even when the school changes principals" and attributes this, as an interpretation, to persistent school characteristics. No stability coefficient is printed on this page.

"School median student growth percentiles, which measure student achievement growth during the school year, exhibit year-to-year stability even when the school changes principals. This may reflect persistent school characteristics,"
