### Daniel R. Espinas 2024

Daniel R. Espinas, Brennan W. Chandler. (2024). Correlates of K-12 Students' Intertextual Integration. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09889-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review with systematic search, selection, and coding procedures across PsycInfo, ERIC, and ProQuest plus backward, forward, and snowball searching, yielding 25 included records. The review reports that "results generally showed that variation in literacy, cognition, metacognition, knowledge, beliefs, and motivation are positively and moderately associated with intertextual integration."

> "We identified 25 studies published in 23 peer-reviewed journal articles and two dissertations/theses. These examined a wide range of individual difference factors, which we organized into four categories"
