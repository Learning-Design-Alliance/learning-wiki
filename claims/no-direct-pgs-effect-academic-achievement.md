Associations between genetic predisposition to mental health problems and academic achievement: a developmental perspective using two population‐based cohorts. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the Generation R SEM (N = 2,992), direct paths were non-significant (PGS-NDD standardized estimate −0.014, p = .387; PGS-EXT 0.016, p = .267, Table 3); the authors report "We did not find a direct association between PGS‐NDD and PGS‐EXT and academic achievement after adjusting for the set of covariates". The same null held in ALSPAC. Equivalence was not tested.
