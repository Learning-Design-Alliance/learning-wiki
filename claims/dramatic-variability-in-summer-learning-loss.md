## Evidence

### Atteberry 2021

Atteberry, A., & McEachin, A. (2021). School's Out: The Role of Summers in Understanding Achievement Disparities. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831220937285

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Results section, RQ2: empirical Bayes estimates and variance components from the multilevel growth models show the spread of summer gains/losses. The article reports ELA summer SD-to-mean ratios "ranging from 1.4 to as high as 5.2" versus school-year ratios reaching only 1.4; no effect size is printed.

> "In sum, some students experience accelerated learning during the summer relative to the preceding school year, while others lose nearly all of their prior gains."
