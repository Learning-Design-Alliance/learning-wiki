## Evidence

### Yucetoker 2016

Yucetoker, I. (2016). The visual memory-based memorization techniques in piano education. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 65, 111-128. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.65.07

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test/post-test control group study with 12 sophomore piano students (6 per group) at Bolu University; experimental group received 6 weeks of visual memorization technique instruction. Mann-Whitney U and Wilcoxon tests showed the experimental group improved significantly while the control group did not.

> "in the final test, a substantial difference of level emerged in favor of the experiment group"
