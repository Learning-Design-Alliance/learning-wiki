## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Olu Oyinloye 2013

Olu Oyinloye, Abiodun.A. Popoola. (2013). Activating Junior Secondary School Students' Prior Knowledge for the Development of Vocabulary, Concepts and Mathematics through Instructional Strategies. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 1(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.1n.2p.1

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors' observational claim, drawn from their personal experiences and discussions with professional colleagues and secondary school teachers during workshops, plus teaching-practice supervision observations that pre-service teachers failed to activate students' prior knowledge. No data are reported for this assertion.

> "At times they only state it in their lesson notes but fail to find out if the prior knowledge of the students is relevant to the new lessons. In this sense, they do not activate the prior knowledge of the students."
