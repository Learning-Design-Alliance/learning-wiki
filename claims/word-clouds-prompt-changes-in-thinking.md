`q2 i?` Students in the word cloud condition described the activity as fostering objective thinking, problem solving, creativity, and purposeful reflection on their own thought processes. [→ deNoyelles 2015](#denoyelles-2015)

## Evidence

### deNoyelles 2015

deNoyelles, A., & Reyes-Foster, B. (2015). Using Word Clouds in Online Discussions to Support Critical Thinking and Engagement. Online Learning, 19(4). https://olj.onlinelearningconsortium.org/index.php/olj/article/view/478

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of open-ended survey comments from word cloud condition students identified themes of change in thinking, including objectivity, problem solving, creativity, and purposeful reflection. One student noted the assignment 'forced me to think more objectively.'

> "While encouraging students to think more freely, the word clouds subtly fostered objective thinking."
