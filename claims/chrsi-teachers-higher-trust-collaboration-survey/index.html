Evidence¶

Stevens 2006¶

Stevens, W. D., with Kahne, J. (2006). Professional Communities and Instructional Improvement Practices: A study of small high schools in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/professional-communities-and-instructional-improvement-practices-study-small-high

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Earlier Consortium survey research on CHSRI schools, cited in the report's introduction as background, found significant differences in trust and collaboration; the report provides no effect size for this comparison.

"On survey measures, teachers in CHSRI schools had statistically signiﬁ- cantly higher perceptions of trust and collaboration between colleagues than teachers from non-CHSRI schools."
