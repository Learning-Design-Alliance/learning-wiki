## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Compared to students, more items were consistently right or wrong for ChatGPT, and it left far more answer options almost never selected (61 vs 19 below 5%). [→ Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic 2024](#giulia-polverini-and-bor-gregorcic-2024)

## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic 2024

Giulia Polverini and Bor Gregorcic. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT on the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010109)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of the relative frequency of selected answer options across all 26 items (130 answer options in total), shown in Figs. 2 and 3: ChatGPT's responses were "less spread among the available answer options than those of students".

> "students selected 19 answer options less than 5% of the time, while for ChatGPT this number is 61. This indicates that ChatGPT is more likely to exclude certain answer options from its responses. Options selected more than 70% of the time are also overrepresented in ChatGPT's responses"
