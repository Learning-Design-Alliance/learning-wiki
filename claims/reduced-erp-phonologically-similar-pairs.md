## Evidence

### Cao 2026

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ERP Condition effects across 62 adults during training: "SM pairs elicited significantly less negative ERP responses compared to DM pairs" in S2 temporoparietal (η²P = .12) and occipitoparietal (η²P = .11) clusters and S3 frontotemporal cluster (η²P = .18).

> "SM pairs elicited significantly less negative ERP responses compared to DM pairs in the S2 window in the temporoparietal cluster (Condition: F(1, 60) = 8.50, p = .005, η²P = .12; Δ= .25, SE = .09) and the occipitoparietal cluster (Condition: F(1, 60) = 7.83, p = .007, η²P = .11; Δ = .24, SE = .09) and in the S3 window in the frontotemporal cluster (Condition: F(1, 60) = 13.27, p = .001, η²P = .18; Δ = .30, SE = .08)."
