`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory bivariate analysis in the survey of 141 first-year medical students comparing WP and non-WP students on the mindset scale. The printed correlation is r = − 0.223 with p = 0.08, which does not reach significance; qualitative comments suggested these students' growth mindsets preceded the course.

> "Identifying as a student from a ‘widening participation’ (WP) background, relating to being from a disadvantaged and/or under-represented group was associated with a moderately higher mindset score (r = − 0.223, p = 0.08)."
