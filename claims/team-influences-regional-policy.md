Developing a programme theory to explain how primary health care teams learn to respond to intimate partner violence: a realist case-study. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Micro-to-macro mechanism from interviews in the single-case study. La Virgen's social worker and family doctors joined the Technical Commission of the Gender Based Violence Training Plan, ran workshops, and shaped the regional protocol and Women's Health Plan, including a chapter on women's groups and IPV detection.

> "The Murcia Regional Health System IPV Programme managers involved service providers in the design of policies and programmes and thus created a context of inclusive decision-making processes, in which IPV professionals could operate as legitimate and respected experts."
