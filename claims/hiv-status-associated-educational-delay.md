## Evidence

### Toska Elona 2019

Toska Elona, Cluver Lucie, Orkin Mark, Bains Anurita, Sherr Lorraine, Berezin McKenzie, Gulaid Laurie. (2019). Screening and supporting through schools: educational experiences and needs of adolescents living with HIV in a South African cohort. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-019-6580-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression (N = 1519) with Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted p-values found HIV-positive status associated with educational delay, "aOR = 1.65 95%CI 1.25–2.17 p < 0.001", controlling for socio-demographic and health variables.

> "adolescents living with HIV were more likely to report past-term absenteeism (aOR = 1.69 95%CI 1.08–2.64 p = 0.021) and educational delay – being at least one grade behind (aOR = 1.65 95%CI 1.25–2.17 p < 0.001)."
