### Bernard Aaron W. 2017

Bernard Aaron W., Ceccolini Gabbriel, Feinn Richard, Rockfeld Jennifer, Rosenberg Ilene, Thomas Listy, Cassese Todd. (2017). Medical students review of formative OSCE scores, checklists, and videos improves with student-faculty debriefing meetings. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1324718

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of simulation-software access logs from 92 first-year and 86 second-year students (460 and 687 review opportunities) during 2015-16. The study reports students "reviewed 64% of the scores, 42% of the checklists, and 28% of the videos on their own."

> "Together year 1 and year 2 students reviewed 64% of the scores, 42% of the checklists, and 28% of the videos on their own."
