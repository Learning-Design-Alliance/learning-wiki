## Evidence

### Hissbach Johanna C 2014

Hissbach Johanna C, Sehner Susanne, Harendza Sigrid, Hampe Wolfgang. (2014). Cutting costs of multiple mini-interviews – changes in reliability and efficiency of the Hamburg medical school admission test between two applications. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-54

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational psychometric analysis of two high-stakes admission cohorts (78 candidates tested in 2009, 193 in 2010) using multilevel random-intercept models with REML. The article reports "the ICC remained stable at 0.76" after the procedure was reduced to nine stations and 2.17 raters per station.

> "The overall reliability of the initial 2009 HAM-Int procedure with twelve stations and an average of 2.33 raters per station was ICC=0.75. Following the improvement actions, in 2010 the ICC remained stable at 0.76, despite the reduction of the process to nine stations and 2.17 raters per station."
