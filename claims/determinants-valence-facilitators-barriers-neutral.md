## Evidence

### Mirata 2023

Mirata, V., & Bergamin, P. (2023). Role of organisational readiness and stakeholder acceptance: an implementation framework of adaptive learning for higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10248-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Secondary analysis of qualitative Delphi data from 51 experts at two universities, coded inductively with axial coding in NVivo 12 Pro. The article reports the valence classification and that "a specific determinant could act as a prerequisite, moderator, and strategy or an outcome according to its empirical connection with other elements".

> "The first finding of the study was that all challenges associated with effective implementation were classified into three determinant groups according to their valence, namely, facilitators with a positive impact (+); barriers with a negative impact (−) and challenges with a neutral impact (0)."
