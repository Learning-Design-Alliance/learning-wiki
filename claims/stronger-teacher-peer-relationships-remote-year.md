# Students reported stronger relationships with teachers and peers during the remote/hybrid year, especially high school students, but these gains did not persist after returning in person

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` From 2019 to 2021, high school students' reports of Academic Personalism, Student-Teacher Trust, and Peer Relationships improved by 0.13, 0.17, and 0.30 s.d., reaching levels 0.40 s.d. above 2011; middle-grade trust and peer relationships improved by 0.07 and 0.17 s.d. [→ Gwynne 2024](#gwynne-2024)
`q2 i?` After returning to in-person schooling, high school relationship reports returned to pre-pandemic levels, while middle-grade reports declined below 2019 levels to as low as 2011 levels. [→ Gwynne 2024](#gwynne-2024)
