## Evidence

### Pessa Valente Emanuelle 2021

Pessa Valente Emanuelle, Cattaneo Adriano, Sola Maria Vittoria, Travan Laura, Quintero Romero Sofia, Milinco Mariarosa, Decorti Cinzia, Giornelli Roberta, Braida Cinzia, Dalmin Patrizia, Giangreco Manuela, Ronfani Luca. (2021). Problem-based learning for in-service training on breastfeeding in Friuli Venezia Giulia, Italy. International Breastfeeding Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13006-021-00439-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Longitudinal KAP survey of 105 of 137 participants (76.6%) from nine 2019 courses, assessed at T0, T1 and T2 with Wilcoxon signed ranks tests; the article reports "statistically significant differences between T0 and T1 and between T0 and T2, but not between T1 and T2". Table 3 prints p < 0.0001 for knowledge, attitude and total scores, p = 0.002 (T1-T0) and p = 0.001 (T2-T0) for practice.

> "There were statistically significant differences between T0 and T1 and between T0 and T2, but not between T1 and T2, indicating a general improvement after the course and good retention of knowledge, attitude and practice after 4–6 months."

### Pessa Valente Emanuelle 2021 (2)

Pessa Valente Emanuelle, Cattaneo Adriano, Sola Maria Vittoria, Travan Laura, Quintero Romero Sofia, Milinco Mariarosa, Decorti Cinzia, Giornelli Roberta, Braida Cinzia, Dalmin Patrizia, Giangreco Manuela, Ronfani Luca. (2021). Problem-based learning for in-service training on breastfeeding in Friuli Venezia Giulia, Italy. International Breastfeeding Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13006-021-00439-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Item-level McNemar analysis of the same 105-participant KAP survey found significant improvement with retention for "11 out of 20 knowledge questions, seven out of ten attitude questions, and two out of five practice questions"; non-significant items were those where more than 90% already answered correctly at T0, or concerned skin-to-skin duration and hand pumping.

> "The detailed analysis of the scores obtained for each question showed a statistically significant improvement between T0 and T1 and between T0 and T2, with retention of a good score between T1 and T2, for 11 out of 20 knowledge questions, seven out of ten attitude questions, and two out of five practice questions."
