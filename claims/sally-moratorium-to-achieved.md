## Evidence

### Paul W Irving 2015

Paul W Irving, Eleanor C Sayre. (2015). Identity statuses in upper-division physics students. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1505.07801](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1505.07801)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study analysis of one student interviewed three times across her academic career, with statements about her status mapped over time and triangulated with AdLab observational data. The analysis reports the switch from moratorium to identity achieved for both identities after she declared the major.

> "After exploring her identity as a physicist through legitimate peripheral practice in both communities of practice Sally then decided to declare a major in physics and as a result her subject-specific identity and her professional identity switch to an identity achieved status."
