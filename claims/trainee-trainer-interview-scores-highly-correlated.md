# Non-anonymised interview feedback from trainees and their trainers on the same training posts is highly correlated (Pearson's R = 0.968)

## Evidence

### Bizrah M 2013

Bizrah M, Mukhtar; Iacoponi, Eduardo; Parker, Elizabeth; Rymer, Janice; Iversen, Amy; Wessely, Simon. (2013). A novel method of assessing quality of postgraduate psychiatry training: experiences from a large training programme. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-85

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional interview study of the Maudsley psychiatry training scheme: 99 trainees and 109 trainers interviewed separately about the same posts. The article reports "Pearson's R = 0.968, p< 0.001" and adds that the paired-samples t-test difference was not significant (p = 0.154).

> "There is a highly significant correlation between the total average scores of trainees and trainers for the interview domains displayed in Figure 2 (Pearson's R = 0.968, p< 0.001)."
