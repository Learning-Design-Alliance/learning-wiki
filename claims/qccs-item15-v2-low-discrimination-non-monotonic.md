`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Large-scale pilot administration of QCCS v2.0 collected N=621 viable student responses across 43 courses at 40 unique US institutions. The item's discrimination was ρ*=0.14(4), below the authors' target, "unacceptably low discrimination" despite excellent difficulty.

> "Despite the revisions above and an excellent item difficulty, this item on v2.0 had unacceptably low discrimination (ρ∗ = 0.14(4))."
