Adrian Nugraha Utama, Jianwei Lee, and Mathias Alexander Seidler. (2020). A Hands-On Quantum Cryptography Workshop For Pre-University Students. arXiv:1911.02565v2 [physics.ed-ph]. [doi:10.1119/10.0001895](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0001895)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-workshop surveys aggregated from 59 pre-university students over four runs in Jun 2018 and Jun 2019 report the enjoyment mean "2.67± 0.57" and difficulty mean "0.04± 0.33". The article states students generally liked the workshop and could apply the concepts taught.
