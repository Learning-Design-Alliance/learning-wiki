## Evidence

### Jang Kyeongmin 2025

Jang Kyeongmin. (2025). Effect of Repeated Education on ICU Nurses' Knowledge and Performance in MDRO Infection Control: A Pretest–Posttest Study. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70123

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on 43 ICU nurses' knowledge scores (26-item tool, KR-20 = 0.82) across T0, T1 and T2. The article reports "a large effect size (η 2 = 0.388)" and Bonferroni-adjusted gains from T0 to T1 (MD = 2.19, p < 0.001) and T0 to T2 (MD = 1.81, p < 0.001).

> "A repeated measures ANOVA indicated a significant main effect of time on knowledge scores, F(2, 84) = 26.61, p < 0.001, with a large effect size (η 2 = 0.388)."

### Jang Kyeongmin 2025 (2)

Jang Kyeongmin. (2025). Effect of Repeated Education on ICU Nurses' Knowledge and Performance in MDRO Infection Control: A Pretest–Posttest Study. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70123

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparison in the same repeated measures ANOVA showed no significant difference between 2 weeks and 3 months post-intervention; the article interprets this as gains "maintained over time". Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "However, no statistically significant difference was found between T1 and T2 (MD = −0.37, SE = 0.32, p = 0.766), indicating that the gains in knowledge were maintained over time."
