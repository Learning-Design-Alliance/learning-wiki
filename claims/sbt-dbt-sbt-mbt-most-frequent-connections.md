`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Frequency analysis of connection types in rubric-coded CPDEs from the 14 teams, averaged within performance categories; the article reports SBT–DBT occurring eight times among high- and low-performing teams and five times among mid-performing teams, with SBT–MBT at 11, 7, and 7 respectively.

> "Across high-, mid-, and low-performing teams, students most frequently integrated Science-Based Thinking with Design-Based Thinking (SBT–DBT) and Science-Based Thinking with Math-Based Thinking (SBT–MBT). SBT–DBT connections occurred eight times among high-and low-performing teams, and five times among mid-performing teams."
