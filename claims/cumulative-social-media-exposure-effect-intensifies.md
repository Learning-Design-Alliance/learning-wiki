### Gui Marco 2026

Gui Marco, Respi Chiara, Abbiati Giovanni, Paladini Cristiana, Ercolanoni Sofia, Milzani Francesca, Pirola Tiziana, Vezzoli Giovanni. (2026). Longitudinal effect of early social media use on standardized learning outcomes during school career. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-026-02522-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal DiD results across the four matched adoption groups show graded effects (grade-6 adopters most affected, grade-8 least), with effects intensifying by grade 10. The authors note this challenges the belief that delayed access later produces catch-up penalties once adopters gain access. No single effect size is printed in this sentence.

> "Moreover, the adverse impact not only persists in grade 10, but intensifies—particularly in mathematics and among those exposed before grade 8—despite the fact that most late adopters had become social media users by grade 10."
