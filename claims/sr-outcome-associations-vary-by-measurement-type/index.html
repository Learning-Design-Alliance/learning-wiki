The current review reports, citing Robson et al. (2020), a meta-analytic review of 150 studies identifying 67 measurement tools, that "direct assessments showing the strongest associations, followed by teacher reports, and then parent reports." The reviewed study's design is not independently verified here.

"The review found that the strength of associations between self-regulation and developmental outcomes varied by measurement type, with direct assessments showing the strongest associations, followed by teacher reports, and then parent reports."
