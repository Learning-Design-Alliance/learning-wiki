## Evidence

### Ronimus 2019

Ronimus, M., Eklund, K., Pesu, L., & Lyytinen, H. (2019). Supporting struggling readers with digital game-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev, 67, 639–663. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09658-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on the word reading composite in an RCT with 17 GL and 20 control second graders. The time × group interaction was significant (F(1, 35) = 5.46, p = .025, "the time × group interaction" significant), and an independent samples t test on difference scores showed "the training group developed faster than the control group t(35) = 2.37, p < .05".

> "the main effect of time was significant, F(1, 35) = 122.03, p < .001, /u1D7022 p = .78 , as was the time × group interaction, F(1, 35) = 5.46, p = .025, /u1D7022 p = .14"
