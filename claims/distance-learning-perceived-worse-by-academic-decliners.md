`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey ratings on a 0–4 scale of educational reorganization impacts, compared between decliners and non-decliners (Table 4); all seven items reached p < 0.001, including focus difficulty (mean 2.90 vs. 2.15).

> "Specifically, it was found that distance/mixed exams negatively affected academic performance more in students who reported a decline in academic performance (mean: 2.00 vs. 0.93, p < 0.001). In addition, students with a decline in academic performance were more likely to report that distance/mixed learning required more study hours (mean: 2.04 vs. 1.27, p < 0.001) and made it more difficult to obtain insights (mean: 2.15 vs. 1.52, p < 0.001) and to focus during the lectures (mean: 2.90 vs. 2.15, p < 0.001)."
