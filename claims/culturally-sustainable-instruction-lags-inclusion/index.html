Morgan 2024¶

Morgan, I., Gillispie, C., & Waller, A. (2024). Supporting Young Learners Through Family Engagement: Engaging First. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED658182

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey 2. 85% of parents and 85% of teachers said their school is welcoming to people from different cultures; 80% of teachers agreed their school regularly incorporates cultural diversity into school activities.

"most parents (80%) agree that the adults in their child’s school are respectful of their family’s customs and culture (this question was not included in the first survey), and that the school’s staff communicates well with people from their culture (79%)."

Morgan 2024 (2)¶

Morgan, I., Gillispie, C., & Waller, A. (2024). Supporting Young Learners Through Family Engagement: Engaging First. EdTrust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED658182

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey 2. About half of teachers (55%) and parents (46%) are concerned schools are not adequately incorporating students' cultures into the curriculum, and 63% of teachers worry they lack resources to accommodate students' customs and cultures.

"Specifically, one-fifth of parents (19%) and one-quarter of teachers (25%) disagreed that their school regularly connects students’ cultures and customs to what they are learning in the classroom."
