Evidence¶

Ganganath 2026¶

Ganganath, G., Bolonghege, P., Lyu, Q., Varakantham, P., & Kandappu, T. (2026). Curiosity as Linguistic Intervention: Using LLM Tutoring Dialogues to Influence Exploratory Learning Behavior. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22349

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Controlled 3×3×3 factorial study (§5.1) with 45 participants and 270 tutoring conversations comparing CURIOBOT vs Baseline across Claude, Gemini, and GPT. The article reports gains in "exploratory questioning (L1: +21–32%)" and productive struggle (L2: +44–47%); only p-values printed, no effect sizes.

"Across all three model families, CURIOBOTconsistently improves all learner-side dimensions ( L1–L4) relative to Baseline, with all differences significant at p <0.005."
