## Evidence

### Deravi Noosha D. 2026

Deravi Noosha D., Behrens Nathan, Warnock Brielle, Hunt Maya L., Stefanidis Dimitrios, Holmstrom Amy, Maxey Katie Stanton, Tarras Samantha, Ritter E. Matthew. (2026). Training benchmarks for the Fundamentals of Robotic Surgery virtual reality tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12957-5

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

One-sample Wilcoxon signed-rank tests comparing novice first-attempt scores to expert medians; r values ranged from 0.7 to 0.9. The article notes "scores for Puzzle Piece Dissection and Vessel Energy Dissection did overlap between novices and experts."

> "Differences in total score between novices’ first attempts and the expert-derived median were statistically discernible (p < 0.05) with a large effect size for all tasks (r > 0.08)."
