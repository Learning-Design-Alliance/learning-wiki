## Evidence

### Kalfsvel L 2022

Kalfsvel L, Versmissen J, van Doorn A, van den Broek W, van der Kuy H, van Rosse F. (2022). Better performance of medical students on pharmacotherapy knowledge and skills tests is associated with practising with e‐learning program P‐scribe. Br J Clin Pharmacol. https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.15077

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective analysis of P-scribe data-tracking logs for 270 medical students starting the 2017–2018 master's curriculum at Erasmus MC. The study reports a statistically significant difference in e-learning time favouring first-time passers, quoting "a mean practice time of five more hours (300 minutes)". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "students who passed their Dutch National Pharmacotherapy Assessment first time had a mean practice time of five more hours (300 minutes) than students who did not pass this assessment first time (P < .05; 95% CI of difference [3.4–6.6])"
