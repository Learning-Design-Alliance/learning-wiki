# Stand-alone TK classrooms increased to 77% of TK classrooms in 2023–24 while TK–kindergarten combination classes fell to 20%

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Between 2022–23 and 2023–24, the share of stand-alone TK classrooms rose from 69% to 77%, and TK–kindergarten combination classes fell from 28% to 20%. [→ Wang 2025](#wang-2025)

## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, V., Melnick, H., Leung-Gagné, M., Parker, S., & Wechsler, M. (2025). California's universal prekindergarten implementation progress, 2023–24. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/600.551

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of LEAs covering 9,520 TK classrooms statewide, reported in the TK Service Delivery Models section and Figure 2. The report states LEAs "offered a higher percentage of stand-alone TK classrooms and a lower percentage of TK–kindergarten combination classes."

> "In 2023–24, over three fourths (77%) of TK classrooms in the state were TK stand-alone classes, compared to 69% in 2022–23. fewer TK classrooms were TK–kindergarten combination classes, 20% in 2023–24, compared to 28% in 2022–23."
