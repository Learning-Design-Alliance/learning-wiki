Evidence¶

Hamidreza Fatemipour & Shiva Hemmati 2015¶

Hamidreza Fatemipour & Shiva Hemmati. (2015). Impact of Consciousness-Raising Activities on Young English Language Learners’ Grammar Performance. English Language Teaching, 8(9). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n9p1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study with 30 experimental and 30 control participants (independent samples t test on posttest). Posttest means were 42.27 (experimental) and 33.60 (control); the article reports "a significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups on grammar posttest." No effect size is printed.

"the probability associated with the t-observed value (0.00) was lower than the significant level of (0.05). Based on these results, it can be concluded that there was a significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups on grammar posttest."
