Kraus 2016¶

Kraus, S. E. C., & Cleveland, R. E. (2016). The Effects of a Cross-age Peer Mentoring Program on School Connectedness with Rural Populations. Georgia School Counselor Association Journal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1140852

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Narrative literature review attributing this finding to the CDC (2009); the article also cites Resnick et al. (1997) that youth feeling connected to school were less likely to engage in risky behaviors. No independent analysis is reported here.
