`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive recruitment figures reported in the discussion of the multicenter trial. The article reports "only 88 out of 532 parents" showed interest and 16 refused over screen-time concerns; the authors call the reluctance a potentially culturally specific attitude.

> "only 88 out of 532 parents of preterm children who fulfilled the inclusion criteria showed interest to participate in a computerized training. Of these 88, however, 16 voiced critical concerns about increased daily screen time as part of the intervention and refused to participate."
