### Daane 2014

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis across multiple episodes and scenarios (hot plate, ball pushed into water, light bulb, braking) in courses for elementary and secondary teachers; teachers generalize that all energy ultimately becomes thermal.
