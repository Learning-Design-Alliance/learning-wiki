## Evidence

### Hough J 2019

Hough J, Levan D, Steele M, Kelly K, Dalton M. (2019). Simulation-based education improves student self-efficacy in physiotherapy assessment and management of paediatric patients. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1894-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of baseline (pre-session) scores across the three scenarios in the combined cohort; "self-efficacy scores returned to baseline levels" at each new scenario. Baseline differences between scenarios were non-significant (p > 0.07).

> "Although self-efficacy improved from the start to the end of each SBE scenario, there was no improvement in pre self-efficacy scores over the course of the entire academic unit. At the start of each new SBE scenario, self-efficacy scores returned to baseline levels."
