## Evidence

### Lichtenfeld 2022

Lichtenfeld, S., Pekrun, R., Marsh, H. W., Nett, U., & Reiss, K. (2022). Achievement Emotions and Elementary School Children’s Academic Performance: Longitudinal Models of Developmental Ordering. Journal of Educational Psychology, in press. https://osf.io/ywt8q/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Reciprocal effects models for learning-related boredom and anxiety with grades showed significant negative cross-lagged effects in both directions while controlling for gender and SES; Year 2 latent correlations were rs = -.28 and -.38, ps < .001. No standardized cross-lagged coefficients are printed in the quoted text.

> "In line with Hypothesis 3, boredom and anxiety were significant negative predictors of subsequent grades, and grades were significant negative predictors of subsequent anxiety and boredom."
