q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review reporting study characteristics: 90 included qualitative and mixed-methods studies, with the review noting "more than half (n=70) appearing from 2019 onwards" and representation across 26 countries. Sample sizes ranged from one to 3,665 participants (median = 29).

"The included studies were published between 2014 and 2025, with more than half (n=70) appearing from 2019 onwards. Of the 90 studies, 26 countries were represented across Europe (n = 34), North America (n = 23), Oceania (n = 13)"
