## Evidence

### Shirazi 2019

Shirazi, F., & Heidari, S. (2019). The relationship between critical thinking skills and learning styles and academic achievement of nursing students. The Journal of Nursing Research, 27(4), e38. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000307

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 139 nursing students; between-group comparison by educational level. The article reports a significant relationship (p = .04) but no effect size for this finding.

> "However, a significant relationship was found between the total score of critical thinking and educational level. Therefore, the senior students in this study earned a higher mean score for critical thinking than their lower-grade peers (p = .04; Table 2)."
