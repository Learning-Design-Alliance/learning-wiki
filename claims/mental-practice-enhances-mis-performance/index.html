Evidence¶

Vajsbaher Tina 2018¶

Vajsbaher Tina, Schultheis Holger, Francis Nader K. (2018). Spatial cognition in minimally invasive surgery: a systematic review. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-018-0416-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis of three mental practice studies (Komesu et al. 2009; Arora et al. 2009; Louridas et al. 2014). One reported residents practicing mental imagery pre-operatively "performed considerably better on a real cystoscopy procedure" than controls; the other two found improved imagery, skill acquisition and stress response.

"All three studies who aimed to explore the impact of mental practice on MIS performance, both in simulated and real-life environment, found mental practice and its form of mental imagery to enhance surgical performance if used pre-operatively"
