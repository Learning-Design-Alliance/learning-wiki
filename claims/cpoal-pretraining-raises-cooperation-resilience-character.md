### Wang Tianlin 2025

Wang Tianlin, Liang Jinmei, Chen Li, Li Shu, Zhu Man, Cai Yun, Chen Mengli. (2025). Enhancing professional adaptability and clinical soft skills of new clinical pharmacists through a pre-training model in China: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07951-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled trial of 20 newly recruited pharmacists (10 per group) stratified by academic qualification; between-group comparisons used independent samples t-tests on a 5-point Likert questionnaire. Table 2 shows post-training differences of 3.00 (p = 0.00) for cooperative ability, 3.89 (p = 0.01) for resilience, and 2.11 (p = 0.03) for good character; "individuals who had undergone career-adapted learning displayed significantly higher scores".
