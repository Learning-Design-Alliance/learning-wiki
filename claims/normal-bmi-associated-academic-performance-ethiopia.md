## Evidence

### Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet 2025

Melaku Ermiyas Endewunet, Tefera Aklile Semu. (2025). Impact of Nutrition on Academic Performance of First-cycle Primary School Children in Amhara Region, Ethiopia: A Multicenter Cross-Sectional Study. Ethiopian Journal of Health Sciences. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13077153/

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study with clinical nutritional assessment (weight and height measured with standardized instruments); multivariable logistic regression. Normal-BMI students showed "approximately four times higher academic performance" with adjusted odds ratio AOR = 4.115393 (CI = 3.0414–5.568, p=0.0001).

> "Students who had a normal BMI showed approximately four times higher academic performance compared to those students with underweight BMI (AOR=4.115393, CI=3.0414-5.568)."
