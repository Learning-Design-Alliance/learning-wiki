### Maria Araceli Ruiz-Primo 1993

Maria Araceli Ruiz-Primo, Gail P. Baxter, and Richard J. Shavelson. (1993). On the Stability of Performance Assessments. Journal of Educational Measurement, 30(1), 41-53. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17453984

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Person x occasion generalizability study of 29 seventh-grade students completing three hands-on science investigations on two occasions separated by 5 months. The average G coefficient for relative decisions was 0.52 for observed investigations and 0.50 for notebooks, described as "roughly of the same magnitude".

> "For both observed investigations and their notebook surrogates, G coefficients were roughly of the same magnitude (Table 1); the average over the three investigations was 0.52 and 0.50, respectively."
