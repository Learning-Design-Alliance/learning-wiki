## Evidence

### Hyde Sarah 2018

Hyde Sarah, Hannigan Ailish, Dornan Tim, McGrath Deirdre. (2018). Medical school clinical placements – the optimal method for assessing the clinical educational environment from a graduate entry perspective. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1113-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional anonymous online survey of graduate entry medical students on clinical placement; Pearson's correlation between total DREEM and total MCPI scores for students who answered all questions (n = 154). The study found a "strong, positive correlation" with r = 0.71, p < 0.001. No effect size beyond r is printed.
