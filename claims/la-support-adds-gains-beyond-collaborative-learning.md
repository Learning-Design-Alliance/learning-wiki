## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For average-performing students in average-performing classes, predicted posttest scores were 56.8% in LA-supported courses versus 53.2% in collaborative courses without LAs and 49.6% in traditional courses. [→ Xochith Herrera 2019](#xochith-herrera-2019)

## Evidence

### Xochith Herrera 2019

Xochith Herrera, Jayson Nissen, and Ben Van Dusen. (2019). Student Outcomes Across Collaborative-Learning Environments. arXiv:1808.07076. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.herrera](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.herrera)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear model analysis (Model 2) of LASSO database data from first-semester physics courses predicted "the posttest scores for average students in traditional (49.6%), collaborative learning (53.2%), and LA-supported (56.8%) courses", with student and class mean pretest scores included as predictors.

> "It allows us to predict the the posttest scores for average students in traditional (49.6%), collaborative learning (53.2%), and LA-supported (56.8%) courses (Figure 1)."
