## Evidence

### Michael B. Lund 2019

Michael B. Lund, Savannah R. Jacklin, and David Ciardi. (2019). Enabling Terminal Master's Degrees as a Step Towards a Ph.D. Astro2020 White Paper. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1909.02103](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1909.02103)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The white paper reports, citing Lange (2006) and Clune et al. (2001), that underrepresented groups are more likely to follow the master's-first path; the reviewed studies' designs are not described in the paper.

> "This number undergoes a statistically signiﬁcant rise to 18% for Native American and African American students (Lange, 2006). Women completing bachelor's degrees in mathematics and non-biological sciences and then enrolling in graduate education were also less likely to eventually be enrolled in Ph.D. programs (Clune et al., 2001), with 31% of men reaching a Ph.D. program compared to only 17% of women."
