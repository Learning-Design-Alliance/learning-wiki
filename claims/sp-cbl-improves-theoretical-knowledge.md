## Evidence

### Yu Zizhong 2025

Yu Zizhong, Zhao Zhao, Chen Xinyue, Shi Jiayi, Liang Yiting, Zhang Xicheng, Qu Mengjiao, Huang Meiyue, Li Panpan, Li Dan, Li Man, He Jinbo. (2025). Effects of standardised patients (SP) combined with case-based learning (CBL) in Chinese clinical education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-095705

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 27 RCTs (n=2419) using a random effects model due to considerable heterogeneity (I2=93.9%). The article reports a mean difference of 5.91 points on a 100-point scale, an unstandardised difference rather than a standardised effect size.

> "The meta-analysis revealed that, compared with traditional teaching methods, the combination of SP and CBL resulted in significantly greater theoretical knowledge achievements (MD 5.91, 95% CI 4.63 to 7.18, p<0.001)"
