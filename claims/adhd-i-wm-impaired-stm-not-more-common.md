Prevalence and diagnostic validity of motivational impairments and deficits in visuospatial short-term memory and working memory in ADHD subtypes. Group comparison of 27 children with ADHD-I (restrictive subtype) against 62 typically developing controls on visuospatial WM and STM tasks. The abstract states "In ADHD-I, only WM impairments, not STM impairments, were more prevalent than in controls."
