Wang 2026¶

Wang, S. and Zhang, H. (2026). Pedagogical partnerships with generative AI in higher education: how dual cognitive pathways paradoxically enable transformative learning. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00585-x

PLS-SEM path analysis in the 912-student survey. Delegation of cognitive tasks to GenAI positively predicted "Transformative Learning Experience", opposite to the theory-derived negative hypothesis.

"Contrary to the hypothesized negative relationship, Cognitive Offloading also had a significant positive effect on Transformative Learning Experience (β = 0.333, p < 0.001, f2 = 0.140), thus not supporting Hypothesis 2b"
