## Evidence

### Escribano Nuria 2023

Escribano Nuria, Belliard Virginia, Baracco Bruno, Da Silva Dayana, Ceballos Laura, Fuentes M. Victoria. (2023). Rubric vs. numeric rating scale: agreement among evaluators on endodontic treatments performed by dental students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04187-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null secondary analysis of the 42 assessed portfolios, where evaluators had jointly classified molar difficulty (minimal, moderate, high) and recorded instrumentation technique before scoring. The study reports ratings were "not influenced by the difficulty of the treatment nor the instrumentation technique (p > 0.05)".

> "One-way ANOVA analysis showed that the ratings of each evaluator were not influenced by the difficulty of the treatment nor the instrumentation technique (p > 0.05), irrespective of the evaluation method used, and therefore, they were not considered in the subsequent analyses."
