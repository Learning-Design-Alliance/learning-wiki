## Evidence

### Kittle 2024

Kittle, J. M., Amendum, S. J., & Budde, C. M. (2024). What Does Research Say About the Science of Reading for K-5 Multilingual Learners? A Systematic Review of Systematic Reviews. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09942-6

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of eight independent correlations from eight studies covering 1,039 children, most with English as the L2. The review reports "a moderate to strong positive linear relationship" with r = 0.46, p < 0.001, and that age had a significant impact, the correlation increasing as children become older.

> "A meta-analysis by Melby-Lervåg and Lervåg (2011) found a moderate to strong positive linear relationship between L2 oral language and L2 reading comprehension (r = 0.46, p < 0.001)."
