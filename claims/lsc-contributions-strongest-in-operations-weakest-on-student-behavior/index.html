Evidence¶

Sebring 1995¶

Sebring, P. B., Bryk, A. S., Easton, J. Q., Luppescu, S., Thum, Y. M., Lopez, W. A., & Smith, B. (1995). Charting Reform: Chicago Teachers Take Stock. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-chicago-teachers-take-stock

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Teachers rated whether the LSC helped, hindered, or made no contribution in eight areas; a summary scale found 8 percent extensive, 46 percent significant, 23 percent limited, and about a quarter no contribution. The authors note direct impact on instruction and classroom behavior is less common.

"Approximately half report that the LSC has helped curriculum and instruction, but only about one-third see the LSC as a positive influence on student behavior. Almost 60 percent of the teachers say that the LSC has made no contribution in this last area."
