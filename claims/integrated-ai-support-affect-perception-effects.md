## Evidence

### Heng Zhang 2025

Heng Zhang, Yuhan Liu, Meilin Jiang, Juanjuan Chen, Minhong Wang, Fred Paas. (2025). Emotional Artificial Intelligence in Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10086-4

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of Category 1 studies on affective experience, k = 11. The pooled "g = 0.42" with CI [0.06, 0.78] is near-medium; individual effects ranged from −0.28 to 2.20, with negative effects in three studies.

> "The weighted mean ES ( g) for students’ affective experience is 0.42 (95% CI [0.06, 0.78], k = 11, τ² = 0.27, I² = 45.1%), suggesting a near-medium effect, with individual ESs ranging from − 0.28 to 2.20."

### Heng Zhang 2025 (2)

Heng Zhang, Yuhan Liu, Meilin Jiang, Juanjuan Chen, Minhong Wang, Fred Paas. (2025). Emotional Artificial Intelligence in Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10086-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of Category 1 studies on perception, k = 5. The pooled "g = 0.18" has a confidence interval spanning zero, so the effect on perception was not statistically significant; heterogeneity was high (I² = 63.7%).

> "The overall effect size is very small ( g = 0.18, 95% CI [−0.41, 0.77], k = 5 ,  τ² = 0.41, I² = 63.7%), with individual ESs ranging from − 0.62 to 0.71."
