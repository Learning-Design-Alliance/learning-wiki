## Evidence

### Saedon H 2012

Saedon H, Salleh S, Balakrishnan A, Imray CHE, Saedon M. (2012). The role of feedback in improving the effectiveness of workplace based assessments: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education, 12, 25. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3432628/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review reporting a postal questionnaire to trainees and trainers comparing perceptions of two MSF tools (attributed to Burford et al). The review reports "small but significant preferences for textual feedback" with TAB perceived as more useful than mini-PAT.

> "There were small but significant preferences for textual feedback, shown by the team assessment of behaviour (TAB), which has large free-text boxes, being perceived as more useful than the mini-PAT, which has a numerical scale and only a small space for comments."
