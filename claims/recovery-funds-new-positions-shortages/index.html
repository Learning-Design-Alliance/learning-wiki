Evidence¶

Carver-Thomas 2022¶

Carver-Thomas, D., Burns, D., Leung, M., & Ondrasek, N. (2022). Teacher shortages during the pandemic: How California districts are responding. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/899.809

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey finding from the report's section on district responses: "9 out of 12" districts used recovery funds to recruit and retain teachers. The report also describes Los Angeles USD's expanded 4x4 block scheduling requiring about 25% more teachers, and one district whose annual hires rose from around 140 to 375.

"Most districts (9 out of 12) reported using recovery funds specifically to recruit and retain teachers, as allowed under the law."
