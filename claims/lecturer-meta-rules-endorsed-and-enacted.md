## Evidence

### Karavi 2022

Karavi, Mali, & Avraamidou. (2022). Commognition as an approach to studying proof teaching in university mathematics lectures. EURASIA Journal of Mathematics, Science and Technology Education. https://doi.org/10.29333/ejmste/12173

`q2 · i?`

The review reports, citing Viirman (2021), a re-analysis of earlier lecture data using word searches for normative aspects, categorization of meta-rules, and the constant comparative method. The review states the identification of meta-rules "facilitated the researcher to model mathematical reasoning and behavior".

> "A number of meta-rules emerged from the analysis within the mathematical discourse that were either endorsed (explicitly displayed in the lecturers' discourse) or enacted by the lecturers (implied by their discursive activity)."
