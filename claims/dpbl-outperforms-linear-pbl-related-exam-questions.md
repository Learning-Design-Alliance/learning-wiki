## Evidence

### Poulton T 2014

Poulton T, Ellaway RH, Round J, Jivram T, Kavia S, Hilton S. (2014). Exploring the Efficacy of Replacing Linear Paper-Based Patient Cases in Problem-Based Learning With Dynamic Web-Based Virtual Patients: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.3748

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in crossover design at St George's, University of London with 81 graduate-entry year-2 learners randomized to 11 tutorial groups in two alternating cohorts; 80 sat the end-of-module short-answer exam. The Results report that "D-PBL groups performed better than linear PBL groups on questions related to D-PBL with the difference being statistically significant for all questions." Mann-Whitney U tests were used due to nonnormal distributions.
