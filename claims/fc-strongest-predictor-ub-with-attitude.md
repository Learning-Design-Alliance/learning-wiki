## Evidence

### Or 2023

Or, C. (2023). The role of attitude in the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology: A meta-analytic structural equation modelling study. International Journal of Technology in Education and Science (IJTES), 7(4), 552-570. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijtes.504

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

OSMASEM path analysis of the 21-study pooled correlations. In Model 1, "FC emerged as the strongest predictor of UB (β= .376; p< .001)"; in Model 2 FC predicted UB at β= .476 and in Model 3 at β= .367, exceeding BI's effect on UB in each model.

> "However, in Model 1, FC emerged as the strongest predictor of UB (β= .376; p< .001), while both attitude (β= .261; p< .001) and BI (β= .204; p< .001) had significant positive effects on UB."
