# County-administered QRIS in California is voluntary, uneven, and destabilized by time-limited categorical funding

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Counties administer QRIS locally with significant latitude, participation is voluntary, and unstable categorical funding makes it difficult for counties to plan and provide consistent provider support. [→ Melnick 2018](#melnick-2018)

## Evidence

### Melnick 2018

Melnick, H., Meloy, B., Gardner, M., Wechsler, M., & Maier, A. (2018). Building an early learning system that works: Next steps for California. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/early-learning-ca

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Report's quality-improvement funding analysis finds QRIS supported through time-limited categorical grants rolled out unevenly across the state (Table 3), limiting which providers can access QRIS and undermining consistent county support for providers.

> "Quality improvement funding for ECE has been administered through time-limited categorical grants. Categorical funding impacts which providers have access to QRIS. Unstable and uncoordinated state funding makes it difficult for counties to plan for the future of QRIS efforts and to provide consistent support for providers."
