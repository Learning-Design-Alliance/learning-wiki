## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Microteaching topped the most-useful lists in 2013–2014 and received the highest Likert usefulness ratings in 2015 (M = 4.38, SD = 1.07) and 2016 (M = 4.32, SD = 0.72). [→ Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018](#emily-alicea-munoz-2018)

## Evidence

### Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2018

Emily Alicea-Muñoz, Joan Espar Masip, Carol Subiño Sullivan, and Michael F. Schatz. (2018). Assessing a GTA professional development program. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.003)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Final Survey Likert usefulness ratings in 2015 and 2016 plus top-3 free-response rankings in 2013–2014; the 2015 group rated Microteaching "M = 4 .38, SD = 1.07" and the 2016 group rated it M = 4.32, SD = 0.72, both highest among activities.

> "The 2015 group indicated Microteaching as being the most useful course activity ( M = 4 .38, SD = 1.07), followed by Individual Classroom Observations (M = 3 .79, SD = 1 .29) and Teaching Physics (M = 3 .76, SD = 1.06)"
