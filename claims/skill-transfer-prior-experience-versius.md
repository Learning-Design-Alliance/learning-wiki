Authors' interpretation of the prospective four-surgeon cohort: two surgeons with >500 Da Vinci RPs and two with >100 laparoscopic RPs but no robotic experience, all proctored. The article states the findings "demonstrate a transfer of skills from prior laparoscopic and robotic surgery experience".

> "The findings demonstrate a transfer of skills from prior laparoscopic and robotic surgery experience, enabling a rapid adaptation to the new system. Furthermore, structured proctoring appears to contribute to reduced setup times and facilitates the achievement of procedural expertise in <40 cases."
