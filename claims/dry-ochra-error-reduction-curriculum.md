## Evidence

### Boal Matthew W. E. 2024

Boal Matthew W. E., Afzal Asma, Gorard Jack, Shah Aishwarya, Tesfai Freweini, Ghamrawi Walaa, Tutton Matthew, Ahmad Jawad, Selvasekar Chelliah, Khan Jim, Francis Nader K. (2024). Development and evaluation of a societal core robotic surgery accreditation curriculum for the UK. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02062-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Error-based assessment via blinded, retrospective video analysis using the bespoke OCHRA tool applied to dry-lab tasks. Errors fell significantly from attempt 1 to attempt 2, with "a mean difference of –30.57" (p < 0.001); no effect size is printed.

> "Dry tasks’ OCHRA assessments demonstrated a significant reduction in participants’ (n = 35) errors with a mean difference of –30.57 (SD 31.36, 95% CI – 41.34 to – 19.80, p < 0.001)."
