## Evidence

### Manav Esra Yildirim 2025

Manav Esra Yildirim, Akbiyik Sema Yazici, Ceylan Aydan Büşra, Çakiroğlu Ayfer Ezgi Yilmaz, Tuncer Duygu. (2025). Effects of virtual reality and layered tooth model training on manual dexterity in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07622-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise Fisher-Freeman-Halton comparisons of depth of cavity (Table 2) showed the control group scored significantly worse than each of the three simulation groups, with the article reporting large effect sizes for these contrasts.

> "The Control Group differed significantly from each of the experimental groups (The layered model + VR, The layered model, and VR) (p < 0.001, p = 0.001, and p = 0.019, respectively)."

### Manav Esra Yildirim 2025 (2)

Manav Esra Yildirim, Akbiyik Sema Yazici, Ceylan Aydan Büşra, Çakiroğlu Ayfer Ezgi Yilmaz, Tuncer Duygu. (2025). Effects of virtual reality and layered tooth model training on manual dexterity in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07622-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same pairwise analysis found no statistically significant depth differences among the layered model + VR, layered model-only, and VR-only groups; equivalence was not tested, only non-significance reported.

> "No significant differences were observed between The layered model + VR, The layered model, and VR groups (p = 0.524, p = 0.229, and p = 0.761)."
