## Evidence

### Cacey S. Stevens 2015

Cacey S. Stevens, Michael Marder, and Sidney R. Nagel. (2015). Patterns in Illinois Educational School Data. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1502.02057](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1502.02057)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of 2013 Illinois State Board of Education exam data for schools outside Chicago, visualized in Fig. 3 and fitted by simple regressions (Table II). The authors note the "linear relationship with poverty concentration" and report slopes such as f = -0.56ci + 83.7 for grade 3 and f = -0.67ci + 76.5 for grade 11, with standard deviations near 11-12 points.

> "Since passing fractions of Illinois schools outside of Chicago clearly show a linear relationship with poverty concentration, we can perform simple regressions to obtain the best ﬁt line fi to describe these patterns."
