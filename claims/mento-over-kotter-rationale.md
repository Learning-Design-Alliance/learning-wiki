## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The authors identify four drawbacks of Kotter's model that Mento's model addresses: stakeholder preparation (Step 6), a Leader Team rather than top-down leadership, progress measurement (Step 11), and explicit handling of resistance (Steps 6 and 10). The authors' comparative analysis of change models, arguing from the models' architectures rather than data. They note Kotter's model "doesn’t delegate a step towards preparing the recipients of change", which Mento's Step 6 addresses, alongside leadership, measurement and resistance advantages.
