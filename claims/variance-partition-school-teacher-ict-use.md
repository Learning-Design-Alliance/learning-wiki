# About 9% of variance in classroom ICT use lies between schools, with the remainder between teachers within schools

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 9.16% of total variance in teachers' classroom ICT use is attributable to differences between schools and 90.83% to differences between teachers within schools. [→ Tondeur 2008](#tondeur-2008)

## Evidence

### Tondeur 2008

Tondeur, J., van Keer, H., van Braak, J., & Valcke, M. (2008). ICT integration in the classroom: Challenging the potential of a school policy. Computers & Education, 51, 212-223. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2007.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Two-level unconditional null model from the multilevel analysis of 574 teachers in 53 schools; both variance components were significantly different from zero (school-level p = 0.013; teacher-level p < 0.000), justifying the multilevel approach.

> "Respectively 9.16% of the total variance in teachers’ use of ICT in the classroom is related to differences between schools, while the remaining part of 90.83% of the variance can be attributed to diﬀerences between teachers within schools."
