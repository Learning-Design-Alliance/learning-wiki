# Training with a simulated patient significantly improves 1-year retention of first aid knowledge, but not self-efficacy, compared with control training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The increase in first aid knowledge at 1 year compared with baseline was statistically significantly higher in the simulated patient group (median 7, 6–8) than the control group (median 6, 5–7; P = 0.02). [→ Avau B 2022](#avau-b-2022)
`q3 i?` Self-efficacy retention at 1 year did not differ between groups (P = 0.96), with medians of 24 in both groups. [→ Avau B 2022 (2)](#avau-b-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Avau B 2022

Avau B, Vanhove A-C, Scheers H, Stroobants S, Lauwers K, Vandekerckhove P, De Buck E. (2022). Impact of the Use of Simulated Patients in Basic First Aid Training on Laypeople Knowledge, Skills, and Self-efficacy. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000657

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-year retention assessed in 237 complete cases (89 simulated patient, 148 control) via the same questionnaire before annual refresher training; the mixed model showed the increase versus baseline was "statistically significantly higher in the simulated patient group" (P = 0.02). No effect size printed.

> "Knowledge scores at T2 remained higher than at T0, with a median score of 6 (5–7) in the control group, compared with 7 (6–8) in the simulated patient group"

### Avau B 2022 (2)

Avau B, Vanhove A-C, Scheers H, Stroobants S, Lauwers K, Vandekerckhove P, De Buck E. (2022). Impact of the Use of Simulated Patients in Basic First Aid Training on Laypeople Knowledge, Skills, and Self-efficacy. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000657

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same 237 participants completed the self-efficacy questionnaire after 1 year; the article reports no influence of a simulated patient at T2 (P = 0.96), with identical medians of 24 in both groups.

> "Self-efficacy scores remained high in both groups at T2, with scores of 24 (23–26) in the simulated patient group and 24 (22–26) in the control group"
