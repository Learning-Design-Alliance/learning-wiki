### Alotaibi Madawi 2026

Alotaibi Madawi, Alghamdi Farah, Almousa Nourah, Almalkia Roaa, Alfaifi Reema, Alturki Hisaah, Alkahal Hathil, Asiri Ruba, Almuzaini Muzun, Alshahrani Ghaday, Alqabbani Samiah, Almuwais Afrah, Alahmari Wafa. (2026). Associations of physical activity, disordered eating, and depressive symptoms with academic performance among Saudi university students. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1769363

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the cross-sectional survey (N = 400) using the PHQ-9 (Cronbach's α = 0.85); moderate depression was the most common severity level (29.3%).

> "According to established clinical cutoffs for the PHQ-9, 50.7% of the sample fell into the category of significant depression, while 49.3% were classified as having non-significant depression"

### Alotaibi Madawi 2026 (2)

Alotaibi Madawi, Alghamdi Farah, Almousa Nourah, Almalkia Roaa, Alfaifi Reema, Alturki Hisaah, Alkahal Hathil, Asiri Ruba, Almuzaini Muzun, Alshahrani Ghaday, Alqabbani Samiah, Almuwais Afrah, Alahmari Wafa. (2026). Associations of physical activity, disordered eating, and depressive symptoms with academic performance among Saudi university students. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1769363

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression predicting continuous GPA found depression scores were not a significant predictor (B = −0.001, p = 0.882); no effect size is printed for this null result.

> "depression scores (B = −0.001, p = 0.882), and age (B = 0.006, p = 0.423) did not emerge as significant predictors"
