## Evidence

### Sabainah Oyebola Akinoso 2015

Sabainah Oyebola Akinoso. (2015). Teaching Mathematics in a Volatile, Uncertain, Complex and Ambiguous (VUCA) World: The Use of Concrete – Representational – Abstract Instructional Strategy. JISTE Vol. 19, No. 1, 2015. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1177074

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus ANCOVA interaction test in the quasi-experiment: Table 5 shows CRAIS means of 20.92 (male) and 21.72 (female) versus control means of 18.55 and 19.60, with the interaction reported as "not significant"; no effect size is printed.

> "From Tables 2 and 5 , the interaction effect of treatment and gender on students’ achievement in mathematics is not significant (F (1, 190) =.01; p < .05). Therefore, hypothesis 3 is rejected."
