## Evidence

### Jesper Bruun 2017

Jesper Bruun, Ida Viola Andersen. (2017). Network maps of student work with physics, other sciences, and math in an integrated science course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1708.01389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1708.01389)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Numerical network analysis of six observation-based networks from five course implementations (60 lessons total), partitioned with Infomap run 1000 times per implementation. Table II reports modularity from 0.5629 to 0.8011; the text states all are "comfortably above the 0.3 threshold".

> "All networks are characterized by high modularities ( Q > 0.5), which is comfortably above the 0.3 threshold for modularity. Thus, they are all modular (many of them very much so)."
