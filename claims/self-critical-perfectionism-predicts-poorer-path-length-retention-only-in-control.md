## Evidence

### Kleinrensink V. E. E. 2026

Kleinrensink V. E. E., Kranenburg L. W., Kleinrensink G. J. (2026) Taxing working memory during laparoscopic training: reducing the impact of perfectionistic concerns on skill learning. Surgical Endoscopy 40(6):4995–5002. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12739-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Simple linear regression within the control condition of this randomized experimental study (n = 27 control participants), from the Perfectionism and retention section. Findings show higher Self-Critical Perfectionism predicted poorer retention of movement efficiency, p = 0.042, R2 = 0.156, beta = 0.395.

> "In the control condition (no working-memory load), higher Self-Critical Perfectionism predicted poorer retention of movement efficiency, reflected by a higher path-length retention slope (B = 9.65, 95% CI [0.40, 18.90], t(25) = 2.15, p = 0.042, R2 = 0.156, β = 0.395)."

### Kleinrensink V. E. E. 2026 (2)

Kleinrensink V. E. E., Kranenburg L. W., Kleinrensink G. J. (2026) Taxing working memory during laparoscopic training: reducing the impact of perfectionistic concerns on skill learning. Surgical Endoscopy 40(6):4995–5002. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12739-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple linear regression within the working-memory load condition of the same randomized experimental study (n = 28 experimental participants), from the Perfectionism and retention section. Findings show neither perfectionism dimension was associated with retention; beta = 0.059 for Self-Critical Perfectionism path-length retention.

> "In the working-memory load condition, neither perfectionism dimension was associated with retention. Self-Critical Perfectionism was not associated with path-length retention (B = 1.80, 95% CI [-10.59, 14.20], t(26) = 0.30, p = 0.767, R2 = 0.003, β = 0.059)"

### Kleinrensink V. E. E. 2026 (3)

Kleinrensink V. E. E., Kranenburg L. W., Kleinrensink G. J. (2026) Taxing working memory during laparoscopic training: reducing the impact of perfectionistic concerns on skill learning. Surgical Endoscopy 40(6):4995–5002. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12739-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Formal interaction testing in additional models including condition and perfectionism main effects plus their interaction term, from the Perfectionism and retention section. Findings show the Condition by Self-Critical Perfectionism interaction did not reach statistical significance, p = 0.521, partial η2 = 0.008, and no main effect of Condition.

> "The Condition by Self-Critical Perfectionism interaction did not reach statistical significance, F(1, 51) = 0.42, p = 0.521, partial η2 = 0.008. We found no main effect of Condition, F(1, 51) = 0.12, p = 0.728, partial η2 = 0.002"
