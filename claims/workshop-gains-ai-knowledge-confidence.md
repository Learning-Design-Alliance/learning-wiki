### Christian Bergh 2026

Christian Bergh, Alexandra Vassar, Natasha Banks, Jessica Xu, and Jake Renzella. (2026). AI Literacy, Safety Awareness, and STEM Career Aspirations of Australian Secondary Students: Evaluating the Impact of Workshop Interventions. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29773-0_7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29773-0_7)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U tests on unpaired pre/post Likert responses (Knowledge about AI: n=200 pre, 158 post; U=7945.0, p<.001, z=-8.428, r=.445). The article reports "a medium and small effect size respectively" for knowledge and confidence.

> "Students reported a significant increase in general 'AI Knowledge' (r=.445) and 'AI Confidence' (r=.279) with a medium and small effect size respectively."

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`
