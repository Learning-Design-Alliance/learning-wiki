## Evidence

### Chyl 2021

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that "Children at risk of dyslexia aged 5–6 years hypoactivated the reading network at the early stage of reading training", with later bilateral temporo-parietal and frontal activity while controls employed left-lateralized regions; it also cites a compensatory shift to articulatory inferior frontal sites.

> "Children at risk of dyslexia aged 5–6 years hypoactivated the reading network at the early stage of reading training."

### Chyl 2021 (2)

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports ERP studies showing dyslexic readers lacked the expected reduction in N1 word sensitivity and showed reversed LPC trajectories after second grade, concluding "The results of these two studies are compatible with the notion of a protracted development of specialized responses in dyslexic readers."

> "The results of these two studies are compatible with the notion of a protracted development of specialized responses in dyslexic readers."
