## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wang Enna 2026

Wang Enna, Zhang Junjie. (2026). Disentangling the dynamic directional associations between positive and negative risk-taking behaviors and academic development in adolescents: a four-wave longitudinal analysis. BMC Psychology, 14, 1273. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05248-0

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged panel analyses (both models) in the four-wave sample of 672 adolescents. The article reports "negative risk-taking behaviors at T3 significantly negatively predicted subsequent academic engagement at T4 (β = − 0.13, p = 0.002)" and NRT at T2 negatively predicted achievement at T3 (β = −0.12, p = 0.002).
