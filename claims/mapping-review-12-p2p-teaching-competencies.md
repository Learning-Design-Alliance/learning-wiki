## Evidence

### Bernhard-Skala Christian 2026

Bernhard-Skala Christian, Steinweg Bernhard. (2026). Towards a standardized competency-profile for peer-to-peer-teachers in medical education - a systematic mapping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09997-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic mapping review applying PICOS and PRISMA, reducing search results "from 322 down to 18 peer-review articles for in-depth analysis"; contents of qualification measures were analyzed via inductive Qualitative Content Analysis. The twelve competencies include learning theories, course planning, teaching methodologies, small-group leadership, time management, and feedback provision.

> "The analysis brings three medical and 12 student P2P-teaching competencies to the fore. Together these competencies form a competency-profile for student P2P-teachers."
