## Evidence

### Grissom TE 2020

Grissom TE, Samet RE. (2020). The Anesthesiologist's Role in Teaching Airway Management to Nonanesthesiologists: Who, Where, and How. Advances in Anesthesia 38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aan.2020.08.002

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Observational performance assessment of an anesthesiology airway rotation for EM residents and critical care fellows, reported with Figs. 1 and 2 (P<.05 for week 1 versus week 4). Printed percentages are descriptive.

> "In an assessment of 4571 airway procedures by 96 airway trainees (EM residents and critical care fellows), trainee performance improved over the course of 1 month, with first-pass success increasing from 85% to 94% from week 1 to week 4"

### Grissom TE 2020 (2)

Grissom TE, Samet RE. (2020). The Anesthesiologist's Role in Teaching Airway Management to Nonanesthesiologists: Who, Where, and How. Advances in Anesthesia 38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aan.2020.08.002

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The same observational rotation assessment reported improved laryngoscopic view over 4 weeks, plotted in Fig. 2. Printed percentages are descriptive; no effect size is reported.

> "In addition, an improvement in the initial laryngoscopic grade of view improved over the course of the rotation (grade 1 view 61%–74%)"
