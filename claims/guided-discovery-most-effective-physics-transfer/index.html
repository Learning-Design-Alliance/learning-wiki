Akinbobola 2015¶

Akinbobola, Akinyemi Olufunminiyi. (2015). Enhancing Transfer of Knowledge in Physics through Effective Teaching Strategies. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.16. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1079935

Scheffe post hoc pairwise comparison of adjusted transfer-of-knowledge means from the MCA, following a significant ANCOVA main effect. The article reports mean differences of 6.83 (GDY-DEM), 14.23 (GDY-EXP) and 7.39 (DEM-EXP), each significant at the .05 level.
