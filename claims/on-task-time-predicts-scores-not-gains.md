### Ido Davidesco 2026 (2)

Ido Davidesco, Yushuang Liu, Kim Chaloner, Emma Laurent, Gabriella A. Ali, Sarah C. Creider, Sean Hughes, Laura Noejovich, Henry Valk, Dana Bevilacqua, David Poeppel & Suzanne Dikker. (2026). The dynamics of student engagement in cooperative science learning: a multimodal approach. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00457-z

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Same linear mixed-effects design predicting pre-to-post change from on-task percentage; the null result is printed with β = 0.30 and marginal R² = 0, a negligible printed association.

> "Time on-task did not predict pre-to-post changes in student test performance (β = 0.30, SE = 5.34, t(28) = 0.06, p = 0.96, marginal R² = 0). In other words, it seems that time on-task is associated with students ’ prior knowledge rather than learning gains."
