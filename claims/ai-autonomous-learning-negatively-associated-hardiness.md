Wang W and Zhang Q (2026). AI-assisted autonomous learning and reduced academic accomplishment in vocational higher education: the mediating role of hardiness. Front. Psychol. 17:1848291. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1848291

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of survey data from 1,264 vocational college students in China. The structural path from AI-assisted autonomous learning to hardiness was "significantly and negatively associated with hardiness" with β = −0.185, SE = 0.033, CR = −4.576, p < 0.001.
