## Evidence

### Baraza W. 2026

Baraza W., Staples G., Wells C., Barbier L., Tonga I., Singh P., Puttick M., Webster C. (2026). Artificially Intelligent Simulated Patients in Undergraduate Surgical Teaching: A Single Blinded Randomized Controlled Study. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70486

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-intervention-group comparison of AISP conversation assessments against vCSA scores in history, judgement and management domains (Table 2), by two-tailed t-test. The article reports "did not show a statistically significant difference"; no effect sizes were printed.

> "the clinical skills assessments of the AISP transcripts of student interactions did not show a statistically significant difference in their performances in comparison to their final vCSA scores in identical domains, a result consistent with statistical noninferiority of the AISP as a teaching resource"
