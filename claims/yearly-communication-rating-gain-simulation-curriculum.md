# Residents' overall communication ratings increased significantly by 0.25 points per year over the three-year simulation-based curriculum

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across three consecutive years of the simulation curriculum, residents' overall mean communication rating was 3.60 at baseline and rose by 0.25 points per year (95% CI: 0.10, 0.40; p < 0.001) as rated by all assessors including self. [→ Beaudoin Pier-Luc 2019](#beaudoin-pier-luc-2019)
`q3 i?` Learners who began with difficulty in communication improved enough to reach the average of their peers' performance in subsequent years. [→ Beaudoin Pier-Luc 2019 (2)](#beaudoin-pier-luc-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Beaudoin Pier-Luc 2019

Beaudoin Pier-Luc, Labbé Mathilde, Fanous Amanda, Young Meredith, Rappaport Jamie, Park Yoon Soo, Manoukian John, Nguyen Lily H. P. (2019). Teaching communication skills to OTL-HNS residents: multisource feedback and simulated scenarios. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-019-0329-8

`q3 · i?`

Random-intercept regression on multisource ratings from 17 residents consented for at least two consecutive years (2009–2011). The article reports the score was "3.60 at baseline with an increase of 0.25 points per year" (p < 0.001); no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The random-intercept regression, which comes from the score provided by all of the evaluators including self, showed the learner's overall mean communication score to be 3.60 at baseline with an increase of 0.25 points per year (95% CI: 0.10, 0.40) (p < 0.001)."

### Beaudoin Pier-Luc 2019 (2)

Beaudoin Pier-Luc, Labbé Mathilde, Fanous Amanda, Young Meredith, Rappaport Jamie, Park Yoon Soo, Manoukian John, Nguyen Lily H. P. (2019). Teaching communication skills to OTL-HNS residents: multisource feedback and simulated scenarios. Journal of Otolaryngology - Head & Neck Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40463-019-0329-8

`q3 · i?`

Multisource assessment data from the same cohort showed learners with initial difficulty "improved enough to reach the average of their peers' performance" in subsequent years; the article reports this descriptively with no test statistic.

> "The results of the multisource assessment also showed that learners that had initial difficulty with communication skills (as indicated by their score on the first year of the module) improved enough to reach the average of their peers' performance on the subsequent years."
