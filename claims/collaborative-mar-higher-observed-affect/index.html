Evidence¶

López-Faican 2020¶

López-Faican, L.; Jaén Martínez, FJ. (2020). EmoFindAR: Evaluation of a mobile multiplayer augmented reality game for primary school children. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103814

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Observational analysis of mood (P1Obs1) at three time points during the study of 19 pairs of children. Mean affect was 1.7900 collaborative vs 1.5570 competitive, p = 0.000; the null hypothesis H0c was rejected because "the collaborative version had a higher number of euphoric children". Children began at low mood and ended cheerful to euphoric in both modes.

"The collaborative version had a higher number of euphoric children than the competitive one (21% vs. 4%)."
