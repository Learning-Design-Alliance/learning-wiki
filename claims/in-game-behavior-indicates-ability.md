## Evidence

### Bijl 2024

Bijl, A., Veldkamp, B. P., Wools, S., & de Klerk, S. (2024). Serious games in high-stakes assessment contexts: a systematic literature review into the game design principles for valid game-based performance assessment. Education Tech Research Dev, 72, 2041–2064. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10362-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's unobtrusive data collection synthesis (Table 6) identifies accuracy measures in multiple games, completion-time measures (e.g., Wii Laparoscopy correlating with a validated laparoscopic skills measure), and log data in one cognitive-skills game. It quotes that in-game measures had "promising relationships with competency levels".

> "Finally, the use of log data was found in one SG targeted at cognitive skills (Steinrücke et al., 2020). In the Dilemma Game, in-game measures collected during gameplay were found to have promising relationships with competency levels."
