## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After content analysis and expert revision, all items of the competence frameworks reached a CVI of at least 0.85, exceeding the 0.80 acceptability threshold the study set. [→ Miranda Fernanda Berchelli Girão; Alves Gerson Pereira-Junior; Mazzo Alessandra 2021](#miranda-fernanda-berchelli-girao-alves-gerson-pereira-junior-mazzo-alessandra-2021)

## Evidence

### Miranda Fernanda Berchelli Girão; Alves Gerson Pereira-Junior; Mazzo Alessandra 2021

Miranda Fernanda Berchelli Girão; Alves Gerson Pereira-Junior; Mazzo Alessandra. (2021). Competences in the training of nurses to assist the airway of adult patients in urgency and emergency situations. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.3380.3434

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Delphi validation with 15 expert nurses in the first round and 13 in the second; items below the 0.80 threshold were revised with expert suggestions, and the final analysis showed "a final analysis on all items with a CVI≥0.85". No effect size is printed.

> "In the third phase, some items of the first analysis presented a CVI below 0.80; thus, the comments and suggestions of the experts were considered for the possibility of adjustments, with return to the participants, resulting in a final analysis on all items with a CVI≥0.85."
