`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ITT analysis of course deliverables in Microeconomics (Table 6) and Government (Table 5). Practice quizzes were worth 15% of the final grade; participation and reading checks (40% of grade) were unaffected. The authors report no treatment differences in assignment completion in Government.

> "treatment-assigned students are five percentage points more likely to complete the available practice quizzes and thus, earn practice quiz grades 3.59 points higher; these quizzes were worth 15% of their final grade). The Microeconomic chatbot outreach does not affect course participation or reading checks (cumulatively worth 40% of the final grade)"
