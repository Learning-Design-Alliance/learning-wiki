## Evidence

### Kim Young-Suk Grace 2024

Kim Young-Suk Grace. (2024). Writing Fluency: Its Relations With Language, Cognitive, and Transcription Skills, and Writing Quality Using Longitudinal Data From Kindergarten to Grade 2. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(4). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000841

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of Grade 2 data tested whether product-based and process-based measures were dissociable: they were "perfectly related (r = 1.00)", so a single writing fluency latent variable was fitted. The article interprets this as evidence both approaches capture the writing production process.

> "However, results showed that they were perfectly related (r = 1.00), and therefore, a single latent variable of writing fluency was fitted using the six variables (three words-per-time variables and three burst-length variables)."
