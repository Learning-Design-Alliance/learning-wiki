q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey results on TK day length; LEAs may offer part-day (3–4 hours) or full-day (4 or more hours) options, each supplementable with other early education or expanded learning programs (Table 1).

"Overall, 82% of all LEAs offered full-day options in the 2022–23 school year, with 75% offering only full-day, 18% offering only part-day, and 7% offering both options."

Wang 2024 (2)¶

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey comparison by LEA size. The report also states large LEAs were more likely to combine TK with expanded learning programs on an LEA site (97%) than medium (88%) and small (73%) LEAs, "indicating a potential need for partnerships to offer expanded learning in smaller districts."

"Overall, large LEAs were less likely than small and medium-sized LEAs9 to offer only full-day Tk options (48% of large LEAs, compared to 78% and 72%, respectively)."
