Palmer E.C. 2014¶

Palmer E.C., David A.S., Fleming S.M. (2014). Effects of age on metacognitive efficiency. Consciousness and Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4154452/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

General linear model in a subset of 44 participants who completed the Trail Making Test, predicting perceptual meta-d′/d′ from age and set-shifting. The article reports the age effect "remained significant after controlling for changes in executive function (β = −0.0058, p = 0.02)" and no EF association (β = 0.0042, p = 0.26).

"We found that the relationship between age and perceptual metacognitive efficiency remained significant after controlling for changes in executive function (β = −0.0058, p = 0.02), and increased executive function was not associated with better metacognitive efficiency (β = 0.0042, p = 0.26)."
