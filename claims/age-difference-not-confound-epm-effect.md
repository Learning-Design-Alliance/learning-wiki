## Evidence

### Iqbal Tassawar 2018

Iqbal Tassawar, Iqbal Saqib, Hussain Syed Sajid, Khan Iftikhar Ahmed, Khan Hikmat Ullah, Rehman Attiqa. (2018). Fighting adult illiteracy with the help of the environmental print material. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0201902

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Because the randomly divided groups' ages were not controlled, the authors compared group ages via an independent-sample t-test; no significant age difference was found, supporting attribution of outcome differences to content.

> "The results revealed no significant differences between the ages of the two groups (t = 0.16, p > 0.8). Therefore, any effect on the pronunciation, recognition, and recall scores thus could only be attributed to the content."
