# Tracing and pointing does not significantly raise intrinsic motivation, though the point estimate resembles prior meta-analytic estimates

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Self-reported intrinsic motivation did not differ significantly between conditions (d = 0.35, p = 0.058), so H1 was not supported. [→ Yining Wang 2025](#yining-wang-2025)

## Evidence

### Yining Wang 2025

Yining Wang, Kexin Han, Paul Ginns. (2025). Tracing and Pointing Support Multimedia Learning: A Cross-Cultural Replication. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10005-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-item intrinsic motivation scale (omega = 0.91) administered post-lesson; the group difference missed the one-tailed significance threshold ("t(82) = 1.59, p = 0.058").

> "The difference in self-reported intrinsic motivation between the tracing and pointing condition (M = 6.65, SD = 2.11) and the non-tracing and pointing condition (M = 5.88, SD = 2.30) was not statistically significant, t(82) = 1.59, p = 0.058, d = 0.35 [95% CI −0.09, ∞]; thus, H1 was not supported."
