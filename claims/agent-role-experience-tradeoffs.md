Thematic analysis of open-ended responses (inter-rater alpha = 0.875). One Peer participant reported being pandered to; 4/24 Challenger participants described irritation at repeatedly defending answers. Control participants reported confusion (6/24), stress (5/24), and annoyance (9/24).
