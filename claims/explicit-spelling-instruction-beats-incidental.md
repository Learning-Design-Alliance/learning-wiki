## Evidence

### Steven C. Pan 2021

Steven C. Pan, Timothy C. Rickard, Robert A. Bjork. (2021). Does Spelling Still Matter— and If So, How Should It Be Taught? Perspectives from Contemporary and Historical Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09611-y

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

The review reports a meta-analysis of 23 studies directly comparing explicit and incidental approaches: "explicit instruction was reported to increase spelling skills relative to incidental instruction by an effect size of Hedges' g = 0.43, 95% confidence interval (CI) [0.25, 0.60]" and over no instruction by g = 0.54, CI [0.39, 0.70].

> "Graham and Santangelo (2014) meta-analyzed 23 studies that directly compared the explicit and incidental approaches"
