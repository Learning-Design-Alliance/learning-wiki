Evidence¶

Liu Jingxia & Liu Li 2013¶

Liu Jingxia & Liu Li. (2013). An Empirical Study on the Application of Theme Theory in the Field of Writing Pedagogy. English Language Teaching, 6(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n5p117

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of post-test compositions (Table 7): experimental class 287 TP instances versus 141, with linear progression 32.1% versus 27.7%. The authors note the controlled class's constant pattern occupied "45.4%" and say this excess "easily makes the texts dull"; no statistical test is reported.

"But obviously, the experimental class uses more TP patterns than the controlled class."
