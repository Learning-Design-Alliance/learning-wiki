## Evidence

### Ross E. O'Hara 2019

Ross E. O'Hara, Betsy Sparrow. (2019). A Summer Nudge Campaign to Motivate Community College STEM Students to Reenroll. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419875715

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with 2,759 first-year students at three U.S. community colleges randomly assigned via Excel's RAND function; intent-to-treat linear probability and logit models on registrar reenrollment data. The printed treatment effect was 0.072** (OR 1.38), described as improving reenrollment "by 7 percentage points compared with the control group rate of 62%."

> "We first examined the impact of assignment to the treatment group relative to the control group on retention and observed that receiving nudges improved reenrollment by 7 percentage points compared with the control group rate of 62%."
