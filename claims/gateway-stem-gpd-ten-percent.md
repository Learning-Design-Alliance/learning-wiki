## Evidence

### Benjamin P. Koester 2016

Benjamin P. Koester, Galina Grom, and Timothy A. McKay. (2016). Patterns of Gendered Performance Difference in Introductory STEM Courses. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1608.07565)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of first-year introductory physics, chemistry, biology, and mathematics lecture courses. The article reports AGAs averaging -0.41 (ranging -0.54 to -0.15) and GPDs with "the mean is -0.18, ranging from -0.28 to -0.07", corresponding to roughly 10% differences in course point totals; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Female and male students receive diﬀerent average grades in these courses, even when enrolled in the same college, and having earned the same GPAO, standardized test scores, and HS GPA."
