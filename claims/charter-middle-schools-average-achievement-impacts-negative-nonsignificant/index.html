Evidence¶

Melissa A. Clark 2015¶

Melissa A. Clark, Philip M. Gleason, Christina Clark Tuttle, Marsha K. Silverberg. (2015). Do Charter Schools Improve Student Achievement? Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, vol. 37, no. 4. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/do-charter-schools-improve-student-achievement doi:10.3102/0162373714558292

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Lottery-based impact study comparing test score outcomes of students admitted through randomized admissions lotteries with outcomes of applicants not admitted. The study reports that average impacts were "negative but not statistically significant" for both the offer of admission and actual attendance; no effect size is printed.

"On average across the schools in the study, the impacts of charter middle schools on student achievement were negative but not statistically significant, regardless of whether we examined the impact of the offer of admission or actual attendance at these schools."
