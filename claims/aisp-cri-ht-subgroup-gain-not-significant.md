In the prespecified subgroup analysis for the CRI-HT outcome, the low-performer subgroup gained 12.4 points versus 7.9 for high performers, but the difference in gains was not statistically significant (p = 0.058).

> "Although the low-performer subgroup showed a larger mean gain, the between-group difference in CRI-HT gain was not statistically significant (difference in gain 4.6; 95% CI -0.2 to 9.3; p = 0.058; Cohen's d = 1.00)."
