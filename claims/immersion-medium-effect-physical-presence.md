The review reports, citing Cummings and Bailenson (2016), a meta-analysis aggregating 115 effect sizes from 83 studies on immersion and physical presence. It found "immersion had a medium-sized effect on physical presence", and identified user tracking, stereoscopic visuals, and wider fields of view as the most important technological features. No standardized effect size value is printed.

> "Cummings and Bailenson aggregated 115 effect sizes from 83 studies and found that immersion had a medium-sized effect on physical presence. Their results identified increased levels of user tracking, the use of stereoscopic visuals, and wider fields of view of visual displays as the most important technological features influencing physical presence."
