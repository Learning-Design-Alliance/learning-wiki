## Evidence

### Dill-McFarland KA 2021

Dill-McFarland KA, König SG, Mazel F, Oliver DC, McEwen LM, Hong KY, Hallam SJ. (2021). An integrated, modular approach to data science education in microbiology. PLoS Computational Biology. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008661

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational enrollment analysis of cocurricular workshops, not a controlled comparison. The article reports 77 EDUCE students took workshops over 2 years with undergraduate participation rising from 0% to 8.8% to 23% of total registrants; no inferential test or effect size is reported.

> "Over the first 2 years, cocurricular workshops were taken by 77 EDUCE students, which resulted in increasing undergraduate participation from 0% (2016 to 2017, prior to partnership with EDUCE) to 8.8% (2017 to 2018) to 23% (2018 to 2019) of total registrants."
