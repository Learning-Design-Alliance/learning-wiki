## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2014

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2014). Repeated Reading: WWC Intervention Report, Students with Learning Disabilities. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/576

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

WWC review of the Wexler et al. (2010) trial assessing four fluency measures — the TOSCRF and three AIMSweb oral reading fluency assessments of words read correctly per minute — with 62 students in 11 classrooms. The WWC-calculated average effect size across the four outcomes was not substantively important; the rating is no discernible effects with a small extent of evidence.

> "Wexler et al. (2010) found no statistically significant effect of repeated reading on four measures of reading fluency: the Test of Silent Contextual Reading Fluency (TOSCRF) and three assessments of oral reading fluency (number of words read correctly per minute) using the AIMSweb reading system."
