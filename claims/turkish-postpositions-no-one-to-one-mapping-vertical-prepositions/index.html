Evidence¶

Kilimci 2017¶

Kilimci, A. (2017). Integrating cognitive linguistics insights into data-driven learning: teaching vertical prepositions. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 13(2), 681-719. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1159243

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Contrastive linguistic analysis drawing on Kornfilt's description of Turkish postpositions: over and above correspond to two interchangeable postpositions from üst-/üzer-, while both under and below map onto a single postposition from alt-. The article argues that "due to these cross-linguistic differences in spatial language, Turkish L2 learners might experience problems"; this is an analytical argument, not a measured result.

"the Turkish postpositions üzer-/üst- and -alt coding for spatial relations do not exhibit one-to-one correspondence with their English counterparts over/above and under/below"
