### Attash Heba M. 2025

Attash Heba M., Dawood Omar T., Aladul Mohammed I., Essa Narmin S. (2025). Association between ramadan fasting, sleep disturbances, fatigue, and academic performance among Iraqi university students: A cross-sectional study. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-24482-6

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Group comparisons in the same survey (Tables 3–5): headache was associated with higher sleepiness (d = 0.41), physical fatigue (d = 0.93) and mental fatigue (d = 0.69); fasting-affected sleep with d = 0.62, 0.88, and 0.61 respectively; stress/anxiety with d = 1.01 (physical) and d = 0.98 (mental).
