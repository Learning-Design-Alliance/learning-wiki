Conditional-probability analysis of the same 28-student dataset. The article reports "P ( ¯D|C) = 11 /16 ≈ 0.69" and that these students could combine resistors iteratively without understanding the abstract nature of the combinations.

> "For the computational question P ( ¯D|C) = 11 /16 ≈ 0.69 meant 69% of students who successfully carried out a fairly detailed computation involving the reduction of a resistor network were able to do so without demonstrating deeper conceptual understanding."
