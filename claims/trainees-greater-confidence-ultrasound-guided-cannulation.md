Ultrasound reliance and landmark-based proficiency in vascular access training: a multicenter cross-sectional survey of anesthesia trainees and teaching faculty in Eastern China. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported confidence on a 5-point Likert scale from 454 trainees in a multicenter cross-sectional survey, analyzed with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test for paired samples. Table 4 reports median confidence of 4 (4–5) for ultrasound-guided versus 3 (3–4) for landmark-based IJV cannulation (P < 0.001).

> "Trainees reported greater confidence with ultrasound-guided than with landmark-based cannulation for both IJV and RA access (Table 4; Fig. 2)."
