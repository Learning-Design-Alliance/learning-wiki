## Evidence

### Sejdi Sejdiu 2017

Sejdi Sejdiu. (2017). Are Listening Skills Best Enhanced Through the Use of Multimedia Technology. Digital Education Review - Number 32, December 2017. http://greav.ub.edu/der/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Review-reported null: Wang (2010) conducted a quasi-experimental study in China on multimedia in "zero class hours" using questionnaire pre-test and post-test results; "no visible difference" in results was found, while motivation and assignment completion favoured the experimental group.

> "the study was able to demonstrate that there was no visible difference in the results of students who undertook multimedia intervention from those who did not. However, the study was able to glean that students in the experimental group were more motivated to learn"
