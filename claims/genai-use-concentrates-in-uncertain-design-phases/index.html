Leman Figen Gül, Burak Delikanlı, Oğulcan Üneşi, Ertuğrul Ömer Gül. (2025). Development and applications of Generative AI in architectural design studios. ITU A|Z • Vol 22 No 3 • November 2025 • 751-774. https://doi.org/10.58278/0.2025.109

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with a focus group (N=6) in the third semester, comparing sixth-week (W6) and thirteenth-week (W13) coded design processes shown in Figure 7. The article reports the shift in usage across design phases; no effect size is printed.

"However, after the workshops in the studio process, active GenAI usage is observed in more uncertain phases of the design (e.g. Problem Space, Figure 7b). In contrast, the active usage of GenAI either decreased or disappeared in the more certain phases of the design process (e.g. Solution Space, Figure 7b)."
