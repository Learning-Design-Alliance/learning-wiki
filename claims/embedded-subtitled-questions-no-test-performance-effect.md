# Embedded questions in video as synchronized subtitles do not affect SFL audiovisual comprehension test performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Test-takers with embedded subtitled questions scored no differently from those with questions on paper only (Mann-Whitney U = 203.500, z = -.147, p = .885, r = -0.023). [→ Núñez 2017](#nunez-2017)

## Evidence

### Núñez 2017

Casañ Núñez, J. C. (2017). Testing audiovisual comprehension tasks with questions embedded in videos as subtitles: a pilot multimethod study. The EUROCALL Review, 25(1). https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.7062

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 22 control and 19 experimental SFL students taking two variants of the same audiovisual comprehension test; the Mann-Whitney test showed "no statistically significant difference" and the article concludes the subtitled questions "did not have any impact on test performance".

> "The results revealed no statistically significant difference between the test scores obtained by the control group (Mdn= 5.500) and the experimental group (Mdn = 5.000), U = 203.500, z = -.147, p (exact, two -tailed) = .885, r = -0.023, which indicated that the subtitled questions did not have any impact on test performance."
