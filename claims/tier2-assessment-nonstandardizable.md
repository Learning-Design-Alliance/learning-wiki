`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

This is the article's own theoretical argument, stated as the first of three propositions in its assessment section: because open problems have unknown answers, rubrics cannot capture Tier 2 research quality, and the trained professional's repeated judgment is what closes the gap. No empirical data support the proposition in this article.

> "First, Tier 2 tasks are non-standardizable by design: open problems have unknown answers, and a rubric can only cover what can be pre-specified, so any rubric broad enough to span authentic research becomes generic and any rubric tight enough to be informative becomes project-bound and non-reusable."
