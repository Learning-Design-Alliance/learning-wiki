# Written examination score variance was significantly larger in the tutorless group in 2008, with a marginal tendency in 2007

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` WES dispersion was significantly larger in the tutorless group in 2008 (p = 0.039) and marginally in 2007 (p = 0.058), indicating more uneven outcomes without a tutor. [→ Hayashi Shogo 2013](#hayashi-shogo-2013)

## Evidence

### Hayashi Shogo 2013

Hayashi Shogo, Tsunekawa Koji, Inoue Chikako, Fukuzawa Yoshitaka. (2013). Comparison of tutored group with tutorless group in problem-based mixed learning sessions: a randomized cross-matched study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-158

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

F-test comparison of WES dispersion between tutored and tutorless groups in the cross-matched PBL1 study. The article reports "variances tended to be significantly larger" in the tutorless group; the daily report appeared to show quality and quantity of SGD and self-learning varied widely there.

> "However, in 2008, variances tended to be significantly larger than the average score in the tutorless group (2007, p = 0.058; 2008, p = 0.039)."
