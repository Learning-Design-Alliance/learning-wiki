# Patient-specific mental rehearsal with interactive 3D anatomical models improves CAT-assessed operative quality over checklist-only rehearsal on challenging anatomies

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Group B (checklist plus model) achieved significantly higher total CAT scores than Group A (checklist only) for short cystic duct (p = 0.02, η² = 0.32) and double cystic artery (p = 0.03, η² = 0.28) cases. [→ Yiasemidou M 2018](#yiasemidou-m-2018)
`q3 i?` There was no statistically significant difference in total CAT score between groups for normal anatomy (p = 0.2). [→ Yiasemidou M 2018](#yiasemidou-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Yiasemidou M 2018

Yiasemidou M, Galli R, Glassman D, Tang M, Aziz R, Jayne D, Miskovic D. (2018). Patient-specific mental rehearsal with interactive visual aids: a path worth exploring? Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5788-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomised comparison of 16 medical students (8 per group) in a simulation suite; videos scored by two blinded assessors using the competency assessment tool (ICC 0.81). The model group scored higher on challenging anatomies with "η 2 = 0.32" and "η 2 = 0.28", but not on normal anatomy.

> "According to the CAT scores, Group B performed the SCD and DA LC significantly better than the Group A, but there was no statistically significant difference in the performance of the NA LC [checklist vs. model total CAT score—NA: 23.63 vs. 26.69 p = 0.2—SCD: 20.5 vs. 26.31 p = 0.02 η 2 = 0.32—DA: 24.75 vs. 30.5 p = 0.03 η 2 = 0.28]"
