Evidence¶

Hatzigianni 2014¶

Hatzigianni, M. (2014). 'I like computers but my favourite is playing outside with my friends': Young children's beliefs about computers. International Research in Early Childhood Education, 5(1). https://www.education.monash.edu.au/irecejournal/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of interview responses from 52 children aged 44-79 months, coded into emergent categories and examined with frequencies and percentages in SPSS. The article reports computers were "not children's most frequently reported favourite activity at home or at school". Sports and playing outside with friends was most frequently reported at home (n = 23 pre; n = 31 post).

"Importantly, computers were not children's most frequently reported favourite activity at home or at school. Rather, they were children's second most frequently reported favourite activity at home and their fourth (pre -intervention) or th ird (post-intervention) most frequently reported favourite activity at school."
