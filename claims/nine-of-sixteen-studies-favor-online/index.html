Pei L 2019¶

Pei L, Wu H. (2019). Does online learning work better than offline learning in undergraduate medical education? A systematic review and meta-analysis. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2019.1666538

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review's synthesis of its 16 included studies across three statistical genres of outcome comparison. The article states 7 articles reported no significant difference and 9 reported significant improvement in the online learning groups; no effect sizes are attached to this count.

"By comparing post-test scores, pre- and post-test score gains and retention test scores, we identified 7 articles that reported no significant difference between the two teaching methods and 9 articles that reported significant improvement in the online learning groups."
