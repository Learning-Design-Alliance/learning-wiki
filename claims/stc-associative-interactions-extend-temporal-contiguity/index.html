Evidence¶

Wickliffe C. Abraham 2019¶

Wickliffe C. Abraham, Owen D. Jones and David L. Glanzman. (2019). Is plasticity of synapses the mechanism of long-term memory storage? npj Science of Learning 4:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0048-y

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review statement about STC findings in L-LTP research. The review reports the interactions "can occur across intervals of hours", which the authors suggest may bind events occurring in temporal proximity to strongly stored experiences.

"These STC associative interactions can occur across intervals of hours, 30 which extends the range of temporal contiguity needed for association well beyond the tens of milliseconds typical of Hebbian associativity"
