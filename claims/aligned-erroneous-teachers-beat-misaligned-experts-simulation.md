### Ilia Sucholutsky 2024

Pardos, Adrian Weller, Thomas L. Griffiths. (2024). Representational Alignment Supports Effective Teaching. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.04302](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.04302)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simulation study with 1-NN student and teacher agents in the simple-features GRADE setting, sweeping teacher error rate and representational alignment to trace a utility curve (Figure 1F). The authors "uncover instances wherein students can achieve higher performance" with aligned erroneous teachers. No effect size is printed.

> "We uncover instances wherein students can achieve higher performance by learning from teachers who are more erroneous (“less expert”) provided the teachers are representationally aligned with the students than comparatively more expert but representationally misaligned teachers"
