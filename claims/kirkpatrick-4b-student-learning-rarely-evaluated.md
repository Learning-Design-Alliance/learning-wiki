Dannaway Jasan, Ng Heryanto, Schoo Adrian. (2016). Literature review of teaching skills programs for junior medical officers. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5685.14da

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review describes Hill et al. 2012's controlled evaluation: a 1.5-day workshop for 34 interns improved intern teaching effectiveness versus control hospitals, but student OSCE results showed no significant intersite differences; equivalence was not tested.

> "One study Hill et al 201227 implemented a 1.5 day workshop to 34 interns which demonstrated improvement using the intern clinical teaching effectiveness instrument when compared to control hospitals.  However, objective structured clinical examination results of medical students taught by these interns, did not demonstrate any significant intersite differences."
