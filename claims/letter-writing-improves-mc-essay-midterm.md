## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Students in the writing condition scored significantly higher than controls on the multiple-choice plus essay midterm (partial η² = .14). [→ Bersamin 2013](#bersamin-2013)

## Evidence

### Bersamin 2013

Bersamin, M., Zamboanga, B.L. & Orsak-Neff, N. (2013). Understanding course content through letter writing: Do informal writing assignments improve grades? Psychology Teaching Review, 19(1). https://www.bps.org.uk/journal/psychology-teaching-review

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-test 2 (multiple-choice and essay midterm) in the counterbalanced experiment, controlling for pre-test scores; Table 3 reports experimental group F=5.89, partial η²=.14, p=.02. The writing group's adjusted mean was "significantly higher" than controls, an eight-point difference.

> "respondents in the writing condition scored significantly higher (adjusted means: M =80.03, SE=2.03) than those in the control group (adjusted means: M=72.71, SE=2.09)"
