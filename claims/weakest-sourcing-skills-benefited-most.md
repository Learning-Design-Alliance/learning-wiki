## Evidence

### Hämäläinen 2023

Hämäläinen, E. K., Kiili, C., Räikkönen, E., Lakkala, M., Ilomäki, L., Toom, A., & Marttunen, M. (2023). Teaching sourcing during online inquiry – adolescents with the weakest skills benefited the most. Instructional Science, 51, 135–163. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09597-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Individual-level Reliable Change Index analysis of intervention-group students' change across the four sourcing variables; students with a clear positive change had the lowest pre-test scores, and the article adds that students showing a negative change "scored the highest in all the pre-test sourcing variables".

> "the intervention group students who showed a clear positive change (RCI class 4) in their sourcing performance scored the lowest in all the pre-test sourcing variables."
