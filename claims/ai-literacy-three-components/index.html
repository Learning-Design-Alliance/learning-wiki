Theoretical argument in a peer-reviewed opinion/commentary article. The authors state "Our framing of AI literacy has three key components" and elaborate each: understanding AI capabilities, utilizing AI for problem solving, and applying AI in sociocultural contexts. No empirical evidence is offered; this is the authors' conceptual framing.
