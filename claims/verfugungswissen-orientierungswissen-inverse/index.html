Evidence¶

Markus Deimann and Robert Farrow 2013¶

Markus Deimann and Robert Farrow. (2013). Rethinking OER and their Use: Open Education as Bildung. International Review of Research in Open and Distance Learning, Vol 14 No 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1017477

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument distinguishing knowledge of how things are produced from knowledge of why. The article asserts the two forms are "inversely proportional" and that open access to Verfügungswissen exacerbates the acquisition of Orientierungswissen; no empirical evidence is offered.

"Both forms are inversely proportional: Whereas Verfügungswissen is easily accessible due to open formats and open archiving, it does not, however, contribute to an increase of Orientierungswissen. Furthermore, it exacerbates its acquisition."
