## Evidence

### Andreas Müller 2020

Andreas Müller, Madeleine Rousset Grenon. (2020). Athéna – A pre-university study programme at the University of Geneva. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.05478](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.05478)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive completion and withdrawal statistics for the first three Athena editions, reported in the schools'-perspectives section; the article reports that "80 - 90% have obtained a certificate of participation" and withdrawal "ranges around 15 - 20%".

> "Indeed, 80 - 90% have obtained a certificate of participation. The rate of withdrawal from the programme ranges around 15 - 20%, which seems – given the exploratory and tentative nature of the programme – completely acceptable."

### Andreas Müller 2020 (2)

Andreas Müller, Madeleine Rousset Grenon. (2020). Athéna – A pre-university study programme at the University of Geneva. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2007.05478](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2007.05478)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Enrolment data collected at programme entry for the 2015/16–2017/18 editions, reported in the evaluation section; participation averaged "70 – 80 pupils per year" with an approximately equal mathematics–physics split.

> "On average, 70 – 80 pupils per year took part in Athena, with roughly a 50% - 50% split between mathematics and physics, and a gender ratio GR = NF/NM = 0.68 (see. Fig. 2)."
