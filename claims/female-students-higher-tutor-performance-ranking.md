## Evidence

### Barnawi Ahlam 2024

Barnawi Ahlam, Sonbol Ahmed M., Al-Shawwa Lana, Abulaban Alwalla, Asiri Khalil, Bagasi Abdulaziz, Alafari Reem, Alamoudi Aliaa Amr. (2024). Employing students' evaluations and tutors' perceptions to evaluate a faculty development program on problem-based learning at the Faculty of Medicine, King Abdulaziz University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05662-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent t-test on performance rankings from the 284-student questionnaire. A chi-square test also found a significant association (p = 0.001) between gender and sufficient-versus-excellent ranking categories; no effect sizes are printed.

> "However, the female students (6.87 ± 1.9) awarded a significantly higher mean performance ranking (1–10) than the male students (5.98 ± 2.1) (p < 0.001), according to the independent t-test at < 0.05."
