Evidence¶

Punch 2011¶

Punch, R., & Hyde, M. B. (2011). Communication, Psychosocial, and Educational Outcomes of Children with Cochlear Implants and Challenges Remaining for Professionals and Parents. International Journal of Otolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.1155/2011/573280

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Parent (N = 247) and teacher (N = 151) survey reports on children's functional communication, corroborated by interviews. Near-normal speech and language outcomes tended to occur with familiar partners and optimal listening environments, not in classrooms or social gatherings.

"For instance, 20% of parents and 48% of teachers reported that the child could not follow a spoken conversation with a group of people."
