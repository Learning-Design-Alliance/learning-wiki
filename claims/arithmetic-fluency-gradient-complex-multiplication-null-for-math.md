# The prediction power of specific processing speed for mathematics decreases with reduced arithmetic fluency: complex multiplication did not predict mathematics although it predicted Chinese and English

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Complex multiplication failed to account for variance in mathematics achievement in either grade, while simple and complex subtraction did. [→ Cheng Dazhi 2022](#cheng-dazhi-2022)
`q2 i?` Complex multiplication did predict Chinese and English in both grades, with ΔR² between 0.029 and 0.039. [→ Cheng Dazhi 2022 (2)](#cheng-dazhi-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Cheng Dazhi 2022

Cheng Dazhi, Shi Kaihui, Wang Naiyi, Miao Xinyang, Zhou Xinlin. (2022). Examining the Differential Role of General and Specific Processing Speed in Predicting Mathematical Achievement in Junior High School. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence10010001

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the Grade 7 and Grade 9 hierarchical regressions, complex multiplication showed non-significant ΔR² for mathematics (0.020 and 0.014 in Table 2), below the Bonferroni-corrected threshold; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, complex multiplication could not account for the variances in mathematical achievement, even after controlling for age, gender, nonverbal matrix reasoning, spatial abilities, and visual attention."

### Cheng Dazhi 2022 (2)

Cheng Dazhi, Shi Kaihui, Wang Naiyi, Miao Xinyang, Zhou Xinlin. (2022). Examining the Differential Role of General and Specific Processing Speed in Predicting Mathematical Achievement in Junior High School. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence10010001

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same regression models show complex multiplication significantly predicting Chinese, English, and total scores at Bonferroni-corrected p < 0.05 or p < 0.01, with the printed ΔR² values as quoted.

> "complex multiplication predicted variances in Chinese (ΔR2 = 0.033, p < 0.05) and English (ΔR2 = 0.034, p < 0.05) in Grade 7 and Chinese (ΔR2 = 0.029, p < 0.05), English (ΔR2 = 0.039, p < 0.01), and total scores (ΔR2 = 0.033, p < 0.05) in Grade 9."
