### Conlin 2006

Conlin, L. D., Gupta, A., Scherr, R. E., & Hammer, D. (2006). The Dynamics of Students' Behaviors and Reasoning during Collaborative Physics Tutorial Sessions. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings record. https://www.compadre.org/PER/ [doi:10.1063/1.2820949](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2820949)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational finding from the coded tutorial sessions: the pure blue, green, red, and yellow modes accounted for 86% of tutorial time with group-level consistency, which the authors take as evidence of an emergent group dynamic in framing.

> "Out of the individual framing of each student emerges an overarching group dynamic that evolves over the course of the tutorial.  This is evident in that all four students occupy the same behavior modes for 86% of the time."
