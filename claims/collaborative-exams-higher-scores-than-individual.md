## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Mean group scores were significantly higher than mean individual scores on both collaborative exams (p < 0.001). [→ Leight H. 2012](#leight-h-2012)

## Evidence

### Leight H. 2012

Leight H., Saunders C., Calkins R., Withers M. (2012). Collaborative Testing Improves Performance but Not Content Retention in a Large-Enrollment Introductory Biology Class. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-04-0048

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the randomized cross-over study (n = 92 group A, 104 group B), group exams taken immediately after individual computer-based exams scored higher: "mean group scores were significantly higher than mean individual scores (p < 0.001)". Exam 3 showed a similar trend (84.1 vs 63.7%); no effect size is printed.

> "For exams 2 and 3, mean group scores were significantly higher than mean individual scores (p < 0.001). For exam 2, the mean group score (74.5 ± 1.63%) was more than 10% higher than the mean individual score for students in group A (61.9 ± 1.89%)."
