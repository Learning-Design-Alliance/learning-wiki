## Evidence

### Promnont 2015

Promnont, P., & Rattanavich, S. (2015). Concentrated Language Encounter Instruction Model III in Reading and Creative Writing Abilities. English Language Teaching, 8(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n5p1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

In the same one-group repeated-measures study (n = 43), creative writing scores rose significantly from pretest through two posttests, with the article reporting "higher development in creative writing at a .001 level" and a partial η2 = .756, a large effect.

> "the comparison of the development in creative writing indicated that the experimental group registered higher development in creative writing at a .001 level ( = .244 F-statistic = 63.561, p-value = .000)"
