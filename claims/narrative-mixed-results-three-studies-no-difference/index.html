q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Narrative synthesis section of the systematic review of eight studies. The review reports "Three studies reported no significant difference in exam scores"; no effect sizes are printed for these null findings.

"Mixed results were found regarding the impact of OBE on performance compared to CBE. Three studies reported no significant difference in exam scores."
