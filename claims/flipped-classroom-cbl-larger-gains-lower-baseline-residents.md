## Evidence

### Ji Y 2026

Ji Y, Zhang X, Han X, Xiong G, Chen X, Lu Y, Zhang Q, Sui S, Yang Z, Yu S, Xu W, Zheng G, Zhang W, Liu Y, Xin J. (2026). Enhancing nuclear medicine residency standardization training: a comprehensive exploration of integrating a flipped classroom and case-based learning for effective instruction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09808-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subgroup analysis of residents at or below the pre-test median (9.5 points) in the same retrospective single-group study. Scores rose from 7.6 ± 1.51 to 14.2 ± 4.40 points (P < 0.001), a 6.6-point gain versus 4.55 points for the whole cohort.

> "In the subset of participants scoring at or below the median (9.5 points) in the pre-test, the average pre-test score was 7.6 ± 1.51 points. Following the educational intervention, the post-test average score increased to 14.2 ± 4.40 points (P < 0.001)"
