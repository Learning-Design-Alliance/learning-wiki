## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among intervention-group participants, health-knowledge scores rose from pre-test to post-test, with married participants showing greater gains (β = 0.686), and health-belief scores rose from pre-test to post-test, with primary-school-or-below education showing greater gains than junior high (β = −0.888) or senior high/vocational education (β = −1.405).
