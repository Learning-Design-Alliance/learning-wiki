Cohort comparison (Table 6) across 2021 (n=49), 2022 (n=111), 2023 (n=116), 2024 (n=25). The authors attribute the gradient to cumulative curricular immersion: the 2021 cohort completed a full undergraduate programme with progressive motivational scaffolding.

> "An independent samples t-test, stratified by academic cohort (Table 6), shows that the 2021 cohort achieved the highest scores in both 'Using AIGC' and 'Evaluating AIGC', whereas the 2024 cohort scored lowest, with statistically significant differences (F=5.533, p=0.001**; F=2.940, p=0.033*)"
