# ComOn check subscales B, C, and E and the overall sum score show Cronbach's alpha greater than 0.77

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Internal consistency was acceptable to good, with Cronbach's alpha above 0.77 for the three multi-item subscales and the sum score. [→ Radziej K 2017](#radziej-k-2017)

## Evidence

### Radziej K 2017

Radziej K, Loechner J, Engerer C, Niglio de Figueiredo M, Freund J, Sattel H, Bachmann C, Berberat PO, Dinkel A, Wuensch A. (2017). How to assess communication skills? Development of the rating scale ComOn Check. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2017.1392823

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Internal consistency analysis of consensus ratings from the 135-video psychometric evaluation. Table 2 prints Cronbach's alpha of 0.87 for Structure of conversation, 0.82 for Patient's emotions, 0.77 for Communication skills, and 0.83 for the sum score; the authors characterize these as "acceptable to good internal consistencies".

> "Cronbach's α of the domains B, C, and E as well as the overall sum score (A1-E5) indicated acceptable to good internal consistencies with values greater than 0.77."
