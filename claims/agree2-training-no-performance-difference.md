## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants randomized to the tutorial, tutorial plus practice exercise, and PDF User's Manual conditions did not differ significantly in distance from expert domain scores on any of the six AGREE II domains. [→ Brouwers Melissa C 2011](#brouwers-melissa-c-2011)

## Evidence

### Brouwers Melissa C 2011

Brouwers Melissa C, Makarski Julie, Durocher Lisa D, Levinson Anthony J. (2011). E-learning interventions are comparable to user's manual in a randomized trial of training strategies for the AGREE II. Implementation Science. https://doi.org/10.1186/1748-5908-6-81

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized trial, one-way ANOVAs compared the distance function (squared difference between participant and expert domain scores) across the three training conditions for all six domains; the article reports "p > 0.05 for all comparisons" with no effect sizes printed.

> "There were no significant differences in any of the domain distance functions between the three training groups (p > 0.05 for all comparisons)."
