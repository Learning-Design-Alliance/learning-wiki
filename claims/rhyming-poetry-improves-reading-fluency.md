Small-n study by Zavala and Cuevas (2019) comparing Rhyming Poetry and Repeated Listening with 12 first-grade students taught by the same teacher using poem passages with a word family pattern. The review quotes that "five out of six students increased their scaled score" in the RP group.

> "in the rhyming poetry intervention group, five out of six students increased their scaled score and only one saw a decrease in his or her score"
