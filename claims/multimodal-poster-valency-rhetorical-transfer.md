## Evidence

### van Rijt 2022

van Rijt, Jimmy; Myhill, Debra; De Maeyer, Sven; Coppen, Peter-Arno. (2022). Linguistic metaconcepts can improve grammatical understanding in L1 education: evidence from a Dutch quasi-experimental study. PLoS ONE 17(2): e0263123. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0263123

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative analysis of key assignment 2, in which students reflected on a humanitarian aid poster with deliberately reduced valency and considered its rhetorical effects. Of 142 performed and readable tasks, 45.1% failed to detect a valency issue and 15.5% identified one inadequately.

> "47 students (33.1%) had managed to recognize the valency issue, and relate it to some (more or less) appropriate rhetorical effect."
