## Evidence

### Jussila Juuso J 2023

Jussila Juuso J, Pulakka Anna, Halonen Jaana I, Salo Paula, Allaouat Sara, Mikkonen Santtu, Lanki Timo. (2023). Are active school transport and leisure-time physical activity associated with performance and wellbeing at secondary school? A population-based study. The European Journal of Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/ckad128

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Main-model logistic regression in the nationwide cross-sectional study found no AST–burnout association; the OR 0.89 (95% CI 0.81–0.99) for the 10–30 min group appeared only in the sensitivity analysis excluding 2,163 pupils with special educational needs.

> "AST was not associated with school burnout in the main regression model (figure 2). However, 11% (OR 0.89, 95% CI 0.81–0.99) lower odds of burnout were observed for the '10–30 min' group after excluding participants with special educational needs."
