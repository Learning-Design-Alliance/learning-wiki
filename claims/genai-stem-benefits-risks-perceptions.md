`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Qualitative open coding of open-ended benefit and risk responses from 40 students and 28 instructors. The article reports "Misinformation emerged as the primary risk identified by both groups, cited by 67% of faculty and 49% of students", alongside personalized learning support as the top benefit (36% students, 50% faculty) and learning-quality concerns at 50% of instructors versus 16% of students.
