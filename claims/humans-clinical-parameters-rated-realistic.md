Validation study: 15 experienced prehospital professionals rated parameters of 9 automatically generated simulated patients on a 5-point Likert scale from 1 (impossible) to 5 (absolutely realistic). Overall clinical parameters had a "median rating of 5 (absolutely realistic; IQR 4-5)", and 96% of individual clinical parameters had a median rating of 5.

> "Overall, clinical parameters of all patients had a median rating of 5 (absolutely realistic; IQR 4-5). Most (n=52, 96%) individual clinical parameters had a median rating of 5, the remainder (n=2, 4%) being rated 4."
