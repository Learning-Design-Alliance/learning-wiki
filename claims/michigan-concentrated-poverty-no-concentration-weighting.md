## Evidence

### The Education Trust–Midwest 2021

The Education Trust–Midwest. (2021). Engine of Inequality: Michigan's Education System. https://midwest.edtrust.org/advocacy/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

An Education Law Center analysis of Census SAIPE 2017 data, reproduced in the report, shows "37 percent of Michigan’s students from low-income backgrounds live in high-poverty districts" — among the highest rates nationwide. The report states Michigan, unlike other states, has no funding mechanism targeting high-concentration-poverty districts.
