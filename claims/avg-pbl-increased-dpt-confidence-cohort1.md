## Subclaims
`q2 i3` After AVG laboratory experiences embedded in a PBL curriculum, cohort 1 students' self-rated confidence rose from pre-test to post-test in all domains (use, selection, plan of care, set-up, documentation, setting) and total score, in a single-group pre-post study, with large effect sizes. ## Evidence

### Wormley Michelle Elizabeth 2022

Wormley Michelle Elizabeth, Romney Wendy, Veneri Diana, Oberlander Andrea. (2022). Doctoral physical therapy students' increased confidence following exploration of active video gaming systems in a problem-based learning curriculum in the United States: a pre- and post-intervention study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2022.19.7

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Single-group quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 60 first-year DPT students; Wilcoxon signed-rank tests compared pre- and post-intervention confidence. "students' confidence levels increased significantly compared to baseline testing in all domains". The article reports the effect size was large for the 5 domains (range, r=0.52–0.62 and for the total score, r=-0.62).

> "For cohort 1, students' confidence levels increased significantly compared to baseline testing in all domains: use, Z=-6.2 (P<0.01); selection, Z=-5.9 (P<0.01); plan of care, Z=-6.0 (P<0.01); set-up, Z=-5.5 (P<0.01); documentation, Z=-6.0 (P<0.01); setting, Z=-6.3 (P<0.01)"
