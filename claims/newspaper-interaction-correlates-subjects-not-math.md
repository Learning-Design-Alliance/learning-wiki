## Evidence

### Choi Naya 2022

Choi Naya, Sheo Jiyeon, Jung Suji, Choi Jisu. (2022). Newspaper Reading in Families with School-Age Children: Relationship between Parent–Child Interaction Using Newspaper, Reading Motivation, and Academic Achievement. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 19(21), 14423. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192114423

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of 238 households with children in grades 3–6 subscribed to newspapers (Table 3). The study reports positive correlations with "language arts, social studies, science, and English" and no correlation with math; correlation coefficients are printed in Table 3 but no standardized effect size summary is given.

> "Parent–child interaction had a positive correlation with most subfactors of academic achievement, including language arts, social studies, science, and English, whereas no correlations were found with math."
