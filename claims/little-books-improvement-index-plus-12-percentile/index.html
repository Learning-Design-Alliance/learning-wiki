WWC Intervention Report: Little Books 2007¶

WWC Intervention Report: Little Books. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r?

WWC computation from the single study meeting standards (Phillips et al., 1990 randomized controlled trial of 314 analyzed kindergartners). The improvement index represents the percentile-rank difference between the average intervention student and the average comparison student; the report states it was "+12 percentile points for each group".

"The improvement index across the three variations of Little Books for general reading achievement was +12 percentile points for each group, resulting in an average of +12 percentile points for the study."
