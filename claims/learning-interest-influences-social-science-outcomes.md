### Permatasari and Gunarhadi 2019

Berti Dyah Permatasari, Gunarhadi, & Riyadi. (2019). The influence of problem based learning towards social science learning outcomes viewed from learning interest. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), 8(1), 39-46. https://iaescore.com/journals/index.php/IJERE

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA with unequal cells on 109 fourth-graders classified by questionnaire into high, medium, and low learning interest. The interest main effect was significant; no effect size is printed.

> "Anava test results showed F b = 5.409 > 3.085 so the test decision was rejected by H 0B.This means that there is an influence of students' learning interest on social studies learning outcomes."
