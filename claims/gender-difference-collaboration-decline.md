### Tudge 1992

Tudge, J. R. H. (1992). Processes and Consequences of Peer Collaboration: A Vygotskian Analysis. Child Development, 63, 1364-1379. https://doi.org/10.2307/1131562

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-tailed t tests on second posttest-pretest differences by gender in the same experiment: "girls (pretest M = 3.23, SD = 1.29) significantly declined" while boys did not; the ANCOVA gender effect was F(1, 85) = 7.62, p < .008. No effect size printed.

> "boys (pretest M = 3.04, SD = 1.36) did not significantly improve on their pretest rule (M = .21, SD = 0.87, t{42) = 1.30, p > .2), whereas girls (pretest M = 3.23, SD = 1.29) significantly declined"
