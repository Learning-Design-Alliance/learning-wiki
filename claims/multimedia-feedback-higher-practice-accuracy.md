# Multimedia feedback yields higher overall accuracy during practice with feedback, but without iterative improvement over scenarios

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants who received multimedia feedback had higher overall practice accuracy than those who received text-only feedback. [→ Gök 2025](#gok-2025)
`q3 i?` The feedback modality by scenario interaction was not significant, so multimedia feedback did not produce unique iterative changes across the practice phase. [→ Gök 2025](#gok-2025)

## Evidence

### Gök 2025

Gök, S., Zhang, T., Goldstone, R. L., & Fyfe, E. R. (2025). Multimedia Effects in Initial Instruction and Feedback on Problem Solving. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000943

`q3 · i2`

A 3x2x2 mixed ANCOVA on scenario-by-scenario practice accuracy with pretest covariate showed a feedback modality main effect, F (1, 124) = 4.48, p = .036, η p² = .04, plus a scenario main effect (η p² = .08) as performance declined with complexity.

> "there was a main effect of feedback modality, F (1, 124) = 4.48, p = .036, η p² = .04, as participants who received multimedia feedback had higher overall accuracy than those who received text-only feedback."
