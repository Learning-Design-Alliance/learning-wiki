# Basic operative laparoscopic skills do not correlate significantly with laparoscopic suturing performance before or after an advanced training course

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q2` · `i0`–`i1`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Post-course task performance on basic laparoscopic operative skills showed no significant correlation with post-course laparoscopic suturing skills (r = 0.024; P = 0.857). [→ Tang B 2020](#tang-b-2020)
`q2 i1` Pre-course basic operative laparoscopic skills also did not correlate significantly with pre-course suturing performance (r = 0.193; P = 0.149). [→ Tang B 2020 (2)](#tang-b-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Tang B 2020

Tang B, Zhang L, Alijani A. (2020). Evidence to support the early introduction of laparoscopic suturing skills into the surgical training curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1986-z

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis (Pearson correlation and linear regression, significance at 0.01) of task performance among 57 senior surgical trainees assessed before and after an advanced laparoscopic course. The post-course correlation was "not significant (r = 0.024; P = 0.857)".

> "The correlation between the post-course other more basic laparoscopic operative skills (basic operative skills on electrosurgical hook knife dissection, clipping, scissor cutting, port insertion, applying endoloop) and post-course laparoscopic suturing skills was also not significant (r = 0.024; P = 0.857)"

### Tang B 2020 (2)

Tang B, Zhang L, Alijani A. (2020). Evidence to support the early introduction of laparoscopic suturing skills into the surgical training curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1986-z

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Pre-course assessment of the same 57 senior trainees on six tasks using OSCE and OCHRA methods found no significant pre-course correlation, with "before (r = 0.193; P = 0.149)".

> "no correlations were observed between the task performance relating to laparoscopic suturing skills and other more basic laparoscopic operative skills (basic operative skills on electrosurgical hook knife dissection, clipping, scissor cutting, port insertion, applying endoloop) before (r = 0.193; P = 0.149)"
