The review reports, citing Durlak et al. (2022)'s review of meta-analyses of educational interventions, "notable improvements in attitudes (d = 0.07 to d = 0.93), self-regulation (d = 0.00 to d = 0.46), prosocial behaviour (d = 0.11 to d = 0.39)"; academic performance effects ranged d = 0.18 to 0.46.
