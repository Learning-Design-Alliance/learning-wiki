Evidence¶

Serkan Perkmen 2026¶

Serkan Perkmen, Sara Dexter. (2026). From sources of self-efficacy to technology integration intentions: a new social cognitive model. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10623-0

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Path analysis and hierarchical regression with 144 Turkish preservice teachers; hierarchical regression showed self-efficacy and outcome expectations accounted for 50% of intention variance, sources of self-efficacy added 3%, and interests added 2% (R² = 0.55 in the final step).

"Variables in the Social Cognitive Model of Technology Integration accounted for 55% of variation in preservice teachers' intentions to use educational technology in their future classrooms. This is over twice the amount of variation Buabeng-Andoh et al. ( 2019) found could be explained by some other models"
