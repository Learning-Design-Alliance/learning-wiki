## Evidence

### Var Eren 2025

Var Eren, Cam Yaren, Pektas Eren, Kuden Cihan. (2025). Does preoperative anxiety influence endodontic skill performance and students' subjective perceptions in an observational simulation-based study?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07957-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of ten self-reported items between anxiety groups during mandibular root canal treatment (n = 93). Only confidence differed significantly (U = 601.0, Z = −2.208, p = 0.027); all other difficulty, comprehension, and focus items showed p > 0.05.

> "The only significant difference was observed for confidence, with students in the low-anxiety group (mean rank = 55.87) outperforming those in the high-anxiety group (mean rank = 44.09), despite both reporting the same median score (2 [IQR = 2–3]) (U = 601.0, Z = − 2.208, p = 0.027)."
