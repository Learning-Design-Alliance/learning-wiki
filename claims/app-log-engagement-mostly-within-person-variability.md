## Evidence

### Breitwieser 2026

Breitwieser, J., Brod, G., & Malmberg, L.-E. (2026). Testing a three-phase model of the relationship between motivational appraisals, observable engagement, and subjective engagement: Insights from combining mobile learning data and daily diaries. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0001009

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of log data and daily diaries from 189 fifth graders over up to 32 days. The ICC for vocabulary engagement was .12, with the article reporting "the greatest proportion of variability within students (88%)"; self-reports showed 34–61% within-student variance.

> "For satisfaction and task delay, a larger proportion of the variance was at the within-student level (61%). Vocabulary engagement showed the greatest proportion of variability within students (88%)."
