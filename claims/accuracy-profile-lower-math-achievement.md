## Evidence

### Bosma et al. 2017

Tirza Bosma, Claire E. Stevenson, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2017). Differences in Need for Instruction: Dynamic Testing in Children with Arithmetic Difficulties. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 6. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2326

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis analysis comparing the four instruction profiles on cognitive functioning and achievement measures; only the CITO math test differentiated the profiles. Mann-Whitney follow-up showed the Accuracy profile "had significantly lower levels on the CITO-math achievement task" than the General profile (z = -2.95, p = .003).

> "A significant difference between the children allocated to the four profiles was found for the initial CITO- math achievement test: X2 (3) = 9. 12, p = .028."
