Hernández-González Samuel, Gómez-Climent María Ángeles, Clemente-Soriano Albert, Alba Robles Jose Luis, Caurín-Alonso Carlos, Solbes Jordi (2026). Reduced cognitive empathy and differences in EEG activity in low-achieving adolescents. PLOS One 21(10): e0361520. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0361520

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Partial Spearman correlation analyses controlling for age, sex, and group membership in the full behavioural sample (n = 40). The distinctive term cognitive empathy appears inside; global and affective empathy coefficients were near zero and non-significant.

> "Partial Spearman correlations controlling for group, age, and sex showed that FER performance was positively associated with cognitive empathy after FDR correction (ρ = 0.411, p = .0114, FDR-adjusted p = 0.034). FER performance was not significantly associated with global empathy (ρ = 0.112, FDR-adjusted p = 0.508) or affective empathy (ρ = −0.008, FDR-adjusted p = 0.960)."
