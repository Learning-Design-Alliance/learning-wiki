# Registered children's nurses who completed the co-produced digital educational intervention showed a statistically significant increase in knowledge of self-harm in CYP (Cohen's d = 0.69 in completers)

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Among intervention completers (n=33), knowledge of self-harm in CYP increased significantly postintervention, with an increased effect size retained in sensitivity analysis (d = 0.69). [→ Manning Joseph C 2017](#manning-joseph-c-2017)

## Evidence

### Manning Joseph C 2017

Manning Joseph C, Carter Tim, Latif Asam, Horsley Angela, Cooper Joanne, Armstrong Marie, Crew Jamie, Wood Damian, Callaghan Patrick, Wharrad Heather. (2017). 'Our Care through Our Eyes'. Impact of a co-produced digital educational programme on nurses' knowledge, confidence and attitudes in providing care for children and young people who have self-harmed: a mixed-methods study in the UK. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014750

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Uncontrolled pre–post study at a single UK tertiary children's hospital; paired t-test on intervention completers. Knowledge rose from a mean of 6.69 (2.95) to 8.67 (1.96), "0.000**", Cohen's d = 0.69.
