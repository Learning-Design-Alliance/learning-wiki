## Evidence

### Loo Kang WEE and Giam Hwee GOH 2012

Loo Kang WEE and Giam Hwee GOH. (2012). Geostationary Earth Orbit Satellite Model using Easy Java Simulation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1212.3863](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1212.3863)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative survey excerpts and informal interviews from the laboratory lessons; one student theme was improved 3D visualization from different perspectives, e.g. "the 3D simulation allows me to learn better" compared with 2D textbook diagrams. No effect sizes are reported.

> "The lesson allows me to understand the movement of a satellite which we cannot see normally in real life and are unable to comprehend from the 2D <textbook> diagram. Thus, the 3D simulation allows me to learn better"
