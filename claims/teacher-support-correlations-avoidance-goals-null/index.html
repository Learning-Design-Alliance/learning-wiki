Preliminary Pearson correlation analysis of all study variables (Table 1) showed consistent positive associations among major variables; teacher support correlated with engagement (r = 0.435), mastery-approach (r = 0.304), and performance-approach (r = 0.277), while "teacher support was not significantly related to either mastery-avoidance or performance-avoidance goals".

"However, teacher support was not significantly related to either mastery-avoidance or performance-avoidance goals, and the relationship between mastery-avoidance and performance-approach was weak."
