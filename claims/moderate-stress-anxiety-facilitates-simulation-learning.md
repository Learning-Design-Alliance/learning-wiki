## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Madsgaard 2022

Madsgaard, A., Smith‐Strøm, H., Hunskår, I., & Røykenes, K. (2022). A rollercoaster of emotions: An integrative review of emotions and its impact on health professional students' learning in simulation‐based education. Nursing Open, 9, 108–121. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1100

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis found that "moderate stress and anxiety have a positive effect and can facilitate students' learning", citing Behrens et al. (2019), Ko and Choi (2020), Groot et al. (2020) and Al-Ghareeb et al. (2019).

> "However, our results also found that moderate stress and anxiety have a positive effect and can facilitate students' learning because the anxiety associated with SBE improves students' preparedness"

### Madsgaard 2022 (2)

Madsgaard, A., Smith‐Strøm, H., Hunskår, I., & Røykenes, K. (2022). A rollercoaster of emotions: An integrative review of emotions and its impact on health professional students' learning in simulation‐based education. Nursing Open, 9, 108–121. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.1100

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review describes dissonance in Beischel (2013), a mixed-methods study of 124 nursing students: quantitative data indicated anxiety did not mediate cognitive learning outcomes, while qualitative data reported increased anxiety negatively affecting learning.

> "The quantitative aspect of their study found that anxiety does not influence the learning process, whereas the qualitative aspect found that anxiety prevents learning."
