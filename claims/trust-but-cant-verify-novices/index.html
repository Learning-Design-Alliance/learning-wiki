Evidence¶

Liu 2026¶

Liu, D., Fan, G., & Pan, L. (2026). Tool, tutor, or crutch?: A grounded theory of cognitive scaffolding and offloading in AI-assisted programming education. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00592-w

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview and log analysis of self-identified beginners in the AI-enabled section. Logs showed novices accepting multi-line AI code using unfamiliar APIs and later conducting long debugging sessions with commit messages like "another fix". Novices in the human-pair contrast group instead engaged in direct clarification-seeking with partners.

"These students possessed enough knowledge to understand the AI was fallible but lacked the deeper expertise to confidently assess its correctness or efficiency."
