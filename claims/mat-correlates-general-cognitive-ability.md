## Evidence

### Kuncel et al. 2004

Nathan R. Kuncel, Sarah A. Hezlett, and Deniz S. Ones. (2004). Academic Performance, Career Potential, Creativity, and Job Performance: Can One Construct Predict Them All? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.86.1.148

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of MAT correlations with tests classified as assessing g and reasoning (Table 1), corrected for artifacts. The printed correlation effect size is ρ = .75 across k = 15 studies and N = 1,753, with SDρ = .15, slightly larger than for verbal and math categories.

> "Our results indicate that the MAT is also strongly related to performance on other general cognitive ability and reasoning tests (ρ ⫽ .75, k ⫽ 15, N ⫽ 1,753). The associated SDρ is slightly larger (.15)."
