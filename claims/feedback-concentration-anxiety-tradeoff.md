## Evidence

### Ahlborg Liv 2015

Ahlborg Liv, Weurlander Maria, Hedman Leif, Nisell Henry, Lindqvist Pelle G., Felländer-Tsai Li, Enochsson Lars. (2015). Individualized feedback during simulated laparoscopic training: a mixed methods study. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.55a2.218b

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis of focus group interviews with eleven students (five to six per group), held one to seven weeks after training. Students in the feedback group felt "a pressure to do well" while control students found the training exciting.

> "Participants in the control group expressed that they had fun, whereas participants in the feedback group were more concentrated on the task and also more anxious."
