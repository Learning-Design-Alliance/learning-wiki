`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A lecturer in the interview data described inadequate laboratory time and space, stating the skills laboratory "can only hold about 20 students at a time," and advised management to build a purposefully equipped simulation centre.

> "we have over 1,500 students who would want to practice some skills in the skills laboratory which can only hold about 20 students at a time"

### Mwalabu Gertrude 2024 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

A senior lecturer cited two SBE training sessions and examples from South Africa and Tanzania, noting "Our students lack simulation corners for skills acquisition after theory block" and urging liaison with hospital management for space.

> "Simulation corners in health facilities can significantly help in skill acquisition."
