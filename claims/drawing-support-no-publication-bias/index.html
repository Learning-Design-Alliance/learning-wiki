Bias screening in the meta-analysis (Table 2 reports Egger intercepts of 0.57 for comprehension, p = 0.245, and 0.71 for transfer, p = 0.532). The article nonetheless notes the transfer funnel plot appears asymmetrical, so inferences on publication bias cannot be drawn with high confidence.
