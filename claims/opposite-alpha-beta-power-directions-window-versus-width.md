## Evidence

### Jesus G. Cruz-Garza 2021

Jesus G. Cruz-Garza, Michael Darfler, James D. Rounds, Elita Gao, Saleh Kalantari. (2021). EEG-based Investigation of the Impact of Classroom Design on Cognitive Performance of Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.03629](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.03629)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the significant-feature analysis of the EEG dataset, the One-Window comparison showed an increase in relative alpha and beta power; the article states that "In contrast, the Neutral vs. Two Windows, and the Neutral vs Wide, both showed a decrease in relative alpha and beta power in these same regions."

> "For the Neutral vs. One-Window comparison, the EEG band-power feature changes were characterized by an increase in relative alpha and beta power in the centro-parietal and frontal regions of the scalp."
