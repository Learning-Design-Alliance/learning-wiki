# The structural group retained significantly more vocabulary than the contextual group on the delayed retention test, except among high achievers

> **Currency** · newest study 2011 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On the retention test the whole structural group (M = 61.85) scored significantly higher than the contextual group (M = 51.78), t(54) = 2.99, p = .004. [→ Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini 2011](#malik-behlol-mohammad-munir-kaini-2011)
`q3 i?` High achievers taught with the contextual method retained more (M = 76.5) than high achievers taught with the structural method (M = 66.33), t(10) = 4.03, p = .002. [→ Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini 2011 (2)](#malik-behlol-mohammad-munir-kaini-2011-2)

## Evidence

### Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini 2011

Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini. (2011). Comparative Effectiveness of Contextual and Structural Method of Teaching Vocabulary. English Language Teaching, 4(1). http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Delayed retention test administered to control the interaction of time of measurement and treatment; whole-group means were "51.78 and that of group B was 61.85", p = .004 reported as significant.

> "The mean of the group A was found 51.78 and that of group B was 61.85"

### Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini 2011 (2)

Malik Behlol & Mohammad Munir Kaini. (2011). Comparative Effectiveness of Contextual and Structural Method of Teaching Vocabulary. English Language Teaching, 4(1). http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retention-test comparison of high-achiever subgroups; the contextual group's high achievers scored "76.5 and 66.33" for groups A and B, p = .002 reported as significant, reversing the whole-group direction.

> "the mean of the high achievers of the group A and group B were 76.5 and 66.33"
