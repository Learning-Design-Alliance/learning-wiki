## Evidence

### Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox 2025

Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2025). Motivating reflection in problem solving: homework corrections in upper-division physics courses. [doi:10.1103/ntvp-spkc](https://doi.org/10.1103/ntvp-spkc)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Within-case analysis of Ari's semester of homework submissions and think-aloud interview in Instructor A's QM2 course. The summary describes comments "not within the HWCs framework" including reflections and confusion communicated to the grader, not written to earn points back.

> "He writes many comments that are not within the HWCs framework, in- cluding reﬂections on his work and additional informa- tion prompted by the solutions; he commonly reﬂects on his level of understanding of the content, communi- cating when he is still confused about a topic."
