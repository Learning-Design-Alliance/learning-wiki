### Hemi 2025

Hemi, A., Madjar, N., Rich, Y., & Daumiller, M. (2025). Relationships between students' achievement goals and social positioning in the classroom. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 1047–1062. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12762

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model-fit comparison (AIC, BIC, loglikelihood, deviance) across the four two-level models for 472 students showed significant improvement with random effects. Exemplary classrooms illustrated in Figures 1 and 2 show slope differences (e.g. 6.57 vs 1.99 for mastery approach–out-degree), with the figure text noting this "warranting continued investigation into contextual factors possibly moderating this relationship".

> "The models that included random effects had a significantly better fit to the data than the models that included only fixed effects for all independent variables (in‐degree, out‐degree, closeness, betweenness)."
