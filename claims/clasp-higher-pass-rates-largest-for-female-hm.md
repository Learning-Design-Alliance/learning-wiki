### Cassandra A. Paul 2025

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Logistic-regression comparison of pass rates (grade C- or better) among non-dropping students at NU (N = 3095) and OU (N = 8004); all odds ratios exceeded one with most P < 10^-3 (Table IV).

> "At Next University we see an effect size of about 21 more students per year passing, and at Original University we see about 53 more students passing per year."

### Cassandra A. Paul 2025 (2)

Cassandra A. Paul, David J. Webb. (2025). Course Deficit Model and the CLASP curriculum: Examining equity and graduation rates at two institutions. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010107)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-demographic-group fits of pass odds (Equity of Individuality) show the largest odds ratios for female historically marginalized students, "about 3 times higher odds of passing CLASP at NU" and about six times at OU (Table IV).

> "The largest odds ratio across both universities is for female students from historically marginalized groups who had about 3 times higher odds of passing CLASP at NU and about 6 times higher odds of passing CLASP at OU."
