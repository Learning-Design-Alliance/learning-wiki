## Evidence

### Lin Chen-Ta 2025

Lin Chen-Ta, Liang Sheng-Fu & Shaw Fu-Zen. (2025). Better sleep is associated with higher academic performance from an actigraphy-based analysis of sleep consistency and grades in college students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-33775-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis across the 33 participants between final exam scores and 14-day actigraphy sleep parameters (Fig. 2). "A significant positive correlation was found" for SE and negative for SOL; significant negative correlations also held for the MADs of SE, SOL, and TST.

> "A significant positive correlation was found between the final exam score and SE. A significant negative correlation was observed between the final exam score and SOL. No significant correlation was found between the final exam score and WASO or TST."
