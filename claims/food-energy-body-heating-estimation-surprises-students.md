`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Classroom estimation activity described by the author: equating 3000 kcal with calorimetric heating of an 80 kg water-equivalent body gives ∆T ≈ +37.5°C. Students are "normally quite surprised at this number", and discussion covers why the estimate fails (fat storage is ignored) and diet-induced thermogenesis.

> "Students are normally quite surprised at this number. Although wildly unrealistic, ∆T ≈ +6◦C is typically fatal, there is a related phenomena of diet-induced thermogenesis"
