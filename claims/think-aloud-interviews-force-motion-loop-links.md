John M. Aiken, Marcos D. Caballero, Scott S. Douglas, John B. Burk, Erin M. Scanlon, Brian D. Thoms, Michael F. Schatz. (2012). Understanding Student Computational Thinking with Computational Modeling. [doi:10.1063/1.4789648](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789648)
