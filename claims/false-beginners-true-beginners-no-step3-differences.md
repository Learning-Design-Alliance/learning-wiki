# False beginners and true beginners show no significant differences on any STEP 3 written test item

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` According to the STEP 3 written test, the two groups' English competences are the same, with no significant differences on any testing item. [→ Nakamura 1997](#nakamura-1997)

## Evidence

### Nakamura 1997

Nakamura, Tomoko. (1997). What Makes Language Learners False Beginners? Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the Japan Association for Language Teaching. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED420194

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Written-test comparison of true beginners and false beginners taking a STEP 3 test paper under the same conditions; the article reports no significant differences between the two groups on any testing item, so "the two groups' competences in the English language are the same" by this measure.

> "From the results shown in Table 1, it can be said that there are no significant differences between the two groups in regard to any of the testing items."
