Peter Kahn, Lucy Everington, Kathleen Kelm, Iain Reid, Francine Watkins. (2017). Understanding student engagement in online learning environments: the role of reflexivity. Education Tech Research Dev, 65, 203-218. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9484-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview analysis of the eight-student sub-group; meta-reflexive concerns were identified on 35 occasions, most prevalently related to improvement of one's own learning in regularly-undertaken tasks.

> "Furthermore, uncertainty was seen to be a factor in 23 of the identiﬁed meta-reﬂexive concerns, linked by the participants to tasks that were perceived as challenging."
