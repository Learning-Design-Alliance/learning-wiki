# GLAT shows robust structural validity and reliability under a 2-parameter logistic IRT model

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The 2PL model fit better than the Rasch model, while the 3PL model did not significantly improve fit over the 2PL model. [→ Yueqiao Jin 2024](#yueqiao-jin-2024)

## Evidence

### Yueqiao Jin 2024

Yueqiao Jin, Roberto Martinez-Maldonado, Dragan Gašević and Lixiang Yan. (2024). GLAT: The Generative AI Literacy Assessment Test. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00283 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2411.00283](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2411.00283)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Likelihood-ratio tests compared nested IRT models on the 355-student sample. The article reports "asignificantimprovement in fit with the 2PL model" (χ2 = 55.596, df = 19, p < 0.001); the 3PL did not improve on the 2PL (χ2 = 23.888, df = 20, p = 0.247).

### Yueqiao Jin 2024 (2)

Yueqiao Jin, Roberto Martinez-Maldonado, Dragan Gašević and Lixiang Yan. (2024). GLAT: The Generative AI Literacy Assessment Test. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00283

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article reports the final 2PL model's fit (M2 = 225.325, RMSEA 0.031, SRMSR 0.052, TLI 0.967, CFI 0.970) and reliability: "Cronbach's alpha = 0.80; omega total = 0.81", both above the 0.7 acceptability threshold.

> "resulting in a reliable 2-parameter logistic (2PL) model (Cronbach’s alpha = 0.80; omega total = 0.81) with a robust factor structure (RMSEA = 0.03; CFI = 0.97)"
