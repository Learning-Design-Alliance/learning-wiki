Torppa, M., Psyridou, M., Khanolainen, D., Koponen, T., & Salminen, J. (2025). Role of home math environment in the intergenerational transmission of math skills. Learning and Instruction, 97, Article 102080. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102080

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slope analyses of the significant formal HME × parental skill interactions (Fig. 3) in the latent interaction models, showing reversed association direction for lower-risk children across HME measurement ages.

> "Interestingly, however, the association was reversed for the children with lower risk: among them, a high level of HME-formal was associated with worse performance in arithmetic. The lower-risk children with less formal HME had the best arithmetic performance."
