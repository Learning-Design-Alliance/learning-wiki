# During training sessions, experimental-group students were more focused and autonomous while control-group students were more dispersed and often did not complete transfer tasks

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Observed reactions and teacher interviews showed experimental students involved, focused and completing all tasks, whereas some control students did not complete tasks and left transfer tasks incomplete. [→ Melo 2018](#melo-2018)

## Evidence

### Melo 2018

Melo, M. (2018). The 4C/ID-Model in Physics Education: Instructional Design of a Digital Learning Environment to Teach Electrical Circuits. International Journal of Instruction, 11(1), 103-122. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1118a

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative data from an observation sheet applied at the beginning and during the two sessions and from teacher interviews; teachers also reported control-group students were more dispersed and that some could not finish tasks autonomously.

> "The interviewed teachers reported that during the completion of the learning tasks the students of the experimental group were very focused and committed, always showing great care when reading the messages of the corrective and cognitive feedback system."
