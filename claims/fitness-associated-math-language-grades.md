Kjellenberg K, Wang R, Horre JN, Helgadóttir B, Ekblom Ö, Singh A, Nyberg G. (2026). Associations between physical activity, fitness, cognitive and academic performance in Swedish adolescents: Findings from a cross-sectional study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0344087

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional multilevel models (Table 3, adjusted rows) show fitness with math b = 0.18 (0.09, 0.26), β = 0.18 and language b = 0.16 (0.07, 0.25), β = 0.13, both significant at the adjusted alpha of 0.018. Grades were final grade-6 grades from official records, T-scored.

> "Furthermore, a significant association was observed between fitness and academic performance (both math and language grades) in the adjusted models (Table 3)."
