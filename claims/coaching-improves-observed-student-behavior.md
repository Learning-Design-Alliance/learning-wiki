# Students in classrooms of coached teachers showed more cooperation, less noncooperation, and fewer socially disruptive behaviors than students in comparison classrooms

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Observers documented more favorable ratings of student cooperation in coached classrooms (β = 0.34, p = .03; Δ = 0.45). [→ Bradshaw 2018](#bradshaw-2018)

## Evidence

### Bradshaw 2018

Bradshaw, C. P., Pas, E. T., Bottiani, J. H., Debnam, K. J., Reinke, W. M., Herman, K. C., & Rosenberg, M. S. (2018). Promoting Cultural Responsivity and Student Engagement Through Double Check Coaching of Classroom Teachers: An Efficacy Study. School Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.17105/SPR-2017-0119.V47-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experimental contrast on ASSIST student outcomes from the RCT (N = 158 teachers), analyzed with two-level HLM. Average noncooperation tallies were 0.13 in coached classrooms versus 0.31 in noncoached classrooms; the article states students in coached classrooms demonstrated nearly one-half of a standard deviation greater compliance and fewer disruptive behaviors.

> "observers documented more favorable ratings of student cooperation (β = 0.34, p = .03; Δ = 0.45) in coached classrooms relative to noncoached classrooms."
