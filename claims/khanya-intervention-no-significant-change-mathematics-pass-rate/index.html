Evidence¶

Smith and Hardman 2014¶

Garth Spencer Smith and Joanne Hardman. (2014). The impact of computer and mathematics software usage on performance of school leavers in the Western Cape Province of South Africa: A comparative analysis. International Journal of Education and Development using Information and Communication Technology, Vol. 10, Issue 1, pp. 22-40. http://ijedict.dec.uwi.edu/viewarticle.php?id=1678

q2 · i? · causal · r1

For each of the 11 wave 4 and 5 schools, the total number of passes at Higher and Standard Grade level and total candidates were determined for 2003 and 2007 to calculate a percentage pass rate. A paired samples t-test found "no statistically significant change" in the pass percentage.

"The results of this test showed that there was no statistically significant change in the pass percentage from before Khanya (mean = 40.9; std deviation = 16.3) to after Khanya (mean = 35.8; std deviation = 17.4), t(10) = 0.878, p = 0.401"
