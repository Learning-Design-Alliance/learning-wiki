## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Average scores on 42 identical examination items improved from 82.9 without feedback to 88.4 with feedback (P = 0.001, η2 = 0.23). [→ Halliday N 2015](#halliday-n-2015)

## Evidence

### Halliday N 2015

Halliday N, O'Donoghue D, Klump KE, Thompson B. (2015). Human Structure in Six and One-Half Weeks: One Approach to Providing Foundational Anatomical Competency in an Era of Compressed Medical School Anatomy curricula. Anatomical Sciences Education 8(2):149-157. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.1476

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Item analysis of 42 identical summative examination items across the 2010-2011 (no feedback) and 2011-2012 (feedback) cohorts, analyzed with repeated measures ANOVA. The article reports average scores of 82.9 versus 88.4 and "P = 0.001, η2 = 0.23", a large effect by the article's own criteria.

> "Analysis indicated an improvement in average scores of the 42 examination items between the first (no feedback) and the second (feedback) iteration of the course (No feedback: average score = 82.9, 95% CI = 78.8–87.1; Feedback: average score = 88.4, 95% confidence interval = 85.3–91.6). This improvement was statistically and educationally significant (P = 0.001, η2 = 0.23)."
