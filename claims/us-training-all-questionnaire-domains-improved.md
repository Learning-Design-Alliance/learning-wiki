## Evidence

### Lin Xu-Nian 2026

Lin Xu-Nian, Lu Cheng-Wei, Lin Tzu-Yu, Chou San-Fang, Chang Ya-Ying. (2026). Rapid skill acquisition through task-specific ultrasound training for peripheral cannulation in medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09935-9

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on all five questionnaire items from 30 participants. Effect sizes r ranged from 0.67 to 0.81 (all P < 0.001). Internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha) was 0.80 pre-training and 0.88 post-training.

> "Analysis of pre- and post-training questionnaire data demonstrated significant improvements across all evaluated domains (Table 3). The internal consistency reliability was 0.80 before training and 0.88 after training, suggesting acceptable to good internal consistency."
