Latent profile analysis was applied to action-control belief scales (agency beliefs for ability and effort, control expectancy, means-ends beliefs for ability and luck) in a nationally representative sample. The article reports that "four motivational pro ﬁles emerged: agentic, moderate, avoidant, and mixed."

"Analyzing national-level data from Finnish ninth graders with and without SEN status (N = 6,942), four motivational pro ﬁles emerged: agentic, moderate, avoidant, and mixed."
