## Evidence

### Sato Hiroyasu 2025

Sato Hiroyasu, Ogasawara Katsuhiko, Sakurai Hidehiko. (2025). Performance Evaluation of 18 Generative AI Models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity) in 2024 Japanese Pharmacist Licensing Examination: Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/76925

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Subject-level analysis of the 107th JNLEP results across the four flagship models: chemistry accuracy averaged 51.3% and physics 76.3%, while all other subjects exceeded 80%.

> "In the 4 flagship models, the average accuracy based on subject was lowest for chemistry (10.3/20, 51.3%), followed by physics (15.3/20, 76.3%). All the other subjects achieved an accuracy exceeding 80%."

### Sato Hiroyasu 2025 (2)

Sato Hiroyasu, Ogasawara Katsuhiko, Sakurai Hidehiko. (2025). Performance Evaluation of 18 Generative AI Models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity) in 2024 Japanese Pharmacist Licensing Examination: Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/76925

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Question-type analysis (Table 4 reports chemical-structure mean 0.254, SD 0.213): chemical-structure items showed the lowest accuracy, and the small mean and SD across models indicated little improvement.

> "Among the diagram-based questions, those involving chemical structures exhibited the lowest accuracy. The small mean and SD across all models for chemistry indicate that the performance of the current models did not show a considerable improvement."
