Tan Nigel CK, Kandiah Nagaendran, Chan Yiong Huak, Umapathi Thirugnanam, Lee Sze Haur, Tan Kevin. (2011). A controlled study of team-based learning for undergraduate clinical neurology education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-91

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted mixed model ANCOVA analysis of the same 49-student modified crossover study at the 48-hour delayed post-test. The article reports "mean difference 8.1%" favoring TBL, and states the PL group scores peaked then dropped within 48 hours.

> "For post-test 2, the mean percentage change in the TBL group was even greater than in the PL group (13.0% vs 4.9%, mean difference 8.1%, 95%CI 3.7--12.5%, p = 0.001)"
