Evidence¶

Yun Jungmi 2023¶

Yun Jungmi, Lee Yun Ji, Kang Kyoungrim, Park Jongmin. (2023). Effectiveness of SBAR-based simulation programs for nursing students: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04495-8

q3 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of the 12 included studies' outcome measures, which included the CCS, GICC, VAS confidence, and critical thinking instruments. The review reports that "divergent results were obtained" across these non-clarity outcomes, with some individual studies reporting non-significant results (e.g., patient assessment, patient intervention, critical thinking in one RCT).

"Divergent results were obtained regarding communication ability, critical thinking, confidence, learning self-efficacy, and attitude toward patient safety."
