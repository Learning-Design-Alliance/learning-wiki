### Jessica Thompson 2020

Jessica Thompson, Simon White, Stephen Chapman. (2020). Interactive Clinical Avatar Use in Pharmacist Preregistration Training: Design and Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/17146

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive Likert results from the same 10-participant review of the EHC avatar simulation. "clinical reasoning skills being developed to a greater extent than problem-solving or decision-making skills"; no inferential test of the difference is reported.

> "The development of skills for future practice from the simulation was reported by 80% (8/10) of participants, with clinical reasoning skills being developed to a greater extent than problem-solving or decision-making skills (90% [9/10] vs 80% [8/10])."
