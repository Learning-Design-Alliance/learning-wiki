## Evidence

### Nevin Christa R 2014

Nevin Christa R, Westfall Andrew O, Rodriguez J Martin, Dempsey Donald M, Cherrington Andrea, Roy Brita, Patel Mukesh, Willig James H. (2014). Gamification as a tool for enhancing graduate medical education. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2013-132486

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Four focus groups of 17 residents who participated in round 1 of Kaizen-IM, audio recorded and transcribed, coded by two independent reviewers using a combined deductive/inductive approach. Residents named "the single most important motivator for participation identified was the leaderboard". Residents also valued immediate feedback and anonymous usernames.

> "The single most important motivator for participation identified was the leaderboard. Residents enjoyed the opportunity to compete with their peers as both individuals and teams."
