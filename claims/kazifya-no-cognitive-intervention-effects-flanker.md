## Evidence

### Minja 2025

Minja, E. G., Mrimi, E. C., Mponzi, W. P., Beckmann, J., Finda, M. F., Okumu, F. O., Long, K. Z., Lang, C., Utzinger, J., & Gerber, M. (2025). Effect of a School-Based Physical Activity and Multi-Micronutrient Supplementation Intervention on Cognitive Function and Academic Achievement Among Schoolchildren in Tanzania: Secondary Outcome from the KaziAfya Cluster-Randomized Controlled Trial. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 22(9), 1335. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22091335

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Adjusted mixed-effects linear regression with random intercepts for classes within schools, from the KaziAfya cluster-randomized placebo-controlled 2×2 factorial trial (analytic n = 559). The article reports "no significant effects" of any arm on Flanker accuracy.

> "Compared to the placebo group, no significant effects of the MMNS, PA, or the combined PA + MMNS interventions were observed on accuracy for either congruent or incongruent stimuli."

### Minja 2025 (2)

Minja, E. G., Mrimi, E. C., Mponzi, W. P., Beckmann, J., Finda, M. F., Okumu, F. O., Long, K. Z., Lang, C., Utzinger, J., & Gerber, M. (2025). Effect of a School-Based Physical Activity and Multi-Micronutrient Supplementation Intervention on Cognitive Function and Academic Achievement Among Schoolchildren in Tanzania: Secondary Outcome from the KaziAfya Cluster-Randomized Controlled Trial. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 22(9), 1335. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22091335

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same adjusted mixed-model analysis of the KaziAfya trial: reaction time on congruent and incongruent Flanker trials did not differ significantly between any intervention arm and placebo. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The intervention showed no significant effect on reaction time for either congruent or incongruent stimuli when compared to the placebo group."
