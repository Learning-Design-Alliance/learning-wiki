## Evidence

### Song Xiuli 2024

Song Xiuli, Hu Qian. (2024). The relationship between Freshman students' mental health and academic achievement: chain mediating effect of learning adaptation and academic self-efficacy. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-20738-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same 5000-resample bootstrap mediation analysis (Table 3) on the freshman survey. The academic self-efficacy path is significant "with a magnitude of -0.002"; the direct effect was -0.009 with a confidence interval including 0, suggesting full mediation.

> "The confidence interval of “mental health →academic self-efficacy →academic achievement” does not include 0, and the mediating path is significant with a magnitude of -0.002, indicating that academic self-efficacy plays a mediating role between mental health and academic achievement."
