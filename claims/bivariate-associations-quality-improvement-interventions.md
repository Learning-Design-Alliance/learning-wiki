`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate logistic regressions preceding the multivariate models in the Results section of the Oromia survey. All three printed odds ratios and confidence intervals are reported as printed; the supervision association was not significant.

> "Bivariate analyses showed that PRCMM (OR = 6.45, 95% CI 2.22–18.73) and follow–up training (OR = 2.25, 95% CI 1.24–4.07) were significantly associated with the outcome. Supervision on iCCM (OR = 1.15, 95% CI 0.44–2.99) was not significantly associated with correct management."
