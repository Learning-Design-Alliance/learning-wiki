Evidence¶

S. Andrew Garbacz 2015¶

S. Andrew Garbacz, Susan M. Sheridan, Natalie A. Koziol, Kyongboon Kwon, Shannon R. Holmes. (2015). Congruence in Parent–Teacher Communication: Implications for the Efficacy of CBC for Students With Behavioral Concerns. School Psychology Review, 44(2), 150–168. https://www.nasponline.org/publications/periodicals/spr

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Secondary moderation analysis of a randomized CBC trial using three-level (time, dyad, classroom) random-intercept models in SAS GLIMMIX with 166 children and 74 teachers. The article reports the three-way interaction "( /H9252ˆ /H110053.18, SE /H110051.34, p /H11005.019)" for teacher-reported social skills; no standardized effect size is printed for the interaction.
