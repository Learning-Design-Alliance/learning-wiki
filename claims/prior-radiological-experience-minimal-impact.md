`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

GEE analyses including years of prior radiological experience (median 2.7 years among the 10 radiology trainees) and case number as fixed effects. The 95% CI for PI-RADSv2.1 includes 1; the EPE effect (OR per year 1.11 [1.03, 1.24]) was small but significant, and both groups reached similar final EPE agreement.

> "Prior radiological training was not associated with higher agreement in PI-RADSv2.1 assessment (OR per year 1.06, 95% CI: 0.96, 1.16)."
