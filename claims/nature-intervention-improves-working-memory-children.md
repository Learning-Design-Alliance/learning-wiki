## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Two reviewed studies found a significant positive effect of nature on working memory (Digit Span tasks) relative to control conditions, one with active play in a green school playground and one with a passive green window view. ## Evidence

### Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska 2022

Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick · Krystyna Gilowska. (2022). Effects of Nature (Greenspace) on Cognitive Functioning in School Children and Adolescents: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review (2022) 34:1217–1254. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09658-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of two included experiments (Amicone et al. 2018, Rome; Li & Sullivan 2016, Illinois), both using Digit Span Forward and Backward. The review reports "a significant positive effect of nature on working memory performance" in both, with a 30-min green playground and a 10-min green window view.

> "These studies indicate that relative to control conditions, two different nature interventions (one immersive and one passive) involving different age groups can improve cognitive performance that relies on the ability to hold and manipulate information in short-term (working) memory."
