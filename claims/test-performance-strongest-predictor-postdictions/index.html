Hierarchical regression analysis of Study 1 local judgments in the mathematics test, with gender, high school grade, and performance in step 1 and motivational/personality variables in step 2. The analysis found "Performance was found to be the strongest predictor for the judgments."

"Performance was found to be the strongest predictor for the judgments."

Marion Händel & Anique B. H. de Bruin & Markus Dresel 2020 (2)¶

Marion Händel & Anique B. H. de Bruin & Markus Dresel (2020). Individual differences in local and global metacognitive judgments. Metacognition and Learning (2020) 15:51–75. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09220-0

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Study 2 hierarchical regression on global postdictions in a psychology exam replicated Study 1: "Test performance was again the strongest predictor, followed by domain-specific self-concept". No significant relations with gender or high school grade were found.

"The hierarchical regression analysis to explain variance in the global postdiction replicated the prior results: Test performance was again the strongest predictor, followed by domain-specific self-concept"
