### Sarıköse Seda 2026

Sarıköse Seda, Sevim Fatma, Alık Şebnem, Karaçay Pelin, Yaşar Özgen. (2026). Integrating Insights: A Mixed‐Methods Approach to Nurses' Competencies in Evidence‐Based Practice and Clinical Decision‐Making. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70525

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple linear regression (model 4) on 387 nurses' CDMNS scores; adjusted R² = 0.567 as printed. EBP competence was "the largest contribution with an increase of 0.60 points" per the article.

> "increasing education (β = 0.275; p < 0.001) and EBP‐COQ level (β = 0.603; p < 0.001) and experience in research planning and implementation (β = 0.173; p < 0.001) positively affected nurses' CDM"
