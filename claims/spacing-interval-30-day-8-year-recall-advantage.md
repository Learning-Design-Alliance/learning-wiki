`q3 i?` The intersession interval between relearning sessions has a very important effect on recall eight years later, with the 30-day interval yielding 2.5 times the recall probability of the zero interval. [→ Bahrick 1987](#bahrick-1987)

## Evidence

### Bahrick 1987

Bahrick, H. P., & Phelps, E. (1987). Retention of Spanish Vocabulary Over 8 Years. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition, 13(2), 344-349. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/xlm/13/2/344

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Longitudinal follow-up recall test administered to original participants after eight years, comparing primary intersession training intervals; the Results report that 'The recall probability associated with the 30-day interval is 2.5 times the probability associated with the zero interval.'

> "The data show that the intersession interval has a very impor- tant effect on retention after eight years. The recall probability associated with the 30-day interval is 2.5 times the probability associated with the zero interval."
