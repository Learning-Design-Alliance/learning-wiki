Sina Rismanchian & Shayan Doroudi 2025¶

Sina Rismanchian & Shayan Doroudi. (2025). The Evolution of Research on AI and Education Across Four Decades: Insights from the AIxEd Framework. Int J Artif Intell Educ 35:2797–2820. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00483-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

Analysis of AIED 2024 proceedings positioned on the AI×Ed end-user axis. The authors report the middle-top cluster grew and that 60% of its papers used LLMs, citing teacher-facing chatbots for diagnosing problem behavior, LLM-based test-quality tools, and curriculum-teaching facilitation tools.

"As shown in Fig. 2g, the number of papers in the middle of the x-axis has increased. Specifically, more papers lie in the middle top cluster. This might be explained by the percentage of papers that use LLMs in this cluster (60%)."
