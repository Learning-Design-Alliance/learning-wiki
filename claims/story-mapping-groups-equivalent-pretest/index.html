Evidence¶

Salem Saleh Khalaf Ibnian 2010¶

Salem Saleh Khalaf Ibnian. (2010). The Effect of Using the Story-Mapping Technique on Developing Tenth Grade Students' Short Story Writing Skills in EFL. English Language Teaching, Vol. 3, No. 4. http://www.ccsenet.org/elt

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-test equivalence check on both groups (42 each) before treatment. Table 5 prints means of 30.91 (experimental) and 30.37 (control), T = 0.392, p = 0.697; no effect size is printed, so the non-significant result is reported without one.

"Results of the test showed that the two groups were equivalent in their short story writing skills. There was no statistically significant difference between the mean scores of both groups"
