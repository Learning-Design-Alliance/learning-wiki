## Evidence

### Shakir T 2026

Shakir T, Lingam G, Boal M, Francis N, Chand M. (2026). Technology-enhanced training in basic robotic surgical skills: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's own synthesis mapped all 17 included studies' reported outcomes onto the Kirkpatrick model; sixteen studies reported level 2 outcomes such as simulator scores and validated rating tools, and one single-arm study reported only Likert-based usability ratings.

> "No study reported level 3 (transfer to operative behaviour) or level 4 (patient-level) outcomes."

### Shakir T 2026 (2)

Shakir T, Lingam G, Boal M, Francis N, Chand M. (2026). Technology-enhanced training in basic robotic surgical skills: a systematic review. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's GRADE appraisal (Supplementary Appendix S2) of certainty per outcome domain, reported in the risk-of-bias and certainty section. No included study provided evidence on operative-skill transfer or patient-level outcomes.

> "the certainty of evidence was low for composite simulator score, force application, and learning-curve outcomes, and very low for TTC, kinematic precision, validated rating-tool scores, skill retention, and user acceptability."
