## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students using retrieval practice outperformed concept-mapping students on verbatim and inference questions and on a concept-mapping final test. [→ Brame CJ 2015](#brame-cj-2015)

## Evidence

### Brame CJ 2015

Brame CJ, Biel R. (2015). Test-Enhanced Learning: The Potential for Testing to Promote Greater Learning in Undergraduate Science Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-11-0208

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Karpicke and Blunt (2011), an experiment in which undergraduates studied a science text under four conditions including elaborative concept mapping and retrieval practice. The review adds that "84% (101/120) students performed better on the final tests when they used retrieval practice."

> "The authors observed that retrieval practice produced better performance than did elaborative study using concept mapping for both types of texts (enumeration texts and sequence texts) and on both types of final tests (short answer and concept mapping)."
