# Prior genAI use shows some evidence of poorer subsequent unaided reasoning, confined to the no-incentive condition

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 causal `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Within the no-incentive condition, participants with prior genAI access had lower odds of answering a Stage 2 network-reasoning question correctly than participants who had worked without genAI. [→ Dawid Ratajczyk 2026](#dawid-ratajczyk-2026)
`q3 i?` The preregistered test found no significant main effect of prior genAI access on Stage 2 accuracy. [→ Dawid Ratajczyk 2026 (2)](#dawid-ratajczyk-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Dawid Ratajczyk 2026

Dawid Ratajczyk, Agata Dymarska, Adam Matłoka, Michał Tomczyk, Małgorzata Wiącek. (2026). Thinking With AI, Reasoning Without It: Cognitive Offloading to Generative AI Weakens Effort Regulation. Preprint. https://osf.io/59qtm/ [doi:10.31234/osf.io/83jum_v1](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/83jum_v1)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory item-level mixed-effects logistic regression on Stage 2 accuracy in the preregistered lab experiment. The quoted estimate shows prior genAI users had OR = 0.68, approximately 32% lower odds of a correct response in the no-incentive condition.

> "Within the no-incentive reference condition, prior genAI access was associated with lower odds of answering a Stage 2 question correctly (𝑏= −0.392,𝑆𝐸= 0.195,𝑧= −2.01,𝑝=.045,𝑂𝑅= 0.68)."

### Dawid Ratajczyk 2026 (2)

Dawid Ratajczyk, Agata Dymarska, Adam Matłoka, Michał Tomczyk, Małgorzata Wiącek. (2026). Thinking With AI, Reasoning Without It: Cognitive Offloading to Generative AI Weakens Effort Regulation. Preprint. https://osf.io/59qtm/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered 2×2 ANOVA on Stage 2 network-reasoning accuracy in the same lab experiment. The quoted tests show no significant genAI, incentive, or interaction effects, so H1a, H1b and H4 were not supported.

> "There was no significant main effect of prior genAI access (𝐹(1,153) = 1.84,𝑝=.177,𝜂 2 𝑝 =.011), no significant main effect of incentives (𝐹(1,153) = 1.14,𝑝=.287,𝜂 2 𝑝 =.007), and no significant interaction between prior genAI access and incentives (𝐹(1,153) = 2.22,𝑝=.138,𝜂 2 𝑝 =.014)."
