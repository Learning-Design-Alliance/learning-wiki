Kerr Brendan, Hawkins Trisha Lee-Ann, Herman Robert, Barnes Sue, Kaufmann Stephanie, Fraser Kristin, Ma Irene W. Y. (2013). Feasibility of scenario-based simulation training versus traditional workshops in continuing medical education: a randomized controlled trial. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v18i0.21312

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nine-item satisfaction survey (traditional N=10, simulation N=17) showed higher means for the simulation group on all items, with printed p-values ranging from 0.12 to 0.88; the article reports "none of these differences were significant".

> "Although satisfaction with the workshop was higher for all items for the simulation group (Table 3), none of these differences were significant."
