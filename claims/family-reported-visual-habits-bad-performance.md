## Evidence

### Alvarez-Peregrina Cristina 2020

Alvarez-Peregrina Cristina, Sánchez-Tena Miguel Ángel, Andreu-Vázquez Cristina, Villa-Collar Cesar. (2020). Visual Health and Academic Performance in School-Aged Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17072346

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Family survey in the screening study; Table 3 prints ORs for bad versus good performance, e.g. OR = 4.20 (3.62–4.89) for getting up frequently during homework and OR = 0.16 (0.14–0.19) for liking to read, p < 0.001.

> "a higher percentage of the families of the group of children with bad academic performance think that their children do not see properly, get close to TV or paper, get up frequently when they do their homework, and that their children's eyes become red after a long visual effort."
