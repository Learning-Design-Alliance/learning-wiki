### Gove 2017

Gove, A., Brunette, T., Bulat, J., Carrol, B., Henny, C., Macon, W., Nderu, E., & Sitabkhan, Y. (2017). Assessing the impact of early learning programs in Africa. New Directions for Child and Adolescent Development, 158, 25–41. https://doi.org/10.1002/cad.20224

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

An external evaluation (Management Sciences International, 2017) of Kenya's nationally scaled Tusome program. Kiswahili nonreaders declined from 43% to 19% while fluent readers increased from 4% to 12%; the article separately reports effect sizes on targeted skills of between 0.4 and 2.57 SDs, but no single effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "results from a recent external evaluation of Tusome revealed a reduction in grade 2 nonreaders in English from 38% to 12%, and an increase in pupils reading fluently from 12% to 47%."
