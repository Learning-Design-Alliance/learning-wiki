### Hengeveld B 2021

Hengeveld B, Maaskant JM, Lindeboom R, Marshall AP, Vermeulen H, Eskes AM. (2021). Nursing competencies for family‐centred care in the hospital setting: A multinational Q‐methodology study. Journal of Advanced Nursing, 77(4), 1783–1799. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.14719

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Integrative review (Phase 1) searching MEDLINE, CINAHL, ERIC and Cochrane up to 5 July 2018. The authors report the search "yielded 2,366 hits of which 43 full‐text publications were included"; 594 competencies were extracted independently, reduced to 315, then 96, and finally 72 after grouping with CanMEDS key concepts.
