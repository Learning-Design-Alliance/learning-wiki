Yamada 2024¶

E4 Center, Northwestern University. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2024/06/25/new-normal-in-early-elementary-mathematics-learning-part-ii-school-level-variation-in-growth-in-grades-1-and-2/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal transition analysis of 6,274 schools present in both grade levels (Figure 1). Conditional maintenance rates were 22% for overperformers versus 12% (average) and 9% (underperformer); overperformer maintainers were "only 3% of the total number of schools".

"Although schools classified as overperformers in Grade 1 were more likely to maintain the outperforming status (22%) than others (12% for the average and 9% for the underperformer), they corresponded to only 3% of the total number of schools in this sample."
