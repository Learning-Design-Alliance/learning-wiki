## Evidence

### Kaito Kawakami 2024

Kaito Kawakami, Francesca Procopio, Kaili Rimfeld, Margherita Malanchini, Sophie von Stumm, Kathryn Asbury, Robert Plomin. (2024). Exploring the genetic prediction of academic underachievement and overachievement. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00251-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-validated (10-fold, three repeats) multiple regression using five cognition-related GPS to build a weighted multi-GPS GPAΔ in the TEDS sample. The multi-GPS version did not improve prediction over EA4 alone; the authors attribute this to prediction being optimized for age-7 achievement.

> "We found that the multi-GPS model predicted achievement no better than the EA4 GPS alone."
