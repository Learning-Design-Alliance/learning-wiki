## Evidence

### Wiingreen R 2025

Wiingreen R, Greisen G, Esbjørn BH, Løkkegaard ECL, Torp‐Pedersen C, Sørensen KK, Andersen MP, Hansen BM (2025). Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression among the 180 children who completed the final examination. Overall GPA rose from 3.9 (95% CI 2.7–5.0) in the IQ <−2 SD group to 9.1 (95% CI 8.3–9.8) in the >+1 SD group, with parallel gradients for language and mathematics.

> "A stepwise association between IQ and overall GPA was observed (Table 4). A similar association was found when evaluating domain‐specific performance"

### Wiingreen R 2025 (2)

Wiingreen R, Greisen G, Esbjørn BH, Løkkegaard ECL, Torp‐Pedersen C, Sørensen KK, Andersen MP, Hansen BM (2025). Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Comparison of domain-specific slope coefficients using linear regression with a cluster robust variance estimator found the IQ–mathematics gradient steeper than the IQ–language gradient. Only a p-value was printed for the slope comparison, so no effect size is reported.

> "The slope coefficient of mathematics was statistically significant (p = 0.03) steeper than the slope coefficient for language."
