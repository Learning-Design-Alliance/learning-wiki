## Evidence

### Punz A 2026

Punz A, Breakell T, Kühlein T, Harendza S, Kunisch R, Engel B. (2026). FamuPlus – a pilot project to support self-oriented learning through self-created learning outcomes in clinical electives. GMS Journal for Medical Education 43(6), Doc76. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume43/zma001870

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group data from the seven control students surveyed at the end of their electives: they "reported uncertainty about how to define or prioritize their learning" and wished they had pre-defined outcomes for future placements.

> "In contrast, students in the control group who had not participated in FamuPlus reported uncertainty about how to define or prioritize their learning during electives. Several expressed that the idea of pre-defining and discussing learning outcomes with clinical teachers would have been helpful for their future placements."
