Liu W 2026¶

Liu W (2026) AI-peer integrated feedback in second language writing classes: exploring students' engagement and writing performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1871951. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871951

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative analysis of interviews, reflective journals, and first versus revised drafts from 16 focal students per group, coded inductively. Control students predominantly confined themselves to direct correction or no correction; the experimental group showed a broader repertoire including substitution and addition, which the authors read as movement "beyond surface-level error fixing toward more substantive textual refinement".

Liu W 2026 (2)¶

Liu W (2026) AI-peer integrated feedback in second language writing classes: exploring students' engagement and writing performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1871951. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871951

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Interview and journal data from experimental-group focal students showed critical evaluation of AI feedback, memorization of metalinguistic explanations, connecting feedback to prior knowledge, and phase-based planning unique to the experimental group.

"Rather than accepting AI suggestions uncritically, they scrutinized their correctness and assessed whether the proposed changes aligned with their intended meaning."
