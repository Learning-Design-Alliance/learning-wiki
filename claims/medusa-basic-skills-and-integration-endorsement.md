Descriptive questionnaire results from the external validation with 40 neurosurgeons: instrument handling received a mean score of 3.78 and clip application 3.95. The abstract reports most participants agreed the simulator should be integrated into neurosurgical training and standard surgical workflows.

> "Most participants agreed that basic skills for instrument handling (mean score of 3.78) and clip application (mean score of 3.95) can be achieved using this simulator."
