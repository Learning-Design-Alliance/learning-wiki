### Merk 2018 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Level-2 paths of the same Study 2 multi-group multilevel path model M2; the article reports the domain-specific multiplism effect was "only signiﬁcant in the scientiﬁc study group" and no significant STEM-subject effects were found, with no effect sizes printed.

> "Interestingly, the predictive eﬀect of domain-speciﬁc multiplism on topic-speciﬁc multiplism was only signiﬁcant in the scientiﬁc study group (see bottom of Fig. 5; level 2). Furthermore, there were no signiﬁcant eﬀects of the amount of STEM subjects on domain- and topic-speciﬁc multiplism."
