## Evidence

### Yafeng Pan 2023

Yafeng Pan, Ning Hao, Ning Liu, Yijie Zhao, Xiaojun Cheng, Yixuan Ku and Yi Hu. (2023). Mnemonic-trained brain tuning to a regular odd-even pattern subserves digit memory in children. npj Science of Learning 8:27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00177-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two cluster-based validation analyses in the MT group tested for generally decreased neural activity: ERP validation found nothing surviving correction, and oscillatory validation found increased alpha power at 1000–1400 ms (post 18 ± 2% vs pre 6 ± 3%, pcorr < 0.05) that "failed to signi ﬁcantly correlate with memory increment, r = 0.23, p = 0.57".

> "With ERP validation, no signi ﬁcant results survived after the cluster-based correction. However, with oscillatory power validation, we found a signi ﬁcant power difference between pre- and post-training sessions in the window of 1000 –1400 ms"
