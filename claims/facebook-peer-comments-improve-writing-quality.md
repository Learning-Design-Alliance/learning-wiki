## Evidence

### Ho 2020

Ho, P. V. P., Phung, L. T. K., Oanh, T. T. T., & Giao, N. Q. (2020). Should Peer E-Comments Replace Traditional Peer Comments? International Journal of Instruction, 13(1), 295-314. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13120a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-sample t-test on the experimental group (n=32) posting drafts and comments on a Facebook group; mean rose from 5.903 to 6.994, MD=-1.09, p=.000, which the article says indicates Facebook-based peer comment activities positively affect writing quality.

> "The mean difference of the pre -test and post -test scores of the experimental group is MD= -1.09. The Sig. for the comparison of the average scores of the pre -test and post -test is p= .000."
