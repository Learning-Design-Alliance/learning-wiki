# Upper-level students struggle to write position and momentum eigenstate inner products without Dirac notation, often confusing the Kronecker delta with the Dirac delta function

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Only 35% of upper-level students correctly wrote ⟨x|x′⟩ = δ(x−x′) or ⟨p|p′⟩ = δ(p−p′); 6–7% incorrectly wrote the inner product as 1, and interviews revealed confusion between the Kronecker delta and the Dirac delta function. ## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2015

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2015). Student difficulties with quantum states while translating state vectors in Dirac notation to wave functions in position and momentum representations. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.048](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.048)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Open-ended question administered to 46 upper-level undergraduates after traditional instruction; responses were considered correct if students wrote the Dirac delta function. In interviews students claimed ⟨x|x′⟩ = 1 if x = x′ and 0 otherwise.

> "Table III shows that only 35% answered correctly. Some students incorrectly invoked a “normalization condition” when determining ⟨𝑥|𝑥′⟩ or ⟨𝑝|𝑝′⟩."
