Sara Berti, Valentina Grazia, Luisa Molinari. (2023). Active Student Participation in Whole-School Interventions in Secondary School. A Systematic Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09773-x

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Methodological quality assessment of the included reports against Council for Exceptional Children (2014) indicators, with three independent researchers rating each report (Tables 3 and 4). Most studies were strong in contextualizing research, describing the program, and conducting quality analyses; fidelity indicators 4 and 5 were the weak points.

> "we found a weakness concerning the fidelity of implementation, as this indicator was observed in only about half of the considered programs."
