## Evidence

### Maxwell 2015

Maxwell, B., Burnett, C., Reidy, J., Willis, B., & Demack, S. (2015). Oracy Curriculum, Culture and Assessment Toolkit: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581146

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Baseline comparison of Raven's Progressive Matrices scores between School 21 and control students in September 2013. The report prints d = 0.35 for raw scores and d = 0.27 for age-standardised scores, both non-significant, concluding there was no difference in initial non-verbal reasoning ability.

> "the difference between the schools was not statistically significant for either the raw scores (t(36) = 1.05, p = .302, d = 0.35) or the age standardised scores (t(36) = 0.81, p = .423, d = 0.27)."
