## Evidence

### Almutairi et al. 2020

Faisal M. Almutairi, Abdullah A. Almodaires & Alaa J. Zeyab. (2020). Effectiveness of Flipped Learning: Improving Pre-Service Teachers' Prowess in Producing Videos. International Education Studies, 13(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v13n7p163

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of a revalidated five-point Likert questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.912) administered to the 37 experimental-group pre-service teachers; agreement ranged from 68% to 100% across ten items. Only descriptive statistics were reported, no inferential test or effect size.

> "100% of the pre-service teachers 'agreeing' that the videos and lessons were available on the e-learning portal before the Flipped Learning activity (Q1); and over 90% of the participants 'agreeing' with four items (Q2, Q3, Q5, Q9) in the feedback questionnaire"
