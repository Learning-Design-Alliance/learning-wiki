## Evidence

### Harsh Kumar 2024

Harsh Kumar, Ilya Musabirov, Mohi Reza, Jiakai Shi, Xinyuan Wang, Joseph Jay Williams, Anastasia Kuzminykh, and Michael Liut. 2024. Impact of Guidance and Interaction Strategies for LLM Use on Learner Performance and Perception. Proc. ACM Hum.-Comput. Interact. 8, CSCW2, Article 499 (November 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3687038

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of first-query categories in the formative classroom deployment (Table 1 reports 28% verbatim copying and 14% reworked questions overall). The authors report that G1, G2, and G4 show "a positive effect in reducing the likelihood of the student copy-pasting the exact assignment question."

> "G1, G2, and G4 show a positive effect in reducing the likelihood of the student copy-pasting the exact assignment question. However, G3 showed the opposite effect. The introduction of G3-Metacognitive Questioning resulted in a higher percentage of students rephrasing the first assignment question"
