## Evidence

### Miller‐Cotto 2026

Miller‐Cotto, D., & Medrano, J. (2026). Does working memory moderate the effect of fading on math performance? British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 108–126. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12781

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pooled t-tests and a pooled regression (Model 5) on the randomized homework study using a 7-point Paas mental-effort rating (α = .708) administered after each problem set; the collapsed fading-versus-other comparison also yielded no significant difference (t = −.78, p = .44), and the Fading+FSE × CL interaction was non-significant (p = .43).

> "Results indicated no significant differences in cognitive load between conditions (all ps > .40*)."
