### Wisniewski B 2020 (2)

Wisniewski B, Zierer K and Hattie J (2020) The Power of Feedback Revisited: A Meta-Analysis of Educational Feedback Research. Front. Psychol. 10:3087. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Descriptive analysis of the motivational-outcome subset of the meta-analysis. The authors report that "Twenty one percent of the eﬀect sizes related to motivational outcomes in our data were negative", mostly from uninformative rewards or punishments.

> "Twenty one percent of the eﬀect sizes related to motivational outcomes in our data were negative, with 86% of the feedback interventions leading to these negative eﬀects being uninformative (rewards or punishments)."
