## Evidence

### Li Jiuxiang 2024

Li Jiuxiang, Wang Rufeng. (2024). RETRACTED ARTICLE: Determining the role of innovative teaching practices, sustainable learning, and the adoption of e-learning tools in leveraging academic motivation for students' mental well-being. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01639-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis in Mplus of survey data from 308 students at Chinese universities. The structural path from academic motivation to mental well-being was significant ("β = 0.0.317", t = 5.952, p = 0.000). No standardized effect size beyond the coefficient is printed.

> "Findings from H1 demonstrated that MW was significantly predicted by the independent variable ACM, i.e., β = 0.0.317, SE = 0.053, t-value = 5.952, p-value = 0.000."
