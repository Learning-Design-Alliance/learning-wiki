Yimwilai 2015¶

Supaporn Yimwilai. (2015). An Integrated Approach to Teaching Literature in an EFL Classroom. English Language Teaching, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n2p14

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent t-test on 15-item attitude questionnaire (opinions, feelings, inclination to action; Cronbach's Alpha 0.832). At UP means were 3.60 versus 3.09 ("3.08 .005"); at UB 3.79 versus 3.13 ("6.00 .000*"). No effect size is printed.

"the attitudes toward reading literature of the students in the experimental groups at both universities were significantly higher than those of the control groups, at the levels of 0.01 and 0.001, respectively."
