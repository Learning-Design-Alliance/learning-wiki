Ilana Seidel Horn 2025¶

Ilana Seidel Horn, Yeliz Günal Aggül, Shuqin Li, Christine E. Hood. (2025). Teachers’ subjectivities in responsive instruction: when ambitious practice encounters challenging teaching situations. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09742-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Case analysis of Linda, who invited the research team to consult about a student, Bee, whose participation she found irritating. In the debrief, colleagues separated Bee's intentions from how her actions were perceived, and Brette offered that Bee might be earnestly participating without adhering to neurotypical social norms.

"the relational agency provided through the debrief conversation had offered resources for Linda’s epistemic agency and lessened her emotional labor, which had shifted from surface acting to deep acting."
