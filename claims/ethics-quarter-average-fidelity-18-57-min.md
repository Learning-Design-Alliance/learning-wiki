## Evidence

### Laukkanen L 2022

Laukkanen L, Suhonen R, Löyttyniemi E, Leino-Kilpi H. (2022). The usability, feasibility and fidelity of the Ethics Quarter e-learning intervention for nurse managers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03241-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Fidelity was evaluated using Google Analytics web-page user information, measuring time spent per dimension. The intervention was planned at 15 minutes per quarter, 30 minutes per dimension, so actual use was shorter than estimated.

> "According to Google Analytics, NMs spent on average 18.57 minutes to complete one dimension."
