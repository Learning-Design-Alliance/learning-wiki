`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the exam 1 regression, adding referee ratings produced a significant R² change (F2,141 = 3.7, p = .028), but neither individual rating reached significance; for other outcomes referee ratings changed R² non-significantly (e.g. exam 2: F2,141 = 0.4, p = .7).

> "Better referee ratings were associated with lower exam 1 marks, but neither rating is quite statistically significant on its own (0.1 > ps > 0.05)."
