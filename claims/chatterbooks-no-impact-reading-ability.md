## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Styles 2014

Styles, B., Clarkson, R., & Fowler, K. (2014). Chatterbooks: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581155

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Three-arm pupil-randomised controlled trial in 12 secondary schools; 577 Year 7 pupils randomised to Chatterbooks, Chatterbooks Plus or waitlist control. The primary outcome was the GL Assessment NGRT composite reading score; the Chatterbooks vs control effect size was "-0.14" with a confidence interval passing through zero, based on 159 analysed pupils.

> "The headline findings suggest that Chatterbooks and Chatterbooks Plus had a slightly negative impact on reading ability with effect sizes of -0.14 and -0.01 respectively. However, they are not statistically significant, suggesting that the difference in outcomes between the control and intervention group occurred by chance."
