`q3 i?` When the II category task is trained first and the irrelevant contrast task follows at the transfer location, post-test accuracy is comparable across locations (d = 0.05, n.s.). [→ Zhu 2026](#zhu-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhu 2026

Zhu, J.-P., Xie, X.-Y., Yu, C. & Zhang, J.-Y. (2026). Double training promotes retinotopic transfer of category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00396-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Experiment 4: 16 participants completed the II category task first, then the contrast discrimination task at the mirror-symmetric transfer location. Mean TI = 1.01 ± 0.06 did not differ from 1; the authors note the Bayesian evidence (BF10 = 0.26) for complete transfer is moderate rather than conclusive.

> "Post-test performance showed comparable accuracy between trained and transfer locations (Fig. 5c; trained: 0.76 ± 0.03; transfer: 0.76 ± 0.03; difference = 0.005 ± 0.02, t = 0.22, p = 0.83, 95% CI [-0.04, 0.05], Cohen's d = 0.05)"
