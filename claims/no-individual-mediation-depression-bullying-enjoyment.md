Bowman K, Cadman T, Goncalves Soares A, Robinson O, Hughes A, Heron J, Segal AB, Huerta MC, Howe LD. (2023). Mediators of the association between childhood body mass index and educational attainment: Analysis of a UK prospective cohort study. Pediatric Obesity. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijpo.13014

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Individual-mediator models in the ALSPAC imputed sample, each adjusting for intermediate confounders (e.g., bullying and externalizing behaviours for the depression model). The article reports "no evidence of mediation by depressive symptoms, bullying or school enjoyment" in either sex; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no evidence of mediation by depressive symptoms, bullying or school enjoyment in either females or males."
