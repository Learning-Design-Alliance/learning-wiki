## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Impact of instructional approach on students' epistemologies about experimental physics. arXiv:1609.05758. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.092)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of matched E-CLASS data from N = 3601 first-year students; Table II prints pre means 16.4 (Traditional) vs 17.1 (Transformed) with p = 0.07, and post means 14.4 vs 17.3 with "p ≪ 0.01" and d = 0.4. Effect size is moderate as printed.

> "the diﬀerence between the pre-instruction scores for Traditional and Transformed was not statistically signiﬁcant; however, the diﬀerence in post-instruction scores was signiﬁcant with a moderate eﬀect size."
