## Evidence

### Sala Giovanni 2017

Sala Giovanni, Gobet Fernand. (2017). Does Far Transfer Exist? Negative Evidence From Chess, Music, and Working Memory Training. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721417712760

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Three meta-analyses of experimental studies (chess, music instruction, WM training) in typically developing children and young adolescents pooled treatment-control differences; Table 1 prints chess overall 0.34 [0.24, 0.44], music 0.16 [0.09, 0.22], WM 0.12 [0.06, 0.18], described as "small to moderate overall effect sizes."

> "The results showed small to moderate overall effect sizes—that is, the overall quantitative measure of the effectiveness of the training—in all three meta-analyses (Table 1)."
