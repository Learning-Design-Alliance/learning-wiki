Subgroup analysis conducted in line with EEF guidance to assess tutoring effectiveness for pupils eligible for free school meals, reported in the results table. Both printed effect sizes are below 0.2 in magnitude and both FSM rows carry no security rating. The evaluators do not report significance tests for these subgroup contrasts in the quoted material.
