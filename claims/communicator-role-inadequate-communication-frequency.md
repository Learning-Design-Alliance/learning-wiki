### Pulkki KH 2025

Pulkki KH, Pira S, Young M, Scott GM, Nhan C, Fung K, Le Blanc G, Nguyen LHP. (2025). Considering the potential unintended consequences of RateMDs: an exploratory study in one specialty. Canadian Medical Education Journal 16(2). https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.77821

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Frequency counts within the same deductive coding of 878 low-rating reviews. Communicator comments were coded at 34.3% overall (n = 301), with "a lack of active listening and of clear explanations (30%; n = 264)" the largest attribute. No effect size printed.

> "Specifically, comments alluded to a lack of active listening and of clear explanations (30%; n = 264). Issues in communication have the potential to generate conflict, misinterpretation, feelings of resentment, and development of medico-legal issues."
