## Evidence

### Geerts 2018

Geerts, W. M., Steenbeek, H. W., & van Geert, P. L. C. (2018). Effect of Video-Cases on the Acquisition of Situated Knowledge of Teachers. International Education Studies, 11(1), 64. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p64

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Structured interview at pre- and post-test, scored on components of the design pattern 'Dealing with disorder', in the video-case and written-case groups. Both groups showed progress of their applicability of this design pattern, with less strong progress for the group without video cases; the authors report this result as the least ambiguous.

> "Both group s showed progress of their applicability of this design pattern. For the group who attended the course without video cases this progress was less strong."
