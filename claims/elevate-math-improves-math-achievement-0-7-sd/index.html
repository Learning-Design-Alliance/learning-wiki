Evidence¶

Snipes 2016¶

Snipes, J., Huang, C.-W., Jaquet, K., & Finkelstein, N. (2016). The effects of the Elevate Math summer program on math achievement and algebra readiness. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567052

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial conducted in summer 2014 at eight Silicon Valley schools; 461 rising grade 8 students analyzed. The regression model comparing treatment and control outcomes controlled for grade 6 CST scores. The article reports the printed means and that the difference is "the equivalent of 0.7 standard deviation."

"Out of 45 possible points, the average MDTP score for the treatment group was 21 points, compared with 17 points for the control group. This statistically significant difference of 4 points is the equivalent of 0.7 standard deviation."
