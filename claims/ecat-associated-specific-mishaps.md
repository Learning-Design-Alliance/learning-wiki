## Evidence

### Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma 2026

Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma, Özceylan Abdullah, Hatipoğlu Ömer, Brochado Martins Joao Filipe. (2026). Educational case complexity (E‑CAT) and clinical outcomes in undergraduate endodontics: associations with procedural mishaps and postoperative pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09249-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cramér's V bivariate analysis of individual procedural mishaps against E-CAT scores in the prospective cohort. Overfilling showed negligible correlation (V = 0.047). The authors note rotary Ni-Ti file separation likely reflects students' limited experience with rotary instrumentation.

> "When analyzing procedural complications, the E-CAT score showed the strongest associations with file separation (Cramér's V = 0.251) and working length loss (V = 0.216). Moderate-to-weak associations were observed for other mishaps, such as overinstrumentation (V = 0.160) and canal blockage (V = 0.141)"

### Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma 2026 (2)

Pertek Hatipoğlu Fatma, Özceylan Abdullah, Hatipoğlu Ömer, Brochado Martins Joao Filipe. (2026). Educational case complexity (E‑CAT) and clinical outcomes in undergraduate endodontics: associations with procedural mishaps and postoperative pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09249-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same cohort. Treatment duration strongly correlated with the number of sessions (r = 0.85). The authors attribute longer duration to anatomical challenges of molars and potentially limited mouth opening.

> "Weak positive correlations were observed between E-CAT scores and both treatment length (r = 0.21) as number of sessions (r = 0.24)"
