Wilson 1922¶

Wilson, G. M. (1922). Language error tests. Journal of Educational Psychology, 13(6), 341–349. doi:10.1037/h0064444

q2 · i? · design · r2

Same-pupil administration of two parallel forms in a small city system; 103 paired scores yielded "a positive coefficient of 0.901", taken as further evidence of the forms' equality.

"Tests B and C were given at the same time to pupils in grades III to VIII, inclusive, in a small city system. On the basis of 103 pairs thus secured, the coefficient of correlation was figured. The results showed a positive coefficient of 0.901."
