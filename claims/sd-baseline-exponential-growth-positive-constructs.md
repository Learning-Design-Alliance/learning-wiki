### Mohammed A. Alrizqi 2025

Mohammed A. Alrizqi. (2025). From Interviews to Equations: A Multi-Phase System Dynamics Model of Engineering Student Engagement. 2025 International System Dynamics Conference (WIP, withdrawn before publication). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.00382](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.00382)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Numerical Vensim simulation of the interview-derived model over a 16-week horizon. Positive constructs "increase exponentially throughout the 16 weeks" while dissatisfaction and need for validation decrease exponentially. No empirical outcome data or effect sizes are reported.

> "Overall, the results show that learning, belonging, conﬁdence, personal growth, and motivation all increase exponentially throughout the 16 weeks. Similarly, negative emotions and detrimental traits, such as dissatisfaction and need for validation, decrease exponentially"
