Feola 2016¶

Elvia Ilaria Feola. (2016). Digital Literacy and New Technological Perspectives. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2174-2180. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040929

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive percentages of average scores per indicator from the Instant DCA administration in the one-group study, shown in Figure 1. The article reports "a sufficient expertise" on interface identification and technical problem solving and "a very low value (26%)" for technological understanding. These are descriptive percentages, not effect sizes.

"the examines the technological dimension highlights, in students, a sufficient expertise, especially with regard to the identification of the interfaces and symbols (59%) and for the resolution of technical problems (52%). The technological understanding, however, showed a very low value (26%)."
