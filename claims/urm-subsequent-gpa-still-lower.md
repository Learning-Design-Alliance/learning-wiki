Active Learning Outside the Classroom: Implementation and Outcomes of Peer-Led Team-Learning Workshops in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0051

ANOVA on subsequent-course GPAs of students who persisted in biology-related disciplines, comparing before- and after-program cohorts. The study reports a small overall GPA increase after the program (3.52 to 3.54, p < 0.05) but "no interaction between URM status and program implementation."

> "The mean overall (Before and After) biology GPA of URM students was 3.25 compared with 3.58 for non-URM students (ANOVA, p < 0.0001; Figure 5A)."
