## Evidence

### Ebrahim Sumayyah 2024

Ebrahim Sumayyah, Kinoo Suman Mewa, Naidoo Maheshwar, Van Wyk Jacqueline Marina. (2024). The use of household items to support online surgical knot-tying skills training: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05549-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Focus group suggestion theme: students requested an interactive session with immediate feedback and pairing so a friend could assist with videoing; the pairing suggestion was implemented in subsequent student groups.

> "Participants preferred learning the skill in a blended learning format, where they could practice the skill in a simulated setting such as in the skills lab, in group sessions under supervision, and reinforce the skill by watching the video content and practicing the skill on their own."
