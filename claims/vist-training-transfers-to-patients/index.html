Haiser Alexander 2022¶

Haiser Alexander, Aydin Abdullatif, Kunduzi Basir, Ahmed Kamran, Dasgupta Prokar. (2022). A Systematic Review of Simulation-Based Training in Vascular Surgery. The Journal of Surgical Research. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jss.2022.05.009

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review narrative of VIST validation studies. Chaer et al. was a randomized, controlled study per the reference list, and Hseino et al. found practicing superior femoral angioplasty on VIST "translated to real-world performance". Most VIST studies scored LoE 1-2/5.

"Chaer et al. used VIST to trial a simulated iliofemoral angioplasty and found an improvement in all technical skill metrics when later performed on real patients (LoE = 3/5)."

Related Claims¶

  • A systematic review identified 76 eligible articles validating 34 vascular surgery simulators and training courses for open and endovascular procedures — a broader claim this one bears on
  • ANGIO Mentor was the most highly validated and effective vascular simulator and the only simulator to achieve an LoE of 5/5 — related
  • Only seven studies of vascular surgery simulation achieved a level of effectiveness greater than 3/5, with the majority scoring LoE 1-2/5 — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Evidence on transfer to clinical behavior and patient outcomes is scarce: only 2 studies reached Kirkpatrick level 3 and 1 reached level 4 — related
  • Non-expert delivered feedback is as effective as expert feedback on most outcomes in endovascular simulator training — related
