## Evidence

### Gong C 2025

Gong C, Shen Y, Wang J, Zhang P, Li Z. (2025). Application of ultrasound simulation training in intensive care nursing teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06968-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 40 ICU nurses; comparison of post-training practical operation scores between the simulation group (n = 20) and the traditional-teaching control group (n = 20). Table 4 reports t = -13.453 for the between-group post-training comparison, marked *** (p < 0.001).

> "The mean of the theoretical score after the experimental group (M = 38.80; SD = 1.64) is significant compared to that of the control group (M = 32.40; SD = 1.35), p < 0.05."

### Gong C 2025 (2)

Gong C, Shen Y, Wang J, Zhang P, Li Z. (2025). Application of ultrasound simulation training in intensive care nursing teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06968-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pre-post comparison in the experimental group (n = 20): practical scores rose from "M = 17.20; SD = 1.74" before training to "M = 38.80; SD = 1.64" after; Table 4 reports t = -40.442, marked *** (p < 0.001).

> "The mean of the theoretical score after of the experimental group (M = 38.80; SD = 1.64) is significant compared to that of the score before (M = 17.20; SD = 1.74), and the difference was statistically significant (p < 0.05)."
