## Evidence

### Shukla A 2025

Shukla A, Amrutham BV. (2025). Impact of coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic on dental student's performance across disciplines during community site rotations: A comparative analysis. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13707

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre/post two-sample t-test comparison of five-point Likert assessment scores from the CBDE course, reported in Results §3.1. Faculty pre-COVID mean was 4.46 and post-COVID 4.14, "showing a significant decline (p < 0.05)".

> "student mean scores also decreased from 4.45 in 2018‐2020 to 4.02 in 2020‐2022, also showing a significant decline (p < 0.05). Faculty scores slightly surpassed students' self‐reported scores both before and after the COVID‐19 pandemic."

### Shukla A 2025 (2)

Shukla A, Amrutham BV. (2025). Impact of coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic on dental student's performance across disciplines during community site rotations: A comparative analysis. Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13707

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Class-cohort ANOVA comparison across graduating years reported in Results §3.1. The cohort means fell steadily, and the authors attribute this partly to pandemic disruption of the Class of 2022's "foundational/preclinical training".

> "ANOVA results showed a general decline in mean scores over the years for different class cohorts (2019–2022), with the Class of 2019 averaging 4.40, to a score of 3.97 for the Class of 2022. Faculty scores also decreased from 4.47 for the Class of 2019 to 4.11 for the Class of 2022 (p < 0.05)."
