# Teachers exposed to a professional development workshop overwhelmingly supported early teaching of Einsteinian physics (94%), with most (88%) requesting extra training

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2018 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 94% of teachers agreed it is appropriate to teach modern Einsteinian physics concepts to students at a young age, and 94% wanted to use the activity materials. [→ Foppoli 2018](#foppoli-2018)
`q2 i?` 88% of teachers said they would like extra training for teaching these modern ideas, and 62% thought their school could fund the roughly $1000 of materials. [→ Foppoli 2018 (2)](#foppoli-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Foppoli 2018

Foppoli, A., Choudhary, R., Blair, D., Kaur, T., Moschilla, J. and Zadnik, M. (2018). Public and Teacher Response to Einsteinian Physics in Schools. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aae4a4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aae4a4)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Attitudinal post-questionnaire from teachers in both Professional Development Workshops (9 secondary teachers and 25 primary teachers from Rosalie Primary School), combined due to consistency; Table 4 reports yes/no/unsure distributions.

> "The response to question 4(a) show that a large majority (94%) of the teachers supported the concept of Einsteinian physics at primary and middle school."

### Foppoli 2018 (2)

Foppoli, A., Choudhary, R., Blair, D., Kaur, T., Moschilla, J. and Zadnik, M. (2018). Public and Teacher Response to Einsteinian Physics in Schools. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aae4a4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aae4a4)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same combined teacher attitudinal questionnaire (Table 4); question 4(d) on funding the modest equipment cost received 62% yes and 29% unsure.

> "the majority of teachers (88%) believed that they would require additional training and resources in order to feel sufficiently prepared to teach Einsteinian physics in the classroom."
