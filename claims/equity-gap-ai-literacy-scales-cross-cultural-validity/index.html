The review reports, citing Lintner's 2024 systematic review, that no AI literacy scale has been tested for cross-cultural validity. Context given: in Asia-Pacific, only 49% of rural residents use the internet compared to 83% of urban residents, a widening gap. Second-hand, not independently verified here.
