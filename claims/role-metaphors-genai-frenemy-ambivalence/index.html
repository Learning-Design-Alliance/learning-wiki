Evidence¶

Carmen Vallis 2025¶

Carmen Vallis, Stephanie Wilson, Alison Casey. (2025). Fear and Awe: Making Sense of Generative AI Through Metaphor. Journal of Interactive Media Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/jime.972

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative thematic analysis of workshop metaphors. Three themes emerged: GenAI as helper (research assistant, guide), as learning partner (study buddy, personal tutor), and as friend/foe, described as a "double-edged sword", a "friendly terminator" or a "frenemy".

"Overall, participants were cautious about the roles GenAI might play in education, acknowledging the 'relationship' as being different to a human one. A high proportion of metaphors in this category combined words suggesting that GenAI is both friend and foe."
