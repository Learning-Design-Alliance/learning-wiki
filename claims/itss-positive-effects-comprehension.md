# ITSS has positive effects on reading comprehension, based on two studies meeting WWC standards covering 6,724 students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r1` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The WWC found strong evidence of a positive and statistically significant effect of ITSS on comprehension, with an outcome-average effect size of 0.23 and an improvement index of +9 percentile points. [→ wwc-rating-1](#wwc-rating-1)

## Evidence

### wwc-rating-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, April). Web-based Intelligent Tutoring for the Structure Strategy (ITSS). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/703

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

WWC synthesis of two cluster randomized controlled trials (Wijekumar et al., 2012, 2017) in suburban and rural districts across two states, with 6,724 students in grades 4, 5, and 7. The outcome average effect size across all studies was 0.23 and the improvement index +9, described as "positive and statistically significant effect of ITSS on comprehension".

> "The WWC determined that one study that meets WWC group design standards without reservations and one study that meets WWC group design standards with reservations showed evidence of a positive and statistically significant effect of ITSS on comprehension"
