Related Claims¶

  • Studies directly measuring WTC consistently report positive effects of AI on EFL learners' WTC across tool types, contexts, and age groups — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • AI-powered corrective feedback and repeated practice enhance oral language skills (Communicative Capacity Pathway) — related
  • Speaking practice opportunities and teacher support reduced classroom anxiety and increased self-efficacy for one learner — related
  • Fear of making mistakes is the most significant cause of foreign language speaking anxiety among Iranian EFL learners (81%) — related
  • Anxiety in foreign language classroom learning experiences is linked with oral performance among Japanese EFL learners — related
  • Extensive reading yields affective benefits: positive attitudes, motivation, confidence, and reduced anxiety, with difficult texts raising anxiety — related
  • Lack of practice ranks fourth among speaking anxiety sources, which the authors suggest may be due to teacher-centered Iranian classrooms — related
  • EFL students report that games reduce speaking stress and improve speaking skills — related
  • The authors claim that making teachers' own mistakes, struggles, and fears central to the curriculum lessens students' fear of making mistakes in a new endeavor — related
  • Fear of negative evaluation and limited vocabulary knowledge rank second and third as sources of speaking anxiety (78% and 72%) — related
  • Learners valued safe, repeatable, nonjudgmental AI practice environments that reduced anxiety and bridged the theory-practice gap — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • For some students, AI-generated feedback reduces the perceived personal risk of exposing weaknesses, functioning as a psychologically safe space for feedback seeking — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • A 15-week extensive reading course increased Japanese EFL learners' feelings of comfort with foreign-language reading (r = .54) — related
  • Extensive reading reduced Japanese EFL learners' anxiety about foreign-language reading (r = .34) — related
  • EFL learners perceive AI-mediated instruction as engaging, personalized, anxiety-reducing, and empowering for autonomous learning — related
  • 88% of AI-assisted students reported increased self-efficacy and confidence during independent practicals — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Combining AI-generated information with teacher intervention is associated with improved student performance, participation, confidence, and reduced anxiety in K-12 AI-based instruction — related
  • Sociocultural factors, learner motivation, self-efficacy and perceptions of the classroom environment influence FLCA learning outcomes and oral performance — related
  • Students view games as relaxing and fun, building confidence for speaking — related
  • Tablet-PC-supported peer-assisted learning reduced EFL learners' anxiety and promoted motivation and confidence in collaborative reading — related
  • Student-centered writing environments are argued to lower anxiety, increase confidence, and provide natural language contexts — related
  • Extensive reading produced no measurable change in the perceived Practical Value of foreign-language reading (r = .09) — related
  • Immediate task repetition benefits oral performance, including fluency, self-correction and reduced anxiety — related
  • AI-enabled EFL teaching in schools is associated with improvements in student engagement, motivation, and language proficiency, per a systematic review of 22 empirical studies — related
  • An intelligent affective tutoring system recognizing facial expressions reduced anxiety and improved Japanese learning — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Students in both groups valued the AI speaking app for autonomous practice, and AI-GFCA students reported reduced anxiety and more peer interaction — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Peer tutoring contributes to psychological safety and reduced anxiety in demanding simulation learning environments — related
  • Song-based instruction is consistently associated with increased affective engagement in EFL listening classrooms — related
  • 3D printed foot models produced similar anxiety and confidence changes to standard teaching and increased self-confidence and task self-efficacy in scalpel skill practice — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • EFL learners rank speaking as the skill most urgently needing improvement, with pragmatic routines the top speaking sub-skill — related
