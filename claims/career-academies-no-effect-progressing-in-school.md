# Career Academies show no discernible effects on progressing in school: academy students accrued no more course credits than comparison students by the end of twelfth grade (effect size 0.09, not statistically significant or substantively important).

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### progressing-1

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2015, September). Dropout Prevention intervention report: Career Academies. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/70

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

The WWC review reports the progressing in school domain finding from the Kemple & Snipes (2000) randomized controlled trial, with 1,379 students in nine schools. Total course credits were 22.3 (SD 5.6) for the intervention group versus 21.8 (SD 5.5) for the comparison group; the effect size of 0.09 (improvement index +4, p = .08) was not statistically significant or substantively important.
