# Communication skills training substantially increases pharmacy students' confidence in communicating with patients and counseling on special dosage forms

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment
> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Confidence in communicating with patients rose from 2.57 to 4.18 and confidence in counseling on special dosage forms rose from 1.85 to 4.30 on a 5-point scale, both statistically significant. [→ Jin Hye Kyung 2019](#jin-hye-kyung-2019)

## Evidence

### Jin Hye Kyung 2019

Jin Hye Kyung, Park So Hyun, Kang Ji Eun, Choi Kyung Suk, Kim Hong Ah, Jeon Min Seon, Rhie Sandy Jeong. (2019). The influence of a patient counseling training session on pharmacy students’ self-perceived communication skills, confidence levels, and attitudes about communication skills training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1607-x

`q2 · i?`

Two-item confidence scale (5-point Likert) in the sixty-student pre-post survey analyzed with paired t-tests; "Communicating with patients 2.57 (0.98) 4.18 (0.89)" and "Patient counseling on special dosage forms 1.85 (0.80) 4.30 (0.70)" both improved significantly.

> "Communicating with patients	2.57 (0.98)	4.18 (0.89)	−10.018	< 0.001	
Patient counseling on special dosage forms	1.85 (0.80)	4.30 (0.70)	−17.568	< 0.001"
