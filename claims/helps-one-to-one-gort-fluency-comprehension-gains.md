Begeny et al. (2012) evaluated the HELPS program with the Gray Oral Reading Test, Fourth Edition, in brief one-to-one sessions two to three times per week of about 10 minutes; students "significantly outperformed the control group on GORT Fluency and GORT-Comprehension". No effect size is printed.
