## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A model adding direct income-to-youth-perception paths fit well and showed a direct effect of income on youth needs stress (β = −.38, p = .003); the reversed model fit substantially worse (CFI = .81, RMSEA = .15). [→ Mistry 2021](#mistry-2021)

## Evidence

### Mistry 2021

Mistry, R. S., & Elenbaas, L. (2021). It’s All in the Family: Parents’ Economic Worries and Youth’s Perceptions of Financial Stress and Educational Outcomes. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-021-01393-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Alternate-model analyses in the Results: "there was a significant direct effect of income on youth stress around needs, β = −.38, p = .003". The reverse-causality model fit the data worse than the theoretical model (χ2(30) = 157.901, RMSEA = .15, CFI = .81).

> "In addition, there was a significant direct effect of income on youth stress around needs, β = −.38, p = .003. No other effects of income on youth perceptions were significant."
