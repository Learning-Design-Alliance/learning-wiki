## Evidence

### Couper K 2018

Couper K, Velho RM, Quinn T, Devrell A, Lall R, Orriss B, Yeung J, Perkins GD. (2018). Training approaches for the deployment of a mechanical chest compression device: a randomised controlled manikin study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-019009

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation (e), from the discussion: comparing their ~14 s standard-group pause with a 21 s preintervention pause reported in Levy et al's before–after study, they suggest "the opportunity for pit-crew training to have a measurable effect on team performance may have been limited".

> "the mean total pause associated with device deployment in the standard training group in this study was about 14 s, such that the opportunity for pit-crew training to have a measurable effect on team performance may have been limited"
