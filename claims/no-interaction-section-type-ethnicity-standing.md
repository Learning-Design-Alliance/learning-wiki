## Evidence

### Adams A. E. M. 2015

Adams A. E. M., Randall S., Traustadóttir T. (2015). A Tale of Two Sections: An Experiment to Compare the Effectiveness of a Hybrid versus a Traditional Lecture Format in Introductory Microbiology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus interaction tests in the univariate general linear model of final scores (excluding quizzes) in the randomized two-section study. The article reports no significant interaction between class type and ethnicity or class standing; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "There was, however, no significant interaction between the type of class (hybrid vs. traditional) and ethnicity or class standing, meaning that the performance differences between the two sections were the same whether in the hybrid or the traditional section."
