## Evidence

### Andrzej Sokolowski 2015

Andrzej Sokolowski. (2015). The Effects of Mathematical Modelling on Students’ Achievement-Meta-Analysis of Research. The IAFOR Journal of Education, Volume III - Issue I - Winter 2015. https://journals.iafor.org/index.php/joe

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup moderator analysis of the study pool split into semester-length (8 studies) and shorter-than-semester (6 studies) treatments. The article reports "ES = 1.31, SE = 0.10" for shorter versus "ES = 0.46, SE = 0.06" for longer treatments.

> "The effect size computation for this subgroup showed that shorter treatments produced a higher effect of students’ achievement (ES = 1.31, SE = 0.10) than longer (ES = 0.46, SE = 0.06)."
