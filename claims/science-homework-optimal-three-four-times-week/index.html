Evidence¶

Nathan McJames 2023¶

Nathan McJames, Andrew Parnell, and Ann O'Shea. (2023). Little and Often: Causal Inference Machine Learning Demonstrates the Benefits of Homework for Improving Achievement in Mathematics and Science. https://timssandpirls.bc.edu/timss2019/international-results

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Results from the multivariate Bayesian Causal Forests analysis of the TIMSS 2019 Irish sample, shown in Figure 2 and Table 2. Table 2 prints the science ATE for three or four times per week as 5.49 (2.62, 8.44) and for every day as -1.83 (-5.60, 1.93), supporting the conclusion that "the optimal homework frequency in science is three or four times per week".

"In science, however, the effect size peaks at three or four times per week before reaching a frequency of every day which does not show a significant increase relative to assigning homework up to one or two times per week."
