## Evidence

### Osman Tasdelen 2025

Osman Tasdelen, Daniel Bodemer. (2025). Generative AI in the Classroom: Effects of Context-Personalized Learning Material and Tasks on Motivation and Performance. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00491-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple mediation analysis with percentile bootstrapping (10,000 samples) in the classroom experiment. The total effect was significant (B = 0.93, p = .048), but "ab =− 0.28, 95% CI [− 0.70, − 0.02]" — the mediator negatively predicted performance.

> "The indirect effect of learning material on learning performance through intrinsic motivation was found also signiﬁcant, ab =− 0.28, 95% CI [− 0.70, − 0.02]"
