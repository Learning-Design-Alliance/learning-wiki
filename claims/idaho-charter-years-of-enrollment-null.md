## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Idaho charter school students' learning growth in the first and second year of charter attendance is not statistically different from that of VCR students in traditional public schools. [→ CREDO 2019](#credo-2019)

## Evidence

### CREDO 2019

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in Idaho. https://credo.stanford.edu

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Subset analysis limited to students first enrolling in a charter school between the 2015-16 and 2016-17 school years and their VCRs (4,016 reading and 4,005 math student observations). Growth in both the first and second year is not statistically different from TPS VCR growth.

> "Idaho charter school students experience learning growth in the first and the second year of charter attendance that is not statistically different from that of students (VCR) enrolled in traditional public school settings."
