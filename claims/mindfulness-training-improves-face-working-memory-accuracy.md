## Evidence

### Kou 2025

Kou, H., Luo, W., Li, X. et al. (2025). Mindfulness training enhances face working memory: evidence from the drift-diffusion model. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00389-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized waitlist-controlled experiment with 120 undergraduates (60 training, 60 control) using a delayed match-to-sample task with facial stimuli from the Chinese Facial Affective Picture System. Independent-samples t-tests on T2-T1 accuracy improvements showed "significantly larger improvements for the training group than the control group" for happy (Cohen's d=.51), sad (d=.74), angry (d=.57), and neutral (d=.53) faces; the fearful-face difference was nonsignificant (t(118)=1.33, p=.187).

> "significantly larger improvements for the training group than the control group for the happy (t(118)=2.79, p=.031, Cohen's d=.51, 95% CI [.01, .08], Bonferroni corrected), sad (t(118)=4.05, p<.001, Cohen's d =.74"
