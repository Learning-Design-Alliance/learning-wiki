## Evidence

### L Hahn 2024

L Hahn, S Blaue, P Klein. (2024). A research-informed graphical tool to visually approach Gauss' and Stokes' theorems in vector calculus. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad2390](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad2390)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation questionnaire administered after intervention phases in the 2022 and 2023 Physics II recitation implementations, using eight usability and design items on a 6-point Likert-type scale (N = 125; Cronbach's alpha 0.82). The article reports a "scale mean of 0.76±0.16" with high values for user-friendliness, presentation form, and comprehensibility items. No effect size is printed.

> "Evaluation of students' perceived usability and design of the tool revealed a scale mean of 0.76±0.16 (including item inverting) and students indicated high values for items that refer to user-friendliness, presentation form, and comprehensibility of the tool and its features"
