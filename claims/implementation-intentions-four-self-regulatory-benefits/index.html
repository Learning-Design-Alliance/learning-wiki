Gollwitzer 2006¶

Gollwitzer, Peter M., Sheeran, Paschal. (2006). Implementation intentions and goal achievement: a meta-analysis of effects and processes. Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, 38, 69-119. https://nbn-resolving.de/urn:nbn:de:bsz:352-opus-56534. doi:10.1016/s0065-2601(06)38002-1

The meta-analysis reports effectiveness across four self-regulatory functions: "promoting the initiation of goal striving", "shielding of ongoing goal pursuit", disengagement, and conservation of capability. No separate effect sizes for each function are printed in the available text.
