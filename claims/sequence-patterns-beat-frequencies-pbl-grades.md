## Subclaims
`q2 i?` An MLM predicting grades from frequencies of four session sequence clusters explained more variance (conditional R2 = 0.74) than frequency-based models (R2 total 0.65-0.71). [→ Mohammed Saqr 2023](#mohammed-saqr-2023)

## Evidence

### Mohammed Saqr 2023

Mohammed Saqr, Sonsoles López-Pernas. (2023). The temporal dynamics of online problem-based learning: Why and when sequence matters. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09385-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In this observational multilevel regression study of 8,009 coded interactions, a cluster-based sequence model was compared with frequency-based models from RQ3; the article states it was "higher than all previous models (conditional R2 = 0.74)". No effect size for the contrast is printed.

> "The model’s total explanatory power was substantial and higher than all previous models (conditional R2 = 0.74), and the part related to the fixed effects (marginal R2) is of 0.11."
