### Tseng Liang-Wei 2025

Tseng Liang-Wei, Lu Yi-Chin, Tseng Liang-Chi, Chen Yu-Chun, Chen Hsing-Yu. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT-4 on Taiwanese Traditional Chinese Medicine Licensing Examinations: Cross-Sectional Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/58897

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional benchmarking of GPT-4 against two licensed TCM resident doctors on 476 scored questions from the 2022 Taiwanese TCM licensing examination. The Results section reports "an overall accuracy of only 43.9% (209/476)" for GPT-4 versus 70% and 78.4% for the humans; no effect size is printed.

> "The GPT-4 model demonstrated an overall accuracy of only 43.9% (209/476). In comparison, 2 human evaluators achieved accuracy rates of 70% (333/476) and 78.4% (373/476), respectively"
