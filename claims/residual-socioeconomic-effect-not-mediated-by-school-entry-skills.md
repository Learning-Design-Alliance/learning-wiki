## Evidence

### Chittleborough 2014

Chittleborough, C. R., Mittinty, M. N., Lawlor, D. A., & Lynch, J. W. (2014). Effects of Simulated Interventions to Improve School Entry Academic Skills on Socioeconomic Inequalities in Educational Achievement. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12309

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Marginal structural model with stabilized inverse probability weights on the imputed ALSPAC sample (n = 11,764), estimating the controlled direct effect of socioeconomic disadvantage on poor educational achievement not mediated by school entry academic skills. Table 3 prints low-group RR = 2.28 [2.01, 2.59] and medium RR = 1.73 [1.54, 1.95].

> "The marginal structural model (Model 2) using inverse probability weights resulted in children of medium and low socioeconomic position having 73% and 128% increased risk of poor educational achievement, respectively."
