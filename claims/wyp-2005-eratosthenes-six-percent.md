The article recounts the American Physical Society's World Year of Physics project in which students from 700 high schools, teamed in pairs, recreated Eratosthenes' experiment; "the average of all the results" was within 6% of the true radius.

> "The average of all the results so obtained yielded a value for the Earth’s radius that differed from the true value by just 6%."
