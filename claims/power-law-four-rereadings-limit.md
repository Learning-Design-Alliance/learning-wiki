## Evidence

### Still 2012

Still, K. L., & Flynt, C. A. (2012). Does Repeated Reading Improve Reading Fluency and Comprehension for Struggling Adolescent Readers? JAASEP, Winter 2012. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1135781

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The article reports, citing Samuels et al. (2005) who cite O'Shea, Sindelar, and O'Shea (1985), that gains diminish after the fourth rereading; Samuels et al. suggest moving to another passage after four rereadings. Second-hand report; no effect size printed.

> "Research by O’Shea, Sindelar, and O’Shea (1985) showed that significant improvement occurred after each re-reading, up to the fourth reading and then the size of the gains decreased."
