## Evidence

### Tudge 1992

Tudge, J. R. H. (1992). Processes and Consequences of Peer Collaboration: A Vygotskian Analysis. Child Development, 63, 1364-1379. https://doi.org/10.2307/1131562

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-tailed t tests for dependent samples on second posttest-pretest gains in the 4-condition experiment, comparing gains to zero. The less competent partner group "improved significantly from pretest score (M = 0.68, SD = 0.89"; no effect size printed.

> "These analyses revealed that the group of less competent children was the only group that improved significantly from pretest score (M = 0.68, SD = 0.89, *(18) = 3.37, p < .005)."

### Tudge 1992 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same t-test analysis on second posttest-pretest differences: the more competent partner group "was the only group to decline significantly (M = -0.65, SD = 1.06"; no effect size printed.

> "The group of more competent children was the only group to decline significantly (M = -0.65, SD = 1.06, t{16) = -2.52, p < .03)."
