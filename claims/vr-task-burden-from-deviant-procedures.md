### Loeb Daniel 2025

Loeb Daniel, Shoemaker Jamie, Ely Kelly, Zackoff Matthew. (2025). Deconstructing Participant Behaviors in Virtual Reality Simulation: Ethnographic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65886

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Ethnographic analysis inferred increased task burden from observed behaviors such as repeated attempts, expressions of frustration, or prolonged fixation on a single action. The fluid bolus was "intentionally simplified for VR programming feasibility." Qualitative finding; no effect size printed.

> "this simplification created a discrepancy from real-world procedures. Experienced nurses, accustomed to a specific bolus administration technique, appeared frustrated by the VR version. This frustration led to disproportionate task fixation on what would otherwise have been a rudimentary task"
