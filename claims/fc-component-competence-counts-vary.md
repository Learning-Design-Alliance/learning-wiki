## Evidence

### MacKenzie Kenneth R. 2016

MacKenzie Kenneth R., Aning Jonathan. (2016). Defining competency in flexible cystoscopy: a novel approach using cumulative Sum analysis. BMC Urology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12894-016-0143-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CUSUM curve for atraumatic cystoscope passage (n = 409; acceptable failure rate 0.01, unacceptable 0.03). "During the first 51 attempts (Maximal point) there was a failure rate of 12 %"; competence followed 224 procedures.

### MacKenzie Kenneth R. 2016 (2)

MacKenzie Kenneth R., Aning Jonathan. (2016). Defining competency in flexible cystoscopy: a novel approach using cumulative Sum analysis. BMC Urology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12894-016-0143-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CUSUM curves for body/dome of bladder (n = 384) and bladder neck (n = 383) steps. Dome: "Acceptable performance can be concluded following attempt 59", competence at 221. Bladder neck: competence at 229, with an unacceptable control line crossed after a failure at procedure 103.

> "Acceptable performance can be concluded following attempt 59 when the acceptable control line is crossed (crossing the horizontal line in a downwards fashion). No further failures occur and performance continues to improve"
