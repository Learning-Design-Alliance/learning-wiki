The Perspective cites a recent study (Chan et al.) that directly addressed the alternative encoding-deficit hypothesis through a series of experiments, finding that "regardless of retrieval practice performance, the magnitudes of the testing effect were consistent."

> "This study found no causal link between retrieval success and the magnitude of the testing effect. In other words, regardless of retrieval practice performance, the magnitudes of the testing effect were consistent."
