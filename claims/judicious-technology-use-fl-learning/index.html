Evidence¶

Murphy 2020¶

Murphy, V. A., Arndt, H., Briggs Baffoe-Djan, J., Chalmers, H., Macaro, E., Rose, H., Vanderplank, R., & Woore, R. (2020). Foreign language learning and its impact on wider academic outcomes: A rapid evidence assessment. University of Oxford Department of Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED612981

q2 · i? · review · r3

Executive summary finding from the review's synthesis of updated studies, which covered themes including "the value of same language subtitles (captions) in the classroom for enhancing listening comprehension and vocabulary acquisition".

"Technology use just for the sake of it is not advantageous. However, judicious use of technology, video, film and TV (and their captions) can have a place in facilitating the development of foreign language knowledge and skill."
