## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among Chinese medical students, higher academic adaptability significantly predicts higher immersion in learning (absorption, enjoyment, intrinsic learning motivation). [→ Xie Yu Jin 2019](#xie-yu-jin-2019)

## Evidence

### Xie Yu Jin 2019

Xie Yu Jin, Cao De Pin, Sun Tao, Yang Li Bin. (2019). The effects of academic adaptability on academic burnout, immersion in learning, and academic performance among Chinese medical students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1640-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear regression (M2) from the same cross-sectional survey of 1977 medical students, controlling for gender, age, and grade. The article reports "a significant predictive effect on immersion in learning (Beta = 0.655, P<0.01)"; the Pearson correlation was 0.657 (P<0.01).
