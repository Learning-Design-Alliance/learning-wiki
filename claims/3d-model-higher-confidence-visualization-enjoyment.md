`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-studying Likert reflection in the pilot trial. Visualization agreement was 100% (n = 39) vs 45% (n = 19); confidence improvement was 54% vs 21% strongly agreeing; enjoyment was 97% vs 50% agreeing to some extent.

> "100% (n = 39) of the 3D Model Group agreed to some extent with the statement, "My resource helped me visualize the spatial relationship between muscles," compared to 45% (n = 19) of the Textbook Image Group."
