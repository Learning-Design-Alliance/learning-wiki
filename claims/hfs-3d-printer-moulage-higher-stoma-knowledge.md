## Evidence

### Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin 2026

Avcu Cagla and Yildiz Tülin. (2026). The Effect of 3D Printer and Moulage Use on Improving Stoma Care Knowledge and Skills in Nursing Students: A Randomized Controlled Trial. International Wound Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.71068

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

RCT with 76 second-year nursing students randomized via random number table to experimental (n = 38) or control (n = 38) groups. Mann–Whitney U test showed "significantly higher scores than the control group in terms of knowledge (p < 0.001, r = 0.463)". Effect size interpreted as medium-to-large per Cohen's guidelines.

> "Post‐training, the experimental group showed significantly higher scores than the control group in terms of knowledge (p < 0.001, r = 0.463), care skills (p < 0.001, r = 0.472), satisfaction (p = 0.019, r = 0.269) and self‐confidence (p = 0.001, r = 0.378)."
