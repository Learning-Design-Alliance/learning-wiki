## Evidence

### Fulya Turk 2017

Fulya Turk. (2017). Evaluation of the Effects of Conflict Resolution, Peace Education and Peer Mediation: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Education Studies; Vol. 11, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n1p25

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis comparing applications delivered to selected students (12 studies, g = 1.245) versus whole-class delivery (11 studies, g = 1.253). The difference was not statistically significant (Q = 0.001 < 3.841).

> "the effect size of the applications carried  out as a class (g= 1.253) is greater . However, the difference between the effect sizes is not statistically significant since the Q=0,001 value is below the critical value of 3.841"
