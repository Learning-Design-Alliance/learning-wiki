## Evidence

### N.G. Holmes 2017

N.G. Holmes, Jack Olsen, James L. Thomas, Carl E. Wieman. (2017). Value added or misattributed? A multi-institution study on the educational benefit of labs for reinforcing physics content. arXiv:1705.03580. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010129)

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA restricted to conceptual final-exam items (no calculation required) at two institutions where item categorization data was available. The combined average mean conceptual lab benefit was −0.62 ± 1.58%; Group η2partial = 0.001, per Table III.

> "An ANOVA com- paring the diﬀerences between lab-enrolled and non-lab-enrolled students on the diﬀerence scores for conceptual items was non-signiﬁcant: F (1, 1114) = 1 .60,p = .206"
