## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students' self-assessed otolaryngology knowledge and comfort in the operating theatre setting improved significantly after the rotation (both p < 0.05). [→ McInerney NJ 2025](#mcinerney-nj-2025)

## Evidence

### McInerney NJ 2025

McInerney NJ, Sexton GP, Roche A, Donnelly M, Skinner L. (2025). Development of an undergraduate otorhinolaryngology simulation education and human factors module: its impact on students' attitudes and perceptions. The Journal of Laryngology and Otology. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022215125103009

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post questionnaire comparison for 47 students analysed with Mann–Whitney U tests. Self-assessed knowledge rose from 2.09 to 3.88 and theatre comfort from 3.30 to 4.22, with "comfort with otolaryngology knowledge (p < 0.005)". No effect sizes are printed.

> "improvements in comfort with otolaryngology knowledge (p < 0.005) and the operating theatre setting (p < 0.005) suggested the effectiveness of the educational interventions"
