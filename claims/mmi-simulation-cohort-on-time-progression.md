Cohort comparison of on-time didactic-year progression at the same PA program. The article prints the percentages and counts for the MMI cohort and the two prior panel-interview cohorts; no effect size or statistical test for this contrast is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "only 6.4% (n = 4 of 62) students in the MMI cohort were unable to progress on-time through the didactic year of the program for academic reasons. During the two prior years’ cohorts of panel interviews, 8.0% (n = 5 of 62) and 10.1% (n = 6 of 55) of the class was unable to progress with their cohort during their didactic year for academic reasons"
