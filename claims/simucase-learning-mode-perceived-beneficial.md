## Evidence

### Elliott 2021

Elliott, Haley and Brumbaugh, Klaire (2021). Student Perceptions of a Simulated Clinical Experience: A Pilot Study. Teaching and Learning in Communication Sciences & Disorders, 5(1), Article 7. https://doi.org/10.30707/TLCSD5.1.1624982519.538001

`q2 · i?`

Qualitative phenomenological pilot: two focus groups with a total of 10 first- and second-semester graduate SLP students after an eight-week Simucase practicum. Participants "reached consensus that the learning mode aspect of Simucase (2019) was highly beneficial." No effect sizes are reported.

> "The participants reached consensus that the learning mode aspect of Simucase (2019) was highly beneficial. Participants liked the fact that they could attempt the case as many times as needed without their grade being affected."
