Passport to Literacy 2024¶

Passport to Literacy. (2024). Evidence Rating: strong. https://www.evidenceforesight.org/passport-to-literacy

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

A randomized control trial with fourth graders in 16 public schools across multiple districts compared Passport to Literacy to typical school services. The printed effect size for the GMRT Reading Comprehension gain is +0.28: the treatment group "gained significantly more on GMRT Reading Comprehension (effect size = + 0.28)".
