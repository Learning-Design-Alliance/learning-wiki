### Sun 2026

Sun, C., Major, L., Daltry, R., Obradovic, L., & Friedberg, A. (2026). Enhancing literacy and numeracy through teacher dashboards and digital personalised learning: an A/B/C test in Kenyan early years education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10703-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A three-arm A/B/C test with 4,487 Kenyan schools randomly assigned at the school level. Post-intervention mixed-effects modelling of monthly usage found the Grouped Dashboard partition significantly higher than the List Dashboard and control partitions, with no pre-intervention differences.

> "Learners in the Grouped Dashboard partition spent significantly more time on the DPL tool than those in the List Display partition (Z = −.4.04, p <.001) and the control partition (Z = −.4.19, p <.001)."
