# There was no significant difference in music theory post-test scores between pupils using the text-and-image (TI) mode and the audio-image-text (AIT) mode

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Aldalalah and Fong 2010

Osamah (Mohammad Ameen) Ahmad Aldalalah & Soon Fook Fong. (2010). Effects of Modality and Redundancy Principles on the Learning and Attitude of a Computer-Based Music Theory Lesson among Jordanian Primary Pupils. International Education Studies, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/ies

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc LSD pairwise comparison following a significant ANCOVA of post-test scores among the three treatment groups of 405 third-grade pupils. Descriptive means were 16.82 (TI) and 16.63 (AIT); the difference was not significant.

> "Finally there were no significant differences between pupils’ learning in TI group and pupils’ learning in AIT group (p = .994)."
