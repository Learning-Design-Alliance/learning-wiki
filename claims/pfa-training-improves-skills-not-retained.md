# PFA training improves community nurses' self-reported PFA skills immediately after training, but the skills advantage is not retained at 2 months

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Intervention-group nurses scored significantly higher on PFA skills than controls at T1 (r = 0.38), with no significant between-group difference at T2. [→ He Yazhou 2026](#he-yazhou-2026)
`q3 i?` By 2 months, the skills difference between the intervention and control groups was no longer statistically significant. [→ He Yazhou 2026 (2)](#he-yazhou-2026-2)

## Evidence

### He Yazhou 2026

He Yazhou, Ding Yulan, Yan Jiaqin, Xiong Xiaoning, Huang Shan, Li Sijian. (2026). Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Psychological First Aid Training for Community Nurses in China: A Quasi‐Experimental Study on Knowledge and Skills Enhancement. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70497

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-group Mann–Whitney comparison of self-reported PFA skills at T1 in the quasi-experiment (48 intervention vs 50 control nurses). The intervention group was significantly higher at "p < 0.001, r = 0.38", median difference 4.00.

> "immediately after the intervention (T1), the intervention group demonstrated significantly higher PFA skills scores compared to the control group (Z = 3.803, p < 0.001, r = 0.38, median difference = 4.00, 95% CI: 1.00–7.00)"

### He Yazhou 2026 (2)

He Yazhou, Ding Yulan, Yan Jiaqin, Xiong Xiaoning, Huang Shan, Li Sijian. (2026). Evaluation of the Effectiveness of Psychological First Aid Training for Community Nurses in China: A Quasi‐Experimental Study on Knowledge and Skills Enhancement. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70497

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up between-group comparison at T2 in the same study. The skills advantage was no longer significant; equivalence was not tested, and the authors note limited power (Power < 0.10) for small follow-up effects.

> "By 2 months after the intervention (T2), these differences were no longer significant (Z = −0.317, p = 0.751, r = 0.03, median difference = 0.00, 95% CI: −4.00 to 3.00)"
