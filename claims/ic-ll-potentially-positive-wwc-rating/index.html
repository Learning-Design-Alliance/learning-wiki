What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). Instructional Conversations and Literature Logs — WWC Intervention Report. http://crede.berkeley.edu/research/crede/research/llaa/1.5pubs.html

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Systematic WWC synthesis of two studies (one randomized controlled trial, one quasi-experiment), both meeting evidence standards with reservations. The rating fell short of "positive effects" because no study met standards for a strong design; no studies showed negative effects. The average reading achievement improvement index was +29 percentile points and English language development +23.

"Based on these two studies, the WWC found the intervention to have potentially positive effects on reading achievement and potentially positive effects on English language development."
