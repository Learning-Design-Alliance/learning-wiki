## Evidence

### Polónyiová K. 2025

Polónyiová K., Teličák P., Vitovič P., Hnilicová S. (2025). Psychometric analysis of the Slovak version of the satisfaction with simulation experience scale (measurement invariance). Medical Education Online, 30(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2534055

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pilot test of the forward-and-backward translated Slovak SSE version among 168 first-year medical students. The article reports "satisfactory results for the whole SSE scale (α = 0.911)" and subscale alphas of 0.791, 0.825 and 0.840; no scale adjustments were needed.

> "To verify internal consistency and reliability, Cronbach’s alpha was calculated, with satisfactory results for the whole SSE scale (α = 0.911) and its subscales: debriefing and reflection (α = 0.791), clinical reasoning (α = 0.825), and clinical learning (α = 0.840)."
