## Subclaims
`q2 i2` An ANOVA on post-test metacognitive awareness found no significant intervention effects (F(2,119) = .334, p = .717, η2 = .045), so H2 (no differential effect of domain-specific vs domain-general) was not rejected. [→ Eelco Braad 2022](#eelco-braad-2022)

## Evidence

### Eelco Braad 2022

Eelco Braad, Nick Degens, Wolmet Barendregt, Wijnand IJsselsteijn. (2022). Improving metacognition through self-explication in a digital self-regulated learning tool. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10156-2

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on post-test scores in the quasi-experiment, with Levene's test confirming equal error variance (F(2,117) = .080, p = .923). Contrasts between each intervention group and the comparison group were non-significant. The printed η2 = .045 is the only effect size.

> "No significant effects of the intervention on the post-test metacognitive awareness scores were found (H2), F(2,119) = .334, p = .717, η2 = .045."
