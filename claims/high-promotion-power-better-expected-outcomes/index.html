This is the report's own Key Findings statement comparing students at high versus average promotion power schools; no effect size, statistic, or magnitude is printed in the summary text provided.

"The expected outcomes of students attending high schools with high promotion power are substantially better than those of similar students attending high schools with average promotion power."
