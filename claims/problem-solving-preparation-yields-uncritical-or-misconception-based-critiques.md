### Nikhil Sanjay Borse 2026

Nikhil Sanjay Borse, Amir Bralin, Sean Savage, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Probing AI-generated physics solutions and preparing students to critique them. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12533

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of Solution-condition responses identified three patterns: uncritical acceptance of polished output, skepticism grounded in students' own misconceptions, and some targeted critiques of missing diagrams and skipped steps aligned with MAPS intent.

> "Many groups accepted the AI output largely because it appeared polished and organized, assigning high MAPS scores without clear evidence of checking the problem statement, physics, or graphical output."
