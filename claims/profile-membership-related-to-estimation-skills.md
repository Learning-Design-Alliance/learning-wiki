## Evidence

### Kainulainen et al. 2017

Mikko Kainulainen, Jake McMullen, and Erno Lehtinen (2017). Early Developmental Trajectories Toward Concepts of Rational Numbers. Cognition and Instruction, 35:1, 4-19. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1251287

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

As a validity check of the latent profiles, chi-squared tests compared class membership with grade level at each of the three time points, all χ² > 68.23, df = 8, ps < .001, with each profile showing an expected-direction adjusted residual at least at one time point.

> "Chi-squared estimates revealed that class membership and grade level were related at each time point, χ 2 s > 68.23, df = 8, ps < .001."

### Kainulainen et al. 2017 (2)

Mikko Kainulainen, Jake McMullen, and Erno Lehtinen (2017). Early Developmental Trajectories Toward Concepts of Rational Numbers. Cognition and Instruction, 35:1, 4-19. https://doi.org/10.1080/07370008.2016.1251287

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

An analysis of variance compared the five profiles' Time 2 number line estimation scores (placing fractions and decimals on a 13-cm number line, scored within ± 10% of true position), F(4, 246) = 29.28, p < .001.

> "Results reveal differences in rational number estimation skills across the profiles F(4, 246) = 29.28, p < .001."
