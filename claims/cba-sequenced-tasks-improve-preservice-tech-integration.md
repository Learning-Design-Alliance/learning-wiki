### Hyeonjin Kim and Michael J. Hannafin 2008

Hyeonjin Kim and Michael J. Hannafin. (2008). Grounded design of web-enhanced case-based activity. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-006-9010-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case study of five preservice teachers in a web-enhanced CBA course; the article reports that "all five preservice teachers extended their understanding of the potential of technology" during the final WebQuest task, based on preservice teachers' reports and interviews.

> "the final task—planning and developing a student-centered constructivist learning WebQuest lesson—required greater task complexity, content integration, and technology integration than previous projects. During this phase, all five preservice teachers extended their understanding of the potential of technology and connected its role to student thinking and learning."
