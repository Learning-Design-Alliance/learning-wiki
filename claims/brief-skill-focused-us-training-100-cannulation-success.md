### Lin Xu-Nian 2026

Lin Xu-Nian, Lu Cheng-Wei, Lin Tzu-Yu, Chou San-Fang, Chang Ya-Ying. (2026). Rapid skill acquisition through task-specific ultrasound training for peripheral cannulation in medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09935-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training practical assessment of 30 participants (22 clerks, 8 PGY residents) in a single-group pilot study. The median number of attempts required for successful cannulation was 1, and the median total procedure time was 82 s. The article notes "potential ceiling effects or limited assessment stringency".

> "After the training, all participants (100%) successfully achieved the key assessments, including clear visualization of the needle tip, identification of target vessel structures, and successful US-guided cannulation in the simulation model (Table 4)."
