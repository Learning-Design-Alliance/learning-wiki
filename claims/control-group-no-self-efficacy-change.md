## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sare Şengül 2011

Sare Şengül. (2011). Effects of Concept Cartoons on Mathematics Self-Efficacy of 7th Grade Students. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 11(4), 2305-2313. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/estp

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dependent-group t-test on the 48-student control group taught with teacher-centred activities; no significant difference, t(47)= -1.396, p>.05. The article attributes this to limited activities and passive, receptive roles in teacher-centred lessons. No effect size is printed.

> "Results of the dependent group t-test conducted on the mathematics self-efficacy perception scale pre-test and post-test scores of the control group showed no significant difference at a statistical level of significance of 0.05 [t(47)= -1.396, p>.05]."
