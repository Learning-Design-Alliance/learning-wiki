### Yanghee Kim 2021

In the ethnographic session data, a Latinx boy and white girl who initially repeated their own gendered choices ended the exchange agreeing on "Optimus Prime" after the robot asked whether the two could talk first and choose one.

> "The following example shows that these prompts helped another pair of children, a white girl (GWLO) and a Latinx boy (BLEX), to agree on a toy for Skusie's friend."
