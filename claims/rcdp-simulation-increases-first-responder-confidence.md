## Evidence

### Songer K 2025

Songer K, Fiero M, Roberts J. (2025). First 3 Minutes: A Rapid Cycle Deliberate Practice Pediatric Resuscitation Simulation for Multidisciplinary Staff. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11529

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired pre/post analysis of self-reported confidence on a 5-point Likert scale, compared with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests among 154 participants with paired surveys. The article reports confidence "significantly improved in providing high-quality CPR" and in managing airway, breathing, and circulation; only test statistics are printed, no effect size.

> "significantly improved in providing high-quality CPR (n = 151; Z = 9.4; p < .01) and managing airway, breathing, and circulation in the first 3 minutes of a code situation (n = 154; Z = 9.6; p < .01)"
