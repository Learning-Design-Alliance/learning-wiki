q3 · i? · design · r2

t-test comparisons of CTAT scores by gender within each pathway in the main study (293 male, 168 female). The article reports no significant gender differences within either pathway; female students showed slightly higher, non-significant means.

"In contrast, no significant gender differences were observed within either educational pathway, a finding consistent with prior studies reporting minimal gender effects on CT performance"
