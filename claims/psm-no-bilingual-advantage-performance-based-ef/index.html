Evidence¶

Goodrich 2019¶

Goodrich, J. M., Koziol, N. A., Yoon, H., & Leiva, S. (2019). Do Spanish-English Bilingual Children Outperform Monolingual English-Speaking Children on Executive Function Tasks in Early Childhood? A Propensity Score Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/edu

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Propensity score matched comparison (252 pairs) on direct assessments: the WJ-III Numbers Reversed task for working memory and the Dimensional Change Card Sort for cognitive flexibility. The article reports "no effects of bilingualism were observed" on these performance-based measures; no effect sizes are printed.

"In contrast, no effects of bilingualism were observed for performance-based measures of working memory or cognitive flexibility."
