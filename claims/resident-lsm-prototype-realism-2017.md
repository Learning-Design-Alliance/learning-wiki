## Evidence

### Sadovnikova A 2020

Sadovnikova A, Chuisano SA, Ma K, Grabowski A, Stanley KP, Mitchell KB, Eglash A, Plott JS, Zielinski RE, Anderson OS. (2020). Development and evaluation of a high-fidelity lactation simulation model for health professional breastfeeding education. International Breastfeeding Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13006-020-0254-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

User-testing in a 50-min prenatal breastfeeding assessment workshop for first-year residents (N = 17) at the University of Michigan in June 2017, IRB-approved (HUM00125612). Residents agreed the LSM's nipples and breast tissue (5.5/7) looked and felt realistic; 79% identified the large plugged duct and the scar. Descriptive ratings only, no inferential statistics.

> "Participants agreed the LSM's nipples and breast tissue (5.5/7) looked and felt realistic. The majority (79%) of physician residents identified the large plugged duct and the scar when performing a breast assessment"
