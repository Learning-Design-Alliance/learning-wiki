Evidence¶

Vorderobermeier 2024¶

Vorderobermeier, A., Abel, J., & Sailer, M. (2024). Theoretical foundations and approaches in research on educational escape rooms: A systematic review. Educational Research Review, 44, 100625. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100625

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of the 24 included articles (2016-2022, mostly higher education) enumerates the theoretical landscape. The quote lists "nine theories and models" exactly as identified across empirical, developmental, and review articles.

"The systematic review shows that empirical studies, articles about development, and systematic reviews on EERs have used nine theories and models: Socio-cultural Approach to Learning and Social Constructivism, Active Learning, Collaborative Learning, Experiential Learning, Problem-Based Learning, Situated Learning Theory, Self-Determination Theory, Flow Theory and Bloom’s Taxonomy."
