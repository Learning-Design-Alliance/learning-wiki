One-way ANOVA at time point 3 found no significant differences among experts, residents, and allied health professionals on either GRS measure, but a significant OSATS difference; post-hoc testing identified the allied-health-versus-expert contrast (p = 0.005; 95% CI, 0.8 to 5.1).

> "Post-hoc testing of the OSATS scores revealed that the significant difference was between the allied health group and the experts, with the allied health group scoring slightly higher on the OSATS (p = 0.005; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.8 to 5.1)."
