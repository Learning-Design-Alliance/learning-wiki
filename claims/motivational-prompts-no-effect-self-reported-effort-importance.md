## Evidence

### Hawthorne 2015

Hawthorne, K. A., Bol, L., Pribesh, S., & Suh, Y. (2015). Effects of Motivational Prompts on Motivation, Effort, and Performance on a Low-Stakes Standardized Test. Journal of General Education, 64(2).

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report motivation was measured with the 10-item Student Opinion Scale (importance and effort subscales) immediately after the test. Students in the personal condition reported descriptively higher mean importance and effort scores, but neither difference reached statistical significance.

> "the results from the one-way ANOVA indicated that motivational condition had no significant effect on students’ importance scores, F(3, 83) = .676, p > .05, η2 = .023."
