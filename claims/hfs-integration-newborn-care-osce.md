## Evidence

### Ismail Ahmad 2026

Ismail Ahmad, Al-Modallal Hanan, Aldardeir Njoud, Gomma Nada, Eltohamy Nadia Abd ElHamed. (2026). The Impact of High‐Fidelity Simulation and Curriculum Integration on Nursing Students’ Competence and Satisfaction: A Quasiexperimental Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13140285/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test comparison of 20-item newborn care OSCE scores. The intervention group scored "95% vs. 56%"; the largest item difference was "Keep the baby’s face down to prevent aspiration" (94% vs. 33%). No effect size is printed for this comparison.

> "For the students’ OSCE of immediate newborn care, the total score of students exposed to the HFS/curriculum‐integrated session was significantly higher than the control group score (95% vs. 56%, p < 0.05)."
