### Trigg 2026

Trigg, A. B., Keyes, A. W., Gray, S., Hatch, V., Brunet, K. T., & Heller, S. S. (2026). The Effects of Early Childhood Mental Health Consultation on Early Childhood Teachers' Perceptions of Children's Challenging Behaviors, Expulsion Risk, and the Moderating Role of Teaching Stress. Infant Mental Health Journal, 47, e70079. https://doi.org/10.1002/imhj.70079

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results of multilevel regression on teacher-rated DECA outcomes (T scores, mean 50) with classroom and center random intercepts, covarying class size and classroom age. Table 4 shows, for example, DECA Total Protective Factors rising from 42.62 to 46.32 (Est −3.70, p=.000) and Total Behavioral Concerns falling from 59.00 to 56.22 (p=.000).

> "At post‐consultation, teachers reported significantly higher child resilience and protective factors (i.e., total protective factors as well as the subscales of self‐regulation, attachment, and initiative), lower child‐ and classroom‐level behavioral concerns (i.e., DECA total behavior concerns, SDQ‐IS child and classroom behavior problems)"
