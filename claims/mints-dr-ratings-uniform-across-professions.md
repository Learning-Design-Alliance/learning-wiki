## Evidence

### Bracco Fabrizio 2017

Bracco Fabrizio, Masini Michele, De Tonetti Gabriele, Brogioni Francesca, Amidani Arianna, Monichino Sara, Maltoni Alessandra, Dato Andrea, Grattarola Claudia, Cordone Massimo, Torre Giancarlo, Launo Claudio, Chiorri Carlo, Celleno Danilo. `q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Linear Mixed Models (preferred over one-way ANOVA to account for nesting of participants into courses) compared ratings across the four professional categories; the article reports F(3, 81.95) = 1.06, p = .369, η2 = .01 for usefulness and F(3, 86) = 1.75, p = .162, η2 = .02 for usability.

> "Results revealed that ratings did not statistically differ across the professional categories (Table 2, rightmost column)."
