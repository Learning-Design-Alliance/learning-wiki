# The overt-retrieval advantage does not significantly differ by definition complexity (number of idea units), contrary to a retrieval-dynamics prediction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In both experiments, the practice modality × number of idea units interaction on criterion test performance was not significant. [→ Michelle L. Rivers 2025](#michelle-l-rivers-2025)

## Evidence

### Michelle L. Rivers 2025

Michelle L. Rivers, Paige E. Northern, Sarah K. Tauber. (2025). Does Retrieval Demand Moderate the Effectiveness of Covert Retrieval Practice? Comparing Covert and Overt Retrieval Practice for Learning Key Terms and Definitions. Educational Psychology Review (2025) 37:61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10040-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory repeated-measures ANOVA in Experiment 1 on definitions with three versus four idea units: four-unit definitions were harder overall (ηp2 = 0.42), but "the interaction was not significant" (ηp2 = 0.001); Experiment 2 replicated (F(2, 188) = 0.10, p = 0.91).

> "the main effect of idea units (three idea units M = 0.47, SE = 0.02; four idea units M = 0.37, SE = 0.03), F(1, 48) = 34.21, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.42. However, the interaction was not significant, F(2, 96) = 0.04, p = 0.96, ηp 2 = 0.001."
