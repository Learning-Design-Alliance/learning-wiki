## Evidence

### Maloney S 2013

Maloney S, Chamberlain M, Morrison S, Kotsanas G, Keating JL, Ilic D. (2013). Health Professional Learner Attitudes and Use of Digital Learning Resources. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Audit of 2010 log-on session lengths by year level, described with means and tested with a Kruskal-Wallis test. Means were 46.9, 52.2, 55.2, and 59.6 minutes for years 1-4; a post hoc Pearson correlation "returned a significant Pearson correlation coefficient of 0.99 (P=.01)".

> "Average session lengths for each year level ranged from 46 minutes to 59 minutes, with average session times increasing with year level (Table 1). A Kruskal-Wallis test showed that there was a significant (P=.01) difference in mean session length between year levels."
