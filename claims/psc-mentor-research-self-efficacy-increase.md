## Evidence

### Blumer 2020

Blumer, H., McLaughlin, M. A., Stewart, J., Williamson, K., Lorimer, D. R., Heatherly, S. A., Swiggum, J. K., Lynch, R. S., Zabriskie, C., Lewandowska, N., Roy, A., & Au, S. (2020). The Pulsar Search Collaboratory: Current Status and Future Prospects. arXiv:1909.05104. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/10.0000032](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0000032)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post survey of PSC II undergraduate mentors on 5-point Likert-scale items measuring scientific self-efficacy, tested with t-tests and Cohen's d. The article reports "very large eﬀects" for research skills and research understanding self-efficacy.

> "The student mentors signiﬁcantly increased their self-eﬃcacy toward research skills ( p < 0.001, d = 1.5) and research understanding ( p < 0.001, d = 2.2), very large eﬀects."
