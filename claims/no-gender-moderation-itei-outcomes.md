# The ITEI–outcomes model does not differ between gender groups

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2023 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Multigroup moderation analysis found no differences between girls and boys in the predictive association between ITEIs and academic and emotional outcomes. [→ Ana Costa 2023](#ana-costa-2023)

## Evidence

### Ana Costa 2023

Ana Costa, Luísa Faria. (2023). Implicit Theories of Emotional Intelligence and Students’ Emotional and Academic Outcomes. Psychological Reports. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941231183327

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multigroup moderation analysis comparing a model with structural paths free versus constrained equal across gender groups; the constrained model showed "a nonsignificant χ2 difference" (Δχ2(15, N = 130) = 10.308, p = .80), supporting no gender moderation. Equivalence was not directly tested.

> "Model comparison exhibited a nonsignificant χ2 difference between the unconstrained and constrained models, which suggests that the final model did not differ between groups, in this case, gender, Δχ2(15, N = 130) = 10.308, p = .80."
