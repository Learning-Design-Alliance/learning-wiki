Reading, Writing, Math, and Content-Area Interventions for Improving Behavioral and Academic Outcomes of Students With Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(2), 119-138. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1194543

`q2 · i1` · `review · r2`

Meta-analysis of 11 studies (Therrien et al., 2014), 3 single-case and 8 group designs, with 1st-12th grade participants examining classroom science instruction; the review reports a calculated mean effect size of .471, a small to medium impact on achievement.

> "Across all of the group studies, the calculated mean effect size of .471 obtained indicated that the interventions as a whole had a small to medium impact on the students with EBD achievement."
