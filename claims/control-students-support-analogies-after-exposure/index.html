Evidence¶

Dincer 2011¶

Dincer, S. (2011). Exploring the Impacts of Analogies on Computer Hardware. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932231

q2 · i? · causal · r2

After the post-test, control-group students were given several example analogies and asked whether analogies would benefit future lectures. The article reports "88.37% of the students (n=38) have reported that they definitely support the use of analogies in lectures."

"88.37% of the students (n=38) have reported that they definitely support the use of analogies in lectures while 9.30% (n=4) reported that it would not make any difference for them and 2.33% (n=1) reported that they do not want analogies."
