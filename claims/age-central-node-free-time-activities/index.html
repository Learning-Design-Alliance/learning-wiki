Evidence¶

Miccoli 2023¶

Miccoli, L., Arias, M. Á. P., & Santiago, J. (2023). A network analysis on digital media use, reading enjoyment, and orthography precision in a highly educated sample. Computers & Education, 207, 104932. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104932

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Network structure results: older participants watched more TV (.26) and read more (.21); younger participants spent more hours studying (-.31), had greater social network use (-.23), and spent more time with friends in person (-.23).

"Age was a central node in the network structure, as it was strongly related to participants’ use of time. Positive blue edges indicate that older participants watched more TV (.26) and read more (.21)."
