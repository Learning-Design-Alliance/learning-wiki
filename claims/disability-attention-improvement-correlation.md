### Ferguson M. A. 2014 (2)

Ferguson M. A., Henshaw H., Clark D. P. A., Moore D. R. (2014). Benefits of Phoneme Discrimination Training in a Randomized Controlled Trial of 50- to 74-Year-Olds With Mild Hearing Loss. Ear and Hearing. https://doi.org/10.1097/AUD.0000000000000020

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison within the IT group: the 9 dual improvers reported greater baseline disability (42.3% versus 29.0%, p < 0.05) and poorer SSQ Speech (4.7 versus 6.0, p < 0.05). The article states no other baseline factors predicted benefit.

> "these 9 participants reported significantly greater Disability at baseline (t1) than the remaining participants in this group on overall GHABP (42.3% versus 29.0%, p < 0.05)"
