The article reports, citing Ramos et al., a systematic review of 120 studies (43 focused on in-service teachers) spanning 2003 to 2019. The review's categories were "observation and collaborative analysis" (most popular), collaborative video-supported authoring, collaborative learning based on video content, and video-supported synchronous collaboration (least utilized).
