### Katharina Scheiter 2026

Katharina Scheiter, Alexander Renkl. (2026). On the challenges of investigating lasting learning: A commentary. Learning and Instruction 105 (2026) 102417. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102417

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reports a classroom study of elementary school children comparing interleaved with blocked spelling practice; the delayed test showed benefits for "words that they had previously studied during training as well as words not previously seen (near transfer problems)". No effect size is printed in this passage.

> "Klimovich and Richter (2025) demonstrate a lasting learning effect in an 8-week delayed test: elementary school children were better able to correctly spell words that they had previously studied during training as well as words not previously seen (near transfer problems) after applying interleaved compared to blocked practice."

### Katharina Scheiter 2026 (2)

The commentary summarizes four experiments on second-language grammar acquisition using a 1-week delayed posttest, described as "corroborating evidence for the interleaving effect". No effect size is printed in this passage.

> "Pan et al. (2025), using a 1-week delayed posttest, found corroborating evidence for the interleaving effect in a second-language grammar acquisition task (Spanish verb conjugation, tense recognition) across four experiments."
