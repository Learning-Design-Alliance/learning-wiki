Thematic analysis (Table 3) organized student accounts of 'being a good professional' into four contexts, with sub-themes such as empathy, trustworthiness, communication, and teamwork. The first three contexts 'match closely with findings of previous studies', while the intellectual-responsibility context was voiced only by G1 students.

> "qualities, attitudes, and behaviors they perceived as necessary features of physician identity which, in turn, were directly grounded in some distinctive contexts: 1) professional responsibility; 2) physician-patient relationships; 3) physician-physician/healthcare professional relationships; and 4) responsibility of the physician as an intellectual"
