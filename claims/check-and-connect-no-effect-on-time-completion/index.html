Dropout Prevention intervention report: Check & Connect 2015¶

Dropout Prevention intervention report: Check & Connect. (2015). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/78

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

The 2005 replication RCT (144 students) examined whether students completed school within 4 years of entering ninth grade. The WWC table reports 30% vs 29% completion, mean difference 1, effect size 0.03, improvement index +1, p > .05.

"The study indicated that there was no statistically significant or substantially important effect on on-time high school completion."
