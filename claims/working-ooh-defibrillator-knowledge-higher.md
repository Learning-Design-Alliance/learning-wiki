# Working OOHEP know their defibrillator better and answer a defibrillation MCQ correctly more often than inactive OOHEP

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Inactive OOHEP are less likely to know the kind of defibrillator in use, and working OOHEP more often answer the witnessed-arrest ventricular-fibrillation MCQ correctly. [→ Fischer H 2014](#fischer-h-2014)

## Evidence

### Fischer H 2014

Fischer H, Bachmann K, Strunk G, Neuhold S, Zapletal B, Maurer C, Fast A, Stumpf D, Greif R. (2014). Translation of ERC resuscitation guidelines into clinical practice by emergency physicians. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-22-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression from the refresher-course survey shows inactive status predicts not knowing the defibrillator in use, "OR = 0.203, p < 0.01"; one third of inactive OOHEP did not know their defibrillator type, and 80.2% of working versus 60.7% of inactive OOHEP answered the defibrillation MCQ correctly (p < 0.001).

> "Additionally, inactive OOHEP are less likely to know the kind of defibrillator in use (OR = 0.203, p < 0.01)."
