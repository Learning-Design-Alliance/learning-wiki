Dongming Mei 2026¶

Dongming Mei, Katherine Moore, and Ben Sayler. (2026). Preparing Students for AI-Powered Materials Discovery: A Workflow-Aligned Framework for AI Literacy, Equity, and Scientific Judgment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09624 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.09624

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The paper, a workshop-informed white paper and curriculum-oriented position article rather than a review, attributes this to meta-analyses by VanLehn and by Kulik and Fletcher, stating that 'ITS can improve learning outcomes, particularly when systems provide immediate, actionable feedback.' No pooled effect size is printed in the paper's account.

"Meta-analytic evidence indicates that ITS can improve learning outcomes, particularly when systems provide immediate, actionable feedback and when instruction is aligned with clear learning objectives"

Related Claims¶

  • A review reports that pretesting helps paired-associate learning only when correct-answer feedback is immediate, but helps trivia-fact learning even when feedback is delayed up to 24 hours — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • AI-supported adaptive systems are reported to enhance adult learner engagement, motivation, and outcomes when aligned with learner goals and prior knowledge — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Intelligent tutoring systems improve achievement over non-individualised instruction — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Intelligent tutoring systems are reported to improve learning outcomes, especially in structured knowledge domains — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Human tutoring has a medium effect over no tutoring, and in one small study students learned as well when tutors only prompted them as when tutors also explained and gave feedback — related
  • Feedback improves learning by a medium amount on average, but its effect varies widely with the information it carries, and a substantial share of feedback effects are negative — related
  • One-to-one human tutoring lifts an ordinary student well beyond the average classroom with an effect of about d = 0.79 (VanLehn, 2011, as reported) — related
  • Meta-analytic evidence indicates intelligent tutoring systems yield positive learning outcomes compared with non-ITS conditions — related
  • Meta-analytic evidence reviewed reports medium-to-large positive effects of AI-enabled adaptive learning on cognitive outcomes (g = 0.50–0.70) — related
  • Intelligent tutoring systems are a mature, technologically sophisticated implementation of AI-driven personalized learning with mostly positive outcomes in programming, mathematics, and STEM — related
  • Intelligent tutoring systems with dynamic student modeling increase learning gains over regular instruction — related
  • ITS augmentation: meta-analyses find medium to large learning benefits of ITS over unsupported learning and small to medium gains over less adaptive computer-assisted learning — related
