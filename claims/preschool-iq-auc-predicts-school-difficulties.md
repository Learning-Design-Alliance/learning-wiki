### Wiingreen R 2025

Wiingreen R, Greisen G, Esbjørn BH, Løkkegaard ECL, Torp‐Pedersen C, Sørensen KK, Andersen MP, Hansen BM (2025). Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Receiver operator curve analysis in the cohort of 248 children born extremely preterm or with extremely low birthweight. The AUC of 0.80 corresponds to the article's characterisation of moderate/high predictive accuracy for preschool IQ as a screening tool for school difficulties in this population.

> "The receiver operator curve for IQ to predict school difficulties showed an AUC of 0.80 (95% CI 0.74–0.86) (Figure 1)."

### Wiingreen R 2025 (2)

Wiingreen R, Greisen G, Esbjørn BH, Løkkegaard ECL, Torp‐Pedersen C, Sørensen KK, Andersen MP, Hansen BM (2025). Five‐year neurodevelopmental assessment of extremely preterm or extremely low birthweight infants: Association with school performance. Acta Paediatr. 2025;114:131–140. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.17416

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Diagnostic accuracy statistics for two preschool IQ cut-offs against school difficulties. The stricter −2 SD cut-off traded sensitivity (0.44) for specificity (0.96) relative to the −1 SD cut-off. Values are printed proportions with confidence intervals; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "The sensitivity for cut‐offs for IQ <–1 SD and IQ <–2 SD were 0.72 (95% CI 0.61–0.81) and 0.44 (95% CI 0.33–0.55), respectively. Correspondingly, the specificity for IQ <–1 SD and IQ <–2 SD were 0.77 (95% CI 0.70–0.83) and 0.96 (95% CI 0.91–0.98)"
