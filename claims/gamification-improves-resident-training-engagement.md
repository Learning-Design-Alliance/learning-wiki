## Evidence

### Kim T. Edward 2019

Kim T. Edward, Tsui Ban C.H. (2019). Simulation-based ultrasound-guided regional anesthesia curriculum for anesthesiology residents. Korean J Anesthesiol. https://doi.org/10.4097/kja.d.18.00317

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The review reports a study (Enter et al.) of voluntary six-week simulation-based coronary anastomosis training tied to a live competition; residents "increased their competency in task performance" and initial poor performers caught up via self-initiated training.

> "the baseline disparity in skill level observed among residents was nonexistent after six weeks, suggesting that initial poor performers were able to catch up with more skilled residents by engaging in self-initiated training."

### Kim T. Edward 2019 (2)

Kim T. Edward, Tsui Ban C.H. (2019). Simulation-based ultrasound-guided regional anesthesia curriculum for anesthesiology residents. Korean J Anesthesiol. https://doi.org/10.4097/kja.d.18.00317

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a study (Lobo et al.) integrating team competition into a simulation-based point-of-care ultrasound curriculum for emergency medicine residents; participation "resulted in improved knowledge of basic ultrasound technical skills, image interpretation, and patient management", plus improved camaraderie.

> "Resident participation resulted in improved knowledge of basic ultrasound technical skills, image interpretation, and patient management."
