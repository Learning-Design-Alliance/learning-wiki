Document analysis of the AIED 2024 proceedings coded onto AI×Ed. The authors report "three out of four papers that lie on the bottom half have used LLMs", including an award-winning teachable-agent paper leveraging LLM role-playing and papers evaluating LLMs with methods usually applied to people.

"Although still only a few, we see more papers on the bottom half of AI×Ed. Interestingly, three out of four papers that lie on the bottom half have used LLMs."
