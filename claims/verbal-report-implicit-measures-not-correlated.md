## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Correlations between the implicit learning measure and aftereffects, and between the slow-process learning rate and those measures, were small and nonsignificant in both groups. [→ Yamada 2025](#yamada-2025)

## Evidence

### Yamada 2025

Yamada, C., Itaguchi, Y., & Rodríguez-Aranda, C. (2025). Unconscious cultural cognitive biases in explicit processes of visuomotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00335-0

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

A correlation analysis in the cross-cultural experiment (n = 24 per group) found the implicit learning measure was "not strong in both groups" in its correlations with aftereffects (Norwegian r = 0.08 and r = 0.18; Japanese r = −0.23 and r = −0.31); slow-process learning rates also did not correlate with these measures.

> "the correlation coefﬁcients between the implicit learning measure in the last period of the adaptation block and the aftereffects in the terminal- feedback and washout blocks were not strong in both groups"
