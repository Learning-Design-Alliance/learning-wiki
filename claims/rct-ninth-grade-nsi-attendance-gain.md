## Evidence

### Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report 2024

Evaluation of the Networks for School Improvement Initiative: Interim Summary Report. (2024). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA242-1.html

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Randomized controlled design for Cohort 3 NSI comparing schools randomly assigned to immediate participation with schools assigned to delay participation. The report describes the attendance effect as moderate and notes the matched comparison analysis did not detect it.

> "One difference is that the 9th-grade on-track NSI in the random assignment study had a moderate impact on attendance rates (equivalent to a 2 percentage point increase in the attendance rate, from 79 percent to 81 percent), while the 9th-grade on-track NSI in the matched comparison analysis did not."
