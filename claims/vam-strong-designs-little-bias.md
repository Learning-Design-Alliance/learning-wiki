## Evidence

### Koedel 2015

Koedel, C., Mihaly, K., & Rockoff, J. E. (2015). Value-Added Modeling: A Review. Preprint, forthcoming at Economics of Education Review. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Value-Added+Modeling+A+Review+Koedel

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review synthesis of the bias literature, including random-assignment experiments and quasi-experimental teacher-switching studies. The review reports "compelling evidence that estimates of teacher value-added from standard models are not meaningfully biased" and notes no direct counterevidence of substantial bias.

> "To date, the studies that have used the strongest research designs provide compelling evidence that estimates of teacher value-added from standard models are not meaningfully biased by student-teacher sorting along observed or unobserved dimensions."
