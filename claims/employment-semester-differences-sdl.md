Independent t-tests comparing demographic subgroups in the 300-student sample. Later-semester students scored higher (fifth-to-eighth semester mean 153.39 vs 131.39), and employed students scored higher (147.21 vs 124.74) than non-employed peers.

> "The results of t-test analysis have shown that among the demographic variables, academic semester showed a significant difference of 8 points, while employment status showed a significant difference of 6 points in self-directed learning scores."
