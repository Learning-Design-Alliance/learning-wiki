### Liu Hsing-Yuan 2026

Liu Hsing-Yuan. (2026). Skill-based competencies, not traits, are associated with design thinking–aligned teaching behaviours in nursing education: a cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09830-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Supplemental exploratory sensitivity analysis dichotomising 95 faculty at the median CTBS score (4.5) into high (n = 50) and low (n = 45) groups, compared with independent-samples t-tests. The article reports the higher group "demonstrated significantly higher levels of both skill-based" and trait-based competencies.

> "the higher CTBS group demonstrated significantly higher levels of both skill-based design thinking competencies (CSI-TW; p < 0.001) and trait-based design thinking dispositions (DTTQ-TW; p < 0.001)."
