## Evidence

### Pelly T. 2021

Pelly T., Vance-Daniel J., Linder C. (2021). Characteristics of laparoscopic and open hernia repair simulation models: a systematic review. Hernia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8251686/

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomised controlled trial in which 14 surgical residents were assigned to a simulation-based curriculum or control group and assessed with the GOALS-GH tool; the review reports "statistically significant improvement in GOALS-GH scores by a mean of 3.4 points". No effect size is printed.

> "On operating room assessment after 15 days, the simulation-based group was found to have statistically significant improvement in GOALS-GH scores by a mean of 3.4 points, where the control group did not."
