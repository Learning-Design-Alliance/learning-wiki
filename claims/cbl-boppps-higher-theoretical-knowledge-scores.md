## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Master's students taught with CBL combined with BOPPPS scored higher on basic theoretical knowledge, clinical case analysis, and total theoretical scores than students taught with traditional LBL, with statistically significant differences. [→ Gao Ning 2024](#gao-ning-2024)

## Evidence

### Gao Ning 2024

Gao Ning, Fu Kun, Li Na, He Wei. (2024). Research on the application of CBL combined with BOPPPS teaching mode in oral and maxillofacial surgery teaching: a randomized controlled study. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-77432-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled comparison of 19 experimental versus 19 control master's students after a 12-month clinical course. Table 1 reports basic theoretical knowledge "56.05 ± 1.51" versus "48.16 ± 4.56", clinical case analysis "37.95 ± 1.87" versus "29.84 ± 6.06", and total score "94.00 ± 2.19" versus "78.00 ± 9.29" (t = 7.163, 5.573, 7.307; p = 0.000).

> "The theoretical text results of the experimental group including basic theoretical knowledge, clinical case analysis and total scores were better than those in the control group, with statistically significant difference (P < 0.05), as shown in Table 1."
