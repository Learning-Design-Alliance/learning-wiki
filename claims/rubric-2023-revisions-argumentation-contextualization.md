The version-update note records the authors' own 2023 revisions to the rubric definitions, including adding selecting and listing to Level 1 descriptions and other minor modifications. This is a documented design change, not an empirical test.

> "May/June 2023: We specified that historical argumentation should be in relation to evidence and that the evaluation should be the student's. We modified historical contextualization to focus on explaining the relevance of contextual factors rather than providing analyses."
