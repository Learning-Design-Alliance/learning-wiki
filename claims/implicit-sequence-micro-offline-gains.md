# Early learning of an implicit motor sequence is accounted for by micro-offline gains during brief rest periods rather than by gains during active practice

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Total early learning on an implicit SRTT was significant (d = 2.73) and was accounted for by micro-offline performance gains during rest (d = 0.64) rather than micro-online gains within practice, which were not significant. [→ Emily Brooks 2024](#emily-brooks-2024)

## Evidence

### Emily Brooks 2024

Emily Brooks, Sarah Wallis, Joshua Hendrikse & James Coxon. (2024). Micro-consolidation occurs when learning an implicit motor sequence, but is not influenced by HIIT exercise. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00238-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Controlled experiment: 38 adults completed an implicit serial reaction time task alternating 10-s practice and rest; analysis restricted to blocks 1-7. Total learning d = 2.73 and micro-offline gains M = 0.84 were significant; the article reports micro-online gains were not (t(33) = 0.22, p = 0.83). The "early learning was accounted for by performance gains during micro-offline periods".

> "Across the entire sample we observed significant total learning ( M = 0.60, SD = 0.22), t (33) = 15.89, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.52, 0.68], d = 2.73. At the group level, this early learning was accounted for by performance gains during micro-offline periods (M = 0.84, SD = 1.32)"
