## Evidence

### Sze Kai Ping 2026

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). Human-Edited Generative AI-Assisted Multiple-Choice Questions in Postgraduate Family Medicine: Blinded Cross-Sectional Comparative Psychometric Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Item-level comparison of 30 GenAI-assisted and 30 educator-crafted items answered by 73 participants; mean corrected point-biserial was 0.09 (SD 0.15) vs 0.18 (SD 0.15). The printed effect size is "Cohen d=−0.56"; the P=.04 fell below .05 but not the Bonferroni threshold of .025.

> "The mean difference was −0.08 (95% CI −0.16 to −0.01; P=.04; Cohen d=−0.56), which was below 0.05 but did not meet the Bonferroni-corrected threshold of α=.025 for this family of comparisons"

### Sze Kai Ping 2026 (2)

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). Human-Edited Generative AI-Assisted Multiple-Choice Questions in Postgraduate Family Medicine: Blinded Cross-Sectional Comparative Psychometric Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item classification counts from the same item-level analysis: 6 (20.0%) GenAI-assisted versus 3 (10.0%) educator-crafted items showed negative discrimination, and only 2 GenAI-assisted (6.7%) versus 4 educator-crafted (13.3%) achieved the ≥0.30 good-discrimination threshold. No effect size is printed for these counts.

> "More concerningly, 6 GenAI-assisted items (20.0%) had negative corrected point-biserial correlations, compared with 3 educator-crafted items (10.0%). Negative corrected point-biserial values indicate that higher-performing participants were more likely to answer these items incorrectly"
