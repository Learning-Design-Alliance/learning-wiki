## Evidence

### Termos 2013

Termos, M. H. (2013). The Effects of the Classroom Performance System on Student Participation, Attendance, and Achievement. International Journal of Teaching and Learning in Higher Education, 25(1), 66-78. http://www.isetl.org/ijtlhe/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t-tests compared each CPS section's weekly attendance (n = 12 class periods) with the control section P10. Section means ranged from 87.50 to 92.15 versus 78.53 for the control; the article reports "A significant increase in attendance was found" for all four pairs, with p values of .000, .003, .006, and .018.

> "A significant increase in attendance was found in the experimental section P02 as compared to the control group, t(11) = 9.665, p < .001."
