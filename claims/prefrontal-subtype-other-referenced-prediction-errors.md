Review reports Suzuki et al.'s study on predicting which stimulus another person will choose, requiring an other-referenced prediction error; regional specialization emerged as a function of self- versus other-referenced computation.

> "The vmPFC tracked the simulated other's prediction error in a similar manner to self, whereas the simulated other's action prediction error was signaled by the dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (dmPFC) and dlPFC."
