`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive pre-post resource-preference survey of the EBM-BOPPPS group (n = 47), shown in Figure 2; guideline preference rose from 6.4% to 23.4% and search engine preference fell from 17.0% to 4.3%. No inferential test is reported for this shift.

> "Key shifts include a 3.8-fold increase in evidence-based guideline preference and a 74.7% reduction in general search engine preference."
