### Williams Emma 2025

Williams Emma, Dassios Theodore, Alonso-Ojembarrena Almudena, Roehr Charles C., Been Jasper V., Pieter de Boode Willem, Jost Kerstin. (2025). Neonatal Training across Union of European Medical Specialists Member Countries: A Survey from the Next Generation of Neonatologists. Neonatology. https://doi.org/10.1159/000547673

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 105 neonatal trainees from 24 UEMS countries; ordinal regression analysis of EPA scores and confidence levels. The article reports that "Current year of neonatal training was the most strongly associated factor with reported confidence in training requirements and procedures, as opposed to having a formal curriculum."

> "Current year of neonatal training was the most strongly associated factor with reported confidence in training requirements and procedures, as opposed to having a formal curriculum or being exposed to a large number of infants born at less than 32 weeks gestational age."
