## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There were significant differences between PGY-1 and PGY-3 ratings on five of the seven items on the pre-intervention survey, and most of these differences disappeared on the post-intervention survey. [→ Goldonowicz Jessica M. 2018](#goldonowicz-jessica-m-2018)
`q3 i?` Two items showed persistent post-intervention PGY-1 vs PGY-3 differences: frequency of mortality-risk discussions (p = 0.020) and confidence initiating palliative care discussions (p = 0.019), both rated lower by PGY-1 residents. [→ Goldonowicz Jessica M. 2018](#goldonowicz-jessica-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Goldonowicz Jessica M. 2018

Goldonowicz Jessica M., Runyon Michael S., Bullard Mark J. (2018). Palliative care in the emergency department: an educational investigation and intervention. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-018-0293-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis and Dwass-Steel-Critchlow-Fligner pairwise comparisons by postgraduate year among 40 residents (PGY 1 N = 14; PGY 2 N = 14; PGY 3 N = 12). The authors attribute the disappearing differences to "increased PGY 1 confidence and awareness" post-intervention (Table 4 medians).

> "When the survey responses were analyzed by PGY year, there were significant differences between PGY-1 and PGY-3 ratings on five of the seven items on the pre-intervention survey and all but two of these differences disappeared on the post-intervention survey."
