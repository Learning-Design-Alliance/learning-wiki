### Yunusova Asal 2026

Yunusova Asal, Huang Caitlin, Price Stephen, Joshi Kunal, Dutcher Janine M., Brown Kirk Warren, Villalba Daniella K., Tumminia Michael J., Creswell Kasey G., Cohen Sheldon, Creswell J. David. (2026). Depression trajectories and their associations with academic performance and sleep patterns among first-year college students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04404-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory k-means++ clustering on pooled two-wave data from first-year students (N = 271) using CES-D scores at semester start and end; the elbow and silhouette methods set k = 4. The article reports "low-stable (n = 109), increasing (n = 72), decreasing (n = 51), and high-stable" groups, with low-stable at 40.22% of the sample.

> "K-means + + clustering identified four trajectory groups: low-stable (n = 109), increasing (n = 72), decreasing (n = 51), and high-stable depressive symptoms (n = 39)."
