## Evidence

### Jianxiao Jiang 2025

Jianxiao Jiang, Yu Zhang. (2025). Student Development Agent: Risk-free Simulation for Evaluating AIED Innovations. Preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09183

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A case study simulated 42 stratified-sampled student agents on the first module of an MAIC course, comparing predicted post-module questionnaire scores to real students' using RMSE and MAE. The article reports Concept was best on all dimensions except Technology Acceptance, best predicted by Scales; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Compared to the Mean baseline method, our approaches outperformed across all dimensions and both error estimation metrics."
