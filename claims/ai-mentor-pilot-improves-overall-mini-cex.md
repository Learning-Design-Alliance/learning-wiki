## Evidence

### Li Xiao 2026

Li Xiao, Qiu Haitang, Li Xuemei, Teng Teng, Wang Jingxin, Zhong Jiang, Wei Kaiwen, Shen Xiaoshan, Zhou Xinyu. (2026). AI-driven multimodal simulation for psychiatric clinical education: conceptual framework and preliminary validation of the cognitive core. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09307-3

`q2 · i2` · `design · r2`

Single-group pretest–posttest pilot in which 32 students conducted a 15-minute LLM-driven virtual patient interview; senior psychiatrists rated Mini-CEX performance. Overall performance improved, with "mean change: 0.82; P < 0.01; Cohen’s d = 0.57".

> "the Mini-CEX evaluations (n = 32) demonstrated significant pre-to-posttest improvements in overall performance (mean change: 0.82; P < 0.01; Cohen’s d = 0.57)"
