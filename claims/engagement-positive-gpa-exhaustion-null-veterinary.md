## Evidence

### Muca Edlira 2023

Muca Edlira, Molino Monica, Ghislieri Chiara, Baratta Mario, Odore Rosangela, Bergero Domenico, Valle Emanuela. (2023). Relationships between psychological characteristics, academic fit and engagement with academic performance in veterinary medical students. BMC Veterinary Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-023-03695-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the SEM of 231 veterinary students, "only work engagement showed a positive relationship with GPA"; the model explained 9% of GPA variance. Hypothesis 3a was confirmed and Hypothesis 3b (exhaustion negatively related to GPA) was rejected; no effect size is printed for the engagement path.

> "Finally, only work engagement showed a positive relationship with GPA. The model explained about 31% of the variation in academic fit, 72% in work engagement, 25% in exhaustion, and 9% in GPA."
