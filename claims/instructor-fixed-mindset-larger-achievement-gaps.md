The article reports, citing Canning et al. (2019), a study in which 150 instructors from 13 science disciplines at a large US research university answered two Likert-scale items classifying fixed versus growth mindsets about students' ability. It states that gaps were "twice as large" under fixed-mindset instructors.

> "the achievement gaps between the racial and ethnic minority students and white students were twice as large and underrepresented students had worse motivational beliefs in the classes taught by instructors with a fixed mindset compared to instructors with a growth mindset"
