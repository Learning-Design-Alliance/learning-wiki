`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' statement in the Implications section of the Discussion, contrasting the high relative risks between extreme educational categories with the small overall variance explained ("≈1%") in the MoBa extended-family analysis.

> "We observed high relative risks between the highest and lowest educational categories that were comparable to previous studies (Hjern et al., 2010; Russell et al., 2016). Nevertheless, parental education explained only a small proportion of the variance in the child outcomes (≈1%)."
