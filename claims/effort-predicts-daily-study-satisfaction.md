## Evidence

### Liborius 2019

Liborius, P., Bellhäuser, H., & Schmitz, B. (2019). What makes a good study day? An intraindividual study on university students' time investment by means of time-series analyses. Learning and Instruction, 60, 310-321. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.10.006

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel analysis testing Hypothesis 5 in the 105-student diary study, with effort assessed in the evening questionnaire. The article reports the printed coefficient, a better-fitting random-slope model (χ²(7) = 205.53, p < .05), and a further 5.9% residual-variance reduction.

> "Students' effort positively predicted their daily study satisfaction above and beyond the previous predictors in the model (b effort = 0.35)."

### Liborius 2019 (2)

Liborius, P., Bellhäuser, H., & Schmitz, B. (2019). What makes a good study day? An intraindividual study on university students' time investment by means of time-series analyses. Learning and Instruction, 60, 310-321. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.10.006

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Summary of the final hierarchical multilevel model in the same diary study. The article reports the predictor set explained "a total of 49.3% of the intraindividual variance in students' daily study satisfaction" beyond the control variables.

> "Overall, the predictors in the model explained a total of 49.3% of the intraindividual variance in students' daily study satisfaction above and beyond the preliminarily included control variables."
