### Chance-Revels 2026

Chance-Revels, R., Phan, Q., Crawford, K., Steiger, L., & Swan, B. A. (2026). Enhancing Community Health Worker Communication: Evaluating Simulation-Based Training in Motivational Interviewing. Journal of Community Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10900-025-01543-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inductive grounded-theory coding of open-ended survey responses from CHW trainees identified reflection and growth through feedback as a major theme. Trainees emphasized that real-time SP feedback helped them refine communication and that debriefing supported deeper learning.

> "Trainees identified feedback and debriefing as critical to processing their experiences and improving their use of MI techniques. These reflective elements supported deeper learning and increased confidence. Many trainees emphasized how real-time feedback from the SPs helped them refine their communication."
