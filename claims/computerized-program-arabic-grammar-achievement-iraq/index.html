Al-Mashaqbeh and Neama 2015¶

Ibtesam Al-Mashaqbeh, Mushtak Neama. (2015). The Effect of Computerized Instructional Program in the Intermediate First Grade Students Achievement in Arabic language Grammar in Iraq. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.32. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083567

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pre/post two-group study of 120 intermediate first-grade students in Diqar, Iraq (60 experimental, 60 control), six weeks of instruction; ANCOVA on post-test with pre-test covariate gave F = 101.657, p = 0.000 for teaching method, "in favor of the experimental method".

"As showed in table (2) there were statistical significant differences (α = 0.05) due to the impact of the teaching method as the value of "P" (101.657), and a statistical significant difference was (0.000), and the differences were in favor of the experimental method."
