### Meagan Sundstrom 2022

Meagan Sundstrom, Andy Schang, Ashley B. Heim, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Understanding interaction network formation across instructional contexts in remote physics courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ERGM homophily-on-gender coefficients (Fig. 6) in the four remote courses show students in the mechanics courses "tend to interact with peers of their same gender" in both lecture and lab networks, but not in the electromagnetism courses the following semester.

> "students in M-Eng and M-Phys tend to interact with peers of their same gender, however men and women in EM-Eng and EM-Phys proportionately interact with one another"
