## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Garnier Sylvain 2026

Garnier Sylvain, Mattatia Laurent, Boulet Nicolas, Bosselut Gregoire, Serrand Chris, Benenati Sylvain, Cuvillon Philippe, Muller Laurent, Brunel Lionel, Roger Claire. (2026). Impact of named caps on anesthesia and intensive care team performance: a randomized simulation study. Brazilian Journal of Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjane.2026.844812

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Instructor assessments (n = 58) in the randomized simulation study showed higher overall performance and teamwork scores with named caps; the article reports "No statistically significant differences were observed in the leadership (p = 0.12) or task management (p = 0.13) domains."

> "A similar difference was observed for the overall performance rating (7.9 ± 1.54 vs. 6.86 ± 1.76; p = 0.035) and for the teamwork domain (23.7 ± 4.3 vs. 21.1 ± 4.4; p = 0.031) (Fig. 3)."
