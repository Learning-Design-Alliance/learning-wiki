## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the survey sample, years of professional experience did not appear to determine how well acquainted SLTs were with Makaton; some experienced SLTs felt only slightly acquainted while some early-career SLTs reported high familiarity. [→ Mc Geer 2026](#mc-geer-2026)

## Evidence

### Mc Geer 2026

Mc Geer, C., Milton, C., Vorster, C., & Graham, M.A. (2026). South African speech-language therapists’ perceived competencies and use of Makaton. South African Journal of Communication Disorders, 73(1), a1154. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajcd.v73i1.1154

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey finding on familiarity ratings (50.9% moderately acquainted, 26.3% very acquainted, 14.0% neutral, 8.8% slightly acquainted) cross-read against years of experience. The article reports no statistical test for this pattern; it is a descriptive observation.

> "While most of the participants had between 1 year and 5 years of professional experience, participants’ years of experience did not appear to be a determining factor in how well they were acquainted with Makaton."
