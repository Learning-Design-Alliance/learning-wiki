## Subclaims
`q2 i?` No significant effect of condition on procedural learning gain was detected in Germany or the UK, despite combination-condition students using the ITS for only about half the time. [→ Mavrikis 2022](#mavrikis-2022)

## Evidence

### Mavrikis 2022

Mavrikis, M., Rummel, N., Wiedmann, M., Loibl, K., & Holmes, W. (2022). Combining exploratory learning with structured practice educational technologies to foster both conceptual and procedural fractions knowledge. Education Tech Research Dev, 70, 691–712. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10104-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up univariate ANOVAs on procedural subscale scores in both quasi-experimental studies found no significant condition effect; the interaction term was also non-significant (Germany F(1,149) = 2.552; UK F(1,119) = 2.279).

> "We could not detect a significant effect of the conditions on the procedural learning gain for participants from either Germany or the UK."
