Lising, L., & Elby, A. (2005). The impact of epistemology on learning: A case study from introductory physics. Submitted to the American Journal of Physics. [doi:10.1119/1.1848115](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1848115)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of the earliest usable videotaped tutorial hour, in which four students worked on a University of Maryland electric field tutorial. Jan reasoned via functional dependencies yet ignored F's dependence on the very quantities being varied; group members and the TA caught each error.

> "Although she is corrected each time and acknowledges her mistake, four times in a row, Jan makes the same math error, repeatedly reasoning with the equation E = F/q without considering the fact that the force F depends on distance and on the test charge"
