## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A Monte Carlo method combining the Metropolis and Wang-Landau approaches directly computes the partition function Z at all temperatures of interest, and simulated results for a 32x32 Ising lattice show ln Z is nearly flat at low temperature, rises rapidly near the phase transition, and rises slowly at higher temperatures.
