The WWC intervention report rates Reading Mastery in the reading achievement domain. The rating reflects one study meeting WWC evidence standards: 'Reading Mastery was found to have potentially positive effects on the reading achievement of English language learners.' The rating scheme weighs design quality, statistical significance, effect size, and consistency across studies.
