## Evidence

### Limpo 2013

Limpo, T., & Alves, R. A. (2013). Modeling writing development: Contribution of transcription and self-regulation to Portuguese students' text generation quality. Journal of Educational Psychology, 105, 401-413. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031391

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise structural invariance testing in the multiple-group SEM (N = 376) constrained paths across groups; constraining the three text-generation paths significantly degraded fit, indicating "these three paths differed significantly between grade groups."

> "when we constrained the paths from planning, self-efficacy, or transcription on text generation, the fit of the model declined significantly, Δχ2(1) > 4.36, ps < .05; ΔCFI = .01. These analyses indicated that these three paths differed significantly between grade groups."
