Survey results on question A10 (N=39) about wind blowing from a constant-frequency source toward the observer. The author reports "33% examinees express this concept", with a further 23% stating the directional subconcept and only 10% answering correctly.

> "The general form of the alternative conception is: Wind changes the frequency of the sound that a listener receives and its statistics is given in the table I. 33% examinees express this concept."
