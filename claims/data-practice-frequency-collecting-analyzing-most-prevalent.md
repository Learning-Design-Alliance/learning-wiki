`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Frequency analysis of the 42 studies in the systematic review coded each study for the seven core data practices. The review reports Collecting Data in all studies and Analyzing Data in 41, with Cleaning Data (n = 24) and Disseminating Results (n = 19) least frequent.

> "Based on our analysis of 42 studies, the most prevalent core data practices included Collecting Data ( n = 42) and Analyzing Data (n = 41). Other widely identified practices were Evaluating Results (n = 38), Understanding Problems (n = 34), and Designing Experiments (n = 31). Notably, Cleaning Data (n = 24) and Disseminating Results (n = 19) were less frequently reported."
