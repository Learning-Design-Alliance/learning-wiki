## Evidence

### Bautista Alcaine 2024

Bautista Alcaine, P., Vicente Sánchez, E., Orejudo Hernández, S., & Cano Escoriaza, J. (2024). Training pre-service teachers to deal with cyberbullying: Collective intelligence as a mode of learning. Computers & Education, 220, 105123. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105123

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures mixed-model analysis of 180 pre-service teachers' scores across seven phases found significant increases for questions 3–6, with printed effect sizes "R2m = 0.177" (Q5), "R2m = 0.161" (Q6), "R2m = 0.0551" (Q3) and "R2m = 0.0519" (Q4).

> "The effect size obtained through R2marginal would indicate a greater relevance of the differences between means in question 5 (R2m = 0.177) and question 6 (R2m = 0.161) than in questions question 3 (R2m = 0.0551) and question 4 (R2m = 0.0519)."
