Review synthesis of 17 reports stating the guidance moderator, several manipulating it directly. The review reports Roll et al. (2018) found directive guidance "increased knowledge gains for learners with high prior knowledge while suppressing their attitude growth", and Fukuda et al. (2024) found just-in-time prompts facilitated rule learning moderated by prior knowledge.

"Roll et al. (2018) showed that with 97 undergraduates, directive guidance increased knowledge gains for learners with high prior knowledge while suppressing their attitude growth"
