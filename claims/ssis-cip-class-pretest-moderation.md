# SSIS-CIP effects on teacher-rated outcomes are moderated by class-level pretest, favoring lower-pretest classrooms

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was a statistically significant interaction between SSIS-CIP and class-level pretest on teacher ratings of all social skills and academic engagement/motivation measures except assertion and self-control. [→ DiPerna 2014](#diperna-2014)
`q3 i?` Adjusted differences favoring SSIS-CIP were larger for classes with lower average pretest scores; high-pretest classes showed no improvement. [→ DiPerna 2014](#diperna-2014)

## Evidence

### DiPerna 2014

DiPerna, J. C., Lei, P., Bellinger, J., & Cheng, W. (2014). Efficacy of the Social Skills Improvement System – Classwide Intervention Program (SSIS – CIP) in the Primary Grades. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562704

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of the cluster randomized trial tested treatment-by-pretest product terms at the .05 level. The interaction was significant for all teacher-rated outcomes except assertion and self-control; "the adjusted differences between SSIS-CIP and control classrooms were larger for classes that had lower average pretest scores".

> "There was a statistically significant interaction between SSIS-CIP and class-level pretest on teacher ratings of all social skills and academic engagement/motivation measures except assertion and self-control."

### DiPerna 2014 (2)

DiPerna, J. C., Lei, P., Bellinger, J., & Cheng, W. (2014). Efficacy of the Social Skills Improvement System – Classwide Intervention Program (SSIS – CIP) in the Primary Grades. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562704

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same multilevel analysis of the CRT: for high average pretest classes the article reports no improvement when holding other variables constant, so the program's benefit concentrates in lower-pretest classrooms.

> "For classes that had high average pretest scores, SSIS-CIP did not improve their average scores when holding other variables constant."
