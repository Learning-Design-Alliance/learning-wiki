One-way ANOVA across ED, L&D, and Maternity on four PBCCS subscales. Only self-awareness reached significance (p = 0.02), with ED at 3.17 and L&D at 3.83; the article states "No significant differences were found in other PBCCS subscales between the departments."

> "Scores for each PBCCS subscale between departments showed significant differences in mean score for self-awareness (p = 0.02). The mean score for ED staff was 3.17 while those working in L&D scored 3.83 for this subscale (Table 4)."
