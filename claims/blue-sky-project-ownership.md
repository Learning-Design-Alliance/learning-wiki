## Evidence

### Pollack 2026

Pollack, Y. G., Bhattacharyya, K., Hussin, A., Klass, E., Mendozza, R., Iyer V S, K., Wellecke, G., & Zimmer, P. (2026). Hackathons for biophysics education: simulating the cytoskeleton. arXiv. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.35459/tbp.2025.000289](https://doi.org/10.35459/tbp.2025.000289)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Organizer observation across both hackathons (eleven groups total). The authors chose the open-ended format for the project-ownership value of teams developing their own project concept, and consider the final presentations a success, noting also the variety of project themes.

> "the Blue-sky format was initially met with lack of self-confidence in participants' ability to come up with a project that makes sense. However, with encouragement and occasional feedback from the organizers, this hurdle was overcome and in both hackathons all teams managed to conceptualize a project."
