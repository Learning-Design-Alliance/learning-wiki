## Evidence

### Var Eren 2025

Var Eren, Cam Yaren, Pektas Eren, Kuden Cihan. (2025). Does preoperative anxiety influence endodontic skill performance and students' subjective perceptions in an observational simulation-based study?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07957-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fisher's exact test of gender distribution across anxiety groups for mandibular procedures (n = 93). Females comprised 88% of the high-anxiety group versus 12% of the low-anxiety group (p = 0.002); no significant gender difference was found in the maxillary group (p = 0.530).

> "In terms of gender distribution, a significant difference was observed in the mandibular group, with a higher proportion of females in the high anxiety group (88%) compared to the low anxiety group (12%) (p = 0.002)."
