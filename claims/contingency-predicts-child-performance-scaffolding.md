# Maternal contingency is the only scaffolding variable related to child performance during scaffolding across all three time-points

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Dave Neale & David Whitebread 2019

Dave Neale & David Whitebread. (2019). Maternal scaffolding during play with 12- to 24-month-old infants: stability over time and relations with emerging effortful control. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09196-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three regression analyses (one per time-point) predicting child performance during scaffolding from the maternal scaffolding variables (Table 4). At 12 months contingency had β = .78*** with sr2 = .57; at 18 months β = .52*; at 24 months β = .61***.

> "The only scaffolding variable related to child performance across time-points was maternal contingency."
