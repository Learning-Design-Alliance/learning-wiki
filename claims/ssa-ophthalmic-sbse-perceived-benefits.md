### Annoh R 2021

Annoh R, Banks LM, Gichuhi S, Buchan J, Makupa W, Otiti J, Mukome A, Arunga S, Burton MJ, Dean WH. (2021). Experiences and Perceptions of Ophthalmic Simulation-Based Surgical Education in Sub-Saharan Africa. Journal of Surgical Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsurg.2021.04.005

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interviews with 27 trainee ophthalmologists and 12 surgeon educators from six SSA training centers, analyzed by inductive thematic analysis. The article reports that "Almost all trainees and trainers reported patient safety, a calm environment, the possibility of repetitive practice" as benefits of SBSE.

> "Almost all trainees and trainers reported patient safety, a calm environment, the possibility of repetitive practice, and facilitation of reflective learning as beneficial aspects of ophthalmic SBSE."
