## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Thematic analysis of interviews yielded five themes: prior OSCE experience calms, gained knowledge and skill calms, calm leads to better performance, poor interpersonal skills increase anxiety, and perceived theory-practice gaps increase anxiety. [→ Ferreira Érica de Matos Reis 2020](#ferreira-erica-de-matos-reis-2020)
`q2 i?` Students valued the anxiety-provoking experience as a source of self-knowledge and preparation for real clinical encounters, reporting it was meaningful to their education. [→ Ferreira Érica de Matos Reis 2020 (2)](#ferreira-erica-de-matos-reis-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Ferreira Érica de Matos Reis 2020

Ferreira Érica de Matos Reis, Pinto Rafael Zambelli, Arantes Paula Maria Machado, Vieira Érica Leandro Marciano, Teixeira Antônio Lúcio, Ferreira Fabiane Ribeiro, Vaz Daniela Virgínia. (2020). Stress, anxiety, self-efficacy, and the meanings that physical therapy students attribute to their experience with an objective structured clinical examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02202-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of face-to-face semi-structured interviews (11 trios) conducted after the OSCE, coded line by line by two researchers to consensus with inductive saturation and participant checking. Themes were emergent, informed by researchers' experience.

> "The following themes emerged during the interviews: 1) previous experience with an OSCE has a calming effect; 2) gaining knowledge and skill has a calming effect; 3) calm leads to better performance"

### Ferreira Érica de Matos Reis 2020 (2)

Ferreira Érica de Matos Reis, Pinto Rafael Zambelli, Arantes Paula Maria Machado, Vieira Érica Leandro Marciano, Teixeira Antônio Lúcio, Ferreira Fabiane Ribeiro, Vaz Daniela Virgínia. (2020). Stress, anxiety, self-efficacy, and the meanings that physical therapy students attribute to their experience with an objective structured clinical examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02202-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from the same post-OSCE interview analysis; one student said the simulation "has prepared me for the real moment tomorrow", and another said you "learn it by doing it" rather than from lectures. Qualitative self-report only.

> "Students reported that the experience was meaningful to their education"
