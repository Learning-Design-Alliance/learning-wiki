## Evidence

### Wen Zhi 2026

Wen Zhi, Chen Peirui, Lyu Jing, Wu Jianqiang, Qin Biyuan, Yang Yong, Huang Guofei, Wu Changxue. (2026). CBL with integrated SDH versus CBL alone in esophageal surgery internship: a randomized controlled trial on clinical competence development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09914-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous 6-point Likert questionnaires (58 experimental, 60 control valid) compared by Mann–Whitney U with Bonferroni correction; the discussion reports "effect sizes ranging from 0.48 to 0.67".

> "students in the CBL + SDH group scored significantly higher than those in the control group on six dimensions: "knowledge integration ability," "ethical and social thinking," "systematic problem-solving," "individualized treatment planning," "reflection and learning," and "communication adaptability," with effect sizes ranging from 0.48 to 0.67, indicating moderate to large effects."
