Kazuki Kawamura 2025¶

Kazuki Kawamura. (2025). AI-Guided Learning: Research on Knowledge and Skill Acquisition Support Methods Using Deep Learning Audio-Video Processing Techniques. Doctoral dissertation, The University of Tokyo. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08990

q2 · i? · design · r2

Small-n experiment with 10 Japanese learners of English; post-practice speech was rated for intelligibility by five American raters and compared against an elicited-imitation baseline. The improvement is described as observed, with non-overlapping confidence intervals for Profy only; no effect size printed.

"In an experiment with 10 Japanese learners of English evaluated by five American raters, Profy showed a larger observed improvement in pronunciation intelligibility than elicited imitation; unlike elicited imitation, its pre- and post-practice confidence intervals did not overlap."
