Bülent GÜNDÜZ 2013¶

Bülent GÜNDÜZ. (2013). The Contributions of Attachment Styles, Irrational Beliefs and Psychological Symptoms to the Prediction of Cognitive Flexibility. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 13(4), 2079-2085. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2013.4.1702

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multiple regression analysis in the 436-student study. The article reports that hostility and afraid attachment were the two variables that did not predict cognitive flexibility meaningfully; no test statistics or effect sizes are printed for this contrast.

"According to the multiple regression analysis, all of the variables except hostility and afraid attachment predict points of cognitive flexibility meaningfully"
