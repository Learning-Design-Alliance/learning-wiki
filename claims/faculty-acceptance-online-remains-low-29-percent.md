`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

National survey of chief academic officers, 2015 administration. The report notes that for the past twelve years no more than one-third of officers reported faculty acceptance, with little year-to-year change.

> "Only 29.1% of chief academic officers believe their faculty accept the value and legitimacy of online education.  This rate is lower than the rate recorded in 2004."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Survey results broken down by institutional distance enrollment. The report states 60.1% of leaders at institutions with 10,000 or more distance enrollments report faculty acceptance, with intermediate rates at smaller enrollments.

> "only 11.6% of the leaders of institutions with no distance offerings believe their faculty accept the value and legitimacy of online education ."
