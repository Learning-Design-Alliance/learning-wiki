### Ghimire A and Singh C 2025

Ghimire A and Singh C. (2025). Peer Influence on Physics Self-Efficacy and Grades: A comparative study of students in an introductory calculus-based course who typically worked alone or in groups before and during the pandemic. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Chi-square analysis of survey-reported working mode by gender (Table II), with χ2 = 4.78 (p = 0.029) before COVID and χ2 = 18.72 (p < 0.001) during COVID. The article reports women at "62%" versus men at "51%" pre-pandemic, shifting to 66% and 46% during the pandemic.

> "Before the pandemic, a greater percentage of women (62%) worked in groups compared to men (51%). During the pandemic, the percentage of women working in groups increased to 66% while the percentage of men decreased to 46%."
