### Yu Caixia 2026

Yu Caixia, Guo Wanwei, Yu Linna, Huang Chongming, Zhao Guoxiang. (2026). Development of core competency indicators for emergency medicine residents based on the competency model: a Delphi and analytic hierarchy process study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09876-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative phase: five senior emergency specialists with over 10 years of experience were interviewed (average duration 50 min), transcripts coded by two researchers using Braun & Clarke's six-phase thematic analysis. The authors report the interviews "enabled us to identify implicit competency indicators that were not fully captured in existing literature."

> "These qualitative insights enabled us to identify implicit competency indicators that were not fully captured in existing literature, such as stress resilience and rapid communication skills, which were subsequently validated through the Delphi consensus process."
