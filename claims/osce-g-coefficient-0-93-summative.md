Observational cross-sectional G-study of a summative end-of-career OSCE taken by 278 internship students at UNAM; Table 3 reports "G coefficient 0.93", "D coefficient 0.83", SEM relative 1.16 and SEM absolute 3.42. The overall mean score was 63.2±5.7.
