## Subclaims
`q2 i?` HRQoL significantly and indirectly affected academic success via intrinsic motivation (B = 0.005, p ≤ 0.05) and via the serial path through burnout and intrinsic motivation (B = 0.013, p ≤ 0.05). [→ Torkani F 2025](#torkani-f-2025)
`q2 i?` The indirect paths via burnout to intrinsic, extrinsic motivation and amotivation were significant (all p ≤ 0.001), whereas model fit was satisfactory (CFI = 0.992, TLI = 0.979, RMSE = 0.039, SRMR = 0.032, WRMR = 0.863). [→ Torkani F 2025](#torkani-f-2025)

## Evidence

### Torkani F 2025

Torkani F, Ahmadi S-H, Binazir A, Fath MM, Shafigh N, Asadiparvar-Masouleh H, Vasli P. (2025). The relationship between health-related quality of life and academic success in nursing students: the mediating role of academic burnout and academic motivation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06661-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Indirect-effect estimates (Table 4) from the path analysis of 261 nursing students. The article prints significant indirect coefficients of 0.005 (a3c1) and 0.013 (a1b3c1), both with p ≤ 0.05, and satisfactory fit indices including CFI = 0.992.

> "The results indicated that HRQoL significantly indirectly affects AS via intrinsic motivation (a3c1, p ≤ 0.05). Furthermore, the indirect path from the HRQoL to AS via AB and intrinsic motivation was significant (a1b3c1, p ≤ 0.05). This implies that HRQoL can increase AS by 0.005 through mediating intrinsic motivation, and that HRQoL can increase AS through mediating AB and intrinsic motivation by 0.013."
