Monte Carlo simulation (5000 replications, simsem) evaluating statistical power at the observed sample size. The results section reports that "power for the indirect effects ranged from 0.04 to 0.83, with only two of the 18 indirect effects reaching the 0.80 criterion", indicating limited power for several hypothesised associations.
