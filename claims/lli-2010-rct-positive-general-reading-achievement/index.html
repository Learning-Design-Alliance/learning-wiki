Evidence¶

Leveled Literacy Intervention 2017¶

Leveled Literacy Intervention. (2017). What Works Clearinghouse Beginning Reading intervention report. U.S. Department of Education. http://www.fountasandpinnell.com/intervention/

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Randomized controlled trial of LLI for grades K–2 in nine rural and suburban schools, with students matched and randomly assigned within pairs. For Fountas & Pinnell BAS the WWC-calculated effect size was 0.35 with improvement index +14, and "This result was large enough to be considered substantively important."

"For this combined (grades K–2) sample, the WWC found a statistically significant positive difference between the intervention and comparison groups. This result was large enough to be considered substantively important."
