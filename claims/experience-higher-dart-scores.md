### Otiato Maxwell X. 2024

Otiato Maxwell X., Ma Runzhuo, Chu Timothy N., Wong Elyssa Y., Wagner Christian, Hung Andrew J. (2024). Surgical gestures to evaluate apical dissection of robot-assisted radical prostatectomy. Journal of Robotic Surgery 18(1):245. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-01902-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effect model comparison of DART scores (adjusting for clustering within surgeons) across experience groups: gesture selection 3.0 ± 0.2 vs. 2.7 ± 0.5 (p = 0.007), tissue retraction p = 0.042, total DART 13.8 ± 1.1 vs. 12.8 ± 1.7 (p = 0.010).

> "More experienced surgeons were found to have significantly higher Gesture Selection, Tissue Retraction, and total DART scores than less experienced surgeons (p < 0.05, Table 2)."
