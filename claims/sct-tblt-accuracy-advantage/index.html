Tahmasebi and Yamini 2011¶

Soheila Tahmasebi, Morteza Yamini. (2011). Linking Task-based Language Teaching and Sociocultural Theory: Private Speech and Scaffolding in Reading Comprehension. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.2n.1p.41

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive discourse analysis from the ninth session subsample. The article notes both groups had problems with tenses, articles, and plurals, but the experimental group showed advantages on error-free clauses and self-correction.

"percentage of error-free clauses for group two against group one was (70% vs. 66%), moreover group two self-corrected their sentences or their peers' sporadically while group one did not"
