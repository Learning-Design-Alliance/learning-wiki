## Evidence

### Scott Secrest and Ingrid Novodvorsky 2005

Scott Secrest and Ingrid Novodvorsky. (2005). Identifying Student Difficulties with Understanding Induced EMF. Originally published 2001. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0501093](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0501093)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Individual interviews with 30 students after the posttest, using a demonstration in which a coil was rotated near a stationary magnet. "few students answered that a current would be induced", and only one gave the changing cross-sectional area as the reason.

> "For this situation, few students answered that a current would be induced. Of those, only one correctly stated that it was because the cross -sectional area was changing. The others stated that it was because the loop was moving,"
