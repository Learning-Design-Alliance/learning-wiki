`q2 i3` The tablets group outperformed the conventional group on four of five measures, with large effect sizes (r = -0.52, -0.72, -0.59). [→ Fokides and Mastrokoukou 2018](#fokides-and-mastrokoukou-2018)

## Evidence

### Fokides and Mastrokoukou 2018

Emmanuel Fokides and Aikaterini Mastrokoukou. (2018). Results from a Study for Teaching Human Body Systems to Primary School Students Using Tablets. Contemporary Educational Technology, 9(2), 154-170. https://doi.org/10.30935/cet.414808

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 75 sixth-graders in three intact classrooms (N = 25 each). Kruskal-Wallis tests with Bonferroni post-hoc comparisons showed "Group 3 surpassed the other two groups" on ES1, ES4, and the delayed post-test, with large effect sizes versus Group1 (e.g., r = -0.72 on ES4).

> "In Evaluation Sheet 1 (the organs of the respiratory system), Group 3 surpassed the other two groups, but no statistically significant difference was noted between groups 2 and 1."
