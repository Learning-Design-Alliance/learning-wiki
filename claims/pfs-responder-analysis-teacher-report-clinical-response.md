## Evidence

### Feil 2016

Feil, E. G., Small, J. W., Seeley, J. R., Walker, H. M., Golly, A., Frey, A., & Forness, S. R. (2016). Early intervention for preschoolers at risk for attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder: Preschool First Step to Success. Behavioral Disorders, 41(2), 95-106. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581149

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Responder analysis using Jacobson and Truax's Reliable Change Index with a CADS cutoff at T = 59, based on teacher report in the 45-child subsample (26 intervention, 19 control). The article reports "OR[95% CI] 5 9.9[1.9, 51.9]"; responding children averaged a 2 SD improvement in CADS scores. No standardized effect size (d, g, r) is printed for this contrast.

> "Overall, 14 intervention children (54%) either responded or improved after participating in the PFS intervention compared to two children (11%) in the control condition ( χ2 5 8.99, p 5 .002, OR[95% CI] 5 9.9[1.9, 51.9])."
