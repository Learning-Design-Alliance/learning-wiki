### Charles Baily 2015

Charles Baily, Noah D. Finkelstein. (2015). Teaching Quantum Interpretations: Revisiting the goals and practices of introductory quantum physics courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020124](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020124)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post survey analysis (Fig. 10) of the statement that the probabilistic nature of quantum mechanics is mostly due to measurement-instrument limitations. The article reports post-instruction distributions "significantly different (p = 0.001)" across all four courses, while B, INT-1 and INT-2 did not differ (p = 0.24); pre-instruction differences were not significant (p = 0.54).
