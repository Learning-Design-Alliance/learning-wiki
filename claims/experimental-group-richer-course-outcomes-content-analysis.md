## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Content analysis of open-ended answers found seven outcome categories in the experimental group versus three in the control group, with higher frequencies in the experimental group. [→ Derman et al. 2016](#derman-et-al-2016)

## Evidence

### Derman et al. 2016

Aysegul Derman, Elvan Sahin, Esme Hacieminoglu. (2016). Does Outdoor Education Make any Difference in Environmental Literacy of Pre-service Classroom Teachers? INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL OF ENVIRONMENTAL & SCIENCE EDUCATION, 11(15), 8491-8506. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content analysis of answers to the open-ended Environmental Education Outcome Evaluation Survey questions. Table 7 prints frequencies such as knowledge about environmental issues (44 experimental vs 9 control) and concern in environmental issues (45 experimental vs none reported for control).

> "the outcomes the participants had in the experimental group are quite rich (a, b, c, d, e, f, g), and there are more participants who had these outcomes than those of the control group. On the other hand, in the control group in which the traditional lecturing method was used, the outcomes are more limited (a,d,g) and the participants who had outcomes are quite a little."
