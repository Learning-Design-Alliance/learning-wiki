### Kridipaka S 2026

Kridipaka S, Ruengwattanachot K, Leksuwankun S, Pongpitakmetha T, Hiransuthikul A, Surawattanawong T, Anukoolwittaya P, Panjasriprakarn P. (2026). `q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

In the same single-group pre-post study, the CRI-HT scale, which evaluates the relevance and reasoning behind history-taking questions, showed a significant paired increase of 8.8 points (Cohen's d = 1.81).

> "Mean CRI-HT score also increased from 28.2 (SD 5.2) at pre-test to 37.0 (SD 2.2) at post-test (mean difference 8.8; 95% CI 6.8 to 10.8; p < 0.001; Cohen's d = 1.81)."
