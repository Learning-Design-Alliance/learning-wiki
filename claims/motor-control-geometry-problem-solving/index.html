Observational grading of Behavior (motor accompaniments) in Table I across the four exercises. The authors note good student GU's automatic scribbling as an exception, and report the group contrast quoted here.

"All but one of the good group were able to inhibit undesirable motor activities quite satisfactorily, while not one of the poor group showed the ability to do so."
