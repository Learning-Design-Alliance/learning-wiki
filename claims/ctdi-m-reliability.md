## Evidence

### Xiaoxia Wang 2018

Xiaoxia Wang, Xiaoxiao Sun, Tianhao Huang, Renqiang He, Weina Hao, Li Zhang. (2018). Development and validation of Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory for Chinese medical college students (CTDI-M). Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.11428](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.11428)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis in the psychometric-testing stage with 441 undergraduate students. The article reports the questionnaire had substantial reliability by its stated criterion and that test-retest was assessed "with a two-week interval between administrations".

> "Results showed that the correlations between each subscale score and the total CDTI-CM score were all statistically significant, with Conbach's alpha ranging from 0.776 to 0.965"
