## Evidence

### Usta Sıla Nur 2025

Usta Sıla Nur, Silva Emmanuel João Nogueira Leal, Keskin Cangül, Tekkanat Hilal, Liukkonen Mikko, Felszeghy Szabolcs. (2025). A comparison of traditional and virtual reality haptic simulator approaches in preclinical endodontic training: Impacts on skill acquisition, confidence and stress. International Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14236

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 20 students per group (40 analyzed) in preclinical endodontic training; blinded educator scoring of artificial-tooth access cavity preparations. The article reports "significantly higher scores in both preclinical assessments and self‐evaluations" for the VR-first group, with no effect size printed.

> "Group 1 demonstrated significantly higher scores in both preclinical assessments and self‐evaluations compared to Group 2 (p < .05)."
