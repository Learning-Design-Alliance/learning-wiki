Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Most questionnaires used to assess BLS knowledge in secondary school students are ad hoc, differing in item number, response format, and content, which limits comparability across studies — related
  • Instrument type relates to assessed domains: ad hoc questionnaires were virtually limited to practical CPR techniques, while psychometrically validated tools covered scene safety, arrest recognition, emergency activation, and functional AED use — related
  • Only 16 of 36 studies assessed BLS knowledge retention over time, with follow-ups generally between 1 and 6 months and only two exceeding 1 year — related
