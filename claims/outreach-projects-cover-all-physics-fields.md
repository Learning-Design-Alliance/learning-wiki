### Julien Bobroff 2015

Julien Bobroff, Frédéric Bouquet. (2015). A project-based course about outreach in a physics curriculum. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/4/045704](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/4/045704)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of all student projects over three years (23 projects). The article reports 30% of projects on high-school topics (mechanics, acoustics, electromagnetism), 58% on undergraduate fields, and 12% on history, epistemology or sociology of science.

> "Over the twenty-three projects conceived, the topics are found to cover all the fields of physics. No preferred topic was found, contrary to the fact that most students come to this specific physics section based on a motivation toward astrophysics or quantum physics."
