## Evidence

### Yeo Sing Chen 2023

Yeo Sing Chen, Lai Clin K. Y., Tan Jacinda, Lim Samantha, Chandramoghan Yuvan, Tan Teck Kiang, Gooley Joshua J. (2023). Early morning university classes are associated with impaired sleep and academic performance. Nature Human Behaviour. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01531-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Analysis of 336 actigraphy-verified naps in the 181-student actigraphy study, pooled across participants by first class start time. Proportions of school days with naps ranged from 10.9% at 08:00 to 3.5% at 14:00; the article reports the chi-squared statistic but the quote provides no standardised effect size in the rule's sense.

> "The frequency of napping was associated with class start time (two-sided chi-squared test: χ2(6) = 34.7, Cramer's V = 0.10, 95% CI = 0.079 to 0.13, P < 0.001) and was highest when students' first class of the day was in the morning"
