# Gender/sex predicted reporting that getting help from peers increased self-efficacy when modeled alone, but this relationship was attenuated after accounting for initial self-efficacy

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` When gender/sex was the lone predictor, female students had significantly higher odds of reporting Getting Help from Peers (B = 0.62, p = 0.03, odds ratio 1.87). [→ Aikens 2023](#aikens-2023)
`q3 i?` When initial self-efficacy was added, the gender/sex coefficient was no longer significant (B = 0.47, p = 0.10, odds ratio 1.59). [→ Aikens 2023 (2)](#aikens-2023-2)
