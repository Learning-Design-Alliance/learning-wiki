### Ben Van Dusen and Valerie Otero 2014

Ben Van Dusen and Valerie Otero. (2014). From Fear to Self-Expression: The Contextual Nature of Physics Students’ Motivations. School of Education, University of Colorado, Boulder. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.060](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.060)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Literature-review attribution: the article reports classic Self-Determination Theory, citing Ryan and Deci, that internalization occurs as activities fulfill basic psychological needs of competence, autonomy, and relatedness.

> "When people engage in an activity that provides them the experiences of competence, autonomy, and relatedness (as contrasted with overwhelming challenges, excessive controls, and relational insecurity) they will be more likely to internalize the activity and engage in it in the future."
