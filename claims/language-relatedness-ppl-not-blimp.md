### Ionut Constantinescu 2025

Ionut Constantinescu, Tiago Pimentel, Ryan Cotterell, Alex Warstadt. (2025). Investigating Critical Period Effects in Language Acquisition through Neural Language Models. arXiv. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1162/tacl_a_00725](https://doi.org/10.1162/tacl_a_00725)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 4 trained GPT-2 models on eleven L1s (from a second English corpus to Java) with English as L2, 100M tokens, 6 epochs, evaluating with PPL and BLiMP. The paper reports "only our results for PPL support this prior conclusion".

> "However, only our results for PPL support this prior conclusion. Our results for BLiMP do not unless EWC is applied, suggesting that models are ordinarily plastic enough to learn the grammar of L2 regardless of relatedness, unless plasticity is specifically reduced."
