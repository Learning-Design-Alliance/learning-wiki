## Evidence

### Hatzigianni 2014

Hatzigianni, M. (2014). 'I like computers but my favourite is playing outside with my friends': Young children's beliefs about computers. International Research in Early Childhood Education, 5(1). https://www.education.monash.edu.au/irecejournal/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive differentiation of responses across four computer-access sub-groups ('home and school', 'school', 'home', 'no') pre- and post-intervention. Home-only children decreased on all activities-related computer responses; no-access children were stable; home-and-school and school groups increased.

> "children who had computer access only at 'home' showed decreases in the percentage of responses nominating 'computers' (in all the ' activities related questions') and children with 'no' computer access showed stable results in post-intervention (Table 2)."
