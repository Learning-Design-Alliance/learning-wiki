# Embedded source credibility rating scales during video viewing improve middle-schoolers' source identity recognition, especially at delayed posttest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Students confronted with embedded rating scales outperformed students without them on source identity recognition (η²p=.026). [→ Colin Lescarret 2022](#colin-lescarret-2022)
`q3 i1` Recognition scores deteriorated between immediate and delayed posttest only for students without the embedded rating scales; the ERS group showed no significant deterioration (interaction η²p=.034). [→ Colin Lescarret 2022 (2)](#colin-lescarret-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Colin Lescarret 2022

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on recognition scores in the 2×2 between-subjects experiment (N=262 seventh graders), with playback time as covariate. The ERS group outperformed the no-ERS group with effect size "η²p=.026" (F(1,253)=6.682, p=.010), M difference=-0.27.

> "Between-subjects effects revealed a main effect of the factor Embedded Rating Scales, F(1,253)=6.682, p=.010, η²p=.026. Post-hoc tests indicated that the group of students confronted with the ERS outperformed the group of students who were not"

### Colin Lescarret 2022 (2)

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA interaction of experimental phase and embedded rating scales on recognition, effect size "η²p=.034" (F(1,253)=8.994, p=.003). Scores deteriorated significantly from immediate to delayed posttest only in the no-ERS group (M difference=0.392); the ERS group's deterioration (M difference=0.077) was not significant (pbonferroni=1.000).

> "the interaction effect Experimental Phase*Embedded Rating Scales proved significant, F(1,253)=8.994, p=.003, η²p=.034"
