Herbert W. Marsh, Len Cairns, Joseph Relich, Jennifer Barnes, and Ray L. Debus. (1984). The Relationship Between Dimensions of Self-Attribution, Dimensions of Self-Concept, and Academic Achievements. Journal of Educational Psychology, 76(1), 3-32. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/76/1/3/
