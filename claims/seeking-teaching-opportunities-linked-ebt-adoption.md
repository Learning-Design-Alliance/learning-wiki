## Evidence

### Goodwin E. C. 2018

Goodwin E. C., Cao J. N., Fletcher M., Flaiban J. L., Shortlidge E. E. (2018). Catching the Wave: Are Biology Graduate Students on Board with Evidence-Based Teaching? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0281

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of the 32 interviews; participants attended workshops or classes, gave guest lectures, and taught extra classes. The article reports "59% of participants sought out nonmandatory teaching opportunities".

> "Demonstrating their interest in and commitment to gaining instructional experience, 59% of participants sought out nonmandatory teaching opportunities."

### Goodwin E. C. 2018 (2)

Goodwin E. C., Cao J. N., Fletcher M., Flaiban J. L., Shortlidge E. E. (2018). Catching the Wave: Are Biology Graduate Students on Board with Evidence-Based Teaching? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0281

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Informal trend analysis across DOI-stage placements in the interview study. The article reports that early-stage dropouts "had sought out nonmandatory instructional training or teaching experiences" in no cases, while later-stage participants often did.

> "None of the participants who dropped out of the DOI model in the early stages (Knowledge and Persuasion) had sought out nonmandatory instructional training or teaching experiences (seeks out teaching opportunities; Table 2)."
