## Evidence

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis in a single flipped classroom cohort (n = 57) using the validated TOSLS at weeks 1 and 12; the article reports a 16.3% total score increase and "gains in multiple individual practical SL skill categories". Table 2 prints the change from 60.9% to 77.2% correct.

> "Overall, TOSLS scores significantly increased during the semester by 16.3%, and students showed gains in multiple individual practical SL skill categories by the end of the semester."
