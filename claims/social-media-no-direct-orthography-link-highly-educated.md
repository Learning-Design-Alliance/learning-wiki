## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Social media use was not directly connected with orthographic skills in a network analysis of 840 highly educated adults; the relation was mediated by other factors such as education and reading habits. [→ Miccoli 2023](#miccoli-2023)

## Evidence

### Miccoli 2023

Miccoli, L., Arias, M. Á. P., & Santiago, J. (2023). A network analysis on digital media use, reading enjoyment, and orthography precision in a highly educated sample. Computers & Education, 207, 104932. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104932

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Network structure analysis (EBICglasso partial correlation network, 17 nodes) in an exploratory cross-sectional survey of 840 adults, 86.7% with college-level education. "There was not a direct relation between orthography scores and any kind of digital media use." No effect size printed for the null direct edge.

> "There was not a direct relation between orthography scores and any kind of digital media use. The relation was mediated by other factors, chiefly education and reading habits, which connected orthography with age, which in turn connected to digital media use."
