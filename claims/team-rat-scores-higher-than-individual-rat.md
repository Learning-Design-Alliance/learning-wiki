## Evidence

### Peterson 2015

Peterson, E.K. and Carrico, P. (2015). Laboratory Exercise in Behavioral Genetics Using Team-based Learning Strategies. Bioscene 41(2). https://visionandchange.org/files/2011/03/Revised-Vision-and-Change-Final-Report.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison within two sections of a junior/senior-level Behavioral Genetics laboratory (approximately 21-24 students per section). Paired-Samples T-Test compared individual RAT averages to team RAT averages; the article reports students "performed significantly higher (p < 0.0001)" on the team portion, scoring 24.9% higher.

> "We found that students performed significantly higher (p < 0.0001, see Figure 4A) on the team RAT, in comparison to the individual portion; students scored 24.9% higher on the team portion."
