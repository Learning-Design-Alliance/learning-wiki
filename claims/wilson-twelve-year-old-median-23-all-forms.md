### Wilson 1922

Wilson, G. M. (1922). Language error tests. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 13*(6), 341–349. [doi:10.1037/h0064444](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0064444)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Age-based re-analysis of the combined Sioux City and Duluth distributions: the separated 12-year-old group scored "the median for the 12 year olds was 23" on all three forms, with near-identical quartiles.

> "The 12-year-old group was separated and distributed. In all three tests, A, B and C, the median for the 12 year olds was 23 and the quartiles were either on the same point or only one step removed."
