`q3 i3` For the soil science unit, the interaction between sequence of instruction and preference for grasping information was statistically significant with ηp2 = 0.23, superseding the main effect of preference. [→ Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield 2017](#kasee-l-smith-john-rayfield-2017)

## Evidence

### Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield 2017

Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield. (2017). A Quasi-Experimental Examination: Cognitive Sequencing of Instruction Using Experiential Learning Theory for STEM Concepts in Agricultural Education. Journal of Agricultural Education, 58(4), 175-191. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2017.04175

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on change scores for the soil science unit, part of the same crossover quasi-experiment with n = 121 students at four Texas high schools. The article reports the interaction as significant at the Bonferroni-adjusted alpha of p ≤ 0.02.

> "A significant difference ( F(2,115) = 69.17, p = 0.01, ηp2 = 0.55) was found related to student preference for grasping information which was superseded by a significant interaction (F(1,115) = 17.58, p = 0.01, ηp2 = 0.23) between sequence of instruction and preference for grasping information."
