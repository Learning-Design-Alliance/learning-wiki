`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert validation analysis of 300 questions (150 per platform) generated in December 2024, rated by seven urologists using standardized rubrics. The comparison of platform accuracy is reported descriptively with a p-value of 0.86; no effect size is printed.

> "Both AI platforms demonstrated comparable technical accuracy (ChatGPT: 84.3 ± 6.2%, Gemini: 83.8 ± 5.9%, p = 0.86) and retention rates (33.3% each) as detailed in Table 2"
