## Evidence

### Darnell 2019

Darnell, D. K., & Krieg, P. A. (2019). Student engagement, assessed using heart rate, shows no reset following active learning sessions in lectures. PLoS ONE 14(12): e0225709. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225709

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' overall conclusion from the 42-class HR dataset: HR rises during AL but does not remain elevated, returning rapidly to the steadily declining class average (Fig 6B revised model). No effect size printed.

> "However, our results provide no evidence to support the expectation that AL interludes, spaced at 10-15-minute intervals, reset engagement and enhance student attention to didactic instruction."
