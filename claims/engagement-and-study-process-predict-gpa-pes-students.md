## Evidence

### Tannoubi Amayra 2023

Tannoubi Amayra, Quansah Frank, Magouri Iteb, Chalghaf Nasr, Bonsaksen Tore, Srem-Sai Medina, Hagan John Elvis, Handrianto Ciptro, Azaiez Fairouz, Bragazzi Nicola Luigi. (2023). Modelling the associations between academic engagement, study process and grit on academic achievement of physical education and sport university students. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-023-01454-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis of internet survey data from 459 PES students (aged 19–25) measured with PE-SPQ, PE-Grit and A-USEI. The regression coefficients show "a significantly positive effect of ENG (Estimate = 0.299, p < 0.001)" and of SPQ (Estimate = 0.397, p < 0.001) on GPA; no standardized effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "The results indicate that there was a significantly positive effect of ENG (Estimate = 0.299, p < 0.001) and of SPQ on GPA (Estimate = 0.397, p < 0.001), whereas the effect of GRIT was not significant (p > 0.05)."
