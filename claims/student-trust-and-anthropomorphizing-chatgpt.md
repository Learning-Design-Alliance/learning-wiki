## Evidence

### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2025

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, and Jonas Enger. (2025). Generative AI as a lab partner: a case study. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/ggy1-3kjk](https://doi.org/10.1103/ggy1-3kjk)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Post-lab group interviews across seven groups found "most groups stated that their answers from ChatGPT during the lab seemed reliable"; Groups 4, 6, and 7 retained caution about factual claims.

> "most groups stated that their answers from ChatGPT during the lab seemed reliable and felt no need to fact-check theoretical answers."

### Sebastian Kilde-Westberg 2025 (2)

Sebastian Kilde-Westberg, Andreas Johansson, and Jonas Enger. (2025). Generative AI as a lab partner: a case study. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/ggy1-3kjk](https://doi.org/10.1103/ggy1-3kjk)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Pre- and post-lab interview analysis found students "quick to use words such as knowledge", treating ChatGPT as a person; only Groups 6 and 7 reasoned its reliability depended on training data.

> "when asked about the capabilities of ChatGPT, students were quick to use words such as knowledge and talk less about it as some tool and more as a person with much knowledge."
