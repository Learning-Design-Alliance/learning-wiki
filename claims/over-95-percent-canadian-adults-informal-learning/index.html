Livingstone 2001¶

Livingstone, D.W. (2001). Adults' Informal Learning: Definitions, Findings, Gaps, and Future Research. NALL Working Paper #21. http://www.oise.utoronto.ca/depts/sese/csew/nall/res/21adultsifnormallearning.htm

q3 · i? · review · r2

A representative telephone survey of 1562 Canadian adults conducted for NALL between June and November 1998, using random digit dialling across all provinces. The survey found "nearly all Canadian adults (over 95%)" engaged in identifiable informal learning.

"According to the NALL survey, nearly all Canadian adults (over 95%) are involved in some form of informal learning activities that they can identify as significant."
