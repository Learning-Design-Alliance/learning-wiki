### Liu Hsing-Yuan 2026

Liu Hsing-Yuan. (2026). Skill-based competencies, not traits, are associated with design thinking–aligned teaching behaviours in nursing education: a cross-sectional survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09830-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression with 95 faculty members from 12 Taiwanese institutions, with CTBS total score as the dependent variable. In Model 3, the article reports "Skill-based competencies (β = 0.429, p = 0.050)" remained statistically associated with teaching behaviours.

> "Skill-based competencies (β = 0.429, p = 0.050) and DT teaching hours (β = 0.381, p = 0.050) remained statistically associated with teaching behaviours at the conventional 0.05 level."
