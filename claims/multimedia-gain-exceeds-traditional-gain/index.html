Genç Osman Ilhan and Şahin Oruç 2016¶

Genç Osman Ilhan and Şahin Oruç. (2016). Effect of the use of multimedia on students’ performance: A case study of social studies class. Educational Research and Reviews. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099996

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Between-group comparison of pre-to-post gain scores (Table 3: experimental N=35, control N=32). The article reports t(65) = 6.908, p=.000<.05, with a mean gain of 13.4286 versus -0.1875; no effect size is printed.

"there is a significant difference between the reached values of two groups [t(65) = 6.908, p=.000<.05]. The mean difference of the experimental group is 13.4286 while the mean difference of the control groups is -.1875."
