Seth DeVore, Alexandre Gauthier, Jeremy Levy, and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Development and evaluation of a tutorial to improve Students' Understanding of a lock-in amplifier. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01314](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01314)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/posttest evaluation in which physics graduate students took a pretest, worked through the tutorial, and took a posttest, with questions switched for roughly half the students. The article reports that examination of average student scores on the pretests and posttests 'shows considerable improvement for all cases discussed.'
