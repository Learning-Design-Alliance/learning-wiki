Evidence¶

Amin et al. 2018¶

Bunga D. Amin, Helmi Abdullah and Jasruddin D. Malago. (2018). Sketch strategy of knowledge in physics learning and its influence on metacognitive. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3293

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with 33 graduate students in two intact classes (17 SKS, 16 TVS), taught five matriculation sessions on Mechanics, then given a three-question essay metacognitive knowledge test. The t-test showed "a significant difference with the strategy of the target variable"; no effect size is printed.

"th = 2.96> t table (0,05) = 2.04 Thus it can be said that the influence of sketch knowledge strategies in teaching physics has a significant difference with the strategy of the target variable."
