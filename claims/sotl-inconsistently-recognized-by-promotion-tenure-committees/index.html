q3 · i? · review · r3

Descriptive statistics over the corpus: 58 of 130 articles addressed P&T. The review reports 15 articles described guideline revisions while "many articles (n = 40; 69%) warned that recognizing and valuing SoTL contributions had been limited by P&T committees."

"SoTL in the context P&T processes was mentioned in 58 articles (45%) (33 from medicine), with 15 articles (26%) describing how schools and academic teaching hospitals had revised P&T guidelines to recognize and/or support SoTL activities."

q3 · i? · review · r3

Content analysis of obstruction categories across the corpus, with some articles noting limitations in more than one category; 14 articles noted difficulty quantifying SoTL success, and 15 articles addressed neither supports nor obstructions.

"Specifically, 22 articles noted P&T committees were unfamiliar with SoTL, 25 described P&T processes that lacked mechanisms to judge SoTL activities and products, while 25 articles lamented that P&T committees upheld traditional research metrics for academic success"
