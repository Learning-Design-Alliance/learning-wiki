The review's narrative synthesis of global rating scale studies (19 studies). It reports demonstrated content and concurrent validity and reliability, but notes they require expert surgeon evaluators, are time-consuming, and may be susceptible to assessor bias.

> "The ability of global rating scales to distinguish between all levels of performance and the absence of a ceiling effect make them useful for high-stakes, summative assessment1 and the assessment of advanced residents."
