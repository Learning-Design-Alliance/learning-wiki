## Evidence

### Swain Cara 2025

Swain Cara, Cohen Hugo, Routh Jennifer, Helgesson Gert, Rickard Rory, Karlgren Klas. (2025). Optimising the educational utility of live tissue training in trauma surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07846-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Experiencing theme of the Framework Method analysis of fifteen interviews. Participants valued "real-life bleeding, tissue perfusion and responsiveness, and the presence of reactive physiology", which they described as much more realistic than cadavers.

> "Aspects of clinical realism that were considered to be valuable contributions to the experience were real-life bleeding, tissue perfusion and responsiveness, and the presence of reactive physiology."

### Swain Cara 2025 (2)

Swain Cara, Cohen Hugo, Routh Jennifer, Helgesson Gert, Rickard Rory, Karlgren Klas. (2025). Optimising the educational utility of live tissue training in trauma surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07846-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same qualitative study, Experiencing theme. The analysis reports that learners "would receive instantaneous feedback from the animal itself, which was also indicated by the physiological parameters", and that animals, like human patients, are unpredictable.

> "When the learners performed a manoeuvre to stop bleeding, they would receive instantaneous feedback from the animal itself, which was also indicated by the physiological parameters."
