Evidence¶

Serkan Perkmen 2026¶

Serkan Perkmen, Sara Dexter. (2026). From sources of self-efficacy to technology integration intentions: a new social cognitive model. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10623-0

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Path analysis of the Social Cognitive Model of Technology Integration with 144 preservice teachers; mastery experiences predicted self-efficacy (β = 0.45, p < .01) but not outcome expectations, and no other source showed a significant path.

"Mastery experiences were found to have a strong influence on self-efficacy (β = 0.45, p < .01) but not a significant influence on outcome expectations. None of the other sources of self-efficacy was found to have a significant influence on self-efficacy and outcome expectations."

Serkan Perkmen 2026 (2)¶

Serkan Perkmen, Sara Dexter. (2026). From sources of self-efficacy to technology integration intentions: a new social cognitive model. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10623-0

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression with the same sample; the sources-of-self-efficacy block added 3% of variance beyond self-efficacy and outcome expectations, and "only mastery experiences" was significant in that step.

"Among the sources of self-efficacy, only mastery experiences (β = . 23, p < .01) made a significant contribution to the regression equation in the second step."
