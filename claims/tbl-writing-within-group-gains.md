## Evidence

### Cao 2012

Linying Cao. (2012). A Feasibility Study of Task-based Teaching of College English Writing in Chinese EFL Context. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p80

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-test within the experimental class (N = 40 per Table 7, t = -5.231, Sig. = 0.000) over the six-week task-based writing course; the article reports the posttest mean "higher than its pretest mean score by about 1.3 points". No effect size is printed.

> "From Table 7, it can be found out clearly that the posttest mean score of the experimental class is higher than its pretest mean score by about 1.3 points."

### Cao 2012 (2)

Linying Cao. (2012). A Feasibility Study of Task-based Teaching of College English Writing in Chinese EFL Context. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p80

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired samples t-test within the control class (N = 40) taught by the common method; the article reports "no significant shift" between pretest and posttest means (t = 0.918, P = 0.356). Equivalence was not tested beyond this comparison.

> "The results presented in Table 6 reveal  that there is no significant shift in  the comparison between the posttest mean score of the control class and its pretest mean score (t = 0.918, P = 0.356 > 0.05)."
