### Matthew Barr 2020

Matthew Barr. (2020). A cross-sectional study of video game play habits and graduate skills attainment. Research in Learning Technology. https://doi.org/10.25304/rlt.v28.2326

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation and hierarchical clustering analysis (Figures 1 and 2) of the ten survey measures. The article reports communication measures clustering together, adaptability and resourcefulness largely in another, and the Resourcefulness Scale as an outlier.

> "Perhaps the most striking observation that may be made about the correlogram is that all the correlations are positive, as the scores on any one attribute measure increase, so too do the scores on every other measure, to varying degrees."
