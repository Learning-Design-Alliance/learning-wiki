## Evidence

### Heusler Andreas 2019

Heusler Andreas, Molitor Dominik, Spann Martin. (2019). Knowledge Stock Exchanges increase student success in Massive Open Online Courses. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0223064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-survey census of dual-registered participants (N = 364; response rate 39.5%) rating collaboration and competition on 7-point scales; the printed correlation r = 0.81 supports the coopetitive perception. Self-selected respondents limit generalizability.

> "Both items are highly correlated (r = 0.81) which indicates that students perceive the KSX platform as coopetitive, including both collaborative and competitive elements."
