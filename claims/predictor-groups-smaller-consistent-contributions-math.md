`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Summary finding stated in the article's abstract for the two PALMA machine learning analyses (next-year and varying-lag prediction). The abstract reports that these predictor groups showed "relatively smaller yet consistent contributions" relative to prior achievement; no effect sizes are printed in the available text for this summary.

> "In both analyses, additional predictors, including intelligence quotient, grades, motivation and emotion, cognitive strategies, classroom/home environments, and demographics (including socioeconomic status), exhibited relatively smaller yet consistent contributions, underscoring their distinct roles in predicting math achievement over time."
