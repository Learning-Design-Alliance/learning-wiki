## Evidence

### Bossert 2025

Bossert, S., Daumiller, M., Janke, S., Dresel, M., & Dickhäuser, O. (2025). On the influence of social norms on individual achievement goals. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 924–940. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12756

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal multilevel study of 4198 students in 169 classrooms at two time points after the transition to secondary school; Model 4 entered class-level classroom goal structures and peer achievement goals simultaneously. The article reports that "peer mastery goals remained the single significant predictor of changes in individual mastery goals on the class‐level" (b = 0.81, p < .001 in Table 2).

> "However, if both predictors were added simultaneously, peer mastery goals remained the single significant predictor of changes in individual mastery goals on the class‐level."
