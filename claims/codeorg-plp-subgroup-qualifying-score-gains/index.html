Evidence¶

Richard S. Brown & Emily Anne Brown 2019¶

Richard S. Brown & Emily Anne Brown. (2019). Estimating the Effect of a Teacher Training Program on Advanced Placement® Outcomes. International Journal of Computer Science Education in Schools, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207479

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ATT subgroup analysis of Computer Science Principles qualifying scores in the matched school sample. The article reports 2.68 more qualifying scores for female students, 0.40 for Black students, and 2.25 for Hispanic students, all highly significant; it states the effect sizes for these groups are all in the moderate range (d=.45 to d=.65).

"On average, the program resulted in 2.68 more qualifying scores for female students; t(332) = 5.91, p < .001, 0.40 more qualifying scores for Blac k students; t(332) = 4.14, p < .001 , and 2.25 more qualifying scores per school for Hispanic students ; t(332) = 5.37, p < .001."
