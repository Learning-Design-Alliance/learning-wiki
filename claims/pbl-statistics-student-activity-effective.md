## Evidence

### Tatag Yuli Eko SISWONO 2018

Tatag Yuli Eko SISWONO, Sugi HARTONO, Ahmad Wachidul KOHAR. (2018). Effectiveness of Project Based Learning in Statistics for Lower Secondary Schools. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 75, 197-212. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181463

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational data from student activity observation sheets during three PBL meetings, using six activity categories. Most students were in category 3, "discussing or solving the student worksheet". In the conventional class some students did irrelevant activities such as sleeping and playing for as long as 75 minutes.

> "Observation of student activity during three meetings at the PBL class shows that during every five minute observation, the student could do at least one category of student activity."
