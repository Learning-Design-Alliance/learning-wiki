Hou Qin, Zhou Bin, Kang Jing-Ping, Zuo Yun-Xia. (2025). Competency-driven hemostasis training reduces vascular complications in transradial hemodynamic monitoring: a self-controlled trial with early-career residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08048-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of recorded compression characteristics (Table 1) before and after CDHT: pre-training folds varied (0, 1, or 2) with 20–50% retained width, while all post-training cases used rolled gauze at 15–20% width and ≥ 5 min pressure.

> "Post-training all participants uniformly implemented cylindrical gauze folding, standardized the width-to-circumference ratio to 15–20%, and sustained pressure application for at least 5 min."
