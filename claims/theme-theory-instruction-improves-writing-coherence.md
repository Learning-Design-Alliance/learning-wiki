### Liu Jingxia & Liu Li 2013

Liu Jingxia & Liu Li. (2013). An Empirical Study on the Application of Theme Theory in the Field of Writing Pedagogy. English Language Teaching, 6(5). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n5p117

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with two intact classes of 25 English majors each; post-test compositions graded on CET-4 criteria and analyzed in SPSS 13.0. The coherence means were "14.07" versus "11.05" with t = 3.536; no effect size is printed.
