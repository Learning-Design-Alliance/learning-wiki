Evidence¶

LUO Lan 2024¶

LUO Lan, WANG Anqi, ZHOU Muzhi, ZHU Junhua, CAI Jie, YU Ao, and PAN Hui. (2024). ParaTutor: Coordinating Parent-Child Math Tutoring through Role-Separated LLM Scaffolding. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18030 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.18030

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

Formative study finding from semi-structured interviews with 11 parents and 2 experienced primary school teachers. Thematic analysis identified three recurring challenge categories that disrupt parental tutoring of math word problems.

"Although they are motivated to support learning, they may struggle to make problem structures visible, maintain productive communication, and align their familiar methods with the approaches currently taught at school. As shown in Figure 2, these challenges can be understood as cognitive misalignment, emotional escalation, and method mismatch."
