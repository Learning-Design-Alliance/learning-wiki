## Evidence

### Katsantonis I 2023

Katsantonis I, McLellan R. (2023). Profiling students' motivation and metacognitive self-regulation: Links with school language achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0289367

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Manual BCH analysis regressing school language achievement on latent profile membership with fixed-effects estimation (15% of achievement variance was school-level). Means were M = 13.02 (minimal), M = 16.05 (adequate), M = 18.01 (exceptional); the minimal–exceptional contrast showed "Cohen's d = 1.94".

> "Adjusting for covariates (Table 5), the mean language achievement between profiles 2 and 3 differed significantly with a large effect size, Z = -3.08, p = .002, Cohen's d = .90."
