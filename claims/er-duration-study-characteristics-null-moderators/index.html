Learning a Language Through Reading: A Meta-analysis of Studies on the Effects of Extensive Reading on Second and Foreign Language Learning. q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-regression of intervention duration in weeks across all effect measures combined. The article reports the duration moderator was not significant (β = −0.002, SE = 0.004, p =.669); the study-characteristics moderator analyses (Table 4) also revealed no significant effects, and study quality was non-significant (β = 0.06, p =.346).

"The continuous variable that indicated the duration of the interventions in weeks was not found to be significant (β = −0.002, SE = 0.004, p =.669)."
