Silvia Robles 2021¶

Silvia Robles, Max Gross, Robert W. Fairlie. (2021). The Effect of Course Shutouts on Community College Students: Evidence from Waitlist Cutoffs. Journal of Public Economics, vol. 199. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-effect-of-course-shutouts-on-community-college-students-evidence-from-waitlist-cutoffs

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study using administrative course registration data and a research design that exploits discontinuities in course admissions created by waitlists at a community college. The finding that shutout students were "22–28 percent more likely to take zero courses that term" held across a range of bandwidths. No standardized effect size is printed, only the percentage comparison.

"Across a range of bandwidths, we find that students stuck on a waitlist and shut out of a course section were 22–28 percent more likely to take zero courses that term relative to a baseline of about 10 percent."
