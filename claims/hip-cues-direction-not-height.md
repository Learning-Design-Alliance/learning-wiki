## Evidence

### Causer Joe 2017

Causer Joe, Smeeton Nicholas J., Williams A. Mark. (2017). Expertise differences in anticipatory judgements during a temporally and spatially occluded task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171330

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chance-level t-tests in the correct-height analysis: less-skilled keepers were at chance in the -160 ms (t22 = 1.639, p = 0.12) and -80 ms (t22 = 1.045, p = 0.31) hip region conditions, unlike skilled players.

> "In fact, the less-skilled participants were no better than chance at predicting height in the -80 ms and -160 ms hip region conditions, which suggests they are unable to extract any critical cues for height anticipation from this area, unlike the skilled players."
