Error-type analysis of drafts from the two-group experiment (control and experimental, 22 students each), tabulated by sentence structure, grammar and vocabulary. The quote gives the online group's percentage decreases; the conventional group's decreases were 23.2% and 17.6% with no grammar improvement.

"From the first draft to the final draft it was found that students who received online corrective feedback had a decreased number of errors committed for sentence struct ure (47.4%), grammar (70.6%) and vocabulary (45.4%)."
