### Brown Celia 2025

Brown Celia, Sebastian Ann, Khavandi Sarah, Badger Kerry, Westacott Rachel, Reed Malcolm, Gurnell Mark, Sam Amir H. (2025). Does candidate race influence simulated patient ratings in standardised assessments of clinical practice? A single-blinded randomised study in UK medical schools. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-080543

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised video-based study in which 163 simulated patients each scored one white and one non-white candidate per grade; Wilcoxon matched-pairs tests with Sidak correction showed "The black fail candidate scored significantly lower than their white fail counterpart" (table 2: median −1, z 4.878, p<0.001).

> "The black fail candidate scored significantly lower than their white fail counterpart. In contrast, the black and Chinese borderline candidates scored significantly higher than their white counterparts (figure 2)."
