Thomas Keller 2019¶

Thomas Keller, Fabian Hagen and Elke Brucker-Kley. (2019). A FIELD STUDY ABOUT THE IMPACT OF A VR LEARNING UNIT. 16th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2019). https://doi.org/10.33965/celda2019_201911L038

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Item-level analysis of the random sample tests in the field experiment. The conclusion section reports the differential effectiveness between linear and hollow measures as a trend from the data analysis, attributed to virtual-space design criteria.

"The effectiveness of the VR learning unit differs between linear measures and hollow measures. Linear measures performed better than hollow measures. The assumption suggests that the design criteria of the virtual space play a role here."
