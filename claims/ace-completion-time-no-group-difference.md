Ilic Dragan, Nordin Rusli Bin, Glasziou Paul, Tilson Julie K, Villanueva Elmer. (2014). Development and validation of the ACE tool: assessing medical trainees' competency in evidence based medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-114

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of variance of completion times in the 342-participant sample: novice 12.8 (±8.31), intermediate 13.6 (±9.2), advanced 12.1 (±8.3) minutes. The article reports "No significant difference in time taken to complete the ACE tool between the groups"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.
