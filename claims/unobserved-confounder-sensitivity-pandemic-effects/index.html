Robustness analysis assuming unobserved confounder–outcome relationships of d = 0.10, 0.30, or 0.50 and computing the confounder difference between cohorts needed to eliminate the findings; reported in the sensitivity analysis section with details in Table S1.

"Overall, medium to large dif- ferences (d = 0.24 to 0.57) in an unobserved confounder between the stu- dents in Cohort 1 and students in Cohort 2 as well as a strong relationship between the confounder and the outcome variable (d = 0.30 or 0.50) would be necessary to eliminate the previously reported ﬁndings."
