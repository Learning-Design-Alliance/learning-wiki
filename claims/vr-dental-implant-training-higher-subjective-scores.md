## Evidence

### Zhou Yong 2021

Zhou Yong, Chen Wanlu, Zhao Xiaoxian, He Bingwei, Huang Wenxiu, Wu Dong, Chen Jiang. (2021). Application evaluation of virtual reality technology in dental implant training: a new dental implant training system: a CONSORT-compliant trial. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000027355

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 30 young physicians divided into two groups of 15, with the VR group (A) compared against conventional training (B) via questionnaire. The VR group's medians were "9/9/9/8/8" versus "6/7/6/7/7" for controls, with rank-sum Z values from −4.695 to −2.833 and P values from .000 to .005 reported in Table 2.

> "The median scores in the 5 dimensions of postoperative assessment of group A was 9/9/9/8/8, and of group B was 6/7/6/7/7. The scores of the 5 dimensions were significantly higher than those of group B (P < .05)"
