## Evidence

### Arcand KK 2024

Arcand KK, Schonhut-Stasik JS, Kane SG, Sturdevant G, Russo M, Watzke M, Hsu B and Smith LF (2024). A Universe of Sound: processing NASA data into sonifications to explore participant response. Front. Commun. 9:1288896. https://doi.org/10.3389/fcomm.2024.1288896

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey ratings of the prompt about sound enhancing the experience (Fig. 5), compared between groups with K-S tests. Participants felt audio "enhanced their experience, particularly for the Deep Field"; all p-values were high, and no effect size is printed.

> "Generally, participants felt that adding audio to the astronomical images enhanced their experience, particularly for the Deep Field. Interestingly, all the p-values are high, suggesting that both the sighted and BLV participants found their experience enhanced to the same extent."
