# Planning and revision skills were unrelated, and neither predicted self-efficacy, in the studied samples

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The paths Planning→Revision, Planning→Self-efficacy, and Revision→Self-efficacy were non-significant in both groups and were deleted from the final model. [→ Limpo 2013](#limpo-2013)

## Evidence

### Limpo 2013

Limpo, T., & Alves, R. A. (2013). Modeling writing development: Contribution of transcription and self-regulation to Portuguese students' text generation quality. Journal of Educational Psychology, 105, 401-413. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031391

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model respecification in the SEM removed the non-significant paths among the self-regulation constructs for both grade groups; the authors note writers' planning and revising skills "did not develop in tandem" in this sample.

> "to produce the most parsimonious model, the non-significant paths for both groups were deleted (viz., Planning Revision, Planning Self-efficacy, and Revision Self-Efficacy)"
