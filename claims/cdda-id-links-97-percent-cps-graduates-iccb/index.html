Evidence¶

Jenny Nagaoka and Shelby Mahaffie 2020¶

Jenny Nagaoka and Shelby Mahaffie. (2020). Tracking Two-Year College Outcomes: Comparing National Student Clearinghouse and Illinois Community College Board as Sources of Two-Year College Data. Technical Paper, University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/tracking-two-year-college-outcomes

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Linking analysis of the 2012 CPS graduating class (19,801 unique students) using CPS student ID, Illinois state student ID, and CDDA ID. The authors report that 97 percent of records were successfully linked, and that the unmatched 3 percent "did not appear to be missing in any systematic way."

"Of the 19,801 CPS high school graduates in the sample, 19,209 (97 percent) of student records were successfully linked to at least one record in the ICCB data."
