Survey trust ratings (Fig. 7) for the prompt on whether the representation was faithful to the science data, compared between BLV and sighted groups with K-S tests. Frequency of 4 and 5 ratings suggested belief in scientific accuracy; p-values were "all > 0.9"; no effect size printed.
