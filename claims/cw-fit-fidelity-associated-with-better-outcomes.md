## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the treatment group, fidelity was significantly and positively related to SSBS-2 Social Competence Total, Self-Management/Compliance, CPS-E Interpersonal Competence, several problem behavior scales, and academic measures (all p < .05). [→ Caldarella 2017](#caldarella-2017)

## Evidence

### Caldarella 2017

Caldarella, P., Larsen, R. A., Williams, L., Wills, H. P., Kamps, D., & Wehby, J. (2017). Effects of CW-FIT on teachers’ ratings of elementary school students at risk for emotional and behavioral disorders. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300717723353

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory multivariate linear regression of fidelity on outcomes, run separately for treatment and control groups. The article reports significance at p < .05 for these measures and states the analysis "was exploratory and should not be considered to have the same causal impact"; no standardized effect size is treated as printed here.

> "Fidelity results for the treatment group showed that effects for SSBS-2 Social Competence Total, SSBS-2 Self-Management/Compliance, and CPS-E Interpersonal Competence were all significant (p < .05) suggesting that an increase of fidelity affected these social skill measures in a positive way."
