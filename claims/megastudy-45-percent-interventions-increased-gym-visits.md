## Evidence

### Kautz 2021

Kautz, T., Milkman, K. L., Gromet, D., et al. as printed in full author line. (2021). Megastudies Improve the Impact of Applied Behavioural Science. Nature, vol. 600. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/megastudies-improve-the-impact-of-applied-behavioural-science

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Large field experiment (megastudy) with 61,293 fitness-chain members comparing 54 four-week digital exercise programmes on objectively measured gym attendance. The article reports that "45% of these interventions significantly increased weekly gym visits by 9% to 27%"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "We show that 45% of these interventions significantly increased weekly gym visits by 9% to 27%; the top-performing intervention offered microrewards for returning to the gym after a missed workout."
