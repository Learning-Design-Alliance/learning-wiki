`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview study of GLASS trial trainees and SSA-based educators. The article reports that "SBSE was widely accepted by both trainees and surgeon educators", attributing acceptance to patient safety and perceived faster skill acquisition; no effect size is reported.

> "SBSE was widely accepted by both trainees and surgeon educators. Reasons for this related to patient safety concerns during live surgical teaching and apparent faster acquisition of surgical skills compared to traditional surgical teaching."
