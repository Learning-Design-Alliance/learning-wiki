### Gartmeier Martin 2019

Gartmeier Martin, Baumgartner Maria, Burgkart Rainer, Heiniger Susanne, Berberat Pascal O. (2019). Why hand hygiene is not sufficient: modeling hygiene competence of clinical staff as a basis for its development and assessment. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(4):Doc39. [doi:10.3205/zma001247](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001247)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative findings from the think-aloud pilot with two students. Unfamiliarity with areas like the OR caused difficulties; the article also notes students could sometimes "figure out correct answers through more prolonged, thorough contemplation," suggesting time limits as a difficulty lever. No effect sizes are printed.

> "it was apparent that a lack of familiarity with certain medical work areas – e.g. the OR – went along with major difficulties to answer the items correctly."
