## Evidence

### Kuhfeld 2019

Kuhfeld, Megan, Soland, James, Pitts, Christine, & Burchinal, Margaret. (2019). Trends in Children's Academic Skills at School Entry: 2010 to 2017. EdWorkingPaper No. 19-137, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. http://www.edworkingpapers.com/ai19-137

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Gap models re-estimated with school-poverty indicators (mid- and high-poverty FRPL categories) added, using the same weighted kindergarten sample. The authors note student-level SES was unavailable, so school poverty served as the control, and gaps remained "statistically significant across all years in both math and reading."

> "After controlling for school-level poverty, the Black-White and Hispanic-White achievement gaps were greatly reduced (an average reduction of approximately 0.18 SD in math and 0.16 SD in reading)."
