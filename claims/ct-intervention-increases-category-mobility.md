### Kaup 2023

Kaup, C. F., Pedersen, P. L., & Tvedebrink, T. (2023). Integrating computational thinking to enhance students’ mathematical understanding. Journal of Pedagogical Research, 7(2), 127-142. https://doi.org/10.33902/JPR.202318531

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Empirical relative frequencies of mobility rates (Table 8) from the quasi-experiment comparing intervention and reference groups' category movement between pre- and post-test.

> "The table shows that the chance of moving upwards in category (i.e., in terms of ‘performance level’) increases in the intervention group compared to the reference group."

### Kaup 2023 (2)

Kaup, C. F., Pedersen, P. L., & Tvedebrink, T. (2023). Integrating computational thinking to enhance students’ mathematical understanding. Journal of Pedagogical Research, 7(2), 127-142. https://doi.org/10.33902/JPR.202318531

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Ordinal regression on overall-score categories predicting mobility probabilities, visualized in Figure 2, supporting the empirical mobility findings from the same quasi-experiment.

> "It is clear that the intervention group has a higher chance of ending up in the higher categories, and in particular, improving by more than a single category is notably higher for the intervention group."
