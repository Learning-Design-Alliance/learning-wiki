`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the guidelines section, the authors report that G1 was "derived mainly from student concerns about not knowing what data is being collected, how it is stored, and who can access it", and explain they did not force it into the Community of Inquiry framework because it would not increase cognitive, social, or teaching presence.

> "This guideline was derived mainly from student concerns about not knowing what data is being collected, how it is stored, and who can access it."

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The article reports, citing prior work, that students' "implicit trust in their institution to handle data ethically" does not extend to third-party educational tools, supporting the guideline's emphasis on transparency about data sent to third-party vendors.

> "Higher education students have an implicit trust in their institution to handle data ethically [74], but that trust does not extend to data collected by a third-party educational tool [69]."
