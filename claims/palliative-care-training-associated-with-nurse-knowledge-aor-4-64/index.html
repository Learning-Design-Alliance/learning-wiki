Evidence¶

Getie Addisu 2021¶

Getie Addisu, Wondmieneh Adam, Bimerew Melaku, Gedefaw Getnet, Demis Asmamaw. (2021). Knowledge on Palliative Care and Associated Factors among Nurses in Ethiopia: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Pain Research & Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/5557947

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of the association between palliative care training and nurses' knowledge across the included cross-sectional studies. The article prints the effect size AOR = 4.64 (95% CI: 2.37–9.08), described as "4.64 times more likely among nurses who were trained on palliative care".

"The odds of nurses' knowledge were 4.64 times more likely among nurses who were trained on palliative care than their counterparts (AOR = 4.64; 95% CI: 2.37–9.08)."
