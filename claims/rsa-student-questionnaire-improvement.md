## Subclaims
`q2 i?` More than 200 high school students who participated in the structured RSA activity showed, according to pre- and post-questionnaires, a remarkable improvement in knowledge of the topics addressed, with advanced topics such as black holes and gravitational lenses well understood by almost all participants and remembered after months. The authors report the results "show a remarkable improvement in the knowledge of the topics addressed", with details deferred to a future paper; no effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

> "More than 200 high school students of different ages participated to the educational activity; the groups consisted of students belonging to the same class (often the entire class) or to students coming from different classes but selected by their teachers because they were very interested in these scientific topics."
