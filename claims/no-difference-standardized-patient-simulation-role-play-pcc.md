## Evidence

### Cortés-Rodríguez 2024

Cortés-Rodríguez, López-Rodríguez, Roman, Granero-Molina, Fernández-Sola and Hernández-Padilla. (2024). Effect of two teaching methods on nursing students' acquisition of patient-centered communication competence in older people care: a cluster randomized trial. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1510620

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial comparing 124 nursing students in eight teaching groups allocated to standardized patient simulation or role-play; GEE analysis with logit link and Bonferroni correction found "statistically significant differences over time between groups" were absent for all variables (p > 0.05).

> "GEE analysis did not show statistically significant differences over time between groups for any of the variables studied (p > 0.05)."

### Cortés-Rodríguez 2024 (2)

Cortés-Rodríguez, López-Rodríguez, Roman, Granero-Molina, Fernández-Sola and Hernández-Padilla. (2024). Effect of two teaching methods on nursing students' acquisition of patient-centered communication competence in older people care: a cluster randomized trial. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1510620

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-squared between-group comparisons of benchmark success rates across the three time points found "no statistically significant differences" between the standardized patient simulation group and the role-play group on any component or overall competence.

> "When comparing the success rates of the standardized patient simulation group and the role-play group applying the Chi-square test, no statistically significant differences were observed in the pre-test, post-test, or retention-test on any of the three components or in the overall patient-centered communication in older people care competence."
