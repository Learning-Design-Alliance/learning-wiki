Quantitative content analysis of interaction screenshots (Table 4) in the case study. Table 4 shows methodological knowledge or advice as the largest category (61 questions, 20.07%), followed by classroom management (46 questions, 15.13%) and assessment and evaluation (39 questions, 12.83%).
