## Evidence

### Asadi 2026

Asadi, I. (2026). Evaluating social–emotional skills among Arabic‐speaking students: A multi‐informant study of students, parents and teachers. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70044

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA interaction term from the same sample. The article reports the "group and grade level" interaction was significant (η 2 = .056), and t-tests showed students differed from the other groups only from fifth grade onward, with all groups differing in grades 7 and 9.

> "A significant interaction was found between group and grade level, F(2, 168) = 3.23, p < .01, η 2 = .056, which may be related to larger discrepancies between groups with age"

### Asadi 2026 (2)

Asadi, I. (2026). Evaluating social–emotional skills among Arabic‐speaking students: A multi‐informant study of students, parents and teachers. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70044

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bonferroni pairwise comparisons across grades 3, 5, 7 and 9 showed a decreasing trend in SEL composite means (e.g., students 4.12 in grade 3 to 3.60 in grade 9, Table 1); only the grade-3 versus other-grades contrast reached p < .001. No effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "pairwise comparisons using the Bonferroni test revealed a decreasing trend with age, regardless of group. However, these differences between grade levels were primarily related to third graders, who reported significantly (p < .001) higher scores than all other grades"
