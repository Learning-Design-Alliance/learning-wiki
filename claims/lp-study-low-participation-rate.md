## Evidence

### Gaubert Sinead et al 2021

Gaubert Sinead et al. (2021). Positive effects of lumbar puncture simulation training for medical students in clinical practice. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02452-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observation from the randomized study's recruitment: of 194 students per group, 27 (66%) in the simulation group and 14 (34%) in the control group completed the real-life evaluation. The article attributes low participation to students being unused to educational studies and to evaluation complexity in routine practice.

> "Between October 2017 and May 2018, 41 students (10.6%) performed a first LP in a patient and filled in the electronic survey."
