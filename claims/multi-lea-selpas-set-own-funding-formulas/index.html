Jason Willis 2020¶

Jason Willis, Sara Menlove Doutre, Kelsey Krausen, Tyson Barrett, Tye Ripma, and Ruthie Caparas. (2020). Study of California's Special Education Funding System: Phase I Report — A Descriptive Analysis of Special Education Funding in California. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED616102

Descriptive analysis in the executive summary reports that multi-LEA SELPA governing boards, "made up primarily of member LEA superintendents," have wide latitude and "can change the state's funding formula," so state allocation changes may not reach LEAs unless distribution or expenditure requirements also change.

"More than 90 percent of California LEAs, including all charter LEAs, belong to a multi-LEA SELPA."
