# California College Promise programs commonly impose full-time enrollment, GPA maintenance, and support-service participation requirements

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Thirty of the 42 programs require full-time enrollment (at least 12 units per semester), and 25 require a minimum GPA to maintain the award. [→ Rauner 2018](#rauner-2018)
`q2 i?` Thirty-one of 42 programs require engagement with support programs, such as academic planning or summer bridge, as an eligibility condition. [→ Rauner 2018 (2)](#rauner-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Rauner 2018

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Database findings on enrollment and achievement requirements; 25 programs require a specified minimum GPA to maintain eligibility from term to term, and 8 have initial merit requirements, mostly GPA cutoffs between 2.0 and 2.5.

> "Thirty of the 42 programs require program participants to be enrolled full time, which in California is considered to be taking at least 12 units per semester."

### Rauner 2018 (2)

Rauner, M., Perna, L. W., & Kanter, M. J. (2018). California College Promise: Program Characteristics and Perceptions from the Field. WestEd. https://californiacollegepromise.WestEd.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/College-Promise-Landscape-Scan-2018.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Database findings; required services vary and may include meeting a counselor to develop an academic plan, participating in summer bridge programs, and applying for EOPS or other support opportunities.

> "Most (31 of 42) programs also require that students engage with support programs to be eligible for the financial award."
