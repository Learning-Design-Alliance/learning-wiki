## Evidence

### Gedamu Tigist Enyew 2025

Gedamu Tigist Enyew. (2025). The impact of nutritional and socioeconomic status on academic performance of primary urban school children in northern Ethiopia: a cross-sectional study. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-025-02175-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted multivariate logistic regression in the 399-child cross-sectional study reported these three factors as significantly associated with academic performance above the mean of 71.65; the preschool-attendance interval (0.93–2.31) crosses unity even though the article marks it significant.

> "In addition, un absenteeism from school (AOR = 1.73, 95% CI: 1.07–2.79), attending preschool (AOR = 1.47, 95%CI: 0.93–2.31) and having high monthly income (AOR = 1.67, CI: 1.02–2.74) increased academic performance almost by two fold each."
