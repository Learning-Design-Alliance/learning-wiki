## Evidence

### Li Zhang 2024

Li Zhang, Baolige Chao, Yan Gao, Wenjing Wang, Yingzi Yuan, Chuangsheng Chen & Ziqiang Xin. (2024). Gender Inequality is negatively associated with academic achievement for both boys and girls. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00261-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Fixed-effects panel regression on country-year data (N = 149 per model) with country and year fixed effects and GDP controlled shows GII coefficients "negatively signiﬁcant" in all four models; the article reports reading scores decrease by an average of 0.47 units, math 0.18, and science 0.29 per GII unit increase, while Gini is nonsignificant.

> "The results show that the coefﬁcients of GII in all four regression models are negatively signiﬁcant, which suggests that an increase in gender inequality will signi ﬁcantly weaken students’ academic achievement."
