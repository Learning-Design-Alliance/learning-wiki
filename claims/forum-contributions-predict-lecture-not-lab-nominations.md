## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2022

Meagan Sundstrom, Ashley B. Heim, Barum Park, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Introductory physics students' recognition of strong peers: Gender and racial/ethnic bias differ by course level and context. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ERGM outspokenness variable, measurable only for Courses B and C (Course A had no forum); forum data were imputed as zero for students who never used the forum. The authors suspect most forum content concerned lecture material.

> "students who contribute more to the discussion forum are significantly more likely to receive nominations as strong in the lecture context. Contributions to the discussion forum, however, are not a significant predictor of receiving nominations as strong in the lab context."
