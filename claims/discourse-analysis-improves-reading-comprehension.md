## Evidence

### Kapanadze 2018

Dilek Ünveren Kapanadze. (2018). THE EFFECT OF USING DISCOURSE ANALYSIS METHOD ON IMPROVING COGNITIVE AND AFFECTIVE SKILLS IN LANGUAGE AND LITERATURE TEACHING. European Journal of Education Studies, Volume 4, Issue 5. https://www.oapub.org/edu

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group quasi-experiment in a Turkish laboratory school: 20 experimental (11-A) and 24 control (11-B) eleventh-graders, four months of treatment. The article reports the "increase in the experimental group is more significant than the control group"; no effect size is printed.

> "In terms of reading comprehension skill, as a result of t test conducted on pretest and post test scores of both groups; it was identified that the arithmetic mean of control group was increased from 3,00 to 3,95 whereas it increased from 3,60 to 8,15 in experimental group."
