## Evidence

### Randy K Yerrick 2017

Randy K Yerrick, Tiffany Simons. (2017). The Affordances of Fiction for Teaching Chemistry. Science Education International, Volume 28, Issue 3. https://www.stemedu.org/science-education-international/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-control action research design: one 84-minute block received traditional lecture/lab instruction, one received fiction-supplemented instruction; 49 students over 4 weeks. Standard t-tests were assigned to achievement data; the control-versus-intervention comparison yielded p = 0.378 (chemical reaction questions) and p = 0.4999 (total test) in Table 3, described as "statistically insignificant".

> "While the differences between the chemical reaction questions and total final assessments were slightly greater for the intervention group, the differences were statistically insignificant."
