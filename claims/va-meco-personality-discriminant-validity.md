## Evidence

### Reiser S 2025

Reiser S, Schacht L, Thomm E, Janssen L, Pittroff SID, Dörfler E, Schick K, Berberat PO, Gartmeier M, Bauer J. (2025). Online assessment of medical students' communication competence in patient encounters: Validation of the VA-MeCo situational judgement test. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0332957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the N = 395 survey using the BFI-K. The article reports "correlations close to zero for extraversion and neuroticism" and low positive correlations for the other traits; the discussion notes one example of r = .16 between agreeableness and relationship, judged essentially small.

> "For the five sub-scales of the Big Five personality traits, we found correlations close to zero for extraversion and neuroticism. For agreeableness, conscientiousness, and openness, there were low positive correlations for each of the three dimensions, especially for the building relationship dimension."
