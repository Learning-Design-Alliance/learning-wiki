## Evidence

### Nicholas Feroce 2025

Nicholas Feroce, Jenny Liu, Rajendra Chattergoon. (2025). Exploring the impact of a CALL tool for emergent bilinguals. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10462-5

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental regression analysis of spring 2021 ELPAC scores for 2034 K-5 ELs (1478 treatment, 556 comparison) in one California district. The article reports the Lexia English coefficient as 0.197*** (SE 0.051) and interprets SD units as comparable to effect sizes, i.e. an effect size of 0.197 SDs, a small significant effect.

> "For the overall ELPAC, students who used the CALL program were predicted to score, on average, 0.197 SDs above their grade-specific mean after controlling for student demographics and school-related differences, compared to students who did not use the program."
