# Students taught by teachers who received training outperformed those in the control condition, and adding the AIED-U system did not improve learning gains further beyond training alone

## Evidence

### Luiz Rodrigues 2026

Luiz Rodrigues, Guilherme Guerino, Thomaz Edson Silva, Luana Bianchini, Mariana Alves, Geiser Chalco Challco, Thales Vieira, Marcelo Marinho, Valmir Macario, Diego Dermeval, Ig Ibert Bittencourt, Seiji Isotani. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Robust linear mixed models nesting 221 students within 19 classes tested learning gains (IRT proficiency difference, 0–100 scale) across three randomly assigned classroom conditions. The article reports the experimental condition "outperformed the Control one" with Est = 12.31, while experimental and training conditions did not differ.

> "the Experimental condition outperformed the Control one (Est = 12.31, SE = 5.77, t = 2.13, CI [0.34, 21.86]), as did the Training condition (Est = 15.15, SE = 10.15, t = 1.49, CI [0.84, 31.10]). In contrast, the Experimental and Training conditions did not differ (Est = −4.33, SE = 5.93, t = −0.73, CI [−17.29, 7.23])."
