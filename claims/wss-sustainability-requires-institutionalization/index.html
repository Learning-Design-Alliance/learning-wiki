q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

Authors' interpretation in the Interpretive Summary section, not a tested result. The article states this is particularly challenging amid significant transitions in departmental and district leadership at CPS and the CBOE, and that "replicability requires more than a model—it requires deep organizational commitment and cultural change."

"The durability of this work depends not just on the framework or policy itself, but on the district's capacity to institutionalize authentic engagement as a core practice, not just a one-time initiative."
