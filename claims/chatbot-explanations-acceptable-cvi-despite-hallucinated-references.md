Sabaner Mehmet Cem, Yozgat Zübeyir. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT-4 Omni and Gemini 1.5 Pro on Ophthalmology-Related Questions in the Turkish Medical Specialty Exam. Turkish Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4274/tjo.galenos.2025.27895

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Expert validity assessment: two senior ophthalmologists scored each explanation on a 4-point Likert scale and I-CVI values were averaged. Table 3 prints average CVI values ranging 0.92-0.98 across models, languages, and attempts.

> "Content validity, assessed through average CVI values, demonstrated high agreement for both chatbots across all attempts and in both languages, as detailed in Table 3."
