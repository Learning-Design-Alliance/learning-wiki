## Evidence

### Ashley A. August 2011

Ashley A. August, Kenneth C. Bretey, Bryant T. Cory, Elliott R. Finkley III, Robbie D. Jones, Dennis W. Marshall, Phillip C. Rowley, and W. Brian Lane. (2011). Enhancing Introductory Student Motivation with a Major-Managed Course Blog: A Pilot Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1108.1591](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1108.1591)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Biweekly in-class surveys, based on established design principles for self-confidence surveys and interest checklists, administered throughout the semester. The article reports improvement in "self-conﬁdence in each of the identiﬁed skills" and mixed responses on topic interest, with two students crediting the blog.

> "In general, students reported improvement in their self-conﬁdence in each of the identiﬁed skills. When asked which learning activities helped to improve their conﬁdence, students cited the laboratory exercises and in-class activities (par-ticularly the group problem-solving sessions) as being particularly helpful, with a few of the students citing the course blog as being helpful."
