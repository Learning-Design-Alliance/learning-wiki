## Evidence

### Middeke Angélina 2018

Middeke Angélina, Anders Sven, Schuelper Madita, Raupach Tobias, Schuelper Nikolai. (2018). Training of clinical reasoning with a Serious Game versus small-group problem-based learning: A prospective study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203851

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Design comparison from the Methods of the prospective cohort study: both formats received the same curricular learning time, but "the number of different clinical cases each student was exposed to was eight times higher in the EMERGE group (40 cases) than in the PBL group (5 cases)". Combined with the null result on shared cases, the authors conclude exposure breadth did not hamper outcome.

> "While learning time was kept similar in both formats and set by the curricular requirements (ten 90-minute sessions of CR teaching), the number of different clinical cases each student was exposed to was eight times higher in the EMERGE group (40 cases) than in the PBL group (5 cases)."
