Evidence¶

Childs 1915¶

Childs, H. G. (1915). Measurement of the drawing ability of two thousand one hundred and seventy-seven children in Indiana city school systems by a supplemented Thorndike scale. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(7), 391-408. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075575

q2 · i? · design · r2

Analysis of the distribution table for system A's 1,405 pupils, showing how the scale's missing sample ranges distort the score distribution. The author also notes more ratings of 13 to 13.99 than of 12 to 12.99, and points to Table VII for verification of these facts.

"As a result there are fewer ratings of 4 to 4.99 than there are of 3 to 3.99, whereas a normal distribution of ability should give more."
