Second scored task of the same 45-example comparison: explanations of invalidity rated very good, good, fair, poor or none. Tutored observers averaged 24 'very good' explanations versus 9.8 for the class-taught group. No effect size is printed.

> "The quality of the reasons advanced for the decisions of invalidity is very distinctly superior in the inductively taught group, for on the average, they gave 24 'very good' explanations as against 9.8 explanations of that quality on the part of the other observers."
