## Evidence

### Hans van der Meij 2018

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij, Tessa Voerman, Evert Duipmans. (2018). Supporting motivation, task performance and retention in video tutorials for software training. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 597–614. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9560-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA on initial and final self-efficacy reports from 65 upper-elementary students randomly assigned to control, review, or 2-demo conditions. The tutorials "enhanced self-efficacy significantly and substantially" with d = 1.48; final means rose from about 4.33 to 6.11 on a 7-point scale.

> "The video tutorials enhanced self-efficacy significantly and substantially, F(1,61) = 90.02, p \ 0.001, d = 1.48. There was no main effect of condition and no interaction between time and condition, F \ 1."
