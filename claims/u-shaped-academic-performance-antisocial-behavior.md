# Academic performance has a U-shaped relationship with antisocial behavior: antisocial behavior first falls, then rises once performance exceeds a threshold

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The square of academic performance positively predicts antisocial behavior, indicating a curvilinear U-shaped relationship among junior high school students. [→ Zhu Xiaobo 2024](#zhu-xiaobo-2024)

## Evidence

### Zhu Xiaobo 2024

Zhu Xiaobo, Jiang Wenyi, Shi Weijin, Shi Junfeng. (2024). Good learners or trouble makers? Study on the relationship between academic performance and antisocial behavior of junior high school students. PLOS ONE 19(1): e0295705. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0295705

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis (Model 3) of the CEPS sample of 8,270 junior high students on parent-rated antisocial behavior, with the centralized square of academic performance entered as predictor. The authors report "β = .08, p < .001" for the square term and describe the plotted curve (Fig 2) as U-shaped.

> "The results of Model 3 show that there is a positive correlation between the square term of academic performance and antisocial behavior (β = .08, p < .001), which indicates that there is a curvilinear relationship between academic performance and antisocial behavior."

### Zhu Xiaobo 2024 (2)

Zhu Xiaobo, Jiang Wenyi, Shi Weijin, Shi Junfeng. (2024). Good learners or trouble makers? Study on the relationship between academic performance and antisocial behavior of junior high school students. PLOS ONE 19(1): e0295705. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0295705

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slope tests (Table 4) of academic performance on antisocial behavior at -2SD through 2SD. Slopes were negative and significant at -2SD, -SD and 0 (e.g., "β = -.210, p < .001"), nonsignificant at 1SD, and significantly positive at 2SD.

> "When the academic performance rises to 2 SD, academic performance has turned to be significant positively related to antisocial behavior (β = .050, P < .010)."
