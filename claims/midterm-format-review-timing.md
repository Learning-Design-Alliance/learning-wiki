## Evidence

### Bailey Elizabeth G. 2021

Bailey Elizabeth G., Greenall Rebeka F., Tullis Madeleine M., Williams Kurt R. (2021). The retention benefits of cumulative versus non-cumulative midterms in introductory biology may depend on students’ reasoning skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250143

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-course attitudinal survey self-reports analyzed with Mann-Whitney U tests (Fig 3, n = 299); review quality did not differ by section, and weekly study hours were indistinguishable (p = 0.26).

> "students with cumulative midterms were more likely to study a past midterm before the next midterm (Fig 3A, p = 0.037) and students with non-cumulative midterms were more likely to study the midterms in preparation for the final exam (Fig 3B, p = 0.003)."
