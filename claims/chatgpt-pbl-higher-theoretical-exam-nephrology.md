`q3 i?` After eight weeks of training, the ChatGPT-guided PBL group achieved a significantly higher theoretical exam score (91.82 ± 2.25) than the control group (87.69 ± 3.99). [→ Tong Xiaoya 2025](#tong-xiaoya-2025)

## Evidence

### Tong Xiaoya 2025

Tong Xiaoya, Hu Ying, Long Yanjun, Zhang Qian, Yang Yao, Yuan Jing, Zha Yan. (2025). The application of problem-based learning (PBL) guided by ChatGPT in clinical education in the Department of Nephrology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07427-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 54 nephrology residents (28 experimental, 26 control) taught for eight weeks; t-test on post-training theoretical exam scores showed the experimental group scored "91.82 ± 2.25" versus 87.69 ± 3.99, P < 0.001. No effect size was printed.

> "after the training, the experimental group demonstrated a significantly higher score of 91.82 ± 2.25 in the theoretical exam compared to 87.69 ± 3.99 in the control group (P < 0.001)"
