## Evidence

### K. Nikolopoulos 2020

K. Nikolopoulos, M. Pardalaki. (2020). Particle Dance: Particle physics in the dance studio. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ab6952](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ab6952)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative feedback from the pilot workshops' round tables. Each team discussed directly with the musician the music they would like to use; the article reports this was in most cases the first time students had this kind of freedom during a dance class.

> "The aspect of having live music, and in particularly chosing the music on their own proved extremely powerful, to the extend that it was thought to be the cornerstone in the process of the students taking ownership of the choreography."
