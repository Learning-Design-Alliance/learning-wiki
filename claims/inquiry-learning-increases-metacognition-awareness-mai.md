## Evidence

### Asy’ari 2019

Asy’ari, M., Ikhsan, M., & Muhali. (2019). The Effectiveness of Inquiry Learning Model in Improving Prospective Teachers’ Metacognition Knowledge and Metacognition Awareness. International Journal of Instruction, 12(2), 455-470. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12229a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon test on Group A's MAI pretest-posttest data (n = 30, non-normal) gave z = -4.785, p = .000; Table 5 reports paired t-tests for Groups B and C (t = 51.427 and 84.926, p = .000). The tests are on the overall MAI score, not on its eight aspects. The design is one-group pretest-posttest in each of three classes (n = 30 each) with no control class, which the authors call weak-experimental, so the gains cannot be attributed to inquiry learning rather than to testing, maturation or the course itself. Only test statistics printed, no effect size.

> "Table 4 shows that the Z value is of -4,785 within the level of significance value of p < .05. The result indicates that inquiry learning model has given an impact towards metacognition awareness of subjects in Group A."

### Asy’ari 2019 (2)

Asy’ari, M., Ikhsan, M., & Muhali. (2019). The Effectiveness of Inquiry Learning Model in Improving Prospective Teachers’ Metacognition Knowledge and Metacognition Awareness. International Journal of Instruction, 12(2), 455-470. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12229a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive n-gain analysis of MAI scores across eight aspects (declarative, procedural, conditional knowledge, planning, information management, monitoring, debugging, evaluating) in the three groups; the article reports overall moderate n-gains of 0.68, 0.60 and 0.60. By aspect (Table 3), Group A ranged 0.66–0.71, with declarative knowledge at 0.71 (high under the article's > 0.70 threshold) and conditional knowledge at 0.70; Groups B and C ranged 0.58–0.62. These are descriptive pre-post gains with no control class and no per-aspect significance test.

> "Table 3 shows scores of prospective teachers’ metacognition awareness in pre-test and post-test based on MAI during learning fluid in three experimental groups, in which all group members records increase at moderate level of n -gain (0.68; 0.60; and 0.60) after engaged in learning using inquiry learning model."
