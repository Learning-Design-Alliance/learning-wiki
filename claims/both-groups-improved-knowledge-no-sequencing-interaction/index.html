Evidence¶

Sabrina Ludwig 2026¶

Sabrina Ludwig, Andreas Rausch, Michelle Taub. (2026). Effects of instructional design, instructional preferences, and cognitive load on problem solving and knowledge acquisition in a computer-based office simulation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102255

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed ANOVA on pre-post gain scores (12 items per test; KR-20 of .57 pre-test and .50 post-test). Both groups improved; the conceptual knowledge between-group effect reached p = .05 with partial eta-squared = .049, and "An interaction effect was also present but was not significant".

"A mixed ANOVA reveals significant differences between pre- and post-tests within the groups meaning that both groups have a positive learning outcome through the applied treatments (learning material)."
