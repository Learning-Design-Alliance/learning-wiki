Evidence¶

Chen 2026¶

Chen, S., Wang, B.X., Cheung, J.C.-W. et al. (2026). Lab perceptual training and robot-assisted training in improving speech prosody of autistic children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00425-7

q2 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA across the four groups on baseline HKCOLAS narrative scores with Tukey HSD post-hoc tests; no significant group effects on age, IQ or vocabulary. No effect size printed.

"The analyses revealed a significant difference among the the groups on the narrative scores (F(3,88)=3.859, p<0.05). Post-hoc comparisons using Tukey's HSD indicated that only the mean narrative score for the ASD robot-assisted training group (Mean=76.34, SD=23.86) was significantly different from the TD group (Mean=99.56, SD=19.56)."
