## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yan Z 2026

Yan Z, Fang Z and Zou W (2026). The association between generative AI use and university students' critical thinking: a moderated mediation model of GenAI feedback literacy and reflective thinking. Front. Psychol. 17:1939286. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1939286

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM path estimate from a cross-sectional survey of 421 Chinese undergraduates using validated self-report scales. The article reports the total effect "was also significant (β = 0.257, t = 4.864, p < 0.001)". Zero-order correlation between GenAI use and critical thinking was r = 0.244 (p < 0.001).

> "The total effect of GenAI use on critical thinking, equivalent to its estimate when modelled without the mediator, was also significant (β = 0.257, t = 4.864, p < 0.001), supporting H1."

### Yan Z 2026 (2)

Yan Z, Fang Z and Zou W (2026). The association between generative AI use and university students' critical thinking: a moderated mediation model of GenAI feedback literacy and reflective thinking. Front. Psychol. 17:1939286. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1939286

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive correlation analysis (Table 1, N = 421). The article reports the four variables "were significantly and positively inter-correlated (all p < 0.001)", with GenAI use most strongly correlated with feedback literacy (r = 0.451).

> "GenAI use, GenAI feedback literacy, critical thinking and reflective thinking were significantly and positively inter-correlated (all p < 0.001)."
