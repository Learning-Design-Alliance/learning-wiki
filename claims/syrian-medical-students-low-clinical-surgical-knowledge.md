### Ataya J 2026

Ataya J, Ismail A, Kousa A, Shahoud Z, Alhalbi R, Ghanem R, Ranjous Y, Zaitouna M. (2026). Assessing theoretical knowledge related to surgical and clinical skills in Syrian medical students: A cross-sectional study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000051241

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 500 Syrian medical students and recent graduates using a 20-question MCQ instrument. The mean score was "8.78 (SD 2.69) out of 20", with 77.0% intermediate and only 2.8% excellent knowledge.

> "The mean total knowledge score was 8.78 (SD 2.69) out of 20 (range, 2–16). Overall, 101 participants (20.2%) were classified as having weak knowledge, 385 (77.0%) as intermediate, and 14 (2.8%) as excellent."
