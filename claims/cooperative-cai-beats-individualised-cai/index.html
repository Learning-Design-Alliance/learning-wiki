Evidence¶

Yusuf and Afolabi 2010¶

Mudasiru Olalere YUSUF and Adedeji Olufemi AFOLABI. (2010). EFFECTS OF COMPUTER ASSISTED INSTRUCTION (CAI) ON SECONDARY SCHOOL STUDENTS' PERFORMANCE IN BIOLOGY. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 9(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ875764

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Scheffe post hoc comparison within the same quasi-experimental ANCOVA study: the CCAI group (four students per computer) scored significantly higher than the ICAI group (one student per computer), "in favour of experimental group II". No effect size printed.

"there was significant difference in the post test mean scores of students exposed to ICAI (X=17.8750) and those exposed to CCAI (X =20.0500) in favour of experimental group II, that is those exposed to cooperative computer assisted instruction."
