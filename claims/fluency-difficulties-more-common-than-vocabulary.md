## Evidence

### Clemens 2017

Clemens, N. H., Simmons, D., Simmons, L. E., Wang, H., & Kwok, O. (2017). The Prevalence of Reading Fluency and Vocabulary Difficulties Among Adolescents Struggling With Reading Comprehension. Journal of Psychoeducational Assessment, 35(8), 785-798. https://doi.org/10.1177/0734282916662120

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Class sizes from the four-class LCA of the 180 struggling comprehenders: low fluency/average vocabulary n = 41 (23%) versus average fluency/low vocabulary n = 28 (16%). Combined with the 80% fluency-deficit figure, fluency difficulties were more common than vocabulary difficulties.

> "Students with below-average fluency but average vocabulary represented the next largest subgroup (n = 41, 23%), followed by students with average fluency but below-average vocabulary (n = 28, 16%)."
