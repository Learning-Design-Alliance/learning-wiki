# The four aggregated non-hybrid training groups did not differ on the dependent variables of the analyses

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Statistical analyses showed that the four non-hybrid training groups (conducting experiments, text highlighting, reading motivation variants) did not differ on the dependent variables, justifying their aggregation into one comparison group. [→ Stebner 2022](#stebner-2022)

## Evidence

### Stebner 2022

Stebner, F., Schuster, C., Weber, X.-L., Greiff, S., Leutner, D., & Wirth, J. (2022). Transfer of metacognitive skills in self-regulated learning: effects on strategy application and content knowledge acquisition. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 715-744. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09322-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the design and training section of the training study, the authors report a multivariate test across the four aggregated non-hybrid groups, finding no difference on the dependent variables (λ = 0.69, F(24, 244) = 1.37, p = .124). No effect size is printed for this test.

> "Furthermore, statistical analyses showed that these four non-hybrid training groups did not differ on the dependent variables of the present analyses, λ = 0.69, F(24, 244) = 1.37, p = .124."
