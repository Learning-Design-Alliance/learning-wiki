Observational analysis of ECoach gradebook midterm exam data from Physics I students (approximately 6,000 enrollments across four assessment styles). Students' letter grades and difficulty-normalized quintiles were tracked between consecutive midterm exams via transition matrices; "43% of students earn a lower letter grade" on the next exam. Quintile transitions were more uniform (29-40% per outcome).

> "Overall, we find 43% of students earn a lower letter grade on their subsequent midterm exam, 22% earn a higher letter grade on their subsequent midterm exam, and 35% earn the same letter grade on their subsequent midterm exam."
