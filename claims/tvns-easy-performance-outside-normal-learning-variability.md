## Evidence

### Llanos 2020

Llanos, F., McHaney, J. R., Schuerman, W. L., Yi, H. G., Leonard, M. K. and Chandrasekaran, B. (2020). Non-invasive peripheral nerve stimulation selectively enhances speech category learning in adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0070-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Non-parametric Monte Carlo analysis sampling one thousand twelve-subject sub-populations from the Aggregate dataset of 678 prior learners, using each proportion as a p-value. The tVNS-easy group's improvement was outside normal variability; the other groups were within it.

> "While the performances of the tVNS-hard and Control groups were well represented in the Aggregate dataset ( p = 0.46 and 0.51, respectively), the performance of the tVNS-easy was not ( p = 0.019; Fig. 2c)."
