## Evidence

### Pettit RK 2015

Pettit RK, McCoy L, Kinney M, Schwartz FN. (2015). Student perceptions of gamified audience response system interactions in large group lectures and via lecture capture technology. BMC Medical Education 15:92. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0373-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Single-choice survey item asking the 91 respondents in which TP environment they learned best. The article reports "53 % of the students selected individual competition, one clicker per student" and 33% team competition with one clicker per student; the authors link the high clicker-engagement ratings to a preference for holding clickers during team play.

> "53 % of the students selected individual competition, one clicker per student; 33 % selected team competition, one clicker per student; 9 % selected team competition, one clicker per team; and 5 % selected no competition."
