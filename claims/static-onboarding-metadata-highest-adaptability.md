### Bhavishya Tarun 2025

Bhavishya Tarun, Haoze Du, Dinesh Kannan, Edward F. Gehringer. (2025). Human-in-the-Loop Systems for Adaptive Learning Using Generative AI. [doi:10.1109/fie63693.2025.11328658](https://doi.org/10.1109/fie63693.2025.11328658)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative evaluation of four parallel AI pipelines over approximately 200 conversational turns in Object-Oriented Design, scored 1–10 by a GPT-based evaluator. The Personalized pipeline "peaks at 5.43" on Adaptability, the highest of the four.

> "Adaptability varies most dramatically: it peaks at 5.43 for the static “Personalized” pipeline, drops to 4.55 when live feedback tags are added, sits at 4.12 for “RAG,” and reaches its lowest point of 2.78 for the untuned “LLM” model."
