## Evidence

### Zhi Y 2026

Zhi Y, Yang W and Huang K (2026) Modeling and measuring graduate students' generative AI literacy: a study based on Marzano's taxonomy. Front. Psychol. 17:1883978. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1883978

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative grounded theory study interviewing 14 professors from Double First-Class universities. Selective coding of 15 main categories, derived from 96 initial concepts condensed from 329 raw labels, produced the five functional domains; the authors state the formation was "entirely driven by the logical relationships among the categories".

> "five higher-order aggregations were identified, corresponding to the five functional domains: cognitive foundation, operational skills, higher-order thinking, metacognitive reflection, and ethical responsibility."
