## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Ease of use was significantly greater for VR than PC in Experiment 1B (d = .22) and Experiment 2 (d = .62), but not in Experiment 1A. [→ Karen B. Schloss 2021](#karen-b-schloss-2021)

## Evidence

### Karen B. Schloss 2021

Karen B. Schloss, Melissa A. Schoenlein, Ross Tredinnick, Simon Smith, Nathaniel Miller, Chris Racey, Christian Castro, and Bas Rokers. (2021). The UW Virtual Brain Project: An immersive approach to teaching functional neuroanatomy. Preprint. [doi:10.1037/tps0000281](https://doi.org/10.1037/tps0000281)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In Experiment 1B's experience questionnaire (114 of 120 participants completing), "Ease of use was also signiﬁcantly greater for VR than PC" (d = .22); Experiment 2 replicated this (d = .62), while Experiment 1A showed no significant difference.

> "Ease of use was also signiﬁcantly greater for VR than PC ( t(113) = 2.39, p = .02, d = .22), which was not signiﬁcant in Exp. 1A (likely due to the smaller sample size in Exp. 1A)."
