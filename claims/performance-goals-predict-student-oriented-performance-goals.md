### Daumiller 2025

Daumiller, M., Gaspard, H., Dickhäuser, O., & Dresel, M. (2025). Bridging teacher motivation and instruction: Relevance of student‐oriented goals for teaching alongside personal achievement goals and self‐efficacy. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S98–S114. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12776

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-level path modelling at the teacher level in a sample of 70 secondary mathematics teachers. The article reports personal performance goals were "positively linked to student‐oriented performance goals" between teachers.

> "The two‐level path modelling confirmed that personal performance goals were positively linked to student‐oriented performance goals: teachers who reported stronger personal performance goals than other teachers also tended to report stronger student‐oriented performance goals than other teachers."
