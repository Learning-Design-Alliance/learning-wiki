`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Completion monitoring among the 22 elective participants during the December 2021 to July 2022 period. The article reports "varied levels of engagement across each module" with the printed completion percentages; each module took around 25–45 minutes to complete.

> "There were varied levels of engagement across each module, with 73% (n = 16) of trainees completing the OSA module, 100% (n = 22) completing the asthma module, and 82% (n = 18) of trainees completing the chronic cough module and tracheostomy module."
