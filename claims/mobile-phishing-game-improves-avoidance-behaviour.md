## Evidence

### Nalin Asanka Gamagedara Arachchilage 2015

Nalin Asanka Gamagedara Arachchilage. (2015). User-Centred Security Education: A Game Design to Thwart Phishing Attacks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1511.03459](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1511.03459)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Think-aloud study with a pre- and post-test, 20 participants each taking part for approximately one hour. Paired-samples t-test showed "a statistically signiﬁcant increase in the post-test", with means of 56.00 and 84.00. No standardised effect size was printed.

> "Participants, who played the mobile game, scored 56% in the pre-test and 84% in the post-test. There was a statistically signiﬁcant increase in the post-test ((Pre-test: M= 56.00, SD=17.911 and Post-test: M=84.00, SD=13.139), t(19)= -7.97, p<0.005 (two-tailed))."
