## Evidence

### Burkholder 2022

Burkholder, E. W., Sake, M., & Zhang, J. (2022). Preparation for future active learning. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.09021](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.09021)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In-class prediction activity during the second course section asked students to choose among traditional lectures, active learning with a short lecture and practice, and preparation for future learning. The article reports the pattern descriptively: "They overwhelmingly thought that active learning was more effective than lecture." No statistics are printed.

> "They overwhelmingly thought that active learning was more effective than lecture but thought that having the ideas explained to them first would be more effective."
