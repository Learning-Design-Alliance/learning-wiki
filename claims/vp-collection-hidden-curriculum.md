## Evidence

### Urresti-Gundlach M. 2017

Urresti-Gundlach M., Tolks D., Kiessling C., Wagner-Menghin M., Härtl A., Hege I. (2017). Do virtual patients prepare medical students for the real world? Development and application of a framework to compare a virtual patient collection with population data. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1013-1

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Author interpretation drawing on Turbes et al.'s finding that case example demographics can contradict a formal curriculum. The article states that "authors and curriculum managers should be aware of the “hidden curriculum"" in unrepresentative VP collections.

> "Additionally, authors and curriculum managers should be aware of the “hidden curriculum” and the possibility of conveying unintended messages by creating unrepresentative VP collections as described by Turbes et al. [16]."
