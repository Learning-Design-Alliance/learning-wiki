## Evidence

### Chen Lulin 2026

Chen Lulin, Liang Danyan, Zhu Yuhuan, Huang Weihong, Li Chenglin, Liang Xiong, Liang Yutao, Lu Yunyun, Gan Yongjiang, Zhong Yao. (2026). The effect of web-based learning with peer-facilitated discussion on nursing students’ knowledge and practices in work-related biological exposure: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09333-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Six-month follow-up survey of 251 experimental and 259 control nursing students. Exposure experience and reporting compliance were self-reported via two questions. Chi-square tests compared groups (χ² = 9.739, p = 0.002 for exposure; χ² = 4.227, p = 0.040 for reporting).

> "The follow-up survey revealed that 19 nursing students (7.57%) in the experimental group and 43 (16.60%) in the control group reported an experience of work-related biological exposure (p < 0.05)."
