`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Structurally-defined bilateral VS ROI mixed-model ANOVA in the same 304-subject sample, testing load effects on COR>INCOR signal. Found "greater VS activation seen in association with greater load" [F(2,528)=4.80; p=0.0086].

> "In the bilateral ventral striatum, there was a significant effect of working memory load on the correct versus incorrect response, with greater VS activation seen in association with greater load [Figure 3A; F (2, 528)=4.80; p=0.0086]."
