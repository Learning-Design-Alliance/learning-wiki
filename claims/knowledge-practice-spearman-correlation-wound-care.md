`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rank correlation analysis of continuous KAP scores among 178 respondents; the article reports "ρ = 0.398, p < 0.001" for knowledge–practice and "ρ = 0.205, p = 0.006" for attitude–practice.

> "Spearman rank correlation analysis indicated a moderate, statistically significant positive monotonic relationship between Knowledge scores and Practice scores (ρ = 0.398, p < 0.001), and a weak positive correlation between Attitude scores and Practice scores (ρ = 0.205, p = 0.006)."
