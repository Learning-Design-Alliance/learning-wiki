### Yongliang Wang & Yaobo Wang 2026

Yongliang Wang & Yaobo Wang. (2026). Explaining learning engagement in AI-assisted learning through teacher support and achievement goals: insights from goal-setting theory. Humanities and Social Sciences Communications 13:1215. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-07636-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis used bootstrapping with 5000 resamples and 95% bias-corrected CIs. Indirect effects via mastery-approach (β = 0.135, CI 0.096–0.187) and performance-approach (β = 0.121, CI 0.087–0.169) were significant at p < 0.001; the avoidance-goal pathways had CIs including zero.

> "the indirect effects of teacher support on engagement through mastery-approach and performance-approach goals were statistically significant, as their 95% CIs did not include zero. In contrast, the mediating effects via mastery-avoidance and performance-avoidance goals were nonsignificant."
