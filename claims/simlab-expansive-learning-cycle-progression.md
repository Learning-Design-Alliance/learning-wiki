# In a theory-guided in-situ simulation programme, an interprofessional primary care team progressed through the expansive learning cycle from questioning current practice to consolidating new practices

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants in the SimLab intervention collectively mapped a learning process from questioning their current emergency care practice through to consolidation and generalisation of new practices. [→ O'Hare 2025](#ohare-2025)

## Evidence

### O'Hare 2025

O'Hare, S., Kajamaa, A., Conn, R., & Gormley, G. (2025). SimLab: an intervention to promote expansive learning and organisational change in team-based emergency care simulation. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09734-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case-study analysis of 157 minutes of audio-recorded group discussions from four SimLab sessions, coded against the expansive learning cycle. The authors report that "qualitative changes occur in the entire activity system" as contradictions were resolved.

> "The identification and resolution of contradictions have the potential to stimulate the progressing of the expansive learning actions, participants map through the expansive learning process and qualitative changes occur in the entire activity system."
