### Michael J. Obsniuk 2015

Michael J. Obsniuk, Paul W. Irving, and Marcos D. Caballero. (2015). A Case Study: Novel Group Interactions through Introductory Computational Physics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.055](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.055)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysts' temporal segmentation of the group's successful physics debugging over the 2-hour class period. The authors report they "identified these two bounding phases at around (60 ± 5) min into the problem" — recognition at about 55 minutes and resolution at about 65 minutes — and focused on the crucial moments surrounding the final modifications in between.

> "We identiﬁed these two bounding phases at around (60 ± 5) min into the problem, and further examined the process of debugging in-between."
