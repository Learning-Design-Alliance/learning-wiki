## Evidence

### Karaci 2018

Karaci, A., Akyuz, H. I., Bilgici, G., & Arici, N. (2018). Effects of Web-based Intelligent Tutoring Systems on Academic Achievement and Retention. International Journal of Computer Applications, 181(16). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED588768

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of post-test scores sorted by pre-test scores in the same 80-student controlled experiment. Corrected means were 20.685 (experimental) and 16.238 (control), and the group effect was significant at "F(1 -77) = 35.746, p < 0.05".

> "A statistically significant relationship was found between the means of the sorted post -test scores of the experimental and control groups (F(1 -77) = 35.746, p < 0.05)."
