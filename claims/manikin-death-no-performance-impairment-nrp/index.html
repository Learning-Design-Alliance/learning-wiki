Evidence¶

Apara Teleireoluwa 2024¶

Apara Teleireoluwa, Hogan Tom, Peterson Jennifer L H. (2024). Death of the paediatric manikin: a scoping review. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2024-002941

q2 · i? · review · r3

Secondary outcome of the Lizotte 2017 randomised trial reported in the review: participants randomised to scenario order, performance scored against the NRP algorithm. The review reports "no difference between the groups" with the printed medians and p-value.

"Additionally, objective measures of performance using the NRP algorithm showed no difference between the groups (median performance score 80% for survival scenario and 82% for death scenario regardless of the scenario order (p=0.99))."
