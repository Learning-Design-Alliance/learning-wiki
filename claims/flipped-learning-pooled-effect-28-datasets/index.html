Evidence¶

Aydin 2021¶

Aydin, M., Okmen, B., Sahin, S., & Kilic, A. (2021). The Meta-Analysis of the Studies About the Effects of Flipped Learning on Students' Achievement. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 22(1), Article 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1283687

q3 · i?

Findings from the random effects model analysis of 28 datasets from 25 studies; the text reports the relationship between success and flipped learning is positive in all 28 datasets and statistically significant in 20 of them (p<0.05).

"According to the fixed effects model, the value of effect sizes between the flipped learning model and success is 1.068, while it is 0.895 according to the random effects model. Both effect sizes are positive, at high levels, and statistically significant (p<0.05)."
