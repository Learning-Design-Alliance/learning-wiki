## Evidence

### Rozek 2016

Rozek, Svoboda, Harackiewicz, Hulleman, & Hyde. (2016). Utility-value intervention with parents increases students' STEM preparation and career pursuit. PNAS. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1607386114

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal SEM linking 11th/12th-grade course-taking to outcomes measured 5 y later; all four paths were significant. The link is correlational — the authors state they "cannot infer a causal effect of the intervention on these outcomes."

> "High-school mathematics and science course-taking was a significant predictor of students 'college STEM course-taking (z = 3.77, β = 0.34, P < 0.01), the likelihood that students 'desired a STEM major ( z = 2.82, β = 0.29, P < 0.01), perceived STEM value ( z = 2.29, β = 0.22, P = 0.02), and STEM career aspirations (z = 3.13, β = 0.30, P < 0.01)."
