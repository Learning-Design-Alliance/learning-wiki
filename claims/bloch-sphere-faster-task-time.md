## Evidence

### Linda Qerimi 2025

Linda Qerimi, Sarah Malone, Eva Rexigel, Jochen Kuhn and Stefan Küchemann. (2025). Comparing Visual Qubit Representations in Quantum Education: The Bloch sphere Enhances Task Efficiency. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.21721](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.21721)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of task time in the same study (four application-oriented tasks after excluding the phase gate task). Mean task time was 11.40 s (SD = 3.95) in the Bloch sphere group versus 18.20 s (SD = 7.10) in the Quantum Bead group; "a highly significant difference was found for the response time (W = 882, p < .001, r = .56)".

> "However, a highly significant difference was found for the response time (W = 882, p < .001, r = .56), with the group with the Bloch sphere completing the tasks significantly faster."

### Linda Qerimi 2025 (2)

Linda Qerimi, Sarah Malone, Eva Rexigel, Jochen Kuhn and Stefan Küchemann. (2025). Comparing Visual Qubit Representations in Quantum Education: The Bloch sphere Enhances Task Efficiency. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2507.21721](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.21721)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Task accuracy did not differ significantly between groups (Quantum Bead M = 2.99, SD = 1.09; Bloch sphere M = 3.27, SD = 1.00); the speed advantage was not accompanied by an accuracy difference, and no effect size was printed for accuracy.

> "No significant difference was found between the two representations for re- sponse accuracy ( W = 2906 .5, p = .090)."
