Interpretive review reporting Ayene et al.'s (2011) four categories of student depictions of the Heisenberg uncertainty principle, where the fourth category is that "uncertainty is correctly seen as an intrinsic property of quantum systems". Similar inconsistent interpretations are reported independently of course approach, including wave approach and sum-over-paths teaching.
