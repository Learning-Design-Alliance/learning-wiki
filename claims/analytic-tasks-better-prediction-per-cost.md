### E. L. Thorndike and L. M. Terman 1921

E. L. Thorndike and L. M. Terman. (1921). Intelligence and Its Measurement. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://archive.org/search?query=Journal+of+Educational+Psychology+1921

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Thorndike's symposium essay asserts, as the author's judgment rather than a reported study, that analytic and organizing tasks "seem to give much better results per dollar or hour of cost", and that they perhaps sum up simpler associative processes; a straightforward information test is also valuable.

> "tasks which require efficiency in analyzing a situation into elements, selecting and weighting elements to fit a problem and organization or thinking many things together seem to give much better results per dollar or hour of cost."
