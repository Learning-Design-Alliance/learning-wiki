### Chanal J 2020

Chanal J, Paumier D. (2020). The school-subject-specificity hypothesis: Implication in the relationship with grades. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0230103

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations between situational-level regulation constructs and end-of-year grades in four school subjects (Table 3); the article reports "11 (92%) are significant" for autonomous motivation versus 8 of 16 for controlled motivation. Mean convergent correlations printed include r = .232 for intrinsic-stimulation.

> "In total, on the 12 correlations between autonomous motivation and school grades, 11 (92%) are significant. For controlled motivation, on the 16 correlations between controlled motivation and students’ grades, only 8 (50%) are significant, and only 2 are in line with SDT theoretical predictions."
