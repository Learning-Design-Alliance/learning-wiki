## Evidence

### Pechstein 1917

Pechstein, L. A. (1917). Alleged Elements of Waste in Learning a Motor Problem by the "Part" Method. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0071944

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Groups were taught all the motor units as for part learning but given no connecting trials, then retrained on unit I; the relearning expenditure measured the retro-active effect of learning units II, III, and IV. The groups "displayed almost perfect control over unit I."

> "But the groups displayed almost perfect control over unit I. When retrained upon the remaining units, the identical retention of skill was shown."
