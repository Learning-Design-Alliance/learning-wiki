## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hahn Henry 2017

Hahn Henry, Kropp Peter, Kirschstein Timo, Rücker Gernot, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2017). Test anxiety in medical school is unrelated to academic performance but correlates with an effort/reward imbalance. PLoS ONE 12(2): e0171220. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171220

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal repeated-measures assessment in 48 completing second-year medical students, analyzed with Friedman tests (nonparametric repeated measures ANOVA) across four time points; the study found anxiety "increased significantly before the written and even more so before the oral exam" (Fig 1).

> "the self-reported anxiety levels were lowest at the beginning of the term, increased significantly before the written and even more so before the oral exam"
