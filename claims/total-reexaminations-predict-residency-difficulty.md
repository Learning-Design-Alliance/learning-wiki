## Evidence

### O'Neill Lotte Dyhrberg 2014

O'Neill Lotte Dyhrberg, Norberg Karen, Thomsen Maria, Jensen Rune Dall, Brøndt Signe Gjedde, Charles Peder, Mortensen Lene Stouby, Christensen Mette Krogh. (2014). Residents in difficulty—just slower learners? a case–control study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0276-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate conditional logistic regression in a cumulative incidence matched case–control study of 89 cases and 343 controls, matched on graduation year. The article reports total re-examinations as one of "the only significant univariate predictors" of case status; Table 3 prints OR 1.06 (1.00–1.13), p = 0.036.

> "The total number of re-examinations during medical school and the time it took to complete medical school were the only significant univariate predictors of being a resident experiencing deceleration, transferral or dropout (Table 3)."
