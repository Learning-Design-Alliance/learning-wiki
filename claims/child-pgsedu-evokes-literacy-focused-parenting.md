### Austerberry 2026

Austerberry, C., Zayats, T., Ronald, A., Corfield, E., Smajlagic, D., Havdahl, A., Andreassen, O. A., Magnus, P., Njølstad, P. R., Bekkhus, M., & Fearon, P. (2026). Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 67(2), 158–170. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70025

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Trio structural equation model in a longitudinal population-based pregnancy cohort of 83,627 trios, controlling for both parents' PGSedu. The effect of mothers' but not fathers' PGSedu on literacy-focused parenting was significant (β = −0.05, p < .001), and "this significant association was negative".

> "in the trio model (Figure 1D) children's PGSedu were significantly associated with maternal cognitive stimulation (β = 0.03, 95% CI [4 × 10−3, 0.05], p = .021)"
