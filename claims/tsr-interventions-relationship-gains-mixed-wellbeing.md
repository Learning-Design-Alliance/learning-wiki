### Rhoda Lai 2026

Rhoda Lai, Jennifer Southam, Luella Mageean, Sue Roffey, Kelly-Ann Allen. (2026). Associations Between Teacher-Student Relationship Quality and Middle and Secondary School Teachers' Wellbeing: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10107-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's summary of the Lemberger-Truelove et al. (2023) trial (8 middle school teachers, 149 students) reports TSR ratings improved after the intervention, while "decreases in teacher stress were not significantly related to TSR change"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Following a school counsellor consultation intervention, teacher and student ratings of TSRs improved, but decreases in teacher stress were not significantly related to TSR change."

### Rhoda Lai 2026 (2)

Rhoda Lai, Jennifer Southam, Luella Mageean, Sue Roffey, Kelly-Ann Allen. (2026). Associations Between Teacher-Student Relationship Quality and Middle and Secondary School Teachers' Wellbeing: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10107-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's summary of Molina et al. (2022) (30 teachers, 15 middle schools, 15 high schools) reports the intervention group's TSR conflict "decreased significantly more than controls at 3-week follow-up", with a trend of increasing closeness versus decreasing closeness in controls; stress change was a non-statistically significant trend.

> "the intervention group's TSR conflict had decreased significantly more than controls at 3-week follow-up, and showed a trend of increasing TSR closeness"
