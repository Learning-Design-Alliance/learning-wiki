# Illusion of control impairs transfer performance: learners whose expected control matched actual control outperformed learners with an illusion of control

## Evidence

### Pieter Wouters 2008

Pieter Wouters, Fred Paas, Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2008). Observational learning from animated models: effects of studying–practicing alternation and illusion of control on transfer. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9079-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized 2×3 factorial experiment with 90 pre-university pupils assigned to six conditions (15 each). ANOVA on transfer scores (0–12) showed a significant main effect of illusion of control, "F(1, 84) = 4.29, MSE = 6.73, p = 0.041, w2 = 4%", favoring the no-illusion conditions (M=5.46 vs M=4.32).

> "With regard to performance on transfer a main effect of Illusion of control was observed (F(1, 84) = 4.29, MSE = 6.73, p = 0.041, w2 = 4%). Learners in the conditions without illusion of control performed better on transfer than learners in the conditions with illusion of control ( M = 5.46, SD = 2.74 vs. M = 4.32, SD = 2.42)."
