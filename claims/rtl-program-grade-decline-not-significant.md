## Evidence

### Russell Kelly 2019

Russell Kelly, Selci Erin, Black Brian, Cochrane Karis, Ellis Michael. (2019). Academic outcomes following adolescent sport-related concussion or fracture injury: A prospective cohort study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215900

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup comparison of grade change between SRC students who did and did not receive an individually tailored Return-to-Learn program; those on an RTL program showed a decline but the difference was "not statistically significant" for overall (p = 0.052) or core (p = 0.110) grades.

> "While those who were placed on a RTL program experienced a decline in grades compared to those who were not placed on an RTL grogram, the change in overall grades (p = 0.052) or core grades (p = 0.110) was not statistically significant."
