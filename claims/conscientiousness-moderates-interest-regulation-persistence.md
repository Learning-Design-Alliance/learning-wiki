`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attributing to Sansone et al. (1999) an experimental study finding that interest-enhancing strategies predicted persistence on a repetitive task for college students lower in conscientiousness but not for those higher in conscientiousness.

> "Sansone et al. ( 1999) found in an experimental study that college students lower in conscientiousness were more likely to persist on a repetitive task if they engaged in strategies to enhance interest; use of these strategies did not predict persistence for those higher in conscientiousness"

### Carol Sansone 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reporting CONIC-model research (Bareis et al., 2024; Trautwein et al., 2015): interest and conscientiousness showed positive main effects on high school and college students' reported effort, plus the reported interaction favoring lower-conscientiousness students.

> "these factors interacted such that the relationship between interest and reported effort was stronger for students lower in conscientiousness (Bareis et al., 2024; Trautwein et al., 2015)"
