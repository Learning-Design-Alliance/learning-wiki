Ozbey Furkan, Yasa Yasin. (2026). Impact of self-confidence and personality on dental students’ radiographic interpretation skills. BMC Medical Education, 26, 207. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08481-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of diagnostic accuracy across all four confidence levels (Very sure, Sure, Unsure, Very unsure) for 83 students' responses to 11 cases. The article reports accuracy "decreased systematically" from 74.7% (Sure) to 23.8% (Very unsure); Very Sure was 68.9%.

> "As shown in Table 5, accuracy decreased systematically from the “Sure” group (74.7%) to the “Very unsure” group (23.8%)."
