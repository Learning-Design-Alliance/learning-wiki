## Evidence

### Zając-Lamparska Ludmiła 2019

Zając-Lamparska Ludmiła, Wiłkość-Dębczyńska Monika, Wojciechowski Adam, Podhorecka Marta, Polak-Szabela Anna, Warchoł Łukasz, Kędziora-Kornatowska Kornelia, Araszkiewicz Aleksander, Izdebski Paweł. (2019). Effects of virtual reality-based cognitive training in older adults living without and with mild dementia: a pretest–posttest design pilot study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-019-4810-2

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Repeated measures multivariate analysis of variance on the full battery (Table 2). The article prints Wilks' Lambda = 0.613, F = 1.840, p = 0.023, partial eta squared = 0.39 for the training effect in both groups combined.

> "The RM MANOVA with group as a between-subject variable (Table 2) revealed a significant pre-post training difference in cognitive functioning."

### Zając-Lamparska Ludmiła 2019 (2)

Zając-Lamparska Ludmiła, Wiłkość-Dębczyńska Monika, Wojciechowski Adam, Podhorecka Marta, Polak-Szabela Anna, Warchoł Łukasz, Kędziora-Kornatowska Kornelia, Araszkiewicz Aleksander, Izdebski Paweł. (2019). Effects of virtual reality-based cognitive training in older adults living without and with mild dementia: a pretest–posttest design pilot study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-019-4810-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Training-by-group interaction from the same RM MANOVA (Table 2): Wilks' Lambda = 0.652, F = 1.562, p = 0.073, partial eta squared = 0.35. The article interprets the large effect size as suggesting group moderation.

> "The interaction effect was not significant. However, the probability value was only slightly above the assumed value of statistical significance (α = 0.05), while partial eta squared indicated a large effect size [48], which suggested that the group may be a factor moderating the effect of training with regard at least to some cognitive measures."
