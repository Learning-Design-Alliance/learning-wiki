Sikström 2024¶

Sikström, P., Valentini, C., Sivunen, A., & Kärkkäinen, T. (2024). Pedagogical Agents Communicating and Scaffolding Students' Learning : High School Teachers' and Students' Perspectives. Computers and Education, 222, Article 105140. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105140

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

From the technological aspects theme of the thematic analysis. Teachers were "especially interested in applying learning analytics to PAs" to monitor performance and identify students needing assistance; emotion detection was named as needed to respond to students' communication.

"Above all, it is crucial that PAs are easy to use, convenient, and reliable for students and teachers."
