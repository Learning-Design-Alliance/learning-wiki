Zerrouk Deyae 2026¶

Zerrouk Deyae, Kassab Kenza, El Asri Achraf, Zarrouq Btissame. (2026). The relationship between Problematic Social Media Use and academic performance among university students in the MENA region: a systematic review and exploratory meta-analysis. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04354-3

q2 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Exploratory random-effects meta-analysis (JASP) of correlation-based studies, Fisher's Z-transformed. The pooled association was "non-statistically significant" at r = -0.114 with I2 = 76.86%, indicating high between-study variability.

"An exploratory meta-analysis, conducted to examine variability in effect sizes, yielded a negative, non-statistically significant pooled association (r = -0.114, 95% CI: -0.283 to 0.054), with substantial heterogeneity observed across studies (I2 = 76.86%)."
