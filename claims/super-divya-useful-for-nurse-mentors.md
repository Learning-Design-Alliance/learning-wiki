Relevance-for-NM theme from the interview study. Participants felt the Hindi-language, animated format would help Nurse Mentors, with one noting mentors could "go home and watch the video and revise" before a simulation.

> "In fact, all NMS but one agreed that Super Divya videos would be useful for NM to learn how to facilitate simulations. They believed the videos would be enjoyable, relatable, and easy for them to understand."
