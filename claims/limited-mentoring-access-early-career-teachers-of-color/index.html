In 2018, fewer than half of teachers overall (38%) or of teachers of color (44%) received regular mentoring, and in 2021 early-career teachers of color were less likely than White peers to report receiving mentoring (79% vs. 83%) or induction (71% vs. 74%) in their first year. → Carver-Thomas 2025

Descriptive analysis of national teacher survey data on access to coaching and mentoring by race/ethnicity (Tables 9–10). The report states "fewer than half of teachers overall (38%) or of teachers of color (44%) received regular mentoring" in 2018.

"In 2018, fewer than half of teachers overall (38%) or of teachers of color (44%) received regular mentoring (at least once per month), and even fewer received mentoring weekly (14% and 17%, respectively)."
