Mogali Sreenivasulu Reddy, Yan Qi, Jiang Nan, Teng Peng, Xu Youran, Qian Kaoliang, Lu Ming, Hu Jun. (2025). Case-Based Virtual Reality Simulation for Severe Pelvic Trauma Clinical Skill Training in Medical Students: Design and Pilot Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/59850

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' report of learner feedback from the pilot; the teacher interviews separately noted that the ESP "could not recognize the semantics of the trainer" and lacked language-style variation. No effect size is printed.

> "In our pilot study, most learners reported difficulty in direct communication with the ESP in the virtual environment, including eye and body language, and in discerning the nuances of the real language environment (such as tone and intonation); hence, they did not fully practice effective doctor-patient communication skills."
