## Evidence

### Hughes 2021

Hughes, A., Wade, K. H., Dickson, M., Rice, F., Davies, A., Davies, N. M. & Howe, L. D. (2021). Common health conditions in childhood and adolescence, school absence, and educational attainment: Mendelian randomization study. npj Science of Learning 6:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00080-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-sample Mendelian randomization in ALSPAC (N=6113), the only PGS strong enough to be an instrumental variable. The article states "GCSE points scores were 16.79 lower" per unit BMI z-score, with absences 8.73% greater; no standardised effect size is printed for this IV estimate.

> "Using the BMI PGS as an instrumental variable showed that, for each unit increase in BMI z-score at age 10, GCSE points scores were 16.79 lower (95% CI: −24.38, −9.19) and absences were 8.73% greater (95% CI: 1.82, 16.12)."
