Four-year retention analysis by grade span (figure 3) using state staffing data; Nevada's elementary rate of 39 percent was lower than middle (48 percent) and high school (55 percent) rates, while Arizona and Utah showed different grade-span patterns.

"In Nevada, where the overall four-year principal retention rate was 43 percent, the rate was lower for elementary school principals, at 39 percent, and higher for middle school principals, at 48 percent, and among high school principals, at 55 percent."
