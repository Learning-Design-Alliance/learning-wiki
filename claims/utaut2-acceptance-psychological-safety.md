### Shilling V 2025

Shilling V, Starkings R, Fallowfield L. (2025). Feasibility and acceptability of a virtual learning module for navigating angry conversations in clinical encounters. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06726-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of a modified 18-item UTAUT2 questionnaire completed post-intervention by 20 students. "Participants in this study were positive about VL; 12 of the 18 items had a median and/or mode of maximum 7." The psychological safety item scored 6.45 (1.10), median/mode 7/7. No effect sizes were reported.
