## Evidence

### Martinuk 2010

Martinuk, M. and Ives, J. (2010). Do Prescribed Prompts Prime Sensemaking During Group Problem Solving? Physics Education Research conference proceedings style manuscript, University of British Columbia. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1111.5400](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1111.5400)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

A benchmark was built from a sub-section of one episode in which a well-functioning group (Group #4) raised a question, discussed it, and recorded results; Figure 1 reports frame ratios of W = 53%, PD = 8%, and CD = 38%. This is a single-episode estimate, not a test.

> "Based on this episode, we estimate that a well functioning group could spend as much as 38% of their time on Conceptual Discussion while working on a quantitative context-rich problem."
