Binary coding of title text and thumbnail features across 51 of 52 videos (one thumbnail unavailable), with group-level percentages. Titles and thumbnails showed parallel differences: G3 led in urgency (41.2%) and productivity language (76.5%), G1 in learning imagery (64.0%) and study context (48.0%).

"𝐺1(68.0%) and𝐺2(70.0%) most frequently used learning-oriented languages, whereas𝐺3showed substantially lower use (29.4%). In contrast, urgency language was highest in 𝐺3(41.2%), compared to 𝐺1(20.0%) and 𝐺2(10.0%)."

Related Claims¶

  • Educational YouTube videos about ChatGPT fall into three structurally distinct discourse groups: learning-oriented scaffolding (G1), skill-building retrieval practice (G2), and output/productivity orientation (G3) — related
  • Productivity-oriented ChatGPT videos achieved platform reach comparable to skill-oriented videos despite weaker learning-oriented framing — related
  • Learning-oriented video groups differ significantly from the productivity-oriented group on the primary ENA axis, with large effect sizes — related
