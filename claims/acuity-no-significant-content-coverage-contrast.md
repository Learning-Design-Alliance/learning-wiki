## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chojnacki 2013

Chojnacki, G., Eno, J., Liu, F., Meyers, C., Konstantopoulos, S., Miller, S., & van der Ploeg, A. (2013). Do Interim Assessments Influence Instructional Practice in Year One? Evidence from Indiana Elementary School Teachers. SREE Fall 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563059

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical generalized linear model analysis of instructional logs from the Indiana RCT, with logs nested in students, teachers, and schools. Odds ratios for the seven math content areas ranged from 0.93 (Geometry) to 1.25 (Algebra and Functions), none statistically significant. The article reports "statistically significant contrasts do not emerge in any of the seven content areas."

> "Panel A of Table 2 presents estimates of the treatment control contrast estimated using a three-level adaptation of Equation 1 over all logs; statistically significant contrasts do not emerge in any of the seven content areas."
