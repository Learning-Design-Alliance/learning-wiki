# 1st graders in Amplify Science intervention classrooms significantly outperformed comparison classrooms on science vocabulary in-use (g = 0.46)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Students in intervention classrooms scored significantly higher on the NGSS-aligned science-vocabulary-in-use assessment, an effect of 0.46 (p < .01). [→ Harris 2023](#harris-2023)

## Evidence

### Harris 2023

Harris, C. J., Murphy, R., Feng, M., & Rutstein, D. (2023). Supporting science learning and literacy development together: Initial results from a curriculum study in 1st grade classrooms. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638494

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same multilevel regression analysis of the randomized trial, the science-vocabulary-in-use effect was "statistically significant (p < .01)" with "an effect size of 0.46," which the authors equate to an 18-percentile-point improvement for the average comparison student.

> "The estimated impact on performance on the science-vocabulary-in-use assessment was also statistically significant (p < .01) and corresponds to an effect size of 0.46."
