# Prior UBE experience moderates e-learning knowledge gains but not simulator skill gains

> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants without prior UBE experience showed lower baseline knowledge and less e-learning knowledge improvement than experienced participants. [→ Motov Stefan 2024](#motov-stefan-2024)
`q2 i?` Despite different baseline skills, simulator skill improvement was identical for participants with and without prior UBE experience. [→ Motov Stefan 2024 (2)](#motov-stefan-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Motov Stefan 2024

Motov Stefan, Santander X., Stengel F. C., Mohme M., Schwake M., Zoia C., Butenschoen V. M., Bauer M., Lippa L., Belo D., Kaprovoy S., Lepič M., Stastna D., Drosos E., Spiriev T., Giamundo M., Torregrossa F., Aldea C., Raffa G., Ostendorp C., Bozinov O., Stienen Martin N. (2024). Sequential adaptive e-learning and hands-on simulator training for unilateral biportal endoscopy (UBE) of the lumbar spine - results from an EANS Young Neurosurgeons hands-on course. Acta Neurochirurgica. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-024-06359-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive subgroup comparison within the e-learning feedback sample (n = 8). Novices started lower and improved less than experienced participants; the printed values are descriptive NRS means with no test statistic.

> "The participants without prior experience with UBE indicated lower NRS baseline scores (NRS 2, SD 2 vs. NRS 4, SD 1.4) and less improvement of their knowledge (NRS 6, SD 1.9 vs. NRS 8, SD 0) than the ones with prior experience."

### Motov Stefan 2024 (2)

Motov Stefan, Santander X., Stengel F. C., Mohme M., Schwake M., Zoia C., Butenschoen V. M., Bauer M., Lippa L., Belo D., Kaprovoy S., Lepič M., Stastna D., Drosos E., Spiriev T., Giamundo M., Torregrossa F., Aldea C., Raffa G., Ostendorp C., Bozinov O., Stienen Martin N. (2024). Sequential adaptive e-learning and hands-on simulator training for unilateral biportal endoscopy (UBE) of the lumbar spine - results from an EANS Young Neurosurgeons hands-on course. Acta Neurochirurgica. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00701-024-06359-6

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive subgroup comparison within the simulator course sample (n = 18). Baseline skills differed between experienced and inexperienced participants, yet "the skills improvement in both groups was identical (NRS 7, SD 2)"; no test was reported.

> "Even though baseline skills assessment differed between those who had prior experience with the technique (NRS 6, SD 2.4) and those without (2, SD 1.4), the skills improvement in both groups was identical (NRS 7, SD 2)."
