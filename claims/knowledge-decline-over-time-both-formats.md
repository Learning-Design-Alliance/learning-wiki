### Courteille Olivier 2018

Courteille Olivier, Fahlstedt Madelen, Ho Johnson, Hedman Leif, Fors Uno, von Holst Hans, Felländer-Tsai Li, Möller Hans. (2018). Learning through a virtual patient vs. recorded lecture: a comparison of knowledge retention in a trauma case. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5aa3.ccf2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on knowledge scores in the randomized trial showed a significant time effect, "F (1, 135) = 18.154, p = 0.00", with the decline described as small and occurring in both the VP and lecture groups; Figure 2 plots mean values with 0.95 confidence intervals.

> "As shown in Figure 2, a small but significant decline in knowledge retention over time (time factor from Test 1 to Test 2, F (1, 135) = 18.154, p = 0.00) was observed in both groups."
