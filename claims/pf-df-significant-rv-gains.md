## Evidence

### Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang 2017

Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang. (2017). Comparing the efficacy of digital flashcards versus paper flashcards to improve receptive and productive L2 vocabulary. The EUROCALL Review, Volume 25, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.6964

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test within the PF group (n = 26) in the 12-week study, comparing pre- and post-test receptive vocabulary scores. The article reports a significant improvement, t(25) = 2.81, p = .009, with means rising from "M = 12.65" to 15.19.

> "A paired t-test indicated that the PF group was able to make a significant improvement in RV from the pre-test (M = 12.65, SD = 4.10) to the post-test (M = 15.19, SD = 2.74), t(25) = 2.81, p =.009."

### Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang 2017 (2)

Gilbert Dizon and Daniel Tang. (2017). Comparing the efficacy of digital flashcards versus paper flashcards to improve receptive and productive L2 vocabulary. The EUROCALL Review, Volume 25, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.4995/eurocall.2017.6964

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test within the DF group (n = 26) studying with Quizlet and Cram over 12 weeks. The article reports a significant receptive vocabulary increase, t(25) = 4.15, p = .0003, from "M = 15.38" to 17.85.

> "The DF group also had a significant increase in RV from pre-test (M = 15.38, SD = 3.13) to the post-test (M = 17.85, SD = 2.36), t(25) = 4.15, p = .0003."
