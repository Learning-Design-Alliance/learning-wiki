Zahra 2025¶

Zahra, F. T. (2025). Three Pathways of Student-AI Interaction: Constraint-First Design for Higher-Order Thinking. Manuscript under blind review; framework first presented at EAAI-25, Philadelphia, PA. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00338 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2610.00338

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Descriptive examination (not formally coded) of 57 messages from 10 students in the same course, from an assignment requiring hypotheses, policy implications, and applicative reasoning. The article states the contrast is "not a controlled comparison" and causal claims are unwarranted.

"This contrast is offered as evidence that students can produce Path 3 interactions when an assignment requires them to do so. This study is not a controlled comparison; the assignments differed in content and AI prompt structure, making causal claims unwarranted."
