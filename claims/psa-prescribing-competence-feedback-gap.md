The review cites the EQUIP study of prescribing errors by foundation year 1 trainee doctors as a prompt for the Prescribing Safety Assessment, required since August 2016 in the UK Foundation Programme.

> "an empirical study of prescribing errors made by F1 trainee doctors reported a prescribing error rate of 8.4%"

### Hepburn CY 2026 (2)

Hepburn CY. (2026). Evidence to support integrating feedback best practice for computer‐based assessment in pharmacology education. Br J Clin Pharmacol. https://doi.org/10.1002/bcp.70302

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review observes that educational interventions accompanying the PSA roll-out lacked explicit feedback mechanisms, motivating its argument for evidence-based feedback in computer-based pharmacology assessment.

> "Despite the increase in educational interventions to teach pharmacology, which sat alongside the roll‐out of the PSA, approaches to explicitly address mechanisms for providing feedback to students were often absent"
