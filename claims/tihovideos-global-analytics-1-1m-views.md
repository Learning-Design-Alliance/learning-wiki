### Müller Lina R. 2019

Müller Lina R., Tipold Andrea, Ehlers Jan P., Schaper Elisabeth. (2019). TiHoVideos: veterinary students' utilization of instructional videos on clinical skills. BMC Veterinary Research 15, 326. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-019-2079-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Google Analytics analysis of channel usage data from launch on 26 April 2012 through 31 December 2017. The article reports "Most of the 1,103,732 views for TiHoVideos", device shares, and that "Views are logged for 225 different regions of the world" with Germany at 45.3% and the United States at 9.0%. As of June 2018 the channel had 109 videos, 3160 subscribers and approximately 35,000 views per month.

> "Most of the 1,103,732 views for TiHoVideos occurred between 27 April 2012 and 31 December 2017 using computers (49.2%), followed by mobile phones (38.2%), tablets (11.5%), unknown devices (0.5%), televisions (0.4%) and game consoles (0.1%)."
