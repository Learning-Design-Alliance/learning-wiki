## Evidence

### Kalyva 2026

Kalyva, M., Kavvadia, K., Louca, L., Televantou, I., & Michaelides, M. P. (2026). The “Testing Effect” in Undergraduate Endodontic Education: The Impact of Regular Low Stakes Testing on Learning Outcomes and Student Engagement. International Endodontic Journal, 59(6), 1227–1235. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.70022

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis on the questionnaire subset (n = 44): the three scales were positively inter-correlated but "did not correlate with the two main outcome scores", the total exam score, or the previous course grade. No correlation coefficients with outcomes are printed.

> "However, they did not correlate with the two main outcome scores (final exam score on tested and non‐tested material), the total final exam score, or the previous course grade."
