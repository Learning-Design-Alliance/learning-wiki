## Evidence

### Stahl Aimee E. 2019

Stahl Aimee E., Feigenson Lisa. (2019). Violations of Core Knowledge Shape Early Learning. Topics in Cognitive Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/tops.12389

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Lab experiment in which 11-month-olds saw an expected or surprising solidity or spatiotemporal continuity event, then were taught that the ball had a hidden squeaking property and tested on mapping sound to object. Only the surprising-outcome groups learned.

> "infants who saw the expected outcome of either event failed to learn the new information – as predicted, because we had designed the one-shot-learning task to be hard. In contrast, infants who saw the surprising outcome of either event successfully learned the object's hidden property"
