## Evidence

### Luigi A. E. Degni 2024

Luigi A. E. Degni, Sara Garofalo, Gianluca Finotti, Francesca Starita, Trevor W. Robbins & Giuseppe di Pellegrino. (2024). Sex differences in motivational biases over instrumental actions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00246-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 tested the intensity bias with a 2x2 mixed ANOVA on reaction times (n = 42; reaction times for a sample of 35 due to technical problems); the CS by sex interaction was significant (F1,40 = 8.61; p = 0.01; BF10 = 7.46) with printed effect size ηp2 = 0.18, with "only males showed faster reaction times to the CS+."

> "More specifically, only males showed faster reaction times to the CS + (M = 692.13; SD = 72.1) compared with the CS − (M = 731.47; SD = 92.63), while females did not report differences in reaction times between CS + (M = 743.57; SD = 60.95) and CS − (M = 736.45; SD = 69.4)."
