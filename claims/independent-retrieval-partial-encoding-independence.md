## Evidence

### Yonelinas 2002

Andrew P. Yonelinas. (2002). The Nature of Recollection and Familiarity: A Review of 30 Years of Research. Journal of Memory and Language. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.2002.2864

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of task-dissociation and process-estimation studies reports that "the two processes are independent at retrieval" based on test-phase double dissociations, and partially independent during encoding based on study-phase single dissociations. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Double dissociations produced by experimental manipulations at time of test indicate that the two processes are independent at retrieval, and sin- gle dissociations produced by study manipulations indicate that they are partially independent during encoding."

### Yonelinas 2002 (2)

Andrew P. Yonelinas. (2002). The Nature of Recollection and Familiarity: A Review of 30 Years of Research. Journal of Memory and Language. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.2002.2864

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review anticipates from its exhaustive assessment that the two processes "operate independently under most experimental conditions", while flagging boundary conditions where independence may fail.

> "recollection and familiarity are found to operate independently under most experimental conditions, but there are boundary conditions, outside of which the independence assumption may not hold."
