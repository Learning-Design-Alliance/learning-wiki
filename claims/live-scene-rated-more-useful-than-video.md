## Evidence

### Robles M. J. 2019

Robles M. J., Miralles Ramón, Esperanza Ascension, Riera Mercedes. (2019). Different ways to present clinical cases in a classroom: video projection versus live representation of a simulated clinical scene with actors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1494-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-seminar opinion question (linear scale 0–10) on the usefulness of the scene or video for understanding and treating delirium, compared between the scene group (n = 68) and video group (n = 63). No standardised effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Students of the scene group opined that live scene was very useful for learning with a mean score of 9.04 ± 1.16 (range 0–10), and opinion in the student’s video group scored 8.21 ± 1.22 (p = 0.001)."
