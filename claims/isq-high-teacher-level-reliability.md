## Evidence

### Knol Mariska H. 2016

Knol Mariska H., Dolan Conor V., Mellenbergh Gideon J., van der Maas Han L. J. (2016). Measuring the Quality of University Lectures: Development and Validation of the Instructional Skills Questionnaire (ISQ). PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149163

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Psychometric analysis of ISQ ratings from students nested under 75 professors at a Dutch university, computed via Cronbach's equation applied separately to teacher-level covariance matrices at three occasions. The article reports "a mean alpha of .94 at T1" and comparable means at T2 and T3, with per-scale alphas from .90 to .99.

> "The reliability of the subscales at the teacher level was high, with a mean alpha of .94 at T1, a mean alpha of .97 at T2, and a mean alpha of .96 at T3."
