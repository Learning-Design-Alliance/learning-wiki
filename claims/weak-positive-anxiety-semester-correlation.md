## Evidence

### Zile Inta 2021

Zile Inta, Bite Ieva, Krumina Indra, Folkmanis Valdis, Tzivian Lilian. (2021). Association between Anxiety, Quality of Life and Academic Performance of the Final-Year-Students in Latvia. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18115784

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in the 287-student cohort linking baseline anxiety to semester marks; the printed effect sizes were "rs from 0.24 to 0.31", all significant at p < 0.01 but weak.

> "The level of anxiety significantly positively correlated with semester academic achievement in all subject matters in the first semester (p < 0.01) assuming better academic performance for more anxious children. However, all correlation coefficients in these cases were weak (rs from 0.24 to 0.31)."

### Zile Inta 2021 (2)

Zile Inta, Bite Ieva, Krumina Indra, Folkmanis Valdis, Tzivian Lilian. (2021). Association between Anxiety, Quality of Life and Academic Performance of the Final-Year-Students in Latvia. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18115784

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up Spearman correlations between follow-up anxiety and state-exam marks; the only significant subject was Latvian language with the printed effect size "rs = 0.18", with no association for foreign language or mathematics.

> "For the final state exam, this weak positive correlation between the level of anxiety and academic achievement was observed only for Latvian language (rs = 0.18; p < 0.01) but not for the foreign language (p = 0.17) and not for mathematics (p = 0.24)"
