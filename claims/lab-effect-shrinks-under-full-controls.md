## Evidence

### DeFeo 2020

DeFeo, D. J., Bibler, A., & Gerken, S. (2020). The Effect of a Paired Lab on Course Completion and Grades in Nonmajors Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 19(3). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-03-0041

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Regression analysis of Table 2 across four outcomes (completion, grade, Pr(A), Pr(A or B)) for 10,793 observations in 291 lecture sections, adding classroom fixed effects then demographic and academic controls. Grade coefficient fell from 0.331 to 0.117, "about one-tenth of a letter grade higher."

> "For the Completed Course outcome, after conditioning on all of the observable characteristics, the estimated differences is 2.5 percentage points. This is about half the size of the unconditional correlation"
