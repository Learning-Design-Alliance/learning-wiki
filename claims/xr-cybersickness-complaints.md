### Mulder Tessa A. 2026

Mulder Tessa A., Jacobs Robin C., Zuurmond Manon S., Qaddoumi Adel, Regeer Madelien V., Dekker Friedo W., Bauer Martijn P., Srámek Alexandr, Langers Alexandra M.J., Hierck Beerend P. (2026). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In this RCT, XR-group participants completed a cybersickness questionnaire; "Moderate cybersickness symptoms were reported by four novices" and three reported severe issues. There was no correlation with ultrasound test performance.

> "Moderate cybersickness symptoms were reported by four novices. Three novices reported severe issues that could be related to cybersickness (fatigue, headache, stomach awareness), though one had pre-existing stomach complaints."
