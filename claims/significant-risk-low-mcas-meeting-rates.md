# Few students identified as significantly below benchmark in grades 1–3 met MCAS expectations 1 or 2 years later, with higher rates for earlier identification

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 11% of students identified in grade 1 met MCAS expectations by grade 3, versus 10% identified in grade 2 (tested grade 4) and 6% identified in grade 3 (tested grade 5). [→ Lemke 2025](#lemke-2025)

## Evidence

### Lemke 2025

Lemke, M., Soo Ping Chow, A., LeBeau, B., & Quesen, S. (2025). Stuck at red: Persistent early literacy gaps and later state test performance. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677637

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linkage of screening data with state-provided MCAS data for just under 6,000 students with MCAS scores 1 year after significant-risk identification and about the same number 2 years later. The brief reports "only 6 percent of those identified in grade 3 and tested in grade 5" met expectations.

> "Eleven percent of students identified in grade 1 met expectations by grade 3, compared with 10 percent of those identified in grade 2 and tested in grade 4 and only 6 percent of those identified in grade 3 and tested in grade 5"
