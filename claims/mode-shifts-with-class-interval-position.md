## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Repositioning five-point class intervals moved the girls' and boys' modes from ten points apart to five points apart, halving the reported sex difference. [→ Frasier 1915](#frasier-1915)

## Evidence

### Frasier 1915

Frasier, George W. (1915). An Inquiry Into the Statistical Basis of a Conclusion Concerning Sex Differences. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Numerical regrouping of the Yerkes monograph data for 53 ten-year-olds: the same interval with a shifted starting point brings the modes nearer together. The article reports the modes are "separated by five points instead of ten as before".

> "We still have two "surprisingly pronounced modes" but they are fifty per cent, closer together; that is, they are now separated by five points instead of ten as before."
