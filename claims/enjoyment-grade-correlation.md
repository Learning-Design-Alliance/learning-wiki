## Evidence

### Kolb U. 2018

Kolb U., Brodeur M., Braithwaite NStJ., and Minocha S. (2018). A Robotic Telescope For University-Level Distance Teaching. RTSRE Proceedings Vol. 1, No. 1. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.02989](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.02989)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis linking survey responses to assessment scores from student records in the OU modules with embedded telescope activities; the article reports the association as a trend, with no effect size printed.

> "Students who agree with the notion that VEs make practical work more enjoyable tend to obtain higher grades."

### Kolb U. 2018 (2)

Kolb U., Brodeur M., Braithwaite NStJ., and Minocha S. (2018). A Robotic Telescope For University-Level Distance Teaching. RTSRE Proceedings Vol. 1, No. 1. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.02989](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.02989)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same correlational survey-and-records analysis; the article reports the reverse association for students doubting remote experiments' value for teaching collaboration, with no effect size printed.

> "Mirroring this, students who replied that REs are not effective for teaching collaboration tend to obtain lower grades on the module."
