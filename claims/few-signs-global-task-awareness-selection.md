## Evidence

### Gonny L. M. Schellings & Hein Broekkamp 2011

Gonny L. M. Schellings & Hein Broekkamp. (2011). Signaling task awareness in think-aloud protocols from students selecting relevant information from text. Metacognition and Learning 6:65–82. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-010-9067-z

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud study of twelve 10th-graders, each performing three selection tasks (36 protocols). Instruction rereading occurred in only 5 of 36 protocols; the article reports that reflections about selection goals were not often found and there was no dependence between task instruction and global-level reflection.

> "In 5 of the 36 protocols the instruction was reread; in one protocol the instruction was reread twice."
