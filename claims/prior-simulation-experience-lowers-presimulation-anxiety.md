## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-simulation anxiety was significantly lower before the second simulation (M = 56.86, SD = 11.36) than before the first (M = 61.41, SD = 12.01), t = 2.78, p < 0.01; pre-simulation confidence was significantly higher before the second (M = 29.76, SD = 5.81) than the first (M = 26.59, SD = 6.93), t = -3.57, p < 0.01. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-experience comparison within the same 37 students, comparing pre-scores of the first and second simulation sessions using paired t-tests. The article reports pre-simulation anxiety "significantly lower in second simulation" and pre-simulation confidence "significantly higher in the second simulation" (t = -3.57, p<0.01).
