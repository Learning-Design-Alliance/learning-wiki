### Xiomara Gonzalez 2026

Xiomara Gonzalez, Gabriella Coloyan Fleming, Andrew Katz, Maya Denton, Jessica Deters. (2026). LLM-assisted sentiment analysis for integrated computational and qualitative mixed methods education research: A case study of students' written reflection assignments. https://www.google.com/search?q=LLM-assisted+sentiment+analysis+for+integrated+computational+and+qualitative+mixed+methods+education+research

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative observation from Stage 4 discrepancy analysis. The authors give the example of a student statement about experience in Tagalog and Spanish, which the model labeled positive while "the human coder coded this excerpt as neutral, as it reflects prior experience rather than an evaluative stance".

> "The LLM demonstrated a tendency to assign affective labels (e.g., positive or negative) to statements the human coder interpreted as neutral or descriptive in nature."
