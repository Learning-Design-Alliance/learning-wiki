### Gull Ambreen 2026

Gull Ambreen, Yasmin Rahila, Mahboob Usman, Golnik Karl C., Moin Muhammad. (2026). Validation of the ICO-OCEX tool for competency-based assessment in ophthalmology residency programs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09154-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Internal consistency was evaluated across 402 forms from 134 raters. 'Subscale-level analysis showed Cronbach's alpha values ranging from 0.793 to 0.949 across sets A, B, and C, indicating acceptable to excellent reliability within each domain.' Corrected item-to-total correlations exceeded 0.2 for all items, supporting adequate alignment with their respective constructs.

> "The tool's internal consistency was high, with a Cronbach's alpha of 0.995."
