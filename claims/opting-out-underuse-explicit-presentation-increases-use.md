## Evidence

### Yael Sidi · Rakefet Ackerman 2024

Yael Sidi · Rakefet Ackerman. (2024). Opting Out as an Untapped Resource in Instructional Design: Review and Implications. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09879-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Weber and Perfect (2012), an eyewitness study of mock crime video reports comparing spontaneous open-ended reporting with an explicit "don't know" option: "only 2.2% of the participants in the spontaneous condition opted out, compared to 19.3%" with the explicit option.

> "They found that only 2.2% of the participants in the spontaneous condition opted out, compared to 19.3% who opted out when this option was explicitly presented."
