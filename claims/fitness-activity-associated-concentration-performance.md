## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Self-reported physical activity, grip strength, and VO2 max each independently and positively predicted post-intervention concentration performance. [→ Gall S 2018](#gall-s-2018)

## Evidence

### Gall S 2018

Gall S, Adams L, Joubert N, Ludyga S, Müller I, Nqweniso S, Pühse U, du Randt R, Seelig H, Smith D, Steinmann P, Utzinger J, Walter C, Gerber M. (2018). Effect of a 20-week physical activity intervention on selective attention and academic performance in children living in disadvantaged neighborhoods: A cluster randomized control trial. PLoS ONE 13(11): e0206908. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206908

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-sectional associations from Model 1 of the mixed linear regression: CP increased by 1.27 per self-reported activity score point, 1.16 per kg grip strength, and 0.77 per VO2 max unit. These are associations, not intervention effects.

> "Furthermore, physically active and fit children tend to have better concentration performance (CP) than their less fit peers (self-reported activity; p<0.016, grip strength; p<0.009, VO2 max p>0.021)"
