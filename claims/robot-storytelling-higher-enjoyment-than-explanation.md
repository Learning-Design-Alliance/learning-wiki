# Students exposed to robot storytelling report significantly higher enjoyment than students exposed to robot explanation in a 1-week classroom study

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Year 2-1 (storytelling) students enjoyed the Monday and Tuesday activities significantly more than Year 2-2 (explanation) students. [→ Tozadore 2025](#tozadore-2025)
`q2 i?` The enjoyment difference also appeared in the Thursday language activity, which both groups experienced as storytelling. [→ Tozadore 2025 (2)](#tozadore-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Tozadore 2025

Tozadore, D., Ertug, N., Chaker, Y., & Abderrahim, M. (2025). RoboBuddy in the Classroom: Exploring LLM-Powered Social Robots for Storytelling in Learning and Integration Activities. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16706 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.16706](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2508.16706)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the 1-week classroom study, one class (Year 2-1) received Lecture-to-Storytelling mode and the other (Year 2-2) Robot Lecture Explanation mode; students rated enjoyment on a 5-point Likert scale. The article reports "A significant difference was found" on both days, with the printed W and p-values; no effect size is given.

> "A significant difference was found between the enjoyment of activities with storytelling, performed with student from Year 2-1, and with explanation group, Year 2-2, both on Monday (W=25, p-value=0.033) and on Tuesday (W=86, p-value=0.024)."

### Tozadore 2025 (2)

Tozadore, D., Ertug, N., Chaker, Y., & Abderrahim, M. (2025). RoboBuddy in the Classroom: Exploring LLM-Powered Social Robots for Storytelling in Learning and Integration Activities. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16706. [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.16706](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2508.16706)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the Thursday activity, where both classes did storytelling with words in children's primary languages, the article reports "a significant difference was found" between the classes (W=72, p-value=0.04); no effect size is printed.

> "Again, a significant difference was found (W=72, p-value=0.04) in the language activity on Thursday."
