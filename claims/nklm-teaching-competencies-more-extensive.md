# Teaching and educating competencies are described in much greater extent in the NKLM than in comparable international frameworks

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The NKLM specifies teaching competencies (e.g. adult education methods, assessment, conveying content to patients) in greater quantitative and qualitative detail than comparable frameworks. [→ Hautz SC 2015](#hautz-sc-2015)

## Evidence

### Hautz SC 2015

Hautz SC, Hautz WE, Keller N, Feufel MA, Spies C. (2015). The scholar role in the National Competence Based Catalogues of Learning Objectives for Undergraduate Medical Education (NKLM) compared to other international frameworks. GMS German Medical Science. [doi:10.3205/000224](https://doi.org/10.3205/000224)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the qualitative text analysis of the 13 frameworks, the authors found teaching competences "described in much greater extend both quantitatively as well as qualitatively in the NKLM", contrasting with frameworks requiring only that graduates "recognize the value of teaching and training others".

> "Competences necessary for teaching are described in much greater extend both quantitatively as well as qualitatively in the NKLM than in comparable frameworks."
