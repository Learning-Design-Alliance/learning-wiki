## Evidence

### Saqr Mohammed 2018

Saqr Mohammed, Fors Uno, Tedre Matti. (2018). How the study of online collaborative learning can guide teachers and predict students' performance in a medical course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1126-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kendall's Tau-b correlation analysis (permutation-based, 9999 replicates) of SNA parameters against grades for 35 students in a Surgery course forum. The study reports "in-degree centrality, which was moderately significantly correlated (τb (33) = 0.32, p = 0.01)" with final grade; degree and out-degree were not significant.

> "We found that parameters corresponding to the quantity of interactions (degree and out-degree) did not significantly correlate with student grade except for in-degree centrality, which was moderately significantly correlated (τb (33) = 0.32, p = 0.01)"
