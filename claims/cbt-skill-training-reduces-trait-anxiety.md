## Evidence

### Kaya 2016

Kaya, S., & Avci, R. (2016). Effects of cognitive-behavioral-theory-based skill-training on university students' future anxiety and trait anxiety. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 66, 281-298. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.66.16

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann Whitney U test of TAI posttest scores, n=14 per group. Experimental TAI fell from 51.64 to 38.79 while the control stayed near 47; the article reports "significantly different" with U=49.00, p<.05, no effect size printed.

> "the TAI and  AMM scores of the experimental group were significantly different (TAI, U=49.00 p<.05; AMM, U=44.00, p< .05). As expected, the mean TAI and AMM scores decreased"
