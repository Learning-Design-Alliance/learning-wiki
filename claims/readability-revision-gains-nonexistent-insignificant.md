## Evidence

### Mary H. Sawyer 1991

Mary H. Sawyer. (1991). A Review of Research in Revising Instructional Text. Journal of Reading Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1080/10862969109547744

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Duffy and Kabance (1982) among others, that a readable-writing approach to revision "failed to produce practical improvements in comprehension on several reading-to-do tasks" even after a reduction of six grade levels.

> "when materials are revised to make them more "readable," gains in comprehension are often either nonexistent or insignificant"
