Evidence¶

Ouhao Chen 2022¶

Ouhao Chen, Fred Paas, John Sweller. (2022). Reply to Sana et al.'s (2022) Commentary on Rest-from-Deliberate-Learning as a Mechanism for the Spacing Effect. Educational Psychology Review, 34, 1851–1858. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09678-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The authors' narrative review of the spacing and interleaving literatures reports, citing Kang and Pashler (2012) and Zulkiply and Burt (2013), that "the effect is more likely to be obtained using more similar than less similar interleaved tasks". The review attributes this pattern to the cited studies.

"The discriminative-contrast hypothesis assumes they are distinct effects because the effect is more likely to be obtained using more similar than less similar interleaved tasks (Kang & Pashler, 2012; Zulkiply & Burt, 2013)."
