## Evidence

### Puljak Livia 2020

Puljak Livia, Čivljak Marta, Haramina Ana, Mališa Snježana, Čavić Dalibor, Klinec Dinko, Aranza Diana, Mesarić Jasna, Skitarelić Nataša, Zoranić Sanja, Majstorović Dijana, Neuberg Marijana, Mikšić Štefica, Ivanišević Kata. (2020). Attitudes and concerns of undergraduate university health sciences students in Croatia regarding complete switch to e-learning during COVID-19 pandemic: a survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02343-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 2520 health sciences students at nine Croatian institutions (70.3% response rate), April–May 2020. Satisfaction was rated on a 5-point scale; the mean was "3.7 ± 1.1 out of maximum 5".

> "The average participants’ satisfaction with e-learning was 3.7 ± 1.1 out of maximum 5."

### Puljak Livia 2020 (2)

Puljak Livia, Čivljak Marta, Haramina Ana, Mališa Snježana, Čavić Dalibor, Klinec Dinko, Aranza Diana, Mesarić Jasna, Skitarelić Nataša, Zoranić Sanja, Majstorović Dijana, Neuberg Marijana, Mikšić Štefica, Ivanišević Kata. (2020). Attitudes and concerns of undergraduate university health sciences students in Croatia regarding complete switch to e-learning during COVID-19 pandemic: a survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02343-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same survey; students compared exclusive e-learning with their previous classroom education. About a third were neutral, while "39.6% found e-learning better and 24.9% found e-learning worse".

> "Compared with classic learning mode, about a third of participants had a neutral opinion, 39.6% found e-learning better and 24.9% found e-learning worse (detailed responses are shown in Additional file 3: Table S2)."
