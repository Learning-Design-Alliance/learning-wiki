## Evidence

### Xiao 2026

Xiao, R., Ye, R., Hou, X., Wen, J., Kumar, H., Liut, M., & Stamper, J. (2026). Bridging GenAI policy and practice: Brief instruction improves learning-oriented prompting in an introductory programming course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Component-level analysis of RCT gains (Supplementary Table S2; Fig. 4). Components students already largely supplied at baseline (54–61%) showed smallest gains; the authors group the six components into three instructional groups.

> "the gains of the brief reminder (Condition 1) were concentrated in the two components its message implies, guardrails ( +21.7 pp) and tutoring protocol ( +22.3 pp), with little movement elsewhere ( −5.2 to +7.8 pp)"
