## Evidence

### Lucía de Hoyos 2025

Lucía de Hoyos, Ellen Verhoef, Aysu Okbay, Janne R. Vermeulen, Celeste Figaroa, Miriam Lense, Simon E. Fisher, Reyna L. Gordon & Beate St Pourcain. (2025). Preschool musicality is associated with school-age communication abilities through genes related to rhythmicity. npj Science of Learning 10:39. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00329-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Phenotypic regressions were re-run with PGS EA added to the covariates; the article reports "little change in the strength of the associations". A parallel GWAS-by-subtraction analysis (PGS rhythmicity-EA) showed "a similar pattern of genetic overlap across the studied measures", suggesting limited confounding through EA.

> "We conﬁrmed the robustness of theﬁndings (Supplementary Table 1), observing little change in the strength of the associations (Supplementary Fig. 2 vs. Fig. 1)."
