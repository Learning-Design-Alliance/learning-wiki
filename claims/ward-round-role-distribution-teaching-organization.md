## Evidence

### Vietz E 2019

Vietz E, März E, Lottspeich C, Wölfel T, Fischer MR, Schmidmaier R. (2019). Ward round competences in surgery and psychiatry - a comparative multidisciplinary interview study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1554-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Role-contrast analysis of the tasks-and-skills interview part, reported with Fig. 1 (N Surgery-Senior = 16, N Psychiatry-Senior = 26, N Surgery-Resident = 16, N Psychiatry-Resident = 26). Only McNemar p-values are printed, so no effect size is reported.

> "In both specialities teaching was much more frequently described as a competence of a senior doctor with statistically significant differences in surgery, p = .031, and psychiatry, p = .002. In surgery organizational competence was more often assigned to a resident, p = .008."
