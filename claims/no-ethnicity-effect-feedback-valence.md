## Evidence

### Yeates Peter 2017

Yeates Peter, Woolf Katherine, Benbow Emyr, Davies Ben, Boohan Mairhead, Eva Kevin. (2017). A randomised trial of the influence of racial stereotype bias on examiners' scores, feedback and recollections in undergraduate clinical exams. BMC Medicine 15:179. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-017-0943-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Valence was coded from segmented free-text feedback (quadratic weighted kappa = 0.92) and analysed with GLM GEE; both groups received identical mean valence of –2.4. No effect size was printed.

> "There was no influence of students' ethnicity on the valence of feedback: Asian students –2.4 (–2.6 to –2.3); white students –2.4 (–2.6 to –2.3), p = 0.82. The interaction of performance by student ethnicity was also non-significant for feedback valence at p = 0.57."
