## Evidence

### Soemantri Diantha 2018

Soemantri Diantha, Dodds Agnes, Mccoll Geoff. (2018). Examining the nature of feedback within the Mini Clinical Evaluation Exercise (Mini-CEX): an analysis of 1427 Mini-CEX assessment forms. GMS Journal for Medical Education, Volume 35, Issue 4, Doc47. [doi:10.3205/zma001193](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001193)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis relating students' clinical performance ratings to feedback specificity across the 1427 Mini-CEX forms; the article reports "a negative weak significant relationship" with printed correlations r = -.22 to -.28, and cautions these relationships need treating with caution due to the large sample.

> "For every domain of clinical performance listed in the Mini-CEX form there was a negative weak significant relationship with the feedback provided for students’ development (r value ranging from -.22 to -.28, p<.01)."
