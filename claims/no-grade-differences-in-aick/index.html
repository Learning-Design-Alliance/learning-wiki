Evidence¶

Meijuan Xie 2025¶

Meijuan Xie, Liling Luo. (2025). The Status Quo and Future of AI-TPACK for Mathematics Teacher Education Students: A Case Study in Chinese Universities. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13533 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2503.13533

q2 · i? · associational · r?

One-way ANOVA across three grades (senior university, first-year graduate, second-year graduate; N=412) in the post-test survey. No equivalence was tested; only F statistics and significance levels were printed, so no effect sizes are reported for any contrast.

"AI-TK (F =1.83, Sig =0.161), AI-TCK (F =1.90, Sig =0.151), AI-TPK (F=0.37, Sig=0.708) and AI-TPACK (F=0.83, Sig=0.435) have no significant difference between grades. Significance differences existed in Self-efficacy (F=4.68, Sig=0.010) and Teaching Beliefs (F=7.58, Sig=0.001)."
