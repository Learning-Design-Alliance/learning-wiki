## Evidence

### Madden J. 2020

Madden J., Pandita S., Schuldt J. P., Kim B., S. Won A., Holmes N. G. (2020). Ready student one: Exploring the predictors of student learning in virtual reality. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0229788

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up learning test emailed to all participants about four months after the intervention; 56 responses were received, which the authors note made breakdowns by demographics difficult. Scores averaged 39.0% with no between-condition differences.

> "The average score on the delayed post-test(completed approximately four months after participation in the activity) was 39.0%, again with no differences between conditions: F(2,51) = 0.571, p = .568."
