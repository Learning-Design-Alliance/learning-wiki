## Evidence

### Navlekar Anisha S. 2025

Navlekar Anisha S., Theobald Elli J., Griffith Ken, Limeri Lisa B. (2025). Strategically creating maximally heterogeneous lab groups did not improve group performance in an introductory biology lab class. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323799

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis of post-semester survey measures (teamwork satisfaction, 4 items; perceptions of collaborative learning, 7 items) found unacceptable initial fit and, after revision, failed measurement invariance, so the authors "were not able to analyze how group formation influenced students' teamwork satisfaction or perceptions of collaborative learning".

> "However, we were unable to obtain satisfactory evidence of measurement invariance across the two types of group formation in either semester (see S3 File for more details on this analysis and results)."
