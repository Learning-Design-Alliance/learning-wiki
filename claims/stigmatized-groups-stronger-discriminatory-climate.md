## Evidence

### Heppt 2025

Heppt, Birgit; Schwarzenthal, Miriam; Scharf, Jan. (2025). Discriminatory Climate and School Adjustment in Ethnically Minoritized Adolescents and Majority Adolescents: An Investigation of the Mediating Role of Teaching Quality. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-025-02147-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multiple linear regression on PISA 2018 German supplementary Grade 9 data (N = 2947) predicting student-reported discriminatory climate (four items, α = 0.85). The regression showed stigmatized-group students "perceived a stronger discriminatory climate than ethnic majority students and minoritized students from other countries did".

> "minoritized students from Turkey, the SWANA region, sub-Saharan Africa, or Kurdish areas, who were assumed to be strongly stigmatized in German society, indeed perceived a stronger discriminatory climate than ethnic majority students and minoritized students from other countries did"
