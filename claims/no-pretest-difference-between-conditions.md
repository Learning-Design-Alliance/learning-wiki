## Evidence

### Lancaster 2019

Lancaster, J., & Bain, A. (2019). Designing University Courses to Improve Pre-Service Teachers' Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Evidence-Based Inclusive Practice. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 44(2). https://ro.ecu.edu.au/ajte/vol44/iss2/4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest equivalence check in the 40-participant quasi-experiment; participants were matched on age, prior exposure to students with disabilities, and grade point average. Pretest means were 4.00 (applied experience) and 3.75 (embedded design). No statistically significant difference; equivalence was not otherwise tested.

> "An omnibus two-factor Analysis of Variance using pre-test means for the cumulative mastery test (repeated measures factor) and condition (between factor) revealed no statistically significant differences on the pretest (F) 1, 38= .225 p= .63)."
