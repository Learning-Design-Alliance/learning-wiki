## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Although theory predicts an isotropic distribution of product-electron tracks, the detection method never yields a clear image of an upward-moving electron, and sparks form only when the electron's path deviates up to roughly 30° from the chamber's axis. ## Evidence

### Benjamin Brau 2009

Benjamin Brau, Christopher May, Robert Ormond, and John Essick. (2009). Determining the Muon Mass in an Instructional Laboratory. https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.5461 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0907.5461](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0907.5461)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Empirical observation during image inspection of the data run. The authors attribute the absence of upward-moving electron images to the fact that each spark gap can only produce a single spark, and note the electric field is along the chamber's axis.

> "Second, for a downward directed electron, we ﬁnd that a spark is created only if the electron’s path deviates up to roughly 30 ◦ from the chamber’s axis."
