Hirschboeck 2022¶

Hirschboeck, K., Eiler White, M., Brannegan, A., & Reade, F. (2022). Teacher residency programs in California: Financial sustainability challenges and opportunities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED620723

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Spring 2021 Partnership Lead Survey: half of leads reported placement challenges, attributed mainly to "issues matching open positions with residents' credentials"; COVID-influenced layoffs also contributed per several leads.

"Half of residency program leads indicated that they faced challenges placing residents in LEA teaching jobs upon completion of the program. Most leads reported that challenges were due to issues matching open positions with residents' credentials"

Hirschboeck 2022 (2)¶

Hirschboeck, K., Eiler White, M., Brannegan, A., & Reade, F. (2022). Teacher residency programs in California: Financial sustainability challenges and opportunities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED620723

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Partnership Team survey data show administrative capacity gaps: "40 percent of partnership team members indicated that their program could have allocated more resources" to administrative costs. One member described a Catch 22: small programs cannot fund dedicated staff, so no one grows the program.

"Forty percent of partnership team members indicated that their program could have allocated more resources to support the administrative costs of running the residency program, and 36 percent of partnership team members indicated challenges balancing residency work with their other responsibilities"
