## Evidence

### Limeri 2025

Limeri, Lisa B. (2025). Intelligence in Context: A Context-specific Mindset Measure Better Predicts Outcomes for Science and Math Undergraduates. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-09-0229

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Combined SEM with both measures as predictors of each outcome in the 1537-student dataset. The original measure "became a significant predictor with small effect size" with a positive, unexpected direction for both academic outcomes.

> "However, in the combined model in which both the original mindset measure and the ULTrA fixed factor were included as predictors, the original mindset measure became a significant predictor with small effect size. However, the direction of the effect was positive, which was the opposite of what would be expected."
