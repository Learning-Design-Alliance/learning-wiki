## Evidence

### Lovaas Model of Applied Behavior Analysis 2010

Lovaas Model of Applied Behavior Analysis. (2010). WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/295

`q3 · i0` · `review · r3`

WWC review of the Sallows and Graupner (2005) randomized controlled trial (23 children randomly assigned to therapist-led or parent-directed Lovaas treatment). "neither effect was statistically significant" and neither effect size met the substantively-important threshold; average improvement index –3 percentile points, range –4 to –3.

> "Sallows and Graupner (2005) examined two outcomes. The authors found, and the WWC confirmed, that neither effect was statistically significant. Neither effect size was large enough to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria."
