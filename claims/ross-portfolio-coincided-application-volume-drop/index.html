Descriptive institutional data reported in the case study's findings section: application counts before and after portfolio implementation. The report states the drop "may be because it tested applicants’ level of interest in the BBA program." No effect size is printed.

"Implementing the Ross Admissions Portfolio did coincide with a decrease in applicants to the BBA program. Compared to the 13,000 applications in 2015, the Ross School received under 6,500 applications in 2018. This may be because it tested applicants’ level of interest in the BBA program."
