Weiler Richard, Chew Stephen, Coombs Ngaire, Hamer Mark, Stamatakis Emmanuel. (2012). Physical activity education in the undergraduate curricula of all UK medical schools. Are tomorrow's doctors equipped to follow clinical guidelines? British Journal of Sports Medicine 46(14):1024-1026. https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2012-091380

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the discussion the authors compare the 4.2 hours of PA teaching with "the mean 109 hours (range 18 to 336 hours) spent teaching pharmacology", arguing the allocation does not reflect the public health impact doctors could have.
