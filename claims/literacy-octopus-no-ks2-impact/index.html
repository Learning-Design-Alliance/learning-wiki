Evidence¶

Lord et al. 2017¶

Pippa Lord, Adam Rabiasz, Palak Roy, Jennie Harland, Ben Styles, Katherine Fowler. (2017). Evidence-based literacy support: the 'Literacy Octopus' trial — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN84508118

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Intention-to-treat analysis of a well-powered, school-level randomised controlled trial involving 823 primary schools, using National Pupil Database KS2 English scores (reading plus grammar, punctuation and spelling scaled scores) with KS1 baseline. All nine arm-versus-control effect sizes were between -0.04 and 0.03, with zero estimated months' progress.

"There was no evidence that any of the interventions had an impact on pupils' English results at KS2 (2015/2016)."
