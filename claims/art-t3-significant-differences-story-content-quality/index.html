Evidence¶

Dunn 2013¶

Michael Dunn. (2013). Comparing two story-writing mnemonic strategies: A randomized control trial study. International Journal of Special Education, 28(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1024423

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Randomized control trial analysis of 12 fourth-graders assigned to ART, T3, or control groups; probe scores were compared with paired-samples t-tests and MANOVA at a .25 alpha level given the small sample. The article reports "significant differences computed amongst groups" for story content and for T3's story quality; no effect size accompanies this test.

"Significant differences computed amongst groups resulted between ART (Dunn & Finley, 2008) and T3 (Katahira, 2012) for intervention story content; T3 also had a significant difference for story quality."
