# Exposure to the HP Learning Studio raised student awareness that spread peer-to-peer and broadened students' sense of future possibilities

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After the launch event, students who attended described peers asking about the studio and expressing interest in visiting, indicating awareness spreading through social exposure. [→ awareness-student](#awareness-student)
`q2 i?` A student reported that exposure to the studio's opportunities broadened students' ideas of what they are capable of in the future. [→ awareness-broaden](#awareness-broaden)

## Evidence

### awareness-student

Wortman, A., and Schiner, N. (2024, June). Access to powerful technology as a catalyst for career pathway engagement [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/217

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In an interview, 12th-grade student Yahaira described how, after experimenting with the podcasting station at the ribbon-cutting launch, other students came to her to learn about the space: "A bunch of people asked me about it." The paper treats this as evidence of awareness building among students.

> "A bunch of people asked me about it, like, 'What's in there or what can you do in there?' And I explained to them: they gave us new gaming systems. They gave us a new podcast system."

### awareness-broaden

Wortman, A., and Schiner, N. (2024, June). Access to powerful technology as a catalyst for career pathway engagement [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/217

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview evidence from the same student in the Awareness section: she stated the studio "opens a bigger field for us" because some students hold narrow views of what school is about. The paper interprets this as exposure broadening perceived future possibilities.

> "It opens a bigger field for us because some students are very limited to what they think school is about. But it's not really like that. There's a bunch more opportunities"
