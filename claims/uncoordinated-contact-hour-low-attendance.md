## Evidence

### Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto 2015

Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto. (2015). Report on Development of strategies to improve the students' learning by participation in class and out-of-classroom. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational attendance data for the University College's weekly contact hour for calculus, physics, chemistry, and biology students, organized without instructor consultation. For physics contact hours the report notes "very often none, or only 1 student showed up".

> "However, due to lack of coordination the attendance is extremely low, less than 1 percent."

### Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto 2015 (2)

Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto. (2015). Report on Development of strategies to improve the students' learning by participation in class and out-of-classroom. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The instructor organized a voluntary weekly study hour run by his own Korean-speaking teaching assistants, where students collect graded assignments and ask questions. Attendance was voluntary and did not affect grades; questionnaires in 2009 and 2010 suggested roughly 20% of students made good use of it.

> "On average 15 to 20% of the students in my classes attend the weekly study hour."
