# Online flipped learning improves self-regulation more than online learning in a basic math course

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` The online flipped learning group outperformed the online learning group on post-test self-regulation (partial η2 = .039). [→ Yorganci 2025](#yorganci-2025)

## Evidence

### Yorganci 2025

Yorganci, S. (2025). The impact of synchronous online discussions and online flipped learning on student engagement and self-regulation among preliminary undergraduates in a basic math course. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10459-0

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANCOVA (pretest self-regulation as covariate) among 121 undergraduates found a significant main effect of learning approach on self-regulation, F(1, 116) = 4.694, p < .05, "η 2 = .039". The flipped group (M = 3.46) outperformed the online learning group (M = 3.32).

> "the main effect of the learning approach showed a significant effect on self-regulation (F (1, 116) = 4.694, p < .05, η 2 = .039). According to the group means, the online flipped learning group (M = 3.46, SD = .29) outperformed the online learning group (M = 3.32, SD = .39) in self-regulation."
