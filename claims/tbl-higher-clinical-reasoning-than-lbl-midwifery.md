## Evidence

### Ulfa Yunefit 2021

Ulfa Yunefit, Igarashi Yukari, Takahata Kaori, Shishido Eri, Horiuchi Shigeko. (2021). A comparison of team-based learning and lecture-based learning on clinical reasoning and classroom engagement: a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02881-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Cluster randomized controlled trial with 115 second-year midwifery students in two Indonesian schools (62 TBL, 53 LBL), three 90-minute sessions each. Unpaired t-tests showed "significantly higher" clinical reasoning in TBL at both time points, with large effect sizes d = 1.41 and d = 1.50.

> "the mean clinical reasoning on PPH scores of the TBL group were significantly higher than those of the control group at post-test (t = 7.52; p < .001, Cohen's d = 1.41) and 2 weeks post-test (t = 8.0; p < .001, Cohen's d = 1.50)"

### Ulfa Yunefit 2021 (2)

Ulfa Yunefit, Igarashi Yukari, Takahata Kaori, Shishido Eri, Horiuchi Shigeko. (2021). A comparison of team-based learning and lecture-based learning on clinical reasoning and classroom engagement: a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02881-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Baseline comparison in the same cluster RCT found no significant pre-test difference between the TBL and LBL groups (t = 1.86; p = .65). The printed d = 0.35 is a descriptive baseline difference, not a treatment effect; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in the mean clinical scores at pre-test between the intervention group and the control group (t = 1.86; p = .65, Cohen's d = 0.35)"
