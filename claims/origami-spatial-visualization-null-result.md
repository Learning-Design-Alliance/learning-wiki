## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Akayuure et al. 2016

Peter Akayuure, S. K. Asiedu-Addo, Victor Alebna. (2016). Investigating the Effect of Origami Instruction on Preservice Teachers’ Spatial Ability and Geometric Knowledge for Teaching. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology. https://doi.org/10.18404/ijemst.78424

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on spatial visualization (punched holes test) posttest scores in the quasi-experiment (experimental n = 52, control n = 42). Descriptive posttest means were 52.29 versus 47.13 on an 80-point max, but the difference was not statistically significant; equivalence was not tested.

> "The analysis showed no statistical significant difference in posttest mean score between the experimental and control groups regarding spatial visualization."
