Shaun Bangay 2024¶

The scoping review reports, citing sources [72], [64] and [38], that "Trust in reviews is highest for voluntary reviews" and that fake reviews are usually created for payment. This is a narrative synthesis claim from the reviewed literature with no effect size printed.

"Trust in reviews is highest for voluntary reviews [72] as fake reviews are usually created for payment [64, 38]."
