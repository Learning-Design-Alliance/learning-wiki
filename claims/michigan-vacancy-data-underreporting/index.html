Evidence¶

Jen DeNeal 2025¶

Jen DeNeal, Charlotte Pierce, and the EdTrust-Midwest team. (2025). Addressing Michigan's Teacher Shortage Problem for Students Most in Need: Closing the Opportunity Divide. EdTrust-Midwest. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED671388

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The report's review of state vacancy reporting found that because districts were historically not required to report vacancies, "nearly 90% of the school districts in Michigan reported zero teacher vacancies" in 2021-22, which the authors call a severe undercounting of actual vacancies.

"Until recently, Michigan school districts were not required to report vacancy data at all, and many chose not to. In 2021-22, nearly 90% of the school districts in Michigan reported zero teacher vacancies."
