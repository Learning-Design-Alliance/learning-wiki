# Students reported that individual exercise assignments helped them plan and monitor their learning but did not necessarily increase learning motivation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Answers to four self-regulated-learning questions in Q2 indicated the individual exercises helped students plan and monitor their learning processes, but did not necessarily have a positive effect on their learning motivation. [→ Gfrerer 2023](#gfrerer-2023)

## Evidence

### Gfrerer 2023

Gfrerer, M. H.; Marussig, B.; Maitz, K.; Bangerl, M. M. (2023). Teaching mechanics with individual exercise assignments and automated correction. [doi:10.1002/pamm.202300168](https://doi.org/10.1002/pamm.202300168)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Four questions on self-regulated and reflective learning were part of the end-of-semester questionnaire (Q2); the authors report that "the individual exercises helped them plan and monitor their learning processes" while the effect on motivation was not necessarily positive.

> "students’ answers indicate that the individual exercises helped them plan and monitor their learning processes, but did not necessarily have a positive effect on their learning motivation."

### Gfrerer 2023 (2)

Gfrerer, M. H.; Marussig, B.; Maitz, K.; Bangerl, M. M. (2023). Teaching mechanics with individual exercise assignments and automated correction. [doi:10.1002/pamm.202300168](https://doi.org/10.1002/pamm.202300168)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A review of open-text responses from the same survey identified two advantages students highlighted: the assignments "encouraged self-directed and reflective learning" and supported learning the course content and identifying essential learning goals.

> "1. Students appreciated that the individual exercise assignments encouraged self-directed and reflective learning.
2. Students liked that the individual exercise assignment supports learning the course content and helps identify essential learning goals."
