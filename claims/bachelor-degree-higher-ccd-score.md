Independent t-test comparing 97 personnel by education level; the article reports bachelor's degree holders scored "38.4" versus "30.0" for associate degree holders in managing CCD.

> "Regarding the skill of managing CCD, statistical analyses showed that participants with a bachelor’s degree had a higher mean score (38.4, p = 0.004) compared to those with an associate degree (30.0, p = 0.004)."
