## Evidence

### Emily M. Levesque 2015

Emily M. Levesque, Rachel Bezanson, & Grant R. Tremblay. (2015). Physics GRE Scores of Prize Postdoctoral Fellows in Astronomy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Numerical breakdown in Table 1 of fellows below PGRE percentile thresholds from 10-90%, split by gender, illustrated in Figure 3. A 60% cutoff excludes 65 (43.6%) of all fellows and 32 (60.4%) of female fellows per the table; the discussion quotes 43.6% and 60.4%.

> "while this would exclude 43.6% of the total applicant pool, the cutoﬀ would disproportionately impact women, excluding 60.4% of female applicants. Those prospective applicants would have gone on to write 267 (133) papers during their graduate careers."
