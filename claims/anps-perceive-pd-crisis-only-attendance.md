`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the interview study, thematic analysis found many participants believed that individuals with personality disorder were not regular attenders in primary or emergency care settings. The authors note this perception appeared to stem from limited awareness of the diverse and subtle presentations of PD, representing a gap in recognition of chronic or milder manifestations.

> "Many participants believed that individuals with personality disorder (PD) were not regular attenders in primary or emergency care settings, but this perception appeared to stem from limited awareness of the diverse and subtle presentations of PD."
