## Evidence

### Santaló María Isabel 2019

Santaló María Isabel, Gibbons Sandra, Naylor Patti-Jean. (2019). Using Food Models to Enhance Sugar Literacy among Older Adolescents: Evaluation of a Brief Experiential Nutrition Education Intervention. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11081763

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial, repeated measures ANOVA (Table 2): label-interpretation score rose from 4.6 to 5.3 in the intervention group versus a slight decline in controls, with printed effect size η2 = 0.07, an intermediate effect as the article states.

> "Intention to consume less added sugar increased significantly after intervention (F(1, 201) = 4.93, p = 0.03) as did label reading confidence (F(1, 200) = 14.94, p < 0.001)."

### Santaló María Isabel 2019 (2)

Santaló María Isabel, Gibbons Sandra, Naylor Patti-Jean. (2019). Using Food Models to Enhance Sugar Literacy among Older Adolescents: Evaluation of a Brief Experiential Nutrition Education Intervention. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11081763

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized trial: intention scores rose from 14.9 to 15.3 in the intervention group versus a decline in controls; the article reports the intention effect as small with printed effect size η2 = 0.02.

> "Effect sizes were respectively small and intermediate (η2 = 0.02, η2 = 0.07) however."
