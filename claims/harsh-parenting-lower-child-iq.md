`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Secondary analysis contrasting the parenting–IQ association in the school-achievement subsample with the full dataset of children with parenting and IQ information. The article reports the coefficients and confidence intervals as printed.

> "harsh parenting and corporal punishment were negatively associated with child IQ in both datasets [Bs ≥ −0.56 95% CI (−1.10, −0.02) P = 0.04] (Supplementary table S5) and family routines were not related to child IQ [Bs ≤ 0.89 95% CI (−0.02, 1.80) P = 0.05]."
