`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sensitivity analyses defined a priori on prior NIHSS knowledge, prior NIHSS course, ICU/neurology ward experience over 3 months, and completion over multiple days (>12 hours elapsed). All preserved the direction of effect; no effect sizes printed.

> "None of the 4 preplanned sensitivity analyses showed any major changes in the direction of the effect. The multivariable linear regression only showed a minor change in the coefficient (<15%), confirming these results."
