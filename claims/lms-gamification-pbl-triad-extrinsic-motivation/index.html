q1 · i? · design · r2

The authors' literature- and practice-based assertion, citing Machajewski (2017), that academic LMSs lag corporate learning platforms and that "Blackboard traditionally had a very few built-in features directly supporting gamification". Examples given include modules as levels, adaptive release, badges, and discussion leaderboards.
