## Evidence

### Zhou Tian 2025

Zhou Tian, Zhou Xuejun, Wang Baozhu. (2025). Latent profiles of learning engagement and anxiety in high school students: the mediating role of academic self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03114-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (5,000 samples) with engagement profiles as a four-category independent variable, self-efficacy as mediator, controlling for grade and gender. The overall mediation effect confidence interval [-0.429, -0.234] excluded 0, and 'academic self-efficacy significantly negatively predicted learning anxiety'.

> "Additionally, academic self-efficacy significantly negatively predicted learning anxiety."
