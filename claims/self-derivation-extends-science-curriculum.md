## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Open-ended self-derivation of science-curriculum facts did not differ from general-knowledge facts across experiments (d = .03), demonstrating the process generalizes to academic content. [→ Bauer 2020](#bauer-2020)

## Evidence

### Bauer 2020

Bauer, P. J., Esposito, A. G., & Daly, J. J. (2020). Self-derivation through memory integration: A model for accumulation of semantic knowledge. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2019.101271

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Between-experiment comparison of 96 (Experiment 1, general knowledge) and 103 (Experiment 2, science curriculum) second-graders. "levels of open-ended self-derivation of the integration facts and open-ended recall of the stem facts did not differ between the experiments" (t(189) = 0.22, p = .83, d = .03).

> "levels of open-ended self-derivation of the integration facts and open-ended recall of the stem facts did not differ between the experiments, even though each used entirely different stimuli, t(189) = 0.22, p = .83, d = .03"
