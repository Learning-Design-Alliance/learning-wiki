# In the Linkow et al. (2019) scale-up QED, coaching showed no statistically significant effect on cumulative college GPA (effect size 0.01, p = 0.82)

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` The 2019 study found intervention students earned a 2.37 cumulative GPA versus 2.36 for comparison students, an effect size of 0.01 and improvement index of 0, not statistically significant (p = 0.82). [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2020](#what-works-clearinghouse-2020)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2020

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020). Success Boston Coaching (WWC 2021-001). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/708

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

QED with 814 intervention and 2,739 comparison students matched via propensity score blocking. Table 4 reports GPAs of 2.37 (SD 1.03) versus 2.36 (SD 1.10), effect size 0.01, improvement index 0, p = 0.82; the study is characterized as having an indeterminate effect.

> "The finding on the academic achievement domain, which shows evidence of an indeterminate effect, is based on an outcome analysis that includes 3,553 students."
