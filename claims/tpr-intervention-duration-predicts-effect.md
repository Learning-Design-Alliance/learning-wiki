## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Meta-regression found a significant positive relationship between implementation duration (weeks) and effect sizes, with duration explaining 35% of the variance. [→ Tugba Inciman Celik 2021](#tugba-inciman-celik-2021)

## Evidence

### Tugba Inciman Celik 2021

Tugba Inciman Celik, Tolga Cay & Sedat Kanadli. (2021). The Effect of Total Physical Response Method on Vocabulary Learning/Teaching: A Mixed Research Synthesis. English Language Teaching, 14(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v14n12p154

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression over the 8 studies that reported administration duration (five were excluded; lesson hours were converted to weeks at 40 minutes per hour). Duration significantly predicted effect sizes (p<.05), explaining "35% of the variance".

> "As a result of meta-regression, there is a significant (p<.05) relationship between the duration of applying the experimental procedure and the effect sizes, and the implementation period explains 35% of the variance."
