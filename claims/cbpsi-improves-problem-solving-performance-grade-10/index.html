Evidence¶

Dhlamini 2016¶

Dhlamini, J. J. (2016). Enhancing learners’ problem solving performance in mathematics: A cognitive load perspective. European Journal of STEM Education, 1(1), 27-36. https://doi.org/10.20897/lectito.201604

q2 · i? · causal · r1

One-group pre-test post-test study with 57 Grade 10 learners in a Gauteng township school; FMAT scores compared by t-test at the 0.05 level. The article reports the improvement was "improved significantly (p<0.05)"; no effect size is printed.

"The results in Table 2 suggest that participants’ problem solving performance in a context-based problem solving achievement test (FMAT) improved significantly (p<0.05) as a function of the experiment."
