## Evidence

### Lei 2018

Lei, H., Cui, Y., & Zhou, W. (2018). Relationships between student engagement and academic achievement: A meta-analysis. Social Behavior and Personality, 46(3), 517–528. https://doi.org/10.2224/sbp.7054

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Pooled effect sizes from the same meta-analysis of 69 samples. The article reports r = .350 for behavioral engagement, r = .216 for emotional engagement, and r = .245 for cognitive engagement, all significant, fitting a mesomeric effect.

> "behavioral engagement, rBE = .350 (z = 108.688, p \< .001, k = 55, 95% CI = .344, .355); emotional engagement, rEE = .216 (z = 48.333, p \< .001, k = 47, 95% CI = .208, .224); and cognitive engagement, rCE = .245 (z = 43.968, p \< .001, k = 31, 95% CI = .235, .256)"
