## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Nursing undergraduates taught anaphylactic shock with combined PBL-CBL scored significantly higher on the total post-class test than students taught with LBL (87.33±4.21 vs. 73.32±3.66, P<0.001). [→ Jiang Xin 2026](#jiang-xin-2026)

## Evidence

### Jiang Xin 2026

Jiang Xin, Hui Yiqing, Tong Chuan, Li Dongxiao, Zhang Chaoqun, Chang Ying. (2026). Application of the combined PBL-CBL teaching method of anaphylactic shock prevention and care in the fundamental nursing course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08746-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized two-group trial of 100 nursing undergraduates (n=50 per group) comparing PBL-CBL with LBL for anaphylactic shock teaching; the "significantly higher total test scores (87.33±4.21 vs. 73.32±3.66, P<0.001)" were reported in Results (Table 1).

> "The PBL-CBL group had significantly higher total test scores (87.33±4.21 vs. 73.32±3.66, P<0.001) and better performance in all test sections."
