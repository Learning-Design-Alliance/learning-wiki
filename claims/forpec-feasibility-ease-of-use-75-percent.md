# Facilitators' feasibility agreement was at least 96.43% on every statement except ease of use, which 25% did not agree with

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among 28 facilitators who built a scenario with ForPEC version 1.3, agreement was 100% on statements 1 and 2, 100% on statement 5 (27 respondents; one did not use the examples) and 96.43% on statement 4, but 25% did not agree that the form was easy to use: 4 (14.29%) neither agreed nor disagreed and 3 (10.71%) disagreed. Kaneko Regina Mayumi Utiyama, Monteiro Inês, Lopes Maria Helena Baena de Moraes. (2022). Form for planning and elaborating high fidelity simulation scenarios: A validation study. PLoS ONE 17(9): e0274239. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0274239

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Feasibility assessment questionnaire completed by 28 test participants (nurses and physicians) after scenario development and piloting, rated on a five-point Likert-type agreement scale. The article reports that "25% indicated I do not agree and disagree" on the ease-of-use statement.

> "Statements 1, 2 and 5 reached 100% agreement, and statement 4 96.43% (strongly agree and agree). In relation to statement 3 (It was easy to use the ForPEC form to build your scenario), it was observed that 25% indicated I do not agree and disagree"
