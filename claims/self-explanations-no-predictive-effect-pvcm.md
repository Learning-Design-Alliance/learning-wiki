## Evidence

### Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025

Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld, Bernadette Gold. (2025). Complete or faded double-content examples of video-based analyses: How do they impact student teachers' professional vision of classroom management? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09738-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple regression analyses with self-explanation scores as predictors and residualized change scores as criteria showed no significant overall prediction for any criterion, e.g. noticing + reasoning, F(8,49) = 1.61, p = .145, R² = .209.

> "Multiple regression analyses (see Table 4) showed that all the self-explanations together did not predict change in noticing + reasoning, F(8,49) = 1.61, p =.145, alternatives of action, F(8,49) = 0.48, p =.865"

### Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld 2025 (2)

Jasmin Lilian Bauersfeld, Bernadette Gold. (2025). Complete or faded double-content examples of video-based analyses: How do they impact student teachers' professional vision of classroom management? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09738-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within the regression models, self-explaining concepts of classroom management in time block 2 significantly predicted change in multiple perspectives, and self-explanations of focus on students in time block 3 negatively predicted change in focus on students, β = −.385, p = .036.

> "we found that self-explaining aspects of concepts of classroom management from the double-content example in time block 2 significantly predicted the change in multiple perspectives. However, self-explanations of the focus on students negatively predicted the change in the focus on students."
