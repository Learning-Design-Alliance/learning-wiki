## Evidence

### Yan Jingxin 2024

Yan Jingxin, Wen Yonghao, Liu Xinlian, Deng Manjun, Ye Bin, Li Ting, Wang Huanwei, Jia Cui, Liao Jinsong, Zhang Lushun. (2024). The effectiveness of problem-based learning and case-based learning teaching methods in clinical practical teaching in TACE treatment for hepatocellular carcinoma in China: a bayesian network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05615-8

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Bayesian network meta-analysis of 7 studies (five RCTs and two observational studies) comparing PBL, CBL, PBL+CBL, PBL+TBL and LBL in TACE teaching. The authors state the "combination of PBL and CBL showed more effectiveness" across all nine listed outcomes; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The combination of PBL and CBL showed more effectiveness in clinical thinking capacity, clinical practice capacity, knowledge understanding degree, literature reading ability, method satisfaction degree, learning efficiency, learning interest, practical skills examination scores and theoretical knowledge examination scores."
