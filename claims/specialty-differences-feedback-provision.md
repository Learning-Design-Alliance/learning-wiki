Quality analysis of feedback by specialty on paper-format mini-CEXs: EPs provided all three components on 19.0% of assessments versus 12.4% for trauma surgeons, and trauma surgeons gave feedback on only 70.3% of mini-CEXs versus 97.7% for EPs. Percentages printed; no effect size.

> "Only 5.1 % of assessments completed by EPs, but 29.7 % of those by trauma surgeons, provided no feedback at all."
