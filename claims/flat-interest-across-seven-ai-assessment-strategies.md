Registration data from the 28 July 2026 workshop: of 118 registrants, interest divided almost evenly between redesigning what gets assigned (37 registrants) and redesigning how work is verified or valued (40). The report notes instructors pursue both at once rather than choosing between them.

> "Redesigning what gets assigned—open-ended and authentic tasks, ambitious AI-leveraged projects—drew 37 registrants. Redesigning how the work is verified or valued—oral assessment, rubrics and grading—drew 40."
