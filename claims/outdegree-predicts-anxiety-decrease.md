## Evidence

### Remy Dou and Justyna P. Zwolak 2019

Remy Dou and Justyna P. Zwolak. (2019). Practitioner’s guide to social network analysis: Examining physics anxiety in an active-learning setting. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020105)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Permutation-based simple linear regressions on network data from the fifth collection in two active-learning Introductory Physics I sections (N=126 combined). The article reports that "the greater a students’ outdegree, the more likely that student is to experience a larger decrease" in anxiety, with "standardized beta estimates β range from −0.22 to −0.28" (average −0.26), significant for all tested α values.

> "The negative estimates suggest that the greater a students’ outdegree, the more likely that student is to experience a larger decrease in their anxiety from the beginning to the end of the semester (see Table III for the regression estimates for outdegree from ﬁfth collection). The standardized beta estimates β range from −0.22 to −0.28, with an average of −0.26."
