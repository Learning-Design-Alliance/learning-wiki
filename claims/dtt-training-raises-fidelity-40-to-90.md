# Training in discrete trial implementation raises implementation fidelity from about 40.61% at baseline to about 90.58% at the end of training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across 46 SCED studies, the mean change in DTT implementation fidelity between baseline and the last intervention point was 49.97%, bringing average fidelity to 90.58%. [→ Joelle Fingerhut and Mariola Moeyaert 2022](#joelle-fingerhut-and-mariola-moeyaert-2022)

## Evidence

### Joelle Fingerhut and Mariola Moeyaert 2022

Joelle Fingerhut and Mariola Moeyaert. (2022). Training Individuals to Implement Discrete Trials with Fidelity: A Meta-Analysis. Focus on Autism and Other Developmental Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/10883576221081076

`q3 · i?`

Hierarchical linear modeling meta-analysis of 46 single-case experimental design studies (224 participants) estimated the baseline-to-intervention change in implementation fidelity. The article reports "49.97%, γ̂100 = 49.97, t(3693) = 18.25, p < .0125", yielding 90.58% average fidelity at the end of training.

> "The mean change in DTT implementation fidelity between the baseline phase and the intervention phase at the last point of the intervention was estimated to be 49.97%, γ̂100 = 49.97, t(3693) = 18.25, p < .0125."
