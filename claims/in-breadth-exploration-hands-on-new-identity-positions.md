## Evidence

### Verhoeven 2019

Verhoeven, M., Poorthuis, A. M. G., & Volman, M. (2019). The Role of School in Adolescents' Identity Development. A Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9457-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of ten articles (three theoretical, seven ethnographic) on in-breadth explorative learning experiences, which argued or demonstrated this irrespective of their perspective on identity development. One study of a 4-week summer program for 30 American adolescents found interviews with 13 participants indicated increased engagement with environmental issues.

> "providing adolescents with such experiences may invite them to adopt new interests, to identify undiscovered talents, and to try out new identity positions."

### Verhoeven 2019 (2)

Verhoeven, M., Poorthuis, A. M. G., & Volman, M. (2019). The Role of School in Adolescents' Identity Development. A Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9457-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's interpretive synthesis across the in-breadth studies, attributing the effect to the authentic, real-life ways in which on-site and hands-on activities introduce adolescents to new contents, activities, and positions.

> "introducing adolescents to unfamiliar learning contents, learning activities, and identity positions through on-site and hands-on activities especially helps adolescents to imagine the identity implications thereof."
