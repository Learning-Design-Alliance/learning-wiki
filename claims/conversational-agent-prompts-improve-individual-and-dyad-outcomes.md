## Evidence

### Sten Ludvigsen 2016

Sten Ludvigsen, Ulrike Cress, Nancy Law, Carolyn P. Rosé, & Gerry Stahl. (2016). Collaboration scripts and scaffolding. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 11, 381–385. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-016-9247-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The editors report the experimental study by Tegos, Demetriadis, Papadopoulos, and Weinberger with second-year computer science students in a human-computer interaction course, using pre- and post-test treatment and control groups. They state the prompts "lead to improved outcomes for the students"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "The results show that the prompt by the conversational agents lead to improved outcomes for the students, not only at the individual level but also at the level of the dyad as a whole."
