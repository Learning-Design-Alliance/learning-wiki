## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Daily performance (in-class performance and lab reports) was significantly lower in the MOOC-based blended teaching group than in the face-to-face classroom group. [→ Cao W 2021](#cao-w-2021)

## Evidence

### Cao W 2021

Cao W, Hu L, Li X, Li X, Chen C, Zhang Q, Cao S. (2021). Massive Open Online Courses-based blended versus face-to-face classroom teaching methods for fundamental nursing course. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000024829

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same cluster randomized controlled trial (N = 181), Table 3 reports median daily performance of 87.00 (80.00∼94.00) in the blended group versus 91.00 (85.00∼94.00) in the face-to-face group; the article reports the difference as "significantly lower" with P = .014 and prints no effect size.

> "However, the daily performance in MOOC-based blended teaching group was significantly lower than face-to-face classroom group (P = .014)."
