Blanchard 2015¶

The review reports Arnett's (2008) systematic analysis of six flagship APA journals, 2003–2007. It states "Strong imbalances are observed for each of the investigated features", with WEIRD totals of 97% first authors, 98% other authors and 95% samples, against 5–12% of world population.

"Strong imbalances are observed for each of the investigated features: 73 % of first authors, 74 % of other authors, and 68 % of samples are related to the USA."
