`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Instructor-rated GRS rubric scores from the skills test were analyzed against procedure duration and attempts without adjustment (SAS/STAT v.9.4, P ≤ 0.05); the article reports "a shorter procedure length was consistently associated with higher performance scores."

> "When evaluated using the GRS, a shorter procedure length was consistently associated with higher performance scores."
