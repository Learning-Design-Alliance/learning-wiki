Ikram M 2026¶

Thematic synthesis of the reviewed studies (Section 4.1). The review reports that AI facilitates real-time feedback, flexible content adjustment, gamification and continuous evaluation, and concludes "AI-enabled personalization benefits both academic performance and students’ motivation".

"Overall, the findings imply that AI-enabled personalization benefits both academic performance and students’ motivation to take an active role in their learning journey."
