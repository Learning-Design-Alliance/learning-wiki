## Evidence

### Tuncel 2019

Tuncel, İ. (2019). The effectiveness of intercultural sensitivity development activities integrated with scientific research methods course. European Journal of Educational Research, 8(1), 301-312. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.8.1.301

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Item-level pretest-posttest comparison for the experimental group (Table 7): items 9 and 20 moved from Agree to Disagree, and items on obtaining information and avoiding culturally-distinct persons moved from partially agree to strongly agree responses.

> "while students answered the scale items "I get upset easily when interacting with people from different cultures" and "I think my culture is better than other cultures" as "Agree" before the experiment, a significant cha nge was found in these answers after the experiment and the students answered these items as "disagree"."
