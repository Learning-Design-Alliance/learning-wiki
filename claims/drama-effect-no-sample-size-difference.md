## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Between-group homogeneity testing showed no statistically significant difference in effect sizes across sample-size groups (QB=2.075 < X2=5.991; p=0.354), though descriptively effect sizes fell from 1.267 (1–50 students) to 0.909 (65 and over). [→ Yildirim 2022](#yildirim-2022)

## Evidence

### Yildirim 2022

Yildirim, E., Tikman, F., & Senturk, M. (2022). Meta-analysis on the effectiveness of using the drama approach in social studies course. International Journal on Social and Education Sciences (IJonSES), 4(1), 17-33. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijonses.301

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis across 13 studies with 1–50 students (d = 1.267), 13 with 51–64 (d = 1.158), and 12 with 65 and over (d = 0.909). The test found "no statistically significant difference between the groups"; the authors interpret a slight decrease in effect sizes as sample size increases.

> "Since the homogeneity value of QB=2.075, calculated in this study, was smaller than the accepted critical value of 5.991 (QB=2.075<X2=5,991; p=0.354>.05), it could be asserted that there was no statistically significant difference between the groups."
