AlMarzooqi LM 2026¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's comparative synthesis (Theme 4) across 15 intervention studies, including one randomized controlled trial comparing a 3-year stepwise program with a traditional format. The review characterizes the pattern as "generally demonstrated" more durable improvements.

"Longitudinal, integrated programs that combined structured instruction with direct practice, formative feedback, and ongoing coaching generally demonstrated more durable improvements in teaching skills than stand-alone workshops."
