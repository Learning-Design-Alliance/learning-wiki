### Li Jialan 2025

Li Jialan, Wu Ningling, Lu Jianyun, Tong Xiaoliang, Gao Lihua, Zeng Jinrong. (2025). Implementation and evaluation of a Sim + LBL hybrid teaching model in dermatology and venereology: a prospective study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08440-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test comparison between the Sim + LBL (n=182) and LBL (n=164) cohorts on the same assessment. Median score differences of +6.0, +1.5, and +4.0 with Cliff's δ effects of medium (−0.389), small (−0.165), and large (−0.605) are reported in Table 2; δ used the LBL group as reference.

> "Students in the Sim + LBL group scored significantly higher than those in the LBL group across total, subjective, and case analysis measures (all P < 0.05)."
