### Cai Manyuan 2024

Cai Manyuan, Chen Yanping, Luo Fangting, Zheng Yanqun, Liu Ying, Xiao Bing, Wang Xiaoyan, Sun Lulu, Lin Yi, Zeng Xianhu, Tan Shuni, Liu Ke, Gu Yuanbo, Wang Jinghua, Ning Xianjia, Yuan Jing, Wen Min, Cao Jing. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate comparison in a cross-sectional study of 1916 medical staff at the Third People's Hospital of Shenzhen: "women exhibiting a higher qualification rate (74.0%) than men (65.0%)". Multivariate analysis reported OR 0.73 (95% CI: 0.56–0.96, P = 0.023) for women versus men.

> "Table 3 delineates a significant difference in qualification status based on sex (P < 0.001), with women exhibiting a higher qualification rate (74.0%) than men (65.0%)."
