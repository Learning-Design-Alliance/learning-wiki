The author's own survey of PREP program participants (Appendix, November 1998) asked how the all-female environment affected learning. Students "commented that the all-female atmosphere had made them more willing to speak out"; no effect size is reported.

> "These 1998 high school students, children of the generation that declares feminism is dead and tells us that we have destroyed all the barriers, told me that they felt the presence of boys in the classroom would inhibit their learning"
