### Amy Voss Farris 2016

Proceedings of the 12th International Conference of the Learning Sciences (ICLS 2016). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06963](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06963)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the acceleration modeling cycle, in which students recorded marbles rolling down acrylic tracks. Initial action-perspective videos made measurement impossible; after classroom-wide discussion the class "developed a norm" for good measurement videos, and students marked marble positions at regular frame intervals with flags.

> "the class developed a norm for what counts as a “good” video for measuring motion in a frame-by-frame analysis:  the camera has to stay still and the field of view must show the whole motion."
