## Evidence

### Cornillie 2015

Cornillie, F., & Desmet, P. (2015). Design and empirical evaluation of controlled L2 practice through mini-games—moving beyond drill-and-kill?. Critical CALL – Proceedings of the 2015 EUROCALL Conference, Padova, Italy. http://dx.doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2015.000325

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects experiment with one control group (N=61) and two treatment groups (N=125); treatment groups received mini-game practice on English quantifiers and the double object construction. The article reports higher accuracy and faster responses for treatment groups, with no effect size printed.

> "The data show that the treatment groups outperformed the control group on the post-tests of the TGJT in terms of accuracy rate and response times (see Figure 3)."
