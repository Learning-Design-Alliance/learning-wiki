Systematic scoping review search across six databases from inception to February 2026, screened by two independent reviewers. The review reports that "A total of 76 studies met the inclusion criteria" and that evidence was more extensive for physical activity exposures than nutrition-related variables.

> "A total of 76 studies met the inclusion criteria. The available evidence was more extensive for physical activity, sport participation, and fitness-related exposures than for nutrition-related variables or integrated lifestyle models."
