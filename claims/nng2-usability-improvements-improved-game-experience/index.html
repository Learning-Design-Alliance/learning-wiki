Evidence¶

Phuong Bui 2020¶

Phuong Bui, Gabriela Rodríguez-Aflecht, Boglárka Brezovszky, Minna M. Hannula-Sormunen, Samuli Laato, Erno Lehtinen. (2020). Understanding students' game experiences throughout the developmental process of the number navigation game. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09755-8

q2 · i1 · design · r3

Independent-samples t test comparing GEQ post-play ratings of students playing NNG 1 (N = 642, grades 4–6) and NNG 2 (N = 526) in the 2014 large-scale study. The article reports "effect sizes ranged from close to medium (d = 0.36) to close to large (d = 0.7)", with Competence non-significant (p = 0.509).

"Results from an independent t test showed that there were significant differences between game experiences in all dimensions, except Competence with effect sizes ranged from close to medium (d = 0.36) to close to large (d = 0.7)."
