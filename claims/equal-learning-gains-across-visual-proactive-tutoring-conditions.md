## Evidence

### Nachiket Kotalwar 2026

Nachiket Kotalwar, Conrad Borchers, Shubham Gandhi, Partha Sarathi Purkayastha, David Yaron, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2026). Exploring Visual Access and Proactive AI Tutoring for Handwritten Mathematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.26411)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized online study of 117 adults solving calculus problems with AI tutoring, pre-to-post paired t-test on the whole sample: paired t(116)=6.85, p<.001, d=0.63. The article reports "a significant increase of moderate size".

> "Across the whole sample, scores rose from 3.3 to 4.6 of nine, a significant increase of moderate size, and every condition improved (Table 4)."
