# Full HPSF schools outperformed partial HPSF schools (physical activity only) in mathematics from year 2 onward

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Full HPSF schools scored significantly higher in mathematics than partial HPSF schools in years 2-4, with medium to large effect sizes (0.47, 0.43, 0.86). [→ van Engelen Bo H. W. 2026](#van-engelen-bo-h-w-2026)

## Evidence

### van Engelen Bo H. W. 2026

van Engelen Bo H. W., Hahnraths Marla T. H., Winkens Bjorn, Schils Trudie, van Schayck Onno C. P. (2026). Food for thought? The effects of the Healthy Primary School of the Future on children's educational outcomes. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0334638

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MMRM analysis comparing full HPSF (lunch plus physical activity) with partial HPSF (physical activity only) schools on standardised mathematics tests, with printed effect sizes of "0.47, 0.43, and 0.86" for years 2, 3, and 4, the largest (ES = 0.86) exceeding the large-effect threshold.

> "The full HPSF group also showed a significant improvement in mathematics score compared to the partial HPSF group in years 2–4 (all p ≤ 0.006) with medium to large ES (0.47, 0.43, and 0.86 for years 2, 3, and 4, respectively)"
