## Evidence

### Götze K 2025

Götze K, Otten-Marré S, Loupatatzis B, in der Schmitten J. (2025). Training supported by simulated persons to promote the development of specific communication skills in advance care planning. GMS Journal for Medical Education 42(1):Doc11. https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001735

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative observations across five ACP facilitator qualifications in 2017 and 2018 by ten ACP coaches and three SP coaches; the authors report 'no indications that the professional actors were better able to cope with the requirements', with no effect size.

> "There were no indications that the professional actors were better able to cope with the requirements than were the well trained laypersons."

### Götze K 2025 (2)

Götze K, Otten-Marré S, Loupatatzis B, in der Schmitten J. (2025). Training supported by simulated persons to promote the development of specific communication skills in advance care planning. GMS Journal for Medical Education 42(1):Doc11. https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001735

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same observational work identified the qualities needed for SPs: spontaneous interaction, detailed feedback, and openness to the taboo subjects of death and dying, which 'has proven to be central to these interactions'. The article gives descriptive counts (observations from five ACP facilitator qualifications with ten ACP coaches and three SP coaches; participant groups of four) but no quantitative outcome or comparison data.

> "An openness to allow, endure and reflect on the taboo subjects of death and dying has proven to be central to these interactions."
