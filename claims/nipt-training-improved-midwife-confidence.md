## Evidence

### Oxenford K 2017

Oxenford K, Daley R, Lewis C, Hill M, Chitty LS. (2017). Development and evaluation of training resources to prepare health professionals for counselling pregnant women about non-invasive prenatal testing for Down syndrome: a mixed methods study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1315-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Questionnaire study of 381 training attendees (97% response rate) with 151 completing the one-month follow-up (39% response rate); Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the 13-item self-perceived confidence scale showed "a statistically significant improvement in the midwives confidence scores". No effect size was printed.

> "There was a statistically significant improvement in the midwives confidence scores between Q1 (Mdn = 7, IQR = 9) and Q3 (Mdn = 23, IQR = 3), Z = 10.027, p < 0.001"

### Oxenford K 2017 (2)

Oxenford K, Daley R, Lewis C, Hill M, Chitty LS. (2017). Development and evaluation of training resources to prepare health professionals for counselling pregnant women about non-invasive prenatal testing for Down syndrome: a mixed methods study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1315-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-training questionnaire (Q2) completed by 374 attendees (95% response rate) immediately after the 40-minute session; a descriptive finding reporting that 354 (96%) of attendees felt confident discussing NIPT, with no inferential test or effect size printed.

> "Following the training session analysis of Q2 showed 354 (96%) of attendees felt that they would feel confident in discussing NIPT with women in their clinic."
