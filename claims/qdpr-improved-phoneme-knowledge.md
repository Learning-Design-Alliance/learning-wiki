## Evidence

### Moedjito 2017

Moedjito. (2017). Improving EFL Learners' Pronunciation of English through Quiz-Demonstration-Practice-Revision (QDPR). English Language Teaching, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n1p10

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Published online 2 December 2017 (Crossref's issued date) in the volume 11(1) issue dated 2018. Paired-sample t-tests on pretest-posttest data from 31 student participants taught with QDPR over 11 sessions. Knowledge mean rose from 62.26 (SD 4.80) to 84.68 (SD 5.41); the article reports "t (df =30) = 22.07 at p < .01" for this variable. No standardized effect size is printed for the pretest-posttest change. The R² = .53 the article reports for knowledge comes from a separate simple regression on scores for the QDPR learning model, a correlational analysis (r = .73) that cannot show cause, and is not an effect size for this gain.

> "there was a significant difference in the mean scores between the pre-test and the post for both of the investigated variables, t (df =30) = 22.07 at p < .01 and t (df =30) = 44.17 at p < .01 respectively"
