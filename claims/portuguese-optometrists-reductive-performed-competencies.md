National questionnaire survey of the Portuguese optometric population (444 validated responses, 28.41% of the study population). Median procedure frequencies showed practice was "based on Refraction, Basic Ocular Health, Basic Contactology and School Age Paediatric Patient Assessment (>6 years)".
