## Evidence

### Sarican 2018

Sarican, G., Akgunduz, D. (2018). The impact of integrated STEM education on academic achievement, reflective thinking skills towards problem solving and permanence in learning in science education. Cypriot Journal of Educational Sciences, 13(1), 94-113. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1196043

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-test on RTSSPS permanence-test scores in the 44-student quasi-experiment: t = −1.028, p = 0.310. The article notes the experimental group's RTSSPS PT mean (55.45) was higher than the control group's (52.27) but not significantly.

> "However, no significant difference was found between the two groups in terms of the RTSSPS PT score (p > 0.05)."
