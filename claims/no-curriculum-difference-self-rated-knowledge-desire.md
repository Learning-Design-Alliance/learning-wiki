## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Self-ranked knowledge (41 vs 43) and desire to learn more (75.3 vs 75.3) did not differ significantly between curricula. [→ Fraser S 2019](#fraser-s-2019)

## Evidence

### Fraser S 2019

Fraser S, Wright AD, van Donkelaar P, Smirl JD. (2019). Cross-sectional comparison of spiral versus block integrated curriculums in preparing medical students to diagnose and manage concussions. BMC Medical Education 19(17). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1439-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey results (Table 3): spiral students scored 41 ± 22.9 and block students 43 ± 19.4 on self-rated knowledge; desire to learn was 75.3 in both groups; neither Mann-Whitney U test was significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "This was not statistically significant as determined by a Mann-Whitney U test (U = 2863, z = 0.511, p = 0.609). Similarly, when asked to rank their desire to learn more about concussions on a scale of 1-100, from Not at All to Very Much, students ranked 75.3 ± 24 and 75.3 ± 18.4 for the Spiral and Block curriculums, respectively (U = 2778.5, z = 0.187, p = 0.852)"
