## Evidence

### Ian M. Hoffman 2011

Ian M. Hoffman. (2011). The Buddy Quiz: A collaborative assessment and a representation of the scientific enterprise. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/47/1/52](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/47/1/52)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive implementation report of two sections: Section X had 11 students (four pairs and one trio) and Section Y had 14 students (four trios and one pair). Grades comprised three equal parts: individual 55-minute exams, Buddy Quizzes, and individual lab reports; grades were not curved.
