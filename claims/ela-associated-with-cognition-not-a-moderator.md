## Evidence

### Corley E 2023

Corley E, Fahey L, Fitzgerald J, Holleran L, Walton E, Morris DW, Donohoe G. (2023). The impact of early adversity and education on genetic and brain morphological predictors of cognitive ability. Genes, Brain and Behavior. https://doi.org/10.1111/gbb.12850

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation SEM (Model 2) in the same UK Biobank sample, explaining 15% of cognitive ability variance. ELA showed direct negative associations with GMV and cognition, but "ELA was not a significant moderator in this moderated mediation model".

> "a direct negative association was found between ELA and GMV (β = −0.062, SE = 0.010 p < 0.001) and between ELA and cognitive ability (β = −0.077, SE = 0.006, p < 0.001). Contrary to our hypothesis, ELA was not a significant moderator in this moderated mediation model (indirect effect β = −0.003, 95% CIs = −0.021; 0.014, SE = 0.009, p = 0.714)"
