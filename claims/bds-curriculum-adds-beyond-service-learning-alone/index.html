Building Decision Skills 2006¶

Building Decision Skills. (2006). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse. https://www.globalethics.org/services/edu/bds.htm

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The WWC reports (footnote to Appendix A3) that Leming (2001) also compared the combined condition with a service-learning-only condition; differences were not found for acceptance of ethical responsibility, social responsibility within the school or in society, self-esteem, or anticipated future community participation.

"In that comparison, differences favoring the curriculum plus service-learning were reported for ethical awareness and ethical perspective in comparison with service-learning alone."
