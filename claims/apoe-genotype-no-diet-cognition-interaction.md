## Evidence

### Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020

Keenan Tiarnán D., Agrón Elvira, Mares Julie A., Clemons Traci E., van Asten Freekje, Swaroop Anand, Chew Emily Y.; AREDS and AREDS2 Research Groups. (2020). Adherence to a Mediterranean diet and cognitive function in the Age-Related Eye Disease Studies 1 & 2. Alzheimer's & Dementia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8328271/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Genotype interaction analysis in AREDS2 longitudinal models testing aMED by APOE haplotype for rate of cognitive decline. The article reports "no significant interactions were observed between aMED and APOE haplotype, in terms of rate of decline".

> "However, no significant interactions were observed between aMED and APOE haplotype, in terms of rate of decline (P-value for interaction = 0.80 for TICS-M and P-value for interaction = 0.51 for the composite)."

### Keenan Tiarnán D. 2020 (2)

Keenan Tiarnán D., Agrón Elvira, Mares Julie A., Clemons Traci E., van Asten Freekje, Swaroop Anand, Chew Emily Y.; AREDS and AREDS2 Research Groups. (2020). Adherence to a Mediterranean diet and cognitive function in the Age-Related Eye Disease Studies 1 & 2. Alzheimer's & Dementia. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8328271/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional interaction analysis in both AREDS and AREDS2 testing fish intake by APOE haplotype for cognitive impairment. The article reports "no significant interactions were observed".

> "In analyses of interactions between fish intake and APOE haplotype, for the outcome of cognitive impairment, no significant interactions were observed."
