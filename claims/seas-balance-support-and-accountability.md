# SEA staff describe their role as providing both support and accountability to LEAs, and describe struggling to balance the two.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` SEA leaders report providing both support and accountability to districts, a balance made harder as the SEA role has shifted from monitoring outcomes to providing resources and technical assistance. [→ Dunn 2019](#dunn-2019)

## Evidence

### Dunn 2019

Dunn, L., & Ambroso, E. (2019). Balancing act: State and district roles in school improvement under ESSA. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED602966

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview finding across the 10 SEAs. Vermont leaders emphasized "forging relationships with LEA leaders and creating a safe environment in which people can be honest about school improvement efforts"; South Carolina prioritized clear expectations with support to meet them.

> "SEA staff described their role as providing both support and accountability to LEAs. Several SEA leaders described the struggle of balancing these two roles"
