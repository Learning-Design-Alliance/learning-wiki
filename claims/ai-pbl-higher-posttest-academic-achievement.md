## Evidence

### Ayanwale 2026

Ayanwale, M. A., & Omeh, C. B. (2026). AI-supported problem-based learning for enhancing computational thinking skills in STEM education. Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans 7 (2026) 100263. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2026.100263

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANCOVA on Post_Academic_Achievement with instructional group as independent variable and Pre_Academic_Achievement and gender as covariates, in the 103-student quasi-experiment. The adjusted mean for the experimental group (M = 76.10, SE = 1.33) exceeded the control group (M = 49.98, SE = 1.27), "partial η2 = .741".

> "The analysis indicated a statistically significant main effect of instructional group, F (1, 70) = 200.67, p < .001, partial η2 = .741 (see Table 2a), suggesting a very strong effect"
