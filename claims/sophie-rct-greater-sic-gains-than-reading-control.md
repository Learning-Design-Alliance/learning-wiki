# In a randomized controlled trial, SOPHIE training produced significantly greater pre-post gains in serious illness communication skills than a written reading module, with large effect sizes for the SOPHIE arm

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Unpaired t-tests on pre-post change scores showed the SOPHIE group improved significantly more than control on Empower (d = 0.85), Be Explicit (d = 0.87), Empathy (d = 0.59), and Overall (d = 0.92). [→ Kurtis Haut 2025](#kurtis-haut-2025)
`q3 i?` Inter-rater reliability among blinded third-party reviewers was strong (ICC = 0.882), supporting the validity of the outcome ratings. [→ Kurtis Haut 2025](#kurtis-haut-2025)

## Evidence

### Kurtis Haut 2025

Kurtis Haut, Masam Hasan, Thomas Carroll, Ronald Epstein, Taylan Sen, Ehsan Hoque. (2025). AI Standardized Patient Improves Human Conversations in Advanced Cancer Care. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.02694](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.02694)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial with 51 healthcare students and professionals (SOPHIE n=26, control n=25); 102 standardized patient conversations each rated by five raters. The between-arm comparison of change scores showed "significant improvements across Empower" and the other 3E skills with the printed Cohen's d values.

> "Unpaired t-tests between Control and SOPHIE group ∆ show significant improvements across Empower (p = 0.004, Cohens d = 0.85), Be Explicit ( p = 0.003, Cohens d = 0.87), Empathy (p = 0.002, Cohens d = 0.59), and Overall ( p = 0.002, Cohens d = 0.92)."
