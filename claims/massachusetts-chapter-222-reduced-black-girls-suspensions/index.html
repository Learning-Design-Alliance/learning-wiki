“...and they cared”: How to create better 2020¶

Ed Trust calculations from SSDR data retrieved from the Massachusetts Department of Education show "from 16 to 9 suspensions per 100 Black girls" after Chapter 222 required educators to try alternatives first. No effect size is printed.
