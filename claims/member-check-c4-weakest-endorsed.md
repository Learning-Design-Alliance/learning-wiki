Through the WordStream Glass: Revisiting Quantitative Encoding for Qualitative Learning Analytics. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18692 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.18692](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.18692)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Member-checking step in Cycle 2 of the thematic analysis, where five confirmatory statements returned candidate themes to the six new participants. The weak endorsement of C4 led the authors to drop the initial uncertainty/confidence theme and highlighted the deeper disagreement of Theme 4.

> "Confirmatory item C4 (distrust when functions evoke uncer- tainty) was the weakest-endorsed of the five: among the six Cycle 2 respondents, only one agreed, one disagreed, and four were neu- tral, with several reframing what “distrust” meant"
