# Statistical learning in the ASRT task increases during practice periods and decreases during rest periods

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Statistical learning increased during practice in both ASRT experiments (Experiment 2 d = 0.34; Experiment 3 d = 1.10). [→ Romain Quentin 2021](#romain-quentin-2021)
`q3 i?` Statistical learning decreased during rest periods in both ASRT experiments. [→ Romain Quentin 2021 (2)](#romain-quentin-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Romain Quentin 2021

Romain Quentin, Lison Fanuel, Mariann Kiss, Marine Vernet, Teodóra Vékony, Karolina Janacsek, Leonardo G. Cohen and Dezso Nemeth. (2021). Statistical learning occurs during practice while high-order rule learning during rest period. npj Science of Learning 6:14. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00093-9

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Results of two ASRT experiments (Experiment 2: 180 participants, one session; Experiment 3: 25 participants, 8 weekly sessions). The article reports that "statistical learning increased during practice" with the printed t and d values.

> "statistical learning increased during practice (Experiment 2: Monline = 5.22 ± 15.27 s, t(179) = 4.58, p <1 0−5, d = 0.34; Experiment 3: Monline = 7.37 ± 6.70 s, t(179) = 5.40, p <1 0−4, d = 1.10)"

### Romain Quentin 2021 (2)

Romain Quentin, Lison Fanuel, Mariann Kiss, Marine Vernet, Teodóra Vékony, Karolina Janacsek, Leonardo G. Cohen and Dezso Nemeth. (2021). Statistical learning occurs during practice while high-order rule learning during rest period. npj Science of Learning 6:14. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00093-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same ASRT experiments, offline measure computed as the difference between the first bin of a block and the last bin of the previous block. The article reports statistical learning "decreased during rest periods" with printed statistics; no standardized effect size is printed for this contrast as a whole.

> "and decreased during rest periods (Experiment 2: Mofﬂine = −5.06 ± 13.97 s, t(179) = −4.85, p <1 0−5, d = 0.36; Experiment 3: Mofﬂine = −5.05 ± 13.97 s, t(179) = 4.84, p <1 0−4, d = 0.98)"
