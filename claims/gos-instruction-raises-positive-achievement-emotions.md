## Evidence

### İlter 2016

İlter, İ. (2016). The Power of Graphic Organizers: Effects on Students' Word-Learning and Achievement Emotions in Social Studies. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 41(1). http://ro.ecu.edu.au/ajte/vol41/iss1/3

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental nonequivalent control group pretest-posttest study with fourth-graders (experimental N=23, control N=25) in Bayburt, Turkey; AEQ posttest ANOVA showed enjoyment (F=7.01), hope (F=9.77) and pride (F=7.21) significantly higher for the GOs group, "small to large (d= .23 to .45)".

> "the results demonstrated that positive emotions such as enjoyment, hope and pride were significantly higher in the experimental group than in the comparison group after the treatment (p <.05). Effect sizes for the differences between the two groups were small to large (d= .23 to .45"
