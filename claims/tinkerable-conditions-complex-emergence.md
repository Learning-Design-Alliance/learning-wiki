`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Authors' interpretive claim in Section 6.1, drawing on complexity research (referring readers to Davis & Sumara, 2006) that some necessary conditions for complex emergence are manipulable in social settings, unlike conditions such as feedback loops or reproductive instability.

> "However, within the sorts of complex unities that concern educators and policy-makers, a few conditions can be tinkered with effectively. These include internal diversity, internal redundancy, decentralized agency and control, and neighbor interactions."
