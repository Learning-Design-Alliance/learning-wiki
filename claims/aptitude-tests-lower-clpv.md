## Evidence

### McManus IC 2013

McManus IC, Dewberry Chris, Nicholson Sandra, Dowell Jonathan S, Woolf Katherine, Potts Henry WW. (2013). Construct-level predictive validity of educational attainment and intellectual aptitude tests in medical student selection: meta-regression of six UK longitudinal studies. BMC Medicine. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3827328/

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-regression of nine aptitude-test CLPVs from three cohorts. The article reports the overall mean .208 (CI .113–.299, t = 4.89, P < .00001) and states in the abstract that "Intellectual aptitude tests have lower CLPVs than A-levels or GCSEs/O-levels".

> "Nine construct-level predictive validities were available for aptitude tests, two from the Westminster Cohort (AH5), six from the 1990 Cohort (aAH5), and one from UKCAT-12 (UKCAT total score), with a highly significant effect overall (mean = .208; CI .113 to .299, t = 4.89, 9df, P < .00001)."
