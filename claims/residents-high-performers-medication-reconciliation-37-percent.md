## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a 15-minute standardized patient discharge counseling encounter, only 37.2% of 226 residents were considered high performers in medication reconciliation. [→ Ramjaun Aliya 2015](#ramjaun-aliya-2015)

## Evidence

### Ramjaun Aliya 2015

Ramjaun Aliya, Sudarshan Monisha, Patakfalvi Laura, Tamblyn Robyn, Meguerditchian Ari N. (2015). Educating medical trainees on medication reconciliation: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4373246/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Experimental standardized patient encounter (n=226) in which residents counseled a soon-to-be discharged patient and SPs evaluated performance quality; 79% found the encounter realistic and 61% reported increased confidence, yet "only 37.2% of the residents were considered 'high performers'".

> "Although most residents (79%) felt that the encounter was realistic and 61% reported increased confidence as a result of the exercise, only 37.2% of the residents were considered 'high performers' in carrying out effective medication reconciliation"

### Ramjaun Aliya 2015 (2)

Ramjaun Aliya, Sudarshan Monisha, Patakfalvi Laura, Tamblyn Robyn, Meguerditchian Ari N. (2015). Educating medical trainees on medication reconciliation: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4373246/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Comparison across residency programs within the same standardized patient study: "Residents from programs with primary care component had more high performers (p=0.031)" in the medication reconciliation domain, per the review's Table 1.

> "Residents from programs with primary care component had more high performers (p=0.031)"
