## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a multivariable regression model, current GPA (B = 0.39, p < 0.001) and knowledge test scores (B = 0.20, p < 0.001) were associated with the final statistics score, while learning method was not (B = 0.15, p = 0.124). [→ Milic Natasa M. 2016](#milic-natasa-m-2016)
