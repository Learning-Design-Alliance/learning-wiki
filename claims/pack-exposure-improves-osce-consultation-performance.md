## Evidence

### Mash R 2017

Mash R, Pather M, Rhode H, Fairall L. (2017). Evaluating the effect of the Practical Approach to Care Kit on teaching medical students primary care: Quasi-experimental study. Afr J Prm Health Care Fam Med. https://doi.org/10.4102/phcfm.v9i1.1602

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 75 first-semester versus 83 second-semester students on an 11-station OSCE using the same stations in both examinations, analysed with the Mann–Whitney U test because data were not normally distributed. The mean grade was "significantly better (p < 0.001)" with PACK.

> "The mean grade for the OSCE was significantly better (p < 0.001) in those with the PACK (64.4%, SD 7.0) compared to those without (58.8%, SD 10.9)."
