# Feedback content and process differences between formats held after adjusting for feedback length, but several process items showed no significant difference between formats

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2016 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Content and process differences persisted after adjustment for feedback duration, e.g., elaboration on clinical reasoning (adjusted delta p=0.007) and structure of feedback (adjusted p=0.002). [→ Junod Perron Noëlle 2016](#junod-perron-noelle-2016)
`q2 i?` Several process and content items showed no significant difference between formats, including history taking (adjusted p=0.076), physical exam items (p=0.476), empathy (p=0.832), pedagogical effectiveness (p=0.216), and students' ratings of usefulness of the session (p=0.529). [→ Junod Perron Noëlle 2016 (2)](#junod-perron-noelle-2016-2)
