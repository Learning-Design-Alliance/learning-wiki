Evidence¶

Juan C. Castro-Alonso 2024¶

Juan C. Castro-Alonso, Paul Ayres, Shirong Zhang, Björn B. de Koning, Fred Paas. (2024). Research Avenues Supporting Embodied Cognition in Learning and Instruction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09847-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Beege et al. (2020), two experiments (N = 229, 80% females) comparing observation of a male lecturer making meaningful pointing gestures versus less meaningful beat gestures in instructional videos. No effect size is printed.

"Both experiments showed that retention test scores were higher in the groups of students learning from meaningful pointing gesturing, compared to beat gesturing."
