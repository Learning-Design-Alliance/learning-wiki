Evidence¶

Victoria Menzies 2015¶

Victoria Menzies, Clare Collyer, Dimitra Kokotsaki, Catherine Hewitt, Kirsty Younger, Andy Wiggins and Carole Torgerson. (2015). SHINE in Secondaries: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581429

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-specified subgroup analysis repeating the primary RDD analysis for FSM eligibility, including an FSM-by-group interaction for each school. The report states it "showed no evidence of the intervention differentially benefiting pupils eligible for free school meals"; no effect size is printed, so the null is not an equivalence finding.

"A subgroup analysis of children eligible for free school meals (FSM) was conducted. This showed no evidence of the intervention differentially benefiting pupils eligible for free school meals in any of the schools."
