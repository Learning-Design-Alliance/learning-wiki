## Evidence

### Molenaar 2011

Molenaar, I., Chiu, M. M., Sleegers, P., & van Boxtel, C. (2011). Scaffolding of small groups' metacognitive activities with an avatar. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 6, 601-624. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-011-9130-z

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel mediation tests (Krull and MacKinnon) on metacognitive knowledge: coefficients reduced by 86% and 77%, with printed mediation correlations r=.39 and r=.50 (r=.50 exceeds .37, hence impact 3). A separate test showed a 35% reduction for problematizing scaffolds on domain knowledge.

> "Controlling for individual proportion of metacognitive actions reduced the structuring scaffold and problematizing scaffold conditions ' regression coefficients by 86% and 77% respectively (multilevel mediation tests: z=2.02; p<.05;"
