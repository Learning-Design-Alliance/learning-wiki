Yakymchuk 2018¶

Yakymchuk, N. V., Kazachenok, V. V. (2018). Developing Cognitive Independence of Future Informatics Teachers by Multimedia Tools. European Journal of Contemporary Education, 7(3), 581-597. https://doi.org/10.13187/ejced.2018.3.581

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The forming experiment compared the coefficient of complete content mastery across stages (Table 6: EG 53, 63, 72 vs CG 52, 57, 59). The article reports "t = 0.51" at stage 1 and "t = 2.62" and "t = 4.54" at stages 2 and 3 against a table value of 1.97.

"As a result of the calculations using the cognitive independence indicators for the first stage, we obtained t = 0.51. In this case, the table value t (tab) = 1.97 with a significance level of 0.05. For the second stage, we received t = 2.62, and for the third stage, t = 4.54"
