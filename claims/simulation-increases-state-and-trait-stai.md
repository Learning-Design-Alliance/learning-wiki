## Evidence

### Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al 2025

Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al. (2025). Pupilometer efficacy in monitoring anxiety in undergraduate medical students during high-fidelity clinical simulation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-95290-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post comparison on the STAI state subscale in the 61-student trial (Table 1 prints p < 0.001). The article reports the median rising from "56 points (IQR: 49–63)" to "66 points (IQR: 58–71)".

> "The median pretest score was 56 points (IQR: 49–63), whereas it was 66 points (IQR: 58–71) postsimulation (17% increase)."

### Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al 2025 (2)

Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al. (2025). Pupilometer efficacy in monitoring anxiety in undergraduate medical students during high-fidelity clinical simulation. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-95290-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' report of the trait subscale result in the same trial; Table 1 prints trait medians of 41 to 47 points with p < 0.001. The discussion calls this increase unexpected, since trait anxiety theoretically should not change.

> "Indeed, this was the stressful response induced by clinical simulation in which the trait subscale scores, presimulation vs. postsimulation, even increased significantly."
