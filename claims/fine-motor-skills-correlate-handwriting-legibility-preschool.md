# Fine motor precision and manual dexterity are positively correlated with handwriting legibility in preschool children, and Functional Dexterity Test time is inversely correlated

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Handwriting legibility correlates significantly with fine motor precision (r=0.78). [→ Seo S-M 2018](#seo-s-m-2018)
`q3 i3` Handwriting legibility correlates significantly with manual dexterity (r=0.49). [→ Seo S-M 2018 (2)](#seo-s-m-2018-2)
`q3 i3` Handwriting legibility shows a statistically significant inverse correlation with Functional Dexterity Test performance time (r=−0.68). [→ Seo S-M 2018 (3)](#seo-s-m-2018-3)

## Evidence

### Seo S-M 2018

Seo S-M. (2018). The effect of fine motor skills on handwriting legibility in preschool age children. J. Phys. Ther. Sci. 30: 324–327. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson product-moment correlation analysis in 52 preschool children (mean age 69.19 ± 3.71 months) between Korean Alphabet Writing Assessment scores and BOT-2 fine motor precision. The study reports a "statistically significant correlation with fine motor precision (r=0.78)".

> "handwriting legibility had a statistically significant correlation with fine motor precision (r=0.78) and a high correlation with manual dexterity (r=0.49)"

### Seo S-M 2018 (2)

Seo S-M. (2018). The effect of fine motor skills on handwriting legibility in preschool age children. J. Phys. Ther. Sci. 30: 324–327. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same Pearson correlation analysis of 52 preschool children, for the BOT-2 manual dexterity sub-item against handwriting legibility, reporting "a high correlation with manual dexterity (r=0.49)".

> "handwriting legibility had a statistically significant correlation with fine motor precision (r=0.78) and a high correlation with manual dexterity (r=0.49)"

### Seo S-M 2018 (3)

Seo S-M. (2018). The effect of fine motor skills on handwriting legibility in preschool age children. J. Phys. Ther. Sci. 30: 324–327. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation in the same 52-child sample between handwriting legibility and Functional Dexterity Test time (mean 32.98 ± 5.19 sec), showing "a statistically significant inverse correlation" with r=−0.68; faster in-hand manipulation related to better legibility.

> "It also showed a statistically significant inverse correlation with the performance time of the Functional Dexterity Test (r=−0.68)"
