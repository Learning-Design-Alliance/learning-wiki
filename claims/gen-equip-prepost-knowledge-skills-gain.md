## Evidence

### Jackson L 2018

Jackson L, O'Connor A, Paneque M, Curtisova V, Lunt PW, Pourova RK, Macek M Jr, Stefansdottir V, Turchetti D, Campos M, Henneman L, Godino L, Skirton H, Cornel MC. (2018). The Gen-Equip Project: evaluation and impact of genetics e-learning resources for primary care in six European languages. Genetics in Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0132-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phase 1 pre/post intervention test of registered learners across nine modules, analyzed with paired samples t tests. The article reports "the average pretest score was 66.7%" rising to 89.9% posttest, significant at p < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "Combining data from all modules (N = 423) the average pretest score was 66.7% and the average posttest score was 89.9% with a paired t test significance of p < 0.001."

### Jackson L 2018 (2)

Jackson L, O'Connor A, Paneque M, Curtisova V, Lunt PW, Pourova RK, Macek M Jr, Stefansdottir V, Turchetti D, Campos M, Henneman L, Godino L, Skirton H, Cornel MC. (2018). The Gen-Equip Project: evaluation and impact of genetics e-learning resources for primary care in six European languages. Genetics in Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0132-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phase 1 module-level paired t tests: all modules significant except the child with a genetic condition module (printed p = 0.526), attributed by the authors to a too-small sample because it went live last. No effect sizes are printed.

> "In every module a statistically significant increase in scores was observed apart from the module on a child with a genetic condition, where the sample size was too small, due to being the last module to go live and only having a few days available to learners."
