`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative comparison of pre- and postcourse map presentations. The article reports the equity terms appeared in "Three of eight postcourse maps" and suggests their origin in a course reading on inclusive classrooms.

> "GTAs included the terms “bias” and “equity” in the postcourse maps, which were not included in the precourse maps, and may have been a result of discussing a paper on inclusive classrooms."
