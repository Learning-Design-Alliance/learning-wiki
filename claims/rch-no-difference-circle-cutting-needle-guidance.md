# No significant difference between RCH and human camera assistant groups in reaching competency in circle cutting or needle guidance tasks

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Circle cutting competency showed no significant difference (P = 0.18), with only four HCA and two RCH participants gaining competency. [→ Amin Mohammad S. A. 2020](#amin-mohammad-s-a-2020)

## Evidence

### Amin Mohammad S. A. 2020

Amin Mohammad S. A., Aydin Abdullatif, Abbud Nurhan, Van Cleynenbreugel Ben, Veneziano Domenico, Somani Bhaskar, Gözen Ali Serdar, Redorta Juan Palou, Khan M. Shamim, Dasgupta Prokar, Makanjuoala Jonathan, Ahmed Kamran. (2020). Evaluation of a remote-controlled laparoscopic camera holder for basic laparoscopic skills acquisition: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-07899-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the trial's circle cutting task, both groups experienced widespread plateaus; "Only four participants of the HCA group gained competency compared to the two of the RCH group," a non-significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "Only four participants of the HCA group gained competency compared to the two of the RCH group. This difference was not statistically significant (P = 0.18)"

### Amin Mohammad S. A. 2020 (2)

Amin Mohammad S. A., Aydin Abdullatif, Abbud Nurhan, Van Cleynenbreugel Ben, Veneziano Domenico, Somani Bhaskar, Gözen Ali Serdar, Redorta Juan Palou, Khan M. Shamim, Dasgupta Prokar, Makanjuoala Jonathan, Ahmed Kamran. (2020). Evaluation of a remote-controlled laparoscopic camera holder for basic laparoscopic skills acquisition: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-07899-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the needle guidance task both groups progressed rapidly; "There was no significant difference in the RCH gaining competency by 4.7 mean sessions compared to a mean of 5 sessions of the HCA"; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in the RCH gaining competency by 4.7 mean sessions compared to a mean of 5 sessions of the HCA (P = 0.32)"
