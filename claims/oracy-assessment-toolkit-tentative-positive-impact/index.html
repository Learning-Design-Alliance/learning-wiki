Evidence¶

Maxwell 2015¶

Maxwell, B., Burnett, C., Reidy, J., Willis, B., & Demack, S. (2015). Oracy Curriculum, Culture and Assessment Toolkit: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581146

q2 · i? · design · r3

The University of Cambridge used its pilot Oracy Assessment Toolkit with 12 School 21 Year 7 students and 12 comparison-school students on a pre and post basis spanning five months, comparing mean task ratings. The report calls this only "a tentative indication" of positive impact; no effect size is printed.

"However, tests undertaken by the University of Cambridge using the prototype Oracy Assessment Toolkit developed for this project provided a tentative indication that the intervention had a positive impact on students' oracy skills."
