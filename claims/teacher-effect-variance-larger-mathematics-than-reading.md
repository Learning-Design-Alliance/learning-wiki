## Evidence

### Nye 2004

Nye, B., Konstantopoulos, S., & Hedges, L. V. (2004). How Large Are Teacher Effects? Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 26(3), 237-257. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=How+Large+Are+Teacher+Effects

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of variance components from the randomized HLM analyses of mathematics and reading gains and status in Project STAR. The authors offer the interpretation that mathematics is mostly learned in school and thus may be more directly influenced by teachers, with reading learned partly outside school. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "It is also interesting that across all grades, the variance of the teacher effects in mathematics is much larger than that in reading."
