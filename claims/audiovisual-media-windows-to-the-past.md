## Evidence

### Gómez Galán 2018

Gómez Galán, J. (2018). Learning Historical and Chronological Time: Practical Applications. European Journal of Science and Theology, 12(1), 5-16. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1803.01680](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.01680)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Authors' interpretive argument (Section 3): computer-generated reconstructions of the past, as in Jurassic Park and historical films and video games, multiply possibilities for approaching the past. The article adds these possibilities are "also very dangerous" and require rigorous pedagogical examination; no empirical data are reported.

> "today it is possible to have access to, as we said previously, genuine windows to the past  children can look through in different ways: movies, web pages, computer programs, video games, etc."
