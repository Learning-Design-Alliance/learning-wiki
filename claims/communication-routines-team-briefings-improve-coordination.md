Finding from the qualitative thematic analysis (Theme 2). Participants initially reported persistent communication challenges between professional groups, one-way communication about joint patient treatment, and nurses being excluded from decision-making.

> "Over time, the clinics introduced clearer communication routines, including regular team briefings and increased nurse involvement in meetings. These adjustments helped address earlier gaps and promoted more coordinated care."
