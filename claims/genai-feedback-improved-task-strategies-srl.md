# The GenAI feedback group showed a statistically significant post-intervention improvement in the Task Strategies dimension of SRL compared with the tutor group

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Post-intervention Task Strategies scores favoured the treatment group with a moderate effect size (p = .044, r = .35), with no baseline difference. [→ Yilmaz 2026](#yilmaz-2026)

## Evidence

### Yilmaz 2026

Yilmaz, M., Temur, H. B., Emmungil, L., Çelik, E., Gauthier, A., & Cukurova, M. (2026). Supporting self-regulated learning through generative AI feedback in online higher education: the importance of student perceptions of the source of feedback. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 23(16). https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00592-y

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U test comparing standardised trace-based SRL proxy scores (Ye & Pennisi framework, ~48,000 MOODLE log records) between GenAI (n = 23) and tutor (n = 23) groups; pre-scores showed no significant differences, and post Task Strategies favoured the treatment group.

> "However, a significant difference was found in post scores for the Task Strategies skill, favouring the treatment group and yielding a moderate effect size ( p = .044, r = .35)."
