Follow-up multilevel models in the intention-to-treat sample showed no significant group differences on the three motivational measures; printed values include "d = .164", "d = .205" and "d = −.043". Equivalence was not tested.

> "No statistically significant differences were found at follow‐up between groups for math self‐efficacy (B = .695, p = .132, d = .164), math utility value (B = .664, p = .100, d = .205) or STEM vocational interest (B = −.122, p = .803, d = −.043)."
