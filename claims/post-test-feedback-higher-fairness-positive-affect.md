`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Planned contrasts against the Summative Test Profile group. Table 4 prints for contrast 3 (PR vs. TP) "0.59 3.89 264 <.001*** .23" and contrast 4 (PE vs. TP) "0.44 2.88 264 .004** .18", giving Pearson's r = .23 and r = .18.
