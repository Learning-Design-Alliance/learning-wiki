Reinhard Pekrun 2022¶

Reinhard Pekrun, Herbert W. Marsh, Felix Suessenbach, Anne C. Frenzel, Thomas Goetz. (2022). School grades and students' emotions: Longitudinal models of within-person reciprocal effects. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101626

Comparison of the present RI-CLPM results with the earlier CLPM analysis of the same PALMA dataset. The article prints medians of "|r| = 0.46" for random-intercept correlations versus "|0.34|" for the undecomposed correlations; no standardized effect size for the difference is printed.

"The average (median) of the RI-CLPM correlations between random intercepts was |r| = 0.46 (Table 3). The average (median) of the undecomposed correlations was |0.34| (Pekrun et al., 2017, Table 1)."
