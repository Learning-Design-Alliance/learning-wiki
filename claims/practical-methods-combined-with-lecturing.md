## Evidence

### Lörzing R 2025

Lörzing R, Bartelmeß T. (2025). Enhancing sustainability competencies: A systematic review of educational interventions for dietitians and nutrition professionals. Nutrition & Dietetics. https://doi.org/10.1111/1747-0080.70046

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's narrative analysis of the 19 included interventions observes that less effective methods "were consistently combined with at least one practical, community‐oriented approach"; this is the review's own interpretive synthesis rather than a tested comparison.

> "Analysis of the identified interventions indicates that these methods were consistently combined with at least one practical, community‐oriented approach, including project and/or problem‐based learning, place‐based environmental education or community service learning, ensuring a more comprehensive learning experience."
