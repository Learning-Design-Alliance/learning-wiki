## Evidence

### Miners Alec 2012

Miners Alec, Harris Jody, Felix Lambert, Murray Elizabeth, Michie Susan, Edwards Phil. (2012). An economic evaluation of adaptive e-learning devices to promote weight loss via dietary change for people with obesity. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-12-190

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Discrete event simulation economic model (e-LEEM) run to a lifetime horizon for eight patient starting-characteristic scenarios (Table 3). In scenario A the e-LD cost £5,646 versus £4,884 for CC, and "The lowest reported ICER was approximately £102,000 per additional QALY (scenario A)."
