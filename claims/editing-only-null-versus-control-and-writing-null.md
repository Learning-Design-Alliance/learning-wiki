## Evidence

### Thomas-Tate et al. 2013

Shurita Thomas-Tate, Carol McDonald Connor, & Lakeisha Johnson. (2013). Design Experiments: Developing and Testing an Intervention for Elementary School-age Students who use Non-mainstream American English Dialects. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563056

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null and trend findings from the randomized design experiment's pairwise analyses. The writing-sample null means equivalence was not tested; the article adds that means suggest the editing + dialect awareness "condition used fewer target Home English forms than did students in the other conditions".

> "Students in the editing alone intervention condition did not perform significantly differently than the control group except on the editing task, where there was a trend (mean difference = 9.39, p = .095). There were no significant differences among groups with regard to frequency of target intervention features used in the writing sample"
