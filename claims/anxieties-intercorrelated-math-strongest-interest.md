## Evidence

### Pizzie 2025

Pizzie, R. G., Sortino, R. M., Kim, C. E.-Y., & Inghram, R. (2025). Math anxiety and science anxiety are associated with spatial cognition and STEM interest in deaf, hard of hearing, and hearing people. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00336-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation-strength Z-tests (Table 1) showed math anxiety's relation with STEM interest (r = -0.61) was significantly stronger than science anxiety's (Z(115) = 2.40, p = 0.01) and spatial anxiety's (Z(115) = 2.74, p = 0.006). The article reports "math anxiety was most strongly related to STEM interest".

> "math anxiety was most strongly related to STEM interest (r = −0.61), and this relation was almost twice the size of the correlations between STEM interest and science anxiety ( r = −0.37, Z(115) = 2.40, p = 0.01) and spatial anxiety (r = −0.33, Z(115) = 2.74, p = 0.006)."
