## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2006

What Works Clearinghouse. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Facing History and Ourselves. U.S. Department of Education. www.facinghistory.org. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/169

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

WWC review of the evidence base: seven studies failed screens for lacking valid or reliable outcome measures, lacking a comparison group, or lacking a quantitative design. The single qualifying study included 346 eighth-grade students in 14 FHAO and 8 comparison classrooms in the northeastern U.S.

> "Eight studies reviewed by WWC investigated the effects of the Facing History and Ourselves program. One study (Schultz, Barr, & Selman, 2001) was a quasi-experimental design that met WWC evidence standards with reservations."
