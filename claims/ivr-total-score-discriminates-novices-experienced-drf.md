## Evidence

### Jacobsen Mads Emil 2026

Jacobsen Mads Emil, Ghidinelli Monica, Subramaniam Chitra, Hare Kristoffer Borbjerg, Nayahangan Leizl Joy, Konge Lars, Gustafsson Amandus. (2026). Assessment of surgical skills on a novel expert-informed immersive virtual reality simulator for distal radius fracture fixation: a cross-sectional validation study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08983-5

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional validation study at two Danish sites: 12 first-year orthopedic residents (novices) and 11 experienced surgeons each completed one distal radius fracture fixation in the iVR simulator. The composite score showed "Cohen's d = 4.2 (CI 2.7 to 5.7)" between groups.

> "Novices had a mean total simulator score of -1.6 points (CI -12.3 to 9.2), while experienced surgeons scored 61.9 points (CI 53.6 to 70.3), p < .001, Cohen's d = 4.2 (CI 2.7 to 5.7)."
