# Teacher-student relationships are the strongest interpersonal predictor of hedonic, occupational, and overall teacher wellbeing

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Teacher-student relationships showed the strongest interpersonal associations with hedonic (ρ = .55), occupational (ρ = .60), and overall wellbeing (ρ = .51), while perceived student disruptive behavior was negatively associated (−.32 to −.42). [→ Sijing Zhou 2024](#sijing-zhou-2024)

## Evidence

### Sijing Zhou 2024

Sijing Zhou, Gavin R. Slemp & Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick. (2024). Factors Associated with Teacher Wellbeing: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09886-x

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Results on interpersonal predictors: "the strongest effect size with hedonic (ρ = .55), occupational (ρ = .60), and overall wellbeing (ρ = .51) was found for teacher-student relationships." Student disruptive behavior correlated −.32 to −.42 with hedonic, occupational, and overall wellbeing.

> "Among four interpersonal indicators, the strongest effect size with hedonic (ρ = .55), occupational (ρ = .60), and overall wellbeing (ρ = .51) was found for teacher-student relationships."
