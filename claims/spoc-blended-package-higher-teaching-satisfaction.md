## Evidence

### Chen J 2026

Chen J, Cui J, Li X, Yang J, Yang X, Zhong H, Wang F, Gu Q, Duan C. (2026). Application of a SPOC-based blended teaching system in full-cycle training for medical imaging interns: A randomized controlled educational intervention study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000049908

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Self-report teaching satisfaction scale completed at the immediate post-intervention assessment in the randomized study. The "unadjusted mean difference of 8.30 points" favored the experimental group; Table 4 reports r = 0.870 for the total score, and the authors report the experimental mean represented 96.0% of the maximum possible score and 10 of 30 participants reached the maximum score of 25, indicating a marked ceiling pattern.

> "The mean self-reported teaching satisfaction score was 24.00 ± 0.91 in the experimental group and 15.70 ± 1.21 in the control group, with an unadjusted mean difference of 8.30 points (95% CI, 7.75–8.85; P < .001)"
