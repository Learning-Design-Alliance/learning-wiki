# Formative assessment embedded with explicit-reflective instruction yields higher post-test NOS and SK scores than explicit-reflective instruction alone among pre-school prospective teachers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-test NOSS means were significantly higher in the experimental group than the control group (t = 6.678 in the article's Table 5; its prose prints 6.964, the control group's SD in that table; p < 0.05). [→ Mustafa Metin 2022](#mustafa-metin-2022)
`q2 i?` Post-test SKS means were significantly higher in the experimental group than the control group (t = 5.620; p < 0.05). [→ Mustafa Metin 2022 (2)](#mustafa-metin-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Mustafa Metin 2022

Mustafa Metin. (2022). Comparing Effects of Two Different Explicit–Reflective Instructions on Pre-School Prospective Teachers' View about Nature of Science and Scientific Knowledge. Journal of Science Learning, 5(1), 165-175. https://doi.org/10.17509/jsl.v5i1.33190

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on post-test NOSS scores in a pretest-posttest nonequivalent control group design with 33 prospective teachers per group; experimental mean 101.68 vs control 92.47, "a significant difference (t = 6.964; p < 0.05 ) in favor of the experimental group." Table 5 gives t = 6.678 for this comparison and lists 6.964 as the control group's SD, so the prose figure is a transcription error in the article. No effect size printed for this contrast.

> "the means of pre-service teachers in the experimental group in NOSS is higher than the control group's (x̄Experimental = 101.68; x̄Control = 92.47). The results of the t-test show that there is a significant difference (t = 6.964; p < 0.05 ) in favor of the experimental group."

### Mustafa Metin 2022 (2)

Mustafa Metin. (2022). Comparing Effects of Two Different Explicit–Reflective Instructions on Pre-School Prospective Teachers' View about Nature of Science and Scientific Knowledge. Journal of Science Learning, 5(1), 165-175. https://doi.org/10.17509/jsl.v5i1.33190

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on post-test SKS scores in the same quasi-experimental study; experimental mean 108.15 vs control 100.71, "a significant difference (t = 5.620; p < 0.05) in favor of the experimental group." No effect size printed for this contrast.

> "the means of pre-service teachers in the experimental group in SKS is higher than the control group's (x̄Experimental = 108.15; x̄Control = 100.71). The results of the t-test show that there is a significant difference (t = 5.620; p < 0.05) in favor of the experimental group."
