## Evidence

### Kulp MT 2026

Kulp MT, Ciner EB, Candy TR, Ying G-s, Hall C, Quinn G, Moore B. (2026). Impact of uncorrected hyperopia on visual function and academic performance in preschool and school-age children. Optometry and Vision Science. https://doi.org/10.1002/ovs2.70018

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports the Vision In Preschoolers-Hyperopia in Preschoolers study comparing moderately hyperopic children with age-typical refractive error, finding "significant deficits in near visual function" with the printed stereoacuity, logMAR, and accommodative lag values. A related report found reduced near visual function in 64% of moderately hyperopic children.

> "the VIP-HIP study found significant deficits in near visual function in children with moderate uncorrected hyperopia for stereoacuity (120 s arc vs. 40 s arc), near visual acuity (0.21 vs. 0.13 logMAR), and/or accommodative accuracy (lag by MEM 2.03 D vs. 1.03 D and lag by Grand Seiko 0.99 D vs. 0.46 D)."
