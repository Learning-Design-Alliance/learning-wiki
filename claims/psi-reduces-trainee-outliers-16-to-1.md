### Jones Gareth G. 2018

Jones Gareth G., Logishetty K., Clarke S., Collins R., Jaere M., Harris S., Cobb J. P. (2018). Do patient-specific instruments (PSI) for UKA allow non-expert surgeons to achieve the same saw cut accuracy as expert surgeons? Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-018-3031-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive outlier analysis reported in the discussion of the sawbone study, benchmarking achieved positions against the manufacturer's ±5° acceptable implant position guidelines; the authors note the result is comparable to three outliers in Kerens et al.'s first 30 commercial PSI cases.

> "According to the manufacturer’s guidelines on acceptable implant position in the sagittal (± 5°) and coronal planes (± 5°), for the trainees PSI reduced the number of outliers from 16 to 1"
