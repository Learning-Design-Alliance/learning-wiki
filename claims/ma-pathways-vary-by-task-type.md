# The relative strength of MA's pathways varies by mathematical task type: WM dominates for numeracy, both WM and number processing operate for arithmetic

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The pathways through which MA affects math performance differ in relative strength depending on whether arithmetic calculation or numeracy is being solved. [→ Skagerlund K 2019](#skagerlund-k-2019)

## Evidence

### Skagerlund K 2019

Skagerlund K, Östergren R, Västfjäll D, Träff U (2019) How does mathematics anxiety impair mathematical abilities? Investigating the link between math anxiety, working memory, and number processing. PLoS ONE 14(1): e0211283. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0211283

`q3 · i?`

Cross-model comparison of the two fitted SEMs (arithmetic vs. numeracy; N = 170 adults). For arithmetic, both indirect pathways were significant; for numeracy, only the WM pathway was significant. The authors state MA "works through both WM and basic number processing" for arithmetic, whereas numeracy is "to a greater degree linked to WM resources".

> "Importantly, the pathways vary in terms of their relative strength depending on what type of mathematical problems are being solved."
