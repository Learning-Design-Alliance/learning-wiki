### Cano García 2024

Cano García, E., Halbaut Bellowa, L., Martins Gironelli, L., & Lluch Molins, L. (2024). Online peer assessment in Galenic Pharmacy: enhancing evaluative judgement in higher education. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2409487

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of 326 students' ratings in the first Moodle Workshop peer assessment. The article reports that "the comments on the PPT revision were very well rated, with 85% to 90% of students rating both the feedback received and the feedback given with a 4 or 5."

> "It can be noted that, in general, the comments on the PPT revision were very well rated, with 85% to 90% of students rating both the feedback received and the feedback given with a 4 or 5."
