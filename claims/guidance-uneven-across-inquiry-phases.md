West, R.E., Sansom, R., Nielson, J., Wright, G., Turley, R.S., Jensen, J., & Johnson, M. (2021). Ideas for supporting student-centered stem learning through remote labs: a response. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09905-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

This is a finding the response article attributes to Zacharia et al.'s (2015) literature review of Computer-supported Inquiry Learning; the review identified best practices for each of the five inquiry phases. The article reports that "the orientation/conclusion/discussion phases received the least amount of guidance." No effect size is printed.

> "the orientation/conclusion/discussion phases received the least amount of guidance, while there were many more tools and strategies for providing guidance in the conceptualization/investigation phases"
