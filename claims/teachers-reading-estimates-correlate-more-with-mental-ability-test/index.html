Evidence¶

Wyman 1921¶

Wyman, J. B., & Wendle, M. (1921). What is Reading Ability? The Journal of Educational Psychology, 518-531. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=%22What+is+Reading+Ability%22

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Grade VIII reading study: 36 pupils tested, with two teachers independently ranking reading ability averaged as criterion Reading Ability T. The Terman correlation (+0.77 ± 0.05) exceeded all so-called reading tests; Monroe Rate showed "very little correlation" (+0.10). No standardised effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

"These correlations show that what teachers call "reading ability" correlates more highly with what the Terman test measures than with what the so-called reading tests measure."
