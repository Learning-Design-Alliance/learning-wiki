# Immersive multimedia learning with peer support significantly improves oral production in reading aloud across all rubric aspects compared with no peer support

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` First-year EFL students learning with immersive multimedia plus peer support scored significantly higher than students without peer support on pausing, phrasing, stress, intonation, rate, and integration in reading aloud (p < .05 for all aspects). [→ Muslem 2017](#muslem-2017)

## Evidence

### Muslem 2017

Muslem, A., & Abbas, M. (2017). The Effectiveness of Immersive Multimedia Learning with Peer Support on English Speaking and Reading Aloud. International Journal of Instruction, 10(1). http://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2017_1_13.pdf

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test study of 80 first-year Indonesian university EFL students in intact classes, 40 per condition, analyzed with one-way ANOVA. For example, reading pausing means were 7.15 with peer support versus 6.70 without, F(1,78) = 6.382, p = .014, showing "p < .05 for all the aspects".

> "Students in the peer -support group reported higher mean scores for all dimensions of oral production and results of the ANOVA tests reported significant differences, i.e., that p < .05 for all the aspects."
