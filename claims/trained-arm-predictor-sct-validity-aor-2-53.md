### Gaillet Antoine 2026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic regression with random effect on participants applied to all 460 SCTs. The trained arm showed "aOR 2.53 [1.57; 4.13], p < 0.001" for producing a valid SCT.

> "The multivariate model applied to all SCTs, adjusted for all variables of interest (including those that were imbalanced between the two arms), suggested the superiority of the trained arm over the control arm (aOR 2.53 [1.57; 4.13], p < 0.001, Table 3)"
