Julia B. Smith, BetsAnn Smith, Anthony S. Bryk. (1998). Setting the Pace: Opportunities to Learn in Chicago's Elementary Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/setting-pace-opportunities-learn-chicago-public-elementary-schools

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observation of math instruction across grades 2, 5, 8, 9, and 10 found introductory lessons on parallelograms presented identically across all grades. High school students spent as much time defining basic literary concepts as elementary counterparts.

> "the same topics and skills were being taught again and again, with little to no development in their content, depth, or complexity."
