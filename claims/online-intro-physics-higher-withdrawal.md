The review reports, citing Faulconer et al., that barriers specific to physics e-learning contribute to "higher withdrawal rates for online undergraduate introductory physics courses than in-person classes." No effect size is printed.

> "These factors, when paired with the lack of community students feel in online classes, contribute to higher withdrawal rates for online undergraduate introductory physics courses than in-person classes."
