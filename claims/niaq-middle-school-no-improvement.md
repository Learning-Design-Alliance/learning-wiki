`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of 535 year 8 (pre-test) and 387 year 10 (post-test) students from eight non-randomly selected Oslo-area schools, since year 8 students had not yet studied the standardized astronomy module. The article reports year 10 scores "statistically identical to or even slightly lower" than year 8.
