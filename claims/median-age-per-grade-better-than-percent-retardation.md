`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

This is a methodological argument, not an empirical test. The author contends that summarizing age-grade tables "in terms of percentiles, (particularly median age per grade)" is more convenient and statistically more sound than per cent of retardation.

> "For many practical situations, the best single statement of the age-grade situation is in terms, not of per cent of retardation, but of median age per grade."
