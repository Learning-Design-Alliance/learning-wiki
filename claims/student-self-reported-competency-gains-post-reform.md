`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-reform student self-report surveys (Supplementary Survey 5, n = 25; Survey 6, n = 29; Survey 7, n = 28). The article reports "more than 84% of students reported substantial gains in research-related competencies", over 83% reporting academic communication gains, and more than 86% reporting positive career-development impact. Self-report only, no comparison group.

> "more than 84% of students reported substantial gains in research-related competencies, including the identification of research questions, literature searching and data integration, the integration and application of both fundamental and advanced immunology concepts, critical thinking, innovative thinking, and academic writing"
