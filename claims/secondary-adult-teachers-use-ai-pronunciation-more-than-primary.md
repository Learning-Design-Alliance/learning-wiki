Georgios P. Georgiou. (2026). Artificial Intelligence in Pronunciation Teaching: Use and Beliefs of Foreign Language Teachers. University of Nicosia. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1080/02607476.2026.2676279](https://doi.org/10.1080/02607476.2026.2676279)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Ordinal logistic regression on AI-use ratings (1–7) from 117 Cypriot EFL teachers, with LevelSecondary and LevelAdult contrasted against LevelPrimary. The model showed significant differences for both contrasts (LevelSecondary estimate 1.30, p = 0.03; LevelAdult estimate 1.45, p = 0.04).

> "However, significant differences were observed for LevelSecondary, LevelAdult, and the baseline, highlighting that teachers working with adolescents and adults used AI more frequently than those teaching children."
