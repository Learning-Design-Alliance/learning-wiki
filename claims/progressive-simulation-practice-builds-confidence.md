### Sun Jui-Chiung 2026

Sun Jui-Chiung, Chang Min-Yu, Tsai Yi-Chun. (2026). Changes in Nursing Students' Clinical Handover Performance Following ISBAR-Structured Handover Simulation Teaching: A Mixed-Methods Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70955

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content analysis of focus group interviews, reflective journals and teaching reflections identified progressive practice building handover confidence as a theme. One student noted handovers became clearer and more stable after each simulation.

> "The progressively structured learning design—from didactic instruction and role-play through scenario simulation to OSCE—meant that repeated practice helped reduce students' unfamiliarity with and anxiety about handover, progressively building their confidence."
