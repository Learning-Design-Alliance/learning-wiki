`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Demonstration task in Chapter III using a photo of students in a Jigsaw collaborative learning process with AI speakers. The article reports GPT-4V observed that in panel (a) most students face their own workstations with little physical orientation towards each other, which might limit interaction, and suggested encouraging gestures, rearranging seating, and rotating group members.

> "in panels (b) and (c), the learners are seated closer and are facing each other, which is more conductive to interaction."
