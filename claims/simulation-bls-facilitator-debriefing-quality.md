### Bhuvaneswari S 2026

Bhuvaneswari S, Bamini Devi N, Helen Shaji J C, Lakshmi L. (2026). Voices of learners: a qualitative exploration of nursing students’ satisfaction with simulation-based basic life support training. BMC Medical Education, 26(1). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09260-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic finding from focus group discussions with 40 nursing students after simulation-based BLS training. Participants valued supportive facilitation and reflective debriefing, with one stating "The feedback helped me understand what I did right and wrong," while inconsistent facilitation negatively affected learning.

> "Supportive facilitators who provided clear instructions, constructive feedback, and reflective debriefing were highly valued. Participants reported that debriefing sessions helped them identify strengths, recognize areas for improvement, and consolidate learning. Conversely, inconsistent facilitation or limited feedback negatively affected the learning experience."
