Martin Lonsky, Martin Lang, Samuel Holt, Swapneel Amit Pathak, Robin Klause, Tzu-Hsiang Lo, Marijan Beg, Axel Hoffmann, and Hans Fangohr (2024). Numerical simulation projects in micromagnetics with Jupyter. [doi:10.1119/5.0149038](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0149038)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Case study report based on student evaluations and personal interview surveys with students and teaching staff in an elective magnetic materials course at UIUC. The authors report that the feedback "suggests that computational problem solving can improve the learning experience"; no effect sizes are reported.
