## Evidence

### Geoffrion R 2024

Geoffrion R, Koenig NA, Cundiff GW, Flood C, Hyakutake MT, Schulz J, Brennand EA, Lee T, Singer J, Todd NJ. (2024). Procedure-specific simulation for vaginal surgery training: A randomized controlled trial. Acta Obstetricia et Gynecologica Scandinavica. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis by residency year within the adjusted regression analysis of the randomized trial (Figure 3). The PGY-1 subgroup showed the largest overall GRS difference ("15.6% mean difference, 95% CI: 0.1–31.0, p = 0.048"). Analysis within PGY-4 was not considered due to too few PGY-4 intervention residents.

> "This difference was greatest in the most junior (PGY‐1) residents overall (15.6% mean difference, 95% CI: 0.1–31.0, p = 0.048; Figure 3)."
