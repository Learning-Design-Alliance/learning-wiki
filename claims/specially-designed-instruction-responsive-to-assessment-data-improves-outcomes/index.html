Arnold 2025¶

Arnold, J., Hayes, S., & Zagata, E. (2025). Leading inclusive, coherent, and effective assessment systems: Closing opportunity and outcome gaps for students with disabilities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673562

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

The brief asserts, citing four studies (Al Otaiba et al., 2014; Hurwitz et al., 2020; Jung et al., 2018; Schwartz et al., 2021), that "outcomes of students with disabilities are improved" by assessment-responsive specially designed instruction. The cited studies' designs are not described in the brief.

"Indeed, research shows that the outcomes of students with disabilities are improved when they are provided with specially designed instruction that is responsive to assessment data (Al Otaiba et al., 2014; Hurwitz et al., 2020; Jung et al., 2018; Schwartz et al., 2021)."
