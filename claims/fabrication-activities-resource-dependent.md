### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2020

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Kimme S. Johnson, Soojin E. Park, Jacob T. Stanley, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2020). Student perceptions of laboratory classroom activities and experimental physics practice. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.dounas-frazer](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.dounas-frazer)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of interview data from the optics and lasers lab study (35 of 36 students participating). The article reports fabrication-oriented activities accounted for "almost all instances" of resource-dependent codes and secondhand references, with fabrication most often described as resource-dependent. No effect size is printed.

> "Fabrication-oriented activities were identiﬁed as might be a feature of experimental physics research by a majority of participants, and they accounted for almost all instances in which participants discussed the resource-dependent nature of an activity or referred to their secondhand experiences . Compared to all other activities, fabrication was most frequently described as resource-dependent."
