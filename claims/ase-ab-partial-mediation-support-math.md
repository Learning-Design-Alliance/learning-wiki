## Evidence

### İlter İlhan 2025

İlter İlhan, Aksoy Nuri Can, Ceylan Mehmet. (2025). The relationships between parental involvement, teacher support, and mathematics performance: mediating roles of academic self-efficacy and academic buoyancy. BMC Psychology, 13, 1222. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03547-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bias-corrected bootstrap mediation analysis with 5000 resamples in the PLS-SEM model (n = 363); both indirect paths through self-efficacy were significant, supporting H5 and H6. Only path coefficients are printed.

> "the results of the bootstrap analysis (see Table 4) indicated that ASE served as a significant mediator in the relationship between TS and MP (β = 0.084, p < 0.000), as well as between PI and MP (β = 0.096, p < 0.001), thereby supporting hypotheses H5 and H6."

### İlter İlhan 2025 (2)

İlter İlhan, Aksoy Nuri Can, Ceylan Mehmet. (2025). The relationships between parental involvement, teacher support, and mathematics performance: mediating roles of academic self-efficacy and academic buoyancy. BMC Psychology, 13, 1222. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03547-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same bootstrap mediation analysis; both indirect paths through academic buoyancy were significant (β = 0.108 and 0.098), supporting H7 and H8, and the article states both mediators acted partially.

> "Moreover, AB significantly mediated the relationship between TS and MP (β = 0.108, p < 0.000), and between PI and MP (β = 0.098, p < 0.000), confirming hypotheses H7 and H8. These findings indicate that both ASE and the AB served a partial mediating role in these pathways."
