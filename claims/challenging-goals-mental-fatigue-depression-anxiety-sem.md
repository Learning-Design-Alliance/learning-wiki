# Challenging goals predict lower mental fatigue, and mental fatigue predicts depression and anxiety, in both programming e-learners and other e-learners

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Two-group SEM showed challenging goals statistically significantly predicts mental fatigue, and mental fatigue statistically significantly predicts depression and anxiety in both sample groups, with much larger explained variance in programming participants. ## Evidence

### Dirzyte Aiste 2021

Dirzyte Aiste, Vijaikis Aivaras, Perminas Aidas, Rimasiute-Knabikiene Romualda, Tchounwou Paul B. (2021). Associations between Depression, Anxiety, Fatigue, and Learning Motivating Factors in e-Learning-Based Computer Programming Education. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18179158

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-group SEM comparing non-participants (group 1) and programming participants (group 2). In group 1, "challenging goals explains 5.5% of the variance of mental fatigue" and mental fatigue explained 7.7% and 9.6% of depression and anxiety variance. Model fit was reported as acceptable.

> "In Group 2, challenging goals explains 25.7% of the variance of mental fatigue, and mental fatigue explains 44.1% of the variance of depression and 31.1% of the variance of anxiety."
