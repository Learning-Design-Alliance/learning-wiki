## Evidence

### Catherine De Clercq 2017

Catherine De Clercq, Jérôme De Schauwers, Gwenhaël de Wasseige, Jef van Laer. (2017). A better communicator is always a better scientist, or the reason why every PhD student should engage in science outreach. 35th International Cosmic Ray Conference. [doi:10.22323/1.301.1075](https://doi.org/10.22323/1.301.1075)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Qualitative content observation reported in the Impact on society section: more than 600 postcards were received and almost all illustrated or talked about life at the South Pole. The age-based difference is the authors' informal reading of the postcards, with no systematic coding reported.

> "Based on the content of the postcards, one can see that the adult participants realized the science carried out at the South Pole while the young participants were more interested in the infrastructure in Antarctica as well as the fauna."
