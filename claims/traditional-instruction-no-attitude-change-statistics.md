### Koparan 2014

Koparan, T., & Güven, B. (2014). The Effect on the 8th Grade Students' Attitude towards Statistics of Project Based Learning. European Journal of Educational Research, 3(2), 73-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086040

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control group of 35 students received teacher-supported traditional lessons with exercises and examples. The article reports the dependent t-test "t(34) = -.163, p >.05"; note the article's text calls it significant while the printed p indicates non-significance.

> "Looking at the measures of the control groups the average score of students at the pre -test was .55 (SD = .46), while the average score at post -test was .57  (SD = .55). Results from a dependent t-test indicate that this difference was significant, t(34) = -.163, p >.05."
