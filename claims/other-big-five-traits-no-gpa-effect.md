## Evidence

### Corazzini Luca 2021

Corazzini Luca, D'Arrigo Silvia, Millemaci Emanuele, Navarra Pietro. (2021). The influence of personality traits on university performance: Evidence from Italian freshmen students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258586

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the OLS results (Table 4), emotional stability is significant only in the specification without demographic controls (-0.057, p < .05) and loses significance once controls are added; extraversion and agreeableness are never significant. The article reports "the other Big Five traits do not appear to have a significant impact on GPA."

> "Conscientiousness and openness to experience exhibit positive and statistically significant coefficients, while the other Big Five traits do not appear to have a significant impact on GPA."
