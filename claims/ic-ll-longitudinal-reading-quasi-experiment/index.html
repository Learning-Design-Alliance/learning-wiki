Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). Instructional Conversations and Literature Logs — WWC Intervention Report. http://crede.berkeley.edu/research/crede/research/llaa/1.5pubs.html

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Longitudinal quasi-experimental study comparing 42 intervention and 42 comparison English language learners matched on first-grade Spanish reading and language scores. Appendix A3.1 reports performance assessment effect size 0.94 (improvement index +33, significant) and CTBS Reading effect size 0.63 (improvement index +24, ns after corrections for clustering and multiple comparisons).

"Saunders (1999) reported a statistically significant difference favoring the fifth grade intervention group on performance assessment. The WWC confirmed the statistical significance of this finding."
