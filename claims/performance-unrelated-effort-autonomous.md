## Evidence

### Malmberg and & Martin 2019

Lars-Erik Malmberg, Andrew J. Martin. (2019). Processes of Students' Effort Exertion, Competence Beliefs and Motivation: Cyclic and Dynamic Effects of Learning Experiences within School Days and School Subjects. Contemporary Educational Psychology (manuscript CEDPSYCH 2018-216-R2). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2019.03.013

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Between-level correlations in Model 3. Teacher-rated performance showed null associations with weekly effort and autonomous motivation, and positive associations with competence beliefs and controlled motivation. Equivalence was not tested for the null findings.

> "higher performance was unrelated with effort exertion (ρ = .07; p = .285) and intrinsic motivation (ρ = .08; p = .194) during the week. Higher performance was positively related with competence beliefs (ρ = .49; p < .001) and controlled motivation (ρ = .24; p = .002) during the week."
