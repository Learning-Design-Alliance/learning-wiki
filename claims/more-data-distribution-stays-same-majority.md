## Evidence

### Andy Schang 2023

Andy Schang, Matthew Dew, Emily M. Stump, N. G. Holmes, and Gina Passante. (2023). More or better data: A new perspective on student reasoning about measurement uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020105)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple-choice responses from the survey of advanced physics students across Projectile Motion, Stern-Gerlach, Brownian Motion, and Single-Slit scenarios. The authors identify staying the same as the most expert-like response, since more data with the same procedures and equipment should not affect overall variability.

> "The most common response for how the distribution might change when more data are taken is that the distribution will stay the same. We found this to be the case for all four experimental scenarios."
