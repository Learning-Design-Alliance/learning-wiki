## Evidence

### Daniel López-Fernández 2023

Daniel López-Fernández, Aldo Gordillo, Jennifer Pérez, Edmundo Tovar. (2023). Learning and Motivational Impact of Game-Based Learning: Comparing Face-To-Face and Online Formats on Computer Science Education. IEEE Transactions on Education. https://doi.org/10.1109/TE.2023.3241099

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment in a software engineering fundamentals course at UPM: 217 face-to-face and 104 online students completed a 10-question pre-test and post-test around a 50-minute game session. Paired samples T-tests showed significant gains, "medium to large effect size (Cohen’s d ≥ 0.65)"; Table II reports paired d of 0.71 (face-to-face) and 0.79 (online).

> "statistically significant differences between pre-test and post-test scores were found in both groups using a T-test for paired samples. Both in face-to-face and online group, the difference has a medium to large effect size (Cohen’s d ≥ 0.65)."
