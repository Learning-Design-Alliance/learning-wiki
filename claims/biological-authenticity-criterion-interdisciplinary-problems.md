`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

In this author case study of interdisciplinary course negotiation, the authors report arriving at the criterion of "biological authenticity", quoting that such examples give "the student a deeper understanding of why the biological system behaves the way it does".

> "We came to understand that what would be of value in a physics class is biological authenticity – examples where solving a physics problem in a biological context gives the student a deeper understanding of why the biological system behaves the way it does."
