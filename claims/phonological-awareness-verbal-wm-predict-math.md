# Phonological awareness and verbal working memory are the most consistently unique domain general predictors of grade 1 math outcomes

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Martin Rebecca B. 2014

Martin Rebecca B., Cirino Paul T., Sharp Carla, Barnes Marcia. (2014). Number and counting skills in kindergarten as predictors of grade 1 mathematical skills. Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2014.05.006

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In final hierarchical regression models for the four grade 1 outcomes, "verbal working memory was predictive of math fluency; phonological awareness was predictive of computations" and the other domain general patterns as quoted, in the 193-child longitudinal sample.

> "in final models, verbal working memory was predictive of math fluency; phonological awareness was predictive of computations; verbal working memory and phonological awareness were predictive of applied problem solving; and spatial working memory and phonological awareness were predictive of story problems."
