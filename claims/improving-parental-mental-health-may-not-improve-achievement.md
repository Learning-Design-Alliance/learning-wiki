## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The authors conclude that parental mental health symptoms are unlikely to have a significant average impact on children's academic performance, and that improving parental mental health in general may not in itself lead to improvements in children's educational outcomes. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation (type e) from the Conclusion, based on the within-family findings. The article estimates that such effects, if they exist, are likely small, "explaining little of the variation among children in their standardised test scores."

> "While our results do not diminish the importance of addressing mental health concerns in parents, they do suggest that improving parental mental health in general may not in itself lead to improvements in children’s educational outcomes."
