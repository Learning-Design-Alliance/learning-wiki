A theoretical argument, not a tested result: the authors contend that exploration, learning and experimentation are primitives of the university experience, and that confidence and competence in autonomous enquiry are a key transferable skill research experiences provide. They cite accumulating evidence that active learning can be beneficial and research projects can enhance student learning.
