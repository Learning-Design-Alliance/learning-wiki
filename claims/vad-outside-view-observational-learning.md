## Evidence

### Stefan A. Schreder 2025

Stefan A. Schreder, Helen Jossberger, Hans Gruber. (2025). Mountain rescuers' experiences with video-assisted and verbal debriefings: a qualitative study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09716-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group results on the category 'outside view and its value'. Participants said the outside view revealed colleagues' stress or missing information and "clarified the origin of negative outcomes on the team level", yet "some participants, however, admitted that they mainly focused on themselves when watching the video".

> "Besides understanding how the actions of the team relate to each other, it was also considered helpful to see others making mistakes. According to participants, revisiting the situation with the help of the video clarified the origin of negative outcomes on the team level."

### Stefan A. Schreder 2025 (2)

Stefan A. Schreder, Helen Jossberger, Hans Gruber. (2025). Mountain rescuers' experiences with video-assisted and verbal debriefings: a qualitative study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09716-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Facilitator interview evidence corroborating participant self-focus: FA 1 had the impression that participants watching video were predominantly checking their own behaviour rather than observing teammates, consistent with the focus group findings.

> "when they watched the video, I had the impression that most of them were looking at themselves: How did I behave?"
