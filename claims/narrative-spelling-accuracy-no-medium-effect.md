## Evidence

### Broc Lucie 2025

Broc Lucie, Goudeau Sébastien, Colliot Tiphaine. (2025). Comparing the effects of typing and handwriting on spelling performance in school. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-03369-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Study 2 narrative ANOVA on spelling accuracy (words correctly spelled / words written): no significant modality, grade, or interaction effects; narrative spellings were correct in 89% of cases regardless of medium. Equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant modality effect was observed [F(1, 78) = 2.92, p = .09, η2 = 0.01). There was also no significant effect of students’ grade [F(3, 78) = 0.29, p = .83, η2 = 0.01) and no interaction effect between writing modality and grade [F(3, 78) = 0.04, p = .38, η2 = 0.01)."
