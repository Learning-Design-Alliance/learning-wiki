# Dynamic training shifts children's verbal explanations: fewer non-inductive and more full-inductive explanations, but no significant change for no-answer or partial-inductive categories

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trained children decreased non-inductive explanations while control children increased them (Session × Condition interaction). [→ Touw 2020](#touw-2020)
`q3 i?` Training positively influenced progression toward advanced full-inductive verbal strategies at post-test. [→ Touw 2020 (2)](#touw-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Touw 2020

Touw, K. W. J., Vogelaar, B., Thissen, F., Rovers, S., & Resing, W. C. M. (2020). Progression and individual differences in children's series completion after dynamic testing. The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 90(1), 184-205. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12272

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the 164-child experiment: "Children in the control condition increased their non-inductive verbal explanations from the pre-test to post-test, and the children who received training showed a decrease in this non-advanced verbal strategy." ηp² = .05.

> "The analysis for the non-inductive verbal strategy category revealed a significant interaction effect for Session × Condition: Wilks' λ = .949, F(1, 157) = 8.51, p = .004, ηp 2 = .05."

### Touw 2020 (2)

Touw, K. W. J., Vogelaar, B., Thissen, F., Rovers, S., & Resing, W. C. M. (2020). Progression and individual differences in children's series completion after dynamic testing. The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 90(1), 184-205. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12272

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate repeated-measures ANOVA for the full-inductive category, with Session, Condition, and Session × Condition all significant (ηp² = .11, .04, .04 as printed). Children were assigned to verbal explanation strategy groups from 13 categories.

> "a significant interaction was found for Session × Condition (Wilks' λ = .964, F(1, 157) = 5.91, p = .016, ηp 2 = .04). Children used more advanced full-inductive verbal strategies in the post-test session, and training appeared to positively influence this progression."

### Touw 2020 (3)

Touw, K. W. J., Vogelaar, B., Thissen, F., Rovers, S., & Resing, W. C. M. (2020). Progression and individual differences in children's series completion after dynamic testing. The British Journal of Educational Psychology, 90(1), 184-205. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12272

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate tests for the no-answer and partial-inductive categories revealed no significant effects; the article reports the partial-inductive change was "not significant (p = .107)". No effect sizes printed for these null findings, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Although the children who received training provided a larger number of partial-inductive verbal explanations, and the non-trained children at first sight showed a decrease in these explanations, these changes were not significant (p = .107)."
