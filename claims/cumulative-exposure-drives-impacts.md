### Borman Geoffrey D. 2026

Borman Geoffrey D., Yang Hyunwoo. (2026). Cumulative access to print books improves literacy achievement: Evidence from a five-year randomized trial in high-poverty schools. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2521416123

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the trial's dosage pattern: 61% of grade 1 and 2 treatment students remained in baseline schools and were offered all four distributions, while school changers received one to three distributions. No effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "Consistent with the theory of action summarized in Fig. 1, impacts depended on cumulative and sustained exposure. Students who received all book distributions across the 5 y realized approximately twice the gains of those with more variable exposure, underscoring the importance of continuity in access."
