## Evidence

### Choi S 2018

Choi S, Yuen HM, Annan R, Pickup T, Pulman A, Monroy-Valle M, Aduku NEL, Kyei-Boateng S, Velásquez Monzón CI, Portillo Sermeño CE, Penn A, Ashworth A, Jackson AA. (2018). Effectiveness of the Malnutrition eLearning Course for Global Capacity Building in the Management of Malnutrition: Cross-Country Interrupted Time-Series Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/10396

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman rank correlation analysis of knowledge gain, application, and confidence over time in the cross-country study. The article reports the correlation "was weak, as we had hypothesized" posttraining, strengthening by 6 and 12 months.

> "Figure 4 shows that immediately posttraining, the correlation between the gain in knowledge and confidence was weak, as we had hypothesized. In contrast, a strong correlation emerged at the 6-month follow-up and remained strong at 12 months."

### Choi S 2018 (2)

Choi S, Yuen HM, Annan R, Pickup T, Pulman A, Monroy-Valle M, Aduku NEL, Kyei-Boateng S, Velásquez Monzón CI, Portillo Sermeño CE, Penn A, Ashworth A, Jackson AA. (2018). Effectiveness of the Malnutrition eLearning Course for Global Capacity Building in the Management of Malnutrition: Cross-Country Interrupted Time-Series Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/10396

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Spearman rank correlation comparing appliers versus non-appliers between 6- and 12-month follow-up. The article reports "applied: N=127, rs =.74, P<.001 vs not applied: N=18, rs =.52, P=.03."

> "The relation between knowledge gain and confidence between 6-month and 12-month follow-up was stronger among the participants who applied knowledge than the ones who did not (applied: N=127, rs =.74, P<.001 vs not applied: N=18, rs =.52, P=.03)."
