# AI-enabled personalized STEM education yields a medium-sized, statistically significant overall effect (g = 0.455) compared with traditional non-AI instruction in K-12 schools

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q4` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Across 99 effect sizes from 32 RCTs, students learning STEM with AI technologies scored 0.455 standard deviations above students learning without AI technologies. [→ Li 2025](#li-2025)

## Evidence

### Li 2025

Li, S., Zeng, C., Liu, H., Jia, J., Liang, M., Cha, Y., Lim, C. P., & Wu, X. (2025). A meta-analysis of AI-enabled personalized STEM education in schools. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00566-y

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 99 independent effect sizes extracted from 32 randomized controlled trial studies of AI-enabled personalized K-12 STEM education. The pooled effect size was "g = 0.455 (p < 0.001)", which the authors classify as a small effect under Cohen's benchmarks.

> "The overall effect size of AI-enabled personalized STEM education in K-12 schools is g = 0.455 (p < 0.001), with a 95% confidence interval of 0.327–0.583."
