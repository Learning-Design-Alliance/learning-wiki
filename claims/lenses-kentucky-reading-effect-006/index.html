Lenses on Literature 2013¶

Lenses on Literature. (2013). Lenses on Literature. https://www.literacydesigncollaborative.org

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental study of Lenses (referred to as Literacy Design Collaborative) on eighth-grade reading in Kentucky and Pennsylvania, 2012-2013, with over 2,500 Lenses students in Kentucky from five early-adopter districts. The Kentucky analysis found a "statistically significant positive effect on state reading assessment scores" with effect size +0.06.

"The analysis found a statistically significant positive effect on state reading assessment scores for Kentucky students (effect size = +0.06), earning Lenses a Promising rating."
