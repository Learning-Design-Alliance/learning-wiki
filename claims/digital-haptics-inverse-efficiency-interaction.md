## Evidence

### Ruxandra I. Tivadar 2023

Ruxandra I. Tivadar, Benedetta Franceschiello, Astrid Minier and Micah M. Murray. (2023). Learning and navigating digitally rendered haptic spatial layouts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00208-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Behavioural analysis of the navigation phase: Inverse Efficiency scores (corrected reaction times combining RT and accuracy) showed the significant interaction (p = 0.008, pω2 = 0.17), with post-hoc Wilcoxon tests "significant for Group 1" (d = −1.58) "but not Group 2" — an effect present in one group but not the other.

> "A significant Training × Group interaction was also observed for IE scores (p = 0.008, pω2 = 0.17). Post-hoc Wilcoxon tests compared trained vs untrained trajectories for Group 1 and Group 2. The tests were significant for Group 1: t = −5.7015, p = 0.0008, d = −1.58; mean trained trajectories: 29.6, mean untrained trajectories: 17.7"
