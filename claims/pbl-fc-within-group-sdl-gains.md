## Evidence

### Yang Yu 2026

Yang Yu, Xue Jiarui, Hu Lingfei, Fang Kai, Wei Chaobing, Wu Changfan, Jin Huixiang. (2026). A combined PBL and flipped classroom intervention enhances self-directed learning ability and empathy in ophthalmology undergraduates. Scientific Reports 16, 11696. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-47584-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-group paired t-tests before and after the class (Table 3). The PBL-FC group's total difference was 17.23 ± 1.65 (Cohen's d = 10.44, P < 0.001); the traditional group's total difference was 0.32 ± 2.32 (d = 0.14, P = 0.37).

> "When pre- and post-class scores within the PBL-FC group were compared, all dimensions showed marked improvements after the intervention (P < 0.001). In contrast, no significant differences were noted in the traditional group between pre- and post-class scores for any dimension (P > 0.05)."
