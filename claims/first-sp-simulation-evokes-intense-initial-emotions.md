## Evidence

### García Carpintero-Blas E 2025

García Carpintero-Blas E, Del Pozo-Herce P, Hernández-Cediel MDC, Rodríguez-García M, Navas-Echazarreta N, Chover-Sierra E, Martínez-Sabater A, Juárez-Vela R, Tovar-Reinoso A. (2025). First Contact With Care Through Clinical Simulation in Nursing Students: Qualitative Study. JMIR Nursing. https://doi.org/10.2196/81617

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative descriptive phenomenological study with 59 first-year nursing students using 6 focus groups, field notes, and 43 reflective narratives; inductive thematic analysis in ATLAS.ti. The theme analysis found that "initial emotions ranging from nervousness to insecurity, surprise, and empathy" marked the first simulated encounter.

> "The emotional experience of the students during the clinical simulations was intense and diverse, with initial emotions ranging from nervousness to insecurity, surprise, and empathy."
