### van Dijk 2020

van Dijk, M., Blom, E., Kroesbergen, E. H., & Leseman, P. P. M. (2020). The Influence of Situational Cues on Children’s Creativity in an Alternative Uses Task and the Moderating Effect of Selective Attention. Journal of Intelligence, 8(4), 37. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence8040037

`q3 · i?`

Univariate ANOVA on untransformed fluency scores in the same experiment; no effect size is printed, so no magnitude is claimed. The authors note this absence of a relationship "replicates the findings of Zabelina and Robinson (2010)".

> "The ANOVA for fluency and the ANOVA for originality did not return any significant main or interaction effect."
