## Evidence

### Hoffman M 2014

Hoffman M, Wilkinson JE, Xu J, Wiecha J. (2014). The perceived effects of faculty presence vs. absence on small-group learning and group dynamics: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0258-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired quasi-experimental survey analysis (n = 114 pairs): 'There was no difference in reported success of the case discussion after “in” vs. “out” cases (p = 0.67)'; the 'got the most I could' item averaged 4.3 vs 4.1, and psychosocial-issues consideration did not differ (p = 0.89).
