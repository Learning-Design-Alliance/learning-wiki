## Evidence

### Zhu 2026

Zhu, J.-P., Xie, X.-Y., Yu, C. & Zhang, J.-Y. (2026). Double training promotes retinotopic transfer of category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00396-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects analysis of pooled II task data from Experiments 1-4 (72 participants total): significant main effect of post-test location (F(1, 68) = 38.67, p < 0.0001), no main effect of group (F(3, 68) = 0.72, p = 0.54), and the significant "interaction between group and post-test location".

> "there was a significant interaction between group and post-test location (F(3, 68) = 7.47, p = 0.0002)"

### Zhu 2026 (2)

Zhu, J.-P., Xie, X.-Y., Yu, C. & Zhang, J.-Y. (2026). Double training promotes retinotopic transfer of category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00396-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparing transfer indices of Experiments 1-4 with Experiment 1 as baseline: the printed effect size "𝜂𝑝2 = 0.25"; Bonferroni-corrected post hoc tests showed Experiment 2 (p = 0.016) and Experiment 4 (p < 0.001) higher than Experiment 1, Experiment 3 not different (p = 1.000).

> "Statistical analysis revealed a significant effect of experimental condition on TI (A one-way ANOVA: F(3, 68) = 7.552, p < 0.001, 𝜂𝑝2 = 0.25)"
