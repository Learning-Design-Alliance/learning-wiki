### Sharmin Akter 2026

Sharmin Akter, Mohammad Abu Nasir Rakib, Eshwara Prasad Sridhar, Somik Biswas, Mahmudur Rahman, and Md Rassel Raihan. (2026). Post-Lecture Interactive Environments for Conceptual Learning: A Randomized Comparison of Mixed Reality and Tangible Instruction in Undergraduate STEM Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.03896](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.03896)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Primary ANCOVA in this pilot randomized between-subjects study (24 undergraduates, eight per condition), with baseline quiz as covariate. The article reports "a significant main effect of instructional condition on post-test scores" with partial η² = .263, which it calls large. A Freedman-Lane permutation ANCOVA (5,000 permutations) yielded p = .050.

> "The analysis showed a significant main effect of instructional condition on post-test scores, F(2, 20) = 3.60, p = .048, partial η² = .263."
