Evidence¶

Swain 2025¶

Swain, W., Leung-Gagné, M., Maier, A., & Rubinstein, C. (2025). Community schools impact on student outcomes: Evidence from California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/541.498

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive and interaction analyses of first-cohort CCSPP schools found that schools with "average or larger progress in reducing absences" had the largest achievement gains; the executive summary adds that each standard deviation of attendance improvement was associated with a near doubling of the main effect, with a significant attendance-by-treatment interaction.

"CCSPP schools that exhibited average or larger progress in reducing absences were also the schools with the largest gains in student achievement."
