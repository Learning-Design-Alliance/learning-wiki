### Bortolotti Alessandro 2025

Bortolotti Alessandro, Ricci Fabrizio, Cieri Carmelita, Cocco Federica, Martini Chiara, Panunzi Marcello, Rossi Davide, Sorella Anna, Saraullo Silvio, Scordo Davide, Renda Giulia, Gallina Sabina, Palumbo Riccardo. (2025). Tracing Visual Expertise in ECG Interpretation: An Eye‐Tracking Pilot Study. Annals of Noninvasive Electrocardiology. https://doi.org/10.1111/anec.70082

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective observational pilot in which eight cardiology practitioners (four training levels, two each) interpreted nine ECGs under a five-minute limit with eye-tracking glasses. Experts were faster than residents (107.61 ± 32.78 s vs. 205.31 ± 57.43 s; p < 0.001) and "Diagnostic accuracy was also higher among experts compared to residents (p < 0.001)".

> "Experts interpreted ECGs significantly faster than residents (mean time, 107.61 ± 32.78 s vs. 205.31 ± 57.43 s; p < 0.001). Interpretation times decreased with increasing levels of training"
