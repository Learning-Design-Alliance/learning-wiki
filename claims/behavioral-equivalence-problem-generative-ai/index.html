The article reports, citing Bastani et al. (2025), a randomized field experiment with nearly one thousand high school students in mathematics: GPT-4-assisted practice produced "48 percent better" practice performance but "17 percent worse" unassisted exam scores. No effect size is printed.

"Bastani et al. (2025) conducted a randomized field experiment with nearly one thousand high school students in mathematics. They found that students with access to a standard GPT-4 interface performed 48 percent better on practice problems. On a subsequent unassisted exam, however, they scored 17 percent worse than students who had practiced without AI."
