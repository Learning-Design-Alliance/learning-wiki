## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wile Rachel K. 2023

Wile Rachel K., Riley Brian, Rodriguez Natalie, Chern Hueylan, Cruff Jason, O'Sullivan Patricia S. (2023). Home practice for robotic surgery: a randomized controlled trial of a low-cost simulation model. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-023-01688-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial: 29 novice medical students (15 kit, 14 control) completed baseline and two-week dVSS sessions. The experimental group's mean Sea Spikes 1 score rose 30.26 points (SD 25.82) versus 14.28 points (SD 27) for controls; "The difference between the two groups was not significant by an ANCOVA controlling for pre-score."

> "At the second trial, the experimental group improved their mean overall score by 30.26 points with a standard deviation of 25.82 points for sea spikes 1 (Table 2)."
