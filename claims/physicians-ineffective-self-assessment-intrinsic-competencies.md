## Evidence

### McConnell Meghan 2018

McConnell Meghan, Gu Ada, Arshad Aysha, Mokhtari Arastoo, Azzam Khalid. (2018). An innovative approach to identifying learning needs for intrinsic CanMEDS roles in continuing professional development. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2018.1497374

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

This is background literature cited in the Introduction to motivate the study, not a result tested here; the article cites prior research (refs 10–14) that faculty often avoid assessing intrinsic roles and that self-assessment is unreliable.

> "Given that (a) physicians are not particularly effective at self-assessing gaps in their knowledge and abilities[14], and (b) they do not fully understand the constructs relating to intrinsic competencies [10–13], CPD providers need to find alternative approaches to identify learning needs as they pertain to personal and professional competencies."
