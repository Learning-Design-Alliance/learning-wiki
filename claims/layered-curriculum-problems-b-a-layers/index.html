Evidence¶

Koc Akran 2019¶

Koc Akran, S., & Gurbuzturk, O. (2019). Effect of layered curriculum in problem solving skills of students in science and technology course. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 5(1), 135-150. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.5.1.147

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive and content analysis of interviews with 10 voluntarily chosen experimental students, plus student and researcher diaries. Problems clustered around "organizing information" and not knowing where to find material-related information.

"Experimental group students encountered problems particularly in B and A layers during the period they performed Layered Curriculum activities."

Koc Akran 2019 (2)¶

Koc Akran, S., & Gurbuzturk, O. (2019). Effect of layered curriculum in problem solving skills of students in science and technology course. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 5(1), 135-150. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.5.1.147

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative descriptive analysis of the 10 interviews: 9 students solved problems via collecting information (research from different sources f=7; help from acquaintances f=4) and 6 via identifying the problem.

"descriptional analysis of interviews with 10 students shows that, 9 students tried to solve encountered problems through “collecting information” [research from different sources (f=7) and getting help from acquaintances (f=4)]"
