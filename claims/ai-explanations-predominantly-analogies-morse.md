Amogh Sirnoorkar, Winter Allen, Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Examining Student and AI Generated Personalized Analogies in Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.04290](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.04290)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of AI-generated second-grader descriptions collected alongside student responses in the same survey; "806 (96%) contained comparisons, all of which reflected analogies," with predominant contexts including magnets, friends, roller coaster, spring, tug-of-war, and slide.

> "Of the 839 AI-generated responses, 806 (96%) contained comparisons, all of which reflected analogies. Among these, 190 included multiple embedded analogies within a single description. Only eight responses did not involve any comparisons,"
