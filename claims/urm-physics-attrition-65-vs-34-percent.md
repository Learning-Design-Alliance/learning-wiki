Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2021.1900623](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2021.1900623)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 10 years of institutional records at one large public research university; drop rates by race/ethnicity plotted in Fig. 4. The article reports that "65% of URM physics majors" drop the major versus 34% of White majors, though standard errors are large due to small samples.

> "In particular, 65% of URM physics majors (Fig. 4c), 45% of Asian physics majors (Fig. 4b), and 34% of White physics majors (Fig. 4d) drop the major."
