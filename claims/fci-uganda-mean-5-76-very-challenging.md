### Kirya 2025

Kirya, K. R., Mashood, K. K., & Yadav, L. L. (2025). Use of item response curves to evaluate the validity of the force concept inventory in secondary schools in Uganda and comparison with other educational contexts. European Journal of Mathematics and Science Education, 6(2), 79-95. https://doi.org/10.12973/ejmse.6.2.79

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CTT analysis (ITEMAN) of FCI responses from 941 Ugandan Senior Four students sampled across 24 schools. The article reports "students answered only 5.76 items correctly, with 19% of the test items answered correctly overall"; SD was 2.28, max 25, min 0.

> "On average, students answered only 5.76 items correctly, with 19% of the test items answered correctly overall, indicating the test was very challenging and students had difficulty understanding the concepts."
