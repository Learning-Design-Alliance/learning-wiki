## Evidence

### Keles 2025

Keles, S., ten Braak, D., Hagen, Å. M., & Melby-Lervåg, M. (2025). Validity of Early-Grade Screening Tools in the Three Tier (RTI/MTSS) Model: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Literacy, Language, and Mathematics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10077-5

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Publication bias assessment of the correlational meta-analysis using contour-enhanced funnel plots, PET and PEESE models, and a publication-type moderator. The PET intercept was significant (0.742, t(65) = 3.763, p = 0.0037) but slopes were non-significant in both models; the review concludes "no substantial evidence of small-study effects or publication bias".

> "This lack of significance in the slope indicates no substantial evidence of small-study effects or publication bias."
