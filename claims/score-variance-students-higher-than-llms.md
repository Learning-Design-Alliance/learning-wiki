Evaluating GPT- and Reasoning-based Large Language Models on Physics Olympiad Problems: Surpassing Human Performance and Implications for Educational Assessment. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Visual inspection of fitted normal distributions of simulated aggregated exam scores (Fig. 3), overlaid with means and standard deviations, shows "a substantially higher variance" for students; no effect size is printed for this variance contrast.

> "while student performance exhibits a substantially higher variance, the variance of scores produced by GPT-4o is considerably smaller, and the scores of o1-preview are even more narrowly distributed."
