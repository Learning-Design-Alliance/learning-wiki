## Evidence

### Zante Bjoern 2020

Zante Bjoern, Schefold Joerg C. (2020). Simulation training for emergency skills: effects on ICU fellows’ performance and supervision levels. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02419-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post external assessment for cricothyroidotomy within the same observational evaluation of the full-day cadaver-based training (29 fellows with data). The article reports a positive change for all assessed fellows with p < 0.0001; no effect size is printed.

> "External assessment rated 29 fellows (100%) with a positive change in their supervision level (median change from “Observation but not execution, even with direct supervision” to “Execution with reactive supervision, i.e., on request and quickly available”, p < 0.0001, Fig. 3)."
