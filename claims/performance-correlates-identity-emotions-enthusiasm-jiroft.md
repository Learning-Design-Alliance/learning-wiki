## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bordbar Shima 2025

Bordbar Shima, Mirzaei Saeid, Bahmaei Jamshid, Atashbahar Omolbanin, Yusefi Ali Reza. (2025). Predicting students' academic performance based on academic identity, academic excitement, and academic enthusiasm: evidence from a cross-sectional study in a developing country. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07374-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 290 students at Jiroft University of Medical Sciences using Pearson correlations; the article reports "a statistically significant correlation" between performance and all four constructs, with r values from 0.576 to 0.656 (all P < 0.001).

> "A statistically significant correlation was observed between students’ academic performance and academic identity (r = 0.576, P < 0.001), positive academic excitement (r = 0.627, P < 0.001), negative academic excitement (r = -0.635, P < 0.001), and academic enthusiasm (r = 0.656, P < 0.001)"
