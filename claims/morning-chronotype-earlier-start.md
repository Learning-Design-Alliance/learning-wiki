## Evidence

### Albina Zavgorodniaia 2021

Albina Zavgorodniaia, Raj Shrestha, Juho Leinonen, Arto Hellas, John Edwards. (2021). Morning or Evening? An Examination of Circadian Rhythms of CS1 Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis H tests on hours before deadline (Table III): US H = 58.162, p < 0.001; European H = 35.667, p < 0.001. Table II shows, e.g., US morning students averaged 35 hours before deadline versus 14 for US evening students. No effect sizes printed.

> "Both contexts showed strongly significant differences, with students who work on their assignments in the morning generally working earlier relative to the due date than students who work in the evening and night time."
