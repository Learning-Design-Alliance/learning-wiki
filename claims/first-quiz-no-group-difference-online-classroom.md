## Evidence

### Jorczak and Dupuis 2011

Robert L. Jorczak, Danielle N. Dupuis. (2011). Differences in Classroom Versus Online Exam Performance Due to Asynchronous Discussion. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Differences+in+Classroom+Versus+Online+Exam+Performance+Due+to+Asynchronous+Discussion

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Equivalence check on the first short multiple-choice quiz based on chapter readings, administered before the course's instructional design had time to take effect. The article reports "t(105) = 0.56, p = .572"; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not established, only non-detection.

> "No statistically significant difference was observed between the groups on the first quiz score (t(105) = 0.56, p = .572)."
