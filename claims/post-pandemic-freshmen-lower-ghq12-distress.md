## Evidence

### Buizza Chiara 2023

Buizza Chiara, Ferrari Clarissa, Sbravati Giulio, Dagani Jessica, Cela Herald, Rainieri Giuseppe, Ghilardi Alberto. (2023). Impact of COVID-19 Pandemic on Well-Being, Social Relationships and Academic Performance in a Sample of University Freshmen: A Propensity Score Match Evaluation Pre- and Post-Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20156485

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity score matching of two web-survey freshman samples (n = 553 matched pairs) compared clinical variables; Table 1 reports GHQ-12 mean 6.44 (2019) vs 5.96 (2022), p = 0.007. The article states post-pandemic freshmen "had lower scores than freshmen recruited before the pandemic".

> "The two samples differed by the GHQ-12 total score: freshmen recruited after the COVID-19 pandemic had lower scores than freshmen recruited before the pandemic, even though the score was indicative of psychological distress in both samples (cut-off > 3)."
