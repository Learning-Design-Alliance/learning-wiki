## Subclaims
`q2 i?` DeepSeek-assisted PBL residents rated the course significantly more effective for supporting case analysis (4.40 ± 0.56 vs. 3.97 ± 0.67, p = 0.009) and for feedback improving understanding of medical concepts (4.33 ± 0.55 vs. 3.87 ± 0.63, p = 0.003). [→ Hou Jinxiao 2025](#hou-jinxiao-2025)

## Evidence

### Hou Jinxiao 2025

Hou Jinxiao, An Furun, Qin Hui, Zhang Lulu, Wang Juan, Zhang Cui, Fan Dachuan. (2025). Application of DeepSeek-assisted problem-based learning in hematology residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07852-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Anonymous 15-item five-point Likert questionnaire at the end of both courses, 30 residents per group, analyzed with independent t-tests. The DeepSeek-assisted group's higher ratings on effectiveness and feedback items are printed means with p-values; no standardized effect size was reported.

> "Students in the DeepSeek-assisted PBL group perceived the course as significantly more effective in supporting case analysis (4.40 ± 0.56 vs. 3.97 ± 0.67, p = 0.009) and valued the feedback for substantially enhancing their grasp of medical concepts (4.33 ± 0.55 vs. 3.87 ± 0.63, p = 0.003)"
