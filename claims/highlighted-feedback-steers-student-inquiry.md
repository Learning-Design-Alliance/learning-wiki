## Evidence

### Fares Fawzi 2026

Fares Fawzi, Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Marta Knezevic, Tanya Nazaretsky, and Tanja Käser. (2026). REFINE: Real-world Exploration of Interactive Feedback and Student Behaviour. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29744-0_27)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression with standard errors clustered by student, predicting discussion of a rubric component from whether it was highlighted in the feedback, fit on classroom interaction and feedback annotations (κ = 0.665 feedback, κ = 0.625 chat). The printed odds ratio is OR = 8.10 with 95% CI [2.92, 22.46].

> "Components highlighted in the feedback were substantially more likely to be discussed than non-highlighted components (OR = 8.10, 95% CI [2.92, 22.46],p < .001), after accounting for differences across rubric components and repeated observations per student."
