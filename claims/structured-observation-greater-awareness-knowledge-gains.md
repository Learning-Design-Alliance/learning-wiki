## Evidence

### Zhu Xiaocheng 2026

Zhu Xiaocheng, Tang Anliu, Chin Munle, Chang Huan. (2026). Developing purposeful observation during early clinical exposure in anesthesiology: a cluster-randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10042-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post comparison in the cluster-randomized study: total awareness change was 22.69 ± 1.52 in the structured group versus 10.96 ± 2.32 in the conventional group, and knowledge change 3.54 ± 0.80 versus 2.36 ± 0.81, with P for Δ < 0.001 on all domains.

> "The increases in awareness domains and knowledge score were greater in the structured perioperative observation group than in the conventional clerkship group."
