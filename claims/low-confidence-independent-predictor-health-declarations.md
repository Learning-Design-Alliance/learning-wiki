Paton Lewis W., Tiffin Paul A., Smith Daniel, Dowell Jon S., Mwandigha Lazaro M. (2018). Predictors of fitness to practise declarations in UK medical undergraduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1167-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Backwards stepwise multivariable logistic regression for health declarations (N = 1459); confidence retained at p = 0.017, 95% CI (0.51, 0.94). The article states gender was "not independently associated with the odds of a health declaration".

> "The multivariable model for health declarations contains only one independent variable: ‘confidence’ score on the IVQ33/ITQ50 test. Higher scores on this scale are associated with reduced odds of declaring a health issue (OR 0.69)."
