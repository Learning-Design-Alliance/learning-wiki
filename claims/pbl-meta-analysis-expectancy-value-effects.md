The Wijnia et al. (2019) meta-analysis of pre-post and/or independent-groups studies comparing PBL to lecture-based groups reported effects on expectancy-value constructs: "62 studies (d = 0.403)" for self-efficacy/control beliefs and "31 studies (d = 0.587)" for task value including interest.
