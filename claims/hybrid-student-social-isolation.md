### Joyce P 2025

Joyce P, Cunningham M, Alexander L. (2025). Evaluation of a hybrid model of a Physician Associate Studies programme: students, teachers, and examiner perspectives. Irish Journal of Medical Science 194(2):431-438. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-025-03918-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme from thematic analysis of focus group interviews with six first-year and five second-year hybrid students; one student reported feeling isolated "not by the primary lecturers but the guest ones…not aware of us", and other students agreed some lecturers seemed unaware of online students. The isolation feeling was exacerbated during online exams.

> "Not being present in the classroom caused one student to admit to feeling isolated at the beginning of the programme: At the start, I felt isolated not by the primary lecturers but the guest ones…not aware of us."
