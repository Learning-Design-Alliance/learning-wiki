### Nelson 2014

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article's interpretation of the null Management result: preservice teachers had a significant knowledge base in legal issues and motivational theory, but the program lacked rigor in "retaining the experiential knowledge and skill with managing large groups of students inside the public schools."

> "A possible explanation for gains with the legal and motivation measures but not the management measures could be related to the preservice teachers’ preparation in the program."
