## Evidence

### Edward F. Redish and David Hammer 2009

Edward F. Redish and David Hammer. (2009). Reinventing College Physics for Biologists: Explicating an epistemological curriculum. https://www.physics.umd.edu/perg/papers/redish/AAPT2009RedishHammer.pdf [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0807.4436](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0807.4436)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Spring 2003 split-task FCI administered to ~200 students entering second semester; approximately two-thirds had taken the reformed first-semester class, one-third a traditional one. The authors call the increase in "right and reconciled" answers from ~25% to ~75% dramatic but note many issues remain to establish a convincing research result.

> "We see that not only did the students from the reformed mechanics class answer a larger percentage of the questions cor- rectly (~85% compared to ~45%) but the correct an- swers were split less often (~90% compared to ~60%)."
