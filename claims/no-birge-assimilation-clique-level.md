# No statistically significant assimilation effect (BIRGE) of belonging to a higher-achieving clique within the classroom on ASC change

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Membership in an above-average-achieving clique within the classroom was not significantly associated with ASC change, contrary to the assimilation hypothesis. [→ Tanja Auer 2025](#tanja-auer-2025)

## Evidence

### Tanja Auer 2025

Tanja Auer, Marion Reindl, Burkhard Gniewosz. (2025). Is the clique a pond? The big-fish-little-pond effect and the relative meaning of clique and classroom. Learning and Instruction 95 (2025) 101997. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101997

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

BIRGE was tested with a continuous clique-level indicator (clique-average achievement subtracted from classroom-average achievement) in the same three-level model. The printed interval [–0.323, 0.104] includes zero; no effect size is reported, so the null is not evidence of equivalence.

> "However, no statistically significant assimilations (BIRGE) were found at the clique level: b = 0.10, posterior SD = 0.11, 95% CI [–0.323, 0.104]."
