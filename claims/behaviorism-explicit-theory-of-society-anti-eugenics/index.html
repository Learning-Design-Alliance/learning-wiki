Philip 2020¶

Philip, T., & Sengupta, P. (2020). Theories of Learning as Theories of Society: A Contrapuntal Approach to Expanding Disciplinary Authenticity in Computing. Journal of the Learning Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2020.1828089

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Historical interpretation offered in the article's analysis of behaviorism as theory of society. The authors cite Watson's (1924) dispute with eugenics and Skinner's two books as evidence that behaviorism was explicitly a theory of society in "direct resistance to eugenics."

"Walden Two (Skinner, 1948) and Beyond Freedom and Dignity (Skinner, 1971) are unequivocal testaments that B.F. Skinner's research on human behavior was tied to a utopian vision of society founded on cultural engineering"
