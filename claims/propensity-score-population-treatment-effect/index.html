Roschelle 2018¶

Menlo Park, CA: SRI International. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7a123d3c-22ec-46db-b7cf-709c3aa65583

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Propensity score analysis comparing 92 study schools with 1,713 Texas non-charter schools on 26 school-level covariates; Table 2 shows the post-stratification estimate (1.47, SE 0.15) versus the conventional estimate (1.44, SE 0.14).

"Using the data in this study plus the pilot, we estimate the generalizability index to be β = 0.91."
