### M Tuveri 2024

M Tuveri, A Steri and D Fadda. (2024). Using storytelling to foster the teaching and learning of gravitational waves physics at high-school. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad4b87](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad4b87)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

MANOVA over 75 students (23 humanities, 52 scientific) on five questionnaire domains found a significant multivariate school effect (Wilk's lambda .704, F= 5.05, p < 0.1). Means were higher for humanities on Monologue (4.48 vs 4.11) and Poems (4.41 vs 3.68), higher for scientific on STEM Engagement (4.54 vs 4.26).

> "showed a significant multivariate difference only for the school (Wilkis lambda .704, F= 5.05, p < 0.1).In particular, the differences are associate to “STEM Engagement”, “The Monologue”, “Poems”."
