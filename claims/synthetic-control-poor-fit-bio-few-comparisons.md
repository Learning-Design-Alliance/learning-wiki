## Evidence

### Fesler 2022

Fesler, Lily and Lindsay Fox. (2022). No-deadlines Synthetic Control and Exploratory Outcomes Analysis and Recommendations for a Future Rigorous Evaluation [Memorandum]. Alexandria, VA: National Science Foundation. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/no-deadlines-synthetic-control-and-exploratory-outcomes-analysis-and-recommendations-for-a-future

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Assessment of synthetic control fit across the 12 NDL programs in the analytic sample, referencing Figure A10 and Table A10 in the technical appendix. The memo attributes the poor BIO pre-implementation fit to having "very few (only four) potential comparison programs for BIO" and reports BIO programs achieved the worst fit statistics.

> "This is likely because there are very few (only four) potential comparison programs for BIO, making it difficult to estimate counterfactuals that closely align their outcomes over the pre-implementation years. Indeed, BIO programs generally achieved the worst fit statistics as well"
