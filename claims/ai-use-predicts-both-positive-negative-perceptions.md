## Evidence

### Gyeonggeon “Boaz” Lee 2025

Gyeonggeon “Boaz” Lee (2025). High School Science Profile Predicts Adults’ Views on the Future of AI and STS. [Version 1 – Last modified: Aug 16, 2025]. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.12083](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.12083)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM path results from the 2023 KEEP II wave, where AI use was measured with four service items and perceptions with items imagining AI "in 10 years". The article prints these standardized coefficients and notes positive and negative perceptions were not merged because reliability showed no unidimensionality.

> "AI use showed significantly positive paths both on AI positive perception (β = .412, p < .001) and AI negative perception (β = .295, p < .001). Positive and negative perceptions of AI showed a significant correlation (β = .297, p < .001)."
