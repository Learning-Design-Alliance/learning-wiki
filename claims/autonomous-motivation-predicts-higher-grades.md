## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Autonomous motivation measured at the start of the year positively predicts end-of-year grades, with standardized coefficients of .12 (high school) and .33 (middle school). [→ Mouratidis 2021](#mouratidis-2021)

## Evidence

### Mouratidis 2021

Mouratidis, A., Michou, A., Sayil, M., & Altan, S. (2021). It is autonomous, not controlled motivation that counts: Linear and curvilinear relations of autonomous and controlled motivation to school grades. Learning and Instruction, 73, 101433. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2020.101433

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Two-sample longitudinal study: motivation measured in class at T1, grades collected six months (Sample 1, n = 3094) and two months later (Sample 2). The article reports grades "predicted positively by T1 autonomous motivation" in both samples (β = .12 and .33, printed in Table 2).

> "end-of-school-year grades in either mathematics or Turkish language subject matter were predicted positively by T1 autonomous motivation and negatively by T1 controlled motivation"
