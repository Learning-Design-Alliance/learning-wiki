## Evidence

### Zertuche 2012

Zertuche, A., Gerard, L., & Linn, M. C. (2012). How do Openers Contribute to Student Learning? International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(1), 79-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070484

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of original and revised embedded-assessment responses scored with the knowledge integration rubric: 105 Opener pairs vs 30 no-Opener pairs. Table 3 reports revision rates of 66% (Opener) vs 33% (no Opener).

> "students who had an Opener ( M = .29, SD = .78) doubled the mean gain score of those students who did not have an Opener ( M = .13, SD = .51) on the embedded assessment. There was no significant difference between conditions in students' pre-Opener scores."
