## Evidence

### Tsompanoudi et al. 2015

Despina TSOMPANOUDI, Maya SATRATZEMI, Stelios XINOGALOS. (2015). Distributed Pair Programming Using Collaboration Scripts: An Educational System and Initial Results. Informatics in Education. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Completion-time analysis of logged driving and total times across five projects in the 24-pair evaluation. The article concludes "structured collaboration does not influence the completion time of assignments compared to free collaboration".

> "In contrast to our expectations, the experimental group achieved in average a shorter completion and driving time in each project (Fig. 7). However, analysis of this data showed a statistically significant difference only in the last project (t-test results: t (22) = –3.995, p = 0.001)."
