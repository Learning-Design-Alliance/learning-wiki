# A short-term social skills teaching program increases inclusive classroom teachers' knowledge of teaching social skills

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers trained in the SSTP scored significantly higher on the Social Skills Teaching Knowledge Test at posttest than untrained control teachers, while the groups did not differ at pretest. [→ Elif SAZAK PINAR 2013](#elif-sazak-pinar-2013)
`q2 i?` Within the experimental group, SSTKT scores rose significantly from pretest to posttest, whereas control group scores did not change significantly. [→ Elif SAZAK PINAR 2013 (2)](#elif-sazak-pinar-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Elif SAZAK PINAR 2013

Elif SAZAK PINAR, Bülbin SUCUOGLU. (2013). The Outcomes of a Social Skills Teaching Program for Inclusive Classroom Teachers. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 13(4), 2247-2261. https://www.edam.com.tr/kuyeb

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study of 29 inclusive grade 1-2 teachers (12 experimental, 17 control) in Bolu, Turkey. Mann-Whitney U tests of SSTKT scores showed "a significant difference between the posttest scores of the two groups was found" with U=.00, p=.00 at posttest and no pretest difference (U=91.5, p=.63).

> "although there was no significant difference between the mean ranks of the pretest SSTKT scores of the experiment and control groups, a significant difference between the posttest scores of the two groups was found (pretest for the experiment and control group U=91.5, p=.63, post test for the experiment and control group U=.00, p=.00)"

### Elif SAZAK PINAR 2013 (2)

Elif SAZAK PINAR, Bülbin SUCUOGLU. (2013). The Outcomes of a Social Skills Teaching Program for Inclusive Classroom Teachers. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 13(4), 2247-2261. https://www.edam.com.tr/kuyeb

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests within each group: the experimental group's pretest-posttest difference was significant (z= 3.79; p=.00) while the control group's was not (z= 1.75; p=.08), supporting that the SSTP, not time, produced the knowledge gain.

> "the Wilcoxon signed-rank test results indicate that there was a significant difference between the SSKTK pretest-posttest scores of teachers in the experiment group (z= 3.79; p=.00). However, no significant difference was found between the SSKTK pretest-posttest scores of teachers in the control group (z= 1.75; p=.08)"
