## Evidence

### Trezek and Mayer 2019

Beverly Trezek and Connie Mayer. (2019). Reading and Deafness: State of the Evidence and Implications for Research and Practice. Educ. Sci. 2019, 9, 216. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci9030216

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Large-scale norming studies using the Stanford Achievement Test for Hearing Impaired (SAT-HI), beginning in 1974 and continuing through 2003, with normative samples of nearly 7000 students in 1974 and approximately 3500 in 2003. The article reports "the median grade equivalent never exceeded the fourth grade level for any given cohort."

> "Considering the data for the reading comprehension subtest across 11 cohorts of students over time (i.e., students age 8 through 18 from 1974 to 2003), performance levels were shown to increase slightly as a function of age. However, the median grade equivalent never exceeded the fourth grade level for any given cohort."
