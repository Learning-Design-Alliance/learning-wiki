q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-sectional survey analysis of Question C1 across four subjects in the n = 416 sample, visualized in Fig. 3. The article reports that "51.2% of computer science students view them as an integrated, legitimate tool, with very few reporting total prohibitions (8.2%)" and that economics occupies an intermediate position.

"Across all years, 65.9% of mathematics students and 55.3% of science students report prohibition. A Kruskal-Wallis H test confirmed that these differences in regulatory configurations are statistically significant across the four subjects (H = 360.15, p < .001)"
