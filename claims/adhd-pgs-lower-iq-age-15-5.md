## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In ALSPAC adolescents, higher ADHD polygenic risk scores were associated with decreased IQ at age 15.5 years (β = −0.8 units of IQ per unit increase in polygenic score, 95% CI −1.2 to −0.4, P = 2.4 × 10−4). [→ Stergiakouli Evie 2017](#stergiakouli-evie-2017)
