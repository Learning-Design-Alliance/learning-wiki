## Evidence

### Schaffrath K 2025

Schaffrath K, Ooms M, Bock A, Katz MS, Hölzle F, Modabber A. (2025). Improvement of surgical skills in students using a newly developed 3D printed osteotomy model of a partially retained wisdom tooth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08394-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student evaluation sheets (10-point Likert) after the osteotomy exercise plus the oral-surgeon questionnaire. The article reports all three groups' agreement that combining model practice with clinical experience is best, with the printed medians.

> "All participants (PE: 8.5 (3), CE: 10 (1) and OS: 10 (5)) agreed that the combination of practicing on the model with clinical experience is the best option to prepare for daily practice (Table 5)."

### Schaffrath K 2025 (2)

Schaffrath K, Ooms M, Bock A, Katz MS, Hölzle F, Modabber A. (2025). Improvement of surgical skills in students using a newly developed 3D printed osteotomy model of a partially retained wisdom tooth. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08394-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Oral-surgeon questionnaire on exercise quality (Table 2). The surgeons rated "Phantom models alone cannot replace clinical experience" at a median of 3 (6), consistent with the students' low ratings of model-replaces-clinic.

> "Nevertheless, a combination of model exercises and clinical experience is valued as the best for preparing participants for patients' treatment (10 (5)). Phantom models alone cannot replace clinical experience (3 (6))."
