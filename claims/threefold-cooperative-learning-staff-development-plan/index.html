Rood 1998¶

Rood, M. E. (1998). Cooperative Learning Staff Development. Final Report. Fiscal Year 1997-98. Luzerne County Community College. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED427216

q2 · i? · the article prints no effect size for this finding · design · r1

Project report describing a 353 staff development project in which nine instructors attended three sessions, completed peer review observations, and contributed scripts. The report states the three-part plan "was essential to follow through by instructional staff"; all listed objectives were reported met.

"The three fold staff development plan was essential to follow through by instructional staff to implement cooperative learning structures and techniques: (1) immersion within cooperative learning activities; (2) peer monitoring and review; and (3) contributing to a cooperative learning manual."
