### Yepes-Velasco Andres Felipe 2026

Yepes-Velasco Andres Felipe, Solano Andres, Hernandez Jorge, Mora-Salamanca Andrés Felipe, Moreno-Araque Jeimy Lorena. (2026). From image to clinical judgment: a randomized near-peer teaching trial on systematic chest radiograph interpretation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08971-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled educational trial of 50 final-year medical students; after a common two-hour resident-led lecture, the intervention arm received a one-hour checklist session. The Results section reports the higher intervention medians, and the article states the differences were statistically significant (p < 0.05).

> "In contrast, the intervention group achieved higher median scores, with 6 points for interpretation (IQR: 6–7) and 4 points for diagnosis (IQR: 3–6)."
