Exploratory self-efficacy composites compared across the two interactive conditions (n = 16), with a large Cohen's d of 1.59 for confidence. The Satisfaction composite showed poor reliability (α = .128) and the article recommends treating that comparison cautiously; self-efficacy results are interpreted as exploratory.

> "Confidence, based on Skill and Comprehension, was significantly higher in the Engineering Toolkit group (M = 4.00, SD = .40) than in the Mixed Reality group (M = 3.50, SD = .20), t(14) = 3.18, p = .007, d = 1.59."
