Evidence¶

Marshall A. Baker & J. Shane Robinson 2017¶

Marshall A. Baker & J. Shane Robinson. (2017). The Effects of an Experiential Approach to Learning on Student Motivation. Journal of Agricultural Education, 58(3), 150-167. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2017.03150

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized experimental study; 45 students assigned to the experiential treatment and 42 to direct instruction, taught a four-hour wind turbine design unit. The omnibus MANOVA "yielded no statistically significant differences" (Λ = .92, F = 1.70, p = .16); equivalence was not tested.

"The omnibus multivariate analysis (see Table 2) yielded no statistically significant differences of motivational measures between the experiential learning and direct instruction treatments."
