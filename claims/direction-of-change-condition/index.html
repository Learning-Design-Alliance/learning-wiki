Eitel 2026¶

Eitel, A., Kienitz, A., Krebs, M.-C., & Schöne, C. (2026). Advocating the FoRe-Squares Model of Technology-Augmented Instruction: a Reply to Mayer (2025). Educational Psychology Review, 38, 65. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10163-2

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical prediction in the reply, illustrated with humor in instruction. The authors note recent research (Bender et al., 2026; Tavakolian et al., 2025) found this pattern for relevant versus irrelevant humor in science instruction.

"Because content-irrelevant humor may reduce guidance towards the important concepts to be learned, the increase in reward would be accompanied by a decrease in focus support and should not significantly foster learning."
