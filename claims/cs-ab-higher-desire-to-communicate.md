# The CS+AB agent version produced significantly higher expected desire to communicate than both the CS-only and AB-only versions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learners who interacted with the CS+AB version reported significantly higher expected desire to communicate than learners in the CS-only group (p = 0.012) and the AB-only group (p = 0.011). [→ Emmanuel Ayedoun 2018](#emmanuel-ayedoun-2018)

## Evidence

### Emmanuel Ayedoun 2018

Emmanuel Ayedoun, Yuki Hayashi, Kazuhisa Seta. (2018). Adding Communicative and Affective Strategies to an Embodied Conversational Agent to Enhance Second Language Learners' Willingness to Communicate. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0171-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same ANCOVA analysis of the pilot study's Phase 1 WTC measures, expected desire to communicate differed significantly among the three system-version groups, F(2, 36) = 6.466, p < .05; the CS+AB group exceeded both the CS-only and AB-only groups. No effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant difference in participants ' expected desire to communicate [F(2, 36) = 6.466, p < .05] among the three groups. The post hoc tests (Table 8) showed that Group 1 's desire to communicate was significantly higher than Group 2 's( p = 0.012) and Group 3 's( p = 0.011 )."
