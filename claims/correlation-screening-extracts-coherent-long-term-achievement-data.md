## Evidence

### Satoshi Takahashi 2021

Satoshi Takahashi, Hiroki Kuno, Atsushi Yoshikawa. (2021). Extracting candidate factors affecting long-term trends of student abilities across subjects. A preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.06446](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.06446)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Correlation analysis of correct-answer ratios in national language tests for group 1 (168 students) in evaluation experiment 1. Within-organization A consecutive-test correlations were 0.76 or higher, while cross-organization correlations were "0.62 or lower", so organization B tests were excluded.

> "According to these results, the evaluation criteria between the tests for organizations A and B seemed different. Therefore, we excluded the achievement test for organization B."
