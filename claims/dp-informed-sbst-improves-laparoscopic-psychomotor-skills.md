### Wickramasinghe D 2025

Wickramasinghe D, Vincent J. (2025). The use of deliberate practice in simulation-based surgical training for laparoscopic surgery – a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07613-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review of ten studies (five RCTs, five observational) on DP-informed SBST for laparoscopic surgery; the review reports that "DP-informed SBST was consistently associated with improved psychomotor skills". No pooled effect size is printed.

> "DP-informed SBST was consistently associated with improved psychomotor skills, particularly when feedback was immediate, and training incorporated structured task progression."
