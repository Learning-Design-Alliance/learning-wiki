## Evidence

### Bochatay N 2025

Bochatay N, Ju M, O'Brien BC, van Schaik SM. (2025). A Scoping Review of Interprofessional Simulation-Based Team Training Programs. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000792

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's mapping of assessed behaviors onto IPEC domains across 58 articles; it further reports "values and ethics for interprofessional collaboration were only assessed in 4 (6.9%)."

> "In mapping the assessed collaborative behaviors to IPEC domains, we found that 19 (32.8%) articles reported assessment of 1 competency; 15 (25.9%) assessed 2 competencies; 21 (36.2%) assessed 3 competencies; and 3 (5.2%) articles assessed all 4 competencies."

### Bochatay N 2025 (2)

Bochatay N, Ju M, O'Brien BC, van Schaik SM. (2025). A Scoping Review of Interprofessional Simulation-Based Team Training Programs. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000792

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that teamwork and communication dominated assessment, consistent with CRM reliance, while interprofessional-specific communication was rarely targeted.

> "All but one article described assessment tools that focused on teamwork, and 39 (67.2%) assessed communication, consistent with reliance on CRM and related frameworks by the majority of programs."
