## Evidence

### Telli Serkan 2026

Telli Serkan, Camgöz Süleyman, Yaman Merve, Arslan Seyda Ferah. (2026). Facilitated interactive video versus simulation-based training after a standardized lecture for basic life support in medical students: a randomized trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09128-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank within-group pre–post comparisons in the randomized trial (Table 3). Both groups improved significantly, with "very large effect sizes" of r = -0.856 (video) and r = -0.806 (simulation), both p < 0.001.

> "Both groups demonstrated statistically significant improvements from pre-test to post-test, with very large effect sizes (video group: Z = − 6.631, p < 0.001, r = − 0.856, 95% CI − 0.912 to − 0.769; simulation group: Z = − 6.300, p < 0.001, r = − 0.806, 95% CI − 0.879 to − 0.695)."
