## Evidence

### Alhamed 2023

Alhamed, A. A. (2023). The link among academic stress, sleep disturbances, depressive symptoms, academic performance, and the moderating role of resourcefulness in health professions students during COVID-19 pandemic. Journal of Professional Nursing, 46, 83-91. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.profnurs.2023.02.010

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Stepwise linear regression and moderation analysis (n = 93) tested H3; resourcefulness's regression coefficient was −0.099 (non-significant), and "adding resourcefulness to the model did not change the magnitude or the direction of the relationship between academic stress and depressive symptoms".

> "despite being significantly correlated with depressive symptoms when controlling for academic stress and sleep disturbances, resourcefulness was not a significant predictor of depressive symptoms."
