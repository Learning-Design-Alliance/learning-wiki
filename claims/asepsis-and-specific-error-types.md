## Evidence

### Salomez-Ihl Cordélia 2025

Salomez-Ihl Cordélia, Chapuis Claire, Bedouch Pierrick, Albaladejo Pierre, Picard Julien. (2025). Assessment of a simulation-based implementation method to support the introduction of a new invasive device: a prospective cross-over study. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003029

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Error-type analysis in the cross-over study: asepsis of the sampling site was generally poorly performed, with zero compliance with the 30-second disinfection time in both groups. The authors suggest caregivers can be less rigorous in simulation than in clinical practice.

> "None of the participants in either GA or GB complied with the 30 seconds asepsis recommended by the plant’s hygiene committee."

### Salomez-Ihl Cordélia 2025 (2)

Salomez-Ihl Cordélia, Chapuis Claire, Bedouch Pierrick, Albaladejo Pierre, Picard Julien. (2025). Assessment of a simulation-based implementation method to support the introduction of a new invasive device: a prospective cross-over study. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003029

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Type-of-error analysis in the same study identified a significant difference between GA and GB in the proportion of four mistakes, all related to the ergonomics of the new IABPS; no effect size is printed.

> "There is a significant difference in the proportion of four mistakes: the direction in which the blood saver is attached, insufficient blood savings before blood sampling, sub-optimal use of flush and the removal of IABPS head cap."
