`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Secondary outcome in the same intention-to-treat cluster randomized trial: physical fitness assessed with reference to the 2018 National Physical Fitness Survey Monitoring Programme in China. The intervention group scored "81.49 (6.26) points" versus 76.24 (7.29) in controls; the improvement during follow-up was 4.67 points (95% CI, 3.17-6.18) higher in the intervention group.

> "The mean (SD) overall physical fitness score at the end of 1 academic year was 81.49 (6.26) points in the intervention group and 76.24 (7.29) points in the control group, with an adjusted mean difference of 4.95 points (95% CI, 3.56-6.34; P < .001)"
