### Paul Justice 2020

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Development, validation and in-class evaluation of a sequence of clicker questions on Larmor precession of spin in quantum mechanics. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.justice](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.justice)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Cross-year comparison of the two classes in the in-class evaluation, from the results discussion of Tables 1 and 2. The article concludes the CQS "did not eliminate the performance gap in pretest" and proposes that certain threshold knowledge may be a prerequisite, particularly because peer instruction requires shared knowledge for meaningful peer discussion.

> "Class B, which exhibited higher pretest scores than Class A on all test questions, also exhibited higher posttest scores on five of the six test questions, and exhibited comparable posttest scores on question 4."
