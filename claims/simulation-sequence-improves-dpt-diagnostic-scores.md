## Evidence

### Eugenio Tufino 2026

Eugenio Tufino. (2026). A multi-agent AI classroom based on dual-process reasoning hazards: a pilot with prospective physics teachers. https://huggingface.co/spaces

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Pre/post pilot with n = 11 paired graduate students; nine improved, one stable, one declined by two points. The mean total score rose "from 13.9 to 16.7" with "effect size r = 0.79". POST vignettes used different characters and content, so gains reflect some transfer beyond the five agents.

> "On the 0–18 scale, the mean total score rose by almost three points, from 13.9 to 16.7 — a gain large enough to be statistically reliable even in this small sample (exact Wilcoxon signed -rank test, p = 0.014; effect size r = 0.79, large by conventional standards)."
