## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Retaining only nodes selected by 20% of students, MMA communities on the FMCE conform to prior item groups and are associated with four misconceptions: motion implies active forces, velocity-acceleration undiscriminated, and two Newton's 3rd law misconceptions. ## Evidence

### James Wells 2020

James Wells, Rachel Henderson, Adrienne Traxler, Paul Miller, and John Stewart. (2020). Exploring the Structure of Misconceptions in the Force and Motion Conceptual Evaluation with Modified Module Analysis. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010121](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010121)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Network analysis (MMA) of 3956 FMCE pretest and 3719 post-test incorrect responses from an introductory calculus-based mechanics course, with 1000 bootstrapped replications and communities retained at community fraction C > 0.8. The article reports "Only four misconceptions were identiﬁed" at the 20% threshold, with communities matching item groups from prior work.

> "Only four misconceptions were identiﬁed retaining nodes selected by the 20% of the students: motion implies active forces, velocity-acceleration undiscriminated, and two Newton's 3rd law misconceptions."
