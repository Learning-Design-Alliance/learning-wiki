### Delbene Lara 2026

Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study (Mirzaei et al. 2020, Iran) comparing eight hours of lecturing alone with lecturing plus a two-hour earthquake tabletop exercise in 74 hospital nurses. The review reports both methods improved outcomes ("p < 0.001") with greater gains in the tabletop group; no effect size is printed.

> "Both methods significantly improved nurses’ knowledge, attitude, and performance (p < 0.001). Improvements were consistently greater in the lecturing–tabletop exercise group, with higher total preparedness scores at posttest and follow‐up."
