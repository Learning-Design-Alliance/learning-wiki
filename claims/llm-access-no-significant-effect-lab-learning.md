# In two laboratory experiments, LLM access during learning shows no statistically significant effect on overall learning outcomes in Python coding

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Study 2, the treatment effect of LLM access on post-test performance is not significant after adjusting for pre-treatment covariates. [→ Matthias Lehmann 2024](#matthias-lehmann-2024)
`q2 i?` In Study 3 (with copy and paste), the treatment effect on overall learning outcomes remains insignificant when controlling for observed pre-treatment covariates. [→ Matthias Lehmann 2024 (2)](#matthias-lehmann-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Matthias Lehmann 2024

Matthias Lehmann, Philipp B. Cornelius, and Fabian J. Sting. (2024). AI Meets the Classroom: When Do Large Language Models Harm Learning? arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2409.09047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2409.09047)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Incentivized, pre-registered lab experiment (Study 2, 107 subjects) teaching Python with random assignment to LLM access during a 45-minute learning phase. The regression-adjusted treatment effect on the post-test is not significant: "the treatment effect is not significant (column 1,𝑝 = 0.580)".

> "As in the model-free analysis, the treatment effect is not significant (column 1,𝑝 = 0.580)."

### Matthias Lehmann 2024 (2)

Matthias Lehmann, Philipp B. Cornelius, and Fabian J. Sting. (2024). AI Meets the Classroom: When Do Large Language Models Harm Learning? arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2409.09047](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2409.09047)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 3 replicates Study 2 with copy and paste enabled (69 subjects). The covariate-adjusted treatment effect on the post-test remains insignificant: "the treatment effect on overall learning outcomes remains insignificant if we control for observed pre-treatment covariates(column1, 𝑝 =0.298)".

> "In Table 7, the treatment effect on overall learning outcomes remains insignificant if we control for observed pre-treatment covariates(column1, 𝑝 =0.298)."
