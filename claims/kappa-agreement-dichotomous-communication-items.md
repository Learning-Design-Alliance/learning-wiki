## Evidence

### Akan Hülya 2025

Akan Hülya, Delibaş Hamza Yasin, Koran Serhat, Yiğit Pakize, Tanriöver Özlem. (2025). Enhancing self-regulated learning: a comparison of medical students’ self-assessment and faculty evaluations on communication skills in simulated patient interviews. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07794-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cohen's Kappa analysis of thirteen yes-no items comparing 193 students' self-reports with trainer observations; Table 2 prints kappas of 0.287–0.395 for moderate items and 0.506 and 0.553 for the good-agreement items (p < 0.001).

> "moderate agreement (0.21–0.40) was observed in the answers given to “introducing oneself”, “starting with an open-ended question”, “using medical terms”, “dual question”, “making eye contact”. A better than average agreement (0.41–0.60) was observed for the questions “summarizing the interview” and “what the patient would like to add”"
