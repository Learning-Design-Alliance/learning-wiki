## Evidence

### Roberts 2008

Roberts, G., Torgesen, J. K., Boardman, A., & Scammacca, N. (2008). Evidence-Based Strategies for Reading Instruction of Older Students with Learning Disabilities. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice, 23(2), 63–69. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5826.2008.00264.x

`q3 · i3` · `review · r2`

Review by Scammacca et al. of comprehension strategy interventions for students with LD; the large pooled effect (g = 1.35) rests mostly on unstandardized measures, and the standardized-measures effect (g = .54) has a large standard error.

> "While a large overall effect was reported across the 12 studies in the sample ( g = 1.35, n = 12, 95 percent CI = .72, 1.97), only 2 of the 12 used standardized measures of reading comprehension."
