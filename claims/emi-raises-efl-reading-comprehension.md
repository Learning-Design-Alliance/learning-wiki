### Mohamed Farrag Ahmed Badawi 2019

Mohamed Farrag Ahmed Badawi. (2019). The Effect of Explicit English Morphology Instruction on EFL Secondary School Students’ Morphological Awareness and Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 12(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v12n4p166

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental control group study of 49 experimental and 49 control intact classes of first-year Egyptian secondary EFL students. Post-RCT means: experimental 15.73 (SD 2.79), control 8.22 (SD 2.40); "The t value was 14.29 and the P value was less than 0.0001".

> "Table 1 displays that the mean scores of the experimental group on the Post-RCT (15.73) is higher than the mean scores of the control group (8.22) on the same test. The t value was 14.29 and the P value was less than 0.0001"
