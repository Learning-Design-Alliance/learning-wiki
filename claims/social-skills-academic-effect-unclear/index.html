The review finds that most social-skills studies come from social and emotional learning research that blends other noncognitive factors with social skills, "making it difficult to assess the effect of enhanced social skills on academic outcomes." No effect sizes are printed.

"While there is evidence of the effect of social skills or behaviors on life and work outcomes, their effect on academic performance is unclear from the literature. Evidence is most clear that poor social skills are associated with negative outcomes."
