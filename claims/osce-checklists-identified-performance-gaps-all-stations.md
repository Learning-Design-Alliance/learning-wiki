## Evidence

### Wick Alexandra MD 2026

Wick Alexandra MD, Murray-Bainer Samantha MD, Tischendorf Jessica MD MS, Almasry Mazen MBBS, Coyle Andrew MD, Zamanian Maryam MD MS. (2026). Development and Implementation of an OSCE for Formative Assessment of Core Clinical Skills in Internal Medicine Interns. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11576

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Faculty checklist assessments across the four OSCE stations for 35 interns. For Station D, only "asking the nurse for questions (39.3%)" and "using closed-loop communication (40%)" fell below 80%; in every station more than 70% of assessments included a written comment identifying an area for improvement.

> "For Station C, 44.4% of interns addressed illness severity completely, 95% provided a patient summary, 78.5% provided an action list, 66.7% addressed situational awareness, and 56.8% ensured synthesis by the receiver."
