q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of 78 relevant studies identifying design methods for presence enhancement. The review reports "five primary design methods were identified as critical for enhancing presence in VR environments", with environmental design the most prevalent at 89.7% and biometric integration least at 7.7%.

"five primary design methods were identified as critical for enhancing presence in VR environments, as shown in Figure 9. These methods include environmental design (89.7%), multimodal sensory design (57.7%), Avatar design (57.7%), GUI interface design (29.5%), and biometric data integration (7.7%)"
