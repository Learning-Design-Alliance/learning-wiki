Afraz 2018¶

Afraz, S., Taghizade, L., & Taghinezhad, A. (2018). The Usefulness of Pictorial Aids in Developing Female Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners’ Speaking Proficiency. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.6n.1p.38

Paired-samples t-test comparing the experimental group's pre- and post-test speaking scores over six treatment sessions; the article reports a significant level of p<.001 with means of "14.95 and 18.30".

"According to the pre-test and post-test mean values of 14.95 and 18.30 as well as a significant level of p= (.000), p<.001 (table 3), the experimental group has shown a great difference at the end of the experiment."
