`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review reports, citing Myers et al. (2022), a meta-analysis of word problem-solving interventions for grades 1-5 in which sample difficulty status was a moderator; ten studies included students with co-occurring difficulties. The review also reports Zheng et al. (2013) found a negative effect (g = -0.45) on word problem-solving accuracy for students with co-occurring difficulties.

> "The authors found that studies focused on students with co-occurring difficulties were less effective by 60% of a SD unit than those that focused on students with mathematics difficulties only (Myers et al., 2022)."
