## Evidence

### Vangelis Tsiligkiris 2026

Vangelis Tsiligkiris. (2026). What students ask matters: LLM interaction depth, task quality, and immediate recall in higher education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00617-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical OLS regression with HC3-robust standard errors on 22 students' marked case analyses (blind-marked, ICC = 0.91). Depth was marginal before Volume was added (β = 5.29, p = .092) and "was significant" once Volume was controlled; Volume itself showed a marginal trend (β = 4.16, p = .051).

> "In the parsimony model controlling for Volume, Depth was significant (β = 6.27, p = .006), indicating that a one standard deviation increase in explanation-seeking prompts was associated with approximately 6.3 additional marks on the 0–100 task scale."
