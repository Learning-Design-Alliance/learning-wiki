## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Of 38 physicians completing both post-course CPD-REACTION and the six-month questionnaire, 31/38 (81.6%) reported adopting the targeted behavior, and their post-course intention was significantly higher (6.63, SD = 0.69) than that of the seven non-adopters (6.00, SD = 0.96), a difference of 0.63 (p = 0.02).
