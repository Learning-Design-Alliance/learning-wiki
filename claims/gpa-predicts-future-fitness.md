## Evidence

### Syväoja 2019

Syväoja, H. J., Kankaanpää, A., Joensuu, L., Kallio, J., Hakonen, H., Hillman, C. H., & Tammelin, T. H. (2019). The Longitudinal Associations of Fitness and Motor Skills with Academic Achievement. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise. https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002031

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Bivariate cross-lagged path modeling in the 954-student cohort found GPA at T1 predicted better aerobic fitness (B = 0.08), muscular fitness (B = 0.08), and motor skills (B = 0.09) a year later.

> "Better GPA at T1 predicted better aerobic and muscular fitness (B = 0.08, 99% CI = 0.03–0.13 and B = 0.08, 99% CI = 0.00–0.16, respectively) and motor skills (B = 0.09, 99% CI = 0.01–0.17) a year later, but not vice versa."
