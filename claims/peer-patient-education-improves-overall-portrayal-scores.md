## Evidence

### Pritchard Shane A. 2020

Pritchard Shane A., Keating Jennifer L., Nestel Debra, Blackstock Felicity C. (2020). Physiotherapy students can be educated to portray realistic patient roles in simulation: a pragmatic observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02382-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures observational study: blinded assessors rated pre- and post-program videos of 23 physiotherapy students portraying patient roles in practical exams. The pre-intervention mean total score was "29.1/50 (8.14)" and post-intervention "35.6/50 (7.56)", with the significant paired difference quoted above.

> "A significant difference was observed in favour of the post-intervention mean total scores (mean difference 6.5, 95%CI [1.51–11.45], p = 0.01)."
