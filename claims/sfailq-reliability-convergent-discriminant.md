# SFAILQ shows high reliability, convergent validity with digital literacy (r = 0.49–0.68), and discriminant validity (HTMT below 0.80)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Liu Qingqi 2026

Liu Qingqi, Miao Wenjiao, Li Jingjing, Lei Yuju. (2026). The Six-Facet Artificial Intelligence Literacy Questionnaire (SFAILQ): Assessing AI Literacy in Adolescents, Young Adults, and Midlife Adults. Behavioral Sciences, 16(7), 1110. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071110

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability and validity analysis in Sample 2 (N = 1226): Cronbach's alphas all exceed 0.85, CR values exceed 0.70, AVE values surpass 0.50, √AVE (0.74–0.84) exceeds inter-factor correlations, and inter-dimension correlations ranged from 0.43 to 0.72 without reaching 0.80.

> "the scores for both the individual dimensions and the overall AI literacy scale are significantly correlated with the scores of the digital literacy scale, with correlation coefficients ranging from 0.49 to 0.68."
