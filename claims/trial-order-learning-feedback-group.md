## Evidence

### Milička J 2025

Milička J, Marklová A, Drobil O, Pospíšilová E. (2025). Humans can learn to detect AI-generated texts, or at least learn when they can't. Preprint. Manuscript submitted to PLOS One. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.01877](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.01877)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed effects logistic regression on per-trial correctness in the feedback group of the 254-participant experiment; the article reports the printed estimate, CI and p-value, and notes the no-feedback group's trial-order effect was not significant (p=0.25, estimate -0.012).

> "The variable Trial order reached level of significance p < 0.001 in feedback group (CI 0.027–0.079). The log odds ratio estimate was at 0.053, that means, the later the trial, the higher probability that the participant answers correctly."
