Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of why UPM students and educators were "congruent in their selection of topics for RLO development", citing the smaller topic list (n = 34) and focused scope; this is an explanatory argument, not a tested comparison.

> "This could be because, the personal and professional development curriculum in UPM has fewer topics (n = 34) as compared to UM (n = 84) and TU Pharm (n = 87). The scope of the personal and professional curriculum was more focused, aiming at professionalism, ethics and law, cultural competence, and evidence-based medicine."
