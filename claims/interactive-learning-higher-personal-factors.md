## Evidence

### AlKhaibary AA 2021

AlKhaibary AA, Ramadan FZ, Aboshaiqah AE, Baker OG, AlZaatari SZ, AlZaatari SZ. (2021). Determining the effects of traditional learning approach and interactive learning activities on personal and professional factors among Saudi intern nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.633

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t tests on the five remaining personal-factor CAT-NE items in the 48-nurse comparative study; all favored the interactive group (interactive group means 4.5 vs traditional 2.2-2.3 in Table 2). No effect sizes printed.

> "Furthermore, enthusiasm (t (46) = 8.14, p < .0001), initiative (t (46) = 7.16, p < .0001), realistic self-confidence (t (46) = 8.03, p < .0001), competent cooperation with staff (t (46) = 7.39, p < .0001) and competent cooperation with patients (t (46) = 7.71, p < .0001) were significantly higher among interactive learning activity group, compared with traditional group."
