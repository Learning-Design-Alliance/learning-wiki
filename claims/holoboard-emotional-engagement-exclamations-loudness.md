## Evidence

### Jiangtao Gong 2021

Jiangtao Gong, Teng Han, Siling Gong, Jiannan Li, Siyu Zha, Liuxin Zhang, Feng Tian, Qianying Wang, and Yong Rui. (2021). HoloBoard: a Large-format Immersive Teaching Board based on pseudo HoloGraphics. In The 34th Annual ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST '21). https://doi.org/10.1145/3472749.3474761

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Acoustic analysis in pyAudioAnalysis of classroom audio, with the first quarter of each class as baseline, plus manual coding and Baidu AI Cloud semantic analysis. The article reports "a significant higher level of loudness in the HoloBoard group (p < .001)"; frequency showed no significant difference (p = .27). Only p-values are printed.

> "There was a significant higher level of loudness in the HoloBoard group (p < .001), whereas there was no significant difference in frequency between the two groups, indicated by the zero-crossing rate (p = .27)."
