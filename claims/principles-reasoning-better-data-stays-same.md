Andy Schang, Matthew Dew, Emily M. Stump, N. G. Holmes, and Gina Passante. (2023). More or better data: A new perspective on student reasoning about measurement uncertainty. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020105](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020105)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Coded explanations from the survey (Fig. 5); the Principles code marks reasoning that the distribution shape is caused by variability inherent to a theoretical abstraction of the experiment. Students argued the variability was inherent to the physical system and could not be removed even by experts with expert equipment.

> "For the smaller fraction of students who suggested the distributions would stay the same, the primary reasoning was the inherent principles of the physical or measurement processes in the Stern-Gerlach, Brownian Motion, and Single-Slit scenarios."
