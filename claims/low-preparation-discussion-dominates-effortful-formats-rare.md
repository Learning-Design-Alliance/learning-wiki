### AlRuthia Yazed 2019

The use of active learning strategies in healthcare colleges in the Middle East. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1580-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey results (Table 2) on technique frequencies among 722 responders. The authors interpret that approaches requiring significant effort to develop, such as gallery walk or gamification, were used by less than 3% of faculty.

> "The least used approaches were gallery walk (2.1%), gamification (2.8%) and learning cell (7.1%)."
