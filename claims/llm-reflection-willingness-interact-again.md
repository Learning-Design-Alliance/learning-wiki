> "students who were also given the opportunity to engage in reflection with the tutor reported a higher willingness to interact with the LLM tutor again, compared to students who only used the tutor to solve problems (Wilcoxon rank sum test: 𝑊 = 3097.00, 𝑝 = 0.046)"
