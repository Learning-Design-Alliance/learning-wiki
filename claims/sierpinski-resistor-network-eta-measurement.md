## Evidence

### C.E. Creffield 2022

C.E. Creffield. (2022). Fractals on a benchtop: Observing fractal dimension in a resistor network. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1119/5.0054306](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0054306)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Experimental measurement of corner-corner resistance across fractal levels of student-built networks, plotted log-log (Fig. 7); regression gives "η = 0.743± 0.002" against the theoretical 0.737. Error bars came from the variance of the three corner-pair resistances.

> "Making a linear regression to these data points yields a value of η = 0.743± 0.002, in excellent agreement with the theoretical value of η = 0.737."
