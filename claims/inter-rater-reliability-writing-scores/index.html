Evidence¶

Ho 2020¶

Ho, P. V. P., Phung, L. T. K., Oanh, T. T. T., & Giao, N. Q. (2020). Should Peer E-Comments Replace Traditional Peer Comments? International Journal of Instruction, 13(1), 295-314. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13120a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Reliability analysis of ratings by two trained inter-raters using an 11-band analytic rubric; the article reports correlations of r=.729, .868, .808, and .840 (all p=.000) for the two groups' pre- and post-tests, and states the scores have good correlation coefficient reliability.

"the correlation coefficient between the experimental group’s post -test scores is r=.840; p=.000. These results indicate that the writing scores have good correlation coefficient reliability."
