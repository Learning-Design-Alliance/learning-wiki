Pooja K. Agarwal 2024¶

Pooja K. Agarwal. (2024). Personal reflections on science communication and sharing retrieval practice research with teachers. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09839-w

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

The author's stated gap: while retrieval practice consistently improves learning in classrooms, a direct connection between teachers' learning about it and their implementation has yet to be established. This is an interpretive claim with no data offered.

"it is currently unclear whether science communication efforts on the science of learning influence decision making in the classroom, a critical consideration as the number of blogs, books, social media posts, online courses, and professional development workshops for teachers continues to grow"
