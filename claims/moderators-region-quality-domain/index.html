q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression moderator analyses (mixed-effects models, subgroups with ≥4 studies) across country, publication year, study quality, skill domain, child age group, and risk status. "stronger negative associations between child-caregiver ratio, group size and interactive skills were observed in higher-quality studies."

"Multiple associations differed significantly by region: education and work experience were more strongly linked to interactive skills in North America than in Europe. In contrast, less favorable child-caregiver ratios were more negatively associated with interactive skills in European studies."
