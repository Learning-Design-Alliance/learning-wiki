## Evidence

### Veaudor Martin 2018

Veaudor Martin, Gérinière Laurence, Souquet Pierre-Jean, Druette Loïc, Martin Xavier, Vergnon Jean-Michel, Couraud Sébastien. (2018). High-fidelity simulation self-training enables novice bronchoscopists to acquire basic bronchoscopy skills comparable to their moderately and highly experienced counterparts. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Satisfaction survey (eight items rated 0-6, summed to 0-48) administered after evaluations; the authors frame this as a Kirkpatrick level 1 evaluation suggesting good compliance for future training programs. No effect size printed.

> "The median global satisfaction scores were 44 (IQR = 4) for Group A-bis, 38 (IQR = 8) for Group B, and 35 (IQR = 9) for Group C (p = 0.02) (maximum possible score = 48; Additional file 2: Figure S4)."
