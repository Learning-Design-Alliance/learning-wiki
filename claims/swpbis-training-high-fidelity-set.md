# Schools trained in SWPBIS implement the model with high, sustained fidelity on the School-Wide Evaluation Tool

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` SWPBIS-trained elementary schools showed a significant intervention condition by time interaction on the overall SET score, with large effects. [→ Catherine P. Bradshaw 2010](#catherine-p-bradshaw-2010)

## Evidence

### Catherine P. Bradshaw 2010

Catherine P. Bradshaw, Mary M. Mitchell, & Philip J. Leaf. (2010). Examining the Effects of Schoolwide Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports on Student Outcomes: Results From a Randomized Controlled Effectiveness Trial in Elementary Schools. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions, 12(3), 133-148. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300709334798

`q3 · i3`

Repeated measures GLM on school-level SET data from a 5-year group-randomized trial of 37 Maryland elementary schools (21 SWPBIS, 16 comparison). The interaction was significant: "Wilks's Λ = .38, F(4, 32) = 13.36, p = .001, η2 = .63, d = 3.22".

> "Analysis of the SET data suggested a significant intervention effect (i.e., Intervention Condition  × Time Interaction) for the overall SET score, Wilks's Λ = .38, F(4, 32) = 13.36, p = .001, η2 = .63, d = 3.22"
