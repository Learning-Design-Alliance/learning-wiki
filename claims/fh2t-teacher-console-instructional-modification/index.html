Semi-structured 1:1 interviews with 11 confirmatory study educators after approximately four weeks of implementation. Educator Bianca Summers "used the teacher console to record the problems where the students used the most tries" and led whole-class work on those problems.

"she used the teacher console to record the problems where the students used the most tries. She explained that she then "displayed two of [those] problems on the board and [the class] worked through them together so that students could better understand how to deconstruct and reconstruct the number to reach the goal.""
