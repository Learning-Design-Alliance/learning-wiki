`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post assessment of 835 active birth attendants using the standard 17-item HBB Knowledge Check (pass = 14/17 correct) before and after three-day facility training. The article reports the pass rate rose from "74% who passed it pre-training" to 99% post-training, p < 0.0001; no effect size is printed.

> "Initial HBB training improved knowledge significantly: 99% of the trainees passed the knowledge test post-training compared to 74% who passed it pre-training (p < 0.0001)."
