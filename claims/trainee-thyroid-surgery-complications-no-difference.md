## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no statistically significant difference in postoperative complications between patients operated on by surgical trainees and those operated on by consultants. [→ Ambe Peter C. 2016](#ambe-peter-c-2016)

## Evidence

### Ambe Peter C. 2016

Ambe Peter C., Wassenberg Dirk R. (2016). Is sutureless thyroid surgery safe in the hands of surgical trainees. A single centre retrospective study. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-016-1940-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective single-center chart review of 208 thyroid surgeries at St. Remigius Krankenhaus comparing 61 trainee cases with 147 consultant cases using Fisher's exact test; "There was no significant difference (p = 0.41)" between groups. No effect size was printed.

> "There was no significant difference (p = 0.41) amongst both groups with respect to postoperative complications, Fig. 3."
