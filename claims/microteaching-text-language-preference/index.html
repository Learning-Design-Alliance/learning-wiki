Evidence¶

Mihci 2017¶

Mihci, C. & Ozdener Donmez, N. (2017). Teaching GUI-programming concepts to prospective K12 teachers: MIT App Inventor as an alternative to text based languages. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 3(2), 543-559. https://doi.org/10.21890/ijres.327912

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of 20-minute micro-teaching videos shot one year after the course, with free choice of language: of the visual-language choices, 3% used MIT Scratch, 1% FCPRO3, and 1% MIT App Inventor; 15% used no specific language or environment.

"out of all the students in the Experimental Group 2 (n = 101), 80% have chosen to use a text -based language in their micro -teaching videos; whereas 5% have chosen visual languages."
