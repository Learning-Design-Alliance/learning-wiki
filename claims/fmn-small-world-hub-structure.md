### Massimo Stella 2020

Massimo Stella. (2020). Forma mentis networks reconstruct how Italian high schoolers and international STEM experts perceive teachers, students, scientists, and school. [doi:10.3390/educsci10010017](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10010017)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Network analysis of the students' and researchers' forma mentis networks against configuration-model nulls (Table 1) reports heavy-tailed degree distributions, degree disassortativity, and mean distances of about 4-5 associations between concepts.

> "These results indicate that both the forma mentis networks display a peculiar small-world structure , guaranteeing short distances between words like in randomized networks, but also distributing connectivity mainly through hubs rather than through clustering"
