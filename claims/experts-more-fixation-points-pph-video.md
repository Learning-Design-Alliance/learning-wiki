## Evidence

### Desvergez A 2019

Desvergez A, Winer A, Gouyon J-B, Descoins M. (2019). An observational study using eye tracking to assess resident and senior anesthetists’ situation awareness and visual perception in postpartum hemorrhage high fidelity simulation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0221515

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Eye-tracking analysis (EyeTribe tracker, OGAMA software, 17 regions of interest) of 2958 fixation points from 29 anesthetists viewing the video; an ANOVA on number of fixations showed the group effect with F (1, 2754) = 14.5; p <0.001. The authors interpret increased fixations as more frequent updating of critical data.

> "Analysis of the eye-tracking data revealed that Experts have in average 8.28% more fixating points than Novices (in average per participants respectively n = 791.4 vs. 725.9, F (1, 2754) = 14.5; p <0.001)."
