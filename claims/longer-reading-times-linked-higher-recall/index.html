Evidence¶

Pellicer-Sánchez 2016¶

Pellicer-Sánchez, Ana. (2016). Incidental L2 Vocabulary Acquisition From and While Reading: An Eye-Tracking Study. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, 38(1), 97-130. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263115000224

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The study compared reading behavior on nonwords learned versus not learned in the vocabulary tests using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on summed total reading times per participant; the abstract states the positive relationship between online reading and vocabulary recall outcomes. No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Results also showed a positive relationship between new vocabulary learning outcomes and online reading, with longer reading times associated with higher vocabulary recall test scores."
