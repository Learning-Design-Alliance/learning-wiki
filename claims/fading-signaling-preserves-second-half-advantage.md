## Evidence

### Neslihan Yondemir Çalişkan 2025

Neslihan Yondemir Çalişkan, Emine Şendurur. (2025). The effect of cumulative eye movements' guidance of experts on transition from novice to expert. Education Tech Research Dev, 73:59–89. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10414-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing weeks 5–8 rubric scores, after signaling elements were gradually reduced from the fifth video. The analysis "demonstrated significant mean differences between second-half performance scores of groups with medium effect size, t(28) = 2.965, p < 0.05, r = 0.531."

> "Descriptive statistics show that the second-half performance scores of the experimental group (M = 76.30, SD = 7.93) are higher than the control group (M = 64.17, SD = 13.73)."
