`q2 i2` In Fall 2014, ANOVA revealed no significant effect of VOLTA on gain scores at the 95% level, though the calculated Cohen's d of 0.49 indicated a medium effect. [→ Saleheen 2018](#saleheen-2018)

## Evidence

### Saleheen 2018

Saleheen, F., Wang, Z., Picone, J., Butz, B. P., and Won, C.-H. (2018). Efficacy of a Virtual Teaching Assistant in an Open Laboratory Environment for Electric Circuits. Advances in Engineering Education. http://www.temple.edu/csnap/volta.html

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA on gain scores of 28 students in 11 lab assignments in the Fall 2014 TA vs. VOLTA LAB 1 experiment, where both groups had TA assistance and the experimental group also used VOLTA v1. The article reports "no significant effect" with F(1,10) = 1.878, p = .1714, and separately d = 0.49, a medium effect.

> "The ANOVA test revealed that there is no significant effect of VOL TA on the gain scores of Cohort 1, F(1,10) = 1.878, p   = .1714"
