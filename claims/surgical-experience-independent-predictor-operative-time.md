## Evidence

### Yusuf S 2026

Yusuf S, Eyup DI, Emre E, Necati E, Dursun B, Arda T. (2026). Operative time-based learning curve and surgical outcomes of laparoscopic radical prostatectomy in a surgeon with limited open surgery experience. BMC Surgery 26:184. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03577-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regression (N = 90, Enter method, Adjusted R² = 0.245) identified four independent predictors of operative time; blood loss, LND, and prostate volume prolonged duration while case number shortened it, per the printed coefficients.

> "In the multivariate analysis, four independent predictors of operative duration were identified: Estimated Blood Loss (B = 0.092, p < 0.001), Surgical Experience (Case Number) (B = − 0.491, p = 0.008), Lymph Node Dissection (B = 19.850, p = 0.039), and Prostate Volume (B = 0.412, p = 0.042)."

### Yusuf S 2026 (2)

Yusuf S, Eyup DI, Emre E, Necati E, Dursun B, Arda T. (2026). Operative time-based learning curve and surgical outcomes of laparoscopic radical prostatectomy in a surgeon with limited open surgery experience. BMC Surgery 26:184. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03577-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' discussion notes the logarithmic model left "approximately 75.5% of the variance remained unexplained", attributing remaining variation to unmeasured factors such as anatomy, assistant skill, and surgeon cognition.

> "the logarithmic model accounted for only 11.7% of the total variance. Even after adjusting for blood loss, prostate volume, and lymph node dissection in the multivariate model, approximately 75.5% of the variance remained unexplained (Adjusted R² = 0.245)."
