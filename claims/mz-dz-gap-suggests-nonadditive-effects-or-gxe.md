## Evidence

### Eftedal 2025

Eftedal, N. H., Eilertsen, E. M., Sunde, H. F., Kleppestø, T. H., Ystrom, E., & Czajkowski, N. O. (2025). Beyond additive genetic effects: Explaining family resemblance in school performance across millions of pairs of Norwegian relatives. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America, 122(25). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2419627122

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation (Discussion) of their twin correlations from the national-test dataset, with Table 1 reporting MZ twin r = 0.863 and DZ twin r = 0.487. The article states this gap "constitutes further evidence for genetic effects".

> "The fact that correlations between monozygotic twins ("rMZ") are much higher than those between dizygotic twins ("rDZ") and regular siblings, at ~0.85 compared to ~0.50, constitutes further evidence for genetic effects."
