## Evidence

### Keller 2024

Keller, N. E., Salvi, C., Leiker, E. K., Gruber, M. J. & Dunsmoor, J. E. (2024). States of epistemic curiosity interfere with memory for incidental scholastic facts. npj Science of Learning 9:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00234-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manipulation-check ANOVA of average curiosity ratings in the main task (N = 239), with task version between subjects and pre-determined category within subjects. It showed "a main effect of the pre-determined curiosity category" and "no main effect of task version"; Table 1 gives means (e.g., high 3.244 ± 0.051 vs. low 2.163 ± 0.070 in Early Anticipation).

> "This analysis revealed a main effect of the pre-determined curiosity category (F (1, 236) = 469.80, p <0 . 0 0 1 ,η2G =0 . 3 2 2 ), no main effect of task version ( p = 0.299), and no significant interaction (p = 0.055)."
