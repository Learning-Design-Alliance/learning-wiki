q2 · i? · associational · r3

Finding reported in the Executive Summary of the report, based on analyses of the 1997 and 1999 citywide teacher surveys of professional development sources and quality. No effect size is printed for this comparison.

"We found that when teachers draw on a combination of sources, including teacher networks, external professional groups, and school-based activi- ties, their professional development is overall of higher quality than when they draw prima- rily on only one source."
