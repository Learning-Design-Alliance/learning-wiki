## Evidence

### Tobias Wrigstad and Elias Castegren 2019

Tobias Wrigstad and Elias Castegren. (2019). Mastery Learning-Like Teaching with Achievements. PACM Progr. Lang. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1906.03510](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1906.03510)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-evaluation based on the 2016 non-mandatory course evaluation, which asked 53 questions and was filled out by 80 students. The same evaluation found that "45% of students think that their stress-level increased due to the large number of checks". These are descriptive survey percentages with no inferential tests.

> "90% of the students felt that it was always clear to them what grades were attainable (66% strongly, 24% less strong). 75% of the students felt that the achievement system has helped them absorb the contents of the course."
