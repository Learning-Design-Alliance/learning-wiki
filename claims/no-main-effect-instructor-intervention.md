## Evidence

### Lim Jaeseo 2022

Lim Jaeseo, Ko Hyunwoong, Park Jooyong, Ihm Jungjoon. (2022). Effect of active learning and online discussions on the academic performances of dental students. BMC Medical Education, 22, 312. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03377-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on the online four-group experiment's transfer type item scores. The article reports no significant main effect of worksheet intervention, with "partial eta-squared = 0.030"; no standardized effect size convention is printed beyond the eta-squared, so impact is coded from the small eta-squared value.

> "However, no significant difference was found for the main effect of the discussion condition (discussion with worksheet versus. free discussions); F (1, 78) = 2.40, P = 0.125, partial eta-squared = 0.030;"
