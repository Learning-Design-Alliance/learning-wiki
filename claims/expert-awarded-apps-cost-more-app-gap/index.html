Vaala 2015¶

Vaala, S., Ly, A., & Levine, M.H. (2015). Getting a read on the app stores: A market scan and analysis of children's literacy apps. New York, NY: The Joan Ganz Cooney Center at Sesame Workshop. https://joanganzcooneycenter.org/publication/getting-a-read-on-the-app-stores-a-market-scan-and-analysis-of-childrens-literacy-apps/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Price comparison between the 88%-paid awarded subsample and Top 50 Paid apps in the 183-app market scan; Figure 3 reports t(110) = -2.57, p < .05. The description quotes the report's finding that "Expert-awarded tended to cost $1–2 more than the Top 50 Paid apps on average".

"We found a small but statistically significant difference in the mean price of apps which indicated that Expert-awarded tended to cost $1–2 more than the Top 50 Paid apps on average (see Figure 3)."
