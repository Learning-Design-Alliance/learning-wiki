## Evidence

### Amin Aspanani 2023

Amin Aspanani, Hosein Sadeqhi, Omid Athar. (2023). The relationship between visual memory and spatial intelligence with students’ academic achievement in anatomy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04327-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional correlational analysis of 148 medical students; Table 4 reports visual memory correlations with course grades: neuroanatomy r=0.282 (P=0.001), special senses r=0.238 (P=0.004), head and neck theory r=0.239 (P=0.003), practical head and neck r=0.293 (P>0.001).

> "Pearson correlation coefficient showed that in medical students there was a direct relationship between visual memory score and spatial intelligence score with scores of neuroanatomy courses, special senses, theoretical head and neck and practical head and neck (P-value<0.005)."
