Alvarez-Peregrina Cristina, Sánchez-Tena Miguel Ángel, Andreu-Vázquez Cristina, Villa-Collar Cesar. (2020). Visual Health and Academic Performance in School-Aged Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17072346

In the same screening, binocular vision disorders were roughly 2.6 times more prevalent in the bad-performance cohort (19.98% vs 7.66%, p < 0.001), and the text reports ocular motility was normal in "76.64% of children in the group with bad academic performance" versus 89.98% with good performance (p < 0.001).

> "Regarding binocular vision, 8.88% of children had disorders. The percentage increased in the cohort with bad academic performance (19.98%) and decreased in the cohort with good academic performance (7.66%) (p < 0.001)"
