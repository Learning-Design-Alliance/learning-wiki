## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Post intervention, students in treatment classes were significantly more engaged overall than students in control classes, with partial η = .549 for overall engaged behavior. [→ Katz 2013](#katz-2013)

## Evidence

### Katz 2013

Jennifer Katz. (2013). The Three Block Model of Universal Design for Learning (UDL): Engaging students in inclusive education. Canadian Journal of Education, 36(1). https://journals.sfu.ca/cje/index.php/cje

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental control group pretest-posttest study in 10 Manitoba schools; 631 students Grades 1-12, 94 observed students. MANCOVA controlling for teacher years of experience and education gave partial η = .549 for overall engaged behavior; Table 3 shows treatment means rose from 41.54 to 54.52 per 60 minutes while control means fell from 50.18 to 43.64.

> "Overall engaged behavior was significantly different between the treatment and control groups post intervention. Students in the treatment classes were significantly more engaged than students in control classes, with treatment group students’ scores increasing overall, and control group students’ scores decreasing."
