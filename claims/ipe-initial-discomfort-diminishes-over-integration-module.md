# Undergraduate health students initially experience discomfort in multiprofessional IPE teams, which diminishes over the integrated internship experience

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At the start of the integration module, students reported tension, fear and thoughts of giving up when placed in multiprofessional teams with students from other courses. [→ de Lima 2020](#de-lima-2020)
`q2 i?` Over the semester-long experience, these discomforts diminished and students reported working better with people from different backgrounds. [→ de Lima 2020 (2)](#de-lima-2020-2)

## Evidence

### de Lima 2020

de Lima, Ana Wládia Silva; Alves, Fábia Alexandra Pottes; Linhares, Francisca Márcia Pereira; da Costa, Marcelo Viana; Coriolano-Marinus, Maria Wanderleya de Louvor; de Lima, Luciane Soares. (2020). Perception and manifestation of collaborative competencies among undergraduate health students. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.3227.3240

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative intervention-research study; report from focus groups of 28 undergraduate health students in the internship integration module. A student reported: "I became tense and scared. We had not yet had the opportunity to work with others from different courses" and considered giving up.

> "At the beginning of the curricular internship’s integration module, there was some discomfort with the formation of multiprofessional teams composed of one student from each area"

### de Lima 2020 (2)

de Lima, Ana Wládia Silva; Alves, Fábia Alexandra Pottes; Linhares, Francisca Márcia Pereira; da Costa, Marcelo Viana; Coriolano-Marinus, Maria Wanderleya de Louvor; de Lima, Luciane Soares. (2020). Perception and manifestation of collaborative competencies among undergraduate health students. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.3227.3240

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same qualitative focus-group data, analyzed by dialectical hermeneutics. Students reported increased team resourcefulness and understanding, e.g. "I can work better and I learned from the experience".

> "Throughout the experience, these discomforts diminished, reducing the strangeness with the experimentation of integration."
