## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Martins Francisco Neto et al 2021

Martins Francisco Neto et al. (2021). Development and validation of a simulator for teaching minimally invasive thoracic surgery in Brazil. Acta Cirúrgica Brasileira. https://doi.org/10.1590/ACB360508

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Blind video assessment of 75 recorded sutures by two experienced VATS surgeons using an OSATS-adapted global rating scale and an author-created error scale; changes were not statistically significant. No effect size is printed.

> "The marginal means of OSATS 1 demonstrated a favorable evolution over the three stages of the experiment, with a progressive increase, but without statistical significance, similar to the one of OSATS 2."
