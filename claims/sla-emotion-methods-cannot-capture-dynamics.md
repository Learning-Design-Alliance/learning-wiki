# Existing SLA emotion research methods cannot capture the dynamic nature of emotions in language learning situations

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Cross-sectional, laboratory and longitudinal designs in SLA emotion research share one limitation: they do not allow analysis of the dynamic nature of emotions, because of singular timepoints, low sampling rates, or static statistical analyses. [→ Peng Wang 2024](#peng-wang-2024)

## Evidence

### Peng Wang 2024

Peng Wang, Lesya Ganushchak, Camille Welie, Roel van Steensel. (2024). The Dynamic Nature of Emotions in Language Learning Context: Theory, Method, and Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09946-2

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's methodological critique of quantitative SLA emotion research: cross-sectional studies "only sample at singular timepoints, and therefore cannot infer causality and temporal properties", and longitudinal studies typically use weekly, monthly, or annual intervals that miss subtle fluctuations.

> "These research approaches have important limitations, which together boil down to one issue: the fact that they do not allow to analyse the dynamic nature of emotions in language learning situations."
