## Evidence

### Buljan Ivan 2018

Buljan Ivan, Jerončić Ana, Malički Mario, Marušić Matko, Marušić Ana. (2018). How to choose an evidence-based medicine knowledge test for medical students? Comparison of three knowledge measures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1391-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Distributional analysis of post-test percent-of-maximum scores in the EBM group (Additional file 1: Figure S1; Friedman test P < 0.001, post hoc P ≤ 0.002) showing ACE score clustering at the high end.

> "Specifically, ACE test scores grouped at the high end of the score scale that ranged from 0 to 100%, whereas the groupings of Berlin and Fresno test scores were much more dispersed."

### Buljan Ivan 2018 (2)

Buljan Ivan, Jerončić Ana, Malički Mario, Marušić Matko, Marušić Ana. (2018). How to choose an evidence-based medicine knowledge test for medical students? Comparison of three knowledge measures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1391-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test item-difficulty analysis in the EBM group comparing ACE and Berlin items; most ACE items were answered correctly by more than 90% of students, indicating a ceiling effect.

> "7 (47%) of ACE test items exhibited the difficulty index (DI) of ≥0.9 (i.e., more than 90% of students correctly responded to an item) compared to just 1 (6%) of the items on the Berlin test."
