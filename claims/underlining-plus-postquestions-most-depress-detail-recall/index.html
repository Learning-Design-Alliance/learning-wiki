Evidence¶

Rickards 1979¶

Rickards, J.P., Denner, P.R. (1979). Depressive effects of underlining and adjunct questions on children's recall of text. Instructional Science, 8, 81–90. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00054983

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experimental finding from the N=69 study of ten-year-olds in which half received conceptual postquestions after every paragraph and children either generated underlining, received pre-underlined topic sentences, or received no underlining. Recall of details was "most depressed" under the combined condition.

"Recall of passage details was most depressed when children were provided with both underlining and conceptual postquestions"
