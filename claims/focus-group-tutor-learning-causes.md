## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Focus group analysis identified four categories of tutor learning: leadership of the learning process, peer learning, previous preparation of activities, and motivation. [→ Duran 2017](#duran-2017)

## Evidence

### Duran 2017

Duran, D; Corcelles, M. & Flores, M. (2017). Enhancing Expectations of Cooperative Learning Use through Initial Teacher Training. International Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(3), 278-300. https://doi.org/10.17583/ijep.2017.2504

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Qualitative grounded-theory analysis of 4 focus groups (2 with tutors, 2 with tutees) from the experienced group. The article reports tutor learning is "related to their leadership in the learning process, peer learning, previous preparation of activities, and also greater motivation".

> "Regarding the first one, the aspects that students perceived as responsible for the tutors' learning are related to their leadership in the learning process, peer learning, previous preparation of activities, and also greater motivation, probably due to their taking on the role of tutors."
