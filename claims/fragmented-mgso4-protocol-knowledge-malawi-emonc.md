Kamanga Martha, Kapalamula Felister, Kazembe Abigail, Kabondo Charity, Chakhame Bertha, Lungu Gaily, Kumbani Lilly, Chodzaza Elizabeth. (2026). Competency gaps in emergency obstetric and neonatal care: Assessing knowledge, skills, and confidence in the use of magnesium sulfate among providers in Malawi. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of eight HCD workshops with embedded FGDs involving 48 providers across three Malawi districts found "many struggled to recall maintenance dosing intervals, monitoring requirements, and toxicity signs" despite knowing the loading dose.

> "While most providers recognised the indication and loading dose, many struggled to recall maintenance dosing intervals, monitoring requirements, and toxicity signs."
