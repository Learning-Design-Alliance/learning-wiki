Evidence¶

Cilia Ricarda Rücker 2025¶

Cilia Ricarda Rücker, Sebastian Becker-Genschow. (2025). Enhancing enthusiasm for STEM education with AI: Domain-specific chatbot as personalized learning assistant. Computers and Education Open. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2025.100315

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Group comparison in the cluster-randomized controlled study (N = 195 ninth-graders; EG n = 102, CG n = 93) measured post-intervention with seven situational-interest items. The chatbot group scored M = 2.63 (SD = 0.51) versus CG M = 2.43 (SD = 0.62), a "medium positive effect (d = 0.63)".

"The Mann-Whitney U test revealed a highly significant difference between the EG and the CG for situational interest (p = 0.00005). The effect size analysis using Cohen’s d revealed a medium positive effect (d = 0.63), suggesting a substantial enhancement of situational interest in the EG."
