# Children's self-perceptions jointly and separately partially mediate the SES–reading achievement association, explaining additional ΔR² = 0.105

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The joint standardized indirect effect of SES on reading achievement via the five self-perceptions is significant (βa×b, SMD = 0.064, p < 0.001), with the full model explaining R² = 0.220 versus R² = 0.115 for SES alone (ΔR² = 0.105). [→ Hofer 2024](#hofer-2024)
`q3 i?` Self-perceptions partially mediate the association: the direct c-path remains significant (βc, SMD = 0.257, p < 0.001). [→ Hofer 2024 (2)](#hofer-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Hofer 2024

Hofer, S. I., Heine, J.-H., Besharati, S., Yip, J. C., Reinhold, F., & Brummelman, E. (2024). Self-perceptions as mechanisms of achievement inequality: evidence across 70 countries. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00211-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Meta-analytic multi-mediator analysis over 70 countries (fixed effect model) using PISA 2018 data. The joint indirect effect was "βa×b, SMD = 0.064, p < 0.001" and the full model explained "R2 = 0.220", compared to R² = 0.115 from SES alone, giving ΔR² = 0.105.

> "the standardized indirect effect ( a × b-path) jointly considering the ﬁve self-perception variables was signi ﬁcant ( βa×b, SMD = 0.064, p < 0.001). For the full multi-mediator model, the explained variance was R2 = 0.220"

### Hofer 2024 (2)

Hofer, S. I., Heine, J.-H., Besharati, S., Yip, J. C., Reinhold, F., & Brummelman, E. (2024). Self-perceptions as mechanisms of achievement inequality: evidence across 70 countries. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00211-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same multi-mediator meta-analysis reports the direct c-path as "βc, SMD = 0.257" (p < 0.001), shown across countries in Fig. 2, establishing partial rather than full mediation.

> "the multi-mediator analysis showed a signiﬁcant direct effect ( c-path) of SES on reading achievement across countries ( βc, SMD = 0.257, p < 0.001, meta-analytically summarized over 70 countries, ﬁxed effect model; Fig. 2)."
