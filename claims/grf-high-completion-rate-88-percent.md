## Evidence

### Daniel L. Reinholz 2017

Daniel L. Reinholz, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer. (2017). Personalized instructor responses to guided student reflections: Analysis of two instructors' perspectives and practices. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/1.5002683](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5002683)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Submission counts across 22 enrolled students (10 in Emily's section, 12 in Taylor's), each required to complete 7 reflections. Section completion rates were 90% and 86%; only 1 submitted reflection received no instructor response. Students received small amounts of course credit for completing the activity.

> "Of 154 possible GRF-based reﬂections, 135 were submitted. This corresponds to a completion rate of 88%, which is consistent with the GRF completion rate observed in an- other study."
