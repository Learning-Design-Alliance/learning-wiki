# Practice condition (format/depth) showed no significant differences on posttest performance for either practiced or unpracticed concepts

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Between-subjects ANOVAs on posttest scores (practiced and unpracticed concepts, collapsed across posttest format/depth conditions) found no significant differences between the four practice conditions. [→ Maass 2016](#maass-2016)

## Evidence

### Maass 2016

Maass, J.K. and Pavlik Jr., P.I. (2016). Modeling the Influence of Format and Depth during Effortful Retrieval Practice. Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Educational Data Mining. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED592715

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Posttest analysis in the 2x2 experiment: the eight practiced concepts were each randomly assigned to one of four posttest format/depth conditions, plus eight unpracticed concepts. The ANOVAs "showed no significant differences between conditions for performance on either posttest"; equivalence was not tested. Factual MC appeared most affected by transfer items, though not significantly.
