Evidence¶

Sydney Enock Msonde 2017¶

Sydney Enock Msonde, Jan Van Aalst. (2017). Designing for interaction, thinking and academic achievement in a Tanzanian undergraduate chemistry course. Education Tech Research Dev (2017) 65:1389–1413. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9531-4

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA of chemistry test scores for 102 students tested before and after each of three units. The article also reports a statistically significant interaction effect, F (6, 297) = 14.284, p \ 0.0005, partial η² = 0.224 (large), analyzed with tests for simple effects.

"Repeated-measures ANOVA of the test results indicated a significant main effect for treatment, F (3, 297) = 64.474, p \ 0.0005, partial g2 = 0.394 (large), and a significant main effect for groups, F (2, 99) = 15.130, p \ 0.0005, partial g2 = 0.234 (large)."

Sydney Enock Msonde 2017 (2)¶

Sydney Enock Msonde, Jan Van Aalst. (2017). Designing for interaction, thinking and academic achievement in a Tanzanian undergraduate chemistry course. Education Tech Research Dev (2017) 65:1389–1413. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9531-4

q3 · i3 · design · r3

Simple-effects one-way ANOVAs per iteration: no differences at iteration 1 (F (2, 99) = 0.173, p = 0.841), significant at iteration 2 (partial η² = 0.143, NIL vs HIL p \ 0.0005), and at iteration 3 (partial η² = 0.459) with Tukey HSD differences between NIL and MIL (p = 0.001), NIL and HIL (p \ 0.0005), and MIL and HIL (p \ 0.0005).

"A one-way ANOVA for the third iteration (time 4) indicated significant differences between the groups, F (2, 99) = 42.049, p \ 0.0005, partial g2 = 0.459 (large)."
