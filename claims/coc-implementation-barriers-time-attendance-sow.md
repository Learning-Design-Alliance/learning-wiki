## Evidence

### Rachel Levenstein 2011

Rachel Levenstein, Sue Sporte, Elaine Allensworth. (2011). Findings from an Investigation into the Culture of Calm Initiative. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/findings-investigation-culture-calm-initiative

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Web survey of 30 principals and 31 coordinators plus two vendor focus groups (14 representatives of 12 vendors) identified barriers; Table 3 shows Time reported by 21 schools and Student attendance by 20. One vendor's 15-week program was cut to 9 weeks by slow approval.

> "A consistent barrier to implementation across vendors was gaining approval and funding from CPS central office.  Statements of Work (SOW) were generally seen as onerous, stressful to complete, and difficult to submit."
