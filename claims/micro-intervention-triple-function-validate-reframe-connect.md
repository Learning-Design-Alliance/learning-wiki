Theoretical argument from the article's introduction defining the construct. The author offers this as a conceptual definition grounded in cognitive appraisal theory and situated learning, not an empirically tested result; no outcome data are reported.

> "A micro-intervention is defined as a brief, deliberate instructor response—often verbal, sometimes gestural—that performs three simultaneous functions: (1) it validates the student's emergent emotional state, (2) reframes the cognitive appraisal of the situation, and (3) connects the struggle to a larger narrative of learning or professional practice."
