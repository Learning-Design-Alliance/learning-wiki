## Evidence

### Willingham 2008

Daniel T. Willingham. (2008). Critical Thinking: Why Is It So Hard to Teach? Arts Education Policy Review, 109(4), 21-32. https://doi.org/10.3200/AEPR.109.4.21-32

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Chen and Klahr gave second-, third-, and fourth-graders explicit instruction on controlling variables, then practice with materials such as springs. Students understood the concept and applied it seven months later with new materials and a different experimenter.

> "The experimenters found that students not only understood the concept of controlling variables, they were able to apply it seven months later with different materials and a different experiment- er, although the older children showed more robust transfer than the younger."
