## Evidence

### Jonah E. Rockoff 2004

Jonah E. Rockoff. (2004). The Impact of Individual Teachers on Student Achievement: Evidence from Panel Data. The American Economic Review, 94(2), 247-252. https://www.jstor.org/stable/3592891

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Panel-data fixed-effects analysis of matched student-teacher records from two New Jersey districts, with adjusted standard deviations of teacher fixed effects estimated by maximum likelihood (Table 2, adjusted SDs 0.08 to 0.11). The article reports the "about 0.1 standard deviations" effect on the national scale; no standardized effect-size statistic is printed for this finding.
