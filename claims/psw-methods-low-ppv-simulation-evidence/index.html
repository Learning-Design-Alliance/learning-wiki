Evidence¶

Miciak Jeremy 2016¶

Miciak Jeremy, Williams Jacob L., Taylor W. Pat, Cirino Paul T., Fletcher Jack M., Vaughn Sharon. (2016). Do Processing Patterns of Strengths and Weaknesses Predict Differential Treatment Response? Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(6), 898-909. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000096

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The article narrates a prior simulation study by Stuebing et al. (2012) that generated case-level values from latent variables; the review attributes to it that "identification rates were very low" and "PPVs were consistently low". No effect size is printed.

"identification rates were very low, resulting in high overall agreement and high NPV. However, PPVs were consistently low, suggesting that even if a PSW profile was educationally meaningful, it could not be reliably identified at the level of observation."
