## Evidence

### McKeown 2018

McKeown, M. G., Crosson, A. C., Moore, D. W., & Beck, I. L. (2018). Word Knowledge and Comprehension Effects of an Academic Vocabulary Intervention for Middle School Students. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831217744181

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Year 1 quasi-experiment with 105 sixth graders (RAVE n = 62; control n = 43) analyzed with mixed ANCOVA controlling for GMRT pretest. The article reports a significant treatment-by-time interaction favoring RAVE, with posttest difference t(102) = 7.04, p < .0001; effect sizes were not calculable for the mixed procedure.

> "There was a significant interaction of EAV scores between treatment and time, F(1, 102) = 55.22, p \ .001. There was also a significant main effect of group, F(1, 102) = 13.58, p \ .001"
