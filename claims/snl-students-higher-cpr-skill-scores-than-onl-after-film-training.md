## Evidence

### Hollenberg Jacob 2019

Hollenberg Jacob, Claesson Andreas, Ringh Mattias, Nordberg Per, Hasselqvist-Ax Ingela, Nord Anette. (2019). Effects of native language on CPR skills and willingness to intervene in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest after film-based basic life support training: a subgroup analysis of a randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025531

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup analysis of a cluster randomised trial among 641 seventh-grade students (SNL n=499, ONL n=142) trained with a 31-min Swedish film and individual manikins, assessed with the modified Cardiff test. The Results section reports "34 (SD 4.4) points (61% of maximum score) versus 36 (SD 3.8) points (67% of maximum score), respectively (p<0.001)"; at 6 months 32 vs 34 points (p<0.001). No effect size printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
