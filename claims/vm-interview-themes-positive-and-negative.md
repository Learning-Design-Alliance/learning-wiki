## Evidence

### Çakıroğlu 2014

Çakıroğlu, Ü. (2014). Enriching project-based learning environments with virtual manipulatives: A comparative study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 55, 201-222. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2014.55.12

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Thematic analysis of interviews with eight EG students (two below average, four average, two above average, selected by web usage rates) produced themes of enhancing learning, attitude toward the course, and behaviors during the course. One student said "Sometimes the VMs were a teacher for me; I took quick feedbacks".

> "It was seen that participants stated feeling comfortable with independence from the teacher and they felt comfortable learning from mistakes."

### Çakıroğlu 2014 (2)

Çakıroğlu, Ü. (2014). Enriching project-based learning environments with virtual manipulatives: A comparative study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 55, 201-222. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2014.55.12

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The interview theme table records negative sub-themes with frequencies: get tired (f=2), get stressed (f=6), distracted attention (f=1), similar examples on projects (f=7), and anxiety about exams (f=5). The author notes these responses were parallel to results of other web-based PBL applications.

> "The students indicated that sometimes they got tired and got stressed about not being able to bring up the projects in the limited given time. Also, a few students explained that their attention grew distracted sometimes on the Internet."
