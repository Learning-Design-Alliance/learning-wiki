## Evidence

### Weichao Lin 2023

Weichao Lin, Liang Chen, Wei Xiong, Kang Ran, Anlan Fan. (2023). Measuring the Sense of Presence and Learning Efficacy in Immersive Virtual Assembly Training. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.10387](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.10387)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U testing of the adapted Witmer presence questionnaire (21 desktop, 20 VR students) after normality violations; VR means were higher on "SF" (6.050 vs 5.281) and "RF" (5.360 vs 4.448), with "no significant differences in CF, DF and EF"; no standardized effect size printed.

> "desktop and VR groups exhibited significant differences in the SF and RF, but no significant differences in CF, DF and EF."
