# GenAI-assisted item sets showed lower KR-20 internal consistency reliability, and Spearman-Brown projections implied far more items would be needed to reach KR-20=0.70

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` KR-20 reliability was lower for GenAI-assisted items (0.38, 95% CI 0.12–0.56) than educator-crafted items (0.60, 95% CI 0.44–0.73); an estimated 116 GenAI-assisted versus 48 educator-crafted items would be needed to reach KR-20 of 0.70. [→ Sze Kai Ping 2026](#sze-kai-ping-2026)
`q3 i?` Removing one GenAI-assisted ethics item increased the GenAI-assisted KR-20 from 0.38 to 0.45, an increase of approximately 18%; no single educator-crafted item had a comparable effect. [→ Sze Kai Ping 2026 (2)](#sze-kai-ping-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Sze Kai Ping 2026

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). Human-Edited Generative AI-Assisted Multiple-Choice Questions in Postgraduate Family Medicine: Blinded Cross-Sectional Comparative Psychometric Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Test-level reliability analysis of the two 30-item sets completed by 73 participants. Spearman-Brown projections assumed comparable item properties: a 200-item test would raise projected KR-20 to 0.80 (GenAI-assisted) and 0.91 (educator-crafted).

> "Both item sets demonstrated suboptimal internal consistency reliability, although reliability was lower for GenAI-assisted items (KR-20=0.38, 95% CI 0.12-0.56) than for educator-crafted items (KR-20=0.60, 95% CI 0.44-0.73)"

### Sze Kai Ping 2026 (2)

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). Human-Edited Generative AI-Assisted Multiple-Choice Questions in Postgraduate Family Medicine: Blinded Cross-Sectional Comparative Psychometric Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Alpha-if-item-deleted sensitivity analysis identified a specific problematic GenAI-assisted ethics item; the authors argue this supports intrinsic item flaws, not merely low reliability attenuating point-biserial correlations.

> "Alpha-if-item-deleted analysis showed that removing 1 GenAI-assisted ethics item increased the GenAI-assisted KR-20 from 0.38 to 0.45, an increase of approximately 18%"
