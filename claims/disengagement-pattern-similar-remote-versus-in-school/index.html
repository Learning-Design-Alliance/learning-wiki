Evidence¶

Steven Wise 2022¶

Steven Wise, Megan Kuhfeld, John Cronin. (2022). Assessment in the time of COVID-19: Understanding patterns of student disengagement during remote low-stakes testing. Educational Assessment, 27(2), 136-151. https://doi.org/10.1080/10627197.2022.2087621

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Comparing remote spring 2020 administration with in-school fall 2019 administration of the same adaptive interim assessment, the study found the grade-level disengagement pattern "not meaningfully different between the two testing contexts." No effect size is printed in the available text.

"This pattern was not meaningfully different between the two testing contexts, with the exception of results for American Indian/Alaska Native students, who showed higher disengagement under remote testing."
