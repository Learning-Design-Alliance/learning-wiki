Evidence¶

Nyalapa McDonald William; Gombachika Belinda Thandizo 2025¶

Nyalapa McDonald William; Gombachika Belinda Thandizo. (2025). Diabetes knowledge of nurses in sub-Saharan Africa: a scoping review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-106689

q2 · i? · review · r2

A cross-sectional study of professional nurses drawn from a university teaching hospital and two training centres in Ghana, synthesised descriptively by the review, found "Only 32.4% had good knowledge in type 2 diabetes and the majority (68.4%) had poor knowledge of diabetes complications."

Nyalapa McDonald William; Gombachika Belinda Thandizo 2025 (2)¶

Nyalapa McDonald William; Gombachika Belinda Thandizo. (2025). Diabetes knowledge of nurses in sub-Saharan Africa: a scoping review. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-106689

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that one included study on nurses' diabetes knowledge "found knowledge gaps, particularly in DKA", identifying several content areas as targets for educational programmes.

"another one found knowledge gaps, particularly in DKA, and identified basic knowledge of diabetes, pathology, laboratory investigation, type 2 diabetes treatment, insulin therapy, exercise, diet and complications as areas for educational programme."
