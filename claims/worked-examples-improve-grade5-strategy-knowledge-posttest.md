# Heuristic worked examples improve fifth graders' modeling-specific strategy knowledge at posttest, but the effect fades by follow-up

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Fifth graders in the worked example conditions showed better modeling-specific strategy knowledge than control students at posttest (part. η² = .06). [→ Hänze 2022](#hanze-2022)
`q3 i?` The fifth-grade strategy knowledge treatment effect was no longer significant at the eight-week follow-up (part. η² = .02). [→ Hänze 2022 (2)](#hanze-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Hänze 2022

Hänze, M., & Leiss, D. (2022). Using heuristic worked examples to promote solving of reality-based tasks in mathematics in lower secondary school. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09583-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized field experiment with 134 fifth graders assigned to control, worked example, or prompted worked example conditions; ANCOVA on posttest strategy knowledge with pretest covariates found the worked example conditions outperformed control, "part. η2 = .06".

> "Strategy knowledge was affected by the treatment at posttest measurement, F(2,128) = 4.18, p < .05, part. η2 = .06. Contrast Cworked-examples showed that students in the worked examples conditions had better strategy knowledge than students in the control condition (p < .01) at posttest."

### Hänze 2022 (2)

Hänze, M., & Leiss, D. (2022). Using heuristic worked examples to promote solving of reality-based tasks in mathematics in lower secondary school. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09583-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same fifth-grade ANCOVA, the strategy knowledge treatment effect was not significant at the approximately eight-week follow-up, with only "part. η2 = .02" reported; equivalence was not tested.

> "At follow-up measurement the treatment effect was no longer significant, F(1,121) = 1.40, p > .20, part. η2 = .02)."
