Analysis relating school-level 5Essentials Survey scores to schools' estimated effects on students' fear of bullying in the Chicago study. The survey results "explained 36% of the differences across schools in their effects on bullying," with Supportive Environment alone explaining 35% of the variation.

"Schools' 5Essentials Survey results explained 36% of the differences across schools in their effects on bullying. This was driven by Supportive Environment, which on its own explained 35% of the variation."
