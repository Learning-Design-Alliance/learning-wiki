## Evidence

### Chartash David 2026

Chartash David, Bievre Nicolas, Marušić Ana, Ota Yuma, Asada Yoshikazu, Kubo Saori, Kanno Takeshi, Yagi Machiko Saeki, Matsuyama Yasushi. (2026). Evaluation of a Problem-Based Learning Program’s Effect on Artificial Intelligence Ethics Among Japanese Medical Students: Mixed Methods Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/84535

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test comparing phase-wise MCQ score gains in the same 118-student single-group study; the total score comparison yielded V=3084.5, P=.54, and "no statistically significant difference" for any subscale. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The test found no statistically significant difference between the score gain during the lecture phase (midtest-Pretest) and that during the PBL phase (posttest-midtest) for either the total score (V=3084.5; P=.54) or any of the 8 subscales (Table 5)."
