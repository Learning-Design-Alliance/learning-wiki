## Evidence

### Park Kyung Hye 2018

Park Kyung Hye, Park Seung Guk. (2018). The effect of communication training using standardized patients on nonverbal behaviors in medical students. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2018.90

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post observational comparison of 51 first-year medical students video-recorded in interviews with standardized patients before and after the course; the NVC score was compared with a paired t-test with 2-sided p<0.05. The score "improved from 8.56 (0.20) to 10.03 (0.13) significantly." No effect size was printed.

> "The total NVC score of the 12 nonverbal behaviors improved from 8.56 (0.20) to 10.03 (0.13) significantly."
