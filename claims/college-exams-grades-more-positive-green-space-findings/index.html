Evidence¶

Browning M. H. E. M. 2019¶

Browning M. H. E. M., Rigolon A. (2019). School Green Space and Its Impact on Academic Performance: A Systematic Literature Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16030429

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review's count of findings by academic outcome across the 13 included studies. It reports "seven (88%) showed beneficial associations" for college exams, while writing scores had nine null, two negative, and one positive association. The review notes college exams and grades were studied infrequently.

"The most promising findings for the influence of green space on achievement were for college preparatory exams results; of eight total findings, seven (88%) showed beneficial associations between green space and achievement"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Green space measured within 2000 m of schools shows more positive associations, while 3000 m or greater buffers show mostly null or negative findings
  • Positive associations are limited to greenness, tree cover, and green land cover; agriculture, grass, and shrub cover show no positive findings
  • Most findings on school green space and academic performance are non-significant, with 28% positive and 8% negative across 122 findings in 13 articles
