## Evidence

### Bolado Getachew Nigussie 2025

Bolado Getachew Nigussie, Atinafu Ataro Bizuayehu, Ersino Kebamo Tamirat, Shibabaw Ayana Agumas, Mimani Minuta Worku. (2025). Nurse educators' challenges of problem-based learning implementation at Ethiopian public universities: A phenomenological qualitative study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding under the PBL-strategy theme. Educators each deliver cases differently, producing inconsistency in the learning experience; the article suggests uniform delivery guidelines for facilitation and debriefing would enhance outcomes.

> "Over sixty percent of the participants highlighted that one of the challenges they encountered was the lack of a standardized way of delivering cases in PBL implementation."

### Bolado Getachew Nigussie 2025 (2)

Bolado Getachew Nigussie, Atinafu Ataro Bizuayehu, Ersino Kebamo Tamirat, Shibabaw Ayana Agumas, Mimani Minuta Worku. (2025). Nurse educators' challenges of problem-based learning implementation at Ethiopian public universities: A phenomenological qualitative study. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325976

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding from a few participants under the PBL-strategy theme: educators develop PBL cases on their own because the nursing curriculum lists no cases for some topics; a diverse, readily available case bank is proposed as a remedy.

> "While PBL offers a student-centered approach that promotes critical thinking and problem-solving skills, we often struggle to find any listed case for a particular topic."
