## Evidence

### Liu Mingxin 2025

Liu Mingxin, Okuhara Tsuyoshi, Huang Wenbo, Ogihara Atsushi, Nagao Hikari Sophia, Okada Hiroko, Kiuchi Takahiro. (2025). Large Language Models in Dental Licensing Examinations: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2024.10.014

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 7 studies testing GPT-4 on dental licensing examinations using a common effects model. The review reports "The integrate accuracy rate for GPT-4 was 72% (95% CI: 70%-74%)".

> "In the results of GPT-4, since the I² was 24% (I² < 50%), a common effects model was used. The integrate accuracy rate for GPT-4 was 72% (95% CI: 70%-74%)"
