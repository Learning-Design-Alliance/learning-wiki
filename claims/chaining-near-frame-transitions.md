Physics Education Research Conference proceedings record. https://www.compadre.org/PER/ [doi:10.1063/1.2820949](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2820949)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Single-clip analysis (Figure 1) of a group during a tutorial on Work & Energy, coded from start to finish, showing chaining (arrows) near worksheet–discussion frame transitions. The article reports six of nine chaining instances at such transitions in this case.

> "The arrows indicate chaining, which typically occurs near transitions between worksheet and discussion frames.  For this particular case, six of the nine instances of chaining occurred on the brink of such a transition."
