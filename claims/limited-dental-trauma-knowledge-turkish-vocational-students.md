Altıntaş Elif Defne, Kurt Şebnem Sarıoğlu, Özatik Şebnem, Öztürk Ayşegül. (2026). Assessment of health technician students’ awareness of dental trauma in Turkey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08607-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 333 students from 16 programs across 7 Turkish university-affiliated Health Vocational Schools. Descriptive results show "24 students (7.2%)" received first aid training that included dental trauma management; 262 students (78.7%) rated their knowledge as insufficient and only 70 (21.0%) reported knowing correct avulsion procedures (Table 3).

> "24 students (7.2%) of students reported receiving first aid training that included dental trauma management, while 155 students (46.5%) indicated that their training did not cover dental trauma intervention."
