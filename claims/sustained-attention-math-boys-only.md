## Evidence

### Li Lingshu; Zhang Li 2022

Li Lingshu; Zhang Li. (2022). The Relationship between Physical Activity and Academic Achievement in Multimodal Environment Using Computational Analysis. Computational Intelligence and Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/9418004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Boys' stepwise regression in the cross-sectional sample of 176 students; Table 4 prints sustained attention beta 0.160 with significance 0.041, the smallest retained coefficient in the model.

> "In males, sustained attention had an effect on mathematics scores; selective attention 1, selective attention 2, alternating attention, and BMI were eliminated in the equation."

### Li Lingshu; Zhang Li 2022 (2)

Li Lingshu; Zhang Li. (2022). The Relationship between Physical Activity and Academic Achievement in Multimodal Environment Using Computational Analysis. Computational Intelligence and Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/9418004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Residual/scatter analysis section reporting the girls' regression outcome: sustained attention was not retained, while steps and MVPA behaved as in boys. No effect size is printed for the null sustained-attention result.

> "In females, sustained attention time did not influence the mathematics score. However, similar to males, the greater the average numbers of steps and longer the duration of MVPA, the better the total mathematics score."
