Hui Zhao & Huijuan Gu 2026¶

Hui Zhao & Huijuan Gu. (2026). Thoughtless use of generative artificial intelligence and college students’ self-directed learning: a multi-group SEM analysis of gender differences. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-54337-y

q2 · i? · associational · r1

PLS-MGA with the Welch–Satterthwaite method after MICOM established partial measurement invariance (Table 12: H11 difference 0.22, p = .028). The contrast is between male and female students as printed.

"the negative impact of thoughtless use on learning MOV was significantly more substantial among male students than among female students, suggesting that female students’ learning MOV is more susceptible to the influence of TUGA."

Hui Zhao & Huijuan Gu 2026 (2)¶

Hui Zhao & Huijuan Gu. (2026). Thoughtless use of generative artificial intelligence and college students’ self-directed learning: a multi-group SEM analysis of gender differences. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-54337-y

q2 · i? · associational · r1

PLS-MGA path difference tests (Table 12: H10 difference −0.21, p = .005; H12 difference −0.16, p = .003). Both gender differences were significant in the printed multi-group analysis.

"the effects of thoughtless use on SE and SDL were significantly weaker among male students than among female students, indicating that male students’ SE and SDL are more vulnerable to the detrimental effects of thoughtless technology use."
