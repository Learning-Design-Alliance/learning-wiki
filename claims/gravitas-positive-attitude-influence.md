## Evidence

### Matteo Tuveri 2022

Matteo Tuveri, Daniela Fadda, Viviana Fanti and Walter Bonivento. (2022). When gravity meets philosophy again: the “Gravitas project”. 41st International Conference on High Energy physics - ICHEP2022. [doi:10.22323/1.414.0392](https://doi.org/10.22323/1.414.0392)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-project questionnaire with 70 answers (m=42, f=28) collected April-June 2022 on a 6-point Likert scale, analyzed by calculating means and standard deviations; means exceeded 4 on all three fields. No effect size is printed, so no magnitude is reported.

> "Qualitative analysis based on questionnaire’s results shows an overall good influence on students’ attitude towards physics, philosophy, and science communication (means higher than 4), with higher means in physics with respect to the others."
