## Evidence

### Stanis/suppress law D. G/suppress lazek 2012

Stanis/suppress law D. G/suppress lazek. (2012). Estimate of resources required for a meaningful reform of education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1211.5514](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1211.5514)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

This is the author's numerical extrapolation in Section IV G, from a TC serving 50 schools and 15,000 students a year and a chance of producing "at least 100 great teachers in 10 years" per TC across about 40 TCs. It is an assumed-scenario projection, not a measured result.

> "Since there are about 50 thousand teachers in high schools in Poland, the 4000 would amount to about 8% of all teachers. Thus, one can estimate that after 30 years at least about a quarter of all high school teachers would be the great ones, fully conscious of the value of their services to society."
