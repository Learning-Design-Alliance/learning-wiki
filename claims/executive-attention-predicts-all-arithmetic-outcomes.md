## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the Encoding Complex full measurement model, executive attention significantly and saliently predicted every arithmetic outcome, including Arabic numeral and estimation formats. [→ Rhodes Katherine T. 2017](#rhodes-katherine-t-2017)

## Evidence

### Rhodes Katherine T. 2017

Rhodes Katherine T., Branum-Martin Lee, Washington Julie A., Fuchs Lynn S. (2017). Measuring arithmetic: A psychometric approach to understanding formatting effects and domain specificity. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000189

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Encoding Complex full measurement model (χ2(128)=465.40, RMSEA=.05, CFI=.96) fit with executive attention predicting all arithmetic outcomes and language predicting only two language-formatted outcomes with low loadings, in the N=1314 confirmatory factor analysis.

> "However, executive attention was a significant and salient predictor of all arithmetic outcomes, and language was a significant predictor of WJ Applied Problems and Single Digit Story Problems, though these loadings were quite low."
