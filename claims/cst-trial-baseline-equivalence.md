### McCarrick CA 2025

McCarrick CA, Moynihan A, McEntee PD, Boland PA, Donnelly S, Heneghan H, Cahill RA. (2025). Impact of simulation training on communication skills and informed consent practices in medical students- a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07671-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomised trial (61 per arm), blinded baseline OSCE scores on both rubrics did not differ significantly between groups (Table 1 baseline p values all above 0.05), so post-intervention differences were attributed to CST.

> "This shows no statistically significant differences in either scoring rubric at baseline, but a significant difference between the groups by both scoring methods at the second assessment."
