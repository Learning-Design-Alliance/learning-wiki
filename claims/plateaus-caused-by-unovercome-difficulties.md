## Evidence

### Book 1908

Book, William Frederick. (1908). The Role of the Teacher in the Most Expeditious and Economic Learning. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The+Role+of+the+Teacher+in+the+Most+Expeditious+and+Economic+Learning

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Careful analysis of the learning consciousness, verified by objective writing records and complete working-pulse records, showed plateaus were "caused, not by the order in which the typewriting habits developed" but by unovercome difficulties at critical stages. All plateaus came from the first or second failure alternative or both.

> "showed that the plateaus in these curves were caused, not by the order in which the typewriting habits developed, but by the learners' failure to overcome certain specific difficulties encountered at those stages of advancement where certain elemental habits or groups of such habits were being finally perfected"
