`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Open-ended survey responses from 17 students analysed via content analysis. "All students reported that the near-peer tutorial was superior to traditional learning resources such as lectures, textbooks, or videos", attributing this to the interactive and practice-based format. Descriptive finding; no comparison test.

> "All students reported that the near-peer tutorial was superior to traditional learning resources such as lectures, textbooks, or videos. They explained that the interactive and practice-based format provided a depth of understanding and engagement that static or passive learning methods could not replicate."
