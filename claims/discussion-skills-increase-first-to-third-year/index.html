Evidence¶

Janneke M. Frambach 2014¶

Janneke M. Frambach, Erik W. Driessen, Philip Beh and Cees P.M. van der Vleuten. (2014). Quiet or questioning? Students' discussion behaviors in student-centered education across cultures. Studies in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/03075079.2012.754865

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Cross-sectional qualitative comparison of first- and third-year students' interviews and observations in three medical schools. The discussion section reports skill development across years, sometimes supported by students' own coping strategies such as pre-session meetings.

"In each of the institutions, these skills were found to increase as students moved from first to third year. Generally, students gained confidence and felt more comfortable – sometimes helped by their own coping strategies – in participating in the discussions"

Janneke M. Frambach 2014 (2)¶

Janneke M. Frambach, Erik W. Driessen, Philip Beh and Cees P.M. van der Vleuten. (2014). Quiet or questioning? Students' discussion behaviors in student-centered education across cultures. Studies in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/03075079.2012.754865

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Same qualitative comparative case study; the discussion section reports cross-institution differences in the rate of development, attributed to stronger cultural-factor impact in non-Western schools and to differences in the scope of PBL implementation.

"The students at the three institutions differed considerably, however, in the rate of development of discussion behaviors and skills."
