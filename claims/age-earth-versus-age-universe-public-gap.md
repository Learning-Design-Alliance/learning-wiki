## Evidence

### C. Love 2013

C. Love, A. Murphy, S. Bonora. (2013). Evaluating Astronomy Literacy of the General Public. Preprint submitted to the Astronomical Journal. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1308.2195](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1308.2195)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of approximately 1,000 adult Franklin Institute visitors using six true-false questions. The reported percentages are descriptive with standard errors; no inferential test of the Earth-versus-Universe contrast is printed. The gap was "rarely even mentioned" for Earth but drew skeptical comments for the Universe.

> "although 93 ± 0.8% of the participants correctly answered that scientists can calculate the age of the Earth, only 58 ± 2% provided the correct response that scientists can calculate the age of the Universe"
