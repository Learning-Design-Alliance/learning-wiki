# Experiential learning activities improve children's knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors towards healthy eating

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across 25 included RCTs and CRCTs, experiential learning interventions improved nutrition-related knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors in children aged 0–12 years. [→ Varman Sumantla D. 2021](#varman-sumantla-d-2021)

## Evidence

Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42018103629) of 25 RCTs/CRCTs in children 0–12 years, with Cohen's d calculated per outcome.
