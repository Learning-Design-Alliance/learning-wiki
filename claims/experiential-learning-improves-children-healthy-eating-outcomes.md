Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42018103629) of 25 RCTs/CRCTs in children 0–12 years, with Cohen's d calculated per outcome. The review concludes that "Experiential learning activities are a useful strategy to improve children’s knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors towards healthy eating." No pooled effect size is reported.
