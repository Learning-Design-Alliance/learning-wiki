## Evidence

### Nordmo 2023

Nordmo, M., Kleppestø, T., Sunde, H. F., Flatø, M., Demange, P. & Torvik, F. A. (2023). The association between parental internalizing disorders and child school performance. npj Science of Learning 8:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00182-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion comparing magnitudes across their own models: the sibling lower bound z = 0.04 against the total association z = 0.28 and the gender difference z = 0.476. These are authors' comparative statements, not effect-size statistics.

> "this suggests a likely lower bound on a possible causal effect ( z = 0.04) which is about 1/7 of the total association (z = 0.28). This is small compared to other factors associated with school performance, for instance, the gender difference is 15 times larger ( z = 0.476)."
