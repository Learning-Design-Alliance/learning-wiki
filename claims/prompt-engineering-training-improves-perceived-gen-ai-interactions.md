### Lindsay Dawson 2026

Lindsay Dawson, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Exploring Students' Perceptions of Using Generative AI-Assisted Problem Posing. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12523

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Phenomenological analysis of written reflections from N=49 students in an online first-semester calculus-based physics summer course, comparing pre- and post-training problem-posing interactions with Gen AI. A majority "perceived a positive change from before to after watching the training video"; 13% reported no significant change.

> "Based on their perceptions of the interactions, a majority of participants(76%)perceived a positive change from before to after watching the training video. Of the remaining students,13%reported no significant change between the pre- and post-training interaction."
