### Herath H. M. M. T. B. 2018

Herath H. M. M. T. B., Keragala B. S. D. P., Udeshika W. A. E., Samarawickrama S. S. M., Pahalagamage S. P., Kulatunga Aruna, Rodrigo Chaturaka. (2018). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Practice component of the same cross-sectional questionnaire among 123 NHSL non-specialist doctors, with indicators recorded for the preceding 12 months. "only 10 (8.1%) had received formal training" and a majority had never performed the examination; mean confidence was 2.09 (SD ± 1.05).

> "Regarding practices related to melanoma diagnosis, only 10 (8.1%) had received formal training to perform a total body examination for skin cancer detection. A majority (110, 89.4%) had never done one in their career."
