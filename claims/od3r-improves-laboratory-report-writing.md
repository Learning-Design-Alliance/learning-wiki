## Evidence

### Yunita Arian Sani Anwar 2018

Yunita Arian Sani Anwar, Senam, & Endang W Laksono. (2018). Meaningful Biochemistry Learning Using the Orientation-Decision-Do-Discuss-Reflect (OD3R) Method. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 17-30. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1132a

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment in 4 Indonesian universities (N = 216) measured report writing with a modified Hoyo rubric covering abstracts, information sources, organization, relevance, contents, and presentation. The article reports "a significant increase after the method implementation" for all components; no effect size is printed.

> "The OD3R method implementation in biochemistry learning can improve students' ability in writing laboratory reports. All report components that are assessed experience a significant increase after the method implementation"
