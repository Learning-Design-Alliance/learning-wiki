## Evidence

### Allan L. Alinea 2020

Allan L. Alinea. (2020). Cognitive Reflection Test and the Polarizing Force-Identification Questions in the FCI. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/aba8e9](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/aba8e9)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of the same 163 students' CRT scores against their scores on the six PFI questions (FCI items 5, 11, 13, 18, 29, 30). The article reports "a correla­ tion coeﬃcient of r = 0.096" and notes the rising trend from CRT 0 to 2 was spoiled by a downshift at CRT score 3.

> "All in all, we get a correla­ tion coeﬃcient of r = 0.096 in the approximate range [-0.06, 0.25] at 95% conﬁdence interval."
