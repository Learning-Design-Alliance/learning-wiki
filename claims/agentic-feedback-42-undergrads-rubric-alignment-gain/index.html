Kostopoulos 2025¶

Kostopoulos, G., Gkamas, V., Rigou, M., & Kotsiantis, S. (2025). Agentic AI in Education: State of the Art and Future Directions. IEEE Access. https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3620473

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports a quasi-experimental design-based study (ref [30]) with 42 undergraduate students in higher education, which "reported a 15 % improvement in rubric alignment and faster correction cycles". The review attributes this to mid-level TRL (4-5) implementations; no effect size is printed.

"Recent quasi-experimental work [30] (design-based study with N = 42 undergraduate students) reported a 15 % improvement in rubric alignment and faster correction cycles, confirming the feasibility of proactive feedback loops in higher education."
