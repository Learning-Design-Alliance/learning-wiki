Authors' interpretation of pre/post questionnaire response patterns across the workshops, attributing the persistence to the complexity and unintuitive nature of the parasite's life cycle. No effect size is printed.

> "Acquisition of NCC was and remained poorly understood, with many respondents selecting coughing or sneezing (58%, QS2), or stool (48%, QS1) as transmission methods. Another sustained misconception was that N/CC develops after consuming undercooked infected pork (63% of respondents, QS2)."
