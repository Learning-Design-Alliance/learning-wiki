# Minimum reviewer pool size is computed from anticipated submissions, reviews per submission, and assignments per reviewer

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The minimum number of reviewers needed equals anticipated submissions times reviews per submission divided by assignments per reviewer; the worked example (200*3)/10 = 60 reviewers per month. [→ Michelle Riconscente 2016](#michelle-riconscente-2016)

## Evidence

### Michelle Riconscente 2016

Michelle Riconscente, Designs for Learning, Inc. (2016). Designing a Process for Micro-credentials at Scale. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/ddd022c9-a271-4548-9667-38dbfe9bf9c6

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

A definitional formula with a worked example from the Step 3B section. The article states the pool size is determined by anticipated submissions within a scoring period, associate reviewers per submission, and submissions assigned per reviewer.

> "if the issuer anticipates receiving 200 submissions per month, having three reviewers score each submission, and assigning 10 submissions per month to each reviewer, then the formula would be: (200*3)/10 = 60 reviewers."
