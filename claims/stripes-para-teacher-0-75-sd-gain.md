### Boone Peter 2015

Boone Peter, Camara Alpha, Eble Alex, Elbourne Diana, Fernandes Samory, Frost Chris, Jayanty Chitra, Lenin Maitri, Silva Ana Filipa. (2015). Remedial after-school support classes offered in rural Gambia (The SCORE trial): study protocol for a cluster randomized controlled trial. Trials. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-015-1081-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The protocol reports the authors' own prior cluster randomized controlled trial (STRIPES) in rural Andhra Pradesh, India, run with the Naandi Foundation. It reports that "students in the para-teacher intervention arm of the trial scored 0.75 standard deviations (SD) higher on mathematics and language tests than the control group"; the protocol itself prints no further test statistic for this result, so no standardised effect-size value beyond the reported 0.75 SD contrast is coded here.

> "We found that after 2 years, students in the para-teacher intervention arm of the trial scored 0.75 standard deviations (SD) higher on mathematics and language tests than the control group [7]."
