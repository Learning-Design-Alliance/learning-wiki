### Michael F. J. Fox and Taylor O. Pomfret 2025

Michael F. J. Fox and Taylor O. Pomfret. (2025). Self-Evaluated Expertise in experimental physics: a measure of students' physics self-recognition. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.07885](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.07885)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of a public dataset of more than 70,000 E-CLASS student responses (2016–2019, 494 courses, 120 institutions). The article reports a Spearman rank-order correlation of "0 .51 ± 0.01" between pre-test total YOU and SEE scores, and states the value is not near unity, suggesting the YOU and SEE scores are not identical. No standardized effect-size statistic with a printed symbol-value pair accompanies this finding, so impact is left unquantified.
