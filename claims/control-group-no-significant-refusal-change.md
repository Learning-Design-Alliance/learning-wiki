# Control-group exposure to dialogs without pragmatic instruction produces no significant change in refusal performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the control group, where dialogs served only to improve English proficiency, no significant change occurred in pragmatic production of refusals (Exact Sig. 2-tailed = 1.000). [→ Farrokhi and Atashian 2012](#farrokhi-and-atashian-2012)

## Evidence

### Farrokhi and Atashian 2012

Farahman Farrokhi, Soheil Atashian. (2012). The Role of Refusal Instruction in Pragmatic Development. World Journal of Education, Vol. 2, No. 4. http://dx.doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n4p85

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Control group (n = 20) in the quasi-experiment received the same Spectrum dialogs as a source of English comprehension and production without pragmatic awareness-raising. Table 4 reports Exact Sig. 2-tailed of 1.000 for the control pretest-posttest comparison; equivalence was not tested.

> "It is obvious from the cumulative percent in pretest and postte st that in control group, wher e the presentation of dialogs was merely to ameliorate English proficiency of the subjec ts, no significant change occurs in pragmatic production of refusals."
