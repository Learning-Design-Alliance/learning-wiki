## Evidence

### Triola Marc M 2011

Triola Marc M, Holloway William J. (2011). Enhanced virtual microscopy for collaborative education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational pacing analysis from the same four-week usage-log study: in-lab viewing averaged 21 slides per session at 9 min 8 s per slide; non-lab viewing averaged 10 slides per hour at 10 min 11 s per slide, with "P = 0.07" for the duration difference.

> "During the two-hour laboratory sessions, students on average viewed 21 digital slides and spent 9 minutes and 8 seconds per slide. During non-lab time, when students had greater control over their pacing, students looked at an average of 10 slides per hour and spent 10 minutes and 11 seconds per slide (P = 0.07 for the difference between in-lab and out-of-lab view duration)."
