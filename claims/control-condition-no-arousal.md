## Evidence

### Henk G. Schmidt & Jerome I. Rotgans 2021

Henk G. Schmidt & Jerome I. Rotgans. (2021). Epistemic Curiosity and Situational Interest: Distant Cousins or Identical Twins?. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09539-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVAs in the control condition (n = 74) of the 148-student experiment. No significant pairwise differences emerged ("none of these differences emerged"), consistent with the knowledge-gap hypothesis that prior knowledge prevents arousal.

> "In the control condition, none of these differences emerged. These data suggest that only among students in the treatment condition, having no prior knowledge of the explanation of the Mpemba problem, epistemic curiosity and situational interest increased"
