Evidence¶

Xi Xiang & Michael E. Meadows 2025¶

Xi Xiang & Michael E. Meadows. (2025). Being proactive about anthropogenic environmental changes: augmenting students' decision making with artificial intelligence (AI) technology. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10523-9

q1 · i? · design · r1

Authors' interpretation from the design-based experience, not a tested result. They state AI tools "are also limited in their capacity to envision values", while human judgement incorporates emotions and paradoxes into cohesive reasoning.

"Although AI tools can be trained to better represent human behavioral aspects of decision making, they struggle to transfer and prioritize the relatively accurate prediction results into practical advice towards sustainability."
