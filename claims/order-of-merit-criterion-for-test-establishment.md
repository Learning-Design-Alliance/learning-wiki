A theoretical argument, not a tested result: the author contends that order-of-merit judgment is "the Criterion by which such tests are established" and that many trait valuations in shop, factory and school are obtainable only by ranking. No data are presented.

> "since in determining most objective measures, such as vocational tests, and scales,—Judgment by the order of Merit is the Criterion by which such tests are established, and since the valuation of many traits of individuals in shop, factory and school are still obtainable only by the ranking method, every effort should be made to raise this to as objective a level as possible."
