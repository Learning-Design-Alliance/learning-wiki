### Neil M. Zimmerman and David B. Newell 2016

Neil M. Zimmerman and David B. Newell. (2016). ”What is The SI?” A Proposal for an Educational Adjunct to the SI Redeﬁnition. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1611.05313](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1611.05313)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparing the pared-down Proposal C with the original longer version across the two questionnaires, the authors conclude that "substantially shortening and simplifying Proposal C signiﬁcantly improved students’ understanding of the wording". No effect size or test statistic is printed for this claim.

> "Thus, it is clear that substantially shortening and simplifying Proposal C signiﬁcantly improved students’ understanding of the wording of the Proposal, while not reducing their understanding of the motivation."
