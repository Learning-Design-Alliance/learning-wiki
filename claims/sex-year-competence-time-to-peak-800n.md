## Evidence

### Nim C 2025

Nim C, Smith N, Starmer D, Wang S, Choi G, Alayed A, AlShareef J, Gnjatic A, Sloan K, Wong K, Funabashi M. (2025). Chiropractic students’ characteristics influencing confidence and competence in modulating spinal manipulation force–time characteristics of specific target forces: a secondary analysis of a cross-sectional study. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-025-00577-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression of competence (binary, FSTT®-scored) in 149 chiropractic students; the article reports "Third-year students were substantially more likely to be faster than 1st-year students". No effect sizes printed.

> "Third-year students were substantially more likely to be faster than 1st-year students. The same pattern emerged with 2nd-year students compared to 1st-year students but with more uncertainty and less strong associations."

### Nim C 2025 (2)

Nim C, Smith N, Starmer D, Wang S, Choi G, Alayed A, AlShareef J, Gnjatic A, Sloan K, Wong K, Funabashi M. (2025). Chiropractic students’ characteristics influencing confidence and competence in modulating spinal manipulation force–time characteristics of specific target forces: a secondary analysis of a cross-sectional study. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-025-00577-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same logistic regression analysis of competence outcomes; the article reports "the likelihood of achieving 800N peak forces increased for males" and no other strong sex associations. No effect sizes printed.

> "the time to peak force showed consistently strong associations, and the likelihood of achieving 800N peak forces increased for males. There were no other strong associations between sex and other peak thrust forces or preload forces."
