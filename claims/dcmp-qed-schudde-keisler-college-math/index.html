q3 i3 In the Schudde & Keisler (2019) quasi-experimental design (9,752 students), DCMP students were far more likely than students in a two-to-three-semester developmental sequence to ever pass a college-level math class (70.20% vs. 34.20%, effect size 0.92) and earned more cumulative college-level credits after three years (32.75 vs. 26.88, effect size 0.25). → schudde-keisler-qed

Evidence¶

schudde-keisler-qed¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021, June). Dana Center Mathematics Pathways. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/718.

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental design using propensity score matching across 20 Texas community colleges implementing DCMP in fall 2014; main analytic sample compared 484 DCMP students with 9,268 comparison students; effect size 0.92, p < .01.
