## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Ahlborg Liv 2015

Ahlborg Liv, Weurlander Maria, Hedman Leif, Nisell Henry, Lindqvist Pelle G., Felländer-Tsai Li, Enochsson Lars. (2015). Individualized feedback during simulated laparoscopic training: a mixed methods study. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.55a2.218b

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quantitative analysis of a 3-item self-efficacy questionnaire rated on 7-grade Likert-type scales, administered before and after simulator training to both randomized groups. "Overall self-efficacy scores improved with training in both groups"; no group difference was observed.

> "No differences in flow experience or self-efficacy scores were observed between the feedback and control groups. Overall self-efficacy scores improved with training in both groups (Figure 2)."
