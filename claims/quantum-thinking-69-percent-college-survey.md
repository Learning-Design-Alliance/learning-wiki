### Bayram Akarsu 2010

Questionnaire study of 86 undergraduate students in modern physics, quantum physics, or quantum mechanics courses, analyzed with cluster analysis and multidimensional scaling adapted from Ireson (1999). The author reports these percentages and means descriptively, concluding "more than half of the students 69% participated grasped the ideal thinking in quantum physics class"; no inferential test or effect size is printed.

> "Table 4 illustrates that 43% of the students demonstrate mechanical thinking with average score of 2.18 out of 5, 70% (average score of 3.48) has quantum thinking, and 68% (average score of 3.38) possessed dual thinking."
