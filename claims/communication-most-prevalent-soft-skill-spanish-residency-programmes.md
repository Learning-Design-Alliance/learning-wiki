`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A descriptive cross-sectional observational study reviewed all published training programmes for Health Sciences specialties at the National Council of Specialties in Health Sciences in July 2024. The study found "communication (35 programmes, 62.5%)" was the most prevalent skill, followed by teamwork, decision-making and leadership.

> "The most prevalent skills are communication (35 programmes, 62.5%) and teamwork (23 programmes, 41.1%), followed by decision-making (15 programmes, 26.8%) and leadership (11 programmes, 19.6%)."
