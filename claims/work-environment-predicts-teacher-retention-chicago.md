Elaine Allensworth. (2012). Want to Improve Teaching? Create Collaborative, Supportive Schools. American Educator, Fall 2012. http://www.annenberginstitute.org/VUE

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The Chicago teacher mobility study (The Schools Teachers Leave) found work environment quality strongly predicted retention; teachers stayed where they saw themselves "as part of a team that is working together toward making their school better, supported by school leadership."

> "we found that the quality of the work environment was strongly predictive of whether teachers remained in their schools. One key element in teacher retention is teachers' perceptions of their colleagues as collaborators."
