### Miller 1952

Miller, J., Levine, S., and Kanner, J. (1952). A Study of the Effects of Different Types of Review and of Structuring Sub-Titles on the Amount Learned from a Training Film. Memo Report No. 17, Human Resources Research Laboratories, Bolling Air Force Base. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED063741

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A 36-class, 1263-subject experiment compared four review treatments in training films on elementary electricity; the results section reports that massed review "produced significantly greater learning" than no review or the two spaced review conditions.

> "(b) The conditions of massed review (at end of film) produced significantly greater learning than the condition of ttno review" or the two conditions of ttspaced review" used in the experiment."
