## Evidence

### Caballero 2011

Caballero, M. D., Greco, E. F., Murray, E. R., Schatz, M. F., Bujak, K. R., Marr, M. J., Catrambone, R., & Kohlmyer, M. A. (2011). Comparing large lecture mechanics curricula using the Force Concept Inventory: A five thousand student study. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/1.3703517](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.3703517)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In-class FCI testing of over 5000 students across 22 traditional and 6 M&I sections at Georgia Tech. Pre-test scores did not differ significantly (48.9% TRAD vs. 47.4% M&I), but post-test scores showed a significant advantage for the traditional curriculum.

> "By contrast, on the FCI post-test, traditional students signiﬁcantly outperformed M&I students (mean FCI score, 71.3% for TRAD vs. 59.3% for M&I)."
