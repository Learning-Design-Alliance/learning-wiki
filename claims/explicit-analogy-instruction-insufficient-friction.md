## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Challenges in Analogical Reasoning. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06423](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06423)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Recitation-class study with 37 students in an algebra-based introductory physics course; the tension problem was provided as a fully worked solution, and students were explicitly asked to make and exploit the analogy. Only 35% solved the friction quiz problem, so "two thirds of the students were unable to make a good analogy".

> "We find that, even with an explicit instruction to make an analogy with the problem involving tension, only 35% of the students were able to solve the quiz problem involving friction."
