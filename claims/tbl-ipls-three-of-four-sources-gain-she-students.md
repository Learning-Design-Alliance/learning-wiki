## Evidence

### Brokk Toggerson 2020

Brokk Toggerson, Samyukta Krishnamurthy, Emily E. Hansen, and Chasya Church. (2020). Positive Impacts on Student Self-Efficacy from an Introductory Physics for Life Science Course Using the Team-Based Learning Pedagogy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t-tests on SOSESC-P source-level subscales for she-identifying students (n = 91) in the TBL IPLS course. The article reports "a positive shift across three of the four sources of self-eﬃcacy" with the printed p-value thresholds, and notes "The only source of self-eﬃcacy which did not show a signiﬁcant increase for she-identifying students was in vicarious learning."

> "The bottom portion shows that the signiﬁcant shift for she-identifying individuals overall is a manifes- tation of a positive shift across three of the four sources of self-eﬃcacy: mastery experiences ( p < 0.05), verbal per- suasion (p < 0.005), and physiological state ( p < 0.005)."
