Evidence¶

Dhlamini 2013¶

Dhlamini, J., & Mogari, D. (2013). The effect of a group approach on the performance of high school mathematics learners. Pythagoras, 34(2), Art. #198. https://doi.org/10.4102/pythagoras.v34i2.198

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Self-report cognitive load questionnaire (Paas, Van Merriënboer & Adams instrument, nine-point scale) administered to N = 378 experimental learners after both FMAT pre-test and post-test. Pre-test mean was 6.6 (SD = 1.6) and post-test mean 3.3 (SD = 1.6); the article reports these descriptive means with no significance test.

"after the implementation of a group approach learners' cognitive load was reduced as learners generally rated problem-solving tasks in the post-test as being 'easy' (M = 3.3; SD = 1.6)."
