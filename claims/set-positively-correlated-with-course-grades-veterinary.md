# Mean course evaluations are significantly positively correlated with median course grades and with the proportion of A-grades in veterinary basic-science courses

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Mean course evaluations correlate positively with median course grade (rho = 0.35) and with the proportion of students earning A-grades (rho = 0.38). [→ Gilbert R. O. 2025](#gilbert-r-o-2025)

## Evidence

### Gilbert R. O. 2025

Gilbert R. O., Gilbert D. R. (2025). Student evaluations of teaching do not reflect student learning: an observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06896-3

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Observational correlation analysis of 160 veterinary courses (fall 2018 to summer 2022, 12 cohorts) using Spearman coefficients; the study prints the correlation effect sizes rho = 0.35 for median grades and rho = 0.38 for A-grades, both P < 0.0001, reporting evaluations "significantly positively related to median grades in the courses".

> "Mean course evaluations were significantly positively related to median grades in the courses (rho = 0.35, P < 0.0001). In addition, they were significantly positively correlated with the proportion of students earning final grades in the A-range (rho = 0.38, P < 0.0001)."
