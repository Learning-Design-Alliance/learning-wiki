### Wang Gang 2026

Wang Gang, Liu Yingying, Wang Shengyun, Gao Silong, Lin Hui, Wu Qian, Huang Debin, Pan Yueshuai, Zhao Yuxiao, Wei Huimin, Wei Lili. (2026). Factors Influencing ICU Nurses’ Proficiency in Point‐of‐Care Gastrointestinal Ultrasound: A National Cross‐Sectional Study. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/3178958

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

National cross-sectional online survey of 2009 ICU nurses from 21 provinces in China; proficiency was self-assessed on three levels. The survey found "297/2009 (14.8%)" rated proficiency good and "1205/2009 (59.9%)" rated it poor; no effect size is printed for this descriptive result.

> "Only 297/2009 (14.8%) of ICU nurses rated their GI‐POCUS proficiency as “good,” whereas 1205/2009 (59.9%) rated it as “poor.”"
