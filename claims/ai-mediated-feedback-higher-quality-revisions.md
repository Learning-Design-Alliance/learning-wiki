# Students who received AI-mediated feedback authored in FeedbackWriter produced higher-quality revised drafts than students who received human-only feedback (Cohen's d = 0.50)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In a randomized trial in ECON101, students in the AI-mediated feedback condition produced significantly higher-quality revised drafts than students in the human-only feedback condition, with an effect size of Cohen's d = 0.5. [→ Xinyi Lu 2026](#xinyi-lu-2026)

## Evidence

### Xinyi Lu 2026

Xinyi Lu, Kexin Phyllis Ju, Mitchell Dudley, Larissa Sano, and Xu Wang. 2026. AI-Mediated Feedback Improves Student Revisions: A Randomized Trial with FeedbackWriter in a Large Undergraduate Course. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791121

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects linear regression on final draft quality from an IRB-approved randomized controlled trial with 354 students and 11 TAs, with random intercepts for students and TAs. The condition effect was "𝑏= 0.05, 𝑡= 5.02, 𝑝< 0.001∗∗∗"; the article reports "The effect size is Cohen's d = 0.5, roughly equilavent to moving a student from the 50th to the 70th percentile."

> "Further, there is a significant effect of the Condition, which suggests that when a student is in the AI condition (FeedbackWriter), they are more likely to have higher quality revised drafts ( 𝑏= 0.05, 𝑡= 5.02, 𝑝< 0.001∗∗∗)."
