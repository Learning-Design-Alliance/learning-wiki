Yu-Ju Lan 2015¶

Yu-Ju Lan, Nian-Shing Chen, Ping Li, Scott Grant. (2015). Embodied cognition and language learning in virtual environments. Education Tech Research Dev 63. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9401-x

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Lin, Chao, and Huang's study with college students learning Japanese, in which an intelligent affective tutoring system recognized facial expressions to provide adequate feedback; the editorial reports the system "reducing learning anxiety, and improving learning effectiveness."

"The results show that this proposed system is beneﬁcial for the learning of the Japanese language, reducing learning anxiety, and improving learning effectiveness."
