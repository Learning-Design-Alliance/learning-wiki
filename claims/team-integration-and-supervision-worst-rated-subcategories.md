## Evidence

### Seitz T. 2017

Seitz T., Turk B. R., Löffler-Stastka H. (2017). Can we still stop the migration of physicians from Austria? An evaluation of clinical internships by students of the Medical University of Vienna. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-016-1095-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive subcategory ratings from the 304-student survey: "the worst two rated subcategories were 'Integration into the clinical team' and 'Supervision by medical professionals'" at average ratings 2.93 and 2.98 overall (Table 1); the general average grade for clinical training was 2.8.

> "The highest-rated subcategory for all participants was “Improvement of clinical knowledge” (rated between Grade B+ and C+; 1.63–2.9), the worst two rated subcategories were “Integration into the clinical team” and “Supervision by medical professionals” (Grade B− to D+; 2.13–3.8)."
