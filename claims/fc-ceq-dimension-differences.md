# FC residents rated good teaching and generic skills higher, and appropriate assessment lower, than LB residents, with no differences on clear goals or workload

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hou Weishu 2025

Hou Weishu, Xue Yangyang, Pan Hongli, Zhao Xuemei, Shu Hongmin, Li Xiaohu, Yu Yongqiang. (2025). Comparison of flipped classroom and lecture-based models in a radiology residency training program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07702-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

CEQ dimension analysis from the same 127-resident survey; the article states the three dimension scores were "significantly higher than the scores in the LB group" with the printed means and P values, though the printed appropriate-assessment means show FC lower (2.74 vs. 3.98).

> "the average scores of “good teaching”, “generic skills”, and “appropriate assessment” in the FC group were significantly higher than the scores in the LB group (4.36 ± 0.26 vs. 3.80 ± 0.44, P < 0.001; 4.45 ± 0.47 vs. 3.68 ± 0.65, P = 0.001; 2.74 ± 0.44 vs. 3.98 ± 0.28, P < 0.001, respectively)"

### Hou Weishu 2025 (2)

Hou Weishu, Xue Yangyang, Pan Hongli, Zhao Xuemei, Shu Hongmin, Li Xiaohu, Yu Yongqiang. (2025). Comparison of flipped classroom and lecture-based models in a radiology residency training program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07702-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same CEQ analysis; the two remaining dimensions "did not differ between the FC and LB groups" with the printed means, P = 0.405 and P = 0.205. The authors attribute this to teachers communicating objectives clearly regardless of model.

> "“clear goals and standard” and “appropriate workload” scores did not differ between the FC and LB groups (3.69 ± 0.74 vs. 3.44 ± 0.62, P = 0.405; 2.34 ± 0.31 vs. 2.23 ± 0.34, P = 0.205, respectively)"
