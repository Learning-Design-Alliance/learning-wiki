Evidence¶

Xanthou 2011¶

Maria Xanthou. (2011). The impact of CLIL on L2 vocabulary development and content knowledge. English Teaching: Practice and Critique, 10(4). http://education.waikato.ac.nz/research/files/etpc/files/2011v10n4art7.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-sample t-tests in Experiment 1 (31 children, suburban school) comparing pre/post vocabulary performance. The CLIL group's mean difference was 9.06; the control group's t= 1.89, p= .079 was non-significant. No effect size is printed.

"The CLIL group also showed a significant difference p= .000 on vocabulary knowledge indicating a positive effect of the treatment. The mean difference (9.06) indicated a positive impact of CLIL on L2 vocabulary knowledge."
