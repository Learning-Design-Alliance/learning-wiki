Exploratory subgroup analysis of cultural context (collectivist k = 49; individualist k = 7) within the meta-analysis. The article reports "QBET = 7.594" and subgroup estimates g = 0.815 and g = 0.367; no standardized effect size for the between-subgroup contrast is printed.

"Observed between-subgroup diﬀerences were found across cultural contexts (QBET = 7.594, df = 1, p < 0.05). This eﬀect was larger in countries with predominantly collectivist cultures (g = 0.815,95% CI=[0.585,1.046])thanthosewithindividualisticones (g =0.367,95% CI =[0.147,0.587])."
