## Subclaims
`q3 i?` PICI rose significantly from pre-course to post-course by 5.6 pp (P < 0.001), 5.4 pp (P = 0.003) and 3.9 pp (P = 0.014) in the < 70, 70-140 and > 140 pre-course procedure groups respectively, in one pre-post study with no comparison group. [→ Siau K 2020](#siau-k-2020)

## Evidence

### Siau K 2020

Siau K, Hodson J, Anderson JT, Valori R, Smith G, Hagan P, Iacucci M, Dunckley P. (2020). Impact of a national basic skills in colonoscopy course on trainee performance: An interrupted time series analysis. World Journal of Gastroenterology. https://doi.org/10.3748/wjg.v26.i23.3283

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interrupted time series analysis of 369 UK trainees (35716 procedures logged on the JETS e-portfolio), stratified by pre-course procedure count. The article reports "PICI increasing by 5.6 pp, 5.4 pp and 3.9 pp" with P < 0.001, P = 0.003 and P = 0.014; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "Significant step-change improvements were then observed in all three groups after the course, with PICI increasing by 5.6 pp, 5.4 pp and 3.9 pp in the < 70, 70-140 and > 140 procedure groups, respectively."
