Evidence¶

Muhammad Irfan Arif and Munawar S. Mirza 2017¶

Muhammad Irfan Arif and Munawar S. Mirza. (2017). Effectiveness of an Intervention Program in Fostering Academic Resilience of Students at Risk of Failure at Secondary School Level. Bulletin of Education and Research, 39(1), 251-264. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1210192

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretation from the discussion of the 64-student intervention experiment: the resilience teacher developed a positive relationship with students and fostered resilience through a positive motivational and inspiring attitude. This is the authors' interpretive claim, not a separately tested comparison.

"The role of researcher as a resilience teacher, as a guide, as a mentor and as a facilitator was established to be beneficial for the successful build -up of resiliency skills among at -risk students."
