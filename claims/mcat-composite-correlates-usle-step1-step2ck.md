## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Pearson correlations show significant moderately positive relationships between MCAT composite scores and both USMLE Step 1 (r=0.39) and Step 2 CK (r=0.31) scores. [→ Gauer Jacqueline L. 2016](#gauer-jacqueline-l-2016)

## Evidence

### Gauer Jacqueline L. 2016

Gauer Jacqueline L., Wolff Josephine M., Jackson J. Brooks. (2016). Do MCAT scores predict USMLE scores? An analysis on 5 years of medical student data. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.31795

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of MCAT composite scores and USMLE scores from five graduating classes (2011–2015) at one medical school (N=1,065), using Pearson product-moment coefficients; "positive correlations for both Step 1 (r=0.39, p<0.001) and Step 2 CK (r=0.31, p<0.001)" were found.

> "We found positive correlations for both Step 1 (r=0.39, p<0.001) and Step 2 CK (r=0.31, p<0.001). These results indicate significant moderately positive relationships between MCAT composite scores and USMLE exam scores."
