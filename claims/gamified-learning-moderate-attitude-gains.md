## Evidence

### Chen W.-L. 2026

Chen W.-L., Winitchayothin S., Cheng C.-H., Tonapa S.I., Chou F.-H., & Zimmerman P.-A. (2026). A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effect of gamified clinical skill learning on learning outcomes in undergraduate nursing education. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000772

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of four randomized controlled trials pooling 434 students assessed attitude change. The review reports a moderate pooled effect, SMD = 0.54, quoting "a moderate pooled effect size of 0.54 (95% CI [0.07, 1.00], p=.02)"; the evidence base is small.

> "A meta-analysis of four studies comprising 434 students was used to assess attitude changes following gamified clinical skill learning interventions. The results, showing a moderate pooled effect size of 0.54 (95% CI [0.07, 1.00], p=.02) with substantial heterogeneity"
