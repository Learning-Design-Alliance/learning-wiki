Evidence¶

Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary 2015¶

Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary. (2015). Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581287

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation findings from teacher surveys and interviews. One teacher interviewed said: 'We didn't ever really get guidance as to how to get the whole of the Quest lesson into a lesson.'

"The perceived weaknesses of Quest according to teachers were: that it was overly prescriptive; that the lesson plans contained too much material to be covered in a standard 50 or 60-minute school period; and that it contained insufficient writing opportunities."

Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary 2015 (2)¶

Quest: Evaluation report and Executive summary. (2015). Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581287

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation survey and interview findings; positive feedback was also provided on the range of books and digital texts, although these could be improved for 'lower ability' sets.

"On the whole, teachers were also positive about Quest's co-operative learning structure (particularly the teamwork and opportunities for discussion)."
