Orprayoon 2014¶

Orprayoon, S. (2014). Effects of Cooperative Learning on Learning Achievement and Group Working Behavior of Junior Students in Modern French Literature Course. The Journal of Effective Teaching, Vol. 14, No.1, 80-98. https://uncw.edu/jet

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Teacher appraisals of self-preparation and during-group-work behavior plus member self-assessments on a Five-point Likert Scale were collected during the 11-week treatment. Descriptive means only are printed; no inferential test or effect size is reported for this outcome.

"the mean of group working behavior assessed by the teacher scored at a high level while the mean of self – assessment on group working behavior of the three groups scored at a high level to the highest level (The mean of the first group is 4.67, the mean of the second group is 4.42 and the mean of the third group is 4.40)."
