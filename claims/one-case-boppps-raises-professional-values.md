## Evidence

### Xiao Xuejun 2025

Xiao Xuejun, Xie Ying, Zhao Xiangfeng, Dai Xiaohong, Liang Feng, Wang Wanchao, Zhang Xianhua. (2025). The application effectiveness of the “One Case to the End” teaching model based on BOPPPS in pediatric nursing: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07073-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test on the 26-item Nurse Professional Values Scale (study Cronbach's α 0.829) comparing 138 experimental with 138 control students at semester end. The results section and Table 6 report p < 0.01 for the total score and every dimension.

> "Through the independent samples t-test, the total score of the experimental group’s professional value competence and the scores of each dimension were higher than those of the control group, and the difference was significant (p < 0.05)"
