## Evidence

### Martha Bleeker 2015

Martha Bleeker, Nicholas Beyler, Susanne James-Burdumy, Jane Fortson. (2015). The Impact of Playworks on Boys' and Girls' Physical Activity During Recess. Journal of School Health, vol. 85, no. 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-impact-of-playworks-on-boys-and-girls-physical-activity-during-recess

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational recess data collected alongside accelerometer measurement in the 29-school randomized trial. The article reports significant impacts on girls' activity types, with treatment girls less sedentary and more often jumping, playing tag, and playing playground games; no effect sizes are printed.

> "A significant impact was also found on the types of activities in which girls engaged during recess; girls in the treatment group were less likely than those in the control group to be sedentary and more likely to engage in jumping, tag, and playground games."
