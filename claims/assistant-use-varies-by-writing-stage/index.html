Evidence¶

Divyansh Chandarana 2026¶

Divyansh Chandarana, Sandipan De, Vivek Gupta. (2026). DRAFTTRACE: A Multi-View Analytics Environment for AI-Integrated Writing. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36544 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.36544

q2 · i? · design · r2

Interaction-view analysis of the classroom setting, in which 32 of 81 students used the integrated assistant, issuing 75 prompts (median 2 per student). Sessions were divided into four equal quarters and prompts categorized as clarification, verification, direct help, or collaboration. Descriptive counts only.

"Students whose first prompt came in the first quarter sought clarification (6 of 10) or direct help (4 of 10). Students who first used the assistant in the last quarter almost always sought verification of their answers (9 of 10)."
