## Evidence

### Tsikas Stefanos A. 2024

Tsikas Stefanos A., Dauer Karina. (2024). Examining interviewer bias in medical school admissions: The interplay between applicant and interviewer gender and its effects on interview outcomes. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309293

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression analysis of 5,036 interview observations (2006–2019) with an interaction term commission type × candidate gender, year fixed effects, and covariates; partial effects plotted in Fig 2. The article reports "no significant differences between CF and CM for any committee type"; the FF male advantage was not statistically significant, with wide 95% confidence intervals.

> "Fig 2 corroborates that there are no significant differences between CF and CM for any committee type. However, FF committees scored males approximately one point higher than females, while the differences in MM and MF groups are marginal."
