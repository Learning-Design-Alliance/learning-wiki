## Evidence

### Sung Yao-Ting 2017

Sung Yao-Ting, Yang Je-Ming, Lee Han-Yueh. (2017). The Effects of Mobile-Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning: Meta-Analysis and Critical Synthesis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317704307

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects moderator analysis of dependent variables. Table 4 reports learning achievement g = 0.551 (k = 39), learning attitude g = 0.518 (k = 13), and peer interaction g = 0.415 (k = 7), all significant; QB = 0.465 did not reach significance at the .05 level.

> "When using mobile devices for different learning outcomes, the ES was largest for studies in which the learning outcome was learning achievement, followed by learning attitude (including motivation, interest, and engagement) and peer interaction."
