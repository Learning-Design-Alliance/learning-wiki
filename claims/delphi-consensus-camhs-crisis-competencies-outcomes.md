`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Three-round Delphi study with 69 initial participants, of whom 43 (62%) completed all rounds. Consensus was predefined as ≥70% ratings of 4–5 and <15% ratings of 1–2. The article reports "over 90% of proposed items meeting the consensus threshold".

> "A broad consensus was reached, with over 90% of proposed items meeting the consensus threshold."
