# RIDE communication instruction and RIDE plus CHT both increase team regulation relative to no support

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` A MANCOVA with overall messages as covariate showed a significant main effect of condition on regulation, driven by team regulation (η² = .15 omnibus, .13 univariate). [→ Saab 2012](#saab-2012)
`q3 i?` Post hoc tests showed both RIDE (p=.034) and CHT (p=.000) conditions produced more team regulation messages than Control, with no significant difference between the two experimental conditions. [→ Saab 2012 (2)](#saab-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Saab 2012

Saab, N., van Joolingen, W., & van Hout-Wolters, B. (2012). Support of the collaborative inquiry learning process: influence of support on task and team regulation. Metacognition and Learning, 7, 7-23. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9068-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-way between-groups MANCOVA on 48 dyads (Control n=12, RIDE n=21, CHT n=15) with overall number of messages as covariate; the omnibus effect was η² = .15 and only the team regulation univariate effect was significant (η² = .13).

> "The MANCOV A revealed a significant main effect, F(2,44)=3.95, p<.05: Roy ’s Largest Root=.18 η2 =.15. The univariate result that was significant is Team regulation, F(2,45)= 3.41, p<.05, η2 =.13."

### Saab 2012 (2)

Saab, N., van Joolingen, W., & van Hout-Wolters, B. (2012). Support of the collaborative inquiry learning process: influence of support on task and team regulation. Metacognition and Learning, 7, 7-23. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9068-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni post hoc analysis following the MANCOVA compared team regulation frequencies across the three conditions; both experimental conditions exceeded Control and did not differ from each other.

> "students in the experimental conditions, the RIDE condition (M =97.19, SD=66.48) and the CHT condition ( M=134.13, SD=51.59) produced significantly more chat messages in the team regulation category than students in the Control condition ( M=44.42, SD=31.81), (RIDE condition: p=.034; CHT condition: p=.000)"
