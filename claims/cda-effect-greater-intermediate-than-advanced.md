# The effect of CDA-based instruction on reading comprehension is greater for intermediate than advanced proficiency students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Intermediate experimental students gained 8.32 points on average while advanced students gained 6.32, a significant difference between the two proficiency levels. [→ Javadi 2019](#javadi-2019)

## Evidence

### Javadi 2019

Javadi, J., & Mohammadi, M. (2019). The Effect of Critical Discourse-Based Instruction on Iranian English Major Students’ Reading Comprehension. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 33-52. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201136

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent t-test comparing mean gains of intermediate (n reported as 62 subjects overall pre-assignment) and advanced experimental students; the printed table gives t = 2.285, P = 0.026, and the article reports the intermediate gain exceeded the advanced gain.

> "the mean scores of the intermediate students in the experimental groups increased by 08.32 and the average scores of the advanced level students increased by 06.32. Actually, reading comprehension of the intermediate level students has increased more than reading comprehension of the advanced level students."
