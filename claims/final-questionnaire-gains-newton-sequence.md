# After the didactic sequence, correct questionnaire answers rose from 4% to 51% of total questions, while blank answers fell from 29% to 3%

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Comparing the initial and final questionnaires, correct answers increased from 4% to 51%, incomplete answers decreased from 13% to 10%, wrong answers from 55% to 37%, and blank answers from 28% to 3%.
