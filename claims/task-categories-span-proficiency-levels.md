### A.R. Piña 2025

Analysis of the role-expertise column of the task table (Table I) built from employee interviews. The article reports multiple proficiency levels appear "in two categories (Experimental, Theory and Knowledge of Experiment)" and that the theory-only category is largely experts.

> "there are multiple quantum proficiency levels (e.g., none, aware, conversant, proficient, expert) represented in two categories (Experimental, Theory and Knowledge of Experiment), though a more comprehensive reporting could reveal an even wider range of proficiency levels."
