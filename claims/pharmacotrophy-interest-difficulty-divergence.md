# Question interest correlated negatively with difficulty for students but positively for teachers

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` For students, interest in a question type was negatively correlated with its perceived difficulty (r = −0.6609; p = 0.0375). [→ Delage Clément 2024](#delage-clement-2024)
`q2 i3` For teachers, interest in a question type was positively correlated with its difficulty (r = 0.7296; p = 0.0166). [→ Delage Clément 2024 (2)](#delage-clement-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Delage Clément 2024

Delage Clément, Palayer Maeva, Lerouet Dominique, Besson Valérie C. (2024). “Pharmacotrophy”: a playful tournament for game- and team-based learning in pharmacology education - assessing its impact on students’ performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05157-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation across mean student scores per online question type in 2022, plotted in Fig. 3A. The article reports interest “negatively correlated to difficulty (r = − 0.6609; p = 0.0375)”.

> "Considering the mean participant’s score for each type of question, interest is still negatively correlated to difficulty (r = − 0.6609; p = 0.0375; Fig. 3A)."

### Delage Clément 2024 (2)

Delage Clément, Palayer Maeva, Lerouet Dominique, Besson Valérie C. (2024). “Pharmacotrophy”: a playful tournament for game- and team-based learning in pharmacology education - assessing its impact on students’ performance. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05157-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation across mean teacher scores per question type in 2022 (n = 10 question types), plotted in Fig. 3B. The article reports interest “positively correlated to difficulty (r = 0.7296; p = 0.0166)”.

> "Considering the mean teacher’s score for each type of question, interest was still positively correlated to difficulty (r = 0.7296; p = 0.0166; Fig. 3B)."
