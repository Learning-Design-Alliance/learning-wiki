## Evidence

### Charalambos Lemonidis & Ioanna Kaiafa 2019

Charalambos Lemonidis & Ioanna Kaiafa. (2019). The Effect of Using Storytelling Strategy on Students’ Performance in Fractions. Journal of Education and Learning, 8(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v8n2p165

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA on post-test performance with independent variables group and subgroup; all effects were statistically significant at the 0.05 level, including the interaction F(2,70)=4.04, P=0.022.

> "The main effect for “group” yielded an F ratio of F(1,70)= 44.9, p=0.00<0.05. The main effect for “subgroup” yielded an F ratio of F(2.70) = 147.91, p=0.00<0.05. The interaction effect was significant, F(2.70)=4.04, P=0.022<0.05."
