## Evidence

### Domarecki Patryk 2025

Domarecki Patryk, Plata-Nazar Katarzyna, Sohl Kristin. (2025). An Assessment of the Knowledge of Autism Spectrum Disorder Among Polish Primary Care Physicians. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina61040761

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

U-Mann–Whitney comparison in the survey of 166 physicians (80 in training, 86 specialists). General knowledge favored physicians in training ("10.63 vs. 9.75; p = 0.04"); category differences were not significant.

> "Physicians in training present a higher level of general knowledge than certified physicians (10.63 vs. 9.75; p = 0.04), and pediatricians' knowledge of ASD diagnosis is higher compared to that of general practitioners (3.21 vs. 2.19; p = 0.001), with no evidence of significant differences in other categories."
