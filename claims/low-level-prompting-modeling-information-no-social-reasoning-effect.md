# Low-level prompting, modeling, and information provision do not predict students' social reasoning at any lag

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Three of the seven coded instructional moves — low-level prompting, modeling, and information provision — showed no significant prediction of students' social reasoning at Lag-1, Lag-2, or Lag-3. [→ Nagpal 2025](#nagpal-2025)

Null finding from the same SDA of the 24 Week-4 CSR discussions; the article reports these three moves "did not predict students' social reasoning at any lag turns." No effect sizes are printed for these null results, so no magnitude is reported here.
