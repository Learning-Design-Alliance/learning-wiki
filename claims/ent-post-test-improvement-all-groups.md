## Evidence

### MK Goutham 2023

MK Goutham, Saldanha Marina, Bhat Vadisha S., A Rajeshwary, Vincent Mark Jittu, Ravikumar Aishwarya. (2023). Simulation-based training in ear, nose and throat skills and emergencies. Brazilian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjorl.2022.01.001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective interventional study of 60 interns with paired pre/post questionnaires after a 15-day ENT posting including a 1-hour lecture and scenario-specific hands-on simulation. The article reports all groups improved significantly; Table 1 prints group-specific p-values (e.g. Group A epistaxis p = 0.001).

> "All groups showed significant improvements in the post-test scores regardless of their training method."
