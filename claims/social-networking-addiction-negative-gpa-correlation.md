## Evidence

### Azizi Seyyed Mohsen 2019

Azizi Seyyed Mohsen, Soroush Ali, Khatony Alireza. (2019). The relationship between social networking addiction and academic performance in Iranian students of medical sciences: a cross-sectional study. BMC Psychology 7, 28. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-019-0305-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional study of 360 students at KUMS sampled by stratified random sampling, using the BSMAS and GPA from the previous term; Spearman's correlation was used because variables were non-normal, yielding a printed effect size of r = −0.210, p < 0.01.

> "There was a negative and significant relationship between students’ addiction to social networking and their academic performance (r = − 0.210, p < 0.01)."
