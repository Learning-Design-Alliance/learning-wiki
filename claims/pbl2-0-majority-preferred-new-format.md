## Evidence

### Wiznia Daniel 2012

Wiznia Daniel, Korom Robert, Marzuk Peter, Safdieh Joseph, Grafstein Bernice. (2012). PBL 2.0: enhancing problem-based learning through increased student participation. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v17i0.17375

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course survey self-assessment of learning and format preference, comparing PBL 2.0 in a second-year course to students' recollected first-year traditional PBL. Descriptive percentages; no statistical test or effect size is printed.

> "over 80% indicated that they learned the material better with PBL 2.0, and 56% indicated that PBL 2.0 provided a better climate for learning. Overall, 75% of students preferred PBL 2.0 to the old format, while 12% were indifferent. Only 7% preferred the old format."
