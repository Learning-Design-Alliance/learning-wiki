# Students struggled to complete synchronous e-learning activities: only 53% completed the pretest and 45% the posttest

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Only a minority of enrolled students completed the synchronous pretest and posttest on the e-learning platform. [→ Gachanja Francis 2021](#gachanja-francis-2021)

## Evidence

### Gachanja Francis 2021

Gachanja Francis, Mwangi Nyawira, Gicheru Wagaki. (2021). E-learning in medical education during COVID-19 pandemic: experiences of a research course at Kenya Medical Training College. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-03050-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Routine completion data from the Moodle e-learning platform for the online research course show that "only 53% (323) answered all questions" on the pretest and "only 45% (266/579) completed the posttest exam". No effect size is printed.

> "Our records show that 65% (n = 613) of students attempted the pretest, but only 53% (323) answered all questions. Also, we provided a posttest at the end of the module, but only 62% (579/933) of students accessed it. Of these students, only 45% (266/579) completed the posttest exam."
