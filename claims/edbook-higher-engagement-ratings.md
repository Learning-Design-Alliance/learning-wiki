# Learners rated engagement and the helpfulness of code-writing questions significantly higher in EDBook than with webpage content plus ChatGPT

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants gave significantly higher ratings to the statement 'I was highly engaged in the tutorial when learning' in the EDBook condition (p < 0.05, ANOVA). [→ Steve Oney 2024](#steve-oney-2024)
`q2 i?` Participants reported that code-writing questions were more helpful in the EDBook condition than in the webpage-plus-ChatGPT condition (p < 0.05, ANOVA). [→ Steve Oney 2024 (2)](#steve-oney-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Steve Oney 2024

Steve Oney, Yue Shen, Fei Wu, Young Suh Hong, Ziang Wang, Yamini Khajekar, Jiacheng Zhang, and April Yi Wang. (2024). EDBooks: AI-Enhanced Interactive Narratives for Programming Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.10687](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.10687)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-task questionnaire results from the within-subjects study of 20 learners. The engagement item was rated significantly higher in the EDBook condition (p < 0.05, ANOVA); the article prints no effect size.

> "we found that inCEDBook, participants gave a significantly higher rating to the question "I was highly engaged in the tutorial when learning" (𝑝 < 0.05, ANOVA)"

### Steve Oney 2024 (2)

Steve Oney, Yue Shen, Fei Wu, Young Suh Hong, Ziang Wang, Yamini Khajekar, Jiacheng Zhang, and April Yi Wang. (2024). EDBooks: AI-Enhanced Interactive Narratives for Programming Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.10687](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.10687)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Post-task questionnaire results from the same study. Agreement with the statement that code-writing questions helped improve understanding of the material was significantly higher in the EDBook condition (p < 0.05, ANOVA); no effect size is printed.

> "Participants also reported that the code-writing questions are more helpful inCEDBook thanCWeb+GPT (𝑝 < 0.05, ANOVA)."
