## Evidence

### Rees Philippa 2025

Rees Philippa, Dronavalli Mithilesh, Carter Ben, Dickson Michelle, Green Charles, Lawler Kate, Lee Evelyn, Uebel Hannah, Gale Chris, Oei Ju Lee. (2025). School Performance of Preterm-Born Children After Intraventricular Hemorrhage. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.47584

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted linear regression in the population-based cohort of New South Wales children compared 557 children with low-grade IVH with 2557 very preterm controls on total NAPLAN z scores at age 8 to 9 years; the difference was "(AMD, −0.06; 95% CI, −0.14 to 0.03)" and not statistically significant. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "low-grade IVH had consistently numerically lower total NAPLAN z scores (AMD, −0.06; 95% CI, −0.14 to 0.03) and domain-specific z scores compared with very preterm controls at age 8 to 9 years, although differences were not clinically or statistically significant"
