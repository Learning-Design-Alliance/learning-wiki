## Evidence

### Gong C 2025

Gong C, Shen Y, Wang J, Zhang P, Li Z. (2025). Application of ultrasound simulation training in intensive care nursing teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06968-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square comparison of post-training satisfaction from a self-made questionnaire administered to both groups (n = 20 each) in the randomized controlled study. The simulation group reached "90% (n = 18)" satisfied versus "80% (n = 16)" in the control group.

> "After the test, the teaching satisfaction of the experimental group was higher than that of the control group (90% (n = 18) vs. 80% (n = 16), χ2 = 6, p < 0.014), and the difference was statistically significant (p < 0.05)"
