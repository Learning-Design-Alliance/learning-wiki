# Early CPKs show quantitative advantages beyond their domain-general advantages, while delayed CPKs' quantitative deficits are largely secondary except for enumeration

## Evidence

### Geary David C. 2019

Geary David C., vanMarle Kristy, Chu Felicia W., Hoard Mary K., Nugent Lara. (2019). Predicting Age of Becoming a Cardinal Principle Knower. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000277

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Contrast analyses comparing the early CPK group against the four other CPK timing groups, controlling FSIQ and executive function. A parallel analysis of delayed CPKs found their deficit remained only on enumeration (p = .0028); other quantitative deficits were no longer significant (ps > .10).

> "The results revealed that the early CPKs had advantages on all of the quantitative measures, above and beyond their advantages in FSIQ and executive function (ps < .0025)."
