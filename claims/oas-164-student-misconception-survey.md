`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A preliminary survey gave 164 students one of three popular passages (Q5, Q12, Q15) and asked about scale readings of a twin on a moving train viewed from both frames, with responses in Table I. "less than a fifth of the students gave the completely correct response, (=,=)".

> "Of note is that less than a fifth of the students gave the completely correct response, (=,=), and that more than a third indicated that the weight changes in the rest frame Al."

### Gary Oas 2005 (2)

> "Also interesting is that (Q15) is the only quote of the three to explicitly state the relativity of the observation s in diﬀerent frames, yet less than a ﬁfth (9/52) gave the correct answer."
