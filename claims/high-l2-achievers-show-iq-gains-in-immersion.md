### Cummins 1979

Cummins, James. (1979). Linguistic interdependence and the educational development of bilingual children. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED257312. [doi:10.3102/00346543049002222](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543049002222)

`q2 · i?`

The review reports Barik and Swain's (1976) longitudinal analysis of Ottawa and Toronto immersion program data. High achievers' IQ scores increased over three years while low achievers' remained unchanged, suggesting attainment of high L2 skills is associated with greater cognitive growth.

> "high. French achievers at the grade 3 level performed significantly better than low French achievers on two of the three Otis-Lennon IQ subtests when scores were adjusted for initial IQ and age differences between the two groups."
