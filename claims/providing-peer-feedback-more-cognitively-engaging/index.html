Narrative review section on composing and receiving peer feedback, attributing the finding to Nicol, Thomson, and Breslin (2014). The review reports that giving feedback engages "higher-order processes" more than receiving it.

"Providing comments to peers is often more beneficial than receiving them because it is more cognitively-engaging: involving higher-order processes, such as application of criteria, diagnosing problems and suggesting solutions (Nicol, Thomson, and Breslin 2014)."
