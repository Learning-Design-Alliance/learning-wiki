## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Erin K. Washburn 2022

Erin K. Washburn, Benjamin C. Heddy, Emily Binks-Cantrell, Tiffany Peltier. (2022). What do teachers know about dyslexia? It’s complicated! The State Education Standard. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/what-do-teachers-know-about-dyslexia-its-complicated/ [doi:10.1007/s11145-022-10264-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-022-10264-8)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Item-format analysis within the Dyslexia Knowledge Questionnaire-2 administration to 524 teachers: responses to items presenting a term differed from responses to items presenting an explanation of the term. No effect size is printed.

> "teachers were either distributed between scientific conceptions, misconceptions, and/or unsure or responded differently depending upon whether the item contained a term versus an explanation of the term within the item"
