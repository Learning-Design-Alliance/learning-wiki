### Woodham Luke A. 2019 (2)

Woodham Luke A., Round Jonathan, Stenfors Terese, Bujacz Aleksandra, Karlgren Klas, Jivram Trupti, Riklefs Viktor, Poulton Ella, Poulton Terry. (2019). Virtual patients designed for training against medical error: Exploring the impact of decision-making on learner motivation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0215597

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-institution t-tests at ZSMU: "the mean for the linear group being higher than the branching (p = .001)" for Self-Efficacy of Learning Performance; ZSMU was the only institution reporting this difference.

> "ZSMU was the only institution who reported a significant difference between the groups for Self-Efficacy of Learning Performance, with the mean for the linear group being higher than the branching (p = .001)"
