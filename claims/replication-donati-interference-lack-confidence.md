### Schillinger 2021

Schillinger, F. L., Mosbacher, J. A., Brunner, C., Vogel, S. E., & Grabner, R. H. (2021). Revisiting the Role of Worries in Explaining the Link Between Test Anxiety and Test Performance. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09601-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Replication comparison against Donati et al. (2019), who found interference and lack of confidence but not worry or arousal significantly related to a standardized arithmetic test in Italian secondary school students. The present multiple-regression study extends this to unique variance and to a domain-general intelligence test.

> "The present results replicate the correlations between PAF subscales and arithmetic performance reported by Donati et al. (2019). In addition, we show that interference and lack of confidence explain unique variance in both arithmetic fact retrieval and arithmetic procedures."
