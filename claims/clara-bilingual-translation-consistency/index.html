Sijing Yin, Zirui Wang, Qian Liu, Jiamou Liu. (2026). CLARA: Can AI Assess Developmental Appropriateness in Children's Stories?. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05783

q2 · i? · design · r2

Translated bilingual consistency analysis comparing GPT-4o annotations of Chinese and English versions of the same 100 stories (Table 3). The article reports "overall annotation sim- ilarity of 0.772 and developmental-stage agree- ment of 86.0%", while strict exact full-set match is only 0.180, reflecting difficulty of identical fine-grained annotations under translation.

"As shown in Table 3, developmental represen- tations remain relatively stable across translated bilingual narratives, with overall annotation sim- ilarity of 0.772 and developmental-stage agree- ment of 86.0%."
