# Training on AI interaction strategies slightly increases cognitive load during AI-assisted design

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Receiving training on effective AI interaction strategies slightly increased teachers' cognitive load while designing with AI. [→ Pishtari 2027](#pishtari-2027)

## Evidence

### Pishtari 2027

Pishtari, G., Gnadlinger, F., Ley, T. (2027). When Teachers Use AI Chatbots and Are Trained for It: Impact on Learning Design Quality and Cognitive Effort. In: Blanchard, E.G., Chen, G., Chi, M., Isotani, S. (eds) Artificial Intelligence in Education. AIED 2026. Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol 16585. Springer, Cham. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_28

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects comparison of cognitive-load measures between the AI-access and AI-plus-training conditions among thirteen higher education teachers. The article reports a slight increase in cognitive load; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Training did not further improve design quality but slightly increased cognitive load, likely due to the deliberate application of newly learned strategies."
