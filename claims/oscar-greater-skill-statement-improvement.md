`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-assessment comparison between regular and intervention groups at end-of-rotation in the prospective case-controlled study; the intervention group showed larger gains, e.g. "skill-1 statement: 48%±3% vs 105±9%, p<0.001". No effect size printed.

> "The degree of improvement in trainees' agreement with the aforementioned skills-related statements 1–2 was greater in the intervention group than that in the regular group (regular vs intervention group: skill-1 statement: 48%±3% vs 105±9%, p<0.001); skill-2 statement: 50%±9% vs 71±11%, p<0.05, figure 2A)."
