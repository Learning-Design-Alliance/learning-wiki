Evidence¶

Jeroen Janssen 2020¶

Jeroen Janssen, Paul A. Kirschner. (2020). Applying collaborative cognitive load theory to computer-supported collaborative learning: towards a research agenda. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09729-5

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article reports, citing Zhang et al. (2016), that heterogeneous groups outperformed homogeneous groups on learning achievement, with the benefit for low prior knowledge students and no difference for high prior knowledge students; no effect size is printed.

"In heterogeneous groups, students' learning achievements were greater than in homogeneous groups. However, Zhang et. al. also demonstrated that prior knowledge as a student characteristics plays a role here: for students with little prior knowledge heterogeneous groups were most beneficial, whereas for students with high prior knowledge no difference was found between working in homogeneous or heterogeneous groups."
