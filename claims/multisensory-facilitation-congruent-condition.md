## Evidence

### Ruiter D. J. 2010

Ruiter D. J., van Kesteren M. T. R., Fernandez G. (2010). How to achieve synergy between medical education and cognitive neuroscience? An exercise on prior knowledge in understanding. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-010-9244-5

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Shams and Seitz (2008), that 'the learning advantage of auditory-visual training over visual-alone training was substantial', and that multisensory facilitation of unisensory learning is more pronounced in the congruent condition; three possible neural mechanisms are listed as explanations.

> "An important aspect of multisensory facilitation of unisensory learning is that facilitation is more pronounced in the congruent condition (Shams and Seitz 2008)."
