Briseño-Garzón, A., Han, A., Birol, G., Bates, S., & Whitehead, L. (2016). Faculty perceptions of challenges and enablers of effective teaching in a large research-intensive university: Preliminary findings. Collected Essays on Learning and Teaching, 9, 133-144. https://celt.uwindsor.ca/ceelt/vol9/brisenogarzon

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Open-ended responses identified the research focus of promotion and tenure processes as the third most cited teaching challenge (117 comments). Lack of recognition for teaching excellence was the fourth challenge (91 comments), and a culture change valuing teaching equally with research was the second most popular suggested change (85 comments).
