White 2015¶

White, A. S., Howell Smith, M., Kunz, G. M., & Nugent, G. C. (2015). Active ingredients of instructional coaching: Developing a conceptual framework (R2Ed Working Paper No. 2015-3). https://r2ed.unl.edu/resources_workingpapers.shtml

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Literature review approach section: the authors searched EBSCO Academic Search Premier and identified 53 coaching articles, compiling "182 components attributed by authors as important to successful coaching," many overlapping. No effect sizes are reported; this is a descriptive synthesis.

"We identified 53 articles that examined the coaching of teachers and provided or implied those components that led to success. In all, 182 components attributed by authors as important to successful coaching were identified, although many of these overlapped or were closely related."
