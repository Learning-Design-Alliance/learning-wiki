Compositional multiple linear regression (CoDA) in the GUSTO cohort (n = 432; Model 2 adjusted for sex, ethnicity, maternal age, maternal education, child BMI) found the article reports "no associations of 24 h-movement behaviors at ages 5.5 and 8 years with executive function and academic achievement." No effect sizes were printed for the omnibus composition models.
