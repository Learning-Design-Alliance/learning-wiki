# Pure ADHD symptoms in adolescence are associated with poorer academic performance at age 16 than controls, in both males and females

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r3` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2023 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Male adolescents with ADHD symptoms had significantly lower grades than controls across all subjects (e.g., general subjects 6.63 vs 7.61, p < 0.001). [→ Seppä S 2023](#seppa-s-2023)
`q3 i?` Female adolescents with ADHD symptoms had significantly lower grades than controls across all subjects (e.g., Finnish 7.60 vs 8.37, p < 0.001). [→ Seppä S 2023 (2)](#seppa-s-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Seppä S 2023

Seppä S, Halt A-H, Nordström T, Hurtig T. (2023). Effects of Symptoms of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) on Academic Performance and Educational Attainment. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-023-01598-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

ANOVA/Welch comparison of 9th-grade register grades among NFBC1986 males in the ADHD, ODD, ADHD+ODD, and control groups (Table 2). Controls scored highest in every subject; the ADHD group's general-subjects mean was 6.63 (SD 1.00). Only test statistics and means were printed, no effect size.

> "The control group had the highest grades in all subjects, the mean grade for the control group in general subjects for example being 7.61 (SD 1.10, p < 0.001)"

### Seppä S 2023 (2)

Seppä S, Halt A-H, Nordström T, Hurtig T. (2023). Effects of Symptoms of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) on Academic Performance and Educational Attainment. Child Psychiatry and Human Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-023-01598-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

ANOVA/Welch comparison of register grades among NFBC1986 females (Table 3). Controls scored highest in all subjects; the ADHD group's Finnish mean was 7.60 (SD 0.86). Only means and p-values were printed, no effect size.

> "The control group had the highest grades in all subjects, the mean grade for the control group in Finnish for example being 8.37 (SD 0.94, p < 0.001)"
