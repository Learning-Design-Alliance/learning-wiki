## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Login time was not correlated with satisfaction so its mediation was not tested, and posts showed no significant mediation effect. [→ Chen Tianjiao 2023](#chen-tianjiao-2023)

## Evidence

### Chen Tianjiao 2023

Chen Tianjiao, Luo Heng, Feng Qinna, Li Gege. (2023). Effect of Technology Acceptance on Blended Learning Satisfaction: The Serial Mediation of Emotional Experience, Social Belonging, and Higher-Order Thinking. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054442

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational preliminary analysis of questionnaire and LMS log data from 110 students; login time and posts came from LMS logfile extraction. Hypotheses H2 and H4 on login-time mediation were "not supported"; Table 5 shows the posts mediation path H3 was nonsignificant (effect = −0.013).

> "learning satisfaction was only significantly correlated with the posts, but not with login time. Therefore, the mediation effect test of login time was not conducted, indicating hypotheses H2 and H4 are not supported."

### Chen Tianjiao 2023 (2)

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`
