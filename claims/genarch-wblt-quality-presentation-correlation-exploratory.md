### Xiao 2026

Xiao, Y., Chen, M., Li, Y., Powers, N., Wiesenfeld, M., Smith, G., Farzin, S., Liu, S. (2026). Generative AI and Extended Reality in Collaborative Architectural Design Education: An Exploratory Studio Study. arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.13494. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13494

`q1 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory team-level Spearman correlations between WBLT subscale scores (Learning, Quality, Engagement) and panel-rated presentation scores for the four GenARch teams. The article reports r=1.00 for quality (r=0.40 for learning and engagement) but explicitly retains it as hypothesis-generating, not evidence that perceived tool quality improved design performance.

> "The reported association between perceived tool quality and presentation score wasr= 1.00(p <0.001). Because this estimate is based on only four teams, it is retained as an exploratory, hypothesis-generating association"
