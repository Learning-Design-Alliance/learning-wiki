### Robert A. Mason 2021

Robert A. Mason, Reinhard A. Schumacher and Marcel Adam Just. (2021). The neuroscience of advanced scientific concepts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00107-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group-membership classification on 15 basic classical concepts also presented to a student group in a previous study, using the union of factor locations from separate group factor analyses. The four misclassified students were all recent graduates enrolled in graduate programs; the five correctly classified students were all undergraduates.

> "it achieved a mean accuracy of 0.79, correctly classifying the group membership for 10 of 10 faculty and 5 of 9 students."
