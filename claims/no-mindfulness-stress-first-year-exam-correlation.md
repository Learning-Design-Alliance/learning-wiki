## Evidence

### Lampe Luisa Charlotte 2021

Lampe Luisa Charlotte, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2021). Mindfulness-based intervention helps preclinical medical students to contain stress, maintain mindfulness and improve academic success. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02578-y

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of T1 exam scores (anatomy 1, biology, chemistry) against simultaneously assessed PSS-10 and MAAS scores. The authors call the stress-biology association "a negligible correlation" with r = 0.177, p = 0.0456.

> "While there were no correlations between mindfulness and exam scores, there was a negligible correlation between perceived stress and the results from the biology exam that yielded in a correlation coefficient of 0.177 and a p-value of 0.0456."
