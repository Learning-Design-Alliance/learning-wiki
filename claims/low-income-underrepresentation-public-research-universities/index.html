Haycock 2010¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of national enrollment data reported in the report's trends section. It states that "those from families earning less than $30,000 per year comprise 20 percent of college students but only 13 percent of students in public research-extensive universities," and that $115,000+ families are 30 percent at these universities versus 20 percent overall.

"Today, among dependent students, those from families earning less than $30,000 per year comprise 20 percent of college students but only 13 percent of students in public research-extensive universities."
