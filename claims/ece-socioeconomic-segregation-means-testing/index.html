Building a National Early Childhood Education System That Works 2021¶

Building a National Early Childhood Education System That Works. (2021). Learning Policy Institute. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED614493

q2 · i? · review · r1

The Background section's access discussion argues the ECE system is highly socioeconomically segregated, citing Urban Institute and Penn State segregation analyses and a Century Foundation report on classroom diversity. It states Head Start and state preschool often serve children in poverty separately from higher-income peers.

"Means testing for programs causes children to be sorted, and thus segregated, into classrooms by their family’s income, which, in practice, often translates to racial, ethnic, and linguistic segregation as well."
