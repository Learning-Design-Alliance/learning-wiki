## Evidence

### Hoffman M 2014

Hoffman M, Wilkinson JE, Xu J, Wiecha J. (2014). The perceived effects of faculty presence vs. absence on small-group learning and group dynamics: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0258-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental paired-survey study: the same 114 students completed paired surveys after 'in' and 'out' case discussions. Students 'self-rated the questions assessing and quantifying various elements of group dynamics/group learning process more highly during cases when faculty members were absent'; reported p range <0.001 to 0.015 (e.g., utilized the knowledge of students in the group 4.5 vs 4.7, p <0.01).
