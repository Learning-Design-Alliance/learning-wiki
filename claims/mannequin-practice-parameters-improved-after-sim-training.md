## Evidence

### Maatouk Iyed 2024

Maatouk Iyed, Maatouk Amani, Gafsi Besma, Mili Emna, Chaouch Mohamed Ali, Toumi Samir, Ouanes Islem, Ben Salem Faouzi, Ouanes Besbes Lamia. (2024). Contribution de l'apprentissage de l'échocardiographie par simulation à la formation des résidents en médecine. La Tunisie Médicale. https://doi.org/10.62438/tunismed.v102i7.4793

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Practical evaluation on the Schallware ultrasound mannequin before and after training (Table 3), scoring five reference views on five criteria from 1 (bad) to 5 (very good). All six parameters 'significantly improved after training (p< 10-3)'; e.g. parasternal long axis view quality rose from median 2 to 4.

> "All parameters (time of obtaining the View view, quality of the View view, image quality, visualization of structures, interpretability, image stability) evaluating the practice of echocardiographic views by participants on a mannequin significantly improved after training (p< 10-3)."

### Maatouk Iyed 2024 (2)

Maatouk Iyed, Maatouk Amani, Gafsi Besma, Mili Emna, Chaouch Mohamed Ali, Toumi Samir, Ouanes Islem, Ben Salem Faouzi, Ouanes Besbes Lamia. (2024). Contribution de l'apprentissage de l'échocardiographie par simulation à la formation des résidents en médecine. La Tunisie Médicale. https://doi.org/10.62438/tunismed.v102i7.4793

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Timed post-test practical evaluation conducted by the evaluator on the mannequin. Median view-obtaining times dropped from 25-40 seconds before training to 'less than in less or equal to 10 seconds by all participants' across the five reference views.

> "At the end of the simulation mannequin training, the median time of obtaining views was less than in less or equal to 10 seconds by all participants (Table 3 )."
