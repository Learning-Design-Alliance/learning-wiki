## Evidence

### Arnaud Stanczak 2022

Arnaud Stanczak, Céline Darnon, Anaïs Robert, Marie Demolliens, Camille Sanrey, Pascal Bressoux, Pascal Huguet, Céline Buchs, Fabrizio Butera, and PROFAN Consortium. (2022). Do Jigsaw classrooms improve learning outcomes? Five experiments and an internal meta-analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, 114 (6), pp.1461-1476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000730

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 applied the TOST equivalence procedure to the observed jigsaw-versus-individualistic effect. The authors report both bounds rejected (p upper = .004, p lower < .001), so the "observed effect size can be considered as statistically equivalent to zero".

> "The results demonstrate that both the upper and lower bounds are rejected under the assumption that the null hypothesis is true, indicating that the observed effect size can be considered as statistically equivalent to zero (p upper = .004 and p lower < .001, respectively)"
