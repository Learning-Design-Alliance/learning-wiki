## Evidence

### Omar Alsaiari 2026

Omar Alsaiari, Nilufar Baghaei, Jason M. Lodge, Omid Noroozi, Dragan Gašević, Marie Boden, Hassan Khosravi. (2026). DIRECTIVE, METACOGNITIVE OR A BLEND OF BOTH? A COMPARISON OF AI-GENERATED FEEDBACK TYPES ON STUDENT ENGAGEMENT, CONFIDENCE, AND OUTCOMES. A PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100553](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100553)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Beta GLMM on platform quality scores combining peer ratings, instructor moderation, and algorithmic adjustments, with student ID as random intercept. EMMs were 3.62 (Directive), 3.72 (Metacognitive), 3.75 (Hybrid); no pairwise contrast reached significance.

> "A beta GLMM with a logit link showed that the overall effect of feedback group was not statistically significant, χ2 = 1.40, p=.49 . Estimated marginal means (back-transformed to the original 1-5 scale) indicated that students in the Directive group scored an average of 3.62"
