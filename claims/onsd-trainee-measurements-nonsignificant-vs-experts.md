# After a 5-min didactic and 20-min hands-on session, military trainees' ONSD measurements do not differ significantly from those of emergency physician experts

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trainee and physician ONSD measurements on the same volunteers showed a statistically nonsignificant difference (P = 0.76). [→ Betcher Joseph 2018](#betcher-joseph-2018)

## Evidence

### Betcher Joseph 2018

Betcher Joseph, Becker Torben K., Stoyanoff Peter, Cranford Jim, Theyyunni Nik. (2018). Military trainees can accurately measure optic nerve sheath diameter after a brief training session. Military Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40779-018-0189-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Proof-of-concept study in which 23 SOCM trainees and four emergency physicians measured ONSD on the same healthy volunteers after a brief training session; a mixed design ANOVA found a "statistically nonsignificant difference of − 0.6 mm". No effect size was printed.

> "Both the trainees and physicians obtained similar optic sheath measurements on the same volunteers, with the Mphysician = 0.465 mm vs. Mtrainees = 0.459 mm, P = 0.76."
