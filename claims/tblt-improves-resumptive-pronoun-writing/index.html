Evidence¶

Rajabi 2015¶

Rajabi, B., & Hashemian, M. (2015). A Comparative Study of the Effectiveness of Two Methods of Teaching Resumptive Pronouns in Writing: TBLT and Blended Learning. English Language Teaching, 8(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n9p196

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired pretest-posttest comparison of the task-based group (TBG, N = 20) on the researcher-made resumptive-pronoun test, reported in Results section 4.3. The pretest mean was "enormously less than that of the posttest"; no effect size is printed.

"It can be seen that in the TBG on the pretest, the mean score of the learners ( M = 13.05) is enormously less than that of the posttest (M = 17.70)."
