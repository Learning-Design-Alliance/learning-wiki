## Evidence

### Joshua Wedlock & Christopher Binnie 2025

Joshua Wedlock & Christopher Binnie. (2025). Deliberate and Purposeful Practice for Second Language Learning: A Framework. The Electronic Journal for English as a Second Language. https://doi.org/10.55593/ej.29115a5

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing de Jong and Perfetti (2011), that "structured speech repetition led to gains in oral fluency," with gains maintained only under repetition of the same speech, underscoring task-specificity. No effect size is printed.

> "de Jong and Perfetti (2011) found that structured speech repetition led to gains in oral fluency, and these gains were maintained only when learners repeated the same speech multiple times, highlighting the importance of consistency and task- specificity in productive language practice"

### Joshua Wedlock & Christopher Binnie 2025 (2)

Joshua Wedlock & Christopher Binnie. (2025). Deliberate and Purposeful Practice for Second Language Learning: A Framework. The Electronic Journal for English as a Second Language. https://doi.org/10.55593/ej.29115a5

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing Taguchi et al. (2004), a study of Japanese university students showing "repeated structured reading practice resulted in faster and more fluent reading," a finding it says Yoshimura and MacWhinney (2007) also reported. No effect size is printed.

> "In a study of Japanese university students, Taguchi et al. (2004) showed that repeated structured reading practice resulted in faster and more fluent reading, a finding also reported by Yoshimura and MacWhinney (2007)"
