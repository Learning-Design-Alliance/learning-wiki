## Evidence

### Robert Colvin 2016

Robert Colvin. (2016). Optimising, generalising and integrating educational practice using neuroscience. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.12

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument from the article's generalisation section: overlap in timing and regions between task signatures motivates testing transfer, illustrated hypothetically by a grammatical task sharing a visual processing bottleneck with a geometry task. No empirical data reported.

> "That is, if an intervention improves pro ﬁciency in a task whose signature resembles or overlaps with that of another task, it is reasonable to investigate whether the bene ﬁts of the intervention transfer to the other domain."
