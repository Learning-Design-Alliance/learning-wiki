### Kaśka Porayska-Pomsta 2016

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

Observational report of the LeAM knowledge-elicitation studies with five experienced tutors using a purpose-built factor-selection tool with fuzzy-linguistic values during live WoZ tutoring. "This was reflected in the tutors ’ feedback to the learners, which over time became more positive, more elaborate, and more targeted". The article states no objective effectiveness measures can be offered for these projects.

> "This was reflected in the tutors ’ feedback to the learners, which over time became more positive, more elaborate, and more targeted to the actual factor diagnoses made than was the case initially."
