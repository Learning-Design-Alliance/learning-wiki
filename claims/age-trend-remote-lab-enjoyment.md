## Evidence

### Kolb U. 2018

Kolb U., Brodeur M., Braithwaite NStJ., and Minocha S. (2018). A Robotic Telescope For University-Level Distance Teaching. RTSRE Proceedings Vol. 1, No. 1. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1806.02989](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1806.02989)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative analysis of pre- and post-activity electronic surveys with ranking and Likert-style questions from OU students; the article reports this as one of two notable trends, with no strong demographic correlations otherwise.

> "with increasing age students display an increased agreement with the sentiments that both virtual experiments (VEs) and remote experiments (REs) make practical science enjoyable. By contrast younger students expressed a preference for being co-located with practical equipment."
