## Evidence

### Luke D. Conlin 2015

Luke D. Conlin. (2015). The use of epistemic distancing to create a safe space to sensemake in introductory physics tutorials. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1508.01574](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1508.01574)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The author's concluding interpretation of the case study: students offered ideas tentatively "with hedging and laughter," the instructor took them seriously and pushed for mechanism, and "Their use of epistemic distancing, in response to TA Joey's, helped them switch out of their dismissive approach." This is the author's interpretation, not a tested result.

> "It was his introduction of epistemic distancing that made space for the students to offer their ideas of what was making the “jumps” in the graph."
