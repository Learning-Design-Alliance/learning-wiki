## Evidence

### Pape-Lindstrom 2018

Pape-Lindstrom, P., Eddy, S., & Freeman, S. (2018). Reading Quizzes Improve Exam Scores for Community College Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-08-0160

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bloom's taxonomy analysis of a random 25% sample of exam questions from all six terms, rated by two blind raters reaching consensus; weighted Bloom's index values ranged from 32.1 (control 2009) to 37.8 (quiz 2012). The article reports values "varied over the six terms within a range of 5.7 units."

> "The average values varied over the six terms within a range of 5.7 units on the index’s 100-point scale, and in each term analyzed here, exams averaged close to or slightly above the conceptual level."
