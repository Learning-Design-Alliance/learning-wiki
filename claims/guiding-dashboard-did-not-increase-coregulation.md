# The alerting & guiding dashboard did not increase teacher coregulation more than the mirroring dashboard (H3 not supported); mirroring increased intervention rates (non-significant) while guiding-condition interventions were rare and mostly coregulation strategies

## Evidence

### Reet Kasepalu 2023

Reet Kasepalu, Pankaj Chejara, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley. (2023). Studying teacher withitness in the wild: comparing a mirroring and an alerting & guiding dashboard for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2023) 18:575–606. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09414-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observation-based coding of interventions in the authentic-classroom quasi-experiment: "There was a difference that proved non-significant under a Chi-squared test (5.62, p = 0.06)" between 45.8% intervention in no dashboard and 73.7% in mirroring-only conditions. No effect size printed.

> "Overall, the mirroring only condition activated the teachers into intervening more (the participating teachers carried out an intervention in the classroom in 45.8% (11 teachers out of 24) of the cases in the no dashboard condition to 73.7% (14 out of 19) in the mirroring only condition). There was a difference that proved non-significant under a Chi-squared test (5.62, p = 0.06)."

### Reet Kasepalu 2023 (2)

Reet Kasepalu, Pankaj Chejara, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley. (2023). Studying teacher withitness in the wild: comparing a mirroring and an alerting & guiding dashboard for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2023) 18:575–606. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09414-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same coded intervention data, "only 33.3% of the participating teachers started an intervention during the CL activity, which means that we did not prove the third hypothesis"; the authors note three of those interventions were coregulation strategies asking about process or prompting group members.

> "In the alerting & guiding dashboard condition, however, only 33.3% of the participating teachers started an intervention during the CL activity, which means that we did not prove the third hypothesis."
