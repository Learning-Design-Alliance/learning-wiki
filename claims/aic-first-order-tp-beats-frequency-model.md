# Model comparison by AIC shows first-order TP, not frequency, accounts for learning of the third-item transition times

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A mixed model including first-order TP and block number fit RTTT2 better than a model including frequency and block number, and adding frequency did not improve the model. [→ Lazartigues Laura 2021](#lazartigues-laura-2021)

## Evidence

### Lazartigues Laura 2021

Lazartigues Laura, Mathy Fabien, Lavigne Frédéric. (2021). Statistical learning of unbalanced exclusive-or temporal sequences in humans. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246826

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

AIC comparison of three mixed models for RTTT2 in the learning phase (Table 7): Block * First-order TP (AIC 179 528.1) versus Block * Frequency (AIC 179 559.5); the authors add that "Lower AIC corresponds to a more optimal model".

> "The results showed that the model including first-order TP and block number was better than the model including frequency and block number. Moreover, the model including all factors was not better than the model including only first-order TP and block number."
