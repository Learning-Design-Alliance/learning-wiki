### Nagyová Valéria 2026

Nagyová Valéria, Blaňár Dominik, Kybic Jan, Schwendicke Falk, Tichý Antonín. (2026). Student Performance in Bitewing Caries Detection: Artificial Intelligence Versus Alternative E-learning. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109567

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis tests of pre-to-post improvement across the Lecture (n = 16), Dataset (n = 17), and AI (n = 19) groups found no significant differences involving the AI Group; the article reports "No significant differences were found between the AI Group... and the other groups."

> "No significant differences were found between the AI Group, which underwent training on bitewings labelled with AI-generated bounding boxes, and the other groups."

### Nagyová Valéria 2026 (2)

Nagyová Valéria, Blaňár Dominik, Kybic Jan, Schwendicke Falk, Tichý Antonín. (2026). Student Performance in Bitewing Caries Detection: Artificial Intelligence Versus Alternative E-learning. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109567

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of group-level error reductions in Table 4 and Appendix B medians: the AI Group's mean error reduction was largest, but "its median improvement was comparable to the Lecture Group and smaller than that of the Dataset Group," suggesting less consistent group-wide progress.

> "The AI Group showed the greatest reduction in the average number of errors after training, yet its median improvement was comparable to the Lecture Group and smaller than that of the Dataset Group"
