# School cycle (age/grade level) significantly affects vocabulary test performance, with first-cycle pupils scoring lowest

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pupils in the first cycle achieved significantly lower scores on the post-test than pupils in the second and third cycles. [→ van Hove 2017](#van-hove-2017)

## Evidence

### van Hove 2017

van Hove, S., vanderhoven, E., & Cornillie, F. (2017). The tablet for Second Language Vocabulary Learning: Keyboard, Stylus or Multiple Choice. Comunicar, 50, XXV. https://doi.org/10.3916/C50-2017-05

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

An ANOVA on total mean scores by school cycle in the quasi-experimental study found first-cycle pupils (M=47.15%) scored significantly lower than second-cycle (M=73.24%) and third-cycle (M=77.44%) pupils on the post-test.

> "the school cycle had a significant effect (F(2,232)=60.493; p=.000). Pupils in the first cycle (M=47.15%; Sd=16.84%) achieved a significant lower score on the post-test than the second (M=73.24%; Sd=16.18%) and third cycle (M=77.44%; Sd=17.62%)"
