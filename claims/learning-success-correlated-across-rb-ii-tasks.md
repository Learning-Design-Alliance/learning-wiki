## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran 2023

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran. (2023). Stable, flexible, common, and distinct behaviors support rule-based and information-integration category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00163-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cross-task correlation from the moving-window cumulative accuracy analysis in Experiment 1 (86 participants), replicated in Experiment 2 (93 participants): the printed correlation was r = 0.64 in Experiment 1 and r = 0.67 in Experiment 2, p < 0.001, indicating "better learners were better learners across different tasks".

> "Learning success was strongly correlated across the RB and II tasks ( r(84) = 0.64, 95% CI [0.50, 0.75], p < 0.001; Fig. 3c). This pattern directly replicated in Experiment 2"

### Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran 2023 (2)

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran. (2023). Stable, flexible, common, and distinct behaviors support rule-based and information-integration category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00163-0

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject comparison of maximum cumulative accuracy in Experiment 1 (n = 86): II M = 67% vs RB M = 62%, t(85) = 3.55, d = 0.38; Experiment 2 replicated with M = 61% vs 57%, d = 0.45.

> "participants tended to have higher learning success in the II task ( M = 67%) compared to the RB task ( M = 62%, t(85) = 3.55, p = 0.00063, d = 0.38, 95% CI [2.00, 7.11]; Fig. 3b)"
