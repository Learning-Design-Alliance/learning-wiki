Evidence¶

Matsuda 2013¶

Matsuda, N., Yarzebinski, E., Keiser, V., Raizada, R., Stylianides, G. J., & Koedinger, K. R. (2013). Studying the Effect of a Competitive Game Show in a Learning by Teaching Environment. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0009-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Process-data comparison between Game Show and Baseline conditions in the classroom RCT. Game Show students also entered more "deep" responses (MGS=.24 vs. MBL=.16, t(85)=−2.34, p<.05) but tutored fewer problems in less total time.

"the mean probability of responding to SimStudent’s questions was MGS=.93 (SD=0.12) vs. MBL=.83 (SD=0.25); t(63)=2.27, p<.05"
