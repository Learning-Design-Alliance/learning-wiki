`q3 i?` Schools receiving SES intervention (needing improvement year 2) had significantly lower PI gains in Grade 4 ELA (β = -10.042, p < .01), and restructuring schools had significantly lower gains in Grade 8 ELA (β = -29.484, p < .001), with mostly insignificant effects elsewhere. ## Evidence

### Lee 2013

Lee, J., Shin, H. & Amo, L. C. (2013). Evaluating the impact of NCLB school interventions in New York state: Does one size fit all? Education Policy Analysis Archives, 21 (67). http://epaa.asu.edu/ojs/article/view/1122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multinomial IPTW regression of differentiated intervention effects (Table 4) comparing each of five intervention stages with schools in good standing. Most stage-by-grade-subject coefficients were insignificant or negative; the article also reports restructuring schools "experienced lower gains in Grade 8 ELA (β = -29.484, p < .001)."

> "schools receiving SES intervention (i.e. in need of improvement, year 2) experienced significantly lower PI gains relative to schools in good standing in Grade 4 ELA (β = -10.042, p < .01)."
