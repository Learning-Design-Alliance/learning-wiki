R. Ramesh & B. Sumathi 2010¶

R. Ramesh & B. Sumathi. (2010). CAI in Learning Voice in English Grammar among Eighth Standard Students – An Experimental Study. li-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102768

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group comparison in the CAI (experimental) group of 46 students; Table 4 reports a mean score difference of 8.92 (post-test 59.35 vs pre-test 50.43) with t = 0.32, not significant at 0.01 level. No effect size is printed.

"From Table 4 it is shown that there is a mean score difference in learning Voice in English Grammar between the post and pre test phase among the Eighth Standard Students in CAI group. However, these differences are not statistically significant at 0.01 level."
