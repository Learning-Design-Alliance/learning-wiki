Study protocol describing the planned design as printed in the Methods/Design section: a prospective cluster randomized control trial with 60 facilities in Kogi and Ebonyi State randomly assigned to LDHF (intervention) or TRAD (control) arms. The facility is the unit of randomization, stratified by zone and level of care. No results are available because the article is a protocol.

> "The study will be a prospective cluster randomized control trial. It will include healthcare providers in 60 health facilities in Kogi and Ebonyi State randomly assigned to either LDHF (intervention) or TRAD (control) arms."
