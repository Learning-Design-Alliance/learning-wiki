Evidence¶

Chu 2019¶

Chu, H., Perry, R., Reade, F., & Marple, S. (2019). Understanding complex instructional change: Classroom observations of Math in Common districts. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED601889

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of rating patterns across the full 201-lesson sample over six observation periods. The report found "no statistically significant differences" between earlier and later lessons; equivalence was not tested.

"However, when we examined data for the full sample across all six observation periods, there were no statistically significant differences in lessons that were observed earlier in the initiative, versus those observed later in the initiative, on any of the dimensions."
