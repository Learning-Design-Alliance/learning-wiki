`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Supervised live SICS procedure recorded anonymously at the 12-month mark for both groups before control training began, analyzed by t test. Live scores are "more complex to interpret" because supervising surgeons could take over at their discretion. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "The mean surgical competency score using the ICO-OSCAR was 62.3 of 95 (65.6%) for the intervention group and 45.0 of 95 (47.4%) for the control group (difference, 17.3 points; 95% CI, 5.2-29.3; P = .006)."
