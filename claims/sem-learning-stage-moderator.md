# SEM effectiveness is largest for secondary students, non-significant for elementary students

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` SEM shows a large positive effect for secondary students (ES = 1.055). [→ Yao Gege 2025](#yao-gege-2025)
`q3 i?` SEM shows no statistically significant effect for elementary students (P = 0.404). [→ Yao Gege 2025 (2)](#yao-gege-2025-2)
`q3 i?` SEM shows a small but significant effect for university students (ES = 0.174). [→ Yao Gege 2025 (3)](#yao-gege-2025-3)

## Evidence

### Yao Gege 2025

Yao Gege, Zhang Junlong, Soh Kim Geok, Bai Xiaorong, Xiao Wensheng, Anuar Mohd Ashraff Mohd, Bao Lixia. (2025). Effective implementation of the Sport Education Model in physical education: A meta-analysis of participant and intervention characteristics. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331228

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by learning stage. Secondary students showed "Effect size = 1.055" (95% CI 0.361–1.759, P = 0.003), a large significant effect, the strongest of the three stages.

> "SEM demonstrated significant improvements for secondary (Effect size = 1.055; P = 0.003) and university (Effect size = 0.174; P = 0.042) students"

### Yao Gege 2025 (2)

Yao Gege, Zhang Junlong, Soh Kim Geok, Bai Xiaorong, Xiao Wensheng, Anuar Mohd Ashraff Mohd, Bao Lixia. (2025). Effective implementation of the Sport Education Model in physical education: A meta-analysis of participant and intervention characteristics. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331228

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis for elementary-level studies found no statistically significant effect (P = 0.404, ES = −0.118); equivalence was not tested.

> "SEM did not achieve statistical significance for physical education learning among elementary school students (P = 0.404)"

### Yao Gege 2025 (3)

Yao Gege, Zhang Junlong, Soh Kim Geok, Bai Xiaorong, Xiao Wensheng, Anuar Mohd Ashraff Mohd, Bao Lixia. (2025). Effective implementation of the Sport Education Model in physical education: A meta-analysis of participant and intervention characteristics. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0331228

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subgroup analysis for university-level studies found a small but statistically significant effect (ES = 0.174, 95% CI 0.006–0.342, P = 0.042).

> "its effect on university students was smaller (Effect size = 0.174, P = 0.042)"
