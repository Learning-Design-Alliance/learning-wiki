## Evidence

### Colleen Lanz Countryman and Hong Wang 2018

Colleen Lanz Countryman and Hong Wang, North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC. (2018). Using Mathcasts to Facilitate Student Comprehension of Physical Applications of Math Concepts. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1809.09720](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1809.09720)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre- and post-video assignments were administered in the authors' E&M course, some on paper in class and some through WebAssign, where first responses were used. The article reports that "students' performance increased from pre- to post-video assignments" for all but the first video; results are plotted in figure 1 with 95% confidence intervals. No effect size is printed.

> "With the exception of the first video, students' performance increased from pre- to post-video assignments. Results from all six of the videos are illustrated in figure 1."
