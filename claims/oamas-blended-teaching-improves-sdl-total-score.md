`q2 i?` After O-AMAS-based blended teaching, nursing students' total SDL ability score was significantly higher than before teaching (P < 0.05). [→ Duan Anyan 2024](#duan-anyan-2024)

## Evidence

### Duan Anyan 2024

Duan Anyan, Jiang Fen, Li Ling, Li Qun, Chen Wei. (2024). Design and practice of blended teaching of internal medicine nursing based on O-AMAS effective teaching model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05588-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Self-controlled before-after trial of 76 second-year nursing undergraduates taught with O-AMAS-based blended teaching; paired-sample t-test showed the after-teaching total SDL score was "superior to the before-teaching" (P < 0.05); Table 4 reports total points 66.25 ± 6.37 before and 70.93 ± 10.29 after, t = -3.373, P = 0.001.

> "The after-teaching was superior to the before-teaching in the total score of nursing students’ SDL ability (P < 0.05)."
