## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The authors argue that earlier studies showing picture superiority used target words whose concepts were familiar and simple, making video unnecessary and potentially distracting. [→ Lin 2012](#lin-2012)

## Evidence

### Lin 2012

Lin, C.-C., & Tseng, Y.-F. (2012). Videos and animations for vocabulary learning: A study on difficult words. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 11(4), 346-356. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ989299

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The authors' re-examination of prior comparative studies (e.g., Chun & Plass) concluded that the target words in those studies conveyed familiar concepts, so "the concepts conveyed by the target words in the previous comparative studies may not have needed such dynamic videos."

> "The reason making animations and films inferior to pictures, based on a further examination done in this study, is that the concepts conveyed by the target words in the previous comparative studies may not have needed such dynamic videos as animations and films."
