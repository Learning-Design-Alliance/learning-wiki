## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zertuche 2012

Zertuche, A., Gerard, L., & Linn, M. C. (2012). How do Openers Contribute to Student Learning? International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(1), 79-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070484

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the two Opener designs on revision of the embedded assessment. The researcher-designed Opener added distinguishing and integrating ideas components and ran about 20 minutes versus about 10 minutes for the teacher-designed Opener.

> "Students who participated in the researcher-designed Opener were significantly more likely to revise their work on the embedded assessment ( M = .75, SD = .44) compared to students who participated in the teacher-designed Opener ( M = .56, SD = .50), t(101) = -1.98, p < 0.05 (Table 8). There was no significant difference in learning gains between the two conditions (Table 9)."
