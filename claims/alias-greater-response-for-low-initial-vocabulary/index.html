Lesaux 2012¶

Lesaux, N. K., Kieffer, M. J., Kelley, J., & Russ, J. (2012). Effects of Academic Vocabulary Instruction for Linguistically Diverse Adolescents: Evidence from a Randomized Field Trial. American Educational Research Journal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ992411

q4 · i? · causal · r2

Cluster-randomized field trial testing student-by-treatment interactions using pretest vocabulary quartiles and continuous pretest scores. The article reports larger effects "for those students who began the intervention with underdeveloped vocabulary knowledge."

"The effects were generally larger for students whose primary home language is not English and for those students who began the intervention with underdeveloped vocabulary knowledge"
