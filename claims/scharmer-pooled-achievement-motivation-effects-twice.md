`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Scharmer's (2020) meta-analysis of longitudinal studies published 2011–August 2020, finding "the pooled effect of achievement on motivation was twice ( β = .12) the pooled effect of motivation on achievement ( β = .06)". Both are small effects; no standardized effect size beyond the betas is printed.

> "A recent meta-analysis (Scharmer, 2020), which includes such studies that were published between 2011 and August 2020, showed that overall, the pooled effect of achievement on motivation was twice ( β = .12) the pooled effect of motivation on achievement ( β = .06), though both are what is conventionally considered a small effect."
