Matsuda 2013¶

Matsuda, N., Yarzebinski, E., Keiser, V., Raizada, R., Stylianides, G. J., & Koedinger, K. R. (2013). Studying the Effect of a Competitive Game Show in a Learning by Teaching Environment. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0009-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Correlational analysis within the classroom study linking questionnaire motivation constructs and process-data engagement variables to test gains; a few motivation–engagement correlations existed but were relatively small.

"There was no notable correlation between the motivation factors and the learning gain on the test scores (both on the procedural skill test and the conceptual knowledge test)."
