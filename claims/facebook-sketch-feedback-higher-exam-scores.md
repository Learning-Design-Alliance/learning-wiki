### Adrie A. Koehler 2023

Adrie A. Koehler, Daniela Rezende Vilarinho-Pereira. (2023). Using social media affordances to support Ill-structured problem-solving skills: considering possibilities and challenges. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10060-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports, citing Chen and Luh (2018), that industrial design students uploading sketches to Facebook for broader discussion "achieved higher exam scores compared to the control group not using social media." No statistics are printed in the review.

> "Chen and Luh (2018) required industrial design students to upload sketches of their assignments to Facebook to gain feedback from not only peers but all their connections. Learners required to upload their work for a broader discussion achieved higher exam scores compared to the control group not using social media."
