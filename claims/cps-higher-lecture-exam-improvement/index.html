Evidence¶

Termos 2013¶

Termos, M. H. (2013). The Effects of the Classroom Performance System on Student Participation, Attendance, and Achievement. International Journal of Teaching and Learning in Higher Education, 25(1), 66-78. http://www.isetl.org/ijtlhe/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive comparison of grade averages on four lecture exams across the four CPS sections and the control section, as summarized in the article's Table 7. By the second exam students had used the CPS during three lectures and were more familiar with it and its group discussions.

"The control group improved by 3.1%, while the experimental section P02 improved by 9.1%, the experimental section P03 by 4.6%, the experimental section P05 by 16.9%, and the experimental section P18 by 4.7%."

Termos 2013 (2)¶

Termos, M. H. (2013). The Effects of the Classroom Performance System on Student Participation, Attendance, and Achievement. International Journal of Teaching and Learning in Higher Education, 25(1), 66-78. http://www.isetl.org/ijtlhe/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive results on the third lecture exam, which covered more chapters: two experimental sections declined while two improved slightly. The author offers the heavier, more information-rich exam load as a possible explanation and calls for further research to determine the reason.

"There was no clear reason behind these results, but it could be related to the heavier load and materials covered on the third lecture exam, which was more information-rich."
