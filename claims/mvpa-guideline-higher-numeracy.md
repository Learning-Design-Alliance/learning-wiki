## Evidence

### Watson Amanda 2022

Watson Amanda, Dumuid Dorothea, Maher Carol, Olds Tim. (2022). Associations between meeting 24-hour movement guidelines and academic achievement in Australian primary school-aged children. Journal of Sport and Health Science. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2020.12.004

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bonferroni-adjusted post-hoc comparisons in the self-report model (n = 1270) against the meets-no-guidelines condition. The article reports numeracy differences for MVPA-guideline combinations with d = 0.3-0.7; Table 2 shows numeracy means of 575-593 for MVPA combinations versus 546 for none.

> "There were also significant differences in numeracy achievement, particularly among those who met the MVPA guideline in any combination (p < 0.05, d = 0.3-0.7), compared with those who met none."
