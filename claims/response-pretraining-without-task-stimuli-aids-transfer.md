## Evidence

### Research Abstracts 1953

Research Abstracts. (1953). https://eric.ed.gov/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled laboratory experiment (Wright Air Development Center report) with four groups of approximately 30 men each responding on a vertical display panel. The abstract reports that "All experimental groups receiving response pretraining in absence of the task stimulus were superior" to the no-pretraining control group; no effect size is printed.

> "All experimental groups receiving response pretraining in absence of the task stimulus were superior in performance of the actual training task than the group receiving no pretraining."
