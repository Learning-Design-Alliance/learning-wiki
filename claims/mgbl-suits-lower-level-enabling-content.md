Theoretical commentary drawing on the author's experience designing instructional games and consulting on mobile games to argue mGBL suits lower-level enabling content. The article offers no empirical data for this claim.

> "While not explicitly stated in Giannakas et al. (2018), it seems to me that mGBL makes more sense for teaching lower-level “enabling” content, such as core skills associated with a single learning domain, rather than complex performances that mix multiple learning domains."
