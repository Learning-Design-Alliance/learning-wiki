### Alvaro Suarez 2017

Alvaro Suarez, Sandra Kahan, Genaro Zavala, and Arturo C. Marti. (2017). Student Conceptual Difficulties in Hydrodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview problem 2 asked students why the water jet's diameter decreases as it falls, an unconfined context. "Twenty-ﬁve percent of the students answered E2.3" (atmospheric compression), arguing that the pressure difference between water and atmosphere caused the narrowing, ignoring the continuity equation.

> "Twenty-ﬁve percent of the students answered E2.3. The main argument was that when the jet velocity increased, its pressure decreased. Thus, the diﬀerence of pressures between the water and the atmosphere was responsible for the eﬀect observed."
