### Alqahtani SS 2025

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analyses across kindergarten and grade 1 found PSF non-significant at fall and mid-year, while the grade 1 hierarchical mid-year model printed PSF2 at β = .094, p = .032 In the multiple regressions the printed standardized coefficients are small: kindergarten fall PSF1 β = −.01 (p = .830), kindergarten mid-year PSF2 β = .094 (p = .107), grade 1 fall PSF1 β = −.06 (p = .394) and grade 1 mid-year PSF2 β = −.002 (p = .982). The article reports "not significant predictors of WRF for any grade in any times".
