## Evidence

### Ravesloot C. J. 2015

Ravesloot C. J., Van der Schaaf M. F., Muijtjens A. M. M., Haaring C., Kruitwagen C. L. J. J., Beek F. J. A., Bakker J., Van Schaik J. P. J., Cate Th. J. Ten. (2015). The don’t know option in progress testing. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9604-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reliability analysis of the cross-over experiment comparing Cronbach's alpha per year-test group for tests A and B under FS and NR conditions, tested with the Hakstian and Whalen K-sample test. The article reports "mean year-test group Cronbach’s alphas were 0.62 and 0.74", with FS higher in nine of ten comparisons (one-sided p = 0.011, Sign Test).

> "NR-scores showed considerably lower reliabilities than FS-scores (mean year-test group Cronbach’s alphas were 0.62 and 0.74, respectively)"
