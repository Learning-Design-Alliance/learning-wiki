## Evidence

### Rodriguez 2003

Michael C. Rodriguez. (2003). Construct Equivalence of Multiple-Choice and Constructed-Response Items: A Random Effects Synthesis of Correlations. Journal of Educational Measurement, 40(2). https://www.jstor.org/stable/1435344

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Fixed effects moderator analysis of the non-stem-equivalent correlations. The article reports content-equivalent items averaged 0.87 versus 0.82, a difference the discussion calls "slight but nonsignificant"; essay and short-answer sets both averaged 0.82.

> "Twelve correlations are content equivalent with a mean corrected correlation of 0.87 while 23 correlations are not content equivalent with a mean corrected correlation of 0.82. Of the non-content equivalent items, with 15 correlations based on MC and essay CR formats and 8 on typical short answer CR formats, both sets have mean corrected correlations of 0.82."
