## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Fuhrmann Delia 2019

Fuhrmann Delia, Schweizer Susanne, Leung Jovita, Griffin Cait, Blakemore Sarah-Jayne. (2019). The neurocognitive correlates of academic diligence in adolescent girls. Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1080/17588928.2018.1504762

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural ROI analysis in the same sample of 40 girls; gray matter volumes in IFG, DS and VS showed no significant diligence association. A second entry-level finding from the same paper: ROI functional analyses found no diligence effects for DS or VS, and PPI connectivity from the IFG to DS and VS was not associated with diligence (Table 4).

> "Gray matter volumes did not significantly correlate with diligence for any of our three ROIs (IFG: χ2(1) = 0.10, p = .754; DS: χ2(1) = 0.80, p = .370; VS: χ2(1) = 0.30, p = .584)."
