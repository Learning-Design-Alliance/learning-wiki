## Evidence

### Christodoulou JA 2014

Christodoulou JA, Del Tufo SN, Lymberis J, Saxler PK, Ghosh SS, et al. (2014) Brain Bases of Reading Fluency in Typical Reading and Impaired Fluency in Dyslexia. PLoS ONE 9(7): e100552. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0100552

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlation analysis across participants in both groups related in-scanner performance to the WJ Reading Fluency test. The article reports "significant positive correlations between Reading Fluency scores and accuracy" at Medium (r = .47) and Fast (r = .63) rates, plus negative RT correlations.

> "There were significant positive correlations between Reading Fluency scores and accuracy at the Medium and Fast rates, which did not show ceiling effects for accuracy: Medium (standard scores: r = .47, p, .05); Fast (standard scores: r = .63, p,.01)"

### Christodoulou JA 2014 (2)

Christodoulou JA, Del Tufo SN, Lymberis J, Saxler PK, Ghosh SS, et al. (2014) Brain Bases of Reading Fluency in Typical Reading and Impaired Fluency in Dyslexia. PLoS ONE 9(7): e100552. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0100552

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis across both groups related CTOPP Elision standard scores to in-scanner performance. The article reports significant correlations with reaction time at Medium and Slow rates and accuracy at Fast and Medium rates, with printed r values between .43 and .49.

> "Standard scores from a measure of phonological awareness (''Elision,'' CTOPP) showed significant correlations with in-scanner reaction time for Medium (r = 2.49, p,.05) and Slow ( r = 2.43, p,.05) rates, and accuracy for Fast ( r = .44, p,.05) and Medium ( r = .47, p,.05) rates"
