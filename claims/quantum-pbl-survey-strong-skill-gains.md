Post-course descriptive survey of ten students (six graduate, four undergraduate) in 'Quantum Mechanics for Engineers'; item-level means on five-point Likert items. Students reported gains in "scientific communication (4.36)" and confidence (4.18); no inferential statistics were reported.

> "Students reported that the project increased their motivation to learn quantum mechanics (4.00), made them more engaged than traditional homework assignments (4.09), and improved their confidence in discussing quantum mechanics concepts (4.18). They also reported gains in scientific communication (4.36)"
