Digital Promise reports that successful educator use of micro-credentials focuses on competency-based micro-credentials requiring a portfolio of evidence and a rigorous assessment and feedback process, educator autonomy in choosing which micro-credentials to pursue, incentives such as stipends and advancement pathways, and recognition toward licensure, recertification, and professional learning hours. → Rita Fennelly-Atkinson and April Williamson (Digital Promise) 2023

This is a descriptive synthesis slide from the organization's implementation experience, citing its own reports (Digital Promise 2016, 2017; Lopez & Younge 2021). It states that educator adoption improves when "educator autonomy and agency in which MCs they pursue" is honored and when incentives and formal recognition are offered. No study design, sample, or effect size is printed.
