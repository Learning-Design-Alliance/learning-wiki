Simon Fauvel, Han Yu. (2015). A Survey on Artificial Intelligence and Data Mining for MOOCs. Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862)

The survey reports, citing study [11], that demographics alone are insufficient for predicting achievement in MOOCs. It notes that "learners’ background should be coupled with the activities learners engage in on the platform", and that prior competence in the subject matter helps explain and predict achievement.

> "[11] showed that looking at learners’ demographics alone is not sufficient, as this provides very limited explanatory and predictive information about achievement in MOOC."
