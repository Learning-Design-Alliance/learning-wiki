## Evidence

### Hundertmark Jan 2019

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Salivary cortisol net values (teaching minus HLM-modelled rest-day reference) analysed with RM-ANOVAs. The article reports absolute cortisol levels moderate, ranging from 0.15 to 12.86 ng/ml, M = 2.13 (SD = 1.72), and a significant Type main effect, F = 11.06.

> "Tutors’ overall HPA axis activation (in comparison to individual rest day value) by teaching was low and differed significantly between AaLplus tutors, M = −.43 (SD = 1.82), and sonography tutors, M = 0.73 (SD = 1.68)"

### Hundertmark Jan 2019 (2)

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Continuous HRV recording (Polar V800, RMSSD as primary measure) during sessions and recovery, analysed with RM-ANOVAs. The article reports a total mean of M = 34.82 (SD ± 14.68), a significant Time*Type interaction, and higher sonography baseline RMSSD, F(1, 56) = 4.32, p = .04.

> "The RMSSD, as main parameter for HRV and thus parasympathetic activity, revealed moderate overall physiological stress that decreases over the measurement period"
