## Evidence

### Spinde 2025

Spinde, T., Wu, F., Gaissmaier, W., Demartini, G., & Giese, H. (2025). Enhancing Media Literacy: The Effectiveness of (Human) Annotations and Bias Visualizations on Bias Detection. [doi:10.1016/j.ipm.2025.104244](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2025.104244)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Study 2 ANOVA on test-article F1 scores: highlighting politicized phrases with a dotted underline and political-direction information in an Analysis Bar reduced sentence-level detection with a printed effect size of η2part = 0.005; the article notes the political dimension "may even obscure the ability to detect biases."

> "However, F1-scores were decreased by politicized phrase labels (F(834,1) = 4.16, p = .042, η2part = 0.005; see Fig. 5)."
