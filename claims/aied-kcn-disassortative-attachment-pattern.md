Macro-level analysis of the association between modified average weighted nearest neighbor degree and node degree (Fig. 4). The article finds the majority of nodes have values above one, "indicating a tendency of connecting with nodes that have higher degrees," while hubs connect to lower-degree nodes — a hierarchical feature of the knowledge structure.

> "the nodes with a higher degree in the network (e.g. 'intelligent tutoring system') tend to have a value of average weighted nearest neighbor degree below one, suggesting a tendency of connecting with the nodes that have lower degrees."
