Related Claims¶

  • Adolescents' physics and chemistry ability self-concept and task values from seventh to twelfth grade fall into seven distinct motivational trajectories rather than a single declining pattern — related
  • Adolescents in the Stable High motivation trajectory earned higher science grades, took more AP science courses, and reported higher STEM career aspirations than the other trajectory groups — related
  • Ninth grade marked a turning point where transitory motivation trajectories reversed direction, and these inconsistent trajectories were associated with lower AP enrollment and science performance — related
  • A majority of adolescents (about 63%) followed stable trajectories of physics and chemistry motivation across middle and high school — related
  • Adolescents' ability self-concept and task values in physics and chemistry changed in tandem over time, regardless of trajectory group — related
