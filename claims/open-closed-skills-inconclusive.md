## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The reviewed literature is inconclusive on whether open or closed skills are more, less or equally proficiently practiced through MS; some reviews find no significant difference while others contend MS suits unpredictable open skills. [→ Meer 2009](#meer-2009)

## Evidence

### Meer 2009

Meer, J.P. van & Theunissen, N.C.M. (2009). Prospective educational applications of mental simulation: A meta-review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-009-9097-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of the consulted reviews: Feltz et al. report "no significant difference to be found between open and closed skills", while Suinn contends MS is proficient for skills with unpredictable elements; a compromise holds MS more effective for simple closed tasks unless the subject is familiar with the complex task.

> "However, the investigated literature is inconclusive in determining whether open or closed skills are more, less or equally proficiently practiced through MS."
