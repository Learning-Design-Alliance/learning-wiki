# Low-scoring experimental-group students moved from 25% scoring below 25% at pre-test to none scoring below 25% at post-test, with scores reaching as much as 56.8%

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the experimental group, the 25% of students who scored below 25% on the pre-test scored significantly higher at post-test — as much as 56.8% — with none scoring below 25%, while the control group's 28.5% below-25% share remained unaltered.
