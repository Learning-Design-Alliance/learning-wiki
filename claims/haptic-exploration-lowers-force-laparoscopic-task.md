## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Medical students who manually explored the silicone model before practice applied significantly lower forces on all force parameters in the final trials than students who only looked at the model. [→ Postema Roelf R. 2020](#postema-roelf-r-2020)

## Evidence

### Postema Roelf R. 2020

Postema Roelf R., van Gastel Leonie A., Hardon Sem F., Bonjer H. Jaap, Horeman Tim. (2020). Haptic exploration improves performance of a laparoscopic training task. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-07898-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized experiment with 20 medical students block-randomized into a touch and a no-touch group of ten; the touch group manually explored the model for two minutes before ten task repetitions. Final-trial comparison showed "a significant reduction for all force parameters" in the touch group.

> "Looking at all four force parameters, values are lower and ranges are smaller in the Touch group. Statistical analysis values show a significant reduction for all force parameters (Table 4)."
