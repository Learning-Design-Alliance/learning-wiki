## Evidence

### Boon Leong Lan 2022

Boon Leong Lan, Pooi Mee Lim and Patrick W. C. Ho. (2022). A modified peer instruction protocol: peer versus teacher's instruction. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2201.10804](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2201.10804)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison across five semesters (2020-2021) of a first-year Physics for Engineering course: TI in two semesters (121 students), PI in three (137 students), with the same Q1/Q2 Lenz's law pair. The paper states the gain is "a rough measure of the effectiveness of a method of instruction".

> "The normalized gain (actual gain divided by maximum possible gain) is 0.18 and 0.37 for PI (Semester C & D & E) and TI (Semester A & B), respectively (Table 1). Based on this rough measure, TI is twice as effective as PI"
