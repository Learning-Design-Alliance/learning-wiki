Correlational comparison of partial rates from single students in the qualitative analysis of 25 curricular proposals (Figure 2); the authors report "r = 0.36" linking learning-problem contextualization with transversal-element use, an observational correlation with no inferential test printed.

> "the correlation coefficient between the two set of measures in upper left and lower panels is r = 0.36, meaning a corresponding correlation probability above 75%."
