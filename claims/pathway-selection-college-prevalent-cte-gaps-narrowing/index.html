The Landscape of Career Readiness Opportunities in Chicago Public Schools: Strengthening Career and Technical Education and Establishing Career-Connected Learning for All. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of LPS pathway-selection data for graduating classes of 2020 through 2024. The report compares CTE and non-CTE students' selections descriptively; no test statistics or effect sizes are printed.

"College (two-year or four-year) was the most prevalent selection among CTE and non-CTE students alike. CTE students were less likely to select four-year college and more likely to select two-year than non-CTE students, but these diﬀerences narrowed between the graduating classes of 2020 and 2024."
