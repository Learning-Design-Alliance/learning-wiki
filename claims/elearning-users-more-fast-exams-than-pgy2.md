## Evidence

### Lien Wan-Ching 2022

Lien Wan-Ching, Lin Phone, Chang Chih-Heng, Wu Meng-Che, Wu Cheng-Yi. (2022). The effect of e-learning on point-of-care ultrasound education in novices. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2022.2152522

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective observational study at a Taiwanese ED; 170 PGY-1 (736 FAST exams) and 53 PGY-2 (134 exams) collected on day 30. The abstract/results report that "the 70 e-learning users performed more FAST examinations than the PGY-2" with p = 0.0004.

> "the 70 e-learning users performed more FAST examinations than the PGY-2 (median [IQR], 4 [2–6] vs. 2 [1–3], p = 0.0004)"
