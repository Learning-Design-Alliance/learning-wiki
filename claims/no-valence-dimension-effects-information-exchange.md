# The approach/avoidance valence dimension shows no effects on information giving or information utilization

> **Currency** · newest study 2007 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither main effects of valence nor goal x valence interactions were found for openness or utilization of received information. [→ Poortvliet 2007](#poortvliet-2007)

## Evidence

### Poortvliet 2007

Poortvliet, P. M., Janssen, O., Van Yperen, N. W., & Van de Vliert, E. (2007). Achievement Goals and Interpersonal Behavior: How Mastery and Performance Goals Shape Information Exchange. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167207305536

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Study 2 (N = 125) manipulated approach and avoidance versions of mastery and performance goals; the printed ANOVA results showed no main effect of valence (F(1, 96) = .03, ns for openness; F(1, 96) = 1.56, ns for utilization) and no interactions, consistent with the authors' conclusion quote.

> "we did not find indications that distinguishing between approach and avoidance goals leads to different results in terms of openness in information giving and utilization of received information."
