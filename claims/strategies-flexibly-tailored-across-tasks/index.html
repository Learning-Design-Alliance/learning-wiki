Evidence¶

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran 2023¶

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran. (2023). Stable, flexible, common, and distinct behaviors support rule-based and information-integration category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00163-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Alluvial-plot comparison of final-block strategies across tasks in both experiments: 78% (Exp 1) and 65% (Exp 2) used different strategy subclasses; 56% and 49% differed at the general-class level. Participants "tended to use different strategies across RB and II tasks".

"In both experiments, we found that strategies were generally unrelated across the two tasks, whether considering the specific strategy subclass (e.g., Integration, 2D-Simple, 2D-Complex, 1D-Temporal, 1D-Spectral, Random; Experiment 1: 78% different; Experiment 2: 65% different)"

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran 2023 (2)¶

Casey L. Roark and Bharath Chandrasekaran. (2023). Stable, flexible, common, and distinct behaviors support rule-based and information-integration category learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00163-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Carryover analysis comparing final-block strategy of the first task with first-block strategy of the second task: 53% (Exp 1) and 43% (Exp 2) used a different general strategy, closely matching a control comparison (55% and 56%), leading the authors to conclude strategies were not directly transferred.

"In Experiment 1, 53% of participants used a different general strategy (Integration, 2D, 1D, Random) and 67% of participants used a different specific strategy subclass"
