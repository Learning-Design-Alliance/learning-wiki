Evidence¶

Donnelly 2011¶

Donnelly, D., McGarr, O. and O'Reilly, J. (2011). A Framework for Teachers' Integration of ICT Into Their Classroom Practice. Computers and Education;57(2), 1469-1483. https://hdl.handle.net/10344/2340

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Findings from the follow-on study regarding teachers who declined participation. The article reports the cited reasons were "the organisational difficulties of getting to use the computer room, busy schedules and that the VCL had not enough problems catered to the Irish curriculum", linking the first two to first-order barriers and the curriculum comment to second-order barriers.
