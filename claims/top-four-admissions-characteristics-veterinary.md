## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a paired-comparison survey of 1,312 Ontario veterinary stakeholders, the top-ranked characteristics were ethical behaviour, sound judgment, communication, and critical and creative thinking. [→ Conlon Peter 2012](#conlon-peter-2012)

## Evidence

### Conlon Peter 2012

Conlon Peter, Hecker Kent, Sabatini Susan. (2012). What should we be selecting for? A systematic approach for determining which personal characteristics to assess for during admissions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-105

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Paired-comparison survey sent to all licensed Ontario veterinarians, OVC students, and OVC faculty, interns and residents; 1,312 responded (27.86% response rate). Weighted z-scores from an 11 by 11 matrix produced the rank order, with "the top four characteristics were ethical behaviour, sound judgment, communication, and critical and creative thinking."

> "From the 1,312 responders, the top four characteristics were ethical behaviour, sound judgment, communication, and critical and creative thinking."
