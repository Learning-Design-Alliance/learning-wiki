## Evidence

### Kickert R 2018

Kickert R, Stegers‐Jager KM, Meeuwisse M, Prinzie P, Arends LR. (2018). The role of the assessment policy in the relation between learning and performance. Medical Education 52(3):324–335. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13487

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multi-group structural equation modelling with stepwise equality constraints on factor loadings and structural paths; the final constrained model showed good fit (CFI = 0.947, SRMR = 0.048, RMSEA = 0.044) and the smallest AIC. The article reports the "structural model" was "not significantly different across groups".

> "Additionally, the structural model (i.e. Model 3 versus Model 2) was not significantly different across groups, indicating that the structural relationships were similar in the old and new assessment policies."
