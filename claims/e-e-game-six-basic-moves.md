Bahar Modir, Paul W. Irving, Steven F. Wolf, and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2013). Learning about the Energy of a Hurricane System through an Estimation Epistemic Game. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.3243](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.3243)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-study analysis of a single talk-aloud clinical interview in which an upper-division physics major estimated the total energy of a hurricane. The authors identified the six-move structure from micro-genetic analysis of the student's discourse, stating "There are six basic moves in this game".

> "There are six basic moves in this game (see Fig. 1.): 1. problematize, 2. propose method, 3. what to remember, 4. see if parts are enough, 5. pure calculations and 6. evaluations."
