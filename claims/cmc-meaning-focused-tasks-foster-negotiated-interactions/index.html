Evidence¶

Sarré 2011¶

Sarré, C. (2011). Computer-mediated negotiated interactions: how is meaning negotiated in discussion boards, text chat and videoconferencing? In S. Thouësny & L. Bradley (Eds.), Second language teaching and learning with technology: views of emergent researchers (pp. 189-210). Dublin: Research-publishing.net. https://dx.doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2011.9781908416001

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Analysis of interactions from 12 groups of four French NNS biology students completing five ESP scenarios online (24 chat logs, 24 discussion board files, 24 video files) shows that "the completion of meaning-focused tasks through computer-mediated communication does foster negotiated interactions" in all three modes.

"The very first conclusion that can be drawn from the analysis of the data collected is the fact that the completion of meaning-focused tasks through computer-mediated communication does foster negotiated interactions."
