Nagaoka 2020¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2020). The Educational Attainment of Chicago Public Schools Students: 2019. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/2019/#/milestones

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Figure 5 takeaway from this descriptive report on 2019 CPS attainment. The PAI is a hypothetical projection multiplying high school graduation, immediate enrollment, and six-year completion rates, not a measure of actual attainment for any one cohort. The report notes "Nationally, 50 percent of adults between 25 and 34 years old hold any college degree."

"This means that if the current high school graduation, college enrollment, and program completion rates do not change over the next 10 years, 27 percent of current CPS ninth-graders will complete any college credential, including a bachelor's degree, associate degree, or certificate, within 10 years."
