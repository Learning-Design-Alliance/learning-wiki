# Vocabulary-book revision is inefficient because most recorded items are already known, so students wade through pages to find the few unknown words

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r1` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Revising from a full vocabulary book is inefficient because the student must plough through many pages of already-known items to reach the roughly 100-150 words he does not know. [→ Whiteley 1971](#whiteley-1971)

## Evidence

### Whiteley 1971

Whiteley, Darryl. (1971). Foreign Language Vocabulary Learning. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED062899

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The author's illustrative argument about redundancy: a book of about 1,000 items may contain only 100-150 unknown words, yet revision requires working through all pages; copying unknown words into a separate book risks losing items that may later prove valuable.

> "However, to revise and find, let us say the 100 - 150 words he does not know he student must plouda through ten tiles that number."
