## Evidence

### Shewach 2019

Shewach, O. R., Sackett, P. R., & Quint, S. (2019). Stereotype Threat Effects in Settings With Features Likely Versus Unlikely in Operational Test Settings: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Applied Psychology, 104(12), 1514–1534. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/apl0000420

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analytic moderator test of ability test metric. The article notes accuracy scoring "would not be used in operational settings, as it is readily coached" and excludes accuracy studies from the focal sample.

> "Studies using total number correct found a threat effect of d ⫽ ⫺.30 (k ⫽ 169, N ⫽ 9,929, SD␦ ⫽ .38), whereas studies using test accuracy found a stronger threat effect of d ⫽ ⫺.50 (k ⫽ 12, N ⫽ 507, SD␦ ⫽ .18), which was significant at an alpha of .05, t(179) ⫽ 2.09, p ⫽ .02."
