### Alhassan Robert Kaba 2021

Alhassan Robert Kaba, Ayanore Martin Amogre, Diekuu John-Bosco, Prempeh Emmanuel B. A., Donkor Ernestina Safoa. (2021). Leveraging e-Learning technology to enhance pre-service training for healthcare trainees in Ghana: evidence from a pilot project and pointers to policy reforms. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate probit regression (bootstrap SE, 50 replications, 344 observations) on determinants of e-Learning utilization among trainees. "positive association with high likelihood of using the e-Learning platform to write IAs" held for e-Learning-savvy students across three utilization indicators.

> "Being e-Learning savvy was also found to have positive association with high likelihood of using the e-Learning platform to write IAs [Coef.=1.39 (95 % CI=(0.98 1.81), p < 0.05), download learning materials [Coef.=0.92 (95 % CI=(0.56 1.28), p < 0.05) and uploading class assignments ([Coef.=0.94 (95 % CI=(0.48 1.40), p < 0.05])"
