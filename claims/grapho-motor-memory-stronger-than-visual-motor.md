## Evidence

### Patterson 1909

Patterson, T. L. (1909). Pedagogical Suggestions from Memory Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://books.google.com

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

About 50 tests at Anson Academy in 1909, aged thirteen to nineteen, used Meyerhardt's method with figures written by odd-numbered subjects and looked at by even-numbered subjects, recalled after 40 seconds. Results of 16 subjects were discarded for misunderstanding directions; the remaining 34 "harmonized with those of Meyerhardt."

> "The results from 29 subjects or 85.3 per cent, showed this increase in grapho-motor memory over the visual-motor memory, while the results from the other five subjects showed little difference in their grapho-motor and visual-motor memory capacities."
