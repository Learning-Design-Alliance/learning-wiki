## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Han students in 2019 using Mathematica scored significantly higher (p=0.029<0.05) on the six-question TISE quiz than Han students in 2018 without Mathematica, with an effect size of 0.6. [→ Guangtian Zhu 2024](#guangtian-zhu-2024)

## Evidence

### Guangtian Zhu 2024

Guangtian Zhu, Jing Hu, Chun Du. (2024). Assisting Tibetan Students in Learning Quantum Mechanics via Mathematica. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2406.18810](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2406.18810)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of the 26 Han students in the 2019 Mathematica-assisted course (mean 1.54) with 24 Han students in the 2018 traditional course (mean 0.92) on the same six-question quiz. The article reports the comparison was "significantly better (p=0.029<0.05)" with "effect size is 0.6".

> "The Han students in 2019 who participated in the Mathematica -assisted quantum mechanics course performed significantly better (p=0.029<0.05) in the conceptual quiz than the Han students in 2018. The effect size is 0.6."
