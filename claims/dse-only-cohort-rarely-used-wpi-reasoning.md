# Graduate students who did not work on the MZI tutorial almost never used WPI reasoning on the DSE polarizer questions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Of the 14 graduate students in the DSE only cohort, only one used WPI reasoning, and only on one question, contrasting with frequent WPI use in the MZI→DSE cohort. [→ Alexandru Maries 2020](#alexandru-maries-2020)

## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2020

Alexandru Maries, Ryan Sayer and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Can students apply the concept of "which-path" information learned in the context of Mach Zehnder Interferometer to the double-slit experiment?. [doi:10.1119/10.0001357](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0001357)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control comparison (RQ2.a): the DSE only cohort completed the DSE pretest without working through the MZI tutorial, so they had no opportunity to learn WPI reasoning beforehand; the article reads this contrast as evidence of spontaneous transfer by the tutorial cohort.

> "By comparison, out of the 14 graduate students from the DSE only cohort, only one graduate student used WPI reasoning in only one question."
