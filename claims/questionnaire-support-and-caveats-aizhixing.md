## Evidence

### Zhang Yuejiao 2025

Zhang Yuejiao, Li Xiaolan, Wei Xi, Xiao Jiexuan, Zhang Qi, Mai Sui. (2025). A pilot study on the application of a digital dental teaching evaluation system in Class II cavity preparation teaching. BMC Oral Health 25, 372. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05695-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Anonymous 10-item five-point Likert questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.80) completed by all 18 participants via the Wenjuanxing platform; 100% agreement items included that Aizhixing can improve students' theoretical knowledge and cavity preparation level. Perceptions are self-reported; no effect size is printed.

> "The findings indicated that participants believed the utilization of Aizhixing could improve the clinicians' cavity preparation skills to a significant degree. Furthermore, they emphasized the importance of integrating this tool into education activities"
