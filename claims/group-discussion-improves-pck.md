## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Physics Instructors and Teaching Assistants Using the Force Concept Inventory. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. Originally published 1995. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within the TA training course, 25 graduate students first did the PCK task individually (average 6.01, std. dev. 0.78) and then repeated it in 12 groups of two or three (average 6.59, std. dev. 0.79). The article reports the group performance was significantly better.

> "A t-test shows that the group performance is better than the individual performance (p = 0.040)"

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013 (2)

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Physics Instructors and Teaching Assistants Using the Force Concept Inventory. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. Originally published 1995. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7177)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of 98 instances in which two or three graduate students who did not all select the same incorrect answer individually converged to one of their answers in the group task. In 73 instances (74%) they converged to the answer more common among introductory students by 5% or more.

> "In 73 of those instances (74%) the graduate students converged to the 'better' option (i.e., the more common incorrect answer choice of introductory students by 5% or more)"
