Evidence¶

Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies 2012¶

Peer-Assisted Learning/Literacy Strategies. (2012). WWC Intervention Report, Beginning Reading. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/364

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Study meeting WWC evidence standards with reservations: 130 first graders in five Florida schools, teacher random assignment but non-random student selection. Appendix C.3 shows a mean difference of 2.95, effect size 0.44, and improvement index +17, with p = 0.07 — "not statistically significant."

"The WWC-calculated effect was not statistically significant, but it was large enough to be a substantively important positive ef fect (i.e., an effect size of at least 0.25)."
