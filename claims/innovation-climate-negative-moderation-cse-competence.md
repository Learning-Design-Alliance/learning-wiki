PROCESS Model 1 moderation analysis in the same cross-sectional sample of 233 nurses; the unadjusted interaction was B = −0.139 (p < 0.001) and the adjusted model explained R² = 0.3876. The negative interaction persisted after covariate adjustment.

> "Perceived climate for innovation also moderated the association between creative self‐efficacy and perceived clinical competence after adjustment (interaction B = −0.1672, SE = 0.0447, 95% CI [−0.2552, −0.0792]). This positive association became weaker as perceived climate for innovation increased."
