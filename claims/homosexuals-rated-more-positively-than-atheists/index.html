Evidence¶

Maurer and Keim 2018¶

Trent W. Maurer, Cassidy Keim. (2018). Teaching about prejudice with a Bogardus Social Distance Scale activity: Replication and extension. IJ-SoTL, Vol. 12, No. 1, Art. 7. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2018.120107

q3 · i2 · causal · r1

Between-subjects Target effect in the BSDS MANOVA (N = 240): F(1, 234) = 19.29, p < .001, printed effect size partial etasquared = 0.08; the article reports "BSDS scores for homosexuals were lower than scores for atheists, supporting Hypothesis 3a". For thermometers, Own showed partial eta-squared = 0.14 and University 0.03.

"Significant between-subjects effects emerged for Condition, F (2, 234) = 11.29, p < .001, partial eta-squared = 0.09, and Target, F (1, 234) = 19.29, p < .001, partial etasquared = 0.08, but not the Condition x Target interaction, F (2, 234) = 1.62, ns."

Related Claims¶
