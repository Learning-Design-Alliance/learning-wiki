### Yu Limin 2026

Yu Limin, Chen Zhe, Qin Zeyu. (2026). A novel Z-number based multi-stage assessment framework for problem-based learning in practical courses. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349114

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article describes this as a methodological design choice applied in Stage 1 of the score determination phase, using instructor group Z-number assessments of criteria importance, with a balance coefficient λ typically set to 0.5.

> "a hybrid weighting method combining Entropy and CRITIC is adopted. In this hybrid scheme, the Entropy component reflects the degree of dispersion in the evaluation information, while the CRITIC method is used to determine the weight of each criterion based on contrast intensity and inter-criteria conflict."
