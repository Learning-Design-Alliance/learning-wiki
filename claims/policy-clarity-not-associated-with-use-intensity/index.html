The second pooled OLS model (N = 1254) explained 42.1% of variance in current AI-use intensity (R² = 0.421); the article reports that "Policy clarity was not associated with current AI-use intensity after adjustment for the other variables".

"Perceived usefulness had the strongest positive association ( 𝛽 = 0.322), followed by competence ( 𝛽 = 0.268), trust ( 𝛽 = 0.106), and experience ( 𝛽 = 0.099). Policy clarity was not associated with current AI-use intensity after adjustment for the other variables ( 𝛽 = -0.002, p = 0.947)."
