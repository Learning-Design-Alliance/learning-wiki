## Evidence

### Jang Yujin 2021

Jang Yujin, Suh Youngmeen. (2021). Longitudinal Effects of Parental Academic Support on Academic Achievement in Korea: Will You Be a Companion or a Manager in Your Children’s Academic Support? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182010823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal structural equation analysis (AMOS 21, maximum likelihood) of the nationally representative KELS 2013 panel (n = 6836, wave 1 fifth grade to wave 4 middle school second grade). Table 4 prints companion ⇒ self-regulation standardized 0.22 and companion ⇒ academic achievement standardized 0.12, both p < 0.001, i.e. "a positive effect" as the text states. No standardized effect-size statistic of the d/r family is printed for these paths.

> "PAS as a companion had a positive effect on adolescents’ self-regulation and academic achievements and a negative effect on their amotivation."

### Jang Yujin 2021 (2)

Jang Yujin, Suh Youngmeen. (2021). Longitudinal Effects of Parental Academic Support on Academic Achievement in Korea: Will You Be a Companion or a Manager in Your Children’s Academic Support? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182010823

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same SEM on the KELS panel: the path PAS as companion ⇒ amotivation is printed in Table 4 as −0.25 (S.E. 0.03), standardized −0.23, p < 0.001, supporting "a negative effect on their amotivation". No d/r-family effect size is printed for this path.

> "PAS as a companion had a positive effect on adolescents’ self-regulation and academic achievements and a negative effect on their amotivation."
