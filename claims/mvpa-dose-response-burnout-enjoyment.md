## Evidence

### Jussila Juuso J 2023

Jussila Juuso J, Pulakka Anna, Halonen Jaana I, Salo Paula, Allaouat Sara, Mikkonen Santtu, Lanki Timo. (2023). Are active school transport and leisure-time physical activity associated with performance and wellbeing at secondary school? A population-based study. The European Journal of Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1093/eurpub/ckad128

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted logistic regression in the population-based cohort found dose–response associations "both in a dose–response manner" for lower burnout (24–40% lower odds) and higher enjoyment (35–90% higher odds) across leisure-time MVPA levels from 30 min to 7 h or more per week.

> "Leisure-time MVPA, starting from '30 min' to '7 h or more' a week, was associated with lower odds of school burnout (from 24% to 40%) and higher odds of high school enjoyment (from 35% to 90%) compared with inactivity—both in a dose–response manner (Supplementary figure S2)."
