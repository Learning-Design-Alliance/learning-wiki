### Gimeno A. 2026

Gimeno A., Alberola‐Zorrilla P., Blasco‐Serra A., Sánchez‐Zuriaga D., Zaragozá R. (2026). Online anatomical images are an effective resource to improve knowledge acquisition and practical exam performance in gross anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70254

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous satisfaction survey of 54 students (60% of quiz participants; 30% of all intervention students) on a 5-point Likert scale; percentages are descriptive self-reports with no inferential test.

> "A large majority of students (96.3%) agreed or strongly agreed that the images and quizzes were helpful for retaining anatomical information, while 90.8% found them beneficial for reinforcing practical content and 98.1% believed they were useful in preparing for the practical assessment."
