# In Texas, students of color are more likely than their peers to attend schools with high percentages of novice and uncertified teachers

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 34% of Black students versus 24% of non-Black students attend Texas schools where at least 20% of teachers are novices; disparities also appear for Latino students and for uncertified teachers. [→ The Education Trust 2022](#the-education-trust-2022)

## Evidence

### The Education Trust 2022

The Education Trust. (2022). Texas Educator Diversity State Profile. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of Texas 2018-19 school-level data comparing schools serving the most and fewest Black and Latino students finds "Percent of Students in schools with High (≥20%) percentages of Novice Teachers" of 34% for Black versus 24% for non-Black students, and 29% versus 22% for Latino students; high-uncertifier (≥10%) figures are 11% vs. 6% and 8% vs. 6%.
