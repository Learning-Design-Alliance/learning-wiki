Nele Claes 2024¶

Nele Claes, Annique Smeding, Arnaud Carré, Nicolas Sommet. (2024). The social class test gap: A worldwide investigation of the role of academic anxiety and income inequality in standardized test score disparities. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(6), 871-888. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000881

q2 · i0 · associational · r3

Multilevel interaction models on PISA 2012 and 2015 data with family economic capital. The Study 2a interaction was "only significant when anxiety was included in the model, β = 0.01 [0.0, 0.02], p = .001"; simple slopes showed a higher economically based gap in more unequal countries in both studies (Figure 2, lower panel).

"analyses revealed that the higher income inequality, the stronger the economically based social class test gap, Study 2a: β = 0.01 [-0.01, 0.02], p = .159, Study 2b: β = 0.02 [0.00, 0.03], p = .007"
