## Evidence

### Corvetto Marcia A. 2017

Corvetto Marcia A., Fuentes Carlos, Araneda Andrea, Achurra Pablo, Miranda Pablo, Viviani Paola, Altermatt Fernando R. (2017). Validation of the imperial college surgical assessment device for spinal anesthesia. BMC Anesthesiology. [doi:10.1186/s12871-017-0422-3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-017-0422-3)

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Concurrent validity analysis correlating ICSAD dexterity measures with blinded observational ratings in the same 30-participant simulation study. The article reports a printed effect size r = −0.46 (p = 0.012) — "a Spearman correlation coefficient of −0,46 (p = 0.012)" — which the authors interpret as a moderate inverse association between the two tools.

> "Spearman correlation coefficient between the total path length measured with ICSAD and the validated global rating scale scores demonstrated a moderate negative linear correlation, with a Spearman correlation coefficient of −0,46 (p = 0.012)."
