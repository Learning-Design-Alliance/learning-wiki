# Modeling, when analyzed as a separate moderator, shows no statistically significant effect on DTT implementation fidelity

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The moderation effect of modeling was 1.35 percentage points and not statistically significant (p = .36). [→ Joelle Fingerhut 2022](#joelle-fingerhut-2022)

## Evidence

### Joelle Fingerhut 2022

Joelle Fingerhut, & Mariola Moeyaert. (2022). Training Individuals to Implement Discrete Trials with Fidelity: A Meta-Analysis. Focus on Autism and Other Developmental Disabilities, 37(4), 239–250. https://doi.org/10.1177/10883576221081076

`q2 · i?`

Model 2 of the HLM meta-analysis with modeling as moderator. The moderation effect was "1.35%", γ̂200 = 1.35, t(3692) = 0.91, p =.36; modeling participants averaged 90.59% fidelity. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "This was not statistically significant, meaning that there is no difference in DTT implementation average for those who did and did not receive modeling as an intervention."
