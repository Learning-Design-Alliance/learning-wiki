## Evidence

### de Vries Anna H. 2015

de Vries Anna H., van Luijk Scheltus J., Scherpbier Albert J. J. A., Hendrikx Ad J. M., Koldewijn Evert L., Wagner Cordula, Schout Barbara M. A. (2015). High acceptability of a newly developed urological practical skills training program. BMC Urology 15:93. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12894-015-0084-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Synthesis of the semi-structured focus group interviews (39 residents, 15 program directors) on design characteristics in relation to D-UPS acceptability; the interviews named "structured scheduling, the use of peer teaching and high fidelity models". Qualitative finding; no effect size.

> "In summary, according to residents and program directors, the design characteristics that could increase the acceptability of urological practical skills training programs were structured scheduling, the use of peer teaching and high fidelity models."
