Systematic review of 20 studies covering 18 interventions, all meeting WWC 5.0 standards with or without reservations, reports that only two interventions had strong evidence and one moderate evidence; "The remaining 12 interventions had uncertain evidence of effects."

"Three additional interventions—LetterWorks, Lexia Core5 Reading, and Seeing Stars—had, at most, promising evidence."
