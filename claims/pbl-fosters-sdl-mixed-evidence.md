# Evidence that PBL fosters SDL is mixed across studies

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Several reviewed studies support PBL fostering SDL and SRL, but Lloyd-Jones and Hak found no evidence and group-size results were nuanced. [→ Loyens 2008](#loyens-2008)

## Evidence

### Loyens 2008

Loyens, S. M. M., Magda, J., & Rikers, R. M. J. P. (2008). Self-Directed Learning in Problem-Based Learning and its Relationships with Self-Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-008-9082-7

`q2 · i?`

Narrative review conclusion weighing studies for and against PBL fostering SDL/SRL. The review states "mixed results were found", naming supporting studies and noting Lloyd-Jones and Hak found no evidence that PBL fosters SDL. No pooled effect size is reported.

> "With respect to the question whether PBL fosters SDL and SRL, mixed results were found. Some studies gave evidence that PBL fosters SDL (Blumberg 2000; Hmelo and Lin 2000; Kivela and Kivela 2005) and SRL (Sungur and Tekkaya 2006)"
