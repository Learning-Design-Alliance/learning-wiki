### Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2021

Dina Zohrabi Alaee, Micah K. Campbell, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2021). Impact of virtual REU experiences on students' psychosocial gains during the COVID-19 pandemic. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Self-report ratings from interview six of the longitudinal remote REU study, where mentees rated project confidence on a scale with zero as not at all confident and 10 as very confident; three other mentees rated between 5-8.

> "Within their current REU project, seven out of ten mentees felt very confident around achieving their project's goals and rated between 8-10."

### Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2021 (2)

Dina Zohrabi Alaee, Micah K. Campbell, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2021). Impact of virtual REU experiences on students' psychosocial gains during the COVID-19 pandemic. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Helen's self-report in the longitudinal remote REU interview study about gaining confidence through independent remote work; the article also states there were no lower-level self-efficacy statements in interview nine, reporting no effect size, only coded qualitative levels.

> "I feel more conﬁdent since I’ve worked so independently (due to the remote REU format) but it’s deﬁnitely nice to have a mentor to direct you."
