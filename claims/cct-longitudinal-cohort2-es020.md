# Longitudinal comparison of Cohort 2 teachers' year 2 CCT classrooms against their own year 1 control classrooms shows significant improvement (ES = 0.20)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` With pretest and teaching experience controlled, Treatment 2.2 students scored about 1.1 points higher than their Control 2.1 counterparts (p = .009, ES = 0.20). [→ Irving 2016](#irving-2016)

## Evidence

### Irving 2016

Irving, K. E., Pape, S. J., Owens, D. T., Abrahamson, L., Silver, D., & Sanalan, V. A. (2016). Classroom Connectivity and Algebra 1 Achievement: A Three-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Computers in Mathematics and Science Teaching, 35(2), 131-151. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095322

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal HLM (model 4p) using Cohort 2 teachers as their own year 1 controls, with year 2 as treatment. The article reports the printed effect size ES = 0.20, γ20 = 1.08, p = .009; model 4np without pretest was also significant (γ10 = 1.35, p = .004, ES = 0.20).

> "when controlling for the pretest and years of teaching experience, the treatment group shows significant improvement (γ20 = 1.08, p = .009) with a moderate effect size (ES = 0.20)."
