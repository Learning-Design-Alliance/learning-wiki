## Evidence

### Zamecnik 2024

Zamecnik, A., Kovanović, V., Joksimović, S., Grossmann, G., Ladjal, D., Pardo, A. (2024). The perceptions of task cohesion in collaborative learning teams. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09424-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Performance analysis of mentor- and client-assessed deliverable scores (0.00–1.00) for 194 teams. Aligned rose from 0.84 to 0.89; Multifaceted fell from 0.84 to 0.77. Week 1 scores were approximately the same across groups; the Kruskal–Wallis test found significant Week 3 differences between the four perception groups.

> "The results indicated that teams who maintained similar cohesive per- ceptions increased their performance, and teams who maintained different cohesive per- ceptions decreased their performance."
