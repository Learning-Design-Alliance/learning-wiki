Study 2 (n = 20) collected concurrent think-aloud protocols and retrospective interviews while university students worked with science content containing conceptual discrepancies, "to further explore why discrepancies were noticed (or not) and how they were resolved." The themes above emerged from this qualitative analysis.

> "Results revealed that prior knowledge and epistemic self-efficacy in oneself as an evaluator of knowledge emerged as important themes to detecting and efficiently resolving discrepancies."
