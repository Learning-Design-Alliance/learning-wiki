## Evidence

### Süleyman Özdel 2025

Süleyman Özdel, Can Sarpkaya, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Examining the Role of LLM-Driven Interactions on Attention and Cognitive Engagement in Virtual Classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Visual scanpath analysis of eye-tracking data from the within-subjects VR experiment (Double-Slit topic); mean fixation duration, an indicator of cognitive processing demands, was significantly higher under Peer-QnA.

> "The mean fixation duration for the Peer-QnA condition is M = 233ms, SD = 103 ms, compared to M = 228 ms, SD= 101 ms for the Peer-NoQnA condition, indicating a statistically significant difference (p < .001)."

### Süleyman Özdel 2025 (2)

Süleyman Özdel, Can Sarpkaya, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Examining the Role of LLM-Driven Interactions on Attention and Cognitive Engagement in Virtual Classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.07377)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same scanpath analysis; saccade amplitudes, which indicate how broadly participants scanned the environment, were significantly lower under Peer-QnA, consistent with more concentrated focus on instructional content.

> "The mean saccade amplitude for the Peer-QnA condition is lower,M = 135.99◦, SD = 68 .74◦, compared to M = 137 .87◦, SD = 69.89◦ for the Peer-NoQnA condition, with a statistically significant difference ( p = .015, p < .05)."
