## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bernhard-Skala Christian 2026

Bernhard-Skala Christian, Steinweg Bernhard. (2026). Towards a standardized competency-profile for peer-to-peer-teachers in medical education - a systematic mapping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09997-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review content finding from the in-depth analysis of the 18 studies: medical subject content such as anatomy [8] or internal medicine [11] is explicitly part of training programs, and technical skills such as handling ultrasound devices [33] and interprofessional content such as structured patient handover according to ISBAR [7] also count as medical contents.

> "the studies show that the practices of qualifying P2P-teachers, either explicitly or implicitly integrate medical skills with teaching skills."
