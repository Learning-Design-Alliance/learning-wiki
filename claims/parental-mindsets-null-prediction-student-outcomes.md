### Lee 2025

Lee, H. J., Kim, S., & Bong, M. (2025). Social antecedents of children's mindsets, motivation and achievement in math: Investigating parental beliefs and perceived teacher beliefs. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 849–870. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12764

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null result from the same multilevel path analysis of 507 students in 28 classrooms: "parental mindsets did not significantly predict any of the student outcomes at the student-level". No effect sizes were printed for these null paths, so magnitude is untested.

> "parental mindsets did not significantly predict any of the student outcomes at the student‐level, in the presence of parental expectations and student perceptions of teacher beliefs."
