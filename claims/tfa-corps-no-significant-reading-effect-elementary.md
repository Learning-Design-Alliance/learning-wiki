## Evidence

### Turner H 2018

Turner H, Ncube M, Turner A, Boruch R, Ibekwe N. (2018). What are the effects of Teach For America on Math, English Language Arts, and Science outcomes of K–12 students in the USA? Campbell Systematic Reviews 2018:7. https://doi.org/10.4073/csr.2018.7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review's synthesis of included RCTs and QEDs comparing first- or second-year TFA corps members with non-TFA novice teachers on reading outcomes for PreK–grade 5 students. The review reports "no significant effect on reading"; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not tested.

> "There is no significant effect on reading from teaching by TFA corps members in their first or second year of teaching elementary‐grade students (PreK – grade 5) compared to non‐TFA teachers who are also in their first or second year of teaching elementary‐grade students."
