Evidence¶

Starkey-Perret 2017¶

Starkey-Perret, R., Belan, S., Lê Ngo, T. P., & Rialland, G. (2017). The effect of form-focussed pre-task activities on accuracy in L2 production in an ESP course in French higher education. In C. Sarré & S. Whyte (Eds), New developments in ESP teaching and learning research (pp. 171-195). Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2017.cssw2017.751

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of occurrences of targeted forms and their conformity to L2 norms in students' written productions for three macro-tasks, comparing completers and non-completers of pre-task micro-tasks. The article reports the frequency pattern with an explicit caution that differences were "often very slight" and non-significant.

"out of the 15 targeted forms presented in the results, 12 were found more frequently in the productions of students having done the pre-tasks. Caution must be used, however, when interpreting the results, as the difference in frequency of use of the targeted forms in the two groups is often very slight and none of the differences observed between groups is statistically significant."
