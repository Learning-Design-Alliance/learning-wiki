## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bjork 2016

Bjork, R. A., & Yue, C. L. (2016). Commentary: Is disfluency desirable? Metacognition Learning, 11, 133–137. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-016-9156-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Sidi, Ophir, and Ackerman had participants complete problem-solving tasks on computer or paper in fluent or disfluent font, measuring success rate (Experiments 1 and 2) and confidence (Experiment 2), as summarized in this commentary. Experiment 1 showed no effects; Experiment 2 showed the medium-by-fluency interaction.

> "in Experiment 2, they observed an interaction between medium and fluency: Success rates were higher on the computer for disfluent font than for fluent font, but higher on paper for fluent font than for disfluent font."
