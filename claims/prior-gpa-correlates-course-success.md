## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Prior GPA correlated with overall success in the class, with r = 0.63 overall, r = 0.62 in the hybrid section, and r = 0.68 in the traditional section (all p < 0.001). [→ Adams A. E. M. 2015](#adams-a-e-m-2015)

## Evidence

### Adams A. E. M. 2015

Adams A. E. M., Randall S., Traustadóttir T. (2015). A Tale of Two Sections: An Experiment to Compare the Effectiveness of a Hybrid versus a Traditional Lecture Format in Introductory Microbiology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0118

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis of prior GPA against final scores in the two-section randomized study. The article reports "the overall correlation for both sections combined was r = 0.63"; prior GPA was a significant predictor of final score within each section.

> "prior GPA was associated with overall success in the class: the overall correlation for both sections combined was r = 0.63 (p < 0.001); for the hybrid section, r = 0.62 (p < 0.001); and for the traditional section, r = 0.68 (p < 0.001)."
