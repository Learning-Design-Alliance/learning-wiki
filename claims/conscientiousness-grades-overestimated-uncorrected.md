## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Uncorrected self-reported Conscientiousness predicts higher grades, but the point estimate is much larger than the vignette-corrected estimate, indicating overestimation. [→ Coenen Johan 2021](#coenen-johan-2021)

## Evidence

### Coenen Johan 2021

Coenen Johan, Golsteyn Bart H. H., Stolp Tom, Tempelaar Dirk. (2021). Personality traits and academic performance: Correcting self-assessed traits with vignettes. PLoS ONE 16(3): e0248629. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0248629

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regressions with study-program fixed effects on grades of first-year Dutch economics students (Table 5: uncorrected Conscientiousness coefficient 0.303***, vignette-corrected baseline 0.165, not significant). The text states the point estimates are "much larger if the vignette is not used".

> "Comparing columns 2 and 3 in these tables reveals a similar pattern as we find for Risk Preference; the point estimates of the relationships between these personality traits and grades are much larger if the vignette is not used than if it is used."
