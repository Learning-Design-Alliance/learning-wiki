## Evidence

### Thiago Cabral 2018

Thiago Cabral, Natália B. Mota, Lucia Fraga, Mauro Copelli, Mark A. McDaniel and Sidarta Ribeiro. (2018). Post-class naps boost declarative learning in a naturalistic school setting. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0031-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cross-over classroom experiment: 24 fifth graders, weekly randomization over 6 weeks, retention tested 3-4 days after teacher-led lessons. Long naps beat the break control, "p = 0.01725/Cohen's d = 0.7962" in the nested analysis.

> "Contents followed by long naps were also better retained than contents followed by a break (Control 2) when assessed either by trial averages ( p = 0.0013/Cohen's d = 0.5344, Fig. 1b) or nested per subject ( p = 0.01725/Cohen's d = 0.7962, Fig. 1c)."
