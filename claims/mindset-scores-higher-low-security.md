## Evidence

### Rienzo et al. 2015

Cinzia Rienzo, Heather Rolfe and David Wilkinson (NIESR). (2015). Changing Mindsets: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581132

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Both trials measured pupils' theories of intelligence using three entity theory statements written by Dweck (1999), rated on a 6-point Likert scale. The report states pupils in both interventions "achieved higher scores on these measures than those in the control group" with low security.

> "Pupils involved in both interventions achieved higher scores on these measures than those in the control group, but the security of these findings is low and it is possible that they occurred by chance."
