## Evidence

### Jin Peipei 2025

Jin Peipei, Chen Jinyu, Wang Ping, Xie Yadian, Zhang Cen, Shen Liqiong, He Li, Ye Yanqing. (2025). Application of peer-assisted learning under the LSPPDM mode in resident clinical skill training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06813-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Test-control comparison of 61 residents (Grade 2023, peer-assisted LSPPDM) versus 68 residents (Grade 2022, traditional teaching) in the basic clinical skills course, reported in Results Table 2. The article reports the total score difference as statistically significant (P < 0.001) with "higher than those of the control residents"; no effect size is printed.

> "The statistical results revealed that the total scores of the three items, internal medicine average score, surgical average score and emergency mean score of the experimental residents were 90.62 ± 3.47, 89.49.9 ± 4.82, 88.09 ± 8.06 and 94.29 ± 4.07, respectively, which were higher than those of the control residents"
