Evidence¶

Aymee Alvarez-Rivero 2023¶

Aymee Alvarez-Rivero, Candice Odgers and Daniel Ansari. (2023). Elementary school teachers' perspectives about learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning 8:40. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00191-w

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey of elementary teachers reporting attendance changes during remote instruction; the country comparison was not significant (X2 = 2.97, p < 0.227, Cramer's V = 0.07), so no effect size is claimed for the overall drop beyond the reported proportion.

"more than 65% of participants indicated that attendance to class decreased during the 2020 – 2021 school year. Overall, there was no signi ﬁcant difference in teachers ' reports of attendance across countries ( X2 = 2.97, p < 0.227, df = 2, Cramer′sV = 0.07)"

Aymee Alvarez-Rivero 2023 (2)¶

Aymee Alvarez-Rivero, Candice Odgers and Daniel Ansari. (2023). Elementary school teachers' perspectives about learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning 8:40. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00191-w

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Country-stratified survey analysis: the SES–attendance association was significant in the US (τc = −0.19, p < 0.001) but not in Canada (τc = −0.03, p < 0.517); the article prints no standardised effect size such as d or r for this association.
