Reed 2026¶

Reed, J. M., Ferdig, R. E., Dodson, T. M., Gunstad, J., & Hughes, J. W. (2026). AI Literacy and Scenario-based Ethical Judgment About Generative AI Among Undergraduate Students. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09757-w

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Primary Spearman rank-order correlation in the RQ2 results, chosen because the outcome was discrete with a ceiling effect. The article reports "ρ = 0.234, p < .001, N = 531"; a Pearson sensitivity analysis produced r(529) = 0.213, p < .001.

"A small positive association was found between Non-Ethics GLAT scores and the number of ethical scenarios correctly classified, ρ = 0.234, p < .001, N = 531."
