### White 2020

White, C. L., Stump, E. M., Holmes, N. G., & Passante, G. (2020). Student evaluation of more or better experimental data in classical and quantum mechanics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.white](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.white)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of their interview data, in keeping with literature that students rarely understand what summary statistics represent. "some evidence of possible conflation between a reduction of the standard error and the standard deviation".

> "We found some evidence of possible conflation between a reduction of the standard error and the standard deviation, as it was not uncommon for participants to indicate that the histogram would get more narrow (precise) as a result of more data."
