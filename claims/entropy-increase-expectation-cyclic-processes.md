## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Over one-third of upper-level students answered that final entropy is greater than initial entropy in reversible cyclic processes, a significantly larger proportion than introductory students. [→ Brundage 2024](#brundage-2024)

## Evidence

### Brundage 2024

Brundage, M. J., Meltzer, D. E., & Singh, C. (2024). Investigating introductory and advanced students' difficulties with entropy and the second law of thermodynamics using a validated instrument. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020110](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020110)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Response-rate analysis of items 8 and 24 (reversible cyclic processes on PV diagrams) in the STPFaSL-Long validation data; correct-response rates ranged from 49% to 77%, with Int-alg students highest and upper-level students lowest on both items.

> "the proportion of upper-level students giving that same “greater than” response was significantly larger than among the introductory students, as over one-third of upper-level students gave that answer on both items."
