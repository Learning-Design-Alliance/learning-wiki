# After e-trainer-based training, board-certified pathologists' PD-L1 TC scoring of VENTANA SP263-stained NSCLC samples showed mean OPA of 85–95% across clinically relevant cut-offs

## Evidence

### Jasani Bharat 2020

Jasani Bharat, Bänfer Gudrun, Fish Rebecca, Waelput Wim, Sucaet Yves, Barker Craig, Whiteley Jessica L., Walker Jill, Hovelinck Rudy, Diezko Rolf. (2020). Evaluation of an online training tool for scoring programmed cell death ligand-1 (PD-L1) diagnostic tests for lung cancer. Diagnostic Pathology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13000-020-00953-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of competence-test scores from 59 trained pathologists scoring digital images of SP263-stained NSCLC samples against expert consensus reference scores. Mean OPA "ranged from 85% (95% confidence interval [CI]: 83–88%)" at ≥50% to 95% (95% CI: 93–96%) at ≥1%.

> "After training using the VENTANA PD-L1 (SP263) assay stained slides, mean OPA ranged from 85% (95% confidence interval [CI]: 83–88%) using the ≥50% TC cut-off, to 95% (95% CI: 93–96%) with the ≥1% TC cut-off (n = 59; Fig. 1a)."
