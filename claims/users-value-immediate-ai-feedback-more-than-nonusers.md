# Platform users were more convinced than non-users that immediate, imperfect AI feedback is more useful than delayed demonstrator feedback (79% versus 59%)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On the belief that immediate AI feedback, even with potential errors, beats delayed higher-quality demonstrator feedback, 79% of platform users agreed versus 59% of non-users; on the practice-question shortage the groups were similar (68% versus 62%). [→ Lili Chen 2026](#lili-chen-2026)

Survey comparison of two belief-based items between platform users (n = 19) and non-users (n = 34), cross-referenced against activity logs. The authors report "79% of users agreed, compared with only 59% of non-users"; the comparison is descriptive with no test reported.
