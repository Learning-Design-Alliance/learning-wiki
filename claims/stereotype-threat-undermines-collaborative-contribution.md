### stereotype-threat

Thevenard, P., Shell, A., Victor, L., Glazer, K., Fusco, J., Tyler, C., and Akanbi, K. (2025, May). Foundations of Collaboration. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/253

The primer defines stereotype threat as a psychological phenomenon in which "students fear their performance on a task and may confirm the stereotype of their identity's performance," citing Beilock et al. (2007) for the claim that this fear can interfere with working memory resources needed to think and learn.

> "Stress from threats to emotional safety can sometimes cause stereotype threat—a psychological phenomenon, where students fear their performance on a task and may confirm the stereotype of their identity's performance"
