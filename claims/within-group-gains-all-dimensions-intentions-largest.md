`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank within-group analysis of the intervention arm (n=57 at follow-up) in the RCT. The article reports "mean increases of 0.307, 0.456, 0.175, 0.325, 0.583, and 0.394" for HK, HB, HBV, HS, HI and HL respectively, all p < 0.05; no standardised effect sizes were printed.

> "In the intervention group, follow-up scores for HK, HB, HBV, HS, HI, and HL were significantly higher than baseline values, with mean increases of 0.307, 0.456, 0.175, 0.325, 0.583, and 0.394, respectively (all p < 0.05)."
