## Evidence

### Hursen 2016

Hursen, C., & Salaz, D. (2016). Investigating the effects of authentic childhood games in teaching English. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science. 11(2), 58-62. http://sproc.org/ojs/index.php/cjes/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative findings from observations and semi-structured interviews (15-20 minutes, recorded) with the same 6 teachers who evaluated each lesson with a researcher-prepared evaluation form over 6 weeks.

> "The teachers, whom observed the experimental group for 6 weeks, stated that authentic childhood games motivated and entertained students and also made them participate willingly during lessons."

### Hursen 2016 (2)

Hursen, C., & Salaz, D. (2016). Investigating the effects of authentic childhood games in teaching English. Cypriot Journal of Educational Science. 11(2), 58-62. http://sproc.org/ojs/index.php/cjes/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

From the same teacher interviews: teachers expressed they could have lack of classroom management and that "planning should be done before using games and followed during procedure".

> "On the other hand, most of the teachers also mentioned that a teacher has to have a good classroom management when using authentic childhood games."
