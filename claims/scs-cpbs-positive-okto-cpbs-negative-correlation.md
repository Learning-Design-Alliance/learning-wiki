## Evidence

### Cheung Chiyuen 2026

Cheung Chiyuen, Lu Hongfei, Tu Shaoqin, Chen Xinyuan, Feng Yi, Shao YiTing, Liu Yuyao, Mai Zhihui, Ai Hong, Chen Zheng. (2026). Development and application of a multidimensional instrument to evaluate competency discrepancies in orthodontic practice. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08237-2

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis among the three instrument components in the 434-participant survey. The printed OKTO–CPBS effect size is "rs = − 0.105" (p < 0.05), and the SCS–CPBS correlation is rs = 0.576 (p < 0.01). The article interprets the negative correlation as suggesting "higher objective knowledge scores may correlate with less frequent self-reported clinical practices."

> "the OKTO showed a minimal, non-significant correlation with the SCS (rs = 0.067, p > 0.05). Notably, there is a statistically significant negative correlation between the OKTO and CPBS (rs = − 0.105, p < 0.05)"
