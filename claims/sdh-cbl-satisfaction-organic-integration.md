## Evidence

### Wen Zhi 2026

Wen Zhi, Chen Peirui, Lyu Jing, Wu Jianqiang, Qin Biyuan, Yang Yong, Huang Guofei, Wu Changxue. (2026). CBL with integrated SDH versus CBL alone in esophageal surgery internship: a randomized controlled trial on clinical competence development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09914-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Satisfaction questionnaire (59 experimental, 62 control valid) with Mann–Whitney U tests; Table 4 prints effect sizes r of 0.55, 0.71, 0.52, and 0.68 for the four significant dimensions.

> "Satisfaction evaluations in this study revealed that students in the CBL + SDH group rated the dimensions of "content and context," "thinking training," and "competence relevance" significantly higher than those in the CBL only group, while scoring significantly lower on the reverse-coded item "perceived artificial integration.""
