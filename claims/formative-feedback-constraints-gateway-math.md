# Students want ongoing targeted formative feedback, but faculty time constraints and large class sizes limit providing it

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Effective feedback helps students understand mistakes and refine thinking, but large class sizes and tight grading deadlines limit faculty feedback, and dichotomous automated grading offers little insight and saps motivation. [→ Higgin 2025](#higgin-2025)

## Evidence

### Higgin 2025

Higgin, T., Holl-Cross, C., Moorjani, A., & Edwards, A., with Cornfield, O., Eisen, S., Hosie, L., McNamara, H., Ray, D., & Tsinnajinnie, B. (2025). Faculty and student perspectives on gateway math courses and courseware. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677594

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Secondary qualitative finding from the gateway math faculty and student study. The report states that "dichotomous grading offers little insight into errors and saps student motivation" and automated grading must prioritize supportive personalized feedback.

> "Students want ongoing, targeted feedback that helps them understand mistakes and refine their thinking, but faculty often struggle to provide it, due to time constraints and large class sizes."
