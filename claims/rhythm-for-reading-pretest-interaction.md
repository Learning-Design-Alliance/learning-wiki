## Evidence

### Styles et al. 2014

Dr Ben Styles, Rebecca Clarkson, Katherine Fowler. (2014). Rhythm for Reading: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN10404460/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-specified subgroup analysis in the RCT exploring the interaction between randomised group and pre-test score on the primary outcome; the interaction was significant at p < 0.01, while analysis by National Curriculum level revealed no significant effects.

> "Both were carried out and a significant positive interaction was observed with pre -test score (p < 0.01)."

### Styles et al. 2014 (2)

Dr Ben Styles, Rebecca Clarkson, Katherine Fowler. (2014). Rhythm for Reading: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN10404460/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model-based interpretation shown in Figure 4: the lines cross just below the pre-test score of 320, near the Level 3/Level 4 boundary. The report states the gradients of the lines are significantly different but that it cannot say for certain the intervention was detrimental for one ability group and effective for another.

> "The model suggests that children of lower ability (below 320 on the pre -test) are likely to perform worse in the intervention group than children of similar ability in the control group."
