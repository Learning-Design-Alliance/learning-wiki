### Said Al Faraby 2024

Said Al Faraby, Adiwijaya Adiwijaya, Ade Romadhony. (2024). Review on Neural Question Generation for Education Purposes. Int J Artif Intell Educ 34:1008–1045. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00374-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Xin et al. (2021), that Conceptnet and Wordnet integration improved question quality; motivations include human questions using synonyms of context words and overcoming rare/unknown words in low-resource domains.

> "Xin et al. ( 2021) empirically showed that general domain external knowledge, namely Conceptnet (Speer et al., 2016) and Wordnet (Miller, 1995) could be integrated to improve the quality of questions developed."
