# The second-use participation benefit holds under every conversation-duration threshold from four to ten minutes

## Subclaims
`q4 i?` Redefining qualifying use at thresholds from four up to ten minutes, the effect of a second assigned opportunity on later participation remains positive and statistically significant at every threshold. [→ Wang 2026](#wang-2026)

## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, D. J., Jain, N. M., Burbano, V., Guzman, J., Keum, D., Kim, S., Kogut, B., & Wright, N. (2026). How assigned AI use before class shapes active student engagement in class. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.7585318](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7585318)

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Sensitivity analysis of the randomized field experiment varying the qualifying-use duration threshold from four to ten minutes (Figure 4; Supplementary Table S5). All second-opportunity coefficients remained significant (p < 0.05); no standardized effect size printed.

> "The effect of a second assigned opportunity is positive and statistically significant at every threshold. It ranges from 𝛽2 = 0.099 to 0.183 in the next session (Supplementary Table S5, Panel B, Models 15 to 21; all p < 0.05) and from 𝛽2 = 0.122 to 0.227 across all future sessions (Panel C, Models 22 to 28; all p < 0.05)."
