### Joanne R. Beames 2023

Joanne R. Beames, Samantha Spanos, Anna Roberts, Lauren McGillivray, Sophie Li, Jill M. Newby, Bridianne O'Dea, Aliza Werner-Seidler. (2023). Intervention Programs Targeting the Mental Health, Professional Burnout, and/or Wellbeing of School Teachers: Systematic Review and Meta-Analyses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09720-w

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Short-term (1-6 month) follow-up meta-analysis of RCTs of teacher programs. Stress and burnout effects grew relative to post-test, while anxiety, sleep, and wellbeing effects were non-significant at the p<0.008 threshold.

> "The short-term follow-up intervention effect sizes were significant and of a large size for stress (g=1.79), and of a moderate size for professional burnout ( g=0.82); they were non-significant for anxiety, sleep, and wellbeing."
