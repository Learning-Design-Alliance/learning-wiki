## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Intelligence showed a significant correlation with corrected reward rate in the low WM load condition (r = 0.35), but no significant correlation in the high WM load condition. [→ Chengyan Yang 2025](#chengyan-yang-2025)

## Evidence

### Chengyan Yang 2025

Chengyan Yang, Tongran Liu, Mengxin Wen & Xun Liu. (2025). The distinct functions of working memory and intelligence in model-based and model-free reinforcement learning. npj Science of Learning 10:72. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00363-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Correlation analyses among RL parameters, intelligence (Cattell's Culture Fair Intelligence Test), and corrected reward rate for 50 participants under each WM load condition. The article attributes the attenuation under high load to task-specific cognitive resources becoming more critical than general intellectual ability.

> "intelligence demonstrated a signiﬁcant correlation with the corrected reward rate in the low WM load condition (r = 0.35, 95% CI [0.07, 0.57], p = 0.013). However, no signiﬁcant correlation was observed between learning performance and intelligence in the high WM load condition"
