## Evidence

### Khalafi Ali 2026

Khalafi Ali, Parhizkar Mahdieh, Albooghobeish Masoumeh, Sarvi-sarmeydani Nooshin. (2026). Effect of the BOPPPS model on pre-anesthesia care education for nurse anesthesia students in Iran: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08696-9

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-test analysis of pre-to-post changes in the 30 control students taught by lecture and mannequin practice. Control knowledge and equipment preparation improved significantly, but "the patient preparation subscale showed only a marginal change" and drug preparation "did not differ significantly".

> "while the patient preparation subscale showed only a marginal change (d = − 0.04, 95% CI [0.00, 0.26], p = 0.063) and the drug preparation subscale did not differ significantly (d = − 0.05, 95% CI [–0.03, 0.16], p = 0.161)"
