Evidence¶

Mark A. Smylie 2001¶

Mark A. Smylie, Elaine Allensworth, Rebecca C. Greenberg, Rodney Harris, Stuart Luppescu. (2001). Teacher Professional Development in Chicago: Supporting Effective Practice. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-professional-development-chicago-supporting-effective-practice

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Cross-sectional survey analysis of elementary teachers (1997 and 1999 Consortium surveys) using hierarchical linear models. The same section reports that exposure to basic-skills content "is related at statistically significant levels to their use of didactic methods" and that quality shows a statistically significant positive relationship. No effect sizes are printed.

"In contrast, we found no significant relationship between the frequency with which teachers participate in professional development and their use of di- dactic teaching methods (see Figure 3)."
