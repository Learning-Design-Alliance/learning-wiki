`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Between-group comparison (Welch's t tests) from Table 1 of the cross-sectional study. No significant differences were found for gender, residence type, or family income between admitted and non-admitted candidates.

> "The standard scores of those who were accepted were significantly higher than those who were not in terms of academic foundation (0.37 ± 1.60 vs. -0.44 ± 1.17, P < 0.001), behavioral engagement (0.93 ± 1.29 vs. -1.04 ± 1.84, P < 0.001), psychological state (0.52 ± 0.71 vs. -0.58 ± 0.97, P < 0.001), and support resources (0.20 ± 1.32 vs. -0.16 ± 1.38, P = 0.025)"
