### Lecce 2026

Lecce, S., Stagnitto, S. M., Mascheretti, S., Lampis, V., & Devine, R. T. (2026). Children's Theory of Mind and Academic Achievement: a Meta-analysis on Reading Comprehension and Math Skills. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10185-w

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Three-level meta-analysis of 5 eligible papers and 11 effect sizes, approximately 677 participants (mean age 8.84 years), using zero-order correlations between earlier academic achievement and later ToM. The printed pooled effect size was r = .32 (95% CI [.11, .50], p = .01); nine of 11 effect sizes concerned reading comprehension.

> "The model revealed a pooled Pearson’s correlation of r = .32 CI [.11, .50], p = .01 (Fig. 3B)."
