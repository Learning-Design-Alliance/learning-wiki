## Evidence

### Stefan Küchemann 2023

Stefan Küchemann, Malte Ubben, David Dzsotjan, Sergey Mukhametov, Carrie A. Weidner, Carrie A. Weidner, Linda Qerimi, Jochen Kuhn, Stefan Heusler, and Jacob F. Sherson. (2023). The impact of an interactive visualization and simulation tool on learning quantum physics: Results of an eye-tracking study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS multiple linear regression on the study's Task 2 gaze data found "a signiﬁcant linear relationship between the total dwell time on the graph (p = 0.001) and the total dwell time on the tilt parameter (p = 0.003)" and the Task 2 score; no standardized coefficients are printed.

> "we performed an OLS multiple linear regression and found that there is a signiﬁcant linear relationship between the total dwell time on the graph (p = 0.001) and the total dwell time on the tilt parameter (p = 0.003) as antecedent variables and the score in Task 2 as the dependent variable."

### Stefan Küchemann 2023 (2)

Stefan Küchemann, Malte Ubben, David Dzsotjan, Sergey Mukhametov, Carrie A. Weidner, Carrie A. Weidner, Linda Qerimi, Jochen Kuhn, Stefan Heusler, and Jacob F. Sherson. (2023). The impact of an interactive visualization and simulation tool on learning quantum physics: Results of an eye-tracking study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The same regression model found "a signiﬁcant moderation eﬀect of the total dwell time on the well spacing on the relation between the total dwell time on the tilt parameter and the score in Task 2 (p = 0.037)"; the article reports no standardized coefficient for the moderation.

> "In this linear model, there is a signiﬁcant moderation eﬀect of the total dwell time on the well spacing on the relation between the total dwell time on the tilt parameter and the score in Task 2 (p = 0.037)"
