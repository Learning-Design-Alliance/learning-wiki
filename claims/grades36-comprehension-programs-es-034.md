# Reading comprehension strategy programs in grades 3–6 yield a medium average effect size of 0.34

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In grades 3–6, programs targeting comprehension strategies such as inferences, detection of the main idea, and knowledge of text structure produced an average effect size of 0.34 on reading comprehension. [→ José L. Arco-Tirado 2024](#jose-l-arco-tirado-2024)

## Evidence

### José L. Arco-Tirado 2024

José L. Arco-Tirado, Francisco D. Fernández-Martín, Mirian Hervás-Torres, Gracia Jiménez-Fernández, Nuria Calet, Sylvia Defior, Amanda J. Neitzel, Robert E. Slavin. (2024). `q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Synthesis of four RCT studies in grades 3–6 targeting reading comprehension, including strategy programs (LEE comprensivamente, Leemos en pareja, the Rainbow Program) and main-idea instruction. The article reports ES = 0.34.

> "The average effect size obtained in these studies (ES = 0.34) is medium following Cohen's (1988) benchmark or between the 80th and 90th percentile in reading following Kraft's (2020)."
