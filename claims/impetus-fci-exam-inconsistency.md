## Evidence

### Steinberg and Sabella 1997

Richard N. Steinberg and Mel S. Sabella. (1997). Performance on Multiple-Choice Diagnostics and Complementary Exam Problems. The Physics Teacher 35, 150–155. https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2344625

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Histogram analysis (Fig. 4) of one lecture class broken down by how many of the two FCI impetus-force questions (9 and 22) each student answered correctly, compared with part B of exam problem 1. The article reports that "over one-third of the class did not answer the three virtually identical items in the same way."

> "All students who answered both FCI questions correctly did very well on the complementary examination problem. Almost all of the students who answered neither FCI question correctly were unable to answer the exam problem correctly. However, over one-third of the class did not answer the three virtually identical items in the same way."
