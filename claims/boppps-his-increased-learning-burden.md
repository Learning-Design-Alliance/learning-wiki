Question 10 of the end-of-year anonymous questionnaire (117 respondents, Likert five-level scoring) asked whether the BOPPPS teaching model increased the learning burden.

> "For question 10, 48 students rated it with a score of 3–5 (41.0%), 51 students rated it with a score of 2 (43.6%), and 18 students rated it with a score of 1 (15.4%), indicating that the majority of students believe that the BOPPPS teaching model has increased the learning burden to some extent."
