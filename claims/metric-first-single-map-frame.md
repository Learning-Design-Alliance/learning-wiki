## Evidence

### P. Fraundorf 2011

P. Fraundorf. (2011). Metric-first & entropy-first surprises. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1106.4698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1106.4698)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Qualitative pedagogical argument (Section III) that all variables in the metric equation can be examined with one map-frame and a traveling clock, avoiding dual-frame length-contraction and simultaneity machinery at the outset.

> "The metric-ﬁrst approach allows one to get quite a lot done in the context of a single reference “map-frame” of yardsticks and synchronized clocks, and therefore a single reference-deﬁnition of simultaneity between events."
