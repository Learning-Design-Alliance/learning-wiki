## Evidence

### Atlas Lauren Y 2022

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel model of medium-heat pain ratings prior to reversal (Table 3, n=40). Post-hoc per-group tests showed practically significant cue effects in both groups; the main effect of Cue was practically significant (0% in ROPE), and the Group x Cue x Trial interaction was of undecided Bayesian significance (35.45% in ROPE).

> "post-hoc analyses within groups indicated that both groups reported practically significant effects of Cue on pain prior to the first reversal"
