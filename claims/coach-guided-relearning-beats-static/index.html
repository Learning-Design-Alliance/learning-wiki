Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Contingent scaffolding improves learning more than fixed or absent support. — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • Relearning dynamics depend on unlearning strength: agents with 10% unlearning recover within early interaction rounds, while 30% and 50% agents show prolonged low-accuracy plateaus and delayed improvement — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Teachable agents measurably but incompletely relearn unlearned knowledge, with the recovery gap to the base model widening under higher unlearning ratios (30%–50%) — related
  • Machine unlearning induces progressively stronger novice-level performance in an LLM teachable agent, with accuracy and F1 declining as the unlearning ratio increases — related
  • Dialogue evidence shows coach feedback induces genuine conceptual change, shifting the agent from misconception to conceptually correct explanation rather than superficial answer alignment — related
  • Teaching interactions typically conclude within 30 dialogue turns, motivating a 30-turn cap to prevent dead loops — related
