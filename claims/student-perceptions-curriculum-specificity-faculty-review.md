### Walsh Jason L 2018

Walsh Jason L, Harris Benjamin H L, Denny Paul, Smith Phil. (2018). Formative student-authored question bank: perceptions, question quality and association with summative performance. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2017-135018

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Four semistructured focus groups with 23 medical students, analysed by thematic analysis (25 initial codes refined into 16 themes). Students reported that "faculty question review is highly valued (joint most prevalent theme)" and raised concerns over question quality; curriculum specificity was the other joint most prevalent theme.

> "We identified two major themes relating to question quality. These were (1) faculty question review is highly valued (joint most prevalent theme) and (2) students had concerns over question quality,"
