# Pseudo-agency: students may appear engaged without understanding the epistemic purpose of their actions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Student agency is not automatic and must be intentionally cultivated; pseudo-agency occurs when students follow procedures or use AI outputs without questioning validity or articulating why their actions matter. [→ Wood 2026](#wood-2026)

## Evidence

### Wood 2026

Wood, D., & Moss, S. (2026). Cultivating Agency and Responsible AI Use Through the AI-ARC Framework. Journal of Instructional Design and Technology, 1(1), 8–17. https://doi.org/10.65201/CJHS2205

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper reports, citing González-Howard et al. (2024), an example in which student participation met the surface requirements of inquiry but failed to reflect genuine decision-making or conceptual understanding. The reviewed study is attributed, not reanalyzed, by this paper.

> "students simply followed a checklist provided by the teacher without articulating why their chosen procedures mattered for answering the research question."
