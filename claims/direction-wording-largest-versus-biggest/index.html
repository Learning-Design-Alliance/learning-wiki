Lowell 1917¶

Lowell, Frances. (1917). A Preliminary Report of Some Group Tests of General Intelligence. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

q1 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative tryout observation during test selection: the original 'largest' wording produced puzzling errors; probing revealed a child did not know what 'largest' meant, and the wording was changed to 'biggest', after which "the success of the test was assured."

"The result was puzzling, for the children crossed the smallest square as often as they did the largest."
