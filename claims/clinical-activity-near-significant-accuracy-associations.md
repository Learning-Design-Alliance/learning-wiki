`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney tests on continuous professional characteristics of the 30 psychiatrists. The article reports "close to but nonsignificant associations" for both lower Hirsch index (U = 60, p = 0.059) and clinical experience duration (U = 65, p = 0.09).

> "Regarding the continuous variables, the results revealed close to but nonsignificant associations between diagnostic accuracy and lower Hirsch index (U = 60, p = 0.059), and between diagnostic accuracy and duration of clinical experience (U = 65, p = 0.09)."
