## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Arguments based on the economy's need for engineers and on benefits to women (salaries, helping others) must be rejected if women do not want to study science and engineering, so they are not valid justifications on their own. [→ Mathieu Bouville 2006](#mathieu-bouville-2006)

Theoretical ethical analysis (Section II) of arguments commonly cited to justify greater female enrollment. The author argues from Kantian and utilitarian premises that "these arguments must be rejected if women do not want to study science and engineering"; no empirical data are offered.
