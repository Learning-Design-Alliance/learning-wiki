## Evidence

### Kim Sang Chul 2017

Kim Sang Chul, Ro Young Sun, Shin Sang Do, Wi Dae Han, Jeong Joongsik, Park Ju Ok, Sun Kyong Min, Bae Kwangsoo. (2017). Assessment of Competence in Emergency Medicine among Healthcare Professionals in Cameroon. Journal of Korean Medical Science. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2017.32.12.1931

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired comparison of self-survey and scenario-based scores for 6 Cameroonian doctors who completed both assessments, analyzed with paired t-test. The article reports the mean difference was "statistically insignificant (mean, −0.02; 95% CI, −0.41 to 0.36)" and an agreement rate of 58.3%.
