Bossio Botero Veronica, Yadav Vijay, Ouyang Jacob, Abbas Anzar, Worthington Michelle. (2026). Voice-Enabled Virtual Patients for Interactive Training in Standardized Clinical Assessment: Mixed Methods Pilot Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/89066

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot user study in which 5 expert raters each conducted voice-based MADRS interviews with 4 virtual patient personae (20 interviews total) and scored them on the 10 MADRS items. The article reports the mean difference between rater and preconfigured scores was "0.52 (SD 0.75)".

> "Across all MADRS items, which are each rated on a 0 to 6 scale, the mean difference between rater and preconfigured scores was 0.52 (SD 0.75)."
