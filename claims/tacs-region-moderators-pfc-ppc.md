# tACS effects on cognitive performance depend on targeted brain region: theta on PFC and PPC and online gamma on PPC are effective

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` For online-tACS, theta-band stimulation of PFC (SMD = 0.389) and PPC (SMD = 0.206) and gamma-band stimulation of PPC (SMD = 0.243) significantly improved cognitive performance; for offline-tACS, theta on PFC was effective (SMD = 0.204). [→ Tae Lee Lee 2023](#tae-lee-lee-2023)

## Evidence

### Tae Lee Lee 2023

Tae Lee Lee, Hanall Lee and Nyeonju Kang. (2023). A meta-analysis showing improved cognitive performance in healthy young adults with transcranial alternating current stimulation. npj Science of Learning 8:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00152-9

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Third moderator analysis of online-tACS comparisons by targeted brain region found "significant positive effects" for PFC theta (SMD = 0.389), PPC theta (SMD = 0.206), and PPC gamma (SMD = 0.243); remaining region conditions were not significant. Offline theta on PFC was also significant (SMD = 0.204).

> "(a) 12 PFC in the theta frequency band comparisons from 10 studies: SMD = 0.389; SE = 0.122; 95% CI = 0.149–0.629; Z = 3.180; P = 0.001; Q-statistics = 37.850 with P < 0.001; I2 = 70.9%, (b) 10 PPC in the theta frequency band comparisons from eight studies: SMD = 0.206"
