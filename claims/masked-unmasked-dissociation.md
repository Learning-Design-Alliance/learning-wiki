# Masked and unmasked task performance tap different components of skilled reading

## Evidence

### Roembke Tanja C. 2019

Roembke Tanja C., Hazeltine Eliot, Reed Deborah K., McMurray Bob. (2019). Automaticity of Word Recognition is a Unique Predictor of Reading Fluency in Middle-School Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000279

`q3 · i?`

Communality analysis (vocabulary, then masked, then unmasked entered) across the same sample. The article concludes "masked performance captures a unique component of variance in fluency; whereas, unmasked performance captures unique variance in decoding", ruling out a mere added-trials reliability explanation.

> "Importantly, masked performance captures a unique component of variance in fluency; whereas, unmasked performance captures unique variance in decoding. This apparent dissociation indicates that masked and unmasked performance may tap different components of skilled reading."
