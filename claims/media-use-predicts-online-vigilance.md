## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A multiple linear regression controlling for country found the five media use variables explained 19% of variance in online vigilance (R² = .19), with media multitasking the strongest predictor (β = .31) and instant messaging the only negative predictor (β = −.11). ## Evidence

### le Roux 2021

le Roux, D.B., Parry, D.A., Totolo, A., Iyawa, G., Holloway, J., Prenter, A., Botha, L. (2021). Media Multitasking, Online Vigilance and Academic Performance among Students in three Southern African Countries. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104056

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey regression (N = 1445, controlling for country). The article reports the variables "explained 19% of the variance in online vigilance" and that media multitasking (β = .31) was strongest, video watching non-significant (β = −.04).

> "Combined, the ﬁve media use behaviour variables explained 19% of the variance in online vigilance (R2 =.19;F (7; 1437) = 48.17,p<. 001)."
