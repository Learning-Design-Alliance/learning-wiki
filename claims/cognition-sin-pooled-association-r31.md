# Cognitive performance shows a pooled association of r = .31 with speech-in-noise perception across adult unaided listeners

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across 25 studies collapsing all cognitive subdomains and all SiN conditions, the pooled association between cognitive performance and SiN perception was r = .31. [→ Dryden Adam 2017](#dryden-adam-2017)

## Evidence

### Dryden Adam 2017

Dryden Adam, Allen Harriet A., Henshaw Helen, Heinrich Antje. (2017). The Association Between Cognitive Performance and Speech-in-Noise Perception for Adult Listeners: A Systematic Literature Review and Meta-Analysis. Trends in Hearing. https://doi.org/10.1177/2331216517744675

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis (Hedges and Olkin method) of 25 included studies with a pooled sample size of 1,026 listeners; the pooled association was r = .31 with 95% CI [0.23, 0.39], Z = 7.2, p < .001, I2 = 44%. The review reports "an association of .31".

> "The analysis of the association between a general measure of cognitive performance and a general measure of SiN perception, when considering the full range of listeners, showed an association of .31."
