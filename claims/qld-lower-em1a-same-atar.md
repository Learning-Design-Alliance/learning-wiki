## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Engineering Mathematics 1A, Queensland students score lower than students from other states and territories with the same ATAR, and their mean differs significantly from every other jurisdiction except the ACT. [→ John F. Arnold and Leesa A. Sidhu 2014](#john-f-arnold-and-leesa-a-sidhu-2014)

## Evidence

### John F. Arnold and Leesa A. Sidhu 2014

John F. Arnold and Leesa A. Sidhu. (2014). A Statistical Analysis of the Performance of First Year Engineering Students at UNSW Canberra and the Impact of the State Where They Undertook Year 12 Study. UNSW Canberra. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1506.07400](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1506.07400)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA over 2007-2014 Engineering Mathematics 1A results across seven educational areas rejected equality of means (p=5 x 10 -15); pairwise comparisons show "significantly different (at the 5% significance level) for Queensland and every other state and territory" except the ACT.

> "the pairs of mean  results are significantly different (at the 5% significance level) for Queensland and every other state and territory with the exception of the Australian Capital Territory (p=0.052)."
