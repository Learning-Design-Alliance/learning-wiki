Evidence¶

Meloni et al. 2017¶

Carla Meloni, Rachele Fanari, Andrea Bertucci and Sara Berretti. (2017). IMPACT OF EARLY NUMERACY TRAINING ON KINDERGARTENERS FROM MIDDLE-INCOME FAMILIES. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Follow-up analysis on a subsample of 25 low-ability children (training group n=14 drawn from CL and IL conditions; control group n=11), selected at half a standard deviation below the normative BIN 4-6 mean. The Wilcoxon comparison of improvement scores showed a significant difference with printed effect size |r| = .40 ("W = 114, p = .04; |r| = .40").

"To test if the training group improved more compared with the control one, the same BIN-IS score used in the previous analysis was used as dependent variable, and a Wilcoxon test was carried out to compare the two groups. The results showed a significant difference between the two groups (W = 114, p = .04; |r| = .40)."
