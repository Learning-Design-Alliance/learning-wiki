### Hu Yaojia 2022

Hu Yaojia, Zheng Bingya, Zhu Lihui, Tang Shuo, Lu Qi, Song Qingqing, Zhang Na, Zhong Yan. (2022). The effectiveness of emergency knowledge training of pediatric medical workers based on the knowledge, skills, simulation model: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03267-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive results from three attitude questions administered post-training in the same study; Table 7 also reports about 50% were willing to attend CPR training at their own expense and over 90% wanted further emergency skills training. The article reports "98.59% of the medical workers accepted the training approach".

> "98.59% of the medical workers accepted the training approach, 89.75% of the medical workers thought the current training exercise content was adequate, but more than 400 people still suggested adding other exercise feedback content; the vast majority were willing to participate in the follow-up training"
