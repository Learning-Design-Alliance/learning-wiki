Analysis of selection ratios (SD of predictor in entrants as proportion of SD in applicants) across predictors in the cohort studies. The article reports the mean ratio .732 and the printed per-predictor values, noting the derived GCE composite shows particularly strong selection (.358), implying holistic admissions judgments combining multiple information sources.

> "There is strong selection on the GCE qualifications of A-levels (.656), AS-levels (.667), and GCSEs/O-levels (.676), with less strong selection on the SQA qualifications of Highers (.896), ‘Highers Plus’ (.814), and Advanced Highers (.941). The two derived measures from the UKCAT-12 study [21], Educational Attainment based on GCE results and SQA results, have stronger selection than their component measure (GCE .358; SQA .766)"
