### Yeonsun Yang 2026

Yeonsun Yang, Sang Won Lee, Jean Y. Song, Sangdoo Yun, and Young-Ho Kim. 2026. LingoQ: Bridging the Gap between EFL Learning and Work through AI-Generated Work-Related Quizzes. In Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791342

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online survey with 49 Korean non-native information workers plus follow-up interviews with ten volunteers, analyzed with descriptive statistics and Thematic Analysis. The survey found 14 of 21 current learners "struggled to sustain their learning" due to work-irrelevant materials.

> "However, the majority of them (14/21) struggled to sustain their learning because irrelevant learning materials did not translate into practical support in their work contexts."
