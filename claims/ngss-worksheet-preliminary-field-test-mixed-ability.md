Preliminary field testing involving nine grade VIII students (three high-ability, two medium, three low) selected by teacher input. Aspects covered layout, images, tables, questions, and language; the average of "the four aspects was 3.22". Student suggestions informed phase-2 revisions.

> "The average score of the four aspects was 3.22, a good score, meaning that the NGSS worksheet could be understood by low, middle, or high-ability students."
