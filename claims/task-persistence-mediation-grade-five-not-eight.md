## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In grade five, task persistence significantly mediates the relationship between math anxiety and math achievement; in grade eight the indirect effect is not significant. [→ Kilp-Kabel 2025](#kilp-kabel-2025)

## Evidence

### Kilp-Kabel 2025

Triinu Kilp-Kabel, Kaja Mädamürk. (2025). Expectancies, values, and task persistence can alleviate the negative effects of math anxiety on math performance. European Journal of Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10212-024-00928-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Indirect-effects path analysis (percentile bootstrap, subsample N = 1000) tested task persistence as mediator. Grade five showed a significant indirect path (β = −0.526, 95% CI (−0.954, −0.194), 16% of variance); grade eight's indirect effect "was not significant (p = 0.13)".

> "In grade five, there was a statistically significant indirect path from math anxiety through task persistence to math achievement (β = − 0.526, 95% CI = (− 0.954, − 0.194)) explaining 16% of the variance. In grade eight, the indirect effect was not significant (p = 0.13)."
