## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Treated students reported higher motivation before their exam, a 0.101-unit increase (p = 0.013), d = 0.066, significant at the preregistered threshold of 0.033; the printed effect size is negligible. [→ Keller Tamás 2021](#keller-tamas-2021)

## Evidence

### Keller Tamás 2021

Keller Tamás, Szakál Péter. (2021). Not just words! Effects of a light-touch randomized encouragement intervention on students’ exam grades, self-efficacy, motivation, and test anxiety. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256960

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Secondary analysis of the endline-survey subsample of the crossover field experiment, single-item motivation (0–10) measured before exams. The article reports a “0.101 unit increase in their motivation (p = 0.013)”, d = 0.066, below the 0.2 threshold so coded as negligible magnitude.

> "We have experimentally confirmed that those who received the encouragement message experienced a 0.101 unit increase in their motivation (p = 0.013), equivalent to a Cohen’s d effect size of 0.066."
