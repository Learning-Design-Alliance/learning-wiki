### Hall TR 2025

Hall TR, Mosley CF, Vakil JB, Lambert CK, Savelli DM, Balta JY. (2025). Who is teaching anatomy? An examination of the demographic characteristics, academic backgrounds, and professional responsibilities of US anatomy teachers. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70005

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey results on research time effort: median assigned research effort was 10% (mean 16%, SD 17), below a suggested 20% benchmark; 49% (198 of 408) of professors reported perceived research effort of 5% or less. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size was printed.

> "This was true across professorship levels (i.e., full, associate, or assistant) in the current study, as 36% (144 of 402) of professors indicated having an assigned percent effort of 5% or less dedicated to research."
