## Evidence

### Waters Peadar S 2014

Waters Peadar S, McVeigh Terri, Kelly Brian D, Flaherty Gerard T, Devitt Dara, Barry Kevin, Kerin Michael J. (2014). The acquisition and retention of urinary catheterisation skills using surgical simulator devices: teaching method or student traits. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0264-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Confidence questionnaire data from the three-group study comparing estimated versus actual confidence at retention testing. Interactive (p = 0.19) and observed (p = 0.1) groups showed no significant difference; only the didactic group miscalibrated, p < 0.005.

> "within the didactic group, the level of estimated confidence was significantly overestimated at the time of index examination compared to actual level of confidence measured at retention testing 4 weeks later (6.1 ± 1.07 v 4.53 ± 0.51, p < 0.005)"
