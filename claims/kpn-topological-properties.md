## Evidence

### Min Xia 2024

Min Xia, Zhu Su, Wei-Bing Deng, Xiu-Mei Feng, Ben-Wei Zhang. (2024). How to quantify an examination? Evidence from physics examinations via complex networks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Network analysis of 35 KPNs. The IKPN degree distribution is a fat-tail distribution with α = 1.011. Histograms of r values showed all KPNs except 11V2 positive; 30 of 35 KPNs showed small-world effects versus ER random models; only 5 (10V2, 12V1, 12V2, 16V3, 17V3) did not.

> "the degree distribution of the IKPN follows a pronounced fat-tail distribution, characterized by a power law index of α = 1.011, demonstrating scale-free characteristics"
