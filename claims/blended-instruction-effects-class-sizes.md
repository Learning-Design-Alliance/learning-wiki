## Evidence

### Li 2022

Li, B., Yu, Q., & Yang, F. (2022). The effect of blended instruction on student performance: A meta-analysis of 106 empirical studies from China and abroad. Best Evidence in Chinese Education, 10(2):1395-1403. https://doi.org/10.15354/bece.22.ar018

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis comparing pooled effect sizes across four class-size groups in the meta-analysis. The article reports all four SMDs exceed 0.5 and the between-group test (p = 0.485 > 0.05) shows no significant differences.

> "SMDs of class sizes of 51 - 100 students, 100 + students, 31 - 50 students and 1- 30 students are 0.752, 0.704, 0.663, and 0.562 respectively"
