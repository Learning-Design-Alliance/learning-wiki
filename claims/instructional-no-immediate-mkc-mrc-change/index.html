Evidence¶

Pau Benazet i Montobbio 2026¶

Pau Benazet i Montobbio, Janne Rotter, Davinia Hernández-Leo. (2026). Experiential Versus Instructional Approaches for Eliciting Metacognitive Awareness in AI-Assisted Learning: A Short-Term Longitudinal Study. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20047 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.20047

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Within-group paired analysis of the instructional group (n1 = 61) at T0 to T1 in the quasi-experiment. MRC, MKC and ANC change scores were not significant; the instructional group's significant immediate gains were limited to AIA, AIL and AIE.

"Metacognitive Regulation of Cognition (MRC) (∆M= 0.11,p= .051,d= 0.26), Metacognitive Knowledge of Cognition (MKC) (∆M= 0.05,p= .42) and Autonomy & Control (ANC) (∆M= 0.11,p = .31) did not show any significant difference from pretest scores."
