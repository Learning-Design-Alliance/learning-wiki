## Evidence

### Gandolfi 2026

Gandolfi, E., Soyturk, I., Kosko, K. W., Ferdig, R. E., & Clements, R. (2026). Measuring presence in virtual world teacher education: validation of the eXtended Reality Presence Scale. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10619-w

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations on 74 successfully recorded viewings (twitches ranged 3 to 24, M = 10.51, SD = 6.71); a post hoc power analysis yielded power of 0.99. "A higher sense of presence was associated with a more continuous gaze."

> "The correlation between participants' XRPS Rasch scores and number of twitches was found to be statistically significant with strong negative coefficients for emotional connectivity (r = − 0.69, p <.001), co-presence (r = − 0.71, p <.001), and awareness of self (r = − 0.75, p <.001)."
