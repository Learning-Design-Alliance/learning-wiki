[doi:10.1037/h0062882](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0062882) (read through Allen's 1953 research abstract, [doi:10.1007/bf02713265](https://doi.org/10.1007/bf02713265))

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

An experiment with 815 University of California students exposed to a debate communication; the authors report movement toward pre-existing bias was "confirmed for the male Ss but remains equivocal for the femaies".

> "That in the debate situation, Ss would move in the direction of their pre-exist- ing bias, the mount of this change depending upon the degree of emotional in- volvement in the attitude. The first part of this implication was confirmed for the male Ss but remains equivocal for the femaies"
