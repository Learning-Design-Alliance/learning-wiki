# Four specific sensorimotor connections showed significant TIME × GROUP interaction effects, with post-training increases only in the BCI-SRF group

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` S1M1_R–Cere_R, THA_R–Cere_R, Cere_R–SMA_L, and S1M1_R–THA_L each showed significant TIME × GROUP interaction effects with post vs pre increases only in the BCI-SRF group. [→ Yuan Liu 2024](#yuan-liu-2024)

## Evidence

### Yuan Liu 2024

Yuan Liu, Shuaifei Huang, Weiguo Xu, Zhuang Wang & Dong Ming. (2024). An fMRI study on the generalization of motor learning after brain actuated supernumerary robot training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00294-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way repeated-measures ANOVA of each pairwise FC within the sensorimotor network at pre and post stages. Four connections showed significant TIME × GROUP interactions, and post-hoc tests showed "a signiﬁcant post vs pre increase only for the BCI- SRF group" in S1M1_R–Cere_R (p = 0.03), THA_R–Cere_R (p = 0.004), and Cere_R–SMA_L (p = 0.012).

> "A signiﬁcant TIME × GROUP interaction effect was found between these 4 connections (S1M1_R with Cere_R: F (1, 17) = 4.527, p = 0.0483; THA_R with Cere_R: F (1, 17) = 4.817,p = 0.0424; Cere_R with SMA_L: F (1, 17) = 4.623, p = 0.0462; S1M1_R with THA_L F (1, 17) = 6.182, p = 0.0236)"
