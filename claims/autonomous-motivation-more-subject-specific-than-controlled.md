## Evidence

### Chanal J 2020

Chanal J, Paumier D. (2020). The school-subject-specificity hypothesis: Implication in the relationship with grades. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0230103

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 579 junior high students; CTCM-1 structural equation models partitioned item variance into situational, contextual and item levels for seven regulation types. The article reports that "Autonomous motivation appears to be more specific (between 38 to 56% in total variance) than controlled motivation (between 10 to 23% in total)".
