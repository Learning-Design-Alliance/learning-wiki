## Evidence

### Adrienne L. Traxler 2020

Adrienne L. Traxler, Tyme Suda, Eric Brewe, and Kelley Commeford. (2020). Network positions in active learning environments in physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive statistics (Table II) for the four class networks at weeks 1 and 10, with bootstrap standard errors from 1000 trials. For example, SCALE-UP density rises from 0.022 to 0.041 and ISLE from 0.05 to 0.09; the text notes "the week 10 density roughly doubles from its week 1 value" in three classes.

> "The Peer Instruction network shows only a marginal increase in density over the semester, but in the other three classes, the week 10 density roughly doubles from its week 1 value. A similar pattern exists for average degree."
