Content analysis of the 13 included articles in this systematic mapping literature review characterized publication outlets and geographic locations. The authors report that "all articles included in the review were published between 2009 and 2021" and that 69% were authored exclusively by researchers in Canada, where TES originated.

> "Although no restrictions were placed on the publication dates, all articles included in the review were published between 2009 and 2021 (see Figure 2). Of the articles, 69% were authored by researchers exclusively in Canada"
