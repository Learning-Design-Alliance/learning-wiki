### Witherspoon 2026

Witherspoon, E.B.; Dracopoli, M., Hawkins, M., Dahlke, K. (2026). Developing a measure of middle school math teachers' pedagogical content knowledge of computational thinking (REL 2026–007). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/developing-measure-middle-school-math-teachers-pedagogical-content-knowledge-computational-thinking

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Descriptive analysis of 102 teachers' responses (figure 1, mean = 9.7). Scores ranged from 6 percent to 100 percent correct; "92 percent of teachers scored above the level that could have been achieved through pure chance," suggesting responses were not random. Rasch analysis showed a 25th-percentile teacher would likely answer 6 items correctly and a 75th-percentile teacher all 16.

> "On average, teachers selected the correct response on approximately 10 of the 16 items (61 percent correct) (figure 1). Most teachers (76 percent) identified the correct response on at least half of the 16 items"
