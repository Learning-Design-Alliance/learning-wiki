`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Baseline survey of the 82 participants in the multi-center observational study; Table 1 reports cases of mediolateral episiotomies differing between occupations (p < 0.001), with 57.1% of obstetricians below 10 cases versus 14.9% of midwives.

> "Notably, more than half of the obstetricians had performed less than 10 mediolateral episiotomies (MLEs) or had assisted less than 10 deliveries. In contrast, most midwives had performed more than 50 MLEs or had assisted at least 50 deliveries."
