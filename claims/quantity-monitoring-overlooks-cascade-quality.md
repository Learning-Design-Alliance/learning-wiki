In the qualitative analysis, monitoring was mainly discussed via organizational targets such as "the number of individuals trained"; the article reports this "could also encourage undue focus on numbers rather than the quality of TtT training".

> "Whilst easily estimating the success of the TtT model through the number of individuals trained could be motivating, it could also encourage undue focus on numbers rather than the quality of TtT training and the value of the subsequent cascaded MECC training."
