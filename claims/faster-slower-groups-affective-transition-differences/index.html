ONA difference network comparing groups split by median task duration shows "faster groups experience much stronger aﬀective transitions among curious and optimistic," while slower groups showed stronger transitions among confused, conflicted, and frustrated, with a confused-to-conflicted weight of 0.11 and confused-to-disengaged of -0.05.

"Line weights for this (see Table 3) shows, faster groups experience much stronger aﬀective transitions among curious and optimistic, occasionally transitioning from optimistic to disengaged (-0.05). In contrast, slower groups showed stronger bidirectional transitions among confused, conﬂicted, and frustrated."
