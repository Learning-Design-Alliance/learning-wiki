F1000Research 14:1416. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.172023.1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of tutor interviews (6 tutors) identified developing problem-solving and critical thinking skills as a subtheme; one tutor described beginning to critically assess his own actions after tutee questioning.

> "Tutors valued the chance to ask questions or respond to inquiries that encouraged their critical thinking during sessions. Interviews clearly showed that the tutoring process helped develop problem-solving and critical thinking skills."

### Dias Jacqueline Maria 2025 (2)

Dias Jacqueline Maria, Al Kaabi Fatema Salim Bati Salim, Al Hooti Sara Tamim, Hammadi Nada Osamah, Karim Rana, AlYateem Nabeel, Reefat Ahmed Fatma, Sara Abraham Mini, Seshan Vidya, Arsyad Sub Muhammad, Dias Ambrose Richard, Mottershead Richard (2025). Exploring peer tutoring experiences in improving nursing students’ academic success and performance: A qualitative study among Emirati undergraduate nursing students. F1000Research 14:1416. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.172023.1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Tutor interview data in the communication skills subtheme; tutors reported improved respect, communication, patience and listening, and the discussion notes several tutors expressed interest in clinical instructor or professor roles.

> "I developed a range of communication skills. In the past, I would avoid difficult patients, but through peer-tutoring, I learned to confront challenges and focus on effective communication with tutees."
