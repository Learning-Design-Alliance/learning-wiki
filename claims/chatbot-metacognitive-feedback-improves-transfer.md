`q3 i2` After controlling pre-test knowledge, transfer scores differed significantly by feedback type (F = 6.75, p = 0.002, effect size 0.14), with metacognitive feedback higher than both affective and neutral feedback. [→ Yin 2025](#yin-2025)

## Evidence

### Yin 2025

Yin, J., Xu, H., Pan, Y., & Hu, Y. (2025). Effects of different AI-driven Chatbot feedback on learning outcomes and brain activity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00311-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of transfer scores (new-application questions, maximum 15 points) in the randomized three-group chatbot experiment. MF (M = 9.38, SD = 2.88) exceeded NF (M = 6.72, SD = 3.34; corrected p = 0.002) and AF (M = 7.59; corrected p = 0.050); "transfer scores were comparable between the AF and NF groups".

> "students’transfer scores showed a signiﬁcant difference in the feedback type ( F = 6.75, p = 0.002). The effect size was 0.14, and the statistical power was 0.91. Bonferroni post-hoc comparisons revealed that transfer scores in the MF group ( M = 9.38, SD = 2.88) were sig- niﬁcantly higher than in the NF group"
