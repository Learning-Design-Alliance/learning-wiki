## Evidence

### Jabeen Kauser 2026

Jabeen Kauser, Rehman Javeria, Fatima Syeda Sadia, Ahmed Imran, Ghias Kulsoom. (2026). Does medical students’ perception of team-based learning have an impact on achieving planned learning outcomes?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09185-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

FGD thematic analysis found students valued PBL's group dynamics and pacing, with one noting TBL "went too fast and a bit too accelerated". This contrasts with survey data where 59.4% found TBL more helpful than PBL for understanding basic/clinical concepts.

> "The students preferred PBL as a learning modality as they believed the process ensures the participation of all group members in the small group, with much clearer understanding and more focused learning objectives"
