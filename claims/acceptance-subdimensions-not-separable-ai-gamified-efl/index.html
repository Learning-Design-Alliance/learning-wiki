Zhorabekova A 2026¶

Zhorabekova A, Duisenova M, Kurmasheva M and Yesnazar A (2026). Acceptance of AI-gamified adaptive learning in EFL: bifactor evidence for essential unidimensionality and the limits of subscale scoring. Front. Educ. 11:1929123. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1929123

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-sectional survey of 401 EFL students at a Kazakhstani university; full-sample ML CFA of the four-domain 13-item scale. The article reports "inter-construct correlations were very high (AI–effectiveness r = .93; effectiveness–engagement r = .93)" and HTMT up to .94; only gamification was distinguishable (HTMT = .76–.79).

"Discriminant validity was not established: inter-construct correlations were very high (AI–effectiveness r = .93; effectiveness–engagement r = .93), HTMT exceeded .85 for these pairs (up to .94), and the Fornell–Larcker criterion was violated."
