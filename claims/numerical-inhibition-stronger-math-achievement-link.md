## Evidence

### Camilla Gilmore 2015

Camilla Gilmore, Sarah Keeble, Sophie Richardson, Lucy Cragg. (2015). The role of cognitive inhibition in different components of arithmetic. ZDM Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-014-0659-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression analysis with WIAT mathematics composite standard score as dependent variable and the two inhibition scores entered in reversed steps (Table 2, model R2 = 0.08). The article reports "Numerical inhibition score was a significant predictor, over and above non-numerical inhibition score".

> "Numerical inhibition score was a significant predictor, over and above non-numerical inhibition score, whereas non-numerical inhibition score added nothing to the model after numerical inhibition score."

### Camilla Gilmore 2015 (2)

Camilla Gilmore, Sarah Keeble, Sophie Richardson, Lucy Cragg. (2015). The role of cognitive inhibition in different components of arithmetic. ZDM Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-014-0659-y

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Zero-order correlations in the cross-sectional study of 209 participants aged 11–12, 13–14 and adults. The article reports "Numerical inhibition score was moderately correlated with mathematics achievement (r = −.271, p < .001)" and a small significant non-numerical correlation (r = −.177).

> "Numerical inhibition score was moderately correlated with mathematics achievement (r = −.271, p < .001). Non-numerical inhibition score showed a small significant correlation with mathematics achievement (r = −.177, p = .016)."
