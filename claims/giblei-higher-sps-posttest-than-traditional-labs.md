## Evidence

### Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen 2025

Chengere Ashebir Mekonnen, Bono Beyene Dobo, Zinabu Samuel Assefa, Jilo Kedir Woliy. (2025). Enhancing secondary school students’ science process skills through guided inquiry-based laboratory activities in biology. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320692

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test comparison of two intact Grade 10 classes (EG N=46, CG N=29) after an eight-week intervention. The Welch’s t-test showed the EG "outperformed the CG students in their posttest scores"; the article also reports a large effect size (η² = 0.82).

> "A Welch’s t-test was conducted to compare the differences in the posttest SPS scores between the two groups. The results showed a significant difference (t = 4.54, p < .05), suggesting that the EG students outperformed the CG students in their posttest scores."
