# Physicians engage in SDL programs more when they perceive the content as useful and relevant to their clinical practice

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Perception of SDL program content was the factor most often reported in the beliefs about consequences domain, both as facilitator (perceived usefulness/relevance) and barrier (perceived irrelevance). [→ Dahn J 2018](#dahn-j-2018)

## Evidence
