The review reports Lee and Ahn's category learning experiment with three groups studying 36 paintings by six artists in Block 1, then classifying 48 new paintings from 12 artists; the quote reports the Interim Test group's advantage, showing interim testing of studied concepts enhances learning of new concepts.

> "the Interim Test group substantially outperformed the Interim Restudy and Interim Distractor groups, revealing a forward testing effect in inductive learning"
