### Channegowda Nalini Yelahanka 2025

Channegowda Nalini Yelahanka, Pai Dinker Ramanand, Manivasakan Shivasakthy. (2025). Simulation-based teaching versus traditional small group teaching for first-year medical students among high and low scorers in respiratory physiology, India: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2025.22.8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article states that high scorers performed better than low scorers in both groups, but the statistics it prints for this sub-analysis are within-subgroup pre-post paired t-tests, which do not test high against low scorers: intervention high scorers (n=28) t=2.48 (P=0.009), control high scorers (n=25) t=3.10 (P=0.002); intervention low scorers (n=27) t=1.48 (P=0.074), control low scorers t=–1.118 (P=0.274).

> "Sub-analysis comparing high and low scorers within both groups showed that high scorers performed significantly better than low scorers in both the intervention and control groups"
