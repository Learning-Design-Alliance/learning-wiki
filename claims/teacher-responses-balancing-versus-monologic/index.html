Related Claims¶

  • The increase in noticing dialogue was accompanied by a decrease in noticing mathematical activity, described as attentional tunneling — a design consideration for integrated dialogue-plus-content noticing training — related
  • After a dialogic-focused professional development intervention, mathematics teachers noticed student dialogue significantly more, in both attending (83 to 170 cases) and responding (24 to 71 cases) — a broader claim this one bears on
  • The intervention significantly increased teachers' noticing of six dialogic attributes — persuasive interaction, one leads and another is led, agreement and collective attention, students have equal status, dialogic gap, and monologue — but not of dialogue-expansion attributes — related
  • In RAMPUP field trials, English Learners' mathematical language became more monologic, authoritative, and technical within a single 40-minute jigsaw activity — related
