Multiple regression analysis of teaching styles on demographics and academic variables (Table 4, N = 248). The gender coefficient for authority style was −0.200 (p < 0.05); the printed coefficient is unstandardised, so no effect size is reported.

> "Among the demographic variables and teaching styles, our computed results showed that only authority teaching style and gender had significantly relationship (coefficient value − 0.20), which indicates that females were 20% less authorative in teaching compared to male teachers."
