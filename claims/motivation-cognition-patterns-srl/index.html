The factor structure, in which high-level motivation (self-efficacy, beliefs about control, intrinsic orientation, task value) and high-level learning strategies (organization, metacognition, elaboration, control of environment) form separate but related factors, supports the existence of patterns compatible with Pintrich and García (1993). → Valentín 2013

Evidence¶

Valentín 2013¶

Valentín, A., Mateos, P. M., González-Tablas, M. M., Pérez, L., López, E., & García, I. (2013). Motivation and learning strategies in the use of ICTs among university students. Computers & Education, 61, 52–58. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2012.09.008

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The authors' interpretation of their factor-analytic findings, linking the observed motivation–strategy clustering to prior SRL proposals. This is an interpretive claim rather than a new experimental test.

"These ﬁndings support the existence of patterns of relationships between motivation and cognition that are compatible with the proposal reported by Pintrich and García (1993) to the effect that positive motivational beliefs (high scores on Intrinsic Goals, Task Eval-uation and Self-efﬁcacy) would be associated with a greater degree of cognitive compromise and self-regulation by students."

Related Claims¶

  • Low- and high-proficiency EFL learners differ significantly in self-efficacy, intrinsic goal orientation, and test anxiety — related
  • Self-efficacy positively and test anxiety negatively predicted classroom grades, while test anxiety positively predicted cognitive strategy use but negatively predicted regulatory strategy use — related
  • Pre-actional metacognitive, motivational, and positive-emotional components are positively associated with regulatory behavior in the actional phase, while anger and anxiety are negatively associated — related
  • Learning patterns are associated with personal factors (personality, motivation, beliefs, prior education, age, gender); their associations with personality are weak to moderate, indicating learning patterns are not stable traits — related
  • Learners' task-specific self-efficacy beliefs predict whether they initiate, persist through, and recover from difficulty on academic tasks. — related
  • Creating educational Vlogs improved trainee teachers' motivational beliefs relative to written reflections — related
  • Student factors — older age, personality traits, intrinsic motivation and self-efficacy — are associated with deeper approaches to learning — related
