## Evidence

### Dudek et al. 2019

Christopher M. Dudek, MEd, Linda A. Reddy, PhD, Adam Lekwa, PhD, Anh N. Hua, PhD, and Gregory A. Fabiano, PhD. (2019). Improving Universal Classroom Practices Through Teacher Formative Assessment and Coaching. Assessment for Effective Intervention. https://doi.org/10.1177/1534508418772919

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive TCES results (α = .91 in this study) from the 32 teachers; item means on the 7-point scale ranged from 5.86 to 6.96 with a total of "M = 89.92, SD = 8.06". No inferential test was reported.

> "Overall, teachers rated their satisfaction with the CSC Model as very high (M = 89.92, SD = 8.06). Notably, the means for each item did not go below 5.86 (Item 10, SD = 1.43) and ranged as high as 6.96 (Item 1, SD = .19)."
