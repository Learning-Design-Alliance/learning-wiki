## Evidence

### Ong Kelly 2023

Ong Kelly, Bald Philip, Dryhurst David, Ahmed Saif, Yusuf Gibran Tim, Lunawat Rahul, Sriprasad Seshadri, Thapa Gauri, Tirnoveanu Alice, Webb Richard, Rahman Eqram. (2023). The design and validation of a low-cost trans perineal (TP) prostate biopsy simulator for training: improving trainees’ confidence and cognitive targeting skills. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-023-04387-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Construct validity assessment in a district general hospital with 3 expert and 4 novice participants, each obtaining 12 template biopsies from left and right anterior, mid and posterior zones; accuracy was scored by core colour, with samples of > 50% length of the corresponding colour deemed accurate. The study found "66.7% in experts and 31.3% in novice" (p = 0.02). No effect size was printed.

> "The mean overall score for accuracy from a 12-core systematic biopsy was 66.7% in experts and 31.3% in novice. This was statistically significant between both groups (p = 0.02)."
