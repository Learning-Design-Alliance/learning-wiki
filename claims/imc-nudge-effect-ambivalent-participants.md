Correlational analysis of the two 5-point Likert questions in Survey 1 (2023, 71 respondents), shown as a 3-dimensional histogram in Fig. 5. The article adds: "Some people that were ambivalent about choosing particle physics report positive influence to later go into particle physics research."

> "There is a most clear correlation: those participants who clearly had no ambition to later study particle physics were not influenced in their decision."
