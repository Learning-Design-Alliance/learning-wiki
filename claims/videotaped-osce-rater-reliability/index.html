El Sherif Reem 2024¶

El Sherif Reem, Shrier Ian, Paul-Tellier Pierre, Rodriguez Charo. (2024). What do we know about Objective Structured Clinical Examination in Sport and Exercise Medicine? A scoping review. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.77841

q3 · i? · review · r2

Quantitative descriptive study (Chen 2013) using a videotaped OSCE in a fracture scenario, with raters from different subspecialties and seniority levels assessing at three time points. The review reports "moderate inter-rater reliability" and high internal consistency; no standardised effect size is printed.

"Calculations revealed high construct validity, moderate inter-rater reliability, and high internal consistency."

q2 · i? · review · r2

Authors' interpretation of the videotaped-OSCE rater study, as reported in the review: rater flexibility makes the OSCE "more accessible and practical to run". This is an interpretive statement, not a tested result.

"These findings suggest that the rater does not necessarily have to be a specific person and this finding make an OSCE more accessible and practical to run."
