Analysis of CDE CALPADS suspension data, 2014–15 through 2022–23, across four student groups. Suspension rates dropped during pandemic remote learning but had reverted to pre-pandemic levels by 2022–23.

"students in foster care were consistently three times more likely to be suspended than all other student groups, meaning that an average of about one in seven students in foster care received exclusionary discipline that limited their learning time"
