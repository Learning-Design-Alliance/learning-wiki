# Students tutored by an xPST-created web statistics tutor (xSTAT) scored higher on exam transfer problems than untutored students

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Randomly assigned students using the xSTAT ITS averaged 74% versus 62% on transfer problems at final exam time, a significant difference with d=0.62. [→ Stephen B. Gilbert 2015](#stephen-b-gilbert-2015)

## Evidence

### Stephen B. Gilbert 2015

Stephen B. Gilbert, Stephen B. Blessing, Enruo Guo. (2015). Authoring Effective Embedded Tutors: An Overview of the Extensible Problem Specific Tutor (xPST) System. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25:428–454. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0045-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison in the xSTAT Web xPST statistics tutor study: 24 students used the ITS-enabled web problems and 25 did the same multi-step real-data problems without ITS feedback. The quote reports the final-exam transfer difference, "a significant difference ( t(47)=2.03, p<.05, d=0.62)".

> "The non-ITS condition averaged 62 % ( SD=6.99) and the students with tutored instruction averaged 74 % ( SD=4.27) on these problems, a significant difference ( t(47)=2.03, p<.05, d=0.62)."
