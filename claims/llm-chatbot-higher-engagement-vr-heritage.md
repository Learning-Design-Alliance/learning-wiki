## Evidence

### Ka Hei Carrie Lau 2025

Ka Hei Carrie Lau, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, and Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Evaluating Usability and Engagement of Large Language Models in Virtual Reality for Traditional Scottish Curling. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-91572-7_11](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-91572-7_11)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U test on conversation log data from the between-subjects study showed the CGPT group (M = 13.05, SD = 5.43) had "significantly higher prompting frequency compared to the PD group" (M = 9.00, SD = 5.66). Total playtime also differed significantly (U = 249, p = 0.006, r = 0.462), with CGPT at M = 591.86 seconds vs PD at M = 416.28.

> "the test revealed a significant difference in prompting frequency between the two groups with U = 254, n1 = 19, n2 = 17, p = 0.0034, and a moderate effect size (r = 0.489)."

### Ka Hei Carrie Lau 2025 (2)

Ka Hei Carrie Lau, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, and Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Evaluating Usability and Engagement of Large Language Models in Virtual Reality for Traditional Scottish Curling. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-91572-7_11](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-91572-7_11)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In-game performance metrics from the same study showed "no significant difference in shot attempts between" groups; stones hit also showed no significant difference (p = 0.406). Equivalence was not tested.

> "there was no significant difference in shot attempts between the CGPT (M = 2.58, SD = 1.84) and PD groups (M = 2.41, SD = 1.00), U = 155, n1 = 19, n2 = 17, p = 0.842, r = −0.034."
