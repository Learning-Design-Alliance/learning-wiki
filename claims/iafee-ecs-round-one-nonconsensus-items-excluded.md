`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

First-round e-Delphi with 24 experts. Items with CVI below 0.80 were excluded after author consensus meetings; "item 13 was excluded because it was included in the wording of the previous item" and items 18 and 30 were excluded due to "the impossibility of using them in assessment of students' practice".

> "There was no consensus among experts regarding the title of domain I (CVI 0.79) and also regarding items 13 (CVI 0.73), 18 (CVI 0.79) and 30 (CVI 0.79) (Table 1)."
