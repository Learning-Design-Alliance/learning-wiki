## Evidence

### Orzeszko Z 2025

Orzeszko Z, Gach T, Necka S, Ochwat K, Major P, Szura M. (2025). The implementation of computer-aided detection in an initial endoscopy training improves the quality measures of trainees' future colonoscopies: a retrospective cohort study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11890-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null secondary outcome from the retrospective cohort: AADR was 5.1% (AI-trained) versus 4.6% (conventionally trained). AADR data quality was very low due to a 27% missing-data rate, with polyps not removed or patients lost to follow-up.

> "The impact on AADR was unremarkable (mean difference 0.5%; 95% CI − 0.6% to 1.6%), but the power of the analysis was low (14.5%)."
