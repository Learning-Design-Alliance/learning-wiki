## Evidence

### Spénard Sarah 2022

Spénard Sarah, Postlow Fabiana, Curran Vernon. (2022). E-learning use in the review of neonatal resuscitation program in physicians: a scoping review. Journal of Perinatology. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-022-01411-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-sectional study (N = 50 RNs, RTs, NNPs, neonatal consultants and fellows) using a multiple linear regression on RETAIN computer game performance: the association between recency of NRP course and tries needed was significant only in participants endorsing lower growth mindset; fewer mistakes were noted in participants with higher growth mindset.

> "there was a significant interaction between the last NRP course timing and growth mindset in predicting the number of attempts needed by healthcare providers during the game."
