## Evidence

### Polverini G and Gregorcic B 2024

Polverini G and Gregorcic B (2024). Evaluating vision-capable chatbots in interpreting kinematics graphs: a comparative study of free and subscription-based models. Front. Educ. 9:1452414. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2024.1452414

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pattern analysis across the eight chatbots' item-level TUG-K performance (RQ2), building on the authors' previous studies. Items 10 and 19, which ask for a strategy, showed the best overall performance, indicating "chatbots tend to be more successful when they are asked to deal with language rather than images."

> "tasks that require providing a strategy for solving the problems are, in general, easier for chatbots than those looking for an actual comparison or interpretation of graphs"
