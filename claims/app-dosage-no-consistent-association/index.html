James Kim 2021¶

James Kim, Joshua Gilbert, Qun Yu, Charles Gale. (2021). Measures Matter: A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Educational Apps on Preschool to Grade 3 Children's Literacy and Math Skills. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584211004183

RVE meta-regression Model 2 (Table 6) with dosage moderators; log2 duration coefficient −0.012 and log2 intensity 0.012, both nonsignificant. Log2 frequency was significant (0.126, p < .05), hence the authors call the evidence inconsistent.
