Evidence¶

Gambari 2014¶

Gambari, I. A., Ezenwa, V. I., & Anyanwu, R. C. (2014). Comparative Effects of Two Modes of Computer-Assisted Instructional Package on Solid Geometry Achievement. Contemporary Educational Technology, 5(2), 110-120. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105561

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pretest-posttest experimental control group study with 120 SSII students in Minna, Nigeria, randomly assigned to AT, AN or lecture conditions for six weeks. ANOVA gave F = 11.468, df = 117, p = 0.000, and post-hoc means were AN = 73.80 versus lecture = 54.43, with the "significant difference between CAI(AN) and the lecture method groups".

"the Scheffe's post-hoc test revealed a significant difference between CAI(AN) and the lecture method groups, favoring CAI(AN)"
