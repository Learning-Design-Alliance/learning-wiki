## Evidence

### Almomani Emad 2025

Almomani Emad, Tobin John, Fernandes Sonia, Sullivan Jacqueline, Saadeh Omar, Mustafa Emad, Pattison Natalie, Alinier Guillaume. (2025). A reflective learning conversation debriefing model for interprofessional simulation based education. BMC Medical Education, 25:1434. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07765-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group Friedman repeated-measures tests on CREST, LCJR, and CTR scores (n = 65 per group, per tool), reported in Results. All six tests were significant at p <0.001; the article does not print effect sizes.

> "The results, presented in Table 4, indicated statistically significant changes across the three observations for both the control and experimental groups on all three assessment tools."

### Almomani Emad 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' statement in the Discussion interpreting the diverging direction of the repeated-measures changes between the two debriefing groups; this is an author interpretation of the observed pattern, not a separately tested contrast.

> "In contrast, the control group demonstrated declines in performance over successive observations."
