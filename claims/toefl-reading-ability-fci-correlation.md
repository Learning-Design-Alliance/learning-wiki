## Evidence

### Allan L. Alinea and Wade Naylor 2014

Allan L. Alinea and Wade Naylor. (2014). Polarisation of physics on global courses. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/50/2/210](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/50/2/210)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Appendix correlational analysis of the cohort's TOEFL entry scores against FCI pre/post scores, shown in the Fig. A1 scatter plot; the article prints r² = 0.0048 (pre) and 0.00041 (post).

> "We checked to ﬁnd a very low correlation between pre/post test performance on the FCI and English ability (r2 = 0.0048/0.00041, respectively), see Fig. A1 , including students classed as native."

### Allan L. Alinea and Wade Naylor 2014 (2)

Allan L. Alinea and Wade Naylor. (2014). Polarisation of physics on global courses. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/50/2/210](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/50/2/210)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Same appendix analysis using TOEFL reading scores; the scatter plot shows two distinct groups (natives and non-natives) for non-natives in reading ability. The article prints r² = 0.076/0.097.

> "Perhaps not surprisingly we did ﬁnd a correlation with reading ability on pre/post FCI with r2 = 0.076/0.097, respectively. This is an order of magnitude larger than the total TOEFL score correlation."
