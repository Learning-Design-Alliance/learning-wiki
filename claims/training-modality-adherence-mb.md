## Evidence

### Diebold 2020

Diebold, A., Ciolino, J. D., Johnson, J. K., Yeh, C., Gollan, J. K., & Tandon, S. D. (2020). Comparing Fidelity Outcomes of Paraprofessional and Professional Delivery of a Perinatal Depression Preventive Intervention. Administration and Policy in Mental Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10488-020-01022-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analysis of the 160-session fidelity dataset using linear mixed models with training modality as a fixed effect. The Tukey-adjusted comparison of 1-on-1 MB training plus call versus recording of in-person training yielded a model-estimated difference of 18.7 (95% CI 0.2–37.2; p = 0.046).

> "Facilitators who were trained on the 1-on-1 modality of MB followed by a call had significantly greater adherence than facilitators who were trained by listening to a recording of an in-person training."
