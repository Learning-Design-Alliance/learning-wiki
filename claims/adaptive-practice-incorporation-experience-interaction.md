# Adaptive READS had no significant main effect on teachers' incorporation of READS-related practices into regular classroom practice, but the effect differed significantly by prior READS experience, positive for experienced teachers and statistically zero for novices

### Quinn 2016 (2)

Quinn, D. M., & Kim, J. S. (2016). Predictors and Moderators of Teacher Learning and Changes in Practice: Evidence from a Randomized Trial of a Teacher-Adapted Literacy Program. SREE Spring 2016 Conference abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567622

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same models with an Adaptive*READS Experience interaction (printed coefficient 0.812*, p<0.05, Table 4 column 2); the interaction is depicted graphically in Figure 2. The article prints the conditional effect as .57 sd, p=.05, a regression coefficient rather than a standardized effect size, so no effect-size magnitude is coded.

> "The effect of condition was negatively signed and statistically zero for teachers new to READS, and was positive and significant (.57 sd, p=.05) for teachers experienced with READS"
