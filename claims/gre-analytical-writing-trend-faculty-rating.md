## Evidence

### Burmeister Jay 2014

Burmeister Jay, McSpadden Erin, Rakowski Joseph, Nalichowski Adrian, Yudelev Mark, Snyder Michael. (2014). Correlation of admissions statistics to graduate student success in medical physics. Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics 15(1):375-385. https://doi.org/10.1120/jacmp.v15i1.4451

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective correlational analysis of 108 students; faculty rating was dichotomized at its median of 2.5 and analyzed with t-tests and logistic regression. "a clear trend was observed between faculty rating and GRE analytical writing score (p=0.064)"; no general GRE section reached significance.

> "While a clear trend was observed between faculty rating and GRE analytical writing score (p=0.064), none of the general GRE sections were statistically correlated with faculty rating."
