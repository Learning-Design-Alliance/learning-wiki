Cluster-stability analysis over the 135 learners with complete logs for both activities found "68% of the learners were classiﬁed in the same clus- ters across activities", which the authors read as students mainly using the same strategy across both environments.

### Jade Mai Cock · Ido Roll · Käser Tanja 2025 (2)

Jade Mai Cock · Ido Roll · Käser Tanja. (2025). Different Topics, Similar Patterns: The Consistency of Learners and Learning Across Interactive STEM Simulations. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00512-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of cluster transfer between activity 1 and activity 2 (Fig. 8) showed asymmetric switching: 85% of systematic starters remained systematic, while "only 61% of the learners starting off in the free cluster remained in the free cluster", with 39% moving to systematic.

> "We observe that 85% of the learners starting their ﬁrst activity with asystematic strategy also applied a systematic strategy during their second activity. Y et, only 61% of the learners starting off in the free cluster remained in the free cluster"
