## Subclaims

## Evidence

### da Silva Gisele Cabral 2025

da Silva Gisele Cabral, Campanharo Cássia Regina Vancini, Lopes Maria Carolina Barbosa Teixeira, Okuno Meiry Fernanda Pinto, Miura Carla Roberta Monteiro, Batista Ruth Ester Assayag. (2025). Clinical simulation and deliberate practice in rapid-cycle deliberate practice: a randomized clinical trial. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0632

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 134 fourth-year nursing students randomly assigned to SWSP (n=66) or RCDP (n=68) after a 40-minute lecture; Wilcoxon tests showed significant pre-to-post gains in both groups (P<0.001), and "there was no difference between the groups (p=1.0)".

> "Regarding knowledge, there was an improvement in the mean post-activity scores compared to pre-test scores, regardless of the strategy used: RCDP or SWSP (P<0.001) (Table 1)."
