Masud Mansuripur. (2018). Fourier Optics in the Classroom. Proceedings of the Optical Society of America's Imaging & Applied Optics Congress, Orlando, Florida. [doi:10.1364/3d.2018.jtu5e.3](https://doi.org/10.1364/3d.2018.jtu5e.3)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Author's account in the introduction of three decades of classroom use of optical system simulations at the University of Arizona; it is a descriptive practice report with no evaluation data, stating the simulations "can be used in the classroom to demonstrate" principles and applications.

> "computer simulations that show how Fourier optics, when combined with traditional methods of geometric optics (i.e., ray-tracing) and physical optics (e.g., exact solutions of Maxwell's equations), can be used in the classroom to demonstrate not only the physical principles but also real world applications of optical science and engineering"
