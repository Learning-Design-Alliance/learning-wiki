### Peiris D. L. I. H. K. 2022

Peiris D. L. I. H. K., Duan Yanping, Vandelanotte Corneel, Liang Wei, Yang Min, Baker Julien Steven. (2022). Effects of In-Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Children’s Academic Performance, Cognition, Health Behaviours and Health Outcomes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19159479

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of three RCT samples (n = 605 students) measuring MVPA with accelerometers. The review reports a "large significant pooled effect size" of SMD = 3.55 favoring intervention groups, with I2 = 97% and no publication bias by Egger's test (p = 0.238).

> "Physical activity breaks, which were implemented inside the classroom [1,9,10] indicated a large significant pooled effect size (n = 605 students; SMD = 3.55, 95% CI [3.29 to 3.81], p < 0.00001) favoring the intervention groups for their improved MVPA levels"
