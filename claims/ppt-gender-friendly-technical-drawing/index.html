Evidence¶

Gambari 2015¶

Gambari, A. I., Yusuf, H. T., & Balogun, S. A. (2015). Effectiveness of PowerPoint Presentation on Students' Cognitive Achievement in Technical Drawing. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 3(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085923

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA on the 45-student PPT group with pretest as covariate compared male and female posttest scores. The article reports "F (1, 42) = 1.697, p > .05", so hypothesis two was not rejected; no effect size is printed.

"The F value of 1.697 was not significant at the .05 alp ha level [ F (1, 42) = 1.697, p > .05)]. The result shows that there was no significant difference in the performance of male and female students taught using PPT."
