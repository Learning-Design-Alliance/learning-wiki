Evidence¶

Wang 2013¶

Wang, J., Lin, C.-F. C., Yu, W.-C. W., & Wu, E. (2013). Meaningful engagement in Facebook learning environments: Merging social and academic lives. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 14(1), Article 26. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1006268

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive survey results for the student-institution relationship subscale in the Facebook course sample. The article also reports 69.7% agreed the overall educational experience at the institution is excellent.

"Two-thirds of the respondents (74.2%) agreed that they felt connected with the university as a result of their conversations with classmates on Facebook."

Wang 2013 (2)¶

Wang, J., Lin, C.-F. C., Yu, W.-C. W., & Wu, E. (2013). Meaningful engagement in Facebook learning environments: Merging social and academic lives. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education-TOJDE, 14(1), Article 26. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1006268

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive survey results for the student-student interactions subscale. The article also reports 93.9% enjoyed chatting with friends and 75.7% indicated other students cared about them.

"Almost all of the students (95.5%) described their peers at this institution as very supportive and reported experiencing a strong sense of belonging."
