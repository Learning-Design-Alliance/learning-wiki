q2 · i? · associational · r?

National survey of chief academic officers, 2015 administration, on blended/hybrid course outcomes relative to face-to-face. A majority (ranging 54.7% in 2012 to 56.6% in 2015) rated blended outcomes the same as face-to-face.

"more consider blended learning outcomes to be superior to face -to-face instruction (35.6% this year) than inferior (13.9%) ."

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Survey item on the promise of blended versus fully online courses, tracked since 2003. The report states there has been little change in this belief over time, with 42.3% now supporting the statement.

"Academic leaders consistently rate the promise of blended or hybrid courses as superior to that of fully online courses ."
