Evidence¶

Chunliang Yang 2018¶

Chunliang Yang, Rosalind Potts and David R. Shanks. (2018). Enhancing learning and retrieval of new information: a review of the forward testing effect. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0024-y

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Pastötter et al.'s study in which TBI and healthy individuals studied three lists of line drawings; the quote gives the effect in both groups, attributed by the review to interim testing reducing memory deficits after traumatic brain injury.

"In the List 3 interim test, Pastötter et al. 21 obtained a forward testing effect in both TBI and healthy individuals, indicating that interim testing during learning can be used to reduce memory de ﬁcits in people with TBI"

Chunliang Yang 2018 (2)¶

Chunliang Yang, Rosalind Potts and David R. Shanks. (2018). Enhancing learning and retrieval of new information: a review of the forward testing effect. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0024-y

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Aslan and Bäuml's word-list study with adults, older children (average age = 8.8 years) and younger children (average age = 6.7 years); the quote gives the absent effect in younger children, which the authors speculated reflects their deficits in inhibiting PI since interim tests did not reduce PI accumulation.

"Aslan and Bäuml obtained a forward testing effect in adults and older children but not in younger children"
