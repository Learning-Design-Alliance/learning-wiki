## Evidence

### Burr Steven A 2013

Burr Steven A, Brodier Elizabeth, Wilkinson Simon. (2013). Delivery and use of individualised feedback in large class medical teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-63

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis of learners in years 2 and 3 (N = 44 for the revisit pie charts, Figure 3). A significant gender correlation (p = 0.012) was reported; no effect size was printed. In summary 77% of males did not revisit any lecture content at all, compared with 45% of females.

> "There was a significant correlation (p = 0.012) between gender and level of use. 32% of the total female learners reflected upon both the red and amber light learning objectives, and revisited lecture content of both to improve knowledge. However, no males used the report to this depth, with 23% only revisiting the lecture content of learning objectives lit red."
