Narrative comparison in Section 3.2.2 of models whose participants were limited to school administration, students and teachers. The review reports that HeadOn and SUPEP "improved knowledge and attitudes without corresponding behavioural change." No effect sizes are printed.

"school-only models like HeadOn [93] and SUPEP [88] improved knowledge and attitudes without corresponding behavioural change."
