Cassandra A. Paul and David J. Webb. (2026). Grades, equity and graduation rates: a quantitative analysis of the association of learning assistants with improved student outcomes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.27006](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.27006)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

This is the authors' extrapolation from the per-course multilevel WFD models, combining course-by-course estimates to give "304±31 student grades each year" that would move above the WFD level under universal LA use versus no LA use.

> "For a (hypothetical) situation where all classes in these 10 courses used LAs compared to a situation where none of the 10 courses used LAs, we estimate that 304±31 student grades each year would have moved above the WFD level."
