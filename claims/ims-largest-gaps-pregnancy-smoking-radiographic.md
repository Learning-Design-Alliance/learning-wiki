Comparative evaluation of knowledge levels and attitudes regarding periodontal health and periodontal disease among internal medicine physicians and dentists in Turkey: a cross-sectional survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09365-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pregnancy-domain comparison from the same cross-sectional survey (Table 4); the article also reports only 34.6% of IMS correctly rejected the mineral-transfer misconception versus 90.2% of dentists, and "All four items in this domain remained significant after Bonferroni correction".

> "Less than half of IMS participants (46.7%) recognized the increased risk of adverse pregnancy outcomes in women with periodontal disease, compared to 91% of dentists (p < 0.001)."

BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09365-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Medication/smoking domain comparison (Table 5); the radiographic examination item showed the most pronounced gap, with "only 37.4% of IMS participants answered correctly compared to 88.8% of dentists (p < 0.001)".

> "Only 59.8% of IMS participants knew that smoking accelerates periodontal destruction while suppressing gingival bleeding, compared to 97.7% of dentists (p < 0.001)."
