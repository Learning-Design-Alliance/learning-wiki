The Education Trust's analysis of 2018-19 Texas K-12 public and charter school state data compares student and teacher race/ethnicity, finding "Total Percentage of People of Color" of 72.5% among students versus 41.4% among teachers; Latino students (52.5%) are especially underrepresented among teachers (27.6%).
