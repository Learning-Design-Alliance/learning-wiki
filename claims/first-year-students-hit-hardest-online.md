## Evidence

### Danny Doucette 2021

Danny Doucette, Sonja Cwik, and Chandralekha Singh. (2021). "Everyone is new to this": Student reflections on different aspects of online learning. [doi:10.1119/10.0005795](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0005795)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured hour-long interviews with 37 students, coded for emergent topics separately from surveys. First-year students found the online environment made it much harder to adapt to college science learning.

> "While many of the ﬁrst-year students we interviewed found online lecture courses to be “not real”, ineﬀective and disappointing, students in their second year and beyond described a balance of advantages and disadvantages to studying their lecture-based science courses online."
