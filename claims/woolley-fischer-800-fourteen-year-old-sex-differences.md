Summary of Woolley and Fischer's report on "tests administered to 800 fourteen-year-old children" who were leaving school to work: girls superior in height, weight and co-ordination and somewhat superior in all mental tests; boys superior in strength, rapidity of motion and vital capacity. No effect size is printed.

> "In a very comprehensive Report of a series of tests administered to 800 fourteen-year-old children, Woolley and Fischer make the following generalizations on children leaving school to go to work: the girls are superior to the boys in height, weight, and perfection of co-ordination, while the boys are superior to the girls in strength, rapidity of motion, and vital capacity; the girls are somewhat superior to the boys in all the mental tests."
