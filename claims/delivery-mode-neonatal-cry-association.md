Journal of Health, Population, and Nutrition. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41043-026-01256-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate analysis reported with Fig. 1 in the same study (p = 0.130); a higher proportion of vaginally delivered neonates cried within 1–5 min but this was non-significant. Equivalence was not tested.

> "no statistically significant association was found between mode of delivery and the time taken for neonates to cry after birth."
