## Evidence

### Anders Bouwer 2010

Anders Bouwer, Bert Bredeweg. (2010). Graphical means for inspecting qualitative models of system behaviour. Instr Sci 38:173–208. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9083-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paper-based diagram-comprehension pre/post tests (DIA-A/DIA-B, counterbalanced) in the same 30-participant evaluation. Pre-test mean was "M = 67%, SD = 17.6%" and post-test "M = 73%, SD = 19.5%"; the article reports the difference was not significant and equivalence was not tested.

> "The scores on the pre-test were already reasonably high ( M = 67%, SD = 17.6%). There was a slight increase from pre-test to post-test ( M = 73%, SD = 19.5%), but this difference was not signiﬁcant."
