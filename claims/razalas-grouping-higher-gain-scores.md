## Evidence

### Salazar 2015

Salazar, D. A. (2015). Razalas' Grouping Method and Mathematics Achievement. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.8. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1082724

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental nonequivalent control group study with two existing Math 04 classes; Mann-Whitney Test on gain scores gave z=-1.79, significant at 0.05, with the experimental group showing "a high mean increase (14.33)" versus the control group's mean increase of 9.

> "Results of the Mann-Whitney Test revealed that the Razalas Method of Grouping raised the students' scores in the achievement test in terms of their gain scores as evidenced by accepting the alternative hypothesis"
