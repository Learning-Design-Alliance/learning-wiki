Evidence¶

Busmin Gurning & Aguslani Siregar 2017¶

Busmin Gurning & Aguslani Siregar. (2017). The Effect of Teaching Strategies and Curiosity on Students’ Achievement in Reading Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 10(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n11p191

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 76 grade XI students in two classes (38 each), taught over six meetings in five weeks, analyzed with two-way ANOVA at α = 0.05; the article reports "The students’ achievement in Reading Comprehension taught by using INSERT strategy was higher" with F obs (27.32) > F tab (3.98).
