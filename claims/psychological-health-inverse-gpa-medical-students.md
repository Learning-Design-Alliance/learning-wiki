### Taleb Ola K. 2023

Taleb Ola K., Sarimah Abdullah, Siti-Azrin Ab Hamid, Baharuddin Kamarul Aryffin, Abusafia Ali H. (2023). Structural Relationships between Environmental Factors, Psychological Health, and Academic Performance in Medical Students Engaged in Online Learning during the COVID-19 Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20021494

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM analysis of 207 undergraduate medical students using MLR estimation; model 1 fit the data well and was retained as final. The path from psychological health to GPA was the only significant one of nine hypothesized paths, with "β = −0.21, p = 0.004".

> "The results from Table 7 show that there was an inverse relationship between psychological health scores and academic performance based on GPA (β = −0.21, p = 0.004)."
