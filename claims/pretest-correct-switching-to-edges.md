## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 2006

Michael C. Wittmann, Jeffrey T. Morgan, Roger E. Feeley. (2006). Laboratory-Tutorial activities for teaching probability. Submitted to Physical Review Special Topics Physics Education Research. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.2.020104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Longitudinal analysis of the same students across the free cart pre-test and post-test. The authors interpret the switching as pattern matching to previously taught material and note that sketches counted as higher-at-edges were mostly parabolas or similar shapes.

> "We note that 8 of the 22 students who gave the correct answer on the pre-test switched to the incorrect answer of “higher at the edges” on the post-test. With two, it is clear they were pattern matching to the harmonic oscillator situation, while the sketches and explanations of the other six do not allow us to distinguish what guided their reasoning."
