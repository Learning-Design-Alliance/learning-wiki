### Kane 2008

Kane, M., Connell, J. E., & Pellecchia, M. (2008). A Quantitative Analysis of Language Interventions for Children with Autism. The Behavior Analyst Today, 11(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?q=A+Quantitative+Analysis+of+Language+Interventions+for+Children+with+Autism

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

PND generalization scores were calculated from seven studies with contrived generalization displays (35 scores) and six studies with naturalistic generalization displays (30 scores). The mean for contrived generalization was "87%" versus "72%" for naturalistic.

> "For generalization effects, the mean PND for contrived programming was 87%, and the mean PND for naturalistic programming was 72%."
