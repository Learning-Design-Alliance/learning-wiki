Scaling and linking description (Chapter 5) of the Spanish vertical RIT scale. The report states the scales were "statistically linked... based on a bilingual group design" because the two assessments measure "similar but not identical reading constructs," enabling cross-language score comparison.

"English and Spanish MAP Growth Reading assessments measure similar but not identical reading constructs, so NWEA statistically linked the scales of the English and Spanish versions of MAP Growth based on a bilingual group design to allow comparisons of student scores across assessments."
