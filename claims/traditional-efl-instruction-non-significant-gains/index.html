Evidence¶

Özgül Balcı 2017¶

Özgül Balcı. (2017). The Effects of Learning-Style Based Activities on Students' Reading Comprehension Skills and Self-Efficacy Perceptions in English Foreign Language Classes. Higher Education Studies, 7(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v7n4p35

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive comparison of control-group pre- and post-test scores in the quasi-experiment; the control group received routine teacher-centered instruction without attention to learning styles. No effect size is printed, so the increase is reported as untested for significance.

"The Reading Comprehension post-test scores showed an increase in the control group when compared to pre-test scores, though not statistically significant."
