Coca, V.M., Nagaoka, J., & Seeskin, A. (2017). Patterns of two-year and four-year college enrollment among Chicago Public Schools graduates. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same descriptive analysis of 2006–2015 CPS cohorts. The report reports the two-year share of enrollment fell from 33 to 29 percent and that since 2013 four-year rates rose while two-year rates "decreased slightly."

"two-year enrollment now accounts for a smaller share of overall college enrollment than it has in the past (e.g., 33 percent of all enrollment in 2006 and 29 percent of all enrollment in 2015)"

Coca 2017 (3)¶

Coca, V.M., Nagaoka, J., & Seeskin, A. (2017). Patterns of two-year and four-year college enrollment among Chicago Public Schools graduates. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Headcount analysis of the same CPS administrative enrollment data. The report notes the proportion enrolling in two-year colleges "dropped slightly" while absolute two-year enrollment grew because graduation numbers rose.

"the number of CPS graduates attending two-year colleges actually grew by 1,165 students (a 45 percent increase in enrollment), because CPS now produces more graduates"
