## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Akbaş 2011

Akbaş, Y., & Gençtürk, E. (2011). The Effect of Conceptual Change Approach to Eliminate 9th Grade High School Students' Misconceptions about Air Pressure. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 11(4), 2217-2222. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ962696

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental non-equivalent control group design with two intact 9th-grade classes (n=45 each); t-test on concept test scores showed "a meaningful difference between the groups" favoring the experiment group.

> "The experiment group students' test score average is X exp = 7.15, control group students' test score average is X control = 2.97. Also there is a meaningful difference between the groups based on test results ( p < 0,05)."
