Evidence¶

Braaten EB 2020¶

Braaten EB, Ward AK, Forchelli G, Vuijk PJ, Cook NE, McGuinness P, Lee BA, Samkavitz A, Lind H, O'Keefe SM, Doyle AE. (2020). Characteristics of child psychiatric outpatients with slow processing speed and potential mechanisms of academic impact. European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-019-01455-w

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation modeling in the referred cohort tested PS effects on WIAT Word Reading, controlling for age, sex, and medication. The indirect path through WM was significant; the direct effect was not (ß = .02, z = 0.57, p = .57).

"a significant indirect effect for PS on Word Reading was found, mediated by WM (ß = .13, z = 6.53, p < .001). This indirect effect accounted for 86.7% of the total effect (ß = .15, z = 3.93, p < .001) of PS on Word Reading. No significant direct effect of slow PS on Word Reading was found"

Braaten EB 2020 (2)¶

Braaten EB, Ward AK, Forchelli G, Vuijk PJ, Cook NE, McGuinness P, Lee BA, Samkavitz A, Lind H, O'Keefe SM, Doyle AE. (2020). Characteristics of child psychiatric outpatients with slow processing speed and potential mechanisms of academic impact. European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-019-01455-w

q3 · i? · associational · r3

A second SEM modeled PS effects on WIAT Numerical Operations. The indirect effect through WM accounted for 43.3% of the total effect (ß = .30, z = 8.28, p < .001) and the direct effect the remaining 56.7%.

"there was a significant indirect effect of PS that was mediated by WM (ß = .13, z = 6.55, p < .001) as well as a significant direct effect of slow PS on Numerical Operations (ß = .17, z = 5.20, p < .001)."
