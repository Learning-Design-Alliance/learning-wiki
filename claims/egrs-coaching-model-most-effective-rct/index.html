Related Claims¶

  • Pairing coaching with group trainings is associated with larger effects on instruction and achievement; instructional materials with larger, and video libraries with smaller, effects on instruction — a broader claim this one bears on
  • A design flaw in the EGRS lesson plans is that independent learner tasks stay the same for the whole year, so growing learner proficiency is not factored in and classroom disruptions increase — related
