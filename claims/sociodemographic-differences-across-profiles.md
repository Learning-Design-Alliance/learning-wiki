## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, P., Yu, G., Xu, R., Cao, S., Lin, M., Liu, Y., & Wu, Y. (2026). Factors associated with medication safety competence among clinical nurses in China: A latent profile analysis. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000749

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Chi-square and ANOVA comparisons of sociodemographic variables across the four profiles (N=1,228, Table 3). For example, medication error history χ2=22.074, p<.001; self-perceived nursing achievement χ2=206.979, p<.001.

> "Significant differences were found across profiles in critical thinking total score, age, working years, professional title, medication error history, self-perceived nursing achievement, and professional interest in nursing (all p<.05)."
