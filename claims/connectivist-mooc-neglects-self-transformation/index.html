Markus Deimann and Robert Farrow 2013¶

Markus Deimann and Robert Farrow. (2013). Rethinking OER and their Use: Open Education as Bildung. International Review of Research in Open and Distance Learning, Vol 14 No 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1017477 doi:10.19173/irrodl.v14i3.1370

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical critique of the connectivist four-step model (aggregation, remixing, repurposing, feeding forward) attributed to Downes, Siemens, and Cormier. The article argues this approach "neglects to describe the specific procedure" of personal self-transformation; it is an interpretive claim without data.

"However, being focused on the distributed nature of knowledge and cognition this approach neglects to describe the specific procedure that takes place during the process of personal self- transformation."
