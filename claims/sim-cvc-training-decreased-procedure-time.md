## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Median task-trainer CVC placement procedure time decreased significantly after training, from 264 seconds to 146 seconds. [→ Good Ryan J. MD 2022](#good-ryan-j-md-2022)

## Evidence

### Good Ryan J. MD 2022

Good Ryan J. MD, Mashburn Danielle MPAS, PA-C, Jekich Erika MS, PA-C, Miller Kristen MS, Leroue Matthew K. MD, Woods Jason MD, Czaja Angela S. MD, MSc, PhD. (2022). Simulation-Based Training for Ultrasound-Guided Central Venous Catheter Placement in Pediatric Patients. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11276

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Skill was assessed by timing CVC placement on the task trainer before and after the session in a convenience sample of learners. Median time fell from "a median procedure time of 264 seconds" to 146 seconds (p < .001, n = 20). No effect size was printed.

> "Learner skill in CVC placement also improved significantly after participation, from a median procedure time of 264 seconds before (IQR: 182-398 seconds) to a median procedure time of 146 seconds after (IQR: 134-205 seconds, p < .001 by Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed rank test, n = 20"
