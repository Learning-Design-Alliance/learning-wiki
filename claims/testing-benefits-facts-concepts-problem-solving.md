# Classroom testing benefits facts (g = 0.524), concept learning (g = 0.644), and knowledge application in problem solving (g = 0.453).

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` Testing promotes not only fact learning but also conceptual learning and application of knowledge to solve new problems, countering the drill-and-kill criticism. [→ Yang 2020](#yang-2020)

## Evidence

### Yang 2020

Yang, C., Luo, L., Vadillo, M. A., Yu, R., & Shanks, D. R. (2020). Testing (Quizzing) Boosts Classroom Learning: A Systematic and Meta-Analytic Review. Psychological Bulletin. https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000309

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analytic analysis coding exam content into fact learning, concept learning, and problem-solving categories; the review reports printed effect sizes g = 0.524, g = 0.644, and g = 0.453 for the three levels, addressing the criticism that retrieval practice only enhances "inert knowledge".

> "Testing is not only beneficial for learning facts ( g /H110050.524), but also promotes conceptual learning (g /H110050.644) and facilitates knowledge application in the service of problem solving ( g /H110050.453)."
