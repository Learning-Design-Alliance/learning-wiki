Sun Yanxia 2017¶

Sun Yanxia, Pan Chuxiong, Li Tianzuo, Gan Tong J. (2017). Airway management education: simulation based training versus non-simulation based training-A systematic review and meta-analyses. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-017-0313-7

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Narrative synthesis of complication outcomes across seven studies; pooling was deemed impossible due to heterogeneous reporting of outcomes and methods. Three studies reporting complications such as pain, bleeding and esophageal intubation found no statistically significant differences.

"Three studies [13, 19, 20] reported procedure-related complications (e.g. pain, bleeding, esophageal intubation) and found no statistically significant differences in complications between SBT and NSBT groups."
