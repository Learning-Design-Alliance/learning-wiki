Secondhand report of Lindsay & Hart (2017): same-race teacher pairing reduced suspension rates for African American males "by as much as 15 to 18 percent, depending on the gender of the teacher"; descriptive percentage, no standardized effect size printed.

"pairing African American students with a teacher of the same race led to a decrease in suspension rates for African American males by as much as 15 to 18 percent, depending on the gender of the teacher"

q2 · i? · review · r1

Secondhand report of Gershenson et al. (2017) finding that exposure to one African American elementary teacher reduced dropout rates for low-income Black men by nearly 40 percent; descriptive percentage, no standardized effect size printed.

"One study in North Carolina found that having one African American teacher in elementary school would reduce the dropout rate of low-income black men by nearly 40 percent (Gershenson, et al., 2017)."

q2 · i? · review · r2

Secondhand report of Egalite et al. (2015) finding African American students in Boston performed better on state math and reading tests with an African American teacher versus a white teacher; no effect size is printed.

"African American students performed better on state math and reading tests if they had an African American teacher compared with African American students with white teachers (Egalite, et al., 2015)."
