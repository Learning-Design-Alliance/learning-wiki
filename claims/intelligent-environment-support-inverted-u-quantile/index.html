Quantile regression result from the same 3003-teacher survey, reported in the results section. The article reports the inverted U-shaped pattern of the cross-sectional association; no effect size is printed.

"the estimated association of school-based intelligent environment support with innovative competence followed an inverted U-shaped pattern, with stronger estimated associations at medium-to-low levels of innovative competence."
