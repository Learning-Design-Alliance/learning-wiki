## Evidence

### Gaupp R 2019

Gaupp R, Dinius J, Drazic I, Körner M. (2019). Long-term effects of an e-learning course on patient safety: A controlled longitudinal study with medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210947

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures analysis of the GAPSQ error reporting confidence subscale in the controlled quasi-experimental study; the test was non-significant (F(2, 85) = 0.160, p = .852). No effect size is printed, so the null is reported as a non-detected effect.

> "We also reject the disclosure hypothesis (iv), as the e-learning did not show any effect on error disclosing, neither in the follow-up data (T2), nor directly after the intervention(T2) (F(2, 85) = 0.160, p = .852)."
