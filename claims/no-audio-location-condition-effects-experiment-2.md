## Evidence

### Manuela Glaser 2025

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered three-condition between-subjects experiment (n = 123 analyzed) keeping externalization constant while varying audio-visuo-spatial contiguity and audio-spatial cueing. One-tailed Kruskal–Wallis test; means were close (.45, .46, .44).

> "A Kruskal–Wallis test regarding the memory of audio explanation details about inventors showed no significant differences between the conditions, H (2) = .51, p = .389, r = .05."

### Manuela Glaser 2025 (2)

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-tailed ANOVA on invention-detail recall in Experiment 2 (n = 123); means .41, .44, .44 across close, distant I and distant II. No significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "A one-factorial analysis of variance (ANOVA) regarding the memory of the audio explanation details about inventions, too, showed no significant differences between the conditions, F(2, 120) = .34, p = .358, ηp2 = .01."

### Manuela Glaser 2025 (3)

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-tailed Kruskal–Wallis test on the text-picture integration test in Experiment 2 (n = 123), with the Douglas Engelbart item omitted. No significant difference between the three audio-location conditions.

> "A Kruskal–Wallis test showed no significant difference between the conditions, H(2) = .97, p = .308, r = .09."
