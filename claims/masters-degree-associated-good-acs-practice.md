# Nurses with a master's degree had almost four times higher odds of good practice regarding ACS care than nurses with a bachelor degree (AOR=4.258)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In multivariable logistic regression, the odds of good practice regarding ACS care were about almost four times higher for master's degree holders than for nurses with a bachelor degree (AOR=4.258, 95% CI 1.676–10.820, P=0.002). [→ Lakew Bizuayehu Ashine 2023](#lakew-bizuayehu-ashine-2023)

## Evidence

### Lakew Bizuayehu Ashine 2023

Suboptimal Knowledge and Care of Patients with Acute Coronary Syndrome Among Nurses Working in Selected Hospitals in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia 2021. Vascular Health and Risk Management. https://doi.org/10.2147/VHRM.S422339

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the same cross-sectional survey (N=252); educational level was significantly associated with good ACS care practice, with the article reporting "AOR=4.258, (95% CI:1.676–10.820), P=0.002" for master's versus bachelor degree holders.

> "the odds of nurses having master's degrees were found about almost four times higher than those having good practice regarding care of ACS (AOR=4.258, (95% CI:1.676–10.820), P=0.002) than nurses having a bachelor degree"
