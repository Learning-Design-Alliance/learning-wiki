## Evidence

### Axboe Mette K. 2016

Axboe Mette K., Christensen Kaj S., Kofoed Poul-Erik, Ammentorp Jette. (2016). Development and validation of a self-efficacy questionnaire (SE-12) measuring the clinical communication skills of health care professionals. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0798-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis using principal factor method with oblique rotation on responses from 292 clinicians; the screeplot showed one factor accounting for 87.7% of responses, so "the scale is, therefore, unidimensional, which allows summarization of items."

> "The result, which was based on examination of eigenvalues, loadings, and screen plots, showed a single dominant factor, indicating that the 12 self-efficacy items correlated highly with each other."
