## Subclaims

## Evidence

### lit-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, December). Dual language programs. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/726

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

The WWC's synthesis of the Steele et al. (2017) randomized controlled trial with low attrition, rated meets standards without reservations, reports a literacy effect size of 0.14 and improvement index +5, statistically significant at "Yes (p=0.02)". The rating is potentially positive effects because it rests on one study.

> "End of 6 years of implementation (Steele et al., 2017) 844 students in grade 5 Multiple schools in Portland, Oregon 0.14 +5 Yes (p=0.02)"
