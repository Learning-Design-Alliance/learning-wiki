## Evidence

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer and Jérôme Prado 2023

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer and Jérôme Prado. (2023). Arithmetic skills are associated with left fronto-temporal gray matter volume in 536 children and adolescents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00201-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory surface-based morphometry analysis using the CAT12 pipeline on the same n = 536 sample with the same covariates as the main VBM analysis. The article reports the analysis "did not show any relation between arithmetic score and CT" at the printed corrected thresholds.

> "This analysis did not show any relation between arithmetic score and CT (voxel-level threshold of p ≤ 0.001, cluster-level threshold of p ≤ 0.05, family-wise error corrected for multiple comparisons)."
