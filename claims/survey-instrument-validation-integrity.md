## Evidence

### Innes SI 2022

Innes SI, Goncalves G, Leboeuf-Yde C. (2022). Who are the chiropractic students favouring a limitless scope of practice? Exploring the relationship with personality, magical thinking, and academic achievement. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-022-00440-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Data-quality checks in the same survey: de-identified fourth-year anatomy results (N = 59) were compared with self-reported scores (N = 42), and the authors judged that "the self-reported highest anatomy scores were found to closely approximate the actual results". No effect sizes are printed.

> "The Cronbach Alpha for scales for the three inventories were good for IU-12 (0.87) and MHB (0.85) and acceptable for ChiroCon (0.74)"
