Theme 5 (Ethics in Practice) of the focus-group study. Staff compared paid AI access to disparities in affording books, framing equity of access as essential to responsible adoption alongside critical questioning of AI outputs.

"One participant warned that “the student that has access to and has paid for the upgraded version of ChatGPT, definitely has an advantage over the person who is using the free version… you’re making it a tiered education system”"
