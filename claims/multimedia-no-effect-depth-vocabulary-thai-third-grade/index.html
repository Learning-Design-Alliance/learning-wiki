Evidence¶

Jingjit 2015¶

Mathukorn Jingjit. (2015). The Effects of Multimedia Learning on Thai Primary Pupils' Achievement in Size and Depth of Vocabulary Knowledge. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.32. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083500

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same 42-pupil time-series quasi-experiment; depth was measured with a no-picture vocabulary test of 10 items requiring word meaning, other-word knowledge, and word relationships. The article reports a non-significant ANOVA; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"The Anova evidence that pupils' achievement obtaine d from T1, T2, and T3 is not significant different (p-value = 0.335), meaning that the multimedia lear ning, in this study, is not effective in promoting pupils' achievement in depth of vocabulary knowledge."
