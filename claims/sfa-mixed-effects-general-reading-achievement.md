### Success for All® 2017

Success for All®. (2017). WWC Intervention Report: Beginning Reading. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/672

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The WWC general reading achievement synthesis covered six studies including 2,574 students in 42 schools. Only Madden et al. (1993) showed a substantively important positive effect; the other five studies, including Skindrud and Gersten (2006), were indeterminate.

> "In the six studies that reported findings, the estimated impact of the intervention on outcomes in the general reading achievement domain was positive and substantively important for one study and indeterminate for five studies."
