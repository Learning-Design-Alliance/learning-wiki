Evidence¶

workshop-1¶

Wiley, K., Neisler, J., & Means, B. (2023, February). Partnering to promote equity and digital learning. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/167

q2 · i? · design · r2

Executive-summary finding from the 15-month RPP, drawing on team check-in recordings and evaluator interviews. The report reports that workshops with "concrete mechanisms for integrating equity practices" changed fall-term instructor practice and spring 2022 plans; no effect size is printed.

"Timely RPP workshops featuring concrete mechanisms for integrating equity practices (e.g., Equitizing the Syllabus and Soliciting Early Student Feedback) influenced instructor practice in the fall term as well as their plans for spring 2022."
