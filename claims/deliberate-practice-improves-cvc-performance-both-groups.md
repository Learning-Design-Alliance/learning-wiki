## Evidence

### de la Fuente Rene 2025

de la Fuente Rene, Galvez-Yanjari Victor, Delfino Alejandro, Lira Ricardo, Hurtado Claudia, Munoz-Gama Jorge, Sepúlveda Marcos. (2025). Process-oriented feedback for ultrasound-guided central venous access training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-025-03341-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In this RCT, within-group Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons showed posttest improvement in both groups; e.g., GRS pre-vs-post P value was 0.01 in each group and post execution time improved (P = 0.02 in POG).

> "At the end of the training (POST), both groups showed improved performance in the ultrasound-guided central venous catheter placement compared with their pretraining performance (PRE) (Table 1)."

### de la Fuente Rene 2025 (2)

de la Fuente Rene, Galvez-Yanjari Victor, Delfino Alejandro, Lira Ricardo, Hurtado Claudia, Munoz-Gama Jorge, Sepúlveda Marcos. (2025). Process-oriented feedback for ultrasound-guided central venous access training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-025-03341-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Before the process-oriented interventions, after web-based cognitive training, the randomized groups were comparable: "no differences in the checklist scores, GRS score, execution times, or process indicators" (e.g., pre GRS P = 0.73).

> "There were no differences in the checklist scores, GRS score, execution times, or process indicators (Table 1)."
