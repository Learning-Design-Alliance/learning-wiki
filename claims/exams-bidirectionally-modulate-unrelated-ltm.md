## Subclaims

## Evidence

### P. Lopes da Cunha 2018

P. Lopes da Cunha, D. Ramirez Butavand, L. B. Chisari, F. Ballarini and H. Viola (2018). Exams at classroom have bidirectional effects on the long-term memory of an unrelated graphical task. npj Science of Learning 3:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0036-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Classroom study of 631 students aged 12-17 in six Buenos Aires schools; students copied Rey-Osterrieth's figure and were tested 24 h later, with a single curricular exam occurring at times between -4 h and +4 h relative to copying. The article states exams "could improve or reduce the retention of temporarily associated content."

> "Our results show that exams are not innocuous and that they could improve or reduce the retention of temporarily associated content."
