### Baldwin 1911

Baldwin, B. T. (1911). Educational Psychology at the Cleveland Meeting of the American Psychological Association. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r1`

The report's concluding author position, offered without new data: general psychology "furnishes the substratum" on which educational psychology and experimental education must be built with equal detail of experimental procedure. The article presents this as belief, not a tested result.

> "the writer believes it is neither possible nor desirable to try to formulate or teach educational psychology without a substantial background in the science which is the source of our data and methods."
