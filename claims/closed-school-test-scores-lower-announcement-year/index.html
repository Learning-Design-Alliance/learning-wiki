Gordon 2018¶

Gordon, M.F., de la Torre, M., Cowhy, J.R., Moore, P.T., Sartain, L.S., & Knight, D. (2018). School closings in Chicago: Staff and student experiences and academic outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-closings-chicago-staff-and-student-experiences-and-academic-outcomes

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental comparison of students from closed schools with students in similar unaffected schools using CPS administrative data. The report states scores were "roughly one and a half months behind in reading and two months behind in math" in the announcement-year spring, despite similar fall and winter NWEA performance.
