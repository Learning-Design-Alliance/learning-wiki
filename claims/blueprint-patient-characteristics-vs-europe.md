## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The blueprint's age distribution (12% aged 0–14, 61% aged 15–64, 28% above 65) is older than the 2021 European general population, which the authors attribute to elderly people seeking care more frequently. [→ Mayer Anja 2022](#mayer-anja-2022)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive comparison of blueprint patient-related criteria against WHO and EUROSTAT population statistics, reported in Results section 3.2 and Table 2. The article reports the blueprint's age bands and the European comparison figures.

> "Compared to the general population in Europe in 2021 (15% between 0–14, 64% between 15 and 64, and 21% above 65) [50], our VPs are older, considering that elderly people tend to seek medical care more frequently than the average population."

International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19106175

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive comparison in Results section 3.2 of disability and migration-background representation against European statistics. The article reports 8% disabled VPs against an estimated 6–10% European prevalence, and 13% with migration background.

> "In our blueprint, we have 8% of disabled VPs, compared to an estimated prevalence of 6 to 10% for disabilities in Europe [57]."
