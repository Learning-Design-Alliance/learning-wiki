## Evidence

### Portes 2016

Portes, P. R., Gonzalez Canche, M. S., & Stollberg, R. (2016). Early RCT Findings for ELL Elementary Student Learning Outcomes After a Two-Year Pedagogical Intervention. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567010

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster RCT of the IC pedagogy; OLS analysis of standardized CRCT scores for 1,527 students nested in randomly assigned classrooms across two cohorts. The article reports "treated students performed better in English Language Arts" with β = .120, p<.05 (Table 2 shows Treatment 0.120, SE 0.052). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "More specifically, treated students performed better in English Language Arts than their control peers for the entire sample and across cohorts (β = .120, p<.05)."
