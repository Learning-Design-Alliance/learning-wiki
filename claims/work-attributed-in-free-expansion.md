### Mary Jane Brundage 2024

Investigating introductory and advanced students’ difficulties with change in internal energy, work and heat transfer using a validated instrument. Originally published 1991. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.03795](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.03795)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey analysis of items 22, 49, and 71 involving irreversible spontaneous processes for which W = 0 (Table IV). The paper reports that on item 22 “correct-response rates were much lower” than on item 49, where almost three-quarters of introductory students answered correctly.

> "However, on the free-expansion process, item 22, correct-response rates were much lower: Int-calc, 37%; Int-alg, 39%, and Upper-level, 63%."
