## Evidence

### Liu et al. 2012

Yuan-Chen Liu, Wan-Chun Lee, Tzu-Hua Huang, Hsiao-Mei Hsieh. (2012). IMPROVING STUDENTS’ CHINESE WRITING ABILITIES IN TAIWAN WITH THE “CONDITIONED WRITING SYSTEM”. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 11(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ989211

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test comparison in the quasi-experiment (experimental n=32, control n=35). The article reports no significant pre-test difference for argumentation (t = 1.278, p = .206) and states no significant difference was found for organization or language use either.

> "prior to the experiment the t-value of the protest score of both the experimental group and the control group in “argumentation” is 1.278 and the p-value is .206>.05, which did not indicate a significant difference."
