### Tudge 1986

Tudge, Jon. (1986). Beyond Conflict: The Role of Reasoning in Collaborative Problem Solving. Paper presented at the Annual Symposium of the Jean Piaget Society. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED275395

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article's concluding interpretation of its own results, contrasting with the Piagetian inference that conflict leads to development. It argues conservers regress only temporarily when paired with adult nonconservation models.

> "It does not, however, lead solely to development, as they infer; on the contrary, depending on the situation, it is as likely to lead to regression as to development."
