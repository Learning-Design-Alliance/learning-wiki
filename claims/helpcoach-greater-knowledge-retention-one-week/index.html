Hyoungwook Jin 2026¶

Hyoungwook Jin, Weirui Peng, Jieun Han, Q. Vera Liao, and Xu Wang. (2026). HelpCoach: Scaffolding Targeted AI Help-Seeking During Problem-Solving. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28918 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.28918

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

In the between-subjects lab study (n=40), participants repeated the same six-item knowledge test one week later (Stage 9). The article reports greater knowledge retention one week later with a printed effect size of Cohen's d = 1.100 (p = .005).

"HelpCoachalso led to greater knowledge retention one week later ( 𝑝=. 005, Cohen's 𝑑= 1.100), and participants' comments suggested broader benefits for self-regulation and metacognition."
