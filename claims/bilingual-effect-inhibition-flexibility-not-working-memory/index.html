Giovannoli J, Martella D, Federico F, Pirchio S and Casagrande M (2020) The Impact of Bilingualism on Executive Functions in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review Based on the PRISMA Method. Front. Psychol. 11:574789. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.574789

Systematic review (PRISMA) of 53 studies with bilingual and monolingual groups aged 5-17 and at least one executive function measure. The review's own synthesis states the effect "seems to appear when assessing inhibition and cognitive ﬂexibility, but to disa ppear when working memory is considered." No pooled effect size is printed.
