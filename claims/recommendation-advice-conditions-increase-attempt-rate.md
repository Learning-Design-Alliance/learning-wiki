## Subclaims
`q3 i?` All conditions of the recommendation variation and the advice prompt condition showed statistically significant attempt-rate increases over control. [→ Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy 2022](#angela-zavaleta-bernuy-2022)

## Evidence

### Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy 2022

Angela Zavaleta-Bernuy, Ziwen Han, Hammad Shaikh, Qi Yin Zheng, Lisa-Angelique Lim, Anna Rafferty, Andrew Petersen, and Joseph Jay Williams. 2022. How can Email Interventions Increase Students’ Completion of Online Homework? A Case Study Using A/B Comparisons. In LAK22: 12th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK22), March 21–25, 2022, Online, USA. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3506860.3506874

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two independent samples proportions z-tests on attempt rate (homework attempts as Bernoulli variables) from the week 7 and week 8 randomized deployments. The article reports "statistically significant (p<.05) attempt rate increases" for recommendation (est. 8.1% no prompt / 9.3% showing the prompt) and advice (est. 8.1%). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "We note statistically significant (p<.05) attempt rate increases for all conditions of recommendation over control (est. 8.1% no prompt/9.3% showing the prompt) and advice (control vs. showing the prompt, est. 8.1%)."
