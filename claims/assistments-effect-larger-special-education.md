## Evidence

### Koedinger 2010

Koedinger, K. R., McLaughlin, E. A., & Heffernan, N. T. (2010). A quasi-experimental evaluation of an on-line formative assessment and tutoring system. Journal of Educational Computing Research, 43(4), 489-510. https://doi.org/10.2190/EC.43.4.d

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Simple main effects analysis of the same ANCOVA on adjusted 2007 MCAS means: special education students gained 5.7 points (47.5% vs. 41.9%) while regular students gained 0.9 points (55.8% vs. 54.9%), "a 5.7% gain" versus 0.9%.

> "the treatment difference for special education students is statistically significant,F(1, 1235) = 11.44,p < .001, and the treatment effect is medium ( d = .50). The difference for regular students does not reach significance, F(1, 1235) = 1.16, p = .28, d = .08."
