## Evidence

### Wang P 2025

Wang P, Wu Z, Zhang J, He Y, Jiang M, Zheng J, Wang Z, Yang Y, Chen Y, Zhu J. (2025). Distance Learning During the COVID-19 Lockdown and Self-Assessed Competency Development Among Radiology Residents in China: Cross-Sectional Survey. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/54228

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Independent-samples 2-tailed t tests comparing SEM scores of 1699 distance learners and 682 nondistance learners. The article reports distance learners "outperformed in all subcompetencies" except PC-2 (P=.09); Mann-Whitney tests showed similar results.

> "The average score of participants who received distance learning was 3.46 (SD 1.49), higher than those who did not (mean 3.13, SD 1.39; P<.001)."
