### Hayden Robert 2026

Hayden Robert, Blanco Maria, Ramesh Saradha, Epstein Scott, Trowbridge Robert. (2026). Diagnostic reasoning with and without AI: automation bias in pre-clerkship medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09979-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A pilot study using a single-group pre-post design with 185 second-year pre-clerkship medical students found that "65 students (35%) improved their overall composite diagnostic score following AI use, whereas 26 students (14%) declined and 94 (51%) showed no change" (Wilcoxon signed-rank Z = -4.21, P < .001).
