## Evidence

### Chahal Baldev 2023

Chahal Baldev, Aydin Abdullatif, Ahmed Kamran. (2023). Virtual reality vs. physical models in surgical skills training. An update of the evidence. Current Opinion in Urology. https://doi.org/10.1097/MOU.0000000000001145

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A comparative study of vesico-urethral anastomosis tasks in robotic simulation reported that "performance metrics used exhibited moderate to strong correlations between the two modalities", supporting cross-platform skill transfer.

> "The performance metrics used exhibited moderate to strong correlations between the two modalities, thereby indicating skill transferability."

### Chahal Baldev 2023 (2)

Chahal Baldev, Aydin Abdullatif, Ahmed Kamran. (2023). Virtual reality vs. physical models in surgical skills training. An update of the evidence. Current Opinion in Urology. https://doi.org/10.1097/MOU.0000000000001145

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A trainee feedback assessment study at a urology simulation boot camp using the TURPMentor and Samed simulators "suggested an additive effect of VR and low-fidelity dry-lab models" for transurethral resection skill development.

> "One comparative study [11] suggested an additive effect of VR and low-fidelity dry-lab models in enhancing skill development for transurethral resections."
