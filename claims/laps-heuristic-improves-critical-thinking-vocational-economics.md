## Evidence

### Desi Anggrianto 2016

Desi Anggrianto, Madziatul Churiyah, Mohammad Arief. (2016). Improving Critical Thinking Skills Using Learning Model Logan Avenue Problem Solving (LAPS)-Heuristic. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095745

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with class X APK students at SMK Negeri 1 Ngawi: 32 experimental and 31 control students, assigned by simple random sampling. Independent sample t-test on post-test critical thinking scores gave significance 0.001; mean scores were 6.7559 versus 5.4761, "This shows that H 0 is rejected."

> "Based on the result of hypothesis testing can be seen in Table 10 that significance < 0,05 is 0.001 <0 .05. This shows that H 0 is rejected."
