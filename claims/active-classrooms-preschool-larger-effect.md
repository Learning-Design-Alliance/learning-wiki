## Evidence

### Bedard Chloe 2019

Bedard Chloe, St John Laura, Bremer Emily, Graham Jeffrey D., Cairney John. (2019). A systematic review and meta-analysis on the effects of physically active classrooms on educational and enjoyment outcomes in school age children. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0218633

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup meta-analysis by grade. Six preschool studies (cluster-adjusted n = 122) pooled to SMD = 1.05; ten primary-school studies (n = 666) pooled to SMD = 0.21; the single middle-school study (n = 63) showed SMD = -0.01 with a CI including no effect. The X2 test of subgroup differences was statistically significant.

> "the pooled estimate shows an increase in academic performance (SMD = 1.05) with CI excluding no effect following active classroom lessons compared to traditional classrooms. The pooled estimate from ten studies looking at children in primary school (cluster adjusted n = 666) shows a smaller increase in academic performance (SMD = 0.21)"
