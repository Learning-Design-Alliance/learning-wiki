Hartikainen 2026¶

Hartikainen, S., Nokelainen, P., Puhakka, I., & Kangaslampi, R. (2026). Emotions in flipped and lecture-based math courses. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102474

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel binomial logistic regression (Table 3, Model 3) on 546 learning events from 69 students, with individual learning as reference. The printed coefficient for the teacher-led vs. individual within-student predictor is "0.49 [0.03, 0.95]", a confidence interval excluding zero; no standardised effect size is printed.

"Teacher-led vs. individual learning (cwc) 0.49 [0.03, 0.95]"

Hartikainen 2026 (2)¶

Hartikainen, S., Nokelainen, P., Puhakka, I., & Kangaslampi, R. (2026). Emotions in flipped and lecture-based math courses. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102474

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Table 3 Model 4i prints the within-student teacher-led × teaching implementation interaction (B = -1.31, 95% CI [-2.23, -0.38], excluding zero), supporting the abstract's statement that the teacher-led advantage held "particularly in the lecture-based group". The model reports separate interactions for the peer-versus-individual and teacher-led-versus-individual contrasts; this coefficient is the teacher-led one.

"The teaching implementation × teacher-led (cwc) interaction was negative (B =-1.31, 95% CI [-2.23, -0.38], p = .006; OR = 0.27, 95% CI [0.11, 0.68])"
