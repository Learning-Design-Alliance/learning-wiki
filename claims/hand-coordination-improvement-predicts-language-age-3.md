`q2 i3` Among 3-year-olds, children who improve in hand coordination are 36.4 times more likely to improve in language (OR = 36.4, p = 0.005) and 15.66 times more likely to improve in self-awareness (OR = 15.66, p = 0.02) than those who do not.
