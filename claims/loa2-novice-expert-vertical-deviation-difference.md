## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Novices showed greater vertical platform deviation (0.373 ± 0.566 mm vs. 0.255 ± 0.438 mm, p = 0.007) and vertical apex deviation (0.348 ± 0.488 mm vs. 0.249 ± 0.437 mm, p = 0.009) than experts, while lateral and angular deviations did not differ significantly. ### Wang Miao 2025

Evaluating the relationship between clinician experience and accuracy in robotic-assisted dental implant placement: a retrospective study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07389-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective comparison of 585 implants placed by six novices versus two experts using the Remebot LOA2 system; Wilcoxon rank-sum tests on seven deviation parameters. The article reports novices showed greater vertical deviations, differences it calls small in clinical terms. No effect size is printed.

> "novices showed greater vertical deviations: vertical platform deviation was 0.373 ± 0.566 mm (novices) vs. 0.255 ± 0.438 mm (experts; p = 0.007), and vertical apical deviation was 0.348 ± 0.488 mm vs. 0.249 ± 0.437 mm (p = 0.009)"
