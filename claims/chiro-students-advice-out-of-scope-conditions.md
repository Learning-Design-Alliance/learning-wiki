## Evidence

### Innes SI 2018

Innes SI, Leboeuf-Yde C, Walker BF. (2018). How frequent are non-evidence-based health care beliefs in chiropractic students and do they vary across the pre-professional educational years. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-018-0178-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional anonymous classroom questionnaire of 444 students from two Australian chiropractic programs (53% response rate) in 2016. The abstract-level summary is supported by Table 2 results: "Students were highly likely to offer advice (often/quite often) on a range of non-musculoskeletal conditions."

> "Students were highly likely to offer advice (often/quite often) on a range of non-musculoskeletal conditions. The proportions were lowest in first year and highest the final year."
