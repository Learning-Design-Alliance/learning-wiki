The editor's own editorial statement about journal operations at Educational Psychology Review: the research-into-practice category was "the most misunderstood and results in the majority of my desk rejects" and will no longer appear. This is an editorial decision report, not an empirical study.

"the research-into-practice article is, unfortunately, the most misunderstood and results in the majority of my desk rejects (i.e., without additional peer review). I am granting it a merciful death —it will no longer appear."
