## Evidence

### Kang and Noh 2017

Houn Tae Kang, Suk Goo Noh. (2017). The Effect on Elementary Science Education Based on Student's Pre-inquiry. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050908

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with one 6th-grade experimental class (33 students) taught via meaningful pre-inquiries and one control class (29 students) taught by general textbook methods in two South Korean elementary schools. Posttest t-test showed the experimental group "showed a significant difference ( p <.05)" on total science process skills.

> "Overall, in the case of science process skills, the controlled group showed 16.20 points and the experimental group showed 17.70 points. The experimental group showed a significant difference ( p <.05)."
