## Evidence

### Fielding Alison 2025

Fielding Alison, Mundy Benjamin, Tapley Amanda, Gani Sarah, Ali Rula, Bentley Michael, Boland Rachael, Zbaidi Lina, Holliday Elizabeth, Ball Jean, van Driel Mieke, Klein Linda, Magin Parker. (2025). Educational utility of observational workplace-based assessment modalities in Australian vocational general practice training: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07328-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression (complete case, GEE) of registrar ratings of overall ECTV educational utility (n = 741 observations; 64.1% rated very useful). Quality of feedback carried the largest association ("OR: 12.8, 95 CI%: 8.26 to 19.9"); odds ratios are printed but no standardised effect size, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "Other variables associated with overall educational utility ratings in multivariable analysis included: quality of feedback received (focused/specific, and easy to translate into action), OR: 12.8, 95 CI%: 8.26 to 19.9); and provision of specific/meaningful feedback for three or more consultations/cases (OR: 3.1, 95%CI: 1.43 to 6.87)"
