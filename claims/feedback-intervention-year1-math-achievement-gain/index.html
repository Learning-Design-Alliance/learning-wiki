q3 · i? · causal · r3

Student achievement impact analysis in the randomized experiment covering grades 4–8 reading/ELA and mathematics; the report describes the Year 1 mathematics gain as "about four weeks of learning" and notes the Year 2 estimate was not statistically significant.

"In Year 1, the intervention had a positive impact on students' achievement in mathematics, amounting to about four weeks of learning. In Year 2, the impact on mathematics achievement was similar in magnitude but not statistically significant."
