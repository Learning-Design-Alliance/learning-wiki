## Evidence

### Driessen 2020

Driessen, E. P., Knight, J. K., Smith, M. K., & Ballen, C. J. (2020). Demystifying the Meaning of Active Learning in Postsecondary Biology Education. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 19(4), ar52. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.20-04-0068

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey of 105 SABER members asked to define active learning in their own words, coded into 10 categories via open coding with initial 71% coder agreement resolved to 100%. The article reports "The most common definition of active learning, from the BER community, was “interacting/engagement” with the material."

> "In general, survey participants’ definitions fit into the same categories as those in the published literature surveyed (Figure 2A). The most common definition of active learning, from the BER community, was “interacting/engagement” with the material."
