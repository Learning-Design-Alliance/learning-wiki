Secondary analysis of the 3-year cluster-randomized PAAC trial, assessing a random sub-sample (n = 252) in grades 2 or 3 at baseline with the WIAT-II. The article reports "Significant improvements were shown for all academic measures in students attending PAAC, but not for students attending control schools." No effect size is printed.

> "The impact of PAAC (2–10 minute lessons/day, 5 days/week) on academic achievement was assessed as a secondary analysis in a 3-year cluster randomized trial (PAAC vs. control) at baseline and at the end of year 3 in a random sub-sample of students (n = 252), in grades 2 or 3 at baseline, using the Weschler Individual Achievement Test-Second Edition (WIAT-II)"
