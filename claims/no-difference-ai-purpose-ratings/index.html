Daniusevičiūtė-Brazaitė L, Gaižiūnienė L and Pučėtaitė R (2026) AI in inclusive education: teachers' visions of future-oriented practices. Front. Educ. 11:1890925. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1890925

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Descriptive analysis of 10-point semantic differential ratings from the questionnaire (valid N ranged 43–47). Medians were 10, 10, and 9; the Friedman test showed "no statistically significant differences among the three dimensions".

"The median rating for personal efficiency was 10 (IQR = 8–10) for caring for learners' needs – 10 (IQR = 9–10) for contribution to a progressive and creative society – 9 (IQR = 9–10). The Friedman test indicated no statistically significant differences among the three dimensions, χ2(2) = 2.69, p = 0.261 (Figure 1)."
