Evidence¶

Shen 2013¶

Shen, F.-Y. (2013). Using Group Discussion with Taiwan’s EFL College Students: A Comparison of Comprehension Instruction for Book Club, Literature Circles, and Instructional Conversations. English Language Teaching, 6(12), 58-. https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n12p58

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on post-treatment interpretive comprehension scores across four conditions (n=145 after attrition). Experimental groups (Book Club M=15.59, Literature Circles M=15.29, Instructional Conversations M=14.63) outperformed basal control (M=12.63). The article reports "a significant difference ( F = 6.97; p = .000)" with post-hoc tests confirming each experimental group exceeded control.

"the ANOV A for the Interpretive Comprehension Test revealed a significant difference ( F = 6.97; p = .000). Scheffe post-hoc comparison tests indicated that experimental lessons had a significantly higher score of interpretive comprehension than the control lesson did."
