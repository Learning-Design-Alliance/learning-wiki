## Evidence

### Zhang Wang 2025

Zhang Wang, Li Zonghuan, Li Pengcheng, Liu Changhuan, Wang Zheng, Liu Yuping, Zhu Lekai, Shi Yulong, Fang Xue, Wang Xinghuan, Xie Zhe, Wang Xin. (2025). MOOC-virtual simulation integration enhances surgical clinical skills: a quasi-experimental study. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2579396

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mediation analysis (Hayes' PROCESS Model 4, 5,000 bootstrap samples) with group as X, total training time as M, and assessment score as Y. Total effect "8.40-point Structured Clinical Skills Assessment advantage"; direct effect 5.63 points (p = 0.003); indirect effect 2.77 points, 33.0% of total.

> "The total effect of the intervention was an 8.40-point Structured Clinical Skills Assessment advantage (95% CI: 6.21−10.59, t = 7.52, p < 0.001)."
