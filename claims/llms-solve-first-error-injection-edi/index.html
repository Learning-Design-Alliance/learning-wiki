q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Targeted trace-level analysis reported in the discussion section (pointing to Appendix C.1) of LLM reasoning traces on Eedi; the article states similarity-based strategies are rare (≈3% of traces) and confined to settings such as rounding or magnitude-estimation problems.

"models construct a step-by-step solution first in 95.2% of traces on average across models, and among these solve-first traces, diverge at a specific step to inject an error in 73.8% of cases"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Providing the correct solution in the prompt improves LLM alignment with human-authored distractors, confirming correct-solution recovery as a primary bottleneck — related
  • LLM distractor-generation strategies differ by domain: error description and simulation dominate on math while conceptual linking and discriminability checks dominate on science — related
  • LLM error simulation rarely fails, but the model is less likely to reproduce a targeted distractor on problems with longer correct solutions — related
  • LLM-generated ideas diverge from lesson ideas in grounding, misconception substance, vocabulary, uncertainty markers, and analogy use — related
  • Candidate selection is a second bottleneck: an oracle ranker over proposed candidates substantially raises proportional match, and plausibility checks reject some valid distractors — related
  • Instruction-tuning a student model on a single misconception overgeneralizes the error across problems and degrades correct-solving accuracy — related
