### Abbas Uzair 2026

Abbas Uzair, Fatima Mehreen, Kamran Durre Sameen, Rasheed Binish, Naz Ramlah, Arshad Fatima. (2026). Impact of peer assessment on lecture quality and student learning outcomes in undergraduate medical education: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09042-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparison of pre- and post-intervention student quality ratings within each group. The results report a "significant difference in improvement of lecture quality in intervention group" (65 vs 86, p < 0.001) but not in control (67 vs 73, p = ns), tested by paired sample t-test per Fig. 3C.

> "We also compared pre and post intervention scores of lecturer quality and found a significant difference in improvement of lecture quality in intervention group (65 vs 86; p < 0.001) as compared to control group (67 vs 73; p = ns; Fig. 3C)"
