# Students who generated their own solutions or observed the full process did not significantly outperform students who only viewed the outcomes of failed solution attempts on conceptual knowledge

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Contrary to Hypothesis 2, the contrast comparing PF and VF-process against VF-outcome on conceptual knowledge was not significant (ηp 2 = 0.01, below d = 0.2 equivalent). [→ Christian Hartmann 2021](#christian-hartmann-2021)

## Evidence

### Christian Hartmann 2021

Christian Hartmann, Tamara van Gog, Nikol Rummel. (2021). Preparatory effects of problem solving versus studying examples prior to instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09528-z

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

In the same ANCOVA on conceptual knowledge with covariates, the second a priori contrast testing whether PF and VF-process together outperformed VF-outcome was non-significant: "the contrast showed no significant differences, F(1, 166) = 1.42, p = 0.235". Equivalence was not tested.

> "contrary to our expectation, the contrast showed no significant differences, F(1, 166) = 1.42, p = 0.235, ηp 2 = 0.01"
