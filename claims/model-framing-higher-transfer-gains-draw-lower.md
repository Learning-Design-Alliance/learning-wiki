## Evidence

### Sally P.W. Wu 2019

Sally P.W. Wu, Jainaba Corr, Martina A. Rau. (2019). How instructors frame students' interactions with educational technologies can enhance or reduce learning with multiple representations. Computers & Education 128 (2019) 199–213. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.09.012

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with 565 undergraduate chemistry students nested in lab sections, analyzed with an HLM including a random intercept for TAs and pretest scores as covariate. The article reports a significant main effect of condition on transfer learning gains, F(2, 547) = 5.445, p = .005, with the model condition outperforming control and control outperforming draw.

> "On the transfer posttest, there was a significant main effect of condition on learning gains, F(2, 547) = 5.445, p = .005, such that the model condition outperformed the control condition and the control condition outperformed the draw condition."
