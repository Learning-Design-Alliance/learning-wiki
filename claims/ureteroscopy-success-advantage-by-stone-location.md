Librenjak Davor, Šitum Marijan, Gugić Dijana, Milostić Kazimir, Duvnjak Mario. (2011). Ureterorenoscopic treatment of ureteral stones – influence of operator's experience and skill on the procedure outcome. Croatian Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.3325/cmj.2011.52.55

Results-section comparison between the two operator groups by stone location, reported in Table 2 of the retrospective study of 422 patients. Distal ureter stones were completely removed in 143 (95) first-group vs 65 (74) second-group cases.
