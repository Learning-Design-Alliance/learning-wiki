## Evidence

### EL-Andari R 2023

EL-Andari R, Bozso SJ, Kang JJH, Fialka NM, Adams C, Nagendran J. (2023). Teaching heart valve surgery techniques using simulators: a systematic review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.017921

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Simulation-based study reported in the review: 10 cardiovascular surgery residents (5 junior, 5 senior) trained 3 months on a pressurized bovine heart model allowing "home-alone" surgical practice after 9 instructional videos, assessed monthly on coaptation depth and regurgitation score.

> "Coaptation depth improved significantly over time in both groups (p < 0.001). Similarly, the mean regurgitation score improved each month for the junior residents (p < 0.02) and improved from the first to the second month and from the first to the third month for the senior residents (p < 0.02)."
