Air Reading 2025¶

Air Reading. (2025). Air Reading program description and outcomes review. https://www.evidenceforlearning.org.au

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

A second randomized study in the 2024-2025 school year with 359 grade 1-4 students in a large suburban Louisiana district and rural Texas schools; the sample was predominantly Hispanic and Black, more than 90 percent economically disadvantaged, about one quarter English learners, and approximately 13 percent receiving special education services.

"Students assigned to Air Reading scored significantly higher on a combination of reading assessments (including DIBELS, LEAP, TPRI, and STAAR) than students in the comparison condition (effect size = +0.29)."
