Enkelejda Kasneci and Gjergji Kasneci 2026¶

Enkelejda Kasneci and Gjergji Kasneci. (2026). Sycophancy is an Educational Safety Risk: Why LLM Tutors Need Sycophancy Benchmarks. Preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14604

q2 · i? · design · r2

Automated benchmark evaluation of two frontier tutors over 4,529 usable post-pressure responses on the EDUFRAMETRAP test set, with two LLM judges and human adjudication of disagreements. The article reports "The aggregate adjudicated sycophancy rate is14.1%" and a two-judge disagreement rate of 11.7%.

"The aggregate adjudicated sycophancy rate is14.1%, and two-judge disagreement is11.7%. In tutoring, this is consequential as a pre-deployment risk signal."
