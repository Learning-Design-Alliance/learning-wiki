Menzies 2016¶

Menzies, V., Hewitt, C., Kokotsaki, D., Collyer, C. and Wiggins, A. (2016). Project Based Learning: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/public/files/Projects/EEF_Project_Protocol_ProjectBasedLearning.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative process evaluation based on classroom observations, interviews and focus groups with headteachers, project leads, teachers, pupils and the Innovation Unit delivery team. No effect sizes are reported; the findings are descriptive feedback rather than tested outcomes.

"did provide evidence of positive benefits from doing PBL, in particular in terms of developing oracy, communication, team working, and research skills."
