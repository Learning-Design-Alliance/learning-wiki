Shanette C. Porter 2023¶

Shanette C. Porter, C. Kirabo Jackson, Sebastián Kiguel and John Q. Easton. (2023). Investing in Adolescents: High School Climate and Organizational Context Shape Student Development and Educational Attainment. UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/investing-in-adolescents

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The study used administrative records and 5Essentials Survey measures of socioemotional development from "8th and 9th grade students who attended a CPS high school between 2011 and 2017 (160,148 students)". Schools fostering multi-dimensional growth improved graduation and postsecondary outcomes.

"High schools that fostered 8th to 9th grade student growth across multiple dimensions positively influenced students' social and academic trajectories."
