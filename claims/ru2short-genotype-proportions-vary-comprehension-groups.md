## Evidence

### Li 2018

Li, M., Malins, J. G., DeMille, M. M. C., Lovett, M. W., Truong, D. T., Epstein, K., Lacadie, C., Mehta, C., Bosson-Heenan, J., Gruen, J. R., Frijters, J. C. and the GRaD Study Consortium. (2018). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Genetic analysis comparing READ1 genotype frequencies (RU1-1, RU2Long, RU2Short) against the three comprehension groups in the GRaD sample. The χ2 test showed the RU2Short proportion was highest in UPC and lowest in UGC.

> "The proportion of the RU2Short genotype signi ﬁcantly varied across the three groups: 0.47 for UPC, 0.37 for EAC, and 0.32 for UGC, χ2(2) = 10.30, p = 0.006."
