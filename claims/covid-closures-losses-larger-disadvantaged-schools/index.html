Evidence¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte 2020¶

Joana Elisa Maldonado and Kristof De Witte. (2020). The Effect of School Closures on Standardised Student Test Outcomes. KU Leuven Faculty of Economics and Business Discussion Paper Series DPS20.17. https://www.kuleuven.be/dps20.17

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Marginal-effect estimation by socioeconomic status indicators in grade 6 on the 2015-2020 sample with full controls (Figure 1a and 1b). For mathematics, losses increased in the shares of students with a low educated mother and receiving financial support; for Dutch, losses rose on all four SES proxies. No standardised effect size is printed.

"In summary, we observe for all four proxies of SES a decrease in performance and hence a stronger effect of the COVID-19 school closures for Dutch if the share of low SES students increases."
