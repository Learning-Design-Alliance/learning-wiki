### Loo Kang WEE 2014

Loo Kang WEE. (2014). Open Educational Resources from Performance Task using Video Analysis and Modeling - Tracker and K12 science education framework. OCPA8 Invited Paper, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1408.5992](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1408.5992)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Worked example of mathematical thinking in Tracker's DataTool: a parabola fit of a falling tennis ball video gave parameters A = -2.317, B = -0.1976 and C = 0.2310, and the author interprets the discrepant g as a frame-rate error.

> "In Kinematics terms, A = 0.5 g implying gravitational acceleration g = -4.634 m/s2 (probably due to a video frame rate incorrectly understood as the student could have slow the video down to 30 frame per second instead of the true frame rate), initial velocity u = B = -0.1976 m/s and initial height y0 = C = 0.2310 m."
