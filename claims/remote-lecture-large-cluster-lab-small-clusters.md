# In remote physics courses, lecture interaction networks form one large connected cluster while lab interaction networks form small disconnected clusters of two to four nodes

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Remote lecture interaction networks contain long chain-like formations connecting many nodes in one large cluster, whereas remote lab networks contain smaller formations and many disconnected clusters of two to four nodes, likely indicating separation by lab group. ## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2022

Meagan Sundstrom, Andy Schang, Ashley B. Heim, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Understanding interaction network formation across instructional contexts in remote physics courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Network diagrams and density calculations of survey-reported interaction networks in four remote physics courses (Figs. 2 and 3) show lecture networks with "long chain-like formations that connect many nodes in one large cluster" across both active-learning and traditional lectures.

> "The lecture interaction networks (shown in the left-hand column of Figs. 2 and 3) contain long chain-like formations that connect many nodes in one large cluster, with some additional, smaller clusters not connected to this main cluster."

### Meagan Sundstrom 2022 (2)

Meagan Sundstrom, Andy Schang, Ashley B. Heim, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Understanding interaction network formation across instructional contexts in remote physics courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020141)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same network visualization analysis found lab networks "contain smaller chain-like formations and many disconnected clusters of two to four nodes", which the authors interpret as separation by lab group; densities were comparable across contexts within each course.

> "the lab interaction networks (shown in the right-hand column of Figs. 2 and 3), contain smaller chain-like formations and many disconnected clusters of two to four nodes, likely indicating separation by lab group"
