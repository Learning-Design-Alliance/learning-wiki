### Marotta A 2025

Marotta A, Schena CA, Ascanelli S, Azzolina D, Calabrese P, Iovino DP, Sani V, Gavriilidis P, Laterza V, Marchegiani F, Pellino G, Celentano V, de'Angelis N. (2025). Int J Colorectal Dis 40:152. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-04936-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational rating study of the 25 most-viewed YouTube robotic ventral rectopexy videos, each rated independently by three trainees, three fellows, and three senior surgeons. Reliability was measured with Krippendorff's alpha; "KF = 1" for seniors versus 0.71 for fellows and 0.55 for trainees.

> "Inter-rater reliability was highest among senior surgeons (25/25 videos [100%] received equivalent ratings from all three examiners, KF = 1), followed by fellows (11/25 videos [44%], KF = 0.71) and trainees (6/25 videos [24%], KF = 0.55)."
