### Freundt Paula 2023 (2)

Freundt Paula, Nourkami-Tutdibi Nasenien, Tutdibi Erol, Janzing Paul, von Ostrowski Thomas, Langer Martin, Zemlin Michael, Steinhard Johannes. (2023). Controlled Prospective Study on the Use of Systematic Simulator-Based Training with a Virtual, Moving Fetus for Learning Second-Trimester Scan: FESIM III. Ultraschall in der Medizin. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-1984-8320

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Overall TTC comparison in the same standardized test: the expert group's 443±243 seconds remained significantly faster than the trial group's; the authors note only three standard views were accomplished significantly faster by experts at the end.

> "the expert group remained significantly faster in its overall performance (443±243 seconds, p<0.001)."
