## Evidence

### Andrew J. Haugen and Nathan T. Moore 2014

Andrew J. Haugen and Nathan T. Moore. (2014). A model for including Arduino microcontroller programming in the introductory physics lab. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1407.7613](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1407.7613)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor reflection from the Results and Conclusions section on the two pilot implementations. The authors report that although they found the early exposure to real interfacing, programming, and engineering valuable, "to the students it sometimes came across as extra work, somewhat tangential to both the course syllabus and their future careers." No quantitative data support this observation.

> "In both semesters however, we underestimated the extent to which this idea needed to be "sold.""
