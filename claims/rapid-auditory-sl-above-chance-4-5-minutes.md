One-sample t-test against chance (0.50) on overall 2AFC accuracy for adults after 4.5 minutes of exposure to a continuous speech stream of trisyllabic nonsense words; adults' mean accuracy was M = 0.682, described as "confirming successful acquisition of statistical regularities from continuous speech streams."
