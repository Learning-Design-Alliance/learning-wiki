## Evidence

### Gonzalez-Sanz Juan D. 2026

Gonzalez-Sanz Juan D., Teresa-Morales Cristina, Rodríguez-Pérez Margarita, Torres-Enamorado Dolores, Feria-Ramírez Carmen, Urcola-Pardo Fernando. (2026). Nursing students’ evaluations of the flipped classroom using the FLAS scale: a quantitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08625-w

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis of age against the three FLAS dimensions in the sample of 432 nursing students. The article reports correlations of "ρ = 0.146, p = .002" with D1 and "ρ = 0.182, p < .001" with D2, and a non-significant ρ = 0.069 for D3.

> "Positive and significant correlations were observed between age and D1 (ρ = 0.146, p = .002) and between age and D2 (ρ = 0.182, p < .001), whereas no significant association was found for D3 (ρ = 0.069, p = .150)"
