## Evidence

### Liu Shengjun 2025

Liu Shengjun, Zhao Zhangyi, Chen Xiangyu, Chi Yi, Yuan Siyi, Cai Fuhong, Song Zhangwei, Ma Yue, He Huaiwu, Su Longxiang, Long Yun. (2025). Evaluation of health care providers’ ability to identify patient-ventilator triggering asynchrony in intensive care unit: a translational observational study in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06638-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of the same 109 qualified professionals' entry-test concept matching and evaluation-test waveform recognition. The article reports concept accuracy "97.1% vs. 68.8%" for ineffective triggers and similar gaps for the other asynchrony types.

> "The accuracy of correctly answering basic concepts and correctly identifying patient-ventilator asynchrony was 97.1% vs. 68.8% (p = 1.761 × 10−12) for ineffective triggers, 89.9% vs. 61.0% (p = 4.037 × 10−8) for double triggers, 89.4% vs. 20.2% (p = 2.2 × 10−16) for auto-triggers and 35.8% vs. 22.5% (p = 0.06597) for reverse triggers"
