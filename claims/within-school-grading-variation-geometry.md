## Evidence

### Starch and Elliott 1913

Daniel Starch and Edward C. Elliott. (1913). Reliability of Grading Work in Mathematics. The School Review, 21(4), 254–259. https://doi.org/10.1086/436086

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors address the criticism that variation arose from schools having different standards by reporting within-school grading. In the source school five teachers' grades averaged 64.8 with mean variation 4.2; in a large Ohio high school four teachers graded it 76, 75, 67, and 61, average 69.8, mean variation 5.8. Both schools have passing grade 70.

> "the school from which the paper was obtained has five teachers of geometry, each of whom graded the paper independently as follows: 70, 65, 60, 70, and 59, average 64.8, mean variation 4.2."
