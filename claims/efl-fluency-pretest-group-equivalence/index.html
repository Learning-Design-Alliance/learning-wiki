Alrayah 2018¶

Hassan Alrayah. (2018). The Effectiveness of Cooperative Learning Activities in Enhancing EFL Learners' Fluency. English Language Teaching, 11(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n4p21

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest comparison of the same 48 students (24 per group) using recorded fluency interviews analyzed in SPSS. The printed result is "no significant difference" with t = 1.758, Sig .085, df 46; equivalence was not formally tested.
