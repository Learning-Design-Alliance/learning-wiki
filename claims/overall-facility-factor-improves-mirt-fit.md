## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Adding a factor loaded on all items, capturing overall facility with conceptual Newtonian mechanics, produced a significantly better-fitting model than the same model without it. [→ John Stewart 2018](#john-stewart-2018)

## Evidence

### John Stewart 2018

John Stewart, Cabot Zabriskie, Seth DeVore, and Gay Stewart. (2018). Multi-Dimensional Item Response Theory and the Force Concept Inventory. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010137](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.14.010137)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Constrained MIRT comparison on the 20-item problem set; the overall factor was added to separate general facility with Newtonian mechanics from facility with specific principles, given the FCI's strong internal consistency and mostly positive item correlations.

> "The model with this factor (AIC=90,920, BIC=91,488) was a signiﬁcant im- provement over the model without this overall factor (AIC=94,442, BIC=94,881)[ χ2(20) = 3562, p <0.001]."
