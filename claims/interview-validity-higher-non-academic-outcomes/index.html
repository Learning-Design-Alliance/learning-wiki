Descriptive pattern across the 106 validity coefficients summarized in Table 2; the authors note it as a hedged tendency ('tended to show'), not a tested contrast, and link it to interest in non-academic predictors.

"The results displayed in Table 2 tended to show higher correlations for non-academic outcomes. This may be an important consideration for the use of the interview given the priority and recent emphasis on developing predictors of non-academic performance outcomes [40]."
