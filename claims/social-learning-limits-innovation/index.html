Narrative review statement in which the review attributes the effectiveness of social learning to prior work and cites research showing that children stop searching for solutions when answers are given. No effect sizes are printed; the review reports these as established findings from the cited literature.

"Learning from others is an effective way of acquiring usable information and skills 14 and a sine qua non to be part of a cultural environment 12. Still, it also limits the potential for innovation26, as shown in how children stop searching for solutions when answers are given 24."
