## Evidence

### Kuo 2015

Kuo, E., & Wieman, C. E. (2015). Seeking instructional specificity: an example from analogical instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020133](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020133)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of the two discussion-section activity conditions in a large introductory physics course (N = 231 analyzed). On the magnitude mid-test question there was "a 24% difference in correctness by condition for magnitude" favoring GR, significant at p < .001; the direction question showed no difference. No effect size was printed.

> "Examining the first hypothesis with the mid-test direction and magnitude scores, there is a 24% difference in correctness by condition for magnitude, χ2(1, N=231) = 12.7, p < .001, but no difference for direction, χ2(1, N=231) < .1."
