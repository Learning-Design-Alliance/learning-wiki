## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Alexandra O. Cohen 2020

Alexandra O. Cohen, Kate Nussenbaum, Hayley M. Dorfman, Samuel J. Gershman and Catherine A. Hartley. (2020). The rational use of causal inference to guide reinforcement learning strengthens with age. npj Science of Learning 5:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00075-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Logistic mixed-effects analysis of trial-wise optimal choice by territory, trial number, age and age-squared (N = 90). Main effects of trial number, age, and age-squared were significant; the trial-by-territory interaction, "p < 0.001", qualified them.

> "There was a signi ﬁcant trial number by territory interaction ( χ2(2, N = 90) = 15.40, p < 0.001), such that learning trajectories were steeper for the Millionaire and Sheriff territories than the Robber territory"

### Alexandra O. Cohen 2020 (2)

Alexandra O. Cohen, Kate Nussenbaum, Hayley M. Dorfman, Samuel J. Gershman and Catherine A. Hartley. (2020). The rational use of causal inference to guide reinforcement learning strengthens with age. npj Science of Learning 5:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00075-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Trial-wise optimal choice mixed-effects models with territory by age-squared interactions significant, and simulation of the one learning rate model mirrored children's better learning in the Millionaire territory.

> "younger participants showed better learning in the environment where the agent intervened to generate positive outcomes, relative to the other environments"
