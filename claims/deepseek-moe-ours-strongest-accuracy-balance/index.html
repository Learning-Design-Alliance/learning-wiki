Shen X 2026¶

Shen X, Feng L, Hua S, Liu D, Xie Z and Liu B (2026) Towards sustainable AI knowledge-base assistants in computer science education: on-premise deployment and optimization with open educational resources. Front. Psychol. 17:1843444. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1843444

q2 · i? · design · r2

Benchmark comparison on the study's n = 300 OER-derived query evaluation set (105 factual recall, 135 concept explanation, 60 multi-hop), with McNemar's test against the RAG no-fine-tuning baseline and Holm–Bonferroni correction. The article reports "DeepSeek-MoE (Ours) reaches 78.6% (+12.0 pp., p < 0.001)". No standardized effect size is printed.

"the RAG (no fine-tuning) baseline achieves 66.6% overall accuracy, while Qwen-7B (Ours) reaches 69.8% (+3.2 pp., p = 0.031) and DeepSeek-MoE (Ours) reaches 78.6% (+12.0 pp., p < 0.001)"

Related Claims¶

  • Quantization-aware fine-tuning narrows the FP16-to-4-bit accuracy gap and partially recovers accuracy lost to standard 4-bit quantization — related
  • Hallucination rate increases under 4-bit quantization and is substantially mitigated by quantization-aware fine-tuning — related
  • The most energy-efficient tested configuration consumes 1.8 mWh per query, a 43.8% reduction relative to the Qwen-7B FP16 baseline — related
  • NF4 4-bit quantization reduces VRAM usage by 38.7% for Qwen-7B and 37.9% for DeepSeek-MoE while maintaining practical response times — related
  • Ablation study shows metadata-enriched embeddings produce the largest accuracy drop, while attention masking, KV caching and dynamic batching primarily support inference efficiency — related
  • Retrieval augmentation is essential for on-premise educational QA: a local LLM without retrieval scores below even a TF-IDF baseline — related
