## Evidence

### Juan J. Merelo 2023

Juan J. Merelo, Pedro A. Castillo, Antonio M. Mora, Francisco Barranco, Noorhan Abbas, Alberto Guillén and Olia Tsivitanidou. (2023). Chatbots and messaging platforms in the classroom: an analysis from the teacher's perspective. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-023-11703-x)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-step cluster analysis with log-likelihood distance on the second survey (N=205): cluster 1 (n=78, 60.9%) valued analytics, familiarity and institutional support but not pluggability, connectivity or official training; cluster 2 (n=50, 39.1%) showed the reverse pattern. The authors state the cluster quality is fair, not good (silhouette measure).

> "Interaction media pluggability appears to have the most important predictor importance in the clustering of cases (predictor importance = 1.0), whilst media unidirectionality was the least important factor (predictor importance = 0.04)."
