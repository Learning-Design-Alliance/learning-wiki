## Evidence

### Onyeaso 2016

Adedamola Olutoyin Onyeaso. (2016). Retention of Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Skills in Nigerian Secondary School Students. Journal of Education and Practice. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1103091

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 322 Nigerian SS1 and SS2 students trained with conventional CPR using manikins and AHA guidelines. Paired t-test showed significant retention (t-cal 9.17, P .000), with mean post-test 7.92 and retention 6.95, a "mean CPR skills loss of only 0.97" (12.24% loss).

> "Table 2 provides the paired t- test analysis of the differe nces in the level of CPR skills of the participants six weeks after the CPR training with rejection of the first null hypothesis meaning that statistically significant level of CPR skills was retained by the participants (P < 0.05)."
