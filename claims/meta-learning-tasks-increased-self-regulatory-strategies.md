The review reports, citing Colthorpe et al. (2018), a university study of meta-learning tasks that promoted consideration of prior knowledge and planning of remaining learning; students increased self-regulatory strategy use. No effect size is printed. Supports the management/organization theme.

> "a study using meta-learning tasks (i.e., tasks that promoted consideration of knowledge in the context of what students already know, and planning what they still needed to learn) found that students increased their use of self-regulatory strategies when studying, and that these meta-learning tasks were a useful tool to develop students' reflective and independent learning ability (Colthorpe et al., 2018)."
