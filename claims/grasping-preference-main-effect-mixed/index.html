Evidence¶

Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield 2017¶

Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield. (2017). A Quasi-Experimental Examination: Cognitive Sequencing of Instruction Using Experiential Learning Theory for STEM Concepts in Agricultural Education. Journal of Agricultural Education, 58(4), 175-191. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2017.04175

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Omnibus univariate ANOVA for the water science unit (n = 121). The preference main effect is reported as significant with ηp2 = 0.09, which falls in Cohen's medium range per the article's cited guidelines.

"Significant differences were found for both preference for grasping experience (F(1,115) = 11.07, p = 0.01, ηp2 = 0.09) and cognitive sequence of instruction (F(2,115) = 60.65, p = 0.01, ηp2 = 0.51)."

The soil science ANOVA table (mislabeled Table 3) reports the Grasping row as F = 0.41, p = 0.53, not significant at the adjusted alpha of p ≤ 0.02. The article prints no effect size for this non-significant result.
