# Five TPACK-related moderators are most salient across meta-analyses: grade level, assessment, duration, instructional modality, and mathematics subject matter

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` An inductive review of the 13 coded meta-analyses identified five salient TPACK-related moderator categories: grade level, assessment, duration, instructional modality, and mathematics subject matter. [→ Young 2016](#young-2016)

## Evidence

### Young 2016

Young, J.R. (2016). Unpacking TPACK in Mathematics education research: A systematic review of meta-analyses. International Journal of Educational Methodology, 2(1), 19-29. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijem.2.1.19

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review coded study features in four categories (sample, measurement, design, source) with inter-rater agreement of 94.4% (Cohen's κ = .927), then inductively identified the five moderator categories "through repetition and statistical significance." The moderators were mapped onto TPK, TCK, and the overall TPACK construct.

> "These five categories of TPACK related moderators were: (1) grade level, (2) asses sment, (3) duration, (4) instructional modality, and (5) mathematics subject matter."
