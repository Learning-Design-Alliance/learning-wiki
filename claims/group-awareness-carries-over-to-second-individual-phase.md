## Evidence

### Farrokhnia 2024

Farrokhnia, M., Noroozi, O., Baggen, Y., Biemans, H., & Weinberger, A. (2024). Improving hybrid brainstorming outcomes with computer-supported scaffolds: Prompts and cognitive group awareness. Computers & Education 227 (2025) 105229. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105229

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Individual-level ANCOVAs on the second individual phase (n = 32 vs 33), controlling for group outcomes. Originality: F(1,60) = 10.86, ηp² = 0.15; fluency: F(1,60) = 11.17, ηp² = 0.16; flexibility: F(1,60) = 4.30, ηp² = 0.08; persistence not significant.

> "The results revealed significant differences between experimental 1 and 2 conditions in the second individual phase for idea flexibility (p = .04), average originality (p = .002), and fluency (p = .001), but no significant difference for idea persistence (p = .25)"
