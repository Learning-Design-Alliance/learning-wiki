## Evidence

### Haut KG 2026

Haut KG, Hasan M, Carroll T, Epstein RM, Sen T, Hoque E. (2026). Effects of a Generative AI Virtual Patient on Serious Illness Communication Skills: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/93034

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Postinteraction survey ratings from SOPHIE arm participants on system features. "Actionable feedback on communication skills" was the highest-rated feature (mean: 4.7/5, SD: 0.18), followed by usability (4.2/5) and realism (3.4/5).

> "The training system itself received strong evaluations, particularly for its actionable feedback on communication skills—the highest-rated feature (mean: 4.7/5, SD: 0.18). Users also praised SOPHIE's usability (mean: 4.2/5, SD: 0.36), though ratings for the virtual human's realism were more moderate (mean: 3.4/5, SD: 0.17)."
