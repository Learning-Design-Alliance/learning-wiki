## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yu et al. 2015

Ka Chun Yu, Kamran Sahami, Victoria Sahami, Larry C. Sessions. (2015). Using A Digital Planetarium For Teaching Seasons To Undergraduates. Journal of Astronomy & Earth Sciences Education, 2(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1101071

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Figure 3-based inference from the quasi-experiment: overlapping 95% CIs for Group II versus Group I Cont-Pre gains mean the null cannot be rejected at the .05 level; equivalence was not tested.

> "Although the Group II Cont-Pre gains was 6.7% versus 4.4% for Group I, the 95% CIs for the two groups overlap, meaning p>0.05 when considering the null hypothesis that the Group II mean is within the Group I CI, and vice versa."

### Yu et al. 2015 (2)

Ka Chun Yu, Kamran Sahami, Victoria Sahami, Larry C. Sessions. (2015). Using A Digital Planetarium For Teaching Seasons To Undergraduates. Journal of Astronomy & Earth Sciences Education, 2(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1101071

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Post-Pre results from Table 2: the classroom-visualization group's small effect (ES=0.125) fell below the control group's modest effect (ES=0.556), reversing its contemporaneous advantage.

> "Students in the control classes have learning gains with a modest ES=0.556, while the Group II students have dropped to a small ES=0.125."
