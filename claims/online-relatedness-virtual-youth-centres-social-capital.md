## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Online spaces provided accessible gathering places for support and belonging, functioning like virtual youth centres that reduced distress and removed geographic barriers. [→ Gordon 2025](#gordon-2025)
`q2 i?` Online environments supported both bonding social capital (staying connected with existing friends and family) and bridging social capital (connecting globally with people adolescents would not otherwise meet). [→ Gordon 2025 (2)](#gordon-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Gordon 2025

Gordon, C. S., Brake, N., Hammond, S. P., Picton, I., Notley, T., Rowston, K., Gutierrez, A., Basarkod, G., Costello, K., Gupta, M., West, M., & Dicke, T. (2025). Adolescent flourishing in a digitally connected world: A qualitative systematic review and meta-synthesis. Preprint. https://osf.io/j48k5/?view_only=8b6423082746439abb4106526e953aaf

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Relatedness subtheme from the meta-synthesis: these spaces provided safety, acceptance, and care during difficult times, with affordances such as anonymity, asynchronicity, and instant connectivity enabling connection and alleviating distress and loneliness.

> "For many adolescents, online environments functioned like virtual youth centres, accessible, familiar spaces where they could gather, connect, and be supported."

### Gordon 2025 (2)

Gordon, C. S., Brake, N., Hammond, S. P., Picton, I., Notley, T., Rowston, K., Gutierrez, A., Basarkod, G., Costello, K., Gupta, M., West, M., & Dicke, T. (2025). Adolescent flourishing in a digitally connected world: A qualitative systematic review and meta-synthesis. Preprint. https://osf.io/j48k5/?view_only=8b6423082746439abb4106526e953aaf

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Subtheme on social capital: adolescents described strengthening existing relationships across hospitalisation, pandemic lockdowns, and distance, and bridging to global connections, with one rural participant describing meeting "a whole lot more people…that you never would have met".

> "Online environments function as spaces for both bonding and bridging social capital."
