The team's synthesis of cited literature on oral outcomes states that oral dysfunction with or without dysphagia "leads to modification of food choices with implications for energy and nutrient intakes and subsequently risk of malnutrition." Oral outcomes contributing to dysfunction include edentulism, denture use, orofacial pain, xerostomia, hyposalivation, and loss of soft tissue.
