In the same pilot eye-tracking task, difficult ECGs elicited greater pupil dilation than easy ones (13.45% vs. 7.32%; p = 0.008), with novices exhibiting marked increases while experts showed minimal fluctuation, indicating higher cognitive demand for novices on complex cases.

> "increased task difficulty was associated with greater pupil dilation, particularly among less experienced readers (mean dilation for difficult tasks: 13.45% vs. 7.32% for easy tasks; p = 0.008)"
