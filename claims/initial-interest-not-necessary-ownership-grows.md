`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Second major finding of the cross-case thematic analysis of three Optics/Lasers project groups, drawn from pre-project interviews, weekly surveys, and post-project interviews. The article reports this as a qualitative finding with no effect size.

> "Second, students' interest in the project and perceptions of its value can increase over time; initial student interest in the project topic is not a necessary condition for student ownership of the project."
