`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized controlled trial, the 50-question quiz was repeated at least 1 month after training. The e-learning group scored higher, with mean "38.2, 95% CI 36.7-39.7 vs 35.8, 95% CI 34.8-36.8; P=.007".

> "The participants who followed the e-learning module had significantly higher scores on the retention quiz (38.2, 95% CI 36.7-39.7 vs 35.8, 95% CI 34.8-36.8; P=.007; Figure 4)."
