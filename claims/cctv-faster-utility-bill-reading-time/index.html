Leat et al. 2017¶

Susan J. Leat, Francis Fengqin Si, Deborah Gold, Dawn Pickering, Keith Gordon, and William Hodge. (2017). The Experience of a Randomized Clinical Trial of Closed-Circuit Television Versus Eccentric Viewing Training for People with Age-Related Macular Degeneration.
