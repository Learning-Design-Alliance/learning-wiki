## Evidence

### Smits-van der Nat 2024

Smits-van der Nat, M., van der Wilt, F., Meeter, M., & van der Veen, C. (2024). The Value of Pretend Play for Social Competence in Early Childhood: A Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09884-z

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-regression of 188 associations from 34 studies (overall N = 2952) using random-effects models in JASP, with age centered as covariate. The article reports "higher levels of pretend play generally go together with higher social competence" with g = 0.166, 95% CI [0.042, 0.290].

> "The significant intercept indicates that higher levels of pretend play generally go together with higher social competence (g = 0.166, 95% CI [0.042, 0.290], p < 0.01, kstudies = 34, kES = 188)."
