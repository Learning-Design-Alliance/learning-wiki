### Katy Klauenberg 2025

Katy Klauenberg, Peter Harris, Philipp Möhrke and Francesca Pennecchi. (2025). The three most common needs for training on measurement uncertainty. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.2478/msr-2025-0029](https://doi.org/10.2478/msr-2025-0029)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Course survey (Table 1) and questionnaire (Table 2) evidence for calibration laboratories: the topics are "taught little (33 %)" with knowledge in the lower third, while interest exists.

> "Tables 1 and 2 coherently show that the propagation of distributions via Monte Carlo and the validation of LPU against Monte Carlo results are taught little (33 %) and there is little knowledge about it for European and Italian calibration laboratories (generally in the lower third). However, the interest in both topics exists according to table 2."
