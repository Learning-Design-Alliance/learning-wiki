`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Observation from class presentations of the Escape from Port-au-Prince game on the 2010 Haitian earthquake: several students were shocked, some found the subject too serious for a video game while others defended games as a medium for any subject, and "a spontaneous debate lasting about 30 minutes" followed.

> "As a result, a spontaneous debate lasting about 30 minutes followed the presentation of this serious game."
