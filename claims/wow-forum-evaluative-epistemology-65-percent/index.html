Constance Steinkuehler 2008¶

Constance Steinkuehler, Sean Duncan. (2008). Scientific Habits of Mind in Virtual Worlds. J Sci Educ Technol. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10956-008-9120-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Epistemology coding of the forum corpus (Kuhn 1992 framework) found "65% of the forum posts displayed an evaluative epistemology", 30% absolutist, 5% relativist, with 27% of the corpus uncodable for epistemological disposition.

"we found that 65% of the forum posts displayed an evaluative epistemology through rhetorical moves that treat knowledge as an open-ended process of evaluation and argument"
