## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Schwortz 2020

Schwortz, A. C., & Burrows, A. C. (2020). Authentic Science Experiences with STEM Datasets: Post-secondary Results and Potential Gender Influences. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2004.12448](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2004.12448)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Matched pre/post-test analysis (one-way ANOVA) of the full sample in the three-phase astronomy dataset activity: pre-test means rose from 61% to a post-test mean of 80% "with p<0.01", with matched normalized gain 0.350 and overall effect size 0.96.

> "the pre-test means increasing from 61% to a post-test mean of 80%, with p<0.01 and a sample size of N=87. The matched normalized gain scores were 0.350 for all participants, and the overall effect sizes were 0.96 for all participants."
