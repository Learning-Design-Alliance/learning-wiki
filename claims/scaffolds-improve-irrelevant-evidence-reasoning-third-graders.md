## Evidence

### Hanna Grimm · Peter A. Edelsbrunner · Kornelia Möller 2023

Hanna Grimm · Peter A. Edelsbrunner · Kornelia Möller. (2023). Accommodating heterogeneity: the interaction of instructional scaffolding with student preconditions in the learning of hypothesis-based reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09601-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Descriptive statistics (Table 1) from the final sample of N = 143 third-graders in the two intervention conditions, reporting percentage of solved irrelevant-evidence items. The experimental condition posttest mean of 0.71 exceeded the control mean of 0.54, with Cohen's d = 0.49 and a bootstrapped 95% CI of "[0.12; 0.85]".

> "Posttest irrelevant 0.71 0.32 0.54 0.38 0.49 [0.12; 0.85]"

Hanna Grimm · Peter A. Edelsbrunner · Kornelia Möller. (2023). Accommodating heterogeneity: the interaction of instructional scaffolding with student preconditions in the learning of hypothesis-based reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09601-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The same Table 1 descriptives report the pretest-to-posttest gain on irrelevant-evidence items as 0.44 in the experimental condition versus 0.18 in the control condition, Cohen's d = 0.73 with 95% CI "[0.34; 1.13]".

> "Irrelevant gain 0.44 0.37 0.18 0.33 0.73 [0.34; 1.13]"
