# Performance-tested ERA and teacher-observed emotion regulation are only modestly correlated (r = 0.22), suggesting they measure distinct constructs

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` ERA and teacher-observed emotion regulation were significantly correlated at r = 0.22 (p = 0.001), a low correlation indicating the two measures capture somewhat distinct attributes. [→ Ivcevic Zorana; Eggers Catherine 2021](#ivcevic-zorana-eggers-catherine-2021)

## Evidence

### Ivcevic Zorana; Eggers Catherine 2021

Ivcevic Zorana; Eggers Catherine. (2021). Emotion Regulation Ability: Test Performance and Observer Reports in Predicting Relationship, Achievement and Well-Being Outcomes in Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8003666/

`q3 · i2`

Zero-order correlation analysis in the 220-student sample showed "ERA and teacher-observed emotion regulation were significantly correlated, although the correlation was rather low (r = 0.22, p = 0.001)". The authors interpret this as evidence the measures capture distinct constructs: potential versus enacted behavior.
