## Evidence

### Imran M 2022

Imran M, Halawa TF, Baig M, Almanjoumi AM, Badri MM, Alghamdi WA. (2022). Team-based learning versus interactive lecture in achieving learning outcomes and improving clinical reasoning skills: a randomized crossover study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03411-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire feedback from emergency module students (n = 37) and endocrinology students (n = 35), presented as frequencies; Table 3 shows 27 of 37 (emergency) and 24 of 35 (endocrinology) chose TBL over IL for clinical reasoning. Descriptive data; no inferential test or effect size.

> "Mostly, students enjoyed the interaction in IL and team application in TBL. They perceived that TBL was a better strategy to learn CRS (Table 3)."
