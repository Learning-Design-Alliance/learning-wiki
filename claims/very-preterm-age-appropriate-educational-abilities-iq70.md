## Evidence

### Nyman A 2019

Nyman A, Korhonen T, Lehtonen L, Haataja L, on behalf of the PIPARI Study Group. (2019). School performance is age appropriate with support services in very preterm children at 11 years of age. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.14763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression analysis of teacher-rated educational ability questionnaires in a Finnish regional birth cohort of 123 very preterm children and 133 full-term controls at age 11, comparing groups after excluding IQ <70. The article reports "did not differ significantly from the controls in educational abilities".

> "after excluding children with full‐scale IQ < 70, the preterm group did not differ significantly from the controls in educational abilities but continued to have significantly more support services (Table 2)."
