Authors' theoretical interpretation in the Discussion, not a tested result: NT is characterised as supporting selection, organisation and rehearsal, while MCQ creation requires deeper generative processing whose benefit for novices may be offset by higher intrinsic and extraneous cognitive load.

> "Both strategies therefore engage important but distinct cognitive pathways, which may help explain why neither method produced a clear advantage in exam performance in our study."
