## Evidence

### Göldi 2019

Göldi, M. & Rasch, B. (2019). Effects of targeted memory reactivation during sleep at home depend on sleep disturbances and habituation. npj Science of Learning 4:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0044-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subjective SF-A/R sleep-quality ratings (0–4 scale) in 66 participants; night 1 rated "2.23 ± 0.12" vs 3.04 ± 0.08 on the control night, t(65) = 5.70, P < 0.001; the linear increase across nights had η2 = 0.32; night 3 did not differ from baseline (P > 0.10); sleep duration did not differ.

> "Participants rated their subjective sleep quality signiﬁcantly lower in the ﬁrst experimental night (2.23 ± 0.12) as compared to the night without tone stimulation (3.04 ± 0.08; t(65) = 5.70, P < 0.001)."
