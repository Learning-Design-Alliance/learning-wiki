## Evidence

### Luc T. Paoli 2024

Luc T. Paoli, Pavan K. Inguva, Andrew J. Haslam, and Pierre J. Walker. (2024). Confronting the thermodynamics knowledge gap: A short course on computational thermodynamics in Julia. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1016/j.ece.2024.03.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ece.2024.03.002)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Remote pilot run in summer 2022 with 16 participants (undergraduate beyond second year and graduate students), with pre- and post-course surveys approved by the Ethics Research Committee at Imperial College London. The before-and-after responses in figure 12 showed "a near-total increase" in confidence; no effect size was reported.

> "This is made even more apparent in figure 12 where it is illustrated that, when students were asked whether or not the course improved their confidence with regards to mastering and applying the concepts of computational thermodynamics, there was a near-total increase."
