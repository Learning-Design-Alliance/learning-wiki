## Evidence

### Jitendra 2017

Jitendra, A. K., Harwell, M. R., Karl, S. R., Im, S-H., & Slater, S. C. (2017). Investigating a Tier 1 intervention focused on proportional reasoning: A follow-up study. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 83(4), 340-358. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402917691017

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MD-only longitudinal two-level repeated-measures analysis of PPS data. The article notes this differs from the prior study, where faster growth for SBI students with MD was not statistically significant. No effect size is printed for the growth moderation.

> "The average linear slope was not statistically significant (B = .42, t = 1.86, p = .062) (there was still significant variability among student linear slopes), but treatment was a significant moderator of change over time with SBI students improving at a faster rate than control students (B = .93, t = 3.23, p = .002)."
