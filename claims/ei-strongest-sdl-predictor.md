Hierarchical multiple regression in the same 300-student cross-sectional study. Emotional intelligence showed the largest unstandardized coefficient (B = 0.408) versus MI (0.320) and SI (0.306), making it the strongest predictor of self-directed learning.

> "When analyzing the influence of intelligence variables, it is evident that emotional intelligence has the most significant impact. Specifically, for every one-unit increase in EI, the SDL score increases by 0.41 points."
