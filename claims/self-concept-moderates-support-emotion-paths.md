### Chen Shengdong 2025

Chen, S., Xu, H., Jin, C., Yin, K., Wang, X., & Wang, T. (2025). Social support and english academic achievement: the mediating role of foreign language emotions and the moderating effect of academic self-concept. BMC Psychology, 13, 1286. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03617-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation results from PROCESS Model 7 regression on the 1,543-student survey. The article reports the interaction "significantly predicted foreign language enjoyment in a positive direction"; Table 2 shows B = 0.046, t = 3.081** for enjoyment.

> "the interaction between English academic self-concept and social support significantly predicted foreign language enjoyment in a positive direction; however, the interaction did not significantly predict foreign language boredom."
