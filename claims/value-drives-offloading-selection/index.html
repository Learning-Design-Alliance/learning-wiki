Gilbert 2023¶

The review reports Dupont et al. (in press), showing value-based selection of intentions for offloading, with the corollary that internal memory may be left with only low-value information if the external store fails. No effect size is printed.

"Dupont et al. ( in press ) , who showed that high-value intentions (associated with a larger financial reward) were much more likely to be offloaded than low-value intentions."
