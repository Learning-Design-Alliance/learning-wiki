### Martin-Storey 2014

Martin-Storey, A., & Crosnoe, R. (2014). Perceived Neighborhood Safety and Adolescent School Functioning. Applied Developmental Science, 18(2), 61-75. https://doi.org/10.1080/10888691.2014.876276

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM full model predicting grade-9 GPA in the SECCYD sample (n = 924), controlling for perceived safety, census-based neighborhood measures, and family, school, and demographic covariates. The printed standardized magnitude was 20.7% of an SD per SD of disorder; no d, r, or eta-squared value was printed.

> "A significant association was observed, however, between observer-rated physical neighborhood disorder and GPA, such that each standard deviation increase of physical neighborhood disorder was associated with 20.7% of a standard deviation decrease in GPA"
