### Carolien Duijzer 2019

Carolien Duijzer, Marja Van den Heuvel-Panhuizen, Michiel Veldhuis, Michiel Doorman, & Paul Leseman. (2019). Embodied Learning Environments for Graphing Motion: a Systematic Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09471-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Qualitative coding of the 44 included articles, with mediating factors first recorded in the original authors' terminology and then clustered through iterative reading into eight overarching categories, "uncovered eight mediating factors". These are the factors the reviewed authors presumed to mediate learning.

> "Our analysis uncovered eight mediating factors: real-world context, multimodality, linking motion to graph, multiple representations, semiotics, student control, attention capturing,a n d cognitive conflict."

### Carolien Duijzer 2019 (2)

Carolien Duijzer, Marja Van den Heuvel-Panhuizen, Michiel Veldhuis, Michiel Doorman, & Paul Leseman. (2019). Embodied Learning Environments for Graphing Motion: a Systematic Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09471-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Frequency analysis across the corpus found "127 instances" in which authors mentioned a mediating factor; in two articles with two learning environments each, authors mentioned no mediating factors at all. Different combinations of factors were identified in each class of environments.

> "For the 62 embodied learning environments, we found 127 instances in which authors mentioned a mediating factor."
