## Evidence

### von Stumm S 2020

von Stumm S, Smith‐Woolley E, Ayorech Z, McMillan A, Rimfeld K, Dale P S, Plomin R. (2020). Predicting educational achievement from genomic measures and socioeconomic status. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.12925

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Extreme-group analysis of the TEDS sample comparing mixed GPS/SES groups. The article reports high GPS "help compensate for disadvantages" with mean GCSE of −0.09 and 47% university attendance.

> "at age 16, children with high GPS from low SES backgrounds had mean GCSE scores that were close to average (−0.09 standard score) and 47% of these children went on to attend university, as compared to only 21% in the low–low group."
