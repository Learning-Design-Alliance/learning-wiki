Jones 2024¶

Jones, B., Faulkner-Bond, M., Blitz, J., & Garcia-Arena, P. (2024). Accelerate Success for English Learners: Formative Assessment Support for Integrated and Meaningful Language Learning. Region 15 Comprehensive Center, WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673604

q1 · i? · design · r1

The brief asserts, citing prior research (Pryor & Crossouard, 2008, in an endnote), that "teachers' intentional effort to develop a common understanding of the expected learning" benefits all students. No study design, sample, or effect size is reported in the brief itself.

"Research has found that teachers' intentional effort to develop a common understanding of the expected learning, e.g., the learning goals, success criteria, and elements of student tasks, helps all students to be successful"
