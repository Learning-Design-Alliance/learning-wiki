## Evidence

### Donkin L 2011

Donkin L, Christensen H, Naismith SL, Neal B, Hickie IB, Glozier N. (2011). A Systematic Review of the Impact of Adherence on the Effectiveness of e-Therapies. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1772

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's aggregated analysis of 9 studies correlating logins with outcomes found positive associations for physical health targets and null findings for depression. Table 3 rates logins for depression as "0 = null" (n = 2 studies).

> "logins were found to be positively associated with outcome in studies targeting food and vegetable consumption [32], physical activity [33-37], and weight management [38-40]. Number of logins was not found to be related to outcomes in studies targeting depression [23,41]."
