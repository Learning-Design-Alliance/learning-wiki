## Evidence

### Kim Young-Suk Grace 2022

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Wolters Alissa, Mercado Janet, & Quinn Jamie. (2022). Crosslinguistic Transfer of Higher Order Cognitive Skills and Their Roles in Writing for English-Spanish Dual Language Learners. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000516

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CFA of inference, theory of mind, and comprehension monitoring tasks in English and Spanish for the 317-child sample. The article reports ECV = .61 for the general factor, coefficient omega = .85, and that the language-specific factors "were not reliable (ω = .49 and ω = .43, respectively)".

> "The explained common variance (ECV, Riese, 2012) for the higher order cognitive general factor was .61, indicating that 61% of the common variance among the measured cognitive skills was attributable to the general factor."
