## Evidence

### Wu Bian 2016

Wu Bian, Wang Minhong, Grotzer Tina A., Liu Jun, Johnson Janice M. (2016). Visualizing complex processes using a cognitive-mapping tool to support the learning of clinical reasoning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0734-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent t-tests on the last-case learning products in the two-group quasi-experiment (n = 26 per group). DAT: mean difference .16, p = .011; HYP: .31, p = .005; REA: .27, p = .017; CON and COR each .06 in favour of control, p ≈ .54, not significant; effect size for overall performance d = 0.82.

> "Learners in the experimental group were found to outperform those in the control group in several aspects involving data capture, hypotheses formulation, reasoning with justifications, and overall performance"
