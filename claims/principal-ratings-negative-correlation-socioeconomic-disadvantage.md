Correlational analysis of 2013/14 ratings against school student characteristics found "low, negative correlations with student socioeconomic disadvantage" for multiple component measures and the overall rating.

> "Several component measure ratings—school median student growth percentile ratings, teachers’ student growth objective ratings, and principal practice instrument ratings—as well as the overall rating, had low, negative correlations with student socioeconomic disadvantage."
