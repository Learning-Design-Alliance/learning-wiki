### Dillon H. Murphy 2023

Dillon H. Murphy, Jeri L. Little, Elizabeth L. Bjork. (2023). The Value of Using Tests in Education as Tools for Learning—Not Just for Assessment. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09808-3

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reports, citing Cranney et al. (2009), a study comparing a group quiz, an individual quiz, a restudy condition, and a no-activity control on a surprise test after a psychobiology video; "the group quiz condition outperformed the individual quiz condition." No effect size is printed.

> "the group quiz condition outperformed the individual quiz condition"

### Dillon H. Murphy 2023 (2)

Dillon H. Murphy, Jeri L. Little, Elizabeth L. Bjork. (2023). The Value of Using Tests in Education as Tools for Learning—Not Just for Assessment. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09808-3

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary states that "research on group testing versus individual testing has yielded mixed results," citing several studies (e.g., LoGiudice et al., 2015; Wissman & Rawson, 2018) in which group testing was not superior for long-term retention and transfer.

> "research on group testing versus individual testing has yielded mixed results, with some studies showing that group testing is not superior to individual testing for long-term retention and transfer"
