### Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño 2025

Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño. (2025). Dissociable sensory, motor, and visuomotor predictive functions. npj Science of Learning 10:85. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00377-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Time-locked analysis of full-length discrepancy trials in a joystick interception task with angular perturbations of six magnitudes, fitting regressions of IDD* amplitude and AUC on discrepancy magnitude. The article reports "steep and signi ﬁcant slopes" with significant F statistics after FDR correction.

> "Participants showed robustcompensatory changes in IDD* as a function of angular discrepancy magnitude, with steep and signi ﬁcant slopes"

### Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño 2025 (2)

Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño. (2025). Dissociable sensory, motor, and visuomotor predictive functions. npj Science of Learning 10:85. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00377-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of corrected user velocity (vU*) and inter-dot distance (IDD*) against speed-discrepancy magnitude in the interception task: vU* showed strong graded compensation while "IDD* responses to speed discrepancies were non-signiﬁcant". Results replicated across truncated trials.

> "These results indicate that speed discrepancies engaged velocity-based adjustments, rather than spatial trajectory corrections."
