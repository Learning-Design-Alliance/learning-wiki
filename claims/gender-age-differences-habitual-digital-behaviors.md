## Evidence

### Ana Cebollero-Salinas 2022

Ana Cebollero-Salinas, Jacobo Cano-Escoriaza, Santos Orejudo. (2022). Are socio-emotional e-competencies protective factors against habitual digital behaviors (media multitasking, phubbing, cybergossip) in Spanish secondary students?. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104464

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA of habitual digital behaviors by gender (Table 1), n = 776 (427 girls, 349 boys). Phubbing means were 23.15 for females vs. 21.34 for males, F = 14.452, p = .000, η² = .018; cybergossip and multitasking showed no significant gender differences.

> "In terms of gender, the sole significant differences appear in the case of phubbing, where girls score slightly higher than boys."

### Ana Cebollero-Salinas 2022 (2)

Ana Cebollero-Salinas, Jacobo Cano-Escoriaza, Santos Orejudo. (2022). Are socio-emotional e-competencies protective factors against habitual digital behaviors (media multitasking, phubbing, cybergossip) in Spanish secondary students?. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104464

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA by academic year (Table 1), n = 776: multitasking F = 37.165, η² = .046; phubbing F = 34.063, η² = .042; cybergossip F = 19.487, η² = .025, all p = .000, with the authors judging these "differences are considerable, particularly those among academic years".

> "In terms of academic year, the three behaviors display significant differences: more advanced academic years (3rd and 4th year) obtain higher scores than the lower ones (1st and 2 nd year: Table 1)."
