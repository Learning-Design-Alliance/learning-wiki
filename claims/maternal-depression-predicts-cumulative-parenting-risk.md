### Bechtiger 2022

Pathways from Maternal Depressive Symptoms to Children's Academic Performance in Adolescence: A 13-Year Prospective-Longitudinal Study. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13685

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis of the prospective longitudinal community sample; maternal depressive symptoms were modeled as a latent factor from assessments at ages 2, 4, and 5, predicting a cumulative parenting risk score at age 7. The article reports the association as significant.

> "Elevated maternal depressive symptoms in childhood were significantly associated with higher levels of cumulative parenting risk at age 7."
