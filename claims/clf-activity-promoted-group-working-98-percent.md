## Evidence

### Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario 2014

Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario. (2014). Involving Users to Improve the Collaborative Logical Framework. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/893525

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of adaptation as a whole in the 2009 Madrid Science Week observational study with 56 high-school students running 14 CLF activities. The article reports that "98% of the participants answered that in their experience the CLF activity promoted the working in the group".

> "it was obtained that 98% of the participants answered that in their experience the CLF activity promoted the working in the group. By asking the researchers who have been observing the session, all of them agreed with this result (100%)."

### Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario 2014 (2)

Olga C. Santos and Jesus G. Boticario. (2014). Involving Users to Improve the Collaborative Logical Framework. The Scientific World Journal. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/893525

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Behavioural evidence from the same 2009 observational study: observations and log analysis showed "all the participants worked in a collaborative way", publishing individual solutions, rating group mates, and commenting on at least two contributions.

> "from the observations and the log analysis, it was seen that all the participants worked in a collaborative way, as all of them published their individual solution, rated their group mates, and commented to at least two of the group mates' contributions."
