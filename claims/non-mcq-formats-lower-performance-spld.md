`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Narrative synthesis of quantitative studies on complex assessment formats; Gibson reported significantly lower SpLD scores in EMQ, SAQ and OSCEs, while McKendree reported no significant differences in SAQ and OSCE performance — evidence is inconsistent across studies.

> "One study [63] reported significantly lower scores for students with SpLD in EMQ, SAQ, and OSCEs (data and clinical image interpretation)."

### Pavletič Bernarda 2026 (2)

BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09574-0

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

The review reports additional quantitative outcomes: lower first-year scores (Gibson), delayed graduation and higher leave-of-absence rates (Meeks), and improved performance after early diagnosis with accommodations versus poorer final-year outcomes after late diagnosis (Gray).

> "Reported academic outcomes included lower first-year assessment scores across various formats in one study [63], delayed graduation and higher leave-of-absence rates in another [67]"
