## Evidence

### Ananya Ipsita 2025

Ananya Ipsita, Ramesh Kaki, Mayank Patel, Asim Unmesh, Kylie A Peppler, and Karthik Ramani. (2025). Interactive authoring of outcome-oriented lesson plans for immersive Virtual Reality training. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.5574179](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5574179)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

NASA-TLX ratings from the 8-participant comparative study: mental workload fell with complexity in SystemB (χ2 = 11.27, p = 0.0036; Session1 vs Session3 p = 0.0158) but not in SystemA (p = 0.0578).

> "Friedman tests showed that task complexity had a significant effect on mental workload in SystemB (𝜒2 = 11.27,𝑝 = 0.0036), but not in SystemA (𝑝 = 0.0578). Post-hoc Wilcoxon tests revealed a significant difference in SystemB between Session1 and Session3 (𝑝 = 0.0158), whereas SystemA showed no significant pairwise differences."

### Ananya Ipsita 2025 (2)

Ananya Ipsita, Ramesh Kaki, Mayank Patel, Asim Unmesh, Kylie A Peppler, and Karthik Ramani. (2025). Interactive authoring of outcome-oriented lesson plans for immersive Virtual Reality training. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.5574179](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5574179)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Physical workload increased significantly with complexity in SystemA (χ2 = 6.13, p = 0.0468) but not SystemB (p = 0.1738); no pairwise comparison reached significance after Bonferroni correction in either system.

> "For physical workload, the effect of task complexity was significant in SystemA (𝜒2 = 6.13,𝑝 = 0.0468) but not in SystemB (𝑝 = 0.1738), although post-hoc tests revealed no significant pairwise differences in either system after Bonferroni correction."
