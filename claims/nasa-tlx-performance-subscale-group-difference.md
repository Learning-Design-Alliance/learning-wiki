Exploratory subscale analysis in the randomized trial; "differed significantly between groups (p = 0.025)" with control −12.4 (95% CI: -20.0 to -4.8) vs intervention 0.3 (95% CI: -7.9 to 8.6). No effect size printed.

> "In the performance subscale of the NASA-TLX, the change from baseline to final assessment differed significantly between groups (p = 0.025). The control group showed a reduction in perceived performance-related workload (-12.4, 95% CI: -20.0 to -4.8), whereas no relevant change was observed in the intervention group (0.3, 95% CI: -7.9 to 8.6)."
