## Evidence

### Coşkun and Eker 2018

İsmail Coşkun, Cevat Eker. (2018). The Effect of Teaching Activities Done by Using Activity Based Posters on the Students' Academic Achievements, Retention Levels in Their Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(4): 585-597. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.060402

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparison of the control group's pre-test (mean 8.03, SS = 3.28) and post-test (mean 13.03, SS = 4.00), n = 30, taught for six weeks with teacher's guidebook, course book and workbook only. The article reports a significant difference and concludes this teaching was "positively efficient in the success of the student".

> "The difference between the pre-test and post-test scores of the control group was compared with the t test and it was found there was a significant difference with the value obtained (t = 4.8) and p <0.05)."
