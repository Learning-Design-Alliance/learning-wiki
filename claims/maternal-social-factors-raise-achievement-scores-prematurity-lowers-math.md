## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In multivariate linear mixed models, favorable maternal social factors significantly increased both literacy and mathematics scores, and prematurity significantly decreased mathematics scores. [→ ElHassan NO 2018](#elhassan-no-2018)

## Evidence

### ElHassan NO 2018

ElHassan NO, Bai S, Gibson N, Holland G, Robbins JM, Kaiser JR. (2018). The impact of prematurity and maternal socioeconomic status and education level on achievement-test scores up to 8th grade. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198083

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear mixed models of literacy and mathematics scores for the matched cohort of 1424 infants, reported in Results. The article reports favorable social factors were "associated with increased scores" and prematurity significantly decreased mathematics scores; e.g., the HH social-factor literacy coefficient was 149.9 (95% CI 119.3, 180.5).

> "Prematurity was associated with a significant decrease in mathematics scores. Male gender was associated with a significant reduction in literacy and mathematics scores, while higher grade, white or other race, Apgar score at 1 minute ≥7, and favorable social factors were associated with increased scores."
