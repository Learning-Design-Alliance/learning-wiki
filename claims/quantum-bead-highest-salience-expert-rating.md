## Evidence

### Qerimi 2025

Qerimi, L., Malone, S., Rexigel, E., Mehlhase, S., Kuhn, J., Küchemann, S. (2025). Exploring the mechanisms of qubit representations and introducing a new category system for visual representations: Results from expert ratings. arXiv preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-025-00346-1)

`q2 · i1` · `design · r2`

Expert rating study in which 21 quantum physics experts rated four qubit representations on a 1–5 Likert scale; Friedman test showed significant salience differences (χ2(3) = 21.706, p < .001), with "The Quantum Bead (mean: 3.33±1.03) was rated the most salient, followed by Qake (mean: 3 .11 ± 0.88)".

> "The rating for the Quantum Bead was significantly higher than for the Bloch sphere ( d = 0.36) and the Circle Notation ( d = 0.38). In addition, there was a significant difference between Qake and the Circle Notation with d = 0.31, and between Qake and the Bloch sphere (d = 0.32)."
