## Evidence

### Zijun Yin 2025

Zijun Yin, Bin Xuan, Chengchi Liu, Jingchao Yi, Xiaoyan Zheng & Mingming Zhang. (2025). The influence of task and interpersonal interdependence on cooperative behavior and its neural mechanisms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00303-8

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis across 21 channels relating key-pressing scores to IBS. For friend dyads in HICT, correlations were "(r = 0.500*, p = 0.041)" in DLPFC.R and "(r = 0.510*, p = 0.036)" in PTR.R; the authors suggest MNS-facilitated coordination benefits friend dyads' performance.

> "a signiﬁcant positive correlation were found in the HICT between friend dyad’key-pressing score and IBS in BA9 (CH07: DLPFC.R) (r = 0.500*, p = 0.041), and in the HICT between friend dyad’key-pressing score and IBS in BA45 (CH12: Right Pars Triangularis, PTR.R) (r = 0.510*, p = 0.036)."
