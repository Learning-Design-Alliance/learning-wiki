## Evidence

### Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John 2021

Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John. (2021). Simulation debriefing: a perspective from emergency medical care students at three South African Higher Education Institutions. The Pan African Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.11604/pamj.2021.38.97.23009

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional paper-based questionnaire survey of 153 second- to fourth-year emergency medical care students at three South African HEIs; frequency analysis of responses. In the printed Table 2, 88 (57.5%) agreed and 20 (13.1%) strongly agreed the debriefing environment is "one of trust".

> "Participants indicated that there was a general feeling of psychological safety and that there was generally a common understanding between students and the facilitator related to why debriefing took place."
