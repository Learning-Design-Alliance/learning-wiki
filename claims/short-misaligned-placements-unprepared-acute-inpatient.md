### Alexander 2026

Alexander, L., Burdeu, G., Cappello, A., & Griffin, K. (2026). Qualified but Unprepared: The Readiness Paradox in Early Career Mental Health Nurses—A Descriptive Qualitative Study. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.70285

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Educator interview accounts in Theme 1 reported that placement settings were "often poorly aligned with the demands of acute mental health practice", limiting exposure to complex clinical environments. Short placements of "two weeks or three weeks maximum" were seen as insufficient for learning.

> "…they've done their mental health placements in nursing homes…they don't know what they're in for"
