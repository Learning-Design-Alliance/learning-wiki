## Evidence

### Sintayehu Y 2020

Sintayehu Y, Assefa D, Biftu G, Getahun T, Haymanot M, Kasiye S, Teshale M. (2020). Basic neonatal resuscitation skills of midwives and nurses in Eastern Ethiopia are not well retained: An observational study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0236194

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression on 427 participants found education level independently associated with retention; 14.4% of degree holders versus 4.4% of diploma holders retained skills, and "Those with a BSc degree and above were four times more likely to retain skills (AOR, 4.21 [95% CI: 1.60, 11.00])." The article prints an adjusted odds ratio but no standardized effect size.
