`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the 587-student survey (Table 1); the discussion reports that "the lowest score was recorded for Classroom Evaluation and Recognition (3.442 ± 1.086)", and separately that human-media interaction yielded the lowest interaction score (3.548 ± 0.951).

> "Conversely, the lowest score was recorded for Classroom Evaluation and Recognition (3.442 ± 1.086), a difference that may be attributed to the environmental limitations inherent in online learning, such as variations in personal hardware and network stability."
