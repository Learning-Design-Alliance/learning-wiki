Zhao Jingjie 2020¶

Zhao Jingjie, Xu Xinliang, Jiang Hualin, Ding Yi. (2020). The effectiveness of virtual reality-based technology on anatomy teaching: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled studies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1994-z

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression analysis conducted to explore sources of heterogeneity in the primary outcome, reported in Table 3 with coefficients and p values for each factor. No moderator reached significance; no effect size is printed for this null finding.

"We regressed effect sizes on 7 potential moderators: year, country, learners, course, intervention, comparator, and duration. As shown in Table 3, none of the moderators were significant at a level of p < 0.05."
