## Evidence

### Dong Minghua 2026

Dong Minghua, He Huitao, Wei Bowen. (2026). When digital engagement becomes dysregulated: associations with aggression, achievement motivation, and academic performance in adolescents. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1896602

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary analyses in the same sample examining frequency and intensity dimensions separately in adjusted GPA models. Frequency was "not significantly associated with GPA"; intensity's adjusted association was negative but did not reach significance (p = 0.081).

> "frequency was not significantly associated with GPA (B = −0.033, robust SE = 0.028, β = −0.074, 95% CI [−0.095, 0.029], p = 0.238). Intensity showed somewhat larger associations with total aggression (β = 0.270, p < 0.001) and achievement motivation (β = −0.210, p < 0.001)."
