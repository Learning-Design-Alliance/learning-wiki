## Evidence

### Chang Yen-Lin 2026

Chang Yen-Lin, Lin Yi-Chen, Hung Liang-Ching, Chen Hsiu-Mei, Hsieh Tsu-Yi, Yen Chih-Ho. (2026). Virtual reality simulation for chemotherapy drug spill management training in pharmacy education: A cohort-allocated crossover quasi-experimental study in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09864-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Between-cohort Mann–Whitney U comparison of first-phase change scores (T1 − T0) in the results section: Cohort 1 (n = 17) exceeded Cohort 2 (n = 20) on Q1 (p = 0.006) and Q3 (p = 0.047); Q2 and Q4 were not significant. No effect sizes printed.

> "First-phase change scores were greater in Cohort 1 than in Cohort 2 for Q1 (median change, 2.0 vs. 1.0; p = 0.006) and Q3 (2.0 vs. 1.0; p = 0.047) (Table 4). No statistically significant between-cohort differences were observed for Q2 (p = 0.115) or Q4 (p = 0.242)."

### Chang Yen-Lin 2026 (2)

Chang Yen-Lin, Lin Yi-Chen, Hung Liang-Ching, Chen Hsiu-Mei, Hsieh Tsu-Yi, Yen Chih-Ho. (2026). Virtual reality simulation for chemotherapy drug spill management training in pharmacy education: A cohort-allocated crossover quasi-experimental study in Taiwan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09864-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The results section states the design limitation directly: "the observed differences cannot be attributed to instructional modality alone" because modality was confounded with cohort and calendar period.

> "Because the first instructional modality was completely confounded with training cohort and calendar period, the observed differences cannot be attributed to instructional modality alone."
