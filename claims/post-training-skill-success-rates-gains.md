## Evidence

### Turan D 2026

Turan D, Pekmezci H. (2026). Simulation-based education in paramedic training: effects of perceived competence and practice frequency on clinical skills. BMC Medical Education, 26:487. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08541-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Direct-observation skill assessment of 60 students using standardized checklists derived from American Heart Association and American College of Surgeons guidelines, scored on a four-level system; Table 7 reports post-training success of 100% in oxygen administration and fracture stabilization and 90% in endotracheal intubation.

> "Compared with the pre-training assessment, post-training total performance scores and success rates increased across all eight practical skills. The most pronounced improvements were observed in advanced procedures such as endotracheal intubation, basic and advanced life support, defibrillation, and cardiac arrest management, indicating a substantial enhancement in students' clinical skill performance following the training."
