## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students' cumulative Discovery grades were significantly higher than their cumulative classroom grades (p < 0.0001). [→ Davenport Huyer 2020](#davenport-huyer-2020)

## Evidence

### Davenport Huyer 2020

Davenport Huyer, L., Callaghan, N. I., Dicks, S., Scherer, E., Shukalyuk, A. I., Jou, M. & Kilkenny, D. (2020). Enhancing senior high school student engagement and academic performance using an inclusive and scalable inquiry-based program. npj Science of Learning 5:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00076-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Paired comparison of teacher-assigned grades for 268 student-term instances over five terms, comparing cumulative Discovery grade with final course grade with the Discovery contribution removed, showing students performed "significantly higher in their cumulative Discovery grade" (p < 0.0001).

> "Students performed signiﬁcantly higher in their cumulative Discovery grade than in their cumulative classroom grade ( ﬁnal course grade less the Discovery contribution; p < 0.0001)."

### Davenport Huyer 2020 (2)

Davenport Huyer, L., Callaghan, N. I., Dicks, S., Scherer, E., Shukalyuk, A. I., Jou, M. & Kilkenny, D. (2020). Enhancing senior high school student engagement and academic performance using an inclusive and scalable inquiry-based program. npj Science of Learning 5:17. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00076-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of the full five-term dataset (268 instances) found a highly significant correlation (p < 0.0001) between combined Discovery deliverable grades and final course grade, indicating the two measures track together despite the overall differential.

> "Nevertheless, there was a highly signi ﬁcant correlation ( p < 0.0001) observed between the grade representing combined Discovery deliverables and the ﬁnal course grade (Fig. 2a)."
