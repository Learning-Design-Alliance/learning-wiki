Longitudinal follow-up survey administered to students from the 2017-2022 cohorts during their seventh semester of clinical training (N = 334 respondents, approximately 37.8% of eligible students). The article reports "The highest-rated items were: 'improved my ability to apply basic science knowledge to explain clinical phenomena' (Item 3, M = 4.73, SD = 0.45)".

> "The highest-rated items were: "improved my ability to apply basic science knowledge to explain clinical phenomena" (Item 3, M = 4.73, SD = 0.45), closely followed by "better prepared me for the transition from basic science learning to clinical clerkships" (Item 4, M = 4.67, SD = 0.47)."
