### Garbacz 2019

Garbacz, S. A., Stormshak, E. A., McIntyre, L. L., & Kosty, D. (2019). Examining family-school engagement in a randomized controlled trial of the family check-up. School Psychology, 34, 433–443. https://doi.org/10.1037/spq0000284

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of parent consultant log (PACL) data for the 164 FCU-condition families. Families completing treatment averaged 1.53 visits (range 0 to 17.00), total treatment time averaged 143.24 minutes (range 0 to 915), and therapists averaged 4.05 contacts.

> "Of the 164 families in the FCU condition, 144 (88%) completed the initial meeting, 143 completed a feedback session (87%), and 80 (49%) completed treatment sessions after the feedback session."
