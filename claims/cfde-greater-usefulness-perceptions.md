# CFDE students report significantly greater usefulness perceptions, especially ease of navigating and transitioning between content and discussion

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` CFDE students rated ease of navigation, transitioning to discussion without losing motivation, and returning between discussion and content significantly higher than TDE students (F=7.14, 5.20, 9.96, 10.72; p≤0.05). [→ Yilmaz 2016](#yilmaz-2016)
`q3 i?` Feeling lost in the learning environment did not differ significantly between groups (F=2.18, p=0.14). [→ Yilmaz 2016 (2)](#yilmaz-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Yilmaz 2016

Yilmaz, E. O., & Yurdugul, H. (2016). Design and effects of a concept focused discussion environment in e-learning. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 63, 353-374. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.63.20

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on the researcher-developed five-item usefulness perception scale in the 90-student experiment; CFDE means exceeded TDE on navigation (4.33 vs 3.86), transition to discussion (4.02 vs 3.61), return to discussion (4.15 vs 3.59) and return to content (4.09), all significant.

> "In terms of usefulness perceptions, these differences are determined to be statistically significant to the advantage of the experiment group environment CFDE (P≤0.05)."

### Yilmaz 2016 (2)

Yilmaz, E. O., & Yurdugul, H. (2016). Design and effects of a concept focused discussion environment in e-learning. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 63, 353-374. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.63.20

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The 'I found myself lost' item showed no significant group difference (F=2.18, p=0.14); the article states "students did not feel lost" in either environment.

> "students involved in CFDE experiment groups had an average perception of (2.17), while students involved in TDE had a higher average perception of (2.45); when this difference to the advantage of the experiment group underwent variance analysis, it was not revealed to be statistically significant (p>0.05)."
