Theme Analysis of the narratives found anger and fear the most prevalent CSE emotions. The article states “the use of physical tests (evaluation) as one of the most predominant assessment instruments is the first cause for which participants affirm having felt fear in CSE PE”.

> "However, in CSE PE, the most predominant emotions were anger (30), fear (27), and joy (23), followed by disgust (16), sadness (10), and surprise (6)."
