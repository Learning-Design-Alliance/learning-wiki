Benedict du Boulay 2024¶

Benedict du Boulay. (2024). Pedagogy, Cognition, Human Rights, and Social Justice. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 34:116–121. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00355-0

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary's framing statement, citing critics such as Selwyn, Watters and Williamson, about the commercial and academic field's rollout of AI tools. The author accepts these concerns as legitimate while disputing that blame lies with the academic AIED community.

"It is clear that there are legitimate concerns about aied's rollout of tools and applications in education making use of (or claiming to make use of) Artificial Intelligence in terms of datafication and surveillance and possibly also of poor pedagogy"
