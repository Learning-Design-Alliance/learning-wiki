## Evidence

### Johnels Jakob Åsberg 2025

Johnels Jakob Åsberg, Kuja‐Halkola Ralf, Larsson Henrik, Chang Zheng, Brikell Isabel, Lundström Sebastian. (2025). Does the family situation impact academic achievement differently in students with versus without neurodevelopmental disorders? The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70050

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive and between-group comparison in a register-linked cohort of 12,029 Swedish twins (8% ADHD, 2% autism by screening at age 9). The study reports "increased by approximately a factor of 4–5" risk of non-eligibility and significantly lower total grade scores (both p < .001).

> "With regards to child educational achievement the risk for non‐eligibility to upper secondary school was increased by approximately a factor of 4–5 in both ADHD and autism (p < .001). Similarly, the total grade scores were significantly lower in the ADHD and the autism groups compared with the non‐ADHD/non‐autism group (p < .001)."
