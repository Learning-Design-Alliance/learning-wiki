Selen Galiç 2026¶

Selen Galiç, Mathias Tejera, Sebastián Parodi, Markus Hohenwarter, Zsolt Lavicza. (2026). Preservice mathematics teachers' noticing in AI-based simulations: transitions among attending, interpreting, and shaping. ZDM – Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01805-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Epistemic Network Analysis of coded noticing statements from 17 preservice teachers across a three-week AI-based simulation intervention. The ENA models visualised co-occurrences among attending, interpreting, and shaping, with the article reporting "the strongest co-occurrences were consistently observed between Interpreting and Shaping" with the printed edge values per week.

"Across the three weeks, the strongest co-occurrences were consistently observed between Interpreting and Shaping (Week 1: 0.57; Week 2: 0.54; Week 3: 0.63), indicating a close relationship between sense-making and eliciting."
