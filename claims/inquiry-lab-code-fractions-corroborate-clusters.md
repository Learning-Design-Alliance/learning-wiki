# Averaged code fractions corroborate the cluster result: men handle equipment more and women use laptops more in inquiry labs

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In inquiry labs, men spent a larger fraction of coded time handling equipment than women (13 ± 1% vs 9 ± 1%, p = 0.0097), and women spent a larger fraction on laptops or personal devices than men (31 ± 3% vs 23 ± 2%, p = 0.0082). [→ Katherine N. Quinn 2019](#katherine-n-quinn-2019)
