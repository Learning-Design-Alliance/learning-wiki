`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Synthesis of nine included reviews in an overview of reviews. The authors state that "adaptive performance is best referred to as the visible expression of adaptive expertise", noting the terms are related but not synonymous; adaptive expertise can be present without being visible in performance.

> "Although not used unambiguously by all authors, adaptive performance is best referred to as the visible expression of adaptive expertise."
