## Evidence

### Dickes 2016

Dickes, A., Farris, A., & Sengupta, P. (2016). Integrating Agent-based Programming with Elementary Science: The Role of Sociomathematical Norms. Proceedings of the 24th International Conference on Computers in Education. Asia-Pacific Society for Computers in Education. [doi:10.58459/icce.2016.1152](https://doi.org/10.58459/icce.2016.1152)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

End-of-Phase-II classroom activity: students modeled Car 1 traveling at 45 mph for 3 hours and Car 2 at 35 mph for 4 hours; "all of them were able to produce ViMAP models that used appropriate and non-redundant variables", with multiplicative reasoning evident in repeat and step-size use.

> "As students shared their ViMAP models, we noticed that all of them were able to produce ViMAP models that used appropriate and non-redundant variables."
