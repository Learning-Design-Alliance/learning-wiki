### Moslemi Najarcolaie Fatemeh 2026 (2)

Moslemi Najarcolaie Fatemeh, Kordian Arezoo, Salamat Fatemehzahra, Rezaei Fatemeh, Sanagoo Akram, Firouzbakht Mojgan, Jouybari Leila. (2026). The efficacy of patient-in-classroom instruction compared to traditional lectures: a quasi-experimental study in nursing education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09586-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Sensitivity analyses in the same study: intention-to-treat with 20 imputations (n = 700) gave "mean difference = 10.85, d = 1.91", and a three-level mixed-effects model accounting for clustering by section and semester remained significant (β = 10.89, SE = 0.56).

> "Intention-to-treat analysis using multiple imputation (n = 700) yielded consistent results (mean difference = 10.85, d = 1.91, P < 0.001). Mixed-effects modeling accounting for clustering by section and semester showed that the intervention effect remained significant (β = 10.89, SE = 0.56, P < 0.001)."
