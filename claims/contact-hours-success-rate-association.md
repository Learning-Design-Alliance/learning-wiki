# Higher total contact hours for the paired courses were associated with higher institutional success rates (4 hours: 39%; 5 hours: 61%; 6 hours: 67%)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Institutional success rates rose with total contact hours assigned to the paired courses, with the lowest rate at four contact hours and three of the four highest rates at six contact hours. [→ Strother 2019](#strother-2019)

## Evidence

### Strother 2019

Strother, S., & Klipple, K. (2019). Corequisite remediation in math: A review of first-year implementation and outcomes of Quantway and Statway. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://carnegiemathpathways.org/reports/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-institutional comparison in the review's results section, summarized in Table 2, which prints success rates of 39 percent at 4 contact hours, 61 percent at 5 contact hours, and 67 percent at 6 contact hours. The report describes this as an apparent connection, not a tested effect; no statistic or effect size is printed.

> "Three of the four highest institutional success rates for the new course offering came from the institutions with six contact hours, and the lowest institutional success rate was from the institution with four contact hours."
