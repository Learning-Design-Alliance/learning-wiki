Angeline S. Lillard 2019¶

Angeline S. Lillard. (2019). Shunned and Admired: Montessori, Self-Determination, and a Case for Radical School Reform. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09483-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports the Lillard and Heise (2016) experimental implementation study, in which "the non-Montessori materials were removed" from classrooms and children advanced more in reading and executive function across a semester than in a comparison classroom that retained them.

"Children in classrooms in which the non-Montessori materials were removed advanced more in reading and executive function across a semester than children in a classroom that retained its non-Montessori materials"
