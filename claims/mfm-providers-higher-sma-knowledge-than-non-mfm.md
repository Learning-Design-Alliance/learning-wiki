## Evidence

### Riegel 2026

Riegel, Melissa, Whitney Bender, Elizabeth Critchlow, and Lorraine Dugoff. (2026). Spinal Muscular Atrophy Carrier Screening: Assessment of Provider Knowledge and Clinical Practice. Prenatal Diagnosis. https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.70023

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey results comparing knowledge scores by clinical role (Table 3). MFM attendings scored "5.5 ± 1.4" and MFM fellows "4.3 ± 0.5", significantly higher than other roles (p < 0.001); MFM providers also had significantly higher proportions scoring ≥ 75% and ≥ 50%.

> "MFM providers had the greatest mean total scores compared to non‐MFM providers, with mean scores of 5.5 ± 1.4 for MFM attendings and 4.3 ± 0.5 for MFM fellows (Table 3, p < 0.001)."
