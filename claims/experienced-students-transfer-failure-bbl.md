## Evidence

### Mihci 2017

Mihci, C. & Ozdener Donmez, N. (2017). Teaching GUI-programming concepts to prospective K12 teachers: MIT App Inventor as an alternative to text based languages. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 3(2), 543-559. https://doi.org/10.21890/ijres.327912

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the Phase 1 subgroup results: experienced students, mostly educated in text-based C# in vocational high school and the first-tier course, performed significantly worse with App Inventor (t(19)=2.35 favoring TBL) despite expectations of at least equal success.

> "The ―experienced‖ students have shown difficulty transferring their existing knowledge of programming to the blocks -based environment of App Inventor."
