Emilyn Ruble Whitesell 2016¶

Emilyn Ruble Whitesell, Leanna Stiefel, Amy Ellen Schwartz. (2016). Unexpected Arrivals: The Spillover Effects of Mid-Year Entry on Stable Student Achievement in New York City. Education Evaluation and Policy Analysis, vol. 38, no. 4. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/unexpected-arrivals-the-spillover-effects-of-mid-year-entry-on-stable-student-achievement-in-new

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The introduction states that "much research documents the impact of changing schools on the academic achievement of mobile students themselves" while "less research explores whether new arrivals have negative spillovers on stable classmates." This is the authors' literature-gap statement, offered without cited counts.

"While much research documents the impact of changing schools on the academic achievement of mobile students themselves, less research explores whether new arrivals have negative spillovers on stable classmates."
