### Freundt Paula 2023

Freundt Paula, Nourkami-Tutdibi Nasenien, Tutdibi Erol, Janzing Paul, von Ostrowski Thomas, Langer Martin, Zemlin Michael, Steinhard Johannes. (2023). Controlled Prospective Study on the Use of Systematic Simulator-Based Training with a Virtual, Moving Fetus for Learning Second-Trimester Scan: FESIM III. Ultraschall in der Medizin. https://doi.org/10.1055/a-1984-8320

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Standardized hands-on simulator test in the prospective controlled trial: the novice trial group's TTC of 620±189 seconds beat the physician group's 1036±389 seconds, a result the authors call unexpected and a major significant finding.

> "After six weeks of self-training, the trial group scored a significantly faster TTC than the reference group of physicians (620±189 vs. 1036±389 seconds, p<0.001) while the expert group remained significantly faster in its overall performance (443±243 seconds, p<0.001)."
