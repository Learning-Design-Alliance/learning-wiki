### Marina Babayeva 2025

Marina Babayeva. (2025). Enhancing Physics Hand-on Lab through Online Educational Tools. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.16193](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.16193)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom experience with Miro.com during the pilot: teachers reported "long loading times, connection instability, and a non-intuitive interface". The article also reports Wizer.me required public publishing in the free version with limited question types, while Formative.com offered real-time analytics but limited visual customization causing students to skip content.

> "teachers reported significant technical difficulties including long loading times, connection instability, and a non-intuitive interface, especially for first-time users without touchscreen devices. These usability issues outweighed the collaborative potential in practice."
