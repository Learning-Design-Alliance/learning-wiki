Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-65716-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Within- and between-group comparisons of the five learning outcomes across practice repetitions in the RCT; lower bleeding risk scores indicate better performance. Only CIs and p-values are printed, no standardized effect size.

> "The ICEMS group achieved significantly lower bleeding risk score by the third repetition of the task when compared to the baseline (.135, 95% CI [.021 .249], p = .013) while the other two groups had no statistically significant improvement."

### Yilmaz Recai 2024 (2)

Yilmaz Recai, Bakhaidar Mohamad, Alsayegh Ahmad, Abou Hamdan Nour, Fazlollahi Ali M., Tee Trisha, Langleben Ian, Winkler-Schwartz Alexander, Laroche Denis, Santaguida Carlo, Del Maestro Rolando F. (2024). Real-Time multifaceted artificial intelligence vs In-Person instruction in teaching surgical technical skills: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-65716-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group tissue injury risk contrasts from the learning-outcomes analysis of the RCT, with printed CIs and p-values; lower scores indicate better performance. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "The ICEMS group achieved significantly lower tissue injury risk score in the third repetition of the task and onwards when compared to the post-hoc feedback and in-person expert instruction group − .078, 95% CI [− .113 − .042], p < .001 and − .041, 95% CI [− 077. − .007], p = .009, respectively."

### Yilmaz Recai 2024 (3)

Yilmaz Recai, Bakhaidar Mohamad, Alsayegh Ahmad, Abou Hamdan Nour, Fazlollahi Ali M., Tee Trisha, Langleben Ian, Winkler-Schwartz Alexander, Laroche Denis, Santaguida Carlo, Del Maestro Rolando F. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-65716-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Instrument-utilization metric contrasts from the RCT's learning-outcomes analysis; a value of '0' indicated no difference from expert level. The quote prints both CIs and p-values, no standardized effect size.

> "Students who receive real-time AI feedback applied significantly less bipolar force and they used their instruments closer in the fifth repetition of the task when compared to the expert instruction group − .299, 95% CI [− .503 − .095], p = .001 and − .143, 95% CI [− .229 − .059], p < .001, respectively."
