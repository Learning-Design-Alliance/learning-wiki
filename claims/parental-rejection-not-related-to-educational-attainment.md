## Evidence

### Lorijn 2021

Lorijn, S. J.; Engels, M. C.; Huisman, M.; Veenstra, R. (2021). Long-Term Effects of Acceptance and Rejection by Parents and Peers on Educational Attainment: A Study from Pre-Adolescence to Early Adulthood. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-021-01506-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null result from the same ordinal logistic regression (Model 1, N = 2229) using the shortened EMBU-C scale at T1. The article reports "OR = 0.73, p = 0.086"; equivalence was not tested.

> "In contrast, perceived parental rejection was not significantly associated with educational attainment (OR = 0.73, p = 0.086); thus, the second hypothesis was not supported."
