New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform 2022¶

New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform, while many other places did not. (2022). UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

This is a school-level comparison from the districtwide study of surveys, interviews, and achievement data between 2014-15 and 2017-18. The release reports "stronger gains on assessments" in high-use schools than in low-use schools. No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"In schools where students and teachers reported frequently using standards-aligned practices in their math and science classes, there were stronger gains on assessments than in schools where few students or teachers reported frequent use of standards-aligned practices"
