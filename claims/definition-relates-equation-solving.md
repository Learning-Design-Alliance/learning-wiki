### Şimşek 2021

Şimşek, E., Xenidou-Dervou, I., Hunter, J., Gillon Dowens, M., Pang, J., Lee, Y., McNeil, N., Kirkland, P., & Jones, I. (2021). Factors Associated with Children's Understanding of Mathematical Equivalence: An Investigation across Six Countries. https://figshare.com/s/78625a6b0061200db6b7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel SEM (Model 1) across 2760 students in six countries found a significant positive relation between the equation-solving and definition latent factors. The article reports "a significant positive relation between equation solving and definition" with no effect size printed.

> "At the student level, we found a significant positive relation between equation solving and definition, and age was not associated with equation solving or definition."
