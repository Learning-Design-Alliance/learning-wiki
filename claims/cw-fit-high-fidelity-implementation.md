## Evidence

### Debra Kamps 2015

Debra Kamps, Howard Wills, Harriett Dawson-Bannister, Linda Heitzman-Powell, Esther Kottwitz, Blake Hansen, and Kandace Fleming (2015). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams "CW-FIT" Efficacy Trial Outcomes. Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions. https://doi.org/10.1177/1098300714565244

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity data from a 13-item procedural fidelity checklist collected 1 to 2 times per week, totaling 1,851 checks for the experimental group and 975 for comparison. Fidelity "averaging 92.4%" in CW-FIT classrooms.

> "Teachers in CW-FIT classrooms implemented the intervention with high fidelity averaging 92.4%. The use of procedures was low during baseline/non CW-FIT conditions for all groups (1%-2%)."
