### Michael C. Wittmann 2002

Michael C. Wittmann. (2002). Limitations in predicting student performance on standardized tests. Originally published 1998. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0209006](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0209006)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

MPEX administered to the same 10 matched students pre and post instruction. The class overall score moved from (14, 65) to (20, 68) on the (unfavorable, favorable) plot, with responses becoming "less neutral" in nearly all clusters per the normalized movement plot of Figure 2b.

> "these students had better scores initially (pre: 14, 65). Notably, their MPEX results did not deteriorate during the course of the semester. Instead, student responses became less neutral (post: 20, 68), including more favorable and unfavorable responses."
