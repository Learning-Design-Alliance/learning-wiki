Evidence¶

Willower 1992¶

Willower, Donald J. (1992). Dewey's Theory of Inquiry and Reflective Administration. Paper presented at the annual convention of the University Council for Educational Administration, Minneapolis. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED355669

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical exposition of Dewey's account of internalization, presented in the paper's section on internalization in administrator preparation. The paper adds that Dewey saw internalization as depending on facilitative and supportive social arrangements, making internalization and institutionalization symbiotic. No empirical evidence is offered for this claim in the paper.

"if reflection is to play a part in moral choice in everyday affairs, it has tobe internalized as habit."
