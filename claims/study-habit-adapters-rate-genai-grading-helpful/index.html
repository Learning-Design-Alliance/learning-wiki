Evidence¶

Ethan Dickey 2026¶

Ethan Dickey, Marios Mertzanidis, Alexandros Psomas. (2026). Is Solving Better Than Evaluating GenAI Solutions? https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27586

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Post-final survey (N=200, 91% response rate) with a three-point item on whether the modified homework changed exam preparation; helpfulness showed a neutral median rating (3.0) in both groups.

"the majority of students reported no change (154/200, 77.0%), followed by "Kind of" (32/200, 16.0%) and "Yes" (14/200, 7.0%)."

Ethan Dickey 2026 (2)¶

Ethan Dickey, Marios Mertzanidis, Alexandros Psomas. (2026). Is Solving Better Than Evaluating GenAI Solutions? https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27586

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Association analysis on the post-final survey; pairwise Mann-Whitney tests with Bonferroni adjustment showed No vs. Kind of (padj=.019) and No vs. Yes (padj=.002) significant, Kind of vs. Yes not significant (padj=.122).

"A Kruskal–Wallis test indicated a statistically significant difference in helpfulness ratings across the three study-change categories (𝐻(2)=17.74,𝑝<.001)."
