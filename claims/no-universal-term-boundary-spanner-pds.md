## Evidence

### Burns 2015

Burns, R. W., & Baker, W. (2015). The Boundary Spanner in Professional Development Schools: In Search of Common Nomenclature. School-University Partnerships, 9(2), 28-40. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130688

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative meta-analysis of thirteen empirical studies on PDS boundary-spanning roles. The authors report fourteen distinct terms used across the literature, concluding "there currently is no agreed upon terminology or common understanding for formalized boundary-spanning roles in PDS."

> "In fact, we found fourteen different terms in the empirical literature, and they included: boundary spanners (Christenson, et al., 2008; Fisher & Many, 2014; Many, et al., 2012), clinical educators (Christenson, et al., 2008), clinical faculty (Myers & Price, 2010), clinical faculty associates"
