## Evidence

### Rodrigo 2014

Rodrigo, V., Greenberg, D., & Segal, D. (2014). Changes in reading habits by low literate adults through extensive reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 26(1), 73-91. http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analysis of Reading Pattern Survey data after a 100-hour intervention with adults reading at grade 3-5.9, comparing the merged ER group (ER and DCEF models) with the merged no-ER group (DF and DCF). The article reports the difference as statistically significant with "Eta=.465"; no standardized effect size in d, g, or r form is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "Thirty-six percent of the ER group read 11 to 40 books versus 2% of the no-ER. Chi-square shows that the two groups are now different, and this difference is statistically significant (χ2 = 29.026, df = 3, n = 134, p =.000, Eta=.465) after treatment."
