# Tests disagree about which EBM topics are hardest, and closed-format tests mask pre-course knowledge of application skills

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Fresno test scores were significantly lower than ACE and Berlin scores on Internal validity, Diagnostic accuracy, and Searching, but higher on Asking questions. [→ Buljan Ivan 2018](#buljan-ivan-2018)
`q3 i?` On the Application topic, ACE and Berlin showed no apparent improvement while Fresno showed the largest increase, which the authors attribute to guessing and answer guidance in closed-format questions. [→ Buljan Ivan 2018 (2)](#buljan-ivan-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Buljan Ivan 2018

Buljan Ivan, Jerončić Ana, Malički Mario, Marušić Matko, Marušić Ana. (2018). How to choose an evidence-based medicine knowledge test for medical students? Comparison of three knowledge measures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1391-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Topic-specific post-test comparison of percent-of-maximum scores across the three tests in the EBM group (Table 4); the only topic where Fresno scored higher was Asking questions (P = 0.002 vs ACE).

> "Fresno test scores were significantly lower than those of the other two tests on the topics of Internal validity, Diagnostic accuracy, and Searching (Table 4, Wilcoxon signed rank tests or Friedman test of P < 0.001 for all with post hoc analysis, P ≤ 0.001 for all)."

### Buljan Ivan 2018 (2)

Buljan Ivan, Jerončić Ana, Malički Mario, Marušić Matko, Marušić Ana. (2018). How to choose an evidence-based medicine knowledge test for medical students? Comparison of three knowledge measures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1391-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Topic-level change analysis (Table 4); the authors interpret the Application discrepancy as closed-format questions guiding or allowing guessing (about 50% for binary, 20% for multiple choice), unlike Fresno's open-ended items.

> "In the ACE and Berlin tests, the only EBM topic on which educational intervention had apparently no effect was Application (Table 4, one-sample Wilcoxon signed rank test for change, P ≥ 0.169)."
