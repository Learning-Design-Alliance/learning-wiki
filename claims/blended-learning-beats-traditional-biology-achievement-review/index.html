Suprabha. K 2015¶

Suprabha. K, & Subramonian, G. (2015). Blended learning approach for enhancing students learning experiences in a knowledge society. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 11(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1098578

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review reports, citing Yapici and Akbayin (2012), a study of the effect of a blended learning model on high school students' biology achievement, in which "the blended learning model contributed more to the students' biology achievement than Traditional teaching methods". No effect size is printed.

"Yapici, I. U., and Akbayin, H. (2012), have studied the effect of blended learning model on high school students' biology achievement and on their attitudes towards the internet. Their research results revealed that the blended learning model contributed more to the students' biology achievement than Traditional teaching methods."
