## Evidence

### Petersen Sandra L. 2018

Petersen Sandra L., Erenrich Evelyn S., Levine Dovev L., Vigoreaux Jim, Gile Krista. (2018). Multi-institutional study of GRE scores as predictors of STEM PhD degree completion: GRE gets a low mark. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206570

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression and two-way ANOVA of GRE scores and completion for 1805 U.S./Permanent Resident STEM doctoral students at four state flagship universities (Table 2). Men who did not complete had mean GRE Q "722.8***", significantly higher than men who completed (698.9), p<0.0001; GRE V also significantly higher (551.6 vs 535.7, p<0.05).
