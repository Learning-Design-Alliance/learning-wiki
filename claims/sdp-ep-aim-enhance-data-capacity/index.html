Descriptive statement of the programs' aims as printed in the study overview. The article offers this as the stated purpose of the SDP and EP programs, with no effect size or outcome data on this page.

"The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation contracted with Mathematica to conduct an implementation study of the Strategic Data Project (SDP) and Education Pioneers (EP) programs, which aim to enhance the capacity of school districts and other education agencies to collect, manage, analyze, and use data through the support, training, and placement of additional staff."
