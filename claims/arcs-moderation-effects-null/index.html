PLS-SEM moderation tests (Table 8) in the 842-student Lesotho survey; all four interaction hypotheses were coded Not supported, with printed interaction coefficients between −0.020 and 0.012 and no effect sizes reported.

"None of the interaction effects reached statistical significance (H10a–H10d), including ARCS × performance expectancy, ARCS × effort expectancy, and ARCS × social influence on behavioral intention, as well as ARCS × facilitating conditions on use behavior (all p > .05)."
