## Evidence

### Dorottya Demszky and Heather Hill 2023

Dorottya Demszky and Heather Hill. (2023). The NCTE Transcripts: A Dataset of Elementary Math Classroom Transcripts. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.18653/v1/2023.bea-1.44](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.bea-1.44)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Teacher-year level regressions of value-added scores on aggregated discourse move predictions (Table 5). The article reports the printed coefficients of 0.121, 0.234 and 0.191 (p < 0.05) for uptake, focusing questions and student reasoning respectively.

> "Specifically, each additional High Uptake per 7.5 minute segment in- creases teachers' value added scores by 12% of a standard deviation. Analogically, each additional instance of a Focusing Question and Student Rea- soning per 7.5 minute segment increases teachers' value added scores by 23% and 19% of a stan- dard deviation, respectively."
