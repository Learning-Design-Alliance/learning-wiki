# AU and OC conditions show distinct correlation patterns with external creativity and fluency measures, supporting their differentiation as divergent thinking versus verbal ability conditions

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` AU-score correlated significantly with AUT-brick fluency (r = 0.54) and flexibility (r = 0.36) but not verbal fluency, while OC-score correlated with both fluency measures but not flexibility or originality. [→ Kleibeuker 2013](#kleibeuker-2013)

## Evidence

### Kleibeuker 2013

Kleibeuker, S. W., Koolschijn, P. C. M. P., Jolles, D. D., De Dreu, C. K. W., and Crone, E. A. (2013). The neural coding of creative idea generation across adolescence and early adulthood. Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 7:905. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2013.00905

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Bivariate correlations between scanner task scores and out-of-scanner measures. AU-score correlated with AUT-brick fluency (r = 0.54, p < 0.001) and flexibility (r = 0.36, p = 0.02) but not verbal fluency (r = 0.26, p > 0.1); OC-score correlated with both fluency measures, supporting the conditions' differentiation.

> "AU-score corre- lated signiﬁcantly with AUT-brick ﬂuency ( r = 0.54, p < 0.001) and AUT-brick ﬂexibility ( r = 0.36, p = 0.02), and marginally with AUT-brick originality ( r = 0.30, p = 0.07), but not with verbal ﬂuency ( r = 0.26, p > 0.1)"
