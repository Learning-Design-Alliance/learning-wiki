## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Melissa Roderick 2013

Melissa Roderick, Vanessa Coca, Eliza Moeller, and Thomas Kelley-Kemple. (2013). From High School to the Future: The Challenge of Senior Year in Chicago Public Schools. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-challenge-senior-year-chicago-public-schools

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive transcript analysis of 2003-09 CPS graduates (N=90,041) excluding special education and alternative/charter students. The report states "More than 70 percent of white and Asian American" graduates took four or more core senior courses versus 54 percent of African American and 58 percent of Latino graduates; similar gaps appear for AP and fourth-year math.

> "More than 70 percent of white and Asian American high schools graduates in our sample took four or more courses in core academic subjects during their senior year, compared with 54 percent of African American and 58 percent of Latino graduates."
