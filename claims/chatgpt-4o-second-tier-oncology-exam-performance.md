## Evidence

Erdat, E. C., & Kavak, E. E. (2025). Benchmarking LLM chatbots’ oncological knowledge with the Turkish Society of Medical Oncology’s annual board examination questions. *BMC Cancer*. [doi:10.1186/s12885-025-13596-0](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-025-13596-0)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Category-level results (Table 3) report ChatGPT 4o answering 102 of 118 basic oncology questions correctly (86.4%) versus 64.4% for Llama-3 and 60.2% for Gemini 1.5; its weakest categories were colorectal (52.7%) and genitourinary (56.3%) tumors.
