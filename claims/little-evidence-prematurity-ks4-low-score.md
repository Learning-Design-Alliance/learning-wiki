## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For low KS4 score, adjusted analyses across matching strategies showed little evidence of a prematurity effect (e.g. EDD and same-year fully adjusted OR 1.00, 95% CI 0.80–1.26). [→ Odd David 2016](#odd-david-2016)

## Evidence

### Odd David 2016

Odd David, Evans David, Emond Alan. (2016). Preterm Birth, Age at School Entry and Long Term Educational Achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0155157

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Conditional regression analysis of the ALSPAC cohort (11,405 infants with KS4 outcome data) for the low KS4 score outcome (below 5 GCSEs at A*–C). Table 3 prints fully adjusted ORs including "1.00 (0.80 to 1.26)" for the EDD-and-same-year model; no statistically significant association was detected.

> "At KS3 and KS4 there was little evidence for an impact of prematurity in any of the adjusted analyses."
