`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional latent-factor cognitive models at ages 12 and 14 in 1,673 SCAMP adolescents, using language status as predictor with SES and ethnicity adjustment. The article prints no effect size for this finding; the Discussion notes the largest cognitive effect was on verbal working memory tasks rather than cognitive flexibility.
