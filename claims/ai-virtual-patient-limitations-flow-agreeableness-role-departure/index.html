Evidence¶

Fang Eric Kin Meng 2026¶

Fang Eric Kin Meng, Boon Yuru, Ho Jin Yang, Tey Natasha, Low Matthew Jian Wen. (2026). Artificial intelligence generated virtual patients in health professions education: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10028-w

q3 · i? · review · r3

Qualitative synthesis of limitations charted across 97 included studies of AI-generated virtual patients in HPE. The review reports limitations including "lack of natural conversational flow", "being excessively agreeable", and role departure such as prematurely giving away the diagnosis like a tutor.

"Limitations included a lack of natural conversational flow [48], a lack of emotional depth [96, 125], being excessively agreeable [10, 37, 98], giving overly polished and complete answers"

Fang Eric Kin Meng 2026 (2)¶

Fang Eric Kin Meng, Boon Yuru, Ho Jin Yang, Tey Natasha, Low Matthew Jian Wen. (2026). Artificial intelligence generated virtual patients in health professions education: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10028-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

Subgroup analysis of studies published before 2023, prior to the LLM era: the review reports limitations "centered around unrealistic dialog" from limited response ranges in rule-based scripted systems, with role departure a less prominent concern; multimodal systems added latency and input-recognition challenges.

"prior to the LLM era, limitations centered around unrealistic dialog, especially due to virtual patients having a limited range of responses and related difficulty in adapting to the context of the conversation"
