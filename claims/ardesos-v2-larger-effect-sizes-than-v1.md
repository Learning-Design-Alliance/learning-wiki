# Effect sizes for critical thinking gains were substantially higher in ARDESOS v.2 than in v.1, with practical reasoning (d=.83) and deduction (d=.63) the largest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2` · `i2` medium
> **Currency** · newest study 2015 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Cohen's d values in v.2 exceeded those in v.1 for practical reasoning (.83 vs .03), deduction (.63 vs .45), the total scale (.48 vs ~.10) and problem solving (.44 vs ~.10). [→ Saiz et al. 2015](#saiz-et-al-2015)

## Evidence

### Saiz et al. 2015

Carlos Saiz, Silvia F. Rivas and Sonia Olivares. (2015). Collaborative learning supported by rubrics improves critical thinking. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v15i1.12905

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of Cohen's d effect sizes between the v.1 sample (Saiz & Rivas 2011) and the 144-student v.2 sample. The article reports the total scale (d=.48) and problem solving (d=.44) had moderate effect size in v.2 whereas in v.1 these values were very low, ranging around .10.

> "regarding practical reasoning (d=.83) and deduction (d=.63) effect size has very high values. However, in v.1 these values are lower (Pract. Reasoning: d=.03; deduction, d=.45)"
