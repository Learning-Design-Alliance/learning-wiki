## Evidence

### Mattingly Jameson K MD 2018

Mattingly Jameson K MD, Castellanos Irina PhD, Moberly Aaron C MD. (2018). Nonverbal Reasoning as a Contributor to Sentence Recognition Outcomes in Adults with Cochlear Implants. Otology & Neurotology. https://doi.org/10.1097/MAO.0000000000001998

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Sub-item analysis in the CI group: participants were split by median PRESTO whole-sentence score (21.67%), Fisher's Exact Tests identified 12 RPM items on which low performers scored significantly worse, and the resulting Subset RPM score correlated with both sentence measures, "positively correlated with the percent correct on Harvard Standard whole sentences" (r = .40) and PRESTO whole sentences (r = .48).

> "These Subset RPM scores were positively correlated with the percent correct on Harvard Standard whole sentences (r = .40, p = .01) and PRESTO whole sentences (r = .48, p = .002)."
