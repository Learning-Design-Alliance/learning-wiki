## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Pupils receiving the small group form of the intervention scored significantly higher in extended writing than control pupils (0.78 marks, p=0.05, effect size 0.24, 95% CI 0.00 to 0.49). [→ Torgerson 2014](#torgerson-2014)

## Evidence

### Torgerson 2014

Torgerson, D., Torgerson, C., Mitchell, N., Buckley, H., Ainsworth, H., Heaps, C., & Jefferson, L. (2014). Grammar for Writing: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/evaluation

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Secondary cluster-level analysis comparing 210 small-group pupils with 607 control pupils (817 total, 99 clusters) predicted Level 3c to 4b. The article reports a "statistically significant increase of 0.78 marks" and an effect size of 0.24 (95% CI: 0.00 to 0.49).

> "There was some evidence of a statistically significant difference in extended writing score between the allocated groups, with a statistically significant increase of 0.78 marks for those who received the small group form of the intervention when compared with those in the control group (p=0.05, 95% CI: -0.01 to 1.56)."
