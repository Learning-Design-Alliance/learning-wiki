Klaus Krippendorff 2004¶

Klaus Krippendorff. (2004). Reliability in Content Analysis: Some Common Misconceptions and Recommendations. Human Communication Research 30, 3: 411-433. https://astro.temple.edu/~lombard/carman.htm

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Numerical re-analysis of Lombard et al.'s 20th variable (Figure 5): lumping categories 1 and 2 yields alpha(0|1&2)=.789, above the overall .686; category 1 emerges as the least reliable. The author concludes reporting any frequency difference between categories 1 and 2 would be a mistake.

"The subordinate distinction between 1 and 2, whose data are shown in the right table of Figure 5, is a perfect chance event, =.000 exactly."
