## Evidence

### Farrokhnia 2026

Farrokhnia, M., Latifi, S., Papadopoulos, P. M., Hogenkamp, L., Gijlers, H., Khosravi, H., & Noroozi, O. (2026). Generative AI offers more, but students revise less: comparing the effects of teacher and AI feedback on student essay revisions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00579-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Tukey HSD comparison within the RQ1 ANOVA found "no significant differences" between Zero-shot ChatGPT feedback (M = 11.25) and teacher feedback (M = 11.20); equivalence was not formally tested.

> "However, there were no significant differences between the quality of feedback provided by ChatGPT using a Zero-shot prompt and the teacher-generated feedback (p = .99)."
