# The GSP condition used researcher-prepared worksheets guiding students to discover geometric concepts by dragging and transforming shapes in a computer laboratory

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The experimental intervention consisted of GSP software used with open-ended and gap-filling worksheets in a computer laboratory, with the teacher guiding via questions. [→ Kesan 2013](#kesan-2013)

## Evidence

### Kesan 2013

Kesan, C. & Çalıskan, S. (2013). The effect of learning geometry topics of 7th grade in primary education with dynamic Geometer's Sketchpad geometry software to success and retention. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008875

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive account of the intervention: courses were held in the computer laboratory, students worked on researcher-prepared worksheets of open-ended and gap-filling questions, and "reached the results on their own" while the teacher guided with questions.

> "The students discovered geometric concepts (line, angle, and triangle) by drawing them, moving the shapes, dragging, changing their features by GSP; and reached the results on their own."
