## Evidence

### Niederhauser A 2019

Niederhauser A, Züllig S, Marschall J, Schweiger A, John G, Kuster SP, Schwappach DLB. (2019). Change in staff perspectives on indwelling urinary catheter use after implementation of an intervention bundle in seven Swiss acute care hospitals: results of a before/after survey study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028740

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed ANOVA on the matched subsample (420 respondents; 102 in both trainings, 130 in one, 172 in none). The null interaction indicates training participation alone did not explain knowledge gains; equivalence was not tested.

> "Results from the mixed analysis of variance showed that there was no significant interaction effect between time and training (F 2,401 =1.05, p=0.35)."
