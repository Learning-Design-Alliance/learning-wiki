Inferential analysis of the Delphi survey ratings using R Statistics, tested at the .05 significance level. The article reports the omnibus comparison was "not significantly different, F(2, 42) = 0.91907, p > .05"; no effect size is printed.

"Overall, the importance (M = 7.31, SD = .913) of AI in education was rated higher than impact (M = 7.24, SD = .976) and challenge (M = 6.89, SD = .859), but they are not significantly different, F(2, 42) = 0.91907, p > .05."
