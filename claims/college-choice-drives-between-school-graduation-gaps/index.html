Roderick 2018¶

University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-delivering-dream-college-graduation

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparative analysis of eight CPS high schools (classes of 2003-09): after assigning students an expected graduation rate based on the college attended, the Von Steuben–Gage Park gap for 3.0-GPA students narrowed from more than 30 points to 16 points, and the 4.0-GPA gap halved from 50 to 25 points.

"About half of what looks like differences in the value of a GPA from different high schools can be attributed to differences in college selection patterns across high schools."
