Zhu Xiaocheng, Tang Anliu, Chin Munle, Chang Huan. (2026). Developing purposeful observation during early clinical exposure in anesthesiology: a cluster-randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10042-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Stratified analyses of the primary outcome: anesthesiology-track (22.60 ± 1.30 vs. 16.70 ± 1.83) and non-anesthesiology-track (22.54 ± 1.15 vs. 16.74 ± 1.29) students, and students with and without prior operating room exposure, all favored the structured group; P for interaction 0.649 and 0.798.

> "The structured perioperative observation group had higher primary outcome scores than the conventional clerkship group in both academic track strata and in both prior operating room exposure strata. No significant interactions were observed."
