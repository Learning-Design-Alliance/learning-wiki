`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Simulator testing of 29 participants (nine Generation Alpha, ten Generation Z, ten Generation X) after two practice rounds, reporting descriptive group means with a non-significant ANOVA result. "The difference is conspicuous, however, not statistically significant (p = 0.51)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Overall Scores (OS) gradually declined as the age increased; means of the groups were 91.7 ± 9.9, 89.8 ± 7.6, and 87.7 ± 9.8. The difference is conspicuous, however, not statistically significant (p = 0.51)."
