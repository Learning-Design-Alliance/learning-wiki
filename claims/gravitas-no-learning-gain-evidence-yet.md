The author's own evaluation statement (Section 9) explicitly limits the release to a capability claim: the validation suite tests scientific quantities, not learning. The article states the learning-gain question "requires classroom data" and should be evaluated separately from technical correctness; no classroom data are reported.

> "The version 1.0.0 paper makes a claim about capability, not yet a claim about measured learning gains. The current software demonstrates that a student can open the listed scenarios and investigations, collect the described measurements, save progress, and export the stated products."
