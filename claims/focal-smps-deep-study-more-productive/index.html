Perry 2019¶

Perry, R., Marple, S., & Reade, F. (2019). Roadblocks and routes: Professional development in Math in Common districts. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.wested.org/permissions

q1 · i? · design · r1

Observational finding reported by the report authors from the Math in Common initiative: "selecting a few focal SMPs at a time to dig into deeply was more productive" than studying all simultaneously. SMP 1 and SMP 3 were common early focal standards. No effect size is printed.

"MiC district staff learned that selecting a few focal SMPs at a time to dig into deeply was more productive than asking teachers to study them all simultaneously."
