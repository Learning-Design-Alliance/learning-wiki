### What Works Clearinghouse 2006

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Fast ForWord Language. http://www.scilearn.com

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Randomized controlled trial meeting WWC evidence standards, part of a study of 85 English language learning kindergartners through fifth-graders from nine school districts. It compared English language development outcomes for "53 randomly assigned students who used the program against the outcomes for 32 control students who used their regular curriculum." The WWC rated the effect statistically significant and substantively important.

> "Scientiﬁc Learning Corpora- tion (2004) reported, and the WWC conﬁrmed, that Fast ForWord Language had a statistically signiﬁcant and substantively important positive effect on English language development for elementary school English language learners."
