# Perceived risk mediates the relationship between perceived academic dishonesty and ChatGPT usage (indirect-only mediation)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` PAD is indirectly associated with use frequency through perceived risk (95% CI [−0.05, −0.01]). [→ Ortiz-Bonnin 2025](#ortiz-bonnin-2025)
`q3 i?` PAD is indirectly associated with intention to use through perceived risk (95% CI [−0.11, −0.02]). [→ Ortiz-Bonnin 2025 (2)](#ortiz-bonnin-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Ortiz-Bonnin 2025

Ortiz-Bonnin, S., & Blahopoulou, J. (2025). Chat or cheat? Academic dishonesty, risk perceptions, and ChatGPT usage in higher education students. Social Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11218-025-10080-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped mediation analysis (10,000 samples, bias-corrected CIs) in Jamovi on the 468-student sample. The article reports the indirect effect on use frequency with "95% CI [-0.05, − 0.01]", excluding zero; classified as indirect-only mediation per Meule (2019).

> "Third, PAD was indirectly associated with both use frequency (95% CI [-0.05, − 0.01]) and intention to use (95% CI [-0.11, − 0.02]) through perceived risk."

### Ortiz-Bonnin 2025 (2)

Ortiz-Bonnin, S., & Blahopoulou, J. (2025). Chat or cheat? Academic dishonesty, risk perceptions, and ChatGPT usage in higher education students. Social Psychology of Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11218-025-10080-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same bootstrapped mediation model; the indirect effect on intention to use had "95% CI [-0.11, − 0.02]", excluding zero, supporting H3b. Direct and total effects were not significant.

> "Third, PAD was indirectly associated with both use frequency (95% CI [-0.05, − 0.01]) and intention to use (95% CI [-0.11, − 0.02]) through perceived risk."
