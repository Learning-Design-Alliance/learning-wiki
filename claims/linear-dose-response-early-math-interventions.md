## Evidence

### Anna H. Miller 2025

Anna H. Miller, Daniel R. Espinas, Daniel McNeish, & Marcia A. Barnes. (2025). Dosage Response in Intensive Mathematics Interventions for Early Elementary Students With or At-Risk for Mathematics Learning Disability. Educational Psychology Review, 37, 91. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10070-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analytic model comparison of 164 effect sizes from 24 studies, fitting a re-parameterized quadratic mixed-effect model against a linear meta-regression. The authors report the linear model was more parsimonious, with "a likelihood ratio test comparing the models was not significant (χ 2(1) = 0.20, p = .66)".

> "The BIC from the quadratic model (BIC = 479.1) was larger than the BIC of a linear model (BIC = 474.2), the R2 values were essentially identical between the models (linear R 2 = 23.2%, quadratic R2 = 23.4%), and a likelihood ratio test comparing the models was not significant (χ 2(1) = 0.20, p = .66)."
