Evidence¶

Yilmaz A 2026¶

Yilmaz A (2026) The psychological mechanisms and behavioral determinants of academic integrity in the age of artificial intelligence. Front. Psychol. 17:1853790. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1853790

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Bootstrap resampling with 5,000 samples estimated indirect effects and 95% confidence intervals in the moderated mediation model. The article reports that "cheating tendency mediated the eﬀects of academic procrastination, learned helplessness, and academic self-eﬃcacy" on dishonesty. No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"Conditional indirect eﬀect analyses further demonstrated that cheating tendency mediated the eﬀects of academic procrastination, learned helplessness, and academic self-eﬃcacy on AI-assisted academic dishonesty, and these indirect eﬀects became stronger at higher levels of AI use."
