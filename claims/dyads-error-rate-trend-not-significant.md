## Evidence

### Hausmann 2006

Hausmann, R. G. M., van de Sande, B., & VanLehn, K. (2006). Are Self-explaining and Coached Problem Solving More Effective When Done by Pairs of Students Than Alone? Pittsburgh Science of Learning Center and the Learning Research and Development Center, University of Pittsburgh. https://www.andes.pitt.edu/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Error rate was defined as the ratio of incorrect to correct entries in Andes log files. Dyads averaged "M = .71, SD = .31" versus .97 for individuals; the difference was a trend, F(1, 23) = 2.61, p = .12, d = .68. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The dyads (M = .71, SD = .31) demonstrated a lower error rate than the individuals ( M = .97, SD = .49). However, there was only a trend for this  relationship, with a medium effect size, F(1, 23) = 2.61, p = .12, d = .68."
