John Self 2015¶

John Self. (2015). The Birth of IJAIED. Int J Artif Intell Educ (2016) 26:4–12. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0040-5

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

The memoir describes the 1991 Evanston event, where delegates found the AIED conference had been rebranded as the Fifth International Conference on the Learning Sciences, so that it "had been turned into an advertisement for ILS". In the wake of that conference, Gary Marks and the author wrote to JAIED subscriber members of the AIED division of AACE, and the AIED Society was formed.

"In short, AIED 91 had been turned into an advertisement for ILS, to the exclusion of the majority of the newly-developing international AIED community. This community now had to decide whether 1993 would have an ICLS 93 or an AIED 93 or both or neither."
