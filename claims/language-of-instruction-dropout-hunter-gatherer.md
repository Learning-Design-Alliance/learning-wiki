### Ninkova 2025

Ninkova, V., Hays, J., Lavi, N., Ali, A., Lopes da Silva Macedo, S., Davis, H. E., & Lew-Levy, S. (2025). Hunter-Gatherer Children at School: A View From the Global South. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241255614

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

This is the review's synthesis across the surveyed publications on language of instruction. It reports children "often drop out because they do not understand, or have full command over, the language of instruction," and that hunter-gatherer languages are rarely incorporated because too few speakers make it economically feasible. No effect sizes printed.

> "In a majority of the surveyed texts, authors report that when hunter-gatherer languages are not used in schools, children often drop out because they do not understand, or have full command over, the language of instruction, especially at the beginning of schooling"
