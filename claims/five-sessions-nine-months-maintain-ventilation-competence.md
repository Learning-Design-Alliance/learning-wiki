## Evidence

### Haynes Joanna C. 2024

Haynes Joanna C., Rettedal Siren I., Ushakova Anastasia, Perlman Jeffrey M., Ersdal Hege L. (2024). How Much Training Is Enough? Low-Dose, High-Frequency Simulation Training and Maintenance of Competence in Neonatal Resuscitation. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000783

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective observational study of LDHF simulation training in which 156 HCPs performed 4348 cases over 9 months with automatic logging. GLME modeling identified the training-load cutoff: "The cutoff value of 5 sessions in 9 months predicted 66% (95% CI, 60%–72%) average marginal probability".

> "The cutoff value of 5 sessions in 9 months predicted 66% (95% CI, 60%–72%) average marginal probability of obtaining global competence scores higher than 28 of maximum 30 points"
