Regression predicting post-perceived recognition from pre-perceived recognition and grade group in the same three-cohort Physics 1 study; the model accounted for 43% of variance, with standardized coefficients of -0.24 (Non-passing), -0.29 (Passing C), and -0.17 (B), all p<0.001.
