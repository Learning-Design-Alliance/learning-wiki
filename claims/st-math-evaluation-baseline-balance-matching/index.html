Evidence¶

Wendt 2019¶

Wendt, S., Rice, J., & Nakamoto, J. (2019). A cross-state evaluation of MIND Research Institute's ST Math program and math performance. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED601907

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Baseline t-test comparisons (Appendix A, Exhibits A1-A8) after one-to-many propensity score matching within states using Stata's psmatch2, with frequency weights. The report states differences were "less than a quarter of a standard deviation," though the executive summary says less than a fifth.

"For both the entire sample and the fidelity sample, the magnitude (i.e., Cohen’s d effect size) of the differences between treatment and comparison clusters was less than a quarter of a standard deviation."
