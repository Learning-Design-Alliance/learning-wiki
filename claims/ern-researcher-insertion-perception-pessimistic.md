Survey item asked across editions: "If you are a researcher, do you feel professionally inserted in Italy?". The paper reports that until 2011 about 32% believed researchers were enough inserted, while from 2012 to 2015 the "not at all" answer was predominant, and in 2014 about 59% answered little or not at all.
