## Evidence

### Elina Riivari 2021

Elina Riivari, Marke Kivijärvi, Anna-Maija Lämsä. (2021). Learning teamwork through a computer game: for the sake of performance or collaborative learning? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10009-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Finding summarized in Table 1 from the qualitative analysis of feedback and team reports; students reported good team spirit enabled trial-and-error strategies, humor built cohesion, and positive feedback centered on group morale.

> "For the teams who took longer to finish the game, team spirit was a key factor motivating them to continue playing"

### Elina Riivari 2021 (2)

Elina Riivari, Marke Kivijärvi, Anna-Maija Lämsä. (2021). Learning teamwork through a computer game: for the sake of performance or collaborative learning? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10009-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' observation during game sessions in the computer classroom: students walked to each other's computers, demonstrated mouse movements, and turned monitors so teammates could see their screens, revealing the physical nature of teamwork.

> "Therefore, the students found additional ways of interacting with one another."
