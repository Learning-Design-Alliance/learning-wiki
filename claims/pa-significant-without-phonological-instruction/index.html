q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Reference-group path coefficients from the instructional-method MASEM moderator model for Hong Kong look-and-say learners. The article states this "indicates that, despite not receiving systematic phonological instruction, PA still plays a fundamental role in Chinese word reading, reflecting the fact that written language encodes speech."

"all skills were significant and independent predictors of word reading among Hong Kong children with no phonological instruction, controlling for nonverbal reasoning: RAN: β = -.126 (p = .015), OA: β = .154 (p < .001), PA: β = .089 (p = .012), MA: β = .252 (p < .001), and vocabulary: β = .183 (p < .001)"
