Evidence¶

Tanja Bross 2024¶

Tanja Bross, Ulrike Elisabeth Nett, & Martin Daumiller. (2024). Interrelations Among Achievement Goals and Achievement Emotions: A Meta-Analytic Examination. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09931-9

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

A comprehensive meta-analysis synthesizing correlations among six achievement goals and fifteen achievement emotions, pooling 2,644 effect sizes from 355 studies and 155,208 participants; the authors report the findings "mainly corroborate theoretical assumptions."

"This meta-analysis examined the interrelations among six achievement goals and fifteen achievement emotions and various moderators by analyzing 2,644 effect sizes from 355 studies with 155,208 participants."
