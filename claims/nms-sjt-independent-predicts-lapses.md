## Evidence

### Sahota G 2026

Sahota G, Tiffin PA, Smith D, Tyrrell E, Hampshire M, Taggar J. (2026). Situational judgement testing at different stages of undergraduate medical training and the risk of professionalism lapses: A cohort study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70020

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the same 705-student cohort, adjusted for EPM, advanced qualification tariffs and UCAT cognitive total; the NMS SJT showed "OR 0.68, CIs 0.52 to 0.89, p = 0.005" and predicted lapses in three of four professionalism domains.

> "The only SJT where the scores that remained statistically significant and independent predictors of professionalism lapse, after adjusting for academic and cognitive performance, were those for the NMS SJT (OR 0.68, CIs 0.52 to 0.89, p = 0.005)."
