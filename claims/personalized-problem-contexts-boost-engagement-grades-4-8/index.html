Preparing Problems for Classroom Instruction 2012¶

Preparing Problems for Classroom Instruction. (2012). Improving Mathematical Problem Solving in Grades 4 Through Overview, U.S. Department of Education funded practice-guide presentation. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/16

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The guide's practice recommendation asserts, without cited studies or effect sizes, that "Students tend to engage more actively and perform better" in personalized contexts. This is expert guidance (type e), not a tested result, so it is coded as a weakly supported claim.

"Students tend to engage more actively and perform better when problems are presented in personalized contexts that appeal to their interests."
