## Evidence

### Nguyen Duyen Thi Kim 2013

Nguyen Duyen Thi Kim, Leung Karen K., McIntyre Lynn, Ghali William A., Sauve Reg. (2013). Does Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI) Training Improve the Skills of Health Workers? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0066030

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Stratified meta-analysis using median baseline performance of non-IMCI workers found greater training benefits in lower-baseline strata. The review reports "greater improvements in prescribing medications (RR = 3.08, 95% CI: 2.04–4.66), vaccinating children (RR = 3.45, 95% CI: 1.49–8.01)" with no overlap in confidence intervals across strata for most domains.

> "Studies of workers with lower baseline performance showed greater improvements in prescribing medications (RR = 3.08, 95% CI: 2.04–4.66), vaccinating children (RR = 3.45, 95% CI: 1.49–8.01), and counseling families on adequate nutrition (RR = 10.12, 95% CI: 6.03–16.99) and administering oral therapies (RR = 3.76, 95% CI: 2.30–6.13)."
