Muasaad Alrasheedi and Luiz Fernando Capretz. (2015). Determination of Critical Success Factors Affecting Mobile Learning: A Meta-Analysis Approach. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057286

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of nine shortlisted studies (Table 4). Means are response tendencies on a 5-point Likert scale; the article reports "all six CSFs have an aggregate response higher than 2.5" and gives this mean-based ranking, with learner perceptions present in all nine studies.

> "It is seen that cont ent is considered to have the most influence, followed by technical comp etence of learners, user friendly design, learner community development, and ownership."
