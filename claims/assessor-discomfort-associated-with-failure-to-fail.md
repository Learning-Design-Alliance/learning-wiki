## Evidence

### Scarff Catherine E 2023

Scarff Catherine E, Bearman Margaret, Chiavaroli Neville, Trumble Stephen. (2023). Assessor discomfort and failure to fail in clinical performance assessments. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04688-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey comparison of mean PADS scores between self-reported failure-to-fail and non-failure-to-fail assessors in the dermatology questionnaire study; the article reports a significant difference with p = 0.04 and 95% CI difference 0.512, 1.495. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "A statistically significant relationship between PADS score and self-report of failure to fail was seen with mean PADS score of 3.38 (n = 6) compared with 2.35 (n = 40) (95% CI difference 0.512, 1.495, p = 0.04)"
