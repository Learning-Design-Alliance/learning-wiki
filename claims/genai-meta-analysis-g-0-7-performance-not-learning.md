The article reports, citing Deng et al., a meta-analysis of 69 experimental studies in K-12 and tertiary education reporting improved academic performance with "an effect of (Hedge’s)g= 0.7". The commentary attributes the performance-versus-learning conflation to that literature, not to new data.

> "a meta-analysis of 69 studies aimed to investigate the impact of ChatGPT and other generative AI tools on “student learning” in K-12 and tertiary education. Yet, the meta-analysis reported that these technologies improve student “academic performance”, with an effect of (Hedge’s)g= 0.7"
