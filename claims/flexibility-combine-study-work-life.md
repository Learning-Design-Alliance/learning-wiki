`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Questionnaire results (Table 3) in the UOC case study show strong agreement, e.g. 84.2% of Economics and Business students strongly agree the model allows combining professional and academic life.

> "the results of the questionnaire reaffirm that students across the UOC’s Knowledge Areas consider that the online education educational model at the UOC allows them to combine their studies with the usual organization of their time, and also, with their personal and professional life"
