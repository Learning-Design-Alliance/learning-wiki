### The Dynamic Interplay Between Goal Setting 2022

The Dynamic Interplay Between Goal Setting, Performance, and Emotions in Self-Regulated Learning: A Computational Modeling Approach — Supplementary Materials. (2022). https://gpemodel.shinyapps.io/gpe-model/

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

Simulation study generating data for 300 fictitious participants from the hypothesized model using population-level values chosen with a Shiny app, then re-estimating parameters hierarchically. The article reports that differences between true and estimated values "centered around zero" (Supplementary Figure S2).

> "Results show these differences centered around zero, indicating that the model and Bayesian estimation could recover parameters well (Supplementary Figure S2)."
