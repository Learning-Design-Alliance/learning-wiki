## Evidence

### Tok Yildiz 2020

Tok Yildiz, F., & Kaşikçi, M. (2020). Impact of training based on Orem's theory on self-care agency and quality of life in patients with coronary artery disease. The Journal of Nursing Research, 28(6), Article e125. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000406

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlation analysis in the trial (both groups combined, 102 patients) found pretest and posttest Self-Care Agency Scale and MacNew scores correlated at "the low–medium level on the positive side, ranging from .288 to .587"; printed effect size r = .288 to .587.

> "A statistically significant correlation was found at the low–medium level on the positive side, ranging from .288 to .587 between the average pretest and posttest scores on the Self-Care Agency Scale and MacNew in both groups."

### Tok Yildiz 2020 (2)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlation analysis in the same trial found Self-Care Agency Scale and 15D scores correlated at "the low–medium level on the positive side, ranging from .369 to .482" in both groups; printed effect size r = .369 to .482.

> "Moreover, a statistically significant correlation was found at the low–medium level on the positive side, ranging from .369 to .482 between the average pretest and posttest scores on the Self-Care Agency Scale and 15D in both groups."

### Tok Yildiz 2020 (3)
