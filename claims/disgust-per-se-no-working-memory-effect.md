## Evidence

### Scheibe 2009

Scheibe, S., & Blanchard-Fields, F. (2009). Effects of Regulating Emotions on Cognitive Performance: What Is Costly for Young Adults Is not so Costly for Older Adults. Psychology and Aging, 24(1), 217-223. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0013807

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Planned comparison within the repeated-measures ANOVA contrasting the disgust no-instructions condition with the neutral film condition. The article reports that "No interaction effects involving age or condition were found"; equivalence was not directly tested. Participants in all groups increased performance from baseline to T1 and remained stable thereafter.

> "To determine if disgust per se had an effect on performance we compared the no-instructions control condition with the neutral control condition. No interaction effects involving age or condition were found."
