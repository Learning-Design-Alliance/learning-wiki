Multilevel logistic regression (model 1, N = 5643) of TFS data with age entered as three dummy categories. Age was the most salient teacher predictor, replicating the U-shaped relationship found in prior research.

> "Both younger (less than 30 years) and older (greater than 50 years) teachers are more likely to depart than are middle-aged teachers. For instance, the relative odds of young teachers departing are 171% higher than for middle-aged teachers."
