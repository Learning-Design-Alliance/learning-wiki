Evidence¶

Stuart Webb and Anna C.-S. Chang 2015¶

Stuart Webb and Anna C.-S. Chang. (2015). HOW DOES PRIOR WORD KNOWLEDGE AFFECT VOCABULARY LEARNING PROGRESS IN AN EXTENSIVE READING PROGRAM? Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 37, Issue 4, pp. 651-675. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263114000606

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Study of 60 EFL learners divided into high-, intermediate-, and low-level groups based on pretests of target vocabulary and Vocabulary Levels Test scores; the article reports "the relative gains from pretest to immediate posttest were 63.18%, 44.64%, and 28.12%" for the three groups respectively.

"The results showed that the relative gains from pretest to immediate posttest were 63.18%, 44.64%, and 28.12% for the high-, intermediate-, and low-level groups, respectively."
