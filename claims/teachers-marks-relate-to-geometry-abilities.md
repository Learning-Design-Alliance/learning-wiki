## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the majority of the sixty-three schools, the coefficient of correlation between school grades and test scores exceeded three times its probable error, so teachers' marks usually bear a positive relation to the four abilities. [→ Minnick 1925](#minnick-1925)

## Evidence

### Minnick 1925

Minnick, J. H. (1925). Certain abilities fundamental to the study of geometry. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Certain+Abilities+Fundamental+to+the+Study+of+Geometry

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis across sixty-three schools, computed separately per school by ranking pupils on teachers' grades for the first two books of geometry and on combined positive and negative test scores. The article reports "In the majority of the schools, this is true", treating r > 3 P.E. as significant.

> "If the coefficient is greater than three times the probable error it has scientific significance. In the majority of the schools, this is true, and we may conclude that usually the teachers' marks do bear a positive relation to the abilities with which the investigation is concerned."
