Evidence¶

Smets 2017¶

Wouter Smets. (2017). High Quality Differentiated Instruction – A Checklist for Teacher Professional Development on Handling Differences in the General Education Classroom. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(11): 2074-2080. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.051124

q2 · i? · design · r1

Document-analysis comparison of eight operational characteristics from two DI models against three expert reference works on teaching effectivity (Hattie; Marzano; Muijs & Reynolds). The article concludes the DI framework "may be regarded as good teaching practice". No effect sizes are reported; the result is a qualitative concordance analysis.

"Almost all of the elements in these models are explicitly mentioned by authors of international effectivity research as elements of good or effective teaching."
