## Evidence

### Jabbar Salman AA 2019

Jabbar Salman AA, Ahmed Z, Mirza A, Nassar AHM. (2019). Laparoscopic Training Opportunities in an Emergency Biliary Service. JSLS: Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/JSLS.2019.00031

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Outcome comparison within the prospective 2049-case series, with complications stratified by the Clavien-Dindo classification. The article reports minor complications "comparable for principal surgeon versus trainee cases (5% vs 5%, P = .8)" and equivalent major complications.

> "Minor complication rates (Clavien Dindo classification 1 or 2) were comparable for principal surgeon versus trainee cases (5% vs 5%, P = .8). Rates of major complications were also equivalent (1% vs 0.9%, P = .7) for both groups."
