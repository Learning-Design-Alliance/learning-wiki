Evidence¶

Nair 2016¶

Nair, S. S., Paré, D. and Vicentic, A. (2016). Biologically based neural circuit modelling for the study of fear learning and extinction. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.15

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review states this as an established behavioural finding from the fear conditioning literature, citing single-unit recording studies showing CS-evoked LA firing adapts quickly so that LA firing has almost returned to baseline when the US occurs.

"it is well established that simultaneous CSs and USs are actually less efﬁcient at driving fear learning than when CS onset precedes the US and the two co-terminate tens of seconds later."
