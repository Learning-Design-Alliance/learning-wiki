## Evidence

### Christopher J. Fischer 2021

Christopher J. Fischer, Jennifer Delgado, Sarah LeGresley. (2021). Interplay between physics self-efficacy, calculus transfer ability, and gender. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2110.07364](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2110.07364)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation coefficients for post-test responses on the calculus transfer assessment (Table 2): question 1 with question 2 is 0.064 and with question 3 is 0.106, while questions 2 and 3 correlate at 0.507. The article says this is "consistent with previous work indicating that mathematics transfer to physics is difficult".

> "The correlation between the pure mathematics question and each of the transfer questions is low, consistent with previous work indicating that mathematics transfer to physics is difficult"
