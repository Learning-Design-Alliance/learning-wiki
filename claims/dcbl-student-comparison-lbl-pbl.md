### Li Yidan 2025

Li Yidan, Lei Li, Jiang Ling, Mao Ping, Fu Chuhan, Chen Jing, Zeng Qinghai. (2025). The dynamic case-based learning model: conducive to enhancing the practical learning effectiveness of dermatology for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07474-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Perceptual comparison item in the student questionnaire (n=129); students rated DCBL against LBL and PBL. No head-to-head teaching comparison was conducted; the article reports only these perceived percentages.

> "Regarding the comparison of the DCBL, LBL, and PBL models, students assessed the superiority of the DCBL model to the other two existing learning models in terms of improving learning interest (76.0%), understanding of skin diseases (73.6%), as well as the ability to diagnose and treat skin diseases (77.5%)."
