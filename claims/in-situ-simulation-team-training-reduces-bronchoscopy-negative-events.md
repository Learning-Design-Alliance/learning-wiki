## Evidence

### Fosli CA 2025

Fosli CA, Sperling S, Jensen RD, Skaarup SH. (2025). In-situ simulation-based team training reduces incidence of negative events during bronchoscopy. A prospective educational intervention cohort study. Respiratory Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12931-025-03205-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pre-post interventional education study with real-world data collection at a Danish bronchoscopy unit; 124 bronchoscopies (61 before, 63 after training by 13 trained staff). The primary outcome, any unexpected or unplanned adverse event, fell significantly: "reduced from 62% (38/61) to 41% (26/63), p = 0.019".

> "Following intervention training, the incidence of any negative events was reduced from 62% (38/61) to 41% (26/63), p = 0.019, corresponding to a relative decrease of 32%."
