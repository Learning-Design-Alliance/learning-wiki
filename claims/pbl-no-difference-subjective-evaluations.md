Galvao Tais F., Silva Marcus T., Neiva Celiane S., Ribeiro Laura M., Pereira Mauricio G. (2014). Problem-Based Learning in Pharmaceutical Education: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. The Scientific World Journal. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3950357/

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects meta-analysis of subjective evaluations (student surveys of confidence and perceived preparedness) across the included controlled pharmacy studies. The pooled estimate shows "no difference was found between the comparison groups"; equivalence was not tested.

> "For the subjective assessment, no difference was found between the comparison groups (OR = 0.98; IC 95%: 0.57–1.66)."
