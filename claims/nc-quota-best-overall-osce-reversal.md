## Evidence

### Tsikas 2023

Tsikas, Stefanos A.; Afshar, Kambiz. (2023). Clinical experience can compensate for inferior academic achievements in an undergraduate objective structured clinical examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04082-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Non-parametric Mann–Whitney U-tests of overall OSCE scores (Table 2) for more than 1,200 students across four admission quotas; NC students averaged 85.63% versus 81.34% for waiting list. NC students "perform best, and significantly better (p < 0.001, Mann–Whitney U-test) than all other groups."

> "NC students perform best, and significantly better (p < 0.001, Mann–Whitney U-test) than all other groups."

### Tsikas 2023 (2)

Tsikas, Stefanos A.; Afshar, Kambiz. (2023). Clinical experience can compensate for inferior academic achievements in an undergraduate objective structured clinical examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04082-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression (Table 4, column 3) of overall OSCE score with age, Abitur grade, sociodemographics and cohort fixed effects; waiting-list coefficient 2.782 (p < 0.05). Waiting-list students "score significantly better in the OSCE (almost three percentage points) than their classmates."

> "but waiting list students now score significantly better in the OSCE (almost three percentage points) than their classmates."
