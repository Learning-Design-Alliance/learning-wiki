## Evidence

### Ličen Sabina 2025

Ličen Sabina, Prosen Mirko. (2025). Disaster Nursing Competencies in a Time of Global Conflicts and Climate Crises: A Cross‐Sectional Survey Study. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70052

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the Sl-DNCC core competencies subscale (27 items, range 27–189) in a cross-sectional survey of 379 Slovenian nurses. The median was "significantly higher than the hypothesised median of 108" (Z = 13.565, p = 0.000).

> "The median score for nurses' core competencies in disaster management (M = 157) was significantly higher than the hypothesised median of 108 (Z = 13.565, p = 0.000), indicating that participants view these competencies as highly important."
