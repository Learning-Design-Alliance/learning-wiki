Independent t-test comparison of GPA between female (n = 187) and male (n = 69) students in the cross-sectional sample. The results section reports the significant difference and the printed means: "Female students had a higher mean GPA (2.68, SD = 0.53)". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "Additionally, a statistically significant difference in GPA was found between female and male students. Female students had a higher mean GPA (2.68, SD = 0.53) than their male counterparts (2.45, SD = 0.64)."
