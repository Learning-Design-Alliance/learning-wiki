## Evidence

### Sebastian 2013

Sebastian, J., & Allensworth, E. (2013). How Do Secondary Principals Influence Teaching and Learning? Principal's Research Review, 8(4). https://www.nassp.org/prr

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-school comparison of teachers in the Chicago high school study found differences among individual teachers' instruction attributable to school leaders through professional development and curricular coherence. The authors note such efforts affect only subsets of teachers, so schoolwide influence is small.

> "In particular, teachers who report better professional development and perceptions of curricular coherence have better instructional practices than other teachers in their school."
