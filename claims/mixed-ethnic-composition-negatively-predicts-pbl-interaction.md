## Evidence

### Wang Rong 2023

Wang Rong, Liu Chuanyong, Zhang Shu-Yong. (2023). The quality of group interactions in medical problem–based learning in China: the roles of intercultural sensitivity and group ethnic composition. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04616-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression on questionnaire data from 139 second-year medical undergraduates in 15 PBL groups at Shandong University. Ethnic group composition entered in model 2 showed a negative standardized coefficient (β = -0.24** for exploratory questions, -0.22* for cumulative reasoning in Table 4), showing "Group ethnic composition negatively predicted interactions that involved exploratory questions and cumulative reasoning".

> "Group ethnic composition negatively predicted interactions that involved exploratory questions and cumulative reasoning, whereas intercultural sensitivity significantly and positively predicted those types of group interactions."
