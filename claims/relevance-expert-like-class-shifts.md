## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason 2020

Andrew J. Mason. (2020). Perceived irrelevance and mastery vs. performance achievement goals: Two mindset variables within attitudinal experiences of life science majors in introductory physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.09900](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.09900)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

CLASS pre/post comparison of 50 irrelevant vs. 168 relevant students across six semesters; post-test overall expert-like percentages were 50.0% vs. 58.1% and normalized gains -0.06 vs. +0.04, with the printed effect sizes d = 0.53 (post) and d = 0.37 (gains).

> "There is a large effect size for post-test scores (d = 0.53) and a moderate effect size for normalized gains (d = 0.37) on the overall CLASS survey results."
