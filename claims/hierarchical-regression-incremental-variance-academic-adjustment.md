## Evidence

### Zhang J and Yin Y 2026

Zhang J and Yin Y (2026). A study on AI-enabled course development, AI proficiency, and first-year students' academic and psychological adjustment. Front. Psychol. 17:1909803. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1909803

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression in the 551-student survey entered gender, major, and housing (Model 1), then course development satisfaction (Model 2), then AI proficiency (Model 3). The article reports "course development satisfaction had its largest ΔR2 for academic adjustment (ΔR2 = 0.145)" and AI proficiency ΔR2 = 0.060, with final R2 = 0.240 for academic adjustment.

> "In terms of incremental variance, course development satisfaction had its largest ΔR2 for academic adjustment (ΔR2 = 0.145); AI proficiency also had its largest ΔR2 for academic adjustment (ΔR2 = 0.060)."
