Evidence¶

Fallon 2018¶

Fallon, T. L., Aylett, R., Minnis, H. and Rajendran, G. (2018). Investigating social vulnerability in children using computer mediated role-play. Computers and Education, 125, pp. 458-464. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.06.027

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

The omnibus interaction in the same ANOVA was significant with printed effect size Cohen's f = 0.37. Simple effects showed 6-year-olds (p < 0.001) and 8-year-olds (p = .002) scored higher in role-play, while 10-year-olds did not differ (p = .081).

"There was a significant interaction between task presentation (Computer Mediated Role-Play, Paper Pencil) and the age group of participants [F (2, 47) = 3.27 p = .047 Cohen's f = 0.37]."
