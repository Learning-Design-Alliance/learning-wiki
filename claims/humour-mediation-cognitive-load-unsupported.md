# Mediation hypotheses via extraneous cognitive load and mental effort are unsupported

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2026 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Neither the hypothesized mediation of irrelevant humour's detrimental effects via extraneous cognitive load (H2.1) nor the mediation of relevant humour's benefits via mental effort (H2.2) was supported in either study. [→ Bender 2026](#bender-2026)

## Evidence

### Bender 2026

Bender, L., Renkl, A., & Endres, T. (2026). Punchline with(out) purpose: Integrating research on instructional humour and seductive details. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70080

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analyses using PROCESS in both studies. In Study 1, irrelevant humour raised extraneous cognitive load ratings versus control, but this did not explain learning decrements. No indirect effects emerged for any hypothesis.

> "Four mediation analyses testing our hypotheses regarding extraneous load and mental effort did not reveal any indirect effect."
