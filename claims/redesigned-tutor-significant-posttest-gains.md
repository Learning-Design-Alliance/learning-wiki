## Subclaims
`q3 i0` In a randomized classroom experiment, the redesigned condition scored significantly higher on the post-test (mean 0.85 vs 0.73), with Cohen's d = 0.47 and β = 0.09, p = 0.027 controlling for pre-test. [→ Liu and Koedinger 2017](#liu-and-koedinger-2017)

## Evidence

### Liu and Koedinger 2017

Ran Liu, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2017). Closing the loop: Automated data-driven cognitive model discoveries lead to improved instruction and learning gains. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 9, No 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1155896

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Classroom experiment: 115 students randomly assigned at the individual level to control (57) or redesigned (58) tutors; analyses restricted to 91 students completing pre- and post-test. A linear regression controlling for pre-test also showed condition significantly predicted post-test (β = 0.09, p = 0.027).

> "Post-test scores were significantly different depending on the condition students were in [F(1,89)=5.04, p = 0.027, Cohen's d = 0.47]."

### Liu and Koedinger 2017 (2)

Ran Liu, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2017). Closing the loop: Automated data-driven cognitive model discoveries lead to improved instruction and learning gains. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 9, No 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1155896

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way repeated measures ANOVA across both conditions showed post-test scores (mean = 0.78) significantly higher than pre-test scores (mean = 0.61), confirming learning occurred in both conditions.

> "Results indicated that students' test scores were significantly differ ent at the two testing points [F(1,90)= 61.06, p < 0.0001]."
