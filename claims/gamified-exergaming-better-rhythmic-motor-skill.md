## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Students in the gamified exergaming intervention scored higher on the rhythmic motor skill academic performance test than students in the traditional didactic intervention. [→ Quintas 2020](#quintas-2020)

## Evidence

### Quintas 2020

Quintas, A., Bustamante, J.-C., Pradas, F., & Castellar, C. (2020). Psychological effects of gamified didactics with exergames in Physical Education at primary schools: results from a natural experiment. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103874

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Post-treatment comparison of the final group-choreography performance exam, filmed and rated on a 1-5 rhythm scale, between the gamified exergaming and control groups. The article reports a significant group difference with ηp2=.032 (a small effect) and that rhythmic motor skill academic performance was better in the gamified exergaming intervention.

> "A significant difference appeared i n rhythmic motor skill academic performance between the groups (t(406)=-7.21, p=.000, ηp2=.032). Rhythmic motor skill academic performance was better in t he gamified exergaming intervention than in the non-gamified and non -exergaming intervention."
