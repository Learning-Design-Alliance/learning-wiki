Michael C. Wittmann, Rachel E. Scherr. (2002). Epistemology mediating conceptual knowledge: Constraints on data accessible from interviews. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207077](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207077)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview excerpt from phase 2 in which Sarah revisited her temperature answer after constructing the electron pull description. The interviewer thereby gained insight into her reasoning: flowing electrons must be torn from the atomic lattice, and it is easier to tear electrons in high-temperature wires, creating more current.

> "So it's got a higher energy, so it would be like more electrons can be torn away from these structures, so more current will flow through the higher temperature one."
