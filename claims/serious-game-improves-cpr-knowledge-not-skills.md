Review's summary of the serious-game trial (LISSA-2 groups vs control groups, nursing students in Norway, n = 109): "Improved knowledge among those belonging to LISSA groups" but no skill differences; the original authors judged it supports theory only.

> "Improved knowledge among those belonging to LISSA groups. In terms of skills, there were no differences between the groups. They conclude that this is a good method to support theory but that it does not improve skills."
