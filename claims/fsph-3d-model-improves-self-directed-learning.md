## Evidence

### Zhou Fen 2026

Zhou Fen, Yu Ping, Yu Xiaofen, Han Xuyan, Wang Zheng. (2026). Family-school-hospital collaboration model for urinary system tumor early screening education. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1837667

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Non-randomized controlled pre-post study of 120 clinical medical interns (60 per group). Self-directed learning ability measured with the scale developed by Wang et al. (Cronbach's α = 0.893). The unadjusted post-test scores (117.80 ± 2.03 vs. 117.20 ± 2.01, p = 0.0431, Cohen's d = 0.38) and the change scores (25.92 ± 15.01 vs. 24.34 ± 13.99, p = 0.0307, Cohen's d = 0.39) favoured the intervention group, but after baseline adjustment by ANCOVA, which the authors name as their primary analysis, the difference was not statistically significant (adjusted means 117.74 vs. 117.32, difference 0.42, 95% CI −0.53 to 1.37, F = 0.774, p = 0.381, partial η² = 0.007). The abstract reports this outcome as a significant adjusted improvement, which the results section does not support. Groups were formed by boarding status, not at random.

> "However, after baseline adjustment via analysis of covariance (ANCOVA), the intergroup difference was no longer statistically significant. The adjusted post-test mean was 117.74 for the observation group and 117.32 for controls (mean difference = 0.42, 95% CI: −0.53 to 1.37; F = 0.774, df = 1,117, p = 0.381, partial η2 = 0.007)" (results)
