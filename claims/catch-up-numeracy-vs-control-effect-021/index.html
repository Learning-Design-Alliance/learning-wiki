Evidence¶

National Foundation for Educational Research (NFER) 2014¶

National Foundation for Educational Research (NFER), evaluation team led by Simon Rutt. (2014). Catch Up® Numeracy: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/evaluation

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Intention-to-treat analysis of the three-arm pupil-randomised trial in 54 schools, using a two-level multilevel model controlling for pre-test scores. The post-hoc Catch Up versus control contrast was "found to be significant, p=0. 0478" with a printed effect size of 0.21 (CI 0.42 – 0.01), a small effect.

"While there is no significant difference between the two treatment groups a post-hoc test was carried out to determine any significant difference between the Catch Up group and the control group . This was found to be significant, p=0. 0478. Its effect size is 0.21 (CI 0.42 – 0.01)."
