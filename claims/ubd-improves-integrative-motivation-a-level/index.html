Evidence¶

Yurtseven 2015¶

Yurtseven, N., & Altun, S. (2015). The Investigation of the Impact of Understanding by Design (UbD) on EFL Teaching: Students' Foreign Language Learning Motivation and Views. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i3.1204

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Pre-test post-test comparison of 44 A-level treatment versus 42 control students using independent samples t-test on accessed scores. Treatment mean gain 11.77 versus -2.54 for controls; the article prints d = .95, calling it a high level of effect size.

"it can be seen that there is a significant difference in integrative and instrumental motivation levels of treatment and control groups at p < 0.05 level (p = .00 < .05; p = .02 < .05 )"
