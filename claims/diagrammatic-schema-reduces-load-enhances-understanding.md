### Marcus 1998

Marcus, N. (1998). Understanding Instructions. PhD thesis, University of New South Wales. https://hdl.handle.net/1959.4/70247

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The thesis's overall conclusion from its five experiments, stated in the abstract alongside its cognitive-load measures. It reports that incorporating interacting elements into a diagrammatic schema by modifying presentation mode reduces load and enhances understanding, per the quoted conclusion.

> "However, if interacting elements can be incorporated into a diagrammatic schema by modifying the instructional presentation mode, cognitive load will be reduced and understanding enhanced."
