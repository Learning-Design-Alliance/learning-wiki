intended-outcomes¶

Chelimo, S., Cortez, C., Jackson, J., & Weaver, D. (2024, May). Unintended Impact: How the Verizon Innovative Learning Schools Program Affected Its Educators' Job Satisfaction and Career Trajectories. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/212

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Digital Promise researchers' analysis of three surveys administered across the 2022-23 school year to a recent cohort of teachers, coaches, and district leads found increases in proficiency, confidence, use, and Powerful Learning, and that "more time with coaches was significantly associated with better teaching outcomes." No effect sizes are printed.

"The team's analysis of the survey data showed that teachers' perceived technological proficiency, skills, confidence to use technology, the frequency of its use, and instances of Powerful Learning in classrooms all increased over time. Further, the team found that more time with coaches was significantly associated with better teaching outcomes and significantly higher teacher self-reported technological proficiency and growth in practice."
