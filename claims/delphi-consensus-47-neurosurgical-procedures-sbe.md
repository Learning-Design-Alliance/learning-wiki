### Roche Adam F. 2025

Roche Adam F., Kavanagh Dara O., Crimmins Darach, Healy Vincent, Zilani Gulam, Konge Lars, Nayahangan Leizl Joy, Khan Danyal Z., Murray Daniel, Cuello Javier Francisco, Cahir Caitriona, Murphy Niamh, Condron Claire M. `q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The third-round survey of the modified Delphi process, completed by 31 KOLs, "resulted in a final list of 47 procedures" after eliminating two procedures and merging five others deemed similar. Two procedures were removed and five merged as inherently similar.

> "Thirty-one KOLs completed round three, resulting in a response rate of 3.9%. This round resulted in a final list of 47 procedures, which also highlights the changes in ranking based on analysis from rounds 2 to 3."
