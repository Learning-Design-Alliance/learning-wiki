### Alexandra Werth 2022

Alexandra Werth, Jessica R. Hoehn, Kristin Oliver, Michael F. J. Fox, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Rapid Transition to Remote Instruction of Physics Labs During Spring 2020: Instructor Perspectives. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Likert-scale survey items rated by 111 responses (Fig. 4); the article states "making the remote class as similar to the in-person version as possible" was the most common challenge, with time and technology also major.

> "The most common reported challenge instructors faced was making the remote class as similar to the in-person version as possible. Instructors also cited time and technology constraints as major challenges (Figure 4)."

### Alexandra Werth 2022 (2)

Alexandra Werth, Jessica R. Hoehn, Kristin Oliver, Michael F. J. Fox, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2022). Rapid Transition to Remote Instruction of Physics Labs During Spring 2020: Instructor Perspectives. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of open responses identified challenges beyond the closed-response items, including "student engagement (22 of 129 courses) and missing hands-on components of the lab (18 courses)," plus lack of resources (15 courses) and difficulty fostering collaboration (15 courses).

> "Two challenges that were commonly cited in the open-responses, but not captured in the closed-responses were student engagement (22 of 129 courses) and missing hands-on components of the lab (18 courses)."
