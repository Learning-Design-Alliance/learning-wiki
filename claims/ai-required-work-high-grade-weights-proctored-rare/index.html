Francis Geng 2026¶

Francis Geng, Anshul Shah, Mia Chen, Paul Denny, Juho Leinonen, Bill Griswold, Gerald Soosai Raj, and Leo Porter. (2026). Mapping the Emerging Curriculum for AI-Assisted Software Engineering via Syllabus Analysis. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05898

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Feature analysis of 14 conventional percentage-based courses (Table 4): AI-required work and programming work each appeared in 14 courses with median weight 70% (ranges 20–100% and 20–95%); proctored assessment appeared in 3 courses with median 20%. Weights are non-mutually exclusive.

"AI- required and programming work carried high median grade weights, indicating that graded work commonly centered on producing or improving software artifacts with AI tools."
