## Evidence

### Montalbano 2012

Montalbano, V., Benedetti, R., Mariotti, E., Mariotti, M. A., & Porri, A. (2012). Attempts of Transforming Teacher Practice Through Professional Development. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1212.1042](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1212.1042)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

This is the authors' own concluding interpretation from their multi-year experience with professional development programs, not a tested result. The conclusion states that "active engagement of teachers" in designing laboratory-related learning paths is essential, and that experimenting activities in class builds teacher autonomy for innovation.

> "An active engagement of teachers in the design and implementation of learning paths closely related to laboratory activities is an essential step for trying to change the current teaching practice."
