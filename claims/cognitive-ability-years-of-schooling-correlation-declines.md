## Evidence

### Van Hootegem 2023

Van Hootegem, A., Røgeberg, O., Bratsberg, B., & Lyngstad, T. H. (2023). Correlation between cognitive ability and educational attainment weakens over birth cohorts. Scientific Reports, 13, 17747. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-44605-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Population-wide register analysis of Norwegian male cohorts 1950–1991 correlating conscription stanine scores with schooling years. Cohort-specific correlations "starts out above 0.63" and end "under 0.50 for the cohorts born after 1980" (Fig. 1). No effect size beyond the printed correlations; correlations are standardized within cohort.

> "The correlation for years of education at age of 30 (in green) starts out above 0.63 for the oldest birth cohorts, but declines over cohorts ending with a correlation under 0.50 for the cohorts born after 1980."
