## Evidence

### Cui Liping 2024

Cui Liping, Dong Yuting, Zhang Shan, Ma Wenxia, Li Min. (2024). Construction and application of procedural pathways combined with information management in nursing staff skills training system. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05593-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-training ICU nurse competency scale (58 items, Cronbach's α 0.93) comparison of the 300-nurse experimental group with the 267-nurse control group; Table 4 reports t = 3.331, P = 0.002 for total score, t = 3.400 for knowledge, t = 2.226, P = 0.031 for skills.

> "the total score, professional knowledge score, and professional skills score of nursing interns in the experimental group were 133.27 ± 9.95, 32.20 ± 3.00, and 44.60 ± 4.69, respectively, which were higher than those in the control group"

### Cui Liping 2024 (2)

Cui Liping, Dong Yuting, Zhang Shan, Ma Wenxia, Li Min. (2024). Construction and application of procedural pathways combined with information management in nursing staff skills training system. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05593-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same competency-scale comparison; two of the four dimensions did not differ significantly between groups. The authors attribute non-significant items to low test efficiency from the small sample size.

> "There was no statistically significant difference in professional abilities and psychological traits between the experimental group and the control group (P > 0.05)"
