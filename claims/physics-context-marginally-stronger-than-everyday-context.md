## Evidence

### Suzanne White Brahmia 2016

Suzanne White Brahmia, Andrew Boudreaux, Stephen E. Kanim. (2016). Obstacles to mathematization in introductory physics. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.01235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.01235)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Isomorphic-pair comparison in the large-N assessment of generalized structural reasoning: item pairs I–II and III–IV share identical arithmetic reasoning and differ in surface context. The authors quote "marginally significant, with p-values ~ 0.05" and report this stronger physics-context performance was unexpected.

> "Student performance was slightly stronger on the physics context item in comparison to the item using an everyday context (e.g., students were more successful applying a ratio in the olive oil context than in the traxolene context). The differences, however, were only marginally significant, with p-values ~ 0.05."
