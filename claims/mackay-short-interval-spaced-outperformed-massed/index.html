Randomized control trial (n=41 undergraduate and postgraduate students) on the MIST VR laparoscopic transfer-place task, comparing two 2.5-minute-interval spaced schedules with a 20-minute massed session. The review's table reports "Spaced 2 significantly outperformed the massed group (p < .05)"; no effect size printed.
