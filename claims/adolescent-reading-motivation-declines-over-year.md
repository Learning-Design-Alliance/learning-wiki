## Evidence

### Varuzza 2014

Varuzza, M., Sinatra, R., Eschenauer, R., & Blake, B. E. (2014). The Relationship between English Language Arts Teachers' Use of Instructional Strategies and Young Adolescents' Reading Motivation, Engagement, and Preference. Journal of Education and Learning, 3(2), 108. https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v3n2p108

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post comparison of mean subscale scores on the nine-scale MRQ across 17 intact ELA class groups (N = 414) over one academic year. Social showed the greatest decrease and Challenge the least, with a slight positive score of .02 for seventh graders.

> "young adolescents at the sixth and seventh grade levels at 10 schools decreased in all nine dimensions of the MRQ save one, regardless of the in structional beliefs and practices of their 17 English Language Arts teachers"
