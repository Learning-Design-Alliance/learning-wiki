## Evidence

### Husbands Adrian 2015

Husbands Adrian, Rodgerson Mark J., Dowell Jon, Patterson Fiona. (2015). Evaluating the validity of an integrity-based situational judgement test for medical school admissions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0424-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of 198 MMI candidates at Dundee Medical School who completed the SJT and HEXACO-PI. Pearson's correlations showed significant associations with four personality dimensions, "ranging from .16 to .36 (.22 to .53 disattenuated)", largest with honesty-humility (r = .36; .53 disattenuated).

> "Statistically significant correlations ranging from .16 to .36 (.22 to .53 disattenuated) were observed between SJT score and the honesty-humility (integrity), conscientiousness, extraversion and agreeableness dimensions of the HEXACO inventory."
