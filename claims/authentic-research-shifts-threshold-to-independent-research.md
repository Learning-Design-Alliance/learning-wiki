## Evidence

### Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre 2015

Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2015). Becoming a Physicist: The Roles of Research, Mindsets, and Milestones in upper-division student perceptions. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020120)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretive analysis (Section V) of the Doing Independent Research category, whose members had at least a semester of undergraduate research. The article states "doing research is no longer suﬃcient; one must do independent research to "count" as a physicist," and that perceptions of central practices shift as people participate in them. This is an interpretation, not a tested result.

> "By this we mean that doing research is no longer suﬃcient; one must do independent research to "count" as a physicist."
