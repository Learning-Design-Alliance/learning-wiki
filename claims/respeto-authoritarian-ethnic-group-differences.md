## Evidence

### Kim Yeonwoo 2018

Kim Yeonwoo, Calzada Esther J., Barajas-Gonzalez R. Gabriela, Huang Keng-Yen, Brotman Laurie M., Castro Ashley, Pichardo Catherine. (2018). The Role of Authoritative and Authoritarian Parenting in the Early Academic Achievement of Latino Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000192

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group SEM for DA students; the corresponding respeto-to-authoritarian path was not reported for MA students. Multi-group tests found two of twelve paths significantly different across groups: U.S. identity to respeto (Δχ2 = 7.05, p < .01) and cultural knowledge to respeto (Δχ2 = 7.11, p < .01).

> "Socialization of independence was associated with more authoritative parenting practices (standardized coefficient = .55), while socialization of respeto was associated with more authoritarian parenting practices (standardized coefficient = .41)."
