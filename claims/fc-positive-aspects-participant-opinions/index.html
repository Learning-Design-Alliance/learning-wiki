Evidence¶

Cabi 2018¶

Emine Cabi. (2018). The Impact of the Flipped Classroom Model on Students' Academic Achievement. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 19(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1185114

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative focus-group interviews with 18 students (4 male, 14 female), analyzed by descriptive analysis with peer-reviewed coding. Responses in Table 7 show 4 students citing preparedness, 4 citing no out-of-class assignments, and 12 naming Kahoot as the application they liked most.

"According to the students, coming to classroom prepared and completing the assignments in the class so that they do not have to do them at home are among the positive aspects of this model."
