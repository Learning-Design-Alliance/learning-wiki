The authors' retrospective account of the methodology's iterative development and use by a pan-European project team, reporting the production of 'over 120 design patterns'. This is a reflective case report, not a controlled evaluation.

"We discuss the iterative development and use of our methodology by a pan-European project team of educational researchers, software developers and teachers. We reflect on our experiences of the participatory nature of pattern design and discuss how, as a distributed team, we developed a set of over 120 design patterns"
