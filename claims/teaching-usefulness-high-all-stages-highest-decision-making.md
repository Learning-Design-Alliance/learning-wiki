### Miyagami Taiju 2025

Miyagami Taiju, Nishizaki Yuji, Shimizu Taro, Yamamoto Yu, Shikino Kiyoshi, Kataoka Koshi, Kobayashi Hiroyuki, Naito Toshio, Tokuda Yasuharu (2025). A cross sectional study of the relationship between clinical scores and timing of guidance for resident physicians in outpatient settings. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-30748-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported usefulness on a five-point Likert scale (1: Very useless, 5: Very useful) from the same 4,662-resident cross-sectional survey. More than 90% of respondents rated teaching as "very useful" or "useful" during all stages.

> "the usefulness rate of teaching exceeded 90% during all stages, with teaching during the decision-making stage being rated as particularly useful (Fig. 3)."
