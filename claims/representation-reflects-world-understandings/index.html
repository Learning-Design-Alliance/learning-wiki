q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Theoretical argument in the article's abstract, offered without data. The authors assert that the cognitive representation formed in reading "reflects not only texts' content, but also individuals' understandings of the real world"; no empirical evidence or effect size is presented for this proposition.
