## Evidence

### Alessandro Vanzo 2025

Alessandro Vanzo, Sankalan Pal Chowdhury, and Mrinmaya Sachan. (2025). GPT-4 as a Homework Tutor can Improve Student Engagement and Learning Outcomes. Arxiv Preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.1502](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.1502)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Weekly questionnaires during the 8-week RCT compared treatment and control ratings on four measures using 6-point Likert scales; interestingness (d = 0.593) and level_of_resources (d = 0.586) reached significance with the printed effect sizes.

> "We observed that students in the treatment group gave higher ratings in all 4 categories. Of these interestingness (𝑑 = 0.593, 𝑃 = 0.011) and level_of_resources (𝑑 = 0.586, 𝑃 = 0.015) were significant but usefulness (𝑑 = 0.356, 𝑃 = 0.125) and comprehensiveness (𝑑 = 0.281, 𝑃 = 0.234) were not significant."
