## Evidence

### Crouch 2001

Catherine H. Crouch and Eric Mazur (2001). Peer Instruction: Ten years of experience and results. Am. J. Phys. 69(9), 970–977. https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1374249

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Web-based survey of PI users; instructors at 11 colleges and universities provided matched pre/post FCI data from which per-course normalized gains were computed, with a class average of 0.39 ± 0.09; Harvard data excluded.

> "We find that 27 of 30 (90%) PI courses in our survey fall in the medium-g range, with only three below g = 0.3 (Fig. 2)."

### Crouch 2001 (2)

Catherine H. Crouch and Eric Mazur (2001). Peer Instruction: Ten years of experience and results. Am. J. Phys. 69(9), 970–977. https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1374249

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Instructor self-report from the same survey; the authors note possible selection bias since there is no independent accounting of whether satisfied and dissatisfied users responded at the same rate.

> "Of the 384 identified PI users re- sponding to the survey, 303 definitely planned to use PI again and 29 probably would. Only seven respondents expressed no plans to use PI again."
