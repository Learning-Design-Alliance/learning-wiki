q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Narrative synthesis of self-reported cognitive and wellbeing outcomes in two PAL studies (Grospretre; Jerome). Mental fatigue decreased by 34% to 43%; anxiety reduced by 20% to 40% of participants. These are descriptive self-report percentages, not tested effect sizes.

"Self-reported mental fatigue was shown to decrease by 34% [51] to 43% [58] and anxiety levels were reported to be reduced by 20% [58] to 40% [51] of participants in two PAL studies."
