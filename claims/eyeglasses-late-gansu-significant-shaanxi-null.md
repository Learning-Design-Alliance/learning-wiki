## Subclaims
`q4 i?` Instrumental-variable LATE estimates show wearing eyeglasses improved Gansu students' standardized math scores by 1.770 SD (significant at 1%), but no significant impact was detected in Shaanxi in either unadjusted or adjusted models. [→ Du Kang 2022](#du-kang-2022)

## Evidence

### Du Kang 2022

Du Kang, Wang Huan, Ma Yue, Guan Hongyu, Rozelle Scott. (2022). Effect of Eyeglasses on Student Academic Performance: What Matters? Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191710923

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Instrumental-variable LATE analysis (Table 5) using random assignment as the instrument for actual eyeglass wearing; first-stage F statistics all well above 10, so weak IV is not a problem. Gansu adjusted effect 1.770 SD; Shaanxi null in both models.

> "Controlling baseline variables, wearing eyeglasses improved the standardized math score of myopic students in Gansu by 1.770 SD compared to the control group, which is significant at the 1% level (Table 5, Column 4). However, regardless of whether we use the unadjusted or adjusted LATE models, we detect no significant impact in Shaanxi."
