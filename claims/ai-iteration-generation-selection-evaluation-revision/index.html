Evidence¶

Yu J 2026¶

Yu J, Liu L and Zhu Q (2026). Is hands-on learning still necessary in the age of AI? A thematic review. Front. Psychol. 17:1897168. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1897168

q2 · i? · review · r2

Cross-study thematic synthesis of studies of AI-mediated design learning processes; the review reports that prompting is "not simply the input of keywords" but a way for students to organise ideas and advance the design process.

"Together, these studies show that AI-supported design iteration is not a one-off act of generation, but consists of a sequence of activities including generation, selection, evaluation, and revision."
