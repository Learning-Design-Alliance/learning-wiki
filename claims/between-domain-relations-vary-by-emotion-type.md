## Evidence

### Goetz et al. 2007

Thomas Goetz, Anne C. Frenzel, Reinhard Pekrun, Nathan C. Hall, and Oliver Lüdtke. (2007). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.4.715

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel analysis of between-domain homogeneity for five emotions in both grade samples (542 students total). Differences between enjoyment and pride and between pride and anxiety were significant in both samples; the article notes all differences were weak.

> "Enjoyment and anxiety showed the weakest, and pride the strongest, between-domains relations in both samples. However, all of the differences were weak, even though some were statistically significant."
