## Evidence

### Melby-Lervåg 2016

Melby-Lervåg, M., Redick, T. S., & Hulme, C. (2016). Working Memory Training Does Not Improve Performance on Measures of Intelligence or Other Measures of “Far Transfer”: Evidence From a Meta-Analytic Review. Perspectives on Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691616635612

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analyses (Q tests between subsets) across the broad included corpus, covering age bands, training dose (up to vs. more than 10 hr), learner status, randomization, training program type, and publication type. Significant moderator effects were found on intermediate and near transfer, suggesting adequate power.

> "we found virtually no evidence of significant moderator effects, especially for nonverbal ability."
