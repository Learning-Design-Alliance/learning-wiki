### Auerbach Anna Jo and Schussler Elisabeth 2017

Auerbach Anna Jo and Schussler Elisabeth. (2017). A Vision and Change Reform of Introductory Biology Shifts Faculty Perceptions and Use of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0258

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Per-practice alignment analysis (Table 6) comparing each instructor's interview-theme shifts with observed ALO/GALO: "half of the instructors had shifts in the category of “AL definition”" alongside increased use; four instructors adding assessment discussion also increased use. All but two instructors increased average ALO/GALO from first to last delivery.

> "half of the instructors had shifts in the category of “AL definition” toward themes more consistent with AL (students interacting and constructing knowledge) and also were seen to increase their use of ALO and GALO over the course of the reform."
