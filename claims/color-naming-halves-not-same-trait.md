`q2 i?` First-half and second-half color-naming scores cannot be treated as two measures of the same trait because subjects do not do as well in the second half. [→ Bingham 1916](#bingham-1916)

## Evidence

### Bingham 1916

Bingham, W. V. (1916). Some Norms of Dartmouth Freshmen. The Journal of Educational Psychology, VII(3), 129-142. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Some+Norms+of+Dartmouth+Freshmen%22+Bingham+1916

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Methodological observation from the test-procedure description (Woodworth and Wells number blank, whole blank at one trial, timed in halves). The author attributes the point to the monograph's own writers; the article prints no statistic for the half-to-half decline.

> "the two scores cannot be taken as two measures of the same trait, for subjects do not ordinarily do as well in the second half of this test as in the first half, as the writers of the monograph themselves pointed out."
