## Evidence

### Sehati and Khodabandehlou 2017

Samira Sehati, Morteza Khodabandehlou. (2017). Effect of Power Point Enhanced Teaching (Visual Input) on Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners' Listening Comprehension Ability. Journal of Educational Issues, Vol. 3, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5296/jei.v3i2.12323

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of post-test listening comprehension scores for 40 intermediate EFL learners randomly divided into control and experimental groups of 20, with pre-test as covariate. The article reports a "significance difference be tween experimental and control group" with F = 303.84 and the printed effect size Eta = .89, which exceeds the large-effect threshold.

> "The results (F = 303.84, f = .00, Eta = .89) shows th at there is a difference between two groups. It means that there is significance difference be tween experimental and control group."

### Sehati and Khodabandehlou 2017 (2)

Samira Sehati, Morteza Khodabandehlou. (2017). Effect of Power Point Enhanced Teaching (Visual Input) on Iranian Intermediate EFL Learners' Listening Comprehension Ability. Journal of Educational Issues, Vol. 3, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5296/jei.v3i2.12323

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same ANCOVA analysis reports adjusted post-test means: experimental M = 37.77 (SE = .36) versus control M = 28.72 (SE = .36), showing "the means of experimental group are upper than control group". No effect size is attached to this descriptive comparison.

> "Table 7 shows the corrected means of dependent  variable Listening comprehension ability. The data demonstrate that the means of experimental group are upper than control group."
