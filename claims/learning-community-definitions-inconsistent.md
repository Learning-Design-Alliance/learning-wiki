## Evidence

### Richard E. West 2017

Richard E. West, Gregory S. Williams. (2017). 'I don't think that word means what you think it means': A proposed framework for defining learning communities. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9535-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The authors' narrative review of education, sociology, and psychology literature found that accounts, studies, models, and theories of learning-community design use differing terminology, so that "many become confused about what a learning community is or how to measure it". No empirical measurement is reported; this is the review's own synthesis.

> "However, synthesizing and interpreting this scholarship can be difficult because researchers and practitioners use different terminology and frameworks for conceptualizing the nature of learning communities. Consequently, many become confused about what a learning community is or how to measure it."
