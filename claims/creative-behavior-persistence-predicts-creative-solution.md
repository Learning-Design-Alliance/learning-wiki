## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants producing a creative solution in the second activity spent more time on it (90 s on average) than conservative participants (46 s on average). [→ Anaïs Leroy 2022](#anais-leroy-2022)

## Evidence

### Anaïs Leroy 2022

Anaïs Leroy, Margarida Romero. (2022). Creative intention and persistence in educational robotic. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10128-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis test in the same 115-adult CreaCube experiment tested creative behavior persistence (activity 2 duration) against creative solution: "χ²(1,114) = 10.9; p < 0.001". Creative participants averaged 90 s (+/- 89) versus 46 s (+/- 28) for conservative participants.

> "the creative behavior persistence might be a good predictor of the creative solution (χ²(1,114) = 10.9; p < 0.001). This result agrees with our second hypothesis (H2), the conservative participants spend less time to succeed in the second activity (46 s in average +/- 28) compared to the creative participants (90 s in average +/- 89"
