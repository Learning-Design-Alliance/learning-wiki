Evidence¶

Torgerson 2014¶

Torgerson, D., Torgerson, C., Jefferson, L., Buckley, H., Ainsworth, H., Heaps, C., & Mitchell, N. (2014). Discover Summer School Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581103

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Primary analysis of an individually randomised controlled trial, adjusted for baseline-predicted KS2 writing level, conducted on 34 pupils (20 intervention, 14 control) in seven secondary schools. The article reports "an effect size of 0.25" with a confidence interval crossing zero, so the difference could be due to chance.

"There was no statistically significant evidence of a difference in extended writing score between the intervention and control groups with a non -significant increase of 0.97 marks for those in th e intervention group when compared with those in the control group (p = 0.47, 95% CI: -1.76 to 3.71). This relates to an effect size of 0.25 (95% CI: -0.42 to 0.92)."
