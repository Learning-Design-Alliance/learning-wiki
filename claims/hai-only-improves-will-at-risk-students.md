ITT regression from the same randomized trial shows the HAI-only condition, which featured no evidence-based content presentations, produced higher WILL for at-risk students relative to the ASM reference group at both time points; the abstract states "At-risk students receiving only HAI (100%) also showed higher levels of WILL".
