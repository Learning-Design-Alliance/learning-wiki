Melissa Roderick 2008¶

Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-potholes-road-college

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative case study of Maribel, who scored 15 on the ACT after prepping, raised her weighted GPA from 2.96 to 3.18, yet announced she would wait a year to work and save money for college.

"She felt that her ACT scores made her ineligible for college, and she didn't realize that, given her high grades, colleges might overlook her low ACT score."

Melissa Roderick 2008 (2)¶

Melissa Roderick, Jenny Nagaoka, Vanessa Coca, Eliza Moeller, with Karen Roddie, Jamiliyah Gilliam, and Desmond Patton. (2008). From High School to the Future: Potholes on the Road to College (Case Studies). Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-potholes-road-college

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The case study reports it was "her wariness about the cost of college that sealed her decision"; teachers' advice to wait and her parents' statement about lacking money reinforced the choice. She planned to eventually enroll at Wright College and transfer.

"She never talked to anyone about financial aid options, and she couldn't answer any questions about applying for aid or filling out a FAFSA."
