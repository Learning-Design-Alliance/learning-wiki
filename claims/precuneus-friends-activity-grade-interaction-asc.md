## Evidence

### Rodriguez Buritica 2025

Rodriguez Buritica, J., Berboth, S., Hoferichter, F., & Raufelder, D. (2025). Neural and behavioural correlates of adolescents changing academic self‐concept. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 234–248. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12667

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Brain-behaviour mixed-effects models adding precuneus ROI contrast estimates (friends/teachers conditions) to the behavioural model of 39 adolescents' ASC over time. The analysis found "a time x precuneus activity × grade × other‐evaluation condition interaction (β = 0.77, t = 2.01, p = .047)", specified to the friend condition (β = −0.36, t = −2.37, p = .021). Better achievement and reduced precuneus activity during friends-evaluations predicted higher ASC (Figure 4).

> "we found a time x precuneus activity × grade × other‐evaluation condition interaction (β = 0.77, t = 2.01, p = .047; survived FDR correction for multiple comparisons; see Figure 4)"

### Rodriguez Buritica 2025 (2)

Rodriguez Buritica, J., Berboth, S., Hoferichter, F., & Raufelder, D. (2025). Neural and behavioural correlates of adolescents changing academic self‐concept. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 234–248. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12667

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up median-split analysis of the same 39-participant longitudinal fMRI sample. For higher grades (Median = 4.75) reduced precuneus activity predicted ASC (β = −0.46, t = −2.1, p = .05); for lower grades there was no association (p = .88).

> "Separate analyses for median‐splitted grades (Median = 4.75) showed that for higher grades reduced precuneus activity predicted ASC (β = −0.46, t = −2.1, p = .05), but not for lower grades (p = .88)."
