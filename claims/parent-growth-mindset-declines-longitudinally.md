### Jiao Chen and Chunhui Liu 2023

Jiao Chen and Chunhui Liu. (2023). The longitudinal association between children’s growth mindset in senior primary school and their parents’ growth mindset. Front. Psychol. 14:1110944. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1110944

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariate latent growth curve models for fathers (n = 3,436) and mothers (n = 3,509) across five waves. Both showed significant negative slopes; significant intercept and slope variance indicated individual differences in starting levels and rates of change. A negative intercept–slope correlation was significant for mothers but not fathers.

> "The parental unconditional latent growth curve models were similar to the children’s model, and the slope factor of growth mindset was significant and negative (b = −0.019, p < 0.01, for fathers, b = −0.016, p < 0.01, for mothers)"
