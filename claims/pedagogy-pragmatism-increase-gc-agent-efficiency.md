## Evidence

### Hugo Caselles-Dupré 2023

Hugo Caselles-Dupré, Olivier Sigaud, Mohamed Chetouani. (2023). Show and Tell: Enhancing Agent Communication and Learning through Action and Language. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.10842](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.10842)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simulation study comparing naive teacher + literal learner against pedagogical teacher + pragmatic learner in the Fetch Block Stacking environment across four modality conditions; Table 1 reports AuC of 28.8 vs 35.7 for demonstrations only and 28.3 vs 30.6 for instructions only, among others. The article reports "an additional 2 points in Area Under the Curve on average".

> "As seen in our results in Fig.2 and Tab. 1, the addition of pedagogy and pragmatism increases learning efficiency in AI agents for all tested modalities."
