Sarah Lindley and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Becoming a physicist: Major educational transition points impact women's physics self-efficacy and sense of belonging. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.12024](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.12024)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results section (Melanie case): in a non-supportive environment she "hesitates to go to her instructors’ office hours," fearing she will look like "a complete idiot" for basic questions, and generally seeks help only from a group of female friends. Qualitative; no effect size printed.

> "Due to the non-supportive and non-inclusive environment, Melanie also sometimes hesitates to go to her instructors’ office hours, sharing that “...I don’t want to go in there and look like…a complete idiot if I have a really, like, basic question.”"
