### Pratim Sengupta 2013 (2)

Pratim Sengupta, Gokul Krishnan and Mason Wright. (2013). Integrated STEM in Elementary Grades Using Distributed Agent-based Computation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1402.7252](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1402.7252)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case of Ken and Yang: during user-testing they realized "it is much easier for users to control their machines if they are provided with coordinates for the vertices of target shapes", reflected in their final-day user instructions.

> "They realized during user-testing that it is much easier for users to control their machines if they are provided with coordinates for the vertices of target shapes such as equilateral triangles and rectangles."
