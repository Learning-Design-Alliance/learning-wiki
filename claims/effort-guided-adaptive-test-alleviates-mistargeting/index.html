Modeling student test-taking motivation in the context of an adaptive achievement test 2016¶

Modeling student test-taking motivation in the context of an adaptive achievement test. (2016). Journal of Educational Measurement, 53(1), 86–105. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/modeling-student-test-taking-motivation-in-the-context-of-an-adaptive-achievement-test/

q2 · i? · design · r2

A simulation study (not a human experiment) evaluated the proposed effort-guided adaptive test and showed it could "alleviate item difficulty mistargeting caused by unmotivated test taking." No simulation parameters or effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"an effort‐guided adaptive test was proposed and shown by a simulation study to alleviate item difficulty mistargeting caused by unmotivated test taking."
