## Evidence

### Charu Saxena 2007

Charu Saxena, Rini Kaur & P.Arun. (2007). Reaction Time of a Group of Physics Students. https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.1295 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0706.1295](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0706.1295)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In this measurement study, a practice test with ten students recorded reaction time every 15 minutes; the article states "Practice however did not keep on improving the re action time." The spread "is within ±0.422s of the average values" and "just ±0.075s when the first attempt is neglected", so the authors familiarized subjects with the machine for 20-25 minutes before measuring.

> "Practice however did not keep on improving the re action time. Only four out of ten students had better reaction time on their for th recording (i.e. after 45 minutes of practice)"
