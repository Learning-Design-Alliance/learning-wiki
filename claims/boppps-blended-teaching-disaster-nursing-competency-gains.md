## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Gains on a self-reported competency questionnaire were significantly higher in the BOPPPS elective section than in non-enrolled students who received no disaster-nursing instruction (mean rank 48.79 vs 29.76, Padj=.009), but not significantly higher than in the lecture-taught section of the same elective (mean rank 38.32, Padj=.34). The significant contrast sets the whole elective, with its Chaoxing blended delivery, against no instruction, so it cannot be credited to BOPPPS itself; groups were self-selected course sessions and a convenience sample, analysed as 77 independent students. ## Evidence

### Li P 2026

Li P, Taoyu J, Yaxin L, Xiao-li P, Muzi L, Haoying D. (2026). Evaluation of the Bridge-in, Objective, Preassessment, Participatory Learning, Postassessment, and Summary–Based Instructional Model in Disaster Nursing: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/77728

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with 77 third-year undergraduate nursing students at one university in three groups: students who chose the Tuesday session of a disaster-nursing elective (BOPPPS-based blended teaching on the Chaoxing platform, n=27), students who chose the Monday session (the same elective taught by traditional lecture, n=25), and a convenience sample from the same cohort who did not enrol and received no disaster-nursing education (n=25). Groups were therefore whole course sessions or a non-enrolled cohort, not randomised, and the analysis treated the 77 students as independent. The outcome was gain on the Disaster Nursing Competency Scale, a questionnaire of students' self-perceived knowledge, skills and competencies, not a performance measure. Gains were compared using the Kruskal-Wallis H test with Dunn post hoc correction. The omnibus test was significant (H=8.843, P=.01, η²=0.093), but the BOPPPS section differed significantly only from the no-instruction group, a contrast that bundles the whole elective and its blended delivery against no instruction; against the lecture section of the same elective, which isolates the teaching model, the difference was not significant. The no-instruction group also had the highest pretest score (155 vs 138 and 148).

> "The Kruskal-Wallis H test showed a statistically significant difference among the 3 groups (H=8.843, P=.01, η²=0.093). Dunn post hoc test with Bonferroni correction showed that the experimental group (mean rank=48.79) scored significantly higher than the comparison group (mean rank=29.76, z=2.968, Padj=.009), but no statistically significant difference was observed between the experimental and control groups (mean rank=38.32, z=−1.589, Padj=.34)"
