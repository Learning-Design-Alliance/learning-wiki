## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, R., Zhang, L., Hao, X.-M., Dreher, J.-C., & Qu, C. (2026). Learning to adaptively cooperate through social interactions during childhood and adolescence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00423-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple regression on individual θ estimates from Study 2: BAS remained a significant negative predictor after controlling for grade, indicating stronger reward sensitivity was associated with a greater tendency to choose Go straight.

> "Moreover, a multiple linear regression (θ ~ Grade + BAS)  controlling for grade showed that while grade  positively predicted θ (β = 0.11, p = .024), the BAS score remained a significant negative predictor (β = -0.07, p = .016)"

### Zhang 2026 (2)

Zhang, R., Zhang, L., Hao, X.-M., Dreher, J.-C., & Qu, C. (2026). Learning to adaptively cooperate through social interactions during childhood and adolescence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00423-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

One-way ANOVA on BIS/BAS scores across grade groups: significant BAS main effect (ηp² = 0.15), with grades 1-2 higher than grades 4-5 (d = 1.01, p < .001) and grades 7-9 (d = 0.92, p < .001). BIS showed no grade effect (p = .075).

> "a significant main effect of grades for BAS score (F (3, 137) = 8.00, p < .001, 𝜂𝑝2 = 0.15)"
