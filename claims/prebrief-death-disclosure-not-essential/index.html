Apara Teleireoluwa 2024¶

Apara Teleireoluwa, Hogan Tom, Peterson Jennifer L H. (2024). Death of the paediatric manikin: a scoping review. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2024-002941

q2 · i? · review · r3

Qualitative participant data from the included studies. The review contrasts this with Truog and Meyer's adult-simulation proposal of extensive prebriefing discussion of possible death, noting realism concerns.

"Conversely, participants agreed that a disclosure that the manikin would, or may, die in the scenario was not required in the prebrief or in the predefined learning objectives."
