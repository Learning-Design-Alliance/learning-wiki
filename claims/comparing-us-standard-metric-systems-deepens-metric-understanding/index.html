Evidence¶

Baschek 2022¶

Baschek, E. (2022). Using the US standard system for measurement in Germany – Bilingual education in primary school. https://math-primwq-bilingual.sd.uni-frankfurt.de/length-laengen/introduction-einleitung/

q2 · i? · theoretical · r?

Theoretical argument in the background section, attributed to NCTM (2000): unlike the US standard system, the metric system follows a decimal structure, so comparison of the two systems supports understanding of base 10 numeration. No empirical test is reported.

"By comparing the systems, students can deepen their understanding of the metric system and its base 10 numeration (NCTM, 2000)."

Baschek 2022 (2)¶

Baschek, E. (2022). Using the US standard system for measurement in Germany – Bilingual education in primary school. https://math-primwq-bilingual.sd.uni-frankfurt.de/length-laengen/introduction-einleitung/

q2 · i? · theoretical · r?

Author's argument in the background section that working with a second measurement system in bilingual classes affords intercultural learning. This is a stated rationale, not a tested result.

"Thus, in bilingual classes, dealing with other systems, such as the US standard system, lends itself to intercultural learning."
