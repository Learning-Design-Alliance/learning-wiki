## Evidence

### Karni Ofri 2025

Karni Ofri, Shitrit Itamar Ben, Perlin Amit, Jedwab Roni, Wacht Oren, Fuchs Lior. (2025). AI-enhanced guidance demonstrated improvement in novices' Apical-4-chamber and Apical-5-chamber views. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06905-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial sub-analysis of specific view scores in the proficiency exam of 44 novices; the AI-enhanced group "surpassed the non-AI group" on the combined A4C/A5C score. Table 2 reports the same contrast at p = 0.007.

> "A sub-analysis of specific views revealed that the group using AI-based instructions surpassed the non-AI group in the A4C and A5C views score [mean 88% (± SD 10%) vs. mean 76% (± SD 17%), respectively; p = 0.016]."
