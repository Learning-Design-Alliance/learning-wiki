# Collaborative pedagogies such as problem- and project-based learning are more likely to promote students' sense of belonging than lecture-based pedagogies, but effects depend on the group's attributes

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Pedagogies requiring students to work together are more likely to promote a sense of belonging, at least to other students, than many lecture-based pedagogies, though the degree depends on other students' attributes, values, and attitudes. [→ Kelly-Ann Allen 2025](#kelly-ann-allen-2025)

Expert interview with Vincent Tinto, quoted in the real-world applicability theme of this narrative-analysis study. No effect size printed.
