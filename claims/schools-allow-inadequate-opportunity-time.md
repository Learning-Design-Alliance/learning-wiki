## Evidence

### Carroll 1962

John B. Carroll. (1962). A Model Of School Learning. Harvard University. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Model+Of+School+Learning%22+Carroll

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Interpretive statement in the Opportunity section. Schools respond to differences in learning rates variously: uniform prescribed time, self-paced instruction, or ability grouping; rapid pacing causes slower students to fall behind while fast learners may lose motivation when time is wasted.

> "It may come as a surprise to some to be told that the schools may allow less than adequate time for learning any task, but second thought will make one realize that this is very often the case."
