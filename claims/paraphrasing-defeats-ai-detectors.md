### Craig Wright 2026

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper reports, citing Krishna et al. (2023), that DetectGPT's identification rate 'fell from 70.3 per cent to 4.6 per cent after paraphrasing with nominal semantic alteration'. This is a reported finding of the cited study, not a new experiment in this paper.

> "Krishna et al. ( 2023), applying a paraphrasing tool to GPT-2-XL output, report that DetectGPT' s identification rate fell from 70.3 per cent to 4.6 per cent after paraphrasing with nominal semantic alteration."

### Craig Wright 2026 (2)

Craig Wright. (2026). Transcription is not generation: distinguishing non-generative AI tool use from academic misconduct in higher education assessment. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00234-w

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper reports, citing Liang et al. (2023), two parallel experiments across seven widely-used detectors: for thirty-one AI-generated US college admission essays the literary-language prompt 'reduced detection rates from up to 100 per cent to up to 13 per cent'; a parallel prompt reduced abstract detection from up to 68 per cent to up to 28 per cent.

> "for thirty-one AI-generated US college admission essays, the prompt 'Elevate the provided text by employing literary language' reduced detection rates from up to 100 per cent to up to 13 per cent"
