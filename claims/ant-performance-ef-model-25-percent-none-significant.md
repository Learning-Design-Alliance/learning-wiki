## Evidence

### Dijkhuis Renée 2020

Dijkhuis Renée, de Sonneville Leo, Ziermans Tim, Staal Wouter, Swaab Hanna. (2020). Autism Symptoms, Executive Functioning and Academic Progress in Higher Education Students. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-019-04267-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Second hierarchical regression using ANT performance tasks (n = 38 for ANT correlations; regression F(3, 32)). The model was significant but "none of the predictors remained a significant predictor of academic success"; no effect size printed.

> "The second model, after adding the cognitive flexibility accuracy and working memory measures from the ANT, was statistically significant [F(3, 32) = 3.63, p = .023] and the explained variance in academic progress increased from 12 to 25%. However, in this last model, none of the predictors remained a significant predictor of academic success."
