Content analysis of 24 empirical articles in the review's RQ1 disciplinary distribution results, coded by learning subject. The review reports "The largest number of studies (n = 9)" in science and notes technology, arts, engineering and iSTEAM remain underrepresented.

> "The largest number of studies (n = 9) concentrated on ChatGPT-supported IBL in science-related domains, followed by mathematics (n = 5). By contrast, fewer studies addressed technology (n = 3), the arts (n = 3), engineering (n = 2) and iSTEAM (n = 2)."
