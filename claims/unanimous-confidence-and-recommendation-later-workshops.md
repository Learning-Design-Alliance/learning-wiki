### Gough Melissa 2020

Gough Melissa, Solomou Georgios, Khan Danyal Zaman, Kamel Mohammed, Fountain Daniel, Kumaria Ashwin, Ashpole Richard, Sinha Saurabh, Mendoza Nigel. (2020). The evolution of an SBNS-accredited NANSIG simulated skills workshop for aspiring neurosurgical trainees: an analysis of qualitative and quantitative data. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Closed-ended post-workshop questionnaire responses across the three NANSIG workshops. Positive responses rose from 70% at workshop 1 to 100% at workshops 2 and 3 for confidence assisting in theatre, recommending the workshop to a friend, and interest in an advanced course (80% to 100%).

> "In response to the question regarding whether delegates felt more confident in assisting in theatre after completing the workshop, 70% and 100% responded positively for workshops 1 versus 2 and 3, respectively."
