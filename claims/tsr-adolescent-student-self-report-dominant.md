Emma C. Burns · Penny Van Bergen. (2025). What Are Teacher–Student Relationships in Adolescent Motivation Research? A Systematic Review of Conceptualizations, Measurement, and Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09998-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Descriptive results across the 237 included studies. The review reports "the vast majority of studies (97.05%) collected data from students only"; six studies collected data from both students and teachers, and one relied solely on teacher reports.

> "In contrast to research with primary school students, which typically uses teacher-report scales, the vast majority of studies (97.05%) collected data from students only."
