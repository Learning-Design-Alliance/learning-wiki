`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of a combined focus group with two of ten first-year Astrophysics Ph.D. students and one of three Astrophysics M.S. students, plus open-ended survey responses from later-year students. A participant said it was "very easy to find a community here" and "very welcoming and easy to feel involved."

> "I definitely feel like it's been very easy to find a community here. It's been very welcoming and easy to feel involved. Everybody I've interacted with cares a lot, and you can tell."
