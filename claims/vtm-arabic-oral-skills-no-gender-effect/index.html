Evidence¶

Nail M. Alhajya 2018¶

Nail M. Alhajya, Sumaia S. Alzaghamim, Yousef M. Arouri. (2018). THE IMPACT OF VIRTUAL TRIPS ON THE DEVELOPMENT OF ARABIC LANGUAGE ORAL SKILLS AMONG THIRD GRADE STUDENTS IN JORDAN. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 8(1), 72-85. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.331

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA on the same 48-student quasi-experiment tested the gender main effect on post-test scores. Descriptive means showed experimental males at 36.9 and females at 38.41 versus control males at 26.50 and females at 23.35, but the gender effect was not significant. No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.

"Table 2 indicates that there weren’t statistically significant differences at the level of α=0.05 in the development of Arabic oral skills among the third graders between experimental group and the control in the post-test according to gender variable. The value of F= .180 and sig= .674"
