## Evidence

### Hou Yepo 2026

Hou Yepo, Xiao Chan, Yang Hui, Juan Mei, Zhao Guangye, Wang Chunfeng. (2026). Development and evaluation of a blended learning model integrating ARCS and BOPPPS for Endodontics education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08564-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized controlled trial with 275 fourth-year undergraduate dental students randomly assigned to experimental (n=137) or control (n=138) groups. The experimental group received a one-year ARCS + BOPPPS blended teaching intervention while controls received traditional instruction. ARCS motivation questionnaire results showed "significantly higher positive feedback across all four dimensions" (P < 0.01).

> "the experimental group demonstrated significantly higher positive feedback across all four dimensions, Attention (93.4%), Relevance (92.0%), Confidence (89.1%), and Satisfaction (96.4%), compared to the control group (P < 0.01)"
