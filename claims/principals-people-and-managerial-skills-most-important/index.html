New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals 2023¶

New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals, Explore Their Previous Experiences. (2023). NORC at the University of Chicago. https://www.norc.org

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Semi-structured interviews with 20 early-career CPS principals (12 elementary, eight high school) conducted 2020-2021. "The majority of principals cited people skills and emotional intelligence skills, along with organizational and managerial skills." Qualitative finding; no effect size.

"The majority of principals cited people skills and emotional intelligence skills, along with organizational and managerial skills, as most important for successful leadership."

New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals 2023 (2)¶

New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals, Explore Their Previous Experiences. (2023). NORC at the University of Chicago. https://www.norc.org

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

From the same interview study of 20 early-career principals: "Principals who had first been assistant principals said that role best prepared them to lead their own school," and preparatory programs and peer networks also provided essential skills. Qualitative self-report; no effect size.

"Principals who had first been assistant principals said that role best prepared them to lead their own school. Formal principal preparatory programs and peer networks also provided essential skills."
