### Andrew C. Butler & Shana K. Carpenter 2015

Andrew C. Butler & Shana K. Carpenter. (2015). Separating Myth from Reality in Education: Introduction to the Special Issue. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9344-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editorial reports, citing Rohrer (2015), a review of the widely endorsed spacing principle; the majority of lab and classroom studies support distributed over massed practice for long-term learning.

> "He finds that the overwhelming majority of studies in both the laboratory and the classroom support the idea that long-term learning is maximized when instruction is distributed rather than massed"
