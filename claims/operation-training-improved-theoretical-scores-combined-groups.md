### Sheng Jie 2022

Sheng Jie, Zhang Congdi, Gao Zhengkun, Yan Yimin, Meng Yucheng, Ren Shiqi, Liu Bin, Zhang Baoping. (2022). Virtual versus jaw simulation in inlay preparation preclinical teaching: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03930-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-sample T-tests comparing the two theoretical assessments within each group in the RCT. The article reports "Groups V-J and J-V were significantly improved (P < 0.0001)" and concludes the combination mode better promoted mastery of theoretical knowledge.

> "After the operation training, improvement was shown in the scores of the second theoretical examination of each group in comparison with the first (Fig. 7), as determined by a paired-sample T-test, and the scores of Groups V-J and J-V were significantly improved (P < 0.0001)."
