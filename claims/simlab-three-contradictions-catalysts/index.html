O'Hare 2025¶

O'Hare, S., Kajamaa, A., Conn, R., & Gormley, G. (2025). SimLab: an intervention to promote expansive learning and organisational change in team-based emergency care simulation. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09734-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Analysis of group discussion transcripts using Engeström and Sannino's contradiction framework identified three tensions in the activity system of paediatric emergency care, including the "individualised approach" contradiction.

"Three main tensions, interpreted here as manifestations of contradictions helped to exemplify the process of expansive learning, namely (1) Recognising challenges in their emergency care response using an individualised approach."

Related Claims¶

  • Participants reported increased confidence, readiness and teamwork after completing the SimLab programme — related
  • After implementing collaboratively designed changes (emergency trolley, grab bags, orientation rules), the second in-situ simulation showed smoother, faster emergency care delivery — related
  • In a theory-guided in-situ simulation programme, an interprofessional primary care team progressed through the expansive learning cycle from questioning current practice to consolidating new practices — related
  • A real-life paediatric emergency occurring after the intervention was managed well by staff who had not participated in SimLab, suggesting cascade of the transformed practices — related
