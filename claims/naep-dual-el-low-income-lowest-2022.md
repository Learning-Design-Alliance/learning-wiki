## Evidence

### Griffith 2025

Griffith, M., & Burns, D. (2025). Funding student needs: A review of state funding policies for English learners and students from low-income backgrounds. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/471.440

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report's disaggregation of 2022 NAEP scores into four groups (neither, one, or both of EL and low-income status) found "students who were both ELs and from low-income backgrounds achieved the lowest among the four groups" on all exams.

> "On both math and reading exams for 8th and 12th grades, EL students and students from low-income backgrounds scored well below their peers. However, students who were both ELs and from low-income backgrounds achieved the lowest among the four groups, with less than one third of those students scoring basic or above in reading and less than one quarter scoring basic or above in math."
