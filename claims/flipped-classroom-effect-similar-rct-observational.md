`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Univariate metaregression across 127 studies found no significant association between study design and effect size (P=.79). Both observational (SMD 0.90) and RCT (SMD 0.93) subgroups showed large, statistically significant effects favoring the flipped classroom.

> "no significant relationship between study design (RCT or observational), knowledge assessment reporting (baseline/final vs final), having same versus different instructors in compared groups, study level (undergraduate vs postgraduate), duration (semester vs block), students’ age, sex, period (before, during, or after the COVID-19 pandemic), and the outcome (P>.05 for all analysis)"
