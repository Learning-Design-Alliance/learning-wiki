Rob J.W.E. Lahaye & Natanael Karjanto. (2015). Report on Development of strategies to improve the students' learning by participation in class and out-of-classroom. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1505.06883)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

This is the instructor's own classroom observation from the General Physics courses, reported as practitioner experience rather than a controlled test. The report states "Nobody enjoyed this type of group activity and the effect was contrary to my intentions. I immediately abandoned this strategy."

> "This turned out to be too embarrassing for the students and most presentations became unclear mumbling stories while the rest of the class got bored."
