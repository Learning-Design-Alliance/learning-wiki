## Evidence

### Rudolf Pintner and Florence Fitzgerald 1918

Rudolf Pintner and Florence Fitzgerald. (1918). An Educational Survey Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Class-by-class Spearman rank-difference correlations on homogeneous grade groups, about 450 children across five schools, comparing each short subtest with its source long scale. The authors infer the separate parts are reliable measures of attainment in the separate subjects.

> "The shortened form of the standard educational measurements gives correlation coefficients ranging from .41 to .68 when the separate tests in the scale are compared with the corresponding long scales from which they were derived."
