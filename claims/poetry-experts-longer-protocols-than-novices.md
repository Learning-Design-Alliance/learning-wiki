## Evidence

### Peskin 1998

Peskin, J. (1998). Constructing meaning when reading poetry: An expert-novice study. Cognition and Instruction, 16(3), 235-263. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1603_1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Time-on-task and protocol-length records from the think-aloud study: novices averaged 19.75 minutes (range 8 to 33) and 1101.5 words; experts averaged 26.5 minutes (range 15 to 52) and 1719 words, mirroring Wineburg's findings with historians.

> "However, while the e xperts rapidly made sense of the poems, their protocols differed from those in most other studies on expertise in that they were longer than those of the novices."
