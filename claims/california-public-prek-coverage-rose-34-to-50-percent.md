# Combined enrollment in TK, CSPP, and Head Start raised California's coverage of all 4-year-olds from about 34% in 2019–20 to 50% in 2023–24

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The overall uptake across TK, CSPP, and Head Start combined increased from about 34% of 4-year-olds in 2019–20 to 50% in 2023–24, driven by enrollment growth and a 13% decline in the 4-year-old population. [→ Melnick 2024](#melnick-2024)

## Evidence

### Melnick 2024

Melnick, H., & García, E. (2024). Progressing toward universal prekindergarten in California [Brief]. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/progressing-toward-universal-prekindergarten-california

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis combining TK, CSPP, and Head Start enrollment data (with Head Start reduced 25% for estimated dual CSPP enrollment) against Census population estimates. Enrollment grew from about 166,100 to 217,200 while the 4-year-old population declined 13%, producing the uptake rise from 34% to 50% shown in Figure 2.

> "Combined, the number of 4-year-olds enrolled grew from about 166,100 in 2019–20 to 217,200 in 2023–24 (a 31% increase). At the same time, the estimated number of 4-year-olds in the state (the total number represented by each year’s bar) has declined by 13%, from about 498,000 to 432,000."
