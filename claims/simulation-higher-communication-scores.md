`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experimental between-group comparison of 404 first-semester medical students found the intervention group "achieved significantly higher scores compared with those in the control group". No standardized effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "However, students in the intervention group achieved significantly higher scores compared with those in the control group (median post-intervention score: 10.00 vs. 8.00; p < 0.001; Table 1) across all domains: establishing rapport, explaining procedures, and obtaining informed consent."
