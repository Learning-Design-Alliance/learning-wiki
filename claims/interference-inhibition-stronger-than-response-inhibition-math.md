## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The correlation between interference inhibition and mathematical ability (r = 0.21) is significantly stronger than the correlation between response inhibition and mathematical ability (r = 0.14). [→ Zhu 2025](#zhu-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhu 2025

Zhu, X., Tang, Y., Lu, J., Song, M., Yang, C., & Zhao, X. (2025). Inhibitory Control and Mathematical Ability in Elementary School Children: A Preregistered Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 37(1), 1. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09976-w

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Bivariate moderator analysis in the three-level meta-analysis found a significant moderating effect of inhibitory control type, F(1, 237) = 5.64, p = 0.018. Subgroup analyses showed interference inhibition correlated positively with mathematical ability in all domains (rs = 0.18–0.27), generally stronger than response inhibition (rs = −0.01–0.19).

> "the relationship between interference inhibition and mathematical ability, r = 0.21, 95% CI [0.16, 0.25], p < 0.001, was significantly stronger than the relationship between response inhibition and mathematical ability, r = 0.14, 95% CI [0.08, 0.20], p < 0.001, t = −2.37, p = 0.018."
