Evidence¶

Ahmed Yousif Abdelraheem & Abdelrahman Mohammed Ahmed 2015¶

Ahmed Yousif Abdelraheem & Abdelrahman Mohammed Ahmed. (2015). Effects of Activity Based Blended Learning Strategy on Prospective Teachers' Achievement and Motivation. i-manager's Journal of School Educational Technology, Vol. 11, No. 1, June - August 2015. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097417

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study of two randomly assigned sections of 26 students in TECH 3007 at Sultan Qaboos University, taught over 8 weeks; 2x2x3 ANCOVA with pretest as covariate. Experimental post-test mean was 17.0577 versus control 14.6731, and the article reports the difference as "significant at 0.012" in favor of the experimental group.

"there is a significant difference in the mean scores of the post test between the control group and experimental group (F-value= (6.865) with df = (1, 51) significant at 0.012) in favor of the experimental group"
