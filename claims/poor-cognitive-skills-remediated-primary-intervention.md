## Subclaims
`q2 i?` First-grade pupils who performed worst on cognitive skill measures improved after 27 teaching moments of remedial training spread over two school years, leading the authors to conclude that poor cognitive skills can be remediated. ## Evidence

### Outi Kyrö-Ämmälä & Kaarina Määttä 2011

Outi Kyrö-Ämmälä & Kaarina Määttä. (2011). Towards a Practical Framework of the Remediation of Cognitive Skills at Primary Level. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v1n1p28

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Action research with mixed-method analysis of Finnish first-graders: from 43 school entrants, the 12 worst performers on two school-readiness tests formed test and control groups of six each; the test group received 27 teaching moments over two school years. The article reports that "the poor cognitive skills can be remediated".

> "The results showed that the poor cognitive skills can be remediated. The changes in the scores varied a little between the test group and control group pupils both in the analyses between the preparatory and intermediate measures and between the preparatory and final measures."
