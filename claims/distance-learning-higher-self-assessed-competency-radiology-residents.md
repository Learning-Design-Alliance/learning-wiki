## Evidence

### Wang P 2025

Wang P, Wu Z, Zhang J, He Y, Jiang M, Zheng J, Wang Z, Yang Y, Chen Y, Zhu J. (2025). Distance Learning During the COVID-19 Lockdown and Self-Assessed Competency Development Among Radiology Residents in China: Cross-Sectional Survey. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/54228

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on a nationwide web survey of 2381 radiology residents (29.7% of 8008 targeted), controlling for age, sex, education, training year, working hours, income, and resident type. The article reports distance learners "were more likely to report high competencies" with β=0.35 (90% CI 0.24-0.45).

> "MLR analyses showed that radiology residents who were trained through distance learning were more likely to report high competencies (β=0.35, 90% CI 0.24‐0.45) after adjusted by participants' characteristics"
