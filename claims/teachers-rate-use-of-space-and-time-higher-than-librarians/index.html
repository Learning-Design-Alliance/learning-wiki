Felicia Warden · Andrew A. Tawfik 2026¶

Felicia Warden · Andrew A. Tawfik. (2026). Comparing perceptions of the school librarian using the future ready school—librarian framework. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10670-7

q3 · i? · associational · r2

MANOVA-based survey of 186 Tennessee K-12 stakeholders found a significant librarian–teacher difference on Use of Space and Time (teachers M = 2.30 vs. librarians M = 1.64); "a statistical significance between the school librarian and teacher roles (p = <.001)" while administrator contrasts were not significant.

"Post hoc pair-wise analysis using Tukey honestly significant differences (HSD) and correction identified a statistical significance between the school librarian and teacher roles (p = <.001) on the construct of Use of Space and Time, but did not show a statistically significant difference between the administrator and teacher roles (p =.058)"
