Evidence¶

Hatami 2015¶

Ali Hatami. (2015). The effect of collaborative learning and self-assessment on self-regulation. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2349

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Main effect of teaching method from the repeated-measures ANOVA on MSLQ self-motivation in the three-group quasi-experiment. The article reports the significance level as "less than .05" with no effect size printed.

"With regard to the first hypothesis, we find out that as shown in Table 2, significant level of learning teaching method is less than .05, i.e. cooperative learning and self-assessment have made changes in the students' self-motivation in the posttest."
