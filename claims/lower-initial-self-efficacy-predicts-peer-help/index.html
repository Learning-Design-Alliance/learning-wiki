Evidence¶

Aikens 2023¶

Aikens, M. L., & Kulacki, A. R. (2023). Identifying Group Work Experiences That Increase Students' Self-Efficacy for Quantitative Biology Tasks. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.22-04-0076

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

The abstract summarizes the mixed-effects logistic model of Getting Help from Peers on 429 coded responses; Table 4 prints the initial self-efficacy coefficient as B = −0.45 (odds ratio 0.64, p < 0.001), meaning each unit of initial self-efficacy reduced the odds of reporting peer help.
