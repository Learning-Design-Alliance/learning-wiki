## Evidence

### Latifa Jackson 2020

Latifa Jackson, Heriberto Acosta Maestre. (2020). The Data Science Fire Next Time: Innovative strategies for mentoring in data science. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1145/3394486.3411077](https://doi.org/10.1145/3394486.3411077)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Online pre- and post-workshop surveys of 2019 BPDM participants, not the same instrument (an 80-question pretest, N=26, and a 12-question post-test, N=17), so the samples differ and the rise is not shown for the same participants. Identity rose from 3.5067 (N=25) to 3.75 (N=16), team community from 4.3761 to 4.4792; the authors state the significant change "was also true" for identity, belonging, and team community. No effect sizes are printed.

> "This was also true for their sense of Identity prior (N=25, x=3.5067, SD=1.06009, SEMean=0.21202) and after the workshop (N=16, x=3.75, SD=1.07841, SE Mean=0.2696), their sense of belonging prior to (N=26, x=3.7067, SD=1.04937, SE Mean=0.2058) and after the workshop, and finally their sense of team community prior to (N=26, x=4.3761, SD=0.57183, SE Mean=0.11215), and after (N=16, x=4.4792, SD=0.49644, SE Mean=0.12411) the BPDM workshop."
