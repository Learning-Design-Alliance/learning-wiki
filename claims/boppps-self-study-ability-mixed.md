## Evidence

### Zhu Jing 2025

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects pooling of three studies (501 participants) with continuous self-study ability measures; pooled SMD = 0.17 was not statistically significant (P = 0.74), with I² = 0.96. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Regarding self-study ability, three studies employed continuous variables, but they did not indicate a significant improvement within the BOPPPS group when compared to the control group (SMD 0.17, 95% CI -0.82-1.16; P = 0.74; Table 5)."

### Zhu Jing 2025 (2)

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects pooling of three studies (607 participants) with dichotomous self-study ability variables; effect size OR = 5.70 (95% CI 1.18–27.58), I² = 0.84, favoring the BOPPPS group.

> "In three studies employing dichotomous variables, a significant difference was observed between the BOPPPS group and the control group (OR 5.70, 95% CI 1.18–27.58; P = 0.03; Table 5)."
