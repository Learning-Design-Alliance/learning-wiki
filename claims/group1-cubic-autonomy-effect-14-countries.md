## Evidence

### Núñez-Regueiro 2024

Núñez-Regueiro, F. (2024). Cubic Relations of Autonomous and Controlled Motivation to Achievement: A Cross-National Validation of Self-Determination Theory Using Response Surface Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 36 (3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09905-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural invariance testing of the 'Cubic effect of x' polynomial family across 14 TIMSS 2019 countries (including Australia, England, Finland, France, Singapore, Sweden). The constrained single-group solution "explained 10.7% of the variance in achievement" with excellent fit (CFI = 0.997, RMSEA = 0.011).

> "This model explained 10.7% of the variance in achievement, or an 8.1% increase relative to the additive model (i.e., R2= .099)."
