### Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun 2024

Khünl-Brady-Ertl Gudrun, Oeser Reinhard, Seemann-Hlawati Barbara, Varga Katja, Wagner-Menghin Michaela. (2024). Self-directed learning in post-graduate medical education: Self-judgement and supervisor judgement of competence development in Austrian nine-month basic training. GMS Journal for Medical Education 41(4): Doc42. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001697.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal analysis of 147 pseudonymised logbooks from junior physicians in three WIGEV clinics, rated at six time points (months 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9) on a 10-point percentage scale; box plots showed a "multi-peaked development" with dips after rotations.

> "After a change of department (after the 3rd and 6th months), the JPs downgrade their judgement of the level of completion."
