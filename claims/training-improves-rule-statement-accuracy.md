### Benjamin M. Chen and Hong Bao 2026

Benjamin M. Chen and Hong Bao. (2026). Training for Technology: Adoption and Productive Use of Generative AI in Legal Analysis. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.6217459](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6217459)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Rule Misstatements (0-4 scale counting erroneous statements or omissions of rules across four issues): Group 1 mean 2.469, Group 2 mean 2.579, Group 3 mean 2.155. The printed effect size for the Group 2 vs Group 3 contrast is d = -0.424.

> "Group 2 recorded a significantly higher number of Rule Misstatements compared with Group 3 (d = -0.424; p = 0.014*, one-tailed t-test), which suggests training can effectively help participants to state rule more accurately."
