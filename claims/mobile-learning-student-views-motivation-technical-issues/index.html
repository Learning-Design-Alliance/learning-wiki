Evidence¶

Kadir DEMIR 2018¶

Kadir DEMIR, Ercan AKPINAR. (2018). The effect of mobile learning applications on students' academic achievement and attitudes toward mobile learning. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology. http://dx.doi.org/10.17220/mojet.2018.04.004

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Semi-structured interviews with five randomly chosen mobile learning group students using an 11-question open-ended form; students said access to resources "kept me motivated" and reported technical problems with Internet connection and application notifications.

"Quick access to information, anywhere and anytime learning, interacting with friends and facilitating learning are observed as important key points of mobile learning according to the interviews with students."
