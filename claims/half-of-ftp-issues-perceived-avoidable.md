### Lo Kristin 2017

Lo Kristin, Curtis Heather, Keating Jennifer L., Bearman Margaret. (2017). Physiotherapy clinical educators’ perceptions of student fitness to practise. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0847-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Among 48 educators who supervised students with FTP issues, 25 (52%, CI 38-66) felt issues could have been avoided. Thematic analysis of free-text responses identified feed-forward, student education, clinical competency, clinician education and human resources themes.

> "Clinicians considered 52% (95% CI 38-66) of these issues avoidable through early disclosure, student and clinician education, maximising student competency prior to commencing placements, and human resources."
