## Evidence

### Fosli CA 2025

Fosli CA, Sperling S, Jensen RD, Skaarup SH. (2025). In-situ simulation-based team training reduces incidence of negative events during bronchoscopy. A prospective educational intervention cohort study. Respiratory Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12931-025-03205-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kirkpatrick level 4 patient questionnaire across preprocedural, intraprocedural, postprocedural, and general domains in the 124-patient cohort. Only the consciousness measure changed; the authors state there was "no change in the level of general unpleasantness, cough, dyspnea, chest pain, throat pain, overall satisfaction".

> "patients who underwent bronchoscopy after the simulation training was completed reported lower level of consciousness during bronchoscopy (median 2, IQR 1 – 5), than patients who had bronchoscopy before training (median 5, IQR 3 – 8), p = 0.02."
