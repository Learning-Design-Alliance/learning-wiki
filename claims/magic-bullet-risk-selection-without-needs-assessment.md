Evidence-based improvement: A guide for states to strengthen their frameworks and supports aligned to the evidence requirements of ESSA. San Francisco: WestEd. http://www.wested.org/resources/evidence-based-improvement-essa-guide-for-states

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Authors' interpretive argument in the framework section, not a tested result: the guide states that "a program produces positive outcomes on average" without guaranteeing success "in every case," so selection must be preceded by needs assessment and followed by implementation and local outcome analysis.

> "overfocusing on the decision itself can perpetuate a "magic bullet" concept of improvement: the fact that a program produces positive outcomes on average does not mean that it will do so in every case."
