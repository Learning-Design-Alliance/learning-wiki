## Evidence

### Eric Richter 2022

Eric Richter, Jeffrey P. Carpenter, André Meyer, Dirk Richter. (2022). Instagram as a platform for teacher collaboration and digital social support. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104624

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis (Research Question 2) with enthusiasm for teaching and self-efficacy as independent variables. Both predictors accounted for 13.0% of the variance in seeking information; co-creating was predicted by neither, at 2.9% variance explained.

> "more enthusiastic teachers spend more time on Instagram searching for information. Co-creating was neither related to self-efficacy nor to enthusiasm for teaching."
