Evidence¶

van Leeuwen 2019¶

van Leeuwen, A., Rummel, N., & van Gog, T. (2019). What information should CSCL teacher dashboards provide to help teachers interpret CSCL situations? International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 14, 261–289. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09299-x

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

Mixed ANOVA in the between-subjects experiment (n = 53; 18 mirroring, 17 alerting, 18 advising) with 8 trials as within-subjects factor. A significant trial-number effect on response time, F(1,50) = 93.21, η2 = .68 (large), but "no main effect of condition" on speed; equivalence was not tested. Mean times were about 59-69 seconds per situation.

"showed that there was indeed an effect of trial number on participants’ response time, F(1,50) = 93.21, p <. 0 0 1 ,η2 = .68, but no main effect of condition, F(2,50) = 1.35, p = .27"
