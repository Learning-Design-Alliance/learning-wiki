## Evidence

### Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh 2008

Andrew Mason and Chandralekha Singh. (2008). Reflection and Self-Monitoring in Quantum Mechanics. Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Pittsburgh. [doi:10.1063/1.3266713](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3266713)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Case study of 14 honors quantum mechanics students given the same four problems on midterms and final exam, scored with an adapted rubric with at least 80% inter-scorer agreement. The article reports the "midterm average score of all students was 66%" versus 60% on the final, indicating slightly worse final performance.

> "Overall, the midterm average score of all students was 66% on all four problems and 57% on the three difficult problems (omitting the “easy” problem). The average final exam score of all students was 60% on all four problems and 53% on the three difficult problems."
