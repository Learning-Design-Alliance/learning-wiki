### Martin Lnenicka and Petr Coufal 2026

Martin Lnenicka and Petr Coufal. (2026). Navigating AI in STEM: what secondary students actually do with generative AI-driven tools. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00637-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey results (Section B) from n = 416 Czech secondary students in business-oriented schools on their most-used generative AI tool. The article reports a "stark monoculture dominated by OpenAI's ChatGPT"; counts are as printed: ChatGPT n = 364, Gemini n = 33, Copilot n = 5.

> "When asked which tool they use most often, respondents again overwhelmingly selected ChatGPT ( n = 364), with substantially fewer choosing Gemini ( n = 33) or Copilot (n = 5)."
