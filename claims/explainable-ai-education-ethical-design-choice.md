The commentary's argument, citing Floridi & Cowls (2022): the five-principle framework includes explicability, a combination of intelligibility (how the PLA system works) and accountability (who is responsible for how it works). The article concludes explainable AI in education is "an ethical design choice".

> "a recent synthesis of six major initiatives to establish ethical principles for the adoption of socially beneficial AI has identified a core set of five ethical principles for AI systems: it includes the four core bioethics principles of beneficence, non-maleficence, autonomy, and justice, plus the addition of explicability"
