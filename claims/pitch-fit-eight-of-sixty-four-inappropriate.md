Edward Jay Wang. (2024). Using Peer-Customers to Scalably Pair Student Teams with Customers for Hands-on Curriculum Final Projects. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2409.08299](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2409.08299)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational analysis of pitch appropriateness across two course offerings (N=64 total pitches). The authors report that inappropriate needs "typically had no physical component, and mostly required pure software implementations" and note only 1/3 of pitches needed to be selected, so this posed no big issue.

> "Out of two offerings we implemented peer-customers, which included a total of N=64 (N=29 in Year1 and N=35 in Year 2) pitches, we found only N=8 pitches (N=4 in Year 1 and N=4 in Year 2) that would be inappropriate for a class project."
