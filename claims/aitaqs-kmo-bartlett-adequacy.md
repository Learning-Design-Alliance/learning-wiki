### Zhang 2026

Zhang, Z., Katayoun, M., Tan, L., & Wei, G. (2026). Development and Validation of a Multidimensional Scale for AI Tutoring Acceptance in Higher Education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00431-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Preliminary diagnostic analysis of the validation dataset, reported in the Results section. The article states "the KMO value is 0.758" and the Bartlett test was statistically significant at p<0.001, supporting factor analysis.

> "the KMO value is 0.758, which is higher than the recommended threshold value of 0.80, indicating that the sufficiency of the selected samples is strong. According to the results of Bartlett’s sphericity test, χ²=438.75, degrees of freedom (df) =66, and p<0.001 is statistically significant."
