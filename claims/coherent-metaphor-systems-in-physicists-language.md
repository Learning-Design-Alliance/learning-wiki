### David T. Brookes and Eugenia Etkina 2007

David T. Brookes and Eugenia Etkina. (2007). Using conceptual metaphor and functional grammar to explore how language used in physics affects student learning. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.1319 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0704.1319](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0704.1319)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative linguistic analysis of QM textbook passages and interviews with five physics professors, presented in Tables III–V. The analysis shows the potential well metaphor treats the particle/wave function and the potential energy graph as separate grammatical participants, and "contradicts the conventional view of the potential energy as a property of the particle or the system."

> "Thus the common grammatical structure of the potential well metaphor contradicts the conventional view of the potential energy as a property of the particle or the system."
