## Evidence

### Abdelhai R 2012

Abdelhai R, Yassin S, Ahmad MF, Fors UGH. (2012). An e-learning reproductive health module to support improved student learning and interaction: a prospective interventional study at a medical school in Egypt. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-11

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square comparison of student perceptions of question difficulty: 24.3% (17/70) of e-learning students found questions easy versus 7.7% (9/117) of traditional students, and "43.6% of students attending the traditional lectures" found them difficult versus 5.7% of e-learning students (P < 0.001).

> "43.6% of students attending the traditional lectures (n = 51/117) found questions difficult versus only 5.7% of students from the e-learning group (n = 4/70), and these findings were highly significant with P < 0.001 [Table 3]."
