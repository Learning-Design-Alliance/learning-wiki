### Abuzied Abdelhamid Ibrahim Hassan 2023

Abuzied Abdelhamid Ibrahim Hassan, Nabag Wisal Omer Mohamed. (2023). Structured viva validity, reliability, and acceptability as an assessment tool in health professions education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04524-6

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis in OpenMeta[Analyst] of acceptability percentages from 12 of the 24 included studies, using a binary random-effects model due to high heterogeneity. Learners described the tool as "clear and fair and had a reasonable level of difficulty".

> "Based on the available numeric data of the 12 studies, overall acceptability was (79.8%, P < 0.001) out of all learners who participated in structured viva as examinees at different levels in health professions education. The heterogeneity of the data was high (I^2 = 93.506, P < 0.001)"
