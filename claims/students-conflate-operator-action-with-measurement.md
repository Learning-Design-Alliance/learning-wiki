## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Helping Students Learn Quantum Mechanics for Quantum Computing. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06666](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06666)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview study using a think-aloud protocol with advanced students presented a conversation about measurement of an observable A. Many students "incorrectly stated that both Caroline and John are actually saying the same thing" even though John explicitly disagreed with Caroline.

> "Surprisingly, many interviewed students incorrectly stated that both Caroline and John are actually saying the same thing and they are both correct despite the fact that John explicitly says that he disagrees with the other two."
