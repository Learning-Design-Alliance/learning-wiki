## Evidence

### Rebecca Hey · Mark McDaniel · Flaviu A. Hodis 2024

Rebecca Hey · Mark McDaniel · Flaviu A. Hodis. (2024). How undergraduate students learn: uncovering interrelationships between factors that support self-regulated learning and strategy use. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09389-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis (Table 5) regressing each strategy on self-control, engagement, effort regulation, and MCSR; the article reports engagement predicted "rehearsal, elaboration, and self-testing" and effort regulation predicted "rehearsal, organisation, spacing, and cramming"; R2 ranged from .12 (rereading) to .41 (elaboration).

> "student engagement was a significant predictor of three learning strategies (i.e., rehearsal, elaboration, and self-testing), as well as self-reported academic achievement. Similarly, effort regulation was also a significant predictor of four learning strategies (i.e., rehearsal, organisation, spacing, and cramming), and self-reported academic achievement."
