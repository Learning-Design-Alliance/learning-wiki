## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In think-aloud interviews, many TAs explicitly noted the task was challenging and that it was difficult to think about physics questions from a student's perspective, yet many noted it was worthwhile. [→ Nafis I. Karim 2017](#nafis-i-karim-2017)

## Evidence

### Nafis I. Karim 2017

Nafis I. Karim, Alexandru Maries, and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Teaching assistants' performance at Identifying Common Introductory Student Difficulties Revealed by the Conceptual Survey of Electricity and Magnetism. 2017 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.047

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud interviews with 11 TAs who had more than two semesters of recitation teaching experience. The article reports TAs found the task challenging (e.g., "I don't know introductory students well enough") but worthwhile; this is the authors' reported interview finding with no effect size.

> "many TAs noted that the CSEM-related PCK task was worthwhile and helped them think about the importance of putting themselves in their students' shoes in order for teaching and learning to be effective"
