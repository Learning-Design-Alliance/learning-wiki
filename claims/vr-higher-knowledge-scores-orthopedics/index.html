Evidence¶

Li Ting 2025¶

Li Ting, Yan Jingxin, Gao Xin, Liu Hangyu, Li Jin, Shang Yuanting, Tang Xiaoyu. (2025). Using Virtual Reality to Enhance Surgical Skills and Engagement in Orthopedic Education: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/70266

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis pooling 16 RCTs (n=794) comparing VR teaching with traditional teaching in orthopedic education; random-effects model used due to significant heterogeneity (I2=83%). The pooled result shows "significantly higher knowledge scores" favoring VR; GRADE certainty was rated very low.

"VR teaching demonstrated significantly higher knowledge scores compared to traditional teaching methods (SMD=1.08, 95% Cl 0.71-1.46; P<.001; see Figure 3)."
