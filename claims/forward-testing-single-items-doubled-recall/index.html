Evidence¶

Chunliang Yang 2018¶

Chunliang Yang, Rosalind Potts and David R. Shanks. (2018). Enhancing learning and retrieval of new information: a review of the forward testing effect. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0024-y

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The review reports Szpunar et al.'s three-group single item experiment in which an Interim Test, Interim Restudy, and Interim Distractor group studied five word lists; the quote shows the doubled recall finding, and the review also reports about ten times fewer proactive-interference intrusion errors in the Interim Test group.

"the Interim Test group correctly recalled about twice as many List 5 words as the Interim Distractor and Interim Restudy groups in the List 5 interim test, which did not differ in their levels of recall"
