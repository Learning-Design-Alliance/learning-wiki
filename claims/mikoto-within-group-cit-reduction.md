`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary endpoint analysis of the cluster-randomized trial using mixed-effects models. The mikoto group's within-group reduction in cecal intubation time was significant; the control group's was not, and the between-group post-training difference was not significant (p = 0.383).

> "CIT decreased within the mikoto group from 1117.7 ± 527.4 s to 957.9 ± 462.0 s (p = 0.020), whereas the reduction in the control group from 924.9 ± 481.0 s to 804.1 ± 600.2 s did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.078)."
