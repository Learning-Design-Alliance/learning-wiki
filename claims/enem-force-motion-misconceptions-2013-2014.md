# ENEM force-and-motion items (2013_Q87, 2014_Q67, 2014_Q82) show persistent non-scientific conceptions that force is proportional to velocity and that motion requires a continuous force

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · associational `r?` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2018 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person · **overdue for a recheck since 2023**: baselines and conditions change

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On 2013_Q87 (parachute), only 15% chose the correct answer and the item had negative discrimination (a = -0,012). [→ Barroso 2018](#barroso-2018)
`q2 i?` On 2014_Q67 (inclined plane), distractor C, reflecting the idea that a continuous force is needed to keep a body moving, was the most chosen at 33%. [→ Barroso 2018 (2)](#barroso-2018-2)
`q2 i?` On 2014_Q82 (uniform circular motion), only 12% chose the correct answer, with distractor B most marked at 35%. [→ Barroso 2018 (3)](#barroso-2018-3)

## Evidence

### Barroso 2018

Barroso, M. F., Rubini, G., & Silva, T. (2018). Dificuldades na aprendizagem de Física sob a ótica dos resultados do Enem. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1802.09880](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1802.09880)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

IRT and distractor analysis of ENEM 2013 question 87 (parachute graphs). The text reports "O gabarito, (B), foi escolhido por 15% dos concluintes" and that choice of alternative E evidences the alternative conception that force is proportional to velocity.

> "É curioso notar que esta questão possui índice de discriminação negativo (a = -0,012)."

### Barroso 2018 (2)

Barroso, M. F., Rubini, G., & Silva, T. (2018). Dificuldades na aprendizagem de Física sob a ótica dos resultados do Enem. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1802.09880](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1802.09880)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Distractor analysis of ENEM 2014 question 67 (inclined plane), a high-difficulty item (663 points, about 2.5 standard deviations above the mean). The correct answer A only represents more than half of markings from 680 points.

> "Na Tabela 6, verifica -se que o distrator (C) é o  que tem maior percentual de escolha, 33%."

### Barroso 2018 (3)

Barroso, M. F., Rubini, G., & Silva, T. (2018). Dificuldades na aprendizagem de Física sob a ótica dos resultados do Enem. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1802.09880](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1802.09880)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Distractor analysis of ENEM 2014 question 82 (satellite/circular motion), with difficulty parameter around 700 points. Alternatives B and C, indicating tangential acceleration in the same direction as velocity, were chosen by 48% of completers.

> "O gabarito  (A) é assinalad o por um número muito pequeno de concluintes, 12%."
