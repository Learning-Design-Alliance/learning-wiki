## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Helping Students Learn Quantum Mechanics for Quantum Computing. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06666](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06666)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Interview study with advanced students; one student said "if you wait for a sufﬁciently long time, it has to go back to the wavefunction before measurement." The belief was somewhat more prevalent when the pre-measurement state was the ground state. Very few students re-evaluated even when told the system was completely isolated.

> "While many students believe that a quantum system gets stuck in an eigenfunction after a measurement, a large number of students believe the opposite, i.e., if one waits long enough, the time-evolution will guarantee that the wavefunction after the measurement will go back to the “original” wavefunction (right before the measurement took place)."
