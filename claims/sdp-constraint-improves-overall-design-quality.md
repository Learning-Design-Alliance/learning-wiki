## Evidence

### Hossein Talebzadeh 2025

Hossein Talebzadeh. (2025). The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Green Education: Optimizing Teacher Workflow and Enhancing Pedagogical Design under Sustainable Development Pedagogy (SDP) Constraints. 3rd National Conference on Green Industries Innovation, University of Mazandaran. https://conf.umz.ac.ir/gii [doi:10.35542/osf.io/x6qzy_v1](https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/x6qzy_v1)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest comparison of lesson plans from 28 pre-service teacher teams (91 pre-service geography teachers) at Farhangiyan University, Baseline vs SDP phase, evaluated with a validated 5-point rubric. The paired t-test showed the overall design score advanced from "M = 3.17 to M = 4.66" with d = 2.80. No effect-size-adjusted design controls beyond concurrent feedback were reported.

> "The overall design score advanced from M = 3.17 to M = 4.66 (t(27) = 13.78). The calculated Cohen's d effect size for the overall score was 2.80, denoting an exceptionally powerful and practically meaningful operational impact"
