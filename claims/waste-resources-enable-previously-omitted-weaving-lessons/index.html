Yeboah et al. 2016¶

Rita Yeboah, Eric Appau Asante, Nana Afia Opoku-Asare. (2016). Teaching Interactive Art Lessons with Recycled Waste Materials as Instructional Resources. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.14. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1102965

q2 · i? · causal · r1

From interviews with the 14 participating teachers: the article reports that some Creative Art and BDT teachers taught weaving for the first time during the study because "they lacked the skills to do the weaving" and materials were unavailable.

"The teachers were not teaching the lessons because they lacked the skills to do the weaving, instructi onal resources were not available, and they lacked mater ials teachers and students could work with."
