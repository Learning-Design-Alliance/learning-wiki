Ilya Mikhelson 2026¶

Ilya Mikhelson. (2026). The Theoretical Foundation of Socratic Tests: Dynamic, Multimodal, Conversational Examinations. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29624 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.29624

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative instructor report from the Spring 2026 pilot (Section 6.1). The instructor described the AI as able to "titrates its way to the limit of each student's understanding." Case-level instructor testimony; no quantitative differentiation data are printed.

"Because the proctor scales the cognitive burden dynamically, the instructor was able to“directly compare transcripts to differentiate between students at the D/F boundary as well as the A/B boundary,”while simultaneously presenting“multiple ’stretch’ lines of questioning”to the top quartile of the cohort."
