Evidence¶

Ines Zeitlhofer 2026¶

Ines Zeitlhofer, Joerg Zumbach. (2026). Germane processes moderate the effectiveness of complex desirable difficulty tasks. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09807-1

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

The RQ2 MANOVA showed a moderate multivariate complexity effect (η² = 0.11); univariate effects were η² = 0.07 for ICL and η² = 0.03 for both MAI scales, with "tasks with consistently high complexity resulted in lower ICL and reduced use of metacognitive strategies".

"a MANOV A with complexity as the independent variable and ICL, MAI-complexity, and MAI-difficulty as the dependent variables revealed a moderate main effect of complexity on the dependent variables ( F (3, 128) = 5.08, p = .002, η² = 0.11)."
