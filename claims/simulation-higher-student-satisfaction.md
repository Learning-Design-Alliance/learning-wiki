## Evidence

### Couto Thomaz Bittencourt I 2015

Couto Thomaz Bittencourt I, Farhat Sylvia C.L., Geis Gary L, Olsen Orjan, Schvartsman Claudio. (2015). High-fidelity simulation versus case-based discussion for teaching medical students in Brazil about pediatric emergencies. Clinics. [doi:10.6061/clinics/2015(06)02](https://doi.org/10.6061/clinics/2015(06)02)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Satisfaction survey (8 statements, 3 domains, 5-point Likert scale) completed by 124 students after each activity; paired-samples tests showed simulation rated as more realistic, a more positive experience and more useful to the profession, with "a preference for simulation (p<0.001)".

> "Moreover, the satisfaction survey revealed a preference for simulation (p<0.001)."
