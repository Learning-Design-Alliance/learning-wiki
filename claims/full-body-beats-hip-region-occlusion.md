## Evidence

### Causer Joe 2017

Causer Joe, Smeeton Nicholas J., Williams A. Mark. (2017). Expertise differences in anticipatory judgements during a temporally and spatially occluded task. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0171330

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Main effect of spatial occlusion in the mixed ANOVA on correct response (F1, 22 = 248.16, partial eta squared = 0.92): full body "M = 63, SD = 17%" versus hip region "M = 52, SD = 18%".

> "Response accuracy was significantly higher in the full body condition (M = 63, SD = 17%; p < 0.001) compared to the hip region condition (M = 52, SD = 18%)."
