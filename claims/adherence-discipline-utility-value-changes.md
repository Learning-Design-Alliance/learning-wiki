### Gaspard 2021

The potential of relevance interventions for scaling up: A cluster-randomized trial testing the effectiveness of a relevance intervention in math classrooms. Journal of Educational Psychology. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Implementation fidelity was rated by pairs of trained observers in every intervention session; adherence was rated on a 10-point script-adherence item and discipline problems with three items on a 4-point scale. The summary statement reports both fidelity indicators were "associated with more positive changes in utility value."

> "In both intervention conditions, higher levels of adherence and lower levels of discipline problems were associated with more positive changes in utility value."
