# Unscaffolded GenAI access was not associated with a significant difference in pre-service teachers' critical thinking relative to traditional learning

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` After controlling for pretest scores, adjusted posttest critical thinking scores did not differ significantly between the experimental and control groups, with a negligible effect size. [→ Zhang Jun 2026](#zhang-jun-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhang Jun 2026

Zhang Jun, Peng Yuting, Deng Xinyue, Zeng Qin, Wang Kai. (2026). The Impact of Unscaffolded GenAI Use on Pre-Service Teachers’ AI Readiness, Self-Regulated Learning, Critical Thinking, and Instructional Design Performance: A Quasi-Experimental Study. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071114

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA on critical thinking (six-item scale) with pretest as covariate in the quasi-experiment of 52 pre-service chemistry teachers; adjusted means were 4.05 in both groups. The article reports "F = 0.000, p = 0.997, η2 = 0.000, with a negligible effect size".

> "The ANCOVA analysis showed no significant difference between the experimental and control groups, F = 0.000, p = 0.997, η2 = 0.000, with a negligible effect size."
