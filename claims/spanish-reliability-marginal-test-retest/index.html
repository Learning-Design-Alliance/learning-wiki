MAP Growth Technical Report Spanish Addendum for 2024–2025 2026¶

MAP Growth Technical Report Spanish Addendum for 2024–2025. (2026). HMH Education Company / NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-spanish-technical-report-addendum/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Marginal reliability analysis across terms and grades (Table 4.7). Reliability generally increased across grades, with the lowest estimates in kindergarten; winter and spring administrations tended to yield slightly higher estimates than fall.

"In Math, reliability values range from 0.89 to 0.98, while in Reading, values range from 0.84 to 0.97."

MAP Growth Technical Report Spanish Addendum for 2024–2025 2026 (2)¶

MAP Growth Technical Report Spanish Addendum for 2024–2025. (2026). HMH Education Company / NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-spanish-technical-report-addendum/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Test-retest reliability with alternate forms across consecutive windows (Table 4.8). The report explains "the fall–spring interval spans more time for true academic growth to occur, which reduces stability coefficients"; Math high school fall–spring dropped to 0.52.

"winter–spring reliabilities are highest, fall–winter reliabilities are slightly lower, and fall–spring reliabilities are lowest"
