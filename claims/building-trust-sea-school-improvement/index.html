q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Interview finding from the SEA administrator study: participants reported that many schools and districts experience state oversight as compliance-oriented or punitive, and that "strong, trusting relationships between state and local actors" underpin the shift to support.

"Several state administrators indicated that these shifts are more likely to succeed if they are grounded in strong, trusting relationships between state and local actors, and emphasized the importance of building trust."
