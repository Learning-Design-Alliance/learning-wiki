`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

This is the authors' interpretation of the questionnaire results and their device-testing observations. They report testing "about 10 devices (webcam, endoscope camera, bullet camera, phone connected camera)" and selecting the bullet mini camera because it allows fluid vision of movements and can be manipulated almost like a laparoscope, though it needs a manual focus.

> "The most relevant issue for this laparoscopic box seemed to be related to image quality and camera support; in fact, lower quality results were obtained for specific questions about these aspects."
