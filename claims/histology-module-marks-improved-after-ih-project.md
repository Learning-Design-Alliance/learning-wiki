# Cumulative mean examination marks in the histology-based A11HDT module at UNMC increased significantly after introduction of the international histology project, improving relative to other first-year modules.

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Then 2023

Then, S.-M., Kokolski, M., Mbaki, Y., Merrick, D., & Anderson, S. (2023). An international collaborative approach to learning histology using a virtual microscope. Anatomia, Histologia, Embryologia, 52, 21-30. https://doi.org/10.1111/ahe.12888

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quantitative analysis of UNMC Year 1 cohort performance on the A11HDT module before (2012/13, 2013/14) and after (2014/15–2018/19) the IH project, compared using unpaired t-test. "a significant increase in cumulative mean marks for the A11HDT module" was found; no effect size is printed.

> "Figure 6a shows a significant increase in cumulative mean marks for the A11HDT module after the introduction of the IH project. Prior to the introduction of the IH project in 2014, the A11HDT module had one of the lowest mean scores of all Year 1 modules"

### Then 2023 (2)

Then, S.-M., Kokolski, M., Mbaki, Y., Merrick, D., & Anderson, S. (2023). An international collaborative approach to learning histology using a virtual microscope. Anatomia, Histologia, Embryologia, 52, 21-30. https://doi.org/10.1111/ahe.12888

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of average examination performance across all first-year UNMC modules (Table 1) before (n=2 cohorts) and after (n=3 cohorts) the intervention, analysed with multiple unpaired t-tests. The A11HDT mean score "improved relative to other modules on the course".

> "Following the introduction of the IH project the A11HDT module mean score improved relative to other modules on the course (Figure 6b)."
