## Evidence

### Sutcher 2016

Sutcher, L., Darling-Hammond, L., & Carver-Thomas, D. (2016). A coming crisis in teaching? Teacher supply, demand, and shortages in the U.S. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/coming-crisis-teaching

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The report's own supply-and-demand modeling, built from federal databases (Schools and Staffing Survey, Teacher Follow-Up Survey, Baccalaureate and Beyond, Title II data), found "an estimated teacher shortage of approximately 64,000 teachers in the 2015–16 school year." The report projects annual shortages could reach 112,000 by 2018.

> "Combining estimates of supply and demand, our modeling reveals an estimated teacher shortage of approximately 64,000 teachers in the 2015–16 school year."
