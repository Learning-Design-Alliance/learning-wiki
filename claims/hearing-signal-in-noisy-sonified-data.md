## Evidence

### Jake Noel-Storr and Michelle Willebrands 2022

Jake Noel-Storr and Michelle Willebrands. (2022). Accessibility in astronomy for the visually impaired. [doi:10.1038/s41550-022-01691-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-022-01691-2)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interviewee GF argues that human hearing has "tremendous dynamic range and temporal resolution" and that sonification lets listeners distinguish very faint sounds in a noisy background. NB adds that "it is amazing what signal you can pick out from noisy data in sound". These are expert assertions, not tested results.
