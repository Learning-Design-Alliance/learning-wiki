Çetin 2017¶

Barış Çetin. (2017). The Influence of Pintrich’s Self-regulated Learning Model on Elementary Teacher Candidates in a Life Science Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i8.2460

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-test on the lack of self-directedness sub-scale of the Self-regulated Learning Perception Scale with 39 candidates; the article reports t(38) = 2.117, significant in favor of pretest, i.e. posttest lack of self-directedness was lower.

"A statistically significant difference was found in favor of pretest in sub -factor of "Lack of self-directedness" (t(38) = 2.117, p = .000 < .05)"
