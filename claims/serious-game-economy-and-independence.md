## Evidence

### Haubruck P 2018

Haubruck P, Nickel F, Ober J, Walker T, Bergdolt C, Friedrich M, Müller-Stich BP, Forchheim F, Fischer C, Schmidmaier G, Tanner MC. (2018). Evaluation of App-Based Serious Gaming as a Training Method in Teaching Chest Tube Insertion to Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.9956

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

OSATS subgroup analysis from the direct rating in the same RCT. The economy-of-time-and-motion step favored the intervention group, "4.0 [I50=1.0] vs control group: 3.0 [I50=1.0]; P=.004". Median procedure time was nearly identical (4:15 vs 4:17 min).

> "Participants of the intervention group showed a significantly improved economy of time and motion (intervention group: 4.0 [I50=1.0] vs control group: 3.0 [I50=1.0]; P=.004)"

### Haubruck P 2018 (2)

Haubruck P, Nickel F, Ober J, Walker T, Bergdolt C, Friedrich M, Müller-Stich BP, Forchheim F, Fischer C, Schmidmaier G, Tanner MC. (2018). Evaluation of App-Based Serious Gaming as a Training Method in Teaching Chest Tube Insertion to Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.9956

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Amount-of-help OSATS step from the direct rating in the same RCT; the intervention group "needed significantly less help from the supervising surgeon" with P<.001. The authors link this to greater independency in performing the procedure.

> "needed significantly less help from the supervising surgeon (intervention group: 4.0 [I50=1.0] vs control group: 2.0 [I50=1.0]; P<.001)"
