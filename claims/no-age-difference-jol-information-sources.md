`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Multilevel modeling analysis of item relatedness and reported encoding-strategy use on JOLs in the cross-sectional paired-associate study; the article reports that "age did not interact with relatedness or strategy use in predicting JOLs."

> "Both relatedness and the use of effective encoding strategies positively influenced JOLs. Most important, age did not interact with relatedness or strategy use in predicting JOLs, indicating no age differences in the use of these two sources of information when making JOLs."
