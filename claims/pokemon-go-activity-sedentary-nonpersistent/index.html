Evidence¶

Kurt Squire 2023¶

Kurt Squire, Garrison Wells, Maria J. Anderson-Coto, and Constance Steinkuehler. 2023. Casual Games, Cognition, and Play across the Lifespan: A Critical Synthesis. ACM Games: Research and Practice, Volume 1, Issue 2, Article 14. https://doi.org/10.1145/3594534

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of 36 Pokémon Go studies with a combined sample of 38,724 participants reports that gameplay benefits sedentary individuals but "did not increase activity for already active players" and exercise did not persist after play ended. No effect size is printed.

"Thus, playingPokémon Gocan increase activity when it is used by otherwise sedentary people for the purposes of exercise, but it ought not replace more dedicated physical activity."
