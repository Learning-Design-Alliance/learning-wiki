Sara Coemans, Karin Hannes. (2017). Researchers Under the Spell of the Arts: Two Decades of Using Arts-Based Methods in Community-Based Inquiry with Vulnerable Populations. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.08.003

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of rationales across the 131 included studies. The review reports authors used artistic techniques to conduct research "with participants rather than on them", to articulate unique experiences, and to share knowledge beyond academic communities. Authors often gave multiple reasons but were less explicit about why a specific technique was chosen.

> "A frequently cited reason was that they were used to challenge the limitations of conventional language-oriented research methods"
