### Weatherton M. and Aikens M 2026

Weatherton M. and Aikens M. (2026). Resource Use in Introductory Biology: Evaluating Latent Patterns, Demographic Covariates, and Effects on Final Course Grade. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-09-0212

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-step DIF analysis (Vermunt and Magidson approach) testing direct effects of demographic covariates on each indicator; all 12 indicators exhibited significant direct effects from at least one covariate, with institution most extensive. Benjamini–Yekutieli correction applied.

> "The “institution” variable showed the most extensive DIF, with significant direct effects on all 12 indicator variables, suggesting that institutional context may shape how students perceive and report their engagement with different resources."
