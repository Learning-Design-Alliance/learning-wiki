## Evidence

### Chen Tianjiao 2023

Chen Tianjiao, Luo Heng, Feng Qinna, Li Gege. (2023). Effect of Technology Acceptance on Blended Learning Satisfaction: The Serial Mediation of Emotional Experience, Social Belonging, and Higher-Order Thinking. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054442

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression-path analysis of questionnaire data from 110 undergraduates after 11 weeks of blended learning, controlling for gender. Technology acceptance "significantly predicted ... learning satisfaction (B = 0.721, p < 0.001)"; Table 5 also reports a direct effect of 0.392 with a 95% CI of 0.172 to 0.587.

> "technology acceptance significantly predicted emotional experience (B = 0.520, p < 0.001), higher-order thinking (B = 0.258, p < 0.01), posts (B = 0.266, p < 0.01), and learning satisfaction (B = 0.721, p < 0.001)"
