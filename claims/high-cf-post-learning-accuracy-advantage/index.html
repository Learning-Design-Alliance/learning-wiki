Evidence¶

Feng X 2020¶

Feng X, Perceval GJ, Feng W and Feng C (2020) High Cognitive Flexibility Learners Perform Better in Probabilistic Rule Learning. Front. Psychol. 11:415. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00415

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Mixed-measures ANOVA on accuracy in the coin-searching task (2 flexibility groups × 4 probability pairs × 2 learning stages). The group difference in accuracy emerged "after rule acquisition only" (F(1,34) = 12.651, p < 0.05, η²p = 0.271).

"An analysis of simple eﬀects of high/low ﬂexibility group and learning stage on accuracy showed a signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the two groups after rule acquisition only [ F(1,34) = 12.651, p < 0.05, η2 p = 0.271]"

Feng X 2020 (2)¶

Feng X, Perceval GJ, Feng W and Feng C (2020) High Cognitive Flexibility Learners Perform Better in Probabilistic Rule Learning. Front. Psychol. 11:415. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00415

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

Post-learning-stage ANOVA on accuracy across the four probability pairs. A significant main effect of group (F(1,37) = 11.662, p < 0.05, η²p = 0.240) accompanied the probability-pair effect (η²p = 0.350).

"We observed a signiﬁcant main eﬀect of probability pair [ F(3,111) = 19.889, p < 0.001, η2 p = 0.350] and group [F(1,37) = 11.662, p < 0.05, η2 p = 0.240]."
