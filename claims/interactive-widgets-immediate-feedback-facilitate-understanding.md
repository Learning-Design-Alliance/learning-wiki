### Dou Du 2024

Dou Du, Taylor J. Baird, Kristjan Eimre, Sara Bonella, Giovanni Pizzi. (2024). Jupyter widgets and extensions for education and research in computational physics and chemistry. [doi:10.1016/j.cpc.2024.109353](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2024.109353)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article's design rationale for the reciprocal-space notebook, stated as an author assertion with no learning measurement reported. The authors claim that manipulating crystal parameters with immediate visual feedback 'translates relatively abstract concepts and mathematical relationships into interactive 3D visualizations that facilitate understanding'. No empirical evaluation of learning gains is presented.

> "Coupling these graphical components with a set of interactive elements (such as sliders, radio buttons, and buttons) enables users to manipulate the parameters of the crystal and obtain immediate visual feedback on the changes. This directly translates relatively abstract concepts and mathematical relationships into interactive 3D visualizations that facilitate understanding."
