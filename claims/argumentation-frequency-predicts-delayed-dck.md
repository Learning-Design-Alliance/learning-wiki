`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear regression on delayed content knowledge post-test with individual proportional frequency of argumentative utterances, content knowledge pre-test, and total time devoted to group work as predictors. "The proportional frequency of individual argumentation significantly predicted DCK post-delayed scores"; the model accounted for 62.7% of variance.
