Marion Händel & Anique B. H. de Bruin & Markus Dresel 2020¶

Marion Händel & Anique B. H. de Bruin & Markus Dresel (2020). Individual differences in local and global metacognitive judgments. Metacognition and Learning (2020) 15:51–75. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09220-0

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Study 2 hierarchical regression on global predictions one week before the psychology exam. The article reports the judgment "relied mostly on domain-specific self-concept" and that "Test performance itself had no predictive power for predictions."

"When students had not yet processed the task, their judgment relied mostly on domain-specific self-concept. Test performance itself had no predictive power for predictions."

Marion Händel & Anique B. H. de Bruin & Markus Dresel 2020 (2)¶

Marion Händel & Anique B. H. de Bruin & Markus Dresel (2020). Individual differences in local and global metacognitive judgments. Metacognition and Learning (2020) 15:51–75. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09220-0

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Study 2 general linear mixed model comparing regression coefficients between pre- and postdictions. The article reports "test performance was of higher relevance for postdictions than for predictions (p < .001)", partly confirming Hypothesis 3; direct comparisons of self-concept betas were not significant.

"Vice versa, however, test performance was of higher relevance for postdictions than for predictions (p < .001). That is, Hypothesis 3 could be partly confirmed."
