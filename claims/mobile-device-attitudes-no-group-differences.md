## Evidence

### Aziz Amaaz 2024

Aziz Amaaz, Abderrahman Mouradi, Moahamed Erradi, Ali Allouch. (2024). Moroccan pre-service elementary teachers: attitudes toward STEM education and mobile devices. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). https://doi.org/10.11591/ijere.v13i5.28205

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U test on the 226-student survey comparing male and female BEES students' mobile-device attitude scores; no significant difference found.

> "The test examining the relationship between the gender of BEES students and their attitudes toward the use of mobile devices in integrated STEM education revealed no statistically significant difference in attitudes between male and female BEES students (U=4442.5, Z= -0.484, p=0.628)."

### Aziz Amaaz 2024 (2)

Aziz Amaaz, Abderrahman Mouradi, Moahamed Erradi, Ali Allouch. (2024). Moroccan pre-service elementary teachers: attitudes toward STEM education and mobile devices. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE). https://doi.org/10.11591/ijere.v13i5.28205

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U test comparing scientific- and literary-baccalaureate BEES students' mobile-device attitudes; no significant difference. The authors attribute this to all students owning mobile devices and none using them in pre-university learning activities.

> "The Mann -Whitney test used to examine the relationship between students' pre -university studies and their attitudes toward the use of mobile devices in integrated STEM education revealed no significant difference in attitudes between students with a scientific baccalaureate and those with a literary baccalaureate (U=4256.5, Z= -0.650, p=0.516)."
