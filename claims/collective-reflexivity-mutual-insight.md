## Evidence

### Peter Kahn 2017

Peter Kahn, Lucy Everington, Kathleen Kelm, Iain Reid, Francine Watkins. (2017). Understanding student engagement in online learning environments: the role of reflexivity. Education Tech Research Dev, 65, 203-218. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9484-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview-based case study analysis of eight online postgraduate students. The shared goals pertained to the development of mutual insight or to the completion of a group project, characterised as collective reflexivity following Donati and Archer.

> "We identiﬁed 42 occasions when the sub-group students spoke of concerns that were speciﬁcally shared with others, and in which a shared goal was at stake."

### Peter Kahn 2017 (2)

Peter Kahn, Lucy Everington, Kathleen Kelm, Iain Reid, Francine Watkins. (2017). Understanding student engagement in online learning environments: the role of reflexivity. Education Tech Research Dev, 65, 203-218. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9484-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of interview transcripts from the eight-student sub-group identified social practices supporting mutual learning through discussion boards; every participant manifested at least one such practice.

> "We were able to identify 73 instances within the transcripts of speciﬁc social practices relevant to this mutual learning, with each of the participants manifesting at least one such practice."
