## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trust in teachers was not associated with dropout and did not moderate the relations of institutional trust and system justification with dropout. [→ Finkenauer 2023](#finkenauer-2023)

## Evidence

### Finkenauer 2023

Finkenauer, C., Boer, M., Spitzer, J., Weinberg, D., Visser, K., Jonker, M. & Stevens, G. W. J. M. (2023). Examining the role of civic attitudes in the link between family wealth and school dropout among tertiary vocational students. npj Science of Learning 8:38. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00189-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 3 of the vocational-student study added trust in teachers as main effect and moderator of both civic-attitude indicators; "their estimates were non-significant" and model fit did not improve. Trust in teachers did correlate negatively with dropout in bivariate analyses.

> "Adding trust in teachers as main effect and moderator of the eﬀects of both indicators of civic attitudes on dropout did not improve model ﬁt and their estimates were non-signiﬁcant (M3; see Supplementary Tables 1 and 2)."
