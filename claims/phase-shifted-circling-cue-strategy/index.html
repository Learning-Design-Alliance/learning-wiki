q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Preliminary observations on the 90° phase-shifted inward circling exercise without cues; the article explicitly labels the support as "Anecdotal evidence and unpublished pilot observations". No sample size, statistics, or effect size are printed.

"Anecdotal evidence and unpublished pilot observations by Mechsner suggest that most people are hardly able to maintain the relationship at all and, in any case, lose the pattern when speeding up."

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Preliminary observations with marks at 12 o'clock of the left circle and 9 o'clock of the right circle, with the instruction to pass both marks simultaneously; the article states the support is "Anecdotal evidence and unpublished pilot data". No statistics or effect size printed.

"Anecdotal evidence and unpublished pilot data suggest that people can perform the pattern even if they only imagine the marks."
