Evidence¶

Conroy 2018¶

Conroy, M. A., Sutherland, K. S., Algina, J., Werch, B., & Ladwig, C. (2018). Prevention and Treatment of Problem Behaviors in Young Children: Clinical Implications From a Randomized Controlled Trial of BEST in CLASS. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858417750376

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Logistic regression analysis of posttest status from a multisite cluster-randomized trial (230 treatment, 232 comparison children). The article reports the condition effect was "significant and negative" on all three C-TRF subscales, with SRD effect sizes of −.119, −.194, and −.173 for Externalizing, Internalizing, and Total Problems.

"For all three C-TRF subscales, the effect of condition was significant and negative, indicating that the BEST in CLASS intervention reduced the percentage of children in the clinical or borderline range."
