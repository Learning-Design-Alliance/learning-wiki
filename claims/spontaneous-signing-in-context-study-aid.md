## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sherman 2011

Sherman, J. (2011). Signing for Success: Using American Sign Language to Learn Sight Vocabulary. SRATE Journal, 20(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ959527

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-case qualitative observation from one intern's field notes: after initially needing the SEE-SAY-SIGN procedure modeled, the student performed it on her own when rereading a section. The intern theorized it worked "almost as if the sign were a study aid"; the article notes this practice was noted before by Hafer & Wilson (1986).

> "M. would spontaneously sign the vocabulary word when she would see it in context during thereading activity."
