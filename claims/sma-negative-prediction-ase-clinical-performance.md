`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Covariance-based SEM of 585 nursing interns (Table 5) showed the emerging model "exhibiting a direct but negative correlation with both ASE (β = −0.07, p = 0.030) and clinical performance (β = −0.04, p = 0.002)". Both standardized coefficients were small though statistically significant, supporting Hypothesis 1.

> "The emerging model illustrated that SMA has a direct and positive association with nomophobia (β = 0.12, p = 0.002), while exhibiting a direct but negative correlation with both ASE (β = −0.07, p = 0.030) and clinical performance (β = −0.04, p = 0.002)."
