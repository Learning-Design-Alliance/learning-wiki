T. Sima Paribakht and Marjorie Wesche. (1996). Vocabulary enhancement activities and reading for meaning in second language vocabulary acquisition. Second Language Vocabulary Acquisition: A Rationale for Pedagogy, Cambridge University Press. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139524643.013

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The chapter's introduction reviews prior second language research, citing Elley and Mangubhai (1983) and Krashen (1989), and states that "extensive reading programs are generally more effective than systematic vocabulary instruction using decontextualized exercises." No effect sizes are reported for this comparison.

> "Second language research on this issue is sparse, but what there is indicates that extensive reading programs are generally more effective than systematic vocabulary instruction using decontextualized exercises"
