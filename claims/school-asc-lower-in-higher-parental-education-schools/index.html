Horoz 2025¶

Horoz, N., van Atteveldt, N. M., Oude Groeniger, J., Koot, J. R., ..., & Buil, J. M. (2025). Household- and school-level parental education and academic self-concept development in elementary school. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00354-x

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel model at the school level (18 schools) showed school-level parental education significantly associated with school-level ASC (β = 0.863, p < 0.001); "school-level ASC scores were lower in higher parental education schools than in lower parental education schools".
