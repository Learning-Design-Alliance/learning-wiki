### Lehmann Ronny 2024

Lehmann Ronny, Klinke Petrowsky Michaela, Seitz Anke, Meyburg Jochen, Eppich Walter, Hoffmann Georg F., Tönshoff Burkhard, Huwendiek Sören. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated self-assessment study at a tertiary pediatric center: 55 medical and 48 nursing staff completed questionnaires before and after working through preparatory virtual patients. The article reports the profession-specific T2 pattern quoted, with no effect size printed.

> "Prior to this, after preparation with VPs at T2, only medical staff had a statistically significant increase in self-assessed knowledge and skills; nurses did not report significant improvement."
