Evidence¶

Keith Topping 1998¶

Keith Topping. (1998). Peer Assessment Between Students in Colleges and Universities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543068003249

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's summary of its writing-outcome section, covering descriptive and outcome studies of peer assessment of writing across many subjects and ESL contexts. It reports peer assessment "at least as good as teacher assessment and sometimes better".

"Peer assessment appears capable of yielding outcomes at least as good as teacher assessment and sometimes better. Formative feedback has been oral, written, and oral and written combined."

Keith Topping 1998 (2)¶

Keith Topping. (1998). Peer Assessment Between Students in Colleges and Universities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543068003249

q3 · i? · review · r3

Outcome study by Richer (1992) comparing peer group discussion of essays with teacher discussion and feedback; grading of 174 pretest and posttest essays from 87 first-year students indicated "greater gains in writing proficiency in the peer feedback group (p = .009)".

"Grading of 174 pretest and posttest essays from 87 first-year students indicated greater gains in writing proficiency in the peer feedback group (p = .009)."
