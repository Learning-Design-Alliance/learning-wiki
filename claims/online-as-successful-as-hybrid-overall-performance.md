### Aly 2013

Aly, I. (2013). Performance in an online introductory course in a hybrid classroom setting. Canadian Journal of Higher Education, 43(2), 85-99. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1013589

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of online (n = 104) and hybrid (n = 203) sections of an introductory managerial accounting course taught by the same instructor in the same semester, using four learning measures. The study found that "students receiving only online instruction were as successful as students receiving hybrid classroom instruction".
