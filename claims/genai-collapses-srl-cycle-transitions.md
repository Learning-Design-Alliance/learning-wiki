## Evidence

### H. Chad Lane 2026

H. Chad Lane. (2026). AIED's Unfinished Mission: Centering Agency and Motivation in the Age of Effortless Bypass. AIED 2026, CCIS 3032, pp. 502–516. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29791-4_38

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The paper reports, citing Fan et al.'s study, that the SRL cycle is vulnerable to GenAI: "collapsed transitions between orientation, planning, monitoring, and evaluation" occurred, with metacognitive activity restructuring around the tool. The paper calls this pattern metacognitive laziness.

> "students using ChatGPT showed collapsed transitions between orientation, planning, monitoring, and evaluation, with metacognitive activity restructuring around the tool r ather than around learning."
