### Su 2026

Su, Dongying; Meijuan, Gai; Aikebaier, Simayilijiang. (2026). Cognitive load and teachers' innovative behavior in AI-enhanced English language instruction: A mediation analysis of technological adaptability. PLOS One. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0343002](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0343002)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural model results from a cross-sectional survey of 600 English teachers analyzed with SEM and WLSMV estimation. The direct path from AI-assisted Teaching Cognitive Load to Innovative Teaching Behavior was significant: "β = −.134, SE = .037, CR = −3.635, p < .001".

> "The analysis confirmed a significant direct negative effect of cognitive load on innovative teaching behavior (β = −.134, SE = .037, CR = −3.635, p < .001), supporting H1."
