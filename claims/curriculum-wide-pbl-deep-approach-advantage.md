# Curriculum-wide PBL implementation has a more positive impact on the deep approach (ES = .18) than single-course implementation (ES = −.05)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Across 12 curriculum-wide versus 9 single-course PBL studies, curriculum-wide implementation shows a small positive effect on deep learning (ES = .18) while single-course implementation shows a near-zero negative effect (ES = −.05). [→ Dolmans Diana H. J. M. 2015](#dolmans-diana-h-j-m-2015)

## Evidence

### Dolmans Diana H. J. M. 2015

Dolmans Diana H. J. M., Loyens Sofie M. M., Marcq Hélène, Gijbels David. (2015). Deep and surface learning in problem-based learning: a review of the literature. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9645-6

`q2 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Vote-count comparison across implementation context in the review: for curriculum-wide PBL (n = 12 studies) seven showed an increase in deep learning; the single-course subgroup (n = 9) had effect sizes for deep (−0.05) and surface (.07) close to zero. Sign tests were not significant.

> "The effect size of .18 indicates a small effect of PBL in a curriculum wide implementation on students’ deep approach."
