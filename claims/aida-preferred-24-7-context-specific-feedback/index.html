Evidence¶

Rienties 2026¶

Rienties, Bart; Coughlan, Tim; Domingue, John and Herodotou, Christothea (2026). New systems of learning for distance learning institutions? A six-study review of implementing AIDA. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100607

q2 · i? · design · r3

Perception studies 1-3, in which 315 distance students reacted to prototypes and screenshots without using a real AIDA. The article reports that "roughly 60% of students across the three Studies indicated they would like to use an AIDA to support their learning."

"The most preferred reason for the 315 participants was that AIDA would be available 24/7 and would provide immediate feedback in the academic setting of the learner, rather than providing generic content such as ChatGPT"
