## Evidence

### Manuaba Ida Bagus Amertha Putra 2022

Manuaba Ida Bagus Amertha Putra, No Yi, Wu Chien-Chih. (2022). The effectiveness of problem based learning in improving critical thinking, problem-solving and self-directed learning in first-year medical students: A meta-analysis. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0277339

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Subgroup analysis of critical thinking/knowledge studies regrouped by intervention duration (≤ 6 months vs > 6 months) and countries (Asia vs. western), assessed with random- and fixed-effect models. Heterogeneity fell to I2 = 0% only for >6 months and Western subgroups; no subgroup showed a significant PBL difference.

> "The studies with the duration intervention at more than six months and learning method comparison in western countries subgroup were found to have low heterogeneity ((I2 = 0% (might not be important)). However, high heterogeneity scores were found in the studies with duration intervention less than six months (I2 = 96%) and learning method comparison in Asian countries subgroup (I2 = 95%)."
