## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Median GPA, MCAT, faculty-student ratio, NIH funds, NIH funds per faculty, residency director assessment, and peer assessment score each correlate significantly with Step 1 and Step 2 scores; total enrollment does not. [→ Ghaffari-Rafi Arash 2019](#ghaffari-rafi-arash-2019)

## Evidence

### Ghaffari-Rafi Arash 2019

Ghaffari-Rafi Arash, Lee Rachel Elizabeth, Fang Rui, Miles J. Douglas. (2019). Multivariable analysis of factors associated with USMLE scores across U.S. medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1605-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate analysis (Table 2) of the 100-school USN&WR dataset reports significant correlations for GPA, MCAT, faculty ratio, NIH funds, NIH funds per faculty, residency director and peer assessment with both Step scores; enrollment correlations were not significant (−0.10, P = 0.33; 0.02, P = 0.86).

> "There are statistically significant correlations between average Step 1 score and median GPA (corr = 0.55, P < .0001), median MCAT total score (corr = 0.72, P < .0001), full-time faculty-to-student ratio (corr = 0.47, P < .0001)"
