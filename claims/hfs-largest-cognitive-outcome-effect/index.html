Evidence¶

Kim Junghee 2016¶

Kim Junghee, Park Jin-Hwa, Shin Sujin. (2016). Effectiveness of simulation-based nursing education depending on fidelity: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0672-7

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Subgroup analysis of learning outcomes by fidelity level in the 40-study meta-analysis (Table 4). The cognitive effect size for HFS was ES = 0.50; the results section states it was "the highest for HFS (0.50), followed by LFS (0.47), SP (0.32), and MFS (0.06)"; the MFS cognitive estimate rested on a single effect size.

"For cognitive outcome, which is a sub-domain of learning, the effect size was the highest for HFS (0.50), followed by LFS (0.47), SP (0.32), and MFS (0.06)."
