## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Taylor Silas 2019

Taylor Silas, Haywood Matthew, Shulruf Boaz. (2019). Comparison of the effects of simulated patient clinical skill training and student roleplay on objective structured clinical examination performance among medical students in Australia. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2019.16.3

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Retrospective quasi-experimental comparison of two cohorts' OSCE data (2014 RP-only, N=182; 2016 SP-only, N=148), with estimated marginal means adjusted for age and admission scores. The "low-moderate effect size range (0.26–0.34)" held with statistical significance (P<0.01); Table 4 shows OSCE total EMM of 74.52 (SP) versus 72.75 (RP), d=0.32.

> "Further, univariate analysis of variance was performed to identify EMMs by cohort, while controlling for the impact of scores on the admission tools. The results show that the impact (Cohen’s d) was slightly decreased but remained within the low-moderate effect size range (0.26–0.34)"
