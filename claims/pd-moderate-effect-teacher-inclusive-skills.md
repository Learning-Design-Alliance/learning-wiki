# Professional development produces moderate improvements in teachers' skills for implementing inclusive education (g = 0.49)

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Professional development significantly improves in-service teachers' skills to implement inclusive education, with a moderate pooled effect of g = 0.49. [→ Johanna L. Donath 2023](#johanna-l-donath-2023)

## Evidence

### Johanna L. Donath 2023

Johanna L. Donath, Timo Lüke, Elisabeth Graf, Ulrich S. Tran, Thomas Götz. (2023). Does Professional Development Effectively Support the Implementation of Inclusive Education? A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09752-2

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Multi-level random-effects meta-analysis of 371 skills effect sizes from 153 studies. The pooled effect was "a moder ate effect ... ( g = 0.49 [0.41; 0.56])"; the three skills subcategories did not differ from each other.

> "A moder ate effect was observed on skills to implement inclusive education ( g = 0.49 [0.41; 0.56]) and on its subcategor ies (see Fig. 3). The subcategories (implementation quality, use of inclusive methods, self-efficacy for inclusive teaching) did not differ from each other"
