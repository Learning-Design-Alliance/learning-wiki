`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Criterion validity was assessed by regressing end-of-semester test scores on iRAT averages under multiple scopes using linear regression in two consecutive cohorts. R² was estimated at R²≈0.42 for Semester 1 of 2023, R²≈0.38 for Semester 2 of 2023, and R²≈0.41 for Semester 1 of 2024.

> "using all themes in Semester 1 of 2023 (Fig. 3F; R²≈0.42), Semester 2 of 2023 (Fig. 3G; R²≈0.38) and Semester 1 of 2024 (Fig. 3F; R²≈0.41)"

### Sheader Elizabeth 2026 (2)

Sheader Elizabeth, Donlan Lisa, Allen Connor, Betteridge Nathan, Dixon Michael, Gouldsborough Ingrid, Grady Ruth, Kingston Margaret, Kouskoff Valerie, Parris Kerry, Pocock Tristan, Raza Maiedha, Shore Paul, Stafford Nicholas, Tournier Cathy, Webb Michelle, Smith Michael P. `q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Early-warning utility was tested by restricting iRAT to Themes 1–4 and repeating regressions in two consecutive cohorts. iRAT performance by Theme 4 already correlated strongly with end-of-semester test outcomes in both 2023 (R² ≈ 0.34) and 2024 (R² ≈ 0.38).

> "Restricting the analysis to the first four themes of Semester 1 showed that iRAT performance by Theme 4 already correlated strongly with end-of-semester test outcomes in both 2023 (R² ≈ 0.34) and 2024 (R² ≈ 0.38)"
