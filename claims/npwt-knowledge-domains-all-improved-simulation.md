## Evidence

### Tseng Yi-Ling 2025

Tseng Yi-Ling, Wu Hua-Shan, Huang Pei-Yu, Hsaio Pei-Shan, Tseng Hui-Chen. (2025). Effects of a Situated Simulation Teaching Strategy on Knowledge of Negative-Pressure Wound Therapy among Surgical Nurses in Taiwan. Advances in Skin & Wound Care. https://doi.org/10.1097/ASW.0000000000000293

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests in the same one-group pretest/posttest study (N = 31) showed significant improvement in all four domains; the article prints z statistics and P values but no effect sizes.

> "Significant postintervention changes were also noted in the participants' scores on the following items: NPWT indications and effects (z = −3.84, P < .001), NPWT device operation (z = −3.71, P < .001), patient assessment and care (z = −3.89, P < .001), and anomaly response (z = −3.93, P < .001)."
