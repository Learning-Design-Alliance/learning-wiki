### Rebecca D. Silverman 2025

Rebecca D. Silverman, Kristin Keane, Elena Hammond, S. Khanna. (2025). The Effects of Educational Technology Interventions on Literacy in Elementary School: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research, 95(5), 972-1012. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241261073

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

The meta-analysis conducted moderator analyses over its study corpus, testing whether effects "differ according to characteristics of the educational interventions or participants"; the abstract reports that effects do differ by particular characteristics.

> "We also conducted analyses to determine whether effects of educational technology interventions differ according to characteristics of the educational interventions or participants in the studies in our corpus."
