# On the water science unit, student preference for grasping information interacted with cognitive sequence of instruction in predicting pretest-to-posttest change scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` For the water science unit, the interaction between preference for grasping experience and cognitive sequence was statistically significant with a large effect size (ηp2 = 0.40), leading the authors to reject the null hypothesis of no interaction. [→ Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield 2017](#kasee-l-smith-john-rayfield-2017)

## Evidence

### Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield 2017

Kasee L. Smith & John Rayfield. (2017). A Quasi-Experimental Examination: Cognitive Sequencing of Instruction Using Experiential Learning Theory for STEM Concepts in Agricultural Education. Journal of Agricultural Education, 58(4), 175-191. https://doi.org/10.5032/jae.2017.04175

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus univariate ANOVA on change scores for the water science unit, conducted after MANOVA assumptions were violated, with Bonferroni-adjusted alpha p < 0.02 and n = 121 students. The article reports the interaction as statistically significant with "a large effect size ηp2 ≥ 0.14" and high power.

> "These findings were superseded by the finding of a single statistically significant (F(2,115) = 38.19, p = 0.01, ηp2 = 0.40) interaction involving both preference for grasping experience and cognitive sequence."
