## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In separate multiple linear regression models for each of eight domains (academic, adaptive, cognitive, executive function, language, motor, play, social), both treatment intensity and duration significantly predicted mastered learning objectives. [→ Linstead E 2017](#linstead-e-2017)

## Evidence

### Linstead E 2017

Linstead E, Dixon DR, Hong E, Burns CO, French R, Novack MN, Granpeesheh D. (2017). An evaluation of the effects of intensity and duration on outcomes across treatment domains for children with autism spectrum disorder. Translational Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/tp.2017.207

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational analysis of the Skills database covering 1468 children with ASD (ages 18 months to 12 years, M=7.57 years) receiving individualized ABA; separate multiple linear regressions per domain. The article reports that "All eight domains demonstrated a strong linear relationship" with both predictors, and each model was significant, p<0.001.

> "All eight domains demonstrated a strong linear relationship between skill acquisition and both treatment intensity and duration. The results of the regression models for each domain are shown in Table 2."
