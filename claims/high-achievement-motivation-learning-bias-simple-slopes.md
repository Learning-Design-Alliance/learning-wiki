## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants high (+1 SD) in achievement motivation more readily acquired a conditioned response to goal-relevant than goal-irrelevant stimuli (η2p = 0.104), while those lower (−1 SD) showed no significant difference. [→ Yoann Stussi 2019](#yoann-stussi-2019)

## Evidence

### Yoann Stussi 2019

Yoann Stussi, Aude Ferrero, Gilles Pourtois and David Sander. (2019). Achievement motivation modulates Pavlovian aversive conditioning to goal-relevant stimuli. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0043-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Simple-slopes analyses of early-acquisition conditioned responses in 72 participants. High (+1 SD) achievers showed "more readily acquired a CR to goal-relevant stimuli" with printed effect size η2p = 0.104; low (−1 SD) achievers showed no significant difference, F(1, 70) = 0.13, p = 0.717, η2p = 0.002.

> "participants with high achievement motivation ( +1 SD) more readily acquired a CR to goal-relevant stimuli ( M = 0.15) than to goal-irrelevant stimuli (M = −0.02), F(1, 70) = 8.11, p = 0.006, η2p = 0.104"
