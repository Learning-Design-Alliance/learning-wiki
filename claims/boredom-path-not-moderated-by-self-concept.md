## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The social support × self-concept interaction did not significantly predict foreign language boredom (B = −0.023, t = −1.437); this is no evidence of moderation in a cross-sectional survey, not a demonstration that the association is the same at every self-concept level. ## Evidence

### Chen Shengdong 2025

Chen, S., Xu, H., Jin, C., Yin, K., Wang, X., & Wang, T. (2025). Social support and english academic achievement: the mediating role of foreign language emotions and the moderating effect of academic self-concept. BMC Psychology, 13, 1286. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03617-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null moderation result from the same moderated mediation analysis. The authors speculate that academic self-concept may buffer high-arousal emotions but not low-arousal boredom; the interaction term B = −0.023, t = −1.437 was not significant.

> "regardless of English academic self-concept levels, the effect of social support on foreign language boredom remained stable."
