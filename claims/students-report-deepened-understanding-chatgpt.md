### Manuel G. Forero and H. J. Herrera-Suárez 2023

Manuel G. Forero and H. J. Herrera-Suárez. (2023). ChatGPT in the Classroom: Boon or Bane for Physics Students' Academic Performance?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02422](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02422)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey question on whether interacting with ChatGPT deepened understanding of physics concepts; combining "A Lot" (19.5 %) and "To Some Extent" (41.5 %) gives 61 %, while the article reports 29.3 % neutral and 9.8 % little or no effect.

> "By combining the responses from the "A Lot" (19.5 %) and "To Some Extent" (41.5 %) categories, it is observed that a significant 61 % of students believe that interacting with ChatGPT has been pivotal in enhancing their grasp of physics concepts"

### Manuel G. Forero and H. J. Herrera-Suárez 2023 (2)

Manuel G. Forero and H. J. Herrera-Suárez. (2023). ChatGPT in the Classroom: Boon or Bane for Physics Students' Academic Performance?. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02422](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02422)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

> "a signiﬁcant majority (70.8 %) of students are either ”Very Satisﬁed” (17.1 %) or ”Satisﬁed” (53.7 %) with ChatGPT’s abil- ity to meet their specialized needs in physics."
