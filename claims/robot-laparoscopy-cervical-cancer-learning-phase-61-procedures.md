### Baeten IGT 2021

Baeten IGT, Hoogendam JP, Schreuder HWR, Jürgenliemk-Schulz IM, Verheijen RHM, Zweemer RP, Gerestein CG. (2021). The influence of learning curve of robot-assisted laparoscopy on oncological outcomes in early-stage cervical cancer: an observational cohort study. BJOG 2021;128:563–571. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.16399

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

RA-CUSUM analysis of 165 consecutive robot-assisted procedures at one tertiary centre, with surgical failure defined as cervical cancer recurrence. The chart "shows a peak at 61 procedures", defining the learning phase; sensitivity analyses with different risk models all showed an identical learning phase of 61 procedures.

> "The RA-CUSUM+ chart shows a peak at 61 procedures, after which a consistent decrease is observed and the RA-CUSUM+ chart moves towards zero, indicating satisfactory results with respect to the predicted recurrence rates."
