Evidence¶

Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton 2024¶

Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton. (2024). English learners learn from worked example comparison in algebra. Instructional Science (2024) 52:831–858. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09668-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Multiple regression on Unit 2 scores, same wait-list crossover design with Teacher 2's classes as treatment. The article reports "0.498 (t(77) = 2.91, p = 0.01) standard deviations higher on the calculation component" and 0.730 on explanation.

"In addition, ELs who received the intervention scored 0.498 (t(77) = 2.91, p = 0.01) standard deviations higher on the calculation component and 0.730 (t(77) = 4.74, p < 0.001) standard deviations higher in the explanation component"
