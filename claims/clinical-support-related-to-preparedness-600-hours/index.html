Evidence¶

Patrick 2023¶

Patrick, S. K., Darling-Hammond, L., & Kini, T. (2023). Educating teachers in California: What matters for teacher preparedness? Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/956.678

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Regression models of completer survey responses accounting for completer and institutional characteristics and self-reported clinical experiences. The report states that completers "who reported more communication, observation, and feedback about teaching" were more likely to rate programs effective, and employers also rated such institutions more highly.

"completers from all clinical pathways who reported more communication, observation, and feedback about teaching from their preparation programs were more likely to rate their preparation programs as effective or very effective."

Patrick 2023 (2)¶

Patrick, S. K., Darling-Hammond, L., & Kini, T. (2023). Educating teachers in California: What matters for teacher preparedness? Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/956.678

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of self-reported student teaching hours from the completer survey. The report notes "43% of completers in preservice programs reported less than 600 hours of student teaching," and the survey lacks comparable questions for interns, who constitute 29% of respondents.

"Although the CTC requires at least 600 hours of supervised clinical practice in all programs, 43% of completers in preservice programs reported less than 600 hours of student teaching."
