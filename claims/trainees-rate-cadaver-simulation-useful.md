### Mason Nena Lundgreen 2025

Mason Nena Lundgreen, Cohen Edan, Ganga Kiran, Zapata Isain, Hawke Aaron, Peck Meredith. (2025). Building ultrasound-guided procedure skills in regional anesthesiology: simulation training with a formalin-embalmed cadaver model. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-025-03577-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-training survey of the 19 participants using a 5-point Likert scale developed specifically for this study; 89.5% chose options 4 or 5. Self-report only; no effect size printed.

> "Results from the post-training survey showed that 89.5% of participants thought that this type of cadaveric simulation training was "extremely useful" or "quite useful" (options 4 and 5 on the 5-point Likert scale) to their personal ability to perform US-guided procedures on patients in future."
