### Hollenberg Jacob 2019

Hollenberg Jacob, Claesson Andreas, Ringh Mattias, Nordberg Per, Hasselqvist-Ax Ingela, Nord Anette. (2019). Effects of native language on CPR skills and willingness to intervene in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest after film-based basic life support training: a subgroup analysis of a randomised trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025531

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported confidence questionnaire administered directly after training and at 6 months to the same student cohort; table 4 shows non-significant p-values (e.g. 0.152, 0.328, 0.873). The article reports "no significant differences"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
