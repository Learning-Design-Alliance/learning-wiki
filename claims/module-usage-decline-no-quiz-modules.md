## Evidence

### Herbert C 2017

Herbert C, Velan GM, Pryor WM, Kumar RK. (2017). A model for the use of blended learning in large group teaching sessions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1057-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational SCORM usage data across 29 modules for 264 students. The first module was attempted 1472 times; the final 16 modules averaged "704.3 ± 61.2" attempts. Four modules (14, 19, 23, 24) without review quizzes averaged only 391.5 ± 33.3 attempts.

> "Usage of modules released in the 2nd half of the semester was noticeably lower with an average of 704.3 ± 61.2 (mean ± SEM) attempts for each of the final 16 modules."
