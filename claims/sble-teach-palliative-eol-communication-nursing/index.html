Systematic review (PROSPERO-registered) of 30 articles meeting inclusion criteria requiring a nursing role, technology, palliative/EOL communication focus, and an evaluative component. The review concludes that "Simulation-based learning experience are being used to teach palliative and end-of-life communication skills to nursing students and clinicians."

Related Claims¶

  • Only a minority of palliative/EOL communication SBLEs included other interprofessional team members, and half of those involved only actors in those roles

  • Pre-post evaluations in reviewed SBLE studies report significant gains in EOL knowledge and attitudes, but not uniformly across studies — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • A simulation-based group showed stronger emotional and clinical preparedness and knowledge-to-practice conversion than a seminar-based comparison group — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
