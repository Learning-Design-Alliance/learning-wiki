## Evidence

### Brender 2025

Brender, J., El-Hamamsy, L., Uittenhove, K., Mondada, F., Bumbacher, E. (2025). Structured Prompts, Better Outcomes? Exploring the Effects of a Structured Interface with ChatGPT in a Graduate Robotics Course. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-03870-8_7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-03870-8_7)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multiple regression on session 3 data with learning as dependent variable; the model accounts for a small portion of the variance (adj R2 = .041, p = .075). The authors call the pattern exploratory and say additional data is required.

> "We found a marginally significant positive effect of proportion of student-generated input on learning gain, β = 0.43, p= .075, 95% CI [–0.05, 0.91]"

### Brender 2025 (2)

Brender, J., El-Hamamsy, L., Uittenhove, K., Mondada, F., Bumbacher, E. (2025). Structured Prompts, Better Outcomes? Exploring the Effects of a Structured Interface with ChatGPT in a Graduate Robotics Course. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-03870-8_7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-03870-8_7)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

T-test comparing conditions on annotated proportions of personally written prompt text (session 2: 71 ± 57 self-written vs 457 ± 564 copy-pasted characters on average). No difference in session 3 when all used the normal interface.

> "We found that students using the structured interface in session 2 had a significantly higher average of self-written text in prompts, t(56) = 2 .07, p = .048."
