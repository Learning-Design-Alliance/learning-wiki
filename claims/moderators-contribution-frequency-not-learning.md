# In the main study, NCC, metacognitive strategies, and intrinsic motivation moderated contribution frequency but showed no significant conditional effects on learning gains

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Conditional-effect models showed significant moderation of contribution frequency by metacognitive strategies (p = .028, R² = .13) and intrinsic motivation (p = .026, R² = .29), with NCC marginal (p = .060, R² = .10); no standardized effect size for the moderation is printed. [→ Heimbuch 2018](#heimbuch-2018)
`q2 i?` For learning gains, no Johnson-Neyman regions of significance were found for any of the three moderators. [→ Heimbuch 2018 (2)](#heimbuch-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Heimbuch 2018

Heimbuch, S., Ollesch, L., & Bodemer, D. (2018). Comparing effects of two collaboration scripts on learning activities for wiki-based environments. University of Duisburg-Essen. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9283-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main-study moderation regressions (PROCESS macro, Johnson-Neyman technique, Table 8): NCC F(3,65) = 2.60, p = .060, R² = .10; metacognitive strategies F(3,65) = 3.23, p = .028, R² = .13; intrinsic motivation F(3,65) = 3.29, p = .026, R² = .29. DDR encouraged contributions from students with high NCC or less pronounced metacognitive strategies; low-motivation DDR students contributed almost as rarely as BRD students.

> "Students with a high Need for Cognitive Closure contributed more to the wiki when they were in the DDR script group (Figure 10, left). For students with a low NCC the overall contribution frequency was not affected by the wiki script group they used."

### Heimbuch 2018 (2)

Heimbuch, S., Ollesch, L., & Bodemer, D. (2018). Comparing effects of two collaboration scripts on learning activities for wiki-based environments. University of Duisburg-Essen. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9283-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Main-study moderation models on learning gains (Table 9) were non-significant: NCC F(3,65) = 0.87, p = .460; metacognitive strategies F(3,65) = 0.61, p = .610; intrinsic motivation F(3,65) = 0.10, p = .960. Simple slopes show only minor, low-precision differences.

> "For all analyses of the three influe ncing variables on learning gains from the pre - to the post-test, the Johnson-Neyman floodlight analysis did not find any regions of significance."
