## Evidence

### Daker 2023

Daker, R.J., Gattas, S.U., Necka, E.A., Green, A.E. & Lyons, I.M. (2023). Does anxiety explain why math-anxious people underperform in math? npj Science of Learning, 8:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00156-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderation analysis of math anxiety-performance link by baseline HF-HRV, controlling for trait anxiety and word performance. Anticipatory HF-HRV (β = 0.052, p = 0.579) and task HF-HRV (β = 0.061, p = 0.515) similarly failed to moderate. Equivalence was not tested.

> "this prediction was not supported by the data: the relation between math anxiety and math performance did not significantly depend on HF-HRV levels ( β = −0.032, t(73) = −0.400, p = 0.691; trait anxiety and word performance were included in the model as covariates)."
