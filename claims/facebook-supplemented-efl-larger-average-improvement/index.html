Evidence¶

Shams 2014¶

Shaila Shams. (2014). Efficacy of Online Social Networks on Language Teaching: A Bangladeshi Perspective. The IAFOR Journal of Education, Volume II - Issue II - Summer 2014. https://iafor.org/journal

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of two English 101 sections (Control n=30, Experiment n=27) taught by the same instructor, tested with matched diagnostic Start and End tests. The article reports the Experiment group's improvement "stood at 12.82%" versus 6.28% for the Control group; no significance test or effect size is printed.

"This meant that the average improvement i n the Control group was 6.28% while that of the Experiment group stood at 12.82% which was almost double."
