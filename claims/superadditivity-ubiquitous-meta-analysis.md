## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the meta-analysis, the proportion of data points above the independence baseline increases with lag, and mean performance at longer lags exceeds predicted levels. [→ Benjamin 2010](#benjamin-2010)

## Evidence

### Benjamin 2010

Benjamin, Aaron S., & Tullis, Jonathan. (2010). What makes distributed practice effective? http://www.psych.uiuc.edu/~asbenjam/SuperadditivityReferences.htm

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 829 conditions from 72 articles on spacing with explicit memory tests; 735 conditions grouped into five lag ranges. Mean performance exceeded the independence baseline at the two longer lag intervals, with t [134] = 3.76 and t [171] = 6.36.

> "Similarly, mean levels of actual performance lie above the predicted levels for those latter two lag intervals, as shown in Figure 2 (t [134] = 3.76; t [171] = 6.36)."
