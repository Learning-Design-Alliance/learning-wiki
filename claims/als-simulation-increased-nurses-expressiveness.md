### Pérez-Fuentes María del Carmen 2026

Pérez-Fuentes María del Carmen, Molero Jurado María del Mar, Romanos-Rodríguez Armando, Martos Martínez África, Gómez-Gómez Francisco J., Aguado-Campos Javier. (2026). `q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples Wilcoxon signed-rank analysis of the nurses' subsample (n = 55), pre- versus post-intervention (Table 5). Expressiveness mean rose from 10.43 to 11.05; the article reports 26 of 55 nurses obtained better expressiveness scores after training.

> "a significant improvement in Expressiveness scores is observed, with a medium effect size (rrb = 0.35) after participation in the training programme, compared to the scores obtained in the pre-test"
