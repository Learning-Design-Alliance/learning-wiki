# Latent learner profiles did not moderate the effectiveness of the adaptive ITS

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A five-profile latent profile solution based on prior knowledge, conscientiousness, interest, and academic self-concept produced no significant condition × profile interactions on posttest performance, mental effort, subjective difficulty, or enjoyment. [→ Sibley 2026](#sibley-2026)

## Evidence

### Sibley 2026

Sibley, L., Berner, T., & Schmalfeldt, T. (2026). Effectiveness of adaptive versus non-adaptive intelligent tutoring systems in early primary mathematics. Computers and Education Open 11. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100420

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory latent profile analysis (BIC-supported five-profile solution) followed by regression of posttest performance on condition and profile in the N = 132 experiment. The article reports the printed β, p, and η²p values; no standardized effect size for the interactions is printed, so impact is null.

> "The main effect of condition was not significant (β = −0.22, p = .511, η²p = .00), and no significant interaction effects between condition and profile were observed (ps ≥ .198, η²p = .02), indicating that the profiles did not moderate the effectiveness of the adaptive ITS system."
