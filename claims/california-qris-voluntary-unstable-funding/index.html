Report's quality-improvement funding analysis finds QRIS supported through time-limited categorical grants rolled out unevenly across the state (Table 3), limiting which providers can access QRIS and undermining consistent county support for providers.

"Quality improvement funding for ECE has been administered through time-limited categorical grants. Categorical funding impacts which providers have access to QRIS. Unstable and uncoordinated state funding makes it difficult for counties to plan for the future of QRIS efforts and to provide consistent support for providers."
