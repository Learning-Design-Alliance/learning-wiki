Yijun Liu 2026¶

Yijun Liu, Yifan Song, John Gallagher, Sarah Sterman, Tal August. (2026). FOXGLOVE: Understanding Goal-Oriented and Anchored Writing Feedback from Experts and LLMs on Argumentative Essays. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06271 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.06271

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of the FOXGLOVE dataset (§3.5) comparing goal distributions of 558 instructor sentence-level comments and 1,368 model sentence-level comments. The article reports "Claimsreceived the most feedback from both groups (39.96% instruc- tor, 40.79% model)", suggesting convergent attention to higher-level argumentative components.

"Humans and models dis- tributed feedback across the five argumentative goals in similar proportions.Claimsreceived the most feedback from both groups (39.96% instruc- tor, 40.79% model), followed byPosition(21.68%) for instructor andEvidence(20.47%) for models."
