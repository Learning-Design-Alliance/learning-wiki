## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski. (2017). Improvement or selection? A longitudinal analysis of students' views about experimental physics in their lab courses. arXiv:1706.03681. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.023101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.023101)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Matched longitudinal comparison of three populations (N = 106, 61, 63) against general populations in each lower-level course (Table IV). Differences are "statistically significant (Mann-Whitney U p < 0.05) in all cases except one" — the preinstruction PHYS 2150 score for the 1140-to-2150 population.

> "the students for whom we have longitudinal data start and end the lower level course with higher E-CLASS scores than the general population in that course."
