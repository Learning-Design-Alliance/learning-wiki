Ophthalmic Simulated Surgical Competency Assessment Rubric for manual small-incision cataract surgery. Journal of Cataract and Refractive Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcrs.2019.04.010

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Construct validity analysis comparing masked Sim-OSSCAR grades of 4 novice trainee surgeons and 4 experienced ophthalmologists (more than 100 SICS procedures) performing on the SICS artificial eye. Novice mean was 1.7 ± 1.0 versus 31.0 ± 2.7 for competent surgeons.
