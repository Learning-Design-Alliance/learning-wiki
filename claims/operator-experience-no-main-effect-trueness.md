# Operator experience shows no significant main effect on full-arch scanning trueness

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a model comparing experienced operators and inexperienced dental students, operator experience did not reach statistical significance for full-arch trueness. [→ Chen Yuanyuan 2026](#chen-yuanyuan-2026)

## Evidence

### Chen Yuanyuan 2026

Chen Yuanyuan, Dong Yiyun, Tang Yiqun, Wu Yiwen, Chen Tiannan, Tao Jiang, Zhao Junjun. (2026). Impact of dentition alignment, scanning strategy and operator experience on the accuracy and time of intraoral scanning: an in vitro study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-09114-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In vitro repeated-measures experiment with three experienced operators and three dental students without prior scanning experience; the generalized linear mixed model found "operator experience did not reach statistical significance" for trueness (F = 1.68, P = 0.196, Table 2). No equivalence test was reported.

> "the generalized linear mixed model revealed significant main effects of scanning strategy (P < 0.001, Table 2) and dentition alignment on trueness (P < 0.001, Table 2), while operator experience did not reach statistical significance."
