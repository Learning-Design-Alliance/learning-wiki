## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox, Benjamin M. Zwickl, Robert D. Hobbs, John M. Aiken, Nathan M. Welch, and H. J. Lewandowski (2016). An alternative model for administration and analysis of research-based assessments. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010139](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010139)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phone interviews (15-30 minutes) with nine instructors who had received E-CLASS reports in previous semesters: 'All nine interviewees made positive comments about the E-CLASS and/or the ﬁnal report', and visual representations of student scores and shifts were noted as facilitating interpretation.

> "Interviews were conducted by one of the authors and typ- ically lasted between 15-30 minutes. All nine interviewees made positive comments about the E-CLASS and/or the ﬁnal report."

### Bethany R. Wilcox 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox, Benjamin M. Zwickl, Robert D. Hobbs, John M. Aiken, Nathan M. Welch, and H. J. Lewandowski (2016). An alternative model for administration and analysis of research-based assessments. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010139](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.010139)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Theme from the same instructor interviews: 'the perceived value of having signiﬁcant and appropriate national data with which to compare individual course results', which instructors said helped them determine what questions and trends to pay attention to when interpreting performance.

> "A signiﬁcant theme from these interviews was related to the perceived value of having signiﬁcant and appropriate national data with which to compare individual course results."
