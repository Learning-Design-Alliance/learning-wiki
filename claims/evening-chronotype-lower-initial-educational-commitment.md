## Evidence

### Bacaro 2025

Bacaro, V., De Lise, F., Natale, V., Tonetti, L., & Crocetti, E. (2025). Night Owls and Early Birds: The Role of Adolescents' Chronotype on Educational Identity Trajectories. International Journal of Psychology, 60(5), e70108. https://doi.org/10.1002/ijop.70108

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent growth curve multigroup analysis of 1134 Italian adolescents across four assessments over one year, comparing intercepts of educational commitment across three chronotype groups. The evening group's initial commitment was significantly lower than both other groups ("significantly lower in adolescents with an evening chronotype"). No effect size was printed for this contrast.

> "the initial levels of commitment were significantly lower in adolescents with an evening chronotype compared to those with an intermediate (Wald = 14.547, p < 0.01) and a morning one (Wald = 16.740, p < 0.001)"
