## Evidence

### Horisberger Karoline MD 2022

Horisberger Karoline MD, Di Natale Samuela MD, Gutschow Christian A. MD, Clavien Pierre-Alain MD, PhD. (2022). Towards a Virtual “Global Academia of Surgeons”. Annals of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/SLA.0000000000005642

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey findings on didactic format preferences among the 708 professional online users. The article reports 69% wanting learning goals and end-of-video questions, and 44% finding "3 to 4 questions per video appropriate"; 23% preferred 5 or more questions.

> "More than two thirds of the respondents (69%) would appreciate the designation of learning goals at the beginning as well as corresponding questions at the end of the video. 44% would find 3 to 4 questions per video appropriate."
