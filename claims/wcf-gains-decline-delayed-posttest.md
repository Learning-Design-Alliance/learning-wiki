## Evidence

### Al-Hazzani and Altalhab 2018

Nahla Al-Hazzani, Sultan Altalhab. (2018). Can Explicit Written Corrective Feedback Develop Grammatical and Lexical Accuracy of Saudi EFL Learners? International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.6n.4p.16

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired Sample t-test within the experimental group (n=29) comparing post-test and delayed post-test overall writing scores; the article reports t=2.256, p=0.028, favoring the post-test. The authors say this "reflected the inefficiency of corrective feedback for helping the female students retain their acquired writing skills".

> "As shown in Table 2, there are statistically significant differences between the female students’ scores in the experimental group for both post and post-delayed tests. These were in favor of the students’ post-test scores with an average of (7.83) compared to (6.55) for the students’ post-de - layed tests."
