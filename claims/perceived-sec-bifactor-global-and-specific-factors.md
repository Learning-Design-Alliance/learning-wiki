## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A bifactor ESEM with a global perceived-SEC factor and five specific factors (assertiveness, tolerance, social regulation, emotion regulation, emotional awareness) fit better than CFA and ESEM alternatives. [→ Collie 2022](#collie-2022)

## Evidence

### Collie 2022

Collie, R.J. (2022). Perceived social-emotional competence: A multidimensional examination and links with social-emotional motivation and behaviors. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101656

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Confirmatory factor analyses of student self-reports from 414 Australian secondary students compared CFA, ESEM, and bifactor ESEM specifications. The article reports the bifactor ESEM "yielded better fit than the ESEM" with a well-defined global factor and relatively well-defined specific factors.

> "The bifactor ESEM yielded better fit than the ESEM, it had a well-defined global-factor (|λ| = .44-.78; M = .63), and relatively well-defined specific-factors (|λ| = .23-.66; M = .38)."
