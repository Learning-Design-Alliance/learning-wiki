The report's magnet schools section synthesizes multiple studies of magnet outcomes across several states, listing positive effects across achievement, attainment, and relational measures. A related study of neighborhood-to-magnet conversions found racial and income composition often remained the same.

"A number of studies of magnet schools in California, Connecticut, and elsewhere have found positive effects on student achievement, graduation rates, student motivation and satisfaction with school, intergroup relationships, teacher motivation and morale, and parent satisfaction with the school."
