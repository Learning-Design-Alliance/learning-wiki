Effectiveness of game-based learning in mental health nursing education on self-directed learning and academic self-efficacy among nursing students. `q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Between-group independent t-tests in the final analytical sample (n = 40; 20 per group) compared the game-based intervention group with the conventionally taught control group; the printed posttest contrast showed "P = 0.037, Cohen’s d = 0.68" for self-directed learning.

> "Independent t-tests at posttest revealed significantly higher scores in the intervention group compared to the control group for both self-directed learning (154.75 ± 19.34 vs. 141.90 ± 18.28; t (38) = 2.16, P = 0.037, Cohen’s d = 0.68)"
