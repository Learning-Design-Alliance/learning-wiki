Among the motivational constructs examined in the Flemish seventh-grade sample, "academic self-concept proved to relate most strongly to achievement," with the other named constructs each explaining a unique portion of variance. No per-construct effect sizes are printed in the available text.

> "In particular, academic self-concept proved to relate most strongly to achievement, while achievement goals, achievement motivation, autonomous motivation, and effort beliefs additionally explained a unique portion of the variance in academic achievement."
