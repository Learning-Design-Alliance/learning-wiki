## Evidence

### Yamada 2025

Yamada, C., Itaguchi, Y., & Rodríguez-Aranda, C. (2025). Unconscious cultural cognitive biases in explicit processes of visuomotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00335-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

In the cross-cultural experiment, a two-way mixed ANOVA on the implicit learning measure (angular error minus explicit aim) showed growth across the adaptation block, "ηp 2 = 0 . 5 4" (ηp² = 0.54), in both cultural groups.

> "the implicit learning measures developed through the adaptation block (Fig. 2a, b) and were larger in the late period than in the early period (F(1, 46) = 54.48, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0 . 5 4 )"

### Yamada 2025 (2)

Yamada, C., Itaguchi, Y., & Rodríguez-Aranda, C. (2025). Unconscious cultural cognitive biases in explicit processes of visuomotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00335-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The same ANOVA on the implicit learning measure found "no main effect of the cultural group" (ηp² = 0.04) and a nonsignificant learning-phase × cultural-group interaction, indicating comparable implicit estimates across the Norwegian and Japanese groups.

> "There was no main effect of the cultural group ( F(1, 46) = 1.71, p = 0.20, ηp 2 = 0.04) and the interaction between the learning phase and the cultural group was not signiﬁcant"

### Yamada 2025 (3)

Yamada, C., Itaguchi, Y., & Rodríguez-Aranda, C. (2025). Unconscious cultural cognitive biases in explicit processes of visuomotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00335-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A two-way mixed ANOVA on aftereffects and residual aftereffects in the terminal-feedback and washout blocks found that "these measures did not differ signiﬁcantly between groups" (aftereffects ηp² = 0.02; residual aftereffects ηp² = 0.01); the block × culture interactions were also not significant.

> "The results showed that these measures did not differ signiﬁcantly between groups (for the aftereffects, F(1, 46) = 1.07, p = 0.31, ηp 2 = 0.02; for the residual aftereffects, F(1, 46) = 0.33, p = 0.57, ηp 2 = 0.01)"

### Yamada 2025 (4)

Yamada, C., Itaguchi, Y., & Rodríguez-Aranda, C. (2025). Unconscious cultural cognitive biases in explicit processes of visuomotor adaptation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00335-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The same ANOVA showed "larger in the terminal-feedback block than that in the washout block" aftereffects for both cultural groups (ηp² = 0.52), while residual aftereffects did not differ significantly between the two blocks (ηp² = 0.02).

> "The aftereffects were larger in the terminal-feedback block than that in the washout block for both groups ( F(1, 46) = 50.44, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.52)"
