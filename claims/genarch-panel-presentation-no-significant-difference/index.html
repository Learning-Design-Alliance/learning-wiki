q2 · i? · causal · r1

A blinded seven-member panel (two professors, five senior students) rated recorded team presentations on a 1–5 rubric. Overall median was 3.45 [2.89, 3.92] for GenARch and 3.87 [3.63, 3.88] for Control, with greater variability in the GenARch condition; comparisons are descriptive given few team products and low-to-moderate rater agreement.

"Across the seven team presentations, the GenARch teams showed lower median scores than the Control teams on all five criteria (Fig. 4a), but the between-condition differences were not statistically significant."
