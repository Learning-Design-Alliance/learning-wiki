## Evidence

### Susan Verbunt-Janssen 2026

Susan Verbunt-Janssen, Carolien A. N. Knoop-van Campen, Martine Baars, Inge Molenaar. (2026). Teacher dashboard design for self-regulated learning: a participatory approach. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10632-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

KANO questionnaire analysis of 45 teachers: 12 of 18 interface items were Indifferent; the remaining six, including saving settings, progress per lesson and by domain, quick overview, and easy access with few clicks, were One-dimensional or Attractive.

> "The highest satisfaction rate was ascribed to 'offering a quick overview of student status' (TS 0.90, DS -0.61), highlighting its significance as the most crucial interface feature according to teachers."

### Susan Verbunt-Janssen 2026 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

> "Teachers exhibit a more pronounced inclination towards interface features, as the presence or absence of specific features demonstrates a greater impact on satisfaction compared to metacognitive indicators."
