`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of CTC credential data and California Department of Education hire estimates (Figure 4). The report also states new credentials "have remained constant at 11,500 since 2013–14, while projected annual new hires have grown and now exceed 20,000." Enrollment figures come from Figure 2: 77,705 in 2001–02 versus 20,881 in 2014–15.

> "Between 2013–14 and 2015–16, California preliminary credentials issued by the Commission on Teacher Credentialing stagnated at about 11,500 each year (see Figure 4). Meanwhile, district-estimated teacher hires continued to grow, exceeding 22,300 estimated hires in 2016–17."
