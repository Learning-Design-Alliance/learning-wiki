### AlFaris Eiad 2018

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

External validation study in which 84 volunteer medical students completed the SSI and ASSIST simultaneously. The correlation r = 0.511 (p<0.0001) supports convergent validity; respondents took longer on ASSIST and raised clarification queries about some ASSIST items.

> "The result of the external validation showed a significant positive correlation (r = 0.511, p<0.0001) between the scores of SSI and ASSIST inventories."
