## Evidence

### Kim Eunyoung 2020

Kim Eunyoung, Kim Sang Suk, Kim Sunghee. (2020). Effects of Infection Control Education for Nursing Students Using Standardized Patients vs. Peer Role-Play. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18010107

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subdomain analysis of the 15-item researcher-developed performance tool (scored 0–30) found "a statistically significant difference between the two" in four of the five assessed subdomains, all favoring the SP group (Table 5). No effect sizes were printed.

> "We found a statistically significant difference between the two in the subdomains of hand hygiene (z = −2.453, p = 0.017), PPE (z = −3.614, p = 0.001), respiratory etiquette (z = −5.032, p < 0.001), and worker safety (z = −4.005, p < 0.001) (Table 5)."

### Kim Eunyoung 2020 (2)
