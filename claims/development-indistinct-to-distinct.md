### De Sanctis 1911

De Sanctis, S. (1911). Mental development and the measurement of the level of intelligence. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 2, 498-507. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069835

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument about developmental direction. The author rejects the free-will account of development, holding that as incessant stimuli determine the nervous system, so incessant experience determines the psychic development of the infant and the child.

> "Thus one may say that mental development proceeds from the indistinct to the distinct, from the passive to the active, or, as it is usually called, will."
