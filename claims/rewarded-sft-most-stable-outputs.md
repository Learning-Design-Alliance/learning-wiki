# Rewarded SFT produces the most stable outputs among the SFT designs, nearly free of hallucinations and story-irrelevant material

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Qian Shen 2026

Qian Shen, Fanghua Cao, Min Yao, Shlok Gilda, Bonnie J. Dorr, Walter L. Leite. (2026). Children's English Reading Story Generation via Supervised Fine-Tuning of Compact LLMs with Controllable Difficulty and Safety. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13709

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational stability analysis in the Results section of the 1,290 stories generated per model per experiment; Rewarded SFT outputs were "almost entirely free of hallucinations", and other experiments stayed below 10% undesirable outputs.

> "the stories generated by LLMs fine-tuned with Rewarded SFT exhibited high stability, being almost entirely free of hallucinations, garbled text, story-irrelevant outputs, or null values. In the other experiments, while the stories from fine-tuned LLMs did contain a small portion of these undesirable outputs, the proportion was less than 10% of the 1,290 stories generated per model in each experiment."
