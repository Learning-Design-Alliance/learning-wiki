## Evidence

### Poorhassan M 2026

Poorhassan M, Hooshmandja M, Karimian Z, Seifali E. (2026). Enhancing histology education: Iranian medical students’ experience with eHistoLab web-application virtual microscopy. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09829-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental two-group pre-test/post-test study of 100 medical, dentistry, and pharmacy students randomly assigned to eHistoLab plus recorded lectures or e-lecturing alone. The between-group post-test comparison showed "significantly higher" scores with "Cohen’s d = 1.31".

> "However, post-test scores in the eHistoLab web application group were significantly higher than those in the e-Lecturing group (P < 0.001). The effect size for the between-group post-test comparison was large (Cohen’s d = 1.31)."
