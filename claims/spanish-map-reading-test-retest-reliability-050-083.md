Reliability analysis of pilot data from the Fall 2018, Winter 2019, and Spring 2019 administrations of the Spanish MAP Growth Reading pilot. The report states "Test-retest reliability coefficients range from 0.50 at Grade K to 0.83 at Grade 5," with no coefficient for Grades 6–8.

> "Test-retest reliability coefficients range from 0.50 at Grade K to 0.83 at Grade 5. There is no test-retest reliability for Grades 6–8 because only one test administration was taken by these grades."
