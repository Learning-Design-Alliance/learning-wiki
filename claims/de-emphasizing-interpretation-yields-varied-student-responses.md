## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a Copenhagen/Agnostic course, student responses on the double-slit experiment were more varied than in other courses, with 30% preferring a Realist interpretation and nearly half preferring a Realist stance on atomic electrons. [→ Baily 2010](#baily-2010)

## Evidence

### Baily 2010

Baily, C., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2010). Interpretation in Quantum Physics as Hidden Curriculum. AIP Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3515251](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-instruction survey data from Instructor C's Copenhagen/Agnostic course. Students were not only likely to prefer an Agnostic stance but "a significant number of students (30%) preferred a Realist interpretation" of the double-slit experiment.

> "a significant number of students (30%) preferred a Realist interpretation – more than with the Matter-Wave courses, but less so than with the Realist/Statistical course"
