### Sackstein S 2015

Sackstein S, Spark L & Jenkins A. (2015). Are e-books effective tools for learning? Reading speed and comprehension: iPad vs. paper. South African Journal of Education, 35(4), Art. #1202. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v35n4a1202

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the quasi-experimental within-subjects study, School B students (n = 35) read significantly faster on iPad than paper (t = 2.076, p = .046). Authors suggest prior iPad exposure from a school pilot project may explain this.

> "The students at School B read from the iPad statistically significantly faster (t = 2.076, p = .046) than they did from paper."
