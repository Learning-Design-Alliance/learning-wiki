### New Publications 1921

New Publications. (1921). New Publications. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

`q1 · i?` · `review · r?`

A review of Coursault's The Principles of Education (1920), described as based primarily on theoretical writings in education rather than scientific investigations, with the influence of Dewey, Royce, MacVannel, Bagley and McMurry said to appear clearly. The reviewer describes the harmonizing aim quoted here.

> "The author attempts to harmonize the views of man as a psycho-physical organism in a process of adjustment through stimuli and responses with the tleeological view of man as n person controlled by purposes and ideas."

### New Publications 1921 (2)

The same review reports the book's emphasis: "the appreciation of values and the development of purposes as compared to the importance of information is stressed." The reviewer adds that the author analyzes the processes of thinking in a fashion similar to Dewey.

> "The importance in the educative process of the appreciation of values and the development of purposes as compared to the importance of information is stressed."
