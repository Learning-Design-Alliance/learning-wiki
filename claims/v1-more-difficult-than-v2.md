## Evidence

### Min Xia 2024

Min Xia, Zhu Su, Wei-Bing Deng, Xiu-Mei Feng, Ben-Wei Zhang. (2024). How to quantify an examination? Evidence from physics examinations via complex networks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of integrated KPNs for National Volume 1 versus Volume 2 (15 papers each). V1 exceeded V2 on most indicators and on Fd; annual Fd comparisons showed V1 more difficult in 11 of 15 years, with the gap increasing after 2013.

> "Most network indicators, including the number of nodes, number of edges, average degree, network density, network transitivity and average clustering coefficient, are larger for the V1 KPN than for the V2 KPN."
