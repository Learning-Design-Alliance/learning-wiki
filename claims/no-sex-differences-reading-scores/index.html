Independent-samples t-test comparing female (mean 26.50, SD 14.97) and male (mean 23.25, SD 14.22) learners in the cognitive group; Table 9 prints t = .609, df = 28, p = .547. No effect size is printed.

"Based on results obtained of independent t-test evaluation, significant level of test is p=0.54 and it is above 0.05, so null hypothesis is approved and alternative hypothesis is rejected. At last, there are not significant differences in the reading scores of male and female learners in cognitive group."
