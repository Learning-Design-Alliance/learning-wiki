## Evidence

### Maya Usher 2025

Maya Usher, Ido Roll, Orly Fuhrman, Ofra Amir. (2025). Supporting Coordination and Peer Editing in Students’ Online Collaborative Writing Processes. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00450-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Peer editing outcomes extracted from the same 62-student lab experiment's screen recordings. Words added to paragraphs written by peers: "was doubled" in the experimental group, with r = .41.

> "the average number of words added by the experimental group students was doubled (M = 72.97, SD = 46.34) compared with the control group (M = 36.40, SD = 29.51), with statistical significance: U(32,30) = 252, Z = -3.20, p =.00, r =.41."

### Maya Usher 2025 (2)

Maya Usher, Ido Roll, Orly Fuhrman, Ofra Amir. (2025). Supporting Coordination and Peer Editing in Students’ Online Collaborative Writing Processes. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00450-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

Same outcome analysis: words deleted from peers' paragraphs were "five times greater among the experimental group", with r = .47.

> "the average number of words deleted from paragraphs that their peers wrote was five times greater among the experimental group (M = 37.72, SD = 45.52) compared with the control group ( M = 7.20, SD = 9.36), with statistical significance: U(32,30) = 223, Z = -3.68, p <.001, r =.47."
