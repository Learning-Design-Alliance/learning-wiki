Subgroup estimates from the same moderator analysis of evaluation types in the multilevel meta-analysis, reporting smaller but significant negative effects for behavioral (g = −0.45), personality (g = −0.46), and other evaluation categories.

> "Effects of labeling on behavioral evaluations (g = −0.45), on personality evaluations (g = −0.46), and on other evaluations (g = −0.37) were less negative and differed significantly from zero, too (Table 1 and Fig. 2)."
