# GenAI feedback literacy mediates the association between GenAI use and critical thinking, with near-complete mediation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` The indirect effect of GenAI use on critical thinking via feedback literacy was significant (β = 0.185, 95% CI [0.134, 0.254]; VAF = 71.98%), and the direct effect became non-significant. [→ Yan Z 2026](#yan-z-2026)

## Evidence

### Yan Z 2026

Yan Z, Fang Z and Zou W (2026). The association between generative AI use and university students' critical thinking: a moderated mediation model of GenAI feedback literacy and reflective thinking. Front. Psychol. 17:1939286. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1939286

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation test (5,000 bias-corrected resamples) in the PLS-SEM model of 421 undergraduates. The indirect effect was β = 0.185 (bootstrap SD = 0.031, p < 0.001), CI [0.134, 0.254]; the direct effect dropped to β = 0.072, p = 0.227, "a pattern consistent with near-complete mediation".

> "The variance accounted for (V AF) was 71.98% (0.185/0.257), indicating that nearly three-quarters of the total association between GenAI use and critical thinking is statistically accounted for by feedback literacy, a pattern consistent with near-complete mediation (Hair et al., 2014)."
