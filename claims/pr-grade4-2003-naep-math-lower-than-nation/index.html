Baxter 2007¶

U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Statistics, Washington, D.C. http://nationsreportcard.gov/puertorico_2005/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Large-scale representative NAEP assessment of Puerto Rico grade 4 public school students in 2003, reported as average scale scores. The report states Puerto Rico students scored "179, compared with 234 for the nation," and notes scores were 42 points lower than national low-income students and 55 points lower than all national students.

"On average, in 2003, students at grade 4 in Puerto Rico scored lower on the mathematics assessment than students in the nation. The average scale score for students in Puerto Rico was 179, compared with 234 for the nation."

Related Claims¶

  • Only small percentages of Puerto Rico students performed at or above Basic in NAEP mathematics: 9 percent of fourth-graders and 4 percent of eighth-graders in 2003, versus 76 and 67 percent nationally. — related
  • In 2005, public school students in Puerto Rico scored lower on average in NAEP mathematics than students in the nation at both grades (grade 4: 183 vs. 237; grade 8: 218 vs. 278). — related
  • In 2003, eighth-grade public school students in Puerto Rico averaged 212 in NAEP mathematics, 64 points lower than the national average of 276. — related
  • Puerto Rico–nation score gaps in NAEP mathematics were largest in the measurement content area (80 points at grade 8 in 2003; 76 points in 2005), with smaller gaps in geometry. — related
  • A trial Spanish-language NAEP mathematics assessment was administered to Puerto Rico public school students in grades 4 and 8 in 2003 — related
  • Gender differences within Puerto Rico were absent overall but appeared in single content areas: females scored higher in geometry and spatial sense at grade 4 in 2003 (5 points) and 2005 (4 points), and in data analysis and probability at grade 8 in 2005 (8 points). — related
  • A special validity analysis was conducted on the Puerto Rico trial assessment data — related
