### L. Gajewski 2016

L. Gajewski, J. Chołoniewski, J. A. Hołyst. (2016). Key courses of academic curriculum uncovered by data mining of students' grades. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.12693/aphyspola.129.1071](https://doi.org/10.12693/aphyspola.129.1071)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Analysis of final grades of 217 applied-physics students in 27 core courses at Warsaw University of Technology; the authors report a "slight negative linear correlation" between course means and variances, plotted in Fig. 6a with a linear fit.

> "A slight negative linear correlation (R2 = 0 .37) has been found between mean and variance of course grades (Fig. 6a), i.e. courses with higher mean scores tend to display lower scores variances."
