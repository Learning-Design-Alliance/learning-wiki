Todd Rosenkranz 2002¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of yearly reading and math gain scores by grade cohort, 1994-2001, reported in the Trends in Learning Gains section, Figure 4, and Table E. The brief treats rising gains as increasing productivity and stagnant gains as stagnant or declining productivity.

"While the gain scores indicate that CPS made improvements in stu- dent learning throughout much of the 1990s, the im- provements stalled after 1997. There is no evidence of any significant productivity growth in elementary schools since that time."
