### Balziano Snir 2026

Balziano Snir, Nesher Gilad, Ben Nun Nitzan, Segev Tomer, Freidlander Alon, Prat Dan. (2026). Resident autonomy in orthopedic trauma: independent femoral IMN by senior residents yields comparable outcomes to fellowship-trained surgeons. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-026-06460-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective cohort analysis at a level I trauma center with 1:1 propensity score matching (75 residents vs. 75 attendings). The study reports "Overall surgical complications occurred in 9/75 (12.0%) resident-led and 15/75 (20.0%) attending-led cases" with no statistically significant difference. Minor complications were 3/75 (4.0%) in each group.

> "Overall surgical complications occurred in 9/75 (12.0%) resident-led and 15/75 (20.0%) attending-led cases (p = 0.265). Major complications were observed in 6/75 (8.0%) residents and 11/75 (14.7%) attendings (p = 0.303)"
