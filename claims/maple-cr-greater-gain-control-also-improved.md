### Zhao Zhengqi 2026

Scaffolded AI-Supported Problem-Based Learning for Medical Interns: Exploratory Retrospectively Registered Randomized Controlled Evaluation With a Voluntary Feasibility Follow-Up. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of score gain between the two assigned groups: the intervention group "demonstrated a greater score gain" with median difference 6.60 points (95% CI 1.60-14.95); the wide CI indicates uncertain magnitude.

> "Consistently, the intervention group demonstrated a greater score gain (posttest minus pretest) than the control group (Mann-Whitney U test, P=.01), with a between-group median difference of 6.60 (95% CI 1.60‐14.95) points."

### Zhao Zhengqi 2026 (2)

Zhao Zhengqi, Wu Baijing, Tian Shuyuan, Hu Haigen, Hao Pengyi. (2026). Scaffolded AI-Supported Problem-Based Learning for Medical Interns: Exploratory Retrospectively Registered Randomized Controlled Evaluation With a Voluntary Feasibility Follow-Up. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/95039

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the self-study control group, Wilcoxon signed-rank testing showed the "control group also improved from pretest to posttest" with median gain +6.70 points, consistent with measurable learning under self-study.

> "Within-group analyses indicated that the control group also improved from pretest to posttest (Wilcoxon signed-rank test, P=.006), with a median gain of +6.70 (95% CI 0.10-10.05) points."
