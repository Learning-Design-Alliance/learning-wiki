## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Four significant serial pathways ran from growth mindset through each achievement goal to grit to mathematics achievement, with indirect effects from 0.0013 to 0.0034. [→ Dong Jia 2026](#dong-jia-2026)

## Evidence

### Dong Jia 2026

Dong Jia, Yang Xiqing, Yang Xi, Tan Dingliang, Wang Kexin. (2026). The relationship between growth mindset and mathematics achievement in Chinese middle school students: The role of achievement goals and grit. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05153-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap chain mediation analysis (PROCESS Model 80, 5000 resamples) in the 691-student sample found all four goal-to-grit-to-achievement serial pathways significant, supporting the sequential motivational model.

> "significant indirect serial pathways were observed for the following chains: through mastery-approach goals and grit (indirect effect = 0.0033, 95% Bootstrap CI [0.0007, 0.0410]), through mastery-avoidance goals and grit (indirect effect = 0.0013, 95% Bootstrap CI [0.0000, 0.0040]), through performance-approach goals and grit (indirect effect = 0.0023, 95% Bootstrap CI [0.0001, 0.0058]), and through performance-avoidance goals and grit (indirect effect = 0.0034, 95% Bootstrap CI [0.0001, 0.0083])."
