## Evidence

### Grant Shannan M. 2011

Grant Shannan M., Wolever Thomas M. S. (2011). Perceived Barriers to Application of Glycaemic Index: Valid Concerns or Lost in Translation? Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu3030330

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Burani and Longo (2006), an assessment of low-GI MNT in adults with type 1 and 2 DM (n = 21) one year after education: average dietary GI was 45 post-intervention, a statistically significant decrease was achieved by 95% of participants, and 85% reported adequate understanding of GI.

> "Perhaps most important to note, 100% of participants reported planning to include low-GI as a permanent lifestyle change; indicative of low-GI diet acceptability and sustainability in this sample."
