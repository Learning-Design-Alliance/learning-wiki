## Evidence

### Xu Bu 2025

Xu Bu, Xing Xiaoli, Zhang Guangyun, Zhang Wenting, Zhang Min, Gan Yan, Wei Tao, Xia Xiaodan. (2025). Effect of the situation-background-assessment-recommendation model combined with the mini-CEX assessment on the clinical communication, clinical thinking and comprehensive clinical abilities of nursing interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08022-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison on the Clinical Thinking Ability Teaching Assessment Scale (24 items, three dimensions, Cronbach's α = 0.90) in the RCT. Table 3 prints total 84.60 ± 8.56 vs. 77.31 ± 6.57, t = −9.613, p = 0.000, with dimension t values −5.401, −8.332 and −6.757. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Furthermore, the three dimensions of critical thinking, systematic thinking and evidence-based thinking related to clinical thinking ability were noticeably improved in the observation group compared with the control group (p < 0.001)."
