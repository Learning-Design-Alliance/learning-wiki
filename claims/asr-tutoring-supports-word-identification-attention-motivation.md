## Evidence

### MAP Reading Fluency with Coach Evidence Base 2024

MAP Reading Fluency with Coach Evidence Base. (2024). NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-reading-fluency-with-coach-evidence-base/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The white paper attributes to Mostow & Aist (1999) the finding that ASR technologies support word identification by listening to miscues and giving immediate feedback, including hints for words a child "misreads, or is likely to misread based on previous error patterns."

> "Technologies using automated speech recognition software "listen" to the student's miscue and provide immediate feedback by speaking (or giving a hint for) a word that the child gets stuck on, clicks on for help, misreads, or is likely to misread based on previous error patterns."

### MAP Reading Fluency with Coach Evidence Base 2024 (2)

MAP Reading Fluency with Coach Evidence Base. (2024). NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-reading-fluency-with-coach-evidence-base/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The paper reports, citing Mostow & Aist (1999), that ASR detects disfluent reading and provides scaffolded supports so students can "reread the sentence more fluently," freeing cognitive resources for comprehension.

> "The technology using the automated speech recognition software is able to detect the disfluent reading and provide appropriate scaffolded supports. These supports allow students to reread the sentence more fluently, thus freeing up the students' cognitive load to attend to the meaning of the text."

### MAP Reading Fluency with Coach Evidence Base 2024 (3)

MAP Reading Fluency with Coach Evidence Base. (2024). NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-reading-fluency-with-coach-evidence-base/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The paper reports, citing Mostow & Aist (1999), a motivational benefit: ASR gives striving readers an "attentive, perceptive, and responsive audience without judgment," a safe environment for oral reading practice. No effect sizes are printed for any of the three areas.

> "the usage of the automated speech recognition software allows the students to have an attentive, perceptive, and responsive audience without judgment, thus providing a safe environment for students to practice and improve their oral reading."
