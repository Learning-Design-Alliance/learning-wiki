Evidence¶

Wolf L.K. 2015¶

Wolf L.K., Bazargani N., Kilford E.J., Dumontheil I., Blakemore S.-J. (2015). The audience effect in adolescence depends on who's looking over your shoulder. Journal of Adolescence, 43, 5–14. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adolescence.2015.05.003

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Follow-up one-way ANOVAs within the younger adolescent group (n = 24) splitting by task-level. The low-relational social condition effect was significant (ηp2 = 0.17) with friend-versus-experimenter contrast r = 0.63; the friend-versus-alone contrast was marginal (p = 0.061, r = 0.38).

"The effect of social condition in high-relational trials in younger adolescents was not significant (p = 0.106), however there was a significant effect of social condition on low-relational trials (F(2,46) = 4.66, p = 0.014, ηp2= 0.17). This was due to a lower accuracy in the friend-present condition relative to the experimenter-present condition (t(23) = 3.89, p = 0.001, r = 0.63)"
