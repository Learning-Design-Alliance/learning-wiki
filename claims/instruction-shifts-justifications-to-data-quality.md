## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The distribution of justification categories differs significantly between pre- and post-test with a small effect size (w = 0.19), with data-quality justifications rising to 67% and personal justifications falling to 22%. [→ Micol Alemani 2026](#micol-alemani-2026)

## Evidence

### Micol Alemani 2026

Micol Alemani, Karel Kok, Eva Philippaki. (2026). Students’ reasoning in choosing measurement instruments in an introductory physics laboratory course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.16638](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.16638)

`q3 · i1` · `qualitative · r3`

Coding of 910 pre-test and 636 post-test justification codes across all items: before instruction pers. was 47% and data 36%; after instruction "students mainly use justifications that fall into the category data (67 %)" while pers. fell to 22%; the printed effect size is w = 0.19 (χ2(3) = 153 .41, p < 0.001).

> "The difference in the codes distributions per-category in the pre- and post-test is found to be statistically significant [χ2(3) = 153 .41, p < 0.001] and with a small size effect (w = 0.19)."
