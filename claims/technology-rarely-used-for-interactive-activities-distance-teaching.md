## Evidence

### Fütterer 2023

Fütterer, T., Hoch, E., Lachner, A., Scheiter, K., & Stürmer, K. (2023). High-quality digital distance teaching during COVID-19 school closures: Does familiarity with technology matter? Computers & Education, 199, 104788. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104788

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of student-reported learning activities across 14 technology types (Figs. 3 and 5). The study reports that "technologies in mathematics were rarely used to implement interactive learning activities" and that simulations were used for passive or active rather than constructive activities.

> "technologies in mathematics were rarely used to implement interactive learning activities (e.g., discussing with others, developing something together). For instance, simulations were instead used for initiating passive (e.g., watching) or active (e.g., taking notes) learning activities, even though they presumably are particularly suitable instructing constructive learning activities"
