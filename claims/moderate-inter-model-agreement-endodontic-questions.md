## Evidence

### Adıgüzel Mehmet A 2026

Adıgüzel Mehmet A. (2026). Accuracy, Self-Reported Confidence, and Overconfidence of Large Language Models in Endodontics: An Evaluation Using National Specialty Examination Questions. Medical Science Monitor. https://doi.org/10.12659/MSM.954052

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory agreement analysis based on correctness patterns (not answer-option selections) for each of the 123 questions across the three models; confidence intervals reported but no formal significance testing performed.

> "Agreement analysis showed moderate agreement between ChatGPT and Gemini (κ = 0.51, 95% CI 0.26–0.76), ChatGPT and Claude (κ = 0.44, 95% CI 0.25–0.63), and Claude and Gemini (κ = 0.41, 95% CI 0.21–0.60). Overall inter-model agreement was moderate (Fleiss' κ = 0.44), suggesting that the models did not consistently identify the same questions correctly"
