## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yedigöz-Kara 2022

Yedigöz-Kara, Z., & Bümen, N. T. (2022). The search for an effective curricular change adoption in foreign language education: A meta-synthesis. International Journal of Curriculum and Instructional Studies, 12(2), 337-366. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1373082

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Teachers had difficulty understanding how to assess students according to CLT and interpreted the curricular change by themselves, indicating their lack of information about the curriculum resulted from the lack of guidance.

> "When teachers did not know how to implement curricular change, they might feel nervous and question the curricular change as stated in Dincer and Koç (2020, p.33)."

### Yedigöz-Kara 2022 (2)

Yedigöz-Kara, Z., & Bümen, N. T. (2022). The search for an effective curricular change adoption in foreign language education: A meta-synthesis. International Journal of Curriculum and Instructional Studies, 12(2), 337-366. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1373082

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Teacher trainers were not qualified enough in terms of CLT and multiculturalism, mixed-ability classes, and the number of professional development activities was inadequate.

> "All the training programmes advocate student-centred communicative approaches, but ironically the teacher trainers use didactic methods themselves."
