Wijnen-Meijer M, ten Cate O, van der Schaaf M, Burgers C, Borleffs J, Harendza S. (2015). Vertically integrated medical education and the readiness for practice of graduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0514-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Physician EPA supervision ratings in the UHTRUST assessment of 30 VI and 29 non-VI near-graduates showed the German non-VI group judged more capable at "breaking bad news" (Mann-Whitney r = .37).

> "For "breaking bad news" the German group (M = 3.10; SD = .54) scored higher than the Dutch group (M = 2.62; SD = .70; Mann-Whitney U = 617; z = 2.85; p = .00, r = .37)."
