Fan Chen 2025¶

Fan Chen, Gaowei Chen. (2025). Learning analytics in inquiry-based learning: a systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10507-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review trend analysis (RQ1) of 51 included studies screened via PRISMA from six databases. The review reports the field "peaking in 2019 with seven publications" and a stable rate of at least three studies annually afterwards.

"The earliest instance of LA applied to IBL appeared in a 2012 conference paper. Since then, the number of studies has grown steadily, peaking in 2019 with seven publications"
