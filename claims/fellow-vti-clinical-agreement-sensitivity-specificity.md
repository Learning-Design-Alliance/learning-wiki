## Evidence

### Talan Jordan 2024

Talan Jordan, Mangalick Keshav, Pradhan Deepak, Sauthoff Harald. (2024). Accuracy of Echocardiographic Cardiac Output Assessment by Critical Care Fellows. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2024-0030OC

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Categorical agreement analysis of the 35 fellow-versus-cardiologist VTI comparisons, with VTI transformed into normal (18–22 cm) and abnormal categories. The study reports "clinical agreement in 80% of cases" and specificity of 43%, which the authors call poor.

> "When VTI was clinically stratified into “normal” or “abnormal,” there was clinical agreement in 80% of cases with a Cohen’s κ of 0.379 (Table 2). The sensitivity for a fellow to detect an abnormal VTI was 91%, with a specificity of 43%."
