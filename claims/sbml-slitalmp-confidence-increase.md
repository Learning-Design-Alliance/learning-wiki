# Self-reported confidence in performing and teaching the slit-lamp exam increased significantly after the SBML curriculum and remained elevated at two months

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Confidence in performing a comprehensive slit-lamp exam rose from 20% of EPs pre-curriculum to 86.7% post-curriculum, with significant increases from Time 0 to Time 2 and Time 0 to Time 3 (P < 0.001). [→ Hamou Samara BA 2024](#hamou-samara-ba-2024)
`q2 i?` Confidence in teaching residents the slit-lamp exam also increased significantly from Time 0 to Time 2 and Time 0 to Time 3 (P < 0.001 and P = 0.004). [→ Hamou Samara BA 2024 (2)](#hamou-samara-ba-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Hamou Samara BA 2024

Hamou Samara BA, Ghiaee Shayan MD MS, Chung Christine MD, Lloyd Maureen MD, Khem Kelly MD, Chi Zhang Xiao MD MS. (2024). Emergency Department Slit Lamp Interdisciplinary Training Via Longitudinal Assessment in Medical Practice. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.18514

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Likert-scale survey of the 15 enrolled EPs at three time points (Time 0, 2, 3), analyzed with Bonferroni-adjusted Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. "86.7% of EPs reported feeling confident" post-curriculum versus 20% at baseline; at two months 73% expressed extreme confidence in performing the exam.

> "After completing the slit-lamp curriculum (Time 2), 86.7% of EPs reported feeling confident performing a comprehensive slit-lamp exam for ocular complaints, and 73.3% were more confident in teaching residents how to perform a slit-lamp exam."

### Hamou Samara BA 2024 (2)

Hamou Samara BA, Ghiaee Shayan MD MS, Chung Christine MD, Lloyd Maureen MD, Khem Kelly MD, Chi Zhang Xiao MD MS. (2024). Emergency Department Slit Lamp Interdisciplinary Training Via Longitudinal Assessment in Medical Practice. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.18514

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey analysis across the three study timeframes (Table 3) using Bonferroni-adjusted Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on paired Likert responses. "statistically significant increase in self-reported confidence" was found for both performing and teaching the exam; the teaching comparison Time 0 to Time 3 showed P = 0.004.

> "There was a statistically significant increase in self-reported confidence in 1) performing a comprehensive slit lamp exam and 2) teaching residents to perform this exam between Time 0 to Time 2 and Time 0 to Time 3 (P < 0.001)."
