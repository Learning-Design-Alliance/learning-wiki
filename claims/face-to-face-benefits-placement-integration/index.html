Bower K 2026 (2)¶

Bower K, Scrivener K, Larkins S, Seaton C, Carlisle K. (2026). Technology-based teaching to support health students’ clinical skills in stroke recovery: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08709-7

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing the included studies, that the online tool showed less value in placement than classroom use; the review offers the educators' lack of integration as a possible explanation, not a tested one.

"An online clinical learning tool, ‘Physiotherapy eSkills Training Online’, demonstrated less value in a clinical placement versus a classroom environment, possibly because clinical placement educators did not intentionally integrate the resource into the learning approach [46, 59]."
