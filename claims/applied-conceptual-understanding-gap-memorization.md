## Evidence

### Á. Suárez 2022

Á. Suárez, D. Baccino, M. Monteiro, and A. C. Martí. (2022). Differences in the attitudes and beliefs about science of students in the physics-mathematics and life sciences areas and their impact on teaching. Revista Mexicana de Física E 19 020207. https://doi.org/10.31349/RevMexFisE.19.020207

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Statement-level CLASS results (Table III): for statement 8, 80% of life-science students agree they locate an equation and plug in values, versus 39% of physical-science students; Table II reports Applied Conceptual Understanding effect size 0.8, p=0.0004.

> "In statement 8, a large majority of the LS students (80%) agree with the methodology described for trying to solve physics problems, dropping to 39% in the other group of students."

### Á. Suárez 2022 (2)

Á. Suárez, D. Baccino, M. Monteiro, and A. C. Martí. (2022). Differences in the attitudes and beliefs about science of students in the physics-mathematics and life sciences areas and their impact on teaching. Revista Mexicana de Física E 19 020207. https://doi.org/10.31349/RevMexFisE.19.020207

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Statement-level response percentages (Table III) for the memorization statement, classified by area of study: 41% LS agree versus 26% PhS agree, supporting the memorization-oriented beliefs pattern in the same category.

> "In statement 1, 41% of the LS students agree with the statement regarding the importance of memorizing all the information in physics, while the percentage drops to 26% among PhS students."
