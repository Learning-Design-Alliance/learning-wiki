## Evidence

### Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah 2013

Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah. (2013). The Impact of Using SMS as Learning Support Tool on Students' Learning. International Education Studies, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n10p112

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nonrandomized control group pretest–posttest quasi-experiment with 23 control and 29 experimental students in an introductory Visual Basic course. Independent-samples t-test on posttests: "t (50) = -2.64, p = .011", experimental mean higher.

> "But the mean of the posttest scores for the participants in the control group (M = 69.34, SD = 13.5 0) was significantly different than the mean of the posttest scores for the participants in the experimental group (M = 77.93, SD = 9.86), t (50) = -2.64, p = .011."

### Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah 2013 (2)

Al-Mothana M. Gasaymeh & Osamah M. Aldalalah. (2013). The Impact of Using SMS as Learning Support Tool on Students' Learning. International Education Studies, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v6n10p112

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA with pretest as covariate and teaching method (control vs experimental group) as independent variable confirmed the posttest difference: "F(1,49)=12.55, p < .05".

> "The results indicate that after controlling for pretest scores, the differences  in posttest scores are statistically significantly different between the two groups, F(1,49)=12.55, p < .05."
