Annotation study of generated lecture scripts: each slide was rated by three independent annotators on a 5-point Likert scale across tone, clarity, supportiveness, and alignment. The article reports 'our approach (MAIC-FuncGen) achieves the highest overall score of 4.00' versus S2T (3.67), SCP (3.90), and Human (3.86).

"As shown in Table 1, our approach (MAIC-FuncGen) achieves the highest overall score of 4.00, outperforming all baseline methods."
