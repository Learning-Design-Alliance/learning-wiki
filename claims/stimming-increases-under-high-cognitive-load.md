## Evidence

### Sofia Tancredi 2024

Sofia Tancredi, Dor Abrahamson. (2024). Stimming as Thinking: a Critical Reevaluation of Self-Stimulatory Behavior as an Epistemic Resource for Inclusive Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09904-y

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The authors cite observational findings that fidgeting rises with sustained task effort and with boring texts, treating this as surprising if stimming were merely load-depleting: "Fidgeting, for example, increases with sustained effort on a task". No effect sizes are printed.

> "Fidgeting, for example, increases with sustained effort on a task (Farley et al., 2013) and when reading more boring texts (Witchel et al., 2016)."
