# Speech-in-noise ability and its interaction with year level are not significant predictors of NAPLAN numeracy scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Neither speech-in-noise ability nor its interaction with year level significantly predicted numeracy scores in either year level. [→ Tomlin D 2025](#tomlin-d-2025)

## Evidence

### Tomlin D 2025

Tomlin D, Bowers P, Zonca B, Graydon K, Phillips J, Rance G. (2025). Speech-in-noise recognition as a predictor of academic achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0324998

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 2 mixed effects regression on NAPLAN numeracy scores of 108 children. The numeracy interaction effect size was 0.093 (CI −0.277 to 0.462; p = 0.62), non-significant; no effect size was interpreted as meaningful. The article reports no significant predictor relationship for numeracy.

> "In contrast to the relationship between speech-in-noise performance and literacy outcomes, speech-in-noise ability, nor its interaction with year level were significant predictors of numeracy scores."
