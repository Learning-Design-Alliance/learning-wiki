`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA in the grade-8 follow-up added an interaction term (word decoding × group assignment), which "was found to be significant"; a scatter plot (Figure 1) shows the lowest grade-3 decoders gained most. The overall correlation between grade-3 and grade-8 word decoding is .51.

> "It reveals that the students with the lowest word decoding scores in grade 3 beneﬁtted the most from the intervention."
