Secondhand report of a Michigan study (Drake et al., 2019) of evaluation ratings 2012–2016 finding Black teachers disproportionately received low ratings, even within the same schools. Percentages are descriptive; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "A study in Michigan found that from 2012 to 2016, 19 percent of black teachers in the state received a low rating in their yearly evaluations compared to only seven percent of white teachers. Within the same schools, teachers of color—especially black teachers—were 50 percent more likely than white teachers to receive a low rating in their evaluation."
