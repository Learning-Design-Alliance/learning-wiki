## Evidence

### Peters Philipp 2023

Peters Philipp, Lemos Martin, Bönsch Andrea, Ooms Mark, Ulbrich Max, Rashad Ashkan, Krause Felix, Lipprandt Myriam, Kuhlen Torsten W., Röhrig Rainer, Hölzle Frank, Puladi Behrus. (2023). Effect of head-mounted displays on students' acquisition of surgical suturing techniques compared to an e-learning and tutor-led course: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000464

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Multi-arm RCT of 150 novices randomized to e-learning, HMD-based, or tutor-led suturing courses; blinded OSATS GRS scoring of pre/post videos. The unadjusted HMD-vs-e-learning difference (P=0.039) did not survive multiple-testing correction (adjusted P=0.114), and "the different course modalities were comparable (Kruskal–Wallis test, P=0.15)".

> "In contrast, the median ΔGRS was 12.4 (95% CI: 10.0–12.7) for the e-learning course, 14.1 (95% CI: 13.0–15.0) for the HMD-based course, and 12.7 (95% CI: 10.3–14.2) for the tutor-led course."
