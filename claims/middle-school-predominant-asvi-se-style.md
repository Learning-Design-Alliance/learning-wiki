## Evidence

### Raditya Bayu Rahadian and C. Asri Budiningsih 2017

Raditya Bayu Rahadian and C. Asri Budiningsih. (2017). What Are the Suitable Instructional Strategy and Media for Student Learning Styles in Middle Schools? International Journal on Integrating Technology in Education (IJITE) Vol.6, No.4. https://doi.org/10.5121/ijite.2017.6403

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire administration of the ILS to all 307 participants in three middle schools; Table 4 reports dimension percentages (Active 72%, Reflective 28%; Sensitive 79%, Intuitive 21%; Visual 51%, Verbal 49%; Sequential 63%, Global 37%). The article notes "the students are almost equal both visual and verbal."

> "In the perception dimension there are most significant difference, where 79% students came out to be sensitive and only 21% were intuitif. In the entry channel dimension, it was found that the students are almost equal both visual and verbal."
