Variance-comparison analysis of the two groups' reaction times, following Li Qing's method, found no significant difference (P = 0.797). A second line of evidence notes "the average correct rate of the high score group is lower than that of the low score group (0.49 for the high and 0.67 for the low)", so extra time did not buy accuracy.

> "we can not only see that there is no significant difference between the two groups of data, but also that they are almost identical (P = 0.797)."
