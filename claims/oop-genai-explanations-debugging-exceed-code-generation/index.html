Evidence¶

Marina Lepp 2026¶

Marina Lepp. (2026). Patterns of Learner–AI Interaction and Academic Performance in an Object-Oriented Programming Course. IEEE Frontiers in Education (FIE) 2026 Conference. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24755

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey of 210 respondents in a first-year OOP course; Friedman test across three usage purposes followed by Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Holm correction. The article reports χ²(2) = 47.98, p < 0.001, and "code generation was used significantly less often than both debugging a nd explanation seeking".

"A significant difference in usage frequency across the three purposes was found (χ²(2) = 47.98, p < 0.001). Post-hoc tests showed that code generation was used significantly less often than both debugging a nd explanation seeking (Holm-adjusted p < 0.001 for both)."
