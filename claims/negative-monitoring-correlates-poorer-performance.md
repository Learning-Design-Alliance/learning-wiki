## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The more subjects stated they did not understand, the worse they performed on the post-test, indicated by a strong negative correlation between negative monitoring statements and post-test scores. [→ Renkl 1997](#renkl-1997)

## Evidence

### Renkl 1997

Renkl, A. (1997). Learning from Worked-Out Examples: A Study on Individual Differences. Cognitive Science, 21(1), 1-29. https://www.cogsci.org/journal/21-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order and partial correlations showed negative monitoring was significantly negatively associated with all post-test scores: "the more the subjects felt that they did not understand, the worse they performed on the post-test." This contrasted with previous research findings on positive monitoring.

> "it was found that the more the subjects felt that they did not understand, the worse they performed on the post-test."
