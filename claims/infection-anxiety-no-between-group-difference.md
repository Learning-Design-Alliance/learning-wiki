`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

On the five-item Numeric Rating Scale infection-related anxiety measure (Cronbach's α = 0.97), the SP group's score increased (29.17 ± 9.95 to 32.17 ± 11.19) while the PRP group's decreased (29.91 ± 9.83 to 26.91 ± 11.83), with "no statistically significant difference between them".

> "The difference in infection-related anxiety score was 3.00 ± 16.44 and −3.00 ± 15.11 in the simulation using SP group and PRP group, respectively, with no statistically significant difference between them (z = −1.468, p = 0.142; see Table 2)."
