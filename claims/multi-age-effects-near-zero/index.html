Simon Veenman 1996¶

Simon Veenman. (1996). Effects of multigrade and multi-age classes reconsidered. Review of Educational Research, 66(3), 323-340. https://doi.org/10.2307/1170526

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 11 multi-age studies (Table 2): cognitive effects d = -.05 (N = 4,142, CI -.15 to .04, homogeneous); noncognitive effects d = +.08 (N = 4,104, CI -.03 to .20). The article reports confidence intervals around average effect sizes included 0.00 for all analyses.
