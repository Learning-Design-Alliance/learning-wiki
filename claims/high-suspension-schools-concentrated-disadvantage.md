Observational analysis of Chicago district discipline data. The report identifies a subset of schools with very high suspension rates serving predominantly African-American students from the poorest neighborhoods; at high-suspending high schools, about half of students received a suspension in the 2013-14 school year.

> "A subset of Chicago schools—about a quarter of high schools and 10 percent of schools with middle grades— have very high suspension rates, and almost all of these schools predominantly serve African-American students."
