## Evidence

### Corey McInerney and Phil Sutton 2023

Corey McInerney and Phil Sutton. (2023). Einsteinian gravitational concepts throughout secondary school. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad001a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad001a)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post open-ended questionnaire responses to Q3 (What is gravity?) categorised as force versus spacetime curvature. The article notes that "82% of the pre-questionnaire responses described gravity as a force" and that similar studies also struggled to shift this.

> "Only 1.8% of participants specified that gravity was the cause of spacetime curvature pre-intervention, increasing to only 8.9% post-intervention."

### Corey McInerney and Phil Sutton 2023 (2)

Corey McInerney and Phil Sutton. (2023). Einsteinian gravitational concepts throughout secondary school. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad001a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad001a)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Year-group breakdown of Q3 responses in Table 6 shows the curvature gains concentrate in KS4 and A-Level groups; the article states "the KS4 and A-Level groups best understood the concept of gravity from spacetime curvature".

> "However looking at Table 6 which shows the breakdown of Q3 by year group, reveals that responses related to spacetime curvature increased notably in years 10 (up 17.52%), 12 (up 20.24%), and 13 (up 35.35%)."
