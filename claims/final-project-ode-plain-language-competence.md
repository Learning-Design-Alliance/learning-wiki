`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

The final group project, spanning about 6 weeks, required groups to generate a biological question, develop and test an original differential model, and present findings in an open seminar; it was designed to assess all four course objectives. "All students were competent in explaining systems of differential equations in plain language by the final project."

> "Many students found it difficult or intimidating to describe variables and formulas—much less systems of differential equations—early in the semester. All students were competent in explaining systems of differential equations in plain language by the final project."
