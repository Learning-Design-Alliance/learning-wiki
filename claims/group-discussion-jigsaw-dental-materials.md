### Lin Galvin Sim Siang 2023 (2)

Lin Galvin Sim Siang, Tan Wen-Wu, Tan Hung-Jia, Khoo Chia-Wee, Afrashtehfar Kelvin I. (2023). Innovative Pedagogical Strategies in Health Professions Education: Active Learning in Dental Materials Science. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20032041

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

RCT (Sagsoz et al., 50 third-year undergraduate students per Table 2) comparing Jigsaw learning with lecture-based instruction; no significant difference on pre-test and post-test, but Jigsaw performed better on the retention test, with no effect size printed.

> "The student performances were noted to improve with the Jigsaw method despite no significant difference from the traditional lecture method. However, the Jigsaw method was found to be more effective than lectures in students' retention tests."
