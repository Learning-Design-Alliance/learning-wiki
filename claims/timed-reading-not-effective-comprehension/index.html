Evidence¶

Mahdizadeh and Aminafshar 2018¶

Molood Mahdizadeh, Naeemeh Aminafshar. (2018). The Impact of Timed Reading Activity on Iranian EFL learners Reading Speed, Comprehension, and Attitudes at Intermediate Level. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.5p.11

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Independent-samples t-test on post-test comprehension scores (out of 10) of the same 40-learner experiment: control mean 6.80, experimental mean 7.10; the article reports "the two groups are not significantly different" (p = .427). Equivalence was not tested.

"The value of t at the level of 38 degree of freedom is significant that is the value of t is higher than 0.05 (0.42 sig > 0.05). This reveals that the two groups are not significantly different regarding the comprehension scores."
