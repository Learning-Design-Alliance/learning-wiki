## Evidence

### Carolyn Baer 2021

Carolyn Baer, Puja Malik, & Darko Odic. (2021). Are children's judgments of another's accuracy linked to their metacognitive confidence judgments? Metacognition and Learning (2021) 16:485–516. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09263-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Experiment 1 (N = 80 children aged 4–7) correlated individual differences on a selective social learning task with area, number, and emotion confidence judgments, controlling for age. The article reports no correlations "between the Winner, Ask, or Endorse choices and any of the three dimensions".

> "between the Winner, Ask, or Endorse choices and any of the three dimensions for the confi- dence task when controlling for age."

### Carolyn Baer 2021 (2)

Carolyn Baer, Puja Malik, & Darko Odic. (2021). Are children's judgments of another's accuracy linked to their metacognitive confidence judgments? Metacognition and Learning (2021) 16:485–516. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09263-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Experiment 3 (N = 81 children, ages 4–8) used a retrospective confidence paradigm and measured area discrimination accuracy. Replicating Experiment 1 but not Experiment 2, correlations with Winner, Ask, and Endorse were non-significant (e.g., Winner r(78) = 0.14, p = 0.227).

> "children’s performance on the Confidence Task did not predict their Winner, r(78)  = 0.14, p = 0.227, Ask, r(78)  = 0.08, p = 0.499, or Endorse answers, r(78)  = 0.12, p = 0.270, when controlling for age."
