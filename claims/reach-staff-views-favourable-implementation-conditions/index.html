Sibieta 2016¶

Luke Sibieta. (2016). REACH: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581275

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Key conclusion from the mixed-method process evaluation (TA survey plus interviews with staff from six case-study schools). The report states "Staff views were more positive in schools where the interventions were delivered by experienced teaching assistants, supported by senior staff, and allocated a dedicated space for delivery." No effect size is printed.
