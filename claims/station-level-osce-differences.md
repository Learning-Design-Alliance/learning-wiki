`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-station analysis (Table 3) of summative OSCE scores by group and subgroup, Mann-Whitney U test. Station 1 was a topic not covered in the mock OSCE; Station 5 (LGBTQIA+ health) closely resembled a mock station, which the authors suggest may reflect content familiarity.

> "In Station 1, the examiner subgroup outperformed the control group (9.25 ± 0.63 vs. 9.00 ± 1.25; p = 0.01). For Station 5, the intervention group (10 ± 1.00) scored higher than the control group (9.00 ± 2.00; p = 0.033). No significant differences were observed in Stations 2, 3, and 4"
