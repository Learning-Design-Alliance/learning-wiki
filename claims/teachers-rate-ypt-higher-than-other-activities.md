# Teachers rate YPT-related activities as more useful for soft-skill development than regular physics classes and other extracurricular activities, but a self-selection caveat applies

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Teachers rated YPT-related activities 8 out of 10 for soft-skill development versus 5 out of 10 for regular physics classes and other extracurricular activities, with paired t-tests confirming greater perceived usefulness of YPT across all soft-skill types. [→ Aneva 2022](#aneva-2022)
`q2 i?` Only teachers with some YPT experience participated, so self-selection bias may explain why teachers rated YPT more positively than students did, with students reporting YPT as more useful for only 4 of 6 soft skills. [→ Aneva 2022 (2)](#aneva-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Aneva 2022

Aneva, B., Faletič, S., Hömöstrei, M., Jenei, P., Kundracik, F., Kyuldjiev, A., Lindner, T., Němec, H., Puhr, H., & Plesch, M. (2022). Development of Soft Skills via IYPT. Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Teacher survey (stage two) with 33 responses; paired t-tests (section 2.3.1) show significant differences favouring YPT over regular classes for all six soft skills, e.g. Teamwork t = -6.503, df = 31, p < .001.

> "Teachers generally considered YPT-related activities as highly beneficial for students’ soft -skill development (8 out of 10, see 2.2.2). This result is particularly strong when compared to the usefulness ascribed to regular physics classes: 5 out of 10 (see 2.2.1)."

### Aneva 2022 (2)

Aneva, B., Faletič, S., Hömöstrei, M., Jenei, P., Kundracik, F., Kyuldjiev, A., Lindner, T., Němec, H., Puhr, H., & Plesch, M. (2022). Development of Soft Skills via IYPT. Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.09648)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The authors' stated caveat on the teacher survey: the sample comprised only YPT-experienced teachers, and a comparison of student and teacher evaluations (section 2.5) found teachers perceived YPT as significantly more useful across all soft-skill types.

> "Only teachers who have some experience with YPT activities participated in the teacher survey for IO2. Therefore, we have to consider the possibility of a self -selection bias by teachers."
