## Evidence

### Prakash S.S. 2017

Prakash S.S., Muthuraman N., Anand R. (2017). Short-duration podcasts as a supplementary learning tool: perceptions of medical students and impact on assessment performance. BMC Medical Education 17:167. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1001-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Closed-ended questionnaire item (Part B) administered to students who used 3MTLs, with non-users (n = 19) excluded; responses analysed descriptively and plotted in Fig. 1iii. The paper notes preferred podcast duration varies with content type in prior work (Matava et al.).

> "When questioned about their perception of the length of the podcast, 37 students (49.3%; Fig. 1iii) indicated that the time duration of podcasts was optimal, while an equal proportion felt that duration can be increased, preferably to 5 min."
