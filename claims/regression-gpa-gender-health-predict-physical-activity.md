## Evidence

### AlZahrani EM 2025

AlZahrani EM. (2025). Profile of physical activity and sedentary lifestyle in health sciences students: Prevalence and its association with academic performance. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000046733

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression in a cross-sectional study of 401 students, adjusted for gender, age and health status; overall model significant with R2 = 0.174, and the GPA coefficient of 629 MET-min/wk was significant (P < .001).

> "Students’ GPA was a significant positive predictor of total physical activity, with each one-point increase in GPA linked with an extra 629 MET-min/wk (P < .001, 95% CI [430–827])."

### AlZahrani EM 2025 (2)

AlZahrani EM. (2025). Profile of physical activity and sedentary lifestyle in health sciences students: Prevalence and its association with academic performance. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000046733

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same adjusted regression model of 401 students; unstandardised coefficients B = −776.91 for female gender and B = 424.77 for healthy status were significant, while age, days missed and warnings were not (P > .05).

> "Gender was a significant predictor, indicating that female students engaged in less physical activity compared to male students (B = −776.91, 95% CI [−1070 to −482], P < .001). Further, healthy students were associated with greater physical activity than their peers with comorbidities (B = 424.77, 95% CI [72–777], P = .018)."
