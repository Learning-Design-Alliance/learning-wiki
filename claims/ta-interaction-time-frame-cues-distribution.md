### Meagan Sundstrom 2023

Meagan Sundstrom, Rebeckah K. Fussell, Anna McLean Phillips, Mark Akubo, Scott E. Allen, David Hammer, Rachel E. Scherr, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Instructing nontraditional physics labs: Toward responsiveness to student epistemic framing. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020140)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video coding of the TA's framing during interactions with individual groups (Fig. 4). The article reports the TA predominantly, about 40% of interaction time, attempted to cue the High Agency-Falsification frame, aligning with the TA's stated intent.

> "Combined with the time spent cuing the High Agency-Falsiﬁcation frame mentioned above, the TA spends roughly 60% of the interaction time trying to shift students into a falsiﬁcation-oriented frame (sum of dark blue and red bars in instructor frame in Fig. 4)."

### Meagan Sundstrom 2023 (2)

Meagan Sundstrom, Rebeckah K. Fussell, Anna McLean Phillips, Mark Akubo, Scott E. Allen, David Hammer, Rachel E. Scherr, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Instructing nontraditional physics labs: Toward responsiveness to student epistemic framing. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020140)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interaction analysis of Group C, which struggled to design an experiment and expressed frustration; the TA told them they did not actually have to succeed, cuing the Hoops frame to ensure the group had an experiment to report.

> "Surprisingly, the TA cues the Hoops frame with Group C (yellow bar of instructor frame in Fig. 4) despite this framing contradicting the goals of the lab."
