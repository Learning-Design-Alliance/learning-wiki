### Wörner 2022

Wörner, S., Becker, S., Küchemann, S., Scheiter, K., & Kuhn, J. (2022). Development and validation of the Converging Lenses Concept Inventory for middle school physics education. Receipt Date: 19.05.2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020131](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020131)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Histogram analysis of total test scores (maximum 30 points, excluding item Q2) for pre- and posttest, supporting the statement that "the test can differentiate well between different levels of students’ conceptual understanding". The article reports no student scored 0 or 1 point in the pretest and ceiling effects in the posttest for only 2 out of 318 students (0.63%).

> "Therefore, the test can differentiate well between different levels of students’ conceptual understanding both before and after the teaching of converging lenses at school."
