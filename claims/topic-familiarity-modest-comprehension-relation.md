## Evidence

### Keller 2024

Keller, T. A., Mason, R. A., Legg, A. E., & Just, M. A. (2024). The neural and cognitive basis of expository text comprehension. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00232-y

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis in the fMRI study; topics were rated relatively unfamiliar (mean rating = 2.83, SD = 0.88 on a 7-point scale), and the mean correlation of r = 0.24 "was nevertheless reliably greater than zero (t(30) = 4.65,p < 0.000005)".

> "The mean of the correlations between a partici pant's familiarity rating for a topic (based on having seen only the topic title) and their comprehension scores on the passage on that topic was modest (mean Fisher's z-transformedr = 0.24, SD = 0.28)"
