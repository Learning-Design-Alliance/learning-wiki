### Schreuder MM 2025

Schreuder MM, Renkema A-M, Kuijpers-Jagtman AM, Padmos JAD. (2025). Dutch dentists’ involvement in orthodontic retention: monitoring, opinions, competence and communication gaps. The European Journal of Orthodontics. https://doi.org/10.1093/ejo/cjaf020

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey competence section among the 236 GDPs. The printed percentages are 57.4% (n = 120) wanting additional theoretical education and 39.4% (n = 82) practical education, with 33.5% (n = 70) wanting both; GDPs who studied in Utrecht or abroad expressed a greater need (χ² = 13.10, P = .022).

> "GDPs indicated the need for additional theoretical and practical education (57.4%, n = 120 and 39.4%, n = 82 respectively). Of these, 33.5% (n = 70) indicated to need both."
