## Evidence

### Lejus-Bourdeau Corinne 2021

Lejus-Bourdeau Corinne, Pousset Florence, Magne Cécile, Bazin Olivier, Grillot Nicolas, Pichenot Vincent. (2021). Low-cost versus high-fidelity pediatric simulators for difficult airway management training: a randomized study in continuing medical education. Brazilian Journal of Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjane.2021.05.006

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated variance analysis with Bonferroni-Dunn tests on the 18 LC and 12 HF participants who completed all three EQS questionnaires. The article reports a time effect (p = 0.0001) with T0 EQS higher than T3 and T6, and no group effect (p = 0.6624).

> "No group effect was noted (p = 0.6624). In contrast, a time effect was observed (p = 0.0001) with a T0 EQS higher than the T3 and T6 EQS."
