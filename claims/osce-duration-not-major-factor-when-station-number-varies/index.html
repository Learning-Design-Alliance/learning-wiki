Evidence¶

Peng Qiyun 2025¶

Peng Qiyun, Luo Jiani, Wang Chunyan, Chen Lei, Tan Shenglan. (2025). Impact of station number and duration time per station on the reliability of Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) scores: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06691-0

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of studies grouped by duration (<10 min vs >=10 min) across all station numbers. Neither group reached the alpha >= 0.8 threshold, and the authors state this may be because time was already considered in assessment design or mitigated by optimized station content.

"the cumulative Cronbach's alpha for both groups indicated not reaching good internal consistency (0.73 and 0.77, respectively)."
