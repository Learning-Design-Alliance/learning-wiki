Evidence¶

marcs-1¶

Murphy, A. (2026, July). Classroom-Based experimentation in a digital learning platform. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/306

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Experiments conducted under the IES-funded MARCS collaboration with CAST, embedded in MATHia for students with reading difficulties; over 97,000 students participated across the two-year grant. The report prints the 30% completion-speed figure but no standardized effect size.

"The most significant results of this work found that students who received word problems written by either human experts or LLMs, compared with a business-as-usual condition, completed math problem sets 30% more quickly and improved overall mastery rate."
