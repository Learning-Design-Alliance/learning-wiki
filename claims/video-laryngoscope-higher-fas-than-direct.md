## Evidence

### Lee Gun Tak 2022

Lee Gun Tak, Park Jong Eun, Woo Sook-young, Shin Tae Gun, Jeong Daun, Kim Taerim, Lee Se Uk, Yoon Hee, Hwang Sung Yeon. (2022). Defining the learning curve for endotracheal intubation in the emergency department. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-19337-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable GLMM on the 2077-case registry dataset; device was selected at the intubator's or faculty's discretion, an observational contrast rather than a randomized comparison. VL use was "significantly associated with increased FAS" with adjusted odds ratio = 1.890 versus DL — a medium-sized effect.

> "Compared with DL use, VL use was significantly associated with increased FAS (aOR 1.890; 95% CI 1.506–2.370; p < 0.001)."
