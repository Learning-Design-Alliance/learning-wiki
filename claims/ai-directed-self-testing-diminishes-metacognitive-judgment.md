# AI-directed problem selection may diminish the metacognitive dimension of self-testing while preserving retrieval practice

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` When students generate their own practice problems they exercise metacognitive judgment that is itself generative; when an AI selects and delivers problems through an adaptive algorithm, this metacognitive dimension may be diminished even though retrieval practice is preserved. [→ Hoyun Cho 2026](#hoyun-cho-2026)

## Evidence

### Hoyun Cho 2026

Hoyun Cho. (2026). Relocating the Locus of Generation: The AI-GLM Framework for Generative Learning in AI-Mediated Mathematics Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10210-y

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual analysis of the self-testing strategy under AI mediation. The article states the tension is between retrieval-practice benefits, which AI can preserve or enhance, and metacognitive self-regulation benefits, which AI may diminish; no empirical test of this dissociation is reported.

> "When students generate their own practice problems or decide what to test themselves on, they exercise metacognitive judgment about what they know and need to practice, which is itself a form of generative activity."
