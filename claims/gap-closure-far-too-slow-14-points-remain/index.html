Evidence¶

Kimberlee Eberle-Sudré 2015¶

Kimberlee Eberle-Sudré, Meredith Welch, and Andrew Howard Nichols. (2015). Rising Tide: Do College Grad Rate Gains Benefit All Students? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566656

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of the 255 improving public institutions. The report concludes gains were "hardly at a pace needed to close the outcome gap — at least not in this century," with the gap narrowing only 0.6 points.

"In over a decade, the gap between white students and underrepresented students at the improving institutions narrowed by slightly more than half a percentage point (0.6), leaving a 14-point completion gap."
