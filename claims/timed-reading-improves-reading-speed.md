## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Intermediate EFL learners who did timed reading activity read significantly faster on the post-test than control learners who read the same passages without time pressure. [→ Mahdizadeh and Aminafshar 2018](#mahdizadeh-and-aminafshar-2018)

## Evidence

### Mahdizadeh and Aminafshar 2018

Molood Mahdizadeh, Naeemeh Aminafshar. (2018). The Impact of Timed Reading Activity on Iranian EFL learners Reading Speed, Comprehension, and Attitudes at Intermediate Level. Advances in Language and Literary Studies. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.9n.5p.11

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A pre-test/post-test experiment with 40 intermediate learners randomly divided into an experimental and a control group of 20 each; the experimental group did timed reading. Post-test reading speed means were 103 for the control group and 122 for the experimental group, with a statistically significant t-test difference ("the significance level is 0.00").

> "As shown in the table, the significance level is 0.00 (p =.000), which is below 0.05. (0.00 < 0.05); therefore, there is a statistically significant difference in the post-test results of the two groups."
