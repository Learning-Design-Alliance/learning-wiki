## Evidence

### Davies E. Bethan 2018

Davies E. Bethan, Beever Emmeline, Glazebrook Cris. (2018). A pilot randomised controlled study of the mental health first aid eLearning course with UK medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1154-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat repeated measures ANOVA in the two-arm pilot RCT (N = 55). The finding concerns "a significant interaction between time and group" on first aid intentions. Intentions improved in the intervention group but not in controls.

> "ITT analysis found no main effect for change in mental health first aid intentions between baseline and follow-up (F(1,53) = 3.67, p = .06), but there was a significant interaction between time and group (F(1,53) = 16.03, p = <.001)."
