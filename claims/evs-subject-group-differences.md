## Evidence

### Marija Cubric 2015

Marija Cubric, and Amanda Jefferies. (2015). The benefits and challenges of large-scale deployment of electronic voting systems: University student views from across different subject groups. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.04.004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Non-parametric group comparison across three subject groups (HHS, SSAH, ST) using survey data from 590 students. Mann-Whitney follow-up tests with Bonferroni correction confirmed HHS reported higher learning benefits and SSAH reported higher challenges.

> "The Kruskal-Wallis test revealed that “learning benefits” and “challenges” (1st and 2nd component in Table 5) were both significantly affected by the subject group (Table 9) where H(2) =29.82, p< 0.001 and H(2)= 24.97, p<0.001 respectively."
