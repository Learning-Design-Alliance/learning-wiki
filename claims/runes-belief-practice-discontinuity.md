## Evidence

### Reinholz 2014

Reinholz, D. L., Corbo, J. C., Dancy, M. H., Finkelstein, N., & Deetz, S. (2014). Towards a Model of Systemic Change in University STEM Education. [doi:10.2307/j.ctv2x00vcx.13](https://doi.org/10.2307/j.ctv2x00vcx.13)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Survey of the Runes Department: 31 of 34 faculty responded (91% response rate). Faculty rated active participation 4.75 out of 5 and interactive learning 4.66 out of 5, while small group work was 2.6 out of 4 and lecture-style guiding was rated 3.2 out of 4, showing "a discontinuity between professed beliefs and actual teaching practices". Means are descriptive; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "These survey responses seem to indicate a discontinuity between professed beliefs and actual teaching practices, indicative of the institutional culture and incentive structures."
