### Zhang Yuejiao 2025

Zhang Yuejiao, Li Xiaolan, Wei Xi, Xiao Jiexuan, Zhang Qi, Mai Sui. (2025). A pilot study on the application of a digital dental teaching evaluation system in Class II cavity preparation teaching. BMC Oral Health 25, 372. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05695-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests of baseline versus final Fair Grader 2000 scores: ongoing group 59.21 ± 5.78 to 57.73 ± 6.36 (t = −0.80, P = 0.453); completed group 64.57 ± 5.93 to 67.8 ± 6.469 (t = 1.25, P = 0.244). Equivalence was not tested; no effect size is printed.
