Evidence¶

Temel 2014¶

Senar Temel. (2014). The effects of problem-based learning on pre-service teachers’ critical thinking dispositions and perceptions of problem-solving ability. South African Journal of Education, 34(1), Art. #769. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136398

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Two-way ANOVA on PSI scores in the same randomised pre-test–post-test study (PBL n = 22, traditional n = 27) found a "significant difference" between teaching methods (F = 4.067, p = 0.049); no effect size was printed.

"A significant difference was found between the PSI pre-test and post-test scores of the pre-service teachers in both the experimental group and control group 1,47 according to the different teaching methods implemented (F = 4.067, p = 0.049)."

Temel 2014 (2)¶

Senar Temel. (2014). The effects of problem-based learning on pre-service teachers’ critical thinking dispositions and perceptions of problem-solving ability. South African Journal of Education, 34(1), Art. #769. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136398

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' interpretation of the PSI results: because high PSI scores indicate perceived insufficiency, the greater decrease in the PBL group's scores (post-test means 84.045 vs 92.814) was read as PBL being "more influential than the traditional teaching method".

"It was also found that the PBL approach, in which a greater decrease was observed in the scores of perceptions of problem-solving ability, was more influential than the traditional teaching method in raising pre-service teachers’ perceptions of problem-solving ability."
