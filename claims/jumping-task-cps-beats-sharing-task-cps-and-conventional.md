## Evidence

### Hobri 2020

Hobri, Ummah, I. K., Yuliati, N., & Dafik (2020). The Effect of Jumping Task Based on Creative Problem Solving on Students’ Problem Solving Ability. International Journal of Instruction, 13(1), 387-406. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13126a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni post hoc multiple comparisons following a significant F-test on post-test scores of three classes of 34 students each. The printed mean differences were 11.235 (x1 vs x2), 19.588 (x1 vs x3), and 8.353 (x2 vs x3), all marked significant.

> "From these results, it can be concluded that the experimental class (x 1) has more significant influence than experiment class (x2) and the control class (x3) and experiment class (x2) have more significant influence than the control class (x 3)."

### Hobri 2020 (2)

Hobri, Ummah, I. K., Yuliati, N., & Dafik (2020). The Effect of Jumping Task Based on Creative Problem Solving on Students’ Problem Solving Ability. International Journal of Instruction, 13(1), 387-406. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13126a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA (F-test) on post-test problem-solving scores of the three classes (N = 102 total per the printed df), conducted after normality and homogeneity checks passed; the article reports sig. 0.000 at the 0.05 level.

> "Table 11 shows the variant test result of problem -solving abilities gained sig.0.000, which is less than the significant level of 0.05."
