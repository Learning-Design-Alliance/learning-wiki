## Evidence

### Cherry Youn 2022

Cherry Youn, Andrew D. Grotzinger, Christina M. Lill, Lars Bertram, Florian Schmiedek, Martin Lövdén, Ulman Lindenberger, Michel Nivard, K. Paige Harden, Elliot M. Tucker-Drob. (2022). Genetic associations with learning over 100 days of practice. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00121-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nonlinear growth curve models (NLMIXED in SAS) fitted to cognitive performance across 100 days for 131 participants, with EAPGS entered as a predictor of all random-effect parameters. The article reports "no statistically significant associations between the EAPGS and this variation".

> "Results indicated substantial variability in the shape and rate of learning. However, there were no statistically significant associations between the EAPGS and this variation."
