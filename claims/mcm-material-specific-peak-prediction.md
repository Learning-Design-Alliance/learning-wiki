`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Simulation analysis of two experiments (panels b and c of Figure 2) that explored identical ISIs and RIs with two different types of material; MCM's fits to their forgetting functions yield different predicted peaks. The article offers this as a prediction, not a tested result.

> "With the coarse range of ISIs explored, the authors of these experiments concluded that the peak ISI was the same independent of the material (28 days). MCM suggests a different peak for the two sets of material, a prediction that can be evaluated empirically."
