### Balzer Katrin 2016

Balzer Katrin, Schröder Rhian, Junghans Anne, Stahl Ute, Träder Jens-Martin, Köpke Sascha. (2016). Improving competencies in evidence-based dementia care: Results from a pilot study on a novel inter-professional training course (the KOMPIDEM project). GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001034](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001034)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Observational report from the first piloting in Summer Term 2014, where six nursing students and two medical students signed up. The article lists the students' stated concerns, including "Obligatory course participation for nursing students compared to voluntary course participation for medical students" and perceived scheduling difficulties.

> "Despite multiple discussions with the students themselves, the course teacher and other students' mentors, the students could not be convinced to continue participation, leading to premature course termination before the PbL tutorials and visitations to care facilities began."
