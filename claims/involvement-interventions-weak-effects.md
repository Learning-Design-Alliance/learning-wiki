## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Pomerantz et al. 2007

Eva M. Pomerantz, Elizabeth A. Moorman and Scott D. Litwack. (2007). The How, Whom, and Why of Parents' Involvement in Children's Academic Lives: More Is Not Always Better. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/003465430305567

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports Mattingly et al.'s (2002) meta-analysis of parent-involvement programs: 17 pre-post studies without controls yielded positive effects, but 'these more internally valid studies yielded negative effects.' The review notes serious methodological problems in many studies and cautions against firm conclusions.

> "this was not the case for the 14 studies using designs in which the interventions were compared with unmatched or matched control groups. If anything, these more internally valid studies yielded negative effects"
