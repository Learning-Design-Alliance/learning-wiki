## Evidence

### Brouwers Melissa C 2011

Brouwers Melissa C, Makarski Julie, Durocher Lisa D, Levinson Anthony J. (2011). E-learning interventions are comparable to user's manual in a randomized trial of training strategies for the AGREE II. Implementation Science. https://doi.org/10.1186/1748-5908-6-81

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Learner's scale ratings on a 7-point response scale were compared across the three conditions via multivariate one-way ANOVAs. Means printed in Table 3 range from about 5.2 to 6.45; no standardized effect sizes are reported. The article notes the scale items' reliability and validity testing was not undertaken.

> "Participants reported high levels of training satisfaction (means 6.0+) and self-efficacy (means 5.4+). There were no significant differences in any measure as a function of training condition (p > 0.05 for all comparisons)."
