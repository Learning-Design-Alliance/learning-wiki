`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Selectivity analysis of the ESM data examined correlations between lecture attendance and gender, academic performance, and student averages of situational motivation. The article reports "better grades; r = .33, p < .001" and notes systematic missingness may have overrepresented more motivated students.

> "Higher attendance was related to academic performance (better grades; r = .33, p < .001) and lower average opportunity cost (r = .24, p = .010), but unrelated to gender (r = .04, p = .650)."
