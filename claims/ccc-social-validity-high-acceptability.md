### Frampton 2025

Frampton, S. E., Vesely, S. E., & Jackson, K. (2025). Teaching undergraduates to solve equivalence problems by using cover, copy, and compare: A translational study. Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 58(4), 805–820. https://doi.org/10.1002/jaba.70026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A four-item social validity survey adapted from Moser et al. (2012) using a 4-point Likert scale. Participants "strongly agreed" with liking the procedure (M = 3.85), memorization help (M = 4), and future use (M = 3.57).

> "On average, participants strongly agreed with the statements "I liked using this procedure for notetaking" (M = 3.85, range: 3–4), "This procedure helped my memorization of the images and their relationships," (M = 4), and "I would use this method of notetaking in the future" (M = 3.57, range: 3–4)."
