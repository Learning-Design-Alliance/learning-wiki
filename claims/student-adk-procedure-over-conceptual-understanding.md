`q1 i?` An element of students' ADK in physics class is to follow a defined procedure which, if applied correctly, will result in a correct answer verifiable in the back of the textbook, and following procedure is more important than conceptual understanding, critical thinking, or real-world reasonability of results. ## Evidence

### Eleanor C. Sayre 2016

Eleanor C. Sayre, Claudia Fracchiolla, Ben Van Dusen. (2016). iPad Screencasts as Evidence of Accountable Disciplinary Knowledge. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1605.08105](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1605.08105)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Case-based micro-genetic analysis of a single student's screencast solving an air pressure problem. The authors report Stu emphasized operational steps without explaining conceptual reasoning, stating "no explanation is given about why he decided to use that particular equation".

> "Our claim is that an element of students' ADK in physics class is to follow a deﬁned procedure, which (if applied correctly) will certainly result in a correct answer. The answer can be veriﬁed in the back of the textbook."
