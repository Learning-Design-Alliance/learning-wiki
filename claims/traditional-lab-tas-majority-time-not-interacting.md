Eric M. Hickok and Cassandra A. Paul. (2025). Examining teaching assistant pedagogies in traditional laboratories and recitations. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.09136](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.09136)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational RIOT analysis of eight hours of lab observations across three TAs teaching four sections. TAs spent nearly 28% of time talking at students, just over 8% in dialogue, and "60.5% of total observation timeNot Interactingwith their students"; during small-group work they were inattentive nearly 70% of that time.
