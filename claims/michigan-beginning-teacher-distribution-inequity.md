## Evidence

### Jen DeNeal 2025

Jen DeNeal, Charlotte Pierce, and the EdTrust-Midwest team. (2025). Addressing Michigan's Teacher Shortage Problem for Students Most in Need: Closing the Opportunity Divide. EdTrust-Midwest. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED671388

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of Michigan workforce data by Opportunity Index band found students in the highest-poverty districts are "nearly three times more likely" to have a beginning teacher; in band 6, one-third of teachers are in their first year versus 16% in the wealthiest districts.

> "Students who learn in school districts with the highest concentrations of poverty are nearly three times more likely than their peers in low-poverty districts to learn from a beginning teacher with fewer than three years of teaching experience."
