## Evidence

### Celik N 2025

Celik N, Sarmasoglu Kilikcier S, Tasdelen Teker G, Keskin G, Akın M, Ozmert S, Elcin M, Senel E. (2025). Anaesthesiologists' Non-Technical Skills in Denmark Form: cross-cultural adaptation, reliability and validity of Turkish version in paediatric surgery team. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-003249

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-rater reliability analysis of simultaneous independent ratings by two observers of real paediatric surgery and urology operating room teams using ANTStr; ICC values were 0.95–0.98 by category, and 'for the overall score, it was also 0.98'.

> "For the category of situational awareness, the ICC was estimated at 0.97; for decision-making, it was 0.98; for teamwork, it was 0.95; for leadership, it was 0.98; and for the overall score, it was also 0.98."
