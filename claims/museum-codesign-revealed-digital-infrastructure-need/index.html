Results section of a bounded case study of two undergraduates in a 7-week CBL course partnered with a regional museum. The authors report that the co-design dialogue surfaced "it lacked digital infrastructure to extend spatial storytelling into urban cultural contexts," motivating the map design.

"Co-design dialogue with Museum educators revealed an unmet need: while the Museum possessed expertise in physical exhibitions, it lacked digital infrastructure to extend spatial storytelling into urban cultural contexts"
