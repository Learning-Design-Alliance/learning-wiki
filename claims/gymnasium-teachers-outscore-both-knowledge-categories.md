## Evidence

### Krauss 2008

Krauss, Stefan; Brunner, M.; Kunter, M.; Baumert, J.; Blum, W.; Neubrand, M.; Jordan, A. (2008). Pedagogical content knowledge and content knowledge of secondary mathematics teachers. Journal of Educational Psychology 100 (3), S. 716-725. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.100.3.716

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Comparative finding from the study of 198 German mathematics teachers assessed with theory-based PCK and CK tests. The article reports that Gymnasium-qualified teachers "outscore teachers from other school types on both knowledge categories." No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "Findings show that mathematics teachers with an in-depth mathematical training (i.e., teachers qualified to teach at the academic-track Gymnasium) outscore teachers from other school types on both knowledge categories"
