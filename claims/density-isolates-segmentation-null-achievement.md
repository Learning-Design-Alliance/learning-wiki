### van Rijsewijk 2018

van Rijsewijk, L.G.M., Oldenburg, B., Snijders, T.A.B., Dijkstra, J.K., & Veenstra, R. (2018). A description of classroom help networks, individual network position, and their associations with academic achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208173

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the multilevel full model, density (b = 2.09, p = .33), proportion of isolates (b = −0.13, p = .87), and segmentation (b = −0.31, p = .55) showed no significant associations with achievement. "Hypothesis 1 and 2 were rejected, as there were no associations of density, proportion of isolates, or segmentation with achievement."
