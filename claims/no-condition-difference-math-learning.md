# Math learning gains did not differ between the Mindful and Cognitive conditions

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

In Rief et al. (2026), seventh graders practised algebra in the "Math with Matt" tutoring system. The Mindful version added an LLM-based mindful chat, in which the pedagogical agent Matt gave context-sensitive emotional support, and mindful (not only evaluative) feedback and hint messages; the Cognitive version gave cognitive support only.

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Both conditions improved from pretest to posttest with no group or interaction effect. [→ Rief 2026](#rief-2026)

## Evidence

### Rief 2026

Rief, V., Hladký, M., Yoo, M., Heel, S., Sato, S., Nagashima, T. (2026). Beyond Problem Solving: Large Language Models for Emotional and Reflective Support in Mathematics Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02611 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.02611](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.02611)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post algebra scores in the 42-student classroom experiment: Cognitive rose from M=4.38 to 5.33 and Mindful from M=4.39 to 4.72; the mixed-effect model found no group or interaction effect, so the improvement did not depend on condition.

> "the Cognitive condition increased fromM=4.38 (SD=2.06) toM=5.33 (SD=2.08) and the Mindful condition fromM=4.39 (SD=1.72) toM=4.72 (SD=1.93)."
