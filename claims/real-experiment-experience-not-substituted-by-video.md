## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The majority of students (79%) agree that the experience gained from executing a real experiment cannot be substituted by measurements from a video-recorded experiment. [→ M. Pilakouta 2012](#m-pilakouta-2012)

## Evidence

### M. Pilakouta 2012

M. Pilakouta, K. Mitritsakis, E. Fragkedakis, C.P. Varsamis. (2012). Videos of physics experiments: A supplementary educational tool for students and teachers. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1211.4112](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1211.4112)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Voluntary five-point questionnaire answered by 33 of 51 students attending a mixed theory-and-experiment course at TEI Piraeus, March to June 2012. Statement S1 results: "the majority of 79% believe that the experience from the execution of a real experiment cannot be substituted by that from a video recorded experiment" (12% maybe, 9% disagree).
