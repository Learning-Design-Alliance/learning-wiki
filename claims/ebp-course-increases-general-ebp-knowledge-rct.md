### Marqueze 2026

Transforming Hospital Care: Impact of an Evidence‐Based Practice Course on Healthcare Professionals' Competencies in a Randomized Clinical Trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Linear Mixed Model analysis of the intervention group (n = 18), adjusted for educational level, job level, time working at the hospital, and weekly workload, showed a "statistically significant effect of the educational intervention on the general knowledge in EBP score"; Table 3 reports a 19.1% difference (F = 54.2, p < 0.001).

> "By contrast, analysis of the Intervention Group (IG) revealed a statistically significant effect of the educational intervention on the general knowledge in EBP score."
