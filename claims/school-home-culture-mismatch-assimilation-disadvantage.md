A conclusion-section argument of the paper: the "mismatch between the school and home cultures" disadvantages learning, and the cause is "the practice of assimilation as opposed to inclusion." This is the authors' interpretive claim, not a tested result.

> "The mismatch between the school and home cultures puts students at a disadvantage for learning. The disadvantage is not created by the diversity among teachers and students, but by the practice of assimilation as opposed to inclusion of the student's culture."
