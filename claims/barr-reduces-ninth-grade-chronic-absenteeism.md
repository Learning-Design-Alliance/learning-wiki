### Building Assets Reducing Risks (BARR) program evidence summary 2020

Building Assets Reducing Risks (BARR) program evidence summary. (2020). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/building-assets-reducing-risks-barr-attendance/

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomized study with school-level random assignment examined BARR's impact on ninth-grade chronic absenteeism across 66 schools in 12 states and Washington, DC, including 21,355 students across three cohorts. Students in "BARR schools had significantly lower rates of chronic absenteeism than students in control schools (19.0% vs. 21.8%)" with an effect size of +0.11.

> "Across all three cohorts combined, students in BARR schools had significantly lower rates of chronic absenteeism than students in control schools (19.0% vs. 21.8%), with an effect size of +0.11."
