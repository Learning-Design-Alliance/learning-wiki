# Ninth-graders learning logic with contextualized courseware plus teacher support achieved higher conceptual knowledge than courseware-only or teacher-only groups

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2018 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Groups studying single- or multiple-context courseware with teacher support significantly outperformed the single-context-only and teacher-only groups on conceptual knowledge of the logic unit. [→ Yavuz Akpinar 2018](#yavuz-akpinar-2018)
`q3 i?` The single-context-with-teacher and multiple-context-with-teacher groups did not differ significantly in conceptual knowledge. [→ Yavuz Akpinar 2018](#yavuz-akpinar-2018)

## Evidence

### Yavuz Akpinar 2018

Yavuz Akpinar, Özlem Şengül. (2018). Two Modalities of the Contextualized Courseware in Three Modalities of Classroom Use. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165740

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc Bonferroni tests following an ANOVA on achievement scores [F(3, 147)=11,452; p=0,0001] in the quasi-experimental four-group study of 151 ninth-graders. Group A (multiple context with teacher) and group C (single context with teacher) both "significantly outperformed the group B" and group D; A and C did not differ from each other.

> "achievement of conceptual knowledge of Logic unit of the group A (M=2,3; SD=2,053) is significantly higher than the group B (M=,487; SD=2,001) and the group D (M=,550; SD=2,074); the group C (M=2,7; SD=2,135) significantly outperformed the group B (M=,487; SD=2,001) and the group D (M=,550; SD=2,074)"
