### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (Koca and Arkan 2020, Turkey) of a Jennings Disaster Nursing Management Model program with six learning-management-system modules in 235 third-year nursing students. The review reports "R 2 = 0.331" for preparedness perception and "R 2 = 0.317" for DRSES, with a moderate effect size.

> "After the intervention, the experimental group showed a significant increase in disaster preparedness perception and DRSES compared with the control group (p < 0.05). The intervention explained 33.1% of the variance in disaster preparedness perception (R 2 = 0.331) and 31.7% of the variance in DRSES (R 2 = 0.317), with a moderate effect size."
