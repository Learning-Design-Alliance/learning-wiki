# Flipped students rated technology-facilitated activities more negatively but saw clearer purpose in activities, with other attitudes equal

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Flipped-condition students held a more negative attitude toward technology-facilitated activities than nonflipped students. [→ Jensen Jamie L. 2015](#jensen-jamie-l-2015)
`q3 i?` Flipped-condition students felt activities had a clearer purpose for their learning; no differences emerged on structure, at-home or in-class activity usefulness, or constructivist quality. [→ Jensen Jamie L. 2015 (2)](#jensen-jamie-l-2015-2)

## Evidence

### Jensen Jamie L. 2015

Jensen Jamie L., Kummer Tyler A., Godoy Patricia D. d. M. (2015). Improvements from a Flipped Classroom May Simply Be the Fruits of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0129

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mann-Whitney U-test on end-of-semester 6-point Likert surveys found flipped students significantly more negative about technology-facilitated activities (p = 0.03); a common improvement suggestion in the flipped section was that the CMS was a hindrance (27.7%).

> "Students in the flipped condition had a more negative attitude toward the technology-facilitated activities (i.e., online tutorials, videos, and homework assignments), with an average rank of 43.03 compared with an average rank of 55.35 in the control condition (z = −2.25, p = 0.03; Table 2, item 4)."

### Jensen Jamie L. 2015 (2)

Jensen Jamie L., Kummer Tyler A., Godoy Patricia D. d. M. (2015). Improvements from a Flipped Classroom May Simply Be the Fruits of Active Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.14-08-0129

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same survey showed flipped students rated the course as better organized with clearer activity purpose (p = 0.02), yet this did not increase homework completion rates, which did not differ between conditions (p = 0.09).

> "However, students in the flipped condition felt that the activities had a more clear purpose for their learning, with an average rank of 55.32 in comparison with an average rank of 42.28 in the control condition (z = −2.39, p = 0.02; Table 2, item 5)."
