`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Co-occurrence analysis (Table VII) of the 546 affectively coded segments. On average 71.2% of each negative code's occurrences and 60.9% of each positive code's occurrences appeared in multiply-coded segments; exam was the low outlier for both valences.

> "Notably, the EXAM code has the smallest percentage of occurrences in multiply-coded segments for both positive and negative experiences, indicating that it is the least overlapping, most separable course experience."
