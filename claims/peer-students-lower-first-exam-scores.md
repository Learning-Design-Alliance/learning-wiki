### Meaders Clara L. 2025

Meaders Clara L., Mendez Lilyan, Aguilar Alfonso Godínez, Rivera Angelita T., Vasquez Izabella, Mueller Liam O., Owens Melinda T. (2025). An Asynchronous Chemistry-in-biology Intervention Improves Student Content Knowledge and Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education 24(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.24-05-0151

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate permutation-median analyses of first midterm scores from two instructors in Winter 2022 and Spring 2022 (N = 539) found "PEER students receiving lower exam scores than their classmates". No effect size was printed for this comparison.

> "The median difference permutation tests (with Holm-corrected p-values) revealed that there were statistically significant differences between exam scores for students from historically underserved communities, with PEER students receiving lower exam scores than their classmates"
