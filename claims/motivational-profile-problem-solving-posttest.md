# Students with good-quality or moderately positive motivational profiles scored higher on the problem-solving posttest than students with poor-quality or moderately negative profiles

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i1`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Controlling for pretest, the two higher-quality profiles outperformed the two lower-quality profiles on problem-solving posttest (d = 0.642). [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021](#lisette-wijnia-2021)
`q2 i1` The poor-quality group scored significantly worse than the moderately negative group (d = 0.465). [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021 (2)](#lisette-wijnia-2021-2)
`q2 i?` Good-quality and moderately positive profiles did not differ significantly from each other (d = 0.065). [→ Lisette Wijnia 2021 (3)](#lisette-wijnia-2021-3)

## Evidence

### Lisette Wijnia 2021

Lisette Wijnia, Martine Baars. (2021). The role of motivational profiles in learning problem-solving and self-assessment skills with video modeling examples. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09531-4

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA planned contrast in a one-group pretest-posttest study of 342 secondary students, with pretest as covariate; the omnibus profile effect was F(3, 337) = 10.61, p < .001, η2p = .086. "students with good-quality or moderately positive profiles scored significantly higher on the posttest (controlling for the pretest score) than students with poor-quality or moderately negative profiles, β = .31, p < .001, d = 0.642"

> "students with good-quality or moderately positive profiles scored significantly higher on the posttest (controlling for the pretest score) than students with poor-quality or moderately negative profiles, β = .31, p < .001, d = 0.642"

### Lisette Wijnia 2021 (2)

Lisette Wijnia, Martine Baars. (2021). The role of motivational profiles in learning problem-solving and self-assessment skills with video modeling examples. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09531-4

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bonferroni-adjusted planned contrast (alpha .017) on problem-solving posttest controlling for pretest. "there was a significant difference between the poor-quality and moderately negative groups, β = .19, p = .001, d = 0.465, indicating that the poor-quality group scored significantly worse than the moderately negative group."

> "there was a significant difference between the poor-quality and moderately negative groups, β = .19, p = .001, d = 0.465, indicating that the poor-quality group scored significantly worse than the moderately negative group."

### Lisette Wijnia 2021 (3)

Lisette Wijnia, Martine Baars. (2021). The role of motivational profiles in learning problem-solving and self-assessment skills with video modeling examples. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09531-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Planned contrast between the two higher-quality profiles on problem-solving posttest. "There was no significant difference between the good-quality and moderately positive groups, β = .03, p = .646, d = 0.065, indicating that both profiles were equally beneficial for learning to solve the biology problems."

> "There was no significant difference between the good-quality and moderately positive groups, β = .03, p = .646, d = 0.065, indicating that both profiles were equally beneficial for learning to solve the biology problems."
