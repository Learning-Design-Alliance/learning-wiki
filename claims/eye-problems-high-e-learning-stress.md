## Evidence

### Kabir Humayun 2021

Kabir Humayun, Hasan Md. Kamrul, Mitra Dipak Kumar. (2021). E-learning readiness and perceived stress among the university students of Bangladesh during COVID-19: a countrywide cross-sectional study. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2021.2009908

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted multinomial logistic regression result from the 1145-student survey; "high-level e-learning stress (AOR = 2.53, 95% CI: 1.45–4.39)". No standardised effect size was printed. The moderate-stress contrast for eye problems was not significant in the adjusted model.

> "Students who have any eye problems were more likely to report high-level e-learning stress (AOR = 2.53, 95% CI: 1.45–4.39) than students without any eye problems."
