## Evidence

### Bansal Sandeep 2025

Bansal Sandeep, Pagidas Kelly. (2025). Strength of motivation and academic performance of medical students: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07733-3

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis of demographic factors in the same longitudinal cohort; the article reports positive age–motivation associations at the first three measurement points, with effect sizes rs = 0.39 (p = 0.005), rs = 0.38, and rs = 0.37.

> "Age was found to be positively associated with total motivation strengths (total composite SMMS-R scores). It was significant for the first, second, and third measurement points (Phase 1), with rs =0.39, p = 0.005; rs =0.38, p = 0.006; rs = 0.37, p = 0.007, respectively."

### Bansal Sandeep 2025 (2)

Bansal Sandeep, Pagidas Kelly. (2025). Strength of motivation and academic performance of medical students: a longitudinal study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07733-3

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same cohort; the article reports negative age–examination associations at the first two measurement points, with effect sizes rs = −0.38 (p = 0.006) and rs = −0.29 (p = 0.04), "indicating that increasing age negatively impacted examination performance in the early period of medical training."

> "However, age and examination scores were found to be negatively associated across first and second measurement points, rs= −0.38, p = 0.006 and rs= −0.29, p = 0.04, respectively, indicating that increasing age negatively impacted examination performance in the early period of medical training."
