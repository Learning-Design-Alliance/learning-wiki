## Evidence

### Reijers Stefanie N.H. 2025

Reijers Stefanie N.H., Vroegop Jantien L., Jansen Pauline W., Kremer Bernd, van der Schroeff Marc P. (2025). The Impact of Slight to Mild Hearing Loss on Academic Performance and Behavior of 9–15‐Year‐Olds. The Laryngoscope. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.31908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multiple linear regression on YSR self-report T-scores (girls n = 2387) showed an association between LPTA and "higher attention problem scores among girls", with per-1-dB betas of 0.01 (better ear) and 0.02 (worse ear); the article notes this corresponded with 0.05 and 0.11 higher scores.

> "The analyses of hearing acuity and behavior, measured by the YSR, showed an association between LPTA in the better‐ and worse‐hearing ear and higher attention problem scores among girls (per 1 dB LPTA: β = 0.01; 95% CI [0.00, 0.01] and β = 0.02; 95% CI [0.01, 0.02])"
