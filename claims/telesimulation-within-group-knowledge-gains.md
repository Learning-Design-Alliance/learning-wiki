## Evidence

### Mileder LP 2021

Mileder LP, Bereiter M, Wegscheider T. (2021). Telesimulation as a modality for neonatal resuscitation training. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1892017

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sub-group analysis of the nine medical students in the pilot study; the number of correct answers increased from "a median of 16/20 (IQR 14–17) to 20/20" (p = 0.011).

> "In the sub-group of medical students, the number of correct answers increased from a median of 16/20 (IQR 14–17) to 20/20 (IQR 18–20; p = 0.011)."

### Mileder LP 2021 (2)

Mileder LP, Bereiter M, Wegscheider T. (2021). Telesimulation as a modality for neonatal resuscitation training. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1892017

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Sub-group analysis of the nine neonatal nurses; median correct answers rose from "17/20 (IQR 14–18)" to "20/20 (IQR 19–20)" after the session (p = 0.018).

> "Prior to the telesimulation session, nursing personnel had a median of 17/20 (IQR 14–18) correct answers on the questionnaire, compared to a median of 20/20 (IQR 19–20) afterwards (p = 0.018)."
