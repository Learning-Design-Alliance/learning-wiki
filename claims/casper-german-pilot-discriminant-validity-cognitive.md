## Evidence

### Knorr Mirjana 2024

Knorr Mirjana, Mielke Ina, Amelung Dorothee, Safari Mahla, Gröne Oana R., Breil Simon M., MacIntosh Alexander. (2024). Measuring personal characteristics in applicants to German medical schools: Piloting an online Situational Judgement Test with an open-ended response format. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001685.shtml

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations between Casper and three cognitive admission criteria in the pilot sample. The authors interpret the pattern as suggesting Casper also measures cognitive characteristics, pointing to "a somewhat higher cognitive load in Casper" than German SJTs.

> "Regarding cognitive admission measures, Casper performance had significant correlations with the Abitur grade (r=-.15, p=.01, n=354; i.e. the better the Abitur grade, the better Casper performance), TMS performance (r=.18, p=.001, n=371), and the logical reasoning subtest of the HAM-Nat (r=.23, p<.001, n=270)."

### Knorr Mirjana 2024 (2)

Knorr Mirjana, Mielke Ina, Amelung Dorothee, Safari Mahla, Gröne Oana R., Breil Simon M., MacIntosh Alexander. (2024). Measuring personal characteristics in applicants to German medical schools: Piloting an online Situational Judgement Test with an open-ended response format. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001685.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations with the HAM-Nat science knowledge and arithmetic problem solving subtests. The authors note the weak reliability of these subtests might have affected the significance and magnitude of correlations; equivalence was not tested.

> "On the other hand, it did not correlate with the HAM-Nat science (r=.04, p=.46, n=270) nor with the arithmetic problem solving subtest (r=.08, p=.18, n=270)"
