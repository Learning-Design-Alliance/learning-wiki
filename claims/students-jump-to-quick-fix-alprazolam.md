## Evidence

### Shenai Neeta MD 2024

Shenai Neeta MD, Fulmer Valerie, Gowl Catherine MFA, See Jordan MD, Peterson Ryan MD, Van Deusen Reed MD. (2024). A Verbal De-escalation Standardized Patient Workshop for Third- and Fourth-Year Medical Students. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11417

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative review of 12 randomly selected video-recorded encounters by two team members, documenting common student responses and behaviors. The review found students "jumped straight to fixing the problem before utilizing any other de-escalation technique."

> "In general, students commonly jumped straight to fixing the problem before utilizing any other de-escalation technique."

### Shenai Neeta MD 2024 (2)

Shenai Neeta MD, Fulmer Valerie, Gowl Catherine MFA, See Jordan MD, Peterson Ryan MD, Van Deusen Reed MD. (2024). A Verbal De-escalation Standardized Patient Workshop for Third- and Fourth-Year Medical Students. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.11417

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

From the same video review of 12 encounters: in the outpatient case, students "inappropriately promised them alprazolam," which the authors note de-escalated the patient quickly but not in a clinically appropriate manner.

> "On multiple occasions during the encounter where the patient was requesting a controlled substance to sleep, the students inappropriately promised them alprazolam, which led to the encounter ending prematurely."
