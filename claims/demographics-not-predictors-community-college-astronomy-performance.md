### Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly 2023

Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly. (2023). Predicting Community College Astronomy Performance through Logistic Regression. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119)

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in the independent-variable-selection section of an observational transcript study (N=1909). Printed correlations were negligible: gender rS=0.012 (p=0.613), ethnicity rS=0.000 (p=0.993), socioeconomic status rS=0.002 (p=0.915), age rS=-0.005 (p=0.841), so "None of the demographic variables were significant predictors of astronomy performance."
