## Evidence

### Cristi-Montero 2022

Cristi-Montero, C., Solis-Urra, P., Sanchez-Martinez, J., et al. as printed in full byline. (2022). Which one? A comparative study of traditional and sports uniforms on academic achievement, cognitive performance, playtime, bullying, and discrimination in adolescents: The Cogni-Action Project. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2022.917970

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of bullying feelings (daily stress scale items scored 1–4) in the 988-adolescent sample, model 2 adjusted for SVI. The article reports "no significant differences between uniform types in feeling of bullying"; a trend appeared in model 1 (p = 0.064). No effect size printed.

> "Figure 3A shows no significant differences between uniform types in feeling of bullying (random effect LTR was 8.20; p < 0.01; ICC = 0.026; see Supplementary material)."

### Cristi-Montero 2022 (2)

Cristi-Montero, C., Solis-Urra, P., Sanchez-Martinez, J., et al. as printed in full byline. (2022). Which one? A comparative study of traditional and sports uniforms on academic achievement, cognitive performance, playtime, bullying, and discrimination in adolescents: The Cogni-Action Project. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2022.917970

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of discrimination feelings in the same sample. Model 2 showed no significant difference between uniform types; model 1, without SVI, showed higher discrimination feelings in TU wearers (1.41 ± 0.05 vs 1.13 ± 0.11, p = 0.041). No effect size printed.

> "While Figure 3B displays no significant discrimination differences between groups in model 2 (random effect LTR was 7.60; p < 0.01; ICC = 0.056), a higher discrimination feeling was found in adolescents wearing TUs in model 1 (TUs: 1.41 ± 0.05 vs. SUs: 1.13 ± 0.11, p = 0.041)."
