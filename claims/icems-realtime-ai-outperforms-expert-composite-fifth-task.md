## Evidence

### Yilmaz Recai 2024

Yilmaz Recai, Bakhaidar Mohamad, Alsayegh Ahmad, Abou Hamdan Nour, Fazlollahi Ali M., Tee Trisha, Langleben Ian, Winkler-Schwartz Alexander, Laroche Denis, Santaguida Carlo, Del Maestro Rolando F. (2024). Real-Time multifaceted artificial intelligence vs In-Person instruction in teaching surgical technical skills: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-65716-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparisons from the three-arm RCT (n = 97 analyzed, intention-to-treat) using Kruskal–Wallis tests with Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons. The "ICEMS group outperformed both post hoc and expert instruction groups" on the fifth repetition of the practice task.

> "In the fifth task, the ICEMS group outperformed both post hoc and expert instruction groups, .266, 95% CI [.107 .425], p < .001 and .332, 95% CI [.173 .491], p = .005, respectively."

### Yilmaz Recai 2024 (2)

Yilmaz Recai, Bakhaidar Mohamad, Alsayegh Ahmad, Abou Hamdan Nour, Fazlollahi Ali M., Tee Trisha, Langleben Ian, Winkler-Schwartz Alexander, Laroche Denis, Santaguida Carlo, Del Maestro Rolando F. (2024). Real-Time multifaceted artificial intelligence vs In-Person instruction in teaching surgical technical skills: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-65716-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Omnibus two-way mixed model ANOVA on the ICEMS composite score across five practice repetitions in the RCT, reported with Greenhouse–Geisser correction. The article prints partial η2 = .097 for this interaction, a medium-sized effect by the η2 thresholds.

> "There was a statistically significant interaction between feedback allocation and task repetition in a two-way mixed model ANOVA on the ICEMS composite score, F(6.8, 319.5) = 5.06, p < .001, partial η2 = .097."
