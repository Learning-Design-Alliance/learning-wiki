# Half of CPS 2017 graduates who immediately enrolled in a four-year college completed a bachelor's degree within six years

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among the CPS class of 2017, 50.0% of immediate four-year college enrollees completed a bachelor's degree within six years, up about 2 percentage points since 2010. [→ Mahaffie 2024](#mahaffie-2024)
`q2 i?` Among immediate two-year enrollees, 8.1% completed a bachelor's degree and an additional 23.0% completed an associate degree or certificate within six years. [→ Mahaffie 2024 (2)](#mahaffie-2024-2)
`q2 i?` Among graduates who did not immediately enroll in college, only 1.8% completed a bachelor's degree within six years, and total completion declined to 4.3%. [→ Mahaffie 2024 (3)](#mahaffie-2024-3)

## Evidence

### Mahaffie 2024

Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., Nagaoka, J., & McKoy, D. (2024). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2023. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Six-year completion analysis of 9,987 immediate four-year enrollees from the CPS class of 2017 (Figure 11); bachelor's completion rose from 47.7% in 2010 to 50.0%, while associate/certificate-only completion fell to 4.4%.

> "One-half of 2017 CPS graduates (50.0%) who immediately enrolled in a four-year college completed a bachelor's degree within six years."

### Mahaffie 2024 (2)

Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., Nagaoka, J., & McKoy, D. (2024). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2023. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Six-year completion analysis of 4,820 immediate two-year enrollees from the CPS class of 2017 (Figure 12); the report notes 68.9% had not completed any credential within six years.

> "Among immediate two-year college enrollees, 8.1% of 2017 graduates completed a bachelor's degree within six years (by 2023), and an additional 23.0% completed an associate degree or certificate."

### Mahaffie 2024 (3)

Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., Nagaoka, J., & McKoy, D. (2024). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2023. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Six-year completion analysis of 8,217 delayed and non-enrollees from the CPS class of 2017 (Figure 13); total completion declined to 4.3%, continuing a decline since 2010.

> "Among CPS graduates who did not immediately enroll in college, only 1.8% completed a bachelor's degree within six years."
