### Weitz G 2014

Weitz G, Vinzentius C, Twesten C, Lehnert H, Bonnemeier H, König IR. (2014). Effects of a rater training on rating accuracy in a physical examination skills assessment. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000933](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000933)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Video-based re-assessment of 218 recorded examinations by three independent observers (one faculty member, two fifth-year students): the faculty member graded more stringently, matching the group median in 30% of ratings versus 61% and 75% for the students.

> "Comparing the grades among observers, the faculty's median grade [1st;3rd quartile] was more stringent than the students' grades (2- [2+;3] versus 2 [1-;2-] both)."
