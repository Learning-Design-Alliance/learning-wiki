## Evidence

### Bouwer 2018

Bouwer, R., Koster, M., & van den Bergh, H. (2018). Effects of a Strategy-Focused Instructional Program on the Writing Quality of Upper Elementary Students in the Netherlands. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(1), 58–71. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000206

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Specific contrast analysis of Group 1 students, who completed Tekster in the first 8-week phase and were retested at M3 as a delayed posttest. Scores stayed significantly above pretest and showed no significant decline from posttest; no effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "There was a statistically significant increase in students’ scores between M1 and M3, /H92732(1) /H1100523.14, p /H11021.001, but the difference between M2 and M3 was not statistically significant, /H92732(1) /H110052.06, p /H11005.15."
