## Evidence

### Arzu Akbaba 2026

Arzu Akbaba, Afra Calik Kus. (2026). Nursing students’ and faculty experiences with artificial intelligence in education: a qualitative study using the technology acceptance model. BMC Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12912-026-04986-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview findings from the discussion of concerns; faculty worried about "excessive reliance on AI", and students reported inaccurate pharmacological information, raising patient-safety concerns. Participants also cited premium-access costs creating inequality.

> "Faculty members often expressed concern that excessive reliance on AI could weaken analytical reasoning and academic integrity. Students reported finding inaccurate information, especially about pharmacological content."
