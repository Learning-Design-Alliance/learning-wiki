## Evidence

### Jacob Whitehill and Margo Seltzer 2017

Jacob Whitehill and Margo Seltzer. (2017). A Crowdsourcing Approach To Collecting Tutorial Videos – Toward Personalized Learning-at-Scale. [doi:10.1145/3051457.3053973](https://doi.org/10.1145/3051457.3053973)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment III: N = 250 Mechanical Turk participants were assigned uniformly at random to one of four crowdsourced videos (those estimated best in Experiment II) or a popular Khan Academy logarithm video. The printed means and t-test are as reported in Table 1 and Results.

> "the learning gains associated with the Khan Academy video compared to the best of the 4 crowdsourced videos were very similar – 0.1506 versus 0.1416, respectively. The difference between them was not statistically significant (t(114) = 0.2277, p = 0.82, two-tailed)."
