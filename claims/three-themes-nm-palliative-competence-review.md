`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic mixed studies review following PRISMA, registered in PROSPERO, screening 6289 initially retrieved articles plus 489 from an updated search. Narrative synthesis of eight included studies produced the three named themes; the review reports "three themes emerged from the narrative synthesis".

> "Eight studies met the inclusion criteria and three themes emerged from the narrative synthesis: (1) Conceptual grasp of palliative care, (2) Recognition of gaps and needs, and (3) Competence in fulfilling complex responsibilities."
