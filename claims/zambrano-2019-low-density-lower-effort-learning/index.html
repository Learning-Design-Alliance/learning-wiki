Evidence¶

Jimmy Zambrano 2019¶

Jimmy Zambrano, Femke Kirschner, John Sweller, Paul A. Kirschner. (2019). Effects of group experience and information distribution on collaborative learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-019-09495-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Learning-phase ANOVA of the 2×2 experiment, mental effort rated on a 9-point Paas scale averaged across group members: "groups with low information density (M = 6.80, SD = 1.80) perceived lower mental effort than groups with high information density (M = 7.89, SD = 1.68)".

"For mental effort, groups with low information density (M = 6.80, SD = 1.80) perceived lower mental effort than groups with high information density (M = 7.89, SD = 1.68)."

Learning-phase efficiency contrast in the same ANOVA series: within this sentence "low information density (M = .29, SD = 1.09) was more effi- cient than high information density (M = − .30, SD = 1.11)"; no effect size is printed for this contrast here.
