## Evidence

### Zhan Leilei 2026

Zhan Leilei, Liang Ruiyin, Li Yimin, Huang Nuanqin. (2026). Research on the application of scenario-based simulation ward rounds combined with video feedback method in standardized residency training of gastroenterology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09434-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Open-label randomized parallel-group controlled trial with 80 gastroenterology residents randomized 1:1 to intervention or control. Independent t-test showed the intervention group scored significantly higher, with "the difference was statistically significant (t = 10.980, P < 0.001)". Baseline scores did not differ (P = 0.714).

> "After the training, the average score of the control group was (80.20 ± 2.30) points, while that of the intervention group was (87.10 ± 3.24) points. These results indicate that both groups improved their teaching ward round assessment scores after the training; however, the intervention group scored significantly higher than the control group, and the difference was statistically significant (t = 10.980, P < 0.001)"
