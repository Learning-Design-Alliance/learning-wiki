## Evidence

### Ramasamy 2011

Ramasamy, S. (2011). An Analysis of Informal Reasoning Fallacy and Critical Thinking Dispositions among Malaysian Undergraduates. Asia e University. https://www.aeu.edu.my

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Composite CCTDI results for the 189-respondent sample, using the interpretive guidelines of positive disposition at 280 and above and ambivalence at 210-279. The overall CCTDI mean was 277.11 (SD 24.39), range 226 to 363.

> "Eighty eight students (47%) scored above 280 points, which indicates positive disposition towards critical thinking, 101 (53%) scored between 210 and 280 which indicate ambivalence towards critical thinking. No respondents scored lower than 210, which indicate that there is no significant opposition to critical thinking."
