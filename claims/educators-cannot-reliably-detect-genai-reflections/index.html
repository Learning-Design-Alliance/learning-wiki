q2 · i? · design · r1

The review reports this educator detection study, citing Wraith et al. [45], finding "low to moderate sensitivity and specificity across various reflections". The review notes the study's major limitation is its small sample size of only 28 educators and a limited set of four reflection types. No effect size is printed.

"educators struggled to reliably dif- ferentiate between reflections written by medical students and those generated by GenAI, with low to moderate sensitivity and specificity across various reflections [45]."
