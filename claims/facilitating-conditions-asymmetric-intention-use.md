# Facilitating conditions showed an asymmetric pattern: no significant effect on behavioral intention but a significant positive effect on use behavior

## Evidence

### Xin Tian 2026

Xin Tian, Musa Adekunle Ayanwale, Rethabile Rosemary Molefi, Preeti Manchanda, Nurgün Gençel and Barakat Olabisi Ogunjoun. (2026). Decoding symmetric and asymmetric pathways in generative AI learning adoption: a multi-method study. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00613-w

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM structural results (Table 8) from the 842-student Lesotho survey show the asymmetric facilitating-conditions pattern, with f2 = 0.009 for the intention path and f2 = 0.033 for the use-behavior path as printed in the table.

> "Although the direct effect of facilitating conditions on behavioral intention (H4a) was not significant (β = 0.062, t = 1.642, p = 0.050), facilitating conditions exerted a significant positive effect on use behavior (H4b: β = 0.077, t = 2.194, p = 0.014)."
