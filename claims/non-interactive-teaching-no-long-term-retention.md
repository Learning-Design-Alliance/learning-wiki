`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

The IPD synthesis used a longitudinal design with a delayed posttest administered eight weeks after the intervention; the synthesis reports the intervention "did not enhance long-term retention" despite immediate gains.

> "Results showed that non-interactive teaching resulted in higher immediate scientific knowledge and stronger under-confidence compared to restudy but did not enhance long-term retention."
