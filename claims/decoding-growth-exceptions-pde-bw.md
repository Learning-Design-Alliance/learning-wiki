## Evidence

### Little 2021

Little, C. W., Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2021). Differential Patterns of Growth in Reading and Math Skills during Elementary School. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 462-476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000635

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the two-stage latent-growth-curve results in the 921-child sample. TOWRE PDE was the exception to the dissipation of reading-growth individual differences after grade 2; the authors suggest mastery of the alphabetic principle and processing speed demands may contribute. No effect size was printed.

> "For TOWRE PDE-pseudo word decoding, significant variance in growth was present in both early and late elementary school, indicating individual differences in growth of this skill persisted across the elementary school years, rather than dissipating in third through fifth grades"

### Little 2021 (2)

Little, C. W., Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2021). Differential Patterns of Growth in Reading and Math Skills during Elementary School. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 462-476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000635

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion of the same growth-curve results: CTOPP Blending Words showed no growth-variability at any point. The authors note slightly lower standard deviations for this task suggesting some restriction of range, though no floor or ceiling effects were detected.

> "For CTOPP BW-phonological awareness, no significant variance in growth was detected throughout the elementary years, indicating no individual differences were present for growth on this task, even in early elementary school"
