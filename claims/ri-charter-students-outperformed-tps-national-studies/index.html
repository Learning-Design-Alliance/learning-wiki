Margaret E. Raymond 2025¶

Margaret E. Raymond, PhD; Marzena Sasnal, PhD. (2025). The Impact of Rhode Island's Charter School Performance Review System on the Education Landscape. Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/rhode-island-2025/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

This is background cited by the report, attributed to CREDO's National Charter School Study 2013 and the National Charter School Study III 2023; the reviewed studies' designs are not described in this document. The report states charter students "outperformed their peers in traditional public schools (TPS)" with several additional weeks of learning yearly in reading and math. No effect sizes are printed here.

"National studies conducted in 20133 and 20234 indicate that charter students in the state outperformed their peers in traditional public schools (TPS), achieving several additional weeks of learning each year in reading and math."
