## Evidence

### Albina Zavgorodniaia 2021

Albina Zavgorodniaia, Raj Shrestha, Juho Leinonen, Arto Hellas, John Edwards. (2021). Morning or Evening? An Examination of Circadian Rhythms of CS1 Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U and chi-squared tests comparing weekday and weekend keystroke distributions for each chronotype, shifted by 7 hours for unimodality. All effect sizes were "0.2 or less"; the authors interpret this as external factors having little effect on when students work.

> "Both tests for each of the four chronotypes yielded p < 0.0001. However, all effect sizes (Cohen's d for Mann-Whitney U and Cramer's V for chi-squared) were 0.2 or less, so, while behavior is different between weekday and weekend, the difference is small."
