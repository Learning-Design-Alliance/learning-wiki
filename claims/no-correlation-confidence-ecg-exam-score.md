`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of the 52 enrolled students' baseline survey confidence ratings and baseline test scores; no correlation reached significance in the overall population or either group.

> "There was no significant correlation between baseline confidence in ECG interpretation and baseline exam scores (coefficient correlation of rs = 0.15, p = 0.28 in the overall population; rs = 0.20, p = 0.33 in the workshop group and rs = 0.03, p = 0.87 in the control group)."
