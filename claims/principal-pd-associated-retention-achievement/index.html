The report cites this California study as background evidence motivating leadership investment; the report attributes the finding to the study's researchers and describes it only as summarized here. No effect size is printed.

"A recent study in California examined the outcomes associated with principals’ access to professional development and found that the frequency and content of principals’ professional learning opportunities is significantly associated with teacher retention and student achievement."
