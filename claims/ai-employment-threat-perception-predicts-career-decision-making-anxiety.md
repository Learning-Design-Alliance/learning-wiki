Chen H (2026) When AI threatens my future career: growth mindset and perceived employability in Chinese college students' career decision making anxiety. Front. Psychol. 17:1933842. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1933842

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 526 college students at a university in Zhejiang Province, China (effective response rate 87.67%). Hierarchical regression with six control variables showed "AI employment threat perception significantly and positively predicted career decision making anxiety" (b = 0.240, p < 0.001), supporting H1.
