Adjusted logistic regressions on LISA register data for the full cohort of 2 238 837, with milestone-specific subcohorts defined by age cut-offs. The article reports "81% lower odds of finishing upper secondary education" with the printed aORs for each milestone assessed.

> "SAD cases had 81% lower odds of finishing upper secondary education [aOR = 0.19 (95% CI 0.19–0.20)], 53% lower odds of starting a university degree [aOR = 0.47 (95% CI 0.45–0.49)], 65% lower odds of obtaining a university degree [aOR = 0.35 (95% CI 0.33–0.37)]"
