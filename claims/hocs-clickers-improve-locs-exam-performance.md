# HOCS clicker questions during learning improve performance on LOCS exam questions more than LOCS clicker questions do

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across two semesters, units taught with HOCS clicker questions produced better LOCS exam performance than units taught with LOCS clicker questions (d = 0.47). [→ Deborah K. Anderson 2023](#deborah-k-anderson-2023)

## Evidence

### Deborah K. Anderson 2023

Deborah K. Anderson, Michelle Schoenleber, and Stuart Korshavn. (2023). Higher-Order Clicker Questions Engage Students and Prepare Them for Higher-Order Thinking Activities. Journal of Microbiology and Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1128/jmbe.00151-22

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Counterbalanced quasi-experiment over two semesters of a nonmajors Human Biology course; paired-sample t tests across both semesters compared the four unit-level exam scores. The study found "HOCS clicker questions contributed to better performance on LOCS exam questions than did LOCS clicker questions" with a small-to-medium effect size (d = 0.47).

> "HOCS clicker questions contributed to better performance on LOCS exam questions than did LOCS clicker questions (M LCL = 72% versus MHCL = 76%; t [52] = 4.25, P < 0.001, Cohen’s d = 0.47)"
