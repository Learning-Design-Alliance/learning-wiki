## Evidence

### Ali 2025

Ali, F., Wang, Y., Wang, S. J.-W. & Zhu, G. (2025). Triggers of curiosity in social constructivist classroom discourse. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00330-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The exploratory third-grade DAG found "non-scaffolding utterances (T:NS1) seemed to trigger curiosity"; qualitative analysis showed these were affirmations like "good question" and safe invitations like "sure" that supported students' self-expression and further questioning.

> "In third grade, non-scaffolding utterances (T:NS1) seemed to trigger curiosity, and, unlike in second grade, teacher restating/revoicing did not play a role in third grade."
