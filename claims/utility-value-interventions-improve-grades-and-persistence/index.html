q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Harackiewicz et al. (2015), a utility-value intervention with college students including first-generation and underrepresented minority students; "Intervention group students' grades improved more than did the control group students", reducing the achievement gap.

"Harackiewicz et al. (2015) implemented a utility value intervention in different groups of college students, including both first-generation and underrepresented minority students. Intervention group students' grades improved more than did the control group students."

q4 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Asher et al. (2023) as a large-scale RCT of a utility-value intervention in a gateway chemistry course; the intervention "improved college students' per- sistence in STEM fields 2.5 years later", with effects stronger for underrepresented minority students.

"implemented in a gateway chemistry course improved college students' per- sistence in STEM fields 2.5 years later, a rather remarkable finding."
