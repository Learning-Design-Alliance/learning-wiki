## Evidence

### Sidney L. Johnson 2023

Sidney L. Johnson, Athanasios Hatzikoutelis, and Christopher L. Smallwood. (2023). Reflections on the Physics and Astronomy Student Reading Society (PhASRS) at San José State University. [doi:10.1119/5.0100363](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0100363)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interview reflections from fall 2021. Chin described the environment as an "easy-going, non-stressful environment to learn"; Hawksley noted the voluntary course and lack of severe penalties made it okay to say "I don't know, I'm not an expert." Students reported it built a cohort that later took classes and formed study groups together.

> "The main selling point of the program is that it was an easy-going, non-stressful environment to learn"

### Sidney L. Johnson 2023 (2)

Sidney L. Johnson, Athanasios Hatzikoutelis, and Christopher L. Smallwood. (2023). Reflections on the Physics and Astronomy Student Reading Society (PhASRS) at San José State University. [doi:10.1119/5.0100363](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0100363)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

From the same interview set: students in the fall felt the time crunch more acutely, with presentations assembled as midterms loomed; Chin called PhASRS "kind of like an 'extra' on top of school." Faculty mentor Wharton noted carving out an hour was challenging.

> "This lack of time was in fact the biggest reason that students gave that would stop them from participating again in the program."
