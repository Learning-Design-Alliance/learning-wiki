Leung-Gagné, M., McCombs, J., Scott, C., & Losen, D. J. (2022). Pushed out: Trends and disparities in out-of-school suspension. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/235.277

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CRDC analysis of intersecting identities and suspension risk. The report contrasts the 0.1% rate for "Asian girls not receiving special education services in elementary schools" with 27% for Black boys with disabilities in secondary schools.

> "in 2017–18, 1 in 1,000 Asian girls not receiving special education services in elementary schools were suspended (0.1%). However, during that same school year, more than 1 in 4 Black boys with disabilities (27%) in secondary schools were suspended."
