## Evidence

### Şahin Eda Adeviye 2026

Şahin Eda Adeviye, Şahin Turan, Ağlamış Savaş Özgür, Tekin Arzu Bilge, Şahin Hanifi. (2026). Outcomes of first 60 vaginal myomectomies by one gyn surgeon with 20 years of extensive vaginal surgical expertise. BMC Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12905-026-04715-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Grouped comparison of the same 60-case series, with hospitalization recorded in hours. Group 1 mean was 52.8 ± 13.4 h versus 32.0–36.8 h in later groups (p = 0.001); "hospitalization duration decreased by 12.0 h (95% CI, 4.8–19.2 h)".

> "Similarly, hospitalization duration decreased by 12.0 h (95% CI, 4.8–19.2 h), also demonstrating a large effect size (Cohen's d = 0.84)."
