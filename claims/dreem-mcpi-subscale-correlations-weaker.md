# DREEM and MCPI subscale correlations are weaker than the total-score correlation (atmosphere–learning r = 0.63; teachers–training r = 0.50)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The DREEM perceptions of atmosphere subscale correlates strongly with the MCPI learning environment subscale (r = 0.63), and the DREEM perceptions of teachers subscale correlates moderately with the MCPI training subscale (r = 0.50). [→ Hyde Sarah 2018](#hyde-sarah-2018)

## Evidence

### Hyde Sarah 2018

Hyde Sarah, Hannigan Ailish, Dornan Tim, McGrath Deirdre. (2018). Medical school clinical placements – the optimal method for assessing the clinical educational environment from a graduate entry perspective. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1113-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same survey analysis (n = 154); Pearson correlations between matched subscales, pairing atmosphere with learning environment and teachers with training. Both correlations were statistically significant, with r = 0.63 and r = 0.50 as printed.

> "There was a strong, positive correlation between the DREEM perceptions of atmosphere subscale and MCPI learning environment subscale (r = 0.63, p < 0.001). The correlation between subscales DREEM perceptions of teachers and MCPI training was moderate (r = 0.50, p < 0.001)."
