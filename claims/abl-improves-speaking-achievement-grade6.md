### Zahoor-UL-HAQ 2017

Zahoor-UL-HAQ, Khurram, B. A., & Bangash, A. K. (2017). Development of Speaking Skills through Activity Based Learning at the Elementary Level. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 69, 241-252. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2017.69.13

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pretest-posttest equivalent group experiment: 50 male students randomly selected from two sections, 25 per group; experimental group taught via ABL, control via traditional method for seven weeks. Post-test means were 21.72 (SD 4.05) versus 10.48 (SD 5.40); "the obtained result of t was 8.319". No effect size printed.
