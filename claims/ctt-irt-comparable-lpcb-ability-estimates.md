## Evidence

### Purwoko Haryadi Santoso 2024

Purwoko Haryadi Santoso, Edi Istiyono, Haryanto, Heri Retnawati. (2024). Validating Light Phenomena Conceptual Assessment Through The Lens of CTT and IRT Framework. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Re-analysis of the LPCA pretest data from 283 Rwandan secondary school students, comparing CTT total-score ability estimates with 2-PL IRT theta estimates after z-transformation. The article reports an "identical distribution of students' ability" (CTT skewness = 0.32, IRT skewness = 0.25) and the non-significant t-test quoted above.

> "Using a two -tailed 𝑡 test, it was found that the difference between the means was not statistically significant (𝑡 = 8.638e-16, 𝑑𝑓 = 564, 𝑝 > 0.05)."
