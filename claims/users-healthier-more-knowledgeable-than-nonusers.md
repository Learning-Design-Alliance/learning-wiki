## Evidence

### Kelders SM 2011

Kelders SM, Van Gemert-Pijnen JEWC, Werkman A, Nijland N, Seydel ER. (2011). Effectiveness of a Web-based Intervention Aimed at Healthy Dietary and Physical Activity Behavior: A Randomized Controlled Trial About Users and Usage. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1624

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparisons between users (n=81) and nonusers (n=46) within the intervention group using chi-square and ANOVA. Table 4 shows 35% of users versus 13% of nonusers had healthy diets; knowledge means were 7.9 versus 7.4.

> "Within the intervention group, users scored better on dietary behavior and on knowledge about healthy behavior than nonusers (self-reported diet: χ2 2 = 8.4, P = .02; knowledge: F1,125 = 4.194, P = .04)."
