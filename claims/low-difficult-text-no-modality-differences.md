## Evidence

### Jacob 2019

Jacob, L., Lachner, A., & Scheiter, K. (2019). Learning by Explaining Orally or In Written Form? Text Difficulty Matters. https://aspredicted.org/sv3zv.pdf

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

ANCOVA on text-based questions from the same mixed-design experiment. The article reports no main effect of learning activity and no interaction, "indicating that the learning activity groups performed comparable on the text-based questions"; equivalence was not directly tested.

> "There was no main effect of learning activity, F(1, 111) = 0.78, p = .459, η²p = .007, and no interaction effect between text difficulty and learning activity, F(2, 111) = 0.58, p = .563, η²p  = .003, indicating that the learning activity groups performed comparable on the text-based questions."
