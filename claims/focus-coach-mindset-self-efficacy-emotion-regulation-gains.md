## Evidence

### Michael D. Mrazek 2026

Michael D. Mrazek, Dustyn S. Ransom, Amy R. Texter, Calvin V. Tower, Bryce A. Rutila, Thomas F. Kennedy, Elijah J. Woodson, Jonathan W. Schooler, Alissa J. Mrazek. (2026). Theory-driven design and validation of a tool to enhance academic focus. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10730-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 1 pre-post survey analysis found growth mindset and self-efficacy gains (Table 1: SMD = 0.30 and 0.58, p < .001), replicated in Study 2 (Table 2: SMD = 0.34 and 0.60). These outcomes replicated hypotheses from prior course-only research.

> "From pre-test to post-test, students reported a higher growth mindset regarding their ability to focus and felt greater self-efficacy in knowing how to improve this ability (Table 1)."

### Michael D. Mrazek 2026 (2)

Michael D. Mrazek, Dustyn S. Ransom, Amy R. Texter, Calvin V. Tower, Bryce A. Rutila, Thomas F. Kennedy, Elijah J. Woodson, Jonathan W. Schooler, Alissa J. Mrazek. (2026). Theory-driven design and validation of a tool to enhance academic focus. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10730-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 1 measured emotion regulation with the ERQ-CA cognitive reappraisal subscale; Table 1 reports b = 0.20, p < .001, SMD = 0.24. The ERQ was removed in Study 2 to shorten the survey, so this outcome was not replicated.

> "From pre-test to post-test, students reported higher levels of emotional regulation via reappraisal (Table 1)."
