Arens, A. Katrin; Schmidt, Isabelle; Preckel, Franzis. (2019). Longitudinal relations among self-concept, intrinsic value, and attainment value across secondary school years in three academic domains. The Journal of educational psychology 111 (2019) 4, S. 663-684. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000313

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analyses across 5 annual measurement waves covering Grades 5 to 9 with German secondary school students (N = 2,116) showed the cross-lagged relations "did not change in size across students' secondary school years."

> "The relations among academic self-concept, intrinsic value, and attainment value in the 3 domains did not change in size across students' secondary school years."
