# Self-assessed learning progress is strongly stratified by baseline level: students rating themselves 1 or 2 at T0 improved by 2.2 points on average, those rating 3 by 1.1, and those rating 4 or 5 by 0.1

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a baseline-stratified analysis, average improvement across all items was 2.2 points for students with T0 values of 1 or 2, 1.1 for those with 3, and 0.1 for those with 4 or 5. [→ von Lengerke Thomas 2011](#von-lengerke-thomas-2011)

## Evidence

### von Lengerke Thomas 2011

von Lengerke Thomas, Kursch Angelika, Lange Karin, APG-Lehrteam MHH. (2011). The communication skills course for second year medical students at Hannover Medical School: An evaluation study based on students' self-assessments. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 28(4), Doc54. [doi:10.3205/zma000766](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000766)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Stratified pre-post analysis of the same T0–T1 self-assessment data, in which students "with the values 1 or 2 on average improved by 2.2 points across all items"; the stratified results are reported as not shown in a table or figure.

> "students who on the respective scales had rated themselves with the values 1 or 2 on average improved by 2.2 points across all items, those who had rated themselves with 3 improved by 1.1 points, and those with 4 or 5 by 0.1 (not shown)."
