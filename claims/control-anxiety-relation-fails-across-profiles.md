### Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu 2026

Dirk Tempelaar · Alexandra Niculescu. (2026). Testing the Relative Universalism of Psychological Theories: A Person-Centered Analysis of Control-Value Theory. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10155-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Mixture regression (SEM within latent profiles) of control-value relations. The article reports control–anxiety unstandardized coefficients falling "from − 0.686 in the full sample" to a range across profiles that includes non-significant positive effects, a directional reversal inconsistent with CVT.

> "The most notable deviation from universality concerns the relation between Academic Control and Anxiety: unstandardized coefficients decrease from − 0.686 in the full sample (with similar values across demographic groups) to values ranging from − 0.292 to + 0.024 across profiles, including non-significant positive effects."
