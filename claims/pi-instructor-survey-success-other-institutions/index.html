Crouch 2003¶

Crouch, C. H., Watkins, J., Fagen, A. P., & Mazur, E. (2003). Peer Instruction: Engaging Students One-on-One, All At Once. Research-Based Reform of University Physics. https://scholar.google.com

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey of PI users at other institutions conducted by the authors, gathering data on gains in student understanding and implementation information. The article reports that "most of these instructors have had success using PI" and characterizes their students' conceptual level relative to interactive-engagement courses.

"We find that most of these instructors have had success using PI, and that their students understand basic mechanics concepts at the level characteristic of courses taught with interactive engagement methods."
