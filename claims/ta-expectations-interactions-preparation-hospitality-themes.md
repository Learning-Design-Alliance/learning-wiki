## Evidence

### Wilson 2025

Wilson, D., & Kardam, N. (2025). What do students expect from teaching assistants (TAs)? Considerations of gender, race, and international status. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0334904

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of short-answer survey responses from 1,678 undergraduate engineering students at a single large institution (2016-2023). The study reports "interactions (42.9%), followed by preparation (37.6%), and care and hospitality (7.61%)" as the primary theme distribution; inter-rater Cohen's kappa was 0.815.

> "Of all themes assigned to student responses, students most often expressed preferences for TA support associated with interactions (42.9%), followed by preparation (37.6%), and care and hospitality (7.61%)."
