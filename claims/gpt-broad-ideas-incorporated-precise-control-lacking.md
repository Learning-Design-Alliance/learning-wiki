# GPT responses reliably incorporated broad ideas from instructions but resisted precise control

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students observed that the only consistent relationship between cGPT materials and instructions and GPT responses was that broad ideas included in instructions would likely appear in responses, while identical instructions could still yield divergent response structures. [→ Lo S 2026](#lo-s-2026)

## Evidence

### Lo S 2026

Lo S, Lea T, Mavromoustakos A, Joseph B, Yii M, Exintaris B, Karunaratne N, Pearson DL, Ritchie T, Yuriev E and Pyun J (2026) Co-creating custom GPTs: an autoethnographic study of undergraduate students as partners in generative AI innovation. Front. Educ. 11:1818781. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1818781

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Inductive thematic analysis of summer-scholarship SPAR logs, particularly the consolidation phase where GPTs with identical instructions diverged in response structure. Students interpreted this as generative AI internalising high-level patterns while remaining resistant to precise control.

> "The only consistent relationship between GPT materials and instructions, and GPT responses, is if a broad idea is included, then it will likely appear in a response"
