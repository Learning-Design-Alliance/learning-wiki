Evidence¶

Torgerson et al. 2016¶

Carole Torgerson, Hannah Ainsworth, Hannah Buckley, Gillen Hampden-Thompson, Catherine Hewitt, Deborah Humphry, Laura Jefferson, Natasha Mitchell, David Torgerson. (2016). Affordable Online Maths Tuition: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581116

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis repeating the primary model using data only from pupils eligible for free school meals, as required by the funder. Table 1 reports an effect size of -0.08 (95% CI -1.23 to 0.74), described as "-1" estimated month of progress under the original conversion. The report cautions the trial was not powered to detect interactions.

"Affordable Maths vs. control group (FSM) -0.08 (-1.23 to 0.74)"
