Price 2024¶

Price, H., Burns, D., Loewe, S., Shields, P., Kaplan, J., & Lee, H. (2024). Long-term English learners in California. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/496.998

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of CELDT data for students designated LTEL7 in 2022–23, all of whom began schooling when California used CELDT. The report states they were "50% more likely to enter the California schooling system scoring at the most basic" level. No inferential test reported.

"students designated as LTEL7 were 50% more likely to enter the California schooling system scoring at the most basic "Beginning" English proficiency level compared to their other ever-EL peers (64% vs. 40%, respectively; see figure 11)."
