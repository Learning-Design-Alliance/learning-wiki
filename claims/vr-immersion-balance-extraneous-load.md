## Evidence

### Jordan Talan 2025

Jordan Talan, MHPE, MD; Forster Molly, MD; Joseph Leian, MD; Pradhan Deepak, MHPE, MD. (2025). Exploring the Role of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation in Health Professions Education: Thematic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/62803

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 4 of the thematic analysis reports immersion's benefits: participants valued VR for increasing presence, stimulating curiosity, and minimizing distractions more than other simulation technologies.

> "At its best, immersion increased learner presence, stimulated intellectual curiosity, and accelerated learning."

### Jordan Talan 2025 (2)

Jordan Talan, MHPE, MD; Forster Molly, MD; Joseph Leian, MD; Pradhan Deepak, MHPE, MD. (2025). Exploring the Role of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation in Health Professions Education: Thematic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/62803

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 4 reports that unnecessary environmental elements and unintuitive controller interactions created extraneous cognitive load; participants found an ideal balance when the virtual world accomplished learning objectives without frustrating navigation.

> "However, immersion could also create extraneous cognitive load, detracting from learning. Participants described unnecessary environmental elements that distracted from learning objectives, along with some tasks that were frustrating to simulate in VR."
