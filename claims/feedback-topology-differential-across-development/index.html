Gibson 2025¶

Gibson, D. C., Azukas, M. E., & Yilmaz Soylu, M. (2025). A Taxonomy of Metacognitive Learning Scenarios in Professional Contexts: Integrating Systems Theory with Empirical Constraints. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24142 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.24142

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Cross-cutting pattern analysis of the 24 priority scenarios in the results section. The authors present this as a pattern "not explicitly predicted by existing theories", indicating that metacognitive development involves increasingly sophisticated environmental engagement, a dimension they argue is inadequately theorized.
