### Wang 2026

Wang, V. (2026). California's universal prekindergarten implementation, 2024–25. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/540.887

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Annual survey trend on facility capacity (Figure 4). LEAs lacking space reported needing 185 additional classrooms statewide for 2025–26, down from 488 and 946 in prior years.

> "when LEAs were asked in 2024–25 if they expected to have sufficient space to meet projected enrollment by 2025–26, 95% reported having enough classroom space, compared to 88% in 2023–24 and 82% in 2022–23"

### Wang 2026 (2)

Wang, V. (2026). California's universal prekindergarten implementation, 2024–25. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/540.887

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Thematic analysis of LEAs' open-ended responses on biggest UPK challenges. Respondents described limited age-appropriate restrooms, outdoor learning areas, portable classrooms, and classrooms lacking direct bathroom access creating logistical and safety concerns.

> "About one fourth of LEAs mentioned facilities when describing their biggest challenge facing UPK implementation."
