Mike Verostek, Molly Griston, Jes´us Botello, Benjamin Zwickl. (2022). Making expert processes visible: how and why theorists use assumptions and analogies in their research. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020143](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020143)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of retrospective interviews with 11 theoretical physicists; themes of analogy use emerged from coding all interviews with a revised analogy/assumption codebook, and the authors report theorists "deliberately sought out or constructed analogies."

> "We found that theorists used analogies to generate new project ideas as well as overcome conceptual challenges."
