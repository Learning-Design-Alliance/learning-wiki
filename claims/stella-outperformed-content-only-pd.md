# STeLLA content-plus-pedagogy PD produced greater student science gains than content-only training

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students of teachers in the STeLLA program achieved greater science learning gains than students whose teachers received content training only, and STeLLA teachers retained their content learning while content-only teachers did not. [→ Darling-Hammond 2017](#darling-hammond-2017)

## Evidence

### Darling-Hammond 2017

Darling-Hammond, L., Hyler, M. E., Gardner, M. (2017). Effective Teacher Professional Development. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/teacher-prof-dev. [doi:10.54300/122.311](https://doi.org/10.54300/122.311)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Comparison study by Roth et al. (2011) of STeLLA teachers versus teachers receiving only 44 hours of science content PD not connected to classroom contexts; gains measured by pre- and post-test science content exams. The report notes "STeLLA teachers outperformed the content-only teachers" on retention of content learning.

> "Results of the study showed that teachers who participated in the STeLLA program had students who achieved greater learning gains than comparison students whose teachers received content training only, as determined by pre- and post-test science content exams."
