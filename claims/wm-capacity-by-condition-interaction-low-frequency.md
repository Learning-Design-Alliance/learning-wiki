### Yicong Zheng 2023 (2)

Yicong Zheng, Pengyuan Sun and Xiaonan L. Liu. (2023). Retrieval practice is costly and is beneficial only when working memory capacity is abundant. npj Science of Learning 8:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00159-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Simple slopes analysis of the LF Condition by WM interaction, visualized in Fig. 2 of the 30-participant experiment; the lower-WM result is a non-significant trend, not a confirmed reversal.

> "participants with higher WM capacity (+ 1 SD) exhibited a signiﬁcant testing effect ( b = 0.50, z = 2.43, p = 0.015), whereas participants with lower WM capacity ( −1 SD) exhibited a trend towards a negative testing effect (b = −0.22, z = −1.15, p = 0.249, Fig. 2)."
