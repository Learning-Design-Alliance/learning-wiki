## Evidence

### Schripsema NR 2016

Schripsema NR, van Trigt AM, van der Wal MA, Cohen-Schotanus J. (2016). How Different Medical School Selection Processes Call upon Different Personality Characteristics. PLoS ONE 11(3): e0150645. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0150645

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA (gender as covariate) of NEO-FFI scores in 1019 Groningen medical students across four admission groups; Bonferroni post hoc comparisons showed "a higher mean extraversion score than all other groups" (MD 1.38–2.60, p<0.01). No standardized effect size printed.

> "The group that was accepted in the multifaceted selection process had a higher mean extraversion score than all other groups (MD: 1.38–2.60; SE:0.42–0.53; p<0.01)."
