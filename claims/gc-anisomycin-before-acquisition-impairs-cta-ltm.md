## Evidence

### Levitan 2016

Levitan, D., Gal-Ben-Ari, S., Heise, C., Rosenberg, T., Elkobi, A., Inberg, S., Sala, C. & Rosenblum, K. (2016). The differential role of cortical protein synthesis in taste memory formation and persistence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled rat experiment: anisomycin or vehicle was infused into the GC 20 min before CTA acquisition, and aversion index was measured at test. The article reports "signi ﬁcantly lower aversion toward saccharin" in the anisomycin group (n = 6 per group, t10 = 6.3, P = 8.9E − 5, t test). No effect size is printed.
