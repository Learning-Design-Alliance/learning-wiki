Bivariate correlation analysis across MAC, self-efficacy and methodology dimensions in the survey of 607 aquatic educators; Table 1 reports coefficients such as r = .67 between game and interpersonal style. No effect sizes beyond the printed correlations are interpreted here.

> "Correlation analysis revealed the existence of a positive and significant relationship between all the dimensions (p < .01), except between the game and behaviourist dimensions (p < .01), which was negative and significant."
