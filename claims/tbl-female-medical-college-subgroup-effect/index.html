Evidence¶

Chen Minjian 2018¶

Chen Minjian, Ni Chunhui, Hu Yanhui, Wang Meilin, Liu Lu, Ji Xiaoming, Chu Haiyan, Wu Wei, Lu Chuncheng, Wang Shouyu, Wang Shoulin, Zhao Liping, Li Zhong, Zhu Huijuan, Wang Jianming, Xia Yankai, Wang Xinru. (2018). Meta-analysis on the effectiveness of team-based learning on medical education in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1179-1

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analysis of 3 female-only medical college (3-year program) studies, motivated by meta-regression identifying gender as a potential heterogeneity source (P = 0.059). The fixed-effects pooled effect was "SMD = 0.47, 95% CI: 0.24–0.70" with I2 = 9.4%.

"There was no heterogeneity in the pooled analysis of the medical college students of only females (I2 = 9.4%, Pheterogeneity = 0.332, Fig. 4, Table 5), in which we also found TBL significantly increased female students’ examination scores in fixed-effects model (SMD = 0.47, 95% CI: 0.24–0.70, I2 = 9.4%, Pheterogeneity = 0.332)"
