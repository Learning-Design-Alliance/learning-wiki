## Evidence

### Pásztor et al. 2010

Attila PÁSZTOR, Róbert PAP-SZIGETI, Erika LAKATOS TÖRÖK. (2010). Effects of Using Model Robots in the Education of Programming. Informatics in Education, 9(1), 133–140. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2010.09

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Control-group study over a three-and-a-half-month semester with an experimental group (nexp = 73) using LEGO NXT robots and a control group (nctrl = 76) taught conventionally; the same 15-item test (Cronbach-alpha 0.86) was used at pre- and post-test. The article reports "programming knowledge developed signiﬁcantly" in neither group; only means and t/p values are printed, no effect size.

> "Neither the experimental groups’ (xpre =4 4.6%p; xpost =4 7.9%p; t = −1.23; p =0 .23) nor the control groups’ (xpre =4 1 .3%p; xpost =4 3 .6%p; t = −1.01; p =0 .34) programming knowledge developed signiﬁcantly during this semester."
