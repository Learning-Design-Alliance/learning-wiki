Multiple linear mixed model correlation analysis in the same cross-sectional sample of 250 students; the article reports "r ≈ 0.89, p < 0.001" between prior music training and MBEA scores.

> "A very strong positive correlation was found between prior music training and MBEA scores (r ≈ 0.89, p < 0.001), indicating that individuals with more musical experience tend to perform better on measures of general musical ability"
