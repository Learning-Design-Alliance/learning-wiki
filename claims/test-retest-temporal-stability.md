### De Witte Benjamin 2021

De Witte Benjamin, Barnouin Charles, Moreau Richard, Lelevé Arnaud, Martin Xavier, Collet Christian, Hoyek Nady. (2021). A haptic laparoscopic trainer based on affine velocity analysis: engineering and preliminary results. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01128-z

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Temporal stability (reliability) test with five randomly selected experts, sessions separated by at least 1 month without practice on the simulator. "r = 0.89; p = 0.04" for collisions and time variables; small sample, no effect size beyond the correlation.

> "Finally, regarding the temporal stability of the simulator’s measure, we found a correlation for the number of collisions and for the time variables between the two sessions (r = 0.89; p = 0.04)."
