### Buljan Ivan 2021

Buljan Ivan, Marušić Matko, Tokalić Ružica, Viđak Marin, Peričić Tina Poklepović, Hren Darko, Marušić Ana. (2021). Cognitive levels in testing knowledge in evidence-based medicine: a cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02449-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of topic scores between student cohorts. First-year students scored higher on Diagnostic studies (Md = 2 vs 1) and third-year students higher on Clinical importance (Md = 2 vs 2 with significant pairwise difference, P < 0.001), consistent with topics taught in each study year.

> "First- and third-year students performed better on specific course topics taught in that study year (Diagnostic studies and Clinical relevance, respectively)."
