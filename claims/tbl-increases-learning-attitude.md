# TBL significantly increases students' learning attitude compared with LBL (pooled SMD = 3.23)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In four studies reporting questionnaire-based learning attitude, the pooled TBL effect versus LBL was significant (SMD = 3.23, 95% CI: 2.27–4.20). [→ Chen Minjian 2018](#chen-minjian-2018)

## Evidence

### Chen Minjian 2018

Chen Minjian, Ni Chunhui, Hu Yanhui, Wang Meilin, Liu Lu, Ji Xiaoming, Chu Haiyan, Wu Wei, Lu Chuncheng, Wang Shouyu, Wang Shoulin, Zhao Liping, Li Zhong, Zhu Huijuan, Wang Jianming, Xia Yankai, Wang Xinru. (2018). Meta-analysis on the effectiveness of team-based learning on medical education in China. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1179-1

`q3 · i3`

Random-effects pooling of learning attitude outcomes from questionnaire surveys in 4 of the 13 included studies (505 subjects). The pooled effect was "SMD = 3.23, 95% CI: 2.27–4.20" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 92.1%).
