Evidence¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler 2024¶

Yvonne M. Hemmler, Dirk Ifenthaler. (2024). Self-regulated learning strategies in continuing education: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100629

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Three-level random-effects meta-analysis pooling 58 studies (N = 48,213 learners) from 57 peer-reviewed publications on CE. The pooled analysis found "moderate to strong positive relationships for learning performance (r= .36)" with SRL strategies.

"moderate to strong positive relationships for learning performance (r= .36) and learner engagement (r= .39)"
