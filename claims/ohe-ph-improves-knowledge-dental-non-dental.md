## Evidence

### Wu Zhiwu 2021

Wu Zhiwu, Li Mingming, Zhu Fangzhi, Lei Lei, Cheng Ran, Hu Tao. (2021). The effects of oral health education regarding periodontal health on non-dental undergraduates in southwestern China—exploring the feasibility of an e-learning course for oral health promotion. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-021-01476-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study: 217 dental students and 134 non-dental students took a traditional 90-min OHE-PH course; knowledge was measured with an 11-question test across 5 items. The study reports "significant improvements after completing the course" for both groups, with P < 0.001 (except item 3, P = 0.003 for dental students).

> "Encouragingly, dental (P < 0.001, except item 3 P = 0.003) and non-dental students (P < 0.001) showed significant improvements after completing the course."
