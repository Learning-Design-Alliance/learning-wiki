q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Same Mann-Whitney U analysis of sentiment counts partitioned by lived-abroad status at Reflection 3. The not-LA versus LA contrast for negative sentiment printed the effect size "rg = .326", a medium association on the authors' stated scale (medium rg = 0.30).

"Students who have not lived abroad (not-LA) expressed a greater number of negative sentiments about their verbal language and communication behaviors in Reflection 3 compared to LA students (U = 166.5, p < .05, rg = .326)."
