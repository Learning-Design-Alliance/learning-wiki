## Evidence

### Patrick D. Gajewski 2020

Patrick D. Gajewski, Michael Falkenstein, Sven Th€ones, Edmund Wascher. (2020). Stroop task performance across the lifespan: High cognitive reserve in older age is associated with enhanced proactive and reactive interference control. NeuroImage 207 (2020) 116430. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.116430

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cue-locked ERP analysis at FCz in the preparatory interval of the Stroop task. The CNV ANOVA showed a main effect of Group (ηp2 = 0.055) and post-hoc tests showed "a larger (more negative) CNV in OH relative to OM and OL performers" with no difference from younger groups, suggesting preserved proactive control.

> "Bonferroni corrected post-hoc tests yielded a larger (more negative) CNV in OH relative to OM and OL performers (both p's < 0.05). No differences were found between old high and middle-aged or young adults (all p's > 0.05)."
