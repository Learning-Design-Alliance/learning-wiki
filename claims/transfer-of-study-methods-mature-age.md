Discussion comment in the journal's communications section interpreting figures on whether college honors predict professional-school success. The writer offers two alternative readings: "ability acquired in one branch of study as a method of thought and work is transferable to another to a very considerable extent," or general attitude matters more. No data or test statistics are printed here.

> "Pedagogically the figures seem to mean for persons of mature age either that ability acquired in one branch of study as a method of thought and work is transferable to another to a very considerable extent, or that the general intellectual and moral attitude is far more important than the method."
