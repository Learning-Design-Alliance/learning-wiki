Evidence¶

F. Egert 2018¶

F. Egert, R.G. Fukkink, A.G. Eckhardt. (2018). Impact of In-Service Professional Development Programs for Early Childhood Teachers on Quality Ratings and Child Outcomes: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317751918

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analysis of (quasi)-experimental studies evaluating in-service training for ECEC professionals, aggregating at teacher level across 36 studies with 2,891 teachers. The printed effect size for this finding is ES = 0.68 (SE = 0.07, p < .001), which the review characterizes as "a medium in-service training effect on process quality".

"The aggregation of findings at teacher level (including 36 studies with 2,891 teachers) revealed a medium in-service training effect on process quality (effect size [ES] = 0.68, SE = 0.07, p < .001)."
