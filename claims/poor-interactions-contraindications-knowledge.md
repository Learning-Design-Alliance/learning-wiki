In the same 24-MCQ knowledge assessment across the two cohorts (n=36 traditional, n=54 PBL), the interactions/contraindications topic showed no significant difference ("48% (SD 18) vs 45% (SD 22); p = 0.62"); the discussion notes students in both groups scored below 50%.

> "'Interaction and contraindications' was the only drug topic for which scores were not significantly different between the two groups (48% (SD 18) vs 45% (SD 22); p = 0.62)."
