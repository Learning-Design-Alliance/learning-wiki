## Evidence

### Gehlhar Kirsten 2014

Gehlhar Kirsten, Klimke-Jung Kathrin, Stosch Christoph, Fischer Martin R. (2014). Do different medical curricula influence self-assessed clinical thinking of students? GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 31(2), Doc23. [doi:10.3205/zma000915](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000915)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretive statement in the Discussion about the instrument's nature, noting that differing degrees of self-criticism across cohorts could influence scores and that no further instrument was implemented to standardise this aspect.

> "although the DTI is an established instrument, it does not objectively gauge clinical reasoning as such but serves as a measure of the self-assessment of students in this discipline."
