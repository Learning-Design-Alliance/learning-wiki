Evidence¶

Cowhy 2023¶

Cowhy, J.R., Gordon, M.F., & de la Torre, M. (2023). Forced to choose: Lessons learned from families of students within special education. Educational Policy. https://doi.org/10.1177/08959048231198814

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

In the interview study of 29 parents after Chicago's 2013 closures, parents "struggled to find information on how, if at all, their child’s IEP would be implemented in potential new schools," making IEP implementation the first factor in enrollment decisions.

"A school’s (in)ability to implement their child’s IEP was the first factor in parents’ enrollment decisions, and a lack of clarity or confidence in a school’s ability limited parents’ opportunities to choose schools based on characteristics other than ability to implement the IEP."
