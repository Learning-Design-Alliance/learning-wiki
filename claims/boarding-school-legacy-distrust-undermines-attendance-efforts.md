## Evidence

### Chang 2024

Chang, H.N., & Leong, C. (2024). Missing more than school: Reducing chronic absence for American Indian and Alaska Native students. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655926

`q1 · i?` · `review · r1`

The brief argues, citing prior scholarship, that boarding schools founded to eliminate traditional cultures inflicted long-term damage and that generational distrust is compounded when curricula lack cultural responsiveness. It also reports AI/AN students are more likely to have absences labeled unexcused, which can lead to denied credit and legal actions. No effect size is printed.

> "The relationships of AI/AN students, families, and communities with educational institutions continue to be deeply and adversely affected by the historical legacy of American Indian boarding schools."
