## Evidence

### Williams Adrienne E. 2026

Williams Adrienne E., Denaro Kameryn. (2026). Studying with Friends in Introductory Biology Is Associated with Low Impact Study Strategies and No Improvement of Exam Scores. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-04-0074

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression of total midterm scores on Week 10 strategy use, demographics, high school GPA, and study-with-friends frequency (n = 375; adjusted R² = 0.423). Study-with-friends was "not included in the final (reduced) model", indicating no significant association with exam performance.

> "Studying with friends at the end of the term was in the initial (full) model (Supplemental Table S31), but was not included in the final (reduced) model (Table 4), implying that studying with friends during the course did not add additional predictive value for exam performance"
