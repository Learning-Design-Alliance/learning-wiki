`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of group-level ONA network visualizations (Fig. 3) for high- vs low-performing teams. The text reports stronger input-to-implementation links for high performers and "fewer transitions to Implementation behaviors" for low performers; no effect size is printed for this specific contrast.

> "The strongest associations were also seen between Actively inviting input and Implementation as well as Explicitly assessing the situation and Actively inviting input for high-performing teams. Low performing teams frequently cycled between Explicitly assessing the situation and Actively inviting input as well but with fewer transitions to Implementation behaviors in comparison to high performing teams."
