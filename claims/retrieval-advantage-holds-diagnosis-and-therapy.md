## Evidence

### Sayyah Ensan Ladan 2011

Sayyah Ensan Ladan, Faghankhani Masoomeh, Javanbakht Anna, Ahmadi Seyed-Foad, Baradaran Hamid Reza. (2011). To Compare PubMed Clinical Queries and UpToDate in Teaching Information Mastery to Clinical Residents: A Crossover Randomized Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0023487

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of answer retrieval by question type in the crossover trial, each participant receiving one diagnosis and one therapy question. The therapy contrast is reported as 29 (66%) versus 13 (29%) with P = 0.002. No effect sizes are printed.
