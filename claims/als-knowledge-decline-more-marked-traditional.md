# Knowledge declined significantly in both groups at seven months, with a significantly more marked decrease in the traditional group

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At the seven-month follow-up, knowledge declined significantly in both groups, but the paired-difference decrease was larger in the traditional group (1.595 questions, p = 0.000) than in the e-learning group (1.034, p = 0.012). [→ Chaves Juan 2020](#chaves-juan-2020)
`q2 i?` Despite the differential decline, no significant between-group difference in deferred knowledge scores was found (20.14 vs 20.72, p = 0.310). [→ Chaves Juan 2020 (2)](#chaves-juan-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Chaves Juan 2020

Chaves Juan, Lorca-Marín Antonio A., Delgado-Algarra Emilio José. (2020). Methodology of Specialist Physicians Training: From Traditional to e-Learning. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17207681

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests on knowledge scores from post-test to seven-month follow-up. The traditional group's mean related difference was "1.595 (95% CI: 0.864–2.325; p = 0.000)" versus 1.034 in the e-learning group.

> "Knowledge declined statistically significantly in both groups, but the decrease in retention was significantly more marked in the control group: the mean of the related differences (t with paired data, post follow-up test) was 1.595 (95% CI: 0.864–2.325; p = 0.000) vs. 1.034 (95% CI: 0.250–1.819; p = 0.012)."

### Chaves Juan 2020 (2)

Chaves Juan, Lorca-Marín Antonio A., Delgado-Algarra Emilio José. (2020). Methodology of Specialist Physicians Training: From Traditional to e-Learning. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17207681

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Deferred knowledge test comparison with heavy attrition: 37 control and 29 experimental participants (28.85% and 50% lost). The article states the result "must be considered with prudence" due to dropouts.

> "In group T, an average of 20.14 correct questions (SD = 2.323) was obtained compared to 20.72 in group EL (SD = 2.313) (p = 0.310). No significant difference was found between the groups, although, due to the abandonment produced, the result must be considered with prudence."
