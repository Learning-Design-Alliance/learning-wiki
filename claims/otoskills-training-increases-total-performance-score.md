# Residents' total performance score on microscopic ossiculoplasty tasks rose from pre-test to post-test after repeated low-fidelity simulator training, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (+0.52 points/min, p < 0.001)

`q3 i?` After three one-hour training sessions on the Otoskills simulator, residents showed a statistically significant increase in total performance score of 0.52 points per minute (95% CI 0.40–0.64, p < 0.001). [→ Fieux Maxime 2021](#fieux-maxime-2021)

## Evidence

### Fieux Maxime 2021

Fieux Maxime, Gavoille Antoine, Subtil Fabien, Bartier Sophie, Tringali Stéphane. (2021). Otoskills training during covid-19 pandemic: a before-after study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02706-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before-after study with blinded rater evaluation of ENT residents (novices and intermediates) performing microscopic ossiculoplasty tasks before (T1) and after (T2) three one-hour training sessions; a linear mixed-effects model showed the improvement was "significant", with effect size 0.52 points/min [95% CI, 0.40–0.64], p < 0.001.

> "Overall, there was a significant improvement in the total PS score after training, and the size of the effect was 0.52 points per minute ([95 % CI, 0.40–0.64], p < 0.001)."
