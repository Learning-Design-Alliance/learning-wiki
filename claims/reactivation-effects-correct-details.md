## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Descriptive reactivation yielded significantly more correct details than both controls; component reactivation beat the distractor group but not the Control group on details recalled. [→ MacLeod 2018](#macleod-2018)

## Evidence

### MacLeod 2018

MacLeod, S., Reynolds, M. G. & Lehmann, H. (2018). The mitigating effect of repeated memory reactivations on forgetting. npj Science of Learning 3:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0025-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

For the correct-details dependent measure, the ANOVA showed a significant reactivation main effect, F(3, 155) = 5.24, p = 0.002. The Descriptive and Component groups "did not signi ﬁcantly differ" from each other (p = 0.19).

> "the participants in the Descriptive group remembered signi ﬁcantly more details than the participants in the Distractor group ( p < 0.001) and the no reactivation Control group ( p < 0.05), demon- strating mitigated forgetting. The participants in the Component group remembered more details than the Distractor group ( p < 0.05), but did not signi ﬁcantly differ from the no reactivation Control group ( p = 0.31)."
