## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Assignment to verbal or monetary encouragement increased post-search policy knowledge scores relative to control across the child support and cannabis legalization waves, and for monetary encouragement in the energy transition wave. [→ Ulloa 2026](#ulloa-2026)

## Evidence

### Ulloa 2026

Ulloa, Roberto; Leonard Tiedemann; Selb, Peter; Kacperski, Celina. (2026). The Knowledge Gap in a High-Choice Media Environment: Experimental Evidence from Online Search. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.21019](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.21019)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat OLS regressions across three topic-specific waves (child support N=1066; energy transition N=950; cannabis N=954) tested encouragement effects on knowledge scores. The article reports the encouragement effect held "for all waves except the verbal encouragement in the energy transition wave."

> "In the main effect models, the verbal and monetary encouragement have an effect on the knowledge score (H1a) for all waves except the verbal encouragement in the energy transition wave."
