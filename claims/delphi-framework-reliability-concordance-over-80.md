`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two-round Delphi scoring with 21 experts using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests and a concordance criterion of ratings of 4 or 5 from over 80% of interviewees. The article reports "considerable reliability of the framework" based on this concordance; no effect size is printed.

> "The concordance rate regarding to score of importance of the core competences are all >80% in both the first and second round Delphi survey, suggesting considerable reliability of the framework."
