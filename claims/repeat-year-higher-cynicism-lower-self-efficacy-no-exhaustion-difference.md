## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Repeaters scored significantly higher in cynicism (d = -0.23) and lower in self-efficacy (d = 0.29 for non-repeaters) than students who had never repeated. [→ Molero Jurado María del Mar 2021](#molero-jurado-maria-del-mar-2021)

## Evidence

### Molero Jurado María del Mar 2021

Molero Jurado María del Mar, Pérez-Fuentes María del Carmen, Martos Martínez África, Barragán Martín Ana Belén, Simón Márquez María del Mar, Gázquez Linares José Jesús. (2021). Emotional intelligence as a mediator in the relationship between academic performance and burnout in high school students. PLoS ONE 16(6): e0253552. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253552

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian independent-samples t-test comparing 976 non-repeaters with 310 repeaters on the MBI-SS subscales. The study found repeaters "had the highest scores in cynicism" and lower self-efficacy; for exhaustion the data were 12.17 times more likely under H0 than H1 (BF01).

> "repeaters had the highest scores in cynicism (t(1284) = -3.53, p < 0.001, d = -0.23). However, those who had never repeated a year had a higher mean score in self-efficacy (t(1284) = 4.52, p < 0.001, d = 0.29). No statistically significant between-group differences were observed in exhaustion (t(1284) = -0.49, p = 0.624)"
