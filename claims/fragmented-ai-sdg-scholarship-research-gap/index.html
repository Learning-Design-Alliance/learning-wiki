Md. Masudul Islam 2025¶

Md. Masudul Islam, Mirza Niaz Morshed, Md. Shafiqul Islam. (2025). Artificial Intelligence Literacy and Sustainable Development: An Ethical Governance and Development Goals Framework. https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7643-5420

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The authors' synthesis-based assessment of the field (Section 2.4), identifying that scholarship "largely examines fragmented connections" and that empirical frameworks remain scarce for assessing how AI literacy influences sustainability outcomes. This is an interpretive claim about the literature, not a tested result.

"Existing scholarship largely examines fragmented connections most often within education, climate action, or health without establishing an integrative framework that systematically links AI literacy competencies to the full spectrum of Sustainable Development Goals."
