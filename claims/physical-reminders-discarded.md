# Physical reminder gadgets as an add-on to the digital training were discarded because no participant saw added value in them

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` None of the participants appreciated the concept of tangible physical reminders, and prototypes of them were not further developed. [→ Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020](#stuij-sebastiaan-m-2020)

## Evidence

### Stuij Sebastiaan M. 2020

Stuij Sebastiaan M., Drossaert Constance H. C., Labrie Nanon H. M., Hulsman Robert L., Kersten Marie José, van Dulmen Sandra, Smets Ellen M. A. (2020). Developing a digital training tool to support oncologists in the skill of information-provision: a user centred approach. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1985-0

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

User group assessment sessions of mock-ups (six hemato-oncologists) showed the reminder idea generally well-received digitally, but "none of the participants appreciated the concept" of tangible physical gadgets; development of physical reminders was not pursued further.

> "The idea of physical reminders was quickly discarded, as none of the participants appreciated the concept."
