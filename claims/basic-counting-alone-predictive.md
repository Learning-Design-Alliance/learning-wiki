## Evidence

### Nguyen 2016

Nguyen, T., Watts, T. W., Duncan, G. J., Clements, D. H., Sarama, J. S., Wolfe, C., & Spitler, M. E. (2016). Which Preschool Mathematics Competencies Are Most Predictive of Fifth Grade Achievement? Early Childhood Research Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2016.02.003

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Regression Model 3 on the 781-child TRIAD subsample entered only basic counting (verbal counting, number recognition, one-to-one correspondence) alongside the other three competency domains, covariates, and classroom fixed effects.

> "we included only the basic counting competencies and found that it was considerably predictive of later mathematics achievement (β = 0.25, SE = 0.04, p < 0.001)."
