## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Controlled studies comparing inquiry- or exploration-based investigation with various forms of direct explicit instruction found students demonstrated greater learning of content and skills from explicit instruction. [→ Lin Zhang 2022](#lin-zhang-2022)

## Evidence

### Lin Zhang 2022

Lin Zhang, Paul A. Kirschner, William W. Cobern, John Sweller. (2022). There is an Evidence Crisis in Science Educational Policy. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09646-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The commentary's synthesis of controlled studies (Hsu et al., Klahr & Nigam, Matlen & Klahr, Zhang, among others) comparing investigation-based teaching with explicit instruction, reporting that "learners receiving these forms of direct, explicit instruction demonstrated greater learning". No effect sizes are printed.

> "These studies found that learners receiving these forms of direct, explicit instruction demonstrated greater learning of content and skills than those who learned through practicing investigations"
