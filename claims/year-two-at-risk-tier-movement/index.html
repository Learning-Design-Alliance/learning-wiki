Elizabeth R. Kazakoff 2018¶

Elizabeth R. Kazakoff, Paul Macaruso, Pam Hook. (2018). Efficacy of a blended learning approach to elementary school reading instruction for students who are English Learners. Education Tech Research Dev 66:429–449. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9565-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive tier-status analysis of Year Two students beginning in Tier 3 (24% of ELs, n = 17; 32% of non-ELs, n = 19), reported against Fig. 1. Tier 3 percentages fell to "18% ELs, 16% non-ELs" from 33% and 35% at end of Year One.

"As seen in Fig. 1, at-risk students in both groups grew significantly over the Year Two study. More than 80% of at-risk ELs and non-ELs moved up at least one tier."
