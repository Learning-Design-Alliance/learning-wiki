## Evidence

### Robert M. DeKeyser 1997

Robert M. DeKeyser. (1997). Beyond Explicit Rule Learning: Automatizing Second Language Morphosyntax. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 19, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263197002040

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Experimental study of automatization: 61 subjects first reached criterion on metalinguistic tests of grammar and vocabulary, then engaged in systematic, computer-controlled comprehension and production practice for 8 weeks, allowing explicit rules to be practiced toward automatization.
