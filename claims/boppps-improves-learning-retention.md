## Evidence

### Zhu Jing 2025

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects pooling of two studies (141 participants) with continuous learning-retention measures; pooled effect size SMD = 0.64 (95% CI 0.35–0.93), with I² = 0 reported in Table 5.

> "In the context of learning retention skills, two studies employed continuous variables, revealing a significant improvement within the BOPPPS group compared to the control group (SMD 0.64, 95% CI 0.35–0.93; P < 0.001; Table 5)."
