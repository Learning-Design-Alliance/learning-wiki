`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's narrative synthesis across the 81 included studies identifies three moderating factors: "the type of gamification elements used, the degree of personalization, and the characteristics of the learners." No effect sizes are printed for these moderators.

> "However, the effectiveness of personalized gamification varies depending on various factors, such as the type of gamification elements used, the degree of personalization, and the characteristics of the learners."
