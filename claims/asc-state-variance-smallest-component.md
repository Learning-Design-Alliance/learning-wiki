## Evidence

### Jansen et al. 2020

Malte Jansen, Oliver Lüdtke, and Alexander Robitzsch. (2020). Disentangling Different Sources of Stability and Change in Students' Academic Self-Concepts: An Integrative Data Analysis Using the STARTS Model. Journal of Educational Psychology. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000448

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Pooled STARTS estimates across the 11 studies; the authors take the small state component as an indication of the high retest reliability of self-concept instruments.

> "The completely situational state component was the smallest variance component in each study that included mathematical self-concept with a pooled estimate of 20%. In the verbal domain, the proportion of state variance was a bit higher at 25% (pooled)."
