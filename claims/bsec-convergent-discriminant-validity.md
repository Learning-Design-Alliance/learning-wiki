# The BSEC shows convergent and discriminant validity: CR > .87, AVE > .63, and AVE square roots exceeding inter-factor correlations

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Convergent validity was supported, with composite reliability scores above .87 and average variance extracted above .63 for the three subscales. [→ Cárdenas-Gutiérrez 2021](#cardenas-gutierrez-2021)
`q3 i?` Discriminant validity was supported by the Fornell-Larcker criterion, each factor's AVE square root exceeding its correlations with the other latent variables. [→ Cárdenas-Gutiérrez 2021 (2)](#cardenas-gutierrez-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Cárdenas-Gutiérrez 2021

Cárdenas-Gutiérrez, A. R., Bernal-Guerrero, A., & Montoro-Fernández, E. (2021). Construction and validation of the Basic Scale of Entrepreneurial Competencies for Secondary Education level. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0249903

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

External validity analysis of the BSEC using the Composite Reliability Index and Average Variance Extracted, with reference values of ≥.70 and ≥.50 respectively; all three subscales exceeded the thresholds.

> "The results of the evaluation of the convergent validity via the AVE with scores >.63 and of the CR with scores >.87, respectively, show that the three subscales fulfill the criterion >.40, thus obtaining appropriate properties of convergent validity"

### Cárdenas-Gutiérrez 2021 (2)

Cárdenas-Gutiérrez, A. R., Bernal-Guerrero, A., & Montoro-Fernández, E. (2021). Construction and validation of the Basic Scale of Entrepreneurial Competencies for Secondary Education level. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0249903

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Discriminant validity assessed via the Fornell-Larcker method on the validation sample; diagonal values in Table 7 (.798, .840, .841) exceeded the off-diagonal inter-factor correlations, which the authors read as evidence of discriminant validity.

> "Using the Fornell-Larcker criterion concerning the variance extracted, all the data indicates that the scores of each factor are greater than the square root of the correlations with the rest of the latent variables."
