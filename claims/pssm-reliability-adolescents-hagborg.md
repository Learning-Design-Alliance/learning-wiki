## Evidence

### Hagborg 1994

Winston J. Hagborg. (1994). An Exploration of School Membership among Middle- and High-School Students. Journal of Psychoeducational Assessment. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=An+Exploration+of+School+Membership+among+Middle-+and+High-School+Students

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 2, a large anonymous sample of 30 students per grade from grades five to twelve in one district, administered the PSSM in March 1993. The article reports school-wide alphas were "identical for both the middle- and high-school students (i.e., .88)"; Study 1 separately found test-retest r = .78 over 4 weeks.

> "Internal consistency alphas by grade level ranged from .71 to .94, yet the school-wide alphas were identical for both the middle- and high-school students (i.e., .88)."
