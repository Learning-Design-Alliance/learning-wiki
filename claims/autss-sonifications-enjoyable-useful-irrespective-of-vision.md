`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation study of the original AUTSS planetarium show with nearly 300 audience members from the UK and Italy. The study "revealed that blind audiences celebrated the show's inclusive nature" and that sonifications were perceived as enjoyable and useful irrespective of vision level; no effect sizes are printed.

> "An evaluation study of nearly 300 audience members from the UK and Italy revealed that blind audiences celebrated the show's inclusive nature [15]. Importantly, the study also revealed that the sonifications were perceived to be enjoyable and useful by most audiences, irrespective of their level of vision."
