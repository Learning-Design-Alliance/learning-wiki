## Evidence

### Nicholas Judd 2022

Nicholas Judd, Bruno Sauce and Torkel Klingberg. (2022). Schooling substantially improves intelligence, but neither lessens nor widens the impacts of socioeconomics and genetics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00148-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc sibling analysis (families = 392, n = 792) estimating within- and between-family cogPGS effects. The authors attribute the null fIQ and WM findings to limited statistical power from only 392 families.

> "We found cIQ's within-family effect ( βw = 0.08, pFDR = 0.009) to be roughly half of the between-family effect ( βB = 0.15, pFDR = 0.009)."
