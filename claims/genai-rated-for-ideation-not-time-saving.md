# Students rated the flipped-learning component highest and the GenAI module lower, with GenAI rated stronger for ideation than for time saving or direct output use

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Post-course ratings were highest for the flipped-learning component (mean = 4.17), lower for the GenAI module (mean = 3.63), and GenAI items scored higher for expanding creative boundaries (4.06) than for saving time (3.00) or direct incorporation into final outcomes (2.94). ## Evidence

### Hong Qu 2026

Hong Qu, Zichao Ling, Yadie Yang. (2026). Integrating Flipped Learning and Generative AI for Practice-Based Design Education: Evidence from a Knit Yarn Design Course. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18505

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of post-course questionnaire items in the intervention cohort. GenAI items rated 4.06 for expanding creative boundaries and 4.00 for alignment with creative intentions, but 3.00 for saving time and 2.94 for direct incorporation of AI-generated references.

> "Thus, the GenAI-related ratings were stronger for ideation-oriented items than for time- saving or direct-output-use items."

### Hong Qu 2026 (2)

Hong Qu, Zichao Ling, Yadie Yang. (2026). Integrating Flipped Learning and Generative AI for Practice-Based Design Education: Evidence from a Knit Yarn Design Course. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18505

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Section-level post-course ratings in the intervention cohort: flipped-learning component mean = 4.17, overall class evaluation mean = 4.00, GenAI module mean = 3.63, all above the scale midpoint on the 5-point Likert scale.

> "At the section level, the flipped-learning component received the highest mean rating (mean = 4.17), followed by the overall class evaluation section (mean = 4.00). The GenAI module received a lower but still positive mean rating (mean = 3.63)."
