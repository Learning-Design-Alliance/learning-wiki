## Evidence

### Vaccaro Jr. 2024

Vaccaro Jr., M., Friday, M., and Zaghi, A. (2024). Evaluating the capability of large language models to personalize science texts for diverse middle-school-age learners. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2408.05204](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.05204)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (n = 23) in which GPT-4 rewrote texts to match (experimental) or contradict (control) each student's profiled preferences; students chose between personalized and generic paragraphs. The article reports "significantly greater than the control group at .10 -level (U = 40.5, one-sided p = .059)"; a t-test comparison yielded g = 0.715, t(21) = 1.777, one-sided p = .045.

> "The mean choice score of the experimental group was found to be significantly greater than the control group at .10 -level (U = 40.5, one-sided p = .059)."
