`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of an international anonymous online survey (open June 3–July 19, 2020) completed by 233 HCPs from 33 countries, who rated eight proposed course topics on a four-point relevance scale. The article reports "interpretation of PRO data (226/233, 97.0%)" as the top-rated topic; ratings are descriptive percentages with no inferential test.

> "The relevance of all 8 categories listed was rated > 90%, with interpretation of PRO data (226/233, 97.0%), clinical benefits of assessing PRO data (222/233, 95.3%), implementation of routine PRO data assessment (221/233, 94.8%)"
