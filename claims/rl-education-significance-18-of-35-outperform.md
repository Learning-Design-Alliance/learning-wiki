## Evidence

### Anna Riedmann 2025

Anna Riedmann, Philipp Schaper, Birgit Lugrin. (2025). Reinforcement Learning in Education: A Systematic Literature Review. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00494-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 89 papers (PRISMA, three databases, 2000–2024) categorizing results into six significance levels. The review reports that "over half of all included papers (n = 54) did not statistically analyze their results for significance"; of the 35 that tested, 18 had an RL policy outperforming all baselines and two showed ATI effects.

> "over half of all included papers (n = 54) did not statistically analyze their results for significance, specifying only descriptive values (e.g., means and standard deviations) or figures"
