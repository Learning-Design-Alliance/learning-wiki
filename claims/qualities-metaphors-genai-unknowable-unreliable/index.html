Evidence¶

Carmen Vallis 2025¶

Carmen Vallis, Stephanie Wilson, Alison Casey. (2025). Fear and Awe: Making Sense of Generative AI Through Metaphor. Journal of Interactive Media Education. https://doi.org/10.5334/jime.972

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative analysis of workshop metaphors. Participants were unsettled by the "black box" metaphor emphasising opacity and lack of algorithmic transparency, described outputs as an unreliable "hallucinator, plagiarism machine", and feared uncontrollable spread like "mold" or a "slippery slope".

"Similarly, participant metaphors mirrored much of this discussion, clustering around themes of GenAI as an unknowable, unreliable, unbounded force that was almost magical."
