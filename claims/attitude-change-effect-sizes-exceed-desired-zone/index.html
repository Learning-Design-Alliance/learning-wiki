Maurer and Keim 2018¶

Trent W. Maurer, Cassidy Keim. (2018). Teaching about prejudice with a Bogardus Social Distance Scale activity: Replication and extension. IJ-SoTL, Vol. 12, No. 1, Art. 7. https://doi.org/10.20429/ijsotl.2018.120107

q1 · i3 · causal · r1

Authors' interpretive discussion applying Cohen's (1988) equivalence and Hattie's (2009) threshold to their own printed effect sizes, the largest being partial eta-squared = 0.22 for Open; the article states these changes are "significantly in excess of the minimum threshold for the zone of desired effects and represent pedagogical success".

"In this investigation, the partial etasquared effect sizes for change in BSDS scores over time were 0.18 and 0.14 for the linear and quadratic effects, respectively. Similar effect sizes were observed for chang- es over time in Own (0.17), Open (0.22), and Feel (0.20)."
