## Subclaims
`q3 i0` In the Year-2 cross-sectional comparison, ELM-ELM teachers had non-significantly different rates than CON-ELM teachers, with moderate effect sizes for teacher demonstrations (g = 0.33), group responses (g = 0.42), and academic feedback (g = 0.43), and a quite small effect for individual responses (g = -0.10).
