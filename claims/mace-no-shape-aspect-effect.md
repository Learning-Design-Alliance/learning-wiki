# The MACE visual arts aspect of shape showed no improvement and no condition effect

## Evidence

### Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen 2019

Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen. (2019). Enriching Mathematics Education with Visual Arts: Effects on Elementary School Students' Ability in Geometry and Visual Arts. International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10763-019-10018-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of the VAA shape aspect in the three-condition quasi-experiment found no growth over time and no condition effect (χ2(94) = 94.96, p = 0.45). The authors attribute this to shapes playing a smaller role in the lesson series.

> "Regarding the aspect of shape used in the V AA, no linear relationship with time was found (t(4206) = 0.39, p = 0.70), indicating that students, on average, did not describe this aspect more frequently in the V AA posttest compared with the V AA pretest."
