## Evidence

### Causby RS 2017

Causby RS, McDonnell MN, Reed L, Fryer CE, Hillier SL. (2017). A qualitative evaluation of scalpel skill teaching of podiatry students. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-017-0202-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview-based content analysis across eight programs; the article reports this as the greatest diversity between programs. It describes six programs using a large tutorial block or skills laboratory (massed practice) versus one program using short repeated sessions (distributed practice).

> "The greatest diversity in teaching practice of scalpel skills between programs was the duration and structure of clinical practice arrangements, including scalpel use."
