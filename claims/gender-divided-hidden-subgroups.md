## Evidence

### Chai A. 2019

Chai A., Le J. P., Lee A. S., Lo S. M. (2019). Applying Graph Theory to Examine the Dynamics of Student Discussions in Small-Group Learning. CBE—Life Sciences Education 18(2):ar29. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-11-0222

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Case study of an eight-node group (case 3) analyzed with the Girvan–Newman algorithm; subgroups were not obvious from visual inspection of the graph. The article reports the graph had 17 edges, density 0.61, and centralization 0.33, and that "two subgroups were divided by gender."

> "These two subgroups were divided by gender, with one subgroup consisting of the female participants and the other subgroup the male participants, suggesting that participants of the same gender were more likely to talk before and after one another."
