# Non-UK fee status is associated with higher extracurricular participation and authorship among UK medical students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In adjusted models, non-UK fee status was associated with higher overall extracurricular participation (OR 1.63, CI 1.39 to 1.92, p<0.0001) and authorship (OR 1.77, CI 1.38 to 2.26, p<0.0001) relative to UK fee status. [→ Ferreira Tomas 2025](#ferreira-tomas-2025)
