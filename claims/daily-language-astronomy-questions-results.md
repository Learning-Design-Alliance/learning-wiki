### Alejandro Gangui 2010

Alejandro Gangui, María Iglesias & Cynthia Quinteros. (2010). Some remarks on a current study involving preservice elementary teachers and some basic astronomical phenomena. Proceedings IAU Symposium No. 260. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1006.0179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1006.0179)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results from Q11 and Q12 on astronomical elements in ordinary language: most answers were wrong, e.g. "dead stars falling down in the atmosphere" for shooting stars. "Just 10 out of 51 answers (roughly 20%)" got Q11 right, and only 3 out of 51 identified planet Venus as the lucero in the Borges poem.
