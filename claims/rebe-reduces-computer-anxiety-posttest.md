# Rational emotive behavior education significantly reduces computer anxiety among primary school teachers enrolled in e-learning at posttest

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Teachers randomly assigned to the 12-session REBE intervention showed a statistically significant reduction in mean computer anxiety score at posttreatment compared to the waitlisted control group (ηp2 = 0.611). [→ Onah BI 2023](#onah-bi-2023)

## Evidence

### Onah BI 2023

Onah BI, Ede MO, Eze BN, Uzoegwu CR, Mgboji C, Agbo GC, Uzuagu AU, Chiamagu C, Agbo PA, Oneli JO, Ogbueghu SN. (2023). Effectiveness of rational emotive behavior education (REBE) on computer anxiety among school teachers enrolled in e-learning. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000034303

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial in which 112 Nigerian primary school teachers were assigned to REBE or waitlisted control; ANOVA on Computer Anxiety Scale scores at posttreatment showed the reported effect, with "ηp2 = 0.611" indicating a large effect.

> "At the posttreatment the effect of REBE was significant in reducing the mean computer anxiety score among teachers, F (1101) = 136.523, P < .001, ηp2 = 0.611, ∆R2 = 0.678."
