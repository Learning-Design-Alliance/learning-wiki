Michael, D. N. (1953). Some Factors Influencing the Effects of Audience Participation on Learning from a Factual Film. Memo Report No. 13A, Human Resources Research Laboratories, Bolling Air Force Base. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Michael+audience+participation+factual+film

In the film experiment, half the groups were told they would have a test after the film, intended to raise motivation. The review reports that "Warning that a test would follow did not produce significantly different learning." No effect size is printed; equivalence was not tested.
