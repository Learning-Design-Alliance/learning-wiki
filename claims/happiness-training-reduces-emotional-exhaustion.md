## Evidence

### Liu Yongmei 2025

Liu Yongmei, Xie Jian. (2025). Happiness training effect on primary school teachers’ academic burnout and teaching effectiveness: narratives from teachers. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-02985-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA of pretest, post-test and follow-up burnout scores from 200 primary school teachers who attended the workshop; Table 2 reports emotional exhaustion means 4.2 (0.6), 2.8 (0.5), 3.0 (0.6), F = 35.72, p = 0.001, "Effect Size (η²)" = 0.28.

> "The results showed a significant reduction in emotional exhaustion and depersonalization after the happiness training workshop, with the effects being sustained at follow-up, though slightly diminished."
