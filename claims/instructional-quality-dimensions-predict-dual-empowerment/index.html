Min Jou 2026¶

Cross-sectional structural mediation model at T2 in a study of 216 semester-long welding students, estimated with covariance-based SEM. The article reports that "All four instructional quality dimensions significantly predicted DE (β = .21–.30, p < .01)"; the model fit CFI = .93, RMSEA = .056.
