## Evidence

### Jie Yang 2020

Jie Yang, James Wells, Rachel Henderson, Elaine Christman, Gay Stewart, and John Stewart. (2020). Extending Modiﬁed Module Analysis to Include Correct Responses: An Analysis of the Force Concept Inventory. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010124](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.010124)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

MMA-P community detection identiﬁed mixed communities such as {8B*, 9E*, 15A*, 21C, 22A}. The article reports this "provides evidence that these items are being incorrectly answered for reasons not completely related to Newtonian reasoning ability," relating to items 21 and 22 identiﬁed as unfair to women by Traxler et al.

> "The relation of the correct response 15A* to 22A and correct response 8B* to 21C correcting for overall test score provides evidence that these items are being incorrectly answered for reasons not completely related to Newtonian reasoning ability."
