## Evidence

### van Maarseveen O. E. C. 2020

van Maarseveen O. E. C., Ham W. H. W., Huijsmans R. L. N., Dolmans R. G. F., Leenen L. P. H. (2020). Reliability of the assessment of non-technical skills by using video-recorded trauma resuscitations. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-020-01401-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of T-NOTECHS scores from 18 consecutive trauma team resuscitations; the total mean score was 19 out of 25, and "the domain ‘leadership’ the lowest mean score (3.6/5)". These are descriptive means with no inferential test reported.

> "The domain ‘situation awareness and coping with stress’ had the highest mean score (4.1/5) and the domain ‘leadership’ the lowest mean score (3.6/5) (Table 1)."
