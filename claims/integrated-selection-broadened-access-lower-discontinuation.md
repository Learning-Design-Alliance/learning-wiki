## Evidence

### Simpson Paul L 2014

Simpson Paul L, Scicluna Helen A, Jones Philip D, Cole Andrew MD, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter G, Velan Gary, McNeil H Patrick. (2014). Predictive validity of a new integrated selection process for medical school admission. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-86

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of the 2002 cohort (145 students, UAI-only selection) with the 2003 cohort (159 students, integrated selection) in the old discipline-based program. No statistically different socio-demographic changes; UAI-performance correlations were significant in the 2003 cohort but not evaluable in the 2002 cohort due to range restriction.

> "Whereas all students admitted in 2002 achieved a UAI ≥ 99.75, only 42.6% of students admitted in 2003 were above this cut off."
