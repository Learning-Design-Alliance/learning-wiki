## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Inadequate responders with comprehension and word reading deficits (IR-CW) scored significantly lower than inadequate responders with comprehension-only deficits (IR-C) on phonological awareness and rapid naming. [→ Cho Eunsoo 2015](#cho-eunsoo-2015)

## Evidence

### Cho Eunsoo 2015

Cho Eunsoo, Roberts Garrett J., Capin Philip, Roberts Greg, Miciak Jeremy, Vaughn Sharon. (2015). Cognitive Attributes, Attention, and Self-Efficacy of Adequate and Inadequate Responders in a Fourth Grade Reading Intervention. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4793275/

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Planned pairwise contrast from the MANOVA profile analysis comparing the two inadequate responder subgroups. The contrast on "phonological awareness, t (96) = 4.42, corrected p < .001 and rapid naming, t (96) = 2.93, corrected p = .04" separated IR-CW from IR-C. Table 3 reports Hedges' g = .90 for phonological awareness and g = .62 for rapid naming on this contrast.

> "Inadequate responders with word reading problems (IR-CW) differed from inadequate responders without word reading difficulties (IR-C) on the phonological processing measures - phonological awareness, t (96) = 4.42, corrected p < .001 and rapid naming, t (96) = 2.93, corrected p = .04."
