## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Staff nurses (vs supervisor nurse or above), clinical nurses (vs head nurses) and 6–15 years' work experience (vs >15 years) each significantly increased the odds of belonging to the medium rather than the high competency group. [→ Guan Q 2025](#guan-q-2025)

## Evidence

### Guan Q 2025

Guan Q, Zhu X, Xue Z, Peng M. (2025). Core competency in palliative care among intensive care unit nurses: A latent profile analysis. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70021

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

In the multivariate logistic regression (n = 342, high group as reference), "Professional title (OR = 0.161, 95% CI: 0.038–0.673, p = .012)", position and work experiences were significant for the medium group; the printed odds ratios are below 1, indicating lower odds for staff nurses, clinical nurses and shorter-experience nurses.

> "Professional title (OR = 0.161, 95% CI: 0.038–0.673, p = .012), position (OR = 0.111, 95% CI: 0.013–0.975, p = .047), work experiences (OR = 0.169, 95% CI: 0.030–0.965, p = .046) and autonomous learning capacity (OR = 3.298, 95% CI: 1.390–7.822, p = .007) all had a statistically significant effect on the medium palliative care core competency group."
