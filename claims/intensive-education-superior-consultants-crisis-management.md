Independent-sample t-test comparing percentage score change from 0 to 12 months between intensive (Group B) and basic (Group C) education hospitals (Section 3.4, Table 5). The article reports significance for consultants only; no effect size is printed.

> "The consultants who received intensive education presented statistically significantly better scores in their questionnaires on the management of asthmatic crises from the beginning to the end of the intervention, compared to those who received basic education (p = 0.033)."
