Evidence¶

Rana 2025¶

Rana, V., Verhoeven, B., & Sharma, M. (2025). Generative AI in Design Thinking Pedagogy: Enhancing Creativity, Critical Thinking, and Ethical Reasoning in Higher Education. Journal of University Teaching and Learning Practice, 22(4). https://doi.org/10.53761/tjse2f36

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Mixed-methods analysis of 112 student reflections from a 12-week undergraduate design thinking course integrating GenAI tools; the authors' headline finding is that "GenAI, when pedagogically scaffolded, augments rather than replaces human judgment." No effect size is printed.

"Findings demonstrate that GenAI, when pedagogically scaffolded, augments rather than replaces human judgment."
