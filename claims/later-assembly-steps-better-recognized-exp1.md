### Benjamin A. Clegg 2024

Benjamin A. Clegg, Alex Karduna, Ethan Holen, Jason Garcia, Matthew G. Rhodes, & Francisco R. Ortega. (2024). Multimedia and Immersive Training Materials Influence Impressions of Learning But Not Learning Outcomes. 2022 Interservice/Industry Training, Simulation, and Education Conference (I/ITSEC), Paper No. 22459. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.05504](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.05504)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed 2 (instruction type) x 2 (middle or late assembly step) ANOVA on Experiment 1 recognition data. The step main effect carried a large printed effect size of "h2p = 0.44" (F(1,128) = 102.05, p < .0005), while instruction type and the interaction were not significant.

> "later steps (M: 78.2%, SD: 15.5) were better identified than earlier steps (M: 66.4%, SD: 15.1), F(1,128) = 102.05, p < .0005, h2p = 0.44. There was again no effect of instruction type (F(1,128) < 1, h2p = 0.01), nor an instruction by step interaction (F(1,128) < 1, h2p = 0.01)"
