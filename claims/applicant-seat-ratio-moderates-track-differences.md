R. D. Matheus, E. M. Gennaro, and M. T. Yamashita. (2025). Effectiveness of Quota Policies Across STEM, Biological, and Humanities Programs. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.08261](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.08261)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article reports 2025 entrance exam applicant-to-seat ratios that 'are 1.6±0.3 for Physics, 4.5±0.6 for Biology, and 2.21±0.24 for Pedagogy', arguing that in Physics quotas are essentially irrelevant because virtually all candidates are admitted.

> "The average applicant-to-seat ratios for the 2025 entrance exam, considering all campuses and the three modalities (full-time, daytime and evening), are 1.6±0.3 for Physics, 4.5±0.6 for Biology, and 2.21±0.24 for Pedagogy."
