`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome measured with the unweighted NASA-TLX completed immediately after each intubation, compared between randomized groups using generalized estimating equations. No differences were seen for physical demand (P=0.21) or performance (P=0.11) domains; no standardized effect sizes were printed.

> "Mental workload scores, measured by the NASA cognitive task load index, were significantly lower for mental demand (coefficient −9.5 (95% CI −16 to −3), P=0.004), temporal demand (−9.1 (−16.1 to −2.1), P=0.01), effort (−10.1 (−16 to −4.4), P=0.001), and frustration (−7.1 (−12.6 to −1.7), P=0.01) in the treatment group."
