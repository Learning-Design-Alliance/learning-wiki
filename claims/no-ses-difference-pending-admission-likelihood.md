## Evidence

### Boon-Falleur 2025

Boon-Falleur, M., Huillery, E., & Chevallier, C. (2025). Waiting time during admission procedures increases social inequalities in higher education. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 122(47), e2426604122. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2426604122

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of admission chances into pending programs using waitlist ranks combined with final admission cutoffs, controlling for Baccalaureate grades and program prestige; the study found "no significant difference between financial aid beneficiaries and the rest of the students." Equivalence was not formally tested.

> "We found that there was no significant difference between financial aid beneficiaries and the rest of the students in terms of their likelihood of being admitted into a pending program, whether during Phase 2 or during Phase 3, see Table 1."
