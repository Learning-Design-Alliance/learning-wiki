Descriptive results from the same cross-sectional survey of 450 outpatient nurses, reporting mean domain scores of the CCQ. The article prints "48.4±3.6" for professional behaviors and "22.8±1.5" for advanced nursing skills; these are descriptive means with no test statistic or effect size.

> "In terms of professional behaviors and general performance, the average score for clinical competence was 48.4±3.6 and 40.7±4.1 respectively. Additionally, the average score for clinical competence regarding core and advanced nursing skills was 43.4±3.0 and 22.8±1.5 respectively."
