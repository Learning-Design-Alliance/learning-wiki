## Evidence

### Heller Sebastian 2024

Heller Sebastian, Reichel Jennifer L., Mülder Lina M., Schäfer Markus, Schwab Lisa, Werner Antonia M., Letzel Stephan, Rigotti Thomas, Dietz Pavel. (2024). The association between health behaviours and academic performance moderated by trait mindfulness amongst university students: an observational study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1340235

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise linear regression (Model 1, Step 4, N = 571) including covariates and all seven health behaviours simultaneously; sleep (insomnia severity) was significantly related to academic performance with "β = 0.12, p = 0.004". The overall model was statistically significant.

> "Sleep (insomnia severity) (β = 0.12, p = 0.004), fruit and vegetable consumption (β = −0.11, p = 0.011), and gender “open” (β = −0.09, p = 0.047) were significantly related to academic performance."
