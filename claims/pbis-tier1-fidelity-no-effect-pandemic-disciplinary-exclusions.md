# Implementing PBIS with at least Tier 1 fidelity showed no statistically significant effect on school disciplinary exclusions during the 2020–21 pandemic year

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Across all five disciplinary exclusion outcomes, schools implementing PBIS with at least Tier 1 fidelity did not differ significantly from propensity-score-matched comparison schools in 2020–21. [→ Gage 2025](#gage-2025)

`q2 i?` The four-condition models found no significant difference between treatment conditions for either ISS or students receiving only one OSS, though the Tiers 1, 2, and 3 coefficient for only one OSS was marginal (IRR = 0.53, p = 0.059). [→ Gage 2025](#gage-2025)

## Evidence

### Gage 2025

Gage, N. A., Grasley-Boy, N. M., MacSuga-Gage, A. S., & Ulrich, T. G. (2025). Implementing Positive Behavior Interventions and Supports During the COVID-19 Pandemic: Three Tiers of Prevention and Intervention as Protective Factors. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673553

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental analysis comparing 152 PSM-matched schools implementing PBIS with at least Tier 1 fidelity to 152 matched comparison schools using two-level random-intercept negative binomial GLMMs on 2020–21 CRDC disciplinary exclusion counts; "there was no statistically significant treatment effect" on any outcome.

> "Across all five outcomes (ISS, only one OSS, OSS, days missed from OSS, and OSS incidents), there was no statistically significant treatment effect."
