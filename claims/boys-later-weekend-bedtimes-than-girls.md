Descriptive comparison of sleep habits in the 177-adolescent sample (52.0% female) found "Boys had on average later bedtimes on weekends than girls"; no other statistically significant gender differences in sleep habits were noted.

> "Boys had on average later bedtimes on weekends than girls (mean bedtime on weekends boys 23:40 ± 1:08 vs. girls 23:18 ± 00:59; independent samples t-test p = 0.020, t = −2.354)."
