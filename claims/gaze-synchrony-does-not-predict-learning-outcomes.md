## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Neither average KLD (Estimate = 0.14) nor average MultiMatch similarity (Estimate = −0.17) showed a significant relationship with post-test scores; equivalence was not tested. [→ Babette Bühler 2024](#babette-buhler-2024)

## Evidence

### Babette Bühler 2024

Babette Bühler, Efe Bozkir, Hannah Deininger, Peter Gerjets, Ulrich Trautwein, and Enkelejda Kasneci. 2024. On Task and in Sync: Examining the Relationship between Gaze Synchrony and Self-Reported Attention During Video Lecture Learning. Proceedings of ACM Symposium on Eye Tracking Research & Applications (ETRA '24). [doi:10.1145/3655604](https://doi.org/10.1145/3655604)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Participant-level linear regressions of average KLD (Estimate = 0.14, CI -0.74 to 1.02) and average MultiMatch similarity (Estimate = -0.17, CI -1.06 to 0.72) on post-test scores for 84 participants, controlling for pre-test. No significant relationship with learning outcomes was found; prior knowledge was significant in these models.

> "In contrast, we could not find a significant relationship between the computed gaze (a-)synchrony measures KLD and MultiMatch similarity and learning outcomes. However, in these models, previous knowledge becomes significant."
