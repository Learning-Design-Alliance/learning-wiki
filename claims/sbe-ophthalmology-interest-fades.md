# Students' self-rated interest in pursuing an ophthalmology residency rose from pre-workshop to immediately after the workshop and was not statistically different from either pre-workshop or immediate post-workshop levels at three months in one single-group pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Three months after the workshop, students' self-reported interest in pursuing ophthalmology residency did not differ significantly from either pre- or immediate post-workshop levels. [→ Shikha B 2025](#shikha-b-2025)

## Evidence

### Shikha B 2025

Shikha B, Bhambhwani V. (2025). Evaluation of a simulation-based ophthalmology education workshop for medical students: a pilot project. BMC Medical Education 25:153. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06712-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Three-month follow-up in the pilot study (n = 9); mean interest was 2.8 ± 1.6/5 versus 2.6 ± 1.5 pre-workshop and 3.2 ± 1.5 immediately post-workshop. The test was "not statistically different"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size was printed.

> "The learners' interest in pursuing an ophthalmology residency three months after the workshop was not statistically different compared with their interest before (p = 0.5943) and immediately after the workshop (p = 0.1038)."
