Theoretical argument about the input component of the definition. The authors reason that anchoring learning in environmental information processing distinguishes it from injury, hormonal influence, maturation and senescence without negative qualifiers.

> "This necessary condition would exclude genetically programmed maturation while allowing for learning in gene-environment interactions during maturation, such as the development of sensory systems programmed to expect information through the senses during maturation (i.e., experience-expectant plasticity)."
