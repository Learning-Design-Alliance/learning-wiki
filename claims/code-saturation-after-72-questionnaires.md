## Evidence

### Ferreira 2024

Ferreira, A., Lima, D. A., Oliveira, W., Bittencourt, I. I. B., Dermeval, D., Reimers, F., Isotani, S. (2024). Exploring Brazilian Teachers’ Perceptions and a priori Needs to Design Smart Classrooms. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00410-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Saturation assessment of the thematic analysis following Hennink et al. (2017) and Guest et al. (2006): over half of the 20 codes (65%) were developed in the first ten questionnaires, and "the remaining 375 questionnaires yielded only one additional code". No effect size applies.

> "the results demonstrate that code saturation was achieved after seventy-two questionnaires, indicating that the sample size was sufﬁcient for the analysis."
