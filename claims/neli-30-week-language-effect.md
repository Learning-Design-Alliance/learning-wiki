## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Children receiving the 30-week version of NELI made the equivalent of about four months of additional progress in language skills compared with untreated controls, an effect unlikely to have occurred by chance. [→ Sibieta 2016](#sibieta-2016)

## Evidence

### Sibieta 2016

Sibieta, L., Kotecha, M., & Skipp, A. (2016). Nuffield Early Language Intervention: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects/language-for-learning/

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial with child-level randomisation within 34 schools' nurseries; 350 pupils in the final common sample. The summary impact table reports an effect size of "0.27 (0.07; 0.46)" for the 30-week group versus untreated control, estimated as "4 months" of additional progress; the confidence interval excludes zero.

> "30-week vs. untreated control 0.27 (0.07; 0.46) 4 months"
