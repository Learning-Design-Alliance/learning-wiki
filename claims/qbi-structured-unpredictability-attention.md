`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 18 students (six per group, quota-sampled) coded in NVivo. The theme "structured unpredictability" was defined as "a planned but unpredictable need for engagement"; 83% of interviewees covered the sub-theme.

> "One student explained, "Quiz questions could appear anytime, so we had to stay focused" (S3). This suggests anticipatory readiness instead of reactive attention."
