## Evidence

### Little 2021

Little, C. W., Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2021). Differential Patterns of Growth in Reading and Math Skills during Elementary School. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 462-476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000635

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent-growth-curve analyses of 921 children assessed K-5 on decoding, phonological awareness, and reading comprehension measures, split into two linear model sets (K-2 and 3-5). The study reports "the variances of the slope parameters were statistically significant" only in the early period. No effect size was printed.

> "the variances of the slope parameters were statistically significant for outcomes between kindergarten and grade 2, but the variances of the slope parameters were not statistically significant for outcomes between grades 3 and 5"
