Analysis of the 90 academic achievement items by outcome area. The review reports "61 items (67.8%) reporting on reading outcomes and slightly over half (n = 48, 53.3%) reporting on maths outcomes", with only 13 items covering other academic areas.

> "Reading and maths outcomes were the most commonly reported, with 61 items (67.8%) reporting on reading outcomes and slightly over half (n = 48, 53.3%) reporting on maths outcomes. Only 13 items (14.4%) reported outcomes other than reading, writing and maths."
