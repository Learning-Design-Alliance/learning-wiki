## Evidence

### Yujing Zhang 2026

Yujing Zhang, Xianghui Meng, Shihui Feng, and Jionghao Lin. (2026). Human–AI Collaboration Reconfigures Group Regulation from Socially Shared to Hybrid Co-Regulation. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_35](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_35)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Parallel-group randomised experiment with 71 university students in 24 groups; mixed-design ANOVA on episode-level regulatory-mode proportions found a significant Condition×Mode interaction, F(1.20,26.31) = 222.46, p < .001, with "Human–Human groups exhibited a higher proportion of SSRL episodes". Mean proportions: SSRL 0.940 (Human–Human) vs 0.083 (Human–AI); CoRL&SSRL 0.851 (Human–AI) vs 0.060 (Human–Human).

> "Holm-adjusted contrasts (see Table 4) showed that Human–Human groups exhibited a higher proportion of SSRL episodes, whereas Human–AI groups showed higher proportions of hybrid CoRL&SSRL and CoRL-only episodes."
