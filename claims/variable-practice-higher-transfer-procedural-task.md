## Evidence

### Anishan V 2024

Anishan V, Konge L, Bjerrum F. (2024). Variable practice is superior to self-directed training for laparoscopic simulator training: a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10688-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

In the transfer test, proficiency-based training on a salpingectomy with bleeding, mean time to proficiency was 103 min (SD: 57) for the variable group versus 183 min (SD: 64) for the self-directed group, p < 0.001; "reached proficiency significantly faster during the transfer test".

> "We found that the variable practice group reached proficiency significantly faster during the transfer test on the procedural task, as well (p < 0.001)"
