## Evidence

### Talan JW 2026

Talan JW, Adelman MH, Forster M, Reuland B, Kaufman B, Hafiz A, Nair SS, Kramer VE, Mendelson JS, Andriotis A. (2026). Virtual reality simulation for tracheostomy emergencies: a randomized educational intervention. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.1093/atsscholar/aapag023

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on Likert PCS items (1-6). VR pre-post means rose, for example, from "2.9 ± 1.20" to "4.43 ± 0.95" (P = .008) on the confidence item; small group P-values of .065 and .086 on two items were not significant.
