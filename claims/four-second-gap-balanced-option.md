## Evidence

### Janaka 2025

Janaka, N., Zhao, S., Ram, A., Sun, R., Tan, S. T. J. W., Li, D., & Hsu, D. (2025). Progressive Sentences: Combining the Benefits of Word and Sentence Learning. MobileHCI '25 Adjunct. [doi:10.1145/3737821.3749564](https://doi.org/10.1145/3737821.3749564)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot 3 (N=2) tested gap durations of 2s, 4s, 6s, and 8s during Walking. A 4-second gap was identified as balanced—neither too short to be unnoticeable nor too long to cause forgetting of word connections—and selected for Study 1.

> "The findings suggested that the gap should be neither too short—making it unnoticeable—nor too long, which could cause participants to forget the connection between words in a sentence. A 4-second gap was identified as a balanced option and selected for further study."
