## Evidence

### Danny Doucette 2020

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh (2020). All aboard! Challenges and successes in professional development for physics lab TAs. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.doucette](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.doucette)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of student E-CLASS attitude change in fall 2018 by whether their TA engaged with the nature-of-science module: "by comparing the 74 students who received this NoS treatment from their TA to the 207 who did not".

> "Thus, by comparing the 74 students who received this NoS treatment from their TA to the 207 who did not, we estimate the impact of having a TA engaged with NoS instruction."

### Danny Doucette 2020 (2)

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh (2020). All aboard! Challenges and successes in professional development for physics lab TAs. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.doucette](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.doucette)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A mixed-effect ANCOVA controlling for overall pre-test E-CLASS score, with satisfactory normality and homogeneity of variance, found "a statistically signiﬁcant difference between our two TA groups and the two item categories (F (1, 279) = 11 .5, p < 0.001)".

> "Using a mixed-effect ANCOV A model controlling for overall pre-test E-CLASS score, and with sat- isfactory normality and homogeneity of variance, we ﬁnd a statistically signiﬁcant difference between our two TA groups and the two item categories (F (1, 279) = 11 .5, p < 0.001)."
