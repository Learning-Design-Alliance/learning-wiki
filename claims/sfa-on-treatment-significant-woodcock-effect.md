## Evidence

### Miller et al. 2017

Sarah Miller, Andy Biggart, Seaneen Sloan, Liam O'Hare. (2017). Success for All: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581417

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

On-treatment re-analysis of the cluster RCT excluding data from the seven intervention schools that withdrew at the start of the trial. Mid-point (g=0.06, p=0.19) and phonics (g=0.07, p=0.22) effects remained non-significant.

> "There is however some evidence of a small statistically significant positive effect in favour of the intervention group on the Woodcock literacy test at post-test (Hedges’ g=0.10, p=0.05)."
