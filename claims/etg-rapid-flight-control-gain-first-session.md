## Evidence

### Zhang Yang 2025

Zhang Yang, Chen Jie, Zhong Xiao, Shao Yongcong, Fu Weiwei. (2025). Evaluating the effectiveness of flight simulator training on developing perceptual-motor skills among flight cadets: a pilot study. Scientific Reports 15, 28062. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-12929-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA of flight control scores in the same 2×8 randomized design (n = 40). The article states the ETG "achieved a rapid improvement in flight control performance after the first training session, whereas the TTG only showed a significant difference from baseline after the third training session", with interaction "η² = 0.11".

> "a significant interaction between training method and measurement time (F (2.74, 104.28) = 4.58, p = 0.006, η² = 0.11, 95% CI [0.039, 0.151]). Post-hoc comparisons indicated that, The ETG showed significant differences at all time"
