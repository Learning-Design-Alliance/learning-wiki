### Bosse Hans Martin 2015

Bosse Hans Martin, Mohr Jonathan, Buss Beate, Krautter Markus, Weyrich Peter, Herzog Wolfgang, Jünger Jana, Nikendei Christoph. (2015). The benefit of repetitive skills training and frequency of expert feedback in the early acquisition of procedural skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0286-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group improvement in the randomized mannequin training study: both groups scored higher at the final sixth repetition than at the first independent performance; the article reports "both groups scored higher on binary checklist ratings compared to T1 (p < .001)". No effect size is printed.

> "At T2, both groups scored higher on binary checklist ratings compared to T1 (p < .001) with the HFF group showing higher scores at T2, although not significantly (p < .093; see Table 4)."
