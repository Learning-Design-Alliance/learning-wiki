The review attributes to Booth (2012) the conclusion that "trust is among the key enablers for knowledge sharing in online communities", and suggests perceived knowledge sharing and resulting trust could be used to judge the strength of a community's emotional ties. This is a second-hand attribution from the review's literature synthesis.
