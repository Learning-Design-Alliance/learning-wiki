# Dynamic spatial ability is distinguishable from static spatial ability as independent factors

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Factor-analytic studies using computer-based dynamic tests support distinguishing static and dynamic spatial ability, proposing independent dynamic factors involving directional judgement, speed judgement and movement detection. [→ Jeffrey Buckley 2018](#jeffrey-buckley-2018)

## Evidence

### Jeffrey Buckley 2018

Jeffrey Buckley, Niall Seery, Donal Canty. (2018). A Heuristic Framework of Spatial Ability: a Review and Synthesis of Spatial Factor Literature to Support its Translation into STEM Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9432-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that exploratory and confirmatory factor analyses with multidisciplinary cohorts (Contreras et al.; D'Oliveria) "support the distinction between static and dynamic spatial ability", identifying factors for directing moving stimuli and for arrival-time and intercept judgement.

> "The results from each of these studies support the distinction between static and dynamic spatial ability and propose independent dynamic factors which are distinct from static factors."
