## Evidence

### Catherine Lewis & Rebecca Perry 2014

Catherine Lewis & Rebecca Perry. (2014). Lesson Study with Mathematical Resources: A Sustainable Model for Locally-led Teacher Professional Learning. Mathematics Teacher Education and Development. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1046670

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial: 39 educator groups randomly assigned to three conditions; this report focuses on 13 experimental lesson study groups (N = 73 teachers) versus control groups (N = 140). The article reports "significant increases in three of the four facets" with paired-difference t tests (e.g., linear representation t = 7.25, 72 df, p < 0.001).

> "Teachers who participated in lesson study with mathematical resources showed significant increases in three of the four facets of fractions knowledge, and in use of linear measurement representations."
