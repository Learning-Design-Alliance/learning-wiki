## Evidence

### Datu 2025

Datu, J. A. D., Sun, X. H., Liu, S., Pang, F., & Watt, H. (2025). Are gritty children more engaged in math? Triarchic model of grit, math self-efficacy beliefs, and math engagement in Chinese children in Macau. School Psychology International, 46(6), 582–602. https://doi.org/10.1177/01430343251386902

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM results from the cross-sectional survey of 794 grade 4–5 students in Macau. The article reports that "Adaptability positively predicted cognitive, emotional, and social math engagement" and notes relations were especially strong with cognitive and social engagement; no specific coefficients for these paths are printed in the text provided.

> "Adaptability positively predicted cognitive, emotional, and social math engagement, which partly confirmed Hypothesis 1."
