### Swagata Sarkar and N. Sanjay Rebello 2025

Swagata Sarkar and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2025). Sense of Belonging and Intent to Persist: Mediating Role of Motivation and Moderating Role of Gender in Physics and Astronomy Graduate Students. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sarkar](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sarkar)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Quantitative Qualtrics survey of graduate students in a physics and astronomy department; 44 responded, 35 completed in full. Probit regression of binary intent to persist on sense of belonging yielded "a statistically significant slope of 0.489" (Table I, p < 0.05).

> "The probit regression of IP on SB yields a statistically significant slope of 0.489 (see Table I), indicating that a stronger sense of belonging is consistently associated with a greater intent to persist."
