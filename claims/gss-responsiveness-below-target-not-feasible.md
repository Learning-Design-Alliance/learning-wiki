## Evidence

### Rogan Slavko 2021

Rogan Slavko, Taeymans Jan, Zuber Stefan, Zinzen Evert. (2021). Impact of guided self-study on learning success in undergraduate physiotherapy students in Switzerland – a feasibility study of a higher education intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02794-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Fidelity-of-implementation monitoring in this single-center randomized feasibility trial of 51 first-semester physiotherapy students documented attendance at day-8 sessions; the printed result was that "Only six students (23.1%) instead of the expected 83% participated in five G-SS sessions at day 8."

> "Only six students (23.1%) instead of the expected 83% participated in five G-SS sessions at day 8."

### Rogan Slavko 2021 (2)

Rogan Slavko, Taeymans Jan, Zuber Stefan, Zinzen Evert. (2021). Impact of guided self-study on learning success in undergraduate physiotherapy students in Switzerland – a feasibility study of a higher education intervention. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02794-6

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-study oral group interview survey reported by tutors; students rated case clarity and curriculum alignment as good but reported the preparation time frame "seemed not optimally embedded in the academic timetable" because workload was very high.

> "However, the time frame for the preparation of the clinical cases seemed not optimally embedded in the academic timetable. G-SS was scheduled into the timetable, where the student’s workload was very high."
