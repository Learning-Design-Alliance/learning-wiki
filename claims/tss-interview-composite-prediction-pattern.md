## Evidence

### Sartania Nana 2014

Sartania Nana, McClure John D, Sweeting Helen, Browitt Allison. (2014). Predictive power of UKCAT and other pre-admission measures for performance in a medical school in Glasgow: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mutually adjusted regressions (Table 4) in the Glasgow cohort: TSS beta 0.224 (p = 0.009) for EPM after confounder adjustment; interview score beta 0.125 (p = 0.086). The authors note the TSS effect "was due to the effects of age". Only regression betas are printed, so no standardised effect size.

> "TSS was significantly associated with EPM following adjustment for socio-demographic confounders. Interview score was associated with EPM in the unadjusted model, but this relationship weakened to insignificance following adjustment for confounders."
