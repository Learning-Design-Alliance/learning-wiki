## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the ToGRA regression the treatment group showed a mean gain of 0.379, statistically significant at P=<0.0001, controlling for gender, ethnicity and other categories. [→ Richardson 2013](#richardson-2013)
`q3 i?` GATE participation was a significant positive covariate on both the NCGE (estimate 2.577, p=0.0025) and ToGRA (estimate 0.366, p=0.0010). [→ Richardson 2013 (2)](#richardson-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Richardson 2013

Richardson, Ronald; Brouillette, Liane. (2013). Historic and Ethnic Music Found to Increase Geographic Understanding: A Quasi-Experimental Study. Journal for Learning through the Arts, 9(1). http://escholarship.org/uc/item/71g4x3cr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis (Table 9) of ToGRA post scores on pre score, group and covariates for the 215-student sample. Only the group coefficient and its significance are printed; no standardized effect size is given for this regression, so impact is null.

> "When controlling for all identified categories (gender, ethnic group, etc.), the treatment group showed a mean gain of 0.379. As shown in the last column, this is statistically significant at P= <0.0001."

### Richardson 2013 (2)

Richardson, Ronald; Brouillette, Liane. (2013). Historic and Ethnic Music Found to Increase Geographic Understanding: A Quasi-Experimental Study. Journal for Learning through the Arts, 9(1). http://escholarship.org/uc/item/71g4x3cr

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

From the NCGE regression table (Table 5), the GATE term carries estimate 2.577 with t ratio 3.062 and Prob>|t| 0.0025; the ToGRA regression (Table 9) shows GATE 0.366, p=0.0010. Hispanic, Asian and free-lunch terms were not significant in either model.
