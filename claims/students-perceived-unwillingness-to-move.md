### Rupp Robert 2025

Rupp Robert, Wallmann-Sperlich Birgit, Bucksch Jens. (2025). Strategies and barriers to implementing physically active teaching in universities from the perspective of lecturers: a qualitative study. BMC Public Health 25, Article 855. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22075-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding: reluctance was observed with large activations (e.g. excursions) and small ones such as group work, where students said "Oh, no, no, we'd rather stay here". Some lecturers counter it with methodological variation, e.g. pairing with someone not yet spoken to.

> "Five of the sixteen lecturers interviewed reported that they had already experienced that students were sometimes reluctant to engage in such physical activations when setting physically activating impulses as part of their courses."
