### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Electronics lab instructors' approaches to troubleshooting instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.113.010102)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From interviews with 20 electronics instructors: all endorsed learnability (e.g., "It's learned, it's learned"); about half additionally cited innate logical thinking or temperaments, with one instructor expressing uncertainty via the theorizer-versus-experimentalist stereotype.

> "When asked whether troubleshooting was a learnable or innate skill, all instructors said that they believe students can learn to troubleshoot"
