Study 2 educator interview in the Virtual Implementation Successes section: one teacher noted it was "particularly great for them to have five different types of activities," each helpful for different students, so learners "can see the content in different ways, in different modalities."

"the range of different activities (videos, digital games, hands-on activities, eBooks, and a digital notebook) and multiple representations of key NGSS-aligned science concepts woven throughout each of the activities engaged students with different learning preferences and abilities."
