## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In intent-to-treat analyses, students who received only the pre-K TRIAD intervention scored significantly higher in mathematics than control students at the end of first grade (g = .21). [→ Clements 2013](#clements-2013)

## Evidence

### Clements 2013

Clements, D. H., Sarama, J., Wolfe, C. B., & Spitler, M. E. (2013). Longitudinal Evaluation of a Scale-Up Model for Teaching Mathematics With Trajectories and Technologies: Persistence of Effects in the Third Year. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831212469270

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Intent-to-treat HLM analysis of the same cluster randomized trial (N = 1,079). The article reports the pre-K-only group "demonstrated significantly higher mathematics scores relative to the control condition" with "g = .21".

> "The TRIAD-NFT condition also demonstrated significantly higher mathematics scores relative to the control condition (RP: b = .139; SE = .054, p = .01, g = .21)."
