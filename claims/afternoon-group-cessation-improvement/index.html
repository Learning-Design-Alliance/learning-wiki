Winch 1912¶

Winch, W. H. (1912). Mental fatigue in day school children as measured by immediate memory: Part I. Journal of Educational Psychology, 3(1), 18–28. doi:10.1037/h0070728

q2 · i? · causal · r1

From the summarized conclusions, drawing on the individual improvement data of Table V, which showed only one non-improving case in Group A against six in Group B. The author argues this is not likely due to boredom and suggests these pupils were being generally overworked or had reached their saturation point for the function.
