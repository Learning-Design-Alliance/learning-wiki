## Evidence

### Darnell 2019

Darnell, D. K., & Krieg, P. A. (2019). Student engagement, assessed using heart rate, shows no reset following active learning sessions in lectures. PLoS ONE 14(12): e0225709. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0225709

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of ending and starting HR across 10 between-class breaks with the same teacher on both sides; no significant difference, and equivalence was not tested. No effect size printed.

> "Analysis of HR on either side of 10 between-class breaks, with the same teacher on both sides of the break, shows no significant difference between the ending and starting HR (average reset in HR is +0.15 bpm, P = 0.80; S2 Table)."
