## Evidence

### Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly 2023

Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly. (2023). Predicting Community College Astronomy Performance through Logistic Regression. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression (Table VII): mathematics performance b=0.432 (SE 0.068), Wald=40.726, odds ratio 1.540 [1.349, 1.759], p<0.001 — "54% more likely to pass their astronomy course" per grade point.

> "For every increased grade point earned in a mathematics course, students were 54% more likely to pass their astronomy course."

### Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly 2023 (2)

Zachary Richards and Angela M. Kelly. (2023). Predicting Community College Astronomy Performance through Logistic Regression. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010119)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression (Table VII): repeated mathematics odds ratio 0.671 [0.513, 0.877], p=0.004; developmental mathematics enrollment odds ratio 0.568 [0.456, 0.709], p<0.001, both negative predictors of passing.

> "If students repeated their mathematics courses, they were 33% more likely to fall in the DFW category in astronomy than those who did not repeat mathematics courses. Students enrolling in a developmental mathematics course were 43% less likely to pass astronomy than those who did not take developmental mathematics."
