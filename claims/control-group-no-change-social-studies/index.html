Evidence¶

Tanrıkulu and Baysal 2022¶

Derya Duygu Tanrıkulu, Z. Nurdan Baysal. (2022). The effect of cartoon-supported problem-based learning method in primary school, fourth-grade social studies course on students' perceptions of their problem-solving skills and their level of achievement. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 15(1). https://orcid.org/0000-0001-6713-1396

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests for the control group (n=27) comparing pre-test and post-test scores on the total PSIC inventory and its sub-dimensions. The article reports no statistically significant differences (p = .940, .679, .231, and .097 respectively).

"According to Table 6, the test performed for the control group showed that there is no statistically significant difference between the mean rankings of the participants' pre-test and post-test scores from the entire inventory and its self-confidence, self-control, and avoidance sub-dimensions (P<.05)."

Tanrıkulu and Baysal 2022 (2)¶

Derya Duygu Tanrıkulu, Z. Nurdan Baysal. (2022). The effect of cartoon-supported problem-based learning method in primary school, fourth-grade social studies course on students' perceptions of their problem-solving skills and their level of achievement. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 15(1). https://orcid.org/0000-0001-6713-1396

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon signed-rank test for the control group (n=27) comparing pre-test and post-test achievement test scores. The article reports no statistically significant difference (z = -.940, p = .347).

"According to Table 12, the test performed for the control group showed that there is no statistically significant difference between the mean rankings of the participants' pre-test and post-test scores from the achievement test (P<.05)."
