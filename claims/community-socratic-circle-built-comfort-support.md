`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Findings from the Community Post-Implementation Survey administered after the final implementation cycle, in which district leaders, teachers, students, parents, and community members gathered in a Community Socratic Circle led by two RCS Core Team students. The report states

> "Students’ responses mirrored their teachers’ perceptions and community members indicated that participating in the community Socratic circle helped them feel comfortable with teachers using Socratic Circles to facilitate classroom racial discourse."
