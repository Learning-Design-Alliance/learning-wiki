Evidence¶

Luo 2025¶

Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of interviews (RQ3). Four participants described "utilizing AI to streamline the design process as if GenAI was a design partner or personal assistant," providing virtual staff support and reducing scripting and writing time.

"The participants (N = 4) outlined the advantages and functionalities of utilizing AI to streamline the design process as if GenAI was a design partner or personal assistant to optimize their workflow, promote creativity, and offer administrative and technical support."

Luo 2025 (2)¶

Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of interviews (RQ3). Six IDs "believed that AI could facilitate unstructured problem-solving in ID, catering to both linear and non-linear approaches," aiding course restructuring and curriculum design.

"IDs (N = 6) believed that AI could facilitate unstructured problem-solving in ID, catering to both linear and non-linear approaches."
