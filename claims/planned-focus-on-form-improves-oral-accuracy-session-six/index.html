Evidence¶

Farahman Farrokhi 2012¶

Farahman Farrokhi, Mohammad Hassan Chehrazad. (2012). The Effects of Planned Focus on Form on Iranian EFL Learners' Oral Accuracy. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v2n1p70

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of three intact classes (control n=17, recast n=19, delayed explicit/metalinguistic n=21) over six story-retelling sessions. One-way ANOVA on oral accuracy (error-free T-units) reached significance only in session 6: "F (2, 49) =3.736, p-value =.031".

"in the session6 it was 0.31; F (2, 49) =3.736, p-value =.031 which was smaller than =0.05 and therefore the inequality of groups is accepted at the 0.95 level of significance in the session 6"
