# After using the digital inlay platform, preclinical dental students averaged 43.32 ± 16.17 out of 60 on a restorative-concepts exam, with 48.03% scoring 50 or higher

## Evidence

### Sun Jiyu 2025

Sun Jiyu, Zhang Xidan, Qin Ziqi, Tang Zihua, Ruan Yifeng, Shen Yifei, Zhu Zhuoli, Yu Haiyang, Shen Jiefei, Gan Xueqi. (2025). Enhancing preclinical dental education through a VR-based digital platform for inlay tooth preparation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08138-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-institution training evaluation: 166 volunteer preclinical dental students used the platform self-paced, and 152 completed the 12-question exam. The article reports "The average score in the final exam was 43.32 ± 16.17" with no control group or effect size.

> "The average score in the final exam was 43.32 ± 16.17 (maximum score: 60). Figure 3A depicts the distribution of the scores."

### Sun Jiyu 2025 (2)

Sun Jiyu, Zhang Xidan, Qin Ziqi, Tang Zihua, Ruan Yifeng, Shen Yifei, Zhu Zhuoli, Yu Haiyang, Shen Jiefei, Gan Xueqi. (2025). Enhancing preclinical dental education through a VR-based digital platform for inlay tooth preparation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08138-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive result from the same exam of 152 completing students: "73 answer sheets scored or above 50, accounting for approximately 48.03% of all participants"; no inferential test or effect size is reported.

> "Notably, 73 answer sheets scored or above 50, accounting for approximately 48.03% of all participants, highlighting their strong comprehension and proficiency in tooth preparation for dental restorations."
