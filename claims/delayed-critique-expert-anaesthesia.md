### Laurence Viennot 2020

Laurence Viennot. (2020). How to choose which explanation to use with students? Discussing the tensiometer with beginning teachers. International Journal of Science Education. [doi:10.1080/09500693.2020.1843082](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2020.1843082)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Background synthesis attributed by the article to Viennot and Décamp (2018), based on their studies of four physics themes (e.g. radiocarbon dating, osmosis); the reviewed studies' designs are not described here. The article defines a 'teaching ritual' as a very contestable, common, undiscussed explanation. No effect size reported.

> "These authors found that when asked to analyse flawed written physics explanations, in response to questions such as Do you find this explanation satisfying? Would you add or change something? STs tend to refrain from any criticism."
