# In a Vietnamese EFL sample, all six Meta-UTAUT hypotheses on MALL adoption were accepted with p-values below 0.05

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` PLS-SEM analysis of 396 survey responses supported all six hypothesized positive paths (SI→ATT, PE→BI, EE→BI, ATT→BI, PBC→BI, BI→UB) at p < 0.05. [→ Tuan Thanh Nguyen 2023](#tuan-thanh-nguyen-2023)

## Evidence

### Tuan Thanh Nguyen 2023

Tuan Thanh Nguyen. (2023). Exploring the Interaction of Meta-UTAUT in the Mobile-Assisted Language Learning Environment for EFL Learning. Journal of Educators Online. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1445637

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 396 MALL users in EFL contexts, analyzed with PLS-SEM in SmartPLS 3.0. The article reports "P-values below 0.05 (Table 6)" for all six hypotheses; Table 6 prints path coefficients (e.g., 0.035, 0.064) and p-values but no standardized effect sizes.

> "P-values below 0.05 (Table 6) led to the acceptance of all hypotheses, affirming their statistical significance within our research context."
