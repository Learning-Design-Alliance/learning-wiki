## Evidence

### Hibbert Emily J 2013

Hibbert Emily J, Lambert Tim, Carter John N, Learoyd Diana L, Twigg Stephen, Clarke Stephen. (2013). A randomized controlled pilot trial comparing the impact of access to clinical endocrinology video demonstrations with access to usual revision resources on medical student performance of clinical endocrinology skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same randomized pilot trial, the diabetes history-taking station showed "83.3% (n = 10/12) of students randomized to the video" rated globally satisfactory versus 20.0% (n = 2/10) of controls, a printed difference of 63.3% (95% CI 30.7-95.9%).

> "For the DMH, 83.3% (n = 10/12) of students randomized to the video were rated globally as competent at the skill compared with 20% (n = 2/10) of students not randomized to the video (p = 0.007)."
