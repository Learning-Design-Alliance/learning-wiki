`q3 i?` Students who claimed to have watched the BMV demonstration video before class scored higher on BMV performance (7.8 ± 1.0 vs 6.3 ± 1.7; p < 0.01). [→ Chan Enoch 2021](#chan-enoch-2021)

## Evidence

### Chan Enoch 2021

Chan Enoch, Botelho Michael George, Wong Gordon Tin Chun. (2021). A flipped classroom, same-level peer-assisted learning approach to clinical skill teaching for medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0258926

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of BMV performance scores (mean of two blinded assessors) among 5th-year medical students who watched the video before class versus those who did not, analyzed by Mann-Whitney U test. The article reports "those who watched before class: 7.8 ± 1.0; those who did not: 6.3 ± 1.7; p < 0.01". No effect size is printed.

> "For bag mask ventilation, participants who claimed to have watched the corresponding demonstration video before class achieved higher performance scores (those who watched before class: 7.8 ± 1.0; those who did not: 6.3 ± 1.7; p < 0.01)."
