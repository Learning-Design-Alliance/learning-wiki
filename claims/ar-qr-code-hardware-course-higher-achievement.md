## Evidence

### Godoy 2022

Godoy, C. H., Jr. (2022). A Review of Augmented Reality Apps for an AR-Based STEM Education Framework. Southeast Asian Journal of STEM Education, 3(1). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.07024](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.07024)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Experimental study reported in the review: 50 volunteer undergraduates split into experimental and control groups of 25 each; the experimental group examined computer hardware with QR code cards and AR from mobile devices all semester. The review reports the "experimental group’s level of achievement was higher." No effect size is printed.

> "Results from the study showed that the experimental group’s level of achievement was higher. Results showed in this context that computer hardware course implementation with augmented reality and QR code integration has a positive impact on students and their academic accomplishments with positive views towards this course."
