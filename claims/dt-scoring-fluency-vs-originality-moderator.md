## Evidence

### Gerwig Anne 2021

Gerwig Anne, Miroshnik Kirill, Forthmann Boris, Benedek Mathias, Karwowski Maciej, Holling Heinz. (2021). The Relationship between Intelligence and Divergent Thinking—A Meta-Analytic Update. Journal of Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence9020023

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression of DT scoring category on the intelligence–DT correlation, with printed correlations "r = .20 (fluency) to r = .37 (composite score". Specific contrasts showed fluency correlated significantly lower with intelligence than originality/creative quality (z = −3.75, p < .001); this contrast was robust across checks.

> "DT scoring was found to be a significant moderator of the relationship between DT and intelligence, QM(2) = 22.47, p < .001, with correlations ranging between r = .20 (fluency) to r = .37 (composite score; see Table 5)."
