# Swift informal outreach reached mass audiences: the Black Holes planetarium show played in over 30 venues, the PBS Nova episode was initially viewed by over 10 million people, and the Supernova! toolkit reached over 138,000 attendees through 1,284 events

## Evidence

### Lynn Cominsky 2014

Lynn Cominsky, Kevin McLin, Aurore Simonnet and the Swift E/PO Team. (2014). Fourteen Years of Education and Public Outreach for the Swift Gamma-ray Burst Explorer Mission. eConf C1304143. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.2104](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.2104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Program-reported reach of the full-dome digital planetarium show Black Holes: The Other Side of Infinity, which premiered in January 2006, features the Swift launch and GRB detections, and supplied elements of the PBS Nova episode Monster of the Milky Way, initially viewed by over 10 million people.

> "It is now been shown in over 30 venues worldwide and has reached millions of people."

### Lynn Cominsky 2014 (2)

Lynn Cominsky, Kevin McLin, Aurore Simonnet and the Swift E/PO Team. (2014). Fourteen Years of Education and Public Outreach for the Swift Gamma-ray Burst Explorer Mission. eConf C1304143. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1405.2104](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1405.2104)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Program-reported attendance figures for the Supernova! toolkit developed with the Astronomical Society of the Pacific for over 200 amateur astronomy clubs in the Night Sky Network, after NASA product approval and nationwide distribution.

> "Through 2011, the toolkit reached over 13 8,000 attendees through 1,284 events. Of these events, 679 events reported inc luding almost 25,000 minorities and over 39,000 women/girls."
