### Soares 2023

Soares, S., Boyes, M. E., Parrila, R., & Badcock, N. A. (2023). Does reading anxiety impact on academic achievement in higher education students? Dyslexia, 29(3), 179–198. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1738

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two bootstrapped parallel multiple mediation analyses (comprehension and reading history as predictors, social and non-social reading anxiety as mediators, WAM as outcome, controlling for trait anxiety; 5000 resampling draws) found indirect effect CIs spanning zero (e.g., comprehension–social 0.05 [−0.01, 0.02]). "no evidence of mediation was found".

> "As seen in Table 8, none of the indirect effects were statistically significant. Overall, no evidence of mediation was found."
