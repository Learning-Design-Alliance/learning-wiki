# Free recall of expository text is higher under a study goal than under an entertainment goal

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Readers with a study goal recalled more text clauses than readers with an entertainment goal, remembering about 20% more statements. [→ P. van den Broek 2001](#p-van-den-broek-2001)

## Evidence

### P. van den Broek 2001

P. van den Broek, R. F. Lorch, Jr., T. Linderholm, & M. Gustafson. (2001). The effects of readers' goals on inference generation and memory for texts. Memory & Cognition, 29(8), 1081-1087. https://link.springer.com/journal/13414

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Free-recall ANOVA (factors: instructional condition, text, text version) after a written mathematics distractor task. "Recall was higher in the study condition ( M = 18.20 clauses) than in the entertainment condition (M = 15.08 clauses)"; the discussion states study-goal readers remembered 20% more statements.

> "As predicted, recall was higher in the study condition( M = 18.20 clauses) than in the enter-tainment condition (M = 15.08 clauses) [F(1,65) = 4.22,MSe = 37.99]."
