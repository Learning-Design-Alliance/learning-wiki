### Pawlaczyk-Kamieńska T 2026

Pawlaczyk-Kamieńska T, Marushko O, Marushko O, Kamieńska P, Jastrzębski B, Kamieńska W. (2026). Factors influencing the accuracy of clinical decision-making in dental trauma: knowledge, experience, and educational attitudes among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09447-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 291 medical students using scenario-based vignettes scored against IADT guidelines. The abstract reports "Only 9.6% of participants selected the correct management for avulsion, compared to 40.9% for crown fractures."

> "Overall, clinical decision-making accuracy was limited, particularly for tooth avulsion. Only 9.6% of participants selected the correct management for avulsion, compared to 40.9% for crown fractures."
