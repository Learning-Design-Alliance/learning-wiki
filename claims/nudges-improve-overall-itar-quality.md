## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Changing the ITAR form structure increased the mean total CCERR score from 18.0 to 18.9 and 18.8 out of 45 across successive form versions. [→ Dory V 2019](#dory-v-2019)

## Evidence

### Dory V 2019

Dory V, Cummings B-A, Mondou M, Young M. (2019). Nudging clinical supervisors to provide better in-training assessment reports. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-019-00554-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Naturalistic comparison of 360 de-identified ITARs sampled across three academic years and three form versions, scored by two independent raters with the CCERR scale and compared by one-way ANOVA. The article reports the total score rose "from 18.0 (SD 2.6) to 18.9 (SD 3.1) and 18.8 (SD 2.6)".

> "The overall quality of completed ITARs increased (from 18.0 (SD 2.6) to 18.9 (SD 3.1) and 18.8 (SD 2.6), p < 0.05)."
