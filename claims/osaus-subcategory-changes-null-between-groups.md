## Evidence

### Lintschinger Josef Michael et al 2026

Lintschinger Josef Michael et al. (2026). A serious video game for teaching basic point-of-care echocardiography skills: a non-inferiority trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08995-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory two-sample t-tests on OSAUS subcategory changes in the randomized trial; "image optimization (-0.16 ± 1.40 vs. -0.23 ± 1.18, p = 0.780)" and systematic examination (p = 0.211) and image interpretation (p = 0.571) were also non-significant. No effect sizes printed.

> "revealed no statistically significant differences between the control and intervention group: applied knowledge of ultrasound equipment (-0.20 ± 0.88 vs. 0.09 ± 0.86, p = 0.110), image optimization (-0.16 ± 1.40 vs. -0.23 ± 1.18, p = 0.780)"
