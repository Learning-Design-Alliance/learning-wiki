## Evidence

### Steven W. Tarr and Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2024

Steven W. Tarr and Emily Alicea-Muñoz. (2024). Reflecting to learn in a physics multimedia communication course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2024.pr.tarr](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2024.pr.tarr)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Observational analysis of student presentations rated against seven CTML principles, with quiz scores compared for presentations adhering or not adhering to each principle (Table I). Personalization showed d = 0.53; Coherence d = 0.14; Signaling d = 0.13; Modality d = 0.030; Embodiment d = 0.014.

> "Across all seven principles, we observed noticeably smaller effect size magnitudes than Mayer. Our students benefited most from observing presentations that followed the Personalization principle."
