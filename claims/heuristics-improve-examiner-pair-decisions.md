## Evidence

### Schauber 2024

Schauber, S. K., Olsen, A. O., Werner, E. L., & Magelssen, M. (2024). Inconsistencies in rater-based assessments mainly affect borderline candidates: but using simple heuristics might improve pass-fail decisions. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 29(5), 1749–1767. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10328-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Simulation over 10,000 randomly sampled examiner pairs from the ten-examiner pool, comparing five decision rules against a random benchmark (Table 2). The decision tree gave "> 90% pass" for 'Lars'; 'Kari' ranged from 64.6 to 73.5% passes. No standardised effect size is printed.

> "in all conditions, the heuristics ratings of dyads exceeded the performance of the benchmark approach where verdicts were determined randomly."
