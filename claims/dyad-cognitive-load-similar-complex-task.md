## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paas cognitive load scores did not differ between dyad and individual groups on day 1 (6.8 vs 6.8, p = 0.89) or day 4 (5.7 vs 6.3, p = 0.28). [→ Abbott Eduardo F. 2021](#abbott-eduardo-f-2021)

## Evidence

### Abbott Eduardo F. 2021

Abbott Eduardo F., Laack Torrey A., Licatino Lauren K., Wood-Wentz Christina M., Warner Paul A., Torsher Laurence C., Newman James S., Rieck Katie M. (2021). Comparison of dyad versus individual simulation-based training on stress, anxiety, cognitive load, and performance: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02786-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Day 1 Paas scale ratings in the randomized trial were equal (6.8 for both groups); the day 4 comparison was also non-significant. The authors call this an unexpected finding against collaborative-load-reduction assumptions.

> "Cognitive load was assessed after each session and was similar between groups (6.8 ± 1.8 for individuals vs 6.8 ± 1.5 for dyads, p = 0.89)."
