### Nagle 2016

Nagle, K., Pratt-Williams, J., Schmidt, R., Swantek, C., Lyulchenko, M., & McGhee, R. (2016). Evaluation of the Rural Math Excel Partnership Project: Final Report. SRI International. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/projects/other/i3_ruralmath.asp

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

This finding comes from event evaluation surveys administered to FMN attendees (Year 2: parents n=62, students n=70). Year 3 responses mirrored Year 2, with 93 percent of students and 97 percent of parents reporting the event was worth attending.

> "In Year 2, 93 percent of students and 97 percent of parents reported that the event was worth attending. Similarly, 89 percent of students and 95 percent of parents said that the FMN helped them understand why STEM courses are important."
