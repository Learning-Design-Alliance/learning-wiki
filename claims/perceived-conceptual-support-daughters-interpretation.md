### Farran 2025

Farran, E. K., Fink, E., Hughes, C., Gilligan-Lee, K. A., & The NewFAMS team. (2025). Limited sex differences in spatial language in parent-child dyads. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102006. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102006

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation offered in the abstract and discussion, not a tested result; it is consistent with their citation of Mascaro et al. (2017) on fathers' analytical language and with prior correlations of parental support with girls' performance. No direct evidence for the mechanism is presented in this study.

> "Higher spatial language quality with daughters could reflect a higher (perceived) need to provide
conceptual support to daughters than sons."
