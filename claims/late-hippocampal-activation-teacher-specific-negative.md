## Evidence

### Samuel Fynes-Clinton 2022

Samuel Fynes-Clinton, Chase Sherwell, Maryam Ziaei, Ashley York, Emma Sanders O'Connor, Kylee Forrest, Libby Flynn, Julie Bower, David Reutens and Annemaree Carroll. (2022). Neural activation during emotional interference corresponds to emotion dysregulation in stressed teachers. npj Science of Learning 7:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00123-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second PLS analysis (LV 42.06% covariance, p = 0.032) of control, GenNeg, GenNeut, SpeNeg conditions in 49 stressed teachers. "The late peak was associated with activation in the hippocampus for the SpeNeg condition only" at 12–15 s post stimulus onset.

> "The late peak was associated with activation in the hippocampus for the SpeNeg condition only, which differentiated from a pattern of increased engagement during general negative, general neutral and the control condition."
