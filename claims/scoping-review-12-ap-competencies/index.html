Yoshimatsu Yuki 2023¶

Yoshimatsu Yuki, Ohtake Yoichi, Ukai Mamiko, Miyagami Taiju, Morikawa Toru, Shimamura Yoshinosuke, Kataoka Yuki, Hashimoto Tadayuki. (2023). “Diagnose, Treat, and SUPPORT”. Clinical competencies in the management of older adults with aspiration pneumonia: a scoping review. European Geriatric Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-023-00898-4

q2 · i? · review · r2

Scoping review following JBI methodology and the PRISMA-ScR extension, searching PubMed and CINAHL for studies of patients aged 65 and older with aspiration pneumonia (2011–July 2022). The review reports that "3 competencies were renamed, and 3 new competencies were added, to create a list of 12 competencies" from 99 included studies.

"Ninety-nine studies were included. Following data extraction from these studies, 3 competencies were renamed, and 3 new competencies were added, to create a list of 12 competencies."
