### Martin Erik Horn 2006

Martin Erik Horn. (2006). The Didactical Relevance of the Pauli Pascal Triangle. GDCP Annual Conference, Berne. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0611277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0611277)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Didactical analysis in the section on the Pascal triangle: it is described as a "cognitive attractor" in commutative mathematics, deeply linked to the Taylor expansion, and an easy entry into hypergeometric functions. The paper quotes Sawyer that 95 to 100 per cent of functions studied by physics students are covered by 2F1[a, b; c; x].

> "The Pascal triangle codifies the binomial coefficients and consequently the binomial formulae. In addition it is structurally deeply linked to the Taylor expansion. And it offers a didactical introduction into hypergeometric functions which is easy to implement and can be taught in a straightforward way."
