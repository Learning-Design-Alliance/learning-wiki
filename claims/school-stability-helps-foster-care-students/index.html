Expert-interview report section on school mobility, school type, and school success. Experts characterized existing research as confirming the stability benefit, while noting the evidence stops short of answering how many school changes produce which outcomes.

"Research seems to confirm that "school stability helps kids" and that changes in school placements resulting from changes in home placements are "a powerful risk factor" for poor educational outcomes."
