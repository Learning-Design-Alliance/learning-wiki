# A general self-report metacognition questionnaire shows no relationship with word problem solving performance and no convergence with on-line metacognitive measures in grade 5

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The self-report questionnaire was not related to the mathematics test and showed no convergence with the think-aloud or VisA on-line measures. [→ Jacobse 2012](#jacobse-2012)

## Evidence

### Jacobse 2012

Jacobse, A. E., & Harskamp, E. G. (2012). Towards efficient measurement of metacognition in mathematical problem solving. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-012-9088-x

`q2 · i?`

Correlational analysis of three metacognition measures in a grade 5 sample (effective n=39). The MSLQ metacognitive self-regulation subscale correlated 0.03 with problem solving, 0.16 with think-aloud, and -0.20 with VisA; "the self-report questionnaire showed no convergence with on-line metacognitive measures."

> "Moreover, the self-report questionnaire showed no convergence with on-line metacognitive measures. Scores on the TA measure and the VisA instrument on the other hand were related, although the bivariate correlation is modest."
