## Evidence

### Keith Oliver and Lei Bao 2002

Keith Oliver and Lei Bao. (2002). Student Resources in quantum mechanics, or why students need meta-resources. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207090](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207090)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The article reports, citing Rosenberg's study of personal epistemology in quantum mechanics learning, that students treated 'the wave function collapse onto one energy eigenvalue as a person deciding between two options'. The study's design is not described in this article.

> "He has shown students treating the wave function collapse onto one energy eigenvalue as a person deciding between two options, such as what to have for dinner."

### Keith Oliver and Lei Bao 2002 (2)

Keith Oliver and Lei Bao. (2002). Student Resources in quantum mechanics, or why students need meta-resources. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207090](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0207090)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Second-hand observation attributed to Rosenberg: the deciding-resource behavior appeared 'from sophomore physics majors to first year graduate students'. No counts or measures are printed.

> "He has seen similar behavior in a number of students at various levels, from sophomore physics majors to first year graduate students."
