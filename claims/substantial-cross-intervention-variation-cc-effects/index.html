Evidence¶

Michael J. Weiss 2023¶

Michael J. Weiss, Marie-Andrée Somers, & Colin Hill. (2023). Empirical Benchmarks for Planning and Interpreting Causal Effects of Community College Interventions. Journal of Postsecondary Student Success 3 (1). https://doi.org/10.33009/fsop_jpss132759

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

FIRC estimates for the semester-1 credits-earned distribution in the Results section: mean β = 0.48 credits, τ = 0.55 credits, and percentiles of −0.03, 0.13, 0.48, 0.60, and 1.29. The article states τ being larger than β indicates "substantial variation in the effectiveness of interventions."

"This estimate of τ is larger in magnitude than the mean impact (β), indicating substantial variation in the effectiveness of interventions under study on this outcome at this time point."
