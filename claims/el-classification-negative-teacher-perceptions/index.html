The brief reports, citing Umansky & Dumont (2021), a nationally representative study finding that "EL classification in kindergarten negatively impacts teachers’ perceptions of students’ academic abilities," with the negative effect reduced in bilingual classroom settings. The brief does not describe the study's design beyond this.

"A nationally representative study highlighted that EL classification in kindergarten negatively impacts teachers’ perceptions of students’ academic abilities across various subjects and grade levels, although this negative effect is reduced in bilingual classroom settings (Umansky & Dumont, 2021)."
