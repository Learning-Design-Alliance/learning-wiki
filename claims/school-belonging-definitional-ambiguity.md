# School belonging research is marked by definitional ambiguity and terminology confusion, including conflation of belonging with acceptance, inclusion, and participation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Expert scholars report that research labeled as belonging actually combines multiple related psychological processes and that belonging is often conflated with acceptance, inclusion, and participation. [→ Kelly-Ann Allen 2025](#kelly-ann-allen-2025)

## Evidence

### Kelly-Ann Allen 2025

Kelly-Ann Allen. (2025). School Belonging: Evidence, Experts, and Everyday Gaps. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10055-x

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In a semi-structured email interview with expert scholar Mark Leary conducted for this narrative-analysis study, Leary identifies a twofold definitional problem, stating researchers "often combine under a single label" several related processes. No effect size is reported.

> "we are actually dealing with a number of different, though related, psychological processes that we often combine under a single label (such as "belonging")"
