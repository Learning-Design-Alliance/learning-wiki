### Mooney SS 2022

Mooney SS, Hiscock RJ, Hicks L, Narula S, Maher PJ, Readman E, Pendlebury A, Ellett L. (2022). We live in a virtual world: Training the trainee using an integrated visual reality simulator curriculum. Aust N Z J Obstet Gynaecol. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13521

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Regression analysis of logged simulator hours against six-month OSA-LS scores in the prospective cohort found "no association found between time on the LapSim® and six‐month OSA‐LS scores" for novice-trained and experienced-trained groups alike.

> "There was no association found between time on the LapSim® and six‐month OSA‐LS scores for either of the VRS‐trained groups (adjusted P‐values >0.05 for all regression analyses)."
