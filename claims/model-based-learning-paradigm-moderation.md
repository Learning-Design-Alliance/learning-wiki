The article reports, citing developmental decision-making studies discussed at the workshop, that the emergence of model-based (goal-directed, flexible but costly) learning depends on paradigm simplification and reward level, not just age.

> "Several studies found that model-based learning does not emerge until adolescence 21,22, yet when model-based learning is tested in a simpli ﬁed paradigm and with higher rewards, children as young as 5 years used these strategies 23."
