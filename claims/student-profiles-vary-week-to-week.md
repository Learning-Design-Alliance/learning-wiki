## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Most students' profiles appear in more than one cluster across the semester (87 ± 4% in traditional labs, 86 ± 4% in inquiry labs), so profiles cannot be collapsed into semester-long behaviour categories. [→ Katherine N. Quinn 2019](#katherine-n-quinn-2019)

## Evidence

### Katherine N. Quinn 2019

Katherine N. Quinn, Kathryn L. McGill, Michelle M. Kelley, Emily M. Smith, and N. G. Holmes (2019). Who does what now? How physics lab instruction impacts student behaviors. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.quinn](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.quinn)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Secondary analysis within the observational study: because each student has one profile per lab period, the authors checked whether individual profiles recur across clusters. They report the printed percentages and attribute week-to-week variation to lab content variability and students changing lab partners.

> "In the traditional labs, 87 ± 4% of students have proﬁles in more than one cluster. Similarly, in the inquiry labs 86 ± 4% of students have proﬁles in more than one cluster."
