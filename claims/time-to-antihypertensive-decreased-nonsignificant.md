## Evidence

### Raney Julia H. 2019

Raney Julia H., Morgan Melissa C., Christmas Amelia, Sterling Mona, Spindler Hilary, Ghosh Rakesh, Gore Aboli, Mahapatra Tanmay, Walker Dilys M. (2019). Simulation-enhanced nurse mentoring to improve preeclampsia and eclampsia care: an education intervention study in Bihar, India. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-019-2186-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

GEE linear regression adjusted for duration between simulations, on continuous time-interval indicators coded from 39 matched simulation videos. Median time to antihypertensive fell from 5.8 to 2.6 min; the decrease was not statistically significant.

> "Time from 'BP measured to magnesium sulfate given' remained relatively constant (p = 0.69), while time from 'BP measured to antihypertensive given' decreased by 3.1 min (p = 0.06) between first and last participation in PE/E simulations."
