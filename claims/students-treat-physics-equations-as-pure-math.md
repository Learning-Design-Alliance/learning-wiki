## Evidence

### Edward F. Redish 2015

Edward F. Redish. (2015). Analysing the Competency of Mathematical Modelling in Physics. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-44887-9_3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-44887-9_3)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Clicker question in a lecture on the electric field given to a class of about 200 students in algebra-based introductory physics who had seen the derivation and could state that the field is independent of the test charge. The article reports "nearly half chose answer (c)"; no effect size is printed.

> "Nonetheless, when asked the question in Figure 1, nearly half chose answer (c). These students treated the physics as a pure math problem"
