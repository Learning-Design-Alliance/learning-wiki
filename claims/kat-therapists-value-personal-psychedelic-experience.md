## Evidence

### Argento E 2024

Argento E, Petker T, Vig J, Robertson C, Jaeger A, Necyk C, Thielking P, Walsh Z. (2024). "This is you teaching you:" Exploring providers' perspectives on experiential learning and enhancing patient safety and outcomes in ketamine-assisted therapy. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0306381

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative IPA analysis of semi-structured interviews with eight KAT therapists (5 female, 3 male) at Numinus clinics; one of the most prominent themes was that personal experience builds rapport and therapeutic alliance, bridging intellectual understanding and a felt sense of the psychedelic experience.

> "All participants, regardless of their prior experiences with psychedelics, expressed that having personal experience allows the therapist to gain a deeper understanding and insights into the client's experience."
