# Students with higher initial achievement in German benefited more from higher class mean achievement levels

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In German classes, the effect of class mean achievement on subsequent individual achievement was stronger for students with higher initial achievement (cross-level interaction). [→ Kopatz 2025](#kopatz-2025)

The abstract reports this cross-level interaction from multilevel models on the German sample: "students with higher initial achievement in German benefited more from higher class mean levels." The Educational Impact statement adds that being in a class with higher achieving classmates benefits higher achieving students in German but not in mathematics.
