## Evidence

### Lin Chen-Ta 2025

Lin Chen-Ta, Liang Sheng-Fu & Shaw Fu-Zen. (2025). Better sleep is associated with higher academic performance from an actigraphy-based analysis of sleep consistency and grades in college students. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-33775-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Mixed-model ANOVA (group × weekday/weekend time) on 14-day actigraphy TST in the median-split sample (Table 3). "A significant main effect of time" reflected longer weekend TST (LS 6.7 h, HS 6.5 h) than weekday TST (LS 5.7 h, HS 6.3 h).

> "A significant main effect of time with medium effect size (Cohen's f = 0.29) was observed for TST, but no group or interaction effects were found."
