## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The overall fresh stillbirth rate dropped from around 9 per 1000 total births in 2017 to 5 per 1000 in 2019, but the ANOVA year effect was not significant (F = 0.766, p = 0.506). [→ Munyaw Yuda 2022](#munyaw-yuda-2022)

## Evidence

### Munyaw Yuda 2022

Munyaw Yuda, Gidabayda Joshua, Yeconia Anita, Guga Godfrey, Mduma Esto, Mdoe Paschal. (2022). Beyond research: improved perinatal care through scale-up of a Moyo fetal heart rate monitor coupled with simulation training in northern Tanzania for helping babies breathe. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-022-03249-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational analysis of DHIS2 routine data on 10,255 total births in 2017 and 9,861 in 2019 across three district hospitals. The article's ANOVA table prints F = 0.766, p = 0.506 for the year effect on fresh stillbirths; no effect size is printed.

> "An overall FSB rate was around 9 cases per 1000 births in 2017 and dropped to 5 cases per 1000 births in 2019, where the rates vary from site to site."
