## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Alqahtani ND 2015

Alqahtani ND, Al-Jewair T, AL-Moammar K, Albarakati SF, ALkofide EA. (2015). Live demonstration versus procedural video: a comparison of two methods for teaching an orthodontic laboratory procedure. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0479-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Questionnaire completed by 42 of 49 students (response rate 85.7%); Table 4 shows means of 3.18 (live) vs 3.80 (video) for the clarity statement, analyzed with independent samples t-test. "A higher mean response for group B was found compared to group A".

> "Independent samples t-test indicated statistically significant differences in the mean response between the two groups for statement 6 on the questionnaire, “The steps in the teaching method were presented in a clear fashion and were easy to understand”. A higher mean response for group B was found compared to group A (P = 0.049)."
