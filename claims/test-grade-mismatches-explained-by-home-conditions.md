## Evidence

### Woolley 1915

Woolley, Helen Thompson. (1915). A new scale of mental and physical measurements for adolescents, and some of its uses. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(9), 521-550. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075644

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Case-file study of the exceptional children from the testing series: records of physical and educational history and home conditions were examined for eleven superior fifth-graders and seventeen inferior eighth-graders. The authors report their hypothesis about social causation of the mismatches was 'absolutely verified'.

> "There were eleven of the very superior fifth grade children—children who ranked 70 or above either at fourteen, 01 at fifteen, or both years; and there were seventeen of the very inferior eighth grade chil- dren, children who ranked 50 or below either at fourteen or at fifteen or at both ages."
