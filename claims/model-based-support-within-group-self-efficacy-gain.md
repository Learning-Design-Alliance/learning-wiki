## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Within-group pre-to-post gains in teaching self-efficacy were significant only for the model-based support group, not for the virtual-student-only or control groups. [→ Chih-Pu Dai · Fengfeng Ke 2026](#chih-pu-dai-fengfeng-ke-2026)

## Evidence

### Chih-Pu Dai · Fengfeng Ke 2026

Chih-Pu Dai · Fengfeng Ke. (2026). Model-based support for teaching practice and self-efficacy in artificial intelligence-enhanced virtual reality teaching simulation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10592-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A paired-sample t-test analysis found that "only the model-based support group (n = 19) showed a significant improvement" in teaching self-efficacy from pre- to post-intervention.

> "Among the study groups, only the model-based support group (n = 19) showed a significant improvement (t (18) = − 3.03, p =.007) from pre-intervention (M = 4.27, SD =.63) to post-intervention (M = 4.58, SD =.43)."
