### Tejinder Kaur 2022

Tejinder Kaur, Aishwarya Banavathu, David Blair, Rahul Choudhary, and Alex Foppolli. (2022). Can 8-Year-Olds Understand Concepts of Einsteinian Physics?. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The authors compressed the whole program into ten days, which they identify as a limitation; at this age, reinforcement is critical for comprehension.

> "This paper concludes that children aged 7-8 are not too young to learn key Einsteinian concepts, and the benefits of reinforcement indicate that longer programs are needed for ideas to be consolidated."

### Tejinder Kaur 2022 (2)

Tejinder Kaur, Aishwarya Banavathu, David Blair, Rahul Choudhary, and Alex Foppolli. (2022). Can 8-Year-Olds Understand Concepts of Einsteinian Physics?. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.00842)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Class 1's regular teacher revised content the day after each presentation and prepared students before lessons, while Class 2's relief teachers did not repeat any activities or lessons.

> "In this study, class 1 teacher reinforced the learning through repetition, but this was not done for Class 2."
