`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of LLM performance on an LLM-translated versus an expert-translated Czech version of question 29. The LLM's translated question subtly implied the air force had to be considered; the overall LLM-translated Czech FCI performance was 73% with one incorrect text-only response.

> "A Czech translation of the FCI done by a physicist is available on PhysPort. When given the physicist's translation of the question, GPT-4o answered correctly 3 out of 3 times."
