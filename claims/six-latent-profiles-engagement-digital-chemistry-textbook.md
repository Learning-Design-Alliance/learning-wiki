## Evidence

### Yoana Omarchevska 2026

Yoana Omarchevska, Brendan A. Schuetze, Juliane Richter, Sascha Schanze, Nina Ulrich, Katharina Scheiter. (2026). From Passive to Metacognitive: The Role of Mode of Engagement in Enabling Lasting Learning Using Digital Media. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10120-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis of log-file engagement indicators from 235 students using the mclust algorithm in the tidyLPA package; models with 1 to 10 profiles were compared. The authors report the six-profile solution was best by BIC and parsimony, with "entropy = 0.98".

> "A comparison of Bayesian Information Criteria along with a preference for parsimony suggested that a six-profile solution was the best solution among the tested models, see Fig. 6."
