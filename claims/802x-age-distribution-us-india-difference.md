## Evidence

### Saif Rayyan 2013

Saif Rayyan, Daniel T. Seaton, John Belcher, David E. Pritchard, Isaac Chuang. (2013). Participation And performance In 8.02x Electricity And Magnetism: The First Physics MOOC From MITx. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive demographic analysis of the 8.02x enrollment survey data (Figure 1). The article reports the age distribution is "skewed toward college-aged students" with mean 27.5, median 28, and mode 20.

> "The distribution of age (Left) is skewed toward college-aged students and has a long tail extending to higher ages. The mean, median, and mode of the age distribution are 27.5, 28, and 20, respectively."

### Saif Rayyan 2013 (2)

Saif Rayyan, Daniel T. Seaton, John Belcher, David E. Pritchard, Isaac Chuang. (2013). Participation And performance In 8.02x Electricity And Magnetism: The First Physics MOOC From MITx. arXiv. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.060)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Country-of-origin comparison from Table 1 of the enrollment demographics. US participants' mean age is 32.7 versus India's 22.7, with about 40% versus 12% older than 25 respectively; the article notes similar trends in other MOOCs.

> "The mean age of participants from the US is 32.7, with ≈ 40% of the population older than 25. In contrast, India has a lower average age of 22.7, with only 12% older than 25."
