Evidence¶

Luis de-la-Fuente-Valentín 2011¶

Luis de-la-Fuente-Valentín, Abelardo Pardo, Carlos Delgado Kloos. (2011). Generic service integration in adaptive learning experiences using IMS learning design. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2010.12.007

q2 · i? · design · r2

Analysis of open-ended questionnaire answers (Q3.f) and group discussion in the workshop case study. The researchers found it surprising that only two participants proposed tools, summarizing the prevailing opinion as one participant's statement that any tool was a novelty given their limited formation in the field.

"Despite that educators perceived the potential of Web 2.0 tools in their learning context, they do not currently consider these tools to be included as part of their courses and the lack of training is perceived as one of the major determining factors."
