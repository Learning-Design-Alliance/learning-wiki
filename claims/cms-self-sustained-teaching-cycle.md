# The CMS User Support operates a self-sustained cycle in which expert users teach new users, who become experts and improve the instruction materials

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The CMS User Support draws manpower from the user community itself: expert users help new users learn the analysis tools, and those new users become experts who provide feedback to improve instruction material and recruit further helpers. ## Evidence

### S. Malik and K. Lassila-Perini 2011

S. Malik and K. Lassila-Perini. (2011). An outlook of the user support model to educate the users community at the CMS Experiment. Proceedings of the DPF-2011 Conference, Providence, RI. [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/331/8/082006](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/331/8/082006)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive account from the Organization section: because only a very small number of dedicated staff do User Support, manpower is drawn from the community, creating what the authors call a self-sustained cycle of teaching and learning. No quantitative evaluation is reported.

> "The users who are expert in different physics analysis tools help new users learn and master them. They in turn become experts and provide feedback to further improve the instruction material and style."
