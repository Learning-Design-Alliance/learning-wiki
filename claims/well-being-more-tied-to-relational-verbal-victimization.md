### Herbert W. Marsh 2023

Herbert W. Marsh, Jiesi Guo, Philip D. Parker, Reinhard Pekrun, Geetanjali Basarkod, Theresa Dicke, et al. (2023). Peer Victimization: an Integrative Review and Cross-National Test of a Tripartite Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09765-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The article's cross-national PISA2018 analysis of positive and negative well-being indicators (affects, sense-of-belonging, eudaimonia, life satisfaction, fear of failure) found "all 13 well-being indicators were more strongly related" to verbal and relational than physical victimization. No effect sizes are printed in the supplied text.

### Herbert W. Marsh 2023 (2)

Herbert W. Marsh, Jiesi Guo, Philip D. Parker, Reinhard Pekrun, Geetanjali Basarkod, Theresa Dicke, et al. (2023). Peer Victimization: an Integrative Review and Cross-National Test of a Tripartite Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09765-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The review attributes to Marsh et al. (2011), in Australian student data, that "depression and internalization of anger correlated more highly with verbal (r = .38 and r = .32) and relational (r = .40 and r = .33) than with physical (r = .26 and r = .19) victimization."

> "They found, for example, that depression and internalization of anger correlated more highly with verbal (r = .38 and r = .32) and relational (r = .40 and r = .33) than with physical (r = .26 and r = .19) victimization."
