## Evidence

### Behringer Hannah K. 2022

Behringer Hannah K., Saksvig Emilie R., Boedeker Peter J., Elish Paul N., Kay Christi M., Calvert Hannah G., Meyer Adria M., Gazmararian Julie A. (2022). Physical activity and academic achievement: an analysis of potential student- and school-level moderators. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-022-01348-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal multilevel models used Grade 4 MVPA to predict Grade 5 fall teacher-assigned course marks in math, reading, spelling, and writing. No interaction reached the Bonferroni-adjusted threshold; the article prints no effect sizes, so i is null.

> "Longitudinal analyses indicated that there was no significant moderation of the MVPA-academic achievement relationship by student gender, free/reduced-price lunch eligibility, nor race/ethnicity for any of the academic outcomes. Potential school-level moderators were also not found to be significant in the longitudinal analysis"
