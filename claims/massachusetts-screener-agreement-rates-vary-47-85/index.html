Analysis of the roughly 3 to 4 percent of students per period who took more than one screening assessment, comparing risk classifications across assessment combinations. The report prints agreement rates varying "from 47 percent to 85 percent"; no effect size is printed.

"Rates of agreement between screening assessments— that is, whether students would be considered at risk of reading difficulty— varied from 47 percent to 85 percent depending on screening assessment combinations."
