## Evidence

### Ishari Amarasinghe; Davinia Hernández-Leo; H. Ulrich Hoppe 2021

Ishari Amarasinghe; Davinia Hernández-Leo; H. Ulrich Hoppe (2021). Deconstructing orchestration load: comparing teacher support through mirroring and guiding. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09351-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Epistemic network analysis of coded teacher actions across six teachers' classroom sessions, with three conditions per teacher in a within-subject design. The ENA difference network showed stronger edges linking announcements to dashboard tabs and interventions under guiding support. The abstract states guiding "enabled teachers to perform more orchestration actions" than mirroring.

> "the results show that the guiding support enabled teachers to perform more orchestration actions, more targeted interactions and to make more announcements to the class (regarding time, phase transitions, and students’ activity participation) when compared to the mirroring support."
