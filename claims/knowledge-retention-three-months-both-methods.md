## Evidence

### Stokstad JM 2026

Stokstad JM, Nedrehagen ES, Hufthammer KO. (2026). The Jigsaw teaching method compared to traditional teaching on anatomy and physiology knowledge in higher education – a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26:252. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08608-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Longitudinal model estimates in the RCT (41 students scored at both post-intervention and follow-up) show a small mean decline from post-test, while both groups remained significantly above baseline at three months.

> "From post-intervention to the 3-month follow-up, there was a mean deterioration of 1.3 points (CI: 0.1–2.6, p < 0.03) for the TT group and 1.4 points (CI: 0.3–2.5, p < 0.02) for the JS group."
