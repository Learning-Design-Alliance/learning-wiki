## Evidence

### Egenberg Signe 2017

Egenberg Signe, Karlsen Bjørg, Massay Deodatus, Kimaro Happiness, Bru Lars Edvin. (2017). “No patient should die of PPH just for the lack of training!” Experiences from multi-professional simulation training on postpartum hemorrhage in northern Tanzania: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0957-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of ten focus group discussions with 42 nurse midwives, doctors, and medical attendants at two Tanzanian hospitals two months after simulation training. The analysis found "the overall emphasis within the FGDs was the importance of training in multi-professional teams during an emergency."

> "Improvements in clinical PPH management were experienced after the training, and the overall emphasis within the FGDs was the importance of training in multi-professional teams during an emergency."
