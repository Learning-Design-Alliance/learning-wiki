## Evidence

### Brender 2025

Brender, J., El-Hamamsy, L., Giang, C., Mathex, L., Käser, T., & Mondada, F. (2025). The handwriting programming language for primary school: aligning computer science education with established teaching practices. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10503-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Study with 49 pre-service teachers from Canton Ticino, Switzerland, who evaluated HPL and a paper-based TPL using a TAM survey adapted from Teo (2009). "84% of teachers were willing to continue to do other activities with HPL, while only 54% were willing to do so with TPL." Spearman correlations between demographics and perception yielded no significant correlations.

> "When asked whether they would like to continue doing other activities with these platforms, 49% chose both, 35% chose HPL, and 6% chose TPL, with only 4% expressing they would not want to use either. This means that 84% of teachers were willing to continue to do other activities with HPL"
