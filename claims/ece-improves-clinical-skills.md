### Shaban Eman E. 2025

Shaban Eman E., Elmelliti Hussam, Shaban Amira, Shaban Ahmed, Zaki Hany A. (2025). Impact of early clinical exposure on medical students’ competency development: a systematic review of evidence from Asian and African countries. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08103-9

Narrative synthesis of an interventional study reported within the systematic review: post-OSCE scores were "significantly higher among medical students who received the ECE intervention" (7.45 vs. 5.08; p < 0.0001).

> "Rawekar and colleagues observed that the post-OSCE scores were significantly higher among medical students who received the ECE intervention compared to those subjected to the traditional teaching schedule (7.45 vs. 5.08; p < 0.0001)"

### Shaban Eman E. 2025 (2)

Shaban Eman E., Elmelliti Hussam, Shaban Amira, Shaban Ahmed, Zaki Hany A. (2025). Impact of early clinical exposure on medical students’ competency development: a systematic review of evidence from Asian and African countries. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08103-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quasi-experimental study synthesized narratively: ECE-group students had "significantly higher mean scores than those in the conventional teaching group" (18.01 vs. 14.32; p < 0.001) for blood pressure measuring skills.

> "Sekkizhar et al. [14] found that students in the ECE group had significantly higher mean scores than those in the conventional teaching group (18.01 vs. 14.32; p < 0.001), implying that students in the ECE group acquired and demonstrated improved blood pressure measuring skills."
