Randomized controlled trial of TLTS in two southeastern U.S. school districts examining two-year student achievement outcomes. The report states students in TLTS schools "performed 0.05 standard deviations higher in ELA than students in comparison schools (p-value = 0.019)" — a printed effect size of 0.05 SD, below d = 0.2.
