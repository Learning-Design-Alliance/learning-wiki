Doxey, J., Baty, B. J., Bisordi, K., & Dixon, S. (2026). Assessing genetic counselors' teaching experience and approaches to teaching in the classroom. Journal of Genetic Counseling. https://doi.org/10.1002/jgc4.70207

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Zero-order correlation analysis among 89 ATI-R respondents testing the hypothesis that more teaching experience relates to more learner-centered approaches. The printed effect sizes are r = 0.23 (lectures taught) and r = 0.22 (percentage of time spent teaching), both small positive correlations; the article reports "r(87) = 0.23, p = 0.028" and "r(87) = 0.22, p = 0.042".
