# Non-radiology residents taught with an image case database in gastroenterology scored higher at mid-term (P = 0.006) and exit (P = 0.002) examinations than residents taught conventionally, with no admission difference (P = 0.728)

## Evidence

### He Xiayin 2025

He Xiayin, Li Yangsheng, Gao Chengcheng, Zhu Xiangwen, Xia Qianqian, Han Zhijiang, Zhu Jiying. (2025). Study on the improvement of image interpretation skill of non-radiology residents based on an image case database in gastroenterology department. BMC Medical Education 25:936. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07537-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-center quasi-experiment with sequential cohorts of non-radiology residents in gastroenterology (experimental n = 27, control n = 24), compared with Mann-Whitney U tests. The article reports "significantly higher" scores for the experimental group at mid-term and exit, with medians of 70 vs 60 and 90 vs 85 respectively; no effect size is printed.

> "The scores of non-radiology residents in the experimental group and the control group showed no difference in the admission examination ( P = 0.728). However, the scores in the experimental group were significantly higher than those in the control group at the mid-term (P = 0.006) and exit examination (P = 0.002)"
