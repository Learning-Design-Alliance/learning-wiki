ENHANCING EMOTIONAL AND CONTENT ACQUISITION PERFORMANCE THROUGH GARDNER'S MULTIPLE INTELLIGENCES THEORY IN TECHNOLOGY AND DESIGN LESSONS. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA comparing post-test emotional scores of the experimental group (87 subjects) and control group (73 subjects), reported in section 3.3. The article reports a statistically significant difference favouring the experimental group; Table 1 means are 63.28 (experimental) versus 54.74 (control).

> "ANOVA test also gives us the statistical data of a p-value of 0.00 for emotional performance and 0.34 for content acquisition (cognitive yield)."
