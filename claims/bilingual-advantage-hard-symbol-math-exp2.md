## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Experiment 2 (N=105) replicated the bilingual advantage on hard Symbol Math items (ηp² = 0.06) after controlling for basic math. [→ Mielicki 2017](#mielicki-2017)

## Evidence

### Mielicki 2017

Mielicki, M. K., Kacinik, N. A., & Wiley, J. (2017). Bilingualism and symbolic abstraction: Implications for algebra learning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.03.002

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Experiment 2 with 105 undergraduates (64 bilingual, 41 monolingual) replicated Experiment 1's ANCOVA design with basic math as covariate, adding executive control and SES measures. The follow-up test again showed the effect "onlyontheharditems,F(1,102)=6.49,p <0.01, ηp 2 =0.06", a printed effect size of ηp² = 0.06.
