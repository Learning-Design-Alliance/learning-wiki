Nor Fadzlinda Ishak and Paul Seedhouse 2012¶

Nor Fadzlinda Ishak and Paul Seedhouse. (2012). Interactive Digital Kitchen: The impact on Language learning. EUROCALL Conference, Gothenburg, Sweden, Proceedings. https://digitalinstitute.ncl.ac.uk/ilablearn/kitchen

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Open-ended questionnaire administered to the experimental group after cooking. The article also reports "All of them claimed that they had learnt new words and five stated that they had learnt instruction patterns too" and that only 53% used the online dictionary or websites.

"100% of the students enjoyed the session. 82% said the task was easy. 14% claimed it was neither easy nor hard but one student (3.6%) thought that it was 'a bit difficult'."
