`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Development study across six pilot versions administered January–November 2022, combining three rounds of student interviews with three full-class beta pilots. Participants were recruited from different types of institutions and introductory physics courses. The authors report "39 interviews and beta testing with around 2000 students" as the evidence base for their evidentiary arguments.

> "Even with fairly robust evidentiary arguments (as exemplified in Table IV) resulting from 39 interviews and beta testing with around 2000 students, it is likely there are examples of student reasoning that we did not observe."
