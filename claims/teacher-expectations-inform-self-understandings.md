## Evidence

### Verhoeven 2019

Verhoeven, M., Poorthuis, A. M. G., & Volman, M. (2019). The Role of School in Adolescents' Identity Development. A Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9457-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of 17 articles (one theoretical, 16 ethnographic) on teacher expectations, based on classroom observations, teacher interviews, and student interviews. One study showed a student changed her story of herself as a mathematics learner in relation to how her teacher positioned her.

> "the studies do suggest that adolescents 'self-understandings are informed by their perceptions of their teachers ' expectations"

### Verhoeven 2019 (2)

Verhoeven, M., Poorthuis, A. M. G., & Volman, M. (2019). The Role of School in Adolescents' Identity Development. A Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9457-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that ethnographic studies employing different perspectives found teachers (perceivably) hold different expectations of groups distinguished by ethnic background, perceived academic abilities, and/or gender; one interview and questionnaire study of 33 diverse high school students found students spoke about ethnic/racial biases in science classes.

> "this suggests that inequalities may occur in (perceived) teacher expectations across different groups of students."
