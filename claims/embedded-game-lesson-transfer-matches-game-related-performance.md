## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Posttest scores on transfer questions did not differ significantly from scores on matched game-related questions, which the authors take to indicate that transfer was fostered. [→ Anne van der Linden 2023](#anne-van-der-linden-2023)

## Evidence

### Anne van der Linden 2023

Anne van der Linden, Ralph F. G. Meulenbroeks and Wouter R. van Joolingen. (2023). Implementing an intrinsically integrated game on Newtonian mechanics in the classroom: outcomes in terms of conceptual understanding and transfer. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.01714](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.01714)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired samples t-test within the posttest of the same case study, comparing three transfer questions with matched game-related questions. Means were "M=2.63, SD= .63" versus "M=2.56, SD= .64"; "No significant difference was found; t(26) = 1.000, p = .327".

> "Another paired samples t-test was performed to examine the mean differences between posttest questions on game related situations (M=2.63, SD= .63) and transfer situations (M=2.56, SD= .64), No significant difference was found; t(26) = 1.000, p = .327."
