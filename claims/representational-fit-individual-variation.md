# Identical representational designs produced wide individual variation in what felt helpful versus overwhelming (representational fit)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` What counted as concrete or overwhelming was not a property of the representation alone but depended on the learner's interpretive capacity, prior habits, and current goals. [→ fit-theme](#fit-theme)

## Evidence

### fit-theme

Naaz Sibia, Jessica Wen, Amber Richardson, Yashika Jain, Khushi Malik, Bogdan Simion, Carolina Nobre, Angela Zavaleta Bernuy, Andrew Petersen, and Michael Liut. 2026. Code as Anchor, Memory and Metaphor as Support: Learner Experiences with Multi-View Visualizations. ICER 2026 Vol. 1. https://doi.org/10.1145/3765964.3811662

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative theme from interview accounts in the same study: some students found metaphors essential while others found even memory-view arrows overwhelming, despite identical designs and similar experience levels. Interpretive finding; no effect size.

> "This variation reveals that what counts as "concrete" or "overwhelming" is not a property of the representation alone but depends on the learner's interpretive capacity, prior habits, and current goals."
