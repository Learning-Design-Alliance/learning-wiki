## Evidence

### Buhumaid R 2025

Buhumaid R, Kilian P, Zidan MA, Salman H, AlSharhan R, Khansaheb H, Zary N. (2025). From novice to proficient: a longitudinal study of POCUS skill development through a hybrid certification program in the United Arab Emirates. BMC Med Educ. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07138-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA of self-assessment survey data from six participants with complete data at four evaluation points. The abstract-level result is supported here; the improvement was significant (p < 0.001), with "mean difference = 17.3" reported in Results.

> "Mean total confidence scores increased from 64.0 (SD = 9.9) pre-program to 81.3 (SD = 6.7) post-program (p < 0.001)."
