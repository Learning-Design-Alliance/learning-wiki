### Tyrrell Edward G 2025

Tyrrell Edward G, Sandhu Sardip K, Berry Kathryn, Ghannam Suzan F, Lewis Sarah A, Crowfoot Daniel, Sahota Gurvinder Singh, Carson Julie, Wilson Emma E, Taggar Jaspal. (2025). Web-Based AI-Driven Virtual Patient Simulator Versus Actor-Based Simulation for Teaching Consultation Skills: Multicenter Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/71667

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cost comparison analysis of the two interventions as delivered. AB-CST costs included GP tutor and actor time plus 10% overheads across 54 sessions; AI-CST costs were based on 9 allocated usage credits per student. The article reports AI-CST at £33.48 (US $42.22) versus £61.75 (US $77.87) per student. Nonrecurring setup costs were excluded.

> "The steady-state direct cost of delivering AI-CST (£33.48; US $42.22 per student) was substantially lower than for AB-CST (£61.75; US $77.87 per student)."
