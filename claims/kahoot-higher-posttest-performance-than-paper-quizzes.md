## Evidence

### Saovapa Wichadee and Fasawang Pattanapichet 2017

Saovapa Wichadee and Fasawang Pattanapichet. (2017). Enhancement of Performance and Motivation through Application of Digital Games in an English Language Class. Teaching English with Technology, 18(1), 77-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1170635

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of two intact sections (38 experimental, 39 control) of second-year Thai university students; one-way ANCOVA compared adjusted post-test means with pre-test as covariate. The adjusted means were "22.74 and 19.91 accordingly", with F = 25.039, p = .000.

> "the outcome revealed that the mean scores of both the control group and the experimental group carried a significant difference ( F = 25.039, p = .000). The assumption then was substantiated. Th at is, students in the experimental group achieved higher test scores than those in the control group. The mean scores were 22.74 and 19.91 accordingly."
