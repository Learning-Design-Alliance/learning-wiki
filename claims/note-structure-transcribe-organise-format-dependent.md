## Evidence

### Jansen 2017

Jansen, R. S., Lakens, D., & IJsselsteijn, W. (2017). An integrative review of the cognitive costs and benefits of note-taking. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.10.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review of three Bui et al. (2013) experiments (n = 80, n = 76, n = 76) using an 11-minute audio lecture with organised versus transcribed note instructions, tested with free recall and short answer tests.

> "Results consistently indicated better memory performance with transcribed notes compared to the organised note-taking condition. It therefore appears more important to write down as much information as possible, than to organise the information."
