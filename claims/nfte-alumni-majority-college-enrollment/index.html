Nakamoto 2017¶

Nakamoto, J. & Rice, J. (2017). Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship (NFTE) 2016 Alumni Survey Findings. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED582976

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive survey analysis of alumni respondents. The report also states 91 percent of respondents who finished college completed a four-year degree or higher, and 38 percent majored in business, twice the national average it cites.

"the majority of survey respondents who were no longer enrolled in high school are currently attending college (58 percent) or graduated from college (21 percent)"
