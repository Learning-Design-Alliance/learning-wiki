## Evidence

### Brambila-Tapia Aniel Jessica Leticia 2022

Brambila-Tapia Aniel Jessica Leticia, Miranda-Lavastida Aris Judit, Vázquez-Sánchez Nancy Araceli, Franco-López Nancy Lizbeth, Pérez-González Martha Catalina, Nava-Bustos Gonzalo, Gutiérrez-Rodríguez Francisco José, Mora-Moreno Francisco Fabián. (2022). Association of Health and Psychological Factors with Academic Achievement and Non-Verbal Intelligence in University Students with Low Academic Performance: The Influence of Sex. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19084804

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Sex-stratified bivariate correlation analysis (Spearman/Pearson) in 59 women who had failed the same subject twice. The article reports a "significant positive correlation between GPA and non-verbal intelligence (r = 0.535)".

> "we observed that women presented a significant positive correlation between GPA and non-verbal intelligence (r = 0.535), admission exam test (r = 0.445) and preparatory GPA (r = 0.381)"

### Brambila-Tapia Aniel Jessica Leticia 2022 (2)

Brambila-Tapia Aniel Jessica Leticia, Miranda-Lavastida Aris Judit, Vázquez-Sánchez Nancy Araceli, Franco-López Nancy Lizbeth, Pérez-González Martha Catalina, Nava-Bustos Gonzalo, Gutiérrez-Rodríguez Francisco José, Mora-Moreno Francisco Fabián. (2022). Association of Health and Psychological Factors with Academic Achievement and Non-Verbal Intelligence in University Students with Low Academic Performance: The Influence of Sex. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19084804

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Sex-stratified bivariate correlations in 65 men from the same cross-sectional sample. GPA correlated significantly with having a romantic partner (r = 0.275); the GPA–non-verbal intelligence correlation in men was r = 0.163 and not significant.

> "In the case of men, positive significant correlations were observed between GPA and having a romantic partner (r = 0.275), and positive tendencies were observed with having a job (r = 0.224, p = 0.08), emotion regulation (r = 0.201, p = 0.108), self-motivation (r = 0.189, p = 0.132)"
