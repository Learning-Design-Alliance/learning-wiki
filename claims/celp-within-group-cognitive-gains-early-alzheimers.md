`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group Wilcoxon signed rank analysis of the pilot randomized trial's CELP arm (n = 7 randomized, 19 completers overall). The article reports "significant positive changes in MMSE" and DRS and BAPM, with HKLLT marginal; no effect sizes are printed.

> "For CELP, there were significant positive changes in MMSE (mean change 2.67, P = 0.04), DRS (mean change 4.67, P = 0.03), and BAPM (mean change −0.26, P = 0.03), respectively, as well as a marginal significant change in HKLLT (mean change 4.17, P = 0.06)."
