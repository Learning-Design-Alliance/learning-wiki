## Evidence

### Zhang W 2024

Zhang W, Peng Y, Zhuang H, Yu H, Liu Q, Gu Y, Yao J. (2024). Application of direct observation of operational skills in nursing skill evaluation of pressure injury: A randomized clinical trial. Int Wound J. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.14498

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Critical thinking disposition was measured with the CTDI-CV (70 items, seven dimensions) before training and three months after. Table 5 prints follow-up total scores of 278.94 (SD 12.77) control versus 296.25 (SD 13.09) study, t = 5.680, p = 0.000; baseline difference was not significant (p = 0.566).

> "three months after the completion of the training, the study group of nurses had an average total score of >280 points, indicating a positive critical thinking disposition. In contrast, the control group of nurses had an average total score of <280 points"

### Zhang W 2024 (2)

Zhang W, Peng Y, Zhuang H, Yu H, Liu Q, Gu Y, Yao J. (2024). Application of direct observation of operational skills in nursing skill evaluation of pressure injury: A randomized clinical trial. Int Wound J. https://doi.org/10.1111/iwj.14498

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Dimension-level follow-up comparisons in the same trial: analytical ability t = 3.084, p = 0.003; self-confidence t = 3.856, p = 0.000; curiosity t = 3.016, p = 0.004; the other four dimensions showed no statistically significant differences (p > 0.05).

> "there were significant statistical differences between the two groups of nurses in terms of analytical ability, self-confidence and curiosity (p < 0.01). However, there were no statistically significant differences between the two groups in terms of truth-seeking, open-mindedness, systematic ability and cognitive maturity"
