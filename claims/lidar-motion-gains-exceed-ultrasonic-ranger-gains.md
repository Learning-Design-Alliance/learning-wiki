## Evidence

### O'Brien 2023

O'Brien, D. J., Vieyra, R. E., Vieyra Cortés, C., Johnson-Glenberg, M. C., & Megowan-Romanowicz, C. (2023). Evaluating Learning of Motion Graphs with a LiDAR-Based Smartphone Application. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1119/perc.2023.pr.megowan-romanowicz](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2023.pr.megowan-romanowicz)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of eight laboratory sections in a non-major introductory physics course (four LiDAR Motion, four ultrasonic ranger). An ANOVA on post-minus-pre gains found the LiDAR group's increase "greater than that of the sonic group's at a statistically significant level, F(1, 104) = 4.02, p < .05."

> "Per an ANOVA comparing these gains (post - pretest), the increase in the LiDAR group's mean score after intervention was greater than that of the sonic group's at a statistically significant level, F(1, 104) = 4.02, p < .05."
