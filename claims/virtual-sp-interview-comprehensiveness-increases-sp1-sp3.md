`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group analysis of DoctorU platform virtual standardized patient interview scores across three sequential cases (SP1, SP2, SP3). The article reports a progressive increase in inquiry comprehensiveness with statistically significant improvement between successive consultations; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "A progressive increase was observed in the comprehensiveness of students' patient inquiries from SP1 to SP3. Statistical analysis confirmed that the improvement was significant between SP1 and SP2 (P < 0.01) and highly significant between SP1 and SP3 (P < 0.001)"
