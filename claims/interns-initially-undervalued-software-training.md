## Evidence

### Tulika Bose 2022

Tulika Bose, Sudhir Malik, Meenakshi Narain. (2022). U.S. CMS - PURSUE (Program for Undergraduate Research SUmmer Experience). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.10109](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.10109)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational feedback from the initial three-week survey of the 2022 PURSUE interns. The authors report that interns "quickly realised its indispensability for their projects"; no effect size or test statistic is printed, so the finding is descriptive.

> "Most interns were not appreciative of software skills initially. One intern remarked I do believe we are not getting a lot of time to actually do research. It seems like a lot of learning and rather than research. but quickly realised its indispensability for their projects."
