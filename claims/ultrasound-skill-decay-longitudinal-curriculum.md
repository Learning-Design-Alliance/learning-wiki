## Evidence

### Neubauer R 2025

Neubauer R, Recker F, Weimer J, Jenssen C, Nürnberg D, Karlas T, Westerway SC, Ruppert JP, Daum N, Gschmack A, Dong Y, Möller K, Nourkami-Tutdibi N, Barth G, Şirli R, Lucius C, Prats M, Sänger SL, Keil M, Zervides C, Hoffmann B, Dietrich CF. (2025). Verifying learning success: assessment and certification of student ultrasound education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07469-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The review asserts, citing prior work, that "acquired ultrasound skills will decrease over time if not continually reinforced by repeated practice", noting students often lack access to equipment, patients, and curricular time that clinicians use to maintain skills.

> "As with any form of knowledge or practical skill, acquired ultrasound skills will decrease over time if not continually reinforced by repeated practice"

### Neubauer R 2025 (2)

Neubauer R, Recker F, Weimer J, Jenssen C, Nürnberg D, Karlas T, Westerway SC, Ruppert JP, Daum N, Gschmack A, Dong Y, Möller K, Nourkami-Tutdibi N, Barth G, Şirli R, Lucius C, Prats M, Sänger SL, Keil M, Zervides C, Hoffmann B, Dietrich CF. (2025). Verifying learning success: assessment and certification of student ultrasound education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07469-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review identifies an evidence gap: "only few studies examine the long-time retention of ultrasound skills", so the optimal timing of refresher training remains undetermined, and appropriate timing may differ by examination type and curricular structure.

> "To date, only few studies examine the long-time retention of ultrasound skills to identify the time when further training should take place"
