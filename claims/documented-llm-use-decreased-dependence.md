## Evidence

### Yuan-Sen Ting & Teaghan O'Briain 2026

Yuan-Sen Ting & Teaghan O'Briain. (2026). Teaching Astronomy with Large Language Models. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.33232/001c.160220](https://doi.org/10.33232/001c.160220)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational self-report analysis across four homework assignments in a 12-student astronomy course, with post-course survey confirmation. The authors report this as their key observational finding: students "rely on LLMs progressively less as the course advanced". No effect size is printed.

> "instead of increasing LLM usage, actively asking students to document and think critically about their usage appeared to lead many to rely on LLMs progressively less as the course advanced"
