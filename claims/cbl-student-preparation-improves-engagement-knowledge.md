`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's preparation programme theory cites Lu (2024), where a flipped-classroom CBL design shifting content to pre-class preparation produced improved knowledge outcomes versus lectures. Nordquist (2012) conversely found that poor student preparation 'had a negative impact on the quality of the interactive discussions'.
