`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive reporting from the LPC section: the LPC serves about seven hundred US physicists, hosts many CMS conveners, and its fellows focus on Higgs, SUSY and Exotica. The one-third figure is a descriptive attribution, not a causal estimate.

> "A measure of the LPC contribution to the CMS physics and its success can be gathered from the fact that, of the over 100 publications so far, by the CMS experiment, LPC has a direct contribution in a third of these."
