## Evidence

### Laura Ríos 2019

Laura Ríos, Benjamin Pollard, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2019). Using think-aloud interviews to characterize model-based reasoning in electronics for a laboratory course assessment. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010140)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of revision episodes in the 10 TAPS interviews found revisions dominated by test/measurement apparatus changes (function generator, power supply) and resistor swaps. The authors report "no evidence that revisions to either the physical or measurement model were made," and note revisions were often disconnected from proposed causes.

> "Interestingly, we found no evidence that revisions to either the physical or measurement model were made, even though in principle students could have used a revised equation for the gain which includes a frequency dependence."
