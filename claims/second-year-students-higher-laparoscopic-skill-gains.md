## Evidence

### Pereira CWL 2014

Pereira CWL, Paggi CCM, Daniellson D, Filho CAM, Aimoré BE, de Paula LM, Salvalaggio P. (2014). Development of laparoscopic skills in Medical students naive to surgical training. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.1590/S1679-45082014AO3237

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate and multivariate analyses of predictors of performance gain (final placement among the 20 best scores) in the 68-student cohort. Table 2 prints OR 4.8 (95% CI 1.5-14.9, p=0.007) for second-year undergraduates, the only significant predictor; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "The univariate analysis identified that being a second-year student was a favorable factor for skill gain. In the multivariate analysis after training, this was the only variable significantly associated with improvement in skill acquisition."
