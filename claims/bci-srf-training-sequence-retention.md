### Yuan Liu 2024

Yuan Liu, Shuaifei Huang, Weiguo Xu, Zhuang Wang & Dong Ming. (2024). An fMRI study on the generalization of motor learning after brain actuated supernumerary robot training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00294-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA of training sequence accuracy showed a significant TIME × GROUP interaction (F(2,34)=4.794, p=0.0147), but "no signiﬁcant differences at Post and FU among 2 groups" (Post p=0.8375; FU p=0.3342). Post-hoc tests showed the FU vs Pre difference was significant only in the BCI-SRF group.

> "Post-hoc tests revealed a signi ﬁcant increase not only for the BCI-SRF group (Post vs Pre:p = 0.0001) but also for the ﬁnger training group (Post vs Pre:p = 0.0045). However, improvement in FU vs Pre was found only in the BCI-SRF group (FU vs Pre:p = 0.0003), but not for the ﬁnger training group (FU vs Pre:p =0 . 4 1 8 )"
