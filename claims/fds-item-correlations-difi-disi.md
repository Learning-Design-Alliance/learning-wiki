## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In the 350-item analysis, DIFi showed a significant negative correlation with FDs/item and DISi a significant positive correlation with FDs/item, with Pearson r = -0.66 and r = 0.317 respectively. [→ Puthiaparampil Thomas 2021](#puthiaparampil-thomas-2021)

## Evidence

### Puthiaparampil Thomas 2021

Puthiaparampil Thomas, Rahman Mizanur. (2021). How important is distractor efficiency for grading Best Answer Questions? BMC Medical Education, 21:29. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02463-0

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of the 350 BAQ items (Table 4), with a printed effect size of Pearson r = -0.66 for DIFi-FD, a large correlation, and r = 0.317 for DISi-FD, in the medium range. Spearman's rho values were -0.705 and 0.347, with the Pearson DISi-FD correlation of "0.317 (Sig. 0.000)" significant at the 0.01 level.
