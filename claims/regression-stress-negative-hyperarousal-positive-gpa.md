# In multiple regression, GPA was negatively associated with stress but positively associated with hyperarousal, anxiety, and depression, while intrusion and avoidance showed no significant influence

## Evidence

### Korda Mykhaylo 2025

Korda Mykhaylo, Shulhai Arkadii, Shevchuk Oksana, Shulhai Oleksandra, Shulhai Anna-Mariia. (2025). Psychological well-being and academic performance of Ukrainian medical students under the burden of war: a cross-sectional study. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1457026

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression with GPA as dependent variable and depression, anxiety, stress, avoidance, intrusion, and hyperarousal as predictors; "a negative association with stress and a positive with hyperarousal, anxiety, and even depression", with intrusion and avoidance non-significant (p > 0.05).

> "Interestingly, we have found that the direction of the relationship varies, showing a negative association with stress and a positive with hyperarousal, anxiety, and even depression. In contrast, the subscales of IES-R intrusion and avoidance did not show a significant influence on student academic achievements (p > 0.05)."
