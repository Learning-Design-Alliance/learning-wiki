## Evidence

### Jamie Costley 2021

Jamie Costley, Mik Fanguy. (2021). Collaborative note-taking affects cognitive load: the interplay of completeness and interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09979-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-sample t-test comparing treatment (n = 74, collaborative Google Docs note-taking) and control (n = 97) groups in a flipped scientific writing course. The treatment mean was "4.16 higher germane cognitive load" and the difference was "statistically significant (t = − 4.156, p < .000)". No effect size was printed.

> "The treatment group had a 4.16 higher germane cognitive load mean than the control group (hypothesis 1 is held), and this difference was statistically significant (t = − 4.156, p < .000)."
