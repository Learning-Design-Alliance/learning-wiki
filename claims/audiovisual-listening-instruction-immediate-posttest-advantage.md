# Second-semester French learners who received four weeks of daily audiovisual-media listening activities scored 14.18% higher than the control group on an immediate listening posttest, a medium-to-large effect (d = −0.68) that did not reach statistical significance

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` After a four-week treatment phase, the experimental group's mean immediate posttest score exceeded the control group's by 14.18%, with a medium-to-large effect size (d = −0.68), but the difference was not statistically significant (p = .08). ## Evidence

### Shannon R. Becker and Jessica L. Sturm 2017

Shannon R. Becker and Jessica L. Sturm. (2017). Effects of Audiovisual Media on L2 Listening Comprehension: A Preliminary Study in French. CALICO Journal. https://doi.org/10.1558/cj.26754

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pretest–treatment–posttest classroom quasi-experiment with 31 native English-speaking second-semester French students in two intact sections (14 control, 17 experimental). The treatment group's mean posttest score "exceeded that of the control group by 14.18%"; the article reports d = −0.68 and F(1, 29) = 3.32, p = .08, not significant.

> "At the end of the four-week learning period, the average score of the experimental group on the posttest exceeded that of the control group by 14.18% (see Table 1)."
