## Evidence

### Banerjee Snigdha 2015

Banerjee Snigdha, Frey Hans-Peter, Molholm Sophie, Foxe John J. (2015). Interests shape how adolescents pay attention: The interaction of motivation and top-down attentional processes in biasing sensory activations to anticipated events. The European Journal of Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.12810

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anticipatory ERP ANOVA (motivation × ROI interaction F(1,23) = 5.96, p = .023) in the LDAP timeframe during the cued spatial attention task with 24 adolescents. Motivation and cue did not interact.

> "Follow-up paired t-tests showed that this interaction was driven by greater amplitude over the right ROI for the high versus low interest condition (t(23) =2.49, p = .021)."
