## Evidence

### Demedts 2024

Demedts, D., Magerman, J., Goossens, E., Tricas-Sauras, S., Bilsen, J., De Smet, S., & Fobelets, M. (2024). Using simulation to teach nursing students how to deal with a euthanasia request. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0299049

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Group-interview findings from the qualitative study of 17 nursing students. Students appreciated that the module was "brief and concise, as well as a useful tool as background information." However, not all students completed the preparatory task before the session.

> "The preparatory online learning module was considered useful by participants. For some students it was an update of the legislation procedures, for others it was something totally new."
