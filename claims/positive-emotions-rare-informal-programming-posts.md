## Evidence

### Alif Al Hasan* 2026

Alif Al Hasan*, Subarna Saha*, and Mia Mohammad Imran. (2026). Learning Programming in Informal Spaces: Using Emotion as a Lens to Understand Novice Struggles on r/learnprogramming. ICSE SEET '26. [doi:10.1145/3786580.3786946](https://doi.org/10.1145/3786580.3786946)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive finding from the same manual annotation of 1,500 posts (Figure 3). The authors report Delight and Engagement/Flow "were rare, with only 14 and 28 instances respectively" and link Delight to overcoming persistent challenges. No effect size reported.

> "Although positive emotions such as Delight and Engagement/Flow were rare, with only 14 and 28 instances respectively, they offer valuable insight into the conditions that support productive learning."
