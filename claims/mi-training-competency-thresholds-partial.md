Svensson C, Wickström H, Emanuelson U, Bard AM, Reyher KK, Forsberg L. (2020). Training in motivational interviewing improves cattle veterinarians' communication skills for herd health management. The Veterinary Record. https://doi.org/10.1136/vr.105646

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of post-training MITI summary measurements for 31 veterinarians against competency thresholds suggested by Moyers et al. The article reports 9 (29 per cent) reached moderate competency and 2 (6 per cent) above moderate competency after training; the discussion adds only 6 per cent reached overall fair competency and none good competency.

> "Two (6 per cent), 9 (29 per cent) and 14 (45 per cent) of the veterinarians reached all criteria for 'above moderate competency', 'moderate competency' and 'near moderate competency', respectively, after training."
