## Evidence

### Purwoko Haryadi Santoso 2024

Purwoko Haryadi Santoso, Edi Istiyono, Haryanto, Heri Retnawati. (2024). Validating Light Phenomena Conceptual Assessment Through The Lens of CTT and IRT Framework. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad183b)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Statistical comparison of the 30 LPCA items' difficulty (CTT P vs reversed IRT b) and discrimination (CTT D vs IRT a) indices, computed across pairings of the two parameter sets after z-transformation. Both t-tests quoted above were non-significant.

> "There was no significant difference in terms of item difficulty index between the CTT and IRT frameworks (𝑡 = -0.043673, 𝑑𝑓 = 58, 𝑝 > 0.05). Regarding the item discrimination index, we found that the difference between the means was also not statistically significant ( 𝑡 = -1.402, 𝑑𝑓 = 58, 𝑝 > 0.05)."
