Authors' overall interpretation in the Discussion of the combined ANOVA and regression findings from the three-group comparison of 51 adults on the DANVA. "Thus, affect recognition deficits were certainly not found unanimously in the ADHD groups, suggesting a more nuanced view of affect recognition deficits in adults with ADHD."
