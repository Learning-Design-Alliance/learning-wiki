Evidence¶

Husnu 2018¶

Muhammad Husnu. (2018). Demonstration Technique to Improve Vocabulary and Grammar Element in Teaching Speaking at EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 11(2). http://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n2p26

q3 · i? · causal · r1

True-experimental study with 32 experimental (class IIA) and 32 control (class IIB) students selected by random sampling from 122; pre-test, treatment, post-test design analyzed in SPSS 17. The paired-samples t-test gave "ttest= 21.4" against t(df=31) = 1.69 at sig (2-tailed) = 0.000, so the null hypothesis was rejected. No effect size is printed.

"After performing a paired-samples t-test, the researcher found that there was a significant difference in the mean scores between the pre-test and the post-test, ttest= 21.4 and t(df=31) = 1.69 at sig (2-tailed) = 0.000"
