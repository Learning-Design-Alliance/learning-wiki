Xin et al. 2015¶

Huaibo Xin, Monica Kempland, Faustina H. Blankson. (2015). ADAPTABILITY AND REPLICABILITY OF WEB-FACILITATED, HYBRID, AND ONLINE LEARNING IN AN UNDERGRADUATE EXERCISE PSYCHOLOGY COURSE. q3 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive comparison of final letter grade distribution across the three sections as reported in the Findings section. The final letter grade ANOVA was statistically significant (F(2,100) = 3.93, p < .05).

"more than 92.3% of the students in the web-facilitated section earned an overall grade of A, while 83.3% of the students in the hybrid section earned a grade of A, and only 62.5% in the online section earned a grade of A for the semester."
