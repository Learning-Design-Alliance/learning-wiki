## Evidence

### Kilgour 2016

Kilgour, J. M., & Saadia, T. (2016). An investigation into the optimal number of distractors in single-best answer exams. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 21(3), 571-585. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9652-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of four past examination papers from the 2013/2014 Cardiff University undergraduate medical programme using ITEMAN psychometric software. The average number of functional distractors per question was 1.82, with only 34 questions (7.1%) containing four functional distractors and 68 (14.2%) containing none.

> "In total, 480 question items and 1920 distractors were examined in this study. The analysis of question performance (Table 2) reveals that across the papers included in this study, the average number of functional distractors per question (those which were chosen with a frequency equal to or >5% of the cohort) was 1.82."
