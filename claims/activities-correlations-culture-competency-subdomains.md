## Evidence

### Lee 2025

Lee, J., Nam, K., Suh, Y., Lee, Y., and Lee, D. 2025. "Factors Associated With Patient Safety Activities of Clinical Nurses: A Cross‐Sectional Secondary Data Analysis." International Nursing Review 72, no. 4: e70127. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70127

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of 291 nurses' data (Table 2): activities–skills r = 0.50 (p < 0.001), activities–attitudes r = 0.42, activities–knowledge r = 0.41; staffing and work pace r = 0.06 (p = 0.293) and response to error r = 0.09 (p = 0.118) were not significant.

> "Significant positive correlations were observed between patient safety activities and all subdomains of patient safety culture, except for staffing and work pace, and response to error. Patient safety activities were positively correlated with all subdomains of competency."
