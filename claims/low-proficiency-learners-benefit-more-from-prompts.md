## Evidence

### Ahlem Ammar and Nina Spada 2006

Ahlem Ammar and Nina Spada. (2006). ONE SIZE FITS ALL?: Recasts, Prompts, and L2 Learning. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 28, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263106060268

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of recast and prompt conditions with grade 6 Francophone ESL learners, contrasting outcomes by proficiency level. The article reports low-proficiency learners "benefited significantly more from prompts than recasts." No effect size is printed in the available text.

> "In particular, high-proficiency learners benefited equally from both prompts and recasts, whereas low-proficiency learners benefited significantly more from prompts than recasts."
