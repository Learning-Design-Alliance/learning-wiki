Cansu Koyuturk 2026¶

Cansu Koyuturk, Sabrina Guidotti, and Dimitri Ognibene. (2026). The Hidden Cost of Contextual Sycophancy: an AI Literacy Intervention in Human–AI Collaboration. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18372 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.18372

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear regression on data from 60 participants in a mixed-design experiment predicting final ranking accuracy from baseline ranking, condition, time, their interaction, and scenario. The article reports "b = 0.414, SE = 0.075, z = 5.53, p < .001" for baseline accuracy, with all other effects non-significant (ps > .44).

"Users’ baseline accuracy significantly predicted final performance, b = 0.414, SE = 0.075, z = 5.53, p < .001, while no other effects were significant (all ps > .44)."
