### Aydede Yalçın 2016

Aydede Yalçın, M. N. (2016). The effect of active learning based science camp activities on primary school students' opinions towards scientific knowledge and scientific process skills. International Electronic Journal of Environmental Education, 6(2), 108-125. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1104840

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

An observation form was filled by two observers for 4 primary school students at different times, using a 3-point scale. The highest-scoring item was 'Listened to suggestions and telling of his/her friends', while the lowest were 'Encouraged his/her friends without hurting them' and 'Asked questions when he/she could not understand'.

> "As a result of the analysis of data gathered from primary school students, the lowest scores that students got among items were 'Encouraged his/her friends without hurting them' and 'Asked questions when he/she could not understand'. The item that students got the highest score was 'Listened to suggestions and telling of his/her friends'."
