## Evidence

### Ying Hu 2026

Ying Hu, Yuhang Shu & Xin Zhao. (2026). Children consider changes in performance over time when reasoning about academic achievements. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00401-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered Study 1 smart-question analysis with 4- to 10-year-olds: children across ages inferred the constant character as smarter (M = 0.20, SD = 0.40, p < 0.001), i.e., selections of the improving character were well below chance.

> "Specifically, children across ages inferred the constant character as smarter (M = 0.20, SD = 0.40, intercept-only binomial linear mixed effects model, p < 0.001)"

### Ying Hu 2026 (2)

Ying Hu, Yuhang Shu & Xin Zhao. (2026). Children consider changes in performance over time when reasoning about academic achievements. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00401-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Study 2 improving condition using honor-roll rankings replicated the smart inference: children of all ages chose the constant character as smarter (M = 0.37, SD = 0.48, p = 0.007) despite matched final performance.
