# A ten-week in-class fitness circuit intervention significantly improved cardiovascular endurance among Malaysian secondary school girls

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Experimental girls showed significantly greater cardiovascular endurance than control girls after ten weeks, with a small effect size (Cohen d = 0.35). [→ Rengasamy et al. 2011](#rengasamy-et-al-2011)

## Evidence

### Rengasamy et al. 2011

Shabeshan Rengasamy, Subramaniam Raju, Wee Akina Sia Seng Lee, Ramachandran Roa. (2011). A Fitness Intervention Program within a Physical Education Class on Cardiovascular Endurance among Malaysia Secondary School Students. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Science, 2(1). http://www.moj-es.net

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with intact classes of Form Four girls (experimental n = 48, control n = 38); ANCOVA on posttest Cooper's Twelve Minute Run scores with pretest covariate found "a significant main effect" with "Cohen d = 0.35".

> "As for the girls, there was also a significant main effect F (1,83) = 44.69 , p < .05; Cohen d = 0.35."
