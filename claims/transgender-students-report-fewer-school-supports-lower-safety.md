## Evidence

### Cerna 2021

Cerna, R., Stern, A., Austin, G., Betz, J., Zhang, G., & Hashmi, S. (2021). Understanding the experiences of LGBTQ high school students in California by race/ethnicity. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/lgbtq-students-in-california/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of pooled 2017–19 California Healthy Kids Survey responses from 559,120 California 9th- and 11th-graders, disaggregated by gender identity and race/ethnicity (Table 5). The report states transgender students were "markedly less likely than their non-transgender peers" to report these supports and outcomes; only percentages are reported, no effect sizes.

> "Across nearly all of the race and ethnicity groups, transgender students were markedly less likely than their non-transgender peers to indicate the presence of key school supports, feeling safe at school, academic motivation, school connectedness, and positive academic outcomes."
