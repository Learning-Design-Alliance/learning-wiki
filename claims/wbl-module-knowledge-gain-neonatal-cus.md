## Evidence

### Ben Fadel Nadya; McAleer Sean 2020

Ben Fadel Nadya; McAleer Sean. (2020). Impact of a web-based module on trainees’ ability to interpret neonatal cranial ultrasound. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02400-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-group pre/post knowledge assessment of 22 trainees completing both 12-item tests during NICU rotations, analyzed with a paired t-test. The mean change was "5.11 ± 2.45" with 95% CI 4.02–6.20 and p < 0.001; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The Mean baseline knowledge test score (pre-test) was 6.23 out of a total score of 15 (42%). The mean score post-test was 11.34 out of 15 (76%). The change in score from pre-test to post-test was positive and statistically significant (p < 0.001)"
