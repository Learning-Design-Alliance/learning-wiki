# Treatment students significantly increased in prosocial behaviors and social responsibility, and were less excluded by peers, while control students decreased in these behaviors.

## Evidence

### Katz 2011

Katz, J., & Porath, M. (2011). Teaching to diversity: Creating compassionate learning communities for diverse elementary school students. International Journal of Special Education, 26(2), 29–39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ937173

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Teacher-report Child Behavior Scale data analyzed via repeated measures MANCOVA controlling for grade showed significant increases in prosocial behaviors for treatment students and decreases for control students (partial η = .15).

> "However, prosocial behaviors increased for students in treatment classes, and decreased for students in control group classes, F(1, 209) = 5.15, p = .028, partial η = .15."

### Katz 2011 (2)

Katz, J., & Porath, M. (2011). Teaching to diversity: Creating compassionate learning communities for diverse elementary school students. International Journal of Special Education, 26(2), 29–39. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ937173

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Teacher-report Child Behavior Scale data analyzed via repeated measures MANCOVA controlling for grade showed treatment students were less excluded by peers (partial η = .10) and increased in social responsibility.

> "As well, students in treatment group classes were less excluded by peers F (1, 209) = 3.72, p = .05, partial η = .10, and increased in social responsibility F(1, 209) = 3.9, p = .05 partial η = .97."
