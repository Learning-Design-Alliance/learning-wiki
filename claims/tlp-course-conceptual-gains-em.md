## Evidence

### Noah D. Finkelstein 2005

Noah D. Finkelstein. (2005). Teaching and learning physics: A model for coordinating physics instruction, outreach, and research. Originally published 2004. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0505091](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0505091)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post conceptual survey (35 items drawn from CSEM, ECCE, and two original questions) in the first 10-week offering enrolling 14 students; N=13 completers. The article reports "The average of individual student gains is 51%" with p<0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "The mean pre- and post-test scores are respectively 54% (σ= 25%) and 74% (σ= 24%).  The average of individual student gains is 51% (σ=30%; N=13; p<0.001)."
