## Evidence

### Johnson D 2026

Johnson D, Houdmont J, Levy N, Lobo DN. (2026). Investigating the fluid and electrolyte prescribing knowledge of Foundation Year doctors. Ann R Coll Surg Engl. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2025.0004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-reported recall of teaching hours at medical school from the 190-respondent survey; the remainder (33, 17%) responded ‘no idea’.

> "72 (38%) reported more than four hours. By contrast, nearly half (85, 45%) reported less than four hours of teaching on the subject."

### Johnson D 2026 (2)

Johnson D, Houdmont J, Levy N, Lobo DN. (2026). Investigating the fluid and electrolyte prescribing knowledge of Foundation Year doctors. Ann R Coll Surg Engl. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2025.0004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-reported confidence item from the same survey; the discussion suggests perceived unpreparedness may stem from medical school and from little post-start guidance (39% reported receiving any).

> "The majority (139, 73%) of participants claimed to be ‘somewhat confident’ in their ability to safely prescribe fluid and electrolytes, 37 (19%) reported feeling ‘very confident’"
