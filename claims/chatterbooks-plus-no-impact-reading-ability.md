## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Chatterbooks Plus, which added 15 minutes of dialogic reading per session, had no statistically significant effect on reading ability (Hedges' g = -0.01, 95% CI -0.18 to 0.16). [→ Styles 2014](#styles-2014)

## Evidence

### Styles 2014

Styles, B., Clarkson, R., & Fowler, K. (2014). Chatterbooks: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581155

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Pupil-randomised controlled trial comparing Chatterbooks Plus against a waitlist control on the NGRT composite reading score. The printed effect size was "-0.01" with an estimated zero months' progress and the finding marked "No" for statistical significance, based on 150 analysed pupils.
