# Most Australian IT-degree-offering universities offer MA-degrees and/or SP-degrees rather than only general IT degrees

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At each degree level, far more Australian offering universities offer MA-degrees and/or SP-degrees (possibly with GL-degrees) than offer only GL-degrees. [→ Pak-Lok Poon 2026](#pak-lok-poon-2026)

## Evidence

### Pak-Lok Poon 2026

Pak-Lok Poon, Sau-Fun Tang, Santoso Wibowo, & Srimannarayana Grandhi. (2026). Information technology curriculum: General or specialized? An Australia's census study. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Census of all 39 Australian IT-degree-offering universities, website data collected October-November 2025. Universities offering only GL-degrees (Group 1) were far fewer than those offering MA/SP-degrees (Group 2); at the bachelor's level "Group 1 = 3; Group 2 = 35".

> "Since the number of universities in Group-1 is much smaller than that in Group-2 at each level (e.g., bachelor's: Group 1 = 3; Group 2 = 35)"
