## Evidence

### Hanna Onyi Yusuf & Sadiq Mohammed 2013

Hanna Onyi Yusuf & Sadiq Mohammed. (2013). Influence of Prior Knowledge Questions on Pupils' Performance in Reading Comprehension in Primary Schools in Kaduna, Nigeria. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 4(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.4n.1p.134

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' observational account from lessons conducted during the eight-week treatment: pupils and teachers discussed passages before, during and after reading, and each pupil was eager to share experiences. Control pupils, not exposed to prior knowledge questions, were described as passive most of the time.

> "There is a general increase in the level of involvement and participation of pupils in the experimental group probably because of the activities pupils were engaged in."
