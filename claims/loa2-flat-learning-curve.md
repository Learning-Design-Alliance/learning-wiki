## Evidence

### Wang Miao 2025

Wang Miao, Zhou Hui Xia, Lv Cheng Peng, Tong Wai Man, Leung Wai Keung, Cui Xiao Xuan, Wang Xin Xuan, Sriprasert Intad, Zhou Qin, He Long Long. (2025). Evaluating the relationship between clinician experience and accuracy in robotic-assisted dental implant placement: a retrospective study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-07389-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Loess-smoothed learning-curve analysis over the April 2022–April 2024 case series (585 implants), comparing novice and expert groups with Chow's test; the article reports linear trend coefficients below 0.001. No effect size is printed.

> "Controlling surgical difficulty factors (bone density, tooth location, implant length) did not alter this flat trend, with no significant accuracy improvement in novice group as case volume rose; linear trend coefficients were negligible (< 0.001, Fig. 3)"
