## Evidence

### Ramani Soumya; Pradeep Thanuja Gopal; Sundaresh Divya D 2018

Ramani Soumya; Pradeep Thanuja Gopal; Sundaresh Divya D. (2018). Effect of wet-laboratory training on resident performed manual small-incision cataract surgery. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/ijo.IJO_1041_17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of postoperative day-1 vision between the two cohorts of the retrospective study of 464 resident-performed MSICS cases, as reported in the article's results summary of Table 4.

> "The postoperative visual outcome also was better in Group B than in Group A, with 62.06% of patients in Group B, having a postoperative day 1 vision of better than 6/18 as compared to only 38.36% in Group A."

### Ramani Soumya; Pradeep Thanuja Gopal; Sundaresh Divya D 2018 (2)

Ramani Soumya; Pradeep Thanuja Gopal; Sundaresh Divya D. (2018). Effect of wet-laboratory training on resident performed manual small-incision cataract surgery. Indian Journal of Ophthalmology. https://doi.org/10.4103/ijo.IJO_1041_17

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results-section comparison of poor day-1 vision (less than counting fingers at 3 m) between the before-training and after-training cohorts, reported with P = 0.00.

> "The postoperative vision mirrored the higher occurrence of intraoperative and postoperative complications in Group A with 13.36% of patients in Group A having vision of less than counting fingers at 3 m as compared to Group B (P = 0.00)."
