Evidence¶

Jonathan Firth 2021¶

Jonathan Firth, Ian Rivers and James Boyle. (2021). A systematic review of interleaving as a concept learning strategy. Review of Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/rev3.3266

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analysis of a subset of 17 studies (32 constituent datasets) from the 26-study systematic review, restricted to experiments with a clear interleaved-versus-blocked comparison during initial learning. The review reports an interleaving benefit for memory with "effect sizes (Hedges' g) of up to 0.65".

"Memory (as tested by presenting studied items from a learned category) showed an interleaving benefit with effect sizes (Hedges' g) of up to 0.65"
