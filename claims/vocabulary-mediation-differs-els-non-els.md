# Vocabulary's relation to reading comprehension is completely mediated by listening comprehension and word reading for ELs, but exerts a direct effect for non-ELs

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For ELs, a complete mediation model, in which vocabulary's effects on reading comprehension pass through listening comprehension and word reading, fit best. [→ Cho 2019](#cho-2019)
`q3 i?` For non-ELs, a partial mediation model with a direct vocabulary effect on reading comprehension fit best, and indirect effects were not significant. [→ Cho 2019 (2)](#cho-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Cho 2019

Cho, E., Capin, P., Roberts, G., Roberts, G. J., & Vaughn, S. (2019). Examining Sources and Mechanisms of Reading Comprehension Difficulties: Comparing English Learners and Non-English Learners within the Simple View of Reading. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000332

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Nested alternative-model comparison within the MG-SEM of struggling fourth graders; for the EL group the complete mediation model fit best, with significant indirect effects of vocabulary via listening comprehension (β = .43) and word reading (β = .12).

> "for ELs, the complete mediation model where the effects of vocabulary were completely mediated by both listening comprehension and word reading was the most parsimonious and best fitting model (Δ𝜒2(1) = 1.11, p = .14; RMSEA [90% CI] = 0.04 [0.00, 0.07])."

### Cho 2019 (2)

Cho, E., Capin, P., Roberts, G., Roberts, G. J., & Vaughn, S. (2019). Examining Sources and Mechanisms of Reading Comprehension Difficulties: Comparing English Learners and Non-English Learners within the Simple View of Reading. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000332

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same alternative-model comparison for the non-EL group favored the partial mediation model; indirect effects of vocabulary via listening comprehension (β = .01, p = .78) and word reading (β = −.01, p = .92) were not significant.

> "However, for non-ELs, the partial mediation model where vocabulary has direct effects on reading comprehension represented the data best (Δ𝜒𝜒2(1) = 4.91, p < .05; RMSEA [90% CI] = 0.08 [0.05, 0.10])."
