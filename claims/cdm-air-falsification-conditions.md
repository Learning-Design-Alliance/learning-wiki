Section 5.3 of the conceptual article specifies four disconfirmation conditions: mediation failure against an additive specification, discriminant collapse, moderation nullity, and context redundancy. Reporting against these conditions, not just path significance, is what would allow cumulative evaluation.

> "if the H12 and H13 interaction terms are repeatedly indistinguishable from zero in adequately powered studies (N ≥ 400 with latent interaction estimation), the model’s account of the intention–behaviour gap fails, and CDM-AIR reduces to TPB with a re-labelled antecedent."
