## Evidence

### Mirofora Pilakouta and John Sinatkas 2020

Mirofora Pilakouta and John Sinatkas. (2020). Surveying Undrergraduare Greek Student's Knowledge on Some Issues of Radiations and Nuclear Energy. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.03549](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.03549)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup analysis by prior education: 77% of the sample came from General Lyceums and 23% from Vocational Lyceums. The article reports only two statistically significant differences, in opposite directions, and reads the pattern as indicating the poor contribution of school to this knowledge.

> "Overall only in two questions the difference was statistically significant. In questio n B2 the students from GL gave better results but in question B4 the students from EPAL had a better performance."
