## Evidence

### Moganadas SR 2026

Moganadas SR, Marín-González F, Nun SH and Gan CL (2026) Generative AI as a didactic-pedagogical mediator: rethinking human roles and pedagogical design in higher education. Front. Educ. 11:1856839. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1856839

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article argues from its synthesis that GenAI can facilitate teaching by generating resources, examples, feedback, and activities, yet instructors retain responsibility for pedagogical design and validation. The authors note this transition has implications for workload and professional identity. No effect size is reported; the support is conceptual.

> "In GenAI-mediated HE, the instructor's role shifts from being the exclusive source of information toward orchestrating, critiquing, and regulating AI-mediated learning activities."
