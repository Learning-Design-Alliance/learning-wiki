This is a narrative review assertion, not a new empirical result from this study.

> "studies have found that scaffolding strategies, particularly scripted instructions and self-monitoring, lead to higher levels of engagement compared to no scaffolding. This is evidenced by the greater length of text (e.g., segments and words) produced by feedback providers in groups that received these scaffolds"
