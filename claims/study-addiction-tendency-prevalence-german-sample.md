`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive prevalence analysis of the excessive study behaviour scale in the 624-student sample. The proportion was "slightly higher than the international prevalence rates of 5.9–20.9%" but lower than the 27% in the German validation sample. No effect size applies.

> "it was found that 23.42% of the students answered “often” or “always” to at least four of the seven items on the scale of excessive study behaviour, indicating a tendency towards “study addiction” as defined by Atroszko et al."
