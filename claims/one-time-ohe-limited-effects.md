## Evidence

### Haleem Abdul 2016

Haleem Abdul, Khan Muhammad Khalil, Sufia Shamta, Chaudhry Saima, Siddiqui Muhammad Irfanullah, Khan Ayyaz Ali. (2016). The role of repetition and reinforcement in school-based oral health education-a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-2676-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial results after a single OHE session in DL and PL groups. Gingivitis-prevention behavior improved significantly (p < 0.05) but oral cancer prevention behavior did not change.

> "Although one-time OHE resulted in a significant improvement in adolescents' oral health behavior (OHB) related to the prevention of gingivitis in the two groups (p < 0.05), no significant change was observed in their behavior towards prevention of oral cancer."

### Haleem Abdul 2016 (2)

Haleem Abdul, Khan Muhammad Khalil, Sufia Shamta, Chaudhry Saima, Siddiqui Muhammad Irfanullah, Khan Ayyaz Ali. (2016). The role of repetition and reinforcement in school-based oral health education-a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-2676-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Oral hygiene status measured by clinical examination showed no statistically significant change after one-time OHE in any of the three educator-led strategies.

> "The oral hygiene status (OHS) of the participants in all three groups did not change statistically after one-time OHE."
