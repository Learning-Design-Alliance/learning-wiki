## Evidence

### Zhang Meng-lu 2025

Zhang Meng-lu, Bai Wen, Yan Zhi-peng, Ge Hui-min, Zhang Qiu-yang, Ding Xi-yan, Liu Yan-qing, Li Xiang, Jiang Qin, Li Ke-ran, Shang Wei-hong. (2025). Virtual simulation system in cataract education for medical undergraduates: a prospective quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08497-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of the two training groups with dual-mode assessment. Table 2 reports, e.g., VR clinical diagnostic proficiency 52.17 ± 2.83 vs 43.36 ± 4.01 (t = −10.67, P < 0.0001) and traditional surgical technical skills 20.64 ± 0.79 vs 18.79 ± 1.67 (W = 226, P < 0.0001).

> "In both assessment modes, the experimental group outperformed the control group in clinical reasoning competency, clinical diagnostic proficiency, and surgical technical skills. These differences were statistically significant for both the traditional assessment and the virtual simulation assessment"
