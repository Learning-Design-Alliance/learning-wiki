Rachmawati 2019¶

Rachmawati, E., Prodjosantoso, A. K., & Wilujeng, I. (2019). Next Generation Science Standard in Science Learning to Improve Student's Practice Skill. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 299-310. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12120a

q2 · i? · design · r3

Validation study of the developed lesson plan by two expert lecturers and one science teacher across two stages; stage 1 scored 3.65, requiring revisions, and stage 2 scored 3.82, exceeding the article's >3.0 feasibility criterion. Descriptive scores only; no inferential test.

"The second stage validation result obtained a score of 3.82 which was very good. The results of the lesson plan assessment at this stage have shown that the NGSS-oriented lesson plan is suitable for use in science learning."
