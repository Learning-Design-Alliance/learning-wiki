## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Standard Captions were rated significantly worse on UEQ-S hedonic quality than Timed Keyword Highlights (d = -0.97) and Keyword Highlights (d = -0.91), with a smaller difference versus Timed Keywords (d = -0.48). [→ Fiona Draxler 2023](#fiona-draxler-2023)

## Evidence

### Fiona Draxler 2023

Fiona Draxler, Henrike Weingärtner, Maximiliane Windl, Albrecht Schmidt, and Lewis L. Chuang. (2023). Useful but Distracting: Keyword Highlights and Time-Synchronization in Captions for Language Learning. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.05870)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject online study with 49 participants comparing four caption types on four Marriage Story clips, analyzed with repeated-measures ANOVA and Holm-corrected pairwise tests. Pairwise post-hoc tests show Standard Captions "fare worse than Timed Keyword Highlights" on hedonic quality with d = -0.97, d = -0.91 versus Keyword Highlights, and d = -0.48 versus Timed Keywords.

> "Standard Captions fare worse than Timed Keyword Highlights (𝑡 =−5.17,𝑝 < 0.001,𝑑 =−0.97), Keyword Highlights (𝑡 =−4.86, 𝑝 < 0.001,𝑑 =−0.91), and Timed Keywords (𝑡 =−2.56,𝑝 = 0.041,𝑑 =−0.48)."
