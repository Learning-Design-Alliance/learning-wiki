# ROOTS students also had higher covariate-adjusted SESAT posttest scores than control students

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Covariate-adjusted SESAT scores favored ROOTS over control (difference 8.95, g = 0.24). [→ Doabler 2016](#doabler-2016)

## Evidence

### Doabler 2016

Doabler, C. T., Clarke, B., Kosty, D. B., Kurtz-Nelson, E., Fien, H., Smolkowski, K., & Baker, S. K. (2016). Testing the efficacy of a tier 2 mathematics intervention: A conceptual replication study. Exceptional Children, 83, 92–110. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402916660084

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Analysis-of-covariance model for the SESAT, available only at posttest, with ASPENS and TEMA-3 as pretest covariates, in the same 319-student partially nested RCT. The estimated covariate-adjusted difference was 8.95 (g = 0.24), described as "higher covariate-adjusted SESAT scores" for ROOTS students.

> "Students in the ROOTS condition also had higher covariate-adjusted SESAT scores using ASPENS and TEMA-3 as pretest covariates (t = 2.45, df = 136, p = .0158)."
