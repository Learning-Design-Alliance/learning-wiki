## Evidence

### Goldin S 2022

Goldin S, Hood N, Pascutto A, Bennett C, Barbosa de Lima AC, Devereaux N, Caric A, Rai K, Desai S, Lindstrand A, Struminger B. (2022). Building global capacity for COVID-19 vaccination through interactive virtual learning. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-022-00710-7

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-session survey analysis (n = 3062 survey respondents) comparing self-reported knowledge before and after each session using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test; ratings were "significantly higher than before the session" with P < 0.001. Table 2 shows 64.4% reported knowledge increased. No effect size is printed.

> "In post-session surveys, respondents rated their post-session knowledge significantly higher than before the session (Fig. 2) (P < 0.001)."
