`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online questionnaire (KINDS) of 125 UK-based nutrition professionals, reported in the Results overall-knowledge section. The survey found "71% of participants who took part in this survey had heard about inorganic nitrate, and 51% of participants believed that this polyatomic ion is primarily beneficial"; no effect size was reported.
