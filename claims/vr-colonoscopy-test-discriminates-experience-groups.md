`q2 i?` In a prospective nonrandomized study, experienced endoscopists scored significantly higher than novices on the virtual-reality simulator aggregated score (mean 27.0 vs 7.2 points per minute, P < 0.001). [→ Preisler Louise MD 2015](#preisler-louise-md-2015)

## Evidence

### Preisler Louise MD 2015

Preisler Louise MD, Svendsen Morten Bo Søndergaard MScEng, Nerup Nikolaj MD, Svendsen Lars Bo MD, DMSc, Konge Lars MD, PhD. (2015). Simulation-Based Training for Colonoscopy: Establishing Criteria for Competency. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000000440

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results of the virtual-reality simulator test comparing 15 novices with 10 experienced endoscopists. The experienced group's mean aggregated score per minute of 27.0 (SD 3.2) was significantly higher than the novices' 7.2 (SD 1.1), "P < 0.001"; no effect size was printed.

> "The mean aggregated score per minute in the novice group was 7.2 (standard deviation [SD] 1.1) and for the experienced group was 27.0 (SD 3.2), P < 0.001."
