# Each individual learning condition predicts I am Valued gains, with Classroom Community the largest (15.0 middle grades, 16.9 high school percentile points)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A one standard deviation fall-to-spring increase in Classroom Community predicts a 15.0 percentile point increase in I am Valued for middle grades students (47th to 62nd percentile). [→ Porter 2025](#porter-2025)
