## Evidence

### Jiang Jiahui 2026

Jiang Jiahui, Zhang Zikai, Wu Yiming, Luo Yingquan, Wang Chen, Fan Tengyang, Thai Jessica, Lei Si, Gao Yuan, Huang Lei. (2026). Psychosomatic medicine service competency training in China's GP residency programs: a national survey from a CBME perspective. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09162-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 193 valid responses from training base representatives across mainland China, administered May–August 2024. Descriptive statistics summarized competency coverage; one-sample proportion and χ² goodness-of-fit tests examined deviations from expected distributions, with no formal adjustment for multiple testing.

> "Communication skills (75.13%) and respect for patient autonomy (90.16%) were frequently included in training programs (defined as fully or often utilized). In contrast, psychological education, understanding mental health legislation, emergency psychiatry, psychotherapy, and psychiatric medication use were reported as fully or often utilized in fewer than 75% of programs"

### Jiang Jiahui 2026 (2)

Jiang Jiahui, Zhang Zikai, Wu Yiming, Luo Yingquan, Wang Chen, Fan Tengyang, Thai Jessica, Lei Si, Gao Yuan, Huang Lei. (2026). Psychosomatic medicine service competency training in China's GP residency programs: a national survey from a CBME perspective. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09162-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same cross-sectional survey of training base representatives; Fig. 4 summarized familiarity with psychosomatic medicine-related diseases. No effect sizes were reported; analyses were exploratory and hypothesis-generating.

> "Training content differed by condition. Sleep–wake disorders and anxiety disorders were more frequently reported as covered at a moderate or high level. In contrast, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and somatoform disorders were commonly reported as minimally covered or not included"
