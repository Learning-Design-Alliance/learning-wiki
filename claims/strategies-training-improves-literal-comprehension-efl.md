## Evidence

### Mistar et al. 2016

Junaidi Mistar, Alfan Zuhairi & Nofita Yanti. (2016). Strategies Training in the Teaching of Reading Comprehension for EFL Learners in Indonesia. English Language Teaching, 9(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n2p49

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with two intact grade-ten classes (N=71) in a Mojokerto vocational school; ANCOVA with mid-semester teacher-made test as covariate. The treatment effect on literal comprehension was significant: "an F-value 9.85 ( p<.003)". Descriptive post-test means were 88.33 (experimental) vs 81.81 (control).

> "For the literal comprehension, for example, the analysis of the significance of the difference between the experimental group and the control group found an F-value 9.85 ( p<.003)."
