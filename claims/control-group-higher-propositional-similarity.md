## Evidence

### Inka Sara Hähnlein 2024

Inka Sara Hähnlein, Pablo Pirnay-Dummer. (2024). Promoting pre-service teachers' knowledge integration from multiple text sources across domains with instructional prompts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10363-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Tukey HSD post-hoc on propositional matching from the same T-MITOCAR comparison of N = 109 student texts with the reference model. The contrast "control group ... than experimental group 1" was significant at p = .009; the BSM contrasts were not significant (p > .05).

> "The control group (PROP: M = 0.08, SD = 0.03) achieved significantly higher propositional similarity to the reference model than experimental group 1 (PROP: M = 0.06, SD = 0.03, p = .009; see Figure 5)."
