## Evidence

### Vietz E 2019

Vietz E, März E, Lottspeich C, Wölfel T, Fischer MR, Schmidmaier R. (2019). Ward round competences in surgery and psychiatry - a comparative multidisciplinary interview study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1554-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Frequency analysis of the procedural (open-ended) interview part across both specialities (N = 30 each). The article reports the remaining literature-based competences (6)-(10) were rarely described spontaneously, which the authors interpret as possibly pointing to a lack of awareness.

> "There, the competences: (1) collaborative clinical reasoning, (2) clinician - patient communication, (3) clinician - team communication, (4) organization, and (5) teamwork were reported with a frequency of over 30% (Table 4)."
