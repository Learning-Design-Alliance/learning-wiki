From the exploratory interview study with introductory physics for life sciences students. The authors found that "students sometimes applied it to new scenarios spontaneously" after introduction to a strategy, suggesting transfer within the interview context.

> "Once introduced to a strategy, students sometimes applied it to new scenarios spontaneously or applied it in new ways to the present scenario, acknowledging it as a useful, general purpose problem-solving technique."
