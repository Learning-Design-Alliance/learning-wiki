Related Claims¶

  • Included studies suggest engagement increases when adaptive platform activities are tailored to learners' personal needs and emotional state — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • A systematic review of 14 studies (8,305 participants) found AI-based tools enhanced comprehension, engagement, problem-solving, and critical thinking by personalizing learning experiences — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • AI tools can enhance short-term task performance while undermining durable learning (the learning-performance paradox) — reports the opposite
  • The review found AI was effective for improving basic problem-solving but required teacher mediation to handle more complex mathematical tasks — related
  • Educators' use of AI in PBL is accompanied by persistent ethical concerns about data privacy, algorithmic bias, and educator autonomy — related
  • The review identifies scarcity of long-term impact assessment, limited empirical validation of integrity interventions, and missing equity-focused policy research as key research gaps — related
  • AI raises the ceiling on problem complexity by reducing access complexity rather than cognitive demand — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Students reported that ChatGPT enhanced engagement, comprehension, problem-solving, and perceived relevance in Quantum Theory — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • AI-driven PBL environments enhance educator and student collaboration via real-time feedback, intelligent scaffolding, and data-informed instructional decisions — related
  • ChatGPT use in STEAM IBL is reported to enhance learner outcomes including motivation, critical thinking, engagement, and problem-solving — related
  • AI supporting SRL aims at end outcomes beyond SRL itself, most frequently improved academic performance — related
  • A systematic review identifies four overarching themes of factors affecting student engagement in adaptive learning platforms: system quality, information quality, learner characteristics and preferences, and affordances that mimic real-life social interactions — related
