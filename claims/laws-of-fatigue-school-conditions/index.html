G. M. W 1910¶

G. M. W. (1910). Abstracts and Reviews: Some Recent Literature upon Fatigue; Studies in Arithmetic. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069029

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review describes Offner's discussion of the "laws of fatigue" as showing how much has been established about these dependencies, including school-program and teacher factors; no specific effect sizes are printed.

"the dependence of fatigue upon age, sex, length and number of lessons, time of day, day in the week, time and length of recesses, holidays, and vacations, amount of rest, sleep, and other recuperative agencies"
