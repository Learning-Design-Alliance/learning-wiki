`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "All preschoolers performed with an accuracy signiﬁcantly above the chance level (50%) in both the word-object task (78% and 77% for Control and Study group, respectively) and the letter- sound task (63.97% and 64.14% for Control and Study group, respectively)."
