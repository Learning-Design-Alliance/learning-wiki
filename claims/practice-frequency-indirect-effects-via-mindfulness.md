Montero-Marin et al. (2021). Teachers “Finding Peace in a Frantic World”: An Experimental Study of Self-Taught and Instructor-Led Mindfulness Program Formats on Acceptability, Effectiveness, and Mechanisms. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000542

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analyses testing the indirect effects of practice frequency on pre-post improvements through gains in mindfulness, run separately for each treatment group. The article reports indirect effects through mindfulness in both arms; only practice frequency was examined because the authors state there is not enough evidence to consider other implementation variables.
