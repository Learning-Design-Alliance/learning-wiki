## Evidence

### Bonsaksen T 2017

Bonsaksen T, Brown T, Lim HB, Fong K. (2017). Approaches to studying predict academic performance in undergraduate occupational therapy students: a cross-cultural study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0914-3

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical linear regression (block 1: demographics and education) in a cross-sectional study of n = 712 students from four countries. Table 3 reports std. β = 0.11 for age (p < 0.01), β = −0.10 for gender (p = 0.01), β = 0.10 for self-study (p < 0.01); block explained "2.6%" of variance. Effects remained after controlling for ASSIST subscales.

> "Being of higher age, being female, and spending more time on self-study activities were all directly associated with higher GPA."
