## Evidence

### Bacon Paul 2021

Bacon Paul, Lord Rachel N. (2021). The impact of physically active learning during the school day on children's physical activity levels, time on task and learning behaviours and academic outcomes. Health Education Research. https://doi.org/10.1093/her/cyab020

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Secondary analysis of the same repeated-measures academic assessments (n = 29), comparing end-of-intervention and 7-day follow-up scores against the pre-intervention baseline in both conditions; the null result was reported at P > 0.05 with no effect size.

> "The retention of learning from the end of the intervention week (post-PAL1/post-NAL1) to the assessment 7 days later (post-PAL2/post-NAL2), indicated that for both learning conditions, knowledge had not been significantly retained (P > 0.05) compared to the pre-intervention assessment"
