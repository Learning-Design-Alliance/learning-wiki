## Evidence

### Yasumitsu et al. 2012

Tatsuo Yasumitsu, Haruo Nogawa, Yoshiro Hatano. (2012). Effects of the Coordination Exercise Program on School Children's Agility: Short-Time Program during School Recess. Journal of Research, volume 6, issue 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ954491

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pretest-posttest control group study of 60 third-grade students in Tokyo; the experimental group (n = 29) did the coordination program in 12 recesses over five weeks while controls did usual recess activities. Posttest means were "35.69 (7.24) for the experimental group and 32.87 (5.73) for the control group".

> "After the intervention, the scores of the repetitive side steps were 35.69 (7.24) for the experimental group and 32.87 (5.73) for the control group. The differences between the two groups were statistically significant in favor of the experiment group, with t (59) = 2.90, p < .005."
