Evidence¶

Mohammad Reza Amirian 2014¶

Mohammad Reza Amirian, Mohammad Davoudi, Mohsen Ramazanian. (2014). The Effect of Dynamic Assessment on Iranian EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 5(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.5n.3p.191

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test comparing post-test scores of the DA experimental group and the non-DA control group (14 learners each, randomly assigned). The article reports "t(26)= 3.36, P= 0.02<0.05" and an eta squared of 0.31, a medium effect size.

"There was a statistically significant difference in scores for control group (M=23.50 SD=2.76) and experimental group (M=26.85, SD=2.50); t(26)= 3.36, P= 0.02<0.05 two-tailed). The eta squared statistic (0.31) indicated a medium effect size based on Cohen (1988)."
