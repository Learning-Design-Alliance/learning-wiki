### Jasmin Breitwieser 2026

Jasmin Breitwieser, André Bittermann, Maria Theobald, Tim Lauer, Mirijam Schaaf, Garvin Brod. (2026). Digital Prompting in Education: A Design Framework and Bibliometric Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10164-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bibliometric analysis of 1,238 publications on digital prompting, with 4-gram extraction from abstracts and titles yielding 283 unique labels after synonym grouping. The authors report that "158 (55.83%) appeared in only one record across the whole dataset," evidencing terminological fragmentation.

> "Of the 283 identified prompt labels, 158 (55.83%) appeared in only one record across the whole dataset, indicating a long tail of idiosyncratic labels. This points to substantial variation in terminology and highlights a lack of standardization in how digital prompts are described."
