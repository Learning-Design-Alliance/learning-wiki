`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Cross-case synthesis of the landscape scan and state interviews. The report found states "are integrating performance assessments into a broader assessment strategy," citing Virginia's Balanced Assessment Plan and Colorado's Graduation Menu of Options as examples.

> "The information synthesized through the scan and case studies demonstrated that states are integrating performance assessments into a broader assessment strategy. Even within states where performance assessments are required for certain grades and subjects, this is only one of the assessments that students take during the school year."
