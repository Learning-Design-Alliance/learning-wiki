Leman Figen Gül 2025¶

Leman Figen Gül, Burak Delikanlı, Oğulcan Üneşi, Ertuğrul Ömer Gül. (2025). Development and applications of Generative AI in architectural design studios. ITU A|Z • Vol 22 No 3 • November 2025 • 751-774. https://doi.org/10.58278/0.2025.109

q2 · i? · design · r2

Semi-structured surveys in the third semester (Case-III) with third-year architecture students, using a 5-point Likert scale. The article reports "95% of all students (N:18)" in week six and "96% of all students (N:24)" in week thirteen felt GenAI positively affected their creativity; no effect size is printed.

"Overall, the majority of students (95% of all students (N:18) in sixth week (e.g. Survey I, Figure 1) and 96% of all students (N:24) in thirteenth week (e.g. Survey II, Figure 1) feel that the used GenAI models positively affect their creativity during the design process"
