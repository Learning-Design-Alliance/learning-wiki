## Evidence

### Seeskin 2020

Seeskin, A., Mahaffie, S., & Usher, A. (2020). The forgotten year: Applying lessons from Freshman success to Sophomore year. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/the-forgotten-year

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort analysis of CPS students (Figure 4) shows sophomore off-track status is highly predictive: "only 28 percent" of off-track sophomores graduated within four years. A second analysis found 30 percent of all non-graduates were on-track as sophomores, showing the indicator's limits.

> "only 28 percent of the 2014–15 freshman cohort (graduating class of 2018) who were off-track at the end of sophomore year graduated from high school within four years"
