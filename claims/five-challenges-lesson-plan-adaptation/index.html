A two-stage formative study (system use experiment plus semi-structured interviews with eight participants) distilled five core challenges: lack of systematic student profile data, difficulty translating student differences into targeted changes, low adoption of structured plans, difficulty crafting contextualized tasks, and fragmented non-iterative refinement. → Yanjie Zhang 2026

Qualitative formative study: a system use experiment with a structured lesson plan authoring tool followed by semi-structured interviews with six in-service teachers and two education experts. The authors report they "distilled five core challenges that informed our design requirements" (C1–C5).

"Through participants' reflections on lesson plan adaptation across diverse student groups and school contexts, we distilled five core challenges that informed our design requirements."
