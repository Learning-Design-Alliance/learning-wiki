Analysis of 9 course periods across 5 online biomedical science courses with 3,802 enrollments used first-attempt quiz scores to rank topics. "we analyzed quiz results across the 9 courses (3 cohorts) for each subject to derive the most challenging topics", filtering for topics appearing in the top 10 most difficult in at least two of three cohorts.
