### Weeks 1919

Weeks, A. L. (1919). Terman Vocabulary as a Group Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational pacing data from the group administration to 35 elementary children. Older grades finished the full list; the youngest grades depended on the spoken word and did not refer to the printed lists.

> "Grades five, six, seven, and eight, who were tested in one group, completed the list of fifty words in fourteen minutes. Few defined words after the thirty-fourth. In* grades three and four, only twenty-five words were completed in twenty-four minutes."
