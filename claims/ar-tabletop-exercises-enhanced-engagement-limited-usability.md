`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Mixed-method pilot study (Ahayalimudin et al. 2025, Malaysia) of an AR-based tabletop exercise application developed using Rapid Application Development and pilot tested with 20 final-year nursing students using the System Usability Scale. The review reports a "SUS score of 58.4"; the review classifies this study as low quality.

> "AR‐based TTX enhanced immersion, engagement, real‐time interaction, and perceived decision‐making skills compared to conventional TTX; however, usability was limited with a SUS score of 58.4 (“Not acceptable”), highlighting technical and usability challenges (device compatibility, need for technical support)."
