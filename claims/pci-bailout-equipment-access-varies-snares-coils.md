`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of 308 cardiologists at EuroPCR 2023–2024 on device availability in their usual cathlab. Respondents reported high availability of common bailout items, but "significantly reduced availability of coronary/aortic snares and coronary coils"; among European operators availability was 64.5%, 64.5%, and 71.0% respectively.

> "Over 93% of operators had access to covered stents, temporary pacing, pericardiocentesis, echocardiography and vasopressor drugs. However, there was significantly reduced availability of coronary/aortic snares and coronary coils, with availability rates of 65.7%, 59.4%, and 50.9%, respectively."
