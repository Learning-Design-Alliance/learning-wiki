Evidence¶

Juandi 2022¶

Juandi, D., Kusumah, Y. S., & Tamur, M. (2022). A Meta-Analysis of the Last Two Decades of Realistic Mathematics Education Approaches. International Journal of Instruction, 15(1), 381-400. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2022.15122a

q3 · i2

Meta-analysis of 54 effect sizes from 38 experimental studies with control classes, estimated with a random-effects model in CMA software. The pooled Hedges's g was "0.97" with a 95% CI of 0.80-1.14; the fixed-effects estimate was 0.67 with significant heterogeneity (Q = 487.19, p = 0.00).

"The resulting analysis (see Table 3) found that this study's overall effect size is measured at 0.97 based on the random -effect model, which suggests that RME implementation has a major positive effect on the mathematical abilities of students."
