Citywide self-report survey of teachers in grades four through eight; the analysis was restricted to 2,700 teachers in 345 schools, with Grow questions given to a random half of respondents. "About 60 percent indicated that they had used the Grow Web account to see results for their students," and roughly half endorsed each component; no effect sizes are reported.

"About 60 percent indicated that they had used the Grow Web account to see results for their students. Fifty-four percent found that the Grow Class Profile helped them to plan instruction; 52 percent reported that the Grow Report was a priority at their school; 52 percent believed that the Grow Parent Report was useful for communicating with parents;"
