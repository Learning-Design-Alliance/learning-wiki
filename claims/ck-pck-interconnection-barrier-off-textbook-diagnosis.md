### Markus Sebastian Feser & Ingrid Krumphals 2022

Markus Sebastian Feser & Ingrid Krumphals. (2022). Preparing pre-service physics teachers to diagnose students' conceptions not covered by physics education textbooks. The Physics Educator. https://doi.org/10.1142/S2661339522500081

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative analysis of transcribed video recordings of group activities in phases 4 and 5 of the two pilots found participants "perceived the learning environment's content ... as fragmented rather than interlinked"; only in exceptional cases did they build on synergies between the two knowledge bases.

> "they perceived the learning environment's content (especially the physics content and the physics education content) as fragmented rather than interlinked, and they found it too demanding to connect their content knowledge with their pedagogical content knowledge"
