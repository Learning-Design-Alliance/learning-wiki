Molly F. Gordon 2017¶

UChicago Consortium Research Report. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-closings-chicago-staff-and-student-experiences-and-academic-outcomes

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Quantitative portion comparing GPA outcomes against prior outcomes and similar-school students using administrative data through 2016-17. The delayed negative GPA effects were subgroup-specific and, per the report, these students' GPAs "were improving over time".

"Core GPA was not affected immediately after closures, although there were some negative effects three and four years post-closures for students from closed schools who had been in grades 3-5 in the announcement year (2012–13)."
