### Wick Katharina 2018

Wick Katharina, Faude Oliver, Manes Susanne, Zahner Lukas, Donath Lars. (2018). I Can Stand Learning: A Controlled Pilot Intervention Study on the Effects of Increased Standing Time on Cognitive Function in Primary School Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15020356

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Agreement analysis in the intervention class only (three students excluded), comparing control-sheet self-reports (mean 40.7 ± 6.5 min/day) with external observers (40.4 ± 4.9 min/day) via paired t-test, ICC, and a Bland–Altman plot (Figure 2).

> "The comparison of the two subjective assessments yielded similar results of self-report and external report (t(15) = −0.40, p = 0.70). The intra-class correlation coefficients between both subjective measure were excellent (ICC = 0.94)."
