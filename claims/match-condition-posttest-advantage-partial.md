## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Multilevel analysis showed match-condition students scored significantly higher on the post-test (b = 1.93, p = .04), but the separate comparison with the conceptual condition was only a trend (b = 1.89, p = .07). [→ B. Slof 2010](#b-slof-2010)

## Evidence

### B. Slof 2010

B. Slof, G. Erkens, P. A. Kirschner, J. G. M. Jaspers. (2010). Design and effects of representational scripting on group performance. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-010-9148-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of post-test scores of 90 students nested in 32 triads, correcting for pre-test differences (F(3, 86) = 3.34, p < .05 on the pre-test); the model fit the data (χ2(3) = 28.65, p = .00).

> "MLA revealed that students in the match condition scored signiﬁcantly higher than those in the other conditions ( b = 1.93, p = .04). When comparing the conditions sepa-rately, a trend was found; students in the match condition scored higher than students in the conceptual condition (b = 1.89, p = .07)."
