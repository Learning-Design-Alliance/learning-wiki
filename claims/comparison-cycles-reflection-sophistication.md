## Evidence

### N.G. Holmes 2015

N.G. Holmes, Carl E. Wieman, D.A. Bonn. (2015). Teaching critical thinking. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1505329112

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Coding of written reflective comments into four sophistication levels (analogous to Bloom's Taxonomy) in the first-year lab cohort comparison. The article describes the week-16 pattern descriptively; the supplementary regression for this outcome printed no standardized effect size.

> "By week 16, there is a larger diﬀerence between the groups, with the control group shifting to lower levels of comment sophistication and the experimental group maintaining higher levels of comment sophistication, despite the removal of the behavioural support."

### N.G. Holmes 2015 (2)

N.G. Holmes, Carl E. Wieman, D.A. Bonn. (2015). Teaching critical thinking. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1505329112

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Poisson-regression follow-up χ2 tests of proportions on maximum comment level. The sophomore-lab test was non-significant (χ2(3) = 7.58, p = .056 as printed elsewhere in the article), though the authors describe experimental students as still well above controls descriptively. No effect size printed.

> "This gave signiﬁcant diﬀerences between groups on all three ﬁrst-year experiments, but non-signiﬁcant diﬀerences on the sophomore-lab: Week 2, χ2(3) = 25.44,p < .001; Week 16, χ2(3) = 51.86,p < .0001; Week 17, χ2(3) = 155.83,p < .0001;"
