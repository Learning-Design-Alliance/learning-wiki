## Evidence

### Rahimi Hamed 2025

Rahimi Hamed, Etedali Hooman, Latifhadad Mohammad. (2025). The impact of social well-being on students’ academic motivation and academic achievement: a case study from Iran. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08109-3

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Component-level Spearman correlation analysis (Table 3) in the 261-student cross-sectional study; the largest printed component correlation was social contribution with to know (r = 0.499).

> "The Spearman's correlation coefficient showed that there was a significant and positive correlation between social well-being and the five components of academic motivation (to know, to accomplish things, to experience stimulation, identified regulation, and injectable regulation), while there was a significant negative correlation with the lack of motivation (P < 0.01)."
