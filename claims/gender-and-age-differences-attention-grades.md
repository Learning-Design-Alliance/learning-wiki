## Evidence

### Gall S 2018

Gall S, Adams L, Joubert N, Ludyga S, Müller I, Nqweniso S, Pühse U, du Randt R, Seelig H, Smith D, Steinmann P, Utzinger J, Walter C, Gerber M. (2018). Effect of a 20-week physical activity intervention on selective attention and academic performance in children living in disadvantaged neighborhoods: A cluster randomized control trial. PLoS ONE 13(11): e0206908. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206908

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Covariate effects from Model 1 of the mixed linear regression (n = 521 complete cases), with girls coded 1 and boys 0. Model 3 additionally shows girls had 0.35 grades higher EoYR than boys.

> "At post-intervention, girls had a 7.05 point higher CP score than boys, and younger children scored 4.65 points higher per additional year of age"

### Gall S 2018 (2)

Gall S, Adams L, Joubert N, Ludyga S, Müller I, Nqweniso S, Pühse U, du Randt R, Seelig H, Smith D, Steinmann P, Utzinger J, Walter C, Gerber M. (2018). Effect of a 20-week physical activity intervention on selective attention and academic performance in children living in disadvantaged neighborhoods: A cluster randomized control trial. PLoS ONE 13(11): e0206908. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0206908

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model 3 covariate effect; authors explain that older retained learners may include children with unmet special needs such as ADHD or reading difficulties in communities lacking resources for support.

> "Finally, for every year younger a child was, children had 0.18 grades higher EoYR at post-intervention"
