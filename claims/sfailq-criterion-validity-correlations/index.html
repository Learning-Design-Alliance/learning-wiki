Liu Qingqi, Miao Wenjiao, Li Jingjing, Lei Yuju. (2026). The Six-Facet Artificial Intelligence Literacy Questionnaire (SFAILQ): Assessing AI Literacy in Adolescents, Young Adults, and Midlife Adults. Behavioral Sciences, 16(7), 1110. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071110

Criterion-related validity analysis in adolescents and university students using the ASES-C academic self-efficacy scale and the Utrecht Work Engagement Scale–Student–9 as criteria. The article also reports all dimensions correlated significantly positively with employees' work self-efficacy, self-development strongest.

"All six dimensions and the total score of the SFAILQ were significantly positively correlated with students’ academic self-efficacy, with the usage skills dimension showing the strongest correlation."
