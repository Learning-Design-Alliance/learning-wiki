## Evidence

### Gull Ambreen 2026

Gull Ambreen, Yasmin Rahila, Mahboob Usman, Golnik Karl C., Moin Muhammad. (2026). Validation of the ICO-OCEX tool for competency-based assessment in ophthalmology residency programs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09154-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Twelve ophthalmology faculty from four teaching hospitals participated in think-aloud and verbal probing interviews lasting 15-20 minutes each. Items were coded by two independent reviewers on a cognitive validity scale from 0 to 3. No item was categorized as having major problems, so none was merged or removed due to redundancy.

> "Cognitive pre-testing with 12 participants led to rephrasing of 7 items requiring further clarification or modification, and conversion of one item (IPS 3) into two separate items, resulting in the final ICO-OCEX instrument with 34 items measuring four constructs."
