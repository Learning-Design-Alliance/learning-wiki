Harris 2014¶

Harris, A.R., Jennings, P.A., Abenavoli, R.M., Katz, D.A., Greenberg, M.T., & Schussler, D. (2014). A daily dose of CALM: Supporting middle school educators' wellbeing and classroom functioning through a brief stress reduction intervention. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562741

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

ANCOVA analysis of the quasi-experimental trial with 64 educators found the intervention condition scored significantly higher in "their degree of mindful observation" (F = 4.91*, d = 0.41).

"Participants in the intervention condition scored significantly higher in one aspect of mindfulness: their degree of mindful observation."
