## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The article draws an inverse relationship between the quantity of materials and their effects on conceptual understanding, observed especially in the ethernet card and sound card units. [→ Unal Cakiroglu 2017](#unal-cakiroglu-2017)

## Evidence

### Unal Cakiroglu 2017

Unal Cakiroglu, Huseyin Yilmaz. (2017). Using Videos and 3D Animations for Conceptual Learning in Basic Computer Units. Contemporary Educational Technology, 8(4), 390-405. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1158165

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation from the Discussion, based on observed post-test patterns across units; the article also states that when the number of 3D animations was less than the number of videos, conceptual changes were not realized at the expected level. No statistical test is reported for this relationship.

> "Hence, w hen the number of videos of 3D animati ons was quite limited, the number of incorrect or partly true answers was high."
