## Evidence

### Sartania Nana 2014

Sartania Nana, McClure John D, Sweeting Helen, Browitt Allison. (2014). Predictive power of UKCAT and other pre-admission measures for performance in a medical school in Glasgow: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-116

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations (Table 2) between UKCAT measures and the other pre-admission criteria in the cohort: quantitative reasoning r = 0.265 (p = 0.000) with TSS; decision analysis r = -0.161 (p = 0.027) with interview score; UKCAT total r = 0.095 (p = 0.197) with TSS.

> "There were only two significant associations; quantitative reasoning was positively associated with TSS and decision analysis was negatively associated with interview score."
