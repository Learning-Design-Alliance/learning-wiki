`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation in the magic-angle section: with parallel dipoles the scalar products reduce to the condition sin²θ − 2cos²θ = 0, i.e. tanθ = √2. The paper notes the literature derives the same angle from the original formula via 3cos²θ = 1. No effect size applies.

> "This gives the magic angle 54.74 degrees. In the literature this angle is usually derived from the original formula where number 3 appears which leads simply to the condition 3 cos2θ = 1 giving naturally the same magic angle."
