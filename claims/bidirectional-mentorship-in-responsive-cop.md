## Evidence

### Talafian 2025

Talafian, H., Lundsgaard, M., Mahmood, M., Shafer, D., Stelzer, T., & Kuo, E. (2025). Responsive professional development: A facilitation approach for teachers' development in a physics teaching community of practice. Teaching and Teacher Education, 153, 104812. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tate.2024.104812

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of 29 coded segments from 12 teachers on mentorship opportunities showed collaboration transcended the mentor-mentee dyad described in the original CoP conceptualization. A veteran teacher reflected on gaining new approaches from other teachers, and an early career teacher described learning from a veteran's lab walkthroughs.

> "The results revealed that both novice and experienced teachers benefited from the mentorship opportunity, each in different ways. Novice teachers appreciated getting help from more experienced teachers, and veteran teachers valued novice teachers' new perspectives."
