## Evidence

### Antonio Ruiz-Martínez 2022

Antonio Ruiz-Martínez, Linda Castañeda, and Jesualdo T. Fernández Breis. (2022). A systematic literature review on the development and use of mobile learning (web) apps by early adopters. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.13480](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.13480)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's tally of educational contexts across the 62 analysed publications shows formal contexts dominate: "49 experiences - 79,03%", with informal (11.29%) and semi-formal (10.94%) contexts far less common.

> "Analysing the educational context where the diﬀerent experiences were developed, the results show (see Figure 11) that most of them were developed in a formal context (49 experiences - 79,03%). Then, we have found 7 experiences (11,29%) developed in an informal context and 4 experiences (10,94%) in a semi-formal context."

### Antonio Ruiz-Martínez 2022 (2)

Antonio Ruiz-Martínez, Linda Castañeda, and Jesualdo T. Fernández Breis. (2022). A systematic literature review on the development and use of mobile learning (web) apps by early adopters. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2212.13480](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2212.13480)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Duration analysis of the reviewed experiences shows short-term designs dominate: "most of the experiences last one session or at most 10 weeks", with only 7 long-term experiences reported.

> "In Figure 9 we can see that most of the experiences last one session or at most 10 weeks (short term experiences). The number of experiences that report long term periods is quite reduced (only 7)."
