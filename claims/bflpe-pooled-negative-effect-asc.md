# Class/school average achievement has a significant negative effect on student academic self-concept (BFLPE), pooled β = −0.28

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across 33 studies with 56 effect sizes, class/school average achievement negatively predicts student academic self-concept with a pooled random-effects β of −0.28. [→ Fang J 2018](#fang-j-2018)

## Evidence

### Fang J 2018

Fang J, Huang X, Zhang M, Huang F, Li Z and Yuan Q (2018) The Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect on Academic Self-Concept: A Meta-Analysis. Front. Psychol. 9:1569. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01569

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 33 studies with 56 effect sizes (total N = 1,276,838), chosen after heterogeneity testing (Q = 25,478.88, I2 = 99.78%). The pooled effect β = −0.28 means students in a class/school with average ability one standard deviation above the mean have ASC "0.28 of a standard deviation below the average ASC level."

> "The integrated results showed a significant negative effect of class/school average achievement on student ASC: β = − 0.28 (Z = −13.84, p < 0.001, 95% CI = [−0.32, −0.24])"
