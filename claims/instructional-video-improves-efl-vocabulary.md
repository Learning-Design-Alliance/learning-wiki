# Instructional video materials significantly improve vocabulary learning of Iranian EFL learners compared with no video treatment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learners taught with instructional video materials scored significantly higher on a researcher-made vocabulary posttest than control learners taught by input-based instruction with mechanical drills. [→ Isazadeh 2016](#isazadeh-2016)

## Evidence

### Isazadeh 2016

Isazadeh, P., Makui, S. M. Z., & Ansarian, L. (2016). Effect of Instructional vs. Authentic Video Materials on Introvert and Extrovert Iranian EFL Learners' Vocabulary Learning. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 4(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.4n.4p.1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing the two instructional-video groups (combined) against the control group after 10 treatment sessions of 45 minutes each. The instructional-video groups scored "M = 20.72, SD = 3.411" versus the control's 17.40, t(48) = 3.988, p < .0005.

> "the scores were significantly higher for experimental groups (combination of groups 3 and 4) ( M = 20.72, SD = 3.411) than for control group ( M = 17.40, SD = 2.778), t (48) = 3.988, p< .0005"
