Interaction analysis of the Academic Year 1 student work analysis episode, in a qualitative case study of the RepTaLs PD project. Sarah characterized an English learner's mixed-up sentence as humorous, and "Facilitator Heather redirected the group to the artifacts themselves" rather than challenging the deficit framing.

"This statement positioned MLs' writing as a mistake to be fixed—a deficit perspective that privileged English—rather than seeing the student's writing, for example, as evidence of complex thinking."
