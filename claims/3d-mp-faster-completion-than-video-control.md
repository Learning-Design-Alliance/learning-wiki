## Evidence

### Yiasemidou M 2017

Yiasemidou M, Glassman D, Mushtaq F, Athanasiou C, Williams M-M, Jayne D, Miskovic D. (2017). Mental practice with interactive 3D visual aids enhances surgical performance. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5459-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory case-matched simulator study of 20 surgical trainees allocated 1:1:2 to MPO, 3D&MP and control. One-way ANOVA on completion time showed "a main effect in the amount of time taken" with the control group slower (M = 1447, SD = 341) than the 3D&MP group (M = 670, SD = 326).

> "a main effect in the amount of time taken by participants to complete the simulated LC (F(2,17) = 8.77, p = .002, η p ² = 0.51), with the Control group (M = 1447, SD = 341) taking significantly longer (p = .002) relative to the 3D & MP group (M = 670, SD = 326)"
