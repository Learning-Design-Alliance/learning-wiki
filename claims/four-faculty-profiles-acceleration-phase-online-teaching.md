## Evidence

### Trevisan 2023

Trevisan, O., De Rossi, M., Christensen, R., Knezek, G., & Smits, A. (2023). Factors shaping faculty online teaching competencies during the Covid-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Snapshot survey of 120 higher education faculty in early 2020, analyzed with two-step cluster analysis. The four profiles were Cautious (22%), Open (23%), Optimistic (35%) and Enthusiastic (21%); the article reports "keeping up with technologies (η2 = .07, medium effect size), attending recent training (η2 = .16, very large effect size)" as differing among clusters.

> "A 2-step cluster analysis of the respondents for S1 (N = 120) revealed four response patterns across the three main factors of the questionnaire, i.e., Enthusiasm, Resolutions and Support."
