### Alshareef N 2024

Alshareef N, Fletcher I, Giga S. (2024). The role of emotions in academic performance of undergraduate medical students: a narrative review. BMC Medical Education 24:907. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05894-1

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

The review reports Green's (2016) cross-sectional and RCT study of 25 second-year medical students before USMLE Step 1, in which "Test anxiety negatively correlated with USMLE step 1 (r= − 0.24)" — a printed correlation of i=2 magnitude; a test-taking strategy course reduced anxiety but did not raise scores.

### Alshareef N 2024 (2)

Alshareef N, Fletcher I, Giga S. (2024). The role of emotions in academic performance of undergraduate medical students: a narrative review. BMC Medical Education 24:907. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05894-1

`q2 · i1` · `review · r2`

The review reports Nazir et al.'s (2021) cross-sectional study of 680 medical students using the Test Anxiety Inventory, finding excellent students exhibited lower test anxiety with the printed odds ratio "OR = 1.47 (95% CI:1.05, 2.06), P = 0.02".
