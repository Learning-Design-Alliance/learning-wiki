## Evidence

### Chiu 2014

Fu-Yuan Chiu. (2014). The Application of a Cloud-Based Student, Teacher, and Parent Platform in English as a Foreign Language Education. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 13(3), 178-187. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1034232

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Conclusions section, based on the three-group quasi-experiment; it is an explanatory reading of the Group A finding rather than a separately tested result.

> "This finding indicates that the CSTPP is better suited to home-based self-study. Compared with using the CSTPP in classrooms without parental participation, when students conducted self-study at home, and teachers interacted closely with parents via the telephone or messages to collaboratively supervise students’ learning progress, students attitudes’ toward instruction were more effectively enhanced, and a learner-centered innovative teaching mode was achieved."
