## Evidence

### Touchie Claire 2013

Touchie Claire, Humphrey-Murto Susan, Varpio Lara. (2013). Teaching and assessing procedural skills: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-69

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group study of 18 Internal Medicine residents (8 R1s, 7 R2s, 3 R3s) conducted after a formative OSCE; fundamental qualitative description yielded five themes from 174 distinct comments. Residents stated they had to "actively seek out opportunities to do a procedure" and received feedback only sporadically.

> "Participants reported that proficiency was acquired through resident directed learning with no formal mechanism to ensure acquisition or maintenance of skills."
