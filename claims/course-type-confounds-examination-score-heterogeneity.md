## Evidence

### Zhang Yanqi 2015

Zhang Yanqi, Zhou Liang, Liu Xiaoyu, Liu Ling, Wu Yazhou, Zhao Zengwei, Yi Dali, Yi Dong. (2015). The Effectiveness of the Problem-Based Learning Teaching Model for Use in Introductory Chinese Undergraduate Medical Courses: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0120884

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression across the three outcomes examined six confounding factors: degree major, teaching pattern, course type, PBL group tutor scale, study type and modified Jadad score. Only course type was significant for examination scores (t = 0.410, P<0.001); other factors were non-significant.

> "we discovered that course type is the significant confounding factor that causes examination-score meta-regression heterogeneity (t = 0.410, P<0.001)"
