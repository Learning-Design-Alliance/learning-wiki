## Evidence

### diSessa et al. 2004

Andrea A. diSessa, Nicole M. Gillespie, Jennifer B. Esterly. (2004). Coherence versus fragmentation in the development of the concept of force. Cognitive Science 28 (2004) 843–900. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogsci.2004.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The extension study, based on a more encompassing specification of the concept of force, surfaced reasoning beyond I&V's small universe of meanings. The authors conclude that I&V's "claims to have demonstrated coherence seem strongly undermined."

> "We found much reasoning of subjects about forces that cannot be accounted for by the meanings specified by Ioannides and Vosniadou."
