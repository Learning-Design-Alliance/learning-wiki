`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the first round of structured interviews with the middle-school students, using four open questions about science and BVI people. Most students named curing blindness or special glasses; "a minority of students suggesting that other senses, such as hearing or touch, could be used."

> "Most students thought that the best way in which technologies could help BVI people to access scientific knowledge was to cure blindness or to invent special glasses that somehow would allow BVI to see. There was a minority of students suggesting that other senses, such as hearing or touch, could be used."
