## Evidence

### Dillenbourg 2016

Dillenbourg, P., Lemaignan, S., Sangin, M., Nova, N., & Molinari, G. (2016). The symmetry of partner modelling. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-016-9235-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation of the symmetry results from the five collaborative experiments: assessed model accuracies were correlated between partners, which the article reads as pointing to interaction quality over individual ability. The article frames this as an implication ("seems to imply"), and uses no effect size, so none is reported.
