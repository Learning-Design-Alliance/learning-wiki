## Evidence

### Dickman N 2017

Dickman N, Barash A, Reis S, Karasik D. (2017). Students as anatomy near-peer teachers: a double-edged sword for an ancient skill. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0996-y

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative free-language comments in the end-of-program debriefing questionnaire. Participants reported feeling at ease in the dissecting room, preparing and leading a CBL session with confidence, and some "expressed their surprise in finding out that their classmates are good teachers".

> "The students pointed out that the course helped them to develop essential teaching skills and universal competencies, which would support their professional growth throughout the medical school"
