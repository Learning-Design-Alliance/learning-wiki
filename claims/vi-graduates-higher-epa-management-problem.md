## Evidence

### Wijnen-Meijer M 2015

Wijnen-Meijer M, ten Cate O, van der Schaaf M, Burgers C, Borleffs J, Harendza S. (2015). Vertically integrated medical education and the readiness for practice of graduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0514-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

In the same 59-candidate UHTRUST assessment, physicians rated required supervision on nine EPAs unrelated to the observed scenarios on a 5-point entrustment scale; the Dutch VI group scored higher on "solving a management problem" (Mann-Whitney r = .35).

> "In contrast, the Dutch candidates (M = 3.70; SD = .53) received higher scores for "solving a management problem" than the German candidates (M = 3.19; SD = .75; Mann-Whitney U = 265; z = 2.67; p = .01, r = .35)."
