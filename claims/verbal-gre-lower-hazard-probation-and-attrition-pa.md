## Evidence

### Waynick 2026

Waynick, J., Hussaini, S. S., Stonerook, E., Hiatt, T., Sisson, C., Peacock, B., O'Neal, S., McKinnond, A., Steele, L., & Gillette, C. (2026). Using academic admissions data to predict PA training attrition and academic difficulties: a survival analysis approach. Medical Education Online, 31(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2745370

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cox proportional hazards analysis of the full PA cohort found verbal GRE scores associated with time to attrition ("aHR = 0.87, 95% CI = 0.80–0.94"); the abstract additionally reports aHR = 0.91 (95% CI 0.87–0.94) for time to first probation.

> "Verbal GRE scores (aHR = 0.87, 95% CI = 0.80–0.94) and number of undergraduate course withdrawals (aHR = 1.24, 95% CI = 1.08–1.43) were associated with time to attrition."
