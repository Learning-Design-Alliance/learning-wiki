### Horner 2018

Horner, R. H., Newton, J. S., Todd, A. W., Algozzine, B., Algozzine, K., Cusumano, D., & Preston, A. (2018). A Randomized Waitlist Controlled Analysis of Team-Initiated Problem Solving Professional Development and Use. Behavioral Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1177/0198742917745638

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

School outcomes analysis of SWIS-reported OSS rates per month; the article reports the group difference as statistically significant (χ 2 = 5.46, df = 1, p = .02), with no effect size printed and no additional change at O4 (p = .22).

> "At O 3, a total of 13 of 18 (72%) IM teams documented a reduction in OSS per 100 students while only six of 18 (33%) WL teams documented a reduction in OSS per 100 students"
