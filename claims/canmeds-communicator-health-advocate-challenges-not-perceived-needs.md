## Evidence

### McConnell Meghan 2018

McConnell Meghan, Gu Ada, Arshad Aysha, Mokhtari Arastoo, Azzam Khalid. (2018). An innovative approach to identifying learning needs for intrinsic CanMEDS roles in continuing professional development. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2018.1497374

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Content analysis of open-ended survey responses from 411 physicians compared frequencies of intrinsic CanMEDS roles across two questions; the study reports Communicator and Health Advocate "were frequently described in narratives surrounding challenges in practice, but appeared in <10% of descriptions of perceived learning needs".

> "Conversely, Communicator 39%, Health Advocate 39%, and to a lesser extent Collaborator 11%) roles were frequently described in narratives surrounding challenges in practice, but appeared in <10% of descriptions of perceived learning needs (Communicator: 4%; Health Advocate: 6%; Collaborator: 3%)."
