## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among 1,398 Ukrainian university students surveyed in 2023, 85.8% screened depression symptoms, 66.1% anxiety symptoms, 56.9% sleep problems and 48.1% PTSD symptoms. [→ Pinchuk Irina 2025](#pinchuk-irina-2025)

## Evidence

### Pinchuk Irina 2025

Pinchuk Irina, Feldman Inna, Seleznova Violetta, Virchenko Volodymyr. (2025). Braving the dark: mental health challenges and academic performance of Ukrainian university students during the war. Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00127-025-02867-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of Ukrainian university students conducted September–November 2023 using GAD-7, PHQ-9, PC-PTSD-5 and ISI screening instruments; "66.1% of all respondents screened anxiety symptoms" and 85.8% depression symptoms. No effect sizes are printed for prevalence.

> "Regarding mental health problems, 66.1% of all respondents screened anxiety symptoms (mild, moderate and severe) according to the obtained GAD-7 scores, 85.8%– depression symptoms (mild, moderate, moderately severe and severe) according to the obtained PHQ-9 scores, 48.1%– PTSD symptoms according to the obtained PC-PTSD-5 scores, 56.9%– symptoms of sleep disorder"
