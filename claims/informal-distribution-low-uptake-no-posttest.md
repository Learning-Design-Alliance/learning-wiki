`q2 i?` Of 68 students given a download link with a request to play over four weeks, only 26 downloaded the game, and a post-test was not carried out. [→ P. Klein 2021](#p-klein-2021)

## Evidence

### P. Klein 2021

P. Klein, N. Burkard, L. Hahn, M. N. Dahlkemper, K. Eberle, T. Jaeger, J. Kuhn and M. Herrlich. (2021). Coordinating vector field equations and diagrams with a serious game in introductory physics. arXiv:2008.05520. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/abef5c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/abef5c)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-report and download-log survey after informal distribution of the game in January 2020. The article reports "only 26 actually downloaded the game" of 68 invited; of the downloaders, 13 played under 10 minutes, 9 between 10 minutes and 1 hour, and 6 to the end. No effect size is printed.

> "Of the 68 students to whom the download link for the game was issued with the request to play the game during the upcoming four weeks, only 26 actually downloaded the game."
