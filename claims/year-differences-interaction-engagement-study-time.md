## Evidence

### Gherghel 2023

Gherghel, C., Yasuda, S., & Kita, Y. (2023). Interaction during online classes fosters engagement with learning and self-directed study both in the first and second years of the COVID-19 pandemic. Computers & Education, 200, 104795. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104795

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-year comparisons of the two questionnaire waves (659 respondents in 2020, 508 in 2021). The article reports 2020 students perceived more interactive opportunities and studied more hours outside class; 2021 students reported higher emotional engagement. The authors caution the interaction scale lacked scalar invariance across years.

> "There were significant differences between 2020 and 2021 in students’ evaluations of opportunities for social interaction during online classes (t[1159] = 23.74, p < .001). In 2020, first-year students reported more interactive opportunities than first-year students in 2021"
