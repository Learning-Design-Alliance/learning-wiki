### Lobão Gonçalves Anna Luiza 2019

Lobão Gonçalves Anna Luiza, Ayroza-Ribeiro Helizabet Abdala, Lima Raquel Ferreira, Yonamine Aline Estefane Eras, Ohara Fabio, Ayroza-Ribeiro Paulo Augusto Galvão. (2019). RBGO Gynecology & Obstetrics. https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0039-1700587

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional observational review of 244 TLH charts in three time-frame groups (I-1: 24; I-2: 55; I-3: 165). Mean operative times were 228.8 min (I-2) versus 204.5 min (I-3); the article reports "p = 0.01" for the I-2 versus I-3 contrast.

> "The operative time was longer in group I-2 when compared to group I-3 (p = 0.01); there was no difference between groups I-1/I-2 and I-1/I-3 (Table 5)."
