`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review coding of 56 studies identified 32 motivational constructs, categorized per Troia et al. (2012). The review reports attitude most often investigated (n = 20), and the interest‑and‑value component most frequent overall (n = 39), followed by self‑beliefs (n = 28).

> "Frequency numbers for associated constructs show that attitude was clearly investigated most often (n = 20), followed by self-efficacy (n = 11), and motivation (n = 9). These fre -quency numbers also correspond with total numbers for each category, showing that the theorized component of interest and value was investigated most often (n = 39), followed by self-beliefs (n = 28), and motivation (n = 11)."
