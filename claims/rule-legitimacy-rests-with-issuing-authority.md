### Scott Williams and Michael Courtney 2010

Scott Williams and Michael Courtney. (2010). Why Cheating is Wrong. U.S. Air Force Academy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1102.1506](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1102.1506)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Philosophical argument rebutting Bouville's claim that breaking a rule is illegitimate only if the rule has rational justification. The authors argue that enrollment constitutes implicit or explicit agreement to institutional rules, and that individual veto over rules promotes anarchy.

> "The legitimacy of rules rests in the legitimacy of the issuing authority; it is not given to individual students to whimsically decide whether or not the rules apply to them, especially when the student has agreed (implicitly or explicitly) to the rules by virtue of enrollment."
