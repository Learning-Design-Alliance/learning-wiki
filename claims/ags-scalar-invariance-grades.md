### Lin Rongmao 2024

Lin Rongmao, Chen Yanping, Shen Yilin, Hu Ting, Huang Ying, Yang Yishan, Yu Xueting, Ding Jinliang. (2024). Academic grit scale for Chinese middle- and upper-grade primary school students: testing its factor structure and measurement invariance. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-01622-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multi-group CFA across the four grade groups (n = 371, 557, 451, and 515 as printed). Metric invariance held with ΔCFI = 0.003 and scalar invariance with ΔCFI = 0.004, "indicating scalar invariance across grades" despite significant chi-squared increases.

> "In the scalar invariance model (Model 12), except for S-B χ2 significantly increasing (p = .003), the change in CFI (0.004) was smaller than.005, indicating scalar invariance across grades."
