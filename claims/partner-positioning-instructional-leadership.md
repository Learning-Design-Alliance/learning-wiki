`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative findings from teacher interviews in the Topeka study. Partner teachers described instruction as a patchwork of self-made and Topeka materials, with one noting "I still teach the same way" despite valuing quick feedback, and used data for re-teaching decisions.

> "Teachers who positioned AI as a partner also used score data in more sophisticated ways, such as using it to determine what to stop and re-teach, which resources to provide, or which students needed extra help."
