### Del Rey 2018

Del Rey, R., Mora-Merchán, J.A., Casas, J.A., Ortega-Ruiz, R., & Elipe, P. (2018). 'Asegúrate' Program: Effects on cyber-aggression and its risk factors. Comunicar, 56, XXVI. https://doi.org/10.3916/C56-2018-04

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 479 secondary students (292 intervention, 187 control) across seven schools in Andalucía, with two measures over time. Percent variation showed cyber aggression involvement "diminished by 17.5% in the quasi-experimental group and increased by 52% in the control group".
