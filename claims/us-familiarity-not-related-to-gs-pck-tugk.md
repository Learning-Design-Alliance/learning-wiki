## Subclaims

`q2 i?` American graduate students exposed to US teaching did not perform better at identifying introductory difficulties than foreign graduate students (60%, 63% and 66% of maximum respectively). [→ Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013](#alexandru-maries-and-chandralekha-singh-2013)

`q2 i?` A Kruskal-Wallis test revealed comparable TUG-K related PCK performance across American, Chinese and other foreign graduate student groups. [→ Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2014](#alexandru-maries-and-chandralekha-singh-2014)

## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Teaching Assistants Using the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of average PCK scores of nine American, nine Chinese and seven other foreign graduate students (Table 1). The article states the group sizes are "too small for meaningful statistics" and offers no effect size or significance test for this contrast.

> "it appears that the averages of the America n, Chinese and Other foreign graduate students (60%, 63% and 66% of the maximum PCK score, 6.70, respectively) are comparable."

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2014

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2014). Performance of Graduate Students at Identifying Introductory Physics Students’ Difficulties Related to Kinematics Graphs. 2014 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.039

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison in the Results section of a study of twenty-five first-year graduate students in a TA training class (nine American, nine Chinese, seven from other countries). The Kruskal-Wallis test "reveals that the three groups exhibit comparable performance"; group averages were 4.00, 4.24 and 4.46. No effect size is printed.

> "A non-parametric test (Kruskal-Wallis) reveals that the three groups exhibit comparable performance on the TUG -K related PCK task"
