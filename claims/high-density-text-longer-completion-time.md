## Evidence

### Ross 1988

Ross, S. M., Morrison, G. R., & O'Dell, J. K. (1988). Obtaining More out of Less Text in CBI: Effects of Varied Text Density Levels as a Function of Learner Characteristics and Control Strategy. Educational Communication and Technology, 36(3), 131-142. http://www.jstor.org/stable/30218252

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on completion time excluding LC treatments, from the same randomized experiment. The density effect remained significant after subtracting estimated paging times: "the significant density condition effect was maintained, F (1,44) = 5.12, p < .05."

> "completion times were longer for CBI (M = 25.8 min.) than for print (M = 21.5), F (1,144) = 4.45, p < .05, and for high-density (M = 26.5) than for low-density text (M = 21.0), F (1,144) = 8.60, p < .01."
