`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Air leak measured at moderate ventilation intensity with three tube diameters (2.0, 2.5, 3.0 mm) in each simulator. The article reports "leaks greater than 80%" for Premature AirwayPaul and Preterm Baby, no measurable leak for Premature Anne, and a diameter-dependent leak for Premie HAL S2209.

> "Premature Anne (Laerdal Medical) had no measurable air leak regardless of the tube diameter. In contrast, we measured leaks greater than 80% for both Premature AirwayPaul (SIMCharacters) and Preterm Baby (Lifecast Body Simulation) regardless of the tube diameter."
