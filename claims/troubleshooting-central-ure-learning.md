### N.G. Holmes and Carl E. Wieman 2016

N.G. Holmes and Carl E. Wieman. (2016). Examining and contrasting the cognitive activities engaged in undergraduate research experiences and lab courses. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020103](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020103)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group results on construction and testing of apparatus: in almost every interview the majority of time was spent discussing this task. Most students said no single course taught them troubleshooting, though some said it was picked up along the way to some degree.

> "Troubleshooting was both frustrating and rewarding, and often cited as an element where the most learning occurred. When asked about content students felt was needed to prepare them for their URE, troubleshooting was the most common response."
