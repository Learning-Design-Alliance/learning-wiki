Multigroup multilevel mediation model for support for mathematical discourse in the TRIAD RCT: treatment raised ratings (0.22 points, p = .01), but the achievement paths were "not statistically significant for both groups" and bootstrap mediation tests were non-significant.

> "The path from support for mathematical discourse to post-treatment mathematics achievement was not statistically significant for both groups (b = −0.11, p = .24; b = 0.16, p = .13, for non-African American and African American students, respectively)."
