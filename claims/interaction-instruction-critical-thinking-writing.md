## Evidence

### Hasani 2016

Hasani, A. (2016). Enhancing argumentative writing skill through contextual teaching and learning. Educational Research and Reviews, 11(16), 1573-1578. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1113515

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-way ANOVA on the 52-student quasi-experiment reports the interaction as significant: "the F value (4.61) > F table (4. 030)". The same table reports a significant critical thinking main effect (F = 18.15) and group effect (F = 9.65).

> "The findings also suggest that there is an interaction between instructional model and critical thinking ability; the F value (4.61) > F table (4. 030)"
