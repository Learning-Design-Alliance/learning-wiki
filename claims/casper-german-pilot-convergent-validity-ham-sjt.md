# Casper correlates with the German closed-ended SJT (HAM-SJT) but not with OSCE communication station performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Casper scores correlated significantly with HAM-SJT performance (r=.18), supporting convergent validity. [→ Knorr Mirjana 2024](#knorr-mirjana-2024)
`q2 i?` Casper was neither related to OSCE history taking station performance (r=-.09) nor to the communication skills station (r=.08), failing to replicate North-American predictive validity evidence. [→ Knorr Mirjana 2024 (2)](#knorr-mirjana-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Knorr Mirjana 2024

Knorr Mirjana, Mielke Ina, Amelung Dorothee, Safari Mahla, Gröne Oana R., Breil Simon M., MacIntosh Alexander. (2024). Measuring personal characteristics in applicants to German medical schools: Piloting an online Situational Judgement Test with an open-ended response format. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001685.shtml

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation between Casper and the closed-ended paper-pencil HAM-SJT in the pilot sample. The discussion cites this as support for the convergent validity of the test.

> "With respect to other measures of personal characteristics, Casper had a significant relationship with HAM-SJT performance (r=.18, p=.004, n=263)"

### Knorr Mirjana 2024 (2)

Knorr Mirjana, Mielke Ina, Amelung Dorothee, Safari Mahla, Gröne Oana R., Breil Simon M., MacIntosh Alexander. (2024). Measuring personal characteristics in applicants to German medical schools: Piloting an online Situational Judgement Test with an open-ended response format. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001685.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations with two communication-targeting OSCE stations in small subsamples of admitted students. The authors note range restriction (OSCE scores 52.5%–100%) and single-station unreliability as potential limiting factors; equivalence was not tested.

> "but was neither related to performance at the OSCE history taking station (r=-.09, p=.37, n=94) nor to the communication skills station (r=.08, p=.57, n=55)."
