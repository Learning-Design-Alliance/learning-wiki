## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across 43 studies analyzed with a random effects model, students taught with the constructivist approach held more positive attitudes towards lessons than students taught by traditional methods (ES = 0.728, Z = 5.115, p < .05). [→ Toraman 2016](#toraman-2016)

## Evidence

### Toraman 2016

Toraman, C., & Demir, E. (2016). The effect of constructivism on attitudes towards lessons: A meta–analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 62, 115-142. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2016.62.8

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of 43 studies (28 theses, 15 articles) conducted in Turkey between 2004 and 2015, with N = 1,529 experimental and N = 1,539 control participants, analyzed in CMA software under the random effect model. The printed effect size is ES = 0.728 (Z = 5.115, p < .05); the article reports "the value of the average effect size was found to be 0,728 and positive", favouring the experimental group.

> "When the results of our study were analyzed according the random effect model, the effect size was found to be significant with a confidence interval of 95% (Z = 5,115, p<.05). The value of the average effect size was found to be 0,728 and positive."
