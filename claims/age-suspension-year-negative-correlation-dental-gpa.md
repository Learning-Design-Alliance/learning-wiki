# Age, suspension history, and later academic year correlate negatively with dental students' GPA

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Suspension history correlates inversely with GPA (r = -0.236), as does academic year (r = -0.219) and age (r = -0.336), all significant at p < 0.01. [→ Almalki Sultan A. 2024](#almalki-sultan-a-2024)

## Evidence

### Almalki Sultan A. 2024

Almalki Sultan A., AlJameel AlBandary H., Alghomlas Ziyad, Alothman Tameem, Alhajri Fawaz. (2024). Assessing the predictive validity of pre-admission criteria on dental students’ academic performance: a cross-sectional study. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-03839-6

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 3) of the 900-student cross-sectional questionnaire dataset. The study also prints a negative age–GPA correlation of -0.336 (p < 0.01) in the same table, described as "an inverse relationship with GPA."

> "The number of times a student had been suspended showed an inverse relationship with GPA (r = -0.236, p < 0.01), suggesting that disciplinary issues might be a hindrance to academic achievement. Similarly, a negative correlation between the academic year of the student and GPA (r = -0.219, p < 0.01) was observed"
