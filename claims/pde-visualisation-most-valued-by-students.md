### Pavan Inguva 2021

Pavan Inguva, Vijesh J. Bhute, Thomas N.H. Cheng, Pierre J. Walker. (2021). Introducing students to research codes: A short course on solving partial differential equations in Python. Preprint submitted to Education for Chemical Engineers. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1016/j.ece.2021.01.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ece.2021.01.011)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Focus group discussions and post-course survey feedback from the 9-student remote course. One student commented that "The graphs gave a more visual approach than when it was taught". The authors attribute this to visual components facilitating understanding of abstract topics such as advanced mathematics.

> "Students broadly enjoyed the content of the course, with the visualisation aspect of the numerical solutions being the most well received."
