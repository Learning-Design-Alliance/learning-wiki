## Evidence

### Wu Hang-Zhou; Wu Qiu-Ting 2020

Wu Hang-Zhou; Wu Qiu-Ting. (2020). Impact of mind mapping on the critical thinking ability of clinical nursing students and teaching application. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060519893225

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre/post study of 64 nursing students using the CTDI-CV; paired t-test showed the total score moved from a neutral to a positive inclination, "significantly improved compared with that before intervention (t = −0.74, P = 0.045)". No effect size printed.

> "On comparing the critical thinking inclination before and after intervention, the inclination toward critical thinking of nursing students after the intervention was significantly improved compared with that before intervention (t = −0.74, P = 0.045)."
