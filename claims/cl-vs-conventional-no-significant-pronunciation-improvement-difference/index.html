Evidence¶

Hsuan-Yu Chen (Jack) & Jaya S. Goswami 2011¶

Hsuan-Yu Chen (Jack) & Jaya S. Goswami. (2011). Structuring Cooperative Learning in Teaching English Pronunciation. English Language Teaching, 4(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v4n3p26

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with pre-post two-treatment design: 19 ELLs taught pronunciation in a CL setting and 25 in a conventional setting. The independent t-test on improvement scores showed no significant difference; equivalence was not tested. Means were "M = 1.38, SD = 2.00" versus "M = 1.35, SD = 2.07".

"An independent t-test showedt(44) = 0.2404, p = .8101. That is, as shown in Table 4, the overall performance score of the CL (experimental) group (M = 1.38, SD = 2.00) was not statistically significantly different from that of the CV (control) group (M = 1.35, SD = 2.07)."
