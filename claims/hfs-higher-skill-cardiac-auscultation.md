## Evidence

### Al-Mugheed K 2025

Al-Mugheed K, Al-Oweidat I, Alzoubi MM, Alnaeem MM, Alshehri AS, Hendy A, Baghdadi NA, Farghaly Abdelaliem SM. (2025). Effectiveness of a high-fidelity simulation in terms of cardiac auscultation knowledge and skills among nursing students. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000044970

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison from Table 2 of the randomized experimental study: intervention skills rose from 11.4 ± 1.1 to 15.6 ± 1.3 while control rose from 6.3 ± 1.4 to 9.2 ± 2.7, post-test between-group P = .003. The article reports that "clear improvements in the intervention group's scores across all domain's knowledge, skills, and overall performance" were found.

> "The findings demonstrated clear improvements in the intervention group's scores across all domain's knowledge, skills, and overall performance when compared to the control group."
