Post-instruction interview study with nineteen students from modern physics courses. Interviews probed student perspectives on quantum interpretation in depth, revealing splits between intuition and perceived authority.

> "Only three of the seven students who preferred local, realist interpretations of quantum physics expressed confidence in the correctness of their perspectives, whereas four others differentiated between what made intuitive sense to them (Realist) and what they perceived as a correct response (Quantum)"
