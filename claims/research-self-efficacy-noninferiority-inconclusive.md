## Evidence

### Ommering 2021

Ommering, Belinda W. C., Van Blankenstein, Floris M., van Diepen, Merel, Gruis, Nelleke A., Kool, Ada, Dekker, Friedo W. (2021). The importance of motivation in selecting undergraduate medical students for extracurricular research programmes. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0260193

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same non-inferiority regression on third-year questionnaire data (n = 54 of 59 surveyed). Research self-efficacy adjusted "β = -.40, 95%CI = -.89 - .09"; the margin was inside the interval, and means were descriptively somewhat higher in the ≥7 group.

> "When looking at research self-efficacy, -0.5 is within the confidence interval (β = -.40, 95%CI = -.89 - .09). Therefore, we cannot conclude that the <7 group was not inferior to the ≥7 group."
