## Evidence

### U.S. Department of Education 2013

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2013). Pre-K Mathematics with DLM Early Childhood Express Math. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/381

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

PCER Consortium (2008) randomized trial, 270 children in 36 schools, assessed phonological processing with the Pre-CTOPPP Elision subtest. Intervention mean 9.67 (3.87) versus comparison 9.24 (4.35); effect size 0.04, improvement index +2, p > 0.05, characterized as an indeterminate effect.

> "The authors reported that differences between the Pre-K Mathematics with DLM Early Childhood Express Math group and the comparison group were not statistically significant, and the WWC confirmed this calculation."
