### Kapsalis 2026

Kapsalis, T. (2026). Gen-AI-tecture: using generative AI to support architectural students in design tasks. https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1598-6426

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r2`

Questionnaire and reflexive-discussion data from the two focus groups (n = 16). Inclusivity items (B1-B3) clustered at 3.8-4.1; B2 and B3 correlated with qualitative indices at ρ = .78 and ρ = .72 (p < .01). Students with limited AI experience "frequently reported not feeling disadvantaged".

> "The mean B-subscale score correlated strongly with qualitative indices for feeling the session was “approachable/welcoming” (ρ = .74, p < .01) and for “keeping up regardless of prior AI use” (ρ = .81, p < .001), evidencing clear convergent validity between questionnaire ratings and reﬂexive narratives."

### Kapsalis 2026 (2)

Kapsalis, T. (2026). Gen-AI-tecture: using generative AI to support architectural students in design tasks. https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1598-6426

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' convergent interpretation of the inclusivity findings. The discussion links this to UDL, noting that "the combination of masked editing, text prompts and reference imagery provided alternative routes into spatial thinking" for students with difficulties in freehand drawing, technical English or orthographic representation.

> "Together, these convergent quantitative and qualitative signals suggest that the bespoke GenAI workﬂow operated as a modest equalising mechanism, cultivating a culture of inclusion in the classroom."
