## Evidence

### Ebina Koki 2024

Ebina Koki, Abe Takashige, Yan Lingbo, Hotta Kiyohiko, Shichinohe Toshiaki, Higuchi Madoka, Iwahara Naoya, Hosaka Yukino, Harada Shigeru, Kikuchi Hiroshi, Miyata Haruka, Matsumoto Ryuji, Osawa Takahiro, Kurashima Yo, Watanabe Masahiko, Kon Masafumi, Murai Sachiyo, Komizunai Shunsuke, Tsujita Teppei, Sase Kazuya, Chen Xiaoshuai, Senoo Taku, Shinohara Nobuo, Konno Atsushi. (2024). A surgical instrument motion measurement system for skill evaluation in practical laparoscopic surgery training. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0305693

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire after the wet-lab experiment: 15 subjects rated interference of markers on five instruments on a 5-point scale (Table 3); averages included grasping forceps 1.60 and clip applier 1.20.

> "regarding the operational feel of the surgical instruments, the mean evaluation values of all surgical instruments were less than 2, indicating that the surgical instruments with markers developed in this study slightly affect the operation of the instruments."

### Ebina Koki 2024 (2)

Ebina Koki, Abe Takashige, Yan Lingbo, Hotta Kiyohiko, Shichinohe Toshiaki, Higuchi Madoka, Iwahara Naoya, Hosaka Yukino, Harada Shigeru, Kikuchi Hiroshi, Miyata Haruka, Matsumoto Ryuji, Osawa Takahiro, Kurashima Yo, Watanabe Masahiko, Kon Masafumi, Murai Sachiyo, Komizunai Shunsuke, Tsujita Teppei, Sase Kazuya, Chen Xiaoshuai, Senoo Taku, Shinohara Nobuo, Konno Atsushi. (2024). A surgical instrument motion measurement system for skill evaluation in practical laparoscopic surgery training. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0305693

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire during cadaver surgical training: responses from 31 subjects on six instruments (Table 5), e.g., suction instrument 1.29 and scissor forceps 2.06; questionnaires were also collected from 11 failed or excluded experiments.

> "the mean evaluation values of the operational feel of the surgical instruments range from 1.31 to 2.03, suggesting that the instruments with markers developed in this study slightly affect the surgeries."
