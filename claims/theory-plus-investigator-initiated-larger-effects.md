Narrative moderator analysis across the 9 heterogeneous RCTs; the review found theory-grounded, investigator-initiated interventions "more likely to show more significant between-group differences and larger effect sizes." No pooled test of this moderator was possible.

> "We found that ICT-based interventions that were grounded in behavior change theory and utilized an investigator-initiation strategy were more likely to show more significant between-group differences and larger effect sizes than those did not (See Table 1)."
