Qualitative analysis of the interview transition between phases. Immediately after the prompt, Sarah proposed that electrons are bound to molecules and that it takes certain energies to tear them away, bringing up the electron pull description and building a response to what "seems like … an easy question."

> "When the interviewer prompts her to think of "any explanation you find," the nature of Sarah's explanations changes."
