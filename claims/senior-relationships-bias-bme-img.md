## Evidence

### Woolf K 2016

Woolf K, Rich A, Viney R, Needleman S, Griffin A. (2016). Perceived causes of differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical training: a national qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013429

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis found seniors build trainee confidence through responsibility, feedback and reassurance; damaged confidence "could follow them into subsequent jobs" when seniors did not believe in trainees.

> "Relationships with senior doctors were perceived as crucial to learning. At best seniors gave trainees confidence by providing them with opportunities to take responsibility for patients, giving constructive feedback and reassuring about problems including examination failure."

### Woolf K 2016 (2)

Woolf K, Rich A, Viney R, Needleman S, Griffin A. (2016). Perceived causes of differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical training: a national qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013429

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the same qualitative dataset, the group contrast was reported as perceived asymmetry: BME UKGs and IMGs "less likely to report support from seniors in pressurised situations" than other trainees.

> "BME UKGs and IMGs in our sample were less likely to report support from seniors in pressurised situations and more likely to say seniors did not believe in them."
