`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-test System Usability Scale ratings completed after the KLM-FA essay activity; the mean SUS score was '82.0 (SD = 10.2)', placed in the 'Good to Excellent' band (SUS score from 71.4 to 85.5) of a benchmark dataset of nearly 1000 SUS surveys.

> "KLM-FA received a mean SUS score of 82.0 (SD = 10.2). According to a dataset of nearly 1000 SUS surveys [11], this means that students found KLM-FA as “Good to Excellent” (SUS score from 71.4 to 85.5) in terms of perceived usability."
