### Abdullahi Umar 2026

Abdullahi Umar, Shehu Abdullah, Garba Sanusi, Mohammed Bello Alhaji, Sada Kabiru Bello, Akogwu Harrison Sunday, Mande Kabiru Muhammad, Muhammad Jelani, Ahmad Sani, Umar Lawan, Bala Mujtaba. (2026). Bridging the know-do gap: a cross-sectional assessment of hypertension management amongst primary healthcare workers in Northwestern Nigeria. BMC Cardiovascular Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12872-026-05726-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire survey of 320 PHC workers in Gusau, Zamfara State, using a 12-item knowledge score with a 75% cutoff for good knowledge. The survey found "198 (61.9%) of respondents had good knowledge" while only 180 (56.3%) knew the ≥140/90 mmHg threshold and 191 (59.7%) could list major complications.

> "Overall, 198 (61.9%) of respondents had good knowledge of hypertension management. While knowledge of basic definitions of hypertension (271, 84.7%) and common risk factors like increased age (254, 79.4%), excessive salt intake (261, 81.6%), and overweight (234, 73.1%) were high, specific clinical knowledge gaps were observed."
