## Evidence

### Larose 2025

Larose, Marie‐Pier; Schuurmans, Isabel; Barker, Edward D.; Garcia Mondragon, Liliana; Tiemeier, Henning; Waldman, Irwin; Cecil, Charlotte. (2025). Associations between genetic predisposition to mental health problems and academic achievement: a developmental perspective using two population‐based cohorts. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpp.70043

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Supplementary hierarchical regression analysis in both cohorts examined incremental value of PGS-EXT, PGS-NDD, PGS-educational attainment, internalizing and thought-problem PGSs; the authors report "adding PGSs did not substantially improve the variance explained for academic achievement beyond the phenotypic covariates", while for aggression and conduct problems explained variance rose from 8.3% to 12.2% and 11.4% to 15.7%.

> "adding PGSs did not substantially improve the variance explained for academic achievement beyond the phenotypic covariates, with R 2 increasing modestly from 42.2% to 44.6% in Generation R and from 42.7% to 44.1% in ALSPAC"
