Evidence¶

Erdem Cahit 2026¶

Erdem Cahit, Kaya Metin, Polat Mustafa, Eğmir Eray. (2026). Thinking and feeling matter: a second-order meta-analysis of student characteristics across developmental domains and their effect on academic outcomes. BMC Psychology, 14, 919. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-05035-x

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analysis across 71 effect sizes coded by grade level (kindergarten k = 4, elementary k = 38, secondary k = 23, mixed k = 6). The kindergarten relationship was "high-level" (r = 0.37), with medium-level associations at elementary (r = 0.27) and secondary (r = 0.22) levels.

"The distribution of effect sizes in terms of school grade levels differed significantly (Qb = 8.00; p = 0.04). At the kindergarten level, there was a high-level relationship between students’ characteristics and academic achievement (r = 0.37)."
