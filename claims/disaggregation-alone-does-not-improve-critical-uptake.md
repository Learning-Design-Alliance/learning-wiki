## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Wholesale acceptance rates were nearly identical in the batch condition and the selection-only condition, suggesting interface disaggregation by itself did not change how students decide. [→ Yan Tao 2026](#yan-tao-2026)

## Evidence

### Yan Tao 2026

Yan Tao, Jennifer Meyer & René F. Kizilcec. (2026). Think Before You Accept: Can Written Justification Reduce Uncritical Uptake of AI Writing Suggestions? Preprint. Under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Discussion of the batch (n=62) versus selection-only (n=65) descriptive comparison: nearly one third of batch-condition students accepted all suggestions wholesale, and almost as many did so under per-suggestion selection. The authors conclude "the effective ingredient is demanding an articulation of reasoning, not merely increasing the number of clicks".

> "Almost as many did so when students were required to make a separate decision for each suggestion (32% vs. 31%)."
