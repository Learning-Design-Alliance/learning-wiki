## Evidence

### Dunn Kelly E. 2017

Dunn Kelly E., Yepez-Laubach Claudia, Nuzzo Paul A., Fingerhood Michael, Kelly Anne, Berman Suzan, Bigelow George E. (2017). Randomized controlled trial of a computerized opioid overdose education intervention. Drug and alcohol dependence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2016.12.003

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intent-to-treat two-factor (group, session) model on opioid knowledge for all randomized participants. The group main effect favored Computer over Pamphlet (p < 0.01); no effect size was printed. Opioid OD knowledge showed a session effect only.

> "The ITT analyses of mean percent correct revealed a significant main effect of session between the pre and post-test (F(1,73) = 9.73, p < 0.01) and group (F(2,73) = 5.21, p < 0.01) on opioid knowledge, driven by differences between the Pamphlet and Computer Groups (p = < 0.01)."
