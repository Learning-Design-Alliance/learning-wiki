## Evidence

### Glevitzky 2026

Glevitzky, L., Cyris, M., Sutor, S., Springer, C., Kahl, M., Mekhemar, M., Sälzer, S. & Graetz, C. (2026). Effect of massed versus distributed preclinical training on subgingival instrumentation skill across the preclinical-to-clinical transition. Scientific Reports 16, 23355. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-62235-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Adjusted ordinal logistic regression on the eight-item Likert self-assessment in the two-cohort study. The quote reports the adjusted ORs for learnability and perceived effectiveness; unadjusted post-DTP medians favoured nGDLR (F1 p = 0.024; F5 p = 0.016).

> "oGDLR students had a lower probability of a more favourable rating for F1 (OR 0.40; 95% CI 0.17–0.97; p = 0.042) and F5 (OR 0.48; 95% CI 0.23–1.00; p = 0.049; Table 5)"

### Glevitzky 2026 (2)

Glevitzky, L., Cyris, M., Sutor, S., Springer, C., Kahl, M., Mekhemar, M., Sälzer, S. & Graetz, C. (2026). Effect of massed versus distributed preclinical training on subgingival instrumentation skill across the preclinical-to-clinical transition. Scientific Reports 16, 23355. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-62235-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of self-assessment versus objective performance in the same cohort study. The quote reports the null F5 correlation and the significant F3 correlations (nGDLR ρ = −0.30; oGDLR ρ = −0.28).

> "no correlation was found between the subjective assessment of effectiveness (F5) and the objectively measured difference in biofilm removal in either cohort. In contrast, perceived time efficiency (F3) showed a significant correlation with the actual difference in treatment time (nGDLR: ρ = −0.30, p = 0.013; oGDLR: ρ = −0.28, p = 0.020)"
