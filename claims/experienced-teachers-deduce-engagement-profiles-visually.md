## Evidence

### Jarodzka 2020

Jarodzka, H., Skuballa, I., & Gruber, H. (2020). Eye-Tracking in Educational Practice: Investigating Visual Perception Underlying Teaching and Learning in the Classroom. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09565-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The review reports Seidel, Schnitzler, Kosel, Stürmer, and Holzberger's study, which showed video clips of authentic classroom lessons to more- versus less-experienced teachers, combining eye-tracking with diagnoses of pupils' behavior and elaborations. The review attributes the finding to the authors' own report.

> "The authors showed that experienced teachers can do so quite proficiently thereby also contributing to our knowledge on teachers'professional vision (see next section), while beginning teachers struggle with it."
