Demir, S., & Başol, G. (2014). Effectiveness of Computer-Assisted Mathematics Education (CAME) over Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis Study. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 14(5), 2026-2035. http://www.edam.com.tr/estp

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Moderator analysis grouping the included studies by the region of Turkey where they were conducted. The article reports significant heterogeneity by region and that the maximum and minimum effect sizes were obtained in the Aegean and Mediterranean region groups, respectively; I2 was 62.1759%. No standardized effect size is printed for this contrast.

> "the QB statistic, observed as a result of the homogeneity test of chi-square distribution calculated with a 0.05 confidence interval and five degrees of freedom(QB = 13.2191; p = .0162), reveals that the current study is statistically heterogeneous."
