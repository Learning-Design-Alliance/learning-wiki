# Psychometric reporting in character education evaluation research is inconsistent: reliability was reported for 62 of 95 scales and validity information was available for only 5 of 36 programs

`q2 i?` Reliability statistics were reported for 62 of 95 multiple-item scales (30 at .70 or above, 27 mixed across contexts, 5 below .70), no reliability statistics were reported for 33 scales, and validity was addressed for measures in only 5 of 36 programs. [→ Person 2009](#person-2009)

## Evidence

### Person 2009

Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/survey-of-outcomes-measurement-in-research-on-character-education-programs

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-case analysis of psychometric reporting across the 95 scaled measures in studies of the 36 programs. The review found reliability reported for "62 of the 95 multiple-item scales," with 30 at .70 or above, and validity information for measures in just 5 of 36 programs.

> "Researchers reported reliability statistics for 62 of the 95 multiple-item scales applied in the studies under review, with 30 of these exhibiting reliability of .70 or above, 27 exhibiting mixed reliability across contexts, and 5 exhibiting less than .70 reliability."
