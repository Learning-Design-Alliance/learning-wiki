Multivariable GEE moderator analysis in the 66-nurse quasi-experiment, comparing nursing levels and prior experience groups on POAS scores. The article also reports prior workplace violence training decreased scores by 1.58 points (p = 0.02). No standardized effect sizes are printed.

> "Nursing levels of N2 and N3 were scored as 2.18 (p = 0.02) and 5.09 (p < 0.001) points higher than N, respectively. In addition, the scores of the participants with the experience of violence decreased by 2.45 points compared to the participants without the experience of violence (p < 0.001)."
