Under-18 primary network meta-analysis results section. The authors conclude: "The primary analysis therefore does not support the earlier claim that every active or structured teaching node outperformed Control." Traditional's SMD versus Control was approximately null.

"Traditional was approximately null relative to Control (SMD = –0.01, 95% CI –0.95 to 0.93), while Linear had a negative point estimate with substantial uncertainty (SMD = –0.45, 95% CI –1.57 to 0.66)."
