### Success for All® 2017

Success for All®. (2017). WWC Intervention Report: Beginning Reading. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/672

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The WWC fluency synthesis rests on two quasi-experimental studies including 1,186 students in 45 schools. Madden et al. (1993) showed a substantively important positive effect on the GORT Passage subtest that was not statistically significant after clustering adjustment; Tracey et al. (2014) showed no significant difference.

> "Thus, for the reading fluency domain, one study reported a substantively important positive effect, and one study reported an indeterminate effect. This results in a rating of potentially positive effects, with a medium to large extent of evidence."
