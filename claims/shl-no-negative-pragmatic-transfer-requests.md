`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test analysis of 18 SHL students in a Spanish heritage language course, using written and oral discourse completion tasks with 10 scenarios each. The article reports the averages "was a 96.67%" (written) and "a 97.78%" (oral), both above the 70% proficiency threshold the researchers adopted from Ross (2008).
