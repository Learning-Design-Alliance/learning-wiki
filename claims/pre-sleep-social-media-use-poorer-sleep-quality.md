Mutually adjusted multivariable linear regression of the PSQI global score in a cross-sectional survey of 364 students; the model explained 26.3% of variance (F(13, 350) = 9.58, p < 0.001), with "the largest standardized association in the model by a wide margin (β = 0.392" for pre-sleep use.

> "One exposure was independently associated with poorer sleep, specifically the timing of use: duration of pre-sleep social media use (B = 1.594, 95% CI 1.081–2.106, p < 0.001). It had the largest standardized association in the model by a wide margin (β = 0.392"
