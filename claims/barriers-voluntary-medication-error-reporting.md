## Evidence

### Jung Sua 2025

Jung Sua, Park Jinkyung. (2025). Educational needs for medication safety competence among nurses by clinical ladder stage. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0319483

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding: a participant described error reports feeling like "a letter of apology" and "kind of a criminal offence," illustrating a culture discouraging voluntary reporting of errors including near-misses.

> "In Group 4, the voluntary reporting of medication errors is not encouraged because managers tend to focus on problem resolution and individual accountability rather than on providing constructive feedback."

### Jung Sua 2025 (2)

Jung Sua, Park Jinkyung. (2025). Educational needs for medication safety competence among nurses by clinical ladder stage. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0319483

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the Discussion, the authors summarize the reported barriers and state this finding corresponds with barriers to voluntary reporting identified in previous studies.

> "The participants reported that they were discouraged from voluntarily reporting medication errors because of cumbersome reporting procedures, fear of disclosing their identity, fear of being held accountable for patient deterioration, an atmosphere of blame, and coercion."
