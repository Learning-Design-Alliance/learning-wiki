## Evidence

### Peng Yizhong 2026

Peng Yizhong, Hu Juanjuan, Wang Minjie, Zhou Xing, Bing Kaijian, Li Song, Yu Haiyan. (2026). Evaluation of an integrated micro-skills training and clinical pathway model in surgical internship education. PeerJ 14:e21501. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21501

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Mini-CEX assessment after eight subspecialty rotations in the randomized trial. The physical examination domain showed "Cohen’s d = 1.59"; the article reports significant advantages in all seven domains with effect sizes 0.36–1.59.

> "in physical examination, the experimental group achieved a significantly better score (8.06 ± 0.73) compared with the control group’s (6.51 ± 1.15) (mean difference 1.55, 95% CI [1.21–1.89]; Cohen’s d = 1.59, 95% CI [1.20–1.98]; P < 0.001)"
