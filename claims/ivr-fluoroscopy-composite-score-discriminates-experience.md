## Evidence

### Sønderup M 2024

Sønderup M, Gustafsson A, Konge L, Jacobsen M E. (2024). Intraoperative fluoroscopy skills in distal radius fracture surgery: valid and reliable assessment on a novel immersive virtual reality simulator. Acta Orthopaedica 95: 477–484. https://doi.org/10.2340/17453674.2024.41345

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Contrasting-groups analysis of 11 novices and 9 experienced surgeons who completed two iVR simulator sessions with three repetitions each. The article reports estimated mean composite z-scores of "4.39 (CI 2.7–6.08)" for experienced surgeons versus "9.34 (CI 7.81–10.87)" for novices, P < 0.001, partial eta squared = 0.54.

> "Estimated mean composite z-scores were 4.39 (CI 2.7–6.08) for the experienced surgeons and 9.34 (CI 7.81–10.87) for the novices, P < 0.001, with a very large effect size, partial eta squared = 0.54 indicating a large difference in performance scores between groups."
