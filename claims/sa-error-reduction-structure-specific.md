## Evidence

### Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich 2023

Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Seifert Laura, Kohlhas Laura, Schmidt Mona Wanda, Ali Seher, Fan Carolyn, Köppinger Karl Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Nickel Felix. (2023). Video-based training of situation awareness enhances minimally invasive surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-10006-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analysis of individual error-score sub-items in the randomized trial; the artery-or-duct sub-item showed a similar pattern with p value of the group difference = 0.069, not reaching significance.

> "clip misplacement: SA: 55.9% no mistake, 32.4% minor mistakes, 11.8% major mistakes versus Control: 25.9% no mistakes, 40.7% minor mistakes, 33.3% major mistakes; p value of the group difference = 0.034"

### Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich 2023 (2)

Kowalewski Karl-Friedrich, Seifert Laura, Kohlhas Laura, Schmidt Mona Wanda, Ali Seher, Fan Carolyn, Köppinger Karl Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Nickel Felix. (2023). Video-based training of situation awareness enhances minimally invasive surgical performance: a randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-023-10006-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analysis of the remaining error-score sub-items in the same trial found no significant group differences for liver bed preparation, liver injury, or gallbladder perforation.

> "There were no significant differences among both groups in preparation of the liver bed, liver injury, or in perforation of the gallbladder."
