Systematic literature review following PRISMA guidelines, searching multiple databases for peer-reviewed studies published 2020-2024 on AI interventions in mathematics classrooms. The review's scope covered "14 studies involving 8,305 participants across diverse educational settings." No pooled effect size is printed in the available text.
