## Evidence

### Pustejovsky 2018

Pustejovsky, J. E. (2018). Procedural sensitivities of effect sizes for single-case designs with directly observed behavioral outcome measures. Psychological Methods. https://osf.io/hkzsm/

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Monte Carlo simulation results for PEM (row 5 of Figure 1), described as "very similar to those for NAP." Sensitivity to session length and recording procedure appears when behavioral characteristics fall within PEM's discriminating range.

> "Like NAP, PEM is mostly unaﬀected by the number of observations in either phase and its expected value is 50% when treatment has no eﬀect on the outcome. PEM is also sensitive to observation session length and recording procedure when the behavioral characteristics are in the range within which PEM can discriminate."
