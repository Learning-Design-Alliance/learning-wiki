Evidence¶

Kaplan 2015¶

Kaplan, A., Ozturk, M., & Ocal, M. F. (2015). Relieving of misconceptions of derivative concept with derive. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 1(1), 64-74. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105126

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-test descriptive analysis of the 12-question tool administered to both groups before instruction. Students solved using practical derivative rules without considering the domain, so the derivative did not exist; the article also reports difficulties with definition-based, geometric, physical, and tangent/normal interpretations.

"Since majority of the students does not take the d omain of the function into account, they made misconceptions. 58 % of experiment group students and 53 % of control group students made misconceptions in this question."
