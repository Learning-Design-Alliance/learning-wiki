## Evidence

### Madsen 2013

Madsen, McKagan, and Sayre (2013). Gender gap on concept inventories in physics: What is consistent, what is inconsistent, and what factors influence the gap? Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research 9, 020121. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.9.020121

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Kost et al.'s regression models on FMCE and BEMA posttest scores used predictive variables including pretest, combined math score, CLASS pretest, and semester taken. Matching 20 pairs of men and women on background variables eliminated the gap, though SAT covariates did not.

> "For posttest FMCE scores [17], the final regression model accounted for 7.5 points of the 10.7 point gender gap or 70% of the gap."
