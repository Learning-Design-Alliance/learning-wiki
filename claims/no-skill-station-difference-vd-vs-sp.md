### Steenstra Ivan 2026

Steenstra Ivan, Flanagan Octavia, Benni Jyoti M, Schreckenbach Teresa, Gayan Mondeep MS, Shrivastava Sandeep MS, Sonowal Kiran MS, Gogoi Amilee MD, Goswami Hrishikesh MS, Pathak Prasenjit MS, Kumar Bhawesh MS. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of the two observed psychomotor OSCE stations, performed on mannequins under two blinded observers with a preprepared checklist, found no significant intergroup differences: "2.27 for the VD group and 2.33 for the SP group (P=.74)" and P=.79 for immobilization.

> "For the log-rolling maneuver station (out of 4 marks), the mean scores were 2.27 for the VD group and 2.33 for the SP group (P=.74). Similarly, for the cervical spine immobilization station (out of 4 marks), the mean scores were 3.20 and 3.27 for the VD and SP groups, respectively (P=.79; Figures 3-4)."
