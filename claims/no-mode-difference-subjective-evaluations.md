## Evidence

### Lin Li and Shek Daniel T. L 2021

Lin Li and Shek Daniel T. L. (2021). Serving Children and Adolescents in Need during the COVID-19 Pandemic: Evaluation of Service-Learning Subjects with and without Face-to-Face Interaction. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18042114

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Three ANOVAs on subjective outcome evaluation data from 345 students. The article reports "The service mode did not make any difference in the students' evaluations of subject content, teacher's performance, and subject effectiveness." Equivalence was not formally tested; only non-significant differences were found.

> "The service mode did not make any difference in the students' evaluations of subject content (F(3343) = 2.41, p > 0.05, ηp2 = 0.01), teacher's performance (F(3343) = 0.02, p > 0.05, ηp2 = 0.00), and subject effectiveness (F(3343) = 1.72, p > 0.05, ηp2 = 0.01)."
