## Evidence

### Lydia G. Bender 2020

Lydia G. Bender, James T. Laverty. (2020). Influences on Faculty Uptake from a Faculty Learning Community. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2010.10384](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2010.10384)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results-section cross-case comparison of how material placement (lecture, recitation, studio) shaped each Fellow's participation in instruction and subsequent outlook. Charlie described student learning as "the total amount they learned was no more no less" and grades "being within the noise."

> "Ron and Molly were both able to be present for the instruction of their material, and therefore reﬂect on how the class felt and how students were responding to the new material. On the other hand, Charlie was not available for the instruction of his material and spends his time reﬂecting on student grades rather than his perception of how the material went."
