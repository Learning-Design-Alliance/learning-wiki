## Evidence

### Miller Nathan P 2016

Miller Nathan P, Amouzou Agbessi, Hazel Elizabeth, Legesse Hailemariam, Degefie Tedbabe, Tafesse Mengistu, Black Robert E, Bryce Jennifer. (2016). Assessment of the impact of quality improvement interventions on the quality of sick child care provided by Health Extension Workers in Ethiopia. Journal of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.06.020404

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression (two-level random intercept models with child and health post levels) from a cross-sectional survey of 103 health posts and 257 sick children in Jimma and West Hararghe Zones, Oromia, Ethiopia, 2012. The article reports "OR = 8.3, 95% CI 2.34–29.51" with P < 0.001; 68.2% of 233 children with PRCMM-attending HEWs were correctly treated vs 25.0% of 24.

> "Children who were managed by an HEW who had attended a PRCMM had about eight times the odds of being correctly managed (OR = 8.3, 95% CI 2.34–29.51), compared to children managed by an HEW who had not attended a PRCMM."
