## Evidence

### Maya Jesús; Maraver Jesús 2020

Maya Jesús; Maraver Jesús. (2020). Teaching-Learning Processes: Application of Educational Psychodrama in the University Setting. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17113922

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

End-of-course focus groups found role-playing positively valued, especially its variants; teachers valued planned role-playing for cognitive processing and transfer. As a negative aspect, they noted the considerable time needed, making it potentially incompatible with extensive curricula.

> "They also agreed that role-playing promotes an active teaching-learning process and increases the emotional component of learning. They also valued the opportunity that role-playing offers to practice the professional skills and competences that students will need to develop in their field."
