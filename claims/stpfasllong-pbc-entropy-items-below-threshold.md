### Mary Jane Brundage and Chandralekha Singh 2024 (2)

Mary Jane Brundage and Chandralekha Singh. (2024). Development and validation of a conceptual multiple-choice survey instrument to assess student understanding of introductory thermodynamics. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.07906](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.07906)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of the below-threshold items in the in-class administration. The article reports many advanced students "incorrectly thought that the entropy of a system always increases", consistent with Meltzer's findings.

> "Five of the seven questions below the threshold for the in-class administration (items 5, 8, 24, 48 and 64) are related to entropy on which advanced students’ average performance is comparable to introductory physics students’ average performance."
