## Evidence

### Schewior 2024

Schewior, L., & Lindner, M. A. (2024). Revisiting Picture Functions in Multimedia Testing: A Systematic Narrative Review and Taxonomy Extension. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09883-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of sixteen articles examining informational pictures' effects on performance. Six found increases, three no effects, five decreases, and two reported task type as a key moderator, reflecting "the strong heterogeneity in studies that investigate IPs."

> "Six articles found that items with IPs increased the performance compared to text-only"

### Schewior 2024 (2)

Schewior, L., & Lindner, M. A. (2024). Revisiting Picture Functions in Multimedia Testing: A Systematic Narrative Review and Taxonomy Extension. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09883-0

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review highlights that among the four picture function categories, only informational pictures showed performance-reducing effects, attributed partly to the category's broad definition fostering heterogeneity across studies.

> "Remarkably, informational pictures constitute the only category for which significant performance-reducing effects were reported"
