## Evidence

### Stella Y. Kim 2026

Stella Y. Kim, Ji Yae Bong, Daniel Maxwell, Beth Oyarzun. (2026). Development and validation of Generative Artificial Intelligence Readiness and Perception (GenAI-RP) Scale for faculty and students in higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10713-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

CFA of the challenge scale: the student version (N=450) showed good fit (CFI 0.91, RMSEA 0.066) with all factor loadings exceeding .40; the faculty version (N=128) showed poor fit (CFI 0.87, RMSEA 0.110, SRMR 0.091), and no modification-index suggestion was theoretically justifiable.

> "Unlike the student version, the faculty version of the challenge scale did not yield a satisfactory model fit, as shown in Table 7. The CFI and TLI values did not meet the 0.90 threshold, and both RMSEA and SRMR were higher than .08, indicating poor fit."
