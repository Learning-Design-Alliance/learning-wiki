The literature review of all 396 AIED 2025 proceedings papers recorded whether each reported computational costs and mentioned environmental issues, finding "sustainability issues were only mentioned in 57 papers" and 85 reported computational cost information. Model specifications, by contrast, were often named (e.g., GPT-4/4o, Claude, Gemini).
