Mann-Whitney U tests in the descriptive survey. Beliefs "significantly differ by education" for Progressivism (U=33282.00), Perennialism (U=34749.00) and Essentialism (U=24160.50), p<0.05, but not Existentialist Education (U=41953.50) or Reconstructivism (U=42237.00), p>0.05.

"significantly differ by education in the sub-dimensions of Progressivism (U=33282.00; p<0.05), Perennialism (U=34749.00; p<0.05) and Essentialism (U=24160.50; p<0.05) while educational philosophy beliefs do not differ significantly by education in the sub-dimensions of Existentialist Education (U=41953.50; p>0.05) and Reconstructivism (U=42237.00; p>0.05)."
