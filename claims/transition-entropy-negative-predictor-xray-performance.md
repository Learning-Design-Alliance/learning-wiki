## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students with better anomaly detection performance exhibit less random scanning paths, as transition entropy significantly negatively predicts normalized BFD scores. [→ Pingjing Yang 2025](#pingjing-yang-2025)

## Evidence

### Pingjing Yang 2025

Pingjing Yang, Jennifer Cromley, and Jana Diesner. (2025). More Expert-like Eye Gaze Movement Patterns are Related to Better X-ray Reading. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98414-3_28](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98414-3_28)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed linear model (REML) predicting normalized BFD scores from network metrics, with 3,425 observations nested within 165 participants. The fixed effect for transition entropy was "significant negative" (β = -0.065, p = 0.022); the β is an unstandardized coefficient, so no effect size is printed.

> "transition entropy (β = -0.065, SE = 0.028, z = -2.291, p = 0.022) are significant negative predictors of the normalized BFD score, suggesting that students with better performance tend to exhibit less random scanning paths when completing tasks."
