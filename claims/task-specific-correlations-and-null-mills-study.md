`q2 i3` Aghazadeh et al. found good correlations between dVSS scores and GEARS scores for most tasks (r = 0.582–0.784), with suturing and object-transfer tasks correlating best with bimanual dexterity (r = 0.716–0.763) and pick-and-place tasks with depth perception (r = 0.675–0.810); Mills et al. found no correlation between dVSS simulator scores and intraoperative GEARS scores. ## Evidence

### Schmidt MW 2021

Schmidt MW, Köppinger KF, Fan C, Kowalewski K-F, Schmidt LP, Vey J, Proctor T, Probst P, Bintintan VV, Müller-Stich B-P, Nickel F. (2021). Virtual reality simulation in robot-assisted surgery: meta-analysis of skill transfer and predictability of skill. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zraa066

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Cross-sectional study (Table 4) of 21 urological trainees and experts completing eight dVSS tasks before endopelvic fascia dissection assessment on human patients. The article reports suturing and object-transfer tasks "correlated best with bimanual dexterity (r = 0.716–0.763; P ≤ 0.001)" and pick-and-place tasks with depth perception (r = 0.675–0.810).

> "suturing exercises (suture sponge 3 and tubes) as well as exercises moving objects in space (ring and rail 2) correlated best with bimanual dexterity (r = 0.716–0.763; P ≤ 0.001), whereas pick and place tasks (peg board 1 and 2, match board 3) and ring walk 3 correlated best with depth perception (r = 0.675–0.810, P ≤ 0.003)"
