`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey item on learning sources (multiple choice) among 256 residents. The authors note the dominance of traditional classroom teaching and low online resource use indicates "a disconnection between current teaching models and clinical skill development needs".

> "Traditional classroom teaching was the most commonly used method (90.6%), followed by internet resources, which had the lowest uptake among Oral Surgery residents (9.7%) (P = 0.002)."
