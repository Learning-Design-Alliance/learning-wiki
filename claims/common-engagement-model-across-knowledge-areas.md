### Sahan Bulathwela 2020

Sahan Bulathwela, María Pérez-Ortiz, Aldo Lipani, Emine Yilmaz and John Shawe-T aylor. (2020). Predicting Engagement in Video Lectures. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.00592](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.00592)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Experiment E7 partitions lectures into STEM (Life Sciences, Physics, Technology, Mathematics) and Miscellaneous (Social Sciences, Humanities, Arts, Philosophy) and compares subject-agnostic versus subject-specific training (Table 5). The authors conclude "a common engagement model can be assumed throughout knowledge areas."

> "This is shown in the fact that both training with all knowledge ar- eas or dividing into two, the models obtain very similar test accuracy for each category (.737 vs .732 and .708 vs .704)."
