Evidence¶

Zakani 2019¶

Zakani, S., Kaupp, J., Turner, R. D., & Frank, B. (2019). Analyzing implicit science and math outcomes in engineering and technology programs. The Canadian Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.5206/cjsotl-rcacea.2019.1.7994

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Instructor survey scoring of scaffolding on physics exam questions from Engineering Technology 3 and 6 and Engineering 2 and 5, covering 10 university and 8 college questions. The article reports that "exam questions from Engineering Technology programs were often found to require higher scaffolding" and notes the conclusions drawn from this very small sample are very weak.

"Questions from Engineering programs mostly required the students to choose from or synthetize a range of approaches while questions from Technology programs often required the students to use a specific approach."
