In the post-survey of the school study, children were asked whether they would tell AMA a secret or ask its advice; "about half being willing to confide in it and half not", with 7th graders' responses ranging from distrust to openness.

"Children showed more caution towards AMA, with about half being willing to confide in it and half not (Figure 9)."
