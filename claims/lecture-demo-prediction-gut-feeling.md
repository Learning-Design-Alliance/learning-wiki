### Chandralekha Singh 2017 (2)

Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Improving Students' Understanding of Magnetism. Originally published 2007. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1701.01523)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

During prediction interviews with an electron beam and bar magnets, the concrete setup "prompted some students to use their gut feeling and slip back to the idea that force and field must be in the same direction" rather than helping them reason.

> "The concrete experimental setup involving electron beam (moving electrons) and bar magnets prompted some students to use their gut feeling and slip back to the idea that force and field must be in the same direction"
