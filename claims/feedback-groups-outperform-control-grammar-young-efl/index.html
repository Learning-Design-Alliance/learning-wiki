Masjedi 2019¶

Masjedi, H., & Tabatabaei, S. P. (2019). Analogy-based CF or Metalinguistic Feedback: Which One Is Better in EFL Classrooms? International Journal of Instruction, 12(3), 513-528. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12331a

Post hoc comparisons in the same quasi-experiment: Table 9 prints analogy-based vs control mean difference 2.933* (Sig. .000) and metalinguistic vs control 1.733* (Sig. .000), each significant at the 0.05 level, with 15 learners per group.
