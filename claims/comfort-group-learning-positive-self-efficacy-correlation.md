### Lenes A 2026

Lenes A, Klasen M, Bohorquez-Mendoza G, Gecht J, Sopka S, Vogt L. (2026). Psychological determinants of successful practical teaching: personality traits, self-efficacy, and subjective perception in a hands-on clinical skills course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09788-2

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis (H1) among third-semester medical students in a peer-led physical examination course; comfort measured with a custom 4-item scale, self-efficacy with the General Self-Efficacy Scale. The study reports a "positively associated with self-efficacy (r = .309; p < .001)".

> "As hypothesized, general comfort in group learning settings was positively associated with self-efficacy (r = .309; p < .001; n = 144)."
