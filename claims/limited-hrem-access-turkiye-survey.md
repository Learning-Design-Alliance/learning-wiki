## Evidence

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive results from the nationwide survey (n = 675); an additional one-third (33.3%) were uncertain about local HREM availability, reflecting institutional variability.

> "Structural barriers were significant: only 1.6% reported easy access to HREM, 59.4% found access difficult, and 5.6% considered it completely unavailable."

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026 (2)

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results by specialty; even the highest-knowledge group (gastroenterologists) reported limited access, supporting the authors' interpretation of infrastructural constraints.

> "Despite this expertise, only 9.1% reported easy access to HREM, highlighting systemic rather than educational barriers to optimal care."
