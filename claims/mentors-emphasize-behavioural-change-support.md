`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive comparison of Top 10 high-scoring experienced situations (question 1) between mentees (n = 103) and mentors (n = 20); the mentors' top item was Situation 10 on translating competencies into learning objectives (4.40 ± 0.13, Scale 4).

> "However, mentors also put emphasis on the support of behavioural change (Scale 4, Mentor stimulating conditions and perspectives for behavioural change), this was presented more often in the Top 10 of situations from mentors."
