# Learners report more positive attitudes toward externally regulated prompts than self-regulated prompts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Across the four role-of-instructional-prompts items, externally regulated prompts received significantly higher ratings (M = 4.18 vs. 3.73), F(1,47) = 5.40, p = .03, Cohen's f = .34. [→ Reisslein 2004](#reisslein-2004)
`q2 i?` On the single item about hints helping learning, externally regulated prompts scored significantly higher (M = 4.37 vs. 3.75), F(1,49) = 6.18, p = .02. [→ Reisslein 2004 (2)](#reisslein-2004-2)

## Evidence

### Reisslein 2004

Reisslein, J., Atkinson, R. K., & Reisslein, M. (2004). Exploring the Presentation and Format of Help in a Computer-Based Electrical Engineering Learning Environment. Arizona State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484994

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA of attitude survey data from the 51-participant experiment found a "significant overall main effect  across the four items relating to the presentation of prompts", favoring externally regulated prompts with Cohen's f = .34.

> "there was a significant overall main effect  across the four items relating to the presentation of prompts, ( M = 4.18 for externally regulated prompts and M = 3.73 for self-regulated prompts), F(1,47) = 5.40, MSE = .45, p = .03. Cohen's f statistic for these data yields an effect size of .34, which corresponds to medium to large effect."

### Reisslein 2004 (2)

Reisslein, J., Atkinson, R. K., & Reisslein, M. (2004). Exploring the Presentation and Format of Help in a Computer-Based Electrical Engineering Learning Environment. Arizona State University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484994

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A univariate ANOVA on the hint-helpfulness item in the same attitude data showed "significantly higher scores on the item“The instructional explanations (hint s) helped me to learn”" for externally regulated prompts.

> "revealed significantly higher scores on the item “The instructional explanations (hint s) helped me to learn” for the externally regulated prompts ( M = 4.37 for externally regulated prompts and 3.75 for self -regulated prompts, F(1,49) = 6.18, MSE = .79, p = .02)."
