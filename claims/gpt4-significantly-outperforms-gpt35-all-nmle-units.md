# GPT-4's accuracy was significantly higher than GPT-3.5's in all four NMLE units (2018-2024)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` GPT-4 outperformed GPT-3.5 comprehensively in all units and years of the Chinese NMLE, with statistically significant chi-square differences in each unit. [→ Diao Y 2025](#diao-y-2025)

## Evidence

### Diao Y 2025

Diao Y, Wu M, Xu J, Pan Y. (2025). Multiple Large Language Models’ Performance on the Chinese Medical Licensing Examination: Quantitative Comparative Study. JMIR Human Factors. https://doi.org/10.2196/77978

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square test on correct and incorrect answers across all 4200 NMLE questions (2018-2024), comparing GPT-4 against GPT-3.5 per unit. GPT-4's average accuracies (66.57%, 69.05%, 71.71%, 80.67%) exceeded GPT-3.5's (61.24%, 58.67%, 56.86%, 66.38%); "GPT-4’s accuracy was significantly higher than that of GPT-3.5 in all units."

> "The results showed that GPT-4’s accuracy was significantly higher than that of GPT-3.5 in all units (unit 1: χ²₁=12.4, df=1; P<.001, unit 2: χ²₁=15.7, df=1; P<.001, unit 3: χ²₁=19.0, df=1; P<.001, and unit 4: χ²₁=23.1, df=1; P<.001)"

### Diao Y 2025 (2)

Diao Y, Wu M, Xu J, Pan Y. (2025). Multiple Large Language Models’ Performance on the Chinese Medical Licensing Examination: Quantitative Comparative Study. JMIR Human Factors. https://doi.org/10.2196/77978

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Unit-level chi-square tests on each unit's questions; GPT-3.5's relative advantage in unit 4 was weaker (χ²₁=4.2, P=.04), while "GPT-4 had a clear and statistically significant edge" in unit 3 and unit 4.

> "GPT-4 had a clear and statistically significant edge in both unit 3 (χ²₁=27.6, df=1; P<.001) and unit 4 (χ²₁=19.9, df=1; P<.001)"
