## Evidence

### EL Hussein Mohamed Toufic 2026

EL Hussein Mohamed Toufic, Sheehan Dawson S., Hirst Sandra. (2026). Simulation‐Based Education to Support Nurses' Critical Thinking in Delirium Recognition: A Scoping Review. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70640

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

This repeated cross-sectional survey involved nursing staff on a single 24-bed general medical ward exposed to a three-element delirium prevention programme including monthly 1-h high-fidelity simulation with debrief, online modules and expert-facilitated case discussions. Correct responses "increased over time from 74.5% (T0) to 86.4% (T3), with a significant T0 → T3 improvement"; simulation participation was moderate at 48% versus 83% for online modules.
