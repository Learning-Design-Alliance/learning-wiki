## Evidence

### von Stumm 2021

von Stumm, S. & Wertz, J. (2021). Who's learning? Using within-family studies to understand personalized learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00082-4

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical proposal, not a reported result: the commentary states no prior empirical study has modeled how child–environment alignment influenced learning outcomes and proposes a sibling–parent match hypothesis that could be tested. The authors expect such matching effects to be smaller than the main effects of child ability.

> "We are not aware of a prior empirical study that modeled the extent to which the alignment between children's characteristics and their environments influenced their learning outcomes. A within-family study could, for example, test the hypothesis that the sibling, whose cognitive ability resembled that of their parents more closely, performed better in school"
