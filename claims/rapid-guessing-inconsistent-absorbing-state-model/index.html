Wise 2015¶

Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, Chicago. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Modeling+Student+Test-Taking+Motivation+in+the+Context+of+an+Adaptive+Achievement+Test

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of rapid-guessing patterns in mathematics MAP CAT data from 285,230 students in grades 2-12, spring 2012-2013. The article reports that "After the occurrence of the first rapid guess, 81% of the remaining responses were solution behaviors; after 6 rapid guesses, 67% were solution behaviors," contrary to absorbing state predictions.
