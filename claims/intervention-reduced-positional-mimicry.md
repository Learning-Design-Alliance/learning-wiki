`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Binomial generalized linear model using a stricter alignment measure — whether incorrect items were reproduced at the same rank position — in the 60-participant pre/post experiment. The article reports a significant interaction (b = -1.344, p = .010) and "OR = 0.26, 95% CI [0.09, 0.73]".

> "Following the intervention, the assistant was substantially less likely to mirror users’ incorrect rankings at the same positions compared to the control condition (OR = 0.26, 95% CI [0.09, 0.73])."
