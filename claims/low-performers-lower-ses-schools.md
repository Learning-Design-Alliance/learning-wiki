### Denis Pasco 2025

Denis Pasco, Erik Gustafsson, Lucas Burget, Thibault Asselborn, Pierre Dillenbourg, Consortium Lamue, Marie Mazerolle. (2025). Rapid in-class assessment of handwriting using a digital tablet: Multidimensional analysis of gender and socioeconomic status. Computers & Education: X Reality, 232, 105311. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105311

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney test comparing schools of 35 low-performing children (below a score of 18.61) with the remaining 1326 found a significant difference; a chi-squared test found no significant gender association (p = .132).

> "The test revealed a significant difference between the two groups, U = 18,207.5, p = .03, suggesting that children scoring below the threshold tend to attend schools characterized by a lower socioeconomic index, indicating a more disadvantaged status"
