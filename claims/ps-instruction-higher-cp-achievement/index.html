Evidence¶

Uysal 2014¶

Uysal, M. P. (2014). Improving First Computer Programming Experiences: The Case of Adapting a Web-Supported and Well-Structured Problem-Solving Method to a Traditional Course. Contemporary Educational Technology, 5(3), 198-217. https://doi.org/10.30935/cedtech/6125

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental posttest-only study with control group; 18 sections cluster-randomly assigned, 219 PS-group and 214 control students. Mann-Whitney test on the 20-item achievement posttest showed the experimental group mean (82,46) exceeded the control mean (79,83); "a statistically significant difference was found". No effect size printed.

"As a result, a statistically significant difference was found in the academic achievements at the α = .05 level of significance (z= -3,211; p< .05). It is possible to state that the learners instructed by the web-supported PS method displayed higher academic performances"
