## Evidence

### Carpentier Philippe 2022

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Sensitivity analysis testing an alternate reverse model in which fluid abilities were posited to mediate crystallized abilities (S7 and S8 Figs). In QLSCD the fluid factor "did not predict school achievement at any time in grade school"; in QNTS its predictions were marginal or limited to grade 4.

> "The case of QLSCD is clearer: although well predicted by crystallized abilities as the putative mediator, the fluid factor score did not predict school achievement at any time in grade school."
