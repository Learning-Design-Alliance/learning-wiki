Baracho Valéria da Silva, Chaves Maria Emília de Abreu, Lucas Thabata Coaglio. (2020). Application of the educational method of realistic simulation in the treatment of pressure injuries. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.3946.3357
