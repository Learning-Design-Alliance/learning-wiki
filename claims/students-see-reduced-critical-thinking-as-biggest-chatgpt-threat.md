### Singh 2023

Singh, H.; Tayarani-Najaran, M.-H.; Yaqoob, M. (2023). Exploring Computer Science Students' Perception of ChatGPT in Higher Education: A Descriptive and Correlation Study. Educ. Sci. 2023, 13, 924. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13090924

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive result from Question 8 of the questionnaire with 430 MSc computer science students on the biggest threats of ChatGPT; around 20% chose plagiarism and dishonesty, about a quarter over-dependency, 11% misinformation and 6% biased responses.

> "the highest number of students chose the second option, where 39% of the students believe that the largest threat comes from reduced critical thinking and originality."
