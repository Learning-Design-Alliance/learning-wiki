### Michael J. Baker 2015

Michael J. Baker. (2015). The Negotiation of Meaning in Epistemic Situations. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0050-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Finding reported from the Lund et al. (2007) study of semiotically structured argumentation tools in CSCL, comparing task sequencing within a fixed duration of around 1 hour. The article states the free-debate-then-diagram order was "more beneficial for argumentation-based collaborative learning"; no effect size is printed.

> "it is more beneficial for argumentation-based collaborative learning to first ask students to debate 'freely'(with a CHA T), then to re-analyse their own debate in the form of a diagram, than to allow students to discuss and co-create argument diagrams concurrently (Lund et al. 2007)."
