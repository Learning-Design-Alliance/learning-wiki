## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Two weeks after the Python module concluded, learners with no programming experience scored significantly lower than those with Python experience on one LLM practice quiz, the only significant post-intervention group difference. [→ Ian McCulloh 2025](#ian-mcculloh-2025)

## Evidence

### Ian McCulloh 2025

Ian McCulloh, Pedro Rodriguez, Srivaths Kumar, Manu Gupta, Viplove Raj Sharma, Benjamin Johnson, Anthony N. Johnson. (2025). Generative AI in Computer Science Education: Accelerating Python Learning with ChatGPT. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.20329](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.20329)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA follow-up on the Week 6 LLM quiz. The group effect was significant, F(2, 116) = 3.97, p = .021, with no-experience learners "scoring significantly lower than those with Python experience (p = .017)"; the article calls it the only significant post-intervention difference.

> "A statistically significant group difference also appeared on a practice quiz on large language models administered during Week six, F(2, 116) = 3.97, p = .021, with learners who had no programming experience scoring significantly lower than those with Python experience (p = .017)"
