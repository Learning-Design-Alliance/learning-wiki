## Evidence

### Kelc Robi 2020

Kelc Robi, Vogrin Matjaz, Kelc Janja. (2020). Cognitive training for the prevention of skill decay in temporarily non-performing orthopedic surgeons. Acta Orthopaedica 91(5). https://doi.org/10.1080/17453674.2020.1771520

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article asserts, citing Sugihara et al. (2018), that the "cognitive and integrational phases" of surgical skill acquisition are the most "decay-susceptible" stages, motivating mental practice as a retaining tool for novices. The article prints no effect size for this assertion.

> "Novice surgeons and surgical trainees usually find themselves in cognitive and integrational phases of learning, thus being especially vulnerable to the decay of their skills, which can happen after a short retention interval (Sugihara et al. 2018)"
