## Evidence

### Saterbak Ann 2018

Saterbak Ann, Moturu Anoosha, Volz Tracy. (2018). Using a Teaching Intervention and Calibrated Peer Review™ Diagnostics to Improve Visual Communication Skills. Annals of Biomedical Engineering. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-017-1946-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-squared comparison of peer-vs-instructor (PvI) contingency tables for Statement 1 across standard (2007–2009, 105 posters) and intervention (2010–2012, 103 posters) conditions; "agreed with the instructor increased from 40% to 55%" and the P value for Statement 1 was 0.000084 with convergence. No effect size printed.

> "The number and percentages of students who agreed with the instructor increased from 40% to 55% (169 of 309 times). After the intervention, 10% of the peers’ ratings for Statement 1 were more critical than the instructor’s."
