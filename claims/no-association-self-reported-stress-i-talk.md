## Evidence

### Westphal 2024

Westphal, A., Richter, E., Lazarides, R., & Huang, Y. (2024). More I-talk in student teachers’ written reflections indicates higher stress during VR teaching. Computers & Education, 212, 104987. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.104987

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cross-sectional regressions (Table 2, Models 1a-b) found no significant association between self-reported stress and either subjective (β = 0.21, p = .100) or objective (β = 0.20, p = .160) pronoun use, indicating that "self-reported stress... was not statistically significantly associated with the use of subjective and objective first-person singular pronouns".

> "Our results showed that self-reported stress experienced during the first VR teaching session was not statistically significantly associated with the use of subjective and objective first-person singular pronouns (Table 2)."
