Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026¶

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Sensitivity analysis of the primary three-level model using study-clustered CR2 robust variance estimation; the authors state the pooled result was insensitive to plausible sampling-dependence assumptions, whereas nominal significance for guidance and feedback timing was not.

"Cluster-robust CR2 inference yielded the same point estimate, g = 0.653, robust SE = 0.065, 95% CI [0.519, 0.787], p <.001."

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du 2026 (2)¶

Yijia Yuan & Yiran Du. (2026). Technology-Supported Interventions and Learners' Metacognition: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10227-3

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Additional robustness checks in the same meta-analysis: baseline-adjusted subset analysis (43 effects, 16 studies), exclusion of serious-risk studies (g = 0.624, 95% CI [0.492, 0.756]), and direct-only refit (g = 0.724); all confidence intervals excluded zero.

"Leave-one-study-out estimates ranged from g = 0.625 to 0.675, with the confidence interval excluding zero in every iteration."
