# Strength is strongly positively correlated with second-pass reading time, while betweenness, harmonic centrality and local clustering coefficient are not related to duration measures

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Strength shows a strong positive correlation with second-pass reading time (r = 0.645). [→ Catrysse 2025](#catrysse-2025)
`q3 i?` Betweenness, harmonic centrality and local clustering coefficient are not (or only weakly) correlated with first-pass and second-pass duration measures, indicating they capture integration aspects duration measures cannot. [→ Catrysse 2025](#catrysse-2025)

## Evidence

### Catrysse 2025

Catrysse, L., van Daal, T., Jarodzka, H., Kaakinen, J. K., Donche, V., & Gijbels, D. (2025). How Eye Read: A Social Network Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10000-y

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of eye-tracking data from 31 higher education freshmen reading three expository texts; Table 3 reports strength–second-pass r = 0.645, a "strong positive correlation between strength and second-pass reading time", while betweenness correlations were near zero (e.g., 0.022).

> "There is a strong positive correlation between strength and second-pass reading time. Betweenness is not correlated with first-pass forward reading time, first-pass rereading time, or second-pass reading time."
