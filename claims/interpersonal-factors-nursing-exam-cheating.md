### Mousavi SK 2025

Mousavi SK, Kamali M. (2025). Explaining reasons for cheating in exams among undergraduate nursing students: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07693-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

From the same qualitative interview study, participants described family pressures such as unreasonably high expectations. A participant reported, "some families' expectations of students are too high and unreasonable. This issue causes students to bear much pressure and have to behave like this" (P 13).

> "an important factor contributing to the tendency of nursing students to cheat in exams is their surrounding environment, which is categorized as interpersonal factors and comprises three subclasses: family, peers, and society."
