## Evidence

### Lahza 2025

Lahza, H. F., Demartini, G., Noroozi, O., Gašević, D., Sadiq, S., & Khosravi, H. (2025). Enhancing peer feedback provision through user interface scaffolding: A comparative examination of scripting and self-monitoring techniques. Computers & Education, 230, 105260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105260

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A randomised controlled field experiment compared a scripting interface with a self-monitoring interface for peer feedback among 149 higher education students. The study reports that "Students in the scripted condition provided longer and superior-quality feedback" than the self-monitoring condition.

> "Students in the scripted condition provided longer and superior-quality feedback and also exhibited a more critical perspective in their evaluations compared to the self-monitoring condition."
