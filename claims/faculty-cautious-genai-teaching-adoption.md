### Karen D. Wang 2024

Karen D. Wang, Zhangyang Wu, L'Nard Tufts II, Carl Wieman, Shima Salehi, Nick Haber. (2024). Scaffold or Crutch? Examining College Students' Use and Views of Generative AI Tools for STEM Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.02653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.02653)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Faculty survey (November 2023, recruited via APS listserv and Slack) on genAI use for teaching tasks (Fig. 3). The article reports "The majority of faculty members reported not using genAI for any of the teaching tasks listed", with 39% preparing teaching materials and 36% assessment materials, and lower adoption for student-facing tasks.

> "The majority of faculty members reported not using genAI for any of the teaching tasks listed, though a moderate number have begun to explore using such tools for preparing teaching materials (n = 11, 39%) and assessment materials (n = 10, 36%)."
