# Students’ drawings of an IPLS studio community of practice are dominated by practice-related elements, with goals fewer and sparsely connected

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### W. Brian Lane 2025

W. Brian Lane, Charlotte Dries, Gabriella Khazal, and Tiffany Snow. (2025). Network analysis of students’ drawn representations of an introductory lab. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.02681](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.02681)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Whole-network analysis of N = 74 drawings (71 cataloged elements) from a two-semester studio IPLS sequence. Drawings averaged 18.7(8.1) elements, with “an average of 11 .9(6.1) practice-related elements” and 47 practice elements total.

> "Most nodes in the center, and throughout the network, are categorized as practice-related, with the student drawings depicting an average of 11 .9(6.1) practice-related elements and a total of 47 practice-related elements in the network."

### W. Brian Lane 2025 (2)

W. Brian Lane, Charlotte Dries, Gabriella Khazal, and Tiffany Snow. (2025). Network analysis of students’ drawn representations of an introductory lab. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.02681](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.02681)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same whole-network analysis: goal elements averaged “2.3(2.0) goal-related elements” per drawing with 13 goal elements total, and drawings “tended to depict fewer goals, without a strong sense of common goals across the network.”

> "Goal-related elements are fewer in number and more sparsely connected throughout the network. The student drawings depicted an average of 2.3(2.0) goal-related elements and a total of 13 goal-related elements in the network. Students’ drawings tended to depict fewer goals, without a strong sense of common goals across the network."
