Bell 1921¶

Bell, J. C. (1921). Group tests of intelligence: An annotated list. Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(2), 103–108. doi:10.1037/h0073682

q1 · i? · review · r2

Annotated-list entry for the Myers Mental Measure by Caroline E. and Garry C. Myers. The correlation is hedged as "said to show", i.e. attributed rather than independently verified by the article's author.

"An outgrowth of experience with the Army Mental Tests. Requires only 20 minutes to give, and is said to show a correlation of .80 with the Standard Revision of the Binet Scale."
