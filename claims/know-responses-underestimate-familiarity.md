# Know responses in the remember/know procedure underestimate familiarity; an independence correction is required

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Because remember and know responses are mutually exclusive, raw know response proportions underestimate the probability that an item is familiar; the independence remember/know method corrects for this. [→ Yonelinas 2002](#yonelinas-2002)

## Evidence

### Yonelinas 2002

Andrew P. Yonelinas. (2002). The Nature of Recollection and Familiarity: A Review of 30 Years of Research. Journal of Memory and Language. https://doi.org/10.1006/jmla.2002.2864

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's methodological analysis of the remember/know procedure reports that because subjects respond know only when an item is "familiar and not recollected," know responses "will tend to underestimate the probability that an item is familiar"; the independence remember/know method corrects for this.

> "Thus the proportion of know responses will tend to underestimate the probability that an item is familiar."
