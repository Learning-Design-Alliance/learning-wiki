# Where identical elements exist in two situations, training in one produces positive transfer to the other

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Training in canceling 3's and 5's separately improved cancellation of groups containing both 3 and 5, with a trained-control difference of 12.2 seconds attributed to transfer of identical elements. [→ Poffenberger 1915](#poffenberger-1915)

## Evidence

### Poffenberger 1915

Poffenberger, A. T., Jr. (1915). The influence of improvement in one simple mental process upon other related processes. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 6(8), 459-474. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075269

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled training experiment on cancellation: trained group averaged 23.7 seconds gain versus 11.5 for controls, a 12.2-second difference about 2.5 times its probable error, with chances of being real about 900 in 1000, roughly 8 per cent of initial performance.

> "This difference of 12.2 seconds in favor of the trained group may be attributed to the transfer of the effect of the training. The reliability of this difference expressed in terms of the P. E. of the difference is about 5.0."
