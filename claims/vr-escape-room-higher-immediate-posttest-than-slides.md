# MetaRoundWorm participants achieved significantly higher immediate post-test scores than interactive-slides participants

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The MetaRoundWorm group scored significantly higher on the post-test than the Interactive Slides group (r = .35). [→ Xuanru Cheng 2025](#xuanru-cheng-2025)

## Evidence

### Xuanru Cheng 2025

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects experiment with 48 university students randomly assigned to MetaRoundWorm VR or interactive slides; Mann-Whitney U comparison of post-test scores found the VR group "significantly higher post-test scores than the Interactive Slides group (U = 138.5, p < .05, r = .35)".

> "Notably, the MetaRoundWorm group achieved significantly higher post-test scores than the Interactive Slides group (U = 138.5, p < .05, r = .35), indicating a more robust immediate learning effect."
