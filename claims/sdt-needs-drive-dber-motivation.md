`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The article applies Self-Determination Theory to interpret self-reactiveness in interview data, defining competence as feeling proficient in DBER, relatedness as connection to the DBER community, and autonomy as sense of choice in DBER tasks.

> "SDT suggests that three psychological needs: competence, relatedness and autonomy have to be satisﬁed to have the most self-determined form of motivation [38]."
