## Evidence

### Cizmic Amila 2025

Cizmic Amila, Reichert Paulina, Häberle Frida, Preukschas Anas A., Pianka Frank, Mehrabi Arianeb, Nießen Anna, Müller-Stich Beat P., Hackert Thilo, Grotelüschen Rainer, Nickel Felix. (2025). Effects of training in pairs versus training alone on reaching proficiency in minimally invasive Roux-en-Y-gastric bypass on a virtual reality trainer in medical students: a randomized-controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-11701-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Two-arm randomized-controlled trial of 60 medical students randomized to pair training (n = 30, fifteen pairs) or solo training (n = 30); primary outcome was repetitions until BOSATS proficiency (≥105/110). The article reports "significantly fewer repetitions to reach MIS RYGB proficiency than the control group (p = 0.002)"; no effect size printed.

> "The intervention group needed significantly fewer repetitions to reach MIS RYGB proficiency than the control group (p = 0.002). Most participants in the intervention group reached proficiency after the fifth repetition, and none needed the eighth repetition."
