## Evidence

### Kohnle A 2013

Kohnle A, Bozhinova I, Browne D, Everitt M, Fomins A, Kok P, Kulaitis G, Prokopas M, Raine D and Swinbank E. (2013). A new introductory quantum mechanics curriculum. [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/35/1/015001](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/35/1/015001)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation sessions in which students "freely explored the simulations and worked through the activities while thinking aloud", then answered survey questions and suggested improvements. The authors report that observation outcomes and analysis of homework and workshop responses "led to substantial revisions in both simulations and activities"; further evaluation details will be reported elsewhere.

> "We optimized simulations and activities using a total of 38 hours of observation sessions with 17 student volunteers from the appropriate introductory level at the University of St Andrews."
