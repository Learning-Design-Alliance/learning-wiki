Descriptive analysis of FLAS dimension scores for 432 nursing students after the flipped classroom intervention. The article reports an "average total of 4.2 (0.6) points" with D1 at 4.2, D2 at 4.5 and D3 at 3.8; no effect size is printed for these descriptive means.

> "FLAS Scale scores show an average total of 4.2 (0.6) points, with similar values across the three dimensions: Dimension 1 (D1): 4.2 (0.6); Dimension 2 (D2): 4.5 (0.6); and Dimension 3 (D3): 3.8 (0.9)"
