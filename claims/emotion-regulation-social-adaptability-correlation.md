### Feng Wanyun 2026

Feng Wanyun. (2026). Effects of different pedagogical swimming teaching modes on teenagers' emotional adjustment ability and social adaptability. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1829856

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of post-intervention scores in the RCT (n = 120). "the total scores of emotion regulation ability and social adaptability of adolescents were significantly strongly positive correlated (r > 0.7, P < 0.001)" across all three groups, shown in Figure 3 scatter plots.

> "the total scores of emotion regulation ability and social adaptability of adolescents were significantly strongly positive correlated (r > 0.7, P < 0.001), and the slope of regression line in cooperative learning group was significantly higher than that in comprehensive teaching group and traditional instruction group"
