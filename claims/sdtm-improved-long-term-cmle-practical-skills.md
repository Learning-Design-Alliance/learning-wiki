## Evidence

### Yang Liu 2022

Yang Liu, Li Wen, Zou Jian, An Junnan, Zeng Bin, Zheng Yitao, Yang Jiming, Ren Jia. (2022). The application of the spot the difference teaching method in clinical skills training for residents. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03612-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Long-term retention test: CMLE practical skill scores (max 100) were 83.43 ± 5.424 (SDTM) vs 79.28 ± 9.714 (control), T = 2.103, P = 0.039, d = 0.47; pass rates 95.2% vs 87.2%, χ2 = 1.663, P = 0.197, d = 0.29.

> "the results revealed that although the passing rate between the two groups showed no significant difference (P = 0.197), the item scores in the SDTM group were higher than those in the control group (P < 0.005)."
