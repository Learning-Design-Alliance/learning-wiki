# Simulator-based training was more effective than traditional training at improving obstetric ultrasound performance, with large ANCOVA effect sizes

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` ANCOVA showed a significant main effect of training method on performance in the assessment on the simulator (F = 16.1, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.459) and on pregnant volunteers (F = 7.1, p = 0.015, partial η² = 0.273), favoring simulator-based training. ### Zhou Jinguang 2026

Evaluating the effectiveness of high-fidelity simulation-based training on clinical skill transfer in obstetric ultrasound residents: a prospective randomized controlled trial. `q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA (Table 3) in the RCT comparing training methods with pre-test score as covariate; both main effects exceeded Cohen's threshold, with "partial η² values greater than 0.14 indicate large effect sizes". Improvement slopes were steeper in the simulator group.

> "A significant main effect of training method was observed for participants’ performance in the assessment on the simulator (F = 16.1, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.459), as well as in the assessment on pregnant volunteers (F = 7.1, p = 0.015, partial η² = 0.273)."
