## Evidence

### Nicholas Feroce 2025

Nicholas Feroce, Jenny Liu, Rajendra Chattergoon. (2025). Exploring the impact of a CALL tool for emergent bilinguals. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10462-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analyses with a CALL-use-by-grade interaction term and pairwise contrasts by grade (Table 4). Significant oral-subdomain effects appeared in grades 2, 4, and 5; grade 3 approached significance (p = 0.087). Positive but non-significant coefficients appeared in other grades.

> "For the ELPAC oral subdomain, significant positive effects of using the CALL program were seen for grades 2, 4, and 5, with the largest effect for grade 2, and a positive effect approaching significance for grade 3 (p = 0.087)."
