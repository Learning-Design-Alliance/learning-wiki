## Evidence

### Rees Philippa 2025

Rees Philippa, Dronavalli Mithilesh, Carter Ben, Dickson Michelle, Green Charles, Lawler Kate, Lee Evelyn, Uebel Hannah, Gale Chris, Oei Ju Lee. (2025). School Performance of Preterm-Born Children After Intraventricular Hemorrhage. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.47584

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted domain-specific linear regression at age 8 to 9 years in the cohort found high-grade IVH children "performed particularly poorly in numeracy compared with very preterm controls (AMD, −0.49; 95% CI, −0.70 to −0.28)". No standardized effect size was printed.

> "Children with high-grade IVH performed particularly poorly in numeracy compared with very preterm controls (AMD, −0.49; 95% CI, −0.70 to −0.28)"

### Rees Philippa 2025 (2)

Rees Philippa, Dronavalli Mithilesh, Carter Ben, Dickson Michelle, Green Charles, Lawler Kate, Lee Evelyn, Uebel Hannah, Gale Chris, Oei Ju Lee. (2025). School Performance of Preterm-Born Children After Intraventricular Hemorrhage. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.47584

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

At age 12 to 13 years the cohort's adjusted domain differences persisted, "especially numeracy (AMD, −0.65; 95% CI, −1.12 to −0.19) and spelling (AMD, −0.66; 95% CI, −1.09 to −0.23)". No standardized effect size was printed.

> "in total attainment scores (AMD, −0.61; 95% CI, −1.05 to −0.17) and domain-specific scores, especially numeracy (AMD, −0.65; 95% CI, −1.12 to −0.19) and spelling (AMD, −0.66; 95% CI, −1.09 to −0.23)"
