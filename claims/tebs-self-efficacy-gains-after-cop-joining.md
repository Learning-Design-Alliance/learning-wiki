### Talafian 2026

Talafian, H., Mahmood, M., Kuo, E., Lundsgaard, M., & Stelzer, T. (2026). Teacher Leader Identity Development in a Physics Teaching Community of Practice: A Narrative Case Study. University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.08924](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.08924)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Retrospective administration of the validated Teachers' Self-Efficacy Beliefs survey in 2023, rated before and after joining the program. Table 3 prints means rising from 3 to 3.8 (communication/clarification), 2.7 to 3.4 (accommodating individual differences), and 2.2 to 3.2 (higher-order thinking), while motivation stayed at 3.6 and managing learning routines at 3.3. Single-participant survey data; no effect size printed.

> "Using a four-point scale (1 = very weak beliefs; 4 = very strong beliefs), Kayla demonstrated gains in self-efficacy in communication and clarification practices, accommodating individual differences, and fostering higher-order thinking skills."
