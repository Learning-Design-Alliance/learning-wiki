Evidence¶

Yinqi Huang 2026¶

Yinqi Huang, Yilin Cai, Mengyao Li, Yue Chen & Robert C. Wilson. (2026). Human learning dynamics during teleoperation of surgical robots. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00442-6

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Same LQG transfer simulation: contact metrics showed significant Complexity main effects (e.g., collisions Level F(2, 18) = 23.190, p < 0.001) with non-significant Ratio effects, per the article "largely insensitive to the mapping ratio in this LQG setting".

"In contrast, contact-related metrics showed a different pattern: collisions and collision duration both showed signiﬁcant main effects of Complexity, whereas their Ratio effects and interactions were not signiﬁcant, suggesting that contact dynamics during transfer were largely insensitive to the mapping ratio in this LQG setting."

Related Claims¶

  • Human trainees show no clear asymmetric transfer of motion scaling ratio, and scaling ratio effects are small relative to complexity effects — related
  • Rotational path length does not reliably decrease over teleoperation training, suggesting continued exploration of joint-level strategies — related
