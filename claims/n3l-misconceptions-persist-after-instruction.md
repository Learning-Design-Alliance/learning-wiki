### Lei Bao and Joseph C. Fritchman 2021

Lei Bao and Joseph C. Fritchman. (2021). Development of a Conceptual Framework for Knowledge Integration in Learning Newton's Third Law. The Ohio State University, Department of Physics. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2104.05944](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2104.05944)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Narrative review of physics education research on N3L difficulties. The article reports these misconceptions "seem to persist even after instruction" and that naive beliefs appear even among trained educators, citing prior studies.

> "Common examples of misconceptions include the application of a dominance principle where the faster, more massive, or acting object applies a greater force than the other object, difficulty identifying forces of a N3L pair, thinking action and reaction forces must balance each other, thinking forces are properties of objects rather than results of an interaction, and assigning causal relationships to objects and forces."
