## Evidence

### Hong Yucai 2017

Hong Yucai, Cai Xiujun. (2017). Effect of team training on efficiency of trauma care in a Chinese hospital. The Journal of International Medical Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/0300060517717401

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Raw-cohort rank-sum comparison of 51 team versus 93 control grade I trauma patients. Median time to TXA administration was 31 minutes for the trained team versus 90 minutes for traditional practice, with P < 0.01 in the Results text.

> "tranexamic acid (TXA) administration (31 [13–65] vs. 90 [65–200] min; P < 0.01)"
