## Evidence

### Hart 2015

Hart, Sara A.; Logan, Jessica A.R.; Thompson, Lee; Kovas, Yulia; McLoughlin, Gráinne; & Petrill, Stephen A. (2015). A latent profile analysis of math achievement, numerosity, and math anxiety in twins. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000045

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three small LPA classes (classes 1, 2, 3; n = 18, 17, 29) each showed a different combination of numerosity deficit and achievement level, indicating the numerosity-achievement link is not uniform across struggling children.

> "Some children showed low achievement with low non-symbolic number approximation only, others showed low achievement with low symbolic number approximation only, and finally some children showed average achievement with low non-symbolic number approximation."
