## Evidence

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlational analysis (n = 57) between end-of-semester TOSLS scores and course final grade; the article reports r = 0.526, P < 0.001, with low-to-moderate positive correlations for nearly all individual skills.

> "Total TOSLS scores were moderately positively correlated with final grades in the course (r = 0.526; P < 0.001). Analysis of individual TOSLS skill questions answered correctly, and final grades in the course revealed low to moderate positive relationships for all skill categories and final grades (P < 0.05)."

### Brendel Elaina B. K. 2025 (2)

Brendel Elaina B. K., Alzubi Ala, Rai Shrujan, Mariathasan Christine, Snook Laelie A., Monk Jennifer M. (2025). Using a flipped classroom teaching and learning approach to promote scientific literacy skill development and retention. FEBS Open Bio. https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.13938

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Null Pearson correlation for TOSLS Skill #3 with final grade in the same cohort; the article prints P = 0.340 and no effect size, and highlights this as an area for further skill development relevant to combating misinformation.

> "The one practical SL skill that showed no relationship with the final grade in the course was the ability to evaluate the use and misuse of scientific information (P = 0.340)."
