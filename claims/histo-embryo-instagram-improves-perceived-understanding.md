`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported survey data from 125 medical students responding to a structured online questionnaire about the Histo.Embryo Instagram account. The authors note this reflects perceived usefulness rather than objective learning outcomes, and the survey instrument was not formally validated.

> "A total of 98% (122/125) of respondents reported that the account improved their understanding of histology, embryology, and cytogenetics to varying degrees."
