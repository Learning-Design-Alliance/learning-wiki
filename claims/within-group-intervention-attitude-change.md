## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired t-tests within the intervention group showed significant increases in total attitude (P = 0.001), subscale 1 (P = 0.034) and subscale 2 (P = 0.005). [→ Mohseni Fatemeh 2024](#mohseni-fatemeh-2024)

## Evidence

### Mohseni Fatemeh 2024

Mohseni Fatemeh, Mohammadi Aeen, Khabaz Mafinejad Mahboobeh, Khajavirad Nasim, Basiri Kamal, Gruppen Larry D. (2024). Teaching conflict management to medical students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06514-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group paired t-test comparison (intervention group, n = 46 completers) across pre- and post-test; the article reports "no significant change in the mean score for subscale 3". No effect size printed.

> "the intervention group showed that the educational intervention significantly increased the mean total attitude score, as well as the mean scores for subscales 1 and 2 (P = 0.001, P = 0.034, and P = 0.005, respectively). However, there was no significant change in the mean score for subscale 3 (P = 0.149)"
