Al Otaiba 2015¶

To Wait in Tier 1 or Intervene Immediately: A Randomized Experiment Examining First Grade Response to Intervention (RTI) in Reading. SREE Spring 2015 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562485

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup contrast reported in the Findings section and illustrated in Figure 1 (growth curve analysis). The article reports the direction of the difference but prints no effect size or test statistic for it.

"students initially eligible for Tier 3 had the weakest scores over the school year, but students initially eligible for Tier 3 who received the Tier 3 intervention immediately because they were in Dynamic RTI achieved higher Brief Reading scores compared to Tier 3 students in Typical RTI who had to wait until the beginning of Session 3 (see Figure 1)."
