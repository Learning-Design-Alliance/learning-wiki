# Self-assessed clinical reasoning competency rises significantly on all six measured items after team-based learning

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` All six self-assessed clinical reasoning competency items on a 7-point Likert scale were significantly higher after the TBL intervention, with effect sizes r = 0.612 to r = 0.776. [→ Ishizuka K 2025](#ishizuka-k-2025)

## Evidence

### Ishizuka K 2025

Ishizuka K, Shikino K, Takada N, et al. (2025). Enhancing clinical reasoning skills in medical students through team-based learning: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06784-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Self-administered 7-point Likert questionnaire before and after TBL in the same 89-student before-and-after evaluation. All six items improved significantly (all p < 0.001); the largest effect was for "practicing the appropriate clinical reasoning process" with "r = 0.776".

> ""practicing the appropriate clinical reasoning process" (2.5 ± 1.1 to 4.4 ± 1.2, p < 0.001, r = 0.776)"
