Descriptive synthesis of the review's included qualitative studies (from the US, China and Ireland, spanning 1993–2020). The review reports smaller classes were "the preferred option" for individualised instruction time and teacher attention; no effect size is printed.

> "Findings from the summary of included qualitative studies reflected that to the special education students and staff members participating in these studies, smaller class sizes were the preferred option because they allowed for more individualised instruction time and increased teacher attention to students' diverse needs."
