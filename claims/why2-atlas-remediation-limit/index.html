Evidence¶

VanLehn 2015¶

VanLehn, K. (2015). Regulative Loops, Step Loops and Task Loops. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0056-x

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reports, citing VanLehn et al. 2007, that the Why2-Atlas physics tutoring system's hints assumed students already knew about vectors. This is an attributed report of a prior comparison, not a new experiment in this article.

"When it was used to teach students who did not know vectors, it was less effective than human tutors, who were able to adapt their advice and teach the required remedial concepts."
