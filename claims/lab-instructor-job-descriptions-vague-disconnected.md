`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 11 semi-structured interviews; the "Unoﬃcial / unclear" code emerged under organizational structure. One staff interviewee could not state their job description in their own words. The authors describe how "these descriptions are often dis- connected from the actual work of instructors." Survey respondents also wrote "Not at 20% level, but I do all of these."

> "This passage il- lustrates that while lab instructor positions usually have oﬃcial job descriptions, these descriptions are often dis- connected from the actual work of instructors and tend 11 to lose relevant meaning “on the ground.”"
