# Test anxiety correlates negatively with math achievement at a pooled r = −.23, weaker than math anxiety's correlation

`q4 i2` Across 51 independent samples, test anxiety shows a negative pooled correlation with math achievement (r = −.23), notably weaker than the math anxiety correlation. [→ Caviola 2022](#caviola-2022)

## Evidence

### Caviola 2022

Caviola, S., Toffalini, E., Giofrè, D., Mercader Ruiz, J., Szűcs, D., & Mammarella, I. C. (2022). Math Performance and Academic Anxiety Forms, from Sociodemographic to Cognitive Aspects: a Meta-analysis on 906,311 Participants. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09618-5

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of test anxiety and math achievement correlations (41 studies, 51 independent samples, 19,733 participants pooled). The pooled estimate was "r = − .23", p < .001, with high heterogeneity (I2 = 88%).

> "The overall correlation between test anxiety and math achievement was notably weaker than the correlation between math anxiety and math achievement,r = − .23 [− .26, − .19; p <. 0 0 1 ] ."
