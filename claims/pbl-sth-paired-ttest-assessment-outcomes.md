Figure 2 caption reporting the paired t-test over five assessment parameters for the 15 residents. The test is statistically significant, t(14) = -8.06, p < 0.0001; however, the caption's printed means (PBL-STH 5.375 vs Traditional 8.375) point in the opposite direction to the article's narrative of improvement, an internal inconsistency.
