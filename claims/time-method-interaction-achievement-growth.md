## Evidence

### Gürgil 2018

Fitnat Gürgil. (2018). The Effect of Authentic Learning Approach in Social Studies Teaching on the Academic Success. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(10): 2061-2068. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061002

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed between-within ANOVA on 184 students' pretest-posttest scores, with group (AL vs AP) and time as factors. The omnibus time-group interaction was significant with "partial eta square (η2) =70"; the time main effect was also significant (η² = .93).

> "Table 2 shows that there is a significant interaction between time and the two different methods applied, Wilks' Lambda = .31, F (1,182) = 415.58, p <.05, partial eta square (η2) =70."
