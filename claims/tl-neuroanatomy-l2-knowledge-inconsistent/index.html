Effectiveness of technology-enhanced learning in neuroanatomy education among health professionals - a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09764-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis across the nine included RCTs assessing Kirkpatrick Level 2 learning outcomes. The review reports both significant assessment gains and "comparable learning outcomes between technology-enhanced and conventional teaching approaches"; no pooled effect estimate is printed.

"While several investigations reported significant gains in assessment performance, others demonstrated comparable learning outcomes between technology-enhanced and conventional teaching approaches."
