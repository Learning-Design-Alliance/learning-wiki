## Evidence

### Hossein Meysam Pour 2025

Hossein Meysam Pour, Ansari Mojgan, Fekri Nazanin, Yazdimoghaddam Hamideh. (2025). The effects of e-learning vs. gamification-based training on ICU nurses’ knowledge and attitudes toward organ donation candidates: a study based on the psychological security and empowerment model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07299-0

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Three-group pre-post trial of 45 ICU nurses (15 per group) assigned by hospital-level lottery; between-group One-Way ANOVA after intervention gave P < 0.001 with generalized Eta-Squared of 0.67, indicating "a more substantial impact on knowledge acquisition" for gamification.

> "The knowledge increase in the gamification-based training group surpassed that observed in both the e-learning and control groups, indicating that the gamification-based intervention had a more substantial impact on knowledge acquisition."
