q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects Q-tests across the 21 studies for the seven remaining moderators (Table 5); none reached significance, e.g., intervention length QB(3) = 3.76, p > .05, and language proficiency QB(3) = 5.08, p > .05.

"factors including intervention length, intervention modeling, intervention status, language proficiency, measures of proficiency, publication type, and research setting did not show an effect on feedback in CALL."

Adnan F. Saad Mohamed 2020 (2)¶

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Research-setting moderator analysis found no significant difference between foreign language (g = 0.59, k = 18) and second language (g = 0.35, k = 3) settings, though the foreign-language subgroup mean was larger, consistent with Li (2010) and contrary to Kang and Han (2015).

"the research setting ( (1) = 0.62, p >.05) does not affect feedback in CALL on student language learning."
