## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Almost no exam students (3 of 255) explicitly checked the total charge in a region without being prompted, and only one interview student did so spontaneously. [→ Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015](#bethany-r-wilcox-and-steven-j-pollock-2015)

## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2015

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Upper-division student difficulties with the Dirac delta function. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.010108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.010108)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of exam solutions for spontaneous verification of the charge in a region (element R3). "almost no students (N=3 of 255)" executed such a check unprompted; only one interview student spontaneously attempted the check, though prompting later helped several students find unit errors.

> "While this is something that we saw experts do spontaneously, almost no students (N=3 of 255) explicitly executed such a check on the exams when not prompted to calculate total charge."
