## Evidence

### Hofferber 2014

Hofferber, N., Eckes, A., & Wilde, M. (2014). Effects of Autonomy Supportive vs. Controlling Teachers' Behavior on Students' Achievements. European Journal of Educational Research, 3(4), 177-184. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085991

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The repeated measures ANOVA on the multiple-choice rote knowledge test showed significant pre-to-post gains for both the autonomy supportive and controlling groups (η2 = 0.278), confirming the lessons were successful.

> "The repeated measures ANOVA showed significant learning improvements with an effect size from pre- to post-test in rote learning for both treatment groups (F1, 84 = 32.270; p < 0.001.; η2 = 0.278)."

### Hofferber 2014 (2)

Hofferber, N., Eckes, A., & Wilde, M. (2014). Effects of Autonomy Supportive vs. Controlling Teachers' Behavior on Students' Achievements. European Journal of Educational Research, 3(4), 177-184. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085991

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The repeated measures ANOVA on the open-ended conceptual knowledge test showed a very large significant pre-to-post learning advantage in both treatment groups (η2 = 0.844).

> "In both treatment groups, the ANOVA with repeated measures shows a significant and very clear learning advantage from pre- to post-test in Knowledge Test 2 (F1, 84 = 454.650; p < 0.001; η2 = 0.844)."
