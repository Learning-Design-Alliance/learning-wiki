## Evidence

### Dijkhuis Renée 2020

Dijkhuis Renée, de Sonneville Leo, Ziermans Tim, Staal Wouter, Swaab Hanna. (2020). Autism Symptoms, Executive Functioning and Academic Progress in Higher Education Students. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-019-04267-8

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations with academic progress in the ASD sample (n = 37 for BRIEF-A). Negative correlations indicate "a higher autism score and poorer daily EF were associated with poorer academic progress"; plan/organize r = −.57 is a large printed effect size.

> "Significant correlations with academic progress also emerged for some scales of the BRIEF-A, ordered from strong to moderate correlations: plan/organize (r = − .57, p < .001), initiate (r = − .49, p = .002), total score (r = − .45, p = .005), task monitor (r = − .40, p = .014) and working memory (r = − .38, p = .021)"
