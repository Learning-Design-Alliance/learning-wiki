## Evidence

### Althewini Abdulaziz A 2025

Althewini Abdulaziz A. (2025). How well do admission criteria predict student performance in one medical course? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08378-y

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate Pearson correlation analysis of archival admission and grade records at one Saudi medical college. The study found "weak but significant positive correlations with GAT (r = 0.22, p < 0.01)" and with SAAT (r = 0.23, p < 0.01).

> "Biology grades showed weak but significant positive correlations with GAT (r = 0.22, p < 0.01) and SAAT (r = 0.23, p < 0.01)."

### Althewini Abdulaziz A 2025 (2)

Althewini Abdulaziz A. (2025). How well do admission criteria predict student performance in one medical course? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08378-y

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Same Pearson correlation matrix for the student cohort. "moderate associations were observed for English proficiency measures": the reading comprehension test correlated r = 0.44 (p < 0.001) and the communication skills test r = 0.43 (p < 0.001) with biology grades.

> "By contrast, moderate associations were observed for English proficiency measures, particularly the reading comprehension test (r = 0.44, p < 0.001) and the communication skills test (r = 0.43, p < 0.001)."
