Quasi-experimental pilot study with 17 nursing students (10 intervention, 7 control) using an application overlaying animated 3D organ models on mannequins, as reported in the review's Chart 4; Cohen's d was used for effect size but no value is printed.

> "The use of high-fidelity simulation demonstrated significant improvement in the IG for auscultation of the broncho-vesicular sounds and the aortic, pulmonary, tricuspid, and mitral valves."
