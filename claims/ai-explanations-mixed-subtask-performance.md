## Evidence

### Julia Lademann 2024

Julia Lademann, Jannik Henze, and Sebastian Becker-Genschow. (2024). Building Bridges – AI Custom Chatbots as Mediators between Mathematics and Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In the performance test of the randomized controlled study, subtask C required calculating the constant of proportionality of a given proportional relationship. The EG outscored the CG, with a printed effect size of d = 0.32 ("small effect size").

> "The EG achieved a significantly higher score than the CG (𝑝𝑝 =  0. 022, 𝑑𝑑 =  0. 32, small effect size)."

### Julia Lademann 2024 (2)

Julia Lademann, Jannik Henze, and Sebastian Becker-Genschow. (2024). Building Bridges – AI Custom Chatbots as Mediators between Mathematics and Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15747)

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

In subtask B, students computed six missing values of a proportional relationship in a table. For the overall subtask B score there was no significant difference, but the CG performed significantly better on value 3 (d = 0.33) and value 5 (d = 0.29), both printed as small effect sizes.

> "the solutions for value 3 ( 𝑝𝑝 =  0. 016, 𝑑𝑑 =  0. 33) and value 5 ( 𝑝𝑝 =  0. 038, 𝑑𝑑 =  0. 29) exhibited significant differences with small effect size"
