### de la Varre 2010

de la Varre, C., Keane, J., & Irvin, M. J. (2010). Enhancing online distance education in small rural US schools: A hybrid, learner-centered model. Journal of Asynchronous Learning Networks, 15(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ951828

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative findings from year-end debriefing meetings with online instructors about facilitator performance in communication and responsiveness. Instructors valued those who emailed regularly and encouraged student-instructor contact.

> "Online instructors noted that their "best" facilitators were those with whom they communicated frequently and developed a personal relationship, as well as those who also encouraged their students to contact the instructor when they needed help"
