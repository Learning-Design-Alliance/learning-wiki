## Evidence

### David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter 2021

David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter. (2021). Gender-grade-gap zeroed out under a specific intro-physics assessment regime. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

HLM analysis of the single M2Q mastery class (N = 321 grades) shows a gender gap of -0.21 with standard error 0.12 and P-value 0.072, similar to the usual-classes gap. The article labels this class likely anomalous.

> "The course that offered two quizzes in most of the lectures had a quite different result. Table 8.2 shows that the gender gap in this course was similar to that in CLASP courses with the usual kind of grading."
