## Evidence

### Gormally C. 2012

Gormally C., Brickman P., Lutz M. (2012). Developing a Test of Scientific Literacy Skills (TOSLS): Measuring Undergraduates' Evaluation of Scientific Information and Arguments. CBE—Life Sciences Education 11(4):364–377. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-03-0026

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Online survey of life science faculty teaching Gen Ed biology courses nationwide (n = 188 respondents; Figure 1 reports n = 167 teaching a Gen Ed course), asking faculty to rate, teach, and assess the nine TOSLS skills. The article reports faculty "assess their students' proficiencies in using these skills at lower rates than they report teaching these skills."

> "A large majority of faculty report that they currently teach all these skills (≥58.7% teach all skills, with the exception of skill 8: understanding and interpreting basic statistics, which only 44.9% of faculty report teaching)."
