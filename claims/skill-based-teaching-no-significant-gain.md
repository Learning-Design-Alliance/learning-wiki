## Evidence

### Bogale 2018

Bogale, Y. N. (2018). Conceptualizing Reading to Learn: Strategy Instruction and EFL Students' Reading Comprehension. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 10(2), 93–117. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1207221

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-test on the control group (n=54); Table 3 reports pre-test mean 58.61 (SD 8.710) and post-test mean 60.56 (SD 7.811). No effect size is printed, so the null is reported as a non-significant test, not as equivalence.

> "the posttest means scores of the participants instructed with the skill based reading instruction did not show any significant difference  from their pretest mean score at a .094 level, indicating that the skill based reading instruction did not help in enhancing students' reading comprehension in the control group."
