Başar 2017¶

Başar, M. & Gürbüz, M. (2017). Effect of the SQ4R Technique on the Reading Comprehension of Elementary School 4th Grade Elementary School Students. International Journal of Instruction, 10(2), 131-144. http://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2017_2_9.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Permanence test applied to both groups four weeks after the post-test. The article reports the experimental group's mean remained higher and "significant [t(55)=1.064, p<.001]"; experimental scores partially increased while control scores declined. No effect size is printed.

"the score average of experimental group (Avg=4.12) is higher than that of the control group (Avg=2.92) and is found to be quite significant [t(55)=1.064, p<.001]"
