### Rebeca Cerezo 2024

Rebeca Cerezo, Vicente Calderón, Cristóbal Romero. (2024). A holographic mobile-based application for practicing pronunciation of basic English vocabulary for Spanish speaking children. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way ANOVA on pre-test scores in the three-group experiment (Table 3). "This analysis (F=0.13, p≥0.05)" indicated no significant difference in prior knowledge; equivalence beyond the non-significant test was not tested. Levene's test showed homogeneity (F = 0.269, p > 0.05).

> "This analysis (F=0.13, p≥0.05) indicated that there was no significant difference in the three group’s levels of prior knowledge before the experimental teaching activities began."
