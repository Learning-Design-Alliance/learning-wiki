Evidence¶

Jo Tondeur 2025¶

Jo Tondeur, Ottavia Trevisan, Sarah K. Howard, Johan van Braak. (2025). Preparing preservice teachers to teach with digital technologies: An update of effective SQD-strategies. Computers & Education 232. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105262

q3 · i? · review · r2

Meta-aggregation of qualitative studies in the Reflective Practice synthesis, drawing on 21 studies with sub-categories digital pedagogies, research, teacher identity and technology scaffolding. The review reports research tasks such as producing essays or presentations fostered reflection.

"This suggests that incorporating research offers a valuable strategy for bridging the gap between theory and practice (cf. Butler & Leahy, 2021; Eutsler, 2022)."

Jo Tondeur 2025 (2)¶

Jo Tondeur, Ottavia Trevisan, Sarah K. Howard, Johan van Braak. (2025). Preparing preservice teachers to teach with digital technologies: An update of effective SQD-strategies. Computers & Education 232. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105262

q3 · i? · review · r2

Discussion-section interpretation of the updated synthesis: the review identifies digital identity as a new theme, described via Engeness (2021) as a continuous process of interpreting beliefs, values and educational experiences in response to contemporary digital contexts.

"Another new theme in this respect is the importance of preservice teachers' "digital identity" (see Park, 2019)."
