### Bobos Pavlos 2021

Bobos Pavlos, Pouliopoulou Dimitra V., Harriss Alexandra, Sadi Jackie, Rushton Alison, MacDermid Joy C. (2021). A systematic review and meta-analysis of measurement properties of objective structured clinical examinations used in physical therapy licensure and a structured review of licensure practices in countries with well-developed regulation systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255696

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random effects meta-analysis of kappa coefficients for inter-rater reliability between standardised patients. The pooled estimate was "0.84 (95% CI: 0.72, 0.91)" with I2 = 0%. No standardised effect size was printed for this finding.

> "For the inter-rater reliability between SP, the pooled estimate of kappa Coefficient based on all the available data was 0.84 (95% CI: 0.72, 0.91) with no statistical heterogeneity, I2 = 0% (Fig 7)."

### Bobos Pavlos 2021 (2)

Bobos Pavlos, Pouliopoulou Dimitra V., Harriss Alexandra, Sadi Jackie, Rushton Alison, MacDermid Joy C. (2021). A systematic review and meta-analysis of measurement properties of objective structured clinical examinations used in physical therapy licensure and a structured review of licensure practices in countries with well-developed regulation systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255696

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Random effects meta-analysis of kappa coefficients for intra-rater reliability of the standardised patient's self-evaluation ratings. The pooled estimate was "0.75 (95% CI: 0.58, 0.86)" with I2 = 0%. No standardised effect size was printed for this finding.

> "For the intra-rater reliability between SP, the pooled estimate of kappa Coefficient was 0.75 (95% CI: 0.58, 0.86) with no presence of statistical heterogeneity, I2 = 0% (Fig 8)."
