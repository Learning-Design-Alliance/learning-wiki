Forming-stage characterization of pupils' difficulties by teacher-researchers working with the 'I want to learn' technology. The percentages are printed descriptively; the article adds that many pupils did not believe their difficulties could be overcome.

"They all have some way of compensating their learning failures: sport (13%), television and computer (21%), idling away the time with friends (35%), no hobbies (31%)."
