## Evidence

### Karamdokht Parya 2026

Karamdokht Parya, Babaee Hemmati Yasamin, Dadgaran Ideh, Falahchai Mehran. (2026). Item analysis and performance of EMQ, MCQ, and SAQ in dental education and students’ perception of extended-matching questions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09791-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures analysis of performance percentages in the same 29-student cohort; all Bonferroni pairwise comparisons were significant (P < 0.05). SAQ scores were highest and EMQ scores weakest, though SAQ scoring may retain "examiner-related variability".

> "Descriptively, students performed best in the SAQs (78.17 ± 13) and then MCQs (72.84 ± 9.23), and the weakest performance was related to the EMQs (55.87 ± 17.84)"
