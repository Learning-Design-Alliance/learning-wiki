# First-year Biology students report high satisfaction with a QR-based active learning bioinformatics lesson, with 94.6% finding the lesson important and about 89% finding it effective for information-management skills

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a 10-question anonymous online survey, 94.6% found the lesson important, 85.7% and 83.9% found it useful for acquiring and consolidating subject knowledge, and 89.3% felt it was effective in helping them learn how to find, use and manage information.
