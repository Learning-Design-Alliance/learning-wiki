Mann Whitney U tests on pre-intervention scores in the randomised feasibility study found no significant baseline differences in Ball Transfer, Circle Cutting, String, or confidence. The authors attribute the Peg Transfer disparity to chance.

> "However, Group 1 outperformed Group 2 in the “Peg Transfer” station (Median difference = 12, p = 0.05, Effect size = 0.36)."
