## Evidence

### Ong C.K.Y. 2021

Ong C.K.Y., Hutchesson M.J., Patterson A.J., Whatnall M.C. (2021). Is There an Association between Health Risk Behaviours and Academic Achievement among University Students? International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18168314

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted linear regression in the cross-sectional survey of 1543 Australian university students. The article reports the breakfast association remained significant after adjustment for socio-demographic and student characteristics; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "not consuming breakfast on a daily basis (β = −0.261, p ≤ 0.001), not meeting age-based sleep recommendations (β = −0.163, p = 0.018)"
