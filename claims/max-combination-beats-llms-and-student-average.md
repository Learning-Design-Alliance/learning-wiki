`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Performance evaluation per year (Table II) on the 200-question VNHSGE physics set, with Max scoring 1 if either ChatGPT or BingChat answered correctly and Min only if both did. The article reports Max performed better than the student average (AVNS) and most-attained student score (MVNS) in Table III.

> "Max performed consistently well, 78.5%, while Min’s performance is 48.5%. Interestingly, ChatGPT outperformed BingChat only in 2019. These findings suggest that while each LLM has its strengths and weaknesses, Max is the most consistent performer across all years."
