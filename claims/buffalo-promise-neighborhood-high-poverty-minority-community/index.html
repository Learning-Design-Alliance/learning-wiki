q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

Descriptive site profile prepared for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink. It reports community demographics: "The population is 76 percent minority, including 69 percent African American," with 38 percent of residents below the federal poverty level and 88 percent of students qualifying for free or reduced-price meals. No effect sizes or outcomes are reported.

"The Buffalo Promise Neighborhood serves one of Buffalo's poorest communities. The population is 76 percent minority, including 69 percent African American. More than a third of Buffalo Promise Neighborhood residents (38 percent) live in households with incomes below the federal poverty level, and 88 percent of students qualify for free or reduced-price school meals."
