## Evidence

### El Ansari Walid 2025

El Ansari Walid, El-Ansari Kareem, Hany Mohamed. (2025). Healthcare research and education: actively constructed research knowledge–a model for online systematic reviews and meta-analyses courses. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-025-07111-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The scoping review of literature and online courses identified that available online SR/MA courses lack key features including experiential interactivity and individualized expert feedback. The article states that "a lack of self-paced, self-directed online SR/MA courses that offer learners experiential interactivity" exists.

> "These above-mentioned knowledge gaps suggest a lack of self-paced, self-directed online SR/MA courses that offer learners experiential interactivity embedded in the training, from study design to data collection and synthesis of results, supplemented with individual feedback from expert trainer/s."
