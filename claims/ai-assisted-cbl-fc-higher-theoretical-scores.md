`q3 i?` Residents in the AI-assisted CBL+FC group scored significantly higher on the post-course theoretical knowledge test than the lecture-based control group (81.4 vs. 76.2 points, P < 0.01). [→ Wang 2026](#wang-2026)

## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, Q., Hu, C., Li, Y., Zhang, T., & Zhang, S. (2026). AI-assisted case-based learning and flipped classroom to improve clinical decision-making: a randomized controlled trial in reproductive medicine. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2670047

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 50 residents (n=25 per group) at a single centre. The intervention group completed pre-class virtual standardized patient work and case analysis, followed by interactive in-class discussion. The article reports a statistically significant post-test advantage with mean scores of 81.4 vs. 76.2 points; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "the AI-assisted CBL + FC group significantly outperformed the control group on the post-test (mean score: 81.4 vs. 76.2 points; P < 0.01) as shown in Figure 3B and C"
