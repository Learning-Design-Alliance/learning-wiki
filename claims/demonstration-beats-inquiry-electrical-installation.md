## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with demonstration strategy achieved higher post-test scores and mean gains than students taught with inquiry strategy, a statistically significant difference. [→ Vincent E. Onweh and Udeme Timothy Akpan 2014](#vincent-e-onweh-and-udeme-timothy-akpan-2014)

## Evidence

### Vincent E. Onweh and Udeme Timothy Akpan 2014

Vincent E. Onweh and Udeme Timothy Akpan. (2014). Instructional strategies and students’ academic performance in electrical installation in technical colleges in Akwa Ibom State: Instructional skills for structuring appropriate learning experiences for students. International Journal of Educational Administration and Policy Studies. https://doi.org/10.5897/IJEAPS2014.0347

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of intact classes taught with demonstration versus inquiry, analysed by ANCOVA with pre-test as covariate. The article reports the mean gains quoted above; ANCOVA gave a calculated F of 28.832 against a critical F of 3.07, so the null hypothesis of no difference was rejected.

> "Table 2 indicates that the mean gain score of demonstration strategy (15.58) is greater than that of inquiry strategy (12.32). In addition, the post-test mean score of demonstration strategy (21.19) is greater than the post-test mean score of inquiry strategy (18.00), making a difference of 3.19."
