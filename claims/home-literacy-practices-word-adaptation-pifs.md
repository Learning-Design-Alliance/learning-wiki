## Evidence

### Girard 2021

Girard, C., Bastelica, T., Léone, J., Epinat-Duclos, J., Longo, L. & Prado, J. (2021). Nurturing the reading brain: home literacy practices are associated with children's neural response to printed words through vocabulary skills. npj Science of Learning 6:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00112-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ROI analysis in the fMRI sample of 44 French 8-year-olds using the Perrachione et al. word adaptation task found a positive relation between home literacy practices and word adaptation in the pIFs (r(42) = 0.322, pcorr = 0.048), "but not in the pdSTS ( r(42) = 0.052, pcorr > 0.999)".

> "After correcting for multiple comparisons, there was a positive relation between frequency of home literacy practices and word adaptation in the pIFs ( r(42) = 0.322, pcorr = 0.048)"
