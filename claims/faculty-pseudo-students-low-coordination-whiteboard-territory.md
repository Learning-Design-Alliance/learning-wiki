### Alice Olmstead and Chandra Turpen 2015

Alice Olmstead and Chandra Turpen. (2015). “I got in trouble”: A case study of faculty “doing school” during professional development. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.056](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.056)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case analysis of a 9-minute small-group episode in which four faculty, positioned as physics students, drew a grid on a shared whiteboard. The authors interpret the group's negotiation of who draws as contested territory, noting "conflicts of insistence" and simultaneous talk.

> "Treating a group artifact as contested “territory” is a marker of low coordination in group work, as are “conflicts of insistence” (their conflict does not build meaning), and violation of turn-taking norms (faculty repeatedly interrupt each other and talk simultaneously)"
