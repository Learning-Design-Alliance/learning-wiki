### Höbaus Clemens 2025

Höbaus Clemens, Schmidmaier Ralf. (2025). Individual and group reflection in lecture-based large groups lead to comparable learning success. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-025-02610-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of baseline and follow-up MCQ scores by reflection method showed "for L1 memory retention was reduced by 14% for IR and 18% for GR at follow-up", while L2 retention was stable with slight increases of 4% and 2%.
