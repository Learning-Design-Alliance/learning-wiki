Finding attributed to Driskell et al. (1994) within the reviewed literature; the same review context notes fifty years of research indicate distributed MS is better than massed MS, suggesting an optimum inter-session span could be determined.

"One review reports a significant negative relationship between retention interval and effect of MS, indicating that the longer the time span between pre- and post-test, the weaker the effect of MS"
