`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Confirmatory factor analysis with robust ML estimation on 15 items, 158 Prolific participants rating a nerve-cell learning material. The article states "The initial model showed an acceptable fit of the data" with the printed indices.

> "The initial model showed an acceptable fit of the data (CFI = 0.92; RMSEA  = 0.10; GFI  = 0.92; SRMR  = 0.07). Factor loadings revealed that all items load on the intended factor ( p < 0.001)."
