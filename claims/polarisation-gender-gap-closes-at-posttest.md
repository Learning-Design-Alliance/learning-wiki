Chi-squared test on posttest responses for the same cohort. The same pattern held (males taller in five of six questions) but with a smaller relative difference, "an average P-value of 79% versus 66%".

> "For the posttest, our chi-squared test returns a p-value of 0.055. This means that at the 95% conﬁdence level, we do not have statistically signiﬁcant evidence suggesting a gender aspect of polarisation."
