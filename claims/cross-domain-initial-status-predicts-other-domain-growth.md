### Scammacca Nancy 2020

Scammacca Nancy, Fall Anna-Mária, Capin Philip, Roberts Greg, Swanson Elizabeth (2020). Examining Factors Affecting Reading and Math Growth and Achievement Gaps in Grades 1–5: A Cohort-Sequential Longitudinal Approach. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000400

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

From the conditional LGC models, which included math quartile in the reading model and reading quartile in the math model to test cross-domain effects of initial status on growth.

> "math quartile was a positive predictor of reading slope and reading quartile was a positive predictor of math slope, indicating that students who started out with greater proficiency in one domain tended to grow at a somewhat faster rate in the other domain."
