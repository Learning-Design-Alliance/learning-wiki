## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Any exam on which students receive different grades can be challenged as underestimating some talent, so fairness of opportunity requires equal outcomes. [→ Mathieu Bouville 2008](#mathieu-bouville-2008)

## Evidence

### Mathieu Bouville 2008

Mathieu Bouville. (2008). Exam fairness. https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4235 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0803.4235)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in the Fairness of Opportunity section: because talents are numerous and incommensurable, any ranking undervalues some student's talent unless an external hierarchy of talents is supplied. The article concludes this conception "holds all claims to be equally valid."

> "Fairness of opportunity must le ad to identical grades, lest losers claim that the exam was unfair to them."
