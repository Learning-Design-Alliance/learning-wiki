## Evidence

### Lederer AM 2025

Lederer AM, Oswalt SB, Eddens KS. (2025). Individual and collective positive health behaviors and academic achievement among U.S. undergraduate students. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323610

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Log-binomial models in Table 3 of the Spring 2023 ACHA-NCHA III (N = 50,792) with D/F GPA as referent; A-GPA students were 16% more likely to watch ≤15 hours of television per week (p < .0001) and 6% more likely to game ≤15 hours (p = .0002). The same models found "There were no statistically significant differences between GPA categories in using social media for ≤15 hours a week." No standardized effect size is printed.
