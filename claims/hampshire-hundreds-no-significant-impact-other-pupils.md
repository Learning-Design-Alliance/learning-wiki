## Evidence

### Sandra McNally 2014

Sandra McNally, Amy Challen, Gill Wyness, Anne West, Philip Noden. (2014). Hampshire Hundreds Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581163

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat regression for 1124 non-vulnerable pupils (Table 6b: effect size -0.012, SE 0.044, 95% CI [-0.103, 0.080]), not significant. The report notes that with additional controls the point estimate became "negative and significant at the 5 per cent level" at -0.06 standard deviations.

> "In the case of other (non -vulnerable) pupils, estimates are sensitive to including controls, with the point estimate becoming negative and significant at the 5 per cent level (although small in magnitude at -0.06 standard deviations)."
