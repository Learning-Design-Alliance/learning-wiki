## Evidence

### Woolf K 2016

Woolf K, Rich A, Viney R, Needleman S, Griffin A. (2016). Perceived causes of differential attainment in UK postgraduate medical training: a national qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013429

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Hidden-curriculum subtheme: reputations were thought to follow trainees between jobs, making it hard to report bullying including race-related problems, amplified for IMGs and BME UKGs.

> "Experiencing difficulties was seen as a sign of weakness, meaning trainees felt they were not always given the support needed to learn or were blamed for problems that were not their fault. IMGs could feel stigmatised or disadvantaged by attending extra courses."
