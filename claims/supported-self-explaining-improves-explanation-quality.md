# Supported self-explaining produces higher-quality explanations of fraction magnitude differences than a word-problem component

## Subclaims

`q3 i2` EXP provided higher quality explanations than WP (ES = 0.93) and CON (ES = 1.37). [→ Fuchs 2016](#fuchs-2016)

`q3 i2` The explaining condition's explanations, scored for conceptual content, were of higher quality than the word-problem condition's (ES = 0.93) and the control's (ES = 1.37). [→ Fuchs 2015](#fuchs-2015)

## Evidence

### Fuchs 2016

Fuchs, L. S., Malone, A., Schumacher, R. F., Namkung, J., Hamlett, C. L., Jordan, N. C., Siegler, R. S., Gersten, R., & Changas, P. (2016). Supported Self-Explaining during Fraction Intervention. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000073

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on explanation quality posttest (max 27, alpha .92) in the RCT; explanations scored for conceptual content by two independent coders with 99.1% agreement. The article reports EXP "provided higher quality explanations than WP (ES = 0.93)".

> "On explanation quality, EXP provided higher quality explanations than WP (ES = 0.93) and CON (ES = 1.37), and WP provided higher quality explanations than CON (ES = 0.60)."

### Fuchs 2015

Fuchs, L. S., Malone, A., Schumacher, R. F., Namkung, J., Hamlett, C. L., Jordan, N. C., Siegler, R., Gersten, R., & Changas, P. (2015). Supported Self-Explaining during Fraction Intervention. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED557580

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

One-way ANOVA on posttest explanation quality (max score 27, alpha .92) in the same RCT. The article reports EXP "provided higher quality explanations than WP (ES = 0.93)"; WP also exceeded CON (ES = 0.60).

> "On explanation quality, EXP provided higher quality explanations than WP (ES = 0.93) and CON (ES = 1.37), and WP provided higher quality explanations than CON (ES = 0.60)."
