## Evidence

### Khalafi Ali 2026

Khalafi Ali, Parhizkar Mahdieh, Albooghobeish Masoumeh, Sarvi-sarmeydani Nooshin. (2026). Effect of the BOPPPS model on pre-anesthesia care education for nurse anesthesia students in Iran: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08696-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA validation analysis conducted "with post-test scores as dependent variables and pre-test scores as covariates" in the quasi-experiment. The intervention group "still outperformed the control group in knowledge" (partial η² = 0.45) and skills (partial η² = 0.66), both p < 0.001.

> "the intervention group still outperformed the control group in knowledge (F(1, 57) = 45.72, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.45) and skills (F(1, 57) = 112.84, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.66)."
