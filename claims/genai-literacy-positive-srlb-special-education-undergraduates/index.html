Evidence¶

Yang J 2026¶

Yang J, Liu X, Zhao W and Wang T (2026). GenAI literacy and GenAI-assisted self-regulated learning behaviors: the roles of learning agency and challenge emotions. Front. Psychol. 17:1902668. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1902668

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Cross-sectional online survey of 434 special education undergraduates in four Chinese provinces; regression with gender and year of study as covariates reported β = 0.148, p = 0.006 for the GenAI literacy–SRLB path. The article reports the association as "positively associated with GenAI-assisted self-regulated learning behaviors".
