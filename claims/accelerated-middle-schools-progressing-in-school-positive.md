# Accelerated middle schools show positive effects on progressing in school, with an average improvement index of +35 percentile points

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` All three studies found statistically significant and substantively important effects on progressing in school; the average improvement index was +35 percentile points, ranging from +15 to +44. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2008](#what-works-clearinghouse-2008)

### What Works Clearinghouse 2008 (2)

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Accelerated Middle Schools. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/11

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Georgia RCT (140 applicants for the 1993/94 school year) outcome on years of school completed at two-year follow-up: 8.6 for the intervention versus 7.9 for controls, reported as statistically significant and substantively important. Means are descriptive; no effect size statistic is printed for this measure.

> "In the Georgia study the average number of school years completed at the two-year follow-up was 8.6 for accelerated middle school students and 7.9 for control-group students."

### What Works Clearinghouse 2008 (3)
