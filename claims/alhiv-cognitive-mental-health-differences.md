`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Chi-square comparisons in the full sample (Table 1, N = 1519) show ALHIV reported more cognitive difficulties "(40.2% versus 25.4%)" and mental health challenges "(49.7% versus 40.2%)"; school-related externalising issues did not differ by HIV status (p = 0.820).
