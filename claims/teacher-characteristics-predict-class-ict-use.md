# Teacher characteristics (computer attitudes, innovativeness, intensity of use) significantly predict classroom ICT use alongside policy factors

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Attitudes towards computers in education, technological innovativeness, and intensity of computer use each have a significant positive effect on class ICT use. [→ Tondeur 2008](#tondeur-2008)

## Evidence

### Tondeur 2008

Tondeur, J., van Keer, H., van Braak, J., & Valcke, M. (2008). ICT integration in the classroom: Challenging the potential of a school policy. Computers & Education, 51, 212-223. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2007.05.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel final-model results for the 574-teacher sample; the article reports statistically significant positive effects with the printed test statistics for the three teacher characteristics. The article states these findings confirm earlier results by van Braak et al. (2004).

> "The results highlight the signiﬁcant positive eﬀect of teachers’ ‘attitudes towards computers in education’ ( v2 = 8.410, d f =1 , p = 0.003), ‘technological innovative- ness’ ( v2 = 4.453, d f =1 , p = 0.030), ‘intensity of computer use’ ( v2 = 10.552, d f =1 , p = 0.001)"
