Evidence¶

Todd 2019¶

Todd, A. W., Algozzine, B., Horner, R. H., Preston, A. I., Cusumano, D., & Algozzine, K. (2019). A Descriptive Study of School-Based Problem-Solving. Journal of Emotional and Behavioral Disorders, 27(1), 14-24. https://doi.org/10.1177/1063426617733717

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive analysis of 233 problems discussed in 152 meetings by 38 elementary school teams, with solutions developed and implemented for 196 (84.1%). The printed percentages name the most prevalent problem behavior types; attendance (3.9%) and theft (2.3%) were less prevalent.

"The most common problem behaviors identified by teams were physical aggression (42.0%), defiance (19.3%), disruption (10.2%), and disrespect (9.1%)."

Todd 2019 (2)¶

Todd, A. W., Algozzine, B., Horner, R. H., Preston, A. I., Cusumano, D., & Algozzine, K. (2019). A Descriptive Study of School-Based Problem-Solving. Journal of Emotional and Behavioral Disorders, 27(1), 14-24. https://doi.org/10.1177/1063426617733717

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Cross-tabulation of team-reported problem behavior type by location for the most common behaviors (physical aggression, defiance, disruption) and locations (classroom, bus, playground, cafeteria). The printed counts show aggression leading across settings and defiance and disruption concentrated in classrooms.

"Results indicated that physical aggression was the most likely reported problem behavior on the bus (n = 15), in the classroom (n = 13), and on the playground (n = 12). Defiance (n = 7) and disruption (n = 5) were more common problem behaviors reported in classrooms."
