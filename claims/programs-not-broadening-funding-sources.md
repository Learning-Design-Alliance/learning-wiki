# Most grant-funded residency programs have not yet broadened funding sources or adopted cost-reduction strategies beyond the one-time grant

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Federal Title I, Title II, and other federal grant funds did not factor into most program leads' financial models, revealing a gap between available funding sources and uptake. [→ Hirschboeck 2022](#hirschboeck-2022)
`q2 i?` Only a minority of program leads used or considered IHE-focused cost-reduction strategies such as coursework redesign or residency-specific financial aid. [→ Hirschboeck 2022 (2)](#hirschboeck-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Hirschboeck 2022

Hirschboeck, K., Eiler White, M., Brannegan, A., & Reade, F. (2022). Teacher residency programs in California: Financial sustainability challenges and opportunities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED620723

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Spring 2021 Partnership Lead Survey (n=26), Exhibit 2: federal and other nongrant sources "did not factor into most program leads' thinking" — e.g., ESSA Title I at 4 percent currently used, 30 percent not considering.

> "Several key potential funding sources and strategies—for example, federal Title I or Title II funds or other federal grants (which could potentially be strategically "braided" together to support residencies)—did not factor into most program leads' thinking regarding their program models"

### Hirschboeck 2022 (2)

Hirschboeck, K., Eiler White, M., Brannegan, A., & Reade, F. (2022). Teacher residency programs in California: Financial sustainability challenges and opportunities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED620723

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exhibit 3 (Partnership Lead Survey, n=26) shows low uptake of IHE-focused cost strategies: e.g., 57 percent were not considering work-study jobs integrating with the residency, and only small shares used coursework redesign or tuition reduction.

> "a minority of program leads reported that their program used or was considering using IHE-focused strategies aimed at reducing residency program costs"
