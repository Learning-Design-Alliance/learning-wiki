## Evidence

### Yang Hexin 2022

Yang Hexin, Wu Chaoyue, Chen Ji-Kang. (2022). Interparental and Intergenerational Co-parenting Conflict and Adolescent Academic Performance: The Mediating Roles of Adolescent Academic Engagement and Depression. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315952

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' comparison of the two bootstrapped indirect effects in the SEM of 1989 CFPS dyads: engagement −0.053 versus depression −0.028. The article states this is "in line with previous studies suggesting that academic engagement has a more salient effect than depression on student academic performance".

> "this study indicated that academic engagement plays a stronger mediating role and accounts for a larger amount of the explained variance in academic performance than depression."
