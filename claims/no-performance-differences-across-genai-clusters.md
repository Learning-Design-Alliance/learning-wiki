## Evidence

### Marina Lepp 2026

Marina Lepp. (2026). Patterns of Learner–AI Interaction and Academic Performance in an Object-Oriented Programming Course. IEEE Frontiers in Education (FIE) 2026 Conference. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24755

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison of the five clusters on AI-prohibited assessments (Test 1, Test 2, exam, total points) among the 210 respondents; equivalence was not tested, and the authors note the study may have been underpowered for small differences.

> "No significant differences were found in scores on either of the two midterm programming tests, the final exam, or total course points (all p > 0.05)."

### Marina Lepp 2026 (2)

Marina Lepp. (2026). Patterns of Learner–AI Interaction and Academic Performance in an Object-Oriented Programming Course. IEEE Frontiers in Education (FIE) 2026 Conference. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24755

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square and Kruskal–Wallis checks of prerequisite Python course grades across clusters, reported as χ²(20) = 16.47, p = 0.687 and H(4) = 3.59, p = 0.464.

> "No statistically significant differences were found in grade distributions across clusters (χ²(20) = 16.47, p = 0.687) or in average grade ranks (H(4) = 3.59, p = 0.464)."
