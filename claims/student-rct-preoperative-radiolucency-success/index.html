Weighted pooled success rates from the same three‐study random‐effects meta‐analysis of student‐performed treatment (294 roots with lesions, 477 without, per Table 3). The review reports "86.8% vs. 56.3%"; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Similarly, teeth without preoperative periapical lesions demonstrated higher success rates compared to those with and without preoperative radiolucencies (86.8% vs. 56.3%)."
