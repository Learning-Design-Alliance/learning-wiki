`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of mental health outcomes in structured-interview cohorts at University Sains Malaysia, measured by the self-administered General Health Questionnaire. The early advantage was not sustained: "by the final year of medical school, however, both cohorts had similar levels of stress, anxiety, and depression."

> "Students admitted through structured interviews had a lower risk of developing psychological distress according to the self-administered General Health Questionnaire two months after enrollment;27 by the final year of medical school, however, both cohorts had similar levels of stress, anxiety, and depression.26"
