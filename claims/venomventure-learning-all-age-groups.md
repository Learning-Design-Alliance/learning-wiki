### Thanukos Anastasia 2026

Thanukos Anastasia, MacDonald Teresa, Quimby Claire, White Lisa D. (2026). VENOMventure, an Immersive Escape-Style Game, Teaches Families the Foundations of Phylogenetics. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0172

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sign tests on pre/post change scores for younger children (8 and under), the 9–13 target group, and participants aged 14 and older each showed the quiz score "increased from pre to post in each individual age group".

> "These tests showed that the knowledge quiz score increased from pre to post in each individual age group (Table 2)"

### Thanukos Anastasia 2026 (2)

Thanukos Anastasia, MacDonald Teresa, Quimby Claire, White Lisa D. (2026). VENOMventure, an Immersive Escape-Style Game, Teaches Families the Foundations of Phylogenetics. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0172

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

A Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the 9–13 target group's symmetric change-score distribution obtained an effect the authors describe as "large" (r = 0.561, N = 157).

> "we performed a related-samples Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the data for this group in order to obtain an effect size, and found that it was large (N = 157, Z = −7.031, p < 0.001, r = 0.561)"
