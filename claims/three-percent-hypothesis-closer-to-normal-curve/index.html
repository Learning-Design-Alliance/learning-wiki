Pintner 1917¶

Pintner, R. (1917). The Mentality of the Dependent Child, Together with a Plan for a Mental Survey of an Institution. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075825

q2 · i? · design · r2

Graphical comparison (Graph I) of the 106 home children's distributions under the two diagnostic methods. The author cautions the curves "prove nothing" but calls the differing distributions suggestive.

"It will be noted that the Three Per Cent. Hypothesis gives a curve much closer to the normal curve than the C. M. A. The cuive showing the distribution according to the Three Per Cent. Hypothesis is skewed to the left and the majority of the cases fall in the backward group."
