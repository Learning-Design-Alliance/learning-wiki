## Evidence

A Meta-Analysis of the Development of Divergent Thinking in School-Age Children and Adolescents. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Does+the+Fourth-Grade+Slump+in+Creativity+Actually+Exist

Sensitivity analysis in Results removing one outlier SMD yielded a Grade 3–4 SMD of "0.07 (p = .38)", a non-significant difference supporting a plateau rather than a slump. Equivalence was not formally tested.
