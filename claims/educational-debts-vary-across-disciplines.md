Ben Van Dusen, Jayson Nissen and Odis Johnson. (2024). Society's educational debts in biology, chemistry, and physics across race, gender, and class. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2404.01314](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2404.01314)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

This observational analysis of 18,791 students in 305 introductory courses at 45 institutions used three-level Bayesian regression models to estimate educational debts. The article reports "12.3% in physics, 5.3% in biology, and 9.0% in chemistry" as average pretest debts.

> "Society's average educational debt on the pretest was 12.3% in physics, 5.3% in biology, and 9.0% in chemistry, see Appendix Table 7."
