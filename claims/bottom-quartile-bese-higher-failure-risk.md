## Evidence

### Wallihan Rebecca 2018

Wallihan Rebecca, Smith Keely G., Hormann Mark D., Donthi Rajesh R., Boland Kimberly, Mahan John D. (2018). Utility of intermittent online quizzes as an early warning for residents at risk of failing the pediatric board certification examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1366-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Contingency-table analysis of 329 residents' composite BESE quartiles against first-attempt ABP CE failure, reported in Results. Failure rates increased monotonically from the top to bottom quartile, and the bottom-quartile odds ratio was 4.6 (95% CI: 1.9–10.3), p = 0.006.

> "Rates of ABP CE failure increased with composite score quartile rank, with 3, 5, 7, and 19%, of residents failing the ABP CE in quartiles 1 (top) - 4 (bottom), respectively (p = 0.0005, χ2 for trend). Residents with composite scores in the bottom quartile were more likely to fail the ABP CE than residents in the top 3 quartiles combined [OR 4.6 (95% CI: 1.9–10.3), p = 0.006]."
