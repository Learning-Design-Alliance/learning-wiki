Evidence¶

Wang 2020¶

Wang, S., & Li, S. (2020). Calibration of Spanish MAP Growth math tests. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/calibration-of-spanish-map-growth-math-tests/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Paired sample t-test analysis of old versus new item RITs for the selected sample (Table 3.3), comparing "old and new item parameters across item calibration status". The XX-only pair (N = 2,762, mean difference -0.09) yielded t = -0.84, p = 0.40; the XX-and-10 pair (N = 3,900, mean difference -0.10) yielded t = -1.09, p = 0.28. No effect size is printed.

"All statistical test null hypotheses have been retained, and there are no statistically significant differences between old and new item parameters across item calibration status."
