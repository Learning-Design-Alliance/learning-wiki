## Evidence

### Sambeth A 2026

Sambeth A, Toth M, Blokland A (2026). Methylphenidate (Ritalin) does not improve exam performance in an experimental setting. Psychopharmacology 243(3):593–600. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00213-025-06864-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVAs on total points (max 72) and final grade in the same between-subjects experiment; printed means were 37.8 (PLA-PLA), 39.4 (MPH-PLA), 40.9 (PLA-MPH) points, and grades of 6.4, 6.6, and 7.0 respectively, with "no group differences were found" and Bayes factor 0.026.

> "When considering the total exam points and final grades, no group differences were found (see Fig. 3; F(2,72) = 0.70, p > 0.10, η2 = 0.02 and F(2,72) = 0.96, p > 0.10, η2 = 0.03, respectively)"
