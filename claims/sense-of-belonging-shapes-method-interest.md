## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Feeling encouraged and recognized enhances interest, while exclusion and lack of belonging (e.g., gender imbalance, not being taken seriously) contribute to low interest. [→ Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2023](#dina-zohrabi-alaee-2023)

## Evidence

### Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2023

Dina Zohrabi Alaee, Keegan Shea Tonry, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2023). Analyzing Physics Majors’ Specialization Low Interest Using Social Cognitive Career Theory. [doi:10.1119/perc.2023.pr.zohrabi_alaee](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2023.pr.zohrabi_alaee)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative finding from interviews: one student felt out of place in computation classes noting "a significant gender imbalance in computation classes", while peer and mentor support in a research setting led to a more positive experience; another felt excluded in theory classes.

> "Creating a sense of connectedness and being recognized by others through the different learning experiences can enhance interest in a particular method."
