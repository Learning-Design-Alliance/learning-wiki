Related Claims¶

  • RAN explained more unique variance in reading speed than phonological measures in English, French, German and Hungarian (16.7 to 22.8%), with Finnish the exception where all proximal factors contributed low, roughly equal amounts — related
  • Phonological awareness accounted for more unique variance in reading accuracy than RAN in the orthographies studied, while English was the only orthography where RAN added substantial unique variance (14.2%) and total explained variance was highest (39%) — related
  • T1 decoding precursors strongly predicted T2 decoding skills in French first-graders, explaining 75% of their variance — related
  • The direct effect of T1 decoding precursors on T2 reading comprehension held only when phonemic awareness and letter knowledge were both included, while RAN strengthened the prediction of decoding skills — related
