## Evidence

### Datu 2025

Datu, J. A. D., Sun, X. H., Liu, S., Pang, F., & Watt, H. (2025). Are gritty children more engaged in math? Triarchic model of grit, math self-efficacy beliefs, and math engagement in Chinese children in Macau. School Psychology International, 46(6), 582–602. https://doi.org/10.1177/01430343251386902

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling with 794 grade 4–5 students in two Macau primary schools, with grit dimensions as predictors, math self-efficacy as mediator, and age, gender, and conscientiousness as covariates. The article reports that "Only perseverance significantly predicted math self-efficacy beliefs"; no standardized coefficient for this path is printed in the text provided.

> "Only perseverance significantly predicted math self-efficacy beliefs, which partly supported Hypothesis 1."
