## Evidence

### Paul Justice 2026

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2026). Supporting physics instructors to use a variety of evidence-based approaches to improve student learning: An example from quantum mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.00329](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.00329)

`q2 · i1` · `design · r3`

In-class implementation in an upper-level undergraduate QM class (N=16) with pretest after traditional lecture and posttest after the CQS; Table 1 reports total pretest 31%, posttest 69%, normalized gain 0.54, effect size d = 0.35, with poor posttest scores on questions (b) and (d).

> "Table 1 also shows that students did not perform well on questions (b) and (d) even after engaging with the CQS."
