AlSheikh MH, Zaini R, ALmulhem MA and Ahmad S (2026). Mapping artificial intelligence integration in higher education: a systematic review using the FACETS and SAMR frameworks. Front. Educ. 11:1871468. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1871468

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of 22 studies of AI in undergraduate higher education, coded with the SAMR model during data extraction. The narrative synthesis reports most applications at "the Substitution and Augmentation tiers", with generative AI mainly replacing or enhancing existing tools and feedback mechanisms.

"When scrutinized through the SAMR model lens, most artificial intelligence applications were categorized within the Substitution and Augmentation tiers. For instance, generative AI was predominantly employed as a replacement for traditional writing tools or an enhancement to pre-existing feedback mechanisms."
