Evidence¶

Deliang Wang and Cunling Bian 2026¶

Deliang Wang and Cunling Bian. (2026). Scaffolding Critical Engagement with GenAI: Transforming Ethnic Minority Preparatory Students’ Collaborative Discourse in Prompt Engineering Tasks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28630 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.28630

q2 · i? · design · r2

Thematic analysis of reflective essays found students experienced the scaffolding not merely as technical training but as a challenge to blind trust in algorithmic outputs; many reported a shift toward seeing themselves as active evaluators or gatekeepers of AI-generated content rather than passive recipients.

"the teacher intervention—particularly the modeling with contrasting cases—functioned as an important turning point that reconfigured their collaborative norms with GenAI."

Discussion¶

The evidence is students' own account in reflective essays from one design study, analysed thematically; it records how students described the teacher's modelling with contrasting cases, not a measured change in how they checked AI output against a comparison group.
