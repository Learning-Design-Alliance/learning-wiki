University of Chicago School Mathematics Project 2016¶

University of Chicago School Mathematics Project. (2016). University of Chicago School Mathematics Project (UCSMP) — WWC Intervention Report, Secondary Mathematics. What Works Clearinghouse. https://ucsmp.uchicago.edu

q2 · i2 · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental matched-pairs study (Thompson et al., 2006) in three high schools comparing UCSMP Algebra (second edition) to non-UCSMP textbooks across six matched pairs of classrooms (189 students). The WWC confirmed a positive, statistically significant difference on the UCSMP Algebra Test, "after applying a correction for classroom-level clustering"; the printed effect size is 0.81 with improvement index +29.

"Thompson et al. (2006) found, and the WWC confirmed (after applying a correction for classroom-level clustering), one positive and statistically significant difference between the UCSMP Algebra and comparison groups in the general mathematics achievement domain."
