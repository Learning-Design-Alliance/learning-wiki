`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Simulation-based result the review describes from modelling of the amygdala and perirhinal cortex, where modelers reran simulations after selectively ablating potentiated cells, "effectively going back in time to determine whether they are necessary for learning".

> "when ‘engram’neurons are ablated, others emerge to support memory, highlighting the fact that competitive neuronal interactions underlie learning."
