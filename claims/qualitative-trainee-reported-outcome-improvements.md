Qualitative component of the mixed-methods trial: six focus group discussions and in-depth interviews analyzed by content analysis in ATLAS.ti. The article states "The respondents reported reduction in maternal and neonatal morbidity and mortality as common theme across states"; these are self-reports, not objectively measured patient outcomes.
