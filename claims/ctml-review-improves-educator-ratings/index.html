Within-subject study with 23 educators across three topics of differing cognitive demand; participants rated 13 items covering all 12 CTML principles. The article reports "The mean rating rose from 3.07 to 3.86 on a 5-point scale (+0.79, 𝑝<. 01)" with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests; no standardized effect size is printed for the overall gain.
