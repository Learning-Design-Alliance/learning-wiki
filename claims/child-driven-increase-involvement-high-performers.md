## Evidence

### Yi Yang 2025

Yi Yang. (2025). Reciprocal Relationships Between Parental Involvement and Academic Performance in Early Adolescence: A Two-Year Longitudinal Study in China. Journal of Youth and Adolescence, 54(4), 876–889. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-024-02102-7

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Child-driven cross-lagged paths in the CEPS reciprocal SEM. The article reports "β = 0.08" and "β = 0.06" as printed; both are below the study's small-effect benchmark of 0.03–0.07 range boundary and coded as very small positive effects. The direction contradicts the reactive hypothesis.

> "adolescents' academic performance in 7th grade predicted two aspects of parental involvement in 8th grade, namely, education discussion (β = 0.08, p < 0.001) and behavioral control (β = 0.06, p < 0.001)."
