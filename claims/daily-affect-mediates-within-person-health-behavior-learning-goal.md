## Evidence

### Flueckiger Lavinia 2014

Flueckiger Lavinia, Lieb Roselind, Meyer Andrea H., Mata Jutta. (2014). How Health Behaviors Relate to Academic Performance via Affect: An Intensive Longitudinal Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111080

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Within-person mediation models for each predictor–mediator–outcome triplet in 72 students over 32 days; sleep quality was fully mediated (direct effects non-significant), and physical activity was mediated via both affects with "positive affect mediated 27%, and negative affect 23%".

> "Forty-one percent of the effect of sleep quality on learning goal achievement was mediated by positive affect; 52% by negative affect."
