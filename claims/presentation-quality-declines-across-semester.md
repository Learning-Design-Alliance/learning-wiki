## Evidence

### Steven W. Tarr and Emily Alicea-Muñoz 2024

Steven W. Tarr and Emily Alicea-Muñoz. (2024). Reflecting to learn in a physics multimedia communication course. [doi:10.1119/perc.2024.pr.tarr](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2024.pr.tarr)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational analysis substituting number of peer evaluation forms completed for time as a proxy for observation experience. F23 correlations were ρ = −0.10 (control) and ρ = −0.26 (treatment); stronger negative correlations appeared in Sp24 (control ρ = −0.51, p = 0.04; treatment ρ = −0.52, p = 0.03), possibly owed to smaller sample size.

> "Spearman’s ρ indicates weak neg- ative correlations between presentation quality and comple- tion of control (ρ = −0.10 ± 0.18, p = 0.60) and treatment (ρ = −0.26 ± 0.18, p = 0.15) forms in F23."
