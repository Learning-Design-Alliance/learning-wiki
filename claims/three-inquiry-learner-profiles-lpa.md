## Evidence

### Gerdesmann 2026

Gerdesmann, D., Schwichow, M., & Koerber, S. (2026). Factors that explain simulation-based inquiry performance in physics learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09806-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The three profiles were pessimistic averagers (n = 103), stable performers (n = 87) with superior performance and skills, and optimistic strugglers (n = 42) with lower performance and skills but higher self-concept.

> "Profile 1 (red, n = 103), termed the pessimistic averagers, had average inquiry performance and cognitive skills but lower self-concept and higher extraneous load."
