The brief reports, citing a randomized experiment of a mobile phone-based adult education program in Los Angeles (Ksoll et al., 2014), that Cell-Ed learners using micro-modules of short audio lessons, reflection texts, and quizzes "gained the equivalent of two to three years of schooling in just four months." No effect size is printed.

> "Learners using the program gained the equivalent of two to three years of schooling in just four months using these micro-modules."
