## Evidence

### Limeri Lisa B. 2023

Limeri Lisa B., Carter Nathan T., Lyra Franchesca, Martin Joel, Mastronardo Halle, Patel Jay, Dolan Erin L. (2023). Undergraduate Lay Theories of Abilities: Mindset, universality, and brilliance beliefs uniquely predict undergraduate educational outcomes. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.22-12-0250

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM results using second-order factors as predictors in the phase-4 sample (n = 1396) show the pattern the authors summarize: "Sense of belonging, mastery-approach goals, and self-handicapping were each predicted by only mindset." They further report evaluative concern was predicted by all three lay theories, and intent to persist and course grades by mindset and universality.
