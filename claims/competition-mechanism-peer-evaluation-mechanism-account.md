Zhou Huiqin, Huang Liyuan, Du Yuanzhuo, Lio Jonathan, Luo Bin, Xiao Xingyuan, Yang Kun. (2025). Group competition mechanism in surgical robot training: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08080-z

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

This is the authors' Discussion interpretation, not a separately tested result: they argue grouped competition formed "a learning competitive relationship between groups", stimulating motivation and enabling self-reflection and peer guidance. No effect size is printed for this account.

> "Grouped competitive training helped students participate in the competition to form a learning competitive relationship between groups, thereby stimulating learning motivation [11]. Each change in participants during the operation reconstructed the knowledge of robot operation. Hence, self-reflection and peer guidance could be realized, thereby highlighting the superiority of the competitive mechanism."
