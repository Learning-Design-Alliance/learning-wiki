## Evidence

### Syväoja 2019

Syväoja, H. J., Kankaanpää, A., Joensuu, L., Kallio, J., Hakonen, H., Hillman, C. H., & Tammelin, T. H. (2019). The Longitudinal Associations of Fitness and Motor Skills with Academic Achievement. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise. https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002031

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-lagged path modeling in the 954-student cohort showed better motor skills at T2 predicted better GPA at T3 (B = 0.06, 99% CI = 0.00–0.11), but the equivalent prediction from T1 to T2 was not significant.

> "Better motor skills at T2 predicted better GPA at T3 (B = 0.06, 99% CI = 0.00–0.11, Fig. 2A; B = 0.06, 99% CI = 0.01–0.11, Fig. 2B). However, motor skills at T1 did not predict GPA at T2."
