Development article reporting a one-year co-design phase with a Swiss high school in which 69 students used the tool and qualitative data (group discussions, stimulated recall, individual interviews) evaluated implementation. The authors state they "demonstrated the feasibility of developing adaptive and portable systems" for practice.
