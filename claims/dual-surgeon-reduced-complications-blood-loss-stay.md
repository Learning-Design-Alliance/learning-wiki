## Evidence

### Wichmann M. W. 2025

Wichmann M. W., McCullough T. K., Gunning T., and Maddern G. J. (2025). Dual Surgeon Operating to Prevent Colorectal Cancer Surgery Skill Fatigue in Rural Surgery—A Prospective Audit of 154 Patients. ANZ Journal of Surgery 95, no. 10: 2060–2065. https://doi.org/10.1111/ans.70272

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective audit of 154 consecutive dual-surgeon colorectal cancer cases (Nov 2015–Feb 2025) benchmarked against a historic control of 233 patients from the same hospital before the approach was adopted. The Results section reports "a significant reduction in wound infections (3.2% vs. 12.4%, p = 0.0038)" alongside blood loss and stay reductions; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Compared to the control group (N = 233) there was a significant reduction in wound infections (3.2% vs. 12.4%, p = 0.0038), complications (24% vs. 40%, p = 0.02), intraoperative blood loss (60 vs. 250 mL, p < 0.001) and length of stay (6 vs. 8 days, p < 0.001)."
