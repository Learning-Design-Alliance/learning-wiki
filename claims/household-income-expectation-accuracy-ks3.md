### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of teacher expectation accuracy on weekly household income (reported at cohort member age four) at Key Stage 3 in the ALSPAC imputed sample. The article reports the lowest two income categories "underperformed teacher expectations at Key Stage 3 compared to those in the top category".

> "Children from families in the lowest two income categories also underperformed teacher expectations at Key Stage 3 compared to those in the top category £100–199 per week: − 0.12; 95% CI − 0.23, − 0.005; Less than £100 per week: − 0.17; 95% CI − 0.33, − 0.018)."
