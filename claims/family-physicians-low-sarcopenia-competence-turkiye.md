### Almatar Ahmed and Koçakoğlu Şenay 2025

Almatar Ahmed and Koçakoğlu Şenay. (2025). Addressing sarcopenia management in primary care in Türkiye: gaps in the field and training needs of family physicians. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1660782

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 405 family physicians across Türkiye using a 33-item online purpose-designed survey (April–June 2024). The self-assessment item yielded a mean of "3.37 ± 1.98", which the authors describe as "a generally low level of confidence in managing this condition". No effect size is printed.

> "Participants were asked to self-assess their competence in the management of sarcopenia on a scale ranging from 1 to 10. The mean self-rated competence score was 3.37 ± 1.98, indicating a generally low level of confidence in managing this condition"
