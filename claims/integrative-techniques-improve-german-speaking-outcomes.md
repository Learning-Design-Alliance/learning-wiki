## Evidence

### Litualy 2016

Litualy, S. J. (2016). Integrative Teaching Techniques and Improvement of German Speaking Learning Skills. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095832

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental single-group pre-test/post-test study of 20 students in the Sprechfertigkeit 2 course, analyzed with a t-test. The article reports "t count = 8.23 > t tabel = 1.73" at α = 0.05, df = 18, and concludes a significant influence of integrative teaching techniques on speaking outcomes.

> "From the results of data analyzed, the result of learning t = 8.23 with T table at significant level α = 0.05 and df = 18 obtained t table = 1.73. The results showed that t count > t tabel (t count = 8.23 > t tabel = 1.73)."
