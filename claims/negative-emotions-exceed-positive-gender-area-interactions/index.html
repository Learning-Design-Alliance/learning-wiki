q3 · i? · associational · r2

Linear mixed model on N = 16517 with gender, area of study, and country as between-subject factors and valence as within-subject: "Positive emotions (M = 2.81, SD = 0.82, 95% CI [2.80, 2.83]) were lower than negative emotions (M = 2.98, SD = 0.79, 95% CI [2.96, 2.99])".

"The LMM revealed a significant effect of valence, Χ 2(1, N = 16517) = 268.784, p < .001: Positive emotions (M = 2.81, SD = 0.82, 95% CI [2.80, 2.83]) were lower than negative emotions (M = 2.98, SD = 0.79, 95% CI [2.96, 2.99])"
