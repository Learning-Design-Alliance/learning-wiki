## Evidence

### Moezzi Masoumeh 2024

Moezzi Masoumeh, Rasekh Sara, Zare Elahe, Karimi Masoud. (2024). Evaluating clinical communication skills of medical students, assistants, and professors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-05015-4

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis in the cross-sectional survey; Table 3 prints r = -0.246* (assistants) and r = -0.258 (externs), both p < 0.05. The article states "the more negative attitude about the importance of communication skills, the weaker communication performance was reported."

> "In the groups of assistants and externs, significant negative correlations were seen between negative attitude and communication performance, which means that the more negative attitude about the importance of communication skills, the weaker communication performance was reported."
