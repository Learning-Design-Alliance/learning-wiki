## Evidence

### Heidarian H 2025

Heidarian H, Mehrabi M, Ghaemmaghami P, Janghorban R. (2025). Effect of E-learning clinical management of substance-dependent pregnant women on the knowledge and clinical skill performance of midwives: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-024-07130-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA in a blocked-randomized controlled trial of 93 midwives (intervention n = 46, control n = 47) assessed with a 40-item researcher-made knowledge questionnaire; Greenhouse-Geisser correction was used. Table 2 reports the between-group Cohen's d as 3.34 immediately after and 2.69 one month after the intervention. The article reports "a significant difference in the mean knowledge score between the control and intervention groups".

> "There was a significant difference in the mean knowledge score between the control and intervention groups, all three times before, immediately after, and one month after the intervention (P < .001)."
