## Evidence

### Günther V 2025

Günther V, Nees F, Maass N, von Otte S, Ruchay Z, Pape J, Ackermann J, Alkatout I. (2025). How effective and sustainable is proctoring in robotic surgery? A retrospective analysis based on interviews with surgeons. Surgical Endoscopy 39(3):1985–1995. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11503-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison of learning-needs ratings across three robotic-surgery frequency groups in the 40-surgeon telephone interview study; the Results report the twice-a-week vs once-a-week contrast with p = 0.0019. No effect size is printed.

> "Surgeons who performed robotic surgery more often (specifically twice a week) rated the duration of proctoring as being more appropriate in terms of fulfilling their learning needs than those who performed robotic surgery less often (once a week) (p = 0.0019)."
