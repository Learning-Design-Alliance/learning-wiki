# Under general reading instructions, pre-service teachers significantly overestimate their application test performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In the pretest phase of Experiment 1, students showed statistically significant overconfidence regarding application performance bias for both texts (CLT d = 1.12; CVT d = 0.49). [→ Knellesen 2024](#knellesen-2024)

## Evidence

### Knellesen 2024

Knellesen, J., Händel, M., & Golke, S. (2024). Effects of reading instructions on pre-service teachers' judgment bias when learning from texts. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09371-w

`q3 · i3`

Pretest phase of Experiment 1 with general reading instructions only. One-sample t-tests against zero on application performance bias showed "a statistically significant overconfidence regarding students’ application performance bias for both texts", with d = 1.12 (CLT) and d = 0.49 (CVT) as printed.

> "Results showed a statistically significant overconfidence regarding students’ application performance bias for both texts, CLT: t(63) = 8.96, p < .001, d = 1.12 and CVT: t(65) = 4.03, p < .001, d = 0.49."
