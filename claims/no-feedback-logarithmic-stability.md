## Evidence

### Opfer 2007

Opfer, J. E., & Siegler, R. S. (2007). Representational change and children's numerical estimation. Cognitive Psychology 55 (2007) 169–195. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2006.09.002

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Control condition of Experiment 2, in which children completed the same estimation trials without feedback. The stability of the logarithmic pattern (d = .78) shows the pretest assessment reflected a stable representation and rules out regression to the mean for feedback effects.

> "Children in the no-feedback group continued to generate estimates that Wt the logarithmic function better than the linear one (log R2 D .90, lin R2 D .61, t [21] D 2.78, p < .05, d D .78)"
