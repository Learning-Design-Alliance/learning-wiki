## Evidence

### Kao 2013

Kao, C.-W. (2013). Effects of Focused Feedback on the Acquisition of Two English Articles. The Electronic Journal for English as a Second Language, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1007571

`q2 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Author's synthesis across the meta-analysis: focused feedback on English article errors produced large delayed post-test effects (d = 0.814 direct correction; d = 0.879 metalinguistic explanation), described as "a large beneficial effect on students' long-term learning".

> "We can state that when correction is targeted at a specified error type, correction has a large beneficial effect on students' long-term learning."
