The introduction states this as the authors' hypothesis motivating the simulation design; the article reports no empirical test of whether students "gain a greater physical intuition of the resonance concept", so the claim rests on the stated rationale alone.

> "allowing students to visualize the effects of an array of equal spring-masses being acted on by increasing driving frequencies plotted on the traditional "amplitude versus driving frequency" resonance graph, students will gain a greater physical intuition of the resonance concept."
