## Evidence

### Gerit Wagner 2025

Gerit Wagner, Mickaël Ringeval, Louis Raymond, Guy Paré. (2025). Digital health competences and AI beliefs as conditions for the practice of evidence-based medicine: a study of prospective physicians in Canada. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2459910

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

PLS-SEM analysis of the 177-student survey found "dHealth experiential competences were correlated with the perceived role of AI in the future of medicine (β = 0.39, p < 0.001)"; knowledge competences had no significant effect on AI perceptions.

> "Additionally, dHealth experiential competences were correlated with the perceived role of AI in the future of medicine (β = 0.39, p < 0.001), suggesting that a deeper understanding of dHealth may foster more optimistic views on the potential of AI in medicine."
