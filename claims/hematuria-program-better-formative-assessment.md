### Peng Shuang 2026

Peng Shuang, Wei Yongbao, Dai Caixia, Liang Yuxuan, Huang Li. (2026). Impact of a vertically integrated, symptom-oriented teaching program for hematuria management on clinical competency transfer among urology clerkship students. BMC Medical Education, 26, Article 1348. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09680-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-group Welch t-test comparisons of formative evaluations in the quasi-experimental cohort. The mean weekly test score difference was more stable (94.09 ± 2.58 vs. 86.50 ± 8.10, P < 0.001).

### Peng Shuang 2026 (2)

Peng Shuang, Wei Yongbao, Dai Caixia, Liang Yuxuan, Huang Li. (2026). Impact of a vertically integrated, symptom-oriented teaching program for hematuria management on clinical competency transfer among urology clerkship students. BMC Medical Education, 26, Article 1348. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09680-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comprehensive rotation evaluation comparisons in the same quasi-experimental cohort. The intervention group had a slightly higher case presentation score (7.39 ± 0.60 vs. 7.14 ± 0.46), while there was no statistical difference in the end-of-rotation comprehensive score.

> "the intervention group had a slightly higher case presentation score (7.39 ± 0.60 vs. 7.14 ± 0.46, P = 0.0446). Meanwhile, there was no statistical difference in the end-of-rotation comprehensive score (92.2 ± 8.55 vs. 92.75 ± 8.28, P = 0.7709)"
