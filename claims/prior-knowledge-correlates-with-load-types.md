## Evidence

### Krieglstein 2025

Krieglstein, F., Beege, M., Wesenberg, L., Rey, G. D., & Schneider, S. (2025). The Distorting Influence of Primacy Effects on Reporting Cognitive Load in Learning Materials of Varying Complexity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09980-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Correlational analysis in the main study between self-assessed prior knowledge (M = 3.03, SD = 1.12 on a 7-point scale) and delayed cognitive load measures (Table 3): "negatively correlated with ICL and ECL, but positively correlated with GCL"; printed values r = −0.24 (ICL), r = −0.28 (ECL), r = 0.31 (GCL).

> "the prior knowledge measure demonstrated satisfactory criterion validity, as it was negatively correlated with ICL and ECL, but positively correlated with GCL, which is consistent with meta-analytic findings (Krieglstein et al., 2022a)."
