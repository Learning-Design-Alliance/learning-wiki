## Evidence

### de Lotbiniere-Bassett M 2022

de Lotbiniere-Bassett M, Volpato Batista A, Lai C, El Chemaly T, Dort J, Blevins N, Lui J. (2022). The user experience design of a novel microscope within SurgiSim, a virtual reality surgical simulator. Int J Comput Assist Radiol Surg. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-022-02727-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Supplemental usability testing with four participants (two graduate students, one professor, one Neurotology Fellow; two microscope naive, one expert) collected feedback at the end of VR microscope testing. All "found the appearance of the microscope to be realistic" and were satisfied with the graphics; no appearance updates were needed.

> "Based on the feedback collected at the end of the VR microscope testing, all of the participants found the appearance of the microscope to be realistic. All of the participants consistently indicated that they were satisfied with graphics within the VR environment."
