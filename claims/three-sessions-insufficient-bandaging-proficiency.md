Study 2's post-test analysis of the 52 students showed that across both instruction groups only a small proportion achieved bandage pressure within the desired range in the retention and transfer tests, which the authors read as three sessions being insufficient for proficiency.

> "However, across both groups, only a small proportion of students in Study 2 successfully applied the bandage within the desired pressure range in the retention and transfer tests. This suggests that three sessions may not be sufficient to achieve proficiency in bandaging."
