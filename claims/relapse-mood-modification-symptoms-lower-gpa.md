## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Aquino-Blanco A 2025

Aquino-Blanco A, Quiroz-Cárdenas F, Montenegro-Espinosa JA, Martín-Calvo N, Yañéz-Sepúlveda R, Gutiérrez-Espinoza H, Olivares-Arancibia J, Rahmati M, Stubbs B, Smith L, López-Gil JF, Jiménez-López E. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-025-02180-8

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Symptom-level generalized linear models in the 583-adolescent EHDLA sample using dichotomous SNAddS-6S items, adjusted for the same covariates. The article reports significant negative associations for relapse and mood modification; Table 2 shows salience, tolerance, withdrawal, and conflict were not significant.

> "With respect to addictive behavior symptoms, a significant negative association was found between GPA and relapse (p = 0.019; β = -0.10) and mood modification (p = 0.008; β = -0.11)."
