## Evidence

### Margunayasa 2019

Margunayasa, I G., Dantes, N., Marhaeni, A.A.I.N., & Suastra, I W. (2019). The Effect of Guided Inquiry Learning and Cognitive Style on Science Learning Achievement. International Journal of Instruction, 12(1), 737-750. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA in a quasi-experimental 2×2 factorial study of fifth graders (experimental n=132, control n=107) in Singaraja City; the main effect of teaching model was significant, with guided inquiry mean 23.93 vs conventional 22.35. The article reports "F = 13.27 and p<0.05".

> "there is a significant difference in science learning achievement between the students who learned through guided inquiry learning model and those who learned through conventional teaching ( F = 13.27 and p<0.05)"
