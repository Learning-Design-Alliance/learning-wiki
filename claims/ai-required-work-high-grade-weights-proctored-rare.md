Feature analysis of 14 conventional percentage-based courses (Table 4): AI-required work and programming work each appeared in 14 courses with median weight 70% (ranges 20–100% and 20–95%); proctored assessment appeared in 3 courses with median 20%. Weights are non-mutually exclusive.
