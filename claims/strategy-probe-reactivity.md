## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Probed subjects showed faster RT improvement than nonprobed subjects, with RT curves diverging by approximately 800 ms at peak, indicating the shift to retrieval occurred earlier for probed subjects. [→ Rickard 2004](#rickard-2004)

## Evidence

### Rickard 2004

Rickard, T. C. (2004). Strategy execution in cognitive skill learning: An item-level test of candidate models. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition, 30(1), 65-82. https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.30.1.65

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Probed and nonprobed subjects had similar initial RTs but showed "a large divergence in the RT curves that peaks at around 800 ms on Block 25," indicating probed subjects shifted to retrieval earlier. Faster accuracy growth for probed subjects corroborated this.

> "Reactivity is clearly indicated. Although probed and nonprobed subjects had similar RTs at the beginning of practice, there is a large divergence in the RT curves that peaks at around 800 ms on Block 25"
