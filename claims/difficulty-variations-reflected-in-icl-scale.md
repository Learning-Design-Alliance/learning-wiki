# Variations in task difficulty are reflected in the intrinsic cognitive load scale, with boredom conditions reporting the lowest ICL

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In the quiz study, participants in the boredom condition reported significantly lower ICL than participants in the flow or overload conditions. [→ Melina Klepsch 2020](#melina-klepsch-2020)

## Evidence

### Melina Klepsch 2020

Melina Klepsch, Tina Seufert. (2020). Understanding instructional design effects by differentiated measurement of intrinsic, extraneous, and germane cognitive load. Instructional Science (2020) 48:45–77. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09502-9

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-subject experiment (N = 62) in which quiz question difficulty was varied across boredom, flow, and overload conditions and ICL measured with the differentiated questionnaire. The article reports a significant main effect (F(2,59) = 8.76, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.23) and "significantly lower ICL" in the boredom condition.

> "Contrasts showed that ICL was lowest for participants in the boredom condition. They reported significantly lower ICL than participants in the flow (T(59) = 3.40, p < 0.01, d = 1.24) or overload (T(59) = 3.84, p < 0.001, d = 1.32) conditions."
