Chiara Marcoccia 2026 (2)¶

Chiara Marcoccia, Walter Quattrociocchi, Valerio Capraro. (2026). AI advice suppresses people's willingness to say "I don't know", even when the advice is wrong and accuracy is incentivized. https://osf.io/nwx6r/overview?view_only=a03ae0ca587c4c84a442c44d55ab97b6

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Study 3 (N = 843) made incentives salient by reminding participants of the stakes before every question and dropped confidence elicitation. Stakes "significantly increased judgment suspension" in both AI conditions; no effect size printed.

"Unlike Study 2, monetary stakes here significantly increased judgment suspension, both when AI advice was unavailable (0.32 vs. 0.41; b = 0.089, SE = 0.039, t = 2.296, p = .022) and when it was available (0.02 vs. 0.08; b = 0.062, SE = 0.016, t = 3.911, p < .001)."
