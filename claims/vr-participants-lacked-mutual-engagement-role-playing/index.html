Ke 2021¶

Ke, F., Dai, Z., Pachman, M. et al. (2021). Exploring multiuser virtual teaching simulation as an alternative learning environment for student instructors. Instr Sci, 49, 831–854. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09555-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The study's qualitative data revealed a limitation of the VR condition: "VR participants lacked mutual engagement in collaborative role-playing." This contrasts with the participatory, collaborative character of live classroom role-play. No quantitative engagement measure is reported in the available text.

"However, VR participants lacked mutual engagement in collaborative role-playing."
