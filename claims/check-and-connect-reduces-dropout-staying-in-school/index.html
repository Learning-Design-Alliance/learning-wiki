Dropout Prevention intervention report: Check & Connect 2015¶

Dropout Prevention intervention report: Check & Connect. (2015). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/78

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial of 94 Minneapolis ninth-graders with learning, emotional, or behavioral disabilities; both groups received Check & Connect in grades 7-8 but only the intervention group continued in ninth grade. The table reports an effect size of 0.89 and improvement index +31, p < .01, for dropout at end of first year after random assignment.

"ninth-grade students enrolled in Check & Connect were statistically significantly less likely than similar comparison group students to have dropped out of school by the end of ninth grade"

Dropout Prevention intervention report: Check & Connect 2015 (2)¶

Dropout Prevention intervention report: Check & Connect. (2015). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/78

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Replication RCT with 144 ninth-graders with emotional or behavioral disabilities in seven Minneapolis high schools, assigned at the family level; intervention students received Check & Connect throughout high school. The WWC table row for dropout at end of fourth year reports "19 0.47 +18 < .01" — mean difference 19 percentage points, effect size 0.47, improvement index +18, p < .01.
