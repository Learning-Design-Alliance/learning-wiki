Andrew Ash and John Hu 2026¶

Andrew Ash and John Hu. (2026). WIP: Chat-Debugging: Large Language Model as a Hardware Debugging Assistant. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02420 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.02420

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview data from the single-participant case study. Daniel agreed the assistant reduced frustration, trusted it to research malfunctioning components, and reported more confidence "particularly those where he lacked personal experience," mainly on the software integration side.

"Daniel also found that using an LLM-assistant gave him more conﬁdence in debugging tasks, particularly those where he lacked personal experience."
