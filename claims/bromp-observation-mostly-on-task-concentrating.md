## Evidence

### Li 2019

Li, L., Maher, B., Weiser, G., Orenstein, A., Chow, E., & Atienza, S. (2019). Hero Elementary playlist pilot study in after-school programs. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED608115

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational study using the BROMP protocol across four sessions; researchers coded behavior (on task, conversation, off task, undefined) and affect (concentrating, bored, confused, frustrated, undefined). Earlier sessions showed 83–94% on-task, concentrating behavior; Session 4 off-task behavior was attributed to notebook repetition.

> "In Session 4, students were far more frequently off task, with only 52% of recorded observations (128 total) showing on-task behavior."
