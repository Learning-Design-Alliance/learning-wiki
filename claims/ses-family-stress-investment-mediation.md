### Tan 2024

Tan, C. Y. (2024). Socioeconomic Status and Student Learning: Insights from an Umbrella Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09929-3

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The umbrella review's thematic analysis identifies mediating processes including the family stress model (economic stress affecting parenting in low-SES families) and the family investment model (high-SES parents' learning support), drawn from reviews such as Bradley and Corwyn (2002).

> "it reviews the extant literature to identify different processes that mediate SES effects on student learning, thereby elucidating how economic stress affects parenting in low-SES families (family stress model) and what high-SES parents do to support their children’s learning (family investment model)."
