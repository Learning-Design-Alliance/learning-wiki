## Evidence

### Styles 2014

Styles, B., Clarkson, R., & Fowler, K. (2014). Chatterbooks: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581155

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process evaluation based on observations of intervention sessions, telephone interviews with four deliverers, review of training materials and tutor logs. Deliverers reported that time spent managing behaviour meant not all required activities could be delivered, and some pupils saw sessions as a 'doss session'.

> "From the observations and interviews with staff in the process evaluation, a lack of engagement and poor behaviour spoiled the delivery of some sessions. This may have contributed to the lack of impact in the intervention."
