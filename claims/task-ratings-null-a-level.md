## Evidence

### Ginty 2022

Ginty, A. T., Tyra, A. T., Young, D. A., Brindle, R. C., de Rooij, S. R. & Williams, S. E. (2022). Cardiovascular reactions to acute psychological stress and academic achievement. Psychophysiology, 59, e14064. https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.14064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlation analysis in the prospective sample. Participants reported being generally engaged (M = 4.10), finding the task difficult (M = 4.23), and stressful (M = 4.53), yet none of these ratings related to A-Level scores.

> "Similarly, there were no associations between self‐reported difficulty, stress, or engagement ratings of the acute psychological laboratory stress task with total A‐Level performance."
