Cross-sectional survey of 434 practicing Chinese orthodontists; within-scale comparisons of the four competency domains used Friedman tests with Nemenyi post-hoc comparisons. The article reports "significant overall differences among the four domains" on all three scales, and post-hoc tests confirmed TMJ and myofunctional domains scored significantly lower than dental and periodontal domains.
