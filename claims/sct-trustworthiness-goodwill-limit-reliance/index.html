Serpil, H., & Mor, M. K. (2026). Bounded reliance: A source credibility perspective on EFL students' engagement with AI-generated writing feedback. Journal of Language Pedagogy and Linguistics, 1(1), 1-27. https://doi.org/10.0.20.161/zenodo.22828386

Authors' synthesis of the three themes through Source Credibility Theory. GROK was perceived as expert but less trustworthy given data privacy concerns and lower in goodwill, lacking care and contextual understanding students associated with a human reader.

"the study extends Source Credibility Theory to AI-generated feedback, showing that learners apply to an algorithmic provider the same multidimensional source judgments the theory was developed to explain for human communicators"
