Descriptive analysis of the researcher-designed 21-item Likert questionnaire plus two open-ended questions administered after the delayed post-test: 82% found the system "enjoyable and very easy to use", 90% reported no significant technical problems, and 70% felt encouraged to take responsibility for their own learning.
