`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

From the same longitudinal predictive-validity analysis (Tartas et al., 22). The article reports variance-explained R² values from F-tests; no standardised effect size is printed, so impact is uncoded. SOC also predicted life satisfaction (R² = 0.40) and self-reported income (R² = 0.54).

> "Sense of coherence at admission accounted for 40% of variance in career satisfaction 4 years post-graduation [F(11,43) = 2.60, p < 0.05], 16% of variance in burnout [F(1,54) = 10.66, p < 0.01], and 88% of variance in general wellbeing [F(12,42) = 7.03, p < 0.01]."
