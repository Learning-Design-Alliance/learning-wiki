## Evidence

### González-Abós C 2026

González-Abós C, Reyes LG, Lorenzo C, Ausania F. (2026). MyIntuitive telemetry identifies proficiency thresholds in robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12591-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational study of 100 consecutive RPD attempts by a single surgeon with prospectively collected da Vinci Xi telemetry. The primary endpoint comparison between phases was significant: "fell from 42 to 32 min between the initial (cases 1–60) and proficiency (> 60) phases (P = 0.001)".

> "Median Full-Case idle time, the primary endpoint, was 38 min (IQR 27–47) and fell from 42 to 32 min between the initial (cases 1–60) and proficiency (> 60) phases (P = 0.001)"
