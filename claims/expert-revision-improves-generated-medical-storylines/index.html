Expert-in-the-loop refinement study on 10 low-scoring generated story drafts per model from Gemini-3-Pro and Qwen3.5-27B∗; experts marked targeted revision regions, averaging 8.3 and 8.9 regions per sample. No effect size printed.

"As shown in Table 16, full expert revision substan- tially improves both medical accuracy and educa- tional quality: for Gemini, the average Medical Accuracy / Educational scores increase from 6.40 / 6.45 to 8.07 / 7.95; for Qwen3.5-27B∗, they in- crease from 6.20 / 6.35 to 7.87 / 7.75."
