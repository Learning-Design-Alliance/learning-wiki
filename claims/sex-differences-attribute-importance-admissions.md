## Evidence

### Conlon Peter 2012

Conlon Peter, Hecker Kent, Sabatini Susan. (2012). What should we be selecting for? A systematic approach for determining which personal characteristics to assess for during admissions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-105

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

MANOVA on paired-comparison attribute scores from the survey (overall Wilk's Lambda = .93, F(11,1279) = 8.45, p < .001) with univariate follow-ups. Females scored communication and empathy higher; males scored knowledge of profession, leadership and sound judgment higher, with small effect sizes as printed (d = .26, d = 0.42, d = 0.20).

> "Females scored communication (F(1, 1289) = 20.24, p < .001, d = .26) and empathy (F(1, 1289) = 55.41, p < .001, d = 0.42) significantly higher than males, while males scored knowledge of profession (F(1, 1289) = 12.81, p < .001, d = 0.20)"
