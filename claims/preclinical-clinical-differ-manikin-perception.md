### Nauman Ammarah Muhammad 2025

Nauman Ammarah Muhammad, Mohiuddin Sidra, Panhwar Maryam, Altaf Yousra. (2025). Exploring the frontier of dental education: a cross-sectional study of VR simulation and manikin-based training at Ziauddin university. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07221-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 229 BDS students; independent t-test compared preclinical (n=90) and clinical (n=139) groups' manikin perception scores, reported as "statistically significant" with mean difference -1.29 (95% CI -2.93, 0.35).

> "There is a statistically significant difference in perception of dental manikin training among pre-clinical and clinical students (p < 0.001, 95% CI -2.93, 0.35)."
