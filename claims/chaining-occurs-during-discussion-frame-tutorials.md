## Evidence

### Conlin 2006

Conlin, L. D., Gupta, A., Scherr, R. E., & Hammer, D. (2006). The Dynamics of Students' Behaviors and Reasoning during Collaborative Physics Tutorial Sessions. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings record. https://www.compadre.org/PER/ [doi:10.1063/1.2820949](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2820949)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational coding of 6 tutorial video clips from 24 students, with behavior modes and mechanistic reasoning matched per 5 seconds. The authors report that chaining codes were "disproportionately high" in the discussion frame, indicating the nature of reasoning differs by frame.

> "The number of codes for chaining in particular is disproportionately high: nearly all of the chaining occurred during the discussion frame (81%), which shows that the nature of student reasoning is different during this frame."
