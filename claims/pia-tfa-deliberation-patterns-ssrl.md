# Two distinct group deliberation patterns emerge in response to regulation triggers: the Plan and Implementation Approach (PIA) and the Trials and Failure Approach (TFA)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Cluster analysis of 42 deliberation sequences identified two types: Type 1 (PIA, 15 sequences) with balanced cognitive, metacognitive and task-execution interactions, and Type 2 (TFA, 27 sequences) with higher metacognitive engagement. [→ Belle Dang 2024](#belle-dang-2024)

## Evidence

### Belle Dang 2024

Belle Dang, Andy Nguyen and Sanna Järvelä. (2024). Deliberative Interactions for Socially Shared Regulation in Collaborative Learning: An AI-Driven Learning Analytics Study. Journal of Learning Analytics, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8393

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Agglomerative hierarchical clustering of 42 three-minute deliberation sequences (from 1,894 coded utterances) revealed two cluster types. Type 1 (PIA) contained 15 sequences and Type 2 (TFA) 27 sequences, with "Type 1 consists of 15 sequences and Type 2 consists of 27 sequences."

> "The optimal clustering results generated from Layer 2 reveal two types of deliberative characteristics. Figure 3(a) illustrates the AHC results of a hierarchical tree cluster of deliberation sequences. Type 1 consists of 15 sequences and Type 2 consists of 27 sequences."
