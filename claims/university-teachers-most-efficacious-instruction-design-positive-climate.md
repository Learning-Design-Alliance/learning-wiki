### Douwes‐van Ark 2026

Douwes‐van Ark, I. M. E., Fokkens‐Bruinsma, M., Deinum, J. F., & Korpershoek, H. (2026). University teachers' self‐efficacy beliefs. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70088

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross‐sectional survey of 472 university teachers completing professional development programmes at Dutch universities; descriptive domain means from Table 2 show instruction/design highest (M = 4.22, SD = .46) and "creating a positive learning climate" (M = 4.06, SD = .50) above the other domains.

> "The descriptive statistics (Table 2) reveal that university teachers felt efficacious in the six teaching domains. Teachers felt most efficacious in instruction/design and creating a positive learning climate. Their scores on assessment, student engagement, professional development and differentiation were slightly lower."
