Herz Marco M., Schamuhn Judith, Krumm Boris, Bartha Valentin. (2025). Student-performed periodontal therapy: retrospective cohort study on outcomes and related recommendations for enhancing undergraduate periodontal education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07699-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons across T0, T1 and T2 in the retrospective cohort study of 107 student-treated patients. The article reports "only the PCR deteriorated statistically significantly (p < 0.001)" between T1 and T2, with mean PCR rising from 40% at T1 to 49% at T2.

> "However, the changes between T1 and T2 were mostly not statistically significant, only the PCR deteriorated statistically significantly (p < 0.001); see Table 2."
