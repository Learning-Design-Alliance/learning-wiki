# The only significant curriculum-by-semester interaction on SRL skills is a large effect on evaluation, driven by a decrease in the lecture-based curriculum rather than growth in the PBL curriculum

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` The interaction effect of semester and curriculum was significant only for evaluation, with a large η2 of .256. [→ Lucieer Susanna M. 2016](#lucieer-susanna-m-2016)

## Evidence

### Lucieer Susanna M. 2016

Lucieer Susanna M., van der Geest Jos N., Elói-Santos Silvana M., de Faria Rosa M. Delbone, Jonker Laura, Visscher Chris, Rikers Remy M. J. P., Themmen Axel P. N. (2016). The development of self-regulated learning during the pre-clinical stage of medical school: a comparison between a lecture-based and a problem-based curriculum. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 21, 93-104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9613-1

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA general linear model testing the semester-by-curriculum interaction on each of the six SRL-SRS subscales in the cross-sectional sample of 384 students; only evaluation reached significance, with the article printing "η2 = .256" as a large effect.

> "The only, large, significant interaction effect was found on the skill evaluation, F(1382) = 6.718, p = .010, η2 = .256."

### Lucieer Susanna M. 2016 (2)

Lucieer Susanna M., van der Geest Jos N., Elói-Santos Silvana M., de Faria Rosa M. Delbone, Jonker Laura, Visscher Chris, Rikers Remy M. J. P., Themmen Axel P. N. (2016). The development of self-regulated learning during the pre-clinical stage of medical school: a comparison between a lecture-based and a problem-based curriculum. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 21, 93-104. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9613-1

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Follow-up ANOVA within the LB curriculum comparing second-semester (n=111) and sixth-semester (n=139) students shows evaluation dropped from 33.7 to 31.5, with "η2 = .059" (medium by the article's own thresholds); the PBL curriculum's evaluation did not significantly change.

> "this effect is caused by a decrease in the subscale evaluation in the LB curriculum F(1249) = 15.506, p < 001, η2 = .059"
