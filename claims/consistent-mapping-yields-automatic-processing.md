## Evidence

### Kellman 2009

Kellman, P. J., & Garrigan, P. (2009). Perceptual Learning and Human Expertise. Physics of Life Reviews, 6(2), 53-84. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2008.12.001

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Schneider & Shiffrin (1977), a series of studies in which subjects judged whether letters in a target set appeared in arrays; consistent mapping produced load-insensitive performance labeled automatic processing.

> "For subjects trained with a consistent mapping between targets and distractors (i.e., an item could appear as one or the other but not both), subjects not only became much more efficient in doing the task, but they came to perform the task equally well over a range of target set sizes and array sizes."
