# Post-test completion time was significantly shorter in the AI-assisted training group than in the conventional group

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students trained with AI assistance completed the post-test significantly faster than conventionally trained students (p = 0.016, Cohen's d = 0.55). [→ Alkattan Rana 2026](#alkattan-rana-2026)

## Evidence

### Alkattan Rana 2026

Alkattan Rana, Alreshaid Lulwah, Alfallaj Hayam, Pillai Arun Gopinathan, Alqahtani Hussam M., Suhaym Omar, Almohefer Sami, Madfa Ahmed A. (2026). Artificial intelligence-assisted training for proximal caries diagnosis by undergraduate dental students: a randomized experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09982-2

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experimental study measuring completion time in minutes for 79 students. Post-test completion time was significantly shorter in the AI group (p = 0.016, d = 0.55); no pre-test group difference was found.

> "Regarding completion time, no significant differences were found between study groups in the pre-test; however, in the post-test, completion time was significantly shorter in the AI group than in the conventional group (p = 0.016)"
