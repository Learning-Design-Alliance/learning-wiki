Gregor Benz, Sarah Taha, Andreas Vorholzer. (2026). Data evaluation processes of different-sized datasets: an eye-tracking with concurrent think-aloud study. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.31045](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.31045)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of think-aloud and written-justification data from the N = 20 study: small-dataset evaluation showed frequent uncertainty references and an articulated "need for data", with participants compensating by assigning greater interpretive weight to individual observations. The authors state this mechanism is a hypothesis, not directly supported by their data.

> "when evaluating the small dataset, partici- pants were often less confident due to the limited num- ber of data points, as reflected in frequent references to aspects of uncertainty, and they articulated a "need for data.""
