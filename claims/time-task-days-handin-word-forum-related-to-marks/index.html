Evidence¶

Cerezo 2016¶

Cerezo, R., Sanchez-Santillan, M., Paule-Ruiz, M. P., & Núñez, J. C. (2016). Students' LMS interaction patterns and their relationship with achievement: A case study in higher education. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2016.02.006

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of Moodle log variables against final marks in a sample of 140 undergraduates; the article reports "time task (r = .447, p <.001 d = 1)" and "days “hand in ” (r = -.352, p< .001 d = 0.75)" and "word forum (r = .195, p <.01 d = 0.41)".

"the three variables significantly related with Final Marks are time task (r = .447, p <.001 d = 1), with a large effect size; followed by days “hand in ” (r = -.352, p< .001 d = 0.75), with a moderate -high effect size; and word forum (r = .195, p <.01 d = 0.41), with an almost medium effect size."
