## Evidence

### Hsiao 2022

Hsiao, J. H., An, J., Hui, V. K. S., Zheng, Y. & Chan, A. B. (2022). Understanding the role of eye movement consistency in face recognition and autism through integrating deep neural networks and hidden Markov models. npj Science of Learning 7:28. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00139-6

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Human comparison study of 22 children with ASD and 22 age- and IQ-matched TD children performing the face recognition task. "the ASD children ... had significantly higher overall entropy than TD children", printed effect size d = 0.68.

> "the ASD children (M = 10.13, SD = 0.58) had significantly higher overall entropy than TD children (M = 9.74, SD = 0.55), t(42) = 2.24, p = 0.015, d = 0.68, 95% CI = [0.06, 1.28]"

### Hsiao 2022 (2)

Hsiao, J. H., An, J., Hui, V. K. S., Zheng, Y. & Chan, A. B. (2022). Understanding the role of eye movement consistency in face recognition and autism through integrating deep neural networks and hidden Markov models. npj Science of Learning 7:28. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00139-6

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same study: the group comparison on eye movement pattern (EN scale) was not significant; equivalence was not tested. The article notes ASD and TD groups also did not differ in AQ Attention to Detail.

> "they (M = 0.005, SD = 0.04) did not have lower EN scale than TD children (M = 0.16, SD = 0.03), t(42) = −1.06, p = 0.147, d = −0.32, 95% CI = [−0.91, 0.27]"
