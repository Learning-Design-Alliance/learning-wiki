Evidence¶

Zeller 2013¶

Zeller, P. J., Carpenter, S., Lacefield, W. E., & Applegate, E. B. (2013). Graduation coaching in a rural district school. GEAR UP Learning Centers, Western Michigan University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1033208

q3 · i?

Planned contrast at the middle-school-to-high-school transition comparing coached versus uncoached at-risk students' GPA trajectories. The contrast was "highly significant" and located the effect at the start of coaching.

"This also was highly significant (Wald χ2 df=1 = 22.84, p < .0001) and showed (1) that the trends for at-risk students who were coached and those who were not coached go in different directions and (2) that this effect begins at the onset of coaching."
