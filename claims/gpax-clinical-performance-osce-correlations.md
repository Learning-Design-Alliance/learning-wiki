## Evidence

### Tawanwongsri W 2019

Tawanwongsri W, Phenwan T. (2019). Reflective and feedback performances on Thai medical students’ patient history-taking skills. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1585-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational (regression) analysis in the retrospective study of 48 students. The article prints "GPAX and history-taking OSCE scores, r (48) = + 0.56, p < 0.05" and two r = +0.36 correlations; r = .56 is a large correlation by the code table.

> "We found a correlation between participants’ GPAX and their clinical performance scores, r (48) = + 0.36, p = 0.05; clinical performance scores and history-taking OSCE scores, r (48) = + 0.36, p < 0.05; and GPAX and history-taking OSCE scores, r (48) = + 0.56, p < 0.05."
