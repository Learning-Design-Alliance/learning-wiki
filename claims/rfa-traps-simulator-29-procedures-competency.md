## Evidence

### Chaiperm Tanchanok 2025

Chaiperm Tanchanok, Phithakwatchara Nisarat, Nawapun Katika, Viboonchart Sommai, Jaingam Suparat, Watananirun Kanokwaroon, Wataganara Tuangsit. (2025). Assessment of Learning Curve for Radiofrequency Ablation in Twin Reversed Arterial Perfusion Sequence: A Simulation Model Study. Prenatal Diagnosis. https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.6801

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective simulation study in which 12 maternal–fetal medicine specialists and trainees without prior RFA experience practiced in a TRAPS box model; LC-CUSUM analysis defined competency, and "On average, 28.92 ± 4.68 procedures were required to achieve proficiency."

> "Figure 3 presents the LC‐CUSUM and CUSUM plots for each participant. On average, 28.92 ± 4.68 procedures were required to achieve proficiency."

### Chaiperm Tanchanok 2025 (2)

Chaiperm Tanchanok, Phithakwatchara Nisarat, Nawapun Katika, Viboonchart Sommai, Jaingam Suparat, Watananirun Kanokwaroon, Wataganara Tuangsit. (2025). Assessment of Learning Curve for Radiofrequency Ablation in Twin Reversed Arterial Perfusion Sequence: A Simulation Model Study. Prenatal Diagnosis. https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.6801

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-squared comparison of learning versus control phases across the cohort's 767 procedures found total success "significantly higher during the control phase than during the learning phase (96.7% vs. 88.2%; p < 0.001)."

> "A comparison of the two phases before and after proficiency revealed that the total success rate was significantly higher during the control phase than during the learning phase (96.7% vs. 88.2%; p < 0.001)."
