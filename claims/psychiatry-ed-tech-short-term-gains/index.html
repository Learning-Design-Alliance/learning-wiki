Evidence¶

Mokhtari Saba 2026¶

Mokhtari Saba, Mokhtari Asieh, Shariat Seyed Vahid, Nazeri Astaneh Ali, Shalbafan Mohammadreza. (2026). Innovative technologies in psychiatry education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09573-1

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review per PRISMA searching five databases to May 2025; nine studies met inclusion from 2,005 records. The review reports that "Most studies demonstrated positive short-term effects on knowledge, diagnostic accuracy, skills, confidence, and empathy." Risk of bias varied from low to moderate.

"Out of 2,005 initial records, nine studies met the inclusion criteria. Interventions included VPs (n = 4), HFSs (n = 2), comprehensive clinic simulators (n = 1), VR simulations (n = 1), and gamified mobile microlearning (n = 1). Most studies demonstrated positive short-term effects on knowledge, diagnostic accuracy, skills, confidence, and empathy."
