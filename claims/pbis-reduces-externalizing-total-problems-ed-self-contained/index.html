Benner 2008¶

The Impact of Intensive Positive Behavioral Supports on the Behavioral Functioning of Students with Emotional Disturbance: How Much Does Fidelity Matter? JBAIC, Volume 1, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Impact+of+Intensive+Positive+Behavioral+Supports

Pre-post naturalistic study of 37 students with ED in self-contained classrooms; Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Tests on TRF scores showed "statistically significant reductions" on Externalizing (p = .028) and Total Problems (p = .004). No effect size printed.

"Inspection of Table 1 reveals that statistically significant reductions on the TRF Externalizing Problems (p = .028) and Total Problems (p = .004) broadband scores were found from pre- to post-test."
