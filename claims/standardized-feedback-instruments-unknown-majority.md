### Kunz Kevin 2019

Kunz Kevin, Burkert Mirka, Heindl Felix, Schüttpelz-Brauns Katrin, Giesler Marianne. (2019). The frequency of using certain feedback methods in the teaching of medicine: a survey of teachers at the medical faculties in Baden-Wurttemberg. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001253](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001253)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey of instructors' self-rated familiarity with feedback methods from the literature on a four-point scale (1=method unknown to 4=known, used), shown in Figure 2. "CEC is unknown to 72% of the instructors", DOPS 62%, mini-CEX 59%, and 360° feedback 65%; video feedback, standardized-patient feedback, audience response systems and peer feedback were the best known.

> "360° feedback was unknown to 65% of those surveyed; this lack of familiarity is also very relevant for the checklists. CEC is unknown to 72% of the instructors. A total of 62% are not familiar with DOPS and 59% of the lecturers are not familiar with mini-CEX."
