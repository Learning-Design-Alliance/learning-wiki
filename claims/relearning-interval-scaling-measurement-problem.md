## Evidence

### Gary D. Phye 2012

Gary D. Phye. (2012). Commentary on the Special Issue. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-012-9211-1

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The commentary's own psychometric analysis of the Rawson and Dunlosky relearning model: proportions on an ordinal measurement scale are not comparable across learning episodes of different size and scope, so "mapping an optimal relearning schedule would be a daunting task." No effect size applies; this is an analytical argument.

> "The quandary involves the fact that as learning episodes vary in size and scope, proportions of the same value means quite different things. This is a characteristic of any ordinal measurement scale and makes generalizability to the level of principles quite difficult."
