### Bukowiec Lainey G. 2026

Bukowiec Lainey G., Damon Aaron, Todderud Julia, Lee Seung J., Huddleston Paul. (2026). Novel Simulation With 3D-Printed Spine for Teaching Durotomy Repair: A Technique Guide and Validation Study. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000889

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired t tests compared first versus last attempt times within groups. "For first-year residents, the average time decreased by 8.34 minutes" with P = 0.027; PGY-2s improved 6.34 minutes with P = 0.023.

> "For first-year residents, the average time decreased by 8.34 minutes from the first to the last attempt (SD = 6.60, P = 0.027). Second-year residents showed a similar improvement, with an average reduction of 6.34 minutes (SD = 5.53, P = 0.023)."
