## Evidence

### Hawley Chelsea E. 2019

Hawley Chelsea E., Triantafylidis Laura K., Phillips Sarah C., Schwartz Andrea Wershof. (2019). Brown Bag Simulation to Improve Medication Management in Older Adults. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10857

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mandatory multiple-choice knowledge assessment administered 4 weeks before and 2 days after the simulation; 135 (99%) completed the presurvey and 132 (96%) the postsurvey among 137 second-year medical students. The article reports the increase from 85% to 92% as significant (p = .009) with no effect size printed.

> "The average percentage of correctly answered knowledge questions significantly increased from 85% on the presurvey to 92% on the delayed postsurvey (p = .009; see the Table)."
