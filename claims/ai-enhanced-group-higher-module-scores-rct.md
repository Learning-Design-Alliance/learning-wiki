# In a three-arm RCT, nursing students using an AI-enhanced blended teaching model achieved significantly higher module scores than blended and traditional teaching groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The AI-Enhanced Group scored significantly higher on module examinations than the Blended Group (mean difference 4.9 points) and the Traditional Teaching Group (mean difference 8.4 points), with a large overall effect (η² = 0.16). [→ Zhao Can 2026](#zhao-can-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhao Can 2026

Zhao Can, Zhu Jianzhong, Liu Jianhui, Zhao Wentao, Pang Yin. (2026). Effectiveness of a generative AI-powered digital tutor integrated with a knowledge graph in anatomy education for nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09469-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Three-arm parallel randomized controlled trial of 301 first-year nursing students; one-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post-hoc tests. Module scores: AI-Enhanced 82.5 ± 7.3, Blended 77.6 ± 8.1, Traditional 74.1 ± 8.5 (F = 28.64, p < 0.001, "η² = 0.16").

> "Post-hoc pairwise comparisons showed that the AI-Enhanced Group scored significantly higher than the Blended Group (mean difference = 4.9 points, 95% CI: 2.1–7.7, p = 0.003) and the Traditional Teaching Group (mean difference = 8.4 points, 95% CI: 5.6–11.2, p < 0.001)."
