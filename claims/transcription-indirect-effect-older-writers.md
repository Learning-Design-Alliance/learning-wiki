## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Indirect effects of transcription on text generation via planning (β = .15, Sobel z = 2.55, p = .01) and self-efficacy (β = .21, Sobel z = 2.05, p = .04) were significant in Grades 7-9 but not in Grades 4-6 (β = .03 for both mediators). [→ Limpo 2013](#limpo-2013)

## Evidence

### Limpo 2013

Limpo, T., & Alves, R. A. (2013). Modeling writing development: Contribution of transcription and self-regulation to Portuguese students' text generation quality. Journal of Educational Psychology, 105, 401-413. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031391

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Modified Sobel tests in the multiple-group SEM of 376 Portuguese students showed the mediated paths "significant in Grades 7-9" with the printed β and Sobel values, and non-significant in Grades 4-6 (β = .03 for both mediators), a negligible printed magnitude.

> "The indirect effects mediated by planning and self-efficacy were significant in Grades 7-9 (βT→P→TG = .15, Sobel z = 2.55, p = .01; βT→SE→TG = .21, Sobel z = 2.05, p = .04), but they were not in Grades 4-6"
