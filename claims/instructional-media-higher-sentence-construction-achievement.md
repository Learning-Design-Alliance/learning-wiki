## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Arulselvi 2011

Evangelin Arulselvi. (2011). Effect of Instructional Media in the Learning of English Grammar on the Achievement of Teacher Training Students at Namakkal District. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 3 July - September 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071032

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Component-wise analysis of the parallel-group experiment reports the instructional media group's post-test sentence construction mean of 34 against the control group's 24, with a t value of 7.1 significant at .05, leading to rejection of the third null hypothesis.

> "But after the treatment it is found that students instructed through instructional media were attaining higher mean scores (M = 34) in comparison to the mean scores (M = 24) of the students of control group. The calculated 't' value is 7.1 which is significant at .05 level of confidence."

### Arulselvi 2011 (2)

Evangelin Arulselvi. (2011). Effect of Instructional Media in the Learning of English Grammar on the Achievement of Teacher Training Students at Namakkal District. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 3 July - September 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071032

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test analysis for sentence construction shows both groups obtaining nearly equal mean scores with a non-significant t value of 0.3, establishing baseline equivalence on this component.

> "It was found that before the treatment both experimental group and control group were obtaining nearly equal mean scores for making Sentence construction (M = 28, 29). The calculated 't' value 0.3 which is not significant at the acceptable level of confidence."
