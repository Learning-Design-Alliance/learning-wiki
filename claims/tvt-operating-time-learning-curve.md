## Evidence

### Nüssler EK 2021

Nüssler EK, Löfgren M, Lindkvist H, Idahl A. (2021). The tension‐free vaginal tape operation: Is the inexperienced surgeon a risk factor? Learning curve and Swedish quality reference. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14033

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Learning-curve analysis of register data plotting trainee operations 1–50 against the experienced surgeons' averaged results, with group averages compared in Table 2. The article reports the decline as a plotted and tabulated pattern.

> "There was a difference in the secondary outcome operation time, demonstrating a learning process: an additional 11.6 minutes for trainees on average for the first 10 trainee operations (33.8 vs 22.2 min, P < .001), declining thereafter and approaching the experienced surgeons' benchmark after approximately 50 operations"
