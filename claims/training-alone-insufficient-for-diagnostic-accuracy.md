## Evidence

### Moura Sofia 2014

Moura Sofia, Fançony Cláudia, Mirante Clara, Neves Marcela, Bernardino Luís, Fortes Filomeno, Sambo Maria do Rosário, Brito Miguel. (2014). Impact of a training course on the quality of malaria diagnosis by microscopy in Angola. Malaria Journal, Volume 13, Article 437. https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2875-13-437

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The authors' concluding interpretation (type e) of the study's pattern: written test and smear quality rose while pooled sensitivity fell slightly from 68.5% to 66.7% and specificity from 84.7% to 83.8%, both non-significant.

> "This study only measured the impact of the training course, but for a correct diagnosis it is important to have other conditions as the necessary equipment and reagents for staining and visualization, good working conditions, motivated and qualified personnel."
