q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative design study: 12 clinical-year medical students in Years 4–6 completed semi-structured interviews and three co-design workshops; hybrid thematic analysis of transcripts and workshop artifacts "produced 235 statement cards: 169 from the formative interviews and 66 from the workshops," from which four design-oriented themes emerged.
