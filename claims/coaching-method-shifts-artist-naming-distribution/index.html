Evidence¶

Faizrakhmanova 2018¶

Faizrakhmanova, A., Averianova, T., Aitov, V., Gibatova, G., & Lebedeva, I. (2018). Coaching Method in Teaching History of Visual Arts to Students. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 9(2), 80-88. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1181965

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Survey results (Table 1, Figure 1) from the Kazan Federal University quasi-experiment. The experimental group's highest value was 22, given by twenty-one students (15%); the control group's highest was 15, given by eleven students (4.8%). The experimental group's lowest value of 9-10 artists matches the control group's average.

"In the experimental group, the highest number of artists mentioned by one student is 22 (in twenty-one students' responses, which amounts to 15% of the total number of students in the experimental group)"
