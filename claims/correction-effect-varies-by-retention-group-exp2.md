### Swire-Thompson 2025

Swire-Thompson, Briony, Butler, Lucy, & Rapp, David N. (2025). The truth sandwich format does not enhance the correction of misinformation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 117(6), 851–862. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000961

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

A 2 × 2 × 3 between-within-subjects ANOVA on belief in Experiment 2 found a significant three-way interaction, ηp2 = .06, "indicating that the impact of the correction condition on belief varied across retention groups".

> "There was a significant three-way interaction, F(1.98, 1042.35) = 32.59, p < .001, MSE = 1.65, ηp2 = .06, indicating that the impact of the correction condition on belief varied across retention groups."
