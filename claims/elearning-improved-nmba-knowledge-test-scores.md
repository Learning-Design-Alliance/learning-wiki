# Anaesthetists' scores on a ten-question multiple-choice test of neuromuscular monitoring knowledge rose from pre-course to post-course after completing the e-learning module, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Median test scores rose from 7 to 9 out of 10 from pre-course to post-course among 367 anaesthetists with complete pre- and post-course data, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (p < .001, Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks Test). [→ Thomsen JLD 2022](#thomsen-jld-2022)

## Evidence

### Thomsen JLD 2022

Thomsen JLD, Mathiesen O, Hägi‐Pedersen D, Skovgaard LT, Østergaard D, Gätke MR; the INVERT collaborator group. (2022). Improving neuromuscular monitoring and reducing residual neuromuscular blockade via e‐learning: A multicentre interrupted time‐series study (INVERT study). Acta Anaesthesiol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aas.14038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre- and post-course ten-question multiple-choice test completed by 367 of 429 anaesthetists who finished the module; the paired Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks Test reached p < .001. The authors interpret this as "an increase in knowledge in this field." No effect size was printed.

> "The median score was seven (IQR 5–8, range 1–10) on the pre‐course test and nine (IQR 8–10, range 3–10) on the post‐course test (p < .001, Wilcoxon Signed‐Ranks Test)."
