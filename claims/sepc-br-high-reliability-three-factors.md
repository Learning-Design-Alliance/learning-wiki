## Evidence

### Gryschek Guilherme 2020

Gryschek Guilherme, Cecilio-Fernandes Dario, Mason Stephen, de Carvalho-Filho Marco Antonio. (2020). Assessing palliative care education in undergraduate medical students: translation and validation of the Self-Efficacy in Palliative Care and Thanatophobia Scales for Brazilian Portuguese. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-034567

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the SEPC-Br subscale scores from the same 111-student validation sample. Each subsection was analysed independently, and "Cronbach's alpha and composite reliability were 0.97 and 0.96, respectively" for multidisciplinary teamwork, the highest of the three factors.

> "For the first factor, multidisciplinary teamwork, Cronbach's alpha and composite reliability were 0.97 and 0.96, respectively. For the second factor, communication, Cronbach's alpha and composite reliability were 0.93 and 0.93, respectively. For the third factor, patient management, Cronbach's alpha and composite reliability were 0.92 and 0.91, respectively."
