Same retrospective comparative study of resident-performed MSICS before and after wet-laboratory training. The article reports the statistically significant difference in PC rupture and vitreous loss between the untrained (Group A) and trained (Group B) cohorts.

> "The occurrence of posterior capsule (PC) rupture and vitreous loss showed a statistically significant difference, with Group A showing a high rate of PC rent and vitreous loss (14.3%) while only 6.9% had this complication in Group B."
