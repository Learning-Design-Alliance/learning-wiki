## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and David Rosengrant 2016

Chandralekha Singh and David Rosengrant. (2016). Multiple-choice test of energy and momentum concepts. arXiv:1602.06497. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06497](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06497)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-test response comparison between two conceptually similar items showed 10% of group L chose the mass-dependence option for the projectile item versus 60% for the slide item. The article interprets this contrast as showing "students’ knowledge is fragmented and their recall is triggered by surface features". Percentages are descriptive; no effect size is printed.

> "in the post-test, only 10% from group L chose (e) indicating that the speed of a ball at a given height depends on the mass, but 60% indicated in item 15 that the speed of an object sliding down th e frictionless slide depends upon mass."
