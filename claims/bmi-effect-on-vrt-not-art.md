### Prabu Kumar Archana 2020

Prabu Kumar Archana, Omprakash Abirami, Kuppusamy Maheshkumar, K.N. Maruthy, B.W.C. Sathiyasekaran, P.V. Vijayaraghavan, Ramaswamy Padmavathi. (2020). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across underweight (n = 69), normal (n = 340) and obese (n = 209) students found the BMI effect only for VRT, where the normal-weight mean of 249.92 msec was faster than the underweight mean of 284.70 msec, with no normal-versus-obese difference.

> "There was a statistically significant effect of VRT [F (2, 615) = 4.39, p = 0.01] on BMI, whereas no such effects were found in the case of ART [F (2, 615) = 0.02, p = 0.97]. Tukey's post hoc assessments showed that students with normal weight have faster VRT, compared to underweight students (p < 0.05) but not compared to obese students"
