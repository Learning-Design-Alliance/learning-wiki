## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across experiments, the four-way interaction of experiment, reward, interest, and delay on memory accuracy was significant (F(1, 81) = 11.59, p = 0.001, ηp2 = 0.13). [→ Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang 2025](#zhongyu-hu-jiongjiong-yang-2025)
`q3 i2` At 1 day, the reward effect was significant for low-interest clips when cued before (p < 0.001) and for high-interest clips when cued after (p = 0.005); neither pattern was observed at 10 min. [→ Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang 2025](#zhongyu-hu-jiongjiong-yang-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhongyu Hu & Jiongjiong Yang 2025

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mixed ANOVA across both experiments (experiment as between-subjects factor; 1-week delay excluded) on memory accuracy, with a printed effect size of ηp2 = 0.13. The article reports the interactive effects "need time to consolidate", appearing at 1 day but not 10 min.
