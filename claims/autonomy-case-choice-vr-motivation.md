`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interpretive finding from the interview analysis, reported in the Discussion. For the second residency cohort, VR was tied to learning objectives in the competence portal; participants said the objectives were diffuse and suggested a list of recommended cases linked to relevant learning objectives.

> "Furthermore, the option to choose cases themselves appeared to affect their sense of autonomy and consequently increased their motivation."
