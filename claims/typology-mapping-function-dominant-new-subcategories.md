Jasmin Breitwieser, André Bittermann, Maria Theobald, Tim Lauer, Mirijam Schaaf, Garvin Brod. (2026). Digital Prompting in Education: A Design Framework and Bibliometric Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10164-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Coding of 283 labels onto the typology adapted from the feedback literature, with two coders reaching 80.92% overall agreement for main categories. The article reports the printed label counts per category and that three additional presentation subcategories were introduced after discussion.

> "Most labels referred to function (n = 114), primarily targeting learning/performance, fewer addressing self-regulated learning, and only very few related to motivation/affect. The second-largest group described presentation (n = 93), with many labels referring to adaptivity and modality."
