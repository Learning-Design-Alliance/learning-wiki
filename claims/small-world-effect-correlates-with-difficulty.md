## Evidence

### Min Xia 2024

Min Xia, Zhu Su, Wei-Bing Deng, Xiu-Mei Feng, Ben-Wei Zhang. (2024). How to quantify an examination? Evidence from physics examinations via complex networks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis of the 30 small-world KPNs, repeated separately per volume (V3 excluded with only 3 small-world samples). The article reports "a positive correlation (0.608, p <0.001)" between σ and Fd, i.e., r = 0.608; the correlation held within each volume.

> "We found a positive correlation (0.608, p <0.001) between the comprehensive difficulty coefficient (Fd) of the 30 KPNs exhibiting small-world properties and their small-coefficient σ value."
