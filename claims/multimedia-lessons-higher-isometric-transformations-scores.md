## Evidence

### Marina Milovanoviü 2013

Marina Milovanoviü, Jasmina Obradoviü, Aleksandar Milajiü. (2013). APPLICATION OF INTERACTIVE MULTIMEDIA TOOLS IN TEACHING MATHEMATICS – EXAMPLES OF LESSONS FROM GEOMETRY. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008863

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of two randomly formed groups of 25 first-year Architecture students, one taught traditionally and one with multimedia lectures, tested after lectures without computers. The multimedia group averaged 86.96 (SD 17.72) versus 76.56 (SD 18.64), and the article reports "multimedia group had significantly higher scores" with t = –2.022, p = 0.049.

> "Average score of the Test 1 (isometric transformations) in the traditional group from the Faculty of Architecture was 76.56 with standard deviation 18.64, and in multimedia group, average score was 86.96"

### Marina Milovanoviü 2013 (2)

Marina Milovanoviü, Jasmina Obradoviü, Aleksandar Milajiü. (2013). APPLICATION OF INTERACTIVE MULTIMEDIA TOOLS IN TEACHING MATHEMATICS – EXAMPLES OF LESSONS FROM GEOMETRY. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008863

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same design at the Faculty of Civil Construction Management: multimedia group averaged 88.12 (SD 18.11) versus 76.64 (SD 18.53), with a reported significant difference (t = 2.216, p = 0.031). Pre-test scores were statistically similar (I: 72.35, II: 71.25 out of 100).

> "Average score of the same test in the traditional group from the Faculty of Civil Construction Management was 76.64 with standard deviation 18.53, and in multimedia group, average score was 88.12"
