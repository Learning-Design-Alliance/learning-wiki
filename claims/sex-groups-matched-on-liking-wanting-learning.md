## Evidence

### Luigi A. E. Degni 2024

Luigi A. E. Degni, Sara Garofalo, Gianluca Finotti, Francesca Starita, Trevor W. Robbins & Giuseppe di Pellegrino. (2024). Sex differences in motivational biases over instrumental actions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00246-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 ran 2x3x2 mixed ANOVAs on liking and wanting scores (pre/post, three outcomes, sex); the ANOVAs found no significant sex effects or interactions, while wanting increased over time comparably for all participants, confirming a general hunger increase.

> "1 7 ) .I ns u m m a r y ,t h e s e results conﬁrm absence of difference between the two groups in liking and wanting."

### Luigi A. E. Degni 2024 (2)

Luigi A. E. Degni, Sara Garofalo, Gianluca Finotti, Francesca Starita, Trevor W. Robbins & Giuseppe di Pellegrino. (2024). Sex differences in motivational biases over instrumental actions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00246-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1 tested baseline response preferences and vigor with 2x2 mixed ANOVAs on percentage of responses and reaction times during instrumental learning; all main effects and interactions were non-significant with Bayesian evidence for the null, confirming "absence of difference between the two groups in Instrumental learning."

> "These results are conﬁrmed even for the reaction times, both for the main effects (response: F1,33 = 0.35; p = 0.56; ηp 2 = 0.01; BF10 = 0.29; sex: F1,33 = 0.77; p = 0.39; ηp 2 = 0.02; BF10 = 0.56) and for the interaction between response and sex ( F1,33 = 0.07; p = 0.79; ηp 2 = 0.002; BF 10 = 0.33)."
