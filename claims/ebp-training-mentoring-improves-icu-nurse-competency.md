# Among ICU nurses, scores for skills, knowledge, practice, motivation and work engagement for VAP prevention rose from pre-test to post-test after EBP training with mentoring, in one uncontrolled pre-post study (all p < 0.001)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In one uncontrolled pre-post study, scores for all measured competency outcomes (skills, knowledge, practice, motivation and work engagement) rose from pre-test to post-test after the two-month EBP training intervention (all p < 0.001). [→ Akhter N 2026](#akhter-n-2026)

## Evidence

### Akhter N 2026

Akhter N, Zhou X, Elhabashy S, Huq KATE, Okamoto M, Latif MA, Babaita AO, Moriyama M. (2026). Effect of evidence-based education program with mentoring system for prevention of ventilator-associated pneumonia on nurses' competency to improve quality care for patients in the intensive care unit: a quasi-experimental study in a tertiary hospital in Bangladesh. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1674223

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post quasi-experimental study of 52 ICU nurses at Dhaka Medical College Hospital; Friedman and Wilcoxon signed ranks tests compared scores across three time points. Overall skill mean rose from 31.7 (12.8) to 120.9 (7.4) after training, with "statistically significant improvement immediately after the training (all, p < 0.001)".

> "All 4 items showed statistically significant improvement immediately after the training (all, p < 0.001). Scores of skill components improved uniformly (all, p < 0.001), and were maintained or slightly increased during the implementation period."
