## Evidence

### Tiffin Paul A 2018

Tiffin Paul A, Orr James, Paton Lewis W, Smith Daniel T, Norcini John J. (2018). UK nationals who received their medical degrees abroad: selection into, and subsequent performance in postgraduate training: a national data linkage study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-023060

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational national data-linkage study using multilevel logistic regression on Oriel specialty recruitment records (2012–2016) for 34,755 trainee doctors, including 1,108 UK OGs. The study reports "OR 1.29, 95% CI 1.16 to 1.42" for UK OGs vs non-UK IMGs, with lower odds versus UK and European graduates.

> "UK overseas graduates were more likely to be deemed appointable compared with non-EU medical graduates who were not UK citizens (OR 1.29, 95% CI 1.16 to 1.42), although less so than UK (OR 0.25, 95% CI 0.23 to 0.27) or European graduates (OR 0.66, 95% CI 0.58 to 0.75)."
