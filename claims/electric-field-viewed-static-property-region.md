## Evidence

### Cristian Raduta 2005

Cristian Raduta. (2005). General Students' Misconceptions Related to Electricity and Magnetism. The Ohio State University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503132](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0503132)

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

Interview by Bagno et al. (Ref. 4) giving students the statement 'A charged particle enters a region with a constant electric field. The field in this area changes because of the new charge.' The authors explain the perception by textbook presentations that present the field merely as a force applier. No effect size is printed.

> "40% of the students answered incorrectly, from which 82% of them were saying that " the electric field is a "property" of the region-its task is to apply force on a charge in it. " The authors (Bagno et al., Ref. 4) explain this misconception by reference to the presentations of the most textbooks"
