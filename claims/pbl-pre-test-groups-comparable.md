## Evidence

### Wekesa 2016

Wekesa, Noah Wafula and Ongunya, Raphael Odhiambo. (2016). Project Based Learning on Students’ Performance in the Concept of Classification of Organisms Among Secondary Schools in Kenya. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.16. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105278

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA of pre-test mean scores on the CAT across three school categories showed F-values below the tabulated critical value. The authors conclude "the two groups (experim ental and control) are comparable" and homogenous before treatment.

> "The results from all the three categories of schools re veal F- values less than one while the tabulated values are greater than unity. Hence, the two groups (experim ental and control) are comparable."
