## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The feedback group scored higher than the control group on global OSATS (26.5 ± 7.3 vs. 23.4 ± 5.1, p = 0.003), task-specific OSATS (47.6 ± 12.9 vs. 36.0 ± 12.8, p < 0.001), global GOALS (17.5 ± 4.4 vs. 16.0 ± 3.8, p < 0.001), and task-specific GOALS (6.6 ± 2.3 vs. 5.9 ± 2.1, p = 0.005). ## Evidence

### Cizmic A 2024

Cizmic A, Häberle F, Wise PA, Müller F, Gabel F, Mascagni P, Namazi B, Wagner M, Hashimoto DA, Madani A, Alseidi A, Hackert T, Müller-Stich BP, Nickel F. (2024). Structured feedback and operative video debriefing with critical view of safety annotation in training of laparoscopic cholecystectomy: a randomized controlled study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10843-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled study of 80 medical students randomized to feedback (n = 40) or control (n = 40) groups after a multi-modal MIS curriculum; four ex-vivo porcine LCs each were rated with OSATS and GOALS. The article reports "significantly higher in the feedback compared to the control group" with the printed means and p-values.

> "The total global and task-specific GOALS performance scores were significantly higher in the feedback compared to the control group (17.5 ± 4.4 vs. 16.0 ± 3.8, p < 0.001; 6.6 ± 2.3 vs. 5.9 ± 2.1, p = 0.001, respectively)."
