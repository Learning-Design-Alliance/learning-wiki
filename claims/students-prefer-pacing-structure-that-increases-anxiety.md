### obj3-main-effect

Bentley DC, Sotto A, Lombardi P. Comparison of examination performance and test anxiety during bell‐paced and self‐paced gross anatomy laboratory examinations. Anat Sci Educ. 2026;19:732–742. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70215

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The third exploratory mixed-effects model in the randomized crossover study assessed the effect of preferred pacing structure on reactive test anxiety. Findings showed a significant main effect, with one verbatim fragment of source_quote: β = −13.19, 95% CI [−20.35, −6.04].

> "There was a significant main effect of preferred pacing structure (β = −13.19, 95% CI [−20.35, −6.04]) on reactive test anxiety, suggesting that students who preferred BP, overall, experienced lower reactive test anxiety than those who preferred SP when controlling for assigned pacing condition and gender."
