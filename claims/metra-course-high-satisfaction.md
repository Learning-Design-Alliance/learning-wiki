## Evidence

### Omori Kazuhiko 2025

Omori Kazuhiko, Takahashi Jiro, Watanabe Noriko, Iwasaki Hiroko, Mineyama Sachiko, Sakata Kumiko, Yamada Kentaro, Ichikawa Susumu, Takamatsu Manabu, Ogino Ryukoh, Hayakawa Tatsuya. (2025). Effectiveness of a new basic course incorporating medical trainer simulator for HEMS education in Japan: a pre-post intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07047-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-course overall assessment on 5-point scales covering simulator effectiveness, future usefulness, desire for future use, and course satisfaction (Kirkpatrick Level 1 reactions).

> "For overall appraisal, mean scores were MeTra simulator effectiveness 4.71 ± 0.61 (93.75% ≥ 4), usefulness for future activities 4.78 ± 0.57 (96.15% ≥ 4), desire for future use 4.70 ± 0.63 (92.18% ≥ 4), and overall course satisfaction 4.74 ± 0.55 (95.68% ≥ 4)."
