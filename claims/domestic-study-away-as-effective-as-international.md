## Evidence

### Natalie I. Cruz & Melissa Whatley 2024

Natalie I. Cruz & Melissa Whatley. (2024). Domestic Study Away in Higher Education: A Systematic Literature Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241279296

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing Johnstone et al. (2018), a hierarchical OLS analysis using Astin's Input-Environment-Output model predicting critical thinking and multicultural competence; college experiences explained variance but demographic inputs did not. It found study "away experiences that are intentionally experiential and have cross-cultural elements can be as effective as international experiences".

> "away experiences that are intentionally experiential and have cross-cultural elements can be as effective as international experiences in developing multicultural competence and critical thinking skills."

### Natalie I. Cruz & Melissa Whatley 2024 (2)

Natalie I. Cruz & Melissa Whatley. (2024). Domestic Study Away in Higher Education: A Systematic Literature Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543241279296

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes to Johnstone et al. (2018) the finding that domestic study away with service learning elements outperformed short-term study abroad without service learning on the study's outcome variables; no effect size is printed.

> "They also found that students who participated in domestic study away experiences with service learning elements showed greater gains in the outcome variables than students who engaged in short-term study abroad experiences without service learning components."
