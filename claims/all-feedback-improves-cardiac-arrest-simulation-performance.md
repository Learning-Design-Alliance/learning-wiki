Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-025-01456-6

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

This finding comes from the thematic synthesis of a mixed-method systematic review that included 160 studies (154 quantitative, 3 qualitative, 3 mixed method) on feedback in cardiac arrest simulation education. The review's first theme states that "All feedback, whether human or device-based, improves performance." No pooled effect size is reported for this theme.
