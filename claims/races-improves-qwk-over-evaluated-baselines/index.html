Zhenxin Zhang, Ziyu Ding, Mengyun Liu and Haiwei Sang. (2026). RACES: reward-aligned consistent essay scoring with large language models. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00607-8

q2 · i? · design · r2

Numerical evaluation shown in Fig. 5a of four policy–reward configurations on the ASAP 2.0 benchmark (QWK reported as percentages, e.g. 82.7 for LLaMA2-7B with Mistral-7B reward model vs 71.6 pretrained). The article reports "all four configurations achieve competitive performance under the evaluated setting". No confidence intervals or significance tests were reported.

"As shown, all four configurations achieve competitive performance under the evaluated setting. The LLaMA2-7B-based configurations slightly outperform the Qwen2.5-Instruct-7B-based configurations, which may be related to differences in their adaptation to English argumentative essays"
