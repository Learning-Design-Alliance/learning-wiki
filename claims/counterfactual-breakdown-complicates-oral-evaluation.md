`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Workshop discussion in the Ambitious AI-Leveraged Projects room about evaluation practices combining deliverables, presentations, oral examinations, and counterfactual questioning. Participants report the distinction between weak understanding and weak communication has become harder to draw.

> "a student’s inability to explain a system may reflect weak understanding, weak communication skills, or both—a distinction some participants observed has become harder, with students breaking down on counterfactual questions even when they had done the work."
