## Evidence

### Zeng Yanglin 2025

Zeng Yanglin, Wang Ya, Dong Bo, Liu Liu, Cao Yubin, Huang Dingming, Wang Shida, Zheng Qinghua. (2025). Effectiveness of “colored teeth and digital evaluation” on preclinical tooth preparation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07963-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of peer evaluation (n = 233) and digital evaluation (n = 226) of the first standard-tooth preparation in the digital group. Digital compliance rates included "76.55%" for lateral wall smoothness and "35.84%" for dovetail width.

> "The results showed that more than 90% of the tasks were deemed to meet the feature of the cavity in peer evaluation. However, the digital evaluation showed that only the shape of the proximal box met the criteria in 90% of the works."
