## Evidence

### Nim Casper et al 2024

Nim Casper et al. (2024). The association between students’ confidence and ability to modulate spinal manipulation force–time characteristics of specific target forces: a cross-sectional study. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-024-00557-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional measurement of 149 students' SMT performance on a force-sensing table (900 valid trials), with target-force differences plotted in Fig. 4. The article reports "minimal differences" at 200N and 400N targets and "substantially more variability" at the 800N target.

> "Accordingly, participants performed impulse forces close to the 200N and 400N but had difficulty accurately modulating to 800N forces."
