## Evidence

### Huang Yue 2024

Huang Yue, Jia Jin, Zhan Jun, Li Bo, Gu Qiong, Li Zhengyu. (2024). Porcine stomach surgical simulation model for cesarean section and cervical laceration suturing. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05936-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire study in which "Twenty-three junior OB/GYN residents (first- to third-year)" completed anonymous pre/post self-ratings analyzed with paired-samples t test. Ratings on a 1-10 scale rose, e.g. understanding of cervical laceration suturing from a median 1.17 to 7.13, with P < 0.01. No effect size printed.

> "There was statistically improvement in understanding and confidence of performing uterus suturing in cesarean section and cervical laceration suturing for junior OB/GYN residents (P < 0.01)."
