# Pass/Fail exams in undergraduate medical education show a higher fail rate than Tiered grade exams (3.3% vs 2.0%)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Among 7164 clinical training exams, students failed Pass/Fail exams at 3.3% versus 2.0% for Tiered grade exams, a statistically significant difference. [→ Modrau B 2025](#modrau-b-2025)

## Evidence

### Modrau B 2025

Modrau B, Kirk KF, Said SMA, Bjarkam CR, Sunde L, Bodilsen J, Dal J, Kristensen JK, Emmersen J, Astorp MB, Andersen S. (2025). Pass/Fail Versus Tiered Grades and Academic Performance in Undergraduate Medical Education: Crossover Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/74975

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retrospective cohort analysis of 7164 fourth- and fifth-year clinical exams (3047 Pass/Fail, 4117 Tiered) at Aalborg University, 2015-2023, comparing fail proportions with a χ2 test: "3.3% (n=101/3047)" versus "2.0% (81/4117)".

> "The frequency of students failing exams (dark gray bars) was 3.3% (n=101/3047) when using Pass/Fail and 2.0% (81/4117) with Tiered grade evaluations (χ2 test, P<.001)."
