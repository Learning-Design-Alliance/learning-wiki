## Evidence

### Deane Richard P 2015

Deane Richard P, Murphy Deirdre J. (2015). Impact of a personal learning plan supported by an induction meeting on academic performance in undergraduate Obstetrics and Gynaecology: a cluster randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0325-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomised controlled trial over a full academic year, randomising rotation units to intervention or control, with the overall examination score as primary outcome compared by unpaired t-test. The study found "no significant difference in mean overall examination score between the groups", with means of 56.3 ± 4.8% versus 56.7 ± 5.6% (p = 0.64).

> "There was no significant difference in mean overall examination score between the groups; 56.3 ± 4.8% in the intervention group and 56.7 ± 5.6% in the control group (p = 0.64) (Table 2)."
