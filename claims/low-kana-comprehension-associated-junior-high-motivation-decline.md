# Low kana pick-out story comprehension score is associated with decreased intrinsic academic motivation in junior high school students

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In multivariate logistic regression adjusted for grade and gender, lower story comprehension scores on the kana pick-out test were positively correlated with prevalence of decrease in intrinsic academic motivation (p for trend = .006). [→ Mizuno K 2011](#mizuno-k-2011)

## Evidence

### Mizuno K 2011

Mizuno K, Tanaka M, Fukuda S, Imai-Matsumura K, Watanabe Y. (2011). Relationship between cognitive function and prevalence of decrease in intrinsic academic motivation in adolescents. Behavioral and Brain Functions 7:4. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-7-4

`q3 · i?`

Multivariate logistic regression in 133 junior high students adjusted for grade and gender. Tertile 3 (≤3) versus Tertile 1 (≥6) showed "OR: 4.95, 95% CI: 1.82 to 13.43"; p for trend = .006. The kana pick-out test requires divided attention during reading comprehension.

> "score for comprehension of the story in the kana pick-out test ([Tertile 2 (4-5) vs. Tertile 1 ( ≥6); OR: 2.95, 95% CI: 1.17 to 7.46; Tertile 3 ( ≤3) vs. Tertile 1 ( ≥6); OR: 4.95, 95% CI: 1.82 to 13.43; p = .006])"
