## Evidence

### Schönburg S 2021

Schönburg S, Anheuser P, Kranz J, Fornara P, Oubaid V. (2021). Cognitive training for robotic surgery: a chance to optimize surgical training? A pilot study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-020-01167-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized three-group pilot study (25 analyzed: n=8 simulator, n=7 cognitive training, n=10 control) of student volunteers; da Vinci task times measured at T1 and T2. The abstract states "There were no significant differences in T1 and T2 among all three groups." Descriptive means in Table 1 show group 2 slowest at both tests.

> "In test 2, group 3 achieved the best result, and group 2 took the most time, as in test 1. However, in an indirect comparison, the greatest time reduction was presented group 1"
