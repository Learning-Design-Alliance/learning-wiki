## Evidence

### Mátyás Barczy 2025

Mátyás Barczy, Imre Kocsis, Csaba Gábor Kézi. (2025). A stochastic approach in physics exercises of mathematics education. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1080/0020739x.2025.2563118](https://doi.org/10.1080/0020739x.2025.2563118)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

The article's analytical derivations across five projectile motion exercises show that randomizing inputs such as initial height and velocity produces outputs like horizontal distance whose distributions, expectations, variances and relative standard deviations must be derived probabilistically. The article states that "even if the inputs are uniformly distributed, the outputs are usually not uniformly distributed."

> "The outputs of stochastic models are random variables, and even if the inputs are uniformly distributed, the outputs are usually not uniformly distributed."
