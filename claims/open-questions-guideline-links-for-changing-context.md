### Hege I 2020

Hege I, Sudacka M, Kononowicz AA, Nonnenmann J, Banholzer J, Schelling J, Adler M, Espinoza B, Garrido MA, Radon K. (2020). Adaptation of an international virtual patient collection to the COVID-19 pandemic. GMS Journal for Medical Education 37(7): Doc92. [doi:10.3205/zma001385](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001385)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Design report from the project's process section: adapting VPs for an international learner group faced constantly changing guidelines differing between countries, regions and hospitals. The authors "solved this by asking open questions concerning the process" with feedback linking to WHO and ECDC guidance, and encourage learners to seek local information. No evaluation data are reported.

> "Since it is not possible to factor in all these variations, we solved this by asking open questions concerning the process and providing feedback by linking to up-to-date guidelines from global institutions such as the World Health Organization (WHO)"
