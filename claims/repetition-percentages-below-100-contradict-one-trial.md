Phase-2 analysis of the authors' own experiments plus the Brown-Avard data (91% for a 5-item list, 94% for a 10-item list), contrasted with Estes et al.'s 71% and another cited study's 54%. The authors conclude the Phase-2 facts do not support one-trial learning.
