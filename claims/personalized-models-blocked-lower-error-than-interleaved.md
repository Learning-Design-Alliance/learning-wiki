Advances in Cognitive Systems, 10. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2408.13684](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.13684)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Counterfactual simulations (20 runs per condition) with personalized agents for a high- and a low-performing student; the low performer's predictions show "a lower error rate" in blocked than interleaved, matching human findings. No effect size printed.

> "Our agent also predicts that error in the interleaved condition will be higher than in the blocked condition. This agrees with Patel et al.'s (2016) finding that students make more errors in the interleaved condition than in the blocked condition."
