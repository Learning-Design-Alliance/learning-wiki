Ariyan Hossain 2026¶

Ariyan Hossain, Kazi Kamruzzaman Rabbi, Farig Sadeque, S M Taiabul Haque. (2026). Contaminated Collaboration: Measuring Gender Bias Transfer in LLM-Assisted Student Writing. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15914

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Stereotype Congruence Rate analysis of the 123-participant experiment's essays, with pairwise Fisher's exact tests. The overall chi-square was significant (χ²(2)=9.90, p=.007); biased vs. neutral OR=3.91 (p=.004), biased vs. control OR=3.06 (p=.024); control and neutral did not differ (p=.656).

"The biased condition yields the highest SCR (0.714), well above both the control ( 0.450) and neutral (0.390) conditions, indicating that 71.4% of essays under biased LLM exposure recommended a gender-stereotypic occupation for the target."
