Evidence¶

Duckworth Angela L. 2016¶

Duckworth Angela L., White Rachel E., Matteucci Alyssa J., Shearer Annie, Gross James J. (2016). A Stitch in Time: Strategic Self-Control in High School and College Students. Journal of educational psychology, 108(3), 329-341. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000062

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Ratings (0–100) of exemplar strategies for the texting, Internet/videogame, and procrastination scenarios in the N = 577 survey. The article reports ηp2 = .44, d = .47 between situation selection and modification, "and ds from 1.05 to 1.23" for situational versus cognitive strategies; the three later strategies did not differ from each other.

"effectiveness ratings varied by strategy type, F(4, 2216) = 436.94, p < .001, ηp2 = .44. As illustrated in Figure 3, post-hoc pairwise comparisons using a Bonferroni correction showed that situation selection strategies were rated as more effective than situation modification strategies, p < .001, d = .47"
