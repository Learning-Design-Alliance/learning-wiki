## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2019

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Validation and Administration of a Conceptual Survey on the Formalism and Postulates of Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020128)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Related-measures construct-validity study: 82 undergraduates in the first semester of an upper-division QM course took both the QMFPS and the QMS after traditional instruction. The figure caption reports the correlation coefficient R=0.78. The authors note the QMFPS tends to be more difficult than the QMS.

> "Figure 2 shows that there is a strong correlation between students' scores on the QMS and the QMFPS."
