Review synthesis of seven studies: six reported significant positive-emotion improvements; the review notes breaks 'enhance students' enjoyment and well-being' and alleviate stress and boredom.

"seven studies explored positive emotions, with six reporting significant improvements [14, 22, 24–26, 36] and one showing no effect [38], resulting in relatively strong evidence strength (85.7%)."
