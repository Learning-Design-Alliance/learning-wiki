# Parents used more spatial language at 24 months than at 48 months, an effect attributable to differences in overall talkativeness

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r3` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Parents used significantly more spatial language at 24 months than 48 months, with a large quantity effect. [→ Farran 2025](#farran-2025)
`q3 i?` Once parent talkativeness was controlled, the timepoint effect was no longer significant. [→ Farran 2025 (2)](#farran-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Farran 2025

Farran, E. K., Fink, E., Hughes, C., Gilligan-Lee, K. A., & The NewFAMS team. (2025). Limited sex differences in spatial language in parent-child dyads. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102006. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102006

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r3`

MANOVA (N = 104) showed a timepoint effect, "Λ = .209, F(2, 101) = 191.59, p < .001"; follow-up ANOVAs gave quantity ηp2 = .730 and quality ηp2 = .058, both favouring 24 months.

> "A significant effect of timepoint was found; parents
used significantly more spatial language at 24 months than at 48
months, Λ = .209, F(2, 101) = 191.59, p < .001."

### Farran 2025 (2)

Farran, E. K., Fink, E., Hughes, C., Gilligan-Lee, K. A., & The NewFAMS team. (2025). Limited sex differences in spatial language in parent-child dyads. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102006. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102006

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the MANCOVA controlling for SES and parent total words, the timepoint effect disappeared ("Λ = .994, F < 1"), while parent total words was a significant covariate (Λ = .450, F(2, 99) = 60.567, p < .001, ηp2 = .556).

> "Note, however, that the effect of timepoint was no longer significant Λ =
.994, F < 1."
