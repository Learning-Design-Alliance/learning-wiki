## Evidence

### Xu Liyuan 2025

Xu Liyuan, Xu Qinrong, Liu Chunya, Chen Baozhen, Wang Chunxia. (2025). Virtual Standardized Patients for Improving Clinical Thinking Ability Training in Residents: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/73196

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Systematic knowledge test results from the trial's formative evaluation, a two-arm randomized controlled trial comparing VSP training with traditional problem-based learning and real-patient exposure. The improvement was "significantly greater in the experimental group compared to the control group" with Cohen d=1.15 and P<.001.

> "Additionally, the improvement in theoretical scores was significantly greater in the experimental group compared to the control group (mean 17.07, SD 4.24 vs mean 10.67, SD 4.91; F1, 59=29.20; Cohen d=1.15; 95% CI 12.43-15.31; P<.001)."
