Learning Salon Post-Engagement Survey responses from 17 solution providers, items measured from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 4 (very satisfied). The report states "At least one solution provider indicated dissatisfaction with the Lightning Talks, Root Cause Analysis, and Co-Design", while 100% were satisfied with preparedness, networking, and solution mapping. One provider attributed dissatisfaction to limited time per component.
