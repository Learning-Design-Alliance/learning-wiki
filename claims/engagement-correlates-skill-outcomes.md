# Learning behavior engagement correlates positively with operational skill outcomes, with no significant correlation for flexibility

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Engagement correlated with accuracy of procedures (r = 0.33, p < 0.01), fluency (r = 0.25, p < 0.05), and independence (r = 0.27, p < 0.05), but not flexibility (r = 0.12, p > 0.05). [→ Li Kaiyue 2025](#li-kaiyue-2025)

## Evidence

### Li Kaiyue 2025

Li Kaiyue, Li Qun, Wang Linding, Wang Xiaonan, Ding Xiaojuan, Liu Boyu. (2025). Virtual simulation experiments in medical education: technology acceptance, learning outcomes, and motivational impacts. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08025-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis within the experimental study related engagement questionnaire scores to instructor-rated skill dimensions. Accuracy showed "experimental accuracy (r = 0.33, p < 0.01)"; flexibility and adaptability showed r = 0.12, p > 0.05, a non-significant association.

> "The results revealed a strong positive correlation between student engagement and skill acquisition, particularly in experimental accuracy (r = 0.33, p < 0.01), fluency of experimental steps (r = 0.25, p < 0.05), and independence and decision-making ability (r = 0.27, p < 0.05)"
