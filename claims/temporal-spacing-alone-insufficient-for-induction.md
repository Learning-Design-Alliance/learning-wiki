## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kang and & Pashler 2012

Sean H. K. Kang and Harold Pashler. (2012). Learning Painting Styles: Spacing is Advantageous when it Promotes Discriminative Contrast. Applied Cognitive Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.1801

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same 88-subject Experiment 1, the Temporal Spaced condition matched the Interleaved condition's temporal spacing via inserted cartoons but kept paintings blocked by artist; differences among the non-interleaved conditions were not reliable (ts < 1). The article reports no effect size for this null contrast, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "They also indicate that increasing the temporal spacing between paintings alone was not sufﬁcient to improve induction."
