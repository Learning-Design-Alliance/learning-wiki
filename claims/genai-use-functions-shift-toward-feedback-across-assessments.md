### Zachery Quince and Emily Faulconer 2026 (2)

Zachery Quince and Emily Faulconer. (2026). Student GenAI use under changing institutional policies: a mixed-methods case study in undergraduate physics. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00651-w

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Frequency analysis of coded declarations for A3, a take-home quiz under open use; students described using GenAI to check calculations, verify answers, and clarify concepts. Formatting fell to 14.81% and Feedback was 0% in A1.

> "By Assessment 3 (A3), Feedback had become the dominant application, accounting for 66.7% of all reported uses."
