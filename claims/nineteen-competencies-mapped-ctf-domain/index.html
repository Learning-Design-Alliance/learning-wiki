Mapping and discursive analysis phase conducted by the lead researcher and independently checked by two co-authors. The article reports "The remaining behaviour competencies (n = 19) were reviewed for contextual national appropriateness"; most originated from the PSI, RPS and SHPA frameworks.

"A total of 360 behaviour competencies were removed (duplicates (n = 354) between the CTF and one or more other frameworks & n = 6 behaviour competencies for community pharmacists). The remaining behaviour competencies (n = 19) were reviewed for contextual national appropriateness by discursive analysis"
