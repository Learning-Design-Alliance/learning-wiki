### Youjie Chen 2026

Youjie Chen, Xixi Shi, Xinyu Liu, Shuaiguo Wang, Tracy Xiao Liu, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Not All Students Engage Alike: Multi-Institution Patterns in GenAI Tutor Use. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00447

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Process mining with a First-Order Markov Model over ordered session sequences characterized student-level transition patterns. The Shallow Engagement Cycle showed "repeated shallow engagement (TP = 0.47)" with limited exits, whereas the Deep Engagement Cycle transitioned flexibly to routine-learning (TP = 0.43) and exam-driven (TP = 0.21) engagement and rarely to shallow (TP = 0.07).

> "This engagement pattern was characterized by repeated shallow engagement (TP = 0.47), with very limited transitions to other engagement types (deep engagement: TP = 0.08; routine-learning engagement: TP = 0.24; exam-driven engagement: TP = 0.13)."
