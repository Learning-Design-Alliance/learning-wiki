## Evidence

### Alshahrani and Windeatt 2012

Ali Alshahrani and Scott Windeatt. (2012). Using an e-Portfolio System to Improve the Academic Writing Performance of ESL Students. EUROCALL Conference, Gothenburg, Sweden, Proceedings. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED574901

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of self-report questionnaires and interview sessions in the second and final weeks, plus online tracking, revealed "improvements in the perceived value of writing and writing self-concept beliefs" in the experimental group; no effect size is printed.

> "revealed improvements in the perceived value of writing and writing self-concept beliefs among the experimental group of ESL students"
