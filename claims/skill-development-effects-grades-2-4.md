## Evidence

### Weidinger 2019

Weidinger, A. F., Steinmayr, R., & Spinath, B. (2019). Ability Self-Concept Formation in Elementary School: No Dimensional Comparison Effects Across Time. Developmental Psychology, 55(5), 1005–1018. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0000695

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

In the 4-Months Interval RI/E model, positive skill development effects within domains indicate young children use achievement feedback (grades) to form same-domain ability self-concepts from early elementary school, with α = .29 at end of Grade 2 in math and .21–.28 in German.

> "These effects were significant and moderate at the end of Grade 2 (␣ ⫽ .29, p ⬍ .001) and significant and weak at the beginning of Grade 4 (␣ ⫽ .12, p ⬍ .001; Table 3)"
