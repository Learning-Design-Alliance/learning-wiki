### Okuyama K 2025

Okuyama K, Lönn SL, Khoshnood AM, Assari S, Sundquist J, Sundquist K. (2025). School performance gap between non-immigrant and second-generation immigrant children in Sweden—time trends and contributing factors. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1521387

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted linear mixed models (model 2) on Swedish register data showed the female year*immigration-status coefficient became positive (+0.26, 95%CI 0.09–0.42), whereas for males it stayed negative (−0.16 to −0.21), so the increasing trend persisted only among males.

> "after accounting for parental education and neighborhood socioeconomic status, a decreasing trend of the gap was observed among females, i.e., the gap decreased by 0.26 points every year, as seen in Figure 1."
