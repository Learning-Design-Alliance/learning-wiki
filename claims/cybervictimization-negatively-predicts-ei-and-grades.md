Martínez-Martínez, A.M.; López-Liria, R.; Aguilar-Parra, J.M.; Trigueros, R.; Morales-Gázquez, M.J.; Rocamora-Pérez, P. (2020). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM results section: the tested model showed cybervictimization negatively predicting both EI and academic performance among 3451 Spanish secondary students.

> "The cybervictimization negatively predicted EI (β = –0.61, p < 0.001) and academic performance (β = –0.31, p < 0.01). In other words, cybervictimization has a negative influence on the EI levels of students, affecting their academic performance negatively."
