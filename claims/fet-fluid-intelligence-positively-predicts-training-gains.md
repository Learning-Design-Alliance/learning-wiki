## Evidence

### Lee Hyunkyu 2015

Lee Hyunkyu, Boot Walter R., Baniqued Pauline L., Voss Michelle W., Prakash Ruchika Shaurya, Basak Chandramallika, Kramer Arthur F. (2015). The Relationship between Intelligence and Training Gains Is Moderated by Training Strategy. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0123259

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc correlation analysis in the FET group of a 30-hour Space Fortress training experiment (25 participants per regimen), controlling for gender and baseline performance. The review of the FET correlation shows "r = .408, p<.05 for FET", a moderate positive association between fluid intelligence and training gain.

> "Post-hoc examination of the correlation between training gain and fluid intelligence showed that the direction of the effect differed for the two training groups (r = .408, p<.05 for FET, r = -.023, p = n.s. for HVT, see Fig 4)."
