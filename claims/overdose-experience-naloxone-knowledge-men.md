## Evidence

### Gicquelais Rachel E. 2019

Gicquelais Rachel E., Mezuk Briana, Foxman Betsy, Thomas Laura, Bohnert Amy S. B. (2019). Justice involvement patterns, overdose experiences, and naloxone knowledge among men and women in criminal justice diversion addiction treatment. Harm Reduction Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12954-019-0317-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Quasi-Poisson regression with robust standard errors in the 514-participant cross-sectional sample, comparing men with and without lifetime overdose experience. The adjusted prevalence ratio was "PR [95% CI] 1.5 [1.1–2.0]"; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "Men who experienced an overdose in their lifetime were 50% more likely to have naloxone knowledge than men who had not experienced an overdose after adjustment for age, race, education level, residence in temporary housing, lifetime heroin use, and injection drug use in the 30 days before treatment (PR [95% CI] 1.5 [1.1–2.0])."
