Evidence¶

Gül Kaleli Yılmaz 2015¶

Gül Kaleli Yılmaz. (2015). The Effect of Dynamic Geometry Software and Physical Manipulatives on Candidate Teachers’ Transformational Geometry Success. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.2610

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

ANCOVA on the construction section with its pre-test as covariate; CG vs. TG mean difference = 3.78 and MG vs. TG = 3.52, both p < .001, but CG vs. MG = .25, p > .05. The article attributes the equality to both groups applying transformations one-by-one themselves.

"There was a significant mean difference in the gain scores of students in the Computer Group, Manipulatives Group, and Traditional Group with respect to the construction section of the test [F(2,111) = 86.92, p < .001, η 2 = .61]"
