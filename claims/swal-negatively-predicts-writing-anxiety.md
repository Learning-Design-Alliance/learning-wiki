## Evidence

### Siyi Cao 2025

Siyi Cao, Linping Zhong, Chao Wang. (2025). The Impact of Student Writing Assessment Literacy on Psychological Factors: An Ordinal Logistic Regression Analysis. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.00004](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.00004)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Ordinal logistic regression in the same 103-student questionnaire study, predicting percentile-based writing anxiety levels from SWAL. The model was statistically significant, and the prediction probability chart showed the low anxiety group dominant at higher SWAL levels.

> "The model demonstrated statistical significance (χ²(1) = 45.13, p < .001), indicating that SWAL is a significant predictor of writing anxiety. The results revealed that for each one-unit increase in SWAL, the likelihood of being classified into a higher level of writing anxiety decreased significantly(B=-3.32, SE=0.59, p<.001)."
