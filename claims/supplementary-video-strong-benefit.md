## Evidence

### Noetel 2021

Noetel, M., Griffith, S., Delaney, O., Sanders, T., Parker, P., del Pozo Cruz, B., & Lonsdale, C. (2021). Video Improves Learning in Higher Education: A Systematic Review. Review of Educational Research, 91(2), 204–236. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654321990713

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 34 randomised comparisons (n = 64 effect sizes) where one condition received existing content plus videos. The review reports "a strong, significant effect of providing students with supplemental videos (g = 0.88, 95% CI [0.62, 1.13])" with high heterogeneity (I²₂ = 0.55, I²₃ = 0.37).

> "There was a strong, significant effect of providing students with supplemental videos (g = 0.88, 95% CI [0.62, 1.13], n = 64, k = 34)."
