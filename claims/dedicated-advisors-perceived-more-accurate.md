## Evidence

### Nodine 2012

Nodine, T., Jaeger, L., Venezia, A., and Bracco, K. R., with research support from Public Agenda. (2012). Connection by Design: Students' Perceptions of Their Community College Experiences. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED541651

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the focus groups, participants with program-dedicated advisors described tailored support, such as an advisor who mapped out required courses at their first meeting. The report states "Focus group participants were more likely to be positive about their experience with advisors if their advisor was linked with a specific area or focus." Such advisors were perceived as providing more accurate, consistent, and personalized information.
