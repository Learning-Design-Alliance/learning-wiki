Bird T. Baldwin 1922¶

Bird T. Baldwin. (1922). The Relation Between Mental and Physical Growth. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XIII, No. 1. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/13/1/193

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Correlational analysis of consecutive height measurements in the Iowa longitudinal growth-curve series. The author reports that "the coefficients of correlation for 125 boys and girls at 6 or 9 years of age and 6 years later range from +0.735 to +0.944."

"Using the Pearson method, the coefficients of correlation for 125 boys and girls at 6 or 9 years of age and 6 years later range from +0.735 to +0.944."
