New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform 2022¶

New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform, while many other places did not. (2022). UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

This finding comes from the mixed-methods study combining districtwide surveys with interviews of educators, school leaders, and district officials. The release identifies "the most important support strategy for instructional change" as professional learning. No effect size is printed.

"Professional learning—defined broadly to include teacher collaboration, coaching, and workshops—was the most important support strategy for instructional change."
