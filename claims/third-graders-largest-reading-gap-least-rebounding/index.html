Karyn Lewis 2022¶

Karyn Lewis, Megan Kuhfeld. (2022). Progress towards pandemic recovery: Continued signs of rebounding achievement at the start of the 2022-23 school year. NWEA Research brief. https://www.nwea.org/research-publications/

The brief reports that "The youngest students (current third-graders who were kindergarteners when the pandemic began) have the largest reading achievement gap and showed the least rebounding" in MAP Growth reading data. The authors attribute this to disrupted learning during a critical juncture for foundational reading skills.
