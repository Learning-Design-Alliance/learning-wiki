## Evidence

### Hartmann Lilly 2021

Hartmann Lilly, Kaden Jens J., Strohmer Renate. (2021). Authentic SP-based teaching in spite of COVID-19 – is that possible?. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001417](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001417)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the post-unit oral feedback, "a majority of the SPs and students interviewed" criticized non-verbal aspects of the conversation and the group dynamics and atmosphere of the classroom relative to usual teaching.

> "One point of criticism, which was mentioned by a majority of the SPs and students interviewed, related to the non-verbal aspects of the conversation as well as the group dynamics and atmosphere of the classroom."

### Hartmann Lilly 2021 (2)

Hartmann Lilly, Kaden Jens J., Strohmer Renate. (2021). Authentic SP-based teaching in spite of COVID-19 – is that possible?. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001417](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001417)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observation of the structured feedback given after conversations in the digital units: feedback centered on verbal communication and technique application, while "non-verbal aspects such as eye contact, posture and distance were assessed less intensively".

> "The feedback at the end of a conversation was mainly related to verbal aspects of communication and the application of the conversation techniques learned, whereas non-verbal aspects such as eye contact, posture and distance were assessed less intensively."

### Hartmann Lilly 2021 (3)

Hartmann Lilly, Kaden Jens J., Strohmer Renate. (2021). Authentic SP-based teaching in spite of COVID-19 – is that possible?. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001417](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001417)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of the post-encounter discussion phase with prior face-to-face sessions: group emotionality and group-dynamics intensity "fell significantly compared to the face-to-face sessions", which the authors say limited authenticity and quality of the learning experience.

> "The emotionality of the group and the intensity of the group dynamics in the discussion phase after the SP encounter fell significantly compared to the face-to-face sessions, which limited the authenticity and quality of the learning experience."
