Dawei Xie 2026¶

Dawei Xie, Khalil Anderson, Tochukwu Eze, Chenghong Lin, Bookyung Shin, and Marcelo Worsley. (2026). CLARA: An AI-Augmented Analytics Dashboard for Collaboration Literacy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17259

q2 · i? · design · r2

LLM-judged structured comparison of written analyses and key-evidence fields across all human-human and human-LLM pairs for 10 discussions, run three times with randomized presentation order. The article reports no overall difference on either 1-5 criterion; equivalence was not otherwise tested.

"No significant difference between human-human and human-LLM pairs was found on either criterion(Table 2; behavioral alignment:3.50vs.3.43,p=.370; evidence correspondence:2.82 vs.2.65,p=.277)."

Dawei Xie 2026 (2)¶

Dawei Xie, Khalil Anderson, Tochukwu Eze, Chenghong Lin, Bookyung Shin, and Marcelo Worsley. (2026). CLARA: An AI-Augmented Analytics Dashboard for Collaboration Literacy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17259

q2 · i? · design · r2

Per-dimension Mann-Whitney U comparisons of judge-rated behavioral alignment found the LLM sometimes characterized collaborative behaviors differently from experts on Communication and Constructive despite comparable scores. Evidence correspondence did not differ significantly on any individual dimension.

"Two dimensions showed significantly lower human-LLM behavioral alignment: Communication (3.00vs.3.63,p=.042) and Constructive (3.03vs.3.67,p=.020), indicating that on these dimensions the LLM sometimes characterized collaborative behaviors differently from experts despite assigning comparable scores."
