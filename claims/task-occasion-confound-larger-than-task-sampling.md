## Evidence

### Shavelson et al. 1999

Richard J. Shavelson, Maria Araceli Ruiz-Primo, Edward W. Wiley. (1999). Note on Sources of Sampling Variability in Science Performance Assessments. Journal of Educational Measurement, 36(1), 61-71. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Note+on+Sources+of+Sampling+Variability+in+Science+Performance+Assessments

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Re-analysis of the Ruiz-Primo et al. (1993) retest data in a person (n = 26) x rater (n = 2) x task (n = 2) x occasion (n = 2) G study, reproduced as Table 1. The authors note "the variance component for pt is .063 and for pto is 1.16", so the confounded task-occasion source dominates error variance.

> "As can be seen, the variance component for pt is .063 and for pto is 1.16."
