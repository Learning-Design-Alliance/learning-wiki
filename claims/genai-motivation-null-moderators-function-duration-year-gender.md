`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Null moderator analyses within the meta-analysis: intervention duration and motivation type subgroups were non-significant, as were publication year and gender meta-regressions ("QModel (1, k = 25) = 0.25, p = 0.614" for gender). No effect sizes are printed for these null contrasts.

> "Between-subgroup diﬀerences were not signiﬁcant across intervention duration (QBET = 1.475, df = 2, p > 0.05) and type ofmotivation( QBET =1.415,df =1,p >0.05).
