Descriptive analysis of college enrollment among CPS high school graduates. Differences were substantial for long-term English Learners without IEPs and late-arriving English Learners.

"While 20% of CPS high school graduates enrolled in a two-year college, English Learners had enrollment rates of 25% or higher ( see Figure 6 )."

Regression models adjusting for student characteristics (Table A.6) found gaps diminished for most groups, as Latinx students were generally more likely to attend two-year colleges and most English Learners were Latinx.

"However, the substantial differences in two-year enrollment rates remained for late-arriving English Learners, when compared to similar students."
