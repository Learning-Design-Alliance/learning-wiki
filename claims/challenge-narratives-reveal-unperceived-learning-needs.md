## Evidence

### McConnell Meghan 2018

McConnell Meghan, Gu Ada, Arshad Aysha, Mokhtari Arastoo, Azzam Khalid. (2018). An innovative approach to identifying learning needs for intrinsic CanMEDS roles in continuing professional development. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2018.1497374

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

This is the authors' interpretation offered in the Discussion, not a separately tested result; the article reports that the frequency discrepancies observed between the two questions motivate this interpretation, hedged with "may provide insight into unperceived learning needs".

> "Such discrepancies suggest that narratives describing challenges encountered in practice may provide information not obtained by asking individuals to explicitly identify their learning needs. In this way, qualitatively analyzing challenges may provide insight into unperceived learning needs in personal and professional competencies."
