## Evidence

### Öz 2015

Öz, H., & Efecioğlu, E. (2015). Graphic novels: An alternative approach to teach English as a foreign language. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 11(1), 75-90. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105203

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same study, independent-samples t-tests showed no significant differences between groups on comprehension, quotations, compare/contrast, cause-effect, or writing subtests. The authors note that "the findings of the t-test showed no significant difference, t (54) =0.350, p=0.365, p>0.05, between reading comprehension and writing skills of experimental and those of control group."
