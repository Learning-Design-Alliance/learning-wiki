Evidence¶

Peiris D. L. I. H. K. 2022¶

Peiris D. L. I. H. K., Duan Yanping, Vandelanotte Corneel, Liang Wei, Yang Min, Baker Julien Steven. (2022). Effects of In-Classroom Physical Activity Breaks on Children’s Academic Performance, Cognition, Health Behaviours and Health Outcomes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19159479

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of two intervention samples (n = 137 students) from a single Swiss RCT (Schmidt et al.) in which children enacted movements while learning French animal words. The pooled SMD = 0.80 was significant (p = 0.008) with I2 = 64% heterogeneity.

"The analysis reported a statistically significant, moderate to large effect size (SMD = 0.80, 95% CI [0.21 to 1.39], p = 0.008) for foreign language learning (Panel 4 in Figure 2) with a moderate to substantial amount of heterogeneity (I2 = 64%, p = 0.09)"
