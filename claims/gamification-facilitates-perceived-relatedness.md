### Liuyufeng Li 2024

Liuyufeng Li, Khe Foon Hew, Jiahui Du. (2024). Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10337-7

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis pooling the four interventions that reported relatedness data. The article reports a large significant effect, but the estimate rests on only four interventions, so it should be treated cautiously.

> "Only four independent interventions reported statistical data on relatedness, and these data showed that gamification significantly facilitated the students' perceptions of relatedness (Hedges' g = 1.776, 95% CI [0.737, 2.814], p = 0.001)"
