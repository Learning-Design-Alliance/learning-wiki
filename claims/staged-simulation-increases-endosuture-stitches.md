## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a paired study of 11 surgeons, the number of stitches completed in the allotted time rose across the three training stages, with an average increase of 1.36 points and a median improvement of 1 point per stage, statistically significant at the 0.1 level. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective, analytical, observational paired study of 11 surgeons in a surgical skills laboratory, comparing pre-test (V.1) and post-test (V.3) performance. The article reports an "average increase of 1.36 points" and median improvement of 1 point per stage, significant at the 0.1 level; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "There was an average increase of 1.36 points with the progression of training. In terms of median, each stage was identified improvement of 1 point accomplished. This improvement was statistically significant at the level of 0.1 (Table 1B)."
