### Simon Fauvel 2015

Simon Fauvel, Han Yu. (2015). A Survey on Artificial Intelligence and Data Mining for MOOCs. Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1601.06862)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The survey reports study [71], an A/B testing analysis that "challenged altogether the assumption that instructor involvement actually matters in MOOCs". No statistically significant impact of instructor participation was found on dropout rates, participation, or satisfaction.

> "Through A/B testing and statistical analysis, they demonstrated that instructor participation has no statistically significant impact on dropout rates, participation, or satisfaction."
