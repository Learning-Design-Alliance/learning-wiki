## Evidence

### Jaya Shivangani Kashyap 2026

Jaya Shivangani Kashyap, Christian D. Schunn, and Chandralekha Singh. (2026). The Gendered Cost of Lower Grades: Women's Physics Perceived Recognition and Identity Suffer Disproportionately If They Earn Less Than A Grade. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.11568](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.11568)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap mediation analysis in STATA controlling for pre-identity and pre-perceived recognition. Post-perceived recognition predicted post-identity (unstandardized β = 0.92, p<0.001), while direct grade effects on identity were non-significant (e.g., Non-passing β = -0.00, p = 0.92); the authors state the difference in significance "is not a matter of low power".

> "the direct effects of grades on changes in identity are non-significant, including even the two kinds of C."
