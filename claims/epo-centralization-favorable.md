Open-ended survey item Q24 on the value of centralizing EPO; 81 responses were double-coded into four categories with inter-rater reliability of 0.96, reported in Section 4.3.2. The most common objection was that centralized efforts would replace locally-organized events.

> "Roughly 84% of respondents expressed ideas in favor of centralization. The most prominent response in favor highlighted that respondents believe centralization saves time and avoids duplication of effort."
