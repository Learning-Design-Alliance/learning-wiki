## Evidence

### Padilla L 2022

Padilla L, Meleski WB, Dominick C, Athing C, Jones CL, Burns D, Cathers LA, Fields EC. (2022). Introductory patient communication training for medical physics graduate students: Pilot experience. Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics. https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13449

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the pilot, rubric answers for each participant's simulated patient encounters were combined into a single percentage per encounter, and preparation and application encounter results were paired for analysis. Simulated patient scores showed a statistically significant increase ("meanpre = 77%, meanpost = 91%, p = 0.022").

> "Performance scores assigned by the simulated patients showed a statistically significant increase (meanpre = 77%, meanpost = 91%, p = 0.022) between the preparation and application encounters (pre‐ and postinstruction)."
