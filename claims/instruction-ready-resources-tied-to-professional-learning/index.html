New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform 2022¶

New research: Chicago showed instructional improvements under standards reform, while many other places did not. (2022). UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

A districtwide analysis of survey and interview data on Chicago's standards implementation examined the relationship between instructional resources and classroom practices. The release reports other resources "showed a null or negative relationship with practices". No effect size is printed.

"Instructional resources provided by the district were helpful for implementing strong practices when they were instruction-ready and tied to professional learning around instructional practices, while other instructional resources showed a null or negative relationship with practices"
