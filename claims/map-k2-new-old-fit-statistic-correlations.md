# New and old fit statistics are highly correlated after difficulty adjustment, with outfit correlations near 0.99 for Mathematics and infit correlations around 0.7 for Mathematics and mid-to-high 0.8–0.9 for Reading

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pearson correlations between new and old outfit statistics reach 0.99 for Mathematics in both samples, while new–old infit correlations are around 0.7 for Mathematics and 0.85 for Reading. [→ He 2020](#he-2020)

## Evidence
