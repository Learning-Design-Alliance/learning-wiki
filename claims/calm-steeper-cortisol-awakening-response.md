## Evidence

### Harris 2014

Harris, A.R., Jennings, P.A., Abenavoli, R.M., Katz, D.A., Greenberg, M.T., & Schussler, D. (2014). A daily dose of CALM: Supporting middle school educators' wellbeing and classroom functioning through a brief stress reduction intervention. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562741

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Salivary cortisol assays in the ANCOVA analysis found "a significant intervention effect on the cortisol awakening response" (F = 5.88*, d = 0.62); control participants had higher cortisol at awakening.

> "There was a significant intervention effect on the cortisol awakening response, suggesting that the intervention was associated with a steeper awakening response than that observed in the control group."
