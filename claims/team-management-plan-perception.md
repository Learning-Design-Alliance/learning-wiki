Questionnaire analysis of the team management plan intervention per grade group (Fig 5), introduced at the second hackathon event to help teams document task assignments, responsibilities, and deadlines. All teams rated it above average. No effect size was printed.

> "High grade teams (𝑀 = 3.88,𝐼𝑄𝑅 = 0.06, see Team management plan Outcome in Fig 5) reported a higher values related to the team management plan intervention than other teams followed by low grade teams (𝑀 = 3.75,𝐼𝑄𝑅 = 0.38) followed by middle grade teams (𝑀 = 3.00,𝐼𝑄𝑅 = 0.00)."
