`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Ethnographic video study (24 hours of video during the unit) of two Grade 4 mathematics classes in a Canadian elementary school. The author's overarching argument from the findings is that “opportunities to gain agentive locally constructed identities” were intertwined with a geopolitical matrix of power.
