Correlational analysis of the transfer conceptual problem on exam 3 against quiz performance. The printed phi coefficient of phi = 0.2 is a correlation effect size in the 0.20–0.36 band; the coefficient "remains roughly the same for all attempts at around 0.2", with all correlations significant at α = 0.01.
