### Dversdal Renee K. 2018

Dversdal Renee K., Gold Jeffrey A., Richards Matthew H., Chiovaro Joseph C., Iossi Katherine A., Mansoor André M., Hunter Alan J., Desai Sima S. (2018). A 5-day intensive curriculum for interns utilizing simulation and active-learning techniques: addressing domains important across internal medicine practice. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-4011-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Final evaluation feedback collected from the 2014 and 2015 intern cohorts after the curriculum. The article reports "97% of interns in the included classes felt the curriculum was a good use of their time" and 89% endorsed expansion. Team building and camaraderie were described as unforeseen benefits.

> "97% of interns in the included classes felt the curriculum was a good use of their time, 94% felt it should be continued (.02% no, .046% unsure), and 89% felt it should be expanded across the second and third years of residency"
