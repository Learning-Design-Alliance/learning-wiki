Cook 1913¶

Cook, W. A. (1913). The problem of sex education. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 4(5), 253-260. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22The+problem+of+sex+education%22+Cook+1913

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the 'A Second Requirement' section. The author cites the failure of hygienic instruction to affect the tea, coffee, beer and tobacco markets as evidence that mere factual teaching does not shape later life, and draws on general pedagogy's question of how teaching affects the pupil's later life.

"It is therefore not enough that simple facts of sex shall be imparted, but these facts must be given in a vital, dynamic way."
