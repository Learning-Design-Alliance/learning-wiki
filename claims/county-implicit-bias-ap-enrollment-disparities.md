## Evidence

### Park Yeji 2026

Park Yeji, Riddle Travis, Sinclair Stacey. (2026). Associations between county-level racial attitude bias and disparities in Advanced Placement participation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2512555123

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression analysis of CRDC data combined with Project Implicit county-level bias estimates across three academic years. The association held across all three years with county covariates included. In 2017-18, a 2 SD move in implicit bias was associated with a "1.6 percentage point increase" in the disparity.

> "County-level implicit racial bias was associated with greater Black–White disparities in AP course enrollment across all 3 y (Ps < 0.001), even after controlling for various county characteristics (Fig. 1, Left)."
