## Evidence

### Chiu 2024

Chiu, T. K. F. (2024). A classification tool to foster self-regulated learning with generative artificial intelligence by applying self-determination theory: a case of ChatGPT. Education Tech Research Dev, 72, 2401–2416. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10366-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' interpretation, in the Discussion, of the consensus mappings in Table 2 from the 36-teacher Delphi panel: "the only one that ChatGPT learning activities satisfy among all three SRL phases is competence," while relatedness and autonomy fall short in some phases. No effect sizes were reported.

> "the only one that ChatGPT learning activities satisfy among all three SRL phases is competence. ChatGPT cannot adequately satisfy relatedness and autonomy at every SRL phase."
