### D. Sánchez-Guzmán 2009

D. Sánchez-Guzmán, César Mora, R. García-Salcedo. (2009). Intelligent Agents: A Physics Education Opportunity in Latin-America. Lat. Am. J. Phys. Educ. Vol. 3, No. 2. https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.4085 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0905.4085](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0905.4085)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports on the Andes Physics Tutor, an intelligent homework helper for physics developed at the University of Pittsburgh and the US Naval Academy, stating "Andes does increase student learning" while noting time costs and that it is free to use. No effect size is printed.

> "In short, although Andes does increase student learning, and it currently costs no money to use it, it does cost some time from both instructors and students as they learn how to use it."
