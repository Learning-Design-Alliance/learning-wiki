`q2 i?` 78% of simulation groups versus 52% of physical-apparatus groups reached a t′-score of 3 or greater, the threshold for a significant difference between periods. [→ Jeff Bale 2026](#jeff-bale-2026)

## Evidence

### Jeff Bale 2026

Jeff Bale, James Day, J. Ives, and D. A. Bonn. (2026). Constraints From Simulation Improve Experiential Outcomes in Laboratory Environment. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.30624](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.30624)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of students' reported t′-scores across rounds (Fig. 2 bean plots) in the randomized apparatus comparison shows the simulation condition placed more groups above the t′ ≥ 3 threshold for concluding the periods differ. Percentages are as printed; no effect size is reported.

> "52% of the student groups using the physical apparatus arrive at a t ′-score that is 3 or greater, whereas 78% of those using a simulation reach that level."
