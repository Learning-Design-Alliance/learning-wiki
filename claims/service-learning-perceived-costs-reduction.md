`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

In the same matched pre–post sample (n = 77), repeated-measures ANOVA showed the costs subscale fell from 4.21 to 3.44, "corresponding to a large effect (g = − 1.12; 95% CI − 1.41 to − 0.84)", indicating reduced perceived barriers.

> "Scores on the costs subscale decreased after the intervention, with LS Mean scores dropping from 4.21 (95% CI 4.10–4.33) at pre-test to 3.44 (95% CI 3.24–3.63) at post-test (p < 0.001), corresponding to a large effect (g = − 1.12; 95% CI − 1.41 to − 0.84)"
