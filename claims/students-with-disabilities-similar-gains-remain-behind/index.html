Evidence¶

Jeanne Wanzek 2019¶

Jeanne Wanzek, Greg Roberts, Sharon Vaughn, Elizabeth Swanson, and Katherine Sargent. (2019). Examining the Role of Pre-Instruction Academic Performance Within A Text-based Approach to Improving Student Content Knowledge and Understanding. Exceptional Children, 85(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918783187

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Descriptive subgroup comparison reported in the discussion of the class-randomized trial, where about 8% of students were identified with a disability. Students with disabilities gained "approximately 4.5 correct questions" in PACT versus about 2.5 in typical instruction; no inferential test or effect size is printed for this contrast.

"Students with disabilities receiving the PACT treatment gained approximately 4.5 correct questions on aver- age on the ASK content acquisition compared to about seven correct questions gained by students without disabilities receiving the PACT treatment."
