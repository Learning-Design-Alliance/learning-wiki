## Evidence

### Wakefield 2018

Wakefield, J., Frawley, J. K., Tyler, J., & Dyson, L. E. (2018). The impact of an iPad-supported annotation and sharing technology on university students’ learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.03.013

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sensitivity analysis split the sample on median mid-semester exam mark, final exam mark, GPA and class-mark option selection. While tablet coefficients varied, "the differences are not substantial and are all statistically significant at the one percent level".

> "These results are consistent with the main results, indicating the performance effect findings are robust and these variables are not associated with moderation effects."
