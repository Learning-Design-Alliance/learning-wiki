## Evidence

### Fredrik D. Johansson 2018

Fredrik D. Johansson. (2018). Machine Learning Analysis of Heterogeneity in the Eﬀect of Student Mindset Interventions. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.05975](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.05975)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Summary statement of the NSLM observational ML analysis of imputed CATEs; the article reports heterogeneity "in the range of [0 .1, 0.4]" moderated by the four listed covariates. No standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

> "and that heterogeneity in the range of [0 .1, 0.4] is moderated by school-level achievement level, poverty concentration, urbanicity, and student prior expectations."

### Fredrik D. Johansson 2018 (2)

Fredrik D. Johansson. (2018). Machine Learning Analysis of Heterogeneity in the Eﬀect of Student Mindset Interventions. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.05975](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.05975)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

From the stratified CATE analysis (Figure 4), where imputed effects were stratified by the five most important random-forest variables; urbanicity category D "appears to be associated with substantially lower eﬀect". This is a visual/plotted pattern, not a tested contrast.

> "The urbanicity of the school, XC, is a categorical variable for which Category D appears to be associated with substantially lower eﬀect."
