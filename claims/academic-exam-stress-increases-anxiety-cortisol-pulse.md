## Evidence

### Karbownik Michał Seweryn 2020

Karbownik Michał Seweryn, Kręczyńska Joanna, Kwarta Paulina, Cybula Magdalena, Wiktorowska-Owczarek Anna, Kowalczyk Edward, Pietras Tadeusz, Szemraj Janusz. (2020). Effect of Supplementation with Saccharomyces Boulardii on Academic Examination Performance and Related Stress in Healthy Medical Students: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12051469

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-protocol one-way repeated measures ANOVA within each group of the trial's stress-model validation. State anxiety rose from "35.9 (95% CI 32.1–39.6) points to 52.7 (95% CI 48.5–56.9) points" in the Saccharomyces group and similarly in placebo, both p < 0.0001.

> "A PP analysis found state anxiety to significantly increase from "basal" to "preexamination" settings in both Saccharomyces (from 35.9 (95% CI 32.1–39.6) points to 52.7 (95% CI 48.5–56.9) points, F(1,24) = 39.11, p < 0.0001) and placebo groups (from 37.1 (95% CI 33.2–41.1) points to 53.1 (95% CI 49.1–57.1) points, F(1,24) = 86.69, p < 0.0001."

### Karbownik Michał Seweryn 2020 (2)

Karbownik Michał Seweryn, Kręczyńska Joanna, Kwarta Paulina, Cybula Magdalena, Wiktorowska-Owczarek Anna, Kowalczyk Edward, Pietras Tadeusz, Szemraj Janusz. (2020). Effect of Supplementation with Saccharomyces Boulardii on Academic Examination Performance and Related Stress in Healthy Medical Students: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12051469

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-protocol repeated measures ANOVA of salivary cortisol measured by ELISA at basal and preexamination time points. Cortisol rose from "2.22 (95% CI 1.59–3.09) ng/mL to 3.20 (95% CI 2.51–4.08) ng/mL" in the Saccharomyces group (p = 0.030) and comparably in placebo (p = 0.034).

> "A PP analysis found salivary cortisol level to significantly increase from "basal" to "preexamination" settings in both Saccharomyces (from 2.22 (95% CI 1.59–3.09) ng/mL to 3.20 (95% CI 2.51–4.08) ng/mL, F(1,24) = 5.29, p = 0.030) and placebo groups (from 2.13 (95% CI 1.66–2.74) ng/mL to 3.06 (95% CI 2.24–4.18) ng/mL, F(1,24) = 5.03, p = 0.034)."

### Karbownik Michał Seweryn 2020 (3)

Karbownik Michał Seweryn, Kręczyńska Joanna, Kwarta Paulina, Cybula Magdalena, Wiktorowska-Owczarek Anna, Kowalczyk Edward, Pietras Tadeusz, Szemraj Janusz. (2020). Effect of Supplementation with Saccharomyces Boulardii on Academic Examination Performance and Related Stress in Healthy Medical Students: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12051469

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-protocol repeated measures ANOVA of self-recorded radial pulse rate at basal and preexamination time points. Pulse rose from "68.9 (95% CI 65.1–72.9) min−1 to 90.3 (95% CI 84.9–96.1) min−1" in the Saccharomyces group and from 72.9 to 83.9 min−1 in placebo, both p < 0.0001.

> "Pulse rate significantly increased from "basal" to "preexamination" time points in both Saccharomyces (from 68.9 (95% CI 65.1–72.9) min−1 to 90.3 (95% CI 84.9–96.1) min−1, F(1,24) = 74.93, p < 0.0001) and placebo groups (from 72.9 (95% CI 68.4–77.7) min−1 to 83.9 (95% CI 78.2–90.0) min−1, F(1,24) = 35.22, p < 0.0001) in a PP analysis."
