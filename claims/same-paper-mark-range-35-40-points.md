## Evidence

### Starch and & Elliott 1911

Daniel Starch and Edward C. Elliott. (1911). Reliability of the Grading of High-School Work in English. The School Review. http://www.journals.uchicago.edu/t-and-c

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Distribution analysis of the marks 142 teachers assigned to the same two photocopied English examination papers. The authors report that the range of marks given by different teachers to the same paper "may be as large as 35 or 40 points", far beyond the 10 points teachers typically expect.

> "It is almost shocking to a mind of more than ordinary exactness to find that the range of marks given by different teachers to the same paper may be as large as 35 or 40 points."
