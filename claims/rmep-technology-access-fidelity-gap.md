`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

This finding comes from the implementation study's fidelity assessment across all six core components. The technology access component required at least 70 percent of families to have internet and device access and at least 75 percent of students to complete assigned videos; neither metric was met in Year 2, and by end of Year 3 the video completion threshold was still not met overall.

> "The RMEP team completed five of the six core implementation activities meeting the standard of performance set by the evaluation team. The only activity that did not meet performance standards was technology access."
