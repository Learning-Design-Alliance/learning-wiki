## Evidence

### Logan 1988

Logan, G. D. (1988). Toward an instance theory of automatization. Psychological Review, 95(4), 492-527. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.95.4.492

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical analysis (type e) of why automatization appears slower in search studies: memorability can be modeled by slowing retrieval or by reducing the probability that a stimulus is encoded and retrieved, which lowers the power-function exponent.

> "The effect of varying retrieval probability is to slow the rate of learning by reducing the effective number of traces in the race."
