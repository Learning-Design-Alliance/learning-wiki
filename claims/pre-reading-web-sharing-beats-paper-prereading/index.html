Evidence¶

Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu 2011¶

Wu-Yuin Hwang & Guo-Liang Hsu. (2011). The Effects of Pre-reading and Sharing Mechanisms on Learning with the Use of Annotations. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932242

q3 · i? · causal · r1

T-test comparing the Experiment class (M=86.2, N=30) with Control B (M=75.58, N=31) in phase 2; the article concludes that "implementing a pre-reading sharing mechanism with Web-based annotation enlarge the effectiveness of pre-reading".

"the learning achievement ( M=86.2, N=30) of the pre-reading class with Web-based sharing annotation (“Experiment”) was significantly higher than the learning achievement (M=75.58, N=31) of traditional pre-reading class with paper and pen annotation tool (“Control B”) ( t=4.862, p<.01)"

Related Claims¶

  • The Web-based annotation tool alone did not affect achievement, but with a sharing mechanism it produced significantly higher achievement than paper-and-pen annotation — related
  • Pre-reading classes produced more annotations, and the sharing mechanism widened the annotation-quantity gap between pre-reading and non-pre-reading classes — related
  • Pre-reading significantly improves sixth-grade social-course learning achievement in both phases of an 8-week quasi-experiment — related
  • Pre-reading annotations made with VPEN revealed students' prior knowledge and preparation, helping the instructor prepare the lecture in advance — related
  • Students perceived the sharing annotation mechanism as useful for pre-reading and learning, and most found VPEN easy to use though some were frustrated — related
