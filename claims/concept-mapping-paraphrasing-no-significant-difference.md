## Evidence

### Marashi 2015

Marashi, H., & Bagheri, N. (2015). Using concept mapping and paraphrasing for reading comprehension. li-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 5(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097361

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test on post-test reading comprehension scores of 60 intermediate EFL learners randomly assigned to concept mapping (n = 30) or paraphrasing (n = 28) groups after 10 sessions of instruction. The article reports "no significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups at the posttest." No effect size is printed.

> "The results (t = -0.613, p = 0.542 > 0.05) indicate that there was no significant difference between the mean scores of the two groups at the posttest."
