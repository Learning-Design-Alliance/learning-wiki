Evidence¶

Gregorio Orlando 2026¶

Gregorio Orlando, Milan Groshev, Eduardo Castelló Ferrer. (2026). AIfred: Augmented Learning through Functional Robotic Embodiment at the Desk. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38737

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Three independent art and design professors ranked each participant's three drawings (baseline, ChatGPT, AIfred); inter-rater agreement was Kendall's W=.86. AIfred drawings were "ranked 1st in 33 of 36 cases (92%)". No standardized effect size is printed.

"Drawings made with AIfred were ranked 1st in 33 of 36 cases (92%), 2nd in the remaining 3, and never 3rd."

Gregorio Orlando 2026 (2)¶

Gregorio Orlando, Milan Groshev, Eduardo Castelló Ferrer. (2026). AIfred: Augmented Learning through Functional Robotic Embodiment at the Desk. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38737

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Inferential tests on the professor rankings: a Friedman test gave χ2 = 57.06, p < .0001, and pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed "every pairwise ordering is individually significant". Only test statistics are printed.

"A Friedman test confirmed that the three conditions differ overall (χ 2 = 57.06,p < .0001), and pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank tests confirmed that every pairwise ordering is individually significant"
