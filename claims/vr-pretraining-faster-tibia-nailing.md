# A single VR training session before a tibial nailing synthetic-bone exercise significantly reduces completion time (about 5 minutes faster, P=.007)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Orthopedic residents who practiced a VR tibia nailing module before the synthetic bone exercise completed it on average 5 minutes faster than those who did the synthetic bone exercise first (P=.007). [→ Sandipan Chatterjee 2026](#sandipan-chatterjee-2026)

## Evidence

### Sandipan Chatterjee 2026

Sandipan Chatterjee, Khairul Faizi Mohammad, Monica Ghidinelli. (2026). Evaluating the Impact of Virtual Reality on Orthopedic Trauma Skills Acquisition Among Surgical Residents: Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Perioperative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2196/79343

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized 2-period crossover study (cohort 1, AO Trauma Basic Principles course) comparing synthetic-bone tibia nailing completion time with versus without prior VR practice; paired 2-tailed t test gave P=.007, with mean times 18.48 vs 23.43 minutes (Table 2).

> "For the tibia nailing exercise, participants who trained on the VR module prior to performing the synthetic bone practical exercise were, on average, 5 minutes faster in completing the nailing exercise (P=.007; Table 2)."
