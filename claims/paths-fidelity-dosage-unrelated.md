## Evidence

### Alexandra Barlow 2015

Alexandra Barlow, Michael Wigelsworth, Ann Lendrum, Kirsty Pert, Craig Joyce, Emma Stephens, Lawrence Wo, Garry Squires, Kevin Woods, Rachel Calam, Mark Harrison, Alex Turner, and Neil Humphrey. (2015). Promoting Alternative Thinking Strategies (PATHS) Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581278

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational implementation analysis from the trial's IPE using four-level hierarchical linear models. The report suggests fidelity's influence may lessen once delivery quality is accounted for, and that classes averaged 20 lessons behind schedule.

> "Implementation fidelity and dosage of PATHS appear to be unrelated to academic outcomes. Given the extensive literature base supporting these two aspects of implementation as moderators of intervention outcomes, this was somewhat surprising."
