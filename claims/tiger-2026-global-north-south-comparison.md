A subgroup comparison of survey ratings for the Clinical Nursing role found near-identical priorities across Global North and Global South experts. The authors state that the Global South experts "voted for principles of nursing informatics and information and knowledge management in patient care" instead of two other areas.

> "While the core competency areas in the list of the global north (n = 98 experts) were identical to the overall list (Table 2), there were two differences compared with those from the global south (n = 18)."
