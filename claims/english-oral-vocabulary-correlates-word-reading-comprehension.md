# English oral vocabulary scores correlated significantly with word identification and reading comprehension posttest scores in this severely struggling sample

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` English PPVT standard scores correlated significantly with WJ III Letter-Word Identification (r = .35, p < .05) and Passage Comprehension (r = .56, p < .01) scores. [→ Denton 2008](#denton-2008)

## Evidence

### Denton 2008

Denton, C. A., Wexler, J., Vaughn, S., & Bryan, D. (2008). Intervention Provided to Linguistically Diverse Middle School Students with Severe Reading Difficulties. Learning Disabilities Research & Practice, 23(2), 79–89. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5826.2008.00266.x

`q3 · i2`

Exploratory correlational analysis linking English receptive vocabulary (PPVT III) to posttest reading measures. The authors note that although the comprehension correlation was expected, "a signiﬁcant relationship between students’ oral vocabulary levels and word reading skills" was noteworthy.

> "In addition, signiﬁcant correlations were detected between students’ English PPVT standard scores and their scores on the WJ III Letter-Word Identiﬁcation (r = .35; p < .05) and Passage Comprehension subtests (r = .56; p < .01)."
