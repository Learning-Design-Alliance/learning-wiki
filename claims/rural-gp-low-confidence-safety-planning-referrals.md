`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 25 GPs in a high-IPV/FV regional Western Australian area using an adapted PREMIS questionnaire. Results show "GPs were least confident (less than 50%)" for safety planning (42%) and referrals (48%), with 50% confident identifying IPV/FV signs and symptoms.

> "GPs were least confident (less than 50%) in helping a patient who has experienced IPV/FV to create a safety plan (42%) and in making appropriate referrals (48%). Only half of the respondents felt confident or very confident in identifying history, signs and symptoms associated with IPV/FV."
