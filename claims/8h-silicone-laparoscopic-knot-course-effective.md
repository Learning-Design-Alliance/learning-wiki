## Evidence

### Osorio Luiz Felipe Fernandes; Maciel Clarice Fraga Esteves; Gallo Carla Braga Mano; Sampaio Francisco J 2023

Osorio Luiz Felipe Fernandes; Maciel Clarice Fraga Esteves; Gallo Carla Braga Mano; Sampaio Francisco J. (2023). Evaluation of laparoscopic knot training in a silicone model. Revista do Colégio Brasileiro de Cirurgiões. https://doi.org/10.1590/0100-6991e-20233476-en

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructor-graded evaluation of knots on silicone molds after the 8-hour courses, in this prospective study of 56 physicians. Average good-knot percentages ranged from "21,4% (18-23%)" for the 45º needle exercise to "28.6% (15-33%)" for the back hand exercise; no effect size was printed.

> "an average of 26,0% (19-41%) of good knots when performed with the right hand; 24.0% (22-26%) when performed with the left hand; 21,4% (18-23%) when performed with the needle at 45º and 28.6% (15-33%) when performed with the back hand."
