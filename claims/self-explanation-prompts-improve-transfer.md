## Evidence

### Koedinger 2012

Koedinger, K.R., Corbett, A.C., & Perfetti, C. (2012). The Knowledge-Learning-Instruction (KLI) Framework: Bridging the Science-Practice Chasm to Enhance Robust Student Learning. Cognitive Science, 36(5), 757-798. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1551-6709.2012.01245.x

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article describes the Aleven and Koedinger (2002) experiment in KLI terms: changing instruction from pure practice to practice with self-explanation engaged more verbally mediated explanation-based learning, producing more verbal declarative knowledge in addition to non-verbal procedures, as inferred from contrasting isomorphic and transfer test items.

> "They found that adding prompts for self-explanation to tutored problem solving practice (without adding extra instructional time) produced greater explanation ability and conceptual transfer to novel problems while maintaining performance on isomorphic problems."
