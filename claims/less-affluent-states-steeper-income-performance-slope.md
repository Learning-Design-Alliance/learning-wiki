### Wodarz M.N. 2025

Wodarz M.N., Komarova N.L., Ma T. (2025). The functional form of the association between K-12 student performance and household income in U.S. school districts. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0329296

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-state comparison grouping states by whether their fitted rate parameter a falls below or above the overall mean (Results, Fig 6). The Fig 6 caption reports the low-a vs high-a income difference "is significant (p<0.05 by T-test) except for the median household income for ELA 8"; the low-b vs high-b comparison is significant for all cases. Only p-values are printed, no effect size.

> "For all the subject-grade combinations, the states with lower values of the rate of change, a, were characterized by a higher (on average) median household income (represented by the yellow bars). The states with higher values of the rate of change, a, were characterized by a lower (on average) median household income"
