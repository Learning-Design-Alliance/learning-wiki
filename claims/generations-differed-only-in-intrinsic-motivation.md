## Evidence

### Žuljević Marija Franka 2022

Žuljević Marija Franka, Buljan Ivan. (2022). Academic and non-academic predictors of academic performance in medical school: an exploratory cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03436-1

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort comparison of the 2010/2011 (N=26) and 2011/2012 (N=41) generations using Mann-Whitney tests with Bonferroni correction (cut-off P=0.004), followed by stepwise logistic regression. The regression "only confirmed intrinsic motivation to be significantly different" with OR=1.47 (95%CI=1.12-1.93, P=0.005); no standardised effect size is printed.

> "A stepwise logistic regression model only confirmed intrinsic motivation to be significantly different (OR=1.47, 95%CI=1.12-1.93, P=0.005), while extrinsic motivation and other variables were not."
