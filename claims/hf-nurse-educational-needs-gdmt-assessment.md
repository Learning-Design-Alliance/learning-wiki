Open-ended responses from the Experience and education section of the survey, in which nurses documented key educational needs: "improved knowledge on GDMT" and "advanced clinical knowledge and skills to undertake comprehensive clinical assessment". Many nurses also sought greater awareness of educational opportunities, external and internal.

> "The main needs expressed included: (i) improved knowledge on GDMT 'know and optimize evidence‐based medicine', (ii) advanced clinical knowledge and skills to undertake comprehensive clinical assessment 'thorough physical examinations to recognize decompensation'."
