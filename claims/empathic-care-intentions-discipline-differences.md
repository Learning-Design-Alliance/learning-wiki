## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Empathic care intentions differed significantly across disciplines (p < 0.001), with Dentistry and Medicine students scoring significantly higher than Nursing and Pharmacy students. [→ Müller AM 2026](#muller-am-2026)

## Evidence

### Müller AM 2026

Müller AM, Tan CGL, Lau STL, Gallagher PJ. (2026). Selecting for empathy? A cross-sectional study on empathy and empathic care intentions in newly enrolled dentistry, medicine, nursing, and pharmacy students in Singapore to inform admissions and training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09510-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 632 students using a custom-designed 7-item empathic care intentions instrument; the Results section reports the discipline pairwise comparisons quoted above.

> "Pairwise comparisons indicated that Dentistry and Medicine students had significantly higher scores compared to Nursing and Pharmacy students (p = 0.006 for the comparison between Dentistry and Nursing students, p < 0.005 for the comparison between Dentistry and Pharmacy students, p < 0.001 for comparisons between Medicine and Nursing as well as Pharmacy students)."
