## Evidence

### Dr Jo Rose 2017

Dr Jo Rose, Professor Sally Thomas, Dr Lei Zhang, Dr Anna Edwards, Dr Andres Augero, Ms Pooneh Roney. (2017). Research Learning Communities: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581267

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Multi-level modelling of round three Teacher Research Use Survey responses comparing intervention and control schools, taking account of nesting of teachers within schools. The report cautions that measures 3 to 6 have poor internal reliability, limiting conclusions.

> "teachers in intervention schools typically reported a greater disposition towards use of research evidence to inform their practice, in comparison to teachers in control schools, although once teacher background characteristics (such as leadership status and holding a postgraduate qualification) were controlled for this result was not statistically significant."
