## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A pre-test t-test found no statistically significant difference between the TIP group and the educational games group in prior literacy knowledge. [→ Gahwaji 2016](#gahwaji-2016)

## Evidence

### Gahwaji 2016

Gahwaji, N. (2016). The Effects Of Two Different Instructional Programmes On Literacy Skills Of Kindergarten Children. Journal of International Education Research, 12(1). https://journals.cluteonline.com/index.php/JIER

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-test administration of the literacy skill assessment to both groups: TIP group M=11.619 (SD=3.154), games group M=11.550 (SD=2.929); the t-test showed "a lack of statistical significant different" at a=0.05. Equivalence was established, not tested for equivalence margins.

> "As a result of the t-test, it was found that there is a lack of statistical significant different at the level of significance (a=0.05) as the calculated value for the “t” was 0.073."
