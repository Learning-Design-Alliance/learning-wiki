Shin Sujin, Choi Jaehwa, Hong Eunmin, Lee Miji, Go Jinseon, Yu Subin, Lee Minjae. (2026). Development of vignette-based evaluation with generative artificial intelligence (VEGA) for nursing students: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09685-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Preliminary survey with ten separate undergraduate nursing students who used VEGA at least three times (three item sets, 18 items) on a 4-point Likert scale. The article reports "the case scenarios were rated as easy to understand (3.7 ± 0.5), the items as understandable (3.5 ± 0.5), and the feedback as clear (3.6 ± 0.5)."

> "Specifically, the case scenarios were rated as easy to understand (3.7 ± 0.5), the items as understandable (3.5 ± 0.5), and the feedback as clear (3.6 ± 0.5)."
