### S. Bansal 2025

S. Bansal, M. Yadav, J. Zhou, R. A. Ebert‐Allen, R. M. Klute, W. F. Bond, S. Bhat. (2025). Medical Students' Perception of Automated Note Feedback After Simulated Encounters. The Clinical Teacher 22(6): e70273. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70273

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of five focus groups (28 students, 7–10 per group) using an inductive approach with MaxQDA. Students described the feedback as "easy to go through the whole thing broken down into different components" and noted higher learning and retention value.

> "students found the automated feedback visually appealing, with an example quote stating that 'it was easy to go through the whole thing broken down into different components'. Automated feedback has higher learning and retention value."
