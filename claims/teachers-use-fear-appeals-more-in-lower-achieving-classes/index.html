Putwain 2021¶

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Kutuk, G. (2021). Warning Students of the Consequences of Examination Failure: An Effective Strategy for Promoting Student Engagement? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1329876

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Between-level ML-SEM finding. The article states this is the first study to show teachers use more frequent fear appeals in lower achieving classes, supporting the conceptualisation of fear appeals as a teacher strategy to raise engagement and achievement.

"it was notable that there were negative class-level relations between Year 9 mathematics achievement and subsequent use of fear appeals by teachers"
