Pre/post responses to Q6 on the shape of space. The article reports the no-shape decrease and states the intervention "had limited success in conveying the geometry of space around celestial objects", while noting new conceptualised answers like 'the shape changes with gravity'.

> "9.2% of post-intervention participants shown deeper understanding by correctly noting that space is curved around masses but flat otherwise."
