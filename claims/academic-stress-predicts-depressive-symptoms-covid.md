The link among academic stress, sleep disturbances, depressive symptoms, academic performance, and the moderating role of resourcefulness in health professions students during COVID-19 pandemic. Journal of Professional Nursing, 46, 83-91. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.profnurs.2023.02.010

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression analysis (n = 93) in this cross-sectional survey of health professions students found academic stress explained 34% of variance in depressive symptoms; "Academic stress significantly explained 34 % of the variance in depressive symptoms (model 1)". Model 1 standardized beta was 0.603 (p ≤ .001).
