Evidence¶

Benjamin Lira 2025¶

Benjamin Lira, Todd Rogers, Daniel G. Goldstein, Lyle Ungar, and Angela L. Duckworth. (2025). Coach not crutch: Evidence that AI can improve writing skill despite reducing effort. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02880

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Exit questionnaire in the Study 2 experiment (N=2,238). Participants who practiced with AI "reported learning about as much as participants who had practiced without it or not practiced", with all self-report differences |ds|≤0.10 and non-significant.

"participants who had practiced with AI reported learning about as much as participants who had practiced without it or not practiced. Likewise, they judged their writing skill similarly to those who had either practiced alone or done no practice at all (|ds|≤ 0.10,ps >.05)"
