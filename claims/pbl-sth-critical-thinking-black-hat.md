`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section of the comparative cohort evaluation of 15 residents. The article reports that in traditional sessions 30% engaged in critical discussions, while at the "Black Hat" phase "93.3% of the residents critically evaluated potential pitfalls in the diagnosis". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "In traditional method, only 30% of the residents actively engaged in critical discussions regarding differential diagnoses or potential risks during the session. In PBL- STH, at the "Black Hat" phase, 93.3% of the residents critically evaluated potential pitfalls in the diagnosis"
