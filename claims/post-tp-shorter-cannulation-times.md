## Evidence

### Cheng X 2025

Cheng X, Zhang J, Huang G, Zhou J, Xiong J. (2025). Evaluation of the learning curve for ultrasound-guided distal radial artery cannulation in novices using a cumulative sum analysis: A prospective observational study. Medicine 2025;104:46(e45962). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000045962

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pre-TP versus post-TP comparison of 60 learning-phase and 120 proficiency-phase procedures; Table 2 additionally reports identification time 210 vs 112 s and cannulation time 165 vs 114 s, each P < .001.

> "The overall procedural time was significantly shorter in the post-TP group (mean 226 seconds; 95% CI: 218–234 seconds) compared to the pre-TP group (mean 374 seconds; 95% CI: 348–400 seconds) (P < .001)."
