Chun-Yi Lee. (2015). The Effects of Online Peer Assessment and Family Entrepreneurial Experience on Students' Business Planning Performance. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 14(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1057346

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

3x2 ANCOVA with first-round scores as covariate in the quasi-experiment; the interaction term was significant (F = 3.659, P = 0.029); main effects of family experience (F=19.785) and mode (F=10.708) were also significant at p<.001.

> "The ANCOVA results (see Table 6) indicate a significant interaction between the entrepreneurial experience of the family and assessment mode with regard to business plan writing (F = 3.659, P = 0.029)."
