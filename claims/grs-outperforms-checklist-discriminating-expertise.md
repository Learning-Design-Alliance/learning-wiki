### Corvetto Marcia A. 2017

Corvetto Marcia A., Fuentes Carlos, Araneda Andrea, Achurra Pablo, Miranda Pablo, Viviani Paola, Altermatt Fernando R. (2017). Validation of the imperial college surgical assessment device for spinal anesthesia. BMC Anesthesiology. [doi:10.1186/s12871-017-0422-3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-017-0422-3)

Blinded video ratings by two evaluators in the 30-participant simulation study (Table 2). GRS showed significant differences across groups (ABC p < 0.001), while the checklist did not (p = 0.095); inter-rater Kappa for GRS was 0.76. No effect sizes printed.

> "However, there was no difference in checklist scores between novices and intermediates and between intermediates and experts."
