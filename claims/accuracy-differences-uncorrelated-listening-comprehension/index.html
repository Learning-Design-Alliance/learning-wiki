Patsy M. Lightbown and Nina Spada 1990¶

Observational analysis of classroom data from the same four intact intensive ESL classes; the article states the accuracy differences "were not correlated with performance on listening comprehension tests". No correlation coefficient is printed.

"There was some evidence that these differences (which were not correlated with performance on listening comprehension tests) were due to differences in teachers' form-focused instruction."
