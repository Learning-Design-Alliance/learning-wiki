## Evidence

### Gladstone 2025

Gladstone, J. R., Schroeder, N. L., Heidig, S., Zhang, S., Palaguachi, C., & Pitera, M. (2025). Do Pedagogical Agents Enhance Student Motivation? Unraveling the Evidence Through Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10050-2

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Three-level meta-analysis with CHERVE of PA-versus-non-PA comparisons measuring engagement. The pooled effect was near zero and non-significant, with significant heterogeneity (Q(56) = 217.68, p < 0.001).

> "The result of a random effects 3LMA with CHERVE meta-analysis found that the impact of PAs on learners' engagement was small and non-significant (g = − 0.02, p = 0.73, kcomparisons = 57, kstudies = 8)."
