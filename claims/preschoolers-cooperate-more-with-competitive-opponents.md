## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Preschoolers' cooperation depended jointly on opponent type and learning over time, with higher cooperation toward the high-competitive opponent emerging in later blocks. [→ Zhang 2026](#zhang-2026)

## Evidence

### Zhang 2026

Zhang, R., Zhang, L., Hao, X.-M., Dreher, J.-C., & Qu, C. (2026). Learning to adaptively cooperate through social interactions during childhood and adolescence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00423-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on cooperation (Swerve) rates per 10-trial block for 52 preschoolers (Mage = 5.88) interacting with both opponents. The Opponent × Block interaction was significant ("F (2.29, 117.14) = 4.00, p = .016"), ηp² = 0.07, with block 3-4 differences favoring HCO (d = -0.39 and d = -0.46).

> "We found a significant Opponent × Block interaction, F (2.29, 117.14) = 4.00, p = .016, 𝜂𝑝2 = 0.07, indicating that preschoolers’ cooperation depended jointly on opponent type and learning over time."

### Zhang 2026 (2)
