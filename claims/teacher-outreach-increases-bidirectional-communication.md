## Evidence

### Bennett-Conroy 2012

Waveline Bennett-Conroy. (2012). Engaging Parents of Eighth Grade Students in Parent–Teacher Bidirectional Communication. School Community Journal, 22(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1001614

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Group-randomized quasi-experiment in nine 8th grade English classes (61 intervention, 131 control students); three classes randomly selected for teacher outreach. The printed effect size was phi coefficient = .607, a large association, for the difference in parent–teacher conversation rates.

> "The difference between the proportions of intervention class parents and control class parents (90.2% vs. 25.2%) who had bidirectional conversations with the teacher was significant (Mantel-Haenszel chi-square result = 70.40 (df = 1), p < .001, phi coefficient = .607)."
