## Evidence

### van Wijk 2024

van Wijk, E. V., van Blankenstein, F. M., Donkers, J., Janse, R. J., Bustraan, J., Adelmeijer, L. G. M., Dubois, E. A., Dekker, F. W., & Langers, A. M. J. (2024). Does 'summative' count? The influence of the awarding of study credits on feedback use and test-taking motivation in medical progress testing. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10324-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire analysis using the Active Use of Feedback scale (Cronbach's α 0.85) comparing formative and summative PT-groups via unpaired t-test; "3.2 (0.9) versus 3.1 (0.9)" with t(223): 1.09, no significant difference.

> "We found no difference in the mean total score on the items of AUF and ENJ (3.2 (0.9) versus 3.1 (0.9), t(223): 1.09, 95% CI -0.10-0.36)."
