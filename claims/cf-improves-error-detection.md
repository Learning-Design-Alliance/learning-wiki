# Exposure to corrective feedback significantly improves EFL students' grammatical error detection ability

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After a 12-week corrective feedback intervention, students' mean error detection score nearly doubled from pretest to posttest, a statistically significant increase. [→ Asassfeh 2013](#asassfeh-2013)

## Evidence

### Asassfeh 2013

Sahail M. Asassfeh. (2013). Corrective Feedback (CF) and English-Major EFL Learners' Ability in Grammatical Error Detection and Correction. English Language Teaching, 6(8). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p85

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study of 139 second-year English-major undergraduates in a Paragraph Writing Course receiving 12 weeks of corrective feedback. Mean detection score rose from "10.20, SD= 5.43" to "19.91, SD= 5.97"; the paired t-test was significant (t=-19.96, df=136, p=.000).

> "the mean response for the total nu mber of participants on the pretest for detection was 10.20, SD= 5.43 compared to a mean of 19.91, SD= 5.97 on the posttest"
