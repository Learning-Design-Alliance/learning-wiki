# ERP students with first-year GPA below 7 are not inferior to students with GPA ≥7 on intrinsic motivation for research, perceptions of research, and curiosity

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a non-inferiority analysis with a 0.5-point margin, the <7 group was not inferior to the ≥7 group on intrinsic motivation for research, perceptions of research, and curiosity in the third year. [→ Ommering 2021](#ommering-2021)

## Evidence

### Ommering 2021

Ommering, Belinda W. C., Van Blankenstein, Floris M., van Diepen, Merel, Gruis, Nelleke A., Kool, Ada, Dekker, Friedo W. (2021). The importance of motivation in selecting undergraduate medical students for extracurricular research programmes. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0260193

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Non-inferiority regression analysis (margin 0.5 on 7-point scales) of third-year questionnaire data from 54 of 59 ERP students, adjusted for gender and baseline scores. Confidence intervals for intrinsic motivation, perceptions of research, and curiosity excluded -0.5; adjusted βs were -.13, .29, and .12 respectively.

> "with 95% confidence, the difference in these motivational factors is smaller than 0.5 and students in the <7 group were not inferior to ≥7 group when it comes to these motivational factors."
