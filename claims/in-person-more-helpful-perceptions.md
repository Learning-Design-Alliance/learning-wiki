## Subclaims
`q3 i?` 89% of in-person versus 63% of remote students identified helpful aspects (Cramer's V = 0.28), while 14% of in-person versus 36% of remote students identified unhelpful aspects (V = 0.22). [→ Mueller 2026](#mueller-2026)

## Evidence

### Mueller 2026

Mueller, L. O., Bamrah, M., Petrie, K. L., Tour, E., McDonnell, L., Lo, S. M., & Meaders, C. L. (2026). The Fourth Credit Hour: How do we Teach in Biology Discussion Sections? CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.25-08-0185

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Contingency table analyses of coded open responses disaggregated by modality (Tables 5 and 6, N = 4053). In-person students identified all helpful codes more often except scheduled time and generic helpful; remote students identified all unhelpful codes more often except generic unhelpful.

> "A greater proportion of students enrolled in in-person sections (89%) identified aspects of the section that were helpful compared with only 63% of students enrolled in remote sections χ² (1, N = 4052) = 306, p = <0.001, Cramer's V = 0.28."
