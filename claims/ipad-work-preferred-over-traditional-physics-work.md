## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Van Dusen 2012

Van Dusen, B., & Otero, V. (2012). Influencing Students' Relationships With Physics Through Culturally Relevant Tools. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.4789739](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789739)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Student survey responses reported in FINDINGS (Fig. 2 comparison of iPad to traditional work). Students who found iPad work less enjoyable expressed a preference for laptops.

> "When asked how much they enjoyed doing iPad work versus traditional work , students articulated a strong preference for iPad work (Fig. 2)."

### Van Dusen 2012 (2)

Van Dusen, B., & Otero, V. (2012). Influencing Students' Relationships With Physics Through Culturally Relevant Tools. Physics Education Research Conference proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.4789739](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789739)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Observational finding from the same qualitative study: after iPad implementation students voluntarily used the physics classroom outside class time, which the authors classify under facilitated student play.

> "Unlike previous years, student began to regularly go into the physics classroom outside of class time to work on  physics projects."
