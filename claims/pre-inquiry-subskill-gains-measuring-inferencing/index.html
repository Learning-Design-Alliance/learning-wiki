Kang and Noh 2017¶

Houn Tae Kang, Suk Goo Noh. (2017). The Effect on Elementary Science Education Based on Student's Pre-inquiry. Universal Journal of Educational Research 5(9). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2017.050908

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Posttest t-tests on the Test of Science Process Skills subareas in the same two-class quasi-experiment. The measuring subskill favored the experimental group (2.39 vs 2.13, "significant difference (p <.05)"); the article likewise reports significant differences for inferencing, controlling variables and generalization.

"When we look at the difference of scientific process skills by group in the case of the measuring, 2.13 points in the controlled group and 2.39 points in the experimental group showed a significant difference (p <.05)."
