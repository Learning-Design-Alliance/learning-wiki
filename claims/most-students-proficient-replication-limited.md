Summative assessment of the 42-student activity using a four-level rubric covering planning investigations, analyzing data, constructing explanations, and designing solutions, combined with teacher observation notes. The author reports "most students demonstrated, at a minimum, proficiency"; no statistics are printed.

> "In all practices, most students demonstrated, at a minimum, proficiency. While most students were able to replicate their data, only some groups could do it a minimum of three times."
