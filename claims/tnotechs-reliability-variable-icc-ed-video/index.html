Evidence¶

Alexander Emily G 2023¶

Alexander Emily G, Denny Fraser, Gordon Malcom WG, McKiernan Cieran, Lowe David J. (2023). Evaluation of video review tools for assessing non-technical skills in emergency department resuscitation teams: a systematic review. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-023-00895-7

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative synthesis of reliability data across included T-NOTECHS studies; Steinemann reported "an ICC score of 0.48" and van Maarseveen 0.94 (0.87–0.98), which the review characterises as poor versus excellent reliability. Bhangu reported ICC 0.52 without commenting on reliability.

"The T-NOTECHS papers which used ICC were Steinemann et al [23] who had an ICC score of 0.48 for real-life resuscitations and van Maarseveen [20] et al. had an ICC of 0.94 (0.87–0.98)."
