## Evidence

### Cecilio-Fernandes D 2017

Cecilio-Fernandes D, Medema H, Collares CF, Schuwirth L, Cohen-Schotanus J, Tio RA. (2017). Comparison of formula and number-right scoring in undergraduate medical training: a Rasch model analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1051-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Preliminary Rasch analysis of the four tests. The article also reports "the highest correlation of the standardized residual was 0.35", below the 0.7 threshold for local independence, and unidimensionality checks with variance explained by items over five times the first contrast.

> "Regarding the items, the fit parameters were in the optimal interval from 0.50 to 1.50 [36], and the means were near 1.00, which is the optimal value for the infit and outfit."
