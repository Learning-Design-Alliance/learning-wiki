Evidence¶

Margarida Romero 2012¶

Margarida Romero, Pirkko Hyvönen, Elena Barberà. (2012). Creativity in Collaborative Learning across the Life Span. Creative Education. https://doi.org/10.4236/ce.2012.34066

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Nielsen (2002), that older adults "manifest more than twice as many user difficulties as younger users" in the context of ICT use. The review also notes they feel less confident about their own computer knowledge, citing Marquié, Jourdan-Boddaert and Huet (2002).

"but experience specific challenges in the context of the use of ICT, where they manifest more than twice as many user difficulties as younger users (Niels en, 2002)"
