`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Construct validity comparison of the full sample (n = 12 novices, n = 12 experts): novices incurred "n = 6" critical errors versus n = 3 for experts, p = 0.400; no effect size was printed, so the difference was not established as significant.

> "Novices were twice more likely to incur CEs during task completion (n = 6 [3 anastomosis leakage, 2 broken sutures, 1 catheter fixation] vs. n = 3 [3 anastomosis leakage]), but the difference did not reach the level of significance (p = 0.400)."
