## Evidence

### Balideh Donya 2026

Balideh Donya, Farokhzadian Jamileh, Miri Sakineh, Shahraki Sedigheh Khodabandeh, Motamed‐Jahromi Mohadeseh. (2026). Effects of an Aesthetics‐Based Educational Intervention on Compassion Competence and Aesthetic Care Quality Among Psychiatric Nurses: A Quasi‐Experimental Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70854

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest–posttest study with a control group of 90 psychiatric nurses randomly assigned (45 per group) at a single Iranian psychiatric hospital. The intervention group's total aesthetic care score rose from "43.26 ± 2.18" to 81.02 ± 6.26 (p < 0.001) while the control group showed no significant change (p = 0.11); Table 3 reports a between-group Cohen's d of 4.10.

> "Following the intervention, the mean total aesthetic nursing care score increased significantly in the intervention group compared with baseline and the control group (81.02 ± 6.26 vs. 43.26 ± 2.18, p < 0.001). In contrast, no significant change was observed in the control group (p = 0.11)."
