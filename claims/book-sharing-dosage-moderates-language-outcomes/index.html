Evidence¶

Dowdall 2020¶

Dowdall, N., Melendez-Torres, G. J., Murray, L., Gardiner, F., Harrtford, L. & Cooper, P.J. (2020). Shared picture book reading interventions for child language development: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Child Development, 91(2). https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13225

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Pre-specified subgroup meta-regression on expressive and receptive language outcomes, splitting studies at 60 minutes of contact time. Medium-to-high intensity showed d=0.54 (p=0.002) for expressive language; low intensity showed d=0.19 (p=0.15), i.e. no significant effect.

"For those receiving medium to high intensity interventions, the effect size for expressive language was d = 0.54 (CI: 0.26, 0.82; p=0.002), whereas for those receiving the low intensity interventions it was d=0.19 (CI:-0.10, 0.50; p=0.15)."
