### Paparo 2024

Paparo, G., Finsterhoelzl, R., Waldvogel, B., and Grillenberger, M. (2024). Teaching Quantum Informatics at School: Computer Science Principles and Standards. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.12340](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.12340)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' review of exemplary teaching approaches (not a systematic review) found gaps: outcomes like describing fault tolerance or discussing risks and opportunities of quantum computing lack direct coverage by existing approaches.

> "although not all defined learning outcomes, such as the ability to describe the concept of fault tolerance or the discussion of responses to the opportunities and risks of quantum computing, are directly addressed by existing approaches."

### Paparo 2024 (2)

Paparo, G., Finsterhoelzl, R., Waldvogel, B., and Grillenberger, M. (2024). Teaching Quantum Informatics at School: Computer Science Principles and Standards. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.12340](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.12340)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors state the standards are a first theoretical formulation; they report no empirical evaluation and call for use, testing and critical evaluation to refine them.

> "It is important to note that this is only preliminary theoretical work and that the standards need to be tested and evaluated empirically."
