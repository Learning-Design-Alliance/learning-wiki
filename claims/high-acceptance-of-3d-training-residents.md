## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Residents across years reported high acceptance: mean scores near 4 or higher for 3D simplification, enjoyment, and the necessity of combining 3D with 2D learning at the start of residency, with no between-year differences (p = 0.37–0.59). [→ Lin Chen 2019](#lin-chen-2019)

## Evidence

### Lin Chen 2019

Lin Chen, Gao Junyi, Zheng Hua, Zhao Jun, Yang Hua, Zheng Yue, Cao Yihan, Chen Yufei, Wu Guoliang, Lin Guole, Yu Jianchun, Li Hanzhong, Pan Hui, Liao Quan, Zhao Yupei. (2019). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the post-test questionnaire of the randomized trial, Table 1 reports items such as "3D images make complex anatomy easier" (3.88–4.11) and "It is necessary to combine 3D images with 2D learning during the beginning of the residency" (4.41–4.30), with non-significant p-values between PGY1 and PGY3&4 (0.37–0.59). No standardized effect size was printed.

> "the residents in the different training years responded similarly, and the mean scores were all close to 4 points or higher"
