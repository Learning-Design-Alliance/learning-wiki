## Evidence

### Finstad Anne Strand 2023

Finstad Anne Strand, Aase Ingunn, Bjørshol Conrad Arnfinn, Ballangrud Randi. (2023). In situ simulation-based team training and its significance for transfer of learning to clinical practice—A qualitative focus group interview study of anaesthesia personnel. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04201-8

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interview findings (interview 1, two weeks after SBTT). Participants described negative emotions after the first scenario; one said "Without the second scenario, I would probably gone home with a bad feeling of not coping with this teamwork in a real situation".

> "They suggested that the fear of failure and the ‘feeling of being tested’ influenced their performance; however, the second scenario was an opportunity to correct earlier mistakes and provided an experience of coping."
