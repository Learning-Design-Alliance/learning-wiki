### Armstrong M J 2013

Armstrong M J, Corbett C, Hodson J, Marwah N, Parker R, Houlihan D D, Rowe I A, Hazlehurst J M, Brown R, Hübscher S G, Mutimer D. (2013). Operator training requirements and diagnostic accuracy of Fibroscan in routine clinical practice. Postgraduate Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/postgradmedj-2012-131640

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Spearman's rho analysis in the 153-patient biopsy subgroup, comparing separate coefficients for valid (n=97) and invalid (n=56) LSEs against the modified Ishak score. The article reports "rs 0.577 vs 0.259; p=0.022"; no standardised effect size for the difference is printed.
