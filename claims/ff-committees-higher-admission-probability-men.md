`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Probit regression of the binary selection indicator, adjusted for the same covariates as the interview-grade regressions, with predictive probabilities plotted in Fig 4 for all six interviewer-interviewee pairs. The article reports the printed admission probabilities of 45.6% for CM and 40.6% in FF committees as a significant difference.

> "When two female interviewers conducted the interview, CM (45.6%) had a significantly higher probability of admission than CF (40.6%). Across commission types, the probabilities of admission for CF in Fig 4 do not differ significantly (the 95%-confidence intervals overlap)."
