## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kai 2018

Kai, S., Heffernan, C., Almeda, M. V., Heffernan, N., & Baker, R. S. (2018). Decision Tree Modeling of Wheel-Spinning and Productive Persistence in Skill Builders. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 10, No 1. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/210

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Frequency and proportion analysis of std-timeBetweenProblems across student-problem set pairs (Figure 6), a numerical/log-data result; the single feature's cross-validated AUC ROC was 0.554, so predictive power lies mainly in feature combinations.

> "The line graph in Figure 6 thus shows the proportion of students defined as wheel-spinning to be highest at very low standard deviation values of the length of time spent between encountering problems in a problem set. This implies that students who have consistent short delays between problems in a problem set are more likely to be wheel-spinning."
