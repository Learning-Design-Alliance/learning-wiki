## Evidence

### von Reibnitz D 2024

von Reibnitz D, Weinzierl A, Grünherz L, Giovanoli P, Lindenblatt N. (2024). Learning curve of robotic assisted microsurgery in surgeons with different skill levels: a prospective preclinical study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02114-2

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlation analysis in the 13-participant preclinical trial: "years of practice in surgery was significantly correlated with the mean time" (r = −0.706, p = 0.007) and mean SARMS score (r = 0.657, p = 0.015). Years of microsurgery correlated only with mean time (r = −0.638, p = 0.019).

> "The number of years of practice in surgery was significantly correlated with the mean time (Pearson r = −0.706, p = 0.007) as well as the mean SARMS score (r = 0.657, p = 0.015) across all the sessions."

### von Reibnitz D 2024 (2)

von Reibnitz D, Weinzierl A, Grünherz L, Giovanoli P, Lindenblatt N. (2024). Learning curve of robotic assisted microsurgery in surgeons with different skill levels: a prospective preclinical study. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02114-2

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Session-wise correlations: surgery experience with session 1 and 2 time (r = −0.595, p = 0.032; r = −0.713, p = 0.006) and session 1 score (r = 0.559, p = 0.047); microsurgery experience with session 2 time (r = −0.684, p = 0.01) and session 1 score (r = 0.587, p = 0.035). No correlation with Δ time or Δ score.

> "when looking at the three study sessions separately, a correlation between experience in surgery or microsurgery was only seen in the first and/or second session for time and SARMS score"
