Sommet 2015¶

Sommet, N., Darnon, C., & Butera, F. (2015). To Confirm or to Conform? Performance Goals as a Regulator of Conflict With More-Competent Others. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(2), 580–598. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037240

Same Study 1 sample of 139 university students; self-reported protective regulation measured with three items (e.g., compliance with partner's proposition). The predicted interaction was significant with partial η² = .05 (a medium effect by the η² convention), the simple slope significant only in the superior-competence partner condition.
