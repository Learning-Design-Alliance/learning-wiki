Kaplan 2025¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

Quasi-experimental analyses (Johnson, 2023, as reported here) exploiting the staggered LCFF rollout and the funding kink at the 55% high-need enrollment threshold. The report states the kink in funding allowed researchers "to show how additional concentration grant funding has caused improvements in outcomes for students."

"The study found that per-pupil funding increases in school districts that received LCFF concentration grants caused improvements in both math and reading achievement (see Figure 2 and Figure 3)."
