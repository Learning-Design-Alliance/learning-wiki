Bouchta El Batri 2019¶

Promotion of the Environmental Knowledge and Behavior through the Moroccan Syllabus of Sciences in the Middle School. Quasi-experimental intervention with three experimental groups (61 students) and control groups (100 students) from the same urban public school of precarious environment; Table 10 reports pre-test percentages below 5% in both and post-test results of 37% versus 98%, with "this percentage rose to 37% for the control groups and 98% for the experimental groups."
