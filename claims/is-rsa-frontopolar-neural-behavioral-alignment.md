## Subclaims
`q3 i2` IS-RSA showed significant neural-behavioral correlation in the frontopolar area (r = 0.23, FDR-corrected) for older learners under passive learning and in the intergenerational group, but not in the active condition, intragenerational group, or younger learners. ## Evidence

### Zi-Wei Liang 2025

Zi-Wei Liang, Zhi-Jun Zhan, Ying-Chen Liu, Hong-Zhou Xu & Jing Yu. (2025). Interpersonal neural synchronization underlies interactive concept learning in older adults. npj Science of Learning 10:75. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00368-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

IS-RSA in Study 1 correlated behavioral and neural dissimilarity matrices (Euclidean distances, permutation-tested over 1000 iterations); the significant frontopolar correlation appeared "for older learners under the passive condition" only, with no significant correlations in the active condition or for younger learners. Study 2 replicated this pattern in the intergenerational group (CH11: r = 0.23, p < 0.001, FDR-corrected).
