# Journal analysis shows heavy GenAI reliance in secondary research and transcription but student autonomy in planning and interpretation of primary research

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r1`–`r2` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Students reported heavy GenAI use for finding and organising information in secondary research, and for transcribing and summarising interviews, while planning, execution and analysis of primary research were mostly conducted with limited GenAI support. [→ Stefana Broadbent 2026](#stefana-broadbent-2026)

## Evidence

### Stefana Broadbent 2026

Stefana Broadbent. (2026). A study of GenAI usage by Design Students: Analysis of Survey Results and Journals of AI practices at the Politecnico di Milano in 2025/2026. Department of Design, Politecnico di Milano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17094

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-report journals from 100 students in the PSSD Research Methods Course (2025/2026), covering 15 subtasks of secondary and primary research on the topic of water scarcity in Sicily. The author cautions the journals are self-reports in an evaluation context.

> "what we see reported by students, is a heavy reliance on GenAI agents during secondary research for finding and organising information and greater autonomy in the tasks that require planning and interpretation."

### Stefana Broadbent 2026 (2)

Stefana Broadbent. (2026). A study of GenAI usage by Design Students: Analysis of Survey Results and Journals of AI practices at the Politecnico di Milano in 2025/2026. Department of Design, Politecnico di Milano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17094

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Journal analysis of reported verification workflows. Common strategies included cross-checking GenAI suggestions with academic databases, using multiple GenAI tools to diversify insights, and reviewing AI summaries for accuracy.

> "All respondents said that they manually verified AI-generated sources or summaries, often through group discussions or by consulting institutional reports and academic databases."
