### Cecil Julia 2026

Cecil Julia, Kokje Eesha, Kleine Anne-Kathrin, Schaffernak Insa, Vogel Lea, Angerer Selina, Lermer Eva, Gaube Susanne. (2026). The effect of AI-enabled virtual patient simulation on training outcomes and insecurities in psychotherapy education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09917-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized N = 87 experiment, an LMM on ICD-10-based multiple-choice knowledge scores showed no significant effects. The authors attribute this to the cognitive load of simultaneously navigating the interface, managing the diagnostic task, and conversing.

> "No significant main or interaction effects emerged for knowledge about mental disorders (Table 6), failing to support H3, which predicted greater knowledge improvements in the VP group compared to video-based training."
