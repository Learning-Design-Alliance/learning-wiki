Evidence¶

Lauc 2020¶

Lauc, T., Jagodić, G. K., & Bistrović, J. (2020). Effects of Multimedia Instructional Message on Motivation and Academic Performance of Elementary School Students in Croatia. International Journal of Instruction, 13(4), 491-508. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13431a

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired t-tests averaged across both study parts showed experimental-group students rated the activities as "significantly less fun than instructive and interesting" (fun M = 4.22 vs instructive M = 4.59 and interesting M = 4.58). Control-group ratings did not differ across features (e.g. instructive vs interesting t = 1.93, p < .058).
