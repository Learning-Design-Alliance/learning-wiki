### Ju Yubin 2026

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling on 691 valid questionnaire responses from medical students tested the hypothesized paths. The SEM results report that perceived interactivity acts on willingness to use "through performance expectancy (β = 0.180, p < 0.001) and effort expectancy (β = 0.428, p < 0.001)"; the corresponding Table 3 paths PI→EE (standardized 0.428) and PI→PE (0.180) were marked Support.
