## Evidence

### Li A 2026

Li A, Ma L, Peng R, Shi J, Zhang M, Zheng M, Wang Y, Huang Y, Ren J. (2026). Efficacy of an Imaging Anatomy Virtual Simulation Experiment Course in Undergraduate Medical Education: Nonrandomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13171472/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Nonrandomized controlled trial comparing 50 IAVSE elective students with 52 traditional-laboratory students on a 100-point anatomy theory examination; the experimental median was higher, "and the difference was statistically significant (P<.001, Figure 3)". No effect size printed.

> "The theoretical assessment scores of the experimental group were higher than those of the control group (median 85.00, IQR 84.25‐85.50 vs median 81.75, IQR 78.13‐83.00), and the difference was statistically significant (P<.001, Figure 3)."
