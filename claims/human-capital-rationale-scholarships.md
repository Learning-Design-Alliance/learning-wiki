### Anne C. Campbell and Emelye Neff 2020

Anne C. Campbell and Emelye Neff. (2020). A Systematic Review of International Higher Education Scholarships for Students From the Global South. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654320947783

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Systematic review of 105 peer-reviewed items (2010-2019) identified via the Scholarship Program Research Network bibliography, Google Scholar, and ERIC. Thematic analysis found human capital development the most common rationale, with the long-range outcome that skills contribute to innovation and development in home countries. The review reports skills are assumed "portable."

> "The first major rationale in international scholarship programs is based on the theory of human capital development, or the idea that skills and knowledge can be acquired through overseas education and brought home to advance the individual (micro-level), their workplace and their community (meso-level)"
