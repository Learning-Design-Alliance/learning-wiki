## Evidence

### Amy Courtney and Michael Courtney 2016

Amy Courtney and Michael Courtney. (2016). Secrets of Successful Science Projects. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1612.08947](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1612.08947)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Expert-opinion article reporting the authors' observations from mentoring dozens of student science projects; they state the tight hypothesis-method correspondence is "the most important feature of a project for success" and that a poorly considered hypothesis is a main reason for poorly executed projects.

> "The most important feature of a project for success in most competitions, symposia, and publication venues is a tight correspondence between a scientific hypothesis and the experimental method chosen to test the hypothesis."
