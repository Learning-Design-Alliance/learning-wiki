## Evidence

### Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes 2022

Mehak Maniktala · Min Chi · Tiffany Barnes. (2022). Enhancing a Student Productivity Model for Adaptive Problem-Solving Assistance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11257-022-09338-7)

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Controlled study results comparing posttest accuracy of the two HNU conditions; Adaptive mean = .76 (SD = .11) versus Control mean = .71 (SD = .11), with "Cohen's d = .45". The authors hypothesize this stems from effective prediction of when students need help, giving targeted rule practice.

> "we found signiﬁcant diﬀerences in the posttest accuracy between the two HNU conditions (t(72) = 1.75, p = .04), with the Adaptive condition ( Mean = .76, SD = .11) having signiﬁcantly higher accuracy than the Control (Mean = .71, SD = .11), with a moderate eﬀect size (Cohen's d = .45)."
