## Evidence

### Gordillo 2024

Gordillo, A.; López-Fernández, D.; Mayor, J. (2024). Examining and Comparing the Effectiveness of Virtual Reality Serious Games and LEGO Serious Play for Learning Scrum. Appl. Sci. 2024, 14, 830. https://doi.org/10.3390/app14020830

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann–Whitney U test comparing post-test scores of the two quasi-experimental groups; the article reports the difference was "non-statistically significant" with r = 0.09. Equivalence was not tested; both groups ended with similar mean scores (7.3 vs 6.8, Table 2).

> "the difference between post-test scores obtained by both groups was non-statistically significant and had a less than small effect size (p-value = 0.50; r = 0.09)."
