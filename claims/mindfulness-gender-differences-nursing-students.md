## Evidence

### Mathkor Darin Mansor 2025

Mathkor Darin Mansor. (2025). Mindfulness and academic performance among nursing students in Saudi Arabia: a cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports 15, 27381. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-11911-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney U tests comparing 51 male and 225 female students; Table 2 prints Cohen's d of -0.52 (non-reactive), 0.46 (non-judging), and 0.58 (awareness), showing "significant differences in non-reactive (p = 0.001), non-judging (p = 0.003), and awareness (p < 0.001) between both sexes".
