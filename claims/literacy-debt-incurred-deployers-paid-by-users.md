## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Nama 2026

Nama, R. (2026). Agentic Literacy Debt: A Structural Problem the AI Literacy Field Has Not Yet Named. Author's accepted manuscript, AI and Ethics (Springer Nature). https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27396

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Conceptual argument extending Petrozzino's observation that those who incur ethical debt differ from those who pay for it. The author argues this asymmetry is "what makes agentic literacy debt an AI ethics problem rather than merely an educational one." No data reported.

> "Critically, the debt is not borne by the organizations that incur it. It is paid by the users, patients, consumers, and citizens the agents act upon."
