## Evidence

### Baily 2010

Baily, C., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2010). Interpretation in Quantum Physics as Hidden Curriculum. AIP Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1063/1.3515251](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515251)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of Instructor B's second course (B2) with the first (B1). Despite adding explicit interpretive discussion late in the course, student responses on atomic electrons remained predominantly realist, similar to B1.

> "devoted two days of lecture time near the end of the course to interpretive themes in quantum mechanics, including a discussion of the interpretive aspects of the double-slit experiment, but without reference to atomic systems"
