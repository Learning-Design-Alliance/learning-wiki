`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Single cross-sectional survey study (Puljak et al., 9 health science institutions, 2,520 medical and nursing students) narrated by the review; motivation was measured with a 2-item author-developed questionnaire. The review reports 64.4% showed "an equal or higher motivational level to attend exclusive e-learning".

> "The first item focused on motivation to participate in online learning compared to classroom courses, and 64.4% of participants demonstrated an equal or higher motivational level to attend exclusive e-learning."
