Weighted analysis of IPEDS and College Results Online data for 543 four-year nonprofit institutions with at least 30 African-American students in both study years shows "41.2 percent of black students grad- uated in six years, compared to 40.6 percent in 2010," while the overall rate rose 2.8 points to 60.1 percent.
