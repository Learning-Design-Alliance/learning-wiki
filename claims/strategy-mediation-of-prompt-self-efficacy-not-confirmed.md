# The hypothesised mediation of prompt effects on self-efficacy via strategy use was not confirmed because prompts did not affect strategy use

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert 2020

Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert. (2020). The Double-Edged Interactions of Prompts and Self-efficacy. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09227-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression-based mediator analysis (Hayes 2018 approach) testing whether prompts affected self-efficacy indirectly via strategy use. Prompts predicted neither self-reported cognitive (β = 0.16, p = .18) nor metacognitive strategy use (β = 0.03, p = .78), nor note quality (β = -0.18, p = .12; β = -0.19, p = .11), so the mediation could not be tested further.
