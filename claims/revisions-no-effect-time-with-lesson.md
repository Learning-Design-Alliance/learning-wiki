`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on total seconds spent with the lesson. The article notes that, based on the means, "the revised lesson appeared to be read more quickly than the original lesson for the low prior knowledge group", but the interaction was not reliable.

> "Condition and did not affect the time spent with the lesson, F(1, 51) = .36, p = .55 and there was a marginal effect of prior knowledge group,  F(2, 51) = 2.91, p = .06, with time with the lesson generally being less with more prior knowledge."
