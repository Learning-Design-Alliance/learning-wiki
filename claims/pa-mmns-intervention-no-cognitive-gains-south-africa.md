## Evidence

### Beckmann Johanna 2022

Beckmann Johanna, Nqweniso Siphesihle, Ludyga Sebastian, du Randt Rosa, Gresse Annelie, Long Kurt Z., Nienaber Madeleine, Seelig Harald, Pühse Uwe, Steinmann Peter, Utzinger Jürg, Walter Cheryl, Gerber Markus, Lang Christin. (2022). Evaluation of a Physical Activity and Multi-Micronutrient Intervention on Cognitive and Academic Performance in South African Primary Schoolchildren. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14132609

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized controlled trial with 932 children in four quintile-3 South African primary schools, analyzed with linear mixed models adjusting for baseline, school class and age. The article reports "No significant time × group differences were observed" on the Flanker task; equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant time × group differences were observed in reaction time and accuracy on either the congruent or incongruent trials after adjusting for age and school class."
