`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate analysis in the same 100-CT eye-tracking study. The regression estimate for LPF main object fixation duration was negative (–0.055), "indicating that shorter fixation duration is associated with higher diagnostic accuracy". Neither years of experience (p = .0637) nor sample information fixation (p = .3194) were significant.
