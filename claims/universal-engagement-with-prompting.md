## Evidence

### H. J. Lewandowski and Noah Finkelstein 2015

H. J. Lewandowski and Noah Finkelstein. (2015). Redesigning a junior-level electronics course to support engagement in scientific practices. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.043](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.043)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' summary of the transformed-course coding results (N = 44), where 100% of students both identified the discrepancy and refined the model to include the meter's input impedance. Reported as a descriptive outcome of the quasi-experimental comparison.

> "Despite our students having come through transformed theory courses in the prior sequences, they do not resolve discrepancies be- tween model (theory) and measurement, even if they are aware of them. With explicit prompting students univer- sally engage in this expert-like scientiﬁc practice."
