## Evidence

### Conlin 2009

Conlin, L., Gupta, A., & Hammer, D. (2009). Where to find the mind: Identifying the scale of cognitive dynamics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.0491](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.0491)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative case analysis of video from a tutorial session in which four students worked on a truck-car collision question. After Dianna's outburst about the word 'intuitively,' the group shifted together from the blue behavioral cluster and worksheet framing to the green cluster and discussion framing.

> "The coordinated nature of the transition in behavior (from blue cluster to the green cluster) and framing (from doing the worksheet to having a discussion) makes it appropriate to think about the group as transitioning rather than four individuals separately making these transitions."
