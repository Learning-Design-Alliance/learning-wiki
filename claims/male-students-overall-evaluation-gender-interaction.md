## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a 2x2 ANOVA of overall evaluation scores, the student-sex by lecturer-sex interaction was significant, driven by male students favoring male lecturers. [→ Graves 2017](#graves-2017)

## Evidence

### Graves 2017

Graves, A.L., Hoshino-Browne, E., & Lui, K.P.H. (2017). Swimming against the Tide: Gender Bias in the Physics Classroom. Journal of Women and Minorities in Science and Engineering 23(1), 15-36. [doi:10.1615/jwomenminorscieneng.2017013584](https://doi.org/10.1615/jwomenminorscieneng.2017013584)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment with 121 analyzed participants (42 female, 79 male) watching identical video lectures by four actors; 2(lecturer sex) x 2(student sex) ANOVA on a 15-item overall composite (Cronbach's alpha .92). The interaction was significant, "F(1, 117) = 4.91, p =.029"; male students rated male lecturers (M = 3.88) above female lecturers (M = 3.44).

> "the interaction of two variables was significant, F(1, 117) = 4.91, p =.029 (Table 1 and Figure 1). Female students evaluated female lecturers (M = 3.73, SD = 0.70, n = 21) and male lecturers (M = 3.55, SD = 0.75, n = 21) more or less equally."
