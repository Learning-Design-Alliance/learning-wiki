Document review of MÜDEK-accredited computer engineering departments' published course programs (Sonuç section). The review found the programs give place to topics forming the foundations of needed information systems, and that "Her bölümün ağırlık verdikleri programlama dillerinin de farklı olduğu görülmüştür."
