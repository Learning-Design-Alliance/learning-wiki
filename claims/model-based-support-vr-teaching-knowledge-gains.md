## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Preservice teachers using model-based support with AI-powered virtual students showed significantly greater gains in teaching knowledge and skills than the no-treatment control group. [→ Chih-Pu Dai · Fengfeng Ke 2026](#chih-pu-dai-fengfeng-ke-2026)

## Evidence

### Chih-Pu Dai · Fengfeng Ke 2026

Chih-Pu Dai · Fengfeng Ke. (2026). Model-based support for teaching practice and self-efficacy in artificial intelligence-enhanced virtual reality teaching simulation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10592-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

An experimental study with 57 preservice teachers found that the model-based support group achieved a significantly higher adjusted posttest score on a teaching knowledge and skills test than the control group, with "η2 =.30".

> "The ANCOVA analysis results, controlling for the pretest, demonstrated a significant difference between the experimental groups and the control group, F (2, 57) = 11.59, p <.001, η2 =.30, R2 =.47."
