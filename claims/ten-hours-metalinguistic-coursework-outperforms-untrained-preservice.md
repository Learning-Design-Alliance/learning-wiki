## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After 10 hours of metalinguistic coursework, preservice teachers scored higher on phoneme identification (78.8%) and morpheme identification (72.9%) than preservice teachers who had not received metalinguistic content (49.7% and 64.5%). [→ McNeill 2018](#mcneill-2018)

## Evidence

### McNeill 2018

McNeill, B. C. (2018). Improving Preservice Teachers' Phonemic Awareness, Morphological Awareness and Orthographic Knowledge. Australian Journal of Teacher Education, 43(1). http://ro.ecu.edu.au/ajte/vol43/iss1/2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-course group comparison (Table 1) of 86 experimental preservice teachers against 36 comparison preservice teachers from Carreker et al. (2010), using percent-correct scores on the phoneme and morpheme identification tasks. The experimental cohort scored "78.8%" on phoneme identification versus 49.7%, and 72.9% versus 64.5% on morpheme identification.

> "The comparison showed that the experimental cohort out-performed the comparison preservice group in phonemic awareness and morphological awareness knowledge."
