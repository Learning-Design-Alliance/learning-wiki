`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Friedman test with Bonferroni correction comparing pre-test scores across the three WSLA sessions in both cohorts, showing "a marked and statistically significant decline (p < 0.01)" with printed medians for each session. No effect size printed.

> "Significantly, scores showed a marked and statistically significant decline (p < 0.01) as the WSLA sessions progressed, with scores of 6.67, 5.00, and 5.33 for sessions 1, 2, and 3 in the 2019–2020 academic year and 6, 5.01, and 4.68 for the 2020–2021 academic year."
