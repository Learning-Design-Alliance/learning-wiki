## Evidence

### Nian Zhixuan 2026

Nian Zhixuan, Li Mingchun, Liang Junwen, Li Yijia, Wei Ziting, Pan Liqiang, Fang Sen, Yang Xudong, Du Wenjia, Yun Xiangdong. (2026). Effectiveness of an MDT-CDIO integrated teaching model in orthopedic clinical education: a controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09758-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-designed 40-item satisfaction questionnaire (Cronbach's α = 0.921, CVI = 0.92) administered to all 60 interns after rotation; each dimension scored 0–20. Experimental group means exceeded control on all five dimensions, e.g. engaging teaching methods "17.3 ± 1.05" vs. "15.7 ± 1.12", t = 5.71, P < 0.001.

> "The results indicated that the experimental group reported higher satisfaction scores across all evaluated indicators than the control group, with statistically significant differences observed in all measures (P < 0.05). Details are presented in Table 5."
