## Evidence

### Fuoad Sura Ali 2022

Fuoad Sura Ali, El-Sayed Walid, Marei Hesham. (2022). Effect of different teaching/learning approaches using virtual patients on student’s situational interest and cognitive load: a comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03831-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intragroup repeated-measures comparison in Group 2 (n=70, collaborative VPs within a lecture); Table 1 prints a Friedman p value of 0.051, which did not reach the stated p < .05 threshold.

> "Group 2: Collaborative VPs session showed no significant difference between the median score at repeated time points (Table 1)."

### Fuoad Sura Ali 2022 (2)

Fuoad Sura Ali, El-Sayed Walid, Marei Hesham. (2022). Effect of different teaching/learning approaches using virtual patients on student’s situational interest and cognitive load: a comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03831-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Intersession Kruskal Wallis comparison across the three sessions among the two dental cohorts (n=69 and n=70); Table 1 prints p = 0.033 at 10 min, p = 0.002 at 30 min, and p < 0.001 at 40 min favouring collaborative VPs.

> "At 10 minutes, the VPs’ collaborative session showed a high median score of situational interest than both lecture and independent VPs sessions, and the difference was significant (p-value: < 0.033)."
