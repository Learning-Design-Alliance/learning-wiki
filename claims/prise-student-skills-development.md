## Evidence

### Martin O. Archer and Jennifer DeWitt 2021

Martin O. Archer and Jennifer DeWitt. (2021). “Thanks for helping me ﬁnd my enthusiasm for physics!” The lasting impacts ‘research in schools’ projects can have on students, teachers, and schools. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2104.04431](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2104.04431)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Keyword clustering of open-ended questionnaire responses from 140 students and 40 teachers produced a dataset of 79 unique skills; a word cloud showed "a large amount of agreement between the two groups". Skills were not mentioned in project resources, supporting validity.

> "All the skills listed are highly relevant to being a scientist, with the most cited being (in descending order) teamwork, research, data analysis, programming and presentation skills."
