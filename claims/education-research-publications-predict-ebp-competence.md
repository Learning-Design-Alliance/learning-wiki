## Evidence

### Sarıköse Seda 2026

Sarıköse Seda, Sevim Fatma, Alık Şebnem, Karaçay Pelin, Yaşar Özgen. (2026). Integrating Insights: A Mixed‐Methods Approach to Nurses' Competencies in Evidence‐Based Practice and Clinical Decision‐Making. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70525

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple linear regression (model 5) on 387 nurses' EBP-COQ scores; the article reports adjusted R² = 0.643. Standardized βs are as printed; the article notes education contributed "a 0.55-point increase" per unit.

> "increasing educational level (β = 0.554; p < 0.001), experience in research planning and implementation (β = 0.287; p < 0.001) and following professional scientific publications (β = 0.092; p = 0.009) positively associated with nurses' level of competence towards EBPs"
