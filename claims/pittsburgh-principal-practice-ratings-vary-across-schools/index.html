Duncan Chaplin 2014¶

Duncan Chaplin, Brian Gill, Allison Thompkins, Hannah Miller. (2014). Professional Practice, Student Surveys, and Value-Added: Multiple Measures of Teacher Effectiveness in the Pittsburgh Public Schools. Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic, Institute of Education Sciences. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Analysis of the principal-assigned professional practice rating found "Variation across schools" on that rating, which the authors read as a sign that principals' standards might not be fully consistent across schools. No variance statistics are printed in the available text.

"Variation across schools on the professional practice rating (which is assigned to teachers by the school principal) suggests that principals’ standards might not be fully consistent across schools"
