Evidence¶

Çetin 2017¶

Barış Çetin. (2017). The Influence of Pintrich’s Self-regulated Learning Model on Elementary Teacher Candidates in a Life Science Course. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i8.2460

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-samples t-test comparing pretest and posttest SRL total scores of 39 elementary teacher candidates in a quasi-experimental pre/post design; the article reports t(38) = -4.042, p = .000, a statistically significant increase in "self -regulated learning perception total scores".

"there is a statistically significant difference in the level of self -regulated learning perception total scores of classroom teacher candidates (t(38) = -4.042, p = .000 < . 01)"
