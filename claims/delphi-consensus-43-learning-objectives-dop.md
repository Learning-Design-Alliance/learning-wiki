`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Two‐round online Delphi survey (July–August 2024) with German university dental education experts in gerodontology or special care dentistry; 14 of 20 invited experts participated in round 1 and 12 in round 2. The article reports that "A total of six competence areas and 43 LOs were consented".

> "A consensus was reached for all LOs in the second round, with consensus defined as at least ‘moderate agreement’. A total of six competence areas and 43 LOs were consented (Table 1, Figure 2)."
