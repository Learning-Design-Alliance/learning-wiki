Secondary outcome of the same single-blind RCT (74 peer-led, 58 expert-led). Exact testing "does not reject the null hypothesis (p = 0.286)", with an upper confidence limit of 29.6%, so non-inferiority for High Passes was not established. No effect size printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "High passes were achieved in the OSCE by 31/74 (42%) in the peer-led group vs. 33/58 (58%) in the expert-led group. Exact testing in this case does not reject the null hypothesis (p = 0.286) as the upper limit of the one-sided 95% confidence limit is 29.6%, and therefore the true difference could exceed the 20% non-inferiority boundary."
