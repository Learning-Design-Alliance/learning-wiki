Descriptive analysis of the 123 between-group studies' effect sizes, presented in a boxplot (Figure 2). The article reports the range, median, and skew, noting "most of the distribution includes values larger than zero."

"The SMD ranged from −0.76 to 1.12 with a median of 0.18, and a slightly positive skewed distribution; most of the distribution includes values larger than zero, indicating that most studies to some degree reported positive effect of ADHD interventions."
