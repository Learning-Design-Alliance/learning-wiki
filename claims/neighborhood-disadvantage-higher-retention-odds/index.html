q2 · i? · associational · r2

Findings section of the brief reports observational analysis of Chicago Public Schools data on neighborhood census-tract factors and grade retention, comparing students with similar test scores, grades, suspension rates, and attendance. The brief states retention rates were "higher in these census tracts, even when comparing students with similar test scores, grades, suspension rates, and attendance."

"Compared to students living in census tracts with the most resources, the odds of being retained were: o 67% higher in census tracts where the most families spent 30% or more of their income on housing; o 56% higher in census tracts where the fewest families owned their home; and o 37% higher in census tracts where there was the least green space."
