Eisenberg 2025¶

Eisenberg, L., Sabalburo, E., & Ford, J. (2025). Navigating a sea of reforms: Sustaining school health centers through statewide initiatives. Alameda County Health, Healthy Schools and Communities. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673564

q2 · i? · design · r2

Annual evaluation of the 28 Alameda County SHCs for 2023–24 reports that "34 percent of SHC users" discussed feelings with a provider versus 17 percent of nonusers, and 42 percent of users received counseling when needed versus 28 percent of nonusers. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size or significance test is printed.
