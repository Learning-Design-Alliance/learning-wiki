## Evidence

### Lykke et al. 2015

Marianne Lykke, Mayela Coto, Christian Jantzen, Sonia Mora, Niels Vandel. (2015). Motivating Students Through Positive Learning Experiences: A Comparison of Three Learning Designs for Computer Programming Courses. Journal of Problem Based Learning in Higher Education, Vol. 3, No. 2, 80-108. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1108370

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semantic differential questionnaire results across the three designs: nervousness percentages were 33% (Control, N=70), 16% (PBL, N=86), and 28% (PBL+LM, N=71). The authors found it "somewhat surprising that the Control group course scores the highest percentage".

> "Looking at the percentages of students who feel some degree of nervousness, once again, the PBL course has the lowest score, cf. fig. 6. It is also somewhat surprising that the Control group course scores the highest percentage, considering the novelty of the learning designs in both the PBL and the PBL+LM courses."
