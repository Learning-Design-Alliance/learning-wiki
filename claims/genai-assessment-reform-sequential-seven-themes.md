`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Document analysis of 80 publicly available web-based documents using Braun and Clarke's six-phase thematic analysis; coding reached saturation after 72 documents, producing 185 initial codes. The analysis found "a whole-of-institution approach" structured around seven themes from values to assessment methods.

> "The thematic analysis yielded 7 key themes and 70 subthemes that collectively address the research question. The findings indicate that adapting ICT assessment in response to GenAI necessitates a whole-of-institution approach."
