Noah Leibnitz and Yangqiuting Li. (2026). Investigating students’ gender expression and its relation to sense of belonging in introductory physics courses. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.17966](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.17966)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Survey-based cross-institutional replication at the institution hosting the present qualitative study. The authors "observed that negative self-reflected appraisal discrepancies in femininity continued to be associated with lower sense of belonging"; no effect size is printed. Figure 2 shows lower average belonging for positive masculinity discrepancy groups.

> "Specifically, we observed that negative self-reflected appraisal discrepancies in femininity continued to be associated with lower sense of belonging. Additionally, we found that positive masculinity discrepancy, meaning that students felt their masculinity was underrecognized by others,  was also associated with lower levels of belonging (Fig. 2) [29]."
