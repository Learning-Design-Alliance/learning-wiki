## Evidence

### Aung Pyae 2026

Aung Pyae. (2026). AI-Automation Tooling in Computer Engineering Education: Mixed-Methods TAM/UTAUT Evidence for a General Acceptance Attitude. [doi:10.1109/icteched69022.2026.11682906](https://doi.org/10.1109/icteched69022.2026.11682906)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 103 undergraduate computer engineering students after three workshops using n8n. The article reports all six tests "significant at p < 0.0001" after Holm correction, with effect sizes r ranging from 0.68 (BI) to 0.81 (PE), described as large in Cohen's terms.

> "Table II reports one-sample Wilcoxon signed-rank tests of each construct score against the neutral midpoint 3.0. All six tests were significant at p < 0.0001 and remained significant after Holm correction."
