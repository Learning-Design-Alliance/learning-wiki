## Evidence

### Tanja Fritz 2023

Tanja Fritz, Hernán González Cruz, Stefan Janke, Martin Daumiller. (2023). Elucidating the Associations Between Achievement Goals and Academic Dishonesty: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09753-1

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression moderator analysis of dishonesty measure type: behavioral measures showed r = 0.237, 95% CI = [0.083, 0.373] for performance-approach goals, while self-reports showed no significant association.

> "For performance-approach goals, self-report and intention measures were not significantly associated with academic dishonesty, but behavioral measures were significantly different from self-report measures by being positively associated with academic dishonesty (r = 0.237, 95% CI  = [0.083, 0.373])"

### Tanja Fritz 2023 (2)

Tanja Fritz, Hernán González Cruz, Stefan Janke, Martin Daumiller. (2023). Elucidating the Associations Between Achievement Goals and Academic Dishonesty: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09753-1

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression moderator analysis: intention measures showed r = 0.148, 95% CI = [0.068, 0.300] for performance-avoidance goals; the difference remained significant when the single behavioral measure was removed.

> "For performance-avoidance goals, self-report and behavioral measures (based on one effect size) were not significantly associ ated with academic dishonesty, but intention measures were significantly different from self-report measures by being positively associated with academic dishonesty (r = 0.148, 95% CI  = [0.068, 0.300])"
