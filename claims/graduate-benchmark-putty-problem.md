Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2012). Using an Isomorphic Problem Pair to Learn Introductory Physics: Transferring from a Two-step Problem to a Three-step Problem. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.020114](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.020114)

Graduate students solved the quiz problem on their own without any solved problem provided; their performance serves as a benchmark for how well undergraduates can achieve as an upper limit. The 26 graduate students on average scored 9.2 out of 10.

> "Twenty three out of 26 graduate students were able to figure out the 3 -step nature of the solution even though some of them erroneously used 1 2 𝑚𝑣 instead of 1 2 𝑚𝑣2 to calculate the kinetic energy"
