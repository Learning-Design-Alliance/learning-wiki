## Evidence

### Ulrik Lyngs 2020

Ulrik Lyngs, Kai Lukoff, Petr Slovak, William Seymour, Helena Webb, Marina Jirotka, Jun Zhao, Max Van Kleek, Nigel Shadbolt. (2020). 'I Just Want to Hack Myself to Not Get Distracted': Evaluating Design Interventions for Self-Control on Facebook. CHI '20, April 25–30, 2020, Honolulu, HI, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376672

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Usage-logging analysis (RQ2) for the newsfeed-removal condition: scrolling "declined by 73%" relative to baseline, "t(13) = 4.15, p = 0.001, d = 1.11".

> "Thus, usage logging showed that average daily scrolling de- clined by 42% in C goal (comparing intervention to baseline, t(14) = 2.39, p = 0.03, d = 0.62), and by 73% in Cno-feed (t(13) = 4.15, p = 0.001, d = 1.11)."

### Ulrik Lyngs 2020 (2)

Ulrik Lyngs, Kai Lukoff, Petr Slovak, William Seymour, Helena Webb, Marina Jirotka, Jun Zhao, Max Van Kleek, Nigel Shadbolt. (2020). 'I Just Want to Hack Myself to Not Get Distracted': Evaluating Design Interventions for Self-Control on Facebook. CHI '20, April 25–30, 2020, Honolulu, HI, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376672

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Logging analysis (RQ2) for the newsfeed-removal condition: median likes fell from 16 to 7 ("p = 0.002, r = 0.88"). Separately, PAUM scores showed "substantially lower scores on 'passive' use" (d = 1.28) and per-item reductions in passive (r = 0.89) and active (r = 0.69) browsing.

> "in Cno-feed, the number of times content was liked declined (median number of likes during baseline = 16, median during intervention = 7, p = 0.002, r = 0.88, Wilcoxon signed rank test)"
