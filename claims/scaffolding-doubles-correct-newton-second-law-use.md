## Evidence

### Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh 2017

Shih-Yin Lin and Chandralekha Singh. (2017). Effect of Scaffolding on Helping Introductory Physics Students Solve Quantitative Problems Involving Strong Alternative Conceptions. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05697](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05697)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analyses of problem-solving-approach categories (Tables 5-7): percentages of students correctly using Newton's 2nd Law in the direction parallel to the inclined plane in the three intervention groups were "more than two times higher than that for the comparison group" in the corresponding course.

> "With the interventions provided, the percentage of students who solved the problem correctly increased from 21% to an average of 51% in the calculus-based course, and from 15% to an average of 39% in the algebra-based course."
