q3 · i? · causal · r3

Descriptive comparison of self-reported math-related PD during the study year. Treatment teachers participated in "95 more hours of math-related PD than did control teachers," and reported a greater focus on K–8 math content and student thinking (differences of 0.7 to 1.3 on a 4-point scale, statistically significant).

"Overall, treatment teachers participated in 95 more hours of math-related PD than did control teachers, which is close to the approximately 93 hours of math PD provided by the study."
