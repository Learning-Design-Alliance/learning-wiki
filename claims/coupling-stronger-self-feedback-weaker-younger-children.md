## Evidence

### Kievit R. A. 2019

Kievit R. A., Hofman A. D., & Nation K. (2019). Mutualistic Coupling Between Vocabulary and Reasoning in Young Children: A Replication and Extension of the Study by Kievit et al. (2017). Psychological Science, 30(8), 1245–1252. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797619841265

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Comparison of standardized parameter estimates across the two independent samples. The article reports reasoning-to-vocabulary coupling of .155 in adolescents versus .218 and .276 in children, and vocabulary-to-reasoning coupling of .203 versus .329 and .449, with Steiger's z tests.

> "the coupling parameter (r) from reasoning to changes in vocabulary was .155 (standardized estimate), whereas in the younger Oxford sample, it was .218 and .276 for Waves 2 and 3"
