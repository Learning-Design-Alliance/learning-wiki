## Evidence

### Munro Malcolm Gordon MD 2011

Munro Malcolm Gordon MD, Behling David Paul MD. (2011). Virtual Reality Uterine Resectoscopic Simulator: Face and Construct Validation and Comparative Evaluation in an Educational Environment. JSLS : Journal of the Society of Laparoendoscopic Surgeons. https://doi.org/10.4293/108680811X13071180406312

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Phase 1 face-validation comparison of 3 experts and 11 novices on the simulator's Manipulation Module, a 60-second targeting exercise. The article reports the expert-versus-novice difference was significant (P=0.0034 for time per the abstract).

> "each of the experts successfully touched all 4 targets (Figure 4), without perforation, with a mean exercise time of 33 seconds (Figure 5). For the 11 novices, the median number of targets touched was 2 (range, 0 to 4)"
