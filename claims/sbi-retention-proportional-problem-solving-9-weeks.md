## Evidence

### Jitendra 2015

Jitendra, A. K., Harwell, M. R., Dupuis, D. N., Karl, S. R., Lein, A. E., Simonson, G., & Slater, S. C. (2015). Effects of a research-based intervention to improve seventh-grade students’ proportional problem solving: A cluster randomized trial. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000039

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

The abstract reports that "the SBI group scored on average significantly higher than the control group on the posttest and retention test (9 weeks later)". The delayed posttest ICC was .18 (p < .001), with 18% of variance between classrooms.

> "the SBI group scored on average significantly higher than the control group on the posttest and retention test (9 weeks later) and also showed significantly more growth in proportional problem solving"
