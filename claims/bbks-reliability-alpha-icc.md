### Bernal-Guerrero Antonio; Cárdenas-Gutiérrez Antonio Ramón; Montoro-Fernández Elisabet 2020

Bernal-Guerrero Antonio; Cárdenas-Gutiérrez Antonio Ramón; Montoro-Fernández Elisabet. (2020). Basic business knowledge scale for secondary education students. Development and validation with Spanish teenagers. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235681

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the BBKS: internal consistency via Cronbach alpha and composite reliability, plus test-retest with a two-week interval administered to a subgroup of 50 students. The article reports "The intraclass correlation coefficient was .92" for the total scale and alphas of .83 (KBM), .78 (LK) and .76 (SK).

> "The Cronbach alpha coefficient for each of the domains varied between .76 and .83. The AVE scores were above .50 and the composite reliability has been greater than the criterion of .70. The intraclass correlation coefficient was .92."
