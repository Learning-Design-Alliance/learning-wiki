`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Proof-of-concept classroom project (Section IV): three consecutive 45-minute lessons in twelfth grade of a local German Gymnasium, in which students reconstructed the lensing equation and its solutions from Einstein's notes. Motivation evidence comes from students' answers and comments on evaluation sheets; no effect size is reported.

> "We found that the authenticity of Einstein's calculations and the challenge to explore his thinking directly from his notes very much added to the motivation of the stu- dents."
