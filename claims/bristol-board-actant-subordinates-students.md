## Evidence

### Baruch B. Schwarz 2025

Baruch B. Schwarz, Udi Tsemach, Mirit Israeli, Erez Nir. (2025). Actor-network theory as a new direction in research on educational dialogues. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09669-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative ANT analysis of an episode from the third focus day's interdisciplinary session, in which a group of five girls arranged arguments on a Bristol board axis. The analysis shows the board and instructions acting as non-human actants that subordinated the students' actions.

> "The Bristol board functions as an actor, which almost ''gives orders'' to the human actors, like in line 56 (''Let's split it into halves and then we will know'') as if the board 'says' ''split me in two halves and then you will know''"
