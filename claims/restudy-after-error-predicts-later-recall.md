# Activation during unsuccessful retrieval did not predict later recall, but re-study following an error showed subsequent-memory effects including caudate and putamen

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For incorrect Test 1 trials, no ROI showed subsequent-memory effects during the retrieval attempt itself. [→ Liu Xiaonan L. 2014](#liu-xiaonan-l-2014)
`q3 i?` Re-study following an incorrect recall showed marginally significant left hippocampus and left PFC effects, and whole-brain analysis identified left caudate and putamen activations higher for pairs later correct on Test 2. [→ Liu Xiaonan L. 2014 (2)](#liu-xiaonan-l-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Liu Xiaonan L. 2014

Liu Xiaonan L., Liang Peipeng, Li Kuncheng, Reder Lynne M. (2014). Brain Correlates of Testing Effect. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0092025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

ROI analysis of activation during unsuccessful Test 1 recall attempts partitioned by Test 2 accuracy. The article reports "no significant activation patterns found in the six ROIs (all t's<1)".

> "we further compared brain activity for incorrect Test1 trials as a function of Test 2 accuracy. There were no significant activation patterns found in the six ROIs (all t's<1)"

### Liu Xiaonan L. 2014 (2)

Liu Xiaonan L., Liang Peipeng, Li Kuncheng, Reder Lynne M. (2014). Brain Correlates of Testing Effect. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0092025

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory whole-brain analysis (p<0.001, cluster-corrected) of re-study activation following an incorrect Test 1 recall. The article reports "Left caudate and putamen were identified in re-study following incorrect Test 1" with higher activation for pairs later correct; ROI effects in left hippocampus and left PFC were only marginal (p = .073, p = .088).

> "Left caudate and putamen were identified in re-study following incorrect Test 1 and activations in these two regions were higher when subsequent Test 2 trials were correct than when Test 2 trials were incorrect."
