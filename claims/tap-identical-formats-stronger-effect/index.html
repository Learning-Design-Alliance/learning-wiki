Evidence¶

Adesope 2017¶

Adesope, O. O., Trevisan, D. A., & Sundararajan, N. (2017). Rethinking the Use of Tests: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654316689306

q4 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analysis of transfer-appropriate processing (Table 2): identical formats k = 175 (g = 0.63), dissimilar k = 69 (g = 0.53), mixed k = 28 (g = 0.75). Post hoc analysis confirmed the identical-versus-dissimilar difference was significant.

"This indicates that testing effects were stronger when the practice and final test formats were identical (g = 0.63) compared to when the practice and final test formats were dissimilar (g = 0.53). The between-levels variance was statistically significant, QΒ(3) = 15.73, p < .001."
