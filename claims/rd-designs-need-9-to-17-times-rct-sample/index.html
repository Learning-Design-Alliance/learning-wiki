Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/statistical-power-for-regression-discontinuity-designs-in-education-empirical-estimates-of-design-effects-relative-to-randomized-controlled-trials

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Empirical estimates computed from data drawn from four previously published education studies, comparing the sample sizes an RD design and an RCT each require for equal precision; the paper reports RD needs "between 9 and 17 times as many schools or students as a randomized controlled trial." No effect size is printed for this design comparison.

"Using data from four previously published education studies, this working paper finds that a study using a regression discontinuity design needs between 9 and 17 times as many schools or students as a randomized controlled trial to produce an impact with the same level of statistical precision."
