QiRui Ju. (2023). Experimental Evidence on Negative Impact of Generative AI on Scientific Learning Outcomes (A Pilot Research). The Samuel Dubois Cook Center on Social Equity, Duke University. [doi:10.2139/ssrn.4567696](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4567696)

Writing-quality and time outcomes from the pilot experiment (Figures 3 and 4). The article reports AI group writings "often have fewer spelling errors , better flow, and coverage compared to human writings" and shorter completion times (9 minutes for Active, 8.7 for AI).
