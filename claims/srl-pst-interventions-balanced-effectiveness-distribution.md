# Among 53 quantitative SRL interventions for pre-service teachers, effect sizes were distributed evenly across highly effective (n = 17), moderately effective (n = 16) and less effective (n = 20) categories

## Evidence

### Alazne Fernández Ortube 2024

Alazne Fernández Ortube, Ernesto Panadero, Charlotte Dignath. (2024). Self-Regulated Learning Interventions for Pre-service Teachers: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09919-5

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review of 66 intervention studies; effect sizes (Cohen's d) were extracted or calculated for the 53 studies providing quantitative data and grouped using Cohen (1988) ranges enlarged per Hattie (2008). The review reports a "balanced" distribution across the three effectiveness categories.

> "The distribution of the quantitative SRL interventions was balanced in terms of effect sizes, with a comparable number of interventions in each category: highly effective (n = 17), moderate effective (n = 16), and less effective (n = 20)."
