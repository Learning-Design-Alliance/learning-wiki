## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Belief that OSPEs help improve clinical skills correlated positively with OSPE benefit score (r = 0.542, P = .000) and OSCE benefit score (r = 0.312, P = .000), but not with career development impact score (r = 0.47, P = .318). [→ Alrashed FA 2026](#alrashed-fa-2026)

## Evidence

### Alrashed FA 2026

Alrashed FA, Ahmad T, Alsubiheen AM, Alhammad SA, Aldaihan MM, Albishi AM, Hasan Z. (2026). Medical students’ perspectives on the role of OSPE and OSCE in the educational journey and contribution to career development: A cross-sectional study. Medicine 2026;105:3(e47233). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047233

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis in the Results section of the cross-sectional survey (n = 451). The article prints r = 0.542 for the OSPE benefit score association; the career development correlation was not significant, and the authors interpret that immediate skill development benefits are not directly linked to long-term career contribution.

> "The extent to which students believe OSPEs help in improving their clinical skills showed a significant positive correlation with OSPEs benefit score (r = 0.542, P = .000) and OSCEs benefit score (r = 0.312, P = .000). However, the correlation with contribution to career development impact score was not significant (r = 0.47, P = .318)"
