## Evidence

### Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan 2021

Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan. (2021). Does Touching Real Objects Affect Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09551-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

In the 2×2 between-subjects experiment (N = 163), ANCOVA controlling for prior knowledge and interest on immediate free recall of the six tools (M = 4.27, SD = 1.27 of 6) showed "significant main effects for vision" and haptics; post hoc tests showed the no-objects condition recalled significantly fewer objects than the other conditions.

> "we found significant main effects for vision, F(1,157) = 15.40, p <. 0 0 1 , ηp2 = 0.09, and haptics, F(1,157) = 13.98, p <. 0 0 1 ,ηp2 = 0.08."
