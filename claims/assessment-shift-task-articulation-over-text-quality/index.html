Authors' design recommendation in the classroom-practice section, where students define tasks, formulate prompts, analyse AI outputs, and critically evaluate ethical risks before revising their instructions. This is a stated pedagogical proposal, not a tested result; the paper notes the taxonomy requires classroom validation.

"Rather than evaluating stu- dents on the ‘quality’ of AI-generated text, this approach shifts the focus of assess- ment towards articulating tasks, justifying prompt choices, and reflexively evaluat- ing AI behaviour."
