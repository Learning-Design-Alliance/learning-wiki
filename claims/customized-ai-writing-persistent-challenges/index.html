Systematic review synthesis of challenges across the 23 studies (RQ4, Fig. 7), identifying 17 challenge instances. Technical limitations included inconsistent accuracy, overgeneralized or unstable feedback, and substantial computational or financial costs.

"Pedagogical and learner-related challenges involved overreliance on AI feedback, reduced critical engagement, limited transparency of system operations, and uneven learner uptake of optional learning features"
