## Evidence

### Abed 2016

Mohaned Ghazi Abed. (2016). Inculcating Positive Thinking in the Self-Concept of Children with Learning Difficulties. i-manager's Journal on Educational Psychology, Vol. 10, No. 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1138669

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive pretest/posttest means from the SDQ impact supplement for the intervention group (Tables 4–10, n = 20). Distress decreased from 2.25 to 1.8; similar decreases are reported for everyday-life interference (1.9 to 1.3), friendships (2.15 to 1.35), classroom learning (2.1 to 1.7), leisure (1.95 to 1.4) and burden on others (2.2 to 1.55).

> "Table 5 shows that the average mean in the pretest intervention was 2.25 that decreased to 1.8; thus, proving the effectiveness of the intervention in reducing the distress, caused by the difficulties, of the participants."
