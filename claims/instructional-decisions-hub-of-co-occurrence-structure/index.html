Co-occurrence analysis of lecturer decision types across the 27 reviewed studies, presented as a matrix (Fig. 5). The review reports the instructional–assessment pair as strongest and states "the four strongest pairs in the matrix all involved instructional decisions," with learning environment management, curriculum, and ethical decisions forming the sparsest region.
