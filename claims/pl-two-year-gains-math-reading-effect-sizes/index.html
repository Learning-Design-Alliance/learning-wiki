Evidence¶

John F. Pane 2015¶

John F. Pane, Elizabeth D. Steiner, Matthew D. Baird, Laura S. Hamilton. (2015). Continued Progress: Promising Evidence on Personalized Learning. RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/t/RR1365z2 doi:10.7249/rr1365

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Matched comparison group (NWEA virtual comparison group) analysis of MAP scores for approximately 11,000 students in 62 schools over 2013–14 and 2014–15. Printed effect sizes: 0.27 in mathematics and 0.19 in reading; the report states "The two-year effect sizes for this student population across all schools were 0.27 in mathematics and 0.19 in reading."

"The two-year effect sizes for this student population across all schools were 0.27 in mathematics and 0.19 in reading. There were positive results across all grade levels"
