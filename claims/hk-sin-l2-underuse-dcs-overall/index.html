Related Claims¶

  • Hong Kong and Singaporean students are more formal in tone than L1 and Thai students when using adversative and resultive connectives — related
  • Thai students overuse the causal connective because, a pattern the article attributes to L1 influence — related

  • L2 writers tend to overuse more discourse connectives than they underuse, and both L1 and L2 students have a limited range of DCs at their disposal — related
