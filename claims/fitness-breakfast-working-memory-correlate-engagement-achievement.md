## Evidence

### Latino Francesca 2026

Latino Francesca, Tafuri Domenico, Saraiello Emma, Tafuri Maria Giovanna, Byerley Lauri. (2026). Integrated Structured Breakfast and Morning Sport Program and Its Associations with Attention, Executive Functions, and Academic Performance in Students. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18132103

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of individual change scores within the experimental group. The article reports "positive correlations with both school engagement (r = 0.38, p = 0.006) and academic achievement (r = 0.35, p = 0.011)", plus fitness–engagement (r = 0.43), fitness–grades (r = 0.31), and breakfast habits–engagement (r = 0.40) associations.

> "Digit Span Backward improvements demonstrated positive correlations with both school engagement (r = 0.38, p = 0.006) and academic achievement (r = 0.35, p = 0.011), indicating that greater improvements in working memory tended to co-occur with higher school engagement and academic achievement."
