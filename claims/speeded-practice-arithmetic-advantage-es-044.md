## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Speeded practice produced significantly better arithmetic outcomes than nonspeeded practice, with no significant advantage on calculations or number knowledge. [→ Fuchs et al. 2019](#fuchs-et-al-2019)

## Evidence

### Fuchs et al. 2019

Lynn S. Fuchs, Douglas Fuchs, and Jennifer K. Gilbert. (2019). Does the Severity of Students' Pre-Intervention Math Deficits Affect Responsiveness to Generally Effective First-Grade Intervention? Exceptional Children, 85(2), 147–162. https://doi.org/10.1177/0014402918782628

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Main-effects multilevel model comparing the two practice variants within the RCT; the A+SP versus A+NSP contrast on arithmetic was significant with ES = 0.44. On calculations (ES = 0.17) and number knowledge (ES = 0.11) the contrast was not significant after correction.

> "β3 = 5.65, SE = 1.02, p < .001, ES = 0.44 (A+SP vs. A+NSP)"
