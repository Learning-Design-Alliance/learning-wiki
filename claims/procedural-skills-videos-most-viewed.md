## Evidence

### Kim Kyong-Jee 2019

Kim Kyong-Jee, Kim Giwoon. (2019). Development of e-learning in medical education: 10 years’ experience of Korean medical schools. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.131

`q2 · i?`

Descriptive usage statistics on online videos (Table 4) from the same 7-year hit dataset. Procedural-skills videos averaged 4,163.26 hits per clip versus 1,043.47 for patient-encounter clips; no inferential comparison is reported.

> "Among the online videos, those on procedural skills received the greatest hits for the duration of 7 years, where an average number of hits per video clip was 4,163.26. This was followed by video clips on patient encounters (1,043.47 hits per clip)."

### Kim Kyong-Jee 2019 (2)

Kim Kyong-Jee, Kim Giwoon. (2019). Development of e-learning in medical education: 10 years’ experience of Korean medical schools. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.131

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Secondary descriptive analysis in the same Results section showing subject-level variation in video usage. Hits on procedural-skills videos "ranged from 2,505.00 to 6,717.86" across subjects. No test is reported.

> "The number of hits on video clips differed across subjects; they ranged from 2,505.00 to 6,717.86 across different subjects on procedural skills, and the number of hits on videos on patient encounter ranged from 681.89 to 1,594.50."
