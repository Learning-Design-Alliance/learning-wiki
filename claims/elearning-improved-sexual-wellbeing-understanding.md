## Evidence

### McCaughan E.M. 2021

McCaughan E.M., Flannagan C., Parahoo K., Bingham S.L., Brady N., Connaghan J., Maguire R., Thompson S., Jain S., Kirby M., O'Connor S.R. (2021). Effects of a Brief E-Learning Resource on Sexual Attitudes and Beliefs of Healthcare Professionals Working in Prostate Cancer Care: A Pilot Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph181910045

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way repeated measures ANOVA on modified SABS item scores from 44 healthcare professionals at baseline, immediately post e-learning, and 4 weeks. Understanding rose from mean 3.18 to 3.79 (T1) and 3.61 (T2), with "significant differences ... between T0 and T1 ... and between T0 and T2" (P = 0.001).

> "These included 'awareness and understanding of how prostate cancer and its treatment might affect men's sexual wellbeing' (F = 23.657; P = 0.001)"
