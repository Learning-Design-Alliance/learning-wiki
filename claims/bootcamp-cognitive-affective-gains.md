## Evidence

### Dversdal Renee K. 2018

Dversdal Renee K., Gold Jeffrey A., Richards Matthew H., Chiovaro Joseph C., Iossi Katherine A., Mansoor André M., Hunter Alan J., Desai Sima S. (2018). A 5-day intensive curriculum for interns utilizing simulation and active-learning techniques: addressing domains important across internal medicine practice. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-4011-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post paired t-test analysis of pooled 2014 and 2015 cohorts on Likert-scale self-ratings of cognitive and affective skills. Table 2 shows significant post gains for managing acutely ill patients, difficult conversations (p = .029), and EHR navigation (p = .001).

> "the percentage of interns who had at least “average” self-perceived confidence increased from 77.3 to 89.7% (p < .001). Parallel results were observed with self-perceived competence (77.2 vs 90.8%; p < .001)."
