## Evidence

### Oudman 2024

Oudman, S., van de Pol, J., Janssen, E., & van Gog, T. (2024). Primary school students' awareness of their monitoring and regulation judgment accuracy. Learning and Instruction, 92, 101907. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101907

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel regression analysis (day, student, class levels) of data from 564 Dutch sixth-grade students (495 multiplication, 359 division) who completed parallel problem-solving tasks on two days. The main effect of monitoring accuracy on SOJ-m was not significant for either task, indicating students were "not aware of their monitoring judgment (in)accuracy before self-scoring."

> "The main effect of students' monitoring judgment accuracy on their SOJ-m was not significant for either of the tasks (see M1 in Table 2), indicating that on average, students were not aware of their monitoring judgment (in)accuracy before self-scoring."
