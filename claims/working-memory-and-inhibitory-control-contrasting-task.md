## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Participants with better verbal working memory were more accurate throughout, while participants with less inhibitory control were more accurate during learning. [→ Baxter 2022](#baxter-2022)

## Evidence

### Baxter 2022

Baxter, P.A.; Bekkering, H.; Dijkstra, A.F.J.; Droop, W.; Hurk, M.M. van den; Léoné, F.T.M. (2022). Contrasting orthographically similar words facilitates adult second language vocabulary learning. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101582

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Covariate analyses in Experiment 1 (reading span and Flanker tasks as z-scored controls) showed verbal working memory positively related to learning and posttest accuracy, while lower inhibitory control predicted higher learning-phase accuracy.

> "participants with less inhibitory control were more accurate during learning than those with more inhibitory control (see Fig. 8, left); and that participants with better verbal working memory were more accurate during learning (see Fig. 8, right)."
