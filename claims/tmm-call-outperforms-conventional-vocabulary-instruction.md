## Evidence

### Enayati and Gilakjani 2020

Fatemeh Enayati, & Abbas Pourhosein Gilakjani. (2020). The Impact of Computer Assisted Language Learning (CALL) on Improving Intermediate EFL Learners' Vocabulary Learning. International Journal of Language Education, 4(1). https://doi.org/10.26858/ijole.v4i2.10560

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental control-group study: 31 experimental learners used TMM software and 30 control learners received conventional instruction for 12 sessions; independent samples t-test on post-test scores showed "a statistically significant difference" with means 42.16 vs 37.47.

> "there is a statistically significant difference (t (59) = 2.59, p = .01, p < .05) in vocabulary learning measures for the experimental group ( 𝑥̅ = 42.16) , who experienced vocabulary in struction through TMM, and control group ( 𝑥̅ = 37.47)"
