## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A single-factor CFA over subscale means fits well only after removing the Professionalization (PF) and Attitude (AT) dimensions, meeting CFI, TLI and RMSEA criteria. [→ Delcker 2025](#delcker-2025)

## Evidence

### Delcker 2025

Delcker, J., Heil, J., & Ifenthaler, D. (2025). Evidence-based development of an instrument for the assessment of teachers' self-perceptions of their artificial intelligence competence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10418-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A single-factor CFA on the means of the six subscales, based on the survey of vocational school teachers. The one-factor model with all six subscales fit poorly (RMSEA = .269); removing PF and then AT yielded the final well-fitting model (χ2 = 3.373, DF = 2, RMSEA = .038, CFI = .998, TLI = .995).

> "The final model meets the criteria of a good fit concerning the CFI and the TLI, as well as the RMSEA (see Fig. 2)."
