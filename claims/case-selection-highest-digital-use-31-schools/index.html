Schmid 2023¶

Schmid, R., Pauli, C., & Petko, D. (2023). Examining the use of digital technology in schools with a school-wide approach to personalized learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10167-z

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Sampling procedure of the multiple-case study, part of the Swiss perLen project (2013–2015). Based on students' questionnaire answers, the researchers "identified the three schools with the most frequent use of digital technology" and invited them into the case study, conducting 11 interviews of 22 to 65 minutes.

"The overall sample of the study on digital technology use consisted of 31 lower-secondary schools with a total of 1017 8th-grade students. They completed an online questionnaire that included the question of how often they use digital technology for different classroom activities."
