# The general organization reference factor is weakly negatively related to cognitive activation, student support, and teacher enthusiasm, but these associations become non-significant after controlling for covariates

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2`–`r3` · `q2` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` In the bifactor-(S-1) model, the general organization reference factor was weakly but negatively related to cognitive activation (β = -.097), student support (β = -.082), and teacher enthusiasm (β = .099 as printed). [→ Burić 2023](#buric-2023)
`q2 i?` After adding years of teaching experience and class-average achievement as covariates, these negative organization associations became statistically non-significant. [→ Burić 2023 (2)](#buric-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Burić 2023

Burić, Irena, Butković, Ana and Kim, Lisa (2023) Teacher personality domains and facets: Their unique associations with student-ratings of teaching quality and teacher enthusiasm. Learning and Instruction. 101827. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101827

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Conscientiousness bifactor-(S-1) MSEM; the article reports the general organization reference factor "weakly but negatively related to cognitive activation" and student support, with the printed coefficients, and suggests students may perceive preference for order and structure as less desirable.

> "the general organization reference factor was weakly but negatively related to cognitive activation (β = -.097, p = .008), student support (β = -.082, p = .024), and teacher enthusiasm (β = .099, p = .022)."

### Burić 2023 (2)

Burić, Irena, Butković, Ana and Kim, Lisa (2023) Teacher personality domains and facets: Their unique associations with student-ratings of teaching quality and teacher enthusiasm. Learning and Instruction. 101827. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101827

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Covariate-adjusted bifactor-(S-1) models adding years of teaching experience and class-average school grade; the organization effects "became statistically non-significant". The article attributes this to organization being negatively related to class average achievement (β = -.111, p = .003) and positively to experience (β = .104, p = .005).

> "after controlling for the covariates, negative relationships between the general organization reference facet and cognitive activation, student support, and teacher enthusiasm became statistically non-significant (β = -.057, β = -.036, and β = -.048, ps > .05, respectively)."
