Tesfai FM 2024¶

Tesfai FM, Nagi J, Morrison I, Boal M, Olaitan A, Chandrasekaran D, Stoyanov D, Lanceley A, Francis N. (2024). Objective assessment tools in laparoscopic or robotic‐assisted gynecological surgery: A systematic review. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14840

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review validity appraisal using a Messick-adapted scoring system (each aspect scored 0–3, maximum 15; 11–15 = substantial). Table 8 lists OSA-TLH and RobotiX Mentor simulator at score 11 as the only substantial-evidence tools; the review states "Only two tools showed substantive evidence of validity."
