## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ESP students taught technical vocabulary through a task-based approach scored significantly higher on the post-test than students taught by the traditional method (t = -6.153, df = 48, p < .05). [→ Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi 2012](#abdullah-sarani-leila-farzaneh-sahebi-2012)

## Evidence

### Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi 2012

Abdullah Sarani & Leila Farzaneh Sahebi. (2012). The Impact of Task-based Approach on Vocabulary Learning in ESP Courses. English Language Teaching, 5(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n10p118

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test comparing post-test means of the randomly assigned control (traditional) and experimental (task-based) groups of 25 students each after thirteen weeks of instruction. The article reports t = -6.153 with 48 df, exceeding the critical value at the .05 level, and concludes the task-based approach had a significant effect.

> "As it is demonstrated in the Table 5 the t-observed with its 48 degrees of freedom, was -6.153. In fact this t is larger than t-critical at 0.05 level of sign ificance (p< .05) which is (2.0 11). It means teaching technical vocabulary through task-based approach has a signif icant effect on ESP learners’ technical vocabulary improvement."
