## Evidence

### Al Awaji Nisreen 2022

Al Awaji Nisreen, Zaidi Uzma, Awad Salwa S., Alroqaiba Nouf, Aldhahi Monira I., Alsaleh Hadeel, Akil Shahnaz, Mortada Eman M. (2022). Moderating Effects of Self-Esteem on the Relationship between Communication Anxiety and Academic Performance among Female Health College Students during the COVID-19 Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192113960

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison of PRCA across four departments in the survey of 287 students. The article reports mean ranks "higher in the students in the health communication sciences department than... the radiological sciences department (167.8 vs. 121.6)".

> "A post hoc Dunn test (Bonferroni p-value adjusted = 0.05) confirmed that significant differences exist between health communication sciences and radiological science departments; the mean ranks of PRCA scores were statistically significantly higher in the students in the health communication sciences department than the mean ranks of students in the radiological sciences department (167.8 vs. 121.6)."

### Al Awaji Nisreen 2022 (2)

Al Awaji Nisreen, Zaidi Uzma, Awad Salwa S., Alroqaiba Nouf, Aldhahi Monira I., Alsaleh Hadeel, Akil Shahnaz, Mortada Eman M. (2022). Moderating Effects of Self-Esteem on the Relationship between Communication Anxiety and Academic Performance among Female Health College Students during the COVID-19 Pandemic. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192113960

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis comparison of the public speaking subscale across departments in the same survey. The article reports health communication sciences students "scored higher (p = 0.0003) in public speaking than the students in the rehabilitation sciences department (175.6 vs. 123.2)".

> "Bonferroni's post hoc test revealed that the students in the health communication sciences department scored higher (p = 0.0003) in public speaking than the students in the rehabilitation sciences department (175.6 vs. 123.2)"
