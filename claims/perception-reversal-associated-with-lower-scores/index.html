Evidence¶

Laura Graf 2026¶

Laura Graf, Ramona Beinstingel, Stephan Krusche, Oleksandra Poquet. (2026). Students' Perception Accuracy of Partners' AI Use and its Relation to Collaboration Performance. Preprint, Technical University of Munich. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23237 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.23237

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Mann–Whitney U comparison of teams with versus without a t1→t3 perception reversal in the 103-pair study; the article prints the rank-biserial effect size "r_rb = .427" (with U = 192, p = .011). Only 25 teams experienced a reversal, so the authors call the result indicative rather than conclusive.

"Teams experiencing a direction reversal achieved lower scores than teams with stable perceptions (medians: 94.5 vs. 98 percentage points; Mann–Whitney U = 192, p = .011, r_rb = .427)."
