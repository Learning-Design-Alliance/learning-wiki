# NIG-head predictive uncertainty is dominated by epistemic uncertainty and shows negligible correlation with the absolute engagement score (Pearson r 0.016 aleatoric, 0.025 epistemic)

Uncertainty decomposition analysis (Table 4) grouping test samples into five equal-width bins on the 1–5 engagement scale. The printed correlations are r 0.016 (aleatoric) and r 0.025 (epistemic).

> "Pearson correlation coefficients show negligible linear relationships between raw engagement scores and both aleatoric and epistemic uncertainties, indicating that model confidence is driven by factors other than the absolute magnitude of the engagement score itself."
