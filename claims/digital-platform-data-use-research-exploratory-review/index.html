Evidence¶

Alina Hase 2024¶

Alina Hase, Poldi Kuhl. (2024). Teachers’ use of data from digital learning platforms for instructional design: a systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10356-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review following the PRISMA-P scheme; searches of five databases in February 2022 yielded N = 2119 records, narrowed to n = 11 studies after screening. The review reports the field "has so far been researched exploratively", with studies published only from 2011 to 2021 and n = 9 of 11 qualitative in design.

"The analysis of n = 11 studies revealed that the use of data from digital learning platforms for instructional design has so far been researched exploratively."
