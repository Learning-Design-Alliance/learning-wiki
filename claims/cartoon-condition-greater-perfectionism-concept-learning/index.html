Evidence¶

Zousel 2013¶

Zousel, M. L., Rule, A. C., & Logan, S. R. (2013). Teaching Primary Grade Students Perfectionism through Cartoons Compared to Bibliotherapy. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education, 5(2), 199-218. www.iejee.com. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070469

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Pretest-posttest experimental group-control group study of 46 primary students in three grades at one suburban Iowa school, with intact classes randomly assigned to conditions. The experimental cartoon group scored significantly higher on the posttest (p = 0.02), and "The effect size, Cohen's d, was 0.5 indicating a medium effect size".

"However, students in the experimental condition supplied significantly more correct responses on the posttest than those in the control condition. A t -test was c onducted to determine if the higher posttest score for the experimental group were statistically significant. The p-value was 0.02"
