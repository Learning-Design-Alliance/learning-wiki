## Subclaims
`q3 i?` CAI used as a tutorial (d = 0.849), for computation (d = 0.525), and for simulations (d = 0.461) produced the largest effects; drill-and-practice (d = 0.361) and enhanced lecture (d = 0.372) were smaller but significant; strictly online CAI produced a negative effect (d = -0.035).
