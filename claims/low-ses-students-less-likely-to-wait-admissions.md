## Evidence

### Boon-Falleur 2025

Boon-Falleur, M., Huillery, E., & Chevallier, C. (2025). Waiting time during admission procedures increases social inequalities in higher education. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 122(47), e2426604122. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2426604122

`q4 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Pre-registered observational analysis of the full APB national admission database (274,316 waitlisted students, 2015–2017 cohorts, 18.3% financial aid recipients), using linear probability models with year fixed effects and SE clustered at the high school level; the study found financial aid beneficiaries were "5 percentage points less likely to wait."

> "financial aid beneficiaries were 5 percentage points less likely to wait to be accepted into a preferred program (SE = 0.002, P < 0.001), see Fig. 2."
