# Using Edukoi in science classes did not significantly change students' mATSI science-attitude scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired t-tests on 67 students who completed both questionnaire administrations showed no significant change in the comprehensive mATSI score or any subscore. [→ Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024](#lucrezia-guiotto-nai-fovino-2024)

## Evidence

### Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino 2024

Lucrezia Guiotto Nai Fovino, Anita Zanella, Luca Di Mascolo, Michele Ginolfi, Nicolò Carpita, Francesco Trovato Manuncola, Massimo Grassi. (2024). Evaluating the effectiveness of sonification in science education using Edukoi. Personal and Ubiquitous Computing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00779-024-01809-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired-sample t-tests comparing mATSI scores of 67 students who responded in both Q1 and Q2 and participated in at least one testing session; the comprehensive score t(132) = 1.15, p = 0.25, Cohen's d = 0.19. Equivalence was not tested.

> "Overall, the questionnaire revealed no significant change in mATSI scores before and after the testing sessions with Edukoi."
