## Evidence

### Quail Michelle 2016

Quail Michelle, Brundage Shelley B, Spitalnick Josh, Allen Peter J, Beilby Janet. (2016). Student self-reported communication skills, knowledge and confidence across standardised patient, virtual and traditional clinical learning environments. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0577-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-groups ANOVA on the summed 12-item global evaluation index (α = .89) in the randomised study; η2 = .178. Tukey's HSD showed nursing home > VLE (d = .89), nursing home not > SP (d = .21), and "the SP placement was evaluated more favourably than the VLE placement (d = .79)".

> "which was statistically significant, F (2, 58) = 6.27, p = .003, η2 = .178. Follow-up post-hoc comparisons using Tukey's HSD indicated that the nursing home condition participants (M = 4.97, SD = 0.98) evaluated their placement more favourably than the VLE condition participants (M = 3.77, SD = 1.08; d = .89)"
