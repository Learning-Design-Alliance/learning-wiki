Evidence¶

Husain et al. 2016¶

Fatima Husain, Nico Jabin, Sarah Haywood, Adetayo Kasim, Jonathan Paylor. (2016). Parent Academy: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN42518213

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Secondary CACE-style analysis of the trial estimating the intervention effect taking account of the dose of treatment received, measured as the proportion of possible sessions attended by each invited parent; the evaluators note this estimate was biased because non-attendance factors were likely linked to the outcome.

"There was no evidence that the intervention had more impact for parents who attended more sessions."
