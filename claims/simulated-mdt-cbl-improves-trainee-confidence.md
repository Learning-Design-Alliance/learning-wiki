## Evidence

### Vundum Jonathon 2025

Vundum Jonathon, Day Fiona. (2025). Evaluating the Efficacy of Case-Based Learning for Medical Oncology Trainees: A Systematic Review. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-025-02651-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Educational pilot study (Mackay et al., 2024) with 11 oncology and 8 respiratory specialty registrars completing pre- and post-course questionnaires around a single 3-hour interactive CBL session discussing four patient scenarios. The review reports confidence improvement "statistically significant (p < 0.01)" with an "increase of 2 on a 10-point scale"; no confidence intervals were generated.

> "Improvement in trainee confidence was found to be statistically significant (p < 0.01) using a paired t test. The magnitude of effect, an increase of 2 on a 10-point scale, was a meaningful increase. No confidence intervals were generated and as such it was unclear where the true mean difference existed."
