## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Above-chance decoding of congruent vs. incongruent began earlier under self-reference conditions (220 ms) than under non-self-reference conditions (340 ms). [→ Li 2024](#li-2024)

## Evidence

### Li 2024

Li, Z., Wang, J., Chen, Y., Li, Q., Yin, S. & Chen, A. (2024). Attenuated conflict self-referential information facilitating conflict resolution. npj Science of Learning 9, 47. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00256-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MVPA decoding analysis (LDA classifiers on 60 EEG electrodes, ADAM toolbox) from the 33-participant experiment. Congruency was decodable from 220 to 1000 ms in the self-reference comparison and from 340 to 1000 ms in the non-self-reference comparison (both p < 0.001, cluster-corrected); the authors conclude "the self-reference condition could classify congruent and incongruent conditions earlier than the non-self-reference condition".

> "MVPA revealed a signi ﬁcant above-chance (AUC > 0.5) difference between the two classes from 220 to 1000 ms after stimulus onset (p < 0.001, cluster-corrected, both-sided). Similarly, for non-self-reference-congruent and non -self-reference-incongruent comparison, MVPA revealed a signiﬁcant above-chance difference between the two classes from 340 to 1000 ms after stimulus onset"
