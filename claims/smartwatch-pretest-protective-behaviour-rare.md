## Evidence

### Meredydd Williams 2019

Meredydd Williams, Jason R C Nurse, Sadie Creese. (2019). Smartwatch Games: Encouraging Privacy-Protective Behaviour in a Longitudinal Study. [doi:10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of monitoring-app logs collected every five minutes from 10 participants' Wear OS watches during the 18-day pretest phase. The logs showed "not a single participant had restricted their permissions" and two participants installed additional apps with sensitive permissions.

> "Throughout this 18-day period, not a single participant had restricted their permissions. In fact, these settings had been loosened by 9/10 users."

### Meredydd Williams 2019 (2)

Meredydd Williams, Jason R C Nurse, Sadie Creese. (2019). Smartwatch Games: Encouraging Privacy-Protective Behaviour in a Longitudinal Study. [doi:10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2019.04.026)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The same pretest log analysis of GPS state showed "all 10 users had their GPS enabled", implying participant locations were accessible for the first 18 days of the study.

> "At this pretest stage, all 10 users had their GPS enabled. Indeed, not a single person had adjusted this setting."
