### Touchie Claire 2013

Touchie Claire, Humphrey-Murto Susan, Varpio Lara. (2013). Teaching and assessing procedural skills: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-69

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group finding from the same 18-resident, three-group qualitative study; one participant explained "the main benefit of the exam was getting the supervision", calling the feedback hugely beneficial despite being artificial. Stations were eight minutes with two minutes of examiner feedback.
