# Resolution of JOLs increases across study–test cycles, improving between Cycles 1 and 2 but not from Cycle 2 to Cycle 3

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learners' resolution (within-participant gamma) improved from Cycle 1 to Cycle 2 but not from Cycle 2 to Cycle 3, for both encoding types. [→ Kubik 2022](#kubik-2022)

## Evidence

### Kubik 2022

Kubik, V., Jemstedt, A., Mahjub Eshratabadi, H., Schwartz, B. L., & Jönsson, F. U. (2022). The underconfidence-with-practice effect in action memory: The contribution of retrieval practice to metacognitive monitoring. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09288-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the same experiment, resolution for study–test items was computed as within-participant Goodman–Kruskal gamma; the main effect of cycle showed "F(1.76, 82.86) = 8.50, p < 0.001, ω2 = 0.078", with means rising from 0.37 to 0.69, then not further to 0.60 in Cycle 3.

> "As indicated by a main effect of cycle, F(1.76, 82.86) = 8.50, p < 0.001, ω2 = 0.078, learners improved their resolution scores from Cycle 1 ( M = 0.37, SE = 0.06) to Cycle 2 ( M = 0.69, SE = 0.06)"
