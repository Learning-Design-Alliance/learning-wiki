# Baseline simple response times were faster in the SID-Video study than the SID-Photo study and faster in young than older adults, with no study × age interaction

## Evidence

### Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck 2023

Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck. (2023). Effects of positive and negative social feedback on motivation, evaluative learning, and socio-emotional processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00178-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline simple RT task (50 trials) preceding the SID in both studies; mean RT was 379 ms (young) and 427 ms (older) in SID-Photo vs 259 ms and 312 ms in SID-Video. The interaction was not significant (F(1,202) = 0.1, p = 0.6663). RT was z-transformed in the SID task to account for these baseline differences.

> "The 2 × 2 design (study x age) permutation F-test on RT in the simple RT task showed a signi ﬁcant main effect of study ( F(1,202) = 257.9, p = 0.0001, CI 95% = [−0.0001 0.0005], η2p = 0.5608; Fig. 2a) and a signiﬁcant main effect of age ( F(1,202) = 47.9, p = 0.0001"
