## Evidence

### Masaaki Hiramatsu 2022

Masaaki Hiramatsu, S_Asagiri, Stella. G. Amano, Naohiro Takanashi, Shio K. Kawagoe, Kazuhisa Kamegai. (2022). Virtual ALMA Tour in VRChat: A Whole New Experience. Communicating Astronomy with the Public Journal, Volume 30, p.18. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2208.10740](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2208.10740)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey of 44 respondents (30 HMD, 8 Desktop mode, 6 YouTube) summarised in Figure 6. The article reports no significant difference in overall satisfaction by medium; equivalence was not formally tested and no effect size is printed.

> "There was no significant difference in the overall satisfaction level with the tour based on the medium through which they participated (HMD, Desktop mode, or YouTube) as shown in Figure 6."
