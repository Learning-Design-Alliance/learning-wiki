## Evidence

### McCarthy Nora 2023

McCarthy Nora, Neville Karen, Pope Andrew, Barry Lee, Livingstone Vicki. (2023). Effectiveness of a proficiency-based progression e-learning approach to training in communication in the context of clinically deteriorating patients: a multi-arm randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open 13(8):e072488. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072488

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Same RCT, change-in-proficiency analysis categorising each participant as became proficient, no change, or no longer proficient, compared with Fisher's exact test. "an extra 67.6% (25/37) of participants in the (PBP) group achieved proficiency"; pairwise PBP vs S p=0.020, PBP vs HSE p<0.001.

> "Changes in proficiency were significantly different between the three groups (p=0.001). Post-intervention, an extra 67.6% (25/37) of participants in the (PBP) group achieved proficiency in the low-fidelity simulation environment compared with 38.5% (15/39) in the (S) group and 26.3% (10/38) in the (HSE) group."
