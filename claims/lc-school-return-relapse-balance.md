### MacLean A 2023

MacLean A, Wild C, Hunt K, Nettleton S, Skea ZC, Ziebland S. (2023). Impact of Long Covid on the school experiences of children and young people: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2023-075756

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview accounts from CYP and parents in the theme on returning to school with Long Covid. Participants reported that "attempts to return to school invariably led to 'crashes', 'huge relapses' or feeling 'exhausted'", requiring further absence and trade-offs such as concentrating on fewer subjects.
