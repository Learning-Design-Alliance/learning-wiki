### Meagan Sundstrom 2025

Meagan Sundstrom, Justin Gambrell, Adrienne L. Traxler, and Eric Brewe. (2025). Evaluating recognition and recall formats of social network surveys in physics education research. [doi:10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sundstrom](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2025.pr.sundstrom)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Examination of the six surveys (of 54) with significant correlations (p<0.05) between roster position and peer selections; the authors state the effects "may occur by random chance" since they were not systematic in class size or survey timing.

> "The surveys with significant name order effects are not systematically large or small courses (the red triangles span a range of class sizes) or pre- or post-semester surveys (three red triangles occur on pre-semester surveys and three red triangles occur on post-semester surveys)."
