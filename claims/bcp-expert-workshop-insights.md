The Results section reports insights from the expert workshop with 5 licensed cognitive-behavioral psychotherapists conducted in September 2023. Participants critically examined the preliminary framework through structured discussion rounds and a SWOT analysis.

> "First, participants emphasized that adopting an educational-psychological perspective within psychotherapy is both valuable and necessary, particularly for structuring training and competence development. Second, they highlighted the importance of differentiating psychotherapeutic practice according to its various Modalities in order to adequately capture the diversity of competencies required in face-to-face, digital, and blended formats."
