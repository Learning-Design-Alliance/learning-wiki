The review reports, citing Watt et al.'s integrative review (reference 30), that no best practice method of ensuring cultural competency training has been established, while formal education is more likely to improve cultural competency than exposure alone. Second-hand attribution; no effect size printed.

> "A review concluded that a best practice method of ensuring cultural competency training has not yet been established [30]. However, formal education is more likely to improve cultural competency than merely being exposed to culturally diverse patients, even when having access to good role models [30]."
