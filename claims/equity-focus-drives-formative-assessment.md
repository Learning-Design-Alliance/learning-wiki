`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Observation-based finding from the four-school case study: teachers look at evidence regularly, use technology-based programs with formative-like assessments and ongoing performance data, and hold individual student conferencing to help students master skills before final assessment. Principals report their schools improved substantially in formative assessment use over recent years.

> "Although she found that none had yet undergone extensive training in formative assessment practices, the schools' focus on equity and supporting individual needs was leading teachers to apply elements of formative assessment."
