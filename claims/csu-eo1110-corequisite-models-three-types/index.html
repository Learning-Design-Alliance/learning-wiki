Bracco 2019¶

Bracco, K. R., Schrager, C., Calisi, G., Gutierrez, P., Salciccioli, M., & Finkelstein, N. (2019). College-ready in the California State University system: Campus experiences implementing EO 1110. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.calstate.edu/eo/EO-1110.html

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Cross-campus qualitative analysis of interviews, focus groups, and course-catalog review at nine CSU campuses categorized corequisite designs. The report states the three categories and notes variation in "the degree and type of articulation between parent and support courses." No outcome data are reported.

"The types of corequisite courses offered on the nine campuses fall into three major categories: (1) support courses that serve students in multiple parent courses, covering content and study skills"
