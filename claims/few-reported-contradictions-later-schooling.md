### Kyla Adams 2021 (2)

Kyla Adams, Roshan Dattatri, Tejinder Kaur and David Blair. (2021). Long-term impact of a primary school intervention on aspects of Einsteinian physics. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac12a9](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac12a9)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

> "Of the eleven participants, one mentioned a contradiction between what was taught in this intervention and what they were taught later in school."
