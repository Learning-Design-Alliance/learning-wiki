### Tsuchiya Miyako 2026

Tsuchiya Miyako, Adachi Keiichiro, Kimata Akiko, Kumagai Kaori. (2026). Development of a Web-Based Experiential Learning Intervention for the Public to Reduce Cancer Stigma: Tutorial on the Application of Intervention Mapping. JMIR Cancer. https://doi.org/10.2196/71166

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square comparison within the same public survey (n=1076) between participants with and without friends diagnosed with cancer. The group without such friends more often wanted "survival rates of all types of cancer" (χ21 =7.3; P=.007).

> "Chi-square tests revealed that the participants whose friends had not been diagnosed with cancer were more likely to require information concerning “survival rates of all types of cancer” (χ21 =7.3; P=.007) compared with those whose friends had been diagnosed"
