Höbaus Clemens, Schmidmaier Ralf. (2025). Individual and group reflection in lecture-based large groups lead to comparable learning success. Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-025-02610-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student feedback collected via ARS at the end of the lecture and again at follow-up showed "a preference for GR with 69% during the response in the lecture hall", which shifted to nearly equal preference over time.

> "There was a preference for GR with 69% during the response in the lecture hall. Over time those results shifted to a nearly equal number of students thinking that individual or group reflection would be better for the study results"
