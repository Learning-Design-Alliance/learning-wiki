## Evidence

### Couper K 2018

Couper K, Velho RM, Quinn T, Devrell A, Lall R, Orriss B, Yeung J, Perkins GD. (2018). Training approaches for the deployment of a mechanical chest compression device: a randomised controlled manikin study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-019009

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Non-technical skills in the randomised manikin trial were rated from video using the Team Emergency Assessment Measure. Component scores were "38.0 (35.7 to 40.3)" vs "37.6 (34.8 to 40.4)", P=0.804; the global score difference was 0.15 (95% CI −0.87 to 1.17), P=0.760.
