## Evidence

### Vogel Daniela 2016

Vogel Daniela, Harendza Sigrid. (2016). Basic practical skills teaching and learning in undergraduate medical education – a review on methodological evidence. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001063](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001063)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of controlled studies on self-guided and self-directed suturing practice, including one study where adding expert feedback brought no further improvement.

> "other studies reported that self-guided suturing practice [3] or a self-directed schedule for suturing practice [47] were associated with better suturing skill acquisition and additional expert feedback lead to no further skill improvement [31]"

### Vogel Daniela 2016 (2)

Vogel Daniela, Harendza Sigrid. (2016). Basic practical skills teaching and learning in undergraduate medical education – a review on methodological evidence. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001063](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001063)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review-reported controlled studies on voluntary reflective writing with skills practice and voluntary practice with positively deviant peers.

> "voluntary participation in reflective writing and skills practice [54] and voluntary practice with positively deviant peers [62] led to better clinical skill performance of participating students"
