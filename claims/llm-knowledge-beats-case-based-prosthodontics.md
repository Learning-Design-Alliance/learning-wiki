### Erdal Selda Gökçe 2026

Erdal Selda Gökçe, Güdül Aynur Beyza Çavuşculu, Köroğlu Ayşegül. (2026). The effectiveness of large language models in dental specialty questions: a comparative study in the field of prosthodontics. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08808-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison across the 106 knowledge and 22 case-based items tested under both protocols; for example GPT-5 scored 91% on knowledge versus 64% on case questions under independent querying. The authors state this pattern is consistent with prior reports of LLM limits in case interpretation.

> "Overall, the evaluated LLMs demonstrated higher accuracy in knowledge-based questions than in case-based questions, indicating stronger performance in theoretical content than in clinically oriented reasoning."
