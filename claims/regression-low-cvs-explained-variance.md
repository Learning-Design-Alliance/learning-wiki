## Evidence

### Edelsbrunner et al. 2018

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Lennart Schalk, Ralph Schumacher, Elsbeth Stern. (2018). Variable control and conceptual change: A large-scale quantitative study in elementary school. Learning and Individual Differences 66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2018.02.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Basic regression-based change score model estimated for comparison with the mixture modeling approach; the article states "the estimated explained variance in knowledge development by students' CVS scores is low". Including grade or gender did not change results.

> "The model estimates indicated that students' CVS score explained 2% of variance in change in their misconceptionsscores from pretest to posttest, 1% of variance in change in their intermediate conceptions-scores, and 2% of variance in change in their scientiﬁc concepts-scores."
