In the usage example, students predicted the output of a given loop program; common wrong answers were traced to the misconception Iteration-(3)-(b)-(ii)-(A) (update before body) alone or combined with Iteration-(3)-(a)-(ii). The authors conclude "four seemingly different errors actually consist of three mental models".
