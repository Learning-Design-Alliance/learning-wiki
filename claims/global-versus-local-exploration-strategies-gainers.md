# The two gainer profiles differ in problem-solving strategy: Expressive Explorers enact global exploration with reflection on past solutions, Calm Tinkerers enact local exploration with in-the-moment correction

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Expressive Explorers do more actions overall, more edge additions, higher add-to-delete ratio, and open history more, while Calm Tinkerers show more deletions and T1_T1/T1_T2 addition-deletion sequences (most log behaviors significant at p = 0.00*). [→ Jauwairia Nasir 2021](#jauwairia-nasir-2021)

## Evidence

### Jauwairia Nasir 2021

Jauwairia Nasir, Aditi Kothiyal, Barbara Bruno, Pierre Dillenbourg. (2021). Many are the ways to learn identifying multi-modal behavioral profiles of collaborative learning in constructivist activities. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2021) 16:485–523. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09358-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis comparison of the two gainer clusters (Table 4: T_add p = 0.03*, T_remove p = 0.00*, T_action p = 0.00*, T_hist p = 0.01*) supports the interpretation that the two learning groups reach similar gains via different exploratory log-behavior profiles.

> "these findings suggest that Expressive Explorers enact a global exploratory approach characterized by global reflection on previous solutions while Calm Tinkerers exhibit a local exploratory approach where they carry out in-the-moment reflection and correct their own and their partners’ actions on the go, which can be described as local reflection."
