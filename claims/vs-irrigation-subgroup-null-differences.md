## Evidence

### Yue Xin 2025

Yue Xin, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Sun Jianping, Xu Li, Shen Jing. (2025). Evaluation of a virtual simulation system for root canal irrigation training in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08387-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing undergraduates (n = 26) and postgraduates (n = 8) across five outcome measures; no comparison was significant. Baseline T1 showed a trend (p = 0.096, d = 0.67).

> "The postgraduate group consistently achieved higher scores than the undergraduate group across theoretical knowledge (S1, S2), practical performance (T1, T2), and virtual simulation outcomes (V1). However, none of these differences reached statistical significance (all p > 0.05; Table 3)."

### Yue Xin 2025 (2)

Yue Xin, Nie Shuai, Dai Yanmei, Sun Jianping, Xu Li, Shen Jing. (2025). Evaluation of a virtual simulation system for root canal irrigation training in preclinical dental education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08387-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive subgroup comparison of pre-post practical gains reported in the Results section; the between-group comparison was underpowered (power 1 − β = 0.20), so these descriptive differences are not significance-tested.

> "The improvement in practical scores was greater among undergraduates (ΔT = 3.30) than postgraduates (ΔT = 2.50), suggesting that the simulation system provided relatively greater benefits for undergraduate students by accelerating the acquisition of procedural skills."
