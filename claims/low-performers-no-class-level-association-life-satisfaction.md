## Evidence

### Rathmann Katharina 2018

Rathmann Katharina, Herke Max, Bilz Ludwig, Rimpelä Arja, Hurrelmann Klaus, Richter Matthias. (2018). Class-Level School Performance and Life Satisfaction: Differential Sensitivity for Low- and High-Performing School-Aged Children. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15122750

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slopes analysis (Table 3) of the NEPS multilevel models: for students at −1 SD individual performance, the slope of class-level performance was 0.116 (SE 0.108, p < 0.284), not significant. Hypothesis 2 was not supported; low-performing students revealed the lowest life satisfaction.

> "Although the simple slope between lower individual performance and higher class-level average performance was not statistical significant, this finding is quite surprising from the viewpoint of social comparison theory"
