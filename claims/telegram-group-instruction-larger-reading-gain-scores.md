## Evidence

### Naderi 2018

Naderi, S., & Akrami, A. (2018). EFL Learners' Reading Comprehension Development through MALL: Telegram Groups in Focus. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 339-350. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11223a

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U test on gain scores (experimental M=8.88, SD=2.26; control M=6.33, SD=2.19) because gain-score distributions were non-normal for the control group. The article states "The calculated effect size was .50" and calls the difference strong.
