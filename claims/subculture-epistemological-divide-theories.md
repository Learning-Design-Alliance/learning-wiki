### M.E. McCracken 2015

M.E. McCracken. (2015). The Two Subcultures: The teaching of theory and Physics' place in the college curriculum. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1508.00273](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1508.00273)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The author reproduces verbatim, for fear of paraphrasing inaccurately, the explanation colleagues gave for dropping theoretical investigation: in the biological sciences a theory is a fact, not a mere prediction, and "What we investigate are hypotheses and not theories." The author responds from a Popperian position that all theories are provisional. This is a single-institution report, not a systematic survey.

> "It is a guiding principle in other investigations, but it in itself is not being investigated. What we investigate are hypotheses and not theories."
