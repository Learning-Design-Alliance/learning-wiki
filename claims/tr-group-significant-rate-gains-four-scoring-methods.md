# The TR group (timed reading only) made statistically significant reading rate gains of about 15 to 27 swpm across four scoring methods

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The TR group's reading rate increased significantly under all four scoring methods, including a medium effect size (d = -1.01) for the last-minus-first method. [→ Shimono 2018](#shimono-2018)

## Evidence

### Shimono 2018

Shimono, T. R. (2018). L2 reading fluency progression using timed reading and repeated oral reading. Reading in a Foreign Language, 30(1). http://nflrc.hawaii.edu/rfl

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t tests for the TR group (n = 18, middle-proficiency classes) reading 30 timed readings over 10 weeks; the pretest-to-posttest gain was about 15 swpm with a medium effect size, and all four scoring methods reached significance under the Holm-Bonferroni procedure.

> "The last minus the first scoring method saw a gain of about 15 swpm, t(17) = -4.26, p = .001, and a medium effect size (d = -1.01)."
