`q2 i?` When the PRT results are combined with the overlapping UAT and EQT segments, the percent of the actual population not taking the assessment seriously is 1.8% for the FCI, 2.2% for the CSEM, and 1.5% for the BEMA. [→ David P. Waters 2019](#david-p-waters-2019)

## Evidence

### David P. Waters 2019

David P. Waters, Dragos Amarie, Rebecca A. Booth, Christopher Conover and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2019). Investigating students seriousness during selected conceptual inventory surveys. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020118)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of pre- and post-test responses stored in PhysPort's database, combining the PRT circle with the UAT–EQT overlap, found '1,120 out of 63,896 assessments (1.8%) for the FCI' caught as non-serious, with 2.2% for the CSEM and 1.5% for the BEMA.

> "1,120 out of 63,896 assessments (1.8%) for the FCI, 329 out of 14,876 assessments (2.2%) for the CSEM, and 133 out of 8,642 assessments (1.5%) for the BEMA."
