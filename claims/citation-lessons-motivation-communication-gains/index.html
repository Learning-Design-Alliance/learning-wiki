Sadykova 2014¶

Sadykova, A. G., Yashina, M. E., & Sharafieva, A. D. (2014). Citation as a Stimulus to Boost Students' Communication Skills at the English Lessons. English Language Teaching, 7(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n12p12

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' summary of the ascertaining stage comparing the experimental group 10 "A" with control group 10 "B" after the formative lessons; the quote reports increased motivation, significantly increased knowledge of aphorisms and proverbs, and improved communication skills only in the experimental group.

"can be characterized by increase of motivation of students, indicator of the level of knowledge of aphorisms, proverbs increased significantly, moreover, communication skills improved, to the final lesson, students were able to express their points of view freely, to use aphorisms, proverbs in their speech, while in the control group, in which this technique was not tested, the result remained virtually unchanged"
