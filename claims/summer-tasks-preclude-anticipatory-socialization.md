Arnold, Karen D.; Chewning, Alexandra; Castleman, Benjamin; Page, Lindsay C. (2015). Advisor and Student Experiences of Summer Support for College-Intending, Low-Income High School Graduates. Journal of College Access. http://scholarworks.wmich.edu/jca/vol1/iss1/3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative findings from the 2011 advisor interviews show the intervention concentrated on matriculation tasks all summer; advisors had little time for academic, emotional, and socialization transition issues that might influence college success once enrolled.

> "Given the pressure of financial and informational concerns, few Summer College Connect students were able to have conversations with their advisors about, choosing courses, finding and using campus resources, connecting with future classmates, dealing with academic weaknesses, affording books, and other transition issues."
