## Evidence

### Luckie Douglas B. 2017

Luckie Douglas B., Hoskinson Anne-Marie, Griffin Caleigh E., Hess Andrea L., Price Katrina J., Tawa Alex, Thacker Samantha M. (2017). Integrating Concepts in Biology Textbook Increases Learning: Assessment Triangulation Using Concept Inventory, Card Sorting, and MCAT Instruments, Followed by Longitudinal Tracking. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-06-0204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

40-question MCAT-style content posttest compared ICB students (n = 77) with historical cohorts (n = 417) taught by the same instructor since 2000, ACT-normalized. ICB scored 62.22%, within the top quintile of the 2000–2013 range.

> "ICB student performance (62.22 ± 1.43%) was statistically greater than historical cohorts from years 2000–2001 (53.39 ± 1.96%, p < 0.05) and lower than the highest historical MCAT score achieved in 2011 (64.21 ± 1.84%; Figure 3A and inset)."
