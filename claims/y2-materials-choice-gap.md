## Evidence

### Debra Bernstein 2022

Debra Bernstein, Gillian Puttick, Kristen Wendell, Fayette Shaw, Ethan Danahy, Michael Cassidy. (2022). Designing biomimetic robots: iterative development of an integrated technology design curriculum. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10061-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Observation of Y2 focal groups found students used pliable materials such as flat cardboard rather than recognizing that digging structures needed to be stronger or reinforced, prompting the Y3 focus on internal anatomy.

> "However, we noticed that students in Year 2 were not always extending their animal structure–function understanding to the choice or use of building materials. Students were relying on pliable materials (e.g., cardboard used only in flat pieces) instead of recognizing that digging structures needed to be stronger."
