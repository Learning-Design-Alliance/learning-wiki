## Discussion

One study is recorded (Brewer & Klein 2003). Affiliation motive was measured (Work Motivation Inventory) and used to block participants, not assigned, so this comparison is associational even though the interdependence conditions in the same experiment were randomised; it is coded accordingly. The outcome is agreement with attitude statements (six of twelve differed), not achievement, which did not differ by affiliation motive. This study's entry on this page does not describe the participants. The same study's entry on [the achievement claim](interdependence-type-no-achievement-effect-asynchronous.md) describes them as 280 undergraduate business majors, blocked by affiliation motive and randomly assigned to triads; an earlier title called them adult online learners, which no entry for this study says, so the scope now follows that entry.
