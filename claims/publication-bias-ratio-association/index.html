q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Publication-bias assessment within the three-level model: funnel plots showed no substantial asymmetry, but PET gave a small bias-adjusted effect (r = .21, p = .046) and PEESE an estimate near zero (r = −.01, p = .840); effect sizes decreased with greater study precision.

"the extended Egger's regression showed significant small-study effects for the association between child-caregiver ratio and interactive skills ( p < .001), suggesting potential publication bias."
