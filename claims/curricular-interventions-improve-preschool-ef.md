## Evidence

### Ackerman 2017

Ackerman, D. J., & Friedman-Krauss, A. H. (2017). Preschoolers’ Executive Function: Importance, Contributors, Research Needs and Assessment Options. Policy Information Report and ETS Research Report Series No. RR-17-22. https://doi.org/10.1002/ets2.12148

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The report reviews intervention studies including Tools of the Mind, which showed improved EF in a New Jersey study but findings were not replicated in Tennessee. A more recent randomized trial in Massachusetts "found significant positive impacts on measures of children’s working memory, inhibitory control, and cognitive flexibility." PATHS showed small but statistically significant improvements in working memory, inhibitory control, and attention-shifting.
