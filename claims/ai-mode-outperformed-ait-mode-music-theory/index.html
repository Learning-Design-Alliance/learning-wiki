Evidence¶

Aldalalah and Fong 2010¶

Osamah (Mohammad Ameen) Ahmad Aldalalah & Soon Fook Fong. (2010). Effects of Modality and Redundancy Principles on the Learning and Attitude of a Computer-Based Music Theory Lesson among Jordanian Primary Pupils. International Education Studies, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/ies

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANCOVA post hoc pairwise comparison with pre-test covariate among the three treatment groups (N = 405 third-grade pupils). The AI group significantly outperformed the AIT group (Mean = 16.63, SD = 4.33) at "p = 0.000".

"The AI (Mean = 25.17, SD = 3.35,) pupils’ learning significantly outperformed the TI (Mean = 16.82, SD = 3.77) and the AIT (Mean = 16.63, SD = 4.33)."
