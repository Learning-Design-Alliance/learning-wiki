## Subclaims
`q1 i?` In the pretest, the frequency of correct answers for determining the numbers of poles and their locations was lower for the other types of magnets (disk, ring, and sphere) compared to bar and horseshoe magnets. [→ Okulu 2018](#okulu-2018)

## Evidence

### Okulu 2018

Okulu, H. Z., & Oğuz Ünver, A. (2018). The Process of Facilitating Knowledge Acquisition and Retention: An Inquiry into Magnetic Poles with Challenging Questions. International Education Studies, 11(5), 25-37. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v11n5p25

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest analysis of 65 pre-service teachers' responses, in which correct answer frequencies were fewer in the other types of magnets compared to bar and horseshoe. The study interprets this as evidence that learning still bases on memorization at schools, an author explanation rather than a tested contrast.
