### Tachibana Tetsuya 2025

Tachibana Tetsuya, Katagiri Hiroki, Ogawa Takahisa, Koyano Gaku, Jinno Tetsuya. (2025). Enhancing the accuracy of cup placement in total hip arthroplasty using CT-based navigation by a trainee surgeon: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-025-08493-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Safety outcomes from the retrospective cohort of 112 navigated trainee cases: "there were no cup revisions, loosening, or postoperative dislocations within 6 months after surgery". The authors interpret CT-based navigation as a safe educational tool for inexperienced trainees.

> "In all cases, cup placement was performed according to the planned size and stable press-fit fixation, and there were no cup revisions, loosening, or postoperative dislocations within 6 months after surgery."
