## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Adileh 2012

Adileh, M. (2012). Teaching music as a university elective course through e-learning. Australian Journal of Music Education, 2012(1), 71-79. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1000246

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Narrative literature review (type e) covering Jennings (2005), Reynolds (2005), Gall & Breeze (2005), and Seddon (2007). The review notes Seddon's e-learning composition study confirmed collaborative composition was technically possible and that adolescent participants described the experience as 'fun', 'interesting', 'enjoyable' and 'exciting'.

> "These prior studies in the music domain examined the use of music technology but were conducted in face-to-face environments."
