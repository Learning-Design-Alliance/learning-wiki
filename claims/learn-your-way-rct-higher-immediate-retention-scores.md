## Evidence

### LearnLM Team 2025

LearnLM Team, Google. (2025). Towards an AI-Augmented Textbook. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial: 60 students aged 15-18 from Chicago-area schools studied an unfamiliar textbook chapter with Learn Your Way or Adobe Acrobat Digital Reader, then took a 15-minute expert-written Immediate Assessment. Learn Your Way scored higher; Mann-Whitney U gave "p= 0.03".

> "The students who used Learn Your Way received higher scores than those who used the Digital Reader, in both the immediate (p= 0.03) and retention (p= 0.03) assessments."

### LearnLM Team 2025 (2)

LearnLM Team, Google. (2025). Towards an AI-Augmented Textbook. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same RCT's Retention Assessment, emailed three days post-session and designed to take 5-10 minutes; 58 of 60 participants completed it. Learn Your Way again scored higher with "p= 0.03" on the Mann-Whitney U test.

> "The students who used Learn Your Way received higher scores than those who used the Digital Reader, in both the immediate (p= 0.03) and retention (p= 0.03) assessments."
