## Evidence

### Santos 2021

Santos, D. J. D., Goto, T., Lu, T.-Y., Ho, S. C.-C., Wang, T.-W., On, A. Y. L., Hashimoto, T., & Young, S.-C. (2021). Investigative Study on Preprint Journal Club as an Effective Method of Teaching Latest Knowledge in Astronomy. arXiv:2106.01688. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010145](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010145)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of the NTHU AC class on comfort reading papers outside one's field: 8/10 enrollees scored ≤ 3 versus 2/8 volunteers. The tests: "H(2) = 7.24, p = 0.027" and Dunn "p = 0.041". The authors attribute this to volunteers being senior graduate students and postdocs with more prerequisite knowledge.

> "Our countercheck with Kruskal-Wallis test, H(2) = 7.24, p = 0.027, and a pairwise post-hoc Dunn test with Bonferroni adjustments (p = 0.041) suggests that this may be signiﬁcant."
