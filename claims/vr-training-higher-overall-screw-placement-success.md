## Evidence

### Pasqualotto Eric 2026

Pasqualotto Eric, Pasqualotto Tales, Lopes Victor Hugo Pinheiro, Lunelli Laura, Santos Lucas Loiola. (2026). Virtual reality surgical simulator training of residents and early-career surgeons for pedicle screw placement: a systematic review and meta-analysis. European Journal of Orthopaedic Surgery & Traumatology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00590-026-05008-1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Random-effects meta-analysis of six RCTs on overall screw placement success printed "RR 1.17; 95% CI 1.04 to 1.32; p < 0.01; I2 = 83%". A leave-one-out sensitivity analysis showed stability without major changes in significance.

> "VR-trained residents and early-career surgeons also demonstrated a higher overall success rate (RR 1.17; 95% CI 1.04 to 1.32; p < 0.01; I2 = 83%; Fig. 3A)"
