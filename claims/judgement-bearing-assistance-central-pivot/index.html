Evidence¶

Du Y 2026¶

Du Y, Yuan Y (2026) Epistemic dependence in AI-mediated learning. AI & SOCIETY. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-026-03294-1

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Conceptual distinction drawn in the review's conceptual analysis of epistemic dependence. The review defines judgement-bearing assistance as assistance that "evaluates correctness, relevance, quality, persuasiveness, ethical acceptability or evidential sufficiency", distinguishing it from instrumental or representational assistance. The argument is theoretical; no empirical test is reported.

"Nevertheless, judgement-bearing assistance is normatively more demanding, because it delegates not only production but the standards by which production is assessed. The distinction is therefore the paper's central analytic pivot."
