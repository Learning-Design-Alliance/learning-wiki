The Meetings at Mobile 1911¶

The Meetings at Mobile, Alabama, February 22-25, 1911. (1911). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Editorial recommendation arising from the reporter's observation that at both the College Teachers' Society and the general meetings, distractions prevented concentrated discussions. The small attendance at the society's business session is also noted.

"If the meetings of these societies are to be productive of worthy results, they should be held before the general sessions of the Departmnt of Superintendence begin"
