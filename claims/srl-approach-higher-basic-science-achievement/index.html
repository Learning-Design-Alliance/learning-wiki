Nwafor Chika E 2015¶

Nwafor Chika E, Abigail Chikaodinaka Obodo, Gabriel Okafor. (2015). Effect of Self Regulated Learning Approach on Junior Secondary School Students' Achievement in Basic Science. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.5. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083647

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental pretest posttest non-equivalent control group study with four intact JSS II classes in two Nigerian schools; hypotheses tested with ANCOVA at alpha 0.05. The "calculated F-ratio (F-cal) due to teaching method is 217.427" exceeded the critical value 3.89, so the null hypothesis was rejected. Adjusted means were 55.07 (treatment) versus 40.24 (control).
