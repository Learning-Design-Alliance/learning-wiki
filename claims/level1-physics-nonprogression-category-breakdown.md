`q2 i?` The 2006-2007 cohort analysis printed five reasons for non-progression to level 2 physics: inability to progress without level 1 maths (13.3%), no intention to continue (20.2%), withdrawal during first year (4.0%), grades insufficient for entry (13.3%), and choosing another subject (7.0%).
