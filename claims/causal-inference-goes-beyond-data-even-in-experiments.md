## Evidence

### Grosz 2023

Grosz, M. P. (2023). Should researchers make causal inferences and recommendations for practice on the basis of nonexperimental studies? Educational Psychology Review, 35, 57. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09777-7

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument in the commentary: causal inference "always goes beyond the data, even in a randomized experiment". The author illustrates with a reading-program study where teacher adherence, not program content, might have caused comprehension differences.

> "In a randomized experiment, the experimental manipulation might, for example, not only affect the treatment variable of interest but also other variables and these other variables (not the treatment variable of interest) might have caused the effect"
