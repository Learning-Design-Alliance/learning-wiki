## Evidence

### Knorr M 2019

Knorr M, Meyer H, Sehner S, Hampe W, Zimmermann S. (2019). Exploring sociodemographic subgroup differences in multiple mini-interview (MMI) performance based on MMI station type and the implications for the predictive fairness of the Hamburg MMI. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1674-z

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of the student sample (N = 400 admitted students with first OSCE results after 1.5 years, cohorts 2012-2015). The authors therefore concentrated on the communication station as the criterion for predictive fairness analyses.

> "MMI performance (zMMI overall) demonstrated a weak but significant positive correlation with performance at the OSCE communication station (r = 0.17, p = 0.001). However, the correlation between zMMI overall and OSCE overall performance was non-significant (r = 0.06, p = 0.20)."
