## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The Condition × Block interaction showed real stimulation at right and left PMd produced similar performance superior to sham for almost all training blocks (except Block 9), while RP-sham improved faster than IP-sham. [→ Taewon Kim 2021](#taewon-kim-2021)

## Evidence

### Taewon Kim 2021

Taewon Kim, John J. Buchanan, Jessica A. Bernard and David L. Wright. (2021). Improving online and offline gain from repetitive practice using anodal tDCS at dorsal premotor cortex. npj Science of Learning 6:31. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00109-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The 4 (Condition) × 9 (Block) mixed ANOVA on training-block total time showed a significant Condition × Block interaction with printed effect size ηp² = 0.16. Simple main effect analysis revealed the stimulation conditions beat sham on nearly all blocks except Block 9.

> "This analysis also revealed a signi ﬁcant Condition × Block interaction, F(24, 480) = 3.67, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.16."

### Taewon Kim 2021 (2)

Taewon Kim, John J. Buchanan, Jessica A. Bernard and David L. Wright. (2021). Improving online and offline gain from repetitive practice using anodal tDCS at dorsal premotor cortex. npj Science of Learning 6:31. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00109-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

The same 4 (Condition) × 9 (Block) mixed ANOVA on mean total time during training blocks revealed significant main effects of Condition with printed effect size ηp² = 0.67 and of Block with printed effect size ηp² = 0.39.

> "revealed signi ﬁcant main effects of Condition, F(3,60) = 41.07, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.67, and Block, F(8, 480) = 101.08, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.39."
