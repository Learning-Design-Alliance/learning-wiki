## Evidence

### Carlos González 2023

Carlos González, Daniel Ponce, Violeta Fernández. (2023). Teachers' experiences of teaching online during COVID-19: implications for postpandemic professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10200-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview-based qualitative study of 151 Chilean higher education teachers analyzed with hybrid thematic analysis; the results section reports that teachers had different self-perceptions of their digital pedagogical skills and that those "well positioned to respond to the pandemic" had prior LMS and multimedia skills, while others struggled.

> "At the beginning of the COVID-19 pandemic, teachers had different self-perceptions of their digital pedagogical skills. Teachers with good self-perceived skills, e.g., teachers who were advanced in the use of the institutional learning management systems and/or had the ability to create multimedia content before the disruption, were well positioned to respond to the pandemic."
