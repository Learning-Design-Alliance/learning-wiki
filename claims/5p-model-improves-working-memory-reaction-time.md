### Srikoon et al. 2017

Srikoon, S., Bunterm, T., Nethanomsak, T., & Ngang, T. K. (2017). A comparative study of the effects of the neurocognitive-based model and the conventional model on learner attention, working memory and mood. Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 14(1), 83-110. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1150726

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA multivariate test of working memory reaction time in the randomized Grade 9 study. The article reports the effect explained "the variance of 78.7 percent of the reaction time of working memory" (partial eta-squared = 0.787).

> "the instruction models have significantly influenced the reaction time of working memory at a 0.05 significance level (F(13,17)= 4.845, p=0.002) and can explain the variance of 78.7 percent of the reaction time of working memory"
