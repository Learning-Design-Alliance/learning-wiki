### Helena Salminen 2014

Helena Salminen, Nabil Zary, Karin Björklund, Eva Toth-Pal, Charlotte Leanderson. (2014). Virtual Patients in Primary Care: Developing a Reusable Model That Fosters Reflective Practice and Clinical Reasoning. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2616

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative content analysis of semistructured focus group interviews with three groups of final-semester students (n=14) after a VP pilot session. Students reported "a generally positive learning experience" and that the model "supported self-directed learning as it could activate students and stimulate reflection."

> "Students reported that they had had a generally positive learning experience working with the VP. They felt that the VP-model supported self-directed learning as it could activate students and stimulate reflection."
