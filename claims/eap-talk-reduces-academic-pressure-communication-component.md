## Evidence

### Zou 2026

Zou, B., Yuan, Y., Wang, C., & Du, Y. (2026). Can AI Reduce Acculturative Stress? Exploring the Role of AI-Mediated Speaking Practice in Chinese International Students' Perceived Language Insufficiency, Social Isolation, and Academic Pressure. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.16653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.16653)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model in the RCT (N = 126). The academic pressure interaction was "β = -0.42, SE = 0.14, t = -3.00, p = .003"; experimental means fell from 4.71 to 4.15, control from 4.66 to 4.52.

> "Significant interaction effects were also found for social isolation, β = -0.56, SE = 0.16, t = -3.50, p < .001, and academic pressure, β = -0.42, SE = 0.14, t = -3.00, p = .003."
