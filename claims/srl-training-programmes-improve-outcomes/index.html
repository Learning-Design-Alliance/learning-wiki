The review reports meta-analyses of SRL training programmes (citing de Boer et al. 2018; Dignath and Büttner 2008; Donker et al. 2014; Hattie et al. 1996; Theobald 2021) showing "improvements in their SRL strategies, as well as increased motivation and academic achievement, and reduced anxiety". No effect sizes are printed in the quoted text.

"Students who participated in these programmes demonstrated improvements in their SRL strategies, as well as increased motivation and academic achievement, and reduced anxiety (de Boer et al. 2018; Dignath and Büttner 2008; Donker et al. 2014; Hattie et al. 1996; Theobald 2021)."
