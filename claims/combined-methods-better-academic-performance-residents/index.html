Authors' interpretation in the discussion of their NMA findings; they explicitly speculate about the mechanism, offering no direct test of it.

"Our study found that compared to a single teaching method, the combination of multiple methods, such as PBL + Wechat and CBL + Wechat, can achieve better results. We speculate that this may be related to the combined use of multiple methods to achieve the effect of learning from each other's strengths and weaknesses."

Related Claims¶

  • TBL alone and TBL combined with other methods both outperform LBL, with no significant difference between the two TBL formats — related
