## Evidence

### Jana Gonnermann-Müller 2026

Jana Gonnermann-Müller, Jennifer Haase, Nicolas Leins, Thomas Kosch, Sebastian Pokutta. (2026). LLM-Based Educational Simulation: Evaluating Temporal Student Persona Stability Across ADHD-Related Persona Specifications. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06307 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment I education scenario with default personas receiving only the minimal instruction "You are a student" and a neutral task. Default self-reports clustered at M = 18.2 (SD = 3.61) versus observer M = 4.44 (SD = 2.60); the same directional gap appeared in the workplace scenario (self-report M = 13.9, observer M = 2.78). Descriptive only.

> "In the education scenario, default personas produced self-report scores aligned with the moderate-intensity condition, while their observer ratings remained much closer to the low-intensity condition."
