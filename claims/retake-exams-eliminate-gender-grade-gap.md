## Evidence

### David J. Webb and Cassandra A. Paul 2023

David J. Webb and Cassandra A. Paul. (2023). Attributing equity gaps to course structure in introductory physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020126](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020126)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

HLM fit comparing 4 retake classes (N = 610) with 52 non-retake classes (N = 12,884) in the same course series; b_Female*Retake = 0.229, z = 3.15, P = 0.002, and b_Female = -0.210 in non-retake classes. The retake gap difference was about 3.15 standard errors above the background gap (P = 0.002).

> "From bF emale∗Retake we find that female students in the retake classes had an additional 1/4 of a grade point so that the gender gap is essentially gone"
