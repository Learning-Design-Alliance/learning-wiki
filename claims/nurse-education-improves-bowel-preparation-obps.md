## Evidence

### Lee Yoo Jin MD; Kim Eun Soo MD 2015

Lee Yoo Jin MD; Kim Eun Soo MD, PhD; Park Kyung Sik MD; Cho Kwang Bum MD, PhD; Jang Byoung Kuk MD, PhD; Chung Woo Jin MD, PhD; Hwang Jae Seok MD, PhD; Somchai Amornyotin. (2015). Education for Ward Nurses Influences the Quality of Inpatient's Bowel Preparation for Colonoscopy. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000001423

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective, double-blinded, non-randomized controlled trial with 103 educated-ward and 102 control-ward inpatients rated by blinded expert colonoscopists. Mean "total OBPS score" was 4.42 ± 2.23 vs 6.15 ± 2.38 (P < 0.001), favoring the educated ward.

> "More patients in the educated ward were found to have a lower total OBPS score, which suggests a better quality bowel preparation in the educated ward (4.42 ± 2.23 vs 6.15 ± 2.38, P < 0.001)."
