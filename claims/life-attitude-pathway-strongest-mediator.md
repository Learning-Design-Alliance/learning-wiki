Pairwise contrast tests of indirect effects in the 1,019-student bootstrap analysis. The article reports "the indirect effect through life attitude (Ind2) was significantly stronger than both the AIP-only pathway (Ind1)" and the chain pathway, highlighting life attitude as the most prominent mediator.

> "the indirect effect through life attitude (Ind2) was significantly stronger than both the AIP-only pathway (Ind1) (contrast = -0.115, 95% CI [−0.188, −0.032]) and the chain pathway (Ind3) (contrast = 0.108, 95% CI [0.039, 0.166])"
