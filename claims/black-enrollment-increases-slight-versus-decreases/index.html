Ed Trust analysis of IPEDS Fall 2000-2001 and Fall 2019-2021 enrollment data for 122 institutions. Of the 90 institutions with increases, 81 (90%) saw rises of less than 5 percentage points; "the average increase was 2.3 percentage points."

"Ninety colleges and universities (74%) increased their Black undergraduate student enrollment; but the average increase was 2.3 percentage points."

Same IPEDS-based analysis of the 32 declining institutions: 22 had decreases under 2 percentage points and four schools (13%) had decreases exceeding 10 percentage points. The report states the "average decrease was 3.7 percentage points."

"Among the 26.2% of institutions that had a decrease in Black undergraduate student enrollment, the average decrease was 3.7 percentage points, which is larger than the average percentage point increase."
