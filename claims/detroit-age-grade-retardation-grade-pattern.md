Conference report of an age-grade survey of Detroit schools in which children were classified by nationalities, grades, and schools. The printed pattern shows retardation peaking at fifth grade and acceleration rising to eleventh, with nationality differences as quoted. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Retardation rises to a peak at the fifth grade, falling to a minimum at the eighth. Acceleration increases gradually to the eleventh grade. On the whole, the greatest retardation is found among negroes and Italians; the greatest acceleration among United States whites, Scotch, and Russians."
