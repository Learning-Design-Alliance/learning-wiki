## Evidence

### Baron Tal 2020

Baron Tal, Grossman Robert I., Abramson Steven B., Pusic Martin V., Rivera Rafael, Triola Marc M., Yanai Itai. (2020). Signatures of medical student applicants and academic success. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0227108

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of the training group (90% of 946 records with 23 selected features) using a custom greedy feature-selection algorithm and K-means clustering; the number four was chosen where the "second differential is greatest" on the sum-of-squared-distances plot (S1 Fig).

> "Feature selection and clustering on our database of applicant data yielded four signatures with two main distinguishing attributes: the absolute level of the uGPA and its trajectory throughout the undergraduate studies of the student (Fig 2a)."
