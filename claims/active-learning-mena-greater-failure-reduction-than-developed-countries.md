## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The odds ratio of failure (average ORTA = 3.52) in this study is considerably larger than the ORTA = 2.07 reported for physics in the Freeman et al. meta-analysis, indicating a 36% greater failure reduction for MENA students under similar active-learning pedagogies. ## Evidence

### Ibrahim Alaa I. 2022

Ibrahim Alaa I., Sulaiman Nidhal, Ali Issam. (2022). Simultaneous multidimensional impacts of active learning revealed in a first implementation in the MENA region. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America 119(47), e2108666119. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2108666119

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-study comparison between this implementation and the Freeman et al. (2014) meta-analysis. The authors state their average ORTA of 3.52 "is considerably larger than" the 2.07 reported for physics, attributing this to disproportionate impact on low-performing students.

> "Our improvement for the odds ratios of failure (ORTA = 1.67 to 13.73 and its average of 3.52; that is, students being 3.52 times as likely to fail with T teaching) is considerably larger than that associated with the similar performance improvement in the ref. (22) meta-analysis, which reported an odds ratio of failure ORTA = 2.07 for physics"
