## Evidence

### Akinwumi 2017

Akinwumi, J. O. (2017). Effects of Gender and School Location on the Ekiti State Secondary Schools Students' Achievement in Reading Comprehension in English Language. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(5). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1133080

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A 2 x 3 ANCOVA of reading comprehension by school location and treatment in the 270-student quasi-experiment. The location-by-group interaction was significant ("F = 9.119, p < 0.05"), as were the location and treatment main effects.

> "Table 3 revealed that there was a significant difference in the achievement of students in rural and urban areas in reading comprehension between the experimental and control groups (F = 9.119, p < 0.05). The null hypothesis was rejected."
