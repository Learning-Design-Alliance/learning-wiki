## Evidence

### Hautz SC 2015

Hautz SC, Hautz WE, Keller N, Feufel MA, Spies C. (2015). The scholar role in the National Competence Based Catalogues of Learning Objectives for Undergraduate Medical Education (NKLM) compared to other international frameworks. GMS German Medical Science. [doi:10.3205/000224](https://doi.org/10.3205/000224)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Content comparison found clinical application "extensively required by all included international frameworks", whereas the NKLM covers it only partly; objectives on applying epidemiological data and diagnostic test concepts had "no real equivalence" in the NKLM's Scholar role.

> "The ability to clinically apply scientific results and methods is extensively required by all included international frameworks in great detail. The NKLM list comparable objectives only in part."
