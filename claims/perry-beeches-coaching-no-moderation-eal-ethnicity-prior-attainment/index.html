Evidence¶

Lord 2015¶

Lord, P., Bradshaw, S., Stevens, E. and Styles, B. (2015). Perry Beeches Coaching Programme: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581144

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-specified subgroup analyses in the intention-to-treat regression model. The report prints p = 0.23 for EAL, p = 0.62, p = 0.76 and p = 0.74 for Other, Black and Asian ethnicity, p = 0.59 for FSM Ever 6, and p = 0.62 for the pre-test score interaction; none were significant.

"The interaction between the intervention and EAL was not significant (p = 0.23). Ethnicity was considered as three dummy variables representing Other, Black and Asian, with White as the base category."
