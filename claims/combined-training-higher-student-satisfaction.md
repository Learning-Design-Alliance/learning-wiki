## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Satisfaction questionnaire scores (5-point Likert) were significantly higher for V-J and J-V groups on most items, with "Combine theory with practice" the highest-rated item in both combined groups. [→ Zhang Jie 2021](#zhang-jie-2021)

## Evidence

### Zhang Jie 2021

Zhang Jie, Xing Jiawei, Zheng Min, Sheng Jie, Zhang Kailiang, Zhang Baoping. (2021). Effectiveness of virtual simulation and jaw model for undergraduate periodontal teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-03064-1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-teaching Likert questionnaire (Table 2) in the randomized experiment; items such as course interest (J-V 4.67 ± 0.47) and combine theory with practice (V-J 4.87 ± 0.34) were marked significant versus the J group by one-way ANOVA. The lowest J-group item was "Acquisition of knowledge".

> "The scores on the Student’s Satisfaction Questionnaire showed that participants in Group V-J and Group J-V were highly satisfied with the training compared with subjects in the other groups"
