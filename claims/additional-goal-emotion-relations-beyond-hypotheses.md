Goetz, T., Sticca, F., Pekrun, R., Murayama, K., & Elliot, A. J. (2016). Intraindividual relations between achievement goals and discrete achievement emotions: An experience sampling approach. Learning and Instruction, 41, 115-125. https://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.10.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Additional associations from the same ESM multilevel models (1409 assessments, 120 students), reported in the intraindividual results section with printed unstandardized coefficients; the interindividual analysis found parallel additional relations including performance-avoidance goals predicting anger (b ¼ 0.22, p < .05).

> "mastery goals positively predicted pride ( b ¼ 0.15, p < .001). Further, performance-approach goals positively predicted enjoyment (b ¼ 0.13, p < .01) and shame ( b ¼ 0.07, p < .05)."
