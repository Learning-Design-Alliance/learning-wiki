## Evidence

### Gerd Kortemeyer 2023

Gerd Kortemeyer, Christine Kortemeyer, and Wolfgang Bauer. (2023). Taking introductory physics in studio, lecture, or online format: what difference does it make in subsequent courses, and for whom?. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey-correlational study of 104 valid responses from students in subsequent physics/engineering courses; the authors report no significant correlation between introductory course mode and Grade3rd, and state a multiple linear regression "also showed no significance of the type of course".

> "for neither the sample nor any of the subsamples, significant correlations ( p <0.05) could be found between the introductory course types ( Online, Lecture, or Studio) and the grade in the subsequent course (Grade3rd)"
