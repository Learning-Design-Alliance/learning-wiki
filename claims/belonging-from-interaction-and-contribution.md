## Subclaims
`q2 i?` SoB grew from two sources named by the authors: community and social interactions (feeling welcomed by the lab, an approachable mentor) and scientific contribution (producing results, asking questions, gaining physics knowledge). [→ Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2021](#dina-zohrabi-alaee-2021)

### Dina Zohrabi Alaee 2021

Dina Zohrabi Alaee, Micah K. Campbell, and Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2021). Impact of virtual REU experiences on students' psychosocial gains during the COVID-19 pandemic. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.07620)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' summary of the qualitative coding of mentee SoB quotes (N=46 coded segments) from the longitudinal remote REU interview study, naming the two sources of belonging growth with the article's own examples of "feeling welcomed by the research lab" and "having an approachable mentor".
