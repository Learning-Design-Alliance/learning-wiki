## Evidence

### Mouin Jammal 2025

Mouin Jammal, Antoine Saab, Cynthia Abi Khalil, Charbel Mourad, Rosy Tsopra, Melody Saikali, Jean-Baptiste Lamy. (2025). Impact on clinical guideline adherence of Orient-COVID, a CDSS based on dynamic medical decision trees for COVID19 management: a randomized simulation trial. Preprint. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1016/j.ijmedinf.2024.105772](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmedinf.2024.105772)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-arm parallel-group randomized controlled study with 30 medical students and residents (10 per group) managing six clinical cases. Group C mean was 17.02 versus about 15.4 in both other groups; Welch t-tests gave "p< 0.0003" for both comparisons with C, while A vs B showed "p=0.97".

> "Statistical tests show that there is no significant difference between group A and B, but a significant difference between groups A and C, and groups B and C, respectively (p< 0.0003)."
