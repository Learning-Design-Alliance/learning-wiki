# Team-based Kahoot® quizzes yield significantly higher quiz scores than individual play in histology teaching sessions

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students quizzed in team mode at the beginning of teaching sessions scored 69% versus 58% for individual players, and teams at the end scored 87% versus 82% for individuals (p < 0.05). [→ Felszeghy Szabolcs 2019](#felszeghy-szabolcs-2019)

## Evidence

### Felszeghy Szabolcs 2019

Felszeghy Szabolcs, Pasonen-Seppänen Sanna, Koskela Ali, Nieminen Petteri, Härkönen Kai, Paldanius Kaisa M. A., Gabbouj Sami, Ketola Kirsi, Hiltunen Mikko, Lundin Mikael, Haapaniemi Tommi, Sointu Erkko, Bauman Eric B., Gilbert Gregory E., Morton David, Mahonen Anitta. (2019). Using online game-based platforms to improve student performance and engagement in histology teaching. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1701-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study randomly dividing 215 first-year medical and dental students into five groups; one-way ANOVA with Tukey's HSD compared quiz scores. Team mode outperformed individual mode at both timing points, "team mode (G2 69%) vs. individual player mode (G1 58%; p < 0.05)".

> "Significantly higher percentages of correct test scores were obtained when students completed the tests in either the beginning of the histology teaching sessions in team mode (G2 69%) vs. individual player mode (G1 58%; p < 0.05) or at the end as a team (G4: 87%) vs. individual player mode (G3 82%; p < 0.05)."
