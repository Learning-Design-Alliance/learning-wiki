## Evidence

### Shikino Kiyoshi 2021

Shikino Kiyoshi, Rosu Claudia A., Yokokawa Daiki, Suzuki Shingo, Hirota Yusuke, Nishiya Katsumi, Ikusaka Masatomi. (2021). Flexible e-learning video approach to improve fundus examination skills for medical students: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02857-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-administered five-point Likert questionnaire administered before and after training in the randomized trial. Confidence rose in both groups, but the "post-post comparisons across the two groups revealed a significant difference (intervention group 3.8 vs. control group 2.8, p < 0.001)."

> "The confidence score increased from 2.3 ± 1.3 pre-tests to 3.8 ± 0.9 post-test in the flexible e-learning video group, whereas the score increased from 2.0 ± 0.8 pre-tests to 2.8 ± 1.0 post-test in the F2F lecture group."
