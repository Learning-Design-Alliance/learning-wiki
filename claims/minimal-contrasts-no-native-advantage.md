# On specific minimal phonetic contrasts, models show no significant exposed-vs-foreign difference regardless of the contrast's distribution in the training language

> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · design `r2` · `q2`
> **Currency** · newest study 2024 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For contrasts present in only one language (Mandarin /ɕ/–/tɕʰ/: p = 0.22; English tense-lax vowels: p = 0.619), exposed and foreign models performed equivalently. [→ Frank Lihui Tan & Youngah Do 2024](#frank-lihui-tan-youngah-do-2024)
`q2 i?` For contrasts differing in prevalence between languages (voicing: English 30.27% vs Mandarin 7.45% of consonant tokens, p = 0.695; fricative-affricate: Mandarin 29.37% vs English 2.03%, no significant differences), training language did not predict performance. `q2 i?` For place-of-articulation contrasts equally common in both languages, no significant exposed-vs-foreign difference was found for plosives or fricatives. [→ Frank Lihui Tan & Youngah Do 2024 (3)](#frank-lihui-tan-youngah-do-2024-3)

## Evidence

### Frank Lihui Tan & Youngah Do 2024

Frank Lihui Tan & Youngah Do. (2024). The formation of perceptual space in early phonetic acquisition: a cross-linguistic modeling approach. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.18501](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.18501)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ABX test on the Mandarin-only alveolo-palatal affricate-fricative contrast: exposed mean 0.491 vs foreign 0.497, p = 0.22. The tense-lax English vowel contrast likewise showed no significant difference (p = 0.619).

> "The ABX test on the / ɕ/ – /tɕʰ/ contrast revealed no significant performance difference between models trained on Mandarin and those trained on English ( 𝜇𝐸𝑥𝑝𝑜𝑠𝑒𝑑 =  0.491, 𝜎𝐸𝑥𝑝𝑜𝑠𝑒𝑑 =  0.014; 𝜇𝐹𝑜𝑟𝑒𝑖𝑔𝑛 = 0.497, 𝜎𝐹𝑜𝑟𝑒𝑖𝑔𝑛 =  0.007;  𝑝 =  0.22)."

### Frank Lihui Tan & Youngah Do 2024 (2)

Frank Lihui Tan & Youngah Do. (2024). The formation of perceptual space in early phonetic acquisition: a cross-linguistic modeling approach. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.18501](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.18501)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

ABX test on voicing contrast, which is far more prevalent in English (30.27%) than Mandarin (7.45%) tokens: exposed 0.367 vs foreign 0.375, p = 0.695. Fricative-affricate contrasts, more prevalent in Mandarin, also showed no significant training-language differences.

> "Despite its higher proportions in English than in Mandarin, the results  indicated no significant differences in the learning on voicing contrast (𝜇𝐸𝑥𝑝𝑜𝑠𝑒𝑑 =  0.367, 𝜎𝐸𝑥𝑝𝑜𝑠𝑒𝑑 =  0.062; 𝜇𝐹𝑜𝑟𝑒𝑖𝑔𝑛 = 0.375, 𝜎𝐹𝑜𝑟𝑒𝑖𝑔𝑛 =  0.077;  𝑝 =  0.695)"

### Frank Lihui Tan & Youngah Do 2024 (3)

Frank Lihui Tan & Youngah Do. (2024). The formation of perceptual space in early phonetic acquisition: a cross-linguistic modeling approach. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.18501](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.18501)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

ABX tests on place-of-articulation contrasts among plosives and fricatives, which are equally common in both languages; no significant exposed-vs-foreign differences (plosives EE vs ME p = 0.169, MM vs EM p = 0.078; fricatives similarly non-significant).

> "the results showed no significant difference between exposed and foreign condition for both languages (plosives: 𝜇𝐸𝐸 =  0.445, 𝜎𝐸𝐸 =  0.029; 𝜇𝑀𝑀 =  0.473, 𝜎𝑀𝑀 = 0.024; 𝜇𝑀𝐸 =  0.455, 𝜎𝑀𝐸 =  0.03; 𝜇𝐸𝑀 =  0.462, 𝜎𝐸𝑀 = 0.029;  𝐸𝐸 𝑣𝑠 𝑀𝐸, 𝑝 = 0.169;  𝑀𝑀 𝑣𝑠 𝐸𝑀, 𝑝 =  0.078;"
