## Evidence

### Nathan T. Moore and Carl D. Ferkinhoff 2017

Nathan T. Moore and Carl D. Ferkinhoff. (2017). Inexpensive Student-fabricated Solar Panels and Some Related Classroom Measurements. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1712.04029](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1712.04029)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Measurement of open-circuit voltage for N = 46 student-constructed panels under mixed outdoor sunlight, binned into a histogram (Fig. 5). Clusters at V_OC ≈ 0.6 V and 1.2 V occurred because, in almost all cases, students "accidentally bridged the tabbing wire between the top and bottom of successive cells while soldering."

> "A histogram of student measurements, figure 5, shows a primary voltage peak at about V OC ≈ 1.7 V, which is what you might expect from a 3 cell panel of 0 .55 V per cell."
