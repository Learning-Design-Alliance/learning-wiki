Evidence¶

Ho 2020¶

Ho, P. V. P., Phung, L. T. K., Oanh, T. T. T., & Giao, N. Q. (2020). Should Peer E-Comments Replace Traditional Peer Comments? International Journal of Instruction, 13(1), 295-314. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2020.13120a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Paired-sample t-test on the control group (n=40) in a 15-week quasi-experiment at Ho Chi Minh City University of Science; the mean rose from 5.715 to 6.495 with "p= .000", so the article concludes paper-and-pen peer comment activities affect writing quality.

"The average pre -test score (M=5.715, SD=.878) is lower than the average post -test score (M=6.495; SD= .955), and the Sig. for the comparison of the average scores of the pre -test and post -test is p= .000."
