# Low-performing students tended to procrastinate more on graded problem-solving exercises and on video watching with formative MCQs

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The midterm subtracted network showed low performers had stronger catching-up connections to summative exercises and to videos with formative MCQs, suggesting more procrastination. [→ Raković 2023](#rakovic-2023)

## Evidence

### Raković 2023

Raković, M., Ahmad Uzir, N., Matcha, W., Eagan, B., Jovanović, J., Shaffer, D. W., Pardo, A., & Gašević, D. (2023). Network Analytics to Unveil Links of Learning Strategies, Time Management, and Academic Performance in a Flipped Classroom. Journal of Learning Analytics, 10(3), 64–86. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2023.7843

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subtracted epistemic network (Figure 8) comparing midterm high- and low-performing groups. High performers had stronger connections in almost every activity except M catching.up–S summative assess and M catching.up–S videos and form assess, from which the authors draw the procrastination interpretation.

> "the results suggest that the students with low performance tended to procrastinate more on problem solving and exercises that counted toward the final course mark. They also procrastinated more in watching videos and doing the associated formative MCQs."
