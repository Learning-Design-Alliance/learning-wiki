Related Claims¶

  • In the classifier's expanded sample of over 2.6 million observations, negative student emotional state (r = −0.431) and physical prompt communication (r = 0.341) show the strongest correlations with full student response. — related
  • Teachers' prompt-hierarchy hypothesis is not supported: after unsuccessful communication, physical prompts are not the most common subsequent choice (probability 0.310). — related
  • Performing two teacher communications rather than one is more effective in producing full student responses (60.01% vs 50.58%). — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Sequences beginning with visual prompts produce higher full-response rates across subsequent time steps, while sequences beginning with physical prompts end at the lowest rates. — related
