### Saqr and López-Pernas 2022

Mohammed Saqr, Sonsoles López-Pernas. (2022). The Curious Case of Centrality Measures: A Large-Scale Empirical Investigation. Journal of Learning Analytics. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7415

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis found closeness centrality at "r= 0.49 [CI 0.45:0.55]" but with a negative predictive interval lower bound and I²=79%. The authors conclude "this measure is far from reliable as an indicator of student success."

> "Although the combined correlation coefficient of closeness centrality (r= 0.49 [CI 0.45:0.55]) was moderate, the lower bound of the predictive interval was negative [-0.06;0.81] and there was a high level of heterogeneity (I²=79%)."
