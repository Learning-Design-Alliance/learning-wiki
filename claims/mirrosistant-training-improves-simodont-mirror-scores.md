# Mirror training using Mirrosistant improves dental students' SIMODONT mirror-operation scores compared with control training

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After mirror training with Mirrosistant, the Experimental group scored statistically higher than the Control group on the 2nd SIMODONT mirror operation examination (80.42 ± 6.43 vs. 69.89 ± 15.98, P = 0.0005). [→ Chu Fengqing 2023](#chu-fengqing-2023)

## Evidence

### Chu Fengqing 2023

Chu Fengqing, Zheng Jue, Wang Qirui, Lu Xiaoqing, Chen Yue, Zhong Yi, Li Yingyi, Shi Jiali, Jiang Yue, Zhang Wei, Liu Laikui, Sun Wen. (2023). Mirror training device improves dental students' performance on virtual simulation dental training system. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04300-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized two-group study of 72 second-year dental students (36 per group) at Nanjing Medical University; the Mann–Whitney test compared 2nd-examination SIMODONT scores, showing the Exp group "statistically higher than that of the Ctrl group". No effect size was printed.

> "the 2nd operational score of the Exp group (80.42 ± 6.43) was statistically higher than that of the Ctrl group (69.89 ± 15.98, P = 0.0005, Fig. 3D)"
