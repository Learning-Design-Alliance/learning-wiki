`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Box-trainer simulation with 11 surgeons comparing hands across all 25 model points: left-hand time-points spanned "0.28 to 0.63" versus "0.27 to 0.58" for the right hand, with left-hand access significantly slower (P=0.048).

> "The time-point ranges for the left and right hands were 0.28 to 0.63 and 0.27 to 0.58, respectively. Operating with the left hand required more time to access the points (P=0.048)."
