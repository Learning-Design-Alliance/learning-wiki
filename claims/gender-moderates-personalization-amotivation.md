`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory bootstrap CI analysis (90% confidence level) comparing subgroups on the SIMS constructs. The authors interpret non-overlapping CIs as evidence of a moderation, reporting "suggesting gender moderated personalization's effect, which was only positive for females"; no p-values or effect sizes were computed, per the article's open-science approach.

> "Females’ CIs do not overlap when comparing the amotivation of the personalized [1.31;1.78] and the OSFA [1.95;2.70] conditions."
