## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Eva Erdosne Toth 2000

Eva Erdosne Toth, David Klahr, and Zhe Chen. (2000). Bridging Research and Practice: A Cognitively Based Classroom Intervention for Teaching Experimentation Skills to Elementary School Children. Cognition and Instruction, 18(4), 423–459. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci1804_1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Strategy-fit analysis of the experiment evaluation test: each student's 10 answers were matched to hypothesized strategies (correct CVS, vary focal ignore others, vary any one control others, vary at least one ignore others, other). Correct-strategy use rose from 27% to 77%; distribution difference χ2(1, N = 77) = 32.6, p = .0001.

> "prior to instruction, 27% of the students applied the correct, CVS strategy to evaluate experiments conducted by others, whereas after instruction, 77% of the students used this strategy."
