## Subclaims
`q3 i3` TPJ representation during lecturing positively correlated with subsequent MFC construction during HL recalling (r = 0.465). [→ X. Feng 2025](#x-feng-2025)
`q3 i3` In LH, MFC representation during lecturing correlated with understanding-level learning outcomes (r = 0.374), and MFC pISC negatively correlated with behavioral pISC of recalling (r = −0.383). [→ X. Feng 2025 (2)](#x-feng-2025-2)

## Evidence

### X. Feng 2025

X. Feng, X. Xu, Z. Meng, J. Jiang, M. Pei, Y. Zheng, C. Lu. (2025). A Rapid Cortical Learning Process Supporting Students’ Knowledge Construction During Real Classroom Teaching. Advanced Science. https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202416610

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Partial Pearson correlation analysis across students in the HL condition. The article reports "r = 0.465, p < 0.001", indicating the better the brain representation of the original knowledge structure during lecturing, the better the subsequent knowledge construction during recalling.

> "We also calculated the correlation between the representation of knowledge in the TPJ during the lecturing phase and that of the MFC during the recalling phase immediately after lecturing in the HL condition, and obtained a significantly positive correlation (r = 0.465, p < 0.001)."

### X. Feng 2025 (2)

X. Feng, X. Xu, Z. Meng, J. Jiang, M. Pei, Y. Zheng, C. Lu. (2025). A Rapid Cortical Learning Process Supporting Students’ Knowledge Construction During Real Classroom Teaching. Advanced Science. https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202416610

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Partial Pearson correlations (one-tailed, p < 0.05) between brain representation strength and Bloom's-Taxonomy outcome levels, controlling age and SES. The article reports "r = 0.374, p = 0.008" for MFC-lecturing with understanding level, and r = −0.383, p = 0.043 for MFC pISC with behavioral pISC.

> "the results showed a significant correlation between the strength of the MFC's representation during the lecturing phase (r = 0.374, p = 0.008) with the learning outcome at the understanding level"
