# Corpus-based lexical instruction using the Wikipedia Corpus produced significantly higher lexical gains than conventional instruction for medium-level (Level II) learners.

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Level II learners in the experimental group using the Wikipedia Corpus achieved significantly greater lexical gains than Level II learners in the control group. [→ Shi 2015](#shi-2015)

## Evidence

### Shi 2015

Jing Shi. (2015). An Analysis of the Application of Wikipedia Corpus on the Lexical Learning in the Second Language Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 8(8). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n8p171

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A quasi-experiment with 48 first-year English majors assigned to experimental (corpus-based) and control (conventional) groups over one semester. The author reports that for Level II learners, "learners in Level II in the experimental group did better than those in the control group, and the difference is statistically significant." No effect size is printed.

> "Second, learners in Level II in the experimental group did better than those in the control group, and the difference is statistically significant. Medium-level learners welcome the innovative method and they find it easy to adapt to the new method."
