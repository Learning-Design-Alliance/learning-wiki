`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Conceptual assessment (CURrENT Q4(b)) administered to the St Andrews E&M class during the last week of the course, involving a steady current in a section of wire of gradually decreasing diameter. The article reports "only 38% responded correctly that ∇⋅J=0 inside the wire".

> "Out of 63 students, only 38% responded correctly that ∇⋅J=0 inside the wire, of which 88% provided correct reasoning."
