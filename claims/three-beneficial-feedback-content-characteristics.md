# Three content characteristics of beneficial feedback — dysfunctional routines, underlying lack of confidence, and overlooked problems — distinguished unreservedly positive perceptions

## Evidence

### Somm Irene 2024

Somm Irene, Hajart Marco, Fehr Folkert, Weiß-Becker Christoph. (2024). Perceptions of supervision and feedback in PaedCompenda, the competency-based, post-graduate curriculum in pediatrics (www.paedcompenda.de). GMS Journal for Medical Education, 41(5). https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001710.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Reconstructive grounded-theory analysis of trainee interviews at PaedCompenda practices. Trainees described unnoticed set phrases, imprecisions, and unclear patient explanations; one trainee judged supervision of patient-doctor interactions "one of the best parts" of practice training.

> "Feedback addressing the suboptimal routines acquired and unquestioningly reproduced by physician trainees in the first few years of their professional practice is described as very helpful:"

### Somm Irene 2024 (2)

Somm Irene, Hajart Marco, Fehr Folkert, Weiß-Becker Christoph. (2024). Perceptions of supervision and feedback in PaedCompenda, the competency-based, post-graduate curriculum in pediatrics (www.paedcompenda.de). GMS Journal for Medical Education, 41(5). https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001710.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview-based reconstruction of positive perceptions. Feedback on underlying, often unconscious insecurities — in structuring consultations, clinical reasoning, and conversations with patients — was viewed as extremely beneficial and could mirror back previously unrecognized competencies.

> "Physician trainees view it as extremely beneficial when these underlying issues are focused on in the feedback from observing their work and discussed with the aim of finding solutions."

### Somm Irene 2024 (3)

Somm Irene, Hajart Marco, Fehr Folkert, Weiß-Becker Christoph. (2024). Perceptions of supervision and feedback in PaedCompenda, the competency-based, post-graduate curriculum in pediatrics (www.paedcompenda.de). GMS Journal for Medical Education, 41(5). https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001710.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Grounded-theory analysis of interviews and videotaped early detection exams. A trainee whose attention had been drawn to a routine parent–child handling situation he had seen but not registered described supervision as making "total sense, 100 percent" after video-based feedback conferences.

> "Being supervised makes total sense, 100 percent. As stupid as it is, but again, afterwards, those two videos: It happens a lot, when I am alone in a consultation, screening a patient, I am reminded of things....I find that super important."
