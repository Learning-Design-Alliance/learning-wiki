## Evidence

### Borges 2024

Borges, et al. (2024). Could ChatGPT get an Engineering Degree? Evaluating Higher Education Vulnerability to AI Assistants. [doi:10.1073/pnas.2414955121](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2414955121)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of student performance statistics and GPT-4 majority-vote grades on a subset of 197 questions (Figure 5). The article reports the model "typically struggles" on problems requiring "mathematical derivations and extensive computations" where students score 0.5 to 0.9. No effect size is printed.

> "we observe that for a subset of problems, the model typically struggles, receiving “Mostly Incorrect” or “Incorrect” marks, while students demonstrate relatively strong performance, with scores ranging from 0.5 to 0.9. These problems typically require mathematical derivations and extensive computations."
