## Evidence

### Menezes 2017

Menezes, D. P., Buss, K., Silvano, C. A., D'Ávila, B. N., & Anteneodo, C. (2017). Physics courses of the Federal University of Santa Catarina: evasion and gender. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.09165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.09165)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The abstract states evasion is "always slightly higher for women". The body reports success rates: licenciatura 17,7% (women) vs 19% (men); bacharelado 13,4% vs 16,8%; master's 71,4% vs 85%; doctorate 43,8% vs 55,3%.

> "the evasion being lower in the postgraduate courses and always slightly higher for women"
