Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever 2026¶

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever (2026). Understanding ethical dimensions of AI in higher education: insights from faculty members and students. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00601-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Authors' integration of interview findings across the two stakeholder groups. Faculty interviews yielded sub-themes of ethical inadequacy and unquestioning use among students, while students expressed acceptance of ethical responsibility; the article presents these as contradictory perspectives.

"the "ethical inadequacy" and "tendency to choose the easy way out" expressed by faculty members regarding students' approaches contradict the "acceptance of ethical respon -sibility" expressed by students regarding their own approaches"

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever 2026 (2)¶

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever (2026). Understanding ethical dimensions of AI in higher education: insights from faculty members and students. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00601-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Authors' interpretation of the interview data: both faculty and students acknowledged risks yet pragmatically embraced AI. The article links this attitude to differing user profiles in prior literature and its "potential to normalize unethical behavior".

"In addition to this contradiction, the fact that both groups embrace AI with a pragmatic "conscious acceptance" while being aware of the risks further complicates the situation."
