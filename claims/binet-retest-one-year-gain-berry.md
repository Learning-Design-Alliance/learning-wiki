`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Summary of Berry's retest study of "82 children" with the 1911 Binet revision after one year: normal children gained on average one year in rank (deviation .26), and among the 40 mental defectives the average gain was .5 of a year with a deviation of .31. No effect size is printed.

> "On retesting 82 children by the Binet tests (1911 revision) after an interval of one year, Berry concludes there is an average gain in rank of one year, with an average deviation of .26 of a year for normal children, with a gain of .96 of a year for those who tested below normal age, 1.02 for those at age and 1.17 years for those above age."
