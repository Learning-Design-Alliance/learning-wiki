## Evidence

### Luc T. Paoli 2024

Luc T. Paoli, Pavan K. Inguva, Andrew J. Haslam, and Pierre J. Walker. (2024). Confronting the thermodynamics knowledge gap: A short course on computational thermodynamics in Julia. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1016/j.ece.2024.03.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ece.2024.03.002)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-course survey ratings of Julia programming aspects (figure 10) from the 16-participant pilot. The authors attribute this to the similarity of Julia syntax to MATLAB and to the simplicity of the Julia package manager and Pluto; no effect size was reported.

> "In contrast to our previous experience with Python, students mostly did not find either setting up the environment or learning the syntax of Julia to be a significant activation barrier (see figure 9)."
