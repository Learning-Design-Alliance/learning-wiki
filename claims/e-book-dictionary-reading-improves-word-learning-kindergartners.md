### Korat et al. 2017

Ofra Korat, Olla Kozlov-Peretz, Ora Segal-Drori. (2017). Repeated E-book Reading and Its Contribution to Learning New Words among Kindergartners. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 7. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i7.2498

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized two-group intervention: 78 LSES kindergartners divided into experimental (n=40) and control (n=38); three two-way ANCOVA analyses compared groups over pretest, posttest 1, and posttest 2. The article reports "favoring the experimental group" on all three measures.

> "significant differences were found  between the pre- and post-intervention scores in all three measures (receptive word l earning, explanation and produc tion of words) between the experimental and the control groups, favoring the experimental group."
