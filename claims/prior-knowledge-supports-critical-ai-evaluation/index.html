q2 · i3 · review · r3

The review reports this quantitative descriptive study of 102 university physics students in Section 3.4; Table 5 lists the reported effect size "η2 = 0.26–0.57" for knowledge-related differences in critical evaluation.

"Dahlkemper et al. (2023) found that students did not consistently detect scientific errors in ChatGPT-generated responses, particularly when the responses were linguistically convincing. Students with stronger prior knowledge were more successful in distinguishing problematic AI-generated responses from correct human-prepared answers."
