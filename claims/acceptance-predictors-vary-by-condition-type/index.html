Evidence¶

Swords 2011¶

Swords, L, Heary, C & Hennessy, E 2011, 'Factors associated with acceptance of peers with mental health problems in childhood and adolescence', Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry and Allied Disciplines, vol. 52, no. 9, pp. 933-941. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7610.2010.02351.x

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Conclusions drawn from models fitted to vignette responses of 595 participants in five age groups. The article states the relationships "depend on the type of mental health problem under consideration"; no effect sizes are printed in the supplied text.

"The findings of the study suggest that the relationships between socio-demographic and attributional variables and acceptance of peers with mental health problems depend on the type of mental health problem under consideration."
