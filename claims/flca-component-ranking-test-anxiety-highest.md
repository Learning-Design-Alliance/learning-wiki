### Tanielian 2014

Tanielian, A. R. (2014). Foreign language anxiety in a new English program in Thailand. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1035913

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics across all 424 participants reporting mean scores for the three FLCAS components: test anxiety, communication apprehension, and fear of negative evaluation.

> "Test anxiety ranked the highest among the three components of FLCA, with average for the whole group of 45.84. Communication apprehension followed at an average of 36.40. Fears of negative evaluation were lowest, with a mean score of 23.47 (n = 424)."
