## Evidence

### Kyabayinze Daniel J 2012

Kyabayinze Daniel J, Asiimwe Caroline, Nakanjako Damalie, Nabakooza Jane, Bajabaite Moses, Strachan Clare, Tibenderana James K, Van Geetruyden Jean Pierre. (2012). Programme level implementation of malaria rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs) use: outcomes and cost of training health workers at lower level health care facilities in Uganda. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2458-12-291

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional observational evaluation of 64 trained health workers at their facilities six weeks after a one-day RDT training in eastern Uganda; "51/64 (80%) of all health workers accurately performed the steps critical for performing a RDT". No effect size is printed.

> "During the evaluation, 51/64 (80%) of all health workers accurately performed the steps critical for performing a RDT, and interpreted and recorded the results correctly in the books and registers"
