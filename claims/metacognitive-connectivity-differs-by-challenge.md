### Cindy Paans & Erdem Onan & Inge Molenaar & Ludo Verhoeven & Eliane Segers 2019

Cindy Paans & Erdem Onan & Inge Molenaar & Ludo Verhoeven & Eliane Segers. (2019). How social challenges affect children's regulation and assignment quality in hypermedia: a process mining study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09204-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fuzzy Miner process model of the 18 low-challenge dyads' coded interactions, built in ProM 6.7 with utility ratio .75, edge cut-off .20, node cut-off .25; average conformance 77.22% (SD = 6.76%).

> "This analysis*evaluation cluster has a bi-directional relation with monitoring. This shows there is a high interaction between the various metacognitive activities."

### Cindy Paans & Erdem Onan & Inge Molenaar & Ludo Verhoeven & Eliane Segers 2019 (2)

Cindy Paans & Erdem Onan & Inge Molenaar & Ludo Verhoeven & Eliane Segers. (2019). How social challenges affect children's regulation and assignment quality in hypermedia: a process mining study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09204-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Fuzzy Miner process model of the 12 high-challenge dyads; analysis and evaluation remained individual categories with evaluation leading to analysis, and monitoring isolated from them. Average conformance was 62.85% (SD = 12.33%).

> "Monitoring is not connected to the other metacognitive activities."
