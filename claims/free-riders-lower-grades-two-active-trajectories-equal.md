`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of final-exam grade point averages across the three LCS-derived trajectories; the article reports "The effect size (ε² = 0.30) indicates a large effect of the students' trajectory on their grades."

> "The Kruskal–Wallis test revealed a statistically significant difference among the three learning trajectory groups [H = 40.59, p < 0.0001, confidence interval (CI) (0.22–1)]."

### Ramy Elmoazen 2025 (2)

Ramy Elmoazen, Mohammed Saqr, Laura Hirsto, Matti Tedre. (2025). Capturing temporal pathways of collaborative roles: A multilayered analytical approach using community of inquiry. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09431-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post hoc Dunn's tests with Holm correction on the same trajectory–grade comparison. The article reports "there was no statistically significant difference between students' grades in active constructive and social interactive trajectories"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Dunn's pairwise comparisons showed that free riders have significantly lower mean grades (M = 62.9 ± 11.9) in comparison to active constructive (M = 77.4 ± 8.64) and social interactive (M = 78.2 ± 10.5) trajectories."
