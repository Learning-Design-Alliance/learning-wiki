### Denis Pasco 2025

Denis Pasco, Erik Gustafsson, Lucas Burget, Thibault Asselborn, Pierre Dillenbourg, Consortium Lamue, Marie Mazerolle. (2025). Rapid in-class assessment of handwriting using a digital tablet: Multidimensional analysis of gender and socioeconomic status. Computers & Education: X Reality, 232, 105311. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105311

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of Dynamilis total scores from 1361 second graders found a significant gender effect, with the article reporting "B = -4.693" indicating boys scored lower than girls.

> "A first analysis of total score performance (max. 100) revealed a significant effect of gender on handwriting performance (B = -4.693, CI 95 % [-6.797, -2.589], t(1355) =-4.371, p < .001) indicating that boys have on average a 4.693-point lower handwriting score compared to girls"
