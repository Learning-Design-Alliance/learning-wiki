Evidence¶

Olaniyan 2015¶

Olaniyan, A. O., & Omosewo, E. O. (2015). Effects of a Target-Task Problem-Solving Model on Senior Secondary School Students' Performance in Physics. Science Education International, 25(4), 522-538. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086539

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental, non-randomized pre-test/post-test control group study with 120 Senior Secondary II students (60 per school). ANCOVA of post-test PTCE scores gave Treatment F = 13.076, Sig. .012; mean gain was 15.67 for Target-Task versus 0.98 for lecture, described as "a positive effect for the experimental group".

"Table 2 shows the P value (.012) is less than the P alpha level of 0.050, (P < 0.050). This suggests that the Hypothesis HO1 needs to be rejected."
