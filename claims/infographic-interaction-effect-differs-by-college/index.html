Evidence¶

Alqudah 2019¶

Alqudah, D., Bidin, A., & Md Hussin, M. A. H. (2019). The Impact of Educational Infographic on Students’ Interaction and Perception in Jordanian Higher Education: Experimental Study. International Journal of Instruction, 12(4), 669-688. https://doi.org/10.29333/iji.2019.12443a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Kruskal-Wallis test comparing the two colleges' samples on the interaction variable; Table 8 reports chi-square 5.819, df 1, Asymp. Sig. .016. The null hypothesis of equal impact was rejected, indicating a difference between the scientific college sample (IT students) and the humanistic college sample (ECO students) in infographic impact on interaction.

"As regards the interaction test, the calculated H was 5.819. Consequently, it was more than the approximated chi -square value at 0.05 significance level and 1 degree of freedom, which is 3.841"
