## Evidence

### Karabay 2015

Aysegul Karabay. (2015). The guiding effects of a critical reading program on the use of external reading strategies when confronting an ironical text. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2419

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative content analysis of post-test responses to 'The Neutron Bomb Will Save Civilization'. The article reports 28 experimental versus 12 control teachers reached the deeper ironic meaning; these are descriptive frequencies with no test statistic.

> "Twenty-eight pre-service teachers in the experimental group and 12 pre-service teachers in the control group claimed that the author was against war and the damages of war and against the neutron bomb;"

### Karabay 2015 (2)

Aysegul Karabay. (2015). The guiding effects of a critical reading program on the use of external reading strategies when confronting an ironical text. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2015.2419

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same post-test content analysis: superficial (literal) readings were rare in the experimental group (2) and common in the control group (18). Frequencies are descriptive; no inferential test is reported for this contrast.

> "two pre-service teachers in the experimental group and 18 pre-service teachers in the control group believed that the author was for the war and the neutron bomb;"
