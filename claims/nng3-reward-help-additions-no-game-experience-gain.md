### Phuong Bui 2020

Understanding students' game experiences throughout the developmental process of the number navigation game. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09755-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

One-way ANOVA comparing fourth graders' GEQ ratings across NNG 1 (N = 63), NNG 2 (N = 72) and NNG 3 (N = 40); post-hoc Tukey HSD comparisons. The article reports "no significant differences, except in Flow dimension" between NNG 2 and NNG 3.
