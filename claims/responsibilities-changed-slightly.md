### Niederhauser A 2019

Niederhauser A, Züllig S, Marschall J, Schweiger A, John G, Kuster SP, Schwappach DLB. (2019). Change in staff perspectives on indwelling urinary catheter use after implementation of an intervention bundle in seven Swiss acute care hospitals: results of a before/after survey study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028740

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multiple-response question on responsibilities, reported in figure 1 and text. Nurses mainly felt responsible for placing, maintaining and removing catheters at both time points; physicians mainly for ordering placement and removal, with non-significant changes.

> "However, at T1, fewer nurses felt responsible for prescribing catheter placement (p<0.001) as compared with T0, and a higher percentage felt responsible for assessing the need for continued catheterisation (p=0.002)."
