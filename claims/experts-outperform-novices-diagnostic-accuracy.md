## Evidence

### Cortese 2026

Cortese, F., Hruska, P., McLaughlin, K. J., Coderre, S. P., Protzner, A. B., Krigolson, O. E., Hecker, K. G. (2026). Expertise Related Changes in Resting‐State Functional Connectivity Patterns Following a Clinical Reasoning and Decision‐Making Task. Brain and Behavior 16, no. 1: e71153. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.71153

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Two-way ANOVA of task accuracy from the clinical reasoning task (16 easy and 16 hard gastroenterology scenarios) showed experts at "77% mean ± 11%" versus novices at "62% ± 22%", F (1, 36) = 7.99, p < 0.008. No effect size is printed.

> "expert gastroenterologists (77% mean ± 11% standard deviation) correctly diagnosed more cases than second‐year medical students (62% ± 22%), F (1, 36) = 7.99, p < 0.008"
