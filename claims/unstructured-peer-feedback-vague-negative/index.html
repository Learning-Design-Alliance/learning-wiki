Evidence¶

Vani 2015¶

V. Vijaya Vani. (2015). The Impact of Peer Observation and Feedback on Technical Seminar Presentations: A Constructive Model. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 5, No. 3, July-September. https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/article/peer-observation

q2 · i? · design · r2

Analysis of the first spell, in which a group of 10 students observed presentations by 10 students and gave feedback without guidance. The author reports the feedback was vague, highlighted negatives, and generated confusion; the outcome was judged not useful to either group.

"It is obvious from the above “feedback” that the observers focused on elements of personal interest. It can be considered as vague and it highlighted the negative aspects of the presenters."
