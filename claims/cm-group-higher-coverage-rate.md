## Evidence

### Brahm Fleisch 2011

Brahm Fleisch, Nick Taylor, Roelien Herholdt and Ingrid Sapire. (2011). Evaluation of Back to Basics mathematics workbooks: a randomised control trial of the Primary Mathematics Research Project. South African Journal of Education, Vol 31:488-504. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1136612

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Coverage tracking in the 44-school RCT via three classroom visits to examine learner books. The authors hypothesise CM is more easily readable so classes progress faster, but say this requires case-study investigation.

> "The first point to note is that, whereas 607 (44%) learners in the CM group achieved 79% coverage or more, only 420 (37%) of the PM group achieved this."
