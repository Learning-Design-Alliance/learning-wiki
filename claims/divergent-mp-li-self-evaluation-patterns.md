### Seki Masayasu 2016

Seki Masayasu, Otaki Junji, Breugelmans Raoul, Komoda Takayuki, Nagata-Kobayashi Shizuko, Akaishi Yu, Hiramoto Jun, Ohno Iwao, Harada Yoshimi, Hirayama Yoji, Izumi Miki (2016). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of self-evaluation response distributions in the same study: "More participants in the SNAPPS group than in the OMP group chose to "strongly agree" to the statement, "It was easy to bring up MP,"" while combined agree responses favoured the OMP group.

> "More participants in the SNAPPS group than in the OMP group chose to "strongly agree" to the statement, "It was easy to bring up MP," but when the responses "strongly agree" and "agree" were combined, they were chosen by more participants in the OMP group."

### Seki Masayasu 2016 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of self-evaluation responses: "Most participants in the SNAPPS group strongly agreed with the statement, "It was easy to bring up LI,"" and none chose disagree, whereas a high proportion of the OMP group chose disagree.

> "Most participants in the SNAPPS group strongly agreed with the statement, "It was easy to bring up LI," and none chose the response "strongly disagree" or "disagree.""
