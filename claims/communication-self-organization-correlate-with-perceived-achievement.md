## Evidence

### P. Klein 2021

P. Klein, L. Ivanjek, M.N. Dahlkemper, K. Jeličić, M.-A. Geyer, S. Küchemann, and A. Susac. (2021). Studying physics during the COVID-19 pandemic: Student assessments of learning achievement, perceived effectiveness of online recitations, and online laboratories. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.010117)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Survey of N = 578 physics students at five universities in Germany, Austria, and Croatia; Pearson correlations among questionnaire scales with Bonferroni correction. The article reports that "self-organization skills (r = 0.63, p< 0.001) are positively correlated for perceived learning achievement", the strongest relationship in Table III.

> "We find that good communication abilities ( r = 0.50, p< 0.001) and self-organization skills (r = 0.63, p< 0.001) are positively correlated for perceived learning achievement."
