## Evidence

### Rosen Maya L. 2021

Rosen Maya L., Lurie Lucy A., Sambrook Kelly A., Meltzoff Andrew N., McLaughlin Katie A. (2021). Neural mechanisms underlying the income-achievement gap: The role of the ventral visual stream. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101025

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression in the 62-child sample, controlling for age, sex, and violence exposure, tested log income-to-needs against the HOME-based cognitive stimulation composite. The article reports "INR was positively associated with cognitive stimulation in the home (β = 0.433, p = .001".

> "Consistent with previous studies from which the present sample is a subsample, we found that INR was positively associated with cognitive stimulation in the home (β = 0.433, p = .001; Fig. 5A)."
