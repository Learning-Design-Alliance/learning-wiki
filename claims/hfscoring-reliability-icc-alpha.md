## Evidence

### Ntumi Simon 2025

Ntumi Simon. (2025). Validating high-fidelity simulation for assessing procedural skills in nursing education: a kane framework approach in Ghana. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07724-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Reliability analysis of simulation scoring reported in Results (Table 3), based on two independently rated assessors for each student. The article reports "The Inter-rater Reliability (ICC) ranged from 0.77 to 0.84", Cronbach's alpha of 0.83, split-half reliability of 0.79, and rater Cohen's kappa of 0.72.

> "The Inter-rater Reliability (ICC) ranged from 0.77 to 0.84, with a 95% confidence interval (CI) of [0.72, 0.89], and a p-value less than 0.001."
