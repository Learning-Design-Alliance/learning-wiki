`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Manual expert-instructor assessment of recorded AVT sessions in the CS1 RCT, checking whether novices made significant progress and whether the learner merely gave away the answer. The article also notes signs of some learners being more comfortable "telling" rather than "guiding".

> "In all but one session (98%), the experts agreed that learners helped the novices make progress and did so in an educationally helpful way. For the one session where this was not the case, the novice student was particularly unprepared and struggled with basic syntax."
