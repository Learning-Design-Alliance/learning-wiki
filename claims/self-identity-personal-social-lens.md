`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of coded open-ended explanations of the single-item physics identity measure found "62 of 120, 51.7%" of students used personal/social contexts; multiple identities was the most frequent code (28.3% of responses).

> "Just over half (62 of 120, 51.7%) of students explained their physics identities within personal and/or social contexts. They described their self-image, other identities, and perceived role in the physics community."
