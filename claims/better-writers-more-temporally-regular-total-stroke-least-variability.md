## Evidence

### West 1918

West, P. V. (1918). The Relation of Rhythm to the Handwriting Movement. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22The+Relation+of+Rhythm+to+the+Handwriting+Movement%22

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

First experiment using kymographic records via electric contacts, comparing adult and child groups of good and poor writers under finger and arm movement. The article notes "many cases of overlapping" and that better adult writers held "a slightly superior record of regularity."

> "Although there are many cases of overlapping the better adult writers maintain a slightly superior record of regularity. The rhythmic reaction to the total period as a unit is quite noticeable, for all groups here show less relative variation than in either the stroke or rest."

### West 1918 (2)

West, P. V. (1918). The Relation of Rhythm to the Handwriting Movement. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22The+Relation+of+Rhythm+to+the+Handwriting+Movement%22

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kymographic experiment with finger and arm movement, spontaneous and imposed rhythm conditions. Averages of variability from temporal uniformity were analyzed as percentage of variation within stroke, rest, and total stroke units.

> "The rhythmic reaction to the total period as a unit is quite noticeable, for all groups here show less relative variation than in either the stroke or rest."
