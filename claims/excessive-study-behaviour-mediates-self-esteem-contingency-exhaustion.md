## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Academic self-esteem contingency has a significant total effect on exhaustion (β = 0.41), partially mediated by excessive study behaviour (β = 0.27). [→ Brueckmann M 2025](#brueckmann-m-2025)

## Evidence

### Brueckmann M 2025

Brueckmann M, Wild E, Wolff F. (2025). Planting the seed: how parental negative conditional regard boosts vulnerability to excessive study behaviour and burnout. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03354-z

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional serial mediation SEM in a sample of 624 German university students, controlling for academic engagement and gender. The model showed "significantly mediated by excessive study behaviour (β = 0.27, 95% CI [0.17, 0.37])" for exhaustion, with a total effect of β = 0.41.

> "A significant total effect was observed for the relationship between academic self-esteem contingency and exhaustion during studies (β = 0.41, 95% CI [0.32, 0.50]), which proceeded directly (β = 0.15, 95% CI [0.02, 0.28]) and was significantly mediated by excessive study behaviour (β = 0.27, 95% CI [0.17, 0.37])."
