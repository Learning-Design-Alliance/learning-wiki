## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Group B measured the alpha-particle energy spectrum at the BPM with a silicon-based SSD, identifying peaks from 208-211Fr and daughter isotopes after beam irradiation, and extracted a summed lifetime of 210Fr and 211Fr by fitting an exponential decay. [→ K.S. Tanaka 2022](#ks-tanaka-2022)

## Evidence

### K.S. Tanaka 2022

K.S. Tanaka, K. Harada, T. Hayamizu, R. Kita, R. Kono, K. Maruta, H. Nagahama, N. Ozawa, Y. Sakemi, R. Sugimori. (2022). An accelerator experiment for junior and senior high school students to improve students' involvement in fundamental physics. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac510a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac510a)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Group B measurement during the workshops: after stopping the ion beam, the time structure of SSD counts corresponding to 210Fr or 211Fr was fitted with an exponential function (Fig. 11), giving "τ = 272± 13 s". No standardized effect size printed.

> "The estimated sum lifetime of 210Fr and 211Fr from the SSD counts wasτ = 272± 13 s."

### K.S. Tanaka 2022 (2)

K.S. Tanaka, K. Harada, T. Hayamizu, R. Kita, R. Kono, K. Maruta, H. Nagahama, N. Ozawa, Y. Sakemi, R. Sugimori. (2022). An accelerator experiment for junior and senior high school students to improve students' involvement in fundamental physics. Preprint submitted to Elsevier. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac510a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac510a)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Energy spectrum measured by Group B without and with beam (Fig. 13): peaks from the 241Am calibration source appear in both, and Fr isotope and daughter isotope peaks appear after beam irradiation, identified against NuDat2.

> "After the beam irradiation, they observed peaks from 208Fr, 209Fr and 210Fr, 211Fr and 211Rn,210Rn, which are daughter isotopes of Fr."
