Evidence¶

Gall 1994¶

Gall, Meredith D.; Vojtek, Roseanne O'Brien. (1994). Planning for Effective Staff Development: Six Research-Based Models. ERIC Clearinghouse on Educational Management. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED372464

q2 · i? · review · r2

The monograph reports that Gage and Needels (1989) reviewed experimental studies of staff development aimed directly at student achievement, with nine studies using achievement outcomes in reading and mathematics, comparing experimental and control teachers. It reports: "In all but one of the experiments, the staff development program led to changes in teachers' instruction." No effect sizes are printed.

"In all but one of the experiments, the staff development program led to changes in teachers' instruction, which in turn resulted in their students achieving at a higher level than students whose teachers were in the control group."
