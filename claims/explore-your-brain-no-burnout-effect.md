## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Contrary to hypothesis, the Time × Condition interaction for school burnout symptoms was not significant, and both groups' burnout increased over the year. [→ Janssen 2025](#janssen-2025)

## Evidence

### Janssen 2025

Janssen, T. W. P., & van Atteveldt, N. (2025). Explore your brain: A randomized controlled trial into the effectiveness of a growth mindset intervention with psychosocial and psychophysiological components. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 280–302. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12572

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the same linear mixed model analysis of the cluster RCT (Time × Condition: F(1, 413.52) = 1.02, p = .313). No effect size was printed for the null contrast; equivalence was not tested. "a 4.82 points increase from pre (T0) to 1‐year follow‐up (T2)" occurred in both conditions.

> "In contrast to our hypothesis, the growth mindset intervention did not affect school burnout symptoms from T0 to T2. Rather, both the control and growth mindset intervention groups reported an increase in school burnout symptoms, as indicated by the positive slope of Time, β = 4.82, 95% CI [3.63, 6.02]"
