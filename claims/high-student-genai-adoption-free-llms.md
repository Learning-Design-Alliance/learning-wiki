## Evidence

### Karen D. Wang 2024

Karen D. Wang, Zhangyang Wu, L'Nard Tufts II, Carl Wieman, Shima Salehi, Nick Haber. (2024). Scaffold or Crutch? Examining College Students' Use and Views of Generative AI Tools for STEM Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.02653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.02653)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Online survey of 40 US STEM undergraduates recruited via Prolific in May 2024. The article reports "Only 3 students (7.5%) reported that they had not used any genAI tools as of May 2024", and that the free version of ChatGPT was used by 37 students, with paid versions seeing significantly lower adoption.

> "Only 3 students (7.5%) reported that they had not used any genAI tools as of May 2024. Regarding the adoption timeline, 4 students (10%) began using such tools in 2022 or earlier, 29 (72.5%) in 2023, and 4 (10%) in 2024."
