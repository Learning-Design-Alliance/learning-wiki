Evidence¶

Carlos González 2023¶

Carlos González, Daniel Ponce, Violeta Fernández. (2023). Teachers' experiences of teaching online during COVID-19: implications for postpandemic professional development. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10200-9

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Results section on technology-supported professional development: the interview study reports that prepandemic teaching development was essential for facing the pandemic's challenges and that participants "felt better prepared to face pedagogical and technological challenges"; it also reports compulsory courses led to work overload, particularly in the second semester of 2020.

"Teachers who had participated in these activities felt better prepared to face pedagogical and technological challenges."
