# Doing the synthetic-bone exercise first significantly reduces subsequent VR module completion time for tibial nailing (P=.02) but not for femoral nailing

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After completing the synthetic-bone tibia exercise first, participants finished the VR module significantly faster (P=.02). [→ Sandipan Chatterjee 2026](#sandipan-chatterjee-2026)
`q3 i?` No significant difference in VR module completion time was found after prior synthetic-bone practice for femoral nailing in either cohort (P=.09 and P=.54). [→ Sandipan Chatterjee 2026 (2)](#sandipan-chatterjee-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Sandipan Chatterjee 2026

Sandipan Chatterjee, Khairul Faizi Mohammad, Monica Ghidinelli. (2026). Evaluating the Impact of Virtual Reality on Orthopedic Trauma Skills Acquisition Among Surgical Residents: Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Perioperative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2196/79343

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Crossover analysis of VR module completion times in cohort 1: mean 34.97 minutes with VR first versus 29.82 minutes after synthetic-bone practice, P=.02 (Table 2).

> "A significant decrease in time needed to finish the VR module was also noted when participants first completed the synthetic bone practical exercise (P=.02; Table 2)."

### Sandipan Chatterjee 2026 (2)

Sandipan Chatterjee, Khairul Faizi Mohammad, Monica Ghidinelli. (2026). Evaluating the Impact of Virtual Reality on Orthopedic Trauma Skills Acquisition Among Surgical Residents: Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Perioperative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2196/79343

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cohort 1 femoral nailing crossover: VR module completion time did not differ significantly after prior synthetic-bone practice (P=.09); cohort 2 likewise showed no significant difference (P=.54). Equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant difference in the VR module completion time was noted after participants performed the synthetic bone exercise (P=.09; Table 3)."
