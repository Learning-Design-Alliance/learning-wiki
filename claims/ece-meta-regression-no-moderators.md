### Pang Xuefei 2026

Pang Xuefei, Wang Huilin, Li Jinggui, Gui Haijun, Cao Xia, Cai Ming. (2026). The Impact of Early Clinical Exposure on Competency Development in Dental Students: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109483

`q4 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression across the 27 included studies testing seven predefined covariates. All covariates were non-significant; the authors state further high-quality studies are needed to identify heterogeneity origins.

> "The results indicated that study design (P = .584), different types of ECE (P = .274), discipline (P = .423), assessment method (P = .515), type of competency measured (P = .346), geographical region (P = .448), and student academic year (P = .255) were all not significant sources of heterogeneity"
