The article reports, citing Kosmyna et al. (2025), that independent first engagement before LLM consultation yielded "significantly stronger" outputs; the review attributes this finding to that study and draws no effect size from it.

"Critically, Kosmyna et al. (2025) found that when students first engaged with a task independently before consulting an LLM, their outputs were significantly stronger."
