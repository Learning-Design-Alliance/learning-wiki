## Evidence

### Asher Laura 2021

Asher Laura, Birhane Rahel, Teferra Solomon, Milkias Barkot, Worku Benyam, Habtamu Alehegn, Kohrt Brandon A., Hanlon Charlotte. (2021). “Like a doctor, like a brother”: Achieving competence amongst lay health workers delivering community-based rehabilitation for people with schizophrenia in Ethiopia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246158

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of FGDs and participant/caregiver in-depth interviews at pilot months 2 and 12. Workers "seemed to occupy dual roles of expert and close friend", converging with high 'Building trust' scores, though some participants felt lectured or nagged about medication.

> "CBR workers seemed to occupy dual roles of expert and close friend, succinctly captured by one caregiver: “[the CBR worker] advises and explains to me like a doctor, like a brother” (Caregiver linked to CBR worker 2; pilot month 2)."
