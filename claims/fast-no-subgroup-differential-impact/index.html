Bos 2018¶

Bos, J., Spier, E., Bandeira de Mello, V., González, R., & Huang, F. (2018). Investing in Innovation (i3) Validation Study of Families and Schools Together (FAST) Final Report. American Institutes for Research. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/families-and-schools-together-fast/

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the 60-school cluster RCT (1,396 students), subgroup and school-characteristic analyses found no differential impact: "did not find that FAST worked significantly better or worse for any subgroups of students or in schools with particular characteristics."
