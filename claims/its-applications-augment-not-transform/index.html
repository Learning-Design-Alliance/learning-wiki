Ogunsakin IB 2026¶

Ogunsakin IB, Ayanwale MA, Falebita OS and Felemu OJ (2026) Reimagining mathematics education through intelligent tutoring: evidence, opportunities, and implementation challenges. Front. Educ. 11:1943906. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1943906

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this second-hand from Son's (2024) synthesis, attributing the SAMR-based finding to that source. It supports the review's conclusion that an ITS may automate conventional exercises without substantially improving mathematical reasoning or conceptual learning.

"Son’s (2024) synthesis using the SAMR framework indicated that many ITS applications augmented existing practices by providing automated feedback and individualised assistance. Fewer applications transformed instruction by enabling learning activities that would otherwise have been difficult to implement."
