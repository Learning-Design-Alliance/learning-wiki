## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Geller 2022

Benjamin D. Geller, Jack Rubien, Sara M. Hiebert, and Catherine H. Crouch. (2022). The impact of introductory physics for the life sciences in a senior biology capstone course. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Because all capstone students had taken General Chemistry and two Math or Statistics courses, the authors compared diffusion-task performance between students who had or had not taken at least one computer science course, hypothesizing CS skills would not map onto the task; they found "no significant difference between the CS and non-CS groups". No effect size was printed.

> "Indeed, we found no significant difference between the CS and non-CS groups on quantitative skills elements of the code (Wilcoxon p = 0.62)."
