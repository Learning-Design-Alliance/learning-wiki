## Evidence

### Al Essa A 2026

Al Essa A, Schmidt HG, Mamede S, Knežević K, Zwaan L, Magzoub M. (2026). Premature closure underlies bias in medical diagnosis in students: A randomised controlled experiment. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70229

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same 58-student vignette experiment; expert judges classified incorrect diagnoses as SDF-associated or not (ICC = 0.91). The article reports "SDF‐related errors increased by around 60% when SDFs were present in the case".

> "The effect of the presence of the SDF on SDF‐related mistakes was significant, F2,693 = 16.395, p < 0.001. Pairwise contrasts showed the mean proportion of mistakes under the No‐SDF condition to be significantly different from the other two conditions."
