## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students find that the electrical response of the lipid bilayer matches the model RC circuit, with no difference between the two, supporting the leaky-capacitor model of the passive membrane. [→ Vadim Shlyonsky 2014](#vadim-shlyonsky-2014)

## Evidence

### Vadim Shlyonsky 2014

Vadim Shlyonsky, Freddy Dupuis & David Gall. (2014). The OpenPicoAmp: an open-source planar lipid bilayer amplifier for hands-on learning of neuroscience. arXiv. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0108097](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0108097)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instructional measurement activity in which students compare the model circuit (1 GΩ, 220 pF) with a formed bilayer under triangular and constant voltage. The article reports typical channel-free bilayer resistance "in the 100 GΩ range" with capacitance in the range of hundreds of pF, and that students can estimate bilayer resistivity around 10^3 GΩ·cm, demonstrating lipids act as insulators.

> "This experiment allows the students to understand that the electrical response of a passive cell membrane is well described by an equivalent RC circuit. There is no diﬀerence in the electrical response between the model circuit and the lipid bilayer."
