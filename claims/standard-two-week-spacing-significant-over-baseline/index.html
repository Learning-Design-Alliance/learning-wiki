Evidence¶

John Duplice 2022¶

John Duplice. (2022). The GoldList Notebook Method: A Study on L2 Vocabulary Learning. IAFOR Journal of Education: Language Learning in Education, Volume 10 – Issue 1. https://iafor.org/journal/iafor-journal-of-education/ doi:10.22492/ije.10.1.05

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Generalized linear model with fixed effects in R 4.0.3, pre-test as baseline score and Baseline treatment as baseline condition, on idiom-matching correctness for 74 participants. Only the Standard treatment reached significance (t = 2.457, p = .0143); no effect size was printed.

"Comparing other treatments to the Baseline, the model showed significant interaction of the Standard treatment (t = 2.457, p = .0143)."
