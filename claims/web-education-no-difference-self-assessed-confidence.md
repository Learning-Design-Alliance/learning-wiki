## Evidence

### Bylow Helene 2019

Bylow Helene, Karlsson Thomas, Lepp Margret, Claesson Andreas, Lindqvist Jonny, Herlitz Johan. (2019). Effectiveness of web-based education in addition to basic life support learning activities: A cluster randomised controlled trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0219341

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Null secondary outcome from the questionnaire in the cluster randomised controlled trial of laymen, assessed at post-test and retention test. The Results section reports the groups "did not differ" on self-assessed knowledge, confidence and willingness; no effect size is printed, so the study did not detect an effect rather than measuring a small one.

> "Self-assessed theoretical knowledge of CPR, AED and confidence and willingness to act in an OHCA situation did not differ between the groups directly after training or six months later."
