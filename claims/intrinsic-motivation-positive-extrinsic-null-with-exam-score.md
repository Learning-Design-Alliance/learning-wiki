## Evidence

### Muntean Lorena Mihaela 2022

Muntean Lorena Mihaela, Nireștean Aurel, Sima-Comaniciu Andreea, Mărușteri Marius, Zăgan Cătălin Andrei, Lukacs Emese. (2022). The Relationship between Personality, Motivation and Academic Performance at Medical Students from Romania. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9332223/

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis in the 179-student pilot study relating Motivational Involvement Scale scores to exam performance. The article reports "a statistically significant positive correlation was found between the intrinsic motivation and the score (r = 0.19, p = 0.0079)" and separately a positive correlation with conscientiousness (r = 0.16, p = 0.0249).

> "a statistically significant positive correlation was found between the intrinsic motivation and the score (r = 0.19, p = 0.0079)"

### Muntean Lorena Mihaela 2022 (2)

Muntean Lorena Mihaela, Nireștean Aurel, Sima-Comaniciu Andreea, Mărușteri Marius, Zăgan Cătălin Andrei, Lukacs Emese. (2022). The Relationship between Personality, Motivation and Academic Performance at Medical Students from Romania. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9332223/

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis in the same 179-student pilot study relating motivational involvement dimensions to the conscientiousness dimension. The article reports "between extrinsic motivation and conscientiousness (r = −0.211, p = 0.0046), we found a statistically significant negative correlation"; the score correlation for extrinsic motivation was not significant (r = −0.10, p = 0.178).

> "between extrinsic motivation and conscientiousness (r = −0.211, p = 0.0046), we found a statistically significant negative correlation and between intrinsic motivation and conscientiousness (r = 0.16, p = 0.0249), a statistically significant positive correlation"
