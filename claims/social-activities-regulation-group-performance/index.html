Evidence¶

Nadira Saab 2012¶

Nadira Saab. (2012). Team regulation, regulation of social activities or co-regulation: Different labels for effective regulation of learning in CSCL. Metacognition Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9085-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial reports, attributing to Janssen et al., that "regulation of social activities positively affected group performance" in studies of online collaborative learning on a history task. The editorial prints no effect sizes or statistics for this finding.

"Janssen et al. found that regulation of social activities positively affected group performance."
