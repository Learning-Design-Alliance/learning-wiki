# Accommodator learners gained significantly more in the VR (guided exploration) mode than in VR (non-guided exploration) or Non VR modes

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` After adjusting for pre-test scores, learning mode had a medium main effect on accommodator learners' gain scores (η2 = 0.134), with guided VR highest. [→ Chwen Jen Chen 2005](#chwen-jen-chen-2005)

## Evidence

### Chwen Jen Chen 2005

Chwen Jen Chen, Seong Chong Toh, & Wan Mohd Fauzy Wan Ismail. (2005). Are Learning Styles Relevant To Virtual Reality? Journal of Research on Technology in Education, 38(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ728898

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANCOVA (pre-test covariate) on accommodator learners' gain scores across the three modes. The article reports η2 = 0.134, a medium effect size, with adjusted means of 24.749 (guided VR), 14.813 (non-guided VR) and 12.616 (Non VR).

> "After adjusting for the pre-test scores, there was a signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the accommodator learners of the three learning modes on the gain scores, F(2, 80) = 6.211, p = 0.003."
