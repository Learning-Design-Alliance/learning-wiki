Abugri Bruce Ayabilla 2026¶

Abugri Bruce Ayabilla, Assibi Maxwell Ateni, Amalba Anthony, Gaa Patience Kanyiri, Kpebu Sophia E.A., Karikari Patience Afua Adwaapa, Mogre Victor. (2026). The effects of problem-based learning (PBL) on undergraduate medical students' critical thinking and communication skills development: A scoping review across resource-rich and resource-limited settings (2015-2024). PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0342599

q2 · i? · review · r2

Scoping review of eight studies, all cross-sectional and mostly self-report, most with no comparator. Its settings-comparison theme says studies from resource-limited settings "suggest that PBL remains effective in developing critical thinking and communication skills even when institutional resources are constrained", and that PBL's collaborative, discussion-based nature "appears to compensate" for infrastructural limits. The review's overall conclusion is more cautious: PBL "may be associated with perceived improvements" in these skills, and the lack of longitudinal or experimental designs restricts how far causality may be inferred. No effect sizes were printed.

"In contrast, studies from resource-limited settings such as Pakistan, India, and Nepal [16,35–37] suggest that PBL remains effective in developing critical thinking and communication skills even when institutional resources are constrained."
