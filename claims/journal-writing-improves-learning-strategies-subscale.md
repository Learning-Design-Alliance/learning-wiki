`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on post-administration learning-strategies scores with pre-test as covariate gave F=6.35, p=0.02 for Method of Instruction. Post means were 3.70 (experimental, n=32) versus 3.27 (control, n=30).
