## Evidence

### Si Jihyun 2020

Si Jihyun. (2020). Course-based research experience of undergraduate medical students through project-based learning. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2020.152

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

End-of-course 15-item survey (Likert plus open-ended) administered to 40 second-year premedical students in a three-credit CRE course; descriptive statistics showed all items over 4.0, e.g., "The group work went well." at 4.45±0.88.

> "All of the items in the three areas showed a mean score of over 4.0 out of 5. The findings showed that students perceived the course structure as appropriate and helpful. They considered the group work experience to be positive and productive."
