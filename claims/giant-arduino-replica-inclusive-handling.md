### Fior 2025

Fior, G., Fonda, C. and Canessa, E. (2025). Hands-on STEM learning experiences using digital technologies. [doi:10.3934/steme.2025009](https://doi.org/10.3934/steme.2025009)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Design-experience report from Section 4.1. The authors built a larger (9:1) scale Arduino Uno with prototype board and magnetic-connection components using a laser cutter and 3D printed parts, motivated by the difficulty of handling small electronic components. The inclusion benefit is asserted from their experience, not measured.

> "The fully functioning giant replica in Figure 7 not only allows an instructor to explain and demonstrate how to use the microcontroller to students in a classroom setting, but it also benefits students with visual impairments or motor skill difficulties, enabling them to participate actively in the lectures."
