# Adolescents' online learning self-efficacy during school suspension is negatively associated with their perceived worries after school resumption, for both girls and boys

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` For girls, online learning self-efficacy during suspension was significantly negatively associated with perceived worries after resumption (β = −0.64, p < 0.01). [→ Lan 2022](#lan-2022)
`q2 i2` For boys, online learning self-efficacy during suspension was significantly negatively associated with perceived worries after resumption (β = −0.42, p < 0.01). [→ Lan 2022](#lan-2022)

## Evidence

### Lan 2022

Lan, M., Pan, Q., Tan, C. Y., & Law, N. W. Y. (2022). Understanding protective and risk factors affecting adolescents' well-being during the COVID-19 pandemic. npj Science of Learning 7:32. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00149-4

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-group SEM on self-reported data from 932 Hong Kong secondary student-parent pairs during COVID-19 school suspension. The article reports "OSE was signiﬁcantly associated with perceived WOR" with standardized coefficients β = −0.64 for girls and β = −0.42 for boys, both p < 0.01.

> "OSE was signiﬁcantly associated with perceived WOR both for girls (β = −0.64, p < 0.01) and boys (β = −0.42, p < 0.01)."
