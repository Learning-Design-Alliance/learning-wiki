## Evidence

### Karamdokht Parya 2026

Karamdokht Parya, Babaee Hemmati Yasamin, Dadgaran Ideh, Falahchai Mehran. (2026). Item analysis and performance of EMQ, MCQ, and SAQ in dental education and students’ perception of extended-matching questions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09791-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney comparison of distractor efficiency in the 29-student dental exam item analysis; MCQs' mean DE exceeded EMQs'. Authors attribute this to the "more compact structure and limited number of options in MCQs".

> "the comparison of the EMQs and MCQs with the Mann–Whitney test showed a significant difference (P = 0.014), and the MCQs had a higher mean DE (43.63 ± 29.39) than the EMQs (29.95 ± 21.46)"
