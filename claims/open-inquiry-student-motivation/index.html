Evidence¶

Muhamad Dah 2024¶

Muhamad Dah, N., Mat Noor, M. S. A., Kamarudin, M. Z., & Syed Abdul Azziz, S. S. (2024). The impacts of open inquiry on students' learning in science: A systematic literature review. Educational Research Review, 43. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100601

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Adler et al. (2018), that temporal motivation changed significantly from beginning to end of inquiry, analysed via students' notes on "positive autonomy and positive competence"; no effect size is printed.

"They found that changes in students' temporal motivation from the beginning to the end of the inquiry process showed a significant difference in the analysis of students' notes regarding positive autonomy and positive competence."

Muhamad Dah 2024 (2)¶

Muhamad Dah, N., Mat Noor, M. S. A., Kamarudin, M. Z., & Syed Abdul Azziz, S. S. (2024). The impacts of open inquiry on students' learning in science: A systematic literature review. Educational Research Review, 43. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100601

q2 · i0 · review · r2

The review reports, citing Suryani (2017), a null finding: "no significant difference in the test result concerning students' collaborative attitudes". No effect size is printed, so impact is coded as a printed negligible/null result per the review's wording.

"On the other hand, Suryani (2017) found no significant difference in the test result concerning students' collaborative attitudes due to open inquiry learning."
