Effects of Tutor Scaffolding on Students' Independent LLM Use for Programming. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_37](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_37)

Linear regression on group-level understanding scores for 30 project groups, accounting for partial trace coverage; prompting patterns were predictive while neither total intervention students nor intervention students without chatbot use was significantly related. The model explained 33% of variance (adjusted R² = 0.22).

> "Groups with a higher number ofUnderstanding-driven promptersachieved significantly higher understanding grades than groups with moreDebuggers(β= 1.30,p= 0.025), whereas the difference betweenImplementersandDebuggerswas not significant (β= 0.76,p= 0.19)."
