### Archer 2024

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-way between-group ANOVA with Tukey HSD post hoc tests on student-level normalized gain scores from matched pairs across eleven courses; the article reports the omnibus test as "significant overall with the F statistic, F(3, 788) = 26.11; p < .001". No effect size is printed for the omnibus result.
