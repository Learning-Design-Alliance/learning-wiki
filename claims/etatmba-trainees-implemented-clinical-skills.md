## Evidence

### Ellard David R 2014

Ellard David R, Chimwaza Wanangwa, Davies David, O'Hare Joseph Paul, Kamwendo Francis, Quenby Siobhan, Griffiths Frances, on behalf of The ETATMBA Study Group. (2014). Can training in advanced clinical skills in obstetrics, neonatal care and leadership, of non-physician clinicians in Malawi impact on clinical services improvements (the ETATMBA project): a process evaluation. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2014-005751

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Third interview round with 39 trainees, prompted for examples of applying each key aspect of the training. PPH was the second most discussed practical skill: "19 trainees reported using the skills in practice", with specific techniques including aorta compression, B-Lynch suture, condom tamponade and misoprostol. Thematic analysis of interview transcripts.

> "Twenty-three trainees talked about skills in the management of a PPH. Out of these, 19 trainees reported using the skills in practice. Six trainees had used aorta compression; five had used the B-Lynch suture; five had used the condom tamponade; and three had used misoprostol."

### Ellard David R 2014 (2)

Ellard David R, Chimwaza Wanangwa, Davies David, O'Hare Joseph Paul, Kamwendo Francis, Quenby Siobhan, Griffiths Frances, on behalf of The ETATMBA Study Group. (2014). Can training in advanced clinical skills in obstetrics, neonatal care and leadership, of non-physician clinicians in Malawi impact on clinical services improvements (the ETATMBA project): a process evaluation. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2014-005751

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Same third-round interview set with 39 trainees. Neonatal resuscitation was the most cited practical skill, with 25 giving practice examples and 17 cascading it. The article notes it was not a new skill but that "the training's more systematic approach was previously lacking".

> "Twenty-seven trainees mentioned neonatal resuscitation as a skill they encountered in the training with 25 giving examples of how they had used the skills in practice. Seventeen indicated that they had cascaded the skill to their colleagues in their facilities."
