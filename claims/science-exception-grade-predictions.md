Pre-registered Hypothesis 2A analysis in Experiment 3 tested grade predictions across four core subjects. The visual learner was rated higher in language arts, math, and social studies (d = 1.02, 1.07, 0.97), "but not science", where the difference was non-significant (d = 0.12).
