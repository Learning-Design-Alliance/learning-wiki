## Evidence

### Bautista Alcaine 2024

Bautista Alcaine, P., Vicente Sánchez, E., Orejudo Hernández, S., & Cano Escoriaza, J. (2024). Training pre-service teachers to deal with cyberbullying: Collective intelligence as a mode of learning. Computers & Education, 220, 105123. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105123

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-hoc pairwise comparisons with Holm's correction in the repeated measures mixed-model analysis of 180 participants showed that improvement onset differed by question type; for Questions 3 and 4 significant differences appeared "from Phases 3 and 4 on".

> "in the case of Questions 5 and 6 (on the subject of strategies to cope with cyberbullying), differences already started to be significant from Phase 2 on, i.e., from the very first phase in which users were allowed to interact with other participants"
