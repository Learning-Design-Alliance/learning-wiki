## Evidence

### Schuller BW 2022

Schuller BW, Burch C, Casterton T, Crowther C, Fowler J, Stenmark MH. (2022). Precision patient education using a “flipped classroom” approach. J Appl Clin Med Phys. https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13601

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post survey comparison in the pilot cohort, one-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank test for matched pairs. Mean reported anxiety fell from "Avg, 3.6" to "Avg, 2.3"; the article reports p = 0.031 and no effect size.

> "Patients reported reduced anxiety (before vs. after intervention [Figure 1c]: Avg, 3.6; 95% CI, 2.4–4.7 vs. Avg, 2.3; 95% CI, 1.4–3.3; p = 0.031)"
