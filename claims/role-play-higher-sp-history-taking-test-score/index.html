Evidence¶

Huang et al. 2018¶

Simin Huang, Peng Xu, Lie Feng, Chunting Lu & Jing Yang. (2018). An Effective Teaching Method to Enhance History-Taking Skills for Chinese Medical Students. International Journal of Higher Education, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v7n2p144

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Final-evaluation three-station OSCE with one history-taking station; each student completed a 10-minute interview with a teacher standardized patient after randomly selecting one of ten scenario cases. The observation group (n=25) scored 79.34 ± 9.43 versus 72.05 ± 12.08 in the control group (n=27), P = 0.007.

"Compared to the score for the control group (72.05 ± 12.08), the score for history taking with TSP in the observation group was higher (79.34 ± 9.43). A statistical difference was found between the two groups (P = 0.007)."
