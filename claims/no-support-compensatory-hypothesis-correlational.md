## Evidence

### King 2025

King, R. B., & Wang, F. (2025). The rich get richer: Socioeconomic advantage amplifies the role of growth mindsets in learning. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 792–810. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12755

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Omnibus statement of the Model 2 interaction results across all three moderators and outcomes in the PISA 2018 analysis: no negative moderation was found for any socioeconomic context.

> "We found almost no support for the compensatory hypothesis (H2c) across all the models we tested as socioeconomic contexts (i.e., family SES, school SES and country affluence) did not negatively moderate the association between growth mindsets and focal learning‐related outcomes."
