## Evidence

### Puthiaparampil Thomas 2021

Puthiaparampil Thomas, Rahman Mizanur. (2021). How important is distractor efficiency for grading Best Answer Questions? BMC Medical Education, 21:29. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02463-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' recommendation drawn from the retrospective analysis of 350 BAQ items across 7 tests, in which DE did not track item quality classes and 100% DE items were not the best by DISi standards. They suggest "2 or more FDs per item or 50% or higher DE as optimum."

> "Therefore, based on the findings from this study, we suggest that 2 or more FDs per item or 50% or higher DE as optimum."
