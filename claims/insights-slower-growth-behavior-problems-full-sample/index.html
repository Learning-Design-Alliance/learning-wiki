Evidence¶

O'Connor 2014¶

O'Connor, E. E., McCormick, M. P., Cappella, E., & McClowry, S. G. (2014). Getting a Good Start in School: Differential Effects of INSIGHTS on the Behaviors and Engagement of Children with Challenging Temperaments. SREE Spring 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562775

q3 · i? · causal · r2

School-randomized growth curve analysis (Model 1, Table 3) of N = 435 children in 22 schools, with school and cohort fixed effects. The article reports "slower growth in behavior problems" for INSIGHTS students with γ = -.16, p < .01, E.S. = .54.

"Model 1. Main effect analyses (see Table 3) demonstrated that students in INSIGHTS showed slower growth in behavior problems than students in the control condition (γ = -.16, p < .01, E.S. = .54)."
