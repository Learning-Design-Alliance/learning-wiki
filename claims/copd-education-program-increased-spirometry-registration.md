`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pre-post cohort of 33 Sicilian GPs' COPD patients (n=1,465 at baseline), comparing quality-of-care indicators before and after the educational program. The article reports "increased from 59.7% before intervention to 70.5 and 73.0%" at 12 and 24 months, exceeding the 64.8% in the national HSD comparator; p<0.001 at both follow-ups in Table 4.

> "COPD patients with a spirometry examination registered lifetime (indicator 2) increased from 59.7% before intervention to 70.5 and 73.0%, respectively, after 12 and 24 months from the educational intervention."
