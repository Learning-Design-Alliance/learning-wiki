## Evidence

### Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang 2024

Zheng Zheng & Jun Wang. (2024). Interbrain neural correlates of self and other integration in joint statistical learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00280-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Dual-EEG hyperscanning analysis of theta-band PLV between dyad members, restricted to 3019 channel combinations exceeding surrogate PLVs (all PFDR < 0.05); 31 combinations showed significant phase and phase × context effects (all PFDR < 0.05); no printed effect size.

> "Further analysis on thechannel combinations showed interaction effects demonstrated by the slope of BtBC increasing from Block 1 to Block 6 being steeper in the joint context as compared to the baseline context (phase × context: β = −6.69×10-3, SE =9 . 1 4×1 0-4, t278 = −7.31, P < 0.001)"
