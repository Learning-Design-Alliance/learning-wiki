Related Claims¶

  • For advanced (Level I) learners, conventional instruction was slightly more suitable than corpus-based instruction, though the difference was not statistically significant. — related
  • Corpus-based lexical instruction using the Wikipedia Corpus produced significantly higher lexical gains than conventional instruction for medium-level (Level II) learners. — related
  • Overall, the experimental group's posttest average was significantly higher than the control group's despite both groups declining from pretest to posttest. — related
