## Evidence

### Osman Tasdelen 2025

Osman Tasdelen, Daniel Bodemer. (2025). Generative AI in the Classroom: Effects of Context-Personalized Learning Material and Tasks on Motivation and Performance. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00491-9

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

2×2 repeated measures ANOVA on interest during the post-test in the same classroom experiment. Personalized tasks yielded higher interest (M = 3.32) than standard tasks (M = 2.84), with effect size "η 2 p = .28".

> "a main effect for task type was found, F(1, 112) = 42.80, p <. 001,η 2 p = .28. Students who worked on context-personalized tasks had signiﬁcantly higher inter- est scores (M = 3.32, SD = 1.29) compared to those who worked on standard tasks (M = 2.84, SD = 1.24)"
