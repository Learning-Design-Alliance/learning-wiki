## Evidence

### Hillary Dawkins 2017

Hillary Dawkins, Holly Hedgeland, and Sally Jordan. (2017). The impact of scaffolding and question structure on the gender gap. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020117](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020117)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of iCMA responses from four module presentations (5535 students), stratified by ability using the Mantel-Haenszel method; questions flagged significant if both |α*MH| ≥ 1 and p ≤ .05. The article reports "3 questions of signiﬁcant bias, all in favour of male students."
