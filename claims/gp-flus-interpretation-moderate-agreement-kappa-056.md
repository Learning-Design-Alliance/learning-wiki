GP interpretations of all 91 FLUS examinations were compared to an independent blinded two-specialist reference; percentage agreement and unweighted Cohen's kappa were calculated, with the abstract reporting "78% agreement with the Specialist Reference and Cohen's kappa 0.56" for the 51.7% pathology presence.

> "GPs interpreted presence of pathological findings in about half of patients (51.7%) in moderate agreement with the Specialist Reference (Cohen's kappa k = 0.56)."
