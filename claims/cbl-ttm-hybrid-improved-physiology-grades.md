## Evidence

### Elgheit Rehab E. Abo 2025

Elgheit Rehab E. Abo, Nashat Nagwa. (2025). Bringing theory to life: integrating case-based learning in applied physiology for undergraduate physiotherapy education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06725-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of the same cohort's first-semester (TTMs) and fourth-semester (hybrid) grades; Table 5 reports Z = −11.668, P < 0.001, median 8.5 vs 10.0. No standardised effect size printed.

> "Although the maximum grades were the same between the two methods of teaching (10), the minimum grade was 2 out of 10 when applied physiology was taught through the TTMs in the first semester, compared to a minimum grade of 7.5 out of 10.0 with the integration of CBL with TTMs in the fourth semester."
