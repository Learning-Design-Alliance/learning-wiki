# A formative study with 12 clinical-year medical students produced four design-oriented themes for AI-SP consultation practice

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Interviews and co-design workshops with 12 medical students yielded four design themes: human SPs as reference practices, extending practice beyond dialogue, connecting activities across the encounter, and supporting post-session review. [→ Zhiqi Gao 2026](#zhiqi-gao-2026)

### Zhiqi Gao 2026

MedEasy: Designing AI Standardized Patients for Clinical Consultation Training. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17512 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.17512](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2606.17512)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative design study: 12 clinical-year medical students in Years 4–6 completed semi-structured interviews and three co-design workshops; hybrid thematic analysis of transcripts and workshop artifacts "produced 235 statement cards: 169 from the formative interviews and 66 from the workshops," from which four design-oriented themes emerged.
