### Gaillet Antoine 2026

Multivariate analysis of all SCTs. Table 3 reports aOR 0.45 [0.23; 0.85] for ≥3 years post-residency and aOR 2.22 [1.24; 4.15] for diagnostic vs investigative SCTs, both p < 0.001–0.023.

> "There was also an inverse relationship between age and quality of SCT, as well as an impact of the type of SCT on its quality, with diagnostic SCT more often valid than therapeutic and investigative SCT"
