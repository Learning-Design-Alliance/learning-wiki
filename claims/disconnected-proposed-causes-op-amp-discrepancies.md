## Evidence

### Laura Ríos 2019

Laura Ríos, Benjamin Pollard, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2019). Using think-aloud interviews to characterize model-based reasoning in electronics for a laboratory course assessment. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010140)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of proposed-cause episodes across the 10 TAPS interviews, classifying causes as connected or disconnected to the preceding comparison or measurement. The authors report "Most proposed causes were disconnected," exemplified by a student who began reasoning correctly about the DMM's effect but instead proposed changing the op-amp.

> "Overall, we found that the Propose Cause subtask was the area where students appeared to resort to other subtasks when they were unable to initially propose a cause for an observed discrepancy. Most proposed causes were disconnected, highlighting the diﬃculty around this subtask."
