## Evidence

### Safa A. Elgamal 2011

Safa A. Elgamal, MBBCh, PhD, Eric A. Roy, PhD, Michael T. Sharratt, PhD. (2011). Age and Verbal Fluency: The Mediating Effect of Speed of Processing. Canadian Geriatrics Journal, Volume 14, Issue 3. https://doi.org/10.5770/cgj.v14i3.17

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Univariate ANCOVA on letter fluency (COWAT F-A-S) controlling for the processing speed composite and NART-estimated IQ in the same 130-participant sample. The discussion states "The change in letter fluency as a function of age appeared only after accounting for processing speed," with the between-group effect significant at p = .04 in the MANCOVA model.

> "Initially age had no effect on letter fluency. The change in letter fluency as a function of age appeared only after accounting for processing speed."
