Evidence¶

Pavletič Bernarda 2026¶

Pavletič Bernarda, Babuder Milena Košak, Homar Vesna. (2026). Medical students with specific learning disabilities: mixed-methods systematic review of the prevalence, academic performance, challenges, and perceived impact of support interventions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09574-0

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of three studies (McKendree, Ricketts, Meeks) of MCQ-type assessments with additional examination time as accommodation; pooled d = 0.045, τ² = 0.022. The review rates certainty low-moderate using GRADE and stresses results "should be interpreted with caution".

"Using a random-effects model, the pooled standardized mean difference was d = 0.045 (95% CI: −0.094 to 0.183), indicating no statistically significant difference in MCQ performance between students with and without SpLDs."
