Valdez 2020¶

Valdez, A., Takahashi, S., Krausen, K., Bowman, A., & Gurrola, E. (2020). Getting better at getting more equitable: Opportunities and barriers for using continuous improvement to advance educational equity. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606089

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

In the barriers section of the interview study, a leader described how improvement "becomes a thing on a check - list" when done from a compliance orientation, using the example of completing PDSA cycles perfunctorily.

"The way that improvement can fall short rad- ically is that it becomes a thing on a check - list. It becomes “What I do because you tell me to do it.”"
