## Evidence

### Wen Wu 2019

Wen Wu, Li Chen, Qingchang Yang, You Li. (2019). Inferring Students' Personality from Their Communication Behavior in Web-based Learning Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00173-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive log analysis of the 164-student cohort's activity over the whole course (Table 3), reporting mean frequencies per activity. Descriptive means only, no inferential test, so impact is null.

> "the frequency of posting messages in the chat room is largely higher than other activities, with a mean of 71.46 times, vs. average 17.8 times of taking notes, 3.54 times of sharing materials, 2.10 messages posted in the discussion forum, 1.49 times of using hands-up facility, and 1.02 times of sharing notes."

### Wen Wu 2019 (2)

Wen Wu, Li Chen, Qingchang Yang, You Li. (2019). Inferring Students' Personality from Their Communication Behavior in Web-based Learning Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00173-9

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive participation rates from the same log analysis; the article separately reports 42.1% of students used the discussion forum and 96.3% submitted assignments. Descriptive percentages only.

> "99.3% of the students posted at least one message in the chat room. 73.8% of the students took course notes, and 21.3% of these students shared their written notes with others at least once."
