## Evidence

### Carr Sandra E. 2016

Carr Sandra E., Brand Gabrielle, Wei Li, Wright Helen, Nicol Pam, Metcalfe Helene, Saunders Julie, Payne John, Seubert Liza, Foley Laurie. (2016). “Helping someone with a skill sharpens it in your own mind”: a mixed method study exploring health professions students experiences of Peer Assisted Learning (PAL). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0566-8

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Emergent thematic analysis of surveys, 16 h of observational field notes, focus groups and interviews; students described teaching as helping someone with a skill that “sharpens it in your own mind”.

> "The majority of students expressed how teaching and giving feedback to their peers helped conceptualize their understanding by reflecting on their own learning needs and bridging the gap between theory and practice"
