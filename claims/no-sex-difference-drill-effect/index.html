Evidence¶

Brown 1915¶

Brown, J. C. (1915). An Investigation on the Value of Drill Work in the Fundamental Operations of Arithmetic. Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Null finding from the study's comparisons of drill and non-drill sections, in which the poorest and best 19 subgroups each contained both boys and girls (e.g., 8 boys and 11 girls among the poorest non-drill 19). No test statistic or effect size is printed for this comparison.

"None of the data collected indicates that boys are either more or less influenced by drill than girls. The effect of the drill was practically the same."
