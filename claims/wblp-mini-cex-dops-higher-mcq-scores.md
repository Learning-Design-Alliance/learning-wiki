## Evidence

### Wang Ji-nuo 2026

Wang Ji-nuo, Lv Hanbing, Sun Yi, Cao Wen, Jiang Luhua. (2026). A workplace-based learning program on clerkship of internal medicine improves medical student's clinical competence: a retrospective comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08719-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective cohort of 77 fourth-year students (WBLP n = 39, control n = 38) in a 9-week internal medicine clerkship; between-group t-test comparison of MCQ scores showed "Cohen's d = 0.80, 95% CI 0.33–1.26". Baseline GPA and gender did not differ between groups.

> "The WBLP group achieved significantly higher MCQ scores than the control group (85.3 ± 6.1 vs. 80.2 ± 6.7; p < 0.001) (Fig. 2A). The effect size was large (Cohen's d = 0.80, 95% CI 0.33–1.26)"
