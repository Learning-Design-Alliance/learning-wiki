## Evidence

### Zhang Hui 2025

Zhang Hui, Wang Xiaomei, Li Xiaojin, Zhai Jinghua, Li Xiuguo, Guo Yan. (2025). The impact of large-class flipped classrooms incorporating design thinking on self-awareness, team collaboration, learning efficiency, and comprehensive literacy of clinical medicine undergraduates. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07116-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis of the 150 experimental-group students comparing male and female responses. Tables report χ² = 8.887, P < 0.05 for resource use; χ² = 10.214, P < 0.01 for pre-class mastery; χ² = 7.539, P < 0.05 for perceived burden.

> "Male students showed a stronger preference for interactive resources (e.g., videos, quizzes) and were more likely to perceive the teaching approach as reducing their learning burden (45.3%), while female students tended to favour structured resources (e.g., textbooks, articles) and mostly reported no change in their burden (49.3%)."
