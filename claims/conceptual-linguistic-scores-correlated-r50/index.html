Markus S. Feser & Paul L. Tschisgale. (2026). AI-based scoring systematically underestimates conceptual understanding of linguistically weak students' explanations in physics. Physics Education, Leibniz Institute for Science and Mathematics Education, Kiel. https://orcid.org/0000-0001-8503-0951

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Correlational analysis of the expert reference for the 116 student explanations, illustrated in Fig. 1 with a fitted linear regression. The article reports the two independently assigned scores "are moderately correlated in the present data (r = .50, see Fig. 1)" and used conceptual understanding as a regression covariate to block this indirect route.

"Because expert-assigned scores for conceptual understanding and linguistic quality are moderately correlated in the present data (r = .50, see Fig. 1), an unadjusted model would attribute part of this scale-induced association to linguistic quality"
