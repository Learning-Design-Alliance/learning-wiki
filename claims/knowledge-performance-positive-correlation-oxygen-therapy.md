### Diab Samar Salah Eldin Mohamed 2022

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of the same 105 nurses (Table 5, Pearson correlations): total knowledge–performance r = 0.711, p = 0.000; phase correlations r = 0.642, 0.577, 0.497. Correlation does not establish causation.

> "Table 5 proved that there was a highly statistically positive linear correlation between the total scores for preparation, intervention, and reporting, and the total knowledge score at p value 0.001, 0.003, and 0.009, respectively."
