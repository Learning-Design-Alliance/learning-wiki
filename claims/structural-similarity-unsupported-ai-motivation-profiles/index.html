Liang 2026¶

Liang, S., Yau, K. W., Meng, H., Chiu, T. K. F., King, I., Yam, Y., & Chai, C. S. (2026). From disengaged to self-determined: A latent transition analysis of students' AI learning motivation. Education and Information Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-026-13998-y

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Longitudinal measurement invariance testing (configural, metric, and scalar invariance were supported) was followed by a structural similarity test comparing constrained and unconstrained LTA models. The quote reports the likelihood ratio difference test result; the authors interpret this as indicating that students' experiences of competence, relatedness, and autonomy may evolve during the curriculum.

"A likelihood ratio difference test further confirmed that the constrained model fit significantly worse than the unconstrained model (ΔG² = 192.02, Δdf = 15, p <.001)."
