Professor Lyric described co-editing a theme issue introducing materials scientists to QIST opportunities and argued a welcoming framing beats gatekeeping: "you either have [taken] quantum mechanics [courses]... or you don't". Educators noted QIST is currently physicist-dominated because of the field's 'physical' era, and that skills learned in QIST are transferable to other fields.

> "if someone's background is in computer science, they probably have more tools than they realize to jump into quantum computing, especially if you start to think about circuit-based approaches."
