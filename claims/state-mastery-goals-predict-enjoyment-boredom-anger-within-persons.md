## Evidence

### Goetz 2016

Goetz, T., Sticca, F., Pekrun, R., Murayama, K., & Elliot, A. J. (2016). Intraindividual relations between achievement goals and discrete achievement emotions: An experience sampling approach. Learning and Instruction, 41, 115-125. https://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.10.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Intraindividual multilevel regression of ESM state data (1409 assessments nested in 120 students), with subject dummies at Level 1 and sex, age, achievement at Level 2. The article reports "Mastery goals were positive predictors of enjoyment" with the printed unstandardized coefficients and p-values; no standardized effect size is given.

> "Mastery goals were positive predictors of enjoyment ( b ¼ 0.22, p < .001), and negative predictors of anger (b ¼/C0 0.12, p < .01) and boredom ( b ¼/C0 0.29, p < .001)."
