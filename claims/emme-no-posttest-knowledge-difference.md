## Evidence

### Neslihan Yondemir Çalişkan 2025

Neslihan Yondemir Çalişkan, Emine Şendurur. (2025). The effect of cumulative eye movements' guidance of experts on transition from novice to expert. Education Tech Research Dev, 73:59–89. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10414-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on the 30-item multiple-choice post-test (same questions as pre-test) administered after eight weeks. Means rose slightly from pre-test (M exp = 12.07; M cont = 12.01) to post-test (M exp = 14.27; M cont = 14.47), but "there are no significant mean differences between the post-test scores of groups."

> "The results of the analysis showed that there are no significant mean differences between the post-test scores of groups, t(28) = − 0.14, p > 0.05."
