## Evidence

### Seth Poulsen 2023

Seth Poulsen, Hongxuan Chen, Yael Gertner, Benjamin Cosman, Matthew West, and Geoffrey L. Herman. (2023). Measuring the Impact of Distractors on Student Learning Gains while Using Proof Blocks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.00792](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.00792)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pretest-to-posttest comparisons in the randomized lab study, tested with a paired Mann Whitney U test after Shapiro-Wilk tests showed non-normal scores. All three groups "performed significantly better on the posttest than on the pretest", improving "between 25% and 35%".

> "all three groups performed significantly better on the posttest than on the pretest ( 𝑝 < .001 for all three groups). Groups improved by between 25% and 35%."
