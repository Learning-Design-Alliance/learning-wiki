Qualitative thematic analysis of interview, survey, and design session data. Participants reported bootcamp costs of $15,000, caregiving and work conflicts with training schedules, and education that "did not align with their goals or did not fully align with the demandsof the tech industry."

"some cited how the cost of educational/ training (e.g., $15,000bootcamps) coupled with individuals already experiencing financial struggles (e.g., managing child careand housing costs) make it difficult to upskill in tech."
