## Evidence

### Lilja Helmiina 2024

Lilja Helmiina, Talvisara Maria, Eskola Vesa, Heikkilä Paula, Sievänen Harri, Palmu Sauli. (2024). Novice providers' success in performing lumbar puncture: a randomized controlled phantom study between a conventional spinal needle and a novel bioimpedance needle. BMC Medical Education 24:520. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05505-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled phantom trial's Results, attempts across all attempts were compared between the 30-per-group conditions; medians were "2 (range 1–5)" versus "1 [1–5]" with no significant difference (p = 0.56).

> "Regarding the success of the procedure at any attempt, the median number of attempts was 2 (range 1–5) for the conventional needle and 1 [1–5] for the bioimpedance needle (p = 0.56)."
