# Fraction Face-Off! shows potentially positive effects on number and operations outcomes, with a WWC-calculated average effect size of 0.89 and improvement index of +31

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q4` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q4 i3` In one RCT meeting WWC standards without reservations, Fraction Face-Off! produced a positive and statistically significant effect on number and operations (average effect size 0.89, improvement index +31, 1,152 students). [→ numops-1](#numops-1)

## Evidence

### numops-1

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020, March). Fraction Face-Off!. Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/702

`q4 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Findings from the one RCT meeting WWC standards without reservations, on six researcher-designed number and operations measures with 1,152 fourth-grade students. The fraction calculation measure showed an effect size of 1.68 and improvement index of +45, p < .01; the domain average effect size was 0.89.
