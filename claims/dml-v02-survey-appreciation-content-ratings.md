### Keijser Wouter A. 2019

Keijser Wouter A., Handgraaf Henricus J. M., Isfordink Liz M., Janmaat Vincent T., Vergroesen Pieter-Paul A., Verkade Julia M. J. S., Wieringa Sietse, Wilderom Celeste P. M. (2019). `q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Online SurveyMonkey survey validating version 0.2 with 82 respondents (response rate 65.1%), rating domains on a 5-point Likert scale plus open questions. Respondents "appreciated the initiative of creating a national framework with a relatively satisfactory score: 7.6/10 (SD 1.37)".

> "Survey respondents appreciated the initiative of creating a national framework with a relatively satisfactory score: 7.6/10 (SD 1.37) (Fig. 4)."
