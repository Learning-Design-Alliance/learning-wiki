## Evidence

### Wiggins et al. 2017

Meg Wiggins, Constanza Gonzalez Parrao, Helen Austerberry and Anne Ingold. (2017). Foreign Language Learning in Primary School: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581156

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process evaluation using training observations, unit completion records, teacher surveys, children's questionnaires and four case study schools with lesson observations and semi-structured teacher interviews, analysed with framework analysis. Implementation was variably implemented, with reduced weeks, reduced weekly time, and omission of English literacy aspects in some cases.

> "There was a lot of variation in how the intervention was implemented. Not all teachers delivered the linked English literacy activity and some schools delivered fewer weeks of FLL than prescribed because of staffing or timetabling issues."
