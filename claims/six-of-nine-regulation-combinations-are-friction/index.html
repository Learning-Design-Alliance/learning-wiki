Jan D. Vermunt 1999¶

Jan D. Vermunt, Nico Verloop. (1999). Congruence and friction between learning and teaching. Learning and Instruction 9, 257–280. http://hdl.handle.net/1874/18753

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical analysis presented with Table 4, which cross-tabulates three degrees of teacher-regulation (strong, shared, loose) with three degrees of student-regulation (high, intermediate, low). Congruence holds on one diagonal; the article states "six out of nine, represent friction between teaching and learning".

"The majority of cells, six out of nine, represent friction between teaching and learning. Some of these are destructive in nature, for example when students that are well capable of self-regulated learning have a teacher who prescribes the way they should learn in detail."
