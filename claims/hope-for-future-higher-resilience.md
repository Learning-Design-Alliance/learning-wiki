## Evidence

### Kashiwabara Tomoka 2022

Kashiwabara Tomoka, Fujiwara Takeo, Doi Satomi, Yamaoka Yui. (2022). Association between Hope for the Future and Academic Performance in Adolescents: Results from the K-CHILD Study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191911890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Propensity-score-matched conditional regression on K-CHILD data; resilience was caregiver-rated on the CRCS (Cronbach's alpha = 0.80). The abstract prints the matched estimate "β = 1.48, 95% CI = 0.98 to 1.98".

> "In the model after PS matching, the significant difference in both academic performance and resilience remained (both p < 0.001)."
