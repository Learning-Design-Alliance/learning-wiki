### Ntumi Simon 2025

Ntumi Simon. (2025). Validating high-fidelity simulation for assessing procedural skills in nursing education: a kane framework approach in Ghana. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07724-4

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Concurrent correlation between simulation and OSCE scores reported in Results (Table 4) for the 150-student sample. The article reports "The Pearson's Correlation between simulation and OSCE scores is 0.45 (p < 0.01)" and separately a Cohen's d of 0.56 (95% CI [0.40, 0.72]) for the mean difference between modalities.

> "The Pearson's Correlation between simulation and OSCE scores is 0.45 (p < 0.01), with a 95% CI of [0.30, 0.58], reflecting a moderate positive correlation and suggesting that simulation scores are meaningfully related to OSCE performance."
