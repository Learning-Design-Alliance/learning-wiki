## Evidence

### Hyewon Jang 2015

Hyewon Jang. (2015). Identifying 21st century STEM competencies using workplace data. Journal of Science Education and Technology. [doi:10.1007/s10956-015-9593-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10956-015-9593-1)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of O*NET Work activities importance ratings from 4,362 STEM job incumbents (about 28 per occupation). The article reports the five highest-rated activities with their mean importance values and states "Most of the important work activities were relevant to dealing with and updating information and knowledge."

> "The five most important work activities were: Getting information (4.36), Making decisions and solving problems (4.18), Interacting with computers (4.14), and Communicating with supervisors, peers, or subordinates (4.08), and Updating and using relevant knowledge (4.04) ."
