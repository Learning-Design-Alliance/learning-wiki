Caparas 2024¶

Caparas, R., Eisenberg, L., Krausen, K., & Lias, C. (2024). Schools can't do it alone: Developing sustainable systems of care for school-based behavioral health in California. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED655746

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Analysis of SBHIP project plans found billing Medi-Cal (via the Multi-Payer Fee Schedule and LEA BOP) was the most frequent sustainability strategy, followed by using existing LEA funds such as LCFF and applying for new grants; the brief notes many LEAs currently lack billing infrastructure.

"LEAs' project plans identified Medi-Cal billing as their most frequent strategy for sustaining new behavioral health investments over the long term."
