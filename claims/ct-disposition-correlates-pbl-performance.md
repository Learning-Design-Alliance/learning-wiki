## Evidence

### Pu Dan 2019

Pu Dan, Ni Juhua, Song Demao, Zhang Weiguang, Wang Yuedan, Wu Liling, Wang Xian, Wang Yun. (2019). Influence of critical thinking disposition on the learning efficiency of problem-based learning in undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1418-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Parametric bivariate correlation analysis between CTDI-CV total scores and average scores of eight PBL tutorials (n = 102). The study reports that "the total CT scores had positive correlations significantly with the scores of the PBL performance and all five dimensions"; Table 3 prints a PBL-score coefficient of 0.264**.

> "The results showed that the total CT scores had positive correlations significantly with the scores of the PBL performance and all five dimensions."
