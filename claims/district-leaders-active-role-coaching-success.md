## Evidence

### Hardy 2021

Hardy, A., & Bakhshaei, M. (2021). Funding coaching is not enough: The role of the district administrator in instructional coaching. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/89f20796-22b4-44c1-889c-cc2fd46a19a2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

A three-year research study of instructional coaching across 32 districts, reported by Digital Promise, found that active district-leader engagement in "eliminating barriers to effective coaching" and encouraging innovation was needed to maximize the return on coaching investment. No effect sizes are reported; the finding is qualitative.

> "To maximize the return on their coaching investment, our three-year study of instructional coaching across 32 districts shows that district leaders need to ensure that they are taking an active role in eliminating barriers to effective coaching and fostering a culture that encourages teachers to innovate their practice"
