Evidence¶

Yakmaci-Guzel 2013¶

Yakmaci-Guzel, B., & Adadan, E. (2013). Use of multiple representations in developing preservice chemistry teachers' understanding of the structure of matter. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 8(1), 109-130. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1008597

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Frequency analysis (Table 6) shows states-of-matter misclassifications dropped from 18 to 3 after instruction and 6 at delayed posttest. The article reports many preservice teachers "included submicroscopic level evidence" such as "the distance between particles" in post and delayed posttest justifications.

"The number of scientifically inappropriate classifications for the given particulate representations with respect to their physical states was not high (n=18) before the instruction, and the frequency of scientifically inappropriate classification cases even decreased (n=3) following the instruction"
