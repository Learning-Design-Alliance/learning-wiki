`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square test of homogeneity on 408 qualified applicants in the 2021–2022 cycle ranked by holistic rubric score, comparing ITI versus WLI racial/ethnic frequencies. The White/non-Hispanic ITI–WLI gap narrowed to 2.7 percentage points versus 13.8 points under vGRE ranking.

> "The multinomial distributions of race and ethnicity (White, Asian, Hispanic, and Other Underrepresented Minorities) were not significantly different between the ITI and WLI groups χ2(3) = 3.119, P = .374"
