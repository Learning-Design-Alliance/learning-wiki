## Evidence

### Bonner C 2019

Bonner C, Patel P, Fajardo MA, Zhuang R, Trevena L. (2019). Online decision aids for primary cardiovascular disease prevention: systematic search, evaluation of quality and suitability for low health literacy patients. BMJ Open 9(3):e025173. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-025173

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Readability assessment of all 25 decision aids using Gunning-Fog and Flesch-Kincaid indices, with high inter-rater correlation (0.91 and 0.94) and averaged scores. The abstract reports readability "higher than recommended levels". Descriptive statistics only; no effect size.

> "For readability, the average Gunning–Fog index was 9.9 (SD=1.9) and Flesch–Kincaid was 61.8 (SD=10.3), indicating that a US school grade of 9 is required to understand the information."
