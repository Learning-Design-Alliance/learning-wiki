## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski (2016). Developing skills vs reinforcing concepts in physics labs: Insight from E-CLASS. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Omnibus interaction from the 4-way ANCOVA on FY students (N = 3958) with all possible interaction terms; "the relationship between course focus and postinstruction mean is diﬀerent for men and women". No effect size printed.

> "The 4-way ANCOV A revealed a statistically signiﬁcant interaction between gender and course focus (F-test, p = 0 .001)."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2016 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski (2016). Developing skills vs reinforcing concepts in physics labs: Insight from E-CLASS. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010108)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three separate 3-way ANCOVAs split by course focus; in skills-focused courses "higher adjusted postinstruction means for women than for men", opposite to both-focused courses. No effect size printed.

> "Gender was a signiﬁcant predictor of E-CLASS scores only in skills-focused and both-focused courses (F-test, p < 0.01). In both-focused courses, men had higher adjusted postinstruction means; however, this trend was reversed for the skills-focused courses, with higher adjusted postinstruction means for women than for men."
