### Oliven Arie 2021

Oliven Arie, Nave Rachel, Baruch Adam. (2021). Long experience with a web-based, interactive, conversational virtual patient case simulation for medical students' evaluation: comparison with oral examination. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1946896

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Five-year retrospective analysis of exam records from the internal medicine clerkship at the Technion, covering 586 students examined with the VP level-II system. The article reports "Cronbach's alpha of the exams averaged 0.86"; no effect size is printed.

> "Cronbach's alpha of the exams averaged 0.86, with 0.89 obtained both in the first (2015) and the last (2019) exam."
