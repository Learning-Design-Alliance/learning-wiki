Carver-Thomas 2021¶

Carver-Thomas, D., Leung, M., & Burns, D. (2021). California teachers and COVID-19: How the pandemic is impacting the teacher workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/california-covid-19-teacher-workforce

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Fall 2020 interviews with California district leaders. Five districts reported increased resignations or retirements; in another five districts serving concentrations of students in poverty, retirements contributed to shortages.

"Five districts said that they have noticed more teachers resigning or retiring this year compared to recent years. In some cases, resignations and retirements offset the need for anticipated layoffs due to expected budget cuts."
