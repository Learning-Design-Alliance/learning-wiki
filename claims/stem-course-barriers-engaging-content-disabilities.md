## Evidence

### Westley James 2019

Westley James, Kamryn Lamons, Roberto Spilka, Caroline Bustamante, Erin Scanlon and Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2019). Hidden walls: STEM course barriers identified by students with disabilities. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1909.02905](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1909.02905)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interpretative phenomenological analysis of semi-structured interviews with nine students with disability diagnoses enrolled in introductory physics or chemistry courses at one large southeastern research-intensive university. The analysis found that "Seven of the nine participants identified practices, or lack thereof, in STEM courses which introduced barriers with effectively engaging with class content."
