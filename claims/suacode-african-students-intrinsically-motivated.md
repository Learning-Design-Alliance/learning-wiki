# Most African students enrolled in the SuaCode smartphone coding course for intrinsic rather than extrinsic reasons (68.7% vs 31.3% of coded motivations)

## Evidence

### Michael Addo 2026

Michael Addo, Nana Maryam Munagah, Victor Kumbol, Judith Uchidiuno, and George Boateng. (2026). “Why SuaCode?”: Understanding African Students’ Motivations for Taking a Smartphone-Based Online Coding Course. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22940

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Open-ended survey of SuaCode enrollees analyzed with a grounded theory approach; a systematic random sample of 450 responses from over 3000 was coded by two raters into 19 themes. The results section reports that "intrinsic motivators comprised 68.7%" of codes versus 31.3% extrinsic. No effect size is printed.

> "Extrinsic motivators comprised 31.3 % of codes assigned, while intrinsic motivators comprised 68.7%. This is in line with current literature on motivations in MOOCs [ 4, 8], as participants of SuaCode are intrinsically motivated learners."
