Voluntary surveys of the author's Waterloo math students, plus a cited institutional poll of over 6,000 students on a fall reading week. On potential abuse, 44% of 111 said mental health days would be abused more than sick days and 42% saw no difference; all figures are descriptive.

> "71% of 102 students said: there should be mental health days like there are sick days. 67% of 95 students said: I think mental health days would be useful in a university setting."
