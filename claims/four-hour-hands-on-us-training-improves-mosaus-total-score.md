## Evidence

### Krogh Charlotte Loumann 2016

Krogh Charlotte Loumann, Steinmetz Jacob, Rudolph Søren Steemann, Hesselfeldt Rasmus, Lippert Freddy K., Berlac Peter A., Rasmussen Lars S. (2016). Effect of ultrasound training of physicians working in the prehospital setting. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-016-0289-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective pre-post study of 40 prehospital physicians from Copenhagen EMS MECUs; paired t-tests compared pre- and post-training mOSAUS total scores rated by two blinded raters. Median total score rose from "15.3 [12.0–17.5]" to "17.5 [14.5–21.0]", p < 0.0001; no effect size printed.

> "The participants demonstrated significant improvement in performance in both total score (17.5 [14.5–21.0] vs. 15.3 [12.0–17.5], p < 0.0001) and in each assessment element of the mOSAUS scale."
