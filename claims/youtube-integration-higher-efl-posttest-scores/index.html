Evidence¶

Alwehaibi 2015¶

Alwehaibi, H. O. (2015). The Impact Of Using YouTube In EFL Classroom On Enhancing EFL Students' Content Learning. Journal of College Teaching & Learning, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061416

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental "Non-randomized Control Group Pretest-Posttest Design" with 45 control and 51 experimental second-year EFL students; a t-test for independent samples compared mean gain scores on a 42-item achievement test. The experimental group mean was 28.9412 versus 20.6444 for the control group, "in favour of the experimental group".

"t-value is significant at the 0.05 level for the total test score, a result that indicates the existence of statistically significant difference between the two groups on the post -measurements, in favour of the experimental group."
