Evidence¶

Roschelle 2021¶

Roschelle, J., Mazziotti, C. & Means, B. (2021). Scaling Up Design of Inquiry Environments. In C. Chinn & R.G. Duncan et al (Eds.), International Handbook on Learning and Inquiry. Routledge. ISBN 9781138922600. https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/9781138922600 doi:10.4324/9781315685779-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

The chapter reports, citing Ben-David & Zohar (2009), a benefit of inquiry activities for low-income school students, and frames equity as an added layer of the scaling challenge because required resources, teacher skills, and teacher mobility differ across schools. The chapter prints no effect size for this attributed finding.

"Indeed, investigators have found that inquiry activities can be especially beneficial for students in low-income schools (Ben-David & Zohar, 2009)."
