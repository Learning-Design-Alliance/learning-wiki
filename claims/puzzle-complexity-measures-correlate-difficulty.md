The article reports that "simple complexity measures (size, length of solution) correlate mostly only weakly with difficulty" but that "for some puzzles (Rush hour), even such simple measures can provide a good prediction of difficulty."

> "The results show that simple complexity measures (size, length of solution) correlate mostly only weakly with difficulty. However, for some puzzles (Rush hour), even such simple measures can provide a good prediction of difficulty."
