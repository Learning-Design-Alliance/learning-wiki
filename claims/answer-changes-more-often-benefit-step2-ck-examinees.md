## Evidence

### Ouyang Wenli 2019

Ouyang Wenli, Harik Polina, Clauser Brian E., Paniagua Miguel A. (2019). Investigation of answer changes on the USMLE® Step 2 Clinical Knowledge examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1816-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Observational analysis of item-level response logs from 27,830 examinees on the Step 2 CK examination, block four. Of 18,936 examinees who changed at least one response, "44.7% increased their scores and 27.8% lowered their scores"; the remaining 27.5% had unchanged scores.
