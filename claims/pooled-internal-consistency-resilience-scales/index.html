q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of internal consistency coefficients (log-transformed, REML) from 42 estimates in the systematic review. The pooled estimate was "high" with a wide prediction interval, and heterogeneity was very high (τ² = 0.36, I² = 99.9%).
