Evidence¶

Yavuz Akpinar 2018¶

Yavuz Akpinar, Özlem Şengül. (2018). Two Modalities of the Contextualized Courseware in Three Modalities of Classroom Use. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165740

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANOVA on CTD progress (posttest minus pretest) across the four conditions in the quasi-experiment. The article reports no significant difference among groups; equivalence was not tested.

"an ANOVA test [F(3,147)=1,936; p=0,126] revealed that the groups' CTD progress did not significantly differ"

Yavuz Akpinar 2018 (2)¶

Yavuz Akpinar, Özlem Şengül. (2018). Two Modalities of the Contextualized Courseware in Three Modalities of Classroom Use. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165740

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t tests on pre and post CTD scores per group. The article reports Hedge's g of 0,81 for the teacher-only group and 0,29 for the multiple-context-with-teacher group, both increases statistically meaningful.

"the increase in both CTD scores of the student group (D) who studied the unit with a teacher only (Mpre=89,825, SD=10,007; Mpost=98,00, SD=12,798; t(39)=3,431, p=0,001), and of the student group (A) who studied the unit with a multiple context courseware and a teacher (M pre=93,475, SD =19,098; Mpost = 98,50, SD =15,165; t(39)=2,229, p=0,032) were statistically meaningful"
