> "TC's high school students, who receive regular and repeated individual and whole-group feedback as part of instruction using InkSurvey, have gained traction on mastery of fundamental concepts in Algebra I, as measured by standards-based assessments and in comparison to students who were not exposed to the InkSurvey intervention"
