`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

One-way MANOVA across the 125-student survey's descriptive characteristics (Table 3). Female students were "found to have higher scores in both core competency, and medical error tendency (p < 0.001)"; higher METSN scores indicate lower error tendency.

> "The results of the one-way MANOVA revealed a significant difference in core competency, and medical error tendency based on gender (Wilks' Lambda (λ) = 0.857; F = 10.206; p < 0.001; Partial η2=0.143)."
