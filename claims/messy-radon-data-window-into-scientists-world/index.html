Evidence¶

Ballard 2023¶

Ballard, H. L. (2023). Community and citizen science in schools: nested inquiries, productive tensions, and the role of communities. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09643-7

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The commentary reports, citing Aridor et al. (this issue), a case study of two middle school students navigating uncertainty in science while collecting Radon Concentration Levels data and exploring the data with scientists across a series of lessons. This is the commentary's interpretation of that case.

"The sheer statistical messiness of the Radon data, far from the canned datasets that middle school students are typically presented with in textbooks, offered students a window into the scientists' world and a uniquely intense experience with scientific reasoning practices."
