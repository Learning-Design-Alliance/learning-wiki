## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Δθ and g correlate strongly (r = 0.93), but each value of g corresponds to a range of Δθ values approximately 1 logit or more wide, so g may not be informative about individual students' learning gains. [→ Colin S. Wallace 2017](#colin-s-wallace-2017)

## Evidence

### Colin S. Wallace 2017

Colin S. Wallace, Timothy G. Chambers, Edward E. Prather. (2017). An item response theory evaluation of the Light and Spectroscopy Concept Inventory national data set. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1709.05255](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1709.05255)

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Plot of Δθ versus g for all 3205 students in the LSCI national data set (Fig. 9). The article reports "r = 0.93" and that each g value maps to a Δθ range "approximately 1 logit or more wide", concluding g "may not be as informative an indicator of the learning gains of individual students".

> "There is a deﬁnite correlation between the two measures (r = 0.93). But note that each value of g corresponds to a range of values of ∆θ approximately 1 logit or more wide."
