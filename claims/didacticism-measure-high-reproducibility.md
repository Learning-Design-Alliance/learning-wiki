## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The computer-based didacticism measure showed near-perfect inter-rater reproducibility with an intraclass coefficient of 0.97 (p < 0.0001). [→ Wojewodka G 2017](#wojewodka-g-2017)

## Evidence

### Wojewodka G 2017

Wojewodka G, Hurley S, Taylor SJC, Noble AJ, Ridsdale L, Goldstein LH. (2017). Implementation fidelity of a self-management course for epilepsy: method and assessment. BMC Medical Research Methodology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12874-017-0373-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of the novel didacticism measure, calculated with ELAN annotation software as facilitator speech seconds divided by module duration, rated by two independent raters. The article reports "a coefficient of 0.97 (p < 0.0001)".

> "The results from both raters were highly correlated with a coefficient of 0.97 (p < 0.0001), thus demonstrating the high reproducibility of this method."
