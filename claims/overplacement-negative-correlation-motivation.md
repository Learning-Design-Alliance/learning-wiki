## Evidence

### Bansbach Joachim 2024

Bansbach Joachim, Bentele Michael, Bollinger Matthias, Bentele Stefanie, Langenhan Ronny, Gerber Bianka, Trifunovic-Koenig Milena, Bushuven Stefan. (2024). Self-assessment and learning motivation in emergency point-of-care ultrasound: an online pilot investigation in German physicians. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-024-01154-z

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation analysis (BCa bootstrapped) in the cross-sectional survey testing hypothesis 2. The article reports "r = − 0.41" for both intrinsic motivation and identified regulation and "r = 0.28" for amotivation.

> "Relative self-assessment exhibited a moderate negative correlation with intrinsic motivation (r = − 0.41, p < 0.001) and identified regulation (r = − 0.41, p < 0.001), as well as a weak positive correlation with amotivation (r = 0.28, p = 0.01)."
