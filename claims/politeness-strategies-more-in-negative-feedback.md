## Subclaims

## Evidence

### van Sassen C 2025

van Sassen C, Mamede S, van den Broek W, Bindels P, Zwaan L. (2025). Does Knowledge of Clinical Case Outcome Influence Supervisor Evaluation of Resident Clinical Reasoning? Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000006122

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on politeness strategy use in the 62-supervisor vignette experiment: valence had a large effect (η² = 0.59); vignette outcome did not affect politeness use (F1,61 = 0.15, P = .70), and the interaction was nonsignificant.

> "feedback valence had a significant impact (F1,61 = 87.84, P < .001, η2 = 0.59). Specifically, the proportion of feedback using politeness strategies was higher in negative feedback than in positive feedback (mean [SE], 0.50 [0.04] vs 0.15 [0.02]; P < .001)."
