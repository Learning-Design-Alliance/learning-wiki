`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup comparison within the pre-post study of 207 medical officers (29% PHC, 60.9% CHC, 8.7% tertiary). The article reports the descriptive stratified pattern and attributes it to "greater baseline exposure to liver disease management"; no subgroup test statistics are printed.

> "When stratified by facility type, medical officers at tertiary centres exhibited the highest mean post-test scores, followed by those at CHCs and PHCs. Although all groups demonstrated statistically significant improvement, the gains were more pronounced at the secondary and tertiary levels, likely reflecting greater baseline exposure to liver disease management."
