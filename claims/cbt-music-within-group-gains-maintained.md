# Within the experimental group, test anxiety drops significantly from pretest to posttest and pretest to follow-up, with no significant change between posttest and follow-up

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Paired comparisons in the experimental group show significant pretest-to-posttest and pretest-to-follow-up reductions, and no significant posttest-to-follow-up difference. [→ Agboeze MU 2020](#agboeze-mu-2020)

## Evidence

### Agboeze MU 2020

Agboeze MU, Ugwuanyi CS, Okeke CI, Ugwu GC, Obikwelu CL, Obiozor EE, Oyigbo DN, Mbam D. (2020). Efficacy of music-based cognitive behavior therapy on the management of test-taking behavior of children in basic science using a randomized trial group: Implication for community development. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000021535

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests for the experimental group (n = 27). Pretest-to-posttest (t (26) = 8.849, P < .050) and pretest-to-follow-up (t (26) = 8.834, P < .050) differences were significant; the posttest-to-follow-up difference of "M =  −0.15" was not significant, indicating maintained gains.

> "However, the mean test anxiety rating difference of the experimental group participants at the posttest and follow-up measure (M =  −0.15, SD = 1.29) is not significant, t (26) = −0.851, P = .399."
