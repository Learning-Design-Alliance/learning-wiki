## Evidence

### Seyed Parsa Neshaei 2026

Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Richard Lee Davis, and Tanja Käser. (2026). Moving Beyond Review: Applying Language Models to Planning and Translation in Reflection. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_6)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Depth-rubric analysis in the 93-student study: connection-making increased while support was present but "the effect did not persist to the delayed post-test without support".

> "Connectionscores increased from Pre to Tool in both groups (TG:0.00to0.12; p=.0005; CG:0.01to0.14;p=.0003), but returned to near zero at Post (TG: 0.02; CG:0.01)"

### Seyed Parsa Neshaei 2026 (2)

Seyed Parsa Neshaei, Richard Lee Davis, and Tanja Käser. (2026). Moving Beyond Review: Applying Language Models to Planning and Translation in Reflection. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_6)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Metacognition rubric scores across stages in the same mixed-effects analysis; the delayed-post gain was significant in the static-scaffold group but not the AI-supported group, a scope limit the article reports.

> "From Pre to Post, the improvement was smaller: while both groups had an increased mean, and the effect was significant in CG (from0.21to0.32;p=.016), it was insignificant in TG (from0.20to0.22;p=.692)"
