### Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer 2007

Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer. (2007). Student Behavior and Epistemological Framing: Examples from Collaborative Active-Learning Activities in Physics. Submitted to Cognition and Instruction. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Student+Behavior+and+Epistemological+Framing

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative analysis of the TA-arrival segment in group A's second episode. Students stopped talking, oriented to the TA, and their minimal speech "appears to be entirely aimed at acknowledging the TA's speech," supporting the listening-to-the-TA framing interpretation.

> "Both behavior and speech indicate that they are framing the activity as listening to the TA."
