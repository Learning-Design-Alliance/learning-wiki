## Evidence

### Kawasoe 2026

Kawasoe, R., Matsumura, K., Shinohara, T., Arima, K., Takeo, Y., Ikeda, T. & Sugata, H. (2026). Wakeful targeted memory reactivation during short rest periods modulates early motor learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00407-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc Bonferroni-corrected comparisons following a repeated-measures ANOVA in the randomized experiment, with 16 participants each in the TMRfast and TMRregular groups after exclusions. The early-phase advantage of the 1.3x-speed cue group reached significance ("p = 0.007").

> "post-hoc comparisons demonstrated that, the TMRfast group showed significantly greater total learning gains than the TMRregular group during the early learning phase (p = 0.007, Fig. 3B and Table S 2)"
