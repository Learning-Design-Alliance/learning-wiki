## Evidence

### Qingyang Zhang 2025

Qingyang Zhang, Rose E. Wang, Ana T. Ribeiro, Dora Demszky, Susanna Loeb. (2025). Educator Attention: How computational tools can systematically identify the distribution of a key resource for students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.20135](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.20135)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 3's EL-status analysis finds lower-achieving EL students receive more attention in mixed EL pairs, following the expected pattern, but in EL-EL pairs "the lower-achieving EL student receives 4.6 p.p. less attention than their higher-achieving EL peers" (t(740) = −5.56, p <0.001), especially in content and relationship-building.

> "However, in pairs where both students have EL status, this trend reverses: the lower-achieving EL student receives 4.6 p.p. less attention than their higher-achieving EL peers (t(740) = −5.56, p <0.001)."
