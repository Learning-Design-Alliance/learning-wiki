Harsh Kumar 2026¶

Harsh Kumar, Zi Kang (Jace) Mu, Jonathan Vincentius, Ashton Anderson. (2026). Human Thinking under Plural LLM Assistance: Mathematical Problem Solving and Open-Ended Writing. Working Paper, July 2026, Toronto, Canada. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02677 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.02677

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Exploratory pairwise chi-square tests on unassisted test accuracy in the pre-registered Experiment-1 (315 analyzed participants), comparing each agent arrangement against the no-agent Control condition; the quote reports both printed test statistics and p-values.

"the tutor effect was significant (Control vs. Tutor Only: 𝜒 2(1)= 8.27,𝑝=. 004), while the peers effect alone did not reach significance (Control vs. Peers Only: 𝜒 2(1)= 0.88,𝑝=. 347)."
