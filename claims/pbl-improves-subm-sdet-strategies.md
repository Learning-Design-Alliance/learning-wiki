## Evidence

### Lin 2017

Lin, L.-F. (2017). Impacts of the Problem-based Learning Pedagogy on English Learners’ Reading Comprehension, Strategy Use, and Active Learning Attitudes. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 5(6). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i6.2320

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent sample t tests on post-test strategy subscores (PBL n = 26, non-PBL n = 30). SUBM means were 5.27 vs 4.17 and SDET means 5.35 vs 4.63, both favoring the PBL group. No effect size is printed.

> "the t-test results also revealed that there were significant differences between the two groups’ application of RC strategies, such as the SUBM (t = 3.567, p < .05), and the SDET (t = 2.086, p < .05) strategies."
