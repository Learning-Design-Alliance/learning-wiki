### Fries 2017

Fries, M.-H. (2017). Teaching compound nouns in ESP: insights from cognitive semantics. In C. Sarré & S. Whyte (Eds), New developments in ESP teaching and learning research (pp. 93-107). Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2017.cssw2017.747

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative analysis of the slideshow corpus prepared for internship presentations by the two groups of eight students. The author interprets standalone CN use in bullet points as a compensation strategy allowing students to avoid whole sentences in written English.

> "In the experimental group, almost half of the CNs were used on their own in a bullet point, whereas roughly three-quarters of the CNs written on the control group’s slides were included in whole sentences."
