## Evidence

### Graf Joachim 2020

Graf Joachim, Loda Teresa, Zipfel Stephan, Wosnik Annette, Mohr Daniela, Herrmann-Werner Anne. (2020). Communication skills of medical students: survey of self- and external perception in a longitudinally based trend study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02049-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mean value analysis of the combined four communication dimensions for self- and external perception in each of seven semesters (1027 students; 8484 SP rating sheets). The analysis found that "the difference between the two widened over time"; no effect size is printed.

> "The self-perception of communication skills improved, while the external perception of these skills worsened. Overall, the difference between the two widened over time."
