# Learning style (visual-verbal) shows no significant correlation with the effectiveness of pictorial elucidation for adolescent EFL learners

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i0` negligible

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Among pictorial-group students, learning style scores correlated nonsignificantly with immediate posttest scores (r = .08) and delayed posttest scores (r = .01). [→ Lee 2017](#lee-2017)

## Evidence

### Lee 2017

Lee, Sunjung. (2017). Raising EFL learners' awareness of L2 lexical errors and correct usage: A dual coding approach. English Teaching, 72(2), 29-50. https://doi.org/10.15858/engtea.72.2.201706.29

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations using the validated Index of Learning Styles visual-verbal dimension (scored -11 to +11, α = .76) as a continuous variable found negligible, nonsignificant associations: "r = .08" immediate and "r = .01" delayed.

> "for the pictorial group, there was a nonsignificant correlation between learning style and immediate posttest scores (r = .08). There was also a nonsignificant correlation between learning style and delayed posttest scores (r = .01)."
