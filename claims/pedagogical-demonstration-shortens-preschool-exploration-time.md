## Evidence

### Bonawitz Elizabeth 2011

Bonawitz Elizabeth, Shafto Patrick, Gweon Hyowon, Goodman Noah D., Spelke Elizabeth, Schulz Laura. (2011). The Double-edged Sword of Pedagogy: Instruction limits spontaneous exploration and discovery. Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2010.10.001

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 1, a four-condition between-subjects study with 85 preschoolers (mean age 58 months) in a science museum, tested free play with a novel toy after pedagogical, interrupted, naive, or no demonstration. The planned linear contrast showed "significantly less time" playing in the Pedagogical condition, F(1,81)=4.52, p<0.05.

> "Children in the Pedagogical condition played with the toy for significantly less time (M=119.2s) than children in the Interrupted (M=179.6s), Naïve (M=132.7s), or Baseline (M=205.7) conditions (F(1,81)=4.52, p<0.05)."
