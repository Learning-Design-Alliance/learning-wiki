## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Responses exceeding the 404-word median were associated with lower understanding, an effect that remained significant after controlling for elaboration and affective language (OR = 0.67). [→ Shayla Sharmin 2026](#shayla-sharmin-2026)

## Evidence

### Shayla Sharmin 2026

Shayla Sharmin, Mohammad Fahim Abrar, Roghayeh Leila Barmaki. (2026). Micro-level AI Feedback Features and Student Responses in Consecutive LLM Tutoring Interactions. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.08952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.08952)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

GEE logistic regression on confusion windows from naturalistic LLM tutoring logs, with long response defined as exceeding the 404-word median. The article reports "OR = 0.67, 95% CI [0.51, 0.90], p = .007" for understanding; length did not predict re-confusion.

> "This pattern remained significant in the GEE model (OR = 0.67, 95% CI [0.51, 0.90], p = .007). Response length was not an independent predictor of re-confusion (OR = 1.14, 95% CI [0.91, 1.42], p = .271)"
