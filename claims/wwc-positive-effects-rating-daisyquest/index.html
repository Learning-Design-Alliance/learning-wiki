DaisyQuest for Preschool Children 2006¶

DaisyQuest for Preschool Children. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The WWC's rating determination for the phonological processing domain. Criterion 1 was met because "Both studies met WWC evidence standards for a strong design and had statistically significant positive effects," and Criterion 2 was met because no statistically significant or substantively important negative effects were found.

"For the outcome domain of phonological processing, the WWC rated DaisyQuest as having positive effects."
