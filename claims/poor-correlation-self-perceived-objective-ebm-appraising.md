### Lai Nai Ming 2011

Lai Nai Ming, Teng Cheong Lieng. (2011). Self-perceived competence correlates poorly with objectively measured competence in Evidence Based Medicine among medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-25

Same cross-sectional study of 45 final-year Malaysian medical students. Pearson correlation between self-rated appraising competence and adapted Fresno test scores was r = 0.24 (p = 0.1), not statistically significant.

> "In the domain of "appraising the evidence", the correlation coefficient between the self-rating and test performances was 0.24 (p = 0.1)."
