Evidence¶

Zia Ud Din 2024¶

Zia Ud Din, Payam Mohammad, Rachael Sherman. (2024). A systematic review and analysis of the viability of virtual reality (VR) in construction work and education. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5059219

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review reports, citing Maghool et al. [29], that the DroneSim VR simulator enhanced construction students' UAV operational and flight training skills as "an efficient alternative to traditional real -world inspection training". No effect size is printed.

"The findings revealed that DroneSim, representing a virtual training group, acted as an efficient alternative to traditional real -world inspection training, offering comparable, if not superior, results [29]."
