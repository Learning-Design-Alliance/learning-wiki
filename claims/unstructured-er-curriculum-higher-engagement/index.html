Chrysanthos Socratous 2021¶

Chrysanthos Socratous, Andri Ioannou. (2021). Structured or unstructured educational robotics curriculum? A study of debugging in block-based programming. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10056-x

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Engagement in the learning process was the third outcome in the quasi-experiment with two comparison groups (n = 35); the article reports the unstructured group "outperform their peers in terms of engagement levels". No effect size is printed.

"Yet, the students in the unstructured ER curriculum group outperform their peers in terms of engagement levels."
