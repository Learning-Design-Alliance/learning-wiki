`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview-based regional comparison across grassroots organizations from Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, and South America. The article reports that Global South organizations spend time raising awareness and educating decision-makers, and recruit members with government and business expertise.

> "Most organizations address students of various ages as well as academics and professionals who want to learn, but in countries with lower investments and awareness of QT, the target groups are shifted toward decision-makers in academia, business, and governments."
