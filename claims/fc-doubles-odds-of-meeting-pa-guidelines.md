Generalized linear mixed model on the binary IPAQ-SF-derived indicator (≥150 min/week MVPA), adjusted for baseline PA, sex, BMI, and class-level clustering. The odds ratio of 1.87 indicates flipped students were nearly twice as likely to achieve recommended activity levels.

> "The odds of meeting PA guidelines were 1.87 times higher in FC group than control (95% CI [1.21, 2.89], p = 0.004)."
