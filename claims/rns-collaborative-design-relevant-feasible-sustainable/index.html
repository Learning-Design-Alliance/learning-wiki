Evidence¶

Joke Voogt 2015¶

Joke Voogt, Therese Laferrière, Alain Breuleux, Rebecca C. Itow, Daniel T. Hickey, Susan McKenney (2015). Collaborative design as a form of professional development. Instr Sci (2015) 43:259-282. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-014-9340-7

q2 · i? · design · r2

Design-based research over several iterations of the RNS initiative in Quebec (three school districts and 12 schools in the two-year pilot; over 300 schools engaged overall), drawing on questionnaires, interviews, and online artefacts. The conclusion is the authors' qualitative judgment; no effect sizes are printed.

"Despite these limitations, teacher collaborative design was the result of teachers' exercise of agency and leadership as manifested by boundary crossing, artefacts, stories, and new routines or rituals. Based on these results teacher collaborative design proved to be relevant, feasible, and sustainable."
