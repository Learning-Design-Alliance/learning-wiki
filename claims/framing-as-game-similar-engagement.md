### Romero 2019

Romero, M. (2019). Computers in Secondary Schools: Educational Games. In Encyclopedia of Education and Information Technologies. Springer. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-60013-0_31-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-60013-0_31-1)

The chapter reports, citing Lieberoth (2015), that in some contexts "framing an activity as a game" produced engagement and psychological relation to the activity similar to using game mechanics. The claim is hedged to some contexts and no effect sizes are printed.

> "In some contexts, “framing an activity as a game” has induced the learner to a similar engagement and psychological relation to the activity as using game mechanics (Lieberoth, 2015)."
