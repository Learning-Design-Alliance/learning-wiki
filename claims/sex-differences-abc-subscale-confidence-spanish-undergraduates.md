## Evidence

### Sander Paul 2020

Sander Paul, de la Fuente Jesús. (2020). Undergraduate Student Gender, Personality and Academic Confidence. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 17(15). https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17155567

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Sex-comparison t-tests on the combined Spanish undergraduate dataset (Table 4) compared male and female students on the four ABC subscales. The article reports the differences were "statistically significant, with small effect sizes", and in the Discussion characterises the verbalising difference as "a medium effect size".

> "the female students as more confident in their grades, in studying and in attending, but less confident than the male students in verbalising. All of these differences are statistically significant, with small effect sizes"
