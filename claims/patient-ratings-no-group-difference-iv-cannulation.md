## Evidence

### Lund Frederike 2012

Lund Frederike, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik, Maatouk Imad, Krautter Markus, Möltner Andreas, Werner Anne, Weyrich Peter, Jünger Jana, Nikendei Christoph. (2012). Effectiveness of IV Cannulation Skills Laboratory Training and Its Transfer into Clinical Practice: A Randomized, Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0032831

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Patient-rating outcome of the randomized trial: volunteer students acting as patients assessed each student once with a modified Communication Assessment Tool and a modified IPPI. No significant group difference was found, "p = 0.544 for CAT" and "p = 0.683 for modified IPPI ratings"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Rated by volunteer students acting as patients, students' procedural performance and patient-physician communication did not differ significantly between groups (p = 0.544 for CAT [42]; p = 0.683 for modified IPPI ratings [41]) as shown in Table 3."
