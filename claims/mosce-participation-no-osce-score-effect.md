`q3 i?` MOSCE participation had no influence on mean OSCE scores (P=0.19), with no cohort difference (P=0.60) and no cohort-by-participation interaction (P=0.57). [→ Madrazo Lorenzo 2019](#madrazo-lorenzo-2019)

## Evidence

### Madrazo Lorenzo 2019

Madrazo Lorenzo, Lee Claire Bo, McConnell Meghan, Khamisa Karima, Pugh Debra. (2019). No observed effect of a student-led mock objective structured clinical examination on subsequent performance scores in medical students in Canada. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2019.16.14

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Between-subjects ANOVA on OSCE scores of 330 M3 students (172 from 2016, 158 from 2017), with cohort and MOSCE participation as independent variables. "participation in the MOSCE had no influence on the mean OSCE score (P=0.19)", with no cohort effect or interaction. Equivalence was not tested.

> "participation in the MOSCE had no influence on the mean OSCE score (P=0.19). There was also no difference in mean OSCE scores as a function of student cohort (P=0.60), nor was there an interaction between cohort and MOSCE group (P=0.57)."
