## Evidence

### Hughes 2018

Hughes, J. N., West, S. G., Kim, H., & Bauer, S. S. (2018). Effect of Early Grade Retention on School Completion: A Prospective Study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7), 974-991. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000243

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Same prospective multinomial logistic regression analysis with logistic-regression propensity score weights, contrasting GED receipt against diploma completion. The contrast "did not attain statistical significance"; the printed odds ratio of 2.61 was not significant, so no effect size is claimed.

> "The contrast between students who received a GED versus completed high school did not attain statistical significance, estimate = 0.96, SE = 0.67, z = 1.43, p = .15, odds ratio = 2.61."
