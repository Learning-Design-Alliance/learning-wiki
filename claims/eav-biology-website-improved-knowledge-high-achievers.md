# The e-AV Biology website significantly improved biology knowledge for high achievers compared with conventional teaching

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` High achievers using the e-AV Biology website scored significantly higher on the biology knowledge post-test than high achievers receiving conventional teaching. [→ Nugraini 2013](#nugraini-2013)

## Evidence

### Nugraini 2013

Nugraini, S. H., Choo, K. A., Hin, H. S., & Hoon, T. S. (2013). Impact of e-AV Biology Website for Learning About Renewable Energy. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1015407

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing high achievers in the experiment group (n=102) and control group (n=103) on a biology knowledge post-test. The experiment group's higher post-test mean of 77.46 versus 72.96 was statistically significant, indicating the website benefited high achievers' knowledge.

> "Their mean scores were 77.46 and 72.96 respectively. Descriptively, the experiment group obtain a higher mean score post-test compared to the control group. The result shows that there is a significant difference on Biology knowledge post-test between the two groups at p-value = .005 (p< .05)."
