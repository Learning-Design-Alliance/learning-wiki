## Evidence

### Emily Buchanan 2015

Emily Buchanan, Jack Worth, Helen Aston. (2015). Tutor Trust Primary: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581540

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental evaluation comparing tutored pupils with propensity-weighted comparison pupils from the same secondary-school cohorts using weighted linear regression. The report's results table prints effect sizes of -0.08 (English, all pupils) and -0.04 (maths, all pupils), described as "marginally less progress than pupils in the comparison group".

> "On average, participating pupils, including those eligible for free school meals, made marginally less progress than pupils in the comparison group. However, the differences in outcomes were not statistically significant, meaning that they could have occurred by chance."
