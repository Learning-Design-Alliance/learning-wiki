Evidence¶

Malmberg 2022¶

Malmberg, J., Haataja, E., & Järvelä, S. (2022). Exploring the connection between task difficulty, task perceptions, physiological arousal and learning outcomes in collaborative learning situations. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09320-z

q3 · i? · associational · r1

GEE models predicting quiz scores (mean 4.23, Std 1.26) from perceptions and arousal; Table 2 shows post-task understanding Wald χ2 = 9.710, p = 0.002, pre-task difficulty p = 0.025, post-task difficulty p = 0.002.

"The results of the GEE indicate that the student’s improved perceptions of task understanding after the collaborative task positively predicted the learning outcomes (p < .005). In addition, the level of physiological arousal predicts the students’ learning outcomes (p = < 0.000)."

Related Claims¶

  • Students perceive collaborative physics tasks as less difficult after completing them
  • Physiological arousal is higher in sessions where the task is perceived as less difficult at the end
  • Higher task difficulty ratings before and after collaborative tasks predict higher physiological arousal, while task understanding does not
  • Students' monitoring frequency during the collaborative exam was significantly and positively correlated with their number of EDA peaks. — related
  • Higher equality of participation in monitoring interactions and more monitoring with positive valence predict better collaborative task performance — related
