## Evidence

### Alhamed 2023

Alhamed, A. A. (2023). The link among academic stress, sleep disturbances, depressive symptoms, academic performance, and the moderating role of resourcefulness in health professions students during COVID-19 pandemic. Journal of Professional Nursing, 46, 83-91. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.profnurs.2023.02.010

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the regression analysis (n = 93), "the regression coefficient (standardized beta) of academic stress dropped from b = 0.603 to b = 0.359 while remaining significant", and mediation assumptions were met (academic stress predicted sleep disturbances, b = 0.435***).

> "when sleep disturbances were added to the model, the regression coefficient (standardized beta) of academic stress dropped from b = 0.603 to b = 0.359 while remaining significant, which suggested a possible partial mediation effect for sleep disturbances."
