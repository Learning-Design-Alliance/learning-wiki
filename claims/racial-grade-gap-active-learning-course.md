One-way ANOVAs across racial/ethnic groups found only final grade significant, with white students (M = 81.6) higher than underrepresented minority students (M = 76.5).

> "only final course grade, which was higher for white students (M = 81.6, SD = 10.91) than for underrepresented minority students (M = 76.5, SD = 12.95), was found to be statistically significant (F(1,231) = 6.79, p = 0.01)."
