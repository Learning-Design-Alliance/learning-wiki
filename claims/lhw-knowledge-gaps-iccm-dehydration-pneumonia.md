### Rabbani Fauziah 2016

Rabbani Fauziah, Perveen Shagufta, Aftab Wafa, Zahidie Aysha, Sangrasi Kashif, Qazi Shamim Ahmad. (2016). Health workers’ perspectives, knowledge and skills regarding community case management of childhood diarrhoea and pneumonia: a qualitative inquiry for an implementation research project “Nigraan” in District Badin, Sindh, Pakistan. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 14 FGDs and 20 IDIs with 108 LHWs and LHSs in district Badin found that "classification of dehydration, correctly preparing ORS and prescribing correct antibiotics" were knowledge gaps; none mentioned zinc supplementation or dehydration grading.
