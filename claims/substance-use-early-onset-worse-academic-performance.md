## Evidence

### Sánchez-Hernando 2021

Sánchez-Hernando, B.; Antón-Solanas, I.; Juárez-Vela, R.; Gea-Caballero, V.; Carboneres-Tafaner, M.I.; Ferrer-Gracia, E.; Gállego-Diéguez, J.; Santolalla-Arnedo, I.; Gasch-Gallén, Á. (2021). Healthy Lifestyle and Academic Performance in Middle School Students from the Region of Aragón (Spain). Int. J. Environ. Res. Public Health 18(16): 8624. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18168624

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey; Table 5 reports Kendall tau = −0.075 (hashish) to tau = −0.182 (age of alcohol onset), all p < 0.001; the largest printed effect size is tau = −0.182 for age of alcohol-use onset, with contrast tests (e.g., tobacco users mean 5.85 vs non-users 6.75). The authors note other studies did not establish a clear tobacco/alcohol association.

> "Specifically, the students who consumed tobacco, alcohol and other substances, namely cocaine, hashish, LSD, and glue, obtained worse academic results than those who did not consume them. In addition, abstinence from using tobacco, alcohol, and other substances was linked to better academic performance in general, and early exposure to these substances was linked to worse academic results."
