da Silva Ruana Luiz Ferreira; Oliveira-Kumakura Ana Railka de Souza. (2026). External ventricular drainage management in pediatrics: a simulated scenario for rapid-cycle deliberate practice. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0253

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Scenario testing with five sixth-semester nursing students in a Skills and Simulation Laboratory, using a 23-action checklist to interrupt scenes and give feedback until excellence was achieved. The article reports "No modifications were needed to the setup after testing."

> "The simulated scenario was performed simultaneously by all five participants, with a total duration of 15 minutes. During the scenario, three brief pauses were taken to provide immediate directive feedback to participants, allowing for quick adjustments followed by short repetitions of the scene until correct execution in cycles that did not exceed five minutes each"
