A study of 57 adult Hungarian-speaking immigrants used a grammaticality judgment test to compare late arrivals with child arrivals. The article reports that "very few adult immigrants scored within the range of child arrivals" and that those few had high verbal analytical ability.

> "A study with 57 adult Hungarian-speaking immigrants confirmed the hypothesis in the sense that very few adult immigrants scored within the range of child arrivals on a grammaticality judgment test, and that the few who did had high levels of verbal analytical ability"
