# Parent and youth economic perceptions are selectively linked: needs worries align, parent wants worries relate to lower youth SSS, but parent and youth SSS are unrelated

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Parent needs stress marginally predicted youth needs stress (β = .35, p = .06); lower parent SSS predicted higher youth needs stress (β = −.21, p = .05); parent wants stress predicted lower youth SSS (β = −.38, p = .04); parent and youth SSS were not significantly related. ## Evidence

### Mistry 2021

Mistry, R. S., & Elenbaas, L. (2021). It’s All in the Family: Parents’ Economic Worries and Youth’s Perceptions of Financial Stress and Educational Outcomes. Journal of Youth and Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10964-021-01393-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct-effects section of the full-sample SEM: "parents’ stress about meeting their family’s needs was marginally associated with youth stress about needs, β = .35, p = .06". A second quote reports lower parent SSS predicted higher youth needs stress (β = −.21, p = .05) and parent wants stress predicted lower youth SSS (β = −.38, p = .04).

> "parents’ stress about meeting their family’s needs was marginally associated with youth stress about needs, β = .35, p = .06, but unrelated to youth stress about wants, β = .25, p = .17, or youth SSS, β = .17, p = .42."
