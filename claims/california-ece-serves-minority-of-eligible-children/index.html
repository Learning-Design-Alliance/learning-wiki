Evidence¶

Melnick 2018¶

Melnick, H., Meloy, B., Gardner, M., Wechsler, M., & Maier, A. (2018). Building an early learning system that works: Next steps for California. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/early-learning-ca

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Report's executive summary and access section quantify unmet need across California: of nearly 1 million eligible children birth to age 5, "just 33% of those eligible were served," and only 14% of infants and toddlers receive subsidized care, with ECE a small share of state spending.

"In 2015–16, 1 million California children qualified for subsidized ECE, yet just 33% of those eligible were served. Funding for ECE represents a remarkably small portion of total state spending: Only an estimated 1.8% of California’s budget went to subsidized child care and preschool in the 2017–18 budget."
