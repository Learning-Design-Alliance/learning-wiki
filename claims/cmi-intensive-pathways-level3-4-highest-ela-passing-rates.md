# Students placed into Level 3 and Level 4 intensive pathways had the highest first-attempt passing rates in credit-bearing ELA courses, while Level 2 intensive pathway students had the lowest

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students initially placed into Level 3 and Level 4 intensive pathways had the highest passing rates on credit-bearing ELA courses on their first attempt; Level 2 placements, particularly the Level 2 intensive pathway, had the lowest passing rates. [→ Shannon 2026](#shannon-2026)
`q2 i?` For English 105, 86 percent of Level 4 intensive pathway students passed on the first attempt versus 63 percent of Level 4 regular pathway students; for English 111, 88 percent versus 50 percent. [→ Shannon 2026](#shannon-2026)

Descriptive analysis of first-attempt passing rates by initial placement level and pathway, with chi-square tests significant at p < .05 across pathway placements. The report states "86 percent of students in the Level 4 intensive pathway in their first year passed the course on their first attempt, compared with 63 percent" in the regular pathway.

> "For credit- bearing English 105, 86 percent of students in the Level 4 intensive pathway in their first year passed the course on their first attempt, compared with 63 percent of students in the Level 4 regular pathway."
