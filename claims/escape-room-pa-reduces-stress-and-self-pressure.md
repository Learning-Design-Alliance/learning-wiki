## Evidence

### Latino Francesca; Tafuri Domenico; Tafuri Francesco 2025

Latino Francesca; Tafuri Domenico; Tafuri Francesco. (2025). Academic Self-Pressure and Physiological Responses in Adolescents: A Pilot Experimental Study on the Moderating Role of an Escape Room-Based Physical Activity Intervention on Cognitive and Academic Outcomes. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22060948

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon pre-post comparison within the 16-week pilot RCT. The experimental group's PSS-10 reduction was significant; the control group's was not, with "No significant changes were detected in the control group".

> "A significant reduction in perceived stress was observed in the experimental group (W = 9.0, p < 0.001), indicating a positive effect of the intervention on students' psychological well-being. No significant changes were detected in the control group (W = 106.0, p = 0.13)"

### Latino Francesca; Tafuri Domenico; Tafuri Francesco 2025 (2)

Latino Francesca; Tafuri Domenico; Tafuri Francesco. (2025). Academic Self-Pressure and Physiological Responses in Adolescents: A Pilot Experimental Study on the Moderating Role of an Escape Room-Based Physical Activity Intervention on Cognitive and Academic Outcomes. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22060948

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-test on the AESI Expectations of Self subscale in the experimental group (n = 25) of the pilot RCT; the control group showed no significant difference (t(24) = -0.89, p = 0.38).

> "The experimental group showed a significant reduction in self-imposed academic pressure after the intervention (t(24) = 4.48, p < 0.001), confirming the effectiveness of physical activity in alleviating stress related to academic expectations"
