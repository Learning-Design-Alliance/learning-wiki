From the Computer Science teacher-capabilities case study: the disciplinary expert argues teaching and assessment must shift to "computational thinking and computer science theories"; this is the participant's stated position, not a tested result.

"the introduction of AI programming means teachers cannot rely on asking students to solve programming problems that AI can do, instead they need to focus on computational thinking and computer science theories when teaching and assessing students"
