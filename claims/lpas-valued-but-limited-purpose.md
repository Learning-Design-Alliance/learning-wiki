### Hestenes 1997

Hestenes, D. & Jackson, J. (1997). Partnerships for Physics Teaching Reform — a crucial role for universities and colleges. Proceedings of the International Conference on Undergraduate Physics Education, College Park, August 1996. [doi:10.1063/1.53143](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.53143)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The authors' assessment of the APS Local Physics Alliance program, based on surveys showing members value the LPA and read its newsletter. They judge that "none has come close to realizing its full potential, largely because they have a limited sense of purpose." This is the authors' evaluative interpretation of survey and observational evidence, not a tested comparison.

> "Although some LPAs are very popular, none has come close to realizing its full potential, largely because they have a limited sense of purpose, but also because of meager support by the physics community."
