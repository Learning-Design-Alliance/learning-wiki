## Evidence

### Dünya Baradari 2025

Dünya Baradari, Nataliya Kosmyna, Oscar Petrov, Rebecah Kaplun, and Pattie Maes. 2025. NeuroChat: A Neuroadaptive AI Chatbot for Customizing Learning Experiences. In Proceedings of the 7th ACM Conference on Conversational User Interfaces (CUI '25). https://doi.org/10.1145/3719160.3736623

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Thematic analysis of interviews showing contrasting preferences: eight participants favored the control chatbot's structured style, while six "expressed a preference for NeuroChat's more accessible, conversational tone". Qualitative counts only; no effect size.

> "Eight participants (P7, P10, P13, P14, P17, P19, P24, P26), especially those who identified with STEM or analytic learning styles, explicitly framed the control chatbot's more structured, "bland, " "textbook-style" response style as more aligned with their preferred mode of learning."
