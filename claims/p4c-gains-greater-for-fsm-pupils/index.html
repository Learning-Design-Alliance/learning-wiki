Evidence¶

Gorard et al. 2015¶

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui and Beng Huat See. (2015). Philosophy for Children: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581147

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Pre-specified subgroup analysis of the same school-level randomised trial, restricted to pupils eligible for free school meals. The report's results table prints FSM effect sizes of +0.20 (maths, +3 months), +0.29 (reading, +4 months) and +0.17 (writing, +2 months) for P4C versus comparison FSM pupils.

"It is also important to note that the gains in KS2 were greater in all subjects for students eligible for free school meals (FSM)."
