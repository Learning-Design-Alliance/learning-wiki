`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup results by SES quartile (Tables 2 and 3) use an SES index from principal component analysis of a household possession index and parental education. The article states the young bottom-quartile advantage "attenuates and reverses itself at older ages for white, black, and, to a lesser extent, Hispanic students, but not for Asian ones".

> "The advance for those from households in the lowest quartile of the socioeconomic status (SES) distribution is steeper than for those in the highest quartile among those tested at a younger age (difference in m: 0.08sd, in rd: 0.15sd)."
