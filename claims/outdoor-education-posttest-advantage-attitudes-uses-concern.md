## Evidence

### Derman et al. 2016

Aysegul Derman, Elvan Sahin, Esme Hacieminoglu. (2016). Does Outdoor Education Make any Difference in Environmental Literacy of Pre-service Classroom Teachers? INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL OF ENVIRONMENTAL & SCIENCE EDUCATION, 11(15), 8491-8506. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-tests on post-test scores of a true-experimental pretest-posttest study with 45 experimental and 54 control pre-service teachers (27 control at post-test). The article reports "statistically significant difference" on the three dimensions, "in favor of experimental group"; no effect sizes are printed.

> "When post test scores of experimental and control group were compared results revealed statistically significant difference between experimental and control group with respect to environmental attitudes (p<.05), environmental uses (p<.05), and environmental concern (p<.05) in favor of experimental group."
