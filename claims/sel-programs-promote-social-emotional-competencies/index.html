Evidence¶

Greenberg 2023¶

Greenberg, M. T. (2023). Evidence for social and emotional learning in schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/928.269

q3 · i2 · review · r2

The report's synthesis of 12 independently conducted meta-analyses of school-based SEL research, spanning studies from six continents and all grades PreK–12, reports that SEL programs promote competencies with "effect sizes range from 0.23 to 0.58".

"SEL programs, usually taught by classroom teachers, promote the development of social and emotional competencies (effect sizes range from 0.23 to 0.58)."
