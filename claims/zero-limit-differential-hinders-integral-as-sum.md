The article reports, citing Meredith and Marrongelle (2008), a study of students applying integration in electrostatics: "the notion of a limit going to zero can hinder students’ understanding of integrals as sums". The authors argue students need guided instruction to reinterpret mathematical concepts in physics contexts. No effect size is printed.
