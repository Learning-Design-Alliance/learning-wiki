Survey of 380 pre-service science teachers in Ghana using a structured questionnaire; Figure 1 reports tool frequencies. The article states "ChatGPT emerged as the most widely used tool, with 69% of respondents reporting frequent use"; Microsoft Copilot (25.4%) and other tools (26.8%) saw moderate use.
