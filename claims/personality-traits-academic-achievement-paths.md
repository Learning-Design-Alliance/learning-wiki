## Evidence

### Kvintova Jana 2024

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Czech path model coefficients: AASD ~ PCO B = 0.33 [0.27, 0.39], AASD ~ PEX B = 0.08, AASA ~ PEX B = 0.28 (all P < 0.05). Conscientiousness negatively predicted internet addiction (B = -0.13). No standardised effect size is printed for these paths.

> "Extraversion and Conscientiousness were positively associated with coping with academic demands and social adaptation, which are non-performance components of academic success."

### Kvintova Jana 2024 (2)

Kvintova Jana, Novotny Jan Sebastian, Liu Hongyang, Vachova Lucie, Kantor Jiri. (2024). Path analysis reveals cross-country differences between Czech and Chinese university students in effect of internet and smartphone addiction, mental health, and personality traits on academic achievement in the post-pandemic era. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02069-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chinese path model: AASA ~ PEX B = 0.15, AASA ~ PAG B = 0.11, AASD ~ PCO B = 0.22 (all P < 0.05). The article notes "higher Conscientiousness was positively related to better academic performance". No standardised effect size is printed for these paths.

> "Social adaptation was negatively related to anxiety and positively related to coping with academic demands, Extraversion, and Agreeableness, and, surprisingly, Internet addiction."
