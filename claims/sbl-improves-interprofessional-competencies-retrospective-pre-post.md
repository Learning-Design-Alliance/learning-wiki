## Evidence

### Gomwe Howard 2026

Gomwe Howard, Phiri Lesego, Marange Chioneso Show, Kgongwana Thokozile Harriet, Zikalala-Mabope Lindi, Mothapo Rahab, Dyani Xolelwa. (2026). Simulation-based learning enhances interprofessional competencies in undergraduate health students: a retrospective pre-post study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09125-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective pre-post study of N = 540 first-year health students using the ICCAS after a simulation-based interprofessional program; paired samples t-tests showed significant gains in all six domains, e.g. "communication scores increased significantly from a mean level of 2.65 (SD = 0.83) in the pre-test to 4.30 (SD = 0.53)". No effect sizes were reported, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "A paired samples t-test was conducted to compare participants' interprofessional collaborative competency levels before and after the intervention (see Table 3). The results indicate significant improvements across all competency domains (p < 0.001 for all comparisons)."
