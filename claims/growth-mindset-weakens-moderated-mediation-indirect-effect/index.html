Chen H (2026) When AI threatens my future career: growth mindset and perceived employability in Chinese college students' career decision making anxiety. Front. Psychol. 17:1933842. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1933842

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Moderated mediation analysis with 5,000 bootstrap samples in the 526-student survey: indirect effect significant at low mindset (0.069, CI [0.027, 0.119]) but nonsignificant at high mindset (0.004, CI [−0.033, 0.042]); the index of moderated mediation was significant (Index = −0.037, CI [−0.075, −0.008]).

"the indirect eﬀect of AI employment threat perception on career decision-making anxiety through perceived employability was signiﬁcant under low growth mindset, Eﬀect= 0.069, Boot SE= 0.023, 95% CI [0.027, 0.119]. However, this indirect eﬀect became nonsigniﬁcant under high growth mindset, Eﬀect = 0.004, Boot SE = 0.019, 95% CI [−0.033, 0.042]."
