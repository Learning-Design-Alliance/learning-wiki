The review reports Fägerstam and Blom's quasi-experiment measuring long-term biology knowledge by an essay-type question plus interviews, and notes Wells et al. (2015) found classes with "the greatest increases in their science knowledge, by 0.78 on average" received higher garden-intervention dosages.

> "They found greater long-term knowledge retention for outdoor compared with indoor classes for 7th graders. However, no significant effects of nature were found for 8th graders."
