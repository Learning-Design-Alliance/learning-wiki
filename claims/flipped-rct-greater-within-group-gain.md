## Evidence

### Yousefi Mahboubeh Sadat 2026

Yousefi Mahboubeh Sadat, Fooladzadeh Dehghan Mohsen, Ilkhani Mahnaz, Khajoei Rahimeh, Heidari Saeide, Jokar Mozhgan. (2026). The effects of flipped classroom versus lecture-based learning on academic performance and perceptions of classroom environment among nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09746-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group analyses in the 58-student RCT: the control group's paired-samples t-test showed a significant increase (t = 26.673, P < 0.001) and the intervention group's Wilcoxon signed-rank test confirmed significant improvement (Z = 4.742, P < 0.001). No effect sizes are printed for these within-group tests.

> "While both groups improved, the between‑group ANCOVA results demonstrated that the magnitude of improvement was significantly greater in the flipped classroom condition."
