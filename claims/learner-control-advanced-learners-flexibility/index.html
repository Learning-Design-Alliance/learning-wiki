Evidence¶

Slava Kalyuga 2010¶

Slava Kalyuga, Alexander Renkl, Fred Paas. (2010). Facilitating Flexible Problem Solving: A Cognitive Load Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9132-9

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument by the authors, not a tested result in this paper. They caution that learner-controlled instruction "may not be suitable during the early stages of learning complex skills" because low-expertise students might not use control appropriately.

"we claim that advanced learners may significantly benefit from learning environments that provide more freedom for students in selecting and structuring their learning tasks or, in the present case, the level of generality that is considered"
