### Murayama 2025

Murayama, K., & von Keyserlingk, L. (2025). A critical analysis of the current motivation theories in educational psychology: Why the same theories continue to dominate. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2025.2473894

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The article cites Yan and Schuetze (2023), who showed researchers tested many distinct process models linking growth mindset to academic outcomes (e.g., via perseverance of effort and life satisfaction, or via proactive coping and reduced stress). The authors use this as an illustration that underspecified mechanisms leave theories unchallenged.

> "After more than 30 years of research, there is little-to-no coherence in theories of how growth mindset works across different teams of psychological researchers"
