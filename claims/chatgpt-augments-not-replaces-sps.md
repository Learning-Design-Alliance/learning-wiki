`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' principal finding from the qualitative pre/post study with 9 students, drawn from thematic analysis plus faculty observation of the live role-play sessions. Diagnostic skill conclusions came from faculty observations and student self-report, not formal assessment.

> "Our results suggest that the current version of ChatGPT (ChatGPT version 4.0 at the time of this study) can function effectively as an augmentation to traditional SPs but cannot fully substitute for SPs."
