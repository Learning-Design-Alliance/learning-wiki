### Daria Hall 2013

Daria Hall. (2013). A Step Forward or a Step Back? State Accountability in the Waiver Era. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543222

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The report's review of state intervention plans finds most are "vague at best" on effective teachers in Priority schools and that "no state has articulated a clear plan" for Focus school teacher assignments; Florida is cited as an exception with explicit criteria for Priority schools.

> "Many other state plans, though, are vague at best when it comes to ensuring there are effective teachers and leaders in Priority schools. And no state has articulated a clear plan for addressing teacher assignments within Focus schools to ensure students who need the most support are placed with the strongest educators."
