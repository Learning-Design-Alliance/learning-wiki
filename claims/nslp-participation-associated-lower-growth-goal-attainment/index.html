In the same teacher-level analysis of i-Ready growth outcomes, school NSLP participation rate was the factor associated with growth-goal attainment: students were "less likely to meet their target growth goals in math and reading" where NSLP rates were greater.

"However, students were less likely to meet their target growth goals in math and reading in schools with greater rates of NSLP participation (see Appendix)."
