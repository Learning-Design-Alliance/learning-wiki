## Evidence

### Remy Dou 2016

Remy Dou, Eric Brewe, Justyna P. Zwolak, Geoff Potvin, Eric A. Williams, and Laird H. Kramer. (2016). Beyond performance metrics: Examining a decrease in students' physics self-efficacy through a social networks lens. Physical Review Physics Education Research 12, 020124. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.12.020124

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped gender comparisons of network centrality found no difference in outDegree or PageRank, but "male students on average had slightly higher inDegrees than did female students," with a printed effect size of Cohen's d = 0.37.

> "Nevertheless, male students on average had slightly higher inDegrees than did female students [tð121.9Þ¼ −2.13, p< 0.05, Cohen 's d ¼ 0.37]; see Table III."
