## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Teichmann 2022

Teichmann, E., Lewandowski, H. J., and Alemani, M. (2022). Investigating students' views of experimental physics in German laboratory classes. arXiv:2201.12145. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010135)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Study of GE-CLASS pre/post responses from Potsdam laboratory courses analyzed on a two-point scale, averaging over all students and items; the YOU-question mean moved from "0.640 ±0.008 before instruction and 0.667±0.008 after instruction", tested with a Mann-Whitney U-test (p=0.0058). The article reports a very small Cohen's d=-0.062, so impact is coded null per no-standardized-magnitude convention.

> "the overall GE-CLASS agree- ment with the ER is 0.640 ±0.008 before instruction and 0.667±0.008 after instruction (see Fig.1). This shift is statistically signiﬁcant, as tested using a Mann-Whitney U-test (p=0.0058≪0.05)"
