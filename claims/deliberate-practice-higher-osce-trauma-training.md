## Evidence

### Kanoksin Suksan 2025

Kanoksin Suksan, Songkhla Bhapapak Na, Tansawet Amarit, Laopeamthong Issaree, Sukhvibul Pakkapol, Techapongsatorn Suphakarn. (2025). Effectiveness of deliberate practices versus conventional lecture in trauma training for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06732-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 48 first- to third-year medical students at Vajira Hospital, randomized to deliberate practice (n = 24) or conventional lecture (n = 24). The deliberate practice group scored "significantly higher OSCE scores (mean = 69.79, SD = 9.49)" than the lecture group; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "The deliberate practice group had significantly higher OSCE scores (mean = 69.79, SD = 9.49) than did the conventional lecture group (mean = 51.38, SD = 14.59), with a p value of 0.000002."
