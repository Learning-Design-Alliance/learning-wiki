### Sørensen JL 2015

Sørensen JL, van der Vleuten C, Rosthøj S, Østergaard D, LeBlanc V, Johansen M, Ekelund K, Starkopf L, Lindschou J, Gluud C, Weikop P, Ottesen B. (2015). Simulation-based multiprofessional obstetric anaesthesia training conducted in situ versus off-site leads to similar individual and team outcomes: a randomised educational trial. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis comparisons of 20 evaluation-questionnaire items (p values adjusted for multiple testing) showed authenticity medians of 4 (ISS) versus 3 (OSS) for the CS simulation (p=0.02) and the PPH simulation (p=0.01).

> "However, the two questions addressing the authenticity or fidelity of the simulations were scored significantly higher by the ISS participants compared with the OSS participants (table 7)."
