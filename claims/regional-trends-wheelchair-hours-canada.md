Post-hoc exploration reported in the Discussion found regional trends: "western programs (n = 3; m = 28.4 hours covering 7.7 steps) higher than eastern programs" and central programs lowest, but the authors state the small number of programs precludes meaningful statistical analysis.

> "there appear to be some regional trends with western programs (n = 3; m = 28.4 hours covering 7.7 steps) higher than eastern programs (n = 6; m = 18.8 hours covering 5.7 steps) followed by central programs (n = 5; m = 12.7 hours covering 6.4 steps), although the small number of programs precludes a meaningful statistical analysis."
