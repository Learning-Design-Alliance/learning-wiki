### Carpentier Philippe 2022

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. (2022). A sequential model of the contribution of preschool fluid and crystallized cognitive abilities to later school achievement. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0276532

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Correlational analysis across both samples (Table 2). Achievement stability correlations ranged from .62 to .78, and teacher ratings correlated .55 to .73 with grade 6 national exam scores, motivating autoregressive paths in the models.

> "School achievement was found to be highly stable across grades (rs range: .62 to .78), and teacher ratings in grades 1, 2, 4, as highly predictive of national exams in grade 6 (rs range: .55 to .73)"

### Carpentier Philippe 2022 (2)

Carpentier Philippe, Morneau-Vaillancourt Geneviève, Aubé Sophie, Matte-Gagné Célia, Denault Anne-Sophie, Brendgen Mara, Larose Simon, Petitclerc Amélie, Ouellet-Morin Isabelle, Carbonneau René, Feng Bei, Séguin Jean, Côté Sylvana, Vitaro Frank, Tremblay Richard E., Dionne Ginette, Boivin Michel. (2022). A sequential model of the contribution of preschool fluid and crystallized cognitive abilities to later school achievement. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0276532

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

QNTS path model fit: CFI = .98, TLI = .95, RMSEA = .05. Variance accounted for rose across grades as autoregressive paths were included; the QLSCD model explained 58% to 80% across grades.

> "The overall model, including the control variables, accounted for 50% of the variance in school achievement in grade 1, 54% in grade 3, and 67% in grades 4 and 6, with the latter grades including the autoregressive paths."
