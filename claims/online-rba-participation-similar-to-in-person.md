## Evidence

### Alexis Olsho 2020

Alexis Olsho, Suzanne White Brahmia, Charlotte Zimmerman, Trevor I. Smith, Philip Eaton, Andrew Boudreaux. (2020). Online administration of a reasoning inventory in development. [doi:10.1119/perc.2020.pr.olsho](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2020.pr.olsho)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of participation rates across three quarters of a large-enrollment calculus-based introductory physics sequence, in-person versus online administration. The article reports "the overall participation rate was 91%" in-person and 90% online, with per-course rates 93%–92%–89% and 93%–89%–89%.

> "Overall participation rates were similar for in-person and online administration. For in-person administration, the overall participation rate was 91% (93%–92%–89% rate for C(I)–C(II)–C(III) students); for online administration, the overall participation rate was 90%"
