Fig. 2 reports percentages of faculty and student survey respondents indicating logistical challenges during Spring 2020 online instruction. Student figures include 26% with internet reliability issues and 28% with software access issues; the authors link the quarter of students without reliable internet to concern about widening the digital equity gap.

> "23% of faculty had no reliable internet connection, while 32% had no access to webcam or camera for the online instruction. Finally, 47% of the faculty indicated that they had no access or technical difficulties with online writing tools."
