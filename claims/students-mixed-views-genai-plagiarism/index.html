Feyisa Mulisa and Tesfa Mezgebu 2026¶

Feyisa Mulisa and Tesfa Mezgebu. (2026). Tools facilitate cheating, or partner supports learning? GenAI and academic integrity issues from students’ perspectives. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00231-z

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Reflexive thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 27 undergraduates at Ambo University, Ethiopia, conducted November 2025 to January 2026. The findings section reports that "While some participants consider the creation of ideas using GenAI to not be plagiarism, others argue that it should be plagiarism."

"While some participants consider the creation of ideas using GenAI to not be plagiarism, others argue that it should be plagiarism, since they did not create the entire work themselves."
