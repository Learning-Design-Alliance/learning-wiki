## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants reflected a significantly higher level of satisfaction with the control instructional format than the VR format (p=.001). [→ Hing Lie 2023](#hing-lie-2023)

## Evidence

### Hing Lie 2023

Hing Lie, Kachina Studer, Zhen Zhao, Ben Thomson, Dishita G Turakhia, John Liu. (2023). Training for Open-Ended Drilling through a Virtual Reality Simulation. [doi:10.1109/ismar59233.2023.00051](https://doi.org/10.1109/ismar59233.2023.00051)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subjective learning-experience questionnaire comparing the VR and control groups (Mann-Whitney-U, p=.001 for liking the instructional format received; p=.19 for learning the necessary material). The discussion attributes the difference to VR usability bugs, a learning curve for the VR system, and headset discomfort reported by participants.

> "On average, the students reflected a significantly higher level of satisfaction in using the control format of instructional material compared to using the VR format material."
