## Evidence

### Pritchard Shane A. 2020

Pritchard Shane A., Keating Jennifer L., Nestel Debra, Blackstock Felicity C. (2020). Physiotherapy students can be educated to portray realistic patient roles in simulation: a pragmatic observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02382-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Individual item paired t-tests on the complete data set (n = 23), reported in Table 1. For example, Item 1 accurate history rose from "3.0 (0.8)" to "3.72 (0.9)" (p = 0.01), while Item 6 appropriate appearance was non-significant (p = 0.32).

> "Seven items were significantly higher post-program. The other three items were higher but differences did not attain significance at an alpha level of .05."
