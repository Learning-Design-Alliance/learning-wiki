## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The GbEl group had significantly higher mean scores on enjoying the material, wanting more such material, and feeling confident in laboratory urinalysis (Wilcoxon-test p < 0.001 for all three); no standardized effect size is printed. [→ Boeker Martin 2013](#boeker-martin-2013)

## Evidence

### Boeker Martin 2013

Boeker Martin, Andel Peter, Vach Werner, Frankenschmidt Alexander. (2013). Game-Based E-Learning Is More Effective than a Conventional Instructional Method: A Randomized Controlled Trial with Third-Year Medical Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0082328

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome measured with a three-question questionnaire on a 4-point Likert scale, collected anonymously in the debriefing session; attitudes "were more positive in the GbEl group than in the script group (see Figure 5)". No standardized effect size is printed for these differences.

> "The differences between group means were 1.33 for the question if the students had fun while learning with the respective material, 1.0 for the question if they would like to learn from more of such material, and 0.69 if they felt secure in the topic (Wilcoxon-test: p < 0.001 for all three differences)."
