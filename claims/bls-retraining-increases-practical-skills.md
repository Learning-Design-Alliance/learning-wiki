## Evidence

### Kim Seo-Yoon 2020

Kim Seo-Yoon, Shin Dongmin, Kim Hyun Jeong, Karm Myong-Hwan. (2020). Changes of knowledge and practical skills before and after retraining for basic life support: Focused on students of Dental School. International Journal of Medical Sciences 17(18): 3082-3090. https://doi.org/10.7150/ijms.47343

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Manikin-based skills assessment (Little Anne QCPR) of the same 98 students before and after retraining, scored by instructors on the 2015 AHA assessment sequence. The total rose from "43.2 ± 11.0 to 91.4 ± 7.7 points" (p < 0.001); 16 of 19 items were individually significant (p < 0.05).

> "The total score of BLS practical skills increased from 43.2 ± 11.0 to 91.4 ± 7.7 points when converted into a 100-point scale before and after retraining and was statistically significant (p < 0.001)"
