## Evidence

### Capelo 2009

Capelo, C., & Dias, J. F. (2009). A feedback learning and mental models perspective on strategic decision making. Educational Technology Research and Development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-009-9123-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the simulation experiment, group C (n = 25) using the balanced scorecard interface and causal diagramming showed "the best Mental Model Structure Similarity (mean = 0.443), the best Mental Model Behaviour Similarity (mean = 0.742) and the best Performance (mean = 628)", with t-tests showing significant differences from groups A and B.

> "The participants in group C—high Level of Feedback Information and high Level of Systems Thinking Facilitation—showed on average the best Mental Model Structure Similarity (mean = 0.443), the best Mental Model Behaviour Similarity (mean = 0.742) and the best Performance (mean = 628)."
