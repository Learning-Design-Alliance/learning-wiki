### Nagasaki Kazuya 2026

Clinical competence and attitudes towards LGBT patients among medical residents in Japan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08510-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Self-reported hours of LGBT-related education collected in the same survey of 280 residents; recall-based exposure measures show "81.2% reported no LGBT-related education at their training hospital".

> "Among participating residents, 47.9% reported not receiving LGBT-related education during medical school, and an additional 39.1% reported only 1–3 h. Only 14.0% received 4 or more hours. During residency, exposure was even more limited: 81.2% reported no LGBT-related education at their training hospital."
