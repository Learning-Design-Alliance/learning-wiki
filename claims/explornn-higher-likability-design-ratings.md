### Alex Bäuerle 2022

Alex Bäuerle, Patrick Albus, Raphael Stork, Tina Seufert, and Timo Ropinski. (2022). exploRNN: Teaching Recurrent Neural Networks through Visual Exploration. [doi:10.1007/s00371-022-02593-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00371-022-02593-0)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

ANOVA on the qualitative rating items in the user study found significant advantages for exploRNN on likability (F(1,30)=10.52, p=.003, η²p=.260), recommendability (F(1,30)=11.75, p=.002, η²p=.281), and design (F(1,30)=20.711, p<.001, η²p=.408).

> "Participants also rated our approach as significantly more likable (F (1, 30) = 10 .52, p = .003, η2p = .260), more recommendable (F (1, 30) = 11 .75, p = .002, η2p = .281), and better designed (F (1, 30) = 20 .711, p < .001, η2p = .408) compared to the learning text."
