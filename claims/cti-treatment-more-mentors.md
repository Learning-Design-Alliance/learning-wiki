## Evidence

### Hong 2013

Hong, Y., & Hong, G. (2013). Making Sense of the “Zero Effect” of Comprehensive Teacher Induction Programs: A Mediational Analysis. SREE Spring 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563315

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Phase I analysis of the multi-site cluster randomized trial comparing treatment and control teachers' mentoring experiences. On average, treatment teachers received about 1.42 mentors (sd = .92) while control teachers had 1.08 mentors (sd = .84).

> "We have identified significant differences in the number of mentors (coefficient =.34, se = .07, t = 4.79. p < .001) and in the intensity of mentoring activities (coefficient = 16.90, se = 7.54, t = 2.24. p < .05) between the treatment and control groups."
