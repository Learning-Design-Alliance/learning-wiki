# Reflective thinking moderates the direct GenAI use–critical thinking path: positive at low reflectivity, non-significant at high reflectivity

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` The RT × GenAI use interaction was significant (β = −0.125, p = 0.002); GenAI use predicted critical thinking at low reflective thinking (β = 0.177, p = 0.008) but not at high reflective thinking (β = −0.073, p = 0.245). [→ Yan Z 2026](#yan-z-2026)

## Evidence

### Yan Z 2026

Yan Z, Fang Z and Zou W (2026). The association between generative AI use and university students' critical thinking: a moderated mediation model of GenAI feedback literacy and reflective thinking. Front. Psychol. 17:1939286. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1939286

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Simple-slope analysis at ±1 SD of reflective thinking in the moderated mediation PLS-SEM model (N = 421). The interaction term Reflective Thinking × GenAI Use was significant (β = −0.125, t = 3.138, p = 0.002); the direct path "was non-significant amongst students with high reflective thinking".

> "GenAI use significantly predicted critical thinking amongst students with low reflective thinking (β = 0.177, t = 2.678, p = 0.008), but the slope was non-significant amongst students with high reflective thinking (β = −0.073, t = 1.162, p = 0.245)."
