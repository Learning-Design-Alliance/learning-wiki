Subset analysis of the interview survey covering cases where parents arose in discussion. In "thirty-four cases" parents were mentioned, and in "twenty-five of these" parental wishes differed decidedly from the girl's own; eleven opposed parents suggested no alternative. No effect size is reported.

> "In thirty-four cases the girls spoke of either mother, father or their "family" and in twenty-five of these it was to state that the wishes of their elders differed decidedly from their own."
