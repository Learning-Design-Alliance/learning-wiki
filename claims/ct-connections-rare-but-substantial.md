# Connections involving computational thinking were least frequent, yet SBT–CT and MBT–CT connections were predominantly substantial across all performance levels

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` DBT–CT and CT–MER connections were rare (once or twice per performance team), but SBT–CT and MBT–CT connections were considerably more often substantial than intermediate or superficial across all performance levels. [→ Sibatmika Babu 2026](#sibatmika-babu-2026)

## Evidence

### Sibatmika Babu 2026

Sibatmika Babu, Ravishankar Chatta Subramaniam, Carina M. Rebello, Jason W. Morphew, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Integrative Thinking in Computational Physics Design Essays. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.22154](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.22154)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Frequency analysis of CT-involving connections in the 14 team CPDEs; the article reports DBT–CT connections "occurred only once in each performance team" and CT–MER twice among high-performing teams and once among mid- and low-performing teams, while SBT–CT and MBT–CT showed 2–5 substantial connections versus 0–2 intermediate or superficial ones.

> "In contrast, connections involving Computational Think- ing (CT) were less frequent across all performance teams. Design-Based Thinking–Computational Think- ing (DBT–CT) connections occurred only once in each performance team."
