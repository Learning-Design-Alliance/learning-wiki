# Posttest attitude differences did not vary by parents' educational level

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2019 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Kruskal-Wallis H tests found no statistically significant differences by mother's or father's educational level in pretest or posttest attitude scores. [→ Taş 2019](#tas-2019)

## Evidence

### Taş 2019

Taş, H. & Kıroğlu, K. (2019). The effect of Mesnevî supported values education on the attitudes of 4th grade students in primary school. Pegem Eğitim ve Öğretim Dergisi, 9(1), 209-254. http://dx.doi.org/10.14527/pegegog.2019.008

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis H tests across mother's education levels (primary, high school, university) in both groups; the posttest experimental comparison gave H(3)=4.46, p>.05, reported as "no statistically significant difference". Father's education comparisons were likewise non-significant. No effect sizes are printed.

> "there is no statistically significant difference between mother’s educational level and the attitudes of the students .05 confidence level in the posttest scores of the experimental group [(H(3)=4.46, p>.05]"
