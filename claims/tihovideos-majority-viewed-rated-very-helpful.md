## Evidence

### Müller Lina R. 2019

Müller Lina R., Tipold Andrea, Ehlers Jan P., Schaper Elisabeth. (2019). TiHoVideos: veterinary students' utilization of instructional videos on clinical skills. BMC Veterinary Research 15, 326. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-019-2079-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paper-based and online survey of students at the University of Veterinary Medicine Hannover from July 2015 to January 2016; 835 students participated (63.5% overall response rate). The article reports that "54.2% of the students said that they had watched the videos." On a six-point Likert scale, "54.4% (n = 454) of all students rated the suitability of the videos as learning tools to be on average very helpful (Ø 2.2).
