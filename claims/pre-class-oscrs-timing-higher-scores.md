# Within the P-I-T-D-S model, students who used the OSCRS simulator before class scored significantly higher than those who used it after class

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The pre-class group (N = 19) scored significantly higher than the post-class group (N = 20) on Dental Exam (p = 0.00871), Test (p = 0.04880), and Total Score (p = 0.0101). [→ Ba Ruikai 2026](#ba-ruikai-2026)

## Evidence

### Ba Ruikai 2026

Ba Ruikai, Feng Xinran, Yuan Lixian, Tong Juan, Ban Jinghao, Wu Meiling, Han Bing, Xu Xinyue, Qiu Xinyu, Guo Hao, Xuan Kun. (2026). A novel extended flipped classroom model helps dental undergraduates grow into dentists. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08525-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Randomized subgroup comparison within the 2023 cohort: 39 participants stratified and randomly assigned to pre-class (OSCRS before course, N = 19) versus post-class (after course, N = 20), compared with Wilcoxon rank-sum tests. Total Score rose from 76.6 to 84.1 (p = 0.0101).

> "The Dental Exam score increased from 90.0 [85.0–94.0] in the Post-class group to 95.0 [90.0–100.0] in the Pre-class group (p = 0.00871). Similarly, the Test score improved from 82.0 [79.0–84.5] to 85.5 [82.0–93.2] (p = 0.04880)."
