## Evidence

### Oomens P 2019

Oomens P, Fu VX, Kleinrensink GJ, Jeekel J. (2019). The effect of music on simulated surgical performance: a systematic review. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-019-06868-x

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of one study using EMG of the dominant hand during robot-assisted laparoscopic simulation as an indication of muscle fatigue. The review reports significant reduction in extensor digitorum mean activation across classical, hiphop, Jamaican and jazz music; no effect size printed.

> "Mean electromyography activation of the extensor digitorum muscle was significantly reduced when participants listened to any type of researcher-selected music (i.e. classical, hiphop, Jamaican or jazz), while median electromyography frequency did not differ statistically significantly between groups."
