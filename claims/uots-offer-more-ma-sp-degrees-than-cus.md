## Evidence

### Pak-Lok Poon 2026

Pak-Lok Poon, Sau-Fun Tang, Santoso Wibowo, & Srimannarayana Grandhi. (2026). Information technology curriculum: General or specialized? An Australia's census study. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.02952)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Population-level Cohen's d effect size analysis comparing the four UoTs with the 35 CUs across all Australian offering universities. Mean bachelor's MA-degrees: UoTs = 7.3; CUs = 4.4; mean master's SP-degrees: UoTs = 3.0; CUs = 1.7.

> "when comparing with CUs, UoTs offer: • more master's MA-degrees (Cohen's d = 0.51; medium difference); and • significantly more bachelor's MA-degrees, bachelor's SP-degrees, and master's SP-degrees (Cohen's d ≥ 0.95; large difference)"
