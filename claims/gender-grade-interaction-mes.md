### Xia et al. 2022

Qi Xia, Hongbiao Yin, Ruonan Hu, Xiuhan Li, and Junjie Shang. (2022). Motivation, Engagement, and Mathematics Achievement: An Exploratory Study Among Chinese Primary Students. SAGE Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/21582440221134609

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple effects analysis following significant gender × grade interaction effects (F values 3.81-6.21 in Table 3) among the 1,538 students. Sixth-grade females scored higher on disengagement (F (2, 1,530)=6.61, hp2=.009) and lower on self-belief (F (2, 1,530)=11.66, hp2=.015) than fourth-grade females.

> "Male students scored higher on disengagement (F (1, 1,530) = 6.84, p\.05, hp2=.004), failure avoidance (F (1, 1,530) = 6.42, p\.05, hp2=.004), and maladaptive motivation (F (1, 1,530)=7.19, p\.05, hp2= .005) than female students in fourth grade"
