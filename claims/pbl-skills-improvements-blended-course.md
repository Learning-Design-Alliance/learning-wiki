## Evidence

### Cavicchia María L. 2018

Cavicchia María L., Cusumano Aana M., Bottino Daniela V. (2018). Problem-based learning implementation in a health sciences blended-learning program in Argentina. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5a7e.d85c

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' observational report of the 2013 blended PBL course with 68 senior medical students, based on tutor rubrics and student surveys. They state that "most of the students showed improvements" across the listed PBL skill areas; no effect sizes or test statistics are printed.

> "Regarding PBL strategies applied on the platform and skills development, most of the students showed improvements in areas such as searching for information, reflecting upon presented cases before intervention, debating with peers, regularly attending lessons, and communicating with peers and tutors."
