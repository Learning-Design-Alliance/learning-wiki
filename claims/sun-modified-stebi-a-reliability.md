## Evidence

### Batiza Ann Finney 2013

Batiza Ann Finney, Gruhl Mary, Zhang Bo, Harrington Tom, Roberts Marisa, LaFlamme Donna, Haasch Mary Anne, Knopp Jonathan, Vogt Gina, Goodsell David, Hagedorn Eric, Marcey David, Hoelzer Mark, Nelson Dave. (2013). The Effects of the SUN Project on Teacher Knowledge and Self-Efficacy Regarding Biological Energy Transfer Are Significant and Long-Lasting: Results of a Randomized Controlled Trial. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-09-0155

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Psychometric evaluation of the modified instrument using 42 pretests and 24 posttests. The article reports overall alphas of 0.88 and 0.79, a PSTE subscale alpha of 0.92 (pretest), and an STOE subscale alpha of "only 0.64 in each case," which the authors call "a bit lower than acceptable."

> "Using a sample size of 42 pretests and 24 posttests, we have determined the overall coefficient alpha for the SUN-modified STEBI-A to be 0.88, based on pretest scores, and 0.79, based on posttest scores."
