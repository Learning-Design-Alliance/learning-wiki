## Evidence

### Alyahya D 2025

Alyahya D, Alharbi A, Kashoo F, Alsaheli S, AlMubarak FM, Aljuhni R. (2025). Knowledge and competence in vestibular rehabilitation: a cross-sectional study of physical therapy interns. PeerJ 13. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.20213

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Second multiple linear regression on the same 233-intern survey: significant but modest, F(12, 219) = 2.50, p = .004, "accounted for only 12% of the variance (adjusted R2 = .07"; all other variables non-significant.

> "workshop attendance was the sole positive predictor, B = 0.49, SE = 0.12, t = 4.09, p < .001, 95% CI [0.25–0.73], whereas holding a professional physical-therapy degree was associated with lower case-report knowledge, B = −1.06, SE = 0.53, t = −2.02, p = .045."
