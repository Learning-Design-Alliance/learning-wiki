# Perceived effort is higher and perceived learning lower for interleaving than blocking, and experience-mediated increases in perceived learning raise interleaving choices

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r?` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Learners perceived more effort and less learning for interleaving than for blocking, but these differences decreased with experience, mostly because effort diminished and perceived learning increased for interleaving. [→ Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023](#anique-b-h-de-bruin-2023)
`q2 i?` The effect of the decrease in perceived effort on use of interleaving was mediated by the increase in perceived learning (full mediation); this relation was absent for blocking. [→ Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023 (2)](#anique-b-h-de-bruin-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023

Anique B. H. de Bruin, Felicitas Biwer, Luotong Hui, Erdem Onan, Louise David, Wisnu Wiradhany. (2023). Worth the Effort: the Start and Stick to Desirable Difficulties (S2D2) Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09766-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

The review reports this experiment by Onan and colleagues (2022), in which learners studied painting styles in blocked and interleaved manners, monitored effort and learning, and then chose a strategy for novel paintings.

> "Perceived effort was higher and perceived learning was lower for interleaving than for blocking. These differences between blocking and interleaving decreased after experience with the strategies."

### Anique B. H. de Bruin 2023 (2)

Anique B. H. de Bruin, Felicitas Biwer, Luotong Hui, Erdem Onan, Louise David, Wisnu Wiradhany. (2023). Worth the Effort: the Start and Stick to Desirable Difficulties (S2D2) Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09766-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes this full-mediation finding to the same study, noting the relation was absent for blocking units, where perceived learning was erroneously higher to begin with; familiarity with strategies may play a role.

> "Moreover, the effect of this decrease in perceived effort on use of interleav - ing was mediated by the increase in perceived learning."
