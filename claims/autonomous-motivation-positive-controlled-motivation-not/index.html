q2 · i? · review · r3

Review synthesis of seven studies evaluating motivational variables among medical students. The review reports autonomous or intrinsic motivation "was positively associated with academic performance" in all seven, while controlled or extrinsic motivation showed minimal or negative associations; no pooled effect size is printed.
