Evidence¶

Saltan 2017¶

Fatih Saltan. (2017). The Impact of Online Algorithm Visualization on ICT Students' Achievements in Introduction to Programming Course. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n1p184

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Post-test-only randomized experiment: 40 first-time introduction-to-programming students randomly assigned to treatment (n = 20) or control (n = 20); t-test on the six-question VC# achievement test showed a "significant mean difference" of 13.1 with d = .77.

"There is a significant mean difference between the experimental group ( M = 51.85, SD = 20.34) and the control group ( M = 38.75, SD = 12.86) with the mean difference of 13.1 and large effect size; t (38) = 2.43, p > .05, d = .77."
