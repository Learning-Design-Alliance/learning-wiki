## Evidence

### Gupta R 2021

Gupta R, DeSandro S, Doherty NA, Gardner AK, Pillow MT. (2021). Medical and Physician Assistant Student Competence in Basic Life Support: Opportunities to Improve Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2020.11.48536

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Manikin study of 80 students comparing compression metrics by gender with t-tests. The study reports "56.5 millimeters [mm] ±6.7 vs 50.3 mm ±6.5; P < 0.001" for mean compression depth.

> "male gender was associated with significantly greater mean compression depth compared to female gender (56.5 millimeters [mm] ±6.7 vs 50.3 mm ±6.5; P < 0.001)"

### Gupta R 2021 (2)

Gupta R, DeSandro S, Doherty NA, Gardner AK, Pillow MT. (2021). Medical and Physician Assistant Student Competence in Basic Life Support: Opportunities to Improve Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.2020.11.48536

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup analysis stratifying the same manikin study by student type. The study reports the gender difference "was maintained for medical students, but no differences were observed between male and female PA students."

> "the significant differences in compression metrics between genders was maintained for medical students, but no differences were observed between male and female PA students"
