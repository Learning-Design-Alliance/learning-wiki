Results from the 2019 Mathematics and Reading Assessments. (2020). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Statistics. https://www.nationsreportcard.gov

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The NAEP 2019 national achievement-level results report that percentages at or above NAEP Proficient were "unchanged in mathematics and lower in reading since 2017", printing 2017 and 2019 percentages of 37% to 41% at grade 4 and 34% to 36% at grade 8. No effect sizes are reported.
