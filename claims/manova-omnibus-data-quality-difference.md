MANOVA on 288 pair-level records (578 individual records collapsed by pair, first three rounds analyzed) tested condition differences across rounds in data-taking decisions and measurement quality; Table I reports Pillai's Trace F(2,98) = 16.934, adj. p = .000 (round 1) and F(2,63) = 10.987, adj. p = .000 (round 3), with round 2 at p = .051 and the final round at p = .062. No effect size printed.
