Evidence¶

Daria Hall 2013¶

Daria Hall. (2013). A Step Forward or a Step Back? State Accountability in the Waiver Era. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543222

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

The report's analysis of approved state waiver plans finds that although states must set goals, the waiver guidelines do not require goals to count in ratings; the authors state "performance against overall and group achievement goals doesn't factor at all into a school's accountability rating" in many states.

"But many states took advantage of the Department of Education's signals in the waiver guidelines and have set up systems in which performance against overall and group achievement goals doesn't factor at all into a school's accountability rating."
