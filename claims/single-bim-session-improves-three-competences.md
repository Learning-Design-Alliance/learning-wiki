## Evidence

### Liu Qiqi 2025

Liu Qiqi, Ma Jixuan, Wu Chuansha, Du Yang, Chen Ting, Sun Xiaojie, Zhao Qin, Liu Yunhao, Shi Yuqin, Gu Yingying, Zhou Min, Zhang Ling, Zhou Ting. (2025). The effects of single and multiple BOPPPS-integrated model sessions on public health students' competences: a two-stage study combining quasi-experimental and cross-sectional analyses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07508-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment comparing a BIM class (n=48) with an LBL class (n=50) on the Water Hygiene chapter; independent t-tests on a self-designed competence scale. Information literacy t = 2.87, P < 0.01; expressive communication t = 2.07, P = 0.04; teamwork t = 2.10, P = 0.04. No effect sizes were printed.

> "Specifically, students in the BIM group performed significantly better than the LBL group in information literacy, expressive communication, and teamwork (P < 0.05, Table 1)."
