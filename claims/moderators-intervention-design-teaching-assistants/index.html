Donath, J. L., Lüke, T., Tran, U. S., Botes, E., & Goetz, T. (2025). The Transfer of Teacher Training to Inclusive Classroom Practice: A Meta-Analytic SEM Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10081-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-regression moderator analyses across 1084 effect sizes. The article also reports teaching-assistant inclusion moderated effects (overall r = 0.39 vs 0.29; F[1, 1041] = 7.05, p = 0.008, β = 0.22), while publication year, continent, school type, and teacher background showed no significant influence.

"Intervention studies reported larger effect sizes (overall r = 0.35, 95% CI [0.31; 0.39]) than cross-sectional studies (overall r = 0.24 [0.21; 0.27]; F[1, 1041] = 22.36, p < 0.001)."
