## Evidence

### Brum Roberta 2015

Brum Roberta, Rajani Ronak, Gelandt Elton, Morgan Lisa, Raguseelan Nira, Butt Salman, Nelmes David, Auzinger Georg, Broughton Simon. (2015). Simulation training for extracorporeal membrane oxygenation. Annals of Cardiac Anaesthesia. https://doi.org/10.4103/0971-9784.154472

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Postcourse evaluation questionnaire with open-ended questions completed by all 14 delegates. The authors report delegates "perceived an improvement in their communication skills"; findings are self-reported perceptions rather than observed behaviour.

> "For the nontechnical skills, 8 (57%) perceived an improvement in their communication skills, 7 (50%) an improvement in their leadership skills and 5 (36%) an improvement in their delegation skills."
