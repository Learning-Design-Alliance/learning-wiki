Evidence¶

Armitage 2023¶

Armitage, KL; Suddendorf, T; Bulley, A; Bastos, APM; Taylor, AH; Redshaw, J. (2023). Creativity and Flexibility in Young Children's Use of External Cognitive Strategies. Developmental Psychology, 59 (6), pp. 995-1005. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001562

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Across the six transfer trials of the preregistered study, the article reports that from age 6 children "deployed effective external strategies on most trials," alongside wide within- and between-age variation in strategy use. No effect size is printed for this age contrast.
