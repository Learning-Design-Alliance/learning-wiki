Descriptive implementation data from meeting logbooks and end-of-program questionnaires in the preceptorship group (n=21); sessions were "relatively short" and infrequent, which the authors suggest reduced opportunities to apply cognitive apprenticeship techniques.

> "Implementation data showed that most preceptorship meetings were relatively short (61.9% of sessions lasted ≥30 minutes) and infrequent (28.6% of participants received ≥2 meetings per week)."
