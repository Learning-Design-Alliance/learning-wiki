Pretest analysis of open-ended answers in the quasi-experimental study: both groups showed information deficiencies and misconceptions despite 4 hours of prior traditional instruction on global warming; the ozone-layer-depletion misconception was most frequent in both groups.

> "The most common misconception both in the control (8 people) and experimental (10 people) groups was that "global warming happens due to ozone layer depletion"."
