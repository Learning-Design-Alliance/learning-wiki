Sánchez-Peralta Luisa F., Pagador J. Blas, Sánchez Margallo Francisco M. (2026). Soft skills during residency in Spain: an analysis of the specialized training programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09210-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The study classified 56 programmes according to the NCSHS delegated commissions and found "training programmes for 'Nursing' address the highest number of skills (3.3 ± 0.7)". Surgical and Medical-Surgical specialties have the fewest soft skills (1.0 ± 1.1).

> "When specialties are classified according to the NCSHS classification, training programmes for 'Nursing' address the highest number of skills (3.3 ± 0.7), compared to 'Clinical and Health Care' specialties (1.50 ± 1.1) or 'Diagnostic and Therapeutic Support' (1.20 ± 0.9)."
