## Evidence

### Froud R 2021

Froud R, Hansen SH, Ruud HK, Foss J, Ferguson L, Fredriksen PM. (2021). Relative Performance of Machine Learning and Linear Regression in Predicting Quality of Life and Academic Performance of School Children in Norway: Data Analysis of a Quasi-Experimental Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/22021

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted mixed regression model on HOPP data (n=384 in Table 2) predicting average National Academic Test scores. The article reports "the child’s mother having a master-level education (P<.001; β=1.98)" and dichotomized self-reported exercise (β=2.47) among the best predictors.

> "The best predictors of academic performance in adjusted models were the child’s mother having a master-level education (P<.001; β=1.98, 95% CI 0.25 to 3.71), increased television and computer use (P=.03; β=1.19, 95% CI 0.25 to 3.71), and dichotomized self-reported exercise (P=.001; β=2.47, 95% CI 1.08 to 3.87)."
