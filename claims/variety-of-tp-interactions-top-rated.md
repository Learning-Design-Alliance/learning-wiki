## Evidence

### Pettit RK 2015

Pettit RK, McCoy L, Kinney M, Schwartz FN. (2015). Student perceptions of gamified audience response system interactions in large group lectures and via lecture capture technology. BMC Medical Education 15:92. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0373-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Learning-domain item from the 91-student survey of 22 gamified microbiology TP sessions: "Ninety-one percent of the respondents agreed or strongly agreed that the variety of TP games played helped them stay interested and focused." In the photograph-rating task, the variety item was rated very or somewhat engaging by 91% of respondents.
