### Farran 2025

Farran, E. K., Fink, E., Hughes, C., Gilligan-Lee, K. A., & The NewFAMS team. (2025). Limited sex differences in spatial language in parent-child dyads. Learning and Instruction, 95, 102006. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.102006

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Longitudinal multiple regressions (N = 111) predicted 48-month child spatial language from 24-month parent spatial language plus controls. Step 2 did not significantly improve fit: quantity F-change < 1; quality "F-change (4, 100) = 1.35, p = .255, ΔR2 change = .014".
