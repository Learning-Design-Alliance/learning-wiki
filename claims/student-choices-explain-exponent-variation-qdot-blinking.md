### Laszlo Frazer 2019

Laszlo Frazer, Heather F. Higginbotham, Toby D. M. Bell, and Alison M. Funston. (2019). 'It's fundamental': Quantum dot blinking experiment to teach critical thinking. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00679](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.9b00679)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of exponents from 62 student experiments in the honours course implementation. The article reports the printed range, mean, and standard deviation, and notes random error estimates are typically 0.01, so "students' choices explain most of the variation in the results".

> "The exponents reported by students ranged from 1.1 to 2.1 across 62 experiments, with a mean of 1.5 and standard deviation of 0.2. Since the random error estimates are typically 0.01, students' choices explain most of the variation in the results."
