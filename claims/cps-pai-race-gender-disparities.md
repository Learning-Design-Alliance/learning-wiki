## Evidence

### Mahaffie 2022

Mahaffie, S., Malone, M., Usher, A., Mukherjee, D., & Nagaoka, J. (2022). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2021. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Disaggregated PAI analysis (Figure 2, Table A.2): Asian/Pacific Islander young women 67.3%, White young women 60.5%, Black young women 23.0%, Black young men 11.6%, Latino young men 20.5%. The aspiration benchmark comes from a 2019 5Essentials Survey analysis in which 74.1% of ninth-graders aspired to at least an associate degree or certificate.

> "However, no race/ethnicity and gender group would have 75 percent of students attain a college degree or certificate in 10 years, if the most recent available rates of attainment for CPS students were not to change over the next decade."
