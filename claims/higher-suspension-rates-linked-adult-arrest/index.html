Trout 2022¶

Trout, L., Pate, C., Wu, K., & McKenna, J. (2022). Reimagining school safety: A guide for schools and communities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED628885

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The guide reports, citing Bacher-Hicks et al. (2019), that "students who attend schools with higher suspension rates are 15–20 percent more likely to be arrested and incarcerated as adults" and less likely to attend a 4-year college. No effect size or sample details are printed in the guide.

"research shows that students who attend schools with higher suspension rates are 15–20 percent more likely to be arrested and incarcerated as adults and are also less likely to attend a 4-year college, with male students and students of color being most impacted (Bacher-Hicks et al., 2019)."
