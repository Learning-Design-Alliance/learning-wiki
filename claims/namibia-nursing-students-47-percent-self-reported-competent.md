Descriptive results from a cross-sectional online questionnaire survey of 272 nursing students at two Namibian campuses, reported in the demographic data section. The self-report split was 52.9% competent versus 47.1% not competent, though the abstract reverses the framing.

> "The self-reported perceived competence assessment showed that 52.9% of the students considered themselves competent while 47.1% indicated that they were not."
