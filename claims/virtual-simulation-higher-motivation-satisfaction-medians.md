## Evidence

### Lai Danlin 2026

Lai Danlin, Lai Baoliang, Xu Liang, Huang Xiaohong. (2026). Virtual Simulation Versus Traditional Training for Orthodontic Bracket Positioning: A Pilot RCT. International Dental Journal. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.109530

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pilot RCT questionnaire analysis using the ARCS Learning Motivation Questionnaire and a Learning Experience Satisfaction Questionnaire on 5-point Likert scales, compared by Mann-Whitney U test. Medians were higher for Group B across dimensions; only Confidence reached P = .05.

> "Group B also recorded higher median values for all four ARCS dimensions (Attention, Relevance, Confidence, and Satisfaction) compared to Group A. However, the results showed borderline significance (P = .05) for the Confidence dimension."
