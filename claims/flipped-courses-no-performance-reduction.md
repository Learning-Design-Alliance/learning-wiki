## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Grade point averages were roughly equal across all six courses, and perceived learning was equivalent (8.80 flipped vs. 8.77 traditional). [→ Gross 2015](#gross-2015)

## Evidence

### Gross 2015

Gross, B., Hoffman, M., Marinari, M., DeSimone, K., & Burke, P. (2015). Flipped @ SBU: Student satisfaction and the college classroom. Educational Research Quarterly, 39(2), 37-52. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1166718

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Objective grades and a subjective perceived-learning item were compared across the three flipped and three traditional courses. The authors found "a roughly equal grade point average" with no statistically significant difference between formats.

> "What we found is that all six courses had a roughly equal grade point average, showing no statistical significance for the clear superiority of either format."
