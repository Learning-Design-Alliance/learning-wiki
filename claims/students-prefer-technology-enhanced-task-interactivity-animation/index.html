Meg Guerreiro 2017¶

Meg Guerreiro. (2017). The impact of a technology-enhanced math performance task on student cognitive engagement in mathematics. NWEA Research white paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-impact-of-a-technology-enhanced-math-performance-task-on-student-cognitive-engagement-in-mathematics/

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative interview component of the mixed methods study. Students reported preferring technology-based testing and favorability toward the technology-enhanced platform, "specifically the interactivity and animations to help visualize and work through the problem." No effect size is printed.

"Qualitative interview data indicated that students preferred using technology to take tests and overall showed favorability for the technology-enhanced performance task, specifically the interactivity and animations to help visualize and work through the problem."
