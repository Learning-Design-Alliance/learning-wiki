## Evidence

### Chuang Shu-Ting 2022

Chuang Shu-Ting, Liao Pei-Lin, Lo Shu-Fen, Chang Ya-Ting, Hsu Hsiang-Tzu. (2022). Effectiveness of an E-Book App on the Knowledge, Attitudes and Confidence of Nurses to Prevent and Care for Pressure Injury. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph192315826

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 164 nurses (86 experimental, 78 control) at a Taiwanese teaching hospital; ANCOVA on posttest KPIS with baseline KPIS and in-service PI education as covariates found "a significant difference in the KPIS between the two groups (F = 98.94, p < 0.000)". No effect size printed.

> "Excluding the influence of receiving an in-service PI education course and baseline KPIS, there was a significant difference in the KPIS between the two groups (F = 98.94, p < 0.000)."
