## Evidence

### Hollands 2020

Hollands, F., & Escueta, M. (2020). How research informs educational technology decision-making in higher education: the role of external research versus internal research. Education Tech Research Dev, 68, 163–180. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09678-z

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of educational technology decision-makers at U.S. higher education institutions; activities named as research were coded for whether they were scientifically rigorous, defined as experimental or quasi-experimental design. The authors report that "only a few of the activities described would meet this standard" and these appeared in under 20% of interviews.
