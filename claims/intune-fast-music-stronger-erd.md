## Evidence

### Jäncke L 2010

Jäncke L, Sandmann P. (2010). Music listening while you learn: No influence of background music on verbal learning. Behavioral and Brain Functions. https://doi.org/10.1186/1744-9081-6-3

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

EEG alpha-band analysis in the main experiment (in-tune-fast group, n = 15 per group) found a significant Background × Group interaction at 800-1200 ms, with "a larger event-related desynchronization" for the in-tune-fast group during background+. The printed effect size is η² = 0.397, significant after Bonferroni-Holm correction.

> "The interaction at time segment 3 (800 - 1200 ms after word presentation) was qualified by a larger event-related desynchronization for verbal learning with background music (background+) for the in-tune-fast group (F(1,14) = 9.2, p = 0.009, eta2 = 0.397"
