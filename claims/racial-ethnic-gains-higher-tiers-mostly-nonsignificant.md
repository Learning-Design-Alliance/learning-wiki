## Evidence

### Sussman 2022

Sussman, J., Melnick, H., Newton, E., Kriener-Althen, K., Draney, K., Mangione, P., & Gochyyev, P. (2022). Preschool quality and child development: How are learning gains related to program ratings? Learning Policy Institute.

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis disaggregated by race/ethnicity (Table 4). The article attributes nonsignificance partly to small sample sizes after disaggregation and cluster-adjusted standard errors, citing the 1.7-month Tier 5 mathematics estimate for Black children as educationally meaningful though below significance.

> "The direction of the association between tier and growth consistently showed the benefits of higher-tier programs in almost all cases, but results were statistically significant only for preschool children who are Hispanic/Latino/a and, for a few domains, children who are Multiracial"
