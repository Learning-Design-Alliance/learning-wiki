## Evidence

### Giulia Polverini 2025

Giulia Polverini, Jakob Melin, Elias Önerud, and Bor Gregorcic. (2025). Performance of ChatGPT on tasks involving physics visual representations: the case of the Brief Electricity and Magnetism Assessment. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010154](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.21.010154)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative coding of 420 ChatGPT-4o responses on 14 below-average items (3%–60% performance) by two independent coders, with 89% intercoder agreement on the three difficulty types. The article reports 136 responses (32%) contained vision errors and 14% contained incorrect physics laws or rules.

> "This type of difficulty was present in answers to all of the survey items, except items 8, 9, and 10, and in 60% of responses on items 4, 5, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 28, 29, and 30, making it the most prevalent type of difficulty in our data."
