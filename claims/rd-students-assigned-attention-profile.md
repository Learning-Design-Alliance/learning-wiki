## Evidence

### Kulesz 2024

Kulesz, P. A., Roberts, G. J., Francis, D. J., Cirino, P., Walczak, M., & Vaughn, S. (2024). Latent profiles as predictors of response to instruction for students with reading difficulties. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(3). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000832

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Validation cross-tabulation before mixed modeling: 39 (66%) typical students were assigned to Profile 1 versus 20 (34%) to Profile 2, with odds of 1.95; 71 students with RD were assigned to Profile 1 and 198 to Profile 2. Profiles were unrelated to treatment assignment (odds 0.35 for both conditions).

> "Overall, students with RD were more likely to fall to Profile 2 and typically developing students had a higher chance of being assigned to Profile 1."
