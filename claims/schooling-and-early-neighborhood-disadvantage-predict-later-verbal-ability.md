Table 5 models predict Wave-3 verbal ability conditional on Wave-1 PVT (coefficient 0.49) in the combined sample; most SES measures wash out, but years of education by Wave 3 (p = 0.001) and Wave-1 neighborhood disadvantage remain significant. The article states "It is neighborhood disadvantage in earlier life at Wave 1 rather than at Wave 3 that has turned out to be negatively associated with verbal ability."
