# Personalized quiz generation produced significant pretest-to-posttest gains within the experimental group, while the two groups did not differ significantly on the posttest

> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The experimental group improved significantly from pretest to posttest (p<0.01) while the control group's change was not statistically significant (p>0.05), yet no significant between-group difference on the posttest was found. [→ Yi-Ting Huang 2018](#yi-ting-huang-2018)

## Evidence

### Yi-Ting Huang 2018

Yi-Ting Huang, Meng Chang Chen, and Yeali S. Sun. (2018). Development and Evaluation of a Personalized Computer-aided Question Generation for English Learners to Improve Proficiency and Correct Mistakes. IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computing. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1808.09732](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1808.09732)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pretest-posttest comparisons in the same quasi-experiment (experimental group of 72, control of 21 Taiwanese high school students). The independent t-test (p=0.80 pretest, p=0.46 posttest) and Mann-Whitney U test (p=0.99 pretest, p=0.59 posttest) "showed no significant effect on the post-test between the experimental group and the control group".

> "However, the paired sample t-test and the Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed a significant effect of the pretest and the post-test in the experimental group (p<0.01), while the performance of the control group had no statistically significant effect (p>0.05)."
