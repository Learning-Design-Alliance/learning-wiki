## Evidence

### Xiao 2026

Xiao, R., Ye, R., Hou, X., Wen, J., Kumar, H., Liut, M., & Stamper, J. (2026). Bridging GenAI policy and practice: Brief instruction improves learning-oriented prompting in an introductory programming course. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.16033)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Sensitivity analysis in the RCT re-estimating between-condition differences while controlling for winsorized log time-on-task. Median durations ranged from 0.89 to 36.92 minutes across conditions; durability of gains was unrelated to instruction length.

> "Controlling for log-transformed time-on-task (winsorized at the within-condition 95th percentile to remove idle-session outliers), all between-condition differences in learning gains remained significant and essentially unchanged in magnitude."
