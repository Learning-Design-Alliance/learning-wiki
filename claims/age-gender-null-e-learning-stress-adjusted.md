## Evidence

### Kabir Humayun 2021

Kabir Humayun, Hasan Md. Kamrul, Mitra Dipak Kumar. (2021). E-learning readiness and perceived stress among the university students of Bangladesh during COVID-19: a countrywide cross-sectional study. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2021.2009908

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the adjusted multinomial logistic regression in the 1145-student survey; no effect size printed. The authors note the narrow age range (IQR: 21–23 years) as a possible reason.

> "In the multivariable model, the age of the students had no significant association with the perceived e-learning stress."

### Kabir Humayun 2021 (2)

Kabir Humayun, Hasan Md. Kamrul, Mitra Dipak Kumar. (2021). E-learning readiness and perceived stress among the university students of Bangladesh during COVID-19: a countrywide cross-sectional study. Annals of Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2021.2009908

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate-versus-adjusted contrast from the same 1145-student survey; "UOR = 2.22, 95% CI: 1.35–3.66" for female versus male on high stress, not significant after adjustment. No adjusted effect size was printed.

> "The female was significantly associated with high level of e-learning stress (UOR = 2.22, 95% CI: 1.35–3.66) in the bivariate model compared to the male but lost its significance in the adjusted model."
