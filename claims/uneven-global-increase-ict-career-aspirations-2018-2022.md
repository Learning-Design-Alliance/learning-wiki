`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of harmonized country-level PISA 2018–2022 career-expectation data (Figures 2 and 15). The distribution is "positively skewed", with most changes between 0 and 2 percentage points; a right tail of large positive changes and some negative values indicate uneven growth.

> "The distribution of country-level changes in students' interest in ICT-related careers between 2018 and 2022 is positively skewed, with most observations concentrated between 0 and 2 percentage points, indicating that most education systems experienced moderate increases in ICT career aspirations."
