### Shin Sujin 2026

Shin Sujin, Choi Jaehwa, Hong Eunmin, Lee Miji, Go Jinseon, Yu Subin, Lee Minjae. (2026). Development of vignette-based evaluation with generative artificial intelligence (VEGA) for nursing students: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09685-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inductive content analysis of four online focus group interviews, each about 60 minutes, analyzed independently by two researchers. The article reports "four themes and 12 subthemes related to their user experiences." Themes were individualized learning, indirect clinical experience, applicability, and needed improvements.

> "FGIs were conducted with 12 nursing students who had used VEGA, resulting in the identification of four themes and 12 subthemes related to their user experiences (Table 2)."
