## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Exploratory factor analysis of ASPECT responses identified three factors—value of group activity, personal effort, and instructor contribution—with scale reliabilities of 0.91, 0.84, and 0.78 respectively. [→ Wiggins Benjamin L. 2017](#wiggins-benjamin-l-2017)

## Evidence

### Wiggins Benjamin L. 2017

Wiggins Benjamin L., Eddy Sarah L., Wener-Fligner Leah, Freisem Karen, Grunspan Daniel Z., Theobald Elli J., Timbrook Jerry, Crowe Alison J. (2017). ASPECT: A Survey to Assess Student Perspective of Engagement in an Active-Learning Classroom. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-08-0244

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Exploratory factor analysis of online ASPECT responses in one quarter of introductory biology (n = 425) using oblique promax rotation. The value-of-group-activity scale had "Cronbach's alpha for this scale was 0.91" and explained 30% of response variation; personal effort (alpha 0.84) explained 12% and instructor contribution (alpha 0.78) explained 13%.
