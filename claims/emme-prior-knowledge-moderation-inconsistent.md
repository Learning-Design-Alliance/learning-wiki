### Emhardt 2023

Visualizing a Task Performer's Gaze to Foster Observers' Performance and Learning—a Systematic Literature Review on Eye Movement Modeling Examples. `q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis of relative frequencies across included experiments; the review links the descriptive pattern to the expertise reversal effect but reports that "the moderating effects of prior knowledge on the effectiveness on EMME failed to reach statistical significance" in prior meta-analyses.

> "About 72% of the included studies with learners with no prior knowledge found positive effects of EMME on immediate learning, compared to 60% of studies using EMME with learners who had higher levels of prior knowledge."
