## Evidence

### de Jong Zuzana 2010

de Jong Zuzana, van Nies Jessica AB, Peters Sonja WM, Vink Sylvia, Dekker Friedo W, Scherpbier Albert. (2010). Interactive seminars or small group tutorials in preclinical medical education: results of a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-10-79

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test questionnaire B completed by 83 of 107 RCT participants; 5-point Likert satisfaction ratings compared by chi-square. Both formats rated teachers and problems similarly (87% vs 86% satisfied with tutors).

> "Significantly more participants in small group tutorials than participants in interactive seminars expressed satisfaction with the educational format (86% versus 39%; chi-square; P < 0.001) and would again choose the format to which they had been randomized in this trial (56% versus 24%; chi square; P < 0.001)."
