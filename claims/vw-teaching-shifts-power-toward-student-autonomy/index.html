Evidence¶

Dai 2023¶

Dai, K., Garcia, J., & Olave-Encina, K. (2023). In-between worlds: Chilean university lecturers' experiences of teaching transition between face-to-face and virtual reality contexts during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10217-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of lecturer interviews in the same qualitative study. The authors report that "in SL, they are more independent", contrasting student dependence across the two settings.

"However, in the face-to-face context, students show a stronger dependence on the lecturers' whereas, in SL, they are more independent. This prompts the modification of lecturers' teaching agency."
