Enhanced Proactive Reading 2006¶

Enhanced Proactive Reading. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC synthesis of the English Language Composite (Woodcock Language Proficiency Battery-Revised) in both trials. Effect sizes were –0.17 (Vaughn, Cirino, et al.) and 0.13 (Vaughn, Mathes, et al.), both ns; the domain average effect size was –0.02 and the average improvement index –1 percentile points (range –7 to +5).

"Neither study found a statistically significant or substantively important effect on English language development."

Enhanced Proactive Reading 2006 (2)¶

Enhanced Proactive Reading. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Domain-level WWC calculations for English language development across both studies, with a domain average effect size of –0.02. Subscale findings (Picture Vocabulary, Listening Comprehension, Verbal Analogies) were likewise all non-significant in Appendix A4.

"The average improvement index for the English language development domain is –1 percentile points, with a range of –7 to +5 percentile points."
