## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a general linear model on the Soundfield Effect score, IQ was a mildly significant predictor (F = 4.5, p = 0.036), with children of lower IQ showing greater reading fluency improvement during SFA-On; order, age, baseline reading score, working memory, speech discrimination in noise, and attention were not significant. [→ Shiels 2025](#shiels-2025)
