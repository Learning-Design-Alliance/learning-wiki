Yuqing Zhou 2025¶

Yuqing Zhou, Björn Lindström, Alexander Soutschek, Pyungwon Kang, Shihui Han, Philippe N. Tobler & Grit Hein. (2025). Learning reduces ingroup bias more with perceived losses than gains across cultures. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00362-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Moderation analysis in the East Asian sample (Loss frame) linking the Wneg_in parameter to post-vs-pre intergroup impression change. The main effect of Wneg_in was not significant (β = −0.028, t(47) = −0.21, p = 0.83); the moderation was driven mainly by outgroup impression change (β = −0.41, t(45) = −2.33, p = 0.024).

"ingroup identiﬁcation scores, Wneg_in, and their interactions as predictors and the intergroup impression change as dependent variable showed a signiﬁcant moderation effect of ingroup identiﬁcation ( β = 0.50, t(45) = 2.97, p = 0.005)"

Related Claims¶

  • East Asian participants show stronger ingroup identification and stronger initial ingroup bias than Western participants — related
  • Both cultures show initial ingroup bias: more positive impressions, higher expectancy and closeness for ingroup than outgroup before interaction — related
  • In East Asian participants, the Loss-frame bias reduction occurs only for participants with low ingroup identification; on average outgroup impressions improved more than ingroup impressions changed — related
  • In Western participants, stronger weight on negative ingroup prediction errors (Wneg_in) in the Loss frame predicts greater reduction of ingroup bias, driven by ingroup impression change — related
  • In Western participants, loss-framed but not gain-framed ingroup experiences reduce the initial ingroup bias in impressions
