## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When students choose among alternative historical materials under time pressure, a shortcut toward group understanding can bypass one member's uncertainty, leaving intersubjectivity with partial agreement and a gap between the social and technology objects. [→ Mørch 2024](#mrch-2024)

## Evidence

### Mørch 2024

Mørch, A. I., Andersen, R., Eie, S., & Mifsud, L. (2024). Emergent group understanding: Investigating intersubjectivity in sociotechnical interdependencies. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09432-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interaction analysis (Extract 3) of group 7 choosing between two aerial photos: Nora's questioning utterance ("Is it from 1947?!") was ignored, and the group chose the older picture. The authors interpret the episode as a gap between social and technology objects preventing seamless integration into a knowledge object.

> "When students get stuck in time-consuming technical problem-solving, academic issues are backgrounded. In this case, the group's intersubjectivity proceeded with partial agreement."
