`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's Discussion section reports the interprofessional composition of the 30 included SBLEs. The authors call this concerning evidence that nurses and other interprofessional team members are not consistently trained together.

> "we were surprised to find that only 14 SBLEs included other members of the interprofessional team. Seven of the 14 involved actual participation by a member of another discipline, while in the remaining 7, the interprofessional roles were acted out by nursing students, practicing nurses, faculty, or other actors."
