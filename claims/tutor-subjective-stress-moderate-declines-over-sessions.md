## Evidence

### Hundertmark Jan 2019

Hundertmark Jan, Alvarez Simone, Loukanova Svetla, Schultz Jobst-Hendrik. (2019). Stress and stressors of medical student near-peer tutors during courses: a psychophysiological mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education 19:95. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1521-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Repeated-measures assessment of 60 tutors on days 1, 2 and 4 of their courses using VAS-Stress before, during and after sessions, analysed with RM-ANOVAs. The article reports "medium subjective stress levels" that fall after sessions and recovery, and "progressively decrease over course days".

> "Tutors report medium subjective stress levels of M = 3.48 (SD = 2.36) 30 min and M = 3.80 (SD = 2.42) immediately before session start. After session completion, they report lower values, M = 1.97 (SD = 1.84), and M = 1.22 (SD = 1.41) after recovery. Stress levels progressively decrease over course days"
