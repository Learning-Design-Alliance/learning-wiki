## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Both pre and post E-CLASS scores were indistinguishable from national norms, so the overall effect of the admixture of learning tasks was not different from business-as-usual cookbook labs. [→ Doucette 2019](#doucette-2019)

## Evidence

### Doucette 2019

Doucette, D., Clark, R., and Singh, C. (2019). What's happening in traditional and inquiry-based introductory labs? An integrative analysis at a large research university. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.doucette](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.doucette)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Comparison of the course's E-CLASS responses with the national norms established in the instrument's original study. The article concludes "the overall effect of this admixture of learning tasks was not different from 'business as usual' cookbook labs."

> "Averaging over all the responses to all the statements, we ﬁnd that our pre and post scores are each indistinguishable from their national norms."

### Doucette 2019 (2)

Doucette, D., Clark, R., and Singh, C. (2019). What's happening in traditional and inquiry-based introductory labs? An integrative analysis at a large research university. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.doucette](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.doucette)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The authors state the impact of the six inquiry-based Real-Time Physics labs could not be separated from cookbook-style labs because post scores did not differ from national norms.

> "Nonetheless, we cannot separate the impact of this inquiry-based approach from cookbook-style labs, as the E-CLASS post scores do not differ from the national norms."
