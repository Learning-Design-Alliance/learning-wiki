## Evidence

### Ahmet Basal 2016

Ahmet Basal, Selahattin Yilmaz, Asli Tanriverdi, & Lutfiye Sari. (2016). Effectiveness of Mobile Applications in Vocabulary Teaching. Contemporary Educational Technology, 7(1), 47-59. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/cet

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison in the Procedures section: the pretest on the 40 target idioms showed no significant difference between groups, and Table 3 reports t = -1.045, df = 52, p = .284, with means of 15.24 (control) and 17.48 (experimental). Equivalence was not formally tested beyond this t-test.

> "Based on this pre test results, there  were no significant difference between the two groups, indicating that their knowledge level was similar on the selected idioms."
