Evidence¶

Ana Katalinic 2026¶

Ana Katalinic, Vanja Slavuj, Danijela Jaksic. (2026). Artificial Intelligence in Online Education: A Systematic Review of Its Impact on Learner Engagement and Satisfaction. Educ. Sci. 2026, 16, 389. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16030389

q2 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of 30 studies (2020–2025) identified via PRISMA screening of Scopus and WoS. The review's thematic coding of included studies found that "eight major AI-driven factors" shape satisfaction and engagement, listed as personalized pathways, engagement tracking, feedback and assessment, chatbot support, predictive analytics, teamwork, tutoring, and intuitive interfaces.

"Based on the analysis of the selected publications, eight major AI-driven factors can be identified as central to having effect on learner satisfaction and engagement in digital learning environments."
