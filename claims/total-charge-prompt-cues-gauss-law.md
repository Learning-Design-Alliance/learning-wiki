## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox 2012

Bethany R. Wilcox, Marcos D. Caballero, Rachel E. Pepper and Steven J. Pollock. (2012). Upper-division Student Understanding of Coulomb's Law: Difficulties with Continuous Charge Distributions. [doi:10.1063/1.4789741](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4789741)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-semester comparison of exam prompts in the disk problem. The semester without the total-charge prompt (N=25) produced no Gauss' Law attempts, which the authors interpret as prompt cueing of resources.

> "In the remaining semester (N=25), the students were only asked to describe the angular charge distribution. No students in this semester attempted to use Gauss' Law to solve for the potential."
