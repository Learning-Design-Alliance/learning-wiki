## Evidence

### Parchment Amelia 2025

Parchment Amelia, Lawrence Wendy, Rahman Em, Townsend Nick, Wainwright Elaine, Wainwright David. (2025). Increasing confidence and competence in supporting behaviour change in physiotherapy practice using Making Every Contact Count Healthy Conversation Skills: a before and after evaluation. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12513-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank tests on 10-point confidence ratings taken directly before and directly after training (n = 23). Medians rose from 6 (IQR 5–8) to 8 (IQR 7–8); the article reports a "significantly increased" change. For the subset completing 6-month follow-up (n = 11), confidence remained significantly elevated (Z = −2.714, p = 0.007).

> "Self-reported confidence of physiotherapists in supporting behaviour change in patients significantly increased, pre- (median (IQR) = 6 (5–8)) to post- (median (IQR) = 8 (7–8)) MECC HCS training (Z = −2.742, p = 0.006) (n = 23)."
