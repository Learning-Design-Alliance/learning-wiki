## Evidence

### Wei Tianhong 2026

Wei Tianhong, Ke Mujing, Zhang Bo, He Zhiyou, Xie Xiuyun. (2026). Structured phantom-assisted simulation for early abdominal ultrasound skill training in ultrasound medicine residents: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09395-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with a historical comparison cohort of 58 PGY2 ultrasound medicine residents (intervention n = 32; comparison n = 26). The intervention cohort scored higher on the SP-based OSCE, with "a mean difference of 3.12 points (95% CI 1.06–5.18; P = 0.004; Cohen’s d = 0.80)".

> "The intervention cohort achieved significantly higher OSCE scores than the historical comparison cohort (87.16 ± 3.81 vs. 84.04 ± 4.00), with a mean difference of 3.12 points (95% CI 1.06–5.18; P = 0.004; Cohen’s d = 0.80)."
