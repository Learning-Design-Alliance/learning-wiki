## Evidence

### Selbing 2025

Selbing, I., Becker, N., Pan, Y., Lindström, B., & Olsson, A. (2025). Effects of described demonstrator ability on brain and behavior when learning from others. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00292-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GLMM analysis of 38 participants' choices in the observational shock-avoidance task, with choice optimality as dependent variable and Description and Trial as predictors. The article reports a main effect of Trial but "no signiﬁcant effect of Description on choice optimality (p = 0.996)". Equivalence was not tested.

> "Our analysis showed a main effect of Trial (p = 0.002), indicative of learning over time but, contrary to our predictions, no signiﬁcant effect of Description on choice optimality (p = 0.996)."
