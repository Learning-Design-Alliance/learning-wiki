## Evidence

### Cekiso 2012

Cekiso, M. (2012). Effects of strategy instruction on the reading comprehension and strategy awareness of Grade 11 English Second Language learners in the Eastern Cape. Reading & Writing 3(1), Art. #23. https://doi.org/10.4102/rw.v3i1.23

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group study of 60 Grade 11 Xhosa-speaking ESL learners in one East London high school, with intact classes randomly assigned (N = 30 each). The article reports a statistically significant post-test difference (p < 0.05) favouring the experimental group; Table 3 also prints a practical significance d-value of 0.56 for this contrast.

> "An analysis of the post-test reading comprehension scores of Grade 11 ESL learners in the experimental and control groups indicated that learners in the experimental group achieved statistically significantly (p < 0.05) higher mean scores on the reading comprehension test"
