Jacqueline Żammit 2024¶

Jacqueline Żammit. (2024). Could virtual reality be the next approach for international students learning Maltese? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10375-9

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Online survey of the 25 focus-group teachers, reported in the Survey results section. In the article's words, "A significant 76% of respondents believed in the effectiveness of VR tools in learning", while 56% believed VR effective for language learning; no respondent rejected either idea.

"A significant 76% of respondents believed in the effectiveness of VR tools in learning (see Table 1). However, the potential of VR instruments to facilitate language learning was less evident, with only 56% of respondents reporting a belief in their effectiveness"
