Whitehead 2019¶

Evangelin Whitehead. (2019). Application of Meta-Cognitive Strategy Instruction in Listening Comprehension to the Level III Student-Teachers. The Excellence in Education Journal, 8(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1228050

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group comparison of the experimental group's two post-tests (Post-test I M = 52.4, SD = 7.5; Post-test II M = 64, SD = 8.7), showing continued gains during the training semester; the article reports "significant difference" at 0.05.

"Achievement scores of Post-test 1 and Post-test II of the experimental group were compared and there existed significant difference between these two scores in that the 't' value was 5.85 which is significant at 0.05 level of significance."
