Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

Thematic synthesis of 19 included K–12 mathematics studies; the review reports that high-performing students "profit more from using a DLS with embedded and extracted analytics" and that LA usage overall had a generally positive effect on student learning.

> "High-performing students profit more from using a DLS with embedded and extracted analytics without receiving adaptive teacher instruction (Faber et al., 2017; Kalloo & Mohan, 2011a; Lin & Yang, 2021)."
