### van Tetering AAC 2025

Mono-Professional Simulation-Based Obstetric Training in a Low-Resource Setting: Stepped-Wedge Cluster Randomized Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/54911

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same stepped-wedge cluster randomized trial (17,496 births), generalized linear mixed-effects models with a logit link estimated intervention effects on each mortality outcome. The article reports "No statistically significant change was found in the maternal mortality rate"; no effect size was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "No statistically significant change was found in the maternal mortality rate (OR 0.80, 95% CI 0.27‐2.32; P=.68) or the perinatal mortality rate (OR 0.99, 95% CI 0.87‐1.13; P=.87)."

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`
