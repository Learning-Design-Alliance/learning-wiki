## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students perceived a moderate level of effectiveness of the flipped teaching classroom (overall M=3.49, SD=0.69 on a 5-point scale), with the highest-rated items being development of logical thinking (M=3.77), provision of extra information (M=3.68), and improvement of the application of knowledge (M=3.64).
