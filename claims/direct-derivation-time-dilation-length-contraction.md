# Time dilation and length contraction can be derived directly from the postulates without the Lorentz transformation, using the relativity principle to handle both effects simultaneously

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` A light signal traversing a moving train from front to back and reflected back yields time dilation and length contraction together, with the relativity principle reducing the unknowns via l/t = l′/t′ = v. [→ Alfred Ziegler 2007](#alfred-ziegler-2007)

## Evidence

### Alfred Ziegler 2007

Alfred Ziegler. (2007). The role of the two postulates of special relativity. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.0988 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0708.0988](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0708.0988)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Analytical derivation in Section VI: the constancy postulate gives t′ = 2l/c in the train frame and the platform time from the reflected signal; the relativity principle then yields t′/t = √(1 − v²/c²). The transverse light-clock route is given in Appendix C.

> "Using the relativity principle directly it is possible to deal with time dilation and length con- traction at the same time"
