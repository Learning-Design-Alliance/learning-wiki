## Evidence

### Laura Dörrenbächer-Ulrich 2024

Laura Dörrenbächer-Ulrich, Jörn R. Sparfeldt, Franziska Perels. (2024). Knowing how to learn: development and validation of the strategy knowledge test for self-regulated learning (SKT-SRL) for college students. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09379-w

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Expert rating study with 17 German-speaking SRL researchers who assigned scenarios to phases and components, suggested strategies, ranked strategies, and rated usefulness. The a priori useful strategies were always ranked in the top three by experts, and all useful strategies had mean usefulness ratings higher than three.

> "The three strategies that we classified a priori as useful were always among the top three expert strategies while the three less useful a priori strategies were always ranked in the bottom three expert strategies."
