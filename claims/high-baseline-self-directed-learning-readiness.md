## Evidence

### Reviriego E 2014

Reviriego E, Cidoncha MA, Asua J, Gagnon MP, Mateos M, Gárate L, de Lorenzo E, González RM. (2014). Online training course on critical appraisal for nurses: adaptation and assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-136

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

The pre-course administration of the SDLRSNE in the pre-post study showed a high baseline readiness: "agree" and "totally agree" were selected by most participants on most items, with total scores "from 136 to 183 out of 200" and a mean of 157.92 (SD 14.48).

> "The total scores ranged from 136 to 183 out of 200, with a mean of 157.92 (SD: 14.48), reflecting the overall good level of readiness for self-directed learning among participants at baseline."
