Evidence¶

Koch 2025¶

Koch, M., & Peters Hinton, V. (2025). Voices of Undergraduate Students Taking Introductory Statistics Courses [Project Report]. Digital Promise.

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview finding on courseware content: "Around three-quarters of the interviewees indicated that relating content to the real world helped them retain and comprehend course information." Students said examples contextualized abstract concepts.

Koch 2025 (2)¶

Koch, M., & Peters Hinton, V. (2025). Voices of Undergraduate Students Taking Introductory Statistics Courses [Project Report]. Digital Promise.

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Theme cited by eight students: "Students value diverse and authentic examples that connect to the real-world." One student example notes Lumen One uses "real-world data like unemployment rates among different racial groups."

"They [Lumen One] use real-world data like unemployment rates among different racial groups, which helps understand the application of statistics in societal issues."
