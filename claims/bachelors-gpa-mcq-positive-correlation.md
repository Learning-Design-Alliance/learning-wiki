## Evidence

### Panczyk M 2017

Panczyk M, Juszczyk G, Zarzeka A, Samoliński Ł, Belowska J, Cieślak I, Gotlib J. (2017). Evidence-based selection process to the Master of Public Health program at Medical University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1007-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Pearson product-moment correlation analysis of 605 candidates' data. The total score correlation was rp = 0.31 (P < 0.001), a printed effect size of rp = 0.31; subtest correlations ranged from rp = 0.11 (Scientific Method) to rp = 0.24 (Organization in Health Care). The authors state correlations were statistically significant both within subtests and for the total score.
