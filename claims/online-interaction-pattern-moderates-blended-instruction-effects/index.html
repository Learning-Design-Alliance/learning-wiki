Evidence¶

Li 2022¶

Li, B., Yu, Q., & Yang, F. (2022). The effect of blended instruction on student performance: A meta-analysis of 106 empirical studies from China and abroad. Best Evidence in Chinese Education, 10(2):1395-1403. https://doi.org/10.15354/bece.22.ar018

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Moderator analysis grouping teacher-student online interactions into four patterns across the pooled studies. The between-group test (p = 0.000 < 0.05) indicates "significant difference in the effects of distinct teacher-student online interactive patterns on student learning performance".

"SMDs for synchronous + asynchronous interaction, synchronous interaction, asynchronous interaction, and absence of interaction are 1.189, 1.134, 0.521, and 0.130 respectively, which means that synchronous + asynchronous interaction and synchronous interaction have the most significant effects in promoting student learning performance"
