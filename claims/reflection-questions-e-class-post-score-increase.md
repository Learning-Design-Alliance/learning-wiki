## Evidence

### Danny Doucette 2022

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Students' attitudes toward experimental physics in a conceptual inquiry-based introductory physics lab. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2021-0121](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2021-0121)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear regression on matched pre/post E-CLASS responses collected over four semesters at a large public US university, controlling for pre-score, course stream, semester, high school GPA, and gender; the Reflection coefficient was "3 .80 ± 2.14 points" on the post score.

> "Thus, adding reﬂection questions to the RealTime Physics curriculum is asso ciated with an increase of 3 .80 ± 2.14 points on the E-CLASS post score, all else being equal."

### Danny Doucette 2022 (2)

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh. (2022). Students' attitudes toward experimental physics in a conceptual inquiry-based introductory physics lab. [doi:10.1139/cjp-2021-0121](https://doi.org/10.1139/cjp-2021-0121)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Raw means for the RealTime Physics + Reflections curriculum were "pre E-CLASS scores of 16 .4 ± 0.8 and post E-CLASS scores of 16 .3 ± 0.9", i.e., essentially unchanged across the semester.

> "Meanwhile, students in labs that used the RealTime Physics + Reﬂections curriculum averag ed pre E-CLASS scores of 16 .4 ± 0.8 and post E-CLASS scores of 16 .3 ± 0.9 as well."
