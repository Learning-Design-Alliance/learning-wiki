Evaluation and analysis of theoretical knowledge proficiency and practical skills among parasitic disease practitioners in Hainan province: a cross-sectional survey. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1600908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional assessment of 54 county-level representatives in Hainan using a 50-item electronic theoretical exam. The article reports the species-level scoring-rate difference as significant, with "Plasmodium spp. (82.22%)" highest and other species lowest at 53.31%; no effect size is printed.

> "Further analysis of scoring rates across different parasitic species showed a significant difference (χ2 = 81.563, p < 0.001) (shown in Supplementary Table S2). The highest scoring rate was recorded for Plasmodium spp. (82.22%), while the lowest was recorded for other species (53.31%)."
