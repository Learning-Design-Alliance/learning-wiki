Certificate in Computing students who studied through interactive multimedia CD achieved significantly higher post-test scores than those who studied online (Mean 61.85 vs 49.10, t = 5.37 significant at 0.05), while CD vs print and print vs online differences were not significant. → Dikshit et al. 2013

Evidence¶

Dikshit et al. 2013¶

Jyotsna Dikshit, Suresh Garg, Santosh Panda. (2013). Pedagogic Effectiveness of Print, Interactive Multimedia, and Online Resources: A Case Study of IGNOU. International Journal of Instruction, 6(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085398

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-test–post-test experiment with 60 randomly selected IGNOU Certificate in Computing students in three groups of 20, each studying the same Computer Networks module in a different mode for 15 hours. The t-tests showed "the t 3 of 5.37 was higher than the table value of 2.86, and was therefore significant"; post-test means were 61.85 (CD), 56.30 (print), 49.10 (online).

"while the t–ratio for CD vs Print and Print vs Online were not significant, the t 3 of 5.37 was higher than the table value of 2.86, and was therefore significant."
