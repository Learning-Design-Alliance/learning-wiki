# Groups differed at pre-test on relating knowledge with daily life but not on inquiry learning skill perceptions

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Pre-test relating knowledge with daily life scores differed significantly between groups, while pre-test inquiry learning skill perception scores did not. [→ Balım 2016](#balm-2016)

## Evidence

### Balım 2016

Balım, A. G., İnel-Ekici, D., & Özcan, E. (2016). Concept Cartoons Supported Problem Based Learning Method in Middle School Science Classrooms. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n2p272

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline tests across the three groups (n=553) before the 16-hour intervention: the article reports "F(2,552)=12.23, p=.000" for pre-test relating knowledge with daily life and "F(2,552)=1.52, p=.220" for inquiry skill perceptions, motivating use of MANCOVA.

> "there is a significant difference between groups' pre-test levels of relating knowledge with daily life scores (F(2,552)=12.23, p=.000) while there is no significant difference between groups' inquiry based learning skill perception scores (F(2,552)=1.52, p=.220)."
