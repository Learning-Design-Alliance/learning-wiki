## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across digital modules, videos, web applications and virtual meeting platforms, students reported better engagement and satisfaction with digital clinical skills teaching. [→ McGee RG 2024](#mcgee-rg-2024)

## Evidence

### McGee RG 2024

McGee RG, Wark S, Mwangi F, Drovandi A, Alele F, Malau-Aduli BS, The ACHIEVE Collaboration. (2024). Digital learning of clinical skills and its impact on medical students’ academic performance: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06471-2

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Narrative synthesis of satisfaction findings across the 27 included studies. The review reports "a consensus of better engagement and satisfaction levels" not specific to any one modality; no pooled satisfaction estimate was possible because unit-of-analysis issues precluded proportional meta-analysis.

> "Despite mixed efficacy for improving clinical skills there was a consensus of better engagement and satisfaction levels with digital clinical skills teaching."
