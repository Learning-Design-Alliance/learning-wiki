# Advanced learners outperformed novices on near transfer at immediate post-test but not after the self-study phase

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` There was a significant learners' prior ability × test moment interaction on near transfer: advanced learners outperformed novices on the immediate post-test but no longer on the delayed post-test. [→ Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017](#montpetit-tourangeau-k-2017)

## Evidence

### Montpetit-Tourangeau K 2017

Montpetit-Tourangeau K, Dyer JO, Hudon A, Windsor M, Charlin B, Mamede S, van Gog T. (2017). Fostering clinical reasoning in physiotherapy: comparing the effects of concept map study and concept map completion after example study in novice and advanced learners. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1076-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up t-tests after a significant prior ability × test moment interaction (large effect size, ηp² = .100) on near transfer in the 2×2×2 mixed ANOVA; the prior ability main effect was significant (ηp² = .096) with advanced learners outperforming novices.

> "advanced learners (69.6 ± 19.4%) outperformed (t(59) = −2.87, p = .006, two-tailed) novice learners (56.4 ± 16.6%) on the immediate post-test but no longer on the delayed post-test (after the self-directed study phase; advanced: 86.6 ± 10.8%, novice: 84.4 ± 11.9%; t(59) = .748, p = .457, two-tailed)"
