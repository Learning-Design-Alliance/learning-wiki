# Participants' self-assessed confidence and performance improved after the curriculum

> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r1` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2026 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Confidence performing awake fiberoptic intubation rose significantly from 3.0 (1.5) to 5.1 (1.2), P < .001, from pre-test to post-test after the curriculum, in one single-group pre-post study. [→ Haas D 2026](#haas-d-2026)
`q3 i?` Self-rated performance compared with peers at the same training stage rose significantly from 3.7 (1.5) to 5.0 (1.2), P < .001, from pre-test to post-test after the curriculum, in one single-group pre-post study. [→ Haas D 2026 (2)](#haas-d-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Haas D 2026

Haas D, Fredette J, Murphy KA, Deitchman A, Anderson J, Blodgett M. (2026). Novel Simulation-based Awake Fiberoptic Intubation Curriculum: Pilot Study. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.50778

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre- and post-intervention self-assessment survey on a 1–7 Likert scale in the 46-participant pilot; confidence ratings improved "3.0 (1.5) to 5.1 (1.2), P < .001". No effect size was printed.

> "Improvement was seen among participants’ rating of “how confident do you feel carrying out an awake fiberoptic intubation”: 3.0 (1.5) to 5.1 (1.2), P < .001."

### Haas D 2026 (2)

Haas D, Fredette J, Murphy KA, Deitchman A, Anderson J, Blodgett M. (2026). Novel Simulation-based Awake Fiberoptic Intubation Curriculum: Pilot Study. Western Journal of Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5811/westjem.50778

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same self-assessment survey; self-rated performance relative to peers improved "3.7 (1.5) to 5.0 (1.2), P < .001", as shown in Figure 1. No effect size was printed.

> "Likewise, improvements were seen in response to “how well do you think you can perform an awake fiberoptic intubation compared to others at your stage of training”: 3.7 (1.5) to 5.0 (1.2), P < .001 (Figure 1)."
