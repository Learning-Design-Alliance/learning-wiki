## Evidence

### Gimeno A. 2026

Gimeno A., Alberola‐Zorrilla P., Blasco‐Serra A., Sánchez‐Zuriaga D., Zaragozá R. (2026). Online anatomical images are an effective resource to improve knowledge acquisition and practical exam performance in gross anatomy. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70254

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of Q1 (n=72) and Q2 (n=90) quiz scores among intervention-group students; the article reports median 7.3 on Q2 and a Hodges–Lehmann median difference of 1.250. A paired analysis of both-quiz takers (n=45) found a mean difference of 0.8812 ± 0.2506 (p ≤ 0.001).

> "students achieved higher scores in the second quiz (median 7.3, Inter-quartile range: 6.5–7.9) when they had already access to the images and had enough time for self-study, with the difference being statistically significant (Mann–Whitney test, p < 0.0001****)."
