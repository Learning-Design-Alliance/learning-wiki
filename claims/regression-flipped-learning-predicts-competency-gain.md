## Evidence

### Budak Vildan 2025

Budak Vildan, Kılıç Hülya Fırat, Cevheroğlu Seda. (2025). The Impact of a Flipped Learning on Nursing Students' Patient Safety Competencies and Satisfaction With the Education Method: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Nursing & Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1111/nhs.70068

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate linear regression (enter method) on 69 students with education model, age, gender, perceived knowledge, and error experience as predictors of PSCSE score. The model was significant, "F (5–63) = 8.392; p < 0.001", adjusted R² = 0.352.

> "flipped learning (β = 0.31; t = 2.980; p = 0.004) and an inadequate level of knowledge on patient safety (β = −0.46; t = −4.453; p < 0.001) were the independent variables that positively influenced the observed increase"
