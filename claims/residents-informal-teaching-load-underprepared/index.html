AlMarzooqi LM 2026¶

AlMarzooqi LM, Goswami N. (2026). Residents as Teachers in postgraduate medical education: a structured review of global evidence and emerging gaps. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09920-2

q2 · i? · review · r2

This is the review's thematic synthesis (Theme 1) of 32 studies spanning surveys, qualitative designs and interventions. The review reports this as a cross-study pattern: residents "recognize teaching as integral to their professional identity" while operating "with minimal formal preparation and limited institutional support."

"Across specialties and healthcare systems, studies consistently show that residents recognize teaching as integral to their professional identity while simultaneously operating with minimal formal preparation and limited institutional support."
