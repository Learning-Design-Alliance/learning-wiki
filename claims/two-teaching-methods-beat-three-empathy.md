## Evidence

### Ngo Hazel 2025

Ngo Hazel, Sokolovic Nina, Hu Julia, Jenkins Jennifer. (2025). A systematic review identifying effective teaching methods and their combinations for increasing empathy in physicians: pairwise and network meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07917-x

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-regression of number of teaching methods in the pairwise meta-analysis (Table 2: Q = 10.02, p = .04). The article reports interventions "with two teaching methods (d = 0.96, 95% CI: 0.47, 1.45) performed better than those with three teaching methods". The authors caution only a small number of studies represented these combinations and replication is needed.

> "The number of teaching methods in an intervention was associated with effectiveness, such that interventions with two teaching methods (d = 0.96, 95% CI: 0.47, 1.45) performed better than those with three teaching methods (d = 0.36, 95% CI: 0.17, 0.40)."
