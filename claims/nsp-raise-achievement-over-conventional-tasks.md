## Evidence

### Kuhn 2023

Kuhn, Jochen; Müller, Andreas; Vogt, Patrik. (2023). Newspaper Story Problems and Other Tasks for Context Based Physics Education: A Research Based Report on Classroom Practice. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.11149](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.11149)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison for the electrical energy topic; EC and CC received identical content, difficulty, teacher, and investigation-elaboration procedure, differing only in task format (newspaper vs. textbook). The study found "considerable differences in achievement between EC and CC"; the conclusions report an effect size of d > 0.9 for learning.

> "In our research, considerable differences in achievement between EC and CC were found (see Fig. 6b). Percentage gains of EC compared to CC were more than 15% for the post-test and almost 30% for the follow-up test, see Tab. 5b."
