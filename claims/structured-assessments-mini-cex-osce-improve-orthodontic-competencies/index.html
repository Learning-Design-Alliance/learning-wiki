Evidence¶

Baxmann Martin 2025¶

Baxmann Martin, Kárpáti Krisztina, Baráth Zoltán. (2025). A systematic review of the content and delivery of clinical knowledge in orthodontic postgraduate programs. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07361-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that structured assessments "provide structured feedback that improves diagnostic accuracy, patient communication, and procedural skills". Mini-CEX is used as a work-based assessment in real clinical settings, while OSCEs offer a standardized evaluation environment.

"These approaches provide structured feedback that improves diagnostic accuracy, patient communication, and procedural skills, reinforcing residents' ability to make evidence-based clinical decisions"
