## Evidence

### Bashirian S 2020

Bashirian S, Barati M, Karami M, Hamzeh B, Ezati E. (2020). Effectiveness of E-Learning Program in Preventing WP Smoking in Adolescent Females in West of Iran by Applying Prototype-Willingness Model: A Randomized Controlled Trial. J Res Health Sci. 20(4): e00497. https://doi.org/10.34172/jrhs.2020.31

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 221 adolescent females (110 experimental, 111 control after attrition) in four Kermanshah schools; behavior measured before and 3 months after a 5-session e-learning program. The article reports "a significant difference in frequency of WP smoking in the experimental group" (P=0.003); between-group post-test difference was P=0.020. No effect size is printed.

> "Results of the McNemar’s test showed a significant difference in frequency of WP smoking in the experimental group, in the last month before and after the educational intervention (P=0.003)."
