Supplementary structural equation modelling of RIF platform data comparing a one-factor model (all 12 task groups on one spatial ability factor) against a two-factor hierarchical model. The article reports "The results support the discriminant validity of these measures as two discrete factors."

> "We compare, in Supplementary Analysis 1, two structural equation models using the RIF data for this paper. One model contained a one-factor structure with all 12 task groups from this paper loading onto a single spatial ability factor."
