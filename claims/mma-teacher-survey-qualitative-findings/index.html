My Math Academy evidence summary 2022¶

My Math Academy evidence summary. (2022). Age of Learning. https://www.ageoflearning.com/schools/systemcheck/

q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative component of the Texas matched study (Bang et al., 2022): a survey of 51 early grade math teachers, plus sample interviews highlighting the program as engaging, adaptable to different learning levels, and user friendly.

"a survey of 51 early grade math teachers almost unanimously felt that the program supported student interest, self-confidence, and enjoyment of learning math"

My Math Academy evidence summary 2022 (2)¶

My Math Academy evidence summary. (2022). Age of Learning. https://www.ageoflearning.com/schools/systemcheck/

q1 · i? · associational · r?

Qualitative component of the randomized Southern California study (Thai et al., 2022): a post-participation survey of treatment teachers found unanimously positive experiences, with over 70% reporting meaningful impact on math skills and interest.

"over 70% of teachers reported "meaningful" (medium or high) impact on students' overall math learning, counting, number identification, and addition and subtraction skills"
