## Evidence

### Liang Yao 2024

Liang Yao, Huang Hua, Tan Yu-Bao, Li Tang, Huang Wan, Zhang Qing-Long, Liu Zhuo-Wei, Kuang Ming. (2024). Construction and implementation of a laparoscopic skill training course based on a smartphone application and virtual reality. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06066-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Assessment of 91 residents on a laparoscopic training simulator for Peg Transfer, Precision Cutting, and Suture with Intracorporeal Knot, scored by two senior surgeons using a rating scale and TABLT. The article reports the medians and states "No significant differences in task performance were observed among the batches".

> "The median rating scale scores for the three tasks were 2.75, 7.5, and 11 points, respectively, with a total median score of 21.3 points; median spending times were 55, 118, and 150 s; and median TABLT scores were 532, 465, and 445 s. No significant differences in task performance were observed among the batches (Fig. 2A and F)."
