# Students score significantly higher on a pictorially represented measurement-comparison item than on an isomorphic numerical item

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Post-instruction students answered the pictorially represented item correctly more often than the numerically represented item, a statistically significant difference with a moderate effect size (d = 0.31 ± 0.05). [→ Gayle Geschwind 2023](#gayle-geschwind-2023)

## Evidence

### Gayle Geschwind 2023

Gayle Geschwind, Michael Vignal, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2023). Representational differences in how students compare measurements. [doi:10.1119/perc.2023.pr.geschwind](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2023.pr.geschwind)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Post-instruction SPRUCE administration in 12 courses at eight institutions (N = 670 valid post responses). Scores are (25± 3)% on the NRI and (40± 4)% on the PRI; a Mann-Whitney U test gave p = 2.1 × 10−8 and Cohen's d = 0.31 ± 0.05, "showing a moderate effect size."

> "Students score an average of (25± 3)% on the NRI and an average of (40± 4)% on the PRI on the post-test, with the error indicating 95% confidence interval."
