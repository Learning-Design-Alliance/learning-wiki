## Evidence

### Alghadir Ahmad H. 2020

Alghadir Ahmad H., Gabr Sami A., Iqbal Zaheen A. (2020). Effect of Gender, Physical Activity and Stress-Related Hormones on Adolescent’s Academic Achievements. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17114143

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of 150 adolescents (90 boys, 60 girls) grouped by leisure-time physical activity (mild ≤500, moderate 500–2500, active ≥2500 METs-min/week); grades collected from schools. "The results of academic achievements were significantly higher among students with moderate (p < 0.001) and active (p < 0.001) PA scores"; mild-group means were 4.68 ± 0.71 (boys) and 4.96 ± 0.77 (girls).

> "The results of academic achievements were significantly higher among students with moderate (p < 0.001) and active (p < 0.001) PA scores compared to those with mild PA scores (Table 3)."
