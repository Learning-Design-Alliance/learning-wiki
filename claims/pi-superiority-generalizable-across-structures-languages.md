Rachelle B. Lintao and Jonathan P. Erfe. (2012). Using Processing Instruction for the Acquisition of English Present Perfect of Filipinos. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 2 No. 1, January - March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070206

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The review's synthesis of replication studies (VanPatten & Cadierno 1993a; Cadierno 1995; Cheng 1995; Farley 2001a; Buck 2000; VanPatten & Wong 2004; Benati 2001) concludes effects of PI are generalizable to structures and languages beyond the original Spanish word-order study.

> "There is evidence for the superiority of PI over TI for pronouns and word order in Spanish, for complex verbal morphology in Spanish (the preterit) and Italian (future tense), for lexical-aspectual choice (copular verbs in Spanish, for agent-dative relations and word order in French, for mood selection in subordinate clauses in Spanish, and for the present continuous versus the progressive in English"
