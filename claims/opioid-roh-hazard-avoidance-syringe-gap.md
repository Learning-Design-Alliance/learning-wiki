`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of hazard avoidance across the same 86 paired sessions, with percentages recorded per pair by the two assessors. The wrong-route error occurred "likely influenced by the use of transparent syringes designed for parenteral routes" despite colour-coding.

> "In terms of hazard avoidance, 95% of participants withdrew the correct volume, and nearly all verified the timing of the last administration. However, only 65% used the appropriate violet oral syringe, and while 93% administered the drug orally, the remaining 7% delivered it via the incorrect subcutaneous route."
