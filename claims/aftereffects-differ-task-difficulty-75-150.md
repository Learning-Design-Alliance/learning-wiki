## Evidence

### Linlin Dong 2025

Linlin Dong, Yufeng Ke, Xiaodong Zhu, Shuang Liu & Dong Ming. (2025). Long-term cognitive and neurophysiological effects of mental rotation training. npj Science of Learning 10:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00309-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc bootstrap t-tests with FDR correction after the pooled RM-ANOVAs, contrasting the simple (75°) and complex (150°) task trajectories. The article characterizes the 75° after-effect as "a sustained increase in behavioral performance followed by a decrease" and the 150° after-effect as "a decrease over time," with post3 ACC similar to post1 but RT significantly larger.

> "In the 75° task, the ACC increased signiﬁcantly at the post2-test compared to the post1-test, while the RT did not change signiﬁcantly, and there was a signiﬁcant decrease in the ACC at the post3-test compared to the post2-test, while RT increased signiﬁcantly"
