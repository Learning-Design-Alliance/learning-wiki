### Homeyra Sadaghiani 2013

Homeyra Sadaghiani, John Miller, Steven Pollock, and Daniel Rehn. (2013). Constructing a Multiple-Choice Assessment For Upper-Division Quantum Physics From An Open-Ended Tool. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.066](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.066)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Item analysis of the open-ended QMAT Q6 and its MC conversions at CPP (N=19) and CUB (N=53), with response distributions in Table 1. The article reports "the item difficulty of MC-Q16 is in an ideal range (~50% correct), MC-Q17 is overly challenging for the students." No effect size is printed.

> "The overall average correct response to the open-ended question was 37% [(a) 44%, (b) 39%, (C) 28%] at CPP (N=19) and 54% [(a) 66%, (b) 59%, (C) 38%] at CUB (N=53)."
