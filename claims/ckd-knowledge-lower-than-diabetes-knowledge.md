## Evidence

### Estrella Michelle M 2012

Estrella Michelle M, Sisson Stephen D, Roth Jennifer, Choi Michael J. (2012). Efficacy of an internet-based tool for improving physician knowledge of chronic kidney disease: an observational study. BMC Nephrology. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2369-13-126

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative analysis of 2008–2009 pre-test results from the CKD module against concurrent pre-test results from a similarly structured diabetes module drawn from the same programs. The article reports CKD knowledge "significantly lower" with P < 0.001.

> "Compared with baseline knowledge of the diagnosis and management of diabetes mellitus, baseline CKD knowledge was significantly lower (mean pre-test score: 64.6% vs. 55.2%, respectively; P < 0.001)."
