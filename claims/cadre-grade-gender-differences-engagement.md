### Liu Zihan 2026

Liu Zihan, Tang Wenguang, Tang Liping, Ma Yankun. (2026). The relationship between perceived teacher support and learning engagement of Chinese adolescents: a moderated chain mediation model. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-03990-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

T-tests on eight self-report variables from the 1,193-student survey showed class cadres scored statistically significantly higher levels of perceived teacher support, learning engagement, future orientation, mastery approach goal, and performance approach goal than non-cadres.

> "Students who served as class cadre reported statistically significantly higher levels of perceived teacher support, learning engagement, future orientation, mastery approach goal, and performance approach goal compared to non-cadres."
