Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud 2025¶

Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud. (2025). Immersive auditory-cognitive training improves speech-in-noise perception in older adults with varying hearing and working memory. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00306-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Generalized linear mixed model on comprehension responses (correct vs incorrect) during the training sessions of the cross-over study. A significant main effect of modality, Δχ²(1) = 8.077, p < 0.004, favored the immersive session.

Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud 2025 (2)¶

Vanessa Frei & Nathalie Giroud. (2025). Immersive auditory-cognitive training improves speech-in-noise perception in older adults with varying hearing and working memory. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00306-5

The modality-by-block interaction, Δχ²(1) = 4.463, p = 0.035, indicated performance dropped by the end of the auditory session but not the immersive session; within-modality first-to-last block changes were not significant (p = 0.098 and p = 0.832).
