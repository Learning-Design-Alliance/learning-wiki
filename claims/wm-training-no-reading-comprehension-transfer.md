## Evidence

### Jones JS 2020

Jones JS, Milton F, Mostazir M, Adlam ALR. (2020). The academic outcomes of working memory and metacognitive strategy training in children: A double‐blind randomized controlled trial. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.12870

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVAs on the WIAT-II Reading Comprehension subtest in the RCT found no group differences despite large working memory gains: "no significant group differences in Reading Comprehension at the immediate outcome (all p > 0.05)"; equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no significant group differences in Reading Comprehension at the immediate outcome (all p > 0.05)."

### Jones JS 2020 (2)

Jones JS, Milton F, Mostazir M, Adlam ALR. (2020). The academic outcomes of working memory and metacognitive strategy training in children: A double‐blind randomized controlled trial. Developmental Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.12870

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Mixed models at 3 months confirmed the null reading result for both training groups versus control, with p = 0.488 and p = 0.188 respectively; the authors note this matches prior RCT and meta-analytic evidence.

> "Similarly, there was no significant improvement in Reading scores for the MetaCogmed (p = 0.488) or Cogmed groups (p = 0.188) compared to the Control group at the 3‐month outcome."
