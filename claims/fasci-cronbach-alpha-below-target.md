## Evidence

### Talbot 2017

Talbot, R. M., III. (2017). A Multi-Faceted Approach to Scrutinizing the Reliability of a Measure of STEM Teacher Strategic Knowledge. University of Colorado Denver. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1702.06592](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1702.06592)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classical test theory analysis comparing this study's 60 respondents with two prior pilot tests (n = 63 and 96). The article reports "FA score reliability for this study was 0.62" and SCI 0.51, and concludes the observed reliability is "not very high, indicating a lack of internal consistency in this measure of SK."

> "FA score reliability for this study was 0.62, and in the two previous pilot tests it was found to be 0.69 and 0.43. SCI score reliability was 0.51, and in the two previous pilot tests it was found to be 0.42 and 0.46."
