### Ren Jian-gang 2025

Ren Jian-gang, Li Rui-fang, Zhang Wei, Yu Zi-li, Chen Gang. (2025). Effect of digital virtual reality simulator on pre-clinical dental surgical skill training: a retrospective study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07747-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective comparison of two admission cohorts: 70 students (2017 intake) trained on traditional manikins and 72 students (2018 intake) trained with DVRS, both completing 48-hour theoretical and preclinical courses. The article reports "the average scores of the DVRS group (88.41 ± 4.75) were significantly higher than those of the TM group (86.10 ± 6.21)"; Table 1 prints P = 0.014. No effect size is reported.

> "A comparative analysis revealed that the average scores of the DVRS group (88.41 ± 4.75) were significantly higher than those of the TM group (86.10 ± 6.21) (P < 0.05) (Table 1)."
