Observational classification of teachers' questioning during reading and social studies periods. The article reports that wrong answers were rarely addressed and children never had to justify correct answers, so questioning served assessment, not instruction.

> "All these characteristics explain why only six question-answer sessions were classified in the study as "Comprehension: instruction." All the rest were "Comprehension: assessment.""
