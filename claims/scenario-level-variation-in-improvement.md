Mystery shopping and coaching as a form of audit and feedback to improve community pharmacy management of non-prescription medicine requests: an intervention study. `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Per-scenario point–biserial analyses of appropriate-outcome improvement over the nine visits (table 2). "an improvement over time was seen" in four scenarios with the printed rpb and P values; the remaining six scenarios showed no significant improvement over time.

> "When examining scenarios individually, an improvement over time was seen in the adult cough/cold scenario (rpb=0.281; P=0.044), the allergic rhinitis scenario (rpb=0.334; P=0.018), the paediatric fever scenario (rpb=0.356; P=0.01) and the smoking cessation scenario (rpb=0.390; P=0.005)"
