## Evidence

### El Sherif Reem 2024

El Sherif Reem, Shrier Ian, Paul-Tellier Pierre, Rodriguez Charo. (2024). What do we know about Objective Structured Clinical Examination in Sport and Exercise Medicine? A scoping review. Canadian Medical Education Journal. https://doi.org/10.36834/cmej.77841

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of three included studies (including RCTs by Oswald, Raj, and Smith) comparing patient educators with physician tutors on MSK OSCE scores. The review reports "no significant difference in OSCE scores between the two groups"; no effect size is printed and equivalence was not proven.

> "Three studies compared the OSCE scores of candidates taught by patients trained in MSK examination skills (patient educators) with candidates taught by physicians. All three studies reported no significant difference in OSCE scores between the two groups."
