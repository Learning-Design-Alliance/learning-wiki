Oliver Passon, Thomas Zügge and Johannes Grebe-Ellis. (2018). Pitfalls in the teaching of elementary particle physics. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aadbc7](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aadbc7)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A critical survey of educational papers, semi-technical and popular accounts of the Standard Model, finding that most share a received view of “simpliﬁed particle physics”. The authors argue this narrative leads laypersons to severe misconceptions and omits quantum field theory's novelty.

> "It turns out that beside of many diﬀerences in detail most of this presentations share a common core, that is, something like a received view of “simpliﬁed particle physics” has emerged."
