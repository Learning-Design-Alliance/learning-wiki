Evidence¶

Zhang Z 2024¶

Zhang Z, Zhang Z, Yu Y, Jiang M, Deng L, Guo L. (2024). Impact of different teaching modes on medical students' performance under the scoring criteria for multiple-choice questions: A meta-analysis. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000041008

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials and crossover trials comparing flipped classroom teaching with traditional teaching. The pooled analysis found "the multiple-choice test scores in flipped classroom teaching were higher than those in traditional teaching, with a SMD of 0.39".

"The analysis results indicated that the multiple-choice test scores in flipped classroom teaching were higher than those in traditional teaching, with a SMD of 0.39, 95% CI (0.14, 0.65)"
