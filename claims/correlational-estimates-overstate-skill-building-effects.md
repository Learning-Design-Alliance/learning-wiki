## Evidence

### Bailey 2018

Bailey, D. H., Duncan, G. J., Watts, T., Clements, D. H., & Sarama, J. (2018). Risky Business: Correlation and Causation in Longitudinal Studies of Skill Development. American Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1037/amp0000146

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized cluster trial of the TRIAD preschool math curriculum (42 schools randomly assigned) with follow-ups through fifth grade. The article reports that rescaled impacts "fall to about .46 SD within a year" and are non-significant at the two fourth-grade tests, whereas regression-adjusted correlations exceeded .40 SD in fifth grade.

> "Rescaled impact estimates fall to about .46 SD within a year, and drop to statistically non-significant .08 SD and −.02 SD values for the two fourth-grade tests."
