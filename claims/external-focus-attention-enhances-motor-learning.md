## Evidence

### Olga C. Santos 2016

Olga C. Santos. (2016). Training the Body: The Potential of AIED to Support Personalized Motor Skills Learning. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:730–755. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0103-2

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Wulf 2013, that an external focus — on "the movement's effects rather than on body movements themselves" — enhances motor performance and learning, noting most reviewed systems use an internal focus. Attributed as a reported finding, not tested here.

> "It seems that if the focus is external (that is, on the movement's effects rather than on body movements themselves, which are internal) such as it has been suggested in (Dancu 2012), it enhances motor performance and learning (Wulf 2013). However, in most of the work, the focus considered is internal."
