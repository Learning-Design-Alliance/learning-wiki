### Howe‐Heyman Abigail CNM 2025

Howe‐Heyman Abigail CNM, PhD; Schatz Joseph DNP, PMHNP; Siegel Marissa DeCesaris DNP, PMHNP. (2025). An Interprofessional Approach to Teaching About Postpartum Depression. Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health. https://doi.org/10.1111/jmwh.70056

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired subscale analysis (n = 61) in the pretest–posttest study: professional confidence change 3.98 (3.37), P <.001; therapeutic optimism change 0.49 (3.90), P = .328; generalist perspective change 0.72 (1.86), P = .004. No effect sizes printed for subscales.

> "Regarding subscales, all showed an increase from pretest to posttest, but only the professional confidence in depression care subscale reached statistical significance (P <.001; 95% CI, 3.12‐4.85)."
