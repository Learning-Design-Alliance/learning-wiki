## Evidence

### George Gyamfi 2024

George Gyamfi, Barbara E. Hanna, Hassan Khosravi. (2024). Impact of an instructional guide and examples on the quality of feedback: insights from a randomised controlled study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10346-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per-trait analysis of the sampled coded comments from both groups. The article reports "the chi-square goodness of fit test revealed a statistically significant difference between the groups in the extent to which each S.P.A.R.K. trait was displayed, p < 0.05."

> "the code “specific” was applied to the highest percentage of comments for both the trained (36.1%) and untrained group (23.3%) while the code “actionable” matched the lowest percentage of comments."
