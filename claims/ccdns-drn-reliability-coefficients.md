## Evidence

### Lai Jinjia 2025

Lai Jinjia, Chen Hongyi, Huang Yongqi, Ma Chaoqun, Zeng Ping, Chen Hanxi, Tan Yibing. (2025). Psychometric Evaluation of the Core Competencies in Disaster Nursing Scale for Disaster Rescue Nurses in Mainland China. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/9957270

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Reliability analysis within the 401-nurse cross-sectional survey; test–retest was assessed by re-surveying 27 participants after two weeks, yielding total-scale ICC of 0.898 (domain ICCs 0.727–0.860), which the authors report as good stability across time.

> "The results showed that Cronbach's α of the total scale was 0.978, and Cronbach's α of each domain ranged from 0.853 to 0.938. This demonstrated that the scale exhibited adequate reliability. The split-half reliability of the total scale was 0.918 and that of each domain ranged from 0.704 to 0.907, indicating good internal consistency across the scale and its subdomains."
