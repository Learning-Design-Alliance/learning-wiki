### Bei Cai 2019

Bei Cai, Lindsay Mainhood, and Robert G. Knobel. (2019). Using reflections to explore student learning during the project component of an advanced laboratory course. arXiv. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.cai](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.cai)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis of the modeling prompt found "less than half of the students reﬂected on their experiences with modeling"; students who did reported learning how to model and identified some model limitations, with no further depth.

> "Despite such efforts to emphasize modeling, less than half of the students reﬂected on their experiences with modeling."
