### Samuel Fynes-Clinton 2022

Samuel Fynes-Clinton, Chase Sherwell, Maryam Ziaei, Ashley York, Emma Sanders O'Connor, Kylee Forrest, Libby Flynn, Julie Bower, David Reutens and Annemaree Carroll. (2022). Neural activation during emotional interference corresponds to emotion dysregulation in stressed teachers. npj Science of Learning 7:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00123-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Bivariate Pearson correlation among behavioural and demographic measures in 49 stressed teachers (Table 1 reports r = 0.46 for the PSS–DERS association). The article states "teachers who reported greater stress, displayed greater emotion dysregulation".

> "A signi ﬁcant positive correlation was found between reported stress as measured on the Perceived Stress Scale (PSS 62) and emotion dysregulation as measured by the total score on the DERS 27, indicating that teachers who reported greater stress, displayed greater emotion dysregulation."
