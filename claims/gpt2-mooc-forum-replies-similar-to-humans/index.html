Evidence¶

Filippo Sciarrone 2021¶

Filippo Sciarrone, Carla Limongelli, Olga C. Santos, Marco Temperini. (2021). Preface to the Special Issue AI4MOOCs: Artificial Intelligence, Sensoring, Modeling and Assessment for MOOCs. A Step Beyond. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00255-1

q1 · i? · review · r2

The preface reports, citing the paper by Chenglu Li and Wanli Xing, that deep-learning natural language generation models including recurrent neural networks and GPT-2 were examined for generating "responses similar to human-generated responses to the learners in MOOC forums". No effect size is printed here; this is the preface's second-hand summary.

"The results showed that the GPT-2 model could provide supportive and contextual replies to a similar extent compared to humans."

Related Claims¶

  • A small validation survey found the trained GPT-2 model generated supportive, contextual replies comparable to humans and generalizable to a new similar MOOC — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Human and GPT-2-generated replies differed significantly overall and in informational support, but not in emotional or community support — reports the opposite
  • GPT-2 outperformed an LSTM-based RNN on word perplexity, readability, and cosine-similarity coherence when generating MOOC forum replies — related
  • Gemma and LLaMA produce higher-quality responses than GPT-2 by TIGERSCORE, with accuracy and comprehension gaps remaining — related
