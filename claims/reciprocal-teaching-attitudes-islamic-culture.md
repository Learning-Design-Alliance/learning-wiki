## Evidence

### Al-Harby 2016

Al-Harby, J. S. S. (2016). The Effect of Reciprocal-Teaching Strategy on Learning Outcomes and Attitudes of Qassim-University Students in "Islamic Culture". Journal of Education and Practice, 7(6). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1092493

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test comparison on the attitude scale after three weeks of teaching; the experimental group mean was 99.62 versus 81.97 for the control, t-test significant with magnitude effect η² = 0.70. The article concludes "The magnitude effect is big. This shows the effectiveness of the strategy in rendering its targets."
