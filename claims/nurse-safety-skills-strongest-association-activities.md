# Among South Korean clinical nurses, patient safety skills competency shows the strongest positive association with patient safety activities in multiple regression

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a multiple regression model of 291 nurses, the skills subdomain of patient safety competency was the factor most strongly associated with patient safety activities (β = 0.36, p = 0.000). [→ Lee 2025](#lee-2025)

## Evidence

### Lee 2025

Lee, J., Nam, K., Suh, Y., Lee, Y., and Lee, D. 2025. "Factors Associated With Patient Safety Activities of Clinical Nurses: A Cross‐Sectional Secondary Data Analysis." International Nursing Review 72, no. 4: e70127. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70127

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis of secondary survey data from 291 nurses in general hospitals in Busan, Ulsan, and Gyeongsangnamdo, South Korea. The model was significant with "an explanatory power of 37.0%"; in Table 3 the skills subdomain carried the largest standardized coefficient (β = 0.36, t = 5.27, p = 0.000).

> "Multiple regression analysis demonstrated that the model achieved statistical significance (F = 8.71, p < 0.001) with an explanatory power of 37.0%."
