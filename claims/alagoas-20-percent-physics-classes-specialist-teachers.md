The article reports census data for Alagoas, a state in Brazil's poorer North-East, showing that more mathematicians than physicists teach physics and that only 20% of public-school physics classes are taught by teachers with a tertiary physics degree.

> "According to the oﬃcial 2011 school census in Alagoas, a state in Brazil's poorer North-East, more mathematicians were teaching physics than actual physicists. Only 20 % of the physics classes at public schools were given by teachers with (or at the time pursuing) a tertiary degree in physics [9]."
