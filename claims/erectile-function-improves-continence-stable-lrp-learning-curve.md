## Evidence

### Yusuf S 2026

Yusuf S, Eyup DI, Emre E, Necati E, Dursun B, Arda T. (2026). Operative time-based learning curve and surgical outcomes of laparoscopic radical prostatectomy in a surgeon with limited open surgery experience. BMC Surgery 26:184. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03577-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square analysis of one-year erectile function across the three case groups found a significant improvement in later cases; the abstract reports preservation rising "from 23.3% to 50.0%". Functional assessment used physician-recorded evaluation, not validated questionnaires.

> "The rate of preserved erectile function increased to 50% in Group 3, showing a significant improvement compared with Group 2 (p = 0.012)."

### Yusuf S 2026 (2)

Yusuf S, Eyup DI, Emre E, Necati E, Dursun B, Arda T. (2026). Operative time-based learning curve and surgical outcomes of laparoscopic radical prostatectomy in a surgeon with limited open surgery experience. BMC Surgery 26:184. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-026-03577-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Continence at follow-up showed no significant difference among groups; overall 50% achieved complete continence and 43.3% mild incontinence. No continence-preserving adjunct techniques (e.g., Rocco suture) were applied during the series.

> "Continence outcomes were comparable (p = 0.546); overall, 50% of patients achieved complete continence, while mild incontinence was most common (43.3%) (Table 5)."
