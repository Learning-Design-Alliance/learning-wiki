Evidence¶

Olga Chernikova 2020¶

Olga Chernikova, Nicole Heitzmann, Maximilian Christian Fink, Venance Timothy, Tina Seidel, Frank Fischer, DFG Research group COSIMA. (2020). Facilitating Diagnostic Competences in Higher Education—a Meta-Analysis in Medical and Teacher Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09492-2

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analytic moderator result reported in the article's abstract summary: problem-solving's facilitation of diagnostic competences did not depend on whether learners had a lower or higher professional knowledge base. No separate effect size for this moderator is printed in the supplied text.

"Diagnostic competences are facilitated effectively through problem-solving independent of the learners'knowledge base."
