## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz 2015

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz. (2015). Attending to lifelong learning skills through guided reflection in a physics class. [doi:10.1119/1.4930083](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4930083)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Coding analysis of 97 weekly reflections submitted by 12 students in an upper-division Modeling Instruction physics course, treated as homework and graded for completion. The article reports that "Almost all (94%) of the 97 reﬂections contained at least one narrative, growth, or action statement."

> "Almost all (94%) of the 97 reﬂections contained at least one narrative, growth, or action statement."

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz 2015 (2)

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz. (2015). Attending to lifelong learning skills through guided reflection in a physics class. [doi:10.1119/1.4930083](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4930083)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Percent agreement and Cohen's unweighted kappa were computed for the 71 reflections coded independently by the two authors. The article concludes there was "substantial to almost perfect agreement on narrative, growth, and action statements", with results summarized in Table I.

> "We conclude that there was substantial to almost perfect agreement on narrative, growth, and action statements 31; results are summarized in Table I."

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz 2015 (3)

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer and Daniel L. Reinholz. (2015). Attending to lifelong learning skills through guided reflection in a physics class. [doi:10.1119/1.4930083](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4930083)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Breakdown of statement-type combinations across the 97 reflections, shown in a Venn diagram. The article reports that "About two thirds of reﬂections contained more than one type of statement", most often narrative coupled with growth, action, or both.

> "About two thirds of reﬂections contained more than one type of statement, usually consisting of a narrative statement coupled with growth, action, or both growth and action statements."
