## Evidence

### Perez Arnaldo 2023

Perez Arnaldo, Green Jacqueline, Moharrami Mohammad, Gianoni-Capenakas Silvia, Kebbe Maryam, Ganatra Seema, Ball Geoff, Sharmin Nazlee. (2023). Active learning in undergraduate classroom dental education- a scoping review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0293206

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of 76 student reaction evaluations in the review; satisfaction was the dominant perceived outcome, and in fifty-nine studies active learning was perceived as superior to lectures, 5 found no differences, and 1 reported lectures as superior.

> "active learning was perceived to improve satisfaction in 66 studies (86.8%) and knowledge acquisition in 4 studies (5.3%)"

### Perez Arnaldo 2023 (2)

Perez Arnaldo, Green Jacqueline, Moharrami Mohammad, Gianoni-Capenakas Silvia, Kebbe Maryam, Ganatra Seema, Ball Geoff, Sharmin Nazlee. (2023). Active learning in undergraduate classroom dental education- a scoping review. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0293206

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Synthesis of learning-level evaluations: 48 of 57 studies compared active learning and lectures; 39 found active learning superior in knowledge acquisition and 9 reported no difference between active learning and traditional lectures.

> "All studies in which learning was directly measured (n = 57) found that active learning was effective to improve knowledge acquisition largely based on test scores"
