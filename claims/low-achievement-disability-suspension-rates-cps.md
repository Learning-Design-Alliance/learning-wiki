### Stevens 2015

Stevens, W. D., Sartain, L., Allensworth, E. M., & Levenstein, R., with Guiltinan, S., Mader, N., Huynh, M. H., & Porter, S. (2015). Discipline Practices in Chicago Schools: Trends in the Use of Suspensions and Arrests. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of CPS administrative data linking prior-year test scores and disability status to suspension rates. The report states "27 percent received an OSS at the high school level" among bottom-quartile students, and 24 percent for students with disabilities versus 15 percent without.

> "Among students with low test scores (scores in the bottom quartile in the prior school year), suspension rates are also very high: 27 percent received an OSS at the high school level and 17 percent received an OSS at the middle school level in the 2013-14 year."
