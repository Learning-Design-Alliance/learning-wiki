## Evidence

### Mahir Akgun 2025

Mahir Akgun, Sacip Toker. (2025). Short-Term Gains, Long-Term Gaps: The Impact of GenAI and Search Technologies on Retention. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-99264-3_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99264-3_6)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVA on Task 2 (applying) assessment scores in the same randomized four-group experiment. "partial η² = .155"; ChatGPT showed the best performance and control the lowest.

> "For Task 2, the univariate ANOV A results also showed significant differences between groups, F(3, 119) = 7.279, p < .001, partial η² = .155, explaining 15.5% of the variance. The control group scored significantly lower than both the Google and ChatGPT groups, with mean differences of 18.81 and 20.15, respectively (p < .01)."
