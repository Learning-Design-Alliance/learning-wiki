### Li Jie BS 2019

Li Jie BS, Li Qiancheng MD, Dai Xiuhong MD, Li Jiong MD, Zhang Xinxian MD. (2019). Does pre-scanning training improve the image quality of children receiving magnetic resonance imaging? A meta-analysis of current studies. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000014323

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis pooling of sedation-use outcomes across the included pediatric MRI training trials, analyzed in Revman 5.2 with risk ratios. The pooled rates were "lower in training (15.28%), compared with control group (60.53%)" but not significant; no effect size was printed.
