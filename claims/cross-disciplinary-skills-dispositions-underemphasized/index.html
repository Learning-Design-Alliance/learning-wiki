Exter 2026¶

Exter, M., & Ashby, I. (2026). What does it mean to be “career ready”? A study of competencies important for instructional designers and educational technologists. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10727-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

The article's introduction reviews existing professional competency sets (IBSTPI, ISTE, AECT, ISPI, ATD) and states that “Most of these models do not incorporate a full list of cross-disciplinary skills and include few to no dispositions.”

"Most of these models do not incorporate a full list of cross-disciplinary skills and include few to no dispositions. Those that do incorporate them may not represent their full breadth, granularity, and contextual expression."

Exter 2026 (2)¶

Exter, M., & Ashby, I. (2026). What does it mean to be “career ready”? A study of competencies important for instructional designers and educational technologists. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10727-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

The abstract reports that knowledge and skills aligned with the literature, but “the cross-disciplinary and dispositions sections revealed findings” of particular value, indicating these areas received notable emphasis in the study's results.

"While the knowledge and skills identified aligned well with the current literature in the area, the cross-disciplinary and dispositions sections revealed findings that will help program directors and designers, faculty, employers, and current and future graduates."
