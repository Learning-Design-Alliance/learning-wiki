## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The change in total time from the immediate to the 6-h test was similar for R-PMd, L-PMd, and RP-sham conditions, and only the IP-sham condition improved over that interval. [→ Taewon Kim 2021](#taewon-kim-2021)

## Evidence

### Taewon Kim 2021

Taewon Kim, John J. Buchanan, Jessica A. Bernard and David L. Wright. (2021). Improving online and offline gain from repetitive practice using anodal tDCS at dorsal premotor cortex. npj Science of Learning 6:31. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00109-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A between-subject ANOVA on difference scores between the immediate and 6-h tests showed a significant condition effect with printed effect size ηp² = 0.19; post hoc tests showed only the IP-sham condition improved performance across the 6-h interval.

> "Stimulation at PMd during RP however failed to induce time-dependent consolidation that is typical of IP but absent after RP during the initial 6-hr following practice, F(3,60) = 4.82, p < 0.01, ηp 2 = 0.19."
