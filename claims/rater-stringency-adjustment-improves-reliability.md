### Hissbach Johanna C 2014

Hissbach Johanna C, Sehner Susanne, Harendza Sigrid, Hampe Wolfgang. (2014). Cutting costs of multiple mini-interviews – changes in reliability and efficiency of the Hamburg medical school admission test between two applications. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-54

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' recommendation in the cost-effectiveness discussion, grounded in their model 2 reliability computation that included a fixed rater effect following Roberts et al. The article reports the adjusted overall reliabilities as ICC=0.75 (2009) and ICC=0.76 (2010).

> "It is generally worthwhile to adjust for rater stringency, as this reduces error and improves reliability without producing further costs."
