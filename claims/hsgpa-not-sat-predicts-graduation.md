## Evidence

### Stephen D.H. Hsu 2010

Stephen D.H. Hsu, James Schombert. (2010). Data Mining the University: College GPA Predictions from SAT Scores. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1004.2731](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1004.2731)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of normalized composite SAT histograms for graduates versus non-graduates (Fig. 3) and of high school GPA histograms (Fig. 4); the article concludes "students leave college for reasons unrelated to their cognitive ability" and that retention efforts should target students with low high school GPAs.

> "Not only do the means agree within the errors, but also the variance and shape of the distributions are identical. We conclude that, as a population, students leave college for reasons unrelated to their cognitive ability."
