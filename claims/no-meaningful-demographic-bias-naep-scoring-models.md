The article reports a post-hoc bias analysis using pairwise SMDs and a linear mixed-effects regression on prediction error. The article states that "No pairwise Standardized Mean Difference value was greater than 1.6e-8" and that the marginal R² was 0.0003, concluding no meaningful demographic bias.
