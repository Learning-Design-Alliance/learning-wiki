### Ma Chen-ying 2026

Ma Chen-ying, Chen Jie, Fu Yi, Liu Lou, Zhang Jing-wen, Zhou Ju-ying. (2026). Evaluating a specialty-specific objective structured clinical examination and workplace-based assessment curriculum for radiation oncology postgraduate training: a prospective educational intervention study. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-cohort pre/post learning-environment measurement using PHEEM in the intervention cohort; the article reports means and P values for total and subdomain scores, with effect sizes in Table 3 (total d = 0.32).

> "PHEEM total score increased from 111.2 ± 13.1 vs. 115.3 ± 12.8 (P = 0.020). Role autonomy and teaching improved modestly (34.1 ± 4.9 vs. 35.5 ± 4.8, P = 0.033; 44.0 ± 5.4 vs. 45.8 ± 5.2, P = 0.013, respectively), whereas the social-support domain showed no clear change over time (33.1 ± 4.3 vs. 33.9 ± 4.1, P = 0.154)."
