## Evidence

### Ivory 2026

Ivory, M., Finnerty, S., Dunn, K., Philpot, R., & Warmelink, L. (2026). The Integrity of Psychology Assessments in the AI Age: A Critical Examination. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09729-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

In the case study, creative application tasks (e.g. describing a novel religion grounded in psychological theory in module PSYC303) repeatedly produced the same output ('Harmonia') across prompting levels and accounts; the authors observe that "creativity in a manner significantly departing from previous content was more difficult".

> "Assignments requiring novelty and originality were more resilient to the Gen-AI output."
