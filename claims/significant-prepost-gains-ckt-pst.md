## Evidence

### Fathima Ameen 2026

Fathima Ameen, Tasmia Shahriar, Aditi Mallavarapu, Shiyan Jiang, Noboru Matsuda. (2026). Beyond prior knowledge: the predictive role of knowledge-building in tutor learning. Behaviormetrika. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41237-026-00294-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Paired t-tests in the pretest-intervention-posttest study with 23 middle school students; Table 2 reports CKT means rising from 0.78 to 0.90 (t(22) = −4.47, p < 0.001) and PST from 0.70 to 0.81 (t(22) = −2.68, p < 0.05).

> "The results of paired t-tests comparing pre- and post-test performance for each category indicate that there is a significant improvement in conceptual and procedural knowledge fol- lowing the intervention."
