(2024), that detection precision is low: "the overall precision of algorithms for the recognition of AI-generated content stands at 39.5% only but decreases to 22% in the case of adversarial methods." Adversarial techniques include misspellings, non-native writing styles, and increased burstiness. No effect size is printed.

> "the overall precision of algorithms for the recognition of AI-generated content stands at 39.5% only but decreases to 22% in the case of adversarial methods. This means that there is a high frequency of false positives where texts written by people are mistaken as having been created by machines."
