## Evidence

### Störmann S 2016

Störmann S, Stankiewicz M, Raes P, Berchtold C, Kosanke Y, Illes G, Loose P, Angstwurm MW. (2016). How well do final year undergraduate medical students master practical clinical skills? GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001057](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001057)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of a voluntary formative 4-station OSCE (mP3) taken by 214 final-year students from mid-2011 through 2014; participants "achieved 72.8%±10.1% of the maximum total score". Twenty students (9.3%) scored insufficiently (<60%).

> "On average participants achieved 72.8%±10.1% of the maximum total score (see Figure 1 (Fig. 1))."
