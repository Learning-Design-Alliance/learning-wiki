## Evidence

### Han Jeong-won 2025

Han Jeong-won, Park Junhee, Lee Hanna. (2025). Development and effects of a chatbot education program for self-directed learning in nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07316-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized controlled trial (31 experimental, 29 control fourth-year nursing students), confidence was measured with a single-item 0-10 Numerical Rating Scale; the chatbot group's post-test mean was 6.13 ± 1.67 versus 4.76 ± 1.35 for control. The authors link the gain to "more opportunities to attempt and learn from mistakes, ultimately boosting their confidence."

> "Confidence (t = -2.62, p =.011), and Satisfaction in education (t = -3.51, p <.001) compared to the control group"
