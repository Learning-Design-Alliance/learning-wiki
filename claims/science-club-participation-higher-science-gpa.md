# Science club participation is positively associated with high school science GPA, significant across all three propensity score matching methods

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participation in science club has a positive, statistically significant relationship with cumulative high school science GPA under OLS with covariates and under block, nearest-neighbor, and kernel matching. [→ Gottfried 2013](#gottfried-2013)

## Evidence

### Gottfried 2013

Gottfried, M. A. & Williams, D. (2013). STEM Club Participation and STEM Schooling Outcomes. Education Policy Analysis Archives, 21 (79). http://epaa.asu.edu/ojs/article/view/1361

`q2 · i?`

Propensity score matching analysis of science club participation on science GPA in the Add Health sample. Block (0.251, p<0.05), nearest-neighbor (0.212, p<0.10), and kernel (0.396, p<0.001) coefficients were all significant; the full-model OLS coefficient was 0.250 (p<0.001). No standardized effect size is printed for the matching models.

> "When matched on their propensities to select into a science club, students in science club had higher science GPAs, as evidenced by significant coefficients in all three propensity score matching methods."
