# User satisfaction with eco modes declines sharply from mode 1 (mean 3.64) to modes 4–5 (means 2.35 and 2.06)

Descriptive survey analysis of 89 participants' 5-point Likert satisfaction ratings per mode; satisfaction and ranking items were reported descriptively rather than inferentially. "mode 1 showed the highest satisfaction (mean = 3.64, SD = 0.82)" and modes 4–5 declined sharply (means = 2.35 and 2.06).
