## Evidence

### Arachchilage 2013

Arachchilage, N. A. G., Love, S., & Maple, C. (2013). Can a Mobile Game Teach Computer Users to Thwart Phishing Attacks. International Journal for Infonomics (IJI), Volume 6, Issues 3/4. [doi:10.20533/iji.1742.4712.2013.0083](https://doi.org/10.20533/iji.1742.4712.2013.0083)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test in the think-aloud user study comparing pre- and post-test scores of the 20 mobile game participants; the article reports "t(19)= -7.97, p<0.005 (two-tailed)" with means 55.00 (SD 17.911) and 84.00 (SD 13.139). No effect size is printed.

> "Participants who played the mobile game, scored 56 percent in the pre-test and 84percent in the post-test of identifying phishing or legitimate websites after playing the mobile game prototype (Table 4 and 5)."
