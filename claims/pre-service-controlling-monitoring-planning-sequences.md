`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Process mining of coded event logs (BupaR in R) comparing aggregated sequential patterns of the two group types; pre-service models showed "stronger transitions between controlling and monitoring (f = 176, 180) than in-service teachers (f = 124, 114)" and stronger controlling–planning transitions.

> "Concerning sociocognitive regulation, pre-service teachers exhibited stronger transitions between controlling and monitoring (f = 176, 180) than in-service teachers (f = 124, 114).
