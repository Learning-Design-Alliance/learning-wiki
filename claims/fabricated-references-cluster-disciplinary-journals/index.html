q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports this classroom verification exercise, citing Sevryugina and Vargas (2026); the study's own design is not described beyond the quoted sentence. It is used as illustrative evidence that fabricated content is not an occasional accident and that assertiveness is weakly coupled to accuracy.

"In a classroom verification exercise in which students checked 456 model-generated references against the literature, 51.3% of those references proved to be real and correctly cited, and the fabricated ones clustered in disciplinary journals rather than in high-profile multidisciplinary ones"
