## Evidence

### Essers G 2011

Essers G, van Dulmen S, van Weel C, van der Vleuten C, Kramer A. (2011). Identifying context factors explaining physician's low performance in communication assessment: an explorative study in general practice. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2296-12-138

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Single-case observational illustration from the synergy section: a female patient presenting for a routine check-up of her vaginal ring. The authors saw "an unexpected combination of doctor-related and patient-related factors, and characteristics of the PE" acting synergistically in this consultation.

> "Clearly, after many previous check-ups, the GP was able to perform the invasive, intimate examination without explaining, instructing, or even without announcing - without being disrespectful."
