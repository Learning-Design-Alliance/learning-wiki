## Evidence

### Du Xiangyu 2025

Du Xiangyu, Du Jiayi, Shang Lin, Yin Zhao, Jiang Li. (2025). Exploring influencing factors and facilitating strategies for self-directed learning from the perspective of medical trainees: a multicentre qualitative study in China. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-088688

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group theme on occupation-related factors. Conversely, "a more robust professional identity was identified to play a crucial role in fostering commitment to continuous SDL in one's field"; perceiving one's skills as valuable to patients was described as intrinsic motivation.

> "Participants emphasised that the sense of burnout due to monotonous tasks and the loss of novelty in work can significantly dampen the motivation to engage in SDL, as the lack of excitement and interest can discourage the pursuit of new knowledge."
