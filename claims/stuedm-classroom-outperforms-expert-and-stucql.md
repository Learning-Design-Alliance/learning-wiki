# In a controlled classroom study, StuEDM significantly improves posttest performance and normalized learning gain over an Expert baseline and pretest-adjusted posttest score over StuCQL, while overriding students' decisions less than half as often

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` StuEDM significantly improves posttest performance and normalized learning gain over a pretest-equivalent Expert baseline with no adaptive guidance, and significantly improves pretest-adjusted posttest score over StuCQL, a CQL-induced policy trained on a much larger historical corpus. [→ Md Mirajul Islam 2026](#md-mirajul-islam-2026)
`q3 i?` StuEDM overrides students' own pedagogical decisions less than half as often as StuCQL, and StuEDM students engaged with the tutor significantly longer than StuCQL students. [→ Md Mirajul Islam 2026 (2)](#md-mirajul-islam-2026-2)

### Md Mirajul Islam 2026

Md Mirajul Islam. (2026). Offline Apprenticeship Learning for Human-Centric Environments. Doctoral dissertation, North Carolina State University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.28716](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.28716)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled classroom study with 104 undergraduate students in the Pyrenees probability ITS; Table 7.1 reports pretest-adjusted ANCOVA contrasts and t-tests by condition. The article reports significant improvements over the Expert baseline and over StuCQL; no effect size is printed.

> "StuEDM significantly improves posttest performance and normalized learning gain over the Expert baseline, and significantly improves the pretest-adjusted posttest score over StuCQL; a decision-level analysis further shows StuEDM overrides students' own decisions less than half as often as StuCQL."
