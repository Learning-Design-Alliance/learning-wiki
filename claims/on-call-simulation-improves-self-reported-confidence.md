Sebastian P, Wells L, Huszka H, Tovell N, Okorie M. (2024). Preparing clinicians for practice: effectiveness and design of on-call simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05495-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of 23 articles: confidence measures were collected immediately before and after the simulation activity in all but one reviewed article, and every study reported improvement. This is the review's own narrative synthesis, not a pooled effect size.

> "Twenty Three unique publications were reviewed which unanimously reported that on-call simulation had a positive effect on self-reported participant confidence in performing on-call roles."
