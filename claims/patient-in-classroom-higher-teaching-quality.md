## Evidence

### Moslemi Najarcolaie Fatemeh 2026

Moslemi Najarcolaie Fatemeh, Kordian Arezoo, Salamat Fatemehzahra, Rezaei Fatemeh, Sanagoo Akram, Firouzbakht Mojgan, Jouybari Leila. (2026). The efficacy of patient-in-classroom instruction compared to traditional lectures: a quasi-experimental study in nursing education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09586-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental two-group post-test-only study of 682 nursing students (341 per group) in intact classroom groups, analyzed with Mann-Whitney U tests. The intervention group's mean total teaching quality score was "11.07 points higher" with Cohen's d = 1.94 and P < 0.001.

> "The mean total teaching quality score was 11.07 points higher in the intervention group (58.13 ± 4.44) compared to the control group (47.06 ± 6.73), with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 1.94; Mann-Whitney U: P < 0.001)."
