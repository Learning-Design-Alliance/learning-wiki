Wu Yanyan, Liu Fang, Zhang Hui. (2026). Application of case-based learning combined with three-dimensional image reconstruction technology in clinical teaching of liver ultrasound. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09839-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the post-course assessment of this RCT (n = 30 per group), qualitative diagnosis scores were 85.33 ± 9.09 versus 85.00 ± 9.56, p = 0.890; the article prints no effect size, so equivalence was not tested.

> "The experimental group scored significantly higher in basic scanning (p < 0.01), hepatic segmentation (p < 0.001), and lesion localization (p < 0.001), while no significant intergroup difference was found in the qualitative diagnosis of focal liver lesions (p = 0.890) (Table 3)."
