# A2i use produced a significant intent-to-treat effect on letter-word identification for kindergarten and first-grade students (d = 0.37)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Kindergarten and first-grade students whose teachers used the revised A2i technology scored significantly higher on the WJ-III Letter-Word Identification subtest in spring (d = 0.37). [→ Connor 2022](#connor-2022)

## Evidence

### Connor 2022

Connor, Carol McDonald; May, Henry; Sparapani, Nicole; Hwang, Jin Kyoung; Adams, Ashley; Wood, Taffeta S.; Siegal, Sarah; Wolfe, Cassidy; Day, Stephanie. (2022). Bringing Assessment-to-Instruction (A2i) Technology to Scale: Exploring the Process from Development to Implementation. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000758

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment during the 2015–2016 academic year in which two large schools were assigned to immediate or delayed (April) A2i treatment; 33 K–3 teachers and their students (N = 763) participated. HLM found "significant effects of treatment (p = .004)" with d = 0.37 on the LW subtest.

> "When we examined the effect of A2i for kindergarten and first grade students on the LW subtest using HLM, we found significant effects of treatment (p = .004) with an effect size (d) of 0.37"
