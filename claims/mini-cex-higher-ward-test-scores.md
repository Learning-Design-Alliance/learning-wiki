# Mini-CEX intervention groups achieved significantly higher ward test scores than control groups (Cohen's d = 0.65)

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Final-year MBBS students who received two to three Mini-CEX encounters scored significantly higher on ward tests than controls (72.3 ± 7.5 vs. 65.4 ± 8.2; p = 0.002; d = 0.65). [→ Durrani Shazia Fakhir 2025](#durrani-shazia-fakhir-2025)

## Evidence

### Durrani Shazia Fakhir 2025

Durrani Shazia Fakhir, Hameed Ammara, Rani Pallavi, Ahmed Shakeel, Baqai Shehla, Ali Rida. (2025). Effect of Mini-CEX as formative learning tool for clinical skills in undergraduate medical students in a private medical university in Karachi, Pakistan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07962-6

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental study of 143 final-year MBBS students in a two-month paediatrics rotation; 76 intervention students received two to three Mini-CEX evaluations and 67 controls continued conventional teaching. The "significantly higher mean ward test scores" reached p = 0.002 with Cohen's d = 0.65.

> "Students in the intervention groups achieved significantly higher mean ward test scores (72.3 ± 7.5) compared with the control groups (65.4 ± 8.2), with a p-value of 0.002 and a medium-to-large effect size (Cohen's d = 0.65)."
