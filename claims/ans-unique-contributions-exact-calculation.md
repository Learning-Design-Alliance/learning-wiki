## Evidence

### Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro 2014

Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro, Wood Guilherme, Knops André, Krinzinger Helga, Lonnemann Jan, Starling-Alves Isabella, Willmes Klaus, Haase Vitor Geraldi. (2014). In How Many Ways is the Approximate Number System Associated with Exact Calculation? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111155

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression with exact calculation as outcome in the combined sample; the covariate block explained 57.8% of variance, and all three ANS measures remained significant predictors afterwards, indicating the link is not generated by general cognitive processes alone.

> "Importantly, all three measures of ANS acuity remained significant predictors of exact calculation after removing the effects of the intervening variables (non-symbolic comparison: β = −0.135, p = 0.005; non-symbolic estimation: β = −0.162, p<0.001; non-symbolic addition: β = −0.167; p<0.001)."

### Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro 2014 (2)

Pinheiro-Chagas Pedro, Wood Guilherme, Knops André, Krinzinger Helga, Lonnemann Jan, Starling-Alves Isabella, Willmes Klaus, Haase Vitor Geraldi. (2014). In How Many Ways is the Approximate Number System Associated with Exact Calculation? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0111155

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A stepwise regression entering the three ANS measures simultaneously kept non-symbolic estimation and addition but excluded non-symbolic comparison; addition raised variance explained to 60.1% and estimation to 61.4%, showing unique contributions only for the latter two.

> "Non-symbolic addition raised the variance explained to 60.1% and non-symbolic estimation to 61.4% (Table 4)."
