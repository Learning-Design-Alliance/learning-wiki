Susan Harrington and Charlott Sellberg 2026¶

Susan Harrington and Charlott Sellberg. (2026). 12 Heuristics for Learning Analytics in Simulation-Based Professional Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1), 130-142. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9141

q2 · i? · design · r2

Findings from a participatory co-design workshop with maritime students at a Swedish university, analyzed using Engeström's theory of tensions. The article reports student enthusiasm for self-comparison over time and comparison with strong peers, alongside concerns about real-time LAD use impacting immersion.

"Students were enthusiastic about the potential to compare their own data over time to see how they were progressing, and also welcomed the opportunity to compare themselves with particularly strong "golden egg" students."

q2 · i? · design · r2

From the same student perspective synthesis, the article reports students' preference for on-demand mobile access, with data valued for "revisiting and reflecting on feedback to improve future performance" rather than in-simulation display.

"Instead, they preferred to receive on-demand access to data and visualizations via a mobile application."
