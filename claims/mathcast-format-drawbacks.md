## Subclaims

### Colleen Lanz Countryman and Hong Wang 2018

Colleen Lanz Countryman and Hong Wang, North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC. (2018). Using Mathcasts to Facilitate Student Comprehension of Physical Applications of Math Concepts. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1809.09720](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1809.09720)

This is the authors' own stated limitations discussion, not a tested result. They also note that despite requiring a password displayed randomly in the video via YouTube's annotation feature, "there is still a chance that students will receive the password from another classmate" without watching.

> "First, there is no easy way to individualize instruction for specific students. In addition, if the videos are made public, there is no clear way to differentiate students' data (in aggregate) from the general public's data."
