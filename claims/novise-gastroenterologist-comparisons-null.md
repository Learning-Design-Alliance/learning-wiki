## Evidence

### Korzeniowski Przemyslaw 2016

Korzeniowski Przemyslaw, Brown Daniel C., Sodergren Mikael H., Barrow Alastair, Bello Fernando. (2016). Validation of NOViSE: A Novel Natural Orifice Virtual Surgery Simulator. Surgical Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1177/1553350616669896

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mann-Whitney U comparisons across the three groups (4 experts, 7 novices, 3 gastroenterologists). The authors note a trend that gastroenterologists were faster with shorter path lengths than novices, correlating with prior findings that endoscopic experience benefits NOTES skill acquisition.

> "Construct validity was also not demonstrated for any metrics between Experts and Gastroenterologists, except for the time diathermy was activated on target tissue (16.4 vs 35.8 seconds, P = .02). Construct validity was not demonstrated for any metrics between Novices and Gastroenterologists."
