Evidence¶

Honoré N 2020¶

Honoré N, Houssa M, Volckaert A, Noël M-P and Nader-Grosbois N (2020) Training Inhibition and Social Cognition in the Classrooms. Front. Psychol. 11:1974. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01974

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Correlational analysis on factorial change scores computed from pre-test principal component analyses of four EF tasks and two social cognition tasks (KMO = 0.56 and 0.50); Table 10 prints r = −0.457 and r = −0.535, both p < 0.001.

"The correlational analyses ( Table 10 ) showed a signiﬁcant negative correlation between the change in the EF factor and the EF factor at pre-test as well as between the change in the social cognition factor and the social cognition factor at pre-test."

Honoré N 2020 (2)¶

Honoré N, Houssa M, Volckaert A, Noël M-P and Nader-Grosbois N (2020) Training Inhibition and Social Cognition in the Classrooms. Front. Psychol. 11:1974. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01974

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The same correlational analysis tested teacher and child characteristics as moderators of gain; none besides pre-test level was significant, so neither attendance nor teacher involvement predicted improvement.

"However, the other correlations are non-signiﬁcant, which means that the change in EF and social cognition does not depend on the age and school year of the children, the number of sessions they attended, or the intensity of their teacher's involvement in the project or their number of years of service."
