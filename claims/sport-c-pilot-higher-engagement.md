### Jeffrey Basoah 2023

Jeffrey Basoah, Dr. William Scherer, Dr. Karis Boyd-Sinkler, Dr. Reid Bailey. (2023). The SPORT-C Intervention: An Integration of Sports, Case-Based Pedagogy and Systems Thinking Learning. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2307.11755](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2307.11755)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative findings from a three-participant focus group and a semi-structured classroom-instructor interview conducted three days after the intervention activity. One participant said they were "actually paying attention in class for once"; the instructor said students were "definitely seemed interested". No effect size is printed.

> "Both the participants and the CI expressed levels of engagement higher than the norm. Participants stated several times that they felt more engaged while participating in the activity."
