`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the 556-student survey, with irregular study as the reference category. The article reports the three adjusted odds ratios with confidence intervals. No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "compared with studying irregularly, studying regularly during the semester (aOR 3.61, 95% CI 1.39–9.33; p = 0.008), studying intensively before exams (aOR 3.20, 95% CI 1.41–7.26; p = 0.005), or combining regular and intensive study (aOR 4.04, 95% CI 1.80–9.04; p = 0.001) were each independently associated with higher odds of excellent performance"
