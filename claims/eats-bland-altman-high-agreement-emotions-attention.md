## Evidence

### Emirhan Gülen 2026

Emirhan Gülen, Yüksel Göktaş. (2026). Facial expression recognition for investigating attention and affective states in synchronous online higher education. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10631-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Bland–Altman analysis comparing EATS measurements with independent video-based evaluations by three psychological counselors of 40 students' lecture recordings. The article reports agreement rates "ranging between 92.5% and 97.5%" for the four named emotions.

> "According to the Bland–Altman analysis, the differences between measurements for hap- piness, surprise, sadness, and fear were found to be quite small, with agreement rates rang- ing between 92.5% and 97.5%."
