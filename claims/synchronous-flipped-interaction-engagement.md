## Evidence

### Yorganci 2025

Yorganci, S. (2025). The impact of synchronous online discussions and online flipped learning on student engagement and self-regulation among preliminary undergraduates in a basic math course. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10459-0

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA among 121 undergraduates found a significant multivariate interaction, Wilks' Λ = .92, p < .05, "η2 = .072". Follow-up univariate tests showed the interaction was significant for behavioral (η2 = .046) and cognitive (η2 = .044) engagement but not emotional engagement.

> "The results of the MANCOVA revealed that there was a significant two-way interaction effect between learning approach and online discussions (Wilks’ /u1D706 = .92, F (3, 114) = 2.940, p < .05, η2 = .072)."
