Evidence¶

Jemimah Nathaniel 2025¶

Jemimah Nathaniel, Solomon Sunday Oyelere, Jarkko Suhonen, Matti Tedre. (2025). Literature Review on the Integration of Generative AI in Programming Education. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00524-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Kazemitabaar et al.'s study of learners interacting with Codex, that performance varied with engagement levels. The review attributes the disparity to how tools were introduced and scaffolded.

"the results of the study by Kazemitabaar et al., ( 2023b) showed that students performance varied based on learner engagement levels, with some participants effectively integrating and interacting with the generated code. While others struggled to incorporate it into their solutions."

Jemimah Nathaniel 2025 (2)¶

Jemimah Nathaniel, Solomon Sunday Oyelere, Jarkko Suhonen, Matti Tedre. (2025). Literature Review on the Integration of Generative AI in Programming Education. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00524-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis (Fig. 5) on alignment of teaching methods, GenAI tools, and assessment strategies; it states skills outcomes "highly vary depending on the actual teaching methodology" and tool and assessment choices.

"the skills resulting from integrating GenAI into programming education highly vary depending on the actual teaching methodology, specific GenAI tool use, and assessment methods put in place for such integration, as shown in Fig. 5."
