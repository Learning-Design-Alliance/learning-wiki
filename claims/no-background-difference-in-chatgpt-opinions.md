## Evidence

### Singh 2023

Singh, H.; Tayarani-Najaran, M.-H.; Yaqoob, M. (2023). Exploring Computer Science Students' Perception of ChatGPT in Higher Education: A Descriptive and Correlation Study. Educ. Sci. 2023, 13, 924. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13090924

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison of questionnaire answers between African and Asian students in the 430-student cohort (88% Asian, 10% African), reported in Results. No statistically significant difference was found; equivalence was not tested.

> "When we compared the answers provided by the African and Asian students using the chi-square test, we did not ﬁnd any statistically signiﬁcant difference between the way they answered the questions."
