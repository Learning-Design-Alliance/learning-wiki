## Subclaims
`q3 i3` ROI analysis revealed a significant age group × condition interaction on left IFG/MFG contrast estimates, with adults showing larger condition effects than adolescents (η2 = 0.15). [→ Kleibeuker 2013](#kleibeuker-2013)

## Evidence

### Kleibeuker 2013

Kleibeuker, S. W., Koolschijn, P. C. M. P., Jolles, D. D., De Dreu, C. K. W., and Crone, E. A. (2013). The neural coding of creative idea generation across adolescence and early adulthood. Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 7:905. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2013.00905

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ROI mixed ANOVA on contrast estimates for AU and OC conditions relative to baseline in the left IFG/MFG cluster. The study reports a "signiﬁcant age group× condition interaction effect" with η2 = 0.15, adults showing larger effects, consistent with the hypothesis that adolescents do not yet recruit left lateral PFC at a mature level.

> "Results revealed a signiﬁcant age group× condition interaction effect [F(1, 41) = 7.21, p = 0.01, η2 = 0.15; see Figure 4C). Adults showed larger condition effects than adolescents"
