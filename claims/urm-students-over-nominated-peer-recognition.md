## Evidence

### Meagan Sundstrom 2022

Meagan Sundstrom, Ashley B. Heim, Barum Park, and N. G. Holmes. (2022). Introductory physics students' recognition of strong peers: Gender and racial/ethnic bias differ by course level and context. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020148](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.020148)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ERGM race/ethnicity tie-type results across six networks. The authors describe this as surprising given documented stereotypes against URM students in science, and note URM students made up less than 30% of each analyzed course.

> "In both networks of Course B and the lecture perception network of Course C (even when imputing the second celebrity of the latter network as a URM or non-URM student), however, URM students were more likely to receive nominations than their non-URM peers."
