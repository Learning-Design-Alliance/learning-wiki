### Bing EG 2019

Bing EG, Parham GP, Cuevas A, Fisher B, Skinner J, Mwanahamuntu M, Sullivan R. (2019). Using Low-Cost Virtual Reality Simulation to Build Surgical Capacity for Cervical Cancer Treatment. Journal of Global Oncology. https://doi.org/10.1200/JGO.18.00263

`q2 · i?`

Generalized estimating equation modeling of repeated movement efficiency scores from the ten-participant Zambian simulation evaluation. The article reports that "Higher number of simulations completed, being a resident or fellow as opposed to a senior medical student, and recent graduation from college were all associated with higher levels of movement efficiency."

> "Movement efficiency was strongly and independently associated with the number of simulations performed, level of experience, and years since graduation (Table 2)."
