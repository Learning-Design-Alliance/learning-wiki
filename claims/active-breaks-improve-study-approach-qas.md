# Classroom-based physical activity breaks improve study-approach variables (motivation, organization, flexibility, concentration) and reduce anxiety, with no change in didactic material development or attitude towards school

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Latino Francesca 2023

Latino Francesca, Tafuri Francesco, Saraiello Emma, Tafuri Domenico. (2023). Classroom-Based Physical Activity as a Means to Improve Self-Efficacy and Academic Achievement among Normal-Weight and Overweight Youth. Nutrients 15(9):2061. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15092061

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on the QAS (49 items, 7 macro-areas) in the 100-student trial; post hoc showed EG increases in motivation (d = 1.44), organization (d = 1.39), study flexibility (d = 1.89), concentration (d = 1.44) and a decrease in anxiety (d = 1.72).

> "A substantial ‘Time × Group’ interaction was obtained for motivation (F1,98 = 162.70, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.62, large effect size), organization (F1,98 = 186.97, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.65, large effect size), study flexibility (F1,98 = 328.50, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.77, large effect size), concentration (F1,98 = 194.89, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.66, large effect size) and anxiety (F1,28 = 188.68, p < 0.001, η2p = 0.65, large effect size)."

### Latino Francesca 2023 (2)

Latino Francesca, Tafuri Francesco, Saraiello Emma, Tafuri Domenico. (2023). Classroom-Based Physical Activity as a Means to Improve Self-Efficacy and Academic Achievement among Normal-Weight and Overweight Youth. Nutrients 15(9):2061. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15092061

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null result from the same QAS analysis: two of the seven macro-areas, didactic material development and attitude towards school, showed no significant interaction; equivalence was not tested.

> "Lastly, no significant “Time × Group” interaction was reached in didactic material development and attitude toward school (p > 0.05)."
