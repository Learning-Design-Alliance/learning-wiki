> "Among the 4 practicals and OVM, only OVM is (again) significant at 0.006, with odds ratio (OR) = 0.348 which implies that males are less likely to get a distinction in OVM compared to females.  Semester 3 is significant with OR=1.366 which means there is significant difference in performance of male over female in semester 3 compared to semester 1."
