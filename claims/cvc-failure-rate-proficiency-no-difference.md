## Evidence

### De Cassai Alessandro 2021

De Cassai Alessandro, Geraldini Federico, Pasin Laura, Boscolo Annalisa, Zarantonello Francesco, Tocco Martina, Pretto Chiara, Perona Matteo, Carron Michele, Navalesi Paolo. (2021). Safety in training for ultrasound guided internal jugular vein CVC placement: a propensity score analysis. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-021-01460-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective cohort study of residents placing ultrasound-guided IJV CVCs at the University Hospital of Padova (2012-2020), with propensity score matching on patient confounders. "There was no difference in the failure rate among the groups (2.8 vs 2.7%, p-value 0.895)"; failure was defined as the need for hands-on assistance by another operator.

> "Among them, two groups of 1060 CVCs each were matched with a propensity score analysis. There was no difference in the failure rate among the groups (2.8 vs 2.7%, p-value 0.895)."
