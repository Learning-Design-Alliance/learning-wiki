`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is the authors' argument, not a tested comparison: the article motivates SJTs by the complexity of preparing, conducting and evaluating simulated conversations, including rater-training demands and rater effects that are difficult to rule out. No empirical comparison data are reported.

> "Compared to assessments with SG/SCs, the SJT offers more efficient implementation and evaluation. In addition, numerous students can take the test at the same time and be assessed in a standardized way."
