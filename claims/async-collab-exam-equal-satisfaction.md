## Evidence

### Callaghan 2025

Callaghan, K., Kestin, G., Klales, A., McCarty, L., & Deslauriers, L. (2025). Active Learning Through Flexible Collaborative Exams: Improving STEM Assessments. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2502.01994](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.01994)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Survey of all 55 enrolled students in a junior-level quantum mechanics course after standard (Midterm 1) and asynchronous online/in-person (Midterm 2) collaborative exams, reported in Results (Fig. 2). The article reports "no significant differences between the standard and asynchronous formats"; no effect sizes were printed.

> "The responses, captured on a 5-point Likert scale, reveal no significant differences between the standard and asynchronous formats in terms of student satisfaction and overall response"
