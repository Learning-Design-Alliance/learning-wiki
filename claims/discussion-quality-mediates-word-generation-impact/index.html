Evidence¶

Snow 2014¶

Catherine E. Snow. (2014). Extended discourse in first and second language acquisition: A challenge and an opportunity! Journal of Japanese Linguistics 30. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED571513

q2 · i? · review · r2

Discourse analysis comparing Word Generation and control classrooms. The analysis "confirmed that quality of discussion mediated an important portion of the impact of the program": effectiveness was higher where discussion quality was high and lower where discussion was not implemented or was implemented with low quality.

"Furthermore, an analysis of the discourse within the Word Generation and control classrooms confirmed that quality of discussion mediated an important portion of the impact of the program."
