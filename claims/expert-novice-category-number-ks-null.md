### Steven F. Wolf 2012

Steven F. Wolf, Daniel P. Dougherty, and Gerd Kortemeyer. (2012). An empirical approach to interpreting card-sorting data. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.8.010124](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.8.010124)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

KS comparison of category-number distributions from the authors' Michigan State University card-sorting experiment, in which physics faculty and novices sorted mechanics problems 'based on similarity of solution'. The test found 'no statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence' (p = 0.4793).

> "A KS-test comparing the ECDFs of expert and novice number of categories (see Figure 3) demonstrated no statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence ( p = 0.4793)."
