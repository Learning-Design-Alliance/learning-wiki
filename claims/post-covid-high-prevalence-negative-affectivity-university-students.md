### Alves Bruna de Oliveira 2024

Alves Bruna de Oliveira, Lourenço Bárbara Guimarães, Araújo Bianca Bacelar Assis, Toledo Luana Vieira, Chaves Rafael Lopes, Chaves Érika de Cássia Lopes, Chianca Tânia Couto Machado, Moura Caroline de Castro. Revista Brasileira de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/0034-7167-2024-0040

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey (STROBE-reported) of 585 undergraduates at a Brazilian federal university, September 2022 to September 2023, scored with the DASS-21. The article reports "a prevalence of 42.6%" for severe anxiety; depression mean 19.48 (SD 11.65), anxiety 17.19 (11.80), stress 24.05 (10.88).

> "The prevalence of depression was 77.8%, anxiety was 74.4%, and stress was 76.8% among university students in the post-COVID-19 pandemic context. Notably, severe levels of negative affectivity were more prevalent, especially anxiety, with a prevalence of 42.6% (Table 2)."
