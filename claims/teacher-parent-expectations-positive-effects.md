## Evidence

### Herbert W. Marsh 2023

Herbert W. Marsh, Reinhard Pekrun, Jiesi Guo, John Hattie, Eyal Karin. (2023). Too Much of a Good Thing Might Be Bad: the Double-Edged Sword of Parental Aspirations and the Adverse Effects of Aspiration-Expectation Gaps. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09768-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal SEM analysis of the nationally representative ELS:2002 sample of 16,197 Year-10 students from 751 US high schools, controlling achievement, SES, gender, age, ethnicity, track, and a composite risk factor. The study reports "consistently positive effects" of teacher and parent expectations on all listed outcomes; no effect sizes are printed in the available text.

> "Year 10 educational expectations of teachers and parents had consistently positive effects on the following: student expectations in Years 10 and 12, Year 10 academic self-concept, final high-school grade-point-averages, and long-term outcomes at age 26"
