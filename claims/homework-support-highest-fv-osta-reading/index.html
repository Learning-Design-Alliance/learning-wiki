Evidence¶

Engell 2020¶

Engell, T., Kirkøen, B., Hammerstrøm, K. T., Kornør, H., Ludvigsen, K. H., & Hagen, K. A. (2020). Common Elements of Practice, Process and Implementation in Out-of-School-Time Academic Interventions for At-risk Children: a Systematic Review. Prevention Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11121-020-01091-w

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review synthesis of 30 effective and six ineffective OSTA interventions using vote-counting frequency values. The review reports "Homework support had the highest FV with 12 for reading"; no standardized effect sizes were calculated.

"Homework support had the highest FV with 12 for reading, followed by training in parental school involvement at home and positive reinforcement with FVs of 10."

Related Claims¶

  • Structured tutoring was the most common practice element but was ineffective in about 25% of studies using it, illustrating popularity bias in common elements reviews
  • The most common combination in effective OSTA interventions was professionals training caregivers in parental school involvement at home and homework support combined with positive reinforcement — related
  • Quality monitoring was the most common implementation element and regular support to the intervention receiver the most common process element in effective OSTA interventions — related
  • Training in parental school involvement at home, positive reinforcement, psychoeducation, and use of explicit goals showed positive frequency values across reading, math, and GPA outcomes — related
