Robert Fischer, Elton Fireman, José Renan Gomes. (2013). Who teaches science in Alagoas? A quantitative analysis of non-specialist teaching at primary and secondary schools in Brazil. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1304.8055](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1304.8055)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Specialist teaching rates computed from 2011 Ministry of Education subject-grade registrations for Alagoas secondary schools (public and private combined) show maths at "73.5 %" and biology at "77.1 %", whereas "physics is affected particularly strongly".

> "Whereas both maths (73.5 %; this and the following rates are calculated for public and private schools combined) and biology (77.1 %) are taught predominately by specialists in the field, physics is affected particularly strongly."
