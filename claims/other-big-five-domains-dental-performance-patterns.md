## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Agreeableness correlates with GPA (r = 0.21) and clinic marks (r = 0.30); extraversion and openness relate to academic achievement but not clinical performance; neuroticism predicts neither criterion. [→ Jaber Mohamed 2022](#jaber-mohamed-2022)

## Evidence

### Jaber Mohamed 2022

Jaber Mohamed, Al-Samarrai Basim, Salah Afraa, Varma Sudhir Rama, Karobari Mohmed Isaqali, Marya Anand. (2022). Does General and Specific Traits of Personality Predict Students' Academic Performance? BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/9422299

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

The discussion characterizes the correlation patterns printed in Table 5 (N = 144), where agreeableness shows printed effect sizes r = 0.21 with GPA and r = 0.30 with clinic, extraversion r = 0.14 with GPA only, openness r = 0.15 with GPA only, and neuroticism near-zero for both criteria.

> "With more minor degrees, agreeableness was also correlated with success criteria. Extraversion and openness were significantly related to academic achievement but not clinical performance. Neuroticism did not predict any of the two criterion variables."
