## Evidence

### Garrud Paul 2012

Garrud Paul, Yates Janet. (2012). Profiling strugglers in a graduate-entry medicine course at Nottingham: a retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-124

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective flag counts for the 38 study-group students who graduated, covering the last 30 months of full-time clinical rotations, including separate Academic Progress Committee flags for each of the three clinical phases (Table 4).

> "Overall, 22 of the 38 (58%) had 2 or more flags, and the highest proportions were in the health-related, and the strugglers group."

### Garrud Paul 2012 (2)

Garrud Paul, Yates Janet. (2012). Profiling strugglers in a graduate-entry medicine course at Nottingham: a retrospective case study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-124

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Free-text review of course files for health-related strugglers who graduated. The article reports mental health predominated, with circumstances including relationship breakdown, accommodation problems, social isolation, and worries about family members; only one admitted to financial difficulty.

> "mental health predominated, with anxiety, depression and chronic fatigue, some of which seemed likely related to recorded social and personal circumstances"
