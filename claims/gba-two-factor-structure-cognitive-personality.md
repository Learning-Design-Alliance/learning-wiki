## Evidence

### Gazit N 2025

Gazit N, Ben-Gal G, Eliashar R. (2025). Game-Based Assessment of Cognitive Abilities and Personality Characteristics for Surgical Resident Selection: A Preliminary Validation Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/72264

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory factor analysis with Promax rotation on intern competency scores (Kaiser–Meyer–Olkin=0.69). The article states "The only exception was conformity, which did not load on either of the factors" and reports a factor correlation of 0.5, interpreted against the prior job analysis classification.

> "The factor analysis yielded a 2-factor solution, such that seven competencies (analytical thinking, learning, flexibility, concentration, working memory, multitasking, and precision) loaded on one factor, and 6 competencies (planning, problem-solving, ingenuity, goal orientation, self-reflection, and endurance) loaded on the second factor. The only exception was conformity, which did not load on either of the factors."
