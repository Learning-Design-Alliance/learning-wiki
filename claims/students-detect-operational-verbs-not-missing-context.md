## Evidence

### Farahnaz Sadidi and Thomas Prestel 2025

Farahnaz Sadidi and Thomas Prestel. (2025). Impact of Criterion-Based Reflection on Prospective Physics Teachers’ Perceptions of ChatGPT-Generated Content. TU Dresden. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/s44217-025-01071-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-025-01071-8)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative analysis of students’ evaluations of ChatGPT answers to the acoustics task (developing context-oriented teaching for grade 10 and formulating learning goals). Students criticized the goal "The students understand that sound is a form of mechanical wave." because "understand" is not directly measurable.

> "All students recognized ChatGPT’s difficulty in using measurable operational verbs for learning goals"

### Farahnaz Sadidi and Thomas Prestel 2025 (2)

Farahnaz Sadidi and Thomas Prestel. (2025). Impact of Criterion-Based Reflection on Prospective Physics Teachers’ Perceptions of ChatGPT-Generated Content. TU Dresden. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/s44217-025-01071-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-025-01071-8)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Same qualitative analysis of the acoustics task: ChatGPT formulated general tasks such as "Explain the meaning of amplitude, frequency and wavelength for acoustic waves." lacking context, which 38 of 39 students failed to recognize despite introductory didactics coursework.

> "However, 38 out of 39 students did not recognize ChatGPT’s lack of context consideration in task formulation."
