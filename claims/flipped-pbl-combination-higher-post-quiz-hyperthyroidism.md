# Randomized trial: flipped classroom combined with PBL yields significantly higher post-quiz total scores than traditional lecture-based teaching in a hyperthyroidism endocrinology internship

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Hu Xiaolei 2019

Hu Xiaolei, Zhang Hengyan, Song Yuan, Wu Chenchen, Yang Qingqing, Shi Zhaoming, Zhang Xiaomei, Chen Weidong. (2019). Implementation of flipped classroom combined with problem-based learning: an approach to promote learning about hyperthyroidism in the endocrinology internship. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1714-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 74 fourth-year medical students allocated to TG (n = 37) or CG (n = 37). Both groups improved significantly (p < 0.001), and the CG post-quiz mean of 87.3 exceeded the TG's 77.1, consistent with the "CG had significantly higher scores" reported for Fig. 2a.

> "After the classes, in the TG, the mean post-quiz score increased by 24.0 to 77.1 (p < 0.001); in the CG, the mean post-quiz score increased by 34.3 to 87.3 (p < 0.001) (Fig. 2a)."
