## Evidence

### Nils Schwinning 2018

Nils Schwinning, Michael Striewe, Till Massing, Christoph Hanck, Michael Goedicke. (2018). Towards digitalisation of summative and formative assessments in academic teaching of statistics. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1811.02391](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1811.02391)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Design-rationale statement about JACK's parameterization feature, in which placeholders are filled dynamically so each attempt presents different content, including R-generated data sets and plot image variables. The authors hedge that it "possibly helps students to understand the abstract concepts"; no empirical test of these benefits is reported.

> "Thus exercises remain challenging for a longer period of time. Moreover, it possibly helps students to understand the abstract concepts and encourages them to talk to each other about solution strategies instead of plain solutions."
