`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

End-of-study 10-item ATSA Likert survey administered to both 15-student classes; the article reports the choice-item contrast (50% vs 8% strongly agree) and that OLC students felt their artwork was better when they researched artists online.

> "In the final category of student choice and motivation, the online group (M = 50%) strongly agreed that online research gave them more choice in what they learned about as compared with the face-to-face group (M = 8%)."
