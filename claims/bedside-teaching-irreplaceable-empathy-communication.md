Sohail Fadieleh A, Iqbal Marium, Malik Marib Ghulam Rasool, Saeed Sana. (2025). Perspectives of faculty, students and patients on clinical skills teaching in undergraduate medical education in Pakistan: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-103139

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2 interview data from clinical faculty at the Pakistani study site; faculty framed bedside teaching as where abstract knowledge becomes real-life decision-making, stating such skills are learned "on real patients only."

> "How to approach patients, how to demonstrate empathy—that cannot be learned on a mannequin. That the students learn on real patients only."
