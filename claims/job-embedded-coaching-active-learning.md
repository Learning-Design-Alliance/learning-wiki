## Evidence

### Mahsa Bakhshaei 2020

Mahsa Bakhshaei, Angela Hardy, Jason Ravitz, John Seylar. (2020). Instructional Coaching Holds Promise as a Method to Improve Instruction with Technology. SITE 2020 - Online, April 7-10, 2020. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/5465e54a-b290-4210-97fb-514a3aa13e21

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

DLP year 2 survey contrast of coached versus non-coached teachers on active-learning time within PD: 49% vs. 38.5% reported enough time to try and evaluate new ideas, with "Chi-Sq p < .001" printed and no effect size. A related survey item found more than 87% of coached teachers ranked 1:1 meetings and classroom visits as most valuable interactions.

> "While 49% of coached teachers reported that their PD to a great extent included enough time during and between events to think carefully about, try, and evaluate new ideas, only 38.5% of their non-coached peers reported this (Chi-Sq p < .001)."
