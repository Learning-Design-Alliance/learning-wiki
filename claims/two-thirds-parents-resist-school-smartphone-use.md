## Evidence

### Hadad 2020

Hadad, S., Meishar-Tal, H., & Blau, I. (2020). The parents' tale: Why parents resist the educational use of smartphones at schools?. Computers & Education, 157, 103984. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103984

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Online questionnaire of 220 parents of elementary and secondary school students distributed via parent Facebook groups in 2018. The distribution of resistance levels (Figure 2) showed that "64.6% of parents resisted their children' use of smartphones"; percentages are descriptive, no effect size printed.

> "As can be seen, 35.4% of the parents did not resist their children 's use of smartphones in learning. Compared to them, 64.6% of parents resisted their children' use of smartphones, 30% of them expressed passive resistance"
