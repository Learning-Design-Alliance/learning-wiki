## Evidence

### Shin 2025

Shin, G.-H., Kweon, Y.-S., Oh, S., & Lee, S.-W. (2025). Personalized targeted memory reactivation enhances consolidation of challenging memories via slow wave and spindle dynamics. npj Science of Learning 10:47. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00340-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SVM with RBF kernel, 5-fold cross-validation with 2 repetitions, classifying groups from SW power, spindle power, and SW-spindle coupling; performance exceeded chance (surrogate decoding with 250 label shuffles), peaking near 3 s post-stimulus.

> "The accuracy values conﬁrmed the strong discriminative power of these features across all conditions, with classi ﬁcation performance exceeding chance levels. Notably, ac curacy values were higher after the second stimulus (2 s) compared to theﬁrst (0 s), peaking at around 3 s."
