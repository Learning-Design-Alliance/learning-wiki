Evaluation study in which six human subject-matter experts judged 288 AI-generated questions, yielding 2,016 SME-Validator rated pairs treated as ground truth in a binary classification framework. The article reports "human-validated success rates exceeding 91%" on five of seven dimensions with "minimal failure rates of 3.1% or below"; no effect size is printed.
