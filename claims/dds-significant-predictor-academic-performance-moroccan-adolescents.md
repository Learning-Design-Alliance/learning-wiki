## Evidence

### Bouchefra Said 2023

Bouchefra Said, El Ghouddany Safouane, Ouali Karim, Bour Abdellatif. (2023). Is good dietary diversity a predictor of academic success? Acta Bio Medica : Atenei Parmensis. [doi:10.23750/abm.v94i2.13940](https://doi.org/10.23750/abm.v94i2.13940)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 528 students in Eastern Morocco; multivariate linear regression with GPAm and GPAf as dependent variables. DDS showed significant positive coefficients (0.830 for GPAf, 0.990 for GPAm, p < 0.01), supporting the article's claim that "dietary diversity score is a significant predictor of academic performance".

> "Results of multivariate linear regression analyses in Table 3 indicated that dietary diversity score is a significant predictor of academic performance (P value < 0.01) both for GPAm and GPAf."
