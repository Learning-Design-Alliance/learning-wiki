`q2 i?` When MCAT and UGPA were excluded, the second LASSI measure significantly predicted Year II academic performance, explaining 18.8% of variance. [→ Zhou Yuanyuan 2016](#zhou-yuanyuan-2016)

## Evidence

### Zhou Yuanyuan 2016

Zhou Yuanyuan, Graham Lori, West Courtney. (2016). The relationship between study strategies and academic performance. Int J Med Educ 7:324-332. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.57dc.fe0f

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of data from 168 medical students who completed the LASSI before their first and second years. Model 10 used LASSI post-test subscales to predict Year II average: "R2(136) = .188, p = .003". No standardized effect size beyond R2 was printed.

> "However, in the second measure, when MCAT and UGPA were excluded, the LASSI became a statistically significant predictor of student academic performance (Model 10: R2(136) = .188, p = .003)."
