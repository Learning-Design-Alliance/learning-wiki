### Macdonald Kirstin 2018

Macdonald Kirstin, Milne Nikki, Orr Robin, Pope Rodney. (2018). Relationships between Motor Proficiency and Academic Performance in Mathematics and Reading in School-Aged Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15081603

`q3 · i2` · `review · r3`

Systematic review synthesis of nine observational studies of total fine motor scores and mathematics. The review reports all studies found significant positive associations, with correlational analyses showing "significant very weak to strong positive associations (r = 0.25–0.73)"; seven studies were of fair to good quality.

> "Overall, summary coding suggests there was a strong level of evidence to support a significant very weak to strong positive relationship between total fine motor scores and mathematics performance (see Table 1)."
