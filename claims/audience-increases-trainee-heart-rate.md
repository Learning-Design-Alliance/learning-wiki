## Evidence

### Bensouda Brahim 2018

Bensouda Brahim, Mandel Romain, Mejri Abdelwaheb, Lachapelle Jean, St-Hilaire Marie, Ali Nabeel. (2018). Effect of an audience on trainee stress and performance during simulated neonatal intubation: a randomized crossover trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1338-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized crossover trial of 49 residents, heart rate was monitored continuously with a wireless monitor from 5 min before to 5 min after NMEI. The paired t-test on within-subject differences showed the "increase in heart rate over baseline" was significantly higher with the audience: 50.3% versus 38.4% over baseline.

> "% heart rate increase over baseline (bpm), mean (95% CI)	38.4 (34.6, 42.2)	50.3 (45.3, 55.3)	−11.9 (−15.98, −7.78)	< 0.001"
