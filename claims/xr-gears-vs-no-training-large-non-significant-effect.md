### Bickford Michael 2025

Bickford Michael, Alruwaili Fayez, Ragab Sara, Rothenberg Hanna, Abedin-Nasab Mohammad (2025). Impact of extended reality on robot-assisted surgery training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-025-02559-z

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis comparing XR training to no additional training on GEARS scores. The effect was described as "large" (d = 0.75) but "not statistically significant" (p = 0.23).

> "A random-effects analysis of the GEARS scores showed that participants with extended reality training had higher GEARS scores than participants without any additional training. With the training having a large effect on the scores (Cohen’s d = 0.75, 95% CI [−0.48, 2.05]). However, this result is not statistically significant (P = 0.23)"
