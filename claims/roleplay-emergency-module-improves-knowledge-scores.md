## Evidence

### Al-Sebaei Maisa O 2023

Al-Sebaei Maisa O. (2023). Evaluating the use of role-play simulations in teaching management of medical emergencies in the dental clinic. BMC Medical Education, Volume 23, Article 831. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04818-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post analysis of the mandatory module assessed with a 16-item multiple-choice test (KR-20 = 0.74) administered 2017-2020. The mean change in overall scores of "4.0 ± 2.7" was significant by paired t-test. No effect size was printed.

> "Overall, the pre-test and post-test mean scores were 9.4 ± 3.0 and 13.5 ± 1.6, respectively. The mean change in overall scores (4.0 ± 2.7) was statistically significant, p < 0.001."
