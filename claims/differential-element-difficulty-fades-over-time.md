## Evidence

### Wilcox 2019

Wilcox, B. R., & Corsiglia, G. (2019). A cross-context look at upper-division student difficulties with integration. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020136)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exam-solution coding of line-distribution questions found the fraction of students with differential line element difficulties decreased from 14% to 9% between courses. The authors interpret this as suggesting "diﬃculty with element C2 may be one that fades over time."

> "the overall fraction of students exibit- ing diﬃculties with the diﬀerential line element decreases from 14% ( N = 10 of 72) to 9% ( N = 20 of 218) from Class. Mech. to E&M. This downward trend across con- secutive exposures suggests that diﬃculty with element C2 may be one that fades over time as students gain more experience."
