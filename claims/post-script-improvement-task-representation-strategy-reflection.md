## Evidence

### Kielstra 2022

Kielstra, J., Molenaar, I., van Steensel, R., & Verhoeven, L. (2022). 'Supporting socially shared regulation during collaborative task‑oriented reading'. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 17, 65–105. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09365-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test/post-test intervention design; a paired-samples t-test compared individual task representation, strategy selection and strategy reflection before and after four script-supported lessons; 38 students were included after attendance-based exclusions. No control group was used.

> "However, after working with the script, students did improve their ability to determine a correct representation of a high-level task and to reflect on the most appropriate reading strategy for these tasks."
