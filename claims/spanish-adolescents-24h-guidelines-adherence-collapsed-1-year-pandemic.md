## Evidence

### Tapia-Serrano Miguel Angel 2022

Tapia-Serrano Miguel Angel, Sánchez-Oliva David, Sevil-Serrano Javier, Marques Adilson, Sánchez-Miguel Pedro Antonio. (2022). 24-h movement behaviours in Spanish youth before and after 1-year into the covid-19 pandemic and its relationship to academic performance. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-21096-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Repeated cross-sectional study comparing two subsamples of Spanish adolescents, one assessed before the pandemic and one 1-year into it, using questionnaires on physical activity, recreational screen time, sleep duration and academic performance. The article reports that "Only 0.2% reported meeting all three 24-Hour Movement Guidelines, while 66.3% did not meet any of the three recommendations."
