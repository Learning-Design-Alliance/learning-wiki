Libertus Melissa E. 2013¶

Libertus Melissa E., Feigenson Lisa, Halberda Justin. (2013). Is Approximate Number Precision a Stable Predictor of Math Ability? Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2013.02.001

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Longitudinal regression (model 2, Table 4) predicting Time 2 expressive vocabulary from Time 1 ANS accuracy, RT, TEMA-3, and vocabulary while controlling for age (R2 = 0.51, F(4,119) = 31.25, p < 0.001); the article reports "initial expressive vocabulary scores were the only significant predictor of later expressive vocabulary size" (partial r2 = 0.5, p < 0.001 for vocab).
