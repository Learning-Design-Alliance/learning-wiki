## Evidence

### Wibbecke G 2015

Wibbecke G, Kahmann J, Pignotti T, Altenberger L, Kadmon M. (2015). Improving teaching on the basis of student evaluation: Integrative teaching consultation. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000944](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000944)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

A single-case qualitative analysis using Mayring's method of free-text comments from three SETs across three semesters (t1 before consultation, t2 six months in, t3 two years after). The article reports "from 1.1% on t1 to 10.9% on t3" in the lecture and 10% to 20.3% in tutorials; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "The number of positive statements (praise) increased both in the lecture of the teacher who had engaged in the counseling (from 1.1% on t1 to 10.9% on t3) and in the respective tutorials (from 10% on t1 to 20.3% on t3)."
