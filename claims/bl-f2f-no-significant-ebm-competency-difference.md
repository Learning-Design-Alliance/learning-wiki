## Evidence

### Maloney S 2015

Maloney S, Nicklen P, Rivers G, Foo J, Ooi YY, Reeves S, Walsh K, Ilic D. (2015). A Cost-Effectiveness Analysis of Blended Versus Face-to-Face Delivery of Evidence-Based Medicine to Medical Students. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.4346

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up competency assessments within the randomized trial, measured with the validated Berlin questionnaire one month after teaching, found no significant difference between delivery modes: "mean difference -0.68, 95% CI -1.71 to 0.34, P=.19". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "EBM competency did not differ significantly between students receiving the BL approach versus those receiving the F2F approach: mean difference -0.68, 95% CI -1.71 to 0.34, P=.19."
