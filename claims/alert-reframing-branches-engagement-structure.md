Episode-based qualitative analysis of one case-study episode anchored in a Dash4Emotion negative alert; the author interprets the reframing as cognitive-affective branching, after which "the situation was redeﬁned" and Mira's utterance about needing to keep looking marked activation of a new exploratory engagement structure. Her SRI comment reported feeling confused before and better after the teacher's push.

> "However, when he re- framed the mathematical meaning of the task—shifting from “ﬁnding a minimum value” to exploring the geometric lo- cus of minimal points—the situation was redeﬁned."
