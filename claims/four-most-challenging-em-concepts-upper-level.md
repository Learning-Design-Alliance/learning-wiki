## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When CSEM questions are grouped by concept, upper-level students have difficulty with most questions related to four concepts: shielding, connection between electric field and electric potential, magnetic force on a charged particle, and Faraday's law/Lenz's law. ## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2023

Alexandru Maries, Mary Jane Brundage and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Using the Conceptual Survey of Electricity and Magnetism to investigate progression in student understanding from introductory to advanced levels. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.17170](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.17170)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Grouping of the 32 CSEM questions by concept based on post-test performance of 85 upper-level undergraduate students, identifying four concept areas where performance was below two-thirds on most or all related questions. The authors note that for magnetic force and E-field/potential connection, "upper-level student performance was not context specific as they performed poorly on all the questions related to them."

> "we find the most challenging concepts to be 1) Shielding (Q13 and Q14), and 2) Connection between electric field and electric potential (Q16, Q18, Q20), 3) Magnetic force on a charge in a magnetic field (Q21, Q22, Q27, Q31), and 4) Faraday's law/Lenz's law (Q29, Q30, and Q32)"
