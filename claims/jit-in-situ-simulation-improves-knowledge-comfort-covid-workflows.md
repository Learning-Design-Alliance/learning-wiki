## Evidence

### Zucco Liana MBBS 2022

Zucco Liana MBBS, Chen Michael J. BS, Levy Nadav MD, Obeidat Salameh S. MD, Needham Matthew J. BM BCh, Hyatt Allison MD, Keane Jeffrey R. RN, Pollard Richard J. MD, Mitchell John D. MD, Ramachandran Satya Krishna MD. (2022). Just-in-Time In Situ Simulation Training as a Preparedness Measure for the Perioperative Care of COVID-19 Patients. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000635

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post survey of 110 of 428 simulation participants (25.7%) using 1-tailed sign tests with Bonferroni-adjusted thresholds (<0.006). The article reports "statistically significant for all 4 simulations (P < 0.001)" with improvements of at least 1.2 Likert points; no effect size is printed.

> "For all 4 simulations, mean values for perceived knowledge in new workflows and comfort levels in applying them in practice improved by at least 1.2 points on the Likert scale (Figs. 2–5). These differences were statistically significant for all 4 simulations (P < 0.001)."
