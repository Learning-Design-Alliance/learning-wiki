## Evidence

### Inka Sara Hähnlein 2024

Inka Sara Hähnlein, Pablo Pirnay-Dummer. (2024). Promoting pre-service teachers' knowledge integration from multiple text sources across domains with instructional prompts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10363-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA on two trained raters' 0–3 ratings of degree of transfer, validity of conclusions, and degree of integration for N = 109 student texts (EG1 n = 37, EG2 n = 37, CG n = 35). The test "indicated no significant differences between type of prompting"; equivalence was not tested.

> "MANOVA with three rating scores per rater as dependent measures and the experimental conditions as independent measures indicated no significant differences between type of prompting (Wilks' λ = 0.85, F[12,202] = 1.43, p = .16)."

### Inka Sara Hähnlein 2024 (2)

Inka Sara Hähnlein, Pablo Pirnay-Dummer. (2024). Promoting pre-service teachers' knowledge integration from multiple text sources across domains with instructional prompts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10363-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Interrater reliability check (Kendall's tau, Type b) on the two raters' scores. Reliability "was high for transfer" but low for conclusion and integration, so "[t]he ratings were treated separately in the following analysis due to low interrater reliability."

> "Interrater reliability (Kendalls-τ, Type b) was high for transfer (r τ = .52, z = 6.08, p < .001) but low for conclusion (r τ = .21, z = 2.23, p = .02) and integration (r τ = .13, z = 1.60, p = .11)"
