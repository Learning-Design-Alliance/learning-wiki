`q2 · i?`

Survey of 12 students after the training course, closed-ended 5-point Likert questions, reported in Section 6.1. Percentages are descriptive; no effect size or significance test is printed. The article reports "90% of positive responses" for the explanation and 70% for tagging.

> "Considering as positive responses the students that answered either “Very helpful” or “Extremely helpful”, the process-oriented explanation of the procedure had 90% of positive responses, the viewing and tagging of their own execution obtained 70%, receiving a process-oriented feedback report of their execution obtained 90%, and receiving a personalized explanation about the feedback report from one of the instructors obtained 100%."
