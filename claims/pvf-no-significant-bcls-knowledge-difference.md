# Personalized video feedback does not produce a significant difference in BCLS knowledge scores compared with in-person feedback alone

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no statistically significant difference in BCLS knowledge mean scores between the experimental and control groups immediately, one week, or one month after training. [→ Mohsenzadeh Habibeh 2025](#mohsenzadeh-habibeh-2025)

## Evidence

### Mohsenzadeh Habibeh 2025

Mohsenzadeh Habibeh, Farsi Zahra, Afaghi Effat. (2025). The effects of personalized video feedback of basic cardiac life support on knowledge and skills levels in undergraduate nursing students: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06935-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-tests in the same randomized trial (40 nursing students) compared knowledge questionnaire scores (0–20 range) between groups at three intervals; all p-values exceeded 0.05, so no significant difference was detected and equivalence was not tested.

> "No significant difference was also spotted in the BCLS knowledge mean values of the nursing students in both groups immediately (p = 0.467), one week (p = 0.904), and one month (p = 0.626) after training."
