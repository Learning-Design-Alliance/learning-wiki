## Evidence

### Jin 2026

Jin, S., Zhong, Z., Cao, X., & Zhang, W. (2026). Cultivating design creativity of vocational students: A model of project-based learning in AI-enabled immersive virtual environments. Research and Practice in Technology Enhanced Learning, 22, 029. https://doi.org/10.58459/rptel.2027.22029

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Two-group quasi-experiment in a Public Space Interior Design course at a vocational college in Fuzhou, China, comparing AI-IVE-PBL with traditional PBL. The article reports "a marked improvement in students' design ability and creative ability" attributed to sustained idea-developing discourse.

> "relative to traditional PBL (T-PBL), the AI-IVE-PBL model significantly enhances learning outcomes by fostering sustained idea-developing discourse (SIDD), as demonstrated by: (1) a marked improvement in students' design ability and creative ability"
