# Students with low prior knowledge show significantly higher proportional learning gains than students with high prior knowledge when learning with MetaTutor

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` While controlling for condition, participants with low prior knowledge gained significantly more (29%) from pre-test to post-test than high prior knowledge participants (16%). [→ Taub 2019](#taub-2019)

## Evidence

### Taub 2019

Taub, M., & Azevedo, R. (2019). How Does Prior Knowledge Influence Eye Fixations and Sequences of Cognitive and Metacognitive SRL Processes during Learning with an Intelligent Tutoring System? Int J Artif Intell Educ (2019) 29:1-28. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0165-4

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA with prior knowledge group (2 levels) as independent variable, proportional learning gain as dependent variable, and condition as covariate in the full sample (N = 194). The article reports F(1,191) = 7.22, p <. 01, ηp2 = .036, "significantly lower proportional learning gains" for HPK.

> "participants with high prior knowledge had significantly lower proportional learning gains ( M =. 1 6 , SD = .42) than participants with low prior knowledge ( M = .29, SD = .22)."
