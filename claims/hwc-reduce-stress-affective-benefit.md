## Evidence

### Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox 2025

Molly Griston and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2025). Motivating reflection in problem solving: homework corrections in upper-division physics courses. [doi:10.1103/ntvp-spkc](https://doi.org/10.1103/ntvp-spkc)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Interview data from case-study student Ari in Instructor A's QM2 course. He states HWCs "reduces his stress when initially completing the assignment" and contrasts with courses without HWCs where he says he is "just chasing a grade now."

> "It's good knowing that I can do homework corrections in the end, just so I'm not chasing, like, a perfect score on a homework assignment."
