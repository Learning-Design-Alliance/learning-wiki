### Wiedermann 2025

Wiedermann, W., Reinke, W. M., & Herman, K. C. (2025). Distributional moderation analysis: Unpacking moderation effects in intervention research. Journal of School Psychology, 108. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsp.2024.101399

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Methodological argument comparing distribution-free models (quantile regression) with complete distribution models (GAMLSS), citing prior comparative work. The article states "GAMLSSshouldbepreferred" for the total distribution, citing advantages in model fit evaluation, accommodation of discrete and mixed distributions, and avoidance of quantile crossing. No empirical test is reported in this article for this comparison.

> "overall, quantile regression should only be preferred over GAMLSS when one is interested in specific value regions (i.e., specific quantiles)oftheoutcomedistribution.Whenoneisinterestedinthetotaloutcomedistribution,GAMLSSshouldbepreferred"
