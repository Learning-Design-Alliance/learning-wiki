Qualitative thematic analysis finding from the co-design workshop with 12 students: participants characterized the probe as a "secretary", "mediator", and "facilitator", and reported that "private prompting surfaced implicit reasoning, while public reorganization made disciplinary positions easier to compare". Descriptive qualitative finding; no effect size printed.
