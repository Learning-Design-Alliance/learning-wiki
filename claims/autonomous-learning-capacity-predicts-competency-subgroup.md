## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Compared with high autonomous learning capacity, moderate capacity was associated with significantly higher odds of belonging to the low (OR = 3.878) and medium (OR = 3.298) rather than the high competency group. [→ Guan Q 2025](#guan-q-2025)

## Evidence

### Guan Q 2025

Guan Q, Zhu X, Xue Z, Peng M. (2025). Core competency in palliative care among intensive care unit nurses: A latent profile analysis. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70021

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression (n = 342) with the high competency group as reference found "autonomous learning capacity (OR = 3.878, 95% CI: 1.447–10.396, p = .007)" for the low group; the medium group's corresponding estimate was OR = 3.298 (95% CI: 1.390–7.822, p = .007).

> "The findings revealed a statistically significant effect of position (odds ratio [OR] = 0.101, 95% confidence interval [CI]: 0.011–0.918, p = .042) and autonomous learning capacity (OR = 3.878, 95% CI: 1.447–10.396, p = .007) on the low palliative care core competency group."
