## Evidence

### Yamada Toshiyuki 2017

Yamada Toshiyuki, Osako Motohiko, Uchimuro Tomoya, Yoon Ryogen, Morikawa Toshiaki, Sugimoto Maki, Suda Hisao, Shimizu Hideyuki. (2017). Three-Dimensional Printing of Life-Like Models for Simulation and Training of Minimally Invasive Cardiac Surgery. Innovations (Philadelphia, Pa.). https://doi.org/10.1097/IMI.0000000000000423

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A feasibility demonstration (no learner outcome data): the authors performed ring annuloplasty, artificial chordal reconstruction, resection and suture, and edge-to-edge repair on the model using real MICS instruments, camera systems, and a da Vinci Surgical System, and judged the feel and working-space difficulty the same as actual surgery.

> "The feel of the replica was remarkably similar to that of the actual biotexture of a patient heart. The difficulty of the simulated procedures, particularly the limited surgical working space caused by the ribs, was the same as that during an actual surgery."
