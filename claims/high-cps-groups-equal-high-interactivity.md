## Evidence

### Cukurova 2017

Cukurova, M., Luckin, R., Millán, E., & Mavrikis, M. (2017). The NISPI framework: Analysing collaborative problem-solving from students' physical interactions. Preprint submitted to Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.08.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of mean activity codes per student (Table 4). High CPS group J's member means were 1.75, 1.81 and 1.68 with total squared differences of 0.02; the article states the difference for group B is "approximately forty times bigger" than in group J. No effect size is printed.

> "the mean physical interactivity scores for member students of U-Group B, which was coded as one of the low CPS groups, were 1.07,1.12, and 1.71, and the average of the differences between the three students' physical interactivity was 0.76."
