Elaine Allensworth 2012¶

Elaine Allensworth. (2012). Want to Improve Teaching? Create Collaborative, Supportive Schools. American Educator, Fall 2012. http://www.annenberginstitute.org/VUE

q2 · i? · review · r2

The Chicago teacher mobility research identified two working conditions accounting for most mobility differences by school racial composition: parent partnerships (elementary) and learning climate—students feeling safe and reporting appropriate academic peer behavior (high schools).

"Especially in elementary schools, teachers are more likely to stay in schools where they feel that parents support their work as partners in educat- ing students. The other, which is particu- larly critical in high schools, is the learning climate at the school."
