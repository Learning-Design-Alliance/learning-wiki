### Şen Olgay Sevim 2025

Şen Olgay Sevim, Çağan Kişin Şevval. (2025). Validating the Turkish version of the team-based learning assessment scale: a psychometric evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06893-6

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity assessment using the Davis technique with 11 experts (nursing faculty and a clinical psychologist) rating each item on a 4-point scale. The article reports the item CVI "ranged between 0.86 and 1.00 and the average CVI was 0.95", exceeding the literature thresholds it cites.
