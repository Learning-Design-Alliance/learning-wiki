## Evidence

### Lewis Murray 2017

Lewis Murray. (2017). A Study in Enhancing L2 Learners’ Utility with Written Academic Formulaic Sequences. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3), 76–92. https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1274

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental comparison of four treatment classes (n = 115) against a control of 54 learners in the same 8-week writing course; AntConc counted target FSs per 200 words in midterm and final papers. The article reports the treatment group's midterm-to-final gain as "significant, T = 477, p < .001, r = -.52."

> "However, gains made by the treatment group between the midterm (Mdn = 1.28) and the final (Mdn = 3.08) were significant, T = 477, p < .001, r = -.52."
