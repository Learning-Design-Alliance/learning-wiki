Evidence¶

Jason M. Lodge and Linda Corrin 2017¶

Jason M. Lodge and Linda Corrin. (2017). What data and analytics can and do say about effective learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0006-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

The authors' theoretical argument that laboratory studies "provide suggestions as to the type of behaviours evident when a student gets confused", enabling interventions such as automated feedback or teacher communication when markers recur online. No empirical data are reported in the commentary.

"When these same markers become evident when students learn in a real online environment, we can say with some con ﬁdence that the student could be confused. Having identi ﬁed potential confusion, appropriate educational interventions can be made."
