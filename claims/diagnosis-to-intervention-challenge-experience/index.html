Evidence¶

Anouschka van Leeuwen 2019¶

Anouschka van Leeuwen. (2019). Teachers’ perceptions of the usability of learning analytics reports in a flipped university course: when and how does information become actionable knowledge? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-09639-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview analysis from the seven-teacher qualitative study; Table 3 lists the transition from diagnosis to intervention as the challenge raised by all seven teachers, with 48 mentions for interpretation and actionable knowledge. Teachers sometimes detected a problem but could not act on it.

"There were also cases, however, when a teacher did detect a problem but was unable to act upon it."

Anouschka van Leeuwen 2019 (2)¶

Anouschka van Leeuwen. (2019). Teachers’ perceptions of the usability of learning analytics reports in a flipped university course: when and how does information become actionable knowledge? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-09639-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion section, based on observed variation among teachers in choosing interventions, partly related to teacher experience. The article identifies two competencies: detecting issues and choosing appropriate interventions.

"The less experienced teachers were more hesitant about what to do with the information offered on the LA report."
