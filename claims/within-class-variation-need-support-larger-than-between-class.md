# Within‐classroom variation in teacher and student perceptions of need support is considerably larger than between‐classroom variation, indicating teachers differentiate between students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Most variance in teacher perceptions of student‐specific autonomy support and structure, and in student‐perceived need support, is situated at the student level within classes. [→ Domen 2020](#domen-2020)

## Evidence

### Domen 2020

Domen, J., Hornstra, L., Weijers, D., van der Veen, I., & Peetsma, T. (2020). Differentiated need support by teachers: Student‐specific provision of autonomy and structure and relations with student motivation. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 90(2), 403–423. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12302

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive ICC analysis of questionnaire data from 24 teachers and 506 upper elementary students in the Netherlands. The printed ICC(1) values were .37 for teacher‐perceived structure and .23 for teacher‐perceived autonomy support; the article states "a much larger share of the variance was situated at the student level".

> "The ICC(1) for teacher perceptions of student‐specific structure was .37, and for student‐specific autonomy support was .23, indicating that for both aspects of need support, there is a substantial proportion of the variance distributed at the class level"
