Evidence¶

Rebecka Rundquist 2024¶

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, from the included study by Faber et al. (2017), that DLS use is more effective for learning when teachers use LA for DBDM.

"Faber et al. (2017) found that using a DLS is more effective for learning if teachers use LA for DBDM."

Rebecka Rundquist 2024 (2)¶

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Kalloo and Mohan's comparison of teacher-supported versus unsupported DLS use: with teacher support, students used the DLS "67% more times and 300% longer"; study 2 also showed 95% of students improving with an average increase of 10.2%.

"When usage was supported by the teacher, students used the DLS 67% more times and 300% longer (Kalloo & Mohan, 2011a)."

Rebecka Rundquist 2024 (3)¶

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Kalloo and Mohan's third study, where an 18-student experimental algebra group was compared with 54 non-using students taught by the same teacher; no group difference was found, though passers used the DLS more.

"There was no difference between the control and experimental group, but evidence suggests that the students who passed the post-test (10/18) used the DLS 22% more and 63% longer than students who failed."
