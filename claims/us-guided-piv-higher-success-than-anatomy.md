## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among logged clinical attempts by the eight nurses, the ultrasound-guided approach had a statistically significant higher success rate than the anatomic approach (77.4% vs 35.9%; p < 0.001 Chi-square test). [→ Schott CK 2024](#schott-ck-2024)

## Evidence

### Schott CK 2024

Schott CK, Devore JA, Kelly MYB, Mayr FB. (2024). Asynchronous training for ultrasound-guided peripheral IV placement among critical care nurses. The Journal of Vascular Access. https://doi.org/10.1177/11297298221129675

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Repeated-measures comparison of 64 anatomy attempts and 84 ultrasound-guided attempts logged by eight nurses. Ultrasound-guided attempts succeeded "77.4% of the time (n = 65/84)" versus 35.9% (23/64) for anatomy; p < 0.001 by Chi-square test.

> "When staff used an anatomy approach, they were successful in placing a PIV 35.9% of the time (n = 23/64) with a mean of 1.5 attempts"
