# Multigraph indegree centrality is consistently and positively correlated with student performance across 12 PBL courses, with stronger correlations than other configurations in eight courses

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Indegree centrality computed on multigraph networks was positively correlated with performance in all 12 courses, ranging r = 0.54 to r = 0.77, and was stronger than other configurations in eight courses. [→ Mohammed Saqr 2020](#mohammed-saqr-2020)

## Evidence

### Mohammed Saqr 2020

Mohammed Saqr, et al. (2020). Capturing the participation and social dimensions of computer-supported collaborative learning through social network analysis: which method and measures matter? International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning 15:227–248. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-020-09322-6

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of LMS forum interaction networks from 12 problem-based learning courses (13,428 interactions, 598 students). The study reports that multigraph indegree was consistently positively correlated with performance, with correlations from "r = 0.54, p < 0.01 to r = 0.77, p < 0.01". Simplified and weighted variants missed correlations in two to three courses.

> "The Spearman correlation between indegree centrality calculated with the multigraph method proved to be consistently positively correlated, and with higher strength of correlation coefficient with performance, in the 12 studied courses (Fig. 3). The correlation coefficient ranged from r = 0.54, p < 0.01 to r = 0.77, p < 0.01; the coefficient value was also stronger than the other configurations in eight courses."
