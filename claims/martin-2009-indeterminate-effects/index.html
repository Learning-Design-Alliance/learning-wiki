What Works Clearinghouse 2020¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020). eMINTS Comprehensive Program Intervention Report │ Teacher Excellence Topic Area. NCEE WWC 2020-011. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/705

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Cluster quasi-experimental design comparing eMINTS-trained and non-eMINTS classrooms in grades 4–5. Table 6 reports average effect sizes of -0.04 (mathematics) and +0.02 (literacy), neither statistically significant after WWC clustering corrections.

"Findings from Martin et al. (2009) show evidence of indeterminate effects of eMINTS on general mathematics achievement and general literacy achievement (Table 6). These findings are based on outcome analyses that include 1,165 and 1,166 students in each domain, respectively."
