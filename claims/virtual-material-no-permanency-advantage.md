## Evidence

### Taş 2015

Erol Taş. (2015). The Effects of Activity and Gain Based Virtual Material on Student’s Success, Permanency and Attitudes towards Science Lesson. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 3(5). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v3i5.817

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Permanency test administered three weeks after the unit ended in the quasi-experiment with 35 students per group. Experimental group scores decreased slightly from 71.8857 to 68.6857, but "no statistically significant difference was found in the permanence of knowledge".

> "When the permanence results of the experimental group students were examined, no statistically significant difference was found in the permanence of knowledge (t(69) = 1.033, p>0.05)."

### Taş 2015 (2)

Erol Taş. (2015). The Effects of Activity and Gain Based Virtual Material on Student’s Success, Permanency and Attitudes towards Science Lesson. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 3(5). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v3i5.817

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same permanency comparison, control group post-test scores fell from 57.6000 to 50.0571 three weeks later; the article reports "a statistically negative difference was found in control group students" (t(69) = 2.736, p<0.05).

> "However, a statistically negative difference was found in control group students (t(69) = 2.736, p<0.05)."
