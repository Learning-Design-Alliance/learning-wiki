## Evidence

### Alaee 2023

Alaee, D. Z., & Zwickl, B. M. (2023). Challenges and outcomes in remote undergraduate research programs during the COVID-19 pandemic. arXiv. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Post-program interviews (interview nine) with all mentees in the phenomenographic study; "almost every mentee" "described having achieved their goals as a result of their research experience," and all stated attending the REU was the right decision in their academic journey.

> "We found that almost every mentee, despite their diﬀerent projects and diﬀerent circumstances during COVID-19, described having achieved their goals as a result of their research experience."

### Alaee 2023 (2)

Alaee, D. Z., & Zwickl, B. M. (2023). Challenges and outcomes in remote undergraduate research programs during the COVID-19 pandemic. arXiv. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010135)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Post-program interviews; most mentees (N=9) "were sure that they would like to apply to graduate programs," citing experiences such as getting a better understanding of how research works and mentor influence on the decision.

> "After ﬁnishing the REU program, most mentees ( N=9) were sure that they would like to apply to graduate programs."
