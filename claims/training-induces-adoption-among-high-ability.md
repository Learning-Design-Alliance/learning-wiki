`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive analysis of DeepSeek adoption rates by grade-point quartile and group (Table 4): Q4 shows 0.0% (0/6) in Group 2 versus 41.7% (5/12) in Group 3. The authors caution the pattern "should be interpreted as descriptive rather than confirmatory" because grade point is a post-treatment outcome.

> "The introduction of training in Group 3 leveled adoption across the ability distribution, with the most dramatic increases occurring among those in the top-grade point quartile (Table 4)."
