### Hassan Ahmed Nour Eldin 2026

Hassan Ahmed Nour Eldin, Lasheen Noha N. (2026). Impact of an integrated interdisciplinary team-based learning session of medicine and health sector programs at Galala University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09732-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive analysis of a 20-item satisfaction survey administered after the session to 25 volunteers. The article reports positive item means of "3.95 to 4.75" and negative item means of "2.05 to 2.85"; these are descriptive, untested values.

> "positive indicators, such as enjoyment of TBL activities and enhanced learning in a team environment, exhibited mean values ranging from 3.95 to 4.75. Conversely, negative indicators, including susceptibility to distraction during TBL activities and boredom during TBL activities, demonstrated a range of 2.05 to 2.85."
