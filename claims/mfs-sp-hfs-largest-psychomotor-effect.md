## Evidence

### Kim Junghee 2016

Kim Junghee, Park Jin-Hwa, Shin Sujin. (2016). Effectiveness of simulation-based nursing education depending on fidelity: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0672-7

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis of psychomotor learning outcomes by fidelity level in the 40-study meta-analysis (Table 4). The psychomotor effect sizes were MFS ES = 1.76, SP ES = 1.27, and HFS ES = 1.03; the discussion states "the order was MFS (1.76), SP (1.27), and HFS (1.03)", which the authors say concurs with the reported lack of positive association between fidelity and process skills.
