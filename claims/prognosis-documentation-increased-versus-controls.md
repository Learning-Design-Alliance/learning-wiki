## Evidence

### Schoenborn Nancy L. 2015

Schoenborn Nancy L., Boyd Cynthia, Cayea Danelle, Nakamura Kelly, Xue Qian-Li, Ray Anushree, McNabney Matthew. (2015). Incorporating prognosis in the care of older adults with multimorbidity: description and evaluation of a novel curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0488-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retrospective, blinded, controlled chart review of 71 charts per group (25 baseline, 46 follow-up) of visits with patients aged at least 60 with at least two Elixhauser conditions; within-group change "p = 0.15". The follow-up inter-group difference (8/46 vs 0/46) was significant (p = 0.006).

> "overall prognosis was documented in 1/25 charts (4 %) at baseline and 8/46 charts (17 %) at follow-up (p = 0.15)."
