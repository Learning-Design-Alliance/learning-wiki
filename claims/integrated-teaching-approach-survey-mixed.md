## Evidence

### Chen Xiaolin 2026

Chen Xiaolin, Huang Yan, Cai Yuli, Chen Qiong. (2026). The integration of clinical pathway and case-based teaching in standardized residency training in the department of endocrinology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09074-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five-point Likert satisfaction survey completed anonymously online by the randomized residents after the theoretical examination; three of five dimensions favored the integrated group with the printed means and p-values above.

> "the integration clinical pathway with a case-based teaching approach significantly enhanced standardized trainees’ learning engagement, improved their comprehension and management of clinical conditions, and promoted the development of clinical reasoning skills (4.54 ± 0.09 vs. 3.81 ± 0.13, p = 0.000; 4.43 ± 0.10 vs. 4.07 ± 0.14, p = 0.040; 4.61 ± 0.07 vs. 4.12 ± 0.13, p = 0.001)"

### Chen Xiaolin 2026 (2)

Chen Xiaolin, Huang Yan, Cai Yuli, Chen Qiong. (2026). The integration of clinical pathway and case-based teaching in standardized residency training in the department of endocrinology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09074-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same survey found no significant group differences on two of its five dimensions; communication skills were marginal (4.57 ± 0.10 vs. 4.23 ± 0.14, p = 0.054) and research awareness clearly null (p = 0.704).

> "However, no statistically significant differences were observed between the two groups of residents in terms of improvements in awareness of emerging technologies and scientific research advancements, or in communication and interpersonal skills."
