## Evidence

### Zafar Sobia 2026

Zafar Sobia, Cheong Luan Lin Jovita, Ler Vonn, Teh Anastasia Min, Abbott Paul. (2026). How Well Are Dental Students Equipped to Handle Traumatic Dental Injuries in Children?. Australian Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.70074

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison using one-way ANOVA across demographic subgroups of 118 dental students. Students aware of IADT guidelines scored higher: "The mean knowledge score for those students who had heard of the IADT guidelines was 27.6 ± 4.8, while those who had not heard of the guidelines had a significantly lower mean score of 18.2 ± 6.0."

> "The mean knowledge score for those students who had heard of the IADT guidelines was 27.6 ± 4.8, while those who had not heard of the guidelines had a significantly lower mean score of 18.2 ± 6.0"

### Zafar Sobia 2026 (2)

Zafar Sobia, Cheong Luan Lin Jovita, Ler Vonn, Teh Anastasia Min, Abbott Paul. (2026). How Well Are Dental Students Equipped to Handle Traumatic Dental Injuries in Children?. Australian Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.70074

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional comparison of knowledge scores by IADT reading status among 118 dental students. "The mean knowledge score for those students who had read the IADT guidelines was 28.1 ± 4.6, while those who had not read the guidelines had a lower mean knowledge score of 26.2 ± 6.0."

> "The mean knowledge score for those students who had read the IADT guidelines was 28.1 ± 4.6, while those who had not read the guidelines had a lower mean knowledge score of 26.2 ± 6.0"
