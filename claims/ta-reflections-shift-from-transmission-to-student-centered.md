# Lab TAs' written reflections shifted from a traditional transmission model of education toward a student-centered view of learning across the professional development program

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` At the beginning of the program, most TAs described their role in terms of a traditional transmission model of education, emphasizing explaining concepts and clarifying procedures. [→ Danny Doucette 2020](#danny-doucette-2020)
`q2 i?` By the end of the program, TAs had generally discarded the transmission model in favor of a student-centered view of learning, celebrating students figuring things out on their own and with lab partners. [→ Danny Doucette 2020 (2)](#danny-doucette-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Danny Doucette 2020

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh (2020). All aboard! Challenges and successes in professional development for physics lab TAs. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.doucette](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.doucette)

`q2 · i?`

Qualitative analysis of lab TAs' written responses to variations on the question of how they would help students have effective learning experiences, collected at the start of the fall and spring programs: "most of the TAs described their role in terms of a traditional transmission model of education".

> "At the beginning of the professional development program, most of the TAs described their role in terms of a traditional transmission model of education [31], as these representative excerpts illustrate"

### Danny Doucette 2020 (2)

Danny Doucette, Russell Clark, and Chandralekha Singh (2020). All aboard! Challenges and successes in professional development for physics lab TAs. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.doucette](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.doucette)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

End-of-program written responses from the same reflection analysis showed a marked transformation: "the TAs have generally discarded the transmission model in favor of a student-centered view of learning", with responses centering the student's experience instead of the TA's work.

> "These final reflections indicate that after the professional development the TAs have generally discarded the transmission model in favor of a student-centered view of learning, which was one of our goals."
