## Evidence

### Estrella Michelle M 2012

Estrella Michelle M, Sisson Stephen D, Roth Jennifer, Choi Michael J. (2012). Efficacy of an internet-based tool for improving physician knowledge of chronic kidney disease: an observational study. BMC Nephrology. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2369-13-126

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Topic-level subset analysis of 2008–2009 data (Table 2), with paired comparisons by question category. The article reports significant increases within each subgroup (all P < 0.01), including a 33.5% increase for hypertension management; anemia was not evaluated due to too few questions.

> "The lowest mean pre-test score were for questions pertaining to CKD recognition and staging (53.1%) followed by hypertension management in CKD (56.9%), then mineral bone disease (57.4%). There was, however, a significant increase in scores between the pre-test and post-test within each question subgroup"
