Dr Jo Rose 2017¶

Dr Jo Rose, Professor Sally Thomas, Dr Lei Zhang, Dr Anna Edwards, Dr Andres Augero, Ms Pooneh Roney. (2017). Research Learning Communities: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581267

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Exploratory Part 2 analysis linking individual teacher round two survey responses to pupil KS2 attainment via class lists, using multi-level modelling. This is a correlational relationship, not a causal effect of the intervention.

"The more positive the disposition of KS2 teachers towards research use, the higher the pupils’ KS2 Grammar, Punctuation, and Spelling outcomes, and KS2 Numeracy outcomes, irrespective of whether teachers were in intervention or control schools."
