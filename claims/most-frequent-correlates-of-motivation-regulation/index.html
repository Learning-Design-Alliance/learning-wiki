Maike Trautner 2025¶

Maike Trautner, Carola Grunschel, Malte Schwinger. (2025). Motivating Motivation Regulation Research—An Evidence and Gap Map Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10019-1

q3 · i? · review · r3

Descriptive synthesis across the 289 included studies in the evidence and gap map, summarizing which variables appear most often in relation to motivation regulation. The review reports that "Achievement, effort-related variables, (meta-)cognitive learning strategies, and goal orientations were the most frequently reported correlates of motivation regulation."
