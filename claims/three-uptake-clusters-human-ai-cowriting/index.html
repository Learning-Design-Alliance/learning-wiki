q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the preregistered study (n = 131 participants who interacted with ChatGPT), recurring n-grams between AI responses and final texts were extracted, binned, and clustered via PCA and k-means. The authors "empirically identified three distinct clusters of participants on the basis of their textual overlap patterns." A scree plot showed the elbow at k = 3.

"Using an argumentative writing task, in which individuals could use ChatGPT, we empirically identified three distinct clusters of participants on the basis of their textual overlap patterns."
