# Awareness of knowledge gaps and curiosity do not mediate the effect of collaboration on conceptual knowledge in PF

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The serial mediation through awareness of knowledge gaps and curiosity showed no indirect effect of condition on conceptual knowledge, and the individual paths were not significant. [→ Charleen Brand 2023](#charleen-brand-2023)

## Evidence

### Charleen Brand 2023

Charleen Brand, Christian Hartmann, Katharina Loibl, Nikol Rummel. (2023). Do students learn more from failing alone or in groups? Insights into the effects of collaborative versus individual problem solving in productive failure. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09619-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mediation analysis (N = 135) of self-reported gap awareness and curiosity collected after the problem-solving phase. The serial indirect effect a3d43b4 = −0.00 had a confidence interval spanning zero; conditions did not differ on either self-report.

> "no indirect effect of the condition on conceptual knowledge as mediated through awareness of knowledge gaps and curiosity could be found, which was indicated by the bootstrap confidence interval, a3d43b4 = − 0.00, SE = 0.01, 95% CI [− 0.02, 0.02]."
