Interview findings from the qualitative study of 27 undergraduates report that almost all participants had used GenAI or had friends who did, and that "It increased their productivity, helped them to refine their thoughts, improved their language clarity."

"It increased their productivity, helped them to refine their thoughts, improved their language clarity, and enabled them to explore ideas immediately with less effort."
