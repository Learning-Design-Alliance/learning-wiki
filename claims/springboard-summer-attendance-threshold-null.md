# Meeting the 80% scholar attendance threshold is not significantly associated with reading growth in Springboard Summer

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Both in t-tests and in regressions controlling for scholar characteristics, meeting the 80% attendance threshold showed no significant effect on change in grade level equivalent. [→ Piccinino 2020](#piccinino-2020)

## Evidence

### Piccinino 2020

Piccinino, K., Pepper, S. K., Salomon, H., Greenfield, S., McClanahan, W., & Robertson-Kraft, C. (2020). Springboard Summer Reading Program Evaluation Report. https://www.cde.state.co.us/cdelib/summerslide

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null finding from t-tests and grade-level regression models within the treatment sample; the report states the t-tests "tell the same story as" the regressions. No effect size is printed, so impact is null.
