Lotvonen S, Parkkila M, Tuomikoski A-M, Kääriäinen M, DiBiasi V, Platani E, Bruno I, Siira H. (2026). Self-Assessed Restorative Care Competence Among Nursing Professionals in Finland: A Cross-Sectional Study. Western Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/01939459261478193

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 438 nursing professionals in geriatric units in Northern Finland using the ReCCA instrument. The article reports the total mean as "good (mean = 3.4 on 1-5 scale; standard deviation = 0.72)" with 46% rating very good and 11% fair.

> "The total self-assessed RC competence was rated as good (mean = 3.4 on 1-5 scale; standard deviation = 0.72). Almost half (46%) of the participants rated their total RC competence as very good, 43% rated their competence as good, and only 11% rated competence as fair."
