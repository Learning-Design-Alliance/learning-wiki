# Precisely coordinated transitions among spatial-orientational behavioral clusters help stabilize local patterns of student thinking

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students moved in and out of worksheet-oriented, strip-oriented, and mutual-attention behavioral clusters with remarkable synchrony, and feedback from spatial arrangement and orientation is proposed to stabilize local patterns of thinking. [→ Frank 2012](#frank-2012)

## Evidence

### Frank 2012

Frank, B. W., and Scherr, R. E. (2012). Interactional processes for stabilizing conceptual coherences in physics. Submitted to PRST-PER. https://journals.aps.org/prper/ [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.8.020101](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.8.020101)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Participation-based video analysis in Section 4.B.1 of three behavioral clusters. During the initial discussion the students transitioned eight times between worksheet- and strip-oriented clusters, and "The transitions are precisely coordinated (though not centrally directed)."

> "The transitions are precisely coordinated (though not centrally directed), suggesting that feedback from participants’ spatial arrangement and orientation helps to stabilize local patterns of thinking."
