Multivariable linear regression on ChatGPT usage scores (n = 551, R2 = 0.117) found "having a very good (Beta = 1.73) and good (Beta = 2.47) GPA compared to excellent was significantly associated with higher ChatGPT usage total scores"; satisfactory GPA was also significant (Beta = 2.49, p = 0.033). Only regression coefficients are printed, no standardized effect size.

> "Moreover, having a very good (Beta = 1.73) and good (Beta = 2.47) GPA compared to excellent was significantly associated with higher ChatGPT usage total scores (Table 4)."
