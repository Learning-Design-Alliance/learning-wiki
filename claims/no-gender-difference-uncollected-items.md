## Evidence

### Cassandra Paul 2026

Cassandra Paul, David J. Webb, and Mary Kate Chessey. (2026). Missing Data on Physics Exams: Demographic Patterns, Course-Level Predictions, and Implications for Equity. [doi:10.1103/b4q5-dkn5](https://doi.org/10.1103/b4q5-dkn5)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Re-analysis with hierarchical linear modeling after removing suspected True/False exam items from the database. The authors report that "the small gender difference seen in our previous paper [3] has disappeared because the gender differences in the apparent True/False questions that we removed were the source of our earlier measured gender difference." Appendix B extends this null result across twenty-three demographic groupings.
