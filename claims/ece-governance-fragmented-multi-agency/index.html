The Background section's fourth systemic challenge describes federal ECE administration, illustrated by Figure 1, a chart of agencies overseeing ECE programs in California. It names HHS's Administration for Children and Families and ED offices as overseers, and states the complexity is passed down to state administrators.

"Multiple federal agencies oversee their administration, including the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) Administration for Children and Families and the Department of Education (ED) Office of Special Education and Office of Elementary and Secondary Education."
