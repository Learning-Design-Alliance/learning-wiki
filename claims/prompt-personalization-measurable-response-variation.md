### Saptarshi Basu 2026

Saptarshi Basu, Sandeep Kakar, Ashok Goel. (2026). A Prompt-Engineering Approach to Develop Scalable, Flexible, and Real-Time Hybrid Micro-Level Personalization in a General Purpose AI Teaching Assistant. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03402 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.03402](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.03402)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Automated NLP analysis of 2,910 responses generated with GPT-4.1 (temperature 0) for 30 real KBAI course questions under 96 personalized profiles plus one baseline, evaluated on lexical similarity, semantic similarity, complexity, and verbosity. The article reports "measurable vari- ation in response expression, length, and complexity".

> "Overall, the NLP analyses demonstrate measurable vari- ation in response expression, length, and complexity across personalization conditions."
