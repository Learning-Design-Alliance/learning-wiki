Descriptive analysis of pre-test/post-test interest scores (Table 1): experimental group pre-test mean 88.68 (SD 2.00), post-test 98.92 (SD 1.89); control group 66.31 (SD 2.78) to 70.92 (SD 17.63). These are descriptive means; the inferential test is reported separately.

> "The mean gain for students taught using Round -robin instructional strategy and conventional method are 10.24 and 4.61 respectively. The result indicates that students taught electrochemistry using Round -robin instructional strategy had higher interest mean scores than their counterparts taught the same topic using conventional teaching method."
