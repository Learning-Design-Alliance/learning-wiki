## Evidence

### Hadar Ram 2024

Hadar Ram, Guy Grinfeld & Nira Liberman. (2024). Anticipated variability increases generalization of predictive learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00269-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Online experiment (final N = 92, random assignment) replicating the predictive learning task with a lightning-bolt outcome; participants expected to apply learning immediately or a week later. Table 2 reports temporal distance F(1, 90) = 4.42, p = 0.038, η2p = 0.047; a similarity-to-S+ main effect (η2p = 0.401 across all blocks) showed generalization decreased with decreasing similarity, with no interaction.

> "As hypothesized, we found broader generalization in the distal condition ( M = 5.80, SD = 2.06) than in the proximal condition (M = 4.79, SD = 2.50; see Table2 and Fig. 2b)."
