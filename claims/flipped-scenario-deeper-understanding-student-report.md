## Evidence

### Seidi Masoumeh 2024

Seidi Masoumeh, Ramezani-Aliakbari Fatemeh, Doosti-Irani Amin. (2024). Effectiveness of the flipped classroom method using clinical scenarios and Educational Technology versus Subject-Based Lectures in a gastrointestinal physiology course for medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05863-8

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of student feedback from the intervention group, reported in the Discussion as a qualitative perception rather than a tested result; the article links it to the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve but offers no separate measure of long-term retention.

> "Students indicated that after studying the pre-presented course materials, discussing them in groups along with clinical scenarios, and solving them with the help of the teacher, they could remember the material for a long time and achieve a deeper understanding of basic and clinical science concepts."
