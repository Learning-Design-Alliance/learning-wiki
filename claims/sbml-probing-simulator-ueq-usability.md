### Theerakulpisut Saralee 2026

Theerakulpisut Saralee, Tengtrisorn Supaporn, Singha Penny, Rattanalert Narisa. (2026). Simulation-based mastery learning for nasolacrimal duct probing in ophthalmology residency: a prospective pre–post educational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09213-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Thai-version UEQ administered post-training to the 20 residents; results were benchmarked against other products using principal component analysis, and internal consistency was excellent (Cronbach's alpha = 0.91).

> "User experience, assessed using the UEQ, showed high scores across all six domains—attractiveness, perspicuity, efficiency, dependability, stimulation, and novelty. When benchmarked against existing products, the model demonstrated superior performance across all domains, with attractiveness being the highest-rated domain"
