## Evidence

### Alvin Daniel Riffel 2015

Alvin Daniel Riffel. (2015). An Insight into a School's Readiness to Implement a CAPS Related Indigenous Knowledge Curriculum for Meteorological Sciences. Universal Journal of Educational Research 3(11): 906-916. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2015.031117

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-post control group study in one Cape Town public secondary school; E group (N = 15) received the DAIM intervention over eight weeks, C group (N = 10) received traditional teaching. The article reports "an overall 10.87 mean value improvement" for E versus 1.36 for C, with critical t-values of 2.131 and 2.201 at alpha = 0.05.

> "The mean results of the E group had significantly improved from an 18.88 mean to a 29.87 mean , with an overall 10.87 mean value improvement after the IKS and DAIM intervention."
