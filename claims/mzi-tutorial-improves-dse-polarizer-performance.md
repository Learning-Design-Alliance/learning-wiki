# Among advanced undergraduate and graduate students, correct-answer rates rose from the MZI pre-test to the isomorphic DSE post-test after the tutorial, except for Q2; the change was measured within the MZI→DSE cohort.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r1` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2020 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Undergraduate and graduate students in the MZI→DSE cohort answered the DSE polarizer questions correctly far more often after the MZI tutorial (65% and 66%) than they answered the isomorphic MZI polarizer questions before it (21% and 36%), and chi-square tests showed better performance in the DSE context for all questions except Q2. [→ Alexandru Maries 2020](#alexandru-maries-2020)

## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2020

Alexandru Maries, Ryan Sayer and Chandralekha Singh. (2020). Can students apply the concept of "which-path" information learned in the context of Mach Zehnder Interferometer to the double-slit experiment?. [doi:10.1119/10.0001357](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0001357)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same-student (MZI→DSE cohort) comparison of MZI pretest polarizer questions before WPI instruction versus isomorphic DSE polarizer questions after the tutorial, reported with chi-square p values in Fig. 6; the article reports "with the exception of Q2" both groups performed better in the DSE context.

> "The average performance of undergraduate and graduate students on all three MZI polarizer questions was 21% and 36%, respectively, while for the DSE polarizer questions it was 65% and 66%, respectively."
