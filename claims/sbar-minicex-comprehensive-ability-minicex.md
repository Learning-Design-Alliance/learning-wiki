## Evidence

### Xu Bu 2025

Xu Bu, Xing Xiaoli, Zhang Guangyun, Zhang Wenting, Zhang Min, Gan Yan, Wei Tao, Xia Xiaodan. (2025). Effect of the situation-background-assessment-recommendation model combined with the mini-CEX assessment on the clinical communication, clinical thinking and comprehensive clinical abilities of nursing interns. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08022-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Between-group mini-CEX comparison in the RCT of 405 interns, evaluated by a blinded nursing expert with >10 years of clinical teaching experience. Table 4 prints total 88.37 ± 8.57 vs. 78.95 ± 8.99, t = −10.803, p = 0.000. No effect sizes are printed.

> "inter-group comparisons after intervention showed that compared with the control group, the observation group had significant improvement in individual scores of the seven core competencies (nursing assessment, nursing physical examination, humanistic care, nursing problems, nursing operations, health education, organisational effectiveness and overall evaluation) and overall scores (p < 0.001)."
