# Cold calling and volunteering to answer questions cause higher student anxiety than clickers, group work, or worksheets

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Students rated anxiety highest for cold calling (3.68 ± 0.11) and volunteering (3.47 ± 0.08), which differed from clickers, group work, and worksheets (Cohen's f = 0.23). [→ England Benjamin J. 2017](#england-benjamin-j-2017)

## Evidence

### England Benjamin J. 2017

England Benjamin J., Brigati Jennifer R., Schussler Elisabeth E. (2017). Student anxiety in introductory biology classrooms: Perceptions about active learning and persistence in the major. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182506

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Survey (n = 327) ratings of anxiety for five classroom practices on a 1–5 scale; one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc found the model significant at p < 0.001 with printed effect size Cohen's f = 0.23, a medium effect.

> "cold calling (3.68 ± 0.11) was found to have the highest student ratings of anxiety, followed by volunteering to answer a posed question (3.47 ± 0.08), completing worksheets (2.93 ± 0.08), working in groups (2.92 ± 0.08), and using clickers (2.73 ± 0.09)."
