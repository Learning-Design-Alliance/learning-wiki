## Evidence

### Eliot 2019

Eliot, J. A. R., & Hirumi, A. (2019). Emotion theory in education research practice: an interdisciplinary critical literature review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-09642-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Starkey-Perret et al. (2017), a psychometric study of an AEQ variant administered to all pupils at an urban French middle school, N = 299, eliminating convenience sampling. The review reports that "15 of the 18 constructs" fell below the α = 0.80 cutoff. No effect size is printed.

> "found that 15 of the 18 constructs in the version of the AEQ used fell below Cronbach's α = 0.80, with the lowest scores clustering around emotions such as pride and shame"

### Eliot 2019 (2)

Eliot, J. A. R., & Hirumi, A. (2019). Emotion theory in education research practice: an interdisciplinary critical literature review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-09642-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Hong et al. (2017), a psychometric study of a teacher variant of the AEQ with N = 358 East Asian K-12 teachers, in which only anxiety among Japanese participants exceeded α = 0.80 and the study "demonstrated considerable cross-contamination between anger and frustration." No effect size is printed.

> "demonstrated considerable cross-contamination between anger and frustration (p. 98)"
