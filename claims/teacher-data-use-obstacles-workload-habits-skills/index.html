Saar 2022¶

Saar, M., Rodríguez-Triana, M. J., & Prieto, L. P. (2022). Towards Data-Informed Teaching Practice: A Model for Integrating Analytics with Teacher Inquiry. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3), 88–103. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7505

q2 · i? · design · r3

Open-ended interview question (Q10) in the think-aloud iteration with ten teachers, analyzed thematically. The article also states that "none of the participants regularly collect data about their teaching" despite admitting regular self-analysis should be routine.

"the main constraints were seen as high workload (n=4), habits/routine (n=3; "comfort, fear of anything new"), low-quality data and skills (n=2; "e-school analytics are not efficient," "I get stuck, need training"), and predisposition (n=3"
