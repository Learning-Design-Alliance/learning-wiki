# Surgeons trained on a 3D-printed biliary-enteric anastomosis model achieve higher GOALS performance scores on an animal-model transfer test than surgeons trained by video observation or general suture practice

## Evidence

### Shen Jiliang PhD 2024

Shen Jiliang PhD, Zhang Yaping PhD, Zhang Bin PhD, Lu Chen PhD, Cao Jiasheng PhD, Chen Mingyu MD, Zheng Bin MD, PhD, Yang Jin PhD. (2024). Simulation training of laparoscopic biliary-enteric anastomosis with a three-dimensional-printed model leads to better skill transfer: a randomized controlled trial. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000001079

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The GOALS score was used "to quantitatively evaluate the surgical performance" on the isolated swine organ model in the randomized controlled trial; the 3D group showed the best GOALS score, with partial eta squared 0.601 reported for GOALS score.

> "The average GOALS score of the 3D model group, simple-suture group and video group were 21.2±1.8, 17.8±1.6, and 15.6±2.7, respectively. There was a significant difference in GOALS scores among the three groups (P=0.004)"
