## Evidence

### Laurence Caroline O 2013

Laurence Caroline O, Zajac Ian T, Lorimer Michelle, Turnbull Deborah A, Sumner Karen E. (2013). The impact of preparatory activities on medical school selection outcomes: a cross-sectional survey of applicants to the university of Adelaide medical school in 2007. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-159

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression in the same 2007 applicant survey related the number of oral assessment preparatory activities to offer of a place. The article reports applicants "increased their odds of being offered a place by 1.36" per additional activity; mean activities were 2.4 for those not offered a place and 3.4 for those offered one.

> "For the oral assessment, for every one additional activity that applicants undertook, they increased their odds of being offered a place by 1.36 (95% CI 1.19 - 1.55; P < 0.001)."
