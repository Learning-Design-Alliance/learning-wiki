## Evidence

### Zhao Zhengqi 2026

Zhao Zhengqi, Wu Baijing, Tian Shuyuan, Hu Haigen, Hao Pengyi. (2026). Scaffolded AI-Supported Problem-Based Learning for Medical Interns: Exploratory Retrospectively Registered Randomized Controlled Evaluation With a Voluntary Feasibility Follow-Up. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/95039

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory randomized evaluation of 52 first-year postgraduate interns randomized 1:1 to MAPLE-CR or case-matched self-study. The intervention group "achieved higher posttest scores than the control group" with median difference 8.25 points and "Cliff δ=0.506 (95% CI 0.21-0.76)".

> "After the session, the intervention group achieved higher posttest scores than the control group (Mann-Whitney U test, P=.001), with a between-group median difference of 8.25 (95% CI 6.60‐11.60) points. The corresponding effect size was Cliff δ=0.506 (95% CI 0.21-0.76), indicating a distributional shift toward higher posttest scores in the intervention group."
