Discussion-section comparison with a prior 2016 survey (30 of 37 sites). The authors attribute the increase in practical year qualification possibly to preparation for a required quarter of general practice under new licensing regulations, and note current course durations are "much shorter than envisioned in the 2020 position paper".

> "At that time, 30 of 37 sites participated: 18 conducted BP qualification (obligatory at 14), 15 conducted qualification for the practical year (obligatory at 9). An increase is visible primarily in qualifications for the practical year, possibly in preparation for a required quarter of general practice that would be included under new medical licensing regulations."
