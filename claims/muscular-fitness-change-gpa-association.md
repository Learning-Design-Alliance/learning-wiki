## Evidence

### Syväoja 2019

Syväoja, H. J., Kankaanpää, A., Joensuu, L., Kallio, J., Hakonen, H., Hillman, C. H., & Tammelin, T. H. (2019). The Longitudinal Associations of Fitness and Motor Skills with Academic Achievement. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise. https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002031

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear growth curve modeling in the same 954-student cohort found the change in muscular fitness was positively associated with change in GPA (B = 0.36, 99% CI = 0.11–0.63), adjusted for confounders.

> "The changes in aerobic fitness and muscular fitness were positively associated with the changes in GPA (B = 0.27, 99% confidence interval [CI] = 0.06–0.48; B = 0.36, 99% CI = 0.11–0.63, respectively)."
