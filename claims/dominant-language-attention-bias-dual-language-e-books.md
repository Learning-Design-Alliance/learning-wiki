## Evidence

### Yow 2019

Yow, W. Q., & Priyashri, S. (2019). Computerized Electronic Features Direct Children's Attention to Print in Single- and Dual-Language e-Books. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419878126

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Study 1 within-subjects eye-tracking experiment with 32 English-Mandarin bilingual kindergartners reading silent dual-language e-books. Children "looked proportionately significantly longer at English sentences than Mandarin sentences during the baseline task" (mean PLT English 0.64 vs. Mandarin 0.36).

> "A 2 (Text Language: PLTE vs. PLTM) × 2 (Age-Group: Young vs. Old) repeated-measures ANOV A revealed a significant effect, F(1, 29) = 7.97, p = .009, partial η2 = .222. Children looked proportionately significantly longer at English sentences than Mandarin sentences during the baseline task"
