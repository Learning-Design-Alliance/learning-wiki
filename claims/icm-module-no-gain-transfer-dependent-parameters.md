## Evidence

### Crome Marius 2021

Crome Marius, Adam Knut, Flohr Marco, Rahman Alexander, Staufenbiel Ingmar. (2021). Application of the inverted classroom model in the teaching module “new classification of periodontal and peri-implant diseases and conditions” during the COVID-19 pandemic. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2021-38/zma001485.shtml

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Detailed error-structure analysis reported in the Discussion of the before-after study. The authors add that the error rate for identifying the interdental space with the largest radiographic bone loss "remained unchanged" and suggest three-dimensional radiograph interpretation "is acquired procedurally, as professional experience increases".

> "Detailed analysis of the error structure showed that especially for the parameters requiring transfer of knowledge and clinical experience (complexity, tooth loss) no improvement had been achieved."
