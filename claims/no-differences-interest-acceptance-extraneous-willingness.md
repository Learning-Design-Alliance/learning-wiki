## Evidence

### Reiser 2026

Reiser, S., Schick, K., Pittroff, S. I. D., Janssen, L., Berberat, P. O., Gartmeier, M., & Bauer, J. (2026). Integrating formative and summative feedback in online situational judgement tests: effects of feedback design on medical students’ motivational and cognitive learning factors. Medical Education Online, 31(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2026.2639198

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null findings from the RCT's planned contrasts and follow-up ANOVAs (N = 269). The results report no significant differences in interest among the four task-based groups; the text likewise reports no significant differences in acceptance, extraneous cognitive load, or willingness to improve. No effect sizes are printed for these null findings.

> "For interest, as part of basic motivational needs, there were no significant differences between the four feedback groups (IR, IE, PR and PE)."
