Luke McCrone & Martyn Kingsbury. (2024). An ecological approach to understanding transitions and tensions in complex learning contexts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00267-1

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Field interviews showed later-year undergraduates changed their use of departmental spaces over their degree, and a PhD student described the space as "Not a place for work, because the undergraduates are here" outside summer. Early-year undergraduates developed cohort familiarity through habitual ingress and egress via the breakout space.

> "Similarly, postgraduate students confessed to a greater sense of ownership during the summer months when undergraduate students no longer had timetabled teaching."
