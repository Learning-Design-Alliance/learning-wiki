`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review, citing Dunlosky et al., lists techniques educators should avoid because they "have not been shown to enhance learning"; no effect sizes are printed for these null findings.

> "Educators should avoid using or encouraging the use of learner-initiated summarization, highlighting and underlining, mnemonics, imagery, and rereading as these techniques have not been shown to enhance learning."
