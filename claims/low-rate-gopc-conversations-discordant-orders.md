Background synthesis in the review's introduction, attributed to Gieniusz et al. (2018) and Mack et al. (2010). The review reports the 14.5–40% range and notes conversations often occur late in illness and neglect psychosocial, emotional, and cultural needs.

### Brown 2025 (2)

Brown, Janie; Hu-Collins, Phoebe. (2025). Communication training for effective Goals of Patient Care conversations in acute care: An integrative review of the literature. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951525000264

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Background synthesis in the review's introduction, attributed to Heyland et al. (2013). The review reports that "nearly 70% of documented orders were discordant with patients' expressed preferences for end-of-life" and links poor documentation to risk of non-goal-concordant care.

> "Heyland et al. (2013) found that nearly 70% of documented orders were discordant with patients' expressed preferences for end-of-life, despite documentation occurring shortly after conversations."
