Inclusion analysis (Figure 4, Table A) of CPS spring enrollment in grades 3-8 plus non-graded special education students, 1992-2000. Of those not included in 2000, 19.1 percent were tested but excluded and 6.7 percent were not tested at all.

"In spring 2000, the test scores of 74.2 percent of the total student enrollment in grades three through eight were included in CPS reporting."

John Q. Easton 2001 (2)¶

John Q. Easton, Todd Rosenkranz, Anthony S. Bryk. (2001). Annual CPS Test Trend Review, 2000: Elementary School Test Score Trends in Chicago Public Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/annual-cps-trend-review-2000

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The brief interprets this as possibly indicating a leveling off in the percent of students included in public reporting, an exception to the overall trend toward lower inclusion rates since 1992.

"The 2000 inclusion rate of 74.2 percent is slightly higher than the 1999 rate (73.9 percent)."
