## Evidence

### Zandieh 2012

Zandieh, Z., & Jafarigohar, M. (2012). The Effects of Hypertext Gloss on Comprehension and Vocabulary Retention under Incidental and Intentional Learning Conditions. English Language Teaching, 5(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n6p60

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison-group posttest-only study of 184 intermediate EFL students; immediate retention test results reported in Results. The intentional group mean was 25.89 vs 22.87 for the incidental group, a difference the article reports as statistically significant at p < .05.

> "On the immediate vocabulary retention test, the intentional group scored better than the incidental group with a mean of 25.89 points comparing to the incidental group’s mean of 22.87."
