The same Arkansas quasi-experimental study of over 2,500 students in 12 PLC at Work schools versus matched comparison schools found the program "did not significantly affect ELA scores". No effect size is printed for ELA; equivalence was not tested.

"PLC at Work had a statistically significant positive effect on the state-administered ACT Aspire math achievement test scores (effect size = +0.08), though it did not significantly affect ELA scores."
