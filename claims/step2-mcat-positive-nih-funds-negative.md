## Evidence

### Ghaffari-Rafi Arash 2019

Ghaffari-Rafi Arash, Lee Rachel Elizabeth, Fang Rui, Miles J. Douglas. (2019). Multivariable analysis of factors associated with USMLE scores across U.S. medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1605-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression (Table 4) on the 100-school dataset. The article reports the nine-variable model explained 46.9% of variance (R2 = 0.469, P < .0001), with MCAT positively and NIH research grant funds per faculty member negatively associated with Step 2.

> "Change of the following variables significantly predicts higher Step 2 scores: higher median MCAT total score (β = 1.11, P = 0.012) and lower NIH research grant funds per faculty member (β = − 0.02, P = 0.039)."
