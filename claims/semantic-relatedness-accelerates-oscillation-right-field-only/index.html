Evidence¶

Noguchi 2024¶

Noguchi, Y. (2024). Harmonic memory signals in the human cerebral cortex induced by semantic relatedness of words. npj Science of Learning, 9:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00221-1

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Experiment 1 EEG mean-IPI analysis during D5: significant Sem < NonSem differences over frontal and temporal regions, but "the change in mean IPI was limited to when participants memorized items in right visualﬁeld". Effect held in one condition only, so evidence is mixed.

"The t-map of mean IPI, a measure for oscillation speed, showed signi ﬁcant differences (Sem < NonSem) over frontal and temporal regions (orange rectangles in Fig.4b). Although these data suggested an acceleration of brain rhythm related to a semantic inte- gration, the change in mean IPI was limited to when participants memor- ized items in right visualﬁeld (SemR vs. NonSemR, right panel)."

Noguchi 2024 (2)¶

Noguchi, Y. (2024). Harmonic memory signals in the human cerebral cortex induced by semantic relatedness of words. npj Science of Learning, 9:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00221-1

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

PSD/FOOOF validation analysis of Experiment 1 data: central frequency "increase... over the left posterior temporal cortex" for SemR > NonSemR at T5, t(33) = 2.35, p = 0.025, d = 0.08, consistent with the mean-IPI result.

"An increase in central frequency was observed over the left posterior temporal cortex when participants retained MWs in the right visual ﬁeld (SemR > NonSemR, T5 in the right panel of Fig.5b: t(33) = 2.35,p = 0.025,d =0 . 0 8 )."

Related Claims¶

  • Semantically associated words induce similar distributions of inter-peak intervals: semantic structure is represented in IPIs, not in oscillation amplitude, and not explained by visual word similarity — related
  • Semantic integration of memorized words induces false memory: false-alarm rates are higher to semantically related lure probes than to unseen new probes — related
  • Semantic integration of memory words inhibits the time-related increase in oscillatory irregularity (IPI-SD) from early to late retention periods — related
  • Memorizing semantically related words increases the regularity of alpha-to-beta EEG oscillations (reduced IPI-SD) over posterior temporal cortex contralateral to the memory field — related
  • Semantic relatedness among memorized words improves verbal working memory recognition (higher d') only when words are memorized in the left visual field — related
