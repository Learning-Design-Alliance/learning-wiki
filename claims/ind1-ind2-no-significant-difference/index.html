The effects of teamwork on critical thinking: A serial mediation analysis of the influence of work skills and educational motivation in secondary school students. Thinking Skills and Creativity. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2022.101063

First post hoc contrast (C1: Ind1 minus Ind2) from the same bootstrap analysis (n=1159). Because "the value “0” is included within the confidence interval", the article concludes there is no statistically significant difference between the two mediational routes; equivalence was not tested.

"Given that the value “0” is included within the confidence interval, it can be concluded that no statistically significant differences exist between indirect effect 1 [Twork→WSk→CThink] and indirect effect 2 [Twork→EdMot→CThink]"
