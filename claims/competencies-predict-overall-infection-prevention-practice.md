## Evidence

### Oh Won-Oak 2026

Oh Won-Oak, Heo Yoo-Jin, Jung Myung Jin, Han Jihee, Lee Eunji. (2026). Competencies influencing childcare providers' infectious-disease prevention in routine and outbreak contexts: a study in South Korea. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1707317

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Stepwise multiple regression on online survey data from 239 childcare providers in South Korea. The model for total infectious-disease prevention practice retained educational guidance and research and empowerment competencies and demand for education, with "Overall explanatory power was 52.5%" and educational guidance the strongest predictor.

> "Overall explanatory power was 52.5%, with the most influential variable being competency in educational guidance (β = 0.32, p < 0.001)."
