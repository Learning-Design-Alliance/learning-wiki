Chen W.-L. 2026¶

A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effect of gamified clinical skill learning on learning outcomes in undergraduate nursing education. The Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000772

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Sensitivity analyses accompanying the review's meta-analyses: exclusion of the two high-risk studies, leave-one-out analysis, and fixed-effect versus random-effects comparison. The review reports pooled effect sizes "remained materially unchanged", and no single study dominated the outcomes (knowledge I2=82%, skills I2=95%). No separate effect size is printed for these analyses.

"When excluding the two studies identified with a high risk of bias (Aljezawi & Albashtawy, 2015; Al-Mugheed et al., 2022), the pooled effect sizes for knowledge, skills, and attitudes remained materially unchanged, further supporting the robustness of the results."
