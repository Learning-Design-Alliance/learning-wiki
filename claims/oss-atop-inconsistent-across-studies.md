The review's synthesis of questionnaire outcomes across included studies reports divergent ATOP results: "One study reported a significant improvement in ATOP scores from pre- to post-intervention" while a second reported no significant change; a third found significant improvement in the experimental group versus controls.

> "The Attitudes Toward Obese Persons (ATOP) scale was used in three studies [25, 30, 32]. One study reported a significant improvement in ATOP scores from pre- to post-intervention [30]. A second reported no significant change following the intervention [32]."
