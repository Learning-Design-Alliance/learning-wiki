## Subclaims
`q3 i?` There was no significant difference in overall scores on the 10 FCI problems between traditional (M = 6.85, SD = 2.50) and M&I (M = 5.57, SD = 2.03) students. [→ Keith R Bujak 2010](#keith-r-bujak-2010)

## Evidence

### Keith R Bujak 2010

Keith R Bujak, Richard Catrambone, Marcos D. Caballero, M Jack Marr, Michael F Schatz, Matthew A Kohlmyer. (2010). Comparing the Matter and Interactions Curriculum with a Traditional Physics Curriculum: A Think Aloud Study. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1011.5449](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1011.5449)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud experiment with 34 Georgia Tech undergraduates (20 traditional, 14 M&I) solving 10 FCI problems. An independent samples t-test found "no significant difference in the overall scores" between groups; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no significant difference in the overall scores on the 10 problems solved by traditional (M = 6.85, SD = 2.50) and M&I (M = 5.57, SD = 2.03) students, t(32) = -1.58, p = .12."
