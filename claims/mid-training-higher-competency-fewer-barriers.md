### de Moura Regina Cardoso; Perazzo Matheus França; Leal Soraya Coelho; Maia Lucianne Cople; Massignan Carla 2026

de Moura Regina Cardoso; Perazzo Matheus França; Leal Soraya Coelho; Maia Lucianne Cople; Massignan Carla. (2026). Dentists' knowledge, skills, attitudes and barriers towards minimal intervention dentistry. Brazilian Oral Research. https://doi.org/10.1590/1807-3107bor-2026.vol40.041

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression from the cross-sectional survey; the abstract reports higher knowledge (β = 2.95), skills/attitudes (β = 3.40) and fewer barriers (β = −1.52) among trained dentists.

> "Dentists who had received formal training in MID achieved higher scores in knowledge (β = 2.95; 95%CI: 2.31 to 3.59; p < 0.001) and in skills and attitudes (β = 3.40; 95%CI: 2.18 to 4.62; p < 0.001), as well as fewer perceived barriers (β = −1.52; 95%CI: −2.35 to −0.68; p < 0.001)"
