### Freytag Julia 2019

Freytag Julia, Stroben Fabian, Hautz Wolf E., Schauber Stefan K., Kämmer Juliane E. (2019). Rating the quality of teamwork—a comparison of novice and expert ratings using the Team Emergency Assessment Measure (TEAM) in simulated emergencies. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-019-0591-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mixed effects model of TEAM GRS scores from the 84-observation simulation study, estimating variance components for raters, rater status, teams, cases and first-order interactions; the model accounted for 71.8% of observed variance.

> "We found that rater status (expert vs. novice) accounted for 11.1% of the variance of scores while the cases explained 10.2% of the variance. Teams accounted for 2.6% percent of variation in the observed scores while the biggest source of variance was the interaction of cases and teams with 43.2%"
