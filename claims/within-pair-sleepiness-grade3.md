## Evidence

### Grasby KL 2026

Grasby KL, Larsen SA, Gregory AM, Blunden S, Lewis KS, Madrid-Valero JJ, Coventry WL, Byrne B, Olson RK. (2026). Associations between sleep habits, performance in reading and mathematics, and inattention and hyperactivity. PLoS One 21(5): e0347892. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347892

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-pair BW effects in Grade 3, which control for family-level confounds, showed less daytime sleepiness predicted higher reading and mathematics scores; the same within-pair effect was associated with all four outcomes in Grade 3.

> "The within-pair effect for daytime sleepiness was significant, such that less tiredness during the day was associated with higher reading and mathematics scores in Grade 3."
