## Evidence

### Johnels Jakob Åsberg 2025

Johnels Jakob Åsberg, Kuja‐Halkola Ralf, Larsson Henrik, Chang Zheng, Brikell Isabel, Lundström Sebastian. (2025). Does the family situation impact academic achievement differently in students with versus without neurodevelopmental disorders? The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70050

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive group comparisons in the 12,029-twin cohort (Table 1). The article reports "closeness was lower in both ADHD (p < .001) and autism families (p < .001) whereas the level of conflict was higher", and parents of children with ADHD or autism were overrepresented in the lowest education group (20% vs. 14%).

> "Also, there were significant differences in ratings of parent–child relations, such that, compared with the non‐ADHD/non‐autism group, closeness was lower in both ADHD (p < .001) and autism families (p < .001) whereas the level of conflict was higher (p < .001 for both ADHD and autism)."
