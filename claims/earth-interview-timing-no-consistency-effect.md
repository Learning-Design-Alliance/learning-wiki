# Completing the EARTH-2 before versus after an interview made no significant difference to children's consistency with mental models

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Children who were interviewed before the EARTH-2 showed no greater consistency with mental models than children who took the EARTH-2 first, contrary to the on-the-spot model-formation hypothesis. [→ Straatemeier 2008](#straatemeier-2008)

## Evidence

### Straatemeier 2008

Straatemeier, M., van der Maas, H. L. J., & Jansen, B. R. J. (2008). Children's knowledge of the earth: A new methodological and statistical approach. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2008.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Main experiment ANCOVA comparing the interview-before condition (n = 25) and interview-after condition (n = 40) against children who completed the EARTH-2 first. The null result tests Vosniadou et al.'s claim that open-ended questioning encourages on-the-spot mental model formation; equivalence was not tested.

> "However, the ANCOVA showed no signiﬁcant differences between the experimental conditions, F(2, 369) = 1.42, p = .24."
