## Evidence

### Roszipal Benjamin 2025

Roszipal Benjamin, Szelesi Gabriella, Ernst Martin, Hoffelner Alexander, Wagner Michael. (2025). Competency development in early nursing training: a cross-sectional OSCE study of self-assessment versus examiner ratings. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08279-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Planned paired-samples t-test within the same OSCE cohort (valid cases 103–104 by domain) comparing self-assessment to examiner ratings immediately post-exam. The social-domain mean difference was −0.141 with "t(103)=–10.14, p <.001" and d = 0.99; self-rated mean was 0.8275 vs examiner 0.6852 (proportions).

> "In contrast, self-ratings were substantially higher than examiner ratings in the social/communication domain, t(103)=–10.14, p <.001, 95% CI [–0.168, − 0.113], d = 0.99."
