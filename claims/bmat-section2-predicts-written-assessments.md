## Subclaims
`q3 i2` At LKCMedicine, BMAT Section 2 scores were significantly associated with higher written assessment performance, with moderate associations in Years 1 and 2 (standardised coefficients β = 0.36 and β = 0.31). [→ Davies DJ 2022](#davies-dj-2022)
`q3 i1` At ICSM, BMAT Section 2 scores showed small associations with written assessment performance in Years 1, 2, 3 and 6 (standardised coefficients β = 0.15–0.19). [→ Davies DJ 2022 (2)](#davies-dj-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Davies DJ 2022

Davies DJ, Sam AH, Murphy KG, Khan SA, Choe R, Cleland J. (2022). BMAT's predictive validity for medical school performance: A retrospective cohort study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14819

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Univariable robust linear regressions in the LKCMedicine retrospective cohort (222 students, 2013-2015 entrants) reported in Results Section 3.1.2. Section 2 showed "moderate associations between BMAT Section 2 scores and written assessment performance" in Years 1 and 2, with standardised coefficients β = 0.36 and β = 0.31, and smaller coefficients (0.22–0.23) in later years.

> "At LKCMedicine, there were moderate associations between BMAT Section 2 scores and written assessment performance in Years 1 and 2 (coefficients = 0.36, 95% CI 0.24–0.48; 0.31, 95% CI 0.18–0.45, respectively)"

### Davies DJ 2022 (2)

Davies DJ, Sam AH, Murphy KG, Khan SA, Choe R, Cleland J. (2022). BMAT's predictive validity for medical school performance: A retrospective cohort study. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14819

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Univariable robust linear regressions in the ICSM retrospective cohort (1188 students, 2010-2013 entrants) reported in Results Section 3.1.2. BMAT Section 2 showed "small associations with written assessment performance in Years 1, 2, 3 and 6" with standardised coefficients β = 0.15–0.19 and an association below 0.1 in Year 5.

> "At ICSM, there were small associations with written assessment performance in Years 1, 2, 3 and 6 (coefficients ranging from 0.15–0.19)"
