Subgroup analyses by students' grade (Figure 3C) and subject (Figure 3D) in the meta-analysis. The article reports significant grade-level effects for 3rd- and 4th-year undergraduates and doctors, a non-significant effect for 5th-year students, and "no difference" in orthodontics and oral and maxillofacial surgery.

"However, there was no difference between emerging teaching methods and LBL in the study of orthodontics (SMD = 0.40, 95% CI = −0.03 to 0.82, P = .105) and oral and maxillofacial surgery (SMD = −0.09, 95% CI = −0.44 to 0.26, P = .852)"
