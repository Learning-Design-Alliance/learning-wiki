Evidence¶

Woo 2009¶

Woo, H.L. (2009). Designing multimedia learning environments using animated pedagogical agents: factors and issues. Journal of Computer Assisted Learning, 25, 203-218. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2729.2008.00299.x

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of studies (e.g. Lester et al. 1997; Atkinson 2002; Baylor & Ryu 2003) reporting that lifelike agent behaviour produced a positive motivational effect, described as the persona effect. The review reports cognitive-domain results as mixed and inconclusive; no effect sizes are printed.

"A good number of the studies that attempted to establish a relationship between APA learning and motivation found a positive effect and attributed it to the persona effect brought about by the lifelike behaviour of the agents"
