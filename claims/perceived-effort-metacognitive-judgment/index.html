q2 · i? · review · r2

Conceptual review claim, attributed to the recent review by Scheiter et al. (2020): self-reports of effort on Likert-type scales "should not be viewed as objective reflections of effort based solely on the difficulty of a task" but are inferred from cues of varying validity.

"ratings of experienced effort are in fact a type of metacognitive judgment that result from heuristic processing and are therefore prone to biases"
