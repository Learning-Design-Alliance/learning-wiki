## Evidence

### Li Yawen 2026

Li Yawen, Yan Chuchu, Ye Han, Liu Mengnan, Xiang Leyuan, Lu Haiying, Shan Yawei. (2026). Optimizing cognitive load in digital nursing education: the role of pre-academic performance and material modalities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09230-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis (N=55) shows strong negative correlations between subjective cognitive load and achievement in all three modules, e.g. "r = -0.74, P < 0.001". Authors describe this as descriptive rather than causal.

> "subjective cognitive load of students and achievement scores were negatively correlated (r = -0.63, P < 0.001; r = -0.58, P < 0.001; r = -0.74, P < 0.001)"
