`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Self-reported transfer to practice, a secondary outcome of the randomized trial, assessed at the final questionnaire. The odds ratio of 2.9 (95% CI 1.2-7.4) favors Hygie; the authors suggest serious games "may engender better transfer of knowledge to real-life situations by actively engaging the learner".
