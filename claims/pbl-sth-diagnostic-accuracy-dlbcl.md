## Evidence

### Das Reena 2025

Das Reena, Ramani Pratibha, Sukumaran Gheena, Ramasubramanian Abilasha, Veeraiyan Deepak Nallasamy. (2025). Evaluating the effectiveness of problem-based learning combined with six thinking hats in oral pathology education. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-06554-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative evaluation of 15 Oral Pathology residents taught a round cell neoplasm case via traditional slide teaching and a DLBCL case via PBL-STH. The article reports "diagnostic accuracy significantly improved" with 86.6% identifying DLBCL versus 40% under traditional teaching; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "only 40% of those taught using traditional slide-based methods could accurately categorize the neoplasm based on the provided clinical and immunohistochemical data. In contrast, when the PBL-STH method was employed, diagnostic accuracy significantly improved. A total of 86.6% of the residents successfully identified the neoplasm as DLBCL"
