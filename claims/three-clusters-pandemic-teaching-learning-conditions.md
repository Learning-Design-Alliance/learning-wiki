# Pandemic-era teaching and learning conditions across 47 countries fall into three clusters: Family-Supported Asynchronous Virtual Instruction, Accentuated Teacher-Led Synchronous Instruction, and Accentuated Instructional Disruption

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` K-means clustering with silhouette validation on PISA 2022 Global Crises Module country-level data identified three subgroups of countries with similar teaching and learning conditions for sustaining learning. [→ Giang Pham 2026](#giang-pham-2026)

## Evidence

### Giang Pham 2026

Giang Pham, Franziska Maria Locher, Dimitra Kolovou, Eliane Arnold. (2026). COVID-19: A cross-national analysis with PISA data. Learning and Instruction 105 (2026) 102366. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102366

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

K-means cluster analysis of country-level PISA 2022 Global Crises Module data (N = 47 countries), validated with the average silhouette approach; "a three-cluster solution was selected, and each country was assigned to one cluster". Clusters were labelled from their largest item differences.

> "Based on the average silhouette analysis and visual inspection of the clustering results, a three-cluster solution was selected, and each country was assigned to one cluster"
