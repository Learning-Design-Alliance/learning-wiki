Descriptive analysis of DPRP administrative enrollment data for all participants enrolling 2012–2019. The article reports that "455,954 participants enrolled in the National DPP lifestyle change program," with online mode enrolling the most (269,004) and distance learning the fewest (4,786). No inferential test was reported.

> "Between January 2012 and December 2019, 455,954 participants enrolled in the National DPP lifestyle change program. Enrollment by delivery mode was 166,691 for in person; 269,004 for online; 4,786 for distance learning; and 15,473 for combination."
