## Evidence

### Tulika Bose 2022

Tulika Bose, Sudhir Malik, Meenakshi Narain. (2022). U.S. CMS - PURSUE (Program for Undergraduate Research SUmmer Experience). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.10109](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.10109)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive finding from the three-week feedback survey of the 2022 virtual PURSUE cohort. Intern comments requested more interaction outside talks and training; the authors conclude an in-person component is essential to build community. No quantitative effect size is printed.

> "It is absolutely essential to have a strong in-person component of the program to build a sense of community. One intern remarked I would like to interact more with the other mentees outside of talks/training and a second said more social opportunities between interns"
