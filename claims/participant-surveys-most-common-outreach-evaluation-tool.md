### Garrett Richards and Svetlana Barkanova 2025

Garrett Richards and Svetlana Barkanova. (2025). How to Do STEM Outreach Evaluation – Recommendations Based on a Review of Self-Evaluation Tools in Canadian STEM Outreach Programs. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2512.20713](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.20713)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative categorization of the 68 shared self-evaluation tools, with commonness counts reported in Table 2: participant survey "35/68 (51.5%)", teacher or chaperone survey "23/68 (33.8%)", external metrics "13/68 (19.1%)", longitudinal tools "2/68 (2.9%)", interview "1/68 (1.5%)". Percentages sum above 100% because programs used multiple tools.

> "The most common tools are participant surveys, chaperone surveys, and external metrics (e.g. simply counting the number of participants). Less common tools are facilitator observations, discussions, testimonials, host organization feedback, pre-surveys, written submissions, longitudinal tools, quizzes, and interviews."
