### Stone 1913

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument about research design in a 1913 methodological paper; no data are reported. The article lists the chief factors that determine arithmetical abilities as capability of learners, efficiency of teachers, methods of teaching, supervision, time allotments, and the course of study, and notes that rarely if ever are all factors except the one being tested found operating equally in different systems of schools.

> "Hence the need of testing the pupils in a sufficient number of systems so that differences in the other factors will equalize each other."
