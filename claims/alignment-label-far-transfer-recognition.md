## Evidence

### Zheng 2025

Zheng, Y. S., Goldwater, M., & Gentner, D. (2025). Structural Alignment and Linguistic Contrast Help Children Learn a Key Principle of Spatial Construction. Cognitive Science, 49(12), e70149. https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.70149

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 Day 2 far-transfer recognition task choosing among horizontally, vertically, and diagonally lined bunkbed drawings; logistic regression showed significant alignment (odds ratio = 1.62, p = .019) and language (odds ratio = 1.75, p = .007) effects.

> "The HA group was more likely to choose the braced bed as the strongest one than the LA group ( M HA = 0.72, SD HA = 0.45, M LA = 0.52, SD LA = 0.50)."
