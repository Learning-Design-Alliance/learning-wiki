### Singh‐Weldon J. 2022

Singh‐Weldon J., Tsianakas V., Murrells T., Grealish A. (2022). Preparing children's nurses for working with children and adolescents who self‐harm: Evaluating the ‘our care through our eyes’ e‐learning training package. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/inm.13041

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Single-group pre–post evaluation with 42 recruited children's nurses (SHAS pre n=39, post n=22); the article reports summary statistics only because the sample was underpowered for statistical testing. The quote shows the "SHAS overall mean total reduced following training from 68.1 to 62.8".

> "SHAS overall mean total reduced following training from 68.1 to 62.8, and a reduction was observed for most of the SHAS sub‐scales although these were small."
