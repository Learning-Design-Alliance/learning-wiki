Descriptive outcome distribution reported in the conclusion section for the 144 participants; the article notes the game "wasn't aimed to be particularly difficult" yet most players did not reach the promotion ending.

> "from the 144 students that participated, only 37 (25.7%) managed to reach the end where Rose gets promoted. At least, only 8 students (5.6%) reached the end where she gets fired. The rest of the 99 students (68.8%) reached the end where Rose remained at her position"
