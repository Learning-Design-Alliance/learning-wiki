## Evidence

### Mughal Z 2026

Mughal Z, Gupta KK, di Traglia R, Fruhstorfer B. (2026). Effectiveness of E‐Learning in Undergraduate ENT Education: A Mixed‐Methods Systematic Review. The Laryngoscope. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.70164

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta‐analysis of proportions using normalized gain across 13 studies of e‐learning in undergraduate ENT education, after leave‐one‐out exclusion of an influential outlier. The review reports "the pooled normalized gain to 26.8% (23.7%–30.0%)" with residual heterogeneity eliminated (I² = 0%).

> "Excluding this study increased the pooled normalized gain to 26.8% (23.7%–30.0%), and reduced the heterogeneity (I 2 = 0%, 95% C.I. 0%–68.4%; Q = 10.6, p = 0.6; τ 2 = 0, 95% C.I. 0–0.01)."
