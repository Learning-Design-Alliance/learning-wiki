## Evidence

### Çevikbaş Pınar 2026

Çevikbaş Pınar, Açıksarı Kurtuluş, Karahaliloğlu Cihad Ünsal. (2026). PHTLS training increases knowledge but paradoxically decreases self-efficacy in emergency healthcare professionals: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09149-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional single-center study using a single dichotomous yes/no self-perceived competence item. The trained group had "significantly lower odds of reporting self-perceived competence" (OR = 0.20, 95% CI: 0.086–0.487; phi = −0.373).

> "Only 25% (n = 12) of the PHTLS-trained participants reported feeling competent in managing trauma patients, compared to 62% (n = 31) of the non-trained participants. This paradoxical finding was statistically significant (p < 0.001)."
