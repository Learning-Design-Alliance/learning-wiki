## Subclaims
`q1 i?` The article concludes that generalizations made on the basis of the modal value have no sound statistical value, because modes shift with arbitrary choices of interval size and position. [→ Frasier 1915](#frasier-1915)

## Evidence

### Frasier 1915

Frasier, George W. (1915). An Inquiry Into the Statistical Basis of a Conclusion Concerning Sex Differences. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Methodological argument from the regrouping demonstrations: the mode-based sex-difference conclusions lose half their significance when intervals are repositioned and lose all statistical basis under an interval of eight.

> "Hence, it seems fair to conclude that generalizations made on the basis of modal value have no sound statistical value."
