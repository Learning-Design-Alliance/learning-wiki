## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Halasa S  2020

Halasa S , Abusalim N , Rayyan M , et al. (2020). Comparing student achievement in traditional learning with a combination of blended and flipped learning. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.492

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Equivalence check at semester start using cumulative GPAs from the university intranet for the same 125 students (experimental mean 2.77, SD 0.566; control mean 2.60, SD 0.535; t = 1.684, df = 123, Sig. .095). The article concludes "the two groups were equivalent in terms of previous academic achievement levels".

> "Table 3 shows that the t‐value of the cumulative averages (entering overall GPAs) of students in the experimental and control groups was 1.684, which is not statistically significant."
