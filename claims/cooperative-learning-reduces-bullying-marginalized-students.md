## Subclaims
`q3 i2` In intervention schools, bullying at follow-up was significantly lower among low-engagement students, with a significant intervention-by-engagement interaction (effect size .37). [→ Van Ryzin 2018](#van-ryzin-2018)

## Evidence

### Van Ryzin 2018

Van Ryzin, M. J., & Roseth, C. J. (2018). Cooperative Learning in Middle School: A Means to Improve Peer Relations and Reduce Victimization, Bullying, and Related Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(8), 1192-1201. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000265

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized trial of 15 rural middle schools (N = 1,460 7th graders) using linear mixed models with intervention-by-baseline-engagement interactions. Table 3 reports the bullying interaction B = .09 (SE .04), p < .05, effect size .37, described as "moderate to large."
