## Evidence

### The Critical 2007

The Critical, Relational Practice of Instructional Design in Higher Education: An Emerging Model of Change Agency. (2007). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-007-9061-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of designer narratives; the article reports that when designers conflict with institutional values and feel powerless, they face an ethical dilemma about staying, and that "institutional change agency is a tug-of-war between values and cultures" moderated by power relationships.

> "This agency includes a felt responsibility to deal with the tacit and explicit values, and advance the interests, of host institutions."
