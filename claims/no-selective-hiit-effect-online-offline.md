# HIIT did not selectively influence online or offline learning subcomponents relative to active control

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Independent-samples comparisons found no group differences for online or offline periods (online d = 0.28, p = .33; offline d = 0.08, p = .78). [→ Brooks 2026](#brooks-2026)

## Evidence

### Brooks 2026

Brooks, E., Hawi, Z., Hosler, J. et al. (2026). High-intensity cardiovascular exercise facilitates online motor skill learning, with no effect of BDNF genotype. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00432-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Independent-samples t-tests comparing HIIT and active control groups on online and offline learning subcomponents in the 51-participant experiment; "no significant differences between groups" on either subcomponent, so equivalence was not tested.

> "There were no significant differences between groups for either the online periods (HIIT: M = 4.03, SD = 5.14; active control: M = 2.67, SD = 4.66), t(49) = -0.99, p = .33, d = 0.28, or the offline periods (HIIT: M = -0.96, SD = 4.52; active control: M = -0.59, SD = 4.69), t(49) = 0.28, p = .78, d = 0.08"
