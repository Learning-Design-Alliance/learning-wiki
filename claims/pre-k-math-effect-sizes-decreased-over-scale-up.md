`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Cross-study comparison of causal estimates from the present state-level cluster RCT against causal estimates from earlier, smaller pilot, efficacy, and effectiveness studies of Pre-K Mathematics. The authors report that "effect sizes have decreased over time," a pattern they describe as consistent with their framework of scale-up dimensions.

> "Using this framework, we compare the causal estimates from the present study to those from earlier, smaller studies. Consistent with our framework, we find that effect sizes have decreased over time."
