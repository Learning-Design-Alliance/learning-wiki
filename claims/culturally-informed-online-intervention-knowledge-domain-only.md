## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (Kula et al. 2021, Israel) of a culturally informed online program in two 60-minute sessions, with a final matched analytic sample of 72 undergraduate nursing students. The review reports "F(1, 69) = 3.05, p ≈ 0.02" for the knowledge domain; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The intervention produced a significant improvement in the knowledge domain of cultural competence compared with the control group at post‐intervention (between‐group difference in post‐intervention knowledge controlling for baseline, F(1, 69) = 3.05, p ≈ 0.02). The effect on the skills domain approached significance (F(1, 68) = 3.33, p = 0.07). No significant group differences emerged for attitudes (F<1) or encounters (F(1, 69) = 1.77, p = 0.19)."
