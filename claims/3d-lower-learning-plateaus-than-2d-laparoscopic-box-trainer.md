## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For both tasks, all learning plateaus (time and error) were lower under 3D vision than under 2D vision. [→ Kunert W 2020](#kunert-w-2020)

## Evidence

### Kunert W 2020

Kunert W, Storz P, Dietz N, Axt S, Falch C, Kirschniak A, Wilhelm P. (2020). Learning curves, potential and speed in training of laparoscopic skills: a randomised comparative study in a box trainer. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-07768-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised comparative study of 40 laparoscopy novices assigned to 2D or 3D vision in a standardised box trainer; exponential decay regression fitted to median performance times and error numbers across repetitions. The article reports "all learning plateaus were lower (better) under 3D vision" with the printed plateau values for both tasks.

> "For both tasks all learning plateaus were lower (better) under 3D vision (Task 1: ttot = 28.25 s, etot = 1.34; Task 2: ttot = 78.34 s, etot = 0.39) than under 2D vision (Task 1: ttot = 40.72 s, etot = 1.66; Task 2: ttot = 123.47 s, etot = 1.15)."
