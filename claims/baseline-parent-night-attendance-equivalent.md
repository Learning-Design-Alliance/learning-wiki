## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Before the intervention, 37.7% of intervention parents versus 31.3% of control parents had attended a parent night, a non-significant difference (chi-square = 0.76, p = 0.38). [→ Bennett-Conroy 2012](#bennett-conroy-2012)

## Evidence

### Bennett-Conroy 2012

Waveline Bennett-Conroy. (2012). Engaging Parents of Eighth Grade Students in Parent–Teacher Bidirectional Communication. School Community Journal, 22(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1001614

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre-intervention check using September–October sign-in sheets at three parent events. The groups "did not differ on the independently measured parent involvement variable"; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "The difference in proportions in parent night attendance early in the school year for intervention class parents and control group parents (37.7% vs. 31.3%) was not significant (Mantel-Haenszel chi-square result = 0.76 (df = 1), p = 0.38)."
