## Evidence

### Michael J. Parker 2024

Michael J. Parker, Caitlin Anderson, Claire Stone, YeaRim Oh. (2024). A Large Language Model Approach to Educational Survey Feedback Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00414-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inter-rater agreement analysis of 2,500 end-of-course survey comments from online biomedical science courses, labeled by the four expert authors, found average Jaccard similarity of "81.24% (Table 4)" across rater pairs. The article states this suggests "a challenging task even for expert human annotators".

> "The average Jaccard similarity coefficient including all 2,500 rows (averaged across the six unique pairings of the four human raters for all rows) was 81.24% (Table 4)"

### Michael J. Parker 2024 (2)

Michael J. Parker, Caitlin Anderson, Claire Stone, YeaRim Oh. (2024). A Large Language Model Approach to Educational Survey Feedback Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00414-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

The same inter-rater agreement analysis reports that "1,413 (57%) of 2,500 rows had all 4 human raters in agreement" and 1,572 (63%) had majority agreement, quantifying the inherent difficulty of the multi-label tagging task.

> "1,413 (57%) of 2,500 rows had all 4 human raters in agreement across all selected labels and 1,572 (63%) had majority (3 of 4) agreement on all selected labels."
