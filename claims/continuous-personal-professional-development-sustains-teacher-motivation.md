## Evidence

### Bahramnezhad F 2025

Bahramnezhad F, Keshmiri F. (2025). "Establishing pathways to excellence in the teaching profession": an exploration of teachers' experiences on sustaining motivation in Medical sciences universities. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06844-1

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of 37 in-depth semi-structured interviews with teachers from 10 Iranian medical sciences universities. Participants described a 'continuous cycle of goal-setting, self-regulation, and reflection' and collaborative exchange with peers and students as sustaining motivation; one participant said collaborative feedback made ideas 'completed, modified, and improved'.

> "Collaboration with others catalyzes personal and professional growth, thereby fostering sustained motivation among educators."
