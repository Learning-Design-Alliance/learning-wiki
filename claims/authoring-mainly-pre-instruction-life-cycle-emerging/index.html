Diego Dermeval 2018¶

Diego Dermeval, Ranilson Paiva, Ig Ibert Bittencourt, Julita Vassileva, Daniel Borges. (2018). Authoring Tools for Designing Intelligent Tutoring Systems: a Systematic Review of the Literature. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0157-9

q3 · i? · review · r3

RQ5 synthesis of authoring time across the 33 included studies, classified as pre-course, during the course and post-course. The review reports that "authoring tools have been mainly targeting ITS design before students’ instruction", while a minority address during/post-instruction authoring.
