Michele D. Dickey 2007¶

Michele D. Dickey. (2007). Barriers and enablers in integrating cognitive apprenticeship methods in a Web-based educational technology course for K–12 (primary and secondary) teacher education. ALT-J, Research in Learning Technology. https://doi.org/10.1080/09687760701482218

Case study observation and email-interview data from the I-TEP course. The study reports "eight more urgent reports of issues with accessing and/or downloading the videos" and notes many students reported waiting one or two hours while material downloaded; six complaints arrived the evening before deadlines.

"There were, however, eight more urgent reports of issues with accessing and/or downloading the videos. Eight students complained about the amount of time it took to download the media."
