## Evidence

### Mulder 2019

Mulder, H., van Ravenswaaij, H., Verhagen, J., Moerbeek, M., & Leseman, P. P. M. (2019). The process of early self-control: an observational study in two- and three-year-olds. Metacognition and Learning, 14, 239–264. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09199-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latency analysis of second-by-second coded behaviours during the snack delay task among successful children (n = 42). Visual attention distraction began at an average of 4 s and hands withholding at 7 s; hands and body distraction began later, between 12 and 18 s on average.

> "The latency data show that initiation of visual attention distraction and hands withholding was rapid in successful children, at an average of 4 and 7 s, respectively, while initiation of hands and body distraction behaviours occurred somewhat later, i.e., between 12 and 18 s on average."
