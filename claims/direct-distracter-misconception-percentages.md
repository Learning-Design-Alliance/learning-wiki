Distracter analysis across both versions of DIRECT; the article prints "28% exhibited current/voltage confusion" and the other percentages for shorts, contacts, and complete circuits. These are descriptive percentages, not effect sizes.

> "An examination of the distracters of both versions showed that 17% of the students could not identify a short in a circuit and/or determine what effect the short had on the circuit, 10% did not know where the contacts are on a light bulb, 6% had trouble identifying a complete circuit, and 28% exhibited current/voltage confusion."
