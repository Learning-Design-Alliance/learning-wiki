## Evidence

### Öncül Hasan 2026

Öncül Hasan, Dertsiz Kozan Betül, Dağ Umut, Çağlayan Mehtap, Alakuş Mehmet Fuat, Temel Cengiz, Utlu Elif Sedanur. (2026). Evaluation of primary care physicians’ knowledge, attitudes, and awareness levels regarding age-related macular degeneration. BMC Primary Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-026-03228-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparisons across the three professional title groups (general practitioners, residents, specialists) in the 440-physician survey found five items with significant title differences; the article reports "family medicine specialists demonstrated higher knowledge levels compared with general practitioners and residents."

> "statistically significant differences were observed among the professional title groups in responses to several questions. Notably, significant differences emerged regarding the following statements: “AMD is commonly known as macular disease in the general population” (p = 0.016), “At what age does AMD begin?” (p = 0.013), “AMD is associated with iris color” (p < 0.001), “Invasive diagnostic methods are used in the diagnosis of AMD” (p = 0.038), and “Anti-VEGF injections are reimbursed by the social security system” (p = 0.003)"
