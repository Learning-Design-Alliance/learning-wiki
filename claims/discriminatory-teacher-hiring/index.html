Secondhand report of a study of "a large school district in a U.S. urban center" finding African American candidates significantly less likely to receive job offers despite similar or more advanced qualifications; no effect size is printed.

"D’Amico, et al., (2017) found that African American candidates were signifcantly less likely to receive a job ofer than their white counterparts, despite having similar or even more advanced qualifcations."

q2 · i? · review · r1

Secondhand report of a meta-study of more than 24 hiring field experiments over 25 years and over 25,000 positions, reporting "white applicants received 36 percent more callbacks than black applicants" and 24 percent more than Latino applicants; these are descriptive callback percentages, not standardized effect sizes.

"since 1990, on average, white applicants received 36 percent more callbacks than black applicants and 24 percent more callbacks than Latino applicants with identical résumés"
