## Evidence

### Robert Winton 2025

Robert Winton, Meenam Pious & Anders Rasmussen. (2025). Training on multiple days results in better learning in embedded eyeblink conditioning in young human adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00347-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Because inter-session intervals varied between participants, the article compared Group A (n = 26, intervals longer than 5 days) with Group B (n = 19, intervals of 5 days or less) using a linear mixed effects model. No significant difference in CR percentage was found between the two groups; equivalence was not tested.

> "The results indicated no signiﬁcant dif- ference in CR percentage between the two groups ( t = −1.13, p = 0.255,
CI = −0.18, 0.047)."
