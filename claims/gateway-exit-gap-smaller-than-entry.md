## Evidence

### Curtis Sally 2020

Curtis Sally, Smith Daniel. (2020). A comparison of undergraduate outcomes for students from gateway courses and standard entry medicine courses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1918-y

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Figure 5 compares Cohen's d between SEMED and gateway students on entry and exit measures, with 95% confidence intervals computed per Hedges and Olkin. Exit differences are uniformly smaller than entry differences across all measures, with the largest printed exit effect size d = 0.616.

> "There is a smaller difference on exit (EPM Cohen’s d = 0.616; PSA Cohen’s d = 0.631; SJT Cohen’s d = 0.1454) than seen on entry (A level Cohen’s d = 1.254 and UCAT Cohen’s d = 1.066) between the two groups."
