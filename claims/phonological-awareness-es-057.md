Outcome-specific synthesis of six studies using either a commercial program (Pallante & Kim, 2013) or researcher-designed programs targeting lexical, syllabic, and phonemic awareness. The article reports an effect size of 0.57.

> "Phonological awareness studies show one of the highest effect sizes (0.57), which corresponds to a medium-high effect size following Cohen's (1988) benchmarks or above the 90th percentile in reading following Kraft's (2020)."
