## Evidence

### Himmelbauer M 2018

Himmelbauer M, Seitz T, Seidman C, Löffler-Stastka H. (2018). Standardized patients in psychiatry – the best way to learn clinical skills? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1184-4

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Questionnaire evaluation after the Doctor-patient-communication workshop-seminars at the Medical University of Vienna, completed by 529 students and 29 teachers (response rates 88% and 91%). The article reports both groups evaluated roleplay "very well" with two items rated "rather good".

> "Overall, students and teachers evaluated the quality of the SP’s role playing very well. Only the statements “SP challenged the students” and “SP adjusted role on students’ level” were rated as “rather good” by both."
