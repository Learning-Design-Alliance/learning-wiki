## Evidence

### Canning 2018

Canning, E. A., Harackiewicz, J. M., Priniski, S. J., Hecht, C. A., Tibbetts, Y., & Hyde, J. S. (2018). Improving Performance and Retention in Introductory Biology with a Utility-Value Intervention. Journal of educational psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000244

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Logistic regression within the fully crossed 2×2×2 design on continuation to the second course (N = 577). The article reports the third-essay main effect and a significant interaction with prior GPA suggesting late UV "was most effective for promoting enrollment for high-performing students"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "There was a positive main effect of UV condition for the third essay, Wald = 4.38, B = .25, p = .036, indicating that students who wrote a UV essay in the final unit of the course were more likely to enroll in the second biology course than students who wrote a control essay in the final unit of course."
