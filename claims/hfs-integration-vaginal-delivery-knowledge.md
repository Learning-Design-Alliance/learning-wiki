The Impact of High‐Fidelity Simulation and Curriculum Integration on Nursing Students’ Competence and Satisfaction: A Quasiexperimental Study. Independent t-test comparison of the two groups' 10-item vaginal delivery knowledge examination. The intervention group scored "86% vs. 46%"; the largest item difference was the hypotonic uterine contractions item (100% vs. 30%). No effect size is printed for this comparison.

> "For the students’ knowledge of nursing care during vaginal delivery, the total knowledge score of the students who were exposed to the HFS/curriculum‐integrated session was significantly higher than the control group’s score (86% vs. 46%, p < 0.05)."
