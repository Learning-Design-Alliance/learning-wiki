Evidence¶

Warren-Gordon 2019¶

Warren-Gordon, K., Hudson, K., & Scott, F. (2019). Voices of Partnerships Within the Critical Service-Learning Framework. Journal of Community Engagement and Higher Education, 12(2). https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1934-5283

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Autoethnographic first-person narrative by the community partner (Frank Scott) within a qualitative case of a criminology CSL course. The partner reports the course "allows for us to have a voice in exactly what the project will be and how it will be implemented." No effect size is reported.

"Dr. Warren-Gordon's course is unique in that it is the first class that allows for us to have a voice in exactly what the project will be and how it will be implemented."
