## Evidence

### Choi S 2018

Choi S, Yuen HM, Annan R, Pickup T, Pulman A, Monroy-Valle M, Aduku NEL, Kyei-Boateng S, Velásquez Monzón CI, Portillo Sermeño CE, Penn A, Ashworth A, Jackson AA. (2018). Effectiveness of the Malnutrition eLearning Course for Global Capacity Building in the Management of Malnutrition: Cross-Country Interrupted Time-Series Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/10396

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of 332 participants who completed all 3 assessments (pre, post, 6-month). The article reports knowledge "remained significantly higher than pretraining" with a mean difference of 7.1 (P<.001).

> "Although overall there was some loss of knowledge by 6 months, the participants' knowledge remained significantly higher than pretraining (mean difference=7.1, 95% CI 5.9-8.4; P<.001)"

### Choi S 2018 (2)

Choi S, Yuen HM, Annan R, Pickup T, Pulman A, Monroy-Valle M, Aduku NEL, Kyei-Boateng S, Velásquez Monzón CI, Portillo Sermeño CE, Penn A, Ashworth A, Jackson AA. (2018). Effectiveness of the Malnutrition eLearning Course for Global Capacity Building in the Management of Malnutrition: Cross-Country Interrupted Time-Series Study. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/10396

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of retained knowledge at 6 months between in-service participants who applied knowledge versus those who did not, from the 332-participant three-assessment subset. The article reports 66% retention "among those who did not apply their knowledge" retained 39%.

> "in-service participants who applied their knowledge retained 66% of the gained knowledge (mean scores for pre, post, and 6 months are 28.5, 46.8, and 40.5, respectively; retained knowledge=12.1/18.4) compared with 39% (mean scores for pre, post, and 6 months are 29.3, 42.2, and 34.3, respectively; retained knowledge=5.0/12.9) among those who did not apply their knowledge."
