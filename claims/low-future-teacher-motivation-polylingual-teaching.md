## Evidence

### Zhorabekova 2015

Zhorabekova, A. N. (2015). Formation of Future Teachers' Professional Competence on the Basis of Polylingual Approach: The State Analysis. International Education Studies, 8(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v8n12p130

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey and individual interviews among students of pedagogical specialties on attitudes to polylingual approach (Table 2). The article reports 73.2% answered no on necessity, 75.2% could not unambiguously positively assess effectiveness, and "The vast majority of future teachers (87.2%) do not learn the subject on their own in the second language."

> "to the question "Do you think it is necessary to teach a school subject in the second (Russian/Kazakh, English) language?" 73.2% of respondents gave a negative answer"
