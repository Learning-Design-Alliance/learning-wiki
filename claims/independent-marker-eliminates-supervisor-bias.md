### Graham Wild 2022

Graham Wild. (2022). Improving Capstone Research Projects: Using Computational Thinking to Provide Choice and Structured Active Learning. School of Engineering and Information Technology, UNSW Canberra. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2203.15947](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.15947)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case-study account of assessment redesign in the aviation capstone: supervisors were invited to mark weekly reports but declined, so "all but the presentations were marked by an independent marker". Proposals and poster presentations were marked collectively by 4 to 6 academic staff members and PhD students.

> "To solve the problem of supervisor bias, marking was handled by an objective 3rd party, and this was only facilitated by the increase in personal workload and being provided marking support."
