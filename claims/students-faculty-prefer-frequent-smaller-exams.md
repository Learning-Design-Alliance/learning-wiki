## Evidence

### Mueller Sophia 2011

Mueller Sophia, Weichert Nina, Stoecklein Veit, Hammitzsch Ariane, Pascuito Giulia, Krug Christian, Holzer Matthias, Pfeiffer Mona, Siebeck Matthias, Schmidmaier Ralf. (2011). Evaluation of effectiveness of instruction and study habits in two consecutive clinical semesters of the medical curriculum munich (MeCuM) reveals the need for more time for self study and higher frequency of assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-62

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey 3: anonymous online questionnaire of students who had finished both semesters (26% response, n = 109) and faculty of internal medicine (n = 79) and surgical specialties (122 data sets), on six-point (students) and four-point (faculty) Likert scales. Teacher views were "in line with the teachers' point of view" on both statements.

> "Most students study mainly prior to exams, but many students would prefer to have multiple smaller exams during the semester (SQ 7) instead of one big final examination (SQ 8). Both statements are in line with the teachers' point of view (FQ 6)."

### Mueller Sophia 2011 (2)

Mueller Sophia, Weichert Nina, Stoecklein Veit, Hammitzsch Ariane, Pascuito Giulia, Krug Christian, Holzer Matthias, Pfeiffer Mona, Siebeck Matthias, Schmidmaier Ralf. (2011). Evaluation of effectiveness of instruction and study habits in two consecutive clinical semesters of the medical curriculum munich (MeCuM) reveals the need for more time for self study and higher frequency of assessment. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-62

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Same survey 3 student questionnaire (n = 109). More than half wished for more self-guided study time and students wanted a more even distribution of compulsory lessons across the two semesters; results reported as percentage distributions.

> "In fact, more than half of the students generally wish to have more time for self-guided studies (SQ 5). Additionally, students wish to have a more even distribution of compulsory lessons over the two semesters (SQ 9)."
