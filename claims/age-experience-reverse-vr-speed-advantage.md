## Evidence

### Sandipan Chatterjee 2026

Sandipan Chatterjee, Khairul Faizi Mohammad, Monica Ghidinelli. (2026). Evaluating the Impact of Virtual Reality on Orthopedic Trauma Skills Acquisition Among Surgical Residents: Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Perioperative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2196/79343

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Unplanned between-cohort subanalysis of femoral nailing completion times, motivated by significant baseline differences in age and years of training (both P<.001, Table 1).

> "Participants in cohort 2 (older, with more years of training) were significantly faster in completing the synthetic bone nailing exercise than those in cohort 1 (20.7 vs 27.5 minutes; P=.01; Table 7)."

### Sandipan Chatterjee 2026 (2)

Sandipan Chatterjee, Khairul Faizi Mohammad, Monica Ghidinelli. (2026). Evaluating the Impact of Virtual Reality on Orthopedic Trauma Skills Acquisition Among Surgical Residents: Randomized Crossover Study. JMIR Perioperative Medicine. https://doi.org/10.2196/79343

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same unplanned subanalysis: cohort 1 completed the VR femoral module in 18.65 minutes versus 21.57 minutes for cohort 2 (P=.03, Table 7); the authors attribute this possibly to greater familiarity with technology.

> "In contrast, the younger and less-experienced cohort completed the VR module significantly faster."
