## Evidence

### Calderón 2015

Calderón, A., & Ruiz, M. (2015). A systematic literature review on serious games evaluation: An application to software project management. Computers & Education, 87, 396-422. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2015.07.011

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Quality assessment of the 119 selected papers using a nine-question instrument. The review reports "nearly 50% of the primary studies did not provide enough information about the models used to create the evaluation method" (QA3 coverage was negative).

> "This is because most of the primary studies did not provide the required information about the set of questions of the evaluation method and nearly 50% of the primary studies did not provide enough information about the models used to create the evaluation method."
