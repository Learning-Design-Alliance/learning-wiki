## Evidence

### Harsh Kumar 2024

Harsh Kumar, Ilya Musabirov, Mohi Reza, Jiakai Shi, Xinyuan Wang, Joseph Jay Williams, Anastasia Kuzminykh, and Michael Liut. 2024. Impact of Guidance and Interaction Strategies for LLM Use on Learner Performance and Perception. Proc. ACM Hum.-Comput. Interact. 8, CSCW2, Article 499 (November 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3687038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary outcome analysis of the Prolific randomized controlled trial (N=356): Kruskal-Wallis across guidance types and Mann-Whitney between LLM types both non-significant. No statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "The performance (average score in the task) was not significantly different between different types of guidance (𝜒 2Kruskal-Wallis(4) = 0.13, 𝑛 = 356, 𝑝 = 1) or whether the LLM was prompted or unprompted (𝑊Mann-Whitney = 15025, 𝑛 = 356, 𝑝 = 0.37)."
