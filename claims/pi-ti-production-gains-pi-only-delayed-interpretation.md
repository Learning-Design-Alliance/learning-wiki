# Both PI and TI produced significant gains in post and delayed production of the English present perfect, but only the PI group gained significantly on delayed interpretation

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Within the TI group, post production and delayed production scores were significantly higher than pre-production scores (p = .000 for both pairs). [→ Rachelle B. Lintao and Jonathan P. Erfe 2012](#rachelle-b-lintao-and-jonathan-p-erfe-2012)
`q2 i?` Within the PI group, delayed interpretation (p = .005), post production (p = .000), and delayed production (p = .000) showed statistically significant gains. [→ Rachelle B. Lintao and Jonathan P. Erfe 2012 (2)](#rachelle-b-lintao-and-jonathan-p-erfe-2012-2)

## Evidence

### Rachelle B. Lintao and Jonathan P. Erfe 2012

Rachelle B. Lintao and Jonathan P. Erfe. (2012). Using Processing Instruction for the Acquisition of English Present Perfect of Filipinos. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 2 No. 1, January - March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070206

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t tests for the TI group (n=40) at the 5% significance level show production gains from pre-production (mean 12.8750) to post production (15.8750) and delayed production (16.2250); the article reports "at .000 p value" for both pairs.

> "there are considerable differences in the mean performance of the respondents from pre-production to post production at .000 p value and from pre-production to delayed production tests at 0.000 p value."

### Rachelle B. Lintao and Jonathan P. Erfe 2012 (2)

Rachelle B. Lintao and Jonathan P. Erfe. (2012). Using Processing Instruction for the Acquisition of English Present Perfect of Filipinos. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 2 No. 1, January - March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070206

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t tests for the PI group (n=40): delayed interpretation rose from a 7.5750 pre-interpretation score to 8.3500, and production from 13.1750 to 17.5000 (post) and 17.5250 (delayed), with the printed p values .005 and .000.

> "Results of the paired-samples ‘t’ test results for the PI group (Table 2) revealed statistically significant scores of the group's delayed interpretation (p value= .005), post production (p value= .000) and delayed production (p value= .000) tests."
