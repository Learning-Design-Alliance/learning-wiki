Evidence¶

Jennifer Wilhelm 2009¶

Jennifer Wilhelm, Christa Jackson, Amber Sullivan, Ron Wilhelm. (2009). Factors Influencing Middle School Students' Spatial Mathematics Development While Participating in an Integrated STEM Unit. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Factors+Influencing+Middle+School+Students+Spatial+Mathematics+Development

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Repeated measures ANOVA on the experimental group's LPCI pre-post scores (N=124) in this quasi-experimental study. The article reports a "significant increase in the mean valXes" with partial eta-squared 0.371; gain scores were 12.1% for males and 13.0% for females.

"A 5MA129A revealed a significant increase in the mean valXes from pre (21.2%) to post (33.7%) on overall test scores, F(1, 123) 72.7, p 0.001, partial 2 0.371."

Jennifer Wilhelm 2009 (2)¶

Jennifer Wilhelm, Christa Jackson, Amber Sullivan, Ron Wilhelm. (2009). Factors Influencing Middle School Students' Spatial Mathematics Development While Participating in an Integrated STEM Unit. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Factors+Influencing+Middle+School+Students+Spatial+Mathematics+Development

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Repeated measures ANOVA on the control group's LPCI pre-post scores (N=66), taught by a teacher with 29 years experience using her regular Earth/Space lessons. The article reports a "significant increase in the mean valXes" with partial eta-squared 0.422.

"A 5MA129A revealed a significant increase in the mean valXes from pre (26.6%) to post (38.5%) on overall test scores, F(1, 65) 48.1, p 0.001, partial 2 0.422."
