Evidence¶

van Houten‐Schat Maaike A 2018¶

van Houten‐Schat Maaike A, Berkhout Joris J, van Dijk Nynke, Endedijk Maaike D, Jaarsma A Debbie, Diemers Agnes D. (2018). Self‐regulated learning in the clinical context: a systematic review. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13615

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review of 18 included studies (10 observational, six qualitative, two mixed-methods) conducted per AMEE guidelines, searching six databases from 1992 to 2016. The review reports "diversity in the use of SRL strategies" linked to individual, contextual and social factors. In its results, patient care is one of the most frequently mentioned positive contextual factors and almost all studies report time pressure as a contextual barrier, while the influence of a coach or supervisor and of peers is classed as a positive social factor; the abstract, quoted below, also lists supervision among the contextual factors.

"We found diversity in the use of SRL strategies by medical students and residents, which is linked to individual (goal setting), contextual (time pressure, patient care and supervision) and social (supervisors and peers) factors."
