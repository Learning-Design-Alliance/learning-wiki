Evidence¶

Kroesbergen 2023¶

Kroesbergen, E. H.; Huijsmans, M. D. E.; Friso-van den Bos, I. (2023). A Meta-Analysis on the Differences in Mathematical and Cognitive Skills Between Individuals With and Without Mathematical Learning Disabilities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543221132773

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator models on mathematics effect sizes. Seriousness also mattered: one-SD-cutoff samples had smaller discrepancies than two-SD-cutoff samples, and a seriousness × persistence interaction (p = .002) counteracted the positive main effect for two-SD samples.

"When specific- ity was used as a selection criterion, samples showed larger mathematics discrep- ancies from control groups compared with when no specificity criterion was used (p = .002)."

Kroesbergen 2023 (2)¶

Kroesbergen, E. H.; Huijsmans, M. D. E.; Friso-van den Bos, I. (2023). A Meta-Analysis on the Differences in Mathematical and Cognitive Skills Between Individuals With and Without Mathematical Learning Disabilities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543221132773

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Age moderator on mathematics effect sizes: teens exceeded children (p < .001); adults exceeded children (p = .03) once the age × persistence interaction was added. The interaction itself did not explain variation.

"Effect sizes were larger for samples consisting of teens than for samples of children, p < .001."

Kroesbergen 2023 (3)¶

Kroesbergen, E. H.; Huijsmans, M. D. E.; Friso-van den Bos, I. (2023). A Meta-Analysis on the Differences in Mathematical and Cognitive Skills Between Individuals With and Without Mathematical Learning Disabilities. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543221132773

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Seriousness moderator on working-memory effect sizes (Table 6). Persistence alone also explained variance (p = .039), with persistence-selected samples showing larger deviations; contrasts did not persist when the seriousness × persistence interaction was added. Specificity showed no effect (p = .974).

"Groups selected with a cutoff of one or two standard deviations deviated less from their typically performing peers regarding working memory than groups selected with a cutoff of 1.5 or more than two standard deviations"
