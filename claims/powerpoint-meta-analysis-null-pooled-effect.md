## Evidence

### Samuel P. León 2021

Samuel P. León, Inmaculada García-Martínez. (2021). Impact of the provision of PowerPoint slides on learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104283

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article reports, citing Baker et al. (2018), a meta-analysis of PowerPoint effects on cognitive learning: 29.17% of studies favoured PowerPoint, 22.92% negative, 47.92% no effects; "evidence generally failed to support the use of PowerPoint by teachers".

> "The effect size produced (k = 48) was not significant g = 0.067 [95%CI -0.103, 0.236], I2 = 91.39% and so the authors concluded that evidence generally failed to support the use of PowerPoint by teachers."
