# Midterm format has no overall effect on retention exam scores five months after the course

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Students from the cumulative and non-cumulative sections did not differ statistically on a cumulative retention exam taken about five months after the final. [→ Bailey Elizabeth G. 2021](#bailey-elizabeth-g-2021)

## Evidence

### Bailey Elizabeth G. 2021

Bailey Elizabeth G., Greenall Rebeka F., Tullis Madeleine M., Williams Kurt R. (2021). The retention benefits of cumulative versus non-cumulative midterms in introductory biology may depend on students’ reasoning skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250143

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test comparing retention exam scores of returning students from the two sections. No statistical difference "(t(1, 60) = 1.16, p = 0.25; n = 62)"; equivalence was not tested, and returners had higher reasoning scores than non-returners.

> "Again, Fig 4A shows no statistical difference in retention between the section that had cumulative midterms and the section that had non-cumulative midterms (t(1, 60) = 1.16, p = 0.25; n = 62)."
