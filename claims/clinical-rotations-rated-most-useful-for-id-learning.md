### Weier N 2017

Weier N, Thursky K, Zaidi STR. (2017). Antimicrobial knowledge and confidence amongst final year medical students in Australia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182460

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey ratings of teaching-method usefulness in the same cross-sectional study; percentages are as printed and no significance test or effect size is reported for these ratings.

> "Students rated clinical rotations and informal teaching by residents and registrars as the most useful forms of teaching methods in teaching ID knowledge and prescribing (51.9% and 56.3% of students respectively rated these teaching methods as ‘most useful’), while lectures and attending ward rounds were most likely to be rated as ‘least useful’ (31.3% of students rated each method as ‘least useful’)."
