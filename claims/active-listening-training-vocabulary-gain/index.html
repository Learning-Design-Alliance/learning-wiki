Evidence¶

Berker Bulut 2017¶

Berker Bulut, Nuri Karasakaloğlu. (2017). Benefiting from Listening in Vocabulary Development. Journal of Education and Training Studies, Vol. 5, No. 12. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v5i12.2688

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Two-Way ANOVA for mixed measures on pre-test–post-test Vocabulary Test scores of 62 experimental and 63 control fourth-grade students in a semi-experimental design; the Group*Measure interaction was significant, "[F (1,123) = 5.76, p < .05]". Means rose from 60.67 to 70.22 (experimental) versus 60.09 to 61.92 (control).

"the common effects of the repeated measures factors on the vocabulary achievement levels were significant in the different treatment groups [F (1,123) = 5.76, p < .05]"
