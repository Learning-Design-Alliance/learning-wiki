## Evidence

### Jia 2025

Jia, Y., Spagnolo, A. B., Barrett, N., Murphy, A. A., Basto, P. M., Rothpletz-Puglia, P., & Luther, S. (2025). Validation of a peer observation and evaluation tool for online teaching in the U.S. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10428-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the four-facet Rasch model, asynchronous lessons scored M = −0.19 (SE = 0.06) versus synchronous M = 0.19 (SE = 0.04) on the logit scale, "indicating that raters scored asynchronous lessons higher than synchronous lessons."

> "The MFRM analysis demonstrated a significant difference between asynchronous and synchronous lessons, where the asynchronous lessons were rated higher."
