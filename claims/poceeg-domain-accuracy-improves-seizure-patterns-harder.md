## Evidence

### Leopold Simma 2025

Leopold Simma, Schneeberger Maurice Henri, von Felten Stefanie, Seiler Michelle, Ramantani Georgia, Bölsterli Bigna Katrin. (2025). E-Learning for Pediatric Emergency Department Staff in Point-of-Care Electroencephalogram Interpretation: Prospective Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/69395

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Binomial generalized linear mixed-effects model on 1044 ratings from 29 participants (random intercept for visit nested within participant); Table 4 reports posttest OR 2.75 (P<.001), retention OR 1.50 (P=.014), and normal-versus-pathological OR 1.40 (P=.049).

> "Overall, the number of correct answers increased markedly in the posttest and remained higher in the retention test compared with baseline (Table 4). We observed minor variations among the 3 domains across these tests."
