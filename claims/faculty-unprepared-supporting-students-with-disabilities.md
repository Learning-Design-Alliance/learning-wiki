### Caroline Bustamante 2019

Caroline Bustamante, Erin Scanlon, & Jacquelyn J. Chini. (2019). Supporting Students with ADHD in Introductory Physics Courses: Four Steps for Instructors. University of Central Florida. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1907.12448](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1907.12448)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The article synthesizes multiple cited studies of faculty preparedness, reporting that faculty "lack awareness of the legal requirements related to accommodations" and do not feel prepared to teach students with disabilities. This is the article's narrative review of background literature, not its own data.

> "Multiple studies have found that faculty lack awareness of the legal requirements related to accommodations,6-7 lack knowledge of inclusive pedagogies,8 state that they do not feel prepared to teach students with disabilities,9 and want more training related to accessibility."
