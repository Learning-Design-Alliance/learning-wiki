## Evidence

### Jacob Whitehill and Margo Seltzer 2017

Jacob Whitehill and Margo Seltzer. (2017). A Crowdsourcing Approach To Collecting Tutorial Videos – Toward Personalized Learning-at-Scale. [doi:10.1145/3051457.3053973](https://doi.org/10.1145/3051457.3053973)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Experiment II: N = 200 Mechanical Turk participants took a pretest, watched a randomly assigned video (a crowdsourced video or control video about the number π), then took a posttest. Learning gains were posttest minus pretest scores; the t-test result is as printed.

> "The average learning gains (0.105) for the 40 crowdsourced videos was higher than for the control video (0.045); the difference was statistically significant (t(39) = 3.715, p< 0.001, two-tailed)."
