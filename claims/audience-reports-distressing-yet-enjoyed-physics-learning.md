Break-in' Point: Somatic Narratives - the convergence of arts and science in the transformation of temporal communities. Originally published 2015. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2208.08441](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2208.08441)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Audience reflections collected after the 2012 Stage@Leeds performances. One audience member reported learning "more about physics and basic human resilience than I ever imagined" in the performance's 30 minutes; self-report, no test or effect size.

> "I never thought I would understand anything having to do with physics, but in this 30 minutes I have learned more about physics and basic human resilience than I ever imagined"

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Another audience member's reflection, quoted by the authors, describes the performance as unexpectedly "distressing" though it was one s/he "really, really enjoyed"; illustrative self-report, no measure reported.

> "I didn’t expect it to be so distressing… I thought that it would be a nice sweet afternoon performance but it wasn’t"
