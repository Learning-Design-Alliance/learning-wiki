The comment states this as an established laboratory principle, citing Carpenter et al.'s review of spacing research, while noting that optimal spacing at longer retention intervals remains under active research.

> "A second principle holds that learning works best when it is distributed over time, rather than crammed all up into one long session. Some of the speciﬁcs of how to optimally space learning episodes over time, especially also at longer retention intervals, have already been identiﬁed but the topic still remains an area of active research."
