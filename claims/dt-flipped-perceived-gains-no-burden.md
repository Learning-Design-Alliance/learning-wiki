### Zhang Hui 2025

Zhang Hui, Wang Xiaomei, Li Xiaojin, Zhai Jinghua, Li Xiuguo, Guo Yan. (2025). The impact of large-class flipped classrooms incorporating design thinking on self-awareness, team collaboration, learning efficiency, and comprehensive literacy of clinical medicine undergraduates. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire survey of the experimental class with a 100% response rate (150 questionnaires). Students self-reported perceived comprehension gains and burden; 120 students (80%) also believed the model better improved comprehensive abilities and qualities.

> "Some 85.3% of students said it had no effect or reduced their burden, while 24.7% believed it added to their learning burden. "Do you think group presentations and comment discussions help understand knowledge?" A total of 113 students (89.3%) thought that they were helpful."
