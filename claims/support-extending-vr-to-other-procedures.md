## Evidence

### Vrillon Agathe 2022

Vrillon Agathe, Gonzales-Marabal Laurent, Ceccaldi Pierre-François, Plaisance Patrick, Desrentes Eric, Paquet Claire, Dumurgier Julien. (2022). Using virtual reality in lumbar puncture training improves students learning experience. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03317-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Survey item on interest in extending the VR video format to other procedures, rated by all 168 participants; the between-group Mann-Whitney comparison was not significant (p = 0.4956). No effect size is printed.

> "Participants believed that the extension of VR to other medical procedures would be of high interest with a mean score of 4.4 ± 0.9, with no difference between groups"
