### Arievitch 2005

Arievitch, I.M., & Haenen, J.P.P. (2005). Connecting Sociocultural Theory and Educational Practice: Galperin's Approach. Educational Psychologist, 40(3), 155-165. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep4003_2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Handwriting teaching experiment by Pantina (1957) based on Galperin's method of identifying basic units of letter contour via indices placed where lines begin, end, or change direction. The article reports students established the inventory of indices themselves and, per the post-test, "were able to transfer the acquired method of analyzing shapes."

> "In the post-test series it was established that the students were able to transfer the acquired method of analyzing shapes to a variety of tasks, such as reproducing Arabic writing, blueprints, and drawings."
