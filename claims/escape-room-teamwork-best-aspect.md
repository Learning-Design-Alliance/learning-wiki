### Baessler F 2025

Baessler F, Hornung TP, Schuster TJ, Hardt N, Felter E, Anzenberger E, Ely DR, Lichtenstein T, Steiner K, Koelkebeck K, Flasbeck V, Spitzer P, Speerforck S, Pinilla S, Khatamzas E, Mueller B, Zafar A. `q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis by two independent coders of open-text answers from all 45 participants; "16 students mentioned collaborative teamwork" as the best aspect, with clinical case and broad competences mentioned 14 times.

> "On the question of "what did you like best about this escape room?", 16 students mentioned collaborative teamwork with comments such as "the interactivity was fantastic!", "teamwork was essential", "collaboration with others" and "working together as a team"."

### Baessler F 2025 (2)

Baessler F, Hornung TP, Schuster TJ, Hardt N, Felter E, Anzenberger E, Ely DR, Lichtenstein T, Steiner K, Koelkebeck K, Flasbeck V, Spitzer P, Speerforck S, Pinilla S, Khatamzas E, Mueller B, Zafar A. (2025). Game-based learning in undergraduate medical education: evaluation of an interdisciplinary escape room. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07990-2

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of the improvement question in the same 45-response dataset; counts are as printed by the authors in the results section and are descriptive, with no effect sizes.

> "Regarding suggestions for improvement, 10 participants indicated a preference for more comprehensive briefing sessions and clearer instructions. Students also suggested small groups of up to a maximum of six players (mentioned 9 times) and expressed a desire for more playing time (6x)."
