Aldo Gordillo and Daniel López-Fernández. (2024). Are Educational Escape Rooms More Effective Than Traditional Lectures for Teaching Software Engineering? A Randomized Controlled Trial. IEEE Transactions on Education. https://doi.org/10.1109/TE.2024.3403913

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The article's literature review section reports, citing Lathwesen and Belova, that most of 93 STEM escape room studies offer scant or no effectiveness evidence, and that only 2 used a control and experimental group design. Reported as the article attributes it; the reviewed studies were not read directly.

> "Lathwesen and Belova [5] analyzed 93 studies on educational escape rooms aimed at STEM education and found that most of these studies describe speciﬁc escape room experiences with scant or no evidence on their learning effectiveness."
