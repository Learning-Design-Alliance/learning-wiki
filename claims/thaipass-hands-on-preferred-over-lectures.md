`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Survey responses from the 2019 ThaiPASS summer school comparing enjoyment of lectures versus coding sessions. Students enjoyed both but showed a "clear preference for the hands-on activities"; the authors suggest changing the balance or interspersing the two. No effect size is printed.

> "While students said they enjoyed both the astronomy lectures and the coding sessions, they expressed a clear preference for the hands-on activities. This suggests it may be beneficial to either change the balance of lectures to activities, or to intersperse the two so as not to lose students' interest."
