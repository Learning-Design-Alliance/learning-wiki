## Evidence

### Joyce Kelly Barreto Silva 2025

Joyce Kelly Barreto Silva, Thomaz Bittencourt Couto, Andreia Melo Coriolano, Alex Aquino, Júlio Cesar Martins Monte. (2025). Analysis of acquisition and retention of cardiopulmonary resuscitation skills according to training frequency. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2025AO1257

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same 43-student randomized controlled trial, first-attempt comparison with and without the feedback-equipped device; both recoil and hand position medians were near ceiling at baseline, e.g. "median=93% [IQR: 78–99%] versus median=93%".

> "No significant differences were found in the total recoil (median=93% [IQR: 78–99%] versus median=93% [IQR: 58–100%]; p=0.991) or correct hand position (median=99% [IQR: 100–100%] versus median=99% [IQR: 100–100%]; p=0.754)."
