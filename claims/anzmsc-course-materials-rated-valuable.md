Solanki Jahnvee, Kerdraon Yves, Petsoglou Constantinos, Cornish Elisa, Wilson Graham. (2025). The Australia New Zealand Microsurgical Skills Course - are we providing a high quality educational programme?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07928-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective analysis of University of Sydney Student Evaluation Forms from all 227 students across twelve ANZMSC courses since 2018, analysed with descriptive statistics. The article reports "64.3% strongly agreed, 30.8% agreed" for course materials and "73.6% strongly agreed, 22.5% agreed" for demonstrator feedback.

> "Participants found the course materials were valuable for their learning (64.3% strongly agreed, 30.8% agreed) and they received valuable feedback from the demonstrators (73.6% strongly agreed, 22.5% agreed)."
