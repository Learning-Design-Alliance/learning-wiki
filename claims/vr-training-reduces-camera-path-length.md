## Evidence

### Andersen C 2011

Andersen C, Winding TN, Vesterby MS. (2011). Development of simulated arthroscopic skills: A randomized trial of virtual-reality training of 21 orthopedic surgeons. Acta Orthopaedica 82(1). https://doi.org/10.3109/17453674.2011.552776

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized trial, distance travelled by the camera during the standard insightMIST test was automatically recorded. The intervention group improved by an average of 283 cm while both control groups increased this distance; the improvement "compared to the inexperienced control group was statistically significant (p = 0.02)".

> "The intervention group showed the worst result in the first test, but the best in the second test. The improvement made by the intervention group compared to the inexperienced control group was statistically significant (p = 0.02)."
