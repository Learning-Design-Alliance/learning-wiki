## Evidence

### Wang Song 2017

Wang Song, Zhou Ming, Chen Taolin, Yang Xun, Chen Guangxiang, Wang Meiyun, Gong Qiyong. (2017). Examining gray matter structure associated with academic performance in a large sample of Chinese high school students. Scientific Reports 7, 893. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-00677-9

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Re-running the brain–achievement regression with family SES as an additional covariate, CNCEE "was still related to the rGMD of the left DLPFC (r = 0.38, p < 0.001)".

> "After adjusting for SES, CNCEE score was still related to the rGMD of the left DLPFC (r = 0.38, p < 0.001)."

### Wang Song 2017 (2)

Wang Song, Zhou Ming, Chen Taolin, Yang Xun, Chen Guangxiang, Wang Meiyun, Gong Qiyong. (2017). Examining gray matter structure associated with academic performance in a large sample of Chinese high school students. Scientific Reports 7, 893. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-00677-9

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

The mediation was re-estimated with SES as an additional covariate; "general intelligence still accounted for the common variance between the rGMD of the left DLPFC and CNCEE score (indirect effect = 0.043, 95% CI = [0.01, 0.09], p < 0.05)".

> "after controlling for SES, general intelligence still accounted for the common variance between the rGMD of the left DLPFC and CNCEE score (indirect effect = 0.043, 95% CI = [0.01, 0.09], p < 0.05)."
