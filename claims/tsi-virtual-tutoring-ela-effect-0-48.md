### Targeted Skills Instruction Evidence Rating Review 2025

Targeted Skills Instruction Evidence Rating Review. (2025). https://www.edmentum.com

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A regression discontinuity study across 23 schools in a large urban New Jersey district during the 2024-2025 academic year, including 1,077 students in grades 3, 4 and 6 assigned to TSI based on fall diagnostic scores at or below the 25th percentile. Students "scored significantly higher than comparison students on the spring NJSLA ELA assessment (effect size = +0.48)".
