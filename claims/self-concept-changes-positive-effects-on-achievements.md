## Subclaims
`q3 i0` Self-concept changes show small positive effects on achievements within subjects (standardized coefficients below 0.2), interpreted as temporal comparisons of self-concepts. [→ Wolff et al. 2020](#wolff-et-al-2020)

## Evidence

### Wolff et al. 2020

Fabian Wolff, Fabio Sticca, Christoph Niepel, Thomas Götz, Jan Van Damme, and Jens Möller. (2020). The Reciprocal 2I/E Model: An Investigation of Mutual Relations Between Achievement and Self-Concept Levels and Changes in the Math and Verbal Domain Across Three Countries. https://osf.io/7f6gb/

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Study 1 r2I/EM sub-model (N = 1,508), first examination of self-concept change effects on achievement. Within-subject effects were β = .17 to .20 (below the d = 0.2 threshold); the math self-concept change effect on German achievement was β = .10. Study 2 replicated (math change on German achievement β = .13, p < .01).

> "small positive effects of self-concept changes on achievements within subjects (all .17 ≤ β ≤ .20, all p < .001). In contrast to our assumptions, however, the effects of self-concept levels on achievements between subjects were close to zero and nonsignificant, rather than negative (all –.01 ≤ β ≤ .03, all p ≥ .36). Moreover, the effect of math self-concept change on German achievement was significantly positive, rather than close to zero (β = .10, p < .01, 90% CI [.047, .160])."
