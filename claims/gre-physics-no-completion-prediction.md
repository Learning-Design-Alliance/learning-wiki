## Evidence

### Casey W. Miller 2019

Casey W. Miller, Benjamin M. Zwickl, Julie R. Posselt, Rachel T. Silvestrini, Theodore Hodapp. (2019). Typical Physics PhD Admissions Criteria Limit Access to Underrepresented Groups but Fail to Predict Doctoral Completion. Draft preprint; journal version in Science Advances. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1126/sciadv.aat7550](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aat7550)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null finding from the multivariate logistic regression across all four analytic samples. The article notes "the large span of scores among graduate students in our sample" and reports only 7% greater completion probability for 90th vs 10th percentile scorers in the US Only model; no effect size is printed.

> "GRE-P scores were not associated with PhD completion at the 0.05 level of statistical significance for any of the models. This is notable because of the large span of scores among graduate students in our sample."
