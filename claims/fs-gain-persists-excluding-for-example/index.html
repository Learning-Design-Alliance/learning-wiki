Evidence¶

Lewis Murray 2017¶

Lewis Murray. (2017). A Study in Enhancing L2 Learners’ Utility with Written Academic Formulaic Sequences. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3), 76–92. https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1274

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Re-analysis excluding for example, attributed by the author to prior knowledge and the final's reasons-and-examples genre. The treatment group's gain remained "a significant difference" with r = -.67; the control group's change was not significant.

"Likewise, a Wilcoxon signed-rank test determined there was no significant difference in use between the control group’s midterm (Mdn = 0) and final (Mdn = 0), T = 78, p = .115, r = -.12, while the treatment group’s midterm (Mdn = 0) to final (Mdn = 2.08) did show a significant difference, T = 146, p < .001, r = -.67."
