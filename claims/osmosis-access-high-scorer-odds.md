# Students with Osmosis access had higher odds of scoring 90% or higher on the Pharmacology final exam (OR = 2.88) and of earning a 90%+ final grade in Podiatric Medicine (OR = 2.71)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Logistic regression found students with Osmosis access were significantly more likely to score 90% or higher on the Pharmacology final exam, with increased odds of high final course grades in Podiatric Medicine. [→ Hoyt Garrik 2024](#hoyt-garrik-2024)

## Evidence

### Hoyt Garrik 2024

Hoyt Garrik, Adegboyega Samuel, Constantouris Gus, Basu Paramita. (2024). Study of the impact of introducing a multimedia learning tool in podiatric medical courses. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1002/jfa2.12018

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Logistic regression analysis of the quasi-experimental cohort data. The abstract reports "students with Osmosis access were 2.88 times more likely to score 90% or higher on the Pharmacology final exam (p < 0.05)"; effects were course-specific, with odds near 1 in the other course contrasts.

> "The odds ratio was 2.88 for Pharmacology and 1.02 for Podiatric Medicine. On the other hand, analyzing the relationship between Osmosis access and a course grade of 90% or higher revealed odds ratio values of 2.71 for Podiatric Medicine and 1.30 for Pharmacology."
