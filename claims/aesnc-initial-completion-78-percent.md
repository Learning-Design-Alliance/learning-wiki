Implementation outcome from the observational study's usage data collected in the Area9 Rhapsode platform. Providers who completed initial learning took on average 4.5 (IQR 2.4–6.1) hours, and median duration to complete all modules was 61 days (IQR 34–111).

> "The average progress of initial learning for all providers was 78% (SD=31%) (online supplemental data C1). Fifty-six (110) completing all initial learning, 13% (25) incomplete but active within 2 weeks of the end of the study, and 31% (60) becoming inactive during the study period."
