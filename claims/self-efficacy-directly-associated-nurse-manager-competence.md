## Evidence

### Ivziku Dhurata 2026

Ivziku Dhurata, Curcio Felice, Filomeno Lucia, Lachi Alessio, D'Agostino Fabio, Giannetta Noemi, Forte Daniela, Gori Nexhmije, Gonzalez Cesar Ivan Aviles, Tartaglini Daniela. (2026). The moderating role of self‐efficacy in influencing the level of competencies among Middle Nurse Managers: A cross‐sectional study. Journal of Nursing Scholarship. https://doi.org/10.1111/jnu.70121

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional multicenter survey of 382 Italian Middle Nurse Managers analyzed with structural equation modeling; the knowledge model reported self-efficacy's direct effect with "Competence Knowledge (β = 0.343, p < 0.001)". The application model reported β = 0.325, p = 0.019.

> "it showed positive effects on Educational Appropriateness (β = 0.144, p = 0.006) and Competence Knowledge (β = 0.343, p < 0.001)"
