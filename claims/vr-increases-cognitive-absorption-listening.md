## Evidence

### Liang 2026

Liang, Y., & Hu, G. (2026). Investigating L2 listening comprehension and experience in immersive and interactive virtual reality: An experimental study. Computers & Education, 248, 105593. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105593

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Hierarchical regression analysis of RCT data from 84 Chinese university EFL learners showed VR mode predicted cognitive state flow. The article reports "explaining an additional 20% of its variance" when listening mode was entered in Model 3.

> "Listening mode, entered in Model 3, significantly predicted the cognitive component of state flow (B = 6.85, β = .36, p < .001), explaining an additional 20% of its variance (r2 partial = .20) beyond the effects of the previously entered predictors."
