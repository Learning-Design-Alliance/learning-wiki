## Evidence

### Östlund Ann-Sofi 2015

Östlund Ann-Sofi, Kristofferzon Marja-Leena, Häggström Elisabeth, Wadensten Barbro. (2015). Primary care nurses’ performance in motivational interviewing: a quantitative descriptive study. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-015-0304-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive MITI 3.1.1 coding of 32 audio-recorded MI sessions from twelve primary care nurses in Swedish primary care, analyzed against MITI beginning-proficiency thresholds. The study found that "none of the nurses achieved beginning proficiency on every variable/summary score or for all sessions"; no effect size is reported.

> "The summary score results are presented in Table 7 and show that none of the nurses achieved beginning proficiency on every variable/summary score or for all sessions. Two nurses did not achieve beginning proficiency on any summary score."
