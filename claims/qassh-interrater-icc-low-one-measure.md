## Evidence

### Rivière E 2025

Rivière E, Der Sahakian G, Tremblay M-L, Chiniara G. (2025). Development and validation of the QASSH scale: a tool for assessing the quality of simulation scenarios in healthcare education. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2486971

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Interrater reliability analysis via intra-class coefficients for rater groups assessing each scenario within each modality. The article reports ICCs of "0.74 and 0.87" for most measures but "0.21" for the high-quality procedural simulation scenario; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "The interrater reliability was 0.66 for one measure (p < 0.0001) and varied between 0.74 and 0.87 for all measures (p < 0.0001), except for the rating of the high-quality scenario in procedural simulation for which the ICC coefficient was 0.21 (95% CI 0.28–0.56)."
