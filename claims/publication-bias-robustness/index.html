q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Three publication-bias tests were applied. Published studies had larger effect sizes than unpublished (Fisher's Z = .222 vs .149) but not significantly (p = .124); fail-safe N analysis suggested at least 2797 null effect sizes would be needed to reduce the pooled effect to non-significance.

"Funnel plot (see Fig. 2) and Egger's test of heterogeneity suggested that the distribution of effect sizes was largely symmetrical, thus showing limited evidence of publication bias, t(18) = 0.111, p = .913."
