## Evidence

### Lee Haesun 2025

Lee Haesun, Lee Seungjin, Hwang Mina, Woo Kyungmi. (2025). Effectiveness of game-based digital intervention for attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder in children and adolescents: a systematic review and meta-analysis using Beard and Wilson's conceptualization of perception in experiential learning. European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-025-02788-5

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of two RCTs (Dovis 2015; Medina 2021) found "CBTT Backward (MD = 1.17; 95% CI = [0.16, 2.17])" significantly improved, indicating enhanced visuospatial working memory. The article prints a mean difference but no standardized effect size.

> "CBTT Forward (MD = 1.67; 95% CI = [0.66, 2.67]) and CBTT Backward (MD = 1.17; 95% CI = [0.16, 2.17]) showed significant improvements after the intervention"
