## Evidence

### İlhan 2018

İlhan, B. (2018). Length in listening texts as a single determiner of difficulty for comprehension. Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(3), 336-346. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1193259

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental post-test-only comparison of two B1 classes (12 per group) on comprehension questions for a 03.37-minute listening text; the experimental group listened in three phases. The article reports "no significant difference" with t (22) = 0.979, p = 0.338; no effect size is printed.

> "T-test analysis of the comprehension scores for both groups indicated that there was no significant difference in scores for experimental group (M = 43.33, SD = 18, 74) and control group (M = 35, SD = 22,76; t (22) = 0.979, p = 0.338, two-tailed)."
