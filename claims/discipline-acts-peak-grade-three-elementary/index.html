Descriptive analysis of disciplinary records from 66,212 K-5 students in one large U.S. school district, reported in Figure 1 as average discipline acts per student by grade. The authors report that "both suspensions and referrals generally increased as grade levels progress, peaking in third grade."

"Our findings indicate that both suspensions and referrals generally increased as grade levels progress, peaking in third grade (Figure 1)."
