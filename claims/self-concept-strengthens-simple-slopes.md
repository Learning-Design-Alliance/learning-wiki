# At high English academic self-concept, social support's positive association with enjoyment and negative association with anxiety are significantly stronger than at low self-concept

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Social support's positive association with enjoyment and negative association with anxiety are both stronger at high self-concept; the survey was cross-sectional, and the authors note it cannot establish causation. [→ Chen Shengdong 2025 (2)](#chen-shengdong-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Chen Shengdong 2025

Chen, S., Xu, H., Jin, C., Yin, K., Wang, X., & Wang, T. (2025). Social support and english academic achievement: the mediating role of foreign language emotions and the moderating effect of academic self-concept. BMC Psychology, 13, 1286. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03617-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slope tests comparing groups split at one standard deviation above and below the self-concept mean. At low self-concept (M − 1SD) the enjoyment slope was βsimple = 0.244, t = 9.523, p < 0.001, CI (0.194, 0.295); at M + 1SD it was βsimple = 0.352.

> "For students with high English academic self-concept (M + 1SD), the positive prediction of foreign language enjoyment by social support remained significant and stronger [βsimple = 0.352, t = 13.214, p < 0.001, 95% CI (0.299, 0.404)]"

### Chen Shengdong 2025 (2)

Chen, S., Xu, H., Jin, C., Yin, K., Wang, X., & Wang, T. (2025). Social support and english academic achievement: the mediating role of foreign language emotions and the moderating effect of academic self-concept. BMC Psychology, 13, 1286. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03617-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slope analysis for the anxiety pathway from the same survey. The low self-concept group showed βsimple = −0.098, t = −2.960, p < 0.05, CI (−0.162, −0.033); the high group's slope was βsimple = −0.233, t = −6.823.

> "For students with high English academic self-concept (M + 1SD), the negative prediction of foreign language anxiety by social support remained significant and stronger [βsimple = −0.233, t = −6.823, p < 0.001, 95% CI (−0.300, −0.166)]"
