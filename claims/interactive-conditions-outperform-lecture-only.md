## Evidence

### Sharmin Akter 2026

Sharmin Akter, Mohammad Abu Nasir Rakib, Eshwara Prasad Sridhar, Somik Biswas, Mahmudur Rahman, and Md Rassel Raihan. (2026). Post-Lecture Interactive Environments for Conceptual Learning: A Randomized Comparison of Mixed Reality and Tangible Instruction in Undergraduate STEM Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.03896](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.03896)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Planned contrasts from the ANCOVA in the pilot randomized study, Holm-corrected, comparing each interactive condition with lecture-only. Adjusted means at grand-mean baseline 6.46 were Traditional M = 6.04, ET M = 7.95, MR M = 8.27; Cohen's d values of 1.24 and 1.06 indicate large effects. The article concludes "the main difference in learning outcomes was between interactive and non-interactive instruction."

> "Mixed Reality outperformed Traditional instruction, t(20) = 2.46, p = .046, d = 1.24. Engineering Toolkit also outperformed Traditional instruction, t(20) = 2.09, p = .050, d = 1.06. The comparison between Mixed Reality and Engineering Toolkit was not statistically significant (p > .05)."
