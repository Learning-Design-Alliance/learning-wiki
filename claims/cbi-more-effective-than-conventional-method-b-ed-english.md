## Evidence

### Arulselvi 2016

Evangelin Arulselvi. (2016). The Effectiveness of Content-Based Instruction in English Language Teaching. Excellence in Education Journal, 5(2). https://www.google.com/search?q=%22The+Effectiveness+of+Content-Based+Instruction+in+English+Language+Teaching%22

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental control-group pretest–post-test study with 30 learners per group from a College of Education in Salem District; the CBI group scored M = 71 vs control M = 52.5 on Post-test I, a "significant difference between these two scores by the t-value 8.1".

> "Achievement scores of the Post-test I of the control and experimental groups were compared and there existed significant difference between these two scores by the t-value 8.1 which is significant at 0.05 level."
