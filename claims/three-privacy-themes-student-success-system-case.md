# In one institution's early-stage learning analytics implementation, inductive coding of interviews and documents surfaced three privacy themes: FERPA compliance, role-based access and security measures, and students' relationship with their data

## Evidence

### Mary Francis 2023

Mary Francis, Mejai Bola Mike Avoseh, Karen Card, Lisa Newland and Kevin Streff. (2023). Student Privacy and Learning Analytics: Investigating the Application of Privacy Within a Student Success Information System in Higher Education. Journal of Learning Analytics 10(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2023.7975

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive open coding of nine interviews with eight individuals plus 53 institutional and company documents in a single-site descriptive case study at a public university of about 2,000 FTE students. The article reports "three main themes were discovered": FERPA adherence, privacy-maintaining methods, and student-data concerns.

> "Overall, three main themes were discovered relating to the main research question: How is the concept of privacy addressed in relation to a student success information system within an institution of public higher education?"
