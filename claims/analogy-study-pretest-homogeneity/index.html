Dincer 2011¶

Dincer, S. (2011). Exploring the Impacts of Analogies on Computer Hardware. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932231

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-test on pre-test scores of the two groups (43 each). The article reports "t(84) = 0.176 (sig. = 0.861), p > 0.05" with pre-test totals of 35.69% and 36.39%; equivalence was not tested beyond this comparison.

"No significant difference was found between groups for pretest, t(84) = 0.176 (sig. = 0.861), p > 0.05, as shown in Table 3. This suggests homogeneous distribution between groups."
