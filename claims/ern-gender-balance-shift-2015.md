Descriptive analysis of questionnaire respondent demographics across the 2006–2015 editions of the European Researchers' Night, reported in the impact section. The paper states that "the number of women participation tended to be equal to that of men" in the years after 2006–2007, and in 2015 "with approximately 9 percentage points of difference in favor of women representation."

> "In 2015 the number of men and women is almost comparable, with approximately 9 percentage points of difference in favor of women representation."
