The report's framing statement about current practice: education RCTs "typically examine the intention-to-treat parameter", estimated by comparing treatment and control group mean outcomes. This is the report's characterization of the field, not a tested result.

"Randomized control trials (RCTs) in the education field typically examine the intention-to-treat parameter, which is estimated by comparing the mean outcomes of treatment group members to those of the control group."
