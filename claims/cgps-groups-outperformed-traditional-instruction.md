## Evidence

### N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls 2007

N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls. (2007). The Effect of Multiple Internal Representations on Context Rich Instruction. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.1119/1.2785190](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2785190)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Third planned comparison in the quasi-experiment (all CGPS sections, combined n=46, vs control n=15): "significant differences (p=0.0497)", replicating prior interactive engagement findings with the new measures.

> "Taking all interactive engagement CGPS groups (nCodHi, nCodMed & nCodLo) and comparing the outcomes with traditional instruct ion yields significant differences (p=0.0497) as was expected from previous findings 26."

### N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls 2007 (2)

N. Lasry & M.W. Aulls. (2007). The Effect of Multiple Internal Representations on Context Rich Instruction. Originally published 2006. [doi:10.1119/1.2785190](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2785190)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Univariate ANOVAs for all CGPS groups vs control: FCI gain p=0.004, wFCI gain p=0.001, RConf gain p=0.062, Exam p=0.217 (non-significant). The authors note the difference extends beyond FCI gains alone.

> "These data show that besides expected di fferences in FCI gains (p=0.004), significant differences were also found in weighted FCI sc ores (p=0.001) and a te ndency in right answer confidence (p=0.062) is also obs erved."
