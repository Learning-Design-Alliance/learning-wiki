## Evidence

### Michael McGuire & Jenifer Larson-Hall 2017

Michael McGuire & Jenifer Larson-Hall. (2017). Teaching Formulaic Sequences in the Classroom: Effects on Spoken Fluency. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 34(3), 1–25. http://dx.doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1271

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Correlational analysis of posttest data from all 19 participants examined whether increased FS use related to fluency. The authors call r = 0.28 a medium-size effect and r = 0.54 a large effect, concluding FS use "plays a moderate role" in fluency.

> "The correlation for all participants between FS/S and MLR is r = 0.28, and the correlation between FS/S and SR is r = 0.54."
