## Evidence

### Nüssler EK 2021

Nüssler EK, Löfgren M, Lindkvist H, Idahl A. (2021). The tension‐free vaginal tape operation: Is the inexperienced surgeon a risk factor? Learning curve and Swedish quality reference. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14033

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Register-based comparison of resource-consumption outcomes for trainee operations 1–10 and 11–50 against the experienced-surgeon reference. The discussion judges the absolute differences (blood loss 3.3 mL, stay 0.1 days) of doubtful clinical importance.

> "Both for trainee operations 1‐10 and 11‐50, the experienced surgeons' hospital stay time was shorter (0.06 days vs 0.16 days, P < .000; 0.06 days vs 0.1 days, P < .000) and perioperative blood loss was less (24.4 vs 27.7 mL, P = .001; 24.4 vs 26.5 mL, P = .004) but the number of days patients took painkillers at home was higher (3.2 vs 2.8 days, P = .011; 3.2 vs 2.9 days, P = .041)"
