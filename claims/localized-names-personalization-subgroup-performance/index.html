Murphy 2026¶

Murphy, A. (2026, July). Classroom-Based experimentation in a digital learning platform. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/306

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized field experiment within the XPRIZE Digital Learning Challenge, conducted in five school districts across five states, assigning students to Control, Personalization, or Notification name conditions in MATHia. The report found "no improvement in sense of belonging" but subgroup-majority schools showed improved performance; no effect size is printed.

"While results showed no improvement in sense of belonging, we found that for schools with the majority of their student populations identifying as from Black or American Indian/Alaska Native subgroups, these groups showed improved MATHia performance in both of the intervention conditions (Personalization and Notification) compared with the Control condition."
