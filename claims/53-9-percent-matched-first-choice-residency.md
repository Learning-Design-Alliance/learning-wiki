Retrospective cohort study of 1726 DO graduates (2010–2017) using mandatory graduation survey data with a 100% response rate. The study reports "53.9% of graduates self-reported matching into their first-choice residency" and 63% matched into low-competitiveness specialties.

> "Additionally, 53.9% of graduates self-reported matching into their first-choice residency and 63% matched into specialties that are considered low competitiveness."
