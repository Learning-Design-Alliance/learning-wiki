He 2019¶

He, W. (2019). MAP Growth K–2 item fit analysis study. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-k-2-item-fit-analysis-study/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Numerical results plotted in Figures 2.1–2.4 for rescored MAP Growth K–2 item responses (2010–2017, five states). The report states the old statistics "are more scattered than their new counterparts (red), with more values larger than 2 regardless of the sample."

"As shown in the figures, the old infit and outfit statistics (green) are more scattered than their new counterparts (red), with more values larger than 2 regardless of the sample. This suggests that the adjustment of item difficulties has helped with the improvement of the item fit."

He 2019 (2)¶

He, W. (2019). MAP Growth K–2 item fit analysis study. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/map-growth-k-2-item-fit-analysis-study/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Summary statistics in Table 2.2: for the AllYr Reading sample, mean absolute differences from 1.00 were 0.04 (new infit) and 0.06 (new outfit) versus 0.06 and 0.09 for old statistics; the same pattern held for LatestYr and Mathematics.

"Overall, these results indicate that the absolute differences between the new statistics and 1.00 are smaller than those between the old statistics and 1.00, which serves as additional evidence that item fit has been improved with the adjusted item difficulties from the scale alignment study."

Related Claims¶

  • Adjusting MAP Growth K–2 item difficulties in the 2020 scale alignment study improved item fit, with the largest gains in outfit statistics for difficult Mathematics items — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Item fit findings were the same regardless of whether the AllYr or LatestYr response sample was used — related
