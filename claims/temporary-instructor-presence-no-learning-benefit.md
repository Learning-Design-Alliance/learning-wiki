## Evidence

### Tim Kühl 2025

Tim Kühl, Felicia Teske, Martin Merkt, Christina Sondermann. (2025). Temporary instructor presence in lecture slides does not enhance online learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10435-0

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered online experiment (Prolific, N = 219 adults) randomly assigned participants to temporary, permanent, or no instructor presence on 16 narrated slides about the BCG-matrix; ANOVAs showed "no differences between conditions" for retention and transfer, with η2p < 0.003.

> "Concerning retention as well as transfer, one-factorial ANOVAs showed no differences between conditions, both Fs < 1, both ps > 0.729, both η2p < 0.003."
