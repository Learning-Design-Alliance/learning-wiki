## Evidence

### Andrew J. Mason 2014

Andrew J. Mason. (2014). Potential relationship of chosen major to problem solving attitudes and course performance. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.049](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.049)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Pre-post CLASS survey analysis of 69 students (23 Framework, 25 Performance, 21 Vague) across two semesters, reported in Table II. Framework students' normalized gains were +0.06 total, +0.25 PS-G, +0.25 PS-C, +0.07 PS-S; Performance gains were negative (e.g., -0.14 PS-S) and Vague gains were negative (e.g., -0.33 PS-S).

> "students who focus on aspects of a problem solving framework during the lab exercise generally gain a benefit in attitudes towards problem solving overall"
