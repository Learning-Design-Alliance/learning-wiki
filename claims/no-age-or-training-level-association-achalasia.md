## Evidence

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square comparison across age groups in the 675-physician survey; no statistically significant difference, and equivalence was not tested. The authors note professional experience beyond age groups was not evaluated.

> "Statistical analysis indicated no significant association between age and achalasia knowledge (P = .784), suggesting that awareness is generally moderate to high among physicians regardless of age."

### Eroğlu Haktanır A 2026 (2)

Eroğlu Haktanır A, Çelebi A. (2026). Achalasia: Physician practices and knowledge in Türkiye. Turk J Gastroenterol. https://doi.org/10.5152/tjg.2026.25713

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square comparison across hospital levels; treatment knowledge was also comparable (83%-88%, P = .512), which the authors interpret as uniform exposure to training and treatment information.

> "No significant differences were observed in achalasia training participation across hospital levels (P = .437), with similar proportions of physicians reporting full, partial, or no training."
