## Evidence

### Gerhardt-Szep Susanne 2016

Gerhardt-Szep Susanne, Kunkel Florian, Moeltner Andreas, Hansen Miriam, Böckers Anja, Rüttermann Stefan, Ochsendorf Falk. (2016). Evaluating differently tutored groups in problem-based learning in a German dental curriculum: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0505-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-model analysis of pre/post multiple-choice test scores in the randomized two-group study; equivalence was not tested. Groups were comparable at pre-test (16.30 vs 15.82 points, p = 0.590).

> "Comparing the post-PBL results in the two tutoring styles (nf = 22.2 correct answers, f = 20.5 correct answers) no significant difference could be observed (p = 0.08)."

### Gerhardt-Szep Susanne 2016 (2)

Gerhardt-Szep Susanne, Kunkel Florian, Moeltner Andreas, Hansen Miriam, Böckers Anja, Rüttermann Stefan, Ochsendorf Falk. (2016). Evaluating differently tutored groups in problem-based learning in a German dental curriculum: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0505-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Overall pre-post comparison across the study population in the quantitative part; the post-PBL test was significantly better than the pre-PBL test overall (p ≤ 0.0001). No effect size printed.

> "The students reached a mean score of 16.05 ± 4.49 points in the pre-PBL test, and a mean of 21.38 ± 4.69 points computed for the post-PBL test (p ≤ 0.0001)."
