### Najjuma Josephine N. 2024

Barriers and facilitators to interprofessional simulation-based learning in a Ugandan medical school: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06521-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis found "Students felt that the lack of academic recognition for simulation participation could lead to reduced motivation and attendance." A student noted logbook simulation marks "were not even accounting for the final mark," and asked that awarding of marks be laid down clearly.

> "Students felt that the lack of academic recognition for simulation participation could lead to reduced motivation and attendance. There is a need for clear marking guidelines."
