Evidence¶

Maxwell 2014¶

Maxwell, B., Connolly, P., Demack, S., O'Hare, L., Stevens, A., & Clague, L. (2014). TextNow Transition Programme: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581131

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation combining online surveys of coordinators (primary 64%, secondary 68% response) and coaches, plus four pupil focus groups with 22 intervention pupils, analysed using descriptive statistics and thematic analysis; findings are the evaluators' interpretive conclusions about implementation, not a tested causal comparison.

"The programme appeared to be more effective when secondary schools worked closely with feeder primaries to co-ordinate all elements of the programme. In addition, more effective coaches were highly trained , enthusiastic and committed."
