## Evidence

### Daniel P. Kelly and Teomara Rutherford 2017

Daniel P. Kelly and Teomara Rutherford. (2017). Khan Academy as Supplemental Instruction: A Controlled Study of a Computer-Based Mathematics Intervention. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 18(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1146230

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-test comparing the Khan Academy treatment class with the traditional math supplemental class within the same quasi-experimental study of 114 seventh graders. The article reports "no difference in test scores" between the two supplemental formats.

> "In comparing the Khan Academy class and traditional math supplemental class, there was no difference in test scores , t(60) = -1.009, p = .842."
