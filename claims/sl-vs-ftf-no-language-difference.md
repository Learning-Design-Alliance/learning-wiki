## Evidence

### Mercedes Rico García and Paula Ferreira da Silva 2017

Mercedes Rico García and Paula Ferreira da Silva. (2017). Acquisition of Language and Intercultural Competences in Tourism and Hospitality Studies through Active Experimentation in Second Life. Teaching English with Technology, 18(2), 69-92. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1177703

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test (Phase 2) comparing 36 SL students with 36 in-class students on language used in role-play transcripts; the article reports p>0.05 and mean values of "6, 10 versus 6, 55 in the case of the experimental group". Equivalence was not tested.

> "Table 2 shows that at a 0.05 level of significance the t-test does not support hypothesis 1 (p>0.05), that is, there is no significant differ ence in the linguistic performance – language used to perform the interaction in the field of tourism between both groups."
