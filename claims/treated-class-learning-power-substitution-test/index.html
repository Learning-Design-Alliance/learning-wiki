Emma Kohnky 1913¶

Emma Kohnky, M.A. (1913). Preliminary Study of the Effect of Dental Treatment upon the Physical and Mental Efficiency of School Children. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Results table for the substitution test (Whipple's Test 37), measuring time needed to approximate 100% accuracy. Room 21 needed 206.5" in October and 104.9" in May on the key sheets; Room 18 needed 201.6" and 110.5". Descriptive medians; no significance test is printed.

"Room 21 leads, with a learning power expressed by a saving of 101.6", compared with 91.1" in Room 18."
