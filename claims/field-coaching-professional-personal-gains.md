## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Conventional content analysis of post-coaching interviews identified professional growth (communication skills, practice-guideline knowledge, ACP as a clinical communication tool, information-provider and coordinator roles) and personal gains (appreciation of the health-illness trajectory, urgency to reflect on end-of-life planning, preparedness for an aging population). `q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative conventional content analysis (Hsieh and Shannon) of semi-structured post-coaching interviews with 9 nurses, 2 superintendents, and 2 social workers, analyzed in MAXQDA 2020. The article reports codes for "communication skills on EOL topics" and integration of ACP as an EOL communication tool. No effect size printed.

> "Codes under the professional growth category comprised the communication skills on EOL topics, knowledge on the ACP process and relevant practice guidelines, rethinking of nurses' role in providing EOL care, and the integration of ACP as an EOL communication tool in their practice."
