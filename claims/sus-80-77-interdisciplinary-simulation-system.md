# Fourth-year medical students rated the interdisciplinary simulation system 'excellent' on a modified SUS (mean 80.77)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Clinical medical students (N=71) gave the system a mean modified SUS score of 80.77, above the 68-point industry standard and in the 'excellent' (80+) range. [→ Qiu Kangli 2025](#qiu-kangli-2025)

## Evidence

### Qiu Kangli 2025

Qiu Kangli, Zeng Tianshu, Xia Wenfang, Peng Miaomiao, Kong Wen. (2025). Interdisciplinary medical education practices: building a case-driven interdisciplinary simulation system based on public datasets. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07631-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Modified SUS questionnaire interviews with 71 fourth-year clinical medical students at Tongji Medical College in 2024. The mean was 80.77 with a standard deviation of 7.34, a median of 80, and 'approximately 65% of the sample scored within the 75–85 point range, with no scores below 70.'

> "the overall performance of the interdisciplinary simulation practice system was found to be excellent, with an average score of 80.77 (out of 100), significantly surpassing the SUS standard average of 68 points and achieving an "excellent" rating (80+)"
