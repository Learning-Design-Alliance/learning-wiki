Huhn D., Lauter J., Roesch Ely D., Koch E., Möltner A., Herzog W., Resch F., Herpertz S. C., Nikendei C. (2017). Performance of International Medical Students In psychosocial medicine. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0950-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of the accompanying 48-item multiple-choice examination (data for 995 of the initial 1033 students) showed "no significant differences between groups" (p = .127), suggesting the OSCE gap is not due to poorer factual knowledge. No effect size interpreted; equivalence was not tested.

> "The analysis of variance for the performance in the written examination showed no significant differences between groups (F(2, 1030) = 2.070: p = .127; η 2 = .004) (see Table 2)."
