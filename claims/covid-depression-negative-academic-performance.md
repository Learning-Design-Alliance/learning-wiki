## Evidence

### Jiang Zuopeng 2022

Jiang Zuopeng, Jia Xuhong, Tao Ran, Dördüncü Hazar. (2022). COVID-19: A Source of Stress and Depression Among University Students and Poor Academic Performance. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2022.898556

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the SEM of the student survey, the depression-to-academic-performance path was negative and significant: "a −0.332 beta value and 0.001 p-values". The authors interpret that the more depressed a student is, the more academic performance suffers.

> "The structural analysis indicated that academic performance is negatively related to students' depression with a −0.332 beta value and 0.001 p-values."
