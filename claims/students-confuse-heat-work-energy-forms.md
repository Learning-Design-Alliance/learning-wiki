### A. Ercoli 2023

A. Ercoli, V. Lubicz. (2023). Thermodynamics and microscopic theory: An educational proposal for the high school. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2310.20505](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.20505)

The authors' expert observation from teaching experience in Italian high schools: students' physical contents of thermodynamics are poorly understood, with concepts often unclear or erroneous. The article offers no empirical data for this assertion.

> "Heat and work are not forms of energy, which, as such, are possessed by the physical system . Rather, they are ways through which energy is exchanged. This concept, if not prop erly assimilated, can easily hinder the understanding of the first law"
