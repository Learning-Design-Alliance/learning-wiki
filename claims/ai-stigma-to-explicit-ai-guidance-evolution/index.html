Evidence¶

Jennie Ren 2026¶

Jennie Ren, Jordan H. McDowell, and Kyrie Zhixuan Zhou. (2026). Beyond Perspectives: A Trio-Ethnography of Interpretation Evolution in LLM-Supported Programming Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22463 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.22463

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative analysis of three recorded trio-ethnographic conversations (two educators, one undergraduate CS student) found this interpretive evolution; the article reports "an emergingAI stigma" reframed as shaped by instructional ambiguity, then explicit guidance. No effect sizes are reported.

"The educators' interpretations of students' AI use evolved from identifying an emergingAI stigma, to recognizing the importance of AI usage transparency, and ultimately to emphasizing explicit guidance for appropriate AI use."
