## Evidence

### Rupp Robert 2025

Rupp Robert, Wallmann-Sperlich Birgit, Bucksch Jens. (2025). Strategies and barriers to implementing physically active teaching in universities from the perspective of lecturers: a qualitative study. BMC Public Health 25, Article 855. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-22075-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative interview finding: lecturers noted the three typical unintentional interruptions (group work, presenting, blackboard writing) occur rarely, activate only individual students, or generate very low activity. One lecturer observed that "the students stay in their seats and just turn their chairs" during group work.

> "the central physically activating teaching method in university courses (group work) often turns out to be just another reason for sitting, which hardly contributes to the interruption of sitting / physical activation."
