The abstract reports this mediation result from multilevel models in which academic orientation (Grade 6) was modeled as a class-level mediator between class mean interest (Grade 5) and individual interest (Grade 7): "Academic orientation mediated the effect of class mean interest on subsequent individual interest in German." No effect size is printed in the available text.
