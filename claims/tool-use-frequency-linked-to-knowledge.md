### Albasha Neah 2025

Assessing physical therapists' knowledge in fall prevention among older adults in Saudi arabia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Geriatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-06668-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal–Wallis analysis in the cross-sectional survey (N = 384) found a significant association between guideline/tool use frequency and knowledge scores, with ε² = 0.128. Table 6 shows median scores of 69.0 for 'always' users versus 56.5 for 'never' users, a dose–response pattern.

> "In contrast, a significant association was observed between the frequency of guideline or tool usage and knowledge scores (H = 52.36, p < 0.001, ε² = 0.128)."
