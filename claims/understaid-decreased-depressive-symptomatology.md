## Subclaims

`q3 i?` In the Nunez-Naveira et al. RCT, caregivers in the experimental group significantly decreased their depressive symptomatology after using understAID, though only 33.3% were satisfied with the application. [→ Klimova Blanka 2019](#klimova-blanka-2019)

`q3 i?` Experimental-group caregivers showed a statistically significant pre-to-post decrease in CES-D depressive symptomatology after three months of using understAID. [→ Núñez-Naveira L. 2016](#nunez-naveira-l-2016)

## Evidence

### Klimova Blanka 2019

Klimova Blanka, Valis Martin, Kuca Kamil, Masopust Jiri. (2019). E-learning as valuable caregivers’ support for people with dementia – A systematic review. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-019-4641-9

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Pilot randomized controlled trial of 61 informal carers (30 experimental, 31 control) using the understAID smartphone/tablet e-learning platform for 3 months, measured with the CES-D and a self-completion questionnaire; the review reports the printed satisfaction percentages.

> "After using understAID the caregivers in the experimental group significantly decreased their depressive symptomatology; overall, 33.3% of the caregivers were satisfied with the application and approximately 50% of the participants assessed it as technically and pedagogically acceptable."

### Núñez-Naveira L. 2016

Núñez-Naveira L., Alonso-Búa B., de Labra C., Gregersen R., Maibom K., Mojs E., Krawczyk-Wasielewska A., Millán-Calenti J. C. (2016). UnderstAID, an ICT Platform to Help Informal Caregivers of People with Dementia: A Pilot Randomized Controlled Study. BioMed Research International. https://doi.org/10.1155/2016/5726465

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot randomized controlled pre-post trial; paired-sample comparison within the experimental group (n = 30 completers) after a three-month intervention. The article reports "statistically significant differences for the CES-D score" with a decrease in depressive symptomatology; no effect size is printed.

> "In the experimental group, there were statistically significant differences for the CES-D score pre- versus postintervention (19.40 ± 9.03 versus 17.03 ± 7.07, P = 0.037), with a decrease in the observed depressive symptomatology."
