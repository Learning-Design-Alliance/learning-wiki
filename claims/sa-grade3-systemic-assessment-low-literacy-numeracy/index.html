Evidence¶

Ona Janse van Rensburg 2015¶

Ona Janse van Rensburg. (2015). An intervention Programme for Promoting More Effective Learning among Foundation Phase Learners in South Africa. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, Vol. 4, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v4n2p110

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The article reports the 2001 systemic assessment of Grade 3 learners, whose sample consisted of 108 000 learners (approximately 5% of the population), as evidence that about half of learners had not learned effectively.

"The findings were disappointing: the national average for Lite racy was only 39%; for Nume racy it was as low as 30% (Dept. of Education, 2002a)."

Ona Janse van Rensburg 2015 (2)¶

Ona Janse van Rensburg. (2015). An intervention Programme for Promoting More Effective Learning among Foundation Phase Learners in South Africa. Journal of Curriculum and Teaching, Vol. 4, No. 2. https://doi.org/10.5430/jct.v4n2p110

q2 · i? · causal · r1

The article reports the 2007 systemic assessment results as showing that scores "stay unacceptably low", which the author takes as evidence of a crisis in South Africa's educational system.

"A next systemic assessment was done in 2007. The average score for Numeracy was 35% and 36% for Literacy (Dept. of Education, 2013)."
