### Sánchez-Hernando 2022

Sánchez-Hernando, B., Gasch-Gallén, Á., Antón-Solanas, I., Gea-Caballero, V., Juárez-Vela, R., Gállego-Diéguez, J., Carboneres-Tafaner, M. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.13041

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Life skills comparison in the same survey, measured with an adapted questionnaire validated by an expert panel; the self-efficacy subscale has 10 items on a four-point Likert scale. The Results report that "Students attending non-HPS demonstrated a higher level of self-efficacy than students attending HPS (p = 0.0019)".
