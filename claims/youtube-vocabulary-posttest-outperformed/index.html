Evidence¶

Kabooha and Elyas 2018¶

Raniah Kabooha & Tariq Elyas. (2018). The Effects of YouTube in Multimedia Instruction for Vocabulary Learning: Perceptions of EFL Students and Teachers. English Language Teaching, 11(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v11n2p72

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-sample t-test comparing post-test scores of the experimental group (75 students who watched YouTube) and control group (25 students) after a seven-week module. The article reports the experimental group 'were significantly higher' with t(98)=7.515, p<0.05; no effect size is printed.

"the post-test scores of the students in the experimental group (M= 46.5, SD= 8.6) were significantly higher ( t (98) = 7.515, p< 0.05) when compared to the scores of the students in the control group (M= 31.3, SD= 9.2)"
