## Evidence

### Cardile Davide 2023

Cardile Davide, Ielo Augusto, Corallo Francesco, Cappadona Irene, D'Aleo Giangaetano, De Cola Maria Cristina, Bramanti Placido, Ciurleo Rosella. (2023). Communication Training: Significance and Effects of a Preliminary Psychological Intervention upon an Audit Team. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054173

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post administration of the 10-item General Self-Efficacy Scale to the 10-member audit team before and after the training course. The median rose from 29.5 to 36.5; the article reports a statistically significant difference between T0 and T1 but prints no effect size.

> "GSE test scores covered a range from 27 to 32, with a median value of 29.5 (29.0 for males and 30.5 for females) at T0, and a range from 34 to 38, with a median value of 36.5 (35.5 for males and 37.5 for females) at T1, as shown in Figure 4."
