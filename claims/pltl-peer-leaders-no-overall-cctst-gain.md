## Evidence

### Snyder Julia J. 2015

Snyder Julia J., Wiles Jason R. (2015). Peer Led Team Learning in Introductory Biology: Effects on Peer Leader Critical Thinking Skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115084

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study of PLTL peer leaders in introductory biology at a large private research university; CCTST administered at semester start and end. The mean pre-test was 18.46 and post-test 18.76 (n = 37), "indicating an average gain of 0.30"; the RM ANOVA was non-significant with partial eta squared = 0.008.

> "A one-way repeated measures ANOVA was conducted to compare CCTST pre-test and post-test scores, and no significant difference was observed for critical thinking skills of undergraduate peer leaders participating in the PLTL training and leadership experience, F(1, 36) = 0.290, p = 0.593, power = 0.082, partial eta squared = 0.008."
