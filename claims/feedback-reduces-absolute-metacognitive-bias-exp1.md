## Evidence

### Ceri Ngai and Sam J. Gilbert 2026

Ceri Ngai and Sam J. Gilbert (2026). Metacognitive training facilitates optimal cognitive offloading. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-026-00714-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Additional non-pre-registered ANOVA on absolute metacognitive bias in Experiment 1 (N = 164). The feedback group was "significantly less biased than the no-feedback group" at pre-task (d = 0.59) and post-task (d = 0.47).

> "we observed a significant main effect of Group (F(1, 162) = 17.6, p < .001, η2ₚ = .098) while the Group x Timepoint interaction was not significant (F(1, 162) = 3.68, p = .06, η2ₚ = .022)."
