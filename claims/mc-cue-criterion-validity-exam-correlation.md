## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2013

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2013). Multiple-choice Assessment for Upper-division Electricity and Magnetism. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.079)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Criterion-validity analysis in the E&M 1 course, correlating CUE scores with two midterms and a final exam. MC: r = 0.78; FR: r = 0.75; both p < 0.05. Correlations with total course score were MC r = 0.78, FR r = 0.68.

> "The MC CUE scores correlate strongly with aggregate exam scores (r=0.78, p < 0.05). For comparison, the correlation for the 25 students who took the FR version was also high (r = 0.75, p < 0.05)."
