Proficiency projection analysis (Table 3.4) from the Minnesota MCA-III Mathematics linking study, computing probabilities via the standard normal cumulative distribution function using expected growth and the conditional standard deviation of growth. The article prints that a fall RIT of 202 carries "a 97% chance of reaching Meets proficiency or higher."
