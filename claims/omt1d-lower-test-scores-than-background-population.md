## Evidence

### Spangmose Anne Lærke 2022

Spangmose Anne Lærke, Skipper Niels, Knorr Sine, Wullum Gundersen Tina, Beck Jensen Rikke, Damm Peter, Lykke Mortensen Erik, Pinborg Anja, Svensson Jannet, Clausen Tine. (2022). School performance in Danish children exposed to maternal type 1 diabetes in utero: A nationwide retrospective cohort study. PLoS Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1003977

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Nationwide retrospective register-based cohort of Danish public-school children (1,704,447 test scores, 2010–2016), multiple linear regression with cluster-robust standard errors and school fixed effects. Adjusted mean difference for O-mT1D versus O-BP was "−1.59 [95% CI −2.48 to −0.71, p < 0.001] (Cohen's d = −0.06)".

> "After adjusting for basic and socioeconomic covariates, O-mT1D and O-fT1D had lower mean test scores than O-BP (mean differences −1.59 [95% CI −2.48 to −0.71, p < 0.001] (Cohen's d = −0.06) and −0.78 [95% CI −1.48 to −0.08, p = 0.03] (Cohen's d = −0.03), respectively)"
