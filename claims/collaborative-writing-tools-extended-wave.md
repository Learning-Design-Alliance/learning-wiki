## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Collaborative writing tools of around 1990 (e.g., Collaborative Writer, DIScourse, Aspects, Group Writer) provided shared spaces for knowledge generation and document editing, and although none of that first wave still exists, they led indirectly or directly to tools such as Slack, Google Docs, and Microsoft Teams.
