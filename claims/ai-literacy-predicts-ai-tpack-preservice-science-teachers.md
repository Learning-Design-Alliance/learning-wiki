## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In a PLS-SEM analysis of 548 pre-service science teachers, AI literacy significantly and positively predicted AI-TPACK (β = 0.570), with a large f² effect size of 0.482. [→ Zou J 2026](#zou-j-2026)

## Evidence

### Zou J 2026

Zou J, Li N, Wang X and Du J (2026) From AI literacy to AI-integrated inquiry-based science teaching: the serial mediating roles of AI-TPACK and science teaching self-efficacy among Chinese pre-service science teachers. Front. Psychol. 17:1911909. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1911909

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 548 Chinese pre-service science teachers analyzed with PLS-SEM; the direct path from AI literacy to AI-TPACK was significant, "β = 0.570, 95% CI [0.513, 0.624]". The article reports f² = 0.482, interpreted as a large effect.
