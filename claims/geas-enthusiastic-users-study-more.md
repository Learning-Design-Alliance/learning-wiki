`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

An observational usage analysis of a distance-learning cohort divided by post-semester ratings of the tutor: 9% rated it acceptable, 41% better than average, and 50% one of the best study tools they had seen. The figure caption reports the better-than-average group answered 102 questions per week on average versus an additional 16 per week for the most enthusiastic group; no standardized effect size is reported.
