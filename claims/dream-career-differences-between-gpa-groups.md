Univariate comparison of academic-related parameters in the questionnaire study found the AA-group "significantly more likely to choose the doctor career (27.5% vs. 9.9%; p-value < 0.001)" and less likely to choose an artist career. The authors attribute this to GPA being science and mathematics-based.

> "The respondents in the AA-group were significantly more likely to choose the doctor career (27.5% vs. 9.9%; p-value < 0.001) and significantly less likely to choose the artist career (16.7% vs. 29.7%; p-value = 0.03) as their dream career."
