Evidence¶

Roembke Tanja C. 2019¶

Roembke Tanja C., Hazeltine Eliot, Reed Deborah K., McMurray Bob. (2019). Automaticity of Word Recognition is a Unique Predictor of Reading Fluency in Middle-School Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000279

q3 · i0 · associational · r2

Hierarchical regressions on decoding (WRMT) and comprehension (GMRT) in the same sample. The article reports masked versions "captured extremely small amounts of variance" (ΔR2 = 0.001 to 0.002), "none of which were significant", and comprehension was predicted only by vocabulary (ΔR2 = 0.242).

"All of the masked versions captured extremely small amounts of variance (masked Find the Picture: ΔR2= 0.001; masked Find the Rhyme: ΔR2 < 0.001; masked Verify: ΔR2 = 0.002), none of which were significant."
