## Evidence

### Adam Sagal Mohamed 2024

Adam Sagal Mohamed, Teshoma Melese Sinaga, Ahmed Awale Sh. Dahir, Tamiru Dessalegn. (2024). Household food insecurity and its association with academic performance among primary school adolescents in Hargeisa City, Somaliland. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0303034

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable logistic regression in the cross-sectional study (n = 630) found absenteeism (any illegal absence of at least one day in the previous semester) significantly associated with academic performance, with "AOR = 3.49, 95%CI = 2.20–5.53".

> "School absenteeism was another factor that was significantly associated with academic performance. Adolescents who were absent from school had poor academic performance as compared to their counterparts."
