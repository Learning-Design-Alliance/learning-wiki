`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Gender comparison within the same quasi-experimental study of 165 SS2 chemistry students; the article reports "Females had higher retention scor e (41.75 ± 3.43) than their male counterparts (39.7 4 ± 3.87)" with a significant main effect of gender (Table 2: F = 10.470, p = .001).

> "Females had higher retention scor e (41.75 ± 3.43) than their male counterparts (39.7 4 ± 3.87). There was statistically significant main effect (p < 0.05) of gender on students’ retention."
