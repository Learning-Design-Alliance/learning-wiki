Narrative and interpretive synthesis of four included studies of simulation-based undergraduate psychiatry education. The review reports that structured checklists and OSCE-based rating systems were the most commonly reported assessment methods, with improvements in communication performance and interviewing skills frequently reported outcomes.

> "Behavioural competence was the most consistently represented domain across studies. Assessment approaches focused primarily on observable interviewing behaviours, including symptom exploration, information gathering, communication techniques and interview organisation."
