## Evidence

### Agláe Kellerer 2016

Agláe Kellerer, Sidney Wright, Sylvestre Lacour. (2016). Coherence and information in a fiber interferometer. [doi:10.1119/1.4964358](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4964358)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Student lab-report measurement of the SLD spectrum via Fourier analysis of fringe contrast versus path-length difference in the fibered Mach-Zehnder; the report finds values "in agreement with the manufacturer test values" and attributes the ripple to remnants of lasing effect in the SLD cavity.

> "The spectrum gives a 3 dB bandwidth of 60 ± 8 nm and a large scale spectral ripple wavelength of 1 .8± 0.2 nm. These are in agreement with the manufacturer test values of 66.4 nm and ∼ 2 nm respectively."

### Agláe Kellerer 2016 (2)

Agláe Kellerer, Sidney Wright, Sylvestre Lacour. (2016). Coherence and information in a fiber interferometer. [doi:10.1119/1.4964358](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4964358)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Fabry-Perot laser spectrum obtained by FTS in the student project; the cavity length inferred from peak spacing in the derived spectrum is reported with its uncertainty and compared to the printed manufacturer specification.

> "In this particular case, the student obtained a cavity length of l = 1.02± 0.03 mm, close to the manufacturer speciﬁcations of approximately 1.07 mm."
