## Evidence

### Singh 2016

Singh, C. and Schunn, C. D. (2016). Connecting Three Pivotal Concepts in K-12 Science State Standards and Maps of Conceptual Growth to Research in Physics Education. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.06024](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.06024)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article describes explorations in which students first predict (e.g., whether the hit force still acts on a baseball), then push sliders on a frictionless air track, record velocity and acceleration with motion sensors, and interpret graphs showing zero acceleration despite different initial forces. The article states these explorations "greatly improve student understanding"; no effect size is printed.

> "Singh has developed several explorations (Singh, 2000; 2002) that greatly improve student understanding of concepts related to force and motion. All the explorations begin by asking students to predict what should happen"
