## Evidence

### Altıntaş Elif Defne 2026

Altıntaş Elif Defne, Kurt Şebnem Sarıoğlu, Özatik Şebnem, Öztürk Ayşegül. (2026). Assessment of health technician students’ awareness of dental trauma in Turkey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08607-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Post-hoc grouping of the 333-student survey by self-reported avulsion knowledge (Yes n=70; No n=131; I don't know n=132), compared with chi-square/Fisher-Freeman-Halton tests. The quoted contamination contrast and the fractured-tooth contrast (p = 0.025) were both significant (Table 5).

> "The proportion of correct answers was significantly higher among students who indicated they had knowledge (48 out of 70; 68.6%) compared to those who answered No (48 out of 131; 36.6%) or I don’t know (50 out of 132; 37.9%) (p = 0.001)."
