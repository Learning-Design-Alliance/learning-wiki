Dromey Brian P. MBChB 2021¶

Dromey Brian P. MBChB, MRCOG; Peebles Donald M. MBBS, MD, MRCOG; Stoyanov Danail V. PhD. (2021). A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of the Use of High-Fidelity Simulation in Obstetric Ultrasound. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000485

Quantitative synthesis of 4 pretest/posttest simulator studies: 214 participants recruited, 129 novices (56%); "All 4 studies reported positive effect on operator performance." Improvements were in CRL (3 studies) and FL (2 studies), seen regardless of simulator model.
