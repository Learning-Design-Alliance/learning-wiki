Turn-level chi-squared analysis of 14,637 paired turns linking each response's scaffolding level to the student's next dialogue act; the article notes the turn-level analyses do not model within-student dependence.

"The distribution of students’ next dialogue acts differs across scaffolding levels ( χ2 = 1,092.6 ,df= 28,p<.001,V=.137; Table 3)."

Suhyeon Lee 2026 (2)¶

Suhyeon Lee, Juneha Baek, Jaehyeong Park, Donghyuk Shin. (2026). LLM Pedagogical Behavior in AI Tutoring Interactions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22993

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Within-dialogue-act stratified analyses (Table 10) repeating the association test inside each request category; the largest contrast was provide-context turns after 16.4% of L4 versus 7.3% of L3 responses among writing requests.

"The association remains statistically significant within all four broad current student dialogue-act categories examined (all p < .01). The pattern therefore persists beyond broad differences in the type of request preceding the LLM response."
