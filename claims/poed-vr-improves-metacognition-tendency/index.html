Evidence¶

Kuan‑Fu Chen 2025¶

Kuan‑Fu Chen, Gwo‑Jen Hwang, Mei‑Rong Alice Chen. (2025). Precision diagnosis in virtual learning contexts: a predict‑observe‑explain‑diagnose‑based approach to scientific inquiry. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10527-5

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

One-way ANCOVA on a five-item 5-point Likert metacognition tendency questionnaire adapted from Lai and Hwang (2014), with pre-questionnaire as covariate. The article reports "the experimental group significantly outperformed the control group (F = 5.27, p = 0.03, η2 = 0.09)".

"The results showed that the experimental group significantly outperformed the control group (F = 5.27, p = 0.03, η2 = 0.09), specifying that the POED-based VR approach could effectively improve students' metacognition tendency."
