## Evidence

### Venegas Carolina Lavin 2019

Venegas Carolina Lavin, Nkangu Miriam N., Duffy Melissa C., Fergusson Dean A., Spilg Edward G. (2019). Interventions to improve resilience in physicians who have completed training: A systematic review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210512

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA-reported) of resilience interventions for physicians who have completed training; 7,579 records screened, 17 studies included, 9 with extractable physician data. The review concludes "there is weak evidence to support one intervention over another".

> "Our systematic review demonstrates that there is weak evidence to support one intervention over another to improve resilience in physicians who have completed training. The quality of evidence for the outcomes ranged from very low to low."
