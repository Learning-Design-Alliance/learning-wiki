## Evidence

### Chan Bibiana C 2013

Chan Bibiana C, Jayasinghe Upali W, Christl Bettina, Laws Rachel A, Orr Neil, Williams Anna, Partington Kate, Harris Mark F. (2013). The impact of a team-based intervention on the lifestyle risk factor management practices of community nurses: outcomes of the community nursing SNAP trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-54

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of survey data from the Community Nursing SNAP trial (four services, quasi-experimental, baseline/6/12 months) found "positive associations between the intervention group and increased frequencies in assessment" for these risk factors; the 12-month nutrition effect size was d = 1.12 in Table 2.

> "There were positive associations between the intervention group and increased frequencies in assessment for nutrition, alcohol and weight."
