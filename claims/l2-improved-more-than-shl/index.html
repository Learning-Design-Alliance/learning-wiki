Evidence¶

Barros García 2018¶

Barros García, M. J., & Bachelor, J. W. (2018). Pragmatic Instruction May Not Be Necessary Among Heritage Learners of Spanish: A Study on Requests. Journal of Foreign Language Education and Technology, 3(1). https://jflet.com/jflet/

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Between-group comparison of pre-to-post growth percentages across the SHL group and the L2 experimental group, computed from the paired t test results. The article reports differences of "14.73% more" (written) and "5.84% more" (oral); no inferential test or effect size is printed for this contrast.

"This indicates that the L2 experimental group (group 2) improved 14.73% more than the SHL group on the written DCT and by 5.84% more on the oral DCT, thus suggesting that the pragmatic interventions helped the L2 group significantly more than the SHL group."
