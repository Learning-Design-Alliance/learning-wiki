# The four-dimensional subjective-experience ratings are validated against classic insight and analytic problems, with Intuition and Suddenness differentiating the problem categories

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Intuition and Suddenness reliably differentiated non-computerized insight from analytic problems, while Pleasure did not differentiate them. [→ Adam Chuderski 2021](#adam-chuderski-2021)

## Evidence

### Adam Chuderski 2021

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Validation comparing ratings after 244 correct solutions on interactive insight problems and 168 correct solutions on paper-and-pencil analytic problems. Suddenness also distinguished the categories (BF10 > 999 both ways); Pleasure always exceeded 10 and did not differentiate the problems.

> "Intuition did validly differentiate the problem categories, reliably exceeding the criterion value 10 for the insight problems, BF 10 = 86.07, but falling below 10 for the analytic problems, BF 10 > 999."
