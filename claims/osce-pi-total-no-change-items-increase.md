## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among 48 occupational therapy students, self-reported professional identity total scores showed no significant pre-post change, while most self-reported items (e.g., choosing occupational therapy again, improving skills) increased significantly. ## Evidence

### Sarsak Hassan Izzeddin 2025

Sarsak Hassan Izzeddin. (2025). Professional Identity and Self-efficacy Among Occupational Therapy Students Engaged in Objective Structured Clinical Examination Utilizing Simulation Learning. Inquiry: A Journal of Medical Care Organization, Provision and Financing. https://doi.org/10.1177/00469580251315351

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post Wilcoxon signed-rank test analysis of 48 occupational therapy students. The article reports "no significant changes in the total scores" of professional identity, while items such as "If I had to choose a job again" (Z = −2.22, P < .01) increased significantly.

> "There were no significant changes in the total scores of the professional identity before and after the OSCE. However, significant changes were seen in most items, such as “If I had to choose a job again, I would choose occupational therapy again” (Z = −2.22, P < .01) and “I want to improve my occupational therapy skills” (Z = −4.62, P < .01)"
