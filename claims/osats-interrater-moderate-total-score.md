## Evidence

### Chartrand G 2021

Chartrand G, Soucisse M, Dubé P, Trépanier J-S, Drolet P, Sideris L. (2021). Self-directed learning by video as a means to improve technical skills in surgery residents: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02524-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Interrater reliability analysis in the RCT: two blinded attending surgeons independently scored all filmed anastomoses. The printed correlation is "r = 0.58 (95% CI 0.34–0.75, P < 0.0001)", lower than the near-0.8 values reported in prior literature the authors cite.

> "There was a statistically significant correlation between the overall score (the tally of each OSATS category) between the two evaluators, r = 0.58 (95% CI 0.34–0.75, P < 0.0001)"
