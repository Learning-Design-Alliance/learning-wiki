## Evidence

### Pettinger Katherine J 2019

Pettinger Katherine J, Kelly Brian, Sheldon Trevor A, Mon-Williams Mark, Wright John, Hill Liam J B. (2019). Starting school: educational development as a function of age of entry and prematurity. Archives of Disease in Childhood, 105(2), 160-165. https://doi.org/10.1136/archdischild-2019-317124

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Hierarchical logistic regression of the Born in Bradford cohort (n=10 337 children entering school in the expected academic year) showed gestational age was a significant predictor of failing to attain a GLD (χ2(1)=651.7, p<0.001), persisting after controlling for academic month of birth (χ2(1)=47.2, p<0.001). The article reports "their odds of not attaining a GLD increased by 1.09 (95% CI 1.06 to 1.11)" per week born early.

> "ORs indicated that for each successive week that a child was born early, their odds of not attaining a GLD increased by 1.09 (95% CI 1.06 to 1.11)."
