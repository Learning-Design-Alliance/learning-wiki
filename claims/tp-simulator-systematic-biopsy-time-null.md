## Evidence

### Ong Kelly 2023

Ong Kelly, Bald Philip, Dryhurst David, Ahmed Saif, Yusuf Gibran Tim, Lunawat Rahul, Sriprasad Seshadri, Thapa Gauri, Tirnoveanu Alice, Webb Richard, Rahman Eqram. (2023). The design and validation of a low-cost trans perineal (TP) prostate biopsy simulator for training: improving trainees’ confidence and cognitive targeting skills. World Journal of Urology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-023-04387-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the construct validity study, participants were tasked with obtaining 12 template biopsies and the time taken was recorded. Experts averaged 561.3 s and novices 847.8 s, but "There was no significant difference between the two groups (p = 0.188)"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size was printed.

> "The average time taken for the expert group to complete their systematic biopsy was 561.3 s (340–942 s). The average time taken for the novice group to complete their systematic biopsy was 847.8 s (517–1217 s). There was no significant difference between the two groups (p = 0.188)."
