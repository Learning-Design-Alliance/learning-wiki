`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Second interview question asked about difficulties and obstacles facing tablet and e-textbook use. The administration listed teacher workload and pressure, batteries needing charging every few hours, forgotten tablets, technical problems, students using games or the camera during instruction, poor software-company cooperation, and some lessons lacking needed multimedia. Qualitative; no effect size.
