AI-driven multimodal simulation for psychiatric clinical education: conceptual framework and preliminary validation of the cognitive core. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional needs assessment survey of 68 undergraduate psychiatry students using an investigator-developed questionnaire. The finding is a self-reported training gap, with "88% (n = 60) of students agreeing" that traditional methods are insufficient for non-verbal skills.

> "The most pronounced gap was observed in the acquisition of non-verbal skills, with 88% (n = 60) of students agreeing that traditional methods (books/lectures) are insufficient for mastering micro-expressions and prosody."
