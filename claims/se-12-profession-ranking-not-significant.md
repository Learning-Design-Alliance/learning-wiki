## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Axboe Mette K. 2016

Axboe Mette K., Christensen Kaj S., Kofoed Poul-Erik, Ammentorp Jette. (2016). Development and validation of a self-efficacy questionnaire (SE-12) measuring the clinical communication skills of health care professionals. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0798-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of SE-12 sum scores across professions in the 292-clinician sample. Nurses scored higher than physicians (t = 0.72, P = 0.47) and than nursing assistants (mean 93.42; t = 1.81, P = 0.07), neither significant.

> "nurses had a higher mean sum score (mean = 100.20, SD = 15.08) than physicians (mean = 98.80, SD = 12.33), although the difference was not statistically significant (t = 0.72, P = 0.47)."
