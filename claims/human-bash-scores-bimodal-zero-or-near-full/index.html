Alonso-Carracedo, M., Fernandez-Boullon, R., Celard, P., Rodríguez-Martínez, F. J., Otero-Cerdeira, L. (2026). Automated grading of Linux/bash examinations using large language models: a four-level cognitive taxonomy approach. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02432

q3 · i? · design · r2

Frequency distribution of normalized item-level scores assigned by the three human evaluators across the 1200 responses (Section 4.1.1, Figure 3). The article notes the 50% bin is the only notable intermediate concentration (n = 133), attributed to rubric provision for partial credit.

"The human distribution exhibits a strongly bimodal structure, with dominant peaks at the 0% bin (n= 334) and the 90% bin (n= 521), and a markedly lower frequency in the intermediate deciles (10%–80%)."
