Descriptive survey results from the daily-practice section of the 35-question HF nurse survey: "education to promote self‐care (n = 465, 56%)" and "symptom monitoring... 55% (n = 463)". Remote monitoring was reported by 24% (n = 199). These are descriptive percentages with no inferential test.

> "Patient education was a key role undertaken by many of the nurses, in particular education to promote self‐care (n = 465, 56%). Similarly, symptom monitoring was a fundamental component of the role of these nurses, as reported by 55% (n = 463) of those who participated in the survey."
