## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a nationwide cross-sectional study, mean overall GM-ITE scores showed no statistically significant difference among the three regional groups, though the small IMG samples limit power and equivalence was not tested. [→ Ishizuka Kosuke 2026](#ishizuka-kosuke-2026)

## Evidence

### Ishizuka Kosuke 2026

Ishizuka Kosuke, Nishizaki Yuji, Sekine Miwa, Shimizu Taro, Shikino Kiyoshi, Yamamoto Yu, Fukui Sho, Nojima Masanori, Ohta Mitsuyasu, Tokuda Yasuharu. (2026). Association between resident physicians from foreign medical schools and general medicine in-training examination scores: a nationwide cross-sectional study in Japan. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08941-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional analysis of consented residents at hospitals hosting IMG residents (Japan N = 440, Non-Japan Asia N = 22, Europe and Other N = 17); one-way ANOVA on total score found "no statistically significant differences were observed by region (p = 0.153)". Means were 43.2, 40.3, and 43.5 respectively.

> "The mean (standard deviation) GM-ITE® scores were 43.2 (6.9) in the Japan group, 40.3 (4.9) in the Non-Japan Asia group, and 43.5 (6.4) in the Europe and Other group, and no statistically significant differences were observed by region (p = 0.153)."
