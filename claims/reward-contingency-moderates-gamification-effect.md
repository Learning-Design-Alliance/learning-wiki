## Evidence

### Liuyufeng Li 2024

Liuyufeng Li, Khe Foon Hew, Jiahui Du. (2024). Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10337-7

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analysis coding reward contingencies using Cameron et al.'s framework. The 'each unit solved' subgroup (n = 10) showed g = 0.401; a three-way combination showed g = 0.633; one four-way combination showed g = −1.246.

> "there was significant variation across reward contingencies (Q = 99.486, p = 0.000). The effect sizes were greater when "each unit solved" or a combination of rewards (e.g., "each unit solved + surpassing a score + exceeding a norm") was used as the contingency for offering rewards"
