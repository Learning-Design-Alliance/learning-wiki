## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Group AC (CLI last) improved needle-guidance time by 136.2 s from first to second attempt while group CA showed no improvement (p < 0.0001). [→ Uysal Daniel 2020](#uysal-daniel-2020)

## Evidence

### Uysal Daniel 2020

Uysal Daniel, Gasch Claudia, Behnisch Rouven, Nickel Felix, Müller-Stich Beat Peter, Hohenfellner Markus, Teber Dogu. (2020). Evaluation of new motorized articulating laparoscopic instruments by laparoscopic novices using a standardized laparoscopic skills curriculum. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-020-08086-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests comparing speed improvement between sequence groups: "NG: AC 136,2 s vs. CA − 70,1 s, p < 0.0001". The article reports that group AC was 136.2 s faster in the second NG run while no improvement was seen in group CA, and ALI-after-CLI performance was significantly slower.

> "Participants were clearly faster when using CLI last (group AC) in LS and NG and showed a great speed improvement from the first to the second attempt compared to CA (LS: AC 86,6 s vs. CA 51,8 s, p = 0.45 and NG: AC 136,2 s vs. CA − 70,1 s, p < 0.0001,)."
