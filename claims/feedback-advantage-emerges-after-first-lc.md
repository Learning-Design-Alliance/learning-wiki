Per-LC comparison of the randomized controlled study (Table 2, Fig. 5): the feedback group "showed continuous performance improvement with comparable baseline scores after the first LC". The article notes no objective reason for the first-LC task-specific OSATS difference.

> "Other than task-specific OSATS, all performance scores of the first LC were comparable between the two groups (Table 2). The performance improvement according to the global and task-specific GOALS and OSATS scores was observed after the second LC (Table 2)."
