## Evidence

### Utama Evelyn Gabriela MD 2024

Utama Evelyn Gabriela MD, BSc (Hons), Saffari Seyed Ehsan PhD, Tang Phua Hwee MBBS, FRCR. (2024). Improving children's cooperativeness during magnetic resonance imaging using interactive educational animated videos: a prospective, randomised, non-inferiority trial. Singapore Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.11622/smedj.2021141

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Univariate and multivariable logistic regression in the trial of 558 randomised children, adjusted for allocated group, age, gender, race, prior MRI experience, contrast requirement and scan duration, yielded "OR 0.859" for age and "OR 1.718" for male gender, as quoted.

> "Logistic regression analysis showed that older children were less likely to have repeated MRI sequences (odds ratio [OR] 0.859, 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.808, 0.913, P < 0.0001), whereas male children were more likely to have repeated or rescheduled scans (OR 1.718, 95% CI 1.175, 2.510, P = 0.005)"
