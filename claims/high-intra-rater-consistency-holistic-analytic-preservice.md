### Lopera-Oquendo 2024

Lopera-Oquendo, C., Lipnevich, A. A., & Mañez, I. (2024). Rating writing: Comparison of holistic and analytic grading approaches in pre-service teachers. Learning and Instruction, 94, 101992. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101992

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Grading task with 231 pre-service teachers who rated strong and weak essays with both approaches; intraclass correlations compared standardized holistic and analytic grades, showing "high intra-rater consistency" with ICC = .849 and .902.

> "Findings showed a high intra-rater consistency among participants using holistic and analytic grades in strong (ICC = .849) and weak essays (ICC = .902)."
