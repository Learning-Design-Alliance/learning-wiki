## Evidence

### Ommering 2021

Ommering, Belinda W. C., Van Blankenstein, Floris M., van Diepen, Merel, Gruis, Nelleke A., Kool, Ada, Dekker, Friedo W. (2021). The importance of motivation in selecting undergraduate medical students for extracurricular research programmes. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0260193

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression on university records of the same 59-student ERP cohort. In-time bachelor completion showed "no effect on in-time bachelor completion" with a wide confidence interval (adjusted OR = .83, 95%CI = .17–4.00); no effect size is printed, so the null is not evidence of equivalence.

> "However, there was no effect on in-time bachelor completion (crude OR = .80, 95% CI = .19–3.33; adjusted OR = .83, 95%CI = .17–4.00)."
