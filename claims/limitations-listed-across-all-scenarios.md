## Evidence

### Emily M. Stump 2023

Emily M. Stump, Matthew Dew, Gina Passante, and N. G. Holmes. (2023). Context affects student thinking about sources of uncertainty in classical and quantum mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.020157)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Scenario survey results (Fig. 4, projectile motion, Stern-Gerlach, Brownian motion, single-slit). "most students (ranging from 75% to 93%) listed at least one source coded as limitations" in every scenario; some limitations (ruler precision, human error) appeared across all experiments.

> "For all four experiments, most students (ranging from 75% to 93%) listed at least one source coded as limitations (see Fig. 4)."
