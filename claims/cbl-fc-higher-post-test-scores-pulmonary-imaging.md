## Evidence

### Mao Haijia 2026

Mao Haijia, Zhao Zhenhua, Yang Jianfeng, Zhao Li. (2026). Effectiveness of a case-based learning combined with flipped classroom approach in undergraduate pulmonary imaging education: a prospective randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09208-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-randomized controlled study of 26 third-year medical imaging students assigned by student ID parity to CBL-FC (n=13) or LBL (n=13). Post-test means: CBL-FC "78.67 ± 6.71" vs LBL "67.07 ± 4.50"; the quote reports the significant between-group difference.

> "Consequently, the post-test scores in the CBL-FC group were significantly higher than those in the LBL group (p<0.001)."
