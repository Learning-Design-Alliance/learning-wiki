# Grammar-learning ability is preserved until about age 17.4 and then declines steadily, per the best-fitting ELSD model

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q2` quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The best-fitting model of syntax acquisition involves a learning-rate change beginning at 17.4 years, fitting better than constant-rate or step-function alternatives. [→ Hartshorne Joshua K. 2018](#hartshorne-joshua-k-2018)

## Evidence

### Hartshorne Joshua K. 2018

Hartshorne Joshua K., Tenenbaum Joshua B., Pinker Steven. (2018). A critical period for second language acquisition: Evidence from 2/3 million English speakers. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6559801/

`q2 · i?`

Numerical model fitting to performance curves from 244,840 monolinguals, 44,412 immersion learners, and 257,998 non-immersion learners. The "best-fitting model (R2 = 0.89) involved a rate change beginning at 17.4 years", beating constant-rate (R2 = 0.66) and step-function (R2 = 0.70) alternatives.

> "The best-fitting model (R2 = 0.89) involved a rate change beginning at 17.4 years (Fig. 4E). The fit was significantly better than the best fit for alternative models in which learning rate did not change (R2 = 0.66) or changed according to a step function with no further decline in the learning rate after the initial drop (R2 = 0.70)."
