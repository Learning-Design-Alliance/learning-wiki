## Evidence

### Khattak Osama 2026

Khattak Osama, Ehsan Aliya, Khalid Ayesha Akbar, Iqbal Azhar, Chaudhary Farooq Ahmad, Baig Muhammad Nadeem, Alsharari Thani, Memon Muhammad Rizwan. (2026). Assessment of clinical readiness, knowledge and attitude, regarding Basic Life Support (BLS) and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) skill among dentists practicing in Saudi Arabia. PeerJ. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21098

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression predicting good knowledge among 400 dentists, adjusted for age, gender, designation, experience, workshop attendance, and prior emergency encounters; the model explained 34.0% of the variance (Nagelkerke R2). The printed effect size for this predictor is an adjusted odds ratio, aOR = 4.91 (95% CI [2.60–9.24], p < 0.001), a large association.

> "dentists who had attended a BLS/CPR workshop within the last 5 years were nearly five times more likely to have good knowledge compared to those who attended more than 5 years ago (aOR = 4.91; 95% CI [2.60–9.24]; p < 0.001)"
