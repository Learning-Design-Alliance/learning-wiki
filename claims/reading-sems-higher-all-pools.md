# Reading SEMs exceed those in mathematics under all pool types, and only the all-grade reading pool meets the operational SEM stopping rule on average

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In reading simulations, all SEMs were larger than 3; the all-grade pool had the lowest average SEM (3.4, the operational stopping rule), followed by the ±1-grade pool (3.41) and the on-grade pool (3.59), with the largest values at extreme scale ends. [→ Li 2019](#li-2019)

## Evidence

### Li 2019

Li, S., & Meyer, J. P. (2019). Simulation study for evaluating MAP® Growth™ grade-level item pools. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/simulation-study-for-evaluating-map-growth-item-pools-with-grade-level-constraints/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Reading CAT simulations (40-item tests) for on-grade simulees. The on-grade pool "had the largest average SEM with a value of 3.59" and the widest range (3.33 to 5.06); values above 3.4 typically occurred at extreme ends of the scale, particularly the low end for the on-grade pool.

> "The all-grade pool was the only pool that had an average SEM of 3.4 (the operational stopping rule for SEM), and it had the smallest range of SEMs (3.34 to 3.87)."
