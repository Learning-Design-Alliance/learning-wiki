Evidence¶

Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests 2016¶

Relations Between the Worked Example and Generation Effects on Immediate and Delayed Tests. (2016). Submitted 29/01/2016. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2016.06.007

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Within the delayed-test ANOVA, Year 11 students showed no guidance x element interactivity interaction (F(1, 44) = .012, p = .915); instead "the main effect of guidance was significant", with low guidance superior for both material types.

"the main effect of guidance was significant, F(1, 44) = 4.40, MSe = 298.65, p = .042, ηр² = .091 with both high and low element interactivity information resulting in higher test scores after low guidance."

Related Claims¶

  • On the delayed test, guidance interacted with element interactivity: generation was superior for low element interactivity material, with no significant difference for high element interactivity material — reports the opposite
  • On the delayed test, a significant three-way interaction showed a worked example effect for high element interactivity material and a generation effect for low element interactivity material for novices — related
  • On the immediate test, novices showed a worked example effect (high guidance superior) while more knowledgeable learners showed a generation effect (low guidance superior) — related
  • Expertise produced large main effects on test scores and interacted with element interactivity, with the expertise advantage much larger for high element interactivity material — related
