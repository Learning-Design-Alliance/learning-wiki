### Cheng Meng 2025

Cheng Meng, Wang Xinle, Zhang Man, Li Sainan, Geng Cuizhi. (2025). Video based on the combination of animation and modules is effective in teaching suture technologies to novices without faculty involvement: a new surgical video course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06915-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-course performance test in which students completed 12 sutures, graded 0-100 with a modified ACS/APDS Global Rating Scale, compared across the three randomized groups (Fig. 3B). The article reports no statistically significant MA-TE difference and both better than MV; equivalence between MA and TE was not tested.
