## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Post-hoc ANOVAs found significant but small sample effects on 11 variables, with selected students lower on conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, openness and narcissism (partial η² .006–.055). [→ Likhanov 2021](#likhanov-2021)

## Evidence

### Likhanov 2021

Likhanov, M. V., Tsigeman, E. S., Papageorgiou, K. A., Akmalov, A. F., Sabitov, I. A., & Kovas, Y. V. (2021). Ordinary extraordinary: Elusive group differences in personality and psychological difficulties between STEM‐gifted adolescents and their peers. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12349

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Thirteen Bonferroni-corrected post-hoc ANOVAs (α = .004) on the selected (n = 973) and unselected (n = 1,261) samples. The article reports that "ES ranging from .006 to .055" for the 11 significant variables; Table 3 prints partial η² of .049 for conscientiousness and .011 for openness to experience, all in the small range; no neuroticism difference was significant.

> "Significant effects of sample were observed for 11 variables, with ES ranging from .006 to .055. On average, selected students showed lower scores on conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and openness to experience as well as lower scores on narcissism."
