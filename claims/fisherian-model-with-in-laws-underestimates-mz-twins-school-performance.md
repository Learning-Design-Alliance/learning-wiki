## Evidence

### Eftedal 2025

Eftedal, N. H., Eilertsen, E. M., Sunde, H. F., Kleppestø, T. H., Ystrom, E., & Czajkowski, N. O. (2025). Beyond additive genetic effects: Explaining family resemblance in school performance across millions of pairs of Norwegian relatives. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America, 122(25). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2419627122

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Weighted least-squares Fisherian model fitted to correlations from 936,708 Norwegian schoolchildren, including relatives-in-law, on averaged 8th-grade national test scores. The article reports "R2, was 0.873" with h² = 0.503 and m = 0.430; correlations were precision-weighted.

> "When fitting such a model to our correlations, the proportion of variance explained, R2, was 0.873. The estimated heritability, h2, was 0.503 and the estimated genotypic partner correlation, m, was 0.430."
