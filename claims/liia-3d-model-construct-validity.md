# The 3D-printed LIIA model shows construct validity: MAOSATS scores differ significantly across expert, intermediate, and novice groups on the model

## Evidence

### Xia Jianfu 2023

Xia Jianfu, Wu Junjie, Chen Hao, Mao Jinlei, Xu Xiaodong, Zhang Jing, Yang Jin, Wang Zhifei. (2023). Assessment of laparoscopic intracorporeal intestinal anastomosis training using simulation-based 3D printed models: exploring surgical performance and learning curves. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000582

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative study of 15 surgeons (five per group) performing LIIA on the 3D-printed model, scored with MAOSATS. Operation score differed across groups: expert "64.1±1.8", intermediate 48.5±1.7, novice 29.5±3.1, P<0.001. No effect size was printed.

> "The scores for the construct validity of the model differ significantly from step 1 to step 4 in the three groups, with the expert and intermediate groups both scoring higher than the novice group (64.1±1.8: 29.5±3.1, P<0.001 and 48.5±1.7: 29.5±3.1, P<0.001)."

### Xia Jianfu 2023 (2)

Xia Jianfu, Wu Junjie, Chen Hao, Mao Jinlei, Xu Xiaodong, Zhang Jing, Yang Jin, Wang Zhifei. (2023). Assessment of laparoscopic intracorporeal intestinal anastomosis training using simulation-based 3D printed models: exploring surgical performance and learning curves. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000582

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same three-group model-based comparison; operation time was significantly shorter for experts (21.5±1.9 min) and intermediates (30.6±2.8 min) than novices (70.7±4.0 min). No effect size was printed.

> "The duration of the surgical operation was shorter in both the expert and intermediate groups than in the novice group, with a significant difference (21.5±1.9: 70.7±4.0. P<0.001 and 30.6±2.8: 70.7±4.0, P<0.001)"
