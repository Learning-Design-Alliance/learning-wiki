### Guido Makransky 2025

Guido Makransky, Ban M. Shiwalia, Tue Herlau, Steven Blurton. (2025). Beyond the "Wow" Factor: Using Generative AI for Increasing Generative Sense-Making. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10039-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r3`

Pre-registered between-subjects experiment in a university cognitive psychology course; the follow-up ANOVA on delayed conceptual knowledge showed "a significant effect of learning condition on performance on the follow-up conceptual knowledge test, F(2, 121) = 5.866, p = 0.004, η2 = 0.088."

> "The one-way ANOVA showed a significant effect of learning condition on performance on the follow-up conceptual knowledge test, F(2, 121) = 5.866, p = 0.004, η2 = 0.088."

### Guido Makransky 2025 (2)

Guido Makransky, Ban M. Shiwalia, Tue Herlau, Steven Blurton. (2025). Beyond the "Wow" Factor: Using Generative AI for Increasing Generative Sense-Making. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10039-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Fisher's LSD post hoc tests on the Study 1 follow-up showed "students in the ChatTutor condition (M = 7.26, SD = 1.50) scored significantly higher than those in the teaching condition (M = 5.93, SD = 2.15), p = 0.001" while the ChatTutor–ChatGPT contrast was not significant (p = 0.229).

> "Fisher's LSD post hoc comparisons revealed that students in the ChatTutor condition (M = 7.26, SD = 1.50) scored significantly higher than those in the teaching condition (M = 5.93, SD = 2.15), p = 0.001, but not the ChatGPT condition (M = 6.72, SD = 1.76), p = 0.229."
