Lee Rebecca, Dhami Neil, Gibson William, Hamza Deena M., Oswald Anna E., Moffat Mandy. (2025). Student Perceptions of Narrative Feedback in Entrustable Professional Activities. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70089

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of closed-ended Likert survey items completed by 35 of 153 Year 3 MD students (23% response). The article reports that "86%, reported that the narrative feedback in EPA observations was individualised some or most of the time."

> "The majority of students, 86%, reported that the narrative feedback in EPA observations was individualised some or most of the time. Over 60% reported that narrative comments were aligned with EPA scores most or all of the time."
