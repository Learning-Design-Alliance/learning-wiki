## Evidence

### Daniel R. Espinas 2024

Daniel R. Espinas, Brennan W. Chandler. (2024). Correlates of K-12 Students' Intertextual Integration. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09889-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Norwegian tenth-grade study (Andresen et al., n = 44), half neurotypical readers and half with school-based diagnoses of developmental dyslexia, reading three webpages and orally responding to open-ended questions. The review reports "r  = 0.45 (p < 0.01)" for the entire sample.

> "They found an association of r  = 0.45 (p < 0.01) between word reading and intertextual integration for the entire sample."
