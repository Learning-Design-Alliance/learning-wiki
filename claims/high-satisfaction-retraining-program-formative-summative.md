## Evidence

### He Peipei 2026

He Peipei, Yin Xiaohong, Xu Ying, Yang Tingjun. (2026). The construction and implementation of a clinical nursing experts-oriented retraining program for peritoneal dialysis specialist nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09379-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Formative assessment after each training session and summative assessment after completion of the entire retraining phase, both using a five-point Likert scale via QR code questionnaires among 36 nurses.

> "During the formative evaluation phase, the satisfaction score of peritoneal dialysis SNs was 4.74 (4.61, 5.00). In the summative evaluation phase, the overall satisfaction score was 5.00 (4.00, 5.00). Satisfaction with the training content reached 5.00 (5.00, 5.00), while satisfaction with the training methods was 4.64 (4.51, 5.00)."
