## Evidence

### Simpson Paul L 2014

Simpson Paul L, Scicluna Helen A, Jones Philip D, Cole Andrew MD, O’Sullivan Anthony J, Harris Peter G, Velan Gary, McNeil H Patrick. (2014). Predictive validity of a new integrated selection process for medical school admission. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-86

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression of admission dimension scores and demographics on six performance criteria for 318 students admitted 2004–2005 (N = 243–271 per model). UAI was a significant predictor of WAM and knowledge outcomes, accounting for 31.1% unique variance in WAM phase 1 and 18.9% in knowledge exam phase 1.

> "Significant predictors for both WAM phase 1 and WAM program final scores were UAI (p < 0.001) and the interview dimension ‘cope with uncertainty’"
