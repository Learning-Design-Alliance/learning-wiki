# Trait emotional intelligence showed no significant association with university GPA, despite correlating with engagement, SRL strategies and self-efficacy

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trait EI did not significantly predict GPA in either correlations or regressions, but correlated significantly with other non-cognitive variables. [→ Juan-Carlos Pérez-González 2022](#juan-carlos-perez-gonzalez-2022)

## Evidence

### Juan-Carlos Pérez-González 2022

Juan-Carlos Pérez-González, Gemma Filella, Anna Soldevila, Yasmine Faiad, Maria-Jose Sanchez-Ruiz. (2022). Integrating self-regulated learning and individual differences in the prediction of university academic achievement across a three-year-long degree. Metacognition and Learning (2022) 17:1141–1165. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09315-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null direct association between global trait EI (TEIQue) and GPA in this sample, contrasting with prior meta-analytic findings the authors cite; trait EI did correlate with engagement, SRL strategies and self-efficacy. No effect size printed for the null.

> "Contrary to previous findings (e.g., MacCann et al., 2020; Sanchez-Ruiz et al., 2013) trait EI showed no significant association to AP in either of the analyses, although it significantly correlated with academic engagement, SRL strategies, and academic self-efficacy."
