## Evidence

### Zurc Joca 2022

Zurc Joca, Planinšec Jurij. (2022). Associations between Physical Activity and Academic Competence: A Cross-Sectional Study among Slovenian Primary School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19020623

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multiple regression analysis (Enter method, five models run separately by sex) in the cross-sectional study of 1520 students. The article reports "model 5 (81.1% for girls and 84.1% for boys)" as the highest explanatory prediction; R² values are printed but no standardized effect size.

> "The highest explanatory prediction for overall academic competence was found in model 5 (81.1% for girls and 84.1% for boys), which included the variables for leisure-time PA and all confounding variables."
