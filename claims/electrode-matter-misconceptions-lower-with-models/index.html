Evidence¶

Ataman Karaçöp 2016¶

Ataman Karaçöp. (2016). Effects of Student Teams-Achievement Divisions Cooperative Learning with Models on Students' Understanding of Electrochemical Cells. International Education Studies, 9(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v9n11p104

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Content analysis of incorrect answers to Task 7 of the EcAT post-test, which asked students to show molecular-level changes in electrodes marked in three parts; Table 5 reports group percentages, e.g. no-change answers of CG: 27%; CLG: 29.2%; CLMG: 8.3%.

"In this research, it was found that these misunderstandings are in lower levels of the student groups to which STAD cooperative learning method was applied along with models compared to other groups."
