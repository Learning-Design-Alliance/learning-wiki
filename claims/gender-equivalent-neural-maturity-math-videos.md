`q3 i?` Whole-brain t tests found no gender differences in neural maturity, and Schuirmann's Test of Equivalence found girls and boys statistically equivalent throughout the brain. [→ Kersey 2019](#kersey-2019)

## Evidence

### Kersey 2019

Kersey, A. J., Csumitta, K. D., & Cantlon, J. F. (2019). Gender similarities in the brain during mathematics development. npj Science of Learning 4:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0057-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Whole-brain fMRI analysis of 104 children (55 girls) aged 3–10 during natural viewing of mathematics education videos, using intersubject correlations to adult baselines. The authors report "statistically equivalent levels of neural maturity throughout the brain"; no mean differences survived correction and the equivalence tests were significant.

> "In fact, girls and boys showed statistically equivalent levels of neural maturity throughout the brain (Fig. 1a, light purple; minimum t(102) ≥ 2.36, maximum one-sided p ≤ 0.01)"
