## Evidence

### Geary David C. 2019

Geary David C., vanMarle Kristy, Chu Felicia W., Hoard Mary K., Nugent Lara. (2019). Predicting Age of Becoming a Cardinal Principle Knower. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000277

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binomial logistic regression contrasting delayed CPKs (not CPK by end of preschool) with all other children. The model with executive function and letter recognition was highly significant, χ2(2) = 47.05, p < .0001, classifying 92% correctly.

> "a 1 SD decrease in executive function resulted in a 5.7-fold increase in the odds of being in the delayed CPK status group [χ2(1) = 8.79, p = .003, 95% CI 1.8 to 17.9]."
