## Evidence

### Najmuldeen 2017

Najmuldeen, H. A. (2017). The Impact of Educational Games-Based iPad Applications on the Development of Social Studies Achievement and Learning Retention among Sixth Grade Students in Jeddah. World Journal of Education, 7(3). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v7n3p21

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study of 24 experimental and 24 control sixth-grade students; ANCOVA controlled pre-achievement. Adjusted post-test total means were 14.38 (experimental) versus 6.04 (control); the article reports the difference as statistically significant with no effect size in this sentence.
