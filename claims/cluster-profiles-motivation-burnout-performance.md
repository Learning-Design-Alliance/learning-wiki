## Subclaims
`q3 i?` K-means cluster analysis divides the sample into three groups: a non-adaptive group with above-average burnout and amotivation and below-average intrinsic motivation and performance, an average group, and an adaptive group with above-average motivations and below-average burnout. [→ Usán Pablo 2022](#usan-pablo-2022)

## Evidence

### Usán Pablo 2022

Usán Pablo, Salavera Carlos, Quílez-Robres Alberto, Lozano-Blasco Raquel. (2022). Behaviour Patterns between Academic Motivation, Burnout and Academic Performance in Primary School Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191912663

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

K-means cluster analysis of 398 students into three groups (N = 42, 10.55%; N = 128, 32.16%; N = 228, 57.28%; Table 4). Group 1 shows "above average values in terms of burnout and amotivation"; Group 3 shows above-average motivations and below-average burnout with the highest marks (4.17).

> "Group 1 is characterized by non-adaptive behaviors, reflected by above average values in terms of burnout and amotivation, and below average values in terms of intrinsic motivation and academic performance."
