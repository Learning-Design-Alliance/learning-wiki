# Environmental analysis via structured observation of morning rounds identified unanticipated stakeholders in surgical discharge

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Structured observations of surgical morning teaching rounds revealed staff nurses and specialties such as physical therapy and social services participating in discharge care coordination, stakeholders not identified during initial planning. [→ Graham LA 2023](#graham-la-2023)

## Evidence

### Graham LA 2023

Graham LA, Gray C, Wagner TH, Illarmo S, Hawn MT, Wren SM, Iannuzzi J, Harris AHS. (2023). Applying cognitive task analysis to health services research. Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-6773.14106

`q2 · i?`

Qualitative pilot study of discharge planning: one study staff member conducted 16 structured observations at two hospitals using a single-page observation guide, with bi-weekly team debriefs. The observations allowed the team to observe interactions across disciplines and identify stakeholders for the knowledge elicitation step.

> "Although not originally known, we also found that staff nurses and other specialties such as physical therapy or social services periodically drop in for these morning rounds."
