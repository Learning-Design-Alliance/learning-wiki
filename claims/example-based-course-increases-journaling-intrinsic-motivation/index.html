Evidence¶

Sven Scheu 2026¶

Sven Scheu, Simone N. Loeffler and Alexander Maedche. (2026). Designing a mobile chatbot-based learning journaling system for intrinsic motivation and engagement. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00589-7

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Type-II ANOVA on IMI enjoyment scores among 157 active participants (those with at least 4 days of journal entries) in the 2×2 randomized field experiment. The course effect was small and significant, "thus confirming H1", with η² = 0.03. Results were similar when all participants were included (η² = 0.03, F(1, 175) = 5.96, p < 0.05).

"This revealed a small significant effect ( η2 =0 .03) of the course (F (1, 153) = 4 .81,p < 0.05), thus confirming H1."
