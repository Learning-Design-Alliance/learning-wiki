### Wei Chung-Jen 2019

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

One-way ANOVA across the three trainee groups (PGY1 n=25, PGY2 n=20, C-PGY n=8) in the main study. The article reports "C-PGY trainees performed significantly better (P < 0.05) than PGY1 trainees" in the two named domains; Table 2 lists post-hoc C-PGY > PGY1 for both.
