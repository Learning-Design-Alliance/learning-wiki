## Evidence

### Stephen Akinyemi Omotayo & Joshua Oluwatoyin Adeleke 2017

Stephen Akinyemi Omotayo & Joshua Oluwatoyin Adeleke. (2017). The 5E Instructional Model: A Constructivist Approach for Enhancing Students' Learning Outcomes in Mathematics. JISTE, Vol. 21, No. 2, 2017. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1176946

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same quasi-experiment, measured with the 20-item Mathematics Interest Inventory (Cronbach alpha .92). The 5E group's posttest interest mean of 63.09 exceeded the control group's 55.25, "t(170) = 4.216, p < 0.05". No effect size printed.

> "Table 6 shows that there was significant effect of treatment on students' interest in mathematics, t(170) = 4.216, p < 0.05. The mean interest score of students taught with the 5E curriculum (M = 63.09, SD = 8.70) is higher than those taught with the traditional method (M = 55.25, SD = 14.79)."
