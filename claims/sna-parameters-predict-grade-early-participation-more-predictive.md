## Evidence

### Saqr Mohammed 2018

Saqr Mohammed, Fors Uno, Tedre Matti. (2018). How the study of online collaborative learning can guide teachers and predict students' performance in a medical course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1126-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Automatic linear regression (SPSS v24) predicting final grade from SNA parameters for 35 students. The article reports "adjusted R square (model accuracy) was 41.6%" with information centrality and eigenvector centrality as top predictors; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "The adjusted R square (model accuracy) was 41.6%. The most important predictors were information centrality (26.71%) and Eigenvector centrality (21.27%)."

### Saqr Mohammed 2018 (2)

Saqr Mohammed, Fors Uno, Tedre Matti. (2018). How the study of online collaborative learning can guide teachers and predict students' performance in a medical course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1126-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

ALM models recomputed using midterm-period network data. The article reports "ALM accuracy for predicting midterm results was 71.6%" and, for predicting final results from midterm data, an accuracy of 70%; no effect size is printed.

> "Interestingly, when centrality measures were calculated for the midterm, early participation was found to be more predictive of performance than it was with the whole course data: ALM accuracy for predicting midterm results was 71.6%"
