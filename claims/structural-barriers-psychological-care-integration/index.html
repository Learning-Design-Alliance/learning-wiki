Winterdijk Per 2026¶

Winterdijk Per, Aanstoot Henk‐Jan, Fransman Christine, Mocan Andreia, Nefs Giesje. (2026). Embedding psychological care in diabetes services: Why it's time to innovate and integrate. Diabetic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1111/dme.70411

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's conclusion section states these structural barriers limit scalability; the review identifies them as interpretive claims reflecting the authors' clinical and behavioural science expertise rather than tested results.

"Structural barriers such as fragmented health systems, insufficient infrastructure, inadequate reimbursement and shortages of trained professionals limit scalability."

Winterdijk Per 2026 (2)¶

Winterdijk Per, Aanstoot Henk‐Jan, Fransman Christine, Mocan Andreia, Nefs Giesje. (2026). Embedding psychological care in diabetes services: Why it's time to innovate and integrate. Diabetic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1111/dme.70411

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this workforce modelling estimate, attributed to Segal et al., and notes no health system currently approaches this level of provision, highlighting the gap between need and workforce capacity.

"Segal et al. estimated that approximately two full‐time equivalent psychologists per 1000 people with diabetes would be required to meet population‐level psychological needs in a primary care context."

  • A low-intensity telehealth problem-solving intervention delivered by trained diabetes educators, nurses and dietitians reduced mild to moderate diabetes distress more than usual care (LISTEN trial) — related
  • Integration of psychological care appears most effective when it operates on multiple levels: routine distress assessment, frontline psycho-education and access to specialist interventions — related
  • Resource mismatch, missing incentives, and discontinuous post-training support constrain the sustainability of training transfer — related
