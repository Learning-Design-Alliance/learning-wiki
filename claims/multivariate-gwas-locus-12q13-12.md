## Evidence

### Shiying Wang 2026

Shiying Wang, Joan Bosson-Heenan, Andrew Grotzinger, Jan C. Frijters & Jeffrey R. Gruen. (2026). Shared genetic architecture of reading and attention disorders using genomic structural equation modeling. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00433-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate GWAS of SNP effects on the common genetic factor Cg, computed from 6,421,479 SNPs after quality control. The article reports rs7969091 reached genome-wide significance (p = 3.86 × 10−8) and states "No genome-wide significant associations were observed for individual traits." No effect size is printed.

> "One locus within 12q13.12 achieved genome-wide significance with the top SNP, rs7969091 (minor allele frequency (MAF) = 0.417, 𝑝-value = 3.86 × 10−8; Figure 3A)."
