Evidence¶

Allen 2018¶

Allen, K., Kern, M.L., Vella-Brodrick, D., Hattie, J., & Waters, L. (2018). What Schools Need to Know About Fostering School Belonging: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 30, 1–34. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-016-9389-8

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 51 studies (N = 67,378) examining the average association between each of the ten themes and school belonging. The article reports that "Teacher support and positive personal characteristics were the strongest predictors of school belonging"; no effect size is printed in the available text.

"Second, the average association between each of these themes and school belonging was meta-analytically examined across 51 studies ( N = 67,378). Teacher support and positive personal characteristics were the strongest predictors of school belonging."

q2 · i? · review · r1

(2018), a meta-analysis on fostering school belonging listed in its references, stating that "Teacher support is one of the strongest predictors of student belonging." No effect size is printed in the primer.

"Teacher support is one of the strongest predictors of student belonging and creating a classroom culture where students can feel safe in taking intellectual risks is a necessary foundation (Allen et al., 2018)."
