## Evidence

### Sattelmayer Martin 2016

Sattelmayer Martin, Elsig Simone, Hilfiker Roger, Baer Gillian. (2016). A systematic review and meta-analysis of selected motor learning principles in physiotherapy and medical education. BMC Medical Education, 16, 15. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0538-z

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of three RCTs comparing whole and part practice, 50 participants on the post-acquisition performance test. The pooled effect of 0.43 SMD was not statistically significant (p: 0.33) and heterogeneity was considerable (I2: 54 %).

> "The results of a post-acquisition test (with 50 participants) immediately after the intervention showed a moderate effect size of 0.43 SMD (95 % CI -0.43 to 1.29) in favour for whole practice (p: 0.33). However, heterogeneity was considerable for this analysis (I2: 54 %)"
