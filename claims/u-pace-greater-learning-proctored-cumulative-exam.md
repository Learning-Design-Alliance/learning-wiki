# U-Pace instruction produced greater learning on a proctored cumulative exam than concept mastery, amplified assistance, and face-to-face instruction in an RCT

## Subclaims
`q4 i2` U-Pace students scored significantly higher on the proctored cumulative exam than students in the concept mastery, amplified assistance, and face-to-face conditions (η² = .101). [→ Fleming 2018](#fleming-2018)

## Evidence

### Fleming 2018

Fleming, R., Pedrick, L.E., Soiber, L., Kienzler, S., Fleming, R.R., & Reddy, D.M. (2018). Increasing undergraduate success: A randomized controlled trial of U-Pace instruction. Online Learning, 22(3), 175-191. https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v22i3.1317

`q4 · i2` · `causal · r3`

In the RCT, 914 undergraduates were randomly assigned to four conditions with course content, textbook, class size, and instructors held constant. ANOVA on the proctored cumulative exam showed "F(3,572) = 21.38, p < .001, η2= .101"; U-Pace (M = 66.92) exceeded concept mastery (M = 59.2), amplified assistance (M = 55.30), and face-to-face (M = 58.38) on Scheffe tests.

> "Significant differences in performance were found by instructional condition on the proctored cumulative exam, the measure of learning independent of final course grades, F(3,572) = 21.38, p < .001, η2= .101."

### Fleming 2018 (2)

Fleming, R., Pedrick, L.E., Soiber, L., Kienzler, S., Fleming, R.R., & Reddy, D.M. (2018). Increasing undergraduate success: A randomized controlled trial of U-Pace instruction. Online Learning, 22(3), 175-191. https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v22i3.1317

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Sensitivity analysis including only students randomly selected a priori to take the exam replicated the finding: "U-Pace = 67.00, concept mastery = 60.31, amplified assistance = 58.47, and face-to-face = 59.28". A 1,200-sample bootstrap indicated the a priori sample was representative (all ps > .15).

> "The mean exam scores were  as follows : U-Pace = 67.00, concept mastery = 60.31, amplified assistance = 58.47, and face-to-face = 59.28, F(3, 263) = 6.35, p < .001."
