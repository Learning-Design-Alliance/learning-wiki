## Evidence

### Liu Zeqi 2024

Liu Zeqi, Yao Qinqi, Wang Peicheng, Shen Lijun, Li Hange, Zhang Jingfeng, Jiang Maoqing, Yang Zhenghan, Wang Zhenchang, Zheng Jianjun, Zhu Jiming, Wu You. (2024). The price of pressure: nationwide survey on lifestyle disturbances, occupational burnout and compromised perceived-competency among radiology residents in China. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1472397

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Adjusted linear regression in the nationwide survey found moderate and low alcohol consumers scored lower on overall perceived-competency than high consumers, with "no significant association between smoke and competency-based performance"; equivalence was not tested.

> "those consuming moderate and low alcohol had lower competence than those consuming high alcohol (β = −0.26, 95% CI = [−0.45, −0.07]; β = −0.35, 95% CI = [−0.58, −0.17]). Nevertheless, there was no significant association between smoke and competency-based performance"
