## Evidence

### Rowe Anna D.; Fitness Julie 2018

Rowe Anna D.; Fitness Julie. (2018). Understanding the Role of Negative Emotions in Adult Learning and Achievement: A Social Functional Perspective. Behavioral Sciences. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5836010/

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Comparison of student and faculty interview responses in the qualitative study. Faculty comments on emotions' impact on learning were almost double those of students despite fewer faculty participants, and faculty comments "ranged across a broader range of areas including behaviors."

> "Interestingly, compared to students who generally viewed negative emotions as detrimental (with the exception of fear), teachers were more likely to emphasize the functional aspect of negative emotions."
