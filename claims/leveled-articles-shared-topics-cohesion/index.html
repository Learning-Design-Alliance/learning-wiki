Tare 2018¶

Tare, M., Nobles, S., & Xiao, W. (2018). Partnerships that work: Tapping research to address learner variability in young readers. Washington, DC: Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/e59c6e47-4209-4187-a0ef-b8da08283ec6

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Teacher interview comment illustrating finding passages on the same topic (water) for readers below, at, and above expected level; the report says this theme of inclusion ran through all interviewees' comments.

"the passages are a little different, but they're still reading about water and then they can come back and share what they read. Everybody is reading the same topic, but they're reading it at their appropriate level."
