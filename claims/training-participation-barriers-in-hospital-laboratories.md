## Evidence

### Moura Sofia 2014

Moura Sofia, Fançony Cláudia, Mirante Clara, Neves Marcela, Bernardino Luís, Fortes Filomeno, Sambo Maria do Rosário, Brito Miguel. (2014). Impact of a training course on the quality of malaria diagnosis by microscopy in Angola. Malaria Journal, Volume 13, Article 437. https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2875-13-437

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' account in the Discussion of participation barriers in the three hospital laboratories; attendance was 9, 8 and 11 participants for Bengo, Benguela and Luanda respectively.

> "In the three laboratories, all technicians were asked to participate in the course, however, a very low participation rate was observed. The major reasons pointed were: schedule incompatibility, lack of financial reward on doing the course and the impossibility of laboratory closure"

### Moura Sofia 2014 (2)

Moura Sofia, Fançony Cláudia, Mirante Clara, Neves Marcela, Bernardino Luís, Fortes Filomeno, Sambo Maria do Rosário, Brito Miguel. (2014). Impact of a training course on the quality of malaria diagnosis by microscopy in Angola. Malaria Journal, Volume 13, Article 437. https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2875-13-437

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' interpretation of staff reactions; this was reflected in difficulties collecting routine microscopy results, slides and DBS, with all three requests obtained in 86.9% of samples.

> "The researchers believe that the laboratory staff felt uncomfortable on being evaluated and viewed this study as punitive and, therefore, good cooperation was very hard to accomplish."
