### Cahill Naomi 2025

Cahill Naomi, Pinzon Rizaldy Taslim, Yang Lin, Koka Avinash MD, Stuby Loric EMT-P, Carrera Emmanuel MD, Gabr Ahmed MD, O'Connor Margaret MD, Missilier Peruzzo Nathalie RN, Waeterloot Olivier RN, Medlin Friedrich MD, Rigolet Fabien RN, Schmutz Thomas MD, Michel Patrik MD, Desmettre Thibaut MD, Suppan Mélanie MD, Suppan Laurent MD. Asynchronous Distance Learning Performance and Knowledge Retention of the National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale Among Health Care Professionals Using Video or e-Learning: Web-based Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/63136

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Web-based randomized controlled trial in 3 Swiss university hospitals; 43 e-learning and 67 video completers took a 50-question quiz after training. The e-learning group scored higher, with "median score of 39, IQR 36-41 vs 37, IQR 34-39; P=.03".

> "Participants who followed the e-learning method performed better in the acquisition quiz than the participants in the video group (median score of 39, IQR 36-41 vs 37, IQR 34-39; P=.03; Figure 3)."
