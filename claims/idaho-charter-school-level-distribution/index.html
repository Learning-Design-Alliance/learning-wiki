Evidence¶

CREDO 2019¶

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in Idaho. https://credo.stanford.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

School-level analysis of 41 Idaho charter schools with sufficient tested students over the two growth periods, each benchmarked against pooled VCRs from its feeder schools. In reading, 17 of 41 schools (41 percent) perform significantly better.

"In reading, 17 out of 41 Idaho charter schools, or 41 percent, perform significantly better that the traditional schooling environments the charter students would have otherwise attended."

CREDO 2019 (2)¶

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in Idaho. https://credo.stanford.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The same school-level analysis in math finds 17 of 41 charter schools (41 percent) post growth significantly higher than their traditional public schooling counterparts. The report notes this share exceeds the 2013 national figures of 25 percent in reading and 29 percent in math.

"In math, the result is the same: 17 of 41 or 41 percent of charter schools post growth that is significantly higher than that of their traditional public schooling counterparts."
