The commentary reports a study comparing problem solving with example study after initial acquisition; the delayed advantage held "regarding one-week delayed posttest performance, but not for performance assessed immediately after learning". No effect size is printed in this passage.

"Ruitenburg et al. (2025) showed that problem solving was superior to example study regarding one-week delayed posttest performance, but not for performance assessed immediately after learning, thereby again supporting a desirable-difficulties approach (E.L. Bjork & R.A. Bjork & Bjork, 2011)."
