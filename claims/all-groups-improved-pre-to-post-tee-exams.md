### Weber U 2019

Weber U, Zapletal B, Base E, Hambrusch M, Ristl R, Mora B, Schaller B. (2019). Resident performance in basic perioperative transesophageal echocardiography: Comparing 3 teaching methods in a randomized controlled trial. Medicine 98(36):e17072. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000017072

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-group pre-to-post comparison in the randomized trial, in which all residents first took a theoretical multiple-choice pre-test and a 2-hour tutorial before their assigned training. The article reports the improvement as significant without printing test statistics or effect sizes.

> "All groups showed significant improvement from the pre- to the post-training exams."
