### Shariman and Talib 2017

Tenku Putri Norishah Tenku Shariman and Othman Talib. (2017). OCRA, A Mobile Learning Prototype for Understanding Chemistry Concepts. 14th International Conference on Cognition and Exploratory Learning in Digital Age (CELDA 2017). https://www.worldcat.org/isbn/978-989-8533-68-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 46 students per group in a Malaysian Science Foundation Introduction to Chemistry course; t-test on post-test achievement between groups. The experimental group mean was 62.24 versus 40.86 for the control group, and the article reports the difference as "significantly higher (p < .05)".

> "Table 4 shows the minimum score for the experimental group is significantly higher (p < .05) compared to the control group."
