## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Biology knowledge significantly predicted scientific inquiry posttest and retention scores, while scientific reasoning predicted inquiry abilities only at retention testing. [→ Wu 2016](#wu-2016)

## Evidence

### Wu 2016

Wu, H.-L., Weng, H.-L., & She, H.-C. (2016). Effects of scaffolds and scientific reasoning ability on web-based scientific inquiry. International Journal of Contemporary Educational Research, 3(1), 12-24. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1216018

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three multiple regression analyses at pretest, posttest, and retention. Retention model: both predictors significant (p < 0.01), adjusted R² = 0.54. Reasoning β = 0.26 at retention vs. β = 0.08 at posttest.

> "for the posttest results, only biology knowledge accounted for a significant amount of unique value in predicting student scientific inquiry scores (p < 0.01); however, reasoning skills did not significantly predict scientific inquiry posttest scores (p> 0.05)."
