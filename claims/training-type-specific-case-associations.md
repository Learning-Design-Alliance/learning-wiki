## Evidence

### Nákity K 2025

Nákity K, Kasza BB, Tatár BB, Szűcs M, Kis D, Barzó P, Domján A. (2025). Assessment of differential diagnostic skills of physiotherapists related to the cervical spine - approaches to improving effectiveness: observational, cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 25:1065. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07682-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Case-level logistic regression in the 128-respondent survey found radiculopathy detection "significantly associated with both McKenzie (95% CI 1.39–11.45, p = 0.014) and manual therapy (95% CI 1.53–20.86, p = 0.016) training"; no point effect size is printed.

> "For MSK cases, the detection of cervical radiculopathy (CS 4) was significantly associated with both McKenzie (95% CI 1.39–11.45, p = 0.014) and manual therapy (95% CI 1.53–20.86, p = 0.016) training."

### Nákity K 2025 (2)

Nákity K, Kasza BB, Tatár BB, Szűcs M, Kis D, Barzó P, Domján A. (2025). Assessment of differential diagnostic skills of physiotherapists related to the cervical spine - approaches to improving effectiveness: observational, cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 25:1065. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07682-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The Results text reports that decisions about the dens fracture, myelopathy, atlantoaxial instability, and metastatic tumor cases "were significantly influenced by continuing education in cervical spine stabilization training", with per-case statistics printed in Table 6.

> "Decisions about CM cases (CS 1, CS 2, CS 7, and CS 8) were significantly influenced by continuing education in cervical spine stabilization training."
