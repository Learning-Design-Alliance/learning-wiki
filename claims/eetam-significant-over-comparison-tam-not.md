## Evidence

### Kızkapan 2021

Kızkapan, O., & Bektaş, O. (2021). Improving 7th Grade Students’ Epistemological Beliefs by Epistemologically Enriched Argumentation Model. Pedagogical Research, 6(3), em0095. https://doi.org/10.29333/pr/10949

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with 98 7th graders in three intact classes (TAM, EETAM, comparison). Post-test ANOVA pairwise (Tukey) comparisons on the epistemological belief scale showed "a statistically significant difference between EETAM group and comparison group (p=.034<.05)"; the other pairs were not significant. No effect size was printed.

> "While there was a statistically significant difference between EETAM group and comparison group (p=.034<.05), there was no statistically significant difference between TAM and comparison group (p=.860>.05) and between EETAM and TAM group (p=.107>.05) in terms of posttest scores."
