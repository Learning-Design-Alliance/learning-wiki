## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a single-institution survey, 79 of 91 respondents (86.8%) found 3-minute lesson videos useful, with the top-rated subcategories being revision, assessment preparation and general learning. [→ Prakash S.S. 2017](#prakash-ss-2017)

## Evidence

### Prakash S.S. 2017

Prakash S.S., Muthuraman N., Anand R. (2017). Short-duration podcasts as a supplementary learning tool: perceptions of medical students and impact on assessment performance. BMC Medical Education 17:167. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1001-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative coding of open-ended questionnaire responses (item A1, N = 91) using an adapted published coding scheme with two independent raters resolving discrepancies by consensus; the revision subcategory had a mean rating of 0.97 across 57 comments. Students said the podcasts were "useful for revision especially at the last-minute".

> "Qualitative analysis for item A1 revealed that the majority of students’ comments were about learning category, specifically to help offered by 3MTLs for revision, preparation for assessments and general learning which were rated the top 3 subcategories."
