## Evidence

### Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski 2016

Jacob T. Stanley and H. J. Lewandowski. (2016). Lab notebooks as scientific communication: investigating development from undergraduate courses to graduate research. arXiv preprint arXiv:1604.00403. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.12.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Semi-structured interviews with 13 physics graduate students at University of Colorado Boulder, coded for notebook experiences across educational stages. The article reports that "11 of the 13 interviewees" said lab courses did not adequately prepare them, and eight either kept no notebook or found it not beneficial.

> "Specifically, 11 of the 13 interviewees (I03 through I13, seen in Table II) described that their experience with notebooks did not adequately prepare them to use a notebook for lab records in research."
