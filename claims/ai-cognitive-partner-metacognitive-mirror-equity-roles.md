## Evidence

### Rashid S 2025

Rashid S, Malik S, Ghauri F. (2025). The Active Learning–GenAI Synergy Framework: Ethical Integration of Generative AI in EFL/ESL Writing in Resource-Constrained Contexts. F1000Research. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.171435.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Semi-structured interviews with eight volunteer students from the experimental group, thematically analysed, found participants framed AI use as "collaborative partnership rather than tool usage", integrating AI suggestions with their own thinking.

> "Students consistently described their relationships with AI tools in terms of collaborative partnership rather than tool usage, indicating successful development of the cognitive partnership model."

### Rashid S 2025 (2)

Rashid S, Malik S, Ghauri F. (2025). The Active Learning–GenAI Synergy Framework: Ethical Integration of Generative AI in EFL/ESL Writing in Resource-Constrained Contexts. F1000Research. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.171435.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Thematic analysis of the eight interviews reported that weekly ethical reflection prompts built awareness of AI dependence; one student reported noticing "when I was depending too much on AI suggestions".

> "The weekly ethical reflection prompts successfully promoted metacognitive awareness and reflective observation—key components of both active learning and cognitive partnership development."

### Rashid S 2025 (3)

Rashid S, Malik S, Ghauri F. (2025). The Active Learning–GenAI Synergy Framework: Ethical Integration of Generative AI in EFL/ESL Writing in Resource-Constrained Contexts. F1000Research. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.171435.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interviews reported AI tools acted as equity tools, supplying personalized feedback "anytime it was necessary" in large classes, and increased student confidence and independent working.

> "Students highlighted the ways AI tools solved age-old resource constraints in bigger EFL/ESL classes by providing individual learning experiences that would be otherwise unattainable in resource-poor settings."
