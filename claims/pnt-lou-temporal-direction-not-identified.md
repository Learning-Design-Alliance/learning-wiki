# Temporal direction between PNT and LOU could not be identified: the reversed specification was observationally equivalent

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A reverse focal specification replacing PNT → LOU with LOU → PNT was observationally equivalent, producing the same global fit, so the cross-sectional design could not identify whether PNT precedes LOU or LOU precedes PNT. [→ Yang Z 2026](#yang-z-2026)

## Evidence

### Yang Z 2026

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Directionality sensitivity analysis on the student sample. The reversed model 'was observationally equivalent to the hypothesised specification' with the same fit; the article does not interpret this as support for the reverse direction.

> "A model reversing the focal association to LOU → PNT was observationally equivalent to the hypothesised specification and therefore produced the same fit, χ2(269) = 400.03, CFI = 0.988, TLI = 0.986, RMSEA = 0.022, and SRMR = 0.055"
