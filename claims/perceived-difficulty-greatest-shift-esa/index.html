Evidence¶

Záhorec 2010¶

Záhorec, J., Hašková, A., & Munk, M. (2010). Impact of Electronic Teaching Materials on Process of Education – Results of an Experiment. Informatics in Education, 9(2), 261–281. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1064255

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Item 3 measured perceived difficulty of physics. Pre-test: ESA median 2, with 50% of answers in range 1–3. Post-test: 50% of ESA answers within scale range 6–3 at median value 4, i.e. the group as a whole evaluated physics as neither difficult nor easy.

"This was the item in which, apart from the surprising result achieved in the ﬁfth item, we recorded the greatest shift in its evaluation before and after the pedagogical experiment."
