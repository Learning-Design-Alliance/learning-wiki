### Mosling Marte 2026

Mosling Marte, Horn Julie, Xanthoulis Athanasios. (2026). Stepping into the virtual ward: a qualitative study on first-year residents' experiences with VR simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08729-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Authors' interpretive finding from the interview data (Theme 3, Recognizing one's reality in a virtual world). Residents valued cases whose diagnosis and treatment quality allowed transfer to everyday clinical work, emphasizing differences between Norwegian and British guidelines.

> "The engagement of the participants in the present study increased in proportion with the degree of realism in the simulation."
