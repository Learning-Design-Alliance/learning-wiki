## Evidence

### Shen L 2024

Shen L, Xu D, Zhang X. (2024). What factors influencing surgical nurses' competence in implementing person-centered care in the perioperative period?: A cross-sectional study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000040035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Demographic comparison in the cross-sectional survey (n = 437); Table 1 reports mean PCC competence of 84.07 (SD 9.36) for the 372 nurses with humanistic training versus 73.88 (SD 7.08) for the 65 without, t = 8.375, P < .001.

> "In addition, 85.1% of the participants reported receiving humanistic nursing training for over 1 hour."

### Shen L 2024 (2)

Shen L, Xu D, Zhang X. (2024). What factors influencing surgical nurses' competence in implementing person-centered care in the perioperative period?: A cross-sectional study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000040035

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Demographic comparisons in the same survey (n = 437). The article reports no statistically significant differences for gender (t = 0.100, P = .921) or one-child family status (t = 0.319, P = .750); equivalence was not tested.

> "No statistically significant differences were found in the mean PCC competence for gender (t = 0.100, P = .921) or whether it belonged to the one-child family (t = 0.319, P = .750)."
