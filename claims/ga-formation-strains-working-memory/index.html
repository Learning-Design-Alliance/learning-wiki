Lenka Schnaubert 2022¶

Lenka Schnaubert, Daniel Bodemer. (2022). Group awareness and regulation in computer-supported collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09361-1

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

This is a theoretical assumption stated in the article's conceptual section, not a tested result. The article cites prior findings, e.g. learners falling back to habitual learning strategies without external aids, as indirect support and notes this has "been discussed in connection with related processes like partner modelling".

"comparable to metacognitive processes (Valcke, 2002), we assume forming and maintaining GA strains the working memory in addition to processing content-related information"
