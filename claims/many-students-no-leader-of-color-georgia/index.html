Evidence¶

Ed Trust and New Leaders 2022¶

Ed Trust and New Leaders. (2022). Increasing School Leader Diversity in Georgia. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED626443

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis across five student groupings (all students, students of color, White, Black, Latino) using Georgia 2021-22 school-level data. The brief reports the shares attending schools with no leaders of color, with White students highest at 54%.

"The data showed that 34% of all students, 21% of students of color, 54% of White students, 14% of Black students, and 30% of Latino students attended a school with no school leaders of color during the most recent school year."
