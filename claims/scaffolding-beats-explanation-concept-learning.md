## Evidence

### Yafeng Pan 2022

Yafeng Pan, Suzanne Dikker, Yi Zhu, Cuirong Yang, Yi Hu and Pavel Goldstein. (2022). Instructor-learner body coupling reflects instruction and learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00131-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measure mixed design with 48 female learners taught two psychological-concept sets, each under both approaches. The linear mixed-effects model showed a main effect of Instructional Approach (β = 5.48, p = 0.02, R²β = 0.12), with learners "acquired the concepts better when the instructors used a scaffolding approach".

> "indicating that the learners acquired the concepts better when the instructors used a scaffolding approach (M ± SE, 5.37 ± 1.10) compared to an explanation approach (1.46 ± 1.10)"
