## Evidence

### Aslan Seda Aktı 2025

Aslan Seda Aktı, Turgut Yigit Emrah, Ünlü Gökhan, Allison Jordan. (2025). Effectiveness of flipped classroom model in distance education: An experimental study in Turkish language course. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335721

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on the 25-item online course interaction scale administered as a post-test in the quasi-experiment found no significant difference; the article reports "The effect size, as measured by Cohen's d, was 0.30, indicating a small effect".

> "When Table 6 is examined, according to the results of the independent samples t-test on the groups' course interaction scores, there is no significant difference between the groups (t(57)=−1.114, p > .05)."
