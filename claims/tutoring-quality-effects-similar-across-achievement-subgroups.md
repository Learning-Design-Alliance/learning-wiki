## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Exploratory multigroup analyses found measurement invariance across achievement subgroups and similar quality-effect results in all three groups. [→ Guill Karin 2019](#guill-karin-2019)

## Evidence

### Guill Karin 2019

Guill Karin, Lüdtke Oliver, Köller Olaf. (2019). Assessing the instructional quality of private tutoring and its effects on student outcomes: Analyses from the German National Educational Panel Study. The British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12281

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multigroup analyses of the mathematics tutoring model across three subgroups (below-average n = 944, average n = 546, above-average n = 196). The article reports "The results of the three groups were similar", so no subgroup differentially profited from high instructional quality.

> "measurement invariance was found in the factor loadings of the three groups of average, above‐, and below‐average performing students (Δχ2[df = 16] = 29.003, p = .023, ΔCFI = .005). The results of the three groups were similar"
