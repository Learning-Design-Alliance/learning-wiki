## Evidence

### Sopka Saša 2012

Sopka Saša, Biermann Henning, Rossaint Rolf, Knott Sebastian, Skorning Max, Brokmann Jörg C, Heussen Nicole, Beckers Stefan K. (2012). Evaluation of a newly developed media-supported 4-step approach for basic life support training. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-20-37

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Course-evaluation questionnaires from 202 participants in the controlled trial, rated on a 6-point Likert scale. 46.5% (n=46) completely agreed the demonstration of BLS skills was explicit, 59.6% (n=59) completely agreed the video provided sufficient explanations, and only 1% (n=1) preferred a lecture.

> "Overall, at least 99% of the intervention group (n = 99; mean 1.5 ± 0.8; 6-point Likert scale: 1 = completely agree, 6 = completely disagree) agreed that the video provided an adequate introduction to BLS skills."
