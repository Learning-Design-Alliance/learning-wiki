# A collaboration script increases references to CRM heuristics in collaborative elaboration during simulation-based training

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Script learners referred to CRM heuristics significantly more during collaborative phases than control learners, who did not refer to them at all (d=1.80). [→ Zottmann Jan M. 2018](#zottmann-jan-m-2018)

## Evidence

### Zottmann Jan M. 2018

Zottmann Jan M., Dieckmann Peter, Taraszow Tatjana, Rall Marcus, Fischer Frank. (2018). Just watching is not enough: Fostering simulation-based learning with collaboration scripts. GMS Journal for Medical Education 35(3), Doc35. [doi:10.3205/zma001181](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001181)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Audio recordings of learners' comments during collaborative and observational phases were coded by two independent raters for content (Cohen's κ=.74 to .93); 11 of 30 phases were examined. No significant difference emerged for medical factual knowledge, t(16)=0.47, p=.645, n.s.

> "Regarding the content of the collaborative elaboration, a significant difference existed between the script learners and the control condition learners, with the latter not referring at all to CRM heuristics during the collaborative phases examined, t(12)=5.63, p<.001, d=1.80."
