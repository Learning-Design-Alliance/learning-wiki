Moral-García J.E., Urchaga-Litago J.D., Ramos-Morcillo A.J., Maneiro R. (2020). Relationship of Parental Support on Healthy Habits, School Motivations and Academic Performance in Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17030882

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive analysis of APAF4 (guidance support) in the 1100-adolescent cross-sectional study. The article reports "those with high support show more boredom" than the low and medium support groups, a counterintuitive direction printed with group means.

> "The IPAF4 factor, the guidance support, was significantly correlated to the variable of boredom (p = 0.001), those with high support show more boredom (M = 2.21) than those with low (M = 2.08) and medium (M = 2.00)."
