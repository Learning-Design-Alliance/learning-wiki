Kılıç 2023¶

Kılıç, A. F., Koyuncu, İ, & Uysal, İ. (2023). Scale development based on item response theory: A systematic review. International Journal of Psychology and Educational Studies, 10(1), 209-223. https://dx.doi.org/10.52380/ijpes.2023.10.1.982

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's model-type analysis found "Six of the reviewed studies (31.58%) used GRM, nine of them (47.37%) used 2PLM", plus single studies using Rasch, PCM, and GPCM, and one not reporting a model; the studies using 2PLM had polytomous items.

"Six of the reviewed studies (31.58%) used GRM, nine of them (47.37%) used 2PLM, one of them (5.26%) used the Rasch model, one of them (5.26%) used the PCM model, one of them (5.26%) used the GPCM model, and one of them did not report any IRT model."
