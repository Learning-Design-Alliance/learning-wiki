### Argento E 2024

Argento E, Petker T, Vig J, Robertson C, Jaeger A, Necyk C, Thielking P, Walsh Z. (2024). "This is you teaching you:" Exploring providers' perspectives on experiential learning and enhancing patient safety and outcomes in ketamine-assisted therapy. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0306381

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme from the IPA analysis of eight therapist interviews: relational safety emerged throughout the narratives as foundational, enabling clients to become more vulnerable and confront and integrate challenging memories; participants described alliance and medicine as interdependent.

> "While the medicine is an important catalyst, relational safety and therapeutic alliance between the therapist and client was identified as a critical prerequisite to facilitate a deeper exploration and healing process through KAT"
