Evidence¶

Mahendran Vimaladhithan 2024¶

Mahendran Vimaladhithan, Turpin Laura, Boal Matthew, Francis Nader K. (2024). Assessment and application of non-technical skills in robotic-assisted surgery: a systematic review. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10713-1

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review of assessment tools; the search yielded 27,824 studies of which 17 were included. The review states "Three novel rater-based objective assessment tools for assessing NTS in RAS were identified" plus one sensor-based method.

"Three novel rater-based objective assessment tools for assessing NTS in RAS were identified, namely: (i) ICARS (Interpersonal and Cognitive Assessment for Robotic Surgery) behavioural rating system; (ii) RAS-NOTECHS (Robotic-Assisted Surgery-Non-Technical Skills), and (iii) NTSRS (Non-technical Skills in Robotic Surgery)"
