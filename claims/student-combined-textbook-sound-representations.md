Qualitative case analysis of Student 9's exam response (Figure 4). The authors report the student "independently combined two separate visual representations of sound" from the textbook without prompt, showing multimodal learning, and also translated textbook information tables into their own words.

> "What stood out in this exam was that the student independently combined two separate visual representations of sound. The picture with the spring and particles were two different representations in the textbook, yet this student was able to successfully combine them and reproduce them on the exam without prompt."
