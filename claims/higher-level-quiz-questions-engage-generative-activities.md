`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's analysis of practice-quizzing research argues that higher-level quiz questions, unlike restudy controls, "engage learners in generative learning activities such as organizing, elaborating, and generating inferences as well," so their effects are likely partly generative. This is the review's interpretive argument, not a new experiment.

> "However, unlike restudy or note-taking (frequent control conditions in practice quizzing research), quiz questions requiring more than just factual answers engage learners in generative learning activities such as organizing, elaborating, and generating inferences as well"
