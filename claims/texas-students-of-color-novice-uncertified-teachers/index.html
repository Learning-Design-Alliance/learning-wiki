The Education Trust 2022¶

The Education Trust. (2022). Texas Educator Diversity State Profile. https://edtrust.org/educator-diversity/

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of Texas 2018-19 school-level data comparing schools serving the most and fewest Black and Latino students finds "Percent of Students in schools with High (≥20%) percentages of Novice Teachers" of 34% for Black versus 24% for non-Black students, and 29% versus 22% for Latino students; high-uncertifier (≥10%) figures are 11% vs. 6% and 8% vs. 6%.
