## Evidence

### Özgür Tutal 2026

Özgür Tutal. (2026). Artificial intelligence assisted design of a novel cooperative learning technique for higher education. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-50770-1

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Paired samples t-test on the co-regulated learning questionnaire within the experimental CCCT group (n = 31). Post-survey mean "X = 65.45, SD = 5.21" versus pre-survey X = 61.32 (SD = 9.97), p = 0.043, g = 0.512.

> "The results revealed a statistically significant difference (p = 0.043) between the post-survey mean scores (X = 65.45, SD = 5.21) and the pre-survey mean scores (X = 61.32, SD = 9.97). Additionally, the effect size for this improvement in co-regulation behaviors was found to be moderate (g = 0.512)"
