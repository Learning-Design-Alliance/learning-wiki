## Evidence

### Bor Gregorcic 2024

Bor Gregorcic, Giulia Polverini and Andreja Sarlah. (2024). ChatGPT as a tool for honing teachers' Socratic dialogue skills. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad3d21](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad3d21)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Case study of a Socratic dialogue with ChatGPT-4 about a bouncy-ball velocity vs. time graph, after the chatbot's initial answer misdescribed the post-bounce slope. The authors report that "ChatGPT-4 immediately recognises that its answer contains inconsistencies" and corrects them.

> "In contrast to its 2022 version, ChatGPT-4 immediately recognises that its answer contains inconsistencies. It then proceeds to address them and does so in a meaningful and correct way. Its new response is correct."
