Investigating Difficulty Adjustment and Practice Behavior in an Open-Ended Learning Task. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1145/3636555.3636876](https://doi.org/10.1145/3636555.3636876)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-condition contingency analysis (Table 2) addressing RQ3. The article reports that statistical testing was not possible and that, judging from the numbers, the distribution seems uniform except for cluster 3, which had most students from the Self-det condition.

> "Because of the small sample size and the large contingency table, we cannot apply the Chi-squared test and the Fisher’s exact test to check for significant differences"
