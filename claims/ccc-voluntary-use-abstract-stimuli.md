### Frampton 2025

Frampton, S. E., Vesely, S. E., & Jackson, K. (2025). Teaching undergraduates to solve equivalence problems by using cover, copy, and compare: A translational study. Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 58(4), 805–820. https://doi.org/10.1002/jaba.70026

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the Set 2 condition, participants were told it was "another studying condition" but were not explicitly directed to engage in CCC. All participants' fidelity scores increased, indicating voluntary use of the trained strategy with new stimuli.

> "All graphic organizer fidelity scores increased in the studying condition as the participants engaged in CCC with Set 2."
