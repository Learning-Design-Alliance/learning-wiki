Evidence¶

Magdin and Turcáni 2015¶

Martin MAGDIN, Milan TURČÁNI. (2015). A Few Observations and Remarks on Time Effectiveness of Interactive Electronic Testing. Informatics in Education. http://dx.doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.06

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Authors' interpretive finding introduced in the Results of the Research section, based on analysis of finished tests and conversations with students rather than a tested comparison of critical-thinking measures.

"Comparison of interactive and classic tests shows that this new form of testing (3) in fact lowers the development of critical thinking and formal notation of students, on the other hand, it increases their motivation and development of their cognitive abilities."

Magdin and Turcáni 2015 (2)¶

Martin MAGDIN, Milan TURČÁNI. (2015). A Few Observations and Remarks on Time Effectiveness of Interactive Electronic Testing. Informatics in Education. http://dx.doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.06

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis via conversations with students: in paper form "students' priority in this form of testing is to finish the test as soon as possible not taking into consideration the score they will be awarded"; in interactive tests, with no time limit, they leave themselves a chance to actively enter the whole process.

"Conversation with the students, which we used as one of the methods of analysis of obtained experiment data, showed that in classic form of verification of gained knowledge and skills, in each of finished tests, the time effectiveness is purely on psycho logical level."
