`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simulator-recorded number of movements (NOM) in the same 20-trainee case-matched study; ANOVA showed the 3D&MP group (M = 627.2, SD = 352) made "fewer movements relative to controls". Bayes factor for NOM was 52.42, the strongest evidence among outcome measures.

> "the NOM was also significantly different across groups (F(2,17) = 11.57, p = .001, η p ² = 0.58), with the 3D & MP groups (M = 627.2, SD = 352) making fewer movements relative to controls (p = .001)"
