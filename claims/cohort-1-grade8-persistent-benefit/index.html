Hanselman 2017¶

Hanselman, P., Rozek, C. S., Grigg, J., & Borman, G. D. (2017). New evidence on self-affirmation effects and theorized sources of heterogeneity from large-scale replications. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(3), 405-424. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000141

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

This is a follow-up analysis of the cohort 1 randomized trial using grade 8 GPA outcomes with parallel multilevel procedures. The article reports that "self-affirmation group students received significantly higher grades in 8th grade (d=0.152)."

"We found that self-affirmation group students received significantly higher grades in 8th grade (d=0.152), bolstering the interpretation that the intervention led to detectable increases in academic performance for African American and Hispanic students."
