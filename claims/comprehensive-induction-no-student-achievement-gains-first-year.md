# Comprehensive teacher induction generated no significant improvements in student achievement in the first year

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Intensive mentoring and professional development for beginning teachers produced no statistically significant improvements in student achievement during the first year of teaching. [→ Steven Glazerman 2008](#steven-glazerman-2008)

Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/impacts-of-comprehensive-teacher-induction-results-from-the-first-year-of-a-randomized-controlled-study

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

First-year results from a large-scale randomized controlled study of two comprehensive teacher induction programs; the report found no significant improvements in "student achievement during the first year of teaching." No effect size is printed on this page.

> "Intensive mentoring and professional development for teachers just entering the profession did not increase teacher retention or generate any significant improvements in classroom practices or student achievement during the first year of teaching."
