## Evidence

### Edward F. Redish 2010

Edward F. Redish. (2010). Introducing students to the culture of physics: Explicating elements of the hidden curriculum. PERC 2010. [doi:10.1063/1.3515245](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3515245)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

A first-person classroom anecdote: during board derivations students ask 'How did you get that step?', and the instructor realizes he has compressed several steps (multiplying by 2, rearranging an equation) recognized without deliberation, which he then had to explicate for students. This is an interpretive observation, not a controlled study.

> "It becomes clear that these intuitive recognitions are cultural norms, learned as we become physicists. They play a role at different level than the formal, but they play a critical role even in interpreting the formal."
