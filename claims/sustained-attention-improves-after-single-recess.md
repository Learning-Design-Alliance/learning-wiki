Acute pre-post experimental study (Brez, 2017) with 99 3rd–5th graders measured sustained attention via a letter-canceling task and creativity via the Alternate Uses Task before and after recess; only sustained attention improved. No effect size printed.

> "Sustained attention, but not creativity, improved following a single recess period among 3rd and 5th graders [34]."
