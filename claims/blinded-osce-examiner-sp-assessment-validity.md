## Evidence

### Lin Mei-Hsiang 2021

Lin Mei-Hsiang, Lin Shu-Chuan, Lee Yu-Hsia, Wang Pao-Yu, Wu Hon-Yen, Hsu Hsiu-Chin. (2021). The effectiveness of simulation education program on shared decision-making attitudes among nurses in Taiwan. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0257902

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Procedural report from the quasi-experimental study's OSCE operation. Examiners and standardized patients scored participants without knowing group assignment; before the actual OSCE, "Cronbach's α was found to be.90 for the examiners' joint evaluation of the training videos," indicating acceptable inter-examiner consistency.

> "The examiners and SPs were blinded to the group to which the participants belonged."
