## Evidence

### Lewis Michael Steven Paul 2026

Lewis Michael Steven Paul, Smith Paula J. W., Howard Ian Lucas, Farhat Hassan, Alinier Guillaume. (2026). Knowledge, attitudes, and practices of pre-hospital emergency medical care practitioners regarding sepsis recognition: a cross-sectional study in Qatar. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-026-01607-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 221 HMCAS practitioners using two scenario-based vignettes as the primary outcome. The results section reports that "33.5% of respondents correctly determined that the patient in Scenario 1 did not have sepsis, whereas 79.2% correctly identified sepsis in Scenario 2".

> "For the primary outcome, 33.5% of respondents correctly determined that the patient in Scenario 1 did not have sepsis, whereas 79.2% correctly identified sepsis in Scenario 2, which represented a more overt septic presentation."
