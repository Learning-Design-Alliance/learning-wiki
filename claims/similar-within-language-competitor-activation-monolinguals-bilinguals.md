## Evidence

### Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica 2011

Blumenfeld Henrike K.; Marian Viorica. (2011). Bilingualism influences inhibitory control in auditory comprehension. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3582323/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Eye-tracking experiment with 30 monolinguals and 30 bilinguals identifying spoken targets among four pictures. Competitor looks exceeded control looks: "participants looked at competitors on average 18.7% (SE = 0.8%) of the time and at controls on average 12.5% (SE = 0.5%) of the time". No main effect of group or condition-by-group interaction, p > .5.

> "Within this time-window, participants looked at competitors on average 18.7% (SE = 0.8%) of the time and at controls on average 12.5% (SE = 0.5%) of the time, consistent with within-language co-activation effects found in previous eye-tracking studies"
