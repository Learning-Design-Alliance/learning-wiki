## Evidence

### Daniell DiFrancesca 2025

Daniell DiFrancesca, Dan Spencer. (2025). The effects of competitive classroom activities on undergraduates with varied levels of competitive preference. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09741-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A moderation analysis with averaged early Kahoot scores predicting quiz 1 (transformed to percentages) found the interaction significant (B = 0.59, p =.012); the quiz 2 interaction (B = -0.05, p =.86) and quiz 3 interaction (B = -0.12, p =.70) were not significant.

> "However, the interaction between Kahoot performance and competitiveness group was significant (B = 0.59, SE = 0.23, β = 1.53, p =.012). Examination of the interaction revealed a significant positive relationship between Kahoot performance and quiz 1 scores for the HC group. No significant association was found for the LC group."
