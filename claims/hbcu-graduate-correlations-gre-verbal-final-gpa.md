Performance measures of racially underrepresented Ph.D. students in biomedical sciences: The UAMS IMSD Program Outcomes. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0246683. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246683

Spearman rank correlation analysis of IMSD graduates who received undergraduate training from HBCUs (Table 4), relating GRE scores and GPAs to time-to-degree, publications, and post-doctoral fellowships. The article reports the printed coefficients -0.901, 0.922, 0.828, and 0.798 as significant.

> "Table 4 shows a Spearman’s Rho correlation coefficient of -0.901 (Sig. = .006; p < .01) between FINAL GRS GPA and GRE-V score. This represents a significant, very strong negative correlation."
