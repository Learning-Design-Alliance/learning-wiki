### Dowlati 2016

Dowlati, E., Musick, D. W., Zhang, L., Thornton, K., & Carvalho, H. (2016). Use of Dramatization to Teach Cardiac Cycle Physiology to Medical Students. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v4i9.1603

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled crossover study of 42 first-year medical students randomly divided into two groups of 21; Group A received dramatization before lecture, Group B lecture before dramatization. The study reports "88% and 89% resp ectively, p=0.48" on the immediate posttest, a Wilcoxon rank sum comparison with no effect size printed.
