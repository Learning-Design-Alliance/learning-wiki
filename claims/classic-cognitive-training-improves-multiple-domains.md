## Evidence

### Kueider Alexandra M. 2012

Kueider Alexandra M., Parisi Jeanine M., Gross Alden L., Rebok George W. (2012). Computerized Cognitive Training with Older Adults: A Systematic Review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0040588

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Review synthesis of 21 classic cognitive training studies in older adults aged 61 to 95. The review reports median effect sizes per domain: "0.69 for reaction time, 1.30 for processing speed, 0.89 for working memory, 0.39 for executive function, 0.52 for memory, 0.39 for visual spatial abilities, and 0.57 for attention."

> "Across all classic cognitive training studies the median effect size for each cognitive domain was 0.69 for reaction time, 1.30 for processing speed, 0.89 for working memory, 0.39 for executive function, 0.52 for memory, 0.39 for visual spatial abilities, and 0.57 for attention."
