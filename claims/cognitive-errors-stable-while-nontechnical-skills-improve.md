### Prakash Shivesh 2017

Prakash Shivesh, Bihari Shailesh, Need Penelope, Sprick Cyle, Schuwirth Lambert. (2017). Immersive high fidelity simulation of critically ill patients to study cognitive errors: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0871-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression of error occurrence on internship time across the 70 sessions (e.g. anchoring OR 0.84, P = 0.15; search satisfying OR 1.2, P = 0.21). The authors state the "odds of occurrence of various cognitive errors did not change with time spent in the internship year".
