`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Lottery-based 2SLS estimates from Table 3 using 4,215 randomized applicants. The first stage shows a preschool offer increases the probability of preschool attendance by 65 percentage points, and the estimate that preschool "increases on-time college enrollment by 8.3 percentage points" is the just-identified ratio of reduced form to first stage.

> "enrollment at a Boston preschool increases on-time college enrollment by 8.3 percentage points. This estimate, which is statistically significant at the 1% level, implies an 18% increase in on-time college enrollment relative to the 46% rate for non-offered students"
