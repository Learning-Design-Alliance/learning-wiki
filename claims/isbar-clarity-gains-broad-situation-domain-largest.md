Sun Jui-Chiung, Chang Min-Yu, Tsai Yi-Chun. (2026). Changes in Nursing Students' Clinical Handover Performance Following ISBAR-Structured Handover Simulation Teaching: A Mixed-Methods Study. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70955

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level exploratory analysis of the Communication Clarity Scale in a single-group pretest-posttest study of 57 nursing students. All 14 items showed significant pre-post differences, remaining significant under Bonferroni adjustment.

> "Post-test mean scores on all 14 items across the five domains—Introduction, Situation, Background, Assessment and Recommendation—exceeded pre-test means and demonstrated statistically significant differences (all p < 0.001 except 'State the location', p = 0.002; all remained significant under the Bonferroni-adjusted alpha of 0.0036)."
