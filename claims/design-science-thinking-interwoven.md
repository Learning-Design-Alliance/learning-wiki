Interpretation of the authors' prior qualitative studies of group discussions and written reports in the introductory physics laboratory (type e claim). The analysis revealed interplay between design and science thinking, supporting "design-science connection as opposed to design-science gap".

> "Rather than unfolding in isolated silos, design thinking and science thinking appear naturally interwoven throughout these activities. This interplay, revealed in our analysis of student data, motivated us to advocate for the usage of design-science connection as opposed to design-science gap in our recent study [21]."
