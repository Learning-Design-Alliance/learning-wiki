## Evidence

### Diana Sachmpazidi 2025

Diana Sachmpazidi, Mike Verostek, Jayna Petrella, Chandra Turpen, and Siwoo (Randy) Lee. (2025). Psychometric Evaluation of the Culture around Systemic Change Survey: A tool for Assessing Departmental Culture in Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.09953](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.09953)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reliability analysis of the CSCS current scale in the same N = 111 pilot sample. The article reports ωh = 0.88, meaning "88% of the total score variance is attributable to a general factor," so the total score can be regarded as a sufficiently reliable measure of the general factor.

> "For the total score, we find ωt = 0 .98 and α = 0 .97, which indicate nearly all of the observed score variance in the 35 items can be attributed to “true score” variance."
