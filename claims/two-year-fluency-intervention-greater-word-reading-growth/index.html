q3 · i? · review · r1

Miciak et al. (2018) randomly assigned 484 fourth graders to one year (n=161), two years (n=162), or control (n=161) of small-group fluency intervention. The two-year group showed the greater word-reading growth quoted; "There were no statistically significant differences between the three groups on reading comprehension".

"students who received two years of intervention showed greater growth than both the one-year intervention group and the control group on the WJ-III Letter/Word Identification and TOWRE Sight Word Efficiency subtests"
