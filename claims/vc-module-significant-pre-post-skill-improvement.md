## Evidence

### Liew Siaw Cheok 2023

Liew Siaw Cheok, Tan Maw Pin, Breen Emer, Krishnan Kuhan, Sivarajah Inthirani, Raviendran Nivashinie, Aung Thidar, Nimir Amal, Pallath Vinod. (2023). Microlearning and online simulation-based virtual consultation training module for the undergraduate medical curriculum – a preliminary evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04777-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post pilot evaluation with 52 students completing a 20-min DOVCS assessment in week 1 and week 6. "The mean pre-test score was 13.22 (SD = 3.4) and mean post-test score was 23.42 (SD = 4.4)"; 45 (86.5%) scored above the 50% passing mark at post-test.

> "The pre-test and post-test DOVCS evaluation showed a significant improvement in the medical students' scores in the post-test assessment compared with the pre-test (t-score = 16.33, p < 0.05). The mean pre-test score was 13.22 (SD = 3.4) and mean post-test score was 23.42 (SD = 4.4)."
