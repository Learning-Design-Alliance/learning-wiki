## Evidence

### Mirata 2023

Mirata, V., & Bergamin, P. (2023). Role of organisational readiness and stakeholder acceptance: an implementation framework of adaptive learning for higher education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10248-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative Delphi data from both university panels; the article reports that instructor motivation was identified as a facilitator positively influencing students' perception that adaptive learning was agreeable or satisfactory (Fig. 1). Experts reported that "motivation was crucial for acceptance and implementation".

> "Only if instructors are engaged in using an adaptive learning system in their courses, all potentials of this technology can be brought to students. The students can use as much of this technology as instructors use it in their courses"
