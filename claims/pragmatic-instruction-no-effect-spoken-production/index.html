Evidence¶

Üstünbaş 2017¶

Üstünbaş, Ü. (2017). Language Learners' Feelings about Pragmatics in Action. Novitas-ROYAL (Research on Youth and Language), 11(2), 155-168. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/novitasroyal

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Participants were administered an interactive conversation task after three weeks and their performances recorded to detect transfer of routines into spoken discourse. The authors state the effect on pragmatic competence "is relatively higher" than on production.

"However, only 3 of the participants used pragmatic routines after 3 weeks of instruction, indicating pragmatic instruction has no effect on spoken production."
