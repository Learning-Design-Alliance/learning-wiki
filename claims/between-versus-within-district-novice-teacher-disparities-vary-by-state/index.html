q2 · i? · associational · r2

Decomposition analysis estimating the intraclass correlation to separate within-district from between-district variation in novice-teacher percentages. The report finds "more than 75% of the variation in student access to experienced teachers occurs between districts" in six named states, while Florida, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Nevada, Oregon, South Carolina, South Dakota, and Wyoming show less than 25% between-district variation.

"in Arizona, Louisiana, North Carolina, Ohio, Rhode Island, and Utah, more than 75% of the variation in student access to experienced teachers occurs between districts."
