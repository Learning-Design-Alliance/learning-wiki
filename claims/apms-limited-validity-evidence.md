## Evidence

### Tesfai FM 2024

Tesfai FM, Nagi J, Morrison I, Boal M, Olaitan A, Chandrasekaran D, Stoyanov D, Lanceley A, Francis N. (2024). Objective assessment tools in laparoscopic or robotic‐assisted gynecological surgery: A systematic review. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14840

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Systematic review synthesis of the 11 identified APMs (VR simulators such as da Vinci Surgical Simulation, LapSim, LapMentor, MIST). The review reports "the majority of APMs (81.8%) has limited validity evidence" and that this hinders transferability beyond research environments.

> "However, this review showed that the majority of APMs (81.8%) has limited validity evidence. This low level of validity hinders transferability outside of the research environment."

### Tesfai FM 2024 (2)

Tesfai FM, Nagi J, Morrison I, Boal M, Olaitan A, Chandrasekaran D, Stoyanov D, Lanceley A, Francis N. (2024). Objective assessment tools in laparoscopic or robotic‐assisted gynecological surgery: A systematic review. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14840

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review analysis of response-process validity for APMs: because metrics are recorded by the simulator, rater bias is absent. The review states APMs are "automated, hence removing rater bias"; it also notes no APMs were used intraoperatively and metrics alone cannot substitute experts' input.

> "All APMs inherently demonstrate response process, as they are automated, hence removing rater bias, and theoretically having 100% reliability."
