### Sidorkin 2025

Sidorkin, A. M. (2025). Form–Substance Discrimination: Concept, Cognition, and Pedagogy. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.00412](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.00412)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing empirical work by Pennycook and colleagues, that experimental participants attributed profundity to vacuous phrases, and that "analytical reasoning capacity and skeptical disposition significantly predict resistance to pseudo -profound content." No effect sizes are printed in the article.

> "Their findings reveal that analytical reasoning capacity and skeptical disposition significantly predict resistance to pseudo -profound content —precisely the cognitive characteristics that underlie effective FSD."
