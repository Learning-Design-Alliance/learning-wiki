## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In SP12 (N=35), perceived utility of tutorials (4.0±0.2) was comparable with other course elements except clicker questions (4.5±0.1), but in FA12 (N=29) tutorials (3.3±0.2) rated significantly lower on utility than pure lecturing (4.4±0.1). [→ Baily 2013](#baily-2013)

## Evidence

### Baily 2013

Baily, C., Dubson, M., & Pollock, S. (2013). Developing Tutorials for Advanced Physics Students: Processes and Lessons Learned. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.003](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.003)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Post-survey ratings on a 1-5 usefulness scale from SP12 (N=35) and FA12 (N=29) students, reported with standard errors on the mean (Fig. 1). The comparison shows "both tutorials (3.3±0.2) and clickers (4.0±0.2) rated significantly lower on utility than pure lecturing (4.4±0.1)" in FA12.

> "In SP12 (N=35), the perceived utility of tutorials (4.0±0.2) was comparable with other course elements, with the exception of the highly rated clicker questions (4.5±0.1). However, in the FA12 term (N=29), where only a subset of the tutorials and concept tests were used, both tutorials (3.3±0.2) and clickers (4.0±0.2) rated significantly lower on utility than pure lecturing (4.4±0.1)."
