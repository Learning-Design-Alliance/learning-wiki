N.G. Holmes, Dhaneesh Kumar and D.A. Bonn. (2017). Toolboxes and handing students a hammer: The effects of cueing and instruction on getting students to think critically. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010116](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.010116)

Ordinal logistic regression on the four-level follow-up coding (comparison through execute/evaluate) from lab notebooks of the three cohorts.
