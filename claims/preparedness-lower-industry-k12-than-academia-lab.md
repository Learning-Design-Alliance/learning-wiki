`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square tests on self-rated 1–7 preparedness across four career paths (Figure 8) showed respondents "rated their preparation for industry and teaching K-12 signiﬁcantly lower ( p < 0.01)" than preparation for academia or laboratory careers.
