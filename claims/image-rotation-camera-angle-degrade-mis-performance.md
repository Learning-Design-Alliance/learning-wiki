# Increased image rotation and suboptimal camera and monitor angles degrade MIS performance

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` All reviewed studies agreed that increased degree of image rotation decreased performance, though conclusions about the optimal angle varied. [→ Vajsbaher Tina 2018](#vajsbaher-tina-2018)
`q2 i?` Camera rotations between 15° and 90° led to a 10 to 30% increase in error due to decreased directional spatial-motor ability. [→ Vajsbaher Tina 2018](#vajsbaher-tina-2018)

## Evidence

### Vajsbaher Tina 2018

Vajsbaher Tina, Schultheis Holger, Francis Nader K. (2018). Spatial cognition in minimally invasive surgery: a systematic review. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-018-0416-1

`q2 · i?`

Review synthesis on ergonomic setup. The review reports monitor displays increased performance when located directly opposite the surgeon at a 180° angle, and that "Reduction in performance was reported with camera rotations of 270° angles" and between 15° to 90° angles.

> "All studies agreed that increased degree of image rotation decreased performance, although the conclusion in regards to the optimal degree of angle varied."
