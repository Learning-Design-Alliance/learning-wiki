## Evidence

### Alavi and Keyvanshekouh 2012

Sepideh Alavi, Afsaneh Keyvanshekouh. (2012). Using the MoodleReader as an Extensive Reading Tool and its Effect on Iranian EFL Students' Incidental Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 5(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v5n6p135

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Matched t-test on the experimental group's gain scores (Table 4 reports t = 5.795, df = 17, p = .000). The article reports the difference "significant (p=.000)" and an effect size of 0.6, described as large.

> "the mean gains for recognition and production vocabularies of the experimental group are 2.3 and 10.72, respectively. In order to determine whether the difference observed between the means was significant or not, a matched t-test was run"
