## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bailey Elizabeth G. 2021

Bailey Elizabeth G., Greenall Rebeka F., Tullis Madeleine M., Williams Kurt R. (2021). The retention benefits of cumulative versus non-cumulative midterms in introductory biology may depend on students’ reasoning skills. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0250143

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-semester attitudinal survey compared by Mann-Whitney U test; overall attitude toward the midterms did not differ by section, but "students in the non-cumulative section were more likely to think the other treatment would have been more helpful".

> "students in the non-cumulative section were more likely to think the other treatment would have been more helpful (Mann-Whitney U test: U = 9273, p = 0.009, n = 299)."
