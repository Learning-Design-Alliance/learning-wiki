## Evidence

### Moeller 2017

Moeller, J., Brackett, M. A., Ivcevic, Z., & White, A. E. (2017). High school students' feelings: Discoveries from a large national survey and an experience sampling study. Preprint / self-archived. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=High+school+students%E2%80%99+feelings+Moeller+Brackett+Ivcevic+White

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Rating-scale results from the same national online survey (N = 21,678), using PANAS-C items plus stressed and bored items on a 0 (never) to 100 (always) scale. The survey found students "most often reported feeling stressed (79.83% of the time) and bored (69.51% of the time)".

> "Students most often reported feeling stressed (79.83% of the time) and bored (69.51% of the time)."

### Moeller 2017 (2)

Moeller, J., Brackett, M. A., Ivcevic, Z., & White, A. E. (2017). High school students' feelings: Discoveries from a large national survey and an experience sampling study. Preprint / self-archived. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=High+school+students%E2%80%99+feelings+Moeller+Brackett+Ivcevic+White

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

PANAS subscale means from the same national survey; the PANAS factor structure was supported in a confirmatory factor analysis (CFI = .974; RMSEA = .056). Students "reported positive feelings on average 46.60% of the time and negative feelings 40.67 of the time".

> "Results from the PANAS subscales of positive affect and negative affect showed that students reported positive feelings on average 46.60% of the time and negative feelings 40.67 of the time."
