q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of longer follow-up periods from the same 205-study corpus; the pooled follow-up effect size is "ES = 0.27, 95% CI = [0.17, 0.36]", and the review reports positive effects up to 2 years after intervention end with evidence of fadeout, only five studies measuring effects beyond 2 years.

"The overall average effect sizes (ES) for short‐term and follow‐up outcomes were positive and statistically significant (ES = 0.30, 95% confidence interval [CI] = [0.25, 0.34] and ES = 0.27, 95% CI = [0.17, 0.36]), respectively)."
