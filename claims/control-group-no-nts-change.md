## Evidence

### Moll-Khosrawi Parisa 2021

Moll-Khosrawi Parisa, Zöllner Christian, Cencin Nadine, Schulte-Uentrop Leonie. (2021). Flipped learning enhances non-technical skill performance in simulation-based education: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02766-w

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Follow-up tests of the two-way ANOVA in the randomised controlled trial. The article prints non-significant control-group changes (e.g. "partial η² = 0.06") and significant intervention-group changes with partial η² = 0.42, 0.31 and 0.36 for dimensions one to three.

> "the control group did not change significantly over time (dimension one: F (1, 34) = 2.15, p = .152, partial η² = 0.06; dimension two: F (1,34) = 1.06, p = .310, partial η² = 0.03; dimension three: F (1,34) = 1.52, p = .226, partial η² = 0.04), whereas the mean scores of the intervention group changed significantly over time (dimension one: F (1,44) = 31.70, p < .001, partial η² = 0.42; dimension two: F (1,44) = 20, p < .001, partial η² = 0.31; dimension three: F (1,44) = 24.44, p < .001, partial η² = 0.36)."
