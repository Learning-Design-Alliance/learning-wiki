## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Feedback-group participants showed significantly reduced inattention duration versus Controls in a between-subjects video-viewing experiment. [→ Songlin Xu 2023](#songlin-xu-2023)
`q3 i?` Feedback-group participants also showed significantly reduced confusion duration versus Controls. [→ Songlin Xu 2023 (2)](#songlin-xu-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Songlin Xu 2023

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects online experiment (N=311 adults from Prolific) comparing video viewing with vs. without visualized peer attention regions; inattention detected via face-lost from webcam gaze tracking. The article reports a "59.8% decrease vs. Controls" with F1,301 = 6.925, P = 0.009; no effect size printed.

> "Analyses of learning experiences revealed significant reductions in inattention duration for Feedback group (59.8% decrease vs. Controls; F1,301 = 6.925,P = 0.009 < 0.01)."

### Songlin Xu 2023 (2)

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same between-subjects experiment; confusion recorded via participants' clicks on slides. The article reports an "81.3% reduction" in confusion duration with F1,299 = 6.384, P = 0.012; no effect size printed.

> "Feedback also decreased confusion duration (81.3% reduction; F1,299 = 6.384,P = 0.012 < 0.05)."
