## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Xiong Yuan 2026

Xiong Yuan, Wang Mingjun. (2026). Psychological resilience as a selective perceptual amplifier in post-crisis learning: a dual-pathway model of academic motivation, perceived school support, and cognitive engagement – a multi-country study based on PISA 2022. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04665-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Design-corrected regression with gender × predictor interaction terms on PISA 2022 data, with male as reference group. Gender moderated only the AM→CES path: "β = -0.026, p < .001". The AM→PSS (β = -0.006, p = 0.248) and PSS→CES (β = 0.011, p = 0.113) paths were not significantly moderated.
