## Evidence

Evidence for ESSA. (2021). Intelligent Tutoring for the Structure Strategy (ITSS): Elementary Reading and Secondary Reading evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/intelligent-tutoring-for-the-structure-strategy-itss-elementary/

Randomized study with "more than 2,000 fourth-grade students," classrooms randomly assigned to ITSS or regular language arts instruction, outcomes assessed after 6 to 7 months. The page reports the effect was "positive but not statistically significant in Grade 4, with an effect size = +0.10."
