Evidence¶

Supandi 2018¶

Supandi, S., Waluya, B., Rochmad, R., Suyitno, H., & Dewi, K. (2018). Think-Talk-Write Model for Improving Students' Abilities in Mathematical Representation. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 77-90. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.1136a

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Omnibus two-way ANOVA in the 2x3 factorial quasi-experiment (64 eighth-grade students); the interaction term reached significance at α = 0.05 with Fcount 3.86 against Ftable 3.16. No effect size is printed.

"Another calculation confirmed that H0AB is rejected, showing that there is an interaction effect between the learning strategies (A) and self-efficacy (B)."
