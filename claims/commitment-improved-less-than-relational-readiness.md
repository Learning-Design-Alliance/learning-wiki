## Evidence

### Cardile Davide 2023

Cardile Davide, Ielo Augusto, Corallo Francesco, Cappadona Irene, D'Aleo Giangaetano, De Cola Maria Cristina, Bramanti Placido, Ciurleo Rosella. (2023). Communication Training: Significance and Effects of a Preliminary Psychological Intervention upon an Audit Team. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20054173

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post administration of the Scale of Perceived Self-Efficacy at Work to the 10-member audit team. The commitment dimension (beliefs about committing to and organizing oneself to achieve work objectives) rose from a median of 20.0 to 21.0; the article reports a statistically significant difference with no effect size.

> "Commitment scores ranged from 18 to 22, with a median value of 20.0 (20.0 for males and 20.5 for females), at T0 and from 20 to 23, with a median value of 21.0 (21.0 for males and 21.0 for females), at T1 (Figure 5)."
