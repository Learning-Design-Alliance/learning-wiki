ANOVA on in-game resource use across SRL groups. High SRL students "run significantly fewer tests than Medium or Low SRL students (as indicated by Tukey post-hoc comparisons)", suggesting more careful test selection.

> "ANOV As for student use of each of these features indicated a significant difference in student use of posters (F(2, 257) = 5.28, p<0.01), and tests (F(2, 257) = 5.59, p<0.01)."
