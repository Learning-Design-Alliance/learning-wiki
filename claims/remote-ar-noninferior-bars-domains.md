## Evidence

### Gianotto-Oliveira R 2026

Gianotto-Oliveira R, Rojas M, Queiroz M, Zanchetta F, Ferrari A, Kojima L, Paula A, Campos B, Cecilio-Fernandes D, Caruso TJ. (2026). Remote Augmented Reality Versus Traditional Simulation for Team Leader Assessment in a Cardiac Arrest Scenario: Noninferiority Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/84367

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary noninferiority analysis of the same RCT using the Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scale across four domains, scored 1-9 by two trained raters. Domain means were, for example, "8.2 (0.7)" (traditional vigilance) versus "8.3 (0.7)" (AR). No effect sizes were reported.

> "The remote AR group demonstrated performance comparable to the in-person group using BARS (Table 3). For each BARS domain, the lower bound of the 95% CI for the AR group exceeded the predefined noninferiority margin, indicating noninferior performance relative to traditional in-person simulation."
