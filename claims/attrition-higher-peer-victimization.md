## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, J., Kaufman, T., Mastrotheodoros, S., & Branje, S. (2025). Longitudinal associations between academic motivation and school-related stressors in adolescents transitioning to secondary school. Learning and Instruction, 98, 102144. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102144

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Attrition analysis in the four-wave longitudinal study (290 consented; 214, 73.8%, completed the final wave). No significant differences were found in gender, age, family structure, SES, or the other study variables; Little's MCAR test was not significant.

> "there was a significant difference between adolescents who participated at all time points and those who dropped out in terms of peer victimization, F (1, 270) = 6.85, p = .009, indicating that participants who withdrew from the study reported more peer victimization."
