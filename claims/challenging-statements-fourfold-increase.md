## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2011

Ben Van Dusen, Mike Ross, and Valerie Otero. (2011). Changing Identities and Evolving Conceptions of Inquiry through Teacher-Driven Professional Development. [doi:10.1063/1.3680073](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3680073)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Video analysis using the generatively developed 'challenging' code, which marks when a teacher or researcher challenges another group member's statement. Early responses "tended to be supportive and reassuring." By year's end teachers engaged in debates about responsibilities and whether activities were inquiry-based.

> "During the middle time period, however, we see over a four-fold increase in the number of challenging statements."
