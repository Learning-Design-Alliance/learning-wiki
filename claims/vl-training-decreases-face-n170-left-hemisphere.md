## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In the VL group, left-hemisphere face N170 amplitude decreased significantly after training, while character and tool amplitudes did not change significantly. [→ Zhao Pei 2015](#zhao-pei-2015)

## Evidence

### Zhao Pei 2015

Zhao Pei, Li Su, Zhao Jing, Gaspar Carl M., Weng Xuchu. (2015). Training by visual identification and writing leads to different visual word expertise N170 effects in preliterate Chinese children. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2015.09.002

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Left-hemisphere (T5) ANOVA with a significant Training group × Time × Category interaction (F(2,54) = 4.41, p = 0.02, η2 = 0.14); post hoc tests showed "the face N170 amplitude decreased significantly after training" (p = 0.03) in the VL group, with no significant changes in the FW group.

> "However, the face N170 amplitude decreased significantly after training compared to before training (p = 0.03). While in the FW group, the N170 amplitudes of characters, faces, and tools did not change significantly after training compared to before training"
