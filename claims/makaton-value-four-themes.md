## Evidence

### Mc Geer 2026

Mc Geer, C., Milton, C., Vorster, C., & Graham, M.A. (2026). South African speech-language therapists’ perceived competencies and use of Makaton. South African Journal of Communication Disorders, 73(1), a1154. https://doi.org/10.4102/sajcd.v73i1.1154

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic analysis using Braun and Clarke’s six-phase framework of open-ended survey responses from 57 SLTs asked why they found Makaton beneficial. Example responses included that it is "a low-cost option for lower socioeconomic areas" and that it "promotes Speech skills while using gestures as building blocks".

> "Four key themes emerged from their responses: (1) multimodal communication, (2) accessibility and practicality, (3) speech and language development and (4) inclusion and social use."
