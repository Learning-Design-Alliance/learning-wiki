# VSP-TCM training improves formative mini-CEX scores in medical interviewing, clinical judgment, and comprehensive ability versus traditional academic training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` After 6 weeks, the VSP-TCM group scored higher than the control group in medical interviewing ability, clinical judgment, and comprehensive ability on the modified mini-CEX. [→ Yang H 2023](#yang-h-2023)
`q2 i?` The VSP-TCM group showed no advantage in physical examination or disease treatment, scoring slightly lower than the control group on these two mini-CEX aspects. [→ Yang H 2023 (2)](#yang-h-2023-2)

## Evidence

### Yang H 2023

Yang H, Xiao X, Wu X, Fu X, Du Q, Luo Y, Li B, Zeng J, Zhang Y. (2023). Virtual Standardized Patients Versus Traditional Academic Training for Improving Clinical Competence Among Traditional Chinese Medicine Students: Prospective Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/43763

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Formative evaluation in a single-blind 2-group randomized controlled trial of 84 TCM sophomores, scored with a modified mini-CEX (inter-rater ICC=0.82). The VSP-TCM group outperformed controls on three of five aspects, with the printed Mann-Whitney results quoted above.

> "After 6 weeks of training, the VSP-TCM group gained higher scores in medical interviewing ability (mean 7.19, SD 0.63, vs mean 6.83, SD 0.81; U=656.5, P=.04), clinical judgment (mean 6.48, SD 0.98, vs mean 5.86, SD 1.04; U=590, P=.006), and comprehensive ability (mean 6.71, SD 0.59, vs mean 6.40, SD 0.58; U=634.5, P=.02) than the control group."

### Yang H 2023 (2)

Yang H, Xiao X, Wu X, Fu X, Du Q, Luo Y, Li B, Zeng J, Zhang Y. (2023). Virtual Standardized Patients Versus Traditional Academic Training for Improving Clinical Competence Among Traditional Chinese Medicine Students: Prospective Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/43763

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null mini-CEX results from the same formative evaluation: no significant difference in physical examination or disease treatment, with VSP-TCM means slightly lower than the control group's.

> "However, the VSP-TCM group did not show the expected advantages in physical examination (mean 6.14, SD 1.19, vs mean 6.29, SD 1.20; U=830, P=.64) and disease treatment (mean 6.88, SD 0.98, vs mean 6.74, SD 1.16; U=827, P=.62), and the score was slightly lower than that of the control group."
