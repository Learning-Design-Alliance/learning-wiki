## Evidence

### Josiah Owolabi & Tobiloba Faith Adaramati 2015

Josiah Owolabi & Tobiloba Faith Adaramati. (2015). Effects of Graphic Organiser on Students' Achievement in Algebraic Word Problems. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.5. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083649

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

ANCOVA of post-test scores in a pre-test/post-test non-equivalent group control design with 40 SS2 students across three groups, pre-test as covariate. The article reports a "significant main effect of treatment" with partial Eta squared of 0.235, implying treatment accounts for 23.5% of variance.

> "The result in table 4 indicate that there is a sign ificant main effect of treatment (Graphic organiser ) on the students' academic achievement in algebraic word problem {F ( 4.775) = 0.016, p < 0.05)}."
