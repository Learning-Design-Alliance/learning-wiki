## Evidence

### Bradley LJ 2025

Bradley LJ, Meyer KE, Kerr MS, Maddux SD, Heck AJ, Reeves RE, Handler EK. (2025). Do active learning techniques promote higher academic performance in an online graduate anatomy course? Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70066

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-course quasi-experimental rotation design in an online Structural Anatomy course: during each of five units, four groups did a different active learning technique while one group served as control. The study reports "no statistically significant differences" in examination scores between groups for units 1-4 and final course performance; equivalence was not tested.

> "Results of Kruskal–Wallis tests revealed no statistically significant differences (p  >  0.05) in learners' academic performance scores between any of the five groups (i.e., four active learning; one control) for units 1, 2, 3, and 4 or final course performance."
