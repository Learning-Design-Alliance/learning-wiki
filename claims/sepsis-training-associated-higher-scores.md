## Evidence

### Roye-Green Karen J. 2025

Roye-Green Karen J., Vickers Ivan, Priestley Sharon, Walker Jerome, Willis Rohan. (2025). Knowledge, practice and attitudes of healthcare students to sepsis management in Jamaica. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07122-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey analysis comparing the 96 (32.8%) students reporting sepsis training with untrained students on the four tabulated scores. Trained students had "higher mean correct knowledge (3.1±1.5 vs. 2.6±1.6, p = 0.011)" and higher correct practice scores; incorrect knowledge showed no difference.

> "Overall, students who had some form of sepsis training had higher mean correct knowledge (3.1±1.5 vs. 2.6±1.6, p = 0.011) and correct practice scores (6.8±2.5 vs. 4.6±2.6, p < 0.001) as well as lower mean incorrect practice scores (2.8±1.3 vs. 3.9±1.4, p < 0.001)"
