Guilding C, Pye RE, Butler S, Atkinson M, Field E. (2021). Answering questions in a co-created formative exam question bank improves summative exam performance, while students perceive benefits from answering, authoring, and peer discussion: A mixed methods analysis of PeerWise. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two researchers categorized a random sample of 100 student-authored questions using Anderson and Krathwohl's revised Bloom's taxonomy, categorizing independently and resolving discrepancies by discussion. Percentages are descriptive.

> "Thirteen percent of the questions were categorized as testing knowledge, 28% comprehension, 18% application, 18% analysis, 12% evaluation, and 11% creation."
