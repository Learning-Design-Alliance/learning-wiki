Kerneža and Košir 2016¶

Maja Kerneža and Katja Košir. (2016). Comics as a Literary-Didactic Method and Their Use for Reducing Gender Differences in Reading Literacy at the Primary Level of Education. c e p s Journal, Vol. 6, No. 2. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1129008

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Discussion of the quasi-experimental study's hypothesis tests: hypotheses H1, H3 and H5, on reducing gender differences, were rejected. The article states "the designed programme was not proved to be a suitable method for reducing gender differences in reading literacy."

"In the present study, the designed programme was not proved to be a suitable method for reducing gender differences in reading literacy. In most cases, improvement was identified in the outcome of one gender or the other."
