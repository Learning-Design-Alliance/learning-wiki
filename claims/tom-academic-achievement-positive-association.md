## Evidence

### Lecce 2026

Lecce, S., Stagnitto, S. M., Mascheretti, S., Lampis, V., & Devine, R. T. (2026). Children's Theory of Mind and Academic Achievement: a Meta-analysis on Reading Comprehension and Math Skills. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10185-w

`q4 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level meta-analysis of 33 eligible cross-sectional papers and 89 effect sizes, approximately 5,192 participants aged 4.30–13.50 years. The pooled effect size was r = 0.32 (95% CI [.27, .36], p < .001), with a 95% prediction interval of 0.08 to 0.58; the printed correlation "r = 0.32, CI [.27, .36]" is a medium-sized effect.
