Evidence¶

Seyyed Kazem Banihashem 2022¶

Seyyed Kazem Banihashem, Omid Noroozi, Stan van Ginkel, Leah P. Macfadyen, Harm J.A. Biemans. (2022). A systematic review of the role of learning analytics in enhancing feedback practices in higher education. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2022.100489

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Karaoglan Yilmaz (2022) and Kohnke et al. (2022), that "students' academic performance has also been improved" when feedback practices are enhanced by LA insights; Table 5 lists academic performance in 17 of 46 studies (37%).

"In a considerable number of reviewed studies (e.g., Karaoglan Yilmaz, 2022; Kohnke et al., 2022) we found that when feedback practices are enhanced by LA insights, students’ academic performance has also been improved."
