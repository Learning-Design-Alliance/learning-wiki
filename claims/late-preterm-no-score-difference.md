### Lock Nicole E. 2024

Lock Nicole E., DeBoer Mark D., Scharf Rebecca J., Miller Sarah E. (2024). Academic performance in moderately and late preterm children in the United States: are they catching up? Journal of Perinatology 44(6). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41372-024-01938-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multivariable linear regression of direct child assessments in the weighted ECLS-K:2011 cohort (773 children born 35-36 6/7 weeks vs 14350 term children) found "no significant difference" in any subject; equivalence was not formally tested and no effect size was printed.
