# For biochemistry students with relatively higher topic-specific prior knowledge, instruction-first (I-PS) outperformed problem-solving-first (PS-I) on a near-transfer test

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Biochemistry students randomly assigned to I-PS scored significantly higher than PS-I students on the near-transfer post-test, controlling for prior knowledge. [→ Cheng-Wen He 2025](#cheng-wen-he-2025)

## Evidence

### Cheng-Wen He 2025

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized classroom experiment with 138 biochemistry students assigned to I-PS (n=64) or PS-I (n=74), analyzed with ANCOVA controlling for prior knowledge test score. The results indicated that I-PS students "significantly outperformed the PS-I students" on near transfer, supporting the cognitive load hypothesis.

> "the I-PS students ( M = 10.72,SD = 5.34 ) significantly outperformed the PS-I students ( M=9 .47,SD = 4.88 ) on the near-transfer test, F(1,136) = 4.51 , p = .036"
