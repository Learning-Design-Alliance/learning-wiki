## Evidence

### Karimi Mehrdad 2026

Karimi Mehrdad, Zareipour MoradAli, Zeynali Esmayil, Sharafkhani Rahim. (2026). Investigating spiritual well-being and its relationship with health behaviors, depression, and academic achievement in medical students: a structural equation modeling study. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04902-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM mediation analysis in the same 362-student model: SWB had a significant total indirect association with GPA (β = 0.15, p = 0.03), carried through SWB → health behaviors (particularly no smoking) → lower depression → higher GPA, and SWB → lower depression → higher GPA. The direct path "was not statistically significant", which the authors read as "consistent with the presence of mediation" and full mediation by health behaviors and depression.

> "The direct path from SWB to GPA was not statistically significant (β = 0.15, p = 0.47), which is consistent with the presence of mediation."
