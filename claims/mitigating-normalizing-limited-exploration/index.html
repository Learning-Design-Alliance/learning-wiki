Dana Vedder-Weiss 2025¶

Dana Vedder-Weiss, Rotem Tractenberg-Maslaton, Karin Sarfati-Shaulov. (2025). Facilitation strategies responding to emotional displays in PD discourse: navigating relational and learning goals. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09737-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Microanalysis of a steering committee meeting episode where a teacher shared stress; facilitators relabeled stress as "alertness, stimulation" and normalized it. The article reports "this discussion included some exploration of the emotion, it was limited to attributions that did not imply a critique of the PD design and facilitation."
