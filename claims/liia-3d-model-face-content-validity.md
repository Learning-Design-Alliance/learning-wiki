## Evidence

### Xia Jianfu 2023

Xia Jianfu, Wu Junjie, Chen Hao, Mao Jinlei, Xu Xiaodong, Zhang Jing, Yang Jin, Wang Zhifei. (2023). Assessment of laparoscopic intracorporeal intestinal anastomosis training using simulation-based 3D printed models: exploring surgical performance and learning curves. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000582

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Face-validity questionnaire completed by five experts using a 15-item 5-point Likert instrument. Medians were 4 for fidelity, materiality, appearance, and haptic suture feedback, with "Texture" rated 4 (P25 3), which the authors read as a need to improve texture for suturing.

> "The median scores for overall evaluation, size, operating conditions, and feedback of the stapler were all five points, meaning that the experts were strongly endorsing the performance of the model."

### Xia Jianfu 2023 (2)

Xia Jianfu, Wu Junjie, Chen Hao, Mao Jinlei, Xu Xiaodong, Zhang Jing, Yang Jin, Wang Zhifei. (2023). Assessment of laparoscopic intracorporeal intestinal anastomosis training using simulation-based 3D printed models: exploring surgical performance and learning curves. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000582

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content-validity questionnaire from the same five experts; all content items had a median of 5, including recommendation for use in LIIA training. No effect size printed.

> "All experts gave the model a median content validity score of 5, indicating that they considered it could simulate the actual surgical settings."
