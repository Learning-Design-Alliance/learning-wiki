## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The average performance was failed by 2 individual examiners but not failed by any examiner pair, and the Wilcoxon signed rank test showed a statistically significant difference between mean scores for the average student (p = 0.0430). [→ Faherty Aileen 2020](#faherty-aileen-2020)

## Evidence

### Faherty Aileen 2020

Faherty Aileen, Counihan Tim, Kropmans Thomas, Finn Yvonne. (2020). Inter-rater reliability in clinical assessments: do examiner pairings influence candidate ratings? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02009-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of candidate scores from 12 individual examiners versus 6 examiner pairs: the average performance received 4 passes, 6 borderline results and 2 fails when marked individually, but no fails when marked in pairs; the good and weak performances were decided identically by all.

> "Wilcoxon signed rank test showed a statistically significant difference between mean scores for the average student (p = 0.0430)."
