## Evidence

### Peikert S. A. 2026

Peikert S. A., Wiegel S. V., Blank J., Woelber J. P., Ratka‐Krüger P. (2026). Assessment of Knowledge Levels in a Blended‐Learning CPD Programme in Periodontology: A Cross‐Sectional Comparative Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70099

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Scheffé post-hoc comparisons of the twelve subject complexes of the patient case, contrasting Curriculum graduates (group 2) with Master graduates (group 1). Eight topic areas showed significant differences, with contrasts ranging from −0.72 to −1.89 points.

> "Table 5 shows that group 2 differed significantly from group 1 in the following areas: anamnesis (2 vs. 1, p = 0.001), periodontitis and general diseases (2 vs. 1, p < 0.001), oral microbiology (2 vs. 1, p < 0.001), oral structural biology (2 vs. 1, p < 0.001), non‐surgical periodontal therapy (2 vs. 1, p = 0.034), drug therapy (2 vs. 1, p = 0.001), oral mucosal disease (2 vs. 1, p = 0.002) and implant therapy (2 vs. 1, p < 0.001)."
