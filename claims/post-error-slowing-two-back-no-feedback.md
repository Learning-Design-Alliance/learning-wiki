### Satterthwaite Theodore D. M.D. 2012

Satterthwaite Theodore D. M.D., M.A.; Ruparel Kosha M.S.; Loughead James Ph.D; Elliott Mark A. Ph.D.; Gerraty Raphael T. B.A.; Calkins Monica E. Ph.D.; Hakonarson Hakon M.D. Ph.D.; Gur Ruben C. Ph.D.; Gur Raquel E. M.D., Ph.D.; Wolf Daniel H. M.D., Ph.D. (2012). Being Right Is Its Own Reward: Load and Performance Related Ventral Striatum Activation to Correct Responses During a Working Memory Task in Youth. Neuroimage. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3358426/

`q3 · i?`

Paired t-test comparing median response times on correct target trials following errors versus following correct responses, within-subject, 2-back only. Found "significantly greater response times to correct targets following errors trials" [t(172)=9.08; p<0.001].

> "Finally, in the two-back condition there was strong evidence for post-error slowing, with significantly greater response times to correct targets following errors trials compared to correct trials [t(172)=9.08; p<0.001]."
