## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Less frequent pinyin users showed stronger activations in the left middle frontal gyrus and left inferior frontal gyrus during reading comprehension, and more frequent typists showed no stronger activation anywhere. [→ Zhou 2020](#zhou-2020)

## Evidence

### Zhou 2020

Zhou, W., Kwok, V. P. Y., Su, M., Luo, J., & Tan, L. H. (2020). Children's neurodevelopment of reading is affected by China's language input system in the information era. npj Science of Learning, 5:3. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0062-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Whole-brain fMRI group contrast (permutation test with TFCE correction, p < 0.05, cluster size > 20) in the reading comprehension task comparing 21 children per group. "More frequent pinyin typists did not show stronger activation in any brain region relative to the less frequent pinyin typists."

> "Less frequent pinyin users showed stronger activations in the left middle frontal gyrus (BA 9/46; x = -48, y = 27, z = 30; cluster size = 69) and left inferior frontal gyrus (BA 44/45; x = -42, y = 15, z = 12)"
