# Medical students who perceive a numerus clausus policy in their department report lower mastery goals than those who do not, mediated by self-efficacy (Study 2)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sommet Nicolas 2013

Sommet Nicolas, Pulfrey Caroline, Butera Fabrizio. (2013). Did My M.D. Really Go to University to Learn? Detrimental Effects of Numerus Clausus on Self-Efficacy, Mastery Goals and Learning. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0084178

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r1`

Field study of 139 first-year Medical School students in one department with no official numerus clausus but divergent perceptions; heteroscedasticity-robust regression gave partial eta-squared η2 = .02, and "Students who perceived a numerus clausus policy in their department reported lower levels of adherence to mastery goals".

> "Results showed a significant effect of perception of numerus clausus, B = -0.31, F(1, 136) = 3.93, p < .05, η2 p = .02 (c path): Students who perceived a numerus clausus policy in their department reported lower levels of adherence to mastery goals (M = 6.00, SE(HC) = 0.15) than those who did not (M = 6.31, SE(HC) = 0.08)."

### Sommet Nicolas 2013 (2)

Sommet Nicolas, Pulfrey Caroline, Butera Fabrizio. (2013). Did My M.D. Really Go to University to Learn? Detrimental Effects of Numerus Clausus on Self-Efficacy, Mastery Goals and Learning. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0084178

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis in the same medical-student sample: perceived numerus clausus predicted self-efficacy with partial eta-squared η2 = .05, and "students who perceived there to be a numerus clausus policy in their department reported less self-efficacy"; the direct effect on mastery goals became non-significant.

> "the perception of numerus clausus was negatively associated with self-efficacy, B = -0.43, F(1, 136) = 7.44, p < .008, η2 p = .05 (a path). In support of hypothesis 1, students who perceived there to be a numerus clausus policy in their department reported less self-efficacy (M = 4.74, SE = 0.13) than those who did not (M = 5.17, SE = 0.10)."
