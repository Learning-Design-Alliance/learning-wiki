Multi-institutional assessment of Peer Instruction implementation and impacts using the Framework for Interactive Learning in Lectures. FILL+ video observation analysis of 21 recordings found six of seven instructors spent more than 50% of class time on non-interactive activities, yet Instructors F and G, "who exhibit the largest conceptual learning gains, spend the least and most class time, respectively, on non-interactive activities."

> "There also does not appear to be a clear relationship between the amount of
class time spent on non-interactive activities and student conceptual learning."
