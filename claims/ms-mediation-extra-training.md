Han Chengjiang, Li Feng, Lian Bizhen, Vencúrik Tomas, Liang Wei. (2022). Relationships between Perfectionism, Extra Training and Academic Performance in Chinese Collegiate Athletes: Mediating Role of Achievement Motivation. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191710764

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrapped indirect effects (5000 resamples, bias-corrected) from the extra-training mediation model in the two-wave study of 243 collegiate basketball players; both quoted indirect paths were significant while paths via motive for avoiding failure were not.

> "motive for success significantly mediated the association between personal standard and extra training (β = 0.10, p = 0.002), and between doubts about action and extra training (β = 0.06, p = 0.027)"
