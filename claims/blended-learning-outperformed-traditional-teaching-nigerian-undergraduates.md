## Evidence

### Gambari 2017

Gambari, A. I., Shittu, A. T., Ogunlade, O. O., & Osunlade, O. R. (2017). Effectiveness Of Blended Learning And E-Learning Modes Of Instruction On The Performance Of Undergraduates In Kwara State, Nigeria. Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Sciences, 5(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1125071

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest control group study of 85 undergraduates in three intact classes in Kwara State, Nigeria; ANCOVA with pretest as covariate showed "a significant main effect of learning strategy on students’ performance" at the 0.05 level; no effect size printed.

> "As illustrated in Table 3, there was a significant main effect of learning strategy on students’ performance, F (1, 81) = 10.641, p < 0.05."
