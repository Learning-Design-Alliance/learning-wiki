## Evidence

### Hyewon Jang 2026

Hyewon Jang. (2026). Measurement of the effect of member performances on team outcomes in two-stage exams. Physical Review Physics Education Research (submitted). Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.05140)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

10-fold and leave-one-out cross-validation of the four regression models on the 44-team dataset: Model 1 had the smallest CV residual sums of squares (0.015 and 0.014) and Model 3 the largest (0.024), indicating "the best score is the best predictor with which to predict team outcomes".

> "A comparison of CV estimates of prediction errors of each model reveals that Model 1 has the smallest estimate, which means the prediction error for new data would be the smallest. The result suggests that the best of member performances can better predict team outcomes than the mean and the standard deviation of member performances in a team."
