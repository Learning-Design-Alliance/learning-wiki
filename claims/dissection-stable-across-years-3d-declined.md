## Subclaims
`q3 i?` For dissection students, ANOVA found no significant year-to-year differences in any evaluation type; for 3D students, Bloom 2, MCQ, and tag scores differed across years, with 2019 students outperforming later cohorts. [→ Sudha R 2024](#sudha-r-2024)

## Evidence

### Sudha R 2024

Sudha R, Kumar GG, Ratnajeet C, Tushar K, Kishlay KM, Das AK, Shekhar S. (2024). Comparative Analysis of Multidimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy: A Randomized Control Trial. Annals of African Medicine 23(3):459-465. https://doi.org/10.4103/aam.aam_214_23

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Year-wise ANOVA across the 2019, 2021, and 2022 cohorts. For 3D students the article reports significant year differences in Bloom 2 (P = 0.0002), MCQ (P = 0.0045), and tag questions (P = 0.0001), with 2019 students outperforming 2021 and 2022; plastic models showed differences only in Bloom 1 (P = 0.0139) and tag questions (P = 0.0088).

> "For students taught via dissection, ANOVA test failed to reveal significant statistical differences in marks obtained over the years in any type of evaluation method or in total marks obtained"
