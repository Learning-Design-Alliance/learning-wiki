Slotnik 2014¶

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2014). Real progress in Maryland: Student learning objectives and teacher and principal evaluation. WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center. http://marylandpublicschools.org/MSDE/programs/tpe/index.html

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Year-to-year comparison of the 2013 and 2014 Maryland TPE surveys (Table 2). The report states "the stresses of implementation during the first year lessen in the second year, and people begin to view the reform more positively," and that "some of the strongest increases relate to SLOs"; printed changes include +27% for principals and +19% for teachers on guidance for selecting pre/post assessments, marked significant at the 95% level. No effect size is printed.
