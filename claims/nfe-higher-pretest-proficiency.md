## Evidence

### Adrian Leis and Kenneth Brown 2018

Adrian Leis and Kenneth Brown. (2018). Flipped learning in an EFL environment: Does the teacher's experience affect learning outcomes? The EUROCALL Review, Volume 26, No. 1. https://www.eurocall-languages.org/the-eurocall-review

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t test comparing the two intact groups at Week 1; the NFE group's mean of 9.89 exceeded the FE group's 7.31, "t(36) = 2.10, p = .043". This pre-test difference motivated the ANCOVA.

> "The results were that the NFE group had higher proficiency (i.e., 9.89) at the pre -test stage, t(36) = 2.10, p = .043, than the FE group (i.e., 7.31)."
