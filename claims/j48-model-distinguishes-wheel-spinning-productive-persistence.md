# A 15-feature J48 decision tree model distinguishes wheel-spinning from productive persistence in ASSISTments Skill Builders at AUC ROC 0.684 under student-skill-level cross-validation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A multi-feature J48 model predicts whether persistent students will wheel-spin or persist productively, achieving cross-validated AUC ROC of 0.684 (student-skill-level) and 0.636 (student-level). [→ Kai 2018](#kai-2018)

## Evidence

### Kai 2018

Kai, S., Heffernan, C., Almeda, M. V., Heffernan, N., & Baker, R. S. (2018). Decision Tree Modeling of Wheel-Spinning and Productive Persistence in Skill Builders. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 10, No 1. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/210

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Log-data modeling of 8,948 persistent student-problem set pairs from ASSISTments Skill Builders (2014-2015 school year), validated with 10-fold cross-validation. The "AUC ROC value of 0.684" was achieved by a 15-feature J48 tree; no single feature performed substantially above chance.

> "The J48 model achieved an AUC ROC value of 0.684 under student-skill-level cross-validation. Under student-level cross-validation, AUC ROC decreased to 0.636."
