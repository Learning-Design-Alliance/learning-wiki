## Evidence

### Brame Cynthia J 2016

Brame Cynthia J. (2016). Effective Educational Videos: Principles and Guidelines for Maximizing Student Learning from Video Content. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0125

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The essay reports, citing Zhang et al. (2006), Guo et al. (2014), and Ibrahim et al. (2012), that "Both types of segmenting have been shown to be important" for engagement and learning. Segmenting lets learners engage with small pieces of information and control the flow of new information.

> "Both types of segmenting have been shown to be important for student engagement with videos (Zhang et al., 2006; Guo et al., 2014) and learning from video (Zhang et al., 2006; Ibrahim et al., 2012)."
