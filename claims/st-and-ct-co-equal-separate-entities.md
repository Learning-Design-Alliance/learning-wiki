An interpretive position in the review's integration section, attributed to Shute et al. (2017) and adopted by the authors. The article reports no data testing this distinction; it is a definitional argument grounding the framework's separate left- and right-side aspects.

> "Shute and colleagues (2017) distinguish CT from ST in that CT aims to design efficient solutions to problems through computation while ST focuses on constructing and analyzing various relationships among elements in a system for explaining and generalizing them to other similar systems."
