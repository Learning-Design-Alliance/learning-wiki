## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Reiser S 2025

Reiser S, Schacht L, Thomm E, Janssen L, Pittroff SID, Dörfler E, Schick K, Berberat PO, Gartmeier M, Bauer J. (2025). Online assessment of medical students' communication competence in patient encounters: Validation of the VA-MeCo situational judgement test. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0332957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in the N = 395 validation survey using the IRI empathy measure and PPOS-D12 patient orientation scale. The empathy sub-scales "had higher correlations for the VA-MeCo's relationship sub-scale", and patient orientation correlated significantly positively with all sub-scales, though content-dimension correlations fell below the expected .30.

> "as expected, both sub-scales contained in the empathy measure had higher correlations for the VA-MeCo's relationship sub-scale than with its content and structure sub-scales. In addition, corroborating our hypothesis, there were significant positive correlations of all VA-MeCo sub-scales with the sharing and caring scales contained in the patient orientation instrument."
