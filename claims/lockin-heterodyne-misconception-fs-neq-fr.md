Findings from think-aloud and pre-tutorial interviews with graduate students, reported in the student difficulties section as two beliefs about the heterodyne case where the signal frequency differs from the reference.

> "Students commonly believe a lock-in will completely filter out every signal not equal to the reference frequency. Students also commonly believe that the lock-in will produce a DC output even under the condition 𝑓𝑆 ≠ 𝑓𝑅."
