## Subclaims
`q2 i?` There was no interaction effect between learning mode (GraspLearn vs CLearn) and cognitive style on WMC; descriptive differences were small. [→ Chau et al. 2018](#chau-et-al-2018)

## Evidence

### Chau et al. 2018

Kien Tsong Chau, Zarina Samsudin, Wan Ahmad Jaafar Wan Yahaya. (2018). Graspable Multimedia: A Study of the Effect of a Multimedia System Embodied with Physical Artefacts on Working Memory Capacity of Preschoolers. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 17(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1165766

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA interaction analysis of 248 preschoolers (127 field-dependent, 121 field-independent) across GraspLearn and CLearn cells. The article reports the descriptive differences of 0.46 and 0.26 and plots the effects in Figure 11; no significant interaction was found.

> "Table 9 reveals that the WMC scores of field-dependent and field-independent subjects in both GraspLearn and CLearn differ by 0.46 (59.85-59.39), with field-independent subjects doing slightly better and differ by only 0.26 (59.75-59.49) for learning mode, with GraspLearn did better."
