Reliability analysis of all pretest and posttest Ottawa CRM GRS scores assigned by two trained EM faculty raters present for all simulation scenarios. A kappa statistic with linear weighting was used; no effect size beyond the kappa is printed.

> "The interrater reliability for the raters was found to be kappa = 0.74, 95% CI (0.66, 0.82). This kappa value represents good agreement between the two raters in this study."
