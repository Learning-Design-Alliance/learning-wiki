# DiLeo (2007) finds a substantively important but statistically non-significant positive effect of Odyssey® Math on grade 5 PSSA math scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a cluster randomized trial of fifth-grade classrooms, the Odyssey® Math group outscored the Odyssey® Language Arts comparison group on the PSSA math scale, but WWC-computed calculations found the difference not statistically significant. [→ dileo-1](#dileo-1)

## Evidence

### dileo-1

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2017, January). Primary Mathematics intervention report: Odyssey® Math. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/669

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomized controlled trial in 11 fifth-grade classrooms (207 students) in Pennsylvania; Odyssey® Math supplemented Houghton Miffllin Mathematics versus Odyssey® Language Arts comparison. WWC computed a p-value of .15 for the PSSA and characterized the finding as a substantively important positive effect.

> "The author did not report the statistical significance of this finding, but the WWC-computed calculations (adjusted for classroom-level clustering) indicated that the results were not statistically significant."
