## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Goldonowicz Jessica M. 2018

Goldonowicz Jessica M., Runyon Michael S., Bullard Mark J. (2018). Palliative care in the emergency department: an educational investigation and intervention. BMC Palliative Care. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-018-0293-5

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired anonymous pre/post surveys of 40 EM residents (95% of 42) at a single academic ED, analyzed with the Wilcoxon signed ranks test and a Bonferroni-adjusted threshold of 0.007. Post-intervention items improved, including "I feel confident with initiating palliative care discussions and treatment in the emergency department" (p < 0.0001) and physician-role importance (p = 0.0003).

> "Residents’ confidence in implementing palliative care skills and the perceived importance of palliative care was significantly improved after this educational intervention"
