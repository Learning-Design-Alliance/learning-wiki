### Christopher J. MacLellan & Kenneth R. Koedinger 2022

Christopher J. MacLellan & Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2022). Domain-General Tutor Authoring with Apprentice Learner Models. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00214-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A case study comparing authoring efficiency for an experimental design tutor. The Example-Tracing model required 168 demonstrations and 24 unordered group specifications across 3 behavior graph templates, estimated at 26.96 minutes using a keystroke-level model. The Decision Tree model was trained on 20 problems interactively.

> "we estimate that the behavior graphs for the experimental design tutor would take 26.96 minutes to build using Example-Tracing"
