DIF analysis comparing male–female and Hispanic–White examinees across terms (Table 5.3). The examinee population was largely Hispanic (about 80% per term), which the report says "limits the number of group comparison that are possible."

"Results indicate the vast majority (91–97%) of items are classified as “A” items (i.e., combined A+ and A- items), which indicates no DIF or negligible amounts of DIF."
