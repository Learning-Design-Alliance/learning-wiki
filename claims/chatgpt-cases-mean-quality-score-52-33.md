Teacher evaluation study in which eight instructors independently scored twelve ChatGPT-generated cases on a 12-item, 60-point rubric. The results section reports the "overall mean score of 52.33 ± 5.44", normally distributed (P = 0.135); the abstract adds range 48–54.25 and max = 60.
