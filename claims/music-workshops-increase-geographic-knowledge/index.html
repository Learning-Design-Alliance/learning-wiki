Evidence¶

Richardson 2013¶

Richardson, Ronald; Brouillette, Liane. (2013). Historic and Ethnic Music Found to Increase Geographic Understanding: A Quasi-Experimental Study. Journal for Learning through the Arts, 9(1). http://escholarship.org/uc/item/71g4x3cr

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study of 215 eighth-graders (84 control, 131 treatment) at one suburban California middle school, with ANCOVA controlling for pre-score, ethnicity, GATE and lunch status. The article reports the treatment advantage of 6.27 points, "statistically significant at P=<0.0001", and an effect size of 0.854 for the NCGE.

"when controlling for all other categories included in the column labeled “Term,” on the 52-question NCGE the mean score for students in the treatment group was 6.27 points higher than the score for students in the control group. This is statistically significant at P=<0.0001"
