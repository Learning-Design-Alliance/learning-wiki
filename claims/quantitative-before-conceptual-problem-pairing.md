## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2023

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Helping students become proficient problem solvers Part I: A brief review. [doi:10.3390/educsci13020156](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci13020156)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a large-class study in which one group solved only a conceptual problem with a strong alternate conception and another first solved a quantitative problem whose calculation was useful for it; "roughly half the students solved the qualitative problem correctly compared to only 16%". No effect size printed.

> "students who solved both were significantly less likely to solve the qualitative problem by using their “gut feeling” (roughly half the students solved the qualitative problem correctly compared to only 16% in the group that only solved one problem)."
