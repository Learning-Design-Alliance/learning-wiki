### Raković 2023

Raković, M., Ahmad Uzir, N., Matcha, W., Eagan, B., Jovanović, J., Shaffer, D. W., Pardo, A., & Gašević, D. (2023). Network Analytics to Unveil Links of Learning Strategies, Time Management, and Academic Performance in a Flipped Classroom. Journal of Learning Analytics, 10(3), 64–86. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2023.7843

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Overall mean epistemic network (Figure 2) of trace data from 290 first-year engineering students in a 13-week flipped computer systems course, aggregating individual adjacency matrices across all students. The authors report that "the students used the M preparing, M revisiting, and S summative assess modes of study more often" based on node sizes in the network.

> "Overall, we observed that the students used the M preparing, M revisiting, and S summative assess modes of study more often, as indicated by the size of the corresponding nodes."
