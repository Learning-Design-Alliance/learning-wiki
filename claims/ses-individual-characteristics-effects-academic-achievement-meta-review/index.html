Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Across 169 meta-analyses, 254 variables with 427 effect sizes influence academic achievement; most variables have small or very small effects and none has a large, very large or huge negative effect — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Teacher variables relate to academic achievement, with teachers' judgments of students' academic achievement (ES = 1.622) and intelligence (ES = 1.540) showing very large effects and teacher-child conflicts a small negative effect (ES = -.408) — related
  • Several learning theories and teaching strategies have very large positive effects on academic achievement (e.g., creative drama ES = 1.453), while the surface approach to learning has a small negative effect (ES = -.366) — related
  • Special education and violence variables relate to academic achievement, with absence of reading disability (ES = 1.266) showing very large effects and peer victimization (ES = -.292) a medium negative effect — related
  • A few decision-tree splits identify very high-risk students, such as low-SES students who doubt they will reach college — related
