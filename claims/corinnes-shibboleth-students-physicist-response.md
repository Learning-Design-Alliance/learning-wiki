## Evidence

### Jeffrey M. Rabin 2024

Jeffrey M. Rabin, Andrew Meyertholen, Brian Shotwell. (2024). Students' Experience of Cultural Differences Between Mathematics and Physics. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2409.02427](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2409.02427)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Survey question 3 with 20 respondents: 17 gave only the physicist's response r², two said f(r,θ) = r, and one student (D4) provided both responses. The authors say this confirms a strong version of Redish and Kuo's observation that physicists load meaning onto variables.

> "Surprisingly, all students gave the physicist’s response (or a variant of it 4), and only one recognized that a mathematician might possibly give a diﬀerent answer because of an assumption made in the physicist’s response"
