## Evidence

### Snoder 2017

Snoder, P. (2017). Improving English Learners’ Productive Collocation Knowledge: The Effects of Involvement Load, Spacing, and Intentionality. TESL Canada Journal, 34(3). https://doi.org/10.18806/tesl.v34i3.1277

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-test (N = 45) comparing within-participant ratios: 3INC M = 71.47%, 2INC+INT M = 90.42%. Quote fragment: "more effective in promoting durable learning".

> "The result of this test was statistically significant: t (44): 3.100, p. = .003. This indicates that the introduction of intentional learning for the third exposure to target collocations that had been learned initially was more effective in promoting durable learning than a third incidental exposure"
