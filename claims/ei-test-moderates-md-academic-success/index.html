Alabbasi Ahmed M. Abdulla 2023¶

Alabbasi Ahmed M. Abdulla, Alabbasi Fatema A., AlSaleh Aseel, Alansari Ahmed M., Sequeira Reginald P. (2023). Emotional intelligence weakly predicts academic success in medical programs: a multilevel meta-analysis and systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04417-8

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analysis within the three-level meta-analysis testing EI test as a categorical moderator. The EQ-i test was "highly correlated with academic success compared with other EI tests"; EQ-i r = .14, MSCEIT r = .10, SSEIT r = .11 (non-significant), other r = .09 per Table 3.
