## Evidence

### Okuyama K 2025

Okuyama K, Lönn SL, Khoshnood AM, Assari S, Sundquist J, Sundquist K. (2025). School performance gap between non-immigrant and second-generation immigrant children in Sweden—time trends and contributing factors. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1521387

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear mixed models including interaction terms of each factor with immigration status, run separately by sex on Swedish register data. Positive interaction coefficients (e.g., low parental education * second-generation immigrant = 32.79 for females) offset the main negative coefficients, indicating smaller factor effects among second-generation children.

> "The interaction terms of parental education, neighborhood socioeconomic status, and parental mental disorders by immigration status suggested weaker associations with these factors among second-generation immigrant than non-immigrant children."
