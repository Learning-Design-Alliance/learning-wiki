## Evidence

### Quinn Antonia 2019

Quinn Antonia, Kaminsky Jennifer, Adler Andrew, Eisner Shirley, Ovitsh Robin. (2019). Cardiac Auscultation Lab Using a Heart Sounds Auscultation Simulation Manikin. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10839

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' stated limitation in the Limitations section: evaluations are based primarily on student and faculty perception and are level one of Kirkpatrick's learning pyramid, despite four multiple-choice questions and an OSCE on the end-of-unit exam.

> "Our session is also limited by a lack of evaluation on our objectives. Even though we have four multiple-choice questions on our end-of-unit summative exam related to our learning objectives and an OSCE, there is not enough data to assess at this point whether the knowledge is retained in actual clinical practice."
