## Evidence

### Garces-Palacios S 2026

Garces-Palacios S, Desir A, Pise A, Holcomb C, Scott DJ, Sankaranarayanan G. (2026). External validity of task-specific metrics for performance assessment in laparoscopic crural repair. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12767-9

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analysis of a random subset of 10 videos independently evaluated by three trained graders at the 2024 SAGES Annual Meeting. The article reports "The ICC was 0.85 (95% CI 0.81–0.89; p < 0.001) for TSMs" with values above 0.90 interpreted as excellent.

> "The ICC was 0.85 (95% CI 0.81–0.89; p < 0.001) for TSMs and 0.88 (95% CI 0.80–0.93; p < 0.001) for OSATS."
