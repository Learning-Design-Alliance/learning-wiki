## Evidence

### Segura-Fornieles 2026

Segura-Fornieles, A. J., Márquez-Hernández, V. V., García-Viola, A., Rodríguez-García, M. C., & Garrido-Molina, J. M. (2026). Improving operational preparedness for armored vehicle casualty rescue: effectiveness of simulation-based training with a novel extrication device. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1910210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest study (N = 156); skill self-rated on a 0–10 scale. The article reports statistically significant differences with no printed effect size.

> "The mean skill score in the pre-test was 5.74 (SD = 2.19), while in the post-test it was 8.67 (SD = 1.18), with statistically significant differences found (p < 0.001)."

### Segura-Fornieles 2026 (2)

Segura-Fornieles, A. J., Márquez-Hernández, V. V., García-Viola, A., Rodríguez-García, M. C., & Garrido-Molina, J. M. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same study (N = 156); confidence self-rated on a 0–10 scale. Statistically significant improvement reported with no printed effect size.

> "Regarding confidence, the mean score in the pre-intervention was 7.08 (SD = 2.16), and in the post-intervention it was 8.72 (SD = 0.93), with statistically significant differences (p < 0.001)."
