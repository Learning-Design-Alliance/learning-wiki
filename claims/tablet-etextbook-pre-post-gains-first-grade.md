`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental control group study in one private school in Irbid-Jordan; the experimental group was taught with tablets loaded with e-textbooks, activities, games and worksheets for one semester. Table 2 reports pre-test mean 67.52 (SD 11.874) and post-test mean 94.50 (SD 1.954), t = -14.410, df 41, Sig. .000. The article reports "a significant improvement on students core curriculum acheivement s" with no effect size printed.
