## Evidence

### Heni Martin 2012

Heni Martin, Lammerding-Köppel Maria, Celebi Nora, Shiozawa Thomas, Riessen Reimer, Nikendei Christoph, Weyrich Peter. (2012). Focused didactic training for skills lab student tutors – which techniques are considered helpful? GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 29(3), Doc41. [doi:10.3205/zma000811](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000811)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Retrospective survey of 23 tutors rated transferability of each module on a 6-point Likert scale; the presentation module received the best score, "1.68±0.75". No effect size or comparative test was reported.

> "The module “How to present effectively” was also seen to be most easily transferable into the skills lab environment (1.68±0.75)."
