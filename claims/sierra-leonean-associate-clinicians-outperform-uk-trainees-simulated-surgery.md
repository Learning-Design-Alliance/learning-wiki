## Evidence

### Liu B. 2019

Liu B., Hunt L. M., Lonsdale R. J., Narula H. S., Mansaray A. F., Bundu I. and Bolkan H. A. (2019). Comparison of surgical skill acquisition by UK surgical trainees and Sierra Leonean associate clinicians in a task‐sharing programme. BJS Open 3(2): 218–223. https://doi.org/10.1002/bjs5.50122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Blinded experimental study: 20 Sierra Leonean trainees (19 associate clinicians, 1 doctor) and 20 UK surgical trainees received identical 1-h training on a simulated venous cut-down; disguised videos were scored by two consultant surgeons using validated OSATS. The study reports "three of the 12 performance parameters and four of the five trainee behaviour characteristics" favouring Sierra Leonean trainees.

> "The Sierra Leonean trainees outperformed the UK trainees on three of the 12 performance parameters and four of the five trainee behaviour characteristics. UK trainees did not outperform the Sierra Leonean trainees on any parameter or characteristic (Table  2)."
