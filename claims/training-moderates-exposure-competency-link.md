## Evidence

### Osondu Oluchi Miracle 2026

Osondu Oluchi Miracle, Ugwu Lawrence Ejike, Ayogu Chijioke Kenneth, Morah Nwando Maryann, Eyisi Doris Chinelo, Ike Obinna Osita, Johnson Jennifer E., Chukwuorji JohnBosco Chika. (2026). Lifetime Exposure to Suicide and Current Use of Suicide Prevention Competencies Among Catholic Clergy in Nigeria: A Multidimensional Structural Analysis. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-026-02754-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multigroup SEM comparing untrained (n = 250) and trained (n = 86) priests with five covariates. In the untrained group the exposure path was "β = 0.32, p = 0.001"; in the trained group exposure was not significant (β = 0.08, p = 0.488), where annual suicidal ideation encounters predicted competencies instead (β = 0.34, p = 0.036).

> "Among priests who have not received a formal suicide prevention training, lifetime exposure to suicide was a significant predictor of the use suicide prevention competencies (β = 0.32, p = 0.001), whereas none of the covariates were statistically significant predictors"

### Osondu Oluchi Miracle 2026 (2)

Osondu Oluchi Miracle, Ugwu Lawrence Ejike, Ayogu Chijioke Kenneth, Morah Nwando Maryann, Eyisi Doris Chinelo, Ike Obinna Osita, Johnson Jennifer E., Chukwuorji JohnBosco Chika. (2026). Lifetime Exposure to Suicide and Current Use of Suicide Prevention Competencies Among Catholic Clergy in Nigeria: A Multidimensional Structural Analysis. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-026-02754-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multigroup SEM moderation test on the same 336-priest sample; the equality-constrained model fit significantly worse, "Δχ2(6) = 13.74, p = 0.033", so the structural path differs between training groups. No effect size is printed for this test.

> "A likelihood ratio test comparing a freely estimated model against a model constraining structural paths to equality across groups indicated significantly worse fit under equality constraints, Δχ2(6) = 13.74, p = 0.033, confirming training status as a moderator"
