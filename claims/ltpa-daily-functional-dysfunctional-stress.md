## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Daily LTPA is positively associated with functional stress (b = 0.003, p < 0.001) and negatively with dysfunctional stress (b = -0.002, p < 0.01) within persons. [→ Teuber Monika 2024](#teuber-monika-2024)

## Evidence

### Teuber Monika 2024

Teuber Monika, Leyhr Daniel, Sudeck Gorden. (2024). Physical activity improves stress load, recovery, and academic performance-related parameters among university students: a longitudinal study on daily level. BMC Public Health 24:598. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-18082-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ten-day daily-diary study (N = 57) with two-level regression models; day-level variables centered within person. The Dysfunctional Stress Model showed LTPA significantly negatively influenced dysfunctional stress (b = 0.002, p < 0.01), while "The same applied to LTPA (b = 0.003, p < 0.001)" for functional stress.

> "The time spent on ESD as well as the time spent on PA in leisure showed a positive significant influence on functional stress (b = 0.032, p < 0.01). The same applied to LTPA (b = 0.003, p < 0.001)."
