### Singh 2023

Singh, C., Maries, A., Heller, K., & Heller, P. (2023). Instructional Strategies that Foster Effective Problem-Solving. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.1063/9780735425477_017](https://doi.org/10.1063/9780735425477_017)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports a controlled introductory physics study (Singh, 2008c) comparing students posed only conceptual problems with students posed "quantitative/conceptual problem pairs"; the pairs group answered conceptual problems at a much larger rate. No effect size is printed.

> "For example, in a controlled study in introductory physics, only conceptual problems were posed to some students and quantitative/conceptual problem pairs were posed to others. The students with both problems gave the correct response to both approximately equally and at a much larger rate than the students who were posed only the qualitative problem to theirs"
