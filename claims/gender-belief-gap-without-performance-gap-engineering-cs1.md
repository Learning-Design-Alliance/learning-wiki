## Evidence

### Griffin Pitts 2026

Griffin Pitts, Ashish Aggarwal. (2026). Engineering Students' Self-Efficacy, Perceptions, and Performance in a Flipped CS1 Course. Preprint, to appear in Proceedings of the 2026 IEEE Frontiers in Education Conference (FIE). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann–Whitney/nonparametric group comparison of belief factor scores by gender among 602 students found "higher levels of self-efficacy (M = 4.193, SD = 0.612) than female students (M = 3.877, SD = 0.646)"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Male students reported significantly higher levels of self-efficacy (M = 4.193, SD = 0.612) than female students (M = 3.877, SD = 0.646), with this difference being statistically significant (F(1,594) = 35.057, p<.001)"

### Griffin Pitts 2026 (2)

Griffin Pitts, Ashish Aggarwal. (2026). Engineering Students' Self-Efficacy, Perceptions, and Performance in a Flipped CS1 Course. Preprint, to appear in Proceedings of the 2026 IEEE Frontiers in Education Conference (FIE). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.01471)
