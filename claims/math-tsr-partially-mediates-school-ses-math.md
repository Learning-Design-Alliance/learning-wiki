# Students’ perception of the math teacher-student relationship partially mediates the link between school SES and math achievement

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` School SES significantly predicted perceived math teacher-student relationship (β = 0.11, p < 0.01), which in turn predicted math achievement (β = 0.15, p < 0.001), while the school SES–math achievement path remained significant (β = 0.30, p < 0.001), indicating partial mediation. [→ Xuan Xin 2019](#xuan-xin-2019)

## Evidence

### Xuan Xin 2019

Xuan Xin, Xue Ye, Zhang Cai, Luo Yuhan, Jiang Wen, Qi Mengdi, Wang Yun. (2019). Relationship among school socioeconomic status, teacher-student relationship, and middle school students’ academic achievement in China: Using the multilevel mediation model. PLoS ONE 14(3): e0213783. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0213783

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Step 3 of the multilevel mediation model for math in the NCSC sample. The article also reports a significant effect of school SES on math teacher-student relationship (β = 0.11, p < 0.01) and of the relationship on math achievement (β = 0.15, p < 0.001), "indicating a partial mediation of math teacher-student relationship".

> "The relationship between school SES and students’ math achievement was still significant (β = 0.30, p < 0.001), when math teacher-student relationship was considered, indicating a partial mediation of math teacher-student relationship."
