Portolese Dias 2013¶

Portolese Dias, L., & Trumpy, R. (2013). Online Instructor's Use of Audio Feedback to Increase Social Presence and Student Satisfaction. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1033260

q2 · i? · causal · r1

SEOI question five compared the fall 2012 written-group-feedback control (mean 4.714285714, 35 observations) with the spring 2013 audio-group-feedback treatment (mean 4.857142857, 35 observations) using a one-tailed independent-samples t-test. The article reports "no statistical difference between the two feedback groups." No effect size is printed.

"Results for question two, “instructor is actively engaged in class” has a p-value of .17, therefore indicates no statistical difference between the two feedback groups."
