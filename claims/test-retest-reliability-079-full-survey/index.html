Evidence¶

Kao 2023¶

Kao, Y., Murphy, D., Hubbard Cheuoua, A., Kannan, P., Tsan, J., Jennings, K. E., Smith, H., Emanuel, S., & Miller, E. R. (2023). Survey development for assessing student computing career intentions: Technical report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638631

q2 · i? · design · r3

The test-retest study re-administered the survey to a stratified subsample 1 to 2 months after initial response; 112 responses remained after data cleaning. The article reports a coefficient of 0.79, "considered acceptable bordering on good"; no effect size is printed.

"The correlation between the participants’ scores from both survey administrations is used as the estimate of test–retest reliability. A correlation coefficient greater than 0.9 represents excellent reliability, between 0.9 and 0.8 represents good reliability, and between 0.8 and 0.7 represents acceptable reliability. The full survey test–retest reliability coefficient is 0.79, which is considered acceptable bordering on good."
