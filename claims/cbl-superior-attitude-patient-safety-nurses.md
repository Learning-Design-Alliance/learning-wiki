## Evidence

### Mirzaei Samaneh 2025

Mirzaei Samaneh, Keshmiri Fatemeh, Norouzinia Roohangiz, Mirjalili Najmeh, Baghshahi Naeimeh, Gholinataj Jelodar Mohsen. (2025). The effect of interactive learning methods and reflection on nurses' learning in patient safety at the ICU and Emergency department. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-025-12320-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc comparisons after a significant ANOVA (F = 12.13, p < 0.0001) on post-test attitude in the three-group quasi-experiment: "CBL was significantly more effective than the other two methods in attitude (p < 0.0001)"; CBL post-test mean 54.68 ± 4.64.

> "According to the post-hoc test, CBL was significantly more effective than the other two methods in attitude (p < 0.0001). However, there was no significant difference between lecture and reflection in this field (p = 0.309)."
