Mathias Mejeh, Yvonne M. Fromm (2026). Fostering self-regulated learning through adaptive learning technology: A differentiated perspective on the role of feedback. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102394

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Interaction tests in the HLM models for the post-actional to pre-actional transition (H4) among 194 students. All feedback moderation effects were non-significant or only marginally significant (p < .10), including a positive satisfaction × informative interaction on planning and a negative satisfaction × directive interaction on hope.

> "Not supporting our fourth hypothesis (H4), no statistically significant interaction effects were observed between satisfaction and adaptive feedback on the subsequent pre-actional phase. However, several marginally significant trends (p < .10) were identified."
