## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Low Matthew Jian Wen 2024

Low Matthew Jian Wen, Chan Gene Wai Han, Li Zisheng, Koh Yiwen, Jen Chi Loong, Lee Zi Yao, Cheng Lenard Tai Win. (2024). Comparison of virtual and in-person simulations for sepsis and trauma resuscitation training in Singapore: a randomized controlled trial. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2024.21.33

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial: trauma resuscitation checklist scores at end-of-rotation assessment on a different scenario (hemorrhagic shock from pelvic fracture and splenic rupture), marked by two blinded raters. Scores "were not significantly different (-0.9; 95% CI, -4.2 to 2.3), with a Cohen’s d of 0.19".

> "The mean±SD trauma checklist scores for VPS (36.2±6.0) and IPS (37.2±4.2) were not significantly different (-0.9; 95% CI, -4.2 to 2.3), with a Cohen’s d of 0.19."
