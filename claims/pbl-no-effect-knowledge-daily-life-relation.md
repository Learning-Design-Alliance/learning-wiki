### Balım 2016

Balım, A. G., İnel-Ekici, D., & Özcan, E. (2016). Concept Cartoons Supported Problem Based Learning Method in Middle School Science Classrooms. Journal of Education and Learning, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v5n2p272

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

MANCOVA of post-test scores on the knowledge-daily life relating open ended questions in the same 553-student quasi-experiment. The article reports "F(2,546)=.70, p=.496, ηp2=.003" and no significant pairwise differences versus control; no effect size is printed for the null contrasts.
