Evidence¶

McElhaney 2024¶

McElhaney, K. W., & Kamdar, D. (2024). Updated summary and synthesis of OpenSciEd research [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/231

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing McNeill, Fine, et al. (2023), an online survey with follow-up interviews on teachers' customizations of OpenSciEd units; the synthesis notes the survey covered 169 teachers across 41 US states. No effect sizes are printed.

"The large majority of teachers reported that they made small changes often or daily, while larger customizations were more likely to occur sometimes."
