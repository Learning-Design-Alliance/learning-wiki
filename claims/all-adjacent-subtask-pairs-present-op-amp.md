## Evidence

### Laura Ríos 2019

Laura Ríos, Benjamin Pollard, Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2019). Using think-aloud interviews to characterize model-based reasoning in electronics for a laboratory course assessment. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010140](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.010140)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of 30-second coded intervals from 10 TAPS interviews counted subtask pairs occurring directly after one another or within the same interval. The authors observe that "all adjacent pairs are present," which they interpret as the interconnectivity of the modeling process, and report the three most prevalent adjacent pairs.

> "From the data, we observe that all adjacent pairs are present. We interpret this as representative of the interconnectivity of the modeling process."
