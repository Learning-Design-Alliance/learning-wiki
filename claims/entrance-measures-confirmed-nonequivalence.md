# Entrance measures confirmed the nonequivalence of underprepared and college-ready groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Math placement, SAT math, and SAT verbal scores were significantly lower for underprepared students than college-ready students at course entry. [→ Hesser 2016](#hesser-2016)

## Evidence

### Hesser 2016

Hesser, T. L., & Gregory, J. L. (2016). Instructional Support Sessions in Chemistry: Alternative to Remediation. Journal of Developmental Education, 39(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1130190

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-sample t-tests on entrance measures confirmed group nonequivalence: math placement t(268) = -14.66, p < .001 (M = 52.88 vs 87.14); SAT math t(254) = -2.76, p < .05 (M = 538.7 vs 568.0); SAT verbal t(254) = -2.27, p < .05 (M = 509.2 vs 534.0). The authors state these results "supported the placement of these students into a course with added instructional support."

> "Overall, the analysis of entrance exam scores indicated that students labeled as underprepared had entrance assessment averages significantly lower than the averages of college-ready students when entering the General Chemistry course."
