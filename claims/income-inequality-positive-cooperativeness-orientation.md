## Evidence

### Nicolas Sommet 2022

Nicolas Sommet, David L. Weissman, and Andrew J. Elliot. (2022). Income Inequality Predicts Competitiveness and Cooperativeness at School. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000731

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel analysis of PISA 2003 for cooperativeness orientation; inconsistent with preregistered Hypothesis 2B, "the higher the income inequality, the higher the domain-speciﬁc coop erativeness orientation" (b = .12, p < .001, bc = .09, pc = .024).

> "the higher the income inequality, the higher the domain-speciﬁc coop erativeness orientation, b = .12 [.05, .20], p , .001, and bc = .09 [.01, .18], pc = .024"

### Nicolas Sommet 2022 (2)

Nicolas Sommet, David L. Weissman, and Andrew J. Elliot. (2022). Income Inequality Predicts Competitiveness and Cooperativeness at School. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000731

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel analysis of PISA 2000 for domain-general cooperativeness orientation; the surprising finding replicated (b = .12, p = .024, bc = .19, pc = .048), a positive rather than negative association.
