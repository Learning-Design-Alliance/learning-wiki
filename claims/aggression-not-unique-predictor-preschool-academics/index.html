Evidence¶

Arnold 2012¶

Arnold, D. H., Kupersmidt, J. B., Voegler-Lee, M. E., & Marshall, N. (2012). The Association between Preschool Children's Social Functioning and Their Emergent Academic Skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2011.12.009

q3 · i? · associational · r3

HLM regression of preliteracy, language, and mathematics on inattention, aggression, and social skills in 467 preschoolers. The article reports "Aggression was not significantly related to any academic measures when controlling for these other variables."
