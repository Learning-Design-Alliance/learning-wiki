Feature-importance analysis of the construct-specific classifiers (Table 2), extracting the vocabulary of correctness for six Talk Moves. For Restating, correct reasoning contains synonyms for exactness (verbatim, repeats); for Pressing for Reasoning, causal language (because, justify); for social moves, collective nouns (class, peer, classmate), whose absence is "a strong predic- tor of misclassifications".
