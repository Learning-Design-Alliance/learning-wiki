# Cluster analysis identifies three stable learner profiles: Engaged and Literate, Uncritical Reliant, and Skeptical Minimal users

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A three-cluster k-means solution on GenAI use, literacy, and reliance yielded Engaged and Literate (n = 355), Uncritical Reliant (n = 244), and Skeptical Minimal (n = 224) profiles. [→ Amzalag M 2026](#amzalag-m-2026)
`q2 i?` The three-cluster solution replicated across two random subsamples with similar sizes, centroids, and explained variance (R2 = .381 and .378). [→ Amzalag M 2026 (2)](#amzalag-m-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Amzalag M 2026

Amzalag M, Zviel-Girshin R and Beimel D (2026) The illusion of cognitive independence in the age of generative AI: unpacking GenAI literacy and learner profiles. Front. Educ. 11:1861548. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1861548

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

K-means cluster analysis on survey data using silhouette and elbow methods to select the three-cluster solution. The article describes Cluster 1 as showing "High levels of GenAI use and high levels of operational and conceptual literacy"; Clusters 2 and 3 are Uncritical Reliant (n = 244) and Skeptical Minimal (n = 224).

> "Cluster 1 (n = 355) – Engaged and Literate GenAI Users: High levels of GenAI use and high levels of operational and conceptual literacy, alongside moderate levels of critical-ethical awareness and reliance."

### Amzalag M 2026 (2)

Amzalag M, Zviel-Girshin R and Beimel D (2026) The illusion of cognitive independence in the age of generative AI: unpacking GenAI literacy and learner profiles. Front. Educ. 11:1861548. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1861548

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Stability check splitting the sample into two equal subsamples and repeating k-means independently; explained variance was nearly identical across subsamples (R2 = .381 and .378), which the article reads as confirming robust stability.

> "The three-cluster solution was successfully replicated across both subsamples, yielding highly similar cluster sizes and centroid profiles."
