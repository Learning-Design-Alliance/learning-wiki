Multi-group PLS-SEM analysis compared the PI–AI-TIB path across groups defined by self-reported GenAI use frequency in the 898-teacher sample. The article reports the relationship "was stronger among teachers with lower reported frequencies of GenAI tool use." The detailed multi-group results section was not available in the supplied text.
