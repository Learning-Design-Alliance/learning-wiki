## Evidence

### Beaugé Yvonne 2026

Beaugé Yvonne, Kusch Simon, Thorsson Lukas, Zentgraf Michael, Schauwienold Julian, Muenzberg Matthias, Verboket Rene, Lefering Rolf, Schweigkofler Uwe, Faul Philipp, Sterz Jasmina, Ruesseler Miriam. (2026). Checklist- and algorithm-based simulation training produce comparable improvements in teamwork-related shared mental models: a cluster-randomized study in trauma resuscitation teams. European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00068-026-03161-0

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cluster-randomized multicenter pre–post study of 29 trauma teams (186 participants) randomized to checklist-based or algorithm-based 6-hour in situ simulation training. RM-ANOVA on team-level similarity scores showed a significant main effect of time for total similarity, "all p ≤.002"; Table 3 reports F(1,27) = 156.50, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.85 for total similarity.

> "Repeated-measures ANOVA demonstrated significant main effects of time for task responsibility, communication, and total similarity (all p ≤.002) (Table 2)."
