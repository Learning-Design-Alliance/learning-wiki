`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Content analysis of open-ended questionnaire responses from the experimental group; Table 10 prints frequencies such as 19 students (76%) for active participation and student-teacher interaction, and 18 (72%) for flexibility and repetitive watching.

> "According to Table 10, the most reported advantages of the flipped classroom method were "ensuring the active participation in the process", "enhancing the student-teacher interaction", "being a flexible method in terms of time and space", "allowing watching repetitively" and "enhancing the peer interaction"."
