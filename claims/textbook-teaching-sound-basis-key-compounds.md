## Evidence

### Fries 2017

Fries, M.-H. (2017). Teaching compound nouns in ESP: insights from cognitive semantics. In C. Sarré & S. Whyte (Eds), New developments in ESP teaching and learning research (pp. 93-107). Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2017.cssw2017.747

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative comparison of written summaries of two videos (on artemisinin and Danish energy transition) produced in class by both groups. The experimental group was more precise on chemical-reaction CNs, but the control group detailed energy production CNs better.

> "The tentative conclusions which can be drawn from these findings is that the textbook-based teaching given to all the students seems a sound basis to help them understand and reuse key CNs, with no clear added value coming from the cognitive perspective."
