## Evidence

### Goetz 2016

Goetz, T., Sticca, F., Pekrun, R., Murayama, K., & Elliot, A. J. (2016). Intraindividual relations between achievement goals and discrete achievement emotions: An experience sampling approach. Learning and Instruction, 41, 115-125. https://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.10.007

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary variance decomposition of the ESM state data (Table 2; 1409 assessments nested in 120 students). The article reports the printed ICC ranges and concludes situational variation dominates; the ICCs are proportions of variance, not effect sizes.

> "The ICCs for achievement goals and achievement emotions are similar in magnitude (ICCs range from .257 to .332 for goals, and from .204 to .325 for emotions). Thus, most of the variance in both constructs originated from situational variation within persons."
