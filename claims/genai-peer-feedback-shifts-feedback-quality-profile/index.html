Evidence¶

Chang 2026¶

Chang, Y., Liu, Q., Lu, Y. & Miao, E. (2026). Leveraging generative AI to facilitate peer feedback in collaborative argumentation learning. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00586-w

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Coding of 48 intergroup feedback reports (756 meaning units) across the three conditions for feedback elements (description, identification, explanation, suggestion). In the PF group, identification had the highest percentage, followed by description.

"Suggestion had the highest percentage in the GenAI-PF group and the PS-GenAI PF group (48.4% and 39.5% respectively), followed by identification (27.1% and 31.2% respectively)."

Chang 2026 (2)¶

Chang, Y., Liu, Q., Lu, Y. & Miao, E. (2026). Leveraging generative AI to facilitate peer feedback in collaborative argumentation learning. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00586-w

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Affective coding of the same 48 feedback reports across the three conditions. Neutral feedback accounted for the highest percentage in all three groups; the reported affective proportions are descriptive percentages.

"positive were significantly higher in the PF group (20.6%) than in the other two groups (7.2% and 14.8% respectively), negative were higher in the PF group (25.8%) and the PS-GenAI PF group (24.4%) than in the GenAI-PF group (14.8%)."
