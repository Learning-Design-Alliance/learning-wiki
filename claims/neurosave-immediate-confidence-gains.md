## Evidence

### Górecki W 2026

Górecki W, Szymoniuk M, Domagalski Ł, Litak J, Turek G, Kus-Budzynska K, Kunicki J, Szmygin P, Nachulewicz P, Ćwik K, Torres K, Baj J, Staśkiewicz G, Czyżewski W. (2026). The NEUROSAVE study – impact of cadaveric neurosurgical workshops on general and paediatric surgeons’ confidence in performing life-saving neurosurgical procedures: a preliminary survey study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08993-3

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective pre/post survey of 15 surgeons (13 pediatric, 2 general) attending the NEUROSAVE cadaveric workshop; paired Wilcoxon tests on 5-point Likert confidence ratings. The article reports "Significant improvements (all p ≤ 0.03)" across the listed steps, e.g. Mayfield fixation 1.00→2.00 (p = 0.01) and decompressive craniectomy 1.00→2.00 (p = 0.01).

> "Significant improvements (all p ≤ 0.03) were observed for Mayfield fixation, burr-hole drilling, frontal and temporal craniotomy, decompressive craniectomy, dural detachment, duroplasty, Dandy-Poppen sutures, dural sealing, bone-flap restoration, and evacuations of epidural, acute subdural, and intraparenchymal hematomas."
