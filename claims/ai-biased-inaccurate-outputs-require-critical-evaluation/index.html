Hoppe T 2026¶

Hoppe T, Loibl K and Leuders T (2026). Rethinking teachers' diagnostic skills in AI-supported formative assessment: from diagnosis to meta-diagnosis. Front. Educ. 11:1857661. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1857661

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Conceptual argument citing fairness and AI-literature (e.g., Barocas et al., 2023; Bender et al., 2021). The article adds that empirical evidence for effectiveness of AI-supported diagnostic systems in educational settings is still limited, and notes automation bias as a risk.

"AI systems can produce biased or inaccurate results, as they are trained on data that may reflect human prejudices, limited representation, or systemic biases. Such distortions can lead to misaligned or unfair diagnostic inferences if they remain unrecognized."
