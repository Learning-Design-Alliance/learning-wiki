## Evidence

### Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche 2017

Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche. (2017). A Learning Patterns Perspective on Student Learning in Higher Education: State of the Art and Moving Forward. Educ Psychol Rev 29:269–299. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9414-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Vermunt et al. (2014) meta-analysis compared factor analyses of ILS scores across eight samples from Hong Kong, Indonesia, Sri Lanka, Spain, Venezuela, Columbia, Mexico and the Netherlands; three context-specific patterns (application-directed, passive-idealistic, passive-motivational) also appeared.

> "In most studies, a meaning-directed, a reproduction-directed, and an undirected pattern emerged, although these looked somewhat differently in different countries"

### Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche 2017 (2)

Jan D. Vermunt & Vincent Donche. (2017). A Learning Patterns Perspective on Student Learning in Higher Education: State of the Art and Moving Forward. Educ Psychol Rev 29:269–299. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9414-6

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports that differences in underlying dimensions between countries from the same continent were often larger than differences between continents; Marambe et al. (2012) found no support for the stereotype 'Asian learner' or an Asian propensity for rote learning.

> "Again, the typical 'Asian', 'European',o r 'Latin-American'student does not seem to exist"
