## Evidence

### Ameya S. Kolarkar and Aimee A. Callender 2016

Ameya S. Kolarkar and Aimee A. Callender. (2016). Learning Physics By Creating Problems: An Experiment. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1609.03517](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1609.03517)

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative evidence from informal interviews of Fall 2014 modders: the authors report impressive depth of understanding and that students correlated knowledge from other scenarios to solve new problems. The authors note the possible confound of increased time-on-task, which they say will need to be systematically tested.

> "And when confronted with a different and difficult scenario, they were able to come up with the solution all by themselves after some thought."
