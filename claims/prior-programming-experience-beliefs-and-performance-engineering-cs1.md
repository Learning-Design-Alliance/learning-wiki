## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students with prior programming experience reported higher self-efficacy (M = 4.274 vs 3.950; F(1,600) = 39.006, p < .001) and lower perceived difficulty (M = 3.822 vs 4.205; F(1,600) = 38.430, p < .001). [→ Griffin Pitts 2026](#griffin-pitts-2026)
`q2 i?` Exam performance differed significantly between students with and without prior programming experience (t = -2.827, p = 0.005). [→ Griffin Pitts 2026 (2)](#griffin-pitts-2026-2)

Group comparison by binary prior programming experience (239 with, 363 without among 602 students) showed "significantly higher self-efficacy (M = 4.274, SD = 0.570)" and significantly lower perceived difficulty for experienced students; no standardized effect size printed.
