Carneiro Vera Lúcia Alves, Jorge Jorge (2020). Competencies and training needs of the Portuguese optometrists - a national inquiry. Journal of Optometry. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optom.2019.12.003

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of procedure frequencies by academic qualification (Graduate vs Masters/PhD) in the national survey (n = 444). The authors report that "individuals with higher qualifications, that is, academic degree of Masters/PhD, more frequently perform the referred procedures".
