### Tachibana Tetsuya 2025

Tachibana Tetsuya, Katagiri Hiroki, Ogawa Takahisa, Koyano Gaku, Jinno Tetsuya. (2025). Enhancing the accuracy of cup placement in total hip arthroplasty using CT-based navigation by a trainee surgeon: a retrospective cohort study. BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-025-08493-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ROC analysis of inclination outliers in 5-case increments across the trainee's 112 navigated THA cases identified the cutoff; the article reports "sensitivity = 0.71, specificity = 0.90" at 20 cases, and the AUC was highest at 20 cases (Fig. 4B).

> "The maximum cutoff point performance (sensitivity and specificity) was identified as 20 cases (sensitivity = 0.71, specificity = 0.90)."
