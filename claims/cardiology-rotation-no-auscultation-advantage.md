# Cardiology rotation training is not associated with greater cardiac auscultation accuracy among junior doctors

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-lesson accuracy did not differ significantly between doctors with cardiology rotation (26%) and without (29%). [→ Tokuda Yasuharu 2020](#tokuda-yasuharu-2020)

## Evidence

### Tokuda Yasuharu 2020

Tokuda Yasuharu, Matayoshi Tetsutaro, Nakama Yasunori, Kurihara Masaru, Suzuki Tomoharu, Kitahara Yusuke, Kitai Yuya, Nakamura Takashi, Itokazu David, Miyazato Tatsuya. (2020). Cardiac auscultation skills among junior doctors: effects of sound simulation lesson. International Journal of Medical Education 11:107-110. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5eb6.70c6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-lesson test comparison of 15 cardiology rotators versus 35 non-rotators among the 50 junior doctors. The article reports "chi-square (1, N = 350) = 0.299, p=0.61"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size was printed.

> "The cardiology rotation training was not associated with greater auscultation accuracy compared with cardiology non-rotation (Fisher’s Exact Test, chi-square (1, N = 350) = 0.299, p=0.61)."
