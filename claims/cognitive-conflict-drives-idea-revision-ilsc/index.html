Related Claims¶

  • Both constructive-conflict conditions produced substantially more idea editing between design iterations than unsupported self-reflection — related
  • Conflict-based instruction improves science conceptual learning, though staged contradictions helped only learners who reported being confused, and no study isolates disequilibrium as the mechanism — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Novice designers deleted ideas only when confronted with stakeholder friction; unsupported self-reflection produced no deletions — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Both stepwise constructive-conflict guidance and the antagonistic agent led novice designers to reconsider what makes a design effective more than unsupported self-reflection — related
  • Only the interactive antagonistic agent, not stepwise guidance, significantly shifted novice designers' design thinking relative to self-reflection — related
  • Dyads showing more Listen/understand and Extend/revise behaviour produced more original ideas regardless of dyad type, while no social-emotional perspective-taking measure was significantly associated with originality — related
