RCT of 120 medical and nursing students comparing VR with live simulation, assessed at baseline, posttest, and 2 months. The review reports the printed P=.047 within-group VR result and no between-group differences.

> "At 2-month follow-up, only the VR group maintained a significant improvement in Interprofessional Socialization and Valuing Scale scores (P=.047), although no statistically significant differences were found between groups at any time point, suggesting comparable effectiveness"
