# KIPP middle school offers did not produce higher five-year four-year graduation or on-track rates than no offer

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the first two lottery cohorts observed for five years, students offered KIPP middle school admission graduated from four-year programs, or were on-track to graduate, at rates similar to students not offered admission. [→ Alicia Demers 2023](#alicia-demers-2023)

Long-term tracking study of the first two lottery cohorts, observable for five years of college persistence, found similar four-year graduation and on-track rates for offer and non-offer students. No effect size or test statistic is printed.
