Chunpeng Zhai 2024¶

Chunpeng Zhai, Santoso Wibowo and Lily D. Li. (2024). The effects of over-reliance on AI dialogue systems on students' cognitive abilities: a systematic review. Smart Learning Environments. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40561-024-00316-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

Study of 16 college students' interactions with AI dialogue systems for academic tasks, reported in the review's decision-making section. The review reports efficiency gains in research and information retrieval alongside complacency and undue dependency; concerns about plagiarism, decreased creativity, data bias, security and discrimination also emerged.

"Duhaylungsod and Chavez (2023) investigated 16 college students' interactions with AI dialogue systems for academic tasks. The results indicated that AI dialogue systems efficiently decreased the time dedicated to research and information retrieval. This over-reliance fostered complacency and an undue dependency on AI dialogue systems."

Chunpeng Zhai 2024 (2)¶

Chunpeng Zhai, Santoso Wibowo and Lily D. Li. (2024). The effects of over-reliance on AI dialogue systems on students' cognitive abilities: a systematic review. Smart Learning Environments. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40561-024-00316-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

Study of 15 non-native English speaking EFL learners using AI dialogue systems for text paraphrasing, reported in the review's decision-making section. The review states that when explanations are overly simplified this "may result in an increased reliance on AI", undermining learners' ability to analyze information independently and impairing decision-making skills.

"It reveals that the main difficulty arises from the lack of comprehensive explanations accompanying AI-generated paraphrases. This deficiency makes it challenging for learners to grasp the context and verify the accuracy of the reformulated content."
