Evidence¶

Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi and N. Sanjay Rebello 2026¶

Syed Furqan Abbas Hashmi and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). A bottom-up taxonomy of student discourse with a Socratic AI physics tutor. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07373 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.07373

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Reliability validation of the LLM coding pipeline: an expert independently coded a stratified random sample of 287 student messages (10% of the corpus) under the same prompt instructions, and labels were mapped onto the consolidated category set. Disagreements clustered on borderline moves such as confirmation versus restatement.

"agreement was Cohen's κ= 0.78(raw agreement 84%), which falls within the range conventionally interpreted as substantial agreement and is consistent with reliability levels reported for human and ma- chine coding in recent PER work"
