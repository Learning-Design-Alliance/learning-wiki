## Evidence

### Mittal 2011

Mittal, A. K. (2011). Developing a diagnostic e-module for testing conceptual understanding of Symmetry and Gauss's Law. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1106.0441](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1106.0441)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r1`

Design argument in the section on designing the diagnostic module: the author reasons that because T/F items are easier to guess, "The probability of randomly answering seven questions is less than 1%", so seven items per category are needed. No empirical trial is reported.

> "The probability of randomly answering seven questions is less than 1%. So a diagnostic module should present at least seven questions for each identified concept category in order to relia bly ascertain that the concept category has been learnt."
