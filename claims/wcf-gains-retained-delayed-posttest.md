# Preposition accuracy gains from written corrective feedback are retained two weeks after treatment

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` On the delayed post-test two weeks after treatment, the experimental group retained and slightly increased its improvement while the control group remained nearly unchanged, with a large effect size (d = 4.62). [→ Ahmed Ali Saleh Al Ajmi 2015](#ahmed-ali-saleh-al-ajmi-2015)

## Evidence

### Ahmed Ali Saleh Al Ajmi 2015

Ahmed Ali Saleh Al Ajmi. (2015). The Effect of Written Corrective Feedback on Omani Students’ Accuracy in the Use of English Prepositions. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.1p.61

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test on the delayed post-test, administered unannounced two weeks after treatment. Experimental group M = 8.08 (SD = 1.75) versus control M = 1.48 (SD = 1.08); "the effect size was large (d= 4.62)".

> "The scores for the delayed post-test also showed a significant difference in the experimental group scores as they remained higher, with a slight increase, than the immediate post-test scores (M =8.08, SD =1.75)  while the control group’s scores showed no noticeable increase (M=1.48, SD=1.08), t(48)=16.001, p=.000, two-tailed), and the effect size was large (d= 4.62)."
