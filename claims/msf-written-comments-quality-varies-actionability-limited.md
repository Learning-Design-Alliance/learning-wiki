## Evidence

### Saedon H 2012

Saedon H, Salleh S, Balakrishnan A, Imray CHE, Saedon M. (2012). The role of feedback in improving the effectiveness of workplace based assessments: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education, 12, 25. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3432628/

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative review reporting Canavan et al's analysis of phrases in written MSF feedback comments for residents and fellows, coding valence, specificity and behaviour-orientation. The review reports "74.5% of surveys contained at least one global judgement" and "Negative feedback phrases were found in 10.3% surveys."

> "74.5% of surveys contained at least one global judgement. Behaviour-oriented phrases occurred less frequently, and general behaviours were mentioned more often than specific behaviours. Negative feedback phrases were found in 10.3% surveys."
