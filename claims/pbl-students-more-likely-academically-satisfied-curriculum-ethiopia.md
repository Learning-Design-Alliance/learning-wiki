## Evidence

### Tadesse Selamawit Girma 2022

Tadesse Selamawit Girma, Tadesse Dereje Girma, Dagnaw Eyaya Habtie. (2022). Problem based learning approach increases the academic satisfaction of health science students in Ethiopian universities: a comparative cross sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03397-5

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Result of a multiple binary logistic regression in an institution-based comparative cross-sectional survey of 850 students (425 PBL at Debretabor University, 425 LBL at Mekelle University), reporting "AOR = 1.50, 95% CI = 1.02, 2.21" for curriculum-type satisfaction. The confidence interval barely excludes 1.00.

> "Similarly, problem based learning students were more likely to be academically satisfied than lecture based learning students in their type of curriculum with (AOR = 1.50, 95% CI = 1.02, 2.21)."
