### Diana Sachmpazidi 2025

Diana Sachmpazidi, Mike Verostek, Jayna Petrella, Chandra Turpen, and Siwoo (Randy) Lee. (2025). Psychometric Evaluation of the Culture around Systemic Change Survey: A tool for Assessing Departmental Culture in Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.09953](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.09953)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Validity analysis using an independent clusters factor model fitted with lavaan on the N = 111 pilot data. The article reports "values of A VE for each construct to be greater than the threshold value of 0.50 to indicate convergent validity," with AVE diagonals ranging 0.57–0.70 in Table V.

> "We also found that the squared inter-construct correlation values were all lower than the A VE values for each construct, providing evidence for discriminant validity."
