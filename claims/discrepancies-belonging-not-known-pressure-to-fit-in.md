### Noah Leibnitz and Yangqiuting Li 2026

Noah Leibnitz and Yangqiuting Li. (2026). Investigating students’ gender expression and its relation to sense of belonging in introductory physics courses. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.17966](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.17966)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Semi-structured interviews with 26 students from various gender identity groups in introductory physics. Students’ accounts suggested "both discrepancies and lower belonging were linked to feeling not personally known by their physics peers," plus self-presentational pressure to fit in.

> "both discrepancies and lower belonging were linked to feeling not personally known by their physics peers, and many interviewees also described pressure to alter  their gender expression in physics to fit in, gain recognition, or establish their presence."
