Evidence¶

Proctor 2011¶

Proctor, C.P., Dalton, B., Uccelli, P., Biancarosa, G., Mo, E., Snow, C., & Neugebauer, S. (2011). Improving comprehension online: effects of deep vocabulary instruction with bilingual and monolingual fifth graders. Read Writ 24, 517–544. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-009-9218-2

q2 · i? · causal · r?

In the same quasi-experimental comparison of intervention and control fifth graders, the article reports effects were "non-significant for comprehension"; no effect size or test statistic is printed, so the null is reported without an equivalence claim.

"In comparison to a control group, there were significant intervention effects on a standardized measure of vocabulary knowledge, but effects were non-significant for comprehension."
