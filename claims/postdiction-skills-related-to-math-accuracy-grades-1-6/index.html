Evidence¶

Desoete 2019¶

Desoete, A., Baten, E., Vercaemst, V., De Busschere, A., Baudonck, M., & Vanhaeke, J. (2019). Metacognition and motivation as predictors for mathematics performance of Belgian elementary school children. ZDM Mathematics Education, 51, 667–677. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-018-01020-w

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Longitudinal study testing Belgian children from Grade 1 till 6 in January and June. The abstract reports that "metacognitive postdiction skills of children were related to accuracy in mathematics" across the whole elementary period; no effect size is printed.

"The metacognitive postdiction skills of children were related to accuracy in mathematics during the whole elementary school period."
