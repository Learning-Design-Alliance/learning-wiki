### Kakiuchi Yoshihiko 2024

Kakiuchi Yoshihiko, Kuroda Shinji, Yoshida Yusuke, Kanaya Nobuhiko, Kashima Hajime, Kikuchi Satoru, Kagawa Shunsuke, Fujiwara Toshiyoshi. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Phase comparison (P1: 11 cases, P2: 11, P3: 14) for DSTs-1 in the retrospective cohort, analyzed with Mann–Whitney U and Steel–Dwass tests; median surgical time fell from 249 min (P1) to 196 min (P3). The article reports a "significant reduction" with the printed p-values.

> "Surgical time in P3 for DSTs-1 showed a significant reduction compared to P1 (p = 0.001) and P2 (p = 0.0027). No significant differences in amount of intraoperative bleeding, postoperative hospital stay, or frequency of complications of CD Grade ≥ III were seen between DSTs-1 and DSTs-2."
