## Evidence

### Asma Shannan Alzahrani 2023

Asma Shannan Alzahrani, Yi-Shan Tsai, Naif Aljohani, Emma Whitelock-wainwright, Dragan Gasevic. (2023). Do teaching staff trust stakeholders and tools in learning analytics? A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10229-w

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Survey paired t-test for item 8 among 65 teaching staff. The article states item 8 "had the largest rating gap between predicted and ideal expectations", indicating the highest distrust; the test was significant (t = 3.301, p = 0.002, Cohen's d = 0.55).

> "The paired t-test showed a significant difference between the two expectation types with a medium effect size (t = 3.301, p = 0.002, Cohen's d = 0.55)."
