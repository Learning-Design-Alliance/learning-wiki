### Mahinpei 2026

Mahinpei, R., Dean, V., Fong, R., Liu, L. T., & Ribeiro, M. H. (2026). AI Assistance for Discretionary Work: Increasing Feedback Provision in Higher Education. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03095

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized field experiment, analysis restricted to submissions that received feedback (N = 664). Within-question regression found "no statistically significant effect" on time per character (p = 0.41); the article interprets this as AI lowering initiation barriers without eliminating review and adaptation effort.

> "We find no statistically significant effect of treatment assignment on time spent per character of feedback (coeff. = 0.29 s/char, SE = 0.35, 95% CI [-0.39, 0.97],𝑝= 0.41)."
