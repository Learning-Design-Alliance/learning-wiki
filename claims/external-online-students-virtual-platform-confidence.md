## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Three external students reported greater confidence and preparedness to practise on online platforms as a byproduct of online enrolment, while face-to-face graduates lacked confidence translating skills to virtual platforms. [→ Jones 2025](#jones-2025)

## Evidence

### Jones 2025

Jones, C. and Cominos, N. (2025). The Future of Healthcare—Simulation‐Based Learning Curricula and Preparing Future Midwives for Effective Rapport‐Building in Telehealth Settings. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.16927

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding from the learner-experiences theme (Section 5.3). One external graduate said "Personally, I feel like I'm quite prepared" because platform use "been integrated into my education just the way that I've done my course"; virtual classes focused on course content, not telehealth.

> "The three external students reported feeling more confidence and preparedness to practice using an online platform as a result of their enrolment as external students, rather than through any specific training in telehealth in the curriculum."
