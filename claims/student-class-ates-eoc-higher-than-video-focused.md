# Students in Video-and-Practice-Focused and Review-Focused classes obtained higher Algebra I EOC scores than Instructional-Video-Focused students

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` ATEs estimated with IPW and Vermunt's three-step method showed Video and Practice Focused (C2) and Review Focused (C4) students scored higher than Instructional Video Focused (C1) students in all classroom latent classes, while Pre-Algebra Video Focused (C3) students did as well as C1. [→ Walter L. Leite 2021](#walter-l-leite-2021)

## Evidence

### Walter L. Leite 2021

Walter L. Leite, Zeyuan Jing, Huan Kuang, Dongho Kim & A. Corinne Huggins-Manley (2021). Multilevel Mixture Modeling with Propensity Score Weights for Quasi-Experimental Evaluation of Virtual Learning Environments. Structural Equation Modeling: A Multidisciplinary Journal, 28:6, 964-982. https://doi.org/10.1080/10705511.2021.1919895

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Planned contrasts of mean intercepts from a multilevel mixture regression of Algebra I EOC scores with inverse propensity weights at the student level, using class C1 as reference because "watching instructional videos is the most passive use of the VLE". No effect sizes were printed for these contrasts.

> "The student-level ATE estimates show that students in the “Video and Practice Focused” class (i.e., C2) and the “Review Focused” class (i.e., C4) obtained higher Algebra 1 EOC scores than students in the “Instructional Video Focused” class in all classroom latent classes."
