The authors' analysis of why adaptive information filtering, a classic technology from Information Retrieval, became relevant for education. They note that MLTutor presents "one of the first interesting examples of applying content-based AIF to education."

> "However, the Web with its abundance of non-indexed “open corpus” educational resources has made AIF very attractive for educationalists."
