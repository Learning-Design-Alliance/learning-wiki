> "The meta-analysis of discrimination indices (Fisher’s z-transformed) across 12 data points yielded a pooled estimate of 1.45 (95% CI [1.21, 1.69]). This result was statistically significant (Z = 11.69, p < 0.00001), suggesting that the assessments included in the review generally demonstrated strong discriminatory power"
