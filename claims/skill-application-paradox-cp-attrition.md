### Azuad Islam Ruhan 2026

Azuad Islam Ruhan, Golam Mostofa Naeem, Rakibul Islam Rafi, Sherin Afrin Mim, Nazira Bani Opi, Dewan Fahad Chowdhury, and Md. Rejaul Korim Sadi. (2026). Predicting Student Attrition in Competitive Programming: A Large-Scale Study Integrating Survey Insights and Global Behavioral Logs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28618 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.28618](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.28618)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multi-institutional survey across 10 Bangladeshi universities (n=73 analyzed here; 22 active, 51 stopped). Independent-samples t-test shows stopped students rate their math skill higher (t=−2.29, p= 0.025); data structure understanding shows the same direction (3.57 vs 3.09).

> "Students who had stopped CP reported a mean math skill rating of 3.88 (out of 5), compared to 3.41 among currently active students (t=−2.29,p= 0.025)."
