## Evidence

### Braun 2017

Braun, D. C., Gormally, C., & Clark, M. D. (2017). The Deaf Mentoring Survey: A Community Cultural Wealth Framework for Measuring Mentoring Effectiveness with Underrepresented Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0155

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Nonparametric Kruskal-Wallis test comparing factor scores across Deaf, Deaf-know, and Deaf-unaware mentor categories (n = 71 mentoring experiences). Post hoc comparisons showed Deaf versus Deaf-unaware differences for all three factors; Deaf-know versus Deaf-unaware differed only for Deaf Community Capital.

> "Being a Scientist (H(2) = 5.02, p = 0.081) scores of mentoring relationships were not significantly affected by the mentor's hearing status. Deaf Community Capital (H(2) = 30.4, p < 0.001), Asking for Accommodations (H(2) = 9.25, p = 0.010), and Communication Access (H(2) = 12.5, p = 0.002) scores of mentoring relationships were highly significantly affected by the mentor's familiarity with deaf people."

### Braun 2017 (2)

Braun, D. C., Gormally, C., & Clark, M. D. (2017). The Deaf Mentoring Survey: A Community Cultural Wealth Framework for Measuring Mentoring Effectiveness with Underrepresented Students. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.15-07-0155

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Jonckheere-Terpstra test for ordered trend across the Deaf, Deaf-know, Deaf-unaware mentor categories, treated by the authors as a scale from greatest to least familiarity with deaf individuals.

> "This test showed a highly significant trend for factor scores reported of mentoring relationships for Deaf Community Capital, Asking for Accommodations, and Communications Access (p < 0.001, p = 0.003, p < 0.001)."
