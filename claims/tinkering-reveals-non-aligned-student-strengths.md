Stefano Rini, Sara Ricciardi. (2026). Tinkering in Primary School: From Episode to Science Practice. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.21776](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.21776)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire completed by 20 teachers after the Officina della Luce project asked whether tinkering revealed unexpected behaviour; most answered positively. One teacher wrote that children "who struggle the most with traditional educational activities showed they could navigate them easily and enthusiastically". No effect size reported.
