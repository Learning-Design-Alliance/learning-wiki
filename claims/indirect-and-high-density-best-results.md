`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Test results for the semi-indirect and direct installations. The authors add that with low and medium density reflectors at ordinary working intensities, the brightness of the source has not been sufficiently reduced to give much relief to the eye.

> "A great deal of loss of visual efficiency has been found to result, however, from the use of semi-indirect reflectors of low and medium density and from direct reflectors of medium and shallow depth."
