## Evidence

### Huang Xin 2019

Huang Xin, Zeng Nan, Ye Sunyue. (2019). Associations of Sedentary Behavior with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance among Chinese Students Aged 8–19 Years. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16224494

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The mixed-effects analysis of the 1040-student cross-sectional sample found an inverse association of screen-based SB with physical fitness "in girls after adjusting for study-based SB and other SB"; boys' association was non-significant in the fully adjusted model.

> "In the present study, we observed an inverse association of screen-based SB with physical fitness in girls after adjusting for study-based SB and other SB."

### Huang Xin 2019 (2)

Huang Xin, Zeng Nan, Ye Sunyue. (2019). Associations of Sedentary Behavior with Physical Fitness and Academic Performance among Chinese Students Aged 8–19 Years. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16224494

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results of the linear mixed-effects models (Table 3, model 3) in the 1040-student sample: the screen-based SB coefficient for boys' physical fitness was not significant after full adjustment (p > 0.05), whereas it remained significant for girls.

> "In model 3, after further adjustment of school, grade, and class, the associations of screen-based SB with physical fitness were not significant in boys (p > 0.05)."
