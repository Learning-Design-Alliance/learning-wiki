## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Disattenuated correlations among domain-specific emotion factors were generally weak, with mathematics–physics relations ranging from .38 to .61 in Grade 8 and .48 to .64 in Grade 11, and most other coefficients below .35 (Grade 8) or .20 (Grade 11). [→ Goetz 2006](#goetz-2006)

## Evidence

### Goetz 2006

Goetz, T., Frenzel, A. C., Pekrun, R., Hall, N. C., & Lüdtke, O. (2006). Between- and Within-Domain Relations of Students' Academic Emotions. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Structural equation modeling of multi-item scales (4 items per emotion per domain) in a correlational study of 542 German high school students. "all coefficients were below .35" in Grade 8 and below .20 in Grade 11, with mathematics–physics relations .38–.61 and .48–.64; coefficients were disattenuated latent-level estimates.

> "all coefficients were below .35 in the Grade 8 sample and below .20 in the Grade 11 sample. The relations between emotions in mathematics and physics ranged from .38 to .61 in the Grade 8 sample and from .48 to .64 in the Grade 11 sample"
