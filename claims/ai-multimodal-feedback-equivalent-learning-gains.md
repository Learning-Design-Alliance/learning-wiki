# AI multimodal feedback produces post-test learning gains equivalent to educator feedback after controlling for pre-test performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized online experiment, post-test scores did not differ significantly between the AI multimodal feedback condition and the business-as-usual educator feedback condition after controlling for pre-test scores. [→ Chloe Qianhui Zhao 2026](#chloe-qianhui-zhao-2026)
`q3 i2` Both conditions improved significantly from pre-test to post-test, with within-group effect sizes of d = 0.5376 (educator feedback) and d = 0.7623 (AI feedback). [→ Chloe Qianhui Zhao 2026 (2)](#chloe-qianhui-zhao-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Chloe Qianhui Zhao 2026

Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Jie Cao, Jionghao Lin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). LLM-based Multimodal Feedback Produces Equivalent Learning and Better Student Perceptions than Educator Feedback. LAK 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15280 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2601.15280](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2601.15280)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA in the RQ1.1 learning evaluation, with post-test score as dependent variable, condition as between-group factor, and pre-test as covariate; homogeneity of regression slopes was satisfied (p = 0.24). The "effect of condition on post-test scores was not significant" with partial η² = 0.0047.

> "After controlling for pre-test performance, the effect of condition on post-test scores was not significant (𝐹=0.91,𝑝=0.34, partial𝜂 2 =0.0047)."

### Chloe Qianhui Zhao 2026 (2)

Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Jie Cao, Jionghao Lin, and Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2026). LLM-based Multimodal Feedback Produces Equivalent Learning and Better Student Perceptions than Educator Feedback. LAK 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15280

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests within each condition in the RQ1.1 analysis of the randomized experiment (197 learners). The AI group improved significantly with Cohen's d = 0.7623; the baseline group's paired test gave d = 0.5376 (t = −3.4841, p = 0.0006).

> "The AI Multimodal Feedback group also demonstrated significant improvement from pre- to post-test (𝑡=− 5.6273, 𝑝<0.001***,𝐶𝑜ℎ𝑒𝑛′𝑠𝑑=0.7623)."
