## Evidence

### Conlin 2019

Conlin, L. D., Kuo, E., and Hallinen, N. R. (2019). Nothing's plenty: The significance of null results in physics education research. Preprint. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020104](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.15.020104)

`q3 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Study of Algebra I students in Midwestern US classrooms completing six problem types; students were "generally quite accurate on both" formats (81% correct, SD=31 for equations; 75% correct, SD=38 for word problems).

> "The primary finding was a null result: there was no significant difference in accuracy between proportions problems presented as equations versus as word problems."
