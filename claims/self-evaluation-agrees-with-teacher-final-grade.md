State University of New York (SUNY) Brockport. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.00970](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.00970)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test (Table 4 reports Z = 1.000, p = .317). Student self-evaluations (n = 74) averaged 1.99 (SD = 0.12); teacher final grades (n = 114) averaged 1.89 (SD = 0.37). Equivalence was not tested beyond non-significance.

> "no statistically significant difference was found between students' self-evaluations and the teacher's final grades (p > .05), suggesting a high level of agreement between students' self-assessments and the teacher's final holistic evaluations."
