## Evidence

### Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang 2026

Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang. (2026). Unpacking grit in exam-oriented education: divergent roles of perseverance and consistency in foreign language achievement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04312-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Path analysis (CB-SEM, AMOS 24.0, N = 310) of direct effects on academic emotions. The model fit the data (χ2/df = 2.257, CFI = 0.942, RMSEA = 0.070); POE was "strongly positively associated with FLE" while COI's association with enjoyment was "non-significant".

> "it was strongly positively associated with FLE (β = 0.894, p < .001) and significantly negatively associated with FLB (β = −0.453, p < .001). In contrast, the association between COI and enjoyment was non-significant; instead, it was significantly and positively associated with boredom (β = 0.301, p < .001)."

### Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang 2026 (2)

Weng Tsung-han and Peng Jinchang. (2026). Unpacking grit in exam-oriented education: divergent roles of perseverance and consistency in foreign language achievement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04312-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of the same 310 participants. POE was "strongly positively correlated with FLE (r = .716, p < .01)"; COI correlated positively with FLB (r = .347) and negatively with FLA (r = −.155), per the article's Table 4 narrative.

> "POE was significantly and positively correlated with FLA (r = .214, p < .01), strongly positively correlated with FLE (r = .716, p < .01), and significantly negatively correlated with FLB (r = −.450, p < .01)."
