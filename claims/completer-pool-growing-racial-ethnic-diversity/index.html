Descriptive analysis of race/ethnicity of completer survey respondents over five years (Figure 3). The report states "The percent identifying as people of color increased from 39% in 2016–17 to 53% in 2020–21," noting California's completers are much more diverse than the national pipeline, where 27% of recent completers identify as people of color.
