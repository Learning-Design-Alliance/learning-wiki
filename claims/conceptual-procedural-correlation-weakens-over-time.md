`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlations between rubric dimensions computed separately at T0, T1, and T2. At T0 several were near zero or slightly negative and nonsignificant; by T1 coefficients were positive but smaller; at T2 all were weak and nonsignificant.

> "At T2, all correlations remained positive yet uniformly weak and nonsignificant, reflecting that, after one year without reinforcement, conceptual understanding and procedural execution became largely independent dimensions of visualization competence."
