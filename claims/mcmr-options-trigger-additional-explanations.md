## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students giving only hand-motion explanations on free response are triggered into additionally giving correct medium-change responses on the MCMR question, more often than the reverse. [→ Michael C. Wittmann 1999](#michael-c-wittmann-1999)

## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 1999

Michael C. Wittmann, Richard N. Steinberg, Edward F. Redish. (1999). Making Sense of How Students Make Sense of Mechanical Waves. The Physics Teacher. [doi:10.1119/1.880142](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.880142)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Analysis of matched pre- and post-instruction response tables (Fall-1997, N=92) comparing free-response and MCMR formats. The most common off-diagonal entries show free-response hand-motion reasoners adding correct medium responses on MCMR; fewer students are triggered in the other direction.

> "students who answer the free response question using only hand motion explanations are triggered into additionally giving correct medium change responses on the MCMR question."
