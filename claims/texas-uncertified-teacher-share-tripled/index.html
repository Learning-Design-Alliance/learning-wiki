Descriptive analysis of statewide Texas Education Agency administrative data reported in the executive summary and introduction. The report states the share of uncertified teachers "more than tripled between 2019–20 and 2024–25, increasing from 3.8% to 12%" and counts 42,103 uncertified teachers in 2024–25.

"Overall, the share of uncertified teachers in the Texas teacher workforce more than tripled between 2019–20 and 2024–25, increasing from 3.8% to 12%."
