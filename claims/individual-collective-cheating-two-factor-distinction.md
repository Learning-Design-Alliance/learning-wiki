## Evidence

### Caroline Pulfrey 2018

Caroline Pulfrey, Kevin Durussel, and Fabrizio Butera. (2018). The Good Cheat: Benevolence and the Justification of Collective Cheating. Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. 110, No. 6. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000247

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Factor analysis of the acceptance-of-cheating items in the Study 1 questionnaire (N = 122) "left two factors with eigenvalues over 1": one loading five individual cheating items and one loading three collective cheating items such as sharing homework answers; a confirmatory factor analysis showed good model fit for the two scales.
