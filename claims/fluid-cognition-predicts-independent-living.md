## Evidence

### Salthouse 2012

Salthouse, T. (2012). Consequences of Age-Related Cognitive Declines. Annual Review of Psychology, 63, 201-226. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-120710-100328

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review of studies using the Everyday Cognition Battery, ETS Basic Skills Test, Everyday Problems Test, and Observed Tasks of Daily Living Test. The review reports moderate path coefficients of 0.48 for fluid reasoning and 0.22 for crystallized knowledge with observed daily-living task factors.

> "the strongest relations have been found with novel problem-solving (or fluid) rather than with knowledge (or crystallized) measures of cognition"
