## Evidence

### Diedhiou A 2015

Diedhiou A, Gilroy KE, Cox CM, Duncan L, Koumtingue D, Pacqué-Margolis S, Fort A, Settle D, Bailey R. (2015). Successful mLearning Pilot in Senegal: Delivering Family Planning Refresher Training Using Interactive Voice Response and SMS. Global Health: Science and Practice. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-14-00220

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Endline survey administered by trained data collectors within 5 weeks of course completion (N=20). The article reports 90% found the phone easy or very easy to use and "learned the same or more compared with an in-person course"; all 20 would like another mobile course.

> "The large majority (90%) noted that using the phone for the course was easy or very easy and that they learned the same or more compared with an in-person course."
