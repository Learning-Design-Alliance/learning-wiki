Evidence¶

Liu and Koedinger 2017¶

Ran Liu, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2017). Closing the loop: Automated data-driven cognitive model discoveries lead to improved instruction and learning gains. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 9, No 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1155896

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory log analysis of the Motivation for Learning HS Geometry 2012 dataset (Dataset #748), from 82 tenth-grade students with 72,404 problem steps. The article reports that except for rectangle area items, forward-backward differences were "specific to cases where the backward calculations required the application of a square root" (Figure 1).

"An exploratory analysis of the 2012 dataset reveals a substantially higher proportion of correct first attempts at forward circle (0.89) and square (0.86) area items than backward circle (0.72) and square area (0.59) items, respectively."
