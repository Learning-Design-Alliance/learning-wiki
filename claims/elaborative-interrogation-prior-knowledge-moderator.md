Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Experimental study by Woloshyn, Pressley, and Schneider (1992) reported in the monograph: Canadian and German students answered why-questions about facts from a high-knowledge and a low-knowledge domain. The high-knowledge domain showed a 24% increase versus 12% in the low-knowledge domain; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "students showed larger effects of elabora- tive interrogation in their high-knowledge domain (a 24% increase) than in their low-knowledge domain (a 12% increase)"
