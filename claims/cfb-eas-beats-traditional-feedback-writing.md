## Evidence

### Gulzar et al. 2013

Malik Ajmal Gulzar, Sartaj Fakhar Jilani & Choudhary Zahid Javid. (2013). Constructive Feedback: An Effective Constituent for Eradicating Impediments in Writing Skills. English Language Teaching, 6(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p21

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post-test comparison of two randomly selected groups of 15 secondary students each; the experimental group received constructive feedback via error analysis, the control group traditional correction. The article reports the experimental group's "improvement ... was better compared to the control group"; no effect size is printed.

> "Inter and intra-lingual types of errors were not fully understood by the participants, but improvement of the experimental group was better compared to the control group."
