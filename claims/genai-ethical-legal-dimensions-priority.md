### Rook 2026

Rook, L., & Plumb, M. (2026). Integrating Generative Artificial Intelligence into University Curricula: Student Insights. Journal of University Teaching and Learning Practice, 23(2). https://doi.org/10.53761/jjzzd330

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

The thematic analysis of 166 capstone business students' qualitative responses listed "navigating ethical and legal dimensions of GenAI" as the third priority area students identified for curriculum development. No effect size is reported.

> "students identifying three priority areas for development: (1) understanding and optimising GenAI functionality; (2) exploring GenAI applications across contexts; (3) navigating ethical and legal dimensions of GenAI"
