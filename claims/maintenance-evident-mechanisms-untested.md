## Evidence

### Melanie Turner 2025

Melanie Turner, Flaviu A. Hodis. (2025). Strengthening Conscientiousness by Means of Interventions: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10016-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis (RQ4) of the six studies with follow-ups ranging from two weeks to one year. The review reports increases "were maintained post-intervention"; only one article discussed maintenance mechanisms.

> "Overall, the findings from these studies indicate that increases in conscientiousness resulting from the interventions were maintained post-intervention."

### Melanie Turner 2025 (2)

Melanie Turner, Flaviu A. Hodis. (2025). Strengthening Conscientiousness by Means of Interventions: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10016-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review synthesis (RQ5): the review reports no mediation analyses across the 11 studies and a single moderation test, which found awareness of the intervention target did not moderate effectiveness.

> "Only one of the interventions we reviewed examined moderation effects. Specifically, Hudson (2021a) reported that the effectiveness of the behavior activation intervention used was not moderated by whether participants were aware that the intervention they received targeted conscientiousness. None of the articles conducted mediation analyses."
