Evidence¶

Roslina 2017¶

Roslina. (2017). The Effect of Picture Story Books on Students’ Reading Comprehension. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 8(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.2p.213

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Observational and interview data from the treatment meetings. Students said they liked picture story books but grew bored with frequent repetition; the article attributes this to "the frequently same types materials came in a short time duration".

"In another case, the students’ enthusiast began to decrease in the fifth meeting."
