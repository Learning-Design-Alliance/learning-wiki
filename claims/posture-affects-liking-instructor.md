# Instructor body posture affects students' liking of the instructor on both dimensions and their interaction, mainly driven by the slumped/closed condition

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In the preregistered experiment, students' liking was significantly affected by the vertical dimension, the horizontal dimension, and their interaction. [→ Traulsen 2024](#traulsen-2024)

## Evidence

### Traulsen 2024

Traulsen, S. J., & Zander, L. (2024). Straighten your back, open your arms! Effects of instructor's body postures in educational videos on students' interest and motivation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101959

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered experiment with 434 students; 2×2 ANOVA on liking (two items, α = .93) showed all three effects significant (partial η2 = .014, .048, .023), with pairwise differences suggesting the pattern was "mainly driven by the slumped/closed condition."

> "The vertical dimension, F (1, 430) = 6.132, p = .014, partial η2 = .014, the horizontal dimension, F (1, 430) = 21.741, p < .001, partial η2 = .048, and the interaction of both dimensions, F (1, 430) = 10.060, p = .002, partial η2 = .023, were significant."
