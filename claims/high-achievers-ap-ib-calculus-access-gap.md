## Evidence

### Melodie Baker 2023

Melodie Baker, Ivy Morgan, and Gizella Wade. (2023). Opportunities Denied: High-Achieving Black and Latino Students Lack Access to Advanced Math. EdTrust and Just Equations. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641831

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive HSLS:09 analysis restricted to high-achieving students (passed Algebra I or higher in middle school or top math-assessment quintile) in public schools offering advanced math courses, to reduce selection bias. Figure 1 reports the same distribution of highest math course taken.

> "While 46% of high-achieving Asian students, 19% of White students, and 29% of students from high-SES backgrounds took AP/IB calculus by the end of high school, the same was true for just 10% of Black and 15% of Latino high achievers, and 11% of high achievers from low-SES backgrounds."
