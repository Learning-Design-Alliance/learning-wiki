### Acosta Tatiana 2020

Acosta Tatiana, Sutton Jill Marie, Dotters-Katz Sarah. (2020). Improving Learners' Comfort With Cesarean Sections Through the Use of High-Fidelity, Low-Cost Simulation. MedEdPORTAL. https://doi.org/10.15766/mep_2374-8265.10878

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

By-education-level comparison (Table 3, N = 19) of mean comfort performing a CS, pre to post: third-year medical students 1.1 to 2.5, first-year residents 1.3 to 2.7, second-year residents 3.3 to 3.3, fourth-year residents 4.3 to 4.3. The article states that earlier trainees "had the lowest comfort level but also had the largest absolute change in comfort performing a CS after the simulation."
