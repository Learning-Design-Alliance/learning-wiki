Mazur M., Jedliński M., Janiszewska-Olszowska J., Ndokaj A., Ardan R., Nardi G.M., Marasca R., Ottolenghi L., Polimeni A., Vozza I. (2021). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression models on the average score of 13 questions, with final variables selected by adjusted R2. The model was significant (F [4.298] = 5.947, p < 0.001), R2 = 0.074; the dentistry-qualification coefficient was 0.058.

> "The model is significant (F [4.298] = 5.947, p < 0.001), R2 = 0.074. The model estimation result suggests that, ceteris paribus, the expected score of a dentist with a qualification in dentistry is 0.058 greater (almost one correct answer more) than in the case of a medicine qualification."
