## Evidence

### Aaron Reinhard 2022

Aaron Reinhard, Alex Felleson, Paula C. Turner, and Maxwell Green. (2022). Assessing the Impact of Metacognitive Post-Reﬂection Exercises on Problem-Solving Skillfulness. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010109)

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r1`

Ordered logistic regression on non-binary rubric items for students who took the FCI pre-test, run on the same sample as the later two-predictor model. The article reports the odds ratio as "exp (0.260)" which "comes out to be 1.30 (95% CI [1.02, 1.66])".

> "An ordered logistic regression run on all scores given for non-binary rubric items agreed with this result: being in the treatment group increased the ordered log odds of a student earning a higher score by 0.260 (p-value = 0.037, 95% CI [0.02, 0.50])."
