## Evidence

### Dawit T. Tiruneh 2014

Dawit T. Tiruneh, An Verburgh & Jan Elen. (2014). Effectiveness of Critical Thinking Instruction in Higher Education: A Systematic Review of Intervention Studies. Higher Education Studies. http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/hes.v4n1p1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's discussion of implicit teaching strategies reports that higher-order questioning practice produced significant improvement on domain-specific CT measures, while "students were not able to transfer" those skills to novel contexts measured by domain-generic measures.

> "We noticed that such practice resulted in significant improvement in measures that are closely related to the original task (domain-specific CT m easures). However, students were not able to transfer the acquired domain-specific thinking skills to novel contexts (as measured by domain-generic CT measures)."
