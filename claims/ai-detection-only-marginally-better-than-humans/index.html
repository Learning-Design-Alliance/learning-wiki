Evidence¶

Sandra Leaton Gray 2025¶

Sandra Leaton Gray, Dominic Edsall, Dimitris Parapadakis. (2025). AI-Based Digital Cheating At University, and the Case for New Ethical Pedagogies. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-025-09642-y

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article reports, citing Wahle et al. (2022), that detection tools show "only a marginally higher success rate than human reviewers". The printed rates are 80% for machines and 78.4% for humans; no effect size or significance test is printed.

"Studies show that these tools have only a marginally higher success rate than human reviewers, with detection rates around 80% for machines compared to 78.4% for humans (Wahle et al., 2022)."
