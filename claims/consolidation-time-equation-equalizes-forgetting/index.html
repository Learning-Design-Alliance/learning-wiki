Evidence¶

Ricker Timothy J. 2014¶

Ricker Timothy J., Cowan Nelson. (2014). Differences between Presentation Methods in Working Memory Procedures: A Matter of Working Memory Consolidation. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0034301

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 3 with 30 college students removed the blank periods between sequential items, holding presentation, encoding, and consolidation time constant across methods. The interaction "F(2,56)= 1.11, p=.34" was not significant; Cowan's k showed similar forgetting (0.41 sequential vs. 0.45 simultaneous items).

"unlike Experiments 1 and 2, in this experiment there was no interaction of Presentation Method with Retention-Interval Duration, F(2,56)= 1.11, p=.34, indicating that the rate of time-based forgetting was similar for sequential and simultaneous presentation methods"
