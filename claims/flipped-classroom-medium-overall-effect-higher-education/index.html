Evidence¶

Hwan Young Jang and Hye Jeong Kim 2020¶

Hwan Young Jang and Hye Jeong Kim. (2020). A Meta-Analysis of the Cognitive, Affective, and Interpersonal Outcomes of Flipped Classrooms in Higher Education. Educ. Sci. 2020, 10, 115. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci10040115

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random effects meta-analysis of 43 empirical studies of flipped classrooms in higher education published 2012–2017. The overall effect size was ES = 0.35 (95% CI = 0.24 to 0.47), described as "a medium eﬀect in terms of the Cohen’s rule of thumb". Effect sizes were heterogeneous (Q[42] = 1022.9, p < 0.05, I2 = 96.0), justifying the random effects model.

"The overall eﬀect size of ﬂipped classrooms was 0.35, indicating that ﬂipped classrooms had a medium eﬀect in terms of the Cohen’s rule of thumb [68]."
