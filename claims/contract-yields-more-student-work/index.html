Evidence¶

Danielewicz 2009¶

Danielewicz, Jane and Peter Elbow. (2009). A Unilateral Grading Contract to Improve Learning and Teaching. College Composition and Communication 61(2). https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.14394/23309

q1 · i? · design · r1

The authors' experiential claim from using contracts in first-year writing: even papers that do "no more than" fulfill the requirements represent a gratifying amount of work, and students tend to get more skilled by term's end. No quantitative data are printed.

"As a result, we find the contract yielding more total work: more tasks done thoroughly by more students—more "student ergs." The effect is most striking with skilled writers who must now engage in learning tasks they used to skip."
