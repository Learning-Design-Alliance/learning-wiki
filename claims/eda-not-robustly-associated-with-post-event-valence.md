## Evidence

### Hartikainen 2026

Hartikainen, S., Nokelainen, P., Puhakka, I., & Kangaslampi, R. (2026). Emotions in flipped and lecture-based math courses. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102474

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Across multilevel logistic models adding event-level and segment-level EDA predictors (Table 3, Model 5, coefficients near zero with CIs spanning zero) for 546 learning events, the article concludes "EDA did not show robust associations with post-event emotional valence". No effect size is printed for this null.

> "EDA did not show robust associations with post-event emotional valence; however, lecture-based events showed a clearer decline in EDA across event segments than flipped learning events."

### Hartikainen 2026 (2)

Hartikainen, S., Nokelainen, P., Puhakka, I., & Kangaslampi, R. (2026). Emotions in flipped and lecture-based math courses. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102474

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Within-student Kendall's τb correlations (Table 2) in the lecture-based sample (n = 34) show a weak, significant middle-segment EDA–valence association with printed effect size "τb = 0.13, p = .005" that did not appear in the flipped sample — a scope limit across implementations.

> "post-event emotional valence was weakly associated with average EDA during the middle part of learning events in the lecture-based group (Mt1.cwc; τb = 0.13, p = .005), whereas this association was not evident in the flipped learning group"
