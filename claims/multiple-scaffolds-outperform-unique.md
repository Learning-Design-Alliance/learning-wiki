# Multiple kinds of scaffolds are more effective than a unique scaffold for academic performance

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Studies using multiple scaffolds (ES = 0.577) outperformed those using a unique scaffold (ES = 0.211), a statistically significant difference (Q = 4.424, df = 1, p = 0.035). [→ Lanqin Zheng 2016](#lanqin-zheng-2016)

## Evidence

### Lanqin Zheng 2016

Lanqin Zheng. (2016). The effectiveness of self-regulated learning scaffolds on academic performance in computer-based learning environments: a meta-analysis. Asia Pacific Education Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s12564-016-9426-9

`q3 · i1`

Moderator analysis under a random effects model comparing 18 studies coded as multiple scaffolds (1869 subjects) with 11 studies coded as unique scaffolds (779 subjects). The difference between the two categories was statistically significant.

> "The higher effect size was associated with the multiple kinds of scaffolds (ES = 0.577), in contrast to the unique scaffold (ES = 0.211). There was a statistically signiﬁcant difference between the multiple and unique scaffolds ( Q = 4.424, df = 1, p = 0.035)"
