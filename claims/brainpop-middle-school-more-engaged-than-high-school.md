### Luke 2017

Luke, Christina C.; Gibson, Sierra; Francisco, Aubrey T. (2017). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Using Edtech to Support English Learners. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/383e02ba-587a-4192-be68-175543798661

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reported usage comparison by school level in Vista, from surveys and developer-provided usage data (Figure 8 shows logins by school). Surveys and focus groups indicated middle school students and educators found the content more engaging than high school students and educators did.

> "students in middle school logged into BrainPOP ESL more than twice as frequently (182 logins) on average as high school students (74 logins) over the course of the pilot. In addition, middle school students completed an average of 109 learning activities compared to 60 for high school students"
