### Faure Theresa 2024 (2)

Faure Theresa, Weyers Imke, Voltmer Jan-Bennet, Westermann Jürgen, Voltmer Edgar. (2024). Test-reduced teaching for stimulation of intrinsic motivation (TRUST): a randomized controlled intervention study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05640-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc analysis of the intrinsic motivation data located the Friendly Feedback advantage over control at t3, t4 and t8; for extrinsic motivation the group differences were not significant, so the effect is specific to intrinsic motivation.

> "In post hoc analysis the scores for intrinsic motivation for the IVFF were significantly higher than those for the CG at t3, t4 and t8 (Fig. 4; see also Additional file 2). For extrinsic motivation the differences between study groups were not significant (data not shown)."
