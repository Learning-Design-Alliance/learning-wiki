## Evidence

### Kamphausen Anne 2026

Kamphausen Anne, Pilz Maximilian, Baumgärtel Matthias, Kubitz Jens Christian, Engelen Christian. (2026). Effect of an app-guided advanced cardiac life support algorithm on teamwork, non-technical skills, and workload in simulated in-hospital cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled feasibility trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-026-01673-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Video-rated TEAM assessment by two trained raters across 40 teams. "Mean (SD) values were closely aligned across all TEAM domains" with all p ≥ 0.4; inter-rater reliability was excellent (ICC = 0.959; 95% CI 0.95–0.97).

> "Mean (SD) values were closely aligned across all TEAM domains (e.g., Leadership 6.8 (1.0) vs. 6.7 (1.7); Teamwork 22.4 (4.4) vs. 21.7 (5.6); Task Management 6.0 (1.6) vs. 5.5 (2.0); Global Rating 7.4 (1.9) vs. 7.2 (2.3); all p ≥ 0.4))."

### Kamphausen Anne 2026 (2)

Kamphausen Anne, Pilz Maximilian, Baumgärtel Matthias, Kubitz Jens Christian, Engelen Christian. (2026). Effect of an app-guided advanced cardiac life support algorithm on teamwork, non-technical skills, and workload in simulated in-hospital cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled feasibility trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-026-01673-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Video-rated ANTS assessment across 40 teams. "Across all four ANTS categories—Task Management, Team Working, Situation Awareness, Decision Making—there were no clear between group differences"; mean category scores ranged from 2.6 to 3.3 with ICC 0.642–0.945.

> "Across all four ANTS categories—Task Management, Team Working, Situation Awareness, Decision Making—there were no clear between group differences (Table 4)."
