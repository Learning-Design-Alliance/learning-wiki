Study protocol hypothesis for aim 1 of the planned cluster randomized trial, with cognitive gain as the primary outcome measured by the DAS-II at the beginning and end of year 1. No results are reported; the power analysis targets detection of a moderate effect of d = 0.43 for student outcomes.

> "We will examine to what extent TeachTown is associated with student gains in cognitive ability, language skills, school readiness, and social skills. We hypothesize that use of TeachTown will be associated with improved student outcomes in these domains."
