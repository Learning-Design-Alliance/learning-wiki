Evidence¶

Mahmoud Abdi Tabari & Iman Abdi Tabari 2015¶

Mahmoud Abdi Tabari & Iman Abdi Tabari. (2015). Links between Bloom's Taxonomy and Gardener's Multiple Intelligences: The issue of Textbook Analysis. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.1p.94

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive frequency analysis of 572 coded learning objectives in the four high school textbooks (Table 1), with comprehension at 172 and application at 161 counts; evaluation had 10. Percentages are descriptive, no effect size printed.

"comprehension (%30) and application (%28) were the most frequent numbers of learning objectives and evaluation was reported as the least frequent one."

Mahmoud Abdi Tabari & Iman Abdi Tabari 2015 (2)¶

Mahmoud Abdi Tabari & Iman Abdi Tabari. (2015). Links between Bloom's Taxonomy and Gardener's Multiple Intelligences: The issue of Textbook Analysis. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.6n.1p.94

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive frequency analysis of 718 coded learning objectives in the four Interchange volumes (Table 2). The article notes "These two series were in much accord" and that the Interchange total outnumbered the high school total.

"comprehension (%27) and application (%29.5) were the most frequent numbers of learning objectives and the least frequent number was evaluation (%2.3) part."
