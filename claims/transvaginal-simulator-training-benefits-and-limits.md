Narrative review reporting simulator training studies for transvaginal ultrasound; a French multicenter study "showed significantly better scores evaluating image quality in the intervention group". No effect size printed.

> "The common challenge of image orientation in transvaginal ultrasound could be taught successfully by simulation sessions in a Danish group of medical students without any ultrasound experience [45]. Likewise, a French multicenter study using simulator training for transvaginal ultrasound for gynecologic emergency showed significantly better scores evaluating image quality in the intervention group [48]."

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reporting a comparative study of transvaginal ultrasound training; real-patient practice "outperformed those students who had only trained on simulators", limiting simulator transferability for patient-sensitive examinations.

> "In a study in which transvaginal ultrasound examinations were exercised, students who had previously practiced on real patients outperformed those students who had only trained on simulators [64]."
