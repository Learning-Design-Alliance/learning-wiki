## Evidence

### Kissel KA 2025

Kissel KA, Krewulak KD, Poulin TG, Parhar KKS, Niven DJ, Doiron VM, Fiest KM. (2025). Understanding ICU Nursing Knowledge, Perceived Barriers, and Facilitators of Sepsis Recognition and Management: A Cross-Sectional Study. Critical Care Explorations. https://doi.org/10.1097/CCE.0000000000001200

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of the survey's Sepsis Knowledge Assessment Tool (single/multiple choice and true/false items) completed by 101 ICU nurses. The most significant knowledge gaps included screening-tool identification, with 11.9% correctly identifying the National Early Warning Score, and missed identification of pregnancy as a sepsis risk factor (31.7% correct).

> "the identification of screening tools to identify sepsis/septic shock, as recommended by the Society of Critical Care Medicine (SCCM) (3) (with 11.9% of RNs correctly identifying the National Early Warning Score as a recommended tool (n = 12) and 77.2% incorrectly selecting the Sequential Organ Failure Score (SOFA) or quick SOFA (n = 78), and the missed identification of pregnancy (current/recent) as a risk factor for sepsis (31.7% correct, n = 32)"
