## Evidence

### Wileman V. 2025

Wileman V., Steed L., Pinnock H., Kelly M., Sohanpal R., Heslop-Marshall K., Taylor SJC. (2025). Assessing competence of primary care respiratory healthcare professionals to deliver a psychologically-based intervention for people with COPD: results from the TANDEM study. NPJ Primary Care Respiratory Medicine 35, 11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41533-025-00416-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Change-score analysis of early versus late cases for the nine facilitators with two assessed cases. The article also reports a within-case trend from a session 1 mean of 33.6 to scores above 40 in sessions 7–9, based on a small sample of later sessions.

> "mean therapeutic competence for early cases (comprising 45 sessions) was 32.3 (SD = 6.8), which increased to 37.6 (SD = 7.4) in later (comprising 41 sessions) cases (MD = 5.4, p = 0.13)."
