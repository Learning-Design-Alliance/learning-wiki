A 2017 meta-analysis on social and emotional learning reported that participating students "demonstrated a 6% increase in high school graduation rates and an 11% increase in college graduation rates." The report cites this as evidence for replacing exclusionary discipline with SEL strategies.

> "According to a 2017 meta-analysis on the effects of social and emotional learning, students participating in social and emotional learning programs demonstrated a 6% increase in high school graduation rates and an 11% increase in college graduation rates."
