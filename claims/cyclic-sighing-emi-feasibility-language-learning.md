## Evidence

### Wang 2026

Wang, P., Ganushchak, L., Welie, C., & van Steensel, R. (2026). Learning proximal cyclic sighing buffers anxiety across learning moments and boredom across days. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00446-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive implementation analysis of app-logged data from a 20-day two-arm RCT (n = 84 EMA contributors) with five daily prompts. The breathwork group "completed 85.1%" of prompted EMAs and the control group 86.9%; breathwork session fidelity was 87.3% (1,092/1,251).

> "EMA adherence was high in both groups: the control group completed 86.9% of prompted EMAs (2,697/3,105), and the breathwork group completed 85.1% (3,584/4,210)."
