### Arnold B. Bakker 2024

Arnold B. Bakker, Karina Mostert. (2024). Study Demands–Resources Theory: Understanding Student Well-Being in Higher Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09940-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Aloia and McTigue (2019), a US college student study in which the demands–well-being link "was weakened when students had access to an abundance of study resources" such as supportive communication.

> "Aloia and McTigue (2019) found evidence for a buffer effect in their study among college students in the USA. Specifically, they found that the impact of study demands (e.g., workload, and pressures to perform) on psychological well-being was weakened when students had access to an abundance of study resources (supportive informational and emotional communication)."
