## Evidence

### Owens et al. 2017

Julie Sarno Owens, Erika K. Coles, Steven W. Evans, Lina K. Himawan, Erin Girio-Herrera, Alex S. Holdaway, Allison K. Zoromski, Terah Schamberg, and Ann Schulte. (2017). Using Multi-Component Consultation to Increase the Integrity with which Teachers Implement Behavioral Classroom Interventions: A Pilot Study. School Mental Health, 9, 218-234. https://doi.org/0.1007/s12310-017-9217-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Latent class analysis of baseline teacher knowledge of ADHD, knowledge of behavior modification, and locus of control for student failure (the success subscale was dropped due to low reliability, KR20 = .46). The two-class model had the lowest BIC and was chosen over three- and four-class solutions; "d effect sizes range from 1.55 to 2.89" between classes.

> "Relative to teachers in Latent Class 1, teachers in Latent Class 2 were less knowledgeable and took less credit for their students’ failure (d effect sizes range from 1.55 to 2.89)."
