`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Perceptual accounts from spring 2025 site-visit interviews, not school-record data. Students described check-ins as keeping them on track, and staff provided strategies for studying and encouraged resources such as Valor's homework hotline and online tools such as Gauth. No quantitative grade data are reported.
