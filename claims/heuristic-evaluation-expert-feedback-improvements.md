### García de Diego Laura 2015

García de Diego Laura, Cuervo Marta, Martínez J. Alfredo. (2015). Development of a Learning-Oriented Computer Assisted Instruction Designed to Improve Skills in the Clinical Assessment of the Nutritional Status: A Pilot Evaluation. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0126345

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

First-phase heuristic evaluation with 8 health professionals recruited for nutrition expertise at the University of Navarra, conducted through face-to-face interviews exploring interface design problems, complexity and functionality. The authors report using this feedback to add tools and modify the presentation of questions and the application programming interface.

> "who appreciated the UNyDIET software in general and the CAI in particular, due to userfriendly interface and its usefulness and practicality, however they considered it was necessary to insert new clinical assessment tools and make changes on the screen format, in the programming and in writing."
