Roderick 2000¶

Roderick, M., Nagaoka, J., Bacon, J., & Easton, J. Q. (2000). Update: Ending Social Promotion: Passing, Retention, and Achievement Trends Among Promoted and Retained Students 1995-1999. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of early-grade retention (Figure 7), 1992-1999. The brief states an unanticipated effect of the policy might be that teachers and schools are becoming more inclined to retain students in grades not affected by the policy.

"The proportion of students who are retained in kin- dergarten through second grade started to rise in 1996 and has continued to do so"

Roderick 2000 (2)¶

Roderick, M., Nagaoka, J., Bacon, J., & Easton, J. Q. (2000). Update: Ending Social Promotion: Passing, Retention, and Achievement Trends Among Promoted and Retained Students 1995-1999. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Tracking of early-grade retainees reaching the third-grade gate. Many were excluded or had left: of 1,398 second graders retained in 1997, only 55 percent were included under the policy by spring 1999. The brief calls the double-retention trend troubling.

"In 1999, 33 percent of third graders who had been re- tained the year before, in second grade, and were included under the policy were retained again."
