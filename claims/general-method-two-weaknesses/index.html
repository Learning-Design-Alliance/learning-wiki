Critical argument in the survey section of the article. The author reasons that a good general psychologist may take a long time to become a good teacher, and that "general psychology is quite a different thing from the psychology of a given subject of instruction." No empirical data are reported; this is the author's reasoned critique.

"General method has ignored or underestimated the fact that general psychology is quite a different thing from the psychology of a given subject of instruction."
