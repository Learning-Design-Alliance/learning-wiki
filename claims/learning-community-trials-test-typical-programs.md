Scope statement from the Conclusions section of the six-trial synthesis, limiting what the pooled null-to-modest short-term results can say about stronger implementations of the model described in prior literature.

> "Notably, these studies purposely selected programs that represent a range of typical learning community programs as they exist in community colleges. As a result, the research provides good tests of learning communities as they appear to be typically enacted, but not a test of the “ideal” or “advanced” learning communities described in the literature."
