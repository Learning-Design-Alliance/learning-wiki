## Evidence

### Gutiérrez-González R 2025

Gutiérrez-González R, Royuela A, Zamarron A. (2025). Student engagement in a flipped undergraduate medical classroom to measure optimal video-based lecture length. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2479752

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression of percentage of video viewing (R² = 0.807) on seminar status, weeks to final exam, academic year, video length and embedded questions; unstandardised percentage-point coefficients as printed, no standardised effect size.

> "Independent variables associated with a higher percentage of video viewing were seminars (+14.2%, p < 0.001), earlier course content regarding the final exams (+5.9%, p < 0.001), and academic years 1 and 2 compared to the third one (+12%, p < 0.001)."

### Gutiérrez-González R 2025 (2)

Gutiérrez-González R, Royuela A, Zamarron A. (2025). Student engagement in a flipped undergraduate medical classroom to measure optimal video-based lecture length. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2479752

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression of the non-access rate (R² = 0.801); seminar-linked videos and videos released earlier in the course were less often never started. Video length had no significant effect on this outcome.

> "Regarding the non-access rate, the independent factors with lower values were seminars (−15.5%, p < 0.001), earlier videos (−5.9%, p < 0.001), and academic years 1 and 2 (−12% and −14%, p < 0.001)."

### Gutiérrez-González R 2025 (3)

Gutiérrez-González R, Royuela A, Zamarron A. (2025). Student engagement in a flipped undergraduate medical classroom to measure optimal video-based lecture length. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2479752

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression of response rate to embedded questions (R² = 0.843); seminar status and earlier release were the strongest independent factors, with videos under 5 minutes at p = 0.055, close to significance.

> "Finally, a higher response rate to embedded questions were independently associated with seminars (+13.8%, p < 0.001), earlier lessons (+6%, p < 0.001), and academic years 1 and 2 (+12.2% and +14.5%, p < 0.001)."
