Design analysis by the authors, not a controlled study. They report systematically mapping iMecaProf's uses onto the mechanics course and organizing the software into levels that "deﬁne a pedagogical progression" from orientation to dynamics and energy.

> "We have systematically identiﬁed where iMecaProf can experimentally accompany a course on Classical Mechanics. Different levels in iMecaProf have then been deﬁned which deﬁne a pedagogical progression."
