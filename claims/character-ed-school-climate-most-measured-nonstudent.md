Cross-program analysis of non-student outcomes across the 36 reviewed programs (Findings chapter, Figure 3). The report states school climate "was measured in studies of 16 programs" and parent participation in school in 11; staff morale was assessed in studies of six programs.

> "At the school level, school climate—the tone or nature of interactions among members of the school community—was measured in studies of 16 programs. At the parent level, studies of 11 programs addressed parent participation in school."
