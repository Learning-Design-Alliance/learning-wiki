## Evidence

### Simonsen Bjoerg O 2014

Simonsen Bjoerg O, Daehlin Gro K, Johansson Inger, Farup Per G. (2014). Improvement of drug dose calculations by classroom teaching or e-learning: a randomised controlled trial in nurses. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2014-006025

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-course evaluation by the randomised groups: usefulness for daily work rated 3.1 (0.7) for e-learning vs 2.7 (0.7) for classroom (p<0.001). Difficulty and satisfaction did not differ between methods. The authors attribute this to e-learning flexibility to concentrate on difficult, work-relevant items.

> "The specific value of the course for working situations was scored 3.1 (0.7) in the e-learning group and 2.7 (0.7) in the classroom group (p<0.001)."
