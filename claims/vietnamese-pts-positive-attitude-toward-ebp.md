## Evidence

### Dao Hiep Thi 2018

Dao Hiep Thi, Pichaiyongwongdee Sopa, Sullivan Patricia E., Prasertsukdee Saipin, Apinonkul Benjawan. (2018). Are physical therapists in Viet Nam ready to implement evidence-based practice? A survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1428-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional self-administered survey of 381 eligible physical therapists in governmental healthcare organizations in Ho Chi Minh City, conducted July–October 2017. Descriptive results on the attitude items showed "Between 80 and 90% of participants agreed that EBP is necessary and useful". No effect size was reported for this descriptive finding.

> "Between 80 and 90% of participants agreed that EBP is necessary and useful, or that EBP improves patient care and helps practitioners remain current with healthcare trends."
