## Evidence

### Ulfa Yunefit 2021

Ulfa Yunefit, Igarashi Yukari, Takahata Kaori, Shishido Eri, Horiuchi Shigeko. (2021). A comparison of team-based learning and lecture-based learning on clinical reasoning and classroom engagement: a cluster randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02881-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-group repeated-measure analysis across the three measurement points in the cluster RCT showed significant increases from pre-test to post-test (p < .001) in both groups, with slight decreases afterward. The accomplishment rate was 30.0 on the 12–60 scale.

> "the intervention group showed a mean clinical reasoning on PPH score that was higher than the accomplishment rate at post-test and 2 weeks post-test compared with the control group which showed a mean clinical reasoning on PPH score below 30.0 at post-test and 2 weeks post-test"
