Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • In one low-risk-of-bias trial, adding mentoring to a multifaceted intervention improved one physician behavior (antiplatelet medication use) while other behaviors showed no difference — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Mentoring alone as a knowledge translation intervention was evaluated in only one included study, which showed improvement in some behavioral outcomes — related
  • Mentoring within multifaceted interventions compared to no intervention showed mixed effects on knowledge use, practitioner, and organizational outcomes — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Mentoring within multifaceted interventions outperformed single interventions (educational materials alone or educational meetings) on knowledge, beliefs, and evidence-use outcomes in several trials — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
