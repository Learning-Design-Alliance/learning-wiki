Germain 2024¶

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Formative assessment trend reported in the Progress section using Istation's Indicators of Progress. Kindergartners "on grade level" increased in ELA from 71.4% to 82.6% and in math from 37.9% to 65.3% between 2021–22 and 2023–24.

"In their local formative assessment (Istation's Indicators of Progress), the percent of students on grade level in ELA increased from 71.4% in 2021–22 to 82.6% in 2023–24, and the percent of students on grade level in math increased from 37.9% to 65.3% over the same period."
