## Evidence

### Gorard et al. 2015

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui, Beng Huat See. (2015). Accelerated Reader Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://www.gl-assessment.co.uk/products/new-group-reading-test/test-detail

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Sub-group analysis of FSM-eligible pupils (56 AR versus 59 control) on the NGRT, printed effect sizes +0.38 (overall reading score) and +0.41 (SAS). The report cautions these results "do not have the force of a trial" because FSM pupils were not randomised as such.

> "Although the next results do not have the force of a trial, because FSM -eligible were no t randomised to groups as such, the evidence is that AR is at least as effective as overall for those pupils eligible for FSM (Tables 6 and 7)."
