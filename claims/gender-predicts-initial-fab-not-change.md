## Evidence

### Asbury 2026

Asbury, K., Carstensen, B., & Klusmann, U. (2026). Changing perspectives: The development of preservice teachers' field‐specific ability beliefs across academic disciplines. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 337–355. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70019

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the conditional latent growth model with covariates, gender significantly predicted the intercept (B = .16, p = .033) but not the slope (B = −.03, p = .479). Prior achievement was "not associated with the intercept (B = −.01, p = .926)" and also did not predict slope (B = .02, p = .517).

> "This variation was partially explained by gender, with male preservice teachers reporting statistically significant stronger FABs (B = .16, p = .033). In contrast, prior achievement was not associated with the intercept (B = −.01, p = .926)."
