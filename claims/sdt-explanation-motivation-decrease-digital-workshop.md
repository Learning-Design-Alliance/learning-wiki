## Evidence

### Nurhan Aktaş & Hayati Akyol 2020

Nurhan Aktaş & Hayati Akyol. (2020). Effect of Digital Writing Workshop Activities on Writing Motivation and Development of Story Writing Skills. https://doi.org/10.29329/ijpe.2020.248.20

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' theoretical interpretation, drawing on self-determination theory, of why motivation fell: applications were determined by the researcher at each writing stage, students had difficulty with them, and no alternatives were offered.

> "The fact that the digital applications were not chosen  by the students limited the need for autonomy, which is important in terms of motivation. The situation led to a decrease in students’ motivation."
