### Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre 2014

Paul W. Irving and Eleanor C. Sayre. (2014). Upper-Level Physics Students' Perceptions of Physicists. Originally published 2000. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1409.1197](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1409.1197)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview-based phenomenographic study of 21 upper-level physics students; the analysis indicates "a relationship between perceptions of physicists and goal orientation is indicated." High-research/mastery and low-research/mastery categories show mastery orientation; the other two categories show performance orientation.

> "A relationship between perceptions of physicists and goal orientation is indicated."
