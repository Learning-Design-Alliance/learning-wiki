The authors' argument in Section II D about the BAC-CAB 'bone' identity (Eq. 18/20): the graphical way is a "quick visual mnemonic" and avoids the bulkiness of dummy indices, with the advantage growing as equations involve more operations and dummy indices.

> "First, it naturally serves as a quick visual mnemonic for Eq. 20. Also, in practical circumstances, the graphical form avoids the bulkiness of dummy indices and significantly simplifies the procedure of index replacement by δij's."
