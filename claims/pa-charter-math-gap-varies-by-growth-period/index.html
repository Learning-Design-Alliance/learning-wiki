Figure 5 analysis of learning gains by growth period (2014-2015 to 2016-2017) against TPS VCR peers. The report finds charter students "perform significantly worse in the first two growth periods" in math, with approximately 53 and 30 fewer days of learning, and similar performance in the final period.
