Center for Research on Education Outcomes 2019¶

Center for Research on Education Outcomes. (2019). Charter School Performance in Pennsylvania. Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-pennsylvania-2/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Figure 5 analysis of learning gains by growth period (2014-2015 to 2016-2017) against TPS VCR peers. The report finds charter students "perform significantly worse in the first two growth periods" in math, with approximately 53 and 30 fewer days of learning, and similar performance in the final period. No standardized effect size is printed.

"charter school students perform significantly worse in the first two growth periods. In the 2014-2015 growth period, students attending charter schools experienced approximately 53 fewer days of learning in math, while in the 2015-2016 growth period"
