Editorial summary of the third paper, which investigated the effects of exploratory strategy type and prior knowledge level on middle school students' performance and motivation in a 3D role-playing game for chemical formulas. The editorial quotes the mixed strength of the worked-example effect across the two knowledge measures.

"signiﬁcant worked-example effect was revealed on knowledge com- prehension and marginal worked-example effect occurred on knowledge application"
