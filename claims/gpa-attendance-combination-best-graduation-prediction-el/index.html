Evidence¶

Allensworth 2025¶

Allensworth, E.M., de la Torre, M., Franklin, K., & Xu, J. (2025). Identifying Indicators to Support Educational Attainment for Different Groups of English Learners in High School. (Working Paper). Providence, RI: Annenberg EdExchange. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai25-1241

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Logistic regression models predicting high school graduation separately for each English Learner subgroup, with pseudo-R2 statistics compared across models, showed that "Combining GPA and attendance data, or course failure rates and attendance data" predicted graduation best, except for long-term English Learners with IEPs.

"Combining GPA and attendance data, or course failure rates and attendance data, provided the best prediction of high school graduation for most English Learner groups. (except long-term English Learners with IEPs)"

Related Claims¶

  • Attainment likelihood thresholds differ: ELs with GPA above 2.0 had ~90% graduation rates except students with IEPs, and only active ELs needed attendance above 95% to be very likely to graduate — related
  • Only ninth-grade students with at least a 3.0 GPA had greater than a 1-in-4 chance of attaining a college credential, across all groups except long-term English Learners with IEPs — related
  • Combining GPA or course failure rates with attendance provides the best prediction of high school graduation for most EL groups, while GPA plus test scores best predicts college degree attainment — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • GPAs are the most predictive single indicator of high school graduation, college enrollment, and degree completion for all English Learner groups, followed by attendance and course failure rates — related
  • Ninth grade GPAs are the single most predictive indicator of high school graduation, college enrollment, and degree completion for all English Learner groups — related
  • Course performance indicators predict ELL graduation better than ELL-specific indicators such as proficiency level or education interruptions — related
  • GPA combined with test scores was the best prediction of college degree attainment for all English Learner groups, but the test-score relationship partly reflects school composition — related
