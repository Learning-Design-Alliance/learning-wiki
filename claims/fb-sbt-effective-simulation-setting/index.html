q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review of 14 studies of FB SBT for novice trainees (pretest-posttest, two-group nonrandomized, and randomized designs). The review reports that "Eleven studies reported significant improvements in more than one-half of their outcome measures," with heterogeneous outcome measures; no pooled effect size was computed.

"Eleven studies reported significant improvements in more than one-half of their outcome measures. Outcome measures were heterogeneous, ranging from simulator metrics (eg, percentage of time in midlumen) to (validated) bronchoscopy assessment tool end scores."
