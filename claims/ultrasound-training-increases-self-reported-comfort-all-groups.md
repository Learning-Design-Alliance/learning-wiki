## Evidence

### Ahmadzia Homa 2018

Ahmadzia Homa, Cigna Sarah, Namagembe Imelda, Macri Charles, Galerneau France, Magriples Urania. (2018). Teaching obstetric ultrasound at Mulago Hospital - Kampala, Uganda. African Health Sciences. https://doi.org/10.4314/ahs.v18i1.21

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive self-report data from the pre-post survey of 40 participants at Mulago Hospital: comfort "increased from 23% to 73%" overall; pre-intervention comfort was 8% of medical students, 24% of JHOs, and 60% of SHOs, with post-test increases for all three groups.

> "Overall the percentage of participants with any comfort performing basic obstetric ultrasound at pre- and post-test increased from 23% to 73%."
