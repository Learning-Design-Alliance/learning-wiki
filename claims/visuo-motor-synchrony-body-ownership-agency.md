The review reports, citing Mottelson et al. (2023), a review and meta-analysis surveying 111 papers including 4925 participants on technological features influencing body ownership. Visuo-motor synchrony rendered the largest effects; appearance congruence, perspective, visuo-tactile stimuli, and avatar abstraction were also effective. No effect size value is printed.

> "Mottelson and colleagues found that the manipulation of visuo-motor synchrony rendered the largest effects on body ownership and that congruence of appearance, perspective, visuo-tactile stimuli, and abstraction of the avatar were also effective manipulations."
