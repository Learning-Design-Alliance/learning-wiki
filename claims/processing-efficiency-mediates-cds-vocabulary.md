# Processing efficiency at 19 months mediates the relation between child-directed speech and 24-month vocabulary

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · `q3` peer-reviewed experiment

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Including 19-month processing efficiency in the model reduced the path coefficient from child-directed speech to vocabulary from 12.61 to 7.41, with a significant bootstrap indirect effect (95% CI 0.44 to 13.61); the final model explained 47% of vocabulary variance. [→ Weisleder Adriana 2013](#weisleder-adriana-2013)

## Evidence

### Weisleder Adriana 2013

Weisleder Adriana, Fernald Anne. (2013). Talking to children matters: Early language experience strengthens processing and builds vocabulary. Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797613488145

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation analysis (bootstrap per Preacher & Hayes) controlling for maternal education, recording length, and infant vocalizations, testing whether 19-month processing efficiency accounts for the child-directed speech to 24-month vocabulary link. "A bootstrap (Preacher & Hayes, 2004) testing the significance of the indirect effect gave a 95% confidence interval (corrected for bias) of 0.44 to 13.61."

> "the parameter estimate for the effect of child-directed speech on vocabulary was reduced from 12.61 to 7.41 when processing efficiency was included in the model"
