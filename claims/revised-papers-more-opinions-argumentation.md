## Evidence

### Koops Willem JM 2012

Koops Willem JM, van der Vleuten Cees PM, de Leng Bas A, Snoeckx Luc HEH. (2012). Computer supported collaborative learning in a clerkship: an exploratory study on the relation of discussion activity and revision of critical appraisal papers. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-79

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Rainbow-system content analysis of 1582 units of analysis; frequencies were 481 vs 269 (Opinions), 186 vs 80 (Argumentation) and 29 vs 8 (Broaden and Deepen) for revised versus unrevised discussions. The article states "Effect sizes (Cohens 'd) on these categories were large, with exception of category 7."

> "A statistically significant higher number of units in the category 5. (Opinions; P < .000), 6. (Argumentation; P < .005), and 7. (Broaden and Deepen; P < .016) was found in the revised paper discussions compared with the unrevised paper discussions."
