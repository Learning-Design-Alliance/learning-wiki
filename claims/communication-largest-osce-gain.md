# The largest OSCE-domain improvement from multi-agent scaffolding was in communication performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2`–`i3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The multi-agent group scored higher than the control group on the OSCE communication domain (3.53 vs 2.64 on a 1–5 scale), a large effect. [→ Yang 2026](#yang-2026)
`q3 i3` Regression associated multi-agent assignment with a 0.90-point increase in the OSCE communication score, while history-taking completeness did not reach significance. [→ Yang 2026 (2)](#yang-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Yang 2026

Yang, L., Liu, H., Li, S., Jia, R., Xiao, Y., Chen, G., & Lu, L. (2026). Evaluating Scaffolding-Oriented Multi-Agent Large Language Model System for Clinical Interview Training. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10939

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

OSCE domain-score analysis of the randomized exam comparison found the most pronounced between-group difference in communication: means of 3.53 (MA) vs 2.64 (CT), "statistically significant(P = 4.14 × 10−4)", with a large effect size g = −0.79 (sign reflects coding direction).

> "On the 1–5 OSCE rating scale, the mean communication score was higher in the multi-agent group than in the control group(3.53 vs. 2.64). This difference was statistically significant(P = 4.14 × 10−4) and corresponded to a large effect size(g = −0.79)"

### Yang 2026 (2)

Yang, L., Liu, H., Li, S., Jia, R., Xiao, Y., Chen, G., & Lu, L. (2026). Evaluating Scaffolding-Oriented Multi-Agent Large Language Model System for Clinical Interview Training. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10939

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Regression analysis of the same randomized exam data yielded a 0.90-point communication increase (β = 0.90; 95% CI, 0.41 to 1.39; P = 4.50 × 10−4). History-taking completeness showed a weaker positive association that "did not reach conventional statistical significance(β = 0.34; 95% CI,− 0.05 to 0.73; P = 0.091)"; clinical reasoning differences were described as small.

> "Assignment to the multi-agent group was associated with a 0.90-point increase in the OSCE communication score(β = 0.90; 95% CI, 0.41 to 1.39; P = 4.50 × 10−4)"
