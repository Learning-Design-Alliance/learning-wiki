Evidence¶

Viterbori 2015¶

Viterbori, P.; Usai, M. C.; Traverso, L.; De Franchis, V. (2015). How preschool executive functioning predicts several aspects of math achievement in Grades 1 and 3: A longitudinal study. https://hdl.handle.net/11567/813133

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal SEM study of 175 children assessed at age 5 on six EF measures and followed to Grades 1 and 3; the article reports that "the WM–flexibility component measured during the preschool period substantially predicts mathematical achievement, especially in Grade 3."

"The results indicate that the WM–flexibility component measured during the preschool period substantially predicts mathematical achievement, especially in Grade 3."
