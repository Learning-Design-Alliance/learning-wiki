# Students who accepted all suggestions wholesale spent significantly less decision time than other students in their condition

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Yan Tao 2026

Yan Tao, Jennifer Meyer & René F. Kizilcec. (2026). Think Before You Accept: Can Written Justification Reduce Uncritical Uptake of AI Writing Suggestions? Preprint. Under review. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.23936)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-condition Mann-Whitney comparisons of decision time in the embedded randomized experiment. In the justification condition non-selective adopters had median 122.6 vs. 296.6 seconds; in the selection-only condition median 53.3 vs. 120.5 seconds (U= 743,p < .001).

> "These responses spent significantly less time making their adoption decisions than other responses in the same condition (median = 122.6 vs. 296.6 seconds, Mann-WhitneyU= 529,p=.003)."
