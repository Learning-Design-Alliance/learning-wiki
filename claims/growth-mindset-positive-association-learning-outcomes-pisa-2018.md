## Evidence

### King 2025

King, R. B., & Wang, F. (2025). The rich get richer: Socioeconomic advantage amplifies the role of growth mindsets in learning. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95, 792–810. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12755

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Three-level hierarchical linear modelling of the PISA 2018 database with 612,004 students from 80 countries, controlling for individual, school and country covariates. Model 1 showed a positive association of growth mindset with all three outcomes; the article reports "effect size = .29" for achievement and smaller effects for motivation and engagement.

> "growth mindsets were positively associated with academic achievement (b = 11.79, SE = .74, p < .001, effect size = .29), intrinsic motivation (b = .09, SE = .01, p < .001, effect size = .17), and academic engagement (b = .07, SE = .01, p < .001, effect size = .10)."
