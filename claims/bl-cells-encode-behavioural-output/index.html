Evidence¶

Nair 2016¶

Nair, S. S., Paré, D. and Vicentic, A. (2016). Biologically based neural circuit modelling for the study of fear learning and extinction. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.15

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports this experimental finding, citing the Lee et al. single-unit study of BL responses in rats producing or omitting the conditioned response. CS-related firing was absent when rats omitted the CR and present when they emitted it, even to the wrong CS.

"it was found that BL cells activated by appetitive CSs mainly encode behavioural output, not CS identity."

  • Auditory input drives feed-forward inhibition onto LA interneurons, with cortical input producing a smaller E/I ratio than thalamic input — related
  • Auditory thalamic and cortical input to BLA interneurons is concentrated in the LA: thalamic EPSCs are larger in LA than BA interneurons (191 vs 53 pA, p < 0.0001) — related
  • NMDAR subunit stoichiometry differs between LA and BA interneurons: LA NMDAR EPSCs decay faster and LA interneurons include a GluN2A-dominant majority plus NMDAR-lacking cells, absent in the BA — related
  • The timing rule of fear conditioning contradicts the Hebbian coincidence rule because simultaneous CS–US pairing is less efficient than CS onset preceding the US with co-termination tens of seconds later — related
  • Glutamatergic synapses on BA interneurons are less rectifying than those on LA interneurons, indicating fewer calcium-permeable GluR2-lacking AMPARs in the BA — related
  • A biophysical model suggested that more excitable LA neurons are preferentially recruited into the fear memory trace via competitive synaptic interactions, validated experimentally — related
