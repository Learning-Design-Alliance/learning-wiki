## Evidence

### Stergiakouli Evie 2017

Stergiakouli Evie, Martin Joanna, Hamshere Marian L., Heron Jon, St Pourcain Beate, Timpson Nicholas J., Thapar Anita, Davey Smith George. (2017). Association between polygenic risk scores for attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder and educational and cognitive outcomes in the general population. International Journal of Epidemiology. [doi:10.1093/ije/dyw216](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyw216)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-generation regression analysis in ALSPAC (Table 3: e.g. capped GCSE points, maternal PGS adjusted for childhood PGS, N = 4263, β = −2.9 (−5.7 to −0.2), P = 0.036). The quote reports the correlation of mother and child polygenic scores as "Pearson’s correlation coefficient: 0.46".

> "Maternal ADHD polygenic risk scores were also associated with worse educational outcomes in their offspring at both Key Stage 3 and GCSE exams (Table 3). Offspring ADHD polygenic score was included in the model to account for the correlation of ADHD polygenic scores in mothers and their adolescent offspring (Pearson’s correlation coefficient: 0.46). Stronger effects were observed when offspring polygenic score was included in the models, indicating that there is a genetic as well as environmental component in the association."

### Stergiakouli Evie 2017 (2)

Stergiakouli Evie, Martin Joanna, Hamshere Marian L., Heron Jon, St Pourcain Beate, Timpson Nicholas J., Thapar Anita, Davey Smith George. (2017). Association between polygenic risk scores for attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder and educational and cognitive outcomes in the general population. International Journal of Epidemiology. [doi:10.1093/ije/dyw216](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyw216)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of mothers' highest qualification on children's ADHD polygenic score adjusted for maternal score (Table 3, N = 5004). The quote reports "β = −0.06 (−0.10 to −0.02), P = 0.002".

> "In addition, polygenic scores for ADHD in adolescents were associated with lower maternal educational achievement [β = −0.06 (−0.10 to −0.02), P = 0.002] when adjusting for maternal ADHD polygenic scores (Table 3)."
