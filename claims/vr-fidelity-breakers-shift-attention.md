## Evidence

### Loeb Daniel 2025

Loeb Daniel, Shoemaker Jamie, Ely Kelly, Zackoff Matthew. (2025). Deconstructing Participant Behaviors in Virtual Reality Simulation: Ethnographic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65886

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Illustrative episode from the ethnographic analysis of the 15 VR sessions (Table 1, Fidelity Breakers): the team spent "140 seconds trying to trigger the capillary refill animation" while the patient's hemodynamic abnormalities went unaddressed.

> "Together, they spend 140 seconds trying to trigger the capillary refill animation in several locations—the foot, the hand, and the sternum. Meanwhile the virtual patient has globally poor perfusion, low blood pressure, and tachycardia, which is not addressed because the team is stuck on trying to trigger the animation."

### Loeb Daniel 2025 (2)

Loeb Daniel, Shoemaker Jamie, Ely Kelly, Zackoff Matthew. (2025). Deconstructing Participant Behaviors in Virtual Reality Simulation: Ethnographic Analysis. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/65886

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Ethnographic observation of navigational challenges: "participants would inadvertently press a button that transported them to an unintended spot," causing confusion and delays; some participants leaned or reached as workarounds, compromising realism.

> "participants would inadvertently press a button that transported them to an unintended spot—sometimes on the opposite side of the bed or even partially on top of the patient"
