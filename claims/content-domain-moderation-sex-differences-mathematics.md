Meta-regression moderator analysis on 422 studies, 706 samples, and 1,175 effect sizes; the four moderators jointly explained R² = 0.176 of heterogeneity. The article reports the domain-specific estimated effects listed in the quote, with type of content a significant moderator (χ²(5) = 37.54, p < .001).

> "Significant male advantages were found for Broad mathematics ( d = 0.145), Computation (d = 0.089), Geometry (d = 0.218), and National tests (d = 0.068). Effects for Advanced mathematics and Basic numeracy were close to zero."
