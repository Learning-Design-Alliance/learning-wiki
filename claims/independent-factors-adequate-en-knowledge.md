## Evidence

### Song Changli 2026

Song Changli, Zhang Wei, Zhu Lijuan, Ge Teng, Cao Min, and Xu Haihong. (2026). Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices Regarding Enteral Nutrition Management Among ICU Nurses. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70260

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression on knowledge dichotomised at the 80% threshold, entering variables significant in univariate analysis. Female gender was negatively associated with adequate knowledge ("OR = 0.542"), while administrative position and lecture attendance were positively associated.

> "Multivariate analysis indicated that being female (OR = 0.542, 95% CI: 0.324–0.907, p = 0.020), holding an administrative position (OR = 1.931, 95% CI: 1.014–3.681, p = 0.045) and participation in enteral nutrition lectures (OR = 1.721, 95% CI: 1.096–2.702, p = 0.018) were independently associated with adequate knowledge"
