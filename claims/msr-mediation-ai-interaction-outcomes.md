## Evidence

### Davor I 2026

Davor I, Larbi E and Boateng FO (2026). Artificial intelligence-supported learning and higher-order cognitive outcomes: the mediating role of metacognitive self-regulation. Front. Educ. 11:1855869. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1855869

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mediation testing used bias-corrected bootstrapping with 5,000 resamples and 95% confidence intervals in the 533-student SEM; all six indirect effects were significant and "All confidence intervals excluded zero."

> "metacognitive self-regulation significantly mediated the negative effects of cognitive offloading tendency on critical thinking (β = -.031, p = .016) and technical problem- solving (β = -.035, p = .006), supporting H6 and H9."
