`q2 i?` Among emotional, behavioral, and cognitive perspectives on school engagement, cognitive engagement is the least well-defined and conflates with motivation, self-regulation, metacognition, and strategy usage. [→ Chi 2018](#chi-2018)

The article's review of the K-12 engagement literature concludes that "cognitive engagement seems to be the least well-deﬁned" and is typically measured by surveys and self-reports, with deep/meaningful engagement not always linked to achievement.

> "cognitive engagement seems to be the least well-deﬁned (Greene, 2015; Sinatra et al., 2015) and conﬂates with related constructs such as motivation, self-regulation, metacognition, and strategy usage"
