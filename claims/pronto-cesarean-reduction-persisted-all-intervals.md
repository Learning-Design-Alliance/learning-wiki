### Walker Dilys M. MD 2016

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Noncumulative negative binomial regression analysis at 4-, 8-, and 12-month follow-up intervals with a difference-in-differences approach, adjusted for NICU presence. The printed interval estimates were "IRRDID = 0.84", 0.80, and 0.80, all with confidence intervals excluding 1; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "Cesarean delivery incidence was significantly lower in the intervention group at all time intervals; 5 (IRRDID = 0.84; 95% CI, 0.72–0.97), 8 (IRRDID = 0.80; 95% CI, 0.68–0.93), and 12 (IRRDID = 0.80; 95% CI, 0.68–0.93) months of postintervention."
