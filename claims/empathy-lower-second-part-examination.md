# Students requiring the second part of the sequential examination had lower empathy ratings (Cohen's d = 0.81)

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Students called to the second part of the examination had lower empathy scores than students not returning (Cohen's d = 0.81). [→ Wright Barry 2014](#wright-barry-2014)

## Evidence

### Wright Barry 2014

Wright Barry, McKendree Jean, Morgan Lewys, Allgar Victoria L, Brown Andrew. (2014). Examiner and simulated patient ratings of empathy in medical student final year clinical examination: are they useful? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-199

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Group comparison in the sequential final examination between students not returning and students called to the second part. The independent t-test was significant and "Cohen's d = 0.81", which the article calls a large effect size.

> "An independent t-test for the two groups indicates a significant difference (t = −4.2, df = 131, p < .0001). The Cohen's d = 0.81, indicating that the two groups' means differ by 0.81 standard deviation, a 'large' effect size [32]."
