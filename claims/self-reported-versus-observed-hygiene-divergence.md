## Evidence

### Gartmeier Martin 2019

Gartmeier Martin, Baumgartner Maria, Burgkart Rainer, Heiniger Susanne, Berberat Pascal O. (2019). Why hand hygiene is not sufficient: modeling hygiene competence of clinical staff as a basis for its development and assessment. GMS Journal for Medical Education 36(4):Doc39. [doi:10.3205/zma001247](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001247)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article asserts, citing prior studies, that "self-reported attitudes and behaviors often do not correspond with the behaviors actually observed," and that professional routine can lead staff to carry out fewer hygiene measures than they believe. This is the article's attribution to cited literature, not a new empirical analysis; no effect sizes are printed.

> "Regarding attitudes about hygiene, it has been shown that self-reported attitudes and behaviors often do not correspond with the behaviors actually observed."
