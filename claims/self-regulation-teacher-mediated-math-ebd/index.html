Evidence¶

Campbell 2018¶

Campbell, A. R., Bowman-Perrott, L., Burke, M. D., & Sallese, M. R. (2018). Reading, Writing, Math, and Content-Area Interventions for Improving Behavioral and Academic Outcomes of Students With Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 16(2), 119-138. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1194543

q2 · i? · review · r2

Best-evidence synthesis of 27 single-case design studies (Ralston et al., 2014) published 1968-2009, categorizing interventions as peer-mediated, self-mediated, and teacher-mediated for elementary and secondary students with EBD.

"Several self-regulation interventions and instructional components were highly effective in improving mathematics skills of students with EBD, including cover, copy, and compare; self-instruction; the five-step cognitive behavior therapy; and self-monitoring."

Campbell 2018 (2)¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

Within the same best-evidence synthesis (Ralston et al., 2014), seven of thirteen teacher-mediated intervention studies were evaluated as very effective, supporting the conclusion that teacher-mediated interventions contribute to treatment effects.

"One of their conclusions was that teacher-mediated interventions contribute to treatment effects."
