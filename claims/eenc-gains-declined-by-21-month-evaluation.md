## Evidence

### Tosif Shidan 2020

Tosif Shidan, Jatobatu Anna, Maepioh Anita, Gray Amy, Sobel Howard, Mannava Priya, Duke Trevor. (2020). Healthcare worker knowledge and skills following coaching in WHO early essential newborn care program in the Solomon Islands: a prospective multi-site cohort study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-020-2739-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired comparison of post-coaching and evaluation scores (median 21 months, IQR 18–26) using the Paired Sign Test. The article reports written scores at "61%, (IQR 50–72) a reduction of 28% (p < 0.001)" yet "still 17% higher than at baseline"; skills fell to 82% and 72%.

> "At the time of the evaluation, median scores were 61%, (IQR 50–72) a reduction of 28% (p < 0.001) from post-coaching levels but still 17% higher than at baseline."
