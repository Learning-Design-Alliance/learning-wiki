Section 3.3 reports consistency of dietitian use of the intervention across the intervention period of the stepped-wedge trial. The article prints no test statistic or effect size for this time-since-training analysis, so only the directional statement is reported.

> "Dietitian use of the EAT intervention throughout the intervention period was found to be consistent across time. Neither the delivery of, nor competence in The EAT Intervention was found to significantly differ as a function of time since training."
