q3 · i? · design · r3

Confirmatory factor analysis with sample 2 (N = 227) preservice English language teachers, estimated with covariance-based SEM in SmartPLS 4 using maximum likelihood. The final model showed "enhanced and satisfactory model fit (CFI = 0.919; TLI = 0.908)"; Chi-square/df = 1.766, RMSEA = 0.058, SRMR = 0.054.

"The third and final CFA model yielded a refined 27-item version of the TAILS (see Appendix 1), demonstrating enhanced and satisfactory model fit (CFI = 0.919; TLI = 0.908)."
