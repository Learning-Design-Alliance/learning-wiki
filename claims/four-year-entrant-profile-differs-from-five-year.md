### Garrud Paul 2018

Garrud Paul, McManus I. C. (2018). Impact of accelerated, graduate-entry medicine courses: a comparison of profile, success, and specialty destination between graduate entrants to accelerated or standard medicine courses in UK. BMC Medical Education 18:250. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1355-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of demographic and educational profiles in the UKMED 2007/2008 graduate-entrant cohorts. Table 3 ANOVAs showed significant differences in HESA tariff, UKCAT and GAMSAT (all ps < 0.001 after imputation); the article reports 4-yr entrants "had lower HESA ... tariff scores, but higher UKCAT ... and GAMSAT ... scores".

> "4-yr entrants were older, less likely to be from Asian communities, had lower HESA (Higher Education Statistics Agency) tariff scores, but higher UKCAT (UK Clinical Aptitude Test) and GAMSAT (Graduate Medical School Admissions Test) scores, than 5-yr entrants."
