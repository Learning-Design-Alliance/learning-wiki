### James H.K. 2020

James H.K., Chapman A.W., Pattison G.T.R., Fisher J.D., Griffin D.R. (2020). Analysis of Tools Used in Assessing Technical Skills and Operative Competence in Trauma and Orthopaedic Surgical Training: A Systematic Review. JBJS Reviews. https://doi.org/10.2106/JBJS.RVW.19.00167

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's narrative synthesis of checklist studies (20 studies used task-specific checklists). It reports reasonable construct, concurrent validity and reliability, while noting rigid binary scoring, inability to capture performance quality, and an early ceiling effect.

> "They show reasonable construct validity68,77,96,98, concurrent validity37,77,96,102,103, and reliability37,88,114. With their limitations in mind, checklists are perhaps most appropriate for novice learners in a formative setting1."
