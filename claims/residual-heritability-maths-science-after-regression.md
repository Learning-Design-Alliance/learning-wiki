`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariate GWAS of attainment scores with other subjects (N = 5895) or IQ (N = 3197–3260) regressed out. The article reports estimates "still significantly larger than 0 for AA-regressed and IQ-regressed science and maths scores (p′s < 0.05)", with Table 4 values from 0.06 to 0.24. No standardised effect size is printed.
