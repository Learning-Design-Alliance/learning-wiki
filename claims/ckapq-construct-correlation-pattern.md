Correlation matrix (Table 4) computed from the validated CKAPQ responses of 960 students. The article reports "PLE and PEI are strongly correlated (0.614)" and negative correlations between PLE/PEI and the other constructs; the SEM model showed construct correlations ranging from −0.45 to 0.55.

> "PUR is positively correlated with CON (0.355) and PAE (0.163), while PLE and PEI have negative correlations with CON, PAE, and PUR. In addition, PLE and PEI are strongly correlated (0.614), indicating a close relationship."
