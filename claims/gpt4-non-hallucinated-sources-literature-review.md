## Evidence

### Pimbblet 2024

Pimbblet, K.A., & Morrell, L.J. (2024). Can ChatGPT pass a physics degree? Making a case for reformation of assessment of undergraduate degrees. European Journal of Physics. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ad9874](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ad9874)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In the Numerical Modelling and Simulation module's project planning element, the authors replicated three student submissions and found GPT-4 "well-able to provide non-hallucinated sources" for literature reviews, scoring 76% on this component.

> "we explicitly note that ChatGPT is well-able to provide non-hallucinated sources and the prospects for identifying AI-generated text in this manner when prompted appropriately are minimal"
