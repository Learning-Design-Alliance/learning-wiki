`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Concluding interpretive statement of the essay, citing Knowles (1998, p. 72), framing andragogy as additive to rather than exclusive of pedagogy. The essay supports this with the example that Skinner's reward-and-consequence work applies to students of all ages.

> "Knowles reminds us well that andragogy is a set of assumptions that includes the pedagogical assumptions and adds to them (p. 72)."
