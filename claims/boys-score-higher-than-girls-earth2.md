`q3 i?` Boys scored significantly higher than girls on the EARTH-2 mean score and on the consistency score, and the groups did not differ in age. [→ Straatemeier 2008](#straatemeier-2008)

## Evidence

### Straatemeier 2008

Straatemeier, M., van der Maas, H. L. J., & Jansen, B. R. J. (2008). Children's knowledge of the earth: A new methodological and statistical approach. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2008.03.004

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Main experiment ANCOVA and Mann-Whitney tests for gender effects on the EARTH-2. The article reports the group means but no standardized effect size, so no magnitude is coded; boys and girls did not differ significantly in mean age (Mann-Whitney U = 17,352.00, p = .47).

> "The boys (M = 3.09, SD = 0.63, n = 192) scored signiﬁcantly higher on the EARTH-2 than did the girls (M = 2.83, SD = 0.67, n = 184), and the boys (M = 0.64, SD = 0.20) had a signiﬁcantly higher consistency score than did the girls (M = 0.60, SD = 0.18)."
