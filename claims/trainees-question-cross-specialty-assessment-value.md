## Evidence

### Burm Sarah 2020

Burm Sarah, Sebok-Syer Stefanie S., Van Koughnett Julie Ann, Watling Christopher J. (2020). Are we generating more assessments without added value? Surgical trainees’ perceptions of and receptiveness to cross-specialty assessment. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-020-00594-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of four focus groups with 27 PGY-1 surgical trainees who were formatively assessed on intracorporeal suturing by trained non-surgeon faculty. The article reports that "a few trainees found the experience motivating" while more questioned the feedback and practicality of the approach.

> "While a few trainees found the experience motivating, more often trainees questioned the feedback they received and the practicality of this assessment approach to advance their procedural skill acquisition."
