## Evidence

### Cevheroğlu Seda 2025

Cevheroğlu Seda, Fırat Kılıç Hülya, Budak Vildan. (2025). The effect of the PechaKucha presentation method on nursing students’ deep breathing and coughing exercise skills and their satisfaction with the educational method: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08258-5

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

In the same randomized controlled trial of 96 nursing students, satisfaction with the training method was rated on a 1–10 Visual Analog Scale. The PechaKucha group scored "9.04 ± 1.62" versus "7.44 ± 2.39" (p = 0.000, t = 4.403, Cohen's d = 0.785).

> "The mean VAS scores were 9.04 ± 1.62 for the intervention group and 7.44 ± 2.39 for the control group (p = 0.000, t = 4.403, Cohen’s d = 0.785, 95% confidence interval)."
