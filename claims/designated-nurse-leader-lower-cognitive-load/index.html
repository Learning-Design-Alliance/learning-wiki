The review reports this from the CANLEAD simulation-based randomised controlled trial (Pallas et al.), which implemented a new cardiac arrest role to facilitate cognitive offload for medical team leaders. The association covers "lower cognitive load for the medical team leader" and objective performance improvements; no effect size is printed.
