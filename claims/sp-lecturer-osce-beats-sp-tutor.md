## Evidence

### Herchenröther Julia 2021

Herchenröther Julia, Tiedemann Elena, Vogel Heiner, Simmenroth Anne. (2021). Which teaching method is more effective in a communication course – role-playing versus simulated patients, taught by tutors or faculty staff? A randomized trial. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001450](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001450)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial comparing lecturer-led versus tutor-led SP training in a three-session history-taking course for third-year medical students. The Results section reports the pairwise OSCE contrast: "significantly better than that of the students in the SP-Tutor group", d=0.59.

> "OSCE performance of students in the SP-Lecturer group (Mdn=86.9%) was significantly better than that of the students in the SP-Tutor group (Mdn=78.2%), U=244.0, z=-2.09, p=.018, d=0.59"
