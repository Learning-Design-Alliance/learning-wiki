Linda Rosenberg 2015¶

Linda Rosenberg, Megan Davis Christianson, Megan Hague Angus. (2015). Improvement Efforts in Rural Schools: Experience of Nine Schools Receiving School Improvement Grants. Peabody Journal of Education, vol. 90, issue 2. https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/hpje20/90/2 doi:10.1080/0161956x.2015.1022109

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The article's framing of a purposive-sample study of rural SIG schools states that "the distance from urban areas and the commute between the schools and the students' and teachers' homes, can exacerbate the challenges that rural schools face." No effect size is printed.

"However, aspects of rural schools' settings, such as the distance from urban areas and the commute between the schools and the students' and teachers' homes, can exacerbate the challenges that rural schools face."
