## Evidence

### Michael A. Gottfried 2010

Michael A. Gottfried. (2010). Evaluating the Relationship Between Student Attendance and Achievement in Urban Elementary and Middle Schools: An Instrumental Variables Approach. American Educational Research Journal, Vol. 47, No. 2, pp. 434-465. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831209350494

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Two-stage least squares analysis of the full Philadelphia panel with distance from school as the instrument. The article reports standardized effect sizes that "range between 0.39 and 0.45a for baseline models and 0.28 and 0.29cr for value-added models", larger than the ordinary least squares estimates in Table 4.

> "The effect sizes of the instrumental variable results on attendance range between 0.39 and 0.45a for baseline models and 0.28 and 0.29cr for value-added models."
