# Achievement gaps between high-poverty and low-poverty schools at kindergarten entry narrowed modestly but significantly between 2010 and 2017

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The math gap between students at high-poverty and low-poverty schools narrowed from 0.95 to 0.90 SD and the reading gap from 0.88 to 0.77 SD, both significantly. [→ Kuhfeld 2019](#kuhfeld-2019)

## Evidence

### Kuhfeld 2019

Kuhfeld, Megan, Soland, James, Pitts, Christine, & Burchinal, Margaret. (2019). Trends in Children's Academic Skills at School Entry: 2010 to 2017. EdWorkingPaper No. 19-137, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. http://www.edworkingpapers.com/ai19-137

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

School-poverty gap models comparing students in schools with over 75% FRPL eligibility to those with 25% or less, on weighted fall kindergarten MAP Growth scores by year. In 2017 the average math RIT in high-poverty schools (133) was lower than in low-poverty schools (144).

> "Specifically, the math gap between students at high-poverty and low-poverty schools (0.90 SD) was modestly but significantly smaller than the corresponding achievement gaps in 2010 (0.95 SD). The low-high poverty gap in reading significantly narrowed from 0.88 SD in 2010 to 0.77 SD in 2017."
