# Educator training improves implementation fidelity of interventions for students with disabilities, with a large mean effect size of g = 1.08

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across 12 group-design studies, training produced a large, statistically significant mean effect on implementation fidelity compared with no training or business-as-usual training. [→ Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter 2017](#matthew-e-brock-and-erik-w-carter-2017)

## Evidence

### Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter 2017

Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter. (2017). A Meta-Analysis of Educator Training to Improve Implementation of Interventions for Students With Disabilities. Remedial and Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932516653477

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 12 RCTs and quasi-experimental studies of educator training (teachers, pre-service teachers, paraprofessionals) with observed implementation fidelity outcomes. The mean effect size of "g = 1.08, 95% CI = [0.71, 1.46]" was reported as large by common benchmarks; study-level effects ranged from g = .06 to 2.57.

> "Study-level effect sizes across individual studies ranged from g = .06 to 2.57, with a mean effect size of g = 1.08, 95% CI = [0.71, 1.46]."
