## Evidence

### A. Power 2026

A. Power, C. Lee, C. Corish, and A. Shaw. (2026). Registered Dietitians' Knowledge, Attitudes and Use of Simulation‐Based Education in Ireland: A Mixed‐Methods Study. Journal of Human Nutrition and Dietetics. https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.70326

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis of 114 dietitians using Spearman correlations and chi-square tests with Cramer's V; the results section reports the familiarity–attitude association at "p < 0.001". No effect size for this association is printed in the quoted passage.

> "Greater familiarity with SBE was strongly associated with more positive beliefs about its educational value, including perceptions of effectiveness, contribution to communication skills, learner engagement and critical thinking (p < 0.001). Familiarity was also associated with greater motivation to use SBE and fewer concerns about cost and resource demands (p < 0.001)."
