## Subclaims
`q3 i2` The prompts factor had a significant main effect on the combined GAMMA and BSM measures. [→ Lehmann 2019](#lehmann-2019)

## Evidence

### Lehmann 2019

Lehmann, T., Pirnay-Dummer, P., & Schmidt-Borcherding, F. (2019). Fostering integrated mental models of different professional knowledge domains: instructional approaches and model-based analyses. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09704-0

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

The omnibus MANOVA on 92 participants found a significant multivariate main effect of prompts, "Wilks' λ = .895, F(2,87) = 5.124, p = .008".

> "However, there was a significant main effect of prompts on the combined GAMMA and BSM measures, Wilks' λ = .895, F(2,87) = 5.124, p = .008, ηp2 = .105."
