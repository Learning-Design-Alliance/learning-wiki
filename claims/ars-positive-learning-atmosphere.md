## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Despite no significant effect on learning success, the authors conclude the ARS creates a more interactive, positive learning environment between instructors and students. [→ Rahman Alexander 2013](#rahman-alexander-2013)

## Evidence

### Rahman Alexander 2013

Rahman Alexander, Jacker-Guhr Silke, Staufenbiel Ingmar, Meyer Karen, Zupanic Michaela, Hahnemann Merle, Lührs Anne-Katrin, Eberhard Jörg. (2013). Use of elaborate feedback and an audience-response-system in dental education. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung 30(3), Doc35. [doi:10.3205/zma000878](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000878)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' conclusion combining the null exam result with the positive evaluation survey results from their randomized study; the atmosphere benefit is an interpretation of evaluation and observation data, not an exam-measured effect.

> "Though no significant difference in the final exams of the study and the control group were found, the positive evaluation results of this study show that an audience response system helps to create a positive and interactive learning atmosphere between instructors and students."
