## Evidence

### Jiayi Zhang 2026

Jiayi Zhang, Conrad Borchers, Canwen Wang, Vishal Kumar, Leah Teffera, Bruce M. McLaren, and Ryan S. Baker. (2026). Understanding Gaming the System by Analyzing Self-Regulated Learning in Think-Aloud Protocols. LAK 2026. [doi:10.1145/3785022.3785025](https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785025)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression with random student intercept on 117 clips (39 gaming, 78 non-gaming) from ten think-aloud participants. Presence of Processing Information rose from 24% to 46% and Realizing Errors from 19% to 26% in gaming clips; "students were more likely to engage inProcessing Information(OR = 3.46, 𝑝=. 021)".

> "Logistic regression results indicated that students were more likely to engage inProcessing Information(OR = 3.46, 𝑝=. 021) andRealizing Errors(OR = 1.42,𝑝<.001) in clips where gaming was observed."
