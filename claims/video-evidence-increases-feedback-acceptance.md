## Evidence

### Stefan A. Schreder 2025

Stefan A. Schreder, Helen Jossberger, Hans Gruber. (2025). Mountain rescuers' experiences with video-assisted and verbal debriefings: a qualitative study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09716-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interview on acceptance of feedback. In VD, acceptance "depends on the type of facilitator", whereas video evidence made feedback undisputable; participants also saw video as a chance to verify their own view ("Let us look at it again").

> "However, if there is video evidence, you simply cannot argue about the feedback, no matter how unpleasant or friendly the way of giving the feedback was."

### Stefan A. Schreder 2025 (2)

Stefan A. Schreder, Helen Jossberger, Hans Gruber. (2025). Mountain rescuers' experiences with video-assisted and verbal debriefings: a qualitative study. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09716-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Facilitator interview on video evidence: FA 4 felt feedback was better accepted under VAD, likening video to a video-referee, while noting some participants explain themselves regardless of video and that acceptance still depends on the receiver.

> "I believe that the feedback is accepted better. It is a bit like being a video-referee."
