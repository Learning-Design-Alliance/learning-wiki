Evidence¶

Asmalı 2018¶

Mehmet Asmalı. (2018). Integrating Technology into ESP Classes: Use of Student Response System in English for Specific Purposes Instruction. Teaching English with Technology, 18(3), 86-104. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1186385

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test study with two intact classes (experimental n=19, control n=24) of tourism and hospitality students taught by the same instructor with the same materials; Mann-Whitney U test showed the experimental group's post-test was significantly higher, with "U = 142.000, p = .035". No effect size was printed.

"the results showed the post-test scores were significantly higher for the experimental group (Mdn = 63.15) than for the control group (Mdn = 46.05), U = 142.000, p = .035"
