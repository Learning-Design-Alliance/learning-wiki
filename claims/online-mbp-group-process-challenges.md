Survey comparison of online versus in-person teaching across several pedagogical dimensions (N = 106 for group process). The article reports "32.1% reported that this was somewhat more challenging" and 13.2% significantly more difficult; 35.1% felt less connected and 25.5% found conveying themes less easy.

> "When asked about their ability to attend to and facilitate group process, 32.1% reported that this was somewhat more challenging and a further 13.2% found this significantly more difficult when teaching online."
