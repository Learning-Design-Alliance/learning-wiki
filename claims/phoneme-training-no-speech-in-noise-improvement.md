## Evidence

### Ferguson M. A. 2014

Ferguson M. A., Henshaw H., Clark D. P. A., Moore D. R. (2014). Benefits of Phoneme Discrimination Training in a Randomized Controlled Trial of 50- to 74-Year-Olds With Mild Hearing Loss. Ear and Hearing. https://doi.org/10.1097/AUD.0000000000000020

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within the same RCT, speech-in-noise SRTs were measured at each visit for both groups. The results section reports no significant between-visit change for either group; equivalence was not tested. The authors suggest high stimulus redundancy may have reduced sensitivity.

> "There was no significant between-visit change in speech-in-noise test SRTs for either the IT group (t1–t2: F(2, 20) = 1.02, p = 0.38) or the DT group (t0–t1: F(2, 19) = 2.51, p = 0.11)"
