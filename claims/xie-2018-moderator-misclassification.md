# Misclassification of moderating variables in the Xie et al. (2018) meta-analysis decreases the trustworthiness of its moderator analysis

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Weissgerber and Reinhard (2017) were wrongly categorized as having no test expectancy, and Weltman and Eakin (2014) were wrongly categorized as 'no image'. [→ Sophia C. Weissgerber 2021](#sophia-c-weissgerber-2021)

## Evidence

### Sophia C. Weissgerber 2021

Sophia C. Weissgerber, Matthias Brunmair & Ralf Rummer. (2021). Null and Void? Errors in Meta-analysis on Perceptual Disfluency and Recommendations to Improve Meta-analytical Reproducibility. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09579-1

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Checking coding decisions against the primary studies' method sections revealed misclassifications: Weissgerber and Reinhard participants "definitely expected a performance test" at immediate posttest, and Weltman and Eakin's method section states both words and figures were used.

> "Given that Weissgerber and Reinhard (2017) found no immediate disfluency effect but a delayed one, while Weltman and Eakin ( 2014) found a disfluency advantage, this misclassification is problematic and decreases the trustworthiness of the moderator analysis."
