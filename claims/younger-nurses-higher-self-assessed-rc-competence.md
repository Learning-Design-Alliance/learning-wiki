## Evidence

### Lotvonen S 2026

Lotvonen S, Parkkila M, Tuomikoski A-M, Kääriäinen M, DiBiasi V, Platani E, Bruno I, Siira H. (2026). Self-Assessed Restorative Care Competence Among Nursing Professionals in Finland: A Cross-Sectional Study. Western Journal of Nursing Research. https://doi.org/10.1177/01939459261478193

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA on survey data from 438 nursing professionals. The article reports significant age associations with four subscales, e.g. "F[2, 142] = 23.2, P = .001, η2p = 0.01" for utilizing cooperation and technology in RC, and states "the best self-assessed competence on all 8 subscales was observed in nursing professionals under 30 years of age".

> "The age of the participants was statistically significantly associated with 4 of the 8 subscales: understanding the impact of values and social factors in RC (F[2, 143] = 4.98, P = .007, η2p = 0.02), understanding the basic principles of RC (F[2, 139] = 5.70, P = .004, η2p = 0.03), utilizing resource-oriented and empowering RC (F[2, 142] = 5.29, P = .005, η2p = 0.02), and utilizing cooperation and technology in RC (F[2, 142] = 23.2, P = .001, η2p = 0.01)."
