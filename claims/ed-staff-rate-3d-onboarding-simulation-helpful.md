Descriptive analysis of an anonymous questionnaire completed by 37 permanent ED staff testing the 3D simulation. "The topology of the ED received 90% (33/37) positive answers" and 81% (30/37) found it useful for new employees; no effect sizes were reported.

> "The topology of the ED received 90% (33/37) positive answers with the lowest SD, indicating strong unanimous agreement. Finally, for the question Do you believe this app is useful for new employees?, 81% (30/37) of respondents found the app useful for new employees"
