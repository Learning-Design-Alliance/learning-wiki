### Zafar Sobia 2026

Zafar Sobia, Cheong Luan Lin Jovita, Ler Vonn, Teh Anastasia Min, Abbott Paul. (2026). How Well Are Dental Students Equipped to Handle Traumatic Dental Injuries in Children?. Australian Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.70074

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 118 fourth-year dental students across two cohorts (2022 and 2023) at UQ, using a 44-item questionnaire based on IADT guidelines. The abstract reports that "Average knowledge was 59.6%, with better understanding of primary dentition management (76.5%) and poor knowledge of avulsion management (42.6%)."
