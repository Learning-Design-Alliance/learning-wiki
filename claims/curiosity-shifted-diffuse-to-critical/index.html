q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive pre/post comparison of emotional responses in the session surveys. The authors state the curiosity change "likely reflects not a loss of interest but a shift from diffuse curiosity to more concrete and critical engagement". No test or effect size was printed.

"we noticed some emotional change between pre- and post-survey results, e.g., curiosity (from 71%, 24 of 34 in pre-survey, to 50%, 11 of 22 in post-survey), which likely reflects not a loss of interest but a shift from diffuse curiosity to more concrete and critical engagement."
