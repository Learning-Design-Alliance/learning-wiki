`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Summary of findings from the 2007 AVID student survey administered in ninth and tenth grade AVID elective classrooms across 32 participating CPS AVID schools, with a 77 percent student response rate. The report states "77% of students in schools in the average category agreed or strongly agreed that they would recommend AVID to their friends" and 89% agreed AVID gives them needed support.

> "77% of students in schools in the average category agreed or strongly agreed that they would recommend AVID to their friends, including 25% who strongly agreed."
