## Evidence

### Zhang Simin 2026

Zhang Simin, Li Jiao, Deng Cui, Zheng Sheng. (2026). CBL combined with MDT mode in clinical teaching of liver cancer: a randomized controlled study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09212-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Anonymous dichotomous-response questionnaire developed for this study, pilot-tested with Cronbach's α = 0.86, administered to all 112 students with 100% response rate. All four dimensions showed significantly higher endorsement in the experimental group.

> "For learning interest stimulation, 94.6% of experimental group students responded affirmatively compared to 78.6% in the control group (χ²=6.42, P = 0.011). Clinical thinking cultivation was recognized by 96.4% versus 76.8% (χ²=9.52, P = 0.002). Team collaboration enhancement was reported by 92.9% versus 71.4% (χ²=9.05, P = 0.003). Communication skills improvement was acknowledged by 94.6% versus 69.6% (χ²=12.37, P < 0.001)."
