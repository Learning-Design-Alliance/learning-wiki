### Liu Yingmin 2023

Liu Yingmin, Qie Dandan, Wang Mengmeng, Li Yuyuan, Guo Dongmei, Chen Ximin, Li Li, Yu Honglian, Wang Jingjing. (2023). Application of role reversal and standardized patient simulation (SPS) in the training of new nurses. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04294-1

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, contrasting traditional one-way teaching, which they say causes "a low degree of knowledge retention after class", with role transformation requiring personalised role-playing per patient and disease. Not a measured outcome of this trial.
