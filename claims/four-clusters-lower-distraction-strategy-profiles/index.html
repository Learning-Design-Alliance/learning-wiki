Shi 2025¶

Shi, H., Bi, R., Lin, X., & Dai, Y. (2025). Decreasing Digital Distraction in College Students: Associated Online Learning Strategies Identified by Unsupervised Data Mining Approaches. https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/P85S3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Partitioning-around-medoids clustering of the 42 ARM rules (not of participants), with cluster number chosen via Elbow Method and Silhouette scores. Cluster sizes were 12, 10, 13, and 7 rules; all clusters underscored SRL strategies combined with engagement behaviors and competencies.

"The PAM cluster analysis classified the 42 identified rules from Step 2 into four clusters, each representing distinctive patterns of learning strategy combinations associated with lower distraction (see Figure 2)."

Related Claims¶

  • Demographic characteristics (gender, age, race, major) showed no significant association with lower digital distraction in the rule-mining profiles — related
  • In association-rule mining of survey data from 530 college students in online courses, goal setting, environment structuring and time management were the strategies most often associated with lower digital distraction — related
  • Peer-related help-seeking and learner-learner engagement strategies appear less often in profiles of students with lower digital distraction — related
  • Learner-instructor and learner-content engagement strategies are negatively associated with digital distraction in online courses — related
  • Technical confidence using digital learning tools is positively associated with lower distraction levels among online learners — related
  • The four profiles differ in academic self-efficacy, time/study management, and effort regulation, with HL highest, but not in help seeking — related
  • K-means clustering of LMS log data identified two distinct SRL profiles (high and low) among online students — related
