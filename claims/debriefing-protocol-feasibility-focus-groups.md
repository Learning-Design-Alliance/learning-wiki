## Evidence

### Bae Juyeon 2019

Bae Juyeon, Lee JuHee, Jang Yeonsoo, Lee Yoonju. (2019). Development of simulation education debriefing protocol with faculty guide for enhancement clinical reasoning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1633-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Applicability assessment with 7 senior undergraduate nursing students (originally 8, one withdrew) across three consecutive high-fidelity simulations, followed by focus group interviews analyzed into two main topics from 8 codes and 16 condensed meanings. Findings are self-reported perceptions; no effect size is printed.

> "Learners mentioned that their reasoning competency was improved and understood the overall flow of reasoning by applying the debriefing protocol."

### Bae Juyeon 2019 (2)

Bae Juyeon, Lee JuHee, Jang Yeonsoo, Lee Yoonju. (2019). Development of simulation education debriefing protocol with faculty guide for enhancement clinical reasoning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1633-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same focus group study: students reported ample time to present opinions, immediate feedback, and interactive opportunity during debriefing, described as "the self-directed learning environment through the debriefing protocol". Qualitative self-report; no effect size printed.

> "The participants also reported that they experienced the self-directed learning environment through the debriefing protocol."
