### Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon 2022

Barry Ciarrah-Jane Shannon, Davies Neil M., Morris Tim T. (2022). Investigating how the accuracy of teacher expectations of pupil performance relate to socioeconomic and genetic factors. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-11347-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression of teacher expectation accuracy on month of delivery (recoded with September as the first month of the UK school year) in the ALSPAC imputed sample. The article reports pupils born later "slightly underperformed compared to those who were born earlier in the year", with a consistent Key Stage 3 estimate of −0.013.

> "Pupils born later in the school year slightly underperformed compared to those who were born earlier in the year (− 0.011, 95% CI − 0.018, − 0.005)."
