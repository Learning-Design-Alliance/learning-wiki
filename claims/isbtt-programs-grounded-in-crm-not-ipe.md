## Evidence

### Bochatay N 2025

Bochatay N, Ju M, O'Brien BC, van Schaik SM. (2025). A Scoping Review of Interprofessional Simulation-Based Team Training Programs. Simulation in Healthcare. https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000792

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Scoping review of 58 articles on ISBTT programs; the review's charting of theoretical frameworks found CRM and TeamSTEPPS most cited, with "Fifteen articles (25.9%) did not refer to any framework." Only 12 articles framed ISBTT as IPE.

> "They often approached ISBTT from a patient safety perspective and typically implicitly or explicitly referred to conceptual frameworks such as CRM (18 articles, 31%) and TeamSTEPPS (14 articles, 24.1%) that guided the content of the reported ISBTT program."
