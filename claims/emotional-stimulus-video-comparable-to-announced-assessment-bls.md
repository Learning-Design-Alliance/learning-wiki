## Evidence

### Kuckuck Karl 2018

Kuckuck Karl, Schröder Hanna, Rossaint Rolf, Stieger Lina, Beckers Stefan K, Sopka Sasa. (2018). Comparison of a newly established emotional stimulus approach to a classical assessment-driven approach in BLS training: a randomised controlled trial. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-017705

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised controlled trial in one German academic university hospital; 104 emotional stimulus group and 120 assessment group participants analysed 1 week after a standardised 4-hour BLS course, reaching "comparable chest-compression performance without significant differences". Compression depth and rate were measured objectively on a reporting mannequin.

> "104 participants from EG and 120 from AG were analysed1 week after the intervention, where they reached comparable chest-compression performance without significant differences (CR P=0.49; CD P=0.28)."
