## Evidence

### Zahoor-UL-HAQ 2017

Zahoor-UL-HAQ, Khurram, B. A., & Bangash, A. K. (2017). Development of Speaking Skills through Activity Based Learning at the Elementary Level. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 69, 241-252. http://dx.doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2017.69.13

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test check in the equivalent group design: the two groups of 25 were formed on the basis of pre-test scores and were "similar with respect to previous knowledge of speaking skills on the pre-test". Low- and high-achiever subgroups were also non-significantly different on the pre-test (t = 0.949 and t = -0.5).

> "Table 1 indicates that the calculated result of t was 0.30 and the table value of t was 1.68. Results were tested at 0.05 (level of significance) and the degree of freedom was 48."
