## Evidence

### Kaiyue Wu 2025

Kaiyue Wu, Xinsheng Wang, Linbo Sun, Jiayi Feng, Changping Song. (2025). Nurse Competency in Providing Spiritual Care and Associated Influencing Factors: A Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70192

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 16 cross-sectional studies of working nurses measured with the SCCS, pooled with a random effects model because heterogeneity was high (I² = 99.7%, p < 0.01). The pooled score was "70.51 (95% CI: 70.23–70.79)". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The meta‐analysis revealed that the spiritual care ability results were heterogeneous (I 2 = 99.7%, p < 0.01), so using the random effects model, the pooled score was 70.51 (95% CI: 70.23–70.79), indicating that the spiritual care ability of nurses was in the middle range."
