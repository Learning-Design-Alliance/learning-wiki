`q2 i?` Across cross-sectional studies, higher empathy scores were associated with better mental health, lower burnout, and higher well-being among medical students. [→ Chen Hao 2024](#chen-hao-2024)

## Evidence

### Chen Hao 2024

Chen Hao, Xuan Hanwen, Cai Jinquan, Liu Meichen, Shi Lei. (2024). The impact of empathy on medical students: an integrative review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05448-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

A multi-institutional cross-sectional survey of 1098 medical students (Thomas et al., USA) measured empathy with IRI and burnout/well-being with validated tools; the review reports empathy was "negatively correlated with burnout and positively correlated with well-being," consistent with other included studies.

> "The results indicated that students' empathy scores derived from IRI were negatively correlated with burnout and positively correlated with well-being."
