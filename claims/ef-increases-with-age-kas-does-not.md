## Evidence

### Arvidsson C. 2022

Arvidsson C., Pagmar D., Uddén J. (2022). When did you stop speaking to yourself? Age-related differences in adolescents' world knowledge-based audience design. Royal Society Open Science 9(11): 220305. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.220305

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multivariate linear regression (Model 2) with age group predicting perseverative errors, non-perseverative errors and KAS; both EF measures differed by age while KAS did not (F1,54 = 1.3, p = 0.8). Descriptive means were 0.18 vs 0.14 perseverative and 0.12 vs 0.07 non-perseverative errors.

> "AGE GROUP was significant for both the EF scores (perseverative errors: F1,54 = 1.71, p < 0.05; non-perseverative errors: F1,54 = 9.55, p < 0.01), but not for KAS (F1,54 = 1.3, p = 0.8)."
