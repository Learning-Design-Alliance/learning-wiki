## Evidence

### Shima Salehi 2019

Shima Salehi, Eric Burkholder, Peter Lepage, Steven Pollock, and Carl Wieman. (2019). The impact of incoming preparation and demographics on performance in Physics I: a multi-institution comparison. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1905.00389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1905.00389)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Failure analysis at HSWC (2018) using the Table III best-fitting model coefficients to weight a summed preparation score, then comparing bottom-quartile exam placement probabilities (Fig. 2). The article reports the factor-of-five difference quoted here and notes the actual score distribution matched the model prediction.

> "Even though the model only explains 34% of the variance, this failure analysis shows that a student who comes in with preparation in the bottom quartile has about a factor of five higher probability of being in the bottom quartile of the grade distribution than a student who starts the course in the upper quartile of preparation."
