Evidence¶

Vega-Hernández 2018¶

Vega-Hernández, M.-C., Patino-Alonso, M.-C., & Galindo-Villardón, M.-P. (2018). Multivariate characterization of university students using the ICT for learning. Computers & Education, 121, 124–130. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.03.004

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey analysis of REATIC responses from 2117 University of Salamanca students across five knowledge areas. The article reports that these use items obtained "scores high, over 50% ( Enough and Much), in the five branches of knowledge".

"In terms of use of ICT, the predominant answers of the students were in the items “Basic programs such as word processor, spreadsheet, slide presentation ”, “Programs of personal interrelation ”, “Search engines in the network ”, “Online video portals ”, “Virtual libraries and encyclopedias ” and “Some web browsers ”, obtaining scores high, over 50% ( Enough and Much), in the five branches of knowledge."
