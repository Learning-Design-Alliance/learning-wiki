Baseline Pearson correlation (T1) between the Vienna Test System 2HAND mean track time T-score and laparoscopic hand–eye coordination was r = 0.341, a moderate positive association in the 33-student sample.

> "In addition, bimanual coordination skills on the 2HAND test correlated positively with HE (r = 0.341) (Table 7)."
