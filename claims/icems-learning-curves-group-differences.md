`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Friedman's test learning-curve analysis across five practice repetitions in the RCT, with Bonferroni-adjusted within-group comparisons; the quote prints the expert group's gains and the fifth-repetition drop. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "The expert instruction group achieved a steep performance improvement in the composite-score where they outperformed their baseline performance in the second, third, and fourth tasks; .252, 95% CI [.070 .434], p = .001, .213, 95% CI [.054 .372], p = .027, .235, 95% CI [.051 .418], p = .016, after which they reached a plateau. There was a decrease in the composite-score and no significant difference was found between the fifth task and the baseline, .138, 95% CI [.023 .253], p = .269."
