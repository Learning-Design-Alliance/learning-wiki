q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis grouping the included studies by the region of Turkey where they were conducted. The article reports significant heterogeneity by region and that the maximum and minimum effect sizes were obtained in the Aegean and Mediterranean region groups, respectively; I2 was 62.1759%. No standardized effect size is printed for this contrast.
