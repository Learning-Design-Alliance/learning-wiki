## Evidence

### Kohnle 2014

Kohnle, A., Baily, C., Campbell, A., Korolkova, N., and Paetkau, M. J. (2014). Enhancing student learning of two-level quantum systems with interactive simulations. [doi:10.1119/1.4913786](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4913786)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Surveys completed directly after working with a simulation in seven in-class trials at introductory and advanced levels, rating usefulness for improving understanding on a Likert scale. The article reports "the majority of students across both levels finding simulations useful in improving understanding".

> "On the whole these results are positive, with the majority of students across both levels finding simulations useful in improving understanding."

### Kohnle 2014 (2)

Kohnle, A., Baily, C., Campbell, A., Korolkova, N., and Paetkau, M. J. (2014). Enhancing student learning of two-level quantum systems with interactive simulations. [doi:10.1119/1.4913786](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4913786)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

End-of-course survey in the introductory Quantum Physics course asking how useful for learning quantum physics students had found the simulations used in the course. The article reports "40% of students stated “very useful” and 43% “useful”" and that no students stated the simulations were not useful.

> "40% of students stated “very useful” and 43% “useful”, and no students stated the simulations were not useful."
