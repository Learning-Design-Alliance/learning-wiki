Zeng Qin 2023¶

Zeng Qin, Wang Kai, Liu Wei-xin, Zeng Jiu-zhi, Li Xing-lan, Zhang Qing-feng, Ren Shang-qing, Xu Wen-ming. (2023). Efficacy of high-fidelity simulation in advanced life support training: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04654-x

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analysis of the skill meta-analysis by comparator type (Supplement 4). Against LF simulation the estimate was "SMD = 0.36; 95% CI: 0.16–0.57, P = 0.0004"; against traditional training "SMD = -0.08; 95% CI: −0.37–0.22, P = 0.62", i.e. no benefit.

"In the subgroup analysis, a moderate benefit was observed for HF simulation compared with LF simulation at the course conclusion (SMD = 0.36; 95% CI: 0.16–0.57, P = 0.0004, I2 = 0%).(Supplement 4) However, no improvement in skill performance was observed in the HF group compared with those who received traditional training (SMD = -0.08; 95% CI: −0.37–0.22, P = 0.62, I2 = 0%)."
