## Evidence

### Giles Westerfield 2015

Giles Westerfield, Antonija Mitrovic & Mark Billinghurst. (2015). Intelligent Augmented Reality Training for Motherboard Assembly. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25:157–172. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0032-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A post-study questionnaire with Likert-scale items covered tutor effectiveness, satisfaction with 3D AR content, and perceived effectiveness versus manuals and videos. The article reports "None of the ratings were significantly different between the two groups"; 81% rated agreement six or higher that the tutor taught the procedure.

> "The questionnaire responses were positive for both tutors (Table 2). The responses were on the Likert scale from 1 (not very much) to 7 (very much). None of the ratings were significantly different between the two groups."
