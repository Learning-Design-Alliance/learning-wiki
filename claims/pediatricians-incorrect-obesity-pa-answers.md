Table 3 results from the congress questionnaire: for weight-loss activity type, 53.4% (102/191) chose aerobic activities only versus 40.8% choosing the correct both; for most recommended activity, 43.5% (81/186) chose walking versus 50.0% choosing the correct swimming.

> "In both questions on physical activity to fight obesity in childhood and adolescence, at least half of pediatricians indicated incorrect alternatives. Many respondents would recommend the practice of isolated aerobic activities and walking as the most appropriate physical activity for obese young individuals (Table 3)."
