## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students who completed the self-assessment at both time points reported a statistically significant increase in total SDL competency from midpoint (M = 16.34) to endpoint (M = 17.61) of the CRE course, with a medium effect size (d = 0.44). [→ Beck Amanda P 2025](#beck-amanda-p-2025)

## Evidence

### Beck Amanda P 2025

Beck Amanda P. (2025). Assessment of first-year medical student perceptions in the development of self-directed learning skills in a single medical school course. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06890-9

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Paired-samples t-test on the 89 students who completed the five-item SDL self-assessment at both midpoint and endpoint of the CRE course. The article reports "a statistically significant increase from the self-assessed total score for competency" with a printed effect size of d = 0.44, described as a medium effect.

> "The results showed a statistically significant increase from the self-assessed total score for competency from the midpoint (M = 16.34) to the endpoint of the CRE course (M = 17.61), (t(88) = 4.121, p < 0.001)"
