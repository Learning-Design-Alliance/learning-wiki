The report cites Buly's (2005) in-depth literacy assessment study of fourth-grade American Indian students at a Washington reservation public school: students were "average and above" in phonemic awareness, word recognition, orally tested vocabulary, and reading rate "but low in comprehension." No effect size is printed.

"An in-depth study of literacy assessment involving fourth-grade American Indian students attending a public school on a reservation in the state of Washington showed that the majority of students were average and above in skills of phonemic awareness, word recognition, orally tested vocabulary, and reading rate but low in comprehension"
