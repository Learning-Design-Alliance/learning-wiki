## Evidence

### Chan Michael 2015

Chan Michael, Bax Nigel, Woodley Caroline, Jennings Michael, Nicolson Rod, Chan Philip. (2015). The first OSCE; does students’ experience of performing in public affect their results? BMC Medical Education, 15, 59. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0343-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Extreme groups comparison in the 265-student cohort comparing students with high verbal activity scores against zero scorers; the article reports "The effect size of verbal activities on OSCE results is calculated at 0.44", alongside a significant t-test (p < 0.05).

> "The effect size of verbal activities on OSCE results is calculated at 0.44. The corresponding effect size for motor activities is −0.11 (Figure 4)."
