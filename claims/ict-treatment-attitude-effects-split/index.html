Evidence¶

Emin Cener 2015¶

Emin Cener, Ismail Acun & Gokhan Demirhan. (2015). The Impact of ICT on Pupils' Achievement and Attitudes in Social Studies. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 6(1), 190-207. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105333

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent-samples t-test on the ICT attitude scale (Cronbach's alpha .97) with 35 pupils per group. Pre-test scores showed no significant difference (t = .772, p = .443); post-test favored the experimental group (t = 1.996, p = .050).

"However, there is a significant difference between experimental and control groups' ICT attitudes post -test scores in favor of experimental group. It seems that the treatment had a positive effect on pupils' attitudes towards ICT."

Emin Cener 2015 (2)¶

Emin Cener, Ismail Acun & Gokhan Demirhan. (2015). The Impact of ICT on Pupils' Achievement and Attitudes in Social Studies. Journal of Social Studies Education Research, 6(1), 190-207. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105333

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Independent-samples t-tests on the social studies attitude scale (Cronbach's alpha .89). The control group held significantly more favorable attitudes at both pre-test (t = -2.347, p = .022) and post-test (t = -2.311, p = .024), and both groups' scores declined from pre- to post-test.

"Post-test scores of both groups were lower than their pre -test scores on social studies attitudes scale. This means the treatment did not improve experimental group's attitudes towards social studies education."
