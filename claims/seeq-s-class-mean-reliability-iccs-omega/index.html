Reliability reporting from the Method section of the large-scale validation sample of Australian secondary schools, in which "ICC2 values ranged from .872 to .900, demonstrating strong reliability for class- average estimates". Omega reliabilities were .90–.97 for SEEQ-S and .77–.86 for TEEQ-S; no effect sizes are printed.

"In our sample, ICC1 values ranged from .254 to .311, and ICC2 values ranged from .872 to .900, demonstrating strong reliability for class- average estimates. Internal consistency was also high, with omega reliability coefficients ranging from .90 to .97 for SEEQ-S and .77 to .86 for TEEQ-S."
