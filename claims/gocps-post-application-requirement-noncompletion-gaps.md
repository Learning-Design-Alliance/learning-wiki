# Many applicants failed to complete post-application admission requirements, with Black and Latino students least likely to complete them

## Evidence

### Barrow 2019

Barrow, L., & Sartain, L. (2019). GoCPS: A first look at ninth-grade applications, offers, and enrollment. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of post-application requirement completion (auditions, interviews, information sessions) in GoCPS data. Overall, 52 percent of applicants applied to at least one program with screens and 54 percent of them did not complete at least one; 64 percent of applying Tier 1 students missed at least one screen.

> "over half (52 percent) Latino students and nearly three in four (71 percent) Black students did not complete at least one screen, making them ineligible for an offer at that program."
