Error scores on the Junior Hayling test were compared in the randomized trial (n = 40). The two groups had equivalent error scores in the automatic condition (part A; F (1, 38) = 0.081, p > 0.05, ἠ2 = 0.002), but "a significant effect of training occurred in the experimental group in part B of the test" with a printed effect size of ἠ2 = 0.673, classified by the article as a large effect.
