## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The experimental e-portfolio group showed significant differences in writing performance while the control group did not. [→ Alshahrani and Windeatt 2012](#alshahrani-and-windeatt-2012)

## Evidence

### Alshahrani and Windeatt 2012

Ali Alshahrani and Scott Windeatt. (2012). Using an e-Portfolio System to Improve the Academic Writing Performance of ESL Students. EUROCALL Conference, Gothenburg, Sweden, Proceedings. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED574901

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of 46 ESL students divided into a conventional group and an e-portfolio group, with a pre-test showing no significant differences between the groups. The article reports "significant differences were found in the experimental group’s writing performance"; no effect size value is printed.

> "The post-intervention test results indicated no significant improvement among the control group’s motivational constructs and performance in writing, but significant differences were found in the experimental group’s writing performance"
