McKenna 2022¶

McKenna, J., Russo, S., Sutherland, H., Boal, A., & Petrosino, A. (2022). States’ school safety centers: A brief look into history, characteristics, and activities. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621272

Descriptive findings from an online survey of 51 identified state key informants conducted May–June 2021, with 43 respondents (about 84 percent response rate). The article reports "Over 65 percent of states responding to the survey reported having an SSSC" and that over 96 percent of those centers were still operational.

"Over 65 percent of states responding to the survey reported having an SSSC, with most of these centers serving their entire state"

  • Nearly all responding SSSCs conducted trainings in 2021, with reporting centers documenting over a thousand training events reaching tens of thousands of participants — related
  • Most SSSCs rely on two or three funding sources, with state-appropriated funding and federal grants the most common — related
