q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of federal National Postsecondary Student Aid Study (NPSAS:UG) data reported in the section on Black Parent PLUS loan borrowers. The brief reports that "took up, on average, 25.3% of a typical Black family’s income, versus 14.2%" for White families, while Black parents typically borrow lower dollar amounts than White, Asian, and multiracial parents.

"Parent PLUS loans awarded during the 2015-16 academic year took up, on average, 25.3% of a typical Black family’s income, versus 14.2% of a typical White family’s income"
