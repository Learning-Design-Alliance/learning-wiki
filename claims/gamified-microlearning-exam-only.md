### Mokhtari Saba 2026

Mokhtari Saba, Mokhtari Asieh, Shariat Seyed Vahid, Nazeri Astaneh Ali, Shalbafan Mohammadreza. (2026). Innovative technologies in psychiatry education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09573-1

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Quasi-experimental study of 72 fifth- or sixth-year medical students (27 app, 35 control) during a four-week psychiatry rotation using a gamified microlearning app with short modules, quizzes, and challenges. The review reports a written exam advantage (p = 0.031) but no difference in global clinical assessment.

> "At the end of the rotation, the intervention group had significantly higher scores on the final written exam (p = 0.031). However, global clinical performance assessments did not differ significantly between the two groups. Participants’ feedback was mainly positive regarding content quality and educational value."
