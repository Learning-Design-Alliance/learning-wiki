### Karoui Haykel 2025

Karoui Haykel, Rwandarwacu Victor P., Niyonzima Jonathan, Makuza Antoinette, Nkuranga John B., D'Acremont Valérie, Kulinkina Alexandra V. (2025). Identifying clinical skill gaps of healthcare workers using a digital clinical decision support algorithm during outpatient pediatric consultations in primary health centers in Rwanda. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0318284

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison within the prospective observational study of 188 consultations: respiratory rate measured with the RRate Android app versus a manual timer. The article reports the RRate app "resulted in better quality assessments, with 29 out of 39 measurements (74%) being sufficient compared to 18 out of 39 (46%) for the timer". Percentages are descriptive; no statistical test or effect size is printed.

> "the RRate app was used as frequently as the timer (39 times each), but the former resulted in better quality assessments, with 29 out of 39 measurements (74%) being sufficient compared to 18 out of 39 (46%) for the timer."
