### Baker 2016

Baker, R. (2016). The Effects of Structured Transfer Pathways in Community Colleges. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373716651491

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

DDD analysis of student-level transcript data from two community colleges in Northern California, examining the average number of credits accumulated before graduation for students graduating from treated departments. The author describes this as a direct test of the policy's efficiency goal.

> "There is suggestive, though not conclusive, evidence that the policy induced students to accumulate fewer credits over the course of their program. The results indicate that students earned more than 10 fewer credits, which equates to two fewer classes."
