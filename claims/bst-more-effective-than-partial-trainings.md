# Complete behavioral skills training is statistically significantly more effective than trainings using only some BST components for DTT implementation fidelity

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The moderation effect of complete BST was 12.69%; participants who received BST averaged 96.06% DTT implementation, significantly higher than those receiving one, two, or three components. [→ Joelle Fingerhut and Mariola Moeyaert 2022](#joelle-fingerhut-and-mariola-moeyaert-2022)

## Evidence

### Joelle Fingerhut and Mariola Moeyaert 2022

Joelle Fingerhut and Mariola Moeyaert. (2022). Training Individuals to Implement Discrete Trials with Fidelity: A Meta-Analysis. Focus on Autism and Other Developmental Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/10883576221081076

`q3 · i?`

HLM moderator analysis (Model 1) across the 46-study SCED meta-analysis compared complete BST with partial trainings. The article reports the moderation effect as "12.69%, γ̂200 = 12.69, t(3692) = 10.69, p < .0125" with BST recipients at 96.06% fidelity.

> "Results showed that the moderation effect of BST was 12.69%, γ̂200 = 12.69, t(3692) = 10.69, p < .0125. Participants who received BST had an average estimated DTT implementation score of 96.06%"
