Unlu, F., Layzer, C., Clements, D., Sarama, J., & Cook, D. (2013). Approaches to Incorporating Late Pretests in Experiments: Evaluation of Two Early Mathematics and Self-Regulation Interventions. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2013f/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Impact estimates on pretest measures from a three-armed cluster RCT in three Southern California districts with 807 students in 84 pre-K classrooms. The article reports "0.24 standard deviations (sds)" on Head-Toes-Knees-Shoulders and 0.17 sds on TEAM.

> "Column 1 suggests that the early impacts of BB were sizeable: 0.24 standard deviations (sds) and statistically significant on Head-Toes-Knees-Shoulders score and 0.17 sds on TEAM score."
