> "We find that the antecedent constructs explain 53% of the variance in 'behaviour intention'. At an intermediate stage of the model, 68% of the variance in attitude, 49% of the variance in 'enjoyment', 40% of the variance in 'perceived usefulness' and 37% of the variance in 'perceived ease of use' are explained in a similar way."
