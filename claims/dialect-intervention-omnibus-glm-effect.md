## Evidence

### Thomas-Tate et al. 2013

Shurita Thomas-Tate, Carol McDonald Connor, & Lakeisha Johnson. (2013). Design Experiments: Developing and Testing an Intervention for Elementary School-age Students who use Non-mainstream American English Dialects. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563056

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized design experiment (n = 116) analyzed with general linear multivariate models across three outcomes: editing-task percentage correct, DELV-S DVAR percent, and frequency of target Home English features in writing. The article reports a significant multivariate effect, "Wilkes Lambda = .878". No effect size is printed.

> "Analysis results revealed significant treatment group differences when considering performance on all three outcomes [Wilkes Lambda = .878, F(6, 222) = 928.53, p = .024] with students in the editing + dialect awareness intervention utilizing more School English and less Home English compared to students in the other groups"
