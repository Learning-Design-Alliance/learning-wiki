## Evidence

### Johnsson V 2026

Johnsson V, Petersen OB, Svendsen MBS, et al. (2026). To guide or not to guide: A randomized study on the use of needle guide for chorionic villus sampling training. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.70108

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 101 ultrasound novices (95 completed) allocated 1:1:1 to context variation, needle-guided, or freehand training; transfer performance on a CVS manikin was rated by blinded experts. The linear mixed-effects model found "no significant main effect of the intervention group" (F[2, 92.0] = 0.06, p = 0.94).

> "Analysis of mean performance scores revealed no significant main effect of the intervention group during training (F[2, 92.0] = 0.06, p = 0.94)."
