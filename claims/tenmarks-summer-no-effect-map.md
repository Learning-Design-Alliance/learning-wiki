# A matched-comparison analysis found no effect of the TenMarks summer program on NWEA MAP math scores

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` TenMarks participants showed no effect on NWEA MAP Math scores pre to post compared with a matched control group who did not use the program. [→ Christina Luke 2016](#christina-luke-2016)

## Evidence

### Christina Luke 2016

Christina Luke, Christa M. Simone, Aubrey T. Francisco. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Summer Ed-Tech Programs. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8017846c-4099-4af3-92e4-462cd5d6262d

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kettle Moraine matched-comparison analysis: 139 pilot participants versus a demographically similar control group, with a linear model controlling for baseline score, grade, gender, race, and free/reduced-price lunch status. No effect was detected; no effect size is printed.

> "We found no effect of TenMarks on NWEA MAP Math scores pre and post compared to a control group who did not use the program.  Because of limited student engagement, results should be treated with caution."

### Christina Luke 2016 (2)

Christina Luke, Christa M. Simone, Aubrey T. Francisco. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Summer Ed-Tech Programs. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8017846c-4099-4af3-92e4-462cd5d6262d

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Table 3 prints pilot participants' mean NWEA MAP scores of 233.3 (SD 14.8) at baseline in May and 229.1 (SD 16.0) at follow-up in August, alongside control-group means of 237.1 and 234.8.

> "Mean Scores- Pilot Participants 139 233.3 (14.8) 229.1 (16.0)"
