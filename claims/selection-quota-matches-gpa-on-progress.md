# Despite inferior pre-university GPAs, selection-quota students did not differ significantly from GPA-quota students on exam attempts, temporal continuity or passing rates

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Mommert A 2020

Mommert A, Wagner J, Jünger J, Westermann J. (2020). Exam performance of different admission quotas in the first part of the state examination in medicine: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02069-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null contrasts from the same cross-sectional quota comparison (N = 655): descriptive pu-GPA means were 1.02 (SD 0.05) for GPA quota and 1.46 (SD 0.24) for selection quota, yet no significant difference emerged on continuity or passing. Equivalence was not formally tested beyond the post-hoc contrasts.

> "The inferior pu-GPAs in the selection quota compared to the GPA quota do not give rise to significant differences in temporal continuity or passing rates between these student groups."
