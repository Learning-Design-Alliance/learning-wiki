## Subclaims
`q3 i0` During the 2020 lockdown, Dutch primary students in grades 4 to 7 lost on average 3.16 percentile points (0.08 SD) on a composite of math, spelling, and reading, compared with progress in the same period of the three previous years. [→ Engzell P. 2021](#engzell-p-2021)

## Evidence

### Engzell P. 2021

Engzell P., Frey A., Verhagen M. D. (2021). Learning loss due to school closures during the COVID-19 pandemic. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2022376118

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-differences analysis of biannual national test scores (n ≈ 350,000 students aged 8 to 11) comparing progress between the 2020 midyear and end-of-year tests to the same interval in 2017 to 2019, adjusting for a linear year trend and time between tests. The study finds students "lost on average 3.16 percentile points in the national distribution, equivalent to 0.08 standard deviations (SD)" — a printed effect size of 0.08 SD, below d = 0.2.
