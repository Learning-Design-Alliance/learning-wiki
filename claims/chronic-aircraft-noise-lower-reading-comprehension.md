# Chronic aircraft noise exposure is associated with lower reading comprehension in children, with a linear exposure–effect relationship

## Evidence

### Klatte M 2013

Klatte M, Bergström K and Lachmann T (2013) Does noise affect learning? A short review on noise effects on cognitive performance in children. Front. Psychol. 4:578. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00578

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports the cross-sectional RANCH study (N = 2844) in the UK, the Netherlands, and Spain, with noise exposure as a continuous variable and SES controlled: "a linear exposure–effect relationship between aircraft noise and decreasing reading comprehension".

> "With SES being controlled, the authors found no effect of aircraft noise exposure on sustained attention , working memory, and delayed recall of orally presented stories, but a linear exposure–effect relationship between aircraft noise and decreasing reading comprehension."

### Klatte M 2013 (2)

Klatte M, Bergström K and Lachmann T (2013) Does noise affect learning? A short review on noise effects on cognitive performance in children. Front. Psychol. 4:578. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00578

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's synthesis of quasi-experimental chronic-exposure studies: aircraft noise effects on attention and memory were mixed, "but exposure to aircraft noise was consistently associated with lower reading per- formance".

> "chronic effects on children’s attention (Stansfeld et al., 2005; van Kempen et al., 2010; Belojevic et al., 2012 ) and mem- ory ( Haines et al., 2001; Matheson et al., 2010 ), but exposure to aircraft noise was consistently associated with lower reading per- formance"
