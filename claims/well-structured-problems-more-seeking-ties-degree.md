## Evidence

### Javier Pulgar 2019

Javier Pulgar, Carlos Rios and Cristian Candia. (2019). Physics problems and instructional strategies for developing social networks in university classrooms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1904.02840](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1904.02840)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

OLS regression model 3 (Table 1) on log degree centrality, with Class 2's coefficient at −0.414*** (p<0.01), comparing a class alternating well- and ill-structured problems against the lecture-based well-structured class. The negative coefficient "shows that the number of social ties measured are higher on the lecture-based Class 1."

> "Diﬀerences on model 3 for logarithm degree centrality between Class 2 and 1 are also signiﬁcant. Again, the negative regression coeﬃcient shows that the number of social ties measured are higher on the lecture-based Class 1."
