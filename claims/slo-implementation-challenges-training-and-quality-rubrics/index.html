Slotnik 2014¶

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2014). Real progress in Maryland: Student learning objectives and teacher and principal evaluation. WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center. http://marylandpublicschools.org/MSDE/programs/tpe/index.html

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative interview and focus-group findings on SLO implementation challenges. The report states "Many interviewees believe that teachers receive insufficient training on how to understand and develop an SLO," that "Most interviewees grapple with understanding what constitutes a high quality SLO," and that "Very few interviewees report having such a rubric" despite wanting a quality rating rubric.
