## Evidence

### Zand Vahid 2026

Zand Vahid, Bazi Ramin, Keshmiri Fatemeh. (2026). Game-based blended learning versus traditional lecture for evidence-based medicine (EBM) competencies in undergraduate medical students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09628-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests within each group showed significant pre-to-post gains. In the intervention group, "the mean score for EBM competency increased significantly from 3.55 (SD = 1.71) before the intervention to 13.11 (SD = 2.24)"; the control group rose from 4.12 (SD = 2.20) to 10.12 (SD = 2.23), both p < 0.001.
