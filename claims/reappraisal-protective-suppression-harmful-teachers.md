# Cognitive reappraisal protects teachers from emotional exhaustion and burnout, while expressive suppression and surface acting are negatively linked to teaching effectiveness

### Elena Savina 2025

Elena Savina, Caroline Fulton, Christina Beaton. (2025). Teacher Emotional Competence: A Conceptual Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10018-2

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The review reports, citing Donker et al. (2020) and Doyle et al. (2024), that "Teachers' use of cognitive reappraisal ... was found adaptive as it can protect teachers from emotional exhaustion and burnout." Reappraisal is also linked to more effective responding to students' negative emotions. No effect size is printed.

> "Teachers' use of cognitive reappraisal (i.e., cognitive change in Gross' model) was found adaptive as it can protect teachers from emotional exhaustion and burnout (Donker et al., 2020; Doyle et al., 2024)."

### Elena Savina 2025 (2)

Elena Savina, Caroline Fulton, Christina Beaton. (2025). Teacher Emotional Competence: A Conceptual Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10018-2

The review attributes to Aldrup et al. (2024) that "surface acting and suppressing the expression of emotions are negatively linked to teaching effectiveness," and cites Braun et al. > "Aldrup and colleagues' (2024) review found that surface acting and suppressing the expression of emotions are negatively linked to teaching effectiveness."
