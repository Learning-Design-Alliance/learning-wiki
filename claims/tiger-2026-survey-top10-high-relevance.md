An online LimeSurvey questionnaire asked participants to rate the relevance of all 43 core competency areas on a 0–100% scale for each of five professional roles. The authors report that ratings "reflected generally high importance ratings for health informatics across the five roles."

> "All top 10 core competency areas received relevance ratings exceeding 79% with means ranging from 83.4 to 87.9% (Table 2). These values reflected generally high importance ratings for health informatics across the five roles."
