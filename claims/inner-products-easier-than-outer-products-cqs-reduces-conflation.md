## Evidence

### Peter Hu 2023

Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, and Chandralekha Singh (2023). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with basics and change of basis for two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/acf5b3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/acf5b3)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of pre-test responses across all three cohorts on questions 4 and 5. The article states "inner products appear to be easier to grasp than outer products" and that many students repeated the inner product calculation; the common scalar-instead-of-matrix error was largely corrected on the post-test.

> "Overall, judging by the pre -test scores, inner products appear to be easier to grasp than outer products, which is reasonable partly because they learn about scalar products but not outer products in introductory courses."
