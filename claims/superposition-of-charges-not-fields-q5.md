Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2015). Validation and analysis of the coupled multiple response Colorado upper-division electrostatics (CUE) diagnostic. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020130)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Analysis of reasoning selections among the 191 students who selected Superposition on Q5 (Fig. 5b). The authors note this distinction between superposition of charges rather than fields was also observed in previous research on the free-response CUE, and that it underscores the importance of asking students to express their reasoning to avoid misinterpreting responses.

> "just under a ﬁfth of students who selected Superposition (18%, N=34
of 191) also selected one or both of reasoning elements 'd' and 'f', suggesting that these students were thinking only about superposition of charges (i.e., Qlarge −Qsmall) rather than ﬁelds (i.e., Elarge −Esmall)."
