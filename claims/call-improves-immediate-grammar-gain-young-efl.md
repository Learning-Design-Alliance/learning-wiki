## Evidence

### Karim Sadeghi & Masoumeh Dousti 2013

Karim Sadeghi & Masoumeh Dousti. (2013). The Effect of Length of Exposure to CALL Technology on Young Iranian EFL Learners' Grammar Gain. English Language Teaching, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n2p14

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with three intact classes of 15 female learners each (control, two CALL experimental groups) after 12 treatment sessions. One way ANOVA on immediate post-test scores found "significant difference at the p < .05 level" with F (2, 42) = 10.36, p = .0.

> "there is significant difference at the p < .05 level in grammar post-test scores for the three groups: F (2, 42) = 10.36, p = .0"

### Karim Sadeghi & Masoumeh Dousti 2013 (2)

Karim Sadeghi & Masoumeh Dousti. (2013). The Effect of Length of Exposure to CALL Technology on Young Iranian EFL Learners' Grammar Gain. English Language Teaching, 6(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n2p14

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Tukey HSD post-hoc comparisons on the immediate post-test located the differences: control (M = 9.73, SD = 1.48) differed significantly from both experimental groups (M = 11.60 and M = 11.93); experimental 1 and 2 did not differ significantly (p = .79).

> "Post-hoc comparisons indicated that the mean score for control group ( M = 9.73, SD =1.48) was significantly different from first experimental group ( M = 11.60, SD =1.18)."
