## Evidence

### Hughes 2021

Hughes, A., Wade, K. H., Dickson, M., Rice, F., Davies, A., Davies, N. M. & Howe, L. D. (2021). Common health conditions in childhood and adolescence, school absence, and educational attainment: Mendelian randomization study. npj Science of Learning 6:1. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00080-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Genetic association analysis in ALSPAC adjusting for gender and 20 ancestry principal components (Table 4). The article reports the ADHD PGS predicted lower GCSE points but "not more absence" (−0.24%, CI −2.13, 1.69), and the PGS explained <1% of phenotype variance.

> "Each SD increase in the PGS for ADHD corresponded to a decrease of 2.70 (95% CI: −4.83, −0.58) GCSE points."
