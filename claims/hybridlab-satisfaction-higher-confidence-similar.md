## Evidence

### Mankute A 2022

Mankute A, Juozapaviciene L, Stucinskas J, Dambrauskas Z, Dobozinskas P, Sinz E, Rodgers DL, Giedraitis M, Vaitkaitis D. (2022). A novel algorithm-driven hybrid simulation learning method to improve acquisition of endotracheal intubation skills: a randomized controlled study. BMC Anesthesiology 22:42. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-021-01557-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-course survey completed by 58 of 77 participants (22 conventional, 36 hybrid) using 5-point Likert items; Table 4 prints p = 0.02 for the enjoyment question. No effect size is printed.

> "However, the satisfaction was significantly higher in the hybrid group as compared to conventional one (Table 4)."

### Mankute A 2022 (2)

Mankute A, Juozapaviciene L, Stucinskas J, Dambrauskas Z, Dobozinskas P, Sinz E, Rodgers DL, Giedraitis M, Vaitkaitis D. (2022). A novel algorithm-driven hybrid simulation learning method to improve acquisition of endotracheal intubation skills: a randomized controlled study. BMC Anesthesiology 22:42. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-021-01557-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same post-course survey; Table 4 prints p = 0.259 for the confidence question, indicating no statistically significant group difference. Equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "A survey of student confidence and satisfaction showed that students’ confidence to pass the final test did not differ significantly between the groups."
