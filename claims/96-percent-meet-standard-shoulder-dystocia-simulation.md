### Stieglitz Verena 2023

Stieglitz Verena, Buggenhagen Holger, Strelow Kai-Uwe, Schmidtmann Irene, Skala Christine, Kurz Sandra. (2023). Action competence in obstetric emergencies—can this be achieved via e-learning? Interprofessional blended learning module on diagnostics and emergency treatment of shoulder dystocia. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04335-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective monocentric study of 160 final-year medical students and 14 midwifery trainees assessed with an evaluation form based on ALSO recommendations. The article reports "96% of the study participants had performed in accordance with the requirements" and a mean score of 38.5 points.

> "Overall, 96% of the study participants had performed in accordance with the requirements (60% pass rate). The mean score of the performances achieved was 38.5 points."
