Double-dose algebra policy evaluation in the Chicago Public Schools 2003¶

Double-dose algebra policy evaluation in the Chicago Public Schools. (2003). Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/ujree20

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Regression discontinuity analysis of the Chicago double-dose algebra policy examined effects by students' incoming math skills. The article reports that students with very low initial abilities benefited less than students close to the national median.
