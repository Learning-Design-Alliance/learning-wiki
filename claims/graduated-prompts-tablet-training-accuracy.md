## Evidence

### Kirsten W. J. Touw 2019

Kirsten W. J. Touw, Bart Vogelaar, Merel Bakker, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2019). Using electronic technology in the dynamic testing of young primary school children: predicting school achievement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09655-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA in a pre-test-training-post-test control-group design with 164 grade-2 children (80 training, 84 control). The trained group's mean accuracy rose from 7.65 to 10.70 items while the control group moved from 7.50 to 7.87; the "significant Session x Condition effect" confirmed the differential gain.

> "children in the training and control condition differed in the level of this progression, as indicated by a significant Session x Condition effect (Wilks' λ = 0.76, F(1, 162) = 51.19, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.24)"
