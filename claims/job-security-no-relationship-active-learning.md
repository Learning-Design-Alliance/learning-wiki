## Subclaims

`q2 i0` Among instructors on a secure track, those who have and have not achieved increased security show negligible difference in lecture (g = 0.11). [→ Apkarian 2021 (2)](#apkarian-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Apkarian 2021

Apkarian, N., Henderson, C., Stains, M., Raker, J., Johnson, E., & Dancy, M. (2021). What really impacts the use of active learning in undergraduate STEM education? Results from a national survey of chemistry, mathematics, and physics instructors. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0247544. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247544

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

t-test comparing self-reported lecture percentage between instructors on and off a secure employment track in the national survey; the article reports a negligible effect, g = 0.03, and no evidence of difference.

> "A. All instructors, grouped by whether or not they are on a track leading to increased job security. There is no evidence of difference between these groups, t(984.7) = 0.71, p > 0.05, g = 0.03 (negligible)."

### Apkarian 2021 (2)

Apkarian, N., Henderson, C., Stains, M., Raker, J., Johnson, E., & Dancy, M. (2021). What really impacts the use of active learning in undergraduate STEM education? Results from a national survey of chemistry, mathematics, and physics instructors. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0247544. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247544

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

t-test among secure-track instructors comparing those who have versus have not achieved increased security; the printed effect is g = 0.11, which the article labels negligible despite p < 0.05.

> "B. Instructors on a secure track, grouped by whether or not they have achieved increased security. There is no evidence of a difference between these groups, t(1185.9) = 2.58, p < 0.05, g = 0.11 (negligible)."
