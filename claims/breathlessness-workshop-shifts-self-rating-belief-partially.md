## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Agreement that people with chronic breathlessness are able to rate their own breathlessness intensity on a 0-10 scale increased significantly (r = 0.5), but only a minority strongly agreed at either time point. [→ Johnston Kylie N. 2020](#johnston-kylie-n-2020)

## Evidence

### Johnston Kylie N. 2020

Johnston Kylie N., Young Mary, Kay Debra, Booth Sara, Spathis Anna, Williams Marie T. (2020). Attitude change and increased confidence with management of chronic breathlessness following a health professional training workshop: a survey evaluation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02006-7

`q2 · i3` · `design · r2`

Wilcoxon signed rank test on matched pairs (n = 37): z = 2.95, p = 0.003, r = 0.5. The article states that "at 81% with any level of agreement post-workshop (only 22% strong) this still remained a disputed concept".

> "Respondents significantly changed their belief toward agreement that people are able to rate their own degree of chronic breathlessness on a scale (effect size, r = 0.5); however only a minority strongly agreed with this statement at both time-points (pre-workshop 11%, post-workshop 22%)."
