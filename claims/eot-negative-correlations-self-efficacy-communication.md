### Yu Jiaying 2026 (2)

Yu Jiaying, Zhu Chengyu, Ren Yuwen, Fan Mingna & Yang Lili (2026). Alexithymia and clinical communication competence in nursing interns in China with the mediating role of self-efficacy. Scientific Reports 16, 10615. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44952-0

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same Spearman analysis of the 208-intern survey (Table 2), showing dimension-specific associations: these two subdomains correlated with EOT only, with r = −0.222 and r = −0.250, while correlations with DIF and DDF were not significant.

> "Recognizing patient problems and verifying feelings were negatively correlated specifically with EOT (r = − 0.222, p < 0.01; r = − 0.250, p < 0.01, respectively), but not with DIF or DDF (p > 0.05)."
