# Some content-based offloading profiles differed in prompting volume even though volume was not a clustering variable, suggesting what is offloaded constrains how much is offloaded

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Content-based profiles differed significantly in number of prompts and prompt pacing despite volume not being used for clustering, indicating a relationship between what is offloaded and how much is offloaded. [→ Poquet 2026](#poquet-2026)

## Evidence

### Poquet 2026

Poquet, O., Nanduri, M. S., Salinas Loyer, M. X., Stadler, M., Sailer, M., Jovanovic, J. (2026). Profiling Cognitive Offloading in LLM-mediated Synthesis Writing: Volume vs. Content. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10434

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the authors' discussion of the content-based clustering of 97 students' prompts, they note that "some of the resulting profiles differed in prompting volume", an emergent rather than imposed difference, and interpret it as what is offloaded constraining how much is offloaded.

> "Although volume was not a clustering variable in the content-based solution, some of the resulting profiles differed in prompting volume, suggesting that sometimes what is offloaded constrains how much is offloaded."
