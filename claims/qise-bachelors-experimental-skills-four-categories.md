### Shams El-Adawy 2026

Shams El-Adawy, A.R. Piña, Benjamin M. Zwickl, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2026). Experimental Skills for Undergraduate Career Preparation in Quantum Information Science and Engineering. [doi:10.1103/56hq-4pns](https://doi.org/10.1103/56hq-4pns)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative thematic analysis of 44 semi-structured interviews with employees and managers in US quantum companies, from which 24 bachelor's-level positions were analyzed. The study reports that interviewees' descriptions of experimental work were synthesized into the four named categories, articulated in learning goal language.

> "we characterize the experimental skills associated with positions described as requiring bachelor's-level preparation and thematically synthesize them into four categories: instrumentation, computation and data analysis, experimental and project design, and communication and collaboration"
