Evidence¶

Zhang Zhen; Fan Siyu; Qi Chunhui 2026¶

Zhang Zhen; Fan Siyu; Qi Chunhui. (2026). Teacher or Artificial Intelligence? The Effect of Decision-Making Agent on Junior High School Students’ Decision Acceptance: A Moderated Mediation Model. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071227

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

In the 2×2 factorial ANOVA on decision acceptance (N = 250), the main effect of explanatory feedback was significant, F(1,246) = 32.01, partial η2 = 0.11, with means of 4.42 versus 3.52. A parallel main effect on perceived fairness was also significant (partial η2 = 0.07).

"A significant main effect of explanatory feedback was also observed, F(1,246) = 32.01, p < 0.001, partial η2= 0.11. Decision acceptance was significantly higher for participants receiving explanatory feedback (M = 4.42, SD = 1.41) than for those without explanations (M = 3.52, SD = 1.30), p < 0.001."

Zhang Zhen; Fan Siyu; Qi Chunhui 2026 (2)¶

Zhang Zhen; Fan Siyu; Qi Chunhui. (2026). Teacher or Artificial Intelligence? The Effect of Decision-Making Agent on Junior High School Students’ Decision Acceptance: A Moderated Mediation Model. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071227

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Conditional indirect effects from the PROCESS analysis (5000 bootstrap resamples) showed a significant indirect effect only for the explanatory subgroup (0.79, CI excluding zero); the non-explanatory subgroup's indirect effect (0.27) was non-significant, though both subgroups had significant direct effects.

"Regarding indirect effects, the indirect effect of the explanatory group was 0.79, with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval of [0.52, 1.09] that excluded zero. By contrast, the indirect effect of the non-explanatory group was 0.27, with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval of [−0.03, 0.59] that contained zero, indicating a non-significant indirect effect for this subgroup."
