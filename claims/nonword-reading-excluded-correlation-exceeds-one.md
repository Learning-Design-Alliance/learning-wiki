`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

LDSC pairwise genetic correlation analysis among the five reading-related traits and ADHD. The article attributes the estimates exceeding 1.0 to the unbounded LDSC estimator under sampling variation and insufficient effective sample size for nonword reading, and therefore excluded it from the SEM.

> "Since genetic correlations between nonword reading and word reading, as well as between nonword reading and spelling, exceeded 1.0, nonword reading was excluded from subsequent Genomic SEM analyses."
