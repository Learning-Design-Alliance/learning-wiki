# Integrative reading prompts improve the quantity of multiple document integration compared to detailed prompts

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants receiving integrative prompts constructed more integrative links in their summaries than those receiving detailed prompts. [→ Ziqian Wei 2025](#ziqian-wei-2025)

## Evidence

### Ziqian Wei 2025

Ziqian Wei, Shuting Yin, Roy B. Clariana, Xuqian Chen. (2025). The influence of pre-reading purpose and extra-textual networks with summary writing on multiple document concept network integration: a replication of Wei et al. (2024). Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10508-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Study 1 (N=102) randomly assigned participants to integrative or detailed prompts. The ANOVA showed a significant main effect of prompts on the proportion of integrative links, F(1,98) = 6.14, p = .015, η² = .051.

> "Participants in the integrative prompts condition (M = 0.22, SD = 0.15) constructed more integrative links in their summaries than the detailed prompts condition (M = 0.16, SD = 0.11) during the whole experiment."
