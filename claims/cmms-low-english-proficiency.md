## Evidence

### Javid 2014

Javid, C. Z. (2014). EMP Needs of Medical Undergraduates in a Saudi Context. Kashmir Journal of Language Research, 14(1), 89-110. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1410.6469](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1410.6469)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interviews with ten randomly selected content-subject faculty members (non-native English speakers from four nationalities); all ten stated dissatisfaction with students' proficiency level.

> ""Are you satisfied with the English proficiency level of your students?" was the next question in the interview protocol and all the faculty members unanimously stated that they were not satisfied with the proficiency level of the medical undergraduates at CMMS."

### Javid 2014 (2)

Javid, C. Z. (2014). EMP Needs of Medical Undergraduates in a Saudi Context. Kashmir Journal of Language Research, 14(1), 89-110. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1410.6469](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1410.6469)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Faculty rated students' skills on a 1-10 scale; Table 2 reports listening mean 2.7, speaking 2.4, reading 3.2, writing 2.3. All ratings fell at scale 4 or below on the 10-point scale.

> "The faculty members' rating reported that the proficiency level of the medical undergraduates at CMMS was quite low as detailed in table 2."
