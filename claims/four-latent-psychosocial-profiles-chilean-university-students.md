## Evidence

### Tornero Bernardita 2026

Tornero Bernardita, Cárcamo Marcela, Rengifo Manuel, Tricio Jorge, A. Rojas-Barahona Cristian, Gaete Jorge. (2026). Latent profiles of grit, self-control, belonging, and college motives: differential effects of self-control on academic performance among Chilean university students. BMC Psychology, 14, 937. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04650-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis conducted on 1,651 students at a private Chilean university using grit, self-control, belonging, and motives scales; fit indices (AIC, BIC, SABIC, entropy) supported "a four-profile solution as the best-fitting and most interpretable model".

> "Model fit indices supported a four-profile solution as the best-fitting and most interpretable model (see Table 2), with acceptable entropy (0.74), indicating adequate classification accuracy."

### Tornero Bernardita 2026 (2)

Tornero Bernardita, Cárcamo Marcela, Rengifo Manuel, Tricio Jorge, A. Rojas-Barahona Cristian, Gaete Jorge. (2026). Latent profiles of grit, self-control, belonging, and college motives: differential effects of self-control on academic performance among Chilean university students. BMC Psychology, 14, 937. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04650-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Profile characterization from estimated marginal means (Table 4, Fig. 1) in the same LPA; the authors report profiles were "primarily differentiated by self-control" and motivational variables, with grit and belonging showing limited variability.

> "Examination of the estimated marginal means revealed that profiles were primarily differentiated by self-control (both work and interpersonal dimensions) and motivational variables."
