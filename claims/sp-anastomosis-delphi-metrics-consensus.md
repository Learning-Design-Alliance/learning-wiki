`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Modified Delphi meeting with n = 14 panelists (12 urologists, 1 hepatobiliary surgeon, 1 veterinarian) voting via the Vevox platform; two initially proposed suturing errors were removed after failing the predefined ≥80% agreement threshold in the first round.

> "Delphi consensus achieved 100% agreement on the proposed performance metrics (n = 5 steps, n = 20 errors, n = 1 failure to progress and n = 4 critical errors [CE])."
