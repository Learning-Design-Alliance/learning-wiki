## Evidence

### Kleine-Borgmann Julian 2021

Kleine-Borgmann Julian, Schmidt Katharina, Billinger Marieke, Forkmann Katarina, Wiech Katja, Bingel Ulrike. (2021). Effects of open-label placebos on test performance and psychological well-being in healthy medical students: a randomized controlled trial. Scientific Reports 11:2130. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-81502-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Randomized controlled trial comparing exam scores (percentage of correct answers on a standardized written midterm medical exam) between an OLP group (N = 79) and a no-treatment control group (N = 75) using a general linear model. The article reports "The comparison of exam scores between groups did not yield a significant result." An intention-to-treat analysis revealed comparable results.
