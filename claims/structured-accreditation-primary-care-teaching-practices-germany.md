### Gehrke-Beck Sabine 2025

Gehrke-Beck Sabine, Kitte Isabel, Streitlein-Böhme Irmgard, Deutsch Tobias, Demmer Iris, Gornostayeva Maryna, Jendyk Ralf. (2025). Accreditation and qualification of primary care teaching practices in Germany – a nationwide online survey of universities. GMS Journal for Medical Education 42(5): Doc56. https://journals.publisso.de/en/journals/jme/volume42/zma001780

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional nationwide online survey of teaching practice coordinators using a self-developed questionnaire, with descriptive analysis. Of 32 responding sites, 29 reported a standardized accreditation process; 16 used GHA/DEGAM/GMA criteria, and accreditation occurred via "personal visits to the medical practices (n=22), telephone calls (n=9) and/or video calls (n=10)".

> "A standardized accreditation process has been defined at 29 sites. At 16 sites, teaching practices are accredited according to criteria set by GHA, DEGAM and the Association for Medical Education (GMA) [4]. Accreditation takes place in the form of personal visits to the medical practices (n=22), telephone calls (n=9) and/or video calls (n=10)."
