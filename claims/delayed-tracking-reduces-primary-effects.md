## Evidence

### Antonie Knigge 2022

Antonie Knigge, Ineke Maas, Kim Stienstra, Eveline L. de Zeeuw and Dorret I. Boomsma. (2022). Delayed tracking and inequality of opportunity: Gene-environment interactions in educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 7:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00122-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate Cholesky ACE decomposition splitting attainment variance into components common to performance and unique to attainment, for the N = 8847 twin sample. The article reports "for a major part driven by a reduction in primary effects"; common shared-environmental variance is 0.57 squared for immediate versus 0.04 squared for delayed tracking.

> "the larger genetic and lower shared environmental effects associated with delayed tracking seem for a major part driven by a reduction in primary effects, and to a much lesser extent by a reduction in secondary effects."
