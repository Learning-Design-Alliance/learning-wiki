## Evidence

### Cremers 2016

Cremers, P. H. M., Wals, A. E. J., Wesselink, R., & Mulder, M. (2016). Utilization of design principles for hybrid learning configurations by interprofessional design teams. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9398-5

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire and oral evaluation of the intervention's form. Almost all respondents (29 out of 32) indicated they had read the guidebook, which was valued as preparation, common prior knowledge, and a reference book when developing the HLC.

> "According to most respondents, the examples of how design principles were manifested in the HLC 'Value in the Valley' helped them to get a better understanding of the design principles."
