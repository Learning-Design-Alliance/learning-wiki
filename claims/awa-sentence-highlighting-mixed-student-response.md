## Evidence

### Passonneau 2017

Passonneau, R. J., McNamara, D., Muresan, S., & Perin, D. (2017). Preface: Special Issue on Multidisciplinary Approaches to AI and Education for Reading and Writing. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0158-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editors report, citing Knight et al., an evaluation within the participatory design of the AWA web application for law students; the parser evaluation used "a small sample of sentences (N =90)". No effect size is printed.

> "On the question of whether an existing rhetorical parser from NLP can be tuned to automtically select important sentences, a small sample of sentences (N =90) judged by a human expert had very positive results."

### Passonneau 2017 (2)

Passonneau, R. J., McNamara, D., Muresan, S., & Perin, D. (2017). Preface: Special Issue on Multidisciplinary Approaches to AI and Education for Reading and Writing. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0158-8

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The editors report the student-facing evaluation of AWA's highlighting feature; "the results were mixed", which the editors read as a need for explanations alongside highlighting.

> "On the question of whether students would find a tool that highlights key sentences in their own writing helpful, the results were mixed, indicating a need to provide explanations to the students along with the highlighting."
