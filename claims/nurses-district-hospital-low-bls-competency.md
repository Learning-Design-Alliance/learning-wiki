## Evidence

### Mahembe GR 2025

Mahembe GR, Mash R. (2025). The competency of nurses in basic life support at district hospitals in Cape Town, South Africa. S Afr Fam Pract. https://doi.org/10.4102/safp.v67i1.6056

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 243 nurses at three district hospitals using an adapted, locally validated questionnaire. The results section reports that "only 3.0% achieved this threshold" on knowledge, while 75.5% scored above 50% on skills; no effect size is printed.

> "Overall, only 34.6% had a knowledge score of > 50%, while 75.5% achieved a skills score of > 50%. If 80% is taken as a cut-off for acceptable knowledge, then only 3.0% achieved this threshold."
