# Resuscitation skills deteriorate after one-day HBB workshops, and ongoing low-dose high-frequency practice maintains skills and is associated with reduced mortality

## Subclaims

`q2 i?` Ongoing low-dose high-frequency practice, including debriefings, case reviews, and brief bag-mask ventilation review, improved early neonatal mortality and decreased stillbirth rates in facility-based settings in Africa and Asia. [→ Kamath-Rayne BD 2018 (2)](#kamath-rayne-bd-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Kamath-Rayne BD 2018

Kamath-Rayne BD, Thukral A, Visick MK, Schoen E, Amick E, Deorari A, Cain CJ, Keenan WJ, Singhal N, Little GA, Niermeyer S. (2018). Helping Babies Breathe, Second Edition: A Model for Strengthening Educational Programs to Increase Global Newborn Survival. Global Health: Science and Practice. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-18-00147

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Narrative synthesis of facility-based studies of doctors, nurses, and midwives showing immediate knowledge and skill uptake after HBB workshops followed by skill deterioration over time.

> "published reports also mentioned the deterioration of skills after HBB workshops, which mirrored the experiences of other resuscitation training programs"

### Kamath-Rayne BD 2018 (2)

Kamath-Rayne BD, Thukral A, Visick MK, Schoen E, Amick E, Deorari A, Cain CJ, Keenan WJ, Singhal N, Little GA, Niermeyer S. (2018). Helping Babies Breathe, Second Edition: A Model for Strengthening Educational Programs to Increase Global Newborn Survival. Global Health: Science and Practice. https://doi.org/10.9745/GHSP-D-18-00147

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Synthesis of facility-based studies in Africa and Asia attributing mortality and stillbirth improvements to debriefings, case reviews, and brief low-dose high-frequency practice sessions such as at the start of a shift.

> "the incorporation of debriefings and case reviews after real-life delivery room situations, and a quick review of bag-mask ventilation in low-dose high-frequency sessions, for example, at the beginning of a shift, improved early neonatal mortality and decreased stillbirth rates in facility-based settings in Africa and Asia"
