# Learners' self-reported confidence in using LLMs rose after guidance and fell significantly after task use, measured within conditions from pre-test to post-test

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Across conditions in the controlled online experiment, self-reported confidence in using LLMs rose after receiving guidance (Mean 4.51 to 5.19) and significantly dropped after task use (Mean 4.57). [→ Harsh Kumar 2024](#harsh-kumar-2024)

## Evidence

### Harsh Kumar 2024

Harsh Kumar, Ilya Musabirov, Mohi Reza, Jiakai Shi, Xinyuan Wang, Joseph Jay Williams, Anastasia Kuzminykh, and Michael Liut. 2024. Impact of Guidance and Interaction Strategies for LLM Use on Learner Performance and Perception. Proc. ACM Hum.-Comput. Interact. 8, CSCW2, Article 499 (November 2024). https://doi.org/10.1145/3687038

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled randomized experiment on Prolific (N=356) with 2 (Prompted vs. Unprompted LLM) x 5 (guidance strategy) between-subjects design; confidence measured at three stages via Friedman test. The authors report "a significant drop (𝑝 < 0.001) after use."

> "we observed significant differences in self-reported confidence in use of LLMs (𝜒 2Friedman(2) = 72.82, 𝑛pairs = 356, 𝑝 < 0.001), with growth (𝑝 < 0.001) between initial confidence (Mean = 4.51, Me = 5, SD = 1.49) and immediately after getting guidance (Mean = 5.19, Me = 6, SD = 1.66), and a significant drop (𝑝 < 0.001) after use (Mean = 4.57, Me = 5, SD = 1.81)."
