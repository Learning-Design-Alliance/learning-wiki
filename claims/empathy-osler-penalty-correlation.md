## Evidence

### Wright Barry 2014

Wright Barry, McKendree Jean, Morgan Lewys, Allgar Victoria L, Brown Andrew. (2014). Examiner and simulated patient ratings of empathy in medical student final year clinical examination: are they useful? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-199

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational analysis relating OSCE empathy scores, rated by examiners and simulated patients, to OSLER penalty points awarded by "a completely different set of raters" for 133 students: "rs = −.38 (n = 133; p < .0001)". The article prints no standardised effect size beyond this correlation coefficient.

> "Using the penalty point scoring on the OSLERs, the correlation between the empathy scores accumulated on the OSCE stations and the OSLER penalty point scores was rs = −.38 (n = 133; p < .0001)."
