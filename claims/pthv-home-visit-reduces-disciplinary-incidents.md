## Evidence

### Academic Performance — Parent Teacher Home Visits 2021

Academic Performance — Parent Teacher Home Visits. (2021). Evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/parent-teacher-home-visits/

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

The Mathematica quasi-experimental study with a propensity-score matched comparison group reported that "a home visit before the start of the school year reduced the likelihood of a student having a disciplinary incident in that school year," with 9.27 percent of visited versus 12.22 percent of non-visited students affected.

> "During the school year following a home visit, 9.27 percent of visited students had a disciplinary incident compared with 12.22 percent of non-visited comparison students (effect size = +0.10)."
