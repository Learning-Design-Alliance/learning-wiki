### Holley 2017

Elizabeth A. Holley. (2017). Engaging Engineering Students in Geoscience Through Case Studies and Active Learning. Journal of Geoscience Education. https://doi.org/10.5408/16-145.1

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Among 30 students, 14 had prior engineering-related internships. Mann-Whitney U-tests found significant associations with interest in prior geoscience (Z = 2.65, p < 0.01) and perceived importance (Z = 2.02, p = 0.04). These are correlational associations, not causal effects.

> "Mann-Whitney U-tests showed significant positive associations ( p /C200.05) between internship experience and interest in the prior geoscience course and between internship experience and perceived importance of the prior geoscience course (Table IV)."
