In the intrinsic-value findings of this qualitative interview study, eight of thirteen AI-using students described reduced accomplishment. P10 stated "I definitely feel less accomplished after I use AI, just because I didn't do it myself."

"Most students who used AI (8 of 13) reported feeling less accomplished, and those who relied on it more heavily described a greater loss of satisfaction."
