### Whipple 1914

Whipple, G. M., & Curtis, J. N. (1914). Preliminary Investigation of Skimming in Reading. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=preliminary+investigation+of+skimming+in+reading

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Preliminary experiments with typewritten sheets; Table 3 records the decrease in time required per sheet when material was read with the definite knowledge that it would not have to be reproduced, with per-observer decreases such as Gou's 31.0 seconds (34.60%) for normal silent.

> "Knowledge that reproduction is to be demanded slows the rate of reading of all subjects by all methods."
