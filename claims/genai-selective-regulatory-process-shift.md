Mixed-design ANOVA on episode-level regulatory-process proportions in the same 24-group randomised experiment found a significant Condition×Process interaction, F(3.56,78.23) = 33.53, p < .001, with "Human–AI groups had higher proportions ofAffective Sup- port,Obstacle Detection,Shared Affective Regulation,andStrategic Action Direc- tion". For example, Affective Support mean proportion was 0.837 (Human–AI) vs 0.00 (Human–Human), t = −14.62; Shared Strategic Negotiation did not differ (t = −0.07).
