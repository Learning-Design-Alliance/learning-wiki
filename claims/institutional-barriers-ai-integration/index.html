Gibson 2025¶

Gibson, D., Azukas, M. E., & Knezek, G. (2025). Practitioner Beliefs and Behaviors in AI-Enhanced Education: DOT Framework Survey Evidence.

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Multi-select survey item on institutional challenges among 72 respondents: ethical/integrity concerns (n = 46, 64%), insufficient training (n = 44, 61%), lack of policies (n = 36, 50%), limited access to tools and infrastructure (n = 34, 47%), resistance (n = 32, 47%).

"Every listed challenge was selected by at least 38% of respondents, suggesting these are widespread, multi-faceted institutional barriers."
