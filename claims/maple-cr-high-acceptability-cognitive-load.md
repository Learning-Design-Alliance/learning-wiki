`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Postsession questionnaire adapted from a simulation-based training instrument, completed by all 26 intervention participants on a 5-point Likert scale. "means ranging from 4.19 to 4.62" across the 12 positively worded items.

> "Across the 12 positively worded items (Q1-Q12), full-sample ratings were consistently favorable, with means ranging from 4.19 to 4.62, indicating high satisfaction, sustained involvement, strong perceived support, and enhanced self-efficacy."

### Zhao Zhengqi 2026 (2)

> "the 3 perceived cognitive-load-related items (Q13-Q15), reflecting perceived mental effort, perceived information overload, and interface-related distraction, showed lower means, greater dispersion, and larger shifts after filtering"
