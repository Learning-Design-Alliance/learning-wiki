## Evidence

### van Lankveld W 2019

van Lankveld W, Maas M, van Wijchen J, Visser V, Staal JB. (2019). Self-regulated learning in physical therapy education: a non-randomized experimental study comparing self-directed and instruction-based learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1484-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Non-randomized experimental study comparing two pre-existing class groups of second-year physiotherapy students using pre-post-test assessments. The article reports knowledge test averages of "6.3 (SD 0.67) in the SDL compared to 6.2 (SD 0.71) in the IBL condition (T = 0.4, ns)" and performance averages of 6.75 versus 6.7 (T = 0.4, ns).

> "There was no statistical difference between average scores in both conditions on either knowledge or performance assessment at the end of year two. Average scores on the knowledge test was 6.3 (SD 0.67) in the SDL compared to 6.2 (SD 0.71) in the IBL condition (T = 0.4, ns)."
