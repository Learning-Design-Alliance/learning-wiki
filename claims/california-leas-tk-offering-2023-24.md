### Wang 2025

Wang, V., Melnick, H., Leung-Gagné, M., Parker, S., & Wechsler, M. (2025). California's universal prekindergarten implementation progress, 2023–24. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/600.551

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Statewide survey of 1,506 LEAs receiving UPK Planning and Implementation Grant funds, administered by the California Department of Education in 2024. The report states "almost all LEAs (94%) offered TK" and reports the all-sites percentage rose from 81% to 85%.

> "In the 2023–24 school year, almost all LEAs (94%) offered TK. Among LEAs with a TK program, 85% offered TK at all elementary sites, compared to 81% of LEAs in 2022–23."
