## Evidence

### Qiao J 2025

Qiao J, Bajpai M, Metcalf M, Yin R, Huang S, Wen C, Bai X, Li S, Wang S, Wang X, Yang D. (2025). Exploring the Application Capability of ChatGPT as an Instructor in Skills Education for Dental Medical Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/68538

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t test on a 10-question expert-validated multiple-choice knowledge test (scored 0-100) administered before the skill acquisition period. The null result served as a baseline comparability check; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "there was no statistically significant difference in the theoretical knowledge test scores between the 2 groups (t185=0.649, P=.52), suggesting that the participants’ knowledge levels before learning operations in the 2 groups were comparable"
