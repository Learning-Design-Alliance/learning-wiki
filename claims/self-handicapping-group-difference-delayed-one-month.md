# Between-group difference in self-handicapping after problem solving training emerged one month after the intervention (p = 0.014) but not immediately after it (p = 0.761)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Independent samples t-tests showed no significant difference between intervention and control groups immediately after the intervention (p = 0.761), but a significant difference one month after it (p = 0.014). [→ Zarshenas Ladan 2019](#zarshenas-ladan-2019)

## Evidence

### Zarshenas Ladan 2019

Zarshenas Ladan, Jahromi Leila Ashrafean, Jahromi Mohsen Faseleh, Manshadi Marieh Dehghan. (2019). Self–handicapping among nursing students: an interventional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1441-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent samples t-test comparing the intervention and control groups' mean self-handicapping scores at two post-intervention time points. The article reports the effect appeared "one month after the intervention, but not immediately after that"; no effect size is printed.

> "The results of independent samples t-test revealed no significant difference between the two groups concerning the mean score of self-handicapping immediately after the intervention (p = 0.761). However, a significant difference was observed between the two groups in this regard one month after the intervention (p = 0.014)."
