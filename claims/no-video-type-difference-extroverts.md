## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The difference in posttest vocabulary scores between the authentic-video extrovert group and the instructional-video extrovert group was not statistically significant. [→ Isazadeh 2016](#isazadeh-2016)

## Evidence

### Isazadeh 2016

Isazadeh, P., Makui, S. M. Z., & Ansarian, L. (2016). Effect of Instructional vs. Authentic Video Materials on Introvert and Extrovert Iranian EFL Learners' Vocabulary Learning. International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 4(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.4n.4p.1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-test comparing group 1 (authentic video / extrovert) with group 3 (instructional video / extrovert) on the posttest. The groups "performed more or less the same" with p = .513; equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "instructional experimental group (group 3) ( M = 20.44, SD = 3.097) and authentic-video extrovert group (group 1) ( M = 19.88, SD = 2.906) performed more or less the same and mean difference between the two groups was not statistically significant, t (48) = 6.59, p = .513 > .05"
