Evidence¶

Bobos Pavlos 2021¶

Bobos Pavlos, Pouliopoulou Dimitra V., Harriss Alexandra, Sadi Jackie, Rushton Alison, MacDermid Joy C. (2021). A systematic review and meta-analysis of measurement properties of objective structured clinical examinations used in physical therapy licensure and a structured review of licensure practices in countries with well-developed regulation systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255696

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random effects meta-analysis of four included studies reporting Cronbach's alpha for OSCE station scores in physical therapy students. The pooled estimate was "0.55, (95% CI: 0.41, 0.67)" with moderate heterogeneity I2 = 65.5%. No standardised effect size was printed for this finding.

"Based on the available data, we found a pooled estimate of Cronbach’s alpha between OSCE station scores of 0.55, (95% CI: 0.41, 0.67) with moderate statistical heterogeneity, I2 = 65.5% (Fig 2)."

Bobos Pavlos 2021 (2)¶

Bobos Pavlos, Pouliopoulou Dimitra V., Harriss Alexandra, Sadi Jackie, Rushton Alison, MacDermid Joy C. (2021). A systematic review and meta-analysis of measurement properties of objective structured clinical examinations used in physical therapy licensure and a structured review of licensure practices in countries with well-developed regulation systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0255696

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Univariate meta-regression investigating sources of the moderate heterogeneity in alpha estimates. The field of OSCE was "statistically significant p<0.0001" with R2 = 97.85% of between-study variance explained. No standardised effect size was printed.

"The regression coefficient for the field of OSCE was statistically significant p<0.0001, (0.25, 95% CI: 0.10, 0.39) with 98% of between-study variance explained (R2 = 97.85%)."
