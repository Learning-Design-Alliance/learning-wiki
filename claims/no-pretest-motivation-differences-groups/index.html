Tran 2019¶

Van Dat Tran. (2019). Does Cooperative Learning Increase Students' Motivation in Learning? International Journal of Higher Education, 8(5), 12. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v8n5p12

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-test comparison of the two intact classes (n = 36 each) on all six motivational components; all p values exceeded .05 (e.g., intrinsic goal orientation p = .276). Equivalence was not formally tested beyond these non-significant t-tests.

"The analysis results of an independent t -test showed there were no significant differences in statistic on pre-test scores on the motivation between the treatment group and the control group (Table 1)."
