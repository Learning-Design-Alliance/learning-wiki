## Evidence

### Leasa et al. 2017

Marleny Leasa, Aloysius D. Corebima, Ibrohim, Hadi Suwono. (2017). Emotional intelligence among auditory, reading, and kinesthetic learning styles of elementary school students in Ambon-Indonesia. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2017131889

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

LSD post-hoc test following ANOVA in the quasi-experiment (210 students) showed kinesthetic learners (mean 69.595) differed significantly from auditory (64.231) and read (65.588) learners; "kinesthetic learning style has significant difference of emotional intelligence compared to the auditory and read learning styles". Percent differences printed; no standardized effect size.

> "Table 3 shows that kinesthetic learning style has significant difference of emotional intelligence compared to the auditory and read learning styles. The emotional intelligence of the kinesthetic students are 8.35% higher than that of the auditory students, and 6.11% higher than that of the reading students."
