## Evidence

### Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa 2025

Wang Li-Chih; Chung Kevin Kien-Hoa. (2025). Relationships of trait anxiety, test anxiety, and academic performance of Chinese undergraduates with typical developments and high- and typical-functioning specific learning disabilities. Annals of Dyslexia. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11881-025-00328-z

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA controlling for age and gender on test anxiety across the three groups. The article reports "significant differences among the three groups, F(2, 234) = 8.92, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.07"; post hoc comparisons showed typical-functioning SLD highest, high-functioning SLD lowest.

> "Regarding test anxiety, the ANCOVA also yielded significant differences among the three groups, F(2, 234) = 8.92, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.07."
