q3 · i? · review · r3

The review describes the typical content of supervision sessions across included trials; most studies did not specify whether sessions were group or individual.

"Supervision primarily consisted of reviewing fidelity ratings and intervention content and discussing any problems that arose during intervention delivery."

The review reports that seven studies used a purpose-designed tool and four used previously validated tools such as the Dartmouth ACT Fidelity Scale; feedback from ratings was often provided in supervision sessions.

"Fourteen studies (33%) provided information about the quality of delivery assessment of peer supporters, which authors often referred to as fidelity."
