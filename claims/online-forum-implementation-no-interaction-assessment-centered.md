# Moving the activity to an online forum with task-based prompts produced no student-to-student interaction and an assessment-centered discussion

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In Implementation Two (online forum), there were no observed instances of interaction between students despite multiple prompts, and responses were primarily affective or functional assessment. [→ Paul J. Camp 2012](#paul-j-camp-2012)

## Evidence

### Paul J. Camp 2012

Paul J. Camp. (2012). Contrastive sets and framing: A case study in scientific writing. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1306.4944](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1306.4944)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Coding of Implementation Two's online forum responses (30 students across 3 sections, Fall 2011) shows "33 functional assessment responses" all from one rubric-application assignment; every spontaneous student response was affective, mostly positive, with no student interaction.

> "The difference implementations one and two is striking, and even more so if one realizes that the 33 functional assessment responses come entirely from the single assignment on which they were told to apply the provided rubric to their papers, and they did so."
