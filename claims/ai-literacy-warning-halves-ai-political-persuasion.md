## Evidence

### Reed Orchinik 2026

Reed Orchinik, David Rand. (2026). A light-touch AI literacy intervention helps protect against AI political persuasion. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16432

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Multilevel random-effects meta-analysis pooling 32 warning-by-cell estimates from two preregistered experiments (total N = 3,208), estimated by REML in metafor. The pooled percent reduction was "48.1% ([−59.5%,−36.8%],z=−8.32,p <0.001)"; the additive-scale robustness check gave b=−2.06, p= 0.002.
