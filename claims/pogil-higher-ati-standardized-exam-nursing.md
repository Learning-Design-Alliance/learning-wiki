## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The POGIL participant group's mean ATI national standardized test score was higher than the non-participant group's, with a significant 2-tailed t-test (p=0.032, alpha=0.05) and a significant Mann-Whitney result (0.28). [→ Maureen C. Roller 2015](#maureen-c-roller-2015)

## Evidence

### Maureen C. Roller 2015

Maureen C. Roller, DNP, ANP-BC, RN. (2015). Fundamental Nursing: Process-Oriented Guided-Inquiry Learning (POGIL) Research. Journal for Leadership and Instruction. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1080678

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative quantitative study of two fundamentals nursing sections taught by the same professor; 25 POGIL participants and 25 non-participants analyzed. The ATI comparison showed "a significance of p= 0.032"; Table 2 reports means of 79.2% versus 74%. Non-randomized purposive sampling.

> "The participant POGIL group mean (C+) national standardized test scores were higher than the (C) non-participant group (See Table 2). The 2-tailed t-test ATi standardized test equality of means revealed a significance of p= 0.032 with alpha=0.05."
