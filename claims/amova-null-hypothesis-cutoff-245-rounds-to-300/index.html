Definitional statement of the AMOVA decision rule in the methodology section. The article states "The score of 2.45 rounds up to a holistic integer of 3.00 (using AMOVA ⌊x⌉)" and applies this function to all AMOVA calculations. No empirical data support the cutoff choice; it is a stated convention of the method.
