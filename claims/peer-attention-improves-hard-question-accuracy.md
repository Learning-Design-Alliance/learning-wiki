## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Songlin Xu 2023

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-video test accuracy in the same N=311 between-subjects experiment. Feedback mean 67.9 ± 24.8% vs Control 56.9 ± 24.3% on hard questions, "19.2% increase", F1,301 = 16.086, P < 0.001; no standardized effect size printed.

> "on hard questions (comprehensive questions whose answers are not simply listed in the course video), the Feedback group showed significantly higher accuracy versus Controls (19.2% increase; F1,301 = 16.086,P < 0.001; Feedback: 67.9 ± 24.8%, Control: 56.9 ± 24.3%)"

### Songlin Xu 2023 (2)

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same experiment's overall post-test accuracy: Feedback 69.6 ± 15.7% vs Control 63.0 ± 17.8%, a "10.4% increase", F1,301 = 10.862, P = 0.001; no standardized effect size printed.

> "For the overall test, the Feedback group also performed significantly better (10.4% increase; F1,301 = 10.862,P = 0.001; Feedback: 69.6 ± 15.7%, Control: 63.0 ± 17.8%)"

### Songlin Xu 2023 (3)

Songlin Xu, Dongyin Hu, Ru Wang, and Xinyu Zhang. (2023). Peer attention enhances student learning. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2312.02358)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same experiment; easy recognition questions answered directly from the video showed no significant group difference (F1,301 = 3.303, P = 0.07). Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "On easy recognition questions (recognition questions whose answers are directly listed in the course video), performance did not significantly differ between groups (F1,301 = 3.303,P = 0.07 > 0.05)."
