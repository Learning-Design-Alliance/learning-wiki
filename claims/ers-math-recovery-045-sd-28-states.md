# Math achievement improved by about .045 standard deviations from 2022 to 2023 in the 28 states included in the ERS math sample

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Between Spring 2022 and Spring 2023, grade 3-8 math scores in the 28 ERS states improved by .045 standard deviations, making up about a third of the pandemic-era decline. [→ Dan Dewey 2024](#dan-dewey-2024)

Reconciliation analysis comparing ERS and NWEA district-level estimates of 2022-2023 recovery. The authors judge the ERS estimate of a ".045 standard deviation improvement in achievement between 2022 and 2023" accurate for the 28 ERS math states after matching samples.

> "in math, we believe that the Education Recovery Scorecard estimate of a .045 standard deviation improvement in achievement between 2022 and 2023 accurately characterizes the improvement in achievement in the 28 states included in the ERS sample."
