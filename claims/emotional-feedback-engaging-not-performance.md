## Evidence

### Vassileva 2015

Vassileva, J., McCalla, G. I., & Greer, J. E. (2015). From Small Seeds Grow Fruitful Trees: How the PHelpS Peer Help System Stimulated a Diverse and Innovative Research Agenda over 15 Years. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0073-9

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The article reports this result from its own lab's study (Okonkwo and Vassileva 2001) on the persuasive impact of an animated pedagogical agent displaying emotions. No significant learning performance gain was found; emotional feedback changed how students perceived the learning process, making it more engaging. No effect size is printed.

> "The results showed that, while not contribut ing any significant performance gain in learning, the incorporation of emotional feedback changed the way students perceived the learning process, making it more engaging."
