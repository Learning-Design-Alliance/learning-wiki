### Perl-Nussbaum 2026

Perl-Nussbaum, D., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2026). A framework for institutional change in the age of AI. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00649-4

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Campus-wide survey (N ≈ 3000) presented in the first workshop session; a leading reason students gave for not using AI to produce finished work was to respect their instructors' policies. The article reports these findings descriptively, with no effect sizes.

> "It showed that student AI use is wide - spread, but that students are also concerned about it and want guidance from their instructors on appropri - ate use in their courses."

### Perl-Nussbaum 2026 (2)

Perl-Nussbaum, D., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2026). A framework for institutional change in the age of AI. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00649-4

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Survey on AI use in coursework designed for physics students, administered in a middle-division majors' course (N = 37) and expanded across sessions to N ≈ 350 across five courses. Descriptive finding; no effect sizes printed.

> "One surprising result was that students reported “checking correctness” of their solutions as one of their most frequent uses of AI."
