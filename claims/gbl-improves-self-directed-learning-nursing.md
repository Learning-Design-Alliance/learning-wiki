## Evidence

### Rafi Alireza 2026

Rafi Alireza, Heidari Maryam, Behbahani Seyed Mohammad Salehi, Bostan Hassan, Tamimi Ali, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Naeimi Samaneh. (2026). Effectiveness of game-based learning in mental health nursing education on self-directed learning and academic self-efficacy among nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09923-z

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental two-group pretest–posttest study with one-month follow-up; repeated measures ANOVA in the intervention group (n = 20) showed a significant time effect for self-directed learning (F(2,38) = 38.74, P < 0.001, partial η² = 0.67), with "mean difference = 16.25, P < 0.001".

> "self-directed learning scores increased significantly from pretest (138.50 ± 20.69) to posttest (154.75 ± 19.34; mean difference = 16.25, P < 0.001), and this improvement was maintained at the one-month follow-up (153.95 ± 18.42)"
