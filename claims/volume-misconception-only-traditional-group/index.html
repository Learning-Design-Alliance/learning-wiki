Sari 2018¶

Sari, D.P., Darhim, & Rosjanuardi, R. (2018). Errors of students learning with REACT strategy in solving the problems of mathematical representation ability. Journal on Mathematics Education, 9(1), 121-128. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1173659

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis of posttest answers on problem 2, a black forest cake volume problem with two size options (31 cm x 20 cm x 10 cm and 38 cm x 25 cm x 10 cm). The article states this occurred "only in students who learning with traditional learning".

"A small percentage of students (12.5%) who obtained traditional learning does misconception by writing the formula to find the volume of cake = s × s × s (s = edge length)."
