Evidence¶

Sovrano Francesco 2024¶

Sovrano Francesco, Ashley Kevin, Brusilovsky Peter Leonid, Vitali Fabio. (2024). How to Improve the Explanatory Power of an Intelligent Textbook: a Case Study in Legal Writing. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00399-w

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Within-subjects user study with more than 100 English-speaking students who rated explanations on a 0-to-5-star scale for how well they explained a topic. The article reports the ratings were "statistically better than two baseline alternatives (P values below .005)"; no effect size is printed.

"The students rated YAI4Edu’s explanations the highest. According to the students, the explanatory content generated by YAI4Edu is, on average, statistically better than two baseline alternatives (P values below .005)."
