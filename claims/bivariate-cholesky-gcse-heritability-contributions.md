## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Cholesky bivariate analyses attribute the greatest shares of GCSE heritability to intelligence (51%) and self-efficacy (37%), with smaller contributions from personality (21%), parent-rated behavior problems (21%), school environment (20%), child-rated behavior problems (16%), and well-being (8%).
