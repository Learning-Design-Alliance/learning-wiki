### William Thielicke and Steffen Risius 2026

William Thielicke and Steffen Risius. (2026). Educational experiments with Particle Image Velocimetry in a small Prandtl water tunnel. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-11115-9_75](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-11115-9_75)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

PIV analysis of 100 image pairs behind a 15 mm cylinder at flow velocity 0.19 m/s (Re ≈ 2900) in the water tunnel; the shedding frequency was obtained by counting maxima of the spatially averaged perpendicular velocity component, giving "an average frequency of 2 .73 Hz" and St = 0.214.

> "The current example gives an average frequency of 2 .73 Hz with a Strouhal number of St = 0.214, which is in excellent agreement with theory."
