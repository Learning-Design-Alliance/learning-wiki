### Rodrigues Luiz 2023

Rodrigues Luiz, Palomino Paula T., Toda Armando M., Klock Ana C. T., Pessoa Marcela, Pereira Filipe D., Oliveira Elaine H. T., Oliveira David F., Cristea Alexandra I., Gasparini Isabela, Isotani Seiji. (2023). How Personalization Affects Motivation in Gamified Review Assessments. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00326-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory 90% bootstrap CI subgroup analyses on preferred game genre and playing setting. For adventure games, "those of the OSFA condition reported better identified regulation and amotivation" than personalized users; the singleplayer contrast above was also interpreted as negative for personalization. No effect sizes were printed.

> "Those who prefer singleplayer reported higher intrinsic motivation and identified regulation than those of the personalized condition."
