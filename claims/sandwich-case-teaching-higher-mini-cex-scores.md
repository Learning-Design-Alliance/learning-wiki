`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mini-CEX was applied after teaching on a 9-point scale per dimension, with scores of 7 or above indicating excellent. Table 2 prints experimental-group means from 7.30 to 8.10 versus control-group means from 6.50 to 6.88, with t values from 3.481 to 10.233 and P values from .000 to .001.
