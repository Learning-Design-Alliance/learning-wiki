## Evidence

### Cen et al. 2008

Hao Cen, Kenneth Koedinger, and Brian Junker. (2008). Comparing Two IRT Models for Conjunctive Skills. ITS 2008, LNCS 5091, pp. 796–798. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-69132-7_111

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Real-data comparison on the EAPS dataset from a difficulty factor study of 247 U.S. algebra students, with 1976 observations, 96 distinctive items, and a simplified coding of 3 skills; the learning term was removed because students were not exposed to repetitive learning opportunities. Table 2 reports CVMean 0.202 (AFM-P) versus 0.187 (CFM-P).

> "In the real data set, CFM-P is not clearly better. However, AFM-P is essentially performing as a conjunctive model because the estimates are all negative."
