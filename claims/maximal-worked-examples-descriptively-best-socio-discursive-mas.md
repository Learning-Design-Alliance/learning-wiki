# In Experiment 2, the heuristic worked examples condition significantly affected socio-discursive MAS, with maximal worked examples descriptively best and no significant post-hoc differences

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Repeated-measures ANOVA showed a significant omnibus effect of worked examples condition on socio-discursive MAS, though post-hoc pairwise differences were not significant. [→ Vogel 2022](#vogel-2022)

## Evidence

### Vogel 2022

Vogel, F., Kollar, I., Fischer, F., Reiss, K., & Ufer, S. (2022). Adaptable scaffolding of mathematical argumentation skills: The role of self-regulation when scaffolded with CSCL scripts and heuristic worked examples. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09363-z

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 comparison of adaptable, maximal, and minimal heuristic worked examples on socio-discursive MAS. Omnibus ANOVA significant (F(2,95)=3.31, p=.04, part. η²=.07); "Post-hoc comparisons did not show significant results" for specific pairwise differences.

> "A repeated-measures ANOVA with socio-discursive MAS as dependent variable and the type of heuristic worked examples as independent variable revealed a significant effect of the heuristic worked examples conditions (F(2,95) = 3.31, p = 0.04, part. η 2 = 0.07). Descriptively, students in the maximal worked examples condition outperformed learners of both the minimal worked examples condition and the adaptable worked examples condition."
