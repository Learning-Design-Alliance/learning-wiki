## Evidence

### Chang 2014

Chang, Shu, Liang, Tseng, and Hsu. (2014). Is Blended E-Learning Better than Traditional Classroom Learning for Vocational High School Students? Vol 15 | No 2 April/14. http://el.evta.gov.tw/

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Paired-samples t-test within the experimental group (n = 33): cognition t = 2.801 (d = .66), skill t = 3.294 (d = .81), attitude t = 5.766 (d = 1.40), overall t = 5.742 (d = 1.40). The article states the effect sizes were "medium to large" and says the result confirmed Chen and Lin (2002).

> "there were significant differences on the overall self -assessment, including three aspects, cognition, skill , and attitude, before and after blended e -learning"
