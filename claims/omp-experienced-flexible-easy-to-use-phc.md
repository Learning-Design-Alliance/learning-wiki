## Evidence

### Rothlind E 2025

Rothlind E, Salminen H, Rolfhamre K, Karlgren K, Toth-Pal E. (2025). Adapting the one minute preceptor model to a Swedish context – clinical tutors’ experiences of its usage in primary health care. Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care. https://doi.org/10.1080/02813432.2025.2508370

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of eight follow-up group interviews with 26 tutors from eight PHC centres found informants' spontaneous evaluations of the OMP were positive: "the model offered a logical structure which was easy to use and facilitated clinical tutoring." Informants applied it with students at all clinical levels and in scheduled appointments, emergency visits and house calls.

> "the model offered a logical structure which was easy to use and facilitated clinical tutoring. Some also commented on how the OMP increased their confidence as clinical tutors"
