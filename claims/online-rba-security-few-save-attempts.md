An observational study of online RBA administration found "very few students (less than 1-2%) attempted to save the test using print commands". The article notes well-used assessments such as the FMCE or BEMA are already available on paid sites like Chegg or CourseHero.

> "In one study, very few students (less than 1-2%) attempted to save the test using print commands during online assessments [ 23]. However, it is likely inevitable that questions (and solutions) will become increasingly available to students over time"
