## Evidence

### Popa Călin 2023

Popa Călin, Schlanger Diana, Prunoiu Virgiliu Mihail, Puia Ion Cosmin, Zaharie Florin. (2023). A novel step-by-step training program for transanal endoscopic surgery. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04296-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Prospective analytical validation study of 40 surgeons (12 young residents, 16 senior residents, 12 specialists) with no prior transanal endoscopic experience, each performing timed synthetic exercises over repeated rounds. The article reports "a statistically significant improvement of the performance time for all exercises (p < 0.01)"; no effect size is printed.

> "The progress from one round to another was shown through a statistically significant improvement of the performance time for all exercises (p < 0.01). The time improvement was also statistically significant when analyzing it on the three different subgroups (p < 0.01)."
