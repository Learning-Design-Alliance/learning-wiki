## Subclaims
`q2 i?` F23 interviewees showed better Bloch sphere understanding than F21 interviewees, which the authors indicate may reflect the mini-tutorial requiring all three representations. [→ Thacker 2025](#thacker-2025)

## Evidence

### Thacker 2025

Thacker, B., Wang, J., Zhang, Y., Tran, Q. B., Vemula, D. S., & Kushimo, T. (2025). From Research to Resources: Assessing Student Understanding and Skills in Quantum Computing. NSF grant DUE 2235464. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ae21a4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ae21a4)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview-based comparison across the two cohorts: three of four F23 students correctly identified |0> and |1> positions on the z-axis, drew axes for |+>, |->, |i>, |-i>, and discussed how a Hadamard gate rotates |0> to |+>. The authors state the mini-tutorial "may have been helpful."

> "The fact that the F23 students demonstrated a better understanding of the Bloch sphere than the F21 students, indicates that the mini-tutorial may have been helpful. However, it will need some modification or some supplementation on matrix notation"
