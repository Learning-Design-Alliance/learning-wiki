q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

OSMASEM moderation analysis restricted to the 100 articles reporting SES measurement. The omnibus moderation tests were only marginally significant; the specific SES-to-PA path moderation reached p = 0.033 and p = 0.03, explaining about 19.43% and 21.2% of heterogeneity.

"the omnibus tests with SES measurement type in the reading accuracy and reading fluency models are marginally significant (reading accuracy: χ2(5) = 10.67, p = 0.058; reading fluency: χ2(5) = 10.33, p = 0.066)"
