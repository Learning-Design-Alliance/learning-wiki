## Evidence

### Higuita-Gutiérrez LF 2020

Higuita-Gutiérrez LF, Molina-Garcia V, Acevedo Guiral J, Gómez Cadena L, Roncancio Villamil GE, Jiménez Quiceno JN. (2020). Knowledge regarding antibiotic use among students of three medical schools in Medellin, Colombia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 20:22. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1934-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Response frequencies from the survey of 536 medical students on upper respiratory tract infection items (Fig. 1). The article reports "46.3% (n = 242)" for otitis media and "29.1% (n = 150)" for azithromycin; these are descriptive percentages with no test statistic.

> "46.3% (n = 242) of the medical students considered that each case of otitis media in children should be treated with antibiotics and 29.1% (n = 150) of the students stated that the treatment of choice for these infections should be azithromycin"

### Higuita-Gutiérrez LF 2020 (2)

Higuita-Gutiérrez LF, Molina-Garcia V, Acevedo Guiral J, Gómez Cadena L, Roncancio Villamil GE, Jiménez Quiceno JN. (2020). Knowledge regarding antibiotic use among students of three medical schools in Medellin, Colombia: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education 20:22. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1934-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Response frequencies from the survey of 536 medical students on urinary tract infection items (Fig. 3). The article reports "42.8% (n = 216)" for asymptomatic bacteriuria in diabetes and ampicillin/sulbactam percentages; descriptive, no test statistic.

> "42.8% (n = 216) of the medical students stated that every asymptomatic bacteriuria in women with diabetes should be treated. Moreover, they stated that the follow-up urine culture in patients undergoing treatment for UTI should be performed after the completion of the antimicrobial therapy, and 55.8% (n = 281) and 26% (n = 130) of the students stated that the first choice of treatment for UTI should be ampicillin/sulbactam"
