## Evidence

### Favero 2026

Favero, L., Pérez-Ortiz, J.A., Käser, T., Oliver, N. (2026). AI in Education Beyond Learning Outcomes: Cognition, Agency, Emotion, and Ethics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.04598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.04598)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's ethics analysis argues that continuous data collection in AI-driven educational tools creates environments where students feel constantly monitored, and that perceived surveillance leads learners to avoid experimentation, intellectual risk-taking, and learning through error.

> "when learners perceive that every interaction is stored or analyzed, they may avoid experimentation, intellectual risk-taking, and learning through error, processes essential for critical thinking and deep understanding"

### Favero 2026 (2)

Favero, L., Pérez-Ortiz, J.A., Käser, T., Oliver, N. (2026). AI in Education Beyond Learning Outcomes: Cognition, Agency, Emotion, and Ethics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.04598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.04598)
