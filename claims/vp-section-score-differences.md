## Evidence

### Oliven Arie 2021

Oliven Arie, Nave Rachel, Baruch Adam. (2021). Long experience with a web-based, interactive, conversational virtual patient case simulation for medical students' evaluation: comparison with oral examination. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2021.1946896

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of per-section average grades across five annual VP exams (Table 1), compared with t-test. The article reports history mean 78.8 ± 5.71 and treatment 79.6 ± 6.21 vs lab 86.3 ± 3.9 and imaging 86.3 ± 1.9, with p < 0.01 for the comparison; no effect size is printed.
