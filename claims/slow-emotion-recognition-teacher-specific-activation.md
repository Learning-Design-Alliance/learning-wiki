## Evidence

### Samuel Fynes-Clinton 2022

Samuel Fynes-Clinton, Chase Sherwell, Maryam Ziaei, Ashley York, Emma Sanders O'Connor, Kylee Forrest, Libby Flynn, Julie Bower, David Reutens and Annemaree Carroll. (2022). Neural activation during emotional interference corresponds to emotion dysregulation in stressed teachers. npj Science of Learning 7:5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00123-0

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Brain-behaviour PLS analysis of ERT median reaction time with Stroop-task activation (LV p = 0.012, 35.72% covariance) in 49 stressed teachers. "slower emotion recognition perfor- mance was associated with increased activation of this network during SpeNeg trials ( r = 0.35)"; faster ERT related to greater activation during GenNeg and GenNeut.

> "Faster reaction times on the ERT were strongly correlated with increased activation in these regions during GenNeg trials ( r = −0.57) and moderately so during GenNeut trials ( r = −0.44). Conversely, a moderate correlation indicated that slower emotion recognition perfor- mance was associated with increased activation of this network during SpeNeg trials ( r = 0.35)."
