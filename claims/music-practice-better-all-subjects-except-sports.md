## Evidence

### Olive Emil Wetter 2009

Olive Emil Wetter, Fritz Koerner, Adrian Schwaninger. (2009). Does musical training improve school performance? Instr Sci 37:365–374. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9052-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Per-subject comparison of average marks (Fig. 2 and Appendix table) between music group and controls; t tests with Bonferroni-Holm correction (adjusted minimal p .0073), all significant p values "far below this adjusted level". Sports: t=0.363, p=.717.

> "Children playing an instrument were signiﬁcantly better than controls in all subjects except in sports."
