## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Spatial habits (SHOMI-VIS) were positively correlated with decreased MR commission error rate (r = -0.26) and with increased interest in studying STEM (r = 0.41). [→ Pizzie 2025](#pizzie-2025)

## Evidence

### Pizzie 2025

Pizzie, R. G., Sortino, R. M., Kim, C. E.-Y., & Inghram, R. (2025). Math anxiety and science anxiety are associated with spatial cognition and STEM interest in deaf, hard of hearing, and hearing people. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00336-z

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order correlations in the full sample (Table 1): SHOMI-VIS with MR commission error rate r = -0.26, and with STEM interest r = 0.41. The article reports spatial habits "were positively correlated with decreased MR commission error rate".

> "spatial habits, especially those associated with visualization, were positively correlated with decreased MR commission error rate (Table1, r = −0.26)."

### Pizzie 2025 (2)

Pizzie, R. G., Sortino, R. M., Kim, C. E.-Y., & Inghram, R. (2025). Math anxiety and science anxiety are associated with spatial cognition and STEM interest in deaf, hard of hearing, and hearing people. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00336-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Zero-order correlations (Table 1) showed the VVQ-VIS visualizer subscale was not significantly related to MR commission error rate or STEM interest, p > 0.05. The article reports visualizer tendency "was not related to MR commission error rate".

> "However, tendency to be a “visualizer,” or a person who often uses visuospatial tendencies and skills to represent information, was not related to MR commission error rate,p > 0.05."
