# Collaborative versus individual problem solving during PF showed no significant benefit for young students' conceptual knowledge or number of generated solution ideas

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` PF-Coll students did not score significantly higher on conceptual knowledge than PF-Ind students. [→ Claudia Mazziotti 2019](#claudia-mazziotti-2019)
`q2 i?` PF-Coll dyads did not generate significantly more solution ideas than PF-Ind individuals. [→ Claudia Mazziotti 2019 (2)](#claudia-mazziotti-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Claudia Mazziotti 2019

Claudia Mazziotti, Nikol Rummel, Anne Deiglmayr and Katharina Loibl. (2019). Probing boundary conditions of Productive Failure and analyzing the role of young students' collaboration. npj Science of Learning 4:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0041-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A-priori contrast comparing PF-Coll to PF-Ind on the conceptual knowledge posttest in the 228-student quasi-experiment. The contrast was nonsignificant with "ηp² = 0.005"; Hypothesis 2 was rejected.

> "there was no signi ﬁcant bene ﬁt of working collabora- tively as compared with working individually during the problem- solving phase of PF ( F[1, 224] = 1.16, p = 0.28, ηp² = 0.005, PF-Coll: 95% CI [9.75, 10.89] and PF-Ind: 95% CI [10.11, 11.50])"

### Claudia Mazziotti 2019 (2)

Claudia Mazziotti, Nikol Rummel, Anne Deiglmayr and Katharina Loibl. (2019). Probing boundary conditions of Productive Failure and analyzing the role of young students' collaboration. npj Science of Learning 4:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0041-5

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-factor ANOVA on number of solution ideas, a dyad-level variable (n = 34 dyads) in PF-Coll and individual-level (n = 49) in PF-Ind. The effect was nonsignificant with "ηp² = 0.012"; Hypothesis 3 was rejected.

> "No statistically signi ﬁcant effect emerged ( F[1, 81] = 0.949, p = 0.333, ηp² = 0.012, PF-Coll: 95% CI [3.90, 5.16] and PF-Ind: 95% CI [3.57, 4.67]). Hypothesis 3 was therefore also rejected."
