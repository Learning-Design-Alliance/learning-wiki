## Evidence

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer 2023

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer, Julia Reuber, Jörg-Tobias Kuhn, Kristina Moll, Heinz Holling, Christian Dobel. (2023). Cognitive Profiles of Children with Isolated and Comorbid Learning Difficulties in Reading and Math: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09735-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Post hoc moderator analyses on reading outcome categories (RAN, phonological processing, language skills, reading) in the meta-analysis. Effect sizes "increased hierarchically" from RAN to reading, with deficits most pronounced in general reading tasks.

> "In comparing children with RD to those without (C-RD, C-RDMD, MD-RDMD), effect sizes increased hierarchically for the order of the following factor levels: RAN < language skills  < phonological processing  < reading."

### Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer 2023 (2)

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer, Julia Reuber, Jörg-Tobias Kuhn, Kristina Moll, Heinz Holling, Christian Dobel. (2023). Cognitive Profiles of Children with Isolated and Comorbid Learning Difficulties in Reading and Math: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09735-3

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Moderator analysis for the control-vs-MD comparison showed a reversed ordering of phonological processing and reading, which the authors interpret as children with MD struggling more in phonological processing.

> "For group comparisons of C-MD, the sequence of phonological processing and reading was the other way around (RAN < language skills < reading < phonological processing). This finding suggests that children with MD struggle more in phonological processing than in general reading tasks."
