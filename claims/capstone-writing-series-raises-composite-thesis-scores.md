## Evidence

### Weaver K. F. 2016

Weaver K. F., Morales V., Nelson M., Weaver P. F., Toledo A., Godde K. (2016). The Benefits of Peer Review and a Multisemester Capstone Writing Series on Inquiry and Analysis Skills in an Undergraduate Thesis. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-01-0072

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post comparison of 84 senior theses (2006–2013) scored on a six-dimension rubric adapted from AAC&U VALUE rubrics, analyzed as interval composite scores. The t test found scores "significantly different in pretreatment and intervention groups"; no effect size is printed.

> "The two-tailed t test demonstrates that composite scores were significantly different in pretreatment and intervention groups (t value = −4.4465, df = 79, p value < 0.0001), confirming the significant chi-square results"
