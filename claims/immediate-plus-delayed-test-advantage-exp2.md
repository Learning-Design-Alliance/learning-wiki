Tamara van Gog & Liesbeth Kester & Kim Dirkx & Vincent Hoogerheide & Joris Boerboom & Peter P. J. L. Verkoeijen. (2015). Testing After Worked Example Study Does Not Enhance Delayed Problem-Solving Performance Compared to Restudy. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9297-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Experiment 2 delayed final test ANOVA showed a significant main effect of final test moment, F(1,115)=14.13, p<0.001, ηp2=0.109: the "immediate+delayed final test condition (M=63.96 %, SD=30.59) outperforming participants in the delayed final test condition (M=43.88 %, SD=29.16)". Delayed-test effort was also lower after an immediate test (ηp2=0.114).
