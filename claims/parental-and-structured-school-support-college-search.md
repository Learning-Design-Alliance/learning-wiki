# Even highly qualified students need strong parental support combined with structured school support to conduct an effective college search and enroll in a matched college

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · qualitative `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among 105 CPS students studied qualitatively, only a small number left high school qualified for a very selective college, completed a thorough search, and enrolled in their chosen college; these students combined family support with one-on-one adult support at school. [→ Melissa Roderick 2008](#melissa-roderick-2008)

## Evidence

### Melissa Roderick 2008

Melissa Roderick, Jenny Nagaoka, Vanessa Coca, Eliza Moeller, with Karen Roddie, Jamiliyah Gilliam, and Desmond Patton. (2008). From High School to the Future: Potholes on the Road to College (Case Studies). Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/high-school-future-potholes-road-college

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative longitudinal case study of two high-achieving CPS students (Moises and Grady), two of only five students in the 105-study who were qualified for a very selective college, searched thoroughly, and enrolled in their choice. The authors state students need "strong parental support combined with structured sup- port from high schools."

> "in addition to academic quali- fications and personal determination, students need strong parental support combined with structured sup- port from high schools to undertake an extensive and effective college search."
