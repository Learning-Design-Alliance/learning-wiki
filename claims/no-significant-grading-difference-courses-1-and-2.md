## Evidence

### Bialas MJ 2025

Bialas MJ, Schmid JQ, Middelberg C, Stamm T, Blanck‐Lubarsch M. (2025). 15‐Point versus pass/fail grading in orthodontic education: A randomized controlled trial. J Dent Educ. https://doi.org/10.1002/jdd.13815

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Null results from the RCT's first two courses (n = 56 and n = 42), with appliances rated by the five calibrated dentists; equivalence was not tested, only non-significance reported via Mann–Whitney U tests.

> "There was no statistically significant difference between both groups in course 1 (p = 0.8761) with mean values of 11.59 ± 0.99 versus 11.63 ± 0.75 and in course 2 (p = 0.8203) with values of 10.85 ± 0.82 versus 10.96 ± 0.83."
