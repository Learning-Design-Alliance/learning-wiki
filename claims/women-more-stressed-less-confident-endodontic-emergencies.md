## Evidence

### Chuy Virginie 2025

Chuy Virginie, Matyjasik Pierre, Devillard Raphaël, Kérourédan Olivia. (2025). Stress and Confidence of Undergraduate Dental Students With Different Levels of Clinical Experience in Managing Endodontic Emergencies: A Cross‐Sectional Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13072

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Ordinal logistic regression (Table 2, n = 227): women vs. men OR = 4.35 [2.53; 7.60], p < 0.001 for higher stress category. The article reports women "were 4.4 times more likely to be in a higher stress category than men."

> "Women were 4.4 times more likely to be in a higher stress category than men, and fourth‐ and fifth‐year students were approximately 2–2.4 times more likely to be in a higher stress category than sixth‐year students."

### Chuy Virginie 2025 (2)

Chuy Virginie, Matyjasik Pierre, Devillard Raphaël, Kérourédan Olivia. (2025). Stress and Confidence of Undergraduate Dental Students With Different Levels of Clinical Experience in Managing Endodontic Emergencies: A Cross‐Sectional Study. European Journal of Dental Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.13072

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression on the cumulative confidence score (Table 3, n = 227): women vs. men estimated difference −13.23 [−18.90; −7.57], p < 0.001 on the 0–200 scale. The article states "women's confidence was 13 points lower than men's."

> "On average, women's confidence was 13 points lower than men's, and students in their fourth and fifth years had a cumulative confidence score 12 and 9 points lower than sixth‐year students, respectively."
