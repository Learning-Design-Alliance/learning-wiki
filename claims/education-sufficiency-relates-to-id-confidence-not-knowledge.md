Survey analysis of the Australian final-year student sample testing education sufficiency against confidence ratings. The printed statistic is F(2,110) = 4.49, p = 0.013; no effect size is printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

### Weier N 2017 (2)

Weier N, Thursky K, Zaidi STR. (2017). Antimicrobial knowledge and confidence amongst final year medical students in Australia. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182460

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the same survey's clinical-case section: neither self-reported education sufficiency nor confidence predicted demonstrated knowledge. Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "There was no significant relationship observed between sufficiency of education in infectious diseases or confidence in knowledge of infectious diseases and proportion of correct answers to the clinical questions."
