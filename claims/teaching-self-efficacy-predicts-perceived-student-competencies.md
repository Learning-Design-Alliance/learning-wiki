## Evidence

### Dybowski Christoph 2017

Dybowski Christoph, Sehner Susanne, Harendza Sigrid. (2017). Influence of motivation, self-efficacy and situational factors on the teaching quality of clinical educators. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-0923-2

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Exploratory mixed-effects logistic regression (physician as random intercept) predicting dichotomized perceptions of students' competencies from dispositions. Teaching self-efficacy was a significant positive predictor with "OR = 24.66; 95%-CI 1.45–418.18; p = .026", a large odds ratio with a very wide confidence interval.
