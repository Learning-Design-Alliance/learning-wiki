## Evidence

### Xie G 2025

Xie G, Zeng Z, He Y, Liao X, You C. (2025). Development and psychometric test of core competency assessment scale for gastrointestinal endoscopy nurses. Medicine 2025;104:40(e44969). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000044969

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Content validity assessment via expert ratings of item importance, reported in the validity analysis results section. All I-CVI values "exceeding the recommended threshold of 0.78" and S-CVI of 0.96 indicate, in the article's words, excellent content validity.

> "Content validity was assessed using I-CVI, which ranged from 0.80 to 1.00, all exceeding the recommended threshold of 0.78. The S-CVI, computed as 0.96, also surpassed the minimum criterion of 0.90, indicating excellent content validity for the scale."
