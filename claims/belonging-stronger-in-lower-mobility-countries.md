Metaregression predicting the b-path from sense of belonging to reading achievement across 57 countries. The authors interpret this as belonging becoming more consequential for achievement when external resources are scarce.

> "We found a signi ﬁcant negative association with sense of belonging (b = −3.093). In countries with higher social mobility, the association of sense of belonging with achievement was weaker."
