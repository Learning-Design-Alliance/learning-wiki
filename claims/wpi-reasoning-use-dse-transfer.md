`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of written reasoning on the DSE transfer questions, reported in Table II as the percentage of undergraduate and graduate students using WPI reasoning on questions 2 to 5 among those who provided reasoning; students often used WPI reasoning "learned in the MZI tutorial" to answer the DSE transfer questions, and a majority of those students used it correctly.

> "Another indication of transfer is that students often used WPI reasoning learned in the MZI tutorial to answer the DSE transfer questions."
