## Evidence

### Loosveld LM 2020

Loosveld LM, Van Gerven PWM, Vanassche E, Driessen EW. (2020). Mentors’ Beliefs About Their Roles in Health Care Education: A Qualitative Study of Mentors’ Personal Interpretative Framework. Academic Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000003159

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Multiple-case study of 18 mentors from 3 undergraduate programs at Maastricht University, interviewed December 2017–January 2018. The cross-case clustering of biographical case reports "constituted the following 4 mentoring positions": facilitator, coach, monitor, and exemplar (Table 2 reports 5, 3, 6, and 4 mentors respectively).

> "From our within-case and cross-case analyses, we distilled 3 types of PSU and 3 types of SET, which, when combined, constituted the following 4 mentoring positions"
