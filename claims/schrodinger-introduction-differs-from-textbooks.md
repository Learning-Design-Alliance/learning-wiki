### T. Mart 2021

T. Mart. (2021). How do I introduce Schrödinger equation during the quantum mechanics course? arXiv preprint. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abc8db](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abc8db)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Comparative analysis of the author's own approach against quantum mechanics textbooks (Gasiorowicz, Griffiths, Dicke and Wittke, Weinberg, and more recent texts) and the original Nuffield A level, described in the abstract and Section 5. The review "is in line with the original Nuﬃeld A level one"; most recent books introduce the equation abruptly, often as an eigenvalue equation. This is an interpretive comparison, not an empirical test.

> "It is found that the present approach is diﬀerent from those given by these authors, except by Weinberg or Dicke and Wittke. However, the approach is in line with the original Nuﬃeld A level one."
