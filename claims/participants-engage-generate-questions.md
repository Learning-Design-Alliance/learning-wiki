### C. Aa. Diget 2016

Binding Blocks: building the Universe one nucleus at the time. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aa550c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aa550c)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors' concluding account of engagement across the events they delivered: participants "immediately engaged with the chart" and it generated wide-ranging questions. This is the authors' own interpretive observation rather than a systematic evaluation, and no effect size or comparison is reported.

> "During each event, participants have immediately engaged with the chart, which immediately generates a wide range of questions about many aspects of nuclear physics."

### C. Aa. Diget 2016 (2)

C. Aa. Diget, A. Pastore, K. Leech, T. Haylett, S. Lock, T. Sanders, M. Shelley, H. V. Willett, J. Keegans, L. Sinclair, E. C. Simpson, and the Binding Blocks collaboration. (2016). Binding Blocks: building the Universe one nucleus at the time. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aa550c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aa550c)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

An observational participation count from one outreach event: the article reports "over 1,000 of the 7,500 visitors" took part in the exhibition and engaged with building the chart, which was completed over four hours. No learning outcome, comparison or effect size is reported.

> "at the recent STFC Daresbury Laboratory Open Day (July 2016), over 1,000 of the 7,500 visitors at the Open Day participated in the Binding Blocks exhibition and engaged with the construction of the chart."

### C. Aa. Diget 2016 (3)

C. Aa. Diget, A. Pastore, K. Leech, T. Haylett, S. Lock, T. Sanders, M. Shelley, H. V. Willett, J. Keegans, L. Sinclair, E. C. Simpson, and the Binding Blocks collaboration. (2016). Binding Blocks: building the Universe one nucleus at the time. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aa550c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aa550c)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

The authors report that at teacher training events the participating teachers generated many ideas for complementary activities in physics, chemistry and mathematics built on the completed chart. This is an unquantified observational statement with no systematic data collection reported.

> "During training events for teachers, the participating teachers have come up with many ideas for complementing the workshop with activities in physics, chemistry, and mathematics, where the completed chart of nuclides facilitates further learning."
