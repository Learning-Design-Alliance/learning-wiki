# Control group members had ready access to transition-related services, and Way2Work services may have substituted for existing services

## Evidence

### David Mann 2021

David Mann, Kathleen Feeney, Todd Honeycutt, Marlena Luhr. (2021). Way2Work Maryland Demonstration: Impacts 24 Months After Enrollment. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/way2work-maryland-demonstration-impacts-24-months-after-enrollment

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

RCT service-use comparison between treatment and control groups at 24 months. The report states "almost half of control group members also used services from the agency," though not at treatment-group intensity.

> "However, almost half of control group members also used services from the agency, though they did not do so at an intensity similar to that of the treatment group."

### David Mann 2021 (2)

David Mann, Kathleen Feeney, Todd Honeycutt, Marlena Luhr. (2021). Way2Work Maryland Demonstration: Impacts 24 Months After Enrollment. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/way2work-maryland-demonstration-impacts-24-months-after-enrollment

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the service-use findings: control members had ready access to transition-related services, and Way2Work services "might have been substitutes for other existing services." This is an interpretive explanation, not a tested result.

> "These findings suggest that (1) control group members had ready access to transition-related services and (2) though Way2Work Maryland connected youth with the vocational rehabilitation agency, its services might have been substitutes for other existing services, such as those provided by secondary and postsecondary schools."
