## Evidence

### Erkan Cer & Ertugrul Sahin 2017

Erkan Cer & Ertugrul Sahin. (2017). The Effects of Quality Books for Children and the Metacognitive Strategy on Students’ Self-Esteem Levels. Journal of Education and Learning, 6(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v6n1p72

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison of the two assigned groups using the independent samples t-test as printed in the Results section. The article reports no significant difference, with control M=19.50 (SD=2.60) and experimental M=18.62 (SD=1.91); the printed d=.38 is described as "a medium (.38 standard deviation) effect size in favor of the experimental group".

> "The independent samples t-test result showed that there was no significant difference ( t (86.05)=1.86, p>.05, d=.38) in pre-test scores between the students in the control ( M=19.50, SD=2.60) and the experimental (M=18.62, SD=1.91) groups."
