# Adding the PLAN A tabletop serious game to standard airway training does not produce a statistically significant between-group difference in total case-based assessment scores in novice trainees

## Evidence

### Mohar Sebastian 2026

Mohar Sebastian, Crawford Ana María, Vásquez Ulises Sánchez, Serna-Muñoz Ricardo, Martínez Rodrigo Rubio. (2026). The educational impact of a serious game for airway management skills of medical trainees. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09552-6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pilot trial at Centro Médico ABC with 34 medical interns and first-year residents randomized 1:1; the intervention group received an additional 40-minute PLAN A session. The Mann–Whitney U comparison of post-test totals was non-significant, with the article reporting "U = 137.5, p = 0.83".

> "A direct comparison of post-intervention scores between groups showed no statistically significant difference (U = 137.5, p = 0.83, Mann–Whitney U test)."
