## Evidence

### Tianlong Zu 2017

Tianlong Zu, John Hutson, Lester C. Loschky, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2017). Use of Eye-Tracking Technology to Investigate Cognitive Load Theory. 2017 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.113

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t-test on end-of-lesson difficulty ratings in the electric-motor lesson study. The high-GCL group rated the material as less difficult, "t(44) = 2.157, p = .037"; the redundancy contrast was not significant, t(44) = .423, ns. No standardized effect size was printed.

> "A significant difference in means  of difficulty  rating between students from  low- and high-GCL load groups was found,  t(44) = 2.157, p = .037. Students from high-GCL group rated the material as less difficult than those from low -GCL group."
