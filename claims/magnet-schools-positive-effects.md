## Evidence

### Cookson 2018

Cookson, P. W., Jr., Darling-Hammond, L., Rothman, R., & Shields, P. M. (2018). The tapestry of American public education: How we can create a system of schools worth choosing for all? Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/tapestry-american-public-education

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The report's synthesis of magnet school research, spanning large-scale national studies, statewide program studies, and rigorous local analyses. About 2.6 million students are enrolled in roughly 3,400 magnet schools in more than 600 districts across 34 states.

> "Syntheses of the research on magnet schools have generally found positive effects on achievement, graduation rates, student motivation and satisfaction with school, teacher motivation and morale, parent satisfaction, intergroup relationships, and integration."

### Cookson 2018 (2)

Cookson, P. W., Jr., Darling-Hammond, L., Rothman, R., & Shields, P. M. (2018). The tapestry of American public education: How we can create a system of schools worth choosing for all? Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/tapestry-american-public-education

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report cites district-level studies showing wide variation in integration outcomes depending on district demographics and how magnet admissions and attendance are structured.

> "For example, one district study found that magnets reduced segregation by race, ethnicity, and parental education but not by test scores or language status."
