### van Wijk 2024

van Wijk, E. V., van Blankenstein, F. M., Donkers, J., Janse, R. J., Bustraan, J., Adelmeijer, L. G. M., Dubois, E. A., Dekker, F. W., & Langers, A. M. J. (2024). Does 'summative' count? The influence of the awarding of study credits on feedback use and test-taking motivation in medical progress testing. Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10324-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire-based logistic regression comparing test preparation between the formative and summative PT-groups. The main analysis found "adjusted OR [aOR] 1.26, 95% CI 0.57–2.76"; the perceived-condition subgroup analysis was similar (aOR 1.83, 95% CI 0.72–4.64).

> "Logistic regression showed no significant association between assessment condition and preparation for the PT (adjusted OR [aOR] 1.26, 95% CI 0.57–2.76) (Table 2). A similar result was found in the subgroup analysis (aOR 1.83, 95% CI 0.72–4.64)."
