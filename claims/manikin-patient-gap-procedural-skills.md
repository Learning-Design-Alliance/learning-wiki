## Evidence

### Widyandana Doni; Majoor Gerard; Scherpbier Albert 2012

Widyandana Doni; Majoor Gerard; Scherpbier Albert. (2012). Preclinical students' experiences in early clerkships after skills training partly offered in primary health care centers: a qualitative study from Indonesia. BMC Medical Education. [doi:10.1186/1472-6920-12-35](https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-35)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the FGD analysis, differences between manikins and patients were raised 17 times by NPT students versus 2 by PT students, and fear of harming the patient 10 versus 1 times. One NPT student noted manikins "are not screaming, but pediatric patients are always screaming".

> "With therapeutic skills NPT students had suffered most from differences between manikins and patients, e.g. with insertion of intravenous lines, minor surgery, tracheal intubation, urethral catheterization, and assisting at a delivery."
