### Browning 2020

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review-style brief reports, citing Frank, Reibel, Broderick, Cantrell, and Metz (2015), that teacher mindfulness training "may be linked to significant gains in educators' job satisfaction" and related self-regulation skills. The brief offers no effect sizes; attributed narrative evidence only.

> "equipping teachers with mindfulness and other stress management skills may be linked to significant gains in educators’ job satisfaction, as well as in self-regulation, self-compassion, and skills such as observation, nonjudgment, and the ability to be nonreactive"
