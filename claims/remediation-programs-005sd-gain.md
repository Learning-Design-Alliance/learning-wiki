## Evidence

### Jacobs 2025

Jacobs, M., Haelermans, C., & Meeter, M. (2025). Effective remediation programs for vulnerable students to overcome learning loss. PLoS One, 20(5). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0323352

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Difference-in-Differences analysis with OLS, school-level clustered standard errors and school fixed effects on 66,439 students in 456 schools (Table 3: School year * Participation 0.052**, 0.050**, 0.054** for composite, reading, mathematics). The printed effect size of 0.05 SD is below d = 0.2, so the impact is coded 0 despite statistical significance.

> "The DiD-estimator shows a significant and positive interaction effect, suggesting that students who participated in remediation programs scored an additional 0.05 SD (composite score) on their tests at time t1."
