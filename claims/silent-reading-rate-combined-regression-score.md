### E. B. Skaggs 1918

E. B. Skaggs. (1918). The Correlation of General Intelligence Tests and School Standing. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

A methodological report on test development at Indiana University: the scale is being compared with other reading tests by correlation with an independent statement of reading ability based on pooled ratings from four teachers for each child in a large junior high school. The scientific combination of the two scores is felt to be a particularly important feature.

> "rate score and comprehension score are being combined into a single score in silent reading by means of the partial regression equations of rate score and comprehension score on reading ability, as thus judged."
