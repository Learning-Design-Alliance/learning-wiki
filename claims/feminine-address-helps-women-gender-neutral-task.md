# On a relatively gender-neutral word association task, women perform better when addressed in the feminine than the masculine

## Evidence

### Tamar Kricheli-Katz and Tali Regev 2021

Tamar Kricheli-Katz and Tali Regev. (2021). The effect of language on performance: do gendered languages fail women in maths? npj Science of Learning 6:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00087-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A supplementary experiment (674 participants, 334 women and 340 men, randomly assigned) used a 60-second alphabet word association task, relatively gender-neutral. Women produced more letters under feminine address, though the difference is marginal (p = 0.058); men's difference was nonsignificant.

> "When addressed in the masculine, the average number of letters women provided was 40.8, whereas when addressed in the feminine it was 45 (t-test, p = 0.058)."
