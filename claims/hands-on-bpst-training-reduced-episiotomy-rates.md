## Evidence

### Zimmo Kaled Mikki 2018

Zimmo Kaled Mikki, Laine Katariina, Fosse Erik, Zimmo Mohammed, Ali-Masri Hadil, Böttcher Bettina, Zucknick Manuela, Vikanes Åse, Hassan Sahar. (2018). Impact of animated instruction on tablets and hands-on training in applying bimanual perineal support on episiotomy rates: an intervention study. International Urogynecology Journal. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00192-018-3711-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interventional cohort study of 12,841 primiparous women with singleton noninstrumental vaginal deliveries at six Palestinian government hospitals, October 2015 to January 2017. The Results section reports the significant reduction "from 61.1 to 38.1% (OR = 0.39, 95% CI 0.33–0.47)" after hands-on training in Hospitals 1 and 2.

> "After Intervention 2 (hands-on training), the episiotomy rate in intervention hospitals (1 and 2) was significantly reduced when compared with the preintervention period, from 61.1 to 38.1% (OR = 0.39, 95% CI 0.33–0.47)."
