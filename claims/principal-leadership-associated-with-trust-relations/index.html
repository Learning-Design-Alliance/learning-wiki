Evidence¶

Bryk 1996¶

Bryk, A. S., & Schneider, B. (1996). Social Trust: A Moral Resource for School Improvement. University of Chicago, Center for School Improvement (draft). https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/social-trust-moral-resource-school-improvement

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Three-level HLM analysis with trust relations as level-1 outcomes, teacher predictors at level 2, and school composition and facilitating factors at level 3. The article also reports "significant effects for principal supervision on both teacher-teacher and teacher-parent trust."

"As expected, principal leadership was associated with the positive trust relations, especially for teacher-principal trust."
