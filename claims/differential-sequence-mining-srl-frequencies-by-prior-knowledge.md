## Evidence

### Taub 2019

Taub, M., & Azevedo, R. (2019). How Does Prior Knowledge Influence Eye Fixations and Sequences of Cognitive and Metacognitive SRL Processes during Learning with an Intelligent Tutoring System? Int J Artif Intell Educ (2019) 29:1-28. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0165-4

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Differential sequence mining (Kinnebrew et al. method) with a MANCOVA comparing instance support values of eight 2-length sequences between prior knowledge groups, condition as covariate (n = 30). Multivariate effect Wilks' λ = .49, F(8, 20) = 2.65, p = .037, ηp2 = .52; univariate ηp2 values .14 to .23.

> "participants with high prior knowledge had significantly higher frequencies of sequences 1 ➔ 1[ C o g H u➔ CogHu], 1 ➔ 5 [CogHu ➔ MetacogHu], 5 ➔ 1 [MetacogHu ➔ CogHu], and 1 ➔ 2 [CogHu ➔ CogLu], and significantly lower frequencies of sequence 6 ➔ 6[ M e t a c o g L u➔ MetacogLu] than participants with low prior knowledge."
