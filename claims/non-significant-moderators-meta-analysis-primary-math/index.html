Evidence¶

Hakan Ulum 2022¶

Hakan Ulum, Songül Tümkaya. (2022). The effect of student-centred methods and techniques on primary school students' mathematics achievement: A meta-analysis study. Malikussaleh Journal of Mathematics Learning (MJML). https://ojs.unimal.ac.id/mjml/index

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Analog ANOVA moderator analyses across eight categorical moderators all returned non-significant between-group variance (application period QB = 4.058; publication type QB = 0.084; database QB = 2.248; grade level QB = 0.634; scale QB = 2.861; school starting age QB = 5.122; country QB = 6.562).

"According to the results obtained from the m oderator analysis for the experimental application period, the variance between studies was not statistically significant (QB = 4.058, p> 0.05)."

Hakan Ulum 2022 (2)¶

Hakan Ulum, Songül Tümkaya. (2022). The effect of student-centred methods and techniques on primary school students' mathematics achievement: A meta-analysis study. Malikussaleh Journal of Mathematics Learning (MJML). https://ojs.unimal.ac.id/mjml/index

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression for the continuous sample-size moderator (studies ranged between 30 and 261 participants) reports an intercept of 0.8605 and a sample coefficient of -0.0009 with p = 0.4635, indicating sample size does not change the effect size.

"the regression coeffici ent (d = -0.0009) value for the sample will correspond to a decrease (-) of 0.0009 in the effect size of each participant included in the sample."
