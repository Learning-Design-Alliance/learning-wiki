Sedat Turgut 2017¶

Sedat Turgut, Özlem Dogan Temur. (2017). The Effect of Game-Assisted Mathematics Education on Academic Achievement in Turkey: A Meta-Analysis Study. International Electronic Journal of Elementary Education. https://doi.org/10.26822/iejee.2017236115

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Subgroup analysis of learning domain (mathematics, geometry, mathematics and geometry) with QB = 0.618 below the critical chi-square value; the article reports "medium effect" for all three domain groups.

"Accordingly, a statistically significant difference between groups in terms of learrning domain is not found. Therefore, the academic achievement attained by using games does not show a statistically significant differentiation in terms of different learning domains."
