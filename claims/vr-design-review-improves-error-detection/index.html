Evidence¶

Zia Ud Din 2024¶

Zia Ud Din, Payam Mohammad, Rachael Sherman. (2024). A systematic review and analysis of the viability of virtual reality (VR) in construction work and education. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5059219

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review reports, citing Kandi et al., that a VR game Design Review Simulation "significantly enhanced the ability of students to identify mistakes" versus 2D drawings. No effect size is printed.

"a game Design Review Simulation in VR significantly enhanced the ability of students to identify mistakes, with results showing that they detected a notably higher number of errors compared to reviewing 2D drawings [21]."
