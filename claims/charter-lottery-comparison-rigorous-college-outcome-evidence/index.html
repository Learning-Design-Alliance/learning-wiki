Evidence¶

Kate Place 2019¶

Kate Place, Philip Gleason. (2019). Do Charter Middle Schools Improve Students' College Outcomes? (Study Highlights). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/do-charter-middle-schools-improve-students-college-outcomes-study-highlights

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The study design randomly selected 1,723 lottery winners offered admission to the 31 charter middle schools while 1,150 lottery losers were not, so the authors state that "Comparing lottery winners and lottery losers is a rigorous way to assess" effectiveness on college outcomes.

"Comparing lottery winners and lottery losers is a rigorous way to assess how effective the charter middle schools were in improving college outcomes."
