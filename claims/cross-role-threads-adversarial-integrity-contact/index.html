Pelin Yüce 2026¶

Pelin Yüce, Xiangruo Dai, Rebecca Owens, Tuğrulcan Elmas. (2026). ChatGPT vs Teachers vs Students: Large-Scale Analysis of Generative AI Discourse in Education Communities on Reddit. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17712 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.17712

q2 · i? · associational · r2

LLM-based role classification of 109,581 authors (82,373 binary-labelled: 35,466 faculty, 46,907 students) enables thread-level cross-role analysis. "17% of threads are cross-role, and one third of such contact occurs in the adversarial themes".

Pelin Yüce, Xiangruo Dai, Rebecca Owens, Tuğrulcan Elmas. (2026). ChatGPT vs Teachers vs Students: Large-Scale Analysis of Generative AI Discourse in Education Communities on Reddit. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17712
