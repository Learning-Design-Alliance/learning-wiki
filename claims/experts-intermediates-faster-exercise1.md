## Evidence

### De Witte Benjamin 2021

De Witte Benjamin, Barnouin Charles, Moreau Richard, Lelevé Arnaud, Martin Xavier, Collet Christian, Hoyek Nady. (2021). A haptic laparoscopic trainer based on affine velocity analysis: engineering and preliminary results. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-021-01128-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional discriminative validation comparing four expertise groups (17 experts, 12 intermediates, 28 inexperienced interns, 20 novices) on one trial of exercise 1. "Mean times were 82.4 s (SD = 22.1 s)" for experts versus 156.8 s for novices; Kruskal–Wallis Chi-square = 32.80, p < 0.001. No effect size printed.

> "Experts and intermediates completed this exercise faster than inexperienced interns and novices. Mean times were 82.4 s (SD = 22.1 s), 81.5 s (SD = 23.7 s), 122.3 s (SD = 50 s), and 156.8 s (SD = 53 s) for experts, intermediates, interns, and novices respectively."
