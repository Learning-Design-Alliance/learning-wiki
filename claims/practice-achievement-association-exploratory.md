# Exploratory analyses found classroom practice positively associated with student achievement, but no association between the leadership measures and achievement

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Classroom practice was positively associated with mathematics achievement (0.06 for CLASS, 0.07 for FFT) and reading achievement (0.03 for both rubrics). [→ Garet 2017](#garet-2017)
`q2 i?` Exploratory analyses found no association between the study's leadership measures and achievement. [→ Garet 2017 (2)](#garet-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Garet 2017

Garet, M.S., Wayne, A.J., Brown, S., Rickles, J., Song, M., and Manzeske, D. (2017). The Impact of Providing Performance Feedback to Teachers and Principals, Executive Summary (NCEE 2018-4000). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory correlational analysis controlling for students' prior achievement and background characteristics; the article states the study "was not designed to provide a rigorous causal test" of the theory of action linking practice to achievement.

> "We found an association with students' mathematics achievement of 0.06 for classroom practice as measured by the CLASS and 0.07 as measured by the FFT."

### Garet 2017 (2)

Garet, M.S., Wayne, A.J., Brown, S., Rickles, J., Song, M., and Manzeske, D. (2017). The Impact of Providing Performance Feedback to Teachers and Principals, Executive Summary (NCEE 2018-4000). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. http://ies.ed.gov/ncee

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Exploratory analysis of the principal leadership survey measures against student achievement; no association was found, so the leadership pathway of the theory of action was not supported in these analyses.

> "Similar exploratory analyses found no association between the study measures of leadership and achievement."
