Qusay H. Mahmoud 2026¶

Qusay H. Mahmoud. (2026). Judgment-Centred Software Engineering Education: A Post-Hype Review and Framework for AI-Augmented Learning. Preprint, September 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29473

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports the Boolzen et al. 2026 STEM meta-analysis: a conventional random-effects model found a positive pooled effect, with "heterogeneity was extreme (I 2 = 96 .32%)" and a prediction interval spanning negative to positive effects; a robust Bayesian analysis adjusting for publication bias favoured no overall effect.

"Its conventional random-effects model found a positive pooled effect on externally assessed cognitive outcomes, but heterogeneity was extreme (I 2 = 96 .32%) and the prediction interval included negative, null, and positive effects."

Qusay H. Mahmoud 2026 (2)¶

Qusay H. Mahmoud. (2026). Judgment-Centred Software Engineering Education: A Post-Hype Review and Framework for AI-Augmented Learning. Preprint, September 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29473

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes to Kumar et al.'s systematic review of 72 primary studies "robust short-term efficiency gains" and "weak transfer from AI-assisted completion to independent skill"; other 2026 reviews (Katona; Adejumo; Agbo) converge on benefits conditional on pedagogical strategy.

"Kumar, Wongsirichot, and Nanthaamornphong’s systematic review of 72 primary studies reports robust short-term efficiency gains, recurring over-reliance, weak transfer from AI-assisted completion to independent skill, and strong evidence for assessment redesign."
