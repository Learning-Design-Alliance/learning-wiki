`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 40 studies (4,542 cases) that reported baseline knowledge assessment. The overall baseline difference was SMD 0.32 (95% CI 0.11-0.53, P=.003), significant in observational studies but not in RCTs, suggesting possible baseline imbalance in non-randomized designs.

> "The subgroup analysis revealed a significant difference in baseline knowledge between the FC and control group in observational studies (SMD 0.36, 95% CI 0.03-0.70), while in RCTs, there were no substantial differences (SMD 0.30, 95% CI 0.00-0.59"
