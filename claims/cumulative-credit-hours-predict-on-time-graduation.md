Aiken, J. M., De Bin, R., Hjorth-Jensen, M., Caballero, M. D. (2020). Predicting time to graduation at a large enrollment American university. arXiv. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0242334](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242334)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Partial-dependence analysis of the xgboost model for cumulative credit hours, weighted by per-semester F1-score (Fig. 9). The article reports credit hours near 120 are "highly predictive of graduating" between semesters 8 and 10; no effect size is printed.

> "Having a total number of credit hours close to 120 is highly predictive of graduating if students graduate between 8 and 10 semesters of enrollment. Outside of this window the impact of the total number of credit hours diminishes."
