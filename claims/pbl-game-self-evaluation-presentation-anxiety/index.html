Evidence¶

Baran 2018¶

Baran, M., Maskan, A., & Yaşar, Ş. (2018). Learning Physics through Project-Based Learning Game Techniques. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 221-234. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11215a

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive analysis of the 16-item self-evaluation form completed by the 21 experimental-group students: total scores ranged from 19.00 to 36.00 with mean 30.4286 (SD 4.73890); average item score 2.5357 on a scale where 3 = above average.

"the participants in the experimental group have scored within the range of average and above average (X = 2.53). Based on these findings, it can be concluded that, in general, the participants in the experimental group evaluation were positive."

Baran 2018 (2)¶

Baran, M., Maskan, A., & Yaşar, Ş. (2018). Learning Physics through Project-Based Learning Game Techniques. International Journal of Instruction, 11(2), 221-234. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11215a

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Content analysis of self-evaluation form themes: presentation was coded 12 times as the most significant challenge, with excitement coded 9 times as the source of the problem; students most enjoyed teamwork and the implementation and preparation phases (coded 8 and 9).

"the students in the experimental group reported that the most challenging phase was the presentation, due to anxiety."
