### Warren et al. 2013

Jeffrey M. Warren, Edwin R. Gerler, Jr. (2013). Effects of School Counselors' Cognitive Behavioral Consultation on Irrational and Efficacy Beliefs of Elementary School Teachers. The Professional Counselor, 3(1), 6-15. https://doi.org/10.15241/jmw.3.1.6

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

In the same quasi-experimental study, the Teacher Sense of Efficacy Scale total score showed no significant group differences. Subscales for instructional strategies (F(2, 33) = .29, p = .752) and classroom management (F(2, 33) = .38, p = .685) also yielded no significant differences.

> "Results indicated no statistical significance across groups in terms of teacher sense of efficacy (TSE), F(2, 33) = 1.56, p = .225."
