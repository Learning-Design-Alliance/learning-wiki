# Scooter the Tutor was associated with a marginally significant reduction in observed gaming among middle-school math students

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` 33% of control students versus 18% of experimental students were observed gaming, a sizeable but only marginally significant reduction; gamers who remained did not game less. [→ Koedinger 2008](#koedinger-2008)

## Evidence

### Koedinger 2008

Koedinger, K., Aleven, V., Roll, I., & Baker, R. (2008). In vivo experiments on whether supporting metacognition in intelligent tutoring systems yields robust learning. https://learnlab.org/

`q3 · i?`

In vivo experiment within a year-long Cognitive Tutor middle-school curriculum, 51 students per condition, with quantitative field observations of gaming. The reduction was "only marginally significant"; no effect size is printed.

> "The Scooter intervention was associated with a sizeable, though only marginally significant, reduction in the frequency of observed gaming. 33% of students were seen gaming in the control condition (using quantitative field observations), while 18% of students were seen gaming in the experimental condition."
