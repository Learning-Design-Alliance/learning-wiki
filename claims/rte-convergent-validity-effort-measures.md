### Wise 2005

Wise, S. L., & Kong, X. (2005). Response Time Effort: A New Measure of Examinee Motivation in Computer-Based Tests. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, Montreal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED490203. Published version, Applied Measurement in Education: [doi:10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Convergent validity analysis correlating RTE scores with the Reported Effort subscale of the Student Opinion Scale and the Modified Caution Index person-fit statistic in the 472-examinee low-stakes testing sample. Correlations were significant, but "the three effort measures may not be measuring exactly the same construct."

> "RTE scores showed significant correlations with both self-reported effort and person fit. The magnitude of the correlations, however, suggests that the three effort measures may not be measuring exactly the same construct."
