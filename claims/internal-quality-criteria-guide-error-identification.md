### Jochem E. J. Aben 2024

Jochem E. J. Aben, Mayra Mascareño Lara, Anneke C. Timmermans, Filitsa Dingyloudi, Jan-Willem Strijbos. (2024). The impact of interpersonal perceptions on the process of dealing with errors while providing and processing peer-feedback on writing. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09660-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative thematic analysis of think-aloud recordings during feedback provision. Eight of the twelve students (Mary, James, John, Michael, David, Thomas, Daniel, Anthony) repeatedly used quality-criteria knowledge; the article states "the comparison with criteria contributed to the identification of errors" by offering a ground for evaluation.

> "During the provision of feedback, students compared the text with internal manifestations of text quality criteria."
