# Explainable three-layer LAD and visual-only LAD produced no significant difference in co-authored abstract quality

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` University students using the fully explainable LAD did not score significantly differently from visual-only LAD users on any of seven abstract-quality dimensions or on total score. [→ Angxuan Chen 2025](#angxuan-chen-2025)

## Evidence

### Angxuan Chen 2025

Angxuan Chen. (2025). When learning analytics dashboard is explainable: An exploratory study on the effect of GenAI-supported learning analytics dashboard. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.16312](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.16312)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests in the randomized experiment compared the experimental group (full three-layer LAD) with the control group (visual-only LAD) on seven rubric dimensions and total score. The article reports "no significant difference between the two groups (t(37) = -0.650, p = .520)" on total score, and "no statistically significant differences between the experimental group and the control group on any of the assessed dimensions".

> "Furthermore, the analysis of the total abstract scores (Sum Score) also revealed no significant difference between the two groups (t(37) = -0.650, p = .520)."
