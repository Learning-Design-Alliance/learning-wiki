## Evidence

### Yamada Masaaki 2019

Yamada Masaaki, Sekine Michikazu, Tatsuse Takashi, Asaka Yukiko. (2019). Association between lifestyle, parental smoke, socioeconomic status, and academic performance in Japanese elementary school children: the Super Diet Education Project. Environmental Health and Preventive Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12199-019-0776-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression in the cross-sectional survey of 1663 children showed "studying at home < 1 h (OR 1.82; 95% CI, 1.34–2.49)" significantly associated with low academic performance. The discussion notes late wakeup and short study duration had higher ORs than SES.

> "studying at home < 1 h (OR 1.82; 95% CI, 1.34–2.49), paternal smoke (OR 1.47; 95% CI, 1.12–1.92)"
