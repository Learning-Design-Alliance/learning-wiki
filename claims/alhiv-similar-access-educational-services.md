## Evidence

### Toska Elona 2019

Toska Elona, Cluver Lucie, Orkin Mark, Bains Anurita, Sherr Lorraine, Berezin McKenzie, Gulaid Laurie. (2019). Screening and supporting through schools: educational experiences and needs of adolescents living with HIV in a South African cohort. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-019-6580-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-sectional survey of N = 1063 adolescents living with HIV and N = 456 HIV-free community controls aged 10–19 in South Africa. Chi-square tests found no difference by HIV status in enrolment (94%), fee-free schools, or feeding schemes; the article reports these rates as "94, 30, and 92% respectively".

> "ALHIV reported accessing educational services (enrolment, free schools, school feeding schemes) at the same rates as other adolescents (94, 30, and 92% respectively), suggesting that school is a valuable site for identification."
