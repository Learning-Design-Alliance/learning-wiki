## Evidence

### Seth DeVore 2015

Seth DeVore, Alexandre Gauthier, Jeremy Levy, and Chandralekha Singh. (2015). Improving Students' Understanding of Lock-In Amplifiers. [doi:10.1119/1.4934957](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4934957)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Interview findings from professors and graduate students with prior lock-in experience, gathered to identify common difficulties before tutorial development. The article reports the oscilloscope-like output belief as a frequent misconception.

> "Students often believe that the lock-in's output should have a frequency equal to  that of the signal frequency. These students believe that the lock -in is providing an output similar to that which would be shown by an oscilloscope measuring the input signal."
