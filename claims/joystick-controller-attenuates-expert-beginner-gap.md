## Evidence

### Park Sunghae 2026

Park Sunghae, Oh Namkee, Rhu Jinsoo, Choi Gyu-Seong, Kim Jongman. (2026). Comparison of learning curve difference of laparoscopist according to controller type of flexible scope during laparoscopic liver resection. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12909-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Stratified analysis of 45 joystick-controlled procedures (25 expert, 20 beginner). The study reports no statistically significant operation-time difference and zero transfusions in both groups; equivalence was not tested, and no effect size is printed.

> "Operation time did not significantly differ between the two groups (174.0 [138.8–211.0] vs 124.0 [106.0–171.0] minutes, p = 0.059), and no transfusions occurred in either group (0.0% vs 0.0%)."
