### Darren Wong 2010

Darren Wong, Sng Peng Poo, Ng Eng Hock and Wee Loo Kang. (2010). Learning with multiple representations: an example of a revision lesson in mechanics. Submitted to IOP Publishing for peer review. [doi:10.1088/0031-9120/46/2/005](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9120/46/2/005)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom observation during the revision lesson: while explaining why velocity at the mid-point was closer to the initial value, a student thought "the velocity was decreasing at a decreasing rate", per the authors' report. This underscores "the need to teach students to discriminate between the slope and height of a graph".

> "This error in thinking persisted despite the teacher directing his attention to the v-t graph being linear. The student obviously was confused over the decrease in velocity with the rate of decrease."
