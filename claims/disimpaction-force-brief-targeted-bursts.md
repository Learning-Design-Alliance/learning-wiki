## Evidence

### Parris Dawn 2025

Parris Dawn, Xue Yang, Navaseelan Thurga, Salvadores Fernandez Carmen, Javidan Maryam, Gupta Rohit, Salaris Nikolaos, Chen Jeremy, Tiwari Manish K., Siassakos Dimitrios. (2025). Characterising Forces Applied During the Simulated Management of Impacted Fetal Head: Pre‐Clinical Methods Study. BJOG: An International Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/1471-0528.70107

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Sensorised-glove force data from 13 obstetricians on a Limbs and Things simulator; Table 1 medians show, for example, dominant-hand sensor 5 at 21.70 N maximum versus 5.01 N time-averaged, supporting that force was applied "in brief, targeted bursts rather than continuously".

> "The notable difference between maximum (Figure 5) and time-averaged (Figure 6) force values indicates that force during fetal disimpaction was applied in brief, targeted bursts rather than continuously."
