## Evidence

### Buljan Ivan 2018

Buljan Ivan, Jerončić Ana, Malički Mario, Marušić Matko, Marušić Ana. (2018). How to choose an evidence-based medicine knowledge test for medical students? Comparison of three knowledge measures. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1391-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Within-EBM-group comparison of percent improvements across the three tests (Fresno n = 35); Table 3 prints median improvements of 31% (95% CI 25–42%) for Fresno and 13% for ACE and Berlin.

> "post-test percent increase in the Fresno test scores was significantly higher than corresponding values on either the ACE or Berlin test (Friedman test for related samples, P = 0.001; post hoc tests P ≤ 0.010), while no difference was found between the ACE and Berlin tests (post hoc test, P = 0.999)."
