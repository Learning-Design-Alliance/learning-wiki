## Evidence

### Laninga-Wijnen 2023

Laninga-Wijnen, L., van den Berg, Y. H. M., Garandeau, C. F., Mulder, S., & de Castro, B. O. (2023). Does Being Defended Relate to Decreases in Victimization and Improved Psychosocial Adjustment Among Victims? Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000712

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Nonpreregistered exploratory analysis on the victims-only sample, run to check whether the large nonvictim proportion masked effects. The article reports this single exception: "defended victims had lower depressive symp toms at T2 compared with nondefended victims."

> "Results remained exactly the same, with one exception: defended victims had lower depressive symp- toms at T2 compared with nondefended victims ( B nondefended victim = .081, SE = .040, p = .041)."
