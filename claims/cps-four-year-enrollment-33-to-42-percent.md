Descriptive trend in college enrollment among CPS graduates from district and National Student Clearinghouse-based data, shown in Figure 2. The brief translates the rate increase into nearly 3,500 more graduates enrolling in 2014 than in 2006; no effect size is printed.

> "The 2014 four-year college enroll- ment rate among CPS high school graduates is 42 per- cent. As shown in Figure 2 , this is up from 33 percent in 2006. In addition, 20 percent of 2014 CPS gradu- ates enrolled in a two-year college, bringing the 2014 combined college enrollment rate to 62 percent."
