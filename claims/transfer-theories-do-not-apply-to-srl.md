# Theories of transfer do not apply to SRL because SRL is a dynamic process rather than static knowledge

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · theoretical `r?` · `q1`

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Transfer of knowledge literature focuses on what was learned, whereas transfer of SRL concerns how it was learned, as a dynamic, adaptive process. [→ McCardle 2015](#mccardle-2015)

## Evidence

### McCardle 2015

McCardle, L. (2015). Self-regulated learning in and across sport and academic domains [Doctoral dissertation, University of Victoria].

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r?`

Theoretical argument in the transfer chapter: transfer research has focused on static domain knowledge (e.g., the law of large numbers, convergence solutions), while "SRL is a dynamic process, continually adapted to different contexts", so investigating SRL transfer requires modified or extended transfer theories.

> "At the most basic level, theories of transfer do not apply to SRL because SRL is not knowledge. SRL is a dynamic process, continually adapted to different contexts."
