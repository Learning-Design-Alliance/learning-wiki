### Weaver Marcia R 2016

Weaver Marcia R, Pillay Erushka, Jed Suzanne L, de Kadt Julia, Galagan Sean, Gilvydis Jennifer, Marumo Eva, Mawandia Shreshth, Naidoo Evasen, Owens Tamara, Prongay Vickery, O'Malley Gabrielle. Sex Transm Infect. https://doi.org/10.1136/sextrans-2015-052107

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pre/post comparison of clinic-module STI knowledge scores among training-arm clinics (complete cases, two-sample t test). Scores rose "from 52.5% pre-test to 65.7% post-test"; increases were significant for each module and each arm's total score.

> "When all training methods were combined, average test scores increased from 52.5% pre-test to 65.7% post-test, an absolute increase of 13.2% (p<0.001)"
