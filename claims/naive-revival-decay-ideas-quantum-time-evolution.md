### Giacomo Zuccarini 2021

Giacomo Zuccarini, Massimiliano Malgieri. (2021). Modeling and Representing Conceptual Change in the Learning of Successive Theories: The Case of the Classical-Quantum Transition. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.06919](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.06919)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Interpretive review of upper-undergraduate-level findings on the concept of time evolution in the absence of measurement. The review states these ideas "appear to be unrelated to the mathematical structure of the theory" and suggests the decay idea might derive from exposure to popular science content.

> "Passante et al. (2015a) identified two naïve ideas on time evolution after a measurement: revival (the state returns to the form it had before the perturbation) and decay (from an excited state to the ground state)."
