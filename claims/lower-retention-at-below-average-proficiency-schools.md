# Proportionally fewer principals remained at lower-performing schools than at higher-performing schools across all three states (three-year retention)

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory West. https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/descriptive-study/principal-retention-patterns-arizona-nevada-and-utah

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-year retention analysis by spring 2019 test proficiency (figure 7) from state staffing data; spring 2020 tests were not administered due to COVID-19. The gap was "most pronounced in Arizona" at approximately 14–16 percentage points and least pronounced in Utah at 4–6 points.

> "All three states had lower three-year principal retention rates (fewer school stayers) from fall 2016 to fall 2019 in schools that had below-average proficiency rates on statewide standardized tests in math and English language arts (figure 7)."
