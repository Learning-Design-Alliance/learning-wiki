Xingbin Tian & Suksan Suppasetseree 2013¶

Xingbin Tian & Suksan Suppasetseree. (2013). Development of an Instructional Model for Online Task-Based Interactive Listening for EFL Learners. English Language Teaching, 6(3). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n3p30

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental comparison of two intact classes of 46 second-year non-English majors each, using ANCOVA to adjust for pre-existing listening ability. The ANCOVA table shows Classes F=91.488, Sig.=.000; post-test means were 75.57 (experimental) and 66.35 (control).
