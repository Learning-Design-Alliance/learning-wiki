## Evidence

### Park 2025

Park, A.T., Colantonio, J., Delgado Reyes, L., Sharp, S.D.S., Koepp, A.E., Bonawitz, E., & Mackey, A.P. (2025). Question asking practice fosters aspects of curiosity in science content in young children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00384-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Apothecary Task exploration measure after training: question-asking children opened fewer cued envelopes in the first minute (QA Mdn = 0 vs L Mdn = 2), marginally surviving FDR correction (pFDR = .05). Other exploration measures showed ceiling effects or no differences.

> "Controlling for race and age, the listening group opened more cued envelopes than the question asking group (Figure 2C, QA: Mdn = 0, L: Mdn = 2,  b = -1, 95% CI [-1.88,-.12], t = 2.27, p = .02,  pFDR = .05)"
