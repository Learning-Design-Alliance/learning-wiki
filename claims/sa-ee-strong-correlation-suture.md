Student self-assessment: feasibility, advantages and limitations; example of a workshop for trainee surgeons using a suture score. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08223-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlation analysis of 70 trainee doctors' scores found "a strong and significant correlation between SA and EE", with the total score most correlated (r = 0.789).

> "There was a strong and significant correlation between SA and EE (Fig. 1). The total score was the most correlated (r = 0.789; p < 0.0001). The checklist score was slightly more correlated between SA and EE (r = 0.715, p < 0.0001) than the OSATS score (r = 0.629, p < 0.0001)."
