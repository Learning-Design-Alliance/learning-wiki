## Evidence

### Ergezen Sahin Gizem 2025

Ergezen Sahin Gizem, Aras Bayram Gulay, Sanchez Sierra Alberto, Akdemir Simay, Kurc Dogukan, Tarakci Devrim, Tunali Ayse Nur. (2025). Effects of artificial intelligence based physiotherapy educational approach in developing clinical reasoning skills: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07926-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Single post-test (T2) Mini-CEX assessment by a blinded independent evaluator in the randomized trial; AI-PBL mean 38.0 ± 13.1 vs PBL 31.3 ± 18.5, with the non-significant difference (p = 0.236, d = 0.42) described as "ranging from small to moderate".

> "Mini-CEX scores, following the two-week intervention, were higher in the AI-supported PBL group compared to the traditional PBL group. Although the AI-PBL group outperformed the control group numerically, the between-group difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.236), with an effect size ranging from small to moderate (Cohen’s d = 0.42)."
