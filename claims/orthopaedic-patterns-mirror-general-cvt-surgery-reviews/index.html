Li Yibo 2024¶

Li Yibo, Chan Robert, Menon Matthew R.G., Ryan Joanna F., Mador Brett, Campbell Sandra M., Turner Simon R. (2024). Validity Evidence for Procedure-specific Competence Assessment Tools in Orthopaedic Surgery: A Scoping Review. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-23-00065

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative comparison in the discussion, attributing the pattern to the authors' companion scoping reviews of general surgery and cardiovascular and thoracic surgery tools (23 and eight tools identified). The review states "general surgery and CVT tools had similarly stronger evidence of content validity".

"Regarding validity evidence, general surgery and CVT tools had similarly stronger evidence of content validity and weaker evidence of response process and consequence, whereas orthopaedic surgery had stronger evidence in relation to other variables."
