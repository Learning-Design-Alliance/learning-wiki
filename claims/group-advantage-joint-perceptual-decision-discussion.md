# Two people discussing perceptual decisions can outperform the better individual, via weighted confidence sharing

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a visual odd-ball detection task, joint decisions after free discussion were significantly better than decisions of the better partner alone. [→ Frith Chris D 2012](#frith-chris-d-2012)

## Evidence

### Frith Chris D 2012

Frith Chris D. (2012). The role of metacognition in human social interactions. Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences. https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2012.0123

`q2 · i?`

A psychophysical experiment (Bahrami et al., as reviewed here) in which pairs judged which interval contained an odd-ball Gabor patch, discussed disagreements, and made joint decisions. The review reports a significant group advantage in psychometric slope over the better partner.

> "In terms of the slope of the psychometric function, the group decisions were significantly better than decisions of the better of the two partners (group advantage)."
