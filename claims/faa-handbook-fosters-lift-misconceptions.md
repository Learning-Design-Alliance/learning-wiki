`q1 · i?` · `design · r3`

The author's analysis of the FAA handbook's chapter on theories of lift argues its wording fosters misconceptions; the lift-always-upwards statement is only correct for horizontal steady flight, and lift exceeding weight is wrong for steady climb, banked, and descending flight.

> "Even though, strictly speaking, this statement is not wrong, it still fosters the misconcept that “aerodynamic lift always points upwards” and “lift must be bigger than weight during flight”."
