### Karakuş Akan 2014

Karakuş Akan, Duran Latif, Yavuz Yücel, Altintop Levent, Çalişkan Fatih. (2014). Computer-based simulation training in emergency medicine designed in the light of malpractice cases. BMC Medical Education, 14, 155. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-155

Per-case analysis in the same controlled study (Table 1 reports OR 4.05, 95% CI 1.21-13.54). Success required evaluating history, securing the scene, removing clothing, skin decontamination, respiratory support and antidotes; the study reports "62.5% and the success rate was 29.2%" (p = 0.020).

> "The success rate for students trained with simulation based training was 62.5% and the success rate was 29.2% for the traditionally trained students (p = 0.020)."
