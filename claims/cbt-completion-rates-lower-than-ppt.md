## Evidence

### Jayson M. Nissen 2018

Jayson M. Nissen, Manher Jariwala, Xochith Herrera, Eleanor W. Close, and Ben Van Dusen. (2018). Performance differences for in-class and online administration of low-stakes research-based assessments. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.044](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.044)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive completion rates reported in the Methods section of the 25-section experiment. Missing data was addressed with hierarchical multiple imputation (m=10 data sets) using the mice package in R, which the authors state maximizes statistical power relative to list-wise deletion.

> "Completion rates for the PPT condition were 94% for the pretest and 74% for the posttest and for the CBT were 68% for the pretest and 54% for the posttest."
