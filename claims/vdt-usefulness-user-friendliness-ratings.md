## Evidence

### Gerardi Davide 2025

Gerardi Davide, Torge Diana, Bernardi Sara, Bianchi Serena, Macchiarelli Guido. (2025). Use of two virtual dissection tables in a postgraduate oral anatomy course: feedback from the students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07840-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Anonymous survey of the entire cohort of 24 first-year postgraduate dental residents (7 oral surgery, 17 orthodontics), none of whom had prior VDT experience, delivered two weeks after the anatomy course. Ratings showed image quality was "considered very satisfying" and both VDTs were "considered mostly very user-friendly and useful".

> "The data survey showed that the quality of the digital images of both VDTs was considered very satisfying and that both VDTs were considered mostly very user-friendly and useful (Tables 1 and 2)."

### Gerardi Davide 2025 (2)

Gerardi Davide, Torge Diana, Bernardi Sara, Bianchi Serena, Macchiarelli Guido. (2025). Use of two virtual dissection tables in a postgraduate oral anatomy course: feedback from the students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07840-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Freeman-Halton extension of Fisher's exact test compared survey responses for the two VDTs on satisfaction and usefulness; the article reports the differences were "not statistically significant (p value > 0.05)". Equivalence between the tables was not tested beyond this null result.

> "Table 1 Data reporting the results regarding the evaluation of the VDTs satisfaction and the usefulness of the administered survey. The differences between the two VDTs were not statistically significant (p value > 0.05)"
