## Evidence

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2020

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2020). Student behavior and test security in online conceptual assessment. arXiv:1912.05698v1 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Behavioral log analysis of browser-focus events among 207 respondents. The study reports 124 students (60%) had at least one hidden focus event; the median duration of a loss-of-focus period was 32 seconds and 386 of 575 sustained periods (67%) were 1 minute or less.

> "Focus events were the most common events in the data set with a total of 124 (60% of 207) students with at least one browser focus event in which their RBA window became hidden for more than 4 seconds."

### Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock 2020 (2)

Bethany R. Wilcox and Steven J. Pollock. (2020). Student behavior and test security in online conceptual assessment. arXiv:1912.05698v1 [physics.ed-ph]. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.wilcox)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis of hidden-browser time versus RBA score. The article reports a small negative Spearman correlation (r = −0.1, p = 0.2) that was not statistically significant, indicating no significant association between time away and performance.

> "We selected the Spearman correlation because it is less sensitive to the presence of outliers than the other coefficients."
