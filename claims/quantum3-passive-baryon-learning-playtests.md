### Özkan 2019

Özkan, Tristan and Lin, Huey-Wen. (2019). Quantum 3: Learning QCD through Intuitive Play. The 36th Annual International Symposium on Lattice Field Theory - LATTICE2018. [doi:10.22323/1.334.0326](https://doi.org/10.22323/1.334.0326)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Informal playtesting at the Traverse City Film Festival, reported with no sample size or systematic measures, indicated learning beyond the intended passive design: "players are not only memorizing the formulae for baryons, but are able to predict how to form baryons they have not previously seen."

> "Although the player will never need to memorize, playtesting at the Traverse City Film Festival showed that players are not only memorizing the formulae for baryons, but are able to predict how to form baryons they have not previously seen."
