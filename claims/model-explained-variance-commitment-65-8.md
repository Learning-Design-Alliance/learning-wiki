Reported explained variances from the structural model of 358 supervisors; the authors describe the 65.8% figure for commitment as indicating "strong explanatory power". These are variance percentages, not standardised effect sizes.

> "The model accounted for 46.4% of the variance in psychological empowerment, 46.2% of the variance in professional competence, and 65.8% of the variance in professional commitment."
