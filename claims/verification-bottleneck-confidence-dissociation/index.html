The article reports, citing Fernandes et al. (2026) and Klingbeil et al. (2024), this dissociation as documented in recent work; the review attributes it to those studies, which were not read directly, and frames it as a failure of metacognitive monitoring.

"recent work shows that as reliance on AI increases, accuracy in detecting AI errors decreases—yet user confidence does not (Fernandes et al., 2026; Klingbeil et al., 2024)."
