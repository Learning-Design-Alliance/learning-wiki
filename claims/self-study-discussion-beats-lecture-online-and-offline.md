## Evidence

### Lim Jaeseo 2022

Lim Jaeseo, Ko Hyunwoong, Park Jooyong, Ihm Jungjoon. (2022). Effect of active learning and online discussions on the academic performances of dental students. BMC Medical Education, 22, 312. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03377-9

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Secondary comparative ANOVA pooling the present online data with Lim et al.'s offline dental student data: LD offline (n = 12), LD online (n = 21), SSD offline (n = 20), SSD online (n = 22). Bonferroni post hoc ranking placed SSD groups first for "the total scores", with LD online lowest.

> "A comparative analysis using ANOVA revealed that the groups had significant differences for the total scores, verbatim type items, and transfer type items; respectively, F (3, 71) = 21.13, P < 0.001, partial eta-squared = 0.472;"

### Lim Jaeseo 2022 (2)

Lim Jaeseo, Ko Hyunwoong, Park Jooyong, Ihm Jungjoon. (2022). Effect of active learning and online discussions on the academic performances of dental students. BMC Medical Education, 22, 312. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03377-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Bonferroni post hoc tests on the four-group offline-online comparison found no statistically significant modality differences within matched conditions; equivalence was not formally tested. For transfer items, SSD offline (M = 8.10) and SSD online (M = 8.05) both exceeded both LD groups, Ps < 0.05.

> "However, there were no significant differences between the SSD offline and online groups, and there were also no significant differences between the LD offline and online groups."
