## Evidence

### Magdaş and Bontea 2011

Ioana Magdaş, Timea Bontea. (2011). Developing digital competences using an educational software. A pedagogical research. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 4, Number 4, ISSN 2065-1430. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056072

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Grade distributions on Worksheet 1 (table operations exercises) after teaching with the software: EG had 18% at grades 9-10 versus CG 8%. The article's statement is descriptive; no significance test is reported for the worksheets.

> "Use of educational software for teaching and l earning of Access has led to better grades for the experimental group."

### Magdaş and Bontea 2011 (2)

Ioana Magdaş, Timea Bontea. (2011). Developing digital competences using an educational software. A pedagogical research. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 4, Number 4, ISSN 2065-1430. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056072

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Grade distributions on Worksheet 2 (queries of different difficulty): EG had 23% at grades 9-10 versus CG 8%, and only 4% of EG below 5 versus 8% of CG. No statistical test is printed for this comparison.

> "We can note that experimental class shows continue to grow and control class remains largely the same."
