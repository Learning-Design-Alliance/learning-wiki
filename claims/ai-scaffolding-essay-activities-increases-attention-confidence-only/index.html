Evidence¶

Jung Sun Sung · Wenhao David Huang 2026¶

Jung Sun Sung · Wenhao David Huang. (2026). Digital learning innovation: engineering students’ learning motivation for AI scaffolding. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10576-w

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Paired t-test comparing post-survey motivational means between conventional (Activity #2) and AI-featured (Activity #2.1) essay-writing activities among 103 engineering students. Significant differences found only in Confidence and Attention.

"the results of writing an essay using conventional research methods and AI-featured methods showed significant differences in participants’ motivation in ‘Confidence’ (p = .02) and ‘Attention’ (p < .001). Additionally, no significant difference in the participants’ motivation was observed in either the ‘Relevance’ or ‘Satisfaction’ categories."
