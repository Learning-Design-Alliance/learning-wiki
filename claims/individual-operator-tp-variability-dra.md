### Cheng X 2025

Cheng X, Zhang J, Huang G, Zhou J, Xiong J. (2025). Evaluation of the learning curve for ultrasound-guided distal radial artery cannulation in novices using a cumulative sum analysis: A prospective observational study. Medicine 2025;104:46(e45962). https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000045962

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subgroup analysis of individual CUSUM curves for the 3 residents (60 procedures each); the printed turning points differ across operators and across the three time measures, indicating individual variability around the pooled estimate.

> "Subgroup analysis revealed that the TP for the overall procedural time was 21, 17, and 22 for the 3 operators (Fig. 3a). The TP for identification time was 21, 17, and 23 (Fig. 3b), and the TP for cannulation time was 18, 11, and 22 (Fig. 3c)."
