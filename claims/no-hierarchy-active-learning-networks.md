## Evidence

### Adrienne L. Traxler 2020

Adrienne L. Traxler, Tyme Suda, Eric Brewe, and Kelley Commeford. (2020). Network positions in active learning environments in physics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020129](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020129)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Concluding discussion of whether students cluster exclusively around the highest-knowledge peers. The authors describe the star-like hierarchy pattern such exclusivity would produce and state that this pattern did not appear in their data; they argue a strong hierarchy is unlikely to be desirable since even initially high-scoring students benefit from collaborating.

> "If a block of students was systematically more popular under the “meaningful interaction” prompt, in an unreciprocated way, the reduced networks would show a more star-like structure with single-direction links go- ing to a central hub."
