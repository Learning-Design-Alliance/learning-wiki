## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh, University of Pittsburgh. (2016). When physical intuition fails. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06637](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06637)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative comparison of professor interviews and student recitation-quiz responses; the authors report students "seldom employed a syste matic approach" while such oversights were rare among professors, who visualized problems and examined limiting cases.

> "Individual discussion shows that students seldom employed a syste matic approach to problem solving and certain types of oversights common in student respons es, were rare in the response of pro- fessors."
