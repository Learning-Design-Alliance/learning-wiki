## Evidence

### Vundum Jonathon 2025

Vundum Jonathon, Day Fiona. (2025). Evaluating the Efficacy of Case-Based Learning for Medical Oncology Trainees: A Systematic Review. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-025-02651-w

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing three prior studies, that PBL appeared more effective than traditional teaching and was associated with higher scores for learning motivation, understanding, communication, clinical thinking, self-learning and teamwork skills, and that CBL increased knowledge and confidence. The review notes the latter conclusion was a non-significant trend and that overall RCT quality was unsatisfactory.

> "Note that the latter's conclusion was stated as a trend that was not statistically significant, possibly as a result of the small sample size (16) with 56% of participants in the intervention group not attending the planned CBL sessions. The overall quality of data from available RCTs was deemed unsatisfactory [25]."
