## Evidence

### Zhang Danqing 2022

Zhang Danqing, Hong Jintao, Chen Sitong, Liu Yang. (2022). Associations of physical activity with academic achievement and academic burden in Chinese children and adolescents: do gender and school grade matter? BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-022-13886-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 2653 Chinese students analysed with binary logistic regression adjusted for gender, grades, parents' education and SES. Meeting the guideline was associated with "a greater likelihood of reporting above-average AA (OR = 1.61, 95% CI: 1.23–2.11)"; the OR is an unstandardised odds ratio, so no effect size is printed.

> "Compared with those who did not meet the PA guidelines, children and adolescents who met the PA guidelines had a greater likelihood of reporting above-average AA (OR = 1.61, 95% CI: 1.23–2.11)."
