`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Narrative synthesis of six teacher-targeted trials (three mindfulness-based, two social-emotional trainings, one self-efficacy coaching). The review attributes significant teacher gains and more adaptive student self-management and social competence to two mindfulness trials, while Fedewa et al. (2022) and Moss Long (2017) reported insignificant findings with small samples.

> "de Car -valho et al. ( 2021) and Brown et al. ( 2023) showed that a mindfulness-based SEC intervention for primary educators produced significant gains in teacher mindfulness practices, compassion, self-efficacy, and SEL instruction."
