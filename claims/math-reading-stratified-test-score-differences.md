## Subclaims
`q2 i?` O-mT1D had lower overall math (−2.61) and reading (−1.10) scores than O-BP, and lower overall math scores than O-fT1D (p = 0.0496); no effect sizes were printed for these contrasts. [→ Spangmose Anne Lærke 2022](#spangmose-anne-lrke-2022)

## Evidence

### Spangmose Anne Lærke 2022

Spangmose Anne Lærke, Skipper Niels, Knorr Sine, Wullum Gundersen Tina, Beck Jensen Rikke, Damm Peter, Lykke Mortensen Erik, Pinborg Anja, Svensson Jannet, Clausen Tine. (2022). School performance in Danish children exposed to maternal type 1 diabetes in utero: A nationwide retrospective cohort study. PLoS Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1003977

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Secondary exploratory analyses stratified by topic and grade in the same cohort, adjusted per Model 3 and shown in Fig 1. The article reports "the overall math test score, which were lower for O-mT1D (p = 0.0496)", and overall math/reading differences versus O-BP of −2.61 and −1.10.

> "Neither math test scores nor reading test scores differed for O-mT1D versus O-fT1D except for the overall math test score, which were lower for O-mT1D (p = 0.0496) (Fig 1)."
