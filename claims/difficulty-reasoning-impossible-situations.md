## Subclaims
`q1 i?` A student refused to accept that the change in entropy of the working substance would be zero for a QL = 0 engine because he believed converting all heat from a single reservoir into work cannot occur in a cyclic process. [→ Trevor I. Smith 2015](#trevor-i-smith-2015)

## Evidence

### Trevor I. Smith 2015

Trevor I. Smith, Warren M. Christensen, Donald B. Mountcastle, and John R. Thompson. (2015). Identifying Student Diﬃculties with Entropy, Heat Engines, and the Carnot Cycle. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020116](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020116)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Videotaped classroom observation of a small group completing the Heat Engines tutorial at School 1 (N = 17 across two groups in three classes), in which one student resisted reasoning about a hypothetical QL = 0 engine despite a groupmate producing the desired violation-of-the-Second-Law argument. No effect size is printed.

> "Jake did not agree that the change in entropy of the working substance would be zero because he claimed that, in order to convert all of the heat from a single reservoir into work, one cannot use a cyclic process."
