Evidence¶

Jenny van Dongen et al 2018¶

Jenny van Dongen et al. (2018). DNA methylation signatures of educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 3:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0020-2

q2 · i3 · associational · r3

Correlation of published EWAS effect sizes (smoking meta-analysis, N = 16,932 sites present) with education meta-analysis effect sizes. For persistent maternal-smoking sites (144 sites significant at average age 6.8), correlation with education was r = −0.70; 13 of these were significant in the education meta-analysis.

"The correlation between effect sizes was r = −0.74 taking the education EWAS results without adjustment for smoking status, and r = −0.56 for the education EWAS with adjustment for smoking status"

Jenny van Dongen et al 2018 (2)¶

Jenny van Dongen et al. (2018). DNA methylation signatures of educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 3:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0020-2

q2 · i0 · associational · r3

Correlation of effect sizes from published EWASs of maternal plasma folate (419 CpGs) and 28-day fine particulate matter (1578 CpGs) with education effect sizes. Alcohol-intake effect sizes (344 sites) did not correlate significantly (r = 0.05, p = 0.3); five CpGs were significantly associated with both education and alcohol intake.

"Maternal plasma folate level 66 (N CpGs = 419, r = 0.11, p = 0.03, Fig. 4d) and 28-day average concentration of airborne fine particulate matter 67 (N CpGs = 1578, r = 0.06, p = 0.01, Fig. 4e) showed small positive correlations with educational attain- ment"
