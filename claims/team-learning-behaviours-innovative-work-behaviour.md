### Andreas Widmann 2025

Andreas Widmann, Regina H. Mulder. (2025). Change in team learning behaviours in work teams – A systematic review. Educational Research Review 49. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2025.100736

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of longitudinal studies examining team reflection, sharing and boundary crossing in relation to innovative work behaviour. The review reports "positive relationships between team reflection and innovative work behaviour" across three studies, and notes the cross-lagged panel model showed different relationships for different parts of innovative work behaviour.

> "The results of the studies indicate positive relationships between team reflection and innovative work behaviour (Bednall et al., 2014; Bednall & Sanders, 2017; Widmann et al., 2019)."
