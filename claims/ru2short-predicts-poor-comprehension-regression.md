## Subclaims
`q3 i1` In a multinomial logistic regression controlling for age, vocabulary, sex, maternal education, home language, bilingual status, SES, and ancestry PCs, READ1 RU2Short presence significantly predicted UPC membership (UGC vs. UPC OR = 0.6; 95% CI [0.38 0.95]). [→ Li 2018](#li-2018)

## Evidence

### Li 2018

Li, M., Malins, J. G., DeMille, M. M. C., Lovett, M. W., Truong, D. T., Epstein, K., Lacadie, C., Mehta, C., Bosson-Heenan, J., Gruen, J. R., Frijters, J. C. and the GRaD Study Consortium. (2018). A molecular-genetic and imaging-genetic approach to specific comprehension difficulties in children. npj Science of Learning 3:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0034-9

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Multinomial logistic regression predicting comprehension group membership (UPC reference), accounting for 19.7% of variance (Nagelkerke pseudo R2). Vocabulary (UGC vs. UPC OR = 5.28) and younger age were also significant predictors.

> "Controlling for all these factors, our target variable – the presence of READ1 RU2Short alleles – was also a signi ﬁcant predictor of having poor comprehension skills despite adequate word reading (OR = 0.6; 95% CI [0.38 0.95])."
