## Evidence

### Anwar 2019

Anwar, F. (2019). The effect of activity-based teaching techniques on student motivation and academic achievement. Journal of Education and Educational Development, 6(1), 154-170. https://journals.iobmresearch.com/index.php/JEED

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest control group experiment; 30 students randomly assigned (15 per group) with the same teacher teaching both groups. Independent samples t-test gave t = 4.65, df = 28, sig .000, described as "a significant difference between mean education achievement scores".

> "Independent samples t-test as seen in Table 1, revealed that there was a significant difference between mean education achievement scores of experimental and female control group students. The mean value indicated that participants from experimental group showed more achievement in post-test 15.6 while control group students scored 10.7."
