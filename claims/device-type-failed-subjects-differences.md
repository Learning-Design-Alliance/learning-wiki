## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among students who connect to the LMS, the number of failed subjects differs significantly by device type, with adjusted pairwise differences between tablets and PCs, smartphones and tablets, and smartphones and PCs. [→ Fernández-Soriano Francisco Luis 2019](#fernandez-soriano-francisco-luis-2019)

## Evidence

### Fernández-Soriano Francisco Luis 2019

Fernández-Soriano Francisco Luis, López Belén, Martínez-España Raquel, Muñoz Andrés, Cantabella Magdalena. (2019). Use of Computing Devices as Sensors to Measure Their Impact on Primary and Secondary Students' Performance. Sensors (Basel, Switzerland). https://doi.org/10.3390/s19143226

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis test with Bonferroni post-hoc adjustment on failed subjects by device type; the article reports "significant differences with a 95% confidence level for the number of failed subjects" across the three device-pair comparisons.

> "There are significant differences with a 95% confidence level for the number of failed subjects when comparing the use between tablets and PCs, between smartphones and tablets and between smartphones and PCs."
