The review's condition analysis identifies shared implementation conditions among positively-outcomed studies, listing "Artifact grounding: Tutoring and feedback anchored in students’ current code, failing tests, and assignment specifications" first, followed by quality assurance loops, graduated scaffolding, and AI literacy integration. This is a narrative co-occurrence analysis across the corpus, not a controlled comparison.
