Stock-Schröer B 2026¶

Stock-Schröer B, Edelhäuser F, Scheffer C, Schweigmann L, Homberg A. (2026). How does culinary medicine training impact the diet-related knowledge, skills and attitudes of undergraduate medical students in Germany?—A systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09580-2

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review (PRISMA 2020) of nine studies at six German medical schools, with two reviewers independently selecting studies and assessing quality using MMERSQI. The review reports that "All studies reported improvements in diet-related knowledge and/or more positive attitudes"; no pooled effect sizes were computed.

"Nine studies of six study sites met the inclusion criteria. All interventions were elective modules integrated into clinical education. All studies reported improvements in diet-related knowledge and/or more positive attitudes toward nutrition counselling in clinical practice."
