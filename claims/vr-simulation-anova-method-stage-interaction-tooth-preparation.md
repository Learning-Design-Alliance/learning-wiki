## Evidence

### Kızılırmak Burcu 2025

Kızılırmak Burcu, Gözen Mehmet, Güntekin Neslihan. (2025). Evaluation of dental students’ tooth preparation skills using conventional phantom labs versus advanced simulation technologies: a comparative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08020-x

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial of 40 second-year dental students assigned to conventional phantom or VR simulation training (10 h each), scored on five criteria. The ANOVA printed η²p = 0.044 for Methods, 0.385 for Stage, and 0.052 for the "interaction effect between method and stage (Methods × Stage)"; criteria effects were not significant (p >.05).

> "According to the results of the Three-Way ANOVA (Table 1), there were statistically significant main effects for the training method (Methods) and stage (Stage) (p <.001). In addition, the interaction effect between method and stage (Methods × Stage) was also statistically significant (p <.001)."
