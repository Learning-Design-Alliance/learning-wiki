How Arizona's Dropout Crisis Affects Communities 2014¶

How Arizona's Dropout Crisis Affects Communities, Creates Economic Losses for the State of Arizona. (2014). A Project of WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559628

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Section 5.4's sensitivity analysis is an authors' interpretive argument, not a tested result. It lists excluded impacts including "broader health and psychological costs to inactivity", family repercussions, community crime losses, and broader costs of deep inequality.

"Importantly, our economic calculations exclude some key impacts either because there is insufficient data on their direct association with youth characteristics or because the impacts cannot be easily expressed in money terms."
