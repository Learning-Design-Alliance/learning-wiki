Described classroom exercise using a stack of 16 years of ESO VLT images of the galactic centre; students fit an ellipse to S2's orbit and invert Kepler's law to get M = 4π²a³/GT². The article reports "the obtained results are close to the 4 million solar masses". No effect size or comparative test is printed.

> "As an example, the S2 star has a revolution period of 14 years, and the obtained results are close to the 4 million solar masses."
