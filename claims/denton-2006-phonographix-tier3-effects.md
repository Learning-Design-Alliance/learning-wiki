# In Denton et al. (2006), a Tier 3 Phono-Graphix intervention for grades 1–3 inadequate responders produced significantly better performance than inadequate-responder controls on all reading measures

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Inadequate responders receiving the Tier 3 Phono-Graphix intervention significantly outperformed inadequate responders in the control condition on all measures of reading, with effects up to d = 1.77. [→ Austin 2017](#austin-2017)

## Evidence

### Austin 2017

Austin, C. R., Vaughn, S., & McClelland, A. M. (2017). Intensive Reading Interventions for Inadequate Responders in Grades K–3: A Synthesis. Learning Disability Quarterly, 40(4), 191–210. https://doi.org/10.1177/0731948717714446

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Quasi-experimental study within the synthesis (Table 6): grades 1–3 inadequate responders received Phono-Graphix in a 2:1 ratio, two 50-min sessions 5×/week for 8 weeks, versus typical school instruction. Effects included "WJ-III Word Attack: d = 1.77" and WJ-III Passage Comprehension d = 0.37.

> "The Tier 3 intervention produced positive effects, with the inadequate responders who received Tier 3 intervention performing significantly better than the inadequate responders who were assigned to the control condition on all measures of reading"
