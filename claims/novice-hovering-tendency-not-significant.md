## Evidence

### Matsumoto Shiro 2024

Matsumoto Shiro, Kawahira Hiroshi, Fukata Kyohei, Doi Yasunori, Kobayashi Nao, Hosoya Yoshinori, Sata Naohiro. (2024). Laparoscopic distal gastrectomy skill evaluation from video: a new artificial intelligence-based instrument identification system. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-63388-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kernel density estimation of distance traveled per second showed the two groups reversed after 120 pixels. The authors interpret hovering as "dwell time" indicating instrument hovering due to confusion. No effect size is printed; the finding is a non-significant tendency.

> "the ratio (ratio of time below 120/120–400) was 0.419 for the Expert group vs. 0.500 for the Novice group, indicating that the Novice group tended to have longer hovering tip motion time with a distance traveled in 1 s below 120 pixels (Fig. 5b, P = 0.133, Mann–Whitney U-test)."
