Authors' stated limitation: in this study both formats presented the same quantitative physics questions, with contexts and presentations identical except that numerical choices were removed from the free-response format. The article notes students' responses to similar problems can be context-dependent.

> "It is possible that if there is more variation between the MC and the corresponding FR questions, the good correlations exhibited in our study may not be found."
