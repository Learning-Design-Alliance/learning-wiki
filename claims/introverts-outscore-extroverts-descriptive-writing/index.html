In the same 2x2 factorial experiment, the 40 students classified as introvert by a personal-traits questionnaire had a mean of "73.600±12.68" against "66.28±10, 87" for the 40 extrovert students; the ANOVA reported Fobserved 4,90 exceeding Ftable 3,97 as significant.
