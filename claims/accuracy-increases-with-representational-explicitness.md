## Evidence

### Critten 2006

Critten, S., Pine, K., & Steffler, D. (2006). Spelling Development in Young Children: A Case of Representational-Redescription? Manuscript submitted for publication (Date of Submission: Thursday 3rd August 2006). https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.1.207

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVAs across the four occupied representational levels (E1A-E3) in the 51-child sample were significant for both production and recognition accuracy; Scheffé tests located production differences between E1A and E1B and between E2 and E3.

> "One-way ANOVAs were significant for both the mean number of words spelled correctly (F (3, 47) = 18.36 p < .05) and the mean number of words recognized correctly (F (3, 47)= 11.12 p < .05)"
