## Evidence

### Wang Chris 2020

Wang Chris, Daniel Ben Kei, Asil Mustafa, Khwaounjoo Prashanna, Cakmak Yusuf Ozgur. (2020). A Randomised Control Trial and Comparative Analysis of Multi-Dimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62855-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations between anatomy test 1 scores and CBS psychometric subscales within the text-only group found a significant positive correlation between nominal question scores and attention scores, r = 0.572, p = 0.016; correlational, so no causal claim.

> "A significant positive correlation was found within the text-only group between students' nominal type questions scores and their attention scores (r = 0.572, p = 0.016)."

### Wang Chris 2020 (2)

Wang Chris, Daniel Ben Kei, Asil Mustafa, Khwaounjoo Prashanna, Cakmak Yusuf Ozgur. (2020). A Randomised Control Trial and Comparative Analysis of Multi-Dimensional Learning Tools in Anatomy. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62855-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Within the MR group, Pearson correlation between anatomy test 1 spatial question scores and spatial short-term memory scores was significant and positive, r = 0.609, p = 0.006; exploratory within-group correlation, no causal inference.

> "Another significant positive correlation was identified within the MR group between students' spatial type questions scores and their spatial short-term memory scores (r = 0.609, p = 0.006)."
