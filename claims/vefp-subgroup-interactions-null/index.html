Styles 2014¶

Styles, B., Stevens, E., Bradshaw, S., & Clarkson, R. (2014). Vocabulary Enrichment Intervention Programme Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN67513320/

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Pre-specified subgroup analysis in the pupil-randomised trial, testing moderation of the intervention effect by pre-test score in a regression model equivalent to the primary analysis. The report states the interaction "was not significant (p=0.47)".

"The interaction between intervention and pre-test score was tested in a model equivalent to that used in the primary analysis but including the interaction term. The interaction was not significant (p=0.47)."

Styles 2014 (2)¶

Styles, B., Stevens, E., Bradshaw, S., & Clarkson, R. (2014). Vocabulary Enrichment Intervention Programme Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.controlled-trials.com/ISRCTN67513320/

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Pre-specified subgroup analysis testing moderation by National Curriculum reading level, using a dummy variable for Level 3 (default Level 4/5) and the interaction term in a model equivalent to the primary analysis; the printed result is p=0.91.

"The interaction was not significant (p=0.91)."
