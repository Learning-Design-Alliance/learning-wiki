## Evidence

### Noll C 2026

Noll C, Mikuteit M, Golon S, Louzek C, Afshar K, Just I, Steffens S. (2026). Feasibility and implementation of a final year Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) in a medical model curriculum. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10350-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Psychometric analysis of the 15 examination circuits in the June 2024 PJ-OSCE. Reliability was assessed using Cronbach's alpha, with a mean of "0.84, SD 0.06" and one circuit at "α = 0.68" attributed to ceiling effects rather than blueprint deficiency.

> "With one exception, the calculated reliability (Cronbach's alpha) of the circuits exceeded 0.80 (mean 0.84, SD 0.06). The lower coefficient in Circuit 3 (α = 0.68) reflects ceiling effects in leniently rated communication items and a narrow spread of candidate ability within this circuit rather than a deficiency of the station blueprint."
