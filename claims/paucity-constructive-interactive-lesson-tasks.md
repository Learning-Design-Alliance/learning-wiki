# In observed Australian secondary classrooms, only a minority of lesson tasks stimulated Constructive or Interactive engagement

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Vosniadou 2024

Vosniadou, S., Bodner, E., Stephenson, H., Jeffries, D., Lawson, M. J., Darmawan, I. G. N., Kang, S., Graham, L., & Dignath, C. (2024). The promotion of self-regulated learning in the classroom: a theoretical framework and an observation study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09374-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational coding of 107 lesson tasks from filmed lessons in 23 Australian classrooms using the ICAP-CG. The article reports that "Only 26% of these tasks involved stimulation of Constructive and Interactive student cognitive engagement," with the remaining tasks Passive, Active, or Active/Collaborative.

> "We identified 107 lesson tasks in the entire sample. The range of lesson tasks identified in each observation was from one to eight, but 62% of the teachers used between five to seven lesson tasks. Only 26% of these tasks involved stimulation of Constructive and Interactive student cognitive engagement."

### Vosniadou 2024 (2)

Vosniadou, S., Bodner, E., Stephenson, H., Jeffries, D., Lawson, M. J., Darmawan, I. G. N., Kang, S., Graham, L., & Dignath, C. (2024). The promotion of self-regulated learning in the classroom: a theoretical framework and an observation study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09374-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Minute-by-minute engagement coding across the full 898 minutes of observed lesson time showed teachers mostly in Passive and Active modes; the student profile was "closely similar" to the teachers' profile.

> "The teachers were assigned 612 min or 67% of the total observation time in the Passive and Active engagement codes and 279 min, or 31% of the total observation time in the combined Constructive and Interactive codes."
