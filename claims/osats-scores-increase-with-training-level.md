## Evidence

### Oquendo Yousi A. 2017

Oquendo Yousi A., Riddle Elijah W., Hiller Dennis, Blinman Thane A., Kuchenbecker Katherine J. (2017). Automatically rating trainee skill at a pediatric laparoscopic suturing task. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5873-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of blinded expert ratings across the 63 recorded trials, grouped by training level (12 medical student, 41 resident, 10 fellow trials). All medical student trials scored 1 or 2 and all fellow trials scored 3 or 4, which the authors treat as validation of the ratings.

> "The mean and standard deviation rounded average OSATS scores were 1.33 ± 0.49 for medical students, 2.93 ± 0.49 for residents, and 3.60 ± 0.52 for fellows."
