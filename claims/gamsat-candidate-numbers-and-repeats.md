## Evidence

### Mercer Annette 2015

Mercer Annette, Crotty Brendan, Alldridge Louise, Le Luc, Vele Veronica. (2015). GAMSAT: A 10-year retrospective overview, with detailed analysis of candidates’ performance in 2014. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0316-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal demographic analysis of ACER data 2005–2014, presented graphically (Figures 1–3). The article reports score differences between first and subsequent attempts are "relatively small (about 4 points)" with little upward trend.

> "From 2005 to 2014 the number of candidates sitting GAMSAT each year has increased from 3184 to 9307. These numbers include repeat takers, who have increased from 31% of the cohort in 2005 to 45% in 2014."
