### Şimşek 2016

İrfan Şimşek. (2016). The Effect of 3D Virtual Learning Environment on Secondary School Third Grade Students' Attitudes toward Mathematics. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 15(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1106361

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest study of 28 secondary third-grade students taught with the Second Life Mathematics Robot; Wilcoxon signed-rank test on total Mathematics Attitude Scale scores showed "no significant difference" (z=0.25, p>.05).

> "According to the test results in Table 1, it is noted that there is no significant difference between the answers given to the questions in attitude scale before and after the SL learning application  (z=0.25, p>.05)."
