The review reports this finding, citing Haraldsrud and Odden (2025), as qualitative classroom evidence that augmentation depends on preserved learner agency. No effect size is printed; the review presents it as exploratory empirical evidence.

> "Haraldsrud and Odden (2025) found that GenAI could function as a self- scaffold when students retained executive control, tested ideas, and integrated outputs with their own models; it became unproductive when reasoning was outsourced or when outputs exceeded student understanding."
