`q2 i?` Some dental studies found significant relationships between total UCAT score and first-year assessments while others found no significant relationship, and subtest findings were similarly inconsistent. [→ Bala Laksha 2021](#bala-laksha-2021)

## Evidence

### Bala Laksha 2021

Bala Laksha, Pedder Stephen, Sam Amir H., Brown Celia. (2021). Assessing the predictive validity of the UCAT—A systematic review and narrative synthesis. Medical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1080/0142159X.2021.1998401

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative synthesis of the dental-outcomes section: only five of 24 studies reported dental findings. Lala et al. found DA subtest correlations (r = 0.203 and r = 0.179, p < 0.05); McAndrew et al. found no correlation with first-year performance.

> "Significant findings were inconsistent across the 5 studies, with some demonstrating a significant relationship between total UCAT score and first-year assessments (Lambe et al. 2018) and others findings no significant relationship (McAndrew et al. 2017)."
