# Cumulative aerobic fitness is associated with exam performance: students highly fit in all three junior high years score higher than those highly fit for fewer years across all five subjects

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Across five academic subjects, exam scores rose with the number of years students were classified highly fit (3F > 2F > F3/1F > 0F), e.g. in math F(4, 382,251) = 721.50, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.007. [→ Hsieh Shu-Shih 2019](#hsieh-shu-shih-2019)

## Evidence

### Hsieh Shu-Shih 2019

Hsieh Shu-Shih, Tsai Jia-Ren, Chang Shao-Hsi, Ho Jen-Yu, Chen Jui-Fu, Chen Po-Hsi, Sung Yao-Ting, Hung Tsung-Min. (2019). The subject-dependent, cumulative, and recency association of aerobic fitness with academic performance in Taiwanese junior high school students. BMC Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-018-1384-4

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Nationwide observational cohort matching three years of Ministry of Education fitness records with BCTJH exam scores for 382,259 students; ANCOVAs with sex, BMI and urbanization covariates. "the effect of Group was significant" with ηp2 = 0.007 for math; the 3F group averaged 56.5 vs 50.8 for the 0F group (d = 0.29).

> "With respect to math, the effect of Group was significant, F(4, 382,251) = 721.50, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.007. Pairwise comparisons showed that the 3F, 2F, F3, and 1F groups had higher scores than the 0F group."
