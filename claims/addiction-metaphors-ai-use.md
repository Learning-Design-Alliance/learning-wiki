`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Results on addiction metaphors: in the interview study, "several participants spontaneously described their experiences using the language of addiction," and students "did not describe addiction as enjoyment or engagement, but rather as a loss of control over their own behavior."

> "To make sense of these cycles, several participants spontaneously described their experiences using the language of addiction. The word ‘addiction’ was never introduced by interviewers."
