Evidence¶

Claudia Mazziotti 2019¶

Claudia Mazziotti, Nikol Rummel, Anne Deiglmayr and Katharina Loibl. (2019). Probing boundary conditions of Productive Failure and analyzing the role of young students' collaboration. npj Science of Learning 4:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0041-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Two-factor ANOVA in a quasi-experimental classroom study of 228 fifth graders learning equivalent fractions, with timing of instruction (PF vs. DI) and social form of learning as factors. The timing effect was nonsignificant, "ηp² = 0.005", with highly overlapping CIs; Hypothesis 1 was rejected.

"No signi ﬁcant effects emerged, neither for the timing of instruction ( F[1, 224] = 1.01, p = 0.32, ηp² = 0.005, PF: 95% con ﬁdence interval (CI) [9.75, 11.5] and DI: 95% CI [9.78, 11.91])"
