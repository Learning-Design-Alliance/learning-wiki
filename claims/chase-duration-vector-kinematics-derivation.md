## Evidence

### O. I. Chashchina 2009

O. I. Chashchina, Z. K. Silagadze. (2009). The dog-and-rabbit chase problem as an exercise in introductory kinematics. https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.3293

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Analytical derivation (Section 2) using the vector relation r2 - r1 = k(t)V and the condition V · dV/dt = 0 for constant speed; the article states "we easily ﬁnd T from (7)" yielding the printed closed-form duration.

> "At the time t = T , when the dog catches the rabbit and the chase terminates, we must have x = uT , and remembering that k(T ) = 0 we easily ﬁnd T from (7): T = LVV 2 − u2 ."
