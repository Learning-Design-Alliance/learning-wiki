Ji Hye Shin & Peggy Albers 2015¶

Ji Hye Shin & Peggy Albers. (2015). An Analysis of the Effect of a Cyber Home Learning System on Korean Secondary School Students' English Language Achievement and Attitude. TESL Canada Journal/Revue TESL du Canada, 32(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083964

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Post-experiment survey of the experimental group (73 respondents, six excluded; α = .85) on perceptions of CHLS. The article reports "Almost half (49.25%) of the respondents thought the English content was slightly or very difficult."

"Almost half (49.25%) of the respondents thought the English content was slightly or very difficult. This response indicates nearly half of the students were not satisfied with the difficulty level of the content that they were asked to use for self-study."
