## Evidence

### Brumpt Eléonore 2023

Brumpt Eléonore, Bertin Eugénie, Tatu Laurent, Louvrier Aurélien. (2023). 3D printing as a pedagogical tool for teaching normal human anatomy: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04744-w

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes to Ye et al.'s meta-analysis that "post-test results of students who received instruction incorporating 3DPAMs were better than those who received instruction in 2D or on a cadaver"; it notes Ye did not differentiate models by complexity.

> "In their first meta-analysis, Ye and colleagues affirm that the post-test results of students who received instruction incorporating 3DPAMs were better than those who received instruction in 2D or on a cadaver [10]."

### Brumpt Eléonore 2023 (2)

Brumpt Eléonore, Bertin Eugénie, Tatu Laurent, Louvrier Aurélien. (2023). 3D printing as a pedagogical tool for teaching normal human anatomy: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04744-w

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports Ye's meta-analysis of 27 articles found "a significant difference in test results in favour of 3DPAMs versus conventional models for medical students but not for residents"; no effect size is printed.

> "Of the 27 articles included in their study, there was a significant difference in test results in favour of 3DPAMs versus conventional models for medical students but not for residents."

### Brumpt Eléonore 2023 (3)

Brumpt Eléonore, Bertin Eugénie, Tatu Laurent, Louvrier Aurélien. (2023). 3D printing as a pedagogical tool for teaching normal human anatomy: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04744-w

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

The review attributes to Salazar et al.'s meta-analysis the conclusion that "the use of 3DPAMs specifically improves the understanding of complex anatomical structures"; no effect size is printed.

> "In their meta-analysis, Salazar and colleagues conclude that the use of 3DPAMs specifically improves the understanding of complex anatomical structures [17]."
