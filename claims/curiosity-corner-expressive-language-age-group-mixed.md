### Curiosity Corner 2009

Quasi-experimental study (Chambers et al., 2001) analyzing MSEL Expressive and Receptive Language scales separately for 3-year-olds in private centers and 4-year-olds in public schools. The authors' reported effect size of 0.36 for 3-year-olds met WWC's substantively important threshold, but WWC calculations found it not statistically significant.
