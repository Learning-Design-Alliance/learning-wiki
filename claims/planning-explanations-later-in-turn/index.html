Qualitative synthesis in the review's results, building on its reassurance finding. Examples include a preceptor confirming a discharge-process answer before telling the novice to proceed, and a surgeon discussing steps beyond the current one ('let's do irrigation, and let's do bipolar').

"experts teaching computer skills were more likely to provide explanations in the second half of the ‘turn’, e.g. after the initial step or action has been completed."
