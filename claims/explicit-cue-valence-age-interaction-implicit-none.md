## Evidence

### Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck 2023

Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck. (2023). Effects of positive and negative social feedback on motivation, evaluative learning, and socio-emotional processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00178-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Explicit cue rating task appended to the SID-Video task (scale −10 to 10). Post-hoc tests showed positive vs neutral (p = 0.0001) and positive vs negative (p = 0.0001) differences; exploratory tests found the positive–neutral (p = 0.0106) and positive–negative (p = 0.0150) differences were significantly larger in young adults.

> "The 2 × 3 mixed design permutation F-test (age × condition) showed a signi ﬁcant main effect of condition ( p = 0.0001; Fig. 4a) and a signi ﬁcant interaction of the two factors ( p = 0.0082; Fig. 4b), but no signi ﬁcant main effect of age."

### Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck 2023 (2)

Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck. (2023). Effects of positive and negative social feedback on motivation, evaluative learning, and socio-emotional processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00178-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same SID-Video study, implicit measure. The authors interpret the explicit-only age effect within a dual-process framework, with explicit ratings influenced by emotion regulation strategies and implicit measures reflecting automatic responses.

> "The 2 × 2 mixed design permutationF-test (age x condition) on D-scores from the SC-IAT showed a signiﬁcant main effect of condition (p = 0.0042; Fig. 5) but no signi ﬁcant main effect of age and no signi ﬁcant interaction."
