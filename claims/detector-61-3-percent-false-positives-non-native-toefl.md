The review reports Liang et al.'s controlled benchmark of seven detectors on authentic TOEFL essays, finding a "mean false-positive rate of 61.3%" for non-native-authored essays against far better recognition of native-speaker comparators. No effect size is printed.

> "Across seven detectors, authentic TOEFL essays written by non-native English speakers were misclassified at a mean false-positive rate of 61.3%, whereas native-speaker comparator essays were recognised far more reliably."
