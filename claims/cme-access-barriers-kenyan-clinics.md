### Hooft Anneka M. 2017

Hooft Anneka M., Ripp Kelsey, Ndenga Bryson, Mutuku Francis, Vu David, Baltzell Kimberly, Masese Linnet N., Vulule John, Mukoko Dunstan, LaBeaud A. Desiree. (2017). Principles, practices and knowledge of clinicians when assessing febrile children: a qualitative study in Kenya. Malaria Journal. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12936-017-2021-7

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme 5 finding from interviews with 20 Kenyan clinicians. One clinician reported weekly CME attempts depending on ward burden, and one had not yet done IMCI training despite its use at the site. Clinicians cited workload relative to staff numbers as limiting both care and training attendance.

> "Clinicians at all of the sites said continuing medical education (CME) was provided and available in their area, but many could not access it due to time constraints, distance, and understaffing."
