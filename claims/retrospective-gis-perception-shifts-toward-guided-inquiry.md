## Evidence

### Talafian 2024

Talafian, H., Stelzer, T., Lundsgaard, M., Mahmood, M., & Kuo, E. (2024). Teachers' Experiences with Implementing Open-ended Labs in High School Physics Classes. University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Department of Physics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective paired-sample t-test of the adapted Guided Inquiry Survey administered in June 2023 to teachers in the program one to three years; all three constructs differed "at a p < .001 level," with teachers valuing open-ended inquiry labs more after participating.

> "Paired sample t-test results reveal a difference in teachers' value of guided inquiry labs, limitations of cook-book style labs, and implementation issues of guided inquiry labs during their engagement with the program for these physics teachers at a p < .001 level."

### Talafian 2024 (2)

Talafian, H., Stelzer, T., Lundsgaard, M., Mahmood, M., & Kuo, E. (2024). Teachers' Experiences with Implementing Open-ended Labs in High School Physics Classes. University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Department of Physics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275)

`q2 · i3` · `qualitative · r2`

Table 4 of the retrospective GIS survey with 14 teachers prints for the value-of-guided-inquiry construct a pre-test mean of 3.43 (0.92) and post-test mean of 4.52 (0.37), t = 4.84, p < .001, d = 1.3.

> "Paired Sample T-test Results of GIS Survey with 14 Teachers Mean and Standard Deviation Pre-test (Before joining IPaSS) Post-test (After joining IPaSS) t p d Value of Guided Inquiry 3.43 (0.92) 4.52 (0.37) 4.84 <.001 1.3"
