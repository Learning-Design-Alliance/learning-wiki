Thematic synthesis finding across included SICP/SICG articles: the review reports that guide use ranges from basic reading to advanced variation, clinical judgement, and creativity, and that "Using the guide effectively is a competence."

"Consequently, clinicians must find a balance between using the structure of the SICG and showing flexibility, 63 for example by adjusting formulations to suit the patient."
