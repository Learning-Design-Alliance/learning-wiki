## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gu Xiaoxia 2024

Gu Xiaoxia, Hassan Norlizah Che, Sulaiman Tajularipin, Wei Zhixia, Dong Jingyi. (2024). The impact of video game playing on Chinese adolescents’ academic achievement: Evidence from a moderated multi-mediation model. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0313405

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Moderated mediation Model 5 in the PROCESS macro on the 824-student CEPS sample. The interaction term was significant, indicating parent-child relationship "enhanced the negative relationship" between playing and achievement.

> "the interaction term between video game playing and parent-child relationship had a significant impact on academic achievement (β = -0.050, p < 0.05). It means that the parent-child relationship enhanced the negative relationship between video game playing and academic achievement"

### Gu Xiaoxia 2024 (2)

Gu Xiaoxia, Hassan Norlizah Che, Sulaiman Tajularipin, Wei Zhixia, Dong Jingyi. (2024). The impact of video game playing on Chinese adolescents’ academic achievement: Evidence from a moderated multi-mediation model. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0313405

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simple slope analysis plotted in Fig 2 for the same moderated mediation model. The negative association held only at high parent-child relationship; at low relationship levels the slope was non-significant, so equivalence was not tested.

> "among adolescents with high parent-child relationship, there was a somewhat strong correlation between video game playing and academic achievement (b = -9.227, p < 0.01), but not for adolescents with low parent-child relationship (b = -1.735, p = 0.502 > 0.05)"
