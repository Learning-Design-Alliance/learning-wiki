# In one uncontrolled pre-post study, students' optimal restoration choices for the case changed: full crown fell from 47.96% to 9.18% while post and core crown rose from 16.33% to 70.41%, both p < 0.001

## Evidence

### Jia Xiaoshi 2025

Jia Xiaoshi, Guo Jiawen, Wu Maodan, Wang Xiaodong, Fan Le, Yao Yichen, Deng Xiaoyi, Yang Ling. (2025). Study on the effect of simulation-based case teaching method on the preclinical teaching of tooth defects restoration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07010-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Question 3 of the before-and-after Case 1 comparison tested understanding of restoration indications and prognosis. Table 3 prints post and core crown 16.33% to 70.41% (p < 0.001), full crown 47.96% to 9.18% (p < 0.001), veneer 35.71% to 17.35% (p = 0.003); resin filling did not change significantly (p = 0.184).

> "Less students considered the plans of veneer or full crown combined with resin filling were best. Most students preferred post and core crown as the best restoration in this case after SCT (Fig. 6, Table 3)."

### Jia Xiaoshi 2025 (2)

Jia Xiaoshi, Guo Jiawen, Wu Maodan, Wang Xiaodong, Fan Le, Yao Yichen, Deng Xiaoyi, Yang Ling. (2025). Study on the effect of simulation-based case teaching method on the preclinical teaching of tooth defects restoration. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07010-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive finding from the same optimal-restoration comparison: "Two new answers were given after SCT", and bleaching as a best option rose from 8.16% to 19.39% (p = 0.018), which the authors link to improved aesthetic effect.

> "Two new answers were given after SCT: post combined with resin filling and teeth bleaching combined with resin filling (Fig. 6)."
