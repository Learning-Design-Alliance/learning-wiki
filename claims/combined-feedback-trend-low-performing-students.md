`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Separate regression analysis of lower-performing students (bottom 20% on initial practice exams). The article reports a trend that Condition 4 might provide additional benefit (β = 0.049, p = 0.067); the overall model did not reach significance (F = 2.48, p = 0.059), and no effect size was printed.

> "While the overall model approached but did not
reach statistical significance (𝐹 = 2.48,𝑝 = 0.059), there was a
trend suggesting that Condition 4 (AI + textbook) might provide
additional benefit compared to baseline ( 𝛽 = 0.049,𝑝 = 0.067)."
