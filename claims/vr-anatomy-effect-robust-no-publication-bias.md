## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Zhao Jingjie 2020

Zhao Jingjie, Xu Xinliang, Jiang Hualin, Ding Yi. (2020). The effectiveness of virtual reality-based technology on anatomy teaching: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled studies. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1994-z

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Sensitivity analysis of the pooled primary outcome, shown in Fig. 5, verifying reliability given heterogeneity above 75%. The funnel plot was symmetrical and Begg's test showed non-significant asymmetry (p = 0.54).

> "When any research was removed from the model, the significant results of the VR effect on examination scores were unchanged in the models (SMD = 0.53, 95% CI: 0.01–1.07)"
