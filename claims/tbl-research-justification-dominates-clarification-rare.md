# Justification studies dominate TBL research (52%) while clarification studies remain rare (15%) and never catch up over time

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Cleland JA 2026

Cleland JA, Pienkowska A, Kitto SC. (2026). Endless justification: A scoping review of team‐based learning research in medical education. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70041

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Scoping review classification of all 288 studies using Cook, Bordage and Schmidt's threefold typology, applied by all three authors iteratively. The corpus is "dominated by justification studies (52%, 150/288)" versus 33% descriptive and 15% clarification.

> "the literature contains a large number of description studies (33%, 94/288) but is dominated by justification studies (52%, 150/288)"

### Cleland JA 2026 (2)

Cleland JA, Pienkowska A, Kitto SC. (2026). Endless justification: A scoping review of team‐based learning research in medical education. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.70041

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Trend analysis of the 236 studies published from 2015 onwards, illustrated in Figure 4. Clarification studies increased after 2015 (only two of 44 were published before then) but remained the smallest category at 17.8%.

> "Of the 236 studies published from 2015 onwards, 76 (32.2%) were descriptive, 118 (50%) justification and 42 (17.8%) clarification."
