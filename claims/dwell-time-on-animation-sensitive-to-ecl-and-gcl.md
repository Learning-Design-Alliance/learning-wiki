## Evidence

### Tianlong Zu 2017

Tianlong Zu, John Hutson, Lester C. Loschky, and N. Sanjay Rebello. (2017). Use of Eye-Tracking Technology to Investigate Cognitive Load Theory. 2017 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.113

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Eye-tracking analysis with an EyeLink 1000+ during the multimedia lesson. The ECL contrast on animation dwell time was significant (t(366) = 29.021, p < .001; 86% vs 38%); within the redundant condition, high-GCL students showed significantly higher animation dwell time (47% vs 31%), "t(182) = -5.752, p < .001". No standardized effect size was printed.

> "An independent samples t -test ( t(366) = 29.021, p < .001.) found that the percentage dwell time spent looking at animation by students from  non-redundant i.e. low ECL condition ( 86%) was significantly higher than  that of those from redundant i.e. high ECL condition (38% )."
