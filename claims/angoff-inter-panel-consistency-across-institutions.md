### Dalum Jesper 2024

Dalum Jesper, Paulsson Liselotte, Christidis Nikolaos, Andersson Franko Mikael, Karlgren Klas, Leanderson Charlotte, Sandborgh-Englund Gunilla. (2024). Consistency between inter-institutional panels using a three-level Angoff-standard setting in licensure tests of foreign-trained dentists in Sweden: A cohort study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0313476

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cohort study comparing two independent Angoff panels rating the same three theoretical examinations (267 items total, 2019–2020). Spearman correlations showed "ρ≥ 0.70, p < .001" across exams, disciplines and qualifications; Table 1 reports total-item correlation of 0.88.

> "The ratings of the two panels of experts from different universities evaluated the difficulty of the items similarly across all exams, dental subjects, and professional qualifications (ρ≥ 0.70, p < .001)."
