## Subclaims
`q2 i3` HRQoL correlated negatively with academic burnout (r = -0.52, p ≤ 0.01) and positively with intrinsic (r = 0.13) and extrinsic motivation (r = 0.25). [→ Torkani F 2025](#torkani-f-2025)
`q2 i2` Intrinsic motivation correlated with academic success at r = 0.27 and extrinsic motivation at r = 0.24 (both p ≤ 0.01), while amotivation showed no significant correlation with success. [→ Torkani F 2025 (2)](#torkani-f-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Torkani F 2025

Torkani F, Ahmadi S-H, Binazir A, Fath MM, Shafigh N, Asadiparvar-Masouleh H, Vasli P. (2025). The relationship between health-related quality of life and academic success in nursing students: the mediating role of academic burnout and academic motivation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06661-6

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlations (Table 3) among 261 nursing students. The article reports a strong negative HRQoL–burnout correlation (r = -0.52, p ≤ 0.01) and smaller positive motivation correlations.

> "A significant association was observed between HRQoL and AB (r = -0.52, p ≤ 0.01), intrinsic motivation (r = 0.13, p ≤ 0.05), extrinsic motivation (r = 0.25, p ≤ 0.01), and amotivation (r = -0.38, p ≤ 0.01)."

### Torkani F 2025 (2)

Torkani F, Ahmadi S-H, Binazir A, Fath MM, Shafigh N, Asadiparvar-Masouleh H, Vasli P. (2025). The relationship between health-related quality of life and academic success in nursing students: the mediating role of academic burnout and academic motivation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06661-6

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate correlations (Table 3) in the same sample. The article reports r = 0.27 (intrinsic motivation–GPA) and r = 0.24 (extrinsic motivation–GPA), both p ≤ 0.01; the amotivation–success correlation printed as r = 0.01 was not significant.

> "There was a significant association between intrinsic motivation and extrinsic motivation (r = 0.78, p ≤ 0.01), amotivation (r = -0.35, p ≤ 0.01), and AS (r = 0.27, p ≤ 0.01). Finally, a significant correlation was observed between extrinsic motivation and amotivation (r = -0.42, p ≤ 0.01) and AS (r = 0.24, p ≤ 0.01)."
