`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Authors' feasibility/usability comparison (Figure 4) of average questionnaire values between the established video procedure and the new robot procedure, against a pre-defined "maximum deviation of 20% from the standard procedure". This is an authors' criterion-based judgment, not a formal statistical equivalence test.

> "Using a 20% deviation criterion, the robot simulation was deemed noninferior to the standard procedure, demonstrating equivalence in realism, symptom recognition, and diagnostic accuracy."
