## Evidence

### Gül Kaleli Yılmaz 2015

Gül Kaleli Yılmaz. (2015). The Effect of Dynamic Geometry Software and Physical Manipulatives on Candidate Teachers’ Transformational Geometry Success. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.2610

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on the recognition section with its pre-test as covariate; Bonferroni comparisons: CG vs. MG mean difference = 1.63, CG vs. TG = 3.83, MG vs. TG = 2.20, all p < .001. The article credits Cabri's visual features and dynamism for instant viewing of transformations.

> "there was a significant mean difference in the gain scores of students in the Computer Group, Manipulatives Group, and Traditional Group with respect to the recognition section of the test [ F(2,111) = 53.53, p < .05, η 2 = .49]"

### Gül Kaleli Yılmaz 2015 (2)

Gül Kaleli Yılmaz. (2015). The Effect of Dynamic Geometry Software and Physical Manipulatives on Candidate Teachers’ Transformational Geometry Success. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.5.2610

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on the features section with its pre-test as covariate; Bonferroni comparisons: CG vs. MG = 1.58, CG vs. TG = 3.47, MG vs. TG = 1.89, all p < .001. The article explains Computer Group students could measure sides and area on screen to check features.

> "There was a significant mean difference in the gain scores of students in the Computer Group, Manipulatives Group, and Traditional Group with respect to the features section of the test [F(2,111) = 52.07, p < .001, η2 = .48]"
