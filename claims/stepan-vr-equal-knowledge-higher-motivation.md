## Evidence

### K Anniesmitha 2026

K Anniesmitha, Rao K G Mohandas, Aithal P Ashwini, Bandekar Nikita N., Mandal Tatiyana, George Bincy M. (2026). Effectiveness of technology-enhanced learning in neuroanatomy education among health professionals - a systematic review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09764-w

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomised controlled study with 66 medical students (33 per group) comparing a VR interactive module against an online textbook, with IMMS motivation assessment. Knowledge quizzes showed "no significant difference"; VR experience ratings were higher (all p < 0.01).

> "there was no significant difference in anatomy knowledge between the 2 groups in terms of preintervention, postintervention, or retention quizzes. The VR group reported that the learning experience was significantly more engaging, enjoyable, and useful (all p < 0.01)"
