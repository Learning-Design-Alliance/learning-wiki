Lyn D English and Jane M Watson 2015¶

Content analysis of 101 workbook responses to the accuracy/best-guess/confidence question in the first scenario. Table 3 reports 35% mode/frequency, 23% nebulous statement, 12% variation in relation to accuracy, 11% average/central tendency. The top category "contained responses related to a modal idea, although the term mode was not used."
