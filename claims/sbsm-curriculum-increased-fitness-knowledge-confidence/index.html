Loukaitou-Sideris 2015¶

Loukaitou-Sideris, A. (2015). Targeting the Body and the Mind: Evaluation of a P.E. Curriculum Intervention for Adolescents. Journal of Education and Training Studies, 3(4). https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v3i4.873

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre/post student surveys in five inner-city LAUSD schools showed significant gains in fitness knowledge and activity frequency.

"There was also a statistically significant increase of 9% in the number of students stating that they know a lot about fitness and exercise from the pretest to the posttest survey (p<0.001), and a statistically significant increase of 8.4% in the number of students reporting being active in P.E. and/or sports at least 4-5 times per week (p<0.01)."
