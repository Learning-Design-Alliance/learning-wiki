Tuba Demircioglu 2015¶

Tuba Demircioglu, Sedat Ucar. (2015). Investigating the Effect of Argument-Driven Inquiry in Laboratory Instruction. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.1.2324

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

ANCOVA of Science Process Skills Test post-test scores with pretest as covariate in the same quasi-experimental study found a significant difference favoring the ADI group, "ŋ 2 = .07", which the article interprets as a moderate effect explaining 7% of the variance.

"after the SPST pretest scores were controlled, there was a significant difference on the SPST post-test scores in favor of the experimental group (F(1-76) = 5.4, p = .023). An effect size of .06 or higher is interpreted as a moderate effect (Perce et al., 2004). When the effect size is analyzed, a moderate effect for the method is seen (ŋ 2 = .07)"
