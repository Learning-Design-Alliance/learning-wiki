## Evidence

### Yao Liwen 2021

Yao Liwen, Liu Jun, Wu Lianlian, Zhang Lihui, Hu Xiao, Liu Jinzhu, Lu Zihua, Gong Dexin, An Ping, Zhang Jun, Hu Guiying, Chen Di, Luo Renquan, Hu Shan, Yang Yanning, Yu Honggang. (2021). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized pretest-posttest gastroscopy arm: 3,515 gastroscopies (1,878 phase 1, 1,637 phase 2) by 16 endoscopists, primary endpoint GPC detection rate. The feedback group "increased from 3% to 7%" (OR 1.866, 95% CI 1.399–2.489); control fell 3.9% to 3.5% (OR 0.856).

> "the feedback group's GPC detection rate increased from 3% to 7% (P < 0.01, OR 1.866, 95% CI 1.399–2.489), whereas the decrease of GPC detection rate in the control group was not improved (3.9%–3.5%, P = 0.489, OR 0.856, 95% CI 0.550–1.332)"
