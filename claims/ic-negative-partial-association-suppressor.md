### Boadum O 2026

Boadum O, Wilson TD, Thompson K, Robertson EM, Notebaert A. (2026). Self-directed learning readiness in preclinical medical education: Patterns, variation, and academic outcomes from a mixed longitudinal and cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09867-4

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

OLS regression with cluster-robust standard errors in the same GPA models. The negative partial standardized β (−0.155 in Year 1) contrasts with near-zero bivariate correlations (r = −.081 in Year 1, an effect size below d = 0.2), which the authors call consistent with a classical statistical suppressor effect.

> "Interpersonal communication showed a significant negative partial association with GPA in Year 1 (β = −0.155, 95% CI − 0.229 to − 0.081, p < .001) and in the combined model (β = −0.104, 95% CI − 0.151 to − 0.058, p < .001)."
