## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Post-test pronunciation scores differed significantly among the three groups after adjusting for pre-test (ηp2 = .273), with the holographic app group (EG2) scoring significantly higher than both the non-holographic app group (EG1) and the control group (CG). ## Evidence

### Rebeca Cerezo 2024

Rebeca Cerezo, Vicente Calderón, Cristóbal Romero. (2024). A holographic mobile-based application for practicing pronunciation of basic English vocabulary for Spanish speaking children. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pre-post quasi-experiment with 70 Spanish pre-school children in three intact classes randomly assigned to conditions. ANCOVA on post-test with pre-test as covariate showed significant group differences (F (2, 66) = 12.397, p<.001, ηp2 = .273); "the post-test scores of EG1 and EG2 are also significantly different (MD(EG1-EG2) = -.496, p< .05)".

> "The comparison shows that the post-test scores of the CG and EG1, and CG and EG2 are significantly different MD(CG-EG1) = -.454, p< .05; MD(CG-EG2) = -.950, p< .001 , and that the post-test scores of EG1 and EG2 are also significantly different (MD(EG1-EG2) = -.496, p< .05)."

### Rebeca Cerezo 2024 (2)

Rebeca Cerezo, Vicente Calderón, Cristóbal Romero. (2024). A holographic mobile-based application for practicing pronunciation of basic English vocabulary for Spanish speaking children. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.07897)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Same three-group pre-post experiment; effect sizes computed on post-test means. The article prints d = 0.47 between CG and EG1, rated moderate, and d = 1.00 between CG and EG2, rated large, "based on the standard difference between the post-test means".

> "Cohen’s d between CG and EG1 (0.47) indicated that the effect size was moderate and Cohen’s d between CG and EG2 (1.00) indicated that the effect size was large."
