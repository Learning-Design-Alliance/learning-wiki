Stavropoulou G, Daniilidou A, Nerantzaki K. (2025). Exploring the Interplay of Motivation, Self-Efficacy, Critical Thinking, and Self-Regulation in Predicting Academic Achievement Among University Students. F1000Research 14:344. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.161821.1

Correlation matrix (Table 2) and discussion of the 250-student sample; the printed correlation between extrinsic motivation and self-regulation is 0.144 (p < .05), versus 0.561 for intrinsic motivation.

> "the correlation between extrinsic motivation and self-regulation was relatively low, indicating that external rewards or pressures may not significantly contribute to self-regulatory behaviors compared to intrinsic factors."
