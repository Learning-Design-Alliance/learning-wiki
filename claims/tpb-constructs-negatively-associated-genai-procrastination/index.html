Li Zilin 2026¶

Li Zilin, Huang Jiali, Cui Zhaodi. (2026). GenAI Use and GenAI-Assisted Learning Procrastination in University Students: The Roles of Planned Behavior Constructs and Learning GenAI Anxiety. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071256

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Path analysis in the same cross-sectional survey of 1243 Chinese university students; all four Theory of Planned Behavior paths to procrastination were negative and significant, consistent with hypotheses H1, H3, H5, and H7.

"Behavioral attitude (β = −0.164, p < 0.001), subjective norm (β = −0.171, p < 0.001), perceived behavioral control (β = −0.331, p < 0.001), and behavioral intention (β = −0.137, p < 0.001) were each negatively associated with GenAI-assisted learning procrastination."
