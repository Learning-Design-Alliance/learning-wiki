### Sumer Kohli 2023

Sumer Kohli, Neelesh Ramachandran, Ana Tudor, Gloria Tumushabe, Olivia Hsu, and Gireeja Ranade. 2023. Inclusive Study Group Formation At Scale. In Proceedings of the 54th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V. 1 (SIGCSE 2023). https://doi.org/10.1145/3545945.3569885

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Week 13 evaluation survey of software-matched students, analyzed by gender with 2-sample proportion z-tests (alpha = 0.05). The article reports "no significant difference between students in the majority group (men) and those who identified as any other gender". Equivalence was not tested; no effect size printed.

> "Using 2-sample proportion z-tests (𝛼 = 0.05) [36], we found no significant difference between students in the majority group (men) and those who identified as any other gender (Fig. 3)."

### Sumer Kohli 2023 (2)

Sumer Kohli, Neelesh Ramachandran, Ana Tudor, Gloria Tumushabe, Olivia Hsu, and Gireeja Ranade. 2023. Inclusive Study Group Formation At Scale. In Proceedings of the 54th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V. 1 (SIGCSE 2023). https://doi.org/10.1145/3545945.3569885

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same evaluation survey analyzed by race with Fisher exact tests (alpha = 0.05) due to small samples; all six Black/AA software-matched students reported positive indicators. The article reports students identifying as Black/African-American "did not show statistically significant differences in any study group indicators".

> "We see that students identifying as Black / African-American (AA) did not show statistically significant differences in any study group indicators, in comparison to non-Black/AA students (using a Fisher exact test, 𝛼 = 0.05, since the sample sizes are small [36])."
