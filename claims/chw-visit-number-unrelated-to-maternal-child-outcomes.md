### Tomlinson M 2025

Tomlinson M, Rotheram-Borus MJ, Stansert Katzen L, Gertsch W, le Roux I, Dippenaar E, le Roux K. (2025). Relationship of perinatal outcomes to the competence and quantity of contact with community health workers. Journal of Global Health. https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.15.04094

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis of total CHW visits on 13 outcomes over 24 months in the intervention group (423 mothers at four clinics), using logistic regression for binary and linear regression for continuous outcomes. The printed coefficients include child support grant OR = 1.10 (P = 0.035) and HIV preventive adherence β = 0.04 (P = 0.047); "our simulations indicated that we needed to find three significant outcomes".

> "We obtained two significant outcomes for the number of visits: receipt of a child support grant and adherence to child HIV prevention measures (Table 1). However, our simulations indicated that we needed to find three significant outcomes to be considered significantly different"
