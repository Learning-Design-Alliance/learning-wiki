# Perceived learning is higher for blocked than interleaved practice at onset but increases over time for interleaved practice only

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r3` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Perceived learning was significantly higher for blocked practice than for interleaved practice. [→ Onan 2022](#onan-2022)
`q3 i?` Perceived learning increased over time for interleaved practice but not for blocked practice (strategy × time interaction). [→ Onan 2022 (2)](#onan-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Onan 2022

Onan, E., Wiradhany, W., Biwer, F., Janssen, E. M., & de Bruin, A. B. H. (2022). Growing Out of the Experience: How Subjective Experiences of Effort and Learning Influence the Use of Interleaved Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09692-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Linear mixed model on judgments of learning collected after each study unit. The main effect of strategy was significant, F(1, 332) = 495.16, p < 0.001; no effect size was printed.

> "We observed a significant main effect of learning strategies: perceived learning was significantly higher for blocked practice than for interleaved practice, F(1, 332) = 495.16, p < 0.001."

### Onan 2022 (2)

Onan, E., Wiradhany, W., Biwer, F., Janssen, E. M., & de Bruin, A. B. H. (2022). Growing Out of the Experience: How Subjective Experiences of Effort and Learning Influence the Use of Interleaved Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09692-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

The same LMM showed perceived learning rose across units only for interleaved practice, F(1, 1498) = 260.81, p < 0.001; no effect size was printed.

> "the main effect of time again appeared to be driven by the strategy × time interaction, which indicated that perceived learning increased over time for interleaved practice, but not for blocked practice, F(1, 1498) = 260.81, p < 0.001."
