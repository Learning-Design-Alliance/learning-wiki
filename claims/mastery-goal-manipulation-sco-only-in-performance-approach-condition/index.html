Evidence¶

Darnon 2010¶

Darnon, C., Dompnier, B., Gilliéron, O., & Butera, F. (2010). The Interplay of Mastery and Performance Goals in Social Comparison: A Multiple-Goal Perspective. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018161

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Study 2 experimentally manipulated goal conditions rather than measuring self-set goals. The article reports that "mastery goals predicted interest in social comparison in the performance-approach goal condition only"; no effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text.
