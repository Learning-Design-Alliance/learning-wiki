## Evidence

### Matthew Dew 2021

Matthew Dew, Jonathan Perry, Lewis Ford, Dawson Nodurft, and Tatiana Erukhimova. (2021). Student Responses to Changes in Introductory Physics Learning due to COVID-19 Pandemic. arXiv. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/5.0027816](https://doi.org/10.1119/5.0027816)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Likert-item survey of 708 summer 2020 remote introductory physics and astronomy students at two institutions. The article reports over half felt "rarely" or "never" connected or engaged with classmates during lecture, and similar responses across course components.

> "Over half of the students reported feeling “rarely” or “never” connected or engaged with classmates during lecture, Figure 3. These responses are similar for all components of the course that students were asked about."
