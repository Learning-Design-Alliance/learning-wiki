## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Nathan W. Whitmore 2024

Nathan W. Whitmore, Erika M. Yamazaki & Ken A. Paller. (2024). Targeted memory reactivation with sleep disruption does not weaken week-old memories. npj Science of Learning 9, 64. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00276-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis comparison with arousal scored offline from EEG by the AASM rule; 62% of cues caused an arousal and 38% did not, similar to the prior study (57.9% and 43.0%).

> "the forgetting ratio for cues that caused an arousal, cues that did not cause an arousal, and uncued objects, was similar [(H(2) = 2.21,p = 0.34]."
