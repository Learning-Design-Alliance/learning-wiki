Evidence¶

Ke 2021¶

Ke, F., Dai, Z., Pachman, M. et al. (2021). Exploring multiuser virtual teaching simulation as an alternative learning environment for student instructors. Instr Sci, 49, 831–854. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09555-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the mixed-method study of 40 university teaching assistants randomly assigned to VR or live classroom simulation, "All participants reported higher teaching self-efficacy after the training." The available text reports no effect size or test statistic for this change.
