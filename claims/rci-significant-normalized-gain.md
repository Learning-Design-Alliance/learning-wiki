## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After a three-week relativity module, mean RCI scores rose from 56% pre-test to 71% post-test, with an average normalized gain of 0.40 that the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test showed to be statistically significant. [→ J. S. Aslanides and C. M. Savage 2013](#j-s-aslanides-and-c-m-savage-2013)

## Evidence

### J. S. Aslanides and C. M. Savage 2013

J. S. Aslanides and C. M. Savage. (2013). The Relativity Concept Inventory: development, analysis and results. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.010118)

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre/post administration of the RCI to the 2012 ANU Physics 2 class (70 pre-test, 63 post-test responses, 53 taking both). The article reports "The mean RCI score on the pre-test was 56%, and on the post-test 71%", against a random-guessing mean of 36%, and an average normalized gain ⟨g⟩ = 0.40 with p = 4 × 10−6.

> "The mean RCI score on the pre-test was 56%, and on the post-test 71%. For comparison, the expected mean score if answers were chosen randomly is 36%"
