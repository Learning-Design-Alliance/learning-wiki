## Evidence

### Bearman Margaret 2019

Bearman Margaret, Greenhill Jennene, Nestel Debra. (2019). The power of simulation: a large‐scale narrative analysis of learners’ experiences. Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13747

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

In the discussion of the thematic analysis of 327 narratives, the authors state that the 'being the patient' theme — the last of the five mechanisms — was uniquely modality-linked, whereas the narrative categories and other themes "tended to work across all simulation modalities". No effect sizes are printed.

> "The last was the only learning feature that was particularly linked to a modality: role‐play."
