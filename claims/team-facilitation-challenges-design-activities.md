Teaching Charge Coupled Devices Using Models as Part of the Engineering Design Process at Maui Community College. ASP Conference Series. `q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' qualitative account of facilitating the CCD modeling activity in 2008 and 2009. They note reluctance to contribute was particularly common among female students paired with a male, and that dominant members were played down by interacting directly with less vocal members. No quantitative measures are reported.
