## Evidence

### Clinton 2019

Virginia Clinton. (2019). Reading from paper compared to screens: A systematic review and metaanalysis. Journal of Research in Reading. https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9817.12269

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random effects meta-analysis of reading time measures across k = 14 studies (n = 1,233) found no reliable effect of medium. Heterogeneity was substantial (I2 = 92.47), indicating great variability among findings. No effect size magnitude was interpretable as an effect given non-significance.

> "Based on the random effects model, reading text from screens had no reliable effect on reading times compared to reading from paper, g = .08, k = 14, SE = .20, 95% CI = [ .32, .48], p = .45"
