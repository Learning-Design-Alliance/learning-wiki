## Evidence

### Cristi-Montero 2022

Cristi-Montero, C., Solis-Urra, P., Sanchez-Martinez, J., et al. as printed in full byline. (2022). Which one? A comparative study of traditional and sports uniforms on academic achievement, cognitive performance, playtime, bullying, and discrimination in adolescents: The Cogni-Action Project. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2022.917970

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional mixed-model analysis of 988 adolescents from 15 Chilean schools, adjusted for sex, PHV, BMIz, physical fitness, and SVI (model 2). The study reports "no significant differences between uniform types in academic achievement"; descriptive means were TU 5.40 ± 0.7 vs SU 5.60 ± 0.7. No effect size was printed.

> "Figure 1A shows no significant differences between uniform types in academic achievement (random effect LTR was 44.0; p < 0.001; ICC = 0.098)."
