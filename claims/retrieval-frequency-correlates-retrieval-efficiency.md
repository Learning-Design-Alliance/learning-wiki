### Ineke Imbo · André Vandierendonck 2008

Ineke Imbo · André Vandierendonck. (2008). Effects of problem size, operation, and working-memory span on simple-arithmetic strategies: differences between children and adults? Psychological Research (2008) 72:331–346. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-007-0112-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Correlational and regression analyses across the three grades showed retrieval use correlated negatively with retrieval RTs (r = −0.239 in second grade, −0.164 in fourth, −0.320 in sixth, all significant at the 0.01 level), while retrieval use did not predict procedural RTs in any grade.
