## Evidence

### Ollé 2022

Ollé, H. and Kovács, T. (2022). What Hides within the Photograph: the Analysis of a Light Curve in the Classroom. Physics Education. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ac68c3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ac68c3)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Classroom estimation activity in which secondary-school students read the transit start and end times of HAT-P-7b from the MAST light curve; the averaged student estimate was "0.1667± 0.009 days", against the printed literature value of 0.1669±0.003 days.

> "In our case, this value becomes 0.1667± 0.009 days, while the lit- erature value is 0.1669± 0.003 days [12]."
