Rana Abou Khamis 2026¶

Rana Abou Khamis, Hala Assal, and Ashraf Matrawy. (2026). Unanticipated Effects of Generative AI on Expertise Pathways and Performance Perception in System Administration. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28650

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview finding from the 14-participant study: participants relied on GenAI to "reduce the hands-on effort required for repetitive operational work"; P13 said it helps with "tweaking or making changes to existing scripts" and P5 observed "hardly any new solutions [are] generated".

"They consistently described GenAI as highly effective, but mainlu for routine tasks such as scripting, troubleshooting, and documentation."
