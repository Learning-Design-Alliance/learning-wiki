# The eLearning course dramatically increases midwives' confidence in providing CMV advice, from 6% to 95% agreeing, maintained at three months

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al 2022

Smithers‐Sheedy H. et al. (2022). eLearning significantly improves maternity professionals' knowledge of the congenital cytomegalovirus prevention guidelines. Australian & New Zealand Journal of Obstetrics & Gynaecology. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.13500

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ordinal regression model with random intercept, controlling for prior CMV education and length of practice, analysed responses to the confidence statement across the three time points. Agreement rose from 6% to 95% and was maintained at T3.

> "At T1, 22/262 (6%) of midwives indicated that they agreed or strongly agreed with this statement compared with 227/238 (95%) at T2 (T1–T2: P < 0.001). Limited data available at T3 indicated that 19/27 (70%) agreed or strongly agreed with this statement (T1–T3: P < 0.001)."
