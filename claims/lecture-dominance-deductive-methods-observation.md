## Evidence

### Edileuza de Freitas Miranda de Mendonça and José Gómez-Galán 2016

Edileuza de Freitas Miranda de Mendonça and José Gómez-Galán. (2016). Professional Practice in Higher Education: A Case Study in Faculty Training and Development in Brazil. International Journal of Educational Excellence, Vol. 2, No. 2, 51-64. https://doi.org/10.18562/IJEE.018

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observation study of 75 observed classes (15 teachers in the State University of Goiás State's University Program for Educational Staff). The article reports "in 97% of classes teachers employed only expository sessions (lectures)" and debate appeared in no observed classroom.

> "Didactic Techniques Used : in 97% of classes teachers employed only expository sessions (lectures), 3% of teachers conducted studies leading to the development of student self-learning; on the other hand, in the selected sample no classroom was found where the debate was used as a teaching technique."

### Edileuza de Freitas Miranda de Mendonça and José Gómez-Galán 2016 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Classroom observation data (Table 3, 75 classes) on exposition methods. The article reports deductive methods in 81% of classes versus inductive methods in 19%.

> "About the Exposition Method: Teachers used the deductive method in 81% of the classes and the inductive method in only 19% of the classes."
