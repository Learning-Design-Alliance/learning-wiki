## Evidence

### Bosch 2021

Bosch, Nigel. (2021). Identifying Supportive Student Factors for Mindset Interventions: A Two-model Machine Learning Approach. Computers & Education, 167, 104190. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104190

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same machine learning analysis of NSLM data, the count of blocked navigation events showed "a clear negative relationship with predicted intervention effect". Students with many such events (4.4%) had a predicted effect of −0.185 GPA points relative to the mean, versus +0.008 for peers.

> "There were few students (4.4% of intervention students, n = 238) who attempted a large number of blocked navigation events; however, for those who did, the predicted intervention effect was −0.185 GPA points relative to the mean. For their peers (95.6% of students, n = 5,187), predicted intervention effect was 0.008 GPA points above the mean."
