## Evidence

### Gilbert R. O. 2025

Gilbert R. O., Gilbert D. R. (2025). Student evaluations of teaching do not reflect student learning: an observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06896-3

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis across 160 courses aligning each course with its relevant VEA section; the study prints the correlation effect size rho = -0.18 (P = 0.025), reporting the relationship "was negative", described by the authors as small but statistically significant.

> "On the other hand, the relationship between course evaluations and the corresponding sectional VEA scores was negative (rho = -0.18, P = 0.025), as was the correlation between the VEA scores and the percentage of grades in the A-range (rho = -0.31, P = 0.0001)."
