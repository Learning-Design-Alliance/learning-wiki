# Basic reading skills were associated with change in confirming credibility but not with change in questioning or justifying credibility

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Reading fluency and comprehension were associated with RCI group membership for confirming credibility (ηp2 = 0.07 and ηp2 = 0.04), with the negative-change group scoring lower. [→ Kiili 2025](#kiili-2025)
`q2 i?` No statistically significant associations were observed between basic reading skills and RCI groups for questioning and justifying credibility. [→ Kiili 2025 (2)](#kiili-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Kiili 2025

Kiili, C., Kiili, K., Räikkönen, E., & Coiro, J. (2025). Explicit Video-Based Instruction Enhanced Students' Online Credibility Evaluation Skills: Did Storifying Instruction Matter?. Computers and Education, 229, Article 105252. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105252

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANOVA on RCI group membership (intervention conditions only, N = 229–230) for confirming credibility. Fluency showed the printed effect size ηp2 = 0.07 ("F(2, 227) = 7.95, p < .001, ηp2 = 0.07") and comprehension ηp2 = 0.04; negative-change students had lower fluency and comprehension scores.

> "Regarding confirming the credibility, we observed a statistically significant association between students' RCI group and reading fluency (F(2, 227) = 7.95, p < .001, ηp2 = 0.07) and reading comprehension skills (F(2, 226) = 4.16, p = .017, ηp2 = 0.04)."

### Kiili 2025 (2)

Kiili, C., Kiili, K., Räikkönen, E., & Coiro, J. (2025). Explicit Video-Based Instruction Enhanced Students' Online Credibility Evaluation Skills: Did Storifying Instruction Matter?. Computers and Education, 229, Article 105252. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105252

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

For the questioning and justifying RCI analyses (RCI groups 1 and 2 combined due to small group sizes), the ANOVAs showed no significant associations with reading fluency or comprehension; no effect sizes were printed.

> "We did not observe any statistically significant associations between the RCI groups and basic reading skills."
