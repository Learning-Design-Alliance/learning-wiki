Analysis of student response distributions for the five most underestimated fraction items (Table 3) shows "errors are not random but reflect systematic misconception patterns"; e.g., 34.2% correct on P32 with common wrong answers 50 and 100.

"Common incorrect responses include “division makes smaller” beliefs, whole-number bias, inappropriate operation selection, and incorrect reasoning about magnitude."
