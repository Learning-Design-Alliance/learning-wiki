### Özdemir O 2026

Özdemir O, Matara BM, Gündoğar M. (2026). Preclinical endodontic training using natural/simulative models: a cross-sectional study involved trainee and educator perspectives on clinical competence. BMC Medical Education, 26:378. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08715-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Trainee-perspective subgroup analysis (Table 6) of 111 current students comparing extracted-tooth versus simulative-model training. The positive response rate on feeling adequately prepared for the clinic "was 33.3% for those using extracted teeth, while a higher rate of 52.4% was obtained for those using simulative models". No effect size is printed.

> "According to the responses of current student participants (n = 111), the preclinical training model they received had a statistically significant content that included simulative models (p < 0,003)."
