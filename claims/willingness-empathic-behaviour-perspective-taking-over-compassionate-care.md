# Fourth-year medical students' willingness to show empathic behaviour is substantially more correlated with perspective taking than with compassionate care

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` The factor correlation between perspective taking and willingness to show empathic behaviour (r = 0.631 pre, r = 0.699 post) is substantially higher than the correlation between compassionate care and willingness (r = 0.244 pre, r = 0.334 post). [→ Son Daisuke 2018](#son-daisuke-2018)

## Evidence

### Son Daisuke 2018

Son Daisuke, Shimizu Ikuo, Ishikawa Hirono, Aomatsu Muneyoshi, Leppink Jimmie. (2018). Communication skills training and the conceptual structure of empathy among medical students. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0431-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Confirmatory factor analysis of survey responses from 295 fourth-year Japanese medical students (288 analysed with full information maximum likelihood) in three universities, administered retrospectively pre-training and post-training. Printed factor correlations: r = 0.699 (perspective taking, post-training) versus r = 0.334 (compassionate care, post-training), i.e. "0.699 for post-training" versus "0.334 for post-training".

> "The correlation between perspective taking and willingness to show empathic behaviour was 0.631 for retrospective pre-training and 0.699 for post-training. The correlation between compassionate care and willingness to show empathic behaviour was 0.244 for retrospective pre-training and 0.334 for post-training."
