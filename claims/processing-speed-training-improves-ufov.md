### Kueider Alexandra M. 2012

Kueider Alexandra M., Parisi Jeanine M., Gross Alden L., Rebok George W. (2012). Computerized Cognitive Training with Older Adults: A Systematic Review. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0040588

`q2 · i2` · `review · r2`

Review synthesis of five processing speed training studies in older adults, with durations varying from two to 12 weeks. The review reports interventions "significantly improved processing speed scores (Useful Field of View)" with Cohen's d ranging from 1.09 to 2.19.

> "Results of five studies [9], [24], [32]–[34], suggested speed of processing interventions, which varied in duration from two to 12 weeks, significantly improved processing speed scores (Useful Field of View) with effect sizes (Cohen’s d) ranging from 1.09 [32] to 2.19 [34]."
