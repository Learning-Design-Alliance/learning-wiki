## Evidence

### Ortlieb 2013

Ortlieb, E. (2013). Using Anticipatory Reading Guides to Improve Elementary Students' Comprehension. International Journal of Instruction, 6(2), 145-162. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1085407

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Tally-sheet observations during the six-week study showed that spontaneous transfer of the anticipatory guide strategy to social studies passages required teacher prompting initially. By the final three weeks without reminders, "Seven students (50%) quickly read the questions prior to reading the passages" while others showed partial or no application.

> "When students were given passages to read in Social Studies and Reading class, they did not apply this strategy until being reminded of this technique, after which all of the students previewed the questions prior to reading."
