Mutlu Cukurova 2025¶

Mutlu Cukurova, Wannapon Suraworachet, Qi Zhou, and Sahan Bulathwela. (2025). Towards Synergistic Teacher-AI Interactions with Generative Artificial Intelligence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19580 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2511.19580

q3 · i? · review · r2

The chapter cites Vaccaro et al.'s meta-analysis of over a hundred experimental studies beyond educational fields as synergistic performance evidence, showing synergic interactions are not naturally achieved. No effect size is printed.

"Results strikingly reveal that in 58% of the reviewed studies, the performance obtained from human-AI integration was lower than the better performer alone (either human or AI)."
