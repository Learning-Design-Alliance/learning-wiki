## Evidence

### Vi Ly · Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick 2024

Vi Ly · Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick. (2024). Effects of School-led Greenspace Interventions on Mental, Physical and Social Wellbeing in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09963-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports that in Harvey et al.'s repeated-measures studies, "positive affect significantly increased among 443 (Study 1) and 105 (Study 2) primary and secondary school students" after school nature-ground activities. No effect size is printed.

> "Harvey et al. (2021) found that positive affect significantly increased among 443 (Study 1) and 105 (Study 2) primary and secondary school students after completing nature-related activities in the school nature grounds."

### Vi Ly · Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick 2024 (2)

Vi Ly · Dianne A. Vella-Brodrick. (2024). Effects of School-led Greenspace Interventions on Mental, Physical and Social Wellbeing in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09963-1

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports a null main effect: Mason et al.'s experimental repeated-measures study of 65 primary students found "a significant effect of school lessons taught in the school garden" was absent for positive affect overall.

> "Mason et al. (2022) did not find a significant effect of school lessons taught in the school garden (compared to indoor lessons) on positive affect among 65 primary school students."
