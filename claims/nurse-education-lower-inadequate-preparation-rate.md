### Lee Yoo Jin MD; Kim Eun Soo MD 2015

Lee Yoo Jin MD; Kim Eun Soo MD, PhD; Park Kyung Sik MD; Cho Kwang Bum MD, PhD; Jang Byoung Kuk MD, PhD; Chung Woo Jin MD, PhD; Hwang Jae Seok MD, PhD; Somchai Amornyotin. (2015). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blinded controlled trial of 205 inpatients across two wards. Poor preparation defined as "OBPS ≥ 6"; rates were 31.1% educated vs 58.8% control (P = 0.001), reported both in the abstract (P < 0.001) and results.

> "The proportion of patients with inadequate bowel preparation (OBPS ≥ 6) was significantly lower in the educated ward compared with the control (31.1% vs 58.8%, P = 0.001)."
