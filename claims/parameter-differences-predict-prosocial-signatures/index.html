Evidence¶

Yangchu Huang 2026¶

Yangchu Huang, Xinyi Du & Shanshan Zhen. (2026). Deciding for others diminishes model-based decision-making but depends on individual prosociality. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00397-0

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Correlation analyses (Fig. 4b) linking RLDDM parameter differences to individual prosocial behavioural signatures in the 92-participant sample, with printed effect sizes r(92) = 0.40 for model-based weights and r(92) = 0.51 for model-free learning rates. A simulation selectively muting parameters confirmed both contributed to prosocial model-based choices.

"positively correlated with self-other differences in model-based weights (r(92) = 0.40, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.21, 0.56]) and model-free learning rates (r(92) = 0.51, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.34, 0.65])"
