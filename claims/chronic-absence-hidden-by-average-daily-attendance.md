# Average daily attendance and truancy metrics mask chronic absence, which reveals individual students missing 10% of school for any reason

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Average daily attendance does not reveal when absences are adding up for individual students, and truancy counts only unexcused absences, so chronic absence (10% missed for any reason) is easily masked. [→ EdTrust 2023](#edtrust-2023)

## Evidence

### EdTrust 2023

EdTrust. (2023). 5 Things for Advocates to Know About Chronic Absenteeism. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED627994

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The brief explains that chronic absenteeism, a metric only widely used in the last decade, is easily masked by long-collected average daily attendance and truancy data: "Average daily attendance reveals how many kids typically show up to school each day" without exposing individual accumulation of absences.

> "Average daily attendance reveals how many kids typically show up to school each day but does not reveal when absences are adding up for individual students."
