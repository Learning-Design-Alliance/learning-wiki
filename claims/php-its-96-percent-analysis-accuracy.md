## Evidence

### Weragama 2014

Weragama, D. & Reye, J. (2014). Analysing Student Programs in the PHP Intelligent Tutoring System. Int J Artif Intell Educ 24:162–188. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0014-z

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation with two groups of postgraduate students at Queensland University of Technology across two semesters; 678 non-duplicate solutions analysed. The final version achieved "an analysis accuracy of 96.31 %", up from 80.53% in earlier versions.

> "Although the final version of the PHP ITS showed an analysis accuracy of 96.31 %, the results obtained from the two earlier versions were somewhat less accurate. Of the 678 solutions analysed, 546 were analysed correctly by those versions. This is 80.53 %"
