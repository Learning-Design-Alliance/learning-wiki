`q2 i?` Cadets who visited the tutoring center to prepare for the first Fundamental Skills Exam improved their average score by 24.4%±3.6% between placement and FSE, compared with 18.3%±0.6% for the complete Calculus 1 cohort. [→ Michael Courtney 2011](#michael-courtney-2011)

## Evidence

### Michael Courtney 2011

Michael Courtney, PhD. (2011). The Evening Tutoring Center at the United States Air Force Academy. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1102.1893](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1102.1893)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational comparison reported in the Outcomes and Discussion section: cadets self-selected into tutoring, and their mean improvement between the Algebra placement exam and the first FSE (24.4%±3.6%) exceeded the full cohort's gain (18.3%±0.6%). The article reports "improved their score by an average of 24.4%±3.6%" with no significance test or effect size.

> "Consequently, cadets who used our services improved their score by an average of 24.4%±3.6% between the two events, compared with the complete Calculus 1 cohort who improved their average score by 18.3%±0.6%."
