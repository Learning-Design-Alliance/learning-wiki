## Evidence

### Behjat 2013

Behjat, F. (2013). Joint Book Reading: How Do Blogs Foster Collaborative Comprehension? i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 3 No. 2, April-June. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1068891

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Scheffe post-hoc pairwise comparisons on gain scores of the four gender-by-condition subgroups in the same quasi-experiment. The article also reports the male-female contrasts within each condition were not significant (sig. = .599 control; sig. = .689 experimental); no effect sizes are printed.

> "Based on Table 5, the highest mean difference was observed between male students in the control group and female students in the experimental group (mean difference = 2.86857). The second highest mean difference was between the male students' gain scores in the control group and male students in the experimental group (mean difference = 2.42857)."
