## Evidence

### Muhidin Mohamed 2025

Muhidin Mohamed, Shubhadeep Mukherjee and Bhavana Baad. (2025). Impact of UK Postgraduate Student Experiences on Academic Performance in Blended Learning: A Data Analytics Approach. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.12320)

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Spearman correlation analysis of survey data from 255 postgraduate students found the association between live online class satisfaction and academic performance significant, with printed effect size (Spearman coefficient) r = 0.312 and p value 3.829×10 −7. The article notes low coefficients reflect limited ordinal scale ranges, not non-significance.
