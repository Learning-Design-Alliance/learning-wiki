## Evidence

### Salim Nabhan 2026

Salim Nabhan, Anita Habók. (2026). Language teachers' AI literacy: A psychometric study based on the ED-AI framework. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence 10 (2026) 100583. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100583

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Mixed quantitative–qualitative expert review with seven experts in teacher education rating items on a 4-point relevance scale. "The S-CVI/Ave for the entire scale was 0.98"; I-CVI ranged 0.86–1.00, S-CVI 0.94–1.00, S-CVI/UA 0.87, modified kappa 0.86–1.00.

> "The S-CVI/Ave for the entire scale was 0.98, exceeding the threshold for excellent content validity."
