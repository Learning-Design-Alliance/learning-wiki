# Unrestricted 24/7 access to CAI materials is associated with better ECG outcomes, while restricted access is not

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` CAI was better than face-to-face teaching when students had unlimited access to CAI learning materials (SMD=0.82, 95% CI 0.57 to 1.07). [→ Viljoen Charle André 2019](#viljoen-charle-andré-2019)
`q3 i?` The access benefit was not apparent when access to CAI learning materials was limited (SMD=−0.34, 95% CI −0.86 to 0.18). [→ Viljoen Charle André 2019 (2)](#viljoen-charle-andré-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Viljoen Charle André 2019

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q3 · i2`

Random-effects subanalysis of four studies (n=461) granting unlimited access to CAI materials. Pooled "SMD=0.82 (95% CI 0.57 to 1.07)" favoured CAI with I2=32% heterogeneity.

> "four studies showed that CAI was better than face-to-face teaching when students had unlimited access (ie, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week) to CAI learning materials (SMD=0.82 (95% CI 0.57 to 1.07); four studies, n=461; I2=32%)"

### Viljoen Charle André 2019 (2)

Viljoen Charle André, Scott Millar Rob, Engel Mark E, Shelton Mary, Burch Vanessa. (2019). Is computer-assisted instruction more effective than other educational methods in achieving ECG competence amongst medical students and residents? A systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-028800

`q3 · i?`

Companion subanalysis of three studies (n=284) with limited access. Pooled "SMD=−0.34 (95% CI −0.86 to 0.18)" did not favour CAI, with I2=74% heterogeneity.

> "This benefit, as shown in a subanalysis, was not apparent when access to CAI learning materials was limited (SMD=−0.34 (95% CI −0.86 to 0.18); three studies, n=284; I2=74%)"
