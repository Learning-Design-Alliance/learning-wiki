Evidence¶

Mingxin Liu 2024¶

Mingxin Liu, Tsuyoshi Okuhara, XinYi Chang, Ritsuko Shirabe, Yuriko Nishiie, Hiroko Okada, Takahiro Kiuchi. (2024). Performance of ChatGPT Across Different Versions in Medical Licensing Examinations Worldwide: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/60807

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Authors' interpretive conclusion from the discussion: GPT-4 exceeded 90% accuracy in only 2 tests, scored 52% on the Korean examination, failed one Japanese test at 67%, and gave detailed, logically sound explanations for incorrect answers that students may believe and memorize.

"However, because the knowledge of ChatGPT is not entirely accurate and its performance can be inconsistent, and because of the challenges posed by differing medical policies and knowledge across countries, we believe that GPT-4 is not yet suitable for use in medical education."
