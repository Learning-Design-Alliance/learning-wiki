## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Perceived stress rose significantly from T1 to T3 in the control cohort (median 19 to 23) but not significantly in the intervention cohort (median 18 to 21). [→ Lampe Luisa Charlotte 2021](#lampe-luisa-charlotte-2021)

## Evidence

### Lampe Luisa Charlotte 2021

Lampe Luisa Charlotte, Müller-Hilke Brigitte. (2021). Mindfulness-based intervention helps preclinical medical students to contain stress, maintain mindfulness and improve academic success. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-021-02578-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-cohort pairwise comparisons (Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-ranks tests) of PSS-10 scores from T1 to T3, six months after the intervention ended (Fig. 4a). Only medians, IQRs and significance are printed; no effect size is reported.

> "while the increase in perceived stress was significant for our control cohort (T1: median = 19, IQR = 10.5, T3: median = 23, IQR = 11.5), the difference between T1 and T3 did not reach statistical significance in the intervention cohort (T1: median = 18, IQR = 11, T3: median = 21, IQR = 10)"
