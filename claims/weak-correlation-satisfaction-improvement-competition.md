`q2 i2` Satisfaction with the competitive experience was significantly but weakly correlated with academic improvement (r = 0.292, p < 0.01). [→ Corell A 2018](#corell-a-2018)

## Evidence

### Corell A 2018

Corell A, Regueras LM, Verdú E, Verdú MJ, de Castro JP. (2018). Effects of competitive learning tools on medical students: A case study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0194096

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pearson correlation computed on survey data from 153 experimental-group students. The article reports a "significant correlation" between satisfaction and academic improvement that "was weak (r = 0.292, p < 0.01)". The correlation is observational and does not establish causation.

> "the results indicated that there was significant correlation between the students’ level of satisfaction with the competitive experience and their academic improvement, but this relation was weak (r = 0.292, p < 0.01)."
