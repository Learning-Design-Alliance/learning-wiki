Evidence¶

Laura Desiree Di Paolo 2026¶

Laura Desiree Di Paolo, Andy Clark & Thomas Wachter. (2026). Educating minds with generative AI. Communications Psychology 4:128. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44271-026-00522-8

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in this Perspective: unlike specialised, temporally bounded, passive tools such as books or Montessori materials, AI-based conversational systems function as active or semi-active participants in epistemic processes. The article states the convergence of functions within a single system is 'a scaling effect that is qualitatively distinct in the case of AI technologies'.

"the convergence of multiple (pedagogical) functions within a single system substantially ampliﬁes both the possibilities and the associated risks, a scaling effect that is qualitatively distinct in the case of AI technologies"
