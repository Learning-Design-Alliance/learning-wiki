## Evidence

### Thiyagu 2014

K. Thiyagu. (2014). Effectiveness of Video Podcast in Learning Psychology among Bachelor of Education Students. i-manager's Journal on School Educational Technology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097634

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Objective-wise analysis of gain scores in the same 48-student experiment. Knowledge and understanding objectives showed significant differences favoring the experimental group, while "the obtained 't' value 1.54 is less than the critical value" for application objectives, so no significant difference emerged there.

> "the obtained 't' values 4.45 and 6.22 are more than the critical value at 0.05 level of significance. And hence, the null hypothesis is rejected"
