## Evidence

### Wood Thomas Charles 2022

Wood Thomas Charles, Maqsood Sundas, Saunders Alex, Sancha William, Nanavaty Mayank A., Wearne Michael, Rajak Saul. (2022). Non-technical skills simulation-based training model for managing intraoperative posterior capsule rupture during cataract surgery. Eye. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41433-022-01962-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired pre-post analysis of seventeen cataract surgeons of varying training grades who completed two identical PCR simulations separated by an NTS-focused training intervention; two blinded assessors rated recorded videos with HUFOES. The article reports a "statistically significant increase in participant’s NTS" with the printed means and p < 0.001; no effect size is printed.

> "Following participation in the training intervention, a statistically significant increase in participant’s NTS was achieved. Mean HUFOES scores increased from 48.7 ± 16.6 to 59.2 ± 14.8 pre- and post-training intervention (p < 0.001)."
