Evidence¶

sound-test-1¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2012, May). Science intervention report: Technology Enhanced Elementary and Middle School Science (TEEMSS). Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/610.

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

WWC review of a quasi-experimental study in which the same teachers taught the TEEMSS sound unit to the 2005–06 treatment group (97 students) and regular methods to the prior-year comparison group (84 students). The WWC-calculated effect size was 0.65 with improvement index +24 and p-value 0.03; intervention posttest mean was 14.78 (3.38) versus 12.81 (2.56).

"Zucker et al. (2008) reported, and the WWC confirmed, statistically significant positive effects on the TEEMSS sound unit test for students in grades 3–4."

sound-test-1 (2)¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2012, May). Science intervention report: Technology Enhanced Elementary and Middle School Science (TEEMSS). Retrieved from https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/610.

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

The WWC findings table reports the sound unit test results for 181 grade 3–4 students: intervention mean 14.78 (SD 3.38), comparison mean 12.81 (SD 2.56), mean difference 1.97, effect size 0.65, improvement index +24, p-value 0.03, described as "Statistically significant".
