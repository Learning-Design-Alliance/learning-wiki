## Evidence

### Rahul K. Choudhary 2018

Rahul K. Choudhary, Alexander Foppoli, Tejinder Kaur, David G. Blair, Marjan Zadnik, Richard Meagher. (2018). Can a short intervention focused on gravitational waves and quantum physics improve students' understanding and attitude?. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/aae26a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/aae26a)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post comparison of 103 gifted and talented students from Years 7-10 after a one-day intervention, analyzed by t-tests with p set at 0.05. The article reports "a significant difference between pre -test and post -test total conceptual scores"; no effect size is printed.

> "The figures indicate a significant difference between pre -test and post -test total conceptual scores. This implies that Einsteinian physics concepts are not covered well in school science curriculum and can be readily introduced across Year 7 to 10 through active learning methods."
