Linear regression models fitted to EAPGS associations with pre-test, post-test, and pre-to-post difference scores for each of nine tasks (one-tailed tests, Bonferroni correction). The article reports results "show small effects that are very close to 0 and failed to reject the null after Bonferroni correction for multiple testing".
