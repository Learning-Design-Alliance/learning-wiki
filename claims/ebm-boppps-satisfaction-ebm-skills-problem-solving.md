# EBM-BOPPPS clerks reported significantly higher satisfaction on EBM-specific skills and problem-solving after Bonferroni correction

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` EBM-specific satisfaction items showed significant between-group differences: formulating clinical questions (r = 0.43, p = 0.001), critically appraising journal articles (r = 0.30, p = 0.003), and EBM's career importance (r = 0.43, p = 0.001). [→ Chen Yaxi 2025](#chen-yaxi-2025)
`q3 i2` Problem-solving skills satisfaction was significantly higher in the EBM-BOPPPS group (r = 0.31, p = 0.003). [→ Chen Yaxi 2025 (2)](#chen-yaxi-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Chen Yaxi 2025

Chen Yaxi, Xiao Zhanqin, Gu Xiang, Lang Qi, Chen Chanxi, Zhang Junhuai. (2025). Effect of the EBM-integrated BOPPPS model on clinical competence and EBM confidence in neurology clerkships for three-year junior college medical clerks. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1676073

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mann–Whitney U comparison of an 11-item five-point Likert satisfaction questionnaire in the RCT; all three EBM-specific items met the corrected significance standard (α = 0.003), with medians of 4 versus 3 and effect sizes up to "r = 0.43".

> "In "I can formulate a clinical question to search the best evidence," the EBM-BOPPPS group had a median of 4 (IQR = 1) versus 3 (IQR = 1) in the BOPPPS group (U = 715, r = 0.43, uncorrected p = 0.001)"

### Chen Yaxi 2025 (2)

Chen Yaxi, Xiao Zhanqin, Gu Xiang, Lang Qi, Chen Chanxi, Zhang Junhuai. (2025). Effect of the EBM-integrated BOPPPS model on clinical competence and EBM confidence in neurology clerkships for three-year junior college medical clerks. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1676073

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

General-competency satisfaction item in the same questionnaire; the difference reached the corrected threshold with "r = 0.31". Learning-attitude items showed only uncorrected trends that did not survive correction.

> ""The course develops my problem-solving skills" saw the EBM-BOPPPS group with a median of 4 (IQR = 0) and the BOPPPS group with 4 (IQR = 1) (U = 744, r = 0.31, uncorrected p = 0.003)"
