# Too Good for Violence produces statistically significant positive effects on elementary students' behavior 20 weeks after the program ends

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized controlled trial, students receiving the Too Good for Violence curriculum showed statistically significant differences favoring the intervention group on a teacher checklist of behavior at 20-week follow-up. [→ What Works Clearinghouse 2006](#what-works-clearinghouse-2006)

## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2006

What Works Clearinghouse. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Too Good for Violence. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.mendezfoundation.org

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A randomized controlled trial of almost 1,000 third-grade students in 10 elementary schools in one large Florida school district compared the Too Good for Violence curriculum with classes using no prevention curriculum. The WWC confirmed "statistically signiﬁcant differences favoring the intervention group" on the teacher behavior checklist.

> "Hall and Bacon (2005) reported, and the WWC conﬁrmed, statistically signiﬁcant differences favoring the intervention group on the teacher checklist of elementary school students’ behavior (total score) 20 weeks after the end of the program."
