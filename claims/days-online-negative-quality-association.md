## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a binary logistic regression based on senior surgeons' ratings, days online, number of likes, and median GEARS score were significantly associated with good/moderate video quality, while the number of visualizations was not; no standardized effect size is printed. ## Evidence

### Marotta A 2025

Marotta A, Schena CA, Ascanelli S, Azzolina D, Calabrese P, Iovino DP, Sani V, Gavriilidis P, Laterza V, Marchegiani F, Pellino G, Celentano V, de'Angelis N. (2025). Robotic ventral rectopexy videos on youtube: reliability of quality and educational value assessment among raters with different degrees of surgical experience. Int J Colorectal Dis 40:152. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-04936-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Binary logistic regression comparing 16 moderate/good versus 9 poor quality videos (per senior surgeons' ratings). The article reports significance for days online, likes, and GEARS score, and non-significance for visualizations; it prints no standardized effect size for this finding.

> "The number of days online, number of likes, and median GEARS score were significantly associated with good/moderate video quality (Table 4)."

### Marotta A 2025 (2)

Marotta A, Schena CA, Ascanelli S, Azzolina D, Calabrese P, Iovino DP, Sani V, Gavriilidis P, Laterza V, Marchegiani F, Pellino G, Celentano V, de'Angelis N. (2025). Robotic ventral rectopexy videos on youtube: reliability of quality and educational value assessment among raters with different degrees of surgical experience. Int J Colorectal Dis 40:152. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00384-025-04936-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bayesian ordinal regression of video quality ratings for the 25 videos; the utility model likewise found "The probability of being perceived as high-utility decreased with increasing days online".

> "The model revealed that perceived video quality decreased slightly with increasing days online. However, other engagement metrics, such as the number of likes or views, did not exhibit a significant relationship with quality."
