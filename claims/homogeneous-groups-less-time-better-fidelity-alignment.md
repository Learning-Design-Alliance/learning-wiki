## Evidence

### Fatima Zehra Allahverdi 2025

Fatima Zehra Allahverdi, Lynn Gelzheiser. (2025). Comparison of more effective and typical teachers' instructional quality and implementation of engaged reader processes. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09725-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational and descriptive analysis of 85 students (47 homogeneous, 38 heterogeneous groups). Table 6 reports means; Table 5 shows fidelity correlated 0.25* with post-test and CLASSIC correlated 0.20 (not significant at 0.05 level).

> "Homogenous groups spent less time on the engaged reader processes, but the fidelity ratings suggested that the engaged reader processes instruction they received was better aligned with the ISA-X intervention, as compared to the heterogeneous groups"
