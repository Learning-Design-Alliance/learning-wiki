Longitudinal logbook study of junior physicians in basic training; Spearman rank correlation of self-judged global completion (LC–JP) against six measurement points gave "r=0.35 (df=880, p<0.001, n=882, r2=0.12, weak effect)", supporting rejection of independence.

> "The Spearman rank correlation between the self-judged level of training goal completion (LC–JP) and training time (6 measurement points) is r=0.35 (df=880, p<0.001, n=882, r2=0.12, weak effect)."
