Estefany Castillo Ventura 2026¶

Estefany Castillo Ventura, Javier Ulises Solis Lastra, Anarosa Alves Franco Brandão. (2026). WIP: Bridging the Gap Between Instructional Design and Pedagogical Use: A Framework for Mathematics Educators. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20934

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Authors' interpretive discussion (type e), not a tested result: they argue from the review's finding of absent L4 operationalization that pedagogical use is delegated to teachers, making the teacher the central agent in integrating digital resources.

"As a consequence, aspects related to the pedagogical use of digital resources remain external to the technological artifact and are effectively delegated to the teacher at the moment of use. This creates a misalignment between theoretically grounded design and the absence of explicit structures to support its implementation in practice."
