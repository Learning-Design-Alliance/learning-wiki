## Evidence

### Ruxandra I. Tivadar 2023

Ruxandra I. Tivadar, Benedetta Franceschiello, Astrid Minier and Micah M. Murray. (2023). Learning and navigating digitally rendered haptic spatial layouts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00208-4

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Reconstruction analysis of the experiment with 25 blindfolded sighted participants: binarized LEGO reconstructions were compared to an ideal reconstruction via the Jaccard index and validated against mean ratings of 3 independent raters. The study reports "all above 0.7" Jaccard indices and the significant Pearson correlation "r = 0.53, t(23) = 2.99, p = 0.006".

> "We observed a signi ﬁcant correlation (measured as a Pearson ’s r) between the Similarity index and these mean scores of the subjective ratings ( r = 0.53, t(23) = 2.99, p = 0.006; 95% con ﬁdence Interval [0.17; 0.77])"
