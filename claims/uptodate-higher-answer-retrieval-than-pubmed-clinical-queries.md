### Sayyah Ensan Ladan 2011

To Compare PubMed Clinical Queries and UpToDate in Teaching Information Mastery to Clinical Residents: A Crossover Randomized Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0023487

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intention-to-treat analysis of a crossover randomized trial in which 44 residents each answered two clinical scenarios per database after equal training. The article reports "67 (76%) questions using UpToDate and 38 (43%) questions using PubMed Clinical Queries (P<0.001)"; no effect size is printed.
