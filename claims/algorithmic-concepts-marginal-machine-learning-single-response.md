International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00621-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The authors' interpretation of the frequency pattern: anthropomorphic attribution coexists with absence of procedural or algorithmic concepts, consistent with the black-box orientation described in the literature review.

> "Students are willing to attribute mind-like qualities to AI but are not activating the procedural or algorithmic concepts that would support critical understanding of how AI produces its outputs."
