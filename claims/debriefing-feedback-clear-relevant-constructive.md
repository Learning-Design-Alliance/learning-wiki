## Evidence

### Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John 2021

Makkink Andrew William & Dreyer Devin John. (2021). Simulation debriefing: a perspective from emergency medical care students at three South African Higher Education Institutions. The Pan African Medical Journal. https://doi.org/10.11604/pamj.2021.38.97.23009

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire survey of 153 students; frequency analysis. Table 3 shows 89 (58.2%) agreed and 19 (12.4%) strongly agreed feedback was easy to understand; 75 (49.0%) agreed and 62 (40.5%) strongly agreed they were positive about debriefing.

> "Generally, participants indicated that the feedback of debrief that they were given was easy to understand and that it was relevant and meaningful in that it was sufficient to identify areas requiring improvement."
