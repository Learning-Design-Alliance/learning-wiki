## Evidence

### Joekes K 2011

Joekes K, Noble LM, Kubacki AM, Potts HWW, Lloyd M. (2011). Does the inclusion of 'professional development' teaching improve medical students' communication skills? BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-41

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures ANOVA on 82 students (35 traditional, 47 professional development) videoed with a simulated patient in years 1 and 2, rated on the Interview Rating Scale. The article reports "a significant effect of time" but no cohort effect.

> "There was a significant effect of time (F1,81 = 30.9, p < 0.001), with students improving in observed communication skills over time, but not of cohort (F1,81 = 2.1, p = 0.15) or of the interaction between time and cohort (F1,81 = 1.6, p = 0.20)."
