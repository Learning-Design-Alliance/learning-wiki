Evidence¶

Fraj-Andrés E. 2018¶

Fraj-Andrés E., Lucia-Palacios L. & Pérez-López R. (2018). How extroversion affects student attitude toward the combined use of a wiki and video recording of group presentations. Computers & Education, 119, 31–43. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2017.12.006

q2 · i? · associational · r2

A survey of 106 first-year undergraduates after a wiki and video-recorded group presentation activity, analysed with PLS path models. Bootstrapped mediation analysis (Preacher and Hayes, 5000 samples) found attitude fully mediates both paths statistically; because all measures come from one cross-sectional questionnaire, these are associations and cannot show that advantages or enjoyment caused attitude or satisfaction; R2 of satisfaction increased when mediation was included. H4a and H5a supported, H4b and H5b not.

"The results show a total mediation effect of attitude. Attitude acts as a mediator between relative advantages and satisfaction, and between enjoyment and satisfaction."
