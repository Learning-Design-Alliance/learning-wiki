`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Thematic narrative synthesis of aetiology findings across the review's included studies. The review also reports nutrition was cited by 12–37% of GPs and other environmental factors by up to 25%. These are descriptive survey proportions; no effect size is printed.

> "Across times and locations, half to three-quarters of GPs (45–77 %) cited biological, neurological or related factors, but there was an equal or stronger emphasis on the influence of parenting, as half to nearly all (45–97 %) cited ineffective discipline, chaotic families, marital or family discord, or parental drug abuse."
