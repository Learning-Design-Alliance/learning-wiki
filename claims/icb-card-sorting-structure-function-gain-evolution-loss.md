## Evidence

### Luckie Douglas B. 2017

Luckie Douglas B., Hoskinson Anne-Marie, Griffin Caleigh E., Hess Andrea L., Price Katrina J., Tawa Alex, Thacker Samantha M. (2017). Integrating Concepts in Biology Textbook Increases Learning: Assessment Triangulation Using Concept Inventory, Card Sorting, and MCAT Instruments, Followed by Longitudinal Tracking. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-06-0204

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Card-sorting task administered pre, mid, and post across 16 introductory biology course sections; ICB students (n = 21) compared with controls (n = 101). Overall gains were equivalent, with subcategory differences in structure/function and evolution.

> "After normalization, no significant difference was found in pre- to posttest gains overall, except that a stronger understanding was seen for ICB students in the subcategory of structure and function for both pair and triplet sorts (pair: 77 ± 10% vs. 28 ± 3%, p < 0.01), and a weaker understanding of evolution for triplet sort data (triplet: −6.9 ± 1.7% vs. 26 ± 6.5%, p < 0.05; Figure 2)."
