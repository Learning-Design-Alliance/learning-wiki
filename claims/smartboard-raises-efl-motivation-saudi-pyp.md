## Evidence

### Ahmad 2017

Ahmad, W., Ali, Z., Sipra, M. A., & Taj, I. H. (2017). The Impact of Smartboard on Preparatory Year EFL Learners’ Motivation at a Saudi University. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 8(3). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.8n.3p.172

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental non-randomised pretest–posttest study with two intact Pre-Intermediate PYP classes (31 experimental, 30 control) taught for seven weeks; independent samples t-test on a 46-item six-point Likert motivation questionnaire (α = 0.911) showed the experimental group mean "(3.64) is higher than the mean score of the control group (3.40)".

> "The mean score of the experimental group (3.64) is higher than the mean score of the control group (3.40). The difference is significant at 0.05 level (p=0.01<0.05)"
