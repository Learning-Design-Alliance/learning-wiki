## Evidence

### Bjarne Skjødt Worm 2013

Bjarne Skjødt Worm. (2013). Learning from Simple Ebooks, Online Cases or Classroom Teaching When Acquiring Complex Knowledge. A Randomized Controlled Trial in Respiratory Physiology and Pulmonology. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0073336

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial with 21 nurses per group comparing eBook, eCase, and classroom teaching. The between-group ANOVA for simple recall was non-significant (F = 0.73, p = 0.49), and improvements were "almost to maximum" in all groups (about 65-68 percentage points).

> "For recall of simple knowledge (the lung volume curve), all educational methods improved knowledge almost to maximum and were equally successful (table 2)."
