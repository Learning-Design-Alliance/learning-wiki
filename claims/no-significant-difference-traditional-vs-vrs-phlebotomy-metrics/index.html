Evidence¶

Abeer William 2016¶

Abeer William, Victoria L. Vidal, Pamela John. (2016). Traditional Instruction Versus Virtual Reality Simulation: A Comparative Study of Phlebotomy Training among Nursing Students in Kuwait. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.9. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1095814

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental comparison of 33 control and 29 experimental ADN students performing phlebotomy on actual patients at Al Amiri Hospital's Phlebotomy Clinic; t-tests on five CathSim metrics showed "no performance metric wa s significant" (e.g., pain factor p=.702; reinsertions p=.943).
