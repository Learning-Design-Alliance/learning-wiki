## Evidence

### Christine Merrell 2015

Christine Merrell, Adetayo Kasim. (2015). Butterfly Phonics: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581118

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the randomised controlled trial restricted to pupils entitled to free school meals, analysed with multilevel models accounting for school effects (Table 10). The evaluators suggest the smaller FSM sample may account for the non-significance.

> "There were no statistically significant intervention effects, with effect sizes of 0.16 (-0.18, 0.49) for the NGRT, 0.42 (-0.06, 0.91) for the SWRT, and 0.18 (-0.14, 0.51) for the PhAB scores."
