The review reports a recent experimental study (Ruggeri, Markant, Gureckis, Bretzke, & Xu, 2019) on how actively controlling study behavior affected later memory performance among kindergarten and elementary-school children. The authors speculated this relates to ongoing development of cognitive and metacognitive abilities for study decisions.
