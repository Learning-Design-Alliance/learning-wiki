## Evidence

### LearnLM Team 2025

LearnLM Team, Google. (2025). Towards an AI-Augmented Textbook. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pedagogical expert evaluation: ten OpenStax PDFs, three grade levels and three interests, each PDF assigned three random grade/interest combinations, each component rated by three raters on rubric criteria (agree 1.0, partial 0.5, disagree 0.0).

> "The results are summarized in Figure 7, which shows that all components have relatively high pedagogy values and the overall experience is rated over0.90across all axes."

### LearnLM Team 2025 (2)

LearnLM Team, Google. (2025). Towards an AI-Augmented Textbook. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.13348)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Closer inspection of the Figure 7 ratings showed slides had the lowest engagement score, while slides with generative narration scored significantly higher, which the authors attribute to slides typically being presented alongside narration.

> "the slides format received the lowest ‘engagement’ score of all capabilities. On the other hand, these same slides but with generative narration received a significantly higher score."
