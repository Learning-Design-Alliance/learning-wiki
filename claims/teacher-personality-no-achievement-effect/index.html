Evidence¶

Kim 2018¶

Kim, Lisa, Dar-Nimrod, Ilan and MacCann, Carolyn (2018) Teacher Personality and Teacher Effectiveness in Secondary School: Personality Predicts Teacher Support and Student Self-Efficacy but not Academic Achievement. Journal of Educational Psychology. pp. 309-323. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000217

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel regressions predicting end-of-semester report-card grades from student-reported teacher personality, controlling for previous semester grades, student gender and student personality. Teacher-level variables predicted only 9% and 13% of level-2 variance; "none of the mean student-reports of teacher personality predicted academic achievement".

"At level 2, none of the mean student-reports of teacher personality predicted academic achievement for both mathematics and English, not in line with H4d."
