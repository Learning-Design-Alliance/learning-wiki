From the Discussion, synthesizing the two discourse-analytic cases: mitigation moves observed in both cases (undermining the critique's validity, distancing into abstraction, offering reasons to justify the criticized practice) failed when negative emotions persisted despite them.

> "colleagues initially employed common threat mitigation strategies (Vedder-Weiss et al., 2019), such as undermining the critique, but the failure of these mitigation strategies to restore the dominant emotional regime led to the production of deficit narratives to perform that function."
