### Scruggs 2019

Proceedings of the 28th International Conference on Computers in Education. Asia-Pacific Society for Computers in Education. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the discussion section, drawing on Corbett and Bhatnagar's (1997) observation that final mastery estimates under sufficient practice over-estimate posttest performance. The authors state "By averaging estimates across problems, we capture student knowledge throughout the learning process."

> "By averaging estimates across problems, we capture student knowledge throughout the learning process rather than apparent knowledge at the end – capturing lower performance on the eventual path to mastery – which appears to be a better estimate of the knowledge students carry out of their learning experience."
