## Evidence

### Abdulghani 2015

Abdulghani, H.M. et al. (2015). Faculty development programs improve the quality of Multiple Choice Questions items' writing. Sci. Rep. 5, 9556. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep09556

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Chi-square comparison of Bloom's cognitive levels in pre- versus post-training items across the three courses. The article reports K2 MCQs increased to n=123 post-training versus n=84 pre-training, and "K1 MCQs were decreased to 117 from 156 after training" with χ²=12.91, p<0.0001.
