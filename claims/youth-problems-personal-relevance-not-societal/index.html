Evidence¶

Gitari 2016¶

Gitari, W. (2016). The Application of School Science by Urban High School Youth through Problem-solving in Everyday Life. Science Education International, 27(3), 344-368. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1118432

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Authors' interpretation of the focus group narratives: youth sought to learn Frisbee, bake a cake, fix a tripped circuit and similar personally relevant tasks. The article notes this contrasts with Hodson's vision of societal-dimension relevance such as food, energy and the environment.

"Finally, the situations that prompt some of the youth to solve problems have personal relevance; they are in the personal spaces of relevance, as opposed to the global/societal spaces of significance."
