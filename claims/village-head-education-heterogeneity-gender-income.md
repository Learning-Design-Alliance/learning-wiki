## Evidence

### Li Jing 2025

Li Jing, Deng Huan, Li Jun. (2025). The impact of village heads' educational levels on adolescent academic performance: Evidence from rural China. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0315822

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Heterogeneity analysis (Table 7, Models 24-28) splitting the 1,720-observation sample by adolescent gender and by income terciles at the 25th and 75th percentiles. Printed coefficients: male 0.083, female 0.096, low 0.160, middle 0.040 (not significant), high 0.084; no standardized effect sizes.

> "For low-income families, the educational level of village heads significantly influences adolescent academic performance at the 1% level, with a coefficient of 0.160. In high-income families, highly educated village heads also positively impact adolescent academic performance, with a coefficient of 0.084; however, this is lower than the effect observed in low-income families."
