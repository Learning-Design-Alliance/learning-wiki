## Evidence

### Kumwenda B 2017

Kumwenda B, Cleland JA, Walker K, Lee AJ, Greatrix R. (2017). The relationship between school type and academic performance at medical school: a national, multi-cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-016291

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Independent samples t-tests comparing pre-entry scores by school type. Independent school students scored higher on UKCAT ("2535.1, SD=209.6" vs "2506.1, SD=224.0, p<0.001") and GAMSAT ("63.9, SD=6.9" vs "60.8, SD=7.1, p<0.001").

> "Students from independent schools had significantly higher mean UKCAT scores (2535.1, SD=209.6) than students from state-funded schools (2506.1, SD=224.0, p<0.001). Similarly, students from independent schools came into medical school with significantly higher mean GAMSAT scores (63.9, SD=6.9) than students from state-funded schools (60.8, SD=7.1, p<0.001)."
