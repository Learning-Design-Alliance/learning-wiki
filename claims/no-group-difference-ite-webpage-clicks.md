Behavioral data analysis in Phase 1 found "about 6.9 % of participants (n = 68)" clicked the government ITE webpage, with no significant difference between groups, χ2(2) = .392, p = .822, not supporting H3.

> "The behavioral data suggested that about 6.9 % of participants (n = 68) clicked on the webpage after participating in the interventions. However, no significant difference was observed between the three groups [χ2(2) = .392, p = .822]."
