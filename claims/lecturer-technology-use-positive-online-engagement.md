Same SEM survey study (n = 659); technology use paths to all three engagement dimensions are significant, with the strongest link to "behavioral engagement (β = 0.37, p < .001)".

> "The results show that lecturer technology use relates positively and significantly to student's cognitive (β = 0.26, p < .001), emotional (β = 0.36, p < .001), and behavioral engagement (β = 0.37, p < .001), supporting H2a, H2b and H2c."
