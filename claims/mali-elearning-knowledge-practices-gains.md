## Evidence

### Mroueh Lara 2025

Mroueh Lara, Bruand Pierre‐Emile, Poudiougou Oumar, Kleinebreil Line, Fall Ndao Amy, Moussaoui Driss, Preux Pierre‐Marie, Bagayoko Cheick Oumar, Boumediene Farid. (2025). Evaluating the Effects of Mental Health e‐Learning on the Knowledge, Attitudes and Practices of Primary Healthcare Professionals in Mali. A Pilot Study. Early Intervention in Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1111/eip.70001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Before/after quasi-experiment comparing online questionnaire scores pre and post each module for rural Malian healthcare professionals (paired module samples varying from 40 to 26). "practices scores increased significantly after the e‐learning for all modules, except the practices score for anxiety disorders"; Wilcoxon p < 0.001 for most knowledge scores and 0.008 for schizophrenia practices.

> "The knowledge, and practices scores increased significantly after the e‐learning for all modules, except the practices score for anxiety disorders."
