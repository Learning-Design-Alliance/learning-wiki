## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Among instructors with non-zero research appointments, research activity level has a negligible effect size on lecture, with very active researchers lecturing more than others in post hoc comparison. [→ Apkarian 2021](#apkarian-2021)
`q2 i2` Instructors involved in education research, scholarship of teaching and learning, or curricular improvement lecture less than those who are not, with a medium effect. [→ Apkarian 2021 (2)](#apkarian-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Apkarian 2021

Apkarian, N., Henderson, C., Stains, M., Raker, J., Johnson, E., & Dancy, M. (2021). What really impacts the use of active learning in undergraduate STEM education? Results from a national survey of chemistry, mathematics, and physics instructors. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0247544. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247544

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA among instructors with non-zero research appointments ranked by self-reported publications, grants, and presentations; the printed effect size is η2 = 0.009, below the small threshold, with very active researchers lecturing more in post hoc comparison.

> "Research activity level is an effect on lecture, F(3,1840) = 5.60, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.009 (small)."

### Apkarian 2021 (2)

Apkarian, N., Henderson, C., Stains, M., Raker, J., Johnson, E., & Dancy, M. (2021). What really impacts the use of active learning in undergraduate STEM education? Results from a national survey of chemistry, mathematics, and physics instructors. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0247544. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247544

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

t-test comparing lecture percentage between instructors with and without involvement in education research or funded instructional improvement in the national survey; the article reports a medium effect, g = 0.49.

> "Instructors who participate in education research, scholarship of teaching and learning, and/or curricular improvement projects lecture less than those who do not t(286.2) = 13.6, p < 0.001, g = 0.49 (medium)."
