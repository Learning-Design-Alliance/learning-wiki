Evidence¶

Luotong Hui 2021¶

Luotong Hui, Anique B. H. de Bruin, Jeroen Donkers, & Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2021). Does Individual Performance Feedback Increase the Use of Retrieval Practice? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09604-x

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Experiment 1, a single-factor within-subjects experiment with 68 university students studying anatomical image-name pairs, compared delayed recall after retrieval practice versus restudy. The paired-samples t-test showed students "performed significantly better after retrieval practice than after restudy" with d = .25.

"A paired-samples t-test revealed that students indeed performed significantly better after retrieval practice than after restudy (t(59) = 1.99, p =. 0 5 ,d = .25) (see Table 1), confirming the testing effect."

Luotong Hui 2021 (2)¶

Luotong Hui, Anique B. H. de Bruin, Jeroen Donkers, & Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2021). Does Individual Performance Feedback Increase the Use of Retrieval Practice? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09604-x

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Experiment 2, a mixed two-factor design with 124 university students (individual vs. general feedback between subjects), replicated the delayed-test advantage of retrieval practice over restudy, "confirmed again" with t(120) = 3.53 and d = .36.

"As in Experiment 1, Hypothesis 1 predicted the testing effect which was confirmed again (t(120) = 3.53, p <. 0 0 1 ,d= .36)."
