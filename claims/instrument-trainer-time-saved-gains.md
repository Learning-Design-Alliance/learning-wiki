## Evidence

### Clarke David B. 2016

Clarke David B., Kureshi Nelofar, Hong Murray, Sadeghi Maryam, D'Arcy Ryan C. N. (2016). Simulation-based training for burr hole surgery instrument recognition. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0669-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures analysis of Group A's Instrument Trainer sessions showed significant additional time saved with each repetition (p = 0.007 between sessions 1 and 2; p = 0.002 between sessions 2 and 3); no effect size was printed.

> "There were additional savings in time with each repetition of the simulation: between session 1 and session 2 (p = 0.007) as well as between session 2 and session 3 (p = 0.002)."
