## Evidence

### Nawka MT 2020

Nawka MT, Hanning U, Guerreiro H, Flottmann F, Van Horn N, Buhk J-H, Fiehler J, Frölich AM. (2020). Feasibility of a customizable training environment for neurointerventional skills assessment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238952

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Spearman rank correlation of catheterization time across consecutive attempts in the six-operator HANNES training protocol. The study reports "rho = -0.493, p = 0.009" for inexperienced operators and rho = -0.318, p = 0.106 for experienced operators; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "the inexperienced physicians became significantly faster during the course of the attempts (rho = -0.493, p = 0.009), whereas the experienced operators showed only a trend towards improvement of catheterization times over time (rho = -0.318, p = 0.106)."
