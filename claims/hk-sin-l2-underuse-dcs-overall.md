# Hong Kong and Singaporean L2 students underuse discourse connectives overall compared with L1 writers, while Thai students do not differ significantly

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Hong Kong and Singaporean students use significantly fewer DCs overall than L1 writers, whereas Thai students' overall frequency shows no significant difference from L1 students. [→ Hu 2015](#hu-2015)

## Evidence

### Hu 2015

Hu, C., & Li, Y. (2015). Discourse Connectives in L1 and L2 Argumentative Writing. Higher Education Studies. https://doi.org/10.5539/hes.v5n4p30

`q2 · i2`

Corpus comparison of DC frequencies per 50,000 words across HKG_B1_2, SIN_B1_2, THA_B1_2 and ENS1 sub-corpora using the log-likelihood ratio test; Table 3 marks the HKG (1155**) and SIN (1100**) totals as highly significant versus ENS1 (1350), with THA (1422) not marked. The study reports "no significant difference in the overall frequencies between Thai students and L1 students".

> "while there is no significant difference in the overall frequencies between Thai students and L1 students, Hong Kong and Singaporean students underuse DCs than do their L1 counterparts."
