## Evidence

### Melodie Baker 2023

Melodie Baker, Ivy Morgan, and Gizella Wade. (2023). Opportunities Denied: High-Achieving Black and Latino Students Lack Access to Advanced Math. EdTrust and Just Equations. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED641831

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

HSLS:09 survey analysis of student-reported school climate by advanced course-taking status (Figure 5), with several group differences marked statistically significant at the .05 level. The report links the Black-student pattern to a possible lack of sense of belonging, citing prior work on racial/ethnic context in math classes.

> "About 80% of high-achieving Black students reported feeling safe and proud to be in their schools — regardless of whether they took advanced math courses. This contrasts with the pattern for all other student groups — where students who did take advanced math courses reported feeling safe and proud in their schools at higher rates than students who did not take advanced math courses."
