Liqun He, Manolis Mavrikis, Mutlu Cukurova. (2025). Towards Mining Effective Pedagogical Strategies from Learner–LLM Educational Dialogues. AIED Doctoral Consortium paper, University College London. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-99261-2_42](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99261-2_42)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive comparison of pilot dialogue data from six dialogues per group (first and last day of practice) for the high-progress and low-progress groups. The paper reports the printed means: "averaging 15.3 words per turn compared to 11.5 words in the LP group"; no statistical test is reported.

> "The average number of dialogue turns per session was similar between the HP group (83.3 turns) and the LP group (86.7 turns). However, students in the HP group produced longer utterances, averaging 15.3 words per turn compared to 11.5 words in the LP group."
