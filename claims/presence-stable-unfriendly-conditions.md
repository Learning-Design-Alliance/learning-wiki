## Evidence

### Francesco Vona 2025

Francesco Vona, Michael Stern, Navid Ashrafi, Julia Schorlemmer, Jessica Stemann, and Jan-Niklas Voigt-Antons. (2025). Sales Skills Training in Virtual Reality: An evaluation utilizing CAVE and Virtual Avatars. arXiv. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-93715-6_16](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-93715-6_16)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the within-subjects study (N=20) show "consistent perceptions of presence across conditions"; the highest IPQ total was in Condition 4 UUxUE (M=3.33, SD=1.15) and the lowest in Condition 2 FUxUE (M=3.17, SD=1.10). No inferential test supported a difference.

> "The total scores of the IPQ demonstrated consistent perceptions of presence across conditions, with mean values spanning from 3.17 to 3.33."
