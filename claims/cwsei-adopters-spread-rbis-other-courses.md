## Evidence

### Carl Wieman 2013

Carl Wieman, Louis Deslauriers, Brett Gilley. (2013). Use of research-based instructional strategies: how to avoid faculty quitting. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.023102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.023102)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Follow-up of EOAS and PHAS adopters via SES knowledge and a survey of remaining faculty; the authors note many results are self-reports and that faculty "know what we would like to hear."

> "Twenty-three of the 25 RBIS users on the PHAS faculty were found to be using RBIS in additional courses. Four of the 29 non-quitting EOAS faculty have not taught a different undergraduate course. Of the remaining 25, 23 have implemented RBIS in additional courses."
