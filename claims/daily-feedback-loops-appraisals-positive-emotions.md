## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Higher cognitive appraisals prior to learning predicted higher positive emotions after learning, and increased positive emotions after learning predicted increased cognitive appraisals the next day prior to learning. [→ Berweger 2024](#berweger-2024)

## Evidence

### Berweger 2024

Berweger, B., Breitwieser, J., Kracke, B., Brod, G., & Dietrich, J. (2024). Daily Affective-Motivational Experiences During Exam Preparation: Feedback Loops, Stability, and Change Over Time. PsychArchives preprint. https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12034/10953

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

DSEM analysis of daily diary reports from medical students during a 40-day exam preparation period. The article reports that "students who reported higher cognitive appraisals prior to learning experienced higher levels of positive emotions after learning", with the reverse path appearing the next day. No effect sizes are printed.

> "students who reported higher cognitive appraisals prior to learning experienced higher levels of positive emotions after learning, and an increase in positive emotions following learning was associated with an increase in cognitive appraisals the next day prior to learning."
