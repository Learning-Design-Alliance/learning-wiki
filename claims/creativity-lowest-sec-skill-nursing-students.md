Kim Sun-Hee, Shin Sujin. (2021). Social–Emotional Competence and Academic Achievement of Nursing Students: A Canonical Correlation Analysis. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. [doi:10.3390/ijerph18041752](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph18041752)

Descriptive statistics (Table 2, n = 195, scores rescaled to 100 points) from the same survey. The article reports "a score of 80.8 was reported for collaboration" and 59.4 for creativity; AA means were 72.6 for clinical performance and 63.5 for subjective AA.

> "For SEC, a score of 80.8 was reported for collaboration, 72.8 for problem-solving, 72.7 for emotional intelligence, 71.0 for critical thinking disposition, 67.6 for self-directed learning, and 59.4 for creativity."
