## Subclaims
`q4 i?` Heterogeneity analysis shows positive interaction terms for disadvantaged subgroups; blackboard usage explains 29.45% and household assets 35.87% of the 0.092 SD impact difference, and together four variables explain about 75%. [→ Du Kang 2022](#du-kang-2022)

## Evidence

### Du Kang 2022

Du Kang, Wang Huan, Ma Yue, Guan Hongyu, Rozelle Scott. (2022). Effect of Eyeglasses on Student Academic Performance: What Matters? Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial in China. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph191710923

`q4 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Heterogeneity regression and decomposition analysis (Tables 7 and 8) pooling both provinces. The article reports the blackboard-usage interaction coefficient of 0.100 significant at the 1% level, and that the four variables together explain about 75% of the difference in impact.

> "Blackboard usage, when considered together, has a differential impact of 0.027 standard deviations (0.271 × 0.100) and contributes 29.45% of the overall impact difference of 0.092 SD (0.027/0.092)."
