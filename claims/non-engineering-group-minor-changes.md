`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative pre/post drawing comparison for seven consenting students in the Non-Engineering control group (Poverty in the Movies course). The article reports "Minor changes were seen in the drawings" with only two students changing their depiction and three indicating HSS connections, findings the authors deemed unsurprising given the course content.

> "Minor changes were seen in the drawings of the students in the “Non-engineering” group (Table 3). Only two students made a change in their depiction of an engineer and three students provided some level of indication that engineers had a connection with the humanities and social sciences."
