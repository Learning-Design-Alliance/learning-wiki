`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

A conference report of Dearborn's Dodge photographic recording of eye-movements of children in the early grades, aimed at formulating a unit of measurement of progress in reading ability; the evidence is described as "highly suggestive but as yet hardly conclusive".

> "He adduced highly suggestive but as yet hardly conclusive evidence to show that the number of ocular fixations in each line may be used as an index of the number of acts of attention, and consequently employed to discover, for example, whether a child really reads by word-wholes or by letters and syllables."
