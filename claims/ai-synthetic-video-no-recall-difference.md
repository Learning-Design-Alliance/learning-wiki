## Evidence

### Lia 2024

Lia, Z. R.-Y., Barry, C., & Cukurova, M. (2024). Adult learners' recall and recognition performance and affective feedback when learning from an AI-generated synthetic video. Technical Report. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.10384](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.10384)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of recall (fill-in-the-blank) post-test scores with pre-test covariate in the same 400-participant four-condition online experiment. Adjusted post-test means were 56.7, 56.5, 56.9 and 52.3 for C1-C4, and the article states the effect "was not significant (F(3,373) = 0.75, p = .53, η2 = .01)".

> "However, the mean difference of the post-test scores between the four conditions was not significant (F(3,373) = 0.75, p = .53, η2 = .01), with only 1% of the variance explained by the conditions."
