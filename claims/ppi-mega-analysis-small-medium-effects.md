The review summarises Carr et al.'s (2023) mega-analysis of PPIs, which aggregates 198 meta-analyses. The review reports that "effect sizes remained within the small–to–medium range even after adjustments" for heterogeneity and publication bias; no single pooled effect size is quoted for this statement.
