## Evidence

### Punja R 2026

Punja R, Punja D, Prasad Kamachi Christdoss Ruben. (2026). Large-group virtual anatomy via a smart digital podium: a preliminary cross-sectional study of student perspectives toward remote anatomage teaching in a medical lecture hall. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10019-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive cross-sectional survey of 94 first-year MBBS students immediately after a RAT session. Combined "positive engagement response of 72/94 (76.6%)" with 17/94 (18.1%) finding it about the same and 5/94 (5.3%) less engaging. No inferential statistics were performed.

> "Thirty-six out of 94 students (38.3%) rated RAT as ‘much more engaging’ and 36/94 (38.3%) as ‘more engaging’ than regular theory classes, giving a combined positive engagement response of 72/94 (76.6%)."
