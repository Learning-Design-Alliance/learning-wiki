Systematic review of the 94 included studies' reported correlations. While most studies described the constructs as positively related, eight reported negative correlations "ranging from r = − 0.01 to r = − 0.43", with five based on teacher reports (e.g., Archambault et al.; Domen et al.; Hornstra et al.; Kurdi et al.).
