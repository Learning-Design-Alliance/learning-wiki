## Evidence

### Kristanto 2018

Kristanto, Y. D. (2018). Technology-enhanced pre-instructional peer assessment: Exploring students' perceptions in a Statistical Methods course. REiD (Research and Evaluation in Education), 4(2), 105-116. [doi:10.21831/reid.v4i2.20951](https://doi.org/10.21831/reid.v4i2.20951)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive statistics of LMS logs from three peer assessment meetings, sorted in Microsoft Excel; the mean total assessment phase duration was 43.50 minutes, and the logs show 'a sharp decrease in the mean of first and second assessment tasks period' per meeting.

> "The table reveals that there has been a sharp decrease in the mean of first and second assessment tasks period carried out by the students in each meeting."

### Kristanto 2018 (2)

Kristanto, Y. D. (2018). Technology-enhanced pre-instructional peer assessment: Exploring students' perceptions in a Statistical Methods course. REiD (Research and Evaluation in Education), 4(2), 105-116. [doi:10.21831/reid.v4i2.20951](https://doi.org/10.21831/reid.v4i2.20951)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Log data from the second meeting, when students first reviewed an instructor-provided example submission before assessing peers: 'reviewed example assessment in nearly a half of an hour (26.83 mins)', then 12.69 and 6.72 mins for the two peers' works.

> "the students reviewed example assessment in nearly a half of an hour (26.83 mins), the first peer's works in almost a quarter of an hour (12.69 mins), and the second peer's works in just over six minutes (6.72 mins)."
