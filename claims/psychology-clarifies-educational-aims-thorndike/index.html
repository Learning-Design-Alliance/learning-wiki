Evidence¶

Thorndike 1910¶

Thorndike, E. L. (1910). The contribution of psychology to education. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070113

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument from the article's opening exposition on aims; no data are offered. The author argues psychology helps by requiring notions of aims to be put "in terms of the exact changes that education is to make" and cites heredity of acquisitions as an aim psychology can declare futile.

"Psychology contributes to a better understanding of the aims of education by denning them, making them clearer; by limiting them, showing us what can be done and what can not"
