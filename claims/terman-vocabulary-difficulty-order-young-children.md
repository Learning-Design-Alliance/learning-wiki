### Cobb 1921

Cobb, Margaret V. (1921). Tentative Order of Difficulty of the Terman Vocabulary with Very Young Children. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Tabulation of per-word success, failure, and half-credit scores from Stanford-Binet vocabulary examinations of kindergarten and Grade I children, conducted by five examiners. The tabulated words "show an order of difficulty for small children which is noticeably different from that indicated on the blank."

> "it became evident that the words in the Vocabulary Test (Stanford Revision) show an order of difficulty for small children which is noticeably different from that indicated on the blank."
