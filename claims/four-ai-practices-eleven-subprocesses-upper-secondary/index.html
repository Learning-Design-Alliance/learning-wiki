Dalsgaard 2026¶

Dalsgaard, C., & Prilop, C. N. (2026). AI as a cognitive partner in learning: Emerging epistemic practices of reflective thought. Journal of Research on Technology in Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2695013

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative reflexive thematic analysis of eight group interviews with 48 students (28 male, 20 female) across eight Danish upper secondary schools, conducted over two intervention cycles. The analysis "identified 4 main themes for student practices involving AI tools" with "11 sub-themes"; no effect sizes are reported.

"The analysis identified 4 main themes for student practices involving AI tools: 1. Explanation, 2. Creation, 3. Inspiration, and 4. Improvement. Within these practices, a total of 11 sub-themes for more specific processes were identified"
