Gamor 2021¶

Gamor, K.I. (2021). Insights on identifying potential types of guidance for supporting student inquiry when using virtual and remote labs in science. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09928-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

A response piece assessing the Zacharia et al. (2015) literature review from a value-add perspective; the author states the review "illuminates the benefits of leveraging the appropriate types of guidance during the relevant phases of inquiry". No new empirical data are reported.

"This literature review illuminates the benefits of leveraging the appropriate types of guidance during the relevant phases of inquiry, a much-needed type of guidance, as it aptly describes how and when to apply the types of guidance for optimal effect."
