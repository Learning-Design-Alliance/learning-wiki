### Abid H. Mujtaba 2016

Abid H. Mujtaba. (2016). Analyzing student conceptual understanding of resistor networks using binary, descriptive, and computational questions. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1611.06583](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1611.06583)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Venn-diagram probability analysis of the 28 students' responses. The article reports "10 students whose answers to the binary and computational questions didn't agree with each other" and "35% of the students failed to make the connection between the two parts."

> "Another interesting tidbit hidden in the data was P (B ∩ ¯C) = 6 /28 and P ( ¯B ∩ C) = 4 /28 which (being disjoint sets) meant there were 10 students whose answers to the binary and computational questions didn't agree with each other. So 35% of the students failed to make the connection between the two parts."
