`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is the authors' own stated limitation in the Limitations section, a theoretical argument rather than an empirical result. The article adds that the authors do not know the extent to which inaccurate assumptions about barriers could negatively impact performance.

> "A primary limitation is that our approach is contingent on the ability of a supervisor to accurately perceive specific barriers for each individual."
