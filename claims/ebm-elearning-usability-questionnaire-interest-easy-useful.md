## Evidence

### Okabayashi 2022

Okabayashi, S., Kitazawa, K., Kawamura, T., & Nakayama, T. (2022). E-Learning Material of Evidence-Based Medicine for Laypersons. HLRP: Health Literacy Research and Practice, 6(4), e290-e299. https://doi.org/10.3928/24748307-20221113-01

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire survey (N = 25) with 5-point Likert ratings of usability, usefulness, effectiveness, and satisfaction after participants used the material on personal computers; descriptive percentages only, no inferential tests reported.

> "Most participants (19; 76%) were interested or very interested in the contents of the material. Further, 22 participants (88%) evaluated the material as easy to follow, and 18 (72%) evaluated it as useful in their daily lives."
