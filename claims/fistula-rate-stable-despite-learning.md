## Evidence

### Conroy Elizabeth J 2025

Conroy Elizabeth J, Blazeby Jane M, Burnside Girvan, Cook Jonathan A, Gamble Carrol. (2025). Investigating the presence of surgical learning in the Timing of Primary Surgery for cleft palate randomised trial. Clinical Trials. https://doi.org/10.1177/17407745241302488

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Two-level multilevel logistic model of fistula occurrence for the 521 infants with complete outcome data, surgeon as random effect. Neither operation sequence nor technique experience was statistically significant, consistent with the cusum plots showing no clear within-surgeon trends in fistula rate.

> "Model B (adjusted for learning and technique), again incorporating operation sequence and technique experience, indicates that neither covariate is a predictive factor of fistula (Operation sequence: OR: 0.991; 95% CI: 0.441 to 2.822; p = 0.3567; Technique experience: OR: 1.115; 95% CI: 0.441 to 2.822; p = 0.8177)"

### Conroy Elizabeth J 2025 (2)

Conroy Elizabeth J, Blazeby Jane M, Burnside Girvan, Cook Jonathan A, Gamble Carrol. (2025). Investigating the presence of surgical learning in the Timing of Primary Surgery for cleft palate randomised trial. Clinical Trials. https://doi.org/10.1177/17407745241302488

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Unadjusted multilevel logistic model comparing fistula rates between the 6-month and 12-month surgery arms (73 fistulas among 521 infants). No significant difference was found; equivalence was not tested and the confidence interval was wide.

> "Model A (unadjusted) shows that there was no significant difference between the fistula rates within the two treatment groups, although the confidence intervals are wide (OR: 1.187; 95% CI: 0.720 to 1.957; p = 0.5008)"
