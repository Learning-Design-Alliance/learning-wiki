## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a 5-year observational cohort, the cumulative pregnancy rate did not differ between experienced clinicians (39%) and trainees (45%) for the first 50 embryo transfers (p=0.41) or the last 50 (40.7% versus 42.7%, p=0.81). [→ Mittal Monica 2019](#mittal-monica-2019)

## Evidence

### Mittal Monica 2019

Mittal Monica, Supramaniam Prasanna Raj, Lim Lee Nai, Hamoda Haitham, Savvas Mike, Narvekar Nitish. (2019). Is the clinician an independent variable in embryo transfer outcomes under standardized direct and indirect supervision? A 5-year observational cohort study. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001215](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001215)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective observational cohort study of all consecutive embryo transfers over 5 years in a single fertility unit, comparing three consultants with three trainee fellows; outcome was cumulative biochemical pregnancy rate via urinary test 16 days after oocyte retrieval. The study found "The CPR did not differ between an experienced clinician (39%) and trainee (45%)" for first and last 50 procedures.

> "The CPR did not differ between an experienced clinician (39%) and trainee (45%) for the first 50 (p=0.41) and last 50 (40.7% versus 42.7%) (p=0.81) ET procedures."
