## Evidence

### Qian He 2025

Qian He, Jiahao Li, Ye Xiao, Jingjing Tian, Ningbo Mao, Sheng Lu, Yu Zhang, Yu Zhao. (2025). An immersive mixed reality surgical self-training system for precise pedicle screw placement: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07978-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

CT-measured screw orientation in the RCT's unaided assessment session: independent t-test showed the IMR group's transverse angle "13.88°±6.98°" significantly smaller than the control group's 20.89°±11.59° (p = 0.049); smaller absolute angles indicate better insertion. Lateral angle did not differ (p = 0.800).

> "In the assessment session, the transverse angle in the IMR group (13.88°±6.98°) was significantly smaller than that in the control group (20.89°±11.59°, p = 0.049)."
