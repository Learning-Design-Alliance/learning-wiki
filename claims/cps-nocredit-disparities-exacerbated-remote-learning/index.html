Evidence¶

Gwynne 2022¶

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., & Liang, Y. (2022). Student engagement in learning during COVID-19: Students' course grades in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/office-of-equity/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of no-credit grade rates by student background among 223,096 grades 4-8 students, with hierarchical linear models comparing students within the same school. The report finds rates "increased from 12 to 46 percent for students eligible for free lunch" and larger increases for students of color.

"Rates of no-credit grades increased from 12 to 46 percent for students eligible for free lunch and from 9 to 38 percent for students eligible for reduced-priced lunch, ( see Figure 5, Panel A ); no-credit rates increased from 14 to 47 percent and 11 to 43 percent for Black and Latinx students, respectively"
