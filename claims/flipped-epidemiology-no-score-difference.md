## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Sohn Sangho 2019

Sohn Sangho, Lee Young-Mee, Jung Jaehun, Cha Eun Shil, Chun Byung Chul. (2019). The flipped classroom model for an undergraduate epidemiology course. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.122

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Quantitative comparison of pre-test and final exam scores in the flipped 'practice of epidemiology' course, analyzed with paired comparisons in SAS and R. The article reports "average score difference=-0.68, p=0.66", with no effect size printed.

> "The overall results did not show significant differences between the pre-tests and final exam (average score difference=-0.68, p=0.66)."

### Sohn Sangho 2019 (2)

Sohn Sangho, Lee Young-Mee, Jung Jaehun, Cha Eun Shil, Chun Byung Chul. (2019). The flipped classroom model for an undergraduate epidemiology course. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subset analysis repeating identical pre-test questions on the final exam (Fig. 1, Sets A and B). The article reports "average score difference=-0.26, p=0.90"; no effect size is printed.

> "Narrowing the results to the subsets of questions on the pre-tests and the same questions again on the final exam did not yield a significant difference either (average score difference=-0.26, p=0.90)."
