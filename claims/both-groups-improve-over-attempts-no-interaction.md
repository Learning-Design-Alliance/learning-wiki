## Evidence

### Østdal TB 2024

Østdal TB, Tang DHY, Olsen RG, Olsen LM, Konge L, Bjerrum F. (2024). 3D monitors improve performance on the HUGO™ RAS system: a randomised trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11615040/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same repeated-measures ANOVA of the randomised simulator trial, main effects of time were significant (e.g. Exercise 4 Time to complete exercise F(4,116) = 136.18, p < 0.001), while time × group interactions were non-significant (p = 0.30–0.89), so "the intervention group did not improve increasingly more over time compared to the control group".

> "There was no statistically significant interaction between time and intervention, meaning that the intervention group did not improve increasingly more over time compared to the control group. This was also consistent for all four exercises."
