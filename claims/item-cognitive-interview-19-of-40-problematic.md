Cognitive interview study with 10 native Arabic-speaking medical students (4 year 3, 6 year 4) after VR clinical skills sessions, using think-aloud and verbal probing. The item-level overview reports "47.5% (19/40)" of questions flagged as problematic, with domain-level frequencies as printed.

> "Across the 40 questions of ITEM, participants identified 47.5% (19/40) as presenting a comprehension or response-process problem. Problems occurred across all 5 domains: immersion (6/9, 66.7%), usability (6/10, 60%), debriefing (4/5, 80%), motivation (2/10, 20%), and cognitive load (1/6, 16.7%)."
