## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught economics concepts with the demonstration method scored significantly higher on post-tests than students taught with the lecture method in both schools (t-calculated 2.721 and 2.731 exceeded the t-value of 2.021 at the 0.05 level). [→ Muhammad 2016](#muhammad-2016)

## Evidence

### Muhammad 2016

Muhammad, A. U., Bala, D., & Ladu, K. M. (2016). Effectiveness of Demonstration and Lecture Methods in Learning Concept in Economics among Secondary School Students in Borno State, Nigeria. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(12). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099610

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental study with 104 SS1 students in two intact-class schools (26 per group per school), taught for four weeks and assessed with 25 WAEC past-question items. The t-test comparison of post-test means favoured demonstration: "In both schools, demonstration groups performed better than the lecture groups".

> "In both schools, demonstration groups performed better than the lecture groups at 0.05% level of significance and t-calculated 2.721 and 2.731 was greater than the t-value of 2.021 in respect of the two schools."
