Leman Figen Gül 2025¶

Leman Figen Gül, Burak Delikanlı, Oğulcan Üneşi, Ertuğrul Ömer Gül. (2025). Development and applications of Generative AI in architectural design studios. ITU A|Z • Vol 22 No 3 • November 2025 • 751-774. https://doi.org/10.58278/0.2025.109

q2 · i? · design · r2

Based on the authors' discussions and observations of students' outcomes in the studios, comparing two prompt types. The article reports the contrast descriptively from student outputs; no statistical test or effect size is printed.

"the images that align more closely with the prompts focused on the theme of the future, resembling stage-like creations seen in science fiction movie sets, yield more favorable results. In contrast, the prompts that reference architectural styles or local architectures don't yield the same desired outcomes."
