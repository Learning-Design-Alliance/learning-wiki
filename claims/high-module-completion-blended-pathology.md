`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational usage data from SCORM packages for 264 Pathology students in Semester 2, 2016. The article reports that "74% of enrolled students" attempted all 29 modules and 85% completed 90% or more. Modules were eventually completed by 83.1% of students.

> "By the completion of the course, 74% of enrolled students (n = 264) had attempted all 29 online modules and 85% of enrolled students had completed 90% or more of the modules."
