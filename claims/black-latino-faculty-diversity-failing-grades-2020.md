## Subclaims
`q2 i?` 57.3% of public four-year institutions earned failing grades for Black faculty diversity relative to student diversity in 2020, and 79.6% earned F grades for Latino faculty diversity. [→ Jinann Bitar 2022](#jinann-bitar-2022)

## Evidence

### Jinann Bitar 2022

Jinann Bitar, Gabriel Montague, Lauren Ilano. (2022). Faculty Diversity and Student Success Go Hand in Hand, So Why Are University Faculties So White? The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED626440

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ed Trust analysis of IPEDS Fall 2020 staff and enrollment data for 543 public four-year institutions, comparing faculty racial composition to student enrollment. The analysis found "More than half (57.3%) of institutions earned failing grades for Black faculty diversity"; Latino faculty diversity fared worse, with 79.6% receiving F grades. No effect size is printed.

> "More than half (57.3%) of institutions earned failing grades for Black faculty diversity, and another 13.3% received a D grade. Only 13.4% earned an A grade, 7% earned a B grade"
