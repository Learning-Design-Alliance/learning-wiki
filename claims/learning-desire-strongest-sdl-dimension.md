## Evidence

### Su Wenhao 2025

Su Wenhao, Fadzil Hidayah Mohd, Rauf Rose Amnah Abd. (2025). Bridging self-directed learning and competency in interdisciplinary teaching among STEM lecturers: How psychological capital connects. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0319305

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Spearman correlation analysis of the 408-lecturer survey data. The article reports "the learning desire dimension within the variable of self-directed learning exhibited the strongest correlation with the dependent variable competency in interdisciplinary teaching (r = 0.630, p < 0.001)".

> "the learning desire dimension within the variable of self-directed learning exhibited the strongest correlation with the dependent variable competency in interdisciplinary teaching (r = 0.630, p < 0.001) and also demonstrated a significant positive correlation with the interdisciplinary teaching evaluation dimension (r = 0.631, p < 0.001)."
