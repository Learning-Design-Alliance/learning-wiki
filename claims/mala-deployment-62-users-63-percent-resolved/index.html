Gabelmann 2026¶

Gabelmann, J., Jahn, F., Baum, K., van Rossum, S., Wuenscher, E., Gros, T. P., Wolf, V. (2026). Modularizing Educational LLM-Agency for Fostering Responsible Learning Assistance. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30187 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.30187

q2 · i? · design · r2

Observational deployment during the final three weeks of an undergraduate statistics lecture, using GPT-4o. An automated GPT-5 analysis classified 95 conversations as genuine learning attempts and found 63% of conversations were either resolved or partially resolved. No learning-outcome measures were collected.

"This prototypical roll-out recorded interactions from 62 distinct usernames over the three-week deployment period. From the 128 total initiated conversations, 97 chats were multi-turn conversations."
