### McInerney Niall 2022

McInerney Niall, Nally D., Khan M.F., Heneghan H., Cahill R.A. (2022). Performance effects of simulation training for medical students – a systematic review. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2022-39/zma001572.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attributing this finding to Perlini's included study of cardiac auscultation simulation. The review reports that "retention of the acquired capability was maintained" at three-year reassessment without further simulator exposure. The review states improved retention was reported in three articles overall.

> "Perlini [32] also demonstrated the impact simulation training has on retention of knowledge, focusing on cardiac auscultation. After three years, a subgroup of his students were reassessed. Without any further exposure to the Harvey simulation over that timeframe, retention of the acquired capability was maintained."
