`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive report of the embedded dual-survey instrument in the Results section; responses use radio buttons with 5 levels ranging from low to high, never to always, or strongly disagree to strongly agree. The presurvey is described as a baseline calibration tool and the postsurvey as a measure of the app's impact.

> "Table 1 shows 14 questions integrated into the assessment questionnaires of the app. Specifically, questions 1 to 9 are included in both the pre- and postsurvey or assessment questionnaires, while questions 10 to 14 are exclusive to the postsurveys or assessment questionnaires."
