## Evidence

### Reijers Stefanie N.H. 2025

Reijers Stefanie N.H., Vroegop Jantien L., Jansen Pauline W., Kremer Bernd, van der Schroeff Marc P. (2025). The Impact of Slight to Mild Hearing Loss on Academic Performance and Behavior of 9–15‐Year‐Olds. The Laryngoscope. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.31908

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Cross-lagged panel model using measurements at ages 9–11 (n = 4779) and 13–15, adjusted for sex, maternal education, household income, gestational age, and ethnicity, showed the hearing-to-social-problems direction "Z‐score difference: 0.01; 95% CI [0.01–0.02]".

> "Cross‐lagged effects showed that children with an elevated LPTA at 9–11 years had more social problems at 13–15 years (Z‐score difference: 0.01; 95% CI [0.01–0.02])."
