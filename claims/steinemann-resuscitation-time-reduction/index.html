The review attributes this Level 4 finding to Steinemann (2011), a before-and-after study without a control group using ED process timing/audit data. The reported "16 percent reduction in total resuscitation time" is a single-study, uncontrolled result.

"Steinemann (2011) reported a 16 percent reduction in total resuscitation time and faster completion of primary surveys following implementation of simulation-based team training in a trauma bay setting."
