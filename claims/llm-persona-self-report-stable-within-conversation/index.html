Evidence¶

Jana Gonnermann-Müller 2026¶

Jana Gonnermann-Müller, Jennifer Haase, Nicolas Leins, Thomas Kosch, Sebastian Pokutta. (2026). LLM-Based Educational Simulation: Evaluating Temporal Student Persona Stability Across ADHD-Related Persona Specifications. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06307 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.06307

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Linear mixed-effects analysis of Experiment II multi-turn conversations (education scenario, unscripted condition) modeling self-report ADHD Index as a function of checkpoint, intensity, prompt, and model. The article reports the turn effect was "non-significant (β = 0.03, SE = 0.04, t = 0.90, p = .366)". No effect size is printed.

"In the unscripted education condition, the effect of turn number on self-reported ADHD scores was non-significant (β = 0.03, SE = 0.04, t = 0.90, p = .366), indicating no statistically detectable drift across turns3, 6, and9."
