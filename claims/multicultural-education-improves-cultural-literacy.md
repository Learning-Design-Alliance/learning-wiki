### Perveen 2014

Shaheen Perveen. (2014). Study of the Effectiveness of Multi-cultural Education on the Attitude Towards National Integration of High School Students. li-manager's Journal of Psychology, Educational, Vol. 8 No. 2, August-October 2014. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Study+of+the+Effectiveness+of+Multi-cultural+Education+on+the+Attitude+towards+National+Integration%22+Perveen

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Controlled comparison of cultural literacy scores between control and experimental groups of high school students, analysed with t-test. Table 1 reports control mean 5.28 (SD 2.01), experimental mean 7.09 (SD 2.75), t-value 5.12, interpreted as significant.

> "The obtained t - value is greater than 2.58 for cultural literacy and hence, is significant at 0.01 level. Hence the null hypothesis is rejected."
