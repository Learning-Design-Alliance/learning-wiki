## Evidence

### Rastegaran 2016

Shadi Rastegaran. (2016). The Effect of Using Authentic Task on Teaching Adjectives. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(2), 18-24. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.2p.18

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test post-test intact-groups comparison of 62 Iranian intermediate EFL learners: group E (32 learners) read authentic texts about zodiac signs for 12 sessions while group C (30 learners) read dictionary extracts. The independent-samples t-test on post-test scores gave t = 3.131 (p = .003, mean difference 5.078); "the experimental group outperformed the control group".

> "Furthermore the t -value observed which 3.131is is more than the t -value crit ical at the 0.05 level of significance (1.67). Therefore, we can safely claim that the experimental group outperformed the control group."
