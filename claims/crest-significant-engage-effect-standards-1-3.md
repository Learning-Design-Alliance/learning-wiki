# The CREST treatment produced a statistically significant main treatment effect on the Engage composite for Standards 1-3 students in Belize City primary schools

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In intent-to-treat HLM models, Standards 1-3 students in CREST treatment classrooms showed a statistically significant main treatment effect on the Engage in Behavior composite, with the treatment indicator explaining an additional 19.93% of intercept variance and 18.41% of slope variance. ## Evidence

### Krystal M. Hinerman 2014

Krystal M. Hinerman, Darrell M. Hull, Darrell M. Hayes, Marvin G. Evans, Maria L. Reyes. (2014). Childhood Resiliency Effects from Schoolwide Treatment: A Cluster Randomized Trial. SREE Fall 2014 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562710

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intent-to-treat hierarchical linear modeling of the cluster-randomized trial (12 CREST and 12 control schools) on the Standards 1-3 Engage composite. The article reports a "statistically significant main treatment effect" with 19.93% additional intercept variance and 18.41% slope variance explained.

> "the final Engage model indicated a statistically significant main treatment effect. Adding the treatment indicator as a Level 2 predictor in the Engage ITT model explained an additional 19.93% of the variance in the intercept and 18.41% of the variance in the slope"
