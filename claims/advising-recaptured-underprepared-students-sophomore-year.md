## Evidence

### K. McCavit and N. E. B. Zellner 2015

K. McCavit and N. E. B. Zellner. (2015). Persistence of Physics and Engineering Students via Peer Mentoring, Active Learning, and Intentional Advising. European Journal of Physics (accepted for publication). [doi:10.1088/0143-0807/37/6/065702](https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/37/6/065702)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Program description of an advising outcome: students identified at summer orientation as interested in physics or engineering but below calculus placement were advised to take mathematics courses first, then enrolled in Analytical Physics I and Calculus I in their sophomore year after "successfully completing the prerequisite mathematics course(s) by the end of their first year". No counts or statistics are printed.

> "students from the entering class of Fall 2013  and 2014 who were at risk of leaving the program  because they were not mathematically prepared for Analytical Physics I were recaptured into introductory classes in the subsequent academic year ."
