## Subclaims
`q2 i?` PGRE percentile scores of 149 prize fellows span the full range, with 44 fellows (~30%) falling below the 50th percentile, contradicting the expectation of a half-Gaussian distribution concentrated in high bins. [→ Emily M. Levesque 2015](#emily-m-levesque-2015)

## Evidence

### Emily M. Levesque 2015

Emily M. Levesque, Rachel Bezanson, & Grant R. Tremblay. (2015). Physics GRE Scores of Prize Postdoctoral Fellows in Astronomy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1512.03709)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Anonymous questionnaire of 271 U.S. prize postdoctoral fellows (Hubble, Einstein, NSF, Sagan, Jansky) held 2010-2015; 173 responded (64%), 149 reported PGRE scores. The actual histogram "spans the full range of percentiles", unlike the expected half-Gaussian under a correlated-with-success model.

> "The score distribution spans the full range of percentiles, with 44 fellows ( ∼30%) falling below the 50th percentile."
