# In Experiment 1, memory of audio explanation details about inventions was significantly better with exhibit-located spatial audio than with headphone-located normal audio

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants recalled details about the inventions significantly better when audio explanations were perceptually located within the portraits (spatial audio) than within the headphones (normal audio), r = .28. [→ Manuela Glaser 2025](#manuela-glaser-2025)

## Evidence

### Manuela Glaser 2025

Manuela Glaser, Laura Hug, Stephan Werner, Stephan Schwan. (2025). Spatial versus normal audio guides in exhibitions: Cognitive mechanisms and effects on learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10424-3

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Preregistered between-subjects experiment (n = 48 after exclusions) in an experimental exhibition with six inventor portraits; one-tailed Mann–Whitney U-test, U = 194.00, p = .027, r = .28 for invention details. The authors call it "a medium instead of the expected large effect and only statistically significant in one-sided testing".

> "While details about the inventions were recalled significantly better with spatial audio settings (M = .46, SD = .22) than with normal audio settings (M = .34, SD = .20), recall of details about the inventors was similar with spatial audio settings (M = .45, SD = .12) and with normal audio settings (M = .43, SD = .14)."
