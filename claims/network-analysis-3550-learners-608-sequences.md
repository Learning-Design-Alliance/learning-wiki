The review describes a case study by Ifenthaler et al. (2020) using navigational sequences and network graph analyses with N = 3550 learners plus linked follow-up studies, showing most-used paths and the topological structure of the learning environment. The review reports that "only 608 sequences out of hundreds of millions of possible sequences were evidenced by learners".

> "Even with open-ended freedom of choice by learners in the initial study, only 608 sequences out of hundreds of millions of possible sequences were evidenced by learners."
