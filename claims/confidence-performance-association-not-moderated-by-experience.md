## Evidence

### Nim Casper et al 2024

Nim Casper et al. (2024). The association between students’ confidence and ability to modulate spinal manipulation force–time characteristics of specific target forces: a cross-sectional study. Chiropractic & Manual Therapies. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12998-024-00557-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear mixed models re-run interacting with years of study and FSTT laboratory time (tutor-led and open-practice analyzed separately) in 149 students. The article reports the association was "not substantially affected" by these moderators.

> "The association between confidence and the ability to modulate SMT force–time characteristics was not substantially affected by either the year of study or the time spent in the FSTT® laboratory (either tutor or open lab)"
