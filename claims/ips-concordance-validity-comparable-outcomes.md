Review synthesis of validation studies (Table 2 lists the concordance validity studies by LeRoy Heinrichs et al, 2008 and Youngblood et al, 2008, each with 30 participants). The review reports simulator training was "comparable to alternative training in terms of outcome".

> "The correlation between the outcome of simulator training and alternative training methods was proved in 2 virtual worlds [16,20] (ie, concordance validity)—in all 3 validation studies, simulator training was comparable to alternative training in terms of outcome."
