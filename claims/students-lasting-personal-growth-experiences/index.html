Sherman 2020¶

Sherman, P., & Boukydis, O. (2020). Framing Undergraduate Perspectives on Experiential Learning Within Soka Education Theory. IAFOR Journal of Education: Undergraduate Education, 8(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1272497 doi:10.22492/ije.8.3.05

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Study 2 thematic analysis produced Student Global Theme 2, "Applying knowledge gained through new meaningful experiences creates lasting personal growth". Students reported gains in confidence, reflection, leadership, time management, organization, and communication skills.

"This global theme emerged from three organizing themes that identified experiential learning as offering students' opportunities to learn through new and different experiences, create lasting experiences, and acquire and implement knowledge gained by virtue of experiences."
