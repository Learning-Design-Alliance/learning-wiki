### Conrad Borchers 2026 (2)

Conrad Borchers, Hannah Deininger, and Zachary A. Pardos. (2026). Toward Trait-Aware Learning Analytics. LAK 2026: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference. https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785057

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

In the same ITS field trial analysis, three learner groups differed in behavior–achievement relationships, posttest performance, motivational profiles, and prior knowledge; the article reports that "identical behaviors could predict opposite outcomes depending on their dispositions."

> "students with lower prior knowledge but high versus low motivation engaged with the system differently and achieved correspondingly better or worse outcomes, showing that identical behaviors could predict opposite outcomes depending on their dispositions."
