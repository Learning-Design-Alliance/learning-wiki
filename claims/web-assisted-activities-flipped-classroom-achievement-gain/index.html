Evidence¶

Murat Çetinkaya 2017¶

Murat Çetinkaya. (2017). Designing and Applying Web Assisted Activities to be Used in Flipped Classroom Model. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol.6, No.2, June 2017, pp. 128~137. http://iaesjournal.com/online/index.php/IJERE

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment with two randomly assigned 7th-grade classes (N=74, 37 per group) taught the Human and Environment Interactions unit for three weeks; both groups used flipped classroom, experimental group additionally used web-assisted activities. The article reports "a significant difference was found in favor of the experimental group".

"In the comparison of post -test results, a significant difference was found in favor of the experimental group ( t(74)= 3.293, p<0.05)."
