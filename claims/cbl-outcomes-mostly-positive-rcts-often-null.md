### Taveira-Gomes T 2016

Taveira-Gomes T, Ferreira P, Taveira-Gomes I, Severo M, Ferreira MA. (2016). What Are We Looking for in Computer-Based Learning Interventions in Medical Education? A Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5461

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's synthesis of knowledge end points across 212 intervention studies; knowledge was assessed in 120 articles, mostly via multiple choice quizzes (81.7%).

> "Of the 120 studies, 90 reported that interventions improved knowledge acquisition (75.0%), while 27 studies did not find significant effects (22.5%) and 3 multicenter randomized controlled trials reported that interventions did not positively affect knowledge acquisition (2.5%)"

### Taveira-Gomes T 2016 (2)

Taveira-Gomes T, Ferreira P, Taveira-Gomes I, Severo M, Ferreira MA. (2016). What Are We Looking for in Computer-Based Learning Interventions in Medical Education? A Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5461

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

### Taveira-Gomes T 2016 (3)

Taveira-Gomes T, Ferreira P, Taveira-Gomes I, Severo M, Ferreira MA. (2016). What Are We Looking for in Computer-Based Learning Interventions in Medical Education? A Systematic Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5461

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review's discussion-level observation contrasting generally positive uncontrolled findings with null results in randomized controlled trials, attributed to lack of comparable arms.

> "Interestingly, studies that found no positive effect in any of the learning outcomes were often randomized controlled trials [66,83-86], some of them running in multiple institutions"
