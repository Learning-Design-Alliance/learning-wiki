q2 · i? · review · r?

The review's synthesis of DA research with clinical and educational groups reports DA was "more accurate in reflecting children's learning potential than static tests," with the advantage noted especially for minority and learning disabled children. No effect sizes are printed.

"DA has been applied with different clinical and educational groups and was found to be more accurate in reflecting children's learning potential than static tests, especially with minority and learning disabled children."
