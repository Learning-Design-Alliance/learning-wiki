## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Benjamin D. Geller 2022

Benjamin D. Geller, Jack Rubien, Sara M. Hiebert, and Catherine H. Crouch. (2022). The impact of introductory physics for the life sciences in a senior biology capstone course. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.010120)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA with average Natural Science and Engineering GPA as covariate on both code-element categories; Table IV prints adjusted mean differences of 1.50 (+0.14) for IPLS 1-specific elements and 1.19 (+0.01) for general quantitative elements, as the authors report that controlling for GPA "actually increases the difference between the two group means".

> "The ANCOVA demonstrates that scores on these task elements have minimal correlation to science course GPA, and controlling for science course GPA actually increases the difference between the two group means in both cases (Table IV)."
