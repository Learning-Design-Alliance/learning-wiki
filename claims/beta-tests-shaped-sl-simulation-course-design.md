## Evidence

### Rhee Jiyoung 2025

Rhee Jiyoung, Park Hyun Soo. (2025). Standardized learner simulation for debriefer training through video conference. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2025.321

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Design-stage beta testing (four beta tests conducted before the training) in which the initial format including pre-briefing and all four PEARLS elements was found to take over 2 hours; the authors also responded to feedback about lacking individual specific feedback by using separate Zoom breakout rooms. No effect size applies.

> "However, it took too much time (over 2 hours) for participants to focus. To reduce the long training time, we decided to skip the pre-briefing and focus on debriefing only one specific element of the PEARLS framework per one training"
