## Evidence

### Yenen 2019

Yenen, E. T., & Dursun, F. (2019). The Effect of Active Learning Approach on Student Achievement in Secondary School 5th Grade English Course. Novitas-ROYAL (Research on Youth and Language), 13(2), 175-186. https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/novitas

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental post-test comparison of the experimental group (n=34) and control group (n=31) after four weeks (12 course hours) of instruction. The experimental mean was "X = 85.21" versus "X = 74.81", with t(63) = -3.38, p < .05. No effect size was printed.

> "However, in the post -tests there was a significant difference between the average score of the experimental group ( X = 85.21) and the average of the control group ( X = 74.81). This difference was found to be significant with respect to t- test results [t (63) = - 3.38, p <.05]."
