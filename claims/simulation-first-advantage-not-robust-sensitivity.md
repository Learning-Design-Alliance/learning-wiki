`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Sensitivity analysis of the randomized crossover trial excluding participants with experience as robotic assistant or with the da Vinci system. The article reports "no difference was seen between groups in combined OSATS score for the second exam (2.9 ± 0.8 vs 2.6 ± 0.6; p = 0.463)"; multivariate analysis was also non-significant (p = 0.539 and p = 0.254).
