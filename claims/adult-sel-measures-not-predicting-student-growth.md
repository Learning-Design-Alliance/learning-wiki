## Evidence

### Tommy Wells & Amy Auletto 2024

Tommy Wells & Amy Auletto. (2024). Understanding the Impact of Educators' Social-emotional Learning (SEL) Skills on Student Academic Growth. E4 Center. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2024/08/21/understanding-the-impact-of-educators-social-emotional-learning-sel-skills-on-student-academic-growth/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 269 K-8 educators (19% response rate) linked to i-Ready Fall 2023 to Spring 2024 growth data; the study found these adult SEL measures "did not predict whether students met either their math or reading growth goal targets."

> "The following measures did not predict whether students met either their math or reading growth goal targets: educators' emotion regulation skills, relationship management skills, well-being, perceptions of school climate, as well as teacher demographics"
