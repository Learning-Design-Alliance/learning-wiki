Evidence¶

Sara Berti 2023¶

Sara Berti, Valentina Grazia, Luisa Molinari. (2023). Active Student Participation in Whole-School Interventions in Secondary School. A Systematic Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09773-x

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic literature search via EBSCO (PsycINFO and Education Research Complete) following PRISMA 2020, with 205 initial records screened and forward snowballing. The review reports that "only 22 reports referring to 16 studies" met the criteria, a result the authors say "calls for further work in the field."

"our selection and data collection process eventually provided only 22 reports referring to 16 studies that fostered ASP during the intervention. This result calls for further work in the field."
