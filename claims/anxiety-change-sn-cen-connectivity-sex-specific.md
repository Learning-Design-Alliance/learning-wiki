Correlation of change scores from pre- to post-instruction in the same 101-student cohort: for female students, as spatial anxiety increased over time, SN–CEN connectivity decreased (r = −0.459); the sex difference in this correlation was significant (Z = 2.208, P = 0.014).

> "Of these, Δanxietyspatial and ΔSN–CEN were signi ﬁcantly negatively correlated for females ( r(46) = −0.459, P = 0.001, αFDR = 0.02), but not males r(55) = −0.041, P = 0.764, αFDR = 0.23), and the difference between sexes was statistically signi ﬁcant, Z = 2.208, P = 0.014"
