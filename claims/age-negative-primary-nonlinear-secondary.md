Flores Jerome, Caqueo-Urízar Alejandra, López Verónica, Acevedo Daniel. (2022). Symptomatology of attention deficit, hyperactivity and defiant behavior as predictors of academic achievement. BMC Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-022-03714-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression analysis reported in the Discussion of both school groups' models. The article states that in secondary school the age–achievement relationship "appears to be non-linear": positive in four models, negative in natural sciences, and not significant for mathematics. Only betas are printed; no effect size summary.

> "Age also shows complex behavior as a variable, since it appears as a significant predictor in all primary models in a negative relationship with academic achievement, indicating that, as primary students advance in their level of education, they obtain progressively worse academic grades."
