Evidence¶

Shi 2020¶

Shi, H., Cheung, E.S.T., & Cheung, A.C.K. (2020) The impact of stratified teaching on the academic performance of Chinese middle school students: A meta-analysis. Science Insights Education Frontiers, 7(1):735-760. https://doi.org/10.15354/sief.20.re012

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 22 experimental and quasi-experimental studies (26 datasets, approximately 3135 junior and senior high school students in China), analyzed with Comprehensive Meta-Analysis 3.0. The article reports a combined effect size with value SMD = 0.53, a "moderate effect" by Cohen's theory; Table 1 also lists a random-effects estimate of 0.50.

"According to Table 1, the combined effect size SMD of the included study was 0.53. According to Cohen's statistical theory of effect size, the study produced a moderate effect."
