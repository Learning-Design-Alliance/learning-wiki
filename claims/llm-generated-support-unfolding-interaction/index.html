The review's synthesis of prior and reviewed systems (Section 2.1.2 and Findings 2) contrasts pre-LLM conversational systems that matched speech to predefined intents with LLM systems in which "LLMs enable educational support to be generated as an interaction unfolds," informed by preceding turns, participant input, and multimodal context.
