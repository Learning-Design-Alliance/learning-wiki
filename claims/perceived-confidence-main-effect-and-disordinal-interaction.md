## Evidence

### Ausburn 2010

Ausburn, F. B., Ausburn, L. J., & Kroutter, P. (2010). An Exploration of Desktop Virtual Reality and Visual Processing Skills in a Technical Training Environment. i-manager's Journal of Educational Technology, 6(4), January - March. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1098351

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Two-way ANOVA on the 5-point perceived confidence rating in the exploratory study of 18 LPN students found a main effect favoring still images, F(1,17) = 3.56, p = .08, η² = .20. The article reports "the still image treatment produced better results overall (M = 3.00; 5 = greatest level of confidence) than the VR treatment (M = 2.44)."
