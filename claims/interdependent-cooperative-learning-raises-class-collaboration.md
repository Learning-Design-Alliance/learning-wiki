## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Study-mean CCOP prosocial behavior (d = 0.48) and discussion practices (d = 0.56) were significantly higher in intervention than comparison classrooms. [→ Premo Joshua 2018](#premo-joshua-2018)

## Evidence

### Premo Joshua 2018

Premo Joshua, Cavagnetto Andy, Davis William B. (2018). Promoting Collaborative Classrooms: The Impacts of Interdependent Cooperative Learning on Undergraduate Interactions and Achievement. CBE—Life Sciences Education 17(2). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-08-0176

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Weekly TA-completed CCOP observations (n = 20 classrooms, df = 358) showed no initial condition differences in week 1, but study means were significantly higher in intervention classrooms for both subscales, with d = 0.48 and d = 0.56 as printed.

> "Both prosocial behavior (mcomparison = 3.53, SE = 0.054, minter= 3.86, SE = 0.047, t = 4.522, df = 358, p < 0.05, d = 0.48) and discussion practices (mcomparison = 3.07, SE = 0.054, minter = 3.48, SE = 0.056, t = 5.296, df = 358, p < 0.05, d = 0.56) were significantly higher in the intervention condition"
