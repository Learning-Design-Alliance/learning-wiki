## Evidence

### Schoen Deborah E. 2016

Schoen Deborah E., Gausia Kaniz, Glance David G., Thompson Sandra C. (2016). Improving rural and remote practitioners’ knowledge of the diabetic foot: findings from an educational intervention. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-016-0157-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired pre/post item analysis of the 11 matched knowledge questions (n = 117): two intermediate-risk items improved, but "even after the training less than half of the participants could stratify intermediate foot risk correctly".

> "Two of the three questions assessing healthcare professionals’ ability to determine people at intermediate risk of diabetic foot complications improved (foot deformity and neuropathy), however, even after the training less than half of the participants could stratify intermediate foot risk correctly."

### Schoen Deborah E. 2016 (2)

Schoen Deborah E., Gausia Kaniz, Glance David G., Thompson Sandra C. (2016). Improving rural and remote practitioners’ knowledge of the diabetic foot: findings from an educational intervention. Journal of Foot and Ankle Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-016-0157-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired item analysis of the non-palpable pulses question: "no improvement with only 32 % answering correctly in the post-test"; the article reports no statistically significant improvement for this item.

> "For the question requiring respondents to classify people with non-palpable pulses as being at intermediate risk, there was no improvement with only 32 % answering correctly in the post-test."
