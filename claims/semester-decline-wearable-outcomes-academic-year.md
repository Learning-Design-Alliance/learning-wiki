# Wearable outcomes declined significantly across the academic year, including a 5.2% reduction in RMSSD and roughly 680 fewer steps per day by Week 33

## Evidence

### semester-rmssd

Tamunotonye Harry, Johanna Hidalgo, Matthew Price, Yuanyuan Feng, Kathryn Stanton, Connie Tompkins, Peter Sheridan Dodds, Mikaela Irene Fudolig, Laura Bloomfield, and Christopher Danforth. 2026. A Formative Study of Brief Affective Text as a Complement to Wearable Sensing for Longitudinal Student Health Monitoring. Proc. ACM Interact. Mob. Wearable Ubiquitous Technol. 10, 4, Article 219 (December 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14360

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-person mixed-effects semester decline model on Oura ring data (N=3,494 observations from 455 participants). The model shows RMSSD "declined from a model-predicted 66.9 ms at Week 1 to 63.4 ms at Week 33" with β=−1.03, p<.001. Only test statistics are printed, so no effect size is coded.

> "RMSSD declined from a model-predicted 66.9 ms at Week 1 to 63.4 ms at Week 33 (𝛽=− 1.03, 𝑆𝐸= 0.17,𝑝<. 001), representing a 5.2% reduction in cardiac autonomic function across the academic year."

### semester-steps

Tamunotonye Harry, Johanna Hidalgo, Matthew Price, Yuanyuan Feng, Kathryn Stanton, Connie Tompkins, Peter Sheridan Dodds, Mikaela Irene Fudolig, Laura Bloomfield, and Christopher Danforth. 2026. A Formative Study of Brief Affective Text as a Complement to Wearable Sensing for Longitudinal Student Health Monitoring. Proc. ACM Interact. Mob. Wearable Ubiquitous Technol. 10, 4, Article 219 (December 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14360

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same semester decline model applied to physical activity outcomes (N=3,475 from 454 participants). Step count "declined from a predicted 11,455 steps/day at Week 1 to 10,777 steps/day at Week 33" with β=−200.1, p<.001. Sleep efficiency and onset latency also changed significantly; six of nine outcomes declined.

> "Step count declined from a predicted 11,455 steps/day at Week 1 to 10,777 steps/day at Week 33 (𝛽=− 200.1,𝑆𝐸= 36.4,𝑝<. 001)"
