## Evidence

### Andersen Nina Bjerre 2014

Andersen Nina Bjerre, O'Neill Lotte, Gormsen Lise Kirstine, Hvidberg Line, Morcke Anne Mette. (2014). A validation study of the psychometric properties of the Groningen Reflection Ability Scale. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-214

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Qualitative analysis of the open-ended comment box included in the questionnaire, used to explore arguments for validity. The article reports students "found the items very abstract"; terminology such as "habits of thinking" was also an issue for some (n = 8).

> "some found the scale lacking context (n = 15), with students commenting that they did not know which part of their life they should relate the items to. Others found the items very abstract and found it hard to answer questions that they had never thought about before (n = 13)."
