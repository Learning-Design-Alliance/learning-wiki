## Evidence

### Sievertsen HH 2016

Sievertsen HH, Gino F, Piovesan M. (2016). Cognitive fatigue influences students' performance on standardized tests. PNAS 113(10): 2621–2624. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1516947113

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same OLS analysis of the full Danish test population with the same fixed effects and covariates: "a break improves the test score by 1.7% (95% CI, 1.2–2.2%) of an SD". Break timing was verified by phone interviews with 95 of the 210 sampled schools. No standardized effect size is printed.
