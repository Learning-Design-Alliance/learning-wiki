## Evidence

### Posner Mareike G. 2023

Posner Mareike G., Dempsey Nina C., Unsworth Amanda J. (2023). The Benefits of Using Case Study Focussed, Problem Based Learning Approaches to Unit Design for Biomedical Science Students. British Journal of Biomedical Science. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10340524/

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Cross-cohort comparison of anonymised mid-unit and end-of-unit 5-point Likert feedback surveys from the 20/21, 21/22 and 22/23 Blood Science cohorts (average 15% completion), reported as percentages of positive, neutral and negative ratings. The article reports that feedback moved “to “overall positive” from “neutral positive,”” with fewer negative ratings; no effect size or significance test is printed.

> "Feedback to all questions was improved in the 21/22 cohort vs. the 20/21 cohort and maintained (or further improved) in the 22/23 cohort (Figure 3) to “overall positive” from “neutral positive,” with fewer students rating activities negatively."
