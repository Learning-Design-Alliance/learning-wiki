## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Among students doing the DbD activity, the extent to which they completed their intentionally buggy projects correlated with significant increases in fascination in design (r = .257*) and programming growth mindset (r = .313**). [→ Morales-Navarro 2023](#morales-navarro-2023)
`q2 i?` Programming growth mindset correlation with project completion was significant in the DbD condition (r = .313**) while in the comparison condition it was marginal (r = .230+), suggesting a DbD-unique association. [→ Morales-Navarro 2023](#morales-navarro-2023)

Correlation analysis within the DbD classes (N=90) in a virtual quasi-experimental study. Table 1 prints "Programming growth mindset .313**" for DbD versus ".230+" for comparison, and "Fascination in design .257*".

> "Among those doing the DbD activity, the extent to which they completed their projects (designing intentionally buggy artifacts and solving their peers’ buggy projects) correlated with significant increases in fascination in design and programming growth mindsets."
