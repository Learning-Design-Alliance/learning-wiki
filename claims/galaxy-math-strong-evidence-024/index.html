Evaluation of Galaxy Math against a no-tutoring control. "On qualifying measures, the effect size was +0.24, which qualifies Galaxy Math for the 'Strong' evidence category." Outcomes favored Galaxy Math on all four measures, though two researcher-made measures did not qualify.
