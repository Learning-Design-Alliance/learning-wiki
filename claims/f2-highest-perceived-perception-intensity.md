## Evidence

### von Bechtolsheim Felix 2024

von Bechtolsheim Felix, Bielert Florian, Schmidt Sofia, Buck Nathalie, Bodenstedt Sebastian, Speidel Stefanie, Lüneburg Lisa-Marie, Müller Thomas, Fan Yichen, Bobbe Tina, Oppici Luca, Krzywinski Jens, Dobroschke Jakob, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian. (2024). Can you feel the force just right? Tactile force feedback for training of minimally invasive surgery—evaluation of vibration feedback for adequate force application. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10919-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subjective evaluation survey after each task repetition in the crossover trial: "F2 was significantly more perceptible than F1 and F3" in both tasks, with 85.9% and 87.8% rating it good or better.

> "Therefore, F2 was significantly more perceptible than F1 and F3 in both, the peg transfer task (p < 0.001) and the laparoscopic suture and knot task (p = 0.004)."

### von Bechtolsheim Felix 2024 (2)

von Bechtolsheim Felix, Bielert Florian, Schmidt Sofia, Buck Nathalie, Bodenstedt Sebastian, Speidel Stefanie, Lüneburg Lisa-Marie, Müller Thomas, Fan Yichen, Bobbe Tina, Oppici Luca, Krzywinski Jens, Dobroschke Jakob, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian. (2024). Can you feel the force just right? Tactile force feedback for training of minimally invasive surgery—evaluation of vibration feedback for adequate force application. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10919-3

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intensity ratings in the same survey: F2 showed significantly greater intensity than F1 and F3 (p < 0.001) in the suture and knot task, and similarly in peg transfer.

> "Compared to F1 and F3, F2 had a significantly greater intensity in the laparoscopic suture and knot task with 21.1% of participants reporting it to be "very strong" (p < 0.001)."
