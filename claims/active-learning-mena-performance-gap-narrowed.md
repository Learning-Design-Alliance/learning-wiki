# Active learning narrowed the performance gap between top-75% and lowest-25% students by 16.56% (from 38.64 to 32.24 points)

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The performance gap, defined as the difference between average final scores of the top 75% and lowest 25% students, narrowed from 38.64 ¬Ī 0.61 points under traditional teaching to 32.24 ¬Ī 0.52 points under active learning, a 16.56% reduction; no standardized effect size is printed for this finding.
