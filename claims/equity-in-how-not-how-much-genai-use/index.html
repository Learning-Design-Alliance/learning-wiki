Evidence¶

Youjie Chen 2026¶

Youjie Chen, Xixi Shi, Xinyu Liu, Shuaiguo Wang, Tracy Xiao Liu, Dragan Gašević. (2026). Not All Students Engage Alike: Multi-Institution Patterns in GenAI Tutor Use. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00447

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Regression analysis of adoption and usage intensity across 200 classes found no significant selectivity association, while session-level and student-level engagement types differed by selectivity. The authors state inequities "may be obscured by whom or how much they use these tools, but revealed in how they use these tools."

"institutional selectivity was not significantly associated with adoption or usage intensity, suggesting that differences in overall engagement were closely related to disciplinary contexts but not institutional selectivity."
