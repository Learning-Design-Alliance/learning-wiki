## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Bereuter Jean-Paul 2026

Bereuter Jean-Paul, Fries Alexa, Grummt Isabell, Geissler Mark Enrik, Geissler Rona Berit, Schmidt Sofia, Buck Nathalie, Weiß Juliane, Krause-Jüttler Grit, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian, von Bechtolsheim Felix. (2026). Impact of acute sleep deprivation on laparoscopic performance: a prospective, randomized crossover trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12872-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student-cohort force analysis (Table 3) from the crossover trial using the ForceSense device. Mean non-zero force fell only in suture and knot ("control: 0.84 N vs. sleep-deprived: 0.72 N, p < 0.001"); peak force showed no significant differences in any task.

> "the students exerted a comparable mean non-zero force in the peg transfer and Circle cutting task, but showed a significantly lower force exertion during sleep deprivation in the Suture and Knot task (control: 0.84 N vs. sleep-deprived: 0.72 N, p < 0.001)"

### Bereuter Jean-Paul 2026 (2)

Bereuter Jean-Paul, Fries Alexa, Grummt Isabell, Geissler Mark Enrik, Geissler Rona Berit, Schmidt Sofia, Buck Nathalie, Weiß Juliane, Krause-Jüttler Grit, Weitz Jürgen, Distler Marius, Oehme Florian, von Bechtolsheim Felix. (2026). Impact of acute sleep deprivation on laparoscopic performance: a prospective, randomized crossover trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-026-12872-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Surgeon-cohort force analysis (Table 3). Mean non-zero force was significantly lower only in peg transfer ("control: 0.71 N vs. sleep-deprived: 0.65 N, p = 0.042"); peak force did not differ in any task.

> "Concerning the mean non-zero force, surgeons applied significantly less force being sleep-deprived in the peg transfer task (control: 0.71 N vs. sleep-deprived: 0.65 N, p = 0.042) but not in the other tasks"
