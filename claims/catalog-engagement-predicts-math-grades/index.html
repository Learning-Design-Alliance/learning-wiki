Daniel L. Schwartz and Dylan Arena 2013¶

Daniel L. Schwartz and Dylan Arena. (2013). Measuring What Matters Most: Choice-Based Assessments for the Digital Age. The MIT Press. http://www.macfound.org

q2 · i? · associational · r?

An initial study using a similar environment with sixth-grade children found children who chose to look at the catalog of charts learned much more about mixing additive color than their counterparts did. The reported 35 percent figure is a prediction of grade variation, not a standardized effect size.

"the amount of time students committed to figuring out the catalog entries predicted about 35 percent of the variation in the students' grades in their mathematics classes"
