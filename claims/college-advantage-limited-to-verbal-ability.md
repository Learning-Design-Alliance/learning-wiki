Preliminary analysis comparing the high school (n=64) and college (n=68) samples on all tests and the experimental task; the article reports t-tests showed "significantdifferences only for Verbal Ability (t=2.11)". Because college subjects recalled more features, accuracy was scored as proportion of recalled features correctly located.
