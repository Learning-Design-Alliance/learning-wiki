## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Sleep-deprived adolescents displayed more inattentive behaviors (looking away from the film for ≥3 consecutive seconds) than when healthy-slept, with a large effect size. [→ Beebe 2010](#beebe-2010)

## Evidence

### Beebe 2010

Beebe, D. W., Rose, D., & Amin, R. (2010). Attention, Learning, and Arousal of Experimentally Sleep-Restricted Adolescents in a Simulated Classroom. The Journal of Adolescent Health. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jadohealth.2010.03.005

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Video/EEG-monitored subset (n=8) of the counterbalanced sleep-manipulation study; a condition-blind rater coded look-aways of ≥3 consecutive seconds in 30-second epochs. Sleep-deprived participants "displayed more inattentive behaviors, t(7)=2.6, p<.05, d=.98".
