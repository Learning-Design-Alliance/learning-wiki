Quantile regression estimates (Koenker & Bassett, 1978) pooled across both courses, plotting treatment effects from the 10th through 95th quantile in Figure 5 with the OLS effect shown at top. Effects were non-uniform along the performance distribution.

"We find treatment assignment results in a statistically significant increase in final course grades between the 35th and 75th quantiles of the course distribution (as well as at the 95th quantile). This indicates that treatment effects are non-uniform along the distribution of course performance"
