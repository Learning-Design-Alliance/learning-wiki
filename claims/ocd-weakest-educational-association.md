## Evidence

### Bortes Cristian 2022

Bortes Cristian, Nilsson Karina, Strandh Mattias. (2022). Associations between children’s diagnosed mental disorders and educational achievements in Sweden. Scandinavian Journal of Public Health, 50(8), 1140–1147. https://doi.org/10.1177/14034948221089056

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r3`

Fully adjusted logistic regression in the Swedish register sample. The printed effect sizes are OR = 1.48 (95% CI, 1.01 to 2.17) for males and OR = 1.38 (95% CI, 1.11 to 1.72) for females; the male standardised grade difference was −0.03, essentially null.

> "Obsessive compulsive disorder had the weakest negative association with educational achievements among both males (OR: 1.48 (95% CI, 1.01 to 2.17)) and females (OR: 1.38 (95% CI, 1.11 to 1.72))."
