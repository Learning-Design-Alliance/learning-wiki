`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Four independent expert raters (licensed obstetricians/gynaecologists) evaluated SP portrayals in two scenarios using structured observational checklists rated on a five-point Likert scale. Fleiss' Kappa showed "moderate to substantial agreement across most subscales"; Professionalism & Communication had the lowest agreement (kappa = 0.22).

> "The kappa values ranged from 0.22 to 0.66, indicating moderate to substantial agreement across most subscales. The highest agreement (kappa = 0.66) was observed for Family/Social History"
