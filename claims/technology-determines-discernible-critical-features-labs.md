## Evidence

### Jonte Bernhard 2018

Jonte Bernhard. (2018). What matters for students' learning in the laboratory? Do not neglect the role of experimental equipment! Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9469-x

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

A comparative phenomenological inquiry (RQ1) of three regular labs in Swedish university courses, analysing the affordances of photogates, tape timer and probeware for studying uniformly accelerated motion in an inclined plane, summarised in Tables 2 and 3, confirming that "what is possible to perceive or discern depends on the technology used".

> "Table 2 summarises the affordances of the measurement technologies used for studying motion in an inclined plane applied in the three cases, and confirms that what is possible to perceive or discern depends on the technology used."
