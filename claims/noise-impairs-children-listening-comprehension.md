# Noise impairs children's performance in complex listening tasks beyond simple identification, with children affected more than adults

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Klatte M 2013

Klatte M, Bergström K and Lachmann T (2013) Does noise affect learning? A short review on noise effects on cognitive performance in children. Front. Psychol. 4:578. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00578

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports this experimental listening-comprehension study: "substantial decrements due to single-talker speech and classroom noise in elementary school children", with adults less affected.

> "Klatte et al. (2010a) used a listening task requiring execution of complex oral instructions and found substantial decrements due to single-talker speech and classroom noise in elementary school children. Adults were less affected."

### Klatte M 2013 (2)

Klatte M, Bergström K and Lachmann T (2013) Does noise affect learning? A short review on noise effects on cognitive performance in children. Front. Psychol. 4:578. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00578

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports this simulated-classroom study found "signiﬁcant impairments in discourse comprehension in 8- to 12-year-olds" under noise plus reverberation, effects not attributable to impaired identification.

> "Valente et al. (2012) reported signiﬁcant impairments in discourse comprehension in 8- to 12-year-olds due to broadband noise combined with reverberation. The noise effects found in these studies could not be attributed to impaired identiﬁcation."
