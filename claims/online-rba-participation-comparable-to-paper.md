## Subclaims
`q3 i?` In a randomized experiment (N = 1310), participation rates were comparable for students taking the same RBA online outside of class versus on paper inside class, and did not differ by gender or final course grade. [→ Ben Van Dusen 2021](#ben-van-dusen-2021)

## Evidence

### Ben Van Dusen 2021

Ben Van Dusen, Mollee Shultz, Jayson M. Nissen, Bethany R. Wilcox, N.G. Holmes, Manher Jariwala, Eleanor W. Close, and Steven Pollock. (2021). Online Administration of Research-Based Assessments. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/10.0002888](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0002888)

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

A randomized experiment at one institution assigned students (N = 1310) to online out-of-class or paper in-class administration of the same RBA. Participation rates were "comparable if instructors administered the RBAs using the recommended practices"; no effect size is printed.

> "In an experiment where researchers randomly assigned students (N =1310) at one institution to take the same RBA online outside of class versus on paper inside class, participation rates were comparable if instructors administered the RBAs using the recommended practices"
