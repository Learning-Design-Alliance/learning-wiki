## Evidence

### Dohms M. C. 2020

Dohms M. C., Collares C. F., Tibério I. C. (2020). Video-based feedback using real consultations for a formative assessment in communication skills. BMC Medical Education 20:57. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1955-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of 54 medical residents' SPIKES-protocol checklist scores in the pre/post controlled study; the article reports "a significant increase in scores with moderate effect size" over time with a null Time*Group effect (Table 1 p = 0.758), no printed effect-size value.

> "For total scores, there was a significant increase in scores with moderate effect size. This difference was not significant between the control and intervention groups and had a null effect."
