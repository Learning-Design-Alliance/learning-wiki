## Evidence

### Juan C. Castro-Alonso 2024

Juan C. Castro-Alonso, Paul Ayres, Shirong Zhang, Björn B. de Koning, Fred Paas. (2024). Research Avenues Supporting Embodied Cognition in Learning and Instruction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09847-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Macken and Ginns (2014), a study of 42 adults (74% females) learning about the human heart through visualizations and texts, with one condition allowing free finger pointing and tracing. The review notes a replication by Ginns and Kydd (2020); no effect size is printed.

> "Results on retention and comprehension tests showed that participants in the gesturing condition outperformed those in the control condition without gesturing (see also a replication by Ginns & Kydd, 2020)."
