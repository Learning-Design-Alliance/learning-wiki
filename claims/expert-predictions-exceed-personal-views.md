# Students' expert predictions exceed their personal views about lab experimentation by a large margin (d = 0.8 BFY, d = 1.1 FY)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Overall scores for students' predictions of experts' beliefs are significantly higher than their scores for their own views about experimentation in their lab courses, with large effects (d = 0.8 BFY; d = 1.1 FY). [→ Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017](#bethany-r-wilcox-and-h-j-lewandowski-2017)
`q3 i?` In all cases, students' expert predictions averaged higher than their personal beliefs, with differences exceeding d = 0.5 on seven items (Q14, Q29, Q7, Q6, Q27, Q5, Q10). [→ Bethany R. Wilcox and H. J. Lewandowski 2017 (2)](#bethany-r-wilcox-and-h-j-lewandowski-2017-2)
