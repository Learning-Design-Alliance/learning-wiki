Dabkowski 2026¶

Dabkowski, Elissa; Missen, Karen; Allen, Louise; Whitehead, Dean; Worn, Ryan. (2026). 'Co-pilot won't teach you to be a nurse': nursing academics' perspectives on GenAI use in undergraduate education. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00220-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Theme One minor theme on divergent academic attitudes in the 22-participant interview study. Attitudes ranged from scepticism ("It's not original thought") to pragmatic acceptance that students will use GenAI.

"Participants expressed a range of attitudes toward the role of GenAI in nursing education, reflecting both uncertainty and an evolving understanding of its implications for teaching, learning, and academic integrity."

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Participant quote (P14) within the divergent-attitudes theme. Academics recognised GenAI's potential to support editing and writing enhancement but cautioned against use that "replaces the student learning".

"It's purely when it replaces the student learning that I have an issue. . they shouldn't be allowed to use it to replace learning"
