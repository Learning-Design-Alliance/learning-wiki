## Subclaims
`q2 i?` One-way ANOVA found statistically significant differences between clusters on F3, F4, F5, and F8 (all p < 0.001), with post-hoc tests showing C1 > C2 > C3 on F3, C2 highest on F4 and F8, and C3 highest on F5. [→ Hsieh 2019](#hsieh-2019)

## Evidence

### Hsieh 2019

Hsieh, M.-C. and Chen, T.-Y. (2019). Promoting innovation in the objective structured teaching examination and feedback: clustering teachers to aid teaching evaluation. Medical Education Online, 24(1). [doi:10.1080/10872981.2019.1620544](https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2019.1620544)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across the three clusters on FIAS category percentages, with Bonferroni post-hoc tests. The table reports F3 means of "35.07 ± 5.25" (C1), "23.95 ± 7.26" (C2), and "16.44 ± 5.77" (C3), p < 0.001, post-hoc C1 > C2 > C3. Similar significant results for F4, F5, and F8.
