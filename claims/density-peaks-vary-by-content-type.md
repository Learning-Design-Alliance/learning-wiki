# Students tolerate higher cultural density for semantically contextualised content (descriptions, images), peaking at 40%, than for linguistically modified hints and feedback, peaking at 20%

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Liked ratings for question descriptions and images (semantically modified with cultural references) peaked at 40% cultural density, while hints and feedback (modified only linguistically) peaked at 20%. [→ Phaedra Mohammed & Permanand Mohan 2015](#phaedra-mohammed-permanand-mohan-2015)

## Evidence

### Phaedra Mohammed & Permanand Mohan 2015

Phaedra Mohammed & Permanand Mohan. (2015). Dynamic Cultural Contextualisation of Educational Content in Intelligent Learning Environments using ICON. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 25:249–270. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0033-9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of Liked ratings within content categories (Table 3, Fig. 6) showed Max1 at 20% density for hint and feedback types and at 40% for question description and image types; the authors state "the peak in responses occurred at 40 % cultural density" for the semantically modified types.

> "For the question description and image content categories, which were modified with the cultural references such as place names, flora, fauna and so on, the peak in responses occurred at 40 % cultural density indicating that students tolerated higher levels of cultural density for these kinds of content and contextualisations."
