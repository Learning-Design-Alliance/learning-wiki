# In laparoscopic suturing training, both positive and negative formative feedback improved one-week skill retention while no feedback produced no significant progression

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` One week after 2 h of box-trainer practice, the positive feedback and negative feedback groups had improved ISAT performance while the no-feedback group made no significant progression. [→ Sun Longci 2025](#sun-longci-2025)
`q3 i?` The negative feedback group's post-test ISAT score was significantly higher than the no-feedback group's (35.8 vs 24.8, p < 0.0001). [→ Sun Longci 2025 (2)](#sun-longci-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Sun Longci 2025

Sun Longci, Su Juncheng, Zhang Yan, Guo Feng, Mu Yifei, Konge Lars, Lu Yihong, Wang Zheng, Bian Zhengqian. (2025). Laparoscopic suturing training using positive or negative feedback: A randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12285-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 42 laparoscopic-naïve medical students in positive, negative, and no feedback groups, with blinded ISAT scoring one week after 2 h of training; the results report the feedback groups "improved after training while trainees receiving no feedback made no significant progression".

> "Results showed that performance of trainees in positive feedback (Group A) and negative feedback group (Group B) improved after training while trainees receiving no feedback made no significant progression (Group C)."

### Sun Longci 2025 (2)

Sun Longci, Su Juncheng, Zhang Yan, Guo Feng, Mu Yifei, Konge Lars, Lu Yihong, Wang Zheng, Bian Zhengqian. (2025). Laparoscopic suturing training using positive or negative feedback: A randomized controlled trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-025-12285-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise post-test comparison in the same trial's results section, showing the negative feedback group "significantly higher than Group C (35.8 vs 24.8, p < 0.0001)" on blinded ISAT scoring.

> "the post-test score of Group B was also significantly higher than Group C (35.8 vs 24.8, p < 0.0001)"
