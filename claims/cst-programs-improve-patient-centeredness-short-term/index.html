The review reports, within its results narrative on communication skills, that structured communication skills training programs "have been shown to improve clinicians' abilities significantly", citing the psychiatrist CST example. No effect size printed; second-hand report, not read here.

"Psychiatrists who participated in CST demonstrated increased patient-centeredness and enhanced competence in using open-ended questions and social communication categories [39]."

Zagalo Daniela Mariana 2026 (2)¶

Towards patient-centered healthcare: a scoping review of soft skills training in medical schools. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09059-0

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's challenges analysis states that CST research shows positive short-term outcomes while long-term retention and clinical performance impact "remains an area that requires further investigation". It also notes variable training approaches make effectiveness difficult to evaluate.

"While the existing research on communication skills training programs has demonstrated positive short-term outcomes, the long-term impact on skill retention and actual clinical performance remains an area that requires further investigation to validate their significance in medical education [40, 60]."
