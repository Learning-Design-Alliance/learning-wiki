### Ka Hei Carrie Lau 2025

Ka Hei Carrie Lau, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, and Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Evaluating Usability and Engagement of Large Language Models in Virtual Reality for Traditional Scottish Curling. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-91572-7_11](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-91572-7_11)

Mann-Whitney U tests on NASA TLX ratings in the between-subjects study found no significant differences for most dimensions (e.g., Overall Workload U = 159.0, p = 0.949); "Trends towards significance were observed" only for performance and effort, with lower scores in the ChatGPT condition.

> "Trends towards significance were observed in Performance (U = 110.5, p = 0.108) and Effort (U = 107.5, p = 0.088), with lower scores in the ChatGPT condition."
