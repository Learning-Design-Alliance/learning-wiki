## Evidence

### Lohre Ryan 2021

Lohre Ryan, Leveille Lise, Goel Danny P. (2021). Novel Application of Immersive Virtual Reality Simulation Training: A Case Report. JAAOS Global Research & Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5435/JAAOSGlobal-D-21-00114

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Single-patient case report. The PGY-5 completed the SCFE iVR module four consecutive times (PrecisionOS) before surgery, then "independently placed two 7.3-mm cannulated screws perpendicular to the physis and lateral to the intertrochanteric line" under attending supervision.

> "We describe the first reported case and evidence of immediate skill acquisition and translation to a real operative environment by a PGY-5 orthopaedic trainee having trained in iVR to revise a failed percutaneous pinning of a SCFE."
