## Evidence

### What Works Clearinghouse 2015

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2015). Head Start: WWC intervention report. http://www.acf.hhs.gov/programs/ohs

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The WWC reviewed 40 eligible studies against group design standards; 39 did not meet WWC design standards, most because they used quasi-experimental designs in which the analytic intervention and comparison groups were not shown to be equivalent. The report states the extent of evidence is small for all three rated domains.

> "One study (U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Adminis-tration for Children and Families [DHHS ACF], 2010) is a randomized controlled trial that meets WWC group design standards without reservations."
