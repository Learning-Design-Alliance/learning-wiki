## Evidence

### Drost RMWA 2016

Drost RMWA, Paulus ATG, Jander AF, Mercken L, de Vries H, Ruwaard D, Evers SMAA. (2016). A Web-Based Computer-Tailored Alcohol Prevention Program for Adolescents: Cost-Effectiveness and Intersectoral Costs and Benefits. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.5223

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Comparison of base-case CEAs from health care versus societal perspective in the Alcoholic Alert cluster RCT. From a health care perspective the intervention was cost-effective for male, lower education, older adolescent, and nonreligious subgroups; from a societal perspective (including ICBs), it was clearly cost-effective only for lower education and older adolescent subgroups.

> "our research also shows that the inclusion of ICBs in the economic evaluation impacted the cost-effectiveness results of the analysis, especially for certain subgroups."
