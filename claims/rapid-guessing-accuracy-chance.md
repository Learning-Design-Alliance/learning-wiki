## Evidence

### Wise 2005

Wise, S. L., & Kong, X. (2005). Response Time Effort: A New Measure of Examinee Motivation in Computer-Based Tests. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, Montreal. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED490203. Published version, Applied Measurement in Education: [doi:10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15324818ame1802_2)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Accuracy analysis for rapid-guessing versus solution responses on a balanced subset of 38 ILT items, comparing observed accuracy with the expected value under random responding. Rapid-guessing accuracy "did not significantly exceed chance level", supporting rapid guessing as random rather than hurried solution behavior.

> "The accuracy of the rapid-guessing responses, which was far lower than that of the solution responses, did not significantly exceed chance level."
