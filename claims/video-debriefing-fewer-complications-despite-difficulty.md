`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary-outcome analysis of the randomized controlled study; complications (perforations, cystic artery/duct injuries, clip misplacement) were assessed on a 3-point Likert scale and difficulty by the supervising tutor on a visual analog scale. The article reports the feedback group "had fewer complications than the control group".

> "Despite having to perform more difficult LCs compared to the control group (33.7 ± 9.2 vs. 29.7 ± 8.4, p < 0.001), the feedback group had fewer complications than the control group (2.9 ± 2.1 vs. 3.7 ± 2.3, p = 0.001) (Table 1) (Fig. 4)."
