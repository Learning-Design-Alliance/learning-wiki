## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Soares 2023

Soares, S., Boyes, M. E., Parrila, R., & Badcock, N. A. (2023). Does reading anxiety impact on academic achievement in higher education students? Dyslexia, 29(3), 179–198. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1738

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the mediation analyses with WAM as outcome (N = 169), the comprehension–WAM correlation was r = 0.260 (p ≤ 0.001) and the total effect beta-weight was 1.10, 95% CI [0.50, 1.81], p < 0.05; the path "between comprehension and WAM was statistically significant".

> "In the comprehension model (1), both paths from comprehension to reading anxiety factors and the path between comprehension and WAM were statistically significant."

### Soares 2023 (2)

Soares, S., Boyes, M. E., Parrila, R., & Badcock, N. A. (2023). Does reading anxiety impact on academic achievement in higher education students? Dyslexia, 29(3), 179–198. https://doi.org/10.1002/dys.1738

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the results, word-choice RT total correlated with WAM at −0.080 and reading history at −0.143, neither significant; the reading history total effect CI [−0.12, 0.00] included zero. The discussion states these "were not related to academic achievement".

> "In contrast, basic word reading skills (phonological and orthographic) and a history of reading difficulties were not related to academic achievement."
