### Danielle R. Thomas 2025

Danielle R. Thomas, Conrad Borchers, Kenneth R. Koedinger. (2025). Beyond Agreement: Rethinking Ground Truth in Educational AI. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.00143](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.00143)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The paper reports, citing Wang et al. (2024a), that Tutor CoPilot access shifted tutor behavior toward expert-aligned strategies and "significant gains in student mastery", illustrating close-the-loop validity linking a classification scheme to learning outcomes. No effect size is printed.

> "tutors with access to Tutor CoPilot—an AI-powered system trained to reflect specific expert pedagogical strategies and reasoning—were more likely to use strategies aligned with high-quality teaching (e.g., asking guiding questions), and that these differences in tutor behavior translated into significant gains in student mastery"
