## Evidence

### Kim Kyong-Jee 2019

Kim Kyong-Jee, Kim Giwoon. (2019). Development of e-learning in medical education: 10 years’ experience of Korean medical schools. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.131

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of 7-year cumulative hits by resource type (Table 2) showing clinical cases drew 5.8% of hits versus 90% for videos. The discussion section states cases "are not being widely used". No effect size applies to a descriptive proportion.

> "Despite the importance of clinical cases in medical education, our findings show they are not being widely used at e-MedEdu."

### Kim Kyong-Jee 2019 (2)

Kim Kyong-Jee, Kim Giwoon. (2019). Development of e-learning in medical education: 10 years’ experience of Korean medical schools. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2019.131

`q2 · i?`

Secondary descriptive analysis of per-case hit variation (Table 3) in the same dataset. Per-case hits "ranged from 98.05 to 360.22" across organ systems; the mean was 243.41 per case over 7 years. No test reported.

> "The number of hits on clinical cases differed across subjects; they ranged from 98.05 to 360.22 per case across different organ-based subjects and from 60.00 to 411.40 across different clinical presentations."
