## Evidence

### Anishan V 2024

Anishan V, Konge L, Bjerrum F. (2024). Variable practice is superior to self-directed training for laparoscopic simulator training: a randomized trial. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-10688-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

The primary outcome was time to reach proficiency for all five tasks at the retention test: mean 51 min (SD: 26) for the variable group versus 109 min (SD: 53) for the self-directed group (p < 0.001); the variable group "achieved proficiency significantly faster overall".

> "After the retention period of 3–5 weeks, the variable practice group achieved proficiency significantly faster overall (p < 0.001), but also for the basic skills (p = 0.026) and the procedural task (p < 0.001) alone, compared to the self-directed group"
