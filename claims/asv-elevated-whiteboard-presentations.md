## Evidence

### Lehesvuori 2026

Lehesvuori, S., Järvinen, M., Lämsä, J., Heilala, V., Hämäläinen, R. (2026). Exploring interconnections between communicative activities, situational stress reactions and cognitive engagement in undergraduate mathematics lesson. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09786-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Meso-level visualisation of z-score-normalised ASV from three sensor-wearing students during one lesson shows elevation during whiteboard tasks; the article notes "considerable variation in ASV both between students and within the course of a single presentation." Student B showed longer duration and more peaks than Student A.

> "the graph (Fig. 3) clearly shows that students’ ASV values were elevated during periods when students were engaged in tasks at the whiteboard."
