## Evidence

### Hinton Daniel P. 2017

Hinton Daniel P., Higson Helen. (2017). A large-scale examination of the effectiveness of anonymous marking in reducing group performance differences in higher education assessment. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0182711

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the full set of regression analyses across the twelve-year archival dataset of 30674 students. The article states that although "these differences do exist, practically they are very small at the group level".

> "The largest observed differences in explained variance–those between White and Black students–only amounts to a difference between these groups of, at most, 5% in terms of their actual examination performance."
