# Teachers' job satisfaction is positively associated with students' motivation (r = 0.29) and achievement (r = 0.10)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Students of more satisfied teachers show higher motivation (r = 0.29) and slightly higher achievement (r = 0.10). [→ Wartenberg 2023](#wartenberg-2023)

## Evidence

### Wartenberg 2023

Wartenberg, G., Aldrup, K., Grund, S., & Klusmann, U. (2023). Satisfied and High Performing? A Meta-Analysis and Systematic Review of the Correlates of Teachers' Job Satisfaction. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09831-4

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random-effects meta-analyses of students' motivation (k = 6, N = 3,144, r = 0.29) and achievement (k = 8, N = 1,869, r = 0.10). Achievement was assessed via school grades, test scores, and general teacher evaluation.

> "a positive relationship also emerged for students' achievement (r = 0.10, CIr = [0.02, 0.17]), indicating that students taught by satisfied teachers were more successful in achieving their learning goals"
