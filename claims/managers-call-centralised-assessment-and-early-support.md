`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme 3 sub-theme 'expert support'. One manager said "Having a centralised framework would make a significant difference, especially for overseas-qualified nurses with ICU backgrounds"; another described accumulated credentials not being effectively recognised, forcing nurses to start from scratch.

### Kurup Chanchal 2025 (2)

Kurup Chanchal, Burston Adam Scott, Betihavas Vasiliki, Jacob Elisabeth Ruth. (2025). Exploring the Utilisation of Internationally Qualified Nurses' Specialty Skills: Analysis of Recruiting Managers' View Points. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70184

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Theme 3 sub-theme 'building success'. Managers suggested leveraging strengths through services or educational opportunities, extending consultation to a pre-start phase, and holding initial conversations to align expectations and ease transitions.

> "Managers advocated for developing programmes that support IQNs from the outset, aiming to optimise their specialty skill utilisation and facilitate career development."
