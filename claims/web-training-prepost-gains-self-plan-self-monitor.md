## Evidence

### Chun-Yi Shen & Hsiu-Chuan Liu 2011

Chun-Yi Shen & Hsiu-Chuan Liu. (2011). Metacognitive Skills Development: A Web-Based Approach in Higher Education. The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 10(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ932234

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pretest-posttest quasi-experiment with 53 Taiwanese college students (experimental n=29, control n=24) measured with the 45-item MSEQ. Paired-samples t-tests showed significant pre-post gains in the experimental group, e.g. self-plan "t=4.257, p<.001"; the control group showed no significant change on any dimension.

> "the results of paired-samples t-test showed that experimental group’s posttest scores were significantly higher than the pretest scores in self-plan (t=4.257, p<.001), self-monitor (t=3.364, p <.01), and total score (t=3.753, p<.001), while there was no significance in the control group."
