# The Pass/Fail versus Tiered grade fail-rate difference levels out when the near-fail Tiered grade is counted as Fail

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Reclassifying the near-fail Tiered grade (02) as Fail eliminated the significant difference in fail rates between grading systems (P=.055). [→ Modrau B 2025](#modrau-b-2025)

## Evidence

### Modrau B 2025

Modrau B, Kirk KF, Said SMA, Bjarkam CR, Sunde L, Bodilsen J, Dal J, Kristensen JK, Emmersen J, Astorp MB, Andersen S. (2025). Pass/Fail Versus Tiered Grades and Academic Performance in Undergraduate Medical Education: Crossover Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/74975

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same retrospective cohort comparison of 7164 exams; with the near-fail grade 02 counted as Fail, the χ2 test no longer reached significance ("P=.055"), so the apparent grading-system difference reflects the near-fail boundary rather than performance.

> "This difference was leveled out (χ2 test, P=.055) when including the near-failure Tiered grade as fail (light gray bar)."
