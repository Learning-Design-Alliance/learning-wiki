## Evidence

### Biesma Regien 2019

Biesma Regien, Kennedy Mary-Claire, Pawlikowska Teresa, Brugha Ruairi, Conroy Ronan, Doyle Frank. (2019). Peer assessment to improve medical student's contributions to team-based projects: randomised controlled trial and qualitative follow-up. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1783-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cluster randomised controlled trial in 37 teams (223 students); linear regression predicted outcomes at follow-up controlling for baseline CATME subscale scores, with robust variance estimators for clustering. The article reports "no statistically significant differences in the means of any of the primary or secondary outcomes between groups"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Table 4 provides the results of the linear regression, which predicts each outcome by group, controlling for the baseline scale score. There were only small and no statistically significant differences in the means of any of the primary or secondary outcomes between groups."
