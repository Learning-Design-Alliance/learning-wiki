## Evidence

### Krage Ralf 2017

Krage Ralf, Zwaan Laura, Tjon Soei Len Lian, Kolenbrander Mark W, van Groeningen Dick, Loer Stephan A, Wagner Cordula, Schober Patrick. (2017). Relationship between non-technical skills and technical performance during cardiopulmonary resuscitation: does stress have an influence? Emergency Medicine Journal. https://doi.org/10.1136/emermed-2016-205754

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in the same 30-participant randomised cross-over simulator study, comparing scenarios with versus without external stressors. The stressor condition showed "a significant positive correlation" (r=0.67, p<0.001) while the control condition showed r=0.15, p=0.42.

> "This overall correlation, however, is explained by a significant positive correlation in scenarios with external stressors (r=0.67, 95% CI 0.40 to 0.83, p<0.001), while no evidence for a relationship between technical and non-technical performance was observed when no external stressors were present (r=0.15, 95% CI −0.22 to 0.49, p=0.42"
