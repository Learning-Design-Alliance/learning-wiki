## Evidence

### Han Jeong-won 2025

Han Jeong-won, Park Junhee, Lee Hanna. (2025). Development and effects of a chatbot education program for self-directed learning in nursing students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07316-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Knowledge was assessed with a 10-item researcher-developed correct/incorrect tool validated by three nursing professors and two ICU nurses; chatbot group post-test mean was 7.32 ± 1.22 versus 6.72 ± 1.33 for control. The article reports "no statistically significant difference in Knowledge (t = -0.09, p =.926)."

> "mechanical ventilation nursing knowledge increased in both the experimental and control groups from pre-test to post-test; however, no statistically significant difference was observed between the groups"
