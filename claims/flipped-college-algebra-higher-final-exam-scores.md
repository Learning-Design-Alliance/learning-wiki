## Evidence

### Jenna Van Sickle 2016

Jenna Van Sickle. (2016). Discrepancies between Student Perception and Achievement of Learning Outcomes in a Flipped Classroom. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 16, No. 2, April 2016, pp. 29-38. https://doi.org/10.14434/josotl.v16i2.19216

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of final exam scores across four flipped college algebra sections (N=58) and six lecture-based control sections (N=54) at a small Catholic university; Table 3 reports means of 77.2 versus 70.5. The article reports a statistically significant difference with "p = .0143" and no effect size.

> "Using a one-tailed t-test to determine the statistical significance in all flipped and all control scores, the flipped classes’ scores are significantly higher than those of the traditional classes, with p = .0143."
