## Evidence

### Oxman 2024

Oxman, Andrew D.; Nsangi, Allen; Martínez García, Laura; Kaseje, Margaret; Samsó Jofra, Laura; Semakula, Daniel; Munthe-Kaas, Heather; Rosenbaum, Sarah E. (2024). The effects of teaching strategies on learning to think critically in primary and secondary schools: an overview of systematic reviews. F1000Research. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.158087.1

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The overview authors prepared plain language summaries for 37 teaching strategies judged most relevant to teaching critical thinking. Among didactic strategies, they concluded that signalling probably improves retention and transfer, and that text signalling may be more effective than graphical signalling.

> "Signalling probably improves retention and transfer. Text signalling may be more effective than graphical signalling."
