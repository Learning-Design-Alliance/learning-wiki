# The direct path from mathematics anxiety to word problem solving is non-significant once metacognition is modeled

## Evidence

### Lai Y 2015

Lai Y, Zhu X, Chen Y, Li Y (2015) Effects of Mathematics Anxiety and Mathematical Metacognition on Word Problem Solving in Children with and without Mathematical Learning Difficulties. PLoS ONE 10(6): e0130570. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0130570

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM with 224 participants: the partially mediated model (Model 1) fit well but its direct paths were non-significant; a chi-square difference test found "No significant Chi-square difference existed between Model 1 and Model 2", so the parsimonious fully mediated model was preferred.

> "However, the standardized path coefficient from mathematics anxiety (MA) to word problem solving (WPS) was non-significant (ß = -0.04, p = .71), as was the path from Metacognition to word problem solving (ß = 0.13, p = .12)."
