Multivariable logistic regression in the survey of 132 senior residents (70 with strong confidence, 62 without). Performing more than 20 IBT cases yielded HR 7.603 (95% CI 1.647–35.099, p = 0.009) versus ≤20 cases; unit brachytherapy workload was also significant (HR 0.999, p = 0.020).
