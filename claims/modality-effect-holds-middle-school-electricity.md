# The modality effect holds for middle school students learning electricity with multimedia animation in real school settings: spoken-text groups significantly outperform written-text groups with both abstract and concrete animation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Spoken text with abstract animation (Sp-A) yielded significantly higher achievement than written text with abstract animation (W-A), d = .35. [→ Kutbay 2020](#kutbay-2020)
`q3 i1` Spoken text with concrete animation (Sp-C) yielded significantly higher achievement than written text with concrete animation (W-C), d = .35. [→ Kutbay 2020 (2)](#kutbay-2020-2)

## Evidence

### Kutbay 2020

Kutbay, E. & Akpınar, Y. (2020). Investigating modality, redundancy and signaling principles with abstract and concrete representation. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology (IJEMST), 8(2), 131-145. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1255527

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test comparing Sp-A and W-A groups in the quasi-experimental study of middle school students studying an electricity unit. The comparison was significant with a small effect: "t (171) = 2.31, p = .022; d = .35".

> "there was a significant difference between the mean achievement scores of the Sp-A group and of the W-A group; t (171) = 2.31, p = .022; d = .35"

### Kutbay 2020 (2)

Kutbay, E. & Akpınar, Y. (2020). Investigating modality, redundancy and signaling principles with abstract and concrete representation. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology (IJEMST), 8(2), 131-145. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1255527

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Independent-sample t-test comparing Sp-C and W-C groups in the same quasi-experiment. The spoken-text concrete-animation group significantly outperformed the written-text concrete-animation group, "t (148) = 2.49, p = .014; d = .35".

> "between the mean achievement scores of the Sp-C group and of the W-C group; t (148) = 2.49, p = .014; d = .35"
