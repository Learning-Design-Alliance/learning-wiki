## Evidence

### Grace Liu 2026

Grace Liu, Brian Christian, Tsvetomira Dumbalska, Michiel A. Bakker, Rachit Dubey. (2026). AI Assistance Reduces Persistence and Hurts Independent Performance. Published as a conference paper at COLM 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04721

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-report multiple-choice question at the end of Experiment 2's AI condition. Chatlog records validated the reports: of 37 self-reported non-users, 83.8% did not message the AI at all and 94.1% messaged fewer than three times.

> "We found that the majority of participants (61%, N= 189) in the AI condition self-report that they used the AI primarily to get answers directly."
