### Liu Hua-yan 2024 (2)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Clinical efficacy at T2 in the same trial: the STOFMS group's total effective rate was 88.1% versus a lower control rate, with the article reporting significance at T2 (p < 0.05 in Table 4); no effect size is printed.

> "The total effective rate dropped at this point of time, yet children in the STOFMS group showed an 88.1% total effective rate, higher than that in the control group."
