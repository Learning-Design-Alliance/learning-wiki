## Evidence

### Cress 2018

Cress, U., Stahl, G., Rose, C., Law, N., & Ludvigsen, S. (2018). Forming social systems by coupling minds at different levels of cognition: Design, tools, and research methods. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 13, 235–240. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-018-9284-z

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Editorial summary of Heimbuch, Ollesch, and Bodemer's comparison of two collaboration scripts for wiki-based writing: the Wikipedia bold-edit script versus an alternative requiring planned edits to be discussed and deliberated before revision. The Wikipedia script yielded "a lower quality of article".

> "The results show that the Wikipedia script leads to a lower quality of article, even if the effect was not as large as the authors may have expected."
