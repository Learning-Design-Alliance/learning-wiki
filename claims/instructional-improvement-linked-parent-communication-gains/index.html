Analysis of 39 high schools surveyed in 1994 and 1997 (Figure 11). In the 15 schools with significant increases in focus on student learning, average teacher reports of communication with parents about academic work and the school's mission also increased significantly (.6); in the 10 schools with no-to-little improvement, mean communication declined.

"Between 1994 and 1997, 15 CPS high schools showed significant increases in the degree to which teachers reported that the instructional environment was focused more on student learning, such as setting high academic standards and providing the support for students to meet those standards"
