Alqahtani SS. (2025). Predictive validity of early and mid-year literacy assessments for end-of-year word reading fluency. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327242

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression comparison of fall versus mid-year models in the longitudinal sample of 412 students. The article reports the kindergarten contrast of 72% versus 53.7% variance explained and notes grade 1 mid-year R2 values exceeded 87% versus 63.9% for fall, a 36% increase. No standardized effect size is printed.

> "For kindergarten, mid-year assessments explained 72% of the variance in WRF, compared to 53.7% in the fall, reflecting a 34% improvement in predictive accuracy."
