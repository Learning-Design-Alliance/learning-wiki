## Evidence

### Zhong Zixing 2025

Zhong Zixing, Lu Yijuan, Pu Yuqun, Luo Qiong, Zhao Baihui. (2025). Educational efficacy of the simulation-based episiotomy training: a multi-center observational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-06887-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post comparison in a multi-center observational study of 82 participants performing simulated MLE on paper pads before and after a 30-minute course; Table 2 shows total means for length (31.5 to 34.3 mm, p = 0.002), angle (48.0 to 50.6 degrees, p = 0.028) and distance (9.1 to 10.8 mm, p = 0.001).
