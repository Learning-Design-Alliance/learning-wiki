Evidence¶

Breakstone 2021¶

Breakstone, J., Smith, M., Connors, P., Ortega, T., Kerr, D., & Wineburg, S. (2021). Lateral reading: College students learn to critically evaluate internet sources in an online course. Harvard Kennedy School (HKS) Misinformation Review. https://doi.org/10.37016/mr-2020-56

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Coded strategy analysis of students' written responses to Task 3 (climate-change denial websites friendsofscience.org or co2science.org), with two independent raters (Cohen's kappa pre = .921, post = .949). At posttest, "67 of 87 students engaged in lateral reading by leaving the target website."

"At pretest, only 3 of 87 students engaged in lateral reading by leaving the original website and consulting at least one other source (Figure 2)."
