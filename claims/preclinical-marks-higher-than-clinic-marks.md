Descriptive analysis of summative mark distributions for 484 students, shown in box plots (Figure 2a–c). Clinic means were 52.8 (Restorative), 54.1 (Perio) and 54.4 (Endo), below simulation means of 63.0, 79.5 and 69.6; students "achieved higher marks in the pre‐clinical assessments".
