Evidence¶

Altintas 2015¶

Altintas, E., & Ozdemir, A. S. (2015). Evaluating a Newly Developed Differentiation Approach in Terms of Student Achievement and Teachers' Opinions. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2015.4.2540

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis of teachers' responses to the question about benefits of the study (Table 16). All 5 teachers reported knowing students better; 60% reported understanding the importance of project studies and experiencing project management. Tables 13 and 14 show 100% agreement on project coordination knowledge and topic suitability. No effect size applies.

"Table 16 shows that teachers perceived the opportunities to get to know their students (to see how students used information they already had, to observe which project preparation stages proved difficult for students, to observe how students used their skills to take an activity and convert it into a product, etc.); to understand the importance of the project studies; and to experience the process of project management with the help of the activities that were conducted in this study."
