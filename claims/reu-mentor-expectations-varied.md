### Jonan-Rohi S. Plueger and Bethany R. Wilcox 2024

Jonan-Rohi S. Plueger and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2024). Perspectives from Physics Graduate Students on Their Experiences in NSF Research Experiences for Undergraduates. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.1119/perc.2024.pr.plueger](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2024.pr.plueger)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus-group theme on mentor relationships. One mentor gave a small project matched to background, then an actual project; another gave an early-career student a choice of projects he did not understand and, when the student struggled, dismissed him with no further training. Another mentor prioritized professional development over new science.

> "Students reported widely varying expectations that their mentors had for them."
