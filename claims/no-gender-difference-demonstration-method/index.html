Evidence¶

Efe M. Omwirhiren 2016¶

Efe M. Omwirhiren, Khalil U. Ibrahim. (2016). The Effects of Two Teachers' Instructional Methods on Students' Learning Outcomes in Chemistry in Selected Senior Secondary School in Kaduna Metropolis, Nigeria. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.7, No.15. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1103149

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Gender comparison within the demonstration condition (25 males, mean 52.52; 25 females, mean 51.96). The article reports t cal = 0.177 < t crit = 0.861 and F cal = 0.728 < F crit = 0.781, accepting Ho4; equivalence was not separately tested.

"From the results obtained, it can be seen that t here is no significant difference in the post-test mean achievement scores between males and females taught using demonstration method. Based on this result, the null hypothesis is accepted."
