Scoping review synthesis of 17 included studies. The review reports that "multi-component interventions resulted in superior outcomes compared to single-approach strategies", citing Miner et al.'s integration of e-learning, clinical protocols, and simulation training as an example.

> "Overall, multi-component interventions resulted in superior outcomes compared to single-approach strategies. For instance, Miner et al. [40] integration of e-learning with clinical protocols and simulation training showed a successful approach."
