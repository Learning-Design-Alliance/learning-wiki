Yunhee Kim 2026¶

Yunhee Kim, Doyeon Chae, Jaehwan Jang, Kyeong Taek Oh, Byeong-Sun Park & Min-gu Kim. (2026). Does tapping in sync reflect cognitive control? Links between sensorimotor synchronization, social context, and executive function in early childhood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00443-5

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Null result across the correlation and regression analyses of EF components in 140 preschoolers; the authors interpret it as suggesting synchronization's cognitive demands are specific to temporal-motor regulation rather than flexible strategy shifting.

"Regarding cognitive flexibility, our findings revealed no significant association with sensorimotor synchronization across any of the conditions."
