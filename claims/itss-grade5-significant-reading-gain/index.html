Evidence for ESSA 2021¶

Randomized classroom-level study of ITSS with "2,485 fifth-grade students in 128 classrooms across 45 schools," assessed after 6 to 7 months. The page reports the Gray Silent Reading Test result was "significant in Grade 5, with an effect size = +0.20."

"a second included 2,485 fifth-grade students in 128 classrooms across 45 schools. In both studies, classrooms were randomly assigned to use ITSS or continue with regular language arts instruction"
