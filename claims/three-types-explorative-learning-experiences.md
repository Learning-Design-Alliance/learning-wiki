# Explorative learning experiences can be organized in three forms — in-breadth, in-depth, and reflective — to intentionally support identity development

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The review distinguishes in-breadth exploration (new identity positions), in-depth exploration (specifying existing self-understandings), and reflective exploration (reflecting on self-understandings) as learning experiences that all support adolescents in exploring who they are and want to be. [→ Verhoeven 2019](#verhoeven-2019)

## Evidence

### Verhoeven 2019

Verhoeven, M., Poorthuis, A. M. G., & Volman, M. (2019). The Role of School in Adolescents' Identity Development. A Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9457-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's constant comparative analysis of 37 articles on intentional fostering of identity development, most conducted in after-school clubs, extracurricular classes, or summer camps (n = 21), yielded the three types of explorative learning experiences.

> "our analysis of the existing body of research caused us to distinguish between in-breadth, in-depth, and reflective explorative learning experiences that all, in their own way, support adolescents in exploring who they are and want to be."
