### Pratim Sengupta & Marie-Claire Shanahan 2016

Pratim Sengupta & Marie-Claire Shanahan. (2016). STEM as Public Computation and Boundary Play. STEM2016, University of Calgary. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1610.06658](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1610.06658)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Retrospective observational case study of a single visit by two school-aged boys and an adult, from a corpus of over 20 recorded visits, analyzed thematically with a phenomenographic approach. The visitors set the force weights to arbitrary high values (separation 2300, alignment 1620, coherence 2073) and named the resulting blob of boids a 'prison', contrasting with the designer's realistic bird-flocking intent.

> "Once they realized that they could easily make substantial changes to the emergent behaviors by altering the relative weights of the three forces, they began changing them to arbitrarily high values: separation (2300), alignment (1620), and coherence (2073)."
