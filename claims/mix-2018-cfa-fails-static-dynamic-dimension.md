# Confirmatory factor analysis failed to support the static-dynamic dimension of the 2x2 spatial classification framework in children

Narrative review attributing this null result to Mix et al. (2018), a confirmatory factor analysis on children in kindergarten, 3rd, and 6th grades. The review concludes other frameworks with less dimensionality may better fit children's spatial abilities. No effect size is printed.

> "an experimental study by Mix et al. (2018) testing the 2 × 2 classification framework using confirmatory factor analysis on data from children in kindergarten, 3rd, and 6th grades failed to find evidence for the static-dynamic dimension at any age or for the overall 2 × 2 classification framework."
