## Evidence

### Ghimire A and Singh C 2025

Ghimire A and Singh C. (2025). Peer Influence on Physics Self-Efficacy and Grades: A comparative study of students in an introductory calculus-based course who typically worked alone or in groups before and during the pandemic. [doi:10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895](https://doi.org/10.1080/0047231x.2025.2555895)

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Gender-stratified comparison of grades by working mode (Fig. 4). The article reports women who TWA "had significantly higher grades than those who worked in groups (TWG), with a medium effect size of 0.33" before the pandemic, reversing during COVID with "an effect size of 0.29"; men's differences were insignificant throughout.

> "Before the pandemic, women who TWA actually had significantly higher grades than those who worked in groups (TWG), with a medium effect size of 0.33 (see Fig. 4). During the COVID-19 period, this trend reversed, with women scoring higher grades when working with others, with an effect size of 0.29."
