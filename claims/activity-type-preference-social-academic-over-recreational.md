## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Participants showed significantly stronger intentions to perform social and academic rather than recreational activities with the target. [→ Eleni Fleva 2014](#eleni-fleva-2014)

## Evidence

### Eleni Fleva 2014

Eleni Fleva. (2014). Imagined Contact Improves Intentions towards a Hypothetical Peer with Asperger Syndrome but not Attitudes towards Peers with Asperger Syndrome in General. World Journal of Education, 5(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v5n1p1

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

A repeated measures ANOVA (Greenhouse-Geisser corrected) on activity type in the 416-participant sample found means of social M= 19.1, academic M= 18.8, recreational M= 17.8, with "Significant differences were found betwee n the three types of activities" (η2= .09).

> "Significant differences were found betwee n the three types of activities, F (1.74, 749.7) = 43.18, p< .001, η2= .09"
