Evidence¶

Saniya Vahedian Movahed and Fred Martin 2025¶

Saniya Vahedian Movahed and Fred Martin. (2025). Ask Me Anything: Exploring children's attitudes toward an age-tailored AI-powered chatbot. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14217 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2502.14217

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of student dialog transcripts identified three themes, including building relationships and anthropomorphizing: "Some interactions revealed students' tendencies to anthropomorphize the chatbot", e.g. asking its favorite shoe brand or shoe size; one student shared excitement when AMA named her favorite brand.
