## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Residual correlations between listening comprehension and passage reading, spelling, and phonemic awareness were near zero (0.08, 0.02, 0.11) despite moderate genetic correlations (0.64, 0.66, 0.66). [→ Chin Yang Shapland 2021](#chin-yang-shapland-2021)

## Evidence

### Chin Yang Shapland 2021

Chin Yang Shapland, Ellen Verhoef, George Davey Smith, Simon E. Fisher, Brad Verhulst, Philip S. Dale and Beate St Pourcain. (2021). Multivariate genome-wide covariance analyses of literacy, language and working memory skills reveal distinct etiologies. npj Science of Learning 6:23. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00101-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

IPC-model correlation comparison in the passage reading subset GSEM (N = 6453): the printed residual correlations for listening comprehension versus passage reading, spelling and phonemic awareness were "0.08 (SE = 0.05), 0.02 (SE = 0.05) and 0.11 (SE = 0.07)" against genetic correlations of 0.64–0.66.

> "respective residual correlations were 0.08 (SE = 0.05), 0.02 (SE = 0.05) and 0.11 (SE = 0.07), while corresponding genetic correlations were 0.64 (SE = 0.11), 0.66 (SE = 0.11), and 0.66 (SE = 0.11)"
