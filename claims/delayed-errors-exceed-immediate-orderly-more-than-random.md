### Arps 1917

Arps, G. P. (1917). Attitude as a determinant in spelling efficiency in immediate and delayed recall. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Arps+attitude+spelling+efficiency

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparison of immediate versus delayed error totals in the same 104-group study: orderly procedure errors rose 4.33 times, random procedure 2.6 times, and the "Relative advantage of the random method times that of the orderly method" was 1.73. Average words wrong were 0.1 (P.E. .07) immediate and 0.31 (P.E. .11) delayed.
