Procedural training for internal medicine residents pursuing subspecialty training: a national survey of fellowship program directors. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2499050

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Survey results across 15 subspecialties (Table 3 percentages). For example, the article reports "the majority of Cardiology FDs indicated they preferred their incoming fellows have achieved competence in arterial lines, central venous lines, drawing arterial and venous blood, peripheral venous line placement, and use of vascular ultrasound for line placement."

> "Procedures that FDs indicated they preferred incoming fellows to have achieved competence in prior to starting their fellowship training varied by specialty."
