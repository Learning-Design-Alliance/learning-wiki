## Evidence

### Shirazi 2019

Shirazi, F., & Heidari, S. (2019). The relationship between critical thinking skills and learning styles and academic achievement of nursing students. The Journal of Nursing Research, 27(4), e38. https://doi.org/10.1097/jnr.0000000000000307

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 139 nursing students using the California Critical Thinking Skills Test Form B and previous-semester GPA; analysis found "No significant relationship between critical thinking and academic achievement was found." No effect size printed.

> "No significant relationship between critical thinking and academic achievement was found. Moreover, the critical thinking subdomains were not significantly related to academic achievement."
