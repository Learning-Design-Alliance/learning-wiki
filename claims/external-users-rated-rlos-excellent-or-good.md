## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of 485 visits to the online feedback forms, 43 (16%) and 49 (22%) completed feedback for the two RLOs; 62 respondents were Nottingham based and 27 non-Nottingham, and all respondents rated the RLOs as excellent or good with no significant differences between Nottingham and non-Nottingham respondents (Wilcoxon paired p > 0.05).
