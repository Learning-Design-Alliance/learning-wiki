# Michigan students in the highest-poverty districts are 16 times more likely to learn from a teacher with emergency or temporary credentials than students in the wealthiest districts

### Jen DeNeal 2025

Jen DeNeal, Charlotte Pierce, and the EdTrust-Midwest team. (2025). Addressing Michigan's Teacher Shortage Problem for Students Most in Need: Closing the Opportunity Divide. EdTrust-Midwest. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED671388

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

EdTrust-Midwest analysis of district Annual Education Reports and state staff headcount data found students in band 6 are "16 times more likely to learn from a teacher with temporary or emergency credentials" than peers in the wealthiest districts; statewide 2.7% of teachers hold emergency credentials versus nearly 8% in high-poverty districts.

> "Students who attend school in Opportunity Index band 6 – the school districts with the highest concentrations of poverty – are 16 times more likely to learn from a teacher with temporary or emergency credentials than their peers in Michigan’s wealthiest school districts."
