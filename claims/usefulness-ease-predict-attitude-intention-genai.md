### Guillén-Gámez 2026

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r1`

PLS-SEM analysis in the 434-teacher sample testing H1–H4. PU→AOU (β = 0.178, f² = 0.032), PU→BI (β = 0.171, f² = 0.038) and PEOU→AOU (β = 0.158, f² = 0.029) showed small effects; PEOU→PU (f² = 0.158) approached a medium effect.

> "The results obtained through the PLS-SEM analysis confirmed a substantial relationship between both factors, supporting both hypothesis H3 (β = 0.158, t-value = 2.283, p <.05) and hypothesis H4 (β = 0.384, t-value = 5.366, p <.05)."
