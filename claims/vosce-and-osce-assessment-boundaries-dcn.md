BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06631-4

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Third-stage focus-group outcomes reported in Results: items with residual disagreement were re-discussed, and the panel concluded "8% of the competencies of the DCNs should not be formally evaluated via the vOSCE and that 3% should not be formally evaluated via either" method.

> "Thus, the evaluators maintained that 8% of the competencies of the DCNs should not be formally evaluated via the vOSCE and that 3% should not be formally evaluated via either the OSCE or vOSCE."

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Discussion of the residual items names physical examinations among items lacking vOSCE consensus. Table 4 shows the item "Performing physical exams" drew 68.8% negative and 31.2% positive views; the article notes these items "require longitudinal academic or professional follow-up."

> "For a few items in this study, such as physical examinations, no consensus was reached regarding the effectiveness of the vOSCE."
