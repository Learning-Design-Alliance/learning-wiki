Evidence¶

Bastani Hamsa 2025¶

Bastani Hamsa, Bastani Osbert, Sungu Alp, Ge Haosen, Kabakcı Özge, Mariman Rei. (2025). Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122

q2 · i? · causal · r3

End-of-session student surveys reported in SI Appendix Appendix B.3; the article notes "This mismatch between perceived and actual learning has been observed in other settings." Details of the survey analysis are in supplementary material not fully printed here.

"While students in the GPT Base arm performed worse on the exam (relative to the control arm), they did not perceive that they performed worse or learned less."
