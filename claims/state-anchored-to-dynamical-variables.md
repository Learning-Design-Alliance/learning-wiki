### Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja 2017

Glauco Cohen Ferreira Pantoja. (2017). A STUDY ON PRE-SERVICE PHYSICS TEACHERS' CONCEPTUALIZATION ON ELEMENTARY QUANTUM MECHANICS. Investigações em Ensino de Ciências – V22 (2), pp. 120-138. https://doi.org/10.22600/1518-8795.ienci2017v22n2p120

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Predicative knowledge analysis of problem-solving and concept-map data from the six-student intervention. The authors report "five out of six" students anchored state to Dynamical Variables, but "students tend to conceive State of Physical System as a set of Dynamical Variables and not as a configuration of these variables".

> "Most of the students (five out of six) anchored the concept of state as an elaboration of the one of Dynamical Variables (c.1.E.). This is partially important, because we can evidence a pattern of Meaningful Learning (learning from something relevant they already know)"
