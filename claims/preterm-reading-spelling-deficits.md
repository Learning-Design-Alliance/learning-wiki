## Evidence

### Twilhaar E Sabrina 2018

Twilhaar E Sabrina, de Kieviet Jorrit F, Aarnoudse-Moens Cornelieke SH, van Elburg Ruurd M, Oosterlaan Jaap. (2018). Academic performance of children born preterm: a meta-analysis and meta-regression. Archives of Disease in Childhood. Fetal and Neonatal Edition. https://doi.org/10.1136/archdischild-2017-312916

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Random effects meta-analysis of 15 studies for reading using validated norm-referenced tests; the printed effect size is SMD = −0.44, a small-sized effect, with high heterogeneity (I2=86.27). The quote reports "0.44 and 0.52 SD lower on reading and spelling".

> "Preterm children scored 0.44 and 0.52 SD lower on reading and spelling, respectively, compared with controls (z=−4.38, p<0.001 and z=−9.53, p<0.001), indicating small-sized and medium-sized effects."

### Twilhaar E Sabrina 2018 (2)

Twilhaar E Sabrina, de Kieviet Jorrit F, Aarnoudse-Moens Cornelieke SH, van Elburg Ruurd M, Oosterlaan Jaap. (2018). Academic performance of children born preterm: a meta-analysis and meta-regression. Archives of Disease in Childhood. Fetal and Neonatal Edition. https://doi.org/10.1136/archdischild-2017-312916

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Same random effects meta-analysis; the printed effect size for spelling is SMD = −0.52, a medium-sized effect, from 6 studies with no significant heterogeneity (I2=0.00). The quote reports the "medium-sized" spelling effect.

> "Preterm children scored 0.44 and 0.52 SD lower on reading and spelling, respectively, compared with controls (z=−4.38, p<0.001 and z=−9.53, p<0.001), indicating small-sized and medium-sized effects."
