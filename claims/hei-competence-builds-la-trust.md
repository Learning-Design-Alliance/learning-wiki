## Evidence

### Asma Shannan Alzahrani 2023

Asma Shannan Alzahrani, Yi-Shan Tsai, Naif Aljohani, Emma Whitelock-wainwright, Dragan Gasevic. (2023). Do teaching staff trust stakeholders and tools in learning analytics? A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10229-w

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 24 semi-structured interviews with teaching staff at three Saudi HEIs. Participants attributed trust in HEI competence to existing e-learning infrastructure and specialised data-analysis capacity, e.g., statistics departments able to "perform the task of analysing data".

> "Four participants from large HEIs shared their perspectives related to the issue of trust in stakeholders to adopt LA based on current competence and expertise in e-learning systems; they believed that their HEIs had a strong e-learning infrastructure that would allow them to adopt LA effectively."
