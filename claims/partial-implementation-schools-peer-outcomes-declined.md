Matthew P. Steinberg, Johanna Lacoe. (2018). Reforming School Discipline: School-Level Policy Implementation and the Consequences for Suspended Students and Their Peers. American Journal of Education, vol. 125, issue 1. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/reforming-school-discipline-school-level-policy-implementation-and-the-consequences-for-suspended

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Difference-in-differences analysis of peer outcomes under the Philadelphia reform reports that "peer math achievement declined and attendance decreased in schools that did not fully implement the district-level reform." No effect size is printed.

> "Postreform changes in peer outcomes varied with school-level implementation: in schools that eliminated classroom disorder OSS, peer math achievement and attendance were unaffected, whereas peer math achievement declined and attendance decreased in schools that did not fully implement the district-level reform."
