MRA predicting first-grade WJ Letter/Word Identification in the 107-child sample was significant overall (R2 = .515, F(9, 97) = 11.44, p = .001), with kindergarten number sense, kindergarten reading, attention problems, nonverbal reasoning, and start age as unique predictors; the executive functioning beta was −.067.
