## Evidence

### Rouble 2023

Rouble, M., Dobbs, M., & Gilbert, A. (2023). WinterLab: Developing a low-cost, portable experiment platform to encourage engagement in the electronics lab. International Journal of Designs for Learning. [doi:10.14434/ijdl.v14i1.33406](https://doi.org/10.14434/ijdl.v14i1.33406)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Field-testing reflection in the 2019 study: only 4 study volunteers were in the 88-student traditional sections (11 additional survey respondents), versus 16 of 25 in the pilot. The authors note they "may have selected for students who were already enthusiastic about electronics."

> "This makes it challenging to meaningfully compare the experience of the students in the pilot section with that of their peers in the traditional laboratory sessions."
