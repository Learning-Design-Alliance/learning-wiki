## Evidence

### Adamson Maheen M. 2014

Adamson Maheen M., Taylor Joy L., Heraldez Daniel, Khorasani Allen, Noda Art, Hernandez Beatriz, Yesavage Jerome A. (2014). Higher Landing Accuracy in Expert Pilots is Associated with Lower Activity in the Caudate Nucleus. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112607

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory age analysis in the same study of approach flight-control measures; the article reports significance without a standardized effect size.

> "Older pilots also made larger joystick (aileron) movements, indicating more roll of the aircraft along its longitudinal axis (p = .013)."

### Adamson Maheen M. 2014 (2)

Adamson Maheen M., Taylor Joy L., Heraldez Daniel, Khorasani Allen, Noda Art, Hernandez Beatriz, Yesavage Jerome A. (2014). Higher Landing Accuracy in Expert Pilots is Associated with Lower Activity in the Caudate Nucleus. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0112607

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Null age contrast on d′ in the same sample, reported with descriptive means only; the article prints no effect size, so non-significance is not evidence of equivalence.

> "In terms of landing decision accuracy, the effect of age on d′ was not significant (older: d′ = 1.79±2.45; younger: d′ = 3.33±2.23; p>.1)."
