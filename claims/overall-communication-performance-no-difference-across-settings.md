## Evidence

### Dewi Sari Puspa 2024

Dewi Sari Puspa, Amanda Wilson, Robbert Duvivier, Brian Kelly, Conor Gilligan. (2024). Do the teaching, practice and assessment of clinical communication skills align? BMC Medical Education 24:609. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05596-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational study of 28 fourth-year medical students videorecorded in classroom workshops, real patient encounters and an OSCE station, rated with the CCOG; overall performance averaged "4.2, 4.3 and 4.2" across the three settings, described as satisfactory. Analysis of variance found no significant overall difference.

> "The average score for the overall performance in classroom, clinical and OSCE settings was 4.2, 4.3 and 4.2, respectively corresponds to performances which were satisfactory and appropriate for their study level."
