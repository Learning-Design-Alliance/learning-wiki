Evidence¶

Bruno Sauce 2021¶

Bruno Sauce, John Wiedenhoeft, Nicholas Judd and Torkel Klingberg. (2021). Change by challenge: A common genetic basis behind childhood cognitive development and cognitive training. npj Science of Learning 6:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00096-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The WM change model on the combined sample of 344 participants tested the cogPGS × sample term. The article reports a non-significant interaction (p = 0.46) and no effect size for this term; equivalence was not tested.

"Importantly, there was no interaction of cogPGS with sample ( p = 0.46), which suggests that the effect of cogPGS does not depend on the sample being developmental or training."
