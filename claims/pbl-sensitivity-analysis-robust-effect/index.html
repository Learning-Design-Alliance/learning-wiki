Evidence¶

Zhou Jiyin 2016¶

Zhou Jiyin, Zhou Shiwen, Huang Chunji, Xu Rufu, Zhang Zuo, Zeng Shengya, Qian Guisheng. (2016). Effectiveness of problem-based learning in Chinese pharmacy education: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0546-z

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Sensitivity analysis performed by sequentially excluding studies with inadequate random-sequence generation from the theoretical-score meta-analysis. The recalculated pooled estimate "favored the PBL group (SMD = 1.06, 95 % CI [0.77, 1.35], P < 0.00001)" and matched the primary analysis.

"After excluding five studies [21–23, 28, 31] with inadequate generation of a randomized sequence from the analyses of theoretical scores, the pooled effect size favored the PBL group (SMD = 1.06, 95 % CI [0.77, 1.35], P < 0.00001) and did not change the effects observed in the primary analysis."
