## Evidence

### Zinzi A. 2020

Zinzi A., Pittori C., Tagliamonte R., Nichelli E. (2020). ASI Space Science Data Center participation to high-school outreach program. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/abc605](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/abc605)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-activity online questionnaire of 20 students (12 females, 8 males) from two schools in the 2017/18 SSDC program. Tagging “partially agree” and “totally agree” as positive, "95% of the students answered positively to all of these 3 questions" on explanation, interest, and question time. The paper also reports 95% would recommend the experience.

> "The possible answers were “totally disagree”, “partially disagree”, “don’t know”, “partially agree”, “totally agree” and, by tagging the last two answers as “positive”, 95% of the students answered positively to all of these 3 questions."
