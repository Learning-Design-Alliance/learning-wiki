## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Chengyan Yang 2025

Chengyan Yang, Tongran Liu, Mengxin Wen & Xun Liu. (2025). The distinct functions of working memory and intelligence in model-based and model-free reinforcement learning. npj Science of Learning 10:72. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00363-w

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Correlation analyses of RL parameters and performance for 50 participants. Under high load, corrected reward rate correlated with inverse temperature (r = 0.41, p = 0.003) and learning rate (r = 0.32, p = 0.026); the article suggests participants proficient at exploitation and information utilization achieved higher rewards.

> "the corrected reward rate was signiﬁcantly correlated with RL parameters, especially with the inverse temperature (r = 0.69, 95% CI [0.51, 0.81], p < 0.001) and the mixing weight (r = 0.44, 95% CI [0.19, 0.64], p = 0.001) in the low WM load condition"
