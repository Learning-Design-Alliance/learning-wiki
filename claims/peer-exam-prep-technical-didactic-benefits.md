## Evidence

### Huhn D 2015

Huhn D, Eckart W, Karimian-Jazi K, Amr A, Herzog W, Nikendei C. (2015). Voluntary peer-led exam preparation course for international first year students: Tutees' perceptions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0391-5

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative content analysis of 10 semi-structured end-of-semester interviews with tutees; 126 statements yielded 20 themes in four categories. Theme B.1 (11 quotations) reported that "gained insight into which topics were relevant for the exams" and that tutors' language was easier to understand than lecturers' jargon.

> "In the tutorial, international students gained insight into which topics were relevant for the exams and what literature was most appropriate to prepare for tests."
