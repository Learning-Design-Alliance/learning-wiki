## Evidence

### Walters T 2022

Walters T, Abeyta A, Bean AJ, Wilson MA. (2022). The impact of holistic review on correlations between doctoral student outcomes, and GPA and GRE scores in the biomedical sciences. PLOS ONE 17(12): e0279258. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0279258

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney test and column analysis comparing GRE percentiles with candidacy exam results in the GSBS cohort (Table 7): verbal percentiles p = 0.0299 (significant), quantitative percentiles p = 0.2345; no effect size printed.

> "Contrary to our findings comparing GRE scores and GPA with candidacy exam results, we observed a significant difference in average verbal GRE percentile between students who passed or failed the candidacy exam (Fig 7A and Table 7)."
