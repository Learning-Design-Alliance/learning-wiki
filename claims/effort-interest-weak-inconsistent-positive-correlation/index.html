q3 · i0 · associational · r1

Pairwise raw-data correlations across the 21 measurement points in the N = 360 sample. Printed coefficients span r = .01 to .28, with three measuring points non-significant; the lower bound r = .01 is negligible, so no meaningful effect size is printed for the pair overall.

"Invested mental effort and interest (Fig. 10, upper left, dark grey line) were found to be generally positively correlated ( r = .01 to .28). However, 14% (i.e. three measuring points) of the correlations were not significant."
