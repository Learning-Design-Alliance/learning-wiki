`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reports, citing Evans et al. (2024) in the special issue, a cross-sectional study linking a motivational teaching style to higher motivation, lower load experiences, and higher achievement; the design precludes causal interpretation.

> "Evans et al. (2024) demonstrate that promoting autonomous motivation (i.e., using a motivational teaching style) was linked to higher student motivation, lower experiences of cognitive load and higher student achievement. Due to the cross-sectional data, the results cannot be interpreted in a causal way."
