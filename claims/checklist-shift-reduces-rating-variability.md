## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Dory V 2019

Dory V, Cummings B-A, Mondou M, Young M. (2019). Nudging clinical supervisors to provide better in-training assessment reports. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-019-00554-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Item-level MANOVA of the 360-ITAR sample. The article reports this decrease was "inevitable with the change from 5-point rating scales to a checklist format", a change made to reflect the pass-fail philosophy of the curriculum.

> "Scores on one item ('Checklist/numeric ratings show sufficient variability to allow identification of relative strengths and weaknesses of the trainee') decreased (from 2.9 (SD 0.9) to 1.2 (SD 0.4) and 1 (SD 0.2), p < 0.001)."
