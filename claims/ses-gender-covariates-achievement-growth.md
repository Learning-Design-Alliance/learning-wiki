# SES predicts initial achievement levels and gender predicts mathematics growth within settings, without altering between-setting growth comparisons

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` SES covaried with achievement intercepts across settings, and gender covaried with the mathematics slope in inclusive classrooms, with boys showing higher growth. [→ Szumski Grzegorz 2022](#szumski-grzegorz-2022)
`q3 i?` Adding these covariants did not produce significant between-group differences in growth in either subject. [→ Szumski Grzegorz 2022 (2)](#szumski-grzegorz-2022-2)

## Evidence

### Szumski Grzegorz 2022

Szumski Grzegorz, Smogorzewska Joanna, Grygiel Paweł. (2022). Academic achievement of students without special educational needs and disabilities in inclusive education–Does the type of inclusion matter? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0270124

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Growth curve model with gender and SES covariants, inclusive classrooms without co-teaching, mathematics. The article reports the gender effect on the slope "Est = -0.164, SE = 0.08, p = .05" and that SES was significant for both intercept and slope in this group.

> "was significant for the slope (Est = -0.164, SE = 0.08, p = .05), showing that among boys the growth of the results in mathematics was higher than among girls"

### Szumski Grzegorz 2022 (2)

Szumski Grzegorz, Smogorzewska Joanna, Grygiel Paweł. (2022). Academic achievement of students without special educational needs and disabilities in inclusive education–Does the type of inclusion matter? PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0270124

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Covariant model for mathematics across settings. The article states "There were, however, no significant differences between groups in the case of changes in the results over time", printing the pairwise estimates; the same held for language.

> "There were, however, no significant differences between groups in the case of changes in the results over time; general education classrooms versus inclusive classrooms without co-teaching: Est = 0.102, SE = 0.093, p = .272"
