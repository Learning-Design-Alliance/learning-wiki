### Adugna Yimer Mihretie 2026

Adugna Yimer Mihretie, Ayelign Abebe, Zerfu Tadesse Alemu. (2026). Associations between exposure to nutrition, WASH interventions and children’s academic performance in Ethiopia: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-26107-4

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Subgroup meta-analysis pooling four studies of school feeding programs in Ethiopian schools. The review reports "an overall nonsignificant pooled effect size of 1.314 (95% CI: −0.885, 3.514)" with high heterogeneity (I² = 94.7%).

> "In contrast, school feeding programs have mixed results, with some studies reporting positive associations and others indicating negative effects, leading to an overall nonsignificant pooled effect size of 1.314 (95% CI: −0.885, 3.514) [23, 24, 31, 33]."

### Adugna Yimer Mihretie 2026 (2)

Adugna Yimer Mihretie, Ayelign Abebe, Zerfu Tadesse Alemu. (2026). Associations between exposure to nutrition, WASH interventions and children’s academic performance in Ethiopia: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Public Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-26107-4

`q1 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Authors' narrative interpretation in the Discussion of why school feeding results vary: some Ethiopian studies report positive impacts on attendance and concentration, while others cite "poor food quality and insufficient rations."

> "While some Ethiopian studies, such as Bekri Mohammed (2023) [22], Desalegn, Tsion A (2021) [23], and Muluken Ayehu, Solomon (2021) [26], report positive impacts on school attendance and concentration, others highlight challenges, including poor food quality and insufficient rations, which undermine the effectiveness of these programs"
