## Evidence

### David W. Putwain 2026

David W. Putwain, Andrea Mallaburn, Hanna Gaspard. (2026). Student perceptions of teacher messages about the value of science in the classroom. Learning and Instruction 104 (2026) 102365. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102365

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null finding from the student-level (L1) paths of the ML-SEMs testing whether individual differences in receptivity to teacher value messages predicted T3 expectancy, utility value, and effort cost beyond T1 levels. No statistically significant paths were reported and no effect size was printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "Perceived teacher value messages in the L1 portion of the model (i.e., students who more or less strongly agreed that their teachers communicated the value of sciences relative to their classmates) were unrelated to motivational beliefs, after controlling for their prior beliefs."
