Self-reported preparation time in the randomized study, reported in Results. The video group's mean time was lower (74 ± 3 vs. 81 ± 3 min) but the difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.09); equivalence was not tested.

> "a trend towards less “time spent preparing” was noticeable for students in the video group, while statistical significance was not reached (Experimental group: 74 ± 3 vs. control: 81 ± 3 min; p = 0.09, see Fig. 3)"
