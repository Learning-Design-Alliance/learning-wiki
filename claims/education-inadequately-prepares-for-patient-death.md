Hinzmann Dominik, Wijnen-Meijer Marjo, Corazza Laura, Becker Veronika, Kagerbauer Simone, Haseneder Rainer, Berberat Pascal O., Jedlicska Nana. (2023). Experiences of medical students and nursing trainees from unexpected death through simulation training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-023-04638-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group finding from the reflections-on-the-experience theme. Interviewees criticised the strong focus on healing, called for death to be addressed normally rather than as a taboo subject, and criticised lack of staff support after patient deaths in clinical settings. No effect sizes are printed.

> "Medical students and nursing trainees agreed that their educational programs do not adequately prepare them to encounter and deal with the death of a patient."
