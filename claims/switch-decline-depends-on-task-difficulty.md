## Evidence

### Harada Hitoshi 2017

Harada Hitoshi, Kanaji Shingo, Nishi Masayasu, Otake Yoshito, Hasegawa Hiroshi, Yamamoto Masashi, Matsuda Yoshiko, Yamashita Kimihiro, Matsuda Takeru, Oshikiri Taro, Sumi Yasuo, Nakamura Tetsu, Suzuki Satoshi, Sato Yoshinobu, Kakeji Yoshihiro. (2017). The learning effect of using stereoscopic vision in the early phase of laparoscopic surgical training for novices. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-017-5654-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of their own crossover data: all task 1 (simple task) scores in the 3D start group "did not differ significantly between just before and after switching to a 2D monitor," while complicated tasks worsened, especially technical errors.

> "However, in more complicated tasks, the scores for the 3D start group obviously worsened just after switching monitors, especially the score for technical errors."
