## Evidence

### López-Brotons Maria 2026

López-Brotons Maria, Javaloy-Ballestero Sergio, Ramos-Rincón José-Manuel. (2026). Evaluation of a peer-assisted, simulation-based clinical skills training program in Spain: a prospective single-group before-and-after study. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2026.23.15

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Prospective single-group pre–post study without a control group; 425 paired pre–post workshop evaluations analyzed across 2 academic years. The article reports a large effect size, "Cohen’s d=1.14", with a significant paired t-test increase.

> "Students reported improved learning, with workshop-specific knowledge test scores increasing significantly from 6.1±2.6 to 8.7±1.6 (Δ=2.5; 95% confidence interval [CI], 2.29–2.71; Cohen’s d=1.14; P<0.001)."
