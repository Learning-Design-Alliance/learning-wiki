Hodza-Beganovic R, Berggren P, Edelbring S. (2025). The role of leadership in enhancing non-technical skills in healthcare: a qualitative study in a Balkan context. Human Resources for Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12960-025-01022-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group statement from Workshop 2 in the qualitative thematic analysis. Early in the project participants described "significant ambiguity around responsibilities, which led to inefficiencies and overlapping tasks," and later recognized leadership as essential for structured routines and shared accountability.

> "When we know our role and responsibilities, we function as a system rather than as isolated individuals. It prevents duplication of tasks and ensures that critical duties do not get overlooked."
