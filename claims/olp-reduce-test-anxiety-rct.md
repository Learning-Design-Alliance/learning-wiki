# Open-label placebos reduce test anxiety in university students more than a no-treatment control group over two weeks before an exam

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Test anxiety improved significantly more in the open-label placebo group than in the control group (group × time interaction, η2 = 0.07). [→ Schaefer Michael 2019](#schaefer-michael-2019)
`q3 i2` Within the OLP group, test anxiety was significantly reduced from pre to post (Cohen's dz = 0.5), while the control group's reduction was small and non-significant. [→ Schaefer Michael 2019 (2)](#schaefer-michael-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Schaefer Michael 2019

Schaefer Michael, Denke Claudia, Harke Rebecca, Olk Nina, Erkovan Merve, Enge Sören. (2019). Open-label placebos reduce test anxiety and improve self-management skills: A randomized-controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-49466-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Two-group randomized controlled trial with 58 students; ANOVA with factors group and time on PAF change showed a significant interaction, with "F (1,56) = 4.23, p < 0.05, η2 = 0.07".

> "Results revealed significant interactions between group and time for test anxiety (ANOVA with factors group and time, F (1,56) = 4.23, p < 0.05, η2 = 0.07)."

### Schaefer Michael 2019 (2)

Schaefer Michael, Denke Claudia, Harke Rebecca, Olk Nina, Erkovan Merve, Enge Sören. (2019). Open-label placebos reduce test anxiety and improve self-management skills: A randomized-controlled trial. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-49466-6

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Post hoc paired t-tests in the same RCT showed the OLP group's test anxiety change score of 4.39 was significant, "Cohen's dz = 0.5"; the control group's change score was 0.07 and non-significant (p > 0.10).

> "Post hoc t-tests demonstrated that test anxiety in the OLP group was significantly reduced (change score: 4.39; t(30) = 2.61, p ≤ 0.01, two-sided, Cohen's dz = 0.5)."
