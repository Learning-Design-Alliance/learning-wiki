SEM path analysis of the 403-respondent dataset. The direct path from learning motivation to learning burnout was significant and negative, supporting the hypothesis that higher motivation predicts lower burnout.

> "Learning motivation has a significant negative effect on learning burnout (β = − 0.250, S.E. = 0.069, C.R. = − 4.061, p < 0.001)."
