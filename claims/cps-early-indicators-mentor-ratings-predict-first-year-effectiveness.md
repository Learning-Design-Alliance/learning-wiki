# Student teachers' self-assessed preparedness did not predict first-year effectiveness (r = 0.03), while mentor teachers' ratings of student teachers did (r = 0.24)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i0`–`i2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i0` Student teachers' perceptions of their preparedness were essentially unrelated to their first-year REACH observation ratings (correlation 0.03). [→ Gordon 2018](#gordon-2018)
`q2 i2` Mentor teachers' ratings of their student teachers positively predicted first-year observation ratings (correlation 0.24). [→ Gordon 2018 (2)](#gordon-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Gordon 2018

Gordon, M.F., Jiang, J.Y., Kapadia Matsko, K., Ronfeldt, M., Greene Nolan, H.G., & Reininger, M. (2018). On the path to becoming a teacher: The landscape of student teaching in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/surveys

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

For the 2014-15 cohort linked to first-year REACH observation scores, "the correlation between student perceptions of their own preparedness and their observation ratings in their first year of teaching was 0.03" — a negligible association.

> "The correlation between student perceptions of their own preparedness and their observation ratings in their first year of teaching was 0.03."

### Gordon 2018 (2)

Gordon, M.F., Jiang, J.Y., Kapadia Matsko, K., Ronfeldt, M., Greene Nolan, H.G., & Reininger, M. (2018). On the path to becoming a teacher: The landscape of student teaching in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/surveys

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

In the same linked cohort analysis, mentor teachers' ratings of their student teachers predicted first-year performance: "the correlation between mentor teachers' ratings of their student teachers and observation ratings of student teachers in their first-year of teaching was 0.24" (r = 0.24).

> "The correlation between mentor teachers' ratings of their student teachers and observation ratings of student teachers in their first-year of teaching was 0.24."
