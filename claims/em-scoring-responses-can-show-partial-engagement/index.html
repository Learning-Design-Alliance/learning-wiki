Steven Wise 2021¶

Steven Wise, Megan Kuhfeld. (2021). A method for identifying partial test-taking engagement. Applied Measurement in Education, 34(2), 150-161. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/a-method-for-identifying-partial-test-taking-engagement/

The article reports, citing a recent study, that responses used in E-M scoring "can sometimes reflect less-than-full, or partial engagement", and that in these instances E-M scores "can still be distorted by disengagement". No statistics are printed in the available text.

"A recent study provided evidence that the responses used in E-M scoring can sometimes reflect less-than-full, or partial engagement. In these instances, E-M scores will be less trustworthy because they can still be distorted by disengagement."
