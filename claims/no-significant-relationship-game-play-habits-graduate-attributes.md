## Evidence

### Matthew Barr 2020

Matthew Barr. (2020). A cross-sectional study of video game play habits and graduate skills attainment. Research in Learning Technology. https://doi.org/10.25304/rlt.v28.2326

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional online survey of 2145 students at a single Scottish university (8.4% of the student population), using validated instruments (CAS, SPCCS, I-ADAPT-M, Resourcefulness Scale). The article reports "no significant relationship between the existing game play habits and graduate attribute scores".

> "It becomes apparent here that there is no significant relationship between the existing game play habits and graduate attribute scores, supporting the idea that the significantly improved scores recorded for participants in the previous experimental study were the result of a combination of factors."
