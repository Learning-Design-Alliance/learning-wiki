Evidence¶

Dai 2023¶

Dai, K., Garcia, J., & Olave-Encina, K. (2023). In-between worlds: Chilean university lecturers' experiences of teaching transition between face-to-face and virtual reality contexts during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10217-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Findings from the interview and focus-group data in the qualitative study. The authors report that lecturers "gradually developed an empowered sense of agency" despite initial apprehension.

"Despite lecturers' initial apprehension towards technology, they gradually developed an empowered sense of agency towards teaching in the transition between face-to-face and VW-based contexts."

Related Claims¶
