## Evidence

### Yeates Peter 2017

Yeates Peter, Woolf Katherine, Benbow Emyr, Davies Ben, Boohan Mairhead, Eva Kevin. (2017). A randomised trial of the influence of racial stereotype bias on examiners' scores, feedback and recollections in undergraduate clinical exams. BMC Medicine 15:179. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-017-0943-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GLM GEE analysis of scores across three performances and three measures found no ethnicity effect; overall scores were 3.1 vs 3.1 (p = 0.88). The study had "81% power to detect a difference of 0.35 out of 7.0", equivalent to d = 0.32. No effect size was printed for the null results.

> "The average knowledge score when performances were acted by Asian students was 3.9 (95% CI 3.8–4.0) vs 3.9 (3.8–4.0) when acted by white students (p = 0.77). The average communication score when the performances were acted by Asian students was 3.9 (3.8–4.1) vs 3.9 (3.7–4.0) when acted by white students (p = 0.31)."
