## Evidence

### Zhang Dongmei 2025

Zhang Dongmei, Huang Anle, Lei Yunxiao, Liu Haiyang, Yang Liu, Wang Congzhi, Yuan Ting, Li Xiaoping, Zhang Lin, Shang Wenyuan. (2025). Nursing students’ experiences and perceptions regarding in-class flipped classroom: a mixed-methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07248-x

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive questionnaire survey of learning satisfaction administered at the end of the pediatric nursing skills course; the top-rated item was “helping me master knowledge effectively” and “the median score for all items was no less than 4/5”.

> "the top three rated aspects were “helping me master knowledge effectively” (4.47; SD = 0.66), “creating a good classroom atmosphere” (4.40; SD = 0.71), and “improving my communication and collaboration skills” (4.39; SD = 0.68). The median score for all items was no less than 4/5"
