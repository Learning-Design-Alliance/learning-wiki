Analytical worked example in Section III: a 3 kg wheel of diameter 1 m spinning at 1.2 rad/s is struck by a 0.3 kg stone; the stone's angular momentum is "h |⃗ p| ⟲ = 2.4 kg m2/s ⟲", giving a total of 1.5 kg m2/s and the printed final angular velocity.

> "When the stone is instantaneously at rest, this is all carried by the wheel, so its final angular velocity is ω↔final = ℓ↔total/I = 2 rad/s ⟲ ."
