Mary C. English 2013¶

The article's abstract states the premise of the paper: PBL success depends on student self-regulation, and "for many students, these processes do not occur naturally or easily." No empirical data are reported for this assertion in the supplied text.

"students must take responsibility for the learning process by setting goals, monitoring, reflecting, and sustaining their motivation from the beginning of the project until the end. However, for many students, these processes do not occur naturally or easily."
