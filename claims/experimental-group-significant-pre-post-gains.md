## Evidence

### Jose 2012

Jose, G. R., & Dharma Raja, B. W. (2012). Multimedia Modular Approach for Augmenting the Speaking Skill of the Student-Teachers. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, 2(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1070215

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

A dependent t-test compared the experimental group's pre-test and post-test speaking-skill scores. The mean improved from 25.38 at pre-test to 67.13 at post-test, a statistically significant gain at the 1% level.

> "In the Table 3, since p value is less than 0.01 in the scores of the speaking skill, the null hypothesis is not accepted at 1% level of significance. Hence, there is a significant difference between the pretest and the posttest scores of the experimental group."
