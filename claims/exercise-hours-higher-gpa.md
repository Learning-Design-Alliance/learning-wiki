## Evidence

### Alhazmi Amani; Aziz Farah; Hawash Manal Mohammed 2021

Alhazmi Amani; Aziz Farah; Hawash Manal Mohammed. (2021). Association of BMI, Physical Activity with Academic Performance among Female Students of Health Colleges of King Khalid University, Saudi Arabia. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182010912

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

One-way ANOVA across weekly exercise-hours categories in the cross-sectional survey: F = 3.219, p = 0.023; the >4 h weekly group (n = 17) averaged 4.79 ± 0.85 GPA versus 3.39 ± 0.78 for non-exercisers. Only the p-value is printed.

> "the studied subjects who exercise more than 4 h weekly have significantly higher overall mean of academic performance than the participants who either did less than 4 h or not exercise at all (p = 0.023)"
