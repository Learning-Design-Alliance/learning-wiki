Evidence¶

Means 2020¶

Means, B., & Neisler, J. (2020). Unmasking Inequality: STEM Course Experiences During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/c978880f-6afe-4778-9df2-dac931a9c471

q2 · i? · associational · r2

National probability-based KnowledgePanel survey (May 13 to June 1, 2020) of undergraduates whose STEM courses shifted online; 620 STEM coursetakers described. The report states satisfaction "was much lower" post-shift, with 47% very satisfied before and 15% after.

"almost half of the students describing STEM courses (47%) said they were very satisfied with their course before it went fully online. Student satisfaction with their STEM course after it went online was much lower, with only 15% of students describing themselves as very satisfied"
