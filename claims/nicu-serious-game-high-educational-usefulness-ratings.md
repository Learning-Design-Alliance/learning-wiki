## Evidence

### Sarvestan Javad 2025

Sarvestan Javad, Thananithichot Stithorn, Miyosawa Yukihide, Hirabayashi Koichi, Yamada Kodai, Kobayashi Fumiya, Ogihara Nanami, Takeda Noa, Okamura Eri, Matsumura Shogo. (2025). Development of a Serious Game to Simulate Neonatal Intensive Care Unit Experiences: Collaborative Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/73009

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Voluntary postgame Google Forms survey of 160 players of the released game, analyzed descriptively. Mean educational usefulness ratings all exceeded 4: "4.24 (SD 0.84), 4.16 (SD 0.96), and 4.37 (SD 0.78)" for empathy, knowledge acquisition, and learning effectiveness. No effect sizes were reported.

> "The mean scores for length, difficulty, gameplay, story empathy, usefulness for knowledge acquisition, and effectiveness of serious games for learning were 3.05 (SD 0.62), 2.49 (SD 0.76), 3.65 (SD 0.77), 4.24 (SD 0.84), 4.16 (SD 0.96), and 4.37 (SD 0.78), respectively"
