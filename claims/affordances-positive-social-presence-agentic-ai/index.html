Same PLS-SEM survey study (N = 398); the model tested the Social Presence Theory pathway in which perceived agentic AI affordances relate to perceived social presence, with the printed path coefficients and 95% confidence intervals as reported.

"Interactivity (β = 0.468, 95% CI: 0.404–0.528), information sharing (β = 0.323, 95% CI: 0.248–0.398), and collaborative environment (β = 0.184, 95% CI: 0.112–0.265) had a positive relationship with perceived social presence."
