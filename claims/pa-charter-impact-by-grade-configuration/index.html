Figure 7 analysis of learning gains by school grade configuration (elementary, middle, high, multi-level) against TPS VCR peers. Elementary charters show about 41 more days of reading learning; multi-level charters show significantly weaker growth in both subjects (about 41 fewer reading days, 59 fewer math days). No standardized effect size is printed.

"students in charter elementary schools experience greater growth than their TPS peers in reading, while performing similarly to their TPS peers in math. These students attending elementary charter schools experience about the equivalent of 41 more days of learning in reading"
