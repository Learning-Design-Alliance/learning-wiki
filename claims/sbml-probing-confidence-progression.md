Theerakulpisut Saralee, Tengtrisorn Supaporn, Singha Penny, Rattanalert Narisa. (2026). Simulation-based mastery learning for nasolacrimal duct probing in ophthalmology residency: a prospective pre–post educational study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09213-8

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Confidence was rated on a 0–4 Likert scale at each of four training stages (Table 3); the questionnaire showed acceptable reliability (Cronbach's alpha = 0.73). Ratings reflect perceived readiness, as all training occurred on the simulator.

> "Following the instructional video and MCQ review with feedback, participants reported a median confidence level of 2, indicating moderate confidence. Continued practice using the simulation model, accompanied by real-time feedback, elevated their confidence to a considerable level (level 3). Ultimately, with repeated self-guided practice and assessment, participants reached a high level of confidence (level 4)."
