## Evidence

### Lee Haesu 2026

Lee Haesu, Woo Young Keun, Park Kyue-nam, Kim Sujin. (2026). Online versus offline Fugl-Meyer lower extremity training for physical therapy students: a randomized study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09634-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects model on the randomized trial's repeated scoring-accuracy measures (44 students, five sessions). The article reports a significant main effect of time with "partial η2 = 0.16". Final-test accuracy was 71.47% ± 15.3% online and 64.45% ± 20.1% offline.

> "A linear mixed-effects model revealed a significant main effect of time, with both groups showing significant improvements in overall accuracy between the first practice session and the test session (F(1, 42) = 9.85, p = .003, partial η2 = 0.16)"
