`q2 i?` In simulated care with and without a co-developed checklist, anesthesiologists completed significantly more critical actions for preeclampsia and PPH when using the checklist. [→ Mahoney B 2021](#mahoney-b-2021)

## Evidence

### Mahoney B 2021

Mahoney B, Luebbert E. (2021). Updates on Simulation in Obstetrical Anesthesiology Through the COVID-19 Pandemic. Anesthesiology Clinics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anclin.2021.08.001

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Alexander and colleagues pilot tested a context-relevant safe anesthesia checklist for cesarean delivery in East Africa using simulated care with and without the checklist. The review reports "a significant increase in the completion of critical actions"; no effect size is printed.

> "By comparing anesthesiologists providing care for a variety of conditions in the simulated environment with and without a checklist developed in conjunction with East African health care professionals, they found a significant increase in the completion of critical actions in the setting of preeclampsia and PPH."
