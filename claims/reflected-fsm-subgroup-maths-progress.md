## Evidence

### Motteram et al. 2016

Gary Motteram, Sophina Choudry, Afroditi Kalambouka, Graeme Hutcheson and Amanda Barton. (2016). ReflectED: Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/public/files/Projects/EEF_Project_Protocol_ReflectED.pdf

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Subgroup analysis of the class-randomised trial restricted to free school meal eligible pupils (334 in intervention, 314 in control at baseline). The report states the subgroup estimate of two months' additional maths progress is "less secure" than the all-pupil finding; no subgroup effect size is printed in the quoted text.

> "On average, FSM-eligible pupils made two months' additional progress in maths, but it should be noted that this result is less secure than the findings for all pupils."
