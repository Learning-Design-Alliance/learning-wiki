Analysis of spring 2018 SBAC ELA and math scores for elementary students by ethnicity (Figures 3 and 4), adjusted for prior scores and demographics. The report states "it did not have a negative impact on any ethnic group"; no effect sizes are printed. Similar patterns were apparent in middle school grades.
