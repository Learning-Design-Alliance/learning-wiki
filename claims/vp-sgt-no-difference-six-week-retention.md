## Evidence

### Seifert Lukas B. 2019

Seifert Lukas B., Socolan Octavian, Sader Robert, Rüsseler Miriam, Sterz Jasmina. (2019). Virtual patients versus small-group teaching in the training of oral and maxillofacial surgery: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1887-1

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Six-week retention test embedded in a formative clerkship examination after the randomized intervention. "no significant difference (p = 0.56; d = 0.1) between the groups could be found"; the VP group scored 65.2 (SD = 7.6) and SGT 64.4 (SD = 7.3). Equivalence was not tested.

> "In the formative Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery examination 6 weeks after the intervention no significant difference (p = 0.56; d = 0.1) between the groups could be found. With 65.2 (SD = 7.6) points the VP group showed a good long-term knowledge retention."
