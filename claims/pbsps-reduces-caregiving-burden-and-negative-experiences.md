## Evidence

### Chien Wai Tong 2020

Chien Wai Tong, Bressington Daniel, Lubman Dan I., Karatzias Thanos. (2020). A Randomised Controlled Trial of a Caregiver-Facilitated Problem-Solving Based Self-Learning Program for Family Carers of People with Early Psychosis. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17249343

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise contrast tests following the GEE analysis in the RCT comparing PBSP with UPC and FPGP. The article reports these mean differences with p = 0.01 and 0.0005 at Time 2, and Table 3 prints the FBIS interaction effect as "Wald χ2 = 19.43, ES = 0.20".

> "the PBSP reported significant greater improvements in: Caregiving burden (FBIS) and experiences (ECI) at Time 2 (mean difference = 4.22 and 19.29, SE = 3.02 and 5.12, p = 0.01 and 0.0005, respectively)"
