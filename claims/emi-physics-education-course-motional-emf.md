## Evidence

### Michela Cavinato 2019

Michela Cavinato, Elia Giliberti and Marco Giliberti (2019). Conceptualization of Electromagnetic Induction at various Educational Levels: a Case Study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1910.04491](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1910.04491)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Qualitative observation from oral exams of five university students (four 1MDM, one 1MDP) who elaborated a didactical path on EMI; the authors explicitly flag this group must not be confused with the questionnaire respondents. The finding is a small-n case observation, not a controlled comparison.

> "these five students we examined – probably for having attended a course in physics education and having deepened their study on EMI, reading specialized literature and elaborating a didactical path for high school – show nearly no difficulties in dealing with motional electromotive force."
