### Pan Ruijun 2024

Pan Ruijun, Zhou Xueliang, Wu Chao, Zhang Luyang, Wang Jiayu, Zheng Minhua, Shi Ting, Cai Wei, Sun Jing. (2024). Impact of laparoscopic training course for surgical trainees based on an evidence-based pedagogical framework: randomized trial. BJS Open. https://doi.org/10.1093/bjsopen/zrae109

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Closed-book theoretical knowledge assessment in the randomized trial (n = 30 per group), analysed with an independent samples t-test. The pedagogical group mean was 41.83(6.66) versus 37.83(5.77), t = 2.49, P = 0.016; no effect size is printed.
