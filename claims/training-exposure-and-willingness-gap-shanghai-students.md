Descriptive results from the 430-student questionnaire survey showing a gap between training exposure, willingness to learn, and willingness to actually perform CPR.

> "A total of 88.1% (379/430) of students had participated in at least one CPR training session, 90.7% (390/430) expressed willingness to learn CPR and believed that regular training should be conducted, but only 68.4% (294/430) were willing to preform CPR"
