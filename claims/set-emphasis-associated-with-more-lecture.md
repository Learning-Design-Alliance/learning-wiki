# Greater emphasis on student evaluations of teaching is associated with more lecturing, especially where teaching assessment matters for review decisions

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Instructors who report teaching assessment is of large importance lecture less than those who report medium or small importance. [→ Apkarian 2021](#apkarian-2021)
`q2 i1` Among instructors for whom teaching assessment is of large importance, those for whom SET is weighted more than other measures lecture more than those for whom it is weighted less. [→ Apkarian 2021 (2)](#apkarian-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Apkarian 2021

Apkarian, N., Henderson, C., Stains, M., Raker, J., Johnson, E., & Dancy, M. (2021). What really impacts the use of active learning in undergraduate STEM education? Results from a national survey of chemistry, mathematics, and physics instructors. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0247544. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247544

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA among all survey instructors grouped by importance of overall teaching assessment for review and promotion; a small effect (η2 = 0.01) with those rating teaching assessment as large in importance lecturing less.

> "This is a main effect on lecture, F(2,2849) = 14.56, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.01 (small)."

### Apkarian 2021 (2)

Apkarian, N., Henderson, C., Stains, M., Raker, J., Johnson, E., & Dancy, M. (2021). What really impacts the use of active learning in undergraduate STEM education? Results from a national survey of chemistry, mathematics, and physics instructors. PLoS ONE 16(2): e0247544. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0247544

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

ANOVA restricted to the subset of instructors reporting teaching assessment is of large importance, comparing levels of relative SET weighting; a small effect (η2 = 0.01) with heavier SET weighting associated with more lecture.

> "B. Instructors for whom teaching assessment is of large importance, grouped by the relative weight of SET. This is a main effect on lecture, F(2,1078) = 5.63, p < 0.01, η2 = 0.01 (small)."
