## Evidence

### Xue Gang 2025

Xue Gang, Chen Siting, Xie JiaBing, Yang Min, Wang Zhengyu. (2025). Application of problem-based learning combined with three-dimensional visualization reconstruction technology in trauma orthopedics teaching and its impact on teaching satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08364-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Results section of the RCT, measured with the Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory for medical students (70–420 point range): "mean difference = 104.4, 95% CI: 95.1–113.7, P < 0.05". No standardized effect size is printed.

> "The total critical thinking score was also significantly higher in the experimental group (361.36 ± 11.46) compared to the control group (256.96 ± 8.89; mean difference = 104.4, 95% CI: 95.1–113.7, P < 0.05)."

### Xue Gang 2025 (2)

Xue Gang, Chen Siting, Xie JiaBing, Yang Min, Wang Zhengyu. (2025). Application of problem-based learning combined with three-dimensional visualization reconstruction technology in trauma orthopedics teaching and its impact on teaching satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08364-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same Results section reports per-dimension contrasts, e.g., truth-seeking 51.36 ± 1.81 vs. 38.28 ± 1.54 and analyticity 54.68 ± 1.52 vs. 39.36 ± 1.60, all significant at P < 0.05.

> "The experimental group exhibited significantly higher scores across all seven dimensions of the Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory compared to the control group (all P < 0.05)."
