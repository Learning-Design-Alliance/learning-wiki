Berwid Olga G. 2012¶

Berwid Olga G., Halperin Jeffrey M. (2012). Emerging Support for a Role of Exercise in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder Intervention Planning. Current Psychiatry Reports. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11920-012-0297-4

q3 · i? · review · r2

Nine-month daily afterschool physical activity program study by Kamijo et al. in preadolescent children using ERP measures. The intervention group showed a larger initial contingent negative variation at the frontal electrode site at post-test relative to the waitlist group, an effect absent at pre-test.

"Increases in cardiorespiratory fitness were found to be associated with improvements in the cognitive control of working memory as measured using a modified Sternberg task"
