Evidence¶

Charleen Brand 2023¶

Charleen Brand, Christian Hartmann, Katharina Loibl, Nikol Rummel. (2023). Do students learn more from failing alone or in groups? Insights into the effects of collaborative versus individual problem solving in productive failure. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09619-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mediation analysis (N = 135) with solution quality (best solution scored 0–4 on canonical components) as mediator. The indirect effect a2b2 = 0.24 had a confidence interval including zero.

"The bootstrap confidence interval of the indirect effect of the condition on conceptual knowledge, mediated through quality of solutions, did not suggest a mediation effect, a2b2 = 0.24, SE = 0.15, 95% CI [− 0.01, 0.59]."

Charleen Brand 2023 (2)¶

Charleen Brand, Christian Hartmann, Katharina Loibl, Nikol Rummel. (2023). Do students learn more from failing alone or in groups? Insights into the effects of collaborative versus individual problem solving in productive failure. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09619-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Path a2 from condition to solution quality was initially significant (p = 0.045) but not after Holm-Bonferroni adjustment (p' = 0.585); the b2 path (quality to conceptual knowledge, b2 = 0.65, p = 0.022, p' = 0.308) likewise did not hold.

"The effect of condition on quality of solution reached significance but failed to remain significant with adjusted p’-values, a2 = 0.36, SE = 0.18, t = 2.03, p = 0.045, p’ = 0.585, 95% CI [0.01, 7.19]."
