## Evidence

### Paul Justice 2019

Paul Justice, Emily Marshman, and Chandralekha Singh. (2019). Student understanding of Fermi energy, the Fermi-Dirac distribution and total electronic energy of a free electron gas. [doi:10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6404/ab537c)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Written pretest question 1b and question 2c responses. The article reports "The calculation of the total electronic energy in question 2c was extremely difficult for most students" because it cannot be done algorithmically; interviewed students gave incorrect integrals such as "𝐸𝑡𝑜𝑡 = ∫ 𝐷(𝜖)𝑑𝜖" and one said "I assume I should integrate but I'm not sure how to set it up."

> "In response to question 1b, some students stated that the total electronic energy would be the same regardless of the number of copper atoms."
