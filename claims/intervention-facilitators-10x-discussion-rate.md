Qualitative findings from facilitator interviews on the online training component. Intervention facilitators valued the scenario-based training, reporting that "The scenarios were exactly what my kids were going through" and helped them manage classroom situations.

> "The intervention group facilitators had a lot to say about their scenario-based training and participated in online discussion at 10 times the rate of their control group peers."
