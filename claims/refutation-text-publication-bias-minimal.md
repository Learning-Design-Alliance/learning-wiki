## Evidence

### Schroeder 2022

Noah L. Schroeder & Aurelia C. Kucera. (2022). Refutation Text Facilitates Learning: a Meta-Analysis of Between-Subjects Experiments. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09656-z

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Duval and Tweedie's trim-and-fill analysis within the same random-effects meta-analysis. Ten studies were imputed to the left of the mean, giving an adjusted effect the authors describe as "a modest change that does not change the overall interpretation" of the key conclusion.

> "However, 10 studies were adjusted to the left of the mean (Fig. 4), which would make the overall effect of refutation text compared to other conditions g = 0.28, 95% CI [0.16, 0.39]."
