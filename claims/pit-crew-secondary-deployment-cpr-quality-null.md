### Couper K 2018

Couper K, Velho RM, Quinn T, Devrell A, Lall R, Orriss B, Yeung J, Perkins GD. (2018). Training approaches for the deployment of a mechanical chest compression device: a randomised controlled manikin study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2017-019009

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary outcome analysis from the same randomised manikin trial (table 3: back plate 3.80 vs 3.82 s, P=0.971; upper part 9.99 vs 9.67 s, P=0.724; total pause 14.33 vs 13.56 s, P=0.572; whole-episode flow fraction 0.94 vs 0.94, P=0.790).
