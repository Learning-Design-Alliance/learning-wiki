## Evidence

### Esposito Vittoria 2018

Esposito Vittoria, Mazzon Giulia, Baiardi Paola, Torreggiani Massimo, Semeraro Luca, Catucci Davide, Colucci Marco, Mariotto Alice, Grosjean Fabrizio, Bovio Giacomo, Esposito Ciro. (2018). Safety and adequacy of percutaneous kidney biopsy performed by nephrology trainees. BMC Nephrology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12882-017-0796-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U comparison of median passes before and after 2012 in the 337-biopsy cohort; the study reports "a statistically significant reduction of the median number of passes" (p < 0.001). The non-diagnostic reduction (7 vs 2) is described as a trend and was not statistically significant (p = 0.170 in Table 2).

> "Our results show a trend towards a reduction of non diagnostic biopsies, 7 (4.1%) vs 2 (1.2%) before and after 2012 respectively, associated to a simultaneous statistically significant reduction of the median number of passes required to gain an adequate core (p < 0.001)."
