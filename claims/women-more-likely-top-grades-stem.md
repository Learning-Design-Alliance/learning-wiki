## Evidence

### Bloodhart 2020

Bloodhart, B., Balgopal, M. M., Casper, A. M. A., Sample McMeeking, L. B., & Fischer, E. V. (2020). Outperforming yet undervalued: Undergraduate women in STEM. PLoS ONE 15(6): e0234685. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0234685

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Generalized linear multilevel logistic model on whether students earned a top grade across the nine courses, controlling for classroom nesting. The article labels the gender effect "marginal" with OR = 1.48 and a confidence interval touching 1; only the logistic coefficient is printed, no standardized effect size.

> "Results indicated a marginal main effect of gender, with women being about 1.5 times more likely than men to earn an A or A+, B = 0.393, SE = 0.205, p = .056, OR = 1.48, 95% CI [0.99–2.21]."
