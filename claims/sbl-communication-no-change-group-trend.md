## Evidence

### Cuyul-Vásquez I 2026

Cuyul-Vásquez I, Aravena-Páez H, Araya-Quintanilla F, Guinez-Molinos S, Núñez-Cortés R, Gutiérrez Espinoza H. (2026). Collaborative Simulation-Based Learning to Develop Competencies in Physical Therapy Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85442

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on Communication Assessment Tool scores (standardized-patient rated): ηp²=0.001 for time, ηp²=0.002 for interaction, both nonsignificant. Descriptive changes were +0.04 (collaborative) and −0.01 (individual).

> "For CAT scores, the mixed ANOVA yielded a negligible main effect of time (F1,21=0.012, ηp²=0.001; P=.91), and the group × time interaction was nonsignificant (F1,21=0.046, ηp²=0.002; P=.83)."

### Cuyul-Vásquez I 2026 (2)

Cuyul-Vásquez I, Aravena-Páez H, Araya-Quintanilla F, Guinez-Molinos S, Núñez-Cortés R, Gutiérrez Espinoza H. (2026). Collaborative Simulation-Based Learning to Develop Competencies in Physical Therapy Students: Randomized Controlled Trial. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/85442

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Same ANOVA: between-group main effect on CAT with ηp²=0.157 and P=.06; the authors state this "strongly suggests a meaningful descriptive difference between the groups that cannot be conclusively confirmed or ruled out due to statistical underpowering."

> "However, the main effect of group was characterized by a large effect size (F1,21=3.906, ηp²=0.157; P=.06), despite missing the conventional threshold for statistical significance."
