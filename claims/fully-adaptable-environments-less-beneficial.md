## Evidence

### Chernikova 2025

Chernikova, O., Sommerhoff, D., Stadler, M., Holzberger, D., Nickl, M., Seidel, T., Kasneci, E., Küchemann, S., Kuhn, J., Fischer, F., & Heitzmann, N. (2025). Personalization through adaptivity or adaptability? A meta-analysis on simulation-based learning in higher education. Educational Research Review 46 (2025) 100662. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100662

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Interaction analysis of scaffolding-by-task-progression combinations across N = 143 studies (Table 2); the fully adaptable combination (adaptable scaffolding with adaptable task progression, N = 7) was the only combination significantly less beneficial than non-personalized environments.

> "fully adaptable learning environments (g = 0.71, SE = 0.20; N = 7) are significantly less beneficial (p < .05) than other combinations but also than non-personalized learning environments (g = 0.85, SE = 0.09; N = 82)"
