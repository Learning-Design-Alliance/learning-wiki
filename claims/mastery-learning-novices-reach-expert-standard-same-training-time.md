## Evidence

### Gustafsson A 2024

Gustafsson A, Rölfing JD, Palm H, Viberg B, Grimstrup S, Konge L. (2024). Setting proficiency standards for simulation-based mastery learning of short antegrade femoral nail osteosynthesis: a multicenter study. Acta Orthopaedica. https://doi.org/10.2340/17453674.2024.40812

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Second study part with a new cohort of 10 first-year novices told they had to achieve the pass/fail standard; all "were able to reach a pass/fail mastery standard of 96%" in 65 minutes (CI 46–84), versus 77 minutes (CI 59–95) for the plateau group.

> "However, all novices in the mastery learning group (n = 10) were able to reach a pass/fail mastery standard of 96%. The hands-on training time for the novices in the mastery learning group was 65 minutes (CI 46–84)."
