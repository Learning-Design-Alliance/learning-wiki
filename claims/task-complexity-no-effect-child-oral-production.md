One-way ANOVA on oral post-test scores across the three task-complexity groups found no significant group differences; equivalence was not tested. Oral means were 0.93, 0.75, and 0.83 for the three groups.

> "no significant differences (F=0.78, p=0.925>0.05) were found among the three groups in post-test of oral production. This indicated that task complexity had no significant effect on oral production of the target structure."
