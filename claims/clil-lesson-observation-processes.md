Maria Xanthou. (2011). The impact of CLIL on L2 vocabulary development and content knowledge. English Teaching: Practice and Critique, 10(4). http://education.waikato.ac.nz/research/files/etpc/files/2011v10n4art7.pdf

Qualitative observation of three video-taped CLIL science lessons. The article describes meaningful contexts for target words such as "deformation", teachers repeating and expanding students' answers, an oral gap-filling strategy, code-switching used "when necessary in order to facilitate knowledge retrieval", and restating conclusions in academic L2 language.
