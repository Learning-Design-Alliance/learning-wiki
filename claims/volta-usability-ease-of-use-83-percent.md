## Evidence

### Saleheen 2018

Saleheen, F., Wang, Z., Picone, J., Butz, B. P., and Won, C.-H. (2018). Efficacy of a Virtual Teaching Assistant in an Open Laboratory Environment for Electric Circuits. Advances in Engineering Education. http://www.temple.edu/csnap/volta.html

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Likert survey of six Cohort 3 experimental-group students covering twenty questions in seven categories. The article reports "83% of the students found VOL TA easy to use", 50% found it intuitive, and most students did not find the help module helpful, possibly because answers required navigating more than one page.

> "The responses from the category C questions (ease of use) show that 83% of the students found VOL TA easy to use and easy to navigate."
