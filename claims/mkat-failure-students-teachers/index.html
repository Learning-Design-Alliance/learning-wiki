Evidence¶

Amin et al. 2018¶

Bunga D. Amin, Helmi Abdullah and Jasruddin D. Malago. (2018). Sketch strategy of knowledge in physics learning and its influence on metacognitive. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3293

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Diagnostic MKAT test given to new physics education students at the start of the odd semester of 2014/2015, using a car-overtaking constant-velocity problem. The article reports that no student answered correctly; no effect size is printed.

"The results showed that none of the 26 new students that wrote the test answered the problems correctly. This indicates that the students have not been able to optimize their ability in cognition process."

Amin et al. 2018 (2)¶

Bunga D. Amin, Helmi Abdullah and Jasruddin D. Malago. (2018). Sketch strategy of knowledge in physics learning and its influence on metacognitive. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2017.3293

q2 · i? · causal · r1

MKAT test administered to 32 physics teachers attending a master degree matriculation program at Universitas Negeri Makassar at the beginning of the first semester of 2015/2016. Percentages are as printed; no effect size is reported.

"The results showed that eight teachers (21.9 %) answered the problems correctly, and the remaining 24 teachers (78.1%) failed to answer correctly."
