Vega Ochoa AD 2026¶

Vega Ochoa AD, Pinto Bautista HL, Weber Ipuana WJ, González Cuello MÁ, Díaz Pérez A. (2026). Student perceptions of technology-enhanced simulation resources in laparoscopic training: a scoping review and implications for surgical instrumentation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-10009-z

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis of the review's own included studies: of 11 studies included after screening 1,800 records, most learner-facing studies reported favorable responses. The review states favorable responses were "expressed through satisfaction, confidence, perceived safety, motivation, self-efficacy and willingness to engage in repeated practice." No pooled effect estimate was computed.

"most learner-facing studies that measured perception, satisfaction, acceptance, confidence or self-efficacy reported favorable responses to technology-enhanced simulation resources"
