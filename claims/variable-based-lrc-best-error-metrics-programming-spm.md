Comparative evaluation of four machine-learning models trained on questionnaire data from 295 students at Federal University, Oye-Ekiti (Table III). The variable-based LRC printed MAE of 0.1638, RMSE of 0.2386, RAE of 20.307% and RRSE of 24.8369%, described as "the least values in all these metrics".
