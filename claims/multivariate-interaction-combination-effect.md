## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Two-way 2 (condition) × 2 (time) multivariate ANOVAs with repeated measures found significant effects for time, condition, and their interaction in both Germany and the UK. [→ Mavrikis 2022](#mavrikis-2022)

## Evidence

### Mavrikis 2022

Mavrikis, M., Rummel, N., Wiedmann, M., Loibl, K., & Holmes, W. (2022). Combining exploratory learning with structured practice educational technologies to foster both conceptual and procedural fractions knowledge. Education Tech Research Dev, 70, 691–712. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10104-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus multivariate tests using Pillai's trace: Germany time × condition V = .109, F(2,148) = 9.068, p < .001; UK V = .144, F(2,118) = 7.604, p < .001, per Table 2.

> "Two-way, 2 (condition: ITS & ELE or ITS only) × 2 (time of measurement: pre-test or post-test) multivariate ANOVAs with repeated measures on the time variable and conceptual and procedural subscale scores as the two dependent measures were conducted for each country separately. We found significant effects for each factor and their interaction."
