### Hou Jinxiao 2025

Hou Jinxiao, An Furun, Qin Hui, Zhang Lulu, Wang Juan, Zhang Cui, Fan Dachuan. (2025). Application of DeepSeek-assisted problem-based learning in hematology residency training. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07852-x

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

End-of-course Likert survey item in the two-group trial (n = 30 per group); the DeepSeek-assisted group rated information accuracy and reliability significantly higher. Only means and a p-value were printed.

> "The DeepSeek-assisted PBL group expressed greater confidence in the accuracy and reliability of the medical information provided (4.23 ± 0.57 vs. 3.50 ± 0.51, p = 0.000)"

### Hou Jinxiao 2025 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`
