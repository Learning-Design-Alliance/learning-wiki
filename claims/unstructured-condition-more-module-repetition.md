## Evidence

### Stanton 1990

Stanton, N. A. and Stammers, R. B. (1990). A comparison of structured and unstructured navigation through a CBT package. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ414518

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A Mann-Whitney U test (P < 0.05) found that some unstructured-condition trainees repeated significantly more training and practice modules than structured-condition trainees, which the authors attribute to a more directive learning mode of actively testing assumptions and seeking information.

> "Some subjects in the unstructured condition also repeated significantly more training and practice modules (Mann-Whitney U, P < 0.05)."
