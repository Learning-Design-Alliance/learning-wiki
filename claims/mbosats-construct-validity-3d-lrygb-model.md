# MBOSATS scores on the 3D-printed LRYGB model differentiate surgical expertise levels, with scores decreasing and operation times increasing from experts to intermediates to residents

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Total MBOSATS scores decreased monotonically across expert (188.20 ± 1.48), intermediate (145.10 ± 11.45), and resident (64.10 ± 4.19) groups (P < 0.001), while total operation time increased (45.94, 78.88, 161.68 minutes). [→ Xia Jianfu 2025](#xia-jianfu-2025)

## Evidence

### Xia Jianfu 2025

Xia Jianfu, Xia Minjun, Zhu ZhiHao, Wu Junjie, Mao Jinglei, Peng Xiaoping, Yu Shijie, Zhou Menghui, Amadi Sidney Moses, Chen Feng, Zhao Fei, Maimaitiming Maimaitiaili, Aili Aikebaier, Yang Wah, Abudureyimu Kelimu, Wang Zhifei. (2025). Printed simulation models versus organ models in laparoscopic Roux-en-Y gastric bypass training: a prospective validation study of efficacy, learning curve, and cost-effectiveness. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03141-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Construct validity testing in which 5 experts, 5 intermediates and 5 residents performed LRYGB on the 3D models, recorded and scored by 2 experts using MBOSATS. Table 5 reports Total Score 188.20 ± 1.48 (experts), 145.10 ± 11.45 (intermediates), 64.10 ± 4.19 (residents), P < 0.001, and Total Time 45.94, 78.88, and 161.68 minutes.

> "the total surgical operation scores exhibited a decreasing trend, while the total operation time showed an increasing trend."

### Xia Jianfu 2025 (2)

Xia Jianfu, Xia Minjun, Zhu ZhiHao, Wu Junjie, Mao Jinglei, Peng Xiaoping, Yu Shijie, Zhou Menghui, Amadi Sidney Moses, Chen Feng, Zhao Fei, Maimaitiming Maimaitiaili, Aili Aikebaier, Yang Wah, Abudureyimu Kelimu, Wang Zhifei. (2025). Printed simulation models versus organ models in laparoscopic Roux-en-Y gastric bypass training: a prospective validation study of efficacy, learning curve, and cost-effectiveness. BMC Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12893-025-03141-y

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same construct validity study analyzed per procedural step. Post-hoc Games-Howell tests showed differences between expert vs intermediate and intermediate vs resident groups for steps 1–4 (e.g., step 1: 84.20 ± 0.97 vs 63.20 ± 3.88 vs 29.10 ± 2.48, P < 0.001); step 5 differences were not significant between experts and intermediates (P = 0.136).

> "The MBOSATS scores showed significant differences across steps 1 to 5 among the three groups."
