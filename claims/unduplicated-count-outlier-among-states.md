### Kaplan 2025

Kaplan, J. (2025). California's Local Control Funding Formula: Next steps toward equity. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/820.131

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Cross-state policy comparison in the section on California's counting method. The report adds that nearly 1 out of every 6 California students is categorized as both an English learner and at-risk but generates no additional funding, and that 18% of students were English learners, 85% of them socioeconomically disadvantaged.

> "In 2023–24, 37 of 42 states that provided funding to support students categorized as English learners and being at-risk allowed the funding to be duplicated for students who met both criteria. California is one of only five states that provides funding based on the unduplicated number of students in these groups."
