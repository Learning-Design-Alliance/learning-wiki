## Evidence

### Glick SB 2010

Glick SB, Fernandez L, Irby DM, Harleman E, Fernandez A. (2010). Teaching About Health Care Disparities in the Clinical Setting. J Gen Intern Med 25(Suppl 2):95–101. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-009-1203-6

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

This is the article's own argument in its introduction, supported by citations to prior work on faculty avoidance of feedback, not a study conducted by the authors. It states the tendency "may be heightened when discussing health care disparities," and that "Emotional reactions, such as shame or anger in teacher and learner, may limit teaching effectiveness."

> "Faculty often avoid giving feedback about behaviors or attitudes that suggest a lack of professionalism and this tendency may be heightened when discussing health care disparities, an emotionally charged subject."
