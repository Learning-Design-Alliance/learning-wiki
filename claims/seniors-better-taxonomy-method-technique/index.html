Independent samples t-tests comparing 69 juniors and 32 seniors (Table 2). Significant differences favored seniors on taxonomy and method-technique; efficacy belief (t = −0.10), outcome expectancy (t = 0.93), self-efficacy (t = 0.44), total TSMTDF (t = 0.27), and strategy (t = 1.61) showed no significant difference (ρ > 0.05).
