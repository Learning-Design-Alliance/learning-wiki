Deductive framework analysis of 88 included studies mapped inductively derived themes to the IPEC framework. The review reports that "Teams and Teamwork: This was the most frequently developed competency. It was identified in 65 instances across the studies". No effect size was reported for this frequency finding.

"Teams and Teamwork: This was the most frequently developed competency. It was identified in 65 instances across the studies and emphasised the ability of students to work collaboratively within diverse healthcare teams."
