## Evidence

### Molinari Giulia 2025

Molinari Giulia, Emiliani Nicolas, Cercenelli Laura, Bortolani Barbara, D'Azzeo Rossana, Burato Arianna, Presutti Livio, Molteni Gabriele, Marcelli Emanuela (2025). A Novel 3D Printed Multi‐Material Simulator for Endoscopic Stapes Surgery: The “3D Stapes Trainer”. The Laryngoscope 135(9): 3356–3363. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.32168

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative questionnaire (Figure 5) administered to the eight course participants after training: the model was rated most useful for "developing visuomotor coordination necessary to perform the endoscopic procedure", with usefulness rated higher by specialists than the youngest participants.

> "The 3D model was considered useful especially for developing visuomotor coordination necessary to perform the endoscopic procedure. The usefulness of the 3D model for endoscopic SS training was considered higher for the specialists than for the youngest."

### Molinari Giulia 2025 (2)

Molinari Giulia, Emiliani Nicolas, Cercenelli Laura, Bortolani Barbara, D'Azzeo Rossana, Burato Arianna, Presutti Livio, Molteni Gabriele, Marcelli Emanuela (2025). A Novel 3D Printed Multi‐Material Simulator for Endoscopic Stapes Surgery: The “3D Stapes Trainer”. The Laryngoscope 135(9): 3356–3363. https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.32168

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Questionnaire items comparing reproducibility of surgical steps on the 3D model versus cadaver: "stapedotomy hole creation and prosthesis placement" received the highest scores. Ratings are descriptive Likert means; no inferential statistics are printed.

> "Regarding the reproducibility of the surgical steps on the 3D model, in comparison to cadaver, the items that received the highest score were stapedotomy hole creation and prosthesis placement."
