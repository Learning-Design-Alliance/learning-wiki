## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries 2025

Alexandru Maries, Ryan Sayer, and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). Investigation of student and faculty problem solving: An example from quantum mechanics. Physics Education Research Conference. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2504.17487](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2504.17487)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-report question (Q4) answered by 43 MP and 22 QM students after Q3b. Answer choices A and B both stated a gut feeling with inability to prove it; 49% MP and 55% QM chose A, 33% MP and 32% QM chose B.

> "Table 2 shows that the vast majority of students in both MP (81%) and QM (86%) used their gut feeling when answering Q 3b because they did not know how to perform a calculation."
