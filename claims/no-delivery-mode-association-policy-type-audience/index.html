q2 · i? · associational · r1

Quantitative analysis of 40 policy documents using Fisher's exact tests due to small contingency table cell counts; the article reports "no significant association between delivery mode ... and policy type (P = .85) or policy audience (P = .71)." No effect size was printed.

"Fisher’s exact tests for association showed no significant association between delivery mode (“Any Online,” “Campus Only”) and policy type (P = .85) or policy audience (P = .71)."
