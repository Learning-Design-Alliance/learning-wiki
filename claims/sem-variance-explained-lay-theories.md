## Evidence

### Limeri Lisa B. 2023

Limeri Lisa B., Carter Nathan T., Lyra Franchesca, Martin Joel, Mastronardo Halle, Patel Jay, Dolan Erin L. (2023). Undergraduate Lay Theories of Abilities: Mindset, universality, and brilliance beliefs uniquely predict undergraduate educational outcomes. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.22-12-0250

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Across the series of SEMs in the phase-4 sample (n = 1396), the authors report the predictors "predicted 13–25% of the variance in the outcomes," with performance-avoid goals reaching only 4%. These R² values are reported as printed; no standardized effect size accompanies them.

> "Collectively, ULTrA second-order factors plus demographics predicted 13–25% of the variance in the outcomes, except for performance-avoid goals, which only reached 4% of the variance explained."
