## Evidence

### Qu Meng 2024

Qu Meng, Hou Qinlong, Li Xushen, Yu Chunyan, Xia Jichen, Dong Zhiheng. (2024). Application of a flipped classroom incorporating modified team-based learning in molecular biology laboratory teaching: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06450-7

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Randomized comparison of 109 third-year clinical medicine students (LBL n = 54, FC-TBL n = 55) with equivalent baseline GPA (P = 0.585). The figure-reported t-test shows the FC-TBL group's scores were "significantly different" and higher, with "Cohen's d = 1.134"; LBL averaged 85.89 ± 4.26 versus 90.27 ± 3.51 for FC-TBL.

> "The scores of the two groups of students were significantly different using independent samples t-test ( t = 5.866,P < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.134)"
