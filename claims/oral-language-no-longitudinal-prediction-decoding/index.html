Evidence¶

Jessica Massonnié 2018¶

Jessica Massonnié, Maryse Bianco, Laurent Lima, Pascal Bressoux. (2018). Longitudinal predictors of reading comprehension in French at first grade: Unpacking the oral comprehension component of the simple view. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.005

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the longitudinal SEM with 556 French first-graders, adding paths from T1 LLL and discourse skills to T2 decoding skills resulted in no convergence; an alternative single oral language factor model had lower fit and revealed a negative prediction, so the authors concluded there was "no significant and positive prediction from oral language skills to decoding skills".

"However, they indicate that, in the present data, there was no significant and positive prediction from oral language skills to decoding skills, during the course of first grade, for French pupils."
