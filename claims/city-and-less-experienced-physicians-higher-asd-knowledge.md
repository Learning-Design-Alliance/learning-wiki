## Evidence

### Domarecki Patryk 2025

Domarecki Patryk, Plata-Nazar Katarzyna, Sohl Kristin. (2025). An Assessment of the Knowledge of Autism Spectrum Disorder Among Polish Primary Care Physicians. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina61040761

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey comparisons across settlement size (76 small-town vs. 90 city physicians) and experience (116 vs. 50 physicians). City physicians scored higher except in etiology; less-experienced physicians scored higher ("10.63 vs. 9.10, p < 0001" for general knowledge).

> "Physicians practicing in more densely populated areas (with more than 100,000 residents) present a higher level of knowledge in all categories but etiology (9.76 vs. 10.53, p = 0.02; 2.75 vs. 3.20, p = 0.003; 4.68 vs. 5.07, p = 0.046 for general, diagnosis, and support, respectively)."
