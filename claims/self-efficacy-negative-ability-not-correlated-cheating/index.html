The article reports the meta-analytic finding of Krou et al. (2021) second-hand: "self-efficacy negatively correlates with cheating" but ability does not. No correlation coefficient is printed, so no magnitude is reported.

"In their meta-analysis, Krou et al. (2021) found that, while self-efficacy negatively correlates with cheating, actual ability does not inversely correlate with dishonesty."
