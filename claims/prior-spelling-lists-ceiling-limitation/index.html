Evidence¶

Starch 1910¶

Starch, D. (1910). The Measurement of Efficiency in Spelling, and the Overlapping of Grades in Combined Measurements of Reading, Writing and Spelling. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=The+Measurement+of+Efficiency+in+Spelling

q1 · i? · design · r2

Methodological critique of earlier test construction, citing Buckingham's published distribution curves as evidence that the words are too easy for better pupils even as far down as the fourth grade. The author also notes the small Ayres lists make individual scores unreliable and prevent measuring grade-to-grade progress.

"In the upper grades more and more of the pupils can spell correctly all the words in the list until in the eighth grade more than half of the pupils can spell all of the words correctly."
