### Jowsey Tanisha 2019

Jowsey Tanisha, Beaver Peter, Long Jennifer, Civil Ian, Garden A L, Henderson Kaylene, Merry Alan, Skilton Carmen, Torrie Jane, Weller Jennifer. (2019). Towards a safer culture: implementing multidisciplinary simulation-based team training in New Zealand operating theatres - a framework analysis. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-027122

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Framework analysis of 31 interviews with staff from five NZ hospitals implementing NetworkZ in Cohort 1. Participants reported capacity to implement depended on management backing: 'DHB senior and middle management supported the programme', enabling scheduled training days and freed theatre time.

> "DHB staff were mostly positive about their capacity to implement NetworkZ when DHB senior and middle management supported the programme."
