Vera Montalbano, Sonia Quattrini. (2011). MEASURES OF RADIOACTIVITY: A TOOL FOR UNDERSTANDING STATISTICAL DATA ANALYSIS. FFP12 2011, Udine 21-23 November 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1201.0553](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1201.0553)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Section 2 describes the authors' observation during the laboratory phase, in which small student groups measured alpha, beta and gamma emission from weak uranium sources. This is an interpretive report of student engagement, with no measured outcome or effect size printed.

> "During measurements, it rose soon the necessity of performing many measures and the problem of valuating uncertainties. The students were ready to recall all their knowledge on statistics for facing this problem."
