## Evidence

### Lin 2013

Lin, Ching-Ying & Hsu, Wei Shu. (2013). Effects of Hierarchy Vocabulary Exercises on English Vocabulary Acquisition. English Language Teaching, 6(9). [doi:10.5539/elt.v6n9p110](https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n9p110)

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Reading comprehension test (multiple-choice format) administered after the treatment with 56 Grade 5 EFL students. Independent t-test showed the hierarchy-exercise group scored significantly higher, with the article reporting "The effect size was defined as being large (d = 1.35)".

> "First, the mean of the experimental group (M = 86.67, SD = 15.44) was higher than that of the control group (M = 60.17, SD = 23.13). A significant difference was found between the two groups (t (49.111) = 5.073, p < .05). The effect size was defined as being large (d = 1.35)."
