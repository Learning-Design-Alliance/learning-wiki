Evidence¶

Diamond Adele 2012¶

Diamond Adele. (2012). Activities and Programs That Improve Children's Executive Functions. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721412453722

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis across EF-intervention studies (citing C. L. Davis et al., 2011; Diamond et al., 2007; Manjunath & Telles, 2001); the review states differences often emerge only when the limits of children's EF skills are pushed. No effect sizes are printed.

"In studies of EF-enhancing activities, the largest differences between intervention groups and controls are consistently found on the most demanding EF tasks and task conditions."
