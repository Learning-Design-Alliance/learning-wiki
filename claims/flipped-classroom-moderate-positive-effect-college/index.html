Evidence¶

Zhang 2021¶

Zhang, Q., Cheung, E.S.T., & Cheung, C.S.T. (2021). The impact of flipped classroom on college students’ academic performance: A meta-analysis based on 20 experimental studies. Science Insights Education Frontiers, 8(2):1059-1080. https://doi.org/10.15354/sief.21.re019

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 20 experimental/quasi-experimental studies (28 datasets) of flipped classroom in higher education, analyzed with a random-effects model in CMA3.0. The printed effect size is SMD = 0.66, which the authors classify as a moderate effect by Cohen's criteria.

"According to Table 1, the combined effect size SMD of the included study was 0.66. According to Cohen’s statistical theory of effect size, the study produced a moderate effect."
