The review's quality assessment of the 24 included studies, which varied in sample sizes between 12 and 216 and used a wide range of measures. The authors state "There is great heterogeneity, many studies are underpowered, and measuring effects of Mindfulness in this setting is challenging."

> "However, the diversity of study samples, variety in implementation and exercises, and wide range of instruments used require a careful and differentiated examination of data. There is great heterogeneity, many studies are underpowered, and measuring effects of Mindfulness in this setting is challenging."
