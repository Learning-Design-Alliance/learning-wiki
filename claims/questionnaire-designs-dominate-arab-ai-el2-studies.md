`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's results section reports the distribution of research designs across the 11 included studies: surveys dominated, with some mixed-method designs and one experimental pre-test/post-test design (Jasim, 2025). Sample sizes ranged from 20 (Jomaa & Jibroo, 2023) to 350 (Othman, 2025).

> "Questionnaire -based surveys were the most common approach (Alsalem, 2024; Kheder, 2025; Othman, 2025; Mudhsh et al., 2025), reflecting researchers’ interest in examining students’ and teachers’ perceptions of AI integration."
