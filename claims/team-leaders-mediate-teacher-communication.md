## Evidence

### N. Kozhevnikova 2022

N. Kozhevnikova. (2022). How does online teamwork change student communication patterns in programming courses? A PREPRINT. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Community extraction on the blended course communication network showed communities nearly matching student teams, with a hierarchical structure of teacher contact routed through one or two leaders per team.

> "Students communicated using some hierarchy, where one or two leaders talked to the teacher and then discussed information inside their team (Figure 5)."

### N. Kozhevnikova 2022 (2)

N. Kozhevnikova. (2022). How does online teamwork change student communication patterns in programming courses? A PREPRINT. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2204.04244)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, offered as an assumption rather than a tested result: students with low self-regulation skills may rely on peers with highly developed self-regulation skills.

> "Thus, they receive all the information from their team leader reducing the number of messages sent to the teacher inquiring about similar problems."
