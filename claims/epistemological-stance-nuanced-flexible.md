## Evidence

### Gupta 2010

Gupta, A., & Elby, A. (2010). Beyond epistemological deficits: Incorporating flexible epistemological views into fine-grained cognitive dynamics. Originally published 2008. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1003.0461](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1003.0461)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Jim's interview utterance shows he treated the equation as "fact-bearing" law applying to every situation, choosing it over perception. Elsewhere he said "I think there is some way that just completely links the two together".

> "For an equation to be given to you it has to be like theory and it has to be fact-bearing. So, fact applies for everything. It is like a law. It applies to every single situation you could be in."
