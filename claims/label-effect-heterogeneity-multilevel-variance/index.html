Evidence¶

Franz 2023¶

Franz, D. J., Richter, T., Lenhard, W., Marx, P., Stein, R., & Ratz, C. (2023). The Influence of Diagnostic Labels on the Evaluation of Students: a Multilevel Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09716-6

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Heterogeneity analysis within the same multilevel meta-analysis of 284 effects. The authors report "considerable variability of effect sizes between studies and effects" and estimate that 7.15% of variance is sampling variance, 45.59% between effects within studies, and 47.26% between studies.

"The overall test of heterogeneity was significant, Q(284) = 2,307.87, p < .001, indicating considerable variability of effect sizes between studies and effects."
