q2 · i? · causal · r?

Post-survey agent ratings (competence, warmth, helpfulness, trustworthiness, 1–5 Likert) in Experiment-1; the authors interpret the asymmetry as trust calibrated to the observability of errors rather than their severity, an interpretation rather than a tested result.

"participants rated Alice (strong concepts, arithmetic errors) more harshly than Charlie (accurate arithmetic, conceptual errors) on nearly every dimension, despite both being designed as error-prone peers."
