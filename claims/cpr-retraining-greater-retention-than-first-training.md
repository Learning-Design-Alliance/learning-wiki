# After retraining nine months after first training, theoretical knowledge and practical skills declined less over nine months than after first training among nursing professionals, in a single-group pre-post study

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The percentage reduction in theoretical knowledge over nine months was smaller after retraining (13.0%) than after the first training (18.2%), p < 0.01, and the reduction in practical skills was likewise smaller after retraining (5.3% vs 7.6%), p < 0.01. [→ de Araujo Nyagra Ribeiro R 2022](#de-araujo-nyagra-ribeiro-r-2022)

## Evidence

### de Araujo Nyagra Ribeiro R 2022

de Araujo Nyagra Ribeiro R, de Araújo Raul Amaral H, Moretti Miguel Antonio A, Chagas Antonio Carlos Palandri C. (2022). Nursing training and retraining on cardiopulmonary resuscitation: a theoretical-practical intervention. Revista da Escola de Enfermagem da USP. https://doi.org/10.1590/1980-220X-REEUSP-2021-0521

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Prospective intervention study of 56 nursing professionals at a single Brazilian hospital, comparing the linear trend of percentage reduction in knowledge and skills after the first training versus after retraining, analyzed with the Wilcoxon test. The article reports "a reduction of 18.2% in knowledge in the theoretical test after T1 vs 13.0% after T2 (p < 0.01)" and 7.6% vs 5.3% for skills.

> "There was a reduction of 18.2% in knowledge in the theoretical test after T1 vs 13.0% after T2 (p < 0.01) and a reduction of 7.6% in skills on the practical test after T1 vs 5.3% after T2 (p < 0.01)."
