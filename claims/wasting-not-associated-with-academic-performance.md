## Evidence

### Asmare Biachew 2018

Asmare Biachew, Taddele Mekuanint, Berihun Sileshi, Wagnew Fasil. (2018). Nutritional status and correlation with academic performance among primary school children, northwest Ethiopia. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3909-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null finding from the same multivariable logistic regression of the 436-child cross-sectional sample: the printed AOR for wasted children was 0.80 (95% CI 0.54–1.2) with p = 0.06, not reaching significance. The article attributes this to wasting being acute, temporary malnutrition. Equivalence was not tested.
