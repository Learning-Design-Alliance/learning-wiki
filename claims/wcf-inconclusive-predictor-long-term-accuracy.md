## Evidence

### Albatool Abalkheel & Tara Brandenburg 2020

Albatool Abalkheel & Tara Brandenburg. (2020). Effects of Written Corrective Feedback: A Synthesis of 10 Quasi-Experimental Studies. English Language Teaching, 13(7). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v13n7p97

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of ten quasi-experimental studies of written corrective feedback, using delayed post-test scores converted to Cohen's d. The synthesis reports "in about 55% the cases, CF has a positive effect on students’ long-term learning", with two neutral, one mixed and one negative study.

> "With five studies clearly demonstrating a large positive ef fect size, two neutral, one mixed and one negative, it appears that in about 55% the cases, CF has a positive effect on students’ long-term learning."
