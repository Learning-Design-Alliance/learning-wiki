### Lucile Favero 2026

Lucile Favero, Juan Antonio Pérez-Ortiz, Tanja Käser, Nuria Oliver. (2026). From Substitution to Scaffolding: Breaking the Self-Reinforcing Harm Cycle of AI in Education (and Beyond). arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17451 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.17451](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.17451)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Descriptive coding of the 49-essay corpus found the AI students recognize as better for learning is not the AI they typically encounter: "replacement,not scaffolding, was their near-universal frame (48 of 49)". A minority of 8 of 49 essays described AI that deepens thinking.

> "Yet this is what students want and need, not what they encounter:replacement,not scaffolding, was their near-universal frame (48 of 49)."
