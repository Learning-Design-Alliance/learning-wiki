Related Claims¶

  • Student satisfaction with CBL, PBL and TBL in public health education was high, reported in 21 of 22 studies (95.4%) — related
  • The review finds flipped learning, PBL, gamification, and collaborative learning adapted to digital environments produced positive effects on motivation, participation, and transversal competences — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Virtual environments favor clinical reasoning, self-regulation and peer collaboration, while physical environments engage critical reasoning, professionalism and communication abilities — related
  • Innovative andragogical strategies — simulation, narrative medicine, reflective writing — enhance empathy, compassion, and communication skills in medical students — related
  • The closer training content is to authentic clinical scenarios, the smoother its translation into practice — related
  • Experiential learning in authentic clinical environments (community engagement, home visits, outreach rotations) was reported to produce the greatest positive impact on student attitudes, confidence, and perceived competence, but is the most resource-intensive — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • A scoping review identified 11 studies (2010–2025) characterizing learning environments for teaching Pharmaceutical Care, predominantly conducted in the United States — related
  • First-year medical students in the single first-year-focused study reported high engagement (97%) and perceived improved teamwork (87%) with PBL — related
  • Systematic review finds PBL improves clinical reasoning in OB/GYN education — related
  • TBL improves ethical reasoning and moral sensitivity — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • TBL shows favorable outcomes for clinical performance, skills and learner engagement — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
