The typewriting study determined that under the law of habit early associations "offer a certain resistance to the new ways of writing," and that leaving old habits behind becomes progressively harder as advancement proceeds, increasing learners' need for a skilled teacher.

> "The associations used in the early writing offer a certain resistance to the new ways of writing which are being or may be developed. They strongly tend to persist long after more economic habits have been developed, a tendency which requires constant effort to overcome."
