## Evidence

### Hsieh 2012

Hsieh, P.-H., & Chen, N.-S. (2012). Effects of reflective thinking in the process of designing software on students' learning performances. Computers & Education, 58(1), 419-431.

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-Samples T-Test on Compeau and Higgins (1995) Computer Self-Efficacy Questionnaire (10 Yes/No questions, 1-10 confidence scale), completed before and after the course by 12 persons (7 experimental, 5 control). All rows not significant.

> "the students in both the control and the experimental groups scored lower for the post-test than for the pre -test, This result  shows that the students' degree of self-confidence for using packaged software declined by  the end of the course."
