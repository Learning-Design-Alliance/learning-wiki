## Evidence

### Paul Stonaha 2024

Paul Stonaha, Stephanie T. Douglas. (2024). Efficacy of a hybrid take home and in class summative assessment for the postsecondary physics classroom. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.1088/1361-6552/ad7fc0](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6552/ad7fc0)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental survey comparison of two cohorts in one calculus-based Physics II course (control NC=31, experimental NE=59), responses aggregated over three tests. Mann-Whitney U test on Q10 gave rMW = 0.236, pMW = 0.019; means were 3.05 (control) vs 2.41 (experimental). The article reports "significant (pMW = 0.019)".

> "The experimental cohort found completing the exam to be less stressful than a typical college exam (Q10), while the control group felt completing the exams induced typical degree of stress.  The difference between the two cohorts’ responses to question Q10 is significant (pMW = 0.019)."
