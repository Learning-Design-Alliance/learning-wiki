## Evidence

### Boal Matthew W. E. 2024

Boal Matthew W. E., Afzal Asma, Gorard Jack, Shah Aishwarya, Tesfai Freweini, Ghamrawi Walaa, Tutton Matthew, Ahmad Jawad, Selvasekar Chelliah, Khan Jim, Francis Nader K. (2024). Development and evaluation of a societal core robotic surgery accreditation curriculum for the UK. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-024-02062-x

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post paired comparison within the society-led pre-clinical curriculum across seven courses, assessed by trained expert raters. The paired t-test showed "a mean difference of 18.72" in dry M-GEARS scores from attempt 1 to attempt 2, significant at p < 0.001.

> "Participants’ dry M-GEARS scores (n = 47) for all four tasks demonstrated a mean difference of 18.72 (SD 20.38, 95% CI 12.74–24.71, p < 0.001)"
