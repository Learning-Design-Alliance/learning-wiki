## Evidence

### Christian Hayley E. 2020

Christian Hayley E., Cross Donna, Rosenberg Michael, Schipperijn Jasper, Shilton Trevor, Trapp Georgina, Trost Stewart G., Nathan Andrea, Maitland Clover, Thornton Ashleigh, Wenden Elizabeth J., George Phoebe (2020). Development of physical activity policy and implementation strategies for early childhood education and care settings using the Delphi process. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-020-01034-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Round two of the national online Delphi survey (123 experts with complete data) asked whether having a physical activity policy was a responsibility of ECEC services. "89% of respondents believed it was definitely the responsibility" and 11% somewhat.

> "Overall, 89% of respondents believed it was definitely the responsibility and 11% somewhat the responsibility of ECEC services to have a physical activity policy."

### Christian Hayley E. 2020 (2)

Christian Hayley E., Cross Donna, Rosenberg Michael, Schipperijn Jasper, Shilton Trevor, Trapp Georgina, Trost Stewart G., Nathan Andrea, Maitland Clover, Thornton Ashleigh, Wenden Elizabeth J., George Phoebe (2020). Development of physical activity policy and implementation strategies for early childhood education and care settings using the Delphi process. The International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-020-01034-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Round two Delphi survey items rated agreement with candidate policy features on a 5-point scale, with consensus set at 70% agreement or more. Agreement ranged from "a rationale (92% agreement)" to "links to legislation (85% agreement)" across the components listed.

> "Consensus was reached that an ECEC specific physical activity policy should include a rationale (92% agreement), scope (91% agreement), key statements (93% agreement), key recommendations (91% agreement), links to legislation (85% agreement)"
