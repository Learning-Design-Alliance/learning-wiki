`q4 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Diagnosis-specific linear regressions in the nationwide cohort, shown in Figure 2. The printed standardized differences were −0.74 for substance use disorders and 0.06 for eating disorders; "the largest school achievement difference was observed for substance use disorders".

> "The largest school achievement difference was observed for substance use disorders, −0.74 [95% CI, −0.76 to −0.72], while the smallest was seen in those with eating disorders, 0.06 [95% CI, 0.04–0.08]."
