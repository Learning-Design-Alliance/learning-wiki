## Evidence

### Angrist N 2020

Angrist N, Bergman P, Evans DK, Hares S, Jukes MCH, Letsomo T. (2020). Practical lessons for phone-based assessments of learning. BMJ Global Health. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2020-003030

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparative pilot analysis in Botswana comparing phone-based numeracy results with face-to-face ASER data from the same regions and largely the same schools and grades (figure 1). The authors report "a similar skill level" across samples and responses "cover the entire distribution of skills"; they state this "is not yet a formal validity assessment".

> "A sample of students from the phone-based assessment of numeracy reflects a similar skill level to the sample of students from the more comprehensive ASER assessment, administered face-to-face before schools shut down"
