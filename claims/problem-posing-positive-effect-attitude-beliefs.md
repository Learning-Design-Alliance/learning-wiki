## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Across six weighted effect sizes, problem posing interventions had a large positive effect on attitude-belief outcomes, with a mean Hedges' g of 0.76 (95% CI 0.59-0.93). [→ Roslinda Rosli 2014](#roslinda-rosli-2014)

## Evidence

### Roslinda Rosli 2014

Roslinda Rosli, Mary Margaret Capraro & Robert M. Capraro. (2014). The Effects of Problem Posing on Student Mathematical Learning: A Meta-Analysis. International Education Studies, 7(13). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/ies.v7n13p227

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of six attitude-belief weighted effect sizes from experimental studies of problem posing interventions. Table 3 reports a mean effect size of .76 with a standard error of .09 and a 95% CI of .59 to .93; all four hypothesized moderators were statistically significant (p < .001).

> "The educational setting, research design, publication source, and presentation type had significant effects statistically (p < .001) on student attitude and beliefs toward mathematics."
