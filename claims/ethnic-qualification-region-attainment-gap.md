### Mehdizadeh Leila 2015

Mehdizadeh Leila, Sturrock Alison, Dacre Jane. (2015). Are the General Medical Council’s Tests of Competence fair to long standing doctors? A retrospective cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0362-x

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Regression and correlational analyses across both cohorts. Investigated international medical graduates scored significantly worse on the knowledge test than UK graduates (B = −9.78, p = 0.001, Table 4); similarly, white UK volunteer graduates outperformed non-white overseas trained peers on the OSCE overall.

> "In the fitness to practise group, white doctors, who were a minority, performed significantly better in the OSCE overall than non-white doctors."
