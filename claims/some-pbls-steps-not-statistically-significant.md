### Kocyigit S 2026

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Tests on individual checklist items in the ten-caregiver pilot. The article attributes the non-significant results to the small sample size while noting a moderate effect size.

> "Although certain steps, such as "calling for help" and "looking for clear signs of life," did not reach statistical significance, post-training mean scores increased with a moderate effect size (Cohen's d > 0.4), suggesting a potential training effect limited by the small sample size."
