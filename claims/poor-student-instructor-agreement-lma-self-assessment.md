## Evidence

### Laiou Elpiniki 2011

Laiou Elpiniki, Clutton-Brock Thomas H, Lilford Richard J, Taylor Celia A. (2011). The effects of laryngeal mask airway passage simulation training on the acquisition of undergraduate clinical skills: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-11-57

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Self-assessment questionnaire data compared with instructor ratings on the first four placements; the correlation between instructor and participant overall success ratings was reported as moderate (Spearman's rho analysis), with participants tending to underrate their first two placements.

> "The agreement between individual participants and their instructors in terms of whether they had achieved an overall LMA placement success score of < 3, 3 or > 3 was poor during the first two assessments and fair during the third and fourth assessments."
