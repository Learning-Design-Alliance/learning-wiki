## Evidence

### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2020

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Laura Ríos, and H. J. Lewandowski (2020). Preliminary model for student ownership of projects. Originally published 2019. [doi:10.1119/perc.2019.pr.dounas-frazer](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2019.pr.dounas-frazer)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview excerpts from students Lance and Jordyn, coded during the study's consensus coding rounds. Execution-phase ownership conceptions were "co-coded with all three interaction categories"; Lance's excerpt concerned intellectual contributions to troubleshooting, and Jordyn's connected ownership to developing depth of understanding.

> "EXECUTION was co-coded with all three interaction categories, as evidenced by Lance and Jordyn. When recalling a time when he felt ownership of a project, Lance described his intellectual CONTRIBUTIONS to the troubleshooting process"
