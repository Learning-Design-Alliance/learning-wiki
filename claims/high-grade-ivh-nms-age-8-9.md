`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Adjusted logistic regression in the cohort at age 8 to 9 years showed high-grade IVH children were "less likely to meet the NMS at age 8 to 9 years compared with preterm controls (AOR, 0.47; 95% CI, 0.29-0.78)"; most (49 [57.7%]) met the standard. No standardized effect size was printed beyond the odds ratio reported directionally.

> "Children with high-grade IVH were also less likely to meet the NMS at age 8 to 9 years compared with preterm controls (AOR, 0.47; 95% CI, 0.29-0.78); however, most of the children with high-grade IVH (49 [57.7%]) did meet the NMS"
