### Kalfsvel L 2022

Kalfsvel L, Versmissen J, van Doorn A, van den Broek W, van der Kuy H, van Rosse F. (2022). Better performance of medical students on pharmacotherapy knowledge and skills tests is associated with practising with e‐learning program P‐scribe. Br J Clin Pharmacol. https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.15077

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Retrospective analysis of P-scribe use for the skill-based prescription test completed by 255 students. The article reports "five more e‐learning modules" for first-time passers, statistically significant at P = .006; no standardised effect size is printed.

> "Students who passed their skill‐based prescription test first time, practised on average five more e‐learning modules (P = .006; 95% CI of difference [1.4–8.3]) than students who failed their first attempt."
