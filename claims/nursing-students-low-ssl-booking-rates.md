### Phehla 2024

Phehla, L.B., Makhene, A. & Matshaka, L., 2024, 'Student nurses' utilisation of self-directed simulation learning at a University in Gauteng,' Curationis 47(1), a2490. https://doi.org/10.4102/curationis.v47i1.2490

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Descriptive comparison from the introduction, drawn from records of the multidisciplinary clinical simulation laboratory. Records showed "only 21 out of 168 (12.5%) undergraduate student nurses booked and used the facility for SSL in the year 2019", against 80% of emergency medical care students; 55 of 58 (95%) complementary medicine students also booked.

> "only 21 out of 168 (12.5%) undergraduate student nurses booked and used the facility for SSL in the year 2019, while 89 out of 111 (80%) undergraduate emergency medical care students booked and used the facility for SSL in the same year."
