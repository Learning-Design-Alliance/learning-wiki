### What Works Clearinghouse 2015

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2015). Head Start: WWC intervention report. http://www.acf.hhs.gov/programs/ohs

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

The WWC report summarizes the DHHS ACF (2010) randomized controlled trial, in which children were randomly assigned to be offered Head Start or a comparison group. The average improvement index for general reading achievement was +13 percentile points (range +12 to +14), and the WWC confirmed a "statistically significant and positive effect of Head Start" on PELS ratings.

> "The authors reported, and the WWC confirmed, a statistically significant and positive effect of Head Start on children’s PELS ratings for both the 3-year-old and 4-year-old cohorts at the end of the intervention year."
