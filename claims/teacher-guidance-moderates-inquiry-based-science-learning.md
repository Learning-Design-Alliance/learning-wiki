## Evidence

### Müller 2018

Müller, A. (2018). (Science) Teacher Education in the Knowledge Society: Good Practice at the University of Geneva. University of Geneva. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1807.00974](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1807.00974)

`q2 · i2` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports, citing the Furtak et al. meta-analysis of inquiry-based science teaching, that "effect sizes are more than twice as large with guidance than without" (d = 0.65 vs d = 0.25). The review presents this as evidence that unguided inquiry is not an effective approach for science learning.

> "a meta-analysis on IBL in science by Furtak et al. [39] has shown that teacher guidance is an decisive moderator: effect sizes are more than twice as large with guidance than without ( d = 0.65 and d = 0.25, respectively)."
