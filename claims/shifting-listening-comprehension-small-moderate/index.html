q2 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Qualitative synthesis of three shifting-listening comprehension studies: two using the Multiple Classification Card Sorting Test with the WJ Oral Comprehension subtest produced effect sizes "varying from small (r = .12) to moderate (r = .45)"; a third (Roberts et al., 2015) showed a small association.

"Two studies (Barber et al., 2020, 2021) using the Multiple Classification Card Sorting Test (general and reading specific) and the WJ Oral Comprehension subtest generate effect sizes varying from small (r = .12) to moderate (r = .45), indicating that other factors (e.g., scoring criteria) may modulate the observed relationships."
