Chien 2025¶

Chien, P.Y.‐H., Hosseinpour, S., Peters, O.A. & Peters, C.I. (2025). Outcomes of root canal treatment performed by undergraduate students: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. International Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14246

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta‐analytic pooling across the 11 included studies displayed in Figure 2, reporting the overall effect as "a significantly higher likelihood of a successful treatment outcome" with the printed confidence interval; no standardized effect size is given.

"The overall pooled risk difference is 0.43 [95% CI: 0.40, 0.46], indicating a significantly higher likelihood of a successful treatment outcome."

Chien 2025 (2)¶

Chien, P.Y.‐H., Hosseinpour, S., Peters, O.A. & Peters, C.I. (2025). Outcomes of root canal treatment performed by undergraduate students: A systematic review and meta‐analysis. International Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14246

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Heterogeneity analysis of the same forest plot (Figure 2), with the review attributing variability to "differences in methodology, patient factors or treatment protocols". Despite this, the overall effect was significant (Z = 30.91, p < .00001).

"The forest plot reveals considerable heterogeneity (Chi2 = 135.30, p < .00001; I 2 = 93%), suggesting variability in study outcomes, possibly influenced by differences in methodology, patient factors or treatment protocols."
