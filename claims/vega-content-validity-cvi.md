## Evidence

### Shin Sujin 2026

Shin Sujin, Choi Jaehwa, Hong Eunmin, Lee Miji, Go Jinseon, Yu Subin, Lee Minjae. (2026). Development of vignette-based evaluation with generative artificial intelligence (VEGA) for nursing students: a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09685-8

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Content validity evaluation by six nursing faculty and clinical nurses using an eight-item 4-point survey. The article reports "Item-level CVIs (I-CVIs) ranged from 0.83 to 1.00, and the scale-level CVI (S-CVI/Ave) was 0.96." Expert suggestions, such as excluding diagnostic labels, were incorporated.

> "Item-level CVIs (I-CVIs) ranged from 0.83 to 1.00, and the scale-level CVI (S-CVI/Ave) was 0.96, both meeting or exceeding the recommended thresholds for a panel of six experts [20], indicating acceptable content validity of the educational content provided by the app."
