## Evidence

### Ivory 2026

Ivory, M., Finnerty, S., Dunn, K., Philpot, R., & Warmelink, L. (2026). The Integrity of Psychology Assessments in the AI Age: A Critical Examination. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09729-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Instrumental case study of one UK BPS-accredited psychology degree (2023/24); two experienced markers made binary pass/fail judgements of unedited ChatGPT output. The study reports "90% of assessments being passable in the present study", comparable to the 94% rate it attributes to Scarfe et al.

> "With 90% of assessments being passable in the present study, and Scarfe et al. ( 2024) reporting a rate of 94%, this suggests that psychology might be more vulnerable than other disciplines."
