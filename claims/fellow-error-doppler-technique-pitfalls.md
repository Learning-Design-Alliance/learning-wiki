## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Review of fellow images revealed Doppler pitfalls — sample volume placement error (8.6%), VTI tracing error (5.7%), caliper placement error (2.9%), and Doppler angle error over 20° (2.9%) — while Bland-Altman analysis showed no systematic bias. [→ Talan Jordan 2024](#talan-jordan-2024)

## Evidence

### Talan Jordan 2024

Talan Jordan, Mangalick Keshav, Pradhan Deepak, Sauthoff Harald. (2024). Accuracy of Echocardiographic Cardiac Output Assessment by Critical Care Fellows. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2024-0030OC

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Image-level review of the Doppler windows and spectral analyses for each fellow measurement in the pilot study, enumerating specific error types with printed percentages. The authors interpret the error as most likely attributable to Doppler measurement techniques.

> "Fellow error in caliper placement for LVOT diameter measurements occurred once (2.9%). Fellow error in pulsed-wave sample volume placement occurred three times (8.6%). Fellow VTI tracing error occurred two times (5.7%), and Doppler angle error greater than 20° occurred once (2.9%)."
