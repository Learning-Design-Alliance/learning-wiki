### N.G. Holmes & D.A. Bonn 2015

N.G. Holmes & D.A. Bonn. (2015). Quantitative comparisons to promote inquiry in the introductory physics lab. The Physics Teacher. [doi:10.1119/1.4928350](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4928350)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative analysis of one student's final conclusion from the SQILab sample (Figure 1), in which the student identifies that the outcome is the opposite of what he expected from class. The article presents this as illustrative evidence of model-evaluation behaviour, not a measured group outcome.

> "He does, however, recognize the limitations of the model, namely that it is only valid for small angles. He then begins to tie together the physical model, the mathematical approximation, and the reality of experimental measurement as he discusses how 20° is not a 'small enough' angle when precision is high."
