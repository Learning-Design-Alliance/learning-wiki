Evidence¶

Arslanyilmaz 2010¶

Arslanyilmaz, A., & Pedersen, S. (2010). Enhancing negotiation of meaning through task familiarity using subtitled videos in an online TBLL environment. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 9(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ898004

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Quasi-experiment with 10 NNS-NNS dyads (5 per condition, intact sections randomly assigned) completing four tasks in a WebCT-Vista chat. Independent-samples t-test on ratio of negotiated turns: control M = .17, SD = .046; experimental M = .30, SD = .046; t = -4.48, df = 8, Sig. = .002. The article reports effect size "d = 2.82", attributing it to extended exposure across four 50-minute tasks.

"dyads provided with subtitled videos produced a significantly higher percentage of negotiated turns than dyads that were not provided with subtitled videos"
