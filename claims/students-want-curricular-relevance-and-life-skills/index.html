q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

High School Today overview of focus-group findings. Students recommended stronger connections between core classes, the real world, and their identities, including fieldtrips, internships, and current events in lessons.

"on the whole, they ﬁnd the majority of the high school curriculum missing application to “the real world” and want more opportunities to learn about potential future careers."

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Same overview: students recognize durable skills like time management, collaboration, communication, and accountability as critical in the work world, often developed through after-school jobs and extracurriculars, but feel teachers infrequently acknowledge or reward them.

"students want more life skills, such as managing personal ﬁnances, ﬁling taxes, writing resumes, and applying for jobs."
