# Children acquiring a second language through immersion begin with formulaic utterances used as wholes, which are gradually analyzed into component words

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The article reports, citing Fillmore's 1976 dissertation, that well over half of the child's speech in the early months of acquiring a second language consists of formulaic utterances, phrases functioning as units that are first used whole and later analyzed into their component words.
