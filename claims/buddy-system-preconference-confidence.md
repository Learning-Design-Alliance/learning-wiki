## Evidence

### Chris M. Harrison 2024

Chris M. Harrison, Leah Morabito and Ann Njeri, on behalf of the Organising Committees. (2024). Bringing together African & European research communities with an inclusive astronomy conference. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.12834](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.12834)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative feedback from the conference evaluation questionnaire (73/107 participants, 68% completion) about the opt-in buddy system pairing African-affiliated and non-African-affiliated early-career researchers; the authors report comments showed it was "extremely helpful for feeling prepared for the conference".

> "The comments we received demonstrated this was extremely helpful for feeling prepared for the conference and to make participants feel more confident to integrate and participate in discussions, because they already knew somebody attending the conference."
