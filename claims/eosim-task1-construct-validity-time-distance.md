## Evidence

### Leijte Erik 2019

Leijte Erik, Arts Elke, Witteman Bart, Jakimowicz Jack, De Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne. (2019). Construct, content and face validity of the eoSim laparoscopic simulator on advanced suturing tasks. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-018-06652-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional validation study of 104 participants (60 novices, 31 residents, 13 experts) performing three suturing tasks on the eoSim; one-way ANOVA with Welch correction compared groups. The abstract reports Task 1 time means of "339, 607 and 1224 s" and distance means of 8.1, 15.6 and 21.7 m, both p < 0.001; Table 3 confirms significant time and distance differences.

> "Construct validity results showed significant differences between experts, target group or novices for Task 1 in terms of time (means 339, 607 and 1224 s, respectively, p < 0.001) and distance (means 8.1, 15.6 and 21.7 m, respectively, p < 0.001)."
