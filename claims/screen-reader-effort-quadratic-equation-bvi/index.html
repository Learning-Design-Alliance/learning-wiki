Evidence¶

Steinbach 2022¶

Steinbach, S. (2022). Looking beyond vision: Supports for students who are blind or visually impaired in mathematics. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/looking-beyond-vision-supports-for-students-who-are-blind-or-visually-impaired-in-mathematics/

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Da Paixão Silva et al. (2017), a keystroke-level model analysis of BVI users extracting information about a quadratic equation via screen readers: "JAWS had the fastest time recorded at 132 seconds" versus NVDA at 203.28 seconds. The model equated a keystroke to 0.28 seconds and a mental operation to 1.2 seconds.

"JAWS had the fastest time recorded at 132 seconds, with 30 keystrokes, 28 mental operations, and 88 seconds of wait time. NVDA had the slowest, with 203.28 seconds, 21 keystrokes, 67 mental operations, and 117 seconds of wait time."
