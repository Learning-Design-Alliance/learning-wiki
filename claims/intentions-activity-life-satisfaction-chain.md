## Evidence

### Moreno-Murcia 2018

Moreno-Murcia, J.A., Huéscar Hernández, E., & Ruiz, L. (2018). Perceptions of Controlling Teaching Behaviors and the Effects on the Motivation and Behavior of High School Physical Education Students. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15102288

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Structural regression model on 416 students; the intention–activity path was significant and the activity–life satisfaction path was significant. Table 1: intention–activity r = 0.66, activity–life satisfaction r = 0.17 (p < 0.001).
