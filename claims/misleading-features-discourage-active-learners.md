### Markus Pössel 2018

Markus Pössel. (2018). Relatively complicated? Using models to teach general relativity at different levels. Invited talk, DPG Spring Meeting 2017. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1812.11589](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1812.11589)

`q1 · i?` · `review · r2`

This is the review author's interpretation of how misleading model features affect learners: passive listeners are unlikely to stumble on misleading features, while those who "try most actively to understand what you are teaching" are hardest hit. No empirical data are reported for this statement.

> "Misleading model features can make reconciliation with other aspects of general relativity difficult, are not a solid foundation for extrapolation, and in this way can have a discouraging influence on learners."
