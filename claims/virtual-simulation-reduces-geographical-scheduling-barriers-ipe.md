## Evidence

### Molabe Manoko 2026

Molabe Manoko, Rasesemola Richard, Matlala Sidwell, Matshaka Lerato. (2026). Virtual simulation as a catalyst for equitable and authentic interprofessional learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09773-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative focus group study with 29 purposively sampled senior students from five health science disciplines, conducted via four online Microsoft Teams focus groups between January and June 2024. A participant described fitting "these crucial team practices into my schedule from my clinical site or home". Findings are self-reported perceptions, not measured outcomes.

> "The virtual platform is a game-changer; I can now fit these crucial team practices into my schedule from my clinical site or home, which means I actually get the interprofessional experience I need"
