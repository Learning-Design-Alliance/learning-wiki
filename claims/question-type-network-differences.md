### Min Xia 2024

Min Xia, Zhu Su, Wei-Bing Deng, Xiu-Mei Feng, Ben-Wei Zhang. (2024). How to quantify an examination? Evidence from physics examinations via complex networks. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.13161)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Question-type comparison of integrated KPNs for choice, experimental, calculation and elective questions. Calculation questions "exhibit the smallest average shortest path, number of communities and average clustering coefficient but the highest network density, average degree and comprehensive difficulty coefficient" (Fd(x10) = 0.21); choice questions had the lowest density and difficulty.
