## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a study of deep-sea divers undergoing a Stroop task, tonic EDA changes were not correlated with stress while phasic EDA showed a correlation with cognitive stress using spectral analysis. [→ Kennett 2025](#kennett-2025)

## Evidence

### Kennett 2025

Kennett, R., Martin, A. J., Pearson, J., Burns, E. C., & Collie, R. (2025). Challenge and Threat Appraisal of Tests in High School: Integrating Educational Psychology and Biopsychology Improves Understanding of How Students Take Tests. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000964.supp

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r?`

The supplementary materials report, citing Posada-Quintero et al. (2018), a study of stress in deep-sea divers in which spectral analysis linked "cognitive stress and phasic EDA" while tonic changes were uncorrelated with stress. No effect size is printed.

> "The authors found that tonic changes were not correlated with stress, but using spectral analysis (Fast Fourier Transform), they showed a correlation between cognitive stress and phasic EDA in divers undergoing a Stroop task."
