## Evidence

### Wang Guoxia 2025

Wang Guoxia, Zhang Qin, Wang Lumeng, Liu Yi. (2025). The impact of teachers’ autonomy support on students’ academic performance from the perspective of hyperscanning: the mediating role of autonomy need satisfaction. Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsaf115

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlations between lPFC INS and behavioral outcomes across the 42 dyads. The article reports INS "positively correlated with group (r = 0.40, P = .008), autonomy need satisfaction (r = 0.40, P = .008; Fig. 7a), and positive emotions (r = 0.36, P = .019; Fig. 7b)"; no significant correlation with post-test scores was found.
