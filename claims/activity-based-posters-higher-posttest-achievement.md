## Evidence

### Coşkun and Eker 2018

İsmail Coşkun, Cevat Eker. (2018). The Effect of Teaching Activities Done by Using Activity Based Posters on the Students' Academic Achievements, Retention Levels in Their Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(4): 585-597. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.060402

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test–post-test control group quasi-experiment with 60 ninth graders (30 per group) in an Anatolian high school in Eskişehir; the experimental group additionally made and presented activity-based posters for six weeks. Post-test means were 19.8 (experimental) versus 13.00 (control), a "significant difference" per the printed t test.

> "The difference between academic achievement post-test scores of the control group and experimental group was compared with the t test and it was found there was a significant difference with the value obtained (t = 5.99) and [p<0.05]."
