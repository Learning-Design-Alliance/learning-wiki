## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Talan Jordan 2024

Talan Jordan, Mangalick Keshav, Pradhan Deepak, Sauthoff Harald. (2024). Accuracy of Echocardiographic Cardiac Output Assessment by Critical Care Fellows. ATS Scholar. https://doi.org/10.34197/ats-scholar.2024-0030OC

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Image quality assessment using an adapted validated scoring tool applied to fellow and technician images from PLAX, A5C, and A3C views. Table 3 reports fellow A3C average 3.71 ± 3.02 versus technician 5.49 ± 2.39, P = 0.006.

> "Images were scored for fellows and compared with the formal images, showing a significant difference in A3C view quality but no significant difference in PLAX or A5C view quality (Table 3)."
