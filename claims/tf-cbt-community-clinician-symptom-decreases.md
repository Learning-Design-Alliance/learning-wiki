## Evidence

### Amaya-Jackson L 2018

Amaya-Jackson L, Hagele D, Sideris J, Potter D, Briggs EC, Keen L, Murphy RA, Dorsey S, Patchett V, Ake GS, Socolar R. (2018). Pilot to policy: statewide dissemination and implementation of evidence-based treatment for traumatized youth. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-018-3395-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post repeated-measures analysis of 156 clients completing fidelity-monitored TF-CBT, analyzed with hierarchical linear models (time nested within child within clinician). PTSD, depression, SDQ and caregiver BSI all decreased significantly (all p < .001); child depression fell by "almost one standard deviation (9.08, p < .001)". No standardized effect sizes are printed.

> "All child clinical outcomes and caregiver distress outcomes decreased significantly from pre-treatment to post-treatment and are listed in Table 4 (all p < .001). Child depression scores decreased by almost one standard deviation (9.08, p < .001)"
