The article reports, citing Sticht's work with native language learners in reading and listening, that "reading skill cannot exceed listening skill." The article offers this as a cited research finding, not a new empirical test; no effect size is printed.

> "shows that the lackof competency in the first stage of native language acquisition severely hinders progress in the next stage. Basically what Stichtwrites is that reading skill cannot exceed listening skill."
