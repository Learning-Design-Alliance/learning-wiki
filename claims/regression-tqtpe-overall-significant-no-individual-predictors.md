## Evidence

### Anapalı Şenel 2025

Anapalı Şenel, A., Göksu, B., Şenel, E., & Solmaz, F. İ. (2025). Predicting academic performance with fuzzy logic in prospective physical education and sports teachers. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-99124-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Multiple regression analysis of data from 40 prospective PE teachers predicting TQT-PE scores. The article reports "an R-value of 0.560, an R2 of 0.313, and an Adjusted R2 of 0.188" with the model significant at p = 0.04.

> "The overall model was statistically significant (F = 2.50, p = 0.04), indicating that the predictors collectively explained approximately 31.3% of the variance in the dependent variable."

### Anapalı Şenel 2025 (2)

Anapalı Şenel, A., Göksu, B., Şenel, E., & Solmaz, F. İ. (2025). Predicting academic performance with fuzzy logic in prospective physical education and sports teachers. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-99124-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the same multiple regression, the surface approach was the only predictor near significance; the article states "none of the individual predictors reached statistical significance at the conventional threshold (p < 0.05)". GPA (p = 0.136), deep approach (p = 0.658), strategic approach (p = 0.751), self-efficacy (p = 0.226) and support (p = 0.343) were non-significant.

> "The Surface Approach exhibited a negative relationship with TQT-PE, approaching statistical significance (β = − 3.87, Standardised β = − 0.211, t = − 1.815, p = 0.079)."
