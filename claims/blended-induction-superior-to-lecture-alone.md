## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In one psychiatry on-call simulation programme, confidence improved significantly more with a didactic lecture followed by a 2-hour virtual simulation than with the lecture alone. [→ Sebastian P 2024](#sebastian-p-2024)

## Evidence

### Sebastian P 2024

Sebastian P, Wells L, Huszka H, Tovell N, Okorie M. (2024). Preparing clinicians for practice: effectiveness and design of on-call simulation. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05495-y

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review attributes this to Blamey et al.'s programme for incoming foundation doctors at a mental health trust, combining a didactic lecture on common pager requests with a 2-hour virtual on-call simulation. Comparison is pre/post confidence ratings, no effect size printed.

> "Self-reported confidence ratings collected immediately prior to and following simulation demonstrated that the simulation improved trainee confidence significantly when compared to the didactic lecture."
