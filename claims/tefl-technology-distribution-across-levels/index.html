Descriptive analysis of technology distribution across the 33 included studies (RQ1, Fig. 2). The review reports VR/AR concentration in tertiary education "likely reflects the substantial hardware requirements and institutional investment needed for immersive technologies," and social media tools were absent from primary-level studies, likely due to age-related platform restrictions.

"Mobile and ubiquitous learning was the most prevalent technology category, appearing in 10 of 33 studies (30.3%) and distributed across all three educational levels."
