The review's synthesis of experimental and correlational evidence on cannabis use in nondependent high school students concludes that use "may moderately impair visuospatial processing and learning" in this population. The review reports this as a moderate impairment, hedged with "may".

> "We conclude that using cannabis may moderately impair visuospatial processing and learning in nondependent high school students, although the effects could disappear under abstinence"
