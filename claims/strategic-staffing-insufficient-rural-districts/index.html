q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Findings from interviews with district and EPP staff and administrators in the TCLAS qualitative study on post-grant sustainability. The report states strategic-staffing funding was "insufficient to continue stipends at the $20,000 TCLAS level" in some districts; no effect size is printed.

"some TCLAS districts—especially smaller and more rural districts—found that the funding freed up by strategic staffing was insufficient to continue stipends at the $20,000 TCLAS level."
