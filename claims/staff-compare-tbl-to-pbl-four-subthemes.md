### Drovandi Aaron 2026

Drovandi Aaron, Sheader Elizabeth, Kingston Margaret. (2026). Integrated Team-Based Learning in a UK Undergraduate Medical Programme. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70470

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Inductive thematic analysis of three staff focus groups and eight session observations (14 academic staff, 2 technical staff), most participants with several years of experience previously facilitating PBL. Findings show discussions fell under four sub-themes comparing the two pedagogies.

> "These discussions fell under four sub‐themes: (1) facilitator interactions with students, (2) facilitator teamworking, (3) effort and reward for facilitators and (4) overall student learning impacts."
