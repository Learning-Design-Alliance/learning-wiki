## Evidence

### Dubois Fatéméh 2026

Dubois Fatéméh, Ambrosetti Damien, Tosca Lucie. (2026). Educational Impact of an Instagram Account on Histology Teaching: Mixed Methods Approach in Medical Education. JMIR Formative Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/89044

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Self-reported survey data from 125 medical students on whether they reused Histo.Embryo Instagram content in academic settings. The authors interpret this as evidence that the platform supports practical knowledge retrieval and application beyond passive consumption.

> "When asked whether they had used information from the posts in academic settings, 50% (63/125) of students stated they had done so once or twice, and an additional 16% (20/125) had used it several times, such as during lectures, tutorials, or exams."
