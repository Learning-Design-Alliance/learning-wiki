Jaime Thomas 2020¶

Jaime Thomas, Naihobe Gonzalez, Nora Paxton, Andrew Wiegand, Leela Hebbar. (2020). The Effects of Expanding Pell Grant Eligibility for Short Occupational Training Programs: Results from the Experimental Sites Initiative. NCEE 2021–001. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/2021001/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Second random-assignment experiment in the same initiative: students were randomly assigned to be offered or not offered experimental Pell funds and outcomes compared 10 to 30 months later. The report states the offer "increased program enrollment and completion by about 10 percentage points." No effect size is printed.
