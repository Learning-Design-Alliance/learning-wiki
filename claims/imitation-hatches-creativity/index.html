Roschelle 2019¶

Roschelle, J., & Burke, Q. (2019). Commentary on Interest-Driven Creator theory: a US perspective on fostering interest, creativity, and habit in school. Research and Practice in Technology Enhanced Learning, 14(13). https://doi.org/10.1186/s41039-019-0107-2

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the commentary's discussion of IDC's creativity loop. The authors describe remix-like processes in which learners develop their own samples from dedicated imitations, then stack, mingle, and mold them into new combinations staged and shared publicly.

"Imitation is often dismissed as the antithesis of creation, when in fact the affection and industry that spur it are the same ones that hatch creativity."
