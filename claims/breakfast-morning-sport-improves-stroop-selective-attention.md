## Evidence

### Latino Francesca 2026

Latino Francesca, Tafuri Domenico, Saraiello Emma, Tafuri Maria Giovanna, Byerley Lauri. (2026). Integrated Structured Breakfast and Morning Sport Program and Its Associations with Attention, Executive Functions, and Academic Performance in Students. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18132103

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA (Group × Time) in a 16-week quasi-experimental pre-post study with class-based allocation of 110 students aged 14-16. The experimental group (n = 55) received structured breakfast plus cognitively oriented morning sport three times weekly; controls continued ordinary activities. The article reports a "significant Group × Time interaction emerged for Stroop Test scores, F(1,108) = 11.94, p = 0.001, partial η2 = 0.100".

> "a significant Group × Time interaction emerged for Stroop Test scores, F(1,108) = 11.94, p = 0.001, partial η2 = 0.100. In particular, the experimental group demonstrated greater improvements in Stroop Test performance over time, from 68.4 ± 10.2 at baseline to 59.8 ± 8.9 at post-intervention, whereas the control group showed only minimal changes from 69.1 ± 10.5 to 67.5 ± 9.8."

### Latino Francesca 2026 (2)

Latino Francesca, Tafuri Domenico, Saraiello Emma, Tafuri Maria Giovanna, Byerley Lauri. (2026). Integrated Structured Breakfast and Morning Sport Program and Its Associations with Attention, Executive Functions, and Academic Performance in Students. Nutrients. https://doi.org/10.3390/nu18132103

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of individual change scores (post minus pre) within the experimental group. The article reports "significant moderate correlations between improvements in Stroop Test performance and school engagement scores (r = −0.46, p = 0.001)" and also a moderate association with academic grades (r = −0.41, p = 0.003).

> "The analyses revealed significant moderate correlations between improvements in Stroop Test performance and school engagement scores (r = −0.46, p = 0.001), indicating that students showing greater improvements in attentional and inhibitory control abilities also tended to report higher levels of school participation and motivation."
