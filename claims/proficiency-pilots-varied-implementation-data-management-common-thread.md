## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Across three pilot sites, programs approached proficiency-based education differently depending on context, but all required a strong data management system for tracking student proficiency over time. [→ Lewis 2013](#lewis-2013)

## Evidence

### Lewis 2013

Lewis, M., Steele, J. L., Santibañez, L., Stecher, B. M., Hamilton, L., Faxon-Mills, S., & Rudnick, M. (2013). Proficiency-Based Pathways in Three Pilot Programs: Examining Implementation and Outcomes. SREE Fall 2013 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED564063

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Implementation findings from site visits, staff and teacher interviews, and classroom observations conducted between June 2012 and May 2013 across the three pilot sites. The authors report that programs "approached proficiency-based education quite differently" and identify data management as the common thread.

> "we find that the programs have approached proficiency-based education quite differently, likely as a function of their contexts, but that a common thread is the necessity of a strong data management system that allows teachers to track student proficiency over time."
