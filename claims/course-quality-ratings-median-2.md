Improving competencies in evidence-based dementia care: Results from a pilot study on a novel inter-professional training course (the KOMPIDEM project). GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001034](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001034)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive pre-post survey of all course participants in the Winter Term 2014/15 piloting (10 medical, 8 nursing students) using a modified version of Zumbach's short scale on teaching evaluation. The article reports the median grade "2" (IQR 2-3) and component ratings from Figure 2.

> "Both sub-samples rated the course as a whole with a grade of “2” (IQR 2-3) on average. Of the individual course components, the PbL tutorials and the visitations to the care facilities were most frequently rated with the grades 1 or 2"
