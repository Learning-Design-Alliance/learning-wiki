### Bailey E. G. 2020

Bailey E. G., Greenall R. F., Baek D. M., Morris C., Nelson N., Quirante T. M., Rice N. S., Rose S., Williams K. R. (2020). Female In-Class Participation and Performance Increase with More Female Peers and/or a Female Instructor in Life Sciences Courses. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-12-0266

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Re-analysis of the 34-class observation data (Figure 2B) counting only each student's first event of each participation type; repeated-measures one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's posttest found female fractions of verbal participation, hands raised, and call-outs each differed from attendance fraction (p < 0.001, n = 34 classes). The article states gaps were "still significant (and of comparable size)".

> "our observed gender gaps in participation were still significant (and of comparable size) when students were only counted the first time they participated"
