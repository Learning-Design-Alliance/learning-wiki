q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis of prayer neuroimaging studies; the review reports, citing Schjoedt et al. 2009, that improvised prayer "selectively activates the TPJ, temporopolar region, medial PFC, and precuneus". No effect size printed.

"Improvised personal prayer selectively activates the TPJ, temporopolar region, medial PFC, and precuneus (the brain's Theory of Mind network), indicating that sincere devotional address to a perceived divine agent is processed as genuine interpersonal communication"
