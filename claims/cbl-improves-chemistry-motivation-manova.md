## Evidence

### Yalçınkaya 2012

Yalçınkaya, E., Boz, Y., & Erdur-Baker, Ö. (2012). Is case-based instruction effective in enhancing high school students' motivation toward chemistry? Science Education International, 23(2), 102-116. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ975553

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment in one Anatolian high school: 45 10th-graders (25 CBL, 20 TDCI) taught the gas topic for 12 weeks; MSLQ motivation section given as pre- and post-test. MANOVA on gain scores: "Pillai's trace = 0.377, F (6, 38) = 3.839, p= 0.004", partial η² = 0.37, power 0.94.

> "MANOVA analysis indicated the significant effect of treatment on students' perceived motivation (Pillai's trace = 0.377, F (6, 38) = 3.839, p= 0.004). The partial eta squared value of 0.37 showed a large effect of treatment on students' perceived motivation."
