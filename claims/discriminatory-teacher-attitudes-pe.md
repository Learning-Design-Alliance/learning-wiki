`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme Analysis of narratives in the teacher–student relationship subcategory identified sexist attitudes of (male) teachers; one participant reported body-critical comments that “created great remorse” and another described a teacher with complaints for inappropriate sexist behaviors.

> "My PE teacher increased the rejection I felt toward my body. He made comments like: “you can't waste your body this way”, or … “you're going to lose all your potential”."
