## Subclaims
`q2 i3` CURrENT scores correlate with final exam performance (r=0.52) and with physics subject GPA (r=0.52), supporting criterion validity. [→ Charles Baily 2017](#charles-baily-2017)

## Evidence

### Charles Baily 2017

Charles Baily, Qing X. Ryan, Cecilia Astolfi and Steven J. Pollock. (2017). Conceptual Assessment Tool for Advanced Undergraduate Electrodynamics. PHYS. REV. – PHYS. ED. RES. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020113](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020113)

`q2 · i3` · `design · r3`

Criterion-validity analysis correlating CURrENT scores with independent measures. The exam correlation (r=0.52, p<0.001, N=276) and the subject-GPA correlation (r=0.52, p<0.001, N=190, all CU students) are characterized by the authors as between medium (0.3–0.5) and strong (0.5–1.0).

> "We find significant correlations between CURrENT scores and students' final exam performance (Pearson correlation coefficient r=0.52, p<0.001, N=276 including both CU and non-CU students)"
