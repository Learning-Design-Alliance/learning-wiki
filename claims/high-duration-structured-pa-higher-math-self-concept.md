# High-duration structured PA is associated with higher mathematical self-concept than low-duration structured PA, with no difference between low-duration participants and non-participants

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Dapp Laura C. 2019

Dapp Laura C., Roebers Claudia M. (2019). The Mediating Role of Self-Concept between Sports-Related Physical Activity and Mathematical Achievement in Fourth Graders. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16152658

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Mann-Whitney-U comparison within the N = 138 sample using the SDQ-I mathematical subscale (Cronbach's α = 0.95). High-duration structured PA children reported significantly higher mathematical self-concept; no effect size is printed.

> "the high-duration children (M = 4.02, SD = 1.06) had significantly higher mathematical self-concepts than the low-duration children (M = 3.24, SD = 1.23), Z = −3.35, p < 0.001."

### Dapp Laura C. 2019 (2)

Dapp Laura C., Roebers Claudia M. (2019). The Mediating Role of Self-Concept between Sports-Related Physical Activity and Mathematical Achievement in Fourth Graders. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16152658

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Null Wilcoxon-Signed-Rank comparison in the same sample: mere sports-club membership did not significantly raise mathematical self-concept; equivalence was not tested.

> "children who engaged in structured PA (i.e., children who were in at least one sports club; M = 3.74, SD = 1.18) and children who did not (M = 3.54, SD = 1.25), did not significantly differ in their mathematical self-concept, Z = −0.77, p = 0.44."
