## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Unsupervised hierarchical clustering of 15 comprehension items yields Command, Modifier, and Syntactic clusters with clear separation, and participant clusters match these three comprehension phenotypes (Command 35.6%, Modifier 47%, Syntactic 17.4%). [→ Gankin 2026](#gankin-2026)

## Evidence

### Gankin 2026

Gankin, K., Venkatesh, R., Khokhlovich, E., & Vyshedskiy, A. (2026). Receptive and expressive language phenotyping in over 62,000 autistic individuals. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00445-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Unsupervised hierarchical cluster analysis (UHCA) of 15 caregiver-reported comprehension items from 62,414 autistic individuals, visually validated by PCA. Clusters were termed Command, Modifier, and Syntactic, with inter-cluster distances "larger than the distances between subclusters, indicating clear separation".

> "The analysis shown in Figure 2A identified the same three clusters previously reported in our earlier studies 49–51. The distances between these three clusters (measured by Height; Figure 2A) were larger than the distances between subclusters, indicating clear separation."

### Gankin 2026 (2)

Gankin, K., Venkatesh, R., Khokhlovich, E., & Vyshedskiy, A. (2026). Receptive and expressive language phenotyping in over 62,000 autistic individuals. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00445-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square test of independence on phenotype-by-severity contingency data (Table 5) from the full cohort of 62,414 participants. The Syntactic Phenotype contained more mild ASD (74.9% vs 5.3% severe), and the association was "significant" per the printed χ2 statistic; no effect size is printed.

> "A Chi-square test of independence revealed a significant association between comprehension phenotypes and ASD severity distribution, χ2 (4, N = 62,414) = 6270.87, p < 0.0001."
