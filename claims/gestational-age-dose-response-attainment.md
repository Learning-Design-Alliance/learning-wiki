## Evidence

### Haider S 2026

Haider S, Tsanas A, Batty GD, Reynolds RM, Roffe M, Whalley HC, Marioni RE, Richardson H, Battersby C, Boardman JP. (2026). Health and Socioeconomic Factors in School Readiness and Achievement Among Children Born Very Preterm. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.23068

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Fully adjusted GEE logistic regression models on 14,129 children with complete outcome data and maternal identifiers. The quote reports the adjusted odds ratio comparing the most preterm group with the 31-week reference group.

> "Children born at GA 23 to 24 weeks were more likely to not achieve expected EYFSP levels compared with those born at GA 31 weeks (adjusted odds ratio [AOR], 2.86 [95% CI, 2.19-3.73])."
