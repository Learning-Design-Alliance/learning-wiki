## Evidence

### Van de Mortel T 2016

Van de Mortel T, Bird J, Chown P, Trigger R, Ahern C. (2016). General practitioners as educators in adolescent health: a training evaluation. BMC Family Practice 17:32. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-016-0432-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post survey component of the mixed-methods evaluation of the training intervention. A paired samples t-test showed the knowledge quiz mean rose "increased significantly from 7.00 (±1.22) to 8.98/10 (±1.11)", t = −8.705, df = 41, p < .001.

> "Participants’ mean scores on the knowledge quiz on adolescent health and general practice access issues increased significantly from 7.00 (±1.22) to 8.98/10 (±1.11)  (t = −8.705; df = 41; p < .001)."
