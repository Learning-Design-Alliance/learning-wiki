## Evidence

### W. K. Black 2023

W. K. Black, Rebecca L. Matz, Mark Mills, and A. E. Evrard. (2023). Practice Makes Better: Quantifying Grade Beneﬁts of Study. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010139](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.19.010139)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Robustness analysis within the same KLLR study of PR usage in PHYSICS 140 and 240: the sample was split into terciles by normalized math test score T, and overall grade gains averaged across the three study indicators were computed for each tercile, which the article says are similar.

> "Dividing the student sample into terciles by T , we ﬁnd similar overall grade gains: 0.60± 0.21, 0.73± 0.19, and 0.75± 0.12 for the lowest, intermediate, and highest T terciles, respectively, averaged across study indicators. All stu­dents beneﬁt similarly from increased study volume."
