## Evidence

### Taghi Ali 2026

Taghi Ali, Atia Mohammad, Mohaisen Mais Alreem, Al-Noufi Bushra, Sibai Mohamad, Nouh Sara, Arfeh Yaman, Alhamid Ahmad Abdul Hakim, Taghi Olya, Kheder Karam, Mahfouz Fawaz Ali. (2026). Assessment of knowledge and awareness of cardiopulmonary resuscitation and heart attack symptoms amongst medical students in Syria - a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08800-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariable linear regression predicting the MI knowledge score (0–18, n = 500) with robust HC3 standard errors; model R² = 0.164. Public versus private enrolment carried "adjusted B 0.99 (95% CI 0.55 to 1.42)"; the CPR model separately showed public versus private B 1.07 (95% CI 0.62 to 1.51).

> "In adjusted analysis, MI knowledge was independently associated with clinical stage (adjusted B 2.06 (95% CI 1.45 to 2.66)), public university enrolment (adjusted B 0.99 (95% CI 0.55 to 1.42)), and prior AED exposure (adjusted B 0.88 (95% CI 0.37 to 1.40))."
