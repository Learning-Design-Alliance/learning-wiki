## Evidence

### Yong Oh Lee 2026

Yong Oh Lee, Byeonghun Bang, Sejun Oh. (2026). LLM-Driven Rubric-Based Assessment of Algebraic Competence in Multi-Stage Block Coding Tasks with Design and Field Evaluation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.3390/app162110595](https://doi.org/10.3390/app162110595)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Exploratory K-means clustering on the five rubric scores of 42 students, standardized before clustering, with PCA used to reduce the five-dimensional space to two dimensions for visualization. Violin plots showed distinct profiles; the analysis is descriptive with no effect sizes.

> "Based on silhouette analysis, the optimal number of clusters was determined to be k = 6."
