### Gifford 2026

Gifford, K., Ehrlich, G. S., Bumbacher, E., & Kuo, E. (2026). “I forgot the formula:” How students can use coherence to reconstruct a (partially) forgotten equation. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1103/yfxm-m4sw](https://doi.org/10.1103/yfxm-m4sw)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focal case from the interviews: Blake, an undergraduate, combined C=Q/V and V=Ed to derive that less distance means greater capacitance, contradicting their initial incorrect "Electrons Jumping" mechanism. The article reports Blake "relied on physical analogy to chain qualitative dependencies and reasoned from equations to determine the intermediate qualitative dependency," and concluded the initial reasoning was wrong.

> "Rather, Blake attended explicitly to an inconsis- tency between their physical and mathematical reasoning and generated a new line of physical reasoning, “Dielec- tric Analogy,” that was consistent with their mathemat- ical reasoning before concluding that it was correct."
