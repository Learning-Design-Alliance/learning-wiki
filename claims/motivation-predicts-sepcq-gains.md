## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pre-course Course-Related Motivation to Learn was a statistically significant predictor of post-course SEPCQ scores for Factor 1, Factor 2, and Total scores after adjusting for pre-course scores; CRSE did not reach significance. [→ Zachariae R 2015](#zachariae-r-2015)

## Evidence

### Zachariae R 2015

Zachariae R, O'Connor M, Lassesen B, Olesen M, Kjær LB, Thygesen M, Mørcke AM. (2015). The self-efficacy in patient-centeredness questionnaire – a new measure of medical student and physician confidence in exhibiting patient-centered behaviors. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0427-x

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Multiple linear regression analyses with post-course SEPCQ scores as dependent variables, adjusting for pre-course scores, in students scheduled to take the course. The article reports "Beta:0.21" for Factors 1 and 2 and "Beta:0.25" for Total; these are printed regression coefficients and no effect size is printed.

> "pre-course CRMTL was a statistical significant predictor of post-course SEPCQ-scores for Factor 1 (Beta:0.21; p = 0.02); Factor 2 (Beta:0.21; p = 0.02), and SEPCQ Total scores (Beta:0.25; p = 0.005)"
