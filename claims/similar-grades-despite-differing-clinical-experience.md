## Evidence

### Duffy Barbara 2023

Duffy Barbara, Tully Roisin, Stanton Alice V. (2023). An online case-based teaching and assessment program on clinical history-taking skills and reasoning using simulated patients in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-022-03950-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of 273 students sitting the online long case examination in May 2020, comparing grade categories across the three rotation groups. Honors/pass/fail percentages were reported per group (e.g. group 1: 39%, 33%, 23%, 6%) and the difference was not significant (p = 0.48); equivalence was not formally tested.

> "Interestingly, grade distribution was similar across groups 1,2 and 3, despite the differing levels of clinical experience prior to the online program commencing (Fig. 2, panels A and B) (p = 0.48)."
