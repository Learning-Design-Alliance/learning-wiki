Descriptive survey data on self-reported preparation time from the same 144-student pilot cohort. The article reports that "a combined 84.0% of students dedicated at least one hour to preparation"; no inferential statistics are given.

> "The most common response regarding time spent preparing for each test was more than two hours, reported by 73 students (50.7%). An additional 48 students (33.3%) spent one to two hours preparing, meaning that a combined 84.0% of students dedicated at least one hour to preparation for each pre-class test."
