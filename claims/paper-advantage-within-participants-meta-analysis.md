## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Delgado 2018

Delgado, P., Vargas, C., Ackerman, R., & Salmerón, L. (2018). Don't throw away your printed books: A meta-analysis on the effects of reading media on reading comprehension. Educational Research Review, 25, 23-38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2018.09.003

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 18 within-participants comparisons from 16 studies (1,531 participants), each participant reading on both media. The printed pooled effect size was dc = -0.21 (95% CI: -0.37, -0.06), a small effect replicating the paper advantage. Heterogeneity was high (I2 = 89.88).

> "The mean effect size of this sample of studies was also significant, and it replicated the advantage of paper-based reading over digital-based reading (dc = -0.21; 95%; CI: -0.37, -0.06; k = 18)."
