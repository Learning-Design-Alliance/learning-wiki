## Evidence

### Aronoff N 2017

Aronoff N, Stellrecht E, Lyons AG, Zafron ML, Glogowski M, Grabowski J, Ohtake PJ. (2017). Teaching evidence-based practice principles to prepare health professions students for an interprofessional learning experience. Journal of the Medical Library Association. https://doi.org/10.5195/jmla.2017.179

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures one-way ANOVA across three time points in a pilot study of 39 students from 8 health professions programs, with the AFT scored blind to order. The article reports "AFT scores changed significantly in response to the program (F(2,76)=8.417, p<0.001)" with means rising from 64 to 73 (p=0.001).

> "AFT scores changed significantly in response to the program (F(2,76)=8.417, p<0.001). The average AFT score before accessing the online learning modules was 64 (95% CI (59, 69)). After accessing the online EBP modules, the average AFT score increased to 73 (95% CI (67, 78); p=0.001)."
