## Evidence

### Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh 2026

Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Hafshejani Nazanin Damsaz. (2026). Locus of control and sense of coherence as predictors of academic achievement among medical and nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09941-x

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis in a cross-sectional study of 348 medical (65.2%) and nursing (34.8%) students at Ahvaz Jundishapur University of Medical Sciences. The study reports "significant positive correlations with GPA (r = 0.519, p < 0.001)" for total SOC and its subdimensions.

> "In contrast, SOC demonstrated significant positive correlations with GPA (r = 0.519, p < 0.001) and its subdimensions (comprehensibility, manageability, meaningfulness)."
