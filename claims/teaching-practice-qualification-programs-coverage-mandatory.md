`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive survey results on qualification programs. The article reports block practicum qualification at 18 sites (required at 15), practical year qualification at 17 (mandatory at 12), and supplemental offerings at 22 sites with "attendance is required at only 8 of them"; formats and duration vary across online, in-person, and combined delivery.

> "There are programs for BP qualification at 18 sites, with required attendance at 15 of these sites. A similar number of programs exist to qualify for the practical year (n=17) and are somewhat less often mandatory (12 von 17). Supplemental offerings, such as regularly scheduled meetings for the teaching physicians, take place at 22 sites, of which attendance is required at only 8 of them."
