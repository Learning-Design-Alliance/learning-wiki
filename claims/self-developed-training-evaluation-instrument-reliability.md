## Evidence

### Mao Jing 2026

Mao Jing, Sun Ruimin, Yang Yanming, Zhong Yuan, Bai Yanhui, Qin Yangyang, Liu Shu, Qin Yuanmei. (2026). Evaluating Standardised Training Effectiveness for Trainee Nurses and Clinical Instructors in a Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital: A Mixed‐Methods Study in Henan, China. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70621

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Reliability testing on pilot data from 10 trainee nurses and 10 clinical instructors, analysed in SPSS version 26. The article reports the three Cronbach's α values and states the items have "high levels of internal consistency".

> "the instrument designed for this study was extremely reliable in terms of measuring the effectiveness of the training programme (α = 0.941) as well as the level of competency of the trainee nurses (α = 0.973 for trainee nurses and α = 0.953 for clinical instructors)."
