Evidence¶

Dupéré 2021¶

Dupéré, V., Dion, E., Cantin, S., Archambault, I., & Lacourse, E. (2021). Social contagion and high school dropout: The role of friends, romantic partners, and siblings. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113, 572-584. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000484

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Model 2 of the logistic regression analyses on cases and matched controls (n = 366) distinguished the three intimate types. The article reports "non-trivial ORs ranging from 1.97 (95% CI 1.25-3.41) for friends, to 3.12 (95% CI 1.23-11.01) for romantic partners", with siblings also independently associated; no standardized effect size was printed.

"Occurrences of dropout among each type of intimate were independently associated with early school leaving, with non-trivial ORs ranging from 1.97 (95% CI 1.25-3.41) for friends, to 3.12 (95% CI 1.23-11.01) for romantic partners"
