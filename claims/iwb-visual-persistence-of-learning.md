## Evidence

### Abdulkadir Kırbas 2018

Abdulkadir Kırbas. (2018). The Effect of Interactive Whiteboard Applications Supported by Visual Materials on Middle School Students' Listening Comprehension and Persistence of Learning. Universal Journal of Educational Research 6(11): 2552-2561. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2018.061120

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired-samples t test comparing the experimental group's (n=36) post-test and persistence-test LAT scores administered four weeks after the intervention: t = 2.563, p = .015, significant at the .05 level. The article prints no effect size for this difference.

> "the post -test achievement score of the students in the experimental group was found to be 76.94, and the of the persistence test re-administered 4 weeks later to the students in the experimental group was found to be 75.08.  The t value, which was calculated based on the experimental and control group scores, was 2.563. The paired -samples t test that was carried out to determine whether there was any difference between the groups yielded p=.015"
