Sabery Mahdieh, Batooli Zahra, Sabbarifard Ebrahim, Ghaffari Faezeh. (2026). Simulation-based feedback systems in cardiopulmonary resuscitation training: a scoping review. BMC Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12873-026-01553-4

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Pavo et al. (2016), that in a two-rescuer scenario with medical students, human feedback was equivalent to mechanical feedback in terms of the effective compression ratio, and the human feedback group initiated compressions more quickly.

> "Feedback provided by trained instructors was, in some settings, found to be as effective as mechanical systems [23]."
