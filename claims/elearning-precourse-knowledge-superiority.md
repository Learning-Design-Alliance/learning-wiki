## Evidence

### Andersson ML 2023

Andersson ML, Duch P, Bessmann EL, Lundstrøm LH, Ekelund K. (2023). Preparing for obstetric anaesthesia—An educational randomised controlled trial comparing e‐learning to written course material. Acta Anaesthesiol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aas.14148

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Per protocol analysis of a blinded parallel group RCT in second-year resident anaesthetists; knowledge measured by type X MCQs scaled 0–35. The article reports "a mean difference of 1.8 (95% CI 0.7–2.9; p = .002)" favouring e-learning (31.9 vs 30.2).

> "The per protocol analysis of the primary outcome included 45 participants and demonstrated a mean difference of 1.8 (95% CI 0.7–2.9; p = .002) in knowledge after completion of e-learning before the course, in favour of e-learning compared to written course material."
