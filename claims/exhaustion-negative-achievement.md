## Evidence

### Madigan 2020

Madigan, D. J. & Curran, T. (2020). Does burnout affect academic achievement? A meta-analysis of over 100,000 students. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09533-1

`q4 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Robust variance random-effects meta-analysis pooling exhaustion–achievement correlations. The pooled corrected correlation was "rc+ = -.15, 95% CI = -.22, -.09"; heterogeneity was small (τ2 = .02, I2 = 87.77).

> "For exhaustion (N = 9,095), analyses revealed a small negative weighted mean effect size (rc+ = -.15, 95% CI = -.22, -.09)."
