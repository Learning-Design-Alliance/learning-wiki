Michael Dannenberg and Mamie Voight 2013¶

Michael Dannenberg and Mamie Voight. (2013). Doing Away With Debt: Using Existing Resources to Ensure College Affordability for Low and Middle-Income Families. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED543214

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Education Trust analysis of NPSAS:08 (Table 1) shows the bottom income band ($0-$30,200) faces 72% of income required after grant aid, versus 14% for families above $115,401. Descriptive tabulation; no effect size printed.

"Even after scholarships and grant aid are counted, the lowest income students must find a way to come up with an amount equivalent to roughly three-quarters of their annual family income to pay for one year at a four-year college"
