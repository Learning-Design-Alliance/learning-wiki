Evidence¶

Rahman 2014¶

Rahman, R. O. & Zeglin, R. J. (2014). Holy psychopathology Batman: The pedagogical use of comic books in the teaching of abnormal psychology. Psychology Teaching Review, 20(1). https://www.bps.org.uk/journals/psychology-teaching-review

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pilot comparing two course sections: an experimental section (N=29) that completed the comic-book assignment and a control section (N=15) with lecture only. Pop quiz means were M=7.41 (SD=2.74) versus M=4.00 (SD=2.90); the t-test showed "such a difference betw een the groups".

"The analysis revealed such a difference betw een the groups, t(42)=3.84, p<.001. A n effect size was calcu lated ( d=1.21) and represented a large effect (C ohen, 1988)."
