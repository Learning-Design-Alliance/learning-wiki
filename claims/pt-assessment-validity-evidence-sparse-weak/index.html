Tappan Rachel S., Holland Elizabeth E., Roth Heidi R. (2026). Educational assessments in entry-level physical therapy education: a scoping review. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08927-z

q3 · i? · review · r2

Descriptive synthesis across the 139 included studies, rating each assessment's validity evidence on a four-point scale adapted from Ghaderi et al. and Beckman et al. The review reports content evidence for "64 (70.0%) of the assessments" and median ratings of 0 or 1 in every category.

"Content evidence was the most prevalent type of validity evidence, reported for 64 (70.0%) of the assessments. The validity evidence ratings had median ratings of 0 or 1 for each category of validity evidence."
