Stepwise multiple linear regression on survey data (Table 3): "interest in interprofessionalism emerged as the only significant predictor of overall satisfaction (β= − 0.337, p < 0.001)"; the model including interest and importance accounted for 12.66% of variance (R² = 0.1266, F(4,177) = 6.41, p < 0.001).

> "interest in interprofessionalism emerged as the only significant predictor of overall satisfaction (β= − 0.337, p < 0.001). The negative regression coefficient indicates that higher levels of interest were associated with greater satisfaction with the learning module, as reflected by lower values on the satisfaction scale."
