Soysal 2021¶

Soysal, S., & Yilmaz Kogar, E. (2021). An Investigation of Item Position Effects by Means of IRT-Based Differential Item Functioning Methods. International Journal of Assessment Tools in Education, 8(2), 239-256. https://doi.org/10.21449/ijate.779963

ICCs were examined for items flagged as DIF in all method-by-purification conditions; four items (item 6 vs Booklet B, items 13 and 16 vs Booklet C, item 10 vs Booklet D) were plotted, and the article reports "these items displayed non-uniform DIF."
