## Evidence

### Dana L. Kirkwood-Watts 2022

Dana L. Kirkwood-Watts, Emily K. Bremers, Emily A. Robinson, Kathleen R. Brazeal, and Brian A. Couch. (2022). Revisiting Clickers: In-Class Questions Followed by At-Home Reflections Are Associated with Higher Student Performance on Related Exam Questions. Journal of Microbiology and Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1128/jmbe.00038-22

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of 346 consenting students in two sections of an introductory biology course using generalized linear mixed models; Table 2 prints clicker participation (P < 0.001) and homework completed (P < 0.001) as significant, and "The interaction term between these variables was not retained."

> "At the whole-class level, we found that being present for a targeted clicker question and completing a homework explanation each had signiﬁcant effects on later exam performance (Fig. 2A and T able 2)."
