Teaching radiology in undergraduate programmes in medical schools in francophone sub-Saharan Africa : a multicenter survey. BMC Medical Education, 26(825). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09165-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 11 faculties in 9 countries. The article reports a wide range of theoretical teaching hours, from "30 to 130 h", with a mean of 59.7(29.7) hours and a median of 60(10) hours.

> "The number of hours allocated to theoretical teaching in the faculties ranged from 30 to 130 h, with a mean of 59.7(29.7) hours and a median of 60 (10) hours."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

### Gbande Pihou 2026 (3)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`
