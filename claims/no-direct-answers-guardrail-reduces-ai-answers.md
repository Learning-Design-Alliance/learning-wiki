Stratified examination of AI safeguard-related behaviors by teacher-authored guardrails across pilot conversations. The article reports the rate "dropped from 16.2% to 7.7%, a reduction of 8.5 percentage points"; contextual review found violations typically followed extended scaffolding attempts.

> "When teachers included explicit "no direct answers" constraints in their prompts, the AI's answer provision rate dropped from 16.2% to 7.7%, a reduction of 8.5 percentage points."
