## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Of 46 previously imperfectly answered words repeated on March 19, 12 were then known both ways, 16 by combination but not definition, 5 by definition but not combination, and 13 by neither. [→ Wells 1922](#wells-1922)

## Evidence

### Wells 1922

Wells, F. L. (1922). Aphasic performance in the Terman vocabulary test. The Journal of Educational Psychology.

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retest observations on March 19 in which words with imperfect responses on March 13-14 were given first by cued combination, then by definition, with a 30-second scoring limit and no prompting. The author reports "16 others are known by combination but not by definition" against 5 the reverse way.

> "Of these 46 words, 12 are now known both by combination and by definition; 16 others are known by combination but not by definition; 5 others are known by definition but not by combination;"
