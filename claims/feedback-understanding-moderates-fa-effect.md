## Evidence

### Minder Stefan P. 2018

Minder Stefan P., Weibel David, Wissmath Bartholomäus, Schmitz Felix M. (2018). Do students achieve the desired learning goals using open-book formative assessments? International Journal of Medical Education, 9, 293-301. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5bc6.fead

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Analysis of covariance on the field experiment data showed the condition effect was weaker for students who did not understand the feedback; adjusted means were MTreatment=0.45 vs. MControl=0.37, "still significant" but weaker than the unadjusted t-test.

> "However, the effect of condition on course-script-knowledge at t2 was moderated by feedback-understanding, F(1, 213)=10.17, p<.01."

### Minder Stefan P. 2018 (2)

Minder Stefan P., Weibel David, Wissmath Bartholomäus, Schmitz Felix M. (2018). Do students achieve the desired learning goals using open-book formative assessments? International Journal of Medical Education, 9, 293-301. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5bc6.fead

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The same ANCOVA series found no moderation of the in-depth-knowledge effect; the article reports only the p-value, so no effect size is available.

> "In contrast, the effect of condition on in-depth-knowledge was independent of feedback-understanding (p=.42)."
