`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Qualitative interview study of residents, mentor teachers, site coordinators, and program leaders at three EPP–district partnerships, plus document analysis. The report finds stipends supported engagement "by allowing them to forego outside employment"; no effect size is printed.
