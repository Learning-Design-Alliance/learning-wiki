## Evidence

### Vasilopoulos Fotini 2023

Vasilopoulos Fotini, Jeffrey Holly, Wu Yanwen, Dumontheil Iroise. (2023). Multi-level meta-analysis of whether fostering creativity during physical activity interventions increases their impact on cognitive and academic outcomes during childhood. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-35082-y

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Three-level meta-regressions for fluid intelligence (8 studies, 9 effect sizes, p = 0.32) and on-task behaviour (5 studies, 6 effect sizes, p = 0.62) found no significant association with creativity rating; the on-task subgroup analysis was not run as all studies were in the lower creativity group.

> "There was no association between creativity rating of the interventions and effect size on creativity measures (Table 3). However, all but one intervention assessing creativity as an outcome had high creativity ratings"
