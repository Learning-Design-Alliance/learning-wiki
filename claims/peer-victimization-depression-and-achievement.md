## Evidence

### López-López José A. 2021

López-López José A., Kwong Alex S.F., Washbrook Liz, Tilling Kate, Fazel Mina S., Pearson Rebecca M. (2021). Depressive symptoms and academic achievement in UK adolescents: a cross-lagged analysis with genetic covariates. Journal of Affective Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2021.01.091

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Covariate estimates from RI-CLPM models in the ALSPAC cohort, with peer victimization measured by a modified Bullying and Friendship Interview Schedule at the ten-year clinic visit. The printed values are standardised regression coefficients, not effect sizes. The authors note peer relationships are modifiable, highlighting the importance of monitoring and supporting peer interactions at schools.

> "peer victimization yielded strong positive associations with depressive symptoms for both sexes (0.45, 0.41 to 0.49) as well as a negative association with academic achievement (-0.07, -0.10 to -0.04)."
