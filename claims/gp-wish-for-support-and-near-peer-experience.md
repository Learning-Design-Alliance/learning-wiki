## Evidence

### Gehrke-Beck Sabine 2025

Gehrke-Beck Sabine, Holzhausen Ylva. (2025). Entrustment of student supervision to GP trainees - a qualitative interview study of factors influencing entrustment decisions. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07870-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview theme on GP factors. A GP in a single-handed practice described constant student contact as "extremely exhausting" and welcomed sharing supervision; another GP recalled finding near-peer teaching from someone "just a few steps ahead" best from his own experience.

> "GPs often expressed their wish for support by sharing supervision of students for time relief but also for varying learning opportunities for students."
