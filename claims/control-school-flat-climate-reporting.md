## Evidence

### Nelson 2012

Nelson, L. P., McMahan, S. K., & Torres, T. (2012). The Impact of a Junior High School Community Intervention Project: Moving Beyond the Testing Juggernaut and Into a Community of Creative Learners. School Community Journal, 22(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ974689

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Control-school repeated-measures analysis (Table 2). Social support showed significant change (F = 12.07 and 11.42); all other scales were non-significant (e.g., Optimism F = .643 and 1.90). Control dropout stayed at 1.1%, 1.1%, 1.2% and attendance at 95%, 94%, 94%.

> "The only measure (out of 10 total) to exhibit the same level of significant change in student reporting (p < .001) was within the social support construct, where measure 2 significantly differed from measures 1 and 3"
