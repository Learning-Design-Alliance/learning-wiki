### Chen J 2026

Chen J, Cui J, Li X, Yang J, Yang X, Zhong H, Wang F, Gu Q, Duan C. (2026). Application of a SPOC-based blended teaching system in full-cycle training for medical imaging interns: A randomized controlled educational intervention study. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000049908

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intervention adherence assessed using platform-generated learning records and instructor attendance logs in the experimental group. The "online quiz participation rate was 100%" and mean seminar attendance was 93.3%, with 28 of 30 interns attending all scheduled seminars. The authors state these findings support the feasibility and consistency of intervention implementation.

> "The mean micro-video completion rate was 96.7%, and all interns completed at least 90% of the assigned micro-video modules. The online quiz participation rate was 100%, with all 30 interns completing the 6 scheduled quizzes within the required time window"
