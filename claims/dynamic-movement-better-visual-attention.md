## Evidence

### Yizhen Huang 2023

Yizhen Huang, Eric Richter, Thilo Kleickmann, Katharina Scheiter, Dirk Richter. (2023). Body in motion, attention in focus: A virtual reality study on teachers' movement patterns and noticing. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104912

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear mixed-effects modeling of visual attention performance (number of fixations, time to first fixation, fixation duration) across 42 individual student disruptions, comparing three movement-pattern clusters. The article reports "a significantly higher number of fixations on each student disruption" for the dynamic subgroup.

> "the dynamic pattern subgroup had significantly better visual attention performance than the immobile and anchored subgroups: The dynamic subgroup had a significantly higher number of fixations on each student disruption"
