Growth Mindset Interventions 2022¶

Growth Mindset Interventions. (2022). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, NCEE 2022-006. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q4 · i3 · causal · r?

Cluster randomized controlled trial in seven sections of a developmental mathematics course at a public four-year university in Indiana; the printed effect size is 0.80 (improvement index +29, p = .17), and the WWC applied clustering and multiple-comparison corrections, characterizing the effect as potentially positive because the mean effect size is ≥ 0.25 but not statistically significant.
