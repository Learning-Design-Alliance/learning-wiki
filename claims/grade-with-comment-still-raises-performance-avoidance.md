## Evidence

### Pulfrey 2011

Pulfrey, C., Buchs, C., & Butera, F. (2011). Why grades engender performance-avoidance goals: The mediating role of autonomous motivation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 103(3), 683–700. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023911

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Field experiment with 122 students in 12 classes randomly assigned to grade-only, grade-with-comment, or comment-only assessment of an EFL exercise. The Helmert-coded grade contrast was significant (β = −0.28, p < .001), "with students reporting higher levels of performance-avoidance goals in the grade conditions" than in the comment-only condition.
