Meta-analysis (generic inverse variance in RevMan 5.4) of three studies reporting GOALS Global during laparoscopic cholecystectomy. The pooled mean difference favored AR: "2.40 points higher than controls" with p < 0.001; GRADE rated the evidence moderate quality (MD: 1.53 points; 95% CI [0.48, 2.57]).
