Evidence¶

Constance Steinkuehler 2008¶

Constance Steinkuehler, Sean Duncan. (2008). Scientific Habits of Mind in Virtual Worlds. J Sci Educ Technol. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10956-008-9120-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Coding of the forum corpus for model-based reasoning codes found proportions the authors call "rather low": 11% model-based reasoning, 5% model testing and prediction, 4% mathematical modeling, and 1% explicit computation, exemplified by posts comparing priest and mage scaling factors (0.687 vs 0.968).

"Generally speaking, the proportion of model-based reasoning, model testing and prediction, use of mathematics, and explicit computation (11%, 5%, 4%, 1%, respectively) were rather low; however, the sophistication of arguments that leverage such models warrants consideration."
