### Heppt 2025

Discriminatory Climate and School Adjustment in Ethnically Minoritized Adolescents and Majority Adolescents: An Investigation of the Mediating Role of Teaching Quality. `q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Multilevel regression analyses of the PISA 2018 German Grade 9 sample confirmed Hypothesis 2: discriminatory climate was "a significant negative predictor" of all three outcomes, controlling for gender, age, home language, SES, school track, and classroom-level predictors.

> "Discriminatory climate emerged as a significant negative predictor of reading comprehension (Model 1a in Table 2), reading motivation (Model 1b in Table 4), and sense of school belonging (Model 1c in Table 5), thus confirming Hypothesis 2"
