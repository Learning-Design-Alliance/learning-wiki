### Alvaro Suarez 2017

Alvaro Suarez, Sandra Kahan, Genaro Zavala, and Arturo C. Marti. (2017). Student Conceptual Difficulties in Hydrodynamics. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.13.020132)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Interview problem 3 presented a fast vertical flow with strongly curved streamlines and asked students to compare pressures at two same-height, equal-velocity points. "AllstudentsansweredwithE3.1": most argued equal velocity and height imply equal pressures, ignoring that curved streamlines require a normal pressure gradient.

> "AllstudentsansweredwithE3.1, explainingthetwoar- guments. Those presenting a simpler view of the problem (two interviewees) argued that the pressures at points A andB should be the same, given that "those points are of equal height.""
