## Evidence

### Öz 2018

Öz, H., & Özturan, T. (2018). Computer-based and paper-based testing: Does the test administration mode influence the reliability and validity of achievement tests? Journal of Language and Linguistic Studies, 14(1), 67-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1175793

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pilot study with 9 prospective English teachers who had previously taken the same course; dichotomous scoring and odd-even split-half method yielded r = .90, with printed coefficients of .896 and .902 for the split-half and Spearman-Brown values.

> "The results of the test revealed that the achievement test had very good psychometric properties ( r = .90) in terms of reliability. As for the split -half coefficient which gives the value belonging to the half of the test, and the Spearman -Brown coefficient, which gives the reliability coefficient of the whole test, the coefficient values are .896 and .902 respectively"
