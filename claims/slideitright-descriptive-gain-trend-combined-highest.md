## Evidence

### Chloe Qianhui Zhao 2025

Chloe Qianhui Zhao, Jie Cao, Eason Chen, Kenneth R. Koedinger, and Jionghao Lin. (2025). SlideItRight: Using AI to Find Relevant Slides and Provide Feedback for Open-Ended Questions. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98459-4_27](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98459-4_27)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive comparison of average learning gains across the four conditions of the crowdsourcing study, shown in Fig. 4a; a one-way ANOVA on pre-test scores (F = 1.73, p = 0.167) found no prior-knowledge differences between groups. These are descriptive means with no effect size.

> "the combined approach of slides and AI feedback (14.8%) showed the highest average gain, followed by AI only feedback (13.4%) and slide-only feedback (12.5%), with human feedback (9.49%) showing the lowest average gain."
