## Evidence

### Barnawi Ahlam 2024

Barnawi Ahlam, Sonbol Ahmed M., Al-Shawwa Lana, Abulaban Alwalla, Asiri Khalil, Bagasi Abdulaziz, Alafari Reem, Alamoudi Aliaa Amr. (2024). Employing students' evaluations and tutors' perceptions to evaluate a faculty development program on problem-based learning at the Faculty of Medicine, King Abdulaziz University. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05662-1

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Pearson correlation analysis of the 284-student questionnaire relating tutors' performance rankings to the five learning-approach categories, all significant at p < 0.001. The text states "a significantly positive correlation between tutors' performance rankings and all the learning approaches".

> "The results reveal a significantly positive correlation between tutors' performance rankings and all the learning approaches: constructive/active learning (p < 0.001, r = 0.640), self-directed learning (p < 0.001, r = 0.537), contextual learning (p < 0.001, r = 0.568), collaborative learning (p < 0.001, r = 0.638), and intra-personal behavior (p < 0.001, r = 0.638)."
