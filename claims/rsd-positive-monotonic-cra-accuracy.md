# Relative semantic distance of CRA trials is positively and monotonically related to CRA accuracy

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Trials with greater relative semantic distance between cues and answer yield higher CRA accuracy, with the slope beyond the breakpoint not significant. [→ Xie 2024](#xie-2024)

## Evidence

### Xie 2024

Xie, C., Zhang, S., Qiao, X., & Hao, N. (2024). Transcranial direct current stimulation targeting the bilateral IFG alters cognitive processes during creative ideation. npj Science of Learning, 9, 75. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00285-z

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-effects modeling of trial-level CRA data (RSD computed via Word2Vec over a Tencent corpus) in the tDCS experiment; RSD significantly affected accuracy (F1,1517 = 5.01, p = 0.02), and the authors report "a positive monotonic relationship with the accuracy of CRA responses", while the slope beyond the breakpoint was not significant.

> "Interestingly, our study revealed that the RSD of CRA trials had a positive monotonic relationship with the accuracy of CRA responses."
