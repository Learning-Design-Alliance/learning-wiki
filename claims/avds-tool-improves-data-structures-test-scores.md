## Evidence

### Kogan 2024

Kogan, G., Chassidim, H., & Rabaev, I. (2024). The efficacy of animation and visualization in teaching data structures: a case study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10382-w

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomised experiment: 78 students randomly assigned to experimental (n=39, using the AVDS tool during the test) and control (n=39, without it) groups, on a six-question AVL-tree performance test. The article reports "a significant difference existed in the test scores" with no printed effect size.

> "A significant difference existed in the test scores of the experimental and control groups (see Fig. 5). For the experimental group, the test scores were considerably higher: more than 75% of students who got grades from 80 to 100 belonged to the group that used the AVDS tool."
