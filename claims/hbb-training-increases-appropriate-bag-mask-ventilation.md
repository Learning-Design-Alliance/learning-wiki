## Evidence

### Ratekin Carly Schade 2025

Ratekin Carly Schade, Auko Erick, Wafula Meshack Malenya, Omwanda Wycliffe Okoth, Wamae Jane, Mudhune Sandra A, Rogers Ash, Mbeya Julius, Ouma Frederick Ochieng, Njoki Lillian, Young William, Starnes Joseph R. (2025). Evaluation of the Helping Babies Breathe programme in Migori County, Kenya. BMJ Paediatrics Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2025-003887

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Unadjusted χ² and adjusted logistic regression of 557 pre- and post-training deliveries requiring resuscitation at 26 facilities; table 3 prints OR 0.45 (95% CI 0.25 to 0.81), adjusted p=0.01, for the outcome the article describes as "not appropriately receiving bag and mask ventilation". No standardised effect size is printed.

> "The percentage of neonates that were not breathing at 1 min postdelivery and not appropriately receiving bag and mask ventilation decreased significantly from 12.73% (n=34) in the pretraining period to 6.90% (n=20) in the post-training period."
