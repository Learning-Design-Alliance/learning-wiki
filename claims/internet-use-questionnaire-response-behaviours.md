## Evidence

### Borgonovi Francesca 2025

Borgonovi Francesca, Andrieu Elodie. (2025). The Relationship Between Internet Use, Achievement, and Persistence in Digital Tasks. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.12503

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Country-specific OLS regressions on PISA 2018 background-questionnaire behaviour indicators for 151,092 students, using balanced repeated replication weights. The article reports no differences in "item nonresponse and non‐differentiation"; no effect size is printed for these nulls, so equivalence was not tested.

> "students who have high and low levels of use of the internet do not differ from students with medium levels of use of the internet in terms of item nonresponse and non‐differentiation."

### Borgonovi Francesca 2025 (2)

Borgonovi Francesca, Andrieu Elodie. (2025). The Relationship Between Internet Use, Achievement, and Persistence in Digital Tasks. Journal of Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1002/jad.12503

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

OLS regression on the non-consistency indicator (151,092 students, balanced repeated replication weights), reported in the article's Table 2 analysis. The article prints "a 32% of a SD difference in the non‐consistency indicator" for low versus medium users, and calls the effect not large; the article prints no standardised effect size statistic for this finding, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "being in the low rather than the medium level of use of the internet group is associated with a 32% of a SD difference in the non‐consistency indicator"

### Borgonovi Francesca 2025 (3)
