Cross-sectional anonymous web-based survey of prenatal providers at the University of Pennsylvania Health System (January 2022–January 2023); 97 of 150 invited providers completed the survey. The results section reports "3.8 ± 1.8 out of 8 (47.5%)" as the mean knowledge score, with 20.6% scoring ≥ 75% and 51.6% scoring ≥ 50%.
