Evidence¶

Constantinidis Christos 2019¶

Constantinidis Christos, Luna Beatriz. (2019). Neural Substrates of Inhibitory Control Maturation in Adolescence. Trends in Neurosciences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tins.2019.07.004

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports a developmental MEG study of the antisaccade preparatory period, citing Hwang, in which oscillatory differences corresponded with performance; alpha rhythms are described as supporting inhibitory dampening of neuronal activity.

"Adolescents showed adult level beta-band power in dlPFC but lower Frontal Eye Field (FEF) alpha-band power (Fig. 3A) and beta/alpha dlPFC/FEF cross-frequency coupling (Fig. 3G)."

Related Claims¶

  • In the one longitudinal fMRI study, dorsolateral prefrontal engagement reached adult levels by adolescence while dorsal anterior cingulate engagement continued increasing and mediated behavioral improvement — related
  • Adolescent anticipatory alpha-band activity lacks the adult-like lateralized suppression pattern, instead showing higher right-parieto-occipital alpha and stronger modulation for cue-left than cue-right — related
  • Impending rewards improve adolescent antisaccade performance toward adult levels, accompanied by greater nucleus accumbens activation — related
  • Monkey neurophysiology shows adolescent-to-adult antisaccade improvement reflects enhanced representation of the goal and vector inversion, not stronger filtering of the stimulus — related
  • Preparatory-period baseline activity before the antisaccade stimulus is higher and ramps faster in adult monkeys and predicts correct trials — related
  • Acute exercise increases cortical delta, theta, alpha, and beta EEG power, with a moderately large effect size of 0.38 to 0.75 standard deviations — related
