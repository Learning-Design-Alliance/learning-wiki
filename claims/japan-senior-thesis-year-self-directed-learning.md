### Nathan Hagen 2024

Nathan Hagen. (2024). Graduate education in optics in Japan and the United States: impact of funding levels on educational structure. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2401.08933](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2401.08933)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

The article describes the mandatory Japanese senior thesis year as often a student's first experience with self-directed learning, lab equipment, presentations, and long report writing; some students find the sudden lack of immediate direction a shock and fail to progress.

> "A common feature of science and engineering depart- ments in Japan is that fourth-year undergraduates are re- quired to join a research laboratory and spend a year work- ing on a senior thesis project."

### Nathan Hagen 2024 (2)

Nathan Hagen. (2024). Graduate education in optics in Japan and the United States: impact of funding levels on educational structure. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2401.08933](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2401.08933)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

The comparative survey reports that whereas struggling US students would simply drop out, this is quite rare in Japan, reflecting a difference in education philosophy; the aggressive student attrition of prestigious US institutions is described as utterly absent in Japan.

> "Japanese professors work hard to help the lowest-performing students and go to extraordinary efforts to make sure that they obtain their degree."
