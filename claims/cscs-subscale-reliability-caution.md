### Diana Sachmpazidi 2025

Diana Sachmpazidi, Mike Verostek, Jayna Petrella, Chandra Turpen, and Siwoo (Randy) Lee. (2025). Psychometric Evaluation of the Culture around Systemic Change Survey: A tool for Assessing Departmental Culture in Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2508.09953](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.09953)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bifactor-style reliability analysis of the five CSCS subscales in the N = 111 pilot. The article concludes that "further evidence of subscale reliability should be established before confidently drawing conclusions from individual subscale scores," since high ωt·sub mostly reflects the general factor.

> "The highest omega hierarchical subscale is ωh·OM = 0.40 and the lowest are ωh·SI and ωh·CE , which are equal to 0 .18."
