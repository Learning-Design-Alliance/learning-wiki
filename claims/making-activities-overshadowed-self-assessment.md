Ourania Miliou, Maria Adamou, Aekaterini Mavri, Andri Ioannou. (2024). An exploratory case study of the use of a digital self-assessment tool of 21st-century skills in makerspace contexts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10314-0

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Theme 2 negative finding from teacher reports and student group interviews. One student said, "Sometimes I felt like I was getting distracted from my work," and students reported forgetting about the tool while immersed in making.

> "They argued that the making activities drew the students' attention, overshadowing self-assessment, which sometimes was perceived as an unnecessary interruption."
