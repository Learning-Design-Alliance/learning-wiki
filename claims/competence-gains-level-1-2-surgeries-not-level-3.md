## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Residents reported statistically significant improvement in self-perceived competence for level 1 and level 2 surgeries and for level 3 myomectomies, while level 3 procedures such as subtotal hysterectomy and sacropexy showed no significant change. [→ Fernandes Carla Ferreira Kikuchi 2016](#fernandes-carla-ferreira-kikuchi-2016)

## Evidence

### Fernandes Carla Ferreira Kikuchi 2016

Fernandes Carla Ferreira Kikuchi, Ruano José Maria Cordeiro, Kati Lea Mina, Noguti Alberto Sinhiti, Girão Manoel João Batista Castello, Sartori Marair Gracio Ferreira. (2016). Assessment of laparoscopic skills of Gynecology and Obstetrics residents after a training program. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.1590/S1679-45082016AO3752

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire study of residents before and after training; Table 3 shows significant competence gains for diagnostic laparoscopy (p=0.005), lysis of adhesions (p=0.001), ectopic gestation (p=0.001), oophorectomy (p=0.004), and myomectomy (p=0.053), but not subtotal hysterectomy (p=0.104) or sacropexy (p=0.192).

> "Besides improving skill in laparoscopy, there was a statistical improvement in the perception of competence in performing level 1 and level 2 surgeries, and in level 3 myomectomies (probably due to the training with sutures), as shown on table 3."
