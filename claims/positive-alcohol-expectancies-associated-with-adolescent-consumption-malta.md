`q3 i3` Enhanced social behaviour expectancy scores (Scale 2) correlate significantly with alcohol consumption at pre-intervention (R = 0.47). [→ Pamela Portelli 2018 (2)](#pamela-portelli-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Pamela Portelli 2018

Pamela Portelli. (2018). A clustered randomized controlled trial for the prevention of alcohol misuse among Maltese teenagers. Special Issue Volume 10, Number 1, April 2018, pp 112-132. https://www.um.edu.mt/ijee

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Cross-sectional Spearman correlation analysis at pre-intervention in the cluster RCT (N = 118). The article reports "a statistically significant relationship between Scale 2 AE scores and alcohol consumption" with R = 0.47, p < 0.001, supporting the first hypothesis.

> "Results of Spearman’s rho correlation coefficient (R) revealed a statistically significant relationship between Scale 2 AE scores and alcohol consumption at pre-intervention (R = 0.47, N = 118, p < 0.001)."
