## Subclaims
`q2 i?` A multiple linear regression controlling for country found media use behaviours predicted 8% of variance in self-reported academic performance (n = 1445), with media multitasking the strongest predictor (β = −.16) and instant messaging a positive predictor (β = .13). ## Evidence

### le Roux 2021

le Roux, D.B., Parry, D.A., Totolo, A., Iyawa, G., Holloway, J., Prenter, A., Botha, L. (2021). Media Multitasking, Online Vigilance and Academic Performance among Students in three Southern African Countries. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104056

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey regression of the full sample of undergraduates from three Southern African universities, controlling for country. The article reports the model "predicts 8% of variance in academic performance" with media multitasking the strongest predictor (β = −.16, p < .001).

> "Overall, the model predicts 8% of variance in academic performance (R2 =.08;F (7; 1437) = 18.31,p<. 001), with media multitasking as the strongest predictor (β =−.16,p < .001)."
