Evidence¶

Lasha Labadze 2023¶

Lasha Labadze, Maya Grigolia and Lela Machaidze. (2023). Role of AI chatbots in education: systematic literature review. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00426-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis in the student-advantages section of the systematic review. The review reports chatbots aid "the enhancement of writing skills", foster problem-solving abilities, and facilitate group discussions and debates, citing multiple studies.

"It can aid in the enhancement of writing skills (by offering sugges- tions for syntactic and grammatical corrections) (Kaharuddin, 2021), foster problem- solving abilities (by providing step-by-step solutions) (Benvenuti et al., 2023), and facili- tate group discussions and debates"
