## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Élisabeth Bélanger 2026

Élisabeth Bélanger, Marine Moyon, Lorie-Marlène Brault Foisy, Steve Masson. (2026). Primary School Learning Topics Involving Inhibitory Control: A Scoping Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10198-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Review of behavioral data collected in all 54 experiments using tasks with congruent and incongruent items. The review states "incongruent items were generally associated with longer response times and lower accuracy", with reported interference effects on response time ranging from 175 to 1993 ms.

> "incongruent items were generally associated with longer response times and lower accuracy, patterns that are consistent with the recruitment of inhibitory control."
