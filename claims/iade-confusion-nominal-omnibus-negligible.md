Cochran Q and GEE analyses of confusion rates across 4 LLMs on 100 IADE questions. The article reports the omnibus test "reached nominal significance" with "negligible" effect size and no significant pairwise contrasts.

> "The omnibus test for confusion rates reached nominal significance (Cochran Q3=7.85; P=.049; W=0.03); however, the effect size was negligible, and no pairwise contrast was significant after Holm adjustment in the GEE analysis (Table 3; adjusted P≥.34 in all cases)."
