`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive injury surveillance in the retrospective cohort found "45 stress fractures were recorded (13 in women; 32 in men)" with "an overall incidence of 0.2%". Table 1 shows stress fractures in 0% of the 28-day course and 1.3% of 80-day-course women. The discussion attributes this pattern to longer chronic loading in the 80-day course.

> "A total of 45 stress fractures were recorded (13 in women; 32 in men), with an overall incidence of 0.2%; all but one occurred in the 80-day course, and all but one were lower extremity stress fractures."
