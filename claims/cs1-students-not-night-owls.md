## Evidence

### Albina Zavgorodniaia 2021

Albina Zavgorodniaia, Raj Shrestha, Juho Leinonen, Arto Hellas, John Edwards. (2021). Morning or Evening? An Examination of Circadian Rhythms of CS1 Students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.01752)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive analysis of pooled keystroke timestamps across both contexts: 54% of keystrokes between 9am and 5pm, 27% between 5pm and 9pm, and only 1% between midnight and 5:00am. The authors state this supports prior work finding most programmers follow typical hours.

> "The majority of keystrokes in our study were done between 9am and 5pm (54%) and most of the remaining keystrokes were executed between 5pm and 9pm (27%). Only 1% of keystrokes occurred between midnight and 5:00am."
