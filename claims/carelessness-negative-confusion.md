## Evidence

### Maria Ofelia Z. San Pedro 2014

Maria Ofelia Z. San Pedro, Ryan S. J. d. Baker, & Ma. Mercedes T. Rodrigo. (2014). Carelessness and Affect in an Intelligent Tutoring System for Mathematics. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0015-y

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the same Philippine Cognitive Tutor dataset. The article reports confusion was "negatively correlated with carelessness" in both conditions, with r=−0.38 and r=−0.32, which the authors deemed reasonable since confusion signals not knowing a skill.

> "Confusion was also negatively correlated with carelessness, r=−0.38, p=0.002 for NoScooter group; r=−0.32, p=0.011 for Scooter group."
