## Evidence

### Mascio R 2026

Mascio R, Lynch S, Phillips J, Best M. (2026). Predictors of nurses’ spiritual care competence: A replication study with Australian palliative care nurses. Palliative & Supportive Care. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1478951526102119

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 21 Australian palliative care nurses; Mann–Whitney tests compared combined model groups after A/B and C/D were merged because too few respondents occupied the A and D cells. The article reports "higher levels of palliative care experience (Z = 2.80, p = 0.003)" and the other printed statistics; no effect sizes were reported.

> "The Model C and D combined group was associated with higher levels of palliative care experience (Z = 2.80, p = 0.003), greater frequency of spiritual care provision (Z = 2.20, p = 0.03), and higher personal spirituality (Z = 2.21, p = 0.02) than the Model A and B combined group."
