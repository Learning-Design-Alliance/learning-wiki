## Evidence

### Meinsma 2026

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Mediation analysis (jAMM) with perceived comprehension as mediator and control as reference: receiving either explanation type significantly decreased perceived comprehension, β = -0.12 (non-metaphorical) and β = -0.11 (metaphorical).

> "We found a small but statistically significant decrease in perceived comprehension when participants received the non-metaphorical (β = -0.12, p <.001) or metaphorical explanations (β = -0.11, p < .001), compared to the control group."

### Meinsma 2026 (2)

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Same mediation models: the indirect effects of explanation type on affect- and cognition-based attitudes through perceived comprehension were "statistically significant (p < .001) but very small", with β's between -0.07 and -0.05; direct and total effects were non-significant.

> "Given these two findings, the indirect effects found between explanation type and affect or cognition, with perceived comprehension as the mediator, were statistically significant (p < .001) but very small (β’s ranged between -0.07 and -0.05)."

### Meinsma 2026 (3)

Meinsma, A. L., Reijnierse, W. G., & Cramer, J. (2026). Like a coin spinning in the air: the effect of (non-)metaphorical explanations on comprehension and attitudes towards quantum technology. PREPRINT. Originally published 2025. [doi:10.1075/msw.25018.mei](https://doi.org/10.1075/msw.25018.mei)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Path b in the same mediation models: higher perceived comprehension of the news article went with a statistically significant increase in affect (β = 0.55) and cognition (β = 0.46) attitudes towards quantum technology.

> "We furthermore found a strong statistically significant increase in both affect (β = 0.55, p < .001) and cognition (β = 0.46, p < .001) when participants scored higher on perceived comprehension."
