## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Transfer of Learning in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05655)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

Written and interview research on quantum measurement shows "many students believe that the measurements of all physical observable, e.g., energy, will collapse the wave function into a position eigenstate".

> "Our research shows that many students believe that the measurements of all physical observable, e.g., energy, will collapse the wave function into a position eigenstate (a delta function in position)."
