# Medical students prioritize medical competence over teaching competence

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In only one of the four included studies targeting students' opinions was competence in teaching rated among the top 5 attributes of a good doctor; students' focus was on medical competence and general interpersonal qualities. [→ Steiner-Hofbauer Verena 2017](#steiner-hofbauer-verena-2017)

## Evidence

### Steiner-Hofbauer Verena 2017

Steiner-Hofbauer Verena, Schrank Beate, Holzinger Anita. (2017). What is a good doctor? Wiener Medizinische Wochenschrift. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10354-017-0597-8

`q2 · i?`

Synthesis of the four included student-targeted studies (e.g. Maudsley et al. 2007, Gillies et al. 2009, Pfeiffer et al. 2011, Cuesta-Briand et al. 2014). The review reports that even for students, who are directly affected by teaching skills, "medical competence" is rated higher than teaching competence.

> "Studies which targeted students’ opinions showed that in only one of the included 4 studies “competence in teaching” (being a good teacher) was rated under the top 5 attributes of a good doctor."
