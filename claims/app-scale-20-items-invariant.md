The article reports, citing Dalton and colleagues' work, the Rasch-analysis validation of the APP scale for assessing entry-level competence of physiotherapy students on clinical placements. This is a second-hand report of the underlying study.

> "All 20 items were invariant, indicating that the scale performed comparably across populations regardless of contextual factors related to the student, clinician, educator, or placement setting."
