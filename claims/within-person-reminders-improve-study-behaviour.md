The review reports two recent within-person experimental studies (citing Breitwieser et al. 2022, 2023) that manipulated SR using technology to remind students of their implementation intentions. On reminder days students were "more likely to achieve their self-set study goals or engage in study activities". No effect size is printed.

> "On days when students received reminders, they were more likely to achieve their self-set study goals or engage in study activities than on days without such reminders."
