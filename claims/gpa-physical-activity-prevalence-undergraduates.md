Log-binomial regression results (Table 3) from the Spring 2023 ACHA-NCHA III national dataset, adjusted for year in school, sex/gender, race/ethnicity, and BMI, clustered by institution; B-GPA students also showed higher prevalence (aerobic 14% higher, p = .009; muscle strengthening 24% higher, p = .002; athletics 50% higher, p = .005).
