`q2 i?` In multivariable logistic regression, underweight (WAZ) was significantly associated with academic performance, with underweight students 37% less likely to score high. [→ Asmare Biachew 2018](#asmare-biachew-2018)

## Evidence

### Asmare Biachew 2018

Asmare Biachew, Taddele Mekuanint, Berihun Sileshi, Wagnew Fasil. (2018). Nutritional status and correlation with academic performance among primary school children, northwest Ethiopia. BMC Research Notes. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-018-3909-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Same multivariable logistic regression on the 436-child cross-sectional sample. Underweight (WAZ) reached significance at p = 0.003 with AOR = 0.63 (95% CI 0.26–0.84); the article states underweight students were "37% less likely to score high academic performance compared with their counterparts". No standardised effect size is printed, so no magnitude is coded.

> "Students whose nutritional status had under-weight were 37% less likely to score high academic performance compared with their counterparts"
