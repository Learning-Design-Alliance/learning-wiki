## Evidence

### Sunny BS 2018

Sunny BS, DeStavola B, Dube A, Kondowe S, Crampin AC, Glynn JR. (2018). Does early linear growth failure influence later school performance? A cohort study in Karonga district, northern Malawi. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0200380

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Comparison of persistently stunted children with improvers (stunted early but not late) using multinomial logistic regression adjusted for confounders including early-childhood HAZ. The article reports "the risk of being overage for grade by the age of 11 for those persistently stunted was four-fold (p<0.01)".

> "Compared to 'improvers', the risk of being overage for grade by the age of 11 for those persistently stunted was four-fold (p<0.01), even after adjusting for confounders, including HAZ in early childhood. Effects on enrolment and grade repetition were smaller with very weak statistical evidence of association."
