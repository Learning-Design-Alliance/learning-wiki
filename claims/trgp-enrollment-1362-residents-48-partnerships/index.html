Analysis of program-reported administrative data on TRGP-funded residents from the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing for SYs 2019-20 through 2022-23. The brief reports "a total of 1,362 TRGP-funded residents enrolled in teacher residency programs in California, spanning 48 funded partnerships" and notes a 44 percent enrollment increase by year 4.

"Between the 2019–20 and 2022–23 school years (SYs), a total of 1,362 TRGP-funded residents enrolled in teacher residency programs in California, spanning 48 funded partnerships"
