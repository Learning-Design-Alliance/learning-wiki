Tanji Reed Marshall 2023¶

Tanji Reed Marshall, Ph.D., and William H. Rodick, Ph.D. (2023). The Search for More: Complex Racial and Ethnic Representation in Grade School Books. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/booktool

q2 · i? · design · r2

Character-identity analysis of the same 300-book sample. The report counts centered people (real or fictional), finding "73 books center White people" and 97 center people of color; Figure 2 states White people are featured in more books than 5 other groups combined.

"Of the 300 books in our sample, 73 books center White people. Meanwhile, about one-third, or 97, center any people of color."
