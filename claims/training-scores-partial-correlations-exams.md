`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Preliminary partial correlational analyses in Study 1 (n = 49) compared training scores to the second and final exam controlling for pretest score. The article reports that "Partial correlations revealed that training of cognitive strategies, metacognitive strategies, and behavioral and environmental regulation strategies significantly related to the second exam and final exam performance."
