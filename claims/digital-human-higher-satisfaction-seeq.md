## Evidence

### Ding Kewen 2024

Ding Kewen, Chen Mingjing, Li Ping, Xie Zichuan, Zhang Haorong, Kou Ruixing, Xu Jionghui, Zou Ting, Luo Zhen, Song Haibo. (2024). The effect of simulation of sectional human anatomy using ultrasound on students' learning outcomes and satisfaction in echocardiography education: a pilot randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05337-x

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Modified Student Evaluation of Educational Quality (SEEQ) questionnaire (Cronbach's alpha 0.755) administered anonymously after the intervention in the pilot RCT. The digital group scored significantly higher on most items, with the largest differences in group-interaction items (P < 0.001).

> "Student satisfaction ratings revealed that the digital group experienced significantly greater satisfaction in areas such as subject interest, teaching style, course alignment, and interaction compared to the traditional group."
