## Evidence

### Rudolph 2013

Rudolph, A. L. (2013). Do You Always Need a Textbook to Teach Astro 101?. Astronomy Education Review. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.3635](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.3635)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The SPCI was administered only in the Spring 2011 optional-textbook class and compared descriptively to a published national survey sample; no statistical test between the two is reported, and the article says the comparison "compares favorably".

> "The class-averaged normalized gain was <g> = 0.41 (N = 59), which compares favorably with the results of a national survey of SPCI scores, which had an average of <g> = 0.30 (N = 334)."
