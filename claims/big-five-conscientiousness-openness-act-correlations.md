## Evidence

### Yoon Hee Jun 2024

Yoon Hee Jun, Roberts Brent W., Sewell Madison N., Napolitano Christopher M., Soto Christopher J., Murano Dana, Casillas Alex. (2024). Examining SEB skills' incremental validity over personality traits in predicting academic achievement. PLOS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0296484

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the same high school ACT sample using the 44-item BFI. The article reports Conscientiousness "(r = .17, p < .05)" and Openness "(r = .14, p < .05)" as the strongest trait correlations with ACT scores.

> "several of the Big Five traits were also positively associated with the ACT composite score, with Conscientiousness (r = .17, p < .05) and Openness (r = .14, p < .05) showing the strongest correlations."
