# Knowledge gains occur in all conditions; collaboration shows no significant advantage, and the no-tool control group outperforms both tool conditions on interrelations and the annotation condition on transfer

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` With the control group included, the control condition outperformed both tool conditions on understanding of interrelations, and outperformed the annotation condition (not the hyperlink condition) on transfer knowledge. [→ Ruf 2023](#ruf-2023)

## Evidence

### Ruf 2023

Ruf, A., Zahn, C., Roos, A.-L., & Opwis, K. (2023). How do enhanced videos support generative learning and conceptual understanding in individuals and groups? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10275-4

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

MANOVA including the control group on the three conceptual-understanding measures; Tukey post-hocs showed control > annotation on interrelations (p = 0.022) and transfer (p = 0.010) and control > hyperlinks on interrelations (p = 0.003).

> "Post-hoc univariate ANOVAs revealed significance for the variables 'understanding of interrelations', F(2,137) = 6.530, p = 0.002, partial η2 = 0.087, and 'transfer knowledge', F(2,137) = 4.294, p = 0.016, partial η2 = 0.059, both indicating that the control group outperformed the two other conditions"
