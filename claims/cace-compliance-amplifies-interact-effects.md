`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

CACE mixture-model analyses of the cluster RCT, classifying compliers by a binary marker combining completed coaching cycles (dosage) and fidelity scores. The article reports effects "increased... to medium to large" for TSI and "small to medium" for emotional and behavioral engagement; for this intervention type these are considered large.

> "Multilevel complier average causal effect (CACE) estimation using a combined dosage and ﬁdelity compliance marker increased the effect sizes for the TSI domains to medium to large, and small to medium effects were documented for emotional and behavioral engagement according to Cohen 's standards."
