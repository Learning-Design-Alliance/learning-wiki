### Xiong Yuan 2026

Xiong Yuan, Wang Mingjun. (2026). Psychological resilience as a selective perceptual amplifier in post-crisis learning: a dual-pathway model of academic motivation, perceived school support, and cognitive engagement – a multi-country study based on PISA 2022. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04665-5

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Design-corrected regression on PISA 2022 data (N = 574,514) with complex sampling weights and 80 replicate weights, controlling for ESCS. The direct path from academic motivation to cognitive engagement strategies was significant: "β = 0.089, SE = 0.003, t = 32.95, p < .001". No standardized effect size beyond β was printed, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).
