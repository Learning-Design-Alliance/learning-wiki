## Evidence

### Danni Chen 2024

Danni Chen, Tao Xia, Ziqing Yao, Lingqi Zhang & Xiaoqing Hu. (2024). Modulating social learning-induced evaluation updating during human sleep. npj Science of Learning 9:43. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00255-5

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Overnight ANOVA on change from post-learning to post-TMR: "the difference between higher vs. lower feedback directions further enlarged" across sleep, F(1, 33) = 4.72, p = 0.037, η²G = 0.03. Contrary to the hypotheses, neither the TMR effect nor the TMR by feedback interaction was significant.
