Stephan Lorenzen, Niklas Hjuler, Stephen Alstrup. (2019). Tracking Behavioral Patterns among Students in an Online Educational System. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1908.08937](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1908.08937)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

WNMF clustering of weekly activity features (f1-f10) from Clio Online logs; the article reports cluster C2's feature profile descriptively, noting students "seem to both have fairly long average session length and high performance". No effect size is printed.

> "Students in this cluster spend a lot of time taking quizzes ( f5). They will spend some time during school hours ( f1) and some time working with language subjects ( f6). Furthermore, students in this cluster seem to both have fairly long average session length and high performance ( f9 and f10)."
