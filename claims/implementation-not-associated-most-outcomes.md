## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Implementation level was not significantly associated with the remaining SCCS scales (student and staff) or with any AK DEED administrative outcomes including test scores, discipline, attendance, graduation, and dropout. [→ Boal 2025](#boal-2025)

## Evidence

### Boal 2025

Boal, A., McCullough, S., & Chin, A. (2025). Exploring implementation of trauma-engaged practices in Alaska schools (REL 2025–011). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Northwest. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/rel/Products/RWR/Publication/108318

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Series of mixed-effects multilevel regressions on 2021/22 SCCS and AK DEED administrative data found no significant associations beyond the two climate scales; the report adds that implementation level "was not significantly associated with any AK DEED administrative outcomes." Equivalence was not tested.

> "Implementation level was not significantly associated with the remaining scales on the elementary and secondary school versions of the SCCS or with any scales on the staff version of the SCCS."
