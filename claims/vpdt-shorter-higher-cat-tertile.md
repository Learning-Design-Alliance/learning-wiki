## Evidence

### de Burlet K 2025

de Burlet K, Tranter-Entwistle I, Tan J, Lin A, Rajaratnam S, Connor S, Eglinton T. (2025). Vascular pedicle dissection time in laparoscopic colectomies as a novel marker of surgical skill: a prospective cohort study. Techniques in Coloproctology 29:82. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10151-025-03121-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Prospective multicentre video-based cohort study; two blinded assessors scored each video with the CAT and median scores were grouped into tertiles. The Results report the right-hemicolectomy VPDT contrast across tertiles, "p = 0.036"; no effect size is printed.

> "For patients undergoing a right hemicolectomy, the median VPDT in the lower tertile was 17 min (10–24), in the middle tertile 13 min (11–16) and in the higher tertile 11 min (8–14), p = 0.036 (Fig. 2)."
