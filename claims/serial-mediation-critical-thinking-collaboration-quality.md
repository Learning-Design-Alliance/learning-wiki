# Critical thinking and human–AI collaboration quality jointly form a significant serial indirect pathway from GenAI dependence to research creativity, larger for functional than existential dependence

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The serial indirect effect through critical thinking then human–AI collaboration quality is significant for functional dependence (Ind3 = 0.0175, 95% CI [0.0072, 0.0301]). [→ Yin S 2026](#yin-s-2026)
`q2 i?` The serial indirect effect is also significant for existential dependence (Ind3 = 0.0062, 95% CI [0.0012, 0.0128]) but smaller. [→ Yin S 2026 (2)](#yin-s-2026-2)
