Descriptive analysis of state ECE enrollment and eligibility data reported in the Access to Care section. The report states that in 2015–16 "only 33% of children under age 5 who qualified" were served, and that nearly 650,000 children birth to age 5 lack access to programs for which they are eligible.

> "In 2015–16, only 33% of children under age 5 who qualified for one of California's publicly funded ECE programs—based on family income and having working parents—were served."
