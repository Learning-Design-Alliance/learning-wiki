# Dual n-back task training has a positive and significant effect on the expansion of phonological memory capacity in adult beginner-level EFL learners

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Adult beginner EFL learners who trained on the dual n-back task for six weeks showed significantly greater phonological memory capacity expansion than untrained controls. [→ Farvardin et al. 2014](#farvardin-et-al-2014)

## Evidence

### Farvardin et al. 2014

Mohammad Taghi Farvardin, Akbar Afghari & Mansour Koosha. (2014). The Effect of Dual N-Back Task Training on Phonological Memory Expansion in Adult EFL Learners at the Beginner Level. English Language Teaching, 7(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n9p137

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized experiment with 126 adult beginner EFL learners assigned to control and experimental groups; experimental groups trained on the dual n-back task four days per week for six weeks. Independent samples t-tests at Time 2 showed "a statistically significant difference between the control and experimental groups" on both PM tests, with experimental means higher (NWRP 16.98 vs 15.50; NWRC 12.30 vs 10.30).

> "According to Table 2, there is a statistically significant difference between the control and experimental groups on both NWRP (t = -4.43, p < .05) and NWRC (t = -5.70, p < .05) tests."
