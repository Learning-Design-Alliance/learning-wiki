## Evidence

### Rienties 2026

Rienties, Bart; Coughlan, Tim; Domingue, John and Herodotou, Christothea (2026). New systems of learning for distance learning institutions? A six-study review of implementing AIDA. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100607

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Study 6 randomised control trial on two short non-credit OpenLearn Create courses; 50 participants completed the post-test (33 experimental, 17 control) with three-item self-assessment knowledge measures. The article states: "given the relatively small sample of volunteers who completed the post-test and the imbalance between the two conditions we would caution about these preliminary results."

> "When combining the knowledge tests across the two courses a higher learning gain in the experimental AIDA group (n = 33) relative to the control group (n = 17) was present (F = 4.796, p < .05)"
