### Liu Zeqi 2024

Liu Zeqi, Yao Qinqi, Wang Peicheng, Shen Lijun, Li Hange, Zhang Jingfeng, Jiang Maoqing, Yang Zhenghan, Wang Zhenchang, Zheng Jianjun, Zhu Jiming, Wu You. (2024). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression in the nationwide survey identified smoking, sleep and physical activity as independently associated with occupational burnout (prevalence 25.78%), with "the effect of alcohol consumption was non-significant"; ORs compare worst to healthiest states.

> "The OR of smoking, alcohol consumption, sleep and physical activity factors were 0.60 (95% CI = [0.40, 0.89], 1.23) (95% CI = [0.09, 17.59]), 7.69 (95% CI = [4.23, 14.67]), and 6.24 (95% CI = [1.33, 29.37]), respectively"
