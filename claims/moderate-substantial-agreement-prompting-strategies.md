`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Question-by-question agreement analysis across three prompting strategies on five PES sessions. The article reports "moderate to substantial concordance between prompting strategies" with the highest pairwise Cohen's κ = 0.632.

> "Agreement analysis demonstrated moderate to substantial concordance between prompting strategies. The highest agreement was observed between the Professor and Resident prompts (Cohen's κ = 0.632), whereas agreement between the Specialist prompt and the remaining strategies was lower."
