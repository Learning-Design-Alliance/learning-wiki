### Karan Taneja 2025

Karan Taneja, Anjali Singh, and Ashok K. Goel. (2025). Towards a Multimodal Document-grounded Conversational AI System for Education. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-98462-4_12](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-98462-4_12)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Section 5.2 reports paired comparisons of self-rated difficulty (1-10), question counts and time across the two randomly assigned transfer problems. Time spent was also greater for ANA (t=3.737, p<0.001; M=20.47 vs M=18.50 minutes).

> "ANA (M=6.67, SD=1.19) was considered far more difficult (t=4.668, p<0.001) than INC (M=4.83, SD=1.53). This likely explains a higher number of questions asked (t=2.469, p=0.019) for solving ANA problem (M=6.07, SD=2.48) compared to INC (M=4.97, SD=2.55)."
