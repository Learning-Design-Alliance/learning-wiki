Evidence¶

Dawei Xie 2026¶

Dawei Xie, Khalil Anderson, Tochukwu Eze, Chenghong Lin, Bookyung Shin, and Marcelo Worsley. (2026). CLARA: An AI-Augmented Analytics Dashboard for Collaboration Literacy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17259 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.17259

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Blind rating experiment in which four external education raters evaluated 46 agent responses (21 matched full-vs-baseline pairs plus 4 artifact-specific queries) on five 5-point Likert dimensions. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests showed the full agent significantly higher on four of five dimensions and overall; the article reports "r=.792,p=.001".

"The overall comparison was significant (r=.792,p=.001), with the full agent averaging 4.24 and the baseline 3.67 on the 5-point scale."

Dawei Xie 2026 (2)¶

Dawei Xie, Khalil Anderson, Tochukwu Eze, Chenghong Lin, Bookyung Shin, and Marcelo Worsley. (2026). CLARA: An AI-Augmented Analytics Dashboard for Collaboration Literacy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17259

q2 · i? · causal · r2

In the same paired rating study, accuracy showed no statistically significant difference between conditions; the article attributes the artifact advantage to depth and grounding of analysis rather than factual correctness. Equivalence was not tested.

"Accuracy did not differ significantly between conditions (r=.476,p=.054)."
