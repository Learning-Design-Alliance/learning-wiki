# Residents' confidence in managing paediatric and neonatal emergencies does not differ by residency year or prior SBMT exposure

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Reported confidence to manage paediatric emergencies did not differ between I–II year residents (median 4) and III–IV–V year residents (median 4, p = 0.737), nor by prior SBMT exposure (p = 0.803). [→ Binotti Marco 2019](#binotti-marco-2019)
`q2 i?` Confidence for neonatal emergencies likewise showed no difference by year (p = 0.202) or prior SBMT exposure (p = 0.901). [→ Binotti Marco 2019 (2)](#binotti-marco-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Binotti Marco 2019

Binotti Marco, Genoni Giulia, Rizzollo Stefano, De Luca Marco, Carenzo Luca, Monzani Alice, Ingrassia Pier Luigi. (2019). Simulation-based medical training for paediatric residents in Italy: a nationwide survey. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1581-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey analysis of perceived preparation to manage emergencies, expressed as median (IQR): paediatric 4 (3–6), neonatal 5 (4–7). No statistically significant difference by year of residency or prior SBMT exposure; equivalence was not tested.

> "the median value (IQR) was 4 (3–5) in residents of the I-II year of residency vs 4 (3–6) in residents of the III-IV-V year for the management of paediatric emergencies (p = 0.737)"

### Binotti Marco 2019 (2)
