### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). A good diagram is valuable despite the choice of a mathematical approach to problem solving. 2013 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.inv.006

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Benchmarking task in which 26 first-year physics graduate students in a TA training course solved the quiz problem; the article reports that "76% of them elected to draw a diagram to solve the problem" despite the equations being provided.

> "we found that 76% of them elected to draw a diagram to solve the problem and completely ignored the equations provided to them, thus confirming our hypothesis that experts are more likely to follow the diagrammatic than the mathematical approach"
