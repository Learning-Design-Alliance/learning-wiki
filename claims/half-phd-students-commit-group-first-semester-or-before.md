## Evidence

### Mike Verostek 2024

Mike Verostek, Casey W. Miller, Benjamin M. Zwickl. (2024). Modeling when and how physics PhD students search for a research group: the role of interests and prior research experiences in timely group integration. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Semi-structured interviews with 40 first- and second-year physics PhD students from 13 institutions asked students to construct timelines of their group search. The study reports "N = 18" committed "during or before their first semester," with 1 in senior year, 7 in senior summer, and 10 in first semester.

> "Nearly half of the physics PhD students in our sample (N = 18) had committed to a research group during or before their first semester of graduate school (see Table II). One student committed to a group during her senior year of undergraduate study"
