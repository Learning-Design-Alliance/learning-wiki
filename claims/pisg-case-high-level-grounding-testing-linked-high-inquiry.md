Graphical coding analysis (Keefer et al.) of one PISG group's complete discourse in the field trip and post-field trip: "three occurrences of high-level grounding (GH) and five occurrences of high-level testing (TH) which led to five occurrences of high-level generation of evidence (GEH)" plus two EEH and one DCH.
