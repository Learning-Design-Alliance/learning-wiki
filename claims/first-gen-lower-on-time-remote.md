## Evidence

### Zhongzhou Chen and Tom Zhang 2021

Zhongzhou Chen and Tom Zhang. (2021). Analyzing the Heterogeneous Impact of Remote Learning on Students' Ability to Stay on Track During the Pandemic. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2108.00601](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2108.00601)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Mann-Whitney U tests comparing on-time submission fractions between demographic groups across semesters (Table II): first-generation vs non-first-generation significant in FA20 (p = 0.03) and FA20 first 41 modules (p = 0.03), not in SP20 (p = 0.69). Figure 3 shows 75% of non-first-generation but about 65% of first-generation students submitted more than 87% on time. No effect size printed.

> "In FA20, ﬁrst generation students were found to have submitted a signiﬁcantly lower fraction of modules on-time compared to non-ﬁrst generation students. The diﬀerence is still signiﬁcant for the ﬁrst 41 modules in FA20, but not for the same OLMs in SP20."
