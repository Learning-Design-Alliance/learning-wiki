Negative binomial regression analysis of retrospective school absenteeism data among 2682 participants with retrospective data, adjusting for school clustering, intervention arm and confounders at Levels 1–4. Findings as printed, including one verbatim fragment: participants with pain at LMP "missed more days of school overall" and "missed almost 3 times as many school days during menstruation" (aIRR = 1.46 and 2.89 as printed; no standardised effect size reported).
