## Evidence

### Ahmad 2016

Ahmad, S. Z. (2016). The Flipped Classroom Model to Develop Egyptian EFL Students’ Listening Comprehension. English Language Teaching, 9(9). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v9n9p166

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

A one-group pre-posttest quasi-experimental study with 34 third-year EFL majors at Suez University. The paired-samples t-test showed a statistically significant difference (t=11.341, p<0.05) with a "large" effect size of 1.30, favoring the posttest.

> "Paired-samples t-test was used to test the difference between the means of scores of the participants on the pretest and the posttest of listening comprehension. This difference was statistically significant (t=11.341, p<0.05)"
