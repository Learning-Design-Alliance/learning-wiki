### Yao Liwen 2021

Yao Liwen, Liu Jun, Wu Lianlian, Zhang Lihui, Hu Xiao, Liu Jinzhu, Lu Zihua, Gong Dexin, An Ping, Zhang Jun, Hu Guiying, Chen Di, Luo Renquan, Hu Shan, Yang Yanning, Yu Honggang. (2021). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Predefined secondary endpoints of the same randomized trial of 16 endoscopists across 1,191 colonoscopies. Advanced ADR "improved significantly in the feedback group (4.4%–8.7%, P = 0.04"; PDR "increased from 40.6% to 53.3%" with OR 1.761, with no control-group increase.

> "Advanced ADR also improved significantly in the feedback group (4.4%–8.7%, P = 0.04, OR 0.96, 95% CI 0.939–0.982). PDR in feedback group endoscopists increased from 40.6% to 53.3% (P < 0.01; OR 1.761, 95% CI 1.030–5.237) while no increase was observed in the control group"
