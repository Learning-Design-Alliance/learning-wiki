`q3 i?` At 6 months after discharge, positive FaMM subscale scores slightly increased in the STOFMS group while decreasing in the control group, and negative subscale scores moved in the opposite direction (p < 0.05). [→ Liu Hua-yan 2024](#liu-hua-yan-2024)

## Evidence

### Liu Hua-yan 2024

Liu Hua-yan, Zeng Shan, Chen Yue-wei, Yi Min, Tan Xiao-yan, Xie Jian-hui, Wu Xia, Zhu Li-hui. (2024). A simulation training of family management for parents of children with epilepsy: a randomized clinical trial. Italian Journal of Pediatrics. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-024-01646-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of FaMM scores in a randomized controlled trial of 384 children with epilepsy and their parents (191 control, 193 STOFMS). The article reports "slightly increased in the STOFMS group" at T2 with p < 0.05; no effect size is printed.

> "At T2, the positive scoring decreased in the control group, and slightly increased in the STOFMS group (p < 0.05), while the negative scoring increased in the control group, and slightly decreased in the STOFMS group (p < 0.05)."
