## Evidence

### Fracchiolla 2020

Fracchiolla, C., Prefontaine, B., and Hinko, K. (2020). Community of Practice: A framework for understanding identity development within informal physics programs. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020115](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.16.020115)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative contextual analysis of the PCA program drawing on website information, conversations with facilitators and staff, session observations, and two authors' program involvement. Members share "a domain of increasing excitement and exploration of physics", the community comprises facilitators and weekly youth participants, and the practice is a range of hands-on physics activities; consistent weekly grouping allows long-term rapport and growth.

> "we can identify the three essential aspects of a CoP- domain, community, and practice- within PCA."
