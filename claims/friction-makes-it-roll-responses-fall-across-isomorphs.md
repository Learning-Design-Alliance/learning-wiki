`q3 i?` Category 2 responses (higher friction implies higher final speed because friction makes the object roll) dropped from 27% to 4% across the two isomorphic problems. [→ Chandralekha Singh 2016](#chandralekha-singh-2016)

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Assessing Student Expertise in Introductory Physics with Isomorphic Problems, Part I: Performance on Non-intuitive Problem Pair from Introductory Physics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.06499](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.06499)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Cross-problem comparison of categorized written responses in two calculus-based introductory physics courses. The article reports Category 2 responses "decreased from 27% for Pr oblem (1) to 4% for Problem (2)", which the authors read as evidence some students analyzed the problem deeply.

> "the response in Category (2) suggesting “a higher µ implies a higher vf because friction is responsible for making the object roll” decreased from 27% for Pr oblem (1) to 4% for Problem (2)"
