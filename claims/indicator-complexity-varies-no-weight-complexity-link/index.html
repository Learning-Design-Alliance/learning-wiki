Evidence¶

Mitchell P. Herz 2023¶

Mitchell P. Herz, Lamar L. Johnson, and Eric W. Crane. (2023). Accountability for Postsecondary Readiness: A 50-State Analysis. Region 15 Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED633301

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Count analysis of postsecondary readiness measures across the 40 state plans where the number could be determined, shown in Figure 3. The analysis found "plans averaged just under seven measures within the indicator," with the stated distribution across five-plus and ten-plus thresholds.

"plans averaged just under seven measures within the indicator. Twenty-nine plans (73%) reflected five or more measures, and ten plans (25%) included ten or more."

Mitchell P. Herz 2023 (2)¶

Mitchell P. Herz, Lamar L. Johnson, and Eric W. Crane. (2023). Accountability for Postsecondary Readiness: A 50-State Analysis. Region 15 Comprehensive Center at WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED633301

q2 · i? · associational · r2

A scatterplot analysis (Figure 4) of indicator weight by number of measures across states. The authors report "there is no apparent relationship between the two," highlighting Louisiana, Michigan, and North Dakota as illustrative cases; this is an observational pattern, not a tested statistical relationship.

"While there was significant variance in both the weighting and the number of measures included in the indicators, there is no apparent relationship between the two (see Figure 4)."
