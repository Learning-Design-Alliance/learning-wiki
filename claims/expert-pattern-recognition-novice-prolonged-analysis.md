## Evidence

### Charlotte E. Wolff 2020

Charlotte E. Wolff, Halszka Jarodzka, Henny P.A. Boshuizen. (2020). Classroom Management Scripts: a Theoretical Model Contrasting Expert and Novice Teachers' Knowledge and Awareness of Classroom Events. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09542-0

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

This is the article's theoretical characterization (drawing on Eraut's mode-of-cognition account) of expertise differences in processing time. Novices, lacking knowledge structures for speedy assessment, revert to prolonged analysis and delayed interpretation of isolated pieces of information, missing other relevant information.

> "Experts' assessing of classroom situations involves almost immediate pattern recognition, leading to rapid interpretations, and their scripts inform the immediate decision-making that is instantaneous and visceral, arising without deliberative thought"
