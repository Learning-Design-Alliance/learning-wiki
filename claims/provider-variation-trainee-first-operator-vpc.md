### Chan Jeremy 2026

Chan Jeremy, Comanici Maria, Narayan Pradeep, Dong Tim, Angelini Gianni D. (2026). Trainee exposure to valve surgery in the UK: safety, decline and organisational inequality in a national cohort. Open Heart. https://doi.org/10.1136/openhrt-2026-004389

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Three-level multilevel logistic regression on the national audit cohort (operations nested in consultants within hospitals) with trainee first-operator status as the dependent variable; the quote prints the volume-quartile odds ratios. VPCs of 59.6%, 57.1% and 44.8% are reported in the same results section.

> "Consultant volume was independently associated with trainee first-operator status in AVR, with the highest consultant volume quartile having higher odds than the lowest quartile (OR 1.74, 95% CI 1.17 to 2.58; p=0.006). In MVr, hospital volume showed the strongest association, with higher odds in the second hospital volume quartile compared with the lowest quartile (OR 4.64, 95% CI 1.44 to 14.96; p=0.010)."
