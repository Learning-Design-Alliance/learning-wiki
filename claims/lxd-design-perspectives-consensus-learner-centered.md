## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Expert panelists reached consensus that LXD is human-centered, considers how learner perceptions influence performance, accounts for learner differences, and concerns the human experience of interacting with learning environments. [→ Tawfik 2024](#tawfik-2024)

## Evidence

### Tawfik 2024

Tawfik, A., Schmidt, M., Payne, L., & Huang, R. (2024). Advancing understanding of learning experience design: refining and clarifying definitions using an eDelphi study approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10355-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

eDelphi study of 16 learning experience designers; consensus defined as IQR ≤ 1, SD ≤ 1, and 80% agreement. Items on human-centered design (Q5) reached "Agreement = 93%" and learner perceptions (Q3) "Agreement = 93%".

> "Experts reached consensus with the general question as to how LXD “is used to describe design practice” (Q1) (IQR = 1.00, STDV = 0.85, Agreement = 80%). A more detailed analysis suggests areas of agreement for design perspective appear to (a) situate the learner at the forefront of design and (b) describe what is included as part of the design process"
