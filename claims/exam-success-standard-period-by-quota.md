## Evidence

### Mommert A 2020

Mommert A, Wagner J, Jünger J, Westermann J. (2020). Exam performance of different admission quotas in the first part of the state examination in medicine: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02069-6

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Logistic regression of examination success in the standard study period (pass within standard period) on admission quota in the 655-student sample; Table 3 reports χ2(4641) = 45.36, p < .001, with passing rates of 92.2% (GPA), 88.7% (selection), 70.1% (WT), 80.6% (ex-ante) and 46.4% (foreign).

> "The data showed that students of the GPA and selection quotas ranked best regarding this criterion, and the foreign students exhibited the lowest passing rate."
