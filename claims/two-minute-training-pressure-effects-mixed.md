# Median PIP decreased from before to after the two-minute training in experienced caregivers but not in inexperienced caregivers; pressure dispersion did not change from before to after in either group, in single-group pre-post measurements with no comparison group

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Experienced caregivers delivered lower median pressures after training (32 (5) vs. 28 (6) cm H2O; p<0.01), while pressures in inexperienced users were not significantly different (34 (6) vs. 33 (3) cm H2O; ns). [→ van Vonderen Jeroen J. 2014](#van-vonderen-jeroen-j-2014)
`q3 i?` Variations (SD) in pressures did not change from before to after training in either experienced or inexperienced caregivers. [→ van Vonderen Jeroen J. 2014 (2)](#van-vonderen-jeroen-j-2014-2)

## Evidence

### van Vonderen Jeroen J. 2014

van Vonderen Jeroen J., Witlox Ruben S., Kraaij Sascha, te Pas Arjan B. (2014). Two-Minute Training for Improving Neonatal Bag and Mask Ventilation. PLoS ONE 9(10): e109049. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0109049

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Manikin experiment with 52 caregivers (27 experienced, 25 inexperienced) measured with a respiratory function monitor before and after the two-minute training. The article reports the printed median (SD) pressures and test outcomes; no effect size is printed.

> "Experienced caregivers delivered lower pressures after training (32 (5) vs. 28 (6) cm H2O; p<0.01) but pressures were not significantly different in inexperienced users (34 (6) vs. 33 (3) cm H2O; ns)"

### van Vonderen Jeroen J. 2014 (2)

van Vonderen Jeroen J., Witlox Ruben S., Kraaij Sascha, te Pas Arjan B. (2014). Two-Minute Training for Improving Neonatal Bag and Mask Ventilation. PLoS ONE 9(10): e109049. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0109049

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same manikin experiment; dispersion of pressures (SD) was compared before and after training in both groups with non-significant results as printed. The article states the training "had no influence in the inconsistency of the given pressures"; no effect size is printed.

> "Variations in pressures were unaffected by training in both experienced and inexperienced caregivers (experienced: 1.0 (0.8-1.9) vs. (1.9 (0.8-3.2) cm H2O; ns, inexperienced: 1.3 (1.1-1.7) vs. 1.0 (0.8-2.3) cm H2O; ns)"
