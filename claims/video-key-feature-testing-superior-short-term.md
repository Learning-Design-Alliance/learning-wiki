## Evidence

### Ludwig Sascha 2018

Ludwig Sascha, Schuelper Nikolai, Brown Jamie, Anders Sven, Raupach Tobias. (2018). How can we teach medical students to choose wisely? A randomised controlled cross-over study of video- versus text-based case scenarios. BMC Medicine 16:107. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-018-1090-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Primary endpoint of a randomised controlled cross-over study in which 93 fourth-year medical students repeatedly answered key feature questions presented as video or text. The paired T-test showed "significantly higher than in text items" with the printed means and p = 0.026; no effect size was reported.

> "exit exam scores in items that had been presented as videos during the learning phase were significantly higher than in text items (76.2 ± 19.4% vs. 72.4 ± 19.1%, T = 2.263; df = 92; p = 0.026)"
