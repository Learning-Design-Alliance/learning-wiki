Equity-focused Schools Carry All Students to High Levels 2014¶

Equity-focused Schools Carry All Students to High Levels. (2014). WestEd R&D Alert, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559627

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

A qualitative multiple-case study by Gleason and Gerzon selected four exemplary schools (Stults Road Elementary, Social Justice Humanitas Academy, Montgomery Center School, Tusculum View Elementary) meeting dual criteria of high poverty and high achievement, including for subgroups such as English learners and students of color. Achievement "surpasses state averages."

"Each of the case study schools serves significant numbers of low-income students ( ranging from 46 to 80 percent of the school's total) and has had rising student achievement for 5 to 10 years"
