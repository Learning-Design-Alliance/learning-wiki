# In focus groups comparing platforms, all participants preferred the larger iPad screen to the iPhone for viewing astronomical images

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` When viewing images without labels on two mobile platforms, all focus group participants in both expert and non-expert groups preferred the larger iPad screen to the iPhone. [→ Lisa F. Smith 2013](#lisa-f-smith-2013)

## Evidence

### Lisa F. Smith 2013

Lisa F. Smith, Kimberly K. Arcand, Jeffrey K. Smith, Randall K. Smith, Jay Bookbinder, Megan Watzke. (2013). Examining Perceptions Of Astronomy Images Across Mobile Platforms. [doi:10.22323/2.13020201](https://doi.org/10.22323/2.13020201)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Focus group study with 12 astrophysicist and 11 non-expert participants who viewed the Sun, G292, and Centaurus A on a projection screen, iPad, and iPhone and compared platforms hands-on. "All participants preferred the larger iPad screen to the iPhone". No effect size is reported.

> "In both groups, it was clear that size mattered. Viewing the images without any labels, all participants preferred the larger iPad screen to the iPhone."
