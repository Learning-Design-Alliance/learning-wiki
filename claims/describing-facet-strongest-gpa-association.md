## Evidence

### Mathkor Darin Mansor 2025

Mathkor Darin Mansor. (2025). Mindfulness and academic performance among nursing students in Saudi Arabia: a cross-sectional study. Scientific Reports 15, 27381. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-11911-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Unadjusted regression (Model 1) on the same 276-student cross-sectional sample; describing had the largest coefficient, "describing (β = 0.389, CI = 0.279, 0.499, p < 0.001)", and remained significant in Model 2 (β = 0.424, R2 = 0.303).

> "as well as with the individual facets of observation (β = 0.154, CI = 0.036, 0.272, p = 0.011), awareness (β = 0.248, CI = 0.133, 0.363, p < 0.001), and describing (β = 0.389, CI = 0.279, 0.499, p < 0.001)"
