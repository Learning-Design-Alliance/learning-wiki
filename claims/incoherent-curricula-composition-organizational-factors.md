`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis of grade-level coherence measured as similarity in math content coverage among teachers within the same grade. In low-coherence schools, students might receive the third-grade curriculum two years running or encounter material they are unprepared to tackle.

> "even after we controlled for prior school achievement, we found that schools with high proportions of impoverished families are much more likely to have incoherent curricula, as are large schools and schools with high levels of student mobility."
