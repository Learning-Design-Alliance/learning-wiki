## Evidence

### Lonshakova 2023

Lonshakova, A., Blair, D. G., Treagust, D. F., & Zadnik, M. (2023). Powers of the Universe: Empowering primary school students with the powers of ten notation. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.18192](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.18192)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre/post questionnaire results (Q2) for the doubling activity in trials 1-3. The article reports scores "rose to 96%, 95% and 85% respectively after the trials"; the higher trial-2 pre-test was attributed to prior class teaching of doubling, and the low trial-3 pre-test to its younger average age of 9.

> "before the three trials, only 26%, 53% and 5% students were able to calculate their number of fifth-generation ancestors(Q2). The scores rose to 96%, 95% and 85% respectively after the trials."
