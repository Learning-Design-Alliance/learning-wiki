# Tekster, a strategy-focused writing instruction program, statistically significantly improves the writing quality of upper-elementary students, with an overall effect size of 0.32

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` The Tekster intervention produced a statistically significant improvement in students' writing quality, with an overall effect size of ES = 0.32 across all students, teachers, and tasks. [→ Bouwer 2018](#bouwer-2018)
`q3 i?` The intervention effect replicated in both groups of the switching replication design, indicating internal validity. [→ Bouwer 2018 (2)](#bouwer-2018-2)

## Evidence

### Bouwer 2018

Bouwer, R., Koster, M., & van den Bergh, H. (2018). Effects of a Strategy-Focused Instructional Program on the Writing Quality of Upper Elementary Students in the Netherlands. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(1), 58–71. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000206

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel analysis of writing quality ratings from 1,420 students in 60 classrooms in a switching replication design. The group × measurement occasion interaction was significant, χ²(1) = 24.98, p < .001; the printed effect size for the intervention was ES = 0.32, which the authors call "a conservative estimate of the actual effect."

> "The overall ES (i.e., across all students, teachers, and tasks; based on the mean number of student-completed lessons) was 0.32."

### Bouwer 2018 (2)

Bouwer, R., Koster, M., & van den Bergh, H. (2018). Effects of a Strategy-Focused Instructional Program on the Writing Quality of Upper Elementary Students in the Netherlands. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(1), 58–71. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000206

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Model comparison removing the restriction that the intervention effect was equal across groups found no difference, χ²(1) = 0.12, p = .73, supporting internal validity of the switching replication design.

> "The effect of the intervention on differences in scores appeared to be the same for students in Group 1 and 2—Model 6 vs. Model 5, /H92732(1) /H110050.12, p /H11005.73."
