# Lower pretest exploratory levels predicted greater gains, but the group difference in this relationship was not significant

## Subclaims
`q2 i3` Pretest exploratory scores significantly predicted gain scores (partial η² = 0.339), but the steeper regression line for experimentals compared to controls was not statistically significant, contrary to H3. [→ Dejonckheere 2016](#dejonckheere-2016)

## Evidence

### Dejonckheere 2016

Dejonckheere, P.J.N., De Wit, N.L., Van de Keere, K., & Vervaet, S. (2016). Exploring the classroom: Teaching science in early childhood. European Journal of Educational Research, 5(3), 149-164. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.5.3.149

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

An ANCOVA with pretest exploratory scores as covariate showed the covariate significantly predicted gain ("partial η² = 0.339"), while the group × pretest interaction on the regression lines was visually steeper for experimentals but not significant.

> "the effect of the covariate on gain was significant, F(1, 55) = 27.17; p < 0.000; partial η² = 0.339 showing that exploratory scores (covariate) in the pretest significantly predicted the difference scores between pre - and post -tests."
