## Evidence

### Knight 1921

Knight, F. B., & Franzen, R. H. (1921). Pitfalls in rating schemes. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0073817

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

In the same 110-junior rating study, the pooled objective order of interests (typical junior as seen by classmates) was correlated with the pooled ideal order. The article reports a correlation of "—0.64" and interprets it as peers being judged below the ideal.

> "The correlation between this order of interests and the "ideal one" was —0.64. There is, then, a tendency for the junior to think his fellow-juniors are not what he believes they should be."
