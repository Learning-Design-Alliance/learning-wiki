## Evidence

### Rachel G. Pizzie and David J. M. Kraemer 2023

Rachel G. Pizzie and David J. M. Kraemer. (2023). Strategies for remediating the impact of math anxiety on high school math performance. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00188-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

LMM with gender, math anxiety, and intervention group as fixed factors: "a main effect of gender on course grades" (χ2(1) = 10.73, p = 0.001) favoring female students, but no gender-by-anxiety (χ2(1) = .23, p = 0.63), gender-by-group (χ2(1) = 0.01, p = 0.89), or three-way interactions (χ2(1) = 0.003, p = 0.96).

> "There was a main effect of gender on course grades, χ2(1) = 10.73, p = 0.001, such that female students had higher course grades (M = 80.8, SE = 5.41, 95% CI : 65.4 –96.2) than male students (M = 72.2, SE = 5.47, 95% CI : 57.0 –87.5)."
