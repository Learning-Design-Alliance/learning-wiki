Evidence¶

Harris 2023¶

Harris, C. J., Murphy, R., Feng, M., & Rutstein, D. (2023). Supporting science learning and literacy development together: Initial results from a curriculum study in 1st grade classrooms. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638494

q4 · i0 · causal · r3

In the randomized trial's multilevel analysis, the standardized science assessment distal to NGSS showed "a small positive effect size of 0.09" that the authors characterize as negligible and "not statistically significant."

"We observed a small positive effect size of 0.09 on the standardized Iowa assessment for science, although the mean difference in scores between students in intervention and comparison classrooms was not statistically significant (p > .60)."
