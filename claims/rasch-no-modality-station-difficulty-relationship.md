## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Monteiro S 2018

Monteiro S, Sibbald D, Coetzee K. (2018). i-Assess: Evaluating the impact of electronic data capture for OSCE. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-018-0410-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-hoc Rasch analysis using Facets software on May exam examiner rating patterns, comparing station difficulty values computed from paper versus tablet modality scores. The article used a 0.50 logit threshold for leniency/stringency and found none; the scatterplot showed similar difficulties for both modalities.

> "The difficulty scores for each station/modality are plotted in Fig. 3, which indicates no relationship between modality and station difficulty."
