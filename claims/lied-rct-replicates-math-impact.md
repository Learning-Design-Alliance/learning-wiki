## Evidence

### Nakamoto 2022

Nakamoto, J., Grant, A., Snipes, J., Allender, S., & Figueroa, T. (2022). Clark County School District Magnet Schools Assistance Program (MSAP): Spring 2022 interim evaluation summary report. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED621271

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled trial exploiting Lied's oversubscription lottery: 1,023 first-choice applicants randomized, with 382 lottery winners and 404 non-winners as complete cases in the intent-to-treat analysis. The report states the RCT "replicated the positive and statistically significant impact" on mathematics; no effect size is printed in the available text.

> "The findings from the RCT, which is a more rigorous design than the QEDs, replicated the positive and statistically significant impact on the 6th graders' mathematics achievement found by the QED."
