Evidence¶

Mark Feng Teng 2026¶

Mark Feng Teng. (2026). Effectiveness of GenAI in enhancing writing performance: a meta-analysis. Asian-Pacific Journal of Second and Foreign Language Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40862-026-00444-2

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird) meta-analysis of 11 study-level Hedges' g effects from the expanded main synthesis, prioritizing between-group post-test overall writing outcomes. The pooled effect was "a statistically significant pooled effect of g = 0.80" with substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 92.21%, τ2 = 0.456).

"The primary random-effects meta-analysis (DerSimonian–Laird) in Table 2 yielded a statistically significant pooled effect of g = 0.80, 95% CI [0.37, 1.22], indicating a large overall advantage for AI-enhanced instructional conditions."
