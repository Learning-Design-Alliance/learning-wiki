## Evidence

### Brender 2025

Brender, J., El-Hamamsy, L., Giang, C., Mathex, L., Käser, T., & Mondada, F. (2025). The handwriting programming language for primary school: aligning computer science education with established teaching practices. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10503-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Direct comparison question administered to 49 pre-service teachers after using both platforms for 30 minutes each. "53% of teachers chose HPL, 31% chose both HPL and TPL, compared to 10% who preferred TPL." Kruskal-Wallis test on separate TAM evaluations indicated no significant difference (p > 0.05) for all criteria.
