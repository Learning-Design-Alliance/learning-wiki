### Gerd Kortemeyer 2014

Gerd Kortemeyer. (2014). Extending Item Response Theory to Online Homework. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.10.010118)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Analysis restricted to the first quarter of the semester's homework data. The article reports that "in the ﬁrst weeks of the semester, the ﬁrst attempt on a home- work problem is still meaningful," with the extracted cosine similarity of learner ability to exam data the highest found in this study (0.72 first attempt, 0.60 all attempts).

> "In other words, in the ﬁrst weeks of the semester, the ﬁrst attempt on a home- work problem is still meaningful — in fact, the extracted cosine similarity of the learner ability to the exam data is the highest found in this study."
