Kusumawati, E., & Widiati, U. (2017). The Effects of Vocabulary Instructions on Students’ Reading Comprehension across Cognitive Styles in ESP. Journal of Education and Practice, 8(2). https://journals.iiste.org/index.php/JEP/article/view/2894

Descriptive posttest comparison of FD students in the quasi-experimental study: FD under EVI (n=9) mean 69.7222 (SD 4.58333) vs FD under IVI (n=6) mean 53.3333 (SD 5.16398). The authors conclude the FD-under-explicit group achieved better.
