## Evidence

### Brini Stefano 2026

Brini Stefano, Metcalf Mary, Li Ting, Fukuzawa Fumitoshi, Yu Chengcheng, Li Fangcai, Zhang Ning, Hu Hejia, Huang Hanxu, Wang Jingkai, Tao Yiqing, Wu Yinan. (2026). Effectiveness of Artificial Intelligence–Assisted Peer Teaching in Orthopedic Clinical Education: Historical Cohort Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/87959

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Follow-up assessment completed by 174 of 190 participants (91.6%); the quote reports the AI-assisted group "maintained higher knowledge scores" with "similar knowledge decay rates between groups".

> "At 3-month follow-up (174/190, 91.6%), the AI-assisted group maintained higher knowledge scores (mean 77.36, SD 8.60 vs mean 72.84, SD 10.42; mean difference=4.52, 95% CI 1.68-7.36; P=.002; d=0.47), with similar knowledge decay rates between groups"
