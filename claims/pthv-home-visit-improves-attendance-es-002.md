### Academic Performance — Parent Teacher Home Visits 2021

Academic Performance — Parent Teacher Home Visits. (2021). Evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/parent-teacher-home-visits/

`q2 · i0` · `causal · r2`

A 2021 quasi-experimental study by the Regional Education Laboratory of the Mid-Atlantic at Mathematica used matched comparison group propensity scores in DC Public Schools grades 1-5. It found "a home visit significantly improved student attendance slightly (ES = +0.02)"; attendance averaged 95.28 percent for visited versus 94.93 percent for nonvisited students.

> "During the school year following a home visit, the study found that, on average, a home visit significantly improved student attendance slightly (ES = +0.02)."
