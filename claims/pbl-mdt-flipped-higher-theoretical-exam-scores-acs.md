## Evidence

### Lin Yuhui 2025

Lin Yuhui, He Xiaoqing, Dai Wenjun, Tan Wenpeng, Xu Yunhong. (2025). The application and effectiveness of PBL combined with MDT and flipped classroom teaching method in acute coronary syndrome education for undergraduate medical students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08150-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized two-group comparison (n = 50 per group) in a two-week ACS clerkship at Guangzhou Medical University, analyzed by t-test in SPSS 23.0. The results section reports "The theoretical exam scores of the experimental group (86.10 ± 4.35) were higher than those of the control group (83.42 ± 5.06)"; Table 2 prints t = 2.837, P = 0.006. No effect size is reported.

> "The theoretical exam scores of the experimental group (86.10 ± 4.35) were higher than those of the control group (83.42 ± 5.06), and the skill exam scores of the experimental group (78.46 ± 4.97) were also higher"
