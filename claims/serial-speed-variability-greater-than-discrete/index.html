Evidence¶

Altani 2019¶

Altani, A., Protopapas, A., Katopodi, K., & Georgiou, G. K. (2019). From Individual Word Recognition to Word List and Text Reading Fluency. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000359

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive inspection of speed distributions in discrete and serial word reading (Table 2, Figures 1 and 2) across grades and languages. The authors state "the latter reaching a plateau by Grade 3 but the former still progressing by Grade 5," indicating word-list fluency is not simply faster isolated word reading.

"variability in the speed dimension was greater in serial word reading than in discrete word reading across grades in both languages, with the latter reaching a plateau by Grade 3 but the former still progressing by Grade 5."
