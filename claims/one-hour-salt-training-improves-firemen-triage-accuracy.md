## Evidence

### Nilsson Abraham 2015

Nilsson Abraham, Åslund Kristian, Lampi Maria, Nilsson Helene, Jonson Carl-Oscar. (2015). Improved and sustained triage skills in firemen after a short training intervention. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. [doi:10.1186/s13049-015-0162-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-015-0162-7)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Prospective randomized controlled trial of 86 firemen completing tests before and after training. Wilcoxon signed rank test showed median post-test score of 10 [9–12] versus pretest 9 [8–10], "a significant improvement (P < 0.01)".

> "The median pretest score was 9 [8–10] and individual participant accuracy ranged from 4 to 12 of a maximum 15 points. The median post-test score was 10 [9–12] with individual accuracy ranging from 4 to 14 points, a significant improvement (P < 0.01)."
