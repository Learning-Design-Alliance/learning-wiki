Would you let a humanoid play storytelling with your child? `q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Post-interaction ad-hoc questionnaire item Q4 asked which age range the application suits; 68% of users chose children under 10 and 46% also chose ages 10-20. The printed χ2(5) = 88.78, p < .001 and Holm-corrected z-tests support the below-10 preference.

> "A Chi-squared goodness-of-fit test indicated that participants did not select all age groups equally ( χ2(5) = 88 .78, p < .001). Pairwise two-proportion z-tests with Holm correction revealed that the below-10 group showed a significantly higher proportion of responses compared to all other age groups (all p< = 0.01)"
