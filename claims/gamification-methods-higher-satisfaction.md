# Students taught with gamification (in a flipped learning environment or alone) were more satisfied than students taught by flipped class or lecture

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Post-intervention satisfaction scores differed significantly across the four groups, with the gamification-containing groups more satisfied than the flipped class and lecture groups (P < 0.01). [→ Ghafouri Raziyeh 2024](#ghafouri-raziyeh-2024)
`q3 i?` Satisfaction did not differ significantly between the gamification-in-flipped-environment and gamification-alone groups (P = 0.49). [→ Ghafouri Raziyeh 2024 (2)](#ghafouri-raziyeh-2024-2)

## Evidence

### Ghafouri Raziyeh 2024

Ghafouri Raziyeh, Zamanzadeh Vahid, Nasiri Malihe. (2024). Comparison of education using the flipped class, gamification and gamification in the flipped learning environment on the performance of nursing students in a client health assessment: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05966-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus ANOVA on post-intervention satisfaction (and knowledge) scores across the four randomized groups of 166 nursing students; the article prints only the P value, no effect size.

> "The ANOVA showed that a statistically significant difference between the mean scores of knowledge and satisfaction after intervention in the four groups (P < 0.001)."

### Ghafouri Raziyeh 2024 (2)

Ghafouri Raziyeh, Zamanzadeh Vahid, Nasiri Malihe. (2024). Comparison of education using the flipped class, gamification and gamification in the flipped learning environment on the performance of nursing students in a client health assessment: a randomized clinical trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05966-2

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Pairwise satisfaction comparisons in the same trial's Results section: the two gamification groups did not differ from each other, while both differed from the flipped class and lecture groups. No effect size is printed.

> "According to the ANOVA results, when comparing the satisfaction scores of the groups, there was no significant difference between the means of gamification in the flipped learning environment and the gamification method (P = 0.49); however, there was a significant difference between the gamification in the flipped learning environment and the gamification with the flipped class and the lecture."
