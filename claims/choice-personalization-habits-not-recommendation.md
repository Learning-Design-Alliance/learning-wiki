## Evidence

### Ilana Ram 2024

Ilana Ram, Sara Harris, & Ido Roll. (2024). Choice-based Personalization in MOOCs: Impact on Activity and Perceived Value. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00334-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square test on the exit-survey yes/no item among 142 survey completers: 63 of 73 Personalized (86.3%) vs. 48 of 69 Generic (69.6%) said they were likely to change habits. No effect size is printed.

> "A Chi Square test found that personalization increased the likelihood of learners to report they will change their climate related habits: X2 (1, N = 142) = 5.82, p = 0.016"

### Ilana Ram 2024 (2)

Ilana Ram, Sara Harris, & Ido Roll. (2024). Choice-based Personalization in MOOCs: Impact on Activity and Perceived Value. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00334-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square test on the recommendation item found no significant difference (53.4% vs. 47.8% positive). The authors note the exit survey may not have been sensitive enough or recommendation may be a stable parameter.

> "A similar test found no significant difference between the groups on the question of recommending the course to a friend, X2 (1, N = 142) = 0.45, p = 0.51"
