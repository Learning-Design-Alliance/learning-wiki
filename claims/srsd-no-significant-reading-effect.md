## Evidence

### Torgerson 2014

Torgerson, D., Torgerson, C., Ainsworth, H., Buckley, H., Heaps, C., Hewitt, C., & Mitchell, N. (2014). Improving Writing Quality: Evaluation Report and Executive Summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581140

`q3 · i0` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis repeating the primary multilevel model with reading score as the response, on 108 intervention and 88 control pupils. The report found "no evidence of a difference in reading scores" with an effect size of -0.09; equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no evidence of a difference in reading scores between the allocated groups, with a non -significant decrease of 0. 31 marks for those in the intervention group when compared with those in the control group (p=0. 72, 95% CI: -2.02 to 1.41). This relates to an effect size of -0.09 (95% CI: -0.59 to 0.41)."
