Evidence¶

Admiraal 2014¶

Admiraal, W., Janssen, T., Huizenga, J., Kranenburg, F., Taconis, R., & Corda, A. (2014). E-assessment of student-teachers’ competence as new teachers. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 13(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1043184

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Repeated measures analyses of master students' questionnaire data (644 master students from 13 universities; Likert-type evaluation scales). The article reports the null result and, separately, that "A marginal trend was found for the evaluation of peer feedback ( F(1,134)= 3.35; p= 0.07)".

"No significant differences were found between students from the experimental and control condition on the evaluation of the e-assessment types (all Fs < 1.85 and all ps >18)."
