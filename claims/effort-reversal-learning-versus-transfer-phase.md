# Task sequence effects on mental effort reverse between learning phase and transfer test

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` EPEP students reported lower effort during learning (η²p = .05) but higher effort on near transfer posttest problems (η²p = .03) than PEPE students. [→ Coppens 2019](#coppens-2019)

## Evidence

### Coppens 2019

Coppens, L. C., Hoogerheide, V., Snippe, E. M., Flunger, B., & van Gog, T. (2019). Effects of problem–example and example–problem pairs on gifted and nongifted primary school students' learning. Instructional Science, 47, 279–297. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-019-09484-3

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA on self-rated mental effort (Paas 9-point scale): during learning, EPEP students reported lower effort (M = 3.49 vs 4.04, p = .016, η²p = .05), but on near transfer problems "students in the EPEP conditions" reported significantly more effort than PEPE students, F(1, 122) = 4.27, p = .041, η²p = .03.

> "students in the EPEP conditions (M = 5.95, SD = 2.01) reported having invested significantly more effort in solving the near transfer posttest problems than students in the PEPE conditions (M = 5.25, SD = 2.01)"
