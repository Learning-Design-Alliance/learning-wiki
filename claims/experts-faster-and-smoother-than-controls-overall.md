## Evidence

### Hewitson 2021

Hewitson, C. L., Shukur, S. T., Cartmill, J., Crossley, M. J., & Kaplan, D. M. (2021). Camera realignment imposes a cost on laparoscopic performance. Scientific Reports, 11, 17634. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-96965-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Group main effect on movement time in the mixed-design ANOVA of the peg-transfer experiment ("η2 = 0.35"; post hoc "d = 1.39"), comparing 10 surgeons with 10 undergraduates.

> "Overall, experts moved more quickly than controls as evidenced by a main effect of group [F(1,18) = 9.62, p = 0.01, η2 = 0.35], and further supported by a post hoc t-test [t(18.0) = 3.1, p = 0.01, d = 1.39]."

### Hewitson 2021 (2)

Hewitson, C. L., Shukur, S. T., Cartmill, J., Crossley, M. J., & Kaplan, D. M. (2021). Camera realignment imposes a cost on laparoscopic performance. Scientific Reports, 11, 17634. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-96965-6

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Group main effect on movement smoothness (total squared jerk) in the same ANOVA ("η2 = 0.34"; post hoc "d = 1.35"), measured across canonical and realigned conditions.

> "Experts moved more smoothly than controls overall as evidenced by a significant main effect of group [F(1,18) = 9.09, p = 0.01, η2 = 0.34], and supported by a post hoc t-test [t(18.0) = 3.02, p < 0.01, d = 1.35]."
