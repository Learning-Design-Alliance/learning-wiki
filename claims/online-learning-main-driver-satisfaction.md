`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Principal component analysis of the 11-item, five-point Likert satisfaction questionnaire completed by 90 of 93 FPCR-BL students; the online learning factor scored "4.67 (4.00–5.00)" median (IQR), the highest of the four factors.

> "The results of the principal component analysis showed that the online learning and preview test of FPCR-BL design had the greatest impact on satisfaction; the percentage of variance was 51.51 and 16.80% respectively, with a cumulative percentage of 68.31%."
