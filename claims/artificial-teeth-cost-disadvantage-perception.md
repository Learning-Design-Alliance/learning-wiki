### Özdemir O 2026

Özdemir O, Matara BM, Gündoğar M. (2026). Preclinical endodontic training using natural/simulative models: a cross-sectional study involved trainee and educator perspectives on clinical competence. BMC Medical Education, 26:378. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08715-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey finding from Questionnaire Part 3 (Table 3) on barriers to artificial-teeth use in preclinical endodontic training. Educators showed a similar pattern (57.8% cost disadvantage; 62.2% cost influencing preference). Self-reported perceptions only.

> "The percentage of respondents who considered the cost of procuring the artificial teeth used in the training model a disadvantage to applicability was 57.5%. 56% of respondents believe that the cost of procuring artificial teeth will influence their decision to use them in the training model."
