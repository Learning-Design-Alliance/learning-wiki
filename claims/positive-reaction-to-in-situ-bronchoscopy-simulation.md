## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On a one-to-five scale, participants rated the training high on feeling safe (4.6, SD 0.7), relevance to clinical practice (4.5, SD 0.6), team competence improvement (4.4, SD 0.7), and realism (4.1, SD 0.8). [→ Fosli CA 2025](#fosli-ca-2025)

## Evidence

### Fosli CA 2025

Fosli CA, Sperling S, Jensen RD, Skaarup SH. (2025). In-situ simulation-based team training reduces incidence of negative events during bronchoscopy. A prospective educational intervention cohort study. Respiratory Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12931-025-03205-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kirkpatrick level 1 questionnaire completed by the 13 participating nurses and physicians shortly after training. Descriptive means with SDs are reported; the authors state "The reaction to simulation-based training (Kirkpatrick level 1) was positive".

> "the participants rated in mean that it was fun to participate in the simulation: 4.3, (SD = 0.7), that they felt safe to participate in the simulation: 4.6, (SD = 0.7), that the simulation was usable for clinical practice: 4.2, (SD = 0.8), that the simulation was relevant to clinical practice: 4.5, (SD 0.6)"
