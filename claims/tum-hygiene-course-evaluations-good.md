Descriptive course-evaluation data collected over four semesters at the TUM Medical Education Center. Course 1: 461 evaluations, mean grade 1.4 (SD=0.24); course 2: 350 evaluations, mean grade 1.9 (SD=0.50) on a 1-6 scale with 1 best. Descriptive only; no inferential test is reported.

> "In summary of a total of 461 evaluations of course 1 (basic module) that have been returned over four semesters (summer 2017, winter 2017/2018, summer 2018, winter 2018/2019), the mean grade is 1.4 (SD=0.24, with a school grading scale of 1-6 and 1 being the best grade)."
