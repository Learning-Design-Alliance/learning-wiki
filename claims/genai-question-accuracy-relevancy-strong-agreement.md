## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Model and expert assessments of generated questions correlate strongly for accuracy (0.96) and relevancy (0.99), though p-values did not fall below 0.05 due to small sample size. [→ Riya Gill 2025](#riya-gill-2025)

## Evidence

### Riya Gill 2025

Riya Gill, Ievgeniia Kuzminykh, Maher Salem, Bogdan Ghita. (2025). Generative AI-Enabled Adaptive Learning Platform: How I Can Help You Pass Your Driving Test?. [doi:10.1108/aiie-05-2025-0088](https://doi.org/10.1108/aiie-05-2025-0088)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Evaluation of 100 application-generated questions by both the Gemini-2.0-Pro-Exp-02-05 model and an eight-person expert focus group, rated on accuracy and relevancy categories. The article reports "a value of 0.96 (p < 0.174)" for accuracy and 0.99 for relevancy, with χ² = 9.35 and p ≈ 0.053.

> "The Pearson correlation coefficient for accuracy and relevancy showed a value of 0.96 (p < 0.174) and 0.99 (p < 0.096), respectively; the Chi-Square test showed χ² = 9.35 and p ≈ 0.053."
