## Evidence

### Ghimire 2025

Ghimire, A. and Singh, C. (2025). Using Unguided Peer Collaboration to Facilitate Early Educators' Pedagogical Development: An Example from Physics TA Training. [doi:10.3390/educsci15081038](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15081038)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Two-year study in which 43 first-year physics TAs completed the 30-item MCS individually (~50 min) and then in self-selected pairs or trios (19 groups) for another 50 min without instructor guidance or feedback. The article reports "the average group score rose to 94%" from 74% individually; no overall effect size is printed.

> "The average individual score was 74% with a standard deviation of 11%, while the average group score rose to 94% with a standard deviation of 6%."
