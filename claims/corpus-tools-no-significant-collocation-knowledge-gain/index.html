Evidence¶

Kartal and Yangineksi 2018¶

Galip Kartal, Gonca Yangin Eksi. (2018). The Effects of Using Corpus Tools on EFL Student Teachers' Learning and Production of Verb-Noun Collocations. PASAA, Vol. 55. https://so04.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/pasaa

q2 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANCOVA on CKT post-test scores with pre-test as covariate in a pre/post quasi-experiment with two intact classes of first-year ELT student teachers in Turkey. Table 2 reports Group F = 2.294, Sig. = .135. The null was not an equivalence test.

"controlling for the pre -test mean scores of CKT, there was not a statistically significant difference between control and experimental groups regarding adjusted CKT mean scores."
