Zhu Jing 2025¶

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects pooling of two studies (196 participants) reporting problem-solving skill scores; pooled SMD = 0.27 was not statistically significant (P = 0.45), with I² = 0.83. Equivalence was not tested.

"Two studies reported scores related to problem-solving skills, with no significant difference observed between the BOPPPS group and the control group (SMD 0.27, 95% CI -0.43-0.97; P = 0.45; Table 5)."

Zhu Jing 2025 (2)¶

Zhu Jing, Xiao Heng, Zhou Rui, Gan Xiaochuang, Gou Qitao, Tie Hongtao. (2025). The efficacy of the BOPPPS teaching model in clinical and health education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07274-9

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects pooling of two studies (420 participants) reporting critical thinking skill scores; pooled SMD = 0.88 was not statistically significant (P = 0.08), with I² = 0.95. Equivalence was not tested.

"Two studies reported scores related to critical thinking skills with no significant improvement observed within the BOPPPS group compared to the control group (SMD 0.88, 95% CI -0.12-1.88; P = 0.08; Table 5)."
