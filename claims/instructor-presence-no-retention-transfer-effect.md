# Instructor video presence produced no significant difference in retention or transfer of Mandarin tone distinction among true-beginner learners

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Retention performance did not differ significantly between the with and without instructor conditions. [→ Yu-Jr Lan 2026](#yu-jr-lan-2026)
`q3 i?` Transfer performance did not differ significantly between the with and without instructor conditions. [→ Yu-Jr Lan 2026 (2)](#yu-jr-lan-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Yu-Jr Lan 2026

Yu-Jr Lan, Emmanuel Manalo. (2026). Incorporating instructor presence in a multimedia language learning context. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09814-2

`q3 · i?`

Repeated-measures ANOVA on 50 participants who each viewed one video with and one without the instructor; retention scores were near-identical (M = 27.68 in both conditions). The study reports "no overall detectable difference in retention between the with and the without conditions"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Results on the retention task was not significant, indicating no overall detectable difference in retention between the with and the without conditions (F(1, 49) = 0.29, p =.59, η² = 0.001)."

### Yu-Jr Lan 2026 (2)

Yu-Jr Lan, Emmanuel Manalo. (2026). Incorporating instructor presence in a multimedia language learning context. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09814-2

`q3 · i?`

Same repeated-measures design; transfer items used disyllabic words not heard before. The article reports no significant difference between conditions (η2 = 0.001); equivalence was not tested.

> "Results on the transfer task also showed no overall significant difference between the conditions ( F(1, 49) = 0.17, p =.687, η2 = 0.001)."
