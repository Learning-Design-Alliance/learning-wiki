## Evidence

### Talafian 2024

Talafian, H., Stelzer, T., Lundsgaard, M., Mahmood, M., & Kuo, E. (2024). Teachers' Experiences with Implementing Open-ended Labs in High School Physics Classes. University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Department of Physics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Pictorial analysis of an in-person embodied rating task at the fourth summer PD, in which teachers physically positioned themselves on a structured-to-open-ended continuum and then repositioned to their desired location, showed "the majority i.e., 19 out of 27 teachers moved to the left towards open-ended labs."

> "As is evident in this figure, the majority i.e., 19 out of 27 teachers moved to the left towards open-ended labs."

### Talafian 2024 (2)

Talafian, H., Stelzer, T., Lundsgaard, M., Mahmood, M., & Kuo, E. (2024). Teachers' Experiences with Implementing Open-ended Labs in High School Physics Classes. University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Department of Physics. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.04275)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

The same pictorial analysis of the embodied rating task showed "nine teachers did not move from their initial positions," three of them closer to the structural end and five closer to the open-ended end of the continuum.

> "Pictorial analysis also showed nine teachers did not move from their initial positions, indicating that they felt confident with their lab style in their classes at the time of the rating task."
