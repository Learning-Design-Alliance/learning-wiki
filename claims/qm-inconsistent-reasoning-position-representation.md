## Evidence

### Marshman 2015

Marshman, E. and Singh, C. (2015). A Framework for Understanding the Patterns of Student Difficulties in Quantum Mechanics. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.11.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.11.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual multiple-choice survey administered to 39 upper-level students, with think-aloud interviews with 23 students, about the wave function in position representation with and without Dirac notation. The article reports that 'In the first question, 90% of the students correctly noted that the position space wave function is Ψ(𝑥) = ⟨𝑥|Ψ⟩.'

> "However, 41% of the students claimed that only one of the answers (𝑄(𝑥)⟨𝑥|Ψ⟩ or 𝑄(𝑥)Ψ(𝑥)) is correct, but not both."
