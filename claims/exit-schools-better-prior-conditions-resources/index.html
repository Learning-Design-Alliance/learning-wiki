Evidence¶

Lee 2013¶

Lee, J., Shin, H. & Amo, L. C. (2013). Evaluating the impact of NCLB school interventions in New York state: Does one size fit all? Education Policy Analysis Archives, 21 (67). http://epaa.asu.edu/ojs/article/view/1122

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired comparisons of three matched treatment subgroups (Exit, Watch, Fail) on standardized prior-year covariates (Tables 5-6, Figures 4-5). For example, in Grade 4 ELA the Exit group had lower pupil-teacher ratio (M = -0.499) than Watch (M = -0.279) and Fail (M = -.304) groups; the article reports similar patterns across grades and subjects.

"schools that performed better with an intervention (i.e. Exit schools) had greater access to resources and less exposure to variables associated with academic risk relative to schools in the Watch and Fail groups."
