## Evidence

### M.M. Chiu 2016

M.M. Chiu, B.W.Y. Chow, C. McBride, S.T. Mol. (2016). Students’ sense of belonging at school in 41 countries: cross-cultural variability. Journal of Cross-Cultural Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022022115617031

`q3 · i?`

Multilevel analyses of survey and test data from 193,073 15-year-old students in 41 countries tested attributes at family, schoolmate, teacher, and student levels; these characteristics "were also linked to students’ SOBAS and accounted for most of its variance". No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

> "family characteristics (immigrant status, language spoken at home, socio-economic status [SES], books at home, family wealth, and family communication), schoolmates’ characteristics (SES and social communication), teacher characteristics (teacher-student relationship, teacher support and disciplinary climate), and student characteristics (reading achievement, self-efficacy, and self-concept) were also linked to students’ SOBAS and accounted for most of its variance."
