## Evidence

### Zsifkovits Monika 2025

Zsifkovits Monika, Amplatz Lukas, Triebner Nina, Utz Janine, Kornhuber Johannes, Spitzer Philipp. (2025). Randomized controlled trial of asynchronous vs. synchronous online teaching formats: equal knowledge after training, greater acceptance and lower intrinsic motivation through asynchronous online learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07481-4

`q3 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Group comparison in the randomized trial of 1044 Styrian paramedics after the two-hour course, using the study's acceptance summary score. The learning platform scored "MLP = 4.16 ± 0.87" versus 3.82 ± 0.88 for the webinar, with "Cohen's d = 0.388, 95% CI [0.266, 0.511]".

> "Group comparisons revealed that the level of acceptance of the learning platform was significantly greater than that of the webinar (MLP = 4.16 ± 0.87; MW = 3.82 ± 0.88; t (1032) = 6.27; p <.001, p_adj = 0.013; Cohen's d = 0.388, 95% CI [0.266, 0.511])"
