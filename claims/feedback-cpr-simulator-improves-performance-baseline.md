## Evidence

### Joyce Kelly Barreto Silva 2025

Joyce Kelly Barreto Silva, Thomaz Bittencourt Couto, Andreia Melo Coriolano, Alex Aquino, Júlio Cesar Martins Monte. (2025). Analysis of acquisition and retention of cardiopulmonary resuscitation skills according to training frequency. Einstein (Sao Paulo). https://doi.org/10.31744/einstein_journal/2025AO1257

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the randomized controlled trial, 43 medical students completed a baseline practice session on a device without feedback, then practiced with a feedback-equipped simulator; medians favored feedback on all three metrics, e.g. "median=95% [IQR: 87–98%] versus median=60%".

> "Significant differences favoring feedback were observed in overall performance (median=95% [IQR: 87–98%] versus median=60% [IQR: 18–89%]; p<0.001), compression depth (median=71% [IQR: 24–92%] versus median=13% [IQR: 0–94%]; p<0.001), and compression rate (median=89% [IQR: 71–98%] versus median=69% [IQR: 23–96%]; p=0.002)."
