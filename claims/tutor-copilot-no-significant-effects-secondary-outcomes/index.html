Evidence¶

Rose E. Wang 2025¶

Rose E. Wang, Ana T. Ribeiro, Carly D. Robinson, Susanna Loeb, and Dora Demszky. (2025). Tutor CoPilot: A Human-AI Approach for Scaling Real-Time Expertise. arXiv:2410.03017v2. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03017 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2410.03017

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Null result on end-of-year NWEA MAP math scores, stated in the Limitations section and analyzed in Appendix K. The authors attribute it to little variation in treatment exposure and the study's two-month duration. No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Additionally, while we observed substantial improvements in students' proximal learning gains (i.e., the students' exit ticket performance), we did not find statistically significant improvements in end-of-year math test scores; please refer to Appendix K for this analysis."
