### Aikens 2023

Aikens, M. L., & Kulacki, A. R. (2023). Identifying Group Work Experiences That Increase Students' Self-Efficacy for Quantitative Biology Tasks. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.22-04-0076

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

Mixed-effects logistic regression on 429 coded responses modeled reporting Accomplishing It as a function of initial self-efficacy, gender/sex, question type, and course section; the printed odds ratio was 1.48 (B = 0.39, p = 0.001). Gender/sex was not significant as a main effect or interaction.

> "For a one-unit increase in self-efficacy levels, holding all other variables constant, the odds of a student reporting Accomplishing It increase by a factor of 1.5 (Table 4)."
