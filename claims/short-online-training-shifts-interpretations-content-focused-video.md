## Evidence

### Henna Vilppu 2019

Henna Vilppu, Ilona Södervik, Liisa Postareff, Mari Murtonen. (2019). The effect of short online pedagogical training on university teachers’ interpretations of teaching–learning situations. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-019-09496-z

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre–post analysis of interpretations of a 60-s video in which a teacher ignores a student’s question as off topic. Changes were from a knowledge-transmission view toward a learning-facilitation view, e.g. participant P50 moved to emphasising “an atmosphere wherein the students feel safe to question”.

> "After participating in university pedagogy courses, a total of 14 respondents changed their interpretations concerning the second video."
