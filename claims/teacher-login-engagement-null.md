## Evidence

### Duckworth 2025

Duckworth, A. L., Ko, A., Milkman, K. L., et al. (2025). A national megastudy shows that email nudges to elementary school teachers boost student math achievement, particularly when personalized. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2418616122

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Preregistered secondary-outcome analysis rerunning the three primary weighted OLS models on teacher dashboard logins among 140,461 randomized teachers. The article attributes the null to the design fact that students can use Zearn Math without their teacher logging in; no effect size is printed.

> "For the secondary outcome of teacher engagement (defined as the number of times teachers logged in to their Zearn Math dashboards during the 4-wk intervention period), however, none of the three models produced significant results"
