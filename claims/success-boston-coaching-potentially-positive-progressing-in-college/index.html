Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2020¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2020). Success Boston Coaching (WWC 2021-001). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/708

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC synthesis of two quasi-experimental studies (statistically/propensity matched comparison groups) of Boston Public Schools graduates. The report describes "evidence of a positive effect with no overriding contrary evidence", an outcome average improvement index of +9 across 8,375 students; no effect size is printed in the quoted finding sentence.

"The WWC determined that two studies that meet WWC group design standards with reservations (Linkow et al., 2017; Linkow et al., 2019) showed evidence of a positive and statistically significant effect of Success Boston Coaching on progressing in college."
