### Amrita Ganguly 2026 (2)

Amrita Ganguly, Sai Sharanya Garika, Aditya Johri. (2026). Uncovering Students' Mental Models of Generative Artificial Intelligence. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Uncovering+Students%27+Mental+Models+of+Generative+Artificial+Intelligence%22

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Knowledge-type overlap analysis of the 64 maps: 31 declarative-only, 16 declarative-plus-procedural, 8 declarative-plus-conditional, and 9 with all three types, the last described as the most sophisticated group.

> "31 concept maps were declarative only. These students only listed and categorized concepts pure factual/definitional knowledge with no process reasoning and no evaluative judgment leading to the most surface -level mental models."
