Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt 2021¶

Stephan E. Vogel and Bert De Smedt. (2021). Developmental brain dynamics of numerical and arithmetic abilities. npj Science of Learning (2021) 6:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00099-3

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review attribution: recent years have shown "consistent impairments in the processing of symbolic numbers" in dyscalculia, persistent over time and coinciding with arithmetic fact retrieval deficits; symbolic measures have been suggested as diagnostic markers. No effect sizes are printed.

"children with dyscalculia also show consistent impairments in the processing of symbolic numbers 143,144, for which reason it has been suggested that measures of symbolic number processing might be useful diagnostic markers for children at risk for dyscalculia"
