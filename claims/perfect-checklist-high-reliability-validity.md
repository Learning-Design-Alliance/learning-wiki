### Häske David 2018

Häske David, Beckers Stefan K., Hofmann Marzellus, Lefering Rolf, Preiser Christine, Gliwitzky Bernhard, Grützner Paul Alfred, Stöckle Ulrich, Münzberg Matthias. (2018). Performance Assessment of Emergency Teams and Communication in Trauma Care (PERFECT checklist)—Explorative analysis, development and validation of the PERFECT checklist: Part of the prospective longitudinal mixed-methods EPPTC trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0202795

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Psychometric evaluation of the checklist on 36 training videos rated by six blinded instructors. The article prints the ICC, Cronbach's alpha and Pearson correlations with p<0.001; no standardised effect size for a training effect is given, so impact is coded i? (no magnitude reported; not a null effect).

> "The inter-rater reliability (ICC = 0.99) and internal consistency (α = 0.99) were high. Concurrent validity was moderate to high (r = 0.65 –r = 0.93 (p<0.001))."

### Häske David 2018 (2)

Häske David, Beckers Stefan K., Hofmann Marzellus, Lefering Rolf, Preiser Christine, Gliwitzky Bernhard, Grützner Paul Alfred, Stöckle Ulrich, Münzberg Matthias. (2018). Performance Assessment of Emergency Teams and Communication in Trauma Care (PERFECT checklist)—Explorative analysis, development and validation of the PERFECT checklist: Part of the prospective longitudinal mixed-methods EPPTC trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0202795

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Principal component factor analysis with varimax rotation on the checklist data to assess construct validity; KMO was 0.848 and Bartlett's test χ2 = 207.456 (p<0.001). The first factor grouped primary assessment, procedures, trauma communication, non-technical skills and global performance.

> "Three factors with eigenvalues ≥0.5 were extracted by PCA and explained 89.7% of the overall variance. The first factor explained 66.6% of the total variance and included 5 items with factor loadings between 0.83 and 0.92."
