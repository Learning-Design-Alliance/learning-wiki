Evidence¶

Noetel 2021¶

Noetel, M., Griffith, S., Delaney, O., Sanders, T., Parker, P., del Pozo Cruz, B., & Lonsdale, C. (2021). Video Improves Learning in Higher Education: A Systematic Review. Review of Educational Research, 91(2), 204–236. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654321990713

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-regression and moderation analyses of absolute and relative intervention length across included trials. The review reports "there was no significant dose-response effect"; no effect size is printed for this null finding.

"The number of minutes of the educational intervention did not moderate effects (b = 0.00, 95% CI [0.00, 0.00], p = 0.73), and there were no significant differences in effects when the video intervention was applied to a single topic or a whole course (R22 = 0.00, R32 = 0.00, p = 0.08). In other words, there was no significant dose-response effect."
