## Evidence

### Claudia Mazziotti 2019

Claudia Mazziotti, Nikol Rummel, Anne Deiglmayr and Katharina Loibl. (2019). Probing boundary conditions of Productive Failure and analyzing the role of young students' collaboration. npj Science of Learning 4:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0041-5

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory correlational analysis at the individual level (n = 68) linking coded utterance relative frequencies to posttest scores in the PF-Coll condition. The printed effect sizes were "r = 0.219" (interactive), "r = 0.233" (constructive), and "r = 0.226" (active); absolute frequencies showed no significant correlations with posttest.

> "The relative number of interactive ( r(66) = 0.219, p = 0.036), constructive ( r(66) = 0.233, p = 0.028), and active (r(66) = 0.226, p = 0.032) utterances correlated signi ﬁcantly with students'conceptual knowledge at posttest"
