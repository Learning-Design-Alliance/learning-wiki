Peter Hu, Yangqiuting Li, and Chandralekha Singh. (2023). Challenges in addressing student difficulties with quantum measurement of two-state quantum systems using a multiple-choice question sequence in online and in-person classes. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Midterm exam analysis in in-person class 2 (Table IV, question 2c appearing in two contexts) of transfer of the basis-change concept, contrasting explicit (93%) with implicit (76%) application two weeks after the post-test.

> "In isolation, when explicitly asked to change the basis, students were able to do so at a nearly perfect rate, 93%, but when implicitly required as one step of another problem related to measurement, 76% of students recognized that a change of the basis was necessary to solve the problem."
