Andria Shyjka 2021¶

Andria Shyjka. (2021). Student Experience with the High School Choice Process in Chicago Public Schools. UChicago Consortium Research Brief, July 2021. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/student-experience-with-the-high-school-choice-process-in-Chicago-Public-Schools

Cross-case qualitative finding: fit was a key component of the 6to16 curriculum, and students' most common advice to future students was "choose the school that is best for you." Counselors facilitated fit thinking through questions about how students learn and their goals.
