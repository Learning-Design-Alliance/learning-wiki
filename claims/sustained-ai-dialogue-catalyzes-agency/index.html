Authors' interpretive discussion contrasting their 14-week interactive ChatGPT integration with Darvishi et al.'s (2023) 8-week automated one-way feedback for undergraduates, which they say allowed minimal integration or critique. Interpretation, not a tested comparison.

"signaling AI's potential to catalyze, rather than constrain, learner agency given sufficient duration and interaction."
