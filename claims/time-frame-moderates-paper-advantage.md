## Evidence

### Delgado 2018

Delgado, P., Vargas, C., Ackerman, R., & Salmerón, L. (2018). Don't throw away your printed books: A meta-analysis on the effects of reading media on reading comprehension. Educational Research Review, 25, 23-38. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2018.09.003

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

ANOVA moderator analysis on between-participants effect sizes (k = 27 limited vs k = 20 self-paced). The printed moderator contrast was Hedges' g = -0.26 (limited) versus Hedges' g = -0.09 (self-paced); time frame explained 5% of the mean effect size variance (R2 = .05).

> "comparisons in studies with time constraints yielded a significantly larger (QB = 4.12, p = .04) print advantage (Hedges' g = -0.26) than comparisons in studies in which participants were allowed to self-pace their reading (Hedges' g = -0.09)."
