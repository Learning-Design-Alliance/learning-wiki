## Evidence

### Beatrici Edoardo 2026

Beatrici Edoardo, Santarelli Valerio, Guidotti Alessio, Pepillo Francesco, de Angelis Mario, Barretta Attilio, Cei Francesco, Frego Nicola, Paciotti Marco, Vanderstraeten Eva, Van Herzeele Isabelle, Puliatti Stefano, De Naeyer Geert, Farinha Rui, De Groote Ruben, Hemal Sij, Wiklund Peter, Crivellaro Simone, Challacombe Ben, Rashidian Niki, Mottrie Alexandre. Journal of Robotic Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03996-0

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Blinded construct validity comparison of recorded SP chicken anastomosis performances by n = 12 experts (≥100 SP RARPs) and n = 12 novices, scored by two independent reviewers. Novices' mean completion time was "23.8 [18.9–28.7] min" versus 12.1 min for experts, p < 0.001.

> "Mean (95% CI) time to completion of the task was significantly longer in the novice surgeons group (23.8 [18.9–28.7] min vs. 12.1 [9.3–14.9] min for novices and experts respectively, p < 0.001)."
