### Frendø M 2022

Frendø M, Frithioff A, Konge L, Cayé-Thomasen P, Sørensen MS, Andersen SAW. (2022). Cochlear implant surgery: virtual reality simulation training and transfer of skills to cadaver dissection—A randomized, controlled trial. The Journal of International Advanced Otology. https://doi.org/10.5152/iao.2022.21429

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, contrasting this trial's null transfer finding with mastoidectomy studies where VR simulation training was highly beneficial for subsequent cadaver surgery: 'results from studies on mastoidectomy training cannot be directly extrapolated to more advanced temporal bone procedures'.

> "This finding is of key relevance to the training of temporal bone surgery: results from studies on mastoidectomy training cannot be directly extrapolated to more advanced temporal bone procedures (such as CI surgery) that require more sophisticated and diverse technical skills."
