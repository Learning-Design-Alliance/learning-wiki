Laura Dörrenbächer-Ulrich, Marius Bregulla. (2024). The Relationship Between Self-Regulated Learning and Executive Functions—a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09932-8

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's cross-study synthesis of the 19 included studies concludes that "Measurement methods used to capture SRL and EF seem to influence the size of the correlations"; the statement is hedged and no pooled effect size is printed.

> "Measurement methods used to capture SRL and EF seem to influence the size of the correlations, with indirect measures correlating higher than direct/indirect measures."

### Laura Dörrenbächer-Ulrich 2024 (2)

Laura Dörrenbächer-Ulrich, Marius Bregulla. (2024). The Relationship Between Self-Regulated Learning and Executive Functions—a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09932-8

> "Strong correlations between indirect EF measures and SRL (metacognition) • No correlation between the three direct EF measures and the MSLQ–MS-R"
