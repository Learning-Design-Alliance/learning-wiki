# Adding lag-2 paths to CLPMs substantially improves fit and provides stronger controls for preexisting differences, positioning extended CLPMs as viable alternatives to RI-CLPMs

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Prior research cited by the review shows lag-2 paths substantially improve CLPM fit and strengthen control for confounding, so goodness-of-fit ceases to be a critical issue in comparing RI-CLPMs and extended CLPMs. [→ Herbert W. Marsh 2022](#herbert-w-marsh-2022)

The review attributes this to Marsh et al. (2018a, 2018b) and Lüdtke & Robitzsch (2021), who showed with simulated data that CLPMs with lag-2 effects fit as well as RI-CLPMs. Reported as second-hand evidence, not this study's own analysis.

> "Marsh and et al., (2018a, 2018b, p. 271; also see Lüdtke & Robitzsch, 2021) argued that the addition of lag-2 paths substantially improved the fit of a CLPM and served for "providing stronger controls for preexisting differences.""
