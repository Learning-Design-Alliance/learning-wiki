## Evidence

### Asher 2025

Asher, M. W., & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2025). Using Choice and Utility Value to Promote Interest: Stimulating Situational Interest in a Lesson and Fostering the Development of Interest in Statistics. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000921

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r?`

Meta-regression across Studies 1–7 of the D-UV contrast on utility value perceptions. The manipulation "increasing reported beliefs by over .4 standard deviations relative to control, d = 0.41, p < .001"; interactions with baseline confidence and interest were non-significant (ps ≥ .474).

> "the directly-communicated utility value manipulation had a strong, positive effect on participants’ beliefs about the usefulness of regression, increasing reported beliefs by over .4 standard deviations relative to control, d = 0.41, p < .001. This overall effect was unmoderated"
