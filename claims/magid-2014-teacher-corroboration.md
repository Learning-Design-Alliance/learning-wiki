## Evidence

### Magid 2014

Michael Magid. (2014). An Application of the L2 Motivational Self System to Motivate Elementary School English Learners in Singapore. Journal of Education and Training Studies. https://doi.org/10.11114/jets.v2i1.232

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-program interviews with the two English teachers, recorded and transcribed; the experimental-group teacher reported students spending more time studying English and being more willing to read aloud, and the control-group teacher noted gains in her two least motivated students.

> "The interviews that I conducted with the English teacher of the control group and the experimental group supported my findings with regard to the participantsʼ increase in motivation, confidence, and positive attitudes toward learning English as a result of my program."
