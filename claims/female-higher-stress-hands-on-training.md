`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Gender comparison of visual analog scale stress ratings across four phases in the 40-student randomized study; the article reports "females reporting significantly higher VAS 2 and VAS 3 scores than males". No effect size is printed.

> "Gender differences were notable, with females reporting significantly higher VAS 2 and VAS 3 scores than males (p < .05), although no significant differences were observed in VAS 0 and VAS 1 scores."
