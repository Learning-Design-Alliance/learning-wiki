## Evidence

### Bathgate M. E. 2019

Bathgate M. E., Aragón O. R., Cavanagh A. J., Frederick J., Graham M. J. (2019). Supports: A Key Factor in Faculty Implementation of Evidence-Based Teaching. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.17-12-0272

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Model 1 multiple linear regression on the 584-faculty survey tested motivation variables alone against reported EBT implementation; standardized betas were 0.13 (mindset, p ≤ 0.05), 0.12 (confidence, p ≤ 0.001), −0.10 (anxiety, p ≤ 0.05); teaching and research value were n.s. Adjusted R2 was 4%.

> "Faculty implemented more EBT practices when they believed their efforts were related to achievement (growth mindset) and when they had greater confidence, whereas being anxious about teaching was related to using less EBT in the classroom."
