Comparison of A Mini-Clinical evaluation exercise with the routine logbook in assessing clinical competence of operating room trainees: A parallel randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07560-6

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Parallel randomized controlled trial of 60 undergraduate operating room students (30 Mini-CEX, 30 Logbook) analyzed with repeated measures ANOVA in SPSS-16. Table 3 prints the between-groups Group effect as significant with an effect size of 0.978, i.e. the "difference in mean scores of two groups (Mini CEX and Logbook)".

> "The results of repeated measures ANOVA show that not only the mean scores of scrub nursing students increase significantly over time (P-value < 0.001), but difference in mean scores of two groups (Mini CEX and Logbook) is also significant (P-value < 0.001)."
