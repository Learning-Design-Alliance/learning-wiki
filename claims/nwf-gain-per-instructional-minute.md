Efficiency analysis based on the roughly 394 average instructional minutes students received, of which vocabulary-added groups spent 30% (118 minutes) on PA and decoding. These are descriptive ratios; the article prints no significance test for them.

> "The average gain on NWF per instructional minute for the PAD group after removing an outlier was .02 points, compared to .12 for SR and .09 for MA."
