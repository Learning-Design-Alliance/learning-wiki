Evidence¶

Jennie Y. Jiang 2014¶

Jennie Y. Jiang, Susan E. Sporte, Stuart Luppescu. (2014). Analytic Memo: Evaluation Data from the First Year of REACH. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.cps.edu/Pages/reachstudents.aspx

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of all 2012-13 observation data for unusual rating patterns: "About 3 percent of principals were found to be" muted and about 5 percent erratic, with erratic evaluators rating easy components low and hard components high. Most evaluators applied the rubric consistently with predicted component difficulty.

"About 3 percent of principals were found to be "muted," meaning that a principal would award almost the same rating to all teachers, regardless of the component difficulty."

Jennie Y. Jiang 2014 (2)¶

Jennie Y. Jiang, Susan E. Sporte, Stuart Luppescu. (2014). Analytic Memo: Evaluation Data from the First Year of REACH. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.cps.edu/Pages/reachstudents.aspx

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Severity analysis using a randomly selected sample of 46 principals and 16 specialists, with approximately 1,400 observations and 125 joint observations. Fewer than ten percent of sampled evaluators were more than half a rating point more severe or lenient than average; specialists tended to give lower ratings than principals.

"Another set of analyses focused on a randomly selected sample of 46 principals across the district. It utilized independent, joint observations with 16 specialists. Using approximately 1,400 observations and 125 joint observations, we were able to determine how severe or lenient each evaluator (principal and specialist) was."
