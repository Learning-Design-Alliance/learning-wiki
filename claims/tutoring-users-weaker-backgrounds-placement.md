## Evidence

### Michael Courtney 2011

Michael Courtney, PhD. (2011). The Evening Tutoring Center at the United States Air Force Academy. Originally published 2009. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1102.1893](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1102.1893)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive comparison of placement-exam Algebra scores in the Outcomes section: tutoring visitors averaged "52.1%±2.9%" versus 60.1%±0.6% for the full cohort, with 38% considered passing for Calculus 1 placement. No significance test is reported.

> "the cadets enrolled in Calculus 1 who visited the tutoring center in the first two weeks to prepare for the first Fundamental Skills Exam (FSE), which covers high -school Algebra, averaged 52.1%±2.9% on the Algebra portion of the departmental placement exam, whereas the cohort of cadets currently enrolled in Calculus 1 averaged 60.1%±0.6%"
