Country-level analysis of the Pandemic-Related Performance Gap (difference between ATEs of lowest and highest ESCS quartiles) across 47 countries, illustrated with bootstrap confidence intervals in Fig. 3a. The article states "This negative difference is statistically significant in mathematics and science".

"In all domains, performance as affected by COVID-19 deteriorated on average more among the most disadvantaged students than among the most advantaged students (Pandemic-Related Performance Gap)."
