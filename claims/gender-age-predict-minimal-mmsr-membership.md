## Evidence

### Katsantonis I 2023

Katsantonis I, McLellan R. (2023). Profiling students' motivation and metacognitive self-regulation: Links with school language achievement. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0289367

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent multinomial logistic regression via the manual BCH method with profile 3 (exceptional) as reference, adjusting for covariates. Age was also significant: "Being older was related to greater odds of belonging to minimal MMSR profile 1 (OR = 1.45, 95% CI 1.06, 1.99)".

> "The BCH method revealed that being male predicted greater odds of membership in the minimal MMSR profile 1 (OR = 3.12, 95% CI 2.05, 4.76) and in the adequate MMSR profile 2 (OR = 1.65, 95% CI 1.12, 2.41) compared to the exceptional MMSR profile."
