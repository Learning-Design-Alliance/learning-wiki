## Evidence

### Michael C. Wittmann 1999

Michael C. Wittmann, Richard N. Steinberg, Edward F. Redish. (1999). Making Sense of How Students Make Sense of Mechanical Waves. The Physics Teacher. [doi:10.1119/1.880142](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.880142)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Matched pre/post diagnostic test data from Fall-1997 (N=92) in a second-semester university physics course, comparing free-response and MCMR wave propagation answers before and after all instruction on waves. Post-instruction, "a majority of the class still believes that changes in hand motion play a role."

> "Nearly all students recognize the correct answer on the MCMR question and nearly three-fourths give it for the free response question, but a majority of the class still believes that changes in hand motion play a role."
