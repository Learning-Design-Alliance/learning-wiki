## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Jorge-Soto Cristina 2019

Jorge-Soto Cristina, Abilleira-González Maite, Otero-Agra Martin, Barcala-Furelos Roberto, Abelairas-Gómez Cristian, Szarpak Łukasz, Rodríguez-Núñez Antonio. (2019). Schoolteachers as candidates to be basic life support trainers: A simulation trial. Cardiology Journal. https://doi.org/10.5603/CJ.a2018.0073

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post questionnaire results from the same sample of 98 university students of Educational Sciences and Sports. The article prints the percentages directly; no statistical test or effect size is reported for this change.

> "The percentage of participants who knew what an AED increased from 87.8% before to 100% after training, and the number who declared to know how to use an AED (pre-test 38.8% vs. post-test 100%). Most subjects declared the willingness to use an AED in an eventual emergency situation both pre-test (70.4%) and post-test (98%)."
