## Evidence

### Dilnot 2017

Dilnot, J., Hamilton, L., Maughan, B. and Snowling, M. (2017). Child and Environmental Risk Factors Predicting Readiness for Learning in Children at High-Risk of Dyslexia. Development & Psychopathology, 29(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.1017/S0954579416000134

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical regression models entering SES, home literacy environment, family stresses, child health and finally family-risk as a dummy variable, in the 251-child longitudinal sample. The article concludes family-risk "is not a significant predictor of either measure of ‘readiness to learn’".

> "Importantly, these analyses confirm that family3risk status is not a significant predictor of either measure of ‘readiness to learn’ when other distal, proximal and child risk factors are taken into account."
