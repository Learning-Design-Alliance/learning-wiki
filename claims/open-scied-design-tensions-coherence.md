# OpenSciEd curriculum design goals, especially coherence from the student perspective, come into tension during unit design

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Curriculum designers experience tensions among design goals and values, particularly between maintaining coherence from the student perspective and other design goals. [→ McElhaney 2024](#mcelhaney-2024)

## Evidence

### McElhaney 2024

McElhaney, K. W., & Kamdar, D. (2024). Updated summary and synthesis of OpenSciEd research [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/231

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The synthesis reports, citing Pomian Bogdanov (2022) and Penuel, Rubin, et al. (in press), that co-design discourse analysis and design-case analysis revealed curricular values including "coherence from the students’ perspective" that came into tension during unit development. No effect sizes are reported.

> "Both papers highlight in particular a tension between maintaining coherence from the student perspective and other design goals."
