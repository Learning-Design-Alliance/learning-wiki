## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Regression-discontinuity findings are only relevant for students just below or just above the cut score, and because they combine interventions across multiple colleges, high-performing institutions may be lost in the averages. [→ Collins 2010](#collins-2010)

## Evidence

### Collins 2010

Collins, M. L. (2010). Bridging the Evidence Gap in Developmental Education. Journal of Developmental Education, 34(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ942875

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The author's analytical argument about the scope limits of regression-discontinuity designs, citing Bailey, Calcagno and Long, and Levin and Calcagno. The article states the risk is "finding that developmental education does not work when, in individual instances, there may be strategies, programs, and practices that actually do work."

> "These studies are considered to be statistically robust, but they are only relevant for students just below or just above the cut score (Bailey, 2009; Calcagno & Long, 2008; Levin & Calcagno, 2007). More importantly, they do not measure the degree of impact of specific institutional programs or practices."
