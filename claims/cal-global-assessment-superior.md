## Evidence

### de Sena David P. 2013

de Sena David P., Fabricio Daniela D., Lopes Maria Helena I., da Silva Vinicius D. (2013). Computer-Assisted Teaching of Skin Flap Surgery: Validation of a Mobile Platform Software for Medical Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0065833

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Blinded OSATS global rating by board-certified plastic surgeons in the same randomized study of 25 students per group; the CAL group scored higher on the summed global assessment scale.

> "Table 1 presents the mean sum score of nine items of gross overall global assessment, which was 22.68±10.53 vs. 29.48±9.40 (p = 0.017)."

### de Sena David P. 2013 (2)

de Sena David P., Fabricio Daniela D., Lopes Maria Helena I., da Silva Vinicius D. (2013). Computer-Assisted Teaching of Skin Flap Surgery: Validation of a Mobile Platform Software for Medical Students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0065833

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary analysis of individual global assessment items in the same randomized comparison; several psychomotor items did not differ significantly between the printed-text and CAL groups.

> "with no statistical differences for items related to tissue handling (p = 0.115), correct use of surgical instruments (p = 0.133) and dexterity (p = 0.084)."
