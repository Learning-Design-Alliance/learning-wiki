Evidence¶

Admiraal 2014¶

Admiraal, W., Janssen, T., Huizenga, J., Kranenburg, F., Taconis, R., & Corda, A. (2014). E-assessment of student-teachers’ competence as new teachers. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 13(4). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1043184

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative analysis of work meetings and evaluation reports (Table 7) across the 27 interventions. The same analyses report that "Participants agreed that (summative) peer assessment of students’ research plans was not feasible" and that "video cannot replace live observation of classroom practice; rather, video is seen as complementary".

"Participants agreed that using rubrics for peer feedback helped to make the assessment criteria more transparent for students and teacher-trainers, and helped to improve the quality of the feedback."
