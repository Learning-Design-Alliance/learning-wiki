## Evidence

### Cortegiani A 2015

Cortegiani A, Russotto V, Montalto F, Iozzo P, Palmeri C, Raineri SM, Giarratano A. (2015). Effect of High-Fidelity Simulation on Medical Students' Knowledge about Advanced Life Support: A Randomized Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0125685

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Baseline comparison in the randomized study (Table 2): overall pre-test objective correct answers 33.8 (SD 18.6) in group S versus 30.2 (SD 18.9) in group C, p = 0.34; subjective section p = 0.48. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The number of correct answers, in both the subjective and objective sections of the pre-test questionnaire, was not significantly different in the two groups."
