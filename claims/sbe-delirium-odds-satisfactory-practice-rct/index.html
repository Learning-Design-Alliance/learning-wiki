Evidence¶

EL Hussein Mohamed Toufic 2026¶

EL Hussein Mohamed Toufic, Sheehan Dawson S., Hirst Sandra. (2026). Simulation‐Based Education to Support Nurses' Critical Thinking in Delirium Recognition: A Scoping Review. Journal of Advanced Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1111/jan.70640

q3 · i? · review · r2

This multisite clustered, observer-blinded RCT randomized 190 post-registration nurses on medical/surgical wards across three hospitals to a DOE package (group education plus OSCE with simulated patient) or group education only, with the primary outcome assessed 6 weeks post-intervention. The intervention showed "higher odds of satisfactory delirium-care performance in the intervention group compared with controls (OR 10.2, p = 0.009)"; no standardised effect size is printed, so the magnitude is coded null.

"Montgomery et al. (2024) reported higher odds of satisfactory delirium-care performance in the intervention group compared with controls (OR 10.2, p = 0.009)."
