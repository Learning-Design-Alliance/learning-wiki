## Evidence

### White 2018

White, Sonia, Graham, Linda, & Blaas, Sabrina (2018). Why do we know so little about the factors associated with gifted underachievement? A systematic literature review. Educational Research Review, 24, pp. 55-66. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2018.03.001

`q3 · i?` · `review · r3`

Review of identification methods across the nine included articles (Table 2). The review reports "the majority of the nine articles (n=6)... relied on only one measure", with the remaining three using multiple measures or sources; cut-offs ranged from ≥115 to ≥130 IQ or percentile thresholds.

> "The majority of the nine articles (n=6) meeting our inclusion criteria relied on only one measure to identify intellectually gifted students (e.g., WISC-R, Raven’s SPM, BIS-HB, CFT, OLSAT)."
