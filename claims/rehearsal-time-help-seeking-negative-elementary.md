## Subclaims
`q3 i?` As students' organization increased, literacy achievement tended to decrease (γ = -2.767, p < .001), and as rehearsal increased, math achievement tended to decrease (γ = -2.014, p < .01). [→ Ha Cheyeon 2023](#ha-cheyeon-2023)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`
