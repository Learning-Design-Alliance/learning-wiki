## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Sharing concepts and statements is positively linked with both equity and goodwill outcomes of the managed company. [→ Van den Bossche 2011](#van-den-bossche-2011)

## Evidence

### Van den Bossche 2011

Van den Bossche, P., Gijselaers, W., Segers, M., Woltjer, G., & Kirschner, P. (2011). Team learning: building shared mental models. Instructional Science, 39, 283-301. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-010-9128-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Regression analyses regressed actual company performance (equity and goodwill) onto two shared mental model indicators in 27 teams; standardized betas ranged from 0.43 to 0.51, all significant, supporting the link between shared cognition and team performance.

> "Sharing concepts in the team is linked with both the equity results (Beta = 0.51, p = 0.007) and the goodwill (Beta = 0.50, p = 0.007), as well as the number of shared statements is positively linked with both equity (Beta = 0.43, p = 0.026) and goodwill (Beta = 0.50, p = 0.008) of the company."
