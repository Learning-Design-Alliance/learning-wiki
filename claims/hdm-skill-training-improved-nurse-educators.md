## Evidence

### Delbene Lara 2026

Delbene Lara, Mezini Sara, Cacciabue Camilla, Watson Roger, Aleo Giuseppe, Di Nitto Marco, Zanini Milko, Catania Gianluca, Sasso Loredana, Bagnasco Annamaria. (2026). Effective Educational Programs for Disaster Preparedness and Management: A Rapid Evidence Assessment. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70193

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

Randomized controlled trial (Rohini et al. 2025, India) of a Hospital Disaster Management Skill Training Program delivered in hybrid mode (online theory plus practical simulation) with a 17-unit module validated using the Delphi technique, in 60 nurse educators (30 per group). The review reports "t = 4.581" through "t = 9.34"; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Significant improvements in the intervention group compared with control: Knowledge (t = 4.581, p = 0.001), skills (t = 3.136, p = 0.003), attitude (t = 2.093, p = 0.003), and behavior at 3 months (t = 9.34, p = 0.001); no loss to follow‐up."
