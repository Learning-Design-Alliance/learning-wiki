## Evidence

### Tanielian 2014

Tanielian, A. R. (2014). Foreign language anxiety in a new English program in Thailand. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1035913

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Linear regression across the entire sample predicting English midterm scores from FLCAS sum. The article reports FLCA accounted for only 2% of explained variability, leading to partial rejection of Hypothesis 2.

> "A linear regression established that FLCA accounted for only 2% of explained variability in English testing within the entire sample (F( 1,421) = 11.429, p = 0.001)."

### Tanielian 2014 (2)

Tanielian, A. R. (2014). Foreign language anxiety in a new English program in Thailand. The International Education Journal: Comparative Perspectives, 13(1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1035913

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Linear regression within the immersion group predicting English midterm exam scores from FLCAS sum. The article reports FLCA as a statistically significant predictor accounting for 25.4% of variability.

> "Linear regression analysis performed with the data from the immersion group showed FLCA was a statistically significant predictor of English exam performance, F(1,35) = 13.273, p = 0.001 and FLCAS scores accounted for 25.4% of variability on the midterm."
