## Evidence

### Yoshioka Ryuji 2021

Yoshioka Ryuji, Imamura Hiroshi, Ichida Hirofumi, Gyoda Yu, Mizuno Tomoya, Mise Yoshihiro, Saiura Akio. (2021). Simulation training in pancreatico-jejunostomy using an inanimate biotissue model improves the technical skills of hepatobiliary-pancreatic surgical fellows. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244915

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Secondary endpoint validating OSATS for the inanimate PJ model: interclass correlation between the two blinded supervisor surgeons across the drills. Average-measure intraclass correlations were 0.81 for OSATS 25 and 0.74 for OSATS summary, both P < 0.001.

> "The average measures for the OSATS 25 and OSATS summary scores were 0.81 (95% CI: 0.63–0.90, P < 0.001) and 0.74 (95% CI: 0.51–0.86, P < 0.001), respectively."
