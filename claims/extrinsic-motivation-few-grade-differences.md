## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Extrinsic motivation scores of third graders differed significantly from all other grades, which did not differ from one another; excluding third grade, no overall grade effect remained. [→ Lepper 2005](#lepper-2005)

## Evidence

### Lepper 2005

Lepper, M. R., Henderlong Corpus, J., & Iyengar, S. S. (2005). Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivational Orientations in the Classroom: Age Differences and Academic Correlates. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.97.2.184

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Post-hoc pairwise comparisons following an omnibus ANOVA across grades in the 797-student sample. A subsequent analysis excluding third graders confirmed no overall effect of grade level, F(4, 645) ⫽ 1.34, ns. No effect size is printed.

> "Student-Newman-Keuls comparisons of all possible pairs of means revealed a significant (p ⬍ .05) difference between the extrinsic motivation scores of third-grade students and those of students at all other grade levels, which were not significantly different from one another."
