## Evidence

### Filippou 2018

Filippou, F., Rokka, S., Pitsi, A., Gargalianos, D., Bebetsos, E., & Filippou, D. A. (2018). Interdisciplinary Greek Traditional Dance Course: Impact on Student Satisfaction and Anxiety. International Journal of Instruction, 11(3), 363-374. https://doi.org/10.12973/iji.2018.11325a

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA (2X2: measurement x group) on the Duda and Nicholls satisfaction scale in a study of 260 8th-grade students; the experimental group improved from M=2.91 to M=3.79 while the control group remained stable. The article reports a "statistically significant interaction" with F(1,258)=169.69, p<.001.

> "The results showed that there was a statistically significant interaction between the variable “measurement” and “group” for the «satisfaction» [F(1,258)=169.69, p<.001]."
