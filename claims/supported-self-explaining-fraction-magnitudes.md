# Supported self-explaining improves fraction magnitude comparison knowledge (ES 0.43) and explanation quality (ES 0.93), and compensates for low working memory

## Evidence

### Fuchs 2015

Fuchs, L. S., Malone, A. S., Schumacher, R. F., Namkung, J., & Wang, A. (2015). Fraction Intervention for Students with Mathematics Difficulties: Lessons Learned from Five Randomized Control Trials. Vanderbilt University. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/Fraction-Intervention-for-Students-with-Mathematics-Difficulties

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Year 4 three-arm RCT isolating supported self-explaining, in which high-quality explanations were modeled, practiced, and discussed, against the same core program with multiplicative word problems substituted to control time. Printed effect sizes are ES = 0.43 on fraction comparison accuracy and ES = 0.93 on explanation quality.

> "the explaining condition outperformed the word-problem condition with a moderate ES of 0.43. On a measure of the quality of explanations about why fraction magnitudes differ (scored for the explanations' conceptual content), effects more dramatically favored the explaining condition over the word-problem condition, with an ES of 0.93."

### Fuchs 2015 (2)

Fuchs, L. S., Malone, A. S., Schumacher, R. F., Namkung, J., & Wang, A. (2015). Fraction Intervention for Students with Mathematics Difficulties: Lessons Learned from Five Randomized Control Trials. Vanderbilt University. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/Fraction-Intervention-for-Students-with-Mathematics-Difficulties

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Moderator analysis in the Year 4 RCT; no effect size is printed for this interaction. In the word-problem condition, students with severe working memory deficits experienced poorer outcomes than those with more adequate working memory; the explaining condition eliminated this dependence.

> "By contrast, the supported explaining intervention compensated for working memory limitations, such that students scored similarly well regardless of their working memory capacity."
