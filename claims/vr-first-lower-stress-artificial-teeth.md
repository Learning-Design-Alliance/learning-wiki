## Evidence

### Usta Sıla Nur 2025

Usta Sıla Nur, Silva Emmanuel João Nogueira Leal, Keskin Cangül, Tekkanat Hilal, Liukkonen Mikko, Felszeghy Szabolcs. (2025). A comparison of traditional and virtual reality haptic simulator approaches in preclinical endodontic training: Impacts on skill acquisition, confidence and stress. International Endodontic Journal. https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.14236

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Visual analog scale stress ratings at four phases in the randomized two-group study of 40 students. The article reports "significantly elevated VAS 2 and VAS 3 levels" for the artificial-teeth-first group and no significant VAS 1 difference; no effect size is printed.

> "Group 2 reported significantly elevated VAS 2 and VAS 3 levels relative to Group 1 (p < .05). No significant differences were noted in VAS 1 scores between the two groups (p > .05)."
