## Evidence

### Tobase L 2017

Tobase L, Peres HHC, Gianotto-Oliveira R, Smith N, Polastri TF, Timerman S. (2017). The effects of an online basic life support course on undergraduate nursing students' learning. Int J Med Educ 8:309-313. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5985.cbce

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Objective CPR-quality data recorded by the manikin's feedback software for 52 participants, benchmarked against parameters from the 2010 CPR Guidelines (rate 100/min, depth at least 5 cm, ventilation 500–600 ml). Metrics are descriptive; no effect sizes or significance tests are printed.

> "According to the results, 93% used proper hand placement, the average depth of compressions was 48.1 mm, the average release of the chest was 100%, the average number of breaths was 8.2 in 2 min, the average ventilation volume was 742.7 ml, and the percentage of flow fraction was 40.3%."
