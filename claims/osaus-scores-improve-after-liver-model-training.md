## Evidence

### Anschuetz Lukas 2025

Anschuetz Lukas, Tschopp Samuel, Trippel Mafalda, Huwendiek Sören, Trepp Roman, Caversaccio Marco, von Werdt Moritz, Borner Urs. (2025). A Novel Training Model to Improve Diagnostic Accuracy of Cervical Fine‐Needle Aspiration. Head & Neck. https://doi.org/10.1002/hed.28039

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

External assessment by two experienced tutors of 15 residents across three training days, using an adapted version of the OSAUS questionnaire; Table 1 reports day 1 to day 3 means rising, e.g. image recognition 4.5 (0.7) to 4.9 (0.4), p = 0.03, and both FNA categories p < 0.01. No effect size is printed.

> "There was a significant improvement for all three categories on OSAUS (image recognition, US‐FNA performance in‐plane, and US‐FNA performance out‐of‐plane) after the training, as summarized in Table 1."
