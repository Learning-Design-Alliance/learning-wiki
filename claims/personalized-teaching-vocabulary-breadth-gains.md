## Evidence

### Zhao Xiaojuan 2026

Zhao Xiaojuan. (2026). Psychological characteristics of metacognitive strategy use and vocabulary development paths among vocational university students in a personalized english teaching environment. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04595-2

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Between-group comparison of Vocabulary Levels Test gain scores in the 16-week quasi-experimental study (N = 120). Cohen's d computed with the standard pooled-SD formulation yielded d = 0.93 for vocabulary breadth, described as a large effect.

> "The experimental group's breadth gain (M = 13.4 points) was roughly twice that of the control group (M = 6.3 points), and the between-group difference on gain scores was highly significant (t = 4.37, p < .001)"
