`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative case study: 52 Chinese-educated undergraduates over four years received GradeMark feedback on writing; questionnaire and interview responses were analysed. The article reports that despite initial technical obstacles, "all the participants revealed a positive attitude toward online feedback". No effect size is printed.
