## Evidence

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Retrospective matched cohort comparison: PEEP usage "significantly increased" from 0.0% to 46.6% of cases (p < 0.001); no significant 2014 difference between laparoscopic (46.0%) and open abdominal (54.5%) surgery (p = 0.260).

> "The usage of PEEP had significantly increased between 2004 and 2014 (1/818 [0.0%] vs. 381/818 [46.6%]; p < 0.001; Table 3);"

### Kim Seung Hyun 2018 (2)

Kim Seung Hyun, Na Sungwon, Lee Woo Kyung, Choi Hyunwoo, Kim Jeongmin. (2018). Application of intraoperative lung-protective ventilation varies in accordance with the knowledge of anaesthesiologists: a single-Centre questionnaire study and a retrospective observational study. BMC Anesthesiology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-018-0495-7

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Obese subgroup of the same retrospective cohort: PEEP use rose from 0% to 55.3% of obese patients (p < 0.001).

> "this trend was also observed among obese patients (0/32 [0%] vs. 21/38 [55.3%]; p < 0.001)."
