Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Ablations show the semi-Markov controller is the primary driver of behavioral fidelity (removal pushes DKL to 6.81) and Strategist/Executor separation is essential for epistemic fidelity and realism — related
  • BEAGLE achieves higher behavioral fidelity to real student cognitive-behavior distributions than LLM prompting and agent baselines on a Particle Simulator task (DKL = 0.31 vs. baselines ≥ 0.53) — related
  • BEAGLE's error recurrence rate (86.2%) falls within the realistic novice envelope, far above vanilla LLMs (7.8%) and below rule-based SimStudent's mechanical saturation (92.0%) — related
  • In a preliminary 2×3 controlled lesson study across five backbone LLMs, structured student agents produce more differentiated mastery and misconception traces than a baseline simulator — related
