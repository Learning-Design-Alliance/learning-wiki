### AlFaris Eiad 2018

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA comparing SSI total scores across BDI-II severity categories among 328 students: minimal 45.61 (SD 8.07, n=236), mild 41.37, moderate 38.20, severe 35.10. The "F ratio = 13.303" was significant at p<0.0001; Tukey and Scheffe post-hoc tests were applied.

> "Similarly, a statistically significant difference was found between the mean SSI total scores of respondents belonging to the four categories of depressive symptoms (F ratio = 13.303; p< 0.0001)."
