## Evidence

### Bechtiger 2022

Bechtiger, Laura; Steinhoff, Annekatrin; Dollar, Jessica M.; Halliday, Simone E.; Keane, Susan P.; Calkins, Susan D.; Shanahan, Lilly. (2022). Pathways from Maternal Depressive Symptoms to Children's Academic Performance in Adolescence: A 13-Year Prospective-Longitudinal Study. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13685

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path model paths from fifth-grade child functioning to tenth-grade weighted GPA and WIAT-II standardized achievement in the longitudinal community sample. Both positive functioning indicators predicted better performance; depressive symptoms predicted worse performance on both measures.

> "Higher levels of adaptive school behaviors and behavioral regulation were associated with higher GPA and standardized achievement scores, whereas higher levels of child depressive symptoms were associated with lower scores for both academic performance measures."
