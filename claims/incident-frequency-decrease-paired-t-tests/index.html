q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive comparison of coded TRIC incident totals across all 58 participants, first 55 days versus last 55 days of the 12-week curriculum implementation. "a 47.76% decrease in frequency of 149 incidents" was reported.

"According to the study results, there was a 47.76% decrease in frequency of 149 incidents, down from 312 to 163."

Fein and Jones 2015 (2)¶

Lance Fein, Ed.D. & Don Jones, Ed.D. (2015). Using Emergence Theory-Based Curriculum to Teach Compromise Skills to Students with Autistic Spectrum Disorders. Administrative Issues Journal: Connecting Education, Practice, and Research, 5(1), 35-54. https://doi.org/10.5929/2015.5.1.4

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Dependent paired-sample t tests on the five coded incident categories (Table 9). Significant decreases for behavior, teacher-sent, self-sent, and aggression; the group-incident decrease was not significant (p = .896).

"The t tests revealed significant decreases in frequency of behavior, teacher sent, and aggression incidents. The only decrease that was not significant from the first half of the study to the second half was the frequency of group incidents reported."
