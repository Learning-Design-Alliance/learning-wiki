### Michael A. Gottfried 2010

Michael A. Gottfried. (2010). Evaluating the Relationship Between Student Attendance and Achievement in Urban Elementary and Middle Schools: An Instrumental Variables Approach. American Educational Research Journal, Vol. 47, No. 2, pp. 434-465. https://doi.org/10.3102/0002831209350494

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

Baseline fixed-effects regression (school, grade, year fixed effects; Huber/White standard errors clustered by classroom) on the Philadelphia student-year panel. The article reports standardized effect sizes that "range from 0.24a to 0.34a" for days present on GPA in Table 3.

> "The effect sizes, as defined by the standardized regression coefficient X,crx/aF, range from 0.24a to 0.34a, thereby indicating that the attendance-achievement relationship is fairly consistent for the full sample and across elementary and middle school samples."
