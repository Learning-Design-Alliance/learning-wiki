# Work experience positively affects teachers' self-innovativeness but negatively affects their DL self-efficacy, with DL teaching practices not differing by experience

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Work experience had a significant positive direct effect on self-innovativeness (.267, p = .000) and a significant negative direct effect on DL self-efficacy (−.206, p = .001). [→ Vidergor Hava E 2023](#vidergor-hava-e-2023)

## Evidence

### Vidergor Hava E 2023

Vidergor Hava E. (2023). The effect of teachers' self- innovativeness on accountability, distance learning self-efficacy, and teaching practices. Computers & Education, 199, 104777. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104777

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Path analysis results in §4.3 (Table 4) report Work Experience → Self-Innovativeness at .267 (t = 4.006, p = .000) and Work Experience → DL Self-Efficacy at −.206 (t = 3.398, p = .001), with a significant negative indirect effect via DL self-efficacy (−.096, p = .003).

> "Work experience indicated a low effect on Teachers' Self-Innovativeness, and a low negative effect on Teachers' DL Self-Efficacy."

### Vidergor Hava E 2023 (2)

Vidergor Hava E. (2023). The effect of teachers' self- innovativeness on accountability, distance learning self-efficacy, and teaching practices. Computers & Education, 199, 104777. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104777

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

One-way MANCOVA comparing teachers with less than 10 years vs 10 or more years of experience (Table 5): self-innovativeness F = 12.31, η² = 0.06; generating ideas F = 14.37, η² = 0.07; DL teaching practices did not differ significantly (F = 1.74).

> "Work experience was found to affect Teachers' Self-Innovativeness. Differences between shorter and longer work experience were detected in three out of the four sub-dimensions of Self-Innovativeness (generating, presenting, and applying ideas)."
