## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In pre-specified exploratory analyses, higher engagement corresponded with higher knowledge (MD 3.06, 95% CI 0.08, 6.03) and greater self-efficacy for group sessions (MD 1.97, 95% CI 0.27, 3.67); analyses were not statistically tested. [→ Richmond Helen 2016](#richmond-helen-2016)

## Evidence

### Richmond Helen 2016

Richmond Helen, Hall Amanda M., Hansen Zara, Williamson Esther, Davies David, Lamb Sarah E. (2016). Using mixed methods evaluation to assess the feasibility of online clinical training in evidence based interventions: a case study of cognitive behavioural treatment for low back pain. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0683-4

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive exploratory analysis of learner analytics (slides visited, time per slide, resources accessed; upper tertile = higher engagement): "Higher engagement with the course (according to the learner analytics) corresponded with higher knowledge scores"; analyses "were not subject to statistical testing".

> "Higher engagement with the course (according to the learner analytics) corresponded with higher knowledge scores (MD (95 % CI) 3.06 (0.08; 6.03)), greater self-efficacy to deliver the majority of the programme (MD (95 % CI) 1.97 (0.27; 3.67)), larger increases in PABS-PT psychosocial subscale score (MD (95 % CI) 2.26 (−1.1; 7.01), and greater decreases in PABS-PT biomedical subscale score - as desired (MD (95 % CI) -3.51 (−9.26; 2.23)."
