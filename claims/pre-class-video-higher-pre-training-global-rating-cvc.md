`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial of 70 PGY physicians at Taipei Veterans General Hospital; pre-training assessment on a phantom scored by a blinded instructor. The quote reports the between-group contrast: "the highest median score (8.0 [6.5–8.0]) in the POV group" versus F-t-F and control, p < 0.001.

> "The pre-training global rating score significantly differed among the three groups (p < 0.001), with the highest median score (8.0 [6.5–8.0]) in the POV group, compared to the F-t-F side-view (6.0 [6.0–7.0]) and control (4.5 [3.0–6.0]) groups"
