## Evidence

### Chen Yi-Chang; Chen Chen-Jung 2026

Chen Yi-Chang; Chen Chen-Jung. (2026). 360° Contextual Simulation Videos for Undergraduate Nursing Students: Electroencephalography-Based Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Nursing. https://doi.org/10.2196/84720

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Eyes-open resting EEG recorded at baseline and after the fourth module in the quasi-experiment. The article reports "significant postexposure reductions in power at C3, Cz, and C4 for the experimental group (all P<.001)"; control-group changes were nonsignificant. ANCOVA was not performed for C3 because homogeneity of regression slopes was violated.

> "EEG analyses revealed significant postexposure reductions in power at C3, Cz, and C4 for the experimental group (all P<.001), with between-group effects at Cz (F1,35=425.29, η²=.926; P<.001) and C4 (F1,35=5.45, η²=.138; P=.03)."
