## Evidence

### Wallihan Rebecca 2018

Wallihan Rebecca, Smith Keely G., Hormann Mark D., Donthi Rajesh R., Boland Kimberly, Mahan John D. (2018). Utility of intermittent online quizzes as an early warning for residents at risk of failing the pediatric board certification examination. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1366-0

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Observational analysis of deidentified resident records from three residency programs (NCH, UTH, UL); Spearman correlation of composite BESE score with first-attempt ABP CE score. The article reports the score "weakly correlated with ABP CE score" with r = 0.39, p < 0.0001, n = 287.

> "The overall BESE score weakly correlated with ABP CE score (n = 287; r = 0.39, p < 0.0001)."
