## Evidence

### Mühlberg Theres 2026

Mühlberg Theres, Bachner Joachim, Mess Filip, Schmid-Ellinger Jan. (2026). Observing teaching principles in school - Development and Validation of an Observation Protocol (OPTIS). PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0355710

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Video-based piloting in which two observers rated 13 video-recorded school lessons twice using the five-point rating scale. The article reports the overall AC2 was "0.898" with category values from "0.514 and 1".

> "When using the five-point rating scale, the overall coefficient for Gwet's AC2 across both observers was 0.898. With regard to the different observation categories, the AC2 coefficient varied between 0.514 and 1."

### Mühlberg Theres 2026 (2)

Mühlberg Theres, Bachner Joachim, Mess Filip, Schmid-Ellinger Jan. (2026). Observing teaching principles in school - Development and Validation of an Observation Protocol (OPTIS). PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0355710

`q3 · i?` · `design · r3`

Video-based piloting with the six-point rating scale on the same doubly-rated lessons. The article reports the overall AC2 was "0.912" with category values from "0.744 and 1".

> "For observations using the six-point rating scale, the overall AC2 coefficient across both observers was 0.912. For the different observation categories, the AC2 coefficient varied between 0.744 and 1."
