`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Exploratory source-level PSD permutation analyses (10,000 randomisations, α = 0.001 with FDR correction) in 39 participants after exclusion of one for EEG data quality. Between-group clusters met the threshold but their spatial and functional interpretation remains exploratory — the distinctive term theta- and alpha-band appears inside.

> "Selected differences meeting the statistical threshold included lower theta- and alpha-band activity in the LAP group within broad left temporal and occipital regions, lower beta-band activity within broad right temporal and parietal regions, and lower activity within broad bilateral anterior temporal regions."
