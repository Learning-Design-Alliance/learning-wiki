Evidence¶

Malanchini 2020¶

Malanchini, M., Rimfeld, K., Shakeshaft, N. G., McMillan, A., Schofield, K. L., Rodic, M., Rossi, V., Kovas, Y., Dale, P. S., Tucker-Drob, E. M. & Plomin, R. (2020). Evidence for a unitary structure of spatial cognition beyond general intelligence. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0067-8

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Common pathway twin model on the six VR orientation tests in TEDS. "The heritability of the common navigation factor was 64%"; between 66 and 100% of each test's heritability was captured by the common factor, while 64–90% of nonshared environmental variance was test-specific.

"We found that the heritability of the common navigation factor was 64% (95% CIs = 0.41–0.91); shared environmental and nonshared environmental factors accounted for smaller proportions of variance, 8% (95% CIs 0.00 –0.43) and 28% (95% CIs 0.21 –0.36), respectively."
