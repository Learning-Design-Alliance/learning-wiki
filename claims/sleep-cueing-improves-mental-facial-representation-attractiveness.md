## Evidence

### Zhao 2025

Zhao, H., Lin, X., Yuan, K., Hu, X., Wang, X., Su, W., Wang, Q., & Lu, L. (2025). Updating social evaluation during sleep. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00356-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Controlled nap experiment: mental facial representations obtained via reverse correlation image classification and rated for attractiveness by 60 independent online raters. The linear mixed-effects model showed "the main effect of the condition was significant" (F(1, 8984) = 107.07, p < 0.001) with cued faces rated higher (B = 0.18). No standardized effect size was printed for this finding.

> "the Linear Mixed Model (LMM) revealed that the main effect of the condition was significant (F(1, 8984) = 107.07, p < 0.001), and the attractiveness ratings of the faces associated with the cued names were significantly higher than those associated with the uncued names (B = 0.18, SE = 0.02, z = 10.348, p < 0.001)"
