# In Study 2, Content Snapping's immediate visual confirmation resolved learners' referential uncertainty about system recognition

## Evidence

### snap-qual

Jeongmin Rhee, Changhee Lee, Hyunwoo Kim, Kiroong Choe, Bohyoung Kim, Sungahn Ko, and Jinwook Seo. 2026. Penquiry: A Pen-based Interactive In-situ Q&A System Leveraging LLMs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19870 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.19870](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2609.19870)

`q3 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Study 2 (N=16, within-subjects V1 vs. V2) qualitative results: V1 users felt uncertainty (P5: "not clear if the system accurately captured my range"), while V2's instant alignment let participants ask "with trust" (P6). The authors interpret this as mitigating the Gulf of Execution.

> "Participants reported that Content Snapping provided immediate visual confirmation of their intended referents, bridging the gap between action and system perception."
