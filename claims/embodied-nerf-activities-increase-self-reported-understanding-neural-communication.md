## Evidence

### B. D. M. Hatin 2020

B. D. M. Hatin. (2020). Introducing students to neural communication: an embodied-learning classroom demonstration. npj Science of Learning 5:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00077-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

A pre-post survey of 39 students who attended a 2-h lecture, then a 1-h seminar with the embodied activities, in February 2020. The article reports the activities "increased how well students felt they understood" and could explain the concepts; ratings were on a 1-5 agreement scale.

> "As shown in Figs. 1 and 2, the active learning activities increased how well students felt they understood the purpose of these key concepts, and also increased how well they felt they could explain these key concepts to others."

### B. D. M. Hatin 2020 (2)

B. D. M. Hatin. (2020). Introducing students to neural communication: an embodied-learning classroom demonstration. npj Science of Learning 5:18. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00077-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired-sample t-tests on the same 39-student pre-post survey; 14 outliers identified but none removed. No standardized effect size is printed, so no magnitude is reported here.

> "Results of two-tailed paired-sample t-tests demonstrated that all pre-post comparisons were statistically significant, t(38) = 5.49–7.60; all ps < 0.001."
