# In the bacharelado (bachelor in physics) course at UFSC, success rates are 13,4% for women and 16,8% for men (19,6% and 21,4% after correction).

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the bacharelado course, only 13,4% of women and 16,8% of men incomers conclude the course; after excluding recent incomers, success rates are 19,6% (women) and 21,4% (men). [→ Menezes 2017](#menezes-2017)

### Menezes 2017

Menezes, D. P., Buss, K., Silvano, C. A., D'Ávila, B. N., & Anteneodo, C. (2017). Physics courses of the Federal University of Santa Catarina: evasion and gender. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.09165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.09165)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The bacharelado section reports 310 female and 1042 male incomers and 43 female and 176 male completers from 2000 to 2017.1; "apenas 13,4% das mulheres e 16,8% dos homens ingressantes concluem o curso".

> "Como se pode ver na figura 12, resulta que apenas 13,4% das mulheres e 16,8% dos homens ingressantes concluem o curso."

### Menezes 2017 (2)

Menezes, D. P., Buss, K., Silvano, C. A., D'Ávila, B. N., & Anteneodo, C. (2017). Physics courses of the Federal University of Santa Catarina: evasion and gender. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.09165](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.09165)
