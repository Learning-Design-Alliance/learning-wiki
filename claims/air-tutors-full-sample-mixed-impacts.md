# In the full-sample analysis, Air Tutors students performed worse than matched peers on the statewide summative assessment (significant overall and in District B) but showed more benchmark growth in District A and no significant benchmark difference in District B

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Air Tutors students performed worse than their matched peers on the statewide summative assessment, significantly for the combined sample and for District B. [→ Fong 2025](#fong-2025)
`q2 i?` On the district benchmark assessment, Air Tutors students in District A experienced more growth than matched peers, with no statistically significant difference in District B. [→ Fong 2025 (2)](#fong-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Fong 2025

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2025). An evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: Findings from an Arnold Ventures grant. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673538

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Full-sample quasi-experimental analysis matching any student who attended at least one Air Tutors session to observationally similar non-tutored peers via Mahalanobis distance matching with OLS regression. The report states Air Tutors students "performed worse than their matched peers did" on the statewide summative assessment, significant for the combined sample and District B; no effect size is printed for this finding.

> "When the outcome measure was the statewide summative assessment, Air Tutors students performed worse than their matched peers did, with the difference being statistically significant for the combined sample and for District B."

### Fong 2025 (2)

Fong, A., Chen-Gaddini, M., & Huang, M. (2025). An evaluation of the Air Tutors Program: Findings from an Arnold Ventures grant. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673538

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same full-sample matched analysis, using growth of adaptive diagnostic benchmark scale scores from fall 2023 to spring 2024 as the outcome. Air Tutors students "experienced more growth on the benchmark assessment in District A"; the District B difference was not statistically significant, and equivalence was not tested. No effect size is printed.

> "When the outcome assessment was the district benchmark assessment, Air Tutors students experienced more growth on the benchmark assessment in District A, but there was no statistically significant difference within District B."
