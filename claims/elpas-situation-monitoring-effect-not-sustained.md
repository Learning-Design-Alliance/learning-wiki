## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gaupp R 2019

Gaupp R, Dinius J, Drazic I, Körner M. (2019). Long-term effects of an e-learning course on patient safety: A controlled longitudinal study with medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210947

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures analysis of the T-TAQ situation-monitoring subscale in the controlled quasi-experimental study; the immediate post-intervention effect was significant with printed "η2 = .046".

> "Although there was a minor, yet statistically significant, effect on situation monitoring directly after the intervention (F(1, 198) = 9.608, p < .002, η2 = .046), this effect did not remain significant in the follow-up data (F(2, 85) = 1.492, p = .231), so the monitoring hypothesis (iii) is rejected."

### Gaupp R 2019 (2)

Gaupp R, Dinius J, Drazic I, Körner M. (2019). Long-term effects of an e-learning course on patient safety: A controlled longitudinal study with medical students. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210947

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Follow-up analysis of the same study's situation-monitoring scale; the one-year effect was no longer evident (F(2, 85) = 1.492, p = .231). No effect size is printed for the follow-up test.

> "In T1, the situation monitoring hypothesis was still supported by the data, however after one year, the effect was not evident anymore."
