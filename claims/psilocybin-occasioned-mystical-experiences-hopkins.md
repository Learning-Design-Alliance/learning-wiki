The article reports, citing Griffiths et al. (2006), that a Johns Hopkins study found psilocybin occasioned mystical-type experiences under supportive conditions. Two-thirds of participants rated the experience as either the single most meaningful experience of their lives or in the top five, and one-third rated it as the most significant spiritual experience of their life.

> "A 2006 study with religiously/spiritually active adults at Johns Hopkins University found that "when administered to volunteers under supportive conditions, psilocybin occasioned experiences similar to spontaneously occurring mystical experiences and which were evaluated by volunteers as having substantial and sustained personal meaning and spiritual significance" (Griffiths et al., 2006)."
