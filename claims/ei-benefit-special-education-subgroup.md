## Evidence

### Stingone JA 2026

Stingone JA, McVeigh KH, Lednyak L. (2026). Early Intervention Developmental Programming and Childhood Academic Outcomes. JAMA Network Open. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.55890

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Subgroup analysis (n = 7580 EI recipients) replicating the full propensity score pipeline within the special education–need stratum of the LSED cohort. The article reports "an incidence ratio 1.28 (95% CI, 1.23-1.34; 28% greater likelihood) for meeting test-based standards in ELA and 1.17 (95% CI, 1.13-1.20; 17% greater likelihood)" for math. Among children without a determined need for special education, no differences in math and ELA test scores were seen.

> "Among children who were determined to need special education services, children who had received EI had an incidence ratio 1.28 (95% CI, 1.23-1.34; 28% greater likelihood) for meeting test-based standards in ELA and 1.17 (95% CI, 1.13-1.20; 17% greater likelihood) for meeting test-based standards in math when compared with individuals who had not received EI services."
