## Evidence

### Jabbour 2014

Khayrazad Kari Jabbour. (2014). An Analysis of the Effect of Mobile Learning on Lebanese Higher Education. Informatics in Education, 13(1), 1–15. https://informatica.vu.lt/journal/informatics-in-education

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment comparing two parallel sections of the same educational technology course taught by the same instructor with identical materials; section B used mobile 3G devices. Table 5 reports mean total scores of 78 (section A, n=20) versus 84 (section B, n=18), with a printed P-Value of 0.017 for the t-test on total scores.

> "A t-test for two independent samples, sections A & B, was drawn on to assess if a significant variation was witness between section A and section B total scores."
