## Evidence

### Stefan Küchemann 2023

Stefan Küchemann, Malte Ubben, David Dzsotjan, Sergey Mukhametov, Carrie A. Weidner, Carrie A. Weidner, Linda Qerimi, Jochen Kuhn, Stefan Heusler, and Jacob F. Sherson. (2023). The impact of an interactive visualization and simulation tool on learning quantum physics: Results of an eye-tracking study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Relative dwell-time analysis of five areas of interest in the eye-tracking study found "a signiﬁcantly higher relative dwell time on the well spacing (p = 0.013,d = 0.87)" for experts in Task 2, with only a tendency on potential height (p> 0.05).

> "In Task 2, there is a signiﬁcantly higher relative dwell time on the well spacing (p = 0.013,d = 0.87) and a tendency towards a higher dwell time on the potential height (p> 0.05) for experts."

### Stefan Küchemann 2023 (2)

Stefan Küchemann, Malte Ubben, David Dzsotjan, Sergey Mukhametov, Carrie A. Weidner, Carrie A. Weidner, Linda Qerimi, Jochen Kuhn, Stefan Heusler, and Jacob F. Sherson. (2023). The impact of an interactive visualization and simulation tool on learning quantum physics: Results of an eye-tracking study. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.06286)

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

The same dwell-time analysis found "a signiﬁcantly lower dwell time on the tilt parameter ( p = 0.018, d = 0.80)" for novices, meaning experts spent relatively less attention on this irrelevant area; the potential-function difference was only a tendency.

> "Apart from this, there is a signiﬁcantly lower dwell time on the tilt parameter ( p = 0.018, d = 0.80) and a tendency towards a lower dwell time on the potential function (p> 0.05) for novices."
