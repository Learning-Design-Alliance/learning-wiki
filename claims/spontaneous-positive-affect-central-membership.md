## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Paul W Irving 2015

Paul W Irving, Eleanor C Sayre. (2015). Identity statuses in upper-division physics students. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1505.07801](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1505.07801)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Ethnographic video observation of one student in the advanced laboratory course (groups of three students, video-recorded twice weekly for three-hour sessions over a semester). Her unsolicited comment about seeing theory in action was interpreted as evidence of acting as a central member of the community.

> "After the interviews, Sally joined the AdLab classroom in which we took observational data at the beginning of her senior year. In this environment, Sally continues to display her positive affect for physics. However, now these opinions are spontaneous and unsolicited."
