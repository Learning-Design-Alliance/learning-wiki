## Evidence

### Samara Osama 2021

Samara Osama, AlRyalat Saif Aldeen, Malkawi Lna, Ali Mo’ath Bani, Kilani Areej, Alkhalaileh Zain, Alkhatib Lean, Hamad Ibrahim, Alawneh Yazan, Al-Ryalat Nosaiba, Ryalat Soukaina. (2021). Assessment of final-year medical students’ performance in diagnosing critical findings on chest X-ray. Emergency Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10140-020-01893-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-confidence was rated 0–10 per image and averaged across seven questions; the confidence questionnaire had a Cronbach’s alpha of 0.70. "338 participants (63.8%) reported very high self-confidence level".

> "Regarding self-confidence for each question, 338 participants (63.8%) reported very high self-confidence level, 168 participants (31.7%) reported high confidence, and 24 (4.5%) reported moderate to low confidence level."

### Samara Osama 2021 (2)

Samara Osama, AlRyalat Saif Aldeen, Malkawi Lna, Ali Mo’ath Bani, Kilani Areej, Alkhalaileh Zain, Alkhatib Lean, Hamad Ibrahim, Alawneh Yazan, Al-Ryalat Nosaiba, Ryalat Soukaina. (2021). Assessment of final-year medical students’ performance in diagnosing critical findings on chest X-ray. Emergency Radiology. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10140-020-01893-z

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Per-image confidence levels (Table 2): "Answers related to tension pneumothorax had the highest confidence level" at 61.1% very high, versus 48.9% very high for the flail chest image.

> "Answers related to tension pneumothorax had the highest confidence level with 324 participants (61.1%) recorded very high confidence level, while it was the lowest for flail chest image, where only 259 participants (48.9%) recorded very high confidence (Table 2)."
