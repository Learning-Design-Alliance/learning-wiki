## Evidence

### Khrisat and Mahmoud 2013

Abdulhafeth A. Khrisat & Salameh Saleem Mahmoud. (2013). Integrating Mobile Phones into the EFL Foundation Year Classroom in King Abdulaziz University/KSA: Effects on Achievement in General English and Students' Attitudes. English Language Teaching, 6(8). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n8p162

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive questionnaire results covering items on battery life, confusion, discussion hindrance, training needs, assessment difficulty and reduced collaboration. The article reports "21.3% were neutral and 45.3% responded positively"; no inferential test is reported.

> "A summary of the percentages of the seven items shows that 25.3% of the sample responded negatively to the seven items of the third subgroup that is “the disadvantages of using mobile phones in the EFL classroom", whereas 21.3% were neutral and 45.3% responded positively."
