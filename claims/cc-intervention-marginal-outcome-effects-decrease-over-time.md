### Michael J. Weiss 2023

Michael J. Weiss, Marie-Andrée Somers, & Colin Hill. (2023). Empirical Benchmarks for Planning and Interpreting Causal Effects of Community College Interventions. Journal of Postsecondary Student Success 3 (1). https://doi.org/10.33009/fsop_jpss132759

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

FIRC analysis of THE-RCT RCT data estimating marginal (single-semester) effect distributions. The article reports the mean credit effect "decreases to 0.43, 0.25, 0.17, 0.05, and 0.02 credits" and that the "downward trend holds for enrollment"; spread also narrows over time.

> "the mean effect on credits earned starts at 0.48 cred- its in semester 1, and decreases to 0.43, 0.25, 0.17, 0.05, and 0.02 credits in semesters 2- 6, respectively. This downward trend holds for enrollment."
