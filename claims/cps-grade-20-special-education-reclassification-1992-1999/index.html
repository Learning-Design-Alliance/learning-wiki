John Q. Easton 2000¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of CPS student information system records (Part 1 and Table A). The brief reports "only 773 were" classified as grade 20 by 1999, and that these students are now counted either as not tested or as tested but excluded. No effect size is printed.

"In 1992, 6,180 students (in the appropriate age range for grades three through eight) were classified as grade 20. By 1999, only 773 were (see Table A)."
