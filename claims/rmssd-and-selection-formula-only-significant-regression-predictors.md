# RMSSD and the selection test formula score, but not the intelligence score, were the only significant independent predictors of pilot-course completion in multivariate logistic regression

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a multivariate logistic regression, RMSSD (O.R = 1.008, p = 0.011) and selection test formula scores (O.R = 1.36, p = 0.002) were the only significant and independent predictors of course completion; the intelligence score was not significant (p = 0.248). ## Evidence

### Kula Yosef 2025

Kula Yosef, Horosov Roy, Gidron Yori, Ekshtein Aya, Gordon Barak, Iversen Zev, Tehori Omer, Ben-Ari Oded. (2025). Pre-training heart rate variability as a predictor of Air Force Academy completion. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0327406

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression (enter approach) on the full sample of 185 candidates, entering HRV alongside intelligence testing score and selection formula. The article reports RMSSD and the selection formula as "the only significant and independent predictors", with the intelligence score non-significant (Sig. 0.248 in Table 2).

> "RMSSD (O.R = 1.008, 95% CI: 1–1.001, p = 0.011) and selection test formula scores (O.R = 1.36, 95% CI: 1.12–1.66, p = 0.002) were the only significant and independent predictors of course completion"
