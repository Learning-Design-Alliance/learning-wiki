## Evidence

### Kuhn Josepha 2022

Kuhn Josepha, Mamede Silvia, van den Berg Pieter, Zwaan Laura, van Peet Petra, Bindels Patrick, van Gog Tamara. (2022). Learning deliberate reflection in medical diagnosis: does learning-by-teaching help? Advances in Health Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-022-10138-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

One-way ANOVAs on mean prior-experience ratings (5-point Likert scale) checked initial group equivalence; only 35 of 56 participants completed the questionnaire. The conditions "did not significantly differ on prior experience with the symptoms" or diagnoses. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The conditions did not significantly differ on prior experience with the symptoms, F (1, 33) = 2.01, p = 0.150, ηp2 = 0.06, or with the diagnoses F (1, 33) = 0.01, p = 0.922, ηp2 < 0.01."
