## Evidence

### Benjamin 2010

Benjamin, Aaron S., & Tullis, Jonathan. (2010). What makes distributed practice effective? http://www.psych.uiuc.edu/~asbenjam/SuperadditivityReferences.htm

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Subset analysis of 148 cued-recall conditions from the same meta-analysis, a test type that does not elicit the list-strength effect. Mean cued recall at long lags was 0.58 versus 0.54 predicted, t [65] = 3.29.

> "In addition, the mean level of cued recall performance on items in this long lag range (0.58) is higher than predicted by the independence baseline (0.54; t [65] = 3.29)."
