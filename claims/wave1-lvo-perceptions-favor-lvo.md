## Evidence

### Pickering 2011

Pickering, L. E., & Walsh, E. J. (2011). Using Videoconferencing Technology to Enhance Classroom Observation Methodology for the Instruction of Preservice Early Childhood Professionals. Journal of Digital Learning in Teacher Education, 27(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ918901

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent-samples t-tests on 16 parallel survey items comparing the OSO-only section (n = 20) with the LVO-only section (n = 21) in Wave 1. Table 2 shows, e.g., instructor's comments helpful: LVO M = 4.48 vs OSO M = 3.15, t = -5.22, p = .000.

> "LVO students reported that instructor comments, peer comments, general class discussion, and the ability to ask questions during the observation period were more beneficial to their overall observational experience."
