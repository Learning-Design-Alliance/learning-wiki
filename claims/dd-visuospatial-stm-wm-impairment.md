# Children with developmental dyscalculia show impaired visuo-spatial short-term and working memory relative to matched controls

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` DD children performed worse than controls on visuo-spatial STM (Dot Matrix) and visuo-spatial WM (OOO Recall, OOO Processing) measures. [→ Szucs Denes 2013](#szucs-denes-2013)

## Evidence

### Szucs Denes 2013

Szucs Denes, Devine Amy, Soltesz Fruzsina, Nobes Alison, Gabriel Florence. (2013). Developmental dyscalculia is related to visuo-spatial memory and inhibition impairment. Cortex, 49(10). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2013.06.007

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Case-control comparison of 12 DD and 12 matched control children (selected from 1004 screened) on standardized AWMA subtests; permutation testing and bootstrapped 95% confidence intervals showed the "DD group performed worse than the control group" on all three visuo-spatial measures.

> "The two groups differed on measures of visuo-spatial STM (Dot Matrix) and WM (OOO Recall, OOO Processing). 95% bootstrapped confidence intervals were robustly below zero for each measure showing a significant group difference"
