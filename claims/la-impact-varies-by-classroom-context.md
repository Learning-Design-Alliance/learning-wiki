## Evidence

### Xochith Herrera 2019

Xochith Herrera, Jayson Nissen, and Ben Van Dusen. (2019). Student Outcomes Across Collaborative-Learning Environments. arXiv:1808.07076. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.herrera](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.herrera)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Model 3 of the hierarchical linear analysis disaggregated LA use by classroom context (lecture, recitation, lab) and found predicted posttest scores "meaningfully and reliably larger for each LA use (55.7% in lecture, 58.5% in recitation, and 64.9% in lab) than in traditional courses", controlling for pretest scores.

> "The predicted posttest scores are meaningfully and reliably larger for each LA use (55.7% in lecture, 58.5% in recitation, and 64.9% in lab) than in traditional courses (Figure 1)."

### Xochith Herrera 2019 (2)

Xochith Herrera, Jayson Nissen, and Ben Van Dusen. (2019). Student Outcomes Across Collaborative-Learning Environments. arXiv:1808.07076. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1119/perc.2018.pr.herrera](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2018.pr.herrera)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The Findings section reports that raw gains "vary signiﬁcantly across LA uses (from 5.9% in lecture to 15.1% in labs)" and that all exceed the 3.3% gain for collaborative learning without LAs, which the authors say aligns with their a priori categorization of learning environments.

> "The raw gains associated with each LA vary signiﬁcantly across LA uses (from 5.9% in lecture to 15.1% in labs) but are all larger than the raw gain associated with collaborative learning (3.3%)."
