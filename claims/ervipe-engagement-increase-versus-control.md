`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experimental 3-group study of 87 undergraduate health professions students (n=29 each) at a Thai university hospital. Adjusted generalized estimating equation analyses found "Group B showing a significant increase compared with Group A (mean difference=3.93; P=.001)" on DSSQ-engagement.

> "After the 1-month post-SIMBIE follow-up, adjusted analyses revealed positive trends in DSSQ-engagement across all groups, with Group B showing a significant increase compared with Group A (mean difference=3.93; P=.001)."
