### Hisham Ranita 2018

Hisham Ranita, Ng Chirk Jenn, Liew Su May, Lai Pauline Siew Mei, Chia Yook Chin, Khoo Ee Ming, Hanafi Nik Sherina, Othman Sajaratulnisah, Lee Ping Yein, Abdullah Khatijah Lim, Chinna Karuthan. `q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Internal consistency analysis of the EBMQ administered to 320 primary care physicians. The article reports "Cronbach alpha for the overall EBMQ was 0.909" and all corrected item-total correlations above 0.2, which the authors interpret as adequate psychometric properties.

> "Cronbach alpha for the overall EBMQ was 0.909, whilst individual domains ranged from 0.657 to 0.933 (Table 4). All corrected item-total correlation (CITC) values were > 0.2."
