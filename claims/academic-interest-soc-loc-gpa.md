## Evidence

### Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh 2026

Dashtegol Ali Nasirzadeh, Ahmadi Mehrnaz, Hafshejani Nazanin Damsaz. (2026). Locus of control and sense of coherence as predictors of academic achievement among medical and nursing students: a cross-sectional study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09941-x

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

One-way ANOVA across interest groups (high 50.9%, moderate 39.9%, low 9.2%) in the 348-student sample; GPA was also significantly higher among students with greater interest (p = 0.005, η² ≈ 0.08, medium effect).

> "Significant differences were also found by interest in the major: students with high interest reported higher scores across all SOC dimensions (p < 0.001, η² ≈ 0.10, medium-to-large effect) and lower LOC scores (p = 0.020, η² ≈ 0.06, medium effect) compared to those with moderate or low interest."
