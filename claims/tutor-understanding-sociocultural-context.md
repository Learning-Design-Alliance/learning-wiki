## Evidence

### Tamachi Shameena 2018

Tamachi Shameena, Giles James A., Dornan Tim, Hill Elspeth J. R. (2018). “You understand that whole big situation they’re in”: interpretative phenomenological analysis of peer-assisted learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1291-2

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview account from a peer tutor explaining empathy with third-year students as understanding “that whole big situation that they’re in because you’ve just done it or you are doing it”.

> "What I mean by empathising is that you understand that whole big situation that they’re in because you’ve just done it or you are doing it."

### Tamachi Shameena 2018 (2)

Tamachi Shameena, Giles James A., Dornan Tim, Hill Elspeth J. R. (2018). “You understand that whole big situation they’re in”: interpretative phenomenological analysis of peer-assisted learning. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1291-2

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

The authors’ interpretative synthesis across themes in the Discussion, stating that tutors’ grasp of the “wider sociocultural context” was “the most important factor underpinning both the PAL environment and tutor-student relationship”.

> "Peer-tutors felt able to understand their students’ wider sociocultural context, which was the most important factor underpinning both the PAL environment and tutor-student relationship."
