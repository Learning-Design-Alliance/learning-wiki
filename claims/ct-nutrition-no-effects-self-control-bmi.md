## Evidence

### Springvloet 2015

Springvloet, L., Lechner, L., de Vries, H., & Oenema, A. (2015). Long-term efficacy of a Web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention for adults including cognitive and environmental feedback: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health, 15, 372. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-1707-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Linear mixed model analysis of the four-item Brief Self Control measure (Cronbach's alpha = 0.77) in the RCT (n = 1348) from baseline to nine months. No group-by-time differences emerged; no effect size was printed for this finding.

> "Self-control increased over time (p < .001), but no differences between the three groups were found (p = .29)"

### Springvloet 2015 (2)

Springvloet, L., Lechner, L., de Vries, H., & Oenema, A. (2015). Long-term efficacy of a Web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention for adults including cognitive and environmental feedback: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Public Health, 15, 372. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-015-1707-4

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed model analysis of self-reported BMI in the RCT (n = 1347). BMI declined over time in all groups without between-group differences; among overweight participants (n = 682) BMI also decreased (p < .001) with no group differences (p = .30).

> "There was a slight decrease in BMI over time in the total sample (p = .001), but this change was not different between the groups (p = .17)"
