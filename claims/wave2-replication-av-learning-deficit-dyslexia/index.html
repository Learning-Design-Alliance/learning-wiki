Evidence¶

Pasqualotto 2025¶

Pasqualotto, A., Cochrane, A., Venuti, P. et al. (2025). Impaired audio-visual associations in dyslexia: evidence beyond linguistic processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00382-7

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Preregistered replication wave with 87 native Italian-speaking children (29 per group), analyzed with the same continuous-time learning model. In Wave 1 (n = 70) the same pattern held (age-matched vs. DD mean = 0.363). The article states the two-wave design "provides robust evidence for the observed patterns".

"In our preregistered Wave 2 (n = 87) 52, we replicated the finding that both age-matched and reading-matched groups demonstrated higher asymptotic accuracy in the learning task than children with DD (age-matched vs. DD mean = 0.706, CI95 = [0.541,0.932]; reading-matched vs. DD mean = 0.28, CI95 = [0.136,0.436]"
