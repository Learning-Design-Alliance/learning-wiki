q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of reliability findings across included primary studies reports that inter-rater reliability "varied from 0.65 to 0.88, indicating good agreement among the panels". One included study (Abd-Rahman et al.) additionally reported an ICC and Cronbach's alpha of 0.94, and Dalum et al. reported correlation coefficients of 0.69 to 0.77.

"Indeed, the inter-rater reliability for the Angoff standard-setting process varied from 0.65 to 0.88, indicating good agreement among the panels [32, 33]."
