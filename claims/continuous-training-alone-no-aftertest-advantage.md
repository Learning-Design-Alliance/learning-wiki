## Evidence

### Hillemans Vera 2024

Hillemans Vera, Buyne Otmar, de Blaauw Ivo, Botden Sanne M.B.I., Verhoeven Bas H., Joosten Maja. (2024). Self-assessment, and not continuous training, improves basic open suturing skills. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2024.2374101

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

After-test between-group comparisons for the three-group design. Printed composite scores and p-values: transcutaneous "89.3 vs. 87.7 vs. 86.7, p = 0.733"; Donati "83.6 vs. 85.4 vs. 83.5, p = 0.818"; intracutaneous "74.8 vs. 79.7 vs. 73.9, p = 0.495".

> "Even though two out of three groups (continuous training group and self-assessment group) had weekly supervised suturing training on top of their clinical exposure, no significant differences in composite score at the after-test were found for transcutaneous, Donati and intracutaneous suturing for these groups, when compared to the clinical exposure group."
