## Evidence

### Harper 2011

Harper, L. J. (2011). Nursery rhyme knowledge and phonological awareness in preschool children. The Journal of Language and Literacy Education [Online], 7(1), 65-78. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1097164

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental comparison of N=11 experimental children's completion task scores before and after ten weeks of nursery rhyme instruction, tested with the Wilcoxon signed-ranks test. The mean rose from 5.64 to 9.45, and the article reports "significantly different (Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, two-tailed, Z=-2.670, P=0.008)".

> "The pre- and post-test completion task scores for the experimental group were significantly different (Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, two-tailed, Z=-2.670, P=0.008)."
