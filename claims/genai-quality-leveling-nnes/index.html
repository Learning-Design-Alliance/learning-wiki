Evidence¶

Allison E. Connell Pensky 2025¶

Allison E. Connell Pensky, Jordan H. Usdan, Harley Chang. (2025). Generative AI's Impact on Graduate Student Professional Writing Productivity and Quality. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00528-z

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Subgroup analysis within the same classroom study: NNES students' median grade rose from 87.5% to 95.8%, while for NES students (n = 12) the test was not significant, Z = −1.14, p =.25, rrb = − 0.23. The article concludes genAI "had a leveling effect for NNES students".

"did statistically significantly improve the quality of the deliverable in non-native English speakers (n = 15), Z = −3.17, p =.002, rrb = − 0.58."
