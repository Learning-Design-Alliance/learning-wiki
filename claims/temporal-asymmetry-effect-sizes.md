`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Effect sizes from the final RI-CCLPM French solution, approximated as Cohen's d representing expected standardized change following a one standard deviation increase. The contemporaneous effect was "of moderate size (d =0 .33)" and the lagged effect d = 0.25.

> "In French, a similar asymmetry emerged, with a contemporaneous effect of skills of moderate size (d =0 .33) and a lagged effect of self-concept of comparable size (d =0 .25)."

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Effect-size analysis of the RI-CCLPM cross-effects across domains. The alternative cross-effects were "null or negligible in size (d = 0.22)", indicating only the specific temporal effects predicted by the CL-REM were reliably present.

> "In contrast, the alternative cross-effects—i.e., lagged effect of skills and contemporaneous effect of self-concept—were null or negligible in size (d = 0.22), suggesting that only specific temporal effects were reliably present."
