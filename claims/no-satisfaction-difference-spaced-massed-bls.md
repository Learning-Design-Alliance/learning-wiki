### Ranjbar Fataneh 2024

Ranjbar Fataneh, Sharif-Nia Hamid, Shiri Maryam, Rahmatpour Pardis. (2024). The effect of spaced E-Learning on knowledge of basic life support and satisfaction of nursing students: a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-05533-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

SLS-OLE satisfaction comparison (34 items, seven-point Likert) between the spaced and massed groups found "no statistically significant differences between the two groups"; domain means were high in both, with perceived learning highest and learner interaction lowest.

> "The results show no statistically significant differences between the two groups. Notably, the highest score was in the perceived learning domain, while the lowest score was in the learner interaction domain."
