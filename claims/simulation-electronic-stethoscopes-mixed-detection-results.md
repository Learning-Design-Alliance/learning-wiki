`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports on simulation-based learning using electronic/digital stethoscopes, noting "Two randomized controlled studies concluded different results in the detection of heart sounds and heart murmurs, depending on the use of electronic stethoscopes".

> "Two randomized controlled studies concluded different results in the detection of heart sounds and heart murmurs, depending on the use of electronic stethoscopes. Høyte et al. found no differences in the detection rates depending on the stethoscope usage [41]. Better detection rates were found by Mesquita et al. in the auscultation of heart sounds generated by a software [39]."
