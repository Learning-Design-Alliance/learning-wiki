q2 · i? · design · r2

One-tailed paired t-test on absolute distance from the normative target value in the same 18-child play-testing study. Scores decreased from 52.22 to 47.44 with "t(17) = –3.11, p = .003"; no effect size is printed.

"For the bully-related item (target = 50), expectation scores decreased from M = 52.22 to 47.44. A one-tailed paired t-test on distance from target showed a significant reduction, t(17) = –3.11, p = .003. This confirmed H2(a)."
