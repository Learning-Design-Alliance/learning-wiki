## Evidence

### Rafieyan et al. 2014

Vahid Rafieyan, Maryam Sharafi-Nejad, Zahra Khavari, Lin Siew Eng & Abdul Rashid Mohamed. (2014). Pragmatic Comprehension Development through Telecollaboration. English Language Teaching, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n2p11

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation of the same randomized experiment: the control group (mean 9.33) received pragmatic instruction three times weekly for 20 minutes, yet scored lower than the experimental group (mean 18.00), which the article reads as showing "insufficiency of pragmatic instructions" alone.

> "This clearly signifies the influential role of first-hand contact with target language speakers and insufficiency of pragmatic instructions in English as Foreign Language contexts in the development of pragmatic comprehension."
