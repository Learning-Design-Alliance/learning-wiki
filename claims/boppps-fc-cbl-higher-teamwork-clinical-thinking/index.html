BOPPPS/FC-CBL residents self-rated their teamwork and clinical thinking higher than LBL residents on a five-point questionnaire

In a 38-resident randomised trial, end-of-training five-point self-ratings of teamwork and clinical thinking were higher in the BOPPPS/FC-CBL group than the lecture-based group (reported d = 1.18 and 0.96); these are self-report ratings, not skill assessments
