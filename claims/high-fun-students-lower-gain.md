## Evidence

### Ole Eggers Bjaelde 2015

Ole Eggers Bjaelde, Mads Kock Pedersen, Jacob Sherson. (2015). Gamification of Quantum Mechanics Teaching. E-Learn 2014 - New Orleans, LA, United States, October 27-30, 2014. Originally published 2014. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1506.08128](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1506.08128)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Cross-check of gain against three evaluation-survey questions in the 64-student intervention; the fun-to-large-extent group comprised 18 students. The printed gains are 0.13 versus 0.20 and the authors report the difference as significant, though no test statistic is printed.

> "The most interesting result is that the students who had fun actually had a significantly lower gain (0.13) that the average student (0.20)."
