# Parents' naturalistic process praise at child ages 14-38 months predicts children's incremental motivational frameworks at age 7-8

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i2` medium

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Process praise (as a percent of total praise) at 14-38 months correlated with children's motivational framework scores at 7-8 years, r(51) = .35, p = .01. [→ Gunderson Elizabeth A. 2013](#gunderson-elizabeth-a-2013)
`q2 i?` In regression, cumulative process praise predicted later frameworks, β = .34, p < .05, accounting for 11.9 percent of the variance, and remained significant after controls for total praise, parents' theories, SES, overall talk, and child gender. ## Evidence

### Gunderson Elizabeth A. 2013

Gunderson Elizabeth A., Gripshover Sarah J., Romero Carissa, Dweck Carol S., Goldin-Meadow Susan, Levine Susan C. (2013). Parent Praise to 1-3 Year-Olds Predicts Children's Motivational Frameworks 5 Years Later. Child development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12064

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal observational analysis of 53 parent-child dyads from the greater Chicago area, coding praise in 90-minute home videos at 14, 26, and 38 months. The printed effect size was r = .35 ("r(51) = .35, p = .01"); the relation held across intelligence (r = .26) and sociomoral (r = .29) domains and at each observed age.

> "we found a significant correlation between parents' use of process praise as a percent of total praise when children were 1-3 years old and children's motivational framework score at age 7-8, r(51) = .35, p = .01"

### Gunderson Elizabeth A. 2013 (2)

Gunderson Elizabeth A., Gripshover Sarah J., Romero Carissa, Dweck Carol S., Goldin-Meadow Susan, Levine Susan C. (2013). Parent Praise to 1-3 Year-Olds Predicts Children's Motivational Frameworks 5 Years Later. Child development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12064

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Simultaneous regression models (Table 3) tested process praise against confounds one at a time; "none of the control variables significantly predicted children's motivational frameworks over and above process praise", and stepwise methods selected the process-praise-only model as best fitting. No standardized effect size is printed for the regression; only β and variance explained are reported.

> "parents' cumulative use of process praise was a significant predictor of children's motivational frameworks at age 7-8 years, β = .34, p < .05, accounting for 11.9 percent of the variance in children's motivational frameworks"
