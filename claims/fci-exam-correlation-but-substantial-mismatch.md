## Evidence

### Steinberg and Sabella 1997

Richard N. Steinberg and Mel S. Sabella. (1997). Performance on Multiple-Choice Diagnostics and Complementary Exam Problems. The Physics Teacher 35, 150–155. https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2344625

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

The article's concluding summary of its cross-instrument comparisons across four introductory calculus-based classes (28 and 128 students completing both instruments). The authors state the two bullet conclusions and warn that "many physics educators assume that the correlation is greater than our results indicate."

> "There is a correlation between student performance on these two very different assessment instruments. • For certain students and certain questions, responses differ greatly."
