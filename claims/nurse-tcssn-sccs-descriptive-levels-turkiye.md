### Akpinar Havva 2025

Akpinar Havva, Ozcan Mircali Fatmanur, Bulut Ugurlu Nezihe. (2025). The Relationship Between Therapeutic Communication Skills and Spiritual Care Competencies of Nurses: The Example of Türkiye. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-025-02348-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive statistics from the cross-sectional survey of 215 nurses. The SCCS mean total was 99.75 ± 16.88; Table 1 shows 91.6% considered spiritual care necessary and 95.3% believed it affects recovery. The authors characterize TCSSN as moderate and SCCS as high; these level labels are interpretive.

> "the mean total score for the TCSSN was 72.28 ± 15.80. The sub-dimension scores were 30.13 ± 8.82 for Non-Therapeutic Communication Skills, 28.07 ± 6.67 for Therapeutic Communication Skills I, and 15.23 ± 3.44 for Therapeutic Communication Skills II."
