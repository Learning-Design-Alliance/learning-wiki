Audio-Visual Communication Review 1953¶

Audio-Visual Communication Review. (1953). Audio-Visual Communication Review, p. 316 (digest of Spiegelman, Terwilliger & Fearing, The Journal of Social Psychology, 37:189-203, May 1953; and Wolf & Fiske, The Children Talk about Comics, in Communications Research 1948-1949, Harper and Brothers, 1948). https://doi.org/10.1007/bf02713301

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Content-analysis result on female characters' goals and means across socio-economic classes in the 52-strip sample; the digest reports the class tendencies without a printed statistic.

"Goals and means of the females of the various classes indicate a greater concern of the upper class with being loved and lovable, the tendency of the middle class to achieve career recognition by hard work, and the tendency of the lower class to serve others and accept their fate."
