# Low MCAT scorers are more likely to remain low USMLE scorers than high MCAT scorers are to remain high scorers, with weak-to-moderate associations (Cramer's V 0.05–0.24)

> **Currency** · newest study 2016 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Chi-square tests found significant associations between top/bottom MCAT groups and top/bottom USMLE groups for almost all comparisons, with low-scoring comparisons showing higher Cramer's V than high-scoring ones. [→ Gauer Jacqueline L. 2016](#gauer-jacqueline-l-2016)

## Evidence

### Gauer Jacqueline L. 2016

Gauer Jacqueline L., Wolff Josephine M., Jackson J. Brooks. (2016). Do MCAT scores predict USMLE scores? An analysis on 5 years of medical student data. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.31795

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-square tests of association using top/bottom (~20%) cutoff groups on each exam for the five-class cohort (N=1,065); "Cramer's V values for these comparisons ranged from 0.05 to 0.24", with p-values at or below 0.001 for all except three comparisons.

> "Cramer's V values for these comparisons ranged from 0.05 to 0.24, indicating very weak to moderate associations."

### Gauer Jacqueline L. 2016 (2)

Gauer Jacqueline L., Wolff Josephine M., Jackson J. Brooks. (2016). Do MCAT scores predict USMLE scores? An analysis on 5 years of medical student data. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.31795

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Discussion-level interpretation of the same chi-square results: the article states "being a low scorer on the MCAT was more predictive of being a low scorer" than the parallel high-scorer relationship, an interpretation the authors draw from Table 2.

> "the low scoring group comparisons showed higher overall values of Cramer's V than their counterpart high scoring group comparisons, indicating that being a low scorer on the MCAT was more predictive of being a low scorer on Step 1 and Step 2 CK than being a high scorer on the MCAT is predictive of being a high scorer on Step 1 and Step 2 CK."
