Ji Ying 2026¶

Ji Ying. (2026). Academic Integrity in the Age of Generative AI: a Scoping Review of Research on Higher Education Student Voices. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09752-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of demographic moderators reports "male students report higher confidence in handling related academic integrity issues when using GenAI, while females express greater concerns about them", citing Bikanga Ada (2024), and notes some studies found no gender differences.

Ji Ying 2026 (2)¶

Ji Ying. (2026). Academic Integrity in the Age of Generative AI: a Scoping Review of Research on Higher Education Student Voices. Journal of Academic Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10805-026-09752-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Harrington et al. (2025) found that, despite identical institutional policies, "Canadian students were consistently more likely than Korean students to perceive the use of GenAI as unethical", which the authors interpret via individualistic versus collectivist cultural frameworks and power distance.

"Canadian students were consistently more likely than Korean students to perceive the use of GenAI as unethical and in violation of institutional policies"
