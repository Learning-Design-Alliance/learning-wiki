## Evidence

### Aboueisha Hadeel 2025

Aboueisha Hadeel, Abouzeid Enjy, Sallam Moataz A., Talaat Wagdy. (2025). A digital recipe for enhancing clinical reasoning: the role of e-learning by concordance (E-LbC): a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08005-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired t-tests on total SCT scores of the same 60 students across the two themes; both pre-post comparisons reached p < 0.001. The article prints Cohen's d = 4.16 for e-LbC and d = 3.75 for lecture.

> "Students in both groups demonstrated statistically significant improvements in post-SCT scores relative to their pre-SCT scores (Table 2). Moreover, the e-LbC, painless vision loss theme, achieved a greater improvement, with a large effect size (Cohen's d = 4.16), compared with the interactive lecture, painful vision loss theme, (Cohen's d = 3.75)"

### Aboueisha Hadeel 2025 (2)

Aboueisha Hadeel, Abouzeid Enjy, Sallam Moataz A., Talaat Wagdy. (2025). A digital recipe for enhancing clinical reasoning: the role of e-learning by concordance (E-LbC): a quasi-experimental study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08005-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject comparison of score change between the two themes in the same 60-student cohort, reported with non-overlapping confidence intervals. The article states the d of 1.30 "reflects a large effect size".

> "The average change in SCT scores was 17.45 (95% CI: 16.39–18.51) in the e-LbC group and 12.37 (95% CI: 11.54–13.20) in the lecture group, yielding a Cohen's d of 1.30, which reflects a large effect size"
