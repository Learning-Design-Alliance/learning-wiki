### Putt O 2022

Putt O, Westacott R, Sam AH, Gurnell M, Brown CA. (2022). Using very short answer errors to guide teaching. The Clinical Teacher. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.13458

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Secondary analysis of incorrect answers from a formative VSA examination taken by 1417 final-year students from 20 UK medical schools; a single researcher performed thematic analysis of errors, with categorisation discussed by four researchers. The article reports "Four types of error were identified in multiple questions."

> "Four types of error were identified in multiple questions, which could be used to inform teaching and learning. These errors were as follows: Inability to identify the most important abnormal value."
