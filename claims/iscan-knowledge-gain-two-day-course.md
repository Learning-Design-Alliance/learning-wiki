## Evidence

### Janjigian Michael 2021

Janjigian Michael, Dembitzer Anne, Srisarajivakul-Klein Caroline, Hardower Khemraj, Cooke Deborah, Zabar Sondra, Sauthoff Harald. (2021). Design and evaluation of the I-SCAN faculty POCUS program. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02453-2

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Observational cohort study of 23 hospitalists at four NYU teaching hospitals; knowledge measured with a novel 20-item online test administered before the program, after the two-day course, and after 1 year, analyzed with paired Wilcoxon signed rank tests. The article reports "58 and 85% correct, respectively, p < 0.001" and a one-year median of 81%.

> "Participant knowledge increased from before the two-day course to post-course (58 and 85% correct, respectively, p < 0.001). Knowledge scores fell slightly at 1 year (81% correct, p = 0.012)."
