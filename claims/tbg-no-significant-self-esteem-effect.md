Univariate F test for self-esteem was non-significant [F(2, 11) = .240, p = .79]. Descriptively, group B showed the largest pre-post gap, followed by A and control, but the article notes improvement "might not be that significant when the duration of playing was only three weeks."

> "Insignificant difference in the average SE scores among the groups suggested that the frequency of playing TBG did not have any significant effect on the improvement of SE scores."
