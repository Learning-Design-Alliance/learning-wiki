## Evidence

### Sasikala 2016

Sasikala, P., & Tanyong, S. (2016). A Study on Simulation Methods in Academic Success With Reference To Teaching Biology for Education Students. Journal of Education and Practice, 7(11). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1099628

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group study with 100 education students (50 control, 50 experimental). Post-test means were 75 ± 3.574 (experimental) versus 58 ± 2.483 (control), a statistically significant difference (p < 0.001), indicating "the experimental students has the improved cognitive skills than that of the control group students."

> "The results of the present study shows that statistically significant variations between the means of control and experimental group students of education (p< 0.001). The results of the present study evidenced that the experimental students has the improved cognitive skills than that of the control group students."
