# Agent perception followed a U-shape across variants: the mixed temporally-humanlike/visually-nonhumanlike Variant 2 was rated least positively on partnership and agent-perception measures

### Michael Yin 2026

Michael Yin, Angela Chiang, Samuel Rhys Cox, and Robert Xiao. (2026). "It Felt a Bit Eerie": Exploring Humanlike Interactions During Collaborative Writing with an Artificial Agent. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24729

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Exploratory analysis of PMQ and ASA questionnaire data (n=48, 16 per variant) with Mann-Whitney tests found "Variant 2 generally scored lower than the other variants on all factors"; the U-shape was strongly suggested by descriptive statistics and somewhat supported by tests.

> "Our analysis shows that Variant 2 generally scored lower than the other variants on all factors of communicative competence and dependability, humanlikeness in communication, and communicative flexibility, whereas Variant 1 and Variant 3 showed minimal significant differences."
