## Evidence

### Ka Hei Carrie Lau 2025

Ka Hei Carrie Lau, Efe Bozkir, Hong Gao, and Enkelejda Kasneci. (2025). Evaluating Usability and Engagement of Large Language Models in Virtual Reality for Traditional Scottish Curling. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-91572-7_11](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-91572-7_11)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-subjects user study with 36 participants randomly assigned to ChatGPT (n=19) or predefined (n=17) chatbot conditions. Mann-Whitney U tests on Likert usability items showed "rated significantly higher than PD" for accuracy (r = 0.761), with similar significant advantages on intuitiveness, communication, overall experience, and satisfaction; response time showed no significant difference.

> "For accuracy, CGPT (M = 5.89, SD = 1.37) was rated significantly higher than PD (M = 2.12, SD = 1.58), U = 17.5, n1 = 19, n2 = 17, p <0.001, with a large effect size (r = 0.761)."
