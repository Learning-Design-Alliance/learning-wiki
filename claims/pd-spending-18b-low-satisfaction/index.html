Barnett Berry and Karen Cator 2016¶

Barnett Berry and Karen Cator. (2016). Micro-credentials: Driving teacher learning & leadership. Center for Teaching Quality and Digital Promise. http://teachingquality.org/micro-credentials

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The report cites a Boston Consulting Group (2014) study funded by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation on teachers' views of professional development. It states the system "spends $18 billion annually" with only 29 percent highly satisfied; no effect size is printed.

"A recent study funded by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation points out that America’s public education system spends $18 billion annually on professional development, but very few teachers (29 percent) are highly satisfied with their formal learning opportunities."
