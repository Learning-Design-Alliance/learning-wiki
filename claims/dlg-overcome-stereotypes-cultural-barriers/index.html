Evidence¶

Ruiz-Eugenio 2023¶

Ruiz-Eugenio, L., Soler-Gallart, M., Racionero-Plaza, S., & Padrós, M. (2023). Dialogic literary gatherings: A systematic review of evidence to overcome social and educational inequalities. Educational Research Review, 39, 100534. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100534

q2 · i? · review · r2

A two-year case study with mothers at an urban public elementary school in a disadvantaged Tarragona neighborhood showed "the DLG has contributed to overcoming stereotypes about Muslim women wearing hijab among the other participating mothers", notably during dialogue on Lorca's The House of Bernarda Alba.

"the DLG has contributed to overcoming stereotypes about Muslim women wearing hijab among the other participating mothers"

Ruiz-Eugenio 2023 (2)¶

Ruiz-Eugenio, L., Soler-Gallart, M., Racionero-Plaza, S., & Padrós, M. (2023). Dialogic literary gatherings: A systematic review of evidence to overcome social and educational inequalities. Educational Research Review, 39, 100534. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100534

q2 · i? · review · r2

A three-month case study of a DLG on The Odyssey in a primary school found that "The reading of The Odyssey and the dialogues derived from it in the DLG among 9-year-old students broke down racist prejudices toward Carmen", after which peers began helping her with her learning difficulties.

"The reading of The Odyssey and the dialogues derived from it in the DLG among 9-year-old students broke down racist prejudices toward Carmen, an immigrant girl of Latin American origin with special educational needs"
