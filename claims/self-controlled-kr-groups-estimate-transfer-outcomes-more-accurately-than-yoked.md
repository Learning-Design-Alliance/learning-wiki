# On a motor transfer test, self-controlled knowledge-of-results groups estimated their outcomes more accurately than yoked groups, and among self-controlled groups Self-After and Self-Both were more accurate than Self-Before.

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` In transfer, only the main effect for Choice on absolute difference was significant (F(1,42) = 19.29, p< 0.001, ηp2 = 0.31): self-controlled groups (M = 14.68) were more accurate than Yoked groups (M = 23.67). [→ Carter 2014](#carter-2014)
`q3 i3` A separate one-way ANOVA across the three self-controlled groups (F(2,21) = 5.24, p= 0.014, ηp2 = 0.33) found Self-After (M = 12.19) and Self-Both (M = 12.73) did not differ but both were more accurate than Self-Before (M = 19.12). [→ Carter 2014 (2)](#carter-2014-2)

## Evidence

### Carter 2014

Carter, M. J., Carlsen, A. N., & Ste-Marie, D. M. (2014). Self-controlled feedback is effective if it is based on the learner’s performance: a replication and extension of Chiviacowsky and Wulf (2005). Frontiers in Psychology, 5, 1325. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.01325

`q3 · i3`

Laboratory experiment in which university volunteers learned a slider-positioning task under six knowledge-of-results (KR) conditions; transfer AD was analysed with a 2 × 3 ANOVA. "only the main effect for Choice was significant, F(1,42) = 19.29, p< 0.001, ηp2 = 0.31", with self-controlled groups more accurate than Yoked groups.

> "In transfer, only the main effect for Choice was significant, F(1,42) = 19.29, p< 0.001, ηp2 = 0.31, with post hoc analysis indicating the self-controlled groups (M = 14.68, SE = 1.14) were significantly more accurate than the Yoked groups (M = 23.67, SE = 1.76)."

### Carter 2014 (2)

Carter, M. J., Carlsen, A. N., & Ste-Marie, D. M. (2014). Self-controlled feedback is effective if it is based on the learner’s performance: a replication and extension of Chiviacowsky and Wulf (2005). Frontiers in Psychology, 5, 1325. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.01325

`q3 · i3`

Follow-up one-way ANOVA restricted to the three self-controlled groups of the same experiment: "a significant main effect, F(2,21) = 5.24, p= 0.014, ηp2 = 0.33", with Self-After and Self-Both both more accurate than Self-Before.

> "The analysis revealed a significant main effect, F(2,21) = 5.24, p= 0.014, ηp2 = 0.33, where post hoc tests revealed no differences between the Self-After (M = 12.19, SE = 1.86) and Self-Both (M = 12.73, SE = 1.79) groups, but both were significantly more accurate than the Self-Before group (M = 19.12, SE, 1.34)."
