### Márton Benedek and Balázs R. Sziklai 2025

Márton Benedek and Balázs R. Sziklai. (2025). Impact of AI Tools on Learning Outcomes: Decreasing Knowledge and Over-Reliance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.16019](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.16019)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Binomial modeling of paper test scores from the randomized course experiment, comparing average scores to knowledge levels of 0 and 50%, shown in Figure 1. The article reports knowledge "closer to 20% (=ten correct answer) or lower" at the stated threshold.

> "If we draw the line at α= 0.05, students could not know more than 20 true-or-false questions correctly (40% performance), but realistically their knowledge levels are closer to 20% (=ten correct answer) or lower."

### Márton Benedek and Balázs R. Sziklai 2025 (2)

Márton Benedek and Balázs R. Sziklai. (2025). Impact of AI Tools on Learning Outcomes: Decreasing Knowledge and Over-Reliance. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.16019](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.16019)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Conclusion-section summary of the paper-test analysis. Direct comparison with previous years is qualified because exam format, student cohorts, and extra credit assignments differed; the article states it is "not unrealistic to claim that knowledge levels dropped by at least 20 percentage points, and likely more."

> "The data indicate an effect likely between 20 and 40percentage pointscompared to previous years."
