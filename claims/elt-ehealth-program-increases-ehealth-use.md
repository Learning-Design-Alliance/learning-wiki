`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

GEE analysis of the self-developed eHealth Care Use Scale (Cronbach α=0.92) in the RCT, scoring variety of eHealth types (computer or internet, mobile apps, health monitoring systems, wearable devices) and monitoring items. Both group×time contrasts favored the intervention.

> "Specifically, the improvement in the intervention group was significantly greater than in the control group at T1 and T2 (β=3.96, SE 0.42; P<.001 and β=3.87, SE 0.49; P<.001)."
