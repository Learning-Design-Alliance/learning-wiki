Evidence¶

Hanna Onyi Yusuf & Sadiq Mohammed 2013¶

Hanna Onyi Yusuf & Sadiq Mohammed. (2013). Influence of Prior Knowledge Questions on Pupils' Performance in Reading Comprehension in Primary Schools in Kaduna, Nigeria. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 4(1). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.4n.1p.134

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental non-randomized control study: 30 experimental pupils at Ungwar Dosa and 30 control pupils at Ungwar Rimi taught for eight weeks; t-test compared post-test performance. The article reports the null hypothesis was rejected at the 0.05 level, "meaning prior knowledge questions influence pupils' performance in reading comprehension."

"Table 3 shows that the calculated value (7.062) is greater than the critical value of (2.201) at degree of freedom 29 and at 0.05 level of significance. Hence the null hypothesis is rejected, meaning prior knowledge questions influence pupils' performance in reading comprehension."
