## Evidence

### Park 2025

Park, A.T., Colantonio, J., Delgado Reyes, L., Sharp, S.D.S., Koepp, A.E., Bonawitz, E., & Mackey, A.P. (2025). Question asking practice fosters aspects of curiosity in science content in young children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00384-5

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Sixteen-item post-test on lesson content (two questions per lesson): quantile regression controlling for age and race found no significant difference (QA Mdn = 62.5% vs L Mdn = 50.0%); equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

> "We also did not find a significant difference on the science learning measure (Figure 1E, QA: Mdn = 62.5%, L: Mdn = 50.0%, b = 7.74, 95% CI [-1.93, 17.40], t = 1.59, p = .12  pFDR = .20, n = 103)"
