## Evidence

### Ma Yuan Ying 2018

Ma Yuan Ying, Wallace Louise L., Qiu Li Qian, Kosmala-Anderson Joanna, Bartle Naomi. (2018). A randomised controlled trial of the effectiveness of a breastfeeding training DVD on improving breastfeeding knowledge and confidence among healthcare professionals in China. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-018-1709-1

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Confidence (CU-BSSES, range 0–80) was analysed in 135 participants with complete pre-post data after 48 cases had missing confidence data. Intervention-group total confidence rose from "M = 66.49" to "M = 71.81"; the control group was unchanged.

> "The total confidence scores were significantly higher post-DVD than pre-DVD in IG (pre-DVD: M = 66.49, SD = 11.27; post- DVD: M = 71.81, SD = 9.33; t (68) = − 4.92, p < 0.01), but no significant difference was seen in CG between pre- and post-DVD total confidence scores"

### Ma Yuan Ying 2018 (2)

Ma Yuan Ying, Wallace Louise L., Qiu Li Qian, Kosmala-Anderson Joanna, Bartle Naomi. (2018). A randomised controlled trial of the effectiveness of a breastfeeding training DVD on improving breastfeeding knowledge and confidence among healthcare professionals in China. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-018-1709-1

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Mixed ANOVA on confidence outcomes in the same RCT subsample. All three group × time interactions were significant, with η² = 0.26 (total), η² = 0.26 (P & A) and η² = 0.20 (HE); the article states the η² values (> 0.2) "indicate large increases in confidence in the IG group".

> "There was a significant interaction between group and time (pre-post DVD) in total confidence score [F(1,133) = 17.92, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.26], P & A confidence score F(1,133) = 15.66, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.26] and HE confidence score [F(1,135) = 13.90, p < 0.001, η2 = 0.20]"
