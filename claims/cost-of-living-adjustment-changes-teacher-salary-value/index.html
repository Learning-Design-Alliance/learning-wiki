Carver-Thomas 2022¶

Carver-Thomas, D., & Patrick, S. (2022). Understanding Teacher Compensation: A State-by-State Analysis. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/443.847

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Methodological rationale in the report's cost-of-living section: "the value of the same wage differs based on the local cost of living", so adjusted comparisons are described as more meaningful across states. Adjustments use Bureau of Economic Analysis Regional Price Parities.

"The purchasing power of a dollar is not equivalent across states. As a result, the value of the same wage differs based on the local cost of living."
