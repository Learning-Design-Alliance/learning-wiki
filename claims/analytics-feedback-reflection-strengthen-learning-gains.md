The review reports, citing Shute and Ke (2012) and Wouters et al. (2013), that feedback and reflection strengthen learning gains, adding that "Teacher-led debriefs translate fast-paced experiences into explicit knowledge and clear takeaways" (Crookall, 2010).

> "Embedded analytics, timely feedback, and structured reflection play an important role in games and educational simulations by strengthening conceptual consolidation and learning gains (Shute & Ke, 2012; Wouters et al., 2013)."
