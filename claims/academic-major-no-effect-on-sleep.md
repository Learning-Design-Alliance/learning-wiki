## Evidence

### Zhao Yao 2026

Zhao Yao, Zhou Haoyu. (2026). Functional data analysis of college students’ sleep patterns and their relationships with academic performance and social networks: A four-year longitudinal study. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0351120

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

A test of major type on average sleep duration in the analytical sample was not significant (F = 0.12, p = 0.726), and major-stratified functional regression found nearly identical sleep–GPA coefficients (29.4 vs 26.4 minutes/GPA point). No standardized effect size is printed.

> "However, academic major did not significantly influence average sleep duration (F = 0.12, p = 0.726). Major-stratified functional regression revealed similar sleep-GPA relationships for STEM majors (29.4 minutes/GPA point) and non-STEM majors (26.4 minutes/GPA point), with a difference of only 2.9 minutes."
