## Evidence

### Var Eren 2025

Var Eren, Cam Yaren, Pektas Eren, Kuden Cihan. (2025). Does preoperative anxiety influence endodontic skill performance and students' subjective perceptions in an observational simulation-based study?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07957-3

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rank correlation analysis of mandibular procedure measures (n = 93). Anxiety showed a weak negative correlation with confidence (r = −0.230, p = 0.026), an effect in the small range.

> "Confidence was also weakly negatively correlated with anxiety (r = − 0.230, p = 0.026), indicating that higher anxiety was linked to lower self-confidence."

### Var Eren 2025 (2)

Var Eren, Cam Yaren, Pektas Eren, Kuden Cihan. (2025). Does preoperative anxiety influence endodontic skill performance and students' subjective perceptions in an observational simulation-based study?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07957-3

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Spearman rank correlation analysis of maxillary procedure measures (n = 93). Anxiety correlated weakly negatively with comprehension (r = −0.237) and weakly positively with obturation difficulty (r = 0.242), both small effects.

> "Anxiety showed a weak negative correlation with comprehension (r = − 0.237, p = 0.022) and a weak positive correlation with perceived difficulty during obturation (r = 0.242, p = 0.019), indicating that students with higher anxiety tended to report lower understanding and greater difficulty during obturation."
