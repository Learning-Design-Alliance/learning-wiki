# Past academic performance is the strongest predictor of future academic performance, with T1 and T2 GPA jointly explaining 82.69% of the variance explained in T3 GPA

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a dominance analysis of the whole sample, T1 and T2 academic performance together explain 82.69% of the R2 for T3 academic performance, ranking first and second. [→ Vera María; Cortés José A 2021](#vera-maria-cortes-jose-a-2021)
`q2 i?` T2 academic performance ranks first 50% of the time and T1 first 30% of the time across the ten separate degree-level dominance analyses. [→ Vera María; Cortés José A 2021 (2)](#vera-maria-cortes-jose-a-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Vera María; Cortés José A 2021

Vera María; Cortés José A. (2021). Emotional and Cognitive Aptitudes and Successful Academic Performance: Using the ECCT. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 18(24), 13184. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413184

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

General dominance analysis in the R program of 796 students' T3 GPA on seven predictors plus gender; all variables explained 25.6% of variance, and "the two past performance measures explaining 82.69% of the R2".

> "It is not surprising that the first two positions in the ranking are for academic performance in T1 and T2, with the two past performance measures explaining 82.69% of the R2."

### Vera María; Cortés José A 2021 (2)

Vera María; Cortés José A. (2021). Emotional and Cognitive Aptitudes and Successful Academic Performance: Using the ECCT. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 18(24), 13184. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413184

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Dominance analyses run separately for each of the ten degrees show past performance measures usually rank first or second, though "there are large differences between the different degrees".

> "T2 academic performance appears as number 1 in the ranking 50% of the time (and 40% of the time as number 2); T1 academic performance appears as number 1 in the ranking 30% of the time (and 40% of the time as number 2)"
