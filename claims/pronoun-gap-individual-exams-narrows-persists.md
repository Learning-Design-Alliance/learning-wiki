## Evidence

### Brokk Toggerson 2020

Brokk Toggerson, Samyukta Krishnamurthy, Emily E. Hansen, and Chasya Church. (2020). Positive Impacts on Student Self-Efficacy from an Introductory Physics for Life Science Course Using the Team-Based Learning Pedagogy. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.07277)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Grade data disaggregated by pronoun identification (Nshe = 91, NHe = 37) from the TBL IPLS course. The article reports "a signiﬁcant gender gap in the average for individual Exam I which is reduced, but still present, by individual Exam III."

> "We see a signiﬁcant gender gap in the average for individual Exam I which is reduced, but still present, by individual Exam III. As an additional note, the gap in individual total percent is lower than any exam, indicating that other, team-based, course components are compensating for these gaps, if incompletely."
