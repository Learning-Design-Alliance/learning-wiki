## Evidence

### Gökduman Keleş Mine; Göçmez Sema 2026

Gökduman Keleş Mine; Göçmez Sema. (2026). The Effect of Personalised Feedback in Simulation on Knowledge and Skills in the Management of Bleeding During Pregnancy: A Parallel Randomized Controlled Trial. Nursing in Critical Care. https://doi.org/10.1111/nicc.70603

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank within-group analyses in the RCT show both conditions improved satisfaction and self-confidence from pre- to post-test, indicating simulation itself was effective independent of feedback type.

> "Within‐group analyses revealed statistically significant increases in the total scores of the Student Satisfaction and Self‐Confidence in Learning Scale in both the study and control groups (Study group: Z = −4.703, p < 0.001; Control group: Z = −4.703, p < 0.001)."
