## Evidence

### Velásquez-Rojas F 2022

Velásquez-Rojas F, Fajardo JE, Zacharias D, Laguna MF. (2022). Effects of the COVID-19 pandemic in higher education: a particular case from the perspective of complex systems. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0275352

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey-based comparison of motivation (M) across face-to-face (2019, 81 students) and virtual (2020, 92 students) contexts in Physics II at UNLP, plotted in Fig. 3b. The article reports "the impact of the virtual context on the motivation of students" as a decrease for all groups; Table 3 shows relative changes of -17.5% (HA), -2.2% (AA), -23.7% (LA).

> "Our results clearly report the impact of the virtual context on the motivation of students, no matter the group they belong to."
