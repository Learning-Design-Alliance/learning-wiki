### Stanisław D. Głażek 2008

Stanisław D. Głażek. (2008). Heuristic model of teaching. https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.4796 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0804.4796](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0804.4796)

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument within the MOT's kinematical part (Section III.C): effective teaching is modeled as establishing the relation between teacher's and student's frames of reference, then tuning communication through hands-on activity associating concrete events with words and concepts. This is a model-derived prediction, not a tested result.

> "T ﬁrst tries to ﬁnd out what statements S already knows regarding the subject of study and what S means by them, i.e., what events correspond to these statements according to S."
