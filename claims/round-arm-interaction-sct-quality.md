`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multivariate analysis of all SCTs found the round effect was confined to the trained arm; the control arm's SCT quality dropped between rounds (p = 0.016).

> "While the second round (all SCTs taken as a whole) was not associated with SCTs quality, there was an interaction between the round and the randomization arm (p = 0.001) meaning that round 2 was a prognostic factor for SCT quality only in the trained arm"
