Interaction between network constraint and classroom section in OLS models predicting physics grades (Fig. 5), for the 67-student analytic sample. The article interprets the positive slopes as consistent with interrogation logic in cohesive networks and the negative Mixed slope as benefiting from structural holes.

> "According to the plot, both Traditional (red) and Treatment sections (green) show positive and statistically the same slopes, whereas the effect of network constraint is negative for the Mixed section (blue)."
