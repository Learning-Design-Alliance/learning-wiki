## Evidence

### Gordillo 2024

Gordillo, A.; López-Fernández, D.; Mayor, J. (2024). Examining and Comparing the Effectiveness of Virtual Reality Serious Games and LEGO Serious Play for Learning Scrum. Appl. Sci. 2024, 14, 830. https://doi.org/10.3390/app14020830

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre/post study: 22 students in group A played the VR serious game for 40–45 min then took a 10-question post-test. Wilcoxon signed-ranks test found significant gains with "large (r = 0.59) in group A". Mean pre-test 3.9, post-test 7.3 (descriptive means from Table 2).

> "The learning performance, determined by the difference between post-test scores and pre-test scores, exhibited statistical significance in both groups. Its effect size was large (r = 0.59) in group A and medium (r = 0.35) in group B."
