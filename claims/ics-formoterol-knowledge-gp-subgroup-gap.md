## Evidence

### Çifci İ. 2025

Çifci İ., Gök U., Yorgancıoğlu A. (2025). A current assessment of family physicians’ knowledge and attitudes toward asthma management in primary care in Turkey according to the GINA strategy report. NPJ Primary Care Respiratory Medicine 36, 5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41533-025-00468-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Knowledge item on reliever/maintenance medication in the cross-sectional survey of 596 physicians: "77.2%" correctly identified the inhaled corticosteroid plus formoterol combination; terbutaline was least chosen at 5.2%.

> "When asked which medication could be used both as a reliever and for maintenance therapy in asthma, the majority of physicians (77.2%) correctly identified the inhaled corticosteroid plus formoterol combination. The least frequently chosen option was terbutaline (5.2%) (Fig. 2)."

### Çifci İ. 2025 (2)

Çifci İ., Gök U., Yorgancıoğlu A. (2025). A current assessment of family physicians’ knowledge and attitudes toward asthma management in primary care in Turkey according to the GINA strategy report. NPJ Primary Care Respiratory Medicine 36, 5. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41533-025-00468-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Subgroup chi-square analysis with adjusted residuals (Table 1): general practitioners chose ICS + formoterol at 73.7% (AR −2.4) and terbutaline at 8.3% (AR 4.0); specialists chose terbutaline 0.0% (AR −3.9). No effect size is reported.

> "In the subgroup analysis of responses regarding the medication used both as a reliever and for maintenance, general practitioners selected the correct options (ICS + formoterol) significantly less frequently than expected, while selecting incorrect options (salmeterol alone and terbutaline) significantly more often (Table 1)."
