`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Ed Trust analysis of the 18 MSIs (about 15% of the sample). The MSI average access score was 187 in 2000 and 182 in 2020, and no MSI earned a failing grade in 2020, in contrast to the overall sample distributions.

> "In both 2000 and 2020, 100% of HBCUs and predominantly Black institutions (PBIs) in the sample earned A grades for Black student access."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Executive-summary finding from the same access-score analysis; the report adds that in 2020, 7 of the top 10 scores were held by HBCUs or PBIs, and most of the highest-scoring institutions were PBIs or HBCUs established to serve Black students.

> "In 2000, 6 of the top 10 Black student access scores were held by HBCUs or Predominantly Black Institutions (PBIs)."
