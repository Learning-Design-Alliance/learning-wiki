## Evidence

### Zhang Feng 2026

Zhang Feng, Chen Zhi Lan, Qi Li En, Wang Xiao Yan, Zhang Li, Zhang Jing, Wang Ping. (2026). Pre–post changes in operating room nurses’ knowledge and attitudes toward medical device–related pressure injuries after a problem-based and simulation-based training program: a single-group pretest–posttest study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09147-1

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Within-participant pre–post comparison across the three PZ-PUKT domains (n = 80); Table 3 reports Holm-adjusted P < 0.001 with Cohen's dz of 1.557, 1.293, and 1.671 respectively. The article notes staging and wound description "remained below levels reported in other nurse populations".

> "Accuracy in the risk factors/prevention domain improved from 64.57% to 78.88%, in the staging domain from 50.75% to 68.90%, and in the wound description domain from 51.46% to 72.33% (all P < 0.001; Table 4)."
