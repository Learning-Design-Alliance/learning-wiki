## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After matching for date of birth, preterm infants had increased odds of special educational needs at KS4 (fully adjusted OR 1.39, 95% CI 1.14–1.68). [→ Odd David 2016](#odd-david-2016)

## Evidence

### Odd David 2016

Odd David, Evans David, Emond Alan. (2016). Preterm Birth, Age at School Entry and Long Term Educational Achievement. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0155157

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Conditional regression analysis of the ALSPAC cohort (SEN measured in 11,100 infants) comparing preterm (<37 weeks) with term infants matched within calendar month of date of birth, fully adjusted for social, antenatal and intrapartum confounders. The fully adjusted SEN odds ratio was "OR 1.39 (1.14–1.68)".

> "However when using SEN as the outcome measure a similar attenuating profile is seen (Matched for DOB; OR 1.39 (1.14–1.68) vs EDD and same year of schooling, OR 1.21 (0.97–1.52))."
