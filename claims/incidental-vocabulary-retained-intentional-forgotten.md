## Evidence

### Ponniah 2011

Ponniah, R. J. (2011). The Effectiveness of the Comprehension Hypothesis: A Review on the Current Research on Incidental Vocabulary Acquisition. i-manager's Journal on English Language Teaching, 1(2), April-June 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071042

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Re-analysis (Mason & Krashen 2010) of File and Adams' data reported in the review. Table 1 gives pretest, posttest and delayed scores for Incidental (Reading), Integrated, and Isolated groups; the incidental group's gains persisted at delayed test while isolated-group gains fell back.

> "Re analysis of the data (Mason and Krashen 2010) of File and Adam confirms that subjects could retain the incidentally acquired words and the focus-on-form words (intentional learning) were rapidly forgotten in 16 days, after the treatment period"
