Sturgiss Elizabeth, Haesler Emily, Elmitt Nicholas, van Weel Chris, Douglas Kirsty. (2017). Increasing general practitioners' confidence and self-efficacy in managing obesity: a mixed methods study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014314

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive pre/post survey results for outcome-expectation items shown in figure 1 of the pilot study. The article reports median-score improvements in outcome expectations; no effect size or hypothesis test was reported.

> "Both the median score for the GPs' perceptions that counselling made a difference to patient behaviour and the median score for belief that the GP can empower a patient to change their behaviour indicated improvements in GPs' expectations of outcomes."
