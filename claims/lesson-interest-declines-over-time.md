Ayame Tamura, Kou Murayama, Ryo Ishii, Michiko Sakaki and Ayumi Tanaka. (2022). The effect of low-intensity exercise on emotional and cognitive engagement in the classroom. npj Science of Learning 7:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00125-y

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

> "The effect of the time trends was not signiﬁcant except for the model that predicted interest ( B = −0.06, t = −4.82, P < 0.001), which indicates that the participants experienced less interest over time (i.e., as the lessons progressed)."
