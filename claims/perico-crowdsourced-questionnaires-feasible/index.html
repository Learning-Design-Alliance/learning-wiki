Vania Dimitrova & Paul Brna 2016¶

Vania Dimitrova & Paul Brna. (2016). From Interactive Open Learner Modelling to Intelligent Mentoring: STyLE-OLM and Beyond. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:332–349. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0087-3

q2 · i? · design · r2

Evaluation studies of the Perico interactive agent, developed in the EU ImREAL project, which modelled learners' cultural knowledge using crowdsourced knowledge extracted from DBpedia rather than bespoke domain models. The article reports feasibility for questionnaire generation; no effect sizes are printed.

"A series of evaluation studies with Perico (Denaux et al. 2014;Thakker et al. 2014) confirmed the feasibility of the approach for generating interactive questionnaires to obtain profiles of users for adaptation."
