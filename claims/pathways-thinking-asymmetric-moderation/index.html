Chen Y 2026¶

Chen Y (2026) When universities advocate GAI but practice falls short: student appraisals and creative process engagement in art education. Front. Psychol. 17:1955192. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1955192

q2 · i0 · associational · r2

PLS-SEM moderation tests in the 425-respondent survey. The buffering predictions for hindrance (H3a, H3c) were not supported; the interaction term was "nonsignificant" for the hindrance pathway.

"Pathways thinking did not significantly moderate the relationship between inconsistency and hindrance appraisal ( β = −0.060, p = 0.182), and the corresponding conditional indirect association was nonsignificant (β = 0.008, p = 0.251)."

Chen Y 2026 (2)¶

Chen Y (2026) When universities advocate GAI but practice falls short: student appraisals and creative process engagement in art education. Front. Psychol. 17:1955192. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1955192

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same PLS-SEM model, challenge-pathway moderation. The interaction was "positively associated with challenge appraisal" and the conditional indirect effect through challenge appraisal was significant, supporting H3b and H3d.

"In contrast, the interaction between pathways thinking and inconsistency was positively associated with challenge appraisal (β = 0.172, p < 0.001), and the corresponding positive conditional indirect association through challenge appraisal was significant (β = 0.075, p < 0.001), supporting H3b and H3d."
