Evidence¶

survey-1¶

Weihao Qu, Ling Zheng, Chris Buzaid, and Daniel Crawford. 2026. LearnAI: Just-in-Time AI Co-Creation Across Disciplines at a University. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19164

q2 · i? · design · r2

Post-session survey in the Wide-Exposure Layer, administered in 7 of 18 courses (estimated combined enrollment approximately 120 students) with 58 responses and 54 consenting to research use, an estimated 48% participation rate. The article reports frequent descriptions of AI as "cheating" or fears of hallucination, underscoring the gap between passive consumption and confident authorship.
