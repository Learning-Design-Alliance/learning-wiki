## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Gerry Stahl 2008

Gerry Stahl. (2008). Yes we can! Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-008-9055-3

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

A case study in which students collaborated, allowing researchers to observe their quandaries, while non-collaboration-support software mediated the task. Computer responses to partially correct answers are "likely to be inscrutable" because the programming is opaque, so students attribute difficulties to the technology.

> "In an insightful case study, Annika Lantz-Andersson shows how students working in a CSCL environment may attribute their problems to the technology rather than to their own work."
