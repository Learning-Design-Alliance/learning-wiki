## Evidence

### Gu Xiaoxia 2024

Gu Xiaoxia, Hassan Norlizah Che, Sulaiman Tajularipin, Wei Zhixia, Dong Jingyi. (2024). The impact of video game playing on Chinese adolescents’ academic achievement: Evidence from a moderated multi-mediation model. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0313405

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap parallel mediation analysis (5,000 samples, 95% bias-corrected CIs) on the 824-student CEPS sample. Video game playing predicted lower self-educational expectation (β = -0.094, p < 0.01), which predicted higher achievement (β = 0.217, p < 0.001); the indirect effect was "significant and negative".

> "through self-educational expectation, the indirect effect of video game playing on academic achievement was significant and negative (β = -0.020, p < 0.01, 95%CI = [-0.037, -0.006])"
