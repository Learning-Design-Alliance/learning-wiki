### Kalani Hettiarachchilage 2023

Kalani Hettiarachchilage, Neel Haldolaarachchige. (2023). Effective Model with Personalized Online Teaching and Learning Science in the Era of ChatGPT. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.09545)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Grading-based observational analysis across four online physics courses over two semesters. The authors identified ChatGPT-generated answers by checking steps, processes, wordings, definitions, and formulas in written student work during manual grading. Percentages are descriptive; no statistical test is reported.

> "Results suggest that the use of ChatGPT on assessments is very low (below 12%) in three courses and close to 25% in one particular course. Further analysis shows that the use of ChatGPT may correlate with students' majors and their attitudes toward learning Physics, other than the difficulty level of the courses."
