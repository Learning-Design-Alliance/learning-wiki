q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Ethnographic analysis of the Cooper's Hawk problem-posing space (Findings). Macky saw the hawk as a sign of a thriving ecosystem, Selena valued safety and wanted it removed, Garret wanted raptors to control House Sparrows, and Isaak and McKenna expressed uncertainty about the hawk's death and diet.

"Because student-ornithologists valued different things and therefore perceived the situation's priorities in different ways, there was no collective and agreed upon definition of what the specific issue was, nor did student-ornithologists seem to have the action-related knowledge to know what to do."
