## Evidence

### Yang Yu 2026

Yang Yu, Xue Jiarui, Hu Lingfei, Fang Kai, Wei Chaobing, Wu Changfan, Jin Huixiang. (2026). A combined PBL and flipped classroom intervention enhances self-directed learning ability and empathy in ophthalmology undergraduates. Scientific Reports 16, 11696. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-47584-6

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Between-group comparison on the 60-item, five-dimension Self-Directed Learning Ability Scale after class. Table 2 prints post-class Cohen's d of 1.23 (learning awareness), 0.75 (learning strategies), 1.11 (learning behavior), 0.85 (learning evaluation) and 0.97 (interpersonal relationships), all P < 0.001; pre-class differences were all non-significant (P > 0.05).

> "After the intervention, the PBL-FC group demonstrated significantly higher scores across all dimensions and total score in comparison to the traditional one (all P < 0.05)."
