### Sundstrom 2024

Sundstrom, M., Simpfendoerfer, L. N., Tan, A., Heim, A. B., & Holmes, N. G. (2024). Who and what gets recognized in peer recognition. arXiv. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010127](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010127)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Thematic coding analysis of explanations for nominations of strong peers, with a codebook grouped into knowledge, processes, interactions, and other categories; Fuzzy Kappa values for three coder pairs were all greater than 0.8. The article reports "men nominate men more than women because of the ways they interacted, such as being helpful" in the lab course only.

> "we find a difference in students' written explanations in the lab course, where men nominate men more than women because of the ways they interacted, such as being helpful, but not in the lecture course, where men and women nominate men and women for similar skill sets."
