## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Photons from the rear side of a fast-moving cube escape forward because the cube's velocity exceeds the photon's horizontal velocity component toward the cube. [→ U Kraus 2007](#u-kraus-2007)

## Evidence

### U Kraus 2007

U Kraus. (2007). First-person visualizations of the special and general theory of relativity. The European Journal of Physics (accepted for publication). https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.3454 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0708.3454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0708.3454)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Explanatory analysis accompanying the simulation in section 2.1 (illustrated in figure 3): the visibility of the rear '4' face, surprising at first sight, is explained because the cube outruns the photon's horizontal component, so the photon escapes.

> "Each component by itself is smaller than the speed of light. The horizontal component can, therefore, be surpassed by the cube velocity. In this case, the cube outruns the photon and the distance between the photon and the cube increases while the cube moves to the right and the photon towards the observer; the photon escapes."
