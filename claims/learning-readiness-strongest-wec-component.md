# Learning readiness is the WEC component most strongly associated with academic outcomes (0.1–0.15 SD)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Among the three WEC constructs, the learning readiness index shows the largest effect sizes on NAPLAN and PAT outcomes, in the 0.1 to 0.15 range. [→ Marrone 2024](#marrone-2024)

## Evidence

### Marrone 2024

Marrone, Lam, Barthakur, Brinkman, Dawson, & Gabriel. (2024). The Relationship between Well-being and Academic Achievement: A Comprehensive Cross-Sectional Analysis of System Wide Data from 2016–2019. Journal of Learning Analytics, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8357

`q2 · i1` · `associational · r2`

In the same hierarchical mixed-model analysis of linked WEC and NAPLAN/PAT data, the learning readiness index (academic self-concept, perseverance, flow) showed the largest standardized coefficients among the three WEC indexes, "in the 0.1 to 0.15 range" (e.g., adjusted NAPLAN numeracy 0.119), all positive and statistically meaningful.

> "Learning readiness has the largest effect sizes in the 0.1 to 0.15 range. All predictor and academic effect sizes estimated were positive and statistically meaningful, with no confidence interval crossing the critical value of zero."
