Mark A. Smylie 2001¶

Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-professional-development-chicago-supporting-effective-practice

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive finding from the 1997 and 1999 citywide teacher surveys comparing professional development experiences across teacher and school groups. The report also notes about 20 percent of elementary and 41 percent of high school teachers reported inadequate professional development support for school changes. No effect sizes are printed.

"Moreover, some teachers who need the strongest support from high-quality professional development get less of it. These include high school teachers generally, beginning elementary school teachers, and teachers who work in large schools."
