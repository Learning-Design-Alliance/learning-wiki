# Post-test van Hiele levels did not differ significantly between the Geometer's Sketchpad group and the traditional instruction group on any angle concept

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Independent samples t-tests found no significant difference between experimental and control groups on posttest 1 (p = .94), posttest 2 (p = .49), or posttest 3 (p = 0.33). [→ Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu 2013](#poh-geik-tieng-leong-kwan-eu-2013)

## Evidence

### Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu 2013

Poh Geik Tieng & Leong Kwan Eu. (2013). Improving Students' Van Hiele Level Of Geometric Thinking Using Geometer's Sketchpad. The Malaysian Online Journal of Educational Technology, 2(3). www.mojet.net. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1086363

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent samples t-tests comparing groups after intervention (n = 16 experimental, n = 15 control). Posttest 3: t(14) = 1.00, p = 0.33, effect size 0.07; posttest 1: t(29) = -0.08, p = .94; posttest 2: t(29) = 0.70, p = .49. No effect sizes above small were reported.

> "the mean van Hiele level of geometric thinking of the students in the experimental group (M = 2.00, SD = 0.00) was not significantly different than the mean van Hiele's level of geometric thinking of the students in the control group (M = 1.93, SD = 0.26), t (14) = 1.00, p = 0.33"
