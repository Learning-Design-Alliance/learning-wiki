## Evidence

### Saqr and López-Pernas 2022

Mohammed Saqr, Sonsoles López-Pernas. (2022). The Curious Case of Centrality Measures: A Large-Scale Empirical Investigation. Journal of Learning Analytics. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7415

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Meta-analysis found betweenness centrality at "r=0.38 [CI 0.33:0.42]" with I²=45%. The authors note "The low heterogeneity points to a consistent indicator of success" despite the lower correlation magnitude.

> "The combined correlation coefficient for betweenness centrality was markedly lower than that of the degree centralities (r=0.38 [CI 0.33:0.42]), with a lower predictive interval [0.07;0.62] and lower heterogeneity (I²=45%)."
