`q1 · i3` · `design · r2`

Experiment 2 correlation analysis among 705 simulated agents without real data, relating cognitive-state actions to question-answering accuracy via Pearson coefficients. The article reports accuracy "negatively correlated with workload (r = -0.37) and confusion ( r = -0.41)" and positively with valid focus, course following, and engagement, echoing Lei et al. per the article.

> "We find that answer accuracy is negatively correlated with workload (r = -0.37) and confusion ( r = -0.41), and positively correlated with curiosity (r = 0.17), valid focus (r = 0.37), course following (r = 0.39), engagement (r = 0.36)."
