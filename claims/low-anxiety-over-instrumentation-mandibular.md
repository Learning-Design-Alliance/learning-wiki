## Evidence

### Var Eren 2025

Var Eren, Cam Yaren, Pektas Eren, Kuden Cihan. (2025). Does preoperative anxiety influence endodontic skill performance and students' subjective perceptions in an observational simulation-based study?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07957-3

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square comparison of apex accuracy categories between anxiety groups during mandibular procedures (n = 93). Over-instrumentation occurred in 30.4% of low-anxiety versus 11.4% of high-anxiety cases (p = 0.048); optimal and short placements did not differ significantly.

> "a statistically significant difference was found in the proportion of over-instrumented cases, which was higher in the low-anxiety group (30.4%) compared to the high-anxiety group (11.4%) (p = 0.048)."
