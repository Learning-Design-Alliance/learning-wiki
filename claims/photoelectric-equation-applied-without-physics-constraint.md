Edward F. Redish and Eric Kuo. (2014). Language of physics, language of math: Disciplinary culture and dynamic epistemology. [doi:10.1007/s11191-015-9749-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11191-015-9749-7)

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Instructor-assigned quiz problem in the modern physics section of a calculus-based physics class for engineers, reported by the authors; the modified problem asked what happens when wavelength is lengthened beyond one that already emitted no electrons, and "nearly a quarter of the class responded that after the modification, there would be electrons knocked out."
