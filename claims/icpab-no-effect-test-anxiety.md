Meta-analysis of two intervention samples (n = 68 students) from a single one-day RCT (Mavilidi et al.) measuring test anxiety. The pooled SMD = 0.16 was not significant (p = 0.50) with I2 = 0%; the article labels it "moderate to large" despite the trivial magnitude.

> "Two intervention samples (n = 68 students) from a single study [60], which was meta analyzed for test-anxiety as a mental health outcome provided a moderate to large pooled effect size (SMD = 0.16, 95% CI [−0.31 to 0.64], p = 0.50; I2 = 0%, p = 0.71) favoring the IcPAB intervention"
