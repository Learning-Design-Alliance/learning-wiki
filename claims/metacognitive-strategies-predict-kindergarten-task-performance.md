## Evidence

### Heyi Zhang 2014

Heyi Zhang, David Whitebread. (2014). Linking parental scaffolding with self-regulated learning in Chinese kindergarten children. Faculty of Education, University of Cambridge. https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Hierarchical multiple regression with bootstrap method on observational ratings from 130 Chinese kindergarten children's child-alone puzzle and origami tasks, controlling for child gender and age. The model was significant: "R2=.72, F (5, 124) =62.36, p<.001"; only metacognitive strategic behaviours significantly predicted performance (t(124)=11.24, p<.001).
