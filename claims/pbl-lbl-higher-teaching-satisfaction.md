## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Liu CX 2020

Liu CX, Ouyang WW, Wang XW, Chen D, Jiang ZL. (2020). Comparing hybrid problem-based and lecture learning (PBL + LBL) with LBL pedagogy on clinical curriculum learning for medical students in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000019687

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-analysis of the 9 studies reporting teaching satisfaction at three levels (satisfactory, ordinary, poor). The satisfactory-rating contrast favored PBL+LBL: "RR = 2.58, 95% CI: 1.84∼3.62".

> "the proportion of students evaluating teaching as satisfactory was significantly higher in PBL + LBL group than in LBL group (RR = 2.58, 95% CI: 1.84∼3.62, P < .00001)"

### Liu CX 2020 (2)

Liu CX, Ouyang WW, Wang XW, Chen D, Jiang ZL. (2020). Comparing hybrid problem-based and lecture learning (PBL + LBL) with LBL pedagogy on clinical curriculum learning for medical students in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000019687

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Null findings from the same satisfaction meta-analysis: the ordinary-rating contrast showed "no statistically significant difference between 2 groups (RR = 0.72, 95% CI: 0.47∼1.10, P = .13)", and the poor-rating contrast was likewise non-significant (RR = 0.49, P = .10). Equivalence was not tested.

> "the proportion of students evaluating teaching as ordinary demonstrated no statistically significant difference between 2 groups (RR = 0.72, 95% CI: 0.47∼1.10, P = .13)"
