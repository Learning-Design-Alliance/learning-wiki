q4 · i? · causal · r3

GMRT-4 reading comprehension subtest in the RCT showed "did not differ between groups" at posttest and follow-up; the follow-up "Hedges’g = .16" was descriptive, not a significant difference.

"Scores on the norm-referenced measure of reading comprehension (see Table 5 ) did not differ between groups at posttest (Hedges’ g = /C0 .02) or at follow-up, although the effect size (Hedges’g = .16) was in the low-average range."
