# Teachers judging student performance mostly use performance cues and, to a lesser extent, student cues

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The review reports, citing prior studies with the same tasks, that teachers mostly use performance cues but also frequently student cues when judging students' performance. [→ van de Pol 2024](#van-de-pol-2024)

## Evidence

### van de Pol 2024

van de Pol, J., & Oudman, S. (2024). Teachers' judgment accuracy of students' monitoring skills: a conceptual and methodological framework and explorative study. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09349-8

`q2 · i?`

The review attributes this to Van de Pol et al. (2019, 2021a, b), in which secondary teachers judged students' performance from diagrams, student names, and task materials, deducing performance, student, and task cues. A study of Oudman et al. (2018) with primary teachers judging mathematics showed similar results.

> "These studies showed that teachers mostly use performance cues but also frequently student cues."
