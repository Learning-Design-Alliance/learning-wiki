## Evidence

### Chan Bibiana C 2013

Chan Bibiana C, Jayasinghe Upali W, Christl Bettina, Laws Rachel A, Orr Neil, Williams Anna, Partington Kate, Harris Mark F. (2013). The impact of a team-based intervention on the lifestyle risk factor management practices of community nurses: outcomes of the community nursing SNAP trial. BMC Health Services Research. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6963-13-54

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired t-tests on survey confidence scores (1–5 scale) at baseline, 6 and 12 months in the SNAP trial; Table 3 shows the motivational interviewing 6-month effect size of 0.74 (0.17–1.29) for the intervention group.

> "Nurses at the early intervention sites reported a significant increase in confidence related to performing SNAP interventions, especially in conducting motivational interviewing, which showed the greatest improvement."
