## Evidence

### Atlas Lauren Y 2022

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Multilevel model of medium-heat pain across the entire task including reversals (Table 4, n=40); Cue x Phase interactions rejected the null of no interaction (<1% in ROPE).

> "Thus predictive cues shape pain perception even as contingencies change, whether or not participants are instructed about contingencies."

### Atlas Lauren Y 2022 (2)

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Same task-wide multilevel model. Post hoc tests per group showed nearly 100% probability of positive Cue x Phase interactions in each group, but evidence was sufficient to reject the null only in the Instructed Group.

> "frequentist analysis approaches revealed significant Group x Cue x Phase interactions, driven by stronger reversals of Cue effects in the Instructed Group"
