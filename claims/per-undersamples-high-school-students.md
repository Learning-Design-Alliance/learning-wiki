### Stephen Kanim and Ximena C. Cid 2017

Stephen Kanim and Ximena C. Cid. (2017). The demographics of physics education research. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.02598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.02598)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of PER papers from AJP, PRPER, and TPT (1970–2015) counting high school research subjects. The authors note about half the high school subjects come from a single 1971 TPT survey, and that inclusion of JRST would raise the high school fraction only to about 10%.

> "only 76 of 417 total papers (24 PRPER, 21 AJP, and 31 TPT) reported data collected from a total of 21,539 students (674 PRPER, 1,590 AJP, 19,275 TPT) in high school classrooms. Whereas about 3 of every 4 American physics students are studying in high school classrooms, fewer than 1 in 10 of our research subjects are high school students."
