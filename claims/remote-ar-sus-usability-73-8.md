Descriptive secondary outcome from the same RCT: the 22 AR participants completed the 10-item SUS after simulation. Most agreed the system was easy to use and worth frequent use; only the technical-support item drew mixed opinions. No inferential test was applied.

> "We computed the average overall SUS score (0‐100), which was 73.8 (SD 13.4) among AR participants (n=22), consistent with good or acceptable usability."
