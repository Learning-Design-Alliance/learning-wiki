### Liu Boqiang 2026

Liu Boqiang, Gong Bin, Zhang Xiaochen, Lu Chen, Zheng Bin, Yang Jin. (2026). Self-directed teaching system driven by video analysis feedback improves surgical skill training for clinical interns: a randomized controlled trial. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09104-y

In the same RCT, the suture removal/dressing change module showed significantly higher post-training scores for traditional teaching (95.36 ± 3.46) than SDTS (88.42 ± 6.95), with P < 0.05 reported in the Results section.

> "Although both teaching methods significantly improved students’ test scores, the traditional teaching method resulted in better outcomes compared to the SDTS group (Fig. 4B)."
