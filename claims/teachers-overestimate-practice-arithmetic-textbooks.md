### Thorndike 1921

Thorndike, E. L. (1921). The psychology of drill in arithmetic: The amount of practice. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(4). https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/12/4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of practice-frequency estimates made by 50 experienced teachers (Table I) for arithmetical facts such as "13, 23, etc., + 2"; medians range from about 1,600 down to 200, showing that teachers "think they give much more practice than they do give."

> "It will be observed in Table I that even experienced teachers vary enormously in their estimates of the amount of practice given by an average text-book in arithmetic, and that most of them are in serious error by over-estimating the amount of practice."
