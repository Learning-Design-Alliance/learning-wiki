## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Teaching Assistants Using the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of normalized graduate-student PCK scores across the eight questions with major and nine with moderate introductory difficulties (17 questions total). The Results section reports the 48% versus 61% contrast; all four lowest-scoring questions (6, 8, 9, 17) contained a major difficulty.

> "Moreover, the average PCK score of graduate students on questions that had major difficulties is 48% compared to 61% on questions that had moderate difficulties."

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2014

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2014). Performance of Graduate Students at Identifying Introductory Physics Students’ Difficulties Related to Kinematics Graphs. 2014 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.039

`q2 · i?`

Results comparison using a researcher-agreed heuristic in which a "major" difficulty was an incorrect choice selected by more than 1/3 of introductory students and a "moderate" one by between 1/5 and 1/3. Table 2 prints major at 48% and moderate at 61%; only these percentages are printed, so no effect size.

> "Table 2 shows that GSs  performed worse at identifying introductory physics students’ major difficulties compared to moderate ones."
