## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2013

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2013). Exploring Pedagogical Content Knowledge of Teaching Assistants Using the Test of Understanding Graphs in Kinematics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1307.7179)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of normalized graduate-student PCK scores across the eight questions with major and nine with moderate introductory difficulties (17 questions total). The Results section reports the 48% versus 61% contrast; all four lowest-scoring questions (6, 8, 9, 17) contained a major difficulty.

> "Moreover, the average PCK score of graduate students on questions that had major difficulties is 48% compared to 61% on questions that had moderate difficulties."
