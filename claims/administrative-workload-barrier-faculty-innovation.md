## Evidence

### Li Xinyan 2025

Li Xinyan, Pei Xiaoge, Zhao Jing. (2025). Intrinsic motivation and self-efficacy as pathways to innovative teaching: a mixed-methods study of faculty in Chinese higher education. BMC Psychology, 13, 859. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03177-y

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of three semi-structured focus groups (18 faculty, ~90-minute sessions, Cohen's Kappa = 0.84). Participants reported that workload demands were "often overwhelming, leaving little time or energy for innovation in teaching"; one participant said paperwork left her "drained and with little time to develop new teaching methods."

> "The demands of teaching loads, committee work, and administrative duties were often overwhelming, leaving little time or energy for innovation in teaching."
