## Evidence

### Lauerer Elias 2021

Lauerer Elias, Tiedemann Elena, Polak Thomas, Simmenroth Anne. (2021). Can smoking cessation be taught online? A prospective study comparing e-learning and role-playing in medical education. International Journal of Medical Education, 12, 12-21. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5ff9.bccc

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exact time-on-task data were collected for the online course, which the authors highlight as a strength since many studies cannot state time spent online, confounding didactic method with educational time.

> "Students in group 1 spent 90 minutes of educational time on the seminar, while those in group 2 completed the web-based training in 74± 25 minutes, t(53.000) =4.795, p.001."
