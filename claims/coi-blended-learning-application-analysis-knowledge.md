# CoI-based blended learning students outperformed controls on application and analysis level knowledge items, though not on knowledge and understanding items

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Experimental-group students scored higher on application and analysis level items (d = 0.45) while knowledge and understanding items showed no significant difference between groups. [→ Liu Weichu 2021](#liu-weichu-2021)

## Evidence

### Liu Weichu 2021

Liu Weichu, Wang Jun, Zhang Han, Yu Changfeng, Liu Shuai, Zhang Cen, Yu Jingya, Liu Qiao, Yang Bing. (2021). Determining the effects of blended learning using the community of inquiry on nursing students' learning gains in sudden patient deterioration module. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.914

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Independent-samples t tests on knowledge-test items grouped by modified Bloom's cognitive levels in the two-group quasi-experimental trial. The application and analysis contrast shows Cohen d = 0.45 favouring the blended learning group; the knowledge and understanding contrast was not significant.

> "the score of application and analysis level in the CoI‐based blended learning group was higher (t = 3.42, p = .001, Cohen d = 0.45), while no significant difference was shown in the level of knowledge and understanding between the two groups."
