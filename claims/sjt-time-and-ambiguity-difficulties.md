### Burgess Annette 2014

Burgess Annette, Roberts Chris, Clark Tyler, Mossman Karyn. (2014). The social validity of a national assessment centre for selection into general practice training. BMC Medical Education 14:261. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0261-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic prevalence from the same post-NAC survey of 886 respondents. Difficulty concerned the "ambiguous nature" of SJT questions and completing within the timeframe, "particularly if English was not their first language".

> "Candidates [380/886 (43%) of survey respondents] also commented on their difficulty in responding to the SJT questions because of their ambiguous nature, and in completing the SJT in the given timeframe, particularly if English was not their first language."

### Burgess Annette 2014 (2)

Burgess Annette, Roberts Chris, Clark Tyler, Mossman Karyn. (2014). The social validity of a national assessment centre for selection into general practice training. BMC Medical Education 14:261. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0261-6

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Survey results comparing candidate perceptions of the two assessment formats: 156/886 (18%) found SJT questions "vague" and "confusing", suggesting questions were not representative of their broader experiences.

> "a significant minority of candidates mentioned that the SJT questions were more difficult to respond to than the MMI questions, with 156/886 (18%) commenting that they found the SJT questions to be "vague" and "confusing""
