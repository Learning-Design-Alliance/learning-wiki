`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel analyses of survey and test data from 193,073 15-year-old students in 41 countries compared SOBAS across cultures differing in egalitarianism, finding "students in more egalitarian cultures often had higher SOBAS". No effect size is printed in the available text.
