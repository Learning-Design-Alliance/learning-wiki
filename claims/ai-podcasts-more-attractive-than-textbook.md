## Evidence

### Tiffany D. Do 2025

Tiffany D. Do, Usama Bin Shafqat, Elsie Ling, and Nikhil Sarda. (2025). PAIGE: Examining Learning Outcomes and Experiences with Personalized AI-Generated Educational Podcasts. PREPRINT. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1145/3706598.3713460](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713460)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the 180-student study's UEQ results, there was no significant interaction with subject, and the ANOVA found "a significant main effect of content style" on Attractiveness, F(2, 171) = 11.06, p < 0.01, with both podcast conditions rated higher than the Textbook. No effect size is printed.

> "We found a significant main effect of content style,𝐹(2, 171) = 11.06,𝑝 < 0.01. A post-hoc test revealed that both the Personalized condition (M=5.25, SD=1.06) and the Generalized condition (M=5.25, SD=0.88) had significantly higher ratings than the Textbook condition (M=4.52, SD=1.03), supporting H1."
