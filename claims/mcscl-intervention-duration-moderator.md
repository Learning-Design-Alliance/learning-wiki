`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects moderator analysis of intervention duration. Table 4 reports g = 0.867 for >1 week and ≤4 weeks (k = 16), g = 0.339 for >4 weeks and ≤6 months (k = 15), g = 0.239 for ≤1 week (k = 8), and g = 0.330 for >6 months (k = 5); QB = 13.519, p < .01.

> "The ES was largest when the mCSCL studies lasted for >1 week and ≤4 weeks. The ESs for studies lasting for ≤1 week and >6 months were not significant. Q reached statistical significance, indicating that the ESs differed significantly among the various categories."
