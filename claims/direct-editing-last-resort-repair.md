Sequential analysis of Thread 4 (question ID 77840468): the OP misread critique of inaccurate vocabulary as criticism of using a screenshot; the commenter marked the dispreferred direct edit with "Sigh", changed 'escape' to 'newline', and the OP then attributed the misunderstanding to limited English proficiency. The thread ended with two answers, including an accepted one.
