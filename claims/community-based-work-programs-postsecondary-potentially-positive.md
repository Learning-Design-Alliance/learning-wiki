`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

The review's summary rates community-based work programs as having "potentially positive effects on postsecondary education" under What Works Clearinghouse standards; no effect sizes are printed on the page.

> "Community-based work programs had mixed effects on employment and potentially positive effects on postsecondary education."

`q2 · i?` · `review · r?`

WWC review finding from one quasi-experimental study (215 students) examining postsecondary education outcomes alongside employment. The review characterizes the rating as "potentially positive effects on postsecondary education outcomes" with a small evidence base.

> "These programs were found to have potentially positive effects on postsecondary education outcomes, although the extent of evidence was small."
