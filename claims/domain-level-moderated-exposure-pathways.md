### Osondu Oluchi Miracle 2026

Osondu Oluchi Miracle, Ugwu Lawrence Ejike, Ayogu Chijioke Kenneth, Morah Nwando Maryann, Eyisi Doris Chinelo, Ike Obinna Osita, Johnson Jennifer E., Chukwuorji JohnBosco Chika. (2026). Lifetime Exposure to Suicide and Current Use of Suicide Prevention Competencies Among Catholic Clergy in Nigeria: A Multidimensional Structural Analysis. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-026-02754-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multigroup path analysis of the three SBX exposure dimensions onto the four MSPCC competency domains in the untrained group (n = 250), controlling for covariates. Indirect exposure showed the printed standardized paths "β = 0.22, p = 0.002" and "β = 0.21, p = 0.005".

> "among those who have never received formal suicide prevention training, indirect exposure to suicide attempt/death significantly predicted pastoral care with a suicidal person (β = 0.22, p = 0.002) and nonjudgmental attitude toward people affected by suicide (β = 0.21, p = 0.005)"

### Osondu Oluchi Miracle 2026 (2)

Osondu Oluchi Miracle, Ugwu Lawrence Ejike, Ayogu Chijioke Kenneth, Morah Nwando Maryann, Eyisi Doris Chinelo, Ike Obinna Osita, Johnson Jennifer E., Chukwuorji JohnBosco Chika. (2026). Lifetime Exposure to Suicide and Current Use of Suicide Prevention Competencies Among Catholic Clergy in Nigeria: A Multidimensional Structural Analysis. Journal of Religion and Health. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10943-026-02754-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Same multigroup path analysis, trained group (n = 86). Direct exposure was positively linked to pastoral care ("β = 0.28, p = 0.039") and negatively to nonjudgmental attitude (β = −0.33, p = 0.016); indirect exposure paths were all non-significant (all p > 0.50).

> "In the trained group, higher direct exposure to suicide attempt/death was associated with higher pastoral care with a suicidal person (β = 0.28, p = 0.039) and lower nonjudgmental attitude toward people affected by suicide (β =  − 0.33, p = 0.016)"
