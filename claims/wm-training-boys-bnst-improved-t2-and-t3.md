## Subclaims
`q2 i?` When analysing boys only (n = 46), the treatment effect on BNST was significant at both T2 and T3. [→ Dahlin 2013](#dahlin-2013)

## Evidence

### Dahlin 2013

Karin I. E. Dahlin. (2013). Working Memory Training and the Effect on Mathematical Achievement in Children with Attention Deficits and Special Needs. Journal of Education and Learning, 2(1). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v2n1p118

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated measures model repeated for boys only after girls performed differently; Table 2 reports Cohen's d for BNST of 0.74 (T2–T1) and 0.90 (T3–T1) for boys in the treatment group compared with boys in the control group. The article calls these effects large.

> "BNST was significant at T2 (treatment effect = 1.891, SE = 0.868, F(1, 17.7) = 4.74, p < .05) as well as at T3 (treatment effect = 2.391, SE = 0.999, F(1, 22.3) = 5.73, p < .05)."
