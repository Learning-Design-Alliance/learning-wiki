## Evidence

### Manal Mohamed Khodary 2017

Manal Mohamed Khodary. (2017). Using the Vocabulary Self-Collection Strategy Plus to Develop University EFL Students' Vocabulary Learning. English Language Teaching, 10(8). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v10n8p135

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment with two intact classes of first-level female EFL students (n = 40 each) taught the same texts for 11 weeks; the VSSPlus group's post VAT mean was 37.92 versus 23.53 for the VSS group, "statistically significant (t = 25.62, p < 0.05)" with an effect size of 0.894, considered large.

> "the mean score of the experimental group on the post VAT was 37.92 with a standard deviation of 2.38, whereas the mean score of the control group on the post VAT was 23.53 with a standard deviation of 2.64"
