## Evidence

### Buchanan H 2015

Buchanan H, Jelsma J, Siegfried N. (2015). Measuring evidence-based practice knowledge and skills in occupational therapy—a brief instrument. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0475-2

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Responsiveness analysis (Study 2) using data from the intervention group of a pragmatic randomised controlled trial (n = 28 receiving the interactive intervention), with intention-to-treat analysis and non-parametric statistics because data were not normally distributed. The article reports the significant Wilcoxon Signed Rank result and the moderate effect size r = 0.55.

> "Median total SAFT scores increased significantly from baseline to 12-weeks (Z = −4.078, p < 0.001) with a moderate effect size (r = 0.55)."
