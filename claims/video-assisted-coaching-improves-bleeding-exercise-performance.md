## Evidence

### Chatziisaak D 2024

Chatziisaak D, Sparn M, Krstic D, Bauci G, Warschkow R, Brunner W, Schmied B, Hahnloser D, Bischofberger S. (2024). Be prepared! Impact of structured video-assisted coaching on performance in a simulated bleeding exercise during laparoscopic surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11173-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mixed-effects regression analysis of 54 blinded GOALS-rated video recordings from 27 trainees at the 40th Annual Davos Course, with random intercepts for reviewers and probands; coaching effect exceeded repetition effect on the sum score (0.36 vs 0.05), and "simple repetition (0.05 with 95%CI: −0.43 to 0.53, P = 0.826)" was not significant. No control group was included.

> "Structured video-assisted coaching improved the GOALS sum score by 0.36 (95%CI: 0.21–0.50, P < 0.001) in contrast to simple repetition (0.05 with 95%CI: −0.43 to 0.53, P = 0.826)."
