# The worked example effect failed to replicate for geometry and kinematics problems, and was reinstated when worked examples were restructured to reduce split attention

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · theoretical `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Studying worked examples produced no superiority over problem solving for geometry and kinematics problems, unlike the robust algebra findings. [→ John Sweller 2023](#john-sweller-2023)
`q2 i?` Physically integrating diagram and statements in worked examples reduced split attention and reinstated the worked example effect. [→ John Sweller 2023 (2)](#john-sweller-2023-2)

## Evidence

### John Sweller 2023

John Sweller. (2023). The Development of Cognitive Load Theory: Replication Crises and Incorporation of Other Theories Can Lead to Theory Expansion. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09817-2

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Narrative report by the author of his own research group's experiments following Sweller and Cooper (1985) and Cooper and Sweller (1987) algebra studies. The article reports these as replication failures that persisted for several years; no statistics are printed in the quoted passage.

> "We tried geometry problems and obtained no evidence of superiority when students studied worked examples instead of solving problems. We tried kinematics problems in physics classes and again found no worked example effect."

### John Sweller 2023 (2)

John Sweller. (2023). The Development of Cognitive Load Theory: Replication Crises and Incorporation of Other Theories Can Lead to Theory Expansion. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09817-2

`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

The article reports that data indicated the worked example effect could be obtained when geometry and kinematics worked examples were restructured to physically integrate multiple sources of information, citing Tarmizi and Sweller (1988) and Ward and Sweller (1990) for examples.

> "Physically integrating worked examples reduced split attention and reinstated the worked example effect by reducing working memory load, thus expanding cognitive load theory."
