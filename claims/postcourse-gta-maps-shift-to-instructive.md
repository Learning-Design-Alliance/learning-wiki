# After a quarter-long pedagogy course, inexperienced GTAs' concept maps shifted significantly toward instructive beliefs, which exceeded traditional and transitional ideas

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Postcourse maps contained 76.97% instructive terms, significantly greater than traditional (p < 0.001) and transitional (p < 0.001) ideas, with a significant time point × TBI category interaction. [→ Star W. Lee 2019](#star-w-lee-2019)

## Evidence

### Star W. Lee 2019

Star W. Lee. (2019). The Impact of a Pedagogy Course on the Teaching Beliefs of Inexperienced Graduate Teaching Assistants. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.18-07-0137

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Repeated-measures two-way ANOVA comparing eight precourse and eight postcourse group concept maps (n = 8 groups). The omnibus interaction was significant: "F(2,14) = 11.41, p < 0.01", with Tukey post hoc tests showing more instructive ideas postcourse (p < 0.05).

> "A two-way ANOVA with repeated measures revealed significant effects for TBI category (F(2,14) = 12.02, p < 0.001). There was a significant interaction between the time point and TBI category (F(2,14) = 11.41, p < 0.01)."
