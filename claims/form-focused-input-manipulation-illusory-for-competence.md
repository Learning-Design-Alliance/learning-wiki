# Successes of form-focused manipulation of input in adult L2 are illusory with respect to linguistic competence, affecting only performance

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · review `r1` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Form-focused manipulation of input in adult second language acquisition produces temporary, superficial performance changes rather than reorganization of the learner's grammar. [→ Young-Scholten 1999](#young-scholten-1999)

## Evidence

### Young-Scholten 1999

Young-Scholten, M. (1999). Focus on Form and Linguistic Competence: Why Krashen Is Still Right about Acquisition. Paper presented at AAAL, Stamford. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED432919

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

The author's review of studies on input manipulation in adult second language acquisition concludes that "successes in the form-focused manipulation of input are illusory in terms of affecting linguistic competence", listing temporary improvement, mastery of simple items, and superficial changes with no grammar reorganization.

> "It seems that successes in the form-focused manipulation of input are illusory in terms of affecting linguistic competence;we find that: (4)performance is affected in some manner"
