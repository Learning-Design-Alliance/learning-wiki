## Evidence

### Nielsen Martine S. 2022

Nielsen Martine S., Clausen Jesper H., Hoffmann-Petersen Joachim, Konge Lars, Nielsen Anders B. (2022). Can virtual-reality simulation ensure transthoracic echocardiography skills before trainees examine patients? International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.6321.8e5d

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional comparison of 36 physicians (16 novices, 10 intermediates, 10 experienced) completing the VR TTE test at Odense University Hospital; group mean scores compared with one-way ANOVA and Bonferroni correction, with "a difference between novice and experienced physicians on all parameters".

> "The Bonferroni post hoc test proved a difference between novice and experienced physicians on all parameters (Table 3)."

### Nielsen Martine S. 2022 (2)

Nielsen Martine S., Clausen Jesper H., Hoffmann-Petersen Joachim, Konge Lars, Nielsen Anders B. (2022). Can virtual-reality simulation ensure transthoracic echocardiography skills before trainees examine patients? International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.6321.8e5d

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same Bonferroni analysis of the 36-participant test data: the novice-intermediate contrast was significant on most parameters but not on the case 5 clinical conclusion (p=.903 in Table 3) or the anatomy quiz (p=.175).

> "A significant difference between the novice and intermediate groups was observed on the parameters, except for the case 5 conclusion and the test in anatomical structures (Table 3)."
