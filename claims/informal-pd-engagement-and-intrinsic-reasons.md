Survey of the nationally representative teacher sample on informal, non-required professional learning. The report states "72 percent of teachers say they do so at least once a year" and that overall participants are more satisfied with informal activities; the reasons table shows "I enjoy learning" at 59 percent for informal versus 45 percent for formal.
