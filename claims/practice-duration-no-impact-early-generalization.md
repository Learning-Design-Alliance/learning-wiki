## Subclaims

## Evidence

### B. P. Johnson 2023

B. P. Johnson, I. Iturrate, R. Y. Fakhreddine, M. Bönstrup, E. R. Buch, E. M. Robertson and L. G. Cohen. (2023). Generalization of procedural motor sequence learning after a single practice trial. npj Science of Learning 8:45. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00194-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Across Experiment 1's randomized practice-duration groups (1, 2, 5, or 12 trials of skill A, n = 551), the article reports "no impact on parsing rule structure generalization"; performance at the onset of skill B was comparable across groups.

> "Manipulating the initial practice exposure to skill A (1 to 12 trials) and inter-practice rest interval (0 –30 s) between skills A and B had no impact on parsing rule structure generalization."
