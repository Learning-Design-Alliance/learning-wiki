Meta-analysis of 9 controlled studies (n = 726 clinical medicine students) pooling clinical skills assessments. The article reports no statistically significant difference with SMD = 0.27 and I² = 88%.

> "the updated pooled analysis showed no statistically significant difference between GenAI and traditional teaching methods (SMD = 0.27, 95% CI: -0.13 to 0.66; P = 0.18; I² = 88%; 9 studies, n = 726, random-effects model)"
