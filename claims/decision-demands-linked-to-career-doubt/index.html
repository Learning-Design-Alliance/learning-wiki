q2 · i? · associational · r2

Person-level analysis linking coded conversation behavior to the randomized trial's post-test outcomes; the survey posed each decision once while the agent asked again when participants hesitated. The article reports this as an observational association, not a tested causal effect, and no effect size is printed in the available text.

"The one behavior that tracked a worse outcome was the agent's habit of pressing participants to decide: the more demands to decide a participant received, the more doubt they reported at the end of the study."
