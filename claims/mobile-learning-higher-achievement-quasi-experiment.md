## Evidence

### Abdellah Ibrahim Mohammed Elfeky & Thouqan Saleem Yakoub Masadeh 2016

Abdellah Ibrahim Mohammed Elfeky & Thouqan Saleem Yakoub Masadeh. (2016). The Effect of Mobile Learning on Students' Achievement and Conversational Skills. International Journal of Higher Education, Vol. 5, No. 3. https://doi.org/10.5430/ijhe.v5n3p20

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 50 students in two equal groups (25 each) studying the Strategies of Teaching and Learning course for fourteen weeks; experimental group received content via Blackboard on mobile devices, control via hardcopy. The article reports a statistically significant difference (α=0.05) in favor of the mobile-learning group, with means of "(M= 27.20)" versus "(M=34.32)".

> "T. value for the difference between participants' modified gain ratio in the control group (M= 27.20) and the experimental one (M=34.32) regarding the academic achievement post-test was (5.533)."
