Evidence¶

Bernstein Brian O. 2021¶

Bernstein Brian O., Lubinski David, Benbow Camilla P. (2021). Academic Acceleration in Gifted Youth and Fruitless Concerns Regarding Psychological Well-Being: A 35-Year Longitudinal Study. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000500

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Norm-referenced appraisal of Study 1 box-and-whisker plots (Appendix A); the article reports the parallel result for Study 2 (Appendix B). Participants were "above average on Psychological Flourishing, Positive Affect, and Life Satisfaction"; no effect sizes are printed, so impact is unquantified.

"Benchmarked against normative standards (Diener et al., 1985; Diener et al., 2010; Judge et al., 2003; Pavot & Diener, 1993), the typical participant in Study 1 scored about the average on the Core Self-Evaluations scale, and above average on Psychological Flourishing, Positive Affect, and Life Satisfaction."
