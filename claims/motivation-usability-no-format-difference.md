## Evidence

### Daniel López-Fernández 2023

Daniel López-Fernández, Aldo Gordillo, Jennifer Pérez, Edmundo Tovar. (2023). Learning and Motivational Impact of Game-Based Learning: Comparing Face-To-Face and Online Formats on Computer Science Education. IEEE Transactions on Education. https://doi.org/10.1109/TE.2023.3241099

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null results from the same questionnaire comparison of the face-to-face (N=217) and online (N=104) groups. Table IV reports item 5 (engaging and motivating): 4.37 vs 4.23, p = 0.14, d = 0.16; item 3: p = 0.28, d = 0.11. No effect size reached the small threshold for these items.

> "no statistically significant differences were found in the items related to ease of use (item 3), required help (item 4), motivation (item 5) and integration with the learning platform (item 7)."
