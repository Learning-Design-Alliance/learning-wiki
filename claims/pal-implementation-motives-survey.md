Blohm M., Lauter J., Branchereau S., Krautter M., Köhl-Hackert N., Jünger J., Herzog W., Nikendei C. (2015). "Peer-Assisted Learning"(PAL) in the Skills-Lab – an inventory at the medical faculties of the Federal Republic of Germany. GMS Zeitschrift für Medizinische Ausbildung. [doi:10.3205/zma000952](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma000952)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Frequency analysis (Mayring) of free-text answers from 26 of 33 faculties with PAL programs. The discussion reports these were "frequently cited as key motivation"; the results section lists five entries each for effectiveness, teacher relief, development of practical teaching and exam preparation.

> "Proven effectiveness of PAL, the desire to increase practical teaching as well as targeted exam preparation were frequently cited as key motivation for the establishment of a PAL program in the Skills-Labs by the representatives."
