# Sensitivity and specificity of trained professionals' readings are high from the first reading at both the 5 mm and 10 mm cutoffs

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q3`
> **Currency** · newest study 2025 · not yet checked against its sources · not reviewed by a person

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` At the 5 mm cutoff, sensitivity and specificity were 96.5% and 93.0% (93.3% and 93.8% at the first reading); at the 10 mm cutoff they were 88.2% and 95.0% (93.0% and 96.3% at the first reading), with no substantive effect over successive readings. [→ Cordeiro DC 2025](#cordeiro-dc-2025)

## Evidence

### Cordeiro DC 2025

Cordeiro DC, Aguiar EB, Luiz RR, Sacramento DS, Paolino PLW, Gonçalves MJF, Trajman A. (2025). Effect of successive tuberculin skin test readings on healthcare professionals’ proficiency: an operational observational study in five high-tuberculosis-burden Brazilian municipalities, 2023-2024. Epidemiologia e Serviços de Saúde. https://doi.org/10.1590/S2237-96222025v34e20240795.en

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Results analysis (Figura 3) using lead-trainer readings as gold standard, paired readings ranging from 168 professionals at the first to 50 at the 50th reading. Variations across readings "não ultrapassam 5,0%".

> "Considerando o ponto de corte de 5 mm, a sensibilidade e a especificidade foram 96,5% e 93,0%, sendo de 93,3% e 93,8% na primeira leitura. Os valores permaneceram elevados nas leituras subsequentes, com variações muito pequenas ao longo das leituras, que não ultrapassam 5,0%"
