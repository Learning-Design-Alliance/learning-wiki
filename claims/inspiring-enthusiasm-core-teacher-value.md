### Hecht 2023

Hecht, C. A., Murphy, M. C., Dweck, C. S., Bryan, C. J., Trzesniewski, K. H., Medrano, F. N., Giani, M., Mhatre, P., & Yeager, D. S. (2023). Shifting the mindset culture to address global educational disparities. npj Science of Learning 8:29. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00181-y

`q3 · i?` · `review · r2`

A confirmatory survey with a nationally representative sample of 965 high school math instructors asked teachers to rank seven characteristics; "81% of teachers in the sample ranked this as the most important characteristic." It followed focus groups identifying the shared value.

> "The results unambiguously confirmed that inspiring students' enthusiasm for learning was a core value among teachers: 81% of teachers in the sample ranked this as the most important characteristic and 97% of teachers ranked it within the top three."
