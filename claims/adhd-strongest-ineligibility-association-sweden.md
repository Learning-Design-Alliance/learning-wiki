## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Among Swedish ninth-grade students, ADHD had the highest odds of ineligibility for upper secondary education of the six disorders studied, for both males (OR = 3.58) and females (OR = 4.31). [→ Bortes Cristian 2022](#bortes-cristian-2022)

## Evidence

### Bortes Cristian 2022

Bortes Cristian, Nilsson Karina, Strandh Mattias. (2022). Associations between children’s diagnosed mental disorders and educational achievements in Sweden. Scandinavian Journal of Public Health, 50(8), 1140–1147. https://doi.org/10.1177/14034948221089056

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression on Swedish administrative register data for 266,664 individuals born 2000–2002, fully adjusted for all diagnoses and socioeconomic position. The printed effect sizes are OR = 3.58 (95% CI, 3.42 to 3.74) for males and OR = 4.31 (95% CI, 4.07 to 4.57) for females with ADHD versus unexposed peers.

> "Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder was the most strongly associated disorder in terms of non-successful completion of compulsory education, among both male and female students (odds ratio (OR): 3.58 (95% confidence interval (CI), 3.42 to 3.74) and 4.31 (95% CI, 4.07 to 4.57), respectively)."
