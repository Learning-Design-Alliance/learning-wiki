### Harry A. Greene 1918

Harry A. Greene. (1918). A Standardization of Certain Opposites Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070589

A reliability comparison analysis in which the present evaluation based on "990 individuals" was correlated with the earlier King and Gold evaluation. The article reports the printed effect size r = .895: "an "r" of .895 was found", which the writer treats as showing the larger-sample standardization is more reliable.

> "A correlation was made between the list of eighty words as evaluated by King and Gold and by the present study. Using the Pearson method adapted to rank differences an "r" of .895 was found."
