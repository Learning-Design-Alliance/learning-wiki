`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with 10 professors of surgery, analysed in NVivo 11, yielded themes on the current state of training. Participants reported that

> "Many of the study participants agreed that the current landscape of basic surgical education is deficient at multiple levels. A recurring theme echoed in all but one interview included the apparent discrepancy between students’ knowledge base and competence in basic surgical skills (BSS)."
