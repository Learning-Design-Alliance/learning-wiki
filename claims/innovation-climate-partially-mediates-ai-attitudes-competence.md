### Karakaya Suzan Ozge 2026

Karakaya Suzan Ozge, Gül Uğur, Koyuncu Oğuz, Akdeniz Canberk. (2026). The Mediating and Moderating Roles of Innovation Climate in the Relationship Between Nurses’ Attitudes Toward Artificial Intelligence, Creative Self‐Efficacy, and Clinical Competence. Journal of Nursing Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/jonm/1528189

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional survey of 233 nurses in Türkiye; PROCESS Model 4 mediation with 5000 bootstrap samples, adjusted for age, gender, AI training/use, education, experience, and institution type. The unadjusted indirect effect was B = 0.1156, "attenuated but remained significant" after adjustment.

> "Attitudes toward AI were indirectly associated with perceived clinical competence through perceived climate for innovation; the adjusted indirect association remained significant (B = 0.0700, SE = 0.0333, 95% CI [0.0173, 0.1453]), consistent with partial statistical mediation."
