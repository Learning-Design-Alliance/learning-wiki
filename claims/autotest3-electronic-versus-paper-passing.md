## Evidence

### Magdin and Turcáni 2015

Martin MAGDIN, Milan TURČÁNI. (2015). A Few Observations and Remarks on Time Effectiveness of Interactive Electronic Testing. Informatics in Education. http://dx.doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2015.06

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of one of the nine taken tests (test no. 3), an interactive task in which students assembled a nonlinear transistor level from predefined components; the paper group solved identical content on paper. The electronic group's mean on T7 was 9.821429 versus 1.428571 on paper.

> "In autotest no. 3, in which the priority was to form a correct connection of nonlinear transistor level, 6 of the total 14 students solving the test in electronic form gained point evaluation 80% (i.e. 8 out of 10 points). In classic (paper) form of testing, only one of the students gained a satisfactory point evaluation (8.21 points = 82%)."
