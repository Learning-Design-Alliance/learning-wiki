Fugelli Caroline Guldberg; Hansen Britt Sætre; Ersdal Hege; Kurz Martin. (2025). Unveiling team needs: a qualitative study of simulation training for endovascular cerebral thrombectomy. BMJ Open Quality. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjoq-2024-002981

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group data: anaesthesia professionals were most often reported missing from sessions; participants suggested EVT training be conducted independently of the overall stroke simulation chain.

> "The hospital managers did not always assign sufficient time from the participant’s work schedule to the simulation process, and participants who felt that they were needed for clinical work could not engage fully in the simulation training."
