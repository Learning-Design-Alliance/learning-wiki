## Evidence

### Herchenröther Julia 2021

Herchenröther Julia, Tiedemann Elena, Vogel Heiner, Simmenroth Anne. (2021). Which teaching method is more effective in a communication course – role-playing versus simulated patients, taught by tutors or faculty staff? A randomized trial. GMS Journal for Medical Education. [doi:10.3205/zma001450](https://doi.org/10.3205/zma001450)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Randomized trial, Results section: the tutor-led group practising mainly role-play with fellow students scored higher on the history-taking OSCE than the tutor-led SP group; the article reports "higher OSCE scores", d=0.42. The authors note this aligns with Bosse et al.

> "The RP-Tutor group (Mdn=85.8%) achieved higher OSCE scores than the SP-Tutor group (Mdn=78.2%), U=429.5, z=-1.74, p=.041, d= 0.42"
