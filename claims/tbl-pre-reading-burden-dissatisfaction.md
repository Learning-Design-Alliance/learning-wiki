Jabeen Kauser, Rehman Javeria, Fatima Syeda Sadia, Ahmed Imran, Ghias Kulsoom. (2026). Does medical students’ perception of team-based learning have an impact on achieving planned learning outcomes?. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09185-9

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of focus group discussions with 15 students purposively sampled across four rating-by-performance strata. Students described pre-reading material as "very vague and lengthy" and reported struggling with retention; they also affirmed quizzes and application exercises clarified difficult concepts.

> "FGD with fifteen students predominantly identified that extensive pre-reading material led to learner dissatisfaction and disengagement."
