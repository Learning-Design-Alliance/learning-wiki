### Reading Go! (formerly Reading Rescue) 2021

Reading Go! (formerly Reading Rescue). (2021). Evidence for ESSA program description (no publication date printed on page). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/reading-rescue/

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r?`

One study of language-minority, low-SES first graders in New York City compared students who received Reading Go! with students who did not. The page reports "significantly positive" effects on Word Attack ("effect size = +0.52"), Word Reading (+0.45), and Word Decoding (+0.38).

> "In comparison to students who did not receive Reading Go!, outcomes were significantly positive on the Woodcock Reading Mastery Word Attack (effect size = +0.52) and Word Reading (effect size = +0.45), as well as on the Gates MacGinitie Word Decoding (effect size = +0.38)."
