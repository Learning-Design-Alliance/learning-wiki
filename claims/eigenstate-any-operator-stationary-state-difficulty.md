Item-by-item analysis of polling data from the 2020 virtual CQS administration (N = 29). The article notes the incorrect percentage rose to 35% in the second polling round and attributes the confusion possibly to instructor comments that no spin component is inherently special.

> "Though 23% of the students had answered CQS 1.1 noting that an eigenstate of any operator is a stationary state, a higher percentage of students (32%) answered in CQS 1.2 that an eigenstate of 𝑆̂𝑥 is a stationary state for a system with Hamiltonian 𝐶𝑆̂𝑧."
