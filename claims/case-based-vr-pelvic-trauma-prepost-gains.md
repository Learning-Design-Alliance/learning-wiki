Mogali Sreenivasulu Reddy, Yan Qi, Jiang Nan, Teng Peng, Xu Youran, Qian Kaoliang, Lu Ming, Hu Jun. (2025). Case-Based Virtual Reality Simulation for Severe Pelvic Trauma Clinical Skill Training in Medical Students: Design and Pilot Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/59850

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Results section of a 1-group pretest-posttest pilot at Nanjing Medical University with 40 students (20 undergraduates, 20 graduates). The article reports significant pre-post improvements across all five domains ("making preliminary diagnoses, the sequence of disease treatment, emergency management of hemorrhagic shock, and performing external fixation of pelvic fractures"); paired t tests, mean differences, and Cohen d values are printed in Table 4.

> "A comparison of theoretical examination scores before and after participants used the digital simulation software for severe pelvic trauma showed significant improvements in their overall scores for diagnosing and treating the condition, making preliminary diagnoses, the sequence of disease treatment, emergency management of hemorrhagic shock, and performing external fixation of pelvic fractures"
