# Spaced practice produces a robust small-to-medium retention advantage over massed practice for mathematics learning overall (g = 0.26).

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · quant-synthesis `r3` · `q3` · `i1` small

## Subclaims
`q3 i1` Across 25 studies with 49 effect sizes, spaced versus massed mathematics practice yields a weighted mean effect of g = 0.259 with a confidence interval excluding zero. [→ Murray 2023](#murray-2023)

## Evidence

### Murray 2023

Murray, E., Horner, A. J., & Göbel, S. M. (2023). A Meta-analytic Review of the Effectiveness of Spacing and Retrieval Practice for Mathematics Learning. https://osf.io/

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Pre-registered meta-analysis using robust variance estimation with correlated hierarchical effects over 25 spacing studies. The weighted mean effect was "g = 0.259 (se = 0.043)" with a 95% CI of [0.168, 0.351], indicating a robust small-to-medium effect.

> "The systematic review revealed 25 studies with 49 effect sizes. We found a weighted mean effect of g = 0.259 (se = 0.043) (95% confidence interval [0.168, 0.351])."
