### Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed 2025

Alismail Abdulaziz Mohammed, Almulla Mazen Omar. (2025). Exploring the interplay between achievement motivation, students’ satisfaction with their major, and successful intelligence in supporting the sustainability of university education in SAUDI ARABIA. `q2 · i2` · `associational · r2`

SEM estimate from the 529-student cross-sectional survey. The direct path from successful intelligence to sustainable education was significant, "β = 0.31, p <.001", described as a parallel stream of influence alongside motivation and satisfaction.

> "Lastly, successful intelligence predicted sustainable education (β = 0.31, p <.001). This underscores that beyond motivation and satisfaction, cognitive–practical skill sets have their own role in ensuring students thrive in a sustainable academic environment."
