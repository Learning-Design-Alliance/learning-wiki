## Evidence

### Malhotra Sanchi 2021

Malhotra Sanchi, Masood Imran, Giglio Noberto, Pruetz Jay D., Pannaraj Pia S. (2021). Current knowledge of Chagas-related heart disease among pediatric cardiologists in the United States. BMC Cardiovascular Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12872-021-01924-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey items on local vector presence and guideline familiarity, analyzed among 119 U.S. respondents. A separate printed result states "Only 7% (95% CI 3–13.2) of respondents reported being familiar with the 2018 American Heart Association (AHA) published scientific statement"; familiarity did not differ by years in practice (p = 0.51).

> "While the vector was found in 82% of our respondents’ states, only 20% (95% CI 13.5–27.8) reported that the vector was present"
