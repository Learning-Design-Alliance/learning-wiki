## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Employed OOHEP report having established intraosseous access since 2005 about four times as often as inactive OOHEP, an effect retained in multivariate logistic regression. [→ Fischer H 2014](#fischer-h-2014)

## Evidence

### Fischer H 2014

Fischer H, Bachmann K, Strunk G, Neuhold S, Zapletal B, Maurer C, Fast A, Stumpf D, Greif R. (2014). Translation of ERC resuscitation guidelines into clinical practice by emergency physicians. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/1757-7241-22-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate logistic regression of survey data from mandatory Austrian OOHEP refresher courses (N = 836 analysed) shows working status predicts intraosseous access, "OR = 4.013, p < 0.01", with age, gender, experience, ERC-ALS course attendance and recent resuscitation as control variables.

> "Working OOHEP are significantly more likely to have initiated intraosseous access since 2005 (OR = 4.013, p < 0.01)."
