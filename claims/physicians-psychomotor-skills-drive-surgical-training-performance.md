## Evidence

### Ruchay Zino 2024

Ruchay Zino, Julian Pape, Julia-Sophie Cordt, Carolina Kerres, Sebastian Siehl, Karina Jansone, Johannes Ackermann, Veronika Guenther, Liselotte Mettler, Leila Allahqoli, Nicolai Maass, Frauke Nees, Ibrahim Alkatout. (2024). Does an aptitude for surgery exist and can we predict it? An experimental study. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000001577

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM analysis of the physician cohort (n=86) in the same randomized crossover trial; the article reports "psychomotor skills contributing the most to physicians' surgical training performance" with adjusted R2=0.238 and fit indices NFI 0.404, CFI 0.510.

> "The latent factors of motivation (explained variance, 2.3%; R2=0.052; R2 corrected=0.023), personality (explained variance, 10.5%; R2=0.181; R2 corrected=0.105), and psychomotor skills (explained variance, 27.4%; R2=0.339; R2 corrected=0.274) are weighted differently, with psychomotor skills contributing the most to physicians' surgical training performance."
