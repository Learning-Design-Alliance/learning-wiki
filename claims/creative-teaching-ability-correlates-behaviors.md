## Evidence

### Liu Hsing-Yuan 2019

Liu Hsing-Yuan, Wang I-Teng. (2019). Creative teaching behaviors of health care school teachers in Taiwan: mediating and moderating effects. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1641-8

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlations in the 53-teacher survey showed the CTETITS total score correlated with all four CTBS subscales, from r = .32 (EO) to r = 0.78 (CM), all statistically significant.

> "Teachers' creative teaching ability (CTA) was correlated with creative teaching behavior; the total score for the CTA and all four subscales of the CTBS was moderate (EO, r = .32; p < .05) to high (Al, r = .58; CT, r = .60; CM, r = 0.78; p < .01)."
