# ASL proficiency correlates positively with English reading comprehension among deaf adults

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` ASL-SRT scores correlated significantly and positively with Passage Comprehension raw scores (r = 0.48). [→ Freel 2011](#freel-2011)

## Evidence

### Freel 2011

Freel, B. L., Clark, M. D., Anderson, M. L., Gilbert, G. L., Musyoka, M. M., & Hauser, P. C. (2011). Deaf Individuals’ Bilingual Abilities: American Sign Language Proficiency, Reading Skills, and Family Characteristics. Psychology, 2(1), 18-23. https://doi.org/10.4236/psych.2011.21003

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pearson correlation in a convenience sample of 55 deaf individuals from Gallaudet University and the Washington, DC area, relating ASL-SRT raw scores to WJ III Passage Comprehension raw scores. The study reports a significant positive relationship with printed effect size r = 0.48 (p < 0.001).

> "A Pearson correlation was conducted to analyze the relationship between the ASL-SRT raw scores and the Passage Comprehension raw scores. A significant relationship occurred; r (53) = 0.48, p < 0.001."
