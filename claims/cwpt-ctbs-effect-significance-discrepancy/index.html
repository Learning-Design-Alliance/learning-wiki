ClassWide Peer Tutoring: WWC Intervention Report 2007¶

ClassWide Peer Tutoring: WWC Intervention Report. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.jgcp.ku.edu

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial with 218 sixth-grade students in six schools; Appendix A3 reports CTBS-U means of 46.17 (CWPT) versus 40.77 (comparison), a mean difference of 5.40, effect size 0.35, "ns" at α = 0.05, improvement index +14. Corrections for clustering were needed.

"Greenwood et al. (1993) reported a statistically significant effect of CWPT on the Comprehensive Test of Basic Skills–Reading. According to WWC analysis, however, the effect was not statistically significant. The effect size was large enough to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria (that is, an effect size of at least 0.25)."
