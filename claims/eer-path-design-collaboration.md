## Evidence

### Vorderobermeier 2024

Vorderobermeier, A., Abel, J., & Sailer, M. (2024). Theoretical foundations and approaches in research on educational escape rooms: A systematic review. Educational Research Review, 44, 100625. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100625

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing Hamilton et al. (2021) and Veldkamp et al., that path structure shapes collaboration. Hamilton et al. empirically compared linear and open path escape rooms; the review narrates the finding without effect sizes.

> "A linear path design tends to foster collaboration skills (Hamilton et al., 2021). This sequential organization of puzzles requires players to work together on a single puzzle at a time (Hamilton et al., 2021). By contrast, path-based, hybrid structures or open-path EERs allow participants to collaborate by splitting off and working on different puzzles independently"
