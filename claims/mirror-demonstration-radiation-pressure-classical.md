`q2 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

A simplified version of a calculation described by Planck: a wave reflects off a near-perfect conductor, induced surface currents are found via Ampère's law, and the Lorentz force on them gives the pressure; "it avoids consideration of the force acting on a point charge" entirely classically.

> "The great advantage of the method is that it avoids consideration of the force acting on a point charge and can therefore be carried out at the purely class ical level."
