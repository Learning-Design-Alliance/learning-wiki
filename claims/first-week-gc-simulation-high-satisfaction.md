## Evidence

### Jacobs 2025

Jacobs, C., & McEwen, A. (2025). Embedding simulation in genetic counselor education from the first week of training: Learning outcomes, standardized clients, and students' satisfaction. Journal of Genetic Counseling, 34, e1857. https://doi.org/10.1002/jgc4.1857

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive survey evaluation of four cohorts of first-year students in an Australasian master of genetic counseling program; the article reports "103 students (97.2%) completed the evaluation study" and mean overall satisfaction of 95.9% (SD 3.5; Table 1). No effect size is printed.

> "Between 2019 and 2022, 106 students (four cohorts) participated in simulation workshops on the fifth day of the first teaching block. 103 students (97.2%) completed the evaluation study."
