## Evidence

### Monica-Iuliana Anca & Muşata Bocoş 2017

Monica-Iuliana Anca & Muşata Bocoş. (2017). The Role of Independent Activities in Development of Strategic Learning Competences and Increase of School Performance Level, within the Study of High School Pedagogy. Educatia 21 Journal, (15). https://doi.org/10.24193/ed21.2017.15.07

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-test comparison using the t test for independent focus groups after a Levene test accepted variance homogeneity; averages were 4.21 (experimental, n=84) and 4.19 (control, n=81). Equivalence beyond the non-significant test was not tested.

> "Whereas p>α (0.05), the H0 hypothesis is accepted, meaning there are no significant difference between the two focus groups in what concerns the grades average values at the initial test."
