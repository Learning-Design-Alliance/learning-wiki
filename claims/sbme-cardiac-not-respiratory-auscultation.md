## Evidence

### McInerney Niall 2022

McInerney Niall, Nally D., Khan M.F., Heneghan H., Cahill R.A. (2022). Performance effects of simulation training for medical students – a systematic review. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2022-39/zma001572.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attributing this to Kern et al.'s study comparing Harvey simulator training plus standard curriculum versus standard curriculum alone, assessed by multi-station OSCE five weeks after learning. The review reports "no difference in pulmonary examination skills."

> "Students who received simulation training performed significantly better in the respective assessed cardiac skills than the control group. Again, there was no difference in pulmonary examination skills."

### McInerney Niall 2022 (2)

McInerney Niall, Nally D., Khan M.F., Heneghan H., Cahill R.A. (2022). Performance effects of simulation training for medical students – a systematic review. GMS Journal for Medical Education. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2022-39/zma001572.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review attributing this to Bernardi et al.'s prospective study of patient simulation in heart and lung auscultation. The review reports improvement in cardiac but "no improvement in respiratory auscultation between the simulation and control group."

> "Equally, Bernardi [28] demonstrated an improvement in cardiac auscultation skills when practiced on a simulator. There was however no improvement in respiratory auscultation between the simulation and control group."
