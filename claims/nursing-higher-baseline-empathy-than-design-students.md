`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Kruskal-Wallis H test across the three study groups at pre-test in the female group. Post-hoc pairwise comparison found "nursing (mean rank = 23.11) and landscape (mean rank = 7.58) in the pre-test measurement (p = 0.004)".
