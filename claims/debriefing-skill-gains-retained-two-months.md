## Evidence

### Jaffrelot M 2024

Jaffrelot M, Boet S, Floch Y, Garg N, Dubois D, Laparra V, Touffet L, Bould MD. (2024). Learning with our peers: peer-led versus instructor-led debriefing for simulated crises, a randomized controlled trial. Korean J Anesthesiol. https://doi.org/10.4097/kja.23317

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Retention post-test two months after training in the same randomized trial; all groups retained large pre-test-to-retention gains with no significant post-test-to-retention difference (P = 0.358). The article reports "the improvement was retained for at least two months."

> "Estimated marginal means for the change in the total OGRS score between the pre-test and retention test were 12.8 (95% CI [9.1, 16.4]) for the instructor debriefing group, 14.8 (95% CI [11.0, 18.6]) for the debriefer group, and 16.3 (95% CI [12.6, 20.1]) for the debriefee group."
