## Evidence

### Almutairi Seham 2026

Almutairi Seham. (2026). Riding toward better math: executive functions mediate the link between horseback activities and children's mathematical achievement. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04596-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Direct-effects SEM model (Model 1) in a cross-sectional correlational study of 262 child–parent dyads aged 7–9 in Riyadh, using club-record riding hours and Noor system math grades. The article reports "horseback-riding hours (β = 0.175, p = 0.004)" as a significant positive predictor.

> "In the direct-effects model, horseback-riding hours (β = 0.175, p = 0.004) and age (β = 0.156, p = 0.011) significantly predicted mathematics scores."
