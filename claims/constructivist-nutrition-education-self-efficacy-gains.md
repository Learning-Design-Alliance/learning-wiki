## Evidence

### McCaughtry N 2011

McCaughtry N, Fahlman M, Martin JJ, Shen B. (2011). Influences of constructivist-oriented nutrition education on urban middle school students' nutrition knowledge, self-efficacy, and behaviors. Am J Health Educ. 2011; 42(5):276-285. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ944012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated measures ANOVA of dietary self-efficacy in the same quasi-experimental study. The article reports "Cohen's f2 = .66 for dietary self-efficacy" and per-item effect sizes including "Cohen's f2 = 1.03" for fruits and vegetables and "Cohen's f2 = .30" for eating healthy at a fast food restaurant.

> "There were significant group F(1, 2130) = 4.921, P < 0.001 and time F(1, 2130) = 7.219, P< 0.001 main effects and a group x time interaction F(1, 2130) = 6.814, P < 0.001; Cohen's f2 = .66 for dietary self-efficacy"
