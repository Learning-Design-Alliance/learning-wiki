## Evidence

### Salah Eldin Kassab; Shadan Mariam; Ziganshina Arina; Khanday Shifan; Hamdy Hossam 2026

Salah Eldin Kassab; Shadan Mariam; Ziganshina Arina; Khanday Shifan; Hamdy Hossam. (2026). AI-Assisted Angoff Standard Setting for Multiple-Choice Examinations in Medical Education: Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/98766

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Paired-sample t test comparing LLM-derived and faculty-derived Angoff cutoff scores for the same 120-item examination; the study reports no statistically significant difference (P=.79), with means of 68.94 (LLMs) and 69.13 (faculty).

> "A paired-sample t test showed that the Angoff cutoff scores produced by the LLMs (mean 68.94, SD 12.39) were closely comparable to those produced by faculty judges (mean 69.13, SD 10.92), and the difference between the 2 groups did not reach statistical significance (95% CI −2.21 to 2.91; t119=0.27; P=.79)."
