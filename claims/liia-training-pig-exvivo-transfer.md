## Evidence

### Xia Jianfu 2023

Xia Jianfu, Wu Junjie, Chen Hao, Mao Jinlei, Xu Xiaodong, Zhang Jing, Yang Jin, Wang Zhifei. (2023). Assessment of laparoscopic intracorporeal intestinal anastomosis training using simulation-based 3D printed models: exploring surgical performance and learning curves. International Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1097/JS9.0000000000000582

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Transfer verification in which eleven surgeons performed LIIA on pig ex vivo organs after model training (Figure 4). The null difference was reported without a test statistic or effect size; equivalence was not formally tested.

> "The operation times and scores of the novices who received repeated training were not different from those of the intermediates who did not receive repeated training."
