### Sattler 2025

Sattler, Kierra; Font, Sarah; Panlilio, Carlomagno. (2025). Foster care entry and later academic achievement among infants involved with child protective services. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.70008

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Logistic regression on linked Wisconsin administrative data for 8,795 children reported to CPS in infancy, controlling for child, family, case, and district covariates. The article reports "removal during early infancy (OR = 1.21, p < .01)" and toddlerhood OR = 1.25 for reading; no standardized effect size is printed.

> "Compared with never experiencing removal by age 5, removal during early infancy (OR = 1.21, p < .01) and toddlerhood (OR = 1.25, p < .01) was associated with higher odds of demonstrating basic or above reading skills in grade 3."
