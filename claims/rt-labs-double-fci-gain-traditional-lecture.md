# Replacing traditional labs with Real Time Physics labs plus recitations doubled algebra-based FCI normalized gain even under traditional lecture, but gains stayed below the interactive-engagement region

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In algebra-based introductory physics, introducing RT labs and recitations with traditional lecture raised FCI normalized gain from 0.09 to 0.18, still closer to traditional scores than to the IE region. [→ Beth Thacker 2013](#beth-thacker-2013)

## Evidence

### Beth Thacker 2013

Beth Thacker, Hani Dulli, Dave Pattillo, and Keith West. (2013). Lessons from a Large-Scale Assessment: Results from Conceptual Inventories. Physics Department, Texas Tech University. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1312.6000)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Large-scale pre/post assessment of algebra-based introductory physics at Texas Tech using the FCI, comparing traditional labs with RT labs plus recitations under traditional lecture; the article reports normalized gains of "0.09 +/- 0.02" and "0.18 +/- 0.01". No effect size beyond the normalized gain is printed.

> "The traditional (T) labs have a very low normalized gain with TL lecture instruction, 0.09 +/- 0.02. When RT labs and recitations were introduced, also with TL instruction, the normalized gain is still not particularly high, but it has increased by 100% above the T labs to 0.18 +/- 0.01."
