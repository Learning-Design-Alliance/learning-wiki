### Back David A 2014

Back David A, Haberstroh Nicole, Antolic Andrea, Sostmann Kai, Schmidmaier Gerhard, Hoff Eike. (2014). Blended learning approach improves teaching in a problem-based learning environment in orthopedics - a pilot study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-17

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Chi-square comparison of evaluation questionnaire responses on prior LMS use between user and non-user groups; the difference was highly significant. The article infers that voluntary e-learning uptake may depend on familiarity with the hosting LMS.

> "91% of NESTOR users were already using Blackboard, compared to 59% of non-users (p < 0.01)."
