### Möller 2020

Möller, J., Zitzmann, S., Helm, F., Machts, N., & Wolff, F. (2020). A Meta-Analysis of Relations Between Achievement and Self-Concept. Review of Educational Research, 90(3), 376–419. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654320919354

`q3 · i0` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Meta-analysis of the classic I/E model correlations (k = 118 data sets). The same table reports "The average self-concept correlation was r = .07, indicating a small positive relation", a negligible effect size, supporting Hypotheses 1 and 2. All achievement correlations except one high-ability study were significantly positive.
