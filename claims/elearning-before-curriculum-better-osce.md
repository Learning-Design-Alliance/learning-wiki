## Evidence

### Lien Wan-Ching 2022

Lien Wan-Ching, Lin Phone, Chang Chih-Heng, Wu Meng-Che, Wu Cheng-Yi. (2022). The effect of e-learning on point-of-care ultrasound education in novices. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2022.2152522

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

OSCE after the day-7 FAST curriculum, scored by an e-learning-blinded instructor on a 5-point global rating scale; Table 1 shows users 4 [4,5] vs. non-users 4 [4], p < 0.0001. The article reports "The e-learning PGY-1 users had better OSCE performance than non-users".

> "The e-learning PGY-1 users had better OSCE performance than non-users (Table 1)."

### Lien Wan-Ching 2022 (2)

Lien Wan-Ching, Lin Phone, Chang Chih-Heng, Wu Meng-Che, Wu Cheng-Yi. (2022). The effect of e-learning on point-of-care ultrasound education in novices. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2022.2152522

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Timing comparison among the 70 e-learning users (48 before vs. 22 after the curriculum); the article reports "better performance in the OSCE than those accessing the e-learning after the curriculum" with p = 0.001, and an adjusted coefficient of 0.56 ± 0.09, p < 0.0001.

> "Forty-eight residents with prior access to the e-learning before the curriculum had better performance in the OSCE than those accessing the e-learning after the curriculum (median [IQR], 5 [4–5] vs. 4 [4], p = 0.001, Supplementary file 3)."
