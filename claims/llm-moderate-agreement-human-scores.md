## Evidence

### Dhillon IK 2026

Dhillon IK, Lee GKY, Hu S. (2026). Evaluating the Pediatric Behavior Guidance of Students Based on Actual Clinical Transcripts Scored by Faculty and Large Language Models: Pilot Comparative Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/83376

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of ChatGPT (GPT-4o free; o4-mini-high paid) scores against human transcript scores across 40 encounters; absolute agreement ICCs of 0.729 and 0.670, both moderate.

> "Comparing transcript scores to the free-to-use LLM’s scores resulted in an absolute agreement ICC of 0.729 (95% CI 0.475‐0.859; P<.001), demonstrating moderate agreement, and comparing transcript scores to the paid LLM’s scores resulted in an absolute agreement ICC of 0.670 (95% CI 0.377‐0.825; P<.001), also demonstrating moderate agreement."
