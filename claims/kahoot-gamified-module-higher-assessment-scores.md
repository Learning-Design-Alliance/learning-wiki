# Mid-stake end-of-block assessment scores were significantly higher in the Kahoot!-gamified cardiovascular module than in the non-gamified respiratory module (median paired difference 4.89%, r = 0.31)

## Subclaims
`q2 i2` Assessment score percentages were significantly higher in the cardiovascular (experimental) module than the respiratory (control) module within the same cohort, with a median paired difference of 4.89% points and effect size r = 0.31. [→ Moin Hira 2026](#moin-hira-2026)

## Evidence

### Moin Hira 2026

Moin Hira, Zafar Sarim, Ashraf Rizwan, Majeed Sadaf, Asad Munazza. (2026). The power of play-based learning: evaluating the impact of collaborative gamified quizzing on medical students’ learning through the lens of Kirkpatrick’s framework. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08766-y

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Within-subject quasi-experimental comparison of mid-stake end-of-block exam scores for the same cohort (N = 101), cardiovascular module with Kahoot! gamification versus respiratory module without. Wilcoxon signed-rank test showed "a median paired difference of 4.89% points" with r = 0.31. Equivalence was not tested; the design is quasi-experimental.

> "The Hodges-Lehmann estimator showed a median paired difference of 4.89% points (95% CI: 1.72–6.95). The effect size was r = 0.31, indicating moderate effect and reflecting a meaningful impact of the intervention"
