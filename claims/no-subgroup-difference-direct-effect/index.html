Evidence¶

Zhang 2023¶

Zhang, D., Ke, S. (E.), & Mo, Y. (2023). Morphology in Reading Comprehension Among School-Aged Readers of English: A Synthesis and Meta-Analytic Structural Equation Modeling Study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 115(5), 683–699. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000797

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Multi-group path analyses placed equality constraints on the direct paths across subgroups defined by language status, age/grade, and task modality. No significant differences emerged in the direct effect magnitude, though the lower elementary subgroup's direct effect was itself non-significant.

"Multi-group path analyses showed no significant subgroup difference in the magnitude of the direct effect of MA on reading comprehension for any moderator."
