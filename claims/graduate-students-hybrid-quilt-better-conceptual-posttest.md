Paul D. Justice, Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2025). A Framework for Understanding the Impact of Integrating Conceptual and Quantitative Reasoning in a Quantum Optics Tutorial on Students' Conceptual Understanding. [doi:10.3390/educsci15101314](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci15101314)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Multi-year comparison at one US research university of matched pre/post conceptual scores for first-year Ph.D. students using the hybrid versus conceptual QuILT; the article reports the hybrid group "performed at least as well or better" and gives normalized gains and Cohen's d per question in Tables 1 and 2 rather than inferential tests.

> "graduate students typically performed at least as well or better on conceptual problems on post-test after engaging with the hybrid MZI QuILT that combines conceptual and quantitative problem-solving compared to when they engaged with the conceptual -only QuILT"
