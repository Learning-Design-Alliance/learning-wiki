### Herpin 2025

Herpin, S. A., Wynveen, K., & Berry, W. (2025). California's Proposition 28 Arts and Music Funding Guarantee and Accountability Act: Initial impact on arts organizations. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED677596

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey findings collected while schools and districts were in their second year of receiving Prop 28 AMS funds. Some organizations' main funding remained federal grants or fundraising; one school used funds for a few classroom visits while a parent-teacher association covered other services as in prior years.

> "Several respondents from community arts organizations indicated it was too early in the implementation of Prop 28 AMS for their organizations to feel an impact."
