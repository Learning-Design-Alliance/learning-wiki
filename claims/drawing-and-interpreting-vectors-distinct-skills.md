## Evidence

### Christoph Hoyer 2024

Christoph Hoyer, Raimund Girwidz. (2024). Vector representations and unit vector representations of fields — problems of understanding and possible teaching strategies. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

From the factor correlation matrix (Table 2) of the exploratory factor analysis: drawing ability (factor 1) is nearly uncorrelated with the interpretation factors, supporting separable skills.

> "Interestingly, the factor relating to the ability to draw vectors (factor 1) shows almost no correlation with the three factors representing the ability to interpret vector representations (factors 2–4)."

### Christoph Hoyer 2024 (2)

Christoph Hoyer, Raimund Girwidz. (2024). Vector representations and unit vector representations of fields — problems of understanding and possible teaching strategies. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.20.010150)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Summary result of the study with 124 11th-graders: generation items were nearly universally solved while interpretation items showed "significant difficulties", indicating the two skill sets differ.

> "The results show that almost all test subjects solved the items for generating vector representations correctly, but significant difficulties were encountered in interpreting vector representations."
