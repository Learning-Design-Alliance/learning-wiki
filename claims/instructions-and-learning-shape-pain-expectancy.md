## Evidence

### Atlas Lauren Y 2022

Atlas Lauren Y, Dildine Troy C, Palacios-Barrios Esther E, Yu Qingbao, Reynolds Richard C, Banker Lauren A, Grant Shara S, Pine Daniel S. (2022). Instructions and experiential learning have similar impacts on pain and pain-related brain responses but produce dissociations in value-based reversal learning. eLife 11. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73353

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Expectancy ratings collected before conditioning and after the first acquisition block in the fMRI task (n=40). The baseline Group x Cue interaction is quoted verbatim; post-acquisition, both groups reported higher expectancy with the high pain cue (Instructed p<0.001, Uninstructed p=0.003).

> "there was a significant Group x Cue interaction on expectancy at baseline (F(1,38) = 8.959, p=0.005), driven by significant differences in the Instructed Group (p=0.0027) but not the Uninstructed Group (p>0.3)"
