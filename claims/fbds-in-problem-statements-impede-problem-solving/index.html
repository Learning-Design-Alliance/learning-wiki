Evidence¶

Vanes Mešić 2017¶

Vanes Mešić, Sabaheta Mahmutović, Elvedin Hasović, Nataša Erceg. (2017). Free-Body Diagrams and Problem Solving in Mechanics: An Example of The Effectiveness of Self-Constructed Representations. European J of Physics Education, Volume 7 Issue 3. https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/1309-7202

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized experiment with 36 first-year physics students (18 per group) solving two versions of a five-problem set; ANCOVA with Mechanics exam as covariate showed a significant effect of FBD inclusion, "F (1, 33) =25.92, p<0.001"; the converted correlation r=0.66 is described as a large effect.

"The results of regular ANCOVA show that that there is a significant effect of inclusion of FBDs into problem statements on students’ score on the five-problem set, F (1, 33) =25.92, p<0.001."
