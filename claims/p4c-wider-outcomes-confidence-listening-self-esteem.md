### Gorard et al. 2015

Stephen Gorard, Nadia Siddiqui and Beng Huat See. (2015). Philosophy for Children: Evaluation report and Executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED581147

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Process-evaluation evidence from informal interviews during 30 evaluator visits to treatment schools and observations of eight P4C sessions; these are perceptions, not tested outcomes. Some teachers also perceived positive impacts on classroom engagement and pupils asking more questions across all lessons.

> "Feedback from teachers and pupils also suggested a belief that P4C had a beneficial impact on wider outcomes such as pupils' confidence to speak, patience when listening to others, and self-esteem."
