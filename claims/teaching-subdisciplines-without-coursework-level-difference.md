## Evidence

### Hall TR 2025

Hall TR, Mosley CF, Vakil JB, Lambert CK, Savelli DM, Balta JY. (2025). Who is teaching anatomy? An examination of the demographic characteristics, academic backgrounds, and professional responsibilities of US anatomy teachers. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70005

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Chi-squared test of independence from the survey (n = 459) comparing level taught and teaching without prior coursework; the article prints a phi coefficient of 0.25 for this association, which the article's threshold (over 0.15) calls a strong effect. Overall, 33% (151 of 460) of subdiscipline teachers lacked comparable coursework.

> "there was a significant association between level taught (graduate/professional vs. not) and traditional anatomical sciences subdiscipline taught without previous graduate coursework (χ 2(1, n = 459) = 27.83, p < 0.01, ϕ = 0.25). Sixty‐seven percent (32 of 48) of respondents who taught at least one traditional anatomical sciences subdiscipline below the graduate/professional level did so without comparable graduate coursework compared to 29% (119 of 412) of respondents who taught at the graduate/professional level."
