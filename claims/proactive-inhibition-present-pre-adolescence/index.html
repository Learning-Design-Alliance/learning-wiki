Evidence¶

Pas P. 2021¶

Pas P., Hulshoff Pol H.E., Raemaekers M., Vink M. (2021). Self-regulation in the pre-adolescent brain. Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2021.101012

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Behavioral analysis of the Stop-Signal Anticipation Task in 640 children showed "Participants slowed their responses more with increasing stop-signal probability, F(2, 638) = 248.71; p < 0.001". No effect size was printed. The authors state this is the first study of sex and developmental effects on both inhibition types at this age.

"Participants slowed their responses more with increasing stop-signal probability, F(2, 638) = 248.71; p < 0.001, regardless of sex, F(2, 638) = 2.52, p = 0.11."
