### Matheus A. S. Pessoa and Rebecca Brosseau 2024

Matheus A. S. Pessoa and Rebecca Brosseau, Benjamin J. Dringoli, Jack C. Sankey, Thomas Brunner, and Kenneth Ragan, April Colosimo, Armin Yazdani, Janette Barrington, and Marcy Slapcoff. (2024). Assessing Students' Understanding of Uncertainty in Undergraduate Physics Laboratory Courses at a Major Canadian University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15382](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15382)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Longitudinal observational analysis of CDPA scores in the two second-year experimental methods courses. The increase was consistent in three of four years; the authors note the 2020 Winter PHYS 258 cohort completed only 60% of the course due to the pandemic, which may partially explain lower scores that year.

> "When comparing PHYS 257 to PHYS 258, we notice an increase in student understanding that is consistent across three out of four years under investigation."

### Matheus A. S. Pessoa and Rebecca Brosseau 2024 (2)

Matheus A. S. Pessoa and Rebecca Brosseau, Benjamin J. Dringoli, Jack C. Sankey, Thomas Brunner, and Kenneth Ragan, April Colosimo, Armin Yazdani, Janette Barrington, and Marcy Slapcoff. (2024). Assessing Students' Understanding of Uncertainty in Undergraduate Physics Laboratory Courses at a Major Canadian University. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.15382](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.15382)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of CDPA average scores for PHYS 257 and PHYS 258 combined across four periods (pre-pandemic, pandemic online, pandemic hybrid, in person), shown in Figure 3. The authors report no significant difference and suggest the pedagogical benefit of labs may relate more to hands-on instrument manipulation than conceptual understanding.

> "Surprisingly, we notice no significant difference in CDPA scores between these pre- and post-pandemic periods."
