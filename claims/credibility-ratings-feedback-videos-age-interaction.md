## Evidence

### Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck 2023

Alexandra Sobczak and Nico Bunzeck. (2023). Effects of positive and negative social feedback on motivation, evaluative learning, and socio-emotional processing. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00178-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Credibility (believability) ratings of feedback videos in the SID-Video study on a −10 to 10 scale. Post-hoc two-sample tests showed significant young-vs-older differences in the neutral (p = 0.0050) and positive (p = 0.0001) but not negative condition; mean positive-video credibility was 1.94 (young) vs 4.94 (older).
