# School-level academic achievement does not mediate the association between school-level parental education and school-level academic self-concept

## Subclaims
`q3 i0` School-level parental education was robustly associated with school-level AA, but the association between school-level AA and school-level ASC was not significant, so the mediation hypothesis was not supported. [→ Horoz 2025](#horoz-2025)

## Evidence

### Horoz 2025

Horoz, N., van Atteveldt, N. M., Oude Groeniger, J., Koot, J. R., ..., & Buil, J. M. (2025). Household- and school-level parental education and academic self-concept development in elementary school. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00354-x

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

School-level mediation analysis (14 schools with achievement data) found school-level parental education associated with lower school-level AA (β = −0.421, p < 0.001) but school-level AA was not associated with school-level ASC (β = −0.072, p = 0.864); "the association between school-level AA and school-level ASC was not signiﬁcant".

> "While we found robust associations between school-level parental education and school-level AA, the association between school-level AA and school-level ASC was not signiﬁcant."
