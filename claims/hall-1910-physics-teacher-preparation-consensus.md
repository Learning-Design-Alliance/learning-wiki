Review report of Hall's questionnaire consensus among sixty college and high-school physics teachers, stating general agreement that secondary teachers "qualifying himself for the Master's degree in physics" should be prepared at the level of college work plus a year of graduate study. Reported as the reviewer's summary of the survey.

> "There seems fo be a general agreement that the physics teacher for the secondary school should have a preparation equiva- lent at least to the ordinary college course plus oue year's graduate work, thus qualifying himself for the Master's degree in physics."
