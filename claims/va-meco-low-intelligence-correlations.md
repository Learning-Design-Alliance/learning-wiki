## Evidence

### Reiser S 2025

Reiser S, Schacht L, Thomm E, Janssen L, Pittroff SID, Dörfler E, Schick K, Berberat PO, Gartmeier M, Bauer J. (2025). Online assessment of medical students' communication competence in patient encounters: Validation of the VA-MeCo situational judgement test. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0332957

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Correlational analysis in the N = 395 survey using the DESIGMA A+ intelligence measure. The article reports "low positive correlations with intelligence", consistent with the a priori hypothesis of at most medium correlations (r ≤ .3) and below the meta-analytic SJT–intelligence mean of ρ = .32 the authors cite.

> "Moreover, we found low positive correlations with intelligence, which supports our expectations."
