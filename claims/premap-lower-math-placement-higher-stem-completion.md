## Evidence

### Sarah Garner 2013

Sarah Garner, Michael J. Tremmel, Sarah J. Schmidt, John P. Wisniewski, Eric Agol. (2013). Evaluation of a College Freshman Diversity Research Program. Originally published 2012. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1311.5486](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1311.5486)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational analysis of UW student database records (Table 2) comparing STEM graduation fractions by initial math course for Pre-MAP versus total UW graduates; e.g., Math 120 Pre-Calculus: 50% of Pre-MAP versus 25% of total UW graduates graduated in STEM. The authors attribute this partly to early mentoring, guidance and support.

> "Further, Pre-MAP students that place into lower math courses are more successful at completing a STEM degree than their counterparts (see columns four and five)."
