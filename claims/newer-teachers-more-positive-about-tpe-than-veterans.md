## Evidence

### Slotnik 2014

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2014). Real progress in Maryland: Student learning objectives and teacher and principal evaluation. WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center. http://marylandpublicschools.org/MSDE/programs/tpe/index.html

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey subgroup comparison across experience levels (Table 3) in the 16,314-respondent Maryland educator survey. The report states "teachers who are relatively new to teaching with 0 to 3 years of experience are even more positive than veteran teachers about TPE and its potential impacts"; for example, 75% of new teachers versus 58% of veterans agree observers are qualified. Differences are marked significant at the 95% level; no effect size is printed.
