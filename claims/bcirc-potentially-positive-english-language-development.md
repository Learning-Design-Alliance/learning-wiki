# BCIRC has potentially positive effects on English language development (effect size 0.29, not statistically significant)

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i1` Third-grade English language learners in BCIRC did not differ statistically significantly from comparison students on the NAPT language scale, though the effect size of 0.29 was substantively important. [→ Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition 2007](#bilingual-cooperative-integrated-reading-and-composition-2007)

## Evidence

### Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition 2007

Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED405428

`q2 · i1` · `causal · r2`

The WWC review of the quasi-experimental study reports no statistically significant difference in English language development; the appendix table shows a printed effect size of 0.29 ("0.29 ns +11"), an improvement index of +11, and means of 34.90 versus 30.36 for 85 third graders. The effect was rated substantively important.

> "Calderón, Hertz- L azarowitz, and Slavin (1998) did not find statistically significant  d ifferences in English language development between the English language learners who participated in the BCIRC program and students in the comparison group."
