## Evidence

### Bartley 2019

Bartley, J. E., Riedel, M. C., Salo, T., Boeving, E. R., Bottenhorn, K. L., Bravo, E. I., Odean, R., Nazareth, A., Laird, R. W., Sutherland, M. T., Pruden, S. M., Brewe, E. & Laird, A. R. (2019). Brain activity links performance in science reasoning with conceptual approach. npj Science of Learning 4:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0059-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Task-based psychophysiological interaction (PPI) analyses with three seeds (left V5/MT+, left dlPFC, RSC) in 107 students, thresholded at P < 0.001 CDT, P < 0.05 CET (FWE corrected). The authors suggest complex visual information may be carried through a dorsal stream to frontoparietal regions directing CEN–DMN exchanges.

> "revealed greater physics problem solving-related coupling (relative to control conditions) of the left V5/MT + with DAN brain areas, the left dlPFC with V5/MT + and DMN areas, and the RSC with frontoparietal, DMN, and salience network (SN) regions."
