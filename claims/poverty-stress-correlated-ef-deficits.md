## Evidence

### Ackerman 2017

Ackerman, D. J., & Friedman-Krauss, A. H. (2017). Preschoolers’ Executive Function: Importance, Contributors, Research Needs and Assessment Options. Policy Information Report and ETS Research Report Series No. RR-17-22. https://doi.org/10.1002/ets2.12148

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The report’s narrative review cites multiple studies linking poverty, low SES, abuse, neglect, violence, and reduced educational resources to EF deficits. It states that such home environments "are correlated with deficits in children’s EF as compared to children growing up in more optimal environments." The report also notes chronic stress exposure can alter brain structure and function, particularly affecting the prefrontal cortex. No effect sizes are printed.
