### Steinberg and Sabella 1997

Richard N. Steinberg and Mel S. Sabella. (1997). Performance on Multiple-Choice Diagnostics and Complementary Exam Problems. The Physics Teacher 35, 150–155. https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2344625

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Comparison of FCI question 18 (elevator at constant velocity) with the first part of exam problem 1 (ball lowered at constant speed) for the 28 students in one spring 1996 lecture class who completed both. The article reports "only 54% answered the FCI question correctly, whereas 90% answered the exam problem correctly."

> "Of the 28 students who took both the FCI and the final exam, only 54% answered the FCI question correctly, whereas 90% answered the exam problem correctly."
