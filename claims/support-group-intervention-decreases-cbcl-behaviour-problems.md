## Evidence

### Irma Eloff and Michelle Finestone; Brian Forsyth 2016

Irma Eloff and Michelle Finestone; Brian Forsyth. (2016). HIV/AIDS infected mothers’ experience of a group intervention to enhance their children’s behavior. South African Journal of Education, Volume 36, Number 2. https://doi.org/10.15700/saje.v36n2a1285

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

One-group pretest-posttest quasi-experiment within the Kgolo Mmogo project; mother-reported CBCL scores at baseline and six-month follow-up. The withdrawn domain showed a "1.10 point decrease" with a significant paired t-test, t(93) = 3.36, p = 0.001; no effect size printed.

> "An analysis of the comparison of the mean using a two -tailed, paired t-test (α  = 0.05) level revealed a significant difference in the withdrawn behavio ural problems reported by the mothers, t (93) = 3.36, p = 0.001."

### Irma Eloff and Michelle Finestone; Brian Forsyth 2016 (2)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Same paired-samples t-test analysis of mother-reported CBCL domain means (N = 94 per Table 1). Social problems decreased "1.42 point" significantly, t(93) = 3.57, p = 0.001; no effect size printed.

> "There was furthermore a 1.42 point decrease in the reported social problems , and a decrease in the standard deviation. An analysis revealed a significant difference in the social problems reported by the mothers, t (93) = 3.57, p = 0.001."
