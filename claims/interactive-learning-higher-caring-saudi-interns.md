## Evidence

### AlKhaibary AA 2021

AlKhaibary AA, Ramadan FZ, Aboshaiqah AE, Baker OG, AlZaatari SZ, AlZaatari SZ. (2021). Determining the effects of traditional learning approach and interactive learning activities on personal and professional factors among Saudi intern nurses. Nursing Open. https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.633

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Comparative study of 48 Saudi intern nurses (24 per group) at two tertiary hospitals using independent sample t test on CAT-NE scores. The caring item contrast showed "significantly higher among interactive learning activity group (mean = 4.5, SD 0.7)". No effect size printed.

> "The mean score for caring was significantly higher among interactive learning activity group (mean = 4.5, SD 0.7) compared with traditional group (mean = 2.7, SD 0.9). The findings have shown a significant difference between both groups, in terms of caring factor (t (46) = 8.30, p < .0001)."
