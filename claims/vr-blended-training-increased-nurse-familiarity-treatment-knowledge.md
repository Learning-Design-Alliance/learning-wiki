### Huang Chia-Chang 2021

Huang Chia-Chang, Huang Shiau-Shian, Yang Ying-Ying, Kao Shou-Yen. (2021). Effects of a simulation-based blended training model on nurses’ treatment decision-related knowledge about oral cancer in Taiwan: a pilot survey. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2021.18.10

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A pre- and post-intervention comparison study of 26 primary nurses in the oral cancer care unit of Taipei Veterans General Hospital, evaluated with a 5-point Likert questionnaire and paired Student t-test. The article reports the training "significantly increased nurses’ familiarity (P<0.01)".

> "The blended training model significantly increased nurses’ familiarity (P<0.01) and confidence (P<0.03) regarding their knowledge of treatments and treatment decision-related knowledge."
