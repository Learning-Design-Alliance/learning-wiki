Üstün 2026¶

Üstün, A. G., & Danacıoğlu, N. (2026). The Role of Artificial Intelligence Anxiety and Attitudes Toward Artificial Intelligence in University Students’ Job Finding Anxiety. Participatory Educational Research, 13(2), 143-164. https://doi.org/10.17275/per.26.23.13.2

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Independent samples t-test by gender in the 1,057-student survey (t=8.579, df=1055, p<.001 for job finding anxiety). The study reports "A moderate effect size in favor of female students" with d=.54, the largest gender-based difference.

"As shown in Figure 4, Job Finding Anxiety showed the largest gender -based difference. A moderate effect size in favor of female students was observed, with a d=.54."

Same gender t-test analysis; for positive AI attitudes the test was non-significant (t=0.258, p=.797). The study states "no significant difference was observed" between female and male students; equivalence was not tested.

"However, no significant difference was observed between female and male students in terms of Positive AI Attitudes."
