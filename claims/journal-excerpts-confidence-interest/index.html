Birjandi and Tamjid 2010¶

Qualitative evidence: the authors cite student journal excerpts (e.g., "At first I was afraid of writing, but now I have self-confidence.") and observation during the term. The article acknowledges there was no measure of self-confidence; journal length growth is offered as informal evidence.
