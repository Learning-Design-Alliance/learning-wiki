## Evidence

### Toale Conor 2024

Toale Conor, Morris Marie, Roche Adam, Voborsky Miroslav, Traynor Oscar, Kavanagh Dara. (2024). Development and validation of a simulation-based assessment of operative competence for higher specialist trainees in general surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11024-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Internal-structure analysis of scores from 13 candidates across eight stations; the article reports α = 0.81 overall and an inter-rater intra-class correlation coefficient of 0.77 for two double-scored stations.

> "Inter-station reliability for the total score awarded was assessed using Cronbach’s alpha (α), calculated as 0.81 for the assessment overall."

### Toale Conor 2024 (2)

Toale Conor, Morris Marie, Roche Adam, Voborsky Miroslav, Traynor Oscar, Kavanagh Dara. (2024). Development and validation of a simulation-based assessment of operative competence for higher specialist trainees in general surgery. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-024-11024-1

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Generalizability theory variance-component analysis of the same assessment scores; trainee variance accounted for 38.244% of observed variance and trainee × station 39.169%, with station-level modeling indicating 14 stations needed for Phi ≥ 0.8.

> "Using Generalizability Theory, a reliability coefficient (G) of 0.7 was calculated for the overall 8-station assessment. Fourteen hypothetical stations would be required to increase the reliability coefficient to > 0.8."
