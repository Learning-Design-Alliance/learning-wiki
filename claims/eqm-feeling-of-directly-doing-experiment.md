## Evidence

### Rikke Magnussen 2015

Rikke Magnussen, Sidse Damgaard Hansen, Tilo Planke, Jacob Friis Sherson. (2015). Games as a Platform for Student Participation in Authentic Scientific Research. Originally published 2013. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1511.01653](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1511.01653)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Qualitative interview with the entire Class 1 after the test, asking the biggest difference between playing the game and normal teaching; one student gave the quoted contrast, indicating an experience of "directly doing the experiment".

> "One student stated that, “In the normal teaching you only calculate the results, while in the game you get the feeling of directly doing the experiment”."
