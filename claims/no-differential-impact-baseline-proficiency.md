### Rolfhus 2013

Rolfhus, E., Gersten, R., Clarke, B., Decker, L., Wilkins, C., & Dimino, J. (2013). From Efficacy Trial to Large Scale Effectiveness Trial: A Tier 2 Mathematics Intervention for First Graders with Difficulties in Mathematics. SREE Spring 2013 Conference Abstract. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/pubs/20124007/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory moderation analysis within the matched-pair RCT testing whether intervention effects on TEMA-3 differed by screener-measured baseline proficiency. No effect size beyond the reported interaction (0.08) was printed; equivalence was not tested.

> "Results indicate that there was no statistically significant interaction between baseline mathematics proficiency (from the screener) and the impact of Number Rockets (effect size = 0.08, p = .564)."
