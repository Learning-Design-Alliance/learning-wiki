### Adrian Madsen 2017

Adrian Madsen, Sarah B. McKagan, Eleanor C. Sayre, and Cassandra A. Paul. (2017). Resource Letter RBAI-2: Research-based assessment instruments: Beyond physics topics. American Association of Physics Teachers. [doi:10.1119/1.4977416](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.4977416)

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

Narrative review reporting the SALG developers' finding about the Student Assessment of Learning Gains, an online 5-point Likert self-assessment developed over 300 student interviews in which students discussed what they had gained from course aspects and what they liked. The review attributes the finding to the developers.

> "The SALG developers found that students' observations about what they gained from the class were useful to help faculty improve the course, whereas their observations about what they liked were not helpful."
