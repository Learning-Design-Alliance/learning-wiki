## Evidence

### Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh 2018

Alexandru Maries and Chandralekha Singh. (2018). Case of two electrostatics problems: Can providing a diagram adversely impact introductory physics students' problem solving performance? Physical Review Physics Education Research 14, 010114. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevPhysEducRes.14.010114

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Study 2 observational interviews with 23 paid volunteers enrolled in an equivalent course; each student acted as their own control, with conceptual planning time timed from reading onset to writing an equation from the provided equation sheet.

> "Including all seventeen students, we found that on average, students spent 71% more time conceptually planning the additional problem (which did not provide a diagram) than the other two problems."
