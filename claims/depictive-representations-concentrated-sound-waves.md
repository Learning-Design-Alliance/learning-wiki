`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r?`

Quantitative analysis of depictive representations by topic in the 21 exam responses. The authors report that of "the overall 39 depictive visual representations, 13 were in the topic of Sound/Waves, 11 of which being some type of waveform," with Force (Friction), Force (Balanced), Planets/Space, and Black Holes each having 4.

> "For depictive visual representations, the topic of Sound/Waves is much more represented than the other topics. From the overall 39 depictive visual representations, 13 were in the topic of Sound/Waves, 11 of which being some type of waveform."
