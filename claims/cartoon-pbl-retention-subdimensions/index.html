Evidence¶

Tanrıkulu and Baysal 2022¶

Derya Duygu Tanrıkulu, Z. Nurdan Baysal. (2022). The effect of cartoon-supported problem-based learning method in primary school, fourth-grade social studies course on students' perceptions of their problem-solving skills and their level of achievement. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 15(1). https://orcid.org/0000-0001-6713-1396

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon signed-rank test for the experimental group (n=28) comparing pre-test and retention test scores on the self-confidence sub-dimension. The article reports a statistically significant difference (z = -2.303, p = .021) in favour of the retention test.

"According to Table 4, the analyses performed for the experimental group showed that there is a statistically significant difference between the mean rankings of the participants' pre-test and retention test scores from the self-confidence sub-dimension (P< .05)."

Tanrıkulu and Baysal 2022 (2)¶

Derya Duygu Tanrıkulu, Z. Nurdan Baysal. (2022). The effect of cartoon-supported problem-based learning method in primary school, fourth-grade social studies course on students' perceptions of their problem-solving skills and their level of achievement. International Journal of Curriculum and Instruction 15(1). https://orcid.org/0000-0001-6713-1396

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Wilcoxon signed-rank tests for the experimental group (n=28) comparing pre-test and retention test scores on the total inventory, self-control, and avoidance sub-dimensions. The article reports no statistically significant differences (p = .107, .188, and .626 respectively).

"However, the relevant table shows that the pre-test and retention test scores obtained from the entire inventory and from its self-control and avoidance sub-dimensions did not significantly differ statistically in terms of mean rankings (P>.05)."
