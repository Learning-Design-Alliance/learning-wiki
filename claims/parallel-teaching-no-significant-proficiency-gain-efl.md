## Evidence

### Aliakbari 2012

Aliakbari, M. & Bazyar, A. (2012). Exploring the Impact of Parallel Teaching on General Language Proficiency of EFL Learners. Journal of Pan-Pacific Association of Applied Linguistics, 16(1), 55-71. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ979922

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with 32 male second-grade junior-high students in Ilam, Iran, assigned to control (n=16) and experimental (n=16) groups by ranked pre-test scores. Post-test means were 68.08 (CG) and 65.93 (EG); the independent-samples test showed "the difference between the subjects’ performance on the tests is not significant at P<0.05 level".

> "since the value of t-observed (.428) is not larger than the critical value of t (2.042) at 31 degree of freedom, the difference between the subjects’ performance on the tests is not significant at P<0.05 level"
