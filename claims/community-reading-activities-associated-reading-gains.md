## Evidence

### Dowd 2016

Dowd, A. J., Friedlander, E., Jonason, C., Leer, J., Sorensen, L. Z., D'Sa, N., Guajardo, J., Pava, C., & Pisani, L. (2016). Life-wide Learning and Early Reading Development in Twelve African and Asian Sites. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567231

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r2`

Cross-country multilevel regression (Model 2) fit separately per site, relating reading gains to percentage of community activities participated in, controlling for baseline scores, HLE, covariates, and treatment assignment. The article reports "effect sizes ranging from 0.2 to 1.13".
