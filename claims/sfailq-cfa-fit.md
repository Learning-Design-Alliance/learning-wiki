Liu Qingqi, Miao Wenjiao, Li Jingjing, Lei Yuju. (2026). The Six-Facet Artificial Intelligence Literacy Questionnaire (SFAILQ): Assessing AI Literacy in Adolescents, Young Adults, and Midlife Adults. Behavioral Sciences, 16(7), 1110. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071110

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis in the second split-half sample (N = 1226) evaluating construct validity of the 32-item six-facet structure. All item factor loadings exceeded 0.60 and were significant at p < 0.01; the printed fit indices are reported here.

> "The six-facet model demonstrated a marginal but acceptable fit to the data: χ2/df = 6.06, CFI = 0.93, RMSEA = 0.06 (90% CI [0.056, 0.064]), and SRMR = 0.05."
