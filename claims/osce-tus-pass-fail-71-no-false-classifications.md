## Evidence

### Pietersen Pia Iben 2023

Objective structured clinical examination in basic thoracic ultrasound: a European study of validity evidence. BMC Pulmonary Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12890-022-02285-4

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Consequences analysis in the 25-candidate validation study: normal distributions of novice and experienced scores intersected at 71 points. In the intermediate group six candidates passed (75%) and two failed (25%); categorisation was significant on Fisher's exact test, p < 0.001.

> "The pass/fail score of 71 points entailed no false positives and no false negatives; no novices passed and no experienced failed the test."
