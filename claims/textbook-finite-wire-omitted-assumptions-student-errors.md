### J.E. García-Farieta and A. Hurtado-Márquez 2021

J.E. García-Farieta and A. Hurtado-Márquez. (2021). Beyond the magnetic field of a finite wire: a teaching approach using the superposition principle. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.1119/10.0004885](https://doi.org/10.1119/10.0004885)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r1`

Theoretical argument in the Introduction, motivated by cited literature on difficulties with introductory magnetic field theory. The authors contend that omitting the assumptions (choosing a symmetry point, applying superposition, reducing from 3D to 2D or 1D) causes students to 'use incorrect assumptions', 'resulting in algebra tricks or unreasonable results'. The article offers no empirical data for this assertion.

> "Omitting these key points to evaluate the magnetic ﬁeld in such systems, leads students to use incorrect assumptions, while they try to adapt the methodology previously used in the simple case of a ﬁnite wire, resulting in algebra tricks or unreasonable results."
