## Evidence

### Mehrali 2025

Mehrali, K., M. Mohsenpour, and A. Abdollahi. 2025. "The Mediating Role of Academic Engagement in the Relationship Between Academic Hope and Academic Adjustment of Undergraduate Students: The Role of Gender as a Moderator." Brain and Behavior 15, no. 11: e71079. https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.71079

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional SEM study of 353 Iranian undergraduates sampled by multi-stage cluster sampling; bootstrapped path analysis tested the indirect path. The article reports the mediated effect as "β = 0.23, p < 0.05"; no standardized effect size beyond β is printed.

> "The bootstrap method was employed to investigate the fourth hypothesis, demonstrating that academic engagement serves as a significant mediator in the relationship between academic hope and academic adjustment (β = 0.23, p < 0.05)."
