# Fewer than one-third of sampled books including groups or cultures of color provide complex representation; over half provide limited representation

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · design `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Of the 118 books (39% of the sample) that include groups or cultures of color, 31% provide complex representation and 54% provide limited representation. [→ Tanji Reed Marshall 2023](#tanji-reed-marshall-2023)

## Evidence

### Tanji Reed Marshall 2023

Tanji Reed Marshall, Ph.D., and William H. Rodick, Ph.D. (2023). The Search for More: Complex Racial and Ethnic Representation in Grade School Books. The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/booktool

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Tool-based coding of the 118 books in the 300-book sample that include groups or cultures of color, on criteria of avoiding stereotypes, immersing people in culture, and portraying groups positively and as equally valuable. The report states "Over half (54%) of the books in our sample that include groups of color do not meet those criteria"; 31% are complex and 15% partial.

> "Over half (54%) of the books in our sample that include groups of color do not meet those criteria, demonstrating what we define as limited representation."
