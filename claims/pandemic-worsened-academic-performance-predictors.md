Stepwise multivariable logistic regression on the post-pandemic survey (Table 4) for 'worsened academic performance' (47% yes vs 53% no), retaining age, UCS, USS, introjected regulation, pride, and boredom. The article reports these "OR = 1.01, 1.05, 1.02, 1.37" values directly.

> "An increased score in such variables was associated with a higher probability of perceiving a negative impact of the pandemic on overall academic performance (OR = 1.01, 1.05, 1.02, 1.37, respectively, see Table 4). Differently, a higher age and a higher score on pride were associated with a reduced probability of 7% and 32% (OR = 0.93 and OR = 0.68, respectively)."
