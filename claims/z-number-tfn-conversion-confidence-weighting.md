### Yu Limin 2026

Yu Limin, Chen Zhe, Qin Zeyu. (2026). A novel Z-number based multi-stage assessment framework for problem-based learning in practical courses. PLOS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0349114

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

The article describes this as an analytical transformation using Zadeh's expected value of the fuzzy set modified by the reliability function, converting each Z-number into a single representative TFN for MABAC processing.

> "This transformation adjusts the evaluated importance or performance by accounting for the evaluator’s confidence, thereby ensuring that assessments with higher uncertainty exert proportionally less influence on the final decision."
