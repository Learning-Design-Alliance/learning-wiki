## Evidence

### Christa Cody 2021

Christa Cody, Mehak Maniktala, Nicholas Lytle, Min Chi, Tiffany Barnes. (2021). The Impact of Looking Further Ahead: A Comparison of Two Data-driven Unsolicited Hint Types on Performance in an Intelligent Data-driven Logic Tutor. https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.05741

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA analysis (controlling for pretest) of 114 students in the Deep Thought tutor study found the Next-Step group significantly outperformed the Waypoint group on posttest Total Steps and Accuracy; "The WP group had 28 more total steps and had a 5% lower accuracy" on the posttest.

> "More importantly, on the posttest, the NS group signiﬁcantly outper- formed the Waypoint group on Total Steps ( F (2, 111) = 3 .73, p = 0.02) and Accuracy ( F (2, 111) = 2 .38, p = 0 .05)."
