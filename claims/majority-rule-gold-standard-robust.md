## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Using the combined judgments of all ten examiners as a gold standard yielded pass decisions for all four candidates, and the same verdicts resulted when the majority rule was applied to the five most experienced or five most confident examiners. [→ Schauber 2024](#schauber-2024)

Inconsistencies in rater-based assessments mainly affect borderline candidates: but using simple heuristics might improve pass-fail decisions. Advances in Health Sciences Education, 29(5), 1749–1767. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10328-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Group-verdict analysis in the ten-examiner video-rating study, applying the majority rule to the full group and, as a robustness check, to experienced and most-confident subgroups. The article reports identical results; no effect size is printed.

> "The results were identical when the majority-rule was used for either the most experienced half of the examiners or the five most confident ones."
