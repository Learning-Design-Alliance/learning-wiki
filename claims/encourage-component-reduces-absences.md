## Evidence

### Rogers and Feller 2016

Todd Rogers, Avi Feller. (2016). Parent Beliefs and Student Absences: Large Absence-Reduction Field Experiment. SREE Spring 2016 Conference Abstract. https://www.sree.org/conferences/2016/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same four-arm randomized field experiment, the Encourage arm (7,679 students) received mail stressing the importance of attendance, parental efficacy, and absence-reduction as part of parental role. The article reports a 0.6-day (3.5%) reduction versus Control, p<0.01.

> "We find that the Encourage component reduced absences by 0.6 days (or a 3.5% decrease) as compared to Control (p<0.01)."
