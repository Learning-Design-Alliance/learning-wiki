## Evidence

### The EVALUATE Group 2019

The EVALUATE Group. (2019). Evaluating the impact of virtual exchange on initial teacher education: a European policy experiment. Research-publishing.net. https://doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2019.29.9782490057337

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The report's background section cites Papatsiba (2005) and Paige et al. (2009) as evidence that "physical mobility does not lead automatically to the development of intercultural competence". Only 3.41% of mobile students come from education.

> "A second reason for using virtual exchange as a complementary approach to student mobility is based on growing evidence in the literature that physical mobility does not lead automatically to the development of intercultural competence or an enhanced transnational identity – which are the very goals of internationalisation policies and mobility programmes."
