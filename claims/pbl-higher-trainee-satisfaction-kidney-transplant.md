### Gao C 2026

Gao C, Li J, Shui K, Xie X, Peng L. (2026). Effectiveness of Problem-Based Learning (PBL) in specialist physician training for kidney transplantation. Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000047684

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Anonymous 5-point Likert satisfaction questionnaire administered at the end of training in the same retrospective cohort. A higher PBL proportion scored ≥4 overall (89.8% vs 64.3%; χ2 = 10.715, P = .001); perceived relevance to clinical practice was also higher (4.5 ± 0.4 vs 3.9 ± 0.5).

> "The PBL group reported higher overall satisfaction scores than the control group (4.4 ± 0.4 vs 3.9 ± 0.5; t = −5.902, P < .001)"
