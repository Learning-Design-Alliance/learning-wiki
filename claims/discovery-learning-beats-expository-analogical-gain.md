# Discovery learning produces significantly greater improvement in junior high students' mathematical analogical ability than expository teaching

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Students taught with the discovery learning method showed significantly higher normalized gain in mathematical analogical ability than students taught with the expository method (sig. 0.001 < 0.05). [→ Maarif 2016](#maarif-2016)

## Evidence

### Maarif 2016

Maarif, S. (2016). Improving junior high school students' mathematical analogical ability using discovery learning method. International Journal of Research in Education and Science (IJRES), 2(1), 114-124. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1105169

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experiment with two intact seventh-grade classes (36 students each) at SMPN 13 Jakarta; experimental class used discovery learning, control class expository. Normalized gain means were 0.631 (discovery) versus 0.493 (expository); the two-way ANOVA learning factor gave "significance Two Line 0.001 < 0.05". Table 4 reports F = 12.737, p = .001 for the learning factor.

> "Based on the value of 0.493 ANOVA test, it is obtained significance Two Line 0.001 < 0.05, which means an increased ability to obtain a mathematical analogy of learning by discovery method better than those gained by learning expository method."
