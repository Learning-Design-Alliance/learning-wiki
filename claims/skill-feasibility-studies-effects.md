## Subclaims
`q2 i3` In a two-classroom quasi-experiment, pre–post effect sizes on a narrative language sample were three times larger for the SKILL classroom (d = .82) than the BAU classroom (d = .21), with differential effects favoring high-risk children. [→ Gillam 2022](#gillam-2022)

## Evidence

### Gillam 2022

Gillam, S. L., Vaughn, S., Roberts, G., Capin, P., Fall, A.-M., Israelsen-Augenstein, M., Holbrook, S., Wada, R., Hancock, A., Fox, C., Dille, J., Magimairaj, B. M., & Gillam, R. B. (2022). Improving Oral and Written Narration and Reading Comprehension of Children at-Risk for Language and Literacy Difficulties: Results of a Randomized Clinical Trial. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000766

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r3`

Quasi-experimental feasibility study with two 1st grade classrooms, 30 minutes of whole-class SKILL three times per week for six weeks. The narrative language sample effect was "three times larger" in the SKILL classroom, "d = .82" versus d = .21.

> "The pre –post effect sizes on a narrative lan- guage sample were three times larger for children in the experi- mental classroom ( d = .82) than children in the BAU class ( d = .21)."

### Gillam 2022 (2)

Gillam, S. L., Vaughn, S., Roberts, G., Capin, P., Fall, A.-M., Israelsen-Augenstein, M., Holbrook, S., Wada, R., Hancock, A., Fox, C., Dille, J., Magimairaj, B. M., & Gillam, R. B. (2022). Improving Oral and Written Narration and Reading Comprehension of Children at-Risk for Language and Literacy Difficulties: Results of a Randomized Clinical Trial. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000766

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Small-scale RCT with 20 elementary-age children with developmental language disorders, ten per condition, in groups of three for 35–40 minutes three times weekly for 6 weeks. SKILL produced "a large Cohen ’s d effect size of 1.45" on the TNL at posttest.

> "The SKILL instruction resulted in statistically signi ﬁcant gains on a standardized measure of narrative pro ﬁciency (Test of Narrative Language, TNL; Gillam & Pearson, 2004 ), yielding a large Cohen ’s d effect size of 1.45 for group differences at posttest."
