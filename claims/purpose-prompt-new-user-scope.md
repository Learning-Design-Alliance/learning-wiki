## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The self-transcendent intervention marginally increased existing users' interest in social change (p = 0.07) with no other area changes. [→ Chen 2024](#chen-2024)

## Evidence

### Chen 2024

Chen, Y., Iyer, P., & Kizilcec, R. F. (2024). Reflection on Purpose Changes Students' Academic Interests: A Scalable Intervention in an Online Course Catalog. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Existing-user topic-probability analyses in the field experiment show social change rising "from 11.5% to 15.2%" at p = 0.07 — marginal, not significant — with no significant change in creative arts, computer and data science, or business.

> "For existing users, the self-transcendent purpose intervention increased their interest in social change from 11.5% to 15.2% (t(989) = 1.83, p = 0.07), but it did not change their interest in other areas"

### Chen 2024 (2)

Chen, Y., Iyer, P., & Kizilcec, R. F. (2024). Reflection on Purpose Changes Students' Academic Interests: A Scalable Intervention in an Online Course Catalog. Originally published 2022. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.19035)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

For existing users, the self-interest condition produced no significant area-of-interest change; the quote prints the null t and p values. Equivalence was not tested.

> "The self-interest purpose intervention did not significantly change students' areas of interest (creative arts: pp change = 1.2%, t(989) = 0.52, p = 0.60; social change: pp change = 0.0%, t(989) = 0.02, p = 0.96"
