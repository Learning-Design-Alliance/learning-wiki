### Lo Kristin 2017

Lo Kristin, Curtis Heather, Keating Jennifer L., Bearman Margaret. (2017). Physiotherapy clinical educators’ perceptions of student fitness to practise. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0847-2

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Of 75 educators completing the work satisfaction question, 62 (83%, CI 75-92) stated 'yes'. Thematic analysis of 55 free-text responses identified emotional response, time pressures, lack of appreciation, quality of care conflict and perception of roles themes.

> "Student FTP issues affects the clinical educator role with 83% (95% CI 75-92) of clinicians reporting that work satisfaction was affected due to time pressures, emotional impact, lack of appreciation of educator time, quality of care conflict and a mismatch in role perception."
