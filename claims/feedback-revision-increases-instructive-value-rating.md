## Evidence

### Sisson SD 2012

Sisson SD, Rastegar DA, Hughes MT, Bertram AK, Yeh HC. (2012). Learner feedback and educational outcomes with an internet-based ambulatory curriculum: a qualitative and quantitative analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-12-55

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental before-and-after comparison of module ratings on the Johns Hopkins Internet Learning Center: 56,254 modules in the three years before feedback-based revision versus 72,596 after, with average instructive value rising from "3.8" to "4.1" (p < 0.001). No effect size is printed.

> "A total of 56,254 modules were completed in the “before” group, with an average instructive value of 3.8. In the “after” group, a total of 72,596 modules were completed, with an average instructive value of 4.1. The difference between these two scores was statistically significant (p < 0.001)."
