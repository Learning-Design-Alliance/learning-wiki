Analysis of the targeted Early Start registration campaign (texts on May 25th and June 8th) within the CSUN randomized controlled trial, comparing treated and control students required to enroll in the summer program. The article prints percentage-point enrollment contrasts of 11 and 20 points; no standardized effect size is reported.
