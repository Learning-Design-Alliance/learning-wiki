## Evidence

### Tedeschi MN 2023

Tedeschi MN, Hose TM, Mehlman EK, Franklin S, Wong TE. (2023). Improving Models for Student Retention and Graduation using Markov Chains. Originally published 2021. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2302.00464)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Markov-model subgroup analysis (Figure 3b) of AALANA science majors in high-attrition College of Science courses, all available data Fall 2013 to Summer 2021, LA-supported versus non-LA-supported students.

> "For AALANA science majors enrolled in high-attrition courses, LA support is associated with an improvement of 21% in SYGR. LA-supported AALANA students had a median SYGR of 81% (71-93%), as compared to the non-LA group of AALANA students, who had a median SYGR of 59% (53-66%)"
