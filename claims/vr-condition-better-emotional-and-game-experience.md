# MetaRoundWorm produced more positive emotional and game-experience outcomes, including higher positive affect and lower negative mood states

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (3 entries) · causal `r1` · `q3` · `i3` large

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` MetaRoundWorm participants showed significantly higher Positive Affect (r = .56) and the slides condition showed significantly higher Negative Affect (r = .51) on the GEQ. [→ Xuanru Cheng 2025](#xuanru-cheng-2025)
`q3 i3` POMS-SF negative mood dimensions (Tension-Anxiety, Depression-Dejection, Anger-Hostility, Fatigue-Inertia, Confusion-Bewilderment) were significantly lower in the VR condition, and Total Mood Disturbance was significantly lower (d = −6.46). [→ Xuanru Cheng 2025 (2)](#xuanru-cheng-2025-2)
`q3 i3` AttrakDiff Hedonic Quality-Stimulation (d = 1.32) and Attractiveness (r = .52) were significantly higher for MetaRoundWorm. [→ Xuanru Cheng 2025 (3)](#xuanru-cheng-2025-3)

## Evidence

### Xuanru Cheng 2025

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Game Experience Questionnaire administered post-session; the article reports the VR condition higher on Sensory Imaginative Immersion, Immersion Flow, Competence, and Positive Affect (U = 447.00, p < .001, r = .56), while "the Slide condition showed significantly higher Negative Affect (U = 116.50, p < .001, r = .51".

> "Conversely, the Slide condition showed significantly higher Negative Affect (U = 116.50, p < .001, r = .51 [large effect size]). compared to the Slide condition."

### Xuanru Cheng 2025 (2)

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r?`

POMS-SF mood assessment after the learning session; five negative dimensions were significantly lower in the VR group and "Total Mood Disturbance was significantly lower in the MetaRoundWorm group (t(37.50) =4.36, p < .001, d = −6.46".

> "Total Mood Disturbance was significantly lower in the MetaRoundWorm group (t(37.50) =4.36, p < .001, d = −6.46 [large effect size]), with the negative value indicating that positive mood exceeded negative mood"

### Xuanru Cheng 2025 (3)

Xuanru Cheng, Xian Wang, Chi-lok Tai and Lik-Hang Lee. (2025). MetaRoundWorm: A Virtual Reality Escape Room Game for Learning the Lifecycle and Immune Response to Parasitic Infections. [doi:10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752](https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2025.3616752)

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

AttrakDiff questionnaire administered post-session; the VR condition "significantly outperformed the Slide condition in terms of Hedonic Quality - Stimulation (HQ-S) (t(46) =4.58, p < .001, d = 1.32" and was also higher on Attractiveness (r = .52).

> "Independent samples t-tests revealed that MetaRoundWorm significantly outperformed the Slide condition in terms of Hedonic Quality - Stimulation (HQ-S) (t(46) =4.58, p < .001, d = 1.32 [large effect size])"
