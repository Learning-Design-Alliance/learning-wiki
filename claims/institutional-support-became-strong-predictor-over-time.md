## Evidence

### Trevisan 2023

Trevisan, O., De Rossi, M., Christensen, R., Knezek, G., & Smits, A. (2023). Factors shaping faculty online teaching competencies during the Covid-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive discriminant function analysis of the S1 snapshot data. Perceived Support determined the second function (correlation .94, eigenvalue .55, 10.7% of variance), discriminating mostly between the Open and Optimistic clusters.

> "At Snapshot 1, the discriminant function analysis highlighted that Resolution and Enthusiasm were the factors with the highest correlation to the first function (respectively, .74 and .60), which discriminated between the Cautious and Optimistic clusters, having an eigenvalue of 4.54 and explaining an overall 88.6% of the variance."

### Trevisan 2023 (2)

Trevisan, O., De Rossi, M., Christensen, R., Knezek, G., & Smits, A. (2023). Factors shaping faculty online teaching competencies during the Covid-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Descriptive discriminant function analysis of the S2 snapshot data. A reverse perception of Support determined the second function (correlation −.62), discriminating mostly between Self-reliant and Complying clusters.

> "Perceived Support and Enthusiasm became the factors with the highest correlation to the first function (respectively, .61 and .53), which discriminated between Struggling and Thriving profiles, having an eigenvalue of 6.29 and explaining an overall 87.4% of the variance."

### Trevisan 2023 (3)

Trevisan, O., De Rossi, M., Christensen, R., Knezek, G., & Smits, A. (2023). Factors shaping faculty online teaching competencies during the Covid-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10197-1

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Predictive discriminant analysis comparing cluster structures across snapshots. The article interprets that the profiles "seemed to have changed, as well as the factors contributing to the profiles", e.g. Enthusiastic respondents would have a 27% likelihood of being clustered as Thriving at S2.

> "A predictive discriminant analysis revealed that the original clustering strategy, accurate at 98.3% at S1, would be accurate only in 43% of the cases at S2."
