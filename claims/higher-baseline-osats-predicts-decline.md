Multivariable logistic regression in the skill decay group identified baseline OSATS as the sole significant predictor of performance decline, reporting "OR 2.72; 95% CI: 1.28–8.40; p = 0.02".

> "the only significant predictor was higher baseline OSATS score (OR 2.72; 95% CI: 1.28–8.40; p = 0.02), indicating that residents who initially performed at a higher level were more likely to experience measurable decline"
