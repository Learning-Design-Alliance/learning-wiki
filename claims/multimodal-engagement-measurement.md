## Evidence

### Kaakinen 2021

Kaakinen, J. K. (2021). What Can Eye Movements Tell us about Visual Perception Processes in Classroom Contexts? Commentary on a Special Issue. Educational Psychology Review, 33, 169-179. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09573-7

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The commentary reports, citing Goldberg et al. in the Special Issue, a machine learning approach using eye gaze, head pose, facial expressions, and peer synchronization as model features. The automated results "correlated with manual coding" and predicted self-reports. No correlation coefficients are printed.

> "The results were promising: the results of the automated analysis correlated with manual coding of the videos, and predicted self-reported cognitive engagement and involvement."
