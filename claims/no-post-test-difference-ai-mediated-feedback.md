## Evidence

### Xinyi Lu 2026

Xinyi Lu, Kexin Phyllis Ju, Mitchell Dudley, Larissa Sano, and Xu Wang. 2026. AI-Mediated Feedback Improves Student Revisions: A Randomized Trial with FeedbackWriter in a Large Undergraduate Course. Proceedings of the 2026 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26). https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791121

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-test scores were manually graded blind to condition; 262 students completed the post-test for Writing Assignment 1 and 305 for Writing Assignment 2. The article reports "no statistical difference between the two conditions on the post-test scores"; no effect size is printed, so equivalence was not established.

> "Having a higher-quality revised draft is associated with higher learning (measured by a post-test following students' submission of the revision). However, there is no statistical difference between the two conditions on the post-test scores."
