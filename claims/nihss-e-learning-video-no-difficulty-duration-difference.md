## Evidence

### Suppan M 2021

Suppan M, Stuby L, Carrera E, Cottet P, Koka A, Assal F, Savoldelli GL, Suppan L. (2021). Asynchronous Distance Learning of the National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale During the COVID-19 Pandemic (E-Learning vs Video): Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/23594

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Secondary Likert outcomes in the randomized trial. Perceived difficulty showed no significant difference (P=.07); perceived duration adequacy also showed no difference (P=.25). Equivalence was not tested; no effect sizes printed.

> "There was no significant difference regarding the perceived difficulty of the course, as 84% (16/19) (95% CI 68%-100%) found it “easy or very easy” in the e-learning group versus 53% (8/15) (95% CI 28%-78%) in the video group (P=.07)."
