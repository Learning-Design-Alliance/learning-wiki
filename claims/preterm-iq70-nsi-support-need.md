## Evidence

### Nyman A 2019

Nyman A, Korhonen T, Lehtonen L, Haataja L, on behalf of the PIPARI Study Group. (2019). School performance is age appropriate with support services in very preterm children at 11 years of age. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.14763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Table 3 chi-square comparisons: 100% of the IQ <70 subset and 100% of the NSI subset received at least one support service, each compared with preterm children without that condition, p <0.001.

> "Receiving at least one support service 13 (100) <0.001 10 (100) <0.001"

### Nyman A 2019 (2)

Nyman A, Korhonen T, Lehtonen L, Haataja L, on behalf of the PIPARI Study Group. (2019). School performance is age appropriate with support services in very preterm children at 11 years of age. Acta Paediatrica. https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.14763

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation of Table 3 patterns: NSI children differed significantly mainly in text production and support services, whereas the IQ <70 group differed across nearly all measures. The authors attribute this to 60% of NSI children having IQ ≥70.

> "NSI was less strongly associated with lower ratings of poor educational performance than was low IQ. Explanation for this may be that 60% of the children with NSI had IQ ≥ 70."
