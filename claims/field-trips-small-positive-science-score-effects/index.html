Emilyn Ruble Whitesell 2016¶

Emilyn Ruble Whitesell. (2016). A Day at the Museum: The Impact of Field Trips on Middle School Science Achievement. Journal of Research in Science Teaching, vol. 53, issue 7. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/a-day-at-the-museum-the-impact-of-field-trips-on-middle-school-science-achievement

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study using 6 years of student-level data on New York City students, with school fixed effects capitalizing on variation in field trip participation within schools over time. The author reports "small positive effects of exposure to field trips on students' science test scores and proficiency." No effect size is printed in the available text.
