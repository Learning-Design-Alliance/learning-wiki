### Premo 2022

Premo, J., Wyatt, B. N., Horn, M., & Wilson-Ashworth, H. (2022). Which Group Dynamics Matter: Social Predictors of Student Achievement in Team-Based Undergraduate Science Classrooms. CBE—Life Sciences Education, 21(3). https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.21-06-0164

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression on CATME peer ratings from 436 students in team-based GI courses. In biology, "a group member's contributions to the group had the largest impacts on group members' continued willingness to work with a student (Bbiology = 0.59, Bchemistry = 0.48)."

> "Results show that greater personal connection and contributions predicted willingness to work with a group member (R2biology = 0.75; R2chemistry = 0.59)."
