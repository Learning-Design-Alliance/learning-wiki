## Evidence

### Kiguel 2023

Kiguel, S. (2023). Moving the needle on bullying and educational outcomes. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.proquest.com/dissertations-theses/docview/30488487

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Subgroup analysis within the same quasi-experimental study of Chicago Public Schools students, comparing students with a history of victimization (fearing bullying 'most of the time' or 'all the time' in eighth grade) across school percentiles. Graduation rates were "8 percentage points higher at schools at the 85th percentile."

> "Students who were afraid of bullying "all the time" in eighth grade had graduation rates that were 8 percentage points higher at schools at the 85th percentile in bullying reduction vs. schools at the 15th percentile."
