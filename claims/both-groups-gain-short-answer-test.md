## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Paired sample t-tests revealed a statistically significant increase in achievement on the Force and Motion Short-Answer Questions Test for both the experimental group (t=9.623, p=0.001) and the control group (t=3.319, p=0.004). [→ Kocakaya and Gönen 2014](#kocakaya-and-gonen-2014)

## Evidence

### Kocakaya and Gönen 2014

F. Kocakaya, S. Gönen. (2014). Influence of Computer-Assisted Roundhouse Diagrams on High School 9th Grade Students’ Understanding the Subjects of “Force and Motion”. Science Education International, 25(3), 283-311. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1044458

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Paired sample t-tests on the short-answer test: experimental group rose from 5.83 to 30.63 (t=9.623, p=0.001, df=29); control group from 7.35 to 12.18 (t=3.319, p=0.004, df=16). No effect size printed.
