### Ben Van Dusen and Jayson M. Nissen 2018

Ben Van Dusen and Jayson M. Nissen. (2018). Systemic inequities in introductory physics courses: the impacts of learning assistants. Originally published 2017. [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.095](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.095)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Finding from the HLM analysis of 4,365 students in 93 courses (56 LA-supported), with posttest as outcome controlling for pretest. Table II prints the LA-Supported fixed effect γ01 = 5.50, p = 0.003. The article reports higher scores "in LA-supported courses than traditional courses across all demographics"; no standardized effect size is printed for this overall difference.

> "Model 3 shows student posttest scores were higher in LA-supported courses than traditional courses across all demographics."
