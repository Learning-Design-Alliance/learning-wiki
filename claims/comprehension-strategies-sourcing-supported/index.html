Evidence¶

Anmarkrud 2022¶

Anmarkrud, Ø., Bråten, I., Florit, E., & Mason, L. (2022). The Role of Individual Differences in Sourcing: a Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09640-7

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review's synthesis of seven studies of strategic competence and sourcing: "Five of these studies... found statistically significant relationships between the use of comprehension strategies and sourcing, whereas two did not". Four of the five positive studies measured complex multiple text strategies in university samples.

"Five of these studies (e.g., Anmarkrud et al., 2014; List, Du, et al., 2019, studies 1 and 2) found statistically significant relationships between the use of comprehension strategies and sourcing, whereas two did not (Florit et al., 2019; Strømsø et al., 2020)."
