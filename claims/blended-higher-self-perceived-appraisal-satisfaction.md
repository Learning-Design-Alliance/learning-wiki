## Subclaims
`q2 i?` BL students reported significantly higher self-perceived competence in critical appraisal of harm, diagnosis and prognosis studies (p = 0.04, 0.01, 0.01) and were significantly more satisfied and found the unit more intellectually stimulating (p = 0.03, 0.01). ## Evidence

### Ilic Dragan 2013

Ilic Dragan, Hart William, Fiddes Patrick, Misso Marie, Villanueva Elmer. (2013). Adopting a blended learning approach to teaching evidence based medicine: a mixed methods study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-169

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire on a five-point Likert scale in the controlled trial (BL n = 27, DID n = 34). Printed means include appraisal of diagnosis 3.82 versus 3.34 (p = 0.01), stimulation 3.72 versus 3.03 (p = 0.01), satisfaction 3.69 versus 3.18 (p = 0.03). No effect sizes printed.

> "the BL group had a significantly higher self-perceived level of competence when completing step 3 of the EBM process (critical appraisal of studies). Students allocated to the BL group were significantly more satisfied with the delivery of the EBM course, finding it to be more stimulating and applicable to their clinical studies"
