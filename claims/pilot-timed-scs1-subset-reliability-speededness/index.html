Pilot evaluation with 10 students from the same recruitment pool, run with the same structure as the main experiment to set time limits and test the platform. The quote shows "Cronbach's alpha = 0.83, Feldt95%CI =[ 0.62, 0.95]"; the article adds that 9 of 10 participants completed all 12 items and 96% of questions were answered, indicating minimal speededness.
