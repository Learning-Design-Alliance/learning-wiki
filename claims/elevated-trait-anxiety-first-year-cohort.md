### Favarato Maria Helena 2026

Favarato Maria Helena, Peres Isabela Chagas, de Fátima Lopes Calvo Tibério Iolanda. (2026). Is simulation always a safe learning environment? Anxiety and social anxiety symptoms in first-year medical students during simulated patient encounters. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09827-y

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Trait anxiety measured weeks before the first simulation with the STAI-T among 57 participants; the mean was "49.03 ± 12.05", with 13 students (22.8%) in the high category and Cronbach's alpha of 0.93.

> "The mean STAI of trait anxiety (T-anxiety) was 49.03 ± 12.05. Based on established cutoffs, 18 students were classified as having low trait anxiety (31.57%); 26 students were classified as having moderate trait anxiety (45.61%); 13 students were classified as having high trait anxiety (22.8%)."
