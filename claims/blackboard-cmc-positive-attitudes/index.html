Evidence¶

Alharbi 2015¶

Alharbi, M. (2015). Effects of Blackboard’s Discussion Boards, Blogs and Wikis on Effective Integration and Development of Literacy Skills in EFL Students. English Language Teaching, 8(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n6p111

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Part A of the attitude scale (administered only to the experimental group, n = 30) showed agreement that the tools fostered peer interaction, helped with sentence/paragraph structure, and supported revision and proofreading of blog and wiki content.

"These responses indicate that the participants in the experimental group have an overall positive attitude towards the usefulness of the interactive e-course tools for literacy development."

Alharbi 2015 (2)¶

Alharbi, M. (2015). Effects of Blackboard’s Discussion Boards, Blogs and Wikis on Effective Integration and Development of Literacy Skills in EFL Students. English Language Teaching, 8(6). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v8n6p111

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Part B of the attitude scale showed participants enjoyed the tools, felt comfortable responding to threads and publishing blogs/wikis, and agreed the tools motivated interactive reading and writing (e.g., 56.67% SA + 43.33% A on motivation).

"These results indicate that the participants’ attitudes toward integrated reading and writing in the e-learning environment using discussion boards, blogs and wikis, f enjoy these e-course tools, and feel comfort with using them."
