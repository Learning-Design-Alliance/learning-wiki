## Evidence

### Chiu Po-Wei 2025

Chiu Po-Wei, Chu Shao-Chung, Yang Chia-Han, Lee Huan-Fang, Hung Hsuan-Man, Hsu Hsiang-Chin. (2025). Peer-assisted learning in critical care: a simulation-based approach for postgraduate medical training. Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2497333

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same-day Ottawa Crisis Resource Management Global Rating Scale self-assessment by all 29 trainees, reported descriptively. The scale evaluates broader crisis resource management competencies including problem-solving and decision-making; no inferential statistics reported.

> "Situational awareness was rated with a mean score of 4.62, overall performance at 4.60, and problem-solving at 5.00. Communication performance, however, was rated higher, with a mean score of 5.17, indicating that trainees excelled in interpersonal interaction during simulations."
