Descriptive survey analysis of device types by specialty. Box trainers (n = 70 overall) were most common in general/visceral surgery (n = 42); "Gelenksimulatoren (n = 9) kommen fast ausschließlich in der Orthopädie/Unfallchirurgie zum Einsatz"; VR simulators were mostly in general/visceral surgery (n = 15).
