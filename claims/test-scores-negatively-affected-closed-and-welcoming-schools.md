# Test scores of students in both closed and welcoming schools were negatively affected, with closed-school students showing the largest negative effect

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r?` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Closed-school students' test scores were lower than expected in the announcement year: roughly 1.5 months lower than expected in reading and 2 months lower than expected in math; reading recovered after one year but the math effect persisted for four years. [→ Molly F. Gordon 2017](#molly-f-gordon-2017)
`q3 i?` Welcoming-school students showed reading scores about 1.5 months lower than expected the first year after the merger, a short-term effect that rebounded the following year (2014-15). [→ Molly F. Gordon 2017 (2)](#molly-f-gordon-2017-2)

## Evidence

### Molly F. Gordon 2017

Molly F. Gordon, Marisa de la Torre, Jennifer R. Cowhy, Paul T. Moore, Lauren Sartain, and David Knight. (2017). School Closings in Chicago: Staff and Student Experiences and Academic Outcomes. UChicago Consortium Research Report. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-closings-chicago-staff-and-student-experiences-and-academic-outcomes

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Quantitative comparison of closed-school students' test scores to expected levels based on prior outcomes and similar-school students. Reading scores recovered after one year; the negative math effect persisted four years, through the last year of data.

> "The test scores of students in closed schools were lower than expected in the year of the announcement: roughly 1.5 months lower than expected in reading and 2 months lower than expected in math."

### Molly F. Gordon 2017 (2)

Molly F. Gordon, Marisa de la Torre, Jennifer R. Cowhy, Paul T. Moore, Lauren Sartain, and David Knight. (2017). School Closings in Chicago: Staff and Student Experiences and Academic Outcomes. UChicago Consortium Research Report. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-closings-chicago-staff-and-student-experiences-and-academic-outcomes

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Quantitative analysis of welcoming-school students' reading outcomes the year after mergers, benchmarked against prior outcomes and similar schools. The roughly 1.5-month reading deficit was short-term, rebounding in 2014-15.

> "Students from welcoming schools showed lower reading test scores, about 1.5 months lower than expected, the first year after the merger. This was a short-term effect, as reading scores rebounded the following year (2014–15)."
