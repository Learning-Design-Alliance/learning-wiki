## Evidence

### Berkeley J. Dietvorst 2014

Berkeley J. Dietvorst, Joseph P. Simmons, and Cade Massey. (2014). Algorithm Aversion: People Erroneously Avoid Algorithms After Seeing Them Err. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000033

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Five lab and MTurk experiments in which participants forecast MBA success or state airline-passenger ranks and chose between betting on a statistical model or a human forecaster after seeing either, both, or neither perform. The article reports the effect was "highly significant, in all four studies in which we manipulated experience with the model."

> "In every experiment, participants in the model-and-human condition were significantly less likely to tie their bonuses to the model than were participants who did not see the model perform."
