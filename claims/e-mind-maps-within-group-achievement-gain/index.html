Afaf M. Aljaser 2017¶

Afaf M. Aljaser. (2017). The Effectiveness of Electronic Mind Maps in Developing Academic Achievement and the Attitude towards Learning English among Primary School Students. International Education Studies, Vol. 10, No. 12. https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n12p80

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-group Wilcoxon test on the 15-student experimental group's achievement ranks before and after the e-Mind Maps intervention. The group "scored 30.97 at the post-test" versus 23.8 at pre-test, significant at 0.001.

"While they scored 30.97 at the post-test, they scored 23.8 in the pre-test. Additionally, 0.001 significance level was significant at ≤ 0.05, indicating that there were statistically significant differences at the level of ≤ 0.05 among the means of the experimental group on the scale of achievement test before and after e-Mind Maps strategy in favor of the post-test."
