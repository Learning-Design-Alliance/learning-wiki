### Joy He-Yueya 2024

Joy He-Yueya, Noah D. Goodman, Emma Brunskill. (2024). Evaluating and Optimizing Educational Content with Large Language Model Judgments. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2403.02795](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.02795)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Simulated expert evaluations with gpt-3.5-turbo-16k over 120 student personas, replicating Paas and Van Merrienboer's geometrical problem-solving experiment in a systems-of-equations math word problem domain; post-test scores plotted in Figure 5. No effect sizes are printed.

> "High variability in problems significantly enhances performance in worked-example conditions, whereas it has no effect in practice conditions."
