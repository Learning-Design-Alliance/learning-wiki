## Evidence

### Möller 2009

Möller, J., Pohlmann, B., Köller, O., & Marsh, H. W. (2009). A Meta-Analytic Path Analysis of the Internal/External Frame of Reference Model of Academic Achievement and Academic Self-Concept. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654309337522

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Random-effects meta-analysis of all k = 69 independent samples with N = 125,308 participants, weighting Fisher's z by inverse sampling variance. The meta-analysis found "the average correlatio achievements was positive (r = .67)" and r = .10 for the two self-concepts, supporting Predictions 1 and 2.

> "As expected, the average correlatio achievements was positive (r = .67), and this was much higher than tha the two self-concepts (r = .10)."
