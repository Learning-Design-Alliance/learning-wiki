# Theoretical post-test scores rose from pre-test to post-test among undergraduate nursing students after an online basic life support course, in one uncontrolled pre-post study

## Subclaims

`q3 i?` Mean pre-test scores of 6.4 rose to 9.3 on the post-test after the online course (p <0.001, N = 62). [→ Tobase Lucia 2017](#tobase-lucia-2017)

`q2 i?` After completing the online course, students' mean post-test score (9.3 ± 0.82) was significantly higher than their mean pre-test score (6.4 ± 1.61), p<0.001. [→ Tobase L 2017](#tobase-l-2017)

## Evidence

### Tobase Lucia 2017

Tobase Lucia, Peres Heloisa Helena Ciqueto, Tomazini Edenir Aparecida Sartorelli, Teodoro Simone Valentim, Ramos Meire Bruna, Polastri Thatiane Facholi. (2017). Basic life support: evaluation of learning using simulation and immediate feedback devices. Revista Latino-Americana de Enfermagem. https://doi.org/10.1590/1518-8345.1957.2942

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental before-and-after study of 62 undergraduate nursing students completing a 20-hour online BLS course. The paired t-test showed "a significant difference" between pre- and post-test means; only the test statistic and means are printed, with no effect size.

> "The mean in the pre-test was 6.4 (SD1.6) and, in the post-test, 9.3 (SD 0.82, p <0.001, N = 62)."

### Tobase L 2017

Tobase L, Peres HHC, Gianotto-Oliveira R, Smith N, Polastri TF, Timerman S. (2017). The effects of an online basic life support course on undergraduate nursing students' learning. Int J Med Educ 8:309-313. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5985.cbce

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental before-after study of 62 nursing students who completed a 20 h online BLS course; learning was measured with a 20-question pre- and post-test. The paired t-test "showed notable improvements and significant differences"; no effect size was printed.

> "The paired t-test was used to compare the mean scores of the pre-test (6.4 ± 1.61) and post-test (9.3 ± 0.82, p<0.001), which showed notable improvements and significant differences."
