## Evidence

### Dempsey 2026

Dempsey, C., & McCoy, S. (2026). The role of academic self‐concepts, parent expectations and teacher–student interactions in socio‐economic gaps in educational attainment. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96, 422–442. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70025

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal change score model (Model 1) on the Growing Up in Ireland cohort comparing economically vulnerable (EV) and non-EV families; multi-group Satorra–Bentler test showed constrained paths fit worse, Δχ2(1) = 29.939, p = .0001. The "steeper decrease in self‐concept" for EV children was significant.

> "EV children also report a steeper decrease in self‐concept as indicated by the mean change score, Est. = −.662, SE = .109, p < .001, compared with non‐EV children, Est. = −.196, SE = .054, p < .001."
