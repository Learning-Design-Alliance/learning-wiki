### Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer 2015

Dimitri R. Dounas-Frazer, Kevin L. Van De Bogart, MacKenzie R. Stetzer, and H. J. Lewandowski. (2015). The role of modeling in troubleshooting: an example from electronics. [doi:10.1119/perc.2015.pr.021](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2015.pr.021)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Qualitative Interaction Analysis of think-aloud interviews in which 4 pairs of physics majors from each of two institutions (16 students total) diagnosed and repaired a faulty inverting cascade amplifier. The summary states the study demonstrated "model-based reasoning throughout the troubleshooting process."

> "We applied the EMF to an activity in which a pair of students troubleshot a malfunctioning electronic circuit, demonstrating that students engage in model-based reasoning throughout the troubleshooting process."
