## Evidence

### Kim et al. 2019

James S. Kim, Catherine A. Asher, Mary Burkhauser, Laura Mesite, & Diana Leyva. (2019). Using a Sequential Multiple Assignment Randomized Trial (SMART) to Develop an Adaptive K–2 Literacy Intervention With Personalized Print Texts and App-Based Digital Activities. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419872701

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Intent-to-treat comparison in a SMART randomized trial of 273 K–2 children, with multilevel models nesting students in classrooms and covariate adjustment for pretest and demographics. The article reports "no statistically significant differences" on MAP, the informational text subtest, and DIBELS; equivalence was not tested.

> "Third, we find no statistically significant differences on the MAP reading comprehension test overall or the informational text comprehension subtest or the DIBELS reading outcomes."
