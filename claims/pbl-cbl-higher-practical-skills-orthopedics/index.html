Evidence¶

Xiong Xianghua 2025¶

Xiong Xianghua, Xu Jiongnan, Luo Mingyan, Niu Dongsheng, Bi Qing, Wang Zhaofu, Zhang Jun. (2025). Efficacy of problem-based learning combined with case-based learning versus lecture-based learning in orthopedic education: a systematic review and meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07741-3

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 12 studies (760 students) on practical skills scores. Pooled estimate favored PBL-CBL: "SMD = 1.53, 95% CI: 1.07-2.00, P < 0.0001" with high heterogeneity (I2 = 87%).

"The PBL-CBL group was found to perform significantly higher than the LBL group in practical skills scores (SMD = 1.53, 95% CI: 1.07-2.00, P < 0.0001, I2 = 87%, Fig. 8)"
