## Evidence

### Mengjia Liu 2026

Mengjia Liu, Harwati Hashim, Nur Ainil Sulaiman. (2026). A systematic review of emerging technology applications for teaching English as a foreign language across different educational levels. Educational Research Review 52 (2026) 100795. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100795

`q3 · i1` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Subgroup analysis by educational level within the random-effects meta-analysis (Table 4). The review states "The overlapping confidence intervals indicate these between-level differences did not reach statistical significance" and attributes the larger tertiary effect to greater learner autonomy, more complex tasks, and stronger infrastructure.

> "Effect sizes showed a trend toward increasing effectiveness at higher levels: primary (k = 5; g = 0.29, 95% CI [0.05, 0.53]; I2 = 72.3%), secondary (k = 11; g = 0.35, 95% CI [0.17, 0.53]; I2 = 85.6%), and tertiary (k = 17; g = 0.44, 95% CI [0.26, 0.62]; I 2 = 92.7%)."
