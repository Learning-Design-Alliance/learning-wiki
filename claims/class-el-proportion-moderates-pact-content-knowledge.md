## Subclaims
`q3 i?` A significant three-way interaction (Intervention × EL × Class % of ELs) on the ASK shows the treatment effect depended on class EL proportion. [→ Vaughn 2017](#vaughn-2017)

## Evidence

### Vaughn 2017

Vaughn, S., Martinez, L. R., Wanzek, J., Roberts, G., Swanson, E., & Fall, A.-M. (2017). Improving Content Knowledge and Comprehension for English Language Learners: Findings From a Randomized Control Trial. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(1), 22–34. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000069

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Three-level regression on ASK posttest scores in the class-randomized RCT; the three-way interaction coefficient was −.08, p < .01. No effect size is printed for the interaction, so impact is null.

> "The significant three-way interaction ( /H9253500 /H11005/H11002.08, p /H11021.01) means that the regression coefficient for the interaction of Intervention and EL, /H9253300 /H110052.35, is conditional on values of Class % of ELs ."

### Vaughn 2017 (2)

Vaughn, S., Martinez, L. R., Wanzek, J., Roberts, G., Swanson, E., & Fall, A.-M. (2017). Improving Content Knowledge and Comprehension for English Language Learners: Findings From a Randomized Control Trial. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(1), 22–34. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000069
