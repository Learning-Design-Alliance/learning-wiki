`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Score comparison in the same cohort-based quasi-experiment: the VSO group's median practical operation score was 92.00 versus 87.00 for the video group, with the article reporting an average score difference of 6.19 (4.99–7.40, Z = −8.47/−8.71 as printed) and P < 0.001.
