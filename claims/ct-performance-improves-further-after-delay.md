# Performance on practiced reasoning items and on transfer items continued to improve after 3-week and 9-month delays

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study (2 entries) · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Learning-item performance improved from the 3-week delayed posttest (M = 53.30) to the 9-month delayed posttest (M = 63.00). [→ Lara M. van Peppen 2021](#lara-m-van-peppen-2021)
`q3 i?` Transfer-item performance was lower on the 3-week delayed test (M = 19.25) than the 9-month delayed test (M = 24.84). [→ Lara M. van Peppen 2021 (2)](#lara-m-van-peppen-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Lara M. van Peppen 2021

Lara M. van Peppen, Peter P. J. L. Verkoeijen, Anita E. G. Heijltjes, Eva M. Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2021). Enhancing students' critical thinking skills: is comparing correct and erroneous examples beneficial? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09559-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory 2x4 mixed ANOVA on learning items for the Safety and Security Management subsample (N = 82) showed a main effect of Test Moment, F(1,78) = 21.36, p < .001, ηp² = .22, with no Practice Type effect or interaction.

> "participants' performance improved from 3-week delayed posttest (M = 53.30, SE = 2.69) to 9-month delayed posttest (M = 63.00, SE = 2.24)"

### Lara M. van Peppen 2021 (2)

Lara M. van Peppen, Peter P. J. L. Verkoeijen, Anita E. G. Heijltjes, Eva M. Janssen, Tamara van Gog. (2021). Enhancing students' critical thinking skills: is comparing correct and erroneous examples beneficial? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09559-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory 2x4 mixed ANOVA on transfer items (N = 82) showed a main effect of Test Moment, F(1,78) = 9.53, p = .003, ηp² = .11, with no Practice Type effect or interaction.

> "participants performed lower on the 3-week delayed test (M = 19.25, SE = 1.60) than the 9-month delayed test (M = 24.84, SE = 1.67)"
