## Evidence

### Alfadda 2021

Alfadda, Hind Abdulaziz, & Mahdi, Hassan Saleh. (2021). Measuring Students’ Use of Zoom Application in Language Course Based on the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM). Journal of Psycholinguistic Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10936-020-09752-1

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Correlation analysis of the 75-student survey data testing Hypotheses 3 and 4 in the Results and Discussion section. The printed Pearson correlations are r = .44 for PEU with perceived usefulness, r = .45 with attitude, and r = .60 with behavioral intention: "a substantial positive relationship between PEU and Behavioral Intention (.60)".
