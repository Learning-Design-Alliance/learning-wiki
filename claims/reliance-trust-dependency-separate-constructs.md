## Evidence

### Daniele Verí 2026

Daniele Verí. (2026). Beyond AI Literacy: A Structured Review and Exploratory Meta-Analysis of Measures for Competent Generative-AI Use. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15624

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Scale-validation evidence as reported in the review: exploratory factor analysis used 800 responses, with confirmatory analyses on holdout samples of 730 and 1,173 responses from a second problem-solving activity. The review notes the scale sits closer to competent collaboration than a generic trust score.

> "The four factors were reflective, cautious, thoughtless, and collaborative use; overall internal consistency wasα=.84[Hou et al., 2025]."

### Daniele Verí 2026 (2)

Daniele Verí. (2026). Beyond AI Literacy: A Structured Review and Exploratory Meta-Analysis of Measures for Competent Generative-AI Use. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15624

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Review's synthesis of the Generative AI Dependency Scale (Goh et al., 2025), developed across six studies with 1,333 participants, showing a three-factor structure of cognitive preoccupation, negative consequences, and withdrawal with α = .92 to .93 and test-retest ICC = .87.

> "Dependency was associated with lower task performance and critical thinking. It can serve as an outcome or risk indicator and should remain separate from an AI-literacy total."
