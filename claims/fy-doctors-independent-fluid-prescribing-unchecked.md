## Evidence

### Johnson D 2026

Johnson D, Houdmont J, Levy N, Lobo DN. (2026). Investigating the fluid and electrolyte prescribing knowledge of Foundation Year doctors. Ann R Coll Surg Engl. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2025.0004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Self-report survey items (Tables 3 and 4) on which grades normally prescribe fluids for uncomplicated and complicated patients; FY1s plus shared junior groups dominate both tables.

> "Respondents reported that doctors in training (defined herein as any doctor who is not a consultant) were responsible for the vast majority of fluid and electrolyte prescriptions"

### Johnson D 2026 (2)

Johnson D, Houdmont J, Levy N, Lobo DN. (2026). Investigating the fluid and electrolyte prescribing knowledge of Foundation Year doctors. Ann R Coll Surg Engl. https://doi.org/10.1308/rcsann.2025.0004

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey items on supervision practices on the ward: most respondents reported FY1 prescriptions were not routinely checked and that balance charts were not reviewed on the ward round.

> "the majority (103, 54%) responded ‘no’, 17 (9%) responded ‘yes’ and the remainder were ‘unsure’. The majority (103, 54%) reported that patients' fluid balance charts were not checked during the daily morning ward round"
