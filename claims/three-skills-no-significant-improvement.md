## Evidence

### Drost-de Klerck Amanda M 2020

Drost-de Klerck Amanda M, Olgers Tycho J, van de Meeberg Evelien K, Schonrock-Adema Johanna, ter Maaten Jan C. (2020). Use of simulation training to teach the ABCDE primary assessment: an observational study in a Dutch University Hospital with a 3–4 months follow-up. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2019-032023

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Among the Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons of 40 separate skills from pre-course (T1) to post-course (T2), mean ranks were "almost all significantly higher at T2 than at T1 (34 out of 40)"; these three skills were the exceptions. Four further skills could not be analysed due to too many 'does not apply' scores.

> "For only three skills—'examines the airway', 'orders additional diagnostics in the B' and 'resuscitates adequately in the E'—we did not find a significant difference between T1 and T2."
