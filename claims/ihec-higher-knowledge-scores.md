## Evidence

### Xie Dili 2026

Xie Dili, Sun Xuechun, Guo Jianshu, Liu Peng, Yang Junling, Zhong Ping. (2026). Integrating iHEC into standardized residency training for chronic disease management: a structural equation analysis of competency outcomes via digital health platforms. Frontiers in Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1926948

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA comparison of post-rotation knowledge scores between cohorts, adjusted for baseline score, specialty, and calendar time, showing an "Adjusted mean difference, 5.42 points" with d = 0.71; all differences remained significant after Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction.

> "Knowledge score: Adjusted mean difference, 5.42 points (95% CI, 3.84–7.00; p < 0.001; Cohen’s d = 0.71), indicating a moderate-to-large effect size."
