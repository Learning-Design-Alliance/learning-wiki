## Evidence

### Yu Tana 2025

Yu Tana, Yang Yanwei, Zeng Qing, Xu Zihang, Li Caiyue, Zhang Hongchen. (2025). Relationship between academic self-concept and psychological truancy among medical college students in China: the chain mediating role of academic motivation and school climate. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07650-5

`q2 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Bootstrap test of the chain pathway ASC→SC→AM→PT in the SEM of 628 medical students. The indirect effect was significant with "β = -0.019, 95% CI = -0.026 to -0.011", the smallest of the three indirect paths at 8.15% of the total effect.

> "academic self-concept can decrease psychological truancy through the chain mediation of school climate and academic motivation (β = -0.019, 95% CI = -0.026 to -0.011, p<0.05)"
