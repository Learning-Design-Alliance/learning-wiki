## Evidence

### Karakoç Hediye 2026

Karakoç Hediye, Ozkan Hava. (2026). The effect of wearable simulated birth model on students’ empathy and privacy protection levels: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26, 383. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08441-8

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Robust mixed ANOVA of the awareness section of the Privacy Protection Scale in Obstetrics and Gynecology across four time points; the time effect was significant (F = 8.056, p < 0.001, partial eta squared 0.237 per Table 4), while the group effect (p = 0.455) and group × time interaction (p = 0.263) were not.

> "Regardless of group, the trimmed mean awareness scores differed over time (p < 0.001). A difference was observed between the post-simulation and pre-counseling follow-ups."
