## Evidence

### Robertson Eleanor 2015

Robertson Eleanor, Morgan Lauren, New Steve, Pickering Sharon, Hadi Mohammed, Collins Gary, Rivero Arias Oliver, Griffin Damian, McCulloch Peter. (2015). Quality Improvement in Surgery Combining Lean Improvement Methods with Teamwork Training: A Controlled Before-After Study. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0138490

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Observational process measure of whether an attempt was made to complete the WHO sign-out, recorded for each of 96 observed operations. The article reports "a significant difference in the attempt rate of S/O between pre (0/21; 0%) and post (12/24; 50%) in the active arm", while the control arm changed from 4% to 12% (p = 0.574).

> "There was a significant difference in the attempt rate of S/O between pre (0/21; 0%) and post (12/24; 50%) in the active arm (difference = 50%; 95% CI 26% to 75%; p<0.001)"
