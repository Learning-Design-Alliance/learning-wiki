## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Fei Qin 2025

Fei Qin, Zhanxin Hao, Jifan Yu, Zhiyuan Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). AI instructional agent improves student's perceived learner control and learning outcome: Empirical evidence from a randomized controlled trial. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Bivariate correlation analyses in the RCT sample (n = 125) between post-test scores and key variables, reported with scatterplots in Figure 3. Behavioral engagement's r = 0.34 is a medium correlation; the cognitive and emotional engagement correlations were not significant.

> "Post-test scores were significantly correlated with PLC (r = 0.23, p <
.01), learning duration (r = –0.28, p < .01), feedback frequency (r = 0.18, p < .05), and behavioral engagement (r = 0.34,
p < .001), suggesting links between subjective perception, behavioral regulation, and learning outcomes. However, the
posttest scores show no significant correlation with cognitive and emotional engagement."

### Fei Qin 2025 (2)

Fei Qin, Zhanxin Hao, Jifan Yu, Zhiyuan Liu, Yu Zhang. (2025). AI instructional agent improves student's perceived learner control and learning outcome: Empirical evidence from a randomized controlled trial. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.22526)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Further bivariate correlations in the same sample linked PLC to learning duration and pre-existing self-regulated learning; the authors read the negative duration correlation as more purposeful time management among students who felt more in control.

> "Further analyses revealed that PLC was negatively correlated with learning duration (r = –0.30, p < .001), and positively
correlated with self-regulated learning (r = 0.23, p < .05), while no significant correlation was found with feedback
frequency."
