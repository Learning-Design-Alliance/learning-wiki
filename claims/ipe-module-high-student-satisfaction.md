## Evidence

### Herberg L 2026

Herberg L, Körner F, Junne F, Geue K. (2026). Evaluation of a learning module on team competencies in medicine utilizing simulation-based scenarios. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09274-9

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Cross-sectional paper-pencil survey of 192 completed questionnaires (97.46% response rate) administered to third-year medical students after the module. "Overall, the participants were quite satisfied with the seminar (M = 1.95, SD = 0.95)"; only 7% (N = 13) were dissatisfied.

> "Overall, the participants were quite satisfied with the seminar (M = 1.95, SD = 0.95). Only 7% (N = 13) stated that they were rather dissatisfied or dissatisfied with the teaching unit."
