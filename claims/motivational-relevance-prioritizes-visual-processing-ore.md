### Collins 2014

Collins, Jessica A., & Olson, Ingrid R. (2014). Knowledge is Power: How Conceptual Knowledge Transforms Visual Cognition. Psychonomic bulletin & review. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-013-0564-3

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports behavioral and electrophysiological findings that motivational relevance and perceiver goals modulate face encoding: relative to same-race faces, the N170 is attenuated for other-race faces when participants attend to race and enhanced when attending to identity. No effect sizes printed.
