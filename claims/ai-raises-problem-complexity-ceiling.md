# AI raises the ceiling on problem complexity by reducing access complexity rather than cognitive demand

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` AI shifts what category of problem PBL can engage with by lowering access barriers, expanding access to complex, wicked problems previously beyond students' reach. [→ Michael Rowe 2026](#michael-rowe-2026)

## Evidence

### Michael Rowe 2026

Michael Rowe. (2026). Problem-based learning and the structural conditions for productive AI integration. https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/haet3_v1

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Conceptual argument (§4): AI lowers the access barriers that kept complex problems out of reach while leaving judgement-layer difficulty intact. The essay distinguishes content complexity, which still needs calibrating, from "access complexity: the logistical and technical barriers to reaching material".

> "AI does not simply fit within existing PBL practice. It shifts what category of problem PBL can engage with, and in doing so changes the curriculum question from what problems are manageable to what problems become possible."
