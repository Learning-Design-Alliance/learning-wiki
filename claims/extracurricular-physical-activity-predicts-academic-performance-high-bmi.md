## Evidence

### Zhang Bao Gen 2026

Zhang Bao Gen, Qian Xiao Fang. (2026). Extracurricular physical activity and academic performance among Chinese adolescents with high body mass index: a longitudinal mediation analysis. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-47332-w

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal path analysis (PROCESS Model 6, 5,000 bootstrap samples) of 221 adolescents with high BMI from the China Education Tracking Survey, controlling for baseline academic performance, age, sex, parental education, and school type. The direct effect was significant, "β = 0.1299; 95% CI [0.0456, 0.2143, excluding 0]".

> "Among children and adolescents with high BMI, extracurricular physical activity at T1 had a statistically significant direct effect on academic performance at T2 (β = 0.1299; 95% CI [0.0456, 0.2143, excluding 0])."
