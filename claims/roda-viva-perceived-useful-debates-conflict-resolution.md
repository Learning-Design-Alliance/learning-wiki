## Evidence

### Maya Jesús; Maraver Jesús 2020

Maya Jesús; Maraver Jesús. (2020). Teaching-Learning Processes: Application of Educational Psychodrama in the University Setting. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17113922

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

End-of-course focus groups across the nine TTS-EP courses identified two educational applications of roda viva: debates fostering reasoning and interpersonal intelligence, and resolution of small-group conflicts. One teacher said it resolves conflicts "without the teacher being responsible for the solution."

> "According to the teachers, the roda viva can promote consensual solutions among students without the direct participation of the teacher."
