Observational analysis of degree-earning rates (Fig. 6) for ten cohorts of institutional records. The article reports 75% of female versus 63% of male physics majors earned physics degrees, but states the large error driven by low sample size makes conclusions difficult.

> "there are fairly wide differences across majors, from the lowest rate in physics of about 65% to the highest in psychology and non-STEM, each at about 94%."
