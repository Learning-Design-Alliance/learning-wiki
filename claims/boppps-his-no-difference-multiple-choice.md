### Yang Ziqing 2025

Yang Ziqing, Zhen Siyu, Pan Ben, Wei Hanyu, Li Qiang, Yue Junyan, Hu Ying, Liang Changhua. (2025). Investigating the use of the HIS-based BOPPPS teaching model in medical imaging experimental course instruction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07607-8

End-of-term exam analysis in the crossover study of 117 interns; Table 4 shows theoretical score comparisons with p = 0.227 (first semester) and p = 0.445 (second semester), Cohen's d = -0.22 and 0.14. Equivalence was not tested.

> "However, no statistically significant differences were identified in the comparison of multiple-choice question scores or theoretical scores between the groups."
