Review discussion attributing findings to included studies: Maloney et al. found readers of the 2020 IADT guidelines had significantly higher knowledge than non-readers, and Matoug-Elwerfelli et al. associated guideline awareness with greater confidence. No effect sizes printed.

"According to Maloney et al. (2024), GDPs who had read the 2020 IADT guidelines had significantly higher knowledge of TDI management than those who had not. Awareness of most recent IADT guidelines has also been associated with greater confidence in management of dental trauma cases amongst GDPs (Matoug-Elwerfelli et al. 2022)."
