## Evidence

### Abegglen S 2020

Abegglen S, Krieg A, Eigenmann H, Greif R. (2020). Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD) in simulation-based medical education: Translation and validation of the German version. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244816

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Reliability analyses across the 57 rated debriefings reported in the abstract and Table 3. The article reports "Cronbach's α = 0.865" and "ω = 0.93", comparable to the original OSAD's α of 0.89, indicating the categories are interrelated. No effect size printed.

> "Interrater reliability (intraclass correlation coefficient) was excellent for the average ratings (three raters: ICC = 0.848; two raters: ICC = 0.790), and good for the single rater (ICC = 0.650). Test-retest reliability was excellent (ICC = 0.976), internal consistency was acceptable (Cronbach's α = 0.865), and composite reliability was excellent (ω = 0.93)."
