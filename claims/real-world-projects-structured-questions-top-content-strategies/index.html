Evidence¶

Martin 2018¶

Martin, F., & Bolliger, D. U. (2018). Engagement Matters: Student Perceptions on the Importance of Engagement Strategies in the Online Learning Environment. Online Learning, 22(1). https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v22i1.1092

q2 · i? · associational · r?

In the survey of 155 online students, the top student-content strategies were that "students mentioned working on real world projects and having discussions with structured or guiding questions were the most beneficial." No effect size is printed in the available text.
