# The DARPA Digital Tutor, an ITS for U.S. Navy information systems technicians, produced the largest effects in the data set (average ES of 1.97 and 3.18 in two summative evaluations)

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Kulik 2016

Kulik, J. A., & Fletcher, J. D. (2016). Effectiveness of Intelligent Tutoring Systems: A Meta-Analytic Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315581420

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Two summative field evaluations compared end-of-course test scores from Digital Tutor courses (8 and 16 weeks) with longer standard classroom courses, measured on locally developed third-party written and oral tests. The printed effect sizes were ES = 1.97 and ES = 3.18: "Average ES in the first evaluation was 1.97"; both were outliers in the data set.

> "Average ES in the first evaluation was 1.97 (Fletcher, 2011); average ES in the second evaluation was 3.18 (Fletcher & Morrison, 2012)."
