Evidence¶

Timur Koparan 2015¶

Timur Koparan, Gül Kaleli Yılmaz. (2015). The Effect of Simulation-based Learning on Prospective Teachers’ Inference Skills in Teaching Probability. Universal Journal of Educational Research 3(11). https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2015.031101

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' qualitative finding on the fourth and fifth questions, where pre-test answers included probabilities not summing to 1 or 100 percent and beliefs that all event probabilities were equal. Simulation trials with TinkerPlots models let teachers compare results with their predictions.

"It was observed that simulations helped the prospective teachers to become aware of the wrong ideas and change their ideas."
