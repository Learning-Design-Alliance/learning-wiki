`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Dynamic Time Warping with k-means clustering assessed learners' compliance with SRL scaffolds, and differences between compliers and non-compliers were mapped onto the ONA space.

> "The results revealed that learners' compliance varied significantly: compliers demonstrated clear adherence to scaffold recommendations and engaged in more frequent metacognitive processes, particularly during writing phases. In contrast, non-compliers exhibited a more linear, less strategic learning trajectory (𝑊= 596,𝑛= 54,𝑝< 0.000,𝑟= 0.548)."
