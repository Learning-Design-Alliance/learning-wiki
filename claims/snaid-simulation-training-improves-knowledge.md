## Evidence

### Segura-Fornieles 2026

Segura-Fornieles, A. J., Márquez-Hernández, V. V., García-Viola, A., Rodríguez-García, M. C., & Garrido-Molina, J. M. (2026). Improving operational preparedness for armored vehicle casualty rescue: effectiveness of simulation-based training with a novel extrication device. Frontiers in Public Health, 14. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1910210

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental one-group pretest-posttest study of 156 military personnel at a Spanish armored battalion; knowledge measured with a 10-question instrument (Cronbach's alpha = 0.863). The article reports "statistically significant differences" with means rising from 5.66 to 8.82; no effect size is printed.

> "Regarding knowledge, statistically significant differences were found when comparing the pre-test and post-test results (p = 0.004). Specifically, participants had an average score of 5.66 (SD = 1.15) in the pre-test and an average score of 8.82 (SD = 1.17) in the post-test."
