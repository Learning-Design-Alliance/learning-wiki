# Flipped classrooms produce a small significant improvement in student learning over traditional classrooms in health professions education (SMD = 0.33)

## Subclaims
`q4 i1` Across 28 comparative studies, learning outcomes favored flipped classrooms over traditional classrooms with a pooled standardized mean difference of 0.33 (95% CI 0.21–0.46, p < 0.001). [→ HEW Khe Foon; LO Chung Kwan 2018](#hew-khe-foon-lo-chung-kwan-2018)

## Evidence

### HEW Khe Foon; LO Chung Kwan 2018

HEW Khe Foon; LO Chung Kwan. (2018). Flipped classroom improves student learning in health professions education: a meta-analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1144-z

`q4 · i1`

Random-effects meta-analysis of 28 between-subject comparative studies in health professions education, pooling standardized mean differences from exam and post-test data. The pooled effect favored flipped classrooms: "SMD = 0.33, 95% CI 0.21–0.46, p < 0.001".

> "A meta-analysis of 28 eligible comparative studies involving 2295 subjects exposed to flipped classroom and 2420 subjects exposed to traditional classroom showed an overall significant effect in favor of the flipped classroom approach for health professions education (SMD = 0.33, 95% CI 0.21–0.46, p < 0.001)"
