`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multivariate linear regression on the 200-nurse cross-sectional sample. The model explained "R2 = 0.20; Adjusted R 2 = 0.16" of variance; competency was higher for general-hospital nurses, longer employment history, and professional involvement. R² is not treated as an effect size here.

> "Forward regression showed that the type of hospital, work experience, and the involvement of professionals in providing religious/spiritual care were significant predictors of nurses’ competencies in integrating patients’ religion/spirituality into nursing practice (R2 = 0.20; Adjusted R 2 = 0.16)"
