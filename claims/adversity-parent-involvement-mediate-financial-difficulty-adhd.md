# Family adversity and lower parental involvement mediate 27.8% of the association between early financial difficulty and ADHD at age 7

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 2 associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In a multiple mediation model, presence of family adversity at age 2–4 was the largest mediator, with lower mother and partner involvement at age 6 also mediating the link. [→ Russell AE 2015](#russell-ae-2015)

## Evidence

### Russell AE 2015

Russell AE, Ford T, Russell G (2015) Socioeconomic Associations with ADHD: Findings from a Mediation Analysis. PLoS ONE 10(6): e0128248. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0128248

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple mediation analysis (Preacher and Hayes products-of-coefficients, bootstrapped bias-corrected CIs, adjusted for gender) with financial difficulties as predictor and ADHD at 7 as outcome. Adversity indirect effect coefficient 0.03 (BC CI 0.01–0.05); mother involvement 0.003 (0.000–0.009); partner involvement 0.008 (0.002–0.016).

> "Overall, 27.8% of the total effect between financial difficulties at age 0 –2 and ADHD at age 7 was mediated, with the majority of mediation occurring through adversity risk at age 2 –4 (coef-ficient 0.03, bias-corrected (BC) CIs 0.01 –0.05)"

### Russell AE 2015 (2)

Russell AE, Ford T, Russell G (2015) Socioeconomic Associations with ADHD: Findings from a Mediation Analysis. PLoS ONE 10(6): e0128248. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0128248

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The same mediation model's bias-corrected confidence intervals excluded zero for the direct effect, total effect and three mediators; the article states "lower levels of parental involvement" and "presence of family adversity" mediated the link.

> "There was evidence that lower levels of parental involvement, both of the mother and partner and presence of family adversity mediated the link between financial difficulty and ADHD."
