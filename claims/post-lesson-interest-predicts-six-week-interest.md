## Evidence

### Lazarides 2025

Lazarides, R., Frenkel, J., Petković, U., Göllner, R., & Hellwich, O. (2025). 'No words'—Machine‐learning classified nonverbal immediacy and its role in connecting teacher self‐efficacy with perceived teaching and student interest. British Journal of Educational Psychology, 95(Suppl. 1), S15–S31. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12732

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multilevel Model 2 path coefficient at classroom level: β = .47 (p = .002). The authors note that because Time 1 interest was not controlled in this path, interest at Time 3 predicted the level, not the change, in interest from Time 1 to Time 4.

> "Students' interest after the lesson (Time 3) was significantly and positively related to students' interest 6 weeks later (Time 4). Given the modelling procedure used, adjusting for students' previous interest means estimating the additional effect of other predictors for students representing an average interest level in their respective classroom at the adjusted measurement time point."
