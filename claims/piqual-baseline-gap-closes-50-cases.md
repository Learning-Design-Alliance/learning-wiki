`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

PI-QUALv2 image quality learning curves fitted with a cubic polynomial GEE model in the 200-case study. Early learning (cases 0–50) was steeper for radiology trainees, resulting in performance convergence by case 50; final agreement showed no significant difference between specialties (OR 0.82 [0.35, 1.72]).

> "Urology trainees demonstrated higher baseline agreement rates (62.54% [56.52, 65.96]) compared to radiology trainees (45.36% [36.86, 54.20]; OR 2.01 [1.35, 3.02], Table 3)."
