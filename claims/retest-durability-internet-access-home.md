## Evidence

### Magdaş and Bontea 2011

Ioana Magdaş, Timea Bontea. (2011). Developing digital competences using an educational software. A pedagogical research. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 4, Number 4, ISSN 2065-1430. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056072

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Retest administered three weeks after the experimental activities, identical for both groups (EG n=22, CG n=24). The article reports a very slight decrease among students who had achieved good grades, attributed to elapsed time; no test statistic is printed.

> "Both the experimental sample and control is observed to the very slight decrease in the retest students who have achieved good grade. This decrease is due to the time elapsed between the posttest and retest."

### Magdaş and Bontea 2011 (2)

Ioana Magdaş, Timea Bontea. (2011). Developing digital competences using an educational software. A pedagogical research. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 4, Number 4, ISSN 2065-1430. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1056072

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

> "we want to mention that students who have Internet access at home in the experimental class, did not decrease after application of retest ratings."
