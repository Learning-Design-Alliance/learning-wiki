### Barrio Eva 2026

Barrio Eva, Solanas Estela, Alcaine Clara, Emperador Sonia, Ayensa-Jimenez Jacobo, Mena Andrés, Ciriza Jesús. (2026). The impact of interactive digital tools on genetics education: a field study across large and small learning groups. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09168-w

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre–post survey of the same 252 students. Before the session only 17.62% in Zaragoza felt able. The article reports Wilcoxon signed-rank and McNemar–Bowker tests "yielded highly significant results (p < 0.0001)" in Zaragoza and p < 0.001 in Huesca.

> "In Zaragoza, 91.87% of students reported feeling able to identify chromosomal abnormalities, with only 0.96% still identifying as unable. Similarly, in Huesca, 95.24% of students considered themselves able, and no students reported feeling neutral or unable."
