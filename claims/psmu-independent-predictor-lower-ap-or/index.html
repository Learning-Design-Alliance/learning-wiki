Zerrouk Deyae 2026¶

Zerrouk Deyae, Kassab Kenza, El Asri Achraf, Zarrouq Btissame. (2026). The relationship between Problematic Social Media Use and academic performance among university students in the MENA region: a systematic review and exploratory meta-analysis. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-026-04354-3

q2 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r3

Multivariate logistic regression in a cross-sectional study of 277 students across Jordan, UAE, and Saudi Arabia. The adjusted odds ratio of 0.92 is a small effect; the same study's ANOVA showed high-AP students had lower BSMAS scores (15.4 vs 18, p = 0.03).

"Alhusban et al. (2022) reported an OR = 0.92 (95% CI: 0.86–0.98, p = 0.009), indicating that higher levels of PSMU decreased the odds of achieving high AP after adjusting for age, gender, university major, SM app usage, and BSMAS scores"
