# Task difficulty shows a u-shaped relation to regulatory activities, with competing CLT-based and motivational explanations that cannot yet be distinguished

## Subclaims
`q1 i?` Seufert proposes a u-shaped relationship between task difficulty and students' regulatory activities, with medium-difficulty tasks as the sweet spot. [→ Katharina Scheiter 2025](#katharina-scheiter-2025)
`q1 i?` Atkinson's achievement motivation theory offers an alternative motivational account predicting highest effort investment for medium-difficulty tasks. [→ Katharina Scheiter 2025 (2)](#katharina-scheiter-2025-2)

## Evidence

### Katharina Scheiter 2025

Katharina Scheiter. (2025). Commentary: How can We Come to Terms when Discussing the Role of Effort from the Perspective of Cognitive Load Theory and Theories of Self-regulated Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10037-z

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

The commentary reports, citing Seufert, that for medium-difficulty tasks available resources and task demands are optimally balanced, so the task is neither under- nor overwhelming; Seufert et al. (2024) provide corroborating evidence.

> "Seufert (2018, 2020) propose that there is a u-shaped relationship between task difficulty and students’ regulatory activities (see Seufert et al., 2024, for corroborating evidence)."

### Katharina Scheiter 2025 (2)

Katharina Scheiter. (2025). Commentary: How can We Come to Terms when Discussing the Role of Effort from the Perspective of Cognitive Load Theory and Theories of Self-regulated Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10037-z

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument from Atkinson's (1957) theory as presented in the commentary: value depends on expectancy (v = 1 – e) and expectancy × value combine multiplicatively, so motivation — and hence effort — should peak at medium difficulty.

> "the tendency to approach performance-related tasks is highest (i.e., motivation) when both the expectation to reach the goal (expectancy) and the attraction of reaching the goal (value) are at a medium level"
