## Evidence

### Li P 2026

Li P, Taoyu J, Yaxin L, Xiao-li P, Muzi L, Haoying D. (2026). Evaluation of the Bridge-in, Objective, Preassessment, Participatory Learning, Postassessment, and Summary–Based Instructional Model in Disaster Nursing: Quasi-Experimental Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/77728

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U tests comparing final examination submodules between experimental (n=27) and control (n=25) groups. The BOPPPS advantage was selective: significant for single-choice questions but not for true/false or case-based essay formats.

> "the score advantage of the experimental group was mainly concentrated in the single-choice questions section, in which the experimental group scored significantly higher than the control group (42.00, IQR 40.00‐46.00 vs 40.00, IQR 34.00‐42.00; z=2.101, P=.04). By contrast, the true or false and case-based essay sections showed no significant differences (true/false: z=1.235, P=.22; case-based essay: z=0.739, P=.46)."
