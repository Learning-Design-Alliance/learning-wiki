## Evidence

### Zheng Hao 2026

Zheng Hao, Zare Zohreh Yousefian, Li Menghong, Pan Yuzhu, Ren Shuyao, Cui Haoyue, Li Yuexiao. (2026). Evaluation of ChatGPT-4o in oral and maxillofacial surgery examinations: a comparative study of performance on U.S. dental decks and Chinese dental licensing examination practice questions. BMC Oral Health. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08551-9

`q3 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Prospective study testing ChatGPT-4o on all 252 OMS-related questions from the U.S. Dental Decks platform, benchmarked against three human cohorts (n = 10 each). The model's 90% accuracy "significantly outperforming all three human groups" was confirmed by chi-squared tests with a moderate effect size (Cramer's V = 0.399).

> "On the English Dental Decks questions (n = 252), ChatGPT-4o achieved a markedly higher accuracy rate of 90%, significantly outperforming all three human groups (χ² = 160.210, p < 0.001, Table 1), with a moderate effect size (Cramer's V = 0.399)"
