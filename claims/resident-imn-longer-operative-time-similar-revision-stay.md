Resident autonomy in orthopedic trauma: independent femoral IMN by senior residents yields comparable outcomes to fellowship-trained surgeons. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-026-06460-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

In the matched cohort of 150 patients, the study reports "Median operative time was longer for residents, 120 min (IQR 91.2–150.0), than for attendings, 91 min (IQR 60.0–133.8)" with statistical significance at p = 0.003.

> "Median operative time was longer for residents, 120 min (IQR 91.2–150.0), than for attendings, 91 min (IQR 60.0–133.8) (p = 0.003)."

### Balziano Snir 2026 (2)

Resident autonomy in orthopedic trauma: independent femoral IMN by senior residents yields comparable outcomes to fellowship-trained surgeons. Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00402-026-06460-8

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The matched cohort analysis reports "Revisions were required in 15/75 (20.0%) resident-led and 14/75 (18.7%) attending-led cases" with no statistically significant difference. Revision indications were comparable between groups, with elective hardware removal for residual pain being the most common in both.

> "Revisions were required in 15/75 (20.0%) resident-led and 14/75 (18.7%) attending-led cases (p = 0.172)."
