## Evidence

### Lazartigues Laura 2021

Lazartigues Laura, Mathy Fabien, Lavigne Frédéric. (2021). Statistical learning of unbalanced exclusive-or temporal sequences in humans. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0246826

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same touchscreen learning experiment, sequences AEF and DBF had equal frequency (.25) but different first-order TPs (.71 vs .38); the mixed-model interaction on TT1 was significant, which the authors say "shows that the first-order TP itself had an impact on learning and processing of transitions from the first and second stimuli."

> "Analyses in Table 4 showed a significant interaction between block number and first-order TP for these two sequences of equal frequency (F(1,7309) = 128.2, p <.001). RTTT1 were longer for the lowest first-order TP value than for the highest value."
