Post-test analysis of 85 upper-level students showing strong performance on Coulomb's law questions (Q3-Q8) and on electric field-force connection questions (Q9, Q10, Q12, Q15, Q19), where performance was above 80% in all contexts except Q19 (64%).

> "One concept in which upper-level students performed well in all contexts was Coulomb's law (Q3, Q4, Q5, Q6, Q7, and Q8), where the post-test performance is greater than 85% in all questions except for one (Q7), on which the performance was 79%"
