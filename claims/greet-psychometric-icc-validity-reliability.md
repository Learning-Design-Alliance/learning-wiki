## Evidence

### Phillips Anna C. 2016

Phillips Anna C., Lewis Lucy K., McEvoy Maureen P., Galipeau James, Glasziou Paul, Moher David, Tilson Julie K., Williams Marie T. (2016). Development and validation of the guideline for reporting evidence-based practice educational interventions and teaching (GREET). BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-016-0759-1

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Observational cross-sectional validation study in which 31 tertiary health professional students (11 undergraduate, 20 postgraduate) rated completeness of reporting of a published EBP study using the GREET checklist. Ratings were compared with a consensus criterion reference measure using intra-class correlation coefficients, showing "criterion validity ICC 0.73" and "inter-rater reliability (ICC 0.96)".

> "Overall, consistency of agreement between participants’ ratings of completeness of reporting and the consensus criterion ratings was strong (criterion validity ICC 0.73, 95 % CI 0.51–0.88, p <0 .0001). There was almost perfect consistency of agreement within the participant group for ratings of completeness (inter-rater reliability) (ICC 0.96, 95 % CI 0.93–0.98, p < 0.0001)"
