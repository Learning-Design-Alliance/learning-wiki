## Evidence

### Thomas C. Toppino and William DiGeorge 1984

Thomas C. Toppino and William DiGeorge. (1984). The spacing effect in free recall emerges with development. Memory & Cognition, 12(2), 118-122. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The+spacing+effect+in+free+recall+emerges+with+development+Memory+%26+Cognition+1984

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In Experiment 2's planned comparisons on 18 first-grade children (mean age 6-8), the same procedures used with preschoolers produced a spacing effect: children "recalled spaced repetitions better than they did massed repetitions" (F = 7.56, p < .01); means were 54.2% spaced vs 37.5% massed.
