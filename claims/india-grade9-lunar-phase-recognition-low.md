`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey question presenting nine images and asking students to pick the correct one for five days of a lunar cycle. The quote shows Day 2 performance; the paper also reports 79% correct for New Moon, 88% for Full Moon, 33% for Day 8, 35% for Day 11, and only 7% correct on all five.

> "For Day 2, only 25% of students correctly identified Image D as depicting the lunar phase, with the correct answer being the second most popular choice rather than the first."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Demographic analysis of phase-identification responses using chi-square tests. The quote gives the significant resource gap; the paper reports gender differences statistically insignificant (p = 0.1, 0.33, 0.19) and overall all-five-correct rates of 9% resource-rich vs 4% resource-poor.

> "Additionally, students from resource-rich schools perform better than their resource-poor counterparts, and in this case, the difference is statistically significant (p-value < 10–6, 10–3, and 10–4 for days 2, 8, and 11 respectively)."
