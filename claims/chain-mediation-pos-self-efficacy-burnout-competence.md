PROCESS Model 6 chain mediation with 5,000 bootstrap samples in the 321-nurse survey. The "X → M1 → M2 → Y" path had β = −0.071, 95% CI −0.119 to −0.033; the total indirect effect was −0.262 (57.84% of total effect).

> "Furthermore, perceived organizational support and self-efficacy exhibited a chain-mediated effect between job burnout and Emergency Response Competence, with a chain mediation effect value of −0.071."
