`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 1 event history analysis: prior mean line, an index of attractor strength computed from angular-velocity time series on trial j-1, significantly predicted discovery (change in -2 LL chi2(1) = 4.80, B = .42).

> "The probability of discovery was greater for those trials, j, with higher values of prior mean line (computed from trial j-1) and peak entropy (computed from trials j-2 through j-5), and lower values of prior entropy"
