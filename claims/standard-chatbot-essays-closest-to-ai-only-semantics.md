# Essays written under Standard Chatbot sat closest to AI-only essays in semantic embedding space, and Engage-to-Unlock differed significantly from Standard Chatbot along PC1

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In exploratory joint PCA of 498 essays, Standard Chatbot had the PC1 centroid (M=−0.014) closest to the AI-only corpus (M=+0.291), and Engage-to-Unlock differed significantly from Standard Chatbot along PC1 (pHolm<.001) but not from Time-Matched Unlock (pHolm=.104). The article labels these analyses exploratory and notes dispersion was descriptively similar across human conditions.
