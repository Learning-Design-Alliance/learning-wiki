## Evidence

### Lowe 2012

Lowe, G., & Belcher, S. (2012). Direct Instruction and music literacy: one approach to augmenting the diminishing? Australian Journal of Music Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1000240

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental control-group study with 74 Year 7 students in four intact classes; two classes (n=40) received the DI intervention and two (n=34) served as controls. Paired t-tests on Iowa Tests of Music Literacy showed the DI combined mean difference of 26.68 was significant (p < .000) while the non-DI difference of -0.84 was not (p = .505).

> "The t-tests indicated that the improvement observed in the DI class results was statistically significant, with a mean difference improvement of 26 points from pre to post-test on both tests, and a lower standard deviation than the non DI classes."
