### Arens 2025

Arens, A. Katrin; Fiedler, Daniel; Hasselhorn, Johannes; Möller, Jens. (2025). The formation of self‐concept and intrinsic value in arts‐related domains: Extending the generalized internal/external frame of reference model to music and visual arts. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.70038

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Structural equation modelling of the GI/E model (Model 2) in a sample of N = 442 German secondary students showed positive within-domain achievement–self-concept paths; the article reports "achievement and self‐concept were positively related within all domains".

> "According to the GI/E model assumptions, achievement and self‐concept were positively related within all domains (Table 3; for the factor correlations, see Tables S4 and S5 of the Online Supplements)."
