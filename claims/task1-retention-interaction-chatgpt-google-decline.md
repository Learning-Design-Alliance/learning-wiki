## Evidence

### Mahir Akgun 2025

Mahir Akgun, Sacip Toker. (2025). Short-Term Gains, Long-Term Gaps: The Impact of GenAI and Search Technologies on Retention. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-99264-3_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99264-3_6)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA comparing immediate assessment and three-week retention scores for Task 1 in the four-group experiment; the significant interaction ("partial η= .115") reflected differential score changes across groups.

> "The interaction effect between assessment and retention scores was significant (Wilk'sλ= .885, F(3, 119) = 5.175, p < .01, partial η= .115), explaining 11.5% of the variance (for Task 1)."
