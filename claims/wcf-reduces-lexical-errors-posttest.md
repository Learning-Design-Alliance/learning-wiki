## Evidence

### Al-Hazzani and Altalhab 2018

Nahla Al-Hazzani, Sultan Altalhab. (2018). Can Explicit Written Corrective Feedback Develop Grammatical and Lexical Accuracy of Saudi EFL Learners? International Journal of Education & Literacy Studies, 6(4). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.ijels.v.6n.4p.16

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Independent Sample t-test on post-test lexical errors: control (n=21, M=3.19, SD=2.60) versus experimental (n=29, M=1.41, SD=1.40); the article reports t=2.846, p=0.008. "The most common errors were spelling and direct translation from L1". No standardised effect size is printed in the quoted finding.

> "there were statistically significant differences between the number of students’ lexical errors in both the experimental and control post-test groups, fa - voring the control group, with the average number of errors amounting to (3.19), compared to (1.41) for the experimental group."
