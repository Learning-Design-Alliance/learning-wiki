## Evidence

### Lee R. 2026

Lee R., Dhami N., Gibson W., Hamza D. M., Oswald A. E., Moffat M. (2026). Assessing the Quality of Narrative Feedback in Entrustable Professional Activities Using the EFeCT Feedback Scoring Tool. The Clinical Teacher 23(1): e70295. https://doi.org/10.1111/tct.70295

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational scoring study of 748 randomly sampled anonymised EPA observations from 2022–2023 Year 3 students at one Canadian institution, scored with the EFeCT tool by three evaluators. The mean score was 4.3, the median was 5 and the mode was 5; "59% of assessed EPA observations meeting all EFeCT criteria for high‐quality feedback".

> "Overall, narrative feedback quality was moderate to high, with 59% of assessed EPA observations meeting all EFeCT criteria for high‐quality feedback."
