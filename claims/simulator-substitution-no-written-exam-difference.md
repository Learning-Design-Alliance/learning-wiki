# Replacing one hour of live-model scanning with SonoSim simulator training per session produced no significant difference on a 30-item written ultrasound exam

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Internal medicine residents trained with one hour of simulator plus one hour of live models scored 23.1±3.4 on the written exam versus 21.8±4.8 for live-model-only trainees, with no significant difference (P>0.05). [→ Silva Jack Philip 2016](#silva-jack-philip-2016)

## Evidence

### Silva Jack Philip 2016

Silva Jack Philip, Plescia Trevor, Molina Nathan, Tonelli Ana Claudia de Oliveira, Langdorf Mark, Fox John Christian. (2016). Randomized study of effectiveness of computerized ultrasound simulators for an introductory course for residents in Brazil. Journal of Educational Evaluation for Health Professions. https://doi.org/10.3352/jeehp.2016.13.16

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled course study: 40 of 53 enrolled residents completed the 30-item image/video-based MCQ written exam; the live-model-plus-simulator group scored "23.1±3.4 (n=21)" versus 21.8±4.8 (n=19) for live-model-only, unpaired t-test, P>0.05. No effect size was printed.

> "The mean score of the LM+S group was 23.1±3.4 (n=21) vs. 21.8±4.8 (n=19) in the LM group (Fig. 2). There was no significant difference between the written exam scores of the two groups (P>0.05)."
