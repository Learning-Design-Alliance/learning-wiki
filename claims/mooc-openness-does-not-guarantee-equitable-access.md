# The openness of a free MOOC does not guarantee equitable access: YWYL enrollment skewed toward developed countries because unrestricted high-speed internet is a necessity

## Subclaims

## Evidence

### John M. Aiken 2013

John M. Aiken, Shih-Yin Lin, Scott S. Douglas, Edwin F. Greco, Brian D. Thoms, Michael F. Schatz and Marcos D. Caballero. (2013). The Initial State of Students Taking an Introductory Physics MOOC. [doi:10.1119/perc.2013.pr.001](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.001)

`q1 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation of enrollment patterns and student reports: while openness should make the course accessible everywhere, developed-country residents dominated enrollment and students with poor or restricted access reported difficulties. No test is reported.

> "those who live in developed countries were far more likely to enroll in YWYL. High-speed internet with no restrictions is a necessity for accessing content in YWYL."
