`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Shapley value decomposition of R² across predictors (Table 3). Sleep's combined contribution is non-trivial but never dominant: family socioeconomic and cultural status contributes more in every subject (e.g., 70.37% in math).

> "the contribution of sleep duration is lower for foreign language and arts, accounting for 2.95% and 4.36%. These ﬁndings suggest that although sleep is nonlinearly associated with performance across all subjects, its impact is more pronounced in cognitively demanding subjects such as mathematics and science"
