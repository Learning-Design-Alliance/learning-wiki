## Evidence

### Hossein Meysam Pour 2025

Hossein Meysam Pour, Ansari Mojgan, Fekri Nazanin, Yazdimoghaddam Hamideh. (2025). The effects of e-learning vs. gamification-based training on ICU nurses’ knowledge and attitudes toward organ donation candidates: a study based on the psychological security and empowerment model. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07299-0

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r1`

In the same three-group trial, between-group One-Way ANOVA on attitude scores after intervention yielded P < 0.001 with generalized Eta-Squared of 0.80 (one month: 0.81); the article reports attitude levels "remained stable after the intervention and one month later".

> "Notably, the gamification group demonstrated a significant increase in attitude levels, showing a greater impact compared to both the e-learning and control groups."
