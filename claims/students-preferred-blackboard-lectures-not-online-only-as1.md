## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Saray Busto 2021

Saray Busto, Michael Dumbser, and Elena Gaburro. (2021). A Simple but Efficient Concept of Blended Teaching of Mathematics for Engineering Students during the COVID-19 Pandemic. [doi:10.3390/educsci11020056](https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci11020056)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Ex ante survey AS1 (n = 445 students) reported by the article: students "expressed a clear preference for traditional lectures at the blackboard" and confirmed the importance of the professor's body language. Table 2 additionally prints 263 answers of no versus 162 of yes to attending only online; no effect size is printed.

> "the students also expressed a clear preference for traditional lectures at the blackboard, confirming that the body language of the professor is important and that the possibility to see the professor renders the lecture more interesting."
