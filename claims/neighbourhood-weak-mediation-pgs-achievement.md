### Zhou Quan 2024

Zhou Quan, Gidziela Agnieszka, Allegrini Andrea G., Cheesman Rosa, Wertz Jasmin, Maxwell Jessye, Plomin Robert, Rimfeld Kaili, Malanchini Margherita. (2024). Gene-environment correlation: the role of family environment in academic development. Molecular Psychiatry. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-024-02716-0

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

SEM mediation analyses of six geocoded neighbourhood composites (occupancy, health, household size, population in households, qualification level, pollution) linked to TEDS postcodes. The study found "significant and consistent, yet weak, mediation effects" with average ß = 0.01; similar findings held for Cog and NonCog PGSs.

> "Neighbourhood occupancy, health, and household size mediated the prediction from the educational attainment polygenic score to academic achievement at ages 7, 12, and 16, but the average indirect effect was weak (average beta coefficient ß = 0.01 [95% CI, 0.01–0.02]; see Fig. 2 and Supplementary Table 8a)."
