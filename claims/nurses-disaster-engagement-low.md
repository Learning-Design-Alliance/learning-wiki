## Evidence

### Ličen Sabina 2025

Ličen Sabina, Prosen Mirko. (2025). Disaster Nursing Competencies in a Time of Global Conflicts and Climate Crises: A Cross‐Sectional Survey Study. International Nursing Review. https://doi.org/10.1111/inr.70052

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the roles and responsibilities subscale (4 items, range 4–28) of the Sl-DNCC in the survey of 379 Slovenian nurses. The median "was significantly lower than the hypothesised median score of 16".

> "the median score for nurses' roles and responsibilities in disaster management (M = 15) was significantly lower than the hypothesised median score of 16 (Z = −4.264, p = 0.000), indicating limited perceived engagement in disaster management."
