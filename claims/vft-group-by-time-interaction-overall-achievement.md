## Evidence

### Norbaizura HARIS & Kamisah OSMAN 2015

Norbaizura HARIS & Kamisah OSMAN. (2015). The Effectiveness of a Virtual Field Trip (VFT) Module in Learning Biology. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 16(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1092841

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Omnibus interaction from the two-way ANOVA in Table 3 of the quasi-experiment (n=60): the Group x Time term was significant. The article prints F = 35.837 and sig = 0.000 in Table 3 but no effect size.

> "The findings also show that there is a significant interaction between group and time of the overall achievement. Because there are significant interactions then the form of increased for pre - and post -test scores is describe d as shown in Figure 1 below."

### Norbaizura HARIS & Kamisah OSMAN 2015 (2)

Norbaizura HARIS & Kamisah OSMAN. (2015). The Effectiveness of a Virtual Field Trip (VFT) Module in Learning Biology. Turkish Online Journal of Distance Education, 16(3). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1092841

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

The plotted pre/post means in Figure 1 of the same quasi-experiment show the treatment group's gain exceeding the control group's gain on overall achievement. The article describes this pattern in text accompanying the figure; no effect size is printed.

> "Figure: 1 shows that students taught using VFT showed a higher improvement in overall achievement than the students taught using conventional method s."
