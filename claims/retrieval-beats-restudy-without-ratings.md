Higham, P. A., Fastrich, G. M., Potts, R., Murayama, K., Pickering, J. S., & Hadwin, J. A. (2023). Spaced Retrieval Practice: Can Restudying Trump Retrieval? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09809-2

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r3`

Experiment 3 removed the memory rating altogether and compared restudy and retrieval performance for items encountered once, twice, or thrice across the three LPSs. Per the article's summary, "retrieval was superior to restudying regardless of the number of times the items were encountered". No effect size was printed in the supplied text.
