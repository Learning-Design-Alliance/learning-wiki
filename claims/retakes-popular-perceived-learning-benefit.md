### Supriya K. 2024

Supriya K., Bang Christofer, Ebie Jessica, Pagliarulo Christopher, Tucker Derek, Villegas Kaela, Wright Christian, Brownell Sara. (2024). Optional Exam Retakes Reduce Anxiety but may Exacerbate Score Disparities Between Students with Different Social Identities. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11440740/

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pooled survey data from Courses 1 and 2 (n = 1337 and n = 1290 respondents) show "exam retakes were very popular with students", with about 86% of retakers reporting substantial study effort and only 20–30% reporting reduced initial-exam studying.

> "Overall, exam retakes were very popular with students and most students, regardless of whether they retook an exam, perceived that exam retakes help their learning. Moreover, about 86% of students who retook the exam said that they put a lot of effort into studying for the retake, while only 20–30% of students said that they studied less for the initial exam."
