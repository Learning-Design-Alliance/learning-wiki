Questionnaire item on perceived optimal roles for AI completed by 29 students in the AI-enhanced flipped classroom group. The article reports "75.86% (n = 22) of students simultaneously selected the three roles of personal tutor, creative partner, and critical thinking partner", while the proportion viewing AI primarily as a content generator was lower (55.17%, n = 16).

> "A notable 75.86% (n = 22) of students simultaneously selected the three roles of personal tutor, creative partner, and critical thinking partner, reflecting a strong expectation for AI to evolve beyond basic information retrieval toward personalized tutoring and cognitive stimulation."
