### Nunez-Rocha RE 2026

Nunez-Rocha RE, Castillo-Flores S, Abreu AA, Ramirez R, Pettigrew M, Garces-Palacios S, Al Abbas A, Guzzetta AA, Scott DJ, Zeh HJ 3rd, Sankaranarayanan G, Polanco PM. (2026). `q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Subdomain analysis in the training beyond proficiency group showed significant improvement across five domains, with the article reporting "significant gains were noted in respect for tissue (p = 0.01), time and motion (p = 0.001)" among others.

> "In the training beyond proficiency group, significant gains were noted in respect for tissue (p = 0.01), time and motion (p = 0.001), instrument handling (p = 0.005), use of assistance (p < 0.001), and knowledge of procedure (p = 0.01). The only domain without a significant change was knowledge of instruments (p = 0.07), which was already high at baseline."
