# First OSPE after ILC introduction showed significantly lower performance in the CSF infection station, but later ILC-related stations were not significantly different from non-integrated stations

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In the first OSPE following ILC introduction, the cerebrospinal fluid infection station score was significantly lower than non-integrated stations (1.32 ± 0.58 vs 4.08 ± 1.97; p < 0.009). [→ Azer Samy A 2013](#azer-samy-a-2013)
`q2 i?` In subsequent blocks and years, mean scores in ILC-related OSPE stations were not significantly different from stations covering non-integrated concepts. [→ Azer Samy A 2013 (2)](#azer-samy-a-2013-2)

## Evidence

### Azer Samy A 2013

Azer Samy A, Hasanato Rana, Al-Nassar Sami, Somily Ali, AlSaadi Muslim M. (2013). Introducing integrated laboratory classes in a PBL curriculum: impact on student's learning and satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-71

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Analysis of end-of-block Objective Structured Practical Examination scores in the Nervous System block, academic year 2010/2011, comparing the ILC-related station with non-integrated stations. The article prints the means and p value but no effect size.

> "The mean scores in a station covering cerebrospinal fluid infection were significantly lower compared to their performance in other stations related to non-integrated classes (1.32 ± 0.58 vs 4.08 ± 1.97; p < 0.009, respectively)."

### Azer Samy A 2013 (2)

Azer Samy A, Hasanato Rana, Al-Nassar Sami, Somily Ali, AlSaadi Muslim M. (2013). Introducing integrated laboratory classes in a PBL curriculum: impact on student's learning and satisfaction. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-13-71

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Comparison of OSPE station scores across the Gastrointestinal & Haematology and Endocrine blocks in 2010/2011 and 2011/2012, with printed p values ranging from 0.299 to 0.420. The article reports no statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

> "As shown in Table 4, the means of students' scores in stations related to ILCs were not significantly different from those obtained in other stations covering concepts discussed in non-integrated classes."
