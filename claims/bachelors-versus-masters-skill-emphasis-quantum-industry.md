Findings from the report's interview-based analysis of 22 non-PhD positions: the authors report that "Master’s roles stand out with thecommunication and collaborationskills needed on top of the other three skill categories," with bachelor's roles centered on building, operating, and troubleshooting hardware.

> "Bachelor’s roles lean towardinstrumentation,computation and data analysis, as well asexperimental and project designskills. Individuals in these roles build, operate, and troubleshoot hardware, and they gather and interpret data to design and carry out experiments. Master’s roles stand out with thecommunication and collaborationskills needed on top of the other three skill categories"
