## Evidence

### Geyer 1921

Geyer, D. L. (1921). The Reliability of Rankings by Group Intelligence Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeduapsy

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Observational comparison of two group tests given by the same person to 120 junior high school pupils at the Chicago Normal School during 1919 and 1920. The article reports the "median change of rank from one test to the other was found to be 18 places" and a rank-difference correlation of 0.642.

> "the median change of rank from one test to the other was found to be 18 places. The maximum change which could have been effected throughout the group was 60 places and the change left to chance would be 40 places."
