### Eric W. Burkholder 2022

Eric W. Burkholder, Carl E. Wieman. (2022). Absence of a COVID-induced academic drop in high-school physics learning. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.023102](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.18.023102)

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Overall finding from the two-cohort comparison (881 students in 2019, 968 in 2021) on a validated diagnostic exam, combining the algebra-based and calculus-based null results. The article states the group "showed no detrimental impact on learning due to COVID" and frames this against faculty perceptions.

> "These results show that, at least in the domain of physics, this group of students showed no detrimental impact on learning due to COVID. In contrast to predictions5 and many faculty perceptions1 this suggests that the negative effects of remote instruction on student learning are small, at least for this population."
