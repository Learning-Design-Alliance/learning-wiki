### Kamphausen Anne 2026

Kamphausen Anne, Pilz Maximilian, Baumgärtel Matthias, Kubitz Jens Christian, Engelen Christian. (2026). Effect of an app-guided advanced cardiac life support algorithm on teamwork, non-technical skills, and workload in simulated in-hospital cardiac arrest: a randomized controlled feasibility trial. Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13049-026-01673-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Asystole scenario with 10 teams per arm. "Across all key ALS performance metrics, no clear differences were detected between the intervention and control group"; deviations from recommended intervals showed large inter-team variability but no systematic intergroup differences.

> "Across all key ALS performance metrics, no clear differences were detected between the intervention and control group. Time to first rhythm analysis was similar between groups (119.6 ± 63.7 s vs. 115.1 ± 28.6 s), as was the time to first administration of adrenaline (185.2 ± 65.7 s vs. 199.2 ± 28.2 s)."
