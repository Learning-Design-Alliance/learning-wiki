`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Qualitative findings from teachers' written responses, focus groups and interviews in the pilot, including one elementary site where an autistic student emerged unexpectedly as the school expert. The report calls this good evidence of opportunities to discover interests and build rapport; it is qualitative, so no effect size.

> "A strong theme that emerged from teachers’ written responses, as well as in focus groups and interviews, was the opportunity for LDD students to develop expertise that gave them confidence as well as new status among their peers."
