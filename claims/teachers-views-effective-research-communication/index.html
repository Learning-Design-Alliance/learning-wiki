Lord et al. 2017¶

Pippa Lord, Adam Rabiasz, Palak Roy, Jennie Harland, Ben Styles, Katherine Fowler. (2017). Evidence-based literacy support: the 'Literacy Octopus' trial — Evaluation report and executive summary. Education Endowment Foundation. http://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN84508118

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Qualitative process-evaluation finding drawing on surveys, telephone interviews, observations of activities and case studies in Literacy Octopus schools about how communication approaches were received. No effect size is reported; this is teachers' reported perception.

"Teachers felt research evidence was most effectively communicated when it was interactive, accessible, relevant, included a balanced and credible discussion of the evidence, and focused on how to apply the evidence in practice."
