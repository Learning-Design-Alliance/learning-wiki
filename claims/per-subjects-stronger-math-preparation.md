## Evidence

### Stephen Kanim and Ximena C. Cid 2017

Stephen Kanim and Ximena C. Cid. (2017). The demographics of physics education research. Originally published 2016. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.02598](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.02598)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Observational comparison of binned SAT Math scores for all SAT takers versus students at the 39 universities weighted by their contribution to the PER research population (Fig. 4). The authors report "only 7% percent of the students at PER research institutions" score below 500 versus 46% overall.

> "while 46% of the overall population has SAT math scores below 500, only 7% percent of the students at PER research institutions do. At the other end of the scale, 25% of the overall population has SAT math scores of 600 or more, but students with these scores account for 70% of our research institutions."
