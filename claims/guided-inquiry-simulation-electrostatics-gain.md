### Espinoza 2020

Espinoza, F. (2020). Impact of Guided Inquiry with Simulations on Knowledge of Electricity and Wave Phenomena. European Scientific Journal, ESJ, 16 (33), 1. https://doi.org/10.19044/esj.2020.v16n33p1

`q2 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Study II: 37 AP physics students completed a 10-question electrostatics pre/post assessment around a PhET static electricity activity (Build an Atom, Balloons and Static Electricity, John Travoltage). The paired t-test was significant, "t = 25.71, p <.001", with Cohen's d = .657 reported in Table 5.

> "as demonstrated here, for the electrostatics task a paired t -test of significance yields a value t = 25.71, p <.001, for the Electric Fields it is t = 6.70, p <.001"
