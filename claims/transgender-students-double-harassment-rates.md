## Evidence

### Cerna 2021

Cerna, R., Stern, A., Austin, G., Betz, J., Zhang, G., & Hashmi, S. (2021). Understanding the experiences of LGBTQ high school students in California by race/ethnicity. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/lgbtq-students-in-california/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive percentages from the CHKS 2017–19 pooled high school sample (Table 6), comparing harassment reports across gender identity within each of seven race/ethnicity groups. The report prints the contrast as "at least twice as likely" with 60%+ versus 32% or less; no inferential tests or effect sizes are reported.

> "Transgender students were at least twice as likely to report experiencing harassment as their non-transgender peers in each race/ethnicity group. Regardless of race/ethnicity, 60 percent or more of all transgender students in the sample indicated that they have experienced harassment."
