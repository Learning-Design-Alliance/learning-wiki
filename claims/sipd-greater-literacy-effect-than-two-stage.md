# The simplified IPD model produced a greater pooled literacy effect size than two-stage meta-analysis models

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The IPD model yielded a larger pooled literacy effect size (0.06) than two-stage fixed-effect (0.02) and random-effect (0.03) models, while most individual estimates corresponded in direction and magnitude. [→ Bilal Ashraf 2021](#bilal-ashraf-2021)

## Evidence

### Bilal Ashraf 2021

Individual participant data meta-analysis of the impact of EEF trials on the educational attainment of pupils on Free School Meals: 2011 - 2019. `q2 · i?`

Methodological comparison of the simplified IPD model against two-stage fixed-effect and random-effect models (Table 6 and Appendix 2). Table 6 prints literacy pooled ES of 0.06 (IPD), 0.02 (FE) and 0.03 (RE); the report attributes the difference to how weights are defined. No effect size for the difference itself is printed.

> "However, the IPD model produced a greater effect size for literacy outcomes than the two -stage model."
