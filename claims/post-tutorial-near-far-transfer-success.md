# After working through the tutorial, all eight interviewed students drew correct free body diagrams and Newton's second law equations for a near-transfer three-box problem

## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Interactive video tutorials for enhancing problem solving, reasoning, and meta-cognitive skills of introductory physics students. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1602.07746](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.07746)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

In eight later think-aloud interviews, students attempted the tutorial then solved two unaided transfer problems on a worksheet. The article reports all students drew correct free body diagrams and Newton's second law equations for the three-box problem; no effect size is printed.

> "All students were able to draw the correct free body diagrams for the three boxes and write down Newton's second law equations for the non-equilibrium situation."
