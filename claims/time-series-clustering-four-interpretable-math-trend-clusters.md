## Evidence

### Satoshi Takahashi 2021

Satoshi Takahashi, Hiroki Kuno, Atsushi Yoshikawa. (2021). Extracting candidate factors affecting long-term trends of student abilities across subjects. A preprint. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.06446](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.06446)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Time series clustering step of evaluation experiment 1 on [Org. A] 5 M., 7 M., and 8 M. deviation scores. Group 1 (168 students) and group 2 (201 students) were each clustered into four clusters, e.g. "stay high stably" (39 and 80 students respectively), matching across datasets.

> "We named the groups of results after their shapes and values: “s tay high stably,” “stay low stably,” “increase from low,” and “decrease from high.” As a result, we clustered the achievement tests of both groups 1 and 2 into the same four clusters."
