Learner Variables¶

  • Belonging — outcome: instruction changes it
  • Identity — moderator: an instructional effect differs with it
  • Mindset — predictor: learners who differ on it differ in outcomes
  • Race and Ethnicity — predictor: outcomes differ by group: a gap reflecting circumstances and treatment, not a cause
