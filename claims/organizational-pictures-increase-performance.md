`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Narrative synthesis of seven articles with text-only control groups examining organizational pictures. Six reported higher accuracy, with one study reporting moderator-dependent effects (participant age). The review concludes a "slightly more stable effect for organizational pictures on performance."
