## Evidence

### Barış Eren Nadiye; Çiftçi Bahar 2025

Barış Eren Nadiye; Çiftçi Bahar. (2025). The Use of Puzzles as a Teaching Technique in Nursing Education: A Randomized Controlled Study. Journal of Evaluation in Clinical Practice. https://doi.org/10.1111/jep.70118

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r1`

Randomized controlled pretest-posttest experiment with 65 analyzed students (32 experimental, 33 control) learning 'excretory activities'. The between-group posttest comparison in Table 2 shows the experimental group's posttest mean of 82.32 ± 7.78 versus the control group's 49.70 ± 8.60, with the printed between-group effect size ES = 3.971.

> "The experimental group's mean “Knowledge Test” score significantly increased from 11.38 ± 9.32 to 82.32 ± 7.78 (t = −34.886, p = 0.001, d = 6.266). Similarly, the control group significantly increased from 15.03 ± 11.40 to 49.70 ± 8.60 (t = −15.244, p = 0.001, d = 2.654)."
