Eunjin Seo, Margarett Clapper, Cameron A. Hecht, Robert Crosnoe, David S. Yeager. (2025). Peer Mindset Culture as a Developmental Context for Belonging. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10082-8

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r3`

Theoretical argument introducing the mindset-culture mismatch concept, defined as incongruence between an individual's beliefs and the dominant mindset norms of their environment. The review attributes supporting evidence to Canning et al. (2020) on organizational growth mindset norms and Wallace et al. (2023).

> "As a result, students with a growth mindset may feel socially and psychologically disconnected, even when no overt stereotype is at play (Muenks et al., 2021; Seo et al., 2025b)."
