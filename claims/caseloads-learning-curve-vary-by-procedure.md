## Evidence

### Alsagheir Ali 2021

Alsagheir Ali, Koziarz Alex, Belley-Côté Emilie P., Whitlock Richard P. (2021). Expertise-based design in surgical trials: a narrative review. Canadian Journal of Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.008520

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's Table 1 lists caseloads used in expertise-based cardiac surgery trials: off-pump CABG 85 cases, minimally invasive CABG 40, robotic-assisted left internal mammary harvest 15, and off-pump endoscopic CABG more than 100. The review reports these as compiled cutoffs, not as newly estimated parameters.

> "Number of procedures performed has initially been a common proxy to define expertise, with several studies in cardiac surgery reporting cutoffs for various procedures (Table 1)."
