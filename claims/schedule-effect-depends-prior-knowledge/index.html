q3 · i? · causal · r?

The classroom experiment found that the effect of practice schedules on learning outcomes depended on students' prior knowledge, noting that "students with low prior knowledge benefit from fully interleaved practice" while no effect was found for high prior knowledge students.

"While students with low prior knowledge benefit from fully interleaved practice, we found no effect of practice schedules for students with high prior knowledge."
