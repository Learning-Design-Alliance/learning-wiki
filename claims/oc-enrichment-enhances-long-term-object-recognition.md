## Evidence

### Gattas 2022

Gattas, S., Collett, H. A., Huff, A. E., Creighton, S. D., Weber, S. E., Buckhalter, S. S., Manning, S. A., Ryait, H. S., McNaughton, B. L., & Winters, B. D. (2022). A rodent obstacle course procedure controls delivery of enrichment and enhances complex cognitive functions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00134-x

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Controlled experiment with four housing/course groups of mice (n = 40 total, 10 per group) tested on spontaneous object recognition with a 7-min sample. The delay x group interaction was significant, F(3, 36) = 16.818, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.584; only the OC group "discriminated the novel object" at 24 h.

> "Further analysis with post hoc independent samples t-tests revealed signi ﬁcant differences in performance between the OC group and the EH group, t(18) = −6.287, p < 0.001, CC group, t(18) = −5.573, p < 0.001 and SH group, t(18) = −5.836, p < 0.001, when the delay was 24 h."

### Gattas 2022 (2)

Gattas, S., Collett, H. A., Huff, A. E., Creighton, S. D., Weber, S. E., Buckhalter, S. S., Manning, S. A., Ryait, H. S., McNaughton, B. L., & Winters, B. D. (2022). A rodent obstacle course procedure controls delivery of enrichment and enhances complex cognitive functions. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00134-x

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

One-month post-enrichment replication of the SOR task (7-min sample, 24-h delay) in the same four-group design, with a significant group main effect, partial η2 = 0.230; only the OC group performed "significantly above chance", t(9) = −5.962, p < 0.001.

> "Observed were signiﬁcant main effects of group, F(3, 36) = 3.575, p = 0.023, partial η2 = 0.230, and delay, F(1, 36) = 7.421, p = 0.010, partial η2 = 0.171, while the interaction between group and delay was not signi ﬁcant, F(3, 36) = 2.745, p = 0.057, partial η2 = 0.186."
