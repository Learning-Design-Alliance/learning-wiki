## Evidence

### Qi Liu 2025

Qi Liu, Siyu Zhu, Can Liu, Yanmiao Yang, Chunmei Lan, Xinwei Song, Benjamin Becker, Keith M. Kendrick, Xinqi Zhou & Weihua Zhao. (2025). Neural signals gradually shift towards inferior frontal gyrus and long-term effects following facial imitation-based social learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00372-9

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Directed phase transfer entropy (dPTE) analysis of NPS time series among IFG, STS and IPL in the three fNIRS sessions: no main coupling direction at the 2nd and 9th sessions (ps > 0.05), but at the 16th session the article reports the flow "progressively moves towards the IFG".

> "These results revealed that as the number of repetitive FISLs increases, theﬂow of information progressively moves towards the IFG, suggesting a shift in the synergy function within MNS towards the IFG taking a dominant role."
