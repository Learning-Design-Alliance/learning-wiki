## Evidence

### Krupa 2013

Krupa, E., Webel, C., & McManus, J. (2013). Evaluating the impact of computer-based and traditional learning environments on students' knowledge of algebra. In Martinez, M. & Castro Superfine, A (Eds.), Proceedings of the 35th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education. Chicago, IL: University of Illinois at Chicago. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED584492

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Level 3 responses to Task 2 part (a) about the ticket-price revenue function t(x) = -25x² + 750x. Additionally, "18% of the CB students did not give any justification" while all F2F students did.

> "Of the F2F students, 78% correctly indicated that income would not continue to increase, justifying this conclusion by appealing to the problem context, the negative coefficient in the equation, or by testing values. In comparison, only 58% of the CB students correctly indicated that the income would not continue to increase."
