# Students' creative ability level has a significant main effect on post-test performance in sculpture skill development in the OAU quasi-experiment

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The main effect of students' creative ability on sculpture performance was statistically significant (F-cal 9.03 vs F-crit 3.22), leading to rejection of the null hypothesis. [→ Abass 2011](#abass-2011)

## Evidence

### Abass 2011

Bada Tayo Abass. (2011). Computer Instructional Approach and Students' Creative Ability in Sculpture Education in Nigeria Universities: Obafemi Awolowo University as a Case Study. World Journal of Education. https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v1n2p130

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A 2x3 ANCOVA of post-test scores with pretest as covariate, on 48 sculpture students in a non-randomized pretest-posttest control group design. The article reports the creative ability main effect was significant: "the F-cal is statically significant" and "the null hypothesis was rejected." No effect size is printed.

> "the F-cal for the main ef fect of the student' creative ability on their performance in textile design, given the instructional approaches is 9.03 while its corresponding critical value, F-crit (3.22). This implies that the F-cal is statically significant."
