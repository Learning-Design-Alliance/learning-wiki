## Evidence

### Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter 2017

Matthew E. Brock and Erik W. Carter. (2017). A Meta-Analysis of Educator Training to Improve Implementation of Interventions for Students With Disabilities. Remedial and Special Education. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932516653477

`q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

Meta-regression moderator analysis across all 12 group-design studies, testing predictors identified from the single-case design literature. The modeling-plus-performance-feedback combination was "statistically significant, β = .77, t(10) = 2.24, p = .04" and explained R2 = 42.03% of random variance.

> "Presence of a combination of modeling and performance feedback was the strongest single predictor. This combination was statistically significant, β = .77, t(10) = 2.24, p = .04, and it explained a substantial proportion of the random variance in the model (R2 = 42.03%)."
