## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Through selective coding of interviews with 12 CME participants, a friendly, trustful, non-threatening context was identified as the core category, systematically related to collaborative knowledge sharing and participants' willingness to discuss deficiencies and reflect on them. ## Evidence

### Dionyssopoulos A 2014

Dionyssopoulos A, Karalis T, Panitsides EA. (2014). Continuing medical education revisited: theoretical assumptions and practical implications: a qualitative study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-014-0278-x

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Grounded-theory analysis of 12 in-depth interviews with EWAPS participants, following open, axial and selective coding. The core category of "friendly collaborative context" was "systematically related to other categories", and participants reported feeling comfortable receiving feedback and discussing deficiencies.

> "through selective coding, “friendly collaborative context” was identified as the core category, being systematically related to other categories. More precisely, the friendly, trustful, non-threatening to the participants' ego, context has been assumed to be intertwined with the collaborative, knowledge sharing approach"
