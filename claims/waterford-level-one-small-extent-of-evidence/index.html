What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Waterford Early Reading Level One™. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/541

q3 · i? · causal · r2

WWC extent-of-evidence categorization: one study, six centers, 19 classrooms/270 children per domain rated "small"; the other four domains had zero qualifying studies. Two additional reviewed studies did not meet evidence screens (no comparison group; incomparable groups at baseline).

"The WWC considers the extent of evidence for Waterford Early Reading Level One™ to be small for oral language and for print knowledge. No studies that met WWC evidence standards with or without reservations addressed phonological processing, early reading/writing, cognition, or math."
