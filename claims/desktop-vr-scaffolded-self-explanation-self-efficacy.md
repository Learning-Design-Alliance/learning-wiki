## Evidence

### Adurangba Victor Oje 2026

Adurangba Victor Oje, Nathaniel J. Hunsu. (2026). Desktop virtual reality with scaffolded interaction and reflective explanation: effects on cognitive, motivational, and learning outcomes in engineering education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10722-y

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Bayesian estimation using a t-distribution model with unequal variances compared self-efficacy between conditions. The VR + self-explanation group reported higher confidence ("β = 0.73 (95% CI [0.10, 1.35])") with a large effect size.

> "For self-efficacy, the posterior estimate indicated a statistically credible difference, with β = 0.73 (95% CI [0.10, 1.35]) and a large effect size (d = 0.78). Students in the VR + self-explanation group reported higher confidence in their ability to understand and apply engineering concepts compared to those in the control group"
