# Learning in the matrix task is instance-specific: performance slows markedly when new stimuli replace practiced ones

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 studies · 1 causal `r2`, 1 associational `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The condition effect comparing first practice, last practice, and new-stimulus presentations was highly significant, with about 94 s decrease with practice and about 75 s increase for new stimuli. [→ Marinelli CV 2021](#marinelli-cv-2021)
`q3 i?` Performance on the new-stimulus B1 matrix (122 s) was much slower than on the practiced A20 matrix (47 s), although slightly faster than on the first A1 presentation (141 s). [→ Marinelli CV 2021 (2)](#marinelli-cv-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Marinelli CV 2021

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA comparing the A1, A20, and new-stimulus B1 presentations: the condition effect was highly significant, F(2, 248) = 484.74, p < 0.0001, with "a significant decrease in times with practice (of about 94 s.)".

> "The condition effect was highly significant (F(2, 248)  = 484.74; p < 0.0001), indicating a significant decrease in times with practice (of about 94 s., p < 0.0001, Tukey's test)"

### Marinelli CV 2021 (2)

Marinelli CV, Angelelli P, Martelli M, Trenta M and Zoccolotti P (2021) Ability to Consolidate Instances as a Proxy for the Association Among Reading, Spelling, and Math Learning Skill. Front. Psychol. 12:761696. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.761696

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Tukey post-hoc comparisons from the same ANOVA: B1 performance (122 s, SD = 32 s) "was much slower than the performance at the A20 matrix (47 s, SD = 12 s)", though slightly faster than A1 (141 s, SD = 40 s), p < 0.0001.

> "performance in the B1 presentation (122 s, SD = 32 s) was much slower than the performance at the A20 matrix (47 s, SD = 12 s), although slightly faster than in the A1 (141 s, SD = 40 s) presentation (of about 19 s., p < 0.0001, Tukey's test)"
