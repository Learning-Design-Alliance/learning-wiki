Distributing mathematics practice over three days instead of one helped seventh graders at one and six weeks, but third graders only at one week

In a classroom study of 213 third and seventh graders, one practice set a day for three days produced better follow-up test performance than three sets in one day for seventh graders at both delays, while for third graders the data supported a benefit only at the one-week test.
