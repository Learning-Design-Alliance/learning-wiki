q2 · i? · associational · r1

Inductive thematic analysis of open-ended responses in the n=20 survey. Faculty reported proving AI use pulled time from instructional design and mentorship, described feeling like "Digital Prosecutors", and reported an escalating detection-evasion arms race and corrosion of student-instructor trust.

"Open-ended responses revealed a pervasive sense of “Pedagogical Burnout”—a chronic erosion of instructional motivation driven by the displacement of teaching by policing."
