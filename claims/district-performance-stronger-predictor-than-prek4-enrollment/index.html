Table 1, Model 1: district ERS measures regressed on fall 2023 i-Ready math scale score and improvement likelihood. Pre-pandemic performance (B = 3.46, SE = 0.73, p < .001) and pandemic recovery (B = 8.98, SE = 3.00, p < .01) were both positively associated with fall scores, and both with improvement likelihood.

Sarah Collier Villaume 2025 (2)¶

Sarah Collier Villaume, Amy Auletto, & Kadir Bahar. (2025). Investing Early: The Impact of State Prekindergarten Funding on Kindergarten Academic Outcomes. E4 Center. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2025/06/30/investing-early-the-impact-of-state-prekindergarten-funding-on-kindergarten-academic-outcomes/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Table 1, Models 2-3: state-funded PreK4 enrollment (2022-23) predicted improvement likelihood in Model 2 (B = 0.14, SE = 0.05, p < .05) but was not significant for fall scores; in Model 3, controlling for both ERS measures, it was not significant for either outcome.

"Taking into account any differences in pre-pandemic performance, state-funded PreK4 enrollment had no association with Fall 2023 kindergarten math scale scores. Yet, it was positively associated with the likelihood of improvement from Fall 2023 to Spring 2024 (Model 2)."
