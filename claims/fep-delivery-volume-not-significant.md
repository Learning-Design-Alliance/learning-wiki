## Evidence

### Thapa R. 2023

Thapa R., Nikolli K., McMahon D., Blakemore S., Tamang S., Bhatta S., Gautam P., Shrestha R., Rajbhandari R. (2023). Novel on-site follow-up and enhancement program (FEP) improves knowledge, clinical skills and enabling environment of skilled birth attendants in Nepal. PLoS One. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0285653

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Pre-post analysis of delivery logs for Cohort A2016/A2017 (n = 73) in the Results section. Normal deliveries rose 4.76 (p = 0.15), breech 0.02 (p = 0.79), and vacuum deliveries decreased 0.1 (p = 0.39), all non-significant.

> "After receiving FEP, SBAs conducted 4.7 additional total deliveries (mostly normal deliveries) over 3 months, although this increase was not found to be statistically significant (p = 0.17)."
