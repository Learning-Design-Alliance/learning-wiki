Evidence¶

Nückles 2021¶

Nückles, M. (2021). Investigating Visual Perception in Teaching and Learning with Advanced Eye-Tracking Methodologies: Rewards and Challenges of an Innovative Research Paradigm. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09567-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

Naturalistic eye-tracking study by Pouta et al. with teacher students and experienced teachers teaching a fractions lesson; retrospective recall protocols from videotaped lessons were coded, yielding this counterintuitive result.

"the teacher students provided significantly more advanced interpretations of students'mathematical thinking in the retrospective recall protocols, whereas the experienced teachers'reasoning remained mostly on a descriptive level"

Nückles 2021 (2)¶

Nückles, M. (2021). Investigating Visual Perception in Teaching and Learning with Advanced Eye-Tracking Methodologies: Rewards and Challenges of an Innovative Research Paradigm. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09567-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

Video coding of the same Pouta et al. lessons shows experienced teachers' instructional support was more effective, dissociating realized scaffolding from retrospective reasoning quality.

"the coding of the lesson videos clearly showed that the experienced teachers were far more successful than the teacher students in providing effective instructional support for students' understanding of fractions."
