Consensus recommendations on peripheral blood smear review: defining curricular standards and fellow competency. Blood Advances. https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2023009843

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Mixed-methods analysis of transcribed statements from focus group 1, inductively coded into seven thematic domains. The authors suggest the minimal discussion of platelet morphology "potentially indicates a relative perceived lack of importance".

> "Most discussion centered on specific cell lineage (32.8% of the statements), specific diagnosis (39.7% of the statements), and specific morphology (26.0% of the statements). The procedure of PBS review was discussed the least (9.2% of the statements)."
