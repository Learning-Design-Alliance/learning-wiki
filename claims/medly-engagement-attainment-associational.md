## Evidence

### Wayne Harrison 2026

Wayne Harrison, Rahil Khowaja, Emma Dobson, Germaine Uwimpuhwe, and Steve Higgins. (2026). Evaluating AI Tutoring at the Speed of Innovation: Practitioner-Led Micro-Randomised Trials of an AI Tutoring Platform in GCSE Science. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14789

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory post-randomisation analysis of platform-log engagement among intervention students only, adjusted for pre-test attainment. The association was positive overall and in each subject, but the authors state these analyses "cannot establish that increasing the number of questions answered would itself cause the observed increase in attainment".

> "Each additional question answered was associated with approximately 0.18 additional post-test marks (95% CI 0.13 to 0.22). Positive associa-tions were also observed separately in Physics, Biology and Chemistry."
