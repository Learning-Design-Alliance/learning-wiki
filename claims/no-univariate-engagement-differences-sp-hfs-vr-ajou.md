## Evidence

### Yu Jihye 2026

Yu Jihye, Lee Sukyung, Kim Mira, Lee Janghoon, Jung Yun Jung. (2026). Evaluating medical students’ engagement and confidence across three simulation-based education methods: standardized patient, high fidelity simulator, and virtual reality. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08634-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate ANOVA on overall engagement scores from 43 fifth-year medical students surveyed after each of the SP, HFS, and VR modules; the article reports "no significant effect of the simulation methods (F = 0.48, p = .618)". No effect size is printed.

> "The analysis results for engagement showed no significant effect of the simulation methods (F = 0.48, p = .618)."

### Yu Jihye 2026 (2)

Yu Jihye, Lee Sukyung, Kim Mira, Lee Janghoon, Jung Yun Jung. (2026). Evaluating medical students’ engagement and confidence across three simulation-based education methods: standardized patient, high fidelity simulator, and virtual reality. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08634-9

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Univariate ANOVA on the relevance sub-scale (two Exit Survey items) after each module; the article reports "no significant effect of the simulation methods (F = 1.38, p = .256)". No effect size is printed.

> "The analysis of variance for relevance also revealed no significant effect of the simulation methods (F = 1.38, p = .256)."

### Yu Jihye 2026 (4)
