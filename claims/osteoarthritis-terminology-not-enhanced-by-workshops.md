### Porcheret M 2018

Porcheret M, Main C, Croft P, Dziedzic K. (2018). Enhancing delivery of osteoarthritis care in the general practice consultation: evaluation of a behaviour change intervention. BMC Family Practice. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-018-0715-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Task-level analysis of the video-recorded simulated consultations; Table 3 shows this task scored 10 at baseline, 6 at one month and 9 at five months. Further analysis (Additional file 4) found the word "arthritis" was often used instead.

> "The task delivery score for "Giving the diagnosis of OA using the word "osteoarthritis"" decreased non-significantly after the workshops (Table 3), an unexpected finding given this task was a key focus of the workshops."
