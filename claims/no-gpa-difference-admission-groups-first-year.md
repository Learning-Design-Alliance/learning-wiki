## Evidence

### Lucieer SM 2015

Lucieer SM, Stegers-Jager KM, Rikers RMJP, Themmen APN. (2015). Non-cognitive selected students do not outperform lottery-admitted students in the pre-clinical stage of medical school. Advances in Health Sciences Education. [doi:10.1007/s10459-015-9610-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-015-9610-4)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Cohort comparison at Erasmus MC of first-year GPA converted to z-scores, analyzed by Welch ANOVA with post hoc Bonferroni tests across the three admission groups; the Welch F for GPA was 1.765, not significant, and no effect size was printed for GPA. The article reports "No statistically significant differences were found".

> "No statistically significant differences were found between the non-cognitive selected, the cognitive selected, and the lottery-admitted students in first-year GPA"
