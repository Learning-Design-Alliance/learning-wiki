Evidence¶

Rosegard 2013¶

Rosegard, E., & Wilson, J. (2013). Capturing students' attention: An empirical study. Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, Vol. 13, No. 5, December 2013. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1017063

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the same four-year randomized experiment, an ANOVA compared exam scores across the four yearly external stimulus types (poem1, game, puzzle, poem2). The article states "The mean difference among each external stimulus was not statistically significant (p>.05)", so the benefit appears attributable to the stimulus itself rather than a specific form. No effect size is printed.

"An ANOVA was performed to test if there were differences between external stimulus types (i.e., poem1, puzzle, game, poem2). The mean difference among each external stimulus was not statistically significant (p>.05)."
