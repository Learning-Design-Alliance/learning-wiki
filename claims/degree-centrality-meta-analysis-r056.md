## Evidence

### Saqr and López-Pernas 2022

Mohammed Saqr, Sonsoles López-Pernas. (2022). The Curious Case of Centrality Measures: A Large-Scale Empirical Investigation. Journal of Learning Analytics. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7415

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Multi-level correlation meta-analysis pooling 69 course offerings found "r=0.56 [CI 0.52:0.60] for degree" with moderate heterogeneity (I²=55%). The authors conclude degree centralities have "a combined correlation coefficient of medium strength, a reasonable predictive interval, and low heterogeneity."

> "The random-effects model combined correlation coefficients for the degree and out-degree centralities were proximate. So were the confidence intervals (r=0.56 [CI 0.52:0.60] for degree; r=0.55 [CI 0.51:0.59] for out-degree)"
