## Evidence

### Glodowski 2025

Glodowski, K. R., & Hayashi, Y. (2025). Toward cross‐disciplinary translation of the testing effect: A systematic replication. Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, 58(3), 653–663. https://doi.org/10.1002/jaba.70012

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of quiz scores in the classroom study: mean quiz scores were 83.56% (5Q) and 79.72% (10Q), and "the quiz scores did not differ significantly between the two conditions, F(1, 14) = 0.48, p = .502, ηp2 = .033". Visual analysis showed substantial variability across participants.
