# The processing speed deficit in adults with ASD appears on each of the three standardized subtests: Trail Making A, Symbol Coding, and Category Fluency

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` ASD participants were significantly slower on Trail Making Test Part A than controls (d = .84). [→ Haigh Sarah M. 2018](#haigh-sarah-m-2018)
`q3 i3` ASD participants completed significantly fewer Symbol Coding items in 90 s than controls (d = -.90). [→ Haigh Sarah M. 2018 (2)](#haigh-sarah-m-2018-2)

### Haigh Sarah M. 2018

Haigh Sarah M., Walsh Jennifer A., Mazefsky Carla A., Minshew Nancy J., Eack Shaun M. (2018). Processing Speed is Impaired in Adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder, and Relates to Social Communication Abilities. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-018-3515-z

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r2`

ANCOVA of Trail Making Test Part A completion time in the same 76 ASD vs 64 control case-control sample, controlling for age, race, and education. ASD mean was 32.82 s versus 23.76 s in controls; the article reports "F(1, 134) = 22.35, p = < .001, d = .84".

> "The ASD group showed significantly slower completion time on the Trail Making Test (M = 32.82 s, SE = 2.08) compared to the control group (M = 23.76 s, SE = 2.07); F(1, 134) = 22.35, p = < .001, d = .84"

> "completed significantly fewer items in 90 s on the Symbol Coding task (M = 54.40, SE = 2.36) compared to the control group (M = 65.38, SE = 2.36) after controlling for age, race, and education level, F(1, 134) = 25.38, p < .001, d = − .90"
