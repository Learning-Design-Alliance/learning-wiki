Marianne Lykke, Mayela Coto, Christian Jantzen, Sonia Mora, Niels Vandel. (2015). Motivating Students Through Positive Learning Experiences: A Comparison of Three Learning Designs for Computer Programming Courses. `q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Questionnaire comparison of insignificance ratings across the three learning designs; the authors found it interesting that "there is no one in the Control group who felt their course was insignificant".

> "The amount of students who felt their course type was insignificant is only 1% in the PBL+LM course and 3% in the PBL course, cf. fig. 9. Interestingly, there is no one in the Control group who felt their course was insignificant."
