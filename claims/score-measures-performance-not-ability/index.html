Evidence¶

Monroe 1917¶

Monroe, W. S. (1917). A report on the use of the Kansas Silent Reading Tests with over one hundred thousand children. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Kansas+Silent+Reading+Tests

q1 · i? · associational · r2

Theoretical methodological argument by the author, not an experimental test. He lists conditions including time allowance, physical conditions, lighting, and the emotional status of the pupil, and warns that examiners may materially affect scores.

"A score represents only a pupil's performance at a given time under given conditions. Any change of conditions will produce corresponding changes in his performance."
