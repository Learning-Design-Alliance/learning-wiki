## Evidence

### Popa 2022

Popa, D. M., Oprea, S.-V., & Bâra, A. (2022). Learning after COVID-19 and the ICT career aspirations: Are students entering the AI era with weaker skills? Department of Economic Informatics and Cybernetics, Bucharest University of Economic Studies. simona.oprea@csie.ase.ro. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27391 [doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.27391](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2605.27391)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Score-based (BIC, hill-climbing) Bayesian network over quantile-discretized country-level aspiration changes from PISA 2018–2022. The null structure result supports interpreting ICT aspirations as a distinct, autonomous domain; no effect size is printed.

> "The learned structure contains no edges, indicating that changes in ICT aspirations are not systematically linked to contemporaneous changes in health, science and engineering, or science-related technical career expectations."
