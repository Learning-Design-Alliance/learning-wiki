`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Chi-square analysis of test answers from a six-month quasi-experiment with fourth-grade pupils in nature and society classes. "Twice as many students in the control group stated that they do not think about connecting previously acquired knowledge with new." No effect size printed.

> "In the tasks where the students were expected to write how the solution method could be connected to previously acquired knowledge, the chi- square test showed a statistically significant difference in the answers between the pupils in the experimental and control groups (χ2(4)=20.546, on the level  0,05 р=0.000)."
