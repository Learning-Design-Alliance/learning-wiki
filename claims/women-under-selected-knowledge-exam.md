## Evidence

### Abbiati Milena 2016

Abbiati Milena, Baroffio Anne, Gerbase Margaret W. (2016). Personal profile of medical students selected through a knowledge-based exam only: are we missing suitable students? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29705

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Among the 311 analyzed students, 118 of 198 women (60%) and 85 of 113 men (77%) were selected over the 2-year follow-up; the chi-square test of the selection-rate difference was significant. No effect size is printed.

> "The difference in the rates of selection between women and men was statistically significant (chi-square, 7.75; p=0.005)."

### Abbiati Milena 2016 (2)

Abbiati Milena, Baroffio Anne, Gerbase Margaret W. (2016). Personal profile of medical students selected through a knowledge-based exam only: are we missing suitable students? Medical Education Online. https://doi.org/10.3402/meo.v21.29705

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

The multivariate ANOVA on the six profile facets found no selection-by-gender interaction, and the logistic regression odds ratio for male gender (1.6) was not statistically significant (p=0.178). The authors note limited statistical power after stratification by sex.

> "However, we found no interaction effect between selection and gender, and the OR of gender on selection, although of 1.6 in favor of male students, was not statistically significant."
