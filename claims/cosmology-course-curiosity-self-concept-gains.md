## Evidence

### A. Gasparini 2025

A. Gasparini, A. Müller, F. Stern, L. Weiss. (2025). Cosmology and general relativity in upper secondary school through new targeted teaching materials: a study on student learning and motivation. Originally published 2024. [doi:10.1103/ss9c-lkpj](https://doi.org/10.1103/ss9c-lkpj)

`q3 · i2` · `design · r2`

Pre-post affective questionnaire (28 statements, 6-point scale, N = 70) with repeated-measures t-tests and Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment; the article reports "curiosity state (t(69) = 3.34, p = 0.001, d = 0.46)" and self-concept d = 0.26 at p = 0.090.

> "We observe an increase in the averages for curiosity state (t(69) = 3.34, p = 0.001, d = 0.46) and self-concept (t(69) = 1.72, p = 0.090, d = 0.26)"
