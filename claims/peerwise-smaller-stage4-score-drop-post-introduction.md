# Stage 4 exam score drop was significantly smaller in cohorts after PeerWise introduction than in cohorts before it

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Comparing pre-PeerWise cohorts (2012, 2013) and post-PeerWise cohorts (2015, 2016) using Stage 4 minus Stage 3 difference scores, the score drop was significantly smaller post-PeerWise. [→ Guilding C 2021](#guilding-c-2021)

## Evidence

### Guilding C 2021

Guilding C, Pye RE, Butler S, Atkinson M, Field E. (2021). Answering questions in a co-created formative exam question bank improves summative exam performance, while students perceive benefits from answering, authoring, and peer discussion: A mixed methods analysis of PeerWise. Pharmacol Res Perspect. https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.833

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Repeated-measures ANOVA of Stage 3 and 4 results across two pre-PeerWise cohorts (2012, 2013) and two post-PeerWise cohorts (2015, 2016), followed by a one-way ANOVA with a priori contrasts on difference scores. No effect size printed.

> "There was a significant difference, with a smaller score drop post‐PeerWise than pre‐PeerWise (t(1563) = 10.46, p < .001), implying a significant benefit of PeerWise on Stage 4 exam performance."
