Distribution analysis of final scores across five evaluation levels for both groups (Table 5); RFPETM students concentrated in the excellent band while TPETM students concentrated in good and slightly good bands.

> "the excellent proportion of the RFPETM group (69.77%, n = 90) was higher than that of the TPETM group (4.62%, n = 6). The proportions of good (22.48%, n = 29) and slightly good (7.75%, n = 10) in the RFPETM group were lower than those in the TPETM group (good 56.15%, n = 73; slightly good 37.69%, n = 49)."
