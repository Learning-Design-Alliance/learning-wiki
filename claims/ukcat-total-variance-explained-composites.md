### Sartania Nana 2014

Sartania Nana, McClure John D, Sweeting Helen, Browitt Allison. (2014). Predictive power of UKCAT and other pre-admission measures for performance in a medical school in Glasgow: a cohort study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6920-14-116

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Bivariate multiple regression analyses (Table 3) of the 2007 Glasgow cohort: UKCAT total beta 0.216 (p = 0.003) for EPM and beta 0.251 (p = 0.001) for H&C unadjusted, "explaining around 5% of the variance in the EPM and 6% of that in H&C".
