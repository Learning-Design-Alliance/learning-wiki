### Yanghee Kim · Michael Tscholl 2021

Yanghee Kim · Michael Tscholl. (2021). Young children's embodied interactions with a social robot. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09978-3

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Observation reported in the Issues and recommendations discussion: the robot's front acted as the center stage for on-task activity and its back as a peripheral space for momentary off-task behavior. The authors interpret this differentiation as supporting self-regulation of engagement.

> "In our studies, the robot's front served as the center stage where on-task activity took place. The robot's back produced a peripheral space the children used for momentary off-task behavior."
