## Evidence

### Paudel 2026

Paudel, K. R., Sotto, M., Jack-Edwards, F., & Shetty, P. (2026). Correlation of NBME pharmacology subject exam performance with medical school internal pharmacology scores and academic metrics. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-57653-5

`q2 · i3` · `associational · r2`

Multiple linear regression on 290 students with NBME score as dependent variable; GPA (β = −0.50, p = 0.70) and BCPM (β = 0.90, p = 0.40) were not statistically significant predictors.

> "Multiple linear regression analysis (Table 2) demonstrated that internal pharmacology exam score (β = 0.60, p < 0.001) and MCAT score (β = 0.30, p < 0.001) were significant positive predictors of NBME Pharmacology subject exam performance."
