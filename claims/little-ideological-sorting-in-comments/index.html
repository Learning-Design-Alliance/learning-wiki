Yeomans 2018¶

Yeomans, M., Stewart, B. M., Mavon, K., Kindel, A., Tingley, D., & Reich, J. (2018). The Civic Mission of MOOCs: Engagement across Political Differences in Online Forums. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 28, 553–589. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0161-0

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Non-parametric correlation analysis of 2,587 reply-comment pairs in course-created content threads (Table 6), split by substantive vs. short-yes comments and high vs. low partisan salience. "little evidence for ideology-based sorting in the forum comments"; most correlations were not significantly different from zero.

"In general, we find little evidence for ideology-based sorting in the forum comments. That is, most of these correlations are not significantly different from zero, which indicates that partisanship is having no aggregate effect on how commenters sort themselves among various replies."
