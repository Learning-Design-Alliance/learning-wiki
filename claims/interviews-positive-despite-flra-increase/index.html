Evidence¶

Çapan and Pektaş 2013¶

Seyit Ahmet Çapan & Rumeysa Pektaş. (2013). An Empirical Analysis of the Relationship between Foreign Language Reading Anxiety and Reading Strategy Training. English Language Teaching, 6(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n12p181

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Semi-structured interviews with six of the 20 experiment-group volunteers, conducted in L1 one week after the semester ended to reduce researcher bias. One participant, Ayşe, said learning strategies "increased my reading pace and helped me understand reading passages in English more easily".

"In contrast to the statistical findings, the interviews were shaped by positive comments about the use of instructions on reading strategies."

Çapan and Pektaş 2013 (2)¶

Seyit Ahmet Çapan & Rumeysa Pektaş. (2013). An Empirical Analysis of the Relationship between Foreign Language Reading Anxiety and Reading Strategy Training. English Language Teaching, 6(12). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n12p181

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Interviewee Seda's criticism of the academic texts echoes Saito et al.'s (1999) proposition that text and topic types affect FLRA; another interviewee, Ali, reported hesitating to ask the instructor questions about strategies, supporting Wu's (2011) claim about instructor impact.

"It was the first time I read such acade mic texts. Actually, they were difficult for me to understand."
