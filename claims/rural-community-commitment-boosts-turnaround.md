Rural Turnaround: Challenges and Opportunities. (2014). WestEd R&D Alert, Vol. 15, No. 1. https://centeronschoolturnaround.org/journeys

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r1`

The CST handbook chapter on rural turnaround argues that people are at the heart of rural communities and schools: a rural school is often a community gathering place and "point of pride for the community," and community investment "can help tremendously to support student motivation and success."

> "The strength and commitment of local community members in a rural area can provide one of the biggest boosts to school turnaround efforts. There are likely to be strong connections and ample opportunities for interaction among staff, parents, and community members, which can provide important support to a school."
