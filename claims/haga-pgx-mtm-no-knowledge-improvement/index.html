q2 · i? · review · r3

Cluster-RCT of community pharmacists in the United States comparing in-person pharmacogenetics training plus medication therapy management integration against training only or testing without MTM, measured by seven multiple-choice questions pre and postintervention. The review reports "No significant knowledge improvement in any group"; equivalence was not tested.
