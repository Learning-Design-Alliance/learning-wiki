Evidence¶

Meijuan Xie 2025¶

Meijuan Xie, Liling Luo. (2025). The Status Quo and Future of AI-TPACK for Mathematics Teacher Education Students: A Case Study in Chinese Universities. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13533 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2503.13533

q2 · i3 · associational · r?

AI-TPACK-SEM path analysis (N=412). The model fit was x2/df = 3.344, RMSEA = 0.076 after deleting insignificant paths H2b and H3c, indicating AI-TK does not directly advance AI-TPACK.

"AI-TK was positively associated with AI-TPK ( β = 0.62) and AI-TCK (β = 0.35)."

Meijuan Xie 2025 (2)¶

Meijuan Xie, Liling Luo. (2025). The Status Quo and Future of AI-TPACK for Mathematics Teacher Education Students: A Case Study in Chinese Universities. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13533

q2 · i3 · associational · r?

AI-TPACK-SEM path analysis (N=412) showing AI-TCK's standardized path "β = 0.79" to AI-TPACK is the strongest component path, with AI-TPK contributing β = 0.19; the authors conclude the AI-TPACK elements are interrelated and mutually reinforcing.

"We also revealed that both AI-TPK (β = 0.19) and AI-TCK (β = 0.79) were positively related to AI-TPACK, supporting H4 and H5."
