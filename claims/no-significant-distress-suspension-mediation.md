# Psychological distress shows no significant total or mediated association with suspensions among Black adolescents

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r3` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For suspensions, neither the total effect nor the sum of indirect effects through developmental assets was statistically significant for either perceived stress or depressive symptoms, and no asset was a significant mediator. [→ Rose 2024](#rose-2024)

## Evidence

### Rose 2024

Rose, T., Joe, S., & Hancock, G. R. (2024). Mediators that Matter: Psychological Distress, Developmental Assets, and Educational Outcomes among Black Youth. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000876

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Null mediation result from the NSAL-A path analysis (n = 1,170): for both perceived stress and depressive symptoms, total, indirect, and direct effects on suspensions were not statistically significant. Equivalence was not tested; no effect size was printed for this finding.

> "Regarding suspensions, for perceived stress the bootstrap confidence interval revealed that neither the total effect nor the sum of all indirect effects on suspensions were statistically significant"
