Disability-category trend analysis of CPS ninth graders, 1993–94 to 1999–00 (Figure 5). The report attributes the increase "almost entirely" to learning disability identification, with the percent of ninth graders with learning disabilities rising from 7.2 to 11.4 while other classifications changed little.

"The increase in the percent of ninth graders eligible for special education services is almost entirely the result of an increase in the percent of students identified as having a learning disability"
