Agor 2014¶

John T. Agor. (2014). Language-Learner Strategy Instruction and English Achievement: Voices from Ghana. English Language Teaching, 7(6). http://dx.doi.org/10.5539/elt.v7n6p175

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative analysis of video-taped reading behaviour during the post-intervention task. Student 16 monitored his reading and derived meaning from context, detecting that "expanse" and "infracture" were miswritten; control student F decoded "assured" as /a+ssu+red/, leading to mispronunciation.
