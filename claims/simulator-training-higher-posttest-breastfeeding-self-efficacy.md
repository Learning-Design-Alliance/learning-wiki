## Subclaims
`q3 i?` One week after training, breastfeeding self-efficacy posttest scores were significantly higher in the simulator group (63.38 ± 5.19) than in the video group (57.21 ± 9.24) and the control group (57.09 ± 9.04). ## Evidence

### Tetik Metin Hatice & Yiğit Feride 2025

Tetik Metin Hatice & Yiğit Feride. (2025). Comparison of the effectiveness of video and breastfeeding simulator support to mothers who could not breastfeed their babies in the neonatal intensive care unit: a randomized controlled study. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12486698/

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomized controlled pretest/posttest trial of 100 mothers of NICU babies in a mother hotel, randomized to video, simulator, or control groups via sealed envelopes. ANOVA with Tukey comparisons found simulator posttest mean 63.38 ± 5.19 versus video 57.21 ± 9.24 and control 57.09 ± 9.04; "The breastfeeding self-efficacy posttest mean scores were significantly different (F: 6.77; p < 0.05)." No effect size printed.
