# Chi-square analysis rejected the null hypothesis for teamwork-skill improvement across all, male and female student responses

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` For the question on whether the mobile application development project in groups helped improve teamwork skills, the chi-square statistics for all students (84.27), male students (27.75) and female students (11.17) each exceeded the critical value, so the null was rejected in all three analyses. ### Khandakar 2021

Khandakar, A., Chowdhury, M. E. H., Khalid, M. S., and Zorba, N. (2021). Case study of Innovative Teaching Practices and their Impact for Electrical Engineering Courses during COVID-19 Pandemic. Originally published 2020. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2107.00746](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2107.00746)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Chi-square analysis of survey responses (Table 3) at α = 5% with critical value 9.49. The teamwork question was the only one where the null was rejected for all students (84.27), male students (27.75) and female students (11.17) alike; the authors read the Table 3 results as verification of a positive response to the innovations.
