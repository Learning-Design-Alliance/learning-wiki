## Evidence

### Felsenreich DM 2025

Felsenreich DM, Batista Dantas AC, Shahabi S, Abdelbaki TN, Zakeri R, Nagy ME, Gero D, Ruiz-Úcar E, Dong Z-Y, Yang W, Jenkins M, Chiappetta S, on behalf of Young-IFSO Collaborative Group. (2025). Young-IFSO Endoscopy Training and Education Survey. Obesity Surgery. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11695-025-07915-4

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional online survey of 273 Young-IFSO members (surgeons aged ≤45) from 49 countries, run September–December 2024 with a 40-question questionnaire. Self-reported training data show "only 26.5% had access to training for more than six months" and 30.8% performed no procedures during residency.

> "Half of the respondents reported having endoscopic education integrated into their general surgery residency curriculum; however, only 26.5% had access to training for more than six months (Fig. 1A). Notably, 30.8% did not perform any endoscopic procedures during residency."
