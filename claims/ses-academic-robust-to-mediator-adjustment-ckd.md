Sensitivity analysis (Table 3) refitting the regressions with additional potential mediator/confounder covariates. The lowest-quartile estimates "0.37 (0.15–0.87) for numeracy, and 0.25 (0.10–0.62) for literacy" were similar to the main models.

> "With reference to the highest SES index quartile, adjusted ORs (95%CI) for better performance by SES quartile in descending order were 1.02 (0.48–2.17), 0.65 (0.30–1.41), and 0.37 (0.15–0.87) for numeracy, and 0.66 (0.30–1.46), 0.55 (0.24–1.24), and 0.25 (0.10–0.62) for literacy."
