McManus IC 2013¶

McManus IC, Dewberry Chris, Nicholson Sandra, Dowell Jonathan S, Woolf Katherine, Potts Henry WW. (2013). Construct-level predictive validity of educational attainment and intellectual aptitude tests in medical student selection: meta-regression of six UK longitudinal studies. BMC Medicine. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3827328/

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Descriptive and power analysis of the 57 raw predictor-outcome correlations in Table 1. The article reports mean unweighted r = .171, the random-effects overall .171 (CI .147–.195), and states "a sample size of 290 would be needed" for 90% power, with n = 189 for the largest single correlation.

"The mean sample size is 935, and the mean unweighted correlation .171, and an overall effect in a random effects meta-analysis of .171 (CI: .147 to .195). The effect size is therefore small, and for P < .05, with 90% power of finding a significant effect in a one-tailed test, a sample size of 290 would be needed"
