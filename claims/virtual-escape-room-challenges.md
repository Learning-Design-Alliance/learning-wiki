`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review's challenges section reports, citing included studies, that "Online platforms such as Zoom meeting ER and Google Meet" are "vulnerable to malfunction", that low survey response rates limit efficacy data, and that without in-person facilitators assessment integrity is hard to monitor.

> "Online platforms such as Zoom meeting ER and Google Meet, which are commonly used for virtual sessions, are vulnerable to malfunction, which can disrupt appropriate learning."
