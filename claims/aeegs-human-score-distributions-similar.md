### Olaoye DD 2026

Olaoye DD, Owolabi HO and Olaoye OT (2026). Automated software scoring of senior school certificate examination mathematical items in economics using a contextual similarity model. Front. Educ. 11:1669504. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1669504

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive frequency analysis of AEEGS scores for all 1,008 students, grouped into five levels using the standard normal curve. The article reports 598 (59.3%) in the good band and 68.8% of scores within good, very good and excellent combined.

> "the 7 (0.7%) of the students had excellent scores, 89 (8.8%) and 598 (59.3%) of the students had very good and good scores, while less than half of the students' scores falls within fair and low with 181 (18.0%) and 133 (13.2%) respectively."

### Olaoye DD 2026 (2)

Olaoye DD, Owolabi HO and Olaoye OT (2026). Automated software scoring of senior school certificate examination mathematical items in economics using a contextual similarity model. Front. Educ. 11:1669504. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1669504

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Descriptive frequency analysis of the 12 human raters' scores for the same 1,008 students, grouped into the same five levels. The article reports 633 (62.8%) good and states the scores are "normally distributed, with majority of the scores cluster around the mean".

> "Out of 1,008 students, 5 (0.5%) of them had excellent scores, 71 (7.0%) of them had very good scores, 633 (62.8%) had good scores, 183 (18.2%) had fair scores, and 116 (11.5%) of them had low scores"
