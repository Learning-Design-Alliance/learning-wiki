# Severe postpartum haemorrhage (≥1000 ml), maternal mortality, and neonatal mortality did not change after training

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` No difference was seen in PPH with blood loss more than 1000 ml (0.4% before and after training), and maternal and neonatal mortality at 24 h did not change. [→ Nelissen Ellen 2017](#nelissen-ellen-2017)

## Evidence

### Nelissen Ellen 2017

Nelissen Ellen, Ersdal Hege, Mduma Estomih, Evjen-Olsen Bjørg, Twisk Jos, Broerse Jacqueline, van Roosmalen Jos, Stekelenburg Jelle. (2017). Clinical performance and patient outcome after simulation-based training in prevention and management of postpartum haemorrhage: an educational intervention study in a low-resource setting. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-017-1481-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Null findings from the same before-after observational study: severe PPH was 0.4% in both periods (p = 0.93); maternal deaths within 24 h were n = 1 before versus n = 2 after (p = 0.86) and neonatal mortality did not change. The article states the study was not powered to investigate maternal mortality (approximately 70,000 births would be needed).

> "No difference was seen in the group with blood loss more than 1000 ml (before training 0.4%, after training 0.4%)."
