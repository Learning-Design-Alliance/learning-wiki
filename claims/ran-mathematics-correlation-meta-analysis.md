## Evidence

### Koponen 2017

Koponen, T., Georgiou, G., Salmi, P., Leskinen, M., & Aro, M. (2017). A Meta-Analysis of the Relation Between RAN and Mathematics. Journal of Educational Psychology, 109(7), 977-992. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000182

`q4 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Systematic meta-analysis following PRISMA with random-effects REML estimation and robust variance estimation, pooling 151 correlations from 33 unique samples (7,135 participants). It reports "a significant correlation between RAN and mathematics (r = .37; 95% confidence interval [CI] .33–.42)" with large heterogeneity (Q(32) = 136.51, p < .001).

> "The results showed a significant correlation between RAN and mathematics (r = .37; 95% confidence interval [CI] .33–.42) as well as a large heterogeneity of individual correlations."
