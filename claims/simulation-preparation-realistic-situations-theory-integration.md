Theme 2 'Preparation for realistic situations' from the qualitative interviews. Students said facilitator interactions and criticism during sessions improved critical thinking and reasoning; one participant said asking why actions were taken "aids with memory recall" and provided more opportunities to gain theory and knowledge.

> "Students found simulation essential to integrating and applying their theoretical knowledge to clinical practice. During the simulation, they believed the facilitators improved their knowledge and abilities by encouraging them to think critically and reason more."
