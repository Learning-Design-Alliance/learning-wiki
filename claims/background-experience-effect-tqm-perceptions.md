# School background experience affects TQM perceptions, with 11-15-year staff less positive in cultural and human-resource dimensions; professional seniority shows no significant difference

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Participants with 11-15 years of school background experience rated TQM applicability lower than the 1-5 and 6-10 year groups in cultural structure (p = .026) and human resources (p = .045), with no significant difference in management structure (p = .167). [→ Altunay 2016](#altunay-2016)
`q2 i?` Professional seniority showed no significant difference in perceptions of TQM applicability. [→ Altunay 2016 (2)](#altunay-2016-2)

## Evidence

### Altunay 2016

Esen Altunay. (2016). The Effect of Training with TQM on the Perceptions of Teachers about the Quality of Schools. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2126-2133. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040925

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis H tests on post-test scores by school background experience (17, 3 and 5 participants as printed in Table 6); cultural structure X2 = 7.305, p = .026 and human resources X2 = 6.206, p = .045 were significant, management structure was not (p = .167).

> "the perceptions of the participants who had 11- 15 years about the applicability of TQM principles were less than 1-5 years and 6 -10 years group in the dimensions of the cultural structure and human resources."

### Altunay 2016 (2)

Esen Altunay. (2016). The Effect of Training with TQM on the Perceptions of Teachers about the Quality of Schools. Universal Journal of Educational Research 4(9): 2126-2133. https://doi.org/10.13189/ujer.2016.040925

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Kruskal-Wallis H test on post-test scores by professional seniority found no significant difference; the article states perceptions showed similar features across seniority groups. No effect size is printed.

> "According to professional seniority the result of Kruskal-Wallis H test was determined no significant difference on the applicability of TQM principles."
