## Evidence

### Nye 2004

Nye, B., Konstantopoulos, S., & Hedges, L. V. (2004). How Large Are Teacher Effects? Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 26(3), 237-257. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=How+Large+Are+Teacher+Effects

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Randomization validity checks reported for the STAR experiment, comparing pre-assignment student variables and teacher characteristics across treatment types within schools; the authors state they replicated Krueger's results. No effect sizes are printed.

> "He found that across three variables (SES, minority group sta- tus, and age), there were no significant differences between treatment types. Krueger also found that there were no significant differences across treat- ment types on the teacher characteristics of race, experience, and education"
