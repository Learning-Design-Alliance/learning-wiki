## Evidence

### Klegeris Andis 2013

Klegeris Andis, Bahniwal Manpreet, Hurren Heather. (2013). Improvement in Generic Problem-Solving Abilities of Students by Use of Tutor-less Problem-Based Learning in a Large Classroom Setting. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.12-06-0081

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Descriptive comparison of absolute course mean scores across the four courses at beginning and end of term; the authors interpret the BIOC ranking change as evidence the improvement should not be attributed to BIOC students lagging behind their peers at the start.

> "the problem-solving abilities of students exposed to PBL (as assessed by their absolute scores on the previously described problem-solving tests) were second highest at the beginning of the term and improved to first place standing at the end of the term"
