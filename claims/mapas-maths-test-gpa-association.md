## Evidence

### Curtis E 2015

Curtis E, Wikaire E, Jiang Y, McMillan L, Loto R, Airini, Reid P. (2015). Quantitative analysis of a Māori and Pacific admission process on first-year health study. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-015-0470-7

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression of 368 First Year Tertiary students showed "MAPAS Maths test (per 10 % increase) 0.14 (0.02, 0.26) 0.0186" for GPA eight courses; the logistic model for passing all courses was non-significant (OR 1.13, CI 0.95-1.33, p = 0.179).
