## Evidence

### Han Mingyu 2025

Han Mingyu, Shi Wei, Yu Ling, Yuan Tianming, Zhang Yuanyuan. (2025). Stamp collecting game improves internship performance and experience in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07764-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Multiple regression (SPSS 27.0) with attendance rate included, reported in Table 4 (F = 7.027, p < 0.001). The article states the game "explains 13.1% (R2 = 0.131)" with β = 0.398.

> "The stamp collecting game explains 13.1% (R2 = 0.131) of the increase in the total score of self-evaluation, resulting in a 0.398 times increase (β = 0.398)."

### Han Mingyu 2025 (2)

Han Mingyu, Shi Wei, Yu Ling, Yuan Tianming, Zhang Yuanyuan. (2025). Stamp collecting game improves internship performance and experience in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-07764-w

`q2 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Regression on post-rotation neonatal test scores (Table 4: R = 0.457, F = 12.027, p < 0.001). The game "accounted for 19.2% (R2 = 0.192)" of the increase, β = 0.447.

> "After the rotation, the stamp collecting game accounted for 19.2% (R2 = 0.192) of the increase in the level of test scores in the experimental group, which represented a 0.447 times improvement compared to control group."
