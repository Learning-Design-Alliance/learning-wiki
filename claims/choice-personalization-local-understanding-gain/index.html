Evidence¶

Ilana Ram 2024¶

Ilana Ram, Sara Harris, & Ido Roll. (2024). Choice-based Personalization in MOOCs: Impact on Activity and Perceived Value. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00334-5

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

ANCOVA on exit-survey self-reported understanding of local issues with entry-survey understanding as covariate; F(1,142) = 11.59, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.075, holding under Bonferroni correction, "a medium effect". Lower scores meant greater reported understanding (2.99 vs. 3.40).

"With regard to local climate issues, the Personalized group reported significantly greater understanding compared to the Generic group: F(1,142) = 11.59, p < 0.001, ηp 2 = 0.075"

Ilana Ram 2024 (2)¶

Ilana Ram, Sara Harris, & Ido Roll. (2024). Choice-based Personalization in MOOCs: Impact on Activity and Perceived Value. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00334-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Parallel ANCOVA on self-reported understanding of global climate issues found no effect of condition (ηp2 = 0.007, not significant); the entry survey was not a significant predictor either.

"There was no effect for personalization on global issues: F(1, 143) = 0.996, p = 0.32, ηp 2 = 0.007"
