## Evidence

### Keller 2020

Keller, L., Preckel, F., & Brunner, M. (2020). Nonlinear Relations Between Achievement and Academic Self-Concepts in Elementary and Secondary School: An Integrative Data Analysis Across 13 Countries. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000533

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Quadratic regressions on representative TIMSS/PIRLS 2011 Grade 4 data across 13 countries, integrated by meta-analysis. The printed coefficient "mean β = 0.04" is negligible in magnitude, though its 95% CI excluded zero.

> "In younger students, the mean quadratic relation between mathematics achievement and mathematics self-concept was positive (mean β = 0.04, 95% CI [0.03, 0.06])."

### Keller 2020 (2)

Keller, L., Preckel, F., & Brunner, M. (2020). Nonlinear Relations Between Achievement and Academic Self-Concepts in Elementary and Secondary School: An Integrative Data Analysis Across 13 Countries. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000533

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r3`

Country-level significance counts from the Grade 4 quadratic regressions reported in the results section: "in 13 out of 25 cases, the quadratic relations were significant", with significant heterogeneity in linear and quadratic relations.

> "across countries, positive quadratic relations were found less consistently for younger students than for older students; in 13 out of 25 cases, the quadratic relations were significant"

### Keller 2020 (3)

Keller, L., Preckel, F., & Brunner, M. (2020). Nonlinear Relations Between Achievement and Academic Self-Concepts in Elementary and Secondary School: An Integrative Data Analysis Across 13 Countries. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000533

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Interrupted regressions with the two-lines test on TIMSS Grade 4 mathematics data; slopes were "on average almost identical in magnitude for higher and lower achieving students", though three countries (Australia, Portugal, Sweden in TIMSS 2011) showed a near-zero relation for lower achievers.

> "For Grade 4, the achievement-related increase in mathematics self-concept was on average almost identical in magnitude for higher and lower achieving students (mean β1 = 0.32, 95% CI [0.26, 0.39]; mean β2 = 0.34, 95% CI [0.29, 0.38]; Figure 1b)."

### Keller 2020 (4)

Keller, L., Preckel, F., & Brunner, M. (2020). Nonlinear Relations Between Achievement and Academic Self-Concepts in Elementary and Secondary School: An Integrative Data Analysis Across 13 Countries. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000533

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Interrupted regressions with the two-lines test on representative PIRLS Grade 4 reading data. The printed coefficient for lower achievers, "mean β1 = 0.45", exceeded that for higher achievers; only 5 of 26 country-level quadratic relations were significant, and for 13% of students a linear model did not describe the relation well.

> "The relation between achievement and self-concept in reading was stronger for lower achieving students than for higher achieving students in Grade 4 (mean β1 = 0.45, 95% CI [0.42, 0.48]; mean β2 = 0.27, 95% CI [0.22, 0.31]; Figure 1b)."
