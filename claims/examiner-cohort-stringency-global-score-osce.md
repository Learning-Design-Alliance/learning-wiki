# In a fully nested multi-circuit OSCE, examiner cohorts differ substantially in stringency or leniency on global scores, with a maximal model-adjusted difference of 0.47 out of 7.0 (Cohen's d = 0.96) for students of the same ability

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Many-facet Rasch modelling of a Year-3 OSCE showed examiner cohorts differed in stringency/leniency by up to 0.47 of 7.0 global score points for the same student ability, a Cohen's d of 0.96. [→ Yeates P 2019](#yeates-p-2019)

## Evidence

### Yeates P 2019

Yeates P, Cope N, Hawarden A, Bradshaw H, McCray G, Homer M. (2019). Developing a video‐based method to compare and adjust examiner effects in fully nested OSCEs. Medical Education, 53(3), 250–263. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.13783

`q3 · i3` · `design · r2`

Many-facet Rasch modelling of 116 students' global scores across eight examiner cohorts in a four-lane OSCE, modelling student, station and cohort facets. "a difference of 0.47 out of 7.0" for cohorts 3 versus 6, "a Cohen's d effect size of 0.96 for the maximal difference between examiner cohorts"; Rasch separation reliability 0.79.

> "Examiner cohorts differed in their stringency or leniency, with a model‐adjusted fair score of 4.53 out of 7.0 units of the assessment's global score for examiner‐cohort 3 and a model‐adjusted fair score of 5.00 out of 7.0 units of the assessment's global score for examiner‐cohort 6, a difference of 0.47 out of 7.0"
