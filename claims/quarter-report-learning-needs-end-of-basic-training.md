Self-directed learning in post-graduate medical education: Self-judgement and supervisor judgement of competence development in Austrian nine-month basic training. GMS Journal for Medical Education 41(4): Doc42. https://www.egms.de/en/journals/zma/2024-41/zma001697.shtml

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Frequency analysis of the 'training goal met' versus 'learning need' criterion (>90% mastery per competence group) at month 9 across the 147 logbooks; the authors read this as willingness to self-judge and state learning needs openly.

> "After nine months of basic training, most JPs (74.15%) considered the learning goal achieved. However, 25.85% of the JPs still perceived a need to learn over 10% of the learning objectives."

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Clinic-level comparison in table 1 of learning-need frequencies across the three WIGEV clinics; the 6.09 percentage-point difference was within the predefined maximum 10% range, taken to indicate comparable training quality.

> "At 23.08%, the least JPs in KDO reported a need to learn, while the most JPs in KLA reported a need to learn at 29.17%."
