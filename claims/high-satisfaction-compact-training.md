## Evidence

### Basu Andrea 2014

Basu Andrea, Kennedy Lynne, Tocque Karen, Jones Sharn. (2014). Eating for 1, Healthy and Active for 2; feasibility of delivering novel, compact training for midwives to build knowledge and confidence in giving nutrition, physical activity and weight management advice during pregnancy. BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2393-14-218

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Supplementary post-training satisfaction questions completed by the 32 attending midwives. Thematic analysis of free-text comments produced themes including Good Quality, Valued Resources, Relevant to Midwives, and Much to fit in.

> "When asked overall how they would rate the training, selecting a response from a 4-point scale (excellent/good/adequate/poor), 81% (n = 26) rated as excellent and the remaining 19% (n = 6) rated it as good. Furthermore when asked if they would recommend the training to others indicating with a simple yes/no response; all stated yes."
