Gates 1922¶

Conference report of Laura M. Chassell's (Ohio State University) predictive study; the printed mean correlations of approximately 0.6 (r = 0.6) with later-success criteria are reported as printed, with no other statistics.

"Laura M. Chassell, Ohio State University, found that grades received in the preliminary examination for the degree of Ph. D. and ratings of the Doctorate thesis gave mean correlations of approximately 0.6 with various criteria of success in later work."

Gates 1922 (2)¶

Gates, Arthur I. (1922). Educational psychology at the Princeton meeting of the American Psychological Association. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

Same conference report section on predictive value of tests; recommendation-letter judgments showed correlations from 0.01 to 0.70 depending on the letter's author, a range reported as printed with no summary effect size.

"Judgments based on letters of recommendation gave correlations with success ranging from 0.01 to 0.70, depending on the author of the letters."
