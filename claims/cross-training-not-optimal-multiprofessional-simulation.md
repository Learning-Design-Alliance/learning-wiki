### Sørensen Jette Led 2015

Sørensen Jette Led, Navne Laura Emdal, Martin Helle Max, Ottesen Bent, Albrecthsen Charlotte Krebs, Pedersen Berit Woetmann, Kjærgaard Hanne, van der Vleuten Cees. (2015). Clarifying the learning experiences of healthcare professionals with in situ and off-site simulation-based medical education: a qualitative study. BMJ Open. `q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Authors' interpretation from the focus groups with multiprofessional obstetric emergency teams: they "concluded that the authenticity and psychological fidelity would have been damaged by changing professional roles". The finding rests on participant perceptions of role caricature rather than measured team outcomes.

> "Since the simulations in the present study were complex and included teams of 10 from 6 different healthcare professional groups, we concluded that the authenticity and psychological fidelity would have been damaged by changing professional roles."
