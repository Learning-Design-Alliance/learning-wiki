EdTrust 2023¶

EdTrust. (2023). 5 Things for Advocates to Know About Chronic Absenteeism. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED627994

q2 · i? · review · r1

The brief cites research that "chronically absent students are less likely to read on grade-level by third grade" and face elevated suspension, dropout, and reduced postsecondary continuation risks; it also notes chronic absenteeism can affect social engagement starting as early as kindergarten.

Related Claims¶

  • Students chronically absent for multiple years between preschool and second grade have significantly lower second-grade reading outcomes, approaching the threshold for needing intensive reading intervention — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Chronically absent preschoolers are much more likely to stay chronically absent, and each successive year of chronic absenteeism compounds risk of low second-grade reading — related
  • Students who fail Algebra I show far more signs of ninth-grade disengagement, including higher absenteeism and suspension rates — related
  • Students in foster care are more than twice as likely to be chronically absent and more than four times as likely to be suspended as non-foster students — related
