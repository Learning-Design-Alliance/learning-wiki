## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Martins van Jaarsveld 2025

Martins van Jaarsveld, G., Wong, J., Baars, M., Specht, M., & Paas, F. (2025). Enhancing goal attainment in higher education with a scripted conversational agent: Effects of monitoring and reflection support in digital learning. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105441

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-design ANOVA on OSLQ-measured SRL skills (pre/post) in the same RCT found a significant time effect with η2p = .116; the article states this trend "was the same for all conditions".

> "Results revealed a significant main effect of time, F(1, 80) = 10.453, p = .002, η2p = .116, a moderate effect, indicating that SRL skills increased during the intervention."
