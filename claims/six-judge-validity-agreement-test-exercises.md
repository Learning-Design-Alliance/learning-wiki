# The test's exercises were validated by six expert judges who reached practical agreement after modification

## Evidence

### Herring 1918

Herring, J. P. (1918). Measurements of some abilities in scientific thinking. The Journal of Educational Psychology, IX(10), 535-558. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075736

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Validity study of the second edition's forty exercises: six judges (philosophy, history, mathematics, engineering) worked separately, spending from two to five hours each, and "At last the judges came to a practical agreement" after modification. No effect size is printed.

> "Each stated which, if any, of the abilities was tested by each exercise. Not even at first was there a discouraging amount of disagreement. Some exercises were discarded, some modified slightly, and some rebuilt. At last the judges came to a practical agreement."
