## Evidence

### Robinson Tracy 2017

Robinson Tracy, Janssen Anna, Kirk Judy, DeFazio Anna, Goodwin Annabel, Tucker Kathy, Shaw Timothy. (2017). New Approaches to Continuing Medical Education: a QStream (spaced education) Program for Research Translation in Ovarian Cancer. Journal of Cancer Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13187-015-0944-7

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Observational comparison of referral data at two metropolitan hospital sites over 12-month periods before and after the QStream program. The study reports the referral increases and notes the increase was not due to a rise in eligible diagnoses. No formal statistical analysis was possible with the small sample.

> "the number of women diagnosed with ovarian cancer that would be eligible for referral to an FCC in the two-time period was similar (97 pre and 110 post), as would be expected; however, there was a 1.8-fold increase in referrals at site A (26 referrals pre and 47 referrals post) and a 1.6-fold increase at site B (28 referrals pre and 46 referrals post)."
