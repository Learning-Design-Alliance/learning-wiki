## Evidence

### Roni Lekar 2025

Roni Lekar, Tatiana Gerth, Sergey Prokudin, Matthias Seibold, Reto Bürgin, Benjamin Vella, Armando Hoch, Siyu Tang, Philipp Fürnstahl, and Helmut Grabner. (2025). Enhancing Orthopedic Surgical Training With Interactive Photorealistic 3D Visualization. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-task questionnaire (NASA-TLX and SUS) in the RCT; the SUS averages were 65.98 for FV and 66.61 for IV. Average NASA-TLX workload was 7.3 on a 1–20 scale, described as moderate. No statistical test reported.

> "The FV group scored an average of 65.98, while the IV group scored 66.61 (on a 0–100 scale, where higher values in-dicate better usability)."

### Roni Lekar 2025 (2)

Roni Lekar, Tatiana Gerth, Sergey Prokudin, Matthias Seibold, Reto Bürgin, Benjamin Vella, Armando Hoch, Siyu Tang, Philipp Fürnstahl, and Helmut Grabner. (2025). Enhancing Orthopedic Surgical Training With Interactive Photorealistic 3D Visualization. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.13389)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Descriptive questionnaire results: "most participants enjoyed the simulation (64%)" and 75% believed it could enhance study materials; regular-use intent was 61% for IV vs 43% for FV. These are descriptive percentages with no inferential test.

> "Across both groups, most participants enjoyed the simulation (64%) and believed it could enhance their current study materials (75%)."
