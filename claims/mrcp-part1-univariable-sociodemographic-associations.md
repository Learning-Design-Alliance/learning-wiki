## Evidence

### Johnston Peter W. 2025

Johnston Peter W., Vieira Rute, Cameron Isobel M., Kumwenda Ben, Walker Kim A., Cleland Jennifer A. (2025). Big data analysis: examination of the relationship between candidates' sociodemographic characteristics and performance in the UK's Membership of the Royal College of Physicians Part 1 examination. Advances in Health Sciences Education 30(1), 53-68. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-024-10406-3

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Univariable chi-squared and rank tests in a retrospective longitudinal cohort of 6040 UK graduates sitting MRCP Part 1 before October 2019, comparing pass and fail groups. The study reports "statistically significant associations between MRCP Part 1 first sitting pass rates and age, sex, ethnicity" plus graduate status, parental occupation and school type, all p < 0.01.

> "We found statistically significant associations between MRCP Part 1 first sitting pass rates and age, sex, ethnicity, graduate status (but see later), parental occupation and school type (all p < 0.01)."
