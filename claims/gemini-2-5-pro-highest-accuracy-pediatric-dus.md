Kayaci Sukriye Turkoglu, Ilhan Hamza Osman, Tassoker Melek, Cap Taha, Demir Helin. (2026). Benchmarking GPT-5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Grok 4, and other LLMs on pediatric dentistry questions from a dental specialization exam. Scientific Reports. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-57392-7

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Benchmark of 119 pediatric dentistry MCQs from the DUS administered to 17 LLM configurations under zero-shot conditions. Gemini 2.5 Pro achieved "92.44%" accuracy, the highest of all models tested.

> "The Gemini 2.5 Pro (92.44%) demonstrated significantly higher mean scores compared to all other models except GPT-4 (78.15%), GPT-5 (90.76%), GPTOSS (78.99, 75.63%), and Grok-4 (88.24%)."
