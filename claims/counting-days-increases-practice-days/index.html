Evidence¶

Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick 2025¶

Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick. (2025). Counting days is a spacing incentive that unlocks the potential of low GPA students. npj Science of Learning 10:35. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00322-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Within-class randomized experiment (n = 143): the counting days group "practiced on more than twice as many days on average (38.5 vs. 17.3)"; Table 2 prints a t-test statistic of 20.436*** (p < 0.001) for Practiced Days. No effect size is printed.

"the counting days group practiced on more than twice as many days on average (38.5 vs. 17.3), but only answered a few more total questions (415 vs. 392.5)."

Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick 2025 (2)¶

Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick. (2025). Counting days is a spacing incentive that unlocks the potential of low GPA students. npj Science of Learning 10:35. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00322-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Between-instructor randomized experiment: 71 instructors teaching 1,648 students were randomized at the instructor level; counting days students averaged 17.4 vs. 12.0 practice days. Table 5 reports t = 5.616*** (p < 0.001).

"Students in the counting days condition averaged 17.4 days of practice vs. 12.0 in the counting questions condition, as shown in Table 4."
