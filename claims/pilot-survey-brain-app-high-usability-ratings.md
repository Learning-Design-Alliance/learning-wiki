McNaughton EP, Bilbie L, Zuljevic M, Allen LK, Pur D‐R, Eagleson R, de Ribaupierre S. (2025). Design of an interactive brain model for neuroanatomy education and MRI training. Anatomical Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/ase.70009

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pilot survey of 12 anatomy students at the University of Western Ontario on the virtual brain model's functionality. The app received "an average rating of 4.67 out of 5" on usefulness; 11 of 12 students agreed it was immersive and conducive to learning and would recommend it to peers. No effect size was reported.

> "The app received an average rating of 4.67 out of 5 across the 12 study participants on its “usefulness”, while 11 of the 12 students agreed that the app was “immersive” and “conducive to [their] learning”."
