# No significant differences in affect recognition errors emerged between the ADHD-Combined group and controls or between the ADHD-C and ADHD-I groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The ADHD-C and control groups did not differ significantly on fearful affect errors (p = .629), nor did the ADHD-I and ADHD-C groups (p = .151); no effect sizes were reported for these null contrasts. [→ Miller 2011](#miller-2011)

## Evidence

### Miller 2011

Miller, M., Hanford, R. B., Fassbender, C., Duke, M., & Schweitzer, J. B. (2011). Affect Recognition in Adults with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder. Journal of Attention Disorders. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3950374/

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Tukey-corrected post hoc contrasts following one-way ANOVAs on DANVA variables across the three diagnostic subgroups (ADHD-I n=16, ADHD-C n=17, control n=18). "The ADHD-I and ADHD-C groups did not differ (p = .151), nor did the ADHD-C and control groups (p = .629)." No significant differences emerged on any DANVA variable for these contrasts.
