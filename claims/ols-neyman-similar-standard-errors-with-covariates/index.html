Peter Z. Schochet 2010¶

Peter Z. Schochet. (2010). Is Regression Adjustment Supported By the Neyman Model for Causal Inference? Journal of Statistical Planning and Inference, vol. 140, issue 1. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/is-regression-adjustment-supported-by-the-neyman-model-for-causal-inference

The paper's stated headline finding, examining both theoretically and empirically whether OLS multivariate regression models estimating average treatment effects under experimental designs are "justified by the Neyman model for causal inference". No effect size is printed in the available text.

"The paper finds that estimated standard errors and significance levels for average treatment effect estimators are similar under the OLS and Neyman models when baseline covariates are included in the models, even though theory suggests that this may not have been the case."
