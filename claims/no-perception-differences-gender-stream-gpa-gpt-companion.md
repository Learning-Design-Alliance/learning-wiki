`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Mann-Whitney U and Kruskal-Wallis comparisons of perception domains across gender, academic stream, and GPA categories (n = 47). The article reports "no statistically significant differences between male and female students in any of the measured domains," non-significant stream differences (p = 0.639, p = 0.703), and non-significant GPA differences (e.g., p = 0.364).

> "Regarding gender, the analysis revealed no statistically significant differences between male and female students in any of the measured domains."
