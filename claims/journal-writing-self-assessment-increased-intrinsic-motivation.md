### Birjandi and Tamjid 2010

Parviz Birjandi, Nasrin Hadidi Tamjid. (2010). The Role of Self-assessment in Promoting Iranian EFL Learners' Motivation. English Language Teaching, 3(3). https://www.ccsenet.org/elt

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Paired sample t-test on the experimental group (n=30) comparing pre- and post-term motivation questionnaire scores. Scores rose from M=140.33 to M=160.63; the article reports "a significant difference" with t(29)=-5.016, p<.01. No effect size is printed.

> "The results show a significant difference between the scores of pre- motivation questionnaire (M=140.33, SD=17.4) and post- motivation questionnaire (M=160.63, SD=14.87), t(29)=-5.016, p<.01."
