# DLP teachers reported more confidence than non-DLP teachers in using technology to develop students' six 21st-century skill categories

## Evidence

### Bakhshaei 2019

Bakhshaei, M., Hardy, A., Ravitz, J., & Seylar, J. (2019). Scaling Up Classroom Coaching for Impactful Technology Use: Results from Year 2 of the Dynamic Learning Project. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/79337f05-0795-4bac-b1d7-f4758c23285c

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Survey of DLP and non-DLP teachers on confidence in using technology to develop agency, collaboration, communication, creativity, critical thinking, and tool selection. The report states teachers "reported feeling more confident in their ability to use technology to engage students in developing those skills across all six indicators."

> "80 percent of DLP teachers agreed that they “can create opportunities for students to use technology to work together to solve problems, complete tasks, and accomplish common goals,” compared to 67 percent of non-DLP teachers"
