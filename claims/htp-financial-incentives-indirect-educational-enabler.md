`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview finding from the grounded theory study of 21 faculty; Participant 18 stated that "With the increased compensation, faculty members are more motivated to stay" and more willing to teach. Participants emphasized incentives alone were not perceived as sufficient without evaluation-framework acknowledgment of educational activities.

> "With the increased compensation, faculty members are more motivated to stay, …, they may be more willing to teach."
