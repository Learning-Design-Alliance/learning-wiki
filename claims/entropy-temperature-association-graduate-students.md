## Evidence

### Nathan Crossette 2021

Nathan Crossette, Michael Vignal, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2021). Investigating graduate student reasoning on a conceptual entropy questionnaire. [doi:10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020119](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevphyseducres.17.020119)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Analysis of graduate student responses to the Blocks question, in which two solids in thermal contact equalize in temperature. The article reports that students reasoned 'as if there were a monotonic function relating entropy and temperature,' e.g., Garth associating entropy with temperature for the heating and cooling blocks.

> "Four students made a connection that higher/increasing temperature means more/increasing entropy, as if there were a monotonic function relating entropy and temperature."
