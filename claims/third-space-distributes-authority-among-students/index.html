Evidence¶

Suarez 2008¶

Suarez, E. and Otero, V. (2008). 3rd grade English language learners making sense of sound. https://www.eric.ed.gov

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative analysis of one session: students took turns interpreting Mahaley's explanation about string length and loudness, checking interpretations with her, and Gabriel conjectured that shorter strings make high-pitched sounds. The authors describe this as evidence that "third spaces distribute authority of knowledge and language."

"Moreover, students responding to their peers' conjectures, and co-constructing knowledge, is evidence how third spaces distribute authority of knowledge and language among students themselves."
