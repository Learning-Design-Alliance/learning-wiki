# Modeling Instruction sections show significant positive CLASS attitude shifts across multiple instructors, with an aggregated effect of d = 0.45

## Subclaims
`q3 i2` Across nine Modeling Instruction sections taught by six instructors, aggregated student attitudes improved significantly from pre- to post-course, with Cohen's d = 0.45 (95% CI 0.26–0.64). [→ Eric Brewe 2013](#eric-brewe-2013)

## Evidence

### Eric Brewe 2013

Eric Brewe, Adrienne Traxler, Jorge de la Garza, Laird H. Kramer. (2013). Extending positive CLASS results across multiple instructors and multiple classes of Modeling Instruction. [doi:10.1103/physrevstper.9.020116](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevstper.9.020116)

`q3 · i2` · `causal · r1`

Pre/post CLASS administration in nine calculus-based Mechanics sections at FIU (Fall 2008–Fall 2012), 221 students after exclusions. Aggregated Cohen's d = 0.45 with 95% CI (0.26 – 0.64), described as "an effect size of d = 0.45".

> "When data from all instructors is aggregated and effect size and confidence intervals on the effect are calculated, we find an effect size of d = 0.45, with a 95% confidence interval of (0.26 – 0.64)."
