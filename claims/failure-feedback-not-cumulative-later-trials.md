## Evidence

### Pavlik Jr 2015

Pavlik Jr, P. I., Yudelson, M., & Koedinger, K. R. (2015). A Measurement Model of Microgenetic Transfer for Improving Instructional Outcomes. International Artificial Intelligence in Education Society. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0039-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

CPFA recomputed on warm-up 1 trials 3–16: success transfer from LCM to Product remained significant (.106, p=.019), but failure parameters were non-significant; the article concludes 'the failure feedback we gave was not cumulative' and that feedback variability may be needed for repeated failures.

> "Generally, the restricted model results suggest that the failure feedback we gave was not cumulative and suggests that at the very least, variability of feedback may be necessary if a student keeps failing"
