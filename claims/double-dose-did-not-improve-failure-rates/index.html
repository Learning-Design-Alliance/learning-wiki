Evidence¶

Claire Durwood 2010¶

Claire Durwood, Emily Krone, and Christopher Mazzeo. (2010). Are Two Algebra Classes Better Than One? The Effects of Double-Dose Instruction in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. http://ccsr.uchicago.edu/content/page.php?cat=3&content_id=34

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The CCSR mixed-design study (regression discontinuity plus time series) found the policy "fell short of its original goal: to reduce failure rates in algebra"; failure rates that had been modestly improving leveled off after implementation.

"Though students' algebra test scores increased under the policy, course-passing rates did not improve."
