`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Exploratory mixed-effects analysis of log-data practice opportunities testing whether Mastered Step Skipping and adaptive selection reduced overpractice; the article reports no reliable evidence, with β = 0.16, p = .080 and β = 0.28, p = .083. Equivalence was not tested.

> "There was no reliable evidence that the redesign reduced overpractice beyond 80% mastery, as neither the main effect (β= 0.16,p=.080) nor the interaction with KC type (β= 0.28,p=.083) reached significance."
