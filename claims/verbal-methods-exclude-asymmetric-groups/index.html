Evidence¶

Juliene Madureira Ferreira 2021¶

Juliene Madureira Ferreira. (2021). What If We Look at the Body? An Embodied Perspective of Collaborative Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09607-8

q1 · i? · review · r2

This is the review's theoretical argument, not a tested result: it reasons that cognitivist, utterance-based methods cannot elucidate all aspects of collaboration and cites work on young children's collaborative capability (Fantasia et al., 2014; Ferreira, submitted).

"Such limitations open gaps with regard to collaboration within groups that have asymmetrical interactions such as groups of small children, who sometimes cannot sufficiently articulate their thinking as arguments but are nevertheless capable of collaboration"
