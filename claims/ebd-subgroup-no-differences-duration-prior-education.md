Impact of Evidence‐Based Dentistry Education on Dental Students' Critical Appraisal Skills: A Prospective Cohort Study. European Journal of Dental Education, 30(3), 1133–1141. https://doi.org/10.1111/eje.70066

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Subgroup analysis comparing 36 undergraduates (4.5 h) with 45 PhD/postgraduate students (16 h) using the McNemar–Bowker test. Both groups showed significant improvements with no between-group difference.

> "No significant differences in correct answers were found between training durations, but both groups improved post‐training (Table 5)."
