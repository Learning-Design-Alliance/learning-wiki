## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After VR training, median total inanimate time decreased from 15.5 to 12.4 min, errors from 5 to 2, and OSATS increased from 61 to 75 (all p < 0.005). [→ Radi Imad 2022](#radi-imad-2022)

## Evidence

### Radi Imad 2022

Radi Imad, Tellez Juan C., Alterio Rodrigo E., Scott Daniel J., Sankaranarayanan Ganesh, Nagaraj Madhuri B., Hogg Melissa E., Zeh Herbert J., Polanco Patricio M. (2022). Feasibility, effectiveness and transferability of a novel mastery-based virtual reality robotic training platform for general surgery residents. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-022-09106-z

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Inanimate tasks (RRC, ATW, IS) were video-recorded and scored by two blinded trained raters using a modified OSATS; the article reports median total errors "decreased from 5 (IQR: 3.5–7.5) to 2" and OSATS rose from 61 to 75 out of 90 (p < 0.005).

> "Median total time to complete all three tasks decreased from 15.5 min (IQR: 14.2–17.8 min) to 12.4 min (IQR: 10.2–14.3 min) (p < 0.001), median total errors decreased from 5 (IQR: 3.5–7.5) to 2 (IQR: 1–3) (p < 0.001)"
