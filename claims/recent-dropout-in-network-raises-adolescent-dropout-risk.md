## Subclaims
`q3 i?` After adjusting for confounders, the presence of at least one dropout among friends, romantic partners, or siblings was independently associated with participants' dropout (OR = 2.85, 95% CI 1.77-5.05). [→ Dupéré 2021](#dupere-2021)

## Evidence

### Dupéré 2021

Dupéré, V., Dion, E., Cantin, S., Archambault, I., & Lacourse, E. (2021). Social contagion and high school dropout: The role of friends, romantic partners, and siblings. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113, 572-584. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000484

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple logistic regression on dropout cases and matched at-risk controls (n = 366) from a two-stage matched case-control study in 12 francophone public high schools, with bootstrapped standard errors and confounders controlled. The adjusted association was "independently associated with dropout among participants, with a non-trivial effect size (OR = 2.85, 95% CI 1.77-5.05)"; no standardized effect size was printed.

> "occurrences of dropout in one's social network, irrespective of the same-age intimates involved, were independently associated with dropout among participants, with a non-trivial effect size (OR = 2.85, 95% CI 1.77-5.05)"
