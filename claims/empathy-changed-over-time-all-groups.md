### Karakoç Hediye 2026

Karakoç Hediye, Ozkan Hava. (2026). The effect of wearable simulated birth model on students’ empathy and privacy protection levels: a randomised controlled trial. BMC Medical Education, 26, 383. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08441-8

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Mixed-model repeated-measures analysis of the Empathic Tendency Scale across four time points in the three-arm trial; the time main effect was significant (F = 3.762, p = 0.011, partial eta squared 0.027 per Table 2), with post-simulation scores higher than pre- and post-counseling scores.

> "Regardless of group, the mean empathic tendency scores differed over time (p = 0.011). A difference was observed between the post-simulation and pre- and post-counseling follow-ups."
