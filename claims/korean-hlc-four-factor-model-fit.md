## Evidence

### Kang Soo Jin 2026

Kang Soo Jin, Hwang Won Ju, Park Chanho, Han Hae-Ra. (2026). Validation of health literacy competencies for healthcare professionals in Korea: a psychometric evaluation study. BMC Medical Education 26:244. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08605-0

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Confirmatory factor analysis (final model) with 359 Korean healthcare professionals via web survey. The four-factor model of the 43-item scale showed "comparative fit index = 0.939" and RMSEA 0.047, meeting the article's thresholds (CFI/TLI > 0.90, RMSEA < 0.08). No effect size is printed.

> "CFA indicated a good model fit (χ² = 1534.94, df = 854, p < 0.001, root mean square error of approximation = 0.047, comparative fit index = 0.939, Tucker–Lewis index = 0.936, and standardized root mean residual = 0.070)."
