## Evidence

### Egilsdottir H Ösp 2021

Egilsdottir H Ösp, Heyn Lena Günterberg, Brembo Espen Andreas, Byermoen Kirsten Røland, Moen Anne, Eide Hilde. (2021). Configuration of Mobile Learning Tools to Support Basic Physical Assessment in Nursing Education: Longitudinal Participatory Design Approach. JMIR mHealth and uHealth. https://doi.org/10.2196/22633

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Thematic content analysis of co-design workshop transcripts and audio files with 20 nursing students across three cohorts; the article reports the 7 stimulation categories as students' own reported ways the resources supported learning, with no effect size printed.

> "According to the students, the digital learning resources stimulated learning in 7 different ways: (1) developing structured assessment, (2) highlighting explicit reasoning processes, (3) giving and receiving feedback, (4) situation-specific learning, (5) communicative and physical learning, (6) individual premises for learning, and (7) the theoretical foundation for understanding the relationships between signs, symptoms, and clinical situation."
