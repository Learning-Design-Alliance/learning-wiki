## Evidence

### Muraskin Jordan 2016

Muraskin Jordan, Dodhia Sonam, Lieberman Gregory, Garcia Javier O., Verstynen Timothy, Vettel Jean M., Sherwin Jason, Sajda Paul. (2016). Brain dynamics of post‐task resting state are influenced by expertise: Insights from baseball players. Human Brain Mapping. https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.23321

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

DTI tractography ROI analysis of streamline counts between ten functionally derived regions plus SMA seeds in 13 experts and 24 novices. "no significant expertise‐related differences survived a conservative FDR correction"; uncorrected trends included higher expert connections for R precentral–L posterior cingulate (t = 3.09, P = 0.004).

> "Although no significant expertise‐related differences survived a conservative FDR correction, several trends for significance are shown in Figure 8B (*) at uncorrected P < 0.05 levels within our sample size of only 13 experts and 24 novices."
