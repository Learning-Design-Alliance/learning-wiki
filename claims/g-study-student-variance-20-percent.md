## Evidence

### Lertsakulbunlue 2025

Lertsakulbunlue, S., Thammasoon, K., & Kantiwong, A. (2025). Reliability and validity of simulation-based Electrocardiogram assessment rubrics for cardiac life support skills among medical students using generalizability theory. Medical Education Online, 30(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/10872981.2025.2479962

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

G-study using a three-way ANOVA (p×i×r design) estimated variance components for the overall rubric score. The person facet accounted for "20.33% of the total variance", which the authors read as score variation being driven mainly by students rather than item count.

> "The findings reveal that the variance percentage attributable to the universe score, students (P), is 20.33% of the total variance."
