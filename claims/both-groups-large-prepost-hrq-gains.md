## Evidence

### Simper Trevor 2020

Simper Trevor, Agley Jon, DeSalle Mallori, Todd Jennifer, Dutta Tapati. (2020). Pilot study of the influence of self-coding on empathy within an introductory motivational interviewing training. BMC Medical Education 20:43. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-1956-5

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Table 1 pre-post comparisons by group using Student's t-test. Both groups show large gains, with Cohen's d of "1.50" (control) and "1.85" (experimental); the article states these separate analyses should not be used to interpret the intervention effect.

> "Control (n = 26) 7.00 (2.74) 12.48 (4.40) 5.48 (3.77–7.19) 1.50 (0.63–2.37) <.001 Experimental (n = 27) 8.17 (3.79) 15.41 (4.05) 7.24 (5.44–9.04) 1.85 (0.95–2.75) <.001"
