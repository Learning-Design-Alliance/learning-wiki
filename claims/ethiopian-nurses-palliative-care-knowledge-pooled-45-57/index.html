Evidence¶

Getie Addisu 2021¶

Getie Addisu, Wondmieneh Adam, Bimerew Melaku, Gedefaw Getnet, Demis Asmamaw. (2021). Knowledge on Palliative Care and Associated Factors among Nurses in Ethiopia: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Pain Research & Management. https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/5557947

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Systematic review and meta-analysis using an inverse-variance random-effects model pooling nine cross-sectional studies of Ethiopian nurses. The pooled estimate was "45.57% (95% CI: 35.27–55.87)", so more than half of nurses were classified as having poor knowledge.

"Nine studies with a total of 2709 study participants were included in the final analysis. The overall pooled prevalence of nurses' knowledge towards palliative care was 45.57% (95% CI: 35.27–55.87)."
