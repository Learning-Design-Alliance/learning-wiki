`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

A randomized study with school-level assignment examined Edpuzzle Original math videos in grades 3–5 in a rural California district; the analytic sample included 1,843 students across eight elementary schools. Treatment students "scored higher on the Spring 2025 CAASPP mathematics assessment than students in control schools (effect size = +0.09)".
