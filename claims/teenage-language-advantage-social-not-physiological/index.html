The article reports, citing Graham (1985), that the teenage advantage in language acquisition is not due to a physiological limit such as solidified brain lateralization or a critical period as Lenneberg maintained, but to social factors. Graham and Brown (1985) are cited for the differing task focus of teenagers versus parents. No effect size is printed.
