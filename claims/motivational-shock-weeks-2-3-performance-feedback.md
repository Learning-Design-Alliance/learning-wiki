## Evidence

### Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann 2021

Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann. (2021). Students' Motivational Trajectories and Academic Success in Math-Intensive Study Programs: Why Short-Term Motivational Assessments Matter. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000708

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r?`

Study 1b weekly assessments (N = 773 subsample) in the first four weeks of the semester identified a motivational shock between Weeks 2 and 3. The shock "coincided with their first performance feedback on mandatory math worksheets."

> "We found that students experienced a "motivational shock" between Weeks 2 and 3 of the semester that coincided with their first performance feedback on mandatory math worksheets."
