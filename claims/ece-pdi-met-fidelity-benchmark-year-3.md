MEd; and Pacchiano, Debra PhD (2016) "A Development Evaluation Study of a Professional Development Initiative to Strengthen Organizational Conditions in Early Education Settings," Journal of Applied Research on Children: Informing Policy for Children at Risk: Vol. 7 : Iss. 2 , Article 9. http://digitalcommons.library.tmc.edu/childrenatrisk/vol7/iss2/9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Implementation study measuring fidelity for 6 key components of the ECE PDI across 4 community-based centers using adherence, duration, and participant responsiveness criteria, measured once per year for 3 years. The article reports the overall program fidelity benchmark was met by the end of Year 3, with "More than 75% of centers implemented each component of the ECE PDI with fidelity".

> "More than 75% of centers implemented each component of the ECE PDI with fidelity, resulting in meeting the overall program fidelity benchmark by the end of Year 3 of implementation"

### Whalen 2016 (2)

Whalen, Samuel P. PhD; Horsley, Heather L. PhD; Parkinson, Kathleen K. MEd; and Pacchiano, Debra PhD (2016) "A Development Evaluation Study of a Professional Development Initiative to Strengthen Organizational Conditions in Early Education Settings," Journal of Applied Research on Children: Informing Policy for Children at Risk: Vol. 7 : Iss. 2 , Article 9. http://digitalcommons.library.tmc.edu/childrenatrisk/vol7/iss2/9

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Implementation fidelity analysis across the 4 intervention centers in Year 2. Two of the 6 components fell below threshold: ECE PDI Implementation (component score 50%) and Direct Supervisor PD (component score 0%), per Table 2. The article attributes this to variation in implementation and attendance "occurring primarily in the initial phases of implementation".

> "treatment centers did not meet fidelity for Key Component 2, ECE PDI Implementation, with indicators measuring the number of expected hours of PD for each participant as well as implementation of those hours in a 2-month time frame; nor for Key Component 5, Direct Supervisor PD"
