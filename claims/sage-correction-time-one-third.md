### Dominik Borovský 2023

Dominik Borovský, Jozef Hanč, and Martina Hančová. (2023). Scientific Computing with Open SageMath not only for Physics Education. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2308.07199](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2308.07199)

`q1 · i?` · `design · r2`

Authors' pilot experience using interactive Sage solutions of Physics Olympiad problems for correcting tasks in an ongoing competition. The article reports the correction time "decreased to about 1/3" and more objective, fair corrections; no effect size or formal test is printed.

> "Pilot testing in last three years showed that during corrections, the correction time decreased to about 1/3, but what we value the most is that the students' solutions were certainly corrected more objectively and fairly, as we did not penalize a student multiple times for a single mistake."
