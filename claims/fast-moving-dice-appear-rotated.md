## Subclaims
`q2 i?` In computed visualizations, dice moving in single file at 95% of the speed of light appear primarily rotated, with slightly bent vertical edges, rather than simply shortened. [→ U Kraus 2007](#u-kraus-2007)

## Evidence

### U Kraus 2007

U Kraus. (2007). First-person visualizations of the special and general theory of relativity. The European Journal of Physics (accepted for publication). https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.3454 [doi:10.48550/arXiv.0708.3454](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0708.3454)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Simulation-based visualization result reported in section 2.1, where a row of dice is rendered at 95% of the speed of light with finite light travel time taken into account. The computed image shows "The moving dice appear primarily rotated" with "vertical edges appear slightly bent", contrasting with the wrong early textbook pictures of plain length contraction.

> "The correctly computed looks of the moving dice are shown in ﬁgure 1(c). The moving dice appear primarily rotated. Also, the vertical edges appear slightly bent, most notably in the dice that is closest to the observer."
