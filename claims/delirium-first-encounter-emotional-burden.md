### Kılıç Gülşen 2026

Kılıç Gülşen, Işık Sevcan Avcı, Çevik Banu. (2026). Nursing students’ first clinical encounters with delirium: a qualitative study of experiential learning and professional development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09980-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of student interviews found fear was "driven by unpredictable patient behaviors, the risk of self-harm, and potential sudden clinical deterioration," intensifying when students felt isolated without sufficient clinical skills.

> "Students experienced intense fear during initial encounters, primarily driven by unpredictable patient behaviors, the risk of self-harm, and potential sudden clinical deterioration."

### Kılıç Gülşen 2026 (2)

Kılıç Gülşen, Işık Sevcan Avcı, Çevik Banu. (2026). Nursing students’ first clinical encounters with delirium: a qualitative study of experiential learning and professional development. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09980-4

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Interview data showed "altered consciousness and inability to communicate evoked deep sadness," with students evaluating situations "through a familial lens."

> "Beyond fear, encounters with delirium triggered profound empathic responses. Patients’ altered consciousness and inability to communicate evoked deep sadness, leading students to project themselves or their loved ones into the patient’s situation."
