`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Follow-up questionnaire asking whether participants implemented changes they had not intended directly after the program. The article reports "114 participants made a total of 180 'unintended' changes", most often in history taking, investigation, and working conditions.

> "In total 114 participants made a total of 180 "unintended" changes in daily practice. These changes mostly concerned the sexual history taking (41.7%), the additional investigation (31.7%), and a change in the working conditions of the STI-consultation (9.4%)."
