q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Self-reported prompting experiments by a World Englishes scholar, reported in the dialogue: LLMs "perform very badly" at generating non-US national standard varieties, including inconsistent British spelling over more than a few paragraphs. Informal, unreported methodology.

"I have carried out prompting experiments to generate text in national standard varieties of English other than the US one and found LLMs to perform very badly at this task."

Ugwuanyi 2025 (2)¶

Ugwuanyi, K., Mair, C., Dovchin, S., Erdocia, I., Kuteeva, M., & Airemionkhale, E. (2025). Generative AI and linguistic diversity in academic writing and publishing: Perspectives from World Englishes. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28505

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Analysis of a ChatGPT-4 one-shot prompt rewriting an abstract in Nigerian English: the output exaggerated enregistered Pidgin-style features, showing minoritised varieties are "underrepresented in the pre-training data". Single-prompt demonstration, not a systematic evaluation.

"the above text generated by ChatGPT confirms that the voices of scholars using minoriti sed varieties of World Englishes are clearly underrepresented in the pre-training data."
