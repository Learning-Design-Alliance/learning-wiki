P. A. Bartlett. (2019). Dont answer the question! How on-line Moodle-based 'Data Retrieval Tests' encourage good record-keeping and a divergent experimental mindset for undergraduate physics students. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1905.13006](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1905.13006)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Practitioner observation at UCL physics teaching laboratories after DRT introduction in 2013; the author reports this behaviour "had not been observed before the implementation of the DRTs". No systematic data collection is described.

> "It was noticed that students would come to the laboratory to work on their notebook outside allotted sessions and that they would form peer-groups to improve their practice through mutual support."
