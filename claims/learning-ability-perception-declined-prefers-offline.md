## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The proportion of students who thought hybrid teaching did not help improve their learning ability rose significantly from 17.2% in 2020 to 29.0% in 2022, and 2022 students perceiving little improvement preferred offline teaching (Fisher's Exact Test P < 0.05). `q3 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Questionnaire items on learning-ability improvement compared across 2020 and 2022 cohorts. The proportion reporting no help rose "17.2% in 2020 vs. 29.0% in 2022", with a decrease in students reporting great improvement (43.0% vs. 34.0%).

> "a significant increase in the proportion of students who thought that hybrid teaching didn’t help improving their learning ability (17.2% in 2020 vs. 29.0% in 2022)"
