`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Cross-sectional survey comparing Likert-scale ratings between participant gender using the Mann–Whitney U nonparametric test with Bonferroni adjustments (n = 253). Findings show female students were more negative about their overall TBL experience (p = 0.004).

> "Female students were more negative about their overall TBL experience (p = 0.004), and ‘non‐White’ students had more positive perceptions of TBL overall (p = 0.008)."
