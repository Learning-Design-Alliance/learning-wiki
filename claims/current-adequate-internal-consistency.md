## Evidence

### Ryan 2014

Ryan, Q. X., Astolfi, C., Baily, C., & Pollock, S. J. (2014). Validation of a Conceptual Assessment Tool in E&M II. 2014 Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings. [doi:10.1119/perc.2014.pr.054](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2014.pr.054)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Internal consistency analysis using Cronbach's alpha on 271 student responses. The authors note Cronbach's α assumes unidimensionality and that they have no evidence the CURrENT measures a single construct, suggesting the values are likely a conservative underestimate.

> "We treat each sub question (e.g. 1a, 1b) as a single test item, and obtained α=0.72 (N=271), where α-values between 0.7-0.9 are traditionally considered adequate [9]. We also computed α more conservatively by treating each question (including all sub parts) as one test item and obtained α=0.69."
