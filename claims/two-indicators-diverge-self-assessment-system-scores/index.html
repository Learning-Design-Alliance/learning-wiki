de Barba, P. G., Oliveira, E. A., & English, N. (2025). Development and validation of a learning analytics rubric for self-regulated learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10521-x

q2 · i? · design · r2

In Phase 3 validation, chi-square goodness-of-fit tests revealed significant discrepancies for the indicators measuring completion of extra activities and early semester engagement with the LMS. Students' self-assessments indicated higher levels of SRL skill use than the learning analytics detected.

"Conversely, indicators 6 and 7 had a significant discrepancy between self-assessment and system-generated scores (p < 0.05), indicating divergence between student perception and learning analytics observations."
