Evidence¶

Schutte et al. 2018¶

Ingrid Schutte, Elanor Kamans, Marca Wolfensberger and Wiel Veugelers. (2018). Effects of an International Undergraduate Honors Course on Awareness of Global Justice. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 82. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8020082

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-posttest with control group on combined ICSSQ subscales (denial and defense: N participants = 19, N control = 18; acceptance and adaptation: N participants = 19, N control = 18); simple effects also showed no changes due to participation (Fs < 1).

"The results of the analysis showed no signiﬁcant interactions for both denial and defense, F < 1, and acceptance and adaptation, F < 1."

Schutte et al. 2018 (2)¶

Ingrid Schutte, Elanor Kamans, Marca Wolfensberger and Wiel Veugelers. (2018). Effects of an International Undergraduate Honors Course on Awareness of Global Justice. Educ. Sci. 2018, 8, 82. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci8020082

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Endpoint simple effect on the combined acceptance and adaptation subscale; the printed effect size is η²p = 0.08 (printed as N2p = 0.08), a medium partial eta-squared, but p = 0.092 is marginal. At the start, the control group scored higher on denial and defense (F(1,35) = 5.35, p = 0.027, N2p = 0.13).

"Simple effect analysis did, however, show a difference between the control group and the participant group at the end of the course, with respect to acceptance and adaptation, F(1,35) = 2.95, p = 0.092, N2p = 0.08, such that the participant group scored slightly higher on the more ethno-relativist orientation."
