Lachowsky 2021¶

Lachowsky, N. J., & Murray, J. (2021). Group Processing: Students Reflections on the Experience and Impact of Group Processing. Journal of Problem Based Learning in Higher Education, 9(2). https://doi.org/10.5278/ojs.jpblhe.v9i2.2883

In the retrospective survey of 46 former seminar students, participants agreed that group processing affected the rest of their university experience (mean=8.1) and their experiences after university (mean=7.9).

"Importantly, there was agreement that group processing affected the rest of their university experience (mean=8.1), and their experiences after university (mean=7.9)."
