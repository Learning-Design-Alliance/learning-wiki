## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The meta-synthesis reports that the vast majority of nursing students felt excitement during VS teaching, and synthesises themes (not counts) of enjoyment, reduced fear and psychological burden, privacy and increased confidence, which it links to the fact that safe virtual settings let them make mistakes without harming patients and repeat practice privately after mistakes. `q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

Synthesized finding from the meta-synthesis of 17 qualitative studies, drawing on student interview accounts such as feeling relieved to experience interventions in advance through vSim. The review reports positive experiences including reduced psychological burden, privacy protection, and confidence gains.

> "The vast majority of nursing students felt excitement after engaging in VS teaching. The highly immersive experimental scenarios provided a novel experience, which they found intriguing and enjoyable"
