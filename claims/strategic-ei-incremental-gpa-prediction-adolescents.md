## Evidence

### Jolić Marjanović Zorana 2021

Jolić Marjanović Zorana, Altaras Dimitrijević Ana, Protić Sonja, Mestre José M. (2021). The Role of Strategic Emotional Intelligence in Predicting Adolescents’ Academic Achievement: Possible Interplays with Verbal Intelligence and Personality. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph182413166

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Three-step hierarchical regression on self-reported GPA from 227 adolescents; the overall model explained 26% of variance (F7219 = 12.12, p < 0.001), and "strategic EI added a significant 2% (p < 0.05) to the overall variance explained". No effect size printed.

> "Entered in the last step, strategic EI added a significant 2% (p < 0.05) to the overall variance explained. The order of significant independent predictors of GPA was C (β = 0.32, p < 0.001), VI (β = 0.23, p < 0.001)"
