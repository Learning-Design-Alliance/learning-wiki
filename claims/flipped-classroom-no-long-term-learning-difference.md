# Long-term learning at eight months did not differ significantly between flipped classroom and lecture-based groups

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` On a ten-question retention test eight months later, FC (mean 6.41, SD 1.57) and LB (mean 6.78, SD 1.70) groups showed no significant difference (p = 0.220), contradicting hypothesis H5. [→ Oliván Blázquez Bárbara 2019](#olivan-blazquez-barbara-2019)

## Evidence

### Oliván Blázquez Bárbara 2019

Oliván Blázquez Bárbara, Masluk Barbara, Gascon Santiago, Fueyo Díaz Ricardo, Aguilar-Latorre Alejandra, Artola Magallón Isabel, Magallón Botaya Rosa. (2019). The use of flipped classroom as an active learning approach improves academic performance in social work: A randomized trial in a university. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0214623

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Eight-month delayed retention test (ten questions, two per topic area) in the cluster-randomized trial; Table 3 prints means of 6.41 (FC) and 6.78 (LB), p = 0.220, with no effect size. The authors caution the eight-month sample was small, with many participants anonymous, and the test may not distinguish in-depth learning.

> "Regarding long-term learning, this study found that both groups obtained results without significant differences."
