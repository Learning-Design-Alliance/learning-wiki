## Evidence

### Zhang Yuejiao 2025

Zhang Yuejiao, Li Xiaolan, Wei Xi, Xiao Jiexuan, Zhang Qi, Mai Sui. (2025). A pilot study on the application of a digital dental teaching evaluation system in Class II cavity preparation teaching. BMC Oral Health 25, 372. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-05695-y

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Descriptive analysis of baseline Fair Grader 2000 scores (maximum 100 points) for the 18 clinicians, shown in Fig. 3: 28.57% of the ongoing-residency group exceeded 60 points versus 90% of the completed-residency group. These are descriptive percentages; no effect size is printed.

> "The baseline scores below 60 were concentrated on operators who were ongoing residency training, and only 28.57% were greater than 60 points. In contrast, the percentage of those who had completed their residency whose scores were greater than 60 points was 90%"
