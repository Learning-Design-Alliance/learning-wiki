## Evidence

### Al-Najafi Dheyaa 2026

Al-Najafi Dheyaa, Krause Katherine D., Wang Yundi, Zuo Qi Kang, Koblanski Maya E, Leong Cameron J., Schmidt Emma, Faran Muhammad, Verma Vanay, Vyas Ravi, Campbell Matthew, Hwang Jaehyun, Deng Jiawen, Palepu Anita. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09671-0

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Linear mixed-effects repeated-measures model with random intercepts for participants assessed pre- versus post-exam self-rated preparedness on a 10-point Likert scale in the ITT population. The article concludes self-perceived preparedness did not meaningfully change and did not differ between formats.

> "no statistically significant main effects of time (pre- versus post-exam; F(1,256) = 3.53, p = 0.062, η²p = 0.01), group assignment (AI-gen versus Student-gen; F(1,256) = 1.59, p = 0.208, η²p = 0.006), or time × group interaction (F(1,256) = 1.68, p = 0.196, η²p = 0.007)"
