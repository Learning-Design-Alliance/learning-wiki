## Evidence

### Julia Schiefer 2022

Julia Schiefer, Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Andrea Bernholt, Nele Kampa, Andreas Nehring. (2022). Epistemic Beliefs in Science—A Systematic Integration of Evidence From Multiple Studies. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09661-w

`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Descriptive analysis of science self-concept covariate means across profiles in eight samples (Fig. 4). The authors note 'a tendency in our results for students with a high self-concept to be assigned to (highly) sophisticated or evidence-based profiles. However, this result was not consistent across samples.'

> "The multiplistic profiles showed similar and sometimes even lower levels than the linear absolutistic profiles; by contrast, the evidence-based profiles showed similarly high and even higher levels than the linear sophisticated profiles."
