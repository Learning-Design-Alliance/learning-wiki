# TK staffing shortages improved: TK assistant teacher vacancies fell from 12% to 3%, and 91% of LEAs reported enough lead teachers meeting 2025–26 requirements

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Only 1% of TK lead teacher positions and 3% of TK assistant teacher positions were vacant at the start of 2023–24, the latter down from 12% the prior year. [→ Wang 2025](#wang-2025)

## Evidence

### Wang 2025

Wang, V., Melnick, H., Leung-Gagné, M., Parker, S., & Wechsler, M. (2025). California's universal prekindergarten implementation progress, 2023–24. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/600.551

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Statewide LEA survey reported in the Staffing TK Classrooms section and Figures 4–5. The report also states 91% of LEAs reported enough TK lead teachers who will meet the 24-unit early childhood coursework and credential requirements taking effect in 2025–26.

> "Only 1% of TK lead teacher positions were vacant at the beginning of the 2023–24 school year, similar to the 2022–23 vacancy rate. Additionally, the vacancy rate for TK assistant teachers at the beginning of 2023–24 was 3%, notably less than the 12% vacancy rate at the beginning of the 2022–23 school year."
