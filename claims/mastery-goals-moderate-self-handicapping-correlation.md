# Sample mastery-approach goal level moderates the correlation: medium mastery-approach samples show stronger negative correlations than high mastery-approach samples

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Correlations are more negative in samples with medium mastery-approach goals (r = -.38) than in samples with high mastery-approach goals (r = -.25). [→ Schwinger 2014](#schwinger-2014)

## Evidence

### Schwinger 2014

Schwinger, M., Wirthwein, L., Lemmer, G., & Steinmayr, R. (2014, February 10). Academic Self-Handicapping and Achievement: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/a0035832

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Mixed-effects moderator analysis of mastery-approach goal level across the 49 effect sizes (QB = 8.93, p < .01). The medium mastery-approach category showed r = -.38 versus r = -.25 in the high mastery-approach category; the authors interpret this as mastery goals buffering the link via controllable attributions of failure.

> "We found higher effect sizes in samples with medium sized mastery-approach goals (r ⫽ ⫺.38; 95% CI: ⫺.45 ⱕ r ⱕ ⫺.30) compared to samples with high mastery-approach goals (r ⫽ ⫺.25; 95% CI: ⫺.28 ⱕ r ⱕ ⫺.20)."
