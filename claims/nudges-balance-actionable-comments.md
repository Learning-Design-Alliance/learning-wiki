## Evidence

### Dory V 2019

Dory V, Cummings B-A, Mondou M, Young M. (2019). Nudging clinical supervisors to provide better in-training assessment reports. Perspectives on Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-019-00554-3

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Analysis of the same 360-ITAR sample using MANOVA over the nine CCERR item scores. The article reports both the "balanced" and "actionable nature of comments" items improved significantly across form versions.

> "Changes in forms successfully increased the balanced (from 1.5 (SD 0.7) to 2.4 (SD 0.8) and 2.3 (SD 0.7), p < 0.001) and actionable nature of comments (from 1.3 (SD 0.6) to 2.3 (SD 0.8) and 2.3 (SD 0.6), p < 0.001)"
