Evidence¶

Filges 2018¶

Filges, T., Sonne‐Schmidt, C. S., Nielsen, B. C. V. (2018). Small class sizes for improving student achievement in primary and secondary schools: a systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews 2018:10. https://doi.org/10.4073/csr.2018.10

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Test of Anderson's knowledge-of-students path on Grade 1 students in 46 Dutch schools in 1999–2000, with personalised instruction operationalised as counts of specific interaction types and engagement as time on task; findings were relational, as the review reports them.

"Smaller classes were related to more interactions of all kinds and more task‐directed and praising interactions resulted in more time spent on task which in turn was related to higher student achievement as expected."
