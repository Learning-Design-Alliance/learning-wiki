Saunders 2024¶

Saunders, R., Fitz, J., DiNapoli, M. A., Jr., & Kini, T. (2024). Teacher residencies: State and federal policy to support comprehensive teacher preparation. Learning Policy Institute & EdPrepLab. https://doi.org/10.54300/358.825

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report aggregates placement data from 47 NCTR-partnered programs: "25% taught in math, science, or technology fields; 25% taught special education; and 18% specialized in teaching English learners" — all described as widespread shortage areas.

"Of the 2021–22 graduates of 47 programs partnered with the National Center for Teacher Residencies (NCTR), 25% taught in math, science, or technology fields; 25% taught special education; and 18% specialized in teaching English learners."
