Six-year randomized follow-up of the Kingsborough Community College learning communities trial (sample size 1,534; 769 program, 765 control). Table 3 also reports total credits earned of 56.3 versus 52.3, a 4.0-credit impact marked significant, and no meaningful difference in four-year enrollment (42.4 vs 42.0 percent).
