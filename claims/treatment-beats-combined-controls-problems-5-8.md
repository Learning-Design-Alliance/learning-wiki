## Evidence

### D. J. Webb 2012

D. J. Webb. (2012). Improving student's problem-solving ability as well as conceptual understanding without sacrificing the physics content of a class. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1210.3385](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1210.3385)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Regression comparison of section I against grouped sections II and IV, which were indistinguishable after controlling for FCI pretest, GPA, and prior physics. The treatment coefficient was "β4 = 4.7 ± 1.2 (p < 0.001)", checking the effect against other instructors.

> "the coeﬃcient measuring the eﬀect of treatment vs control group is β4 = 4.7 ± 1.2 (p < 0.001) so with higher than 99.9% conﬁdence we would say that students in the t reatment group earned higher scores than students in the other sections"
