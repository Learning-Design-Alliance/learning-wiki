`q3 · i2` · `causal · r2`

Quasi-experiment measuring humanistic care with the CAI in 120 nursing interns. The "total score of the intervention group was 203.08 ± 27.52" versus 187.92 ± 38.71 in controls, with d = 0.46.

> "The total score of the intervention group was 203.08 ± 27.52, which was higher than that of the control group (187.92 ± 38.71), and the difference between the groups was statistically significant (t = 2.522, P = 0.013). The effect size was small to medium (d = 0.46, 95% CI: 0.09 to 0.82)."
