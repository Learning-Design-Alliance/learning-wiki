`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The brief's synthesis of 23 longitudinal studies using teacher fixed effects, summarized in Table 1, counts studies suggesting improvement in each experience range: "15 of the 22 studies" for years 6-15 and 6 of 11 for years 16-25 found improvement.

> "Moreover, 15 of the 22 studies that examined this relationship in the next decade of a teachers’ career found evidence of further improvement, as did six of the eleven studies that considered the returns to experience between 16 and 25 years. In contrast, two of the nine studies found evidence of improvement beyond 25 years."
