q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports findings from Benedetto et al. [2024] on simulating student responses to exam questions with varying proficiency levels. The article reports no correlation between rationale quality and simulated-response accuracy; no coefficient is printed.

"However, recent findings in the context of simulating student answers to exam questions indicate no direct correlation between the quality of CoT-generated rationales and the accuracy of simulated responses"
