## Evidence

### Imtiaz Hassan Taj 2016

Imtiaz Hassan Taj, Norrihan Binti Sulan, Muhammad Aslam Sipra, Waqar Ahmad. (2016). Impact of Mobile Assisted Language Learning (MALL) on EFL: A Meta-Analysis. Advances in Language and Literary Studies 7(2). https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.2p.76

`q3 · i2` · `quant-synthesis · r1`

Meta-analysis of 13 studies (2008–2015) meeting criteria of quasi-experimental pretest–posttest control-group design with mobile-device intervention; d-type and r-type effect sizes were calculated and transformed to Fisher's Z, weighted by (n-3). The article reports "Z combined was found to be 8.156" with SE = 0.0521 and 95% CI of 0.323 to 0.527.

> "the overall effect size was 0.425 (ES = 0.425) which is markedly different from 0 as Z combined was found to be 8.156 which is greater than 1.96 for α = 0.05 in standard normal distribution."
