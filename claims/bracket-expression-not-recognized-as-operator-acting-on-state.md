# Students' common difficulty is not knowing that a Dirac-notation bracket expression such as ⟨x|p̂|p′⟩ is conceptually equivalent to the operator acting on the state in that representation, even when they know the pieces separately

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Many students who could separately write p̂ as −iℏ∂/∂x and |p′⟩ as e^{ip′x/ℏ} in the position representation still struggled to write ⟨x|p̂|p′⟩ without Dirac notation. [→ Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2016](#emily-marshman-and-chandralekha-singh-2016)

## Evidence

### Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh 2016

Emily Marshman and Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Student difficulties with representations of quantum operators corresponding to observables. [doi:10.1119/perc.2016.pr.049](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2016.pr.049)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Think-aloud individual interviews with a total of 23 UG and G student volunteers enrolled in QM courses showed students could transform the pieces separately but often did not know that ⟨𝑥|𝑝̂|𝑝′⟩ is equivalent to writing 𝑝̂|𝑝′⟩ in the position representation (projecting onto position eigenstates).

> "Many of them were separately able to write 𝑝̂ as −𝑖ℏ 𝜕 𝜕𝑥⁄  and |𝑝′⟩ as 𝑒𝑖𝑝′𝑥 ℏ⁄  in the position representation. However, students often struggled to write ⟨𝑥|𝑝̂|𝑝′⟩ without using Dirac notation."
