## Evidence

### Lee Young Hwan 2018

Lee Young Hwan, Kim Kyong-Jee. (2018). Enhancement of student perceptions of learner-centeredness and community of inquiry in flipped classrooms. BMC Medical Education, 18, 242. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-018-1347-3

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Pre-post paired t-test with 55 Year 2 medical students in a 15-week flipped introduction-to-medicine course; total mean rose from 2.89 (.42) to 3.43 (.56) on a 1-5 scale where 5 = "student-centered". The article reports the increase as statistically significant (p < .001) with no effect size.
