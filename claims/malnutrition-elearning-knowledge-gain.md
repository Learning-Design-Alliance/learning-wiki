### Choi S 2018

Choi S, Yuen HM, Annan R, Pickup T, Pulman A, Monroy-Valle M, Aduku NEL, Kyei-Boateng S, Velásquez Monzón CI, Portillo Sermeño CE, Penn A, Ashworth A, Jackson AA. (2018). `q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Interrupted time-series study of 606 participants with paired pre- and postassessment data across Ghana, Latin America, and remote online users. The article reports "a relative increase of 41.5%" in knowledge scores after course access, with P<.001.

> "The mean score gain in knowledge after access to the course in 606 participants who had pre- and postassessment data was 11.8 (95% CI 10.8-12.9; P<.001)—a relative increase of 41.5%."
