Background review section: the article attributes to Fredlund a study of third-year physics undergraduates describing refraction, in which the wavefront diagram "promoted reasoning about speed changes but obscured reasoning about angles and directionality". This is reported background, not the present study's data.

> "The ray diagram could help students to reason about the refraction angles at the boundary and also about the direction of propagation, but it could not help students reason about speed changes in the two media."
