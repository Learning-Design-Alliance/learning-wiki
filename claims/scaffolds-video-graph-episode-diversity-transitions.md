# Video-graph analysis shows scaffolded tasks produced more diverse process episodes, more fluent transitions between processes, and more P3–P4 oscillation than non-scaffolded tasks

Parks, T., Shehab, S., & Mercier, E. (2026). Investigating Students' Collaborative Problem-Solving Interactions during Scaffolded and Non-Scaffolded Ill-Structured Tasks. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09816-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Qualitative video-graph comparison of thematic episodes across 11 groups' transcripts. "There are 37 instances depicted in the graphs of groups transitioning between problem-solving realms without a break for the scaffolded task, whereas in the non-scaffolded task this is depicted 28 times." All but two groups oscillated between P3 and P4 on the scaffolded task.
