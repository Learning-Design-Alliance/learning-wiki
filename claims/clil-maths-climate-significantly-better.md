## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Pupils taught Mathematics in English (M/A) rated the teaching climate significantly better than the control group (M) across all seven questionnaire categories. [→ Binterová and Komínková 2013](#binterova-and-kominkova-2013)

## Evidence

### Binterová and Komínková 2013

Helena Binterová, Olga Komínková. (2013). Using Computers in Relation to Learning Climate in CLIL Method. Acta Didactica Napocensia, Volume 6, Number 1, 2013. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1053646

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

A Mann-Whitney test compared the M/A and M groups across seven climate categories at α = 5%. The null hypothesis was rejected in all seven categories, and main values in the M/A group were lower, corresponding to better evaluation.

> "As the values of the p-value clearly show, we must reject the null hypothesis in all seven categories in favour of the alternative hypothesis, i.e. the distribution was different in all observed categories."
