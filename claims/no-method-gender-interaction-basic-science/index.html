Evidence¶

Nwafor Chika E 2015¶

Nwafor Chika E, Abigail Chikaodinaka Obodo, Gabriel Okafor. (2015). Effect of Self Regulated Learning Approach on Junior Secondary School Students' Achievement in Basic Science. Journal of Education and Practice, Vol.6, No.5. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1083647

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA with interaction term (Table 4: Teaching Methods & Gender, F = .425). The researcher upholds the null hypothesis of no significant interaction between gender and method. Adjusted means favoured the treatment group for both males (56.11 vs 40.82) and females (52.86 vs 39.70).

"for the two-way interaction, the F-calculated is .425 which is less than the critical value of 3.89 at alpha value of 0.05"
