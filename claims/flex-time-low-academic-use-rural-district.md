Case-study research findings from a rural school district's systematic study of its flexible scheduling system, conducted with Regional Educational Laboratory support after student listening sessions. The study found "only 19 percent of that time was spent on academic activities" against 78 percent nonacademic use.

> "Students had an average of 80 minutes of flex-time each day, but only 19 percent of that time was spent on academic activities. The majority, 78 percent, was used for nonacademic pursuits like socializing or going off campus."
