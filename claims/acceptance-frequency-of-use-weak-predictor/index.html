Zhorabekova A 2026¶

Zhorabekova A, Duisenova M, Kurmasheva M and Yesnazar A (2026). Acceptance of AI-gamified adaptive learning in EFL: bifactor evidence for essential unidimensionality and the limits of subscale scoring. Front. Educ. 11:1929123. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1929123

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Exploratory ordinal-coded regression of the composite acceptance score on frequency of use, English level, length of study, and year of study (N = 401). The article reports R2 = .034 and model F p = .009; English level (p = .80) and length of study (p = .47) were non-significant.

"The only significant predictor was frequency of use (b = .121, p = .002): more frequent users reported slightly higher acceptance."
