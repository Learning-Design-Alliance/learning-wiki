# Negative in-the-moment emotions toward correct-outcome sentences predict significantly lower open-ended knowledge revision scores

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Explanation posttest scores were significantly lower when participants reported negative emotions toward the correct-outcome sentences than when they reported positive emotions (b = −0.84, p < 0.05). [→ Jheng 2025](#jheng-2025)

### Jheng 2025
