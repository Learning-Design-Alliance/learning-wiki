## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh 2016

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorials. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.03108](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.03108)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre-/post-test case study of the uncertainty principle QuILT in an advanced quantum mechanics course, with 12 students taking both tests. The article reports that "the average performance of 12 students who took the last version of the QuILT improved from 42% to 83% from pre-test to post-test".

> "Table 1 shows that the average performance of 12 students who took the last version of the QuILT improved from 42% to 83% from pre-test to post-test."

### Chandralekha Singh 2016 (2)

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorials. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.03108](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.03108)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Pre-/post-test case study of the uncertainty principle QuILT; on a question common to both tests students sketched the Fourier transform of a delta function and labeled the axes. The article reports that "In the post-test, 10 out of 12 students were able to draw correct diagrams with labeled axes".

> "In the post-test, 10 out of 12 students were able to draw correct diagrams with labeled axes and explain why the Fourier transform should be a constant extended over all space."

### Chandralekha Singh 2016 (3)

Chandralekha Singh. (2016). Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorials. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1603.03108](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1603.03108)

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Same case study; a common pre-/post-test question asked students to agree or disagree with a statement claiming that a fast-moving particle's position cannot be measured precisely. The article reports that "Out of the 12 students who took both pre-/post-tests, 7 students provided incorrect responses on the pre-test".

> "Out of the 12 students who took both pre-/post-tests, 7 students provided incorrect responses on the pre-test."
