## Evidence

### Herrmann et al. 2016

Sarah D. Herrmann, Robert Mark Adelman, Jessica E. Bodford, Oliver Graudejus, Morris A. Okun, and Virginia S. Y. Kwan (2016). The Effects of a Female Role Model on Academic Performance and Persistence of Women in STEM Courses. Basic and Applied Social Psychology, 38(5), 258-268. https://doi.org/10.1080/01973533.2016.1209757

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

DEW outcome in the chemistry replication (n à 65 with consent), coded 0 for A/B/C and 1 for D/E/W. Seven DEWs in control versus two in intervention; "The odds ratio of .23" was printed, with no standardized effect size.

> "The odds of receiving a DEW were .28 to 1 in the control group (seven participants had a DEW and 25 did not) and .06 to 1 in the intervention group (two participants had a DEW and 31 did not). The odds ratio of .23 indicates that the odds of receiving a DEW in the intervention condition were 77% less than in the control condition."
