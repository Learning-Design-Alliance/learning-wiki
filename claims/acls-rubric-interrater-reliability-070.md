Reliability analysis (Pearson's correlation) of 192 ratings of 96 students by two raters in the ACLS simulation course. The printed effect size was r = 0.70 (p < 0.001): "an inter-rater correlation of 0.70 (p < 0.001) was observed, indicating a good correlation between the two raters."
