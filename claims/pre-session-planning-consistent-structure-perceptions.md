## Evidence

### Alotiby Amna 2025

Alotiby Amna. (2025). Maintaining teaching consistency across repeated sessions using structured pre-session planning in medical education. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-025-08506-8

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional survey of 147 second-year medical students in four groups taught by a single tutor over nine weeks. Exact probability tests found no significant differences on structure and organization items; the article reports "p-values ranged from 0.098 to 0.769".

> "Across groups, 85.7–91.4% of students agreed the content was well-organized and aligned with learning objectives (p = 0.098–0.769)"
