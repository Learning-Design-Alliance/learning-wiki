Mutmainah 2019¶

Mutmainah, Rukayah, Mintasih Indriayu. (2019). Effectiveness of experiential learning-based teaching material in Mathematics. International Journal of Evaluation and Research in Education (IJERE), Vol. 8, No. 1, March 2019, pp. 57-63. http://iaescore.com/journals/index.php/IJERE

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest-posttest descriptive results in the quasi-experiment: experimental averages improved from "44.29" to "75.71" and control from "42.95" to "63.72". The article also reports most experimental students passed the KKM of 60 while several control students did not.

"The pretest average score of the experimental group is 44.29 and the posttest score is improved to 75.71. Meanwhile, the pretest average score of the control group is 42.95 and the posttest score is improved to 63.72."
