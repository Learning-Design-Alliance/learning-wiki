Review reports, from AbdElshafy et al. [22], a pre-post training study of 50 nurses caring for diabetic geriatric patients; the moderator (bachelor's degree) was associated with greater gains. No effect size printed.

"nurses' knowledge scores improved significantly post‐training across all content areas, with nurses holding a bachelor's degree in nursing demonstrating significantly greater gains than those with lower educational qualifications (p = 0.001)"
