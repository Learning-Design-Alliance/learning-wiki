# Perceived task difficulty correlates negatively with self-assessed understanding and with interest at essentially all measurement points, while interest and self-assessed understanding correlate positively throughout

## Subclaims
`q3 i3` Perceived task difficulty and self-assessed understanding are significantly negatively correlated at all 21 measuring points (r = −.38 to −.73). [→ Katrin Schuessler 2026](#katrin-schuessler-2026)
`q3 i0` Perceived task difficulty and interest are negatively correlated at all but one measuring point (r = −.09 to −.47). [→ Katrin Schuessler 2026 (2)](#katrin-schuessler-2026-2)

## Evidence

### Katrin Schuessler 2026

Katrin Schuessler, Jenna Koenen, Elke Sumfleth, Marvin Rost. (2026). Variations in Repeated Measures of Cognitive Load and Interest During Complex Learning Tasks. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10105-4

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Pairwise raw-data correlations across the 21 measurement points in the N = 360 sample; printed coefficients r = −.38 to −.73 indicate a large negative relationship, stronger when more content knowledge was provided.

> "Perceived task difficulty and self-assessed understanding (Fig. 10, upper right, light grey line) were significantly negatively correlated at all 21 measuring points. Correlation coefficients ranged from − .38 to − .73."

### Katrin Schuessler 2026 (2)

Katrin Schuessler, Jenna Koenen, Elke Sumfleth, Marvin Rost. (2026). Variations in Repeated Measures of Cognitive Load and Interest During Complex Learning Tasks. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10105-4

`q3 · i0` · `associational · r2`

Same pairwise correlation analysis of the N = 360 sample; printed coefficients r = −.09 to −.47, with the relationship more substantial when more content knowledge was provided.

> "As concerns perceived task difficulty and interest (Fig. 10, upper right, dark grey line), only one correlation (5%) was not significant. The remaining correlations were negative (r = −.09 to − .47)."

### Katrin Schuessler 2026 (3)

Katrin Schuessler, Jenna Koenen, Elke Sumfleth, Marvin Rost. (2026). Variations in Repeated Measures of Cognitive Load and Interest During Complex Learning Tasks. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10105-4

`q3 · i1` · `associational · r2`

> "Interest and self-assessed understanding (Fig. 10, lower left, light grey line) were significantly positively correlated at all 21 measuring points. Correlation coefficients ranged from .18. up to .65."
