Uz, R., & Uzun, A. (2018). The influence of blended learning environment on self-regulated and self-directed learning skills of learners. European Journal of Educational Research, 7(4), 877-886. https://doi.org/10.12973/eu-jer.7.4.877

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative phase of the explanatory sequential mixed method: semi-structured interviews with seven open-ended questions with five volunteer experimental-group students. Students reported increased participation, flexible studying hours, feedback via end-of-video mini tests and forums, and raised motivation.

> "Yes, my participation to the course increased thanks to the Moodle platform. Because the accessibility of the course increased with this platform and it provided chances to follow and understand the course"
