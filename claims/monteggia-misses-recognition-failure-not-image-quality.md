### Choi Min Hyeok 2025

Choi Min Hyeok, Moon Min Hui, Kang Suk Woong, Kim Kyeong Baek, Ahn Tae Young, Bae Jung Yun. (2025). Challenges in Diagnosing Pediatric Monteggia Fractures: Role of Clinical Experience and Imaging. Medicina. https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina61081457

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the retrospective 120-case study, contrasting Monteggia with other fracture types where "suboptimal image quality or splinting were more likely to have contributed". The article states no significant correlation between radiographic quality and diagnostic accuracy was demonstrated.

> "Radiographic factors, such as image quality, splint presence, and patient age, had a minimal impact on the diagnosis of Monteggia in our study. Missed cases were primarily due to recognition failure rather than inadequate imaging."
