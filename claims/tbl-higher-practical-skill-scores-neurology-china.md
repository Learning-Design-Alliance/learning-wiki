### Fu Jie 2026

Fu Jie, Chen Xiu, Li Jinglun, Peng Lilei. (2026). TBL alone or combined with other teaching methods for neurology education in China: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09468-1

`q3 · i3` · `quant-synthesis · r3`

Meta-analysis of 14 RCTs with 1056 students (528 per arm), random-effects model due to heterogeneity (I2 = 92%). The pooled estimate favored TBL: "SMD = 1.72, 95% CI: 1.19 to 2.24, p < 0.00001".

> "The results indicated that compared to traditional LBL, TBL was associated with higher practical skill scores (SMD = 1.72, 95% CI: 1.19 to 2.24, p < 0.00001) (Fig. 5)."
