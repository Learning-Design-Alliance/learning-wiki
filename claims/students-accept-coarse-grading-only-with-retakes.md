### David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter 2021

David J. Webb and Wendell H. Potter. (2021). Gender-grade-gap zeroed out under a specific intro-physics assessment regime. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2102.10451)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Anonymous post-course survey using a 5-point Likert scale on two statements about choosing a CLASP class with acceptable/unacceptable exam grading, without and with retake exams offered. The article concludes students were mostly happy with the grading method only if retakes were offered.

> "Only 36% of the students were either neutral to or supportive of the first statement but 91% of the students were neutral to or supportive of the second statement."
