## Subclaims

## Evidence

### Wadha H. Al-Otaibi 2017

Wadha H. Al-Otaibi. (2017). The Effectiveness of Blackboard-Based Blended Teaching in the Development of Academic Achievement, Study Skills and Self-Confidence among Students of Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University. International Education Studies, 10(11). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n11p100

`q2 · i?`

Quasi-experimental nonequivalent control group study of 38 female students (experimental 21, control 17); ANCOVA on post-achievement test adjusted for pre-test. The article reports "statistically significant differences in the total academic achievement and its sub-levels" favoring the experimental group (means 17.14 vs 6.82).

> "Table 10 shows statistically significant differences in the total academic achievement and its sub-levels. (F) Value of total academic achievement and its lower and higher levels rated (103.3, 27.6 and 87.9,) respectively."
