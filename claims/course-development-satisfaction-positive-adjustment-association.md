## Evidence

### Zhang J and Yin Y 2026

Zhang J and Yin Y (2026). A study on AI-enabled course development, AI proficiency, and first-year students' academic and psychological adjustment. Front. Psychol. 17:1909803. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1909803

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple regression analysis in a cross-sectional survey of 551 first-year students showed standardized β = 0.31 for emotional management, β = 0.28 for stress coping, and β = 0.34 for academic adjustment (all p < 0.001), with the article reporting that "Higher course development satisfaction is associated with better emotional management, stress coping, and academic adjustment."

> "Higher course development satisfaction is associated with better emotional management, stress coping, and academic adjustment; higher AI proficiency is associated with better outcomes on the same three indicators."
