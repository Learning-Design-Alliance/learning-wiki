Vaden-Kiernan 2016¶

Vaden-Kiernan, M., Borman, G., Caverly, S., Bell, N., Ruiz de Castilla, V., Sullivan, K., & Fleming, G. (2016). Findings from a Multi-Year Scale-Up Effectiveness Trial of Open-Court Reading (Imagine It!). SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567512

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Implementation study across two years (304 teachers: 147 Year 1, 157 Year 2; 49.4% from treatment schools), with fidelity captured via classroom observations, interviews, and surveys, analyzed as a latent construct of dosage, adherence, quality of delivery, and student responsiveness. No effect size is printed.

"Implementation study results from the combined (professional development and classroom) OCR fidelity of implementation model indicated that the program was implemented with adequate to high levels of fidelity across the treatment schools in the first and second year and no evidence of contamination in the control schools in the study."
