Merrill, R., Hamdan, A., Bista, A., & Franklin, S. (2024). Physics Faculty and Empathy in Academic Spaces. Originally published 2023. [doi:10.22318/icls2024.398159](https://doi.org/10.22318/icls2024.398159)

`q1 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview study of eight physics faculty using a semi-structured protocol and constructivist grounded theory coding; results from two participants, Sean and Jacob. The article reports that "Contextual information, encompassing personal and environmental details, aids faculty members in noticing and evaluating situations."
