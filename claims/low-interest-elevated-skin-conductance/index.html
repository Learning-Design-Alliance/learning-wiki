Evidence¶

Levy 2025¶

Levy, O., Shadi, T., Korisky, A., Bleichner, M. G., & Zion Golumbic, E. (2025). Differential effects of external noise and situational interest on neurophysiological responses during video based learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00392-5

q3 · i2 · causal · r1

Paired comparisons of GSR metrics across high vs low-interest segments in the lab study (n=32). The authors had expected higher arousal for high-interest content; "Cohen’s d=0.76" (tonic) and "Cohen’s d=0.63" (phasic) were printed for the observed direction.

"Both main metrics showed significantly higher activity during low - compared to high-interest segments [mean tonic activity: t(31)=4.326, p<0.001 , Cohen’s d=0.76 ; mean phasic activity: t(31)=3.566, p=0.001, Cohen’s d=0.63; Figure 4]."
