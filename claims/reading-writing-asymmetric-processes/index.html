Döhla D and Heim S 2016¶

Döhla D and Heim S (2016). Developmental Dyslexia and Dysgraphia: What can We Learn from the One About the Other? Front. Psychol. 6:2045. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.02045

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review summarizes Winkes (2014)'s five reasons why writing is more demanding, including that "Phoneme-to-grapheme-correspondence is much more complex than GPC" and that recall of orthographic representations is a higher function than recognition. Word reading and writing performance correlate highly (r = 0.68 to 0.86, Ehri, 2000).
