### Sze Kai Ping 2026

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i3` · `associational · r1`

Participant-level correlation analysis in the same blinded within-participant evaluation of 73 postgraduate FM doctors. The scores were "positively correlated" (r=0.49, 95% CI 0.30-0.65; P<.001), but the wide CI spans a weak to moderate association, indicating limited precision.

> "GenAI-assisted and educator-crafted total scores were positively correlated (r=0.49, 95% CI 0.30-0.65; P<.001)"

### Sze Kai Ping 2026 (2)

Sze Kai Ping, Lim Jia Qing, Loke Yng Miin, Ding Teck Yong Gabriel, Wee Wei Chieh, Chen Tongyuan, Wu Jingkai Lawrence, Bansal Vivek, Fong Qi Wei, Chow Minyang. (2026). Human-Edited Generative AI-Assisted Multiple-Choice Questions in Postgraduate Family Medicine: Blinded Cross-Sectional Comparative Psychometric Study. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/100179

`q3 · i?` · `design · r2`

Bland-Altman agreement analysis of paired total scores from the same evaluation. Limits of agreement mean an individual's GenAI-assisted score could be "approximately 4 points higher to 8 points lower" than their educator-crafted score, so the forms are not interchangeable.

> "Bland-Altman analysis revealed a mean difference of −1.97 between GenAI-generated and educator-crafted items, with 95% limits of agreement ranging from −8.22 to 4.28"
