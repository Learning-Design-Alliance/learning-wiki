## Evidence

### Aleksandra Bereznikova 2026

Aleksandra Bereznikova, Emily Theophilou, Davinia Hernández-Leo. (2026). The influence of socratic dialogue within GenAI on critical thinking and technology perception. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10693-0

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subjects workshop study; 80 participants (41 undergraduate, 27 graduate, 12 teachers), 66 valid after outlier removal. Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of post-interaction critical thinking scores, "statistically significant (W = 76.0, p < 0.001)". No effect size printed for the overall test.

> "the mean score after engaging with the socratic genAI for the outside-domain condition was 27.8 (SD = 2.6), compared to 30.0 (SD = 2.7) for the within-domain condition. A Wilcoxon signed-rank test confirmed that this increase was statistically significant (W = 76.0, p < 0.001)"
