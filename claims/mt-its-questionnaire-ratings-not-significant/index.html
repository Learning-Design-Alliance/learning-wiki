Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski 2023¶

Vladimir Zotov & Eric Kramkowski. (2023). Moving-Target Intelligent Tutoring System for Marksmanship Training. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-022-00308-z

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Post-experimental five-point questionnaires on structure, number of targets, usefulness, learning something new, and training merit, summarized in Table 7 with higher MT-ITS means on each item. Equivalence was not tested.

"Overall, the participants in the MT-ITS condition gave more favourable ratings than those in the Standard condition, but the differences were not significant."
