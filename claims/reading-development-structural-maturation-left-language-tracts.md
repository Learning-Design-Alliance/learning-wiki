## Evidence

### Chyl 2021

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r2`

The review reports, citing longitudinal structural MRI studies, that "gains in reading were positively correlated with GMV in the left superior temporal gyrus (STG) at the age of 7.5", and that GMV decrease in parietal and inferior frontal regions was associated with better word decoding, reading fluency and rapid automatized naming.

> "gains in reading were positively correlated with GMV in the left superior temporal gyrus (STG) at the age of 7.5"

### Chyl 2021 (2)

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

`q2 · i?` · `review · r3`

The review reports, citing diffusion MRI studies, that "children with above-average reading skills initially presented low FA of the left AF and ILF that increased over a 3 year period", consistent with faster white matter maturation marking typical reading development.

> "children with above-average reading skills initially presented low FA of the left AF and ILF that increased over a 3 year period"
