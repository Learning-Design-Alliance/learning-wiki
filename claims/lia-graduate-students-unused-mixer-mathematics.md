## Evidence

### DeVore 2013

DeVore, S., Gauthier, A., Levy, J., & Singh, C. (2013). Improving Students' Understanding of Lock-In Amplifiers. 2013 PERC Proceedings. https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2013.pr.018

`q2 · i?` · `design · r3`

Think-aloud interviews with six physics graduate students who use LIAs in their research. The article reports that in the pretest "none made use of the relevant equations for the multiplication of the input and reference signals in the mixer".

> "Of the six students interviewed, none made use of the relevant equations for the multiplication of the input and reference signals in the mixer to gain insight into what output they should expect in the questions posed in the pre1test."
