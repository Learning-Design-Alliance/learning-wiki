## Evidence

### L. Gajewski 2016

L. Gajewski, J. Chołoniewski, J. A. Hołyst. (2016). Key courses of academic curriculum uncovered by data mining of students' grades. arXiv preprint. [doi:10.12693/aphyspola.129.1071](https://doi.org/10.12693/aphyspola.129.1071)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Permutation control analyses: S-type shuffling (permuting each student's grades across courses) leaves mean correlations of 0.258±0.005, about 20% below the original 0.326±0.006, while C-type shuffling destroys correlations (mean 0.0001±0.007).

> "The S-shuﬄing of students grades dim inishes the role of the ﬁrst PC that explains 30% of the total variance (Fig. 4) and the ﬁrst PC after C-shuﬄing explains only 10% of the total variance."
