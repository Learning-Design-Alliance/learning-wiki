`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Closed-response challenge rankings from the supplemental student survey (Fig. 3) ranked not being able to do experiments "with physical materials" as the most common major challenge, followed by lacking "a partner/group to help conduct experiments."

> "The most common major challenge that students reported was not being able to do experiments with physical materials (Figure 3). The second most common challenge (on average) was not "having a partner/group to help conduct experiments"."
