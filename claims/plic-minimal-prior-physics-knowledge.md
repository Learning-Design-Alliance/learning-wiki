Katherine N. Quinn, Carl Wieman, and N.G. Holmes. (2018). Interview validation of the Physics Lab Inventory of Critical thinking (PLIC). [doi:10.1119/perc.2017.pr.076](https://doi.org/10.1119/perc.2017.pr.076)

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r2`

Qualitative interview finding from the validation study: because the PLIC aims to measure critical thinking skills rather than general physics knowledge, great care was taken to minimize the need for prior physics concept knowledge, and interviews with non-physics majors supported this design goal.

> "Through interviews conducted with non-physics majors who have taken at least one physics course, we were able to conclude that although some exposure to physics is necessary, the need for prior physics content knowledge is minimal and appropriate for assessing learning in the context of a physics lab course."
