## Evidence

### Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman 2015

Chandralekha Singh and Emily Marshman. (2015). Student difficulties with the probability distribution for measuring energy and position in quantum mechanics. Originally published 2011. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1509.04081](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1509.04081)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Multiple-choice survey of upper-level undergraduates (N = 210) after traditional instruction at six U.S. universities; on questions 1 and 3 about position measurement at t = 0, "approximately 60% of the students incorrectly responded" with the expectation-value integral. Interviews suggest some students confused the probability of measuring position with the expectation value of position.

> "Table I shows that approximately 60% of the students incorrectly responded that the probability of measuring position is ∫ 𝑥|Ψ(𝑥, 0)|2𝑑𝑥𝑥+𝑑𝑥𝑥 ."
