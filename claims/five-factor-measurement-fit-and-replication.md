# The five-factor measurement representation (PNT, LOU, ACS, SRL, CT) showed unusually close fit, replicated in a held-out subsample, and the constructs did not collapse into one general tendency

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 studies · 3 associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` The hypothesised five-factor measurement model showed unusually close approximate fit (CFI = 1.000, RMSEA = 0.000, SRMR = 0.020) with standardised loadings of 0.697–0.772. [→ Yang Z 2026](#yang-z-2026)
`q2 i?` A five-factor representation derived in a calibration subsample was reproduced with excellent fit in the held-out validation subsample (CFI = 0.999, RMSEA = 0.006). [→ Yang Z 2026 (2)](#yang-z-2026-2)
`q2 i?` Collapsing LOU with SRL, with CT, or into a single factor produced markedly poorer fit, indicating the focal constructs did not collapse into a single general self-reported learning tendency. [→ Yang Z 2026 (3)](#yang-z-2026-3)

## Evidence

### Yang Z 2026

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Primary measurement-model assessment in the full student sample; standardised loadings ranged from 0.697 to 0.772 and all HTMT values were below 0.85 (maximum 0.534). The article cautions the unusually close fit should not be treated as definitive evidence of measurement validity.

> "The hypothesised five-factor measurement model showed unusually close approximate fit, χ2(265) = 252.76, p = 0.695, CFI = 1.000, TLI = 1.000, RMSEA = 0.000, 90% CI [0.000, 0.010], and SRMR = 0.020."

### Yang Z 2026 (2)

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Internal split-sample check: parallel analysis in a 502-respondent calibration subsample retained five factors, and the resulting representation was evaluated in the held-out validation subsample. The article notes this is internal replication, not independent external validation.

> "The validation CFA showed excellent fit, χ2(265) = 269.02, p = 0.420, CFI = 0.999, TLI = 0.999, RMSEA = 0.006, and SRMR = 0.030."

### Yang Z 2026 (3)

Yang Z, Yang Y and Liu W (2026) Self-reported learning-oriented GenAI use under perceived teacher-referenced normative tension in Chinese higher education. Front. Psychol. 17:1969743. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1969743

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Alternative measurement-model comparisons. Combining LOU and SRL produced markedly poorer fit (χ2 = 1367.31, CFI = 0.897) and combining LOU, SRL, and CT deteriorated further (χ2 = 2439.30, CFI = 0.798), supporting empirical distinctiveness of the constructs.

> "The single-factor model fit poorly, χ2 = 4869.15, CFI = 0.571, RMSEA = 0.129, indicating that the focal constructs did not collapse into a single general self-reported learning tendency."
