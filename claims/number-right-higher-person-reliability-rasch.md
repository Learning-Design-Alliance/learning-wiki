## Evidence

### Cecilio-Fernandes D 2017

Cecilio-Fernandes D, Medema H, Collares CF, Schuwirth L, Cohen-Schotanus J, Tio RA. (2017). Comparison of formula and number-right scoring in undergraduate medical training: a Rasch model analysis. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-017-1051-8

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Rasch analysis of a 2×2 crossover experiment in which 298 students from four Dutch medical schools took two 100-item tests under both scoring conditions. The article reports the printed coefficients: "0.80 and 0.82" versus "0.73 and 0.77".

> "However, the person reliability coefficients were higher in the number-right (0.80 and 0.82) than in the formula-scoring condition (0.73 and 0.77) on Tests 1 and 2, respectively."
