## Evidence

### Aracı 2023

Aracı, N., Melekoğlu, M. A., & Çetin, M. E. (2023). Impact of a Mnemonic Strategy on Learning Science Concepts for Middle School Students With Specific Learning Disabilities. Learning Disabilities: A Contemporary Journal, 21(1), 69-85. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1386167

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Benchmark administration of the science concept measurement tool to 20 typically developing fourth-grade students in two public primary schools. Students answered 69% of questions correctly; the authors compare this to the experimental group's posttest mean of 7.61.

> "The percentage of questions answered correctly was 69%, and the students with typical development answered seven out of the 10 questions correctly. In summary, primary school students with typical development have learned most of the targeted science concepts."
