## Evidence

### Conroy Elizabeth J. 2015

Conroy Elizabeth J., Kirkham Jamie J., Bellis Jennifer R., Peak Matthew, Smyth Rosalind L., Williamson Paula R., Pirmohamed Munir. (2015). A pilot randomised controlled trial to assess the utility of an e‐learning package that trains users in adverse drug reaction causality. International Journal of Pharmacy Practice. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijpp.12197

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' discussion of the pilot RCT's feasibility, in which 57 trainees were randomised and 35 completed. The article compares this with a similar e-learning RCT by Gordon et al. that recruited 206 of 1150 (17.9%) with 113 completers (54.9%), calling the figures comparable with this study's 57 from 379 (15.0%) and 35 completers (59.5%).

> "First, it shows that a novel trial design where participants can take part remotely is feasible and results in a reasonable proportion of participants completing the trial; overall, 61.4% of participants who were randomised completed the trial."
