## Evidence

### Avi Segal 2018

Avi Segal, Yossi Ben David, Joseph Jay Williams, Kobi Gal, Yaar Shalom. (2018). Combining Difficulty Ranking with Multi-Armed Bandits to Sequence Educational Content. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-93846-2_59](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-93846-2_59)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Field-study satisfaction survey on a 3-point agreement scale covering helpfulness, ease of use, adaptivity, match to class material, and future appeal. A one-way ANOVA found a significant difference across conditions (p < 0.05); no effect size is printed.

> "The average score on the agreement with positive characteristics of the system was signiﬁcantly different across the three conditions, with the highest score for the MAPLE algorithm at 2.39, compared to the Ascending algorithm (2.23) and the YBKT algorithm (2.20) (one way Anova,p< 0.05)."
