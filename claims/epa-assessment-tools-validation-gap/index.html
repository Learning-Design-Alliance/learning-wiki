Frequency analysis of EPA assessment tools across the included studies in this systematic review. The review reports "Global Entrustment Scales and standardised rubrics were the most used tools", and notes validation efforts in 12.8% (n = 61) of studies, with faculty the most common evaluators (58.4%, n = 279).

"Global Entrustment Scales and standardised rubrics were the most used tools for the Assessment of EPAs (referred in 30.8% (n = 147) and 20.9% (n = 100) of the studies respectively)."
