## Evidence

### Ding Chun 2019

Ding Chun, Li Shengguo, Chen Baihua. (2019). Effectiveness of flipped classroom combined with team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning on ophthalmology teaching for eight-year program students. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-019-1861-y

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-class feedback questionnaire (Ramsden's CEQ and Biggs's research process questionnaire) completed by all 67 students; Mann-Whitney U tests found significant group differences on teamwork ability (p = 0.000), analytical skills (p = 0.000), and problem-solving skills (p = 0.002).

> "Significant differences were found between the FC-TCLEBL group and the LBC group with regard to the teamwork ability questionnaire (p = 0.000), analytical skills evaluation scale (p = 0.000), and problem-solving skills evaluation scale (p = 0.002)."
