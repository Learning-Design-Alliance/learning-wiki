5Essentials survey trend analysis (grades 6-12, adjusted for demographics; respondents 138,425-168,648 per year) comparing 2021 to 2019 and 2011 baselines. High school reports "improved by 0.13, 0.17, and 0.30 standard deviations" on the three relationship measures during the remote year; no standardized effect size beyond these s.d. changes is printed.

"Academic Personalism, Student-Teacher Trust, and Peer Relationships improved by 0.13, 0.17, and 0.30 standard deviations (s.d.), respectively, at the high school level from 2019 to 2021, reaching levels that were 0.40 s.d. above reports in 2011."
