## Evidence

### Á. Suárez 2022

Á. Suárez, D. Baccino, M. Monteiro, and A. C. Martí. (2022). Differences in the attitudes and beliefs about science of students in the physics-mathematics and life sciences areas and their impact on teaching. Revista Mexicana de Física E 19 020207. https://doi.org/10.31349/RevMexFisE.19.020207

`q2 · i2` · `associational · r1`

Cross-sectional CLASS survey administered once to first-year General Physics I students at Universidad de la República (79 physical sciences, 69 life sciences), comparing favorable-response percentages with expert responses; the printed overall means are 49.8 (LS) and 64.1 (PhS), difference 14.3, effect size 0.8, U test p=0.0001.

> "First, when considering the overall result of CLASS (All categories), we found a significant difference, with a relatively large effect size (0.8), in the degree of agreement with the experts between both groups of students."
