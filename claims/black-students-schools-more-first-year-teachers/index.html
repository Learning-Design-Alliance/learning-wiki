Sarah Mehrotra 2021¶

Sarah Mehrotra, Ivy S. Morgan, Allison Socol. (2021). Getting Black Students Better Access to Non-Novice Teachers. The Education Trust. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED617544

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive CRDC 2017-18 analysis of first-year teacher percentages by school Black-enrollment quartile. The report states "8% of the teaching workforce consists of first‑year teachers" in highest-quartile schools versus 5% in lowest, and notes Louisiana and Mississippi show almost threefold differences.

"On nationwide average, at schools serving the highest percentages of Black students, 8% of the teaching workforce consists of first‑year teachers; whereas, at schools serving the lowest percentages of Black students, 5% are first‑year teachers."
