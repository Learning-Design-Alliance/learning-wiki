## Evidence

### Czeskleba A. 2020

Czeskleba A., Holzhausen Y., Peters H. (2020). Clinical reasoning for acute dyspnoea: comparison between final-year medical students from discipline- and competency-based undergraduate programmes. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-020-02055-y

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

ASCLIRE results across six video-based dyspnoea cases show case-level variation in all three measures; time was longest for lung oedema and shortest for pneumonia, and accuracy highest for pneumonia and lowest for unstable ventricular tachycardia.

> "The number of chosen relevant diagnostic tests varied across all students (Fig. 1), with the highest number of tests chosen for the case of pulmonary artery embolism and the lowest number chosen for the case of opioid intoxication in comparison to the expert standard."
