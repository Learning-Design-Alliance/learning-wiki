Keyword-based tracking of 'coup theory' mentions across 1,800 API responses in the T=0 audit, shown in Fig. 2. The baseline receives the contested framing "in 7.9% of responses" while Low Tier receives it in 2.6%. The authors argue withholding contested geopolitical framing denies marginalized learners critical interpretive tools.
