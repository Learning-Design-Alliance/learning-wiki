`q2 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r2`

The review reports this finding from Ellis et al. (2018), a retrospective cohort study of 1,320 resident-performed phacoemulsification surgeries rated moderate risk of bias; it adds that early-exposure cohort rates "remained stable at 1–2% throughout their entire training".

> "Ellis et al. highlighted those residents introduced to cataract surgery earlier in their training demonstrated significantly lower rates (2.1% compared to 7.6%) of anterior vitrectomy."
