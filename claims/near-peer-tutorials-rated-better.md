# Students rated their near-peer tutored sessions more positively than staff-tutored sessions on most tutorial-evaluation items in both halves of the module

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` Overall tutorial evaluation favoured near-peer tutors in both halves of the module (tutorials 1-2: 3.59 vs 3.49, p = 0.046; tutorials 3-4: 3.65 vs 3.45, p = 0.001). [→ Widyahening Indah S. 2019](#widyahening-indah-s-2019)
`q3 i?` After crossover, near-peer tutors were rated significantly better on items including tutor competence, enjoyment, learning, application, theory-practice combination and interaction, with fewer unanswered questions reported. [→ Widyahening Indah S. 2019 (2)](#widyahening-indah-s-2019-2)

## Evidence

### Widyahening Indah S. 2019

Widyahening Indah S., Findyartini Ardi, Ranakusuma Respati W., Dewiasty Esthika, Harimurti Kuntjoro. (2019). Evaluation of the role of near-peer teaching in critical appraisal skills learning: a randomized crossover trial. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5c39.b55b

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Student evaluation of tutors on a 5-point Likert questionnaire (adapted from Knobe et al.) after each pair of sessions. Table 3 reports overall means favouring near-peer tutors in both halves and significant item-level differences such as "I was able to learn a lot" (tutorials 3-4: 4.42 vs 3.76, p < 0.001).

> "When the students were asked to evaluate the tutorial process, they assigned more positive scores to the near-peer tutor for the majority of evaluation items (Table 3)."

### Widyahening Indah S. 2019 (2)

Widyahening Indah S., Findyartini Ardi, Ranakusuma Respati W., Dewiasty Esthika, Harimurti Kuntjoro. (2019). Evaluation of the role of near-peer teaching in critical appraisal skills learning: a randomized crossover trial. International Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.5116/ijme.5c39.b55b

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Post-crossover evaluations (tutorials 3-4, n=83 near-peer vs n=150 staff) showed significant advantages for near-peer tutors on items 1-5, 8 and 9 (e.g. tutor competence t = -2.958, p = 0.003; lessons enjoyable t = -4.218, p < 0.001).

> "In the tutor evaluations, the students rated their near-peer tutored sessions as better than those tutored by staff in most respects."
