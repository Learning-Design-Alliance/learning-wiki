# Motivational regulation strategies negatively predict academic performance directly but positively via cognitive learning, which mediates the relationship

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` MRS use had a significant negative direct effect on academic performance (β=-0.345, p<0.01). [→ Yun Heoncheol 2021](#yun-heoncheol-2021)
`q2 i?` MRS use had a significant positive indirect effect on academic performance via CLS (β=0.348, p<0.001), indicating mediation by cognitive learning. [→ Yun Heoncheol 2021 (2)](#yun-heoncheol-2021-2)

## Evidence

### Yun Heoncheol 2021

Yun Heoncheol, Kim Seon, Chung Eun-Kyung. (2021). Exploring the structural relationships between medical students’ motivational regulation, cognitive learning, and academic performance in online learning. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2021.193

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

SEM structural model result from the 510-student online-learning survey; the direct path from MRS to GPA was negative and significant, reported as "a negative influence on academic performance (β=-0.345, p<0.01)".

> "However, the use of MRSs has a negative influence on academic performance (β=-0.345, p<0.01)."

### Yun Heoncheol 2021 (2)

Yun Heoncheol, Kim Seon, Chung Eun-Kyung. (2021). Exploring the structural relationships between medical students’ motivational regulation, cognitive learning, and academic performance in online learning. Korean Journal of Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.3946/kjme.2021.193

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

> "Interestingly, the result of model indirect showed that MRS indirectly affected academic performance via CLS (β=0.348, p<0.001)."
