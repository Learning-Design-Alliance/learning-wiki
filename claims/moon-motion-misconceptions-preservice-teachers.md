### Alejandro Gangui 2010

Alejandro Gangui, María Iglesias & Cynthia Quinteros. (2010). Some remarks on a current study involving preservice elementary teachers and some basic astronomical phenomena. Proceedings IAU Symposium No. 260. [doi:10.1017/s1743921311003164](https://doi.org/10.1017/s1743921311003164)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Results from Q6–Q8 on the Moon's movements and the three-body system: "nearly 50% of the responses" said the Moon just translates. There was no correct answer to Q6 on the same-face phenomenon, and none of the students gave a detailed correct explanation for the origin of a lunar eclipse beyond choosing three-body alignment.

> "most of the inquired students chose to answer that the Moo n does not rotate on its axis (Fig. 5, right panel); for them it just translates through the sky (nearly 50% of the responses)"
