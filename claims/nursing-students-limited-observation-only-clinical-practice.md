## Evidence

### Jeon Jaehee 2020

Jeon Jaehee, Kim Jin Hee, Choi Eun Hee. (2020). Needs Assessment for a VR-Based Adult Nursing Simulation Training Program for Korean Nursing Students: A Qualitative Study Using Focus Group Interviews. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17238880

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Focus group interviews with 14 Korean nursing students who had completed clinical practice and adult nursing simulation training, analyzed with Colaizzi's phenomenological methodology. Participants reported that "they were often only allowed to take vital signs or a blood sugar test during training."

> "Participants felt that the training was extremely limited, as they were often only allowed to take vital signs or a blood sugar test during training; thus, often, all they could do was to follow the nurses around."
