## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Latent profile analysis of 936 high school students identifies four learning engagement profiles, with the third ('High Vigor-High Dedication-High Absorption') type comprising 57.4% of the sample. [→ Zhou Tian 2025](#zhou-tian-2025)

## Evidence

### Zhou Tian 2025

Zhou Tian, Zhou Xuejun, Wang Baozhu. (2025). Latent profiles of learning engagement and anxiety in high school students: the mediating role of academic self-efficacy. BMC Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-025-03114-z

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Latent profile analysis (Mplus 8.0) of cross-sectional questionnaire data from 936 students selected the four-class model, guided by fit indices and parsimony. The profile sizes are as printed: the third category 'accounting for 57.4%'.

> "The first category includes 206 individuals, accounting for 22%; the second category includes 91 individuals, accounting for 9.7%; the third category includes 537 individuals, accounting for 57.4%; and the fourth category includes 102 individuals, accounting for 10.9%."
