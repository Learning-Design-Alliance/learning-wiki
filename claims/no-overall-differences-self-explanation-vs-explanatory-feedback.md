## Evidence

### Killingsworth 2015

Killingsworth, S. S., Clark, D. B., & Adams, D. M. (2015). Self-Explanation and Explanatory Feedback in Games: Individual Differences, Gameplay, and Learning. International Journal of Education in Mathematics, Science and Technology, 3(3), 162-186. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1066360

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Repeated-measures ANOVA comparing self-explanation (collapsed across partial and full) to explanatory feedback conditions. Equivalence was not tested; the null result does not establish equivalence.

> "the multivariate effect of game condition was not significant, λ = .952, F(2, 82) = 2.074, p = .132, η2 = .048, and neither was interaction between game condition and test administration, λ = .978, F(2, 82) = .939, p = .395, η2= .022."
