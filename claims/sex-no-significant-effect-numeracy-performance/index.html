Evidence¶

Gbenga-Akanmu & Jegede 2019¶

Gbenga-Akanmu, T. O., & Jegede, P. O. (2019). Influence of Meta-cognitive Training and Cognitive Styles on Numeracy Achievement of Nigerian Pupils’ in Computer-mediated Classrooms. World Journal of Education, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v9n1p221

q2 · i? · causal · r1

ANCOVA (Table 2) of the quasi-experiment with sex and pre-test score as covariates reports a non-significant sex effect (F = 0.14; p = 0.7152); equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"The results also showed that sex has no significant influence (F = 0.14; p>0.05) on pupils’ performance."
