## Evidence

### Michalis Xenos 2019

Michalis Xenos, Vasiliki Velli. (2019). A Serious Game for Introducing Software Engineering Ethics to University Students. 21st International Conference on Interactive Collaborative Learning, ICL2018, Kos, Greece. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-11932-4_55)

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Within-subject pre-post study of 144 students in the CEID_Y232 Software Engineering course (2017-2018); a 10-item multiple-choice knowledge test was given after the lecture and repeated after playing all six episodes. Mean normalized learning gain was 35.04% (SD=46.01). The article reports "post-test scores were statistically significantly higher than pre-test scores".

> "a Wilcoxon Signed-Ranks test was run, and the output indicated that post-test scores were statistically significantly higher than pre-test scores (Z=8.321, p<.000)"
