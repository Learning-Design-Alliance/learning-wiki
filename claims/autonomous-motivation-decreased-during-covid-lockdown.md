### Benneker 2023

Benneker, I. M. B., Lee, N. C. & van Atteveldt, N. (2023). Mindset and perceived parental support of autonomy safeguard adolescents' autonomous motivation during COVID-19 home-based learning. npj Science of Learning 8:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00153-2

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Within-subject comparison of N = 97 Dutch adolescents measured in February 2020 and again three weeks into the school closure, using the SRQ-A autonomous motivation subscales. Motivation fell from M = 2.80 to M = 2.68, "t (96) = 2.46, p = 0.017"; no effect size printed.

> "A paired t-test showed that adolescents ' autonomous motivation signi fi cantly decreased during the lockdown ( M = 2.68, SD = 0.62) compared to pre-measurement ( M = 2.80, SD = 0.45); (t (96) = 2.46, p = 0.017)."
