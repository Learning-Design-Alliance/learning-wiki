The article cites Kuiper and Ryan's (2018) continuous time modelling result as evidence that regression-based tests do not pin down what theoretical arrows mean, since "the sign of the cross-lagged effects" can change with assessment intervals. This is an attributed statistical finding, not this article's own data.

> "Kuiper and Ryan (2018) statistically showed that even the sign of the cross-lagged effects (e.g., the effects of a motivation construct on an outcome variable) can be different when the time intervals between the assessments are different"
