## Evidence

### Boeker M 2013

Boeker M, Jansen L, Grewe N, Röhl J, Schober D, Seddig-Raufie D, Schulz S. (2013). Effects of Guideline-Based Training on the Quality of Formal Ontologies: A Randomized Controlled Trial. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0061425

`q1 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion of the randomized controlled trial: the interventional training consisted of two 105-minute units per topic plus a free training session within 2.5 days, and recall of fresh knowledge might be inhibited by retroactive interference.

> "The simplest explanation for the result of our study would be that students were overloaded with too much information in too short a time. The complete interventional training sessions were given in 2.5 days."
