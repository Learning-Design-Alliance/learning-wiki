In his inaugural-lecture notes Majorana evaluates two opposite presentation methods — the historical method, which explains how the new formalism arose from experimental indications, and the mathematical method — and states that each, used exclusively, has very serious drawbacks.

> "Each one of these two methods, if exclusively applied, has ve ry serious drawbacks."
