## Evidence

### Lehmann R 2015

Lehmann R, Thiessen C, Frick B, Bosse HM, Nikendei C, Hoffmann GF, Tönshoff B, Huwendiek S. (2015). Improving Pediatric Basic Life Support Performance Through Blended Learning With Web-Based Virtual Patients: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.4141

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Global competence ratings (competent, borderline, not competent) were compared with Fisher's exact test; only performances rated competent concordantly by both blinded raters counted. Differences favored the intervention group at both time points.

> "The global ratings of competence showed significant differences in favor of the intervention group, again already at t1 and continuing at t2 (0/30 CG vs 5/27 IG rated “competent”, P=.02; 17/30 CG vs 23/27 IG, P=.02, respectively)."
