A Systematic Meta-analysis of the Reliability and Validity of Subjective Cognitive Load Questionnaires in Experimental Multimedia Learning Research. `q3 · i?` · `quant-synthesis · r?`

Moderator analyses within the meta-analysis of 67 experiments tested four predefined CLT-related moderators (educational setting, domain, presentation mode, number of response options). The article reports the moderators "did not differ" in their effect on Cronbach's alpha; no effect size is printed for these null moderator effects.

> "moderator analyses showed that reliabil ity estimates of the cognitive load questionnaires did not differ between educational settings, domains of the instructional materials, presentation modes, or number of scale points."
