### Twyford 1955

Twyford, L., abstractor. (1955). Audio-Visual Communication Review research abstracts: training device effectiveness studies. Audio-Visual Communication Review. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Audio-Visual+Communication+Review

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r?`

Controlled comparison at a Naval base: one group trained with a wiring board checked against calculated meter readings, a comparable group learned by a diagram method drawing circuits, compared with regular basic electricity instruction. The abstract reports "Learning how to solve electrical problems was not improved by use of either training device."

> "Learning how to solve electrical problems was not improved by use of either training device. The diagram and the wiring board improved learning how to use meters to an equal extent."
