### Will Yeadon 2026

Will Yeadon, Tom Hardy, Paul Mackay, and Elise Agra. (2026). LLM-as-a-judge validity is strongly task-dependent across physics assessment formats. arXiv. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.14732](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.14732)

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r2`

Inter-rater reliability baseline across five independent blinded markers of 60 physics essay scripts. The article reports "an average pairwise Spearman correlation of justρ= 0.054", showing expert humans fail to reach consensus on rank-ordering.

> "Rank-order agreement between human markers was very limited, with an average pairwise Spearman correlation of justρ= 0.054 and a single-rater Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC2) of 0.035 (95% CI: [−0.01,0.11])."
