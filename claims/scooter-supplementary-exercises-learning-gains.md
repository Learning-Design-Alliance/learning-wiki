# Students receiving the most Scooter supplementary exercises showed significantly better learning and caught up by post-test, though there was no overall between-condition learning effect

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The top third of students by supplementary exercises received had significantly better learning than the other two thirds, starting behind but catching up by post-test. [→ Koedinger 2008](#koedinger-2008)

## Evidence

### Koedinger 2008

Koedinger, K., Aleven, V., Roll, I., & Baker, R. (2008). In vivo experiments on whether supporting metacognition in intelligent tutoring systems yields robust learning. https://learnlab.org/

`q3 · i?`

Secondary analysis of the Scooter in vivo experiment relating supplementary-exercise dosage to pre-post learning gains (Figures 8 and 9). The most-supplemented third "started out behind the rest of the class, but caught up by the post-test"; no effect size printed.

> "The third of students (out of the overall sample) that received the most supplementary exercises had significantly better learning than the other two thirds of the students, as shown in Figure 8."
