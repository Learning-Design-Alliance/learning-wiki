Within-group Wilcoxon tests in a 16-week pilot RCT with 50 secondary students (25 per class-level-randomized group). The experimental group improved significantly; the control group did not, with the article reporting "No changes were observed in the control group".

> "A significant increase in HRV was found in the experimental group (W = 78.0, p = 0.022), indicating better autonomic nervous system regulation and improved stress management. No changes were observed in the control group (W = 143.0, p = 0.615)"
