Engagement analysis of eSpark usage data at Avonworth, where ratings were coded 1 for liked, -1 for disliked, 0 for no rating. The report found "The average rating was 0.68," alongside student interview and classroom observation evidence of engagement.

"The average rating was 0.68, suggesting that on average, students were more likely to like the apps or video than dislike them"
