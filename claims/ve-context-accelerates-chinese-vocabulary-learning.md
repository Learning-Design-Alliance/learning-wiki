## Subclaims
`q2 i?` Learners of Chinese as a foreign language in a virtual environment context showed a larger acceleration in their vocabulary learning trajectory than learners in a traditional web-based context. [→ Yu-Ju Lan 2015](#yu-ju-lan-2015)

## Evidence

### Yu-Ju Lan 2015

Yu-Ju Lan, Nian-Shing Chen, Ping Li, Scott Grant. (2015). Embodied cognition and language learning in virtual environments. Education Tech Research Dev 63. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9401-x

`q2 · i?` · `review · r1`

Training study in which 31 monolingual English speakers were randomly assigned to virtual or traditional web-based learning contexts (zoo, supermarket, kitchen) for 90 Chinese words; accuracy, reaction time, and exposure were analyzed with variance and mixed-effects modeling, showing "a larger acceleration in the learning trajectory" in the VE context.

> "The results showed a larger acceleration in the learning trajectory for the participants in the VE context than those in the traditional learning context. The results suggest that simulated embodied experience in the VE may have aided vocabulary acquisition in a second language."
