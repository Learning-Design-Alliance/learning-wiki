`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

ANCOVA of post-test scores in the RCT, adjusted for academic year and gender, with pretest scores as a covariate. The model was significant (F (4, 43) = 7.44, p = 0.0001; adjusted R-squared 0.3541); gender (coef. = −2.390069, p = 0.512) and academic year effects were not significant.

> "the ANCOVA, which controlled for the impacts of academic year and gender, revealed significant differences in the examination scores between the groups"
