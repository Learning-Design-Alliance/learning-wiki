### Kalyuga 2026

Kalyuga, S. (2026). Instructional Implications of Evolutionary Perspective on Human Cognitive Architecture as an Intelligent Natural Information Processing System. Educational Psychology Review, 38, 33. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10135-6

`q1 · i?` · `theoretical · r2`

Theoretical argument in the section on explicit learning of heuristics: the article reasons that heuristics originating as evolved primary abilities become biologically secondary when consciously used, so "general problem-solving heuristics such as means-ends analysis require explicit learning like any other biologically secondary knowledge." No empirical data are reported.

> "However, when used in explicitly controlled processes in biologically secondary domains, general problem-solving heuristics such as means-ends analysis require explicit learning like any other biologically secondary knowledge."
