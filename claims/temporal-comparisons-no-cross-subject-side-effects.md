From the Educational Impact and Implications Statement summarizing the studies' findings on temporal comparisons. The article reports that "achievement gains in one subject lead to a higher self-concept in the same subject, but usually do not affect self-concept in another subject," consistent with near-zero paths from achievement changes to noncorresponding self-concepts.
