## Evidence

### Melody M. Chao 2025

Melody M. Chao, Allen H. Huang, Anirban Mukhopadhyay & Janghoon Shon. (2025). Divergent effects of mindsets on performance trajectories. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00355-w

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Longitudinal regressions of 915 students' Final CGA and Term GPA on mindset, controlling for gender and intake cohort. The Final CGA effect was marginal (p = 0.086); the term-level effect was significant, "positively associated with their performance at the term level". Only coefficients were printed, no effect size.

> "students who endorsed stronger growth mindsets did indeed show better performance overall (Final CGA: B = 0.023, SE = 0.013, p = 0.086; Column 1). Furthermore, growth mindset was also positively associated with their performance at the term level (Term GPA:B = 0.023,SE = 0.006, p < 0.001; Column 2)"
