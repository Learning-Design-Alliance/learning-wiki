## Evidence

### Maloney S 2013

Maloney S, Chamberlain M, Morrison S, Kotsanas G, Keating JL, Ilic D. (2013). Health Professional Learner Attitudes and Use of Digital Learning Resources. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2094

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Online questionnaire of 39 fourth-year students (81% response rate) with 5-point Likert items. For efficiency, "28 (71.8)" strongly agreed; 38 of 39 agreed or strongly agreed that Physeek helped revise practical skills for clinical placement.

> "Physeek has increased the efficiency of searching for and locating clinical revision resources	1 (2.6)	0 (0)	0 (0)	10 (25.6)	28 (71.8)"

### Maloney S 2013 (2)

Maloney S, Chamberlain M, Morrison S, Kotsanas G, Keating JL, Ilic D. (2013). Health Professional Learner Attitudes and Use of Digital Learning Resources. Journal of Medical Internet Research. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.2094

`q2 · i?` · `qualitative · r3`

Thematic analysis of 3 focus groups (18 fourth-year students in 3 groups of 6) found students were "more confident in using them" because resources were academically produced and updated, contrasting the repository with general web search.

> "Many students felt that because resources were produced and updated by an academic organization (ie, the university, evidence-based resources), they were more confident in using them: You know it’s reliable instead of like Google, at least stuff on Physeek is actually correct."
