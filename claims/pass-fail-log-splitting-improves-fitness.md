## Evidence

### Cerezo 2019

Cerezo, R., Bogarín, A., & Romero, C. (2019). Process Mining for self-regulated learning assessment in eLearning. Originally published 2018. [doi:10.1007/s12528-019-09225-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12528-019-09225-y)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Process discovery analysis comparing Inductive Miner fitness across three groupings (Pass, Fail, all students). The article reports "the improving effect of grouping the data by dividing logs into Pass and Fail students"; Table 3 shows, for example, All Units fitness of 0.872 (Pass), 0.881 (Fail) and 0.659 (all students).

> "The same table also shows the improving effect of grouping the data by dividing logs into Pass and Fail students instead of using all the students."
