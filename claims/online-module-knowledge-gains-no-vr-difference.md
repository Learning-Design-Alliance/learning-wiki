A 15-question multiple-choice test administered pre- and post-training via the learning management system in the pilot RCT. The article reports the pooled improvement at P < 0.0001 and no significant between-group difference; no effect size is printed.

> "The participants across both groups showed significant improvements in theoretical knowledge tests (pre-training: 50% [40-67]; post-training: 80% [73-93]; P < 0.0001), but there was no statistically significant difference between the two groups (Table 2)."
