## Evidence

### Yang Lin 2020

Yang Lin, Li Yanzi, Liu Jianlin, Liu Yamin. (2020). Effect of vascular simulation training on practice performance in residents: a retrospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-037338

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Post-training survey in the retrospective cohort: willingness for retraining favoured ST (93.62% vs 79.17%, p=0.04). No effect size was printed.

> "when asked whether they wished to participate in the training again, the residents in the ST group showed a higher willingness rate than residents in the CT groups (93.62% vs 79.17%, p=0.04)"

### Yang Lin 2020 (2)

Yang Lin, Li Yanzi, Liu Jianlin, Liu Yamin. (2020). Effect of vascular simulation training on practice performance in residents: a retrospective cohort study. BMJ Open. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2020-037338

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Overall satisfaction rates after clinical training did not differ significantly between groups (p>0.05); equivalence was not tested.

> "the training satisfaction rates of all residents were similar (97.87% vs 91.67%, p>0.05)"
