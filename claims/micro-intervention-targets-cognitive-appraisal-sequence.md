Theoretical argument in Part 2.1 applying Lazarus and Folkman's cognitive reappraisal account: the intervention functions as "an external cognitive prosthesis" helping students reinterpret stressful events to promote persistence and problem-focused coping.

> "Alters the primary appraisal: It re-categorizes the "threat" as a common challenge, shifting the student's perception from "This is a sign of my failure" to "This is a normal part of the process.""
