## Evidence

### Chassell 1916

Chassell, L. M. (1916). Tests for Originality. The Journal of Educational Psychology, VII (June, 1916). https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070310

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Correlational finding from the 100-student trial, supplemented by a questionnaire on prior anagram play. The article attributes Word-Building's lowest correlation to practice advantage, noting subjects who had played anagrams "had some advantage over other members of the group."

> "This is probably due to the fact that about one-half of the subjects had played anagrams, as was ascertained by means of a questionnaire, and these subjects consequently had some advantage over other members of the group."
