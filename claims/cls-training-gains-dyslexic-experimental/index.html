Evidence¶

Pfenninger 2015¶

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon pre-post comparisons within groups in the three-month intervention study; the Test Dyslexic group improved on three rapid naming tests while the Control Dyslexic group "showed the opposite pattern", scoring significantly lower on post-PICRAN-L2 and post-PICRAN-L3.

"While the Test Dyslexic group was able to make significant gains over time on the DIGRAN-L2 test (z = 2.42, p = .02), the DIGRAN-L3 test (z = 2.78, p = .005) and the PICRAN-L3 test (z = 2.68, p = .007), the Control Dyslexic group showed the opposite pattern"

Pfenninger 2015 (2)¶

Pfenninger, S. E. (2015). MSL in the digital ages: Effects and effectiveness of computer-mediated intervention for FL learners with dyslexia. Studies in Second Language Learning and Teaching, 5(1), 109-133. https://doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2015.5.1.6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-post Wilcoxon comparison on the L3 receptive vocabulary sentence-comprehension task (maximum = 6) in the intervention study; the Test Dyslexic group reached ceiling-level performance, and the Control Dyslexic group remained significantly lower (U = 100, p < .001).

"The Test Dyslexic group made significant gains over time (pre-LISTEN-L3 vs. post-LISTEN-L3: z = -2.78, p = .005), to the extent that they caught up with the non-dyslexic readers"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • The training proved effective irrespective of reading ability and was more effective for the Test Dyslexic group than for the Test Nondyslexic group, which made greater gains on rapid naming, L3 receptive vocabulary and L3 picture naming — related
  • MANCOVA showed significant treatment effects for 11 of 15 dependent measures with consistently high effect sizes, while L2 reading speed, L2 spelling, L2 rapid digit naming and L3 stimulated vocabulary retrieval did not change as a function of the intervention — related
  • L2 reading speed (SLRT-I) and L2 spelling showed no improvement as a function of the intervention, and no group made significant gains in L2 spelling — related
  • The Test Dyslexic group outperformed the Control Dyslexic group on the post-TOWRE L3 word decoding measures despite equivalent pretest performance, but did not catch up with either nondyslexic group — related
  • Higher L2 vocabulary retrieval performance correlated strongly with higher L3 retrieval performance, and nondyslexic learners outperformed dyslexic learners on all stimulated vocabulary retrieval measures — related
