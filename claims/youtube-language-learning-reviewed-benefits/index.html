Alwehaibi 2015¶

Alwehaibi, H. O. (2015). The Impact Of Using YouTube In EFL Classroom On Enhancing EFL Students' Content Learning. Journal of College Teaching & Learning, 12(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1061416

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Narrative review attribution: the article reports Alimemaj's claim about listening skills and quotes her student interviews calling YouTube a "great online learning tool" that can help students develop learning autonomy. The reviewed studies' designs are not described in this article.

"Alimemaj (2010) claims that technological advancements and the proliferation of broadband in traditional classroom situations means that English classes and learners have an enormously varied new tool to improve listening skills."
