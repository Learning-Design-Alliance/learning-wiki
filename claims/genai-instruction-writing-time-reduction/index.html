Evidence¶

Allison E. Connell Pensky 2025¶

Allison E. Connell Pensky, Jordan H. Usdan, Harley Chang. (2025). Generative AI's Impact on Graduate Student Professional Writing Productivity and Quality. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00528-z

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Within-subjects classroom study of 27 graduate students comparing timed memo assignments without genAI and with genAI assistance. Median time fell from 150 to 65 minutes, "a 56.7% reduction in time"; the test printed Z = −4.32, p <.001, rrb = − 0.61.

"A Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test revealed that the within-subjects variable of writing condition (no genAI, genAI-assisted) significantly impacted productivity, Z = −4.32, p <.001, with a large effect size (rank biserial rrb = − 0.61)."
