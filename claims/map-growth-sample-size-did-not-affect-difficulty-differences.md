# Calibration sample size differences did not affect the size of on-grade versus all-grade item difficulty estimate differences

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · associational `r2` · `q2`

## Subclaims
`q2 i?` On-grade and original item difficulty estimates are highly correlated, and the correlation between calibration sample size difference and parameter estimate difference is small for most grades. [→ Wei He 2021](#wei-he-2021)

## Evidence

### Wei He 2021

Wei He, NWEA Psychometric Solutions. (2021). An Investigation of Item Parameter Invariance Using Focused Calibration Samples for MAP Growth. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/an-investigation-of-item-parameter-invariance-using-focused-calibration-samples-for-map-growth/

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r2`

Study 1 computed correlations between on-grade and original estimates (Table 3.2); the report describes these as close to 1 for most grades and states the sample-size correlations "did not affect the difference between the on-grade and the original item difficulty estimates."

> "implying that the calibration sample sizes used to derive the on-grade and the original item difficulties did not affect the difference between the on-grade and the original item difficulty estimates."
