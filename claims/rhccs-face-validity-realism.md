## Evidence

### Rohr A. 2022

Rohr A., Perrenot C., Pitta A., Celerier I., Labrousse M., Renard Y., Cadiot G., Brugel M. (2022). Reperfused human cadaver as a new simulation model for colonoscopy: a pilot study. Surgical Endoscopy. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00464-022-09763-0

`q2 · i?` · `design · r2`

Face validity assessment: 11 skilled participants completed a simulation realism survey on 7-point Likert scales after their colonoscopy. Items rated very favorable included "endoscopic view" (5.0, IQR: 4.5–6.0) and "resistance to scope advancement" (6.0, IQR: 4.5–6.5).

> "Nineteen out of the 22 items composing the realism survey obtained “very favorable” and “favorable” scores."
