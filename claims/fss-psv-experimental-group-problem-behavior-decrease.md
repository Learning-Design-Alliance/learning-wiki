## Evidence

### Çelik 2016

Çelik, S., Diken, İ. H., Çolak, A., Arıkan, A., Bozkurt, F., & Tomris, G. (2016). Effectiveness of the preschool version of the first step to success early intervention program for preventing antisocial behaviors. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 16, 511-535. https://doi.org/10.12738/estp.2016.2.0317

`q3 · i3` · `causal · r2`

Paired-samples t-tests on the 11 experimental-group children's PKBS problem behavior scores; the article also reports the pretest-follow-up contrast as significant with a large ES (t(10) = 5.57, p < .05, η2 = 0.75).

> "posttest total problem behavior scores of students from experimental group were significantly lower than those of pretest scores, and the implementation of the program had a sizeable effect size (ES) on problem behaviors (t(10) = 3.35, p < .05, η2 = 0.52)."
