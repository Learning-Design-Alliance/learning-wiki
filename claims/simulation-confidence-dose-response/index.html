The review's synthesis of confidence studies reports "single-session interventions often result in only temporary or modest improvements, indicating a possible dose–response effect". Repeated high-fidelity exposure was associated with stronger confidence.

"In contrast, single-session interventions often result in only temporary or modest improvements, indicating a possible dose–response effect."
