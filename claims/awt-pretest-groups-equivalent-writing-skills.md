## Evidence

### Nazife Burcu TAKIL 2016

Nazife Burcu TAKIL. (2016). The use of “ask, write, throw” technique as a writing skill practice when teaching Turkish as a foreign language. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2016.2682

`q2 · i?` · `causal · r1`

Quasi-experimental pre-test comparison of 18 experimental and 17 control B2 learners, evaluated by three specialists on a five-criteria scoring key. The article reports the groups were "at similar levels (p>0.05)" on all criteria before the technique was applied.

> "It can be observed from Table 4 that before the application of the technique, the relevant writing skills of both groups were at similar levels (p>0.05)."
