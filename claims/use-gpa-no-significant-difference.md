## Evidence

### Sejer EPF 2024

Sejer EPF, Ladelund AK, Bruun FJ, Slavensky JA, Mortensen EL, Kesmodel US. (2024). Preterm birth and subsequent intelligence and academic performance in youth: A cohort study. Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.14796

`q3 · i?` · `associational · r3`

Linear regression analysis of USE grade averages in the Danish register cohort (Table 4); Table 2 shows USE total GPA p = 0.13. The article reports the differences "did not reach statistical significance"; equivalence was not tested.

> "Conversely, in USE, preterm birth was associated with higher GPAs in the adjusted analyses (except for core subject GPA in the extremely preterm group); however, these differences did not reach statistical significance."
