# Reading provided examples is not more effective than generating examples for learning relational categories

> **Claim** · [All claims](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 study · causal `r2` · `q3`

## Subclaims
`q3 i?` The planned contrast testing whether reading examples beats generating examples was not significant on the multivariate test or any univariate follow-up. [→ Tim M. Steininger 2022](#tim-m-steininger-2022)

## Evidence

### Tim M. Steininger 2022

Tim M. Steininger, Jörg Wittwer, Thamar Voss. (2022). Classifying Examples is More Effective for Learning Relational Categories Than Reading or Generating Examples. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09584-7

`q3 · i?` · `causal · r2`

In the same N = 83 experiment, the learning-by-reading hypothesis was tested with a planned contrast (reading +1, generating −1, classifying 0). The result was "not significant, V = 0.038 (Pillai’s trace), F(4, 77) = 0.76, p = .277"; no effect size beyond ηp2 = 0.04 was interpreted.

> "The multivariate analysis was not significant, V = 0.038 (Pillai’s trace), F(4, 77) = 0.76, p = .277 ηp2 = 0.04. Follow-up univariate analyses were also not significant (see Table 3). Hence, reading examples was not more effective than generating examples."
