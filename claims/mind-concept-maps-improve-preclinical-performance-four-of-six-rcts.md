Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42024529458, PRISMA) of six RCTs in preclinical undergraduate medical education, synthesized narratively because heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis. The review reports that the majority of studies show significant enhancement of "knowledge retention, recall, and assessment performance compared to traditional study methods."
