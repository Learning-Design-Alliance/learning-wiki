### Josephine C. Meyer 2022

Josephine C. Meyer, Gina Passante, Steven J. Pollock, and Bethany R. Wilcox. (2022). The interdisciplinary quantum information classroom: Themes from a survey of quantum information science instructors. arXiv:2202.05944. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2202.05944](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2202.05944)

`q2 · i?` · `associational · r1`

Instructor survey of introductory QIS courses (N = 28 responses after screening) with thematic analysis and follow-up interviews with six faculty. The survey found "Fully 25% of the surveyed courses ( N = 7 )" cross-listed, and most courses listed in physics and/or computer science departments.

> "Fully 25% of the surveyed courses ( N = 7 ) are cross-listed in two or more departments, a strong indication that the instructor has an interdisciplinary audience in mind."
